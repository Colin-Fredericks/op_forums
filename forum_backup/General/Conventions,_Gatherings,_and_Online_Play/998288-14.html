                Magistrates

                Roll Investigation (Difficulty 3) for your demon to see if Naburo finds the correct people to encourage conversation to be overheard. The Pahua Basin is a big place.

                House of Bells

                Naburo asks if you have any one in mind for him to focus on at the House of Bells. Roll either War or Bureaucracy (Difficulty 2) to give the demon a bonus to overhearing something about Roseblack.

                Scholars Luncheon

                The topic of the Luncheon is the Influence of Wyld Energies on Languages of Creation.                 Kebok Taite gives a talk on his radical language theory. He believes once the population of the Realm spoke on Language. During the Fair Folk Invasion, the Wyld Energies they brought with them fractured the languages into two (Low Realm and High Realm). Taite's research shows a correlation between level of Wyld energies and linguistic drift. He also supposes this could be the reason why Riverspeak emerged, though he has not gotten any readings from the East. (He has not gotten permission to visit the East.) The luncheon devolves into a lively argument about Taite's theory. As the scholars drink more wine, they bellow their criticisms and theory louder and louder. A good time is had by all.

                There are one of the contacts listed on Akashan's list. One is a godblooded Archivist by the name of Heeding Ink. She has pure white skin like paper. Dark marks fade and appear on her skin. You recognize these as the Old Realm transcription of the conversation occurring around the godblooded woman. (She will be a Contact 2, as one of the Archivist in House Ledaal.) She is in conversation with a tall woman with dark skin and hair. The tall woman flashes you a smile when she sees you glance in their direction. The tall woman motions you over to join the conversation.

                What do you do?


                The Preservation Method

                Have Ledaal Darino roll either Craft or Occult (Difficulty 3) to learn enough about the Preservation method to speak intelligently about it. If he fails, he can roll Socialize(Difficulty 3) to attempt to bullshit his way though the conversation.
