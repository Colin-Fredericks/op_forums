    Originally posted by MagicSwordsman View Post
    Out of curiosity, is this going to count as "interacting with an ephemeral entity" for Binder's Obsession?
    Absolutely! You can take an Arcane Beat now so I don't forget later. Now that we're gaining more understanding of the rules, I do think Obsessions are meant to be long-term in nature, so they should be analogous to long-term Aspirations but relate to supernatural phenomena and give Arcane Beats instead of Beats. I will probably not give an Arcane Beat every time you simply interact with an ephemeral entity -- it has to be significant progress. But in this case, I'd say it's pretty significant, and very direct involvement for Binder with significant risk of harm.

    For everyone else: I don't have your Aspirations and Obsessions memorized, and I sometimes forget to check them. Same thing with Virtues/Vices. I'll try to keep them in mind, but it's a lot easier for you to post here or in the IC thread and ask me if you can get credit for satisfying something along those lines. That way you can make sure you're getting fair rewards for roleplay.

                                    So I think I'd like to do some creative thaumaturgy, though I'm honestly surprised something that does this isn't in the book. I'm thinking a Mind Ruling spell, primary Potency, that reduces wound penalties by Potency? Like:

                                    Mind over Matter (Mind **)
                                    Practice: Ruling
                                    Primary Factor: Potency
                                    Suggested Rote Skills: Medicine, Survival, Subterfuge
                                    This spell allows the subject to overcome pain. Reduce penalties suffered by the subject due to pain or discomfort (including wound penalties) by the spell's Potency.
                                    +1 Reach: Spend 1 Mana. The subject does not fall unconscious due to wounds.
                                    I like it! In reviewing the Creative Thaumaturgy rules, it seems like a Shielding spell, because you're offering protection against the effects of fatigue/pain on your mind. But that's still a 2-dot Practice, so I don't think it changes anything. I also don't think the Reach option should require mana. Most mana costs I see for spells include upgrading damage/healing type, or very special effects. You're essentially just shielding yourself from a mundane condition (unconscious) for the duration of the spell.

                                    I agree that Potency makes sense as the PSF. Don't forget to boost Duration when you cast, though.
