                        Mekhane takes a seat at the table and starts focusing on Jack, drawing on his own authority over knowledge to access his psychographic profile. Countless pixels of data emerge from Jack's mind, each one containing a significant event, orbiting his head in a sphere of small screens. He builds an Imago to parse through them all, then with one of the hands he has under the table, he starts taking notes, like writing down lookup references in a library. The data starts to condense into an implant above Jack's amygdala, the pixels falling into place to form a microchip. Its form reminds him of depictions of Baghdad's House of Wisdom, but its design far more optimized, every building and street contributing to the overall efficiency of the infrastructure.

                        The microchip comes to life, the circuitry of the grand library responding to his lookup request, projecting streams of matching results into his eyes. He first received data on the one responsible for Jack in the Order, the name Emma Cottle being returned, the head of the medical clinic, his profile indicating powerful paranoia, sadness and ....a sense Jack knows where her body is. The profiler then examines his connection to Trish, defined by a firm sense of duty.

                        Amidst the data streams, certain factoids draw his eye. Taken independently, they would mean nothing, but Mekhane senses that when put together at the right time, they would undeniably lead to great opportunities. He commits them to memory, sensing the hand of the Chancellor at work.

                        "A mage with medical connections, not one I am familiar with, I will need to ask around later. You certainly are not Nameless. What posed such a threat that it managed to kill her and make you want to hide the evidence ? We will see..."

Though they reign supreme over all. You will understand in time. The realm of Arcadia is a vast domain." He thinks about Tyche. There's something about her voice that reminds him of the posh woman Jo said was Trish.

                                                        He takes his phone and looks up the address on the paper. Its that mega skyscraper. The Spike or something like that. It's where Jo's new job is, she's working as a waitress or something. He chuckles at the idea of Jo being a waitress. They better be nice to her, or she'd spit in their food. Hopefully the tips are good. He looks at out the window and sees the giant thing in the distance. You can't avoid looking at it, now matter where you go in London now. It's always looming in the background. It looks like a...wait, didn't Tyche say something once about a Pyramid. Like an Iron Pyramid. That's how she described the ladder to get to the Exarchs. Is this all connected some how?

"All the kingdoms of this world I will give you, if you bow down and worship me." That's something Mum says in church sometimes. But there is no god. There are only the Exarchs.
