            "I am unsure of the way things are done, but I will also need a more suitable scabbard for my weapon."

            He unslung the large cloth wrapped bundle from his back, glancing at a table before leaning it against a wall. He had learned that lesson the hard way.

            "Also, a cloak for me as well. It wouldn't be good to go around looking as though I am girded for battle."

            Without another word, he started removing the concealing robes. In truth they were simple wraps of spare fabric. Many of the gods who had been present at the calibration party had dressed as characters out of legend. As gods did not possess the flaring souls of the chosen, bright frabric was the usual method of representing the iconic imagery of Exalted. The eagle balanced on a planted sword of Red Tower Burning, the scroll wrapped pillars of Street of Sapphire, even the one hundred and eight stars of Sun-Eclipsing Splendor picked out in red on deepest navy in honor of the great Steward who had briefly ruled the Blessed Isle. These and a half dozen more proudly competed for the eye as Splendor undressed. Then the room seemed to grow dark.

            His armor was forged from the feathers of Anzu, a spirit of fire clad in the soot of its burning. Each shard of the beast had the appearance of smoky glass. They drunk in light and blazed with a heart of red flame, writhing as though in a struggle against the mantras graven on their edges. Stroking the back of his fingers against the effigy of its head as one would pet a bird, it began to retract into itself. While worn it had looked as though great wings enfolded him. What remained was a creature half dragon and half bird, each of its four limbs bearing a folded wing, and its long feathered tail frozen as though mid lash. If it moved it did so with subtlety, if it did not move it almost seemed to. Haughty eyes, each a massive ruby, seemed to judge those present and see nothing worthy of more than contempt.

            "I think there was a little too much of his spirit bound in his remains; and I gave it too much voice, or to little."

            Setting the thing down carefully his own garb was now clear to see. Bright, bright blue, bright and radiant as the morpho wings from which it was woven, and covered on both sides with words in the finest hand. It was fashioned like a single scroll, woven over and under itself into herringbone patterns. Carefully, and gently, he rolled it back into the scroll it had begun as before bringing the two halves of the case together and looking at the title, A Lover Clad in Blue. Ah how clever he had thought he was, just scandalous enough to be talked about without being so low as to be shameful. He had heard a few snips behind his back at the party, positive proof that he had succeeded. How petty it all seemed now.

            Looking down he noticed his shoes were indeed coming apart. They hadn't been meant to last more than the night anyway. Their survival up to this point was nothing short of remarkable. He caught his reflection in a mirror and took a moment to consider it. Iron grey hair, coarse and stiff, had grown longer than he liked to keep it. Pale northern eyes, looked back at him from a narrow face, thinner than he remembered. Skin that looked like it had never seen the sun had finally taken something like a faint tan, something he was thankful for, but the glassy, hand-shaped scar in the middle of his back remained.

            Turning to the tailor, and seemingly unphased by his own nudity, he smiled and said, "I think something in stock that can be altered might be best. I can pay a little extra if it was meant for someone else, but fast is probably more important than perfect right now."
