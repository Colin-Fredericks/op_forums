                HaplessWithDice
                Soundless Forest looks good overall but there are some things:
                - We don't use the Sifu and Familiar backgrounds. A 3-dot Sifu is probably a 3-dot Mentor, but a 2-dot Familiar is probably a 1-dot Ally.
                -                 In addition to Faction backing you should have Backing from a Directional Convention and from the Violet Bier. 1 each is enough. (Lucky Wei doesn't have Convention backing because he's a wastrel, but by default everyone should buy Backing 1 from their Division and from one of the Directional Conventions.)
                -                 A Presence specialty in "persuasion" seems overly broad. When do you figure it would not apply?
                -                 I don't understand your "Philosophy" Intimacies. The others are clear enough, but what does it mean to have an intimacy towards "Death is a reprieve, not a punishment", or ""A sword is an extension of its bearer's soul"? Not everything your character believes needs or ought to be represented as an Intimacy.

                4uk4ata:

                Is there a way to ensure that a godblooded or dragonblooded ally with a landship can be reasonably close at hand on a short notice wherever we need to go? For example, can a god of crossroad inns move the inn from one crossroad to another?
                A god or god-blooded could have a variant of Hurry Home that would work that way. A dragon-blooded not so much. You'll probably have to go to them rather than expect them to come halfway across the world to follow you. On the other hand, Sidereals have pretty awesome traveling capabilities, so even if your ally can't get to wherever you are, you can probably get to them on short notice.
