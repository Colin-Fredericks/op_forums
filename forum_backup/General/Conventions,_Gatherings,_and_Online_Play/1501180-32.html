                                I think Qaf would have studied in LAU for something like a self defense certification or fitness instruction, since he dreams of having his own martial arts school, but he is still is building capital and developing his own style. The Chronicles of Darkness core book has a baseline for skills, along with detailed rules and equipment information, plus lots of interesting GM plots, its a very easy recommendation. But anyway, when looking at skills the baseline is:

                                ● - Basic Competency: You took a shooting class (Firearms), take better care of yourself than most people (Athletics), understand a few forensic techniques (Investigation)
                                ●● - Professional Skill: You could earn a living with your degree of knowledge, you have a solid, reliable level of ability.
                                ●●● - National Level Expert: You are considered an expert at what you do and a reference in your skill.
                                ●●●● - Global Tier Master: You have enough mastery to be a mover and shaker in your field, being considered on par with the world's leading experts.
                                ●●●●● - Peak Human Ability: You can deduce like Sherlock Holmes (Investigation), cook on par with Gordon Ramsay (Crafts) or snipe like Simo Hahya (firearms).

                                However, keep in mind that style merits can still account for a massive gap in efficiency, such as having a fighting style making you spend far less Willpower, professional training giving more consistent results or even having done all the work to have an air tight Alternative Identity when spying. There are also highly skilled experts in the shadows, so who knows who you may run into.
