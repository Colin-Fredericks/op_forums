    Originally posted by Aeroz View Post
    From my existing players please describe your character and your main plans as far as stats. Since your paths have changed and you have been working to cover gaps I am not 100% on where you guys intend to go with them
    I'm playing Alric, a thief, gambler and womanizer from the North. Alric is a good sort, but has spent most of his life fending for himself so he's a bit aloof until you get to know him. He always keeps an escape plan (for himself) in mind in case things go south for the group, but won't admit to himself that he wouldn't abandon his companions. He's a smartass and not afraid of causing a little trouble. He's also a little loose with concepts of "ownership" or "property". As the character least exposed to the supernatural world, he tends to operate as the voice of the uninitiated when dealing with weird stuff. Recently he's been finding he has some affinity for animals (mounts) and finds them to be good, loyal company that he's willing to defend.

    My main areas of long term character development are Athletics, Larceny, Ride, Investigation and Socialize. I will be doing a little rounding out with Melee and Resistance in the short term.

    As for a Black and White game, I hope you're not getting the wrong opinion. This means we generally try to help people, or at a minimum don't make things worse. This doesn't always work out, however. What we are not is thugs, murderers or sociopaths. This is a heroic game, but they are flawed heroes. Much of the fun that I have in the game isn't the powers of the characters, though there's lots of amazing things you can do with the non-combat charms (Athletics especially), it's the interactions between contrary personalities and goals as we try to figure out what it is to be Exalted.

            I wouldn't say that the game is black and white. The players are more the lighter shade of grey. None of us are Captain America (We had a character who was basically Captain America and he left the game.) As Aeroz said, we aren't saints, but we aren't the type to sit by and watch people suffer. This is a cliff notes version of how our characters operate:

            Mayu (Twilight): Oh noes! We did something to cause trouble for these people (Because let's be honest, this is how it usually happens). We should fix this!
            Alric (Night): What? No! That is a bad idea! We need to get out of here! It's too much of a risk of exposing ourselves!
            Mayu and Alric: What do you think, Bloodless?
            Bloodless (Dusk): Umm..... *Goes and punches thing that is causing the problem* Does this help?

            So, yes. We fuck up...a lot. We are definitely flawed heroes (even without limit breaks). Mayu is a bit short sighted (ironically). Alric is a bit self centered. Bloodless is constantly fighting between his desire to not kill people and his abyssal nature. But at the end of the day, the three of us couldn't live with ourselves if we let people suffer for our actions.


            So, what Aeroz he asked for:

            Mayu is one of the many Twilight characters who exalted in Lookshy, though her primary focus isn't crafting. She sees crafting as a means to an end. Mayu is more of the scholarly type who was completely shocked at the general ignorance of everyone outside of the River Provinces. Her main motivation is to start distributing all the lost First Age lore that she has acquired since her exaltation and try to raise the general educational level of the population. As a first step, she wants to build a printing press and start writing books under a pseudonym. She also has some deal with a Library god to increase the number of libraries in Creation (a long term goal).

            Mayu is a bit trigger happy when it comes to the "monsters" of creation. If it is Fae, Demonic, or other dangerous looking creature, she will shoot first and ask questions later.

            As for skills, Mayu is a know it all (Lore, Occult). If she doesn't know, she will use Awareness and Investigation to find out. She is a very competent archer and relies on not getting hit in combat (Dodge).

            As for future plans, Mayu is going to be focusing on Archery and other Combat Charms. Then she will pick up some Linguistics Charms and All-Encompassing Sorcerer's Sight. After that, maybe flesh out her craft? Mayu is on the path to be initiated into Sorcery, but I doubt I am going to take anything besides the counter spell, the messenger spell, and maybe the floating platform. (I'm mostly doing the initiation for story reasons).

As a side quest Bloodless does want to learn malfean drinking songs though.

                                    Bloodless is surprisingly tolerant of supernatural things because mundane things tend to bother him more. Ultimately he is a very simple man with loyalties being built by good food and good company.
