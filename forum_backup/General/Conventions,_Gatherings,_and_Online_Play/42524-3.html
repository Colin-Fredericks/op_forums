                    Hold the presses, I have an idea that changes everything.

                    Open your Wonders of the Lost Age p.157.

                    Unyielding Fury of Righteousness was the red jade warstrider gifted by Chejop Kejak to the Scarlet Empress herself, Forged in her effigy and never used, it was stored in a crate and forgotten by all. Until today.

                    In the wake of the Empress's disappearance, a group of Dynasts feeling the approach of a civil war and wanting to secure an overwhelming asset found the paper trail of the relic and set up the heist of many centuries. Dragon-Blooded and loyal mortal retainers gathered to steal into the winter palace of Arjuf and make off with Fury. One of these mortals was a young red-haired girl of Fire Aspect, here to assist her older brother.

                    Of course, once inside the palace things went sour. The Dragon-Blooded turned on each other in their desire to take the wonder for themselves, and the sorcerous safeties of the place turned on them. Hounded by spirits and their own brothers, mortals and Exalts died one by one. The young Fire Aspect managed to find her way to the warsrider's crate and open it along with her brother, but while she was desperately rushing to reactivate it and open its piloting compartment a whirling cloud of blades killed him and left her alone. Blazing red hot with Essence and excitation, the girl managed to reactivate the Artifact...

                    Waking up a confused and sleepy AI who mistook her for her original owner and happily offered her full control of Fury. The girl escaped in a blaze of fire and jade, and immediately started running for her life. She hasn't stopped; in fact, her desperate trek led her to the middle of the Scavenger Lands, where she offers her service as a warstrider pilot and hopes to delay the moment when the Realm finally catches up to her. She doesn't consider herself a traitor, but she does not believe anyone would stop long enough to listen to her explanations. So she runs, going from kingdom to kingdom and building up a new legend.

                    Because she is a fiery-haired Dragon-Blooded piloting a legendary red jade warstrider depicting a very well-known woman, and her AI isn't the only one to have leaped to certain unfortunate conclusions. She has even taken to naming herself "Scarlet," which she swears is a direct translation of her original High Realm name. The AI likes it, at least.

                                    @Omnicron- Given the restrictive nature of the DB+UFR build I would advise against it. Not saying I won't let you do it, just saying I doubt it would be a good idea, since you would end up having little utility outside of mass-combat situations, and that could make the game boring for you. Something to ponder.

                                    @Dietaku- Looking good.

                                    Okay a reminder to everyone: Once you have finished your character, get it to me PRIVATELY, however you'd like. That way, I can give it a look over and give final approval, and you can post as much or as little you'd like on your character in the OOC thread, which I will be starting as soon as I get the first finished character.

                                    Some other administrative things. I am not sure how y'all want to deal with the mechanical end of the house, during play. I've heard of situations where the ST retained all control over dice rolls, but I think that as limiting your freedom as players. This is how I propose to deal with things, and how things were dealt with in the myriad of PbP games I've been in. You retain direct control over rolling dice and activating charms for your character, unless you give me a contingency, which I will activate if applicable (ex: If you say "If the Abyssal with the sword attacks me before my next action, I'll activate Crimson Palm Counterstrike in response", I'll activate the charm, roll the dice, etc. for your character if the Abyssal with the sword attacks you). Otherwise, I'll apply the results of your rolled actions to your targets, roll any NPC actions, and then tell you the result ("The Abyssal's lunge will connect, and would deal 15L raw damage. Do you respond?") I know this messes up a large part of the step-based combat system, but I feel that doing otherwise would either slow the game to a crawl or force you try and give me dozens of potential contingencies, and neither of those outcomes are fun. If no-one has any objections, that's how it will go.
