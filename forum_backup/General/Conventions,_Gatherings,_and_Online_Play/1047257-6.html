                Hardwire99 Survival gear works a little differently. It gives you a +1 bonus to relevant rolls, and downgrades extreme environments by 1. You can also buy better gear that gives +2 and downgrades by 2, but it's Availability 3.

                Post mundane and magic rolls in the IC thread, preferably at the end of your post, and I'll narrate how the spell turned out. You can always ask about something in the OOC thread if you're making a custom spell or what have you. Try to format spells this way:

                Quick description of what you're doing.
                Instant/Ritual, Touch/Aimed/Sensory, Standard/Advanced Duration, Standard/Advanced Scale, Standard/Advanced Potency, +X for other Reaches
                Reach: 1 free, 1 over
                Factors: Potency (primary) -X, Duration -Y, Scale -Z
                Yantras: whatever +1, something +2
                Nemesis: X from Reach, Y from other sources, -Z from Mana/Tool. Release/Contain. Nemesis roll. Wisdom roll.
                Spellcasting: Gnosis+Arche+Yantras-Penalties. Casting roll.
                Aimed spell: Gnosis+Athletics/Archery-Defense-Penalties. Aimed spell roll.

                Like this!

                Casting Psychic Domination on the guard tower through my telescope, commanding them to jump off.
                Instant, Sensory, Standard Duration, Advanced Scale, Standard Potency, +2 dangerous command (1 Mana), +1 remote viewing, +1 exceeding spell control.
                Reach: 2 free, 5 over
                Factors: Potency 3 (Primary), Duration 3 turns (-4), Scale 5 subjects
                Yantras: High Speech +2, Dedicated Tool +1, Environment +1
                Nemesis: 10 from Reach, 2 from previous Nemesis rolls, -6 from Mana, -2 from Tool. Releasing: 2 Nemesis.
                Spellcasting: Gnosis 3+Mind 3+Yantras 4-Factors 4-released Nemesis 2+Willpower 3. 7 dice, 4 successes.


                I strongly encourage you to use Down and Dirty Spellcasting on Mage 117 to save time. If you're casting a spell that's well within your capabilities, the exact spell factors aren't that important, and failure wouldn't be interesting: just state your intent, roll Gnosis+Arche directly and move on. If you fail this roll, I'll probably give you a Condition or some Nemesis damage. You can do this to give [successes] bonus or penalty dice for [successes] number of specific actions on up to [successes] subjects, learn [successes] interesting facts about a subject, cause a more narrative effect, that kind of thing. Quick and easy!

                Silver_Smoulder Post rolls in the IC thread. Use Orokos!

                Survival gear works as I mentioned above, so just a +1 bonus for this roll. And as hardwire said, just pick 2 of the 4.

                Leather armor is 2/0, strength 2, defense -1, availability 1.

                I don't usually track individual arrows in RPGs, but let's try it this chapter and see if it's interesting or a pain. Just note how many you have left at the end of a post. "Reloading" is an instant action, but you can make it reflexive with a Ruling spell on yourself or the bow.

                Right now you have a basic hunting bow, size 3, range 30/60/120. You might be interested in the compound bow stats: [Strength] damage, range 40/80/160, size 4, availability 2.

                Talaus is no metallurgist, he just knows they make good iron blades, and lots of them. Meroitic tools and weapons are imported throughout Egypt, you could ask around and find one to examine yourself.
