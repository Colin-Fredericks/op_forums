    [Recruitment] Vampire: The Masquerade-New Jersey By Night

    02-18-2014, 09:54 PM
    Hey guys, I am working on a Camarilla chronicle for V20, loosely based on 1e and early 2e material for VTM. The game uses V20 mechanics (with one homebrew rule but more on that later), but has a lot of concepts from 1st Edition and early 2nd Edition introduced in. No material from Revised Edition will be used, and there will be no metaplot at all. The game is set in 2014, but no metaplot stuff happened at all. Even early metaplot events like Under a Blood Red Moon are assumed to have not happened. The Sabbat is much more mysterious and dark, much as they were in First Edition, and the main focus is on the Camarilla and Anarchs with the Sabbat and Independent Clans as nightmarish boogeymen.

    Unlike 1st Edition VTM, the East Coast is NOT under Sabbat control, but Camarilla control, New York City in particular has always been and will always be Camarilla turf in my games, including this one. Detroit and Montreal are still Sabbat domains, but NYC, Buffalo, Philadelphia, and Pittsburgh are all Camarilla instead of Sabbat. Instead, the cities of the Deep South (Atlanta, Savannah, Miami, Tampa, Birmingham, Memphis, Baton Rouge, Mobile, etc.) are all under Sabbat control.

    This particular game will be set in New Jersey, centered around Atlantic City and the Jersey Shore (the actual shoreline area, not that god-awful MTV show), although Newark, Trenton, and Jersey City will play major roles in the chronicle, as will New York City(albeit in an off-screen sort of way). The entire state of New Jersey is under the sway of the Prince of NYC, with the local Princes in New Jersey being subservient to him, although the Prince of Atlantic City has recently begun to build up his domain in an attempt to gain further autonomy and independence from the Prince of NYC's network of vassal domains.

    Also, the gamelines Kindred of the East, Hunter: The Reckoning, and Demon: The Fallen are explicitly non-canon to my games, so in other words, there are no Kuei-Jin (East Asia is dominated by Cainites, but the Camarilla, Sabbat, and Anarchs have no sway in Asia), there are no Fallen (nor is their version of events correct), and the Sixth Great Maelstrom never happened.

    The other cWoD gamelines, both canonical (Werewolf: The Apocalypse, Mage: The Ascension, Wraith: The Oblivion, Changeling: The Dreaming, Mummy: The Resurrection) and fan-made (Senshi: The Merchandising) are semi-canonical in my games. But they are only canon to this game in a broad strokes way. (Think 1st Edition VTM and WTA when Wraith and Changeling were called Ghost and Faerie). Instead of Garou, Awakened, Wraiths, Kithain, Amenti, and Senshi existing alongside the Kindred, you have Lupines, Magi, Ghosts, Faeries, Mummies, and Magical Girls

    Now, as for that one homebrew rule, see I'm trying to make this chronicle a mix of action, adventure, politics, and intrigue rather than personal horror, so to make combat more viable, health levels are done differently.

    Instead of one Bruised health level, a character has multiple Bruised health levels equivalent to their Stamina score. In other words, if a character has a Stamina score of 2, they have two health levels at "Bruised" and after those are depleted, only then will dice penalties kick in,

                      OK then. Are you looking for characters who are based in any particular part of NJ, like AC, or characters who expressly have nothing tying them down to one spot, or does that not matter? I have a rough idea (still working out the kinks in my head) for a Ventrue Anarch. Young, but the childe of one of the more prominent Ventrue in Atlantic City. Preferably the Prince, if he's Ventrue and that works with your plans for him, or failing that a Primogen would do. He was in his late 20s, maybe early 30s before his embrace. A nephew to a prominent NJ crime family (don't worry, I promise not to name him Soprano), he kept his involvements low key growing up and studied political science in college, making contacts around the state. He became a low key political fixer, helping to make problems go away; he also helped hook the politicians and bureaucrats up with their vices of choice discreetly. Drugs, prostitutes, illegal gambling, whatever; he was a conduit between the Mafia and the politicos, and he was good at it. He also amassed huge piles of dirt on people. Despite his usefulness, and his family connections, he never had a chance to work his way up the leadership of the family. Though the Don was his Uncle, my character's father had been killed in prison for (allegedly) talking to the Feds. This left little or no chance that my character would advance to a leadership role, something he found intensely frustrating. He continued his work anyway, setting up a nightclub in Atlantic City which served as both a location for his political contacts to come (discretely, of course), a source of income for him and a source of legitimate income for some of his family members (for tax purposes, of course). This combination of factors (skill, connections, money, pedigree and outsider status) was what drew the attention of his sire. He was embraced to further his sire's ambitions. At first, he was excited, hoping that without his father's shame hanging over him, he could advance based solely on merit. He was pretty damned disappointed when he discovered how Camarilla society actually worked. His ambitions were again stymied and, in this state of frustration, he heard about the Anarchs, including the Anarch Free States out in California. Now, he dreams of turning New Jersey into a new Free State. To his eye, the time is right. The Kindred of NJ are starting to become dissatisfied with being under the thumb of the Prince of NYC. A struggle between him and his vassals could be just the opening the Anarchs of New Jersey need to make their play. My character, in a bold move, has made his conversion to the Anarch Movement public, antagonizing his sire. He is too useful to remove outright, so he is safe for now, but he is trying to make himself the face of the movement in NJ, to gather and network the existing Anarchs in NJ, to recruit more, and to prepare for the right moment to make their move. Anyway, that's my basic idea. I guess I worked out some of the kinks while I was typing it, LOL. Does that fit with the kind of chronicle you had in mind?
