        Kusha
        True Name: Jack Bron
        Age: 30
        Height: 6'2"
        Weight: 150 lbs
        Hair: Bald (intentionally, natural hair is dark brown)
        Eyes: Green
        Path: Mastigos
        Legacy: None (Currently, aiming for Perfected Adept)
        Long-Term Nimbus: Intrusive thoughts occasionally well up when Kusha is near, pertaining to risks or paranoia. "I'm awfully close to the road, and traffic is moving too quickly... what if a driver slips?!", "Was that footsteps? Am I being followed? Is that guy coming in the opposite direction planning on mugging me?"
        Immediate Nimbus: Shadows loom in every corner, movement draws the eye yet nothing is there, sourceless sound tinkle at the edge of hearing (-1 Composure)
        Signature Nimbus: A faint disconnect of the sense of self - am I the body that physically interacts with the world? Or the mind that processes those inputs? Is there a difference at all?

        Dedicated Tool: Siderite-Lunargent-Hermium fountain pen, presented by his first mentor as an initiation gift

        Background: Jack Bron buried himself in academia for almost a decade, graduating from Manchester University with a Doctorate in Clinical Psychology. The Manchester native was immediately shuffled into the Royal Army Medical Corps, who footed the bill for his degree, and spent several years cycling between deployments. Returning from his most recent deployment, Jack Bron tendered his resignation for reasons he refused to elaborate on (see: Awakening). Shortly thereafter, he moved to London, and began looking for a job as a therapist or councilor.

        Awakening: One day, while out for a jog, a man approached Jack Bron, and instructed him to get into the man's car, and sit quietly until told otherwise. Jack found himself compelled to obey, and was soon strapped to a table beneath the man's house. Lo, such is the horror that a Mind Mage can achieve with just a few words, disregard for free will redoubled by Rapture. The Following three days were a blur, as a nondescript figure calling itself "Mr. Fish" drowned and revived Jack for the Rapt's amusement. On the third morning, the third time Jack's consciousness found itself forcibly stitched back into his body, he Awoke screaming.

        The local Consillium raided the building within the hour, though they were unable to apprehend Mr. Fish. That the Rapt was a relatively respected and influential member of the Mysterium resulted in a great deal of embarrassment, and Jack suspects from the timing of the raid that the Consillium had been aware of the experiments for some time.

        After a brief induction into the Arrow -who had several members who he had met as a Sleeper and were willing to vouch for him- "Kusha" decided to escape the bad memories of Manchester and move south. The Manchester Consillium was only too happy to give him a letter of introduction and quietly sweep their embarrassment under the rug.

        Personality: Soft-spoken and somewhat distant, Kusha is significantly less intimidating once you get past him looming over you. Originally rather blasé and sedentary, his experience with Mr. Fish has left him watchful and slightly obsessed with remaining beneath notice.

                            Trish Takehashi
                            Character Concept                            : Japanese Lois Lane
                            Age: 22
                            Height: 5'8"
                            Weight: 110 lb.
                            Hair: Wavy Black
                            Eyes: Dark Brown
                            Virtue: Kindness
                            Vice: Reckless
                            Attributes: Exceptional Social, Above Average Mental, Average Physical
                            Skills: Academics, Investigation, Athletics, Brawl (Akido) , Drive (Horseback ridding), Larceny, Weapons (Kendo), Animal Ken, Empathy, Expression (Journalism), Persuasion, Streetwise, Subterfuge
                            Merits: Professional Training (Journalism), Encyclopedic Knowledge, Multilingual (Japanese, English, Spanish, Navajo), Allies, Contacts, Resources, Taste, Striking Looks.

                            Background
                            Trish was born and raised in Osaka, Japan. Her father is in the Air Defense Force and her mother is a white American woman from Texas who moved to Japan to work as movie actress. Her mother stared in several Japanese science-fiction films in the early eighties that retain somewhat of cult following, including The War in Space in which she played the Princess from Earth. When Trish was 15 she was in an accident where a building collapsed during an earthquake. She doesn't like to talk about it, but something happened to her while she was trapped in the rubble that changed her and her life in dramatic ways. She was involved in some sort of Exam cheating scandal in high school that nearly destroyed her academic carrier, but she was able to recover and graduate with honors. She then spent a gap year in West Texas with her mother's family who own a ranch, and made some "interesting friends", and went missing for several days, during a period where there was increased UFO sightings reported in the area. Trish returned to Japan to attend TODAI, again graduating with honors, and tried her best to not have any more "adventures". After graduating, she decided, against her father's advice, to do post-graduate work at Oxford and a newspaper internship at a respected paper in London. So far she has avoided getting into any trouble, but those who know her best suspect that won't last long. She brought her cat, Michi, to keep her company.

                            Personality
                            Between being raised in Osaka and her eccentric American mother, Trish is outgoing, friendly, sometimes impulsive, and has a somewhat sly sense of humor, though she can be reserved around authority figures, especially if she feels like she is "In Trouble". When she was young she was somewhat bullied for being gaijin and something of a beanpole by Japanese standards, but she has started to shed her insecurities little by little. She is kind, especially to people she feels are being mistreated or left out and has a strong sense of loyalty to those she considers to be her friends. She does seem to almost define the phrase "curiosity killed the cat" sometimes, and often finds herself in the middle of bizarre circumstances by pursuing her passions to the point of stupidly brave recklessness.

                            In addition to knowing her Mental Attributes (Int 3, Wits 4, Res 3) and Social Attributes (Prs 4, Man 4, Cmp 3, Striking Looks 2) and her many "Jack of all trades, master of none" skills, Kusha, with active mage sight, would be aware that Trish is HEALTHY. Not only does she not appear to have been sick recently, it appears that she has never been sick, or significantly injured, since she was 15. She also seems to be remarkably free from any genetic defects, and unless something terrible happens to her will probably live a very long life.

                            Hooke was somewhat reluctant to give away too much information on Trish. He seemed to regard it as a "training exercise", especially when he heard that Kush was interested in learning the Arcanum of Fate. The only advice he gave was to try not to stand too close to Trish if there is danger around - "bullets, heavy objects, fire, car crashes, whatnot."

                                                            Trish has a surprisingly low profile on social media. And there really wasn't that much to find. But after doing some digging you were able to find this


                                                            The Flaggerschmit Gazette August 19, 2012
                                                            Visiting Girl saved by “Alien Abductee”

                                                            Patricia Takehashi, age 18, daughter of local celebrity Margo Lynne Takehashi, who went missing three days ago claims that she was rescued from mysterious kidnappers by a local teen who claims to be Martin Wiley, a high school student who disappeared in the 1940's. Patricia declined to comment on the story, other than to say she is “very grateful to Martin. And to the Iron Ranger”. The “Iron Ranger” is a local legend about a cyborg who goes about like an old Western gunslinger.

                                                            The teenager claiming to be Martin Wiley is a mute, but Takehashi's cousin, Elizabeth Maysle, daughter of Local rancher Robert “Big Bob” Maysle claims to be able to communicate with him through “mindspeak”.

                                                            “He"s very sweet, and misunderstood”, says Elizabeth. She further explained that the teen boy was abducted by space aliens In the 1940's and taken in a flying saucer to a distant planet and transformed into an alien to tend the fungus gardens of the planet's ruler. He escaped when an inter-dimensional portal opened in the mountains near town. Elizabeth claims that she, Patricia, Martin, and the “Iron Ranger” thwarted an
                                                            attempt by an evil wizard to summon ancient alien gods to destroy the state of Texas.

                                                            “Bess has watched too many of her aunt's movies,” says Big Bob, referring to the numerous cult Japanese science fiction films starring Margo Lynne Takehashi, Patricia's mother. “And she watches all those weird cartoons. She's what they call an Odd Taco over there in Japan!”

                                                            “I think the preferred term is Weebly, dear “ interjected Ann Maysle, Elizabeth's mother and town librarian. “I think it's wonderful for young people to use their imaginations and learn about other cultures. Bess has been studying Japanese very hard since she heard her cousin was coming to visit this summer. They've been pen pals since they were little girls.”

                                                            Patricia claims that the “evil wizard” in her cousin's version of events is Dr. Andrew Boothe, the president of Danner College in nearby Misery, TX. The college claims that President Boothe is currently on sabbatical and that they could not disclose his whereabouts. “He was doing some sort of scientific experiment I think, but my cousin's claims that he is a soul stealing Litch may be exagerated,” says Patricia. “I think it's probable that the fungus spores in the cave where Martin keeps his flying saucer have mind-altering properties,” she added, in defense of her cousin's wild claims.

                                                            Local inventor and amateur astronaut Frank Acconda offered an alternative view point. “It's the damned government I tell you! They're always doing weird experiments up there in the mountains. And using our tax money!” Mr. Acconda is a noted Anarco-capitalist activist who's previous claims to have worked on secret government projects and to be the original inventor of both the internet and mobile phones have never been substantiated.

                                                            Whatever the truth may be, the Maysle family and the town of Flaggerschmit are grateful for the safe return of the two girls.

                                                            Editor's note: Patricia worked as an unpaid intern at this newspaper over the summer.
