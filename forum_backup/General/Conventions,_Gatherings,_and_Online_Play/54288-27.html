                    Mere minutes after leavign the Wyld zone, you reach a sprawling metropolis; Ceridyth, seat of the High King, and nexus of the Vicruith civilization. You notice a number of other parties similar to your own, eahc wearing a different color combination that the familiar Donnkad combination. Darmud and Erwan leave to mingle with them, telling Tirien that the clan-moot will begin at sundown.

                    A number of Darmud's warriors' offer to host Dero a round of drinks. They say that they never expected such a scrawny kid to be able to knife a Fae noble, so he's alright in their books. They might even help him with the age-old tradition of lying through his teeth to the local women about his exploits. Dero indicates that he will head out with them and excuses himself, leaving Tirien alone.

                    Tirien's solitude does not last long; she is soon approached by a middle-aged woman who is wearing a pelt of some large, white-furred beasty. She introduces herself as Ceryd, and she asks how a woman such as Tirien ended up alone in what is clearly unfamiliar territory.

                                                        Immediately after the 40 men enter the foundry, his caste mark burns brightly and his aura begins to twist around himself in a brilliant display of twisting reds and golds. As his aura shifts, a giant orichalcum looking hammer and anvil appear behind him and the hall fills with echoing clanking sounds as the hammer hits the anvil. Suddenly, Hyuga begins barking out commands for the workers. "Take this hammer, and work on fixing that molds shape," "You two take this hammer and go fix the shaft for the molten metal," "You five take these shovels and picks and clean up the broken stone over there!" He continuously barks out the orders and as he is speaking, blue and gold streams of essence comes flying out of his mouth as words in Firetongue for the tools he speaks and immediately moves to his hands and coalesce into every tool he mentions.

                                                        He hands out all the necessary tools to the workers and then brings out a hammer and anvil for Jensven. "I need you to help me over there," he says as he points to the main furnace. "We need to un-dent the furnace walls, fix the pipelines, and rebuild the blower and filter. I will remake the pathway for the fire elements essence lines to come through."

                                                        Hyuga rushes off to fix the elements in a blinding speed as his hands glow brightly with swiftly-shifting shapes of essence unraveling and entangle at amazing speeds. In a swift moment, he finishes the lines and gives them to Jensven to put back in. "I am sure you will know how to do this. I am sure you had some sort of furnace in that small village of yours," he says to Jensven as he walks off to aid the other workers. "No, no, no. Not like that! You've got to hit it from every angle! You over there, don't forget to shape the mold properly! If you are done over there, you three need to help these morons fix the major pipeline!"

                                                        As he walks around, he continues to bring out more tools for the workers and continues to bark out orders. With each new tool he brought out, the room glowed brighter and brighter as if the sun itself was beginning to enter the factory. His presence alone seemed to speed the workers up and he continued to work on all the projects as a blindingly fast speed showing each worker how to do their job. Sweat pours from his face, but Hyuga never seems to tire. He was in his zone of concentration for sure.

                                                        Hyuga is using WaWM and CNNT, so the work is done at 12x speed and as if he had a master's workshop eliminating any penalties. With 40 assistants and Jensven, his bonus is 12 dice. He is not using dexterity as it is a job on a very large object/area and uses the next lower stat: perception. I am assuming this is a 3-die stunt. Hyuga is also using 9 dice for his first craft excellency (9m) for the first roll (which sends his anima to a brilliant bonfire).

                                                        This gives a total of 12(bonus)+9(1st ex)+4(perception)+7(craft fire w/hearthstone bonus)+3 stunt dice= 32+3 dice. Thanks to peerless paragon of fire, he will also have 2 additional sux for free.
