    OK!

    Cauthon (Spoilers for other PC)


    Your Arrow cabal does not know who killed Angitia. There have been several deaths and unsolved disappearances of Pentacle mages in London over the past few years. The Hierarch, Civitas, claims that getting to the bottom of things is a top priority, but nothing has been resolved yet, which makes many mages in the city nervous and paranoid. Hooke is convinced it must be the Seers, while Hailey has his own theories about celestial beings ("Wandering Angels" is his exact phrase) from beyond the stars. Trish's claim, veiled of course in the obscurity of a small town sleeper newspaper from the colonies (Yes Hailey refers to the United States as the "colonies" - unironically), is of much interest to both Hooke and Hailey, and they have been secretly paying a panopticon agent to scrub the internet of any mention of Trish other than the article you found. Your reports that Trish appears to have some sort of mysterious power that triggers your PMS has only increased their interest in the matter. Angitia's death, which is tragic of course, seems to Hooke to be an opportunity. They had you burry the body so that you would develop a strong sympathetic connection. A GoV named Donne helped you. He doesn't say much, but he smokes cigarettes like a chimney and has a thousand yard stare that is unsettling. Hooke and Donne seem to think this will help in someway if there is an investigation of Emma's death. The over all impression that Kusha has of London is that Pentacle Mages are even more old fashioned, stuck in the past, unwilling to change, and obsessed with in-fighting and their own petty squabbles than they were in Manchester. And he is still upset about how things turned out in Manchester. Kusha has done a little bit of digging into Angitia's background and she seems like she was an unusually wise and good mage who genuinely cared about helping the sleepers of her community. Peele, the 11Q, pops in now in then to ask annoying questions and go through Emma's files. He doesn't seem like much of a people person.

        Cauthon. And some of this might be relevant to lbeaumanior as well.

        1. (4 successes) Joanna, or "Jo", is a 19 year old British Indian woman (She is able to speak some Hindi and Punjab with other Indian Immigrants but mostly speaks in a thick Multicultural London English accent). She is small and petite, a little under 5 feet (152 cm) weighing about 100 lb. (45 kg), with dark eyes and long black hair which she usually keeps in a single braid in the back. She typically wears baggy cargo pants, military style boots with thick soles that add a little bit to her height, and loose fitting hoodies. Her favorite is a faded red one that says something about donating blood. Jack first met her when a young Japanese woman named Trish Takahashi brought her into the clinic about six months ago in June. While Jo didn't seem that different from most of the other clients in the clinic, Trish seemed like she had walked in from another world (Presence 4 + Striking Looks 2 + Taste [Fashion] + Resources) Trish was interning at a large, well known London newspaper and Jo had come into the office in near hysterics claiming that she was constantly being followed by large rats and that she couldn't sleep at night because of the sound of howling dogs. Trish promised Jo to look into the animal control situation in Walworth Estates, but also thought that Jo might need some professional mental health care.

        p.s. I know that Cauthon cares what characters sound like. Trish sounds like Audrey Hepburn from like Roman Holiday or Breakfast at Tiffany's most of the time, but if you get enough alcohol into her, or she is tired enough, she starts slipping into a weird mix of Texas and Osaka-ben. She also seems to be functionally conversational in an improbable number of languages besides Japanese and English. Joanna has a pronounced MLE accent which is different from cockney. It overemphasizes "h" sounds and typically morphs the "Th" into a "T" or a "D" sound. "Wat da Hell is tis ting?".

            2. (5 successes. Exceptional. Informed Condition) Jo grew up in a relatively stable single parent home in Southall, North-West London, did well in academics and sport, practicing gymnastics and ballet, until her mother died when she was 12. She was sent to live with her grandmother, a longtime resident of the Walworth estates.

            After moving to South London, Joanna became increasingly anti-social. As a teen she became increasingly prone to violent emotional reactions and anger. She typically pushes people away emotionally, is constantly testing boundaries and deliberately setting other people off to get a rise out of them. Her senior year of school she was expelled for starting a fight with another student. Her relationship with her grandmother is strained, and she frequently spends time living on the streets with other homeless people rather than her grandmother’s flat. When Jack first met Jo she was malnourished and underweight and not in good health. She has had some problems with substance abuse in the past, but is currently sober. She has frequent headaches and migraines, stomach throbs and cramps, frequent nose bleeds, and on occasion spontaneous menstruation.

                3. (3 Successes) Jack feels like he has been able to help Jo some, despite the fact that she can be quite disruptive in group therapy sessions and frequently refers to Jack as an “Imperialist Pig” in reference to his military service.

But this has had the unfortunate downside of her getting into trouble with the police on a few occasions, and Jack and Trish have had to bail her out. She has become more physically athletic then when Jack first met her, gaining muscle mass and also increased dexterity and stamina. It’s almost like she took a small dose of some kind of “super soldier serum”.

                5. (6 Successes. Exceptional. 1 Arcane Beat. No success on Occult roll) After careful observation of the mundane and magical varieties, Kusha has determined that Jo’s headaches and bleeding are in some way related to the presence of certain kinds of rats and spiders, but he has not yet determined why this is. Perhaps a Thyrsus mage like Saulin ( nickeldan ) could help.

                6. (3 Successes) Despite a probable ASPD (Anti-Social Personality Disorder, sometimes called "psychopathy") diagnosis, and a strong feeling that Jo spends a good deal of her time thinking about how to murder people in hard to detect ways, Jack strongly believes that Jo wants and needs relationships, and he feels like he and Trish have become touchstones for her. Trish also helped Jo get a job as a waitress at the Spire Hotel restaurant and bar. She is the nervous and tense looking waitress that Mekhaine ( KaiserAfini ) noticed earlier in the evening.

                Which brings us to tonight, January 10th, the night of the New Moon. Kusha has been sensing some kind of tension slowly building in Jo, and has a sense of foreboding about something setting her off. Hopefully Trish will be able to talk to her and see what's wrong.

                I know this is an info dump. Let me know if you have questions or need clarification on anything. I kind of planned on this all playing out over several scenes, so I guess just use your imagination (it is a RPG after all) that Jack has been slowly building all this up over the past six months and has become increasingly worried about Jo. And Trish's safety.

After awakening he was discovered by a Seer that only him seems able to remember (Tyche) that taught him a lot and initiated him in the Seers. To this impressionable youth she is what he aims to be: she was the strongest, best dressed, better looking, smartest and richest person he had ever met and she messed his wordview (in a good way), worked on his vocabulary, speech patterns, manner of dressing, on his magical training, and taught him that he is as special as he thinks himself to be, even his Shadow Name was picked by her. Tyche's dissapearance is a really big blow to him, he has a bit of a crush on her.

                            He has a soft spot for his mum Elaine, and his cat "Dr. Wiggles", and has no idea where his father Michel is currently, last time he heard anything he was in Scotland doing oil rig maintenance, but that was years ago and of course the man sends no money to speak of.

                            The Awakening
                            He really doesn't know! There are gaps in his memory that scare him a lot, probably his old master playing Mind tricks with him.

                            People think him absolutely calm and tranquil, nothing further from the truth, he is just so lazy that even emotions take their time. He used to dress like the stereotypical chav, but since seeing what posh people actually get too, he is quietely trying to improve his wardrobe (hard to do with his frame and resources). Not an impossing figure by any means, he talks his way through most things, he is trying really hard to get fit recently. He has been looking for Tyche but no one even remember the times she went to the school with the "special education" programme.
