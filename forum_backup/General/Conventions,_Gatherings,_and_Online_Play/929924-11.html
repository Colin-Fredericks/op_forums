                                Ragnar
                                You weave the threads of Fate and temporarily Rule over them. You feel a pulsing of energy in your veins and a sense that for a brief time, you could do anything, even something extraordinary.

                                You spin around the room and study the chamber. You feel luck shifting as you glance at different parts of the room in a seemingly random way, but you know that there is something greater tying this place together. Your mind races to connect the dots...but even with your supernaturally enhanced luck, even with your best preparation, this place defies your attempts at logic and intuition. Nevertheless, the experience of shifting Fate leaves you feeling inspired.

                                Sure, gain a WP and the Inspired condition, which will be in regards to understanding this chamber -- you can choose to resolve the condition to make a mundane roll related to this domain to give it the opportunity for an exceptional success on 3 successes rather than 5. Your Nimbus tilt only relates to others watching you, you can't trigger it on yourself. We're also not in combat or initiative, so the effects would be fairly brief and inconsequential for now.


                                Binder
                                You cough up blood and spit it out of your mouth. The act of tearing the dark energy out of you hurts, but as soon as you rip the last tendrils from your Nimbus, the hail stops sizzling on your skin.

                                The black sphere pauses, hovering in midair as if considering its next move. More pieces of concrete begin to vibrate and rip themselves out of the sidewalk; they start to float around the orb and swirl around it in complex patterns.

                                Suddenly, the orb moves forward with surprising speed. A black tendril of energy strikes out at you. It brushes against your left leg, which burns where the tentacle connects.

                                Attack (4) - Defense (3) = 1 die: 1 success. You take 1 bashing damage. It's your turn. Also, please roll Wits + Composure for a perception roll (no penalties).

                                Something I hadn't thought about until now is how to handle reflexive actions in combat, such as activating Mage Armor or Dodging. Include in your next post whether you would take any reflexive actions such as these, and we will modify my post if necessary.


                                Phoenix and Moonstone
                                You proceed down the hallway, continuing to explore this place. The hallway turns to the left and continues straight for several yards, but this section has no doorways. You also have not seen any windows or larger rooms in this area. As you walk, you notice that the hallway begins to incline slightly, and you can tell that you must be slowly ascending now.

                                There are more lanterns fixed to either wall at regular intervals. You feel another gust of wind, and the candles flicker from the motion. Suddenly, a feminine voice echoes in the hallway around you, speaking to your very soul. It reverberates as if humming with power.

                                WE THOUGHT TO RULE THE HEAVENS, she whispers. WE BELIEVED WE COULD CONTROL TRUTH.

                                WE WERE WRONG.

                                As if you had any doubt, your Periphery echoes at her words, suggesting the presence of something supernatural.

                                            Ragnar
                                            You study the area further, trying to piece together your elementary knowledge of the supernatural with your Supernal intuition and natural intelligence. Like a detective, your brain churns through the information and deduces the only logical conclusion. This place can only be an alternate dimension, a place between the Earth you know and some other realm that does not -- or perhaps used to? -- exist. The doorway you entered was a portal to this dimension, bringing you to another place that defies the rules of nature and physics. From what you've experienced in the short time since your Awakening, it even seems to defies some of the Supernal laws you are beginning to understand. As a student of all knowledge, the act of figuring out this part of the puzzle is invigorating.

                                            Actually, because you gained the Inspired condition by getting an exceptional success on a spell, you gain an Arcane Beat. As a result of this exceptional success, which you got from a mundane roll, gain the Steadfast condition, which will grant a regular Beat upon resolving (unless you've already earned a Beat by resolving a different condition by that point).
