        Well, really if you can explain it in a rational way that fits your character you could potentially use any set of skills. As for the rules of Scrutiny itself I'd have to look it up in the 2E Mage book. Also, since I know Basic is reliable and you've posted twice I'm going to reveal the Chronicle idea I've been cooking up, so character creation parameters will be established. And that Chronicle is...

        The Curious Case of Gloria Margaret Calloway. A Crossover Chronicle for Mage the Awakening & Hunter the Vigil.

        Setting: Langley Falls, a fictional New England Town.

        Theme: Locked Doors & Buried Secrets.

        Mood: Deep Waters

        Synopsis: Langley Falls may be the closest thing to heaven on earth for some people. Sure the winters are cold, but the summers are pleasant while spring and fall is beautiful. The fishing industry may be a relic of a bygone era but the tourism industry is alive and well in such a verdant and secluded town. The people are nice and friendly to those who are new, and are eager to welcome new commerce to the city. And it's long time natives cultivate hidden societies and family secrets that span generations and become facets of a hidden community all their own. And most importantly, Langley Falls is a place that has seen few tragedies. To the natives, bad things happen elsewhere. And that's exactly how they like it. Or, at least bad things happen elsewhere until now. Gloria Calloway, the mayors daughter, has been murdered. The community is in a panic, both humble citizens and hidden societies are in an uproar, and those who knew her best start having strange dreams about a shrouded figure beneath the sea.

        Setup: One method I wanted to try to help ensure the success of this project is to actually have our group build the fictional city of Langley Falls from the ground up. Each player can contribute as much as they want to the setting, adding locations and people, and even mysteries. The catch, being that for every facet of the city that's added I'll add a secret for the players to discover. That way the city will be intimately familiar to everyone since you all made it, but it's secrets will still be obfuscated for your enjoyment to uncover. As mentioned, this is potentially both a Mage and Hunter story. If it swings more one way than another than that aspect we'll focus on more, but elements from both games will be present regardless. I hope this is acceptable to everyone's standards?

        P.S. Almost forgot, this is going to be a 2nd Edition CofD game regardless of it's focus. And, the character creation parameters is simply that Gloria Carter's death has profoundly affected you in some way. It could be because you're a family member or friend, it could be because you're part of the investigation to uncover her murderer, or perhaps another reason that I can't think up right now. The other thing as be it Mage or Hunter, this moment is what sets you on your path. For Mages this means Gloria's death or the strange dreams sets off your Awakening. If Hunter then either begins to eat away at you because you know something terrible is happening but can't fully explain why, and this will lead to you taking up the Vigil.
