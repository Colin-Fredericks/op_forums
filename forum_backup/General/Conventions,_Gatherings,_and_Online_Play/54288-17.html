        "I am Yi Zhinan, of late champion and representative of Origenes god of this nation and city. They will be necessary when our forces again clash with the Fair Children if we have any chance at victory."

            Tirien's face maintains its half-smile mask, but inwardly, she frowned. She knew that this would most likely happen, but without introductions and going through the usual posturing, this sort of thing, she'd never get anywhere. She turns to Dero for a second, and gives him a knowing look.

            "This is how you get things done. Well, without murdering someone," she thought.

            "Ah, of course, you do, Ser General," Tirien says, bowing deeply to the Strategoi," I am merely offering my services to assist. "All things in their place under Heaven," and all that. If given a month or so, though, I could train your soldiers, be they man, women, gods or elementals, in the arts of the Realm Military. My lineage has a history of military excellence in Her Majesty's service, after all. In addition, I have two proposals for you: Let me demonstrate my acumen to you, and let me recruit a talon of new soldiers. I'll train them in Realm tactics, and you can use us as you see fit. Of course, a mere talon of troops would be nothing to the might of your nation here, what with your affluence of Essence users. If need be, I'll even finance the creation of such a unit myself. My second proposal is, I also have allied myself with a troupe of Ancient Fae knights who have patrolled this land peacefully since the Age of Dreams. Before I came to the capital, I sought their aid out, as they view this region as their homes, and are loathe to share with these newcomers. If you accept my aid, I would see it fit to call them to aid you. Is this acceptable?"

                                                04-07-2014, 06:01 PM
                                                Yi smirked at being called a Lookshan but did not correct the man. He smirk became a frown as the Chancellor continued.

                                                "Whatever the faults of my city, I will say this in its favor; it is much easier to get things done. I say that as someone who started at the bottom with no family connections."

                                                He shock his head and sighed.

                                                "You will have the Strategoi's order by the end of the day. Provided he does not find someone else to pass responsibility to. There is however one other matter I had hoped to bring up which may still be a useful expenditure of Origenes money.

                                                What provisions can you make for a more universal education system in the nation? As an ideal though initial goal, the whole of the population would be literate and possess basic mathematical proficiency. I have personally observed the advantages that this would bring about. At the very least it would allow for far easier detection of intellectual talent in the population which could produce a dramatic increase in the number of Thaumaturges and trained professionals."

                                                He stood up and made ready to depart.

                                                "Please begin production and meditate on my second proposal. I will return after I have dealt with the prosecution of the War."

                                                He turned to his companion. "I believe I will soon be encountering Tirien again. You may accompany me if you like."

                                                With that he turned on his heel and began walking for the Hall of the Boule, trailing his young priestess and a few hangeroners as he went.

                                                When he arrived he stopped, noting the multiple, guarded entrances.

                                                Always there must be something. It would be faster to just kill them, and no one would even question me about it later. Ah but for that tiny hitch that it would be wrong.

                                                He turned to the priestess.

                                                "Is there some provision for entering, or must I call forth the power divine to whelm them utterly?"
