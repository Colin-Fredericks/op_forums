                                                    Yeah. The main conceit behind Jagger as a character for me is whom is chosen to be a Solar. Lunars are chosen from survivors; Sidereals are chosen from those destined to learn the importance of this or that aspect of fate; Dragon-Blooded are chosen from those who have enough of the right blood. Solars are chosen from leaders. I wanted to play with that idea, with how someone who is chosen to be a priest-king controlling the hearts and faith of the multitudes could have gained the skills and qualifications for that role without having the nobility one would think would be inherent to such a figure.

I can still play with expectations some by making him an Eclipse--Eclipses value harmony, and Jagger values harmony more than most because, as a criminal, he knows discord is both hard on the organization's bank accounts and dangerous for the organization's employees--but I think it's more fun as a Zenith.

                                                            I have narrowed down my concept some.

                                                            The character is a Sidereal, Chosen of Journey/Serenity (probably the latter), who wears a Revelation Blindfold at all times. Her cover identity is that of a goze, a wandering blind musician, though many who know her understand that she is actually an advisor of some kind to the powerful who can hire her. In truth, she is a member of a Gold Faction, sent to the Hundred Kingdoms to survey and advise a newborn Solar (current plans are that he be JimB's character), and to help strengthen the region as it faces outsider forces.

                                                            Mechanically, aside from always-on AESS, the character would mostly have a social and martial focus. She has Performance and Presence Charms, and tries to influence people subtly without them realizing it... Or she brings out the kickass against those who prove too much of a hurdle.

                                                            At this point I am mostly stuck on elements of aesthetics and choice of style. I could have her use Artifact war fans with Crane Style (she would probably be a technical pacifist who punches people to teach them to be better persons), I could have her be an unarmed singer using Silver-Voiced Nightingale and playing an Unsurpassed Sanxian, or I could have her be an Ivory Pestle Stylist wielding an Artifact staff/flute and using ghost-slaying Charms to put the dead to rest; the country seems like it might need it.

                                                            I am not sure which would be best/coolest. I am also toying with the idea of her having the Oathbound merit as a representation of her devotion to the Maidens having led her to take a specific vow that restricts her behavior. Not sure which, though.

                                                            Any opinion/advice?
