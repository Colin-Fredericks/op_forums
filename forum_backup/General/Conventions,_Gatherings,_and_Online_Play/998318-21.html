            The Forks

            The driver of the Amalgama van turns on an intercom, hollering to the mercenaries (conscripts?) in the back "Holy fucking shit! You guys never said these things were this fucking fast!" The desiccated man of dust and stone is a blur charging up a hill and through the snow, snow banks flying into the air in hazy white clouds and falling back to earth, billowing like the dust of a stampede. Mark 5 easily makes it up the hill before the van can pull out of the heavy traffic surrounding Johnston Terminal and the Manitoba Theatre for Young People when Mark, flaring with Azothic Radiance and streaming dust, strikes the earth. The blow is felt all across the Forks. Car alarms go off, glass starts to crack, people scatter further, unsure of what to do. Manitoba is not earthquake country. Some people fall, bleeding, as panes of glass smash over their heads and shoulders. For the time being, buildings and vehicles are mostly unscathed, but as the earthquake continues and intensifies, they will start to crumble. The beating heart of the city will go silent. The sound of it is immense, like a thousand taiko drums being beaten all at once and out of sequence. Like a series of explosions, one after the other. Like a roar of disapproval from something immense living below the earth. The aeolian harp of the Celebration Circle wails like a swiftly vibrating bell. As if a choir of brazen throats cried out in horror at the display.

            The street heaves and tears under the wheels of the van, sending it trembling, and the driver struggles to maintain control of the vehicle... but they manage to pull it off, driving away from the Titan with increasing speed. The mercenaries inside the back of the van look at each other, and at the Captain, who is struggling to her feet to fling open the rear doors, precariously maintaining her balance with a hand clutched to a handhold on the ceiling. The only unharmed mercenary, the one in the clerical collar, stands, bracing themselves against the door frame, and with a muttered prayer exhales Divine Flame. The Pilgrims in the van can smell the Flux sublimating off the man. What the hell was that? He doubles over with the effort, weak at the knees, just barely able to pull it off, but Mark 5 can feel the Pyros draining away from him, the entire Alembic simply vanishing as the ground ceases trembling. The van turns a corner, and it disappears out of sight.

            They're gone.

            Out on the Red River the thick ice that forms the longest skating trail in the world is shattering, great planes and floes of ice heaving up onto the shore as if attempting to escape the turmoil underwater. An ashen cloud starts to form over the Forks, and the remaining Created in the Celebration Circle can feel it warp and change, setting them all just a little on edge. The grey filth of Flux begins to settle over the stones like perverse snowfall, brass and iron begin to tarnish. A Wasteland is festering. This place won't be clean for a week at least, if the compounded fallout of this much alchemy being thrown around doesn't taint it for longer, but the quality that had made the Celebration Circle special, its safety, was forever gone. For now, the Ramble would have to fall silent.

            OOC: Wormwood the Unconquered Alembic just lost all its Pyros charge, and the Wrath of the Gods Distillation immediately ends. Combat is now over, Amalgama has gotten away, and now it's time to start picking up the pieces.
