    How to GM a PBP game?

    02-28-2015, 07:01 PM
    Somewhat what the title says. Exalted 2E is the game I'm looking to run, but I've never run a game before. I'm not sure if it would be a lost cause for me to try, as I've also never played Exalted or even a tabletop RPG. I've taken extensive notes of the relevant sections and have most of the sourcebooks. I've also done copious reading of these forums and Freedom Stone to get a feel for general fan gripes. Anyone have tips on this?

      Be open, direct and honest. You're playing on the internet, and that removes a good chunk of human communication levels. Do everything in your power to make up for that.

      Promote cooperation between your players. No gaming group lasts long without it. This goes doubly so for PbPs.

      Be patient with your players. Real life gets in the way of PbP games a lot, and that's okay.

      Be patient with yourself. See above.

      Find ways to keep your players engaged in the game, outside of the game. Throw out stories about side characters, rumors they hear in town, random bits of world building. It works wonders.

      Read through PbP threads, particularly ones that have lasted a long time, and ones that died shortly after launch. Try to find the common threads. Promote the behaviors you see in the long running threads while discouraging those you see in the shorter ones.

      Join a PbP Exalted game. Reading about the issues people have is one thing. Actually having them is another.

      Just go out there and do it! Your first few attempts may fail, but in many cases starting a PbP is hit-and-miss anyway.

                              A tip that's broadly applicable to all PBP games -

                              Get at least twice as many players as the minimum number you need in order to continue play, and be prepared to shuffle them around. Odds are that a bunch of them will drop out, likely without warning you, and possibly even before character creation is done. You'll need to reorganize those who remain. Hopefully you'll end up with a solid core of people you can depend on to write something at least twice a week.

                                  Semicasual speaks the truth. A lot of times, people will just disappear, and often not even tell you why. Such is the nature of the PbP experience, sadly. However, since you're new to tabletop RPGs, I would recommend joining a game and seeing how it works, not only to get a feel for the game, as Exalted especially has a lot of pretty weird mechanics that can overwhelm newcomers (Hello, Astrology), but also to get a feel for tabletop games in general.

                                  That being said, if you're dead set on running your own game, I'd recommend the Scavenger Lands as the Direction you set it in. There's lots of weirdness over there, gods, demons, outcaste dragonbloods, it's in the Threshold, so the Wyld Hunt isn't as powerful there, and it has the best city (Nexus), so it is a mite bit easier to ease your way into than other Directions.
