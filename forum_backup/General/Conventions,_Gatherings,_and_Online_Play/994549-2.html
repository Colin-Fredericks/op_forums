        All good stuff. I meant in terms of what they think is out there, what weirdness they think might be real, that sort of thing.

        When do you guys want to start the game?
        I was thinking that Earth has a few dots in Occult and the Library (Occult) merit in the form of a bunch of handwritten journals and audio/video recordings Dead Girl made about the inexplicable weirdness they've seen on the road, because that sounds flavorful and cool. She's not really a deep thinker, but she's seen some things- "a building that eats people, I've seen something like this before." Her general view is that human science and rationality isn't adequate to explain the world they live in, and the unnatural is usually ugly and violent and harmful and outsider, and represents a failure of order on some level. Some things that would definitely surprise her: organized and complex supernatural phenomena. Things that appear unnatural, but actually have a rational (if strange) explanation. Human-like societies of multiple intelligent supernatural beings parallel to human civilization, not just rogue bestial vampires and weird loners with a ghost inside them. Supernatural things that are beautiful in some way.

        If you're familiar with Unknown Armies at all, I love that game's take on the unnatural and the unexplained. There's a lot of stuff that's just there, and doesn't really have a good overarching explanation or place in an ordered cosmology, but it does have desperate obsessive cults and cabals fighting to control the magical exhaust it happens to produce. Even for Promethean, I always liked how their whole deal and the Principle and the Divine Fire is sort of orthogonal to the other supernaturals. The Created just kind of...are what they are, and they have this strange connection to a cosmic force and its angels, but no one really has all the puzzle pieces. It's not like Mage where a starting character can just go talk to their boss who thinks she has a pretty good idea of what's going on cosmologically.

        I'd be happy to start whenever characters are in and you're ready. This weekend, maybe?

        Dusksage That would be a good hook! Especially for a Frankenstein where their body isn't really any one person's. It would be a good excuse to get Residual Memory, maybe. Any ideas on Refinement? Plumbum might be a good way to start learning what this weirdo patchwork body is and can do.

                Weekend sounds good to me. I'm having a pretty good idea of where to start, especially when it comes to the local supernatural goings-on, and I'm thinking we start in the present and move forwards. I think we're going to have some fun exploring these characters and seeing where the chips fall. In terms of Merits, pick what's good for your characters, don't worry too much about the fluff. Residual Memory could also be Azothic Memory leaking through, Good Brain could be the Divine Fire pushing you to excellence, that sort of thing.

                Dusksage that sounds like a good character with a lot of powerful pathos behind it; Promethean is sort of purpose-built for adventures of self-discovery, so I can't think of a better game to have that sort of storyline occur. I third the recommendation for your character starting on Plumbum, it's specifically about coming to terms with what you are and who you are. Any thoughts as to the first Role you completed and the Alembics you'll have?

                As a general note to all my players, remain aware of your Refinement Condition! It's an easy source of Beats and can be a great source of power. Plumbum, in particular, is easy to make into an extremely useful tool, since you can convert Bashing damage into Pyros. Here's the dice roller site I mentioned: http://sheetgen.dalines.net/wiki/WikiDiceRoller

                Also, just a few final considerations. Would everyone be alright with using Social Maneuvering? Both on NPCs and, potentially, with NPCs on PCs? And secondly, I'm thinking of a way to adapt the Torches and Pitchforks rules from the Hurt Locker spoilers. Basically, whenever you cause Disquiet, I add the Stage of Disquiet to a dice pool penalized by your Pilgrimage rating. Disquiet ratings go up, dice pool goes up. Success on the Disquiet pool mean the group, collectively, gains the Watched Condition or something similar, to be upgraded to Hunted on a Resolution. Exceptional Success on the Disquiet pool means a jump straight to Hunted.

                It's worth pointing out that, for quite a while, it is very unlikely that you'll be causing Disquiet in any truly noticeable fashion. You'll be rolling one die against an average of four.
