                "The First order of business today is the petition fielded to the Gentes by Lady Larissa, Strategos Eugene and Guest yesterday.                 The representative from Midea, a short, middle-aged woman, rises first, and answers that Gens Midea will offer all troops currently stationed in the city to the Strategos.

                Next is the representative for Althea, who claims that they have already moved all of their house troops to Vera (its city, and the city closest to the Fae army) and will send a message to place them at the command of Strategos Vasil

                After that is the representative for Nikoloi, who offers three maniples for the Capital. they are quickly followed by Zophyrus who seems to mirror Nikoloi's offer of three maniples.


                Will Yi stay, and if so, do you want a run down of all of the subsequent orders of business?

                                        OOC: OK then.

                                        IC:

                                        Blossom waves for Tirien to follow her further into the forest, walking silently with the Lunar for about 20 minutes until she is sure they are alone. She takes one last careful look around, then adopts a look of concentration. She speaks softly in Old Realm, whispering the words of power as her hands move in front of her in arcane gestures. As her arms trace their way through the air, water vapor begins to gather around them, swirling before her. It slowly collects in a thick, white fog, which is in turn lit bright green by her glowing caste mark. She continues drawing in more little puffs and wisps of vapor until the clearing around the two exalts is filled with eerily green glowing fog. She pauses, surveying it for a moment, then clears her throat with annoyance and snaps her fingers. Obediently, the fog pulls in on itself, condensing until it forms a good sized, thick puffy cloud right in front of her, still tinted green by the anima of secrets. She looks at the cloud with dissatisfaction, and clears her throat loudly again. The cloud shifts slightly, forming a few steps at the back, and a pair of big, puffy chairs on top. Finally giving a small smile of satisfaction, she walks up the steps, settling into the right side chair and gesturing for Tirien to join her.

                                        [Assuming that Tirien joins her]

                                        The cloud stairs wisp away into vapor as soon as Tirien climbs each step, until from the bottom and sides it is indistinguishable from a normal puffy white cloud, especially in the dark. Once Tirien is seated in the soft, comfortable cloud chair, Blossom mentally commands the cloud to rise, shooting straight up into the air until they reach an appropriate cruising altitude. "The Puissant Amethyst Court is operating out of a chancerel on the front lines," she tells Tirien. "That's kind of like a mobile freehold, sort of," Blossom explains, looking around at the stars as she speaks. "We'll need to find the exact location, but I can get us close, and it shouldn't be too hard to locate once we get near it. We'll go full speed at first," she continues, still studying the stars as puffy white seat belts encircle her and Tirien's waists, "then slow down when we get closer so we can stay covert." She silently studies the stars for a moment, then nods in satisfaction. "We need to start by heading North," she says, confidently pointing in a direction that Tirien knows to be Southeast.

                                                (Yi)

                                                Eugene takes the floor with a party of other individuals and thanks the Gentes for their generosity. Nereus, Gens Spyrus' high elder, reports that the city's foundry complex has returned to full operation, and, with the assistance of Gens Ptreas, the facility has been secured against a secondary attack. A third member of his party, a tall, swarthy man with a garish fashion sense reports that the First Nautikon is prepared to defend the southern and Western borders, and that a single vessel has been dedicated to moving reinforcements and resupply to the front.

                                                Issues move on from there. A previously proposed economic stimulus package is tabled until the cessation of hostilities. Another previous issue, of an open trade agreement with Theros, is brought to vote, losing by a margin of six votes. The Academy reports that, while the shift in production to military goods has helped somewhat, the influx of raw materials for their alchemy labs have slowed to a trickle due to the fighting, drastically reducing their potential supply production.

                                                Gens Midea reports that one of their townships near the Ordoni border was sacked yesterday. The Ordoni ambassador denies responsibility, saying it was likely the actions of the Fae marauders. If only the Novelskis had rescinded their draconian policies towards Ordoni expansion, this might not have happened. This gets a rise from the Therian ambassador, who takes significant offense at the Ordoni's suggestion that it is Novalesk that keeps them in check. Eventually Linon is able to get them apart and to keep them from blow. He ends the session a bit early, likely in mind to prevent an international incident from occurring.

                                                The council is over. What do?
