        Well, let us see if this god is worthy of the devotion shown him.

        As though shrugging off a cloak, Yi's ragged garments fell away. In their place vestments of marramas and samite shone as bright as polished silver and molten gold. His orichalcum guan flashed as though he was crowned with the sun. He looked at once a priest and a king, each title demanding and deserving of the other. He rubbed a set of crimson prayer beads fashioned from coral then snapped his fingers causing the incense to light. It was a parlor trick, but one that worked surprisingly well for most people. Then he began an ancient Old Realm mantra, raising his voice in a singing chant,

        "On the infinite Wyld and the abodes set therein,
        On that essence which turns all things to glory,
        We meditate, as Princes most highly Exalted,
        That we may be worthy of the task laid before us."

        In Old Realm it was known simply as the hymn, and written at the beginning of many surviving documents. In some cases it was almost unreadable, embellished with such calligraphy that it became an ornate seal of the Anathema's government. Yi had often wondered if that was not a sign of their ruin, that they made their core prayer into a meaningless icon instead of an object of reverence. It was a risk to open with it, some text noted almost casually that great gods had come even before it was finished to await the pleasure of the Lawgivers. While his intention was merely to signal his desire for a direct audience, he could not entirely drive the image of the ancient symbol of the Old Realm from his mind.

        First, this roll allows the Guan of Heaven's Son to activating raising my effective appearance and charisma by 3 dice.

        Next, going to make a prayer roll. The goal is to request a visitation from the god and to favorably incline him toward me.

        Charisma 3+3 + Performance 5 + 1st Excellency 8 + Stunt 2 + Compassion 4 = 25 dice against difficulty 5. (Base 7 -1 from the resource 1 incense -1 from being a Zenith and thus a priest).

        I rolled 9 success. (And grumbled about the 12.5 successes that should have generated on average )

        Total cost: 8 personal motes, 1wp, 1 compassion channel

                                The statue makes a thoughtful noise. It taps its chin for a few moments, then says, "I think we can talk." It snaps its fingers, and an otherworldly door appears on one of the walls.

                                Should Yi enter the door, he finds himself in a strange place; a small island, the size of a modest homestead, floating in a starry vault. A large house, modestly decorated (by divine standards) dominates the landscape, but Yi is himself standing in a substantive garden. In a nearby gazebo, a robed man waves you over. He is not of any particular height or weight. His robes, however, appear to be woven of some silvery-white metal, and he holds in his hand a stave of Green Jade, covered in hypnotic patterns of Starmetal. Once you are close enough to make out his features, you notice that, in general, he would not be particularly out of place on the streets of Novalesk. Save for the fact that his features are a bit too symmetrical, and his is skin without blemish, belying his divine nature. He motions you to take a seat at the cluttered table beneath the gazebo.

                                "Apologies for the mess, honored Lawgiver," the god says casually,"I was trying to catch up on some paperwork." He passes his hand over the table, and the cluttered papers fly out into an organized stack before disappearing with a small pop. A few scattered pieces of ambrosia dot the table, and he frowns slightly. He pushes them into a nearby basket, clearing off the table. He dips his hand into a small bowl hanging above the table, and then sprinkles the liquid onto the table, creating an elaborate tea set complete with a varied assortment of pastries and sweetmeats.

                                "As you likely gathered, I am Origenes, god of both the nation of Novalesk and its founding city," the deity says as he pours himself a glass of steaming tea, "and you are a Zenith Caste, one of the high Priests of the Most High."

                                "I forget myself. Please help yourself," Origenes gestures to the assembled 'feast', "You offer help, which, in this current hour of crisis, is a shower after the drought. What I want to know, however, is your price."

                                        Maia returns the greeting with a nod and says, "Thalia. I would like you to examine this artifact"

                                        Thalia takes the music box and begins handling examining it intently. She dons a strange headpiece with dozens of independent lenses, and, grabbing a pair of small jade tools, pries the lid open.

                                        "There appears to be a series of motonic-kinetic capacitors," Thalia says, "Indicating an influx of essence is required to operate the device." She flips a pair of lenses into her eyes, "Judging from the number, I estimate between thirty and forty motes to bring the device to full operation, depending on the efficiency of the mechanisms. Given the cleanliness of the construction, I would lean closer to the lower estimate."

                                        She flips through several more lenses, "The internal mechanisms are constructed primarily of Orichalcum, with Soulsteel placing an easy second. The actual mechanisms of operation are functionally identical to that of a kinetic music box. The engravings are unlike that which would create music however. It appears similar to a sorcerous formula." Thalia gets up, removes her headpiece, and heads over towards a small bookcase. She sorts through for a moment until she finds the book she was looking for, and leafs through for a moment. Heading back towards the music box, she replaces the headgear and examines the box again. She makes a content noise.

                                        "The formula is consistent with the emotional induction patterns found in The Black and White Treatises. Given the Soulsteel construction, it is likely that this is a negative emotion. Perhaps a spiritually draining effect?"

                                        Thalia shakes her head. "I'm afraid I can't be more specific than that, ma'am. The only other thing I'm certain of is that there are no discrimination patterns in the formula. The device will effect anyone or anything within its area of effect. Given the construction, I would say that the area is based off of line of sound. I advise strongly against experimentation on-site."

                                        Maia nods, her expression thoughtful. She looks over to Hyuga, "That what you expected, Scavenger-boy?"
