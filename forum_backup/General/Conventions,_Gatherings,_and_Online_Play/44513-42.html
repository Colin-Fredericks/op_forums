    Okay, I am thinking of initiating into sorcery and learning demon summoning. How does this work as a demon?

    Black El'nor, the Iron Birds

    Have you seen their eyeless faces, as they mock in dreary places?
    Where brooding kings in raukus halls, suffer pity forever more.
    One word songs forever keening, the iron wails that none are needing,
    Repeat advice to ears unheeding: "Forget the past" they oft implore.
    The reeking ache that dreams are dying, fairy kings can scarce ignore;
    Those demon birds, the black El'nore!

    Does any fool think the Demon Princes do not pity themselves? Does any fool think they hate that pity more than pain? So came about the raven birds that later sages called the El'nor. Standing tall as mortals, wearing iron masks as face, they mock the yozi and unquestionables and are allowed to live. For the endless serfs it is no crime to kill them, and doing so has put a fleeting smile on the faces of the mightiest. For a citizen their blood is a mark of shame, for the third circles it is a death sentence. What it would mean for a yozi to kill one is unknown, for it has never occurred.

    It is not so strange a thing; to slay them is to acknowledge them, and it is the purpose of the multitudes to divert their betters from the endless malaise of imprisonment. Yet for the very souls of the Yozi to do so is to invite the shadow of myth ending doubt and eternity swallowing apathy. For this reason the demon kings hold them less tightly than other spawn, and few ancient kings or savants have claimed not to see a raven perched on some tree or window as they consider a lifetime of regrets.

    The fairfolk fear and hate them, for they stand for the utter wrongness of shape. Once unchanging form has accumulated, history grown fat on expended time, and the sheer weight of existence has become a horror; that is when the black birds come.
