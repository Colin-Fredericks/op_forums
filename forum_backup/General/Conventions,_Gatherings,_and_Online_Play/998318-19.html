    Adonis looks up at Jax, visibly spooked. In this Created he sees someone very similar to him. Adonis was bright eyes and a pretty face and a lithe body, while Jax was a paragon the masculine form. But they had both been created to be tools of fulfillment. The form had been all that mattered, nothing more. Dolls. But Jax had turned his service to other Created in other forms.

    "Yes, please. Not yet, I still have a roll to complete. But I will come to you when I'm ready."

    The Cathar's words scare him. But then again, Cathars scare him in general. Mimics try to understand humans, yes, but so that they might copy them, learn how to do the things that are good and wonderful about humans. Cathars peel away the gold and reveal the dark blue of imperfection and vice. Gold and blue, like the masks that those old kings were buried with. They do look good together... He doesn't know if it's the stray thought or the Cathar's suggestion that makes him shudder.

            The reanimated Earth-creature has been thinking about its flesh lately. Meditating on Cuprum in the garden brought changes through the Azothic Memory. The Earth-creature finds that it can discolor its skin and eyes and grow its hair as easily as breathing. This alchemical procedure is called Verto.

            The creature blinks and when it opens its eyes they are one dark green and one albino pink. It blinks again and now they're matching blue. It spits at its reflection. That symmetry doesn't look right at all.

            The creature forces hair follicles through the skin of its cheeks and throat. Itchy like bugs crawling out of its skin. It swats its own face, but once the sting fades, the creature tries again. The facial hair comes in patchy and erratic. After a couple tries, it gets a coarse mustache and chinstrap together, but the raw material just isn't there. Maybe with Pyros, the fields would grow. It lets the hair burrow back inside.

            ---

            It lounges there for quite a while as the Ramble continues, staring like Narcissus at its own reflection in the cold Red River. Blinking and trying new eyeballs, fattening its lips, wrinkling or smoothing the cheeks, flaring its nostrils and making its nose flat or wide or pointy. It digs its fingers into its tangled white hair and pulls its mane even further out, or retracts it into the bumpy scalp. None of it is really any worse or better than the creature's "natural" features. Earth, the creature thinks, isn't a past monster or a future human. Earth lives in the present. They just are what they are. That is the nature of the Red.

            Let's see what this pound of flesh can do when its charged with Pyros. The creature lights the fire inside that powers Metamorphosis, making its flesh truly malleable. It kneels in the cold shallow mud and cups river water in its thick palms and leers at the little mirror. "It's you." The reflection blinks in surprise. "It's me!"

            The creature drinks greedily from the river and splashes its own face with water. Its flesh melts.

            Spending 3 Pyros to charge Verto alembic with Body Like Clay. I'll take the Atavistic condition.

            Earth attempts some more radical body changes. Dexterity+Medicine+Azoth, 1 die: fail. I'll make that a dramatic failure for a Beat. "The Promethean loses control over how her body shifts...these alterations inflict a -2 penalty to the character's actions for the remainder of the scene."

Bulging eyes, one arm bigger than the other, rubbery legs, lolling tongue, skin like an oily rag...who needs Disfigurements? The creature, shocked, recoils from its own reflection and crawls away from the riverbank. Until it can get its internal organs rebalanced, it's going to lurk in the shadows past the edge of the Ramble.

                            It was eighteen months ago, exactly. The day of the Summer Solstice, 2015. It was the end of the week he had spent at Devils Lake in North Dakota.

                            Adonis had passed over the U.S.-Canada border about a month before arriving at the lake. Or maybe a little longer. He couldn't tell, the days and nights had started to blend together after a while. Some days Adonis had been on foot, but most of the time he had been hitching rides with truckers. Those first conversations pretty much all went the same way:

                            Where you going? Winnipeg? Shit, that's way out of my way. But I can take you along my route, that'll at least get you a little closer. His instinctive ability to make people do stuff for him won him plenty of fans, truckers practically making declarations of undying love- well, overwhelming lust, really. That was the first time the idea of exploring the company of a man crossed his mind. But he never got a chance. Maybe they were frightened by how easily this incredibly attractive man made them swoon and blush. Maybe the Divine Fire burned too hot for them to handle. Maybe a bit of both. But they always snapped, get-out-right-nows accompanied by slurs and fists and sometimes guns. After that, Adonis always took off on foot for a couple days, but desperation and a strange new desire to find a trucker who didn't pull away from him drove him to find another one... and another... and...

                            He had arrived in North Dakota, drawn to the banks of Devils Lake by Pilgrim Marks that he recognized on a gut level. He had been looking south to the far bank when she came: Elektra, a Muse like him, made from the body of a ballerina by another Promethean. She had taught him about himself, why he could bend people to his will. About the Lineages and the Refinements, the Great Work. About Centimani. About Pandorans.

                            It was about an hour before sunset on the Solstice. Adonis and Elektra had been sitting on the lakeside in the sand and mud. The silence was shattered by the sound of something bursting out of the ground, an awful thing of tentacles and twisted growths, and a mouth where a person's belly would be, lined with awful meter-long teeth of glass and bone: a Pandoran. They had both jumped up screaming with shock and horror. The creature had stood for a second, as if thinking about which one to attack. Then it picked- Elektra. It sprang at her with a feral yowl as she                             shrieked. Adonis had jumped backwards, out of its clutches.

He didn't look back, didn't stop until his lungs felt like they were filled with lava and his legs stopped moving, and he collapsed covered with sweat, not moving until dawn as he wept with remorse for Elektra and horror at the Pandoran and anger at himself. And then he had gotten up on trembling feet and started walking north again. And he promised that he would never tell anybody what had happened.

He knows what she was trying to do: this was a sort of flirting, showing him what she had to offer, what she could teach him. Fists clenched, he follows her to the bonfire. At best, the ones I've met try and isolate themselves with a couple of established, complimentary Pilgrims. At worst... they kill other Created. Leave more of the Flame Immortal for themselves."

                                                        To Scout's ear, there is something between disgust and awe in that voice, that these Pilgrims could do something so fundamentally brutal, so fundamentally human, as the destruction of fellow-travellers for their own gain.

                                                        When Adonis takes a hard look at the woman by the fire... she looks like Elektra. Exactly, disturbingly, like Elektra. But frost was not her Disfigurement. He's heard rumours that Created can come back from the dead at least once, maybe more, but that they need to do something in the afterlife first. Has she been down there all this time? Down in the dark, with the howling dead, wandering listless and blind, like the shades of Hades from that book Sharon once read to him, when she was still obsessed with him? When she was still the only other living being he'd ever met? If she's seen him, she hasn't reacted. Maybe she doesn't remember. Alchemy could do that (he had just done it) but there was also a River, down below, a River that stripped memories from the dead and allowed you to be reborn. Was that what Elektra had to do to come back?
