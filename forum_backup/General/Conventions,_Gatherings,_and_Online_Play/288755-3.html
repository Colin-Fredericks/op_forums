    I think you might be reading a bit past my point, Mark. In W:tF I don't believe homosexual couples could produce spirit children, but it was still a social stigma because that was just how the culture worked. So even if in W:tA homosexual werewolf couples can't produce metis, that doesn't necessarily mean that there isn't a social stigma attached to it either. I *know* that in W:tF the Uratha were "spiritual brothers and sisters" which was more important to most than the practical reasons (ie producing spirit children). I *don't know* if in W:tA there is a similar restriction on inter-werewolf relations. It sounds like you're telling me that they're more practical and don't have any social implications of such a relationship as long as no metis are produced. So if that is the case, then it's good for me to know. I mentioned earlier that Scott would want werewolf nookie to be "out of the pack", but if no one in W:tA cares about werewolf nookie then he wouldn't either.

    What I'm talking about isn't really incest or homosexuality: it's tradition. Most traditions start for some practical or pseudo-practical purpose, but just because a tradition isn't practical anymore doesn't mean that people (like Scott) won't keep the tradition anyways!

    Edit: I saw your reply too Josh. That's what I was saying about how different cultures have different views on duty of marriage, procreation, and what sex means in a person's life. I'm also interested in the cultural diversity, and I think that it's an interesting thing in W:tA where the tribes are so different from each other. In W:tF, the tribes were all like I said, very similar in outlook but different in methods. I think trying to be a "Bridge Between People" for so many unique groups will be a fun part of playing this game for me.

        My point was always more along the line of that homosexuality isn't actually a violation of the Litany. Nor in any of the Renoun charts is it discussed being gay is bad and will lose you renoun. However, that there is a clear stigma around it, and it is from a tradition, but the root of that tradition is that pairings of Garou could result in metis. So by the year 2014, it's probably a issue that's still being worked out by the Garou Nation as a whole. There's no rule that flat out say, "hey, gay is wrong," there's a rule that implies that Garou shouldn't bring metis into the world. That rule thus implies that Garou should have strong healthy offspring, and all Garou should be duty bound to do so.

        But that's all a formed tradition that started with a rule about not producing metis. I'm not saying you won't be judged for being gay, or that Werewolf society has no stance on it, just that the stance can probably universally be seen as up to interpretation with little argument by those Garou with a more liberal mindset. There are probably plenty of conservative Garou who will see the letter of the law, and agrue that. The point is that it's down to interpretation, there's no one hardline stance.

                        Hmmm, if you're new to all this, than maybe this was a bit of information overload. Forget the Litany, the most important thing is that garou must protect Gaia, and by extension, fight the Wyrm. So, let's ask each of the tribes the question, 'How do we fight the Wyrm?' We'll start with the Stargazers.


                        Stargazers: The Wyrm isn't the true enemy, the Weaver is. She traps us in a web of delusion, and only by understanding this can we liberate ourselves from her illusions. Garou must spread this message to all people, this is Gaia's will.

                        Get of Fenris: What a load of shit! The Wyrm is the real problem. But we've gotta prepare ourselves for Ragnarok, the Final Battle, otherwise, the Wyrm will win, and everything will end. So stop all the meditating and pseudo-mystical bullcrap, pick up a weapon and fight!

                        Glass Walkers: No, no, the Stargazer’s are onto something. The Weaver is always getting ignored, but maybe she’s our key to success! Forget the wilderness, if we work inside cities, we can change civilization from within! And it’s the Weaver who’ll show us the way, not the Wyld.

                        Red Talons: Miserable little charach! How dare you suggest we abandon the wild places? You betray Gaia! So long as green places exist, the Wyrm cannot win. When the world is smothered in the stinking corruption of mankind, then you will rue your mistake!

                        Bone Gnawers: Aw, what’s wrong wolfey, fleas biting? Ha! You dogs are all stupid, ‘specially you Get-boy. There’s no grand design to fightin’ the Wyrm. You just gotta do what you can when you can. The cities are the best place, ‘cause that’s where all the real rotten stuff is. But forget the Weaver, do it for Gaia. It’s all we can do.

                        Black Furies: What a pathetic, defeatist attitude. Have you forgotten you’re a wolf and not a mangy cur scrounging for scraps? We must protect the wild places, Gaia entrusted us with this sacred task. If we tend Her groves, then Her spirit endures. The Red Talons understand this. But, there is a second duty. So long as womankind remains oppressed from east to west, the Wyrm will win. It feeds on woman’s suffering, and if we lessen it, we will strike a great blow against the Destroyer!

                        Uktena: Ah yes, womankind, because apparently men can’t be oppressed. Bah, what do you know about suffering anyway, white woman? If only you saw as we did, then you’d weep and never cease. But enough of this. The Wyrm can be stopped, if we make sacrifices, if we truly give of ourselves, as our Middle Brother did. There is something, some secret, that will allow us to save Gaia, but why bother enlisting your aid in searching for it? No one ever understands.

                        Children of Gaia: But we must work together! It is the only thing that will save us from the coming darkness! Unity is our strength. If we put aside our old grievances and unite as one, nothing will stand before us. Don’t serve the Weaver or the Wyld, serve only Gaia, spread her love to all sentient beings, and the Wyrm will simply shrivel up and fade away. But we can only do this together.

                        Silver Fangs: Quite correct, we must unite. Thankfully, Gaia has already marked those who will bear the burden of leadership: Us. No, please, don’t bow…Alright, maybe just once. Gather under the banner of the Silver Fangs fellow Garou, and we will lead you to victory.

                        Shadow Lords: Yeees…Of course we shall unite with you brothers, of course. I wonder though, are you so certain Gaia appointed you as our kings? For we have heard a different story, and in fact, you might be surprised to learn who is truly destined to lead the Nation…

                        Wendigo: Typical whitey squabbling. Leave us out of it. We shall serve Gaia as we always have, before you so kindly gave us your…civilization. Pray our efforts are enough. Likely we will fail. So be it. Let it all end, and may the Wyrm choke on us!

                        Silent Striders: Geez, what did I miss? Sorry, I was…elsewhere. You wanna know how to fight the Wyrm? Umm…I’ll get back to you, I’ve gotta visit someone. In Myanmar. Before I go though, I’ve gotta tell you guys, you’re almost there, but maybe stop looking inwards, and look outside for once. Where did I hear it? Oh, a friend. Just a friend.

                        Fianna: Sigh. It’s a funny old world, and there’s not enough to drink.


                        Now if that's not diverse, I don't know what is. And that's only the tribes, not camps or even individual opinions.
