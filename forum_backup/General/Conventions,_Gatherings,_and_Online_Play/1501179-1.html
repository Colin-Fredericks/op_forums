    Elpis nods and then begins to stare into space. Thoughts begin to start whirling across their mind.

    "So Qaf doesn't know either. What does that mean? It means that we are going to be told together, and if we are being told together, we will be working together. No need to tell us separately, when we could be both told at once. What would require the two of us? An Arrow and a Mystagogue? A research mission? Unlikely, I don't have that kind of standing in the Mysterium yet, and I don't think my normal research warrants a guard. No, we would be working together in general, which means...a cabal!"

    Elpis nods, certain of their deduction, and glances at Qaf before starting into space again.

    "Why a cabal? And with an Arrow too...this is about Pandora, isn't it?" Elpis sighs, feeling the increasingly familiar ache return. "We were going to be formally recognized as a Cabal, weren't we? Is that your reason, Cloudchaser? Does the Consilium need another cabal? No, that feels insufficient. Maybe this is in fact a mission for us, I shouldn't dismiss the possibility out of hand. Could this be about my investigation? Does she know? Is that why I need to be in a cabal?" Anger flashes hot and quick. "I'm doing fine. I can find out what happened to Pandora on my own. I don't need help."

    Elpis look up for a moment, anger still arcing through their mind, when see happen to see out a window, to the crashing sea. As they watch the waves, the lyrics to the song they had been listening to come to mind.

    "- thought I heard the captain's voice
    But it's hard to listen while you preach
    Like every broken wave on the shore
    This is as far as I could reach​"

    Suddenly, all the anger melts away, leaving only weariness in its wake.

    "...Am I really doing ok? I haven't made any real progress on my own. I don't know where she was going or what she was doing, and I don't know how I can find out. Almost all of her possessions have been confiscated, and I don't have any real leads. What I really need is to see the past, and see where she went, but I never did study Time. Maybe...Maybe I should try to work together with Qaf. If nothing else, he might know someone." Elpis looks back at Qaf when a new thought strikes them. "I'm going to need to tell him, aren't I? I don't think he recognized me, maybe he doesn't know about Pandora? Maybe he hasn't heard any rumors yet... If we are going to work together, I should tell him. It would be the right thing to do, wouldn't it?... I don't want him to hear it from someone else first. He seems kind. Maybe he will understand. I should tell him... Not about my investigation. Not before the meeting, I don't even know we are working together, much less if he would or could help. But I should tell him about Pandora at least. If we have the potential to work together, he should know. If we are working together, then I'll tell him everything after the meeting."

    Elpis sighs. "Hey​, I think I might know what going on." They pause for a moment, then sigh again, and try to smile. "Ok​ maybe not exactly, but I think it's a pretty safe bet that we will be working together soon. And if that's the case, I think there is something you should know."

            Elpis takes Qaf's change of posture as a good sign and continues.

            "My mentor, Pandora, she...fell into Rapture. I'm told she was a danger to herself, and those around her. She was found and laid to rest. I...wasn't there. She had left on a business trip, and I had been left to guard our sanctum. After about a day, I saw distortions form from her soul stone. I studied them, and when they proved dangerous tried to contain them as best I could. They weren't just limited to the soul stone, everything her nimbus had touched was open to the distortions. I couldn't strip her nimbus, but I warded things as best I could, and tried to mitigate what slipped through. I managed like that for a few days, until suddenly, the distortions ceased. I...guess that's when she died. A few weeks later, I learned from a Mage named Watchtower that Pandora had been killed. I've been..kinda a wreck after that." Elpis laughs weakly and continues. "Anyways after that, everyone started handling things. Lots of stuff has happened since. It's been...rough. But I think I'm considered finished with my apprenticeship, which means I can be assigned work. Since I haven't really had anything yet, I can only imagine I will soon."

            Elpis sighs. "Sorry for hitting you with that all at once, but if we might be working together, you should know."

                    Elpis takes a moment to process the shift in conversation.

                    ”I think it would be accurate to say that I’m still a student, even though my apprenticeship is over. I’m currently studying the field of So​riology, with a heavy focus on Magosophy. Erm, basically I am studying the Awakened condition. My main area of active study though is the Nimbus. It’s simply wonderful how one’s connection to the Supernal manifests in this Fallen World. Everyone has a unique connection, which resonates with their sense of self, which then makes its way into the world around. I’m currently reading a series of books penned by one Ariel of the Silvered Bough detailing her thoughts and experiences​ with the Nimbus of herself and her cabal, noting for example how one’s personality changing over time affects their sense of self, and thereby their Nimbus. She noted how on of her cabal mages had recently had progressed in their chosen legacy, and postulates that the resulting change in their Nimbus was more a result of a change in their sense of self than furthering their connection to the Supernal. While I haven’t really been around someone who was deepening their connection to a legacy, if I get the opportunity I’d love to see the changes that would entail.”

                    Elpis pauses to take a long drink.

                    ”Oh, my bad, i’ve gotten on a tangent. I believe I’m more of a generalist than anything else, though I am more suited to academic pursuits than more physical activities. I’m afraid I’m not much of a warrior.”
