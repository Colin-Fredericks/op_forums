That is, do the BDS want to confront and transcend their Urge and thus extinguish their ego and reunite with the Soul of All? Or did they just ditch that in favour of 'Rahh, we're eeevil!         And now for Rall. I think pressure is good, it drives you onwards. If you look at when most of us posted our character sheets, it was over two weeks ago. And we created our character concepts before that. So I really don't wanna be an ass, but I don't want to wait another two weeks. But see, look how much you've done already! Some pressure's good for you.

        So about Cody:

        Auspice: Okay, I hope I express this properly. Basically, the guy playing our other Galliard, James, is very new to The Apocalypse. I'm guessing you're much more familiar with it seeing as you run games. You've obviously got much more of an idea as to how to play this Auspice, whereas James probably doesn't. I'd hoped James would be able to play his character in a vacuum, that is, express his character without the need to compare and contrast with another player, so everything he does can only be measured against himself. But if you also play a Galliard it'll mean he might get overshadowed by you. I'm not saying you shouldn't pick this Auspice, just be aware of it.

        Tribe: You're right, the Shadowlords are presented as the twisted reflection of the Silver Fangs. Both Tribes believe they're destined to rule, both believe might makes right, both believe they know how things should really be done. I think it's a cool Tribe, but you're not bound to follow the stereotype slavishly. Look at Val, he's an Indian Fenrir who think his Tribe's a joke and doesn't give a damn about 'Great' Fenris. Just because you're playing a Shadowlord doesn't mean you have to play them like a Shadowlord (yeah, that doesn't make sense. Consider it a Zen koan.)

        However...Cody is a tad similar to Hannah, I don't know if the pack can handle two overconfident, arrogant garou who think they should be in charge. The Shadowlords never came across as desiring the spotlight or open adulation. They seemed more like the 'power behind the throne' always watching, always waiting, always planning. In fact, it's Tribes like the Get of Fenris which exult fame and renown.

        Backstory: I have only limited understanding of American culture, but would a male cheerleader really be so popular? I like the idea, but I didn't know male cheerleaders occupied the apex of cool in American highschools.

        And I'm assuming Cody went berserk in the classroom and gutted some students...Again, I'm a terrible pedant, but how likely is that? Yes, I know, werewolves aren't very likely either, but if a bunch of kids turned up in the morgue with their entrails hanging out, there would be investigations. Especially in a developed country like America. In India, in a very rural state (Like Jharkhand), it's just possible for such a thing to occur. Maybe. I'm only saying this to inspire you. When the easy explanations are removed, you're forced to come up with something more intricate. Don't be afraid to try something different. High school student turns into a werewolf? Yeah, okay. Secret Metis child of a prominent Shadow Lord is sent to America to investigate why the original Shadow Lords of the Delaware Valley turned to the Wyrm...or die trying? Don't you think that's got more bite to it? Or at least, more story possibilities. Not that you have to play a Metis...

        I personally don't find Cody that interesting now, he definitely needs some spicing up. I'm more than happy to work with you on ideas, but be warned, they tend towards the...elaborate (and our poor Story Teller has enough plot threads to untangle.)

        Seriously though, I'd prefer not having another High School student in the pack.


        EDIT: Hayaraba! He Ram! By all the Asuras and Devas! What the hell happened? Everything I said is now meaningless! Um...You can still consider playing that secret Metis...ahem...
