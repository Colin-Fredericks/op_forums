                                                            Background

                                                            Jarsiln was born in a small shantytown in the reaches. His parents were part of a group of pacifists who objected to working to support the military. Rather than face persecution for their refusal to work, the group fled the cities to seek sancuatary in the reaches.

                                                            Growing up, Jarsiln was mostly content to live a simple life in his small village. One thing Jarsiln was not content to do, was side idle when so many scavengers or monsters attacked the town. Despite his parents arguements Jarsiln could not content himself with simply defending the town. Once he was old enough to care for himself he began, after each attack, to set from the village to find and punish those that hurt his home.

                                                            It was on one of these trips that he exalted as a chosen of Luna. Cornered by several scavengers he talked despretaly, hoping to convince them to spare him. As he spoke he found the words coming easier to him, the arguements more persuasive and his efforts more successful. The resulting fight, as some scavengers sought to release him and some sought to kill him, gave Jarsiln the perfect opportunity to sneak away to safety.

                                                            Over the next year Jarsiln tracked, infiltrated and then killed all of the nearby scavenger and bandit groups. He might have stayed in the village, but for the image of the Tyrant Sun standing over a beaten and injured Luna that had burned itself into his memory during his exaltion. Driven by the same desire to punish those that injure others, Jarsiln set out to join the other exalted and punish the Tyrant Sun for his crimes.
