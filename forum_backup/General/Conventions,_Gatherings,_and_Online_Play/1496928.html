      Hellhound’s Keeper:
      The Queen of Ivory and Thorn
      She is beautiful, sculpted from a single, enormous chunk of living ivory, bedecked in an exquisite gown scarlet red in color. She ruled a vast, primordial forest in Faerie, a the center of which, atop a vast cliff, stood her castle. Filled with trembling minions and terrified changeling slaves, the Queen of Ivory and Thorn was both a fountain of transcendent benevolence and a pit of endless despair. When she favored a changeling, pleasures of all kinds abounded. When she hated a changeling, that changed, as the changeling was subjected to cruel, meaningless punishments.

      But the Queen could not rest easily, as her estranged husband, a Gentry called the King of Ebony and Pollen constantly sought to kill her. Marshaling great armies of the flower-encrusted undead, the King would wage war upon his Queen. The Queen, for her part, kept vicious, berserker changeling hounds, and the battle at the border of the two Realms consisted on long stretches of boredom punctuated with mindless terror and merciless rage.

      His Keeper’s archnemesis:
      The King of Ebony and Pollen
      Long ago, a great Faerie king of stags wandered Arcadia, til an arrow took him down. Flowers grew throughout his corpse, and, twisting into humanoid shape and now changed beyond reckoning, the King of Ebony and Pollen sought out his killer.

      It did not take long before he found who he sought, a cruel Queen of Ivory and Thorns in a high castle. Digging roots into the earth, the King dredged up flower-encrusted undead and set them upon the Gentry who so foolishly thought to threaten him.

      Though the Queen, his wife, could hold his forces back indefinitely, the earth contains so many dead the King has an unlimited number of troops. Sometimes, though, he would have mortals brought to him in chains. Some were kept as playthings, but most went into the neverending meatgrinder of war.

                  1. Lambry County, Montana, USA: Ironside, only
                  Lambry County is fictitious in the real world, but here it is a large stretch of rural Montana countryside, consisting of several townships and archaeological sites of Native origin.

                  The towns of Lambry consists of:
                  * Lambry, itself (heart of the local freehold), with around 2,000 people; a pair of unknown trods, in addition to the known trod at the freehold, lurk in Lambry township. Marcy’s Diner is in Lambry.

                  * Babbling Brook, a nice little tourist spot. Hundreds come here every year for the quaint culture of the area. It’s unknown if there’re any trods, but most feel it’s highly likely. Juniper Lake is just outside it’s municipal borders.

                  * Cooperfield, where SCA events are held three times a year, drawing in lots of tourists. Huntsmen are sometimes seen here, when such events are held, though abductions are rare. It is nevertheless a source of paranoia for the freehold...and the location of several trods under the freehold’s strict control.

                  * Dylan Ranch, a burgeoning town centered around a series of ranches owned by the influential mortal Dylan family; nearly 3,0000 people, here. Several trods, scattered here and there.

                  * The Estates, a colorful term for people living off-the-grid. Few are native to the area, and they don’t take kindly to interlopers. They’re scattered here and there.

                  * Gnome (a farming community) with just under 1,000 inhabitants; strangely, no trods. Many changelings find this quite suspicious.

                  * Hansentown, built around a floundering coal mine that has seen better days, with less than 1,000 residents?

                  * The Trapper Estate, a local historical landmark, is owned and maintained by the Dylan family. It’s known for its massive indoor swimming pool, open only to the Dylans.

                  2. Ironside sites of interest.
                  The ill-omened Arcadia State Park is an old-growth forest rooted in local superstition. Fae who come here often never return. As a result, the House Up On The Hill has declared Arcadia State Park forever off-limits.

                  Juniper Lake, located just outside Babbling Brook, is a popular place for locals to come and fish at.

                  3. Lambry County’s freehold
                  The House Up On The Hill, a historic manor from the early 1920s, owned by the Fermen family (all four of whom are changelings), this estate is the Lambry County freehold. It is located in Lambry, the town.

                  With a hidden trod in the basement that leads to a hob town in the local Hedge, the House Up On The Hill rents rooms to changelings desperately in need of a roof over their head and a bed to sleep in. The rent is rather low, but duties to the freehold is the true ‘rent’.

                  Each of the Fermen siblings is a changeling, and, by the unknowable hand of the Wyrd, they each represent one of the Four Seasonal Courts.
                  * Archduke Franklin Fermen, a Riddleseeker Fairest, rules as Spring monarch. Under his Mask, Franklin is a feathered serpent-man, elegant, beautiful and extremely cunning....and highly, highly poisonous. His Keeper, Scarecrow Jim, used his ‘pet snake’ for a variety of things, the least of which was as a source of poison.

                  * Countess Francise Fermen, a Fireheart Darkling, is the Summer monarch. A fiery shadow, billowing smoke and searing flame, she is a borderline Bridge-Burner, but refuses to fully commit as her girlfriend was recaptured by a Huntsman, and Francise holds out the hope she might rescue her better half. Her Keeper was Scarecrow Jim.

                  * Prime Minister Richie Fermen, a kithless Elemental, is the Autumn monarch. A cinder that refuses to entirely go out, Richie is the most powerful changeling in Lambry County. His Contracts manifest as elemental effects that have almost ‘fizzled out’, as if barely there at all. A narcissist, Richie believes only intimidation can protect him. His Keeper was Scarecrow Jim.

                  * And finally, Marquesa Leah Fermen, a Flowering Beast, is the Winter monarch. She is a stalk of fungus growing up out of the head of a strangely beautiful human-like insectoid body, a parasite piloting her own body. One day, she will consume herself. Thankfully, that day is not today. She was kept in a pumpkin patch by her Keeper, Scarecrow Jim.

                  Not counting the Seasonal monarchs and the PCs, twenty other Lost gather at the House Up On The Hill, though only one of them doesn’t rent rooms there:
                  * Anansi is a Nigerian immigrant, but he came here fleeing from Grandmother Spider, a horror that had him in her web. She’d pluck that string, and Anansi’d hum out prophecies. He’s symbolically insectile, under his Mask. He’s Courtless. He lives in the House Up On The Hill, but works as a janitor in Lambry township.

                  * Bluefingers, a Notary Wizened, escaped Who Bookbinds With Blood, but the ink on her fingers will never fade. A skilled pledgesmith, Bluefingers belongs to the Autumn Court.

                  * Damien, a young Beast of uncertain kith, who appears as a Sphinx under his Mask, but without wings, refuses to talk about his durance, a fact other changelings can respect. His Keeper is unknown. He’s Summer Court. He shares a Hollow with Old Man Scratch.

                  * Eloi; resembling a stereotypical Grey alien under his Mask, is known throughout the County as the ‘computer guy’. He runs a small one-man tech-support/computer repair operation. Though he volunteers nothing about himself, it IS known he was Kept by a Gentry known as The Laboratory. He’s Autumn Court.

                  * Ember, mortal name unknown is fire-in-water, a Water-Born Elemental with a focus on fire-based magics. He is a loner, but is nominally a member of the Summer Court. His Keeper was an Eldritch flame atop still seas titled Heart of the Ocean. Ember lives in Dylan Ranch, working on a farmstead.

                  * Ethan of the Stars, a Polychromatic Fairest, is the most recent addition to the freehold, having escaped just at a week ago. He’s terrified the King of Colors will find him. Currently Courtless, most expect Ethan to join Autumn or Winter.

                  * Father, known to mortals as Father Andrew Keene, an avid member of the Spring Court, is the spiritual counsel that keeps many Lambry changelings from giving up and dying. His Christian faith is unshakable, and his compassion seems boundless. Under the Mask, he bears the visage of a Church gargoyle. His interpretation of Spring is less “hedonism” and more “let us revel in our freedom from Arcadian tormentors”. He’s a frequent congregant to the Hanging Rafters Church. The Merciless Abbot was his Keeper.

                  * The Numuzo’ho, of native descent, is a Gargantuan Elemental. His true form is that of a humanoid storm of epic size. He’s a Spring Courtier, and runs a small ‘mom-and-pop’ army surplus store open only to the fae, hidden in the Hedge. He refuses to talk about his durance, but snowstorms make him extremely nervous.

                  * Known as Old Man Scratch, a euphemism for the Devil, this Courtless changeling is one of the few fae not to rent a place in the House Up On The Hill. Old, wrinkled and with a patchy white beard, Old Man Scratch is a Runnerswift Ogre, a charging bull that has seen much better days. His Keeper was the Red Rose Matador. He shares a Hollow in the Hedge with Damien.

                  * Redfeather, aka Izzy Montanyo was transformed into a Draconic Fairest by her lecherous Keeper, the Cartographer of Songbirds. Since escaping, Redfeather has taken it upon herself to run several mortal charities. She’s Winter Court. Her and Simurge Sam are boyfriend/girlfriend.

                  * Sara Sumpter is a bit of the sun broken off and made animate. A Windwing Elemental obsessed with the very concept of light, Ms. Sumpter surrounds herself with symbolism of sun gods and solar angels. Her Keeper was Ancient Helios. Magically powerful, she’s a member of the Summer Court.

                  * Simurgh Sam, a Notary Beast, bears the mien of the all-knowing bird of Arabic folklore, maintains the freehold’s library. Silent, grim and despairing. His Keeper was the Djinni in the Candle. Sam belongs to the Winter Court. Sam and Redfeather are an item.

                  * The Somber Sister, aka, Margaret Thresher, is an estranged direct relative of the Dylan family. Taken by a childlike Gentry (‘Little Lord Lukas’), and forced to dance endlessly, the Somber Sister, under her Mask, resembles a giant wind-up toy in the form of a blocky ballerina. She’s Winter Court.

                  * Tank, a member of the Summer Court, is paralyzed below the waist, moving about on a motorized wheelchair. He is a spider, under the Mask, and his wheelchair’s his crawling mass of spider-hob “charioteers”. Angry and bitter. A Bridge-Burner without remorse.

                  4. Lambry County’s Hedge:
                  On the other side of the Lambry trods, the Hedge is worn paths going through areas framed by grass so high one cannot see over it, a blistering sun overhead in a serene blue sky. These pathways are made of dirt and are clausterphobically small.

                  Many strange hobs live here, of which the following three sites are merely a small sample:
                  * Bucketown is a hob town directly connected to the freehold by a trod in the freehold’s basement. To enter this bizarrely morbid town, one must “kick the Bucket”, ie, a very small hob wearing a bucket on his head. The Bucket gets offended if a visitor doesn’t kick him, and will plot out elaborate revenges. Bucketown is a disturbing place, filled with Tim Burton-esque funeral homes, maze-like taxidermy shops, clausterphobic graveyards and impossibly tiny coffinmakers’ houses. Darklings often come from far afield to visit Bucketown. Bucketown is ruled by the Questionable Pigeon, a shady avian hob. A strange fishy Goblin Queen, Witchypoo, runs a candy shop here.

                  * Bulletproof Bill’s Circus of Marvels, Moonlight and Stars Upon Crowns is the Lambry County Goblin Market. Taking the form of a great Vaudeville Circus mixed with a caravan of snake-oil salesmen, Bulletproof Bill’s Circus both entertains changelings, all wonders both terrible and sublime, and offers the rarest goblin fruits. Led by an unusually tall and thin living marionette named Bill, this Goblin Market is indispensable to the freehold. Meanwhile, a brine-soaked monster keeps the peace.

                  * The Hanging Rafters Church is a large hobgoblin community, but it is one stranger than most. Here, in this derelict cathedral in the middle of nowhere, hobs suspended by marionette wires perform strange aerial dances while a vast, towering muppet-preacher, Parrot Preacher Phil, bird-like in appearance, gives nonsensical ‘sermons’. He who donates fresh apples into the church’s poorbox can spend pre-existing Experience to learn a single Goblin Contract, but only ever once per individual. Father is a frequent congregant.

                  Some hobs wander to and fro, though, such as Carp. A silver humanoid carp with very interesting Goblin Contracts for sale, Carp desires nothing more than mischief that spills out of control.

                      Other supernaturals in Lambry County:
                      * Arcadia State Park has strange fishman protectors, benthic monstrosities that keep the park clean of any supernaturals other than their own kind. They are open to no negotiations.

                      * Babbling Brook’s tourist trade is known for its fishing and hunting, with lodges built for paying customers. The Huntsmen avoid the town, but so do the local changelings, if only during the night. Daytime visits are fine, but a huge wolf haunts the area at night, and it seems unfriendly to all things supernatural. In Juniper Lake, meanwhile, something lurks beneath the water.

                      * Dylan Ranch, a township of interconnected ranches all slowly bought out by the mortal Dylan family, appears to have hobs scattered throughout. These hobs are vicious little things, all claws and teeth and stinging tails. They go to sleep when mortals are near, and only awaken at the arrival of disturbing but mortal farmhands hired onto the farmsteads.

                      * The Estates are known for strange residents, a few of which are supernaturals of some sort. They stick to themselves, though, and the freehold leaves them alone in turn.

                      * A single visitor sometimes appears at the House Up On The Hill, an old, wrinkled man wearing rugged clothing and smelling of burnt wood. Many changelings falsely believe him a Huntsman, but the truth is unsure. When he needs to, the Burning Man can bring down a forest fire, and transform himself into a giant fiery figure made of wicker kindling. No trace of this remains after he leaves. As it appears nothing catches on fire and no evidence endures for mortal analysis. The Burning Man appears helpful to the fae, but has a fierce temper.

                      * Those who who hang out near Juniper Lake after dark often feel like something’s watching them.

                      * Persistant rumors place a Sasquatch in the sparse woodlands near Gnome. Is it true? Who knows.

                      * Something hideous lairs in the Hansentown mine, a figure of incredibly advanced age, too wrinkled to be alive, yet miraculously animate. It’s name and gender are unknown, but it will occasionally talk to changelings, who called it the Old Scrap in the Mine. It only appears at night.

                          Halwyn 'Hellhound' Montgomery


                          The tall Irishman with short dark brown hair and hazel eyes who goes by the name Halwyn Montgomery is a day laborer who accepts any job that comes his way. What he lacks in intelligence, he more than makes up for in strength. He is not actively stupid but rather a bit naïve and inexperienced in today's world.
                          The somewhat handsome man in his late twenties or early thirties has been living with his caravan near Marcy's Diner for a little over six months.
                          For someone who can see behind his mask, Halwyn also known as Hellhound doesn't look much different. His teeth and fingernails are more predatory, and his eyes burn with fire. The scars, of which he has many, are blackened and occasionally glow. If you didn't know he was an ogre, you would assume he was a beast or an elemental.
                          He is friendly and helpful, neither the great leader nor the devoted servant, he is just him.
                          When there's trouble, he's one of the first to go into battle to defend the Freehold.

                                      Lambry County’s most recurring Gentry and Huntsmen:
                                      Though the Gentry and their Huntsmen know no geographical boundary, two Gentry and two Huntsmen are known to hunt in the local area.
                                      1. The two Gentry who most frequently hunt here are:
                                      * The Impossibly Tall Man appears infinitely tall, with only his legs and his extraordinarily long arms visible to humans and fae alike. He dresses in what appears to be a tuxedo, with enormous shoes looking like they came from a stereotypical bowling alley. His voice is booming thunder, his steps shake the earth and weather distorts in his passage. He most frequently makes use of a Huntsman called the Stone Dancer.

                                      * Scarecrow Jim is responsible for kidnapping the entire Fermen family, and thus views the House Up On The Hill as ‘his’ in some manner. He runs a nightmarish Arcadian farm. He frequently makes use of a Huntswoman named Tammy-under-the-Forest.

                                      2. The two Huntsmen, each attached most frequently to one of the above-mentioned Keepers, are:
                                      * The Stone Dancer, most frequently containing a Title of the Impossibly Tall Man, shows up every so often at a Cooperfield SCA event, but never seems to try and take anyone. This has the freehold aflame with paranoia...which may be the entire point.

                                      * Tammy-under-the-Forest is a Huntswoman most commonly invested with a Title by Scarecrow Jim. In the Hedge, she’s a two dimensional being that runs under Hedge-soil, and is skilled at Hedgeshaping to one degree or another.

                                          Mystery Cults of Lambry:
                                          Thankfully, only one such group exists: Candlelit Circle, a fraternal organization run by the Dylans. It has people of high station throughout Lambry County as members, and presents itself as a harmless social club. Meeting weekly in the Trapper House, the Candlelit Circle worship what the freehold is certain is a benthic Gentry of some sort.
