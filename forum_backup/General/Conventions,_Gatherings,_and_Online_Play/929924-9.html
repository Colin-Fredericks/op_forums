            As you open the second door, the hinge catches and the door seems to be stuck. You push just a little harder, and the door suddenly jerks open. Your grip on the handle slips and the door smashes against the wall with a loud clatter. In the otherwise quiet chambers, the noise carries down the hallway. You suddenly feel a chill as a breeze stirs the air, coming from further down the hallway.

            The room behind this second door looks like a storage closet, including a broom, a simple gardening hoe, and more quills and inkwells.

            Take a beat for the dramatic failure.


            Lynx, Ragnar, and Dr. Asphodel
            You all take a step right into the Iris. Immediately, you feel a pulling sensation...

            ...and your world is a turbulent, confusing kaleidoscope of darkness and movement. You realize that you're disoriented, but more alarming, when your mind clears you realize you're all underwater.

            I need each of you to roll Dexterity + Athletics. If you fail, roll Stamina + Composure. You also have the Poor Light tilt here at -2 perception. You may roll Wits + Composure for perception, using this penalty.


            Moonstone
            The hail crashes into you, sapping even more of your mana. It burns, although perhaps not as much as it did a few moments ago...odd.

            You touch the Iris, being pulled through, and...

            You experience a foul, gut-wrenching nausea and the sensation of breathing in a cloud of noxious fumes. It's jarring and painful, but only for a second.

            ...and then you blink, and you're in a very strange place. You're lying on a cot in a small room, drenched in sweat like you've just run ten miles. There are a few books stacked on a desk next to you, an oil lantern lit in the corner, and a quill and inkwell next to what looks like parchment paper. The walls are made of what looks like hewn stone. The door to your room is cracked open, peering into an adjacent hallway.

            You realize with dread that your emotions are suddenly rushing back into you. Whatever happened, walking through that Iris seems to have stripped away Binder's magic calming your mind.

            You suddenly hear a loud clatter very close by, and feel a gust of wind pass a moment later.

            Lose 1 more mana. Your Divergent condition returns in the active state.



            Binder
            Moonstone vanishes before your eyes as he passes into the Iris. When he does, the black cloud clinging to the Iris suddenly dislodges itself, breaking away and collapsing into a puddle on the ground. You feel a rising sense of something supernatural happening. This doesn't surprise you, because the black puddle is starting to rise up from the ground. It levitates and congeals into a perfectly spherical ball, although slimy droplets occasionally ooze off off it and fall to the ground beneath it.

            OOC: I forgot if you have active Sight or not currently because it's not clear from your header on your last post, but you would see this even with your mundane vision now.
            You also distinctly sense something snapping, as you feel your Mind spell protecting Moonstone suddenly unravel the moment he disappears. You do have a piece of his hair held firmly in your hands, and his brief instructions on how to somehow use it to reach him.
