                            Elpis is something of a shut in. They have a hard time making friends, and instead focus all their social energy on the few they already have. They also abuse One Mind, Two Thoughts to keep up with life while also keeping up with Mage stuff, and just generally to function, making interactions with them…odd. They regularly had telepathic conversations with Pandora, and have a terrible poker face. Sometimes they just don’t show up to class for a few days, and still make near perfect grades. They also have a tendency to have their head in the clouds even on a good day. They read normal novels and stuff…but also books about magical theory, philosophy and mythology. Walking into walls because they were reading is not uncommon. Basically, anyone who knows of them will probably have thought of them as exceedingly eccentric, maybe spread a few rumors about them, but generally they pass under the radar.

                            Yeah that makes sense. They tend to see that all souls are united by the Astral, basically like cells are united by the body. Even individual souls are made of cells, little aspects of emotion, memory, and reason. They have other reasons too. They at one point got ahold of a book penned by a seasoned Arrow taking about some of the more common monsters, and how to survive them. The sections on Spirits were pretty brutal, and that stuck with Elpis. The second part is they aren’t sure where to place Spirits categorically on the is a person scale. Elpis like categorization. This is a rock with properties xyz, and is not a person. But Spirits blur lots of lines. They are their concept, but also not? Also Elpis is more aware than most that being able to talk with something doesn’t actually make it a person. Goetia can be, but most aren’t, depending on their role/archetype. See dream actors. Ghosts probably are. Spirits? They can talk and think, but can barely understand stuff outside of their related concepts. So they don’t know what is appropriate to say or do around spirits, and that makes them nervous.
