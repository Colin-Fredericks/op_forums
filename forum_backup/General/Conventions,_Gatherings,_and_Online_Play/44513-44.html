    Physical Description

    Standing five foot and six inches tall Amelia is the very image of country girl green eyes, long blond hair that are kept in a braid that fall down her back. She is slim and a bit muscular and move with an almost unnatural grace.

    Personality

    Amelia is a very gentle soul by nature but since her experience in the jungle her character as hardened. she is naturally gifted swordsman but doesn't realize it herself seeing it as another facet of being a chosen of the sun. She doesn't like to kill people but most of the enemies of creation aren't people. Since her exaltation Amelia as shown great anger and rage in certain situations. Her Exaltation as a solar exalted

    Background

    Raised in a small farming community near Greyfall, her life was not bad she had a steady job as a waitress in the local tavern that payed well but she secretly prayed for something to exciting to happen. One day her wish was answered when a priest came to raise a new cult presenting his god as a peaceful deity that would bring prosperity, the fact that he paid a lot of money to the community for lands to build his temple gave them no reason not accept. This new cult had a great impact on the community, helping them cooperate with one another and before long most of the community was preaching the mother of order as their patron goddess including Amelia.

    However after a few years a few people disappeared without any trace this alarmed the community but after a few month without any other incident everything returned to normal. One day Amelia was invited to the temple by the priest, When she arrived at the temple she found him waiting for her and invited her to eat with him as they talk what they talk about she doesn't remember since the drugs in her food knock her out. She regained conscious laying on an altar of bones in the forest. Torches burned with green flames and many cultist were present chanting it was then that the priest appeared with a dagger in his hand. As he was about to stab her she began to trying to free herself until she heard the most beautiful voice she had ever heard it each words where simple but very commanding as they shook her very soul.

    " Rise my champion, you shall slay the wicked and protect the innocent for it is why you have been chosen. Rise sword of heaven !!! "

    The priest was sent flying by the sudden burst of essence from her exaltation as a great dragon with golden scales and fangs like swords appeared and gave her a glowing golden sword, what ensured after was bloody. The cultists died quickly, she showed them no mercy but she took her time with the priest, his death was slow as she cut him apart limb by limb as her wrath spoke with every strike of her sword. After she was done she realized that she didn't know where she was. Lost in the forest and alone without food or water but it didn't alarmed her Amelia the waitress died that night and Amelia the sword of heaven was born.

    She gathered what she could from the cultist and left drifting north along the rock river until she encountered a small paved path leading deep into the jungle something seemed familiar about that path and she decided to follow it. The path lead to a mansion unlike anything she had seen before, it was truly beautiful but protected by a strange creature the size of a man but without any facial feature. the creature attacked her and overpowered her. It was about to kill but stopped when seeing her caste mark. It retreated and extended it's hand to help her get up and it apologized with a soft and gentle voice.

    The creature invited Amelia inside and presented itself as Alamass the caretaker of the mansion which he explained was a manse dating back to the first age built as a summer home for his former master. This more he explained the more Amelia already knew about the house as flashes of memory appeared to her, those flashes Alamass explained meant that she was the reincarnation of his master and welcomed Amelia into her new home. Alamass taught her to read and count among many other things and life was good for a while until a small band of fairfolks came sniffing around the manse it was then that Alamass showed her the true reason as to why he was guarding the manse so ferociously, before she died her former incarnation had hidden her mighty battle armor inside the manse for her future incarnation and ancient piece of equipment that dated back to before the first age.

    With this new armor and Alamass the fairfolks didn't stand a chance after they set forth for the nearest center of civilization that Alamass knew called Novalesk.
