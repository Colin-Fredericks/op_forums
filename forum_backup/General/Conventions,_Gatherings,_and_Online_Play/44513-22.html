                        Gens Origenon
                        While the Cadet family system allows a Gens to foster extended groups of immigrants and refugees, individuals and households are brought in as part of Gens Origenon. Origenon lacks any centralized hierarchy or organization, though it's members gain Native and Citizen status like their cousins in the Gentes or Cadet houses. Over time, members of Gens Origenon usually clump together to form cadet families, or get themselves adopted as members of the Gentes (usually through marriage).

                        Most members of Origenon are clustered in the capital, though they can be found anywhere throughout the nation. Despite being the nominal 'Seventh Gens', Orgienon lacks representation in the Boule. This fails to truly impact the 'Gens' as a whole, as those members that are offended by this typically marry into one of the Six Great Gentes.

                                Ramshackle Street
                                The legends of Ramshackle Street stretch all throughout Creation. It is said to be mystical boulevard that defies space and time, wherein one might find anything they desire. Exotic beings buy and sell and trade wonders forged in the Age of Glories, the First Age, or even the time before Creation, beneath a star-spackled sky.

                                This is not the Ramshackle Street of which I speak.

                                No, the street in Novalesk that has gained fame as 'Ramshackle Street' is merely a street in the poorer section of the city (Novalesk is much too small to have 'slums'). This Ramshackle steet is home to dozens of tiny Bazaars, each one a pale imitation of a shadow of the mythical boulevard. Though, rumors suggest that this Ramshackle Street is one of the locations where a traveller, if they lose their way, may find themselves upon the starry avenue. But those are just rumors, aren't they?

                                Many a child in Creation knows of the rhyme of Ramshackle Street:

                                Ramshackle Street, Ramshackle Street,
                                Where you'll find items that are rather neat.
                                Where are you going? You should watch your feet.
                                You'll get lost forever on Ramshackle Street.
                                Caught in the wonders of Ramshackle Street.
