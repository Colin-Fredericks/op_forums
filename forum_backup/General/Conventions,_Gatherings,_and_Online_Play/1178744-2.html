                                            Alright, guess I'll run my mage up the flagpole and see who salutes:

                                            Mjolnir (Sleeper name TBD) is in many ways the opposite of what your average mage might expect from someone of his Path. He's a bit dim, clumsy and tactless where the stereotypical Acanthus is quick, clever and subtle. The kid might make a decent bruiser character in a standard WoD chronicle, but as a mage he's at something of a starting disadvantage. Strong and athletic, but never really skilled or well-liked enough to be picked for sports teams, certainly not enough for anyone to pull strings when his grades slip. Even the symbolism isn't quite right (hammers traditionally being tools of the Moros)!

                                            He Awakened after a night of misadventure when his supposed friends ditched him to escape the police and he realized that the people he'd trusted were just using him. A vision of fleeing through dark and increasingly narrow back alleys overgrown with barbed-wire ended when he turned and, blindly grabbing the first thing he could reach, took a defiant swing at his pursuers... unwittingly decking one of the arresting officers. He's out of juvie now and, dimly aware that something is different about him, trying to turn his life around.

                                            (Naming yourself after a tool/weapon of the gods is probably not the best starting decision to make in a Gnostic setting when attempting to shake old habits of trusting the wrong people... but hey, the thing followed him back from Arcadia, and it was the first thing to come up when he Googled "magic hammer.")

                                            As implied, I'm planning to spend some of his starting merit dots on an Artifact, if this meets with everyone's approval. Character concept would be "blunt instrument of Fate" on his sheet.
