    Online Vampire 2e Game Looking for players

    12-25-2015, 03:26 AM
    I'm currently running a vampire 2e game and I'm looking for 3 more players. The game is set in NYC 2018 "Current date is Nov 15". This game exist in the same world as other chronicles of darkness games i'm running so your actions can affect other games or there actions can affect your's.

    So here is a bit about the city currently

    NYC is broken up to 5 boroughs and each one is owned completely or partly by different covenants
    Manhattan = Invictus
    Brooklyn = Invictus / Circle of the Crone / Lancea et Sanctum
    Queens = Circle of the Crone
    Bronx = Carthian Movement
    Staten Island = Ordo Dracul

    Each of the Boroughs are at a technical state of war with each other. Mostly a shadow war that is becoming hot. As each borough bickers with each other New Jersey Looms in the horizon wanting to take all the territories of NYC to it self.

    So far the Invictus just got done dealing with an event with the strix, The prince installed in Brooklyn for the Invictus met the final death and now his sister holds the fort out there vs the Crone and the Sanctum. Some strange company called "Immortal Bio Chemicals" seem to be making odd movies that the Invictus has noticed. Also its flu season.

    So we play over skype, google plus, and I do play by post

    If you want to know more just send me a message
