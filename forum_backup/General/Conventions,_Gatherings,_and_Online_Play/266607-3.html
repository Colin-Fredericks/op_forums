        Since there was no commentary, I'll just go with my original thought and use Free.

        Meaghan Scully was born in Chicago during the roaring twenties, the child of factory worker and a waitress. She group during the unfortunate period now know as the Great Depression and wasn't much different from any other young girl at the time. She certainly wasn't the type that was at all interested in the sciences or anything like that. She was quite a bit more down to Earth and centered in the day to day worries of helping her family get enough to eat by doing sewing, laundry and other small jobs for other people in her neighborhood, those that could afford to pay for a little extra help, that is. When she reached high school, she started working as a waitress similar to her mother. Then came Roosevelt and the New Deal producing jobs for all sorts of people that had before been jobless. With her siblings mostly grown into adults and their own lives and her parents being taken care of by the new Social Security measures, she wasn't needed as much any longer, but had already found it hard to break the habits she had developed in that time. She was already focused on doing work.

        In the 1930s, Meaghan a young mechanic's apprentice with a slight hispanic look and a Texan drawl came into her restaurant and started to flirt with her. He hadn't been the first, but she responded with her usual polite dismissal and focusing on business rather than acknowledging the attempt. Strangely enough, the young man wasn't dissuaded and persisted in his efforts, regaling her with his intentions to earn enough money to buy his own ranch, until she finally gave in and began to date him. They were married in late 1938, Meaghan taking his name Reyes, and had a son in 1940 as the world started to descend into war around them. When the Japanese attacked Pearl Harbor and the call went out for volunteers, her husband, Jacob, answered and joined up, even volunteering for the paratroopers. For most of four years, Meaghan raised her son Martin on her own, fretting over the stories of the war, trying to keep calm through the Siege of Bastogne and doing everything she could to save the money that Jacob sent home to her. Finally he returned home...and he'd returned home with a windfall that Meaghan was never able to learn the source of. Between the money that she had saved during the war, her waitressing and his combat pay, and the money that he had brought home with him, they had enough to purchase some fallow land in Colorado and start that ranch that Jacob wanted.

        Things should have gone on from there as happily as any fairy tale. Unfortunately, They came for her in 1946. Alien abductions would not receive widespread attention and enter into the framework of American folklore for at least another eleven years when Antonio Vilas Boas claimed to have been abducted and returned, or four years after that when Betty and Barney Hill would make a similar claim. Meaghan was gone before those things ever happened.

        (reprint of earlier bit in Meaghan's words)

        "It came upon a midnight clear with an old glorious song by beings like angels bending near the earth, but they weren't. I don't remember how they came to me, all I could see was that colour streaming down out from the space above and I couldn't find the will to move as a solid shape formed and I saw my own face flow down over the stardust and shadow stepping past me with a blank expression. Or maybe I was walking past it. Maybe neither of us were moving, but space between us shifted and shrunk. I don't know. But then I was in the light and It was in the fields below. It went on to live with my husband and raise my child. While I remained in that light and pulled up (or was it down) into that brilliant night."

        "I can't say what they were. They were Vorlons and Shadows, they were the Drej, they were the Greys and the Body Snatchers. Silence and Daleks and Weeping Angels. They were things I couldn't really understand at all. I don't think they changed and when they seemed different then I never noticed, or felt it was only because I was realizing something new about them, but something which had only recently come to my attention."

        "I can't remember all that happened in that place, or was it places, it felt like hours (and sometimes days), but when I finally took a left turn where I was told to go right, it was sixty years ahead. The daze cleared and my mind returned. I can't say I escaped. All I did was get distracted a moment and take a wrong turn and suddenly I was back here. My husband dead, that thing masquerading still as me and my child a grandparent of his own. I may just be another experiment being run. Or maybe the entire thing was an experiment, trying to see how long it would be before I found the way out of the maze and returned to the human world. Maybe they've finished with me. Or maybe they haven't."

        "I've studied since then. They appeared like creatures out of the science fiction of your history. Maybe they saw that coming, maybe they're the future and took a trip to the past. That place(s?) only made sense while I was still there, maybe time works differently? But maybe they influenced these things. Anyway, I study, I learn to reach out from my place here using these computers. I'd say they were primitive, by they aren't. What They had weren't computers. They had buttons and displays and processes, but they weren't computers. They were an image of computers, like something a child makes to play pretend with. But still, these work the same way, functionally if not mechanically. And they let me interact with the world without needing to meet it face to face."

        "One thing is certain, They are still here. They still take people. And they still take us back to that place when they can find us, so I learn what I can of them and I hide where they can't find me. And if I keep doing this, maybe, eventually, I might find a way to do more."

        ************************************************** **********

        When Meaghan returned in 2008, she took a long while to find a place for herself. She had peeked in on her Fetch, looking like a woman in her late 80s, but moving with surprising health and vigor. Her son was married and had his own family, even his children had mostly already married and moved on to form there own families. The ranch had been very successful and the little house Meaghan remembered had been replaced by something akin to a mansion. The family had interests in other businesses now, though she wasn't sure where all that money had come from. Jacob Reyes had died some years before, so she assumed she'd never find the answer to why he'd gained such great financial luck. The family had kept a military tradition as well, though given their wealth they were more often officers than grunt soldiers. Her own son had spent some time in Vietnam and one of her grand-daughters had become a career Air Force meterologist.

        Uncertain of what to do and unable to get her old life back, it no longer seemed to exist, she instead slunk into hiding and considered her captors. Returning to her maiden name and taking the nickname of "Free" among other changelings. Meaghan has spent a lot of time studying science as well as the history of alien abductions as well as faerie related disappearances. She prowls the internet message boards where conspiracy theorists gather and take in the stories, trying to separate dross from gold. She is convinced that her science fiction seeming captors had set up the scenario and the experiment with the purpose of trying to understand the concepts of science and the like. She thinks that such scientific knowledge could be tapped in the same way that the concept of the seasons were tapped. Invoking or making deals with some concept or entity alien to the Gentry and using that to further conceal the changelings from their old masters. Some part of her also wonders if such things could be made into a weapon as well.

        When possible, Free prefers to interact over chat-rooms and the like. Even with her Mask, she is aware that her voice is disturbing to other people and likes to avoid having that reminder that she is no longer human pushed into her face. She walks a fine line between purpose and obsession; solitude and isolation.

        She does have one past-time that is not directly related to her changeling nature or her research into the Others. Free has become a major science-fiction fan and even maintains a blog and forums titled "Touched by Vorlons" which discusses science-fiction and fantasy stories.

                    Hey guys.

                    I don't think Mitch fits too well - too much of a 'comic' vibe. I noticed we were a little focused on mental skills and capabilities and thought it would be fun to incorporate a more "brute" strength into the mix. Here is the background for my idea. Tell me what you think:

                    ***

                    "The scars on my chest are from dad, dear old. Took a knife to me and mom, but I managed to survive. I didn’t spend too much longer around Chicago before I joined up. I always liked working on cars so I got to work with the engine - got my degree and everything. Of course, I made some side money fighting in the ring. Never thought I’d be proud of something but my time boxing made me happy.

                    Of course, we got to float all over. I’ve been to Japan, Korea, India… all over Asia. It’s different there. Girls are the same though. Or at least their disease was. Took a bottle to the head in Malaysia, that’s what this bump on my head is.

                    I was fading, getting past my prime when the deal maker came. He didn’t look like the devil, didn’t act like him either. I’m certain there isn’t any devil other than myself though, just so we’re clear. I was shipping home, and I figured I could become a boxer and become the heavyweight champion. This guy says he’s a captain of a private boat, and needs a little help with the engine. He said he pay me in training – had some champions under his employ in the past, all I had to do was sign up for 2 years’ service. I took the deal, and he dragged me through those thorns, sharper than dad’s knife.

                    There was a bay, with a boat, and a captain, but there wasn’t much else. Captain said there was treasure in the boat, but I never saw any. He chained me to the boat, which was sunk under the water, and told me not to let anything in or out of the boat. At low tide, there was just enough length on the chain for me to put my nose above the surface and breathe, but after high tide I learned to hold my breath.

                    Sharks came. Squid came. Men came. Creatures came. I choked them. I smashed their skulls. I strangled them with my irons. My skin tore so much it healed back like scales. I swam so much these webs came up between my fingers. I held my breath so long, I don’t miss the oxygen that much. But those chains scarred my leg – you can still see the ring around my ankle.

                    He held his word though, the Captain. Brought me back and left me somewhere in the middle of the pacific. I guess he thought I would die, but I was picked up by some freighter, and landed in San Fran. I didn’t know where else to go so I hitched back to Chicago. Now the city pays me to work the bridges. I guess its fine work."

                    ***

                    Freddy has endured the life of the brutally abused, and has not been afraid to pay back in kind. He is not cruel, or evil, but in his own estimation, he is fair, maybe even level headed, regardless of what others say.

                    His time in the Navy helped him get him training in engine and mechanical work, skills he is still proud of. It also gave him a place to vent his physical prowess in the boxing ring, both sanctioned and sub rosa.

                    His durance under the Captain warped him into the monster of the deeps he is today. He is still drawn to the water, and likes to be near it, but knows that terrible things, even worse than him live down deep.

                    Upon his return to Chicago, he never really tried to reclaim his old life – there wasn’t much to claim anyway. He fell in with the winter court because it just made sense to him. There is sorrow in this life, and he has seen it and felt it.

                    His whole life he has held onto the memory of his mother, lost to him. He sees her in his dreams sometimes, and it was her memory that he thinks preserved his mind amidst all those bloody tides. He is not meditative, and he is not crazy, but in moments of stress and panic he finds himself retreating mentally to his memories with her. Afterwards, he drowns in his emotions of missing her, and the life he thinks was robbed of him – not by his keeper, but by his dad. Dad did some time in prison, but Freddy has no idea what became of him. He just assumes he is out there somewhere. If he meets him, he’ll drive a knife into his skull. But he doesn’t think that dream will ever come true.

                    ***
                    So, there are my two ideas. Mitch, the burnout public defender, and Freddy, the brutal bridge troll. Any thoughts? I'm leaning towards the bridge troll.

                            Inevitably, he comes across people who get a little too nosy about the world behind the veil, usually his mortal clients or mortals who are just nosy by nature. Some say he's looking for the hairy, mushy, shriveled-up mess that is his heart, and thinks it's beating in someone's chest. Others say he's looking for the perfect nightmare, and thinks you have to gnaw it out the brain of the one who experienced it. Still others say he's looking for himself, but since he died long ago, he never will, so he simply delivers the same fate he suffered out of revenge to fate itself. The police never manage to catch him because he can melt into the darkness. He is the darkness.
