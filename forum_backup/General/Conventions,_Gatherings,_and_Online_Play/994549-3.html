                                            > Name: Regret
                                            > Concept: Seeker of Identity
                                            > Lineage: Frankenstein
                                            > Refinement: Plumbum
                                            ~ Role: Pilgrim
                                            > Elpis: Sorrow
                                            > Torment: Merciless
                                            > Mental Attributes: Intelligence 2, Wits 2, Resolve 2
                                            > Physical Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 2, Stamina 3
                                            > Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 2, Composure 2
                                            > Mental Skills: Crafts 2, Investigation 1, Science 1
                                            > Physical Skills: Athletics 2, Brawl 2, Drive 1, Firearms (Rifles) 2, Larceny 1, Survival (Wilderness Training) 1, Weaponry 2
                                            > Social Skills: Empathy (Suffering) 2, Expression 1, Intimidation 2, Persuasion 1, Streetwise 1
                                            > Merits: Ambidextrous (3), Danger Sense (2), Residual Memory - Expression/Larceny/Science (3), Safe Place - The Far Place (2)
                                            > Bestowments: Titan's Strength
                                            > Transmutations
                                            ~ Disquietism: Externalize
                                            ~ Saturninus: Plumb the Fathoms
                                            > Health: 8 = 00000000
                                            > Willpower: 4 = ++++

                                            > Speed: 10
                                            > Initiative: 4
                                            > Defense: 4
                                            > Pilgrimage: 1
                                            > Azoth: 1
                                            > Pyros: 5/10
                                            > Aspirations
                                            ~ Meet the other PCs
                                            ~ Utilize the Safe Place Merit
                                            ~ Discover an Athanor
                                            > Conditions
                                            ~ Introspective (Refinement)
                                            ~ ?
                                            ~ ?
                                            > Exp: 0
                                            > Vitriol Exp: 0
                                            >Synopsis: Regret was created by a Promethean who always found their own body to be sub par. They were created out of what their Genitor thought was perfect specimens, and in the end they found their body too frail and unprepared for the harshness of the Pilgrimage. So, when they created Regret they made him out of what they thought were the perfect specimens: soldiers. Piece by piece they built their creation out of soldiers, thinking there was no finer material that could handle the rigors of the Pilgrimage. But they didn't understand the horrors of warfare, and so Regret is haunted by the stolen lives that comprise his body. But there is one blessing that came from his creation, Sorrow. The fragments of his being remember the depth of human suffering, even if just instinctively. This has allowed Regret to look upon his own actions through the lens of pointless folly and necessary sacrifice. When one action slides to far towards folly he knows he hasn't acted right, and this guides his morality as he slowly marches towards the New Dawn. His greatest hindrance is how easy it is for him and his body to kill. Violence is second nature, and it almost always after the fact that Regret's Elpis reminds him of why he seeks humanity, and why he shouldn't bring war to the human world.


                                            Pilgrimage Questions

                                            Q: What Sort of Human Do You Want to Be?
                                            A: A peacemaker, someone who can resolve conflict without violence. Someone who can stem human suffering and lead humans away from war.

                                            Q: How Have Humans Taught You To Fear and Hate?
                                            A: Humans are always so quick to point blade and gun at anything they believe dangerous. Human ignorance and pride are the mother an father of suffering. If only is wasn't so easy to understand why they fear for their lives.

                                            Q: How Did You Split With Your Creator?
                                            A: They didn't understand why I hated my body, they built it so they could protect me from their own fears but failed to realize I wasn't them. If only they knew what it's like to have every inch of their being crying out to go home...

                                            Q: What Keeps You on the Pilgrimage?
                                            A: They were never able to go home, so I will bring them all a second chance at life. At the human kindness they were denied at the hour of their deaths.

                                            Q: What Would You Give Up to Become Human?
                                            A: Conventional Morality, after all it is a lie. There is no good and evil, there is only you and the one who has the gun. Every other decision is purely based on personal ignorance and pride. We're all proof of that right?
