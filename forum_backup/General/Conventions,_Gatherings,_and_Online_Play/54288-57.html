                                                    (Yi)

                                                    Feiro sighs and pours himself a tall drink. As he sits onto a couch, the cup frosts over heavily in his grip. The perks of being an Air Dragon, I guess.

                                                    "About three hundred years ago, Hadad came to Novalesk. He was alone, tired and ragged, but Origenes noticed his presence. He and the present Chancellor waged an Epic battle with him, and bound him, body and spirit. They learned not only what he admitted to you today, but of why he had to flee Malfeas.

                                                    "Most of Hell's artificery is built around dissolving demons into Infernal Relics, but doing so with Second Circle souls is difficult not only for their power, but also for fear of offending their greater selves. Drujs are rare prizes indeed, and, as he claimed, the artisans of Hell were quick to snatch Hadad up. It was only a matter of time before the greatest of Hell's artisans learned that Cytherea had cast out one of her souls. Ligier wanted Hadad and his peers for himself, and dispatched a number of his apprentices to collect them. One of them was one of the Devil-Dragons. One of Hell's Exalted.

                                                    "The others fell to this force, but Hadad was a King, and his Domain stretched long a wide at it's height. Though only a shadow of its former glory, his armies felled many of the hunters, and Hadad swept the others away. In the end, Hadad faced the Exalt and in their battle, threw it across the face of Malfeas. And right into the Celestial Embassy."

                                                    Feiro sighs, "Needless to say, the Exalt did not survive. And so, for slaying his champion in such a spectacular and public fashion, Ligier added Hadad to the rolls of his accursed. His light would burn the druj just as it burned a mortal, and any who brought him to the Green Sun would be rewarded with the resulting wonder."

                                                    "By the time he ended up here, he had been fighting non-stop for nearly three years. While the Chancellor of the day proposed slaying him, Origenes was moved by his plight. Or didn't want a useful tool to slip from his fingers," Feiro shrugs, "Or both. He's like that. Casting his sorceries, Origenes released Hadad from his bindings, but cut away his natural progenitive powers."

                                                    "He lost everything. His throne, his rights. He was spiritually castrated and his very essence was altered. He's built something of a new life here, selling his sorcerous knowledge and power. But what he wants is a return to his former station. And that is something he's not likely to have so long as he remains as an enemy of both Heaven and Hell."
