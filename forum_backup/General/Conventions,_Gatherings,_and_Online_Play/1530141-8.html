                                                            Cauthon

                                                            (other players, do not read unless you want major plot spoilers!)



                                                            While deployed in a hospital in Kabul in 2012, Jack treated a platoon that was attacked by insurgents while guarding an archeological dig. The dig leader was an American archaeologist, Dr. Elsa Schlegal who was doing work on a bronze age site that incredibly seemed to contain artifacts from the neolithic period as well. The insurgents completely destroyed the site, and several of the archeology team and soldiers were killed, including the Lieutenant, Lance DuLac. Schlegal was trapped in an underground chamber for a brief period, but was rescued by one of the dig team, a young college intern named Lake Plath. Both Schlegal and Plath were from Danner College in Misery, TX.

                                                            Dr. Schlegal had minor injuries, but seemed to be deeply psychologically broken by something she witnessed while underground. She kept raving about a gigantic metallic spider with a human face giving birth to clockwork monsters, ancient water wheels and gears made of stone, and whispering the phrase, "What rises must fall. What has fallen may rise again,” over and over again and crying hysterically.

                                                            Lake, Schlegel’s intern, also seemed to have witnessed something strange, but she was more closed about what she saw and seemed to have a contemptuous attitude towards Jack, frequently referring to him as being asleep and telling him the whole world was a lie. Another anomaly was that she appeared to be completely different physically from what was described in her medical chart, as if she had metamorphosed into a different person. When Jack rechecked the chart later it seemed to have changed to reflect her current appearance. At the time he dismissed this as him just misremembering the chart, but after awakening he is not sure.

                                                            During his awakening, while being repeatedly drowned and then re-stitched into his body, tortured unendurably, his mind began a journey through Pandemonium, where he was visited by Lake, now appearing as a woman in her thirties and calling herself by the shadow name Deianeira. Kusha thinks that she helped him somehow and gave him a message, but he can’t remember what it was.
