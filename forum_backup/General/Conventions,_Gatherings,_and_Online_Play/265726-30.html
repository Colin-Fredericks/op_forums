    Here is my thought process:

    I know he wants to play a character decent at combat. I am unsure about what other aspects of playing a RP character he will enjoy, as he has never RP'ed before. In video games, he likes playing healing characters, but there really isn't a niche for that in Exlated. For his base character, I focused on combat abilities that I know he will use and dipped into some abilities and charms that sound cool (Presence, Athletics, Occult, Ride, etc) I added Sail because he seemed interested in an Air Ship (or Ice Ship).

    For his XP, I don't think he is going to spend them all (or any) on combat abilities. I am hoping that he will figure out what he likes to do over the course of a few games and spend that XP on that.

    I will also admit, I maxed out his Willpower with Build points. Mostly because if I regret not doing that for Mayu. If you are uncomfortable with that level of Min Maxing, I can take some points out of Willpower and put them into Skill points.

                                    Depends on the lunar honestly, it's tricky to use the bigger artifacts because they lack dedicated weapon/armor elsewhere charms but if it can be used by many pockets meditation the're solid (also tattoo artifacts are awesome). A good way for Brandon to flesh out his character personally is have him name the exact emotion that is tied to the intimacy. For instance, Bloodless has a negative intimacy of weariness with dragon-blooded, while a blanket negative intimacy would imply he highly dislikes or hates them. If we ever convert to 3rd edition this will make it a bit easier as intimacies will be really important instead of just a minor thing. This is kind of why it takes me all day to make a character, I'll sit and ponder multiple choices for all of this stuff.
