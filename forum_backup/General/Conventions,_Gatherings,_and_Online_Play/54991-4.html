                                                            Thank you, also. Glad you liked what I did there. ;-) You know, the language thing always had me curious. In the First Age, why didn't they just call it "Realm"? Why was it "Old Realm"? Anyway, the decision is yours of course, Dietaku, but if you're looking for input, I would suggest that, after 4000 years of living in an environment totally alien to their home, some linguistic drift is in order. At the very least, I suggest two languages- Old Realm, still spoken by spirits and very old Exalts, as in the core setting, with the more commonly spoken Autochthonic (or whatever you want to call it) as a vernacular. Just my opinion, though. The Solar Deliberative certainly could very easily keep everyone speaking Old Realm if you want to keep it straightforward.

                                                            Now I have to go to work shortly, so unfortunately I don't have time right this minute to get into my character sheet; I plan to have it up and posted tonight, after I get home. However, since you do approve my concept, I'll take a little time now to fill in some more details about what I'm thinking, and to ask a few questions. I still haven't settled on a gender yet, btw, but I get tired of writing (s)he and his/her, so I'm just going to use female pronouns for now. These are mostly just disjointed thoughts; I'll obviously write out a more coherent backstory to go with the character sheet tonight.

                                                            Righteous Common Enterprise was exalted in Thutot, the capital of Gulak. Enterprise's purpose when she was created was to help Gulak deal with its overpopulation problems, both through architecture/urban planning and social engineering. As such, she is accomplished at both subjects and, while she did much good in Gulak, was ideal for the mission to help establish the first Realm facility in the Spiral; thus, she volunteered. She trusts the Solar Deliberative and their leadership, but worries that they lose touch of what she considers the true goal of the Realm, which is a better, safer life for all where everyone can flourish according to their merits. Exalted. Human. Spirits. Even Autochthon, once the Titans have been defeated and the need for the "Gunstar" has passed. With the (triumphant?) return to the Spiral to (successfully?) prosecute and conclude the war looming, Enterprise feels that it is the role of the Champions to begin preparing for what comes after the war. While the mission is to establish a military base in the Spiral, Enterprise's plans revolve more around creating the nucleus for a colony. It will fulfill its immediate primary function as a foothold for the invasion, of course, but it will also be the seed of infrastructure from which a true home can grow on this world. She plans to do everything in her power to ensure that humans are involved in the construction and operation of the facility from day one as an integral part, particularly humans from Gulak (which should also help with their overpopulation problem). Righteous Common Enterprise is quite religious, seeing herself as a devout (if free spirited) follower of Autochthon and his (remaining) divine ministers, as much a priestess as an engineer. She feels particularly close to Noi, and feels that many of the citizens of the Realm, human and exalted, focus so much on the war effort that the lose the simple joys that come from curiosity, innovation and even companionship; she works hard to ensure that the people she works with are both happy and connected with one another, and is a strong proponent of the polyamorous relationships that are common in Gulak. This is the first time her soul has been chosen to be an Alchemical Exalt; her past lives' heroism was mostly focused on bringing people together and helping to find innovative and new ways for them to cooperate, even when it went against the wishes of the established leadership. One of those past lives was as a Joybringer, and she feels a certain affinity towards the Maiden of Serenity as a result (though it is nothing like her devotion to Autochthon and Noi).

                                                            How does all that sound?

                                                            Now, a few questions. First, is our mission to simply find a site for the base, or to actually get it started while we're waiting for the fleet and Battlestar Autochthonia to arrive? Will there be a substantial number of humans among our crew? Or any humans, for that matter? Both for the mission and as initial personnel for the base?

                                                            I was considering a familiar, a minor construct or maybe machine spirit, possibly sent to Righteous Common Enterprise by Noi to help and guide her. Maybe something mongoose or ferret like in appearance and function, though I haven't decided for certain yet. Or maybe something monkey-like. Whatever the form, would that be acceptable?

                                                            A Warstrider or Voidfighter would definitely not be appropriate for Righteous Common Enterprise, but I am thinking about something that would allow her freedom of movement in the void, particularly during space battles, so she can make repairs to the ship or to voidfighters etc from the outside if necessary. Maybe some kind of power armor or suit, or maybe a small pod or shuttle or other type of repair ship or something, or maybe just a combination of a few minor artifacts and alchemical charms to enable her to get around. Would you be willing to work with me on this? Do you have any ideas of what would work best?

                                                            Anyway, that's what I have so far. I'll check back in tonight after work.
