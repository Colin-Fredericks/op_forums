Also, the fact that Zekka, the Alchemical Patropolis, despises being overcrowded, and therefore, the Realm military couldn't place the estimated fifteen thousand workers on this mission.

    As for Languages? Well....You guys forced my hand a little.

    Cultural differences between the Spiral and the Gunstar ought to produce significant differences in syntax, as you say, Jim. Therefore, as your point, I will give you this map. You'll need it to understand the next bits.


    As per regular Exalted, I divided the Spiral into five regions. The "arms" are both large twisting nexuses of Essence, holding the Elemental Poles in place, along with large clumps of planets. In the center if the "Elemental Pole of Earth" holding aloft the Viridian Plaza, the seat of Theion's government, the Congress of Daevas, and the center of the Titanic Exalted's operations. There, Ligier, and Rathia, the Crystal Moon (Other Fetich of Theion) both perch in the Sky-Vault over the Spiral. The point being, each arm has its own dialect of Realm, analogous to the directional dialects, so as to allow the Titans to focus more time on themselves, and to a lesser extent, the war against the Exalted. Realm, though, is the language of choice for Daevas and Infernals, thanks in part to Theion being a stickler for it, and Ta'akozoka's Guild using it as their trade language.

                    Well, that's a bit of a question, but here goes.

                    Zekka is outfitted with a Vat Complex, two Factory Cathedrals, and the staff necesary to maintain them all. If he desired, he could produce new Alchemicals, should he possess the required materials, as the required technicians are among his crew as service and maintenance staff, such is the import of this mission.

                    Zekka himself? Well, there's a reason he's called the Radical Mendicant. When he was younger, he strode among the stars, a boon companion of the famous Sun Dragon, that Dawn Caste who cast down the Fog of Ten Thousand Fangs into the lower reaches of the Spiral. He battled Human and Daeva alike, a firebrand who saw the stratified society of both the Spiral and to a lesser extent, the Gunstar stifling. The Gunstar chiefs of staff couldn't do anything about the Starmetal Alchemical; he fought too hard to bench, and they were afraid to do so anyways; he may very well destroy valuable resources out of boredom. Thus, when he ascended to Patropoli-hood, his city was made into a star-faring battleship not only to sate his wanderlust, but also to keep him away from the Gusntar for as long as possible. In his human form, he stands a good 6 feet tall, usually dressed in leather, his distinctive dreaded cable hair a unique sight whenever he deigns to walk around.

                    Zekka is also armed to the teeth, with five implosion bow batteries, four essence cannons, and seven lightning casters. He also possesses the unique weapon set of two gigantic arms sprouting beneath his superstructure. Ever a martial artist, Zekka demanded their addition, so "He can once again smash his enemies with his own fists."

                    Mercury's Delight is a Mela-class fast cruiser, a ship designed for fast and long cruises, and possesses only a fraction of Zekka's firepower and resilience. She can bring to bear three essence cannons, an implosion bow, and one anti-air essence net.

                    Her captain, Kana Kleiner, is a Full Moon Lunar, She is a Void Serpent (Those forty foot feathered snakes who glide through the Void with ease) Totem naval commander, born and bred. However, she's also an eccentric; preferring to wander her ship as tiny forms of whatever form she feels at the time. She also keeps a small zoo along with her for her own amusement. These quirks haven't earned her any favors, but her talents at naval fighting cannot be denied.

                    There are several Sworn Brotherhoods under the Directional Animal Genses, those famous martial families organized by the Sidereal Elder, Peerless Ocelot, for the express purpose of creating strong martial traditions to further the war effort. Also, amongst the Terrestrial host is Sato Hoshi, a mild-mannered man who is related to the great Savant, Ledaal. While his talents are nowhere near his ancestor's, his gift for langauge is great, and a valued asset on this mission.

                    Hopefully, that answers your question.
