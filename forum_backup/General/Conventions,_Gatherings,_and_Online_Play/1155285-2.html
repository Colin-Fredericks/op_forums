    Khalil didn't take his eyes off the ritual. He felt no jealousy that he was not included in it, but he did find it fascinating to watch. Particularly the flames. He whispered his response to Cassidy without removing his attention from the rite. "Escalation," he said. "Further tragedies along the same theme. Greater secrets shall be learned, and the fear for the characters' safety intensifies. This shall get worse before it gets better. I pray this show has only two acts."

    My sweet Miriam... he thought. Are you threatened by this demon? Should I run to you? Would doing so keep you safe? Or would it merely lead the beast to your door? Khalil did not voice his fears aloud, resisting the urge to seek out his precious Miriam. If he was going to keep her safe, he knew his best chance was to catch their foe quickly.

                            11-07-2017, 03:23 PM
                            Cassidy set in front of the desk like a giant spider, weaving patterns as if they were strings. Next to here, Steph recalled everything she knew and thought was important about Gabby, her habits and the streets of Arkahm. On the other side of the table, Micheal was investigating holy texts in search for the behavior of the infernal Host, and Khalil searched for any bit of information he could about the mysterious King in Yellow. In front of her, there was a map of Arkham, together with books about its history, past crimes and supernatural incidents, as well as texts which speak about previous manifestations of the King, or demons similar or related to it.

                            From that vast wealth of information, she tried to find connections, crack patterns, discover the truth which was hidden beneath the surface- and all of those texts were centered around a single picture. The sketch of her vision. She searched the infernal image for any signs she may have found about the possible next move of Gabby and the demon which may have possessed her. While that mission may have been difficult, she had a special.. talent to deal with such case. Hell knew the souls of the sinners, and it has taught her how they may think and act. There was a price to pay, of course- no one can walk the mind of a madman and leave unscratched- but she had to stop the next murder. Innocent lives were at stake. Her sister's lives were at stake.

                            She looked on a blank page of her sketchbook, her pencil in her hand, and then said "If I were to write this play- what tragedy would I write?"

                            And with those words, she started to draw.

                            OOC
                            Cassidy is shedding the Informed Condition about Gabby in order to find out her murder pattern, and where should is likely to strike next. That means that a failure would turn into success, and success into an exceptional success. She also activate the Mind of a Madman, which grants her the 8 again- it may not be necessary, but using a dream to save lives is one of her Aspirations, and it would make sense she would use the ability in such time, and would make her a lot more likely to break. She would also utilize Steph's help, but as Micheal got more successes it should be better he would devout them to Khalil's efforts.

                            As it she is trying to crack the pattern, I roll for Wits+Investigation+1 from Steph. That gives her a dicepool of 6. failure, but thanks to the Informed, it is considered a 1 Success.
