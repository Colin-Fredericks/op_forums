                                            Let's expand a bit on the options :
                                            -We end the game right after we get out of Arcadia : it would mean that our game will end soon, since we're slowly building toward our climax (Derek figuring out the pact, everyone having forged contracts, Nate facing the Keeper...).
                                            -We could also add an epilogue : once they're in the real world, maybe they go back and see their fetches. They could be properly introduced to the Changeling society, and we leave it open ended, now that they have to compose with their new lives.

                                            It could be cool : for all the campaigns I've played, only one had a proper ending, the others all died out. Could be nice to properly end this story, with maybe a "Where are they now" for each of us.

                                            Of course, we could also continue. But then, we're faced with two other options :
                                            -Creating character sheets and stuff and converting this game to a proper Changeling game with the system. Could make it easier on the storyteller, now that he can't use Arcadia as an excuse for "POOF! A fae did it!". It could slow down the game, however.
                                            -Keeping the freeform narration. We'll need to define a proper city, then. More work!

                                            If it continues, I'm down for it, of course. But it may be a lot of work on your part, Thorbes.

                                            As for me, well. I'll be happy with whatever you decide. I like the Durance because, well, DRAMA, but it'll have to end sooner or later.
