    As Bardo reaches out through the Abyss to his Watchtower, the figure in the painting looks almost...eager. Yet the spell flows smoothly into this reality, and this eagerness becomes an expression of disappointment.

    The bodies of the three men convulsed with painful spasms, grunts and cries of pain escaping their throats. Andrei took a quick, instinctive step back, clearly confused and looking around for any water or something that might explain this localized electrical burst.

    The Head Man, the Indian fellow who had gone for the briefcase, threw himself onto the table, protecting himself by lying on the non-conductive wood. Still reeling from pain, he made a clumsy swing at Astoria's head, which whizzed through the air. Part of it was simply intended to get her away from the case, but the part of him that was still hurting was eager to return the favor. One of his companions, in desperation, mimicked this action, and was able to step up onto a nearby table. Face contorted with pain and anger, this 2nd Man sent his metal baton down across Bardo's shoulder. The dense air surrounding the Arcane Doctor absorbed much of the blow, but enough got through to be painful. The 3rd Man simply fell right there on the floor, electricity continuing to cause his body to jerk and contort.

The constant professional, she fired off a series of well rehearsed questions at the Necromancer as she began to examine them. Meanwhile Meristo was standing before the painting, gazing into it as if looking at the infinity of the night sky. A distant, thoughtful expression wore on his aging face, and he held his glasses in his hand, biting down on the tip of one of the temples. He seemed completely oblivious, or at least completely unconcerned about the activity of the medical personnel. With a blink and a start, he came out of his dream like considerations and turned around, seeming genuinely surprised to see the three unconscious men being tended to. He put his hands on Bardo's and Phoenix's shoulders, pulling them closer together, "Remind me never to steal a piece of paper from the two of you, yeah?"
