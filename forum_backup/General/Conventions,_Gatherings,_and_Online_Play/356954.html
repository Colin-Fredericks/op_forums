    VtM Camarilla/Anarch game on Skype

    01-01-2015, 06:13 PM
    This game will likely start in the early spring (March/Early April), unless there are lots of volunteers right out of the gate. I'm running a small Sabbat chronicle that will sometimes act in tandem or in reaction to the events of this Game. It's a long-term, weekly, city game.

    The Borderlands
    Mood: Social Unrest
    Themes: Excess, Rebellion. Mystery and Intrigue
    Albuquerque, New Mexico
    Things in the city are changing. The Sabbat lost territory on the New Mexican border sixty years ago and started to get desperate, spawning masses of their half-crazed shock troops to hurl at the Camarilla strongholds of the state. Albuquerque, having been Anarch territory since the end of the Civil War, was ill-prepared for a siege and nearly fell to the so-called "Sword of Caine".
    Before the city was lost, however, a group of Justicars were sent with a regiment of Archons and their Servirs to wrest control of the city from the Sabbat. Many on both sides met Final Death, and in the aftermath, the city was left with dozens of sireless fledglings and disillusioned Sabbat. These vampires, after much friction and misunderstanding, were vouched for by the Anarchs and grudgingly accepted by the Prince, Santiago, who was the only Justicar to survive the bloodshed.
    You're a Neonate in the city, possibly a childe of one of the Sabbat defectors. Your Coterie is looked at poorly due to your Sabbat or Anarch ties, but the Prince has taken an interest in your group. You've been tasked by the Prince through his Sheriff to investigate rumours of strange activity in the desert on the edge of town that might be Sabbat related. Kindred are being killed, their decomposing and ashen remains found in the desert sands.

    What's going on in the Duke City?
    *******************************************
    *******************************************
    Camarilla standard Clans +
    bloodlines available: Modern Cappadocians*, City Gangrel, Auspex Ventrue, Lasombra Antitribu, Tlacique, Tzimisce
    * http://wiki.stryck.com/WoD/Cappadocians

    Houserules :
    10-again is added to My cWoD rolling system in conjunction with the "Rule of 1"
    1s negate Successes, but not the re-rolls garnered by 10s.
    Botching is still possible, but just easier to avoid.....usually.

    House Disciplines written and designed by Telgar:
    http://wiki.stryck.com/WoD/Disciplines

    RITUALS! They cost EXP now and no Blood Magic Path hands them out for FREE. NO EXP - NO RITUAL. Don't worry, it's only Current Levelx5, or x7 if it's a foreign or rare ritual. Same as a Discipline power.

    The Giovanni killing off the Cappadocians, The Avatar Storm, the 6th Great Maelstrom, the Week of Nightmares. the Gangrel leaving the Camarilla and any Revised "Gehenna is nigh!" supernatural signs, never happened. It's not 2nd ed. style, I'm just trimming the fat off that metaplot.


    All other changes will be discussed openly and every player's input taken into consideration.
