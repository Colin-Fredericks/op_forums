    Looking for a mage game NOT sandbox play by post

    08-05-2014, 05:38 PM
    I'm currently looking to join an online game of Mage, though I'm fine with both Awakening and Ascension. I'm a pretty experienced role-player but my local gaming group just disintegrated as people moved away. I'm pretty flexible with medium: skype, maptools, chat, etc. The one thing I absolutely want to avoid though is a rpol style play-by-post sandbox game where things grind forward at a post a day. I'd much prefer a group of limited size, with a specific meeting time where you don't have to literally wait hours for someone to reply in a conversation.





    --------------------------------------------------------------
    Here's an example character I once played
    --------------------------------------------------------------

    Mundane Life: Retired military with grand children and a good-for-nothing son-in-law.
    Mark joined the ROTC to pay for college, and then enlisted in the military as an Air Force engineer. Early in his twenty five years of service he got married to the love of his life, Lydia. It had been a long and slow engagement, spanning multiple years, but soon after their marriage, they had a daughter on the way. Lydia lost the ability to bear children after giving birth to their daughter, Lucillia. Lydia died while Lucillia was just beginning her first year of college, leading Mark to finally retire from the military and also causing a great deal of turbulence in Lucillia’s schooling. Soon afterwards, Lucillia dropped out of college and announced that she was engaged. Mark was not impressed. He became even less impressed when he met her intended. Despite his misgivings, she married Luke and gave birth to twins 6 months later. The course of the last two years have proven his suspicions about Luke correct, with his daughter occasionally stopping by with little Tommy and Billy. That wouldn’t say anything about Luke were it not for the fact that the purpose of the visits primarily seems to be guilting him into “lending” her some money to supplement her minimum wage job. Mark knew he should have put his foot down about her marrying that worthless alcoholic bum who can’t hold onto a job to save his life. She lives an hour or two away in the next city over and he considers himself lucky that she hasn’t ever tried to recruit him as a semi-permanent baby sitter.

    Awakening: His Awakening happened a week after his wife’s funeral, as the numbness and shock had finally begun wearing off. The trigger, as near as he can tell, was asking himself “What will I do now?” As if called by the question, a bolt of lightning struck a nearby tree. Emerging from the resulting blaze was his wife. She was gentle moonlight born aloft on wings of fire and though she spoke, he could understand her words through their great reverberating roar. She reached out, grasped his hand, and flew him across an ocean of lightning to a distant lighthouse perched upon a craggy shore of fire. Once they had arrived at its peak, he instinctively knew what to do. He spoke his name and the words hung in the air for a single breathless second before crashing against the tower walls and scouring their form onto the blank golden nameplate that hung there-on. Soon afterwards, he took up the Shadow Name “Abdiel” which means “Servant of God”

    Legacy Induction: He’s older than his mentor in the Celestial Masters Legacy (Key to the Supernal Tarot, pg 39), Grey Raven. A lot. Abdiel Awakened when he was well into his forties and has since reached his fifties. Though he truly wished to be part of the Legacy, there was a great deal of resentment about supplicating himself before some Silver Ladder punk less than half his age. It simply ran counter to his internal sense of the natural hierarchy of things. It certainly didn’t help that the Moros in question didn’t even acknowledge the age difference, focusing instead only on the difference in magical experience. She did notice the resentment however and took measures to … deal with it. After being trounc… er … unpleasantly introduced to just how much raw mystical might resided within her small frame, both Abdiel’s resentment and his ability to conceal that anger grew. He evidently managed to satisfy her concerns though, as a year ago he returned from Sangres after learning the First Attainment. He has become distressed at the realization that his teacher might require him to continue proving himself to her for years more before he can learn the Second Attainment, much less the Third. Beyond his induction to the Celestial Masters, Abdiel has been rather quiet and withdrawn from the mage society, especially for a member of the Free Council. Recently though, he has begun leaving his shell little by little.


    Present Day: Mark lives in a trailer park ten minutes from town, and is able to afford devoting the majority of his time to Awakened matters through a combination of frugality, military retirement pay, and a few wise investments when he was younger. Pretty much the only luxury he indulges in is the occasional hunting trip to keep his skills with a rifle from degrading entirely. He doesn’t even own a television, though that is mostly because his old one broke and he can tune in directly with Forces. He plays up the image of an eccentric new age religionist to hide the majority of his activities in plain sight. When he needs privacy, he closes the blinds and hangs up a “do not disturb” sign he swiped from a hotel.


    3)Concept

    Power Hungry Hypocrite

    The phrase that come to mind with regards to the character is "arrogant in his humility". Despite that, I tried to give him the versimilitude of a multifaceted personality.

    4)Goals.

    Goals:

    Recently, Abdiel stumbled across some interesting news that might let him bypass the entire humiliating procedure. He learned that demons can grant gnosis (ala Inferno) enough for him to simply recreate the legacy. The tale was certainly vague enough to possibly be false though, and he has little clue how to go about summoning one. (aka feel free to rule that he either can’t figure out how to summon a demon, or it can’t grant gnosis or it can but only to a certain extent, regardless he’ll keep LOOKING and attempting strange rituals, which you can use as a plot hook. Though he probably won’t hurt people to enact the rituals unless he’s really sure the rituals work.) Really though, this search for demonic power is just a symptom of his true goal, which is to rise to a point that he views as befitting of someone of his age and background. All for the cause of helping the Sleepers of course.

    Obviously though, arcane secrets of any sort pique his interest just as much as any other mage. Furthermore, he has reached a point where he judges that he can form a Cabal around himself, or at least join a pre-existing one on even footing instead of as a junior member. Another of his goals is to convince his daughter to divorce that no-good husband of hers, though as of yet he wants to actual convince her, not trick her. His research goal, as has already been mentioned, is the conversion of energy into matter without magic. While it is not a goal, per se, the fact that he has family in the area opens up a plethora of possible plot hooks.

    Abdiel is of the opinion that if the Spire Perilous were to be mended back to wholeness, then in combination with the existence of the Watchtowers, it would cause everyone to Awaken. He knows that he will likely never be able to accomplish this though, so he regards it much as he regards the conversion of flesh to beings of light within the Fallen Realm. That is to say, it is less a goal and more a hope.

    5)Extra Stuff

    Praxis: His typical method of spellcrafting involves mentally spinning out elaborate metaphors of what he wants to happen as a sort of code. Then he imbeds information in whatever carrier signal he feels will carry it with the least Abyssal static/noise to the Aether. The Aether then alters the fundamental underlying fabric of the universe to carry out the request. Paradox occurs when the noise of the Abyss corrupts the information content of the message. Those known as Sleepers not only fail to meaningfully penetrate the static of the Abyss, but also increase the total interference with their constant uncontrolled signaling. Sleepwalkers reign in and control their signaling but fail to penetrate nonetheless, due to not being able to project the signal with enough power.

    Areas of Study: Thaumatalogical Mineralogy and High Energy Occultism. He knows that Sleepers have already attained a few very rough and explosive methods for converting matter to energy, but has not heard of them achieving the reverse. Thus he is currently focusing on achieving that. His primary area of research right now is attempting to create an “infusion” of a massive amount of energy in one form or another, and then imbuing it into various crystals and cheap semi-precious stones. It is his hope that having enough Forces energy to form a non-negligible amount of matter (according to E=mc^2), stored within a crystal will catalyze the energy to take on a crystal resonance and crystallize itself into a solid form. As of yet, it has failed, but he will continue to attempt various types of Forces and various materials for the crystal.

    Symbolism: Abdiel places a lot of stock in symbolism and metaphor, even more than the average mage. The most obvious manifestation of this is his heavily tattooed body, which he uses to display his personal understanding of the universe.

    The palm of his right hand has the Atlantean rune for the Path of the Mighty. Each of his fingers represents one of the types of Path Tools, with five Obrimos appropriate variants of each tool proceeding down from the tip to the symbol. The location was based off finding deeper meaning lying behind the phrase "right hand of god". Furthermore, Abdiel is Obrimos and he is right handed. His head, being the seat of the mind and also having unique internal spaces, bears the rune for the Path of Scourging upon its crown. Again, five sets of five tools radiate out from it. The heart, which is a great symbol of Life, lies closest to the left arm, and thus his left hand bears the rune of the Path of Ecstasy and the 5 times 5 array of Path Tools. The sole of his left foot is similarly dedicated to the Path of Thistle based upon the fact that a lucky rabbit's foot must come from the rear, left foot to be valid. Both by process of elimination and the phrase "one foot in the grave" he concluded that his right foot should be the appendage dedicated to the Path of Doom.

    Along the back of his knees, head and neck, and the insides of his arm, depictions of the appropriate Watchtowers stretch from the runes to his back. The peaks of the Watchtowers form the tips of a pentacle stylized as a rose and centered opposite his navel. It represents the Rose of the World, the upper half of the World Spire, now shattered by Awakened Hubris and become Spire Perilous. Another name for it is the Omphalos, the Navel of the World, which is why a small star around his navel is dedicated to the base of the broken spire. The actual point where his navel twists shut in a knot as it plunges into the darkness of the interior body represents the breakage point where the Abyss begins. Centered on the Omphalos and spread out across the majority of his stomach is a circular depiction of the night sky, as viewed from Sangres on the day he was initiated into the First Attainment of the Celestial Master Legacy. Along with the obvious personal significance of placing the route to the Supernal at that point in time and space, the star map represents the fact that the Fallen World is made of stardust and starlight. When he reaches the Second Attainment it will be recorded on his right pectoral and the third will be recorded upon his left pectoral.

    He wishes that he could proudly display his map of all the cosmos to the world at large, but alas, it is not yet time and the Arts Supernal must remain hidden Mysteries (especially if he doesn’t want trouble with the Guardians of the Veil or the police). Thus he apes the Japanese criminals known as Yakuza in that his tattoos are mostly concealable despite covering the majority of his body. Only the tiniest tantalizing glimpses of his tattoos are visible to Sleeping eyes behind the concealment of his clothing, much like the Mysteries and the concealment of the Veil.

    Magical Tools: His five chosen magical tools also hold strong symbolism: His Path tool takes the form of a Disc. The interior of his wedding ring is etched with symbols of the 5 Realms Supernal and represents the fact that he is wedded to the Supernal. It also shows that he views his wife as symbolic of the Supernal and her death as representative of the Fall. Thus reuniting with the Realms Supernal would be reuniting with his dead wife. The ring is, of course, worn on his right hand, dedicated to the Path of the Mighty, and his ring finger is the one dedicated to Obrimos pentacles. His Arcana Tool for Forces is lighter, which bears obvious symbolic significance as a metaphor for enlightenment, but also has the ability to ignite the same enlightenment in others. Or cause horrible destruction if misused, but that is just par for the course. For Matter he uses a lead coin melded into the base of his left shoe. When he wears a different pair of shoes he either has to go without the magical tool or take a few minutes to cut the shoes open, transfer the coin, and then magically mend them. It's irritating but he wanted proper symbolic placement without risking lead poisoning from having lead in direct contact with his foot all day, every day. Plus he felt that the monetary value of a coin made it the best choice to represent Matter. For the Arcanum of Space, he was disappointed that he was unable to come up with a workable tool located on his head for proper symbolism, but the very nature of the Space Arcanum mollifies the problem somewhat. For that Arcanum, he uses a small map of the city. His tool for Prime, which ties into and underlies all other forms of magic, is clasped directly onto his Path tool in the form of the diamond.

    Nimbus: The universe leans in to listen to him.

    6) Of Note
    For all Grey Raven is Abdiels teacher, she is not a Mentor. His pride and resentment would never let him ask her for help outside learning the Legacy, and she’d never give it if asked (she’s very much a “sink or swim” style teacher). If it somehow becomes relevant in some way though, keep in mind that a teacher of a Legacy has to have already mastered at least the second attainment. As such, Grey Raven has, at minimum, Gnosis 5, Forces 3, Matter 3, Space 1, and 1 dot in a different Arcana
