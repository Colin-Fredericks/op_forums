                                                    Here is the backstory for Shining Topaz, for your perusal, I am still working on the character sheet.

                                                    Shining Topaz was born the second in line to the throne of the petty kingdom Terek on the coast of the Dreaming Sea. For as long as she can remember she has loved swords: so shiny, so sharp, so beautiful. Shortly after she had learned how to walk she had toddled into the training grounds and was watching carefully how to set her stance, and hold a sword.

                                                    Her life has been dedicated to learning the art of the sword, by the time she was 7 she was a match in skill, if not in physicality, to all but the best swordsmen in her father's employ. Yet she was a princess, and the spare besides, so she has received training in administration, and has undergone a thorough political education that has left her with strong feelings about the nature of legitimacy: that it cannot derive from mere force. Even so, when she was told she needed to learn some form of artistic performance as befit her status as a noble she chose to learn sword dancing rather than an instrument.

                                                    Despite these detours, Shining Topaz has focused most of her life on swordfighting. At 15 she was more skilled than any other mortal in the art of the blade, and she branched out to learn the formal martial arts styles that revolved around the sword, she has mastered the mundane parts of the Single Point Shining Into the Void and Steel Devil Styles.

                                                    To celebrate Shining Topaz's 18th birthday, her father held a grand swordfighting tournament that drew fighters from across the land. Shining Topaz entered herself, but the two dragonblooded warriors from Prasad, a father and a son, were the favorites to win the tournament. It was a great upset when, in the semifinals, Shining Topaz managed to defeat the younger Terrestrial Exalt. Yet while the son was newly exalted only a year ago, the Father had been exalted for a century and would not go down so easily.

                                                    At first the fight seemed to go Topaz's way, but then the Exalt smiled and complimented her skill and started spending Essence. On the back foot, it was apparent that the Princess was losing. Then when the Exalt went in for the final blow the impossible happened, Shining Topaz flared with golden light and turned aside the Terrestrial Exalt's blade. Empowered by the Sun, Shining Topaz defeated the Terrestrial and declared that she would become the Invincible Sword Princess.

                                                    However, she had become Solar Anathema right in front of everybody, and the confrontation with her father soon fallowed. Fortunately for her, her earnest enthusiasm for swords, disgust for bureaucracy, and commitment to the proper line of succession convinced her father that she was still herself and not a demon in the skin of his daughter.

                                                    Unfortunately this sealed the fate of the kingdom. Although a fighter without peer, shining Topaz was but one woman, and could not stop Prasad from destroying her home. Fleeing to the Dreaming Sea, she was forced to take up piracy to survive and avoid the Wyld Hunts that still dog her heels.

                                                    Should the chance arise, she would love to punish Prasad and restore her elder brother to his rightful throne. Unfortunately she doesn't even know if her brother remains alive.
