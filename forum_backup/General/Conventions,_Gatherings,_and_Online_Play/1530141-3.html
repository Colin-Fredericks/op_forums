                Great! So Trish has Composure 3 + Empathy 2 + Striking Looks 2
                8, 7,7,5,5,2,2

                So Bridgette has one more success than Trish. So I am going to say that she is Leveraged. She will comply with any immediate request and for each hour you are in the archives you can attempt to open one of her doors. Once all three of her doors are opened, she will assist you in your research by adding her dice pool to your own.

                Because you have already used intimidation, you must use some other skill to open doors. Sensing motivations, goals, and character asperations are all ways of opening doors. Or just doing something helpful for the character. As a reminder, you do have 2 dots in Mind and 1 dot in Fate, so magical means are also available to you.

                Trish has the Sympathetic merit (you feel sorry for her), so because you have leveraged her with Intimidation, she reduces your doors by two should she need to use social maneuvering with you at some point in the future.

                I'd say go to town. I'm a "Yes, And" kind of ST.

                To help you visualize the situation - based on her appearance and last name, Bridgette might be able to guess that Trish is Japanese. It is unusual for her to have an English first name. She is tall, about 5'8" (173 CM) and weighs about 110 lb. (50 kg). She has Striking Looks 2, so interpret what that means for your character, but most people would perceive her as very beautiful and charismatic. She is 21, soon to be 22 years old, and is dressed in fashionable business professional clothes (maybe to appear older). She is dressed warmly, and has not removed her outer coat even though she is indoors, maybe indicating that she is cold. Bridgette notices that she is wearing a pendant of an arrow inside a round target.

                She speaks English fluently, in an accent that seems halfway between American and British (a "transatlantic" accent") that is reminiscent of an old Hollywood actress (think Audrey Hepburn or Grace Kelly). She speaks somewhat slowly, as if she is consciously trying to "get it right" (Bridgett should appreciate that since her virtue is Precision). Her vocabulary indicates that she has a fair amount of education, but she sometimes stumbles over words that sound differently being read than being pronounced out loud.

                Have fun!
