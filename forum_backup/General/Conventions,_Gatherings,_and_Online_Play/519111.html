                              Shine Talon is a young lupus Silver Fang. He is descended from an unremarkable hero who bred among wolves when the Fangs first came in the territory; those packs have since been reclaimed as Red Talons kinfolk, though his white pelt mark him as unmistakably Fang even if many wolven generations removed from his lineage.
                              The Homid-centric Fangs of this territory don't really know what to make of the young Ahroun. He doesn't show any interest in Sept politics or in dominating other "lesser" tribes. Any important heirloom or title his lineage could give him rights to have since long been claimed by other more prominent Fangs. On his part Shine Talon feels a little out of place in the tribe: they told him he is supposed to lead, but he can't understand who's supposed to follow and why.
                              He has contacts among the Red Talons; he does small favors for them, like lending another set of fangs when the Wyrm intrudes in the wilderness or going into the cities for them when they would need to but would not be able. In return they sometimes reward him with small talens useful for a wolf, of which any other tribe is severely lacking. He has a distaste for their hatred and casual slaughter of humans, though he figures he doens't have to see eye to eye with them for them to work together.

                              Feel free to ask more if you are interested, I won't take it as an invitation unless you explicitly tell me there's a spot.

                                          Originally posted by Al-Haleem View Post
                                          How would he feel about joining a pack? From what you've written, he seems like a beta wolf, who's more used to taking orders than giving them. Will he be comfortable in a leadership position?
                                          This would be kinda complicated for him. First, he's not so much a beta wolf as that he feels that dominance nad leadership beyond the pack level is a bit of a nonsense. Being a wolf he still recognizes the need for people who give orders and people who follow in an unit as small as a pack. And being an Ahroun when the going gets rough he'll probably take point, leading or not.
                                          However: the last time he challenged anyone for leadership was his brother in his native wolf pack. That's when he First Changed, which resulted in his brother being slaughtered and his pack scattered to the four winds. The Talons tell him that those wolves are so traumatized that every one of them has been living, ot dying, as a lone wolf. Despite the reassurances of the Garou this has marked him deeply. He could either make that guilt and the fear it could happen again his defining point or grow beyond that during the story, I don't really know.

                                          Originally posted by Al-Haleem View Post
                                          Also note, Bashir and akashicmonk want to play Ahrouns too, so there may be a bit of personality clashing there.
                                          That would be perfect from the start. If anyone of them really pushed for pack leadership Shine Talon would probably let them decide and accept a position as second in command. He would probably not want to fall behind beta position though.

                                          Originally posted by Al-Haleem View Post
                                          What would have drawn Shine Talon to San Francisco? All the characters will start off at Mistwatch Sept, which is about as urban a caern as you get. Is Shine Talon afraid? Or excited? Does he want to meet new people, or does he prefer being alone?
                                          The Fangs probably wouldn't want him anywhere near Mag Li. He's not really able to take part in Fangs-Fianna politics, and they feel like they couldn't completely trust him if they had to move against any other sept. On the other hand leaving him in the company of the Red Talons for an extended period of time would be unacceptable, what if he forms stronger bonds with a mongrel Tribe than with the Fangs? So they tell him to "represent" the Fangs at Mistwatch or something like that. If he proves his worthiness and loyalty to the Tribe there he can always be posted somewhere else later. Having no idea where to start with the whole Garou business he didn't really object.
                                          As a Lupus Shine Talon isn't too thrilled about spending a lot of time in the city. He fares better than most Lupus though, the Fangs taught him human language, behavior and etiquette. He likes meeting new Garou as he's an inquisitive fellow and meeting new people help him get a perspective on his own place and duties, though he probably wouldn't be able to articulate it like that. He doesn't really feel anything for humans one way or the other, though he understands that Homid have kin among the humans and they are to be respected and never harmed. As long as he can spend some time off-duty in his native woods he'll be fine.

                                          Originally posted by Al-Haleem View Post
                                          Finanlly, you say Shine Talon dislikes the Red Talon's methods. Why does he continue to work with them? Does he have a Red Talon mentor, or some other personal connection to the tribe?
                                          The Talons have a perspective about being a Lupus and a Garou that Shine Talon couldn't find among his own Tribe, immersed in human-think as it is. Plus they are the ones that found him wandering after he Firsted and they are closer to his home territory than any other. That's why he keeps working with them occasionally: he can't get what they are willing to give him anywhere else; plus it's nice to howl with Lupus from time to time.
                                          I wasn't thinking of purchasing a Red Talon mentor, more like some dots of contacts: not really friends, not really personal, just kind of people who give each other a social call and trade favors from time to time. Shine Talon used to have a Homid Ahroun mentor from his own Tribe, butthey parted ways as soon as he learned how to fight and how to live among humans.
