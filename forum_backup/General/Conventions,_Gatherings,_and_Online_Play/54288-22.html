                    05-09-2014, 07:25 PM
                    Hyuga eyeing the vegetables fills up on them whilst pondering the message. He wonders if it was from Mia. He had hinted to her what he was after all. But did this mean that it was actually time for dawn, or did the message come for one of the Sun's chosen. If so, was I mistaken for one of those of "Dawn" or did it speak of another. And who here would be speaking flametongue? It was not all that common around here. Or was this a simple magic that appeared in his native tongue? Could it be Mia, an unknown person, or maybe their new companion Yi? There were so many possibilities. Getting frustrated, Hyuga combs his hands through his hair trying to tame it and get all of the moss and twigs out of it.

                    "I suppose I won't figure anything out like this. Time to head back into the city. Perhaps Mia will have an answer for me. That's the best place to start. I would like to see her again as well. After a quick visit, I should probably do some crafting to make money and gain at least some sort of prestige. Why do these things have to be so complicated?"

                    Hyuga quickly wraps himself in his cape to bring cover from the bright, glaring daystar above. Saying his goodbyes to the forest, he walks into the city along the bustling main road looking left and right at the different games, activities, stalls, and other events happening through the town. He knew he should keep in mind the location of at least one or two of the blacksmith stalls and shops. Recalling the road to the academy where Mia would most likely be, he treks onward, a clear foreigner to all he passes by. He ignores all the calls to see products, jeers, and all the other noises of the crowds. Eventually, he finds himself at his destination waiting for the messenger to appear.

                    As soon as the messenger appears, Hyuga says, "I believe Madame Mia may be looking for me. Could you please let her know that I am here?" Hyuga will then give a short bow and stare off into the distance looking and listening for more news of his friends.

                        (Jensven)
                        Mere moments after Shadow has had his fill, a mouse scurries out from underneath the lip of the fountain betwixt the dog's legs. Falling prey to his instinct, Shadow chases after the mouse, which darts towards a nearby stall. The dog barrels towards a swordsman, a southerner from the looks of him. This southerner sees the oncoming dog at the last moment, narrowly dodging out of the way. Shadow (somehow) looses track of the mouse, and begins sniffing around on the ground around the southerner.

                        (Hyuga)
                        While walking towards the Academy's grounds, Hyuga passes a number smithy stands, and something unusual happens. A stray sunbeam crosses a displayed plow at the exact right moment for it to shine directly into Hyuga's eyes. He turns his head reflexively, seeing a large dog barrel towards him. Narrowly dodging, Hyuga's gaze falls upon a northern swordsman, presumably the dog;s owner.

                        (Both)
                        The two swordsmen take stock of each other. Each one seems so familiar yet alien to the other, as if they were brothers in a past life. The northerner recalls the southerner giving him a gleaming blade of gleaming silver and blazing white-gold, a masterpiece blade that he died swinging. And the southerner recalls the northerner using this blade to save his life, thrusting himself upon traitorous blades to give his brother a chance to flee and live.

                        So very familiar.

                                    In the same instant he saw the foreigner and remembered the details, Hyuga swiftly bear hugged him without thinking. A split second later, Hyuga jolts backwards and apologizes scratching his head. "Ah... sorry about that. I do not know what came over me. Please forgive my rudeness. I just felt an overwhelming feeling of trust and debt to you." Hyuga will survey him once again and look around at the dog chasing a mouse into a corner. "I feel as if this is not the first time we have met. I believe the unconquered sun may have led me to you. I received a most interesting message this morning. The Dawn has come."

                                    Hyuga puts his fingers to his temple and tries to concentrate for a second. "Surely there must be some sort of connection between us for the sun to lead me to you. I have heard tales that groups of the sun's chosen often fought together in tight knit groups in the past. Perhaps this is all a message that we worked together in the past. I am certain that it must have been you that saved my life. I would be honored if you would fight with me once again. I also saw a sword in that vision. It was like a ray of light from the daystar that stopped evil in its path. Maybe I have found yet another grand design to craft once again."

                                    Without skipping yet another beat, he says "I am working with some others to help protect part of this great nation from a Raksha invasion. I believe you might be of great help. Will you come with me old friend?"
