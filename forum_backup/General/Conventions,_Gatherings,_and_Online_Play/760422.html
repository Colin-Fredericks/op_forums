    [W20] Looking for players

    11-07-2015, 10:44 PM
    Hey everyone.

    I'm looking for one to three players for a short werewolf game. It'll be set during the character's rite of passage, amidst political intrigue and some good 'ole savage horror. Heavy on the roleplaying, but with a good mix of combat thrown in.

    My zone is EST, but since I don't feel too comfortable speaking I'd prefer to play over Skype text chat or through another play-by-post forum (RPOL, Myth Weavers, etc.). I should be able to post daily to every other day.

    Please shoot me a PM if you're interested -- thanks!
    System: Werewolf: the Apocalypse 20th Anniversary Edition
    Restrictions: Garou only. No kinfolk or Changing Breeds.
    Breed: Open. Lupus will be scrutinized. I'm leery of Metis - I find them difficult to play well.
    Tribe: Open.

    No eating each other -- you don't have to like your packmates, you might even thrash one another occasionally -- but at the end of the day, you must have their back. Your character needs to be able to work in a diverse pack.

    We'll be starting with their First Change. The setting is the fictional Coalridge, Wyoming, a failing oil 'boom town' near the border of Yellowstone.
