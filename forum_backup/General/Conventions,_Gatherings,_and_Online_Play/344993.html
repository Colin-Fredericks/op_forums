    [Interest Check] PbP Wraith: the Great War

    12-19-2014, 03:07 PM
    So the Wr20 KS is going on right and I've been reading through my small Wraith collection, getting an urge to run or play a game of it but since my current RP is running another game right now so I can't really do it with them right now so I figured I'd see if I could try doing a Post by Post game here.

    Plus some comments by PaladinDemo over in the WtO forums further spurred me on.

    Now I love Wraith: the Great War, from the historical setting which seems completely natural to WtO to the civil war and all the rules for weapons and warmachines of the era to the art in the book so I'm gonna try to run a game.

    I just need to see if there are people interested in play some WtGW.

    My idea is that's its set in Paris on the eve of the Battle of the Somme*, which triggered the 4GM. That also means the Smiling Lord's Insurrection hasn't happened yet but just like the Storm its coming. PCs are all recently deceased and picked up by their Legions, having died some time over the course of the war so far, and have for some reason or another come to live in the Paris Necropolis when the shit begins to hit the fan, followed by Underworld debris and hordes of Spectres.

    *July 1 1916 for those who don't remember.

    For character creation, the defaults of WtGW and you're all wraiths in the Legions and the Hierarchy. If you want to use something from another book, there's two criteria that must be kept in mind, 1) it can't be modern, it has to be something that could exist at this point in time and 2) I have to have the book in question. At present I have the Player's Guide (both normal and Shadow), the Book of Legions, the Hierarchy, the Guildbooks, Doomslayers and Charnel Houses of Europe.

    Once the PCs have been submitted I will randomly chose their Shadowplayers and they will create their Shadows. At present my idea for Shadowguiding is that each update I'll say if the Shadows are active or not and if there's anything special happening with them. If they're active, the Shadowguide can respond after their Player has responded. Or the Player and Shadow can get together and combine their posts together.

    Of course if anyone has other suggestions on Shadowguiding, feel free to say so.

    Homo sapiens. What an inventive, invincible species. It's only a few million years since they crawled up out of the mud and learned to walk. Puny, defenceless bipeds. They've survived flood, famine and plague. They've survived cosmic wars and holocausts. And now, here they are, out among the stars, waiting to begin a new life. Ready to outsit eternity. They're indomitable. Indomitable.

                                                          Thomas Mccabe is a 23 year old from Ashford, Kent in England. Brown hair, blue eyes, 5' 9", 185 pounds. He is married with a five year old daughter. An author by trade, Thomas' favorite genre is science fiction. He has two titles under his belt; Cavernous, an adventure about life and sentient beings in cave; and Nocturne, a story about an adventurer who finds a world of eternal night. When war broke out he was starting to pen his next novel, while his titles have small cult followings, he wanted to be the next Jules Verne of H. G. Wells. His fatherly duties slowed him, the war brought his work to a screeching halt. He joined the Royal Army to not only to serve King and country, but also to gain military experience for his next novel; a soldier waging war against an unearthly force. An alternate view of The War of the Worlds, from a soldier's point of view and the enemy isn't aliens, something familiar yet still foreign. He served as an artillery gunner, his weapon a 26 CWT Howitzer. He served a year until the battle of Verdun. While in his sleep the Germans released their chlorine gas on February 26th, unlike most soldiers in the war, Thomas' death was agonizing slow.
                                                          When he awoke he found out that he was no longer of the living. He learned that even the dead are at war with each other. He quickly learned about his new abilities and his shoulder devil. He joined the Grim Legion to continue his military experience even as a Wraith. For his novel now has it's enemy, the dead, and each side would be blurred since he wasn't aware of his new state of being. If he survives the War of the Dead, he needs to figure out how to return home and how to write his novel in his current form.
