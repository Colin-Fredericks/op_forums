                A combat focus is by no means necessary. However, the Labyrinth Forest is a dangerous place, and having basic protections is highly recommended, even if I try to avoid paranoia combat as much as I can. I assume most times that characters will most all be specced to what seems right, and honestly, it just breaks my immersion if every single Exalt is a combat hobo like this is a Wuxia movie where anything is an excuse for a kung fu battle.

                That being said, have you played Etrian Odyssey or similar dungeon crawlers before? Because while there is definitely a place for combatants, there's also a place for diplomats, survival guides and scholars, what with the trekking through the jungle, fighting the wildlife, meeting the intelligent inhabitants, and recording discoveries. That's sort of my vision for this game, at this stage, in any case.

                                                    I download Anathema to create all of my exalted characters - http://anathema.github.io/

                                                    I also use the following guide when choosing excellencies:
                                                    Also, a general run-down on excellencies.
                                                    The 1st Excellency is great in cases where more successes=more effect, such as combat or crafting.
                                                    The 2nd Excellency is the opposite, most useful when all you need to do is succeed.
                                                    The 3rd Excellency is wonky, and is actually most useful for cases where you have to calculate a static value, due to the way it adds dice in.
                                                    Here's a basic rundown by ability:
                                                    Archery: 1st Excellency
                                                    Martial Arts: 1st Excellency
                                                    Melee: 1st Excellency
                                                    Thrown: 1st Excellency
                                                    War: 2nd Excellency
                                                    Integrity: 2nd Excellency/3rd Excellency
                                                    Performance: 2nd Excellency
                                                    Presence: 2nd Excellency
                                                    Resistance: 2nd Excellency
                                                    Survival: 2nd Excellency
                                                    Craft: 1st Excellency
                                                    Investigation: 2nd Excellency
                                                    Lore: 2nd Excellency (1st Excellency if you want to Wyld Shape)
                                                    Medicine: 2nd Excellency
                                                    Occult: 2nd Excellency
                                                    Athletics: 3rd Excellency
                                                    Awareness: 1st Excellency/2nd Excellency
                                                    Dodge: 2nd Excellency/3rd Excellency
                                                    Larceny: 2nd Excellency
                                                    Stealth: 2nd Excellency
                                                    Bureaucracy: 2nd Excellency
                                                    Linguistics: 2nd Excellency
                                                    Ride: 2nd Excellency
                                                    Sail: 2nd Excellency
                                                    Socialize: 2nd Excellency
