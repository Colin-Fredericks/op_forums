    [OOC] Mistwatch - Werewolf: The Apocalypse

    05-19-2015, 04:41 AM
    Hi guys, I've thrown together some setting information, at the google docs link below. It's lengthy, but I hope it's mildly interesting/entertaining, and should give you a good idea of what to expect. Or not to expect. Use your characters from the Delaware Valley chronicle if you like, tweak them, discard them, whatever. All characters will start off as new arrivals to Mistwatch Sept, and while they'll have already undergone their rite of passage, they'll still have much to prove. If you guys could paste you character sheets into this thread, I'd appreciate it. Even if you want to completely re-do your character, we'll be using the character sheet format Jim provided, as it's pretty darn good.

    That should be enough to get started. Questions and suggestions are most welcome.

    Link to Google Doc:
    https://docs.google.com/document/d/1...it?usp=sharing

    A few other notes:

    - No Rite of Talisman Dedication. In this crazy alternate universe, werewolves don't shred their clothing when they shift. Trust me, it's better this way.
    - No super-powered Fetishes or Talens. This goes without saying.
    - Everyone takes at least one point in the Totem background. The pack will be stuck together for a while, and this will represent this. Remember, the more points in Totem, the more powerful it'll be. You guys will get to choose your totem as the game goes on.
    - No Fate background.
    - And no Dark Fate flaw.
    - Silver Fangs must have a minimum of 3 in Pure Breed.
    - Gnosis and Willpower may not be raised higher than 8 at character creation. No exceptions. Rage can be as high as you wish...

    Attributes will use the standard 7/5/3 split. Ratings of 1 or 5 will require some sort of exceptional explanation. Also, choose a specialization for every attribute rated 4 and above. Custom one's welcome.

    As for abilities, it's the regular 13/9/5 spread. However, there should be something in your character's background to justify all their abilities to some extent. Ratings of 5 in anything will be severely scrutinized. Kailindo, Klaiveskar, Iksakku are off-limits, even if your mentor happens to be an elder Stargazer (ahem). All lupus characters must take at least one dot in Primal Urge to represent their lupine instincts.

                      Personality
                      Seems aloof and distant at first, but he just tends to put on a show to people he doesn't know. In reality he's quite friendly to people / wolves he gets to know, though he doesn't show that well in human shapes. Calm, analytical and patient, he nonetheless tends to snap even at friends if he doesn't get some time alone for self-reflection now and then. Values instinct over human-think and he's brutally honest because he chose to discard the concept of "tact" his tribemates tried to teach him. Overall people who know him well find that as long as they respect his boundaries he's friendly and loyal.

                      Character History
                      When the Fangs first came to the area around Mistwatch, a single lupus among them tried to claim a strong pack of wolves as his Kinfolk. After watching four generations of his descendants in which not even a single pup bred true, he left in search of better stock and the lineage became a footnote in the Homid-written history of the local Fangs.
                      Now, more than a century later, his efforts finally bore fruit. Though dozens of generations removed and unnoticed by a long-dormant Kin-Fetch spirit, a pup was not only bred true, but with enough lingering purity of blood that Falcon would not disdain him.
                      Shine Talon's life was pretty unremarkable for a wolf pup until his First Change. He played and hunted with his brothers, quickly outdoing them in both cunning and strength. When their father became too old to lead and hunt and it was time to make a play for dominance, though, things turned bloody. As he was holding down his struggling brother, the Change came on him. He tore the wolf from piece to piece, and the rest of the pack scattered, never to come together again.
                      The Talons found the young werewolf, wailing in a furless guise, overcome by emotions and understanding neither. They estabilished he was of their breed, but their spirits also showed them that he was not of theirs and they should contact the Silver Fangs.
                      The Fangs didn't really know what to make of the pup. They managed to identify his lineage, but his closest relatives dwelled in woods hundreds of miles away. Besides they had no heirloom, no title, no nothing to offer to a scion so far removed. So it wsa that Shine Talon stayed here in Mistwatch, he underwent tutelage by a Homid warrior and took his Rite of Passage with cubs from other tribes.
                      Nowadays the Fangs stationed him in Mistwatch because they don't trust him enough to keep him close but they don't trust him enough to let him roam free either. They taught him enough human get-by to not threaten the Veil then happily discharged the responsibility to Ragnar and his septmates. As for Shine Talon, he feels more kinship with wolves and the Red Talon tribe than with homids; he frequently visits his home range and trades favors and tutelage with the Talons of the Devourer.

                              Yeah, I thought at first thet I wanted to wait to see someone else's work before I posted mine, but of course if everyone thought like this we'd never go anywhere. So I did hte opposite and hope that seeing us working on my PC will inspire someone else to post.

                              Anyway, on to your questions:

                              1 - Lodges are mentioned in both Silver Fangs tribebook and, I believe, in W20. It's a SF thing where each one of htem is encouraged to choose either the Lodge of the Sun or the Moon, which are kind of administrative bureaus with ritualistic overtones. The Lodge of the Sun is concerned with worldly matters, politics and the human population; the Lodge of the Moon with wilderness, the spirit world and wolves. A proper SF court is attended by at least a steward, shaman and squire from each Lodge, plus a seneschal from one or the other. They also have some Gifts like Camps do, but overall it's just a bit of flavor you can ignore if the story isn't that SF-centric.

                              2 - Feel free to make my major Contacts up. I wouldn't know what REd Talons NPCs would work best in your setting.

                              3 - I thought of that person as just some guy who wanted to score some quick and dirty Honor for mentoring then went their own way as soon as they did the bare minimum. That's why I didn't purchase Mentor. Even if we make it one of your named NPCs I don't relay think they'd have more than a casual, cordial relationship.

                              4 - Well, they can try! Of course a homid could be tricked into thinking Talon is too dense to be useful even if successfully manipulated. (He's not stupid at all, he just thinks very differently and values very different things from the homid around him.)

                              5 - I imagine they knew, and there would be some things he wouldn't be trusted with, but overall the relationship keeps working. Unless that doesn't work for your story in which case I'd have to tweak my backstory a bit.

                              6 - They were so terrified by the experience that they all started living as lone wolves and keep on doing so. Talon feels a lot of guilt over this, but he figures at least he can respect their wishes, watch them from afar and protect them when he can.
