                                                        01-02-2018, 06:15 AM
                                                        Scholar of the Golden Owl looks at Laughter. He holds Laughter's eyes with his own. He is angry, more so than he has been since his sister's abduction. He refuses to be looked down on.

                                                        "I work passage here, I am paying my way to get help finding my sister. But I am not part of your crew. I have told you to stop doing something that I find annoying and yet you persist. You will stop calling me boy or face the consequences."

                                                        His anger flares into a rage that pushes to get out. He does not know the words, but in truth he is a Twilight Solar Exalted and he will not be belittled by anyone. His caste mark flares and his rage feeds into his anima, a glowing golden owl spreading its wings.

                                                        He will not be belittled.
