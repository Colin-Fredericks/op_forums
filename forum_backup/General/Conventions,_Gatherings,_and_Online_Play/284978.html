    [Recruitment] Fractown - A V:tM / W:tA crossover

    10-17-2014, 11:42 AM
    The Wyrm laid its cancer under the skin of Earth eons ago. Now, in a small Texas town that ain't so small anymore, the Wyrm's minions make manifest their dark demiurge's plans. And its all bankrolled by Vampires...

    I'm recruiting 4 players for a V:tM/W:tA crossover game to take place in a fictional group of towns south of San Antonio, right in the midst of a massive oil boom driven by hydraulic fracturing. Your ST happens to live in the middle of a boom town himself, so he has plenty of juicy background to make this work.

    Why would Werewolves care about fracking? Because in the World of Darkness, those chemicals that they are pumping into mother earth to literally break her up and extract oil are toxic brews made with love by her dear sister Wyrm. Because towns teetering on the edge of desertion have found new masters in the massive oil companies that have come to punch holes in their land. Because these rural places once held caerns of iron rock surrounded by dry land and scraggly brush that mankind rejected, and thus Werewolves could embrace.

    Why do Vampires care? Money, baby! A barrel of oil can go for as high as $150 dollars each. A so-so well produces 300 barrels a day, every day. Also, these boom towns have all kinds of people, especially single, working class people, coming in from three or four states over. If they go missing, it might be a while before anyone even finds out. The sheer power that these oil companies wield over metropolitan areas is also staggering, mayors and senators alike are licking the boots of the generous tycoons trying to get the sales tax, capital gains tax and kickbacks that their huge profits can offer.

    The number of factions involved is straggering (and all are joinable). Camarilla executives from up north are pushing into San Antonio, a city that has been Sabbat since the Lasombra missionaries came to enlighten the natives and exterminate the Nuwisha and Garou shape-changers along the San Antonio river. Pentex is wrapping it tentacles tightly around the oil companies, some of which elder vampires have under there sway. The remaining pack of Garou from Apache and Karankwa kinfolk (that are classified under Uktena to their great chagrin) are returning to their ancestral homelands from Oklahoma, either following the call to fight the Wyrm, or because they need a job. Bone Gnawer exiles from San Antonio are being pushed out by the oil, even as Bone Gnawer gang members see their drug sales increase. The one or two Gangrel who subsisted off the close knit, politics-free small towns now see the concrete slowing creeping. A number of Glass Walker packs tasked with infiltrating the oil companies, are beginning to show signs of corruption. And speaking of corruption, Ravnos and Setite alike are taking great advantage of "oil-field trash" and their hunger for gambling, sex, country music and alcohol.

    And the mere mortals are caught in the crossfire.

    PM me or reply to this thread if interested.
