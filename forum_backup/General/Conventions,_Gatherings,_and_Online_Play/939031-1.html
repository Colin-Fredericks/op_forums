                                        Dr. Luck and Simurgh
                                        As you finish your conversation, you arrive at Cadence's office. The door is closed, but he has told you before that you're always welcome to knock, and that he will answer if he isn't busy.



                                        A middle-aged Caucasian man opens the door with a smile. He has gray hair that's partially balding, but he has an energetic step. "Ah, you made it! Please, come in."

                                        The Acanthus Mystagogue invites you each to sit in a padded leather chair. His office has a large, wide bookshelf that stretches to the ceiling and is filled with a mix of mundane and occult books on a range of topics. Various pictures hang on the wall of exotic locations he has visited, all with him grinning excitedly and pointing to different things he finds interesting.

                                        "I hope your studies have been going well. I have a very important task for you both that I think will serve our Order well. But first, have you been learning anything or studying anything interesting of note?"

                                        After he gives you each a chance to share about your recent studies, Cadence begins to explain his request. "It started with this woman Melody knows, a Sleepwalker retainer of hers...I think her name is Jill? Anyway, Melody mentioned in passing that Jill has been having some odd occurrences at her home recently. From the sounds of it, Jill has been seeing odd marks around their home, almost like animal tracks, but unlike anything she's ever seen. From the information I've been able to gather, it also sounds like Sally -- that's her young daughter -- has reported seeing things at night near the house."

                                        Cadence begins to speak eagerly, mirroring the excitement on his face from the many pictures around his office. "The Mysterium has a number of mages who collect information about spirits and other supernatural creatures, especially the ones that interact with Sleepers. But few of them are able to, or choose to, manifest themselves in such a way that they're visible to the naked eye. If Sally has seen them, she might have a special kind of talent that allows her to detect supernatural creatures, which would be fascinating to study. Or, alternatively, these creatures may have interesting properties worth studying.

                                        "I know a few Mystagogues who have been trying to catalogue spirit-related activity all around Denver. If you could contribute to their research, not only would it be a good opportunity for you to learn more about spirits for yourselves, but it could help you to build some relationships within the Order,"                                         Cadence explains. "I'd like you to go and investigate. I believe Melody has asked Spider and Ouroboros Ophis to go as well, to pick up some paperwork or something that Jill needed to pass on to the Free Council. But your presence would certainly be helpful to them." Cadence smiles again. "Do make sure you check on the girl, too. Try and ask her about what she's seen, but more importantly, how she has seen what she has. It might be quite enlightening."

                                                        "Excellent! I hope that your familiar continues to be a source of assistance for you. Certainly it will be a good opportunity to learn about how spirits interact with each other." Cadence smiles at your summary. "Yes, yes, that's it. Do be careful, though. Remember, a mage's greatest strengths are preparation and foresight. I remember a time when I was newly Awakened..." Cadence's eyes are unfocused as he starts retelling a tale, at great length, about one of his early experiences with Supernatural beings back before he had much understanding of the way these creatures work and before he had learned to plan ahead for potentially dangerous situations. Fate magic had saved his life, but he seems to describe the harrowing experience with fondness.

                                                        It's a fascinating story. At least, it was the first time he told it to you both. You're pretty sure this is the fourth or fifth time he's shared this pearl of wisdom in as many weeks. It's gotten longer with each retelling, but the details are generally the same.


                                                        Dr. Luck
                                                        After Cadence finishes his longwinded story, he turns to you with a considering look. "You're certainly not the first mage to write about magic after their Awakening. It's an admirable goal, surely, to try and help others Awaken by planting seeds of truth and nourishing them over time. I have a few colleagues in the Silver Ladder who has made that their life ambition. You may even have read some of their work," he smiles knowingly. Cadence leans forward, a more serious expression on his face. "But please heed my advice, Dr. Luck. The Mysterium values knowledge, obtained through direct experience. We are responsible for sharing that knowledge when appropriate, or safekeeping it when revealing it may be dangerous or even catastrophic. You cannot lead from a position of ignorance. Write your story when you have time, but you must prioritize seeking actual knowledge. I think you will find that writing about half-truths, rather than seeking new truths, will lose its appeal with time. Perhaps I might be willing to look at your writing sometime in the future, but my availability is limited, and I would rather focus on instructing you about the Myesterium and your Path. As a fellow Acanthus, I believe I have much I could offer you. And I'm sure I can trust you to not violate the Veil," he frowns.

                                                        Cadence leans back and strokes his chin. "The idea of creating a cabal theme is certainly an interesting, and beneficial, investment. But it is an investment. It draws not just on your Shadow Names, but on your very magical identities -- who you are at the very core of your being. Most cabals do not meet the necessary requirements to fuel that power. Why, not even Kitpu, our Hierarch here in Denver, has this level of intimacy with his own cabal."

                                                        Cadence's voice takes on a gentle lecturing tone as he continues. "We of The Band made a conscious decision shortly after forming our cabal that we desired this level of connection, and were committed to the task. But we decided our Shadow Names in concert," he smiles at the pun, "and it took considerable time -- months of magical study and dedication -- before we reaped any benefits. Now, looking back, I am indebted to my mentor who urged me to consider this approach with my cabal. But know that it will take effort, by each of you, to reach that level of intimacy."

                                                        The older mage looks to Dr. Luck. "To be honest, I'm not sure you will be able to develop that group theme if you do not change your Shadow Name, Dr. Luck. I may be wrong. I've read some older manuscripts in our archives that suggest other cabals have managed the benefits without the same commitment to theme that my cabal has. But likely at least one other mage in your cabal would need to change their name, too, or the theme may still be too loose. The good news is that, as fledgling mages, your Shadow Names have not quite settled in so firmly that they cannot be changed with effort. The choice, ultimately, is yours."

                                                        Cadence leans back and smiles again. "Well, I've certainly kept you long enough. I'm sure Melody is finished with your colleagues by now. Let me know if you need anything else, and I will stay in touch as my schedule permits."

                                                        You can tell from Cadence's responses that he's not inclined to help you with reviewing your book. If you invest in the Mentor merit, he may be more willing and supportive, but it's outside of his interests currently. You can attempt to change that over time using Social Maneuvering, if you like.
