    Werewolf: the Forsaken
    God Machine Chronicle Update
    I borrowed some of the Uratha form changes from Pendragon, a poster on the Onyx Path forums.
    Two of the new conditions are from the Blood and Smoke: Reap the Whirlwind quickstart.

    Character Creation

    Werewolves receive 10 merit points at character creation. The fifth dot of a trait does not cost two points.
    Use the Virtue and Vice system from the GMC book.

    Experience

    Attribute: 4 Experiences per dot
    Merit: 1 Experience per dot
    Totem: 1 Experience per dot
    Skill Specialty: 1 Experience
    Skill: 2 Experiences per dot
    Affinity Gift:: 3 Experiences per gift
    Other Gift: 4 Experiences per gift
    Primary Renown: 4 Experiences per dot
    Other Renown: 5 Experiences per dot
    Harmony: 3 Experiences per dot
    Primal Urge: 5 Experiences per dot
    Rites: 1 Experience per dot
    Willpower: 1 Experience per dot

    Harmony

    Having seen the way Humanity is handled in Blood and Smoke: the Strix Chronicle, I have decided to leave Harmony as it is in the W:tF core rulebook, except instead of derangements, I will be adding custom conditions for Harmony rolls.
    Roll Results
    Dramatic Failure: The character’s world view has been damaged, perhaps beyond repair. The character suffers from traumatic stress. Lose a dot of Harmony and choose from the following Conditions (or create a new one with Storyteller approval): Broken, Fugue, or Spiritual Obsession. Also, take a Beat.
    Failure: The character’s world view has been shaken and he probably questions his sense of self, his ability to relate to people, his own moral worth, or his sanity. Lose a dot of Harmony and choose one of the following Conditions (or create a new one with Storyteller approval): Guilty, Bestial, or Competitive.
    Success: The character has come through the breaking point intact. He might feel guilty or upset about what happened, but he can cope. Choose one of the following Conditions (or create a new one with Storyteller approval): Guilty, Bestial, or Competitive.
    Exceptional Success:The character somehow manages not only to survive the breaking point, but to find meaning in it, to reaffirm his own self-worth, or to pass through fire and become tempered by it. The character takes a Beat and regains a point of Willpower.

    Harmony Conditions

    Bestial

    Your character acts on primal, physical impulses. Frightening things make him run. He meets aggressive threats with violence and anger. Take -2 to all rolls to resist frenzy or physical impulse. As well, take -2 to Defense due to impulsive action. Any rolls to compel your character to impulsive, aggressive action or escape achieve exceptional success on three successes instead of five. This could apply to Disciplines such as Nightmare, or Dominate under the right circumstances.
    Resolution: Take an action during a frenzy that results in a breaking point. This Condition fades naturally after a number of days equal to your Primal Urge. After resolving Bestial, your character cannot be subject to this Condition again for a full month.
    Beat: n/a

    Competitive

    Your character must assert dominance and superiority. Either she gives it her all, or she falters. Any time she’s in direct competition with another character, she suffers -2 on any rolls where she doesn’t spend Willpower. This includes contested rolls. As well, any rolls to tempt or coerce her into competition achieve exceptional success on three successes instead of five.
    Common Causes: A challenging predatory aura conflict, or a failed Detachment roll.
    Resolution: Win or lose a competition where someone reaches a breaking point. This Condition fades naturally after a number of days equal to your Primal Urge. After resolving Competitive, your character cannot be subject to this Condition again for a full month.
    Beat: n/a

    Spiritual Obsession

    The character gains a ban or compulsion as described on pages 185-186 of Werewolf: the Forsaken. She gains the 9-again quality on all rolls related to pursuing her obsession. On rolls that are unrelated to her obsession, she loses the 10-again quality. Obsession can be a temporary or persistent quality per Storyteller approval. Depending on the nature of the compulsion, the character may take a -2 on some social interactions.
    Resolution: The character sheds or purges her fixation by reaffirming her connection to humanity as a counterweight to her spiritual side.
    Beat: Character fails to fulfill an obligation due to pursuing her obsession.

    Uratha Forms
    • Hishu (The Human): +2 to perception rolls.
    • Dalu (The Near-Human): Strength +1, Stamina +1, Manipulation –1, Size +1, Health +2, Speed +1, +2 to perception rolls. 9-again on all Strength and brawl rolls. Can deal lethal damage unarmed.
    • Gauru (The Wolfman): Rage invoked, Strength +3, Dexterity +1, Stamina +2, Size +2, Health +4, Initiative +1, Speed +4, Armor 1/1, inflict lethal damage (+1 bonus to claw attacks, +2 to bite attacks), +3 to perception rolls, wound penalties ignored and no unconsciousness rolls made, –2 to resist Death Rage, fail most Social and Mental tasks. 8 again on all Strength and brawl rolls. Use (the higher of Dex or Wits) + Athletics to determine defense.
    • Urshul (The Near-Wolf): Strength +2, Dexterity +2, Stamina +2, Manipulation –3, Size +1, Health +3, Initiative +2, Speed +7, +3 to perception rolls, inflict lethal damage, 9-again on all Strength and brawl rolls. Use (the higher of Dex or Wits) + Athletics to determine defense.
    • Urhan (The Wolf): Dexterity +2, Stamina +1, Size –1, Initiative +2, Speed +5, +4 to perception rolls, inflict lethal damage with bite attack. Use (the higher of Dex or Wits) + Athletics to determine defense.
