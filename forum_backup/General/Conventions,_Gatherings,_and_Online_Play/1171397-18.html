                        Diane will make a flurry of quick jabs and slashes at the beast's head and neck. Her attacks are not intended to cause damage, but to distract. To get the beast to focus on her, leaving Laughter the freedom to set up a killing blow. That was the way the Vicriuth fought the beasts that invaded their lands from the Wyld. Strike in teams. One getherin draws the attention, another attacks while distracted. Keep shuffling the focus, making for lethal blows while the beast's attention is away...

                        This is not a beast from the Wyld, nor is Laughter one of the tea-eaters. But the same principles should do.

                        Diane will make a distract gambit on the dhampir. Success transfers 6i to Laughter for an attack on the dhampir next round.
