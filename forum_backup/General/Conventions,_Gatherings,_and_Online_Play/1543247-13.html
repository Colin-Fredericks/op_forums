                                        Week 5

                                        We are so close to the end of chapter 1 "Manifest Destiny" (Should I PUN-ish you all for not incorporating the name of the chapter into game play? Nah, it's cool). We just need to get everyone to that restaurant in Chinatown. Hopefully with some sort of evidence or defense for Gilad to present to the high council.

                                        If I'm understanding you all correctly, the answer to my IMPORTANT ST question "What do you currently think is going on, and what clues are you going to follow to solve this mystery/investigation?"

                                        The Big Idea: to differing degrees, everyone in the party except Amir has experienced some sort of time anomaly. The most immediate result of this time anomaly is that Gilad is being accused by members of his order and the concillium in Buenos Aires of betraying his cabal to their deaths and handing over a large quantity of tass (enough to power a significant spell that would have a global impact) to the Seers. This is different from Gilad's memory, which is that he and his cabal, with the help of his sleepwalker friend Trish Takahashi, were able to recover the tass for the use of the Adamantine Arrow.

                                        Significant clues
                                        • Gilad remembers Trish and Galahad being on the flight with him to San Francisco. But then they disappeared at a certain point in time during the flight. He was unable to find any luggage belonging to them. And the only evidence that Trish was on the flight is that he has her mobile phone. Whenever he mentions Trish's name, no one seems to know who he is talking about, even if it would make sense for them to remember her (mechanically Trish has Fame 2 in this setting. She is a well known newspaper journalist. Like Lois Lane in Metropolis, or Vicky Vale in Gotham).
                                        • Helmut was in his favorite flower shop, which is owned by Trish's older sister, Nadeshiko, who lives on a near by flower farm with her husband, the Ronin Talon Caliburn, and their two daughters. At a certain point in time the flower shop disappeared, and in its place was a new store under construction, Kitsune Books. When Helmut asked sleepers in the area, none of them could recall a flower shop ever being there. Like it never existed. He looked in his phone contacts, and could not find Caliburn's phone number. That's when he called Amir, his very nice lawyer friend.
                                        • Gilad notices that Galahad, who in his memory was part of the Nazi gold/Tass quest because Caliburn sent him to protect Trish, seems like a different person. Instead of a sword wielding, abyss fighting, bad a**, he looks like a bookish academic who might get lost in his own museum.
                                        • Olorian has a vague sense that he is supposed to remember something. But the only way to remember it is going into his own Oneiros.
                                        • Amir knows that several members of his cryptopoly have gone missing recently. Could the missing cult members be connected to Helmut's missing flower shop, and Gilad's missing friend?
