                                                    Gens Ptreas
                                                    Population: ~11000 (6 Boule Seats)
                                                    Capital: Greheisolonikon; Cut down to Greheis or Grey Ice colloquially. (Northernmost Novaleski city on map)
                                                    Major Industries: Following in their founder's footsteps, Gens Ptreas' major industries are Architecture and Engineering. Most structures in Novalesk are designed and/or built by this Gens. Likewise, their capital was positioned to gain easy access to a number of large quarries and the northern forests, ensuring access to the tools of their trade.
                                                    Political Position: Gens Ptreas generally keeps out of Capital politics, and as such lack long-term political alliances with many other Gentes. Gens Spyrus holds a great deal of gratitude for Ptreas, however, as this Gens sheltered the refugees that became Gens Spyrus in the late 3rd century post-contagion. Ptreas has four cadet houses (a high number, most Gentes only have one or two) which effectively double its population. Because of this, Ptreas alternates between advocating for allowing the absorption of cadet houses into their Patron houses and allotting Boule seats based on the population of the Gens and its cadet families. This earns the ire of Gens Althea, who has no cadet houses.
                                                    Cadet Families: Palve (~1200), Ruzha (~2700), Melitta (~3100) Kolinos (~3500)

                                                        Gens Spyrus
                                                        Population: ~10000 (5 Boule Seats)
                                                        Capital: Spyron (across river, South of Novalesk City)
                                                        Major Industries: Descended from a group of nomadic refugees from the north, Gens Spyrus sells their Audacity and Resilience. This Gens maintains several mercenary companies that travel far and wide in the Hundred Kingdoms. The Gens is also skilled metal-workers, maintaining several foundry complexes throughout the nation. The Gens holds an indefinite duration contract, naming them the primary supplier of the national troops' weaponry. Many other Gentes hire Spyrus trainers for their men and buy Spyrus weapons to outfit them.
                                                        Political Position: Being to small and non-influential to effect the overall course of the nation, Spyrus has become something of a 'free agent', siding with whichever power bloc makes them the best deal at the time. This has given them a somewhat mercenary reputation. The closest thing they have to an ally is Ptreas, for whom Gens Spyrus believes it owes a blood debt. Spyrus as a whole believes that the nation needs a stronger central government, but they trust neither Zophyrus or Nikoloi to run it.
                                                        Cadet Families: Zorica (~4000)

                                                            Gens Nikoloi
                                                            Population:                                                             ~26000 (13 Boule Seats)
                                                            Capital: Serres (first city north of Novalesk City)
                                                            Major Industries: Nikoloi continued their founder's heritage, being the most scholarly Gens in the nation. Indeed, the Academy of Sorcerous Wisdom, despite being a national institution, is owned by Gens Nikoloi, and leased to Novlaesk. The Gens' main industry, however, is Trade. Early in the nation's history, a member of the Nikoloi family discovered a rich silver vein. The family took this windfall and invested in building a fleet of trade vessels and construction a network of strong stone roads between the major townships. With control over the nation's arterials, the family gained a strangelhold on internal carrying trade.
                                                            Political Position: Gens Nikoloi strongly belieives that there should be a stronger central government, with itself at the head. It has the lukewarm support of Gens Midea in this regard. Otherwise, Nikoloi's political policies tend to direct it towards more complete control the nation's financial system. Nikoloi also advocates often for a closer relationship with Theros, most likely to secure additional foreign markets. Nikoloi has a strongly negative opinion of the Geherin.
                                                            Cadet Families: Pachis (~2000), Bojani (~900)
