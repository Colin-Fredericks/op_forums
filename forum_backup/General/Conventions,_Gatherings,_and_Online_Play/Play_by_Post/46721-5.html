                            Bio update

                            Hannae Li
                            "Hei"
                            Age: 26

                            Katleen Ulestra
                            “Miss Watson”
                            Location: Canada
                            Age: 20-ish
                            Order: Adamantine Arrow
                            Virtue: unknown
                            Vice: Envy

                            Katleen was a single child living with her maternal family due to her father’s absence. Due to her mother’s family’s negative view of her vagabond father, she was either sympathized as a victim of the whole affair or alienated as a child born under non-matrimonial circumstances. Although not mistreated, Katleen was alone, and kept mostly to herself, to her mother, and to her books from the family private collection, her (temporary) favorites being Conan Doyle’s Sherlock.

                            When Katleen was 14, she became lost in the woods and when night fell, descended into the Pandemonium. Soon after returning back from Awakening, she stumbled into wolves. Details aside, it drove her grandmother into a heart attack when Katleen came home at dawn with a pack of wolves at her side. When her father came back with his rare visits, it did not escape his attention that his biological daughter now controlled the supernal arts. He gave her the choice of apprenticeship at the cost of leaving her mother behind. Curious and tempted at the possibility of power, Katleen left with her father whom she trained under and entered the society of mages. She naturally joined the Adamantine Arrow as her father/mentor was.

                            Unsatisfied with a living centered on the politics of sorcerers, she left her father in interest of “diversifying her experience” and entered a prestigious mortal university suggested by her Free Council friends - or associates as she would call them. She studied for a major in technology, secretly tinkering with the combination of magic and machinery. However, after being released from a police investigation concerning bouts of suicides, she quitted the university and disappeared. Her father hired an Interfector to track her down and bring her back.

                            She was discovered in a state of disarray. She went through 4 mortal psychiatrists, all of them turned back less than a month, and outright refused when suggested a mage psychiatrist. Only after returning back to her mother and recuperating with her for almost a year did she come back to mage society. Soon after, she came across a struggling mage name Hei, whom she becomes partner with for the next 3 years. They later move base together to New Zealand as an operation force.

                            (Possible NPC just because)

                            Frederick von Gregore III
                            Mortal Name: “Harris Maxwell Sr”
                            Shadow Name: “Dutch”
                            Order: Guardian of the Veil

                            Not actually Dutch, but adopted the nickname in amusement of an assumption that he was one.
                            A renowned Interfector in the Washington “sector”
                            Adopted Otti and named him Harris.

                            Otti
                            Mortal Name: “Harris Maxwell Jr”/”Junior”
                            Shadow Name: “Harris”
                            Order: unknown

                            Suspected of insanity because of his magic vision.
                            Adopted by Dutch
                            Rescued Hannae and prevented a school shooting
