        OK, I'm going to copy these to the first post in this thread for easy reference later, but for now, here are the posting guidelines.

        Posting Guidelines and Other General Rules

        1. Please keep the IC thread In Character, and confine OOC comments and questions to the Out of Character thread as much as possible. If it is necessary to put OOC information in the IC thread (such as the results of a roll), please put it in a spoiler tag and clearly label it as OOC.

        2. When you guys need to roll dice, we will be using the Invisible Castle site. Make sure you use the same character name every time you roll, to make it easier for me to look it up. When posting the results of the roll, post the results and the link in a spoiler tag. You won't always know the difficulty of the roll, so it's often best to just copy and paste the die results rather than just saying how many successes you think you got. Also, remember that many rolls will be done by me behind my ST screen, so you'll just find out the effect of the action, not the number of successes you did or didn't roll. This is almost always going to be the case for things like Alertness or Investigation rolls.

        3. This game constitutes a single chronicle which will (hopefully) run from your characters' rite of passage through to the Apocalypse. It will be broken up into a series of four stories, after each of which we can sit down as a group and decide whether we want to end the chronicle there (after which I will write an epilogue to wrap up some plot threads), or whether we are having enough fun to continue into the next story of the chronicle. Each story will be [loosely] organized into chapters and interludes. A chapter will cover about as much play as could be accomplished in a long tabletop gaming session (more or less), and will be done primarily on this list. Interludes cover the time between chapters, and will be more flexible since most will be either one-on-one between me and one player, or done in smaller groups if some of the characters will be spending time together during interludes. Interludes can be handled by blue-booking, direct RP through PM or posts or, if circumstances permit, direct real time RP via a chat client like AIM. We'll take each interlude as it comes. As for scenes, the length of a scene is highly subjective and variable, and largely unimportant except where certain powers have a duration of one scene. In such cases, I'll let you guys know when a scene ends.

        4. Each chapter will start with a single post by me introducing the initial scene. Once I have that post out, you are all free to respond to it, and each other. Please include in your first post a brief description of what your character looks like at the time, including what they are wearing. It doesn't have to be long (especially later on as we all become more familiar with the characters), but I find this is important to help us immerse ourselves. Generally speaking I will let the scene flow as it will, but occasionally I will slow things down if some PCs are having trouble posting frequently enough to keep up and they are being left behind.

        5. When you post, remember that you only control your own character. Do not post the actions, words or thoughts of NPCs without my express permission, and do not ever post the actions, words or thoughts of the other PCs under any circumstances. You may also not make gross changes to the setting or environment without my express permission. For all these things, use common sense. If the scene is in a bar, you can assume that there is, say, a glass on the bar without asking my permission. You could also break said glass. You could talk to the bartender. You could not answer for the bartender; the bartender is an NPC, and I will answer for her. You could not assume that there is a hanging chandelier for your character to swing from- that is pretty unusual scenery for a bar. If the scene was in a fancy ballroom, however, assuming a chandelier would be just fine. In general, just use common sense and good judgment, and I'll let you know if you go too far. If you need to do anything that requires a roll (or that you think requires a roll), feel free to mention OOC what you think you should be rolling, and any modifiers that you think should apply. I'll let you know what to roll.

        6. You guys can generally respond to each other in RP as much as you like; unless you are trying to do something that requires a roll, or involves the reactions of an NPC or something like that, you do not usually need to wait for me. The other exception is if I ask you guys to slow things down to allow another PC or group of PCs time to catch up. We'll try to avoid these kinds of disruptions, but unfortunately in a PbP setting they sometimes crop up.

        7. Under normal circumstances, I would never post an action for your character. Even if they are being mind controlled in some way, I would almost always simply tell you off-list generally what the character must do and let you describe it IC (and such circumstances will be rare). However, just to expedite matters, I will occasionally make basic assumptions. For example, once the group makes the decision to go from point A to point B, rather than go through the tedium of letting everyone describe their characters getting into the cars, buckling their seatbelts and driving across town, I would move the scene along by simply typing "The characters gather into their vehicles and drive across town without incident," then go on to describe the new scene.

        8. IC posting is done in prose, as if writing a story, preferably in the present tense. So, you could post like this:

        Mari steps into the club, looking around. After taking in her surroundings, she walks up to the bar and addresses the bartender. "I'm looking for information about a biker who was in here recently." She flashes a wad of cash, adding "I can pay well for any help you could offer "

        You would NOT post like this:

        I have Mari walk into the club and make a Perception+Alertness roll. Then I have her walk up to the bartender, ask for information about that biker guy from Chapter 2 and offer a bribe.

        The idea is that, when put together, our collective IC roleplay should read like a story (more or less). I'm not a grammar or spelling Nazi, and don't worry if you change tense once in a while. I'm not an English Major, after all, and I know that not everyone here speaks English as a first language. ;-)

        9. Generally speaking, you should not be posting the thoughts or emotions of your character overtly. You can describe or imply them through your characters words, actions or body language, or use basic adjectives, as in "Mari angrily throws her arm across the bar, scattering the glasses while she growls at the uncooperative bartender." However, it's usually not appropriate to post the IC private thoughts of your character in a thread. Blue booking for Interludes is an exception to this rule; there is a lot more latitude in terms of writing style there.

        10. If, for whatever reason, you know you won't be able to post for a period of time, please let us know so that we aren't all waiting for you. The object of the game is to have fun, not to interfere in our lives. The nice thing about a PbP game is that you can check in from day to day as you have time. :-)

        11. Experience will be awarded at the end of every chapter, and in smaller amounts at the end of every interlude. Interlude awards will vary wildly depending on numerous factors. Chapter awards will have a certain base number, which can be as few as one xp and as high as I see fit, based on the length of the chapter and how much xp I feel everyone needs to progress to keep pace with the story. Everyone will receive the base xp at the end of each chapter, plus certain additional awards at my discretion based on various factors. Everyone who played their character especially well will get an extra XP. Everyone who's character accomplished an important personal goal during the chapter will get an extra XP. That sort of thing. In addition, at the end of every chapter, I will allow all of you players to vote by secret ballot (via PM) on who was the best roleplayer during that chapter, doing the most to increase the fun of the game. You can't vote for yourselves, and I will not reveal the final vote count. However, whoever wins the vote at the end of each chapter will get an extra XP.

        12. Except under certain rare circumstances, XP can only be spent during interludes. The main exception will be if the characters learn a gift from a spirit during play, which would be purchased right away. Any increase in a trait via XP will need to be justified, and greater justification will be needed for greater increases. In general, a trait can only be increased by one dot at a time, though I may make exceptions to this rule in extraordinary circumstances. If you can't justify the increase of a trait by the events that happened in a chapter (for example, if you want to increase your character's melee ability, but your character didn't actually use a melee weapon during the entire last chapter), all or part of an interlude can be used for training or other such justification (that's part of what they're for, after all). I'm usually pretty flexible on these issues.

        13. With the exception of Totem (which costs a flat 3xp per dot), Backgrounds can't be increased through XP. They will fluctuate through roleplay. If you want to work on increasing a particular background in an interlude, let me know and we can try to work something out, depending on exact circumstances.

        14. This is an adult game. We will be dealing with adult themes at times within the context of the game. It is not my goal to make anyone feel uncomfortable; we are all here to have a good time, after all. However, it is my goal to make you think. I don't sugar-coat my games, and I especially don't sugar coat my villains. Murder, torture, kidnapping, rape, mind control and other such things are all possibilities in the game. Sometimes these things happen to random NPCs. Sometimes these things happen to NPCs your characters care about. Sometimes these things happen to player characters. Sometimes these things are perpetrated by player characters. If there is anything in particular that makes you uncomfortable or would ruin your enjoyment of the game, talk to me about it. Like I said, it's not my goal to make people uncomfortable, but I don't run games for children. Again, I encourage you to speak to me directly, and in private, if you feel discomfited by any of these possibilities.
