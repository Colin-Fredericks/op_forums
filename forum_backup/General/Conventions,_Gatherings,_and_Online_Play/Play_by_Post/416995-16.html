    The other Exalt has to be around here somewhere. The Prodigal is sure of it. He had seen Merciful make to disembark the ship shortly after it landed. If he did go anywhere in the town, he would have to leave a trace of some sort, and that is exactly what the Daybreak sets out to find. He thinks about all the likely places and starts to mentally list the order of likeliness, until he has a short list of places that would yield the highest chances of finding who he sought.

    That done: he starts at the top of his short list and makes for his first destination.

    I have three (3) Perception + three (3) Investigation = six (6) dice for three successes. I am not going to assume what kind of stunt that was. I think stunt dice are academic at this point, though as I have met the difficulty of three (3).

                                The Prodigal works his way carefully and quietly into the teahouse. He does his utmost to disturb no one or give the impression that he is doing anything other than trying to get a good look at whatever it is that is going on.

                                In truth this is the second time he has seen this princess involved in a disturbance. The way everyone fawns over her is not natural. The Deathknight had heard of spirits and fair folk that would be able to elicit such a reaction, but this didn't seem like one of those situations. This was all together different and oddly familiar. He sits back a safe distance and merely observes. It would not do to act rashly, especially with the Icecrowner right there.

                                I will be activating my Lore Essence Overwhelming for eight (8) dice, spending eight (8) personal motes. I am attempting to get a good bead on what is going on and focusing mainly on the Princess, but will take any information on the Icecrowner I can get if I score well enough. So, I am rolling Perception of three (3) + Lore of five (5) + Excellency for eight (8) = a total of sixteen (16) dice for a total of nine successes. This will leave me with a personal essence pool of six (6).
