    "If it didn't drain you, then my theory was right. It feels the Abyss in me. That means the storm has the Abyss in it too. The Law of Sympathy, fundamental principle of magic: like attracts like. Delusional magical thinking in Sleepers, Supernal truth for us. That's why we were on the train together. Isn't it lovely to see the real world?"

    Moonstone can see the bubble forming around him as Phoenix forms the Imago. He extends an arm outside the protection of the awning, and the hail does not touch him.

    After a few seconds of watching in awe, he turns around, smiling and giggling like he's Phoenix's age. He takes the hand on his shoulder into both of his own and practically shouts: "Thank you!"

    Absolutely elated. Working with a mage again! Going into the trenches with a magical ward on his back! Ride that mood swing! "Come on, let's see what we're up against!"

    Leading him by the hand, or trying to, Moonstone runs out into the black hailstorm and is unharmed (hopefully). The door awaits.

Ragnar relinquishes his focus on his Sight, and allows his field of vision to narrow down to the world of the mundane and tangible. He quickly glances around the room and at the door to make sure they are alone.

            I relinquish my Active Mage Sight. I also roll Wits+Composure to see if I notice anybody in the room or in the doorway.

            Wits 2 + Composure 2 - Poor Lighting 2 = 2 dice -> 0 successes.

"Dr. McCallister, at the risk of sounding insane, if you saw what we did, then that must mean that the three of us of magic uses. As briefly and concisely as he can, Ragnar summarizes the events that immediately preceded his Awakening, and the abilities that he has acquired since then.He looks at the others. ​"I don't know what you guys can do, but Oscar has used magic to enhance his physical senses, which is something I can't even attempt."

            He turns to Oscar slightly. "I took a look at that girl. Something's off about her. She isn't sick or injured, but.." He shakes his head. "She's healthy. Almost too healthy, like she's brimming with life. But I think we can afford to let her wait. I saw this... thing." He walks to the window and points to the spot where he saw the... door. He involuntarily shivers at the memory. ​"It's almost like a portal. I don't know to where, but..." He shudders once again. "This sounds crazy, but when I looked at that thing, it just seemed wrong. Like it shouldn't exist. Whatever's up with that girl, whatever made that portal or could come through that portal is a much bigger problem."

            He turns back to the other two. "If anything bad comes through that portal, I don't know if anybody else will be able to see it. And even if they can, we might be the only ones able to stop it. So I think we need to get down there and see what's up." He pauses, debating something for a moment, and then presses ahead. "By the way. Do you guys have... other names? I got one during my... thing. It's Ragnar. Do... you guys have one? I feel like it's something we should be able to share with at least each other."

                Phoenix
                Perhaps you're more cautious because of Moonstone's current emotional state, or maybe you have a healthier fear of what it would mean for the Abyss to enter you. You strain your will to make your Imago snap into place, and you know it will risk Paradox, but you're ready for it. Your heart skips another beat and you're winded for a moment due to painful chest tightness. But your spell takes effect as intended -- even better than you intended, actually. For creating a spell on the fly, you excelled.

                Unlike your other spells, this one is very much obvious to the naked eye, even for someone without your Sight. When Moonstone takes a small step onto the curb to test it, the black ice bounces off the shield with an audible clatter as if striking a building, leaving a clear sphere around him that the ice cannot penetrate. Given your past as a Hunter and your own experience with Awakening, a part of you does wonder if seeing this magic would really mess with someone's mind, or draw unwanted attention. No one else appears to be out in this storm, though.

                Yes, you can resolve Inspired. Gain an Arcane Beat from the condition resolution, 1 WP from the exceptional success, and your choice of one of the benefits of another exceptional success.

                No Sleepers observed the spell, but I'm going to rule that it's obvious magic. In 2E, that means it is subject to Dissonance at the end of the scene (compared to Unraveling of spells due to Disbelief in 1E, which was an extended roll beginning as soon as Sleepers observed vulgar magic). Still, if a Sleeper witnesses the obvious effects of the spell, it constitutes a breaking point for them (p. 298) and an act of hubris for you (p. 88).


                OOC: I would like to pause the action for this scene and see if Binder chooses to follow or not. He could very easily catch up to you (and sense the new spell via periphery Sight) before you head to the Iris, if he chooses to. If he doesn't comment in the next few days, we'll assume for now that he stays on the train or follows at a distance.

                You don't see any movement at or around the door, but it's pretty dark to tell. As far as you know, it's quiet, too.

                            BINDER
                            Active Spells: Locate Object (on Moonstone and Pheonix), Incognito Presence (self)
                            Willpower: 4/7

                            Feeling the weight of the Moonstone's words, Binder weighs his options as the two mages run off into the unnatural hail. He'd been hoping to follow them under cover of the crowd, but it seemed like the crowd wasn't going anywhere. He knew another way to follow them unnoticed, but the effort may begin to look less like careful curiosity and more like ill-intentioned stalking.

                            I do want to check out that door, Binder rationalizes to himself, so I'll do that, and if it happens that they're doing the same, then it's a happy coincidence, isn't it?

                            Binder begins building a pair of Imagos. First, he wants to be overlooked by any bystanders, to have a little more freedom in how he looks and behaves. Again, he focuses on the knife in his bag, drawing some stability from the imprint of his nimbus.

                            Casting Incognito Presence (Mind 2)
                            2 Free Reach; Spending for instant speed, Advanced Duration, and 1 beyond spell control
                            Attempting to contain: Paradox 1 + 1 (prior risk) - 2 (dedicated tool) = 0 -> 0 successes!

                            Spellcasting: Gnosis 1 + Mind 3 + Willpower 3 = 7 -> 1 success.
                            Potency 1, Adv Duration Week


                            The spell settles over him, and Binder pulls the knife from his bag with the confidence that no one will notice anything strange. He carefully moves the knife around his body, visualizing it cutting the threads that bind him to conventional space and distance.

                            Casting Ground-Eater (Space 1)
                            2 Free Reach; Spending for instant speed, Advanced Duration, and 2 beyond spell control
                            Attempting to contain: Paradox 2 + 2 (prior risk) - 2 (dedicated tool) = 2 -> 0 successes!

                            Spellcasting: Gnosis 1 + Space 2 + Yantra 1 + Willpower 3 - (+2 Potency) 4 = 3 -> 0 successes.


                            Despite his effort, Binder feels the spell slip from his grasp and wither away before it can take hold.

                            Ugh, he thinks, a little winded. Really don't feel like doing that again. Well, nevermind.

                            Binder heads out through the hail, towards the strange door, relying on his spell to keep him safe from notice.

                                                            Ragnar
                                                            You are intimately familiar with your own Awakening, and simply talking about magical names evokes that memory keenly for you. Your experiences at the Watchtower of the Lunargent Thorn on the Path to Arcadia taught you that names have meaning through their symbolism -- or perhaps, symbols give names meaning. Thus, you spoke your True Name, and thus Ragnar became true.

                                                            Based on the intuitive knowledge you have gleaned through your Awakening, you know that names build connections, and magic can manipulate or modify that connection to a number of ends. You suspect that creating a new magical identity might help a mage create symbolism, a new symbolism than their given name, and thus create other connections.


                                                            Binder and Phoenix
                                                            You study the wall, but don't perceive anything of note with your mundane vision. However, on the periphery of your True Sight, you feel something calling to you -- and it's very close by.

                                                            The street appears empty at the moment. No one else seems to want to be out in this freak storm.

                                                            You can choose to activate your Active Sight. For anyone who has spent more than a few turns with Active Sight on this scene, that will require a Willpower to make it stay on. You can choose to use Focused Mage Sight and attempt Revelation or Scrutiny at Opacity 5 (p. 92-93) if you so choose, but remember that it's a non-reflexive action, so doing so would end any concentration-based spells.


                                                            Moonstone
                                                            There it stands -- right there, as if crawling out of the brick wall. To your Active Sight, it's plain as day: a powerful spatial warp, which seems to claw at the Fallen World like a living creature. Its borders are moving, but you couldn't begin to try and describe the way it moves in any English words you know. Colors, and a blackness dark as night, darker than the ice falling from the sky, swirl around the door.

                                                            A wise part of you knows that if you study the door more closely, using your Sight, you might be able to learn more. But the door has a weight of age to it, a sharp quality, that suggests it may be far beyond your current knowledge. Then again, you suspect that you wouldn't be the first mage to try something that others deemed impossible.

                                                            You hear that same quiet, throaty laugh that you heard earlier. It seems to emanate from the mystical doorway. A part of you wants to scream. Another part of you wants to laugh, to cry, to move closer.

                                                            You may use Focused Mage Sight attempt Revelation or Scrutiny at Opacity 5, if you so choose. Don't forget that using Scrutiny costs 1 WP to activate.

                                                            I would also like you to roll Resolve + Composure.
