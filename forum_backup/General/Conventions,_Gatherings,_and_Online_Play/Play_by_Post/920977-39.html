                I activated Time Armor at the beginning of the current scene:

                Originally posted by Caladriu View Post
                All he needed was Time. Time to stop and breathe and think, that's all he's needed since Hakim died, and he hadn't gotten it except for a few precious minutes with Phoenix and Binder- where was Binder?

                Okay. So he can't step outside of Time. But he can make it work for him. He knows the ways.

                Spending 1 Mana to activate Time Armor. Moonstone is still intangible and can only be targeted by Supernal magic (per Shadow Flesh), but when he's out of the shadow he'll have Defense 9 that applies against firearms-like attacks.
                I went back and checked the sheet in this thread, Moonstone should have base Defense 7, not 6. I'll fix that today.

                As for Dodging: I always assumed that for Mage Armor you add the bonus before doubling Defense because that would be the simpler interpretation, but I can't find a ruling either way. Pre-errata, Space Armor alone specified that you add its bonus after doubling Defense, but that line was removed in the final copy. But according to the errata document KKV linked on the last page, the reason for that change was "redundancy."

                Possibly notable: the Time 3 spell "Acceleration", one dot higher than Mage Armor, has this effect among others:
                Acting in such accelerated time makes the subject very hard to
                hit, but only as long as he is able to concentrate; his Defense
                does not change, but add Potency to Defense before doubling
                it for Dodge actions (p. 217). He may employ Defense (and
                Dodges) against firearms.
