    Max looked understandably abashed. "Ah. R-right. Thanks for the tips." He scratched at the back of his neck, flaking off some dried mud. "R-Really didn't know what I was doing, there..."

    'He's right. I've gotta be confident about this. At least, confident enough.' He took a deep breath, trying to borrow Nate's evident calm. "Okay." At the last question, he hesitated, and then said, "Well, k-kind of... both. I-I want you to take Jessica with Summernickel down the Hedge path, and find a way out from there. A-And you need to go quick. Like S-Summernickel said, catching him by surprise helps, s-so the longer you wait..." Max shudders. "I need to get to the end of my own path. I-I'm willing to bet my own life on this, but I'm n-not willing to bet anyone else's. I... I can't stop you if you want to come with me, but..." The pig's eyes settled on the ox, slung over Nate's shoulder. "J-Jessica's still out like a light. And you're the only one strong enough to carry her." He was being honest, but felt a little manipulative here, and his discomfort showed. "I n-need to trust you to get her out of here, okay? I don't know what's down there, but I know you can take it. "

    "Get yourself out, too. Heh, and k-keep that promise you made me when you do." Max forced a smile. "I sh-should see you again. A-A-And if my plan doesn't work, it... I'm glad I met you both."

                Max gave a sad smile to Nate's first response. "J-Just... The way it has to be." He was doing well at keeping himself together, he thought; he kept back the tears. 'Gotta put those aside.' He gulped. "W-We made different deals. That's all. I-I have to go." The little pig still shook a little, but was determined not to let that show, either. He looked bashful at the talk of his plan, murmuring, "I-It's noth--" before catching himself. 'Confidence, remember. And not just for you.' One cough later, he corrected himself: "Something. I-It's something."

                Max had a hollow laugh of his own when Nate suggested he was strong enough to take Jessica. "M-Maybe you didn't notice. W-When she ran, I did so little -- n-not the Wilds, just me." He then decided he probably wasn't alone in needing some encouragement. "L-Look at you, though. Sh-She'd have j-just dragged me with her. But y-you're stronger than you think. B-Better than you think. I-I hesitated, when she ran, but you jumped right to her. I-I trusted you, and you proved why I should."

                Then Max chuckled at the comment of rescue. "I don't doubt. Not for a minute." Though paired with the hug, Nate's next words left him speechless. The Pig's mouth moved, but nothing came out. 'That's not true. You are better than you think. We all brought something. We wouldn't have made it here alone.'* He believed it, but it was like his throat was swollen, as eyes watered. He could at least return the hug. Finally, he managed to croak out something. "... Th-Thank you."

                The advice and guidance was very welcome. Max had had no idea what was up there, but knowing Nate had faced it helped. Not that he knew how he'd get past Aries, but the advance notice for planning was huge. The piglet smiled up at the Goat. "Thanks. That... That does help a lot. I-I might just make it through, now." As Nate turned, he nodded and added. "R-Right on through. It'll go a-as far as the Wilds could manage. J-Just... Keep respecting them like you promised, a-and they should still help where they can."

                Then there wasn't much left, but the last goodbyes. 'Just for now,' Max told himself. "Summernickel, th-thanks for c-coming to help, a-and for saving my neck earlier. Nate... Th-Thank you for everything. T-Tell J-Jessica the s-same, a-alright?" He cleared his throat. "G-Good luck. And good-- No, not goodbye. Just... S-See you after all this is over." And with a smile, Max turned, beginning his ascent toward Aries.

                Only to pause in his tracks at Jessica's voice. 'No... No, not now...' Max turned to look back behind him, bewildered. His throat made an odd sound of disbelief as he processed what she'd asked; at another time it might have amused him, but things were just too heavy for him to know how to respond to it. In any case, his mind wildly changed gears from clever plans for getting past Aries to clever plans for keeping Nate and Jessica safe. And this little piggy had none. "I-I..." He took a breath to get a grip on himself. And decided to just deny that anything had changed, re: the plan. "I-I'm glad to s-see you w-wake up before I head out, J-Jessica." That, at least, was genuine, as was the smile that joined it (tinged with his worry as it was). "G-Go with Nate and S-Summernickel to the H-Hedge. Please." There was more yearning there than he meant. "Th-Thank you for... For everything. I-I'll see you all l-later."

                Max felt cold. Very cold. There was more he wanted to say to Jessica, more he felt his friend deserved to hear if this didn't work. But he'd barely kept himself together with Nate. One more round and his nerves would be shot completely. And so, wiping with his muddy hand at the few tears that had finally slipped past him, he took off at a run toward Aries, and the path the Collector had picked for him.

                * Which, given "here" was a muddy hole with a giant monstrous pig indifferent to whether it ate them, was likely best unsaid.

                                    Max had put only some small distance between himself and the others, and then stopped to try to get a hold of himself. 'Calm... Get calm... C'mon, you can't be all panicked and fidgety. Not.. Not until it's over.' Though that wasn't the only reason he stopped. The little pig left a floppy ear cocked in the direction he'd come. He heard the steps of four hooves and two feet. Moving away from him. '... Guess they listened to me.'

                                    It was bittersweet. Part of Max had hoped all three of them would be facing the end of all this together. He missed them already, snuffling a little and wiping away a few tears. Other parts knew this was selfish: Nate and Jessica reaching the end wouldn't win more than his bargain could, and they deserved to get out if his gambit didn't work. From what Summernickel said, it would do the Collector harm either way. 'This is how it needs to be,' he told himself.

                                    Still, aside the loneliness and the practicality he saw, there was a swelling of pride in the piglet's chest. "They... They trust me... Th-They didn't just say they did. They really... They think I can do this." Whether right or not, Max wasn't sure he'd ever believed anyone felt that sort of confidence in him, but now when he couldn't deny it... He rested a hand against a muddy wall to steady himself. He hadn't realized the weight it could carry. The Pig drank a few deep breaths of the air, his heart rate slowing to something comfortable. He coughed, feeling a little choked up, and wiped away some fresh tears. "S-So I'm gonna," he said, rare determination crossing his face, as he stepped free of the wall and stood as tall as he could.

                                    It was then that Max noticed something. 'When did it get so quiet...?' The disappearance of the great insulting behemoth of a hog had gone unnoticed. He wondered what it meant. Though not for long enough to worry: he became aware of something new, a scent quite a ways ahead. At first he wondered how Nate had gotten up there, but no, definitely not; Nate's musk was just the closest word for it. The poor little piglet then had a brief panic at associating his friend with scent so unthinkingly. He hadn't even noticed. 'H-How human am I actually going to be by the end...?' He shook his head to clear it of those unhelpful thoughts, and started walking. The Pig would find out when he got there. 'The sooner the better, whatever the case... Just... Focus on thinking up how to handle Aries. As long as I don't run into too many more... animal things... It should be okay.'

                                    And so Max gave a frustrated squeal on noticing that his tunnel was narrowing, the roof getting lower. It would force him to all-fours, just as when he'd entered. He considered calling on the Wilds again, but... 'I've asked a lot lately. I needed it then, but now... This would just save some... humiliation. That might insult it. So...' With a resigned grunt, the piglet began crawling through. And tried not to think about how much more natural it felt to move this way than it had before, nor how nice the muddy walls felt brushing against his face as he went. Yeah, Max wasn't having much luck with that.

                                        Sure, the vegetation was thick and difficult to navigate, but this was a welcome respite from the mud pit. And actually, now that he was thinking about it, Nate didn't have the same problems as Jessica with his surroundings : he was smaller and his body was more nimble. Plus, he had passed a contract with the Wilds too, and after having observed Max's way of asking help, he decided to try his hand at it, without the squeals and stutters. He whispered his request, and the plants parted way for him, although he did prick his skin on a few thorns. Before, he would have complained, but now it seemed trivial.

                                        The trio seemed to be mostly silent : it was hard to make small talk after parting with Max and leaving the Pig to his potential death. Which left Nate to focus on his escape... until he became aware of his body's needs. Unlike Jessica and Max, he had been awake for a very long time, running on adrenaline and stamina since... Why, since they had arrived in the ranch! So much had happened since then, and he suspected something magical allowed him to hold on : maybe the laws of his realm, maybe his contracts, or maybe just his new body. He was tired, but the prospect of getting out was beating the need to rest by a long shot.

                                        However... being surrounded by foliage brought another vital need : Nate was starving. His stomach rumbled, as a reminder that his last meal had been the hallucinogenic mushrooms, and they hadn't been really nourishing. He could feel his saliva thickening as the tentalizing smell of the leaves got stronger... I can't just stop and eat them, or I will lose the group. He tried to reject the idea, but the more he thought about, the more eating those leaves made more sense : it'll give him more strength to escape, and it would also honor the Wilds and his animal contract, keeping his word on the both of them! Which is why he finally gave in, grabbing a bunch of stalks and ripping them off to chew while walking to keep up with the Ox and the goblin.

                                        It tasted so wonderful, so green and delicious! His flat teeth were perfect for chewing on them, releasing all the juicy sap in his hungry mouth. The Goat was still enjoying his meal when they stumbled in the clearing, and those glowing leaves caught his yellow eye : they looked perfect. He made his way to them, avoiding the sleeping gobelin, and started to munch on a branch.
