    Nate fell to his knees with a surprised cry, groaning in pain. A thousand insults almost came out of his mouth, but the shock of the attack managed to keep them under control. He grabbed the quill but gasped again, as it seemed to be very comfortable here in his chest, even burying itself a few inches deeper. The Goat then used his two hands, and pulled it out slowly, with another groan. Felt like I was Excalibur's fucking rock...
    The quill now seemed to have found its own ink with the goat's blood. Of course, could it be more twisted? Speaking of twisted, Nate looked as his Keeper, thinking of how his words, and how they could be twisted against him. And maybe it was because of his exhaustion, but the Goat couldn't find any loopholes to his disadventage. It seemed too good. Almost... too easy, which was ironic considering that he just took an arrow in the chest for that.

    He took the quill and paused, breathing deeply to stop himself from shaking. This was a big mistake, he knew that, and he hated himself for pushing his luck that far. But it's too late to turn back now. He then slowly signed the document, after pausing a bit to remember his initials. When did it get so hard to write? Had he forgot already how to do it? Once the deed done, he got back up, and held the quill high so his... Master, apparently, could take it back.

            "Uh, y-yeah," Max nodded, when the Secretary mentioned his Contract of the Wilds. "I'm satisfied with the terms. I don't contest them. I'll-I'll keep to my obligations." He scratched nervously against the side of his snout, cracking free some dirt it had picked up while he was 'keeping to his obligations.' "Er, though, if if there's anything about those terms that weren't explained to me -- anything else I owe or anything else I'm owed -- I'd be glad to understand it better. I-If you please."

            He was also interested in that mention of the Mon-- Derek's Contract. The Pig had been present or informed for all the others described; if there were any more, the Secretary was keeping them to himself for now. Giving up one's 'Family Name and everything associated with it' seemed like a pretty big deal. 'From what Sagittarius said... Wow... I hope the poor guy can still make it out of here.' Max turned a few platforms down to the simian, and gave a look of sympathy. Briefly, at least.

            The Pig got a sense of something ominous going on with the Goat and the Keeper, but he'd split his attention too many ways. He was too distracted by the Secretary to make out any terms discussed. Still, it was hard for a great quill stabbing into his friend's chest to escape him. "Nate!" he half-squealed. Still, after recovering, the Goat signed the scroll he'd been offered. 'Well... Here's hoping that comes out well...'

            Max turned as the other horned ungulate spoke up, almost on the other side of the zodiac platforms. And appreciated that she charged straight to the heart of the matter. Drawing a breath, the Pig decided to back her up. "R-Right. I mean, uh... Only makes sense we'd all be..." His eyes darted to the Keeper, and he chose his word carefully. "... curious about possibilities for leaving. And we haven't all been gathered for a talk like this before, right? S-Seems like a good time to go over things." Max coughed. "On a related note... We've heard some mentions before, but... Seeing we're in a formal setting, could we also go over the contract that brought us here? Like, formally and in-depth?" Another glance at the Keeper. "I-I-I mean, b-best to reassure us all e-everything's in order with the terms." He gave a not-so-convincing smile.

                                    Max's outlook ping-ponged from hopeful, to horrified, and then back to... well, mildly hopeful again. He honestly couldn't believe he'd forgotten about their early ill-fated jailbreak. 'I guess it does feel like it happened ages ago...' Still, even without any sort of real plan, he tried his best to pitch in on defense. "Sh-She's right! If h-he's claiming he got the right to keep us here when we ate the 'food' he gave us," -- the Pig gave a derisive snort -- "then he violated something by k-keeping us here when we tried to leave before that." He didn't think it was just Jessica and himself, either. He'd been overstressed and very out of it in those early days before (and shortly after) the Ranch. But even then, Max was sure he remembered at least a few of the other Zodiacs disappearing from time to time and not generally looking much enriched when he saw them again. He thought the Goat had been among them.

                                    Steeling himself, the little pig tried to reach still further. "And... And given those... f-false pretenses... C-Couldn't that mean any bargains we st-struck afterward were... were..." He managed a glance at Nate, still bleeding from his quill-wound. He drew a breath and forged ahead. "Th-They were made under d-duress. Sh-Shouldn't we be able to void them if we f-felt coerced?" Max didn't have high hopes for this. Aside from the whole 'it would be fair,' thing, coercion didn't usually count for much when it came to bargains and oaths in stories he'd read. Still, a court like this -- mad though it was -- gave it a chance, especially in light of the Keeper's apparent error. All the Pig could do was hold his breath.

                                                            Max naturally flinched at the Keeper's gaze. His heart raced. Well, more than it had been doing since he'd arrived in this room. The poor little Pig was shaking like a leaf. He had been putting together some sort of plan behind that ridiculous face of his, but those eyes on him just dashed his hopes. That look held such wrath, disgust, and condescension toward him. 'And how couldn't he! He's right!' Tears started to well up, even as Jessica and Nate made their requests. 'Even with a chance like this, I can barely stand! I am just a stupid, pathetic little p--!'

                                                            Max stopped mid-thought. With the rude awakening and surprise court proceedings, he'd forgotten something until that moment. A dream he'd had after his meal. It was a bit fractured, but he remembered the spirit. And it gave him something. The swine swallowed, still shaking. He was tempted to match Jessica and Nate's requests, but... No, that felt too easy. Like a trap. Maybe Max was just paranoid, but he couldn't feel comfortable walking into that. 'No... I need something he can't see coming. I've gotta catch him. ... Go big or go home, I guess. Heh,' he thought, without mirth, but with a kind of crazy optimism.

                                                            The Pig's trembling hooves clacked forward on the surface of his platform. "I-I..." he stuttered, keeping his eyes low and head slightly bowed. He snuffled a bit, from the tears. Max hated making that sound. But he made sure he made it loudly, looking as pathetic as he could. "I-I... I-If you w-would allow it... I... I want to... to..." He gulped. "Keeper, I want to r-reset my Contract with you. I-I want to state the new terms myself. A-as though that's what they'd always been. I-If you agree to the new Contract, th-then that'll be that. A-Anyone with t-terms like th-the old ones can switch. P-Please, I..." With a squeal of a sob, Max felt the tears fall and dabbed at his face with a sleeve. "I-I swear it'll say... say th-that you really own me. C-Completely. Heart and... s-soul." He gave another snuffle, the very picture of pathetic. "I... I just want this to stop. B-But I can ask f-for something else, instead..."
