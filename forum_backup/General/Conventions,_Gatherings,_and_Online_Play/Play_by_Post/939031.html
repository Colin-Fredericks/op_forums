    Mountains of Shadow

    The Rocky Mountains and their rural countryside provide a distinct contrast to the growing urbanization around Denver, Colorado. This duality is reflected in the Fallen World, but in many places along the Rockies, the Gauntlet is so razor thin -- or even absent -- that the Shadow and its spirit denizens bleed into the realm of mortals. In and around the Denver metropolitan area, odd fluctuations in the Gauntlet lead to frequent and temporary Irises, and this grand Mystery has been the source of study for quite some time.

    Some Mystagogues claim that these Irises open up not at random times or places, but somehow in response to other events in the region. Thearchs claim that these Irises may be used to their advantage to encourage Awakening among Sleepers. Many mages, regardless of their Order or Path, Obsess over their ability to gain power and influence in the region through their interaction with the Shadow.

    But mages aren't the only supernatural beings with a claim to the Shadow. The Uratha, or werewolves, claim this realm as their birthright and defend its natural order with tooth and nail. Some werewolves and mages create alliances, usually grudgingly or temporarily, and set aside their differences to stop a spirit invasion. Others are outright hostile and will attack other supernatural beings on sight. But many of the rest are cautious, choosing to study the other warily before creating unnecessary risk by inviting retaliation.

    In a city where danger can strike and Mysteries can be found at any time, across multiple realms, who's to say what's safe?

          Prologue: “Ignorance is Bliss”

          You have Awakened.

          The past few weeks have been the most bizarre, but probably the most intensely rewarding, of your lives. It’s not just because the actual experience of Awakening is incredibly strange but inspiring for each mage who survives the process. Since having your eyes opened to the Lie that you have always known, you’ve learned an all-important truth.

          Ignorance is bliss – but so is power.

          For many newly Awakened mages, learning the extent – and limits – of your new power often results in a fair bit of stumbling. You have experimented, tested, and pushed your fledgling control, and in doing so, have learned some of the basics of willworking. It can be a messy process, and even lethal for some, especially in Denver. Through your experimentation, for different reasons and in different ways, you were fortunate enough to stumble across the path of another mage. They found you, realized the implications of being the first to find you, and quickly introduced you to a whole society of other mages living right there in your own community.

          Awakening tends to happen in spurts; in your case, the four of you all gained your new magical abilities within a few days of each other, and found your way to other mages after the first week. Through some twist of fate – or perhaps Fate? It’s never easy to tell with these folks – you were each discovered by a member of The Band.

          The Band is a cabal of five mages who have chosen their cabal and Shadow Names around the central theme of music, using it as a metaphor and symbol of how they choose to weave their magic in this world. The Band shepherded you together and explained the critical social nature of a Denverite mage’s reality: Thrive in numbers, or die in isolation.

          First, you learned about the Orders. Each of you reflected on your calling, your values, and your goals. You were approached by several different mages of the Pentacle Orders, eager to recruit new mages to further their own agendas. Eventually, you found the one that best suited you, and you completed your first true rite of passage by completing the initiation tasks set by each Order.

          For the Council of Free Assemblies, or the Free Council, this process was quite straightforward. You were invited for an interview with several of the leading members and asked a series of seemingly simple questions: Who are you? Why do you wish to join the Free Council? Where do you see yourself in five years? And so on. After hearing your responses, in the spirit of democracy, they voted on whether to admit you to their Order. In your case, they said yes.

          The Mysterium, on the other hand, uses a layered initiation. Your first task, to gain entry into the Order and access to the most basic information stored in their Athenaea, was also simple: Share a piece of knowledge you gained from your Awakening. The Order leader who initiated you pressed you for knowledge, challenged what you shared, and ultimately decided that you had the necessary qualities to partake in – and contribute to – their collective knowledge. Further levels of initiation, they explained, would come with time when they deemed you ready.

          With your Order as your new home, you were thrown into studies, drilled on the basics of Paradox, mana, High Speech, and other vital pieces of occult knowledge. Members of The Band who served in your Order brought you under their wing and instructed you. For each of you, this process emphasized basic knowledge about the greater Mysteries of Denver, and the tension between mages and the Uratha, the werewolves. Learning that information, that werewolves and other things really do go bump in the night, affects everyone differently. For most, though, seeing pictures of other mages who have literally been ripped to pieces is a pretty sobering and humbling experience.

          After learning these basic skills, your mentors instructed you to form a cabal. Seek out other newly Awakened mages, they explained, and form a group that will become your new family. Pick people you feel like you can trust, they told you, because few other bonds in this world are stronger and tighter than those of a cabal. And so you found each other. You’ve only just met each other, although some of you think you’ve crossed paths before in your life. Was that a twist of fate, too, or a Supernal calling?

          Now that you are ready – at least in your mentors’ eyes – to take off your training wheels, you’ve been called together by The Band with a request for your aid...

              Chapter 1: “Howl in the Night”
              Saturday, September 3, 2016. Mid-afternoon.

              Spider and Ouroboros Ophis
              You are in downtown Denver, just about to walk into a meeting with your Order mentor. Melody, a bubbly and amicable African American woman probably in her late-30’s, is an Obrimos Libertine. She works as a researcher in the Department of Physics at the University of Colorado-Denver. Melody has tried to make herself as available as possible to answer any questions you might have, and she is always downright pleasant and approachable when seeing her in person. However, she does have this tendency to take a while to get back to your texts or calls unless it’s an emergency. When she contacts you, though, she expects a prompt response. Any time you’ve been slow to get back to her, she politely asks you at length what took so long, and comments on the fact that she’s really busy.

              You aren’t sure what the urgency is about this time, but she asked you to come to campus on a short notice, and requested that you meet her in her office in the academic building. The two of you meet up outside. It’s a bright, sunny day, and campus is pretty busy with students and faculty roaming about the streets nearby.


              Dr. Luck and Simurgh
              You’ve been called to a meeting with your Order mentor. Upon initiation, you received a special guest pass that can be used to take a special elevator to a lower level of the Denver Central Library. The passes are only given out to Mystagogues or mages invited as special guests to Order events; the elevator itself is under constant sympathetic surveillance to ensure privacy.

              You’re heading down the hallway to the private office of Cadence. He is a middle-aged Caucasian man in his late 40s or early 50s. He’s generally a pretty nice guy and has been a treasure trove of knowledge. He has this tendency to get off track when he is very excited about something, which is endearing but means it takes forever for you to get a word in edgewise when he’s on a roll.

              The two of you meet just after getting off the elevator. Cadence has made it clear that you’re welcome to discuss anything related to Awakened society or magic that you need to once you get off the elevator or anywhere on the lower level, although he has encouraged you to speak about Mysteries and cabal matters in private.

                                                      Dr. Luck
                                                      With adrenaline still pumping through your body from your vigorous workout, you find the first hour of magical practice to be difficult. You do everything right, as you've been trained, but your High Speech is just a little off, and you struggle to form your Imago. Not one to give up easily, you attempt again in earnest, and this time the words roll off your tongue with ease.

                                                      The divination ritual grants you a tiny glimpse of the future in vague, moving swirls of color surrounding the briefest flashes of clarity. After a pause, a voiceless sensation fills you: No. And yet...you can't help but feel like the answer is less firm than usual, somehow. What does that mean?

                                                      You technically can ask 1 more question because you have potency 2, due to your Time 2 arcanum. Because you didn't reach for more thorough responses, this is all you get.

                                                      Because it's an exceptional success, you choose one of the bonuses on p. 115. The most relevant is probably 1 bonus potency (so you can ask a third question for free), or gain a positive condition that can help you and give an Arcane Feat.

                                                      Feel free to have any conversation you like between you and Simurgh before moving on.


                                                      Spider and Ouroboros Ophis
                                                      "Wow!" Melody's reaction of admiration is genuine, evident from the beaming smile on her face. "I have to say, that's pretty ambitious for someone so new to..."

                                                      Melody trails off and looks vexed as she studies the door. "Sorry, but I just always feel so paranoid when I'm talking about those kind of things without complete privacy," she explains.

                                                      Melody's eyes narrow briefly; suddenly, both of your peripheral senses cause you to feel a strange sense of something supernatural happening in the room. When Melody speaks again, the room sounds quieter somehow. "There. That should keep all of the sound completely here between us."

                                                      Melody sighs. "I asked you to come here because I have a favor to ask. And I'm sorry in advance because I know how boring and tedious it will be, but I am just swamped here with work." She sighs again, more heavily this time.

"There is a Sleepwalker who works for me -- well, technically for the Order, but she's a personal friend of mine -- named Jill McCann. She's been working on a project that includes collecting info about some spirit-related issues that I'm sure someone else in The Band could tell you more about -- Cadence will talk your ear off if you let him, trust me, and I just don't have time for it. But basically, I need you to pick up some documents and bring them to our next meeting.
