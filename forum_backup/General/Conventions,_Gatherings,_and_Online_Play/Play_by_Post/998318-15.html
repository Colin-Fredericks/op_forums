    The Firestorm ends. The awful sizzling in his stomach and the back of his throat fades. Adonis looks over at Scout, who has fallen to his knees, his arms outstretched. Wait, did he... stop it? But before Adonis can say anything, he hears a voice in his head, calm and soft but loud at the same time. "YOU ARE STRONGER THAN YOU KNOW, JUST AS STRONG AS THEY ARE. BUT YOUR STRENGTH IS DIFFERENT. YOU WILL REALIZE ITS SOURCE IN TIME."

    The Firestorm was a crucible, and he was fired in it. He went into it made fully of gold, but the gold is receding, with some other metal that he does not yet know forming underneath. But he senses that, once he masters Aurum, it will show itself. But already he feels... firmer. More confident. A Refinement of strength. Cuprum? Ferrum? Stannum? Aes?

    He sees Mark 5 shift uneasily. He looks like he's about to slink away, and Adonis reaches out a hand and grabs him by the arm, and then gives him a long hard look. "Stay. We've had enough trouble trying to find you once today." His voice doesn't rise above a whisper, but it is firmer than it has ever been, especially towards either Named.

    Meanwhile, Earth seems bound and determined to make at least one person hate them today. Fuck, Earth. Do you always have to be at somebody's throat? He walks over and all but butts Earth out of the way, placing himself between the policewoman and the Fury. "Yes, we're fine, officer. Don't mind her, she's always pissy and she's rattled from the storm." He flashes the cadet his trademark grin.

    He's done flinching at the site of Earth's fist crackling with lightning. He doesn't act it, but his body isn't just for display. There are human men who would envy his physical prowess, and he's had to punch his way out of a couple tight spots before. It's time he started acting like it.

    Adonis will take three dots of the Brutal Force Style Merit. He's also going to try and sweet talk the police officer to go away.

    Manipulation 3 + Persuasion 3 = 6 dice -> yowza, nothing.

    Rolling to see if she suffers Disquiet. Azoth 2 = 2 dice -> nothing.

    So she's not leaving, but she's not freaking out, either. Might try to hit her with Lure on the next post.

Maybe she's following reports of him across Canada. Will he have to leave Winnipeg for a while? I ran because I was young and scared. A meat cleaver didn't exactly help matters, either. But that was before he had a Throng on his side. I'd love to see her try and take on Mark. Or better yet, Earth. The thought of Earth visiting her usual brand of vicious on his Demiurge was very satisfying.

                                                        But that didn't change the fact that he knew next to nothing about Mark Austin. Sharon had only mentioned the names of the bodies she had maimed and the man she had murdered towards the end, as Disquiet gripped her more and more. Apparently, Mark had been an actor, a teen heartthrob on some soap opera who had basically disappeared after his big show got cancelled. So he lived by himself working some dead-end job until Sharon showed up at his door and killed him. He needed a way to find out more about "himself."

                                                        Well, uh- Katerina..." He gave her another big smile and gave her his contact information. He then leans in and whispers, "If I could ask a favor Katerina, don't tell any of your friends you bumped into me today, alright? It'll be just our little secret." Then he gave her a wink.

                                                        Adonis is going to try and make her keep quiet. Manipulation 3 + Persuasion 3 + Lure 2 + Famous Face 1 = 9 dice -> holy crap, 3 successes on a single dice! That's another Exceptional Success and the Swooning Condition all over again. I am so glad I decided to Calcify Eros. Hoping to try out Assimilate and Stalker, and Eris for that matter, in the next chapter.

                                                            The Throng

                                                            Katerina almost shudders as the Muse's- Mark's -lips brush her ear, and he slips a piece of paper into a hand that reflexively curls around it to brush his fingers. A little higher up, and she would have felt the ridges of his stitching that attached a pop star's talented hands to the sculpted arms of a heartthrob actor. Adonis might be prettier than the rest of them, his sutures cleaner, but he's as much a patchwork as Regret... maybe even more so. The police cadet nods vigorously, and beams a smile when he offers a wink.

                                                            "Yes, of course! Take all the time you need, I don't want to hold you up. Besides, you need your privacy, right? That's why you never did anything after your show."

                                                            She walks off to fetch a roll of yellow police tape to mark off the street corner torn apart by a Firestorm, and what is left of the bodies of the men that Mark 5 tore limb from limb. The street has gone quiet, the crowd is already dispersing, except for the drunk, lying on the pavement, propped up on one arm, cursing and spitting and weeping just a little. Some people offer Earth a suspicious look or two, but most don't care. Whatever animosity they had for her seems... lessened. Scout wanders ahead, muttering to himself, out of sight, but not out of mind, easily close enough for his potent Azothic Radiance to butt up against theirs. Apart from Katerina, absent-mindedly, as if in a dream, following procedure, the street is empty now.

                                                            Five Pilgrims standing still on a road, looking for a way forwards.

                                                            There are sirens getting closer. The paramedics will be here soon. Time to get out of the area and find themselves a secluded place to heal.

Everyone can make a Wits + Streetwise roll to find an out of the way source of electricity.
