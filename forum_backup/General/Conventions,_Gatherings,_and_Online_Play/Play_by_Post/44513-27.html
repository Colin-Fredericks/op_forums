                            -The Geherin are a race of bloodthirsty cannibals

                            Previous Geherin Rumors
                            Unconfirmed (At least one true)
                            -Horses have the same civil rights as humans in Geherin.

                            -The Geherin are secretly run by a Lunar Anathema.

                            -The Geherin are a species of sentient, bipedal plant.
