    Should I start formatting posts differently? It feels like my points are being lost. Most of the last one was explaining how Craft wouldn't be gamebreaking and giving you levers to reign it in.

    I think the letters I have written clearly demonstrate I am not just here to break the game. Pre-human relations, the Realm's cultural lionization of warriors at the expense of artifice and sorcery, the squalor of the Second Age compared to the Shogunate, Akashan's political marriage, the struggle for the soul of House Ledaal, and the like all show that I don't just want to churn out Five-Metal Shrikes until I am god-emperor of Creation.

    Crafting is a complex subsystem that does not reward dabbling. Especially DB crafting where all their native magic does is break things. (1) It was apparently balanced around the assumption that it gives you backgrounds without spending XP and so is filled with mechanical and lore speedbumps. I have finally gotten over most of them and it has taken politics and adventure to do so.

    However! I was trying to set myself up to facilitate other people's ambitions. I wanted to start working on sorcery artifacts for Aisa, Gunzosha armor for the conspiracy's troops (2), etc. My work in the Imperial City is because I assume there is going to be a fight there with conspiracy members and their retainers on one side and various other factions on the other.

    One person cannot attune to an infinite amount of artifacts or even use all that many even if they could. None of the military skyships I am able to craft are particularly threatening on a strategic scale except for moving troops I don't have. None of the ships really come with guns. What is more, all the other factions have more Dragonblooded! The conspiracy is small and we need force multipliers if we are going to win on anything other than Imperial Manse fiat. The allies the others have been gathering are part of that. I was hoping to be another.


    What are your setting and mechanical assumptions regarding artifacts so I can know what would make you comfortable?


    (1) An exaggeration, but not much of one.
    (2) Since I don't have any and have made no effort to get an army or navy that is personally loyal to me

                                    I suspect PMs are for Resplendent Wood now; I just need to write up what I already had going with wonderandawe for an Ascending Wood letter and then all three of us (there are now three of us, after all) will be caught up.

                                    Right! I needed to write a thing for you!

                                    NPCs:
                                    • Francesco Contarini - Carmela's great-grandfather. Patriarch of House Contarini, a family of Threshold Dragon-Bloods, merchants and bankers and traders and financiers and generally people who sully themselves with money. They are not Imperial unless you squint real hard, but they do constantly poach whatever qualified people they can find and have a number of Imperial Dragons in their lineage. Francesco has ambitions of using the Realm Civil War to put his family into Imperial House levels of wealth and influence.
                                    • Nemias Contarini (Iselsi Nemias) - Carmela's father, an exiled Iselsi who married into the Contarini family. May or may not still be an All-Seeing Eye agent, but either way still maintains a certain degree of loyalty and contacts regarding the Realm. Sent Carmela to look after...
                                    • Geno Husumori - Magistrate of the Pahua Basin, together with his Archons HE IS THE JUSTICE. A Lost Egg who came from the Legions, and was appointed by the Empress to BE THE JUSTICE. Carmela's ally; technically her army actually belongs to him, and she is just the commander. It is important that this be so, otherwise people would ask hard questions like "why is a Threshold Dragon playing warlord on our northern shore? And why have we not killed her yesterday?"
                                    • Geno Jin - Husumori's nephew, a Bright Young Man(tm) who is Carmela's rival for the position of the Magistrate's favorite. Silly boy thinks it's a competition. At least he's good in bed. Was until recently betraying intelligence on the both of them (for entirely good and just reasons) to...
                                    • Sorcerer Bandit - An ambiguously-revolutionary ambiguously-dragonblooded sorcerer that has been building an army of demons and ne'er-do-wells and constantly preying on the region. Carmela's all set to get answers from him on what his deal is, because as of the previous update she shattered his army and captured him! Is either abetted or hindered by...
                                    • Purplesid - Can't remember what their deal is. Carmela can't, that is. I've got a few ideas.
                                    • Peleps Lili - Carmela's second-in-command. An old, drunk, bitter woman who thinks the Realm's ideals and Immaculate doctrines are a load of crap (but she has flaws, too). A damn fine officer, though.
                                    • Tough Moli - A mortal monk of the Immaculate Order, long-suffering because all the Dragons from Houses have deserted and she's intensely lacking in resources. Or was, until Carmela offered her assistance. Thoroughly competent. Carmela is totally not gradually corrupting her.
                                    • Reaches the Sky - A Lost Egg Air Aspect Dragon-blooded monk of the Immaculate Order. Young as the spring dew. Carmela is totally corrupting him. Taught her how to cut spirits in twain, the two of them teamed up to purge disease gods from the refugee camps in the Pahua Basin.
                                    • Ragara Saveria - Ryo's wife! Currently dealing with Carmela, is providing food and supplies to help manage the refugee situation in exchange for refugee labor. Pragmatic and sharp as a tack.
                                    • Tachi-Kun - The God of Central Warfare. Possibly(?) Carmela's new best bud?
                                    Future plans include
                                    • Convince House Contarini and associates that playing blockade-runner to save Quelaana from Mnemon's fleet is anything remotely approaching a good idea.
                                    • Use magisterial, immaculate and espionage connections to help out the remaining Iselsi on the Isle
                                    • Figure out if the Sorcerer Bandit was indeed behind the whole poisoning/assassination plot, or if the rabbit hole goes deeper than that
                                    • Continue making nice with Tachi-Kun and giving him GRAND IDEAS so he won't expose the conspiracy to the Gold Faction
                                    • Build, train and equip her army of refugees, ne'er-do-wells, mercenaries and rag-tags such that a proper Imperial Legion won't just laugh at them.
                                    • Use them to engage in GLORIOUS COMBAT and hopefully not die horribly.
                                    • Figure out how to get a slave collar on Mnemon for... reasons
