                            There was something about Hollidane's attitude that hit a nerve in Yi. Even the months since his "retirement" from Lookshy it was still a little raw. He knew, intellectually, that it wasn't meant in quite the same way it normally had been. Even so, his mouth proved faster than his brain.

                            "Noble one, I am sorry to hear you have been so misinformed about me. I am not a madman as I should be be to make a request so outrageously above my station. I merely wish your permission to assume the mantle of Archon that I might lead Novalesk into a time of glory such as has not been seen since the first age of the world came crashing down and all was given over to sorrow. Or become first slave of the state and work myself two-thirds to death trying to see it survives until Calibration. I am afraid I can offer no guarantees about the second though, I am but a man doing as best he is able."

                                "So you can be self-flagellating and arrogant at the same time. Who knew?" Hollidane says with a half-smile and raised brows. With a soft ahh, she reaches to a nearby set of tools, and grabs a set of wooden tongs. Sticking them into her patient, she extracts a fleshy mass the size of a small apple. An orderly steps in and begins the process of stitching up the patient as Hollidane walks over to a smoldering brazier.

                                "By the grace of Sextes Jylis, I cast you out!" She exclaims as she drops the mass into the flame. Despite the low heat, the fleshy mass is consumed by the fire almost instantly.

                                "Now, if I were to believe that you would so selflessly take up the mantle of supreme leadership for our nation, and believe that you're sleeping with my sister, the former Archon, entirely coincidentally, " she says, punctuating her point with the bloody tongs still in her hands, "It still doesn't answer the question of why you're here to see me."

                                            Yi look around for a chair to sit in, before catching himself with the knowledge that this wasn't Hollidane's office. The General staff might sometimes commission extremely competent carpenters to make their chairs uncomfortable without looking like they were designed that way, but at least they had them. Standing it was then, though it made him feel like a novice again.

                                            "First, I was a metic. Any realistic chances of me actually being accepted as a member of the Legion are gone at this point.

                                            Second, I am fairly certain that particular issue will matter almost not at all. Everyone will recognize why it occurred. Furthermore, I must be up front that I consider my duty to be toward Novalesk as a whole rather than Gens Origenes in particular. I assume every Archon should, in theory, feel the same. If that is the case, Gens should not matter.

                                            Third, the Gens will be too busy make money hand over fist to bother. I have discussed my domestic policy with several of the representatives here in the city and they are all in favor of the mandates of prosperity I suggested.

                                            Fourth, neither the Realm nor Lookshy can effectively project power here. Even if they could, I don't think any of the thousand dooms they are dealing with are going anywhere. Their local proxies are never going to ally and even if they did, I have it on good authority that the Geherin will soon be entering a much stronger relationship with us. The balance of power will be maintained.

                                            Fifth, I am not clear exactly how. I remain human, as much as you or Artemia at any rate, and the only requirements I know of are non-divine and Citizen.

                                            Sixth, I am entirely confident that if Artemia decided to kill me that the legally obligated bodyguards, or your sister, would deal with her before you even heard of it. Assuming my winning personality and self-flagellating arrogance don't win her over first anyway.

                                            Seventh, if such a relationship develops between your sister and me that issue will be dealt with if and when it comes up.

                                            Eighth, that part of the process was not explained to me, and if it so great an issue you could merely offer your belief in my noble intentions to someone ho isn't the sister of my landlord. And I'm not that old..."
