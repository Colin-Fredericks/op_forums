    Mage Sight: Peripheral
    Active Spells: None
    Conditions/Tilts: Inspired, Steadfast
    Health: OOOOOOOO I WP: 3/4 I Mana: 10/10 I Wisdom: 7
    Beats: 0/5 I Arcane Beats: 2/5

    At Lynx's words, Ragnar grabs handfuls of flowers and starts shoving them into his mouth. The taste is bland, and he can't help but quip, "Could use some thousand island." But then the mana hits him all at once, and he's suddenly on something a thousand times better than a sugar rush. Hell, probably better than cocaine. He hadn't realized just how drained he had felt, how long he had been running at less-than-full for. He feels powerful. The task of ending this threat to the Sleepers, of fighting Alux, feels trivial for a brief moment. He can do seven impossible things before breakfast. Despite the seriousness of the situation, he can't help but hum a song as they jog in Moonstone's wake.

    But Ragnar's mood of elation is dampened as they move deeper into the neighborhood, and the dandelions grow thicker and thicker. ​People are going to die. If they haven't already.

    "I don't know how you put up with me. You all see it too, right?"

    "It's a mystery the whole Consilium struggles with," he says with a smirk, but he's not hostile. He looks up at the mass of ghosts, still aided by Ophis' spell. "It's like... tuna going after a school of sardines or something. They're darting in and out. But they're avoiding the center of it. Look how they jerk away, not towards the sardines but away from something. Like tuna do with... sharks..."

    Intelligence 4 + Occult 3 = 7 dice -> 3 successes.

        Everyone
        As long as you are not under any time constraints, you may ignore mana costs for spells or other expenses while outside, as you take time to eat more tass.


        Moonstone
        Yes, you were definitely mind-whammied. The realization makes you irritated. But rather than let your emotions roll over you like a tidal wave, you channel mana (of which there is more readily available) into your Pattern and reinforce your mental fortitude.

        Being a Mage sure is convenient.

        With your increased clarity of mind, you try to evaluate the situation to better understand this puzzle. You have never seen anything like this, not with so many spirits and ghosts in one place. But by watching them feed, you are confident that the cloud itself is simply the consequence of so much mana being released in this area. It's the end result of this Mystery, not the source. You think that if you can identify the true root of this problem, the Essence cloud -- and thus the ephemeral entities feeding on it -- will most likely leave.


        Phoenix
        Your spell takes hold, and the street lights up like lightning. The arcane energy coursing through here is blinding. How could you not have seen it before?

        You've never actually seen a ley line before, but with your spell, you see how the Supernal energy forms what reminds you of a power cable humming with energy within the street. Where the Essence cloud hovers, the cable bulges and crackles with energy. The "dandelions" feed into the line, transmitting even more energy, creating a surge of power that's hard to look at because of its intensity.

        More curiously, though, you see ripples of energy from each dandelion streaming toward a certain area on the street...in fact, they're coming from a single house. Each individual strand is razor-thin, but there are thousands upon thousands of them.

        Turning back to the group, and Moonstone specifically, you have that sensation like someone's watching you. Opening your Sight reveals the ephemeral creature floating near Moonstone. You've never seen something like it before, and don't know what to make of it.

        Your Forces Sight does not reveal anything particularly exciting, just showing that the electricity is still running in these houses nearby. But to your Prime Sight, the Supernal becomes visible here and the heavens open up to you. The dandelions are absolutely a form of tass, and they must be leaking Essence by converting the mana and releasing it into the air. You also see clearly now that there is no active spell holding the Essence cloud in formation or hanging over the ephemeral entities here. The effects you are seeing do not seem to be due to a specific spell.

        There are so many options to pursue, so many thoughts and ideas going through your mind, that you feel overwhelmed. You're just a group of novice mages. How are you supposed to be able to figure this out yourselves?


        Take a beat and the following condition:

        Perplexed. You feel confused and unsure about what to do because your mental focus is wavering. You take -2 on Mental rolls. You also take twice as long to complete extended actions for Mental skills. Whenever the Storyteller asks you to roll for a Mental action, instead of rolling you may voluntarily fail the roll and resolve this Condition.

        Resolution: Opt to fail a Mental action, as described above.

        Beat: N/A.


        Binder
        While you eat a not-so-tasty dandelion and feel the mana course through you, you have a thought: If eating these flowers gives you mana, and they wilt and lose that mana shortly after being plucked from the ground, then something else is fueling them. And if you're eating them and none of you have been harmed or drained that you know of, then they are more likely just a symptom of the larger problem here.

        But what else might you be looking for, then? And where might it be? It's probably something nearby, given how many flowers are on this block compared to the previous street.


        Lynx
        You form an Imago of incense swirling around the cloud, intermingling with the Essence, and revealing hidden secrets through the shapes revealed, the way a shaman or spiritual healer might use incense in a divination ritual. The spell blossoms into existence and you feel a sense of insight as you continue to study the cloud. Even without your Mage Sight active, you are able to watch the Essence cloud with your spell. As you study it, you see a trail of Essence leading back to the dandelions, but then also feel the energy coming in through their roots, and further stretching back to...something else.

        Abruptly, jarring thoughts and memories of that otherworldly place you went to through the Highstorm portal interrupt your focus. It's all you can do to keep your spell from wavering and falling apart.


        Take a beat and the following condition:

        Distracted. Something is running through your head and you have a very difficult time focusing on anything else. You take -2 on Wits+Composure rolls for perception and spellcasting rolls that have a duration of concentration (effectively negating the Yantra bonus for concentration). In addition, your Resolve is 1 lower for purposes of Withstanding spell effects.

        Resolution: Gain 1 Willpower.

        Beat: N/A.


        Ouroboros Ophis
        You know from studying the cloud with your Sight that there is no Anchor here for the ghosts; this is raw Essence being leaked into the sky. But you know that ghosts who move beyond the range of their Anchors might rapidly lose their Essence, and even though you now see that there is an incredible surplus here, there seems to be more than just Essence bringing the ghosts here.

        You wonder if the Essence cloud is some kind of lure, or if something else made nearby ghosts aware of this Essence feast. That would suggest that the cloud is not the heart of this Mystery, just a piece of a larger puzzle.


        Ragnar
        Your first thought is that there must be some kind of quality of this cloud that explains why it's attracting so many creatures and why they aren't leaving. You try to critically analyze the behavior of the ghosts you can see. But the more you watch and try to objectively evaluate your hypothesis, you fail to see any difference between how the ghosts act in relation to where they stand relative to the cloud; they are all just fighting to be as close to the stream of energy as they feel comfortable doing so.

        While watching, you can't help but be impressed at the sheer scope of magic that led to this event. It's certainly more than you know how to create with your limited Spirit mastery.

        And then another thought pops into your head. It's something triggered by one of Moonstone's comments about something draining the Sleepers and fueling the ephemera. What if there was some kind of magical item or Artifact nearby that was causing this? That might explain the scope of this effect.

                                                            Ragnar sighs slightly. It wasn't unexpected, but it still hurts. Part of Ragnar realizes that the Tass they have been consuming may very well contain a little bit of the inhabitants' essence. Soylent Green is people! But he shoves down the thought. He looks back towards the place where the ghosts and- his stomach flips at his first sight of spirits. What the hell would I even title that paper? "Examining the trophic interactions of the meme-fauna of La Grange?" ... On second thought, somebody in that Mysterium that Mathias mentioned has probably beat me to it.

                                                            As the door opens like a horror movie cliché, Ragnar steps into the hallway. Once he crosses the threshold, he gathers himself for a moment and begins to gaze forwards in time, rather than backwards. What threats await us in here? Which room is the source of this disturbance in? Which rooms are the bodies of the inhabitants in?

                                                            Ragnar is casting Time 1 Divination to get some info on the house. Two free Reach, one for Instant Casting, the other for specific answers. He doesn't invoke Paradox. He'll take a -2 to the roll to increase his Potency by one for a third question.

                                                            Gnosis 1 + Time 2 + Path Tool 1 + Concentration 2 - Potency 2 = 4 dice -> 1 success.
