                                            01-25-2015, 01:50 AM
                                            Hmm... I'd again agree that just letting the Zodiacs go would feel like an anti-climax, but I can see a catch involved. Some suggestions:
                                            1. Each of the Zodiac Crew may leave, upon completion of a task or challenge the Keeper sets before each of them. Each would be tailored to each character, and probably pretty unfair. Also likely to highlight flaws, or maybe a "dark road" the character might wind up walking down if incautious. Involving the Western Zodiac for this could be an option, as could some other extant minion, or a magically fashioned entity/situation specifically prepared for the occasion. The Keeper's arrogance may leave openings, though. He might assume they're too swept up in animal instinct to resist something, or he might have missed some personal growth or strength each has found. Given the setting, they may be the sorts of things best beaten with trickery and technicality.
                                            2. Each of the Zodiac Crew has an opportunity to leave... but each must find a new recipient for the animal statue. The Keeper swallows his pride and offers a bargain: the current Zodiac can leave his domain for some period of time to sell each statue to someone of the same sign. In success, they're off the hook and the unlucky new recipients take their place. Any Zodiac animal who doesn't make such a sale by the end of it is returned. Totally a fair bargain: the Keeper gets to maintain his collection one way or another and the Zodiac members get an out -- if they're willing to try to sleep with themselves afterwards, anyway.
                                            3. Probably covering a lot of Evilmarmelade's suggestion, the Secretary could insist on a general renegotiation of the contract, basically from first principles. If the PCs are canny, they may be able to trick the Keeper into binding himself in an ironclad contract. Or he may manage to get them into a worse position than the present terms. It's a high stakes game, here!
                                            4. The Keeper wronged each Zodiac member, and so must compensate them to appease the Wyrd. In exchange for denying them the return home they sought, his servants will bring a piece of that home back to his realm for them. To the characters' horror, this may lead to one or more of each animal's loved ones being dragged through the Thorns. And perhaps suffering a transformation to match the character's. "Who doesn't win from this? You get a comfort from your old lives and I add some accessories to my Collection."

                                            Eh, just some thoughts. I do like the idea of a change-over from Horse to Goat having some impact, whether while in Arcadia or after the crew finagles a way out. Or that Chekov's Gun could linger a while longer. It may be worth noting that the Horse is the Zodiac animal most likely to have eaten faerie food before receiving any punishment. Which... would mean a bit of irony in that getting temporary freedom may deny the Horse the use of the loophole that the rest of the Zodiac are now desperately trying to leverage for a more permanent freedom.
