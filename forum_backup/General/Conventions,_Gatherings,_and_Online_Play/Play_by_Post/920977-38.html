                                            Originally posted by Haberdasher View Post
                                            Okay, just for future reference, if I want to try and Dodge an attack, do I need to declare that intention the turn before the one I anticipate getting attacked on (same for Mage Armor), or is there something about Alux's attack that preempted me?
                                            Well, it's complicated due to our dice rolling conventions for a play-by-post game.

                                            In normal in-person gaming, if you are about to get attacked and haven't acted on that round of initiative yet, you can reflexively Dodge and sacrifice your instant action for the turn. That would then lead me to adjust my dice pool for the attack accordingly. You can also activate Mage Armor reflexively, even against a surprise attack or if you've already acted on that turn.

                                            In our PBP game, our rolling convention for penalizing attack rolls is to subtract dice from the rightmost rolls first. In this particular case, that means Dodging wouldn't make a difference; your Defense is 4, so even with Dodging AND Mage Armor adding 2 to your Defense, you wouldn't subtract the dice that contributed to those successes. Basically, it's a moot point for this roll, so I just provided the results before asking if you wanted to roll.

                                            For saving time, I'm ok with posting attack rolls and giving you all the choice to Dodge or activate Mage Armor reflexively after the roll results are posted. This saves time for PBP games, at the expense of allowing you to metagame about whether dodging is worth it. If players would prefer, I could post that you're being attacked and wait to roll until you announce whether you want to do anything, but that seems a little cumbersome.

                                            EDIT: So I'm an idiot.

                                            I totally forgot that Dodge works differently in 2E vs. 1E. Dodging lets you roll a dice pool, not just subtract from the rolls. Rules are p 217-218.

                                            If you want to dodge, let me know. If you get enough successes, you will cancel out the attack and prevent some of the damage. Sorry about that.

                                                            Ok, when you have time, make your Dodge roll. Your dice pool equals your Defense doubled, which for now let's say includes your Mage Armor bonus (if you apply it) before doubling. This means you'll go up to a Dodge pool of either 8 (if no Mage Armor) or 12 (with Mage Armor). Each success subtracts from a success on the attacking roll. So if you get 5 or more successes on your Dodge roll, you will take no damage. If even 1 success remains, you take a bonus 3 lethal from the Essence Alux spent on the weapon attack.

                                                            Also, FYI, you don't start bleeding out until your rightmost health bar is filled with lethal damage. So you're still conscious, you're not bleeding out, you're just really really hurt. And again, that depends on the outcome of your Dodge roll.
