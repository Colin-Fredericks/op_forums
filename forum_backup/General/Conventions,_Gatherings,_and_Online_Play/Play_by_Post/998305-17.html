                Dusksage no problem, take your time. There's a lot to process.

                Wormwood The very nature of Hard Leverage will mean that you would have to make a Breaking Point roll. Your base dice pool is 5. If you take a -2 penalty to the dice pool due to more horrific and merciless action, you'll open two Doors, which would leave you with another five to open. I would also suggest that you roll for Torment after that, which is Resolve + Composure - Azoth. There is no need to roll for Hard Leverage to succeed, it just works.

                Now, if you wanted to Force Doors, on the other hand, that would be a rolled action. I would suggest Strength + Intimidation. The penalty to Forcing Doors is the current number of Doors left, so it would be a flat penalty of -7 to begin with, or -5 with Hard Leverage. You can take additional Hard Leverage actions, but it would mean more Breaking Points. If you accepted the Tormented Condition, however, as a result of your Hard Leverage action, then not only would you earn a Beat, but you could open three Doors instead.

                For Disquiet, roll Azoth. Uh, holy crap. It appears as though the gangsters not only resisted Disquiet, but are completely immune to Disquiet for the rest of the Chapter unless you get six successes on a dice pool of two?

                ArchonAres Promethean is a well of deep Storytelling and inspiration that I have been thinking about for a long time but had never really managed to tap into through a game. It's also in this weird spot of patchwork urban fantasy, horror, and dark whimsy that my particular brand of writing is great at capturing. Also, Winnipeg has a really fascinating history that I can tap and that keeps getting stranger as it unfolds.

                For example, we were subject to Nazi invasion in WWII. For a few days, Manitoba was occupied by Nazi Germany, and then a group looking to drum up support for wartime donations came forwards and said "this is what other people are living with. Donate money to stop it." This project was called "If Day." People were told about it in advance to avoid widespread panic, but much like the War of the Worlds broadcast, it still caught people by surprise and convinced them it was real.

                I'm glad for all the complements though, and I'm happy everyone is enjoying the story so far. Don't worry! Weirdness is coming! Prepare to get less grounded.

                                            Dusksage I'll answer these one at a time.

                                            1) It's a single Instant Action representing an abstract period of time. Assemble a dice pool for that, apply a -2 penalty due to Disquiet, and apply a penalty equal to the Equipment Bonus you want for the Equipment. You can definitely spend Willpower on it. Because it's tactics, and a plan, the Equipment could apply to Mark 5 as well.

                                            2) I wouldn't say you need a roll to figure out what's happening. It's clear enough. Mark 5 isn't making as much of a splash as Earth is, but he's still building a reputation. He's known in the local community now as Breaker, a troubleshooter who likes to handle gangbangers with his fists and seems to be as strong and fast as a human can be. If you want to mitigate the damage and the rumours, that's a different story. That would be an Extended Manipulation + Stealth roll, with a target number of five. If you want to start an alternative rumour to keep trouble off of Mark 5's back and deflect blame to a different gang, that would be Manipulation + Subterfuge vs. Presence + Composure (which would be a total of four dice, for the Presence + Composure pool).

                                            3) Absolutely. Just need to explain Regret's thought process, how it relates to his Pilgrimage, and how it relates to his own perspective and philosophy, such as it is. I imagine that the Dead Soliders will have something to say about what they're seeing. (And I absolutely love that you're going the route of having the history of the bodies that went into making Regret have a lasting impact on him)
