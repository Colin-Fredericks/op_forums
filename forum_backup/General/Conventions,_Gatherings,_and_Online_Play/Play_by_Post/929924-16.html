                    Binder
                    When you open your Sight, the mundane world around you becomes more muted and the portal on the wall appears again before your eyes. The portal appears more unstable than before: The edges appear to be shaking, and there are bright flashes of lightning shooting across the surface. Black tendrils of energy seem to be colliding with the lightning, as if the two energies are at conflict.


                    Lynx and Ragnar
                    The two of you catch up to Mathias, who offers a smile, but his eyebrows are furrowed with worry. He waves you towards the train station, about half a block away. As you walk, he turns towards you and chuckles, then reaches into a backpack slung over his shoulder. Mathias pulls out a black shirt and tosses it to Lynx. "I forgot to give that to you earlier, sorry. I knew you'd be needing that but wasn't sure why." Mathias seems like he wants to ask, but just grins instead. The shirt is roughly your size, maybe a little smaller -- it fits tightly in the chest and arms. "Better not to draw too much attention after a Highstorm if you can help it."

                    Mathias also pulls out three CTA punch cards, gives one to each of you, and holds onto the third for himself. "I didn't know which of you was going to follow, but I knew it wasn't all three." His voice is tinged with minor annoyance at the mention of not knowing. "I know you don't know me, but please, trust me. I will explain as much as I can and answer your questions if possible."

                    The three of you arrive at the train station, and Mathias leads the way through. He sticks his punch card in and runs up the stairs to the tracks. The train pulls up just moments later, and he walks on board and takes a seat in a vacant booth on the train. There are a handful of people sitting on the opposite end of the train car, but no one pays you any notice in particular.

                    Mathias tries to keep his voice lower, occasionally glancing over at the other side of the train to see if anyone is looking in their direction. "The crazy weather patterns that happen here, like the one that just ended? We call them a Highstorm," he explains. "We mages, I mean. Not all of them are found or reveal themselves, but we have a society here in the greater Chicago metro area of...maybe a couple hundred? Small enough in a city of this size that when we learn about someone who's newly Awakened, that's a big deal.

                    "Anyway, during or immediately after these Highstorms, sometimes...things happen. Doors open up to strange places that we can't remember clearly, only bits and pieces. The mystery behind it all is fascinating, so a lot of us congregate here when one happens. There's danger here, serious danger, so I usually try to...look ahead to see what I might encounter." He smiles again, and for a moment his excitement overcomes the worry on his face. "To find three of you here, and know that there are three others we're about to meet, is incredible."

                    Mathias' tone turns worried again, and his expression darkens. "But something is going wrong with the others. They're in incredible danger. A monster, or creature, or something...I'm not sure what it is. But there's going to be something coming out. We need to get to them quickly, or they're going to be in grave danger. And even if they're ok...Well, a lot of other people might not be. Today, tomorrow, I don't know, but it's not good."
