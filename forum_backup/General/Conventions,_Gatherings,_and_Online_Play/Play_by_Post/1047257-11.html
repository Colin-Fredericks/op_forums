    When you use Fate to get Resources, the idea is that money temporarily comes your way. A merchant's cart tips over, or you find valuable coins left behind in the room, or you get too much change from a transaction. In a backwater town like this where there just isn't that much money unaccounted for, it's especially difficult to plausibly (read: non-Dissonance) get access to say, Resources 3 or more.

    When Shifting the Odds expires, your temporary Allies/Contacts move on and stop being so friendly with you, and might have questions about what happened. Similarly, I assume Resources expiring means that your temporary money is lost by accident or chance as easily as it was found.

    My attitude as ST is that the abstract dramatic rules of the Resources mechanic are more important than the in-character specifics of where the money is going, unless I see an opportunity for an interesting situation. In a small and relatively tight-knit area like this, someone is going to notice when the money stops being Fatebound to you, regardless of the specifics of who has which coins. With a longer Duration, the money will get further away before anyone notices.

    What would probably happen is that the coins you gave him would be stolen or lost in the same way that the money came into your possession.

    Which isn't to say that you can't do it. If you guys feel like cheating the guy who's helping you survive in the desert, go for it, it's just that Sleepers will notice and make your lives harder when (if?) you come back to town.

    Tambov yeah your crit counted as a haggle, he was 4 in my notes and you've dropped his price to 3. I posted a shopping list for you guys which you can reference. Everything still on the list is "priced as marked" though, haggling won't reduce the cost. You can magic or steal it if you want to get your hands dirty.

    If by boost your Resources you mean making a purchase at +1 dot and then dropping it for a month, that's just once per chapter. I'll say this: you can raise Resources that way and have your one purchase be "a specific number of items that would be free at the higher rating," although you can't do that and also make a single costed purchase, if that makes sense. All the Availability 2 items you want OR one Availability 3 item.

        Locating the Hand of U'mat will be an extended Stamina+Survival roll, one roll per day. You don't know the target number. The primary actor, which will be one of the PCs, can make a number of rolls equal to their Stamina+Survival rating, while the dicepool itself is modified with bonuses and penalties. Other PCs can contribute as secondary actors with Wits+Survival or Manipulation+Ride, scouting ahead or preparing food and shelter or guarding against weather conditions.

        At the bare minimum, you will need to rent some camels and bring enough water and rations for a prolonged trip. (no bonus, Availability 2)
        Instead, you could ask the desert nomads for their better-trained camels. (+2 bonus to primary actor and supporting Ride rolls, Availability 3)
        It would be wise to find a skilled guide who knows the desert. (Allows up to 2 additional rolls, but must find and convince someone. Availability ?)

        The Red Land is an Extreme Environment, but its level varies over time. It's always at least Level 1 from heat or cold, and it can reach Level 2 on hot days or cold nights. On dangerous days (not nights), it can reach Level 3 from heat and wind. If you're caught in a sandstorm (Set forbid), it's Level 4, and mundane Survival gear won't protect you. Extreme Environments are explained on Mage 224. Much, but not all of this can be mitigated with Forces.

        Basic Survival gear (protective clothing and canvas tents) gives a +1 bonus to Survival rolls and downgrades the Environment level by 1. (Availability 1 to outfit the group)
        Instead, Advanced Survival gear (desert nomad clothing, tents, and advice) gives a +2 bonus to Survival rolls and downgrades the Environment level by 2. (Availability 3 to outfit the group)

        You can choose to travel by day or by night. The weather is more extreme by day, but it's dark and there are more potential predators by night. Minor bites and stings can be deadly serious in the wasteland.

        If you're planning to travel by night, or just want an easy source of fire, you could buy torches and fuel. (Counters 2 dice of Poor Light penalties, Availability 1)
        The body's natural healing is more difficult in the desert, but if you bring bandages and salves, you can treat injury. (Enables Medicine rolls, Availability 2)
        Originally posted by Caladriu View Post
        It's going to be relevant soon, so here's the Resources/Availability rules. Once per chapter, you can expend the Resources merit to purchase an item or service with Availability equal to your Resources dots or dots-1. You can instead buy something of your Resources+1, but you have to reduce your Resources dots by 1 for the rest of the month. If something costs at least 2 dots less than your Resources, you can buy it without expending Resources for the chapter.

        For example, if you have Resources 3, you can buy as many Availability 1 items as you want, or you can expend the Merit for the chapter to buy one thing of Availability 2 or 3. Or you can buy something of Availability 4, and then have Resources 2 for the rest of the month.

        Instead of using Resources, if you have an appropriate Skill at a rating equal to or greater than Availability, you can procure the item with a single roll of Attribute+Skill-Availability. What counts as an appropriate skill depends on the context and how exactly you plan to get the item or service, but Larceny and Streetwise are usually good bets. You might also be able to convince someone to help with your relevant Animal Ken knowledge, or secure a precious item as a gift after formally Socializing with the owner.
