    "Oh, um...So sorry". The tall, young Asian woman looks down at her shoes. "I don't work here. I'm a journalist...or er...I'm sort of a journalist. I'm interning at a newspaper while I study at University." She gives Bridgitte the impression of a "good student" who has been caught doing something naughty.

    "Are you interested in the Walworth Estate too? There was this woman who came to the paper's offices complaining about pests in her building. My editors told me it was a waste of time to look into it, but it peaked my interest."

    She pointed to a certain section of the stacks. "This section here is all about property maintenance and tenant complaints. Was there a particular subject you were interested in? I might have seen something. I've been here for a few hours. I guess I got obsessed."

    Bridgitte recognizes the type. She is like a Teacher's Pet, an eager overachiever who wants to please. Maybe Bridgitte sees a little bit of her younger self in this girl. She does get the odd sense of being "read" when the young woman mentions the words "waste of time" and "obsessed". Unusual for a mere sleeper.

    "Yes. We should stay focused. They close up in about an hour, though sometimes they stay open latter if council members or government employees are here."

    When she says this Mother Time feels her unseen sense prickle.

        Peering through the windows, Berith notices a young Asian woman sitting at a reading table, looking at some papers like a student who believes in studying as if her life depended on it.

        As he twists his ring and wishes himself good luck, he feels his unseen sense press on his attention. The young woman looks up and smiles at him through the window, and then quickly dives back down into her papers as a short woman in her mid forties approaches with a glowering and determined look. Even though he only glimpsed her face briefly, Thomas is struck by the younger woman's almost otherworldly beauty. She would certainly stand out in a crowd.

        The old gentleman clerk raises an eyebrow at being referred to as "Dove".

        "Three in one day! What the duce?" he mutters under his breath.

        "Yes. Just there sir. And be sure to include the time. We were about to close, but I suppose with a council member here, we can extend the reading hours."

        Thomas looks down at the sign-in book. He sees two names, both in very neat and precise hands. "Trish Takehashi", arrived at 3:03, and "Bridgitte Timothy", arrived at 6:06.

        He looks at his watch. 7:07. That's a little odd.

            "Sorry about that sir," Thomas replied almost absently. "Bit on a tizzy today it would seem." As he signed his time, and noted the peculiarities, he'd begin opening his sense for the supernatural. With a bit of extra exertion, a burning of precious mana, he expanded them into the Arcana of Time as well.

            It came to him in a mix and swath of information. First in the symbolic weight and value of each item, as if the Queen of Numbers, or her servant Mammon set the unspoken offer of money upon each one. Then, the granted information of Death, revealing a secret commodity, a source of power in the still existing dead or in the value of souls. Finally, the scale of time that proved value in action as in the continuing shift in what was considered present.

            He scanned the room around him first before he began toward the archive space itself, his attention settled on Trish rather curiously.
