        Intimacies: Kusuma (Guilt), Shadowed Skies (Reluctant Loyalty), Tribal Elders (Bitter Indifference), the Gods (Resentful Piety), Hasya (Respect), ‘Enoka (Affectionate Pity), Suanggi (Old Love), Jainat Marigami (Disapproval), Outcasts (Kinship), Kava (Inordinate Fondness)
        Archetype: Priest
        Profession: Mendicant
        Attributes
        Physical: Strength 2, Dexterity 2, Stamina 3
        Social: Charisma 1, Manipulation 3, Appearance 2
        Mental: Intelligence 3, Wits 2, Perception 4
        Abilities
        Athletics 1, Craft (Wood) 1, Integrity 4, Larceny 3, Linguistics 1, Lore 1, Medicine 1, Melee 4, Occult 3, Performance 2, Presence 1, Resistance 3, Socialize 2, Survival 3, War 2
        Professional Abilities: Integrity, Resistance, Survival
        Specialties: Craft: Weaponry 2, Melee: Ritual Combat 2, War: Coordinated Attacks 1
        Advantages
        Essence: 1
        Backgrounds: Artifact 2, Backing (Temple Guardians) 1, Familiar 3, Influence 2
        Virtues: Compassion 2, Conviction 2, Valour 3, Temperance 4
        Willpower: 8
        Thaumaturgy: Dishonest (Creature)'s Rebuke, Spirit Sight, Thanks for the Hunt

        BONUS POINTS
        1: Melee 4
        2: War 2
        2: Larceny 3
        2: Lore 1
        1: Melee specialties
        2: Craft (Wood) specialities
        1: War specialty
        1: Artifact 2
        6: Compassion 2, Valour 3
        2: Willpower 8
        1: Thaumaturgical procedures

        NOTES
        Her Artifact rating represents assorted talismans (see page 379 of the corebook), as follows:
        • An ofuda ward against goblins wound around the hilt of her weapon.
        • A herb-scented rag, likewise wrapped around her sword-hilt, serving as a charm against disease.
        • A full-circle pig tusk worn as an anklet. Carved with prayers to the gods, it acts as a good luck charm.
        • Whispering Valley Tribe-style tattoos on her hands and forearms, serving as a double-strength ward against the dead.
        • A ward against water spirits in the form of an ornate whalebone hairstick.

        Her Influence derives from her reputation as a witch or demon, and so mostly applies to attempts at intimidation. It's meaningless against those who know the details of her penance (Hasya, for instance).

        She speaks Old Realm in addition to the local tongue.

                                Just edited the second post, put in a Cast of Characters (useful for me for keeping NPC names and relationships straight as the game goes on, hopefully useful for you too). I'm organizing most of the NPCs by their affiliation, since that social context is where you're most likely to encounter them. In getting up NPCs, I discovered I have a couple of questions which may impact local politics.

                                1. The MG, from your initial writeup of your character concept, you indicated that she was a chieftain's daughter who ended up training with the Temple Guardians due to criminal behavior to get her out of the public eye and to teach her some discipline. While she's now nameless and forsaken by her tribe and accepted only by the Temple Guardians, blood is thicker than water. If I remember right, you wanted to be have been born into the Dreaming River Tribe? That would make Chief Shadowed Skies your mother, and young Palms and Moon would be your younger half-brothers. That sound good to you, or did you want to ditch the disgraced-daughter-of-the-Chief bit of your backstory?

                                2. Hapless With Dice and Jen, I sorta wrote the recent backstory of the Sunset Tribe to give you two an in-Chieftain's children vanish (kidnapped by slavers), Chieftain goes into grieving and dies of illness, his sister's husband takes over. If Huelo is the son of the Sunset Tribe's former chieftain, he's technically the rightful heir to the position and has a claim to be the true chieftain. His mother and those few warriors still loyal to her would likely back his claim, but if his identity is known the current Chief and his supporters (and the Green Flower Tribe) would come down hard on a potential coup in the making. I know you want to do the law-and-order thing- are you keeping your true identity secret and building up popular support, or are you open about who you are and have publicly abandoned your claim to the throne and are serving the existing order? You two are kind of a civil war waiting to happen, especially if you're going to be trying to find out the truth behind your father's death (your mother insists he was poisoned rather than dying of a fever) and before I get too invested in that I want to make sure you're okay with that sorta thing.

                                3. Everyone Else: While character sheets are coming in, I'm looking at starting locations. I'm thinking of splitting the party into Eastern Island Posse and Western Island Posse (and maybe even a third posse, if things shake out that way), so we can get you involved with multiple tribes to get things nice and complicated. The downside of splitting the group is that it might take some of you guys forever to actually meet each other. The upside is that we can tell a slightly more epic story, with schemes and politics and adventure going on in multiple places at once, so you will have affected the other group and will have heard of them by reputation before you ever meet them. If we do go with splitting the party, I'd like to state right out that while I'm fine with inter-tribal warfare, I'd rather avoid PC-versus-PC battles during such a struggle. If some of you will be supporting the Green Flower tribe's goal of uniting the island under one chieftain, and some of you will be supporting a different tribe, I'd like to see you working to defuse the situation rather than just killing one another. If you all end up on one side of the conflict or if you are up against NPCs of the other side but PCs are not there, of course, the out-of-game exhortation to be peacemakers is lifted and you're free to get as bloody as you want.

                                Pretty sure the Maiden and and Huelo start best either in the Sunset Tribe territory, which is their homeland, or in Dreaming River territory, as the chieftain there would try to build them into a wedge to separate the Green Flower and Sunset tribes, so she'd give them aid and local status and try to get on their good side. Hasya and Pakari are both Temple Guardians, so they could be stationed or traveling anywhere, and the same is true of the Bearer of Bones. Wahine is also a wanderer, but she's got an interest plot thread going with Haku in the Green Flower Tribe, and I'd love to get her in a party with Akoli Tane, who'd likely be in their territory as well because the Green Flowers do the most trade with his people. Seskan also fits well into Green Flower Territory, as his homeland is further west where the Wyld and its mutations are even more of a danger. Lyndon could show up anywhere....so...thoughts on where you'd like to start out and who you'd like to start out with would be good?
