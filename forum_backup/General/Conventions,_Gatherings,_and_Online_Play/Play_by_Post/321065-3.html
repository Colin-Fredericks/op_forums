                                            The man seems unaffected by Hannah's imperious tone, taking another drag from his black cigarette. "Man, I love these things. I mean, I know they're just dried up leaves, but they taste so good." He holds it under his nose again for a moment, inhaling the aroma of the cloves. "Smells nice, too. There's something about these things that makes me want to keep smoking them, besides the nice smell. Almost like my body needs them. And if my body needs them, then they must be good for it, right?" He nods firmly at his own logic as the stragglers start coming into view.

                                            "Well, anyway, enough of that," he says, finishing his clove cigarette. Instead of flicking the butt away, he points it down and twists with his fingers until the remaining tobacco falls out, the lit tip going out as it lands in the snow. He slides the remains of black paper and filter into a pocket. "I didn't come out here to talk about cigarettes, or any other human things." He shakes his head firmly. "No, I certainly did not. I'm here to talk about your mission."

                                            "My name is Nose-to-the-sidewalk, and I'm a Bone Gnawer." He nods to Echoes as she approaches. "Your mentor, Sunset Howl, might have mentioned me, Stir of Echoes. We're packmates, you see." He turns back to addressing everyone. "Now, this place you're going, the Piney Sept... it's our place. Bone Gnawer, through and through. Well, except for the Croatany bits. But the rest? All Bone Gnawer. We don't have a lot of places to call our own, you see, and this one has been ours for a long time. It's special to us. I've been working real hard to make the place liveable again for the past few years."

                                            "What I'm trying to say is, when you have to fight whatever it is you're going there to fight, try not to totally wreck the place, all right? It'd be nice if I could make it liv eable again sometime before the Apocalypse, y'know?"

                                            As he speaks, he reaches into a pocket and pulls out a packet of Tastycake Butterscotch Krimpets. The sound of tearing cellophane fills the woods. "Mmm," he says taking a bite out of one. "I love theshe thingsh," he mumbles as he chews the cake, small crumbs spitting from his mouth as he talks. "Anyway, try eshpeshially to avoi' the shemetary. The gravesh? I jusht..." he swallows the cake. "Mmm, that's tasty. I just cleaned and cleansed that place real good. Some of those graves go back to the Croatan days. It's extra sacred, so try not to disturb it. Oh, also, if you go into the Umbra, you might see a raccoon spirit. Raccoon was the caern totem before the place fell, and this raccoon spirit has been helping me cleanse the place. He's very friendly."
