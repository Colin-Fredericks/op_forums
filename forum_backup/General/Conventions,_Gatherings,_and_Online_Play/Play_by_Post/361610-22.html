                            After talking with the Madam at the Magic Gourd, Flynn once again becomes the Musician. He looks for Nahuel and Fovnag, but is unable to find them. Instead he heads to a local inn and procures a room. He stretches out on the bed and tried to go to sleep. After tossing and turning for a few hours, he gets up and paces the small room. Alcohol always quieted his mind after a long day, but he would have to learn to fall asleep without it.

                            He thinks about the events of the day. He might have blown his cover. His little stunt with the deathknight might cause more problems than it solves. But at least he did something. Flynn laid back down on the bed. He was tired of wallowing in grief. It was time to channel all of his grief and anger into more constructive activities. On that thought, Flynn manages to drift off to sleep.

                            The next morning, Flynn rises early, unable to sleep any longer. He shaves and stares at himself in the mirror. His chin and cheeks look pale compared to the rest of his suntanned face. His eyes are bloodshot with pale blue circles underneath. He runs his hands though his long and raggedly hair. Man, I do look like shit, he thinks as he rinses his face. He ties his hair back and grabs his cloak. Pulling out the grocery list that he joked with Swimming Owl about the prior day, he heads to the Apothecary shop to restock his supplies.

If it did not, it was but one more charge to be leveled at the hundred stars.
