                                    There were many things Splendor wished to say, very few of them meant for polite company. However, his tutors had broken him of the habit of speaking out long before he had ever met anyone it would be impolite to speak out in front of.

                                    Mostly he was confused. Slavery on the scale they were suggesting couldn't be merely the gratification of the rich, and infrastructure should have removed all motivation for large scale manual labor. Yet even as he began to open his mouth to comment or question his mind began to race. Situations when the networks began to break down and isolation and loss of tools leading to barbarism might bring back those motivations. The more he thought about it the filthier he felt. He could never accept it, but the fact that he could understand why rational people might engage in it was condemnation enough.

                                    All he did say was, "For what are the majority of slaves used? If your guild is involved in their sale you must have some idea of who purchases them and for what."
