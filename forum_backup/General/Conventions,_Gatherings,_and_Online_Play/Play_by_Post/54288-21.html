        (Yi)
        Larissa beams at Yi, and he has to shield his eyes for a moment from her radiant smile.

        "Good. It makes my life easier to work with someone with a spine," she says. Looking Yi up and down, as if truly noticing him for the first time, she says softly, almost to herself, "Yes, you might do nicely."

        Your conversation is interrupted by your arrival at the Hall of the Boule, at an entrance noted by the Mon of Gens Spyrus. Recognizing the Ex-Archon, the troops allow you in immediately. You pass a long series of aristocrats, who bow to your Divine escort on sight. Larissa acknowledges far more of them by name than not.

        Eventually, Larissa leads you to a heavy door with a large desk and several guards in front of it. The guards are split between the heraldry of Spyrus and that of Novalesk itself. A middle aged man who was seated at the desk rises and bows to Larissa, mumbling, something along the lines of "M'lady Archon"

        "Good morning, Vasil," Larissa says with a faint smile, "Does he have few moments?"

        "He's speaking to the Lord Strategos at the moment," Vasil says, clearly confused by this turn of events.

        "Ah. I believe that's about us. Can you let him know I'm here, and ask for a moment?" Larissa asks. Vasil nods and ducks into the door, emerging a few moments later.

        "He can speak with you now," Vasil says as he ushers you through the door into a spartan writing room. Eugene is there, and is speaking with an elderly man. Upon seeing you enter, the old man looks to Eugene who nods, a wry look on his face.

        Larissa strides in and plants herself in an open chair before introducing Yi to Spyrus Nereus, High Elder of Gens Spyrus and vice-versa.

                    (Tirien)
                    Dero pulls a coin pouches out of his shirt, and hands the man two obols. He indicates that the man will receive the rest upon the return trip.

                    The man shrugs, and says, "Fair 'nuff.". He eats the obols, and then takes a large pack and an odd double-saddle from a nearby rack. He straps the pack to his chest, and the saddle to his back. After following him outside, the man squats down and flexes his muscles. He explodes in a thunder-crack and blast of ozone, revealing a monstrous hawk with rainbow wings and a circlet of lightning 'pon its brow. Tirien and Dero strap themselves into the saddle, its odd straps and handholds now making perfect sense. Once they confirm that they are secured, the thunderbird flaps its wings with a loud thunder-crack and rockets up into the sky.

                    At your periodic stops, you learn that your current ride is named Rain-Upon-His-Wings, and is a relatively young Thunderbird. He flies this route relatively often, and is acquainted with several Geherin, including one of their Skalds. No, he hasn't ever entered Geherin proper, as the local spirits don't take kindly to outsiders poking around in their airspace.

                    After the advertised two days, you fly into a modest market-town, a mile or so from a large, dense forest. The town looks to be constructed in Novaleski styles, and a significant number of the populace are dressed as such as well. The rest, however, are in varying states of advanced undress, with loincloths (and chest wraps for the women) to be the norm. They are prime specimens, fit and strong, and many of them carry multiple bearded axes. What appears to be a ball game is being played on the outskirts of the town in the direction of the forest. The locals, presumably.

                    Rain-Upon-His-Wings touches you down and transforms back into a human. He informs you that he will be taking in the local color, and will wait for you for two weeks before he'll head back without you. He then heads off, chasing (metaphorical) skirts.

                    What do?

                                05-07-2014, 04:56 PM
                                Yi bowed in greeting to Elder Nereus and recognition to Strategos Eugene, acknowledging each in turn.

                                "Honorable Elder, I do not know what you have been told about me. Nor do I think I would succeed if I tried to live up to all the expectations built up around me. I will save us both the annoyance of trying. Though doing so may have weakened my position, I will weaken it further now by saying that I am going to speak with the bluntest of honesty. I have seen far more damage done by secrets kept to long then egos bruised to quickly, and the regard of the goddess and strategos suggests to me your character can stand insults better than flattery.

                                The current difficulties Novalesk faces ultimately emerge from a systemic flaw in the organization of its military resources. While those resources may yet be sufficient to deal with this difficulty, that will not correct the problem that allowed the situation to reach the level of a crisis. Simply put, a significant portion of the military is too enmeshed in political fetters to be easily mobilized for any threat save civil war, though I do not suggest that such is happening or will.

                                It is my goal, modified and expanded from a suggestion of Origenes, to convince all the gens to place their private forces under the Strategos’ command and to capitalize on the public goodwill of victory to never surrender that command. This will entail require drastic military measures, including integration of units so as to dilute individual gens loyalty, an expansion of the officer corps, and a reevaluation of the role of the Strategos. The master says that once an army is assembled it is the province of the general and not the king, and that wisdom is useful here. While it is dangerous to allow the army to set it own objectives, it is equally dangerous to prevent the army from carrying out its objectives once they are assigned.

                                I would like your support for this initiative by releasing your own forces to the Strategos and supporting the later military reorganization. Novalesk is strong, but as it now stands is unable to use its strength when it needs to. Extreme measures may overcome temporary disasters, but it is better to repair the flaw then expect the conditions to always be right for someone to cut through the beuracracy when it is required.

                                I am of course open to whatever insight you have to offer. Heaven would not have caused our paths to cross if Creation could not benefit thereby."

                                                After meditating on his sword and life, Hyuga explores for the forest for a short while. Hyuga pulls himself up into the trees using only his hands, practicing his gripping skills and fine usage of his fingers, carefully calculating where they will go and how they shift. Carefully, he carves his way through the treetops in search of forage-able food, watching the beautiful sunset's dark hue penetrate through the forest bed.

                                                After sunset, Hyuga settles down to start making some designs for decorations of sword hilts. Using a make-shift dagger like rock, Hyuga practices carving intricate designs that might be used for hilts (http://img.photobucket.com/albums/v4...rim-dagger.jpg) into the trees nearby. As the light continues to fade, he continues practicing using only the touch of his fingers to caress and feel the designs whilst continuing to craft. Occasionally, Hyuga will mumble to himself about not being THAT bad at detail work, yet continues to daintily cut the trees.

                                                After a couple of hours, Hyuga feels his finished designs and hopes that it looks as good as he thought it felt. He knew he would have to come back in the morning and check. After not having slept last night, he knew that it was now time to sleep. Carefully, he cuts and rips bark off of the other nearby trees to help make a nicer makeshift sleeping area. Finding a decent spot to lay down, he began to make his preparations. Having traveled through the woods by himself for quite a while, he knew what animals would likely be out during the night and set up small barriers and traps against them. He moved a rock or two here, put a sharpened stick here and there, and even made some tripwires from plants, rocks, and pieces of bark that would alert him if dangers came near. Satisfied, he laid down in a bed of leaves covered by the large sections of bark and went to sleep.

                                                    Yi smiled slightly, pleased by the bluntness of the reply.

                                                    “Your second point is the easier to answer, so I will begin with it if you do not mind. There are multiple answers to the problem of local flexibility which would each remove the difficulty.

                                                    In the first place, a unified military command structure is in no necessary way linked to a loss of local authority. While in practice there may be limitations imposed, such as forbidding responses back across the border, they will present in a broader context of unified command.

                                                    Secondly, there are multiple methods for dealing with communication. Though sorcery is an option, it is an expensive and rare one. More usefully, there are several works of artifice designed for this exact problem, and either myself or the academy is capable of producing them. The specifics would depend on the exact use they were put to, with unique civilian and military concerns.

                                                    Lastly, your own reply is based on a potentially dangerous conflation of roles. The positions of Gens Elder and leader of Gens forces while often linked are not identical. Each possesses a distinct and potentially conflicting responsibility to both the Gens and the nation as a whole. In perfect candor, having what are essentially autonomous warlords within Novalesk produces far more risk than benefit. The Strategos himself has expressed concern for a potential coup made possible by these private forces to say nothing of the fact that a change in elected leadership should not bring about the specter of civil war..

                                                    This is a useful place to point out that part of the reason for integrated units and unified training standards is to ensure that the armed forces will not be in a position to act against Novalesk.

                                                    As for the wealth of your house, I am given to understand that you are the major provider of military weapons and metalwork in the nation. This being the case, my proposed reorganization will result in your family becoming exceptionally wealthy if you handle the situation correctly. While I am leery of offering anything like a fully developed play, the basic should be helpful enough.

                                                    Standardization of training and equipment, as well as a larger available military budget when all current sources are united, means that your contracts will become even more lucrative. In addition, the ability to make larger individual purchases even if the amount is the same means that more specialized material such as siege panoply can be commissioned at an increased rate. Honestly speaking, your metal working infrastructure will prove even more lucrative in the long run especially with the infrastructural expansion proposals I plan make with some of the other Gens.

                                                    I would also suggest making or preserving a sharp distinction between the autonomous mercenary organizations you support and the personal forces of your house. Well it will fool no one intelligent, it is a useful distinction in the realm of politics by creating a zone of separation from the government of Novalesk and the activities of our agents. These revenue streams at the very least would not be affected by the proposal.”
