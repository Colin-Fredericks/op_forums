                Ah, thank you for reminding me. Yes, I have decided. The text of the merit now reads as follows:

                You inspire all around you to greater efforts. Whether by speaking, writing or leading by example, you give anyone who works with you reason to go on and hope of success. You gain +1 die to all social rolls, or +2 if the roll involves someone you are helping or have helped directly in the past, and you give any group effort +1 to its total dice pool.

                How does that sound?

                Does anyone remember those Coterie and Pack diagrams that showed the inner thoughts of a character towards the other members of the group?
                Yeah, I remember those things, though I never really used them for PCs before. Not a bad idea, Bashir. :-) Those looked pretty funny.

                Anyway, moving on, the character sheets all appear to be done (except for Scott's garou astrological sign, which we don't strictly need to start off and I don't want to delay us any longer), and I have just sent Jon's private character background to Bashir via PM. It seems like...

                drumroll, please...

                ...we're finally ready!! Yaaaaaay!!! :-D

                I want to give Bashir a little time to digest his background info, and everyone else one last chance to ask any final questions or make any final changes before we start, so my plan is to put up the posting guidelines tomorrow (and move the character sheets to their new home), then start IC roleplay in its own thread on Wednesday.

                I'm excited. Are you excited? I'm excited. :-)

                I know there have been some delays getting to this point, and for that I apologize, but now that we're finally ready, I'm glad we all stuck through it. I've been looking forward to this for quite a while, and I'm eager to begin.

                -Jim

                P.S.- Just for my own peace of mind, everyone please take a moment to check in so we can make sure we're all still accounted for.
