        I'm sure wonderandawe's notes cover the NPCs, but wanted to give my own take on Aisa's caste of characters as well.

        Important NPCs:
        • Ragara Zara - Ryu's daughter, recently exalted as water aspect while surviving Mnemon's assassination attempt. Was previously Aisa's handmaiden, now a student at the Heptagram. Aisa thinks of her as a protege, and adores her.
        • V'Neef Iphen - Childhood friend. He was in love with Aisa, but she recently broke his heart, and turns out she cared for him more than she thought she did. Knows about her plans to oppose Mnemon / take the throne.
        • Ai'Zul and Jal'Kaith - Retainers. Air and Earth elementals respectively. Flighty+curious spy/messenger and serious fatherly bodyguard.
        • Mnemon Duhalva - Recently elevated Domine of the Heptagram. Aisa's ally, but mostly uninterested in politics. Brilliant researcher.
        • Cathak Nadrian - Cathak Cainan's son. Charming, handsome, almost as well bred as Aisa. They despise each other, but are considering engagement regardless. Sidereal prophecy (which only Aisa knows) that if they marry they'll found a house as great as Mnemon, but one will end up killing the other.
        • Tepet Rowep - Legionaire seeking Aisa's hand (and she's considering it). Probably prompted to do so by his mortal sister, who (sidereal prophecy again) loves or could love Aisa (and it could be mutual).
        • Anuli - Independent Sidereal, she and Aisa are becoming friends. In contrast to two bronze faction observers at the Heptagram (one posing as a professor, the other an immaculate monk)
        • V'Neef - Has met with Aisa repeatedly. They get along reasonably well, and Aisa has associated herself with this house pretty publicly. Doesn't know she seeks the throne, but Aisa thinks V'Neef will be firmly on her side.
        • Unnamed Tepet Elders - Have met with Aisa, and she's promised to act on their behalf. She's mediated an important deal for them, protected the Roseblack from Mnemon, and otherwise is busy getting them firmly in her debt. But honestly so - no manipulation there, just genuinely helping out a desperate house. They don't know she seeks the throne yet.
        • Cynis Belar - One of the three leaders of the house. Was in Mnemon's camp, but supporting Aisa and opposing Mnemon on her behalf (secretly for now) pending full approval of a list of economic concessions from a new Empress.
        • Cathak Cainan - Hasn't supported any candidate yet because they were all too secular and more interested in fighting than preserving the Realm. Last month Aisa met with him, and while he hasn't pledged anything, she's winning him over because she's genuinely pious and cares more about Creation than about herself.
        • Aisa's parents (unnamed) - Her father (from Sessus) was recently poisoned because he tried to keep her secrets. Her mother, from Mnemon, was probably the poisoner, and just this month told Mnemon, and either poisoned her husband or allowed someone else to do it.
        • Mnemon - Just recently learned that Aisa, far from being a loyal and effective supporter (which illusion she'd maintained successfully), is actively opposing her. Hasn't yet had time to act on that knowledge, other than to try and assassinate Zara.


        Upcoming plans:
        • Tell V'Neef and Tepet about her plans for the throne, and get their opinions on the coming civil war.
        • Solidify her relations with Cathak Cainan and House Cynis. Feel out the other great houses.
        • Meet with Ledaal Kes and convince him not to compete with her.
        • Consolidate her hold on the Heptagram and surrounding area to build a safe base of operations.
        • Survive Mnemon's coming wrath.

                    Important NPCs:

Being groomed to lead the biomagitech enhanced special forces group Akashan is putting together. That group has no formal name yet, but consists of DB of proven loyalty receptive to augmentation who have started to receive biomagitech.
                    • Ledaal Leadership: Generally in favor of Akashan's plans but cautious about throwing too many resources into it.
                    • Cynis Leadership: Akashan helped the exchange of territory by entering into an agreement where their wood aspects help him with plant based artifacts.
                    • (Adeptus Ledaalicus): No formal name yet. A group of Ledaal crafters bolstered by talent poached from other houses by marriage as well as from the Threshold and Lost Eggs. The core group are sympathetic to Akashan's ideas about self-enhancement with artifacts. Strong oaths of secrecy and sorcery bind them to not reveal information about his secret craft charms and techniques. Recently had a bit of a struggle over their desire to collaborate outside the group but Akashan's arguments prevailed. One was particularly vocal in opposition.
                    • Ledaal Kes: Effectively Akashan's boss. Connection to the Sidereals who is seeking the throne himself as part of a plan to counter the Deathlords. A bit worried that Akashan's plans for Ledaal will turn them into the craft version of Sesus.
                    • Ascending Stratum: Jadeborn Artisan and Akashan's lover. Commands a smallish colony under Akashan's manse and his connection to the Conclave (Jadeborn leadership)
                    • Fulminous Heart: Akashan's Jadeborn mentor and crafting instructor. Master of a powerful underground factory cathedral and well respected as a crafter.
                    • Jadeborn Conclave: Partners with Akashan on artifact research. Work with him to develop new food preparation methods to allow Ledaal to corner the market on supplying long voyages and the legions in the field. Also, recently developed an artifact that allows the transfers of essence from those in safe places to others far away. Currently there is an arrangement where Ledaal trades food surplus (allowing the JB to carve more soldiers and focus more on manufacturing) in exchange for rare materials and finished goods.
                    • Xochiquetzal: Raptok Dragon King plant magic specialist. She is working as a supplier for Akashan's artifact crafting and is helping to teach other DK the necessary techniques. Her cooperation is contingent on an Eclipse Oath to arrange periodic meetings with Ryo as well as Akashan putting Ledaal resources and Sidereal information to find and bring together civilized and feral DKs.
                    • Peleps Hiro: Owns a broken factory cathedral near the Imperial City. Akashan and Ryo have been trying to ruin it so they can seize the place. Akashan is starting to consider having him killed.
                    Upcoming Plans:
                    • Retrofit an undercapped Wood Manse somewhere in the Arjuf Dominion. Would be built to support Jadeborn food operations below the surface to take advantage of their magic and benefit it with enhanced fertility, provide ideal growing conditions for Xochiquetzal's plant based artifacts and DK operations, and to keep the Cynis gardeners somewhere he can keep an eye on them. Rough plans suggest even without any of those three it could substantually improve food production in the Dominion.
                    • "Acquire" Peleps Hiro's manse and put it back into operation.
                    • Continue expanding and consolidating his group of crafters and enhanced special forces.
                    • Find a way to open the Imperial Manse (He got some V'Neef sorcerers killed recently with his last attempt. V'neef Bilar was ecstatic that they got it to do something)
                    • Finish setting up forces and supplies in the Imperial City in preparation for the battle for the Manse when it opens. (He already did a detailed survey of the place, drew up plans for attack and defense, and figured out how to get the Scarlet Stone, 100 veteran Jadeborn warriors, on their side when the fighting starts)
                    • Build a fleet of ships to break the blockade in the North and allow him to study the geomancy of Whitewall (Before redeploying to the West to check the power of the Silver Prince)
                    • Get an Exotic Material Component supply chain up and running to keep up with all his crafting. (Right now he is trading with the Jadeborn in small amounts, using DK 1 dot artifacts as components in small amounts, and has some being imported through the conspiracy in small amounts.
                    • Bring Kes around to support Aisa (Right now Kes got the Sids to lend Akashan a hidden factory cathedral so he could make anti-deathlord artifacts. They let him have access to the plans for non-doomsday, non-fast transport, non-medical artifacts)


                    Now, the craft problem. I have really optimized crafting pools and lots of assistants for extra successes. I also have a custom speed multiplication charm, a hearthstone that doubles speed, and a lot of money. Now the Sid's lent me a Factory Cathedral and we should have another up and working soon. My subordinates probably can't craft Artifact 4+, but there are a lot of useful E3s.

                    wonderandawe and I agreed that we didn't want my crafting to dominate the game and overshadow the actions of the other players. We were trying crafting slots. Effectively they work like project slots in 3E, each one allows you to be working on one artifact at time and they get built at a rate of 1 dot per week like other backgrounds. Each speed multiplier multiplies slots instead. So the Season->Month conversion of a Factory Cathedral tripled mine to 6. My hearthstone doubled it again to 12. My charm triples it again to 36. In this system FCs don't produce artifacts in

                    We were still deciding what was reasonable to put into craft slots. I had suggested that manses might not be a good idea since then I could produce more Factory-Cathedrals. We were also deciding if Artifact N/A were on the table.

                                                        Some notes on crafting:

                                                        Crafting is worthless until it is gamebreaking. Without speed multipliers a character is lucky to craft one artifact in the entire game. With speed multipliers I have seen craft intervals of minutes. (That was a Solar, and they have better speed charms than everyone else)

                                                        The great restriction is Exotic Material Components. There are basically no rules for them at all and each one can be its own little quest to go find. The way around this is to use artifacts. An artifact one dot lower than the one you are trying to build can be substituted for an EMC. This comes to over 200 Artifact 1s per Artifact 5. First Age production levels were only possible because they had world-spanning collection and transportation infrastructure to keep the FCs supplied with EMCs and artifact components.

                                                        I do not have and Ledaal could not afford to keep me supplied with as many EMCs as I would need.

                                                        My way around this was the Dragon Kings. They have two different charms that let them make an Artifact 1 in a scene. However, this does not solve all my problems. Combining these Artifact 1s into progressively higher level components all take time. Months for every step if we keep to the Craft Slot system. Assuming I have enough crafters to rope into it, it would take 5+4+3+2=14 months to make 1 Artifact 5 from scratch.(1)

                                                        Solars get Design Beyond Limit. This is HUGE in its impact because it pushes artifacts down on level for purposes of crafting. Which means an Artifact 5 would require 4 Artifact 3 components instead of 5 Artifact 4 components. That saves massive amounts of time and material. Art of Permanence + DBL means that for the same cost as modern artificers pay they could craft stuff that doesn't require maintenance, simplifying their supply chains enormously.

                                                        (1) Its actually worse with batches since being able to make a whole bunch of artifacts likewise increases the number of components you need.
