            First the heave... then the ho!... heh heh Freddy loses himself in thought. With his arms aching against the door frame, and his senses inured to the surroundings, his mind is lost amid a sea of bloody memories, all more real than the moment at hand. He pulls on the door, and hears metal snap, plastic crack, and wires tensing. He holds his pull, like a child in game of tug-o-war, or like an anchor in a tide... come on now... easy does it... and he keeps at it.

            The acid builds in his muscles but he can't feel it; the dust stings his face but he can't tell; the door pushes back against his actions but Freddy won't relent. SNAP! CRUNCH! ROOOAAAAR goes the metal, and the door comes off the car. Freddy staggers back one step at a time, steadies himself, and then lowers the door down by his side. Sweat rolls and stings and itches at him under his clothes, and he wipes his brow with the back of his sleeve. He looks inside the car and sees the bloody mess and suddenly remembers seeing someone he knew. He turns and sees the familiar stitched together form of ... Meg? Mal... Malory something? That's right, she's the one that sewed my leg up...

            "Hey Mal" he stutters out, "you OK?" seemingly oblivious to the more pressing concern.
