                                        Lynx groans and flops to his side. He gazes upwards at the sky, trying to draw in his surroundings. He feels the lingering thrill of the dream, trying to relive it, to remember... Then he spots the elderly man.

                                        Oh. There's an elderly man here.

                                        He shifts and sits up in one motion, stretching his shoulders... ...Where's my shirt?

                                        "I'm okay, thank you, how are you?" he replies absently with a slight nod to the man. He rubs his beard, then scrambles up on his feet easily with the help of his body control, absorbs his surroundings, and focuses on Dr. Asphodel and Ragnar.

                                        "Ragnar? Dr. Asphodel? Are you hurt?" He kneels next to them in turn, giving them cursory examinations as his senses instinctively sink into those of Life - and Life only - to make sure they're okay. During his examination, he glances at the man with his exposed senses, drawing in his scent, his breath, his essence, and when his observations are done, he lets go of the Primal Wild.

                                        Lynx activates Life Senses momentarily, checks Ragnar, Dr. Asphodel and the man, and then turns his Senses off.

                                        He's learning!

                                            Moonstone
                                            You hear and see Phoenix is not running after you as you instructed. He is instead turning to face the monster alone, with nothing but a broom. You see it wrap a tentacle around him and squeeze, trying to crush him. It seems to be enjoying it.

                                            Ahead, you see an exit to this room: a cracked door that opens to reveal a tall spiral staircase leading up and away. The rumbling is growing so loud and the floors are vibrating so much that the room seems to be threatening to fall apart.

                                            You rip the Abyssal energy from your soul. It hurts intensely, but you feel cleaner somehow.


                                            Ragnar
                                            The older man studies you curiously and smiles. "You did well, then. I was led here by my own Fate spell. I knew if I came here in time, I might be able to find you all. My name is Mathias. I have so much more to tell you, and to ask you..." He frowns again, his face pained with worry. "But we must go, and quickly. I don't know the full story, but there is another group of fledgling mages not far from here in incredible danger -- and the city may be as well."

                                            Mathias studies his watch. "The power is back on, so that means the next train will be here...yes, in just a moment." He looks at each of you and asks, "Please, come with me!" He motions for you to follow and points down the street. The nearest train stop is about a block away. Mathias looks both ways out of the alley and then begins to jog towards the train station.

                                            Despite the distractions, you notice something odd in your pocket, a narrow object with a sharp end. Inspecting it reveals a small knife with a steel hilt and a blade made from a jagged, black stone. It looks familiar somehow...maybe something from the dream?


                                            Lynx
                                            You breathe in the smells around you and study your companions closely. They give off their natural scents, with no whiff of anything that suggests toxins, disease, or serious injury.

                                            The man appears similarly healthy and uninjured. If anything, he is more healthy than you would expect for an average man of his apparent age.
