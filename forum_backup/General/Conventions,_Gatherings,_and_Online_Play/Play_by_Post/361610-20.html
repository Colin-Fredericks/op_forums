                                It’s complicated, Flynn wanted to reply. Instead, he said, “I am someone with a strong interest in keeping the people of this town out of the hands of the Mask of Winters.” Flynn held out his hands to see how bright the halo of his anima was. A lack of knowledge could mean their lives and Flynn had enough on his conscience.

                                “The woman who threw the spear at you is called “Thorn of Ages”. She is one of the Mask of Winter’s Deathknights. What is most important for you to remember is what a Deathknight actually is. The Solars have returned to Creation, but some of thier Exaltations have been…corrupted. When the deathknight attacked me, the caste mark of Dawn bled on her forehead. “

                                Flynn slowly rose to his feet, not to startle Nahuel. He brushed off some leaves off of his clothes. “Any mortal who dies in this town will become a ghostly slave of the Mask of Winters. Keeping these people alive will upset his plans. “

But it was more likely that the Lunar would let Flynn leave if Nahuel thought he had a way to contact the mysterious man.
