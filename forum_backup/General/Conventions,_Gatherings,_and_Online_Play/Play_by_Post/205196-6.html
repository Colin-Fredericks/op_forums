            Max was quite oblivious to the fishy woman threatening his life. He presumed she was simply going to stroke his chest and then his chin. And that would be nice. But really, what wouldn't be nice? He'd known the ladies were still doing nice things, but he couldn't really tell what they were. Unless they were mushrooms. The mushrooms were nicest of all. Still, into a fog that thick, The shouting of his name cut through like a searchlight. Somewhat. At least enough for him to turn his attention and his head in its direction, even if it felt like doing so from a thousand miles away. There he saw several women holding Jessica down. Though he supposed that couldn't be. She didn't look like she had the horns or the tail or the hooves or... any of the bovine traits he'd come to associate with Jessica. Nevertheless, the apparently human woman still gave a sense of... Jessica-ness to him and his nose, so he could work with that.

            Still, interesting a sight though it was, Max couldn't perceive a reason he should move for it. Not when it was so comfortable where he was, after all. He was sure Jessica would understand. Of course, he got a reason quick enough when that goat from earlier charged, and scattered the blue women like bowling pins. That got an emotion to focus him through the fungal daze. First was shock. And then anger. Ironically, both he and Nate -- had they been able to articulate their feelings -- would have regarded the women as "theirs." But while the Goat saw them through the lens of domination, the Pig saw them as a pet might see its masters. And this pet was going to protect them.

            With an enraged squeal, Max twisted away from the fish woman threatening him. Surprised, by the sudden movement, her grasp came late, though she did grab one of his legs. With no thought beyond that he needed to remove the impediment to getting where he needed to go, he whirled his head around and sank his teeth into her wrist. She screamed something inhuman as the bite broke the skin and her wrist, and released her grip. And then he was off, charging at the goat on all-fours. He needed to because... Because... He couldn't remember. The fog had gone red.

            Yet something else was different about the swine, even if it was only a trick of the spores still circulating. Max seemed... bigger. There was less pink and more in the way of a bristly brown, less baby fat than hard muscle, and less of large floppy ears than short sharp ones. Most of all, while he himself sometimes forgot he had a pair of dinky little tusks, there was no chance of that with the new prominent pair. Well... Sometimes it is called the Year of the Boar...

            This was the Max that barreled into the goat, likely doing extensive harm to the woman Nate had mounted. To say nothing of at least one of the others, rolled over as they were by the big ball of raging horns, tusks, and hooves.
