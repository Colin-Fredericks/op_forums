                Ok. For my convenience with double-checking math, can you indicate on your sheet which skills are asset skills and your Order rote skill specialties? So maybe behind them, put (Rote Skill) or (Asset Skill), or use *, +, etc. next to the name and a key for which symbols mean what.

                Scalpel is totally fine as a weapon Path tool! And you can use it as a literal weapon, just like a knife, in a pinch.

                Some ideas for other short-term Aspirations (remember, these can be things you want for your character, even if he wouldn't wish it on himself): Interact with a werewolf, study a dead body, witness a killing, study an object in Twilight, etc.

                I was about to say that the rules won't let you start with the 3rd dot in a non-Ruling arcanum. Turns out I was wrong! As long as you only have 3 dots in one arcanum, and 3-5 are spent in Ruling arcana, you're good. Now, remember that any improvised casting from Life spells will cost you 1 additional mana, so many people prefer to put them into Ruling arcana simply because it's more efficient and makes sense. But you're welcome to distribute them however you like as the rules allow.

                                                            Reviewing characters again to see how things are coming.

                                                            Retias
                                                            Looks like you're just about done here! Thanks for making the changes. The only thing that I see is I'd like to make a few tweaks to the familiar. I made some comments about this in the other OOC thread, but for a rank 2 familiar, I'd like to start with 11 attribute dots and 4 numina. I will grant additional dots to the familiar as you gain experience, which you can use to help develop the familiar.

                                                            So, to make the changes, I'd like you to add 1 more numina and drop 3 of the attribute points (you're at 14 total currently). Per RAW, you can also stick at 3 numina and add 1 more dot to an Influence domain.

                                                            In terms of ban and bane (p. 255-256), since you're going with rank 2, let's make those more obscure and potentially serious. I'll make some suggestions, which you can either keep or pick something else along a similar vein.

                                                            Ban: The machine spirit must corrupt a computer at least once per month, or lose all Willpower.
                                                            Bane: Rainwater collected during a thunderstorm.


                                                            wtaylorjr2001
                                                            Looking much better. A few more suggestions/tweaks.

                                                            You actually have 1 more attribute dot to put in somewhere. They should be a 3/4/5 split + 1 bonus for Resolve or Composure. I think when you went back and tweaked those, you forgot to add the bonus dot. For skills, you seem to be missing Skill Specialties now; pick 3 related to skills of your choosing. Please also indicate your Rote Skill Specialties for your Order (Investigation, Occult, Survival for Mysterium).

                                                            I am ok with you having Gnosis 3. Your character is going to begin poor. You can't start with any equipment that would be higher than availability 1. You can have an apartment or home, but it's cramped and not great quality. You could have a beat-down vehicle if your character would have one. Because you have Gnosis 3, mark on your sheet that your max mana is 13 and your max mana/turn is 3. You also need to pick a second Obsession, as characters with Gnosis 3 start with 2 of those in play.

                                                            For rotes/praxes, this is your call, but you might not want Exceptional Luck as both a praxis and a rote. You can't have both qualities on any given roll; either you use it as a rote, or you use it as a praxis. Because some of the benefits overlap, it's probably smarter to pick different options, but that's your call.

                                                            For the nimbus, your tilt can have a total of +2 bonuses or penalties to attribute or dice rolls. Remember that these will apply to other characters who view you casting a spell, not to yourself. Check out p. 88-90 for details, with info about creating nimbus tilts on p. 90. For the "fae quality", you could give something like -1 Composure, -1 Wits as onlookers find themselves befuddled by the aura of Arcadia around you. Just an idea.

                                                            As I said before, please make your Obsessions and Aspirations more specific, and specify which Aspirations are short- vs. long-term.
