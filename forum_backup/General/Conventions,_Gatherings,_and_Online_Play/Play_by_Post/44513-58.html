                                        Alrighty, then, smart guy. How about Dero's origin story? What wacky hijinks did he get in before he met Tirien?

                                        As for Magnusar, well, that's because at their core, Sidereals are the wise badass old mentor Exalts. Look back at their mention in 1e core (Or take my word for it, because no one ought go back to 1e for anything.) where they're mentioned as being powerful and wise Exalts, often being sorcerers. Now, to Western eyes, the wise kung fu mentor character resonates with us, so Sidereals, being the Ben Kenobi to the Solar Luke Skywalker, got powerful martial arts to compensate for their lack of higher-end charms. Flash forward after several attempts to "Balance" this, and well, you get where we are. Personally, I'm of a mind that Sidereal MAs are fine as they are. Yes, they're overpowered, but look at their prerequisites; usually in excess of Essence 6, at which time, Solars can sword so hard, they can wield an infinite number of weapons, Terrestrials get access to crazy elemental typhoon blasts and Lunars can become monsters so large the Titan of HUGE gets a little peeved by their existence. So, yeah, Obsidian Shards of Infinity or Border of Kaleidoscopic Logic styles being able to make mincemeat of any reasonable Solar PCs? Well, DUH, they're the last resort weapons of creatures WAY out of their weight class.

                                        But, that's my two cents. You could probably have a "Father Time" style that uses swords, though. Maybe letting you do rerolls as you rewind time to do certain things. Sure, it dances around "No time travel," but, so do many SWILIHN and Sidereal charms, so I see no foul here
