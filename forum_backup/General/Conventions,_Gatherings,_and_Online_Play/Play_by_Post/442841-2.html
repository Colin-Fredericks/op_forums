                        A good way to do this by group consensus might be to each mention three aspects, or qualities, or characteristics you want the spirit/pack to have. Since we're an urban pack, Soonmot, I don't think you need to worry about it not representing the city in SOME fashion, so you so far suggested nightlife or a landmark in the area - throw in some other ideas if you like.

                        Once we all put in our two cents, we'll organize them and see if anyone likes or can combine their characteristics and then we'll come up with an idea from there.

                        For example:

                        Since we don't have an Elodoth yet, I think I'd like an influential or socially confident totem who can help us pick up the slack with a strength we don't have too much of that ourselves.

                        I also like the idea of a spirit tied to a specific place since it can help give us a dedicated locus, a base of operations, and really flavor what we as a group value and want to protect in Victory City.

                        Thirdly, so far we have a very specialized bunch of people here - scouts and trackers and tacticians and defenders and 'support' with rites. We clearly aren't all about barreling in and being made of pure undestructablium, so I think our pack is putting an emphasis on precise execution as we tactically prepare with Aliza's visions to guide Violet's scouting and Indiana's skirmishing before Joseph backs up Akule with the big punch. Looking at what we have I'm a little worried we are too delicate... but then... we're werewolves. So probably not.

                                                    Treaty of the Father Rules

                                                    1. Make no treaspass into another's territory without first acquiring approval from that territory's alpha.

                                                    2. The enemies of Father Wolf are enemies to all the People. All information regarding the Hosts are to be shared freely among Father Wolf's children.

                                                    3. Each wolf's prey is their own and must take responsibility for their hunt should it endanger another Pack's territory.

                                                    4. Any oath breakers of the treaty are subject to the tribal laws of the accusser's Pack. Failure to adhere to the sentence of said tribal laws threaten the Treaty of the Wolf and their lives shall be forfeit.

                                                    5. Should a Pack fall and their territory become disputed between two or more Pack then a Sacred Challenge may be called so that one may rightfully claim said territory.
