    Very true. You live up to your screen name, Wise Old Guru.

    So I have been reading up on Arcane Fate. One success to remember the Sidereal seems to be a bit easy to achieve. However, apparently the dice roll isn't to remember the Sidereal, but remember the event with the Sidereal. The player trying to remember the event has to roll dice each time they try to recall the event, instead of just when the events ends.

    For example, if Swimming Owl and Flynn weren't wearing Resplendent Destinies during their conversation, then the Servant would have to roll Wits + Integrity - 3 each time he thinks about the conversation. If he gets one success he remembers the event at that specific time. If he fails, he forgets that Swimming Owl and Flynn were even there. If he gets three successes, then he will remember the EVENT without having the roll dice each time.

    So, since Flynn and Swimming Owl were in Resplendent Destinies during their conversation, the Servant will remember the conversation until the Destiny ends. Though if Swimming Owl ends her Destiny and Flynn doesn't, the Servant would remember Flynn talking to....someone....

    Seeing through Resplendent Destinies is different that remember events. A Sidereal gets a 3 dice bonus for convincing others that they are a Simple Musician, not a Scheming Fate Ninja. The person trying to see though the False Destiny loses 3 dice on their roll to see through the destiny. I assume you can use Investigation or something for this roll instead of Integrity. I am assuming that if you do see though the destiny, you are subject to the above Wit + Integrity - 3 roll to remember the time you saw through a Sidereal's Resplendent Destiny.

    If Flynn decides to take off his False Fate and break out with some Sweet Ninja Moves, then it would be extremely difficult to connect Flynn the Sidereal with Flynn the musician. And if a player does make the connection, then there is a good chance they will forget about it as time goes on.
