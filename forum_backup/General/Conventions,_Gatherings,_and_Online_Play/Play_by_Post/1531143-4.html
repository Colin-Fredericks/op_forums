    Caerus takes the scrap of paper out of his pocket. The handwriting is very neat and tidy. Like a school teachers. He heaves a sigh, and dials the number. He hears a distant roll of faint thunder and smells ozone, indicating that his peripheral mage sight catches some magical effect. The phone call picks up. He quickly blurts out a bunch of incoherent nonsense of about Jo, and being worried about her, and rats in her Gran's building. Fortunately, somewhere in there he has the presence of mind to mention his name.

    "Oh hello! Edouard is it? From Jo's parkour videos. Um, sure. I don't have long to chat. I'm going over to the housing archives to look for maintenance records. Yeah, if you have information on the rats, I'd love to hear it for my story. And, um, I'm a bit worried about Jo myself. I'll have Nigel and Mr. Singh let you up, and you can meet me in the restaurant. Can you hand the phone over to Nigel?"

    Caerus hands the phone over to the doorman who listens to Trish for a minute, seeming a little surprised, and still eyeing Edouard with suspicion. "Yes, well it is unusual, but if you vouch for him." He hands the phone back to Caerus.

            Earlier in the day, around noon...

            The doorman let's Caerus into the lobby of the massive skyscraper, the Spire. The carpet inside is a rich red and there is high two story glass windows and wood paneling. There are crystal chandeliers high above. This place vibes of more money than Edouard ever thought existed.

            A huge like 2m tall Indian dude in a turban and expensive suit shows him over to the lift. The lift is really fast, like a rocket, and the world below looks like it's getting smaller and smaller through the windows as Caerus ascends.

            He's shown into the restaurant which again just smells like millions of pounds sterling. Everything looks like it's made out of silver and gold and crystal. He looks out at the windows that surround the dining area and it looks like he's on top of the world, looking down at London like he owns the whole city.

            There are two old dudes at the bar, one of them looks Irish, the other one is East Asian or something. Near the bar, sitting at a table is a woman reading a newspaper.

            She looks up and smiles. The expression "knockout" does not even begin to describe the raw beauty and charisma of this woman. She is also Asian, and looks to be in her early twenties, five or six years older than Edouard. She is casually dressed in clothes that don't have flashy labels but look expensive.

            As she stands up to greet Edouard he notices that she is slightly taller than him even though she is wearing flat shoes, about 1.74 m.

            "Hello Edouard. I"m Trish. Trish Takehashi. Please have a seat." If you looked up Posh in the dictionary this woman's photo would be next to the definition. Like in the gossip magazines they are always showing you what celebrities look like when they don't have makeup on and are just going to the shop, and they look like ordinary people. This is the opposite. Being near this woman makes him feel like he's left reality and is inside a movie now.

            "What did you want to talk about? You said you think Jo is in some sort of trouble?"

                    Earlier in the day...

                    Trish begins to take notes in a small booklet while Edouard talks, and talks. She furrows her brow a little at the mention of New Moon productions.

                    "I'm not, uh...familiar with that production company. I could look into it. I guess i do have some connections to the, um, film industry. My friend Saulil has many business connections. This is actually "his" building," she looks around at her surroundings, "...sort of."

                    "Maybe he could help. He's often looking for promising talent. Maybe you could apply to be an intern in his Real Estate development company. It sounds like based on your school performance, and your work ethic and entrepreneurial spirit, you will have a bright future. It would certainly be a crime to let your talents go to waste."

                    It's hard for Edouard to tell, but maybe there was a hint of a smirk on Trish's face as she said this last bit.

                    "I think with Jo...well we will see. I'm glad she's on this fitness routine. I think it will help with her health problems. Jack knows more about that then I do. And if she can hold down this job for awhile, and learn to get along with people a little. I do worry about her not sleeping much at home. Maybe there is something to be done about her housing situation. But you can only help someone as much as they want to be helped."

                    "I'm planning on going over to the government archives in a little bit to do some research on the building maintenance in Jo's Gran's flat building. It seems like something is off there. And if there is some sort of mismanagement, my paper's readers will certainly want to know about it. The Borough Council and the housing authority must be held accountable to their constituents. Maybe I can arrange a meeting between you and Saulil before I go."
