                                            I have ideas to get everyone together, and we'll get there soon! :P Don't worry about your magic "spoiling" or causing issues with the plot, the whole idea of Mage is that you should be able to do amazing things -- but with a potential cost for overreaching.

                                            I do have a question that I would like everyone's feedback on. How do you want to handle dice rolls?

                                            My preference for actually getting the numbers would be for people to use any online site that archives results, so you can direct perma-link to the results. I've used Coyote Code roller for this, but lately my favorite has been Orokos because I know I can load it at work.

                                            Some of the dice rolling issues can be discussed more easily in real time, like when a ST tells you what to subtract for Defense on a roll before you make it. For PbP games, I can tell you what to subtract before I have you roll. I can do Withstand effects for spell effects after the roll results without revealing what the withstand effect is, but I'll have to make Defense obvious. Is that ok? Does anyone else have any suggestions for rolling conventions for PBP games?

                                                07-08-2016, 09:28 PM
                                                Orokos is good for me.

                                                IMO a good way to deal with Defense and other dicepool penalties, if you haven't called for a roll yourself and told us what they are, is to ignore the last X dice in our pool (and tell us that you're doing that.) Like if we're in the middle of a scene and I spontaneously say "I want to roll Wits+Politics (Consilium) to see if I know something about this guy", I would just roll 6d10 or whatever and post the results: 4, 8, 1, 1, 9, 2. Two successes.

                                                When you reply, you could then say "Actually your Consilium specialty does not apply and there's a -1 penalty due to the obscurity of the information, so your dicepool would be 4d10 and the last two dice don't count." My result would be treated as 4, 8, 1, 1. Just the one success. Conversely you could also apply a bonus to the roll that I wasn't aware of, and roll 1 or 2 more dice yourself.

                                                The main advantage to doing things that way, at least having the option to do it that way when we suggest a roll ourselves, is to streamline things for PbP and have less back and forth. On the other hand, some players don't like the GM "altering" their result once they've already rolled, and that's fine.
