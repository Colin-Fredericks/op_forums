                                        "I like you, Dear Golden Flower, and I'd hate for this to end so soon. After all, I--" Zephyr managed, before her captive began his rite, and a great steaming mass surrounds the Archemizer.

                                        Baprath the Devourer, elder god of Smoke, one of Autocthon's first creations, a powerful tool of cleansing and rebirth, his vapors boil the Metody-flesh of the hellstrider, and Zephyr's cries of confusion and pain ring out like a clarion-call, dropping Wren out of harm's way. Under the strain of the assault, the hellstrider wheezes, and finally evaporates, unable to withstand the touch of Baprath. out of the clearing smoke, though, is an unusual sight.Scarlet Sephyr stands there, surrounded by a cycle of moving pictures and the faint glow of a halo about her head.

                                        The pictures tell of a young girl on a distant planet, whose parents died of plague. On their windswept planet of hard rock, the girl was treated with deference and respect, a holy woman in training, whose eccentricities were tolerated due to the power she wielded in dealings with God and Daeva. However...

                                        A pale, towering figure of stunning masculine beauty; the Alabaster Effigy of Glamor, appeared on her planet, and demanded that the natives build a tower to His magnificence. The girl, with no one to defend her, was taken as a slave for the construction. There, the girl saw her neighbors suffering greatly, and gave them the mercy of release from their pains. This brought her higher and higher up the tower until she was face to face with the Effigy. Impressed with her tenacity and beauty in spite of her years of turnoil, the Effigy offered her a new opportunity.

                                        "Become my Champion, and I will make this planet a paradise the likes of which you can only dream."

                                        The girl responded by tearing off the jade nail that held the capstone in place, and leaping off the tower as it collapsed. However, instead of dying terribly, the Wind grabbed her, and granted her the Exaltation due to her holiness.

                                        "You done marveling at my past? The Mandala of Hermit's Life has some embarrassing side effects," Zephyr says, wiping the blood off her brow," I will concede defeat today, but know this, Love; you'll face my ministrations sooner or later." she then leaps off, running off into the sky. With the disappearance of both their commanders, the shades slink off, leaving the battlefield.

                                        The Distraction Team is victorious! What will you do now?
