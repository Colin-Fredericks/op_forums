                                    Scholar turns back and heads to a shop where he could sell one of the golden figurines he created. He wants to gain a little experience in selling and bartering. He has prepared himself emotionally to lose money on the deal as normally he would sell his creations to Laughter for him to resell for a profit. Even though everything in the distance is a blur, when he gets closer to the stores, with a squint he can read the signs on the streets and the stores. He is looking for a specific store. He wants a goldsmith so that he can sell a figurine for golden ingots. These are fairly large figurines as he used one bar or ingot of gold for each figurine. He walks the market streets to answer the question, "is there a goldsmith in this city?"

                                        (Laughter)

                                        The couple's encounter was a roiling, passionate and wild swell, plumbing the depths of their hearts in ways that are causing this metaphor to fall apart, just as foam upon the sea's waves.As Laughter enjoyed the afterglow, though, he could hear singing from abovedecks in a clarion voice he was unfamiliar with.

                                        "Ah, young love. That is the true message of the Booty Call of Cthulhu. Whoever Cthulhu is--oh snap!" the voice called as the angry voices of his crew converged on the singer and Laughter hears the telltale splash of someone leaping overboard. A moment later, a knock on the door brings Laughter's first mate, a stocky veteran of the seas, quietly informing you that Lessons and Diane wish to speak with them. A moment later, the trio enter just in time for Essen to compose herself, entirely human now, donning a new gown from where, Laughter cannot guess.

                                        Well, the gang's here. What will you discuss?

                                        (Lessons and Diane)

                                        The pair are ushered aboard just in time to see a small furry feline chap wearing a dapper bowler hat and carrying a violin, leap overboard with the ship's crew in hot pursuit. Ignoring that, the first mate guides you to the captain's quarters, where you find Essen and Laughter sitting opposite one another, engaging in a pleasant chat.

                                        What will you do?
