    (Vykos)

    The Blood Ape begins his habitual singing whenever doing anything as he tidies up. This time, he's singing a rather over-dramatic song about an alluring Angyalka courtesan in his deep, resonant Baritone. This shocks the metody, who once again retrieves his journal(?)

    "The dumbass Solar is apparently also a sadist," The metody dictates, "It's ordering me to eat a whole deer carcass. Clearly it can't stand letting me get any comfort in this trip, and letting me keep off those liters? Well, the joke's on it. I bet that it doesn't even know that it summoned one of the Demon City's greatest performers is right here, cleaning up it lab.Yeah, I met Erdus the Bloody-Tongued! I'm too nervous to ask him for his autograph, though."

    The Blood-ape ceases his sweeping for a moment, and takes the book for the metody, flipping it to the front page. Retrieving a diamond stylus from a lab table, the signs the book before returning to his task.

    "Erdus signed my book!" the metody dictates, "Anyways, the dumbass solar doesn't seem to have any equipment for making chalcanth. This is a good sign, but I'm not letting my guard down. Hope it doesn't melt me down into Theion To."

    The metody then gets to work consuming and dissolving the remains of his experiments.

    Vykos heads to the library, and looks through the restricted section of the Library. There he finds one of the most respected and ancient tomes of Infernal Lore, the Book of Ten-Thousand Limbs. Or so it seems. While the section on first circle demons is mostly intact (save some water-damage), the sections on second and third circle demons are curiously blank. Flipping through it, Vykos finds a note, in a rough, choppy hand:

    That you are looking for this shows some potentially troublesome designs. The complete tome is in my possession. If your designs are just, then speak to me, and I may grant you access to the the Tome.

    -Chancellor Nikoloi Feiro.

                        (Yi)

                        Yi makes his trip across town, and many a pedestrian stops to gawk and whisper to each other as he passes. Eventually, Yi ends up at a modestly sized townhouse. It is clean and well maintained, though nothing in particular sets it out. A brief conversation with the middle-aged woman that answers the door earns you entry. Once inside, she knocks on a nearby door saying, "Grandfather? Some priest is here to see you."

                        Yi hears a muffled voice in the room; it is clearly male, but it unintelligible. The woman opens the door, revealing a large study. It is cluttered with a papers, covered in maps and complex mathematical formulae, haphazardly placed around the room and tacked into the walls.

                        Nikoloi Argyrus stands about them, stepping over piles of reference books, jotting down notes and calculations on seemingly random sections of a dozen papers.

                        "The priest, Grandfather," the woman says. She glances to a cluttered table, where a tea set sits. "You haven't touched your tea. The apothecary was quite strict, Grandfather."

                        "Bah. It tastes like Haltan swamp water," the elderly looking Earth aspect says, "and the apothecaries are full of shit. I've got another sixty years before I have to worry about my age catching up to me."

                        The woman gives Argyrus a deathly glare before retrieving the tea set. Declaring that she will make him more, she departs.

                        The terrestrial drops himself into a thickly cushioned chair, and gestures for Yi to take another.

                        "You're that priest that took the stage with Little Larissa the other day," Argyrus says, "what do you want with me?"

                                        Blossom leads Tirien a little bit away from the assembled chiefs, speaking softly to her and glancing around occasionally to be sure they are not overheard. The observation of their surroundings, the care not to speak while anyone else is too close... it seems like a reflex action for her, rather than something she has to think about doing. Stealth and subtlety is clearly her way of life.

                                        "Look," she says softly, a note of exasperation in her voice, "I think you need to understand a little about how the Sidereal Exalted work. You Lunars and Solars like to be out in front, bright and shiny and noticeable, inspiring people and leading and all that stuff. But my people don't do that. Well, not usually," she amended with a small shrug. "There are always exceptions. But 9 times out of 10, we work best if we stay in the background, or the shadows, unnoticed, especially if there's a bright Solar or Lunar around to take most of the attention." She stops glancing around for a moment to look Tirien in the eye. "There's a very old saying among my people. 'The Solars lead and reign, the Lunars protect and inspire, the Terrestrials serve and soldier, while the Sidereals do everything else.' It's a simplistic way of looking at it," Blossom continues with a shrug, "but it goes to show our mentality, yes? We let you other Exalted stand in the forefront, because that's what you do best, while we stand in the background, quietly making sure the little details get taken care of, because that's what we do best. Now, this is doubly true for me, since my job is gathering information. I work best when I go unnoticed, or if I am noticed, when no one pays any special attention to me. So, in the future, please, don't call me out like that in front of others unless we've discussed it beforehand, or it's absolutely necessary. And especially don't reveal my true nature."

                                        Blossom continues slowly walking with Tirien, clearly having more to talk about, but pausing to give the Lunar a moment to speak.

                                                It had been a long time since Yi had been able to "talk shop" with anyone. Back in Lookshy the younger engineers had been afraid of his connections and the older ones had looked down on his origin or mortality. Since then he had lack for someone his intellectual equal, knowledgeable in something that interested him, or both. His time with Hyuga had been mostly taken up with training, each trading secrets of solar magic, and left little time for technical details.

                                                Do not screw this up!

                                                "Might I use a sheet of your paper and something to write with?" At a nod he began to draw as he spoke.

                                                "I am afraid you are putting the cart of effect before the horse of cause lord Nikoloi. The geomantic disturbances are a desirable benefit the project should bring about, though I confess that the road network is only a portion of the greater plan. The eight fold pattern..."

                                                The rudiments of an eight pointed star about a circle and within one now covered the paper with the trigram shorthand of the sixty-four changes of essence written near intersections of lines.

                                                "Is the fundamental plan of first age cities, archaeological evidence from the southeast suggests it may predate the first age. Instead of specific structures I mean to anchor the crossings with cities and towns. To be sure, roads will not be appropriate for all the interconnections. Canals will serve for most of the rest, with sky paths and tunnels completing the arrangement. At first the roll will likely be served by underground aqueducts to link groundwater wells to the main river systems for water and semaphore and messenger bird routes for air. True tunnels and skyship lines will serve better once the infrastructure is more developed."

                                                Now he began to add the map of Novalesk and surrounding nations, showing how many of the cities were already located at favorable locations for such a network. Notable were the rivers, forming a great lake around Novalesk City and branching out into give enormous canals.

                                                "Once completed, along with civic alterations, the whole of the nation will serve to magnify all prayers uttered to such an extent that the Sanctum of Origenes will not have room for him to stand and the floor of the Jade Pleasure Dome will flood so that golden ambrosia runs like rivers from its doors. That is but the beginning though. The resonance between the nationwide occult matrix and the infinite energies of the Sun will flood essence into the geomancy, causing the nation to act as a quasi-elemental pole. Demesne will grow about the region like trees in an eastern forest. Secondary magical effects such as speeding all travel along the roads and reducing the fatigue and burden of travelers, normally the province of the surviving artifact roads, will become ubiquitous from the derived magical energies.

                                                It goes without saying that by capping even a fraction of the demesnes a network of war manses bettered only by the Defense Grid could be produced."

                                                Along the sides, notations grew into blocks of text explaining essence gyre formation, static and dynamic resonance of the nth order, necessary resplendent mythitecture construction, and the like.

                                                "When complete, it will be a triumph of sympathetic megafetish worthy to stand with the greatest accomplishments of the any age. A wheel within the wheel and an instrument on which the strings of Creation may be played. Call me mad Lord Nikoloi, say it is foolishness, but look at this and tell me it is not glorious."

                                                He stood to give the elderly man a better look at what he had rendered. He needed no magic to look aglow, fervor burned just as bright within him.

                                                "Know this also, I have power enough to see it done in your lifetime. Before the Sun who chose me and the Dragons I have revered and to the Creation I have sworn to exalted higher then I have been, I swear it."
