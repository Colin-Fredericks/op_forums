                        (Yi)
                        Yi feels an unexpected tug on the back of his robe. Looking for it's source, he finds Larissa's expectantly strong grip. She is looking at the Spyroi, and she simply asks, "Eugene?"

                        Eugene is himself already in motion. He suddenly appears uncomfortable unarmed and unarmored. He nods, saying, "I'll organize this end. You'll get there faster without me."

                        Larissa grins wolfishly, and Yi finds himself thrown over her shoulder without feeling the intervening motion. Larissa sprints out of the building, and into the streets, narrowly avoiding many. The crowd on the streets, however, are apparently too much for her, and she leaps onto a nearby building, avoiding the crowds by leaping from rooftop to rooftop.

                        (Hyuga and Jensven)
                        Terror and panic abound on the riverfront. Shadow indicates that something is going on in that direction, similar to what happened on the day of Jensven's exaltation. Should Jensven follow Hyuga, they find the foundry in the midst of a fierce battle. Small pockets of goblins make battle with some Novaleski soldiers. A particularly large group of goblins guards the large brass doors of the facility.

                        (Novalesk [all])
                        Hyuga and Jensven hear a voice pierce through the din of battle. A young-looking woman clad in armored robes hefts a broad straight sword, its surface covered in faintly glowing runes. Yi is behind her, having been just dumped unceremoniously upon the ground. "You have been Judged, Fae," Larissa says, her voice cold and piercing, "And you have been found wanting." She swings her blade, and despite standing over ten yards from the nearest goblin, the mass of goblins simply unravels, dissolving into ambient essence.

                        Around you, the soldiers appear to have the goblins in hand. Larissa slumps down somewhat, likely tired from sprinting across town and then destroying a goblin strike-team. "Thier leader is likely inside," she says.

                                            (Novalesk)
                                            Larissa strides into the foundry, her blade bare. Those of you who follow her see the interior of the foundry; long lines of clay moldings sit in neat lines along the interior, running parallel to the waterfront (of which you are on the far side). A series of pulley systems run along the rails dangling from the ceiling, holding the buckets which deliver the molten metals to the forge. Two openings lead out to the waterfront at each side of the open complex; judging from the layout of the complex, the one to your right delivers raw materials to the large furnace nearby, while the one on the left presumably is used to ship the processed metals from the complex.

                                            You see a half dozen large insectoid creatures, looking something like a cross between a leaf insect nymph, a spiny lobster and a stag beetle. Four of these monstrosities are busy smashing up the molds and production lines. The other two are busily spraying gouts of freezing mist into the furnace. Each one has a beastial humanoid riding them.

                                            On a catwalk above the furnace, a strange figure with crazily spiked hair and strange erratically belted clothing, stands with a huge daiklaive over his shoulder.

                                            When you enter, he turns and points his sword at you, saying, "YOooUuu are laaate! Don'tYou know YOU've gotto stay ON the script?"
