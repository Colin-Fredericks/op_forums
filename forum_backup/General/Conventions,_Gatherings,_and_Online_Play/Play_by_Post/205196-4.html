    Max had also gotten to around his halfway mark by this time. He'd also probably already eaten a greater volume than Nate's or Jessica's feedbags held in total. Maybe even combined. There was just... a lot of slop in there. It struck him as extraordinarily unfair; he was a city boy, but he'd always thought a full trough was usually intended for several pigs at once. In a fair world, Max would have already... ... No, scratch that. In a fair world, Max wouldn't have had to eat any. Nor would any of them for that matter.

    But alas, the world wasn't so fair. And so not only did Max eat the horrible stuff, he ate at quite a rapid pace, looking all the more so next to Nate's. Anything solid he pulled out disappeared within a few bites. As for the liquid, well... The poor Pig had kept to his aim of not gorging snout to trough in true porcine fashion -- though the practicality and temptation yet lingered. But anyone who hadn't been looking might at first assume otherwise, what with the splashes and speckles of slop-water on his face and front.

    He'd also unfortunately found his thoughts drifting away from British comedies, mental course reviews, and other strictly human things. He'd instead gone with an almost perverse fascination to deciphering what was in his trough, like some sort of puzzle. It would have been impossible for anyone without his snout, and Max actually found himself impressed. Despite how thoroughly mashed up all the food (and "food") had become, he could still pick out individual scents if he tried. If noses were computers, it was as though he'd had his human model replaced with one that had abandoned all sense of style for raw power. 'It better be good for that,' he thought morosely. 'It's certainly using enough facial real estate...'

    Lost in his thoughts, Max was shaken out of it by Nate's question. The Pig had a big mouthful of... oh, something that had probably been green and leafy, long ago. Suddenly self-conscious, he awkwardly swallowed it down, and wound up coughing a bit for his trouble. Breathing deep, he appreciated the excuse to turn from the trough and talk to someone. "Sorry. Um, oh, you know..." he said, putting on a brave face, "I'm doing alright." He felt something stuck on his face, just outside his lower jaw. He brought a slop-stained finger to the site and found a leaf had gotten stuck in one of his little tusks. With a porcine grunt of annoyance, he plucked it free and flicked it away. "It's... pretty disgusting. But it really doesn't taste as bad as it looks. It... honestly doesn't taste that bad at all..." He coughed again, the brave face eroding a bit. "I'm... I'm sorta deciding whether that's a good thing or not... And really hoping I don't have to eat all of it..." He licked his chops. A groan with a hint of a squeal made clear how he wished he hadn't.

    "Anyway!" Max forged on, eager to not to dwell on it. "Thanks for asking. Really. I know this can't be easy for any of us, so... Uh, so, how about you? How are you handling this?" The concern was genuine, even if he also welcomed the chance to get out of his own head. Or pick up another coping mechanism.

His tactic of choice involved jumping at another question, further away. "I mean... What's her name?" Yes, pigs were not especially well known for their long jump.

                                Max winced again on hearing about the breakup over deception. He couldn't really think of anything to add but a sympathetic look, so such was all he did. Though Nate still had things to say. Most of them made sense, and honestly had Max feeling a bit relieved. "You're right," he said, smiling a bit, "If we stare the animal side head-on, it can't take over so easy. Thanks." Though the bit about Jessica did make him wrinkle his snout a bit. "Well... Yeah, she's confused. But who isn't, here? Her plan's risky, but her idea's pretty--"

                                Max turned in time to see his trough sinking. Like Nate with his bag, he lurched toward it and tried to shovel a last few scoops of slop down his throat. Also like Nate, he found his hand brushing the grass on the other side. 'Aww, c'mon! No warning?' He took a deep breath and let out a sigh. Part of it was frustration at the meal getting away, while the rest was relief. He wouldn't wind up shoving his head into that garbage today. And surely he would be fine until the next feeding: he'd never eaten nearly as much in one sitting. Only one issue... '... Why don't I feel very full?' Perhaps the full trough really was a serving size to his new swinish appetite. In a moment's panic, Max was grateful he'd been a messy eater. He began scooping up what slop detritus he could off of snout, stomach, and limbs, and into his mouth. It was maybe a third of a mouthful altogether, and quickly swallowed. Getting something more in him helped enough to calm down.

                                Still, even if Max wasn't especially hungry, he knew it wouldn't be as long as he'd like before his stomach was rumbling for more. 'Aw, man... Pleeease don't let me root around in the dirt for truffles...' he asked of any who might have listened. He then noticed Nate looking his way. It was an odd look; Max had no idea how to place it. Without thinking, he found himself tilting his head to one side at Nate, like a questioning puppy (or piglet). To be answered with a surprise notice of departure.

                                "Huh? Uh." He turned his gaze toward where he knew the truffles were buried. "W-Wait!" Max said, scrambling back up onto this hooves. Moving felt a bit awkward after all he'd eaten, but he made it up just fine, and followed after Nate a bit. "Hey, uh, n-no reason we've gotta part ways right away, r-right? I mean, uh..." He took a breath. "I'm sorry. I-I don't mean to be a burden. It's just, you're so strong." The pig turned his gaze down and away. "And I'm really, really not. I... I need help." He looked up to Nate cautiously, putting on a nervous smile. "You know I still mean what I said before, right, about smacking me upside the head if I started to go a little... h... h-hog wild?" The kid tried for a forced laugh after the 'joke,' but the emerging oinks worked about as well as Nate's laugh-turned-bleat. It only further revealed the anxiety he was already not hiding especially well. "Just... It would really help to have someone sop me where I need it. C-Could I tag along? Please?"

                                                            The sweet scent would lead them through the bushes to come upon a completely unexpected sight. There in front of them was what appeared to be an oasis, complete with a bathing pond and a water fall cascading down into it from the top of a cliff. Where the waterfall was drawing itself from was a complete mystery, because there didn't seem to be any rivers leading up to it. Their ears were pointed like those of an elf, and their bodies were unnaturally lithe and beautiful like they belonged more in a painting than real life. Those who were in the water had a more amphibious appearance, with light blue scales covering their bodies, fin-shaped ears, webbed digits, and hair the color of sea weed. They too had unnaturally beautiful figures and appeared to be having a splash fight with each other while the green women danced about the mushrooms!
