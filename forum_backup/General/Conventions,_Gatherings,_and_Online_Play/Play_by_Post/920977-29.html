                MagicSwordsman - Just a quick clarification question before I continue with your scene. You say in your post that you're doing Psychic Domination again, but I didn't see any indication of what you want to command it to do. Or did you mean that you wanted to use a Mind 3 spell to attack it?

                Also, I see from your Orokos dice roll links that you're not using the correct dice expression. You have been using xd10h8, but you forgot the o10 part. This means that your dice rolls are currently not benefiting from the basic 10-again property. It looks like it hasn't impacted any of your rolls in your past two posts, but I wanted to point that out. So, make sure to use Xd10o10h8 (or o9 for 9-again, or o8 for 8-again).
