    The Ram...no, Nate cried out at the loss of his horns. All the voices inside his mind howled in pain, as if a piece of his soul had been ripped off. He fell on his knees, devastated. How could he compare himself to the others Beasts when...wait. Why should he have to compare himself to beasts? Why...did it seem so important to him?
    He listened to the Keeper's offer without moving an inch, paralyzed as he was by the horn loss. And then, he began to laugh nervously. Softly, but he couldn't contain himself. He was going to suffer, oh yes. "Keeper" he said in a distant voice devoid of the terror that previously plagued him. "I'm no predator. I'm the Ram. It's not in my nature to bring the lost lambs to the ranch, it should be the Dog's task! "He laughed incredulously. "I can't do what you ask of me in my current state. This is how you made me, mind and body." This is going to hurt like hell. He knew that, of course. But at this point, what could he do but go on? Strangely, he didn't care anymore about his fate. He had given up the tricks, the flattering, and even his pride."I don't want to escape, Master. I don't want to go back to my world with a body like mine. " He swallowed. "I could serve you in different ways. I know music. I wanted to make you a surprise present by creating an instrument so that I could show you my talent. Let me do this instead, Keeper. "

    And with that, he raised his head, eyes looking at the Fae. There wasn't any defiance, hate or fear. Just plain... Resignation.

            "I stand witness to one who wishes to sign the Contract of the Wild!" he called out, and with that, a strong gust of wind blew through the forest, causing the trees to sway and the bushes to rustle. "Do you agree to honor and respect the wilds in exchange for the protection it offers?" another gust of wind and again the trees swayed, although this time in a different direction.

Rather it felt more like the plants were embracing him, offering their comfort if he agreed to the terms. They gently spread across more of his body, entwining around his legs, chest, arms, but not his face.

                    Still a bit jumpy, Max's shoulders jerked as the grass started up him. He relaxed quick enough; it felt... nice. Peaceful. He took a deep breath, grunting out a contented sigh. He'd smelled the grass, trees, and other greenery about, but they all seemed fresher to him now, more lively, and more friendly. It all had an interesting contrast with the mud that still clung to him. That still felt nice, of course, but the grass and the air were different. More genuine, that feeling, with a firm undercurrent of affection. He chuckled; the grass tickled a bit as it climbed. The muck suddenly didn't seem as appealing as it had -- though the pig would probably still have to hold himself back at the next chance. He just felt more refreshed than he had in what felt like ages.

                    Max was certainly tired, but wasn't nodding off. There was still some trepidation, but it was overpowered: he was focused, committed, and even a little excited about this. 'This is where it all turns around. Things'll get better. I can feel it.' Body embraced by the grass about him, no doubt seemed to shadow him as he answered. "I swear. I will give the wilds all respect and dignity that is their due."

                            Max nodded and smiled back up at Sagittarius. "I understand. Thank you for your help. I won't forget it." His smile faded a bit as the Hedge was mentioned. Having signed a Contract only made the phrase seem more ominous, somehow. As though it gave him some small insight into the extent of his bargain, enough to know that the Wilds could only do so much to guard him from that place. Still, he gave another nod. "I understand. I-I'll be ready to face that. When the time comes."

                            Then Max was alone again. But the place certainly felt less hostile. Oh, his nose and ears still brought him smells and sounds of danger, but it made a huge difference that the wilderness itself was not against him, now. He'd scarcely realized how oppressive things had seemed before. Now it felt almost like he was in his own backyard. Max felt freer to focus on more important things than an ever present panic.

                            Not the Hedge quite yet, though. As the earlier adrenaline faded, his stomach reminded him that he was famished. Yes, throwing up those mushrooms had probably been a net gain, but hadn't done his hunger any favors. Nor had the intervening time. He resented his swinish nature showing; he couldn't remember when he'd been so hungry.

                            'Ugh... I can't wait for the Ranch. I have to eat something. Soon. Well... Maybe the Contract will help. Keep me from finding trouble. Or... Or anything too disgusting...' So, hoping for better fortune than last time, Max perked up his snout. He had big misgivings, but a much bigger hunger. The little pig started on a search for his next meal.

                                Nate had to restraint himself from bursting in anger and throwing insults at the Keeper. That would have been an... unfortunate move to make. He gritted his teeth and rubbed his head, before taking the pan flute. Oh, you must feel so clever, you crazy fucker. Since his transformation, he had realized that he kinda looked like a satyr, but with this instrument, the association couldn't be clearer. Looking at the instrument, he wondered if he should be even trying, if admitting his defeat wouldn't be less humiliating. Yes it would. But you're going to suffer anyway ,stupid. Try it. He examined it, and blew tentatively on it. The small sound produced wasn't so encouraging. Alright, it's like blowing on a beer bottle. How hard could it be?! He looked around to his audience, and smiled wryly. It was stage fright all over again!
                                He breathed deeply as he tried to remember songs that should be easy to perform. Unfortunately, his mind was blank. Did...Did he even knew songs other than his own? And his composition were mostly hard rock, nothing that could be reproduced with a simple wind instrument. And so, the Ram would have to rely on pure improvisation. This is gonna be terrible.

                                Closing his eyes, he began his recital. He tried to stick to a tune : better to look confident than just admitting that you don't know what you're doing. To his surprise, it sounded...coherent? It wasn't horrible to hear! He opened his eyes in shock, pausing for a second. He still had a chance! Instinctively, he decided to play something whimsical and sweet, relying on his innate musicality. The flute seemed alive in itself : as he blew his notes, the instrument made his hands move on their own, instantly matching his mouth with the right pipe for the right note. The Ram wanted to laugh incredulously, but the staring of the audience kept him from breaking his performance. The happiness of his tune was contagious : he saw two other Beasts tapping their feet (or rather, their paws and hooves) in rythm and Nate even had to restrain himself from dancing!
                                He ended his song on a high note, and couldn't stop himself from smiling proudly. He didn't know how long he had been playing : It had felt like an hour to him, but it might have been the pressure speaking. Looking up at the Keeper, he instantly lost his smile, his eyes drawn to the shears. "Was...Was it to your liking...Master?"

                                                The keeper stared at Nate, absently clicking the shears every few moments. After a time, he began to speak.

                                                "That was an unusual melody." he said, "I could feel your terror and pain mixed with whimsy and joy. It reminded me of those projects humans refer to as creative art." another couple of clicks from the shears before he tossed them aside and they vanished into thin air the moment he released them from his grasp. "Your creation has amused me for a time, but it was still lacking in quality. You should work on it more."

                                                With that, he patted the goat on the head before looking to the rest of his collection that had still assembled. At some point his monkey had also decided to go missing, which began to spark his anger again before he turned quickly back to the goat.

                                                "Again!" he commanded, "I need a distraction, right now!"


                                                -------------------------------

                                                True to Sagittarius' words, the forest did seem much kinder to Max than before. roots sank into the ground to prevent tripping, and shrubs swayed out of the way to give him a clear path. In fact, it almost seemed like they were opening a path for him, showing him the true way to navigate towards his destination. As if reacting to his hunger, the path would open to a small clearing, where a young apple tree grew in the center. There were no mud pits, no sexy monster, no apparent dangers of any kind. Just a tree, baring fruit for a hungry traveler.

                                                Or at least it looked like an apple tree at first glance. But these were not any apples Max had seen before. They were nearly as big as his fist, and their coloration was unnatural, a vibrant blue with dark purple speckles on one side, while being completely blue on the other. At the trunk of the tree was a sign that read in an alphabet that appeared to be a jumbled mess of squiggles, yet at the same time was perfectly legible. It was as if the sign was communicating in every language simultaneously, so that all could understand it.


                                                "If you would partake of my fruit, I ask you spread my legacy." the sign said, "resolve to plant my seeds, and you may eat your fill."
