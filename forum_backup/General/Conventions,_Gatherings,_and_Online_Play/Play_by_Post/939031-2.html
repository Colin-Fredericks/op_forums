            Marvin Bates (Dr. Bates for his patients and students, just Marv for his wife) left Melody's office and, after taking note of the place and hour of their cabal's afternoon meeting, said goodbye to the young woman (still a girl in Marvin's eyes) wich he came to asociate with the name Spider.

            As he left both women behind and walked the corridors and open spaces of the campus back to his class, he left that part of himself behind too. The one he called Ouroboros Ophis. As a snake shed its skin, so did he shed that name and identity, the only part of his life he could not (should not!) share with his wife, and became just Dr. Bates. No small deal of shame and regret was mixed with the joy that magic brought to his life.

            He not only had, actual, factual, proof that shuch a thing as the soul existed, he was in a privileged position to study it. He saw. He. Saw. He could not help it. He took any chemical product in his lab and new all its properties and aplications even before glacing at them. He now knew the secret mudras and mantras that would repair broken bones, stop bleeding, mend flesh and prevent or banish infection and disease away. Yes, a veritable world of wonder.

            And yet...

            And yet the man that called himself Dr Bates, or Marvin, or just Marv, was not (could not!) be completely happy. Because it was a part of his life he should (have to!) keep away from his other half. His wife, Melissa.

            With a long, tired, sigh, he left Ouroboros Ophis behind him, with Spider and Melody. Like a discarded skin, dry, crumbling.

            At least for a couple of hours he would just be the man his wife knew, and nothing more.

                            Ouroboros Ophis
                            Although you regret leaving the magical world behind you, you find solace in teaching. This is something you value, and although you feel the temptation to focus on magical rather than mundane methods of medical instruction, you find yourself enjoying your lecture. Several students stay after class to ask you follow-up questions and thank you for your dedication to their education. It makes you a little late to the meeting, but you still beat Spider there.


                            Dr. Luck
                            You cast your spell and prepare yourself for any possible threats you might encounter today. After all, you have learned all too well that sometimes divinations can change, especially when others manipulate time or threads of fate.

                            Your return ride to the restaurant is remarkably pleasant. You happen to time the traffic lights just right, allowing you to beat most of the traffic. Someone pulls out of the perfect parking spot as you pull into the parking lot across the street from the restaurant.

                            As mentioned in the OOC thread, you can't stack Exceptional Luck multiple times, but you can mix and match the potency effect (4 in this case, due to your exceptional success and advancing the primary spell factor). You gain Charmed, Steadfast, and 2 uses of 9-again that you can apply to mundane rolls in the next 24 hours or until the spell ends.


                            Spider
                            After your meeting with Melody and Ophis, you take a moment to "speak" with The Machine. Although you lack any control over the Spirit Arcanum, Melody has guided you on ideas for how to use technology to convey messages to The Machine. You've learned that your familiar can "hear" your words through the device you wear in your ear, at least when it is in your immediate surroundings, or other technology nearby. Usually you find yourself typing into a word processor software, on which The Machine can also communicate basic ideas. It has also learned to communicate with you by passing "sounds" through the earpiece.

                            You ask The Machine to scout Jill's address. You make sure to do this hours in advance, because The Machine does not always like to do exactly as you ask -- and her home is still several miles west of you. You occasionally shift your senses and see through The Machine's "eyes" as it drifts west in Twilight Form. Occasionally, it pauses to siphon energy from weaker spirits, and it stops for a while at a computer repair shop where an IT technician is trying to fix a busted hard drive on someone's laptop.

                            Confident that The Machine will eventually accomplish the task on which you sent it, you turn your attention to your ritual. You weave Prime magic around yourself, creating a tight shield that will repel or unravel weaker spells attempting to touch you. You glance at your watch and realize you're an hour late for the cabal meeting, so you head to the meeting location as quickly as possible.

                            You may make a Wits + Composure roll to look through the familiar's senses whenever you like as a reflexive action.


                            Simurgh
                            Like a pet happy to see its master return home, Rebounds-on-Ground hops up to you when it sees you. You feel a sense of eagerness as it jumps into the bottle and ribbits quietly as you stow the bottle in your pocket, safely away from the sunlight. You're one of the first to arrive to the restaurant. Eventually, the rest of your cabal gathers to discuss the information your respective mentors have shared with you.
