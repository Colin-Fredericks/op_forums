                                The assembled garou watch as the young cubs move off into the forest, not interfering in their choice. As the six move away from the clearing and into the trees, the last thing they hear is the voice of Steve Bolen fading away behind them. "All right, Battleseer, we gonna throw down, or what?"

                                The Pine Barrens are a sparse woods even at the best of times, with the acidic soil not allowing the thick undergrowth that is characteristic of heavier woods. Traveling through them is not too difficult, though the presence of a snowfall does make the forest a little more treacherous, with roots, rocks and other tripping hazards obscured by the layer of powdery white snow. The two and a half or three inches of snow itself is powdery enough that it doesn't hinder walking, but just wet enough to be packed. Perfect snowball snow; a great many children throughout the Delaware Valley will be waking up on New Years Day with delight, eager to play.

                                Of course, they won't be spending the moonless night tromping 10+ miles through that snow.

                                Despite the darkness of nightfall, visibility is not much of a problem for the cubs. Their eyes quickly adjust and, even without moonlight, the canopy of the pine barrens is not thick enough in most places to totally block out the starlight and the omnipresent snow reflects that starlight to good effect. So, the cubs make their way fairly quickly through the caern and the bawn, passing a few other clearings where other garou of the sept are gathered, a few cabins and other such accoutrements.

                                After a short time, maybe 20 minutes at the pace they are going, the cubs near the edge of the caern proper. The sept's protectorate extends over the entire forest, of course, but this area marks, for lack of a better term, their inner defensive perimeter- the part of the forest where humans are not permitted to penetrate lightly.

                                The first thing they notice is a small light in the distance- the small red spot of a cigarette as someone draws on it, seeming to make no effort to hide it. The smell of smoke reaches Stir of Echoes a moment later, though still too faint for the others to notice. Stir of Echoes can see a man leaning up against a tree, smoking a cigarette casually. He is looking in the direction that the cubs are coming from, squinting his eyes occasionally, but doesn't seem to register their presence. He wears jeans and a thick flannel jacket, though his clothes seem old and worn, spattered with brownish-red stains. He makes no effort to hide himself, and occasionally looks down at some device on his left wrist. Of course, the others only see the light of the cigarette, occasionally moving as the man moves his hand.
