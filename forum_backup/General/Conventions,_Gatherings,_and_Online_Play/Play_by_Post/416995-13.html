        Do I feign, even to myself, that this is for the best in the long run? If that was true there should be no question. And yet...

                                The woman did not bring her coquettish smile to bear. It might work, enough pepper could make any meat bearable, but it was not the best way. No, the best way was something far more direct.

                                Seating herself primly the she regarded Zadro soberly and seriously. "I want exactly want you might expect, your assistance in the expedition I am putting together. There are many fair sounding words I could give you, bits of flattery such as, 'you must chip away the surface ice before you reach the clean block' but we both know such things are general enough to always apply. No, I waited until last to speak to you because someone had to be last and I sensed you would understand that the I had to secure those who questioned their importance before their enthusiasm waned. You know that you are more necessary than any other sole merchant and so you know that I am willing to give up the most in negotiation to secure your aid. You understand business.

                                So let us get to business. I need clothing, sacks, material for tenting, and myriad other things. But I am forgetting my manners. You offered me a gift did you not? Let me recompense your offer. In the Labyrinth I know how to find those who have the secret of drawing the threads of time. From that thread the gods have woven great scrolls to record the passing of the ages, and some say the future may be read in any loom on which the thread is set. I do not know if that is true, but even that is of less value than what I hope to find there."

                                Now "she" smiled.

                                "There are secrets greater and more wondrous still within the Labyrinth and I know how they might be gained. The great family who sent me would not waste my time with scavenged trinkets. Yet I have my own mission. Contacts, connections, friends who have access to such secrets. My secret, sir, is that I want to make you rich. I want to make this town prosperous beyond the dreams of any who live here; and when it is I will be perfectly poised to negotiate trade contracts and set up distribution across half the North. Scavenger Lords grow rich from treasures. Guild Factors grow richer. I am not with the Guild, but I know where real fortunes come from.

                                So, speaking of business, would like to be a part of this?"
