                                                        01-04-2018, 12:40 PM
                                                        Scholar awoke on the ship, Pool Pinto fast asleep at his side. He puts energy into his sense of timing and becomes aware of the exact time of day. It is close to when he usually wakes up. He has to fix breakfast. Not only that, it is crewman Rhy's birthday, and he had planned to make him a nice outfit, one with which he could pick up chicks at the port.

                                                        First things first, he leaves Pool Pinto to sleep and heads to the galley to make breakfast. He would fix a meal of quattle eggs, ham, and potatoes. These eggs, though green, when mixed with certain herbs made it less likely for people to get sick from changes in weather. They were hard to get, fortunately these fish eggs liked the local water climate so he was able to get enough for one day. He added the herbs that would safeguard the crew, and for twenty four hours make them stronger. He set the herbs to simmer in a large pot and went to check his quartermaster station.

                                                        At the station, unexpectedly was crewman Sanders. He was holding his stomach and there was red all over his hands. An accident of some kind. Whatever happened, Scholar would have to deal with the injury first, forgetting everything else. He put healing herbs and wrapped the wound after washing it with other healing herbs. He then game him bark to chew on for the pain.

                                                        He went back to the kitchen to find that the herbs had simmered too long. The medicinal effect would be there but the taste would be off. He did his best to finish up a good meal, but it was off.

                                                        Scholar goes to the morning meeting and informs the captain of the "accident". He doesn't have much information on it, but it looks like it may not have been an accident. His projections on food and medicine was food will last for thirty days, but medicine was lacking, they had not gotten much.

                                                        He tells that he must go to "Agrippa" Island to get wave marble and create the statue in the market, and he must find and kill an evil being that sacraficed human beings for power by the name of Wulf of the depths. He tells the captain what he knows of Wulf and asks for help killing him. He is offensive to the Unconquered Sun, so Scholar wants him dead. The rewards were a letter of marque from the Duke, and the friendship of Corvolo, a mighty creature of the sea.
