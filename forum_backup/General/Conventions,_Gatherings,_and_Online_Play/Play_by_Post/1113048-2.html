                    Diante Johnson, Shadowname: Darwin

                    Diante was tired of corporate life, and near the end of his office tenure, he started having subversive thoughts and visions. He developed an opposition defiance disorder and got terminated from his job due to a spat with middle management. Throughout these events, Diante kept imagining that he and other people were being kept in shadows; he believed life was just a sleepy play put on each day, with the real machinations occurring offstage. He threw out all his possessions, all except for an old lead coin a friend has given him years ago.

                    Unwilling to go quietly into the night, Diante starting reading more, and he stumbled upon a person of the night that offered housing and resources, if Diante worked as their Retainer (this could be a negative quality.) Diante agreed to this, and wisely said nothing about making acquaintances with a vampire.

                    Not long after, Diante found himself shoving aside curtains, mirrors, and other obfuscations in his dreams, until he witnessed, against a Stygian horizon, the Watchtower of the Lead Coin. Diante approached the tower and pressed his coin against its side, and harnessing newfound energy, inscribed his shadowname into it's stone base. Now a mage, Diante wishes to uplift the minds of other, and help forge destinies locked away by the oppressive great Lie.
