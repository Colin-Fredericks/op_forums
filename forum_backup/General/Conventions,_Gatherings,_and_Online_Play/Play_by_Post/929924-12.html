    Moonstone raised a hand and stopped when the voice whispered. He mumbled to Phoenix: "Did you hear that too? Or am I going crazy again?"

    He opened his third eye just for a moment, looking around with Mage Sight for any obvious effect nearby. Advancing further up the hallway (with garden hoe at the ready), he spoke up and hoped the voice could hear him: "Who? Who is we? Whose heavens? What truth?"

    He had an inkling. But maybe if it spoke again, he would see something with the Sight this time.

                Moonstone
                You open your Sight, and you faintly see the outline of a figure floating before you. It's...hazy. That's the best word you can summon to describe what you see. The figure is amorphous to your Sight.

                The information that your Mind Sight reveals is particularly confusing. This...being...does not appear to be thinking, per se. But it clearly moved towards you and Phoenix as you continued through the halls, as if drawn to your presence, suggesting that it has -- or perhaps had at one time -- sentient thought.

                Your mundane vision, still enhanced by your Space spell, allows you to see up and down the hallway in all directions. As far as you can tell, there are no other creatures or threats lying in wait.

                You hear the feminine voice again. It appears to be coming from the figure. REALITY IS FRAGMENTED. WHAT HAVE WE DONE? THE LADDER HAS TORN.


                Phoenix
                Immediately upon opening your Sight, you see a presence floating before you. Through your kaleidoscope of Arcana, it shifts in colors and form, but from your Prime Sight you can tell that this ephemeral presence is a woman. She has long hair, intricately woven into a thick braid that stretches nearly down to her ankles. She wears a simple white dress with a silver rope tied around her waist. You realize that the woman's face has no emotional expression, and her eyes stare off to the distance with an unfocused gaze. She does not blink.

                From your Mind Sight, contrary to what you predicted, this being has no clear thoughts or mind that you can sense...and yet the presence clearly moved here only moments ago.

                When the woman speaks, you notice that her lips do not move, nor does her face change expression in any way.

                Should you desire, either of you can attempt Revelation or Scrutiny on this presence. Opacity is 3, and is unique for each of you.

                                    Binder
                                    The black sphere emits a piercing screech and violently shakes as you assault it with your magic. It clearly had an effect.

                                    The black cloud reforms into a perfect sphere, and lashes out another tentacle at the nearest girl. The other two yell and stumble backwards, but the third loses her footing and drops her camera. Rather than hitting the ground, her camera floats towards the black sphere and orbits around it. The girl screams again as her clothes ripple, and then she starts to slide towards the orb.

                                    You feel a pulse of energy on your Periphery as the tentacle lashes onto the girl; the orb seems to pulse in time as it "feeds" on her.

                                    As you watch, you also notice that it is getting brighter out. The clouds overhead are definitely thinning. The orb suddenly retracts its tentacle, spins in place, and then quickly floats away down an adjacent, covered hallway between two buildings.


                                    Phoenix
                                    Your Sight explodes with colors you've never seen before and couldn't begin to describe. You intensely study the woman, watch the way that her form stands as your strands of mana swirl around her. You study the primal energy making up her supernatural being.

                                    Your mind suddenly reels in shock and understanding.

                                    At one point in your life, you heard stories about the aftermath of the atomic bomb in Hiroshima. The force of the blast was so powerful that near the center of the city, it atomized people and flash-burned the afterimage of their shadows directly into the concrete where they had been standing.

                                    As close as you can tell, this "woman" is no recording -- she is the supernatural afterimage of a violent magical trauma. This is not a ghost, or a spirit, or any kind of supernatural entity. Nor is it alive, or in any way conscious. Instead, she is a remnant, a shadow, a glimpse out of the corner of the universe's eye. The revelation is all the more chilling when she "speaks" again. WHAT HAVE WE DONE?

                                    Take an Arcane Beat for learning something fascinating and dramatic about this place.


                                    Ragnar
                                    You strain yours ears, but hear nothing besides the water occasionally dripping from stalactites into the pool behind you. The hallway is deathly quiet.
