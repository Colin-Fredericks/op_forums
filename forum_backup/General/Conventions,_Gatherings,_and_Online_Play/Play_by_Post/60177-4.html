                        Palanmas and her souls all bow as well, as Jellivens motions you outside, and back to his book.

                        "I am ever so glad that went well, Lady Palanmas is not in the habit of receiving visitors," Jellivens says, "But do drop again soon. She might seem flighty, but Lady Palanmas did enjoy your company."

                        As you leave the book, Jellivens tips his top hat to you, and flies off. As you all board Zekka, Pellias sees a figure walk by onto the ship for a half-moment. Looking again, there is nothing. A mirage?

                        As you all enter the bridge, Zekka chortles, "Man, what a waste of time. Well, time to get this thing going, kyahahahahaha!"

                        With that, you fly off, leaving Palanmas, and reentering the Spiral. There, Hoshi announces a message from Mercury's Delight. A downy-haired woman wearing a snakeskin shawl over her Realm military uniform appears oncscreen.

                        "Captain Kleiner here. Zekka, we're glad that you came out. We thought that you had been lost within that thing. Are you still able to pursue the mission?" she says.

                        When brought abreast of the situation, Kleiner nods, "Very well, then. My charts have four potential avenues we can travel along."

                        Bringing up the charts on the screen, Kleiner outlines a short route to a planet to your northwest. There is another route underneath the Western Arm, into a sparsely populated area much closer to the center of the Spiral. There is also a string of planets on the opposite side with two prospective planets outline. One is a known jungle world, while the other is a plains world.

                        So, where will you go?
                        -Planet right in front of you?
                        -Planet close to the Spiral's core, but sparsely populated?
                        -Plains Planet?
                        -Jungle Planet?

                                                        Hoshi nods to Amelia, and begins to bark orders to the crew about a landing. As the drop ships and voidstars are prepped, the Circle loads up on several of the ships as well, along with the DelArm Divisions.

                                                        As the ships land, they find the world to be cloaked in night, with nary a living thing to confirm the massive life readings from before.

                                                        However, as if on cue, dawn arrives.

                                                        With it, you can see the local landscape to be a massive plain, entirely flat. Flat, of course, save for the gigantic stone monoliths scattered here and there. and around them, are the beasts. They look like ordinary creatures, as one would see in any of the Lunar menageries in the Gunstar, but not quite. They chatter to one another, assuming formations, and many are wearing clothing and armor. They quickly busy themselves with inspecting, cleaning, and further on, you can see them erecting further monoliths. As time goes on, more and more beasts appear, as if from nothing.

                                                        In the extreme distance, you can see a far larger structure, but whether or not it is artificial cannot be discerned from this distance.
