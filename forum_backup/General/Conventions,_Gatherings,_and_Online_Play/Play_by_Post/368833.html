    [OOC] Storm's Eve [Wraith: the Great War]

    01-13-2015, 03:26 PM
    And so begins my first PbP and Wraith game. It's still open to anyone that wants to join.

    Ground Rules for Character Creation:
    Using Wraith: the Great War but if you wish to use a relic, merit/flaw or whatever from another book put [Book name, Page number] so I can find it.
    Your wraith has to have dead recently, between 1914 and June 1 1916.
    It must also be a Hierarchy wraith, so no Renegades, Heretics or foreign wraiths and be sure to list your Legion
    Only one Forbidden Arcanoi per character. You can have as many dots as you like in it but only in that one.
    As previously mentioned you can't have foreign wraiths which means you can't have their Arcanoi either.

    Shadow Rules:
    You will not play as your Shadow, it will be assigned to another player and I will dictate when you can play as them.
    The player must update their PC first before the shadowguide can respond, though players can collaborate and do a shared PC/Shadow post.

    Shadow Actions
    Shadow Speech

    Setting:
    Paris, on the eve of the Battle of the Somme, on the eve of the Fourth Great Maelstrom. On the eve of the Smiling Lord's Insurrection. The Emperor is missing, the ever growing dead of the Great War are flooding the Underworld, tensions both within and without the Hierarchy are rising and in between it all there are countless wraiths just trying to keep on existing, unaware of what's about to happen.

    Just like you...

    Player 1: Thomas Mccabe [PaladinDemo]

    Any questions feel free to ask. And if you want to join up you're more than welcome to. If you want to join let me know as soon as possible.
    Last edited by AkatsukiLeader13; 01-19-2015, 11:12 PM.

    Homo sapiens. What an inventive, invincible species. It's only a few million years since they crawled up out of the mud and learned to walk. Puny, defenceless bipeds. They've survived flood, famine and plague. They've survived cosmic wars and holocausts. And now, here they are, out among the stars, waiting to begin a new life. Ready to outsit eternity. They're indomitable. Indomitable.
