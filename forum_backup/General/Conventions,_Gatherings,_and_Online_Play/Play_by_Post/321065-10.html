                                        02-26-2015, 05:05 PM
                                        As he runs out into view, Val sees three enormous people digging in an old, snow covered graveyard. The graveyard is small, but many of the tombstones look like they've been there since before the revolution, so worn and faded are they. Despite the snow, most of the tombstones can be seen clearly since the white powder has been brushed off almost all of them. All around the open graves are discarded bones or rotting bodies frozen on the cold, tossed aside like so much detritus. Incongruously, there is a six foot tall snowman standing in the exact center of the graveyard with an old skull in place of a head. long femur is in the skull's mouth, as if it is gnawing on the bone.

                                        The owners of the three voices all freeze in the process of digging, turning to stare at the newcomer. At a second glance, the three "enormous people" are clearly three individuals in Glabro form. Val's eyes are first drawn to the most unusual of the three, who is also the smallest of the three even in Glabro form. He is bundled up in winter gear, though most of it is ratty and old, except for his head, which is unaccountably bare. He has no hair at all, not even eyebrows, and appears to be in his early twenties. He licks his lips nervously when he sees Val, and looks questioningly at the woman, glancing back and forth between her and Val.

                                        The second man, Val can only see from the waist up, as he is standing in a deep grave that he is in the process of excavating. He's wearing a heavy leather coat, covered with biker patches, and sunglasses despite the darkness, and looks to be in his mid-twenties. He stops digging when Val comes into view, standing up straight and resting the shovel on his shoulder. He spares a glance for the woman, but keeps most of his focus on Val, his eyes gleaming with predatory intent.

                                        The woman stood closest to Val, besides a fully excavated grave. As Val rounded the corner, she appeared to be moving to the next grave, but stopped, whirling to fully face Val as he emerged. She wears a heavy trenchcoat over her massive glabro form, and drops the shovel once Val comes into view. She glares at him, suspicion painted on her features. "Who the fuck are you?" she growls, taking a step towards him menacingly.
