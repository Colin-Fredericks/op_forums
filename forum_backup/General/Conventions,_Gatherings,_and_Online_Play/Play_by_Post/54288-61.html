    Yi gave the polite smile that in politics meant, "Be sure to offer a generous sacrifice to whatever god you worship that it is not politically expedient for me to have you killed., but that was a nice move and we should have lunch some time." He didn't really mean it, but it was almost a reflex at this point. Virtue was a very difficult road to follow. Even when he did not feel virtuous, he was going to act like it until it because natural to him.

    With his mouth he said, "I am but a humble servant of Novalesk, at the service of its people. Please prepare for my return, as I am given to understand that there may be some logistic difficulties involved."

                        Something clicks for Jensven. Vellus was attempting to gain some measure of power through the current exchange. He had seen it back home when rival chieftans would visit and attempt to gain the services of some of the reavers by commending them and then subtly suggesting they should come over to their service.                         "The only payment I need is to see the Fae driven off. I have them to thank for the loss of friends and family from my home to the North and East of here." Jensven pauses. "If your cousin is looking for warriors, they may have to look elsewhere. I do not mean to cause anger. I am merely new to this place and it's ways. I would like to ensure that I knew what I was doing before I commit to anything so important."

                                                Tirien grabs the hapless Dero, and swings the flailing young man at Brandt. The move was truly unexpected, and Dero's axe swing causes the noble to stagger backwards a step, which allows you to take a single step onto the charred-bone bridge.

                                                Brandt sighs and sheathes his blade, "I acknowledge your strength. You have defeated me, and I will let you pass."

                                                With that, he steps aside, leaving the bridge open for your passage. He seems to be struggling with something internally, and says, "Be warned humans. My master has no love of your ilk. If you continue along this path, it may well lead to your doom."

                                                Provided you continue on:

                                                You walk across the massive bridge, which seems to arch into eternity. Some time later, the fog begins to thin, and the bone-bridge connects to a massive city wrought of a red-orange metal. It is seemingly empty of inhabitants, though you can see a massive spire of a crystalline citadel in the distance.
