                                        Re: the last post you were on, here ya go. There hasn't been much activity since, but here are some short summaries of the interim. Correct me if I'm wrong, anybody.
                                        - Eva and Nate had a brief chat, after which Nate found her a little too weird and wandered off.* He found some, well, outhouses, but they seem to be rather large -- and bigger-on-the-inside, at that. Each appears to be set up as a home for a specific zodiac beast, opening only for the appropriate critter and sporting the finest amenities for the animal -- if not for the human. Perhaps owing to his newly Satyrical nature, Nate's is a winery with a vineyard, in which he's quickly gotten sloshed.
                                        - Derek and Jessica had a chat, in which a loophole was discovered and a plan began taking form. They intend to find and rejoin Jessica's friends and then have a talk with Leo and the Western Zodiac.
                                        - Max made it to the clearing without incident, and got to work planting those seeds. The going was tougher than he'd have thought and he found his arms quickly exhausted from the digging. Determined to get it done, he compromised with his humanity a bit and rooted up holes for the other seeds with his snout. Once planted, the seeds soon sprouted and there's now a small orchard. Exhaustion finally caught up to him and he's fallen fast asleep, curled up in the shade.

                                        * This is owed in part to how it's been nearly a month to the day since wander's last post. ^_^o It happens...
