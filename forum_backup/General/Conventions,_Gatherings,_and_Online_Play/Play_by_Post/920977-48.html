                                                        Originally posted by Haberdasher View Post
                                                        rwknoll How do you want me to handle Scrutinize rolls? Can I make multiple rolls per turn?
                                                        You're not under time pressure in the traditional sense, so you can make as many as you want. Just use the dice roller for each because there are consequences for failing rolls after the first few attempts; see the rules on Scrutiny for a refresher.

                                                        EDIT: To be clear, scrutiny is an extended action on which you can make new roll attempts every turn. So, when you're out of initiative, you can ignore the time limitations but need to be mindful of the consequences of failing after making multiple rolls (e.g., increased Opacity). Because you cast Postcognition using Advanced Duration and the option to "scrub" the feed, you have no time limitations on your roll. Does that make more sense?
