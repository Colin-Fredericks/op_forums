        "Know this, I do not want your job, wish to sully your honor, care if you are a good person, or give an iota of concern for your social position. The nation is in the grip of despair strategos, and to question me will do nothing for that problem. I do not fault you for growing angry with me for questioning you, that is a very human reaction. What will and should be a mark against you is if you are unable to view the current military situation as distinct from yourself. I am aware that by raising this question I strike at the very core of the narrative about yourself you have spent a lifetime building.

        Still the question must be asked and answered. It does not need to be answered to me, but it must be answered. Novelesk deserves an answer strategos, as do you."

            "You speak as if you are not human, Lookshan. As if you are somehow better than me," Eugene says sharply. "But I will explain."

            "I was not born in to the lofty heights of wealth and privilege that I now hold. I enlisted over forty years ago as a spearman, and worked my way up the ranks. A decade ago, I was made Strategos after my victory at Lian's Fell."

            Yi is somewhat shocked by this claim. Lian's fell is a well-known disaster for Lookshy, when Hiyama Gidan and his force of nearly 50 gunzota were ambushed and slain, nearly to a man, by "barbarian forces" at some point "north of Gunzota Redoubt". Those slain were never recovered.

            "Yes, Lookshan, I've bested your finest warriors. I am not some armchair general who's never seen combat. I have served man and boy, linesman and officer. I may not be the most talented strategist in Novalesk. But I am the best trained, most experienced and most capable general this nation will have in the days ahead unless Sunipa herself comes to relieve me of command."

            "Oh, and for the record," Eugene smirks as he leans forward, "I cannot claim the distinction of being a great duelist. But I happen to be a very good duelist."

                                            "If you wish no part of it, I give you leave to depart and neither I nor your god will hold it against you. It is true what you say, that my actions bring great danger. Yet I cannot in good conscience do other than I do. With power comes obligation, the duty to use that power responsibly for the sake of those whom you have power over. Those who become corrupt, who abandon virtue and allow the people to suffer do not deserve their power. Such people have lost the right to rule and their power should be taken from them.

                                            This was the logic by which those who became the Anathema made war on the enemies of the gods and the same logic by which the Ten Thousand Dragons justified the Great Uprising. Perhaps it will be used again when the Age of Dragons ends as well. I do not concern myself with such grand things, yet I know that there is a difference between order and good order and that authority is not equivalent to morality. I will bear whatever peril this brings upon because I have the strength to bear it. Someone must."

                                            As he spoke something like a light came over him. It was nothing so ostentatious as true illumination. Rather, it seemed to reveal his character, to show that he was the kind of person who not only believed what he said, but had the strength and will to back it up. It was the sort of thing that stayed with you, the fire that kindled the hearts of a people and set the world upside down.

                                            Now then: I have obtained divine backing, commissioned the largest production of magical weaponry in the nation's history, and obtained the cooperation of the Strategos. I am going to go out on a limb and call that a three dot stunt when taken together.

                                            First, there is the roll to see if my Guan activates. It does, raising my Charisma and Appearance by 3 each.
                                            Next, I am going to activate a combo of the maxed out Second Socialize Excellency, Wise-Eyed Courtier Method, Mastery of Small Manners, and Heartfelt Honorific Opportunity and channeling Compassion.

                                            Charisma 6 + Socialize 5 + Social Change Specialty 1 + Compassion 4 + Stunt 3 = 19 + 4 successes
                                            Mote Cost: Excellency 8 + MoSM 1 + WECM 3 + HHO 3 = 15
                                            WP Cost: WECM 1 + HHO 1 + Virtue 1 = 3

                                            This is a social attack against the Leader of Novalesk. The magnitude of the nation is considered one less then it actually is and halved then added to the leaders MDV and the attack is unexpected. Assuming they have a social surprise negator of some kind, my effective appearance is 7.

                                            The goal of this is to grant me as much backing and influence as the nation of Navalesk can support. The belief it is imposing would be something along the lines of, "The Stranger who cut through all the red tape, stagnation, and self-doubt has delivered us from ruin and should be the one in charge."
