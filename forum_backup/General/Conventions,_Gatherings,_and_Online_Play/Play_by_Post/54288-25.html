    Part of Hyuga's skin burns off and he grits his teeth through the pain, watching as the book slowly burns. Hyuga gathers all his strength in his well-built biceps gained from blacksmithing.                                             This had gone on long enough. People were getting hurt, damage was being done, and he was allowing his caution to restrain him not from foolishness, but from action. So he gave his last quip, the last boast he would allow himself for this battle.

                                            The world disappeared in a flash of light as the Solar anima blazed so incandescent that those outside saw every piece of the structure outlined in light. Within, all it touched was changed. The sand of the floor glowed like alabaster, the wood of the walls shone like amber and ammolite, the molds and cauldrons glittered like nacre, even the ice the fey had breathed burned like a crystal lightning.

                                            In the center of it all was Yi Zhinan, the Righteous Guide come back to comfort the ailing world. Then he began to move.

                                            Half a dozen hanging chains parted like silk before Yi’s first strike, pouring out a waterfall of molten iron that the Sword of Wu Wei parted as it fell.

                                                        Hyuga winces in pain as he rips off the sleeves from his shirt and then starts to pick up some metal from the destroyed equipment. Carefully, he analyzes the great behemoth left there analyzing its movements and great stature. "I have never ridden anything like this before. Hopefully it is not too crazy."

                                                        Hyuga closes his eyes and concentrates for a second, bringing up images of his finished product in mind. Suddenly, his caste marks starts to glow and a small ball of light appears above the materials. The light reflects along the walls and seem to fill Hyuga's eyes. Slowly, the ball starts to stretch and coil around his hands and begins to twist into shapes of different tools in lightning speeds. Screwdrivers, hammers, picks, and other tools seem to appear and disappear at a dizzying rate as if they were never there to being with. Suddenly, a phantasmal image of a bit a bit and reins seems to overlap on top of the materials. Hyuga then mumbles to himself, "I have been waiting too long. It is time for you to see the light." Immediately after, the image of the bit and reins seems to just fall into existence and the materials seem to disappear as if they were an illusion the entire time.

                                                        This behemoth is nothing like a horse, but I think I can tame him. I've already analyzed its movements and I can predict how it may buck and try to get me off of his back. I can do this.

                                                        Swiftly, Hyuga swipes the bit and reins designed for this great beast off of the ground and walks over towards the beast. "Your master has died and I can be your master now. We will find a place for you." Hyuga jumps upon the behemoth's back and gently caresses its back and neck area with a gentle hand. "You'll be alright. Just be calm." In the same moment, Hyuga snaps the bit flinging it around through the air like a lasso outward fitting the beast with it and holds the reins tightly in his hands whilst bracing himself by holding onto the behemoths thick carapace. Hyuga continues talking to it in a light pleasant voice, hoping that it may have some sort of understanding with him. "Why don't we be friends. I think you could be a great help."
