                                                    Hyuga sits up a bit and says, "Yes. You are right. Some information must be sought yourself. It provides a certain clarity and understanding that will let you recreate something, improve on something, or even bring forth new visions. Now, if you will excuse me, I will be heading to the library."
                                                    On his way to the library, thousands of thoughts seem to fill his head. The realm defense grid. It could be used to fix everything. It had been used for fighting, but it had the capability of showing visions and more. It seemed like an enhancement station that would let everything reach around Creation. I really need to look into it while I have the chance. It could save plenty of people, though I know it comes at a price. Perhaps there will be a way to change that.

                                                    Hyuga's mind was so occupied that he seemed to arrive almost instantly. As he walked in, light flooded the room and shone upon the brilliantly made shelves. He could see his reflection in them and immediately felt happy.

                                                    As he walked around, he gingerly fingered the spines of the books, whilst reading their titles. A few titleless books caught his attention here and there, but they seemed to not be as important. He knew what he must look for: information on the elemental poles, demenses, manses, the Imperial Manse, ancient magitech artifacts, and the creation of Creation itself. They must hold some sort of key.

                                                    Hyuga quickly read a few books about the realm and the Scarlet Empress, a mouse who followed many important individuals and collected exotic items, and about basic manse building. He caressed each page reading intently and memorizing everything thanks to the help of his hearthstone. Not too much later, his forehead began to itch and burn a little, like sun-seared flesh.

                                                    Unknowingly, he had walked into the geomantic center of the library and was bathed in impossibly comfortable light that kept him warm. He began to have a gut feeling that an important piece to the puzzle of the Realm Defense Grid was somewhere nearby, but he just couldn't seem to pinpoint it.

                                                    Light began to cascade around the room filtering out of sight the books he didn't need and showing in a golden glow, which books he needed. He knew this must be a miracle from the Unconquered Sun. I need to remember to pray to him more often. He helps me quite a lot.

                                                    Hygua's brain focused at his max, his eyes taking in all the sights and each individual book. He still could not find the book he knew he needed, yet he could still feel it nearby. He then closed his eyes focusing on his gut feeling for the book that he so desperately needed.
