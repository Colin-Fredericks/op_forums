    Why do I insist on this? I move so openly now that any pretense of hiding from Lookshy is gone. At this juncture though, I have more to fear from knives in the dark.

                        04-25-2014, 09:05 PM
                        Hyuga stands up slowly once the daystar begins to shed its light over the horizon far beyond. He smacks his face a few times to keep the sleepiness away and continues to watch for movements within the city. Knowing that it is only a matter of time before Tirien and Yi attract some attention and head out, he keep his eyes peeled like a hawk waiting for a time to come down. He continues to watch, like a hawk, the crowds that have started to move out into the city like little ants in a field. Hyuga continually watches them seeking for any signs that they know Anathema are in the city. Realizing he may have been a little too careless within his situation in the city, he is determined to never lower his guard, lest they try to have him killed, let alone Tirien or Yi. They are vital to the plans; much more than he. He had started to realize more of his glaring weaknesses. His abilities could only help him so much. He knew he had a long way to go.

                        As his eyes continue to scan for any signs of danger, he reflects back on his thoughts of Anathema before he became one himself. He had been ashamed becoming one at first recalling all the pain they had brought to many people. He vowed to never let anyone do that again with his new-found strength. If only he could communicate that to everyone; he knew not how to himself. He knew only action would help him. Knowing that his allies will gain the power needed to start the fight, he dashes across the rooftops, jumping between them like a streak of light bouncing off the surface of the water and heads to the edge of the town. Cautiously, he clambers down the building at the edge of town and dashes to the nearby forest; his new sanctuary for the time being.

                        Hyuga always had an affinity for the forest; strange for one born in the south such as himself. It brought him a sense of peace, protection, and was a beautiful rest from the heat of the sun. This would be the perfect place to meditate on his blade among other things. Slowly, Hyuga takes his essence filled hand and creates the symbol of the Twilight around him in the dirt. He then sits in a meditative pose placing his sword on his lap, with the pommel still in his hand and meditates on the things that Yi talked to him about on the boat.

                        I am a part of the Sun. I go where I am needed as does my blade. I shine in glory as I wield my sword. Its path is simple and where it should be. It does not yield to any and will hold its ground in my hands. I am unmoved in my determination and blade. I shall not falter nor change to the circumstances like the Sun. It will all change to me.

                            Your escort for the day is none other than Origenes' daughter. The young goddess is clad in the functional robes common among many of Novalesk's citizenry. Her robes are delicately armored, however, and she bears a straight broad blade at her waist.

All of them are influential members of their families, though, or else they wouldn't have the position."

                                            (Yi)
                                            "My recommendation?" Larissa says, arching her brow slightly, "Leave. Short of literally getting in bed with one of the major power-players, nothing is going to completely stop the knives in the dark."

                                            "We will weather this storm, even without your help. In a month or two the situation will deteriorate to the point where the Pyrtanies or I will be able to justify declaring martial law and resuming supreme executive control," her eyes harden, and you can sense her resolve, a lifetime of hard choices and decisions evident in her countenance, "We will survive. We may be battered but we will not be broken. But if you stay, you will be consumed by this."

                                            "If you're set on staying, though, you need to get cozy with one of the factions. My predecessor tells me you're trying to make yourself seem like a neutral party. That's not going to get you far here. That just means that everyone will distrust you, thinking you're secretly working for one of their enemies. You need a formal declaration of support from one of the major powers. Everyone will assume that you have tacit backing from the Temple for the moment, but the lac of a formal declaration will weaken that over time."

                                            "Now, if you want to stay, I presume you wish to head to Gens Spyrus' Boule chambers?"
