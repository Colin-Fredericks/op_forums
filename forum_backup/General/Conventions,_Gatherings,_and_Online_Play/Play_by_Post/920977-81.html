        If I were to say that my life wasn’t always weird, I would be lying. I mean sure, everything with the magic, and the fairies, and the Deathstorm is new, but I’m honestly not all that surprised that my life escalated to… all of this.

        But, I’m getting ahead of myself.

        I was born in Chicago and lived there for most of my life at this point with my dear old dad. He raised me by himself, always juggling being the paternal father and doing enough work to support us. He always struggled with it, trying to be two people at once and having to be in three places at the same time. He dropped the ball a few times, but I try not to blame him for it. He has enough on his plate as it is.

        It always made me wonder where my mom is though, everyone always told how weird it is that my dad’s raising me alone. I’ve brought it up with him a couple of times, but he always either changes the subject or just goes silent and ends the conversation on the spot. Eventually I just, stopped asking.

        I do know he’s my biological dad though, so there’s that.

        Anyway, ever since I was little I’ve always been a bit of a luck magnet. And by a bit, I mean a lot. Good luck, bad luck, and some weird luck here and there. Basically, if something is notably improbable then it’s pretty much guaranteed to happen to me at some point.

        My mentor said it’s something to do with my Nimbus, but I’m not sure if it’s an effect of my Nimbus or if the Nimbus just made it stronger.

        I digress.

                                Here's the links I have right now:
                                • DESTINY: Rene is a locus of fate, often getting tangled up in others problems and stories. Chances are, she's previously met the important people she will encounter and walked away from it with some insight as to their character. Destiny's idea of foreshadowing things to her. This also sometimes manifests as literal foreshadowing, ominous events appearing around her as a portent of things to come.
                                • FAMILY: Rene's brother, Jack, is currently living out his Durance in Arcadia, battling the shapeshifting True Fae that keeps him hostage. The Tragic Hero to the Fae's Villain. Jack's Fetch has only recently appeared, though both Rene and Jack's mother has begun suspecting something is wrong. They've yet to figure out what however. Moving on, Rene's father, Harold Fraser, has recently married Dr. Sylvia Flores, Jack's Mother, and they all currently live in the Fraser's home. Dr. Flores has opted to not change her name so as to not confuse her patients, and Mr. Fraser agreed to it on the grounds of less paperwork they have to waste their time on. Harold and Slyvia do care deeply about their children, biological or otherwise, but they do have a problem in properly understanding them. A product of their busy schedules. It's honestly a miracle they found time to date and get married as it is.
                                • LITERATURE: Rene quite loves books and so does her luck. She loves reading and learning about new things, and is often found seeking out new books in shops and libraries, having quickly read through her last batch. Her luck often helps her with this, as she often quickly finds just the book with the information she needs, even if she didn't know she would need it yet.
