                                *Start of New Scene*

                                Binder
                                The door continues to confuse and frustrate you. Perhaps this experience will become easier for you with time, but for now, you struggle to see through the door or fathom its nature.

                                You draw on your knowledge and fledgling control over the Space arcanum, and in doing so, you gain a thrilling sense of awareness all around you that takes your breath away. You can see through your eyes, but in a dizzying fashion, you can see yourself from every other angle simultaneously. You can look around the corner of the building and still see the people sitting on the bench at the top of the stairs by the train platform. You can see Moonstone and Phoenix's faces fixed in concentration as they study the Iris.

                                More importantly to you in this moment, you can see into the very portal in front of you -- but you still struggle to fully understand what you're seeing. You can sense that the portal does not connect to any realm on Earth, but you know nothing of this strange place you can see. It's clearly not the Shadow, the realm of spirits, and it doesn't share the same feeling as the Astral realm you briefly journeyed to when you Awakened.

                                Per that spell, it explains Distortions and where they lead, which are Space Irises that connect to other places on Earth. I'm ruling that you need access to at least 1 dot of Prime magic to understand more about this particular type of Iris.


                                Ragnar
                                You stop before crossing the courtyard, in sight of the small alleywhere you saw the magical door before, at least while your Sight was open. You can't see it exactly right now, but you suddenly feel like you're rolling a pair of dice and aren't sure what the numbers will show -- a sense that somehow tells you that something magical is nearby.

                                You dip your finger in the river of Time and study its flow. As you form questions in your mind, the answers come to you with brief flashes of the future.

                                An image of hail falling and hitting you as you cross the courtyard, and of Lynx and Dr. Asphodel as they cross after you. The hail will not hurt you or the mages with you, not in your current condition.

                                A flash of bright moving colors muted by a cloud of black, swirling on the wall. Nothing will exit through the portal when you study it.

                                An older man, eyes filled with worry. Studying the portal will lead you to one other mage, who will lead you to more.

                                Your Peripheral Sight tells you that something magical is nearby.


                                Lynx
                                You wait a few moments to make sure no one pays too much attention to you, but it doesn't take long. Everyone is either leaving for home, talking to a friend, or checking out their social media feeds on their phone. You're just another face in the crowd. You are able to reach Ragnar without drawing any additional attention. Some hail hits you, making your skin sting, but it doesn't seem to do anything special to you that you can see or sense.

                                You make it to the back alley behind the adjacent building where Ragnar stands. You don't see any kind of door like he's described, but something is giving off that odd smell that you are learning to associate with the supernatural.

                                Your Peripheral Sight tells you that something magical is nearby.


                                Dr. Asphodel
                                A student or two stop by to apologize for leaving class, but you quickly assure them you'll pick up where you left off during your next class on Thursday afternoon. With only a little effort, you're able to catch up with Lynx and Ragnar near where the door is supposed to be. You don't see anything, not exactly, but you have a crawling sensation in your skin that tells you something supernatural is nearby.

                                Your Peripheral Sight tells you that something magical is nearby.


                                Phoenix
                                For just a moment, you have that sensation like you're on the top of a roller coaster about to begin its first descent -- and then you start to plummet towards the ground. The world shifts around you as you blink...

                                ...And you open your eyes with an involuntary gasp. Your skin prickles with invisible pins and needles, almost like when your leg falls asleep and it's waking up. You're sitting upright in a thick, uncomfortable wooden chair. You're in a small room, maybe 10 feet across in each dimension. The walls look to be made of hewn stone; a hallway leads away from the room and turns to the left after a few feet. A small oil lantern is lit in the corner, and there is a desk beside you with ink, a short quill, and a few books. You are alone.

                                Your Nightvision spell stays active, but your Hail Bubble spell ends immediately, which Phoenix would sense. You may roll Wits+Composure for perception.


                                Moonstone
                                You give yourself fully to your Sight, and this time, the door sings its secrets to you.

                                The information is overwhelming, filtered through three arcana simultaneously. You do not sense any aspect of Mind in this portal; other than confirming that it does not lead to the Astral Realms, which you've only heard about in passing from Hakim, you learn little from that arcanum. To your Space Sight, you can see trails of mana and magic swirling away to another realm, far beyond any Earth you know. It isn't a shadowy portal to the Abyss, that much you can tell. There is no Key, no special passcode or requirement that you can sense. It's as if the door is awaiting to be opened. With your Time Sight, you learn something confusing that you know is true but cannot understand: the portal has existed for precisely 17 minutes, and simultaneously has existed for millennia. How can something be that new and yet that old? How can anything have two ages?

                                While you contemplate this information, Phoenix suddenly vanishes before your eyes. As he does, the shield protecting you from hail also abruptly vanishes. A few more pieces of black ice strike your skin and burn even worse than before. The blackness around the Iris bubbles in response, as if threatening to burst. There are a few awnings nearby that might offer some modicum of protection, or the covered stairwell back towards the train platform.

                                Lose 2 more mana from the hail.

                                            Before you close your Sight, you notice a white dazzling light shining off every surface of the room, from every brick and even the objects lying on the table.                                             To your mundane vision, as you study the room more closely, you notice some odd facts. First, there is a glint of yellow gold shining from near the books; upon closer inspection, you see gilded runes embedded in the book covers. To your astonishment, you know what they mean, to a degree -- some are beyond anything you can comprehend, but some of the text is legible through what you can only assume is some kind of Supernal enlightenment. The runes you can read hint at complex mathematical formulas and philosophical debates.

                                            You notice that the room seems to be some kind of study. The only furniture is the desk, but there is a small mat on the floor next to an unlit piece of something resembling incense and a rune carved in the stone that you read as "Truth."

                                            You hear a rumble through the stone ceiling, echoing softly through the hallways, that sounds like thunder. Otherwise you hear nothing moving or making any particular noise.

                                                        Lynx
                                                        You force open your Sight once more, which feels like a lizard might as it peels open the protective nictitating membrane covering its eyes. In doing so, the shadowy door appears before you. Your senses are bombarded with information that you are still trying to understand how to process.

                                                        To your Life Sight, sniffing the area immediately around you and in front of the door evokes a stale smell, or maybe like a pond filled with standing water -- not death or rot, but no dynamic growth or motion. Through Fate Sight, you sense a dazzling array of colors hidden behind a pitch black veil, both of which swirl in opposing directions on the wall.
