                    Ok here is the Backstory for Five Lessons Learned.


                    To many a visitor Champoor is a wonderous city, it's many illicit thrills accenuated by a subtle sense of danger that only serves to heighten the pleasure one may gain from them. To a native this sense of adventure fades fast, leaving only behind only a feeling of danger and the burning desire to be the abuser, rather than be the abused. The wealthy and powerful of Champoor live a life of decadent wealth, influence and can count on the protection of corrupt gods, bought with sacrifices others make in their name. The majority of the populace however lives a life in squalor. Those who have it good, comparativly, work as bodyguards, assassins, spies or courtesans. Those who have it less good band together as thugs, sell their body for a meal or get sold into slavery outright.

                    When Slippery Shadow was born, he belonged to the latter. His mother was a prostitute, he never learned who his father was. Barely getting by herself, she nevertheless choose to share what little she had with him. Despite the other kids in the slum who beat him up regularily for fun, despite the contempt of those of higher station, it was this kindness that perhaps touched him the most. As long as he and his mother had each other, he knew everything would be allright. And he was going to make sure they'd be allright. So he did the only thing he could think of to pull them out of the swamp of squalor they lived in, he stole what they needed from others. It started with food at the markets, but soon after he figured out how to cut the purse of a careless tourist without their notice. They did good. His mother was uneasy with his theft, but ultimatly only said "As long as a deed is done with good intentions in mind, as long as you act upon love, not greed, it's a good deed." Of course it couldn't last.

                    In the end it was not a disease, not a customers rage, greed or perverse desires that struck down his mother. He didn't steal from the wrong people, either. It was politics. All that Shadow knew was that when he got home one day before the onset of calibration, his mother had been killed. Tortured, too. As he stared in shock he missed the hooded shade in the room completly. It's here that he would have died, hadn't the assassin stepped onto a loose floorboard, shocking him out of his own dazed stupor. The knife left a burning streak on his flesh, but didn't bury into his innards. Desperatly he dodged a second blow, then a third. Before a fourth came at him he slammed the door into the assailants face and ran for his life, hiding in a little nieche, crying in silence as his attacker slipped past him, searching. Rage overcame him. He slipped from the shadows and rammed his blade into the assassins back. He pulled it out and stabbed him again, his voice an incoherent howl. Five times he struck and afterwards the murderer of the only person he held dear in life lay before him in a pool of his own blood, his breath wheezing, pain distorting his features, but alive. Then the man laughed, as well as he could anyhow, silently, ragged, but clearly laughter. With sad eyes he offered Slippery Shadow a deal. The name of the man who had paid him to kill his family in exchange for a quick death. Shadow agreed.

                    As it turned out he had been hired by some powerful buisness tycoon. Rage burned in Shadows chest and so without a thought for the consequences he went to the merhcant princes mansion, sneaking past guards and servants alike. He found his awnsers deep within the mansion. There the merchant prince sat and drank tea with a remarkable man radiating an allmost palpable sense of power. Quiet, reigning in his rage, fear and sorrow he sat and listened. The man sitting together with the merchant prince turned out to be a veritable demigod. A Dragon Blood, prince of the earth from Kamthahar he had buisness in town some 18 years ago. However he'd had a few ... dozen indiscretions while in town, potentially producing offspring that right now, as he was in the midst of preparation for a prestigious marriage, could produce slightly awkward. Loose ends needed to be tied up and the merchant prince ensured that he had allready taken care of it. They just sat there, drank tea and calmly discussed buisness and exchanged pleasantries while men in their employ murdered, tortured women who couldn't defend themselves and their children. Bitter grief wracked his mind. He was older than that, a grown man. His mother hadn't even become pregnant from this piece of human garbage and yet he had had her killed without blinking an eye. He dared not move. At some point they would be distracted. And he would kill this man. He didn't care about what would happen afterwards. This man would die tonight and if that meant his own death, he would die with a smile.

                    His moment eventually came. A man wrapped in dark tunics arrived, bowing before the master of the house and pronouced that the deed was done. The whores and their offspring dead, their lives made an offering to Tenepeshu. However there had been a complication. One of the children had turned out to truly be her fathers daughter, killing three men in a surge of poisionous essence before knocked unconscious by one of their number who died for his heroic act with his own life. They carried her in, barely a grown woman, thin and malnourished. The men rose and the dragon blood unslung his bow, aiming to end the life of his own daughter. Slippery Shadow rose from his hiding place and silently strode over to the men whose attention was focussed solely on the inanimate, beaten figure to their feet. He gripped his knife harder. Just two more paces and he would be in range, plunging his knife into the hated mans back. Then something happened that he did not expect. He smelled something. The girl on the floor smelled like roses. Just like the perfume his mother had used to put on. He grimaced and stopped for just one heartbeat. An innocent was going to die in front of him and all he could think of was murdering another man. He though about what his mother had told him, that good deeds are born of love, not hate or rage. This man in front of him deserved to die, he knew this was true in his heart. He deserved to die for all the suffering he brought to others. But he couldn't kill him, not like he was right now. It didn't matter anyway. An innocent was going to die right in front of him and if he choose his revenge over her life, then he was no better than this man. He came to a descision and as he did he felt new resolve, a reinvigorating power course through his limbs. With a quick motion he threw his knife to the side, against a lavish mirror which loudly shattered. Everyone in the mirror looked towards the mirror for an instant and Shadow ran like he never had before, sliding between two men on the floor and picking up the slightly stirring girl on the ground. The men, merchant, assassin, Dragon Blood all snapped back toward him, their eyes going wide. He didn't pay them any attention and just ran. He ran like he never had before, shouts of rage and fear behind him. Out of the room, dodging the spear of a fightened guard with the woman in his arms, he ran out onto a balcony and then jumped with an ease that suprised himself over to another. Arrows flew through the air, but somehow he anticipated their movements, dodging them with an alacrity he would never had thought possible.

                    Alas powerful and quick as he had become in an instant, he was not quick enough to escape the wrath of an experienced choosen of the dragons and the divine protectors of the mansion. Big dogs with blazing eyes and coiling, shifting shades of black water took on the pursuit, matching his speed with their own and harried him from all sides, leaving him distracted and exposed. The Arrow struck into his lower calf, the pain forcing him to his knees. Quickly black veins spread out from the wound as poison took hold and the Dragon Blood came into view. He smiled cruelly and said with a tone perverse satisfaction "Die, Anathema." He loosened his arrow and while Shadow thought all hope lost something grabbed him from behind and yanked him out of the projectiles path. He was drawn back into the shadows. Literally into them. He could still see the dragon blood and his divine pawns staring on in confusion and impotent rage as if through a dark veil. A dark figure emerged from the shadows, tall, strong with four arm. It made no sound as one of its arms flashed forward and broke the neck of one of the dogs, disappearing effortlessly again back into the shadows before anyone else could see it. While everyone else whirled to meet the attacker that must have just emerged, the dark figure emerged behind the dragon blood and struck his shadow with a blur of his four arms, twisting it into a rope that formed around the dragon bloods neck, ripped him into the air and over the balustrade. It was the last thing Slippery Shadow saw before he passed out.

                    He dreamed fitfully that night, then pure sweet nothingness enveloped him. When he woke he was in a forest, miles south of Champoor. His wounds were healed and the strange dark man with four arms stood before him. He regarded Shadow in silence for a while and just as the young man wanted to speak the dark outline of a man raised his own voice, introducing himself as Five Days Darkness, the first Shadow cast by the Unconquered Sun himself and god of calibration. He explained that Slippery Shadow had inherited the Legacy of Shadows Grace, that an ancient compact stood between his soul and exaltation and the dark god. He told him that he had come to see for himself what form this latest incarnation would take and that he was pleased with Shadows guile, grace and compassion, even for one so low as the dragon blooded who had sullied creation with their treachery. He offered Slippery Shadow a choice, to renew the ancient compact once more. Five Days Darkness would teach the young solar the arts and meditations he would need to bring an end to those whose injustice could no longer be tolerated. He would guide Shadow to become the man he would need to be and help him reach his full potential. In return he would perform one task without question for the dark god, as has every incarnation before him. Slippery Shadow didn't know what to think. But he knew this man had saved his own life and that of the girl just now and that he needed someone to help him make sense of everything that had happened. He agreed. Over the course of the five days of calibration Five Days Darkness thus taught the young solar the five secrets of creation he guards, aswell as a hundred other lessons. Spurred on by the powerful essence of the shade of the most potent of gods Slippery Shadow learned to become like a shadow himself. Finally calibration ended and Five Days Darkness took his leave, gifting Slippery Shadow with words of advise and a potent gift. He told him that he would find something that would help his goals if he would set out to find something called the 'Gilded Throne' and bestowed upon the Fivefold Shadow Bindings. Then he left him with the young woman, which reappeared from the darkness Five Days Darkness had hid her in whole and rejuvenated. Embracing a new life, his second breath, Slippery Shadow renamed himself Five Lessons Learned. He would free Champoor from corrupt gods and men choking it. He would make sure that not even the Princes of the Earth could escape their just punishment. And he would make sure that no innocent in his presence would be hurt. But he also knew that he would need allies and patience to reach his goals.


                    The Fivefold Shadow Bindings are a set of what appears to be black leather bracers, in reality however are pure darkness spun from FDDs essence itself and tied together on the inside with starmetal fibers. It's supposed to be a five dot artifact which I have yet to fully design, but in the end it'll be similar to a stronger version of the belt of shadowwalking, granting a stealth bonus for attunment (prob +5 if the 3dot thing gives +3) and having evocations of its own that help operate in the darkness (seeing in the darkness without issue, merging/stepping through/becoming shadow, maybe permanent evocations that upgrade Ebon Shadow style charms in some way).

                    The rescued dragonblood is supposed to be a 4 dot retainer, Rose's First Petal is a 18 y/o freshly exalted wood aspect. We might be able to use her as a figurehead for our company, because otherwise people will prob get suspicious about why a group of mortal traders is so vastly successful (as we invaribly will be due to solar greatness).

                    FDD is a high ranking god (E7 in 2e), there is a 2e write up for him in the Scavanger Lands book. He came into being the first time Sol shone, the shadow created by his light. He's a creature of darkness due to his nature and can only act at night (and especially during calibration). He does not oppose the incarnae, he is more of a fanboy that can never approach them because he is banished by their light and especially loves the solars (and more specifically night castes), though their anima also makes him uncomfortable. He has invented Ebon Shadow style and practices all the dragon styles (mostly to kick db ass with it because he hates them for supplanting the solars and finds it ironic to use their own techniques against them) and otherwise has very very powerful secret gathering charms (though they only work if the secret has never been touched by the light of day). He can also create wealth, heal people by sending them into darkness to sleep and invest people (exalts/mortals) with power by infusing them with his darkness (has side effects). He is supposed to be Five Lessons Learned's mentor.

                    Not posting the char sheet yet as I want to design the Artifact first and ok that with the st. Also not dead set on charms yet, but he's a stealth supernal night caste who practices ebon shadow style, uses alot of stealth charms (prob going to go more for the combat ones since Hope Last Gasp seems to have the more normally sneaky ones, though he may well learn those in time... Fivefold Shadow Burial is just too good not to take!), some Atheletics and larceny, maybe dodge, investigation too.
