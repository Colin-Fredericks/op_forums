        Hyuga immediately grabs the styli and holds it delicately as if it were a fragile piece of glass. "I have just found some secrets and ancient techniques that will provide most useful to me. I cannot help but be quite happy. If you want, I could always be a tutor in the ways of magitech: the artifact making of the old age. For now though, we will focus on finishing these golems".

        Hyuga takes in a deep breath his lungs filling to the max and slowly closes his eyes. He pictures the complicated designs in his head, but now they seem so simple to do. Without looking, he grabs another styli and begins his work two-handedly. He does not seem to be etching in the design in metal, rather it seems as if he is painting a picture, with his "brushes" flying across the surface of the lackluster metal bringing it life as an artist does to a canvas. His hands seem to never cease moving like a blur, yet perfect arcs and lines seem to magically materialize where they had once been.

        A soft glow fills the room as his caste mark burns, and reflects the golden light ever so lightly off the metal making it seem as if it were made of a gold and steel mixture of the finest quality. The design continues to bring even further beauty to the shell. This seemed to be a masterpiece statue rather than a war golem.

        As Hyuga works, he still manages to focus on his thoughts about his new techniques and the information about the realm defense grid. "Yes Jensven. It is my dream to be able to fix the realm defense grid. I would even want to alter it a bit, making it a beacon of protection and not of fear. It would help us keep safe from any fae invasion or invasion from hell were it possible. I have found the basic parts of the technique for powering it. Later, we may be able to make a smaller more localized version, but it would take the help of many people over quite a bit of time. We would also need permission from the local authorities to do so. I have worked a bit with manses before, but it is not my specialty. I do know the techniques to link them now though amongst other things".

        As he continues to work, his anima begins to flare. The golden light becomes stronger, making the entirety of the metal look like a high carat gold. Wisps of blue come and go and a ghostly orichalcum anvil seems to sneak into existence behind him, making soft tinkering sounds. Hyuga grabs the other shells and begins to work on both simultaneously, one hand for each one. Still, he manages not to miss a beat in etching in the designs. He continues working throughout the evening whilst talking more about the realm defense grid, manses, ancient techniques he discovered, possible ideas he had for powerful artifacts, and seem never to lose energy talking about such things.

        I wonder how long it has been since I have been this happy. It has been a long time since I have found any type of secret about the way to create and fix the realm defense grid too. I need to find more. It should be done soon enough. I need to start working on a design for the best sword too. I have seen so many wonderful artifact swords. My ideas should start coming together soon. I should get more tomes to read; I should ask Maia about it now.

        "Maia. I recall you telling me about some rare tomes. I know you let me see one already, but do you think that the Chancellor would let me see some of his? Also, do you think you could spare some paper for me to start wokring on my designs? It would be greatly appreciated".

        Hyuga will be using CNNT for all 3 shells (21m+3WP) moving 12x faster than normal. He will also be adding in 6 dice for the first two craft-air rolls and 5 for the last using his final 17 motes. I am considering this a 3-die stunt as well. so dex 2 +craft 5 +craft-air 2 (from hearthstone) +3-die stunt.

                                (Artificery)

                                Hyuga speeds through is work on the first golem, but by the second, the script begins to blur together, and he can feel his work becoming less precise. Seeing how fast Hyuga's going, Maia begins assembling the single finished shell using an assembly housing on a wall and a mote of white-hot fire conjured from her own elemental essence.

                                Overhearing Jensven and Hyuga's conversation, Maia mentions that there is a government Department (a joint venture between the Capitial government and the Academy) with the expressed purpose of finding demesnes and potential demesnes. They identify those sites, activate the latent demesnes, and then give their recommendation on what should be done with the demesne. Generally its granted back to whatever Gens owned the land to be capped (either because the essence tokens have no real value [as they see it] or because the demesne's effects are dangerous), but sometimes the land is purchased by the Capital to be either developed into a manse for them (the fate of the strongest demsenes) or left as is to be harvested for the Academy. In a handful of other instances, the land was gifted to the Temple.

                                Anyway, you should probably speak with them about you war-manse network.

                                "The Chancellor is probably busy," Maia says, "But if you write out a request, I'll deliver it to him for you."

                                Maia says she let you have full run of the artificery; she points out a writing room with some basic technical manuals and writing and diagramming equipment.
