    God(s) Away on Business [MtA2E Seer Chronicle OCC]

    02-07-2024, 03:53 AM
    God(s) Away on Business
    A Mage the Awakening 2E, Seers of the Throne Chronicle

    “There's leak, there's a leak, in the boiler room
    The poor, the lame, the blind
    Who are the ones that we kept in charge?
    Killers, thieves, and lawyers”
    Tom Waits, “God’s Away on Business”

    This is a chronicle set in the world of Mage the Awakening, Second Edition focused on a group of Seers of the Throne, will workers who have dedicated their lives to the service of the Exarchs, the secret chiefs of the cosmos.

    This group, or pylon, are based in London, UK and are mostly in the service of the Ministry of Mammon, which dedicates itself to the service of the Exarch of the Arcanum of Matter, commonly known as the Chancellor. The Queen of Numbers controls the sleeping world through the concept of ownership, property, and economics based on scarcity. Everything has a price. Everything can be bought or sold, including human souls. And as all Seers believe…Magic is payment.

    Most of humanity, known as sleepers, think that the world they live in, of cups of tea, tube stations, and the morning news on the telly, is real. A select, chosen few have awakened and know that the phenomenal world is the symbolic manifestation of a higher realm, the Supernal. Some of the Awakened view this world as a lie, and like spoiled, ungrateful children on Christmas morning they tear through the wrapping papers of mystery to get at the truths within, fiddle about with and sometimes break their new toys, and then whine and complain that they are bored and want something else. Others, a very special few, are the dutiful, grateful servants of the rightful rulers of the Supernal, the Exarchs. They view the precious mysteries they have been granted sight of as messages from the Exarchs, precious gifts and tools to help the Seer guide and direct sleepers, shape the phenomenal world according to the Exarch’s will, and aide them on their journey ever higher towards assentation, towards the Supernal, where they may join their masters as faithful servants, justly rewarded, paid in full, for their lives below.

    London is one of the world’s great cities. It is the capital and largest city of the United Kingdom, and has a population of roughly eight million people. While there is some evidence that humans have inhabited the area since the second millennium before the common era, the City of London was first established by the Romans in 47 CE. Once the outer frontier of their empire, London became the center of its own mighty empire in the 18th and 19th centuries, an empire that has since dwindled. While the British no longer rule over the world through the power of their mighty navy and many colonies, London remains a powerful economic and cultural force, in Europe and around the globe.

    This chronicle is set beginning in 2016 when the political and economic hegemony of the European Union was threatened by the proposed withdrawal of the United Kingdom, an agreement that was hoped would boost the power of the UK and the British economy and halt the “Europeanization” of the island nation. Culturally this manifested as a contest between those who felt that British identity was being threatened by immigrants and foreign influence and those who feared that a more isolated Britain would become a more authoritarian, illiberal, and even fascist Britain.

    The Awakened know that hidden within the visible world are invisible realms of Mind and Shadow. The world of Spirit, known in the United Kingdoms as “The Other” is very ancient and filled with wounds and scars as human and inhuman inhabitants add layer after layer of emotional resonance upon the ephemeral landscape. The world of the Mind, the Astral realm, shaped by the collective unconscious of humanity and other entities, both reflects and influences the sense of identity, meaning, and purpose that fill sleepers' heads. The high tension in the hearts and minds of the UK’s peoples was bound to cause change and even conflict in these invisible realms.

    Some Seers see a sign in the shifting alliances of the sleepers of a larger snag in the great tapestry. Could there be some kind of conflict between the wills of the current Archgenitors, The Eye, The Unity, The General and the Father and the other Exarchs, such the Chancellor or the Prophet? Or is something even stranger going on? This intuition on the part of some Seers has led to a feeling of mistrust between different ministries, beyond the normal antagonism expressed by those competing to prove themselves worthy to move higher up the Iron Pyramid.

    Settled on both banks of the Thames river, London is known for such landmarks as the Tower Bridge, Trafalgar Square and Nelson’s Column, the Houses of Parliament and Big Ben, and a large observation wheel known as the Eye of London. The most recent major addition to the city skyline is a pyramid shaped tower of iron and glass on the south bank, which has come to be known by Londoners as the Spire. This mixed-use development supertall skyscraper was designed by an Italian architect who claims that the plans for the building came to him in a dream, and it is managed by a firm headed by the eccentric aristocrat Lord Roland. A corporation owned by the Kingdom of Jordan also has considerable shareholdings in the building. The Spire is one the tallest structures in Europe, after similar buildings in Poland and Russia, and is the second tallest in England, after the Yorkshire Radio Transmission Station tower.
    The Spire houses several corporate headquarters for powerful companies, including energy, high tech, food and beverage, and fashion industries, a prominent media company, and a multinational law firm. It also has a five star hotel and restaurant, The Periya Vellai Hotel, and privately owned apartments, home to some of the wealthiest residents of London. The Spire is a state-of-the-art facility, very energy efficient, with some of the fastest elevators in the world that can take one to the high observation deck and cocktail bar. The Spire has an extremely high-tech security system, and Winterhold Bank, a multinational financial institution based in Switzerland, WBS for short, has a vault there that their experts claim is impenetrable.

    One of the Spire’s newest tenants is the Wellington School of Business, a small university that also sponsors free academy schools for 14-18 age group students throughout the city. Despite complaints from the old government education establishment, these schools promise a brighter future for many of the most underprivileged students in London as it seeks to mold the “brightest minds of the future”.

    London has become an increasingly expensive place to live. The center of the city has been taken over by the rich and powerful, and the less fortunate are slowly being pushed out. On the other side of Southwark from the Spire, the borough council has recently decided that one of its large council estates, low income housing built for the poor in the “brutalist” style after the war, is in need of renovation. Lord Roland’s real estate development firm and their Jordanian backers are being considered for the role of creating a new estate for “modern and upwardly mobile” Londoners. They await a decision from the borough council.

    *Disclaimer*
    This is a fictional game. While some of the places and people depicted by the Storyteller are fictionalized versions of real life people and places, no disrespect is intended towards them. We do not mean to imply that London is controlled by evil wizards, as far as we know.

    *2nd Disclaimer*
    The Storyteller is not from the UK, and has never lived there. We intend to provide as much verisimilitude as we can, but some inaccuracies may occur in the depiction of British society, politics, and culture. Again, no disrespect is intended.

          Mekhane
          Name: Alexis Levidis
          Age: 30
          Height: 1.8m
          Eyes: Brown
          Hair: Black
          Path: Obrimos
          Legacy (eventually):Argus Protocol
          Immediate Nimbus: Data streams appear before one’s eyes and important items are highlighted digitally (+1 Wits).
          Signature Nimbus: The feeling of being disconnected from current events, of missing something important.
          Long Term Nimbus: People become more interested in studying technology and become early adopters of new ones.
          Dedicated Magical Tools: His Shadow Name and his sunglasses, which have High Speech sigils for “knowledge” engraved in steel in one temple and one for “industry” engraved in gold in the other.
          Concept: Information Broker

          Background

          A second generation Greek immigrant, Alexis always had a passion for technology from an early age, taking programming classes, joining the robotics club, learning about digital forensics, entering hackathons and more. It was no surprise to his friends and family when, years later, he was contracted as a researcher at Walsingham Infosec. Calling on every professional contact he had, he assembled a team for the most ambitious project yet: a subcutaneous implant that provides an interface with a paired security system. Initial tests using wireless EEG sensors and AR goggles showed promising results, with custom software refining the level of precision users could achieve and miniaturization of hardware well under way.

          The Awakening

          After 4 years of development, the first prototype was ready to be shown to potential investors, only for the project to be suddenly canceled. Before the team could make a presentation appealing to the executives, the lab was emptied overnight. He was told the project was sold to private investors, the team would receive a bonus and be reassigned and all data on the project would be redacted. The invention would have revolutionized security and perhaps earned the team a technology award, having it capriciously spirited away did not sit well with him, so he began to investigate.

          Hidden in the company's database, he learned the hardware would be sent to a shipping company, which would handle the rest. Entering it at night and looking around, he was drawn to a reproduction of an Antikythera mechanism. Working on instinct, he set it to the date the project was stolen, causing it to shift and turn, like a Japanese puzzle box solving itself. A camera lens appeared, his vision blurring as its flash went off.

          When his vision returned, he saw some kind of factory. Data flowed freely in the air, tangible, making frames for clockwork machines to be built as the pieces were moving, magnetized as if to assemble by their own free will into impossible latticework. As incredible as it all was, something felt amiss, the factory felt outdated.

          Moving deeper into the complex, he was able to follow his data to a command center, a giant combination of golden pipes and alpha channel tvs, with a keyboard and command prompt at its center. The monitors showed beings of kinetic energy and data, animated bioorganic circuit humanoids and many more trying to invade the factory. All moving at the rhythm of a console, which flashed and whirled like a beating heart.

          Whatever the thing was, Alexis instinctively felt it breaching the factory was catastrophic. The machines were fighting it back, but it was all done by the AI running the command console. It was slow, inefficient, the data processing and transmission done one line at a time. It was then he noticed his prototype at the base of the keyboard and he knew what he had to do.

          Working quickly, he grasped the data for his software and the data for the driver of the system, using them as pieces to complete the prototype. Installing the implant in the back of his neck, it quickly began interfacing with the AI. Occult laws and system information flooded into his mind, allowing him to start streamlining the factory's data flow and processing. Between the shared consensus with the AI, the robots routed the invaders, the console and its remaining troops digitized away in retreat. Before he could question further, he too was digitized away.

          Now back at the shipping company, he saw the statue was gone. When he called his team to follow up on the investigation, no one knew him, none of his professional contacts knew him, not even his family remembered he worked for Walsingham. His mind was still full of methods to remain supernaturally discrete and a name echoed in his mind: Mekhane.

          In the following months Alexis found a new job in another tech firm, but he never stopped looking into the incident. He could still access the implant, but doctors found nothing where it was supposed to be.

          Eventually his questions about technological mysteries lead him to an agent of Mammon. She argued that if he wanted to prevent his research from being used by bigger fish, he needed to have personal power, resources, both mundane and supernatural. Mekhane agreed and promptly joined the Seers. Upon completing his training, he decided to focus on the most valuable commodity of all: data. Those that had the most data could navigate the most difficult situations, seize opportunities and make a tidy profit.

                      Mother Time
                      True Name: Bridgitte Timothy
                      Age: 44
                      Height: 5'2"
                      Weight: 140 lbs
                      Hair: Dark Brown
                      Eyes: Brown (+Glasses)
                      Path: Acanthus
                      Legacy: None (Currently)
                      Long-Term Nimbus: People connected to Mother Time tend to meet up with each other at times with matching numbers (i.e. 7:07 AM, 11:11 PM, etc.)
                      Immediate Nimbus: The senses accelerate, causing the outside world to slow down to a crawl (+1 Wits)
                      Signature Nimbus: Anything that comes in contact with the Mage's magic seems to move slower, even though it arrives at its destination at the same speed. A car still moves 60 MPH, it just appears to move there at 30-45 before its suddenly there.
                      Dedicated Tool: A silver pocket watch with the engraving "Time Flies and Flees."

                      Background: Raised with the expectations of a "gifted child," Bridgitte from a young age felt that she was destined to become something great. And for a time, this held true, her grades were exceptional, school was easy, it seemed like great things would come to her in the future. She graduated at the top of her class in secondary school, went to Oxford on a scholarship... and it all came tumbling down. There she learned the truth: She was no more a genius than any of the other thousands of students attending. She was but a slightly large fish in an oh-so large pond, and in the end, she never did become that "destined child" which everyone assumed she would be.

                      Awakening: After settling for a life as an educator, due to an inability to find work in anything else, Bridgitte pondered the life she had been given thus far over the years. The weight of expectations as a child had seemed like vapor, but when the truth of how great she truly was came out, they suddenly felt like an Everest on each shoulder. Had all of the adults around been wrong, to declare her to be someone with enough talent and intellect to make it far in the world? Yes... and no. The issue was the expectations themselves... it was that they weren't true. These almost-divinations that were performed on the younger her by parents and teachers were simply getting incorrect information, even if they had the right idea.

                      And so it was, when she became the school principal, that she found herself in a fae glade, surrounded by soothsayers and horologians, all chained to the will of the Exarch known as the Prophet. The Prophet declared that yes, Bridgitte would be great, under his expectations. With his certainty, she would be a Seer that could finally live up to the great demands of the future set upon her in the past. Like a hobbled man removing his burden, Bridgitte let herself be steered by the Prophet's will, joining their Ministry and embarking to enforce the true future onto the rising generations.

                      Personality: As Ms. Timothy, she serves as a "realist," showing the truth of her student's futures to them to alleviate the pain of false greatness. Why bother deluding James with the promise of a Football career if he's not quick on his feet? Why hide the reality of Margaret's disability in regards to potential prospects? Better to crush that senseless optimism now, and spare them their future suffering. As Mother Time, she is this but on a far grander scale. Every day, some new idiot in society believes that they have what it takes to change the future "for the better," or so they claim. But a certain future is a far kinder one. With no uncertainty, there is no risk of disappointment, only the fulfillment of what was always intended. Mother Time is meant to pass the torch to the New Year, the next generation, and she doesn't wish to deceive them. They will never be as great as they think they can be, and that's just fine.

                              Primark
                              Sympathetic Name: David McDonald
                              Age: 35
                              Height: 172cm (5'7'')
                              Weight: 82kg (180 lbs)
                              Hair: Dark Brown
                              Eyes: Brown
                              Path: Mastigos
                              Legacy: None
                              Long-Term Nimbus: A sense of inspiration, as though ideas stream in faster than before, hangs over Primark
                              Immediate Nimbus: Greater problem-solving and faster application of thought (+1 Intelligence)
                              Signature Nimbus: A sense of completion and accomplishment remains as Primark's magic lingers
                              Dedicated Tool: An iron coin fastened to his neck with a strip of leather
                              Concept: Company man playing the game of the corporate ladder

                              Background: David McDonald grew up in London with a upper-middle class wealthy father and a stay-at-home mother, Jonathan and Anne McDonald. Jonathan and Anne divorced when David was 7, after marital unfaithfulness on his father's part, and David was largely living with his father after this event. He learned much of his life methods from his father, even aiming to follow in his footsteps, even bettering him. David grew his corporate portfolio over many years, and for the past few he has enjoyed the comforts of middle management within Winterhold Banking Services.

                              Awakening: It was while working overtime that David experienced his Awakening. A few other members of his team were working late, and he felt they were, as a whole, falling behind and costing the company, and therefore himself, money. Pushing his team further into their work, David often took over from others at their machines, working quickly to keep up with the looming deadlines. As he continued, he felt his presence over his staff naturally urged them on, though to his team it was like a maddening inspiration had taken hold of him. The Dream became ever more real to David, until he came to its end as the team finished their task. An email later, to an address he now can't recall, and the ecstatic stress of his awakening subsided, his new world suddenly laid out before him. Not long after, he finds himself among Seers, within the ministry of Mammon, an organisation that caters to his exact desires; to climb the pyramid, and to play the games of power

                              Description and Personality: David wears a suit at almost all times, black or navy blue, with a light blue or white shirt beneath. His shoes are either all-black trainers for comfort, or black or brown dress shoes. As Primark, he wears much of the same clothing with a large woollen coat over the top rather than a blazer, covering much of his Sympathetic identifiers. He is clean-shaven, with swept-up hair and a neatly kept facial care routine.

                              Primark is little caring for the people around him, opting to see everyone as tools and obstacles, which he would expect any wise person to do the same of him. He harbours no resentment to any rival or enemy who uses such tools to get ahead, save for making sure those people are kept as rivals so that he may rise above them one day.

                              Consumer of lore, myth, history, tales and songs. Pronoun ambiguous.

                                              Saulin
                                              Real name: Salil Amjad
                                              Age: 35
                                              Concept: Silver-spoon scion with central planning aspirations
                                              Path: Thyrsus
                                              Dedicated magical tool: Petrified acorn gilded in copper and worn on a chain around the neck.

                                              Salil comes from a wealthy family well-entrenched in the Jordanian business world who moved to the UK when Salil was 12. Naturally, he was groomed to follow in his father's footsteps and studied Business at Cambridge. He had been working at his father's company few a years when, one night, after getting high with some co-workers, he had a waking dream. He remembers walking down the sidewalk when the cement beneath his feet cracked and collapsed, dropping him into a subterranean cavern. Stunned, he looked up to see a host of strange creatures moving along the ceiling. They crawled, slithered, burrowed, and pulsated in and out of the living stone. As he watched in wonder, his perspective shifted and he was able to follow the creatures as they climbed through the earth into the world above. He saw them move in and out of buildings, vehicles, even people's heads. He eventually realized that the world he though he understood was inextricably and symbiotically intermixed with this alien ecosystem. It was thriving and beautiful and... chaotic. These creatures were not intelligent agents wisely directing the environment around them. Instead, they were senseless and primal beasts carelessly tromping through our world. As he watched in both wonder and dismay, he saw a red, slime-covered salamander crawl in and out of a man's body as the man, clearly drunk, violently assaulted a woman in an alley. Salil rushed over, wrestled the creature away, and pinned it to the ground. It snarled and spat at him, but he made quick work of it by pummeling it with his fists. Right before he passed out from the ordeal, he saw the creature's blood form his name on the street.

                                              Salil, or Saulin as he eventually chose to be called, was quickly picked up by Seers. While his parents were neither mages nor sleepwalkers, his father's business empire had been thoroughly infiltrated by agents of the Throne. Saulin believes the Spirit world to be a valuable resource if properly managed. Sleepers, who have neither knowledge of nor defenses against such things, need to be protected and shepherded. Saulin, for the greater good, is happy to be that shepherd.

                                              "We’re making a better world. All of them, better worlds."

                                                      Berith
                                                      Sympathetic Name: Thomas Haytham
                                                      Age: 28 years of age
                                                      Concept: Politician, controlling the now with a hand on the past and and eye to the future.
                                                      Path: Moros
                                                      Dedicated tool: A Silver Ring that doubles as a soul stone.
                                                      Long-Term Nimbus: Feelings of stagnation, the air becomes stiller, and things seem more and more unchanging before seeming minutely new
                                                      Hair: Ear-length black and wavy
                                                      Eye-color: Dull Green
                                                      Height: 180 cm
                                                      Weight: About 12 stone, 77kg

                                                      Background: Born as Thomas Haytham, Thomas scraped through shitty job through shitty job, bowing to politicians above him as he interned, managed, and seemed to hit dead ends. As a sleeper, Thomas was hitting a dead end. He lacked the resources, lacked the connections, and lacked the momentum behind him. As a Mage, he finally had a shot and quickly climbed the ladder, meeting the Guardians and bring indiscreet with his seeming defection to the Guardians, carrying a soul stone of his mentor with him to that of the Iron Pyramid under the Ministry of Mammon. Now, Thomas stands with at least his own meager backing of political power in their local borough council, able to shape the community and its resources with time.

                                                      Awakening
                                                      He woke up to the same day, twenty five times. At first, he thought he was simply in a rut. A bad case of Deja vu. The same questions, similar tasks, dutifully following the commands of his superiors. The second time he lived that day, it was an oddity. Maybe he was slipping. The second, the third, when he started to be able to quote back what his boss wanted? It was becoming clear. For the next three weeks he repeated the day, finding each day a little bit more success when he weighed his choices against his past failures. He was provided a perspective never possessed before. That the old Thomas would relive the same, fruitless cycle if he did not take the momentum of his life by the reins from there. The past was behind him, the future was forked ahead of him, and some futures can become pasts to make a new present. An experience culminating to awakening and the death of an identity so that a new one might form.

                                                      Personality
                                                      As Berith, he's a mildly stoic, and seemingly kind man who takes dutiful guardianship over his allies and own. As Thomas Haytham, he is a polite politician who tries to portray themselves as "guiding hand" of his local constituents. .
