    Scholar looks at the address and finds the first person who will tell him how to get there. It is a little before noon so he takes his time. There is an issue on his mind, how will he get the raw materials, precious metals and gems, to craft his repairs. And his sister. He missed his little sister who was stolen by the Fae. He needed a way to track her. It has been several weeks since Alabaster and where was she, how was she. The Fae would rue the day they took her from him. Ever since his parents were killed and he became an orphan, he took care of his little sister. He felt he let her down.

    Enough of this sad thought. In order to survive in the Wyld, he needed his Colossus to provide protection. He needed to fix it and develop the skill to drive it. To fix it required a fortune in metals and gems. He considered. His knowledge of Occult is what served him as he wandered through the streets searching for the Duke's Estate. He needed a series of Elementals. He needed an earth elemental that could travel through the earth and find gems and metals. He needed a few of them. He did not know how long he would be here, he needed the captain's trade route. He would have earth elementals find the precious raw materials, and water elementals that could track him somehow.




    Occult 5 + Intelligence 5 + 5 motes to know about two elementals, a water elemental that could track him through the seas, and an earth elemental that could find and mine gems and metals.

    Earth Elemental 15 Dice 8 successes!
    9 1 8 3 3 7 2 7 10 6 8 8 1 4 6

    Water Elemental 15 Dice 7 successes!
    5 10 6 6 4 9 2 9 8 3 2 2 10 1 5

    He is left with 13 peripheral and 3 personal motes, he does not want his anima flaring.




    He considered various elementals he knew about from his studies in the little gods and elementals. People unknown to him stared at him as he walked through the streets talking to himself. He was oblivious to his surroundings. He wore his cloak and breastplate and leather pants, all black. His cloak had a silver clasp. He didn't notice a pair of men who could be following him. He did not notice anything save the addresses he was passing and the thoughts of elemental types he could summon. A water elemental that could track him through the water, and an earth elemental who could find raw material. And then he saw his destination, the Duke's house.

    He walked to the Dukes house and knocked on the gate getting the attention of the guard protecting the house. He asked, "I was invited here to meet Mr. Jenkins," and he showed the guard the note.

            Scholar is ushered into a large salon, whereupon, he sees Jenkins sitting at a large table surrounded by parchments of all types.

            "Ah, you're here on time. Excellent. I cannot abide by tardiness. Still, it does you credit. in any case, sit, sit, we have much to discuss. I suppose the best place to start is to introduce myself. As you know, I am Hieronymus Jenkins, but you may not know I am the Agent Plenipotentiary of the Tyrant here. I have been assisting the Duke, but sadly, the Duke, like much of his kin, are incompetent to the bone in terms of governance. Alas, if only Lord Joshua Von Rogan was still among the living, he'd sort this all out. However, that is not why I asked you here today. I saw that you are a man of artistry and grace, and the Von Rogans, the Family of Tyrants of Dhaam-Bel, have long sponsored the arts. I wish to commission you to make a statue of the Lord Duke. And not just any statue. I want a colossus that we can set up in the town square, made of the finest wave marble. If you can succeed in this task, not only can I provide you and your allies with a letter of marque, but you will be personally, handsomely rewarded. Well, young man? What do you say?" he asks, looking up at Scholar expectantly.
