                        Just wanted to add some clarification here because this came up a while ago in my other game. You almost have it right, but not quite there.

                        The wording is complicated, but when using Exceptional Luck, you need to treat Potency of the spell and Potency of the boon/hex as two related but separate stats. Anything that references spell potency is based on the full potency, which can be allocated to different bonuses such that the total potency of the boons equals the spell's potency.

                        Your total spell Potency is 5, with Duration of 1 day. That means you can "mix and match" up to 5 boon effects: 9-again on any 1 mundane roll (which does not need to be specified until you choose to use it), +1 bonus to a specific skill for a number of rolls equal to the spell Potency, or 1 condition.

                        You chose Charmed and Steadfast, and split the remaining 3 boon Potency between 3 skills. So you will get +1 (not +5) on each of 5 Brawl, Occult, and Expression rolls. You can choose when to apply the bonuses, but must decide before any roll, and they only last until the end of the day.

                        If you prefer, you could also pick a single skill (say, Occult for example) and make it a +3 boon, which will apply on any 5 Occult rolls of your choosing before the spell ends. You would still have your Charmed and Steadfast conditions.

                        Does that make more sense?

                                                Nope, the boon/hex itself does not have a primary spell factor. That pertains only to the spell itself that creates the boon.

                                                So just to reiterate: The Potency of your Exceptional Luck spell is increased by your Arcana dots per normal rules, and Duration of the spell is 1 turn by default unless you make it longer. When your spell succeeds, you can mix and match the following to "spend" your spell Potency.

                                                For each 1 boon Potency, gain the following:
                                                - A beneficial condition
                                                - A use of 9-again (or 8-again with reach)
                                                - +1 for a specific skill for a number of rolls equal to the spell's total Potency

                                                All bonuses end when resolved or used, or when the spell itself expires, whichever comes first.
