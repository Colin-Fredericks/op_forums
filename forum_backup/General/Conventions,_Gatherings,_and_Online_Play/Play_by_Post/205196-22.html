                    Max had expected some mockery, but his heart leaped into his throat when the spear came down. As Aries lowered himself, the Pig stood, shaken and speechless. He'd soon regret not finding his voice quicker. "I-I-I, n-no, of c-course n-not, I just--" Aries talked over him, cowing him into silence. Still, he'd begun getting together an idea, one that just might let him talk his way past this. Max drew a breath to steady himself and went for it when the First of the Zodiac began to laugh. "N-No! Look, I-I think this isn't what it seems. I--"

                    And then that word struck. Max stood there, stunned, grunting in discomfort as the changes raced over and through him like a fire. The pink flesh and scattered white bristles gave way to a sweeping ocean of hard brown bristles, while his large floppy ears shrank to a smaller, narrower pair, perked and alert. His body exchanged fat for muscle, and that wasn't the only structural change: his stature went from small and childish to the size and build of a much more mature animal. The now-filthy robe in which he'd been dressed hadn't been sized for this and tore like tissue paper. This revealed that his curly tail had straightened and lengthened slightly, a small dark tuft crowning its tip. After a few panicked seconds of confusion, Max spotted his new tusks, gleaming proudly, and realized what was happening. He squealed in alarm. And found that even his voice had groan deeper and huskier.

                    "Wait! Stop! This..." He paused, puzzled at the sound. It was growing more guttural with every word. By this point, the tingling fire had mostly begun to recede, but still simmered in Max's throat and jaw. '... Ohhh, no...' The thought sent a chill through him. "I made a deal! He's rrroing groo--!" 'No no no...' "Wrree neeeeed groo grryyy--!" 'No please no no...' "Grink, runk! Roink! REEEEEE!!!"

                    It was no use.

                    He looked up from the ground, past the end of his snout and his new tusks, and locked fearful eyes with Aries. Even the perfect words couldn't get the Boar out of this one.

                                    08-09-2015, 03:05 AM
                                    Max was not in his clearest frame of mind. He was terrified of losing his voice. He was terrified of Aries's hunting plans. And he was terrified the boar's rage would consume him again. Max remembered those thoughts of fury and bloodlust from when he'd wanted to tear Nate apart. Already, he could feel a flame simmering in his throat as he looked up at Aries. The premonition from Libra had crystallized for him his worst fears about it. There was so much senseless brutality and destruction he could wreak if he lost control. And part of him wanted to; amidst the fear, it was starting to hit him how angry he was at the Zodiac hunter for doing this to him. The Boar wanted to tear Aries into pieces too small for anyone to ever find. Feeling that way frightened him so much.

                                    Only... it wasn't his biggest fear. Nor were his fears of never speaking again, nor of being brutally hunted. No, it struck Max that his biggest fear was betraying the trust that had been placed in him. The thing that scared him most was was falling short of who and what he needed to be to reach the end, to save himself and everyone else. In a moment of clarity, it all seemed so obvious. He realized something about those other fears? They were all subordinate. They would not beat him because he could not allow them that.

                                    Max couldn't speak? He would make himself known, however he must.
                                    The Boar's rage flared? Max would not let it rule him; he would channel it instead.
                                    Aries had already written roles for them both in the script? Well then. Fuck. That. Script!

                                    In the spirit of the third, Max would not become the hunted, and would not allow a hunt at all.
                                    In the spirit of the second, Max would do Aries great injury, but only as needed.
                                    In the spirit of the first... Well. That would be a matter of course.

                                    He felt his fists tighten. Hands! He hadn't been made a complete beast. And those early days of martial arts practice in his newly porcine body... Max decided on his course and something like a plan. "Something like" because a proper plan would need longer than he could give. He needed to act now. So it was fortunate his transformation had made of him a creature adept at charging headlong into danger.

                                    The Boar gave a swinish bellow, not matching the horn's volume, but just barely. Mid-cry, before Aries could set down the horn and otherwise fully prepare himself, Max hurled himself up at the First among the Zodiac. The new muscle in his legs had more than enough power to carry him there, and the strength of his arms -- and the wire-harsh bristles -- promised a hell of an impact from the fist he threw. At the same time, he leaned his neck forward, riding his body's momentum and letting the Boar guide his tusks to where they would leave the most debilitating gash of a wound. His other hand stands ready to seize at the wrist of Aries's spear hand, perhaps to kill his grip on it, but mostly to ensure the combined massive weight of both beasts fell agreeably on either that arm or the spear itself.

                                    If it worked, the element of surprise would end things in seconds. And Max was truly playing every card in his hand: much as Aries might take that animal cry to mean Max had lost all reason (welcome bonus though this would be), it held a different purpose. It was a call to the Wilds. In a voice it surely knew better than his usual stutter. With its aid, the ground itself would lift sharply under the Boar as he jumped, adding even more speed and momentum, and cutting down the window of a reaction time. Nor was it the sole request: Aries's steed would find its footing thrown into chaos as the earth bucked and trembled wildly. Even an experienced rider and hunter would have enough trouble staying steady in the saddle, much less trying to aim a weapon at a sudden attack.

                                    Still, while he had done what he could, Max was not just dealing with a regular steed and an experienced rider and hunter. These were supernatural ones. It was an open question how well this was going to go for him. One way or another, this question would have its answer very, very soon.
