            Mark 5 and Adonis

            It's easy enough to find some people on the street to get talking- Earth cut a pretty distinctive figure, and storming off with such an obvious desire to be left alone more or less projected a vibe of simmering anger and pure 'Screw off'. The people Mark 5 chats up; the homeless, the disenfranchised, informants and information brokers with fading prison tats, all point him in the same direction. Mark 5 isn't exactly the most sociable Promethean Adonis has ever seen, so where did he meet all these people, exactly?

            Mark's own knowledge of Earth gives him a pretty good place to start after getting pointed in the right direction. Parks and gardens stick out like sore thumbs in the crumbling industrial vistas of the North End of Winnipeg. They end up at the Joe Zuken Heritage Park, the first green space that Mark 5 and Adonis stumble across, and see the unexpected sight of Earth, former terror Earth-Dies-Screaming, gardening with a human woman.

            OOC: I'm leaving the details of the people Mark 5 ends up talking to open-ended so that Wormwood has the opportunity to detail some of Mark 5's exploits as a vigilante. What little acts did he perform to get these people to open up to him? Is he actively making the streets a better place, or are his misunderstandings of right, wrong, violence, physical contact, and motivation simply making people too afraid to not talk to him?

            Earth

            The woman sighs, no talking works, but she'd hoped that Earth would open up. Then again, how could she possibly know what Torment does to her? She gestures off-handedly towards a large blue plastic bin, pulls out her own tools, and settles down into the garden. In silence they weed and harvest lettuce and rhubarb, they trim cuttings off of mint and dill, and transplant them into pots for indoor growing. No point in letting perfectly good herbs go to waste over the winter.

            There's a kind of silent communication between Earth and the woman that stumbled across her- something that goes beyond words. It's a kind of language she can understand; there's tension and discomfort communicated with a particular curl of the shoulders that spreads across the upper back, but there's comprehension and shared sorrow in the lines of her face, and practiced skill in the sudden deft movements of fingers.

            Earth can see, coming across the field, as she picks up a bag of soil to follow the wordless direction of her fellow gardener, Adonis and Mark 5. They came after her.

            OOC: So Earth now has the Disconnected Condition, an Experience, a Vitriol Experience, and a whole smattering of other Conditions. Wow, is she ever an Experience generator. In either case, Mark 5 and Adonis have caught up with you, so I'll leave the floor open for you guys to interact with characters. If anyone makes rolls, or I feel the need to mention something, I will, but for now go nuts with character interactions.

                    This is weird. (this isn't weird.) This is so weird.

                    She's working alongside a human, and it feels like she's sleepwalking. She's too numb to really appreciate it. Her own body language isn't instinctively human- she can recognize feelings and motives in the human's movements, but it feels like she can't reflect them back. She just labors and stays silent, keeping her self-pitying melancholy to herself.

                    But they're sharing the work. Whatever else is in the deeper waters of their minds, their conscious focus is the exact same thing. It's nice!

                    The others are here, to her great surprise. No alchemist in sight. Earth frets about it as she carries the bag, and finally sets it down where it's needed. She exposes her palms and raises her eyebrows to the human, trying to convey calm and pause.

                    With her hands and clothes covered in dirt, she lumbers over to the other Created. It's tense. Her Azothic radiance is simmering with Torment. She puts a hand over her mouth and drags it sharply sideways, leaving a black smear around her lips. Speechless.

                    She averts her eyes from Mark 5, her face showing shame. Instead she scowls at Adonis, demanding explanation. Well?

                            Even though the Tammuz was basically brimming with joy upon seeing Earth, he did not miss her ashamed look. This whole business must have brought up some rather unhappy memories. He took notice of the torment, and something within him stirred. Memories of a similar state. He had to help her. But how? This was unknown territory, however, he had seen humans cheer each other up in times like this, so he tried to do the same. Trying his best at smiling sympatheticly, it came out passable... barely. Better than anyone could have expected, though, really. It was a smile, at least. The titan's hand reached out to awkwardly pat Earth on the arm twice, then he immediately stopped the gesture. This was weird. How was this supposed make someone feel better?

                            "If you think he could be useful, we could keep him alive."
                            Now, Adonis was speaking his language. Useful. He might actually be that, since his bosses are the ones they...
                            Everything clicked.
                            A Titan, tasked with keeping a former Centimanus on the path. A Fury, former Centimanus, on the Path of the Vigilante.
                            Apparently, an entire Corporation of Alchemists, guilty of the most horrible crimes against the Created. All three in the same city. How very clear. How very obvious what is meant to happen.
                            "Punish those responsible of the same crimes as you once committed, and find absolvation in distancing yourself from Earth-Dies-Screaming through that". Who better to help with a task like this than a Soldier?
                            And finally, the Mimic. The wonderful Mimic, distracted and curious, defending a potential Alchemist. A potential Alchemist the Tammuz would have probably just... handled rather than listened to. Pretty Boy must have been sent by the Principle, to ensure that this crucial piece of Earth's redemption is not broken before its time.
                            Mark 5 facepalmed and exclaimed "Yes, of course!"
