                        Some ideas to build off that:

                        Court of Clay: Powerless/Slothful seem like things that are not terribly interesting narrative-wise. How about Awe-struck Humility instead? It resonates with the luxury and beauty of Dubai itself, and may draw changelings that are both awe-inspiring and humble, each taking part in a different part of the Court's idea. Perhaps "Court of Spires" might be an interesting take instead?
                        Court of Stars: I feel Burning Ambition might fit better?
                        Court of Song: I think we can do something here with the idea that mankind's creations are ways to praise the divine. Songs, buildings that reach the sky, ceremonies. Maybe it's less Blind Worship and more like Thoughtful Worship? Which would make the emotion more rarefied and harder to find.
                        Court of Tides: I think "Indulgence" might fit here. You escaped, found freedom, and now can indulge in it. Perhaps "Secret Indulgence" as a more specific form?

                        And then you can basically tie all four Courts as different aspects of Dubai itself, with its awe-inspiring, ambitious towers that rise up to the sky as if seeking to touch divinity, where you can indulge in luxury or simply freedom.

                                                    The politics of the city are a big reason why the Shaytan Court is not a great idea, not the least of which is your Mantle comment(Because yes, that would lead to a lot of Mantle loss, people switching from one to the other constantly).

                                                    The simple fact of the matter is that a Court like that is that it relies too much on a foundation that shifts. The goals and motives of the Shaytan, beyond the basics, change with each exile, and that means people with interest in backing the Shaytan changes all the time. Because of that, you just can't have a stable organization.*

                                                    Thematically, as well, there are two aspects to keep in mind. One is that it's more appropriate for Iblis to sway members from the other courts onto his side, to usurp and incite rebellion. The other factor to keep in mind is that in the story we're working off of, there was sin, but no whisperer-the jinn aren't inherently malicious, they just kind of end up that way for all the reasons humans with powers would. Having a pre-established hell faction kills some of the mood of both those factors.

                                                    On top of that, it's overly simplistic and boring. I'm not designing this, but I still wouldn't want to advocate boring and simplistic.

                                                    *Now, if we do want them to have a faction still, this is why I recommended of giving them a way to recruit Huntsmen and Loyalists-Arcadian forces inherently have more invested in the few constants of Iblis, and it plays into the struggle of changelings caught between their clay and smokeless fire identities.
