                                During downtime the pack can conduct investigations, research, or rumor gathering for one of the areas. However, each PC can only make one roll for one area. They may also add their dots in either Allies, Mentor, or Contact if they can justify using them to help with their roll. The states for the rolls are:

                                Investigation: Wits + Investigation - Rival Pack's Subterfuge.

                                Research: Intelligence + Academics - Area's Mystery Rating (A score correlating to the cities dark secrets. Each area' rating is purposefully hidden from the players.)

                                Rumor Gathering: Wits + Socialize or Streetwise (each will yield a different result) - Rival Pack's Subterfuge.

                                P.S. Fully Updated Map of Victory City
