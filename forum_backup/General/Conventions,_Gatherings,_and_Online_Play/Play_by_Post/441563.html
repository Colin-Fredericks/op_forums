                              The names are really really hard, I dont have one down yet. My character though is a tef-ahbi who I see as slightly disillusioned with his overall purpose, so for some descents now he's been trying to rebuild his memory. Its not helping that he forgets most of what he's doing every time. I also see him as unusually active for a mummy, he has his cult wake him for a defined list of emergencies and other than that every century. As a builder it drives him nuts being unable to be continually working. Of course he doesnt realize this schedule is actually more of a recent habit after certain events, and that this time his cult missed the mark again due to other recent events. While if it does seem apparent one of his cult members is directly at fault he'll punish them brutally (he is a mummy after all) he'll also secretly be reevaluating how he runs things, or the elements that failed. He designed his cult after all, he taught them how they should run themselves, if his design was meant to last then there shouldnt have been a failure. This means not only the hands but the vision must have been flawed. By the by he also has a lot of investment in his cult. It currently operates as a company that just moved its headquarters to D.C. and specializes in internet infrastructure.
