                    This is how letter planning is going to go.

                    1. I will post Ryo (and maybe Titus) letters.
                    2. I post OOC updates on your situation and maybe send you new plot. (See above)
                    3. Via PM, you will send me your actions and complications. You will have three backgrounds and two crafting slots (if you are a crafter)
                    4. I will approve complications and send you dice rolls and difficulties.
                    5. You roll dice and send results via PM
                    6. You write letters. Be sure to include your backgrounds/crafting slots/xp spends in a spoiler tag at the bottom of the letter.

                    The basic idea is to decrease the back and forth needed to write letters and to front load my involvement in the letters. That way I can better schedule my time for the game instead of juggling everyone's replies over a period of two weeks.

                    This work for everyone?

                                                        Originally posted by Exthalion View Post

                                                        Photoshop? What tools?
                                                        GIMP. Smudge tool at ~90% rate to remove the nails (drag the surrounding colors over the nails to erase them), then Free Select, at 4-800% zoom to draw an outline for the cloth parts and move them to a new layer (with free select, use small line segments rather than trying to follow a curve by hand). Then Colorize, fiddling with the settings until it looked good (in this case, ~5/44/0).

                                                        The free select is the slowest part, since you have to do detailed selection around things like each bead on the abacus. It took... half an hour or so, I think? Would take longer if I weren't familiar with the process. I used to do this a lot for my games, finding pictures that worked well then changing the hair and eye color to match the character I wanted to use them for.
