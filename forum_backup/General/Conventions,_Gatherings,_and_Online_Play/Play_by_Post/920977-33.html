        Originally posted by crapcarp View Post

        My last post (at time of posting) mentioned Control Heat. It's a Ruling Forces spell on p. 142. The caster can change an area's temperature to an extent that it can increase Extreme Environment levels. Influence Heat is a Compelling Forces spell on p.141 where the caster can move heat around to the point that the subject ignores Extreme Environment levels (either by concentrating or dispersing heat, depending on the Extreme Environment). So is it too much of a stretch to say that a Ruling spell could increase and concentrate heat from friction into a flammable material to ignite it?

        That said, if you do rule that it's a Weaving spell, then Phoenix can't cast it, as he only has 2 dots in Forces (not trying to guilt you or anything, I just wanted to let you know).
        I still think it's too much of a stretch. I saw your post and considered it, and I know you only have Forces 2. If you had Forces 3 I would've been ok with it, because I think you'd be able to more easily justify the ability to create a fire from essentially nothing.

        Basically, the friction generated from rubbing your hands against straw is so minor that the method of starting a fire from that heat alone would need the Perfecting practice. You'd need to get the straw to something like 180 °F. That's far hotter than the temp required for Extreme Environments.
