                        - As to the more mystical abilities of our kind, thanks to my practice in the Black Art, I am capable of seeing, summoning and compelling to service ghosts residing on our side of the Shroud.                         - Then we have no secrets towards each other anymore. Mister. St. John, if you share my desire to scout the Church Rock Cemetery, then I suggest we go now and make advantage of whatever is left of the night, hm?

                        He waits for a response, however unless someone presents a compelling argument against leaving at all, he will go alone if his offer is turned down.

                                        Raul Abrantes

                                        Raul remains unfazed by the eerie atmosphere of the place. As if it was the most normal thing to do he uses his Necromancy to percieve any ghosts that may lurk in the area*. Even if there are any, however, he first turns to the crypt in the middle, trying to find any odd details like mystical symbols or evidence of someone else tampering with the place. In the unlikely event that the crypt is freely accessible, he also checks inside**.

                                        Unless the search uncovers something disturbing, he finally turns to what he came for - disturbing the dead. Since he has no items useful in summoning on himself right now, he looks for graves of children, mindful that friends and relatives often leave at these sites exactly what he needs - toys, photos and the like. Of course, if more promising opportunity presents itself, he shall take it. If the search yields any degree of success, he goes right in for the summoning, using the Summon Soul power***. Anxiously, he awaits the answer.

                                                He settles on trying to peer beyond the Shroud and see if there is anything different compared to the living lands as well as look for any ghosts or other entities in the Shadowlands.

                                                [OOC: Shroudsight Perception + Awareness Difficulty 7: (Dice Pool 5): 7, 1, 8, 6, 1 - Failure]

He begins looking around carefully trying see if he can figure out what the source of the sensation of being watched is coming from.

                                                [OOC: Looking for why he feels like he's being watched Perception + Alertness <Unknown Difficulty>: (Dice Pool 3): 5, 2, 5 (odds are this is a failure so I will continue on as such, if its not let me know and I will edit as necessary)]

                                                    Raul Abrantes

                                                    After a long while of walking around, Raul concludes that there is nothing of note in this place - at least, nothing he can see. The crypt may yet hold something interesting, but until he can find someone that can pick the lock - like that Rosselini fellow - it is all moot anyway. "No matter" he thought to himself, as he appraises the young man's ghost. Not a longtime denizen of this cemetery, but still might know something useful. However, without his posession of some kind there is no guarantee that the ghost will answer truthfully, or that he will cooperate at all.

                                                    The summoning rituall was straining and accompanied by the usual feeling of dread, but it was nonetheless successful - before him stood a spectral figure of a little boy, seemingly confused. Raul crouched to level his face with the boy's and spoke, his words dripping with sweet venom:

                                                    - Easy, child. Everything is fine. Now, tell me what I need to know, so that you may return to your slumber.
                                                    His eyes flared up for a moment, as he moved his left hand in an occult gesture and uttered a few words in Latin, compelling the ghost to obey.

                                                    1. Has anyone entered the cemetery after dark in recent weeks?
