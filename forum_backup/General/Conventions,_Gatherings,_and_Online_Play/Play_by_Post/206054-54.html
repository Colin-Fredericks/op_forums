                                                        No worries, Thorbes. There are a lot of directions this could go. Sometimes just getting thoughts out of your head, talking them through with people, and maybe getting a little outside input can help things fall into place. ^_^

                                                        You've got a good setup, and Evilmarmelade's led off with some great suggestions! Think I'll babble a bit with some notions...

                                                        - Memories Rewoven:
                                                        Portals to the past, at least some of them, lead into memories of particular people -- perhaps just those present, perhaps even more. Past events play out as they did before, and visitors are (mostly) ignored. However, should they change the course of events in some way, this has an effect on whomever held that memory. How this works, well... Options are plentiful:
                                                        + The past actually changes and ripples forward, at least in some limited fashion.
                                                        + The person remembers the new events instead of the old. May make no sense if they stop to think about it, or other memories may change to accommodate it.
                                                        + Memories shift their emotional tenor. E.g. Someone depresses or cheers up memory!person, so memory!person feels worse or better about that memory, perhaps with personal/developmental impacts.
                                                        + People/Things can be taken out of the memory by visitors and back into the Hedge.
                                                        ... To some extent, I guess the above can actually work quite well for present and future portals. Neat possibilities there, too.

                                                        - Thoughts for Visions:
                                                        + What the trio's (and/or maybe even other Zodiac members') loved ones have been up to back on Earth -- or those who may be important if/when they return.
                                                        + Messages of advice/sorrow/comfort/rage/hope/whatevs from their future selves -- or, oddly, from their past selves, perhaps about things they've forgotten.
                                                        + Depictions of what brought the Western and Eastern Zodiac members to be as they are -- ditto the Scribe and Collector, for that matter.
                                                        + Details about Summernickel and Winterdime -- or perhaps those they've helped (or even hurt) in the past.

                                                        - The Guide:
                                                        A strange individual -- trusted by one of the goblins and distrusted by the other -- offers to show Jessica and Nate some important portals/images/paths. These will leave an impact, but...
                                                        + Are they for the better? For the worse? Or is that up to the two of them?
                                                        + Does the guide have some beneficent or malicious intent, or is this simply what the guide does with visitors?
                                                        + What opinions does this guide foster -- and what actions might the guide take -- should there be any damages, tampering, or other impacts to the arcs or portals?
                                                        + What relation, if any, does this guide have to the Collector?

                                                        - Zodiac Pursuit:
                                                        It may be further from their domain than the Collector would want them to be (though oaths could have things covered), but perhaps one or more members of Eastern or Western Zodiac could pursue or put in an appearance. If you thought up any scenarios you were really hoping to use, this could be a good window. It wouldn't really need to be them, either, given the weirdly out-of-space and -time nature of the place.

                                                        - The Collector:
                                                        Oh shit! The Keeper's not about to let Summernickel or Winterdime carry out a repeat performance with his charges, and comes in person!
                                                        + Still, outside the heart of his realm, his powers are merely very powerful instead of outright godlike.
                                                        +Perhaps even something about that particular place leaves him especially vulnerable -- perhaps the goblins choose that location for this reason.
                                                        + Should Max stagger through to the end, the Keeper may be forced back early to address that bit of business, or at least divide his attention/power.
                                                        +The Keeper may aim to simply keep the Goat and Ox preoccupied.
                                                        + Or this may come paired with the above "Zodiac Pursuit," whether to support the Keeper (while he's less imposing than usual), or for the Keeper to support his loyal -- or at least not actively escaping -- Zodiac members with magic or mind games (possibly because direct action is forbidden to him there).
