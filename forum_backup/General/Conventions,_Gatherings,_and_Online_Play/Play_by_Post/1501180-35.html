                I believe neither Elpis or Qaf have Space. Elpis has Mind and Qaf has Time as their extra Arcana.

                The animal options all depend on how skilled Philly is in securing their help. Birds, rats, spiders and ants are great informants, especially if you secure an "exclusivity contract". Speak with Beasts is cast on the mage, rather than the animals, so one with good animal ken can have a network of discrete mundane spies, which don't trigger the Peripheral Sight and leave no Nimbus Signature (unless they are in the Long Term Nimbus' sympathetic range). So if she is discrete in her dealings, this allows her to cultivate an air of omniscience. Qaf's dog is not trained to guard, but she probably could be convinced to help. She is not a barker, so if we heard her, something is off nearby.

                Qaf has contacts in security firms, so he could ask them where to purchase a mundane security system with a good cost benefit, maybe Resources could be used for that ? Alternatively, Philly could make the house's interior be under Machine Invisibility (we don't want people to be filming a home movie and suddenly see an empty lot), but that would shut down our own security as well.
