    Character History
    “Get a fucking job,” he said. “You want that? Work for it!” he said. “You don't deserve anything,” he said.
    Yeah, life with mom was good, but dad just sucked. Whenever he deigned to grace Jon and Ms. Blaszczyk, it was always a cold encounter. Most kids who have divorced parents, or two that seem like they're a divorced couple, usually look forward to visits from the estranged parent. Not Jon.

    It didn't matter if Jon was captain of the debate team. It didn't matter if he ran track and cross country and got trophies. It didn't matter that he was an Eagle Scout. It didn't matter if Jon was on the honor roll. Dad always took the time to mention his many, many more achievements than Jon had.

    Jon was quite isolated, as his pursuits of perfection left him little time to cultivate friendships. Even with his extracurricular resume, Jon had a difficult time finding a job in this tiny town. The only place that teenagers could expect to get a job was at the local mall. Sadly, all the stores weren't hiring. But finally fate smiled upon Jon in the form of a company that was infamous for their cheap “As Viewed on Television” products. All Jon had to do was use his inborn speaking skills to peddle the shit. It slices, it dices, it cuts through aluminum cans and dehydrates a turkey. Jon knew how to present these crap goods and get people to buy them, even knowing full well that those products would break after getting home, or would gather dust in a closet. It was grating on the spirit. After a year, fleecing people for an obviously corrupt company, it began wearing on Jon's soul. Keeping with the debate team, keeping with the track team, keeping up the grades, and now being a lowly huckster built up the rage until one day, after a former customer came back to the mall and started throwing the fragmented pieces of the Juicertron with included Shammytastic cloth at Jon just about lost it. When he got home that night, dad was there, likely to toss some hush-money to Jon and his mom. He wasn't in the mood to see dad or take his money, and after tossing it right back at the old man, heated words were exchanged.

    The next thing Jon remembers is dad having claw marks on his chest and a bite wound on his shoulder. Mom was incommunicado. Life was upended in an instant.

    A few years later and Jon found himself learning the ways of the Garou, in the role of a Lorekeeper and Ritemaster. Thank Gaia for those good study habits he forced himself into learning. These Shadow Lords of the Thundering Pines are tough bastards with higher standards than dad, but at least they are there. One day Jon will be worthy of a name and Grandfather Thunder.

    Daddy Issues:
    Dad didn't want Jon. Even after hearing of the child's existence, dad kept Jon at a distance, rarely visiting his mother, and maybe renewing a Netflix membership in hopes of shutting the kid up. Dad was a thoughtless, careless philanderer. Dad supposedly had a happy marriage, so why would he have women on the side? There were complaints of having to cover up Jon's fucking mess, how he almost ruined everything. Why was dad even angrier than usual? The story goes that his father had an affair with what he thought was Shadow Lord kin was in fact Black Spiral Kin. Somehow someone, somewhere found out. What fantastic blackmail material.
    But father felt obligated to tutor his son in the ways of the Lords. He was determined to not allow his kid fall to the Spiral. The Garou nation could not afford to lose another, despite the cub's unfortunate history. Dad taught as much as he felt his son needed. How to cut someone, how to petition spirits, how to use one's gifts. He taught Cliff's Notes version of Garou hierarchy, rites, and history. Once the bare minimums were passed on, dad handed over the keys to an ancient '87 Dodge Diplomat and sent him to finish off whatever he needed with Shadow Lords of the Thundering Pines sept.

    Jon doesn't understand too much about the concept of Kinfolk, it was obvious his mother was one. Dad didn't know her true lineage, and thus, neither does Jon. Since she was an outsider and working at one of the local chemical plants, it's rumored that she was Black Spiral Kin. After the night of his First Change, mom had disappeared, adding to his father's suspicion. Dad, of course, took the opportunity to belittle his son and tell him of the suspicion. Jon cared deeply for the mother who worked hard and often stayed late to make sure he had a good life. He deeply hopes that she is alive, and when he has more time, he will find her and learn the truth. Even if he learns that she was Spiral Kin, he may find himself torn on protecting her, arguing that she's a good person and needs to be cleansed. He will do anything to clear her name, even if it means telling one of the biggest lies in his life.

    While Jon struggles to ingratiate himself with the new pack, he considers a woman named Gaye McAdams to be as close to a friend as possible. She's his crazy ex-girlfriend from high school. She moved away halfway through senior year, as she proved herself highly intelligent and got to enter college early for a electrical and computer engineering degree at Rowan University. But she's always had a crush on Jon. The problem is, is she's too much of UFO nutter. Aliens are real and the government is covering it up. She spends an inordinate amount of time toying with a HAM radio and a 1960s oscilloscope, when she's not trying to do a pathetic job of flirting. Her computer is almost always on and running SETI algorithms. One week, Gaye had said she was going to visit her mother and asked if Jon would apartment-sit. Naturally, he agreed. One gibbous moon and Jon decided to test his shapeshifting abilities, when Gaye came home early because she forgot a gift she meant to bring her parent. Her response was a dropped jaw, followed by “that... is so fucking awesome!” The platinum-haired nightmare is kinfolk, so he's told by the sept,and that's the limit of his understanding on the matter, and apparently she's unaware of the connection, too. Shit. And you're never supposed to stick your dick in crazy. Well, like the government conspiracies she's trying to uncover, she loves having her own conspiracy to be a part of.
