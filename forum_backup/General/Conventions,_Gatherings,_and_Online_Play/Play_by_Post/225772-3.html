                                                            In terms of rolling dice...generally, I hold to the "only roll dice if there's a significant risk of failure or if failure could make for a dramatic complication" option. Pakari can safely just move tree branches, his whole character concept is based on unusual physical strength. I'm not gonna make him roll for that sorta thing unless failure would be dangerous (maybe he needs to move the branch before some beast eats the person trapped under it) or likely (he's trying to move a damn tree trunk) or interesting (he's trying to impress a pretty spirit with his strength and failing complicates that attempt).

                                                            In terms of rolling for perceptive stuff...I will generally describe the scene and show what's there, but feel free to make perceptive rolls at any time you want, and I'll add additional information and detail to suit. Feel free to roll Investigation or Awareness or Medicine to analyze your surroundings anytime your character is making a particular effort to gather information about them, and I'll give you more info.

                                                            In PBP format, I generally like to avoid asking for dicerolls, because then there's a wait while the diceroll is made and another wait while I say what the result of the roll is. Feel free to make rolls on your own initiative if you think you've got a relevant dicepool (for instance, if Pakari wanted to do a dance to please the local forest spirits and get on their good side, you can just describe his actions and roll your social stat of choice and Performance-though please have a good rationale for the choice of your social stat-and make the roll without asking, and I'll give you the results in my post). Cutting out story-free OOC back-and-forth makes the game more fun and the thread more fun to read. Honestly, if I could just make Perception rolls for you guys and include their results in my posts when stealth or hidden clues or traps comes into play, it would be awesome for that reason...but I want to preserve your ability to spend WP or motes or whatever on those rolls, so they're one of the few times I'll ask for a roll and wait for the PCs to make one before responding.

                                                            In terms of Dicerolling...been using this site to generate dicerolls for another campaign, and I kinda like it. Make an account, make a campaign called The Whitestone Island Campaign, and use the dice roller to roll dice. If you have any technical difficulties, let me know, but it seems to be mostly straightforward, so it shouldn't have much in the way of conflicts.

                                                            Formatting is like so: Make the short description something that clearly indicates what it is within the relevant scene. Make the long description include the math that produced that number of dice (Attribute X+Ability X +Whatever Fancy Mojo You've Got Going X=7), and note any extra successes (and specify their sources). Note them within the long description, not just the post, unless you have a Charm that allows you to add successes post-roll for some reason. Make sure to check the 'Show die detail' box. If you don't, the actual die rolls won't be visible and the roll will be useless. Embed the link (given in the html box on the right after the roll is complete) either in a mechanics spoiler tag (for combat and multiple actions and flurries and so on), or in-text if such detail is unnecessary.
