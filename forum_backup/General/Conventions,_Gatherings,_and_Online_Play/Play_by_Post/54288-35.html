                Hyuga and Jensven enter into a familiar rhythm, assisting each other with nary a word said. It is as if they each learned their craft at the others' side, knowing the other's strengths and shortcomings, and therefore how best to account for them. Hyuga's supernatural crafting is far superior to Jensven's skills, even with the master's tools he has at his disposal, but Jensven's superior Dexterity and strong technique help him apply the skills far better than he could on his own.

                Long story short, you make a good team!

                Working in the pale glow of a slumbering fire elemental, the two Solars work through the night, pounding out the components of four golem shells. You could make more, but you seem to have burned through all of the materiel Maia left for you. Noticing a pair of sleeping pallets in the side of the room (one of which Shadow has already claimed), and some light food on a nearby table, you grab a quick meal before catching a few hours of sleep. Shadow is rather perturbed at having to give up his extravagant sleeping arrangements, however.

                                                        During the night, Hyuga tosses and turns. Nightmares once again come back to haunt his sleep. He was out with Yi, Tirien, and Jensven on the battle field with their weapons aglow and poised for battle. Everything seemed to be going fine. They had made a lot of ground and were felling enemies left and right. It was at that time that Hyuga split off from the group to go investigate and enemy manse. After killing all inhabitants, he ran back to where the others were heading. At first he thought his eyes were deceiving him. Past the hill he ran up, burnt trees stood crippled, bushes burned, and all the ground became desolate. That is when he saw it. Yi lay broken on the ground, Tirien was ripped apart and Jensven was being held in the air like a helpless rag doll. They had come. The jackals had come again, but this time they were great metal beasts of fire. He knew they had evolved and came back to haunt him. A cloaked man slyly moves among the shadow, cackling as he flitted about. He ran as fast as he could toward the tall beasts, but Jensven's neck was broken before he could even get there. Hyuga fell to the ground devastated. He had failed to protect them.... it was all going to happen again. Rage filled him and he ran towards the front line. He started running faster and faster, turning into a great beast himself as he ran, full of rage and unable to think of anything else. He managed to rip off one of the beast's arms as he got there, but his struggling ended up for naught as he was held. Behind him, he could feel the man come float to him and whisper, "Look at what you've become. I guess it is your turn now. Feel the pain that you let them suffer". Despair filled Hyuga as he saw his monsterous form that was almost jackal like... and a sudden clanking noise could be heard from the man's mace. Hyuga knew he was about to be killed and yet was still full of rage...

                                                        Hyuga immediately bolts upright grabbing his sword in an instant and looks around. As he calmed himself down silently, he noticed Jensven and Shadow eating some ham and Maia tinkering with the golems and checking their work. Hyuga puts down his sword quietly and listens to Jensven whom has started talking.

                                                        From sitting up so quickly and the dream, Hyuga's head begins to pound. Rest certainly isn't what he got last night. His eyes were puffy and he was still feeling drained.

                                                        It was all a dream. Calm yourself.

                                                        As Jensven finishes talking to Maia, Hyuga says, "I used essence to bring the shells into existence. Your used just your own two hands. You did quite commendable work there". He will turn to Madame Maia and say, "He was quite bent on helping you. You could tell he was extremely determined. Just that alone made me decide to stay and help. You have him to thank for all this work even being done. I would have gone off by myself to do some research and planning if not for him. Please give him any reward you think they deserve". Hyuga will grab some food and then say, "Thank you for the meal. It is quite appreciated. Now if you don't mind, I am going to go get cleaned up for the day. Please feel free to check anything you need with the shells. I hope they are to your liking".

                                                        With that, Hyuga will bow to Maia and Jensven and go to get cleaned up. The Sun certainly knew he needed it.

                                                            She heads over to the table, sitting down, "Even with Hyuga's anathema sorcery, I expected you to finish one. One. Four will take me three weeks worth of work to finish. I'm not sure what I'd do if you helped me any more. I'd end up with far too much work to do!"

Unwrapping it, it opens to reveal several weapons. A straight sword of fine, clean steel, with hypnotic patterns of black jade on the blade. A lightly curved sword of bronze, that gives off an acrid odor of ionization and crackles with internal lightning.
