    Originally posted by Haberdasher View Post
    rwknoll Since our characters are fairly recently Awakened, do they not have Order status, and with that the rote skills, dot of Occult, and High Speech Merit that they would get with Order status?
    Great question! I forgot to mention this. Assuming you intend your character to join an Order, go ahead and pick your Order and indicate all of the things that normally provides (i.e., free dot of Order Status, rote skills, 6 dots of rotes, free dot of Occult, High Speech merit). However, you will not have access to any of those features in-game until you are initiated into the Order. This will happen early in the game, with the intention of very quickly getting the PCs together, forming a cabal, being presented to the Consilium, and having the opportunity to join the Order of their choice. Those bonuses will become active once you are in the Order.

    Caladriu - Yes, pick Praxes as normal. You can exchange Merit dots for Gnosis per the standard rules. That means you can technically be Gnosis 3, but especially for characters who wanted to have connections with Awakened society before their actual Awakening, it would be harder to pull off while keeping true to your character concept. If someone in that position really wants Gnosis 3, we can talk about it and discuss pros/cons.

            Originally posted by Haberdasher View Post

            Ragnar's Arcana is giving me a little trouble. I think Ragnar, at this point, sees the universe as made from the interaction of seven of the Arcana: woven from Space and Time, given movement and shape by Forces and Matters, and the drama of life being the cycle of Life and Death; and all six ruled over by the laws and patterns governed by Fate. Not sure seven Arcana is feasible, but I do think it ties into the Renaissance man mentality that Ragnar has: try to know at least one thing about every subject, and try to know everything about at least one subject.

            Besides, when I ask myself, "what do I see Ragnar doing as an acknowledged Master in a consilium?", he's the quiet type who can basically corral people into doing what he wants them to do, the mentor type who never seems to take direct action, but is actually working feverishly behind the scenes, and if he ever does act directly, he knows a few ways to really mess your day up.
            I mean, I can see the dilemma. A lot of the Arcana are really neat and provide fascinating abilities and attainments. But don't forget the rules on distribution of dots among starting Arcana (e.g., most spread out allowed could be 2, 1, 1, 1, 1) and the fact that your Path has very important impacts on how Ragnar would understand magic. He can still be a "jack of all trades" if he desires, but his true understanding of magic is always going to be through the lens of his Acanthus Path. There isn't a "universal" Path. If you're stuck, try reading through the Acanthus Path description again and think how the details there might relate to Ragnar's personal philosophy and Obsessions.
