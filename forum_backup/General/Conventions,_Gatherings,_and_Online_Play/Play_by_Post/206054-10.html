                                    RE: towns of origin
                                    Aye, I know it's been established that they're from all over the States before. It was mainly floating the idea of retconning them all to one spot for the basic reason Nyrufa said. Exploring elements of the lives from which they were taken is something I know we're all interested in seeing. It's easier to do if they're all in one spot with all they used to know.

                                    Though of course that's far from the only way to explore things. Some distance could add something, and "commuting" via the Hedge is an option, albeit one that's never completely safe. Another thought could be each of them emerging at a different time (and place), and then ultimately converging somewhere and somewhen, possibly handled by way of timeskip or an accelerated understanding of time, with flashbacks for important stuff that's passed up. I'm open to whatever we do, just so long as the characters have a chance to see and react to whatever may have happened in their absences.

                                    @Nyrufa: Definite boons to be had in none of the characters looking at each other as food. They clearly don't need help creating drama for themselves, anyway. Monkey would also be a flexible sign, and give some contrast to the present group's Old McDonald Singalong. Raw ape strength as you've suggested is a fine option, though my first guess was a greater focus on the nimble, dextrous, flexible traits; the present team could do with some of that.

                                    @Evilmarmelade: Aww, I'm sorry to hear things haven't been going so hot for you lately. Still, I'm glad we've helped, even if just a little. Here's hoping things take a turn for the better, and soon. If you think it might help to vent sometime, just pipe up.
