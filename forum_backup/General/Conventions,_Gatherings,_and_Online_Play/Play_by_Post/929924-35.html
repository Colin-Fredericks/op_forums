                        You find yourself, quite literally, in a completely different world.

The creek here is a wide winding river, and you see large fish swimming under its crystal clear surface. Except they aren't true fish; they are spirits shaped like fish, glowing with their own light as they propel themselves through the water, some leaping into the air and splashing back into the river.

                        And then you realize that the entire forest is teeming with spirits. The trees are alive, beating branches against each other, almost like they're playing. Deer, squirrel, and rabbit spirits run between the trees. You hear a screech as a hawk spirit dives from a tree branch and tears its talons into a rabbit; the smaller spirit trembles, fades to mist, and then vanishes as the hawk feasts on its Essence. There are easily hundreds of spirits within your view at a single glance.

                        Raven moves from your side, and the spirits move around her, giving her a wide berth, almost as if out of respect. You realize, however, that many of the spirits around you are watching you with a hostile look. The smallest spirits, of grass and squirrels and beetles, scamper away as you look at them. Some of the larger spirits seem to bristle at you, staring you down.

                        "Mages are foreign to the Shadow World," Raven explains. "When you gain more mastery of the Spirit Arcanum, they will start to see you as one of their own and treat you accordingly per their natural hierarchy. Until then, it is up to you to assert your will and your dominance with them."

                        Raven's familiar Nimbus flares and this time, she does something you've never seen before: She somehow seems to cast two spells at once, intertwining them together. As she chants in High Speech, a blanket of Spirit magic settles upon her and around her. The spirits that had been watching her suddenly seem to ignore her, as if she wasn't standing there at all. "With these creatures, Spirit magic is your sword, shield, and cloak. Wield it wisely."

                        Raven steps back and a devilish grin crosses her face. She whistles, and her Nimbus flares again. Moments later, a spirit shaped like a large wolf comes springing from between the looming trees. Other spirits near it scatter at its approach, but the wolf spirit ignores them. It runs up near you and stops, sniffing the air.

                        "Attack," Raven commands. The wolf looks at you, bares its teeth, and growls.

She quickly returns with two steaming cups of tea on a serving tray, and she offers you and Dante a porcelain cup. Dante smiles and accepts it, setting the briefcase down and taking a seat in an open chair, and he nods to you to do the same.
