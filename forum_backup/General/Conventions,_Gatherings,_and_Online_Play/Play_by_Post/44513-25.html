            I would like to know about the academy's main focus for the arts and its main production of goods.
            -Academy of Sorcerous Wisdom-

            The Academy of Sorcerous Wisdom lies in a Wood-Aspected Manse in the northern section of Novalesk city. This proud institution has thirteen paths of study; eleven corresponding to each of the Arts of Thaumaturgy, as well as one each for Artificery and Sorcery. The Alchemy and Geomancy programs are the largest, with Enchantment and Exorcism programs trailing at a modest distance (though the Exorcist program's numbers are artificially inflated by the cross-pollination with the Elementalist, Demonology, Spiritism, and Necromancy programs). Following those programs are the cluster of Elementalist, Weather-Working and Husbandry, which are in-turn followed by the Demonology, Spiritism, Artificery programs. The Sorcery program would be the smallest if not for the incredibly tiny Astrology and Necromancy programs (calling them programs is considered something of an in-joke by a majority of the faculty). Thaumaturgy Programs are all designed to teach to the Adept degree, with the more specialized training condensed in a formal tutelage to a Master. Many thaumaturges pursue multiple paths simultaneously or concurrently.

            The Sorcery program is designed to initiate the student into Emerald Circle Sorcery only. It assumes that one who is capable of achieving the level of enlightenment and occult knowledge required to learn sorcery can learn Spells on their own without being spoon fed by a teacher. That said, the openness of the library to such students and the examples given by professors give enough information that most students don't leave the Academy without knowing a spell or two.

            The Artificery program is officially meant only for graduates of the Geomancy and Enchantment Programs. Though promising candidates will not be turned away, the curriculum assumes a certain level of occult knowledge and thaumaturgical expertise. Graduates of this program generally obtain at least the Initiate degree in the non-qualifying art, as well as a smattering of Elementalist rituals.

            The primary production of goods for the Academy are Alchemical solutions, primarily those with medicinal properties, such as the Blood-Stanching Compress, or Draught of Blessed Respite. Such items keep well and fetch a tidy sum in the metropolises in the Confederation of Rivers. Lookshy for one is constantly looking for low-price medicinal compounds, and often attempts to negotiate trade contracts for the goods the Academy produces (though their close relationship with Ordon prevents any such agreement from occurring). A significant percentage of the products produced are purchased by the infirmaries and hospices in Novalesk, who hoard them for emergency cases. This is only the most common grouping of products; if an Alchemical formulae exists, chances are you can find it at the Academy.

            Other products produced are a number of magically treated tools and weapons, designed to be the next-best-thing to artifacts (such as sword blades that won't break and plows that stay good for decades). Many of these goods are sold at profit to local merchants, who distribute them among the nation; the rest are sold to the resident Guild factors.

            The Artificery also produces a small number of artifacts (primarily weapons) each year. They repair man more, however, as Scavenger Lord is a respectable profession among Novalesk's elite.

                                            -The Gilded Triangle-

                                            Of the many Anathema Cults that have sprung up in Theros over the centuries, none are more tenacious than the Gilded Triangle. The Black Cowl may boast a longer history, but it had the advantage of basing its operations outside of Theros' borders.

                                            The Therian authorities first became aware of the Gilded Triangle in RY 658. It had managed to subvert a number of peasants through mild subversion of immaculate dogma. These deviations seemed inconsequential to the average layman, but when examined by the proper authorities, they collectively constituted a major heresy in Immaculate canon. Though the heresy was quickly stamped down, a two decades later, a different heresy of similar structure, but differing dogma appeared. The two incidents seemed unconnected, until a third incident of similar pattern appeared thirteen years later. The pattern continued to repeat itself, reappearing between ten and twenty years after the previous purge. The last resurgence was twelve years ago.

                                            Authorities have been baffled as to the root of these incidents. If the dogma had been the same thing resurging later, they would simply concluded that the purges where incomplete. However, each heresy was similar in structure, but differing in details, suggesting that some intelligence is controlling them from the shadows.
