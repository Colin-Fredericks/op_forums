                            The navigator pulls out four rolls of parchment from the scroll cases inside the cabinet and lays them out on the chart table.The four maps bracket a region south-southeast of Wavecrest, showing some of the islands along the sides of Sharukan's Triangle. At the far southern point of the triangle is Sharukan's Pillar, the column of rock that gives the region its name and the supposed lair of a sea god. The westernmost point is Songcatcher Island, marked on the chart with warnings about fae, while a small cluster of islands called the Wheua Islands makes the northernmost point of the triangle and the chart has markings indicating unfriendly natives. Other marking along the edges of the triangle mention pirate sightings, dangerous reefs, and sea monsters. The four charts, however, don't cover the triangles interior, an obvious lacuna in the navigational information.
