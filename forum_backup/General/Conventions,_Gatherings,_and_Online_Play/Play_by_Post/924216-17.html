            Flinty - Thanks for the update, no worries.

            I finally have some down time this weekend and will be working a lot on my to-do list to get the game up and running, which includes making my own character, reviewing Legacy conversions, and reviewing character sheets.

            I had a few questions for you all. Just wanted to really make sure you are ok with me running my own character. My plan is to treat my PC using the same rules as all other characters, and I will be extra mindful of not meta-gaming. I just have spent over a year trying to find a Mage game to join as a player, and it hasn't worked out, so I would like to have a chance to play.

            I was thinking about taking the Retainer merit to have my pet dog, who would represent an important NPC serving my character. I'm thinking a 2- or 3-dot merit would be right to reflect a professionally trained military working dog. Does that sound about right? Looking to use the "trained guard dog" stats on p.121 of the CoD core book. I would like feedback on whether that seems fair.

            As far as Obsessions go, I want to remind people that these should essentially be long-term magic-related aspirations. You earn Arcane Beats when you make significant progress towards specific goals. So "Learn how and why the Loci are forming in Denver" is acceptable, but "Spirits" is too vague and "Meet a rank 3 Spirit" is more of a short-term aspiration rather than an Obsession.

            Finally, I think I would like to make a few changes to character creation. We are still starting with 5 bonus XP and a cap of Gnosis 2, but I'm giving everyone Gnosis 2 for free. So for most of you, this means you'll get more Merit dots back at character creation. Also, as far as starting equipment goes, you can start with any number of items with Availability equal to your Resources Merit dots, and one or two items with an item rating one dot higher than your Resources. I like using Merits to flesh out characters and give more story hooks for NPCs, to reward players who truly invest in their relationships with NPCs using merits like Mentor or Allies, and to add more background nuance and customization to players.

            Let me know if anyone has any problems with these details.

                                        Age: 32

                                        Concept: U.S. Army Veteran on a journey of personal and spiritual discovery

                                        Shadow Name: Bruiser
                                        John carried a reputation in his U.S. Army unit for being hot-headed but incredibly loyal. He always had his friend’s back, and he never gave up or ran away from a fight. After his injury, John battled with pain and withdrew from the world around him, but his Awakening filled him with a new sense of awe and determination. His Shadow Name is a warning to any who might harm him or his allies: You swing hard, I’ll swing harder.

                                        Pre-Awakening:
                                        John was born in the Chicago suburbs, but he didn't live there for long. Because his father actively served in the U.S. Air Force and had several permanent changes of station, their family was relocated multiple times; John remembers living in Illinois, Ohio, Colorado, and even Alaska. John struggled with these transitions, and their moves always caused tension between John's parents, ultimately leading to divorce. He and his mother moved back to Chicago when he was in middle school, and they stayed there until he graduated.

                                        John remained in close contact with his father and admired his dedication to the military, and he joined the Army to help with college tuition. Because he enjoyed working with animals, he decided to pursue a military career as a dog handler. John was deployed twice to Iraq, where his partner Zeus, a smart labrador retriever trained to identify explosives, identified several IEDs before they were activated and saved the lives of dozens of soldiers, including John.

                                        A few months before his second tour was scheduled to end, however, John was injured by an IED and had to be medically evaucated. He suffered a traumatic brain injury and multiple fractures in his arms, neck, and shoulder, requiring extensive surgery and rehabilitation and forcing him into medical retirement from the Army. Zeus similarly became more agitated and skittish after this incident and was retired from service, and John was able to adopt his partner, but he didn't know how to cope with the chronic back and shoulder pain that threatened to envelop his life. John quickly reached a point when he was heavily prescribed narcotics but couldn't find lasting relief.

                                        The Awakening:
                                        One day, John's father, who had ultimately settled in the Denver area near the local Air Force base, contacted him and told him that he heard from another airman about a "spiritual healer" who might be able to help John recover. John was immediately skeptical, but every other medical treatment he had tried -- surgery, medications, physical therapy -- hadn't worked. Out of desperation and fighting despair, he flew out to Denver to meet this holistic healer, but something about the rural area and the rugged wilderness began to evoke a change in him that he didn't fully understand. John met with this healer, who claimed that he was injured not just physically but spiritually, and he agreed to a ritualistic "cleansing" that supposedly would help him find "inner peace."

                                        What happened next for John in that private, incense-filled room was his first true spiritual experience, an Astral journey to the Primal Wild. John's eyes were opened by the Moon to both the reflection of Life and the Shadow it casts upon the world. No longer bound by worldly knowledge, arguing with medical professionals about the "mind-body link" rather than the true duality of Life and Spirit, John broke the chains forged around his body and shattered them, and he uttered a Primal roar that formed rune-shaped cracks in the dirt where he stood.

                                        The "holistic healer", who was actually just a guy looking for an easy scam target and equipped with some nice incense and cheap weed, was stunned when John emerged from the cramped shack with a radically different take on life. And at first, John did still experience pain; his Awakening hadn't changed that. But once he had experienced and survived the Ecstatic rush of the Primal Wild, pain was just another sensation that proved he was alive.

                                        Post-Awakening:
                                        Unknown to the "spiritual healer" or John, a mage had been closely following and investigating the patients of the "spiritual healer" looking for telltale signs of Awakening, and he quickly found John and introduced him into the Dever Consilium. With his new awarenes of Supernal magic and appreciation for the greater context of his existence, John embarked on a journey of personal transformation and development. He found himself drawn to the doctrine of the Adamantine Arrow, sought training and entrance, and successfully passed his training and testing requirements to earn his way into the Order. Taking the Shadow Name of "Bruiser", he continued with his journal of soul-searching and was initiated into the Legacy of the Perfected Adepts. As a Monk, Bruiser dedicates his waking hours to honing his physical and magical abilities, protecting his cabal and Consilium from danger, and studying the unique oddities of the Shadow in the Denver region.


                                        Experience
                                        EXPERIENCE:
                                        Beats: 0/5, Experience: 0
                                        Arcane Beats: 0/5, Arcane Experience: 0

                                        Experience Expenditure:
                                        Starting: Life 3 (4 XP), Legacy Attainment (1 XP)

                                        Perfected Adept Legacy
                                        DOCTRINE:
                                        Prerequisites: Gnosis 2, Life 2, Athletics 2, and one of the following additional Skills at 2 dots or higher: Medicine, Brawl, or Survival.

                                        Initiation: The perspective adept must display his dedication to perfecting his body and must pass a rigorous fitness test without the use of magic. He must also display his knowledge of at least one fighting style.

                                        MAGIC:
                                        Ruling Arcanum: Life
                                        Optional Arcanum: Prime

                                        Yantras: The weapon of the adepts primary fighting style, A display of martial prowess +1, Analyzing the target’s chi flow +1.

                                        Suggested Oblations: Rigorous martial arts practice, including sparring with other Perfected Adepts; feats of endurance, such as long-distance running, holding weights for long periods and exposure to the elements; exhibiting humility, such as serving another person for an hour by seeing to all his needs.


                                        ATTAINMENTS:

                                        First Attainment: Perfected Senses
                                        Prerequisites: Initiation

                                        As the “Heightened Senses” spell, this Attainment add the Perfected Adept’s Life dots to his perception rolls. Each activation of this attainment takes an instant action and remains in effect for as long as the mage sustains concentrations on the Attainment.

                                        Optional Arcanum: Prime 1
                                        As “Supernal Vision”, but can only be used to examine a living person. Requires an instant activation to use.


                                        Second Attainment: Destroy Foreign Bodies
                                        Prerequisites: Life 2, Athletics 3

                                        As the “Purge Illness” spell. Take an instant action to subtract the Perfected Adept’s dots in Life Arcanum from the severity of any disease in his body. If Potency is less than the illness’ rating, reduce it by the difference, and if greater, the spell eliminates the illness from the subject entirely.

                                        Optional Arcanum: Prime 2
                                        As the “Wards and Signs” spell. When the subject is the target of a spell, that spell is Withstood with the Potency of this attainment. Each activation of this attainment requires one scene.


                                        Third Attainment: Body Temple
                                        Prerequisites: Life 3, Athletics 3. In addition, he must either have 3 dots in the second Skill used to meet the requirements of the Legacy, or 2 dots in a second Skill from the list of possible requirements.

                                        As the “Body Control” spell, this Attainment grants bonuses based on the Perfected Adept’s dots in Life, including slower breathing and heartbeat, reduced metabolism, faster reflexes, and eliminating body odors. The subject’s bashing wounds also heal faster, and he gains 1/0 armor for the duration of the Attainment effect. Activating this Attainment requires one scene.

                                        Optional Arcanum: Prime 3
                                        As “Dispel Magic”, but can only affect spells directly targeting this subject and requires at least one dot in each Arcanum involved in the spell. The Attainment requires an instant action to activate and has a lasting effect.


                                        Fourth Attainment: Chi Force
                                        Prerequisites: Life 4, Athletics 4, and the other Skill requirements of the previous Attainment.

                                        As the “Knit” spell, the subject can channel his chi into his wounds to rapidly restore damage to the body, including lethal damage. The Perfected Adept may only use this Attainment on his own wounds. Each activation requires an instant action and heals a number of wounds equal to his Life dots.

                                        Optional Arcanum: Prime 4
                                        As the “Channel Mana” spell. The Perfected Adept can manipulate the body’s chi force to shift or relocate mana between the subject and any other living person he touches, equal to the Potency of the Attainment. He can ignore his Gnosis-derived mana per turn limit. Each activation requires an instant action.


                                        Fifth Attainment: Perfect Body
                                        Prerequisites: Life 5, Athletics 4. In addition, he must either have 4 dots in the second Skill used to meet the requirements of the Legacy; 3 dots in a second Skill from the list of possible requirements; or 2 dots in a third Skill from the list of possible requirements.

                                        As the “Honing the Form” spell, this Attainment allows the Perfected Adept to improve any combination of his physical Attributes. Gain his Life dots in Potency and assign them between the Perfected Adept’s physical Attributes as extra dots. The affected Attributes cannot be raised above
                                        the subject’s maximum Attribute dots (5 for normal human beings). Each activation requires one scene.

                                        Optional Arcanum: Prime 5
                                        As the “Supernal Dispellation” spell, this attainment allows the Perfected Adept to shrug off the effects of any undesirable spell that specifically targets him or includes him as a subject, with no need to possess the specific arcana involved in the spell. Each activation takes an instant action and has an advanced duration.
