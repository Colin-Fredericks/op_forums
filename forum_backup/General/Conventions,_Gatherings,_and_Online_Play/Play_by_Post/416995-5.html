                            The Daybreak stops his thoughts in mid-stream. Of course there would be something like that here. The only question is what exactly is it and how do we deal with it? He walks slowly, deliberately. His steps a mirror to his thoughts. The Abyssal searches through the memories and histories he has cataloged in his brain for any information that might be of use to his current inquiry.

                            He knows that his source of power is even more precious here than in the underworld, but he pours everything he can into the effort of bringing forth the knowledge he knows he contains in his mind. In his mind he can see musty old tomes that open, pages that fly by in a flash. Yet he can still read them that fast, as it is his sharp mind doing the reading without having to rely on his eyes. He is recalling that which he has already committed to memory. So the tomes will open, flash their contents to him, and close to make way for others as he dismisses them as being not pertinent to his current quest for information.

                            He stops walking only when he feels he has come to the end of the line with his thought process. Whether that is a conclusion that will help or not, time will tell. Prodigal looks up to find that he has stopped in front of a tavern. Feeling like he could use a seat and a decent drink, although nothing the tavernkeep would be willing to serve would fully sate the thirst he developed after using his power. He vowed to keep himself in check, this time.

                            I will be cranking ten (10) personal motes into Occult Essence Overwhelming in order to get ten (10) more dice on the Intelligence + Occult roll that I will be doing in an attempt to see if I can gain any information on what kind of undead creature would be in the area or what specific legendary creature could be in the area. I am going to assume a one (1) die stunt.

                            So this gives me Intelligence (5) + Occult (5) + Excellency (10) + Stunt (1) = Twenty one (21) dice = Eight Successes You will have to forgive me not rolling the stunt die separately. I still have to get used to this other dice roller. Should my stunt be denied we can drop the successes down to six. Also, I have taken the liberty of going to and entering the tavern where the others are as I figured that it would be better to have the group together. Since I have used ten (10) personal essence, this leaves me with four (4) personal in my pool. Should I be granted the stunt, I will add the motes to that pool to bring it up to six (6) motes.

                                                (Prodigal)

                                                Your thoguht processes whirr in your head as you seek out your quarry. As the crowd jostles around you, and you see Zadro slink into a nearby teahouse, you soldier on. Clearly, this town's history of death and suffering would be enough to cause such a reaction. After all, it probably has some link to the Underworld, now, right?

                                                Or maybe it's those blokes over there.

                                                Bedecked in rime-frosted furs and with turbans made of seal leather, five newcomers trudge into the plaza out of the front gate. Steam wafts off them as the heat from the Labyrinth melts the ice off their cloaks. One drops their scarves to reveal a death-grin of white teeth and nary an inch of flesh in sight. The fellow lets out a frozen breath, and continues on with his fellows. They all exude an aura of unpleasantness that jangles your nerves unpleasantly, but also feels pleasant to your starved chakras.

                                                Then, a corpse lands, hitting a wall of a public house nearby. Today is surely a time of wonders.

                                                What will you do?
