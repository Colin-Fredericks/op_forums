    Phoenix
    With a moment of peace to clear your mind, you realize that pulling out your phone and checking GPS will make it easier for you to navigate back to a familiar location. Within a few minutes, you're back in an area you know, and you start your search again for Ragnar or Moonstone.

    You still don't have any concrete evidence that you're in the right area, but you guess that it can't hurt to try a few random searches. And there had been that brief glimmer of recognition in the beggar's eyes, so maybe he had seen one of your cabalmates here recently.

    You instinctually pick a few random directions, and pay more attention to your GPS to make sure you don't get lost. And then after a few minutes, whether by complete luck or some divine intervention, you feel a pulse of energy on your Periphery and hear a familiar voice somewhere in the near vicinity of the alley you're standing in: "Fine, let's do this."


    You resolve Lost. I think that's your first mundane Condition resolved in this scene, so take a Beat.

    I'll say that if you spend your instant action moving and make a successful Wits + Composure perception roll, you can join in the scene on the next round. Alternatively, you can spend an instant action to roll Dexterity + Stealth and try and sneak up on the others, which means you won't be able to act for 2 rounds. Or, you can try any other instant action, and then join in 2 rounds.

    Regardless, roll initiative.


    Lynx
    You assert your will as powerfully as you can, trying to fix your Imago into place with brute force to compel the wolf spirit to obey you. But Raven has far more experience with magic, and on this occasion, you falter.

    The wolf spirit bares its teeth, snarls, and leaps towards you. In the Fallen World, the spirits you've seen appear ephemeral, airy, lacking in substance. While this wolf is still obviously a spirit, there's definitely nothing ephemeral about it here. His claws rip into your skin.


    Attack: 4d10 = 2 successes after accounting for Defense 4. This roll will be modified if you choose to activate your Mage Armor (Life Armor is probably the most effective in this particular scenario, as it both boosts your Defense and offers Armor).

            Moonstone
            You focus your will, take a breath to anchor your thoughts, and channel a thread of mana into the Cloud Gate. At the same time, you create a mental representation of Chicago, drawing lines between your current location and where you sensed Ragnar a moment ago.

            As soon as your thread of mana touches the sculpture, you feel a powerful force of raw energy surround you. To your Space Sight, the image of the Chicago skyline reflected in the sculpture suddenly zooms out and then zooms in, focusing on an alley deep in the city.

            You feel yourself yanked forward by an invisible force as the mana you channeled into the Cloud Gate connects you with the location. There's a dizzying, rushing sensation of movement. A cold wind whips around you.

            And suddenly, you're not in Millennium Park anymore.

            The good news: You can see Ragnar.

            The bad news: You can see Ragnar way down below you. You're standing on the railing to a fourth-story balcony of an old apartment complex. Your foot begins to slip.

            You're 4 stories up from the ground, or 12 meters in game terms. You may make a reflexive Wits + Athletics roll to keep yourself from falling. Per the rules in the CoD core book, if you fall from this height you will take 12 bashing damage, which you can alleviate with successes on a Dexterity + Athletics roll on a 1:1 basis (or find a way to work around with magic).


            Ragnar
            You hear a faint ring of bells and see wisps of energy on your Periphery, coming from above you. When you look up, you see Moonstone there, teetering on the edge of a balcony railing four stories above you. The hostile mage in front of you whips his around, but with his mundane vision blinded, he can't see what's there. His expression darkens with anger.

            I don't think I've had you roll Initiative yet, so go ahead and do it now.
