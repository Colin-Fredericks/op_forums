        A brief announcement. So, yeah, I was planning on an update Monday, but then I was unconscious from 7PM and 6AM due to being suddenly, horrendously, sick. Made a Torches & Pitchforks roll, and no successes. Good job guys, no angry mob!

        ArchonAres so I thing I noticed from your most recent IC post, but you don't have to repay the Pyros cost if you want to reuse a Distillation in a scene unless otherwise marked. If the Alembic is charged, the Distillation is available for the entire scene. This can make Prometheans very cost-efficient, and also horrendous powerful, because there are few other splats that can go around hurling lightning willy nilly with no break or need to recharge, and in a modern setting, access to electricity means access to Pyros if you're willing to risk a little Disquiet.

        Caladriu normally, noting that this becomes a Wasteland would be important, but it was in the middle of a Firestorm, so that burns away whatever Wasteland tried to latch in. More importantly, Earth has satisfied the Vigilante Role, and earns the Milestone. She has mastered the Role, and as such, will no longer inflict Disquiet until she changes Roles... but if she gets stuck in the mindset of the Vigilante, that's all she'll ever be, and using any Pyros, trying to break the mould, will immediately cause a Tainted Wasteland.

                                                Arcanist I'm kinda confused about Role completion and Milestones. I had been under the impression that the Pilgrimage is tagged to specific individual Roles, but as I'm reading the book more closely now, it keeps emphasizing that the ST chooses Refinements on the Pilgrimage. Here's what tripped me up:

                                                Originally posted by p.184
                                                Completing a Role is never a minor milestone. If the related
                                                Refinement is on the character’s Pilgrimage, it is a superlative
                                                milestone; otherwise, it’s a major milestone.
                                                Originally posted by p.299
                                                Completing a Role that’s associated with one of the Refinements
                                                on his Pilgrimage is always a superlative milestone. Completing a
                                                Role associated with any other Refinement still grants the Created
                                                a major milestone, and may help him in other ways — such as
                                                preparing him to create an Athanor. A Promethean always knows
                                                when he’s completed a Role that’s on his Pilgrimage — the rush of
                                                Vitriol is greater than it would otherwise be. While he probably
                                                won’t know if a Role is on his Pilgrimage before he completes it,
                                                after the fact he’s in no doubt.
                                                Just from a mechanics perspective, do I have this right?
                                                • The Storyteller chooses several Refinements for each character's Pilgrimage. They also choose 10 individual Roles within the Refinements. Whenever a character completes any Role within a Pilgrimage Refinement, not just a chosen one, it's a superlative milestone. When they complete any Role not within a Pilgrimage Refinement, it's a major milestone. The significance of chosen Roles is that they must be completed to enable the New Dawn.

                                                Or is it more like:
                                                • ST chooses Refinements and 10 Roles. Only the chosen Roles are superlative milestones. If the character completes a non-chosen Role in a Pilgrimage Refinement, it's still a major milestone.

                                                I'm asking because the first interpretation would mean Earth just hit a superlative, not major milestone, since Stannum is presumably on her Pilgrimage.

                                                Also, looking at the universal milestones and failure states, she's about to fail Separatio by staying in a completed Role. One step forward, one step back.
