        Piero:

        An icy cold pierces through your skin where the blood is making contact. Something shoots up your veins, your muscles, your nerves — it is absolutely piercing, maddeningly chilly. Your head swims for a minute, the colours in your vision greying as the girl's voice hisses in your head.

        "Don't make a move. Don't even make a sound. You're going to listen to me now, and I'll be damned if I'm dragged back into the Pit without taking you with me, so don't even try it."

        (Roll Resolve+Composure to resist, Piero!)
        Eulalia:

        Something here is very, very wrong. You hear chattering whispers all around you, warning you of threats and serpents and stranger things. You barely make out the voices before you are gone again, but you definitely hear the threat the woman makes...even though her lips are not moving. She stares daggers at Piero, gripping her fingers into his skin like a cat into a prey animal's. You feel a tugging at the inside of your brain, and just a hint of fog as the woman's voice echoes through yours, but it's more like the sound is being played back. There is some sort of attachment to it, like an umbilical cord from an unseen womb.

        (Roll Composure+Occult to focus on the sound; it feels like it's "attached" to something, so you can use this roll to try and probe it)

        Vincent:

        The cops swarm the car like ants. Your driver is grabbed, tackled and cuffed, yelping as his shoulders are nearly dislocated with the force. The officers are hopping the car now, trying to get to you as on-lookers yell and start backing away in panic. Stop cars screech to a halt as people run across the road, and in this chaos, there are many chances to escape. It's up to you if you take them, however; you could always confront the officers. Feel free to do your own thing, as well.

        (Roll Dex+Stealth if you want to hide)
