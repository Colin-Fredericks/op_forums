    [IC] (Hunter: the Vigil 2e Playtest) The Lucifuge's Doubting Souls

    11-03-2017, 12:40 AM
    Scene 1: A Murder Most Foul

    Miskatonic Univeristy, Arkham, Massachusetts
    1693

    One Hour Ago

    Harvard may be the most prestigious university in the colonies, but Miskatonic University in Arkham is the premier destination for occult researchers. Relatively insulated from the Witch Trials of Salem to the south, this makes it the perfect cover for the Lucifuge. That doesn’t mean you can throw your endowments around as you wish, though. You are the literal child of the Devil. You may not have made a pact with Satan, but that’s distinction that many a witch hunter or judge would not make.

    Right now you are all crammed into the office of Khalil Aziz on the wrong side of midnight. The candles are burning low, but Dean Jaeger summoned you. When the head of the Lucifuge in Arkham calls, you answer, especially since he’s been hinting for a while now you are nearing the point of being ready to unlock new powers to bring Satan’s wayward children to justice. Joining your cell is Brian Bartholomew. Brian is a renowned, but he’s losing touch with humanity, having lost his best friend to a famine demon some months ago. Wracked by survivor’s guilt, Brian’s bonfire waiting to happen, but he helped you track down a legitimate witch in Boston last month, so you’re happy to have him with you.

    “Children of Baphomet,” intones the Dean. “These are dark times. The colonies are awash in the spawn of Iblis and now blood has been shed on our very door. Murder, my sons and daughters, murder has besmirched our town, and now the library is awash in blood. Michael, your fellow constables will surely proclaim this the work of a deranged killer, but my heart tells me that the supernatural is at work! The four of you must get to the bottom of this before the killer strikes again. We will not have the flame of our Vigil turn into the wildfire that rages in Salem!”

    *************************

    Present

    The Dean wasn’t over exaggerating. The research room where the murders took place is a bloody mess. Your lamp reveals limbs and fingers scattered about the room and bloody smear of a body crushed under a life-sized marble statue of King William III.
    As you search the room, the flickering light falls upon a sigil painted on the wall in blood. You hope it's just the shadows that are making it dance in the gloom, as looking at it makes your stomach turn over.




    Below it, a question is written, again in blood.

    HAVE YE SEEN THE YELLOW SIGN?

      One Hour Ago

      Steph leans against a bookcase, dressed like one of King William's soldiers on leave, brow furrowed and playing with a dagger. About the only books she likes are ledgers.

      "Anything you say," she monotones. "Do we know the victim's name?"

      Murder is a fact of life, but she's not so far gone that a killing at a university library doesn't register as wrong. Especially when the supernatural is (supposedly) involved.

      Present

      Her lip curls as they survey the research room. "Looks like a butcher shop."

      She looks at the sigil, but her eyes wash over it as a defense mechanism. It looks familiar. That's not a thought she wants to linger on.

      Being crushed by a marble statue doesn't usually cut your fingers off. She steels herself and studies the scattered body parts closely. What exactly happened to the deceased?

      Wits+Investigation to study the victim's body, 4 dice: 1 success

                              One Hour Ago
                              Cassidy sat in silence, nervously looking, her arms and legs are crossed. She was annoyed from the Dean's over poetic language, disliked Stephanie's attitude toward the instance, and hated Michael's self righteousness- who was he to tell God what were his plans? She never truly trusted her cellmates, and they they didn't really trusted her "lack of motivation in the war against the devil". And worse of all- she didn't trusted herself, for when Michael has verified that the act was done through a supernatural act, her blood stirred. Suddenly, she had a strong urge to see the demon, to see the spawn of hell which came and brought a rain of blood upon earth. Was that her inner devil? Was that her own, tainted soul? She didn't knew- and so, she simply set there in silence.

                              Present
                              Cassidy looked at the murder scene. She sniffed the air, filling her lungs with the heavy scent of blood. Some part of her mind felt truly sorry for the death of the nameless man before her. Another has calmly wondered if the murderer used the statue as a weapon in order to give a message, or was it simply because it was the most available tool to do the job. And deep down, she felt.. excited. A coppery taste filled her mouth, her fingers trembled with excitement. She stood before the sign, seemingly examining the image- but in truth, she simply tried to think what to do. Unlike her friends, who had to rely on actually searching the room for evidence, Hell has gifted her with a number of ways to see its taint upon the world- each having its own advantages.. and disadvantages. Which path should she take? Should she even turn to Hell's help at such time? She wanted her sister to be with her so much right now..

                              She took a breath, and scent of blood calmed her right away. She had a job to do- and more important than that, she knew what she wanted to do. She wanted to see the demon, lifting the marble statue from the ground with infernal power. She wanted to see the scenery of hell reaching out to the world, raining blood and brimstone upon the world. She wanted to paint a picture.

                              Reaching to her equipment, she drew out a pencil and the notebook she used to practice her sketching skills. She stood near the statue, as if she was drawing the crime scene- but she didn't. Instead, she took a big breath of bloody air, closed her eyes, and invited Hell to her mind. It was the time to draw the demon of blood and death.

                              activating Infernal Visions- Wits + Composure, 7d10o10h8. 2 Successes.

                                                      Cassidy had to hold herself from rolling her eyes. Michael kept talking about God and His plans as if he was God's personal prophet. It must be nice, she thought to herself, to be able to find such comfort in faith- even though you are damned.

                                                      Present
                                                      The images flowed through her, like a river of flames which spilled to the page. The pencil danced beneath her fingers, moving like a spider and turning the visions the Hell has revealed to her into a solid image. While she was drawing, she started mumbling to herself, her eyes see the horrifying scene coming into life on the blank page. "Not the Son of Beast" she whispered "But the Daughter of Eve. She came upon the Son of God. Stab, stab, stab- blood flows, the King falls, a sign in drawn upon the wall. She has turned away from light" she looked at Michael with hazed eyes "No more confessions he shall speak tonight"

                                                      She felt terrible. Not only because of the visions- she was used to the touch that Hell had upon her soul. The memory of her mocking thought about Michael's faith has filled her with guilt, as she looked at him kneeling before the priest's crushed body. While she did felt sorrow and dread, a calmer, colder part of her mind started to analyze the information, reaching into two conclusions. First, Gabby was many things- but she was no demon. The acts of pulling fingers out of place or throwing marble statues was not something she should have been able to do. That may mean that Gabby was possessed- or worse, has turned into a monster. However, it doesn't reveals why she has committed the act- sure, demons were never big supporters of holy men, but it was clear that the demon tries to pass a message. Why?

                                                      The second thing she understood was that the information was, in fact, a pile of gunpowder waiting to explode. Gabby would probably deny her lover being the murderer, and Brian would also try to protect his employer. After all, even if one were to disregard emotional attachments, if Gabby was possessed it would immediately make them into the suspects in the act. Michael, however, was an whole other problem. He has just swore an oath to avenge his priest- and in such state, he may not make the difference between the demon and the mortal, and the last thing they needed at a time like that was an internal strife.

                                                      Oh, Melissa, where are you? she thought to herself. She shook her head, closed her notebook with trembling fingers and returned it to its place. There was only one path open before her- reaching to the only neutral person in the room and hope he would see beyond her low place in the conspiracy. She walked to Khalil, softly touched his arm and whispered "I know who the killer is- even if not the murderer"
