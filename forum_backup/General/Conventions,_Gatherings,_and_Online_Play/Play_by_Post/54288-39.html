                            (Hyuga)

                            Hyuga finds precious little about the Realm Defense Grid, save the normal histories and some rather unsubstantiated rumors. According to one, Defense Grid is powered by a lobotomized titan of raw warfare!

                            In his search for information, however, he finds a tome entitled "Advanced Manse Design for Complete Idiots" by a Kordellia the Librarian. Despite it's ancient appearance, the book looks to be at least three or four steps removed from the original tome.

                            The book claims that networking manses together using sorcerous and geomantic techniques greatly increases the potency of the individual manses powers; even more so if they are focused around a central core. Each subsequent manse, however, destabilizes the network as a whole unless extraordinary steps are taken. Unless those steps are taken, adding any more than ten manses to a network will result in immediate catastrophic failure, and any more than five begins to become susceptible to tampering influences.

                            Additionally, the use of magitechnology "Animating Intelligences" is strengthened by this networking. Unfortunately, much like the geomantic properties, networking manses past a certain point causes the Intelligence to become more and more unstable psychologically.

                                        (Jensven)

                                        Jensven's request is so odd that the librarian he queried directs him to the basement. Therein, in a cage behind five demonic custodians, lies a jagged shard of opaque crystal set in orichalcum and starmetal.

                                        The librarian says that you should direct your questions to it, though any attempt to touch the crystal will be met with deadly force.

                                        Provided you ask about the "Anathema" and "Dawns", the crystal answers in a ringing voice:

                                        "Anathema is the term used by the Immaculate Faith to discredit the Solar and Lunar Exalted and legitimize the Terrestrial's coup in the eyes of the masses. They claimed that the Solars and Lunars were demon-priests who had stolen shards of the Sun and Moon's power, rather than their chosen champions."

                                        "In the context of your previous question: the Dawn caste, called the Forsaken by the Immaculates, were those Solars who were chosen by the Sun for their martial prowess. In the early days of their histories, they were the generals and champions of the exalted host."
