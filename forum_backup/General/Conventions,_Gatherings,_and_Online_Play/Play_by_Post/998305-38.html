                                                        Haberdasher take your time! This is a big moment for Adonis, and will probably involve a couple of Milestones. It might also colour the entire game going forwards. Every time one of these characters creates a little bit of friction with a Milestone, it sparks a fire, and the story starts to burn its way into my mind in a slightly different way.

                                                        As an aside, I just got the Firestorm Chronicle Anthology and holy crap is it good. It also kind of fulfills my approach to Promethean, because it's exactly the sort of tone I've been trying to use here and it's glorious. It also has the same sort of blend of science, mysticism, and everyday weirdness that I'm trying to produce, and that same feeling of the Everyday Gothic that I feel so strongly living in Winnipeg. I got chills reading "La Familia de la Sicaria" because it's about a Galateid (I think) on Aes (I think, might be Aurum) that displays some nifty little things about the Pilgrimage. What if your Role is pushing you to do something unconscionable? And it also helped me realize something that gave a crawling sense of horror to Disquiet, especially sanguine Disquiet. It can give you everything you've ever wanted, as a Created. It can give you a family. It can give you people who will never, ever, give you up. People who would kill to have you back. And isn't it lovely, to feel desired? Isn't it precious, to feel wanted when you've been so alone? That hits on some stuff pretty close to home for me and provoked a pretty visceral response from me, so I think it did its job.
