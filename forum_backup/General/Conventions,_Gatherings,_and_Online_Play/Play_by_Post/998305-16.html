                                                        I really like Earth's. Echoing ArchonAres that I'm gonna be parsing and interpreting it for a while. I like the contrast between the really grounded side of North End crime and the Red River Rebellion versus the weird Pyros-world that the Created live in, it sort of reinforces how our characters are outsiders to mundane humanity. It's fun to read and think about and work through and the imagery will definitely be on my mind this weekend. The lacuna-feast is incredibly disturbing body horror that succeeded in grossing me out in a good way- it puts me in the right mindset by establishing that Earth has done some really vile things even if they aren't highlighted in detail in the present. It's not an abstract "she a villain" thing, but a shocking and inhuman sensory experience. I also really enjoy the image of the Pyros-entity with the broad shoulders (!) and blackened hand, I'm looking forward to deciding what Earth thinks that was about.
