    About the debate of the church and the speakeasy. How about this? A spirit of resistance or community or safehaven is at the heart of all this. The caves were heavily influential in the founding of the city, many of the founders made their fortune mining the gold deposits in those caves, and the building above it may have been the original town hall of Victory City. It was a city formed of wealth and cooperation. This formed a powerful spirit of community.

    As the city grew however, the building was too small and not central enough in location and eventually fell out of use. However, later during the prohibition, the spirit's influence came into play again. Being one of the poorest blue-collar neighbourhoods in the city, prohibition hit this area pretty hard. The community was in need for a place where workers who needed to unwind or let loose or unburden themselves in. So the spirit's influence was behind the speakeasy that formed in the caves underneath the building. It was important in reviving the spirits of this worn down community. Until it was found and shut down.

    Many years later, as crime and gang violence in the neighbourhood reached an all time high, the community grew afraid to leave their homes. The spirit's influence came into play again. The building was purchased and renovated into a church, and that church became a beacon of light in neighbourhood that might have succumbed to the bleakness of its situation. The community took a stand against the violence, they banded together and slowly the influence of the gangs in the area diminished. Until the church was burned down.

    And here we are. So we have a spirit that is very old, and has been looking after this area for centuries. Very influential. Would definitely have an impact on how we run the pack, and sort of combines all the bases so far. The locus itself could be in the recently burned down church, or further down in the caves, a dimming locus somehow still alive and resonant for all these years.

    So the spirit could be of the building itself, or just a spirit of Community or Rebirth/Renewal or Resistance. Could be called something like Safehaven. Though each time the spirit does something for the community, it seems to be foiled so it could even have a rival spirit. A spirit formed at the same time (gold mining, founding the city) of Greed or Corruption, one of the few spirits with more influence in the town.

    Okay, now I'm sort going into Storyteller territory so I'll back off. I don't know. Just trying to find a neat middle for all the great ideas we're all coming up with. We have a combination of the urban and the natural. We have a blue-collar, working-class community by the sea/docks, with fishermen and small shops, that is beset with crime and a potential target for redevelopment goals that we may have to thwart. Yea?

                                                            So I've started working on a wolfblooded character in an attempt to bolster our combat capability as soonmot suggested.

                                                            This is my idea so far.

                                                            Character Sheet: https://drive.google.com/file/d/0BxK...ew?usp=sharing

                                                            His/her name is Teller Stone. Teller comes from a long line of Blood Talons and her family was respected within the tribe. She's the daughter of a Blood Talon named Markos 'Blackheart', a renowned warrior who infamously went mad one day and murdered his entire pack. Soon after, he defected to the Predator Kings. In the years that followed, he led a pack of the Pure on a killing spree of almost unprecedented proportions.all across the West Coast. No one knows why he changed allegiances so suddenly. For years 'Blackheart' became something of a bogeyman among the People. A terror that could show up in your territory and slowly take your pack apart piece by piece. Eventually Markos and his pack were hunted down and killed by a group of his former tribemates, but none of them have ever forgotten the depths of his madness and his treachery.

                                                            Teller lives her life trying to escape the shadow of her father's legacy. She had idolized her father. He was everything she wanted to be. Now she has to distance herself from his name and his memory, or be shunned by her people. She took her mother's last name, Stone, and she's been trying to outrun her father's name ever since.

                                                            Teller is young, but she's been in a pack since she was 16. She's basically a born and raised werewolf, and has been groomed her whole life to be a Blood Talon. She's trained to fight with Silver weaponry. Trained in various combat tactics. She's basically a soldier. Her first change hasn't come yet, but she's sure it's just a matter of time. Even if it doesn't, she's not going to let that get in the way of her goal to rewrite her family's legacy.

                                                            So Teller has something to prove to not just the world, but herself. She's looking to start over because she hates the scrutiny and stigma that follows her around, especially with Blood Talons. She moved to this part of the state in particular because it doesn't have a very heavy Blood Talon presence. She treasures her chance friendship with Indiana because it's the first relationship she ever made with someone who had never even heard of her father. It's the first relationship she's had without that judgement and suspicion. Even more, when she eventually told Indy about her father's history, Indy did not care. She didn't even understand why anyone would judge Teller for her father's sins. No one suspected Indiana might be a Protestant Minister just because her father was. Why should Teller be treated any differently? Because of this Teller is very grateful, and because of that she has decided that this is where she'll finally settle down and start over.

                                                            She's going to have either the Liar's Skin, Second Skin or Anger Issues Tells. Not sure yet. Her gender is also undetermined, I've just been using female pronouns as a placeholder.
