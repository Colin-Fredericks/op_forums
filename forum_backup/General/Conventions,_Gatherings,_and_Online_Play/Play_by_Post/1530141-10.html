        Speaking of story, VictorOfNyke was asking me about Seer order status privately, and I thought it might be useful for me to explain some of my thoughts to the whole group.

        If you have been frustrated up until now because you feel like I haven't been giving you that much to go on in terms of plot information and quest giving NPCs, that was deliberate. Unlike Penticle mages who love magic-splaining to their "inferiors" most Seers are pretty secretive. And your average low level Seer is pretty clueless about what the higher-ups are up to. This is in no small part because the main way to move up the Iron Pyramid is to ruin or terminate the careers of the mages immediately above and below you. Seers are even more paranoid than your average mage.

        Add on top of this that the various ministries do not get along with each other that well. Many Penticle mages think of the Seers as an all powerful monolith. The reality is that Seers spend a lot of their time infighting with other Seers. And that is one of the main reasons that they don't actually control the whole world and haven't eliminated the Penticle orders.

        All of this to say, one of the reasons I was so insistent that at least one of you have some Seer status at character creation was so that it would be easier to feed the party plot. Critian Caceorte went with 3 dots and a prelacy, and Lorekeeper had 2.dots, so that's why they got the most explicit instructions at the beginning of the game. I think Critian Caceorte did a great job of playing a mid level Seer. The way Mother Time sort of forced a perfect stranger she met in a library to "serve" her for a few hours, with calloused indifference to that stranger's feelings, was good RP.

        Unfortunately Mother Time is no longer with us, which means that ironically the character with the most information right now is the Arrow who is attempting to infiltrate the Seers. I'm fine with that. It's an interesting way to go with the story. But if you want your character to move up the Iron Pyramid via merit dots, I'm fine with that too.
