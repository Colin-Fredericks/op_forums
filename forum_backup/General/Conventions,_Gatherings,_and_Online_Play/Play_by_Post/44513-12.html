                            -Geherin-
                            The confederation of the Vicruith clans lives in a densely forested area south-east of the nations of Ordon and Novalesk. They are known to the outsiders as the "Geherin" a word that means "tea-eater" in their tribal dialect. This word is the name of their warrior caste, referencing their consumption of a certain species of tea-leaves before battle. These leaves are said to grant the eater the strength of ten men and steel them against pain.

                            The Geherin are a bellicose and bombastic people. They have a proud warrior tradition, as well as a strong love of aural arts. The only people afforded equality to the clan chieftains are their Skalds.

                            The Geherin's favored weapon is the bearded axe. Many Geherin will travel into battle with nothing more than a half dozen of these axes, which they will use as both melee and thrown weapons. They rarely fight with any form of formation, as that reduces a warrior's chance for individual glory.

                            The clan chieftains each hold absolute power over their clan's territory. The clan chieftains meet in moot once a season to discuss news and trade stories. Any disputes between the clans are adjudicated by the High King, a former chieftain elected for a decade for that sole purpose.

                            The Geherin worship a being known as 'The Silver-Horned Mother' who has taken over the local spirit courts.

                                -Ordon-
                                The Glorious Free City of Ordon sits south of Novalesk along the Rock River. This nation consists of three cities (Ordon, Sayat and Ahal from east to west) and numerous outlying communities. In they few instances where Novalesk has waged war on Ordon's soil, those communities were noted to be highly organized and regimented.

                                The only interactions that have occurred between Novalesk and Ordon have been between either their military and diplomats. Interactions with the diplomats have hinted as a highly stratified caste-based society. The discipline and precision of their military corps supports this notion.

                                The Ordon appear to have a strong technical tradition. Their cities and towns are all walled more often than not, and their nation in crisscrossed with a network of solid stone roads. Their siege engineer rival those of Gens Ptreas in skill and ingenuity.

                                Ordon itself if a heavily fortified city with strong walls and magical artillery, though it is much too small to house its purported population. Nevertheless, Ordon devours large amounts of natural resources, and exports only a fraction of that input. Where the rest goes is anyone's guess.

                                                -Theros-
                                                Theros lies to the west of Novalesk. The people of this nation are rather similar to those of Novalesk in type and disposition. Theros, however, is a highly Immaculate nation, which has caused a great deal of friction between the two nations over the centuries. In RY 528, Theros became a Realm tributary following a losing successive wars with Ordon and Novalesk.

                                                Theros is an Absolute Monarchy with absolute primogeniture succession (oldest child inherits, regardless of sex), though a non-exalted inheritor will often give up the succession in favor their closest Exalted relative (a survival trait due to the two-week life expectancy of non-exalted monarchs). The nation has always been blessed with a small number of Terrestrial Exalted, dating back to its inception. There are a number of noble houses with strong Terrestrial bloodlines, but none with long history. Weaker families are absorbed into the stronger ones with alarming frequency, though once a family reaches a certain critical mass it fractures into multiple smaller ones to repeat the cycle.

                                                Theros has a citizen military, such as it is. Native Therian males are expected to own a shield, spear and linen curiass, and keep them in repair. Their forces are, generally speaking, poorly equipped and untrained. The noble houses each maintain a private military force, modeled on Realm legion forces. The Realm Garrison is composed of two Wings detached from the 19th Imperial Legion. Together, these forces provide a professional core for the untrained levies.
