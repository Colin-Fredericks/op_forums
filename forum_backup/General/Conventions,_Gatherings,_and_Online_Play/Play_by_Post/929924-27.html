    Chapter 2: "All in Order"
    Saturday, September 10, 2016. Mid-morning. One week after the Deathstorm.


    Moonstone
    You wake after a night with troubled dreams. You're dwelling on all of the things you've learned and faced in the past few days: Ace's distancing from you, Mathias' prophetic dreams of an apocalyptic storm wreaking havoc on Chicago, vague visions of The Destroyer. But today is another day. Today will be your first time attending a Consilium meeting as an equal of the mages you once served.

    Your phone has a text on it this morning saying that you'll need to leave the Arrow safe house today. You make sure to pack up everything you brought here.


    Binder
    Sleeping in on the weekend with a young child is never easy, but you're used to the routine by now. You make breakfast for Daisy and Forest and enjoy time together with them. Although she usually assumes childcare and grocery shopping duties on Saturday while you tend to the bookstore, you explain to her that you have the store closed today because of a "networking conference" you need to attend. "That sounds interesting," she comments. "I hope it ends up being helpful and useful. I'm sure the extra business couldn't hurt, either."


    Lynx
    You wake up early feeling fully rested. Ewan is already up getting ready for his morning run, and he invites you along if you're interested. He says that he already has a paper he needs to work on for his class, so he's going to take off for the morning afterwards so he can get that started. "Don't want to let it get in the way of my workout later. Bros don't let bros skip leg day," he jokes. You're not sure if he's using the word "bro" ironically or not.


    Phoenix
    No messages or calls from TJ or Tanya this morning when you wake up. You're eager to see if they can help you track down the guy you saw, but you have a feeling he hasn't even started looking.


    Ragnar
    You wake up to an email this morning from Dr. Jacob Langley, the UChi English professor who has befriended you, asking you if you're available sometime today or tomorrow to help out with some research. He's hoping to get your help writing down some notes while he examines some antique objects he has stumbled across recently. He asks that you call or email him later when you have time, noting that he is willing to work around your schedule if needed.


    Ouroboros Ophis
    You receive a text from Mathias, the théarch whom you met recently and who urged you to attend this week's Consilium meeting to help introduce you to mage society. He asks you to arrive at the meeting around 11 am, where he would like to introduce you to a group of other newly Awakened mages. He thinks that you would be a good influence for them and would help balance out their personalities, so collectively you would have a well-rounded cabal.


    Everyone
    As a reminder, Valentine instructed you here on where and when to arrive for the Consilium meeting, suggesting that you meet early so you can meet some other mages. He emphasized that you should not cast any spells on the church premises; you're of course welcome to ignore his advice if you choose. Please indicate in your posts whether you would cast any spells or rituals the morning before the meeting, or if there is anything else you would do before you go.

        Moonstone wakes up and immediately knows it's going to be a bad day.

        He makes some tea and oatmeal in the sanctum kitchen and gets to work packing his things in a duffel bag. There's a cloud hanging over his head.

        When all the dancing shoes and ritual sacraments are put away, he stares at the stuffed bag on his neatly-made bed. Really not ready to go back home yet. He gestures like a magician and commands the bag: "TELEPORT!"

        "AWAY WITH YOU!"

        "SHAZAM!"

        No luck. It's like trying to flex a muscle you don't have. He's going to have to carry the bag himself, like a Sleeper. Ugh. He leaves a nice thank-you note on the kitchen table, but there's no smiley face. That's how upset he is.

        ---

        Home still doesn't feel like home. He gets back to the apartment he shared with Hakim, sets down his bag, sits down for a second that becomes an hour curled up on the couch and crying. He thinks about skipping the Consilium meeting entirely, but eventually talks himself into at least taking a shower. That gets his forward momentum back. He puts on some more masculine formalwear, vest and tie and all, wraps the mirror in the cloak and tucks them both into his messenger bag. The stormcloud is still there.

        He takes the train (no storms or chance meetings this time) to the familiar Chicago station near St. John Cantius and arrives a little after 10. Listening to traffic and smelling greasy food takes his focus away from his thoughts. When he sees the familiar face of the guard outside the church, he nods and formally recites the passphrase: "I am here to open my eyes to the truth."

            06:00 AM.

            Marvin wokes up to the sound of his clock alarm. He gets up from the couch (their bed felt too big and empty without Melissa) and makes some stretches to ready himself. After his muscles are as awake as his mind, he changes into his sportswear and goes out for some light jogging.

            07:00 AM.

            Back home he goes straight to the shower. Hot water followed by cold to help the circulation flow. He takes his time in front of the mirror, shaving his head and grooming his beard and moustache with the utmost care. Oils and creams to keep it lush and soft, covered a good portion of his side of the bathroom stall. He tried no to look to hers. He cries.

            07:30 AM.

            He treats himself with a great breakfast: coffee, toasts, scrambled eggs and bacon. He smiles drinking his coffee and reading the news. His eyes still a little red.

            07:45 AM.

            He practises his breathing and meditation exercises, followed by some Yoga. His pain recedes, his mind calms down.

            08:10 AM.

            Time to work. He locks himself in his study, reading for the nth time the notes of his predecesor. He checks the class scheudles, the themes, the proposed work and bibliography. Takes another look at each of the files of each of his students, memorizing the names and the faces, noting down his own personal impressions and suggestion on how to motivate or focus each of them so they get the most out of the classes. Who knows? One of this young people could very well be in a position to save his life in the future. Better prepare them the best that he can.

            He likes this, working alone in his study everything seems normal. He can almost pretend Melissa is in the next room. Almost.

            10:10 AM.

            He closes the books, orders his notes and leaves everything tidy and where it belongs. He gets out of his study and goes seeing his recent acquisition, a brand new terrarium reproducing the habitat of his pet, an Atheris Hispida, more commonly know as the spinny bush viper. He has not yet given it a name. He grabs a small mouse he took from the lab and feeds it to the snake. That reminds him that he should go shop for some groceries before attending the meeting. He consults his watch. Less than an hour left. Still, time enough.

            10:35 AM.

            Groceries taken care of, he preapares for the Consilium. White shirt and dark grey pants with a matching vest. No tie. No jacket either, instead of that he takes a long grey coat he is quite fond of. An aniversary present. He caresses the soft yet sturdy fabric with a smile. He polishes his shoes until they shine.
            With calm he prepares his doctors bag, checking his list to make sure everything is there and in perfect condition. He makes sure to have his white doctor's coat at hand too. Mathias said no magic at the Consilium and he plans on observing the rules, but it is always better to be safe than sorry.

            He arrives to the meeting place and inmediately starts looking for the few known faces he expects, Dante, Mathias or Valentine.

                            Changing some minor details about the scene from some of your posts, as I didn't make clear where the guards would be located. See below for details.


                            Moonstone
                            You've been to this church before for Consilium events, but you know from the ones you've attended and what Hakim has told you that many Consilium meetings are spread across various public locations around the city on a rotating schedule. Still, even with the rotating schedule, during your years of service you've been here enough to know the general procedure. Sleeper events are held here on their usual schedule, including mass, various special musical events, and prayer and devotional services. The Consilium events are held in private rooms away from the main sanctuary, down in the basement; inconspicuous guards, almost always théarchs, typically stand outside of a hallway near signs that read "Private Event."

                            When you arrive shortly after 10 am, there are no services currently in progress, but people are lingering from a mass that ended about half an hour ago. Several people are speaking with priests or deacons, reading scripture, or kneeling in silent prayer. You don't immediately recognize anyone or see any signs with guards nearby.



                            For a while, you wait in the narthex, mingling with the people coming and going for their individual religious practices. As it gets closer to 11 am, you start to see a few familiar faces: a few Sleepwalkers in service of cabals and other Orders you don't know that well, and a handful of mages you recognize but don't know by name. Ragnar arrives and seems engrossed in his study of the sanctuary, so you let him be for a while.

                            It's then that you realize the guards have taken their position outside of a hallway from the narthex. You make your way over, smoothly say the passphrase, and they nod and wave you in with hardly a pause; they're trying not to draw too much attention, and they clearly recognize you. You walk along a tiled hallway into a large reception hall. There is a raised platform with simple chairs arranged in a semi-circle, where you know the Hierarch and Councilors (or their Provosts, depending on who attends) will be seated. Various mages and their associates are here, speaking with each other about private affairs. A few look towards you, but no one approaches you yet.

                            That's when you see the surviving members of the Shield of Dawn speaking with a middle-aged Caucasian man with graying hair. You overhear Mathias refer to him as Ouroboros Ophis, and make your way over to greet him.


                            Ouroboros Ophis
                            Your home has a somber atmosphere this morning, as usual since Melissa's absence. Your morning routine helps you to calm your mind and mentally prepare you for the Consilium meeting. Although you don't fully know what to expect, you want to present yourself as calm, collected, and knowledgeable.

                            You arrive at the church with a very polished and pristine outfit, dressed to impress. You don't immediately recognize anyone, however. You see various people and religious officials wandering the sanctuary, but no services are in progress. About 15 minutes after you arrive, Mathias and his cabalmates walk through the front door, and he greets you with a smile. "Good morning, Doctor," he says as he shakes your hand. "I'm glad you could make it today." He formally introduces you to Boomer and Valentine, his cabalmates, whom he has told you about but you haven't met. He escorts you to a hallway from the narthex where two people stand by a sign that reads "Private Event." Mathias speaks the passphrase, and they nod and let you pass. You find yourself in a large reception hall, where sturdy chairs are neatly arranged in long rows facing a raised platform with more chairs in a semi-circle.

                            As Mathias chats with you and begins referring to you by your Shadow Name, another mage approaches you and introduces himself as Moonstone. You recognize the name as one of the mages Mathias said may be interested in forming a cabal with you.


                            Ragnar
                            You receive an email reply from Dr. Langley shortly before leaving your apartment, saying that the time frame you suggested would be excellent. He offers to buy you lunch in exchange for your assistance.

                            When you open yourself to the Supernal world as seen through the lens of Arcadia, you see that this building has a...weight to it. This building stands at the crossroads of fate, but in ways you don't immediately understand. You see large, thick cords of Fate wrapped around the building, filling the sanctuary; this surprises you, considering that you were told no one should be using magic here. But even though you don't yet understand the magic surrounding this place, you can guess based on its sheer size and scale that a strong mage, or perhaps a group of them, were involved in its casting.

                            You may attempt Revelation and/or Scrutiny with Opacity 4.


                            After studying the room, you realize there aren't any guards here in the sanctuary as you thought there would be. You wander back through the narthex, and that's when you see the sign reading "Private Event." You intone your passphrase, and thankfully they wave for you to pass through. You find Mathias and the rest of the Shields of Dawn standing a circle, next to Moonstone and, to your shock, Dr. Bates, the professor who has filled in for Dr. McCallister for your FYS class.


                            Lynx
                            You are impressed at the size and scale of this building. As you approach, you feel the hairs on the back of your neck rise, a sign of something unseen on your Periphery. Glancing around with your Sight reveals quite a few spirits in Twilight circling the building, and within the building itself. They seem to be basking in the presence of this place. You notice some spirits that look like the one you saw at your apartment, tranquility spirits, as well as others you don't recognize.

                            You speak the password to the guards you assume are here for the Consilium event. One's eyes widen a bit in surprise at your bumbling discussion, and the other rolls his eyes. "Go on through," he says curtly, shaking his head with a sigh. As you walk by them, you hear him whisper to the other guard, "Newbies, am I right?" The other guard chuckles.

                            You find the meeting room with little trouble after walking through the hallway. The Shields of Dawn are there conferring with Moonstone, Ragnar, and -- is that Dr. Bates? Your new FYS instructor?
