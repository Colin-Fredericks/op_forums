                                                        Diane rubs her chin, pondering whether or not to remove the spear, when she suddenly remembers the legend of Aengus, one of the tales of her childhood. Aengus, the story went, was a powerful Getherin, both young and handsome. After a long series of adventures, he found a massive axe embedded in a massive oak tree. After pulling the axe from the tree, a procession of Fae descended upon Aengus and declared him their true and rightful king.

                                                        "Truely, this is my destiny," Diane says, as she reaches out to pull the spear out of the mountain, perhaps intentionally forgetting the end of the story where Aengus accidentally and almost immediately caused a populist revolt through his new demesne and causing the formation of a democratic republic.
