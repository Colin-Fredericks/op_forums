    "The Realm is eighteen months away assuming they can magic a road from the Imperial City for their troops to march along, Lookshy will never permit them to travel the river in strength. With the benefit of the river, the Seventh Legion can have forces here within three seasons, they will not dispatch the sky fleet while the Mask of Winters grows stronger every day. For better or worse Novalesk's problems are local.

    As for the annexation or subjugation of its neighbors, Novalesk will not survive if it does not. Whatever the rumors say Theros is expanding. Novalesk is not self sufficient and the river does not supply the quantity of materials at the low prices necessary to allow for the continued growth of the nation. Even so...

    It will not be easy. I would welcome any thoughts you have on the technical or political aspects. The roads are but a portion of the greater plan though, even if you do not support this plan, the cost reduction I propose is not contingent on the whole."

        "You forget of Nasaru, Gunzota and Greyfalls... Though they do a fair job of bottling each other up. Or so they would have you think." Argyrus says.

And us contained by Theros, Ordon and the Barbarians to our East and North. Call me a conservative old man, but I don't see that stalemate breaking in our favor after recent events."

Argyrus pauses for a few moments, clearly pondering his word choice, " the more competent of our Strategoi, and the ex-Archon, you would be of more use to me than I could be to you."

                                That's it! Poweful manses are all about linking the lines of essence in an overlaying pattern through a core point! This concept could easily work with magitech artifacts as well! The essence lines would link together with the wielder's aura through a central point and power an artifact. If this information is correct, I could even create artifacts with up to 10 seperate lines for different functions at an increased power level!

                                After feverishly reading the book and memorizing its concepts, Hyuga will continue to seek more books about ancient technologies and crafting techniques (especially magitech). Pages turn rapidly and Hyuga's eyes seem to reflect the Sun's rays as his mind seems to expand faster than it seemed possible. So many links had been connected, lights were coming on, and everything was beginning to make sense and fall in its natural place.

                                These are all extremely simple concepts requiring just a bit of extra work, but they seem to have just dissapeared from today's world. I don't understand how this was not passed down. I must find more ancient texts. Perhaps I should go to see the Chancellor. Maia did mention that he would have some rare tomes. But first things first, time to start writing plans and see if I can help Maia finish up her work.

                                With his newfound knowledge, Hyuga cheerfully and energetically walks back to the artificery to look for Maia. "Maia! Are you here? I wanted to know if you needed any help infusing the golem spirits into the shells. I am very familiar with the occult after all!"

                                Hyuga will be using his last 6 exp to increase his craft-magitech up to 3 dots immediately.
