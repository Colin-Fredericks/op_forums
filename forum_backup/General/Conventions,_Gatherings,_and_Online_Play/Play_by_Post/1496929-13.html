                                                        More weird cults...Aegis Kai Doru...sounds strange to Halwyn as he speaks the words.
                                                        Artifact Hunter... At the moment it seemed like he was attracting really crazy things like a magnet.

                                                        Even if Eloi writes that the text will be deleted, he at least writes down the names of these hunters in his book. Who knows when he would need the info.

                                                        In bed he looks at his cell phone again, sets an early alarm and falls asleep.


                                                        The next morning, after taking another shower, he gets ready for the morning shift at the diner. Outside, he takes another close look at his car. He really needs to call the insurance company, it looks like what he's doing for his break. He walks to dinner, despite the cold. Again the question arises whether the snow and the cold are normal here or whether it is caused by the huntsman. Arriving at the diner, he greets Marcy. He's kind of happy to see his boss, she always puts his mind at ease with her manner and looks. After a quick look to see which guests have already arrived, he goes straight into the kitchen, puts on his apron and takes care of the pending orders. Again and again he looks out to see if anyone he knows is there...whether from Freehold or from the area itself.

                                                        He's also pondering his next steps. When his shift is over, he goes to said pier where Scratch met the wolf. He then plans to go to a copy shop to copy the pages from the book before driving to House Up On The Hill to bring it back. Perhaps there is already information for him there. Later in the day, when it gets dark, he wants to go to the roofless house.
                                                        What he can do in the meantime, he has no idea.
