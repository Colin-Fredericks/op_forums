There is also the bright green bubble of resentment, simmering beneath the indigo, threatening to bubble to the surface, but even as Earth watches the indigo deepens and the amber glows more brightly, like a conscious effort to keep the resentment from boiling over. The uncomfortably cheerful yellow of discomfort, pale yellow, mustard yellow, the kind of yellow one would make a particularly appalling wallpaper, gleams about his hands, but even as the tension in Solomon's body rises the yellow is smothered by the earthen tones of resolve.
