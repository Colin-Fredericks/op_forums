                                                        ​Wormwood Haberdasher ArchonAres Caladriu Dusksage the update is up! I'm not really sure what's left to handle with the Ramble. You've been introduced to several potential plot hooks (and you have the existing Amalgama LTD plot thread to pursue), you've been introduced to two new characters, and all in all I think the scene got done what it needed to get done. All that's really left to do is for Scout and Regret to attach themselves to the Throng, and for the Throng to decide what to do next.

                                                        Minus Adonis, of course, he's got plans, but the rest of you can decide what you're going to do for the rest of the night.

                                                        I was also wondering if I could get some feedback about the Ramble and what you guys thought. What did you like, what did you feel was missing, did it feel like a Promethean thing? The books really have no guideline for how to handle more than a handful of Created at a time, and a larger group trying to form a community is kind of unprecedented. The Created have a tendency to feel like the kicked dogs of the Chronicles of Darkness, they have it pretty rough, and it would be easy to make every Promethean's life a long slog of one tragedy after another constantly chasing after the hope of the New Dawn, but I figured I would try and show some Created who have their lives a little more together, who are hopeful and determined and vivacious. Promethean can feel pretty dreary, sometimes, but I didn't want all the hope and beauty to come from humans, I wanted some of it to come from the Created themselves, and I hope that came across.
