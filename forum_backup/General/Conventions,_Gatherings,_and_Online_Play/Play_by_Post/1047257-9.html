                    Okay, cool, I'm glad you guys are enjoying it! I'll stay on track then.

                    BTW, don't forget to record your currently active spells in the header, and note when they end.

                    Exthalion I realized what was itching at my brain about Khvar's Fate rolls. In the other Mage PBP, we houseruled that you can't have the 8-again and rote qualities on the same roll (after my PC tried exactly that). The reasoning is that rote actions are the next step in the 9-again to 8-again progression where one overrides the other. It also helps distinguish the Exceptional and Superlative Luck spells by giving them mutually exclusive effects that you choose between for the situation: nearly-guaranteed success on a risky roll, or racking up lots of successes if you can get any in the first place. I'm not 100% sure whether this is or isn't the RAW intent, but I'll go with that rule for this game. Leave your rolls how they are, but in the future those effects are either/or!
