    Talos rose from his bed in the manse's 'master bedroom'. He got dressed in his usual clothes and looked at one of the little automata on the floor.

    "Let's fix up some breakfast for me and our guests. No reason to be a bad host." He says to the automata in Old Realm. The clockwork machine gives a small salute, then scurries off.

    The scavenger lord picks up his spear and leaves the bedroom, thinking about his current situation. Less than a month as an "anathema", and he had already found others like him. Well, Swimming Owl and Splendor at least. Hopefully Flynn and Narathul would return, they seemed like good people.

    Talos returned to the dining room and sat down at the head of the table. Reaching into his pocket, he took out one of the crystals from the Manse's library. He set it down and began to study the automata blueprints, waiting for his new friends to arrive for breakfast.

    "Oh boys! Look what I got here!"
    "Hey, where the Dynast women at?"

                        Several minutes later, four automata clamber back with a couple trays of food. One holds what you assume is an overcooked egg and the other holds some sort of cooked meat. By looking, you would guess crocodile, but who knows what they actually got. It does look as though it is healthy enough to eat. With a few clicks and clacks, they drop off the trays and a few plates for placing food and scurry off somewhere else.
