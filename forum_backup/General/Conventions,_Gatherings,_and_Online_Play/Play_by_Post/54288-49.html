                                    He had to admit that was a neat little trick, but Jensven wasn't about to try and beat this warrior at his own game. Where he came from the warrior that won was the one that beat his opponent into submission. He was never really good at that either. In fact, he was among the least of the warriors where he came from barely able to hold a weapon. This is why he was a smith and not included in the raiding parties.

                                    That all changed when the Sun God talked to him and showed him what he could be come, what was in the past. It was a past life that was now attached to him. In every combat he had participated in it was this experience from long ago that he allowed to guide him. Perhaps later he could try and merge that with what he had known and develop something uniquely his, but for now, this is how he survived. This is how he won.

                                    The Dawn allowed his blade to slide off that of his opponent and then swung it quickly at his adversary's midsection. There was no attempt at deception, no tricks involved. There was only speed and strength. Both were undeniably part of his attack and there was a simple grace to that.                                                     The Dawn leads with his blade and spins into the attack, trusting his motion and his skill to intercept the opposing blade. He trusts his strength to meet the strength of his opponent and prevail. Jensven steps forward and puts his whole body into the force of the deflecting strike. The very idea of the opposing strike being able to get through is utterly alien to him. It may as well have not even been conceived.

                                                    Moving directly through a technique he learned during a his 'golden vision' he jumps upward, sliding his 'klaive off the opposing sword. As Jensven approaches the apex of his jump he slams his knee upward, directly at the bell-warrior's chin. When his momentum slows he will raise his blade over his head in a double-handed grip. On the way down the Ascending Sun will use whatever impetus was gained from the impact of his knee to add power to a downward sword stroke of his own. His attack is aimed squarely at hacking into the point where neck and shoulder meet.

                                                    Okay, depending on how much of a stunt I can get out of the defensive action in the first paragraph there, I will activate Melee Essence Triumphant to get either one or two points added to my PDV. This will cost either two or four motes from peripheral.

                                                    For the second paragraph, I am going to attempt a two attack flurry. The first attack (the knee) is going to be a kick (rate 2) and the second will be with the 'Klaive (rate 2). One attack each allows for me to stay within the limits for rate with both weapons. Since this is a two action flurry the first attack takes a -2 internal penalty and the second takes a -3 internal penalty.

                                                    This means the kick would be at Dexterity (4) + Martial Arts (3) + Kick Accuracy (0) + Flurry (-2) = Five Dice for two successes.

                                                    I am going to assume one die of stunt for the 'Klaive strike. This gives me Dexterity (4) + Melee (3) + 'Klaive Accuracy (3) + Flurry (-3) + Stunt (1) = Seven + One Dice for two + one successes.

                                                    If the flurry doesn't work because of mixed attack types (Martial Art versus Melee) let me know and I will reroll the attack with just the 'Klaive.
