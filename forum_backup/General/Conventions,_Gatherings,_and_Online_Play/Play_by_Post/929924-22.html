                    Everyone
                    For a long pause, Mathias stares at the ground, unable to lift his gaze to any of you. His breathing begins to slow to a steady rhythm, and he wipes the sweat from his forehead.

"The Duel Arcane is a system of combat for settling wrongs and claims by opposing parties," he says wearily. All of the heat has left his tone, and his voice sounds hoarse as if he has been yelling for hours. "By right of force and will, the winner is granted exclusive rights to the stakes and claims over which the Duel were held. It is steeped in millennia of tradition, passed down from generation to generation of mages. The magic of the Duel is such that it leaves the loser mentally and spiritually exhausted, such that failing to accept the terms of losing is incredibly difficult." His voice does indeed sound exhausted. "And even if that were not the case, the code duello and the Lex Magica -- the laws of the Duel Arcane and mage society overall, respectively -- clearly indicate that failing to accept terms of losing after a Duel is a severe crime."

                    And, as if to demonstrate his compliance, Mathias finally makes eye contact with Moonstone, if briefly, and walks towards him. The Mastigos mage slowly kneels before him, takes a deep breath, and nods. "Moonstone, I wish to formally...thank you..." The words are slow in coming, as if being dragged out of him. "...for saving my, and these mages', lives from the Gulmoth known as Alux." Pause, followed by another longer, slower breath. Mathias' eyes are fixated on Moonstone, but they still lack the heat they held before the Duel.

                    He sighs. "As I already said, it was wrong to strike you out of anger. I ask for you to forgive me for harming you, insulting you, and disrespecting you after you...put your life on the line for my sake." By this point, Mathias' eyes are starting to pick up more of the intensity they held before his loss, but his tone remains decidedly neutral. Mathias takes one more ragged breath, as if preparing himself, and then he leans forward and kisses Moonstone's hand. His lips twitch, and he quickly removes them, as if tasting something unsavory.

                    His required tasks completed, at least for now, Mathias quickly stands to his feet. However, he continues to speak. "You truly are a remarkable mage, for one so newly Awakened. In my pride, I underestimated you." He laughs bitterly at the irony. "But I must impress upon you again that you have been blessed by Creation with incredible power, but also an equally incredible responsibility." He closes his eyes and intones softly in High Speech, creating a pulse of energy visible to your Sight or diffusely sensed on your Periphery. "This I prophesize: Alux will return, and it will murder. Your actions have set ripples in motion that I believe cannot be prevented now. So, while I thank you for your efforts to defeat the Gulmoth," he says bitterly, "you must forgive me for lacking satisfaction with the outcome."

                    Mathias turns and walks slowly away. He pauses at the treshold, then half-turns his head over his shoulder to say, "The Consilium meets in downtown Chicago on Saturday at noon, sharp. I will complete our terms then." With that, he walks away, footsteps muted on the carepeted hallway leading away from this room
