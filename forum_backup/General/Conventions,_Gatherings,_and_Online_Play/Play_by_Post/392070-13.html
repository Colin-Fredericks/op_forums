                                        Backstory

                                        Daniil’s parents were librarian and minor bureaucrat. They were diligent, gentle and loyal to Party. All in all they were model citizens. Young Daniil’s friends were nothing like that. Short-tempered and clever he led a band of young ruffians before he even got a beard. They were thieves, vandals and, as they matured, robbers. Once they accidentally killed a man they wanted to beat down and rob. While militsiya (police) didn’t manage to prove gang’s involvement, the resulting scrutiny allowed to solve some of the past crimes. And so, Daniil Sosnovsky met his 16th birthday in a jail.

                                        Some people in jail begin to contemplate their sins. Daniil instead saw an opportunity to make useful contacts. His cunning mind and strict following of thieves law earned him respect of other criminals. They nicknamed him Coffin (Sosnovsky means Pine’s in Russian). Three years later he was once again free. It was 1991. Soviet Union dissolved. It was a time of Free Market. Many (probably most) of businessmen were criminals as well. And this was a time of great opportunity not only for white-collar crime. Daniil organized a new gang. He abandoned all shreds of innocence he had before. Counterfeit, extortion, murder. He skyrocketed through the ranks of Russian organized crime. Six years later, in the age of 25 he was ‘crowned’ as a Thief-in-Law (member of elite of Russian crime world).

                                        Normal citizen’s would’ve called Daniil and his actions evil. But among his peers and subordinates he was actually known not only for his ruthlessness and anger but also for being protective over his people and his territory (which by that time included most of the city). When family member of one of his thugs fell ill, he helped with money. When minor politician decided to close local church, Daniil responded with blackmail. When abomination from the Abyss decided to eat the city in 1999, Daniil wasn’t ready.

                                        It was seemingly normal day. Daniil was driving his car to meet one of his ‘business’ partners. Suddenly he stopped to understand the map of local area. Streets were connected in the wrong way, a whole district was missing and his car was on the wrong side of the street. Daniil wasn’t good enough with driving so the latter anomaly shortly caused car wreck.

                                        He opened his eyes on a colossal graveyard. The closest tombstones were dedicated to his dead friends, subordinates, and victims. Many of them he didn’t even know, but shades that emerged from graves all silently looked at him. In the center of graveyard stood a lead tombstone without a name. Daniil understood that whatever he do, he will die anyway and will have to meet face to face with all sins he committed. Filled with sudden calm he accepted this fate and signed his name on the tombstone.

                                        Shortly after that he was found by magister of Guardians of the Veil who was busy fixing remains of the catastrophe. Almost half of local mages (Hierarch including) died trying to stop Abyssal intruder. So, urgently needing new blood they welcomed recently Awakened crime lord in their mids. But Daniil changed. He desperately wanted to somehow redeem his previous actions. Philosophy of Guardians fitted him perfectly. Daniil (or Yosef, as he became known among mages) remained a Thief-in-Law but often used his organization for various Guardian missions. Now, ten years later he is city’s one of the most powerful and resourceful Guardian. And he is beginning to think that maybe, just maybe, he is getting closer to redemption.
