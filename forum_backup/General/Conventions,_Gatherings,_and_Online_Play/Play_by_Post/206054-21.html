                                            I think you may have misread Nyrufa's meaning. He wasn't saying they're all fighters, skipping out on social and mental stuff. He was saying that to date, when they've gotten into combat -- generally having been surprised or otherwise prevented from thinking/talking their way out -- that's how they've defended themselves. For instance, Max has had some martial arts training, but his first inclination is definitely not to throw down with someone or something. If I were to stat him up, he'd probably have Social or Mental in his primary area.

                                            On which note, just remember that this is a freeform game. Which is not to say there's anything wrong with statting up the character or thinking in mechanical terms: it can be a fun exercise and can help some people get a feel for a character. If that sounds like you, then rock on! Just noting that we've not really referred to or thought about the mechanics, for the most part.
