    "Books are nice," Lynx murmurs to himself as he spots it by the window. Books are nice? We're graduating moron college, surrounded by supernal phenomena, and books are nice? Great fucking job, keeping my fucking priorities. What am I supposed to do!?

    "Lynx!"

    "Oh, hey, Ragnar. Glad it's you joining me-"

    He stiffens as he catches the black creature in the corner of his eye.
    Perception roll → 0 Successes.
    No dramatic failure.

    Intelligence + Occult roll → 0 Successes.
    No dramatic failure.



    "Oh. Okay. These are, uh, spirits right? They aren't going to be any trouble, are they?"

    Lynx rubs his beard apprehensively, his senses scattered everywhere. "I honestly don't know, Ragnar, I'm... fuck me, I'm not sure where I am right now."

    He rubs his temple, taking a deep breath, trying to open his Senses - but it gets him nowhere. He tries to listen, to smell, to touch the air, but his body and mind are both deprived of the will to manifest it. "I'll try to see what that is," he mutters, frowning, and under his breath, "Pretend I'm useful or something." He tries to impose his senses upon the creature, to glean something, anything, to stabilise his thoughts.

    Lynx attempts to casts Spirit 1 "Know Spirit" to garner information about the Essence phenomena with Potency 3, and he succeeds, allowing him to ask 3 questions and netting him up to 3 answers to what the entity is. In order of importance: 1: What is its name, 2: what is its influence, and 3: what is its bane?
    Potency: 3
    Primary Spell Factor: Potency.
    1 target: Black insect-like spirit.
    Withstand: Target's Rank.
    Duration: Instant
    Yantra: High Speech (+2), Dedicated Tool (+1)
    Base Dice Pool: Spirit 1 "Know Spirit": Spirit (2) + Gnosis (1) + High Speech (+2) + Dedicated Tool (+1) - Potency (2)
    Reaches: 2 Free Reaches: 1 on Instant Cast, 1 on Range, 1 on Spell Maintenance.

    Paradox modifiers: -2 from Mana.
    Final Paradox dice: 0.
    If Paradox is obtained, Lynx will release it.

    Roll for Paradox → 0 successes.

    Roll for Spirit 1 "Know Spirit" → 2 successes.


    "Come on, give me answers, damn it," he grumbles angrily under his breath, growing visibly agitated. His imago fixes in place, and he attempts to read ... whatever the creature is.

                                    Moonstone
                                    You create your spell Imago in such a way that it encompasses the computer and the space around it, like a net being thrown into the sea with hopes that it will ensnare its target, and your mind suddenly fills with knowledge about this friendly little ghost.

                                    The "mind" you reveal is more like a recording than a consciousness, much like a voicemail is to the spoken word, except that it seems capable of responding to outside stimuli. The ghostly child seeks to entertain itself by confusing you and hiding from you, but it has no malicious intent.


                                    Virtue: Curious
                                    Vice: Stubborn
                                    Aspirations: I don't see rules for these for ghosts, so I'm just going to give it a single short-term aspiration: "Use its Numina to play games with someone's mind."
                                    Attributes: You learn that its Finesse + Resistance = 4.

                                    You learn the following information: It imagines "playing" by using its Numina to create illusions or hallucinations in the target, to amuse itself and confuse the target. He just wants to show you funny things. He has one Anchor, which is his bedroom just down the hallway.


                                    Immediately upon casting your spell, your skin begins to crawl and you hear a horrible shriek echo throughout the entire house. New words appear on the screen:

                                    You made her angry.


                                    Ragnar and Lynx
                                    Just as the words leave Ragnar's mouth, a chill passes up your spine and you hear a terrible, furious scream. The door to the room in front of you slams open towards you, so close to you that it nearly threatens to knock you off your feet. The visage of a middle-aged woman clearly stalks through the door, her teeth growing into fangs and her hands as claws. She lunges forward -- and slips like a knife between you, shoving you forcefully into the nearby wall as she disappears downstairs.

                                    As you steady yourselves and look into the bedroom, you hear loud hissing. A thick black cloud of those black beetles swarms in the center of the room. It splits into three human-sized swarms. Between them on the ground, you see several unmoving, pale fingers with insects crawling on it and a partial glimpse of a woman's dead face twisted in agony. Now that you are closer, you can see that these insects look like some kind of scarab beetle.

                                    The swarms begin to move towards you in unison.


                                    Phoenix
                                    Your spell reveals the minds of your companions, an unseen presence here in this room with a "playful" Resonance, and suddenly a presence moving rapidly towards you with a "furious" Resonance. Moments later, you see a ghostly presence in the shape of a woman burst into the room. Her eyes are burning with vengeance, and her hands and teeth look sharper than knives.


                                    Everyone
                                    Time to roll Initiative!
