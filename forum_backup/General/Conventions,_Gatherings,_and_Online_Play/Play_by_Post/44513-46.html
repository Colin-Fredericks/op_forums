                                                        Originally posted by Belkra View Post
                                                        This is the one I will bet on. I wonder if our bets have any indicator on what is true. I would suspect they don't and it is decided which rumors are true and which aren't before we even get to look at the list.
                                                        You are correct; I decide what's true and what's not while compiling the list. This is important, cause I have to look at the development of true rumors and untrue ones in an entirely different way.

                                                        True rumors are actually pretty easy for me to make! Usually, this involves think up something, someone or someplace new, or coming with something new about something extant. Then I just have to phrase it right, and bam! True rumor. Since what you've seen is just the tip of the iceburg of what I've come up with brain about the nations, people, etc., This is pretty damn easy. Frankly, the hard part is making sure that the true rumors aren't things that would be common knowledge.

                                                        Untrue rumors are more difficult. I have to think more about the places and ways that people interact in the setting, the constraints that established information impose on me, and the gaps in those interactions and constraints. More importantly, I have to think about how the people in the setting would view a certain place, person or thing, how they perceive it, the gaps in their knowledge, and the place it has in both their culture and their mythos. Being a Hellen-abu and having a Historian whose focus is classical Rome as a brother certainly help with this, but still, this can get pretty hard. Especially with places like Ordon, where I have to be careful about spilling, well, anything really.

                                                        Though... if a rumor is untrue and it gets a lot of attention from you guys, the WAY that it's untrue may change, based on what you zeroed in on. Also, I like seeing what you zero in on. It lets me see what things you think are neat, and that lets me shape the game in ways I think you may enjoy.
