                            "Fantastic! Let's go!"

                            Trish seems to alter her mood slightly, like she has been holding herself back a little but is now shifting into a more free and adventurous mood and throwing caution away.

                            She says to good bye to the old clerk, Giles, and askes about his health and family. He seems to be friendly with Trish even though they probably only met today. He also seems a little bit relived to be able to close up for the night.

                            Jack, Thomas, and Trish walk along the wet, cold winter streets. Trish walks quickly, taking long strides, and seems to be in a cheerful mood. The Spire looms large over South London, the towering pyramid growing larger and more menacing as they approach. Kusha and Berith can both feel the immense magical energy coming from the building, and marvel at what it must have cost the Ministry of Mammon to build such an obviously symbolic structure in the heart of the city. Both Jack and Thomas feel a little taken aback that Trish lives in this building, and seems so casual about it.

                            A door man outside gestures them away from the entrance, but Trish greets him warmly by name, Nigel, and the three enter. The interior is a lavish ultra modern design with rich red carpet, multi storied glass windows and wooden paneling, and large crystal chandeliers hanging from the cavernous ceiling. There is a welcome desk and a very tall Sikh man with a neatly groomed beard and long mustache wearing a Dastar. He might be able to give Jack lessons in ominous looming, but seems to warm slightly as Trish approaches.

                            "Good Evening Ms. Takehashi. Konbanwa." he says in a deep, courteous sounding voice.

                            "Salama Mr. Singh. Mokarimakka?

                            "Bochi-bochi denna. Will the lady be turning in for the evening?"

                            "No way! The night is young, Mr. Singh. Is the restaurant still open?"

                            "Yes of course, my lady. Mr. Salil and a business partner are up there now. Will these two" gesturing with his eyes to Jack and Thomas "be joining you as guests?". As he glares down at them, they both feel their unseen senses awaken, and Kusha feels the assault of someone trying to scan his mind.
