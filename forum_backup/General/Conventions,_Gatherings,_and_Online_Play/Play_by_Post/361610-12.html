                "It's not envy." Swimming Owl grumbled. "She's a goddess. That's cheating, is what that is. Never grows old so she doesn't have to worry about wrinkle lines, subsists on prayer so she always maintains her figure without any effort, she's a creature of essence so she doesn't have to exercise to keep her muscles firm...it's like being jealous of a mountain or a sunset for being beautiful, there's no point to it. I just wish they wouldn't shape themselves like people, y'know? Couldn't she be a twelve-foot high statue of burnished gold and crystal with flame for eyes and some wings or something, maybe wear some antlers like a proper deity?"

                She sighed.

                "Anyway, our major players in the area? Anita Fairhand there has a strong pull in the local court, which also includes Mahish Salar, Locke Varis, The Hand of Faith, and Fairman Naysayer. There's also Blackhawk. Not a member of local government, but he's got a lot of resources: he's managing the Guild's interest in the region, trying to turn the place into a client state...a profitable plan, but more for him than for me, and definitely something a lot of other people, including you lot I suspect, would have problems with. There's also the Lady of the Gray River, she's got a lot of influence in the city. There's a small temple here with a couple of priests who I've heard have some clout with the common people, and once you get out of town the local harvest god gets more and more popular as you head out into farmland."

                She thought for a moment.

                "With the Realm gone, I'd expect worship of the local gods to get more and more overt over time, and they willl a'll probably be interested in setting up a more direct form of control over their followers without the Immaculates as a threat. The Harvest god's probably not a bad guy to get in with, now that I think about it-the city relies on his good graces, even if they don't think about it too much yet, and he's probably gonna be a serious up-and-comer. Might be good to get in on the ground floor, like with you guys...hm. Speaking of hungry gods looking for an opportunity to make the big time, there's also a dark horse in the race. My sources," and here she glanced at Flynn, "think there's another god active in the area, but they have no idea who they are or what their name is, just that they're starting to become active in the region...oh! Also there's a rumor the Mask of Winters might be looking to expand his influence Southwest in this direction. With the Realm withdrawing, it's a much easier target than heading North and having to tangle with the Confederation of Rivers."

                So I'm making God-Kicking Boot, an Exalted webcomic, now. Updates on Sundays. Full-color, mediocre but slowly improving art. It's a thing.

                                                Flynn would never forget the first time he saw a tree in creation. The tree reached hundreds of feet into the sky and he couldn’t even put his small child arms around its trunk. The freshly manicured trees of Yu-Shan paled in comparison. When his guardians came to take him back to the endless city of gods, he sobbed and begged them to let him stay. He wanted to live in this magical forest of giant trees forever. Now, as an adult, the magic had gone out of the forest. One tree was much like another and Flynn was tired of walking through this forest.

                                                Fortunately, forest gave way to plains and swamp. Flynn grew quiet as the group neared their destination. The spirited and energetic Flynn was gone and replaced with a sullen and unwell looking Flynn. Flynn sensed the change in the air.

                                                A manse? Talos' home is a manse? He thought. For a moment, Flynn was homesick for his own manse. But his home was in Yu-Shan and he was in Creation on a mission and he would not fuck this up.

                                                As the putrid smell of the swamp hit his nose, his stomach rebelled against all the abuse he had put it under that afternoon. Flynn began to spew coffee and wine into a patch of grass outside of the small building.
