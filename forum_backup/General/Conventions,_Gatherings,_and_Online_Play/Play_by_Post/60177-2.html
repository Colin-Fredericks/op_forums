    "You boys don't know me very well, do ya? (And not knowing the Sun Dragon, little Bronze Tiger? For shame. Buy me a drink, and I'll tell you some tales that'll put some hair on yer chest) A little thing like this? If I were smaller, I'd have smashed it with my Dragon-Slaying Fist. Sadly, Hime'd yell at me if I used that. But, rest assured, I got ya covered," the elder Alchemical muttered, as rumblings within the ship erupted. Through the viewscreen, the Circle can see Zekka's gigantic arms moving through a short kata before thrusting forward.

    "Void-Shielding Stance," intones Zekka, and a large oscillating field of bluish-green essence flows out from his hands.

    "There, now, for the time being, no one can be affected by the Wyld, so long as you stay within me," Zekka said. If you want to go out, though, well..."

    With that, Zekka's starship body surges through the swirling waves of rainbows and clouds before him, until a great roaring, completely unlike before rocks the ship, and Zekka howls a curse.

    "What in blazes? THIS IS SOLID?" he roars, and the ship stops.

    "Checking status," Hoshi says, "Engineering is reporting that apart from the armor of the arms being damaged, all systems are operating. We musta struck something within the storm. Maybe an errant shard of Creation?"

    "Whatever it is, unless you all get me up, we can't leave," Zekka grumbles.

    "Scanning for hostile Fae activity," Hoshi calls, as the viewscreen shifts to cameras around the ship. The view seems to be the same pink-purple clouds as before, but now, gaggles of beings, as though someone had a rough idea of a person, and made it out of fog, appear pausing and chattering to one another, pointing at the ship.

    Well, this is a thing. What will you do now?

                            As Jin suits up and leaves the ship, he finds the ground to be springy, and the cloudfolk begin to cluster around her, chittering to one another.                                                             "Before you see Palanmas' small-body, I believe it would be best to tell you of her story. It may answer some questions you have.

                                                            You see...In the Beginning. There was Nothing, until the Divine Ignition awoke, and the Tribe of Dreamers realized the emptiness of their world.

                                                            "Let us build a land we can call our own," They decided, while Palanmas was still sleepy.

                                                            "I wish to sleep for five more minutes. Go ahead of me," she said, and wrapped herself in a blanket made of Unshaped. Well, five minutes became five aeons, as these things do, and finally, Palanmas awoke to find her fellows already gone ahead and made their land. Therefore, she took her four closest allies; the Thief of Worlds, Prince Balor, the Duke of Mirrors, and the Princess of the Glittering Train, and went to see her fellows. That's what's happening now. Well, until she paused. She's wondering what you all want, and that's why I am here," Jellivans said, as the book sailed over the cloudy landscape until stopping at a gigantic tower.

                                                            "I must caution you, though. If you know of what Palanmas is; She is not quite as...grown as the others. Her sleeping for so long has stunted her growth of power and insight, you see," Jellivans says, and opens the door.
