    Moonstone
    As you're walking and talking with the rest of the Stormborne, you're also keenly studying the neighborhood. While doing so, you notice something strange: the streets are unusually quiet for this time of day. On a normal weekday morning, there would be a string of cars on the street as people leave for work, especially for those who need to commute to Chicago or one of the other suburbs to the east. Your guess is that the traffic and pedestrian activity here is simply too quiet.


    Lynx
    As far as you can tell, this is just a pleasant suburban neighborhood. Nothing too exciting going on here, right? Something else catches your eye, though. You notice some odd flowering plants cropping up here and there in the lawns in front of people's houses on this street. They look kind of like bright purple dandelions. You only see a few at first, and they look so much like wildflowers or weeds that you didn't even notice them at first. But as your group walks further north along Edgewood Avenue, you see more and more of the plants on the lawns in front of the houses, and in some cases, actually growing on the trees lining the street. You might be able to study them better if you look at some close up.

    When you take a deep breath and open your Sight to the Primal Wild, you are taken aback at the ephemeral activity around you. There isn't just a single spirit here floating in Twilight; there are easily dozens, of various sizes and shapes. They seem intent on studying the odd flowers, but many of them are scuttling or floating to the north or east, heading down another nearby street like insects attracted to a bright light.


    Ouroboros Ophis
    A block or two ahead of you, on the quiet street you notice two different people going about their business. Nothing seems odd at first glance, but you look back and realize that both individuals are moving extremely slowly. They seem lethargic and tired. One is a man probably in his mid-30s, wearing a suit and tie with a briefcase in his hand. He's shuffling his feet but barely moving from his front door to his car parked in the driveway; he looks like the might fall asleep or fall over at any time. The other is a young child, a girl perhaps around 8 years old. She's sitting on a bicycle with her head plopped down and resting on her arms, which are lazily crossed on top of the handlebars. Every minute or so, she idly yawns and rings the bell on her bike. She doesn't even look in your direction as your cabal walks past her house.

    By Seeing the world through the lens of Death, you can tell that both individuals are alive and have intact souls. You also see several ghosts wandering the streets. That doesn't surprise you like it used to, but something seems very odd about their behavior. First, they are moving very quickly and energetically, and with a purpose that the other ghosts you've seen before were seemingly lacking. Secondly, they are all generally moving in the same direction, further north and east towards a nearby part of the neighborhood. Finally, and perhaps most surprisingly, you don't sense any Anchors around here for any of the ghosts. You know from experience that without an Anchor in the area, ghosts will rapidly lose Essence and ultimately cross over to the Underworld. So why are these ghosts ignoring their own "survival" instincts of their afterlife to come here? And where are they going?


    Ragnar
    Something about this place just feels off to you. This isn't something you are detecting on your Periphery; it's just an intuition that something around here seems odd. As you look around for the source of that feeling, you realize that every house on this street has its doors, curtains, shutters, or blinds closed. You feel stifled just being in the proximity of this street. The people around here are either remarkably private, or very withdrawn.

                        Ragnar
                        You run through a few ideas in your head, using your budding knowledge of Supernal magic and the arcane, and generate a few possible working hypotheses.

                        There's the possibility that the magic here was created by a much powerful mage, but you know that Mathias' spells can still give you that odd feeling on your Periphery of your Sight. Perhaps this mage, if one is indeed involved, also used some way to mask or obscure the source of his magic? But you're not even sure if this is caused by a mage or a spell; for all you know, some ephemeral entity could have caused this.

                        You definitely could be seeing the aftermath of a spell. You know that you only detect active effects on your Periphery, not passive ones, so you might need to enhance the focus of your Sight to detect or learn more.

                        There's also the possibility that you're too far away from the source to detect the phenomenon directly. Your Sight only picks up on supernatural effects within the nearby vicinity. With Ouroboros Ophis' spell on you, you can now visibly see the collection of energized ghosts moving further to the north and east, heading down the street to the right and then turning north again. Perhaps they know something you don't.


                        Ouroboros Ophis
                        Studying the small dandelion-like plants with your mundane senses shows that these flowers look remarkably like any other plant. Their apparent biology is natural, other than for their unusual color.

                        With your arcane senses, you are able to discern more useful information to help you better understand this phenomenon. Unsurprisingly, your Matter Sight reveals nothing about these living objects, but you are surprised to see through the lens of Death a faint wisp of energy leaking out of the plant. Are they leaking...Essence?

                        Your Life Sight further reveals that there are no apparent toxins or diseases in this plant. If you had to guess, they would be safe to eat. The flowers are thriving, suffused with energy -- more lively than just alive.

                        Opacity of this Mystery is 4.


                        Moonstone
                        As you approach her, the little girl barely moves. She doesn't react when you move to squat in front of her other than to ring her bell again and move her eyes to track you. She has a confused look on her face. She blinks when you touch her forehead.

                        Opening your Sight reveals that there are no scrying windows or other odd phenomena related to the domain of Space, so at least there is nothing supernatural trapping these people in this location. You can tell at a glance using your Mind Sight that both people here, including the girl, are incredibly lethargic mentally, comparable to a state of relaxed daydreaming but not yet asleep. Your companions, in contrast, all appear focused and alert.

                        Channeling your will into your magic, you envision the smoke and brimstone of Pandemonium swirling around the girl and funneling into two different directions, to the past and the future. You follow the movements of the haze and divine the following: If nothing changes, she will be comatose or lifeless. If removed from this area, her energy will gradually return.

                        Scanning the girl's mind reveals half-formed ideas, sluggish thinking, and an overpowering urge to nap. The best way to describe it is excessive sleepiness. She simply isn't thinking much, or very clearly. She's struggling to form words, but you catch the intent in her mind despite her never opening her mouth: Who are you? and What's wrong with my mom and dad?

                                    I'm modifying your spell because I think the cost needs to be reflected differently in your spell factors. By using a Space Knowing spell in this way, you're essentially modifying Locate Object, but using it to find a specific location rather than an object. I think that the primary factor should be Duration, and the spell gives you an innate sense of the destination as long as it lies within the Area of the spell; if the destination is outside of the Area, you know the most direct route to it. This makes it comparable to the Pathfinder Numen, which makes more sense to me thematically.

                                    The ghost's Numen gives you the Obsession condition until you accomplish its mission, which is to travel to the heart of the Mystery nearby. Until then, any spell cast or action you take that does not directly relate to finding or traveling to this location loses the 10-again quality; any action taken to pursue the mission grants 9-again.
