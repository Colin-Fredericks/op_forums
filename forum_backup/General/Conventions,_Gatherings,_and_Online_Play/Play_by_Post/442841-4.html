                            CHALLENGE ACCEPTED

                            Along the old waterfront (I just realized this area is totally not taken guys, voting we set-up shop)


                            Seas-Luck - A spirit that first came about in the early last century as blue-collar sailors and dockworkers found themselves put out of work. When Prohibition came down they took a personal affront to the law and saw a way to make good money at the same time. No one knew the old coves and caves that dotted the coast like them and they quickly set up shop in one such sea cave, the site of a locus, that led almost right to the speakeasy most it was brought to. Right there in that old cave where these felt at home again with the sounds of the water and the smell of salt the spirit was born out of the mixture of emotions from wealth, to trade, to camaraderie but most of all the desire for the sea. Over time as the spirit and the people coming to this little section of waterfront grew the sailors and bar master unknowingly influenced it further as it influenced them. Soon they came to see that sweet drink arriving on the waves as being given almost by the sea itself and the speakeasy ran with the notion, entertaining its customers with "gifts from our old sea nymph". The people visiting every were mostly locals they all knew each other and were grateful to relax in such troubled times and they made strangers welcome. What agents their were that could have reported it became regulars themselves, let the dangerous mobs in Chicago be rounded up and put behind bars, there little place was no danger to anybody. Soon the spirit found itself rooted in this sense of community.

                            Like most good times though these came crashing down. On Black Friday, the day the stock market crashed a terrible storm tore through the area reminding everyone that the sea was not always so kind. This wrath also affected the spirit and as the wealth brought in by the illegal liqueur was closed with the ending of the Prohibition the spirit lashed out, causing the men and women who had been working together for over ten years to split apart. The people left and the speakeasy closed down, becoming another empty husk on a street lined with those begging for work and food. Times changed and the entire coast was renovated in the modern era now the only ways to find the cave are through its mouth into the sea and the old entrance in the forgotten speakeasy that is now just a closed bar. The spirit lingers still, a force to be reckoned with in the shadow despite the times, surviving off the essence from the locus and what little it could get from the people who still came. It is not satisfied however, it desires a community again, it desires yearning for the sea again. A pack of werewolves once would have been a possible threat to keep an eye on, now, however, perhaps they could give the spirit the means to reclaim itself.

                            I find the notion of a church really cool as well, and this area I think is awesome, but I also want to leave Indy something pretty unique to explore with her character rather than us use it as a base. I'm also worried about the kind of spirit we would get there, as it will want us to foster its elements, and any spirit associated with fire is a risk. If we really want to keep a church perhaps it could be what the speakeasy was rebuilt to that quickly went under. This way we have a modernish building that wont require massive renovation costs, and with a little exploring we find the one thing every pack needs (a locus) and one hell of a rocking basement. One tiny problem though, there's a resident spirit, and its not in the best of moods. Then again, we need a totem, and this spirit is strong and old. It knows much of the area and how to influence others. Territory, a base, and a totem, is this where our pack can claim its own?                             I dunno, here's my two cents, let me know what you guys think.
