                                        Phoenix (and Moonstone, if he follows)
                                        You chase after the woman, bolting along the hallway as quickly as you can manage. The hallway continues to slope upwards for several more yards, then leads to the base of a short staircase. Racing up the stairs brings you to a large set of double doors, seemingly made out of polished onyx. The doors are cracked just slightly, and you hear a faint cry from the "woman" beyond the doors.

                                        When you push the doors open, you see a long corridor made from the same material as the door. Upon closer inspection, the material seems unlike any stone or building material you have ever seen. On your Periphery, you feel a faint, ubiquitous sense of energy pulsing in this room.

                                        As you take your first step through the door, the walls begin to glow with millions of pinpricks of light, arranged in dazzling displays that you recognize as galaxies and constellations of stars. The view is breathtaking.

                                        The woman's voice echoes quietly through the room. WE THOUGHT TO RULE THE HEAVENS, she repeats. Her voice sounds forlorn.

                                        You may each roll Wits + Composure.


                                        Ragnar, Lynx, and Dr. Asphodel
                                        I'm going to assume for now that all three of you continue along this path. If anyone would do otherwise or wants to take actions along the way, feel free to do so.

                                        You follow the draft forward, passing underneath the unnatural archway and stepping onto the smoothly polished stone-like material that fits no description of any building material you have ever seen before. Where your feet touch the stone, you feel a faint pulsing of energy, and the floors emit a pale, swirling green light that softly illuminates the area. Each step also creates a quiet bell tone, like wind chimes moving from a soft breeze.

                                        After a few moments, you feel another draft moving forward. The light from the floors reveals an opening in the cave wall ahead of you, leading to another hallway. The corridor slopes down, gently at first but at an increasingly steep incline, and you have to move slowly to avoid slipping in the dark on the moss underneath your feet. You lose your sense of direction as the corridor drifts right, curves around, then left, but always descending.

                                        Suddenly, the corridor levels out and turns into a flat surface again. More green light appears beneath your feet as you find yourself yet again on that strange polished stone surface. After walking for several minutes, the corridor suddenly opens up into a large cavern with a smooth dome ceiling. The walls in here are again made of natural, rough stone, but the floors have that same polished look and create light as you walk. All around the large room, perhaps 200 feet across in each direction, you can faintly make out other openings leading to other hallways.

                                        Each opening to this cavern, including the one you walk through, have a path leading forward to a raised dais in the center of the room. A small candle flickers on the table besides what appears to be a large stone bowl and several other small objects that you can't make out at this distance.

                                        You may each roll Wits + Composure, at no penalty.
