                        Oh, make no mistake: I definitely enjoy gaming near the free form end of the spectrum. I think that helped this game be as fun and interesting as it wound up being.* There are difficulties, as you note, but if everyone's on the same page about things -- and I get the sense that most C:tL players sitting down to play out a Durance are tacitly agreeing that their characters will see no small amount of hardship -- it tends to work very, very nicely.

                        * And quicker and smoother! I do enjoy dice in some contexts, but I've observed they can sometimes bog things down a bit or inject an unnatural break in the action. In PbP, where things already default to slower than most would prefer, every little bit can make a big difference. Aside from all that: the game's in Arcadia! It's appropriate that things depend less on die-borne random determination.
