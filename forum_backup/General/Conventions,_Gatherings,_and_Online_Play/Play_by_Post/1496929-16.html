                                                With the howls of briarwolves in the distance—-a sad, angry, confused whine—-the two head onwards, til they come across a fast-moving river with a single wooden bridge across. Sitting on a stool in front of said bridge is a huge, lumpen humanoid—-with a pig’s head, a ram’s horns and a horse’s hooves for feet. It wears mismatched clothes several sizes too small for it, and it cradles in its arms a lamppost broken free from the ground, with a chunk of concrete on one end used as a mallet.

                                                Seeing you approach, the giant heaves himself to his hooves. “Ay. Wut we got now? A pott’d plant an’ a lil brother, a runt too. Pay me toll, or piss off.”
