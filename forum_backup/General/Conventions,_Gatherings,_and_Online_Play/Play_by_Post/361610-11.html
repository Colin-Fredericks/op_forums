    Noone seems to be able to tell that Flynn is drunk. He may seem just a bit happy due to the parade that was until just a few moments ago following him around.

    The smell of quite delicious bread fills the small area around each of you. It just seems way too appetizing to leave there for any longer.

    In the background, many people are continuing to celebrate and drink. It appears that yet another guildsman has come with a cart of alcohol and is selling his wares almost instantly. Many stalls are clearing out and the business district seems to be quite a bit less busy than before. It appears that many craftsman are having trouble selling their wares, while others are making a BIG profit off of many drunk patrons. Everyone seems to have forgotten about the mini-earthquakes that had been happening and for some bizarre reason have not even gone to investigate the fallen tree. (I guess celebrating too much can do that.)

    A moment later, a shout of "She's here! She's here!" echoes around the area. A small group has started to gather to look at "her".

    That stunt was worth a two-die stunt Exthalion; please mark 1 extra experience for this story session. You of course can also get the normal mote or WP recovery. (That was a good song btw)

    Noone has truly noticed that Flynn is drunk, but are still a bit suspicious. You may make an inv+perception roll or a social+manip roll to look further into it if you wish.

                    01-27-2015, 06:34 PM
                    Flynn successfully climbs up the pile of boxes to see a very tall woman with light brown hair. Her hair seemed to float to the side as if it was being blown back among an eternal never-ending breeze. When the light of the daystar shined brightly upon her hair, it looked like a golden brown crown appeared from her hair. Even from a distance, you can tell that her skin was very smooth and had no visible imperfections.

                    From his viewpoint, Flynn could only see the left half of her face from behind, but he could tell that it was perfectly proportioned (according to the perception of many men in the town anyway). As she made a quick glance behind her, Flynn could see her intense, blue eyes that seemed to radiate with their own light.

                    She donned some of the finest looking plate armor underneath a jacket that seemed like it was made out of many silken scarves woven together. She also had a beautiful bangle on her left wrist that seemed to have a ruby and an emerald embedded in it.

                    As she passed into the courthouse, the crowd's gaze followed her until they had to stop. She didn't seem to be bothered by the fact. In fact, she seemed like she was contemplating something major as she looked upon the crowd herself.
