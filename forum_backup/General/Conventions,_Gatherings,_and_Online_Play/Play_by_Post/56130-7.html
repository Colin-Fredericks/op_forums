            1) The technologies that you know are in use throughout the Spiral (Guns, essence radios, powered flight, etc.). However, much like how the Exalted use esoteric technologies for their individual weapons, many Peers and Untouchables use demontech much like how the MoEP: Infernals illustrates. However, this is all with that caveat that the Spiral is a massive place, and the technological levels of each planet may very well vary, with stone age servant races all the way up to technologically advanced Chosen who may put the Celestial Exalted to shame with their toys.
            2) You know that, as far as the Deliberative can gather, there are about 600 Titanic Exalted, in about 45 Castes, each corresponding to a single Titan. In addition, there also Chosen of several new behemoth-goddesses called the Yazatas. The Yazatas are six in number, and they maintain the Khotep Tablet, a hypothetical name for a Fate Engine that would operate on the same function as the Loom of Fate in maintaining Causality. The Chosen of the Yazatas are unknown in number, but they seem to be approximately Celestial in power, although their encounters with the Gunstar fleets are few and far between.

            That is, unless you're referring to Enien herself, well, then you may have to ask her.

                        @Jotun: I'm not really trying to draw you either way. I'm just offering a potential way you all can gallivant around the Spiral and not feel railroaded. As for a jungle planet, there is one. It's one of the two planets nearby on the Western Arm.

                        @Magnusar: The numbers are due to the sheerly impossible size I feel the Spiral is. The Titans need a lot of Chosen to maintain it/ fight their war for them, and without the Maidens, things got more weird. True, the Titans COULD maintain Reality, but screw that. Hence, the large number of Titanic Exalts and the existence of the Yazatas and their Chosen. Now, to answer your questions:

                        1) The scientific and alchemical processes of creating and maintaining powerful manses are still maintained, so you have those processes if you'd want to do that.
                        2) All planets have Dragon Lines, along with even moons and dead asteroids. The ability to harness their power, though, may be more difficult than with others.
                        3) There are mundane starships, much as in Vanilla, there are mundane aircraft. These ships are slower and more unwieldy in their maneuvering, but they do exist, and if you want to move between planets, these are probably your best bet of doing so.
