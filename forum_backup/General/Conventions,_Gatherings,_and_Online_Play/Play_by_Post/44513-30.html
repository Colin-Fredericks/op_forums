                The ghost is Zophyrus Linon. He has been Pyrtaneis (essentially the Master of the Floor for the Boule) for over a decade. He was the Archon before Larissa, until his untimely demise thirty-two years ago. It is indeed his job to adhere to the agenda, and him being a ghost makes him more likely to do so. It's one of the reasons that they skirt Origenes' edict of "no temporal power for the dead and the divine" (though it technically doesn't violate the edict since the Pyrtaneis has no actual authority outside of enforcing proper conduct in the Boule).

                Anyways, the objects that were on the agenda before the present, were the renewal of the nation leasing the Academy grounds from Gens Nikoloi. That took up about twenty minutes, and was mostly arguing about the percentage adjustment that would go into it for inflation.

                Next on the agenda was clearly a persistent issue, in which the elderly Nikoloi Argyrus presented fro half an hour on why the nation should increase the roads budget by 250%, and consecrate all of the roads to Mercury and Origenes, turning the entire nation into a mandala therefore increasing the effects of prayer in the nation by 13% and increasing travel along the roads by 16%. This segues into another half-hour in which the Pyrtaneis and Argyrus argue over the issue. You can tell that this is something that has been going for a while, partially due to the strange voice that Argyrus uses when addressing the Pyrtaneis.

                The Augurs made a twenty minute speech on the oncoming Spring-Harvest Little Gods festival, asking the Gentes for donations and volunteers.

                The local guild factor petitioned the Boule to reduce trade tariffs. This sparked a brief, but fierce debate, which was tabled as soon as Nikoloi Argyus brought out some pie-charts to explain that the guild factor actually had some good points. All in all, this took around thirty-five minutes.

                The Gens Midea tanning association petitioned the Boule to alter zoning laws, as their current location forces them to dump their waste directly into the river. This is causing complaints by the local water elementals and fish gods. Their petition was quickly considered, and a sub-committee was formed to investigate the issue. This took another twenty minutes.

                Then Larissa et al got the floor.

                The procedure is thus: You have the floor, so you can make a speech or petition or both to the Boule or Citizenry. I would advise against petitioning; Eugene's political clout would likely help whatever propositions pass far easier than if it came from you (perhaps that might explain why they decided to bracket you like that, huh?). Being polite is advised, but as Argyus showed is not actually necessary.
