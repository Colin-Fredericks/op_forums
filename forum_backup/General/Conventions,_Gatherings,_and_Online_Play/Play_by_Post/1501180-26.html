                            My previous post was consumed by the system, but it should be back now.

                            In the meantime, one of the best descriptions that I heard for the Théarchs: The Silver Ladder are priest and humanity is their deity. They want YOU to be exalted, powerful, wise, prosperous and happy. They want humanity to never bend the knee to any gods or monsters. They want to build systems to ensure everyone gets a chance to climb as high up the ladder of creation as they can. They want to throw the gates of the Supernal wide open and make everyone cosmic lords (minus the psychopaths). They are happy to take on the burden of leadership to make it happen, to do the diplomacy, logistics, economics or whatever else is needed to get you there. Some will focus on motivating people to reach higher in life via Cryptopolis, driving them to push against the limits of the Lie. Some might study the Socratic method to become great mentors and councilors. Could that lead down the path of Hubris ? Certainly, but that is the price of ambition.
