                                            Sorry, didn't see this until now. The only time I've seen the term used in any context of exalted is Counting Coup, a Lunar dueling tradition that was started in the First Age by the Swords of Luna, and later adopted by the rest of the Silver Pact as a means of non-lethally settling disputes.

                                            So basically, a non-lethal duel. Usually to first blood.
