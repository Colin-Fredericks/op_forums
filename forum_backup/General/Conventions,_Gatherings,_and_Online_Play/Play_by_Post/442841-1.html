                                This is a character concept I never got to run with, as the game folded before it got going. That said, I'm not completely sure it'd work here. The game was set in California and the research I did was into the Atsugewi tribe. Still, I think the core of it can work, and I'll present it here. He'd be a Rahu Hunter in Darkness.

                                Appearance

                                Aside from facial features that show his family's native origins, Akule doesn't tend to stand out in a crowd for the most part, preferring casual and reliable clothes that can manage a good amount of wear and tear. His hair is cropped short with a suggestion that he does it himself in front of a mirror, and has little caring for the rather uneven results.

                                Personality

                                Akule has been a rather defensive, often quite hostile young man since his First Change and the resulting death of a family member. While he's far from incapable of interacting with others, he's fairly wary of it and tends to keep it as limited as he can, with his current lack of trust in himself.

                                What it has done is to harden his nature, though also provided the more positive merit of seeing him care about others he sees at risk, a desire to see some good be done closely tied to that.

                                Background

                                Akule isn't sure whether or not his name fully matches the history of the Atsugewi tribe, but neither are his parents. However, in a small hint of cultural rebellion they settled on a name for him that didn't have such strong Anglican roots and made him stand out more. At times he enjoyed this, but at others, he found frustration at being put in a position that made him stand out. Still, that was far from his biggest issue.

                                Spirits do not at all like the wolfblooded and Akule was tormented throughout his childhood by unseen forces that were a puzzle and concern both for him, and the community he grew up in. Ceremonies he took part in were disrupted in small, apparently petty ways and a number of his efforts to connect with others both inside and outside of his local, small community also tended to end badly, to the point where certain, mean spirited others began calling him 'Runs-with-Coyote' behind his back. This did not at all improve his moods as he grew older.

                                Born and raised in <City>, he wound up in a number of incidents as he grew older, culminating in a series of rough fights that got him a sharp warning from both his own elders and the police that interrupted the street based tussle. Sixteen, that time he managed to avoid juvenile court, with a warning he would be unlikely to have that luck next time. In an effort to work out some of his tension, his uncle started to take him on camping trips during the holiday periods with other youth who had an interest in exploring historical practices. They hunted, explored and listened to stories in a refreshing break from the mundane nature of day to day life.

                                Until four years later, where Akule's uncle died.

                                Akule only has scattered memories of the time, but it's enough for him to know what truly happened there. He and Jonas had been tracking a deer, the man having decided to use it as an excuse to try and get to the bottom of his nephew's recent, excessive mood swings. However, the second an arrow pierced the deer's side and spilled blood on the earth, Akule saw red.

                                He remembers his uncle screaming for mercy, the blood on his claws and the red, ugly rage. Finer details escape him, but when it was over, Jonas was very much dead and Akule was huddled on the ground in a naked, bloody, weeping heap. The others concluded the two of them had been attacked by a dangerous predator, and Akule's garbled account of events was put down to the harrowing of his experience and witnessing his uncle's death. Aware of the far worse truth, Akule almost immediately left his family and friends behind and retreated into his own place in the city, cutting off contact with his previous life and taking up a low paying job as a butcher.

                                It was in that self imposed exile that others of the Forsaken would find him.
