                            Khvarenah would have a Medium connection to the statue he created, and could use the chunk of statue as a Material-level sympathy yantra. When you're using yantras to cast at sympathetic range (rather than sensory/touch when the target is right there), they take up a yantra slot but don't provide extra dice.

                            It's going to be relevant soon, so here's the Resources/Availability rules. Once per chapter, you can expend the Resources merit to purchase an item or service with Availability equal to your Resources dots or dots-1. You can instead buy something of your Resources+1, but you have to reduce your Resources dots by 1 for the rest of the month. If something costs at least 2 dots less than your Resources, you can buy it without expending Resources for the chapter.

                            For example, if you have Resources 3, you can buy as many Availability 1 items as you want, or you can expend the Merit for the chapter to buy one thing of Availability 2 or 3. Or you can buy something of Availability 4, and then have Resources 2 for the rest of the month.

                            Instead of using Resources, if you have an appropriate Skill at a rating equal to or greater than Availability, you can procure the item with a single roll of Attribute+Skill-Availability. What counts as an appropriate skill depends on the context and how exactly you plan to get the item or service, but Larceny and Streetwise are usually good bets. You might also be able to convince someone to help with your relevant Animal Ken knowledge, or secure a precious item as a gift after formally Socializing with the owner.

                                                            BTW: How do you want to handle Creative Thaunaturgy? Do you want us to post it here prior to trying to use it or just throw it into the IC thread and hope for the best?

                                                            Below is how I handle it in my games.

                                                            If you decide to cast a spell on the fly then the spell could backfire or just simply not work. There is a whole slew of things the could go wrong. This free form magic is up to my purview alone with no explanation as to how I came to that conclusion.

                                                            If you want to develop a new spell then you should use the OOC thread to discuss it prior to needing it. That is less on the fly and represents a study into the workings of that spell, you have worked the kinks out.

                                                            My view on Spells and Creative Thaumaturgy. This is where I will dissect the "On the Fly" spell and see if it works.
                                                            Arcanum: Does this spell fall under the purview of the Arcanum used. This is a simple succeed/fail.

                                                            Description: The body of the text is everything, with the description you are setting the imago. I will try to determine the raw feel of the text and not look at intent. This is where the spell has the biggest chance of going wrong. This is more likely to cause the spell to go haywire then to cause the spell to fail.

                                                            Practice: This is used to shape the imago into the spell, adding the wrong practice to the description is the second biggest change of causing the spell to fail. This is more of a succeed/fail than the spell going haywire.

                                                            Withstand: If you didn't assign a withstand and I feel that it should have this then the spell could be less effective then you had hoped. This is not a succeed/fail or a haywire thing, it just makes the spell less effective.

                                                            Rote Skills: This does not apply as it isn't a rote. It could be learned as a rote later with study and perfecting the spell.

                                                            Reaches If every thing else works out then the spell is cast. However if I feel that your intent is outside the effects of the spell I may add a reach to the spell and reroll the dice. This is where Paradox can slip in. Since this is going off your intent I will ask you what your intent for the spell was, if you haven't already stated it, and see if it matches the spell. This is where your input matters as it judges your intent.
