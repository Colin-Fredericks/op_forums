                        Meanwhile... The North End

                        "Did you ever notice how, in the Bible, whenever God needed to punish someone, or make an example, or whenever God needed a killing, he sent an angel? Did you ever wonder what a creature like that must be like? A whole existence spent praising your God, but always with one wing dipped in blood. Would you ever really want to see an angel?" - Thomas Daggett, The Prophecy

                        The Titan Mark 5, the Breaker, perches atop a building. He is watching a couple of gangsters muttering to one another, discussing how to deal with this guy, this one guy, who keeps hitting their club houses, who keeps cracking heads and laying out anyone who gets in his way. They're still talking about what he did to those boys at the train yard. Limbs torn clean off, bodies hurled through box cars. Who the hell can do something like that? What the hell can do something like that?

                        He doesn't understand why they aren't getting the message (what isn't there to get?), especially since he has made it so clear. If you step out of line in the Breaker's neighbourhood, then you find out why he got the name. It was easy to do what he did, anyone who knew how could have done it, the human body is a delicate instrument, when you get down to it, and Mark 5 is the fist of a god, shattering everything in his path. The Titan's wrath could level buildings, if he desired it. His Alembics filled to the brim with the Flame Immortal, Mark 5 is invulnerable, unstoppable, invincible. What do these petty things of flesh and bone, who are as broken as the Unborn, think they can do to him?

                        People avert their eyes when he calls on them for aid, now. They know what he can do, but they are glad that it is directed at those who deserve it, and not at them. The Breaker is a wrathful guardian angel, an avenger of injustices. If downtrodden youth aren't smart enough to stay away from those who trade them a sense of belonging for their lives, then by God they are going to stay away from the human predators who take advantage by fear alone.

As a result of the horrific actions taken against IP, Mark 5 gains the Tormented Condition. Mark 5 also gains a Beat from Tormented due to having killed some humans while in Torment. You can also make a Wits + Composure roll to assess the situation further.

                                    Scout

                                    It is easy enough for the potent Azoth of the Originist to find sympathy with Mark 5. It just took a bit of the Osirian's attention to start on the trail. It's a long walk up Saint Mary's road through Norwood, and across the Queen Elizabeth Bridge that crosses the point where the Red and Assiniboine Rivers meet and mix before diverging again. It's a dusty road, one that takes them past the Forks, past the towering gothic edifice of the Fort Gary Hotel, past the artistically rusted iron-workings of the Upper Fort Gary Historical Park, past the limestone opulence of Union Station and the looming, squared-off government buildings, and past the Exchange District. The better part of two hours passes as they make their way towards the errant Titan.

                                    Mark 5

                                    You can hear them whispering about inducting a new member, a scrawny-looking kid with a badly tied bandana, and debating about whether they can risk the usual method of making the pledge into a Striker. The usual method, as you know from interrupting several initiatory 'missions', is arson, armed robbery, or homicide. Commit a violent felony, do it in front of witnesses. It means the pledge hands over blackmail material to their direct superior, trauma bonds them with other members of the gang, and it demonstrates they can follow orders. Very nasty. Very effective. The leader of the group, a shaven-headed Warrior with a red bandana tied across his face and bloody arrows tattooed on his forearms, suggests they go with an older method. The Strikers, who you can see have IP tattooed into their necks or the backs of their hands, glance at one another, but they nod, steel in their expressions.

                                    The three of them turn on the fourth, laying him out with brutal punches to the upper body, and when the kid drops, kicking him as he curls up into a ball to protect himself. He is enduring the worst of it, just lying there, and it's hard to tell if he's unconscious or just letting it happen. They want to see if he can take the pain. They want to see if he can endure.

                                    They want to see if he's made of Iron.

                                                            Mark 5

                                                            The Titan sails off the roof and lands heavily on the asphalt, heavy-soled boots making a flat smack on the pavement. There's a slight jarring shock to his knees as he absorbs the impact, but it's nothing that will prevent him from being to fight. His Azoth seethes as the Flux-taint of Torment sets it boiling. He is merciless, and that suits Flux just fine. It destroys, it breaks down, and isn't that the first step to making something new in the first place? The Titan says it's for the Mission, but is it really? What does this have to do with protecting Earth? What does this have to do with being a Soldier? Is this all a Soldier is, a killer, a weapon pointed at acceptable targets by the Powers That Be? He doesn't have much time to contemplate as the three gangsters standing let out terrified yelps and jump backwards.

                                                            One of them goes for a gun in the waistband of his jeans, pulling it with a frantic motion, the loud click of the hammer acting like the first punch of a stopwatch. Who finishes the fight first? Mark 5, the Titan, the Breaker, or these three gangsters? Two of them make as though to run, but there won't be any escaping. How will they get the message otherwise? Evil should be punished. Even as he leaps towards his targets, the Titan can feel the encroaching Azothic Radiance of his friends, and the strongest of these, Scout's, reaches out to his, trying to burn the Torment from him. He doesn't want to get rid of it, he needs it, it helps him do what must be done.

                                                            OOC: Mark 5 takes three bashing damage from the fall, reduces by one due to the Dexterity + Athletics roll, suffering a total of two bashing damage. Declare an Intent and roll Strength + Brawl, we're resolving this as Down & Dirty Combat. Gangster with the gun rolls Dexterity + Firearms for two successes, the escaping gangsters roll Dexterity + Athletics for three successes. You need to get four or more successes on the roll to handle all of them. Also roll Resolve + Azoth to contest Scout's Sublimation by Fire.

                                                            The Throng

                                                            A single gunshot rings out, and there are cries of pain that echo from buildings into the dreary late afternoon sky. The Breaker is at it again, but at what cost, to him or to others? The Throng starts to make their way towards the noise, moving with urgency, planning going out the window, when a rather disheveled looking man, eyes wide, staggers towards them, waving wildly with his arms and yelling with the slurring voice of the drunk. Somewhere in the background, a beer can blows in the wind and rolls under a bus shack bench. He lurches towards Earth, howling obscenities at the top of his lungs.

                                                            "YOU GODDAMN FUCKING BITCH! WITCHU DOING BACK HERE?! SAID I'D FUCKING KILL YOU IF I SAW YOU AGAIN BITCH! YOU AND ALL YO FREAK FRIENDS!"

                                                            She can see the flickering, like flames, dancing in his bloodshot eyes. Her Azoth has scorched this community hard as she scourges the criminal organizations that plague them with whips of divine lightning and hands that can tear cripple, rip, and tear with the corrosive Flux-taint of her own Torment. People are peeking out of windows and screaming down at the Throng. They heard the gunshot, and now they see a heated argument involving death threats. The police will be called, and the right questions probably won't be asked. Time to get Mark 5 out and go.
