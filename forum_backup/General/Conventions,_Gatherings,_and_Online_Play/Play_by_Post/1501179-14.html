                Clean Sweep looks troubled. "Hyleen? Nasty place.                 "Hawk! Rude!" Clean Sweep gasps, aghast

                "Sweep! Oblivious!" Nighthawk snaps back, gesturing at the two young Mages. "They're not equipped to see Twilight. I doubt they'd be as calm as they are if they could see my companions." She makes an odd motion with one hand, as iff scratching the chin if an unseen pet.

                "I... suppose not... But still! Don't cast on people uninvited!" He sighs as Nighthawk shrugs indifferently. "Anyway... the Lex. Officially, the Lex has no jurisdiction over Legacies, which would be an Iron Law. No Iron Law on Legacies here, beyond which ones we've declared Anathema. White Sand, if I remember what I head at the last Grand Convention, only requires that every initiate be given a full brief on the benefits and drawbacks of the Legacy by a practitioner of at least Adept rank.                         Elpis takes a moment to sigh, before continuing.

                        "Not every Consilium welcomes the Elan, some have driven us out, and declared us Left Handed. The Legacy at large believes that this reaction will fade given time, but they say that the only way for consilii to accept us requires new initiates to have the guidance and support of established Elans. To initiate with a soul stone, or to go to Lilith, would be seen as stealing the legacy's secrets. Even with Pandora's prior approval, I had not been fully initiated. It would be seen as taking advantage of her death, to seize knowledge before I was acknowledged as ready. If I had good intentions, why wouldn't I seek out other Elan to learn from? It might have been understandable, given that I was not aware of the locations of any other Elans who could teach me, and it might have been begrudgingly accepted. But that was before Voter Ferdiad contacted me and gave me his card. While his sponsorship wouldn't guarantee my initiation into the Legacy, it would be extremely likely. Now, if I learned the Legacy from an outside source, I would be insulting his offer, and implicitly the structure of the Legacy as a whole. Even if I found another Elan who would teach me, I would still be likely to upset him. So, now the only way I can see to not anger anyone would be to go to him...or Pandora's Echo."

                        "I guess I'm a bit surprised at the Legacy being in White Sands with that Iron Law. While we don't mind talking about some things, the Legacy likes keeping some of its practices secret until one is fully initiated. Maybe there are secrets kept known only to the Masters Not even I know exactly what trials I might face in initiation, apart from the Summoning of a Supernal Entity of Mind or Prime. While showing off isn't forbidden...it's a bit out of character for what Pandora told me of the Legacy. Maybe they have some other reason for being there?"
