                            10-20-2016, 10:34 AM
                            Originally posted by Kvitebjørn Kong Valemon View Post
                            rwknoll Since we're in downtime mode IC, I was wondering what you think about spellcasting in downtime? If the spell to be cast doesn't require answers from the DM (e.g. Fate or Time spells) and doesn't have any consequence of failure, is there a reason to roll for them or can I just say the spell was cast eventually over the course of a morning hour or similar?

                            E.g., Lynx wanted to cast Life 2 "Body Control" on himself the next morning. With free reach he'd have Advanced Duration and Instant Casting, and to keep the dice pool above 0, he can do Potency 2. He'd have a paradox risk-free spell with 2 dice. Since regular failure would give no consequences at all, can I say Lynx manages to cast and maintain the spell, or do I have to do the rolls in character?
                            Great question, and one I don't think we've addressed here yet. If you're in a tense or time-limited situation, you can make consecutive attempts on a roll but at -1 penalty for each consecutive attempt. However, you don't get penalized for rolls that are free of time pressure.

                            Let's go with this: If the spell is instant cast and does not risk Paradox, and the dice pool is above 0, I'd like you to at least roll it on the dice roller once. If it fails, you can "take 1" -- basically assume you eventually cast it with 1 success, or keep rerolling if preferred. If the spell is cast as a ritual, I'd like you to roll each time; you'll take -1 consecutive rolls on the ritual because it takes up time, so there's time pressure and implications of failing the roll. For spells that risk Paradox, I'd like you to roll each attempt because of the cumulative bonuses to the Paradox roll.

                            Spells that fit multiple categories above use both sets of rules. So for example, instant-cast spells that risk Paradox will have the additional +1 Paradox per roll, but no cumulative -1 penalty to the roll itself. Ritual-cast spells that risk Paradox will have cumulative -1 penalty to the spell casting roll and +1 Paradox.

                            Does that make sense?

                            With my Exceptional success from the Empathy roll earlier, don't I also get a Condition?
                            Yes, you do! Go ahead and pick a beneficial condition of your choosing.
