                            The resplendent sun smiled, though no one saw it.

                            Yi had a lot of smiles. He had the polite smile when paying attention to fools not worth the air they fouled with their next breath. He had the smile for when other people accomplished something and he couldn't bring himself to be genuinely happy for them. He even had a few for when the feelings inside him couldn't stay in the tiny vessel beating in his chest and had to spill over into the muscles of his face. This one was of that last kind, a smile for when what happened was unwanted but expected and he could not bring himself to be upset by it.

                            "You are right, much work shall be required. We will find a way through this, even if it means I must depart. I revealed myself to help this nation and if it is better for this nation I shall remove myself. I do not think it will come to that, there is too much good yet undone for me to be so selfish as to run. Yet whatever troubles or triumphs are or might come about, this too shall pass."

                            After sitting down at the table he said, "Would you like any help in preparing your response? I am hardly in a position to brag, but I was the 'Young Tiger of the River' who beggared the 'Five Silver Kings' and negotiated the Accord of Earth and Water."

                            It was bragging of the most shameless sort, bringing up two of the biggest diplomatic victories Lookshy had won in the Scavenger Lands in living memory. The so called "Five Kings", really four factors and one reigning plutocrat, had attempted to popularize the silver standard in the Confederation of Rivers. When the dust settled all five were paupers and Lookshy sat on a stockpile of exotic materials and silver. The Accord of Earth and Water that followed was Lookshy's response to the collapse of several of the largest fortunes in the Confederation, greatly expanding its own supply networks and prestige.
