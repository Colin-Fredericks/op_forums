        Cloudchaser sighs, nodding. "I suppose it's just one more thing to look into." A grimace momentarily crinkles her tanned features "Or it's a reminder that prophecy is fallible."

        "Indeed" Santiago chuckles "Well, in the interest of reliability - perhaps the three of you should begin your meeting? I will see to the cases, then head down to the beach."

        "My deepest thanks!" Cloudchaser replies mockingly, shedding her coat and settling into a tall-backed chair in front of the coffee table. Everyone's Peripheral Mage Sight tingles as a plastic binder suddenly appears in her outstertched hand, the cover marked with what appear to be High Speech runes.

        "Well now my ducklings, have a seat. You may have some ideas about what I called you here for today, so let me put it bluntly to avoid the wilder speculation. Elpis, regardless of recent tradgedy, you're far enough along to warrant the end of your apprenticeship. I mean, we've even put you through the rituals already. As such, it's time for you to be declared an indepented Leige-person of the Consilium. While normally you'd be slowly shopped around the existing Cabals to find whom you want to join, my judgement is that you'd be a poor fit for the locals. Don't get me wrong!" she adds hurriedly, "we'd love to have you in Skywatch! But you've never shown any interest in the weather, and you'd need to change your Shadow Name to keep with the Cabal's theme.

        "The other two are much the same - as far as I know, you have little skill or interest in the Spirit Arcana, so the Shadowwalkers likely have little appeal to you, and the Hedgemarshals are a touch more violent than I'd peg you for. Nothing for it then but to either let you be an independent, or dictate a Cabal. I've elected to do the latter, as my prerogative. Which is where our Qaf comes in. As another recent graduate, you're in dire need of direction young man. Zephyr and Sirocco both claim you to be a good Arrow, but not one interested in the weather or spirits. The Marshals might hold some appeal, but Dantes, as your Adamant Sage, has agreed to assign you to be Banner Warden to Elpis.

        "Assuming that neither of you has a particular objections -and I will stress that the only objections I will accept are "I've been accepted into another Cabal already" or "I have irrefutable proof this person is Left Handed"- Santiago has rubberstamped the basic paperwork. The two of you should decide on a Cabal name -although we don't need that today, necessarily- and the three of us will see about setting you up with a Hallow."

        She taps the binder meaningfully. "Little Atlantis has quite a few, obviously, but only a handful are reliable. The Consilium holds a few in trust, and I can offer some Mysterium ones if none suit. Dantes was also generous enough to provide some Arrow-owned options, though I will stress those are a last resort."

                    "Well, let's start with the common-property ones. You may have glimpsed some of these in passing." Cloudchaser pulls a map out of the binder, laying it out on the coffee table. Highly detailed, it looks like a city planner's document, but criss-crossed with colorful lines and unusual symbols. It also extends quite far into the surrounding forests. She taps a series of small triangular marks annotated on the paper, reading off of what appear to be dossiers as she does.

                    "Firsty, we have the Prospect Yard Graveyard in the Old Town. It's a relatively strong Hallow, and needs very little maintenance. However, it has several significant limitations for your use. Firstly, the Hallow itself is located in the gazebo at the center of the cemetery. The plots all radiate around it, so you'll likely be in full view when you visit. The Oblation is simple, at least, if a bit depressing. You need to sing a funeral dirge within the gazebo - any song will do, but it needs to be funerary. Which ties into the second thing of note; three of the six arches of the gazebo are Avernian Gates. At least three, we've never quite been able to confirm. Nighthawk -the Free Council Councilor?- is the primary expert on that, and is, herself, thing-of-significance number three. She is to have unlimited access to those gates at all times, and has first claim on all bodies interred on the grounds. I'll note that she hasn't actually exercised that crossing right since she became a Master of Death and could open her own Irises, but the potential for being accused of any grave robbery she might commit might be a turnoff. The Mana, at least, will be all yours.

                    "Second, we have Njord's Draught down by the docks. It's a... pub, I guess? Better food than a bar, but alcohol is definitely what draws the patrons. We partially fund the place, because of the Hallow. It's run by a Changeling, and the place is lousy with Hedgegates, or so the Marshals tell me. As such, the Marshals have crossing rights to talk with their contacts or use the Irises. In it's favor, though, is the built-in security that provides, and the Tass. Y'see, the Hallow is located in the Draught's storeroom, and the proprietor just has to keep liquid in that spot for about a day to infuse it. Gilligan -the Changeling- gets half of all such infused beverages, and usually sells them. If you claim the place, the Consilium’s Tass-shares will start going to you. How much access to the actual premises you get will have to be worked out between you two and Gil.

                    “Third is the Stone Oak, waaaay out here in the woodlands by Braem Wood.” Her brow furrows momentarily in irritation “Of the common-property Hallows, this is probably the thorniest, if you want it. The Stone Oak is a huge oak tree that seems to be growing directly out of the sheer side of a cliff. It’s fascinating. Of course, few people ever see it, because in addition to the Hallow in it’s boughs, the entire thing is a Loci. One owned by the local wolfpack. We’ve a longstanding agreement with them to help keep the place warded and secret, in exchange for access to the Hallow. If you want to claim it, you’ll have to meet with the pack and convince them. Good protection, but primarily as a result of bad neighbors.

                    “Fourth is Hyleen Cottage. It’s… literally a cottage, up on the heights in Old Town. No-one has lived there in years, which is the main downside. You’ll need to do a lot of fixing up if you want it to be a sanctum. The Oblation is to tend a fire in the central hearths - quite pleasant in winter, but murder in the summer. Not a strong Hallow, but it’s out of the way.

                    “Last is Tesslin’s Hollow. It’s the quarry-lake just east of town hall, and boy is that Hallow a bitch to get to. It’s located at the very bottom, with the Tass mostly coalescing in the kelp some lunatic started cultivating a couple decades back. It’s a strong Hallow, and Santiago was always talking about claiming it with a houseboat when we first married, but it’s really too inconvenient for most people.

                    “I’ll tell you about the Mysterium properties next, if none of these strike your fancy. Decisions today don’t have to be final, either. We can go for a tour of any that resonate with you, if you need a tie-breaker.”

                        "Based on the overview, my initial impression is that our our best bets are either Hyleen Cottage or the Njord's Draught.

                        The cottage seems low key, the oblation is practical and I think if we put in some work, it might make for a pretty good Sanctum.

                        The bar would make a good place to build contacts and is well defended. The tass is easy to carry as well and, so long as we keep an amicable relationship with Gil, we should be ok. The main drawback is the possibility of being caught in the crossfire of whatever the Hedgemarshals are currently doing.

                        The Stone Oak is the only one I am strongly against. I cannot perceive spirits and have no diplomatic experience with werewolves. While it is a powerful Hallow and very well defended, if things go poorly, it presents danger we are not equipped to deal with and is in the most isolated location.

                        Looking at Prospect Graveyard, while I would not be unsettled by being in one, I cannot perceive ghosts, so I would have difficulty communicating with them. This might not be an issue if Elpis knows the Death Arcanum.

                        The location of the Hallow means we would have a difficult time mediating into the Astral without looking suspicious. We would also need to be doubly careful not to cause Dissonance near it. Lastly, while I have no issue with Nighthawk's crossing, we have a minimum of 3 Avernian Gates acting as potential backdoors to the location, with probably more unaccounted for. From a security standpoint, those build up to meaningful liabilities.

                        Tesslin's Hollow is tempting, the tass is light and easy to carry. Harvesting is the challenge, its especially inconvenient to do so in winter. It would require a lot of work or a high cost to setup a reliable way to harness it, in addition to the extra work so that we could operate from it.

                        That is all I got so far. What are you thoughts on them ?"​
