    A Search for Lost Time [MtAw 2E]

    10-06-2024, 11:48 PM
    A Search for Lost Time
    A Mage: The Awakening 2E Chronicle

    "The phenomenon that a small alteration in the state of a dynamical system will cause subsequent states to differ greatly from the states that would have followed without the alteration."
    - Definition of the “Butterfly Effect”, Edward Norton Lorenz

    “Man was created alone to teach you that whoever kills one life kills the world entire, and whoever saves one life saves the world entirely.”
    - The Talmud

    “Strange, isn't it? Each man's life touches so many other lives. When he isn't around he leaves an awful hole, doesn't he?”
    - Clarence the Angel, It’s A Wonderful Life

    The tapestry of the phenomenal world is intricate and subtlety woven with threads of time. Mages who have studied this arcanum are able to see into the past, and the future, slow its speed of motion from one moment to another, or speed it, and even move along its path, traveling in time. It is rumored that the hidden, secret chiefs, the archmasters, and the Exarchs and Oracles, if they exist, have perfected this knowledge, and that each of us may be living in a “Ship in a Bottle”, a world entirely separate from the experience of others. To pull on one of the threads of time, woven tightly with all the others, may risk altering the integrity of the entire tapestry, and one side of the tapestry may look like a mess of threads, and the picture it is forming only apparent to those able to look at the other side.

    Mages hunger for the truth beyond the Lie, and knowing the course of time from beginning to end can seem to promise this truth, but in seeking it out, the mage may violate their own wisdom. And as knowledge and power increase, the temptations grow as well. What sort of world would you make, if you could change the past; Or knew with certainty the consequences of the choices you make now into the future?

    All mages that believe in lost Atlantis yearn for a “past” that no longer exists as existence is commonly perceived. And some mages yearn for a future that always seems just out of reach.

    This chronicle will explore the themes of “missing time”, in the various shades of meaning that phrase might hold. Things we have forgotten and can’t seem to remember. Events we can’t seem to forget and scar our present. Baggage. Unfulfilled plans for imagined tomorrows.

    The mood is one of nostalgia, regret, worry, and maybe even hope. Feeling crushed by what has gone before, grief over what you can never get back, dread over what may happen next.

      The Chronicle will begin in the modern day City of San Francisco, California. There has been some kind of habitation near the bay since 5,000 years before the present.

      The Ohlone were the first known people to live there. Spanish colonists began to arrive in the 1770s and built a fort, or Presidio, and a mission dedicated to Saint Francis of Assisi, the patron saint of Nature, Animals, the poor, and stowaways. They first called the settlement Yerba Buena, after an aromatic herb, similar to mint, that grows in the region, and it was a port of call for explorers of the Pacific Coast and fur traders. In 1821 it became independent from Spain along with Mexico, and it was captured by the United States during the Mexico-American war in 1846. About this same time the city was overrun by a ship of Mormon pioneers that doubled the size of the city. The U.S. army officially changed the name of the city to San Francisco, and began plans to fill in the water cove nearby to increase the land area on the peninsula, dramatically altering the environment of the region.

      In 1848 Gold was discovered in California, which led to a massive influx of treasure seekers that increased the size of the city from about 1,000 to 25,000 in the space of a year and flooded the area with human emotions of greed and ambition. California became a state in 1850, and in 1859 silver was also discovered nearby. Around this time a court of changelings and like-minded mages formed an nameless order called the Bay City Marshalls, dedicated to meeting out justice to evil doers on both sides of the Hedge, in a city drowning in vice, corruption and crime. The red light district of the city was known as The Barbary Coast. The rich mining barons of the city lived on stately Nob Hill.

      Thousands of Chinese immigrants poured into the city during this time, making the city one of the largest concentrations of Chinese people outside of China. Chinese were often the victims of prejudice, discrimination, and even violence and lynchings. Chinese mages of the Silver Ladder were some of the first of the Diamond to settle in the city, and they formed an uneasy alliance with the Native American and nameless mages of the area. The Mysterium soon founded an Atheneum, and Guardians of the Veil and Seers alike began to clamp down on supernatural phenomenon, a task made difficult by a seeming uneasiness of the quiescence of the West that led sleepers to often be able to fully recall witnessing mysteries. The expansion of the railroad and the telegraph, and the eventual completion of the Transatlantic Railroad, along with increased shipping traffic through the Pacific, strengthened ties to the rest of Awakened society. The West remained somewhat remote from the intense conflict of the Nameless War in more “civilized” parts of the world, and mages of the city were often more absorbed with local political concerns and mysteries. Amongst sleepers, the cultural reputation of the city increased and it became known as the “Paris of the West”, leading many famous writers, such as Mark Twain, Jack London, and Ambrose Bierce, who first wrote about the imaginary city of Carcosa, to settle there for a while. The Barbary Coast was also one of the birthplaces of Jazz music.

      In 1899, the Bay City Marshalls joined with other Nameless orders from around the world in the Great Refusal against the Seers of the Throne, and remains of the Leafless Tree continue to be the dominant voice amongst the Libertines of the city. A year later the Silver Ladder led the other Diamond orders to accept the Free Council at the European Great Convocation of Paris. Many remain uneasy with this decision and view the Hangmen and other Free Council mages with suspicion.

      In 1906, an earthquake and fire caused massive destruction and loss of life in the city. Because of the great wealth of many of its citizens, the city was able to rebuild quickly, but the emotional resonance of the event continues to affect echoes of the city in the invisible realms.

      San Francisco was an important naval port during WWII. During this time, there were many Japanese citizens that were incarcerated in camps by the government, and many Japanese people never recovered their possessions after the war. After the Second World War among the Sleepers, the Adamantine Arrow made a formal decision to stay out of sleeper conflicts. Many Silver Ladder and Free Council mages, however, aided former Nazi mages in escaping to the new world and starting over. This led to conflict with the other orders of the Pentacle when it was discovered that Seers of the Third Reich and the Soviet Union had been allowed to infiltrate the United States and other American countries secretly. The global politics of the Pentacle have subtle influences on the local Concilium politics of the city.

      In the 1950s and 60s, the Ministry of Geryon, which served Nemesis by exploiting Sleeper’s fear of being controlled by hidden, unseen forces began to wain in power, and eventually collapsed. This affected the city of San Francisco, which had long had a reputation for freedom from forces of social repression, and led many beatniks, bohemians, and hippies to flock to the city, culminating in the “Summer of Love” in 1967. The Fear returned, however, as the Panopticon grew in power. Many sleeper cults and new religious organizations during this time also seemed to hold out hope that was short lived and hollow. In 1978, Jim Jones led his followers, the People’s Temple, to commit mass suicide. The era was also characterized by other forms of violence, including many serial killers such as the Zodiac, and the murder of the politician and Gay Rights leader Harvey Milk that same year.

      Around this same time, engineers and entrepreneurs began to settle in “Silicon Valley”, causing a second “gold rush” and bringing massive amounts of money into the city. Over the past thirty years this has caused the city to gentrify, making it virtually uninhabitable for the poor and marginalized that used to make the city of St. Francis home.

                  Gilad
                  Name: Erik Andersen
                  Age: 21
                  Height: 1.90m
                  Hair: Blonde
                  Eyes: Green
                  Path: Thyrsus
                  Order: Adamantine Arrow
                  Legacy (eventually): Bannerets of Ameretat
                  Immediate Nimbus: The echo of a martial music, which feels profoundly ancient, bolstering one's morale (+2 Resolve).
                  Signature Nimbus: The deep breath before the plunge, the feeling of the approaching eve of battle.
                  Long Term Nimbus: People tend to focus more on long term goals and are resolute in achieving them.
                  Dedicated Magical Tool: A Spilåpipa with Swedish traditional wood carvings spelling the High Speech rune for "Vigilance".
                  Concept: The Eternal Warrior


                  Background

                  Erik Andersen was born in the island of Öland, in the year 773 CE to a family of musicians. Both parents were court musicians, with his father keeping the lore of the Swedish traditions and his mother the Finnish ones.

                  Following in his father’s footsteps, he was trained in traditional music as well, being taught to draw inspiration from the sounds of the land. When warriors would return from great battles or from journeys to the edges of the world, it was his job to ensure their deeds would not be forgotten.

                  His only other sibling, his older sister Alruna, was a skilled singer like their mother. She was known in the village and the Forts as a fortune teller. Women would often forbid her to come anywhere near a house with a pregnancy, even to the point of spitting on her and throwing stones. She carried small bones, possibly from a sea bird, with her and a clay bowl that she found buried somewhere with invisible writing that only she could read. She would sometimes disappear for days at a time without telling anyone where she was going. Sometimes Erik would see her playing in tidal pools and when he would ask her what she was doing she would laugh hysterically as if she was going mad.

                  The last time Erik saw her, the wind was blowing fiercely from the sea, wiping her tunic and long unbraided hair around. It seemed as if the wind was going to blow the whole island away and things seemed to become un-solid as if he could see through them. He could hear her say, “There was a strong man with forty sons. Their mothers suffered ,except the last one. The men of the good island know this strong man. Their land is slowly drowning. One day these sons sailed far away to a place with good wind on a silver river.” Then she started to cry and fell to her knees sobbing. She screamed “Don’t trust the virgin. Her eyes are too big, but she only sees far enough. She has a black pipe. Her song is beautiful, but her knives cut deep. There is another way…” and then she disappeared as if the wind blew her away like sea foam. Despite the family searching for weeks, they never found a trace of her.

                  The Awakening

                  In the year of his 20th winter, on the 23rd of December, Erik undertook the final tradition to anoint him as a inheritor of his family's tradition, the Årsgång (Year Walk). Leaving his house in the dead of night, avoiding looking at any fires and not carrying provisions, he made his way to the forest of Trollskogen in search of insights from beyond.

                  With only the moonlight to show him the path, he followed the tradition and walked deeper into the forest, until he reached an area where the caw of a rooster would never reach him. Walking through the moonlit forest, he noticed the surrounding fading, growing less corporeal, but the soul of the land and its people more vivid. The spirits seemed to be moving towards a mysterious, discordant song in the distance.

                  Following them through the woods, Erik arrived at the shore overlooking the island of Blå Jungfrun, which the ancient tales warned about, the home of a coven practicing the most foul magic. Before he could notice, a deer in the form of a human stood before him. He claimed the music of the land was out of harmony, and that it would soon forget itself. He warned that it must remember its history in order to find the way forward. Almost as if responding to this being, a glowing cerulean river rose up from the sea, forming a path to the island. Erik, working on pure instinct, walked atop the river towards the dreaded shore.

                  When he stepped onto the island, the discordant music sounded clearer than ever. He followed it through the woods and deep into the mountains, descending through a cave into the veins of the earth. In the depths lay an altar of stone and bone, overlooked by a Nachtkrapp, its coal red eyes fixed on the altar, its attention focused on playing a haunting melody using the holes in its wings. In the shadow of the torchlight, instruments made of bone were being played by the desiccated spirit of the departed.

                  In his mind, Erik could hear the sound of the land, its pulse and rhythm like a heartbeat. Its melody was opposing the beast’s. Before he could notice, he had taken his ax in hand and was fighting the monster to the beat of the music. Rhythmic parries and flank attacks slowly carved at the beast’s hide, the fury of the dead becoming his armor.

                  When the beast finally fell and he cast its ruin before him, the silence was broken by the sound of applause from a cloaked warrior. He mentioned that he had taken a chance, setting up this trial to find someone who can join their fight against an enemy from beyond, so he sought Alruna’s brother. Before he could elaborate, reality seemed to flood, pushing them away in a fierce current, which washed reality away.

                  By the time he could regain his footing, he was in a place of impossible architecture, where everything seemed to have a pastel look. The body of the Nachtkrapp was at his feet and the song of the land still rang in his ear. Looking at the holed wings of the creature, he started to carve the bones into a spilåpipa while humming the song.

                  By the time he had finished, the deer humanoid was in front of him, this time wearing an odd outfit and carrying a glass of the cerulean liquid.



                  Giving his blessing to the instrument, he poured the liquid into it, the bone transforming into birch wood, the water carving runes into the instrument. Erik proceeded to play the song on the spilåpipa, the surroundings melted away once more and he arrived back in Öland in the present day. After joining the Arrow, he was placed in a traveling music band called Utopia, which acts as a cover to deploy operatives across the world. With traces of his former civilization lost to the sands of time, Erik hopes that somewhere in the world he will be able to find clues to the events surrounding his Awakening and Alruna’s battle.

                  What does Gilad most regret, or wish that they could change about his life?

                  Gilad greatest regret is that he was unable to prevent his family and village from vanishing. Had he been able to wield Supernal power, maybe he could have understood Alruna's prophecy. If only he was more knowledgeable, maybe he could have done more before being swept away.

                  In the present day he plays in a band called Utopia, using folk music to keep the memories and stories of them alive, refusing to allow deafening silence to fill the space where their voices should have been heard.

                      Background
                      Michael Naqvi grew up in a vibrant Iranian-American family in Los Angeles, steeped in rich traditions and a strong sense of community. His grandparents emigrated from Iran, bringing with them stories of resilience and ambition that instilled a deep appreciation for education and success in their grandchildren. Michael's parents, both professionals, emphasized the importance of hard work, which fueled his ambition from a young age. He was often found with his nose buried in books, not just about the law, but also about history, philosophy, and the mystical aspects of his cultural heritage.

                      Michael excelled in school, ultimately earning a scholarship to a prestigious law school. There, he honed his skills in persuasion, argumentation, and legal research. It was during this time that he first attracted the the Silver Ladder attention.

                      Awakening
                      Michael’s Awakening came during a particularly challenging trial, where the weight of responsibility pressed heavily on him. In a moment of desperation, he reached out to the threads of Fate, channeling his will to sway the jury's opinion. In that instant, he understood the interconnectedness of all things—the power to manipulate the flow of time and the minds of others. This experience solidified his path as an Acanthus and deepened his commitment to the Silver Ladder’s ideals of leadership and responsibility. He got in contact with the Ascended faction of the Ladder, and that marked his magical aspirations, and a brief training with members of the Mysterium, he got inspired to join the Gnosotes as their scholary bent is interesting to him.

                      Now a practicing lawyer, Michael balances his legal career with his magical studies. He specializes in cases involving the intricacies of bureaucracy and justice, using his powers to uncover truths and navigate the complexities of the legal system. His ability to read the fates of people and situations gives him a unique edge in negotiations and courtroom battles. However, this dual life often leads to a struggle with laziness, as the mundane aspects of law can feel burdensome compared to the thrilling nature of magic. As befiting a true believer in the Silver Ladder philosophy Michael helped create the Hypnopompic Circle: that mankind is forced into Sleep is an affront to the greatness of humanity. While we can not make them awake (yet), we can certainly put them halfway there. That is the origin of the Hypnopompic word, the state of leading out of sleep.​

                                                      Ralph Kohler was born precisely on night of 20th of June 1949 ( summer solstice ), in one of newly build San Francisco picket fences suburbs. His parents epitomized ‘American Dream’ ideals of nuclear family, even if German heritage citizens. (Their parents left Germany before WW1 even. ) H                                                      owever, Ralph embraced ‘rebel without cause’ identity – but in very passing ways. He liked to sit in cafes, meet with ‘strange’ people, listen to jazz and rock, join hippies scene in the 1960s as teenager. Being ‘gay‘ in that times ( probably now modern queer or pan label more ), he was seeking modern ways society can be build and open to marginalized people like him. It was in August 1966, having 17 years on his back, he partook in Compton's Cafeteria riot, San Fran so called ‘Stonewall before Stonewall’. Ralph was a frequent guest of the place, sneaking out in the middle of the night to soak up the queer atmosphere of it. However, on riot night, he did not return home. In truth, he found new in Arcadia.

                                                      Returning from Watchtower of Lunargent Thorn to Earth, he found out it’s December 20th of 1972, and last Apollo 17 mission just returned to main land. He also uncovered he is now in his 30s, probably spending 15 or so years in the Realm of Mists. He felling robbed of his youth and started to study Time magic and it’s Legacies to try to get back stolen years by Fate. He meet Lady Peacock, creator of People of the Hour, in late 1974 in San Fran. He was introduced in to Legacy – but had an problem with it. People say about living in moment and looking for future. Ralph, now Helmut, wanted to get back his years. After months of experimentation, he summoned rare Time Fae and demand getting ‘time of his youth’. Fae, being capricious creatures, pushed him decades forward, almost 50 years in future, probably to teach him a life lesson. He shown up on 11th of December of 2022, day Artemis 1 mission returned to Earth.

                                                      From now on Helmut is looking for two things – how to get back his to his proper times ( maybe trying to jump to 1969 to Stonewall also ) – and get back his unlived youth.

                                                      Awakening
                                                      Ralph’s Awakening came during Compton's Cafeteria riot – he came on one ‘hot’ August nights to the titular café, trying to get good tunes and maybe some love life. When riots begun, he even hit police officer with teacup! However, unsuspected by others, he disappeared.

                                                      He awoke in mist bound glade In the forest. Strange animals and plants around him. In the mist he lost the feel of time. He did not know he traveled for circa 15 years in mist. He finally found his way when seeing Watchtower of Lunargent Thorn. Inside it, he was trial by some 17th century looking elaborate Fae, that say he is Puck from Seelie Court and that if Helmut ( yes, used this name to Ralph ) want to reclaim his life – he need to made trial in tower. Acanthus to this day do not remember exactly what happened after that, only that he carved his name as Helmut in the Watchtower.

                                                      The everlasting mists had cleared, and Ralph was back on the streets of San Francisco, but about 15 years older, in worn-out clothes - and it was December 20, 1972. Oddly enough, it just so happened that snow had fallen in area of a few houses here then. In California. And so Ralph became Helmut.
