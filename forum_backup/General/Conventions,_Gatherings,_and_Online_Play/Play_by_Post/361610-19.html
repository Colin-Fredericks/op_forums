            The figure had almost seen everything there was to see in creation now. His sailing the seas taught him much of creatures in the depths, heading into ports from the north to the south taught him about the extremes of weather. Nearly dying to a Solar taught him of powers he never imagined. Being saved by creatures of the dark and receiving power to live again taught him about the tricks and frailty of life, and granted him to see the side of power. The extreme tortures of his deathlord master turned his mind inside out. And now, he has seen two individuals transforming in battle to rip him to shreds. There was not much left for him to see in the world. And now he knew that he was bound to die again.

            As the crow flies behind him, he gets an ominous feeling. He knew that another one must have arrived. Dropping down from above, The Servant transforms into his war form and grapples the figure with his arms and tail. In that second, the figure screams out a very disconcerting scream as memories of death wash over him again. He knew this was his last moment. He would not be saved now. And he knew his master would massacre his body as soon as his armies arrived. There was absolutely no hope for him. He continued to bleed out and looked upon his enemies. The Servant had managed to quickly clinch him and shoved him off to the side, but the disc follows him, keeping them both in the air and causing no damage from a fall.

            "Finish it quickly. I dare not wait long enough for news to get back to my master. Who knows what he could do to me even in my permanent death."

            Since The Servant has already succeeded in a clinch, do you wish to change your move? If not, you can always clinch him still and combine attacks with The Servant for an even more powerful crush attack.



            Flynn lobs the rock, but it merely bounces off a tree about 10 feet away from the figure. It seems not to care as it disappears even further into the forest.
