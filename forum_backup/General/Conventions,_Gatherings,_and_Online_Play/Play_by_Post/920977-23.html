                                                        rwknoll That is a very, very interesting way of looking at it. I originally intended "Ragnar" as Simon's Shadow Name, and thought it would be interesting if he acquired it as part of his Awakening. The Shadow Name merit was something I picked up because I realized that, being a TA at UChicago, it could be easy to figure out his sympathetic name. But this puts an IC explanation on it that I never thought of: "Simon Collins" is still his sympathetic name, but "Ragnar" is the truth of his soul. So, by using "Ragnar" among other mage's as his Shadow Name, he's declaring, rather than concealing, his true identity. Very cool! I like that idea!
