    Mark 5
    Conditions: Tenacious, Steadfast
    Alembics: Hygeius (0), Motus (0), Unbroken (0), Unconquered (0)
    Health: OOOOOOOOOO | Willpower: 5/6 | Pyros: 7/11 | Beats: 3/5 | Vitriol Beats: 2/5

    "Look, kid, you're obviously new at this, but let's get one thing straight. If you've been in Torment, I mean really been in Torment, you've done some things that most humans would probably call evil. Hell, you might have unintentionally done some things they call evil even without Torment, I don't know you, but I know a bit about Mudbreaker and she wasn't exactly the motherly type. Here's the thing- nobody can tell you what 'good' or 'evil' is, you have to figure that crap out for yourself, but I'll tell you one thing I know for sure. When you start talking about evil and sin, it really all starts with treating people like things."

    Mark 5 just... stared ahead, numb. This was like being told you will die, or worse, won't ever manage to end the Pilgrimage. He just wanted to be liked! He wanted people to be nice to him! And now it looked like he had lived a lie. He might have done, most likely he HAS done, very bad things. Evil things. He called himself a hero, but in the end, was he just another thug, harming people because he could? Was he just a monster? He wanted to help! He wanted to improve their lives! And he never treated people as things. did he? He has always seen them as someone who made their own choices, and sometimes those choices were to hurt other people. That was evil, wasn't it? That needed to be stopped, no? His large hands covered his face as he crouched. Why did this have to be so complicated! Why did it not make sense? After all he just wanted to do good!



    "I meant well!"




    That sentence lit up in his mind. I meant well.
    "Sometimes, people do bad things without meaning to. If they knew what they were really doing, they would probably stop. So you need to see if people realize what they are doing. And if they don't, you need to show them what's really going on. But you need to be... subtle about it. You can't just shove their nose in it."

    Adonis voice echoed in his head. He realized. He had been shown. The old man had hit the girl... but maybe he had just done the same as Mark 5 himself? Punished her because she did something she shouldn't do. Was he right to punish him? Had he been right to punish her? Scenes went through his head, and the Titan realized that many of his actions, seen by someone else, someone not understanding why he did what he did, could be considered Evil. And he was not so sure about some of them anymore. BUT! His intentions were pure! He was no monster, nor was he Evil, just another person trying to be good! Strangely, this was a relief. The sudden weight of fear for his identity, his... soul? was gone. Even though he wasn't a Hero, he did not lose hope.
    Because he could still become one.
    Like Earth, who did bad things, but means well, he could become Better than he was.
    He would need to learn more about intention. He would need to be more careful with his judgement. He would need to find out what is Good, and what is Evil. He might even need to atone.
    And then, he would be Better.
    And, maybe, a Hero.
    A tear dropped from his eyes, and his sight became... clearer.
    The Titan smirked to himself as he stood up, then looked at Adonis and the Nepri, and let out a long sigh. "Ahh~... I think I see. Maybe. A bit."
    Then, Mark 5 gathered Adonis in a hug. It was over as quickly as it started. It was weird to do this. But it seemed.... appropriate. "Thank you."
    He nodded at the Nepri "Both of you, too."


    Rambles... what a wonderful thing they are.

                            Evening- Friday November 4th

                            Earth

                            Satin's smile grows when Earth latches on to the visual of the spider and the butterfly. "You know, butterflies are alchemical marvels. They grow, they develop, and then they cocoon themselves. They melt down in a solvent of their own making, and restructure themselves into a new shape. They come out of the cocoon as a butterfly." They see Earth's face and raise their hands "No joke, I read it in a book once. Point being, Azoth remembers, Azoth knows things. Maybe the Messengers are trying to tell you something." Their face falls, however, as Earth continues to speak about Dead Girl. Such ugliness that can grow on the inside, where it can't be seen, only felt.

                            The Riven looks at Earth very carefully, with focused intensity, and takes a calculated risk. They give Earth a hug. "I was wrong," they whispered "you do have ghosts, it's just they live inside you. Listen, Earth, you need to know that what happened, what Dead Girl did? That's on her, not on you. We will keep an eye out for her, trade the stories, pass along the news. The stories about you are already out there, but we'll let you know where she is and if she's coming. We all will." Satin stands, patting Earth on her arm, and shakes out their hair. They move as though standing to leave, and then they stop, as if there was something important they forgot to say.

                            "Look, we aren't human, and never were, you know? Even if we will be, one day. But these bodies aren't ours, and we aren't them. We're in here," they tap their head "and in here," they tap their heart "and that means that we are who we make ourselves to be. I'm not a dude, I'm not a chick, I'm me, and I get to decide who that is. You do too." Satin finally stands, and begins to walk away. Before they leave, they toss one more comment over their shoulder

                            "There's one other thing people use butterflies to represent. They used to stand in for souls."

                            Mark 5, Regret, Adonis & Scout

                            Even as the Titan has his dawning realization, a responding flare of Azoth in his gut, the warm trickle of Vitriol suffusing his flesh, the two Originists and the Muse discuss the potential of uniting their Flames in an alchemical pact. The blue Nepri's eyes widen at the mention of Highwinds, and they turn aside, stiff as the mummified corpse they were made from. How long does it take to make a Nepri, really? Does it start when mummification does, or does it start when the Azoth and phlegm flows into their bodies. The blue Nepri looks stiffly towards Mark 5 and nods curtly before turning to Scout "I'm sorry," he says "Highwinds will be missed."

                            It's getting late. Adonis has to meet with Erwin soon, and talk is dying down. Some of the fellow Pilgrims are dispersing, in throngs, in smaller groups, and alone. The Ramble is dying down.

                            Perhaps it's time to get going.

                                                The interesting thing happens when they go to St. Boniface in the course of his research. She's at least stashed her backpack at Adonis' place (to which she frequently invites herself), but she's still Disconnected from humanity, and it shows. Kindred spirits who not only wanted to understand the Refinements, but also help and protect other Prometheans.

                                                    Their Rambles lingered in the Flame's memory alongside as much as Regret could muster, his own link to the Refinement of Lead allowing these memories to not fade until they could be put down onto something solid. And there was only one item Regret had that could do that: his own Journal. And in order to retrieve that, he needed to go back to the Far Place.

                                                    Leaving the Ramble he headed away from the city, into the wilderness for several miles. Regret had made sure to leave markers at certain parts of his makeshift trail, so that he wouldn't get lost again, and after about three hours of hiking he found it. Hidden in the wilderness was what he called the Far Place: a makeshift campground that he had made himself using what the dead soldiers remembered about hiding out in the wilderness. "Wilderness training" was the exact words he heard in his head when he drew upon those memories. All of the soldier had memories like it, and he used those memories when he set up the tent he stole so many months ago. He camouflaged it with tarps and trash bags and anything he could find in the wilderness. Provisions, he also stole, hung from a net in a tree several yards away, carefully held inside more trash bags and tarp. It wasn't the largest camp, and truly it could only last for so long before the temperate environment ruined the tent and tarps and trash bags just like the last camp he and Frank had, but it was surprisingly well hidden for a young Promethean's first work.

                                                    Retrieving some rations from the food net, Regret retired for the night into the hidden tent in the hidden campsite. Inside he found his most cherished belongings: A sleeping bag (stolen), blankets (also stolen), a portable lantern that's almost out of battery life (need to steal more batteries), a CD player with headphones and a Jazz CD (a gift from Frank, which had the last and newest of the stolen batteries), and a personal journal with several pens of varying degrees of ink (all stolen with the exception of the words that were written within.) After consuming the rations he set to writing down the Rambles he had heard hours previously. He recorded each name at the top of the page that contained their Ramble, and wrote so much down that by the time he'd come to Mark 5's he was completely ran out of ink. A disappointment welled up inside of Regret, he had very much hoped to record all of them tonight. But with that disappointment, a realizations. Because this sadness was his, truly his, it meant that he had truly enjoyed his experience at the Ramble. He had learned so much, and was so eager to put it down into writing that he had an emotional response all his own. Sadness was unpleasant, but it reminded people of what's truly important. And that's why it is precious to Regret. It's why Regret is Regret.

                                                    Looking down at his journal, with the words Mark 5 written at the top of the page with nothing underneath, Regret had an idea. Tomorrow he would journey into town. He would steal some more pens and batteries, and he would accompany Mark 5 on his mission so that he would both help his fellow Promethean in his Mission to find a Home and so that he would have more stories to write about after he had completed writing down the remaining Rambles.
