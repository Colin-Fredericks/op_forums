        Part three of Terrestrials of Novalesk! Here we have a man who recently appeared in the narrative

        -Nikoloi Argyus-

        Well known to be well into his second century of life, this Earth dragon is also a childhood friend of the current Pyrtaneis, Zophyrus Linon. Fascinated with bureaucracy and economics, many in Novalesk would have considered his chosen profession below that of a prince of the Earth. However, Argyus has proven himself to be a wizard in managing projects and performing complex economic calculations. If he were born in the Realm, Argyus would likely be a high-ranking bureaucrat in the thousand scales. Indeed, his (relatively) high breeding and recent Therian ancestry cause some to believe that he has Ragara blood in his veins.

        Argyus has a peculiar fascination with roads. None know exactly what started this; only that he has been instrumental in causing his house in expand its road network, and is constantly pushing the Capital government for funding to do even more.

        Due to their being of the same Gens, many assume that Argyus and Chancellor Feiro are closely related. This is a false assumption; they come from completely different branches of the family, and, at absolute closest, are fourth cousins.

        Argyus looks to be an oldish man of middling height, with a large balding plate. His terrestrial heritage shows through when he smiles, however, as his teeth are seemingly made of the purest white marble. Rumor is that his entire skeleton is like that.

            Part four of Terrestrials of Novalesk! this young lady has been making a lot of waves in the political scene!

            -Althea Sanna-

            This young lady exalted nearly eight months ago, and has caused many a ripple in Novalesk since. This charismatic and strongly opinionated young woman exalted in her fifteenth year, indiciating strong terrestrial lineage. This has caused many to believe that her mother, who was a member of the Novlaeski embassy in Theros for several years before Sanna's birth was not very faithful to her husband.

            While her charisma and leadership talent would make her a strong candidate for the Archon, her lack of experience and general naivete hold many at pause for the moment. The potential of being crushed under long-term Zophyrus-controlled hegemony also brings many to oppose the young idealist.

            Sanna is a short, stocky young woman. She has a pale complexion as if perpetually sea-sick, and she sports long straight navy hair.

                    Part five of Terrestrials of Novalesk! The swashbuckling captain:

                    -Spyrus Thales-

                    A thirty-two year year old water aspect, Thales is the commander of Novalesk's middling naval forces. When he came of age, Thales left his family to make his fortune (an old tradition in Novalesk, though it is only really practiced by Spyrus and Ptreas these days). Rather than joining one of the family mercenary companies, however, he joined a completely unrelated Confederation of Rivers river-boat based mercenary band. Over the next three years, he rose in ranks in the mercenary company, until he eventually became the head and primary owner of the company. He amassed a small fortune over the five following years. During this time frame, he also amassed a reputation as a womanizing, swashbuckling rogue. With a fortune in silver and a fleet of river-borne mercenaries, and a Terrestrial exaltation Thales returned home.

                    That was seven years ago. In the intervening time, he has turned his mercenary band into a crack naval force, patrolling the waterways around his homeland. His reputation as a womanizing scoundrel continues, though none can give any definitive proof to either allegation.

                    Thales wears flamboyant clothes, made by a divine tailor in Great Forks. He is tall and swarthy, and shares his cousin Artemia's flaxen hair (His father is Artemia's mother's older brother). He wields dual wavecrasher diaklaives in battle.

                                                        Sixth and Final installment of Terrestrials of Novalesk!

                                                        -Origenon Hollidane-

                                                        Origenon Hollidane (she prefers Holly) was found on the steps of the Temple of Origenes eighteen years ago by the deity himself. The deity found the babe wrapped in fine silks, with a jade token giving the child's name on one side and an ornate High Realm "R" on the other. Origenes adopted her as his own child, and raised her himself, with the aid of his elderly wife and the support of his then fifty-five year old biological daughter. It came as a shock to many when the young woman, who was seemingly being groomed to enter the priesthood, exalted as a Wood Dragon three years ago.

                                                        Since then, she her skills as a performer and a churegeon have rocketed to supernatural levels, and she has discovered a knack for archery and woodsmanship. She has yet to show any interest in politics, finding simple joy in travelling the nation. She is rumored to be a close friend of the recently-exalted Artemia.

                                                        Holly is a heavily tanned athletic woman of average height. She typically wears red woodsman's leathers, and uses a green jade powerbow. She is rumored to be studying an ancient martial art that uses such weapons. According to those rumors, her teacher is a Lesser Elemental Dragon who Origenes blackmailed into taking the job.
