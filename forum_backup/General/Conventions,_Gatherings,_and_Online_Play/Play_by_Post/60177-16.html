    (Distraction team)
    Airi digs into her pack, and pulls out a silver rocket with scribed Old Realm across it's body.

    "Titan-slayer bullet. We only have three of these, but they're Hell on anything that relies on Essence for structure," she says, and loads it into her cannon. She fires it with adroit accuracy, and the screen shatters, the rocket narrowly missing hitting the dandy on the outer wall. He sighs, and pulls out a bow made of silver wood.

    "You have put this on yourself," he calls, and fires a brass arrow, which leaves his bow, but doesn't hit anything you can see. Hell, you don't even know where it fell. However, at that, several sections of ground rise before you, revealing gun turrets of various sizes. They all turn on your position, and open fire.

    (Complex Team)

    The scan of the room reveals that the essence flow that is powering the terminal is coming from down below. However, something in the walls is preventing further analysis at this juncture. However, the terminal bursts into life, as a video appears on it. Sitting side by side is the robed woman in glasses from before and a new face; a regal looking young Rusemin woman in purple robes. Despite her noble looks, she is visibly uncomfortable sitting beside this woman.

    "Welcome one and all! I'm your hostess, the incomparable genius, Sable Thesis Dream! Today on this illustrious Haomaday, we've got a right excellent crowd for the Run! About a hundred contestants will make the transit through the Gauntlet, and we'll see if any are capable of gaining that August position of Green Sun Prince! We're already blessed by the presence of that Lord of Putrescence, the Floudering Raffelsia, Furfulu. Also, I am joined by the sixth daughter of the House of Ruse, Princess Icthya Pisce! Princess, do you wish to say a few words?" the robed woman says energetically.

    "E-everyone, please do your best. Take it slow and steady, and please be mindful of your surroundings at all times," the Rusemin princess says, bowing.

    "You heard it here, folks! Take it slowly and steadily! More to come as the Run commences on Level J4!" the Infernal chuckles, winking at the camera.
