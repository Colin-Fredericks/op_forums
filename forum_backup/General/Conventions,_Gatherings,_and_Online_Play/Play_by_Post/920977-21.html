                                        Haberdasher - I think we're over complicating this a bit.

                                        Being able to cast the spell reflexively is really just so you can do it in combat or in response to when you're asked for a skill check and want to be better at it. Here, you can just do 1 reach to cast as an instant action. Regardless, Lynx will sense it and see it with Mage Sight.

                                        Whether you want 9- or 8-again should be picked before you see the paradox roll (if relevant) or results of the casting roll. So no, you unfortunately can't use the bonus from the exceptional success to make it 8-again. For now, I would like to stick to RAW on options for exceptional successes on casting; no bonus reach for spell-specific options. The idea is that the outcome of your spell is just naturally more potent, or you're stronger/more confident because you cast something really well. The Imago of the spell isn't changed, so spell-specific reach options shouldn't apply.

                                        If you want 9-again, you don't need to reach on anything else; you could use the 1 free reach to instant cast. In that case, the paradox roll never happened, and then you can pick the bonus from exceptional success. That includes 1 WP back, and you can increase potency (primary spell factor) or a spell factor step, or gain a Condition. Potency does affect aspects of a boon, but because duration is 1 turn by default, I would recommend just giving yourself the Inspired Condition (which will grant an Arcane Beat upon resolving).

                                        If you want 8-again on that skill roll, keep the results of the paradox roll. Then, you can still gain Inspired or make the potency higher (which, as noted above, is not relevant unless you add to the duration before casting).

                                        Let me know what you want to do.

                                                            I've got something to pitch to anyone reading! I'll copy/paste what I originally sent to our ST:

                                                            I was planning to play Moonstone as having some mental health problems which he used to have managed for him with Mind magic, and are now flaring up again with his rapid life changes. I'm aiming more to evoke certain consistent symptoms as part of the character than to literally represent a specific diagnosis or illness, as is kind of the standard dramatic convention, but the general model is borderline personality disorder because it's something I'm somewhat familiar with.

                                                            The keywords for what that would involve: quickly forming very positive or very negative opinions of people and easily being triggered to change those opinions, reacting deeply and strongly to emotional stimuli (maybe lashing out disproportionately), not having a strong sense of his own identity and what he wants as opposed to what other people want for him, and seeking to be around other people rather than alone.

                                                            Moonstone is a functioning, productive, independent person and he's self-aware about his illness and how to manage it, so it's not a constant problem and would mostly just inform my roleplaying. However, I was thinking it would be interesting to take a persistent Mental Condition representing his stuff, largely so that I have something that concretely interacts with Mind spells like Mental Scan and Clear Thoughts that deal with Mental Conditions. It could be fun to discretely (telepathically?) ask Binder to use Clear Thoughts on him before entering a stressful situation, for example, or have his mental state noticed by another mage scanning the party for hostile intentions. Or if we ever get to the point where we're in the Oneiros, the illness could be embodied as an antagonistic Goetia.

                                                            If you were open to that, I could come up with a Condition. I was imagining like a two-stage thing that has no mechanical effect most of the time, but when Moonstone has half or less of his Willpower left, the symptoms become more pronounced and cause a penalty to certain Mental or Social actions. Like other persistent conditions (like Addicted, Amnesia, etc on page 314), it also might have a Beat trigger when his symptoms cause a significant problem for him in a scene (much in the way that a player can choose to get a Beat by turning a failure into a dramatic failure).

                                                            What do you think? If that's not something you would want to attach numbers to, or include in the game at all, I totally understand and can omit it. My goal here is to have something with mild mechanical weight and an incentive to mix things up by doing interesting things that aren't totally in my character's favor (which could also be you saying "hey i'll give you a beat to do X thing i want to happen"), not to be disruptive to the party and self-sabotaging or make other players uncomfortable or frustrated.
                                                            Please speak up if you don't want that as part of the game in the way I describe! On the strictly mechanical side, here is the mental Condition I'm working on. Let me know what you think or if you suggest any changes.

                                                            Divergent [Persistent]
                                                            Your character has a difficult time relating to other people and himself. If the self is a bowl of clear water, his bowl ripples and boils and freezes and spills more easily than most in response to stimuli. The character has learned to manage the Condition most of the time, but stressful situations can make this more difficult. This Condition has an Active and Passive form.
                                                            Passive: The character experiences symptoms, but can set them aside. When they have less than half their Willpower remaining, or when they fail a Resolve+Composure roll in response to a stressor, the Condition switches to Active for the rest of the scene.
                                                            Active: The character's symptoms are more pronounced. Apply a -2 to all Social rolls and rolls involving Resolve. The character has 1 less Withstand against Mind spells targeting them. When they regain their full Willpower pool, or at the end of the scene, this Condition switches to Passive unless they still have less than half their Willpower remaining.

                                                            Resolution: The character's illness is cured by mundane or, more likely, supernatural means.
                                                            Beat: The character acts in a significantly self-defeating way due to their illness, or fails a Resolve+Composure roll called for by the Storyteller to resist a stressor.
