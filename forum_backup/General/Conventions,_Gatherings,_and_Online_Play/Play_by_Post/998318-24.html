    Dr. Roman looks on impassively, his face a tableau of disappointment. He makes a couple of notes on the paper, and writes for a good long couple of minutes, pausing every so often to flip pages, consult some charts. He leaves Adonis breathing hard, strapped upright to a metal slab, staring out with impotent fury through thick glass. He sighs, pushing his glasses up the bridge of his nose, and rubbing a hand down his face. From his reaction, it doesn't look like he's learned anything new. He stands up from the chair where he was sitting, and walks up to the thick glass, resting a hand on it, allowing his full weight to be taken by the glass.

    "You know so little of alchemical theory. You can do things holy men go lifetimes trying to accomplish as a side effect of your occult biology; you cannot even see what a wonder you are. Your arms, legs, torso, head, they should all be rejecting each other, you never should have gotten up off the table you were stitched on, but you did. Your existence is miraculous, but not without... undesirable side effects, I'll grant you that." He shakes his head, lowering it a bit a chuckling "The Great Work. Do you even begin to comprehend what the New Dawn actually is? What your kind does when they grant themselves a soul? It is unison with the One, the guiding Principle of creation. In that moment you could do anything..." The doctor's eyes are alight with possibility, with an awe approaching holy ecstasy. He is interrupted from his reverie by a knock on the heavy metal door at the end of the small hallway.

    "Ah, that would be my new assistant. I requested someone be transferred earlier this evening when I received the news that so many new subjects had been acquired. Hopefully this isn't too much of a shock, we're a little understaffed in the, uh, specialist department, but I've been told he's a brilliant biochemist." He presses a button on a wall intercom and says, in a crisp, friendly tone, "The door is open, come right on in." The thick steel door glides slowly open on greased hinges, and Dr. Roman's lab assistant stands in the doorway, frozen, eyes wide and lips parted ever so slightly in shock. It's a facial expression Adonis remembers fondly on the face looking with confusion at his battered state. Erwin stands slack-jawed at the end of what now feels like a much longer hallway.

                        On the first floor of the building, Earth wanders around, mostly chasing after Satin, whose silvery raven form is keen-eyed and instinctually attracted to shiny baubles. The fire slowly starts to smoulder, but it's small enough that it hasn't triggered the fire alarm yet. Slowly, the flames start to lick their way up the potted plants, but living wood resists burning, sap and sugar and water holding back the flames. They have a few minutes before the alarms go off, and then a couple minutes more after that before the fire department gets there. Satin crows at Earth, flying in tight spirals by the button for the elevator. There's a card reader (Earth recognizes those from the stores they've bought things at. Humans use little plastic things as a promise of payment), but no keyhole. Need to find something like that.

The blue-skinned Nepri pauses, as if about to say something, already rattled by tonight's events, but he breathes deeply and asks his question anyways. "Did you ever figure out what you wanted to about intentions? Why people do things?"

                        Scarlet smiles languidly at Scout, almost smirking in anticipation "I can drive men mad with desire, or terror, or regret. I can cure sickness and worsen the impurities or mind, body, and soul. Everyone is impure, everyone has faults. But not everyone knows what they are or how far they'll go. I can help them find out."

                        *****

                        Inside that suddenly dead quiet room, Adonis' voice rings out. Erwin is still frozen in the doorway, his hand still on the door, his arm visibly shaking, whether from constrained emotion that only emerges from his swiftly flickering eyes and twitching cheeks or from the sheer weight of the metal bearing down on him, trying to seal this awful tableau from his sight. Dr. Roman raises an eyebrow, slowly looking back and forth from his subject and his assistant. He steps slowly towards the door, those footsteps the only sound, and wordlessly pushes past Erwin. "Excuse me- Erwin, is it? I need to make a call." The door swings shut quickly with no one the doorway, clanging shut with absolute finality. Erwin's face is exploding with emotion, hands resting on his head, pacing back and forth, air and curses emerging explosively in a staccato hiss between clenched teeth. He spins quickly on his heel, looking with terrified and confused eyes at the Muse's cell.

                        "What the fuck Mark. What the fuck is this?" His fingers run through his close-cropped hair raking at his scalp. He paces back and forth, shaking his head, hands grabbing onto his own biceps with enough force that if his nails were a little longer he'd draw blood. As it is, you can see his fingernails go white from the pressure cutting off the blood flow. He lets out a frustrated scream that echoes in the narrow hallway and punches the wall, which turns quickly to pained yelps. He has no experience hitting things, and concrete is not forgiving. Erwin worked with chemicals, but if there is one thing you always remembered about those hands it's that they were soft and so terribly gentle. He could touch water without breaking the surface.

                        "Who are you? Who are you really? Shit, I told you everything. I told you about university, I told you about my sister, I told you about the debt these sons of bitches were drowning me in until I turned around and started working for them!" It started as a hiss and ended in a thunderous shout. You've never heard his voice quite that loud before. "Fuck. I was almost out. I was this close. This. Close. I was getting out, Mark! I enrolled at the U of M for the Winter Term starting in January! I was going to do my residency! I was going to tell you this weekend when we met up with my sister for dinner. She's in town just for a few days, wants to be home in time for Christmas with mom and dad." He realizes he's babbling and shakes his head, sliding slowly to the floor, his voice dropping to a hoarse whisper, the outrage bleeding out of him, leaving the hurt behind.

                        "Please just... be open with me. For once. Don't wait for me to tell you to tell me something about yourself."

                                                        Adonis feels the power of Scout's Azoth ripple through the building like a loud clap of lightning. For a moment, he can't help but feel his Azoth rise a little in happiness. They came for us, he thinks. If Scout is here, Mark 5 and Earth have to have followed him, neither Named would let him come here on his own. But even as his happiness grows, he hears the horrible sound of Pandorans scratching and clawing. Somebody just rang the dinner bell. They are going to have to deal with that, and fast.

                                                        But before he can think anything else, Dr. Roman talks back in. He prepares to start taking Adonis' vitriol forcibly, but then a figure uses a Distillation he does not recognize and attacks the doctor. It's Erwin.

                                                        Adonis blinks and says, "Hell yeah. Get me off this thing." As soon as the restraints are loose, he rises up and kisses Erwin full on the cheeks. "I'm sorry about everything. But we need to get out of here. Some of my friends have come to rescue us, we need to find them. But we need to do it fast, there's at least one Pandoran in here. Either a bunch of them or a really big one. We might need to burn down the building to get rid of it. Is there anything you want to bring with you? Research notes, alchemical texts? Anything related to... what did Roman call me? Homunculi? No, wait, first, get me to a power source. A breaker or something. The electricity is close enough to the Divine Fire to heal me."

                                                            Scout
                                                            Conditions: Introspective, Informed (Amalgama LTD), Disquiet 2 (Amalgama), Steadfast, Tormented
                                                            Alembics: Heed the Call (2) Plumb the Fathoms (0) Internalize (3) Weaponize (3)
                                                            Health: OOOOOO | Willpower: 6/6 | Pyros: 5/13 | Beats: 2/5 | Vitriol Beats: 4/5
                                                            XP: 1 | VXP: 1


                                                            Scout could smell Adonis's Azothic Radiance among the others- a familiar scent he'd come to recognize, like a sweet white wine. The various other aromas assaulted his senses, and he recoiled slightly. So many alchemists... they tasted hollow, like their flavor would slip away after but a moment, like a stick of gum. The sour scent of the pandorans warred with it. There were other smells- those he didn't know as well. "I can feel... a score of alchemists. Adonis... others... lots of pandorans that are probably wreaking havoc on the alchemists. And..."

                                                            He paused as he discerned the final fragrance. Highwind had given him a chocolate once. A pure cocao creation, sweet and rich. He had received it from a friend in a far off country that Scout only wished he new. The genitor had made him take it slow with that, scenting it, tasting it, appreciating it. Scout sometimes wished he could recreate it with alchemy.That was what the pure azoth reminded him of.

                                                            Scout looked up, "I... cannot be sure, but I think they have something else. It is an Azoth so pure... no torment, no flux. Just pure, refined Azoth. I... think it may be an Angel." He stopped, and pointed towards where he could feel it all flowing from, "It is that way. They are all close together. If we are lucky, the Unborn and the Fire will keep the Alchemists busy."
