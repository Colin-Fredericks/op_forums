    The Throng

    Things go from bad to worse very quickly, as they come across the charnel ground that Mark 5 has produced in the rainy streets of Winnipeg. The human crowd is distracted by pursuing and berating the drunk, but in time they'll make their way over here, and then things will get ugly. More than ugly, downright violent. There will be police, and there will be more fighting, and chances are if the Created try to stay free there will be more death, more suffering, more Torment, more Disquiet. A Wasteland would begin to fester and bubble, and the fires would start smouldering.

    Fire and blood, that's what will come from this. Fire and blood, ash and death. No creation, only destruction. No Azoth, only Flux. No Pilgrimage. No New Dawn.
    Just futile stagnation.

    Earth flares with alchemical might as electricity arcs from nearby outlets and starts blazing like flame in holes burned into her skin. She seems to swell, to become greater than the sum of her parts, something mighty and divine, glorious and terrible. She lunges at the Titan, fists raised over her head, and that's when the Muse, surprisingly agile, and sanguinely, foolishly, reactive, leaps onto her back and weighs her down. Mark 5 rises and unfolds like a volcanic peak, both arms batting aside Earth's before grabbing hold of the collar of her shirt and hurling her to the ground, hard, with almost contemptuous ease. There's blood on her clothes now, and black bile in her mouth, she bit her tongue in the fall, and Adonis has fallen off, a little bruised and battered. The Titan is stronger than her, better than her, mocking her, like Heartsblood did. Well, lightning took care of Earth's genitor, and it will take care of Mark 5 just the same.

    All she needs to do is reach for it...

    Scout can feel something stirring in the overlap between their Azoth, a swirling cauldron of terror the likes of which he's never felt before, but which Highwinds warned him about. The wrath and creative energy of the Divine Fire unbound, the Flame Immortal let loose on the world in its purest form. Firestorm.

    An explosion of Pyros erupts between Earth and the Titan while Adonis lies on the ground, huddling to try and protect himself from the worst of what is to come. Unfolding like a beautiful and terrible flower, the rain roars into a torrential downpour, each droplet laced with Divine Fire, burning at their skin, setting their humours alight, while the winds of a hurricane lash out and buffet at the still-standing Titan, screaming like an offended god. The sound, the sound, is a screaming loud enough to drown out even the sound of your own voice, sheets of rain so thick that all you can see if the glowing of the divine lightning in Earth's skin, and the hand in front of your face. The Firestorm tears at the environment around them, hurling debris at the bodies, tearing skin and flesh from the bone with whips of wind. You swear you can hear a voice, a small still whisper, somewhere in the heart of the hurricane. Your flesh starts to writhe, and to Earth the sensation is sickeningly familiar as it starts to twist and manifest into sublime monstrosity, the Firestorm shaping you like plague-ridden clay into something like one of the Unborn.

    The Dead Soldiers murmur in Regret's ear, and something within the rain calls to Scout. The whispers sound like 'Witness Them'.

    OOC: Alright, so, Earth gains a Beat from the Dramatic Failure, and a second Beat from her Refinement Condition.
    Earth also suffers the Knocked Down Tilt, and six points of Bashing Damage.
    Mark 5 gains a Beat from the Exceptional Success on the grappling roll.
    Adonis gains a Beat for putting himself at significant risk throwing himself at the two Created-of-Mass-Destruction.

    Earth, Mark 5, Scout, and Regret are each experiencing a Milestone, and are also having an Elpis Vision that will last through the Firestorm, which will last the next three turns.

    The Firestorm is a Extreme Environment 4, which means that after a number of turns equal to your Stamina you'll start taking Lethal Damage. The Firestorm also grants the Heavy Rain and Heavy Winds Tilt. Because it is Strength 3, the Heavy Winds inflict 3B damage per turn from debris and sheer wind strength. Roll Dexterity + Athletics -7 due to Environmental Penalties if you'd like to avoid damage. The Flesh-Warping aspect gives each of you three dots worth of Pandoran Dread Powers, so describe the mutation however you would. This will likely cause Earth to revert to her most familiar shape as Earth-Dies-Screaming. Learning to control the mutations takes a Stamina roll, for which Earth gets a +2 due to being a former Centimanus.
