    Fateful Lotus Insight
    Cost: 5m, 1wp Mins: Martial Arts 5, Essence 3 Type: Reflexive (Step 1 Attacker, Step 2 Defender)
    Keywords: Combo-OK
    Duration: One Scene
    Prerequisites: Lotus Eye Tactics
    The Viziers have a the ability to intuitively understand any fighting style they see. It is a simple step to exploit this knowledge in combat. Reading their opponents movements, they know exactly where they will strike, and therefore, how to block it. Analyzing their opponent's stance, they know what points are most vulnerable, and therefore, where to best to strike. This charm allows the Sidereal to enter into a Trance-like state, gaining them these most-potent of insights. When active, this charm allows the Sidereal to add their (Essence) dice to any Throne Shadow-style attack, and adds +1 to their DVs against any opponent whose presence they are aware off.
