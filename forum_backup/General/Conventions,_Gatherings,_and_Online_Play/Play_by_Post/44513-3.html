                Backstory:
                Yi Zhinan is a Lookshyan not born of Lookshy. The distinction has condemned him to the life of a metic, but he did not resent it overly much. Sent to the city at a young age, he proved himself worthy of admission to the Academy of Sorcery by answering a difficult koan that had troubled researchers there. Shortly after earning a brevet commission as a Sorcerer-Engineer, Yi met Karal Zhan, a Terrestrial prodigy and his commanding Chozei. He was instantly enthralled by the young Fire Aspect's passion and compassion toward the lower ranks. Through great effort Yi eventually rose to the rank of Sorcerer-Technician and traded many powerful favors to remain assigned to Zhan's command.

                For two decades, a blink of a eye in a Terrestrial's lifespan and half a lifetime to a mortal, this situation continued into the Battle of the Kumrath. What was originally a small skirmish with some of the Mask of Winter's forces near the hamlet of Kumrath turned into a major engagement when the dead troops turned out to supported by a Deathknight. It was during the battle, with Yi commanding the artillery in a desperate duel with their necromantic counterparts, that Zhan was cut off from support and surrounded. Yi violated direct orders and had his squad turn their fire to support the commander. At the same time he took up the sword of a comrade and rallied the leaderless portion of the Lookshy forces to link back up with the Zhan and drive the enemy back.

                It was in the midst of that frantic charge when Yi rejoined Zhan and finally told him how he felt. In proof that even the Most High can have terrible timing, that was when he took his second breath. There were no more words between them that day, nor between any of Yi's and any of his men. They neither fled nor attacked him. He joined the battle against the Abyssal and none questioned or hindered him. Though the battle was won, spirits were low both from the terrible casualties and from the birth of an Anathema from among their own.

                There were no words on the march home to Lookshy, and there was a great deal of space about him. The general staff was furious for his violation of orders and he was court martialed and sentenced to death. The night before his execution a familiar figure met him in prison bearing a heavy bundle. The items within were all great treasures, no doubt stolen at great personal risk, and the figure left the door open as he departed. One item, seemingly worthless besides the artifacts and chance at freedom, Yi still treasures above all the rest. It is a simply made scroll on which is written the short message. "Family is more than blood."
