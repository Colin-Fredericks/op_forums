                                The spin is "keeping the Realm strong and delaying the inevitable."
                                Honored Grandmother,

                                I am sure I bring you no new information when I say that the rebellion in the North has powerful backers. Let us call them nothing more than Golden Cranes in an open letter. Knowing this, I sought a way to keep the Realm strong and undistracted at this critical time. My sources say that the root of this rebellion is the horrific drug-addled policies of house Cynis. As it is, the violence will spiral out of control - but if that impetus is removed, the majority of rebels will lay down their arms. There will be nothing for the Cranes to work with.

                                With that out of the way, my plan itself was rather simple. Tepet and Cynis were to exchange control of the Slave Coast and Hohenzon. Tepet can concede the rebel's demands without loss of face, and win back some of their dignity by crushing the remaining forces. Cynis can move troops south to take control of Hohenzon before it breaks from the Realm's orbit entirely. I am sure you can work out yourself the advantages to our house of having Tepet believe I am on their side, so I shall not trouble to list them.

                                Perhaps violence is inevitable - the current state of affairs grows less stable by the day - but I hope the necessary maneuvering to strengthen your position does not weaken the Realm as a whole. "Power through knowledge, mastery through rigor, and conquest through diligence.” I believe there is more we can gain in the ballroom before resorting to the battle field.

                                - Mnemon Aisa
