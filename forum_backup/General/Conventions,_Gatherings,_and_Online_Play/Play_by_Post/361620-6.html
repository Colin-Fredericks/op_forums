                            It's kind of complicated. From what I understand, you recognize Flynn's Resplendent Destiny when you see him, but you won't remember any specific details or features about him. If you try to describe Flynn as you know him, you'll be able to remember that he is a musician. Maybe that he carried some sort of instrument.

                            You'd remember even less if you met Flynn as a Sidereal. You'll know that there was some man present. And he talked to you. Maybe.

                            So if you meet Flynn as the traveling musician and then later on meet Flynn as himself, you'll see them as two different people. If Flynn the Sidereal makes references to something that happens to you and Flynn the Musician, then he gets a point of paradox.

                            Arcane Fate sucks for Sidereals too. It's very difficult to make relationships with non-sidereals. Resplendent Destinies have a expiration date. So if over the course of time The Servant and Flynn become good friends, as soon as Flynn's Resplendent Destiny ends, you won't remember Flynn the Musician. Flynn would have to restart the friendship from scratch and risk gaining paradox.

                            Anyone else feel free to correct me if I got any of the above wrong. As I said, it's kind of complicated.
