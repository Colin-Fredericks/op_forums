    With it's newly planted trees, the forest seemed more pacified than it had been previously. At least around Max, anyways. The sounds of the wilderness went very quiet, as if trying to let him rest after his hard work. Any sounds that continued were too far off to be much of a disturbance. And hey, this time nothing emerged from the ground or the trees or the water to try and eat him! Unfortunately, the environment wasn't what he had to worry about, because as he lost consciousness, another entity casually approached him.

    "You should have listened to Pisces, slave." the figure spoke with a woman's voice, before clasping a pair of shackles to his wrists and ankles.


    ---------------------------------------------


    Despite Nate's fears, the supply of berries seemed limitless, as did the bottles that were being churned out of the machine. The room seemed to be able to contain the limitless supply, almost as if the interior was getting bigger with each bottle added to it's storage! Of course in his drunken stupor, it's not likely Nate could appreciate the magic behind it. Suddenly, the sounds of high pitched giggling and gentle moans could be heard from nearby. A trap door Nate hadn't noticed before hung open at the back of the wine storage, and the feminine sounds were coming from below. It seemed his private room had a basement level, too!


    As if responding to his music, some guests began to ascend from the trap door, and launched into playful dancing as they emerged. True enough, they were all female, and they were all stark naked. They seemed to be gathered from a variety of different races including forest nymphs, elves, and even goat people like himself. Some resembled his previous form being almost entirely goat, others were satyrs having the upper body of a human.


    Having an endless supply of wine, the girls began uncorking bottles and showering each other with the fluids as they danced and drank, and partied with Nate's music! They even began cheering him on, begging him for more entertainment. Complimenting him as being the best party thrower they'd ever seen, and even making sexual advances. If Nate wasn't in paradise before, he certainly was now. It seemed everything in this room was designed to enthrall him!

More so than the Eastern set -- for now, at least -- they were what the Keeper had made them to be. If she an instrument of the Keeper's justice was blind, she wasn't going to be easily swayed. 'Knowing him, that veil probably lets her see everything clearly. As long as it doesn't look anything like real justice.'

                        It struck Max as an important insight, albeit a discouraging one. It was depressing, too. For all that she had kicked him and intended worse, he couldn't help feeling a little sorry for her, and it showed in his expression. Still, the Pig was mainly preoccupied with, well... saving his bacon. 'There's gotta be something I can do... Just... keep talking. Stall until I think of it.'                                                             On the floor of the room appeared 12 small circular platforms, disposed in a semi-circle in front of the throne. One by one in pufs of smoke the members of the Chinese Zodiac appeared there, dressed for the ocasion with oriental looking formal robes. Again, all save for the horse, whose platform remained empty and akward.

                                                            "Better?"-hissed the Keeper to its annoying secretary. The secretary mostly ignored the question save for a quick nod, focused as he was cheking the list on his Scroll.

                                                            "Well, then"-the Keeper smiled and got up from its throne, bidding the Goat to aproach.
