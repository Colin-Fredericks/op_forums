    10-30-2014, 09:43 AM
    Hello everyone! Welcome to the game!

    I found this neat format for creating your vampire or werewolf character. If you find it works for you, go ahead and make your character using this. You can also post the character on this thread if you want. It doesn't matter to me, so long as I get it somehow.

    So far, the roster is as follows.

    FeroKE is Gabriel Montegut, a.k.a. Xochipilli Grins-Through-Blood, a Nuwisha Smiling Wanderer with a very uncertain past.
    The guy from Google + is Daniel, a young shepard turned True Brujah, who's laconic sire is guiding him to a very uncertain future. Yes, there are sheep in the part of Texas that we are playing in.
    AkatsukiLeader13 is ???, who is very uncertain altogether it seems
    MossSocks is ???, who is ALSO very uncertain altogether

    If you guys would like, I can start running the game BEFORE your characters are finished. If you got a concept in your head, that is good enough for now, and you can put numbers on a sheet as we go.

    In fact, I can't remember a game in which I had everyone's character sheet....

    AkatsukiLeader13 and     MossSocks: Reply to this thread with your concept and character name if you want to get started right away. Happy gaming!
