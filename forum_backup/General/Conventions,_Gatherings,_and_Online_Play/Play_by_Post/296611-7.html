                                            I tentatively vote for the golem - we can always use a strong silent type. There are two general possibilities I can see here..

                                            1: His Keeper chose him because he's already a guardian of sorts. So,. bouncer. bodyguard, gang enforcer, or just someone fiercely protective. He could've been present for an attempted abduction, throwing himself at the Fae to protect someone more important.

                                            2: His keeper chose him because he has a strong affinity with earth and metal. Construction worker, welder, miner - lots of salt-of-the-earth manual labor here. He could end up a surprisingly crafty brute - for all our Wizened, we don't really have anyone good at making things, so there's a niche.

                                            As to his Court? Well, Summer seems the obvious choice, embracing his militaristic purpose and the heat of his desert Durance, but I find that nearly all courts can fit nearly all concepts, it's just a matter of mindset. You just escaped from Hell, how do you deal with that?

                                            The Summer courtier says "I want to hurt Them even worse than they hurt me."
                                            The Spring courtier says "I want to reclaim my life as best I can, and try to forget this madness."
                                            The Autumn courtier says "I want to know what just happened, what I am now, and what *they* are."
                                            The Winter courtier says "I want to be safe, at all costs. I never want to be wounded again."

                                            Given these broad strokes, he's probably Summer-ish? If not, he's in the running for some Goodwill.
