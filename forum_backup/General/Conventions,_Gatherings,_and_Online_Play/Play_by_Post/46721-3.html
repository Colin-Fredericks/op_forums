                    Saturday April 12th, 2003

I have information that weapon that I seek is somewhere in Auckland."

                        Hoplite's wooden chair creaked as he leaned back in it and waited for an interesting message. Raw spikes of jagged fear. Disbelieving wonder. The tingle of someone with a Nimbus.
                        But I'm not going to post it. Instead, I'll summarize it:

                        Miss Watson developed a hidden radio system in which she can freely talk to Hei and listen to her conversations. Her plan was to have Hei distract Hoplite while Miss Watson cast a complicated rote in order to get through whatever shield he could have put up and detect his psychological responses. This way, Miss Watson would anonymously watch from a distance and record ANY activity, magical or not, and have Hei ask personal questions and basically lie-detect him. She hopes to use the report as a ticket for Hoplite to gain trust where there is none. She however worries that this plan had the Xanatos Speed Chess problem of being unreliable if anything happened out of expectation. If she couldn't come to a full conclusion, then Hoplite would be most likely be turned away.

                        Hei on the other hand finds free money (Hoplite's by super coincidence) and attempts to buy drink with it. But Morvri, a Mage who is very distrustful of Hoplite's credential as Obediah's friend of a friend, comes by and interrogate Hei about where Miss Watson is in order to stop her operation, not knowing about the hidden radio and that Miss Watson can hear everything. Morvri then tries to convince Hei that this is a stupid idea and explains why he is trying to stop them.

                        Miss Watson, who is listening in, calls Lily and interrupts her oggling at (seemingly) asleep Hoplite's pretty face and tells her to change location. Then Miss Watson tells Hei to shake off Morvri, but he knows what's up. Before Hei can walk away, two people teleports in out of nowhere and blocks her path (source of disbelief and Nimbus). Morvri then whips out a magical Fate warrant permitting him to strip away Hei's Prime protection in order to interrogate her and recites the Mage Miranda Rights. Hei decides it's worth it to                         not kick their asses and let Miss Watson have a second chance at finishing the job. Hei surrenders and gets involuntarily scared (source of jagged fear) when the shield goes away.

                        Now at this point, I was tempted to have the Magic Money read Hei's mind as soon as her Prime shield goes off, which would reveal to Hoplite about the plan, reason why Hei wanted to help him along with her past, and part of Miss Watson's true name. It would have hilariously ridiculous, but I noticed how long it was already and decided not to.

                        Miss Watson, missing half the equipment and Hei, decides Lily is the only option and calls her for the job.

                        After seeing your recent post however, I realized I had a problem: this took too long for me to write, and I needed to scrap half of it. It is my fault for trying to write such a big plot, but I mainly did it because I thought I needed to incorporate that little end information, but didn't know what you expected out of it. I thought you wanted a conflict that Hoplite would get into, but then that would have delayed the Miss Watson's interrogation, which would in turn delay getting into the main plot. So I tried to tie those two together. But now it's become irrelevant, and I back to scratch.

                        So before Hei or Miss Watson can act, may I ask you what you expect to happen?
