    Gens Midea
    Population:     ~14000 (7 Boule Seats)
    Capital: Pirae (East of Novalesk City)
    Major Industries: Descended from a shapeshifter of indeterminable origins, Gens Midea was a Nikoloi cadet house until the mid sixth century post-contagion. Much like their progenitor, Gens Midea believes strongly in Versatility and Flexibility. As such, they have a highly well-rounded economic portfolio. This synergizes well with Gens Nikoloi, as they can provide a wide variety of goods for Nikoloi merchants.
    Political Position: As fitting its former position as a Nikoloi cadet house, Gens Midea is still content to mostly follow Nikoloi's lead. Midea, however, often advocates for increased military activity, especially against Ordon and Theros. This creates some friction between Midea and Nikoloi, but Midea has seen fit to continue to support Nikoloi's power bloc for the time being.
    Cadet Families: Diotus (~1300), Oglica (~800)

        Gens Zophyrus
        Population:         ~27000 (14 Boule Seats)
        Capital: Agio (Central Novlaesk; land-locked)
        Major Industries: Gens Zophyrus dominates Novalesk's Agriculture. Their capital city was purposely located to this effect, giving the family easy access to the fields of central Novlaesk. The Gens controls many farms and ranches, and supplies a majority of the nation's food.
        Political Position: Zophyrus resents Nikoloi's control over the carrying trade, as it cuts into their profit margins as Novalesk's breadbasket. Due to its position in Novalesk's interior, Zophyrus is somewhat desensitized to the military tensions between Novalesk and its neighbors. Due to Nikoloi's reluctance to trade with the Ordon, and Ordon's near-constant desire for foodstuffs, Zophyrus has begun dominating trade with Novalesk's southern neighbor. This hurts their relationship with Spyrus and Midea, who have borne the brunt of Novalesk's somewhat frequent wars with the Ordoni.
        Cadet Families: Metaxas (~2300), Arcadios (~700)

            Gens Althea
            Population:             ~9000 (5 Boule Seats)
            Capital: Vera (North-east Novalesk; farthest from Novalesk City on bank of Lake)
            Major Industries: Althea's primary industry revolves around Raw Material Collection. Althea operates many mines and lumber camps outside of Novalesk's nominal borders, ensuring a steady supply of raw goods to the other Gentes' artisans. Althea also has negotiated trade agreements with the handful of nomadic barbarian tribes to the East, gaining access to even more material.
            Political Position: Gens Althea is an elevated Cadet House, formerly under the patronage of Gens Zophyrus. Elevated in the early eighth century post-contagion, many within Althea have yet to grasp yet that they are no longer bound to their former patron's will. Althea tends to follow Zophyrus in lockstep. Their one notable divergence is their positive relationship with the Geherin, who Zophyrus dislikes due to tensions between the barbarian confederation and Ordon.
            Cadet Families: None

                        -Novalesk-
                        The nation of Novalesk lies along the Rock River, nearly due East of Mount Metagapala. Descended from a handful of survivors of the Dual disasters of the Great Contagion and Balorian Crusade. Founding the town of Novalesk, they slowly grew their numbers, creating a number splinter towns, a handful of which evolved into cities in their own right.

                        The people of Novalesk are, generally speaking, a rough and practical people, lacking in ostentation. This does not make them stupid or provincial; Novalesk has a proud scholarly tradition, dating back centuries. Nor do they despise art, though they much prefer solid, lasting art like statuary and tapestry to more transient art forms. They are stubborn and resilient, though, and that has let them survive and even thrive the strife of the Second Age.

                        -Society-
                        There are three classes in Novlaesk: The Citizen, The National, and the Metic. Nationals and Citizens are members of the Gentes and their Cadet Houses, with the only difference between them being that all Citizens are essence users. The Nationals and citizens each have the same Rights, but the Citizens have the franchise and are able to elect Representatives to the Boule, and any Citizen may attend and address the Boule. Metics are resident aliens, who have been granted leave to live and work in Novalesk.

                        The great houses of Novalesk are the six Gentes. Each Gens contains a cross section of society, from the lowest beggar to the richest aristocrat. The Gentes gain representation in the Boule assembly based on relative population, and may sponsor Metic families (most commonly large groups of refugees) into 'Cadet Family' status. Members of Cadet Families are considered Citizens or Nationals, though their Representatives to the Boule are merely sanctioned observers. Cadet Houses are generally subservient to their sponsor, though some friction between the two is inevitable.

                        Blood ties are highly important to Novalesk; a Cadet Family is only risen Great House Status when the Boule believes that that family integrated their bloodlines and culture with that of the original Novalesk population (With Gens Spyrus being a notable exception, as it was founded before the inception of the Cadet house system).

                        -Government-
                        The Legal authority within each city is the ruling Gens, who each have their own methods for creating and enforcing law. Gens law, however, is superseeded by and cannot contradict National law, who are laid down from two sources: The Archon and the Boule.

                        The Boule consists of fifty Representatives, each elected by their Gens' Citizen population from the Citizen population. The Boule may pass and redact legislation, as well as ordering the Strategoi. The Boule also sets the taxation rate, as well as any tariffs and trade duties. The Boule also elects a new Archon upon the death, ascension or resignation of the previous Archon (A vote requiring a 60% majority).

                        The Archon (who is elected for life) has supreme executive authority in Novalesk, and may appoint and order the Strategoi. The Archon also holds the powers of high, middle and low justice, and is the sole person capable of authorizing binding treaties between Novalesk and any other external power. The Archon may also appoint up to three subordinates, known as Thesmothetes. Each Thesmothete has a lesser set of executive authority, and may speak on their master's behalf. Their power derives from the Archon, however, who may disavow them and their actions at any time. All Thesmothetes are dismissed with the termination of the appointing Archon. An Archon must be a citizen, but in at least two occasions, the Boule has granted citizen status to a national in order to elect them.

                        -Economy-
                        Novalesk has the resources to see to all of its own economic needs, and then some. It has thriving agricultural, manufacturing and intellectual sectors, largely due to its need to be self-sufficient form its neighbors. Novalesk's one major resource scarcity is iron, though the nation has sufficient copper and tin deposits to make this merely an annoyance.

                        It does, however, trade regularly with neighboring nations, albeit at highly variable rates. Novalesk trades manufactured goods to the Geherin in exchange for a vast collection of exotic plants. With Ordon, foodstuffs (particularly grain and game) are traded for technology and lore from the lost ages. With Theros, Novalesk trades thaumaturgical products and the occasional artifact in exchange for iron.

                        Novalesk's primary external trade runs up and down the Rock River, facilitated by the Guild. Novalesk's combination of occult knowledge and natural resources makes it a cheap place to obtain thaumaturgical products. Many a Guild ship pulls into Novalesk laid down with luxury goods from Nexus and Great Forks, and leaves laden down with alchemical solutions and thaumaturgical talismans.

                        -Military-
                        Novalesk is the product of its times; as a child of the Age of Strife, it is a military nation. most members of the Gentes are trained in military tactics and weapons when young, and drill regularly throughout their lives. A small number of residents are career soldiers, forming the skeleton of the military. The meat of Novalesk's military, however, comes from the 'cyclers' a relatively large group which signs up for year-long service stints, after which they return to trade for several years before rotating back into the military. This ensures the military is well stocked with trained artisans, as well as allowing for the maintenance of a larger pool of trained soldiers.

                        The ruling military power in Novalesk is the Strategoi. While the Archon and the Boule have the powers to command the Strategoi and raise troops, they rarely interfere with them in times of War. The Strategoi have the power to raise troops and command them independently from the Gentes, and with authorization from the Archon, may order the Gentes to place thier household troops at their command.

                                                Novalesk is particularly blessed when it comes to Essence users. Because they had what is totally not a Twilight as their Founder and a trio of people who were certainly not Half-castes as their ancestors, they've felt a certain desire to place spiritual enlightenment as a cultural strong point.                                                 Your average well-developed nation likely has somewhere between 10 and 20 essence users per 1000 humans. Highly Immaculate nations like Lookshy and the Realm will be on the lower end, but they're more likely to have thriving Terrestrial bloodlines, so they'll have individually stronger people.
