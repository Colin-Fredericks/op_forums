                    So while you guys are building your character I thought I'd give you some more background info about some of the mages, cabals and structures of the Consilium your chars would likely know about.

                    The Consilium has for security reasons compartmentalized information, thus your characters won't be aware of every mage or even cabal in the city, but here are some things your characters are aware of.
                    Positions


                    Hierach & Council - The Hierach, in theory, reigns supreme. In actuality he or she is dependant on the support of his or her councilors to keep her position. Thus the Council takes the place of advisors that cannot simply be ignored. Should a majority of the Council (3 or more) members vote to depose the current Hierach he or she must resign.

                    Provosts - A sort of personal Herald for the individual Council members, the Provosts speak with the voice of their Councilor and handle most buisness for them, affording the Councilors themselves privacy and more importantly security. It's a highly heterogenous position. Due to the constant strife of the last decades the position has often been filled by less experienced members of the Consilium since more established members didn't want to take the risk. Provosts therefor vary vastly in their magical proficiency and status. A member in an order usually is aware of a contact protocol for their order's Provost.

                    Sentinels - The Sentinels are the protectors of the Consilium and are entitled to commandeer any assistance wether personal or material from any mage of the consilium in line of their duty, as long as no member of the Council (or by extension their Provosts) says otherwise. There are currently two Sentinels, both Arrows, though the post has been known to be held by a Libertine in the past aswell. The current Sentinels are Benkei and Gallahad.

                    Heralds - The Heralds are the lifeline of the Consilium. They are aware of the cabals of the consilium, their members and most of their sancta. In return every mage of the Consilium is aware of ways to contact every Herald. When one mage needs to get into contact with another they usually arrange a meeting via one of them. Because of the high amount of potentially disastrous information they posess every Herald is protected by several powerful protective spells (and restrictive Geas) laid upon them by the council and any attempt to subvert, influence or otherwise mess with the Heralds are summarily punished by death. Heralds usually serve for life and are forbidden from holding any other office for the duration of their term. Should one wish to retire they are required to undergo extensive memory manipulations. Aside from their main tasks each Herald also has special responsibilities such as representing the Concilium to outsders or looking out for specific groups of mages.
                    Territory


                    The docks are disputed territory so to speak, both Seers and some Pentacle mages hold influence here. Tread lightly and don't tread in the water at all. Why? Because something is in there. You can go for a swim (whyever you'd want to) and likely come back in one piece, but mages and others exploring the depths of the bay often disappear.

                    Downtown is Seer country, Mammon Pylons are deeply entrenched in the Skyscrapers of the financial district. Some Libertines try to contest it, but that's risky buisness. ESPECIALLY with the current truce. The Hierach has ordered Pentacle mages to give the area a wide berth.

                    The Foxhall district is off limit to mages. An elusive cabal (The Black Vigil) has taken up residence there and placed a ban around the area barring other mages from entering. They are technically part of the Pentacle but they observe none of the rights and allmost never leave their claimed area. Anyone who makes it past the ban will be killed first and interrogated later. The last guy to try (12 years ago) ended as a particularily bloody spot on the sidewalk outside of Foxhall. Most are simply never seen again.

                    Rushville National Park is a large, heavily forested area about 20 miles away from the city. Claimed by a local Cabal (Animal House) that only allows for crossing along certain paths.

                    Cabals


                    The New Dawn - The Hierachs cabal has made it its goal to usher in a new era of prosperit for the Consilium. It's a rather large cabal that only recently formed. Predominantly Silver Ladder and Adamantine Arrow, though all orders are represented.

                    The Roundtable - The cabal of former Hierach Arthur. Arthur was an Adamantine Arrow and a master of the second degree. He held the title for more than 20 years (quite impressive given the dangers involved) before he died about a year ago. Most of his cabal died in the fighting with him, the remnants keep largely to themselves, though Gallahad holds the position of Sentinel and deputizes his cabalmates frequently. All members of the cabal are Adamantine Arrows and many belong to a Legacy called "Knights of Avalon", said to have been founded by Arthur a decade ago. Arthus was said to posess a powerful artifact in the form of a sword, what has become of it nobody knows.

                    The Swords of Liberty - Alongside the Roundtable the largest and most notorious warcabal in East Bay. They have weathered the storm relativly well and are not happy with the truce. Predominantly Free Council and Arrow, their leader is an experienced Libertine by the name of Theomacha.

                    The Black Vigil - elusive, militaristic isolationist cabal that holds Foxhall. Many silently question wether their enormous secrecy is supposed to cover left handed practices.

                    The Web - predominantly Libertine cabal dedicated to connecting the awakened and spreading information. They have multiple ties to other consilia and cabals near and far.

                    The Melting Pot - After the fierce battles last year many cabals were shattered and since then there has been an influx of new mages in the consilium. The Melting Pot is a cabal founded to give these disenfranchised mages a (temporary) home. It has only a few permanent core members, but refugees and transients are welcome to use communal sancta and hallows until they move on or join a cabal of their own.

                    Known to Tara (and other SL members): The Wise Ones - Predominantly Silver Ladder cabal whose members style themselves after Wise Men (or Women) from history (and myth) and try to guide the lives of sleepers towards awakening. Responsible for most Cryptopoly in East Bay. Cabal of the Silver Ladder Councilor Balthazar and his Provost Ptolemy.

                    Know to Lumiel (and other Mysterium characters, so likely Second Chances' char): The Lords of the Below - A cabal that studies the mysteries of death and specifically of the underworld. Predominantly Mysterium and the keepers of the local Athenaeum. Predominantly Mysterium, the Councilor, her Provost and both Curators are members of this cabal. It's members all styled their shadow names about gods and spirits associated with death.

                    Know to Lumiel (and other Mysterium characters, so likely Second Chances' char): Animal House - The second important Mysterium cabal, though less so than the Lords Below. They have claimed Rushville National Park, about 20 miles from the city, as their territory and allow crossing only along certain paths. Thyrsus heavy, invested in Spirit magics. Their members style their names after animals.
                    Mages


                    Phoenix - The new Hierach. Very young for her position (or so it is said, she looks somewhere between 20 and 30, but due to magic that doesn't mean much) and very talented for her age. Came to East Bay merely five years ago but has gained great respect among the Arrow, Free Council and Ladder in this short time. Didn't ascend to the Hierachy uncontested, but strengthened her claim with the Duel Arcane (she appears to be a brilliant duelist). Far from being a pacifist she fought on the frontlines, but believes it's important to clean house and rebuild before fighting the seers. If both sides destroy each other completly the only ones winning are the Exarchs. She's Obrimos and a Théarch.

                    Hope - A young Obrimos who only finished her apprenticeship 2 years ago. Phoenix' student and now her Provost. Member of the New Dawn and a Théarch.

                    Benkei - A large and ugly fellow with a propensity for swordmanship. Member of the New Dawn, Adamantine Arrow and one of the Consiliums Sentinels.

                    Theomacha - Longtime strategos of the local Assembly and therefore also Councilor for the Free Council, though recently some voices wonder wether the two positions should be decoupled. It's said that if any mage managed to make a personal enemy of the Father, it's her. Fiercly opposed to the Seers of the Throne and the Ministry of Paternoster in particular, she leads the Swords of Liberty and is one of the most vocal proponents for continued warfare. She was a longtime friend of the former Hierach, Arthur, and is one of the few Masters in the Consilium.

                    Gaia - A matronly Théarch who was instrumental in the founding of the Melting Pot. Has connections and allways an open ear for mages new to the Consilium. Member of the Melting Pot.

                    Victoria - Fashionable and Elitist, the Théarch is one of the Consilium's Heralds and tasked with handling external relationships - meaning non-Pentacle mages and other supernatural beings. Member of the New Dawn.

                    Link - Libertine with a profound love for the digital age (and the movie Matrix). One of the Consilium's Heralds and tasked with handling relationships with other Consilia near and far. Member of the Web.

                    Muse - Jovial Libertine who loves art. One of the Consilium's Heralds and tasked with welcoming and advising mages new to the Consilium.

                    Gallahad - Erstwhile Provost of Arthur, now Sentinel of the Consilium. Adamantine Arrow and Member of the Roundtable.

                    Known to Tara (and other SL chars): Balthazar - Elderly black gentleman, Obrimos, Thrice-Great, Master of Spirit, Deacon and Councilor for the Silver Ladder. Used to treat Phoenix as a rising star but their relationship has cooled considerably since she took the Hierachy he deemed his by right and humiliated him in the Duel Arcane. The local caucus is now split between Phoenix and Balthazar causing a fair deal of internal tension. Most prominent member of the Wise Ones.

                    Known to Tara (and other SL chars): Ptolemy - Student and Provost to Balthazar, the aging Obrimos is the first point of contact for all buisness cocerning the local ladder. Member of the Wise Ones.

                    Known to Mysterium chars: Morrigan - Moros, Hierophant and Councilor of the Mysterium. She is a Master of Death and member of the Lords of the Below. She, like the rest of the order, shows little interest in politics but in general the Caucus is in favour of peace as it stops fighting from interfering with their work.

                    Known to Mysterium chars: Banshee - A rather morose woman dressing in funeral wear most of the time. Provost to the more elusive Morrigan and first point of contact for any Mysterium buisness. Member of the Lords of the Below.

                    Known to Mysterium chars: Hades - An allmost skeletal, old man that still carries himself with suprising regality. One of the Curators of the local Athenaeum and member of the Lords of the Below.

                    Known to Mysterium chars: Hecate - A rather strange woman with shifting appearance and personality. One of the Curators of the local Athenaeum and member of the Lords of the Below.

                    These lists are of course far from complete, I've only detailed important people that you would likely know of.

                    My Mage 2e Homebrew
