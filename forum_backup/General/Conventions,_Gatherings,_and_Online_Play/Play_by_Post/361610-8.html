            Swimming Owl frowned.

            Why must I be cursed to always deal with stubborn, stupid, old men?

            She briefly considered leaving the drunk old bastard to his fate. An old man showing up dead in the middle of the place would put some teeth into the rumors she'd just planted. Teeth which would make her mission easier, and hopefully lead in a roundabout way to making her new friends easier to point in the right direction.

            Actually, Swimming Owl, you make a great point. And if he was anybody really important, I'm sure they wouldn't have scheduled a tree to fall on him.

            And with that thought, Owl walked away and let the old bastard enjoy his drink in peace and quiet. She left the pub out the front door, the last to exit, since the old man wouldn't be leaving soon if he ever left at all, and she watched the crowd following Flynn away. As the rumbling from the nearby forest grew louder, she sighed.

            "Always," she muttered, and wandered off to join the crowd following Flynn, in search of high ground or a good vantage point from which to watch whatever happened to the tavern she'd just vacated. Gently waving her hand, she pulled at the skein of Fate and donned once more the garb of the cynical guildswoman who liked shiny objects and plotted with the Anathema. Time to watch and see what these Anathema would do about that rumbling.
