        Dante nods with a serious expression and puts his hands up defensively at your strong reaction. "Understood, my friend. Let us search and see what we can find," he says gently.

        Knowing that Mrs. Parks has a connection to this building, you begin to search for anything that might have meaning for her or reference her. Although the building itself might have a link, you find a plaque near the front entrance to the building indicating that this building was renovated and largely rebuilt from the ground up approximately 15 years ago, long after her death.

        The plaque gives you an idea. Perhaps there is some sort of sign or image that holds meaning for her.         Dante nods eagerly when you find the plaque. "Why, this must be it," he agrees. "People see the image and her name, even occasionally, and it allows her to linger here. You smell a waft of decay and brimstone that you recognize as your Periphery Sight, and the picture warps and erodes at Dante's touch; her name on the plaque simply melts off.             Imprint Image (Forces 2 + Mind 2, CT)
            Reach: 1 free; 2 used for instant casting and advanced duration
            Paradox: Overreach 1 - 2 DT = Chance die → Dramatic Failure
            Containment: None
