                                        I found more information:

                                        Right of Emeritus

                                        Those who have earned respect must be treated with respect. Traditionally, this refers to the practice of respecting one's elders. In practice, it's been broadened today to mean that those who have earned respect, must be respected. Respect is measured through word and deed. And that person's area of expertise should have a direct or substantial benefit to the cabal. Maybe they have a PhD in Mechanical Engineering or maybe they have a Black Belt in three different martial arts; in both cases, they should be given respect and deference within their area of expertise.

                                        Additionally, although not as widely practiced, is the giving of respect to those who have gained a certain position or title such as Councilor or Herald.

                                        Right of Nemesis

                                        When vengeance is declared, let none stand in its way. Nemesis covers overt violence between mages. In other words, when a conflict is approved under the Precept of War, don't get in the middle of the feud. When it is invoked, then the cabal needs to be preparing for it as it stays in effect until the conflict is resolved. Additionally, no mage should interfere with the feud unless it becomes a direct threat to them. This is not a right that should be invoked lightly or with hubris.


                                        Overview:

                                        Emeritus seems to mean, give authority due respect, deference and provide assistance when required. Treat others who have benefited the Consilia or cabal with equal respect. Seems pretty reasonable at a cursory glance.

                                        The key detail is that the Right of Nemesis can only be called after the Precept of War is declared by the Consilia. Which means it requires an attempt at mediation, if that fails a Duel Arcane might be called and if even that is insufficient, then Nemesis is publically notorized and all other mages who recognize the Right do not interfere in the feud. So what I interpreted from that is that it binds you to a system of sanity checks to prevent escalation, and even if it fails, it seeks to reduce the scale of the damage. If our goal is to resolve things peacefully or limit the damage we cause, this seems like the way to go. What do you think ?

                                                Qafsiel chose his Shadow Name due to it embodying his view on magic. As far he knows, he was just running to achieve his goal, then he suddenly started running through Time and beyond that. Now he has magic and also signed up for the journey of a lifetime, so he might as well use the opportunity to leave things a bit better than when he started. But at the same time, the Awakening parsed magic as something that charged his hands and limbs, which left the impression that just like martial arts, it can do a lot of harm if he doesn't use it responsibly.

                                                The archangel Cassiel/Qafsiel embodies that perspective. He is the ruler of Saturn, whose symbolism is that of discipline, responsibility and ambition. He is the known as the archangel of Tears and Solitude, in the sense that he is with people on the lowest points of their life, telling them "you CAN change things for the better". That symbolism of choosing to proactively seize hope, rather than have it delivered (you need to choose to act once you know there is a chance), resonates a lot with Qaf, and he is likely to stand with those that take that perspective, its why his Nimbus reflects it at all levels.

                                                Observation: Qafsiel also has the title of The Death of Kings, and during his Awakening, he was attacking dieselpunk Iron demons/robots. I don't think he has enough context yet to realize that picking that Shadow Name is probably making a statement.
