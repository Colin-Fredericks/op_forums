    The Rusemin are alive, Magnusar.

    Also!

    Places to gather information? Well, I assume you want population centers or trade transit hubs, then? Well, in the West, they have the center of the Guild, the Celestial Nexus of Luxury, where Ta'akozoka, the Many Tentacled One, is said to reside. There's also Mose Verten, a decommissioned battle-station far more spire-ward than we are. That's a hub of commercial traffic and is something of a tourist trap, especially for the Baffe Clan, a human-esque race. Finally, there's Ev Ilta's Paradise, a planet close to the Spire, a sort of amusement park planet made for Exalts and Peers. I went there once. I lost thirteen obols and resolved to never gamble while inebriated again.

    If we're talking more in the South, where I lived for most of my life, I'd say the Scarlet Gem of the Southern Seas, a trade planet that harvests the Fire-Spice that fuels Cecylene's war industries. There's also the Patient Lap of the Eternal Saint, a shrine-planet to the Mountain of Perfection. I like the latter. A cute little place, and they have the nicest bonsai gardens.

    As for temptation? Well, most of those places cater to the basest pursuits and hobbies you can imagine. It'd be hard to answer that, unless you get more specific. Also, I'd recommend not to get indebted to the Guild. They'll have their pound of flesh, sooner or later.

    Of course there's resistance! The Incarnae left a bad precedent, and rebellions erupt every so often. The War of Loarn that I mentioned before was one such attempt. Most rebellions occur in the East, though. The Regional Shogun has been cracking down too hard, and she's probably going to be reprimanded soon for being overbearing.

    As for the most powerful species in the Spiral, that'd definitely be the Ba'alin, Theion's personal servant species. After getting so betrayed by the Dragon Kings, he resolved to learn from his mistake, and make them beholden to Him alone. They are, as a species, usually about 2.5 meters tall, and how to describe them? Well, they have human bodies, leonine faces, caprine horns, two sets of arms, and a tail. They also have patches of fur on their legs and back that make them look poofier than they are. Ba'alin are haughty, proud creatures, and they possess a labyrinthine sense of honor and indebtedness. I recommend being as honest and as forthright with one as you can, if you see them. Oh, and don't underestimate or insult them. They're hard as hell to kill, and their guns are nasty works of business.

                        Mr. X. Thalion is correct: several Titans have sections of their Soul Hierarchies, Joutens and the like, in several places at once. As for the ones in the West, last I checked, Ta'akozoka, the Many-Tentacles One is present in Her Capital in Nexus of Luxury. There's also Capura, the Goat at the Crossroads of Time, who lives in a promontory in the far West. Kimberry, the Sea that Marches against the Flame, makes Her capital in the Elemental Pole of Water, and, depending on how you draw the line at where the "West" ends, Maanus, the Alabaster Effigy of Glamor, lives to the far North of our current position on a planet devoted to Himself.

                        The Pyre of all Knowledge used to live in the West, but she's had a project in the Center for about two centuries now, and no one's sure of what She's doing.

                        There are as many criminals as there are laws of Cecylene, if not more! I'm not entirely familiar with the criminal syndicates of the West, because I haven't really lived there, and I doubt knowing about the Murderous Brotherhood of the All-Seeing Eye would help much, when they're not here.

                        However, there IS a Pirate fleet I know of; a band of buccaneers who wave the flag of a red, winged cog. They call themselves the Pirates of the Star of Disaster, and make their fleets in the planet-sized husk of the dead god Najuu. According to what I know, an evil aura extends over Najuu, thanks to an ancient Exalt who did battle with the god, the Alchemical named Swift Proton Miria. She battled Najuu for a week during the campaign you all know of as the Great Retreat. However, the battle supposedly killed them both, and now Najuu's body is cursed with ill tidings, and these buccaneers are one such symptom, holding fast over their ideology, claiming that an "Angel in Red" came to them, commanding them to take up arms. Reports make the figure to look about like this, but I don't know if that's helpful at all or not.

                        I dunno. I sense there's a loaded question in there. I suppose the worst way would be to get yourself killed, but that would prevent you from running, so that would technically fulfill your motivation. I suppose being stripped of all free will would be bad, too, which makes becoming an akuma of the Pyre a bad idea. However, to become an akuma, you'd have to have performed some huge service to the Titan in question, so, that would be difficult, given your current circumstances. However, you mention temptation. I'm not sure, if its just my experience or if this is true of all Titans, but most are not in the habit of making themselves seem weak to appeal to mortals, even Exalts. Not that they WON'T; Capura is enamored with love and its effect on people, but I don't know Capura personally. You'd have to ask Her yourself.

                                                    Okay, here is my sheet. If anyone has any advice I would greatly appreciate it.

                                                    Name: Ieremiel of the Gate
                                                    Caste: Pyrian Sovereign
                                                    Motivation: Reform the Perfected Hierarchy to elevate Humanity and its Exalted.
                                                    Appearance: Pale, lean, and hairless with watery blue eyes and skin so thin it is almost see through. At least, that is how his corpses have always looked. No one has ever seen him alive outside his faceless armor.
                                                    Personality: Quiet, slow to consider the opinions of others, more benevolent then he will admit, quick to take offense but slow to show it
                                                    Backstory


                                                    He of the shining wings, he of the turning wheel, he who was of old; he, O people, is Ieremiel.
                                                    The Pyre drew him forth from the fundament, clean of Mankind's Disobedience, and the Holy one most highly exalted him to sovereign purpose.
                                                    Warden of the Threshold, architect of ineffable design, who holds the sword and the compass; he, ye multitude, is Ieremiel.
                                                    When he flies by the swift road, Kai, who stands before the Cave of Yesterdays, commands him halt and return, for his his task is yet undone.
                                                    Last of the first men and first of the new, who takes birth with open eyes, in whom the Titans are well pleased; he, ye slaves, is Ieremiel.

                                                    Much he did in repentance of war. Towers he raised and Isidorus toppled them. Gardens he planted and the Pyre razed them. Temple he hallowed and the Chaos smote them.
                                                    Secret author of new Disobedience, weary of labor to another's design, who nurtures rebellion in his heart; he, o traitors, is Ieremiel.

                                                    In the wake of the Great Revolt, Theion began to call for the destruction of all mankind as punishment for daring to rise up against the Primordials and obliteration of their souls for the crime of wounding them. Believing her king was about to commit a grave error, the Principle of Hierarchy exerted her magic against the world and drew forth seven mortals, body and soul, clean of even the suspicion of rebellion. She held them up to the Universe Emperor as the first humans to be unmade. Entranced by the promise of this new race of men the Empyreal Chaos left his command unfinished, willing to wait and see what came. The surrender of the Sun and the rise of the Titanic Exalt seem to have borne out the wisdom of the Pyrian Flame. As for her seven mortals, they became among the first of the Sovereigns.

                                                    So great was their service to the Spiral that it was commanded that they should never have respite. Their souls were marked with sorcerous brands that forced them to turn back from Lethe into waiting bodies. Each has built a mighty legend in their lifetimes of labor for the Titans and against their enemies. Among them was Ieremiel, and the love the humans of the spiral feel for him is equaled only by the hate of the Autochthonian Realm for so great a traitor to his race.

                                                    Yet, Ieremiel has grown tired of lifetimes without respite, the eternal withering and flowering of enlightenment and power, and the suffering of endless war. His recent incarnations have focused on more peaceful tasks, such as creating the post-human deva species known as the Atmanatta and promoting harmony between humanity and the other servant races. For ages he forced himself to be blind to race, yet the continued penance of mankind has begun to anger him. Though he long ago ceased to consider himself human, they remain without patronage or intercession.

                                                    Armor of Iremiel (Sanctified Jade Superheavy Plate)
                                                    For almost everyone how knows of him, and for the Sovereign himself, this armor is the face of Iremiel. Its white jade plates flash like mother of pearl in the light, its orichalcum ornamentation is pale as electrum and bright as a mirror, and the closed face of its crown-like helm obscures the face behind from even the closest inspection. Many suits like it are hung in the treasure vaults of the Gunstar and every one had to be cut away from the corpses within for this armor is not meant to be removed. In womb-sarcophagi deep in the spiral, soulless effigies are fitted with the the raw plates and baptized in Theion-To to bring both artifacts to completion. So it is that the Titanic Exalted who wears it has never seen what lies within.

                                                    The most visible sign of Iremiel's work is the Atmanatta. Though each was born as a human, they have left that state behind to become some of the most highly favored deva races. They seek out the greatest of mortals, the sort who might have been Exalted in other circumstances, and refine them in the crucible of wisdom and enlightenment. The many paths of transcendence have led to many varieties of Atmanatta, distinct only in appearance and philosophy. The most common appear as they did as mortals save with bones of pale crystal, muscle and veins spun from lightning, skin of cold blue fire, and wings like plasma arcs. Some have bodies of rippling mercury, others are silhouettes shaped from clouds of dust and full of stars, and still others are shimmering ripples of solidified sound over skeletons of hardened light.
