    (Recruitment Thread) Zodiac Beasts, OOC

    07-23-2014, 10:13 AM
    Actual RP Thread is located here: http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/g...-zodiac-beasts



    The Zodiac Beast thread is designed for up to 13 people although we do not need to fill out the whole roster if that seems a bit too much for you guys. Not every slave manages to escape, after all. Anyways, these are the spots we currently have available.


    Story Teller: Taking the role of the Keeper, who's toying with the players for their own amusement. Players are pretty much dancing to his tune while simultaneously trying to find a way to escape.


    Keeper - Thorbes (I had to drop out)



    Players: Incarnations of the Chinese Zodiac, all of them Beast seeming. The beasts are somewhat Anthropomorphic, meaning they still have access to their humanoid appendages. That should come in handy when you're trying to pick a lock or solve a Fae puzzle!

    Due to the restrictive nature of everyone being members of the Beasts, and in order for them to not be mindless animals, I encourage players to be Dual Kith, preferably with the second Kith being from an alternate seeming.


    Rat - Open

    Dog - Open

    Goat - Evilmarmelade

    Dragon - Open

    Rooster / Chicken - Open

    Ox - Cire

    Rabbit - Open

    Pig - Gaius

    Horse - Open

    Snake - Open

    Monkey - Open

    Tiger - Open




    The roles are first come, first serve. Whoever claims it gets the spot. Just list the following in your submission

                  Aww, seriously? The Pig had been the one I'd been eying and then BAM, first one gone. XD I admit that's disappointing, but I'm more amused than anything. Let's see if any of the other 10 start speaking to me...

                  Kudos to you on trying to balance your various games, Cire. It's a tricky thing to manage, but very fulfilling.

                  On the matter of Kiths, they generally serve as a "specialization" of sorts within the Seeming (so something playing up strengths of the animal side), but cross-Seeming Kiths are fine with ST approval. This could easily just be baked into this game's assumptions.

                  One thought is also that some Changelings sort of "grow into" their Kiths over time, or develop them based on circumstance. It could make sense for some people to start without a Kith (or with only one), and then pick one/two/another up in play as appropriate.

                  I've sometimes seen houserules that characters can start with the Dual Kith Merit for free, giving them two Kiths to pick out, typically one in-Seeming and one from wherever. It can lend more freedom toward concepts a player finds interesting.* I think that option could work well here: one Kith might reflect the particular animal's traits, while the other (if used) might go toward something else (maybe related to the sorts of tasks the Keeper sets, or further cruelties it inflicts, or whatever else).
                  EDIT: Just saw you'd already mentioned the Dual Kith bit in the game description. Whoops! The rest still seems relevant.

                  * I (regrettably only briefly) played a Nick Bottom-like fellow, using the Broadback and Minstrel Kiths (the latter refluffed as a Thespian). Can't remember if I went with Fairest or Beast with his Seeming...

                  As one final point you'd raised in the game description, I don't think taking a non-Beast Kith is necessary. Strictly speaking, if we were going strictly by what the books say is typical, just having a Beast Seeming would have the mindless animal trait locked in for most of the Durance. The game's being flexible there in the name of an interesting story, so I don't think there's reason for a Kith limitation. If someone just wants to play up a character's animal side in the Kith(s), whether driven mostly by instinct or not, I think that should be fine.

                  Hmm... No idea about a concept...
