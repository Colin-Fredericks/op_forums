                        The Consilium's hospitality rules basically boil down to -

                        1. Don't take Mana from a Hallow unless you're sure it isn't claimed. Little Atlantis is chock full of them, and most claimed Hallows are fairly clearly marked

                        2. Let other people know if you're casting a spell or activating something that will ping Mage Sight, to avoid unfortunate accidents

                        3. Changelings/Werewolves and their brands of Magic should be declared at the door. Likewise, any Invisible Entities need to be announced upon arrival

                        4. Guests can request up to a dram (one point) of Mana per person

                        5. Santiago and Cloudchaser specifically ban Prime Unveiling/Knowing on their premises, but most households have similar impositions against the study of their defenses. This should be declared by the host at the door

                        6. A request for sanctuary must be honored for at least 24 hours, unless irrefutable proof of Left-Handed activty (including Rapture and being a practicing Seer) is presented.

                                                First of all, these are great.

                                                So, if I may summarize the story hook portions of the locations are, as I understand:

                                                Prospect Yard: ghosts. And creepy stuff. And moderate sleeper stuff. Might have Free Council stuff going on, might not.

                                                Njord's Draught: Changeling, and high sleeper stuff. Probably high Mage stuff too, given the Marshals, and what they do.

                                                Stone Oak: Werewolves and Shadow shenanigans.

                                                Hyleen Cottage: low obvious plot hooks, but isolated.

                                                Tesslin’s Hollow: Cultists? Fish people? Mad kelp sprites? Who knows?

                                                So my thoughts:
                                                Elpis is unfortunately probably going to veto Stone Oak since it’s on a loci. They are creeped out by the idea of spirits, and dealing with werewolves isn’t something they want to do on a regular basis. I like the visual though, and the merits are nice.
                                                Prospect Yard is not bad for Elpis, they like singing, and don’t find the dead to be scary, mostly just sad. Not exactly sure what the safe place 1 represents? Need to look up how Prime interacts with seeing Ephemeral Entities.
                                                Njord’s Draught is interesting, I do like Changeling, and Elpis has Mind. Lots of people to deal with though.
                                                Hyleen Cottage: Elpis’ favored option. They like anywhere where they don’t have to deal with too many people, and will probably think of the fixer upper part as a good chance to get to know Qaf. One benefit is that if we want to improve the merits, it doesn’t have as much narrative problems as other options. Down side is less opportunities for story stuff from our home.
                                                Tesslin’s Hollow is interesting, but I think impractical. Might be good for a remote gathering spell, but that takes up spell control.
                                                ​
                                                I think Prospect Yard, Njord’s Draught, and Hyleen Cottage are all good. Thoughts?
