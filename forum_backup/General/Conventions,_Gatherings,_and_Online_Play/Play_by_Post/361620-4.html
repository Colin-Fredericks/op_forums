                                Since we are doing character descriptions....

                                Flynn is a tall, long limbed man with grey eyes that looks like he is in his mid-twenties. His dirty blond hair is long and wavy. It is usually kept in a tail at the base of his head. He is pretty good looking in spite of the day old stubble he usually has. Many years under the harsh southern sun has turned his fair northern skin tan.

                                His dusty travel clothes, though well worn, are in good repair. He wears sandals on his feet and long traveling cloak. His Sanxian case is never far from him, either strapped to his back or sitting next to him.

                                If Flynn is not playing his Sanxian, he is always fidgeting with his hands. He has this restless energy that can only be expressed though music. One would think he would be an awkward man with such long arms and legs, but he is quiet graceful for his size.
