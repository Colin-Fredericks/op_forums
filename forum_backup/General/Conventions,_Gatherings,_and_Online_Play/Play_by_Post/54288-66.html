Since thou wish to aid mine boon companion, were the choice mine, I would open the Way for thee with nary a second thought. Howe'er the mantle of mine kingship hast been stolen away from me, and is hidden deep below. A great and terrible beast guards my crown, and I am forbidden by my oaths to seek it out myself. If thou retrieve mine scepter, I swear upon mine self and nine Heart to aid you in the recovery of Ciprus' mind."
    "Seems fair, alright you have yourself a deal Aurem I will retrive your scepter but tell me about the beast and why do you have Ciprus mind if he is your friend ?"

That is all I know."

                The Solar considers the words of both of his hosts carefully before formulating his response, "I think they should be free to choose. I will not force them to be warriors. The Fae may not give them that choice. I know they didn't give me that choice back home. Every man able to lift an axe was asked to be always ready. The village could need defending. The reavers went out to raid and a different group of reavers attacked. I had to fight. I picked up my smith's hammer and fought."

                He pauses while he remembers the events of that day. Eventually, he will stand and Shadow with him, "Thank you for your hospitality, Dog. I will be back with Shadow so we can train later. I must go and help Yi. Maybe with him and the rest we can ensure that those 'duelists' won't be forced to become warriors."

                With that the Dawn will leave and attempt to find Yi. He realizes it might be as simple as finding the largest crowd in the city.

                        Jensven seeks out to find Yi, using as his guide the levels of commotion in the city. It is scarcely less than an hour later that he finds himself in the midst of a large crowd. They are milling about a large building formed of limestone, bronze and topaz. A massive jeweled worm rises unto the heavens nearby.

                        And, as expected, in the midst of it all, is Yi.

                        What do?

                                        Jensven skirts the crowd looking for easier paths to get closer to Yi. Shadow follows with him, but is stopped frequently by excited children who absolutely have to pet him. One even exclaims that he is 'SO FLUFFY!' before attempting to ride him. The Dawn appears amused by this and waves away an apology by the embarrassed parents as they retrieve their child. Shadow, for his part appears to be only slightly annoyed, but the antics cause him no harm.

                                        He will finally approach the new construction and marvel at it. "I have not become used to the large buildings here. Do you know who built this one, Yi?" Jensven continues to look the building over and the canine yawns. Shadow wants to lay down, but does not for fear that he will get jumped on by more of the excited children who are obviously fascinated with a dog like none they have seen before. He contents himself with sitting next to his Exalt.
