        Hi all! Hope everything is going well for you. A couple orders of business and replies to questions:

        MagicSwordsman - That Sense Consciousness spell looks great.


        Haberdasher - Lucky Number is a Fate 2 spell (p. 136). You can use your Patient Virtue as long as it's a meaningful action, usually something where you do something that requires some kind of commitment or sacrifice. I think of something like refusing to give up on a difficult task that requires perseverance, or maybe playing things cool and standing your ground when it would be easier to leave out of self-preservation. So in this case, sure, you can stand and try to calmly talk to the guy who's very likely to harm you. If you do, you can regenerate WP at the end of your post, but he will get to take his first action against you before rolling initiative. Sound fair?

        Also, this might've been confusing to some of the other players when it was discussed earlier in our threads, but "Anubis" is the pseudonym for the mage that Phoenix is hunting and who killed his old Hunter cell. "Anubis" was actually Hakim. Moonstone is the only person who has even seen this mysterious mage, and only briefly; none of you know who he actually is.

        Thanks to Caladriu for posting the Divergent condition again (the version you have). And yes, you can drop Locate Object reflexively; it still is maintained until you do so, but you no longer detect the object it was targeting.


        Caladriu - Thanks! Glad you like it. I'm going to try and keep adding cool Mysteries throughout the game like this, so hopefully you will have lots of neat things to Obsess over. Sure, you can take an Arcane Beat for the discovery.


        Thorbes - Sure, sounds great! I will type up a description for that as soon as I have time. The next two days might be very busy for me at work, but I'll try to get that ball rolling.


        Kvitebjørn Kong Valemon - Sorry to hear you're having a hard time. Totally understand, no worries. Thanks for letting me know.


        Everyone: This is the longest game I've run. I am having a blast, and really appreciating all of your contributions and really creative ideas. You all can take a Beat for being awesome. #newyearnewyou
