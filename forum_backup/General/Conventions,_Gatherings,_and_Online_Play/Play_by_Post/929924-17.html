        Moonstone
        Terror grips you and refuses to let go. Even in your shadow form with better relative safety thanks to the cloak, you feel paralyzed with fear as you stare into the eyes of the dark monster in front of you. The Abyssal entity reaches out with a claw and gently caresses you, sending a chill through you, as if considering how it might prefer to consume you whole.

        The creature touches both arms to you, and you feel a pulse of energy pass into you, as if it is somehow forcing itself upon or into you. Your vision doubles as if you are being split in two. The creature smiles darkly.

        The vibration in the room reaches a crescendo, and suddenly the hallway is lit with sunlight, and you see a doorway rip open in the air before you, the monster, and Phoenix. Through it, you see the alleyway where Binder is standing, as well as a group of three people hurrying towards him. The Abyssal creature pulls away from you, then smiles maliciously again.

        You shudder as the world stretches and rips apart...

        ...and then you're in the streets of Chicago, still wearing the cloak, still in a pool of shadow moving along the floor.

        I'm ruling that Awe applies the Insensate tilt. Because it was an exceptional success, it lasts for 3 turns, which the creature spends studying you and preparing to go through the portal. Don't forget that you still have Abyssal Backlash (+6) on your next Paradox roll.

        Phoenix
        The sudden, intense shaking forces you into consciousness. You're barely awake, but the adrenaline keeps you focused enough to act. You open your eyes to a sight of the world splitting in two...

        ...and you collapse on the street next to Binder with a grunt.

        I'm ruling that you wake up, but still have all health boxes filled as before, so you can act at the moment but taking more damage will risk falling unconscious again until you heal some damage. You also have the -3 penalty from wound penalties. Before posting any actions, roll 1d10, followed by a chance die.


        Lynx
        You can tell that the man is disgruntled, although the source of his frustration is not immediately clear. His focus on the wall is a little odd, as there is nothing your naked eyes can perceive there other than bricks.

        When you open your Sight, you instantly pick up the scent of blood and sweat from this man; you can tell that he has been seriously injured, and recently. On the wall, you see a dark spot of spiritual energy as black as midnight; it reminds you of the hail you saw falling earlier.

        You see a dizzying light glow brightly around Mathias, and feel a lingering sense of euphoria, as he casts a Fate spell. You also see another man appear out of thin air, collapsing beside the man by the wall. He appears very seriously bruised, and has a number of terrible lacerations on his arms and torso.


        Ragnar
        Mathias smiles, but you can still see worry lines creasing his forehead. "That can't be helped, but don't underestimate what a mage can do with proper preparation, creativity, and...a little bit of luck," he replies.

        The older man pulls out a small crystalline rod, perhaps the size of a large pencil, from his pocket. "We need to get these people out of the area and keep them away, or they might panic." Mathias closes his eyes and murmurs a few words in a language unlike any you've ever heard before in your life -- and yet you somehow recognize it, like a long lost memory resurfaced.

        You feel another pulse on your Periphery. You don't immediately see or feel anything odd...until you notice that every Sleeper on the street has just pulled out their phones with an incoming call. A younger man walks by you, and you hear him talking to a friend urgently asking him to meet up because he's having a really rough day. "Absolutely, dude, I've always told you to call if you wanted to talk about anything," the young man says as he walks briskly away towards the train station. "I just can't believe you're actually taking me up on it. I thought you'd never get around to bringing this stuff up..."

        It's then that you realize all of the Sleepers are quickly leaving the street in various directions. And just in time, too: Looking back towards the adjacent wall reveals a tall, roughly humanoid creature with long, sinewy arms ending in sharp talons. It peers at you and your allies, then smiles, revealing long teeth the size of small daggers. As it stands there, the street seems to darken. You think your eyes may be tricking you, but the shadows around the floor and wall seem to be moving on their own, swirling with activity.

                                Moonstone
                                You blink your eyes, trying to clear away the haze surrounding your mind, but you struggle to remember anything that happened after going through the portal. You don't know why, but you feel exhausted and emotionally taxed like you haven't felt since the aftermath of Hakim's death.

                                When you try to look around you, you realize that you are wearing Hakim's cloak; you don't remember putting that on. You are a formless shadow, lying on the ground beside Phoenix. When you look up, you stare into the face of a creature out of your worst nightmares.

                                One memory is crystal clear: In your arrogance, your hubris, you summoned this creature. It infected your magic, tainting your very soul. There is still some of it within you, threatening to ruin your next spell. The shame of this realization rocks you to your core.


                                Phoenix
                                You groan in pain. Why are you in so much pain? What happened?

                                You blink your eyes and try to dispel your pounding headache. You realize that your memories of what happened after going through the portal are hazy at best. Glimpses of a long hallway, and a sea of stars, flash through your mind's eye. You hear the faintest echo of a woman crying, but don't remember why.

                                When you look up, you see a large humanoid creature of darkness. It sparks another memory: Moonstone, foolishly allowing something into the world, and you trying to fend it off at great personal risk.

                                You blink again as you realize that you're lying on something. Looking down, you see a long, round staff. Its surface is covered with intricate geometric runic carvings. It feels slightly warm to the touch.

                                If you choose to use it, the staff can be used as a weapon with the following stats: +1L damage, -1 initiative, Strength 2, Size 4. Special: +1 Defense bonus against opponents who are unarmed or wield weapons of Size 1; two-handed (+1 Strength required if wielding with one hand).


                                The Abyssal Entity
                                Looking directly at the creature is difficult. Anywhere that it touches the creature's skin, sunlight splits and collides together again, creating impossible pockets or bubbles of prismatic visions in the air around it. Some of the light seems to sizzle, creating pockets of shadow on the wall and street.

                                The Abyssal creature raises its arms into the air and emits a terrible, inhuman roar. The light around it grows darker. You all feel a pulse of energy on your Periphery, and anyone standing near the creature feels a supernatural wave of fear crash into you.

                                The creature will activate its Emotional Aura Numen using 1 WP: Emotional Aura (17) = 8 successes. Everyone is currently standing within a 5-yard circle of this thing, so on your turn, roll Resolve + Composure + Gnosis; if you get less than 8 successes, take -2 on all actions as long as the aura remains (I'll tell you when it's over) or as long as you remain within the aura.


                                Mathias
                                "Shit! It's a Gulmoth!" Mathias exclaims. He holds up his crystal wand and makes a stabbing motion towards the creature. You all feel another burst of energy on your Periphery as he casts a spell on the creature. "I can make it hurt more if someone else hits it," he explains. Mathias stands in front of the creature, trying to goad it into attacking him before hurting anyone else.

                                Mathias will cast Grave Misfortune: Gnosis (3) + Fate (4) + Order Tool (1) - Potency (-2) - Emotional Aura (2) = 4d10: 1 success. He has 2 free Reach; spending 3 on instant casting, sensory range, and advanced duration (1 scene). The spell succeeds with Potency 5, to help overcome some of the creature's Withstand.

                                Paradox (containing, 1st roll this scene): 2d10 = 0 successes.

                                Moonstone may make an Intelligence + Occult roll to see if he remembers anything about Gulmoth.


                                Initiative:
                                Gulmoth (20) -- Emotional Aura
                                Ragnar (17) -- Exceptional Luck
                                Mathias (16) -- Grave Misfortune
                                Phoenix (13) -- Kinetic Blow
                                Moonstone (12) -- Telepathy
                                Binder (12) -- Mind over Matter
                                Lynx (12) -- Waiting

                                For this fight, I'd like to try a posting convention that might help speed things up, given the number of people in combat. You can take your action for the round whenever you have time, but I will resolve actions in order of initiative. If someone ahead of you in initiative order does something that would make you want to change your action, you can edit your post to include a different action up until you're up in order. If this doesn't work well or causes issues, we can try something different for the future.

                                                        Phoenix
                                                        You feel your magic surge through your veins and muscles, and you feel ready to inflict some hurt. Time to put those martial arts skills to the test on this foul creature.

                                                        Your unarmed attacks currently count as +2L weapons. The bonus damage adds after a successful hit and will stack with Grave Misfortune.


                                                        Moonstone
                                                        You've heard of these creatures, and your knowledge is true. They are like twisted reflections of spirits, prone to the same strengths - and weaknesses. This one is Manifested, drawing on Resonance in the area to make itself present in a powerful way here in the Fallen World.

                                                        You stare into this creature's mind and attempt to make contact. It makes sense to you that the only way to defeat such a creature is to first understand it, so you try to understand the patterns and thoughts passing through its mind.

                                                        The problem, you instantly realize, is that there is no pattern to this creature's mind that you can understand. You do see shattered fragments of images, emotions, and impulses. You see an otherworldly place of shadows and foul things you can't even put a name to. You do see it watching you from across the Abyss, waiting for a chance to take advantage of you. You form a name to describe its presence: Alux, the anti-light, the very physical impossibility of the destruction of light and creation of shadow.

                                                        And then there's this Gulmoth's own emotions and urges: It wants to devour you, all mages, all worlds, until nothing but the Abyss exists. It craves mana and magic, and will target mages to exploit their weakness -- just as it used you to arrive here. The torrent of information threatens to overwhelm you.

                                                        Roll Resolve + Composure, including -2 to the roll because of your Divergent condition. If you fail, you gain the Insane Tilt, which gives you +1 to combat rolls but makes you last in Initiative order and impacts your judgment.


                                                        Binder
