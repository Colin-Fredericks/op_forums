                        "No, I suppose I do not have such power, I am too young in years and strength. Yet I will not be young forever. There will come a day when I shall be in skill and surety like they who made the Singing Staves and the Soulbreaker Orbs."

                        While he was speaking a stone block rose from the floor into the air before him. It hovered for a moment before silently bursting into a array of pins, braces, gears, even chains of interlocking links.

                        "The Jade Obelisks which hold back the tides of the Wyld, the War Manses which compose the Realm Defense Grid, the Dam of Days, the Glass Towers of Chiaroscuro; such as me raised such as those. The Craftsmen who need no tools are returned to the world."

                        The pieces before him began to assemble and turn. Teeth found teeth, pegs found holes, chains wound about axles, and so forth. A great ring of interlocked segments and marked with the Glyph of the Sun stood from the flat top and a stone weight hung from its bottom.

                        "No doubt there are many who would see me and my like dead before we ripen to such puissance. We are both smart enough to know that is a fool's errand, futile and wasteful when no one can afford loss. The Solars are returned, the world is changing, and this Age is ending for good or ill. I want to help you and yours, to magnify your power and glory and prosperity so you may have a place of abundance and majesty in the future that is even now unfolding.."

                        Only the slow ticking of the mechanism in his hand filled the silence when he finished.

                        Craftsman Needs No Tools to construct a very simple clock. A pendulum clock only needs something like 4 gears and a simple gimbal will account for minor jolts. This is a stunt for a persuasion roll I plan to make.

                        I would appreciate the knowing the stunt bonuses for mote recovery.
