    Moonstone
    Peripheral Sight | Spells: One Mind Two Thoughts (Potency 1, scene) [Wards and Signs, Potency 5, scene]
    Conditions: Divergent (Passive), Fabled, Guilty

    At the lowest point, he feels like himself again. Clarity. The image refocuses, the pond grows still. I challenged a much more powerful, friendly mage to the Duel Arcane. I hurt Ragnar. I made Phoenix very, very upset. I disappointed Boomer.

    I've really fucked up, haven't I?

    Divergent returns to its Passive state as Moonstone gets full Willpower back. Rolling for Act of Hubris, 3 dice +1 for acting in defense of his Virtue: 2 successes, or 1 success if Virtue doesn't apply. +1 Arcane Beat for risking Wisdom loss.


    He closes his eyes and takes a deep breath for just a second.

    Serene Moonstone thinks: Yes. I have really fucked up.

    Grieving Moonstone thinks: I wish you were here. Is this what you want?

    Serene Moonstone thinks: I wasn't thinking clearly. But I can't back down now.

    Grieving Moonstone thinks: I can't do this without you. If you were with me, it would be okay.

    Serene Moonstone thinks: Am I the villain?

    Grieving Moonstone thinks: I have to believe this is what you would want me to do. You're all I have.

    The second passes. He opens his eyes. A quick word to Boomer: "Thank you."

    As for Mathias, he meets his gaze and speaks clearly. He can't bear to look at Ragnar or Phoenix or anyone else, and he needs to focus, anyway. Center. Relax. Flow.

    So here's what Moonstone is trying to do here. Explicitly invoking Mathias' Vice of Pride, which he learned from his Know Nature spell earlier, he wants to propose stakes that Mathias will find insulting. He should be infuriated that this scrawny little Abyss-tempting Mind-controlling gender-nonconforming Sleepwalker is saying this to his face, in his own Sanctum, in front of his own cabal, at a time like this. Based on everyone's reactions so far, it shouldn't be that difficult, haha.

    The reason that he's doing this is to goad or trick Mathias into attacking rather than defending on his turn for as long as possible. He's trying to mundanely play on his Vice to influence his behavior during the Duel. And he'll use One Mind, Two Thoughts to give himself a better chance of it. He's going to do a Teamwork action with himself, having one train of thought (Mental) develop a plan and choose the best possible approach while the other train (Social) does the talking and presents the stakes. I hope this is okay, this is the reason I cast the spell!

    First train of thought, supporting action with Wits+Politics (Consilium), 7 dice: 2 successes.

    Second train of thought, primary action. Manipulation 2, Subterfuge 2, teamwork +2, and I'll resolve Fabled for +5. 11 dice: 6 successes.

    Resolving Fabled gives me 1 Beat and 1 Willpower, so I'm at 8/8 again. I'd like to apply the following Condition to myself, a variant of Steadfast:

    Determined
    You're at your best in a battle of wills. When you're in a contested action and your opponent rolled more successes than you, you may resolve this Condition to instead treat the action as if you rolled 1 more success than your opponent. This does not count as an exceptional success, even if you would roll 5 or more successes.
    Resolution: This Condition is resolved as described above.


    "Your words are water, and you know nothing about my life, Witch. But your stakes are agreeable, and I accept them. I propose the following terms, in accordance with the Code Duello: both duelists will enter the squared circle with no active spells affecting them. There will be no outside interference, mundane or magical, to affect the outcome of the duel. The outcome of the duel will be publicly announced by the defeated party at the next gathering of the Consilium- in a few days, I believe. These terms will be enforced by our seconds. Is this acceptable?"

    Assuming that it is, he proceeds. He's controlling his breathing, putting his mirror away, trying to calm down. "Now. My stakes. If I win, I want you to explain the significance of victory in the Duel Arcane to the mages gathered here, in order to further their magical education. I want you to get on your knees in front of me, formally thank me for saving your and the others' lives from the Gulmoth, request my forgiveness for striking, insulting, and disrespecting me after I put my life on the line for your sake, and kiss my hand before rising to your feet. I want all consequences for this Duel to fall on myself, rather than the other Nameless mages- as you can see, they are not with me. And when you announce my victory before the Consilium, I want you to acknowledge that the Thunderbolt Guardian Hakim is your better as a mage and a man, and apologize for dishonoring his memory by assaulting his favored student, and...and dear companion."

    His voice quavers a little at the end there, but he catches himself. He's trying to focus on Serious Arcane Duelist mode, but it's hard not to slip into natural language. "I am not your student, Mathias. I am not your servant, or your disciple, or your friend. I am your equal. I will teach you discipline. I will show you the fear I feel when a white man twice my size shoves my queer brown ass against a brick wall in a dark alley and insults me for the crime of saving his life, and the people I saved watch and do nothing. I will show you the power of my magic. Defeating and humiliating you in front of your own cabal will give me special pleasure."

    Deep breath. "This is my gift to you. This is how I display my Wisdom. Are these terms agreeable to you?"

                        As far as the actual rules, I feel like the core book is really vague here. I think it's just Attribute + Arcanum roll, and you only need one success.
