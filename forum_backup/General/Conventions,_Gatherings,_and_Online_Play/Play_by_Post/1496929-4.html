                                            Halwyn goes back to his car. The whole day has been a bit upsetting for him. Although this feeling has only existed since the creature.

                                            His car makes stuttering noises at first, but then it starts. Again he checks his loading area through the mirror to see if there might be something on it. Then he drives off, first slowly because he doesn't know this area that well, then faster to finally be back in his caravan.

                                            Cold creeps up his spine, not from the weather but from the thought of the demon. For a moment he is not paying attention. Something seems to be on its way. At least he thinks so, and he turns his steering wheel hard to one side. The car crashes through the barrier and hits a tree.

                                            "Damn shit!" he yells and after some minutes He carefully gets out of his car. was there really something or did he imagine it?
