                            Here is a reference to a 2ed conversion of the Roses of Eden. However, they do not believe in the Atlantean myth, meaning they are usually associated with Nameless Orders, and they have a history of making a mess of Consilium politics, so most mages would be wary. Building on what Temple said, there was a conversion of the Tellurians that focuses on becoming a Fisher King, drawing one's kingdoms and subjects closer to the Supernal through benevolent rule.

                            I think the main challenge is combining that theme with either a Thyrsus or Silver Ladder Legacy. Perhaps if we can narrow the focus of what this new Eden means and how they approach it, it might be easier to recommend something that resonates with Philly's themes. Right now the only other Legacy that comes to mind is the Anima Meccanica, which are Silver Ladder and Free Council. They recognize the incredible potential of spirits and push back against the Lie by combining them with technology, incentivizing people to think outside the box. They are solarpunk inventors, which means they have a very natural aesthetic, but I am not sure if you are looking for a technognostic approach.

                                I like the emphasis on plants. I agree that's not something you see as much of in RPGs, probably because its harder to RP i.e. plants don't talk. Which is why I was thinking of incorporating the shadow. In the spirit realm talking to the spirits of plants, water, stones, etc. is totally something you can do. And its a different feel from the Uratha, and quite frankly many mages who view spirits as "prey".

                                I didn't take any dots in expression because I felt having a larger dice pool for Animal Ken and Persuasion was more mechanically advantageous. But I feel like seeing gardening as an art is something that would be important to Philly. Gardening and landscaping can be very spiritual, especially in many Eastern practices. The same was true in the West before the industrial revolution.

                                In the short term, with the Cottage sanctum, I see it playing out like a grown up version of My Neighbor Totoro. Fixing up the house, healing the invisible wounds left by the previous tenants, and befriending/ gaining status with the near by spirit court.

                                I feel like both Roses of Eden and Tellurians would take Philly in a darker direction then where she is now. But you never know. It might be interesting for her to meet a Nodding even if she didnt join. In the Seattle game I ST two years ago, the FC were politically ascendant in Seattle, so Atlantis skepticism was typical. Not sure what Cauthon has laid out in this chronicle. I like the idea of "why does the Awakened future have to be a city. Why not a nature reserve?"

                                There's time. I'm not really committed to any one idea at this point. I find that stuff comes out in gameplay that you don't expect, and you have to get to know a character for awhile before you find out what they really want.

                                    So, I chose the skill requirements based on what I was told was the best representations of “Gardening”. I was given Survival, Crafts, and Expression. I chose the first two to be a representation of natural knowledge, and working the land to promote growth. Expression was chosen as the main skill, because I thought the creation and cultivation of beauty was a good summation of the legacy…and I wanted to relate singing, and the myth? that plants grow better with sound. What I kinda failed to account for was handling animals, which is kinda important to an ecosystem/garden. (Especially pollinators) So Animal Ken is important to the concept too, and if you wish to replace Expression/Crafts with Animal Ken, I think it works perfectly well. Honestly, if you like the Legacy, and think it fits what you want except for some bits like skills and stuff, you have my permission to modify it however you want.

                                    I also neglected to think about how the Gardens would impact the Shadow, and how spirits/werewolves might react to Glades becoming more common. That’s a whole side to the legacy that is woefully under-explored. And I now want to take another edit pass on how it affects the Shadow.

                                    But yeah, we don’t have to decide on anything now. The future isn’t set in stone. Elpis *might* not end up in the Elans, though that’s currently the plan. Personally, I was wondering how shaping themselves towards the Egregore might affect the Legacy process. Elans thrive on hive minds, and the Mysterium kinda constitutes a low level one.
