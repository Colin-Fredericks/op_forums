            Night, November 3rd, 2016
            Adonis' Apartment- Earth, Mark 5, and Adonis

            Before leaving the community garden, the woman Earth was gardening with- and how strange it was, that she didn't seem to care to ask for a name or offer one herself -gave a relieved smile at seeing the obvious affection Earth held for the people who came to get and when the Promethean approached she laid a reassuring hand on her arm- the arm knotted with corded muscle and curled talons for fingers. "I'll be here when you do."

            The three Created make their way back through the city, led by the Muse to a run-down tenement not too far off from where they began. There's plenty of them around the university where the youthful looking Galateid would fit right in, and most people are too busy with their own issues to notice any strange comings and goings. The fog has lifted, and there's a bit of a chill to the night air. Even with the streetlights, and the quieter, but still present, bustle of Ellice and Portage Avenue, the city has gone quiet, but the building has not. It hums with human voices of all description at all levels of volume. Some shout, some whisper, some speak in English, some in French, and some in far more distant languages besides.

            The spoils of their impromptu raid against Amalgama LTD are spread out on the floor. A series of potions in thick tempered glass, marked with a series of alchemical symbolism and medical jargon, and a rough journal with a weatherbeaten cover, a patchwork mixture between diary, manifesto, day planner, and alchemical diagram. An Athanor. But where to start?

            OOC: Intelligence + Occult or Intelligence + Science to decipher the labels on the potions. Intelligence + Occult or Intelligence + Empathy to decipher the text within the Athanor. Earth has the Informed (Athanor) Condition, and as it is a Stannum Athanor, she gets a +2 bonus to understanding it. She could also conceivably use her Library at this point.

                            Mark 5

                            You pick up a bottle containing a rust red potion. You can see that the colour mostly stems from a granular, rough powder. As you look closer, you can see that there are flecks of yellow, what might be copper, mixed in with the rest. The first symbol is a downwards facing triangle with an arrow pointing upwards from the base of the triangle, two lines crossing through the middle of the arrow's shaft. This is contained within two Vs, framing it within a diamond that extends upwards and downwards diagonally off of the two side corners of the diamond. A third, and last, symbol sits off to the side of the extended diamond, a gentle upwards swoop and a sharp downwards angle.

                            Crocus of Iron, Alembic, Distillation. A potion that bestows a Distillation of a Ferrum Alembic?

                            Adonis

                            The Muse takes up an elegant looking bottle, something like a perfume spritzer that is filled with a dark blue liquid. There's a word in elegant script scrawled across it; 'Indulgence'. It's contained within a heavily stylized version of the alchemical symbol for cobalt, and etched down near the barcode is the same extended diamond and elegant angled swop that is on the far more utilitarian potion in Mark 5's hands. Cobalt, Alembic, Distillation. A potion that bestows a Distillation of a Cobalus Alembic?

                            You set it down and read some of the first few pages of the Athanor. For your entire life, your attitude has been that of Aurum. You are a Mimic. You have been a mirror and a follower to the humans around you, trying to cater to them, learn their ways, slide seamlessly into their lives. But there's pain, hatred, and a self-loathing to these words that genuinely shocks you. A page or two of seemingly rational journal, with mad scribbles on the margins suggesting either later reflection, or the crushing depths of Torment. You come to a page that was created with some care.

                            Perhaps you need to take a step away from it right now.

                            OOC: Adonis gains the Shaken Condition. Empathizing with someone who had been in the depths of Torment is not a gentle experience.

                            Earth

                            The pretty boy seems a little overwhelmed, but maybe a fellow Fury can internalize it more. Earth reads quickly, turning from one patchwork journal to another. She can easily track the author of the Athanor's frustrations, the constant looming threat of Disquiet, the eternal outsider status. Like wielding lightning and smashing buildings, writing this was a form of release for the author, a kind of catharsis. She could track when they were in Torment, and when they slipped out of it, just through the writing. They wrote more, and much less coherently, when they were Tormented. The author couldn't stand the way humans rejected their own over minuscule differences, it disgusted them, and they endeavoured to make a home for the night creatures, the unloved, the broken things. They would be the eye of a hurricane, a shelter in a hostile world.

                            The Named leaps back and forth from one book to the other. She understands rejection. Dead Girl was an expert at demonstrating it. When she was particularly deep in her Torment she'd string others along and then crush them, body and soul, to prove her devotion to Earth-Dies-Screaming. She sees those words, sees the places where the tip of the pen dug deeply into paper with the intensity of emotion in the writing. Dead Girl understood obsession and rejection intimately, and she professed to love the unloveable, but did she really? You compare the collections of her writings with that of the Athanor, and it's like two warring philosophies- one that seeks to meet rejection with acceptance, and another that seeks to reject the world that rejected them. You compare simple, rough-hewn transmutation equations, and illustrations of a humanoid form full of storm clouds letting them loose into the world around them, with Polaroids from Dead Girl's collections. Images of Wastelands and candid shots of Earth-Dies-Screaming with their pack.

                            You can hardly recognize yourself in that twisted flesh.

                            Eventually, Dead Girl's final gift to you gets put away, and only the Athanor remains. You know what it has to teach you.

                            OOC: Earth gains the Informed (Stannum) Condition. You can use it on any roll that conceivably fits the Stannum philosophy, or more directly, on Transmutations.

                                Adonis reads the journal with growing horror, and he almost feels ill. Hatred, disgust, loneliness, contempt: every sentence of the journal that isn't alchemical jargon oozes these emotions. It rattles him, because he's suddenly confronted with a fact he would rather ignore: these are human emotions. Sooner or later, he's going to feel them, too. This is Torment. He's never had to face it, and he has no idea what he will be like when he finally does. He finally comes to the page covered with scribblings and blood, the one dominated by the drawing of the hanged girl. His stomach lurches and he quickly slams the Athanor shut, and shoves it away from himself. "Shit. This is... bad. What made them like... this?"

                                He looks away from the Athanor and over at the potion he studied, which he picks up again. ​"Cobalus Distillation in this one." Cobalus. Impurity. The Cathars. In a lot of ways, it's the opposite of Aurum: Mimics study what makes humans beautiful and good and perfect, Cathars study what makes humans ugly and bad and imperfect. He shudders at the thought, because it reminds him that humans really can be ugly. He's seen it first hand. He stands and takes a couple steps away from the others. This shit is scary.
