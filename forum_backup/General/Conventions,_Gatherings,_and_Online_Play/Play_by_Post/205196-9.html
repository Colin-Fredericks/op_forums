                    Max felt some relief as Nate held off on flipping out at Leo and tried to give him a grateful smile. He did believe the Ram's talk earlier of how the three of them could probably have taken Leo if it came to a fight. Mostly. In a fair situation, certainly. That just wouldn't be what they'd walk into. He and Nate still weren't at 100%, and all of them were still getting used to their new bodies -- bodies, for that matter, that weren't really designed as murder machines. He of the Western Zodiac had also picked up some unknown tricks from at least one Contract. At least one of them might not have walked away from a fight. It would have been enough that he really knew his way around his body.

                    Judging by the distant sounds that followed, Leo also really knew his way around Jessica's body. Max had told himself she knew what she was doing -- and still did -- but... It definitely unsettled him. He'd had the unfortunate experience once to be kept up at night by some neighbors in the dorm. He would have given a lot to go back there for the moment. Knowing Jessica, having no wall in the way, and hearing some less than human moans from both of them made this much more uncomfortable. 'She knows what she's doing. She knows what she's doing. She knows what she's doing.' At least one clenched fist belied his confidence in that mantra.

                    Max looked up and looked around a bit as Jessica's Contract was struck. Like Nate, he picked up on the stillness. And it was more than without: part of him stirred, as though to stand at respectful attention. He'd felt it stirring before, but not like this. It had seemed all appetite, craving, and simple pleasures before. It was strange to feel it acknowledge something greater, something he could never put into words. Indeed, words seemed paltry before it. This was perhaps the first time he felt more than disgust and shame at what he'd become. Those two yet predominated, of course, make no mistake.

                    On the topic of disgust and shame toward oneself, Jessica returned, a bit less clothed and -- as Nate noted -- more wild. Blushing a bit, he tried to avert his gaze, holding up a hand to assist. Max cringed as he heard the word she used to describe what she'd done, without a hint of self-awareness. 'People don't say they mated unless they're using it in a joke!' Still, he felt hypocritical just thinking it. She'd given up something, something important to her, and was now offering to share what she'd gained by it. Like he had any right to judge. "Nate, please let it go," he mumbled, not laughing at the Ram's joke.

                    Still, the offer definitely disappointed Max as well. Recent new spot of not-disdain he'd found for it, he kind of wanted less to do with the pig he'd become, not more. Though it did make sense to get some greater advantage. Even so, the thought of not only deepening the connection, but of signing onto it with his own hand? Terrified him on several levels. That Nate seemed interested in his take on things put him off-balance, too. 'What if the cravings get worse? What if they just feel too good? What could I possibly get that could balance that!?' Well, there was one way to find out. Max didn't think himself in a position to judge Jessica, but he did feel in a position to look a gift ox in the mouth. So to speak. What with trying not to look directly at the naked lady.

                    "I, uh... Th-Thank you for... for going through what you did for this, Jessica. But, uh... B-Before we say yes, m-maybe you could tell us more about it? I mean... Details? Particulars? What it'll let us do, anything we... might give up? I mean, uh..." He tried a bit of (an apparently grunty) laugh to lighten the mood. "Y'know, not knowing the specifics on a deal got us into this mess, right?" He shivered despite himself. "I-I don't think any of us want a repeat of that. Could you... just tell us more before we think about signing on the dotted line?"

                    Max was aware of how spineless he sounded, but not a word was untrue.

                                        'Already paid the price...' Well, Max certainly couldn't argue their displays weren't at least comparable to Jessica's. But the whole thing still felt fishy. Was making the Contract really just a matter of taking advantage of a price already paid? Or was it committing to continue making such payments? Hearing Jessica's words when issuing the contract only made him sure of the latter. Granted, with strong temptations everywhere, perhaps the difference between taking and not taking was academic. Save that striking the bargain at least gave something. In addition to the knowledge he would no longer resist the dehumanizing humiliations.

                                        The stillness came again, even more intense for the proximity, and Max felt his bristles stand on end. He couldn't have said anything if he wanted to. The pig in him was attentive and respectful again, as the great Ram came to be. Just being before such a creature... It had a palpable certainty in itself, as though all the mountains in all the world could grind to dust with time, and it would still be there, just as it was. The idea that anything could be lesser than it seemed shot through with doubt. The Pig couldn't remember the last time he had felt so small and petty. The beast before them was a Presence.

                                        Max saw... something going on between it and Nate, just from being in the same place. He thought he saw something writhing around and within his friend, but could never be sure his eyes weren't just playing tricks. Whatever the case, the kid couldn't help backing up and hurrying to the side, away from the Ram and the Sheep; he was of one mind with the Pig that it was wrong to intrude on this. Though self-preservation might have played a role, given Nate's defiant posture and the Great Beast's charge. He found his voice only long enough to gasp in horror as it crashed into the musician... And then it was over. The mere memory of it kept the stillness for a few moments more. Its pride stayed with Nate.

                                        The Pig stared for a time. Nate looked a bit wilder, just as Jessica had, all the more noticeable with his other changes. There was a greater surety there, too. It seemed obvious in Jessica as well, in hindsight. It made sense; beasts were less likely to second-guess themselves than humans, and simply were what they were. Given how many years he had fought to win some measure of confidence in himself, the Contract had never seemed more tempting. Though second-guessing came into it again: these were the Ram and the Ox, after all, beasts known for their surefootedness and endurance. What grace could the lowly Pig offer?

                                        Still, for the sake of saying something, Max asked both of the newly anointed ones, "Wow... W-What's it like? How do you both feel?" True, Nate's words hinted at the answer, but they hadn't exactly registered. Distracted piglet was distracted.
