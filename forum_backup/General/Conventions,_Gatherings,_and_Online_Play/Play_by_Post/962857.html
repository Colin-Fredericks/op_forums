    [IC]A Cycle Unending

    09-03-2016, 12:54 PM
    All of you receive messages from your elders telling you to be at Blackness Castle just east of Edinburgh for a meeting the evening of 2 January 2016. It is an old castle that for the most part stands empty. You are going there to meet a William Dunsirn and you are all warned that he is not to be trifled with. Wallace recalls meeting William once at a large family gathering, he is slightly shorter than average but covered in muscle. When he talks you can tell that he is a man of deep cunning.

    When you get to the Castle it is cold and rather dark out. A light dusting of snow has covered everything and it looks like it will snow later in the night. As you enter the Castle you are greeted by a rather large man that looks like he is better suited to threatening people than skulking about in an old castle. "The master is occupied for a few moments if you would wait here for a few moments he will be with you shortly."

    The three players find themselves together for the first time with a few minutes before they meet William.

      Raul Abrantes

      Raul, dressed in an old-fashioned winter coat and a hat, brushed off the last bits of snow from his figure. "Damnation!" he thought to himself. "How am I supposed to work or feed in this unwelcoming climate?". Regaining his composure he turned to the other guests in this dark castle, as if noticing them for the first time.
      "Greetings, gentlemen" he said in a reserved, subtly friendly voice. "I am Raul Abrantes, a physician by trade and a philosopher, at your service. Do you, by any chance, know the specifics of why our kind host wanted to see us? Or maybe you have even met mister Dunsirn?"

      Raul extends his hand in a greeting gesture, waiting for his newly met comrades to do the same.

          William St. John

          William finishes up some business on his phone before returning it to his pocket as he enters. He takes brief note of the muscle as he steps into the building proper, but pays the servant little mind. As he approaches the gathered group they can see William is dressed in a tailored long wool overcoat making it difficult to make out the clothing underneath. There is still a light dusting of snow on his shoulders, and it causes the paleness of his skin to stand out even more.

          He shakes the hand offered, "Evening, William St. John, unfortunately I was not provided with an itinerary for the meeting or its purpose. Most of my business tends to be focused around London so I rarely end up this far north so I have not had the chance to meet Mr. Dunsirn before." William looks around the room in the castle for a moment, "Although given our host's choice of meeting location I think we can assume this is not merely a simple business meeting."
