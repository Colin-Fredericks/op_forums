            In Mirrins nightmares there are chittering things that lurk in the shadows and dark places, waiting to devour any unwary traveller to step beyond the safety of the light. To those they see as kin, the creature's noises reveal secrets from the shadows, secretas that tell of twists of fate and essence. Each night Mirrin is forced to listen to these secrets, all the while helpless to save those who wander blindly into the maw of shadows.

            Nodding at paxraes man who steps forward, Mirrin now speaks some of the words heard whispered in nightmares, each word coalescing into a cloud of essence in the air about the small sorcerers frame. Small hands clutching at the essence-clouds, Mirrin weaves the small motes together to create a modest cloud platform that hovers just beyond the rail of the ship, suspended over the rippling water below.

                                    The black ship seems to ignore the Mollusk as it drives forward towards the Pearl as it nearly misses, prow meeting prow instead of the middle of the Pearl as all ramming maneuvers intend to.

                                    The massive crash causes a loud racket as wood meets wood, the huddled guests hiding below deck screamed in unison as the cruise ship rocks violently.

                                    The pirates however are determined to board whether they hit the prow or their intended section of the ship and come pouring out from their deck, a good two dozen charging onto the Gleaming Pearl.

                                    As the Spry Mollusk turns around to the side of the pirate ship, a dozen archers suddenly reveal themselves from the side of the pirate ship, drawing their bows and readying to fire.

                                    At the helm of the pirate ship stands two men, dressed more impressively than the pirates, and if appearances are important, would indicate that these two are the heads of this group.

                                    One of them has reddish-brown hair, golden eyes, wearing a black undershirt with the top half covered by a red, sleveless jacket along with white pants and a metal plated headband. He is of a muscular build, tall man with an elaborate great axe by his side.

                                    Another one has white hair, heterochromia (left green, right red) eyes, wearing a vibrant red sleeved jacket, a black shirt under it and baggy black pants. He seems leaner than the other man and carries a slashing sword at his side and a greatsword at his back.

                                    OOC: Join Battle, a Size 2 battlegroup has boarded the Pearl. a Size 1 battlegroup remains on the pirate ship. The two heads are on their ship and not moving yet.

                                                            After suddenly losing one man in their ranks and then several, the men spot the little girl with a dull blackish knife darting from their ranks. "Over there!" one of them shouts, pointing a sword in her direction.

                                                            They have spotted her.

                                                            But overhead a flare of bluish energy, whirling and whooshing like the wind erupts as Brilliant Snow Crow leaps out of the Mollusk and back onto the Pearl with his instrument clutched in his hand, something glitters beneath his sleeves as the swirling energies whirl around him, his expensive clothes billowing gracefully as he lands by stomping one of the raiding pirates onto the deck, despite the grace of his movements might indicate a soft landing the pirate is smushed into the boards, definitely dead.

                                                            "Now now, bullying a little girl? Get through me first." comes his confident response as the group surges backwards to get away from the elemental flux.
