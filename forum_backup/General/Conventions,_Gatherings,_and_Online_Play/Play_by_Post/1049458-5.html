    Listening to the conversation he decides that this is getting them nowhere fast and he jumps down into the Tunnel. He thinks of a spell that will allow him to know there everything is in the darkness. Forming the Imago in his mind he thinks of the shadows outlining everything in the area the shadows are thick and where there is an absence of shadow there must be something their. As the Spell takes effect He expends the Inspired Condition that is upon him from Fate and pushes the spell to exceptional heights. He can see everyone and everything in the tunnels and all the rooms attached to the tunnels.

    Knowing where everyone is he now senses the Shadow that stands between them and their goal. Once again he calls upon the imago, this time it is a spell that he knows well. He imagines the shadows solidifying and encasing his subject. As the spell finishes the Shadow is encased in shadows, covering every inch of the Shadow Creature from head to toe in solid air tight shadow. Controlling the thickness around the head to be thinner than the rest so that the creature can still hear me, it will be muffled but he can still hear. Strife will join his companions in the circle.

    "I believe that I have neutralized the Creature for now but we must hurry before the Priests finish their chanting."


    Creative Thaumaturgy
    Know the Shadows (Death •)
    Practice: Knowing
    Primary Factor: Duration
    Withstand: Resolve
    Suggested Rote Skills: Investigation, Occult, Subterfuge.
    The shadows belong to the Death mage and with this spell you cannot hide within them. The subject of this spell can sense with clarity anything that touches the shadows within an area. By sensing the absence of shadow he knows where everything is. This will remove the effects of the Low Light Condition from the subject. The area is centered on the subject and moves with him as he walks. Subtract the potency from stealth attempts make to hide in the shadows, and if someone is actively trying to hide in the shadows they can Withstand using Resolve. Anything moving through the area of effect faster than a walk just leaves a streak through the shadows.
    +1 Reach: With this reach the subject knows the physical dimension of anything in the spells area, as the shadows cling to the object outlining every curve, crease, fold.

    Actions
    Rote/Praxis/Improvised Imago: Know the Shadows (Death •)
    Dice Pool: 3 (Gnosis) + 3 (Arcane) +1 (Yantras) = 7 dice pool.
    Reaches: Free Reaches: 3, Reaches used: 3 (1 to move to instant casting, 1 to change spell to Advanced Duration , 1 to change spell to Advanced Scale.)
    Yantras used: (Mace +1)

    Results: Know the Shadows: 7d10h8 4: Success, use Inspired Condition to make it an Exceptional Success removing withstand.

    Potency: 1

    Range: Self
    Hekau Cost: 0
    Willpower Cost: 0

    Desired Effect: Outline everything that is touched by shadows to get the outline of the tunnels and those in the tunnels.

                    Strife descends down into the tunnel and opens his eyes to the shadows. He does this so well, in fact, that he recovers 1 Mana. He then attempts to bind the Shadow-creature in its own dark aura, only to be countered successfully by Imhotep.

                    Strife sees the subtleties of the shadows obscuring the hidden chamber. The narrow brick tunnel is lined with notches and carved signs that seem to have ritual significance. At the end of the tunnel is a low square room with more elaborate carvings on the solid stone walls. There may be painted details and colors that the shadows still obscure. Strife recognizes on the far end of the wall a carved stone mural depicting the same scene that he meditated before in the temple proper: Set, standing proud on the solar barge of Ra, spearing the serpent Apophis to protect his lord. There are also several clay jars mounted on little altars, a scroll repository set into the wall, and heavy wooden chests for storage.

                    At the center of the low room, some 30 yards away from you down the narrow tunnel, is a raised altar (or sarcophagus?) surrounded by heavy curtains. Perhaps the master sleeps here. There are three unmarked stone sarcophagi on the packed earth floor, flanking the central curtained one.

                    Closer to the group, Strife feels that the boy Shadow is clinging to the ceiling behind them, wearing the darkness like a cloak and mask. He seethes:

                    "Enough of this. Terms have been offered. Will you do business, or shall we withdraw our hospitality?"

                    Accept/Decline the terms originally presented? Or present an alternative? The Shadow is getting antsy.

                    Strife can roll Wits+Crafts (Architecture) to assess the significance of this room if he likes, this is in his area of expertise.

                                Strife picks up on some of the details from his architectural training. Kemet had been under Persian rule for some 2 centuries before Alexander drove them out a few years ago, and the temple and town of Hibis were constructed early in this Persian period. What's strange is that the low chamber ahead of you matches the architectural style of a much earlier era, as far back as the New Kingdom of one thousand years ago. The design of this place is quite unlike the rest of the Hibis temple above it. It suggests that this place came first, and the temple was built later.

                                Also notable is the system of regular notches and designs carved into the stone walls. They are cracked and eroded, remains of a bygone era. He's heard of hidden chambers with patterns like this before in especially ancient temples and guildhalls, places with a subtle but potent Sekhem that eludes Awakened senses. The more senior Weret-Hekau of Abydos, City of Tombs, know the most about these hidden chambers. All Strife knows for sure is that they are highly dangerous and have a way of attracting monsters and other unworthies. No wonder the Greek tomb-robbers broke in.
