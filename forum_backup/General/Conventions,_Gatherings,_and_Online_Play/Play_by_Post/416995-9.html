    "Thank you for the warning." Nergui nodded solemnly. "My goddess loves outcasts and exiles, so hopefully we will be able to get along. I will see you again, Healer. Please continue to work toward your own answer to the problem. I may die without bringing you an answer, but the gods favor those who help themselves."

    With that, Nergui departed and headed back to he bazaar, towards the Guild's local offices. He pressed past the crowd, and he gave the place a wary look before entering. Looming in the doorway, he took in the tables and thugs, as well as the pale woman. He disliked this place instantly. Too much parchment and ink, too many scrolls...and, of course, too many unpleasant looking men with weapons. The place made him nervous. Still...there was at least one friend here, Lin Zarah, which calmed hims lightly.

    He stood straight in the doorway, and in a deep confident voice, he boomed, "I am l-looking for Alma of Ast-ast-ast...t-terberg."

    ...that didn't come out as deep and confident as he had been going for.

        Marian begins to head in the direction the deathly figures from before had went. They were interesting to him in only a way he was sure he could appreciate. He had to know from where they were from and if they were going to cause him problems. If they were allied with or servants of the bishop, he would have to deal with them quietly. Were they independent he could hope to have allies. On the other hand, were they allied to or servants of another Deathlord, they would then be possibly more complicated to deal with. There was no telling if they would want to eliminate, control, or ally with him, an outcast. Approaching them very carefully would be the only option. It was almost certain they would be able to tell his nature right away. His only hope would be that they didn't know him by reputation and that he could attempt to pass himself off as whatever could be the most beneficial to them until he was able to discern their origins and intent.

He is not so quick to shy from any source of potential knowledge. Not anymore...
