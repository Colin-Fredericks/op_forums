                                                        For these last rolls I've made a ton of assumptions. 1.) I don't know whether or not Specialties apply to Contract rolls, so I've decided not to apply it. 2.) I've assumed all four people are average, so that would mean 2-3 Composure, I went with 3 as the highest among them. 3.) Dale has managed to isolate them in a creepy area, so I gave a +1 bonus for spooky ambiance. 4.) Since Dale has worked pretty hard to establish the myth of Sullivan, I've decided that the Catch has been fulfilled.
