      Chapter 1 "Manifest Destiny"

      Gilad looks out from his window seat to the seemingly endless sea of clouds that wisp past the jet liner. Beside him, Trish leans on his shoulder and gently dozes. Across the aisle, Galahad stretches out, enjoying the first class seats that they somehow got bumped up to from coach. A bit of luck. It is about a twenty hour flight from Buenos Aires to San Francisco.

      Trish whispers something gently in her sleep in that language that Gilad thinks is her home language, and then slowly stirs awake.

      "I need to use the garderobe" she says in Old Norse, and then gets up from her seat. "Will you look after my mobile speaking machine?" she says, handing Gilad a small thing that looks like a mirror that people use for seemingly everything these days, including, on occasion, speaking with people over far distances.

      Gilad looks out the window again, thinking over the events of the past few months and how to turn them into a heroic song. After awhile he begins to wonder why Trish has been gone so long. He looks over to Galahad, but he is not in his seat. Which is curious, because Gilad did not notice him getting up. The airplane shakes a little, like a ship on rough waves.

              With fingers drumming on the window arm rest, Olorion stares out the window thinking about how much he already missed the library and picking up Galahad with assorted unknowns ate into his alone quiet stack time. One never knew what surprise Supernal symbol one might find in old books. Olorion sighed, In all fairness I am just pissed that this newest “friend of a friend” of my favorite fence turned out to be totally useless. Money wasted. But I will find The Book again.

              A bump in the road jolts Olorion out of his thoughts and he feels the need to focus. He truthfully had been feeling off for a couple days. Everyone seemed edgy but that just happened in academia. Someone made the wrong comment about someone’s favorite line of epic poetry, and everyone is about to throw things. However the supernatural always had the best timing so who could really tell. Olorion looked up and smiled, this is why he loved being a mage. He did not just have to sit with the feeling, in the immediate he could have protect himself in anticipation of escorting his colleague back to Berkely. Later when he had time he could do some research. He took a breath and closed his eyes. Clearing his mind, he looked up and smiled at the driver in the mirror.

              “I think I am going to rest my eyes a bit, I am going to put up the shade. I would be very grateful if I was not disturbed”, Olorion passed the driver a $20 bill and nodded his head. The privacy partition went up and the audio system would prevent any sounds from the back unless Olorion wanted the driver to hear. Olorion grew up in New York, he had no idea how to drive or the desire to learn. Olorion was glad for the fact his Arrow cabal mate was returning, having a gallant warrior around made life easier for mages.

              Olorion closed his eyes and centered himself. He began by reciting prime numbers. Then define random words. Opening his eyes he found the rhythm, in a second, he began to utter words that were beyond normal meaning. Keeping the rhythm, he began to twist the iron ring on his finger. Focusing his mind, he forms an Imago in his mind.

              Grabbing the threads of time that weaved in his mind’s eye parallel to threads of space, he plucked threads seemingly at random. With finger spinning his iron ring and his words following the rhythm he had found he wrapped the threads of time around himself. He sucked in air as a flutter of primal power left him to power the spell. Casting the Perfect Timing around himself, if he was going into a strange situation, he reasoned he could start with a little precaution. He was going to be doing some divining later when he could do ritual work.

                                      Olorion opens his eyes and sighs. The spell wrapped around him, and he felt the joy of magic done well. The flow of the temporal thread seemed to nudge him just a bit in the direction of the driver, like Olorion had met him before but could not place where.
                                      Looking down he notices a book in the seat next to his for the first time. “Well, hello there beautiful, whom might you be.” Picking it up and inspecting the book Olorion finds it is an old but worn addition of an Old Norse dictionary. The inside cover reveals kanji characters and a small arrangement of characters that he knew was the name of a bookseller. This is also somewhat familiar to him, but Olorion is at a loss to place where. A stylized picture of a fox with six tails is present with the kanji.

                                      Huh, a kitsune. Very pretty, good quality. The book is worn but not too unkempt. The pages are intact, and the spine is still supportive to the overall structure. But why did you appear to me my newest friend.
                                      Olorion looks up and sees the partition still closed. Breathing in and out, Olorion focuses his mind and reaches for the Imago established for one his favorite spells, a rote of the Universal Language spell.
                                      Olorion’s mind began filling with the Imago of the rote. In his mind Olorion sees himself sitting in a circle of concentric rings of books. The rings go out as far as Olorion “can see” and if one were to look closely at them they would be rings of books on language, symbols, and the spoken word. Olorion’s hands move up to his eyes and make rhythmic motions circling around his eyes, then moved to his ears, then his mouth. Then placing one hand on his forehead to symbolize the mind’s ability to use language, and a second on his heart to symbolize the power language had on the emotional part of humanity. Breathing out Olorion pulls the symbols of language into his mind and settles them over his mind like a glove, warm and comfortable.

                                      The inside of the car is filled with the scent of old parchment and leather.
