                                            There are not.
                                            DBL is Craft 5, E4. For Artifacts it lets you craft an artifact as though it were 1 dot lower without changing its actual power. So you could craft a Daiklave (Artifact 2) with 10 successes and only 1 Exotic Material Component. When used with Manses it lets you cap a demesne with a manse one dot higher or to cap a 5 dot demesne with a manse that has twice the normal creation points.

                                            AoP is Craft 6 E6. It raises the Resource or Artifact level of an item by one but removes all need for maintenance and it lasts forever. So a candle would never run down, a sword would never need sharpening, an implosion bow with AoP alchemical reagents would never need to be refilled, etc.
