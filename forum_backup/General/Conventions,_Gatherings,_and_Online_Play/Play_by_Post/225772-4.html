                                Sunset Island is pretty much entirely uninhabited: it's dominated by an active volcano, and the people of Whitestone Island view it as sacred and only go there on pilgrimages in small groups, and they don't set up dwellings there. It's believed to be the home of Konani of the Evening Light, the local volcano god.

                                Whitestone Island, the larger island, is the one the Maiden is actually from. They're scattered around the island in small villages, many of which are hidden from view by forest and mountains, but some of their seaside villages will be visible from the sea-lots of wooden houses and huts with thatched roofs. It is safe to assume that most of the seaside villages have docks full of small fishing boats, but the boats of Whitestone Island generally don't have much of a draw, so their docks are often pretty shallow.

                                There are several sheltered bays where Phi Ho's ship could anchor, but you're going to find yourself drawn to the sheltered inlet where Calm Sea Village was built, on Whitestone Island's southern coast.

                                                The island's population does worship other deities besides the ones I've listed. Those are just the most major and popular gods on the island, the ones running the local spirit courts, who the other smaller local gods answer to.
