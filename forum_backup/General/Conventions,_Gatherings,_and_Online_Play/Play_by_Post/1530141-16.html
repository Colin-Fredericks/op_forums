                    It's a Time spell, so you are not limited by Space. You could theoretically look up a name in a telephone book and say, "Is Sarah Connor home right now?" and receive a yes or no answer. "Is this the Sarah Connor that I am looking for?" is not related to time and the subject, so that would not be a valid question for Divination. "Will I find the Sarah Connor I am looking for if I go to this address?" is more likely to yield a useful answer (a loophole that Berith exploited earlier in this chronicle).

                    The more specific the question, the more likely that the information will be useful to you. "Will Mum be in danger?" does not have a time specificity so the answer would take into account her entire lifespan, including whatever eventually kills her (like cancer forty years from now).

                                            So, I hate to be nit-picky, but divination can tell you "your subject's most likely future" (MtAw 2E Core, pg. 186). Normally this is a yes, no, or irrelevant answer but with the reach you can "ask more specific questions and receive mostly accurate answers".

                                            "Will Joanna (or anyone else) try to kill me in the future?" is an answerable question for divination, but the answer is as vague as the question. "Can Trish save me?" Is not a time related question, and therefore does not fall under the practice of knowing Time.

                                            Your first casting of divination revealed the answer to the question "Am I in danger from something today?" By indicating that something shocking and presumably dangerous will happen to you, Joanna, and another woman who speaks a language you don't recognize.

                                            Your second question, "Will I ever find Tyche?" Was answered with the condition that you must help "her handmaiden first". And then the forgotten note with Trish's phone number presented itself to you, perhaps indicating that Trish is the "handmaiden" of Tyche in some way, and that helping her will lead you to Tyche.

                                            Given that, do you want to change the wording of the questions? You can keep the die results.

                                            My only other quible is that Driven is a condition specifically gifted by the Judges of Duat to the Arisen (to the best of my understanding. I don't have my MtC book with me right now)

                                            MtA is about the personal horror of hubris and so the system mechanics tend to punish "ambition" rather than reward it. Equivalent conditions to Driven for a Mage might be Megelomanical or Rampant, but my guess is that those have effects that you don't want - or maybe you do. Up to you.
