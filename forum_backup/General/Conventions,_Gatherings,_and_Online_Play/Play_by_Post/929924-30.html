    He felt very small and unsteady as he took the podium, but once he started speaking, everything solidified again.

    The muted reactions to his victory, especially among the Knights, struck a discordant note in his mind. He won. He defended Hakim's honor! This isn't how it's supposed to be. Why isn't it like it's supposed to be?

    "I've seen too many Sleepers or Sleepwalkers in service of an Order lose themselves to hubris and temptation upon Awakening. Until you are ready, you will have a mentor like all the others."

    But...what? No. No, no. That can't be right. This isn't hubris. He's doing what Hakim would have wanted. This is how mages are supposed to act. Why isn't everyone cheering?

    He's very obviously confused and unsteady when he walks back to his seat with the others. His ears are burning with embarrassment. He needs to talk to Valentine as soon as possible, not to mention the others.

    ---

    Ragnar speaks up about their shared theory. He'd almost forgotten about this past week. The name flashes in his mind again: the Destroyer. But he doesn't know enough about that yet to say anything useful. He'll keep his Obsession close to his chest for now.

No one else with us experienced the same effect. How could the Consilium investigate this hypothesis further? Perhaps by questioning a Seraph?"

                    Everyone
                    Cielo nods and motions to Estrella, who smiles and stands before addressing you and the other Nameless in a clear, crisp voice. "The official requirements are quite simple: After you agree as a group on your list of members, you must agree upon a group name, and present yourselves as a group at a Consilium meeting or to the Herald or other Council member. Although not a formal requirement, you are also expected to mutually establish and swear oaths to a creed for your new cabal. This creed can and should include your cabal's mission, and serves to join your loyalties and create a shared vision for its members. You are not required to present your creed to the Consilium, but many cabals here have done so; the Silver Ladder keeps these on public record and can use them to settle disputes in certain cases."

                        When they have a few seconds with just the Nameless, he suggests to everyone: "Don't stray too far after this. Let's talk somewhere calm, while we're all here."

                        The church isn't exactly in an arts district. The nearest thing to a cafe in this neighborhood is a certain donut-based fast food chain. But hey, that's where people do business in Chicago.

                        He's swept away by his adoring fans before he can say more. It's another rush of nervous excitement, but he tries to ride the wave like he did on the podium. Shaking hands, bowing with a flourish, animatedly pointing and gesturing and showing off his fluid movements, blushing crimson. Rather Naively, he prattles on and on and tells the crowd a great deal about his dueling technique:

                        "Of course I had seen the Duel Arcane many times before, which may have given me an edge, but I never imagined how it would feel! We think of it like physical combat, but that traps your creativity- I realized that it was a contest of mind and spirit- lowercase, not the Arcana, haha, although I did make use of Mind, among other Arcana- he relied exclusively on Fate, thinking his strongest Arcanum was all he needed. Another trap! Instead of fighting him, I danced! He insisted on a show of force, a boulder of sealed Fate rolling down a mountain, expecting me to raise a shield and be crushed- but the canny magician simply steals the boulder's momentum and redefines the conflict without ever donning armor! I knew that if I just kept him on the attack, my will could endure anything he threw at me while I gave him the so-called 'death of a thousand cuts,' and furthermore I knew- a little birdy told me- that he was too proud to take me seriously, and so I carefully worded my challenge and my terms to infuriate him into a blind offense. Can you believe that he wanted to teach me humility? Ha! Pride before the fall, as they say. It was a victory of the soft and cunning and poison-lipped over the bull-headed and proud- there you go, tire yourself out striking empty air, then charge right into the labyrinth of mirrors I've conjured for you! Aion knows I couldn't throw a fireball or sprout claws for the life of me, but there are so many ways to outwit and out-Will someone without laying a finger on them. Do you have any idea how many years I spent watching every move my master made, imagining every possible thing I could do with the Arcana if I had a chance? And then to see him laid low by the truth-force of my Awakened soul after he had the temerity to strike me, thinking me his lesser, after I saved his life no less- well, I'm not one to brag, but..."


                        They scatter. He looks over his shoulder, and the hair on the back of his neck stands up. Valentine casts a long shadow. Luckily(?) the Knights step in first, and immediately get to business. He clasps his hands for a nervous but warm-hearted greeting. "Rose- Askia- Ghost- it's, um, wonderful to see you again. I'll just get right to it."

                        He briefly relates Tuesday's events, emphasizing that he doesn't know exactly what happened in the portal, only that he and Phoenix came back with a Gulmoth in tow. "...and since then, I can't stop thinking about that thing. What it did to us- it showed me my worst moments in a mirror, it paralyzed us with fear, it would have killed people if Phoenix and I didn't stop it. I've, um...dreamed about it. Every night this week. It isn't magic or a curse, I don't think, but the trauma has a hold on my Mind. And when the others and I face this thing again and banish it for good, I can't let that trauma be a weakness. I want to make it a weapon instead."

                        "I want to take the power my nightmare has over me and use it against the real thing. I'm done being imprisoned by my fear. I want to draw it out and make it serve me. And if Spade can help me with that, I want to at least make my case to him."

                        He forces himself to say a little more in a lower voice, thinking of his conversation with Ace. "Listen. This has been...so, so hard. It's too much, every day. I'm not going to ask you to- I mean- I made my choice when I left with him. We know. The Knights don't owe me anything."

                        His fingers are tied together tight. He's keenly aware of Valentine just over his shoulder. "I'm proud of my victory over Mathias. But I feel like I'm not acting right lately. I won't worry you with the details. The Awakening didn't, um, get rid of my personality stuff, in case you were wondering. But I see it more clearly. And I- you know- obviously, I can't um, rely on him anymore. It's just me now. And maybe the Nameless, but they can't become a new thing to depend on. I need help, so I can get better. For him. And I don't think Sleeping medicine will help- I would have to lie about so, so much. But maybe Oneiros magic will work. Ace told me there are Mind mages who specialize in mental health..."

                        "...I'm trying to ask you whether Spade is safe. Is he someone I can talk to, or will I have to keep it to magic?"

With effort, you might be able to glean more from the magical effect, but this Mystery appears complex at its surface.
