                Originally posted by Haberdasher View Post
                And we're off! I've been waiting for this moment for a while.
                Me too!

                A few things I wanted to comment on now that I've got IC up and running.

                Please post at your own speed as your schedule allows, and don't feel obligated to post on any given day if you don't have time. I will generally try to post a few times a day, or at least daily, but that won't always be realistic for all of us at any given time. I encourage you to post at least once weekly just to keep things moving. If it feels like we're moving too slowly, I may move us along.

                I like what you've all done with the spoiler tag for rolls and OOC info, so I'll do that too.

                I am still learning the 2E rules. I'll try to take time to reference them before posting, but if I mess something up, please post in the OOC thread and let me know! I'm hoping to pick it up quickly but there is a lot to know.

                For Orokos dice roller, make sure your dice expression is set correctly. Normally it's xd10o10h8, where x=dice pool. If you have the 9- or 8-again property or rote quality on a roll, adjust the hX part or oX part accordingly. KKV, I think you had yours set to count 7s as a success. And anyone can ask for help if you need it.

                Periodically I will ask for feedback about my storytelling style and how you feel about the game overall. Please don't hesitate to give constructive criticism. I'm trying to grow as a ST here. I know from experience that I sometimes lean more naturally towards running sandbox kind of games than clear plot/story arcs, even when I'm trying to do the latter, but I'm trying to improve on how I create drama and conflict and clear antagonists. Please speak up or send a PM if you don't like something.

                In your posts, under your name, please add a line to include current spells you have active (even if none currently), and another line for conditions/tilts. On one of those lines you might also want to note when you have active or focused Mage sight on. This will help a lot in active conflict or combat situations, but also helps me remember what extra information you might pick up.

                My current plan for NPC rolls is to not post them here, but I'll still use Orokos. Is that ok with everyone, or would you prefer I post for transparency's sake?

                                Originally posted by Haberdasher View Post

                                Edit: Consulted the scorebook, looks like only one action per turn. I'll just try to pacify the class for now.
                                Eh, I think I am more flexible than that, especially with PbP games. Rather than letting the game slow down because you want back and forth for rolls, I'm ok with you including more than one action per post. If time or turns start to matter, I'll jump in and ask you to wait for something before you can take the second action, or call for initiative.

                                Also, unless there's specifically something you're waiting on me to reply to in your post, you all are welcome to interact with each other in a back and forth fashion without me stepping in. We don't all have to post at a 1-to-1 ratio.

                                On a related note in terms of game time: I'll indicate scene transitions, as that may matter for things like willpower costs for Mage Sight, etc. I'll also be breaking up chapters into reasonable chunks based on story arcs rather than game sessions, for obvious reasons. I'll sometimes grant beats or arcane beats mid-scene; if that gives you enough for a full experience or arcane experience point, you can spend it any time but the effects will kick in after the scene ends. In the case of things that need some kind of in-game time commitment, like some Merits (e.g., Resources), I may require you to wait until time between chapters. I'm planning to give "interlude" downtime between some chapters to indicate longer periods of down time.
