        Everyone:

        The girl screams and flops to the ground, wailing in terror and unsure of what's going on. She is not lucid enough to focus on Vincent, and uses her good arm to pull herself against Piero, curling into his chest. Sobs wrack her body, and she shivers like a baby in the cold against the paramedic. Her cries are enough to call people's attention, others starting to run over to where she lies.

        As you stare at Vincent, Eulalia, you begin to feel a little niggling behind your ear. It's a cold set of pinpricks, like the skin has gone numb; you might be tempted to turn your head. Only you hear the words whispered, hissed into your ear: "North of Laramie.... North of Laramie...." For the briefest second, there is a blurry flash out of the corner of your eye, the steel of cars smudged together with road's stone.

        But as soon as you notice it, it is gone, and there is nothing but the accident scene. A police car pulls up, and officers start marching towards the crowd around the girl. A man in a suit is with them.

                                The officers turn their heads towards Vincent, a couple already moving towards him with guns drawn.                                                     Everyone:

                                                    The police officers don't look impressed, Vincent. In fact, they don't even look convinced. Too coolly and civilly for all things considered, one of them snaps, "Get out of the car, sir!" and motions with his gun, his partner's eyes flickering around. It's not really a show of force...it's more like they want to keep things quiet, all of a sudden. Another man is looking warily over at Piero and the girl.

                                                    And with good reason — like you, Eulalia, he seems to notice that the girl's blood is becoming translucent. It's like her body is filled with red dye, and the dye is finishing flushing out of her system. Piero wouldn't notice it unless he really looked, but you...for some reason, you and the officer can see it clearly. In the back of your mind, you hear and feel a sudden burst of static, scratching at your inner tissues like cat claws against rough boards.

                                                    Meanwhile, the men with Magnusar don't seem to notice you, Piero. They flicker their eyes towards you, but they are behaving...oddly. They don't seem concerned, looking at the girl like she's feigning injury. The girl's sobs have quieted, and now, she is glaring at the men. Your wet shirt suddenly feels cold.
