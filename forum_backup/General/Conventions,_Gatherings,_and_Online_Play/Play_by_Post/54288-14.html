    Noticing the other Solar waving his blade around, Yi observed for a moment before he spoke up. "That isn't the way to go about it. You are not bringing something new into existence, like you did with the shield, but making a statement of what is already so. 'As the sun is unassailable in the sky, so am I beyond my enemies.' It is a thing not of motion, but of stillness. Though all the world shift and change, you are eternal and unmoved."

    He paused for a moment remembering his own first dance with the sword.

    "Rather than use the sword, brandish it. Make each motion its own statement of your ability. Even if one flows into the next, make each an individual sign of your quality. The sun does not flicker, but every second is an affirmation of its light."

                                "Strange as strange that gibberin' though. Can't a preacher speak normal like so as we can understand 'im? All that hollerin for the gods alone never did much for me."

                                            Those of you who head towards the Temple find it to be a broad stone building. I looks to be at least four stories in height, and looks to be taller due to the massive stone base on which it rests. The building and its base are both of a dark brown-grey stone, and appear to have simply been grown from the earth, rather than built. It's appearance is neither similar to the simple monastery-temples of the Immaculate Order or the ostentatious cathedrals found in certain portions of the Threshold. It is a solid building, looking to be as much of a fortress as a temple. Solid, practical, and lacking ostentation. Much like the people who worship there.

                                            The interior of the temple is much like the exterior. It is decorated almost entirely with statues and tapestries; the only metal you can see are coins in the many votive offering bowls, and the weapons in the hands of the temple guards. There appear to be a relatively small number of priests, perhaps a score at most, milling about the temple, identifiable by their deep green stoles. Each stole has a variable series of blue and red stripes at the ends, perhaps indicating their duties or rank. The temple is filled with many people, and their quiet murmurings combining to make a solid buzz of background noise. In a far corner of the temple, you notice a temple Sorcerer conjuring food; the reactions of those within the temple appear to indicate that this is not an unusual occurrence. A small boy in tattered clothing runs straight into Yi, his mother, a ragged middle-age woman, pulls him aside and apologizes profusely. While she shuffles her son out the door, you notice that the basket she's carrying holds a loaf of bread, several pinkish ovoid wafers, and a handful of small denomination coins.

                                            All in all, you get the impression that the gods and priests within the temple genuinely care about spreading the wealth that enters their doors around the community. Or they are incredibly savvy, knowing that doing so earns them a great deal of favor with the lower classes. Or they are incredibly clever, and are doing both.

                                            A young priestess approaches you. Her stole bears a broad red and a narrow blue stripe; the most common combination you've seen. She asks if there is there is anything she can do to assist you.

                                                            (At the Hall of the Boule)
                                                            The hall of the Boule is a large, hexagonal building, built of a light-grey stone. Aside from its size, there is not anything particularly special about its construction. Dero leads you past several entrances, urging you towards a specific entrance on the north-eastern face. Above the entrance, you notice Gens Ptreas' house Mon. Dero indicates that each Gens has a specific entrance to their chambers. A pair of guards bar your passage, but they allow you through when shown Nikon's document. Once through, you find yourself in a large waiting room. There are a half dozen large offices surrounding room, with about the same number of smaller offices. Presumably, these are the offices of Ptreas' representatives and the representatives of their cadet houses, respectively.

                                                            A balding portly man steps out of one of the offices moments after you enter, grumbling something about "Damned-able Zophyrus wheedling". He glances in your direction, and with a shocked expression, heads over to you.

                                                            "Ptreas Artemeon Derontikles, what are you doing here?" He demands. Dero, mildly embarrassed introduces his uncle, Ptreas Nikoloin Isaakos, fourth Representative of Gens Ptreas in the Halls of the Boule.

                                                            (At the Academy)
                                                            The Academy, is strangely enough, a park. A carefully landscaped park, occasionally interspersed with tall, narrow towers, themselves attached to each other with long, delicate walkways. Gazing from the outside, you see that the only people walking around on ground-level are caretakers, who are tending to the park's careful landscape. Taking a step into the Academy, you feel a spike in ambient essence. The Academy is a Wood-aspected manse. Once you step through, a spirit, eerily reminiscent of a humanoid Mantis materializes in front of you.

                                                            "The Academy is not accepting applications at this time," the spirit says in a clicking voice, "Good morning"

                                                            (At the Temple)
                                                            The young priestess smiles at Yi's request. "Of course sir. We understand that there are those who require privacy to commune with the divine."

                                                            She leads you towards one of the building's corners, were what appears to be a small nook turns back onto itself, revealing a narrow hallway dotted with doors. Each door has a simple curtain in front of it. A small basket of matte black stones stands nearby atop a small wooden table. The priestess says, "Simply place one of these stones on the floor outside your chosen room. That will indicate that it is taken." With that, she bows and leaves. You notice several stones on the floor, and taking one from the basket, you do as the priestess suggested with your chosen room.

                                                            Inside the room, you find a simple altar, lit from above by a skylight. On the altar, there is a small bowl of unlit incense, and foot tall wooden statuette of a robed man, holding a staff and with a short, broad blade on his belt.
