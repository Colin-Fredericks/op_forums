                Originally posted by Exthalion View Post
                Noodle, a couple of quick questions:

                Approximately how long will it take for the second force to head out against the fey?
                How long is the trip to Ordon?
                What are the procedures for a new Gens joining Novalesk?
                1) Four days, forced march. A week or so otherwise. Maybe more if the weather is against you.

                2) Ordon the Nation? You could be on its borders in a day if you traveled by boat. Ordon the city? Five days or so, depending on method of transportation.

                3) Ah. I wondered when this would be asked.

                First, one must obtain a sponsor Gens. While the law doesn't say so, after the inclusion of Gens Spyrus and the creation of the Cadet House system, it is expected that the family be a Cadet House, and the sponsor Gens is their patron.

                Second, the prospective Gens must be considered to have been "tied into Novalesk by bonds of culture and blood". This is interpreted as of present to be that at least 75% of the House in question must have been born and raised in Novalesk, as must have their parents.

                Third, the prospective family must be large enough to have at least one Representative in the Boule with population alone. (So, you need to have at least 2% of the total Gentes population after you're added in. At present, that would be slightly over 2000 members).

                Fourth, the sponsored family must have a home town or city. It is expected that this town/city is built by the family (either by their hands or purses) for it's own use. This town must contain a temple to Origenes and the family's genius, as well as a military garrison facility. The house in question must pay for the construction and upkeep of both facilities.

                Fifth; the sponsor Gens and prospective Gens' (patri)/(matri)archs must have a formal audience before the collected Gens heads, Archon and Origenes (if the Archon is indisposed, a Thesmothete or the Pyrtannies takes their place). There, they present their case for the inclusion of another Great House. A majority vote is taken at the end of this audience. However, if there are more than two "no" votes from Orgienes, the Archon and the Original Gentes, the vote is deemed failed even if a majority is otherwise reached.
