                    Yi had entered Grey Ice nearly a day earlier with all the aplomb of a beggar and exactly as much fanfare. (Which is to say he saw more disgusted looks than he did people and was spit at by the one in fifty who didn't have something better to do.) He had planned for this, cultivated it in fact, through the blunt tactics of letting his garments appear dirty and of poor quality, walking with the weariness of one who has walked several miles without sleep, and actually asking for money on account of his having none. All in all, impersonating a vagrant by being one was a fairly effective strategy.

                    He had not looked this way for the whole of his journey, but events at Gunzota Redoubt had convinced him that the eyes of Lookshy were still present and vigilant even out here at the tangential edge of their sphere of interest. It impressed him, and raised a small flush of pride, that the City had extended its reach so far.

                    The city he found did not compare favorably to the City, especially after the reminder of Lookshy's influence. Its fortifications ranged from deplorable to nonexistent, its stocks of arms and materiel were laughable, and, inexcusably, the fea's fifth column of fear and doubt had been allowed to garrison itself. Whatever the risk to himself, his dignity as a human being would not permit him to allow these people to continue as they were.

                    He walked out into the marketplace, noting again the sluggish commerce, and began to sing.

                    "Ashira l'Invicti ki ga-oh ga-ah"

                    It was a prayer, and an ancient one, found engraved in the rusted bronze tang of weapons as old as the Shogunate if not more so. For the longest time he had not known what the markings meant, written as they were in neither Old Realm nor any of the seven tongues of men. Yet as he had reflected back on his life while imprisoned something like memory had come over him and he had known the meaning.

                    "I will praise the Unconquered, for he has triumphed greatly."

                    The song began softly, almost hesitantly. The first time it had been sung the victory it praised was still so new, so unbelievable, that even those who had won it were in wonder of what they had done.

                    Then it grew stronger, louder, more sure. At the final verse he sung out with all his strength and the echoes of his voice seemed to ring with the tiny voices of ten thousand little gods who remembered the first golden dawn when Sol alone rose into the sky.

                    Activating a combo of Gathering the Congregation with Mastery of Small Manners, my Charisma based version of Heartfelt Honorific Opportunity and the Second Socialize Excellency. The combo is enhanced with a Compassion channel and I am activating the Guan of Heaven's Sun.

                    Final Roll is Charisma 3 + Socialize 5 + Specialty in Social Change 1 + Compassion 4 + 3 Artifact = 16 dice: 5 successes + 3 die stunt: http://invisiblecastle.com/roller/view/4410412/"[/COLOR]]1 success + Second Excellency 2 success = 8 successes.

                    The attack is unexpected.meaning anyone without a surprise negator has an MDV of 0. My effective appearance is 7 which should ping even people with a negator with the -3 MDV modifier.

                    Anyone who can see me (which includes dematerialized spirits if there are any) who doesn't spend 3wp(assuming the 0 mdv if they aren't surprised its 1+ every three successes over MDV) (or activate a charm) to resist is now a social unit under my direction.

                                        Where is the sound of hammers falling like rain to make implements of iron. Where is the chaunt of priests to call down the blessing that is your due?
