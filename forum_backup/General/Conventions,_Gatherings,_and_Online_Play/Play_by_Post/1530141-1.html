    I created an In-Character thread for scene descriptions and character interactions.

    The storytelling goal for Chapter 1 is uniting the party.

    Primark ( Lorekeeper ) is given a command by the Meisters of Winterhold to obtain dominion over Walworth Estate, a public housing community in Southwark Borough of London. This command is a condition. Every time Primark makes a substantial advancement towards this goal, such as recruiting a community planner ( nickeldan ) or Borough council member ( VictorOfNyke ), he earns an arcane beat. The condition is resolved when the ministry gains control of the Estate, and further instructions are given.

    Mother Time ( Critian Caceorte ) has a prelacy from the Horologian ministry, so she has a persistent condition of Exarchal command that serves as a secondary obsession. All commands from the Prophet must be resolved before she can pursue her personal obsession (maximizing her brain to the breaking point?). The Prophet commands that Mother Time to help further the ends of the ministry of Mammon in gaining control of the Walworth Estate. The estate is near the school she currently is headmistress of, and many of her students live there. The school building was converted from an old office building and is starting to be badly worn down. Perhaps the school needs a new building?
