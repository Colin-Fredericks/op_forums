                Raul Abrantes

                Raul nods and says "Yes, I think no additional conclusions are needed for now. Do you mind, gentlemen, if I book a train ride to Nottingham and a resting place for tomorrow?" If not met with objections, he adds "Then it's settled. If you excuse me, I had a long journey from America and require some rest".

                He doesn't, however, go to his room immediately. Instead, his forst stop is a library, where he stays for a while, reading and searching for any topic that seems of particular interest to his host. Then he uses this "secure communication" channel to send a message to his sire:

                "Dunsirn is looking for a haven of Thomas Camden, a fabled Necromancer with obscure ties to the Giovanni. Contacted a neonate Nosferatu in Nottingham and scheduled a meeting there in three nights. He sent us - me, and one member of St. John and his own family each - to conclude the deal. I will write if I learn anything more and do my best to ensure a fair part of knowledge stored in the pursued location finds its way to our vaults. Until then, any information on this "Camden" might prove extremely useful.

                Yours,
                R.A."

                He doesn't expect an answer right away, possibly in two or even three nights. With this thought, and having completed all formalities regarding transportation and accomodation in Nottingham, he falls into daysleep.

                                                            Raul Abrantes

                                                            Raul places a suitcase containing his personal belongings in one of the rooms, looks over everyone present and begins to speak in a fashion that can only be compared to a coach, addressing his team just before the game starts. Only that the other vampires are hardly "his" and hardly a "team" just yet. He chose to wave that away, hoping this will not turn into a leadership contest.

                                                            - So, my fellow conspirators... it seems that now, away form prying eyes we can discuss what exactly are we planning to do in the coming nights. I, for one, am going to visit the cemetery in which we are to meet our contact and get the feel of the place, perhaps even ask the local denizens a question or two. Tomorrow night, I suggest we carouse at least a little in the Prince's court, as is customary in this domain - the last thing that we want is an overzealous Sheriff breathing down our necks because we didn't observe some obscure tradition. Additionally, we could wrangle some information from the other Cainites there and see what are the poilitics all about in this city, as dull as this may be. I get the feeling we will be staying here for a while. With that in mind, I also plan to seek out the local community of mortal occultists... however misguided, they may possess some useful information or provide assistance. If nothing else, they could serve as vessels to gains sustenance from. I am ready to take your suggestions, gentlement. Let's make these nights count.

                                                            He finishes with a smile, waiting for response from his companions.
