                            Caladriu fun fact, there's a story about a tribe of Thunderbirds that lived on Vancouver Island and laid down their masks and feather cloaks to live as humans. They stayed like that, living among the people, until one local clan forgot the true nature of the tribe that had been Thunderbirds and attacked with the intent of taking captives for slaves. It didn't end well. I thought it was a pretty cool rumour/urban legend myself, spent a lot of the last year studying folklore and religion academically, so I sort of know how these things work now. You're not wrong about the Begotten angle. Stories have a funny way of working in the CofD, and the Horrors that spawn in the Primordial Dream are as much subconscious human anxieties as independent beings. What came first, the dragon, or fear of the dragon?
