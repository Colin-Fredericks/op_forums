You're going to do what I say and get paid handsomely, or else I'm going to quietly and painfully murder you and there is nobody in three miles dangerous enough to stop me. Understand? Good. I need use of your tavern. You have three minutes to kick everyone out. Tell them the place has been reserved for a private function. Recommend some other tavern nearby."
