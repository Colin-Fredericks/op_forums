                            The eclipse caste checks over his instruments and readings one last time. He lets out a deep breath thinking, You can do this... With a lurch that makes his gut sink but spirit soar, the launch tube fires him into space along with Wood Squad. The sudden jump in speed and the opening of his perceptions as he takes to the sky is exhilarating. He forgets himself for a moment as he tests his controls and lets out a whoop and a laugh. A grizzled voidfighter pilot might just mistake such a display of freedom as a war cry from a soldier. Virtuoso settles into formation alongside Wood Leader. "Orichalcum One, standing by." He calls into the voxcaster. "Thanks for having me, gentlemen. This is your show still, I'm just here to help. Captain, call it." He takes another deep breath as they approach the enemy line. The distractions fall away as his perceptions sharpen. Time seems to slow as he scans the vultures for a likely target, judging when best to strike.
