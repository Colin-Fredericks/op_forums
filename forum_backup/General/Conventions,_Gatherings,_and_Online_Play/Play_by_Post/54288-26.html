        I will weave his heart into shoes, that I might tread upon his dreams with every step!

        The thought was there and gone in a flash, shaming Yi slightly with its vehemence. Even before the Fey were gone the still shining Zenith was over at Larissa's side. While Jenseven looked over her wound he said gently, "Are you alright?"

        She smiled faintly, "I'll be fine. I just need to lay off the heavy lifting for a day or two."

        Relieved he tried to lighten the mood, smirking a bit. "I suppose this isn't going to do much to dispel those rumors of an anathema lover."

        "You paid attention to those old things?" Larissa chuckles, "They started up right after I got elected, and have been popping up every so often since. Never were true."

        Looking around at the damages Yi's grin became rueful. "I... ah... I'll fix the damages." Under his breath he muttered, "Even if the place should be torn down and rebuilt from scratch."

        Larissa shakes her head slightly, "I don't blame you for the damage, and I don't think the Spyroi will either."

        "Still, I feel bad. For all of this. I know I am spending your father's money and have promised to pay him back double, but I want to make this up to you and Spyroi."

        He pinched the bridge of his nose, "Which would be much easier to contemplate if I were not living off your father's money, twice that in debt to him, and overspent on time even more then funds."

He can sense the behemoth's animalistic mind nacient in the weapon. It would likely be a good idea to try to befriend or attune to the behemoth before trying to wield it.

                                        A large unit of men with, oddly enough, the Ptreas House Mon, suddenly enters. They are led by a flaxen-haired young woman, who is wearing the robes common in the nobility bound around her waist like a skirt in conjunction with a light jade breastplate. A Dire Lance sits in one of her hands. Looking about, she orders her troops to sweep the room for Fae, and orders one of her officers out to begin re-organizing the workers and to pass on to the Lord Strategos that the situation appears to be under control.

                                        She then turns to a silver-haired, blue-robed man and says, "You've been of great assistance, sorcerer. The thanks of myself and my house to you and yours."
