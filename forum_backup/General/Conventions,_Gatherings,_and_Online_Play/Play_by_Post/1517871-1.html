                    I am fine with a player starting a scene of their own initiative if there is a clear story motivation, and I will respond to whatever you come up with. But if you would prefer for me to set up a scene and then have your character come in, that is fine too. Just let me know.

                    The purpose of the OOC thread is to facilitate communication between players and storyteller that would normally occur around the table in a in-person game. The IC thread is for things that your character experiences within their own perspective. (Again. I think Cauthon, Temple, and Kaiser did a great job of this in the "In the Hallowed Light" chronicle threads).

                    Example:

                    OCC: I want my character to research that book in the library

                    IC: At around midnight, [Character Name] rubs their eyes. Will I ever find this clue? They wondered.

                    Also, remember that postings might not be instantaneous... because of Real Life. (I need that reminder too!)
