    Alembics: Eros (0), Assimilate (0), Stalker (0), Eris (0)

    Adonis waits a few seconds for Erwin to leave, and then follows. His minds swirls at the events that have just transpired, and he senses that he has achieved a Milestone he wasn't even searching for.

    Up until now, his understanding of human interactions has been through the lens of domination and submission: one party party takes control over another. Done poorly, the dominant party seizes control from the submissive party by force, without consent; that was how Sharon did things. Done well, the submissive party offers up control willingly, and the dominant party accepts it as an offering; that was how Lily did things. This is neither. Erwin is clearly smitten with him- Divine Fire fire within him made sure of that- but he didn't really surrender himself to Adonis, or demand that Adonis surrender to him. This was a compromise. Erwin had requested they see each other again, but he had made no demand of time or place. It was up to him to accept or decline. Erwin had extended his hand, and Adonis was free to accept it or not. Accepting it meant they could build something together. Refusing it meant whatever they could have ended tonight. Was this what being a Companion meant? Meeting somebody halfway, not fully giving or taking control, but sharing it? I need to see him again. I need to learn more...

    He walks out of the cafe and sees the commotion. People are running in the direction of the van. He looks around, trying to get a read on what has happened, though part of him already suspects. What the hell did they do, and where are they?

    Adonis tries to get a handle on what happened based on the reactions of the crowd. Wits 2 + Composure 2 = 4 dice -> 1 success.

    He's also going to try and sense where Mark 5 and Earth are, assuming they are close enough that their Azothic Radiance would be detectable. Azoth 2 = 2 dice -> 1 success.

            Adonis

            The Muse can see cellphones raised high to film the carnage, the flashing lights of a police car, and the siren of a fire truck blares in the distance. You can see sparks periodically flying from and obliterated fuse box inside the loading bay, and smoke rising from the wreckage of the trailer. Erwin is talking to the police, looking utterly crushed. He needed that job, and now it's at risk. Adonis can pick up snippets of conversation "I heard there was a power surge" "Some kind of medical trailer, sensitive chemicals" "All went up in smoke, bad reaction to a shock" "It's a wonder the store didn't light on fire" "Heard the initial charge might have come from the building." "Saw a huge flash of light, and then two people fled the scene. One looked pretty badly burned- you don't think anyone was in there when it happened, do you?"

            The alchemist spots you in the crowd and walks over. He doesn't have anyone else to talk to, and he looks shell-shocked, cellphone in hand. "That trailer was tens of thousands of dollars of company money and resources. Hundreds of paid work hours. Years in research and altered formulas. I am so screwed. Something must have made it through that, right?" He looks pleadingly, hopefully, at his only fixed point in a storm. A drowning man snagging onto a nearby, friendly plank. Even as you process your revelation, even as you see the sudden connection on his face, the tentative, trusting outreach, and feel your internal crucible surge in response... Azoth calls. Earth and Mark 5 didn't go far.

            OOC: Adonis gets two Vitriol Beats from the Minor Milestone. I'm starting to get a better idea of how he's processing relationships, and that helps enormously. He also senses that Mark 5 and Earth are only a block and a bit away- not out of range of their Azothic Radiance (or Adonis' for that matter) by a large margin.

            Earth and Mark 5

            The Fury struggles to contain her own anguish, riding out the wave of Torment within her, trying not to do what comes naturally and vent her rage at everything within striking distance of her divine lightning. The Alembic is still blazing with Pyros- she only needs to reach out and touch it, and she can vent her pain, vent her rage, let the Divine Fire blaze until it smoulders its way back down to a more manageable intensity. She's abandoned Mark 5 after nearly killing him in an overzealous attempt to destroy living reminders of her own monstrous past. She's standing on the precipice of an abyss, but she doesn't believe anything lies back from the edge. There is only the cliff, and the abyss.

            Earth sees a small flock of butterflies near the edge of the building, and she sees a familiar scene. She sees the wreckage of her wrath, gazing down from above, and she sees a frightened horde of humanity surrounding it. She can hear the wailing of people and sirens, and knows she's done this tothem. But there's something different about this than about the paper mill. Perhaps it's the glimmer of Azoth she can see down below, the warm flame of Adonis, as he speaks, comfortable, intimate, with an alchemist. But in this moment- she feels something that manages to pierce her even through the haze of her Torment. She needs only to recognize it for what it is.

            OOC: Earth regains all Willpower, and must roll Resolve + Composure - Azoth to avoid gaining the Tormented Condition. However, she's also experiencing a Minor Milestone. This whole situation reminds her of the paper mill, but there's something different about it. What Earth takes away from this is likely going to be very different depending on whether she succumbs to Torment or not, and how she chooses to interpret the scene.

            The Titan, meanwhile, finds himself a streetlight. It isn't much, but there's enough electricity coursing through it that he needn't risk blowing it out before it can heal him. He stretches out a ruined hand towards the smooth, green-painted metal of its side, and electricity coils up his arm, invigorating his flesh and causing his Azoth to surge in sympathy. He can already feel himself healing, but he also can't shake his resolution that Earth must be helped along her Pilgrimage. He is a Soldier- and Soldiers have missions, don't they? But what is his mission regarding Earth? And who is giving it to him? He pauses for a moment, twinging as the healing power of electricity channeling Pyros meets the Torment-laden lightning Earth had struck him with.

            OOC: So, as it so happens your average streetlamp pumps out enough electricity to be comparable to an electric fence, so it's releasing 6 Bashing Damage per turn. You have three turns before it risks shorting out. This turn, he heals a point of Lethal Damage and a point of Aggravated Damage. This saves a point of Bashing Damage that will roll over next turn. He suffers another point of Lethal Damage, leaving him with a total of Two Aggravated Damage and one Lethal. He also gains a point of Pyros. Mark 5 is also experiencing a Minor Milestone.

                "Who you are is who you build, step by step, through your actions and decisions."
                Earth has truly advanced on her way to Good.
                And now, it was clear why the Angel had sent him to this city: Earth must proceed on the Pilgrimage. And others must learn of her Progress.
                He decided to accept the mission.
                He will see to it, will make sure she does not stray. She may have to face her past, but she will not have to do so alone.
                Redeeming a Villain.
                A daunting task, truly.
                Worthy of a Titan. Truly.

                OOC: To Mark 5's rather simple black-and-white-view, the ever-grey Earth is straight-up white now. His Martyr-insight had been how your choices to take a certain action (or not) are what builds up into your character. Her actions speak a clear language to him: vanquished Evil and healed a friend ('abandonment' rationalized through thinking she's out of juice and 'one streetlight per person').
                Which means, obviously, that helping her turn firmly to the Light Side, so to speak, is his Angel-sent mission. He, on the other hand, decides to answer the divine call. The Principle can hand out tasks, but it is up to the Created to chose to do them. He is no one's minion, after all.
                Earth is also sort of a Living MacGuffin to him, now. As in, her progression on the Pilgrimage is more than just "for her own sake", it also speaks a very clear language to other Prometheans: You might fuck up badly, but do not give up; there is still hope for you! Even the infamous Centimanus Earth-Dies-Screaming managed to come back and progress, after all. This is not about her, society, or Mark 5, this is about Prometheans all over the world!
                Kind of like taking a vital target in war, this will boost the troop's morale. Through this, he will enable others to fight against the frustration and desperation so common on the Pilgrimage.
