    Well, that was terrible. This had been such a huge mistake. He had been tricked into giving in, and he thought he would be smart enough to trick the Keeper...there was no way for him to do that. Who was he kidding? I'm gonna be forced into eternal servitude...
    He stood in awe when the palace was created. This was amazingly beautiful, no one could deny it, not even Nate. A shame that it was populated by such scary monsters. The Goat turned around to face each of the Zodiac with a panicked look on his face. Why were they here? No, they would judge him! They would judge him for his weakness! He tried to avoid looking at Jessica : he had ridiculed her before, and now he stood there, devastated, about to betray his humanity. And Max... He tried to meet his gaze, but he didn't have the courage, or the strength to speak. He just hoped that his eyes alone would express his thoughts.
    I'm sorry for failing you.

    The Goat breathed deeply, eyes closed, before stepping down from his platform, solemn as a prisoner facing execution. "So..." He breathed again, his mouth furred with fear. At least, try to show some fight. Don't give up now, so easily! "Incomparable and Gracious Lord Collector, Keeper and Master." He bowed, thinking that at least, forcing himself to be polite couldn't hurt. In fact, it could even ease the being. "As I asked earlier... what does this pact entail?"

    "And for my next trick" I said, "anvils!"

                                01-10-2015, 05:03 PM
                                With a few grunts of confusion, Max brought a dirt-crusted hand to his head. Blinking, he looked around him, and at himself. He was relieved to be dressed again, for the first time in what seemed a great while. They were... exotic, and likely looked out of place on a mud-splattered pig, but comfortable; they seemed meant to fit one of his shape. As did the costumes of the other elev-- ten zodiac animals in the room. And what a room it was. The universe had apparently changed venues from his being apprehended in a forest to standing in a grand, palatial court of law. 'Oh. Well...'The Pig found his heart briefly lifted on noting he didn't seem to be the defendant, but dipped on spotting the Goat who was. 'Nate... What happened?' He tried to give his friend an apologetic smile, but the... well-shorn fellow tried and failed to meet his gaze. It puzzled Max, but left his confidence ebbing low. He then gave them up entirely, with a little squealing gasp, when he saw the "judge." Any court over which their Keeper presided could hand down no positive verdict.

                                '... Or maybe it can...' Unsure as he was about what to say or do, the Pig said and did nothing, simply trying to get his bearings together. And he got the impression that the real power in this room was not the "Sublime and Magnificent Collector," but the small and unflappable figure at his feet. Or at least the endless document. It was clear there were rules here, and the Keeper either couldn't or wouldn't likely go against them. 'Okay, this place is still crazy. And I don't think the rules'll be fair. But at least he can't just change them because he wants to.' Max had felt moments of hope since he'd been in Arcadia, of things being okay or getting better, or even of their getting away. However, this was the first time part of him wondered if it might just be possible to win.

                                The Pig's ears flopped a bit at a phrase the Secretary had twice used. ' "Owner and owned..." That's interesting...' He filed it away, in case it mattered. For the moment, Max couldn't think of anything that might improve the situation, so he simply wracked his brain, mouth shut and ears open. Snout open, too, evidently, as he sniffed at the air. The Court had a certain austere grandeur that even seeped into its smell, and of course each Zodiac animal had their own natural musks to them, but there was an unexpected sweet -- and familiar -- scent, too. Very, very close. Max looked down and saw, next to the spot his robes met the ground, the apples he'd meant to bring with him. The blue fruits seemed to blend in with his robe and had thus gone unnoticed. '... Well, I guess that's something else going for me. Maybe...' The Pig returned his attention to the trial, hopeful something would hit him. He wanted to know where things were going. Particularly now that Nate had brought the Western Zodiac into things.
