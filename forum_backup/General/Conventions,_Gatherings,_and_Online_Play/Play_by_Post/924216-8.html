                                                Originally posted by wtaylorjr2001 View Post
                                                Slight mistake of omission. I have 3 potency so it will be Charmed, Steadfast and one nine again roll(mundane)
                                                No, you were right the first time - you have Potency 4.

                                                Potency is the primary spell factor. Upon successfully casting the spell, you may increase the Potency by 1 stage for each dot in the associated Arcanum, minus 1. The spell starts with Potency 1 by default, so basically you get a maximum free Potency equal to your dots in Fate (3 in this case).

                                                Then, you scored an exceptional success because you used your Praxis and scored at least 3 successes. With an exceptional success on the casting roll, you can pick one bonus from a list on p. 115 (also listed on p. 328). You chose the option to gain a free increase in the spell's primary spell factor, so that brings you up to Potency 4.

                                                Exceptional Luck states that you can mix and match boons equal to the spell's Potency, so 4. You picked Charmed, Steadfast, and 2 uses of 9-again rolls on mundane actions.

                                                I know these rules are complicated as we're learning them, but I'm pretty sure you'll be using this spell a lot so I'm sure we'll figure it out with practice.


                                                Now, in regards to the spell you're trying to cast on the group. I think you're forgetting the rules on spell control, and on the scale factor.

                                                If you want to give each member of the cabal a blessing of Exceptional Luck, you can either cast it on each mage individually, or you can cast it one time and include everyone as a target. If you cast them individually (like you did in your post), then you need to refer to the rules on spell control on p. 118:

                                                Originally posted by p. 118
                                                A mage can have as many active spells as she has dots in Gnosis. Each additional spell requires a Reach, plus another Reach per spell already over the limit.
                                                You already have Exceptional Luck cast on yourself, so if you try to hold onto additional spells for each cabalmate, that last one is going to require a Reach because you'll be over your spell control limit (3, for your Gnosis 3), and you'll have to spend 2+ reach for every spell after that one for the rest of the duration of those spells. I don't recommend this.

                                                The better approach is to either take -4 and increase the standard scale factor to include 4 subjects, or to use 1 Reach for the Advanced Scale factor (which affects up to 5 subjects simultaneously). As discussed above, the spell will have Potency 3 by default unless you increase the Potency factor on the spell.

                                                If you wanted, you could release the Exceptional Luck spell that you currently have active on yourself, and just include yourself and the other cabalmates in the same spell. That way you each get the boon, but it only takes 1 active spell. It's your call.

                                                Does that make sense?
