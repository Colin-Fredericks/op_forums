            ~~ON THE BRIDGE~~

            "Oh, you'll get used to him," that same female voice called out of the overhead hatch again. After a moment, and some clanging, a head and shoulders emerge from the open hatch. With her flawless skin of pale grey with starmetal highlights, she is clearly an alchemical. Her princess cut sapphire soulgem shone in the center of her forehead, and though her eyes were covered by a shimmering crystal essence visor, her face was still smiling brightly as she hung upside-down. Bright silver hair, long enough to just barely brush her shoulders if she were upright, fell straight down instead, though some of it was held in place with an adamant hairband with a beautifully carved flower on one side, above her right ear. A smudge of purple oil marred her left cheek, and the light blue jumpsuit she seemed to be wearing also appeared to bear some recent purple stains, though it is hard to tell with only the shoulders showing. "He's just grumpy because we're still in spacedock. He hates being stuck in one place, but there's just too much work to do. A lot of specialized charms needed to be installed... well, invented, then built, then installed, for this mission. And, to be frank, they're playing havoc with his systems. For example, apparently the Spiral-Scanning Infinite Argus Array breaks down when exposed to the field from the Paramagnetic Ramjet Apparatus. And it turns out that Glorious Inertial Dampening Compensator interferes with our new Void-Spanning Ansible Transmitter. Or, at least it did. Fixed now. Here, Hoshi, can you have someone take that down to recycling for me?" She casually drops a large piece of shattered crystal through the open hatch down to the communications officer. "Thanks," she says, turning her attention back to Amelia. "The truth is, we really need another week in spacedock at least to prepare. Maybe two. Why the Deliberative is insisting we get underway so quickly, I can't understand, but I got told rather firmly that it's not my place to ask." Her smile dissolved into a grimace of dissatisfaction for a moment before returning.

            "But, I suppose that's neither here nor there. I'm Righteous Common Enterprise. I'm Chief Engineer of this ship, and also the Primary Architect of the expedition. It's a pleasure to meet you. Oh, and sorry I called you a hard ass, but you do have a reputation." She says all this with a confident smile, still hanging upside down through a hatch in the ceiling.
