                        Hm. A doctor? I thought being an actress was your primary thing. Medicine 5 and a Intelligence 5 and specialties makes the Maiden one of the foremost mortal physicians on the planet! How in the world did this amnesiac islander have time to go to Creation's version of medical school while traveling and spending her nights on stage and learning not one but two unfamiliar languages?

                        I'm okay with Intelligence 5, if you are up for playing a character like that, but since it's a maxed stat and you have a couple of 1s in your attributes, I'd like to hear how your character's personality is shaped by these attributes. You're an actress with only Manipulation 1? You're a weakling with Stamina 3? Lots of highs and lows here. Lows can make for fun story stuff, but I want to see what it is you're going for, here.

                        In terms of Medicine, I'm going to rule that "aided by equipment" is not a valid specialty. You might be able to diagnose without it, but you need equipment to treat a sick person in the first place: tourniquets, bandages, medicinal herbs, leeches, a brazier of incense to drive off the flu-spirits, whatever. This specialty would apply every time you treat a patient, it's too broad. Try narrowing down the types of equipment, maybe?

                        I have to admit, I was sorta hoping for a social character here. We already have a couple of doctors and healers and shamans with medicinal training, I'm not sure going full-bore healer like this is going to help you stand out...what sort of story are you interested in telling with those medicine dots? Do you want your mentor /love interest to get sick so you can nurse him back to health? Do you want to deal with a plague outbreak? Did you take it because you want to use a Medicine to in investigate your father's death?
