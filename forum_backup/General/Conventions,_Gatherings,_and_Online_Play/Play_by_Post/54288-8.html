    "Wait!" Orchid shouts as she tackles Hyuga. "I don't know how exactly my presence here keeps us safe, but I've dealt with Fae before. I there's something that they hate more than people screwing around with their hospitality, I haven't seen it."

    (Since discussion seems to be... not happening, I'm just going to move on)

    Ba Rai collects you next morning, and takes you back to the Hall. Esstrayan is present, bedecked in delicate armor of all the colors of the rainbow (and many that aren't). He's speaking with a tall, broad man with rough features, looking very much like a caricature of a Shogunate Taimyo. A large surface floats several feet in the air. As you approach you see that it is a scale model of Novalesk and the surrounding area, complete with tiny cities, towns, and a flashing gold tower, which you presume represents the freehold.

    "Ah, my guests!" Esstrayan says with a smile, "Come! Tao Mei" he gestures towards the tall man, "has come up with a battle strategy."

    Tao Mei nods in greeting before launching into his explanation, his voice deep and resonant, "Honored guests. Here we have the nation of Novalesk, the current target of the Puissant Amethyst Court. The enemy is currently clustered here-" a phantom army of tiny barbarians and Raksha appears on the map "-on the Northeastern bank of the Granite river. The mortal forces have so far kept them from reaching the other side en masse. The Amethysts will have either begun infiltrating their Fae warriors across the river to strike from behind, or will soon. Our objective for the time being is to keep the mortals from being overrun."

    "The mortals have proven that they are capable enough to deal with their ilk among the enemy's ranks. It is up to us to do the same. However, supply lines are crucial. The enemy kin have large groups of mortals on which to feed to maintain their Essence, while we have but this Freehold. My proposal is thus," Tao Mei pauses for a moment while a pair of glowing blue dots appear between the freehold and the enemy army, "these are the locations of two uncapped demesnes. Ba Rai and I can convert them into Freeholds in a very short time. Liu Fan will link their Ways here, giving us a defense in depth in addition to increasing our Essence resources. I will remain there while Lui Fan and Ba Rai begin our counter offensive. Ba Rai will patrol the west bank of the river, keeping any of our kin from slipping past the mortals, while Lui Fan strikes from behind."

    As Tao Mei speaks, you see three phantasmal knights leave the gold tower, and place miniature towers on the blue dots. One of them stays behind as the other two depart; one to marches up and down the western river bank, the other circles around to the east of the enemy army.

    "Lui Fan's mission will be to strike down the enemy's mortals, thereby depriving the enemy of their primary essence source. She will only strike enemy kin if they are alone and victory is certain."

    "Aw, that won't be any fun," a voice from the ceiling says. You look up to see a young girl, of perhaps ten years in age, with a moonsilver eyes and an orichalcum grin standing on the ceiling as if it were the floor. Tao Mei stares daggers at her. Literally.

    "The objective is not to have fun, Lui Fan," Tao Mei says. He turns to the Circle, "This is not a war that we can win alone. Our objectives are to harass and contain the enemy, reducing their Essence resources and buying time for the mortals to regroup and recover. Unless the enemy redeploys their forces to assault us here, we can contain them for perhaps a month or two. If they do redeploy, we can hold them here forever," a corner of Tao Mei's mouth moves upward for a split second, "but I doubt the Shah will be that stupid. You will have to ensure that your mortal forces get reinforced and resupplied in that time."

    Esstrayan gives you one of his toothy grins, "Well, what do you think?"

                                    Sea nods, then quickly goes over the plans again. "So, Lui Fan and Ba Rai, then. Right;" She focuses a brief moment, and a soft azure glow washes over the room. Then she begins to sing. It is a low, but dark tune, a wordless humming with ominous accords, a promise of the drums of wars and of Saturn's shears. It wraps slowly around the two warriors, like an uncomfortable cloak wrapping around their shoulders and whispering deathly promises in their ears.

"On the moment when the enemy first sees you, they will be struck with terror. I cannot promise that it will be enough to send them in a rout, but it should at least shake them."

Both are Charisma+Presence with the First Excellency, Gem of Courtesan's Calculations, and Fateful Excellency, as well as channeling Compassion, for a total of 19 successes + stunts. Total cost is 22m, 3wp, 2 Compassion channels. This is going to take quite some time to recover, so let's hope it's worth it.

                                        Orchid says, "I'm not sure. I'm not part of the big merchant families or any of the banks. Me being in the guild would certainly quicken the process... I'm going to have to talk with my contacts. But since I'm going to head downriver once this is done anyway, I'll look into it."

                                        Ba Rai nods in thanks, and Lui Fan just stands there on the ceiling, grinning. They look to Esstrayan, who nods and grins, waving them from the scene. The Fae sweep out of the room, and Esstrayan says, "You had best prepare to leave. Ba Rai shall see to your transportation."

                                        With that Esstrayan sweeps out himself. A frog-like fae hobbles over, and leads you to Orchid's wagon, Ba Rai standing nearby. You see a large crystalline tower fly out of the Freehold above the walls, while a mass of Homonculi and Goblins march through them. You think you can make out Tao Mei atop the tower, while Lui Fan appears to be in the midst of the army, atop a gold-and-silver horse. Ba Rai draws one of her blades, and flips it about and places its hilt to her lips, playing it as a flute. She plays a short melody before throwing it to the ground, shouting, "Arise, Dragokaiser!"

                                        The blade morphs and expands, growing in a manner of seconds into a monstrous many legged draconian form. Ba Rai mounts the beast, and motions you to do the same. Once you are mounted, Dragokaiser beats its many wings, lifting you up into the air. It maneuvers over the wagon and, after grabbing the wagon (and Orchid's distressed horses), it zooms off. You see familiar countryside flash below you, and in a bare hour, Ba Rai sets a mile or so outside of Grey Ice. She raises a sword in salute before flying off the way you came.

                                        Orchid rides you into town, telling you that she's going to head south towards Novalesk, and then head down towards Gunzota, where she has some contacts. She asks if you want to come with her to the capital city, or if you have business to attend to here first. A significant portion of the leadership of Gens Ptreas is here, after all.
