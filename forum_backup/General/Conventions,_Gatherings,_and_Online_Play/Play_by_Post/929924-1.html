        Moonstone and Binder
        As soon as you open your true Eyes, the world changes in front of you.

        You can see very clearly now that the storm is clearly made of pure magical energy. Through the lens of your Path, you can sense the minds of about 10-15 people in the train car and know instinctually that all of them are wide awake. But it's not your Mind sense that shows the most about this weather. As you look out at the Highstorm, for a brief moment, you think you see out of the corner of your eye something flash down on the street below. If you didn't know any better, it looked like a door made of the same shadowy substance as the hail falling against the train. Whatever it is, you sense a spatial warp around it.

        You can each roll Int+Occult


        Phoenix
        The world seems dull by comparison as you let the Fallen World override your vision once more. Even without your Mage Sight, however, you can definitely see that the train is slowing down.

        Moonstone, Binder, and Phoenix
        You hear a male voice make an announcement on the overhead speaker system, but it's cutting in and out. "Ladi-- ----men, we're hav-- some technic-- ---culties. Ple--- -ain calm and -------."

        Then the speaker cuts out. The lights flicker, and then shut off entirely, flooding the train with darkness. After a shudder and a squeal, the train screeches to a halt, thankfully just as it pulls up to an exit platform. You notice that the lights are flickering or gone entirely on the platform, too.

        You are currently under the effects of the Poor Light environment tilt. All visual-based Perception rolls are currently at -2.


        Ragnar
        Your newly activated Mage Sight reveals waves of magic emanating from the storm hanging over the city, and from the hail clanging against the wall and windows. Through the lens of your Path, you don't perceive the storm or hail to have any direct impact on the threads of destiny or temporal warps. Glancing around the room, nothing along those lines jumps out to you, either.

        What you do see, however, is a shadow around one of the male undergrad students temporarily flash into the shape of some cat-like creature with long pointed ears. The man's nature appears, for just a moment, to be part animal, and you feel an echo of primary energy surge through you for just a moment. You can instinctually tell that he just cast a spell from the Life Arcanum.

        Dr. Asphodel
        It's probably comforting to know that as you study the room with your Mage Sight, your Death sense indicates that everyone in the room does, in fact, have a soul and is currently alive. By studying the storm, you see that the clouds are filled with energy, but beyond that you're not sure what it might be. You are sure, though, that the hail is not causing any significant structural damage to the building itself.

        As your gaze passes over Oscar, you watch him use Awakened magic from the Life Arcanum. You temporarily feel a surge of primal energy pass through your body, and his soul flashes brightly for just a moment, but otherwise you notice no harmful effects to yourself.

        Ragnar, Dr. Asphodel, and Lynx
        The lights dim, flicker again, and then shut off. Lynx can hear the air conditioning shut off with his heightened senses. Several students scream or shout in surprise, and more than a few jump to their feet and try to walk towards the door. "I think we can go over the syllabus on Thursday," someone yells out sarcastically.

        You are currently under the effects of the Poor Light environment tilt. All visual-based Perception rolls are currently at -2.
