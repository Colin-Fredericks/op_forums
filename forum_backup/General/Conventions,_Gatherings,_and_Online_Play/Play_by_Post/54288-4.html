    "Listen to me," she says with that odd not-quite-staring expression, thrusting the fan at his chest to weigh on her words. "Three of your friends are dead. One is broken. You are wounded. You are going to walk home alone, to tell of your abject failure in attacking a bunch of random, unarmed strangers. Maidens' sake, one of them was blind! And then you talked to these strangers, told them things you shouldn't have. You will live in shame, and then you will die miserable in some random battle or at the displeasure of your mistress. I can offer you something different. Tell us where Shah is, and anything else we find useful, and you can abandon your ways, go to the city, live a life of safety and health. I will give you names which will allow this. Which will make people care for you. You have a chance at something different. Seize it now. Or die a nobody."

    Charisma+Presence roll, enhanced with the First and Fateful Excellencies (thanks to Heart-Brightening Presentation Style), and the Gem of the Courtesan's Calculations. 7m, 1wp for an Auspicious success: 14 sux, plus one per stunt die (please tell me what stunt this is worth; I can tell for combat stunt but social stunts I have more trouble).

                                    Orshid glances at Hyuga, making his shovel, and pulls a spade out of her wagon, chuckling to herself. It takes some time, but you are able to dig graves for the barbarians while Orchid moves camp to a smaller clearing to the north.

                                    The next morning, after Orchid makes a small votive offering to Mercury, you set out towards the north. Your journey is... relatively uneventful. Nothing of particular note happens that day, though you notice that Orchid is definitely using Ride charms to increase your movement speed. In the late afternoon of the next day, you come upon a strange ... city of impossible geometries. From the outside the city looks to have a perfectly circular wall, roughly a half mile in diameter. Looking above it, there are way too many towers and spires in there to actually fit. Plus, the trench surrounding the city looks to be infinite in depth, as there's no sign that it has a bottom. When you attempt to cross the sole bridge, a bolt of lightning cracks down from the cloudless skies. A figure, bedecked in glimmering armor, a plethora of strange weapons, and a floppy red hat appears before you. They unsheathe one of their blades and shouts, "What business have you in the dockyard, erg, desk, erg, Domain of Esstrayan, kevin?"

                                                    Sea scowls at Hyuga. What kind of silly joke is that? She shakes her head disapprovingly, and steps forward, bowing deeply.

                                                    "Lady Esstrayan. As you can see, the incidents of life have seen fit to deprive me of the ability to fully enjoy the wondrous sight of a city such as yours; nonetheless I hear its song, and I feel its pavement under my feet, and I smell its life; and I dare say, in front of this city Heaven itself would pale."

                                                    It's a lie and they both know it, but the point is not deceit, the point is to be a polite host, and offer superlative flattering, as one is expected to do.

                                                    "The Story which brought us here is one of trepidation and hope. We have come on behalf of Novalesk, to extend the hand of friendship. Long have the Beautiful Ones and mortals known tensions between each other, and we want to resolve them once and for all; hard times are ahead of us all, and in unity we would find strength. For these are dark times, and darkening still; who knows what lies behind the corners of tomorrow? My lady, I am Sea of Tears, and this is Hyuga, and this is Tirien, and this is Orchid. We claim no title, save that of peaceful envoys."

                                                            Esstrayan laughs, "Your friend, he makes a wonderful joke!" and then makes a polite nod to each member of the Circle as they are introduced.

                                                            In response to Sea, Lady Esstrayan says, "Oh, I'm certain the splendors of the Celestial City make my humble home seem but a hovel by comparison. Though never before have I been host to such a fine creature. It is as if the Maiden of Peace walked through my gates herself."

                                                            As you say, it's a lie and everyone knows it, but Esstrayan is clearly enjoying the game.

                                                            "I know well of the troubles outside our borders. This nation of Novalesk wars with my cousins, who seek a return to the Crusade of dear old Balor. They seek to draw me into it, not remembering that I was here when the sun-children ruled these lands," Esstrayan flashes with anger for a moment, waving in the path the departed Tiki went, "they threatened me with war lest I join them. They bribe, and whine and beg and threaten. Bah."

                                                            Esstrayan calms down a bit, and says, "as much as I loathe them, they offer something I do desire. An end to boredom. Ancient pacts with the sun-children bind me here. The have no weight anymore, but what I allow them to have. But my boredom is not sufficient cause to change my Story, no? But if I were answering the call of my troubled kin. That would be cause sufficient."

                                                            "So, I help them," Esstrayan continues with a flit of his hand, "I do something I do not wish to, but I get something I want. If I sit idle, I do not do something I dislike, but I get nothing. The best course of action is to get something you want by doing something you like, yes?"

                                                            The subtext is clear. You want Esstrayan to help, you're going to have to give him a reason to do so.
