    Dr. Asphodel
    As your mundane vision dims to the world around you, your Sight opens -- and your breath catches in your throat at the sight before you.

    This place -- this world? -- is suffused with Death.

    The water behind you, the hallway ahead of you, even the very walls of this place are filled with a residual element of Death magic. Your Periphery had detected a faint magical aura in this place, but now your Death Sight shows you what you were missing. Vague, amorphous ephemeral shapes are everywhere here. There are some frozen in place on the walls, some lining the hallway...even some in the pool behind you.

    You've seen at least one ghost since your Awakening, but haven't spoken to or interacted with it. But these beings you see don't quite resemble ghosts. They resemble something almost like a shadow of a ghost, if such a thing could exist -- a memory of a ghost. Between the humid air and the shadows, you feel a distinct sense that you are walking in a large mausoleum. This is sacred ground.

    Your Matter Sight reveals additional information that, while less startling, is equally unnerving. You can tell that most of the cavern is a natural formation. The archway over the walls, however, and the ground on the hallway leading away from the pool, appear made of a material that you have never seen. While it looks like stone to the naked eye, you find yourself unable to quantify its strength or quality; dark swirls of shadowy energy flow in the stone-like ground. It does not appear harmful, but is rather a reflection of this place's quality.

    All spell casting rolls for Death spells may use an environment Yantra at +2 and do not risk Paradox, regardless of how many reaches you use.


    Lynx
    You take a deep breath in through your Sight. You can tell from a single whiff that regardless of what else has happened, there are no changes in yours or the others' odors of destiny -- being here has not caused a dramatic change in your future. Otherwise, the senses you are learning to associate with Fate magic feel no different than when you were standing in the classroom.

    Your scent and hearing of Life magic, normally your most potent supernatural senses, feel unusually muted here. You detect no other living beings within your immediate surroundings, save for some mold and mushrooms growing on the rocks around the pool. Interestingly, those mushrooms feel like they have some kind of energy stored within them. Upon closer study, you can tell that they have no toxins or poisons, marking them as safely edible.

    Your Spirit Sight does not reveal any spirits in Twilight, but you do see faint, vague shapes of energy frozen in place all around the walls and in the pool. You can't tell what they are, but they must be at least tangentially related to spirits, or your Sight wouldn't detect them. To your Spirit Sight, the energy in this place smells faintly of incense and sweet spices, and you think you hear a very quiet and low-pitched hum.
