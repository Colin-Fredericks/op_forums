                                                Concept: Lawyer who rebuilt his life from the ground up after hitting rock bottom. Tests the limits of his own will and that of others. Came to the Denver area relativly recently and is still getting his bearings.

                                                Shadow Name: Marduk
                                                A god king who subjugated the Annunaki to gain his rightful station. Stephen chose this shadow name as it to him resonated with the Clavicularian legend of King Solomon, the Annunaki being the demons both within and without the Mageking has to conquer to rise to his rightful station.

                                                History: Born as the single child of a happy and nice middle class family living in the chicago suburbs, Stephen was brought up in a sheltered environment. When he hit puberty his life went from nice to boring and lame. Stephen lived it up with alcohol, parties and the occassional joint. By the time he went to UC to study law he had graduated to harsher drugs. For a few years he walked the edge between coke addiction and a somewhat functional life, but eventually he slipped. Half a year later he lay spasming in a ditch, his body succumbing to an overdose. In his last moments, as his consciouness slipped his many demons came alive, tormenting him with his failures. Harrassed by addictions tearing at his flesh, with the laughter of false friends roaring in his ears and the void of never found love devouring his heart Stephen cried out in pain – and defiance. Confronted with all his failures he saw the potential for what could have been and decided to fight for a second chance. With a strength beyond the physical, with strength borne of will he fought off his demons and carved into their flesh his will to live and to rise beyond his own pettyness. After the last demon retreated, defeated but not conquered, a surge of supernal energy coursed through his body and he awoke leaning against a dumpster
                                                Over the next few days he allmost went insane as Pandemonium bombarded him with his own failings and those of others. He may have turned bitter and went down a left handed path, hadn't a warlock and thearch by the name of Nemesis found him. Nemesis helped Stephen stabilize and cement the discipline he had fought so hard to attain. While Stephen doubled down and finished his degree (to show himself he could), she taught him in the ways of the wise and helped him reforge himself into Marduk. She showed him how to use his gifts to help others earn their own second chances, as he had and initiated him into the ranks of the Silver Ladder. When he showed and interest in Goetia and continued to test his self-discipline Nemesis smiled and unveiled the mysteries of the Clavicularii to him.
                                                Shortly after his initiation into the legacy a scandal rocked the Chicago consilium, as evidence of a Scelestus hiding among the mages of the pentacle surfaced. A Lictor by the name of Hunter was called in to sort out the mess and by the end of the whole ordeal Marduk had impressed Hunter with his methodical analysis of the evidence and social networks within the consilium. As he intended to retire, Hunter offered to train Marduk as a Lictor himself. Uninterested in politicing for a position of power within Ladder and consilum at the time, Marduk gladly accepted. Hunter, a Thyrsus, taught him many ways to approach his new duties that a Mastigos would never have considered and after castigating his own pride, Marduk gladly absorbed every bit of wisdom he could. He had a feeling he might need it. His education complete he has been assigned to the Denver area as the local Deacon wanted a Lictor who would both be wise in the ways of the Shadow and awakened politics for the job. Marduk is afraid he may not be up to the task. Of course such fear is but another battle for enlightenment for a Key-Bearer.

                                                Important NPC:
                                                Nemesis: His original mentor, a respected thearch from Chicago. She helped him bring his life back in order and initiated him into the Arts, Order and her Legacy. To him, she's practically family, perhaps more so than his actual family. During their time together they felt a mutual attraction towards one another, but neither of them choose to act on it. One of the reasons Marduk decided to leave Chicago behind was to prove to himself that he could be self sufficient without his mentor shadow looming over him. Nemesis' personal demons mostly center around feelings of inadequacy, envy and strong desire for vengeance.
                                                Hunter: A former veteran lictor who tired of his duties and trained Marduk as his apprentice. The two of them had their differences, especially when it came to their approach of the job, Hunter preferring direct actions, while Marduk advocates careful observation and deduction. In time they both developed a bond of mutual respect coupled with friendly rivalry. Not wanting to settle down quite yet, Hunter is for now travelling all over the US, visiting friends in various Consilii.
                                                Dirty Secret: A spirit of transgression, allways taking a form that violates societal norms in some fashion.In front of Marduk usually takes the form of a short time college girlfriend that ended up overdosing. It delights in learning the uncomfortable secrets of others and is willing to trade what it knows in exchange. As it only really cares for secrets people are actually ashamed off, it knows suprisingly little about things like organized crime, hufman trafficing or the like, but instead has been known to haunt the halls of politics, the bedrooms of celebrities and the sancti of mages. It has a talent for getting out of sticky situations and is a master in the art of blackmail, often using it's numina to take human form and leverage sleepers into committing more shameful acts, digging themselves deeper and deeper into a deplorable swamp of dirty secrets.
                                                Marshal and Laura Jones: His parents. Boringly average and steadfastly asleep. At first Stephen tried to reconnect with them after his Awakening, but Marduk quickyl saw through the wiles of the demon of family affection steering his mind and castigated those irrational feelings from his mind. The sad truth is that they have nothing to offer to him and he merely keeps up appearances for conviniences sake. Or perhaps because he hasn't fully subjugated his love for his family yet, he himself is not sure and that insecurity is perhaps the only thing that makes Marduk think about them at all.
                                                April Jones: His younger sister recently started college. In an ironic twist of fate she choose to move to Denver to do so rather than staying in Chicago. Her presence makes Marduk a little insecure, as he is suspicious about whether it truly was coincidence (does such a thing even exist? Coincidences are for Sleepers...) that she moved here of all places, before he himself arrived. However he also allways liked her. She is a free spirit, fighteningly unorganized but refreshing. She is now working on an Art degree and Marduk currently considers if and how best to approach her.

                                                Solomonists know their demons and those of others. Whenever someone they perceive fulfills his Virtue or Vice the mage can feel the powerful demons of the person stir. This Attainment emulates "Witness" with Reach assigned to Advanced Duration.

                                                Second: Goetic Bulwark
                                                Prerequisites: Spirit 2,Willpower 6
                                                The Clavicularii know that to defeat an overwhelming foe you first have to take away the source of their strength. Thus they learn to ward those things in the phenomenal world that give demons strength by inscribing marking them with the Solomonic seals. This Attainment replicates "Cap the Well" with Reach assigned to Advanced Duration.
                                                Optional: Mind 2
                                                Solomonists learn to discipline their own minds and tame their own demons. When tempted by his Vice a Key-Bearer may choose to wrestle the Goetic demon of his Vice for control. The player rolls Resolve + Composure and compares the result to the mages Mind dots. If he scored more successes he is overwhelmed by his Goetic demon and thus has to indulge in his vice. Doing so does not replenish a willpower point. If the mages Mind dots equal or exceed the successes of the roll the mage forces his own demon into submission. He does not indulge in the Vice but still regains a willpower point as if he had fulfilled his Vice.

                                                Prerequisites: Spirit 3, the Key-Bearer must increase the other Skill she used to meet the requirements of the Legacy to 3, or must attain two dots in a second skill from the list of possible requirements.

The powers of spiritual entities fail when faced with the Key-Bearers determination and enlightened soul. This Attainment emulates "Ephemeral Shield" with Reach assigned to Advanced Duration and to include the physical attacks of Spirits.
                                                Optional: Mind 3

                                                The benefits of this Attainment extend to Goetia and any affects that target the Solomonists mind, emulating "Mental Shield" with Reach assigned to Advanced Duration and to include the physical attacks of Goetia.


                                                Fourth: Goetic Subjugation
                                                Prerequisites: Spirit 4,Willpower 7
                                                The Clavicularius binds demons to his will. By inscribing the Solomonic seals onto an object and then ritually forcing a vanquished spirit into the item he creates a Fetish. This Attainment emulates "Craft Fetish" with Reach assigned to Advanced Duration. If the Solomonists has Mind 4, this Attainment may be used on Goetia aswell as on Spirits.
                                                Optional: Mind 4
                                                The Key-Bearer learns to call forth his inner demons by the force of his will, forcing them to attend the Solomonists call and take on physical form. This emulates "Goetic Summons" with Reach assigned to Advanced Duration and to Summoning a Goetia from the mages own Oneiros. By adding in Spirit 2, the Spirit gains the Materialized Condition for the duration of the summons. Subjugating the Goetic demon to the mages will is another matter...
