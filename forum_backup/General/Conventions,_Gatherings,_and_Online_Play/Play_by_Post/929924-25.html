There were other sorcerers, and a portal, and a teleporting mansion, and a wizard's duel!" He sighs and shakes his head, but is unable to rid himself of the imaginary conversation.

    I'll have to force it out.

    Casting Alter Mental Pattern (Mind 2)
    2 Reach available: spending for instant casting and advanced duration.

    Potency 3, Duration scene
    Gnosis 1 + Mind 3 = 4 -> 0 successes

    Trying again
    Gnosis 1 + Mind 3 - 1 (successive attempt) = 3 -> 0 successes

    Ugh. I guess magic doesn't want Marlin to lie to his wife, lol. Trying again.
    Gnosis 1 + Mind 3 - 2 (successive attempt) = 2 -> 1 success

    +3 to Subterfuge rolls for the scene.


    Once the spell is in place, he'll call up Daisy to apologize, assure her that everything is all right, and let her know he's on his way. Forgoing any complex story, his general excuse is that he started rearranging some shelves, left his phone in the other room, and "I guess I haven't fully transitioned to the fall schedule yet."

    Manipulation 2 + Subterfuge 1 + 3 (~*magic*~) = 6 -> 4 successes!
    That's probably enough to overcome any contesting roll she would make?
    +1 Beat for "hide magical activities from family." Replacing that Aspiration with "join an Order."


    Conversation over, Daisy irritated but at least satisfied that nothing strange is going on, Marlin hangs up the phone. He cancels the spell. It's not the first time he's lied to his wife; it's not even the first time he's lied about magic. But it hasn't become routine. Yet.

    ---

    Wednesday (+1 WP!)

    Taking a page from Caladriu, I'm going to be scouring for Mana since I'm out and don't have another source of it. -1 Str, +3 Mana


    When Marlin gets to the bookstore, he hangs up a sign that says "Closed for Inventory" and draws the curtains. Satisfied that it will take serious effort to peer inside, he opens a satchel full of ritual components: candles, chalk, incense, and a mirror. Under the flickering candlelight he creates a ritual space focused on the door to the upstairs section of the store.

    He visualizes the door becoming like the mirror, deflecting attention from itself. He visualizes the smoke and scent of the incense shrouding the door, shielding it from scrutiny.

    Casting Secret Door (Space 2)
    1 Reach available: spending for advanced duration
    Yantras: Mirror, incense (sacrament)

    Potency 1, Duration week
    Gnosis 1 + Space 2 + Yantras 2 = 5 -> 1 success


    After three hours of casting, the spell takes hold. Marlin wipes sweat off his face, then for good measure, hangs an "Employees Only" sign on the door.

    The ritual components are relocated upstairs, where he begins to cobble together a proper sanctum.


    At some point that evening, Marlin feels the abyssal taint finish working its way into his pattern. He checks his nimbus under mage sight, examining the taint. Disgusted, he scours it out. (+1 Lethal, +2? Arcane Beats)
