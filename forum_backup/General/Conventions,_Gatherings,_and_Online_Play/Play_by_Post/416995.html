    [IC][Exalted 2.5] The Emerald Labyrinth

    02-23-2015, 08:37 PM
    As you walk off the hustle and bustle of the airship dock, away from the friendly environs, into the new city. You enter into a cobblestone square, with a hustle and bustle of people, centered around a large fountain in the center of the space. On one end is a crowd of people gathered around a large dead carcass on a cart. Cheers and happy singing are coming from the crowd, while on the other side, there is a small market of vendors selling their wares. You can see a variety of merchants, from Guildsmen to the infamous Icecrowners peddling the pale Rimesilk they're so famous for, and many, many others.

    Near the fountain is a full-figured woman in the robes of an Immaculate monk, her Rosary of the Five Dragons, stashed in her sash like a sword, and more unusual of all, an unruly mane of curls the color of the sky flows about her head. She's currently engaged in a Punch and Judy rendition of the Immaculate Dragons slaying the Anathema and sealing their souls in darkness for a gaggle of children and a band of hecklers.

    What will you do?

              Orca steps off the airship, pulling his coat closer to himself, making a 'brrr' noise as he did so, "Who willingly lives up here in this frozen hell, I'll never know." It had been a cold trip on the airship, and nothing made Orca miss home more than the cold. Back in the West, his voyages on his ship (the Storm's Saber) were long, treacherous and more often than not deadly, but at least they weren't cold.

              He tosses a jade piece to a vendor as he picks up an apple from a cart and looked around Last Chance. This had to be the place Plentimon was talking about. Months of traveling from frozen hellhole to frozen hellhole looking for signs hinting towards the gambling god's riddle had finally paid off. And hopefully the people he had met on board the ship would be able to help as well.
