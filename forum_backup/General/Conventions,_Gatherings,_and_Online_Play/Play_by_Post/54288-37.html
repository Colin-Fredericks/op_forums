                    After an appropriate period of time making the rounds of the clearing, and listening to the budding conversations, Blossom lets the natural flow of the gathering bring her back into contact with Tirien. She continues to act in an unobtrusive way, her mannerisms anything but threatening. Every downward flick of her eyes whenever they make contact with the gaze of another, every subtle movement as she glides out of the way of her betters, the slight hunch of her shoulders, the almost imperceptible flinch whenever someone's gaze lingers on her for too long, the self-conscious way she smooths the folds of her dress over her hips... it all screams this person is beneath your notice.

                    Well, it's a very subtle scream.

                    This uninteresting person pauses for the briefest of moments in front of Tirien, moving carefully so as not to be in the Lunar's way, nodding apologetically and seeming to whisper an apology for obstructing her. Instead, she whispers information, in a low, breathy voice. "You may have your work cut out for you, Tirien. These people are far more concerned about an impending invasion of Squirrel-people than about the Puissant Amethyst Court, which they think is shaping up to happen any day now. There's some budding resentment against you, and Darmud, seemingly for distracting from it. Good luck. Now, if you don't mind, snub me. I'm more effective if I'm less associated with you, and given the way these women see me, a mild snubbing of me will increase your standing in some eyes here."

                        As Hyuga starts to walk out into the hall he says, "It's probably from a wild boar Jensven". It was clear that he was still a bit shaken up from the dream, but he tried to pass it off as being tired. "We can study in a while Jensven. Let me get cleaned up". As Jensven thanked him for the help, Hyuga put up his hand and gave a tiny wave as simple recognition as he walked out the door.

                        His mind was still racing and his heartbeat was still going crazy. Why won't you be calm heart? It was just a dream. Dreams cannot hurt you. I must have been exhausted from all the work on the golems. That must be what set it off. It is nothing more.

                        Hyuga immediately walks to the closest bathroom and starts to wash off his face in the sink. He was always fond of doing that. At home it helped with the heat at times, but it just seemed natural. He had taken many baths in the rivers and it comforted him for some reason. He just seemed to have an affinity for water for some reason. I wonder if my essence is of the water aspect... but that would make no sense at all. There are still many things I need to learn about essence and all of the elemental poles. I am sure they have a great deal to do with the realm defense grid. I should do more studying about that. I haven't really looked at that in a long time... at this rate I will never get around to fixing it. It would solve the problem with the Fey really quickly too... I wonder...

                        Hyuga lost himself in thought over his dream of fixing the realm defense grid. Soon enough he had forgotten all about the nightmare that had come to him during the night. Thinking about crafting always took Hyuga's mind off of everything else; it never failed him.

                        After a while, he forgot all about even taking a shower and headed back to the artificery to find Jensven. "Why don't we do some research. Maybe we could find that sorcerer and get his help. I am sure he found something useful about that weapon. Maybe he could help us with some research too. Those scholarly types always seem to have their nose in books and want to be superior to everyone else and know everything. We could probably get plenty of information from him".

                        Well... I do look through plenty of crafting tomes, scrolls, and books when I am around them too... I guess I shouldn't be too judgemental...

                                    (Geherin)

                                    The last light of the sun fades out as an elderly one-armed man hits a tree with a heavy club. Conversation dies out almost immediately, and the individuals move into a circle, with the old man, who is now flanked by Ceryd and the horse, at one point along the circle. They are clustered in groups of like-colored scarves and kilts, and Darmud waves Tirien over towards him.

                                    Once everyone is in thier rightful position, the old man, who wears an iron torc and knarled black-iron circlet looks to Darmud, saying, "Darmud, chieftain of clan Donnkad. For what reason have you called this moot?"

                                    "I called this moot at the order of a Steward, who wishes to address the chiefs of the Vicruith," Darmud replies.

                                    "And this is the Steward?" the old man says, gesturing to Tirien.

                                    "She is."

                                    The old man turns to Ceryd, "Have you examined her claim, Lady Ceryd? Is she moon-kin?"

                                    Ceryd nods. "She is, as she claims, a Steward of Creation. She is also student of Mejan Flesh-weaver."

                                    The old man seems a bit suprised by this last point. He turns to Tirien. "Very well. Steward, you may address the High King and Chiefs of the Vicruith clans."
