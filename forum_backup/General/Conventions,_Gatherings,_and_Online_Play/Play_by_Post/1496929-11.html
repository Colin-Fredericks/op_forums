                    "Minos never told me about a goblin queen. Maybe they didn't know any..." replies Halwyn.

                    The caravan is small but cozy. The three sit together around a table that easily seats seven people. The interior is heated, although Halwyn doesn't normally need it. His bed, which he hadn't made this morning, was closed with a curtain. It's not messy, but you can tell Halwyn rarely if ever has guests here. Halwyn plugs his cell phone in and hopes it's finally charging again.

                    "I really hope that the kids only show up there on Saturdays. I don't want to attract more attention than necessary. I wonder which ghosts you meet there and whether they can really help." With a look out of the window, he fixes on the playground that can be seen in the distance, which is also said to be haunted.

                    "Does the rumor say it must be own blood or something else?" He didn't want to sacrifice an animal or anything.

                    He listens to Leah. "A creature from folklore? Of what origin? Also native? Sounds more European like centaur or minotaur..." he grabs his notebook and adds the information to the name of the creature. "Why does a bullfish seek out an underwater wolf..." he then asks into the room as he pulls out the Bluefingers book and looks there for the Kin of Tar. "Demonic wolves of the ocean...fish with the front end of a bull...can't figure it out yet..." he massages his temples. "...big rock markers under the tide...I doubt I can dive deep enough to see if there's a rock like that in the lake." he laughs briefly.
                    "But I could have one..." he seems to have an idea. "Well first we should visit the house with no roof...After all, a Ghost might have answers there. When not about the creatures in the lake, maybe about the Huntsman."
