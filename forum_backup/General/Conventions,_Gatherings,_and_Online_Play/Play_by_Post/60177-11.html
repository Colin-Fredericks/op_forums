"We're going to need to know where best to set down rather than just pick a point on the map. Tact..." he remarked as he scanned over the features of the world they had arrived at, "going to have to take this with some caution. As much as I'd like a bit of safari, we're looking for candidates for longer settlement"

        Hoshi printed out a sheet describing the planet.

        "There are patches of essence respiration we can see, and there's a lattice of high essence generation here, " he says, pointing near the equator, "But apart from that, there's nothing in terms of industry. According to our scanners, the equator is a swampy bog biome, while as you go north or south, the environment gets more temperate, but no less lush. It seems we'll have to trek through bush no matter where we touch down. There is, however, this," he then points to a large ravine.

        "This seems to have been formed when an Unshaped attacked the region centuries ago, but it has created a rocky area that the plants grow the least in. In any case, then, you'd have to deal with difficult terrain," he says, mater-of-factly.

                            The three beings that walk out of the bush look humanoid, but only to a mind who has the dimmest understanding of what a human looks like. They were over five meters tall, possessing lean, muscular bodies of a mottled brown like the trees around them. They possessed three digits on each overly long limb, and mouths filled with tusks. However, their most striking feature was their four eyes, one pair above the other, who scanned the team before them blankly.

                            "Gojus, totoranbu hess Rusemin ess, Sabbie tessprecht euru" the tallest said, his caprine ears flickering, as a smaller giant walked over to the Circle and leaned down. Gently, he pushed one of the dragonblooded scouts with a huge finger.

                            "Kussus, Rusemin ja nai," the smaller giant called to the leader, who frowned.

                            What will you do?

                                                Choosing to not reply to this, Pellias taps a series of buttons on the wrist of his suit. "I'm not sure how they're going to react to this," he said to the others present, "but be prepared for a hostile reaction.
