                                                    I think I'll try and work all six of you in, although it may get a little unwieldy. Thankfully we don't have as much combat as another system like Pathfinder. Still working through ideas, but some other things to keep in mind:
                                                    - Go take a look at the Megastructures episodes for Abu Dhabi and Dubai, particularly Dubai Mall and Burj Khalifa. There are lots of good hooks there.
                                                    - While you're at it, check out the stores you can find in the Malls there. Dubai Mall is a good one since it has pretty much everything. There's this really weird hybrid between East and West. Seriously, every single mall in UAE has at least one La Senza and one Victoria Secret. A lot of people find it really jarring the first time they visit. Also, food is dirt cheap.
                                                    - Being gay isn't in and of itself a crime. What's criminalizes is the act of homosexual sex, it's still not great but it is an important enough distinction (at least in Emitrati minds)I feel I should note it.
                                                    - PDA is a big nono, and that is enforced. Some people manage to get away with it in bars, but bouncers will call them on it and there are some places where holding hands can get you yelled at.
                                                    - If you are an expat, you must have a job. You will get deported if you don't. Visas and passports often make for methods of control or bargaining chips because if you are not a tourist, you must be employed.
                                                    - All companies other than transnationals have to have a 51% ownership with a local Emirati. This can be one person, or more than one can split the controlling interest, but it has to be there.

                                                    I'll let you know when I think of more. I've always associated Changelings with Jinn, so there are some pretty cool ideas floating here.
