                                            The room they sit in has a long wooden table with mahogany chairs at the side, save for the gold-furnished wooden chair at the head of the table where Talos lounges languidly. As they all take their places, they take a chance to observe the room. The floor was made of a translucent glass, which revealed the hundreds of constantly turning orichalcum gears that seemed to make up the entirety of the manse. The same gears made up the walls, though only a few spots were covered by the translucent glass of the floor. The space between the high vaulted ceiling was also taken up by these gears, though it had a large one in the center containing numerous shining crystals that gave the room it's light. Those who sit still long enough notice the room constantly shifting ever so slowly, as if it were part of the huge machine that was the manse. As the room shifted, as did the passageways out of it. After some time the stairway they came up through was covered by a gear, only to reveal a new exit into a hallway. The whole thing gave the impression of being inside a gigantic pocketwatch.

                                            "Personally, I'm not opposed to getting rich." Talos says as he hangs his hat on the side of his chair, picking up a goblet seemingly from nowhere. As he speaks, the group notices small clockwork automatons, no larger than a housecat, walking rigidly across the glass floor. They each carry a goblet, one for each of the characters. "But most importantly, I wish to help people. All my life I've had great ideas of how I can assist the downtrodden, to make their miserable existence more bearable. Ways that most men would scoff at and deem impossible. Becoming a Chosen of the Unconquered Sun has shown me that not only are my ideas possible, but I have the power to do so."                                                         "I do not know any of you, not in the way I have known those I called friends or mentors. Nor do I trust you, in the sense that I am comfortable speaking about myself. Without any pride I can say that I understand portions of the threat we face better than anyone else here. In the face of that threat I am willing to speak, and in the hope that you will become friends with whom I can tell the whole of my story."

                                                        "It is true that I am Chosen of the Sun, though I would have preferred to say as much in my own time and way." He cast an aside glance at Swimming Owl and Flynn. "There is no point in hiding the fact now. Yet the Great Lights of Heaven and the Dragons of Creation are not the only who Exalt the children of men."

                                                        With his hand he made a subtle sign so that with the twitch of a finger his hidden protector would come forth in might and wrath.

                                                        "Five Maidens there are in Heaven, in whose governance is the Fate of gods and men. Exalted, for they could not have been less, shining in their colors and bearing their marks as brands of caste slew my father in cold blood. I do not know if I was meant to die with him, but I fled and have kept ahead of them. For fear of these chosen I have held my power back, kept my guard ready, and attempted to learn all that I could. Now I see that this was a mistake.

                                                        The Sun did not choose me to hide my light under a basket and whatever risk it is to me personally, Creation needs heroes. I do not know if I deserve that title, but I have been called to rise to it. I do not advocate throwing away all subtlety, but we must be aware that there are more powers in Heaven and Earth to reckon with than you have known.
