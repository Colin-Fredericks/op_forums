                "The first step is usually to ascertain if there's any immediate threat and set up a small camp, nothing that can't be taken down in 30 minutes or so." Pellias turned to the horizon and the increasingly exposed pyramid to his mind's eyes. "Given that we've been on the ground for some time now, I would suggest that preparation for a short stay is in order." He concentrated for a moment, letting what the eye was surveying digest in his mind. "The locals do appear to have intelligence...and leadership. This may quickly become a more diplomatic mission than any extended scouting endeavour."

                He thought about holding his tongue regarding the shimmering figure, knowing full well from his own strategies that any change in observable patterns could cause the spy to abandon their mission. However, he also knew very well that this could cost the life of a valued target. It was interesting to be on the other end of this game for a change.

                "We're also not alone. I've seen what appears to be an invisible or partly-visible target twice now on our equipment. Keep sharp." Pellias quickly flickers out of sight himself a moment later, the helmet of his suit snapping out his face last of all.

                                    Despite whatever Pellias expected, his target revealing itself is nothing like it. Reality seems to peel back, as though a curtain, revealing a maddening world of neither form, function or identity. Out of this mad realm slinks a shapely woman in a gold and green leather uniform, an emblem of seven wings and seven eyes on her chest. She spies Pellias and yelps.

                                    "Footsteps on Glass Practice didn't hide me? Can you gaze on the Shinmas as well?" the woman asked, surprised as much as frightened at Pellias' attention.

                                    As for Amelia, she approaches a trio of beasts tidying a monolith. Appraised of her presence, they all genuflect on the ground, saying as one, "HAIL TO YOU, CHILD OF THE TYRANT SUN!"

                                    What will you do now?
