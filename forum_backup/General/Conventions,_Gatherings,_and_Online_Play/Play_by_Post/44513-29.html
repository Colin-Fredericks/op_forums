            Okay, here's part One of my six-part series, entitled "The Terrestrials of Novalesk". This information is "common knowledge", as native Terrestrials enjoy something of a celebrity status.

            -Nikoloi Feiro-

            Blessed by Mela, Dragon of Air, this man is currently pushing his eighty-sixth year. Though his thin skin and pale hair show the marks of one approaching their end, the Chancellor of the Acadmey of Sorcerous Wisdom has purportedly looked exactly the same since his thirtieth year. He is a master sorcerer, unrivaled by any in Novalesk or the surrounding nations (though it is rumored that Sigmund Rerir of Theros is quickly catching up to the older Air Aspect). His occult mastery extends beyond sorcery, however, as he posesses a Master's ranking in the Arts of Geomancy, Exorcism and Demonology.

            Chanellor Feiro has outlived both his wife and his oldest two children, who all died eight years ago when his country manse was attacked by parties unknown. His foster daughter Maia, and then-seven-year old daughter Thalia were both in Novalesk at the time, and therefore were unharmed. The Chancellor has rather publicly placed large bounties on any information that would lead him to the involved parties. So far, no one has stepped forward or claimed responsibility.

            Chancellor Feiro stands a mere five-foot two (About 157 centimeters for anyone who prefers metric), though he usually slouches, making him appear even shorter. His skin is pale, with a bluish tinge. He typically wears a green and white robe, with a red stole. He is often seen carrying his staff of office, a jade stave topped with a sorcerous mandala.

                Details & Explanations
                Artifact 2: Transformative Aegis- The Transformative Aegis is a special variant of Transformative Clothing designed for Sidereal use. It has all the normal properties of a suit of transformative clothing, with the following several additions. While worn, the transformative aegis provides +3L/+1B soak, with no hardness, mobility penalty or fatigue. This bonus does not count as armor for the purposes of martial arts or other charms, and stacks with other armor (though wearing it with armor would sharply limit the utility of its transformative properties). It contains a single hearthstone socket, fashioned from moonsilver and starmetal threads that come together in a circle on the garment; this socket can be hidden from sight, depending on the design of the garment. The Transformative Aegis has an attunement cost of 4.

                Backing (Forbidding Manse of Ivy) 2: Adverse Blossom is a member in good standing of the Forbidding Manse of Ivy and the Convention of Wood, and a fairly prominent member of the Convention on the Wyld (owing in part to her relationship with Righteous Arrow, the chair of the convention and her Sifu).

                Backing (Gold Faction) 2: Adverse Blossom is a member in good standing of the Gold Faction.

                Celestial Manse 2: Adverse Blossom's celestial manse is a small manor on extensive grounds covered with beautiful gardens. It is Lunar aspected and produces a lunar aspected version of the Mind-Cleansing Gem, which Blossom keeps installed in her transformative aegis. The manse is located deep in the Lunargent Ecological Protectorate, an inconvenient distance from the Loom of Fate and the offices of the Bureau of Destiny, but conveniently close to Gate 17 (the Nexus Gate). It belonged to Blossom's sifu, Righteous Arrow, before she moved to a larger and more powerful Lunar aspected manse closer to the center of the city.

                Connections (Outlaws) 3: Prior to her Exaltation, Adverse Blossom was one of the most well connected information brokers in Nexus. Arcane Fate and her time in Yu Shan training, however, have caused her extensive information networks to largely collapse. She is in the process of rebuilding those connections and expanding them across the Confederation of Rivers.

                Salary 2: As a young Sidereal, Adverse Blossom receives the standard Sidereal pay packet.

                Sifu 3: Adverse Blossom's Sifu is Righteous Arrow, a Chosen of Battles who Exalted during the Balorian Crusade. Righteous Arrow was born in Fadrex City, a mid-sized city deep in the Southeast of Creation, at the edge of the Wyld. She was an only child; both of her parents were human Gunzosha Warriors in the army of the Shogun, part of the garrison of Fadrex City. Fadrex was devastated by the Great Contagion, just like the rest of Creation, but she and her parents were lucky enough to be among the survivors. Unfortunately, Fadrex’s location at the very boundaries of Creation made it one of the first places targeted by the Fae during the Balorian Crusade.

                Righteous Arrow was 15 when the Fae swept past the borders of Creation. The glorious Terrestrial Exalted and their human soldiers were cut down like so much grass as the Raksha moved in on a wave of unreality. The city was completely destroyed, the very ground on which it stood unmade by the essence of chaos. Righteous Arrow watched as her parents were literally ripped apart by the forces of the Wyld. Killed, then destroyed, then reduced to their component parts. That was when she Exalted. She survived the attack, by luck, the grace of fate and her own nascent powers. Her city destroyed, everyone she knew gone, she fled to the pockets of reality that remained at the border of the world. She was taken in by a tribe of barbarians at the edge of the world, and survived the war in their company, learning their ways, instinctively teaching herself Throne Shadow and Violet Bier of Sorrows styles, hazy dreams of her past lives showing her how to pray to the pattern spiders for rudimentary astrological effects. The five-score fellowship had their hands full, with no time to send agents to the edge of the world to collect a newly Exalted Sidereal. She continued to live with the barbarians who had taken her in, fighting back against the Fae where and how they could. Eventually, through the well-known actions of the Scarlet Empress, the war ended, the Fae were pushed back and Righteous Arrow was found by the Bureau of Destiny. She was taken in and trained, joining the Sidereal Exalted in the celestial city, but she never forgot what it was like to be on the front lines when the monsters from outside the world came in.

                Initially, she joined the bronze faction; she was duly impressed by the Scarlet Empress’s actions in ending the war and saving Creation. As the decades passed, however, she could not forget how easily the Terrestrial Host of Fadrex had been rolled over by the invading Fae. In time, and after much soul searching over two centuries, she turned away from the bronze faction and towards the gold, reasoning that only the true heroes of Creation, the Solar Exalted, could stand against the Fae should another crusade come. She eventually came to head the Convention on the Wyld, always arguing that the Bureau of Destiny paid too much attention to the threats of Malfeas and the Underworld. The Yozis were defeated, broken and imprisoned. The Neverborn and their Deathlord minions were already dead. But the Fae, and the Wyld, were the implacable enemies of Creation. They were eternal, and they would not stop until Creation was no more. Her views have never been popular, but her expertise on the Wyld is unparalleled, ensuring her continued advancement. She also had a tendency to make the more refined Sidereals uncomfortable by insisting on dressing in the fashion of the jungle barbarian tribes that she ran with centuries ago, often preferring clothing made from the skins of animals to the fine silks and samites of heaven. In many ways she sees herself as a spokesperson in the bureau for the uncivilized tribes at the edges of Creation.

                She has many goals and projects, but one of her main focuses right now is securing better weapons with which the Sidereal Exalted can fight the Wyld, and she’s not above bending, or even breaking, the laws of Heaven and the rules of the Bureau to get that done. She does have one particular pet project that she's working on, though, on which she has pinned a lot of hopes. She calls it Limitless Spectrum of Transformation Style- a new Sidereal Martial Art designed to control the Wyld and fight it, and the Fae, on their own terms. A number of Sidereals and gods know that she is working on this. She has extensively studied the Wyld itself, Sorcery and Martial Arts in preparation. She has observed and worked with Lunar masters, learned a Fae Shaping Style, had her own cup grace forged, consulted with the most senior gods (many in service to Luna), visited the Silver Chair and studied essence in all its forms, especially those influenced by Luna. She has examined the Mountain Folk, to see the results of Autochthon's hand on the Fae, and has travelled to each of the elemental poles to examine these bastions against the wyld. Through the Cult of the Illuminated, she has even studied young Solars as they slowly relearn their own mighty wyld-shaping abilities. All this is known to her allies, and some of her enemies, and others with an interest. Even her bronze faction adversaries hope she succeeds, as a new SMA would be a potent tool for any Sidereal.

                What they do not know is the full depth of her research. Her studies in Creation and the Wyld were not enough. Examining the Elemental Poles, homes of Gaia's souls, and Autochthon's handiwork in the form of the Jadeborn made her realize that the world-shaping power of the Primordials outstripped anything else she had yet seen. Neither Gaia nor Autochthon were available for intense study, and of course the Neverborn were out of the question. That left the Yozis- specifically Oramus. Using Celestial Circle Sorcery, she has summoned, bound, interrogated, studied and sparred with dozens of Second Circle demons of Oramus over the years, learning much. She has travelled to Malfeas in numerous guises, travelling to and through the 3rd circle souls of Oramus and even studying the Dragon Beyond the World directly in his self-prison (for whatever that was worth). None of this would invite censure, though some of these activities would be questionable. It still wasn't enough. So, in the southeast of Creation, she [REDACTED]. She also fully intends to [REDACTED] and [REDACTED] when she is finished her studies- that is to say, when she has finished designing Limitless Spectrum of Transformation style and fully internalized the Student and Elder Sutras of Shaping.

She especially prefers cherry blossoms, rose petals and lotus flowers.

                Character History

                Adverse Blossom was born in Comfrey, which was a small fishing village on the south bank of the Yellow River, near the eastern edge of the Confederation of Rivers. Comfrey was in a region that was technically claimed by several nations, but was not actually important enough for any of them to press their claims.

                Adverse Blossom’s father was a fisherman, her mother one of the village’s few soldiers. She had two older brothers and three older sisters. You’ll notice that these family members, and the village itself, are all referred to in the past tense.

                On Blossom’s eight birthday, the slavers of the Guild came calling. No nation rode to defend Comfrey and its people. The few soldiers the village could muster in its own defense were no match for the mercenaries of the Guild. Blossom saw her mother go down fighting, a spear in her hand and a sword in her chest. The rest of her family, and every surviving villager, was rounded up by the Guild and taken off as slaves. They weren’t taken far. The Guild had a contract to deliver a certain number of slaves to their client, and they were short. So, the caravan sacked Comfrey to make up the numbers on their way to their customer- the Puissant Amethyst Court.

                It was the Emir Wind-Blown Petals, one of the Shah’s most important ministers, in charge of the Court’s relations with the Guild (among other things), who took possession of the slaves. He apportioned them out to the population of the Freehold, for various uses. Over the course of months, Adverse Blossom watched as, one by one, her family, her friends, her neighbors, everyone she had ever known was tortured, toyed with, abused and fed upon. In different orders, and in different ways. Many were killed; they were the lucky ones. The unlucky ones lost their souls, and were sold back to the Guild as pliant slaves. Adverse Blossom counts herself fortunate that none of her family members were among those pitiable people- she only had to watch them suffer and die.

                For some reason, they saved Adverse Blossom for last. They toyed with her, and tortured her, and entered her dreams, savoring her suffering… but they did not feed on her in any way that led to permanent damage. Blossom has no idea why- something that haunts her to this day. She was changed by her time in the Freehold, no longer able to survive on human food, and was psychologically scarred, but she was not “ravaged,” in the Fae parlance. And, once she was the last survivor of her village, she finally saw a moment when they let their guard down… and she escaped. They did not chase her.

                To this day, she doesn’t know if she escaped, or if they let her go. That uncertainty haunts her nightmares as much as the memories of what they did to her, her family, and her village.

                On her own in the wilderness, she made her way from village to village, town to town, until she arrived in the great metropolis of Nexus. She did what she had to do on the streets of Nexus- begging, stealing, selling her body, even murder, whatever was necessary to survive. She learned one day of the Daimyo of Faces, and went to her, trading a future favor to have the evidence of her mutations at the hands of the Wyld wiped from her face. For years, she lived as an urchin on the streets, scraping by, surviving rather than living, and nursing her hatred of the Wyld, the Fae and those who dealt with them.

                One day, when she was 13, she came across something unexpected when she broke into a rich man’s safe. No jewels, silver or jade- just papers. She took the papers, and sold them to the man’s enemies for more than she would make in a month of her normal activities. It was a profound, life changing revelation- information truly was the most valuable commodity in the world.

                No longer did Adverse Blossom steal jewels, silver and jade (well, not often). She stole secrets, and sold them to others. In time, she had others, especially other street urchins, doing the stealing for her. Over the next six years, she (or, more accurately, her alias, the Seeker in Bronze) became one of the most respected information brokers in Nexus. She had money to spare, a vast network of informants, and, for the first time in her life, power. She used that power, in her own subtle way, making sure to sell certain secrets in certain ways, bringing harm to the Guild, and other slavers who sold human chattel to the Fae, in any way she could. Harming the Fae directly was, of course, beyond her, but their human partners were another story entirely.

                Adverse Blossom finally exalted when she was 19, after discovering a particularly fateful secret. She was quickly gathered up by the Five Score Fellowship and whisked off to Yu-Shan. As a Chosen of Secrets, it was naturally assumed that she would join the Bronze Faction, and they made every effort to recruit her. She was approached, however, by an older Sidereal named Righteous Arrow. The Chosen of Battles had studied the young Starborn’s thread, and had seen her terrible encounter with the Fae in her childhood, and how it had shaped her life. Arrow helped Blossom to adjust to her new life, to the strangeness of Yu-Shan. She helped Blossom see that the Sidereals were tragically mis-focused, spending too much energy in the impotent Yozis and the deceased Neverborn, and not enough on the eternal threat of the Wyld. And she helped Blossom see that the returning Solars were the best tool to help Creation withstand the Fae. Righteous Arrow became Adverse Blossom’s best friend, confidant, sifu and lover, and initiated the young Sidereal into the Gold Faction.

                It has been four years since Adverse Blossom exalted. She has completed her training, and been accepted by the Five Score Fellowship. She is in good standing with both the Forbidding Manse of Ivy and the Gold Faction, having completed a number of missions for both. She has taken a long term assignment with the Convention of Wood, using a resplendent destiny of the Mask to reassume her identity as the Seeker in Bronze and rebuild her informant networks, not just in Nexus but all across the Scavenger Lands. She is a prominent member of the Convention on the Wyld, under the leadership of her sifu, Righteous Arrow. The two trust each other implicitly, and are working together to do whatever is necessary to make sure the Sidereals, the Exalted as a whole, Heaven and Creation are well defended against the Wyld.

                No matter what.

                Appearance

                Adverse Blossom is an attractive young woman in her early twenties. She stands at a slender 5’8” tall, has medium length curly auburn hair and bright green eyes. She has a graceful, willowy neck, long fingers, fair alabaster skin, long legs and small breasts.                     Part 2 of "Terrestrials of Novalesk!"

                    -Ptreas Artemia-

                    Many tales surround this woman. She lifts cattle for her daily exercise regimen. She can slay a Yeddim with a single strike. She regularly sics feral starved dogs on lazy family troops (and according to some rumors, her younger brother). She can best Midea Demetrion (Larissa's former bodyguard) in single combat.

                    The unknowledgeable would look at those rumors, and say that rumors of such feats are to be expected, where the Blessed of Hesiesh are concerned. In response, those in the know will shake their heads and clarify that these rumors have been circulating for years, when Artemia exalted mere months ago.

                    Such is the woman that Artemia is. While her father is the youngest son of Ptreas Nikon (and one of the most renowned Scavenger Lords in the nation), Artemia's mother is the scion of Strategos Eugene's older brother and Vicruith warrior woman. Artemia's mother embodies all of the virtues and vices of her two people, being bellicose and belligerent, but embodying an unbreakable code of honor and duty, and a strong martial ethos. Many have observed that Artemia is spitting image of her mother, both physically and psychologically.

                    Artemia exalted a mere two weeks before her twentieth birthday, during what is now known to be the opening stages of the Fae invasion. A number of individual Fae had been sighted in the north and east of Novalesk, and one, a burning knight, attacked Artemia and her parents as they returned home from a dig. Their guards were left behind with the main body of the convoy, leaving them vulnerable. Rather than balk at the charging, mounted fae knight, however, Artemia charged back, assaulting it with her bronze spear. The Fae lost the subsequent battle, during which Artemia exalted and claimed its Sword for her own.

                    Artemia is a trim, highly athletic woman, standing nearly five-foot-eight (173 centimeters for you metric peeps). She has a pale complexion and flaxen hair, often kept up in a tail. Since exaltation, her typical attire consists of a light jade breastplate, a set of jade bracers, and a long skirt. She wields a spear of unknown providence and material, said to be a relic of the Age of Dreams (and known to be a find of her parents). Her artifacts are all hers, gifts from family members upon her exaltation.
