                        "I take it you are all aware of the carcass that hit the wall?" "Apparently it is a message for the one who has called one known as the King of Hoof. He comes to eat your bones in the passing of one moon. I would be wary, whoever you are..."

                        With that, he will pause and look expectantly around the common room. His intent is to ensure that the message sent by the King was received by its intended recipient.

                                    "You sure that the corpse that hit the tavern wasn't you, friend?" Orca asked in an almost-too-casual tone.

                                                            The Prodigal will first address the humor that seems to pass him by, "As I still have my lower half, and do not have an ominous message carved into my hide I would say I am not the carcass that hit the wall." A slight smirk appears on his face for a brief instant before he turns to Zan.

                                                            He will appraise the man before him and nod respectfully. The Daybreak doesn't wish to cause a scuffle in this public house. Besides, Marian wasn't all that good in a fight. "Good Sir, unless you are the one that called the Hoofed monarch, I won't trouble you." The Deathknight determines that the man should be appeased with that answer.

                                                            Finally, he will address Zarah, "I can certainly take you to see the message. It was left in the carcass of a most interesting creature. It seems to be a mix of a bird and a hoofed quadraped." With that he will start for the door again. He doesn't wait to see if Zarah or anyone else will follow, he just decides that anyone that is interested will come and if they aren't, they won't. It's that simple. Besides, those gentlemen that gave off the aura of death could not have gone far...
