            To maintain my sanity, we will run the Northern Uprising here in OOC topic. It will make coordination easier. All other actions will be run via PM as usual.

            Cheraki
            People are disappearing. Some on the List you found, Quelaana. Some aren't. Rumors abound of Cynis sending in an entire legion. A few hope the Roseblack will be their salvation. Surely she can see how the people suffer. Others say The Roseblack plans to win the Scarlet Throne swimming though the blood of the populace.

            The atmosphere in Cheraki is tense. The Bloody Hand stocks up on weapons. Everyone who can fight from young teenage girls to old men is armed. No one travels unarmed. Cynis Dynasts walk the streets in groups. Merchants pack up their wares to head elsewhere. Overseers whisper in the taverns. Slaves in the fields are more lively than usual.

            It's a powder keg. One spark could set the whole city into chaos.

            Tepet and Cynis
            Tepet and Cynis are negotiating the Tepet Legion's place in the upcoming rebellion.

            Tepet REALLY wants to get involved in the Northern Situation. The Roseblack needs a decisive military victory and this Cynis problem is perfectly situated to provide her with one. Tepet needs permission to intervene or else Cynis can sue for trespassing into their affairs. Also, one house invading another house's territory may start a chain reaction of stronger houses taking out the weak. A situation Tepet wants to avoid.

            However, Cynis believes they can handle it themselves. Cynis says they are sending one of their legions north. However, Cynis legion moves slow. They are plagued with Bureaucratic delays. *cough Ryo cough* Yet, the Cynis elders are playing they have the situation under control.

            Some Tepet house elders are strongly considering throwing caution to the wind and conquering the province in a last attempt for glory. With the Empress gone, there is no one to stop them, except whatever forces Cynis has in the north. More rational minds see this as Suicide. It's a desperate measure for a desperate house.

            What do you do?
