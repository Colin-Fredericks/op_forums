    "I've got it! We must all be fascinated by something. Magic has a way of obsessing you. Let's discuss what we're working on, and decide what we could pursue together and compare notes on. Exploring a Mystery, any Mystery with magic behind it, can make all of us better magicians. I'll start: I will be clearing out some demons from my psyche this week, which I mean quite literally. A mage I don't know will be guiding me through it. Frankly, I could use an escort, especially if any of you are comfortable with demons of the mind. Or trip sitting."


    "Mystery?" Lynx murmurs as he looks for more letter tiles to spell out other Norwegian words. "I'm not sure you could help me. It's a Thyrsus thing. A Primal Wild thing. I'm looking for the one who Awakened me. A Great Lynx. He guided me during my second visit to the Wild. I want to find him again. Pretty sure it's a him, at least. Not really sure if spirits have gender yet..." He hums a little as he looks at the group. "I doubt you can relate to this. If any of you know Spirit magic, I guess that could help. I'll appreciate any help you're willing to give, though."

    "If you want a more realistic Mystery to pursue together from my end, it'll be insight into one another. What's it like being an Acanthus? What's it feel like to wield Space or Forces? What's it like being attuned to... Stygia, was it? There's so much to learn. I think we can teach each other a lot. Reach an understanding. Become friends and allies." He looks to Moonstone, then to Phoenix and Ragnar, with a look of amusement. "Enjoy some friction."

    Wait, what?

        I just sit there and stare at Moonstone as I drink both the information and the coffee. I don't think I even blink, it's a lot to take in all in one sitting. I wince slightly when he talks of our encounter with the Gulmoth. My memories of that place were vague, but the image of those bleeding holes in me are still crisp and clean, as much as I'd love them not to be. When he gets to the part about dealing with it, I do appreciate how he acknowledges at least someone else other than himself, but...

        "As well as the others," I chime in, gesturing towards Ragnar, Lynx, and Binder with my paper mug. It's a start for him, I suppose. Looks like he's on the heroic path, he just tripped up a bit is all. After that, he mentions that the Gulmoth would appear on...

        "The 17th? Doesn't give us a whole lot of time..." I sigh with a concerned breath.

        The subject of a common goal for our cabal pops up, with Moonstone suggesting we all pitch in on our projects. That...is gonna complicate matters. If they pitch in on me searching for Anubis, they might find out I'm a Hunter. Can't let them know that I once dedicated my life to killing mages. Maybe I can change the subject.

        "Do we really need a common goal? At least, to start with, I mean. We're all new to the world of magic, if only as active participants for some of us," I state, glancing at Moonstone with that last part, "What's the rush? Let's just work together and leave it at that for the time being. A common goal will probably crop up naturally that way, anyways."

        I hope that gets them off the subject.
