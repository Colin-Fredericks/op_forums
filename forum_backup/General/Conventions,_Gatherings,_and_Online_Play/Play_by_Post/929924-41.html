                                        Phoenix
                                        By looking around your immediate area, you see that these flowers aren't just odd in their color; they're simply everywhere. You've seen the occasional yard in the summer in which the owners let the dandelions grow out of control, and maybe that's not so strange. But to see multiple yards along the street covered in these strange plants? That's not normal. In addition, they seem even more prevalent as you look to the east and north along the side streets here. You aren't sure why that would happen, except for a supernatural explanation.

                                        Lynx's brief synopsis reminds you of Boomer's talk about tass. You can't be sure without your Prime Sight or a spell, but these plants sound like they are physically imbued with mana. You don't know as much about Essence, but you hazard a guess that it's a similar kind of energy that spirits can feed on, based on what Lynx shares with you.


                                        Lynx
                                        You start to collect several of the mana-infused dandelions. You notice that within a few moments of picking them, the flowers start to wilt and droop. After watching them for a little longer, they start to disintegrate and fall apart in your hands.

                                        It's easy enough to grab more, if you choose, but it's clear to you that you won't be able to take these flowers and save them for later.

                                        You can restore your full mana if you choose, but you can't harvest the tass for later.


                                        Moonstone
                                        You race along the street and turn left to go north onto Malden Avenue.

                                        It's like looking at a sea of purple.

                                        The supernatural dandelions have consumed the lawns on this street. They crowd out all other plants. They cover the trees like thick purple vines, and you can't even see any grass in sight due to the bed of flowers on the lawns. Whether they are attracted to this location or created by something, this is their source.

                                        You look up into the sky, and feel a surge of adrenaline as you see the cloud of Essence that is consuming your thoughts. You know immediately that you can only see the Essence because of Ouroboros Ophis' spell: the mystical energy is obviously invisible to your mundane senses, and it falls outside the purview of Mind or Space magic and the realm of Pandemonium.

                                        A near constant stream of Essence is wafting like perfume from the "dandelions" filling the yards on this street. And swirling like a vortex in the air, you see a small horde of ghosts hovering, arms outstretched as they feed on the energy here. They seem frenzied, some of them grappling with each other and screaming with undead rage to be closest to the largest streams. They rip into each other, but the injured ones regenerate their bodies by inhaling more Essence and channeling it into their Corpus.

                                        Something suddenly shifts in your focus as without thinking about it, your senses transfer and...you're looking through the very eyes of your goetia. The Fear goetia is hovering near the cloud itself, gorging on the energy. You feel it send a stream of Essence to you, feeding it to you by converting it into mana, and your own internal reservoir of arcane energy is almost instantly filled. You also see through the goetia's senses an even larger vortex of spirits, another type of ephemeral entity you can't see without its help. Like the ghosts, the spirits are clamoring to claim the most Essence, gorging on it and using more to heal as other spirits claw at them. Your familiar starts to draw attention from the other spirits and ghosts, so it quickly retreats to relative safety at your side.

                                        Your eyes shift again and your senses snap back to your own. You feel like you could share the familiar's senses again, if you wanted to. At the same time, you feel a release of tension as the obsession lifts from you. You're left standing there, wondering what to do, while your cabal catches up with you.
