    The Keeper's shouts were a brutal slap to Nate's mood. He was slowly beginning to realize that his plan was flawed from the beginning. Of course, no one cared about his feelings. No one ever did.         Max was pleasantly surprised at the wilderness helping him on his path. He'd known the terms of the Contract, and even felt some ease from them, but everything here had just been so... horrible and dishonest. From his transformation and abduction, to the spores and "nymphs" at the lake, to the mud pit that tried to eat him... Forces in this place acting toward his benefit was new. 'I guess direct deals are the only thing you can trust here.' Still, an empty belly didn't leave him much space to ruminate on that nor appreciate the kindness. He was too focused on following the path that the land set before him and his snout urged him down. Wherever the pig was heading smelled wonderful.

        Indeed, the sight of the blue apples was every bit as delicious as their fragrance. Absently wiping his salivating mouth, Max almost missed the sign. The recent thoughts on deals crossed his mind again, and his Contract with the Wilds to boot. He'd have to keep to this one, too. Truly, though, it almost didn't matter that the terms seemed so reasonable; the starving porker might have done much the same if it meant promising a firstborn son. "Deal," he told the tree -- with a hint of delirium and his curly tail awag -- before he lost it. Max plucked a fruit from its branches and took an enormous bite from it.

        It was better than he'd dared hope. The juice, skin and pulp danced across his tongue, bringing an apple to mind, but only for wont of a more apt comparison. He wasn't sure he had the words to describe it. If any food could be called sublime, this was it. The moment seemed to stretch on for Max, even though he hungered. And then it was over. Delighted swinish sounds made their ways out through full mouthfuls, polishing the first fruit off quickly and reaching for another. He absently spat out the single large, round seed from each -- not due to thoughts of his agreement, simply for their being slightly too hard and cumbersome to eat. Yes, he made quite a pig out of himself, juice and pulp splattered all over his face, hands, and stomach, as who knows how many of the delicious fruits disappeared into his mouth and down his throat.

        In time, the Pig's belly filled and he started to slow enough for actual thought to rise up. 'Heh, definitely didn't need Nate's tips for this'. This made Max pause in his chewing, and in bringing another fruit in for a landing.Nate's advice had been a life-saver during his more embarrassing and nauseating meal. He remembered how much he appreciated it, now. Not all of the Goat's other counsel had been as useful, but that wasn't the point: they were given with the best of intent, from a friend with a clue to a friend sorely in need of one, as Max remembered. For Jessica's sake, too; she'd tried to include him on a plan or two to get out, and had saved both of their lives before. Introspective as the Pig was, he'd felt bad about the fight earlier than this, but was now very guilty indeed. '... I have to keep a better handle on things. They deserve better.'

        Max swallowed his current mouthful and looked at the fruit in his hand. He thought of an earlier chat with Nate, and found himself laughing a bit. 'Ha. Guess this stupid thing finally found a fruit tree after all,' he thought, bonking the side of his snout. Max was done eating. Not that the hunger had completely abated, but he felt full. He could stop. And he would. "I'm not a pig," he said aloud. And then licked his chops, slurping up all the juice and pulp around his snout, giving a small squeal of pleasure. "... Not really a pig..." he mumbled weakly to no one, his blush reddening the purple juice stains across his cheeks.

        Max looked to the small pile of seeds in front of him, each with an opalescent sheen, like a pearl in the sun. He remembered the deal. Luckily, there actually weren't as many as he'd thought, given his gluttonous rampage. Perhaps they were just more filling than they looked? Still, he definitely had some gardening to do. "Thank you," he told the tree, giving it a small bow, "I'll keep my word." He began to cradle the seeds between his arms. After a thought, Max added two more fruit to the third he'd plucked earlier: Nate and Jessica could use these. "I'll be sure their seeds get planted, too," he reassured the tree. Then, departure! 'Hm... I'd like to head back to the Ranch... But I could see these pissing off the Keeper, if he really wants us to eat like animals. They'd just get torn down.' He'd risk a few, just in case, and could even get off following the letter of the deal planting all of them there. But Max owed it; he'd honor the spirit, too. Speaking of which... "Um... Hey there, uh, Wilds." He turned from the tree, then paused, and bowed somewhere at the wilderness -- careful not to spill any seeds. "Thank you for bringing me here. I really needed it." He wasn't sure he really needed to talk to it, but, well... It felt respectful. "So... I hate to ask again right away, but... could you help direct me, here? As long as I'm planting seeds, where would you most want 'em to go? I'd love to honor two bargains at once." The pig waited for the wilds to give him a clue.

                                                        Max indeed found his way to the clearing without any trouble. That nearly wasn't so, but a helpful beam of light pointed out a hole just before it had a chance to trip him up. Safely arrived, he watched the central light split itself up into several smaller ones and scattered. It left him with a central sunbeam with a circle around it. 'Well, I guess that's that.' He set down his little collection of seeds and fruit, picked up one little pearl, and started digging where light marked a spot.

                                                        The going was messy and a bit slow; the ground was thicker than he'd have guessed. And he kept running into networks of roots under the ground. All the exertion gave Max a bit of a grunting pant by the end of the first hole. 'I can see people don't just buy garden soil for the nutrients, now...' he thought as he put the seed at the bottom and got it buried. He took a deep breath and started on planting the next seed. He went on this way until he'd covered almost half the seeds. By which point his arms were screaming with fatigue.

                                                        'C'mon... Just a few more. Just... Take a breath, wipe your brow and get through this.' Max forced his arm up to wipe the sweat off his forehead, forgetting too late that 1) his hands were coated in dirt and 2) it wasn't like he had any sweat to wipe off, anyway. It gave him a moment's pause. 'Because the muck hadn't quite gotten everywhere, yet.' But feelings of his own daftness quickly made way for how nice it felt. He hadn't realized how hot his face had gotten -- nor the rest of him -- until he touched the cool dirt to it and felt the instant relief. He briefly recalled a childhood field trip to a farm, and the sight of pigs rooting in the dirt, plunging their faces in practically up to their eyes. They made holes very quickly and seemed to have enough stamina to do it all day. So, with some reluctance and no small embarrassment, Max weighed the pros and cons of putting his snout to work.

                                                        He tried to start the hole with a hand, but the sharp twinge across his arm drew out a wincing squeal. The Pig took a few breaths. He looked at the last pearl before him. He looked at the site of the last hole. And he looked at the small pink blob always at the bottom of his vision. He looked to his left, and to his right, and sighed. "Aww, what the hell..." Max got on his belly before the site of it. With one last breath, he started digging in the dirt with his snout. And he really began to wish he'd done it sooner. Sure, he was ecstatic nobody could see him now, but this was just like night and day. The shovel-like shape of his new nose and the neck muscles made for doing exactly this made the digging so much easier, the cool dirt offered some welcome relief, his inner pig was squealing with elation inside to lend him a sense of rightness about it (albeit an unsettling one), and hey, free grub. Yes, he'd felt briefly conflicted after eating the dirty roots without thinking about it, but, well, done was done, he hadn't been completely full, and it would have been a hassle to pull them up, so he rationalized his way past that one. That they'd been tastier than they ought hadn't hurt.

                                                        In this way, Max wound his way through the rest of the holes in about half the time and a quarter the effort he had on the others. He was probably blushing furiously, but there was no way of knowing with all that dirt on his face. 'That... wasn't so bad, really.' If he wound up needing to every now and then, he wasn't sure he would mind. Just so long as nobody ever found out, of course. It didn't even concern him that he was pretty okay with rooting in the dirt like an animal: it seemed exhaustion had finally left him too tired to care. As though to make this case, after he'd moved away from the planted seeds to check up on his fruit, an ear flopped at a rumbling sound and he lazily turned his head in time to catch his gardening sprout a new little orchard. Max gave no reaction, other than to smile and decide the base of the largest and most central tree looked like a good place for nap. The pig curled up beside it and was out like a light.

As if someone was taking great care of the wood, it had a sheen to it, which looked weird on a ranch. He tried and pulled the first door, but it wouldn't bulge. He heard a snort, which came from the lock. "Try as long as you might, but you can only enter where is your right!" Nate sighed. A riddle, again? "So what, I need a permission to come in here?" The lock didn't answer but... the Goat could swear that the knob just....shrugged. "Fine! I'll find my spot, if that's what you're saying!" He stepped back, and looked under his hooves when he heard a soft crunching sound. He had stepped in...grain? It seemed that...it was coming from the building. So this place was a granary, huh? He looked around him, and found that the Rooster was watching him from afar. Oh... So, this place was the Rooster's "apartments", it seemed. Nate waved at him, and got away from the building.

                                                            It sounded like a trap. Or rather, something destined to lure them over, to separate them, and to appease their anguish and their dreams of escaping. But...right now, Nate wanted only one thing, and it was comfort. Besides...he seemed that he was back to being part human now. Surely, he could afford this respite. And thus, he explored the ranch. He passed a few outhouses, including one that strongly smelled of milk, a kennel, and a locked fence that seemed to guard the entrance to a big pond of mud. It made him think of Max, and he bit his lips, hoping that the kid was okay. And...he also hoped that he was still there, and he didn't escape.
                                                            He was puzzled when he finally found a door that opened smoothly for him. When he opened it, his nostrils were assaulted by a very strong and earthy aroma : it smelled of wine. In fact, once his eyes were accustomed to the darkness of the building, he could see that it was a whole winery, located in some sort of warehouse. Nate didn't know a lot about wine, as he was more of a beer guy, but it looked like it had the whole package: barrels, bottling line, tanks... Why is this my building? This isn't very goat-like... He entered the small factory, and made a tour of his building. It seemed like the production was already well on its way, as there was already a lot of filled bottles, without a label, however. In the back of the winery, there was another door, which led to a small outdoor vineyard. Weird. How did he not see it from the ranch outside? The grapes seemed fine, and very juicy. He picked one, and popped it in his mouth. It was an explosion of flavor. Oh man, it's so good! He took another bunch, and quickly devoured them, not caring if the juices were staining his face and goatee. The aroma was so rich and fruity! He almost went for another grape, but he stopped himself : he needed to keep some for later. Besides, how could the winery function without fruit? He went back to the building, still entranced by the smell... He needed to taste it. It was his building right? And even if he didn't bothered to lock the door behind him, it was in his charge. But...how could he help make the wine if he didn't even know how good it was? Surely, no one would miss a bottle...

                                                            And that's how, after a while, Nate had emptied his third bottle. He was drunk, with spots of red wine on his body and fur, laughing merrily and dancing and playing jaunty tunes on top of a barrel with his flute. Weirdly enough, it seemed that his music was even better than before, and the melody could be heard in the entire ranch. Wine and Music, was it his new life? For now, he didn't care. All he wanted was to drink more of this delicious wine.
