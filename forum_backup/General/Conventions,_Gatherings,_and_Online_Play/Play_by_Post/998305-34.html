                                So a couple very, very interesting things happened this turn. Firstly, on a chance die the Amalgama escape driver managed to maintain control through the earthquake, good for them! Secondly, I couldn't let Mark 5 completely obliterate a significant portion of the Forks (because that would have been very, very bad) and I'm loathe to undercut the first really grand display of alchemy we've seen out of Mark 5, but I had an ace in my back pocket that allowed this to resolve in a relatively peaceful but still oh shit kind of way. Flux Distillations are nasty.

                                Wormwood You are understanding correctly. With three Pyros in Motus you can have Motus 1, 2, and 3 active simultaneously, and if some of the Distillations are Reflexive you can activate multiple Distillations at once. The same with Unconquered as well. If a Promethean cuts loose (I mean really cuts loose, like you did just now and like Earth has done in the past with Arc) not a hell of a lot can stand in their way. Mark 5 absolutely would have destroyed vehicles and levelled buildings over the course of eleven turns of 3L damage per turn, and likely would have killed hundreds of people on top of it. That would have been a pretty serious step backwards.

                                The thing about Promethean is that it is specifically certain Refinements with a predilection for massive destruction of property, notably Furies, Titans, and Hundred-Handed. To a lesser extent Savants, Sentries and Light-Bringers can do some serious damage as well, but nothing like a Fury, Titan, or Hundred-Handed One. On a personal level, Pariahs, Mimics, and Cathars are horrifyingly devastating, but for very different reasons. Adonis, if he was so inclined, could quite probably tear apart whole social groups, or destroy individual people in particular. If he was a Cathar, even more so.
