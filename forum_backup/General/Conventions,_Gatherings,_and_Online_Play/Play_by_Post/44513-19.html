            Hyuga: That action you did IC was a flurry and you didn't include penalties. Since you took 3 actions they would be at -3, -4, and -5 in order. I am going to guess they are in the order listed.

            Your Attack is Dex 2 + Melee 3 + Accuracy 3 + Stunt 2 and is DV -1
            Your Feat of Strength is Strength 4 + Athletics 2 is DV -1 plus subtract 2 dice due to being distracted by doing it in combat (Its also speed five which sets the speed for everything else)
            You Palm thrust is a martial arts attack Dex 2 + MA 2 - Offhand 1 and is DV -1

            So:

            1. 3 successes
            2. 0 dice due to combat and multiple action penalties
            3. 0 dice due to multiple action penalties

            Also, your DV is 4, Protection of Celestial Bliss means your own attacks don't penalize DV but the Feat of Strength wasn't an attack.

            Note: All this is assuming that you activated PoCB before combat, because that would also be an action in the flurry and impose a higher penalty.

                    Originally posted by CitanHyuga View Post
                    My first action should be at full dice though and each one after with a penalty one higher than the last right? Why are you starting with -3?
                    Actually, we were doing it wrong, back then.

                    Originally posted by Exalted 2e Core, p124-125
                    Whatever the situation, characters who take multiple actions suffer a special form of internal penalty. The first action loses dice equal to the number of actions attempted, with each successive action cumulatively increasing the penalty by one die. Therefore, a character taking three actions would be at -3 dice to the first action, -4 to the second and -5 to the third.
                    In combat, multiple actions are extremely common and known as flurries.

                        For why it started at -3 and worked its way down:
                        Originally posted by Core p. 124-125
                        The first action loses dice equal to the number of actions attempted, with each successive action cumulatively increasing the penalty by one die. Therefore, a character taking three actions would be at -3 dice to the first action, -4 to the second and -5 to the third.
                        For why the stunts don't matter for the other two actions:
                        Originally posted by Core p. 121
                        If a character ever has a dice pool of zero dice or less after applying all bonuses and does not benefit from bonus successes, she cannot even attempt that action.
                        The penalties reduce your base pool to zero so you cannot attempt it and so cannot add stunts on top of actions you cannot take.

                        Edit: But you could channel a point of WP for an auto success or to channel a virtue, that would raise your pools into the positive. (Though I don't know if you can edit it in after the fact)

                                        One, The Sorcery Background is above, as Hyuga wrote. It was a 1E background, and never translated up to 2E, though my groups always tended to allow it.
