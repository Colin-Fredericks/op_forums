            Ragnar
            Before replying, I wanted to remind you of a few things. First, just a reminder that Concentration Yantra can only be used on spells with duration longer than 1 turn. Rather than making you re-roll and taking extra time to repost, I will cancel the potency boost for the third question and remove the bonus from Concentration, leaving your dice pool identical and giving you answers to the first two questions.

            Secondly, questions asked as part of Divination need to be worded in such a way that they involve the subject's future. So you can ask "Where will we find this object?", but not "Where is the object located?" It's used to predict the future, not just give you Storyteller insight. If worded creatively, you can figure out a lot of things, but you need to ask them about the future. I will assume for your second question that you worded it along the lines of "Where will we find the source of this disturbance?". For future posts, please adjust accordingly.

            Let me know if you have any problems with these changes.


            The stream of Time surrounding you briefly flows in the opposite direction of your previous spell, this time summoning glimpses of the future rather than the past. Flickers of images dance through your mind faster than thought, but you catch enough to find the answers you seek. A ghost quickened by the energy in this place will fight to defend her home or her child. The source will be found in her room.
