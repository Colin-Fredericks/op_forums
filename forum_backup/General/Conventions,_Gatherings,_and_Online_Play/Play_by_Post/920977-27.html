                        Ok. I reviewed the rules and tried to skim through some posts online to get a better grasp on the rules for sympathetic casting. I think we are both right and both wrong about different things. I think I'm mistaken about the range and sympathy, because I'm probably thinking back to how things worked in 1E.

                        Because Binder has cast spells on Moonstone and communicated with each other, I'll say they have a Weak sympathetic connection together. The hair can be used as a symbolic sympathetic Yantra, and it also has a Strong connection to Moonstone.

                        So, you were right about the fact that the Sympathetic Range attainment uses your sympathetic connection (Weak, currently). You can use Borrowed Threads to steal the Strong sympathetic connection from the hair. It is Withstood at 3, so to be able to transfer a single sympathetic connection, you need a net Potency of 1 (so base Potency 4). I'll say that you can make those repeated rolls to get it to work, as you originally put in your IC post. As you wrote, this means it lasts for 2 turns, and you use one of them up by attempting (and failing) your spell.

                        However, I was right about the reaches. Casting on someone whom you are viewing remotely is different than casting on them sympathetically. Viewing remotely would include something like trying to trigger a heart attack in someone that you're currently watching on a live news channel. You don't need any sympathetic connection or sympathetic Yantra to cast that spell. For example, you could open a Scrying window at Moonstone's location, and then cast something on Phoenix by using 2 reach to boost from touch range to remote viewing range, as long as you can see him through the Scrying window. You wouldn't need any sympathetic Yantra, nor would you need to have any sympathetic connection to Phoenix (although you have a Weak one with him as well).

                        Once you get Space 3, you can use Web-Weaver to temporarily boost sympathetic connections, so you could just directly enhance your own Weak connection to a stronger one. But in the meantime, borrowing one will have to suffice.

                        Based on this, I think nothing changes. You have 1 turn left to attempt a spell on Moonstone with a Strong connection. What you do in your next turn, however, will likely be impacted by what's about to happen.

                                    On p. 112, under common reach effects:
                                    • Move the spell from touch/self to sensory range
                                    • If at sensory range, allow the mage to cast on a subject she is viewing remotely

                                    So, one reach increases from touch to sensory range. Sensory range would be casting at someone you can physically see, but might be a fair distance away -- like half a mile or something, if it's light out and you have clear line of sight. Remote viewing, as I mentioned earlier, would be like targeting someone you're talking to via Skype/Facetime, watching on live television, or viewing through a Scrying window. Casting on someone remotely requires 2 reach, but no mana.

                                    As you quoted, casting sympathetically only requires a sensory range. It thus needs 1 reach, and 1 mana.
