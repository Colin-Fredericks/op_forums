                Second Chances DArchon5 squidheadjax - Just a reminder in case it was lost in the past few pages, but when you have time, please use the following format to pitch your character concept.

                Concept: Summarize your character concept in one phrase or short sentence.
                Sympathetic Name:
                Shadow Name:
                Path:
                Order:
                Legacy:                 (If you have one in mind)
                Virtue:
                Vice:
                Background: Please provide a brief narrative summary of the character concept, taking into consideration how your background may fit into the greater Mysteries described in the introductory post in this thread. You can also include information about the character's Awakening.

                You can go back and edit your post later to add more details. At the very least, if you have an idea on which Path(s) most interests you, that would be helpful to know for the other players working on character creation. I'm not opposed to having multiple players of the same Path, but I do think the cabal and the game would benefit if most/all of the Paths were covered, or at least most of the Arcana.
