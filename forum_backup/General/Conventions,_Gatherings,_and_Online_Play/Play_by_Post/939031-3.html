                            I don't see any rules in the MtAw 2E book about how communication with your familiar works, at least in terms of your ability to make requests at a distance. I do like the idea of an empathic bond, so you can feel its emotion and send your own emotions out.                             Dr. Luck
                            Ok, a few things about your post here.

                            First, from my reading of Fate spells and how that magic works, even the stronger Fate spells don't just automatically let you do what you want. They influence chance so much that it can seem like you can do whatever you want, but at your current mastery of that Arcanum you can't create your own fate. Make sure you give me a chance as the Storyteller to tell you what happens in response to your spells and actions. Because you could use Fate 3 to pretty easily give yourself the rote quality on any mundane roll you want, including driving, I'll skip the roll in this case. But especially since you don't have training in Drive, your car is poor quality, and you're in a big hurry, there could be a potential chance for something to go wrong, even with the rote quality on that roll.

                            Second, you can't use your Charmed condition from Exceptional Luck to reduce someone else's damage, only your own. You're the one Charmed, not them. In addition, Fate magic really can only be used to affect present or future actions; interacting with something from the past requires Time magic. To undo, prevent, or mitigate the effects of what you're about to see, you would need more than the Ruling practice of Time.

                            Third, because you're driving in a different vehicle than most of the others (I think a lot of you arrived separately), you won't be able to affect them with your magic or offer boons unless you can cast on them sympathetically or at sensory range. They could use their 9-again or Charmed boons to affect their own rolls, but I don't think anyone has done that yet.

                            Does this make sense? I'm trying to be fair and allow you to be awesome (I think mages should be able to feel awesome), but still staying within the rules.

                            On an unrelated note, don't forget that spells cast on you by someone else do not affect your own spell control in 2nd edition. You have Ephemeral Shield and Body Control listed under your active spells, but you're not in control of those, so no need to use Reach to cast additional spells because you're not at your spell limit yet.

                                    Dr. Luck
                                    I know we're not currently in initiative, but can you try to keep your posts to one or two actions at a time and then let me respond to give you more information? Otherwise, you can write posts like this one and start rolling for actions that may not end up happening. Casting a few spells in a row when you're not in combat is generally fine, because you're not in a time crunch, but some of the actions you're describing (e.g., examining the woman's body) may or may not be possible depending on the results of a previous action (getting into the house).

                                    If you were trying to break into a mage's sanctum, for example, you can't just list all of your actions in order and safely assume they'll go off without a hitch. Unexpected defenses can cause a lot of grief for you.

                                    Also, just wanted to repeat what I said earlier about how Fate magic works. It can make your mundane actions rolls incredibly likely to succeed, but you still need some kind of action -- you can't make just anything happen by making a spell casting roll, at least not at your current Arcana mastery or with Fate magic. Just because there is a degree of chance to many actions doesn't mean that those actions themselves are directly under the Fate Arcanum's purview.
