            Louis Riel Bridge

            Several hours pass in the frozen dirt beneath the bridge. Mark 5 and Scout discuss strategy. Earth dreams and meditates. Eventually, Satin and Solomon return from their walk, the elder Nepri visibly limping and nursing an injury, the Riven looking regretful but far more relaxed than before, the haze of Torment gone from them. They have settled back into the androgynous form that is most recognizable as them. Solomon takes hold of the generator again, breathing a sigh of relief as mummified flesh knits itself back together. This is not terribly relevant to Earth, however, who is dreaming their way into Azothic Memory, forcing an Elpis Vision to come upon them. And it does so magnificently.

            Earth dreams of endless fields of rolling banks of ash, a field of dark grey and stark white. There is a river of liquid mercury lapping at the ashen shore, and above the sky is a mandala of rolling flames, golden and white predominant, but shot through with cherry reds, royal indigos, verdant greens. Strange formations in the sky formed of smoky cloud and lightning whirl, the symbols for the alchemical metals. The symbols of the Refinements. The black-handed figure of white flame is waiting for Earth. They walk towards them, and where they step flowers and vines covered in thorns sprout forth from the ash. They point with their hand of black flame out into the middle distance, where Earth can see a stumbling porcelain figure, made so thin that you can see the blood that the hollow vessel has been filled with. Nearby, an ashen figure, looming and menacing, a coat of white ash and flickering white flames surrounding a solid black interior, charges towards them at a full sprint. Earth gathers lightning, but the manifestation of Azoth stays their hand, and the two figures freeze just before they collide.

            There are no words, there never are, but there is something there that the storm clouds and wild winds that always surrounded Earth kept them from hearing before. The sound of breathing, light and gentle, like someone in restful repose, and then that breathing began to harmonize with the rush of the wind, with the crackle of flame, with the flow of the river. The universe vibrated in anticipation of the Word, the thing before Creation, the thing before Earth was born. But it would not come.

            Earth can see a stout woman in the distance, starring at the swirling corona of flames in the heavens, as ashen beasts of silver and shadow looking vaguely like the creature Satin had transformed into circle her. They stand defiant, shot through with power, shot through with... Iron.

            Amalgama LTD

            It doesn't take long for the van to reach the Amalgama building. The doors stay closed for a while, but the Captain emerges, careful to keep herself between whoever was outside the van and the contents of its interior. Even through the sound-proofed doors, Adonis can hear the heated argument.

            "What in the fuck do you think you were doing? You are a goddamn biomedical corporation, not a paramilitary group! We were told you had finally gone to Oodena to retrieve that resource stockpile you keep saying you intended to get at, but the Government of Canada sure as shit didn't authorize the use of high explosives to retrieve it! You do know the Forks is one of the biggest tourist destinations in the city, right? People are already suggesting terrorism! The blast was big enough to simulate an earthquake and risk damages to the Canadian Museum for Human Rights. What in the hell did you use? Whatever this universal solvent you keep talking about is, I seriously doubt it warrants underground blasting at that magnitude."

            "Look, if you would like to take it up with my superiors, you can, but then you would have to eventually call up the chain of command to Project Magnet."

            The room suddenly goes dead quiet, even the machinery operating in the loading bay seems to be silent. The person behind the first voice sighs.

            "This facility is intended, first and foremost, for the advancement of medical technology. Your universal catalyst and solvent you people keep talking about had better deliver, because letting you run free like this? Doing whatever the fuck you want? I'd say it definitely counts as breaking the Hippocratic Oath. Thin ice. Thin. Ice."

            The rest of the processing goes more or less without incident. Tom eventually panics, the stress of captivity and the lack of his anchoring object leading to him flaring with immense amounts of Pyros in an attempt to flee, but he very quickly runs out and his manacles still hold firm. Though a Wasteland should have formed from that, it didn't. The facility seems immune to it. Isla stays very quiet, aping what she thinks children should be in an effort to appear harmless, but the people in the hazmat suits appear unmoved. They are stripped of their clothing and belongings, which are stored in small crates marked "Psychological Evaluation" and given simple undyed jumpsuits to wear. The four Pilgrims are separated, put into different cells which each have just enough room for a mostly upright system of restraint and a technician or two to step in and take samples. Adonis is strapped down, hands and feet lashed with manacles, electrodes attached to his head, and two IVs inserted into his arm, except they more strongly resemble siphons to extract material than anything else.

            Eventually, a man in a lab coat marked with the Amalgama logo walks up and taps on the thick glass on the door to the Muse's tiny cell. The doctor is wearing a worn polo shirt under the lab coat, his dark curly hair cropped close to his head, small glasses sitting on a pronounced nose, smiling in a way carefully honed by years of practice in bedside manner to be reassuring.

            "Do you know what you are?"

                        Amalgama, LTD

                        The doctor blinks, frowning, and narrows his eyes ever so slightly. They know something is wrong, but they cannot resist. His face relaxes, with a touch of an upturned smile and the beginning of a blush. They adjust their name tag a little, pulling up a chair from the side just out of view. "I do, actually. I'm a doctor. Doctor Roman. An admittedly unusual doctor, but a doctor nonetheless." He sits himself down, and clicks open a pen to start writing.

                        "I ended up stumbling across a chemical process that taps into what we here at the company labs call a 'universal catalyst'. A substance which can trigger any sort of complex chemical reaction you can imagine. What totipotent stem cells are to the human body, this universal solvent, what old alchemical texts calls Pyros or the Divine Fire, is to chemistry. It can accomplish incredible things, but its effects are... transitory at best. A stimulant incorporating the universal catalyst can elevate someone in a combat situation far above even the limits of humanity, but only for the span of an hour or two at most. Our competitors can produce permanent gains, and the results are stable instead of unpredictable. In order to produce permanent effects, we require a solvent derived from the universal catalyst that 'fixes' results. But it is extremely rare. The only place it is consistently found is in the flesh of homunculi, such as yourself."

                        He adjust his glasses ever so slightly, and continues in a pleasant enough tone

                        "A homunculus is a replica human, most easily constructed from human tissue, but it is not a necessary requirement. Your biology, your thought process, everything is being sustained by the universal catalyst. You are, what, a couple years old at most? Ever wonder how you can speak? How you can walk? The catalyst has harnessed pre-existing neural pathways and then continued to grow them! Do you know how remarkable that is? Scans indicate you are developing new neural pathways daily, even though your host body's brain should be done producing new neurons. Using the catalyst in your veins is instinctual for you, chemical processes human researchers must spend months or years learning and developing the proper compounds for is just a matter of reflex and state of mind. Mixing the catalyst with the solvent produces even more exceptional effects, permanently affixing certain reactions as part of your peripheral nervous system, or producing wondrous objects that are difficult, or even impossible, to imitate. Regrettably, the only means we know of producing more of this solvent is by exposing homunculi to intense heat, retrieving the solvent from the ashes of the catalyst."

                        The doctor does look upset at this, at which point you notice a surgically small blowtorch mounted near one of your arms. They will send you into Torment repeatedly and reap the rewards of the Vitriol that will slowly accumulate.

                        "Fortunately, we are aware of the horrible psychologically scarring side effects, but we know of how to alleviate them. Which is why I am here; to perform a full psychological profile of you."

                                            Amalgama, LTD

                                            Outside the facility the alchemical corporation shares with Government of Canada researchers, seven Pilgrims huddle up against the frosty bricks of a university building. This is Lockhart Hall of the University of Winnipeg, and it is early in the morning after the last day of exams. The moon is starting to set, the wondrous silver glow fading into leaden darkness. The temperature drops as the shadows lengthen, the void that is a Winnipeg Night ready to swallow them whole. Joining Earth, Scout, and Mark 5 is Satin, Solomon, the Scarlet Woman and curiously enough, Joshua. The black-clad Named hangs back, aware that he isn't terribly well-liked at the moment, but he is ready to leap into danger if it is strictly necessary. It isn't really proper to call the facility "a" building, since it is a small, tidy complex. Two buildings that seem rather modest compared to the rambling concrete and brick structure of the university. The one with less windows and direct access to the street is probably the lab. Beneath dim streetlights, the building glows with the not-quite-purple of the tinted glass that forms the buildings' exteriors. According to the blueprints, the labs are nearest to the loading docks, which have street access. The street is dead quiet, the occasional car passing up or down Ellice, the occasional shipping truck following after them, making its way towards the airport several miles down the road.

                                            Joshua eagerly steps forward to address Scout with an earnest grin "I will set the fire, and lure anyone that comes after me. I have the alchemy to make it work."

                                            Satin breathes deeply and warps, changing shape into a silvery raven that alights on Earth's shoulder. The raven quorks a couple of times, clearing its throat, and then Satin's voice emerges, raspier than normal, with visible effort, each word spoken slowly for clarity's sake. "I am quicker and less noticeable in this form. Can cover more ground. And ravens can still speak."

Took much undue psychological stress is dangerous to the subject and the researcher." He adjusts his glasses, clicks his pen a couple of times, and makes a few notes.

                                            "I have a few customary questions to begin. First; who made you? Second; what do you remember of who you were made from? Third; what do you know about the Great Work, Magnum Opus, or New Dawn?"
