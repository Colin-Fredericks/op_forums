                                                    I agree with Wonderandawe, although I've never been in a game that lasted long enough for us to get to that point. Usually, my group's games had us wander around, and begin to get an agenda, and then circumstances would end it there.

                                                    I find that having all these swords over your head can be helpful for STs so that it's not just "Kobolds invade, wat do?" and allows for a variety of enemies with the opportunity for a circle to be able to not only handle them, but use unconventional tactics if they want to. Granted, I personally feel that certain ones are blown out of proportion and others are underutilized, but I can't help the general bad blood that seems to run through the fandom regarding them.

                                                    (In case I wasn't entirely clear, I mean the Reclamation is blown out of proportion. Seriously, five days through a desert that hates you isn't easy, you guys. Especially when Prophet of One-Shot Kill can pop in from a shadowland with an army of ghosts, kill a village and use that to expand the shadowland further. And can GSPs do anything similar? Sure, especially if they want a constant klaxon going off at their hub of operations going "A DEMON THING IS HAPPENING HERE, SEND A WYLD HUNT TO FIGHT BADDIES" to all the Sidereals even casually looking at the Loom. And while I'm usually not a fan of "Surprise sneak attack behind a teacup" Sidereals, I find it hard to justify not having at least some resistance show up when they start pulling that stuff.

                                                    I suppose it was because late 2e, infernals were the new hotness, and the Ink Monkeys were only to happy to provide. I just find it amusing that the guys who gave us the "Let's give Solars a way to do Gilgamesh's thing from Fate/Stay Night" charm and "Here's a way your Lunar can do all that DBT stuff without being an icky furry even when you have pretty much total control over their appearance," knack also say," Nuh-uh, man, Reclamation can't happen, 2e Infernals are kill, Solars are getting weaker, Everything is a MISTERY now.")

                                                    As an aside, I'd like to say that I think the Legend of the Titans is an amazing piece of fluff, and I adore their setting works. Just that I feel like a lot of what is complained about 2e was exacerbated by them, is all.

                                                    Sorry, I'll get off my soapbox now.
