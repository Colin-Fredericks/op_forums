'It... wouldn't even be the most disgusting thing I've eaten or done today...' Still, while his eyes locked on them, he couldn't quite bring himself to do it. Not at the moment, at least. Thankfully. So he tried a different tactic -- though Nate's question did interest him. "I... Is the feet licking really in your best interests, though?" He smiled, back in wheedle mode.
