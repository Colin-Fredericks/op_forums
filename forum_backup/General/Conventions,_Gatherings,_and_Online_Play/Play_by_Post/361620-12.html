                                            Fairly close. To use some modern terms:

                                            The Harvest God Priesthood
                                            This is both where we are funding the scheme and where the money will come at the end. We use the Inventors and some pageantry with the god to drum up interest for our Initial Public Offering of Stock in the venture. (Or, since those don't exist yet, membership). Each membership comes with a percentage, but we are going to oversell such that if they all got together and counted their percentages would come to over 100%. This is not strictly necessary, but we need a lot of money for the whole thing.

                                            Stern Order of Slave Overseers
                                            They are the Shill for the Priesthood. If all the slaves are worked to death or uselessness, as the Slave Owners think the Stern Order will leave them, then the priesthood will need new slaves for the next harvest. So the Owners will agree to sell them to the priesthood because they don't expect to get any more value out of them and to have a repeat client. The Owners think they are taking advantage of the priesthoods bleeding hearts rather than thinking long term about reusing the slaves.

                                            Inventors
                                            Another shill for the priesthood, they build confidence in their methods which then translates to the priesthood when we start selling stock. We don't actually have to show the methods will fail to start the panic, we could just spread rumors that the priesthood has more memberships outstanding than its revenue. (Or that it promised all the harvest to the slave owners) They are also going to have the real devices (and probably sorcery) to produce the yields we need to actually pay all these people back.

                                            Insurance Sellers
                                            These people appear to have nothing to do with the priesthood. They sell insurance to all the members, to be paid if the priesthood fails to deliver its promised monies. Since we don't intend to allow that, the members are paying us money at a steady rate with which to cover our expenses and the payments to members. Essentially, we are paying their promised money with their own money.

                                            For slight clarification, we are not paying the Slave Owners ALL of the harvest for the slaves, just negotiating something like "Buy 5 slaves per ton of wheat, to be paid at harvest time out of our share of the wheat."

                                            We will probably retain a lot of the slaves as actual employees, but we may need to find new income since we are basically paying them with freedom now.
