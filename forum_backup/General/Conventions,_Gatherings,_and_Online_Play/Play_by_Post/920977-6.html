                                        Oooooook.

                                        After reviewing all of the submissions repeatedly today, and taking into consideration all of the info that everyone provided, I have a final list of characters I would like to invite to play in the game. I want to preface this by saying that there were SO many good submissions. This wasn't easy for me. Obviously I would rather give everyone the chance to play, but I just don't think it's feasible for a single cabal.

                                        Due to the strength and overall quality of these submissions, I decided to keep 6 players:
                                        1. Beans - Dr. Asphodel, Moros theoretical physicist
                                        2. Caladriu - Moonstone, Mastigos Sleepwalker-turned-mage
                                        3. Kvitebjørn Kong Valemon - Lynx, Thyrsus animist/college student
                                        4. crapcarp - Phoenix, Obrimos Hunter-turned-mage
                                        5. Haberdasher - Ragnar, Acanthus Renaissance man
                                        6. MagicSwordsman - Binder, Mastigos bookstore owner/family man

                                        Having a cabal of 6 PCs will potentially introduce some challenges for me as a ST, so I'll ask in advance again for your patience as I tackle that learning curve. However, it also means you can potentially split up during certain story points or scenes, which gives me some neat options for adding conflict to the game and offering unique roleplay opportunities.

                                        As long as the 6 of you are good with this final selection, I will post more tonight or tomorrow with info about what I'd like to do next to get the game up and running!

                                        To the other 5 players who submitted characters: I really enjoyed your submissions. Once this game gets underway, depending on how it goes and how much free time I have, I might be able to create a separate game for you and ST that one as well. Alternatively, if any of the active players from this game drop out or need to leave the game for whatever reason, I would be looking at this list next to see if anyone wants to join the full game. If anyone has further questions or concerns, please send me a private message.

                                        Really looking to getting the show on the road with this! Thank you to everyone for your patience.

                                        My Games

                                            Here are some additional ideas I had in mind while reviewing the PCs, now that the party has been selected:

                                            Caladriu - The rest of the party includes another Acanthus and another Mastigos. Consequently, I think you should pick whichever Path would be the most fun for you to play. Just let me know what you think before you start character creation.

                                            Beans - I would suggest that we say that Vo has been asked to teach the occasional "first-year seminar" kind of college class, one geared towards freshman and transfer students to help orient them to the college experience and to make sure they have at least basic knowledge of the academic skills expected of incoming freshmen. Vo may see this as a frustrating chore and waste of her academic skills, or maybe she loves teaching new students and stepping outside of her academic specialty.

                                            Kvitebjørn Kong Valemon - I think we should make Oscar a student at the University of Chicago. It makes a lot more sense to me that an international student would apply and go there. You can still live in La Grange and commute, just like Vo (if she in fact decides to continue pursuing her academic career after Awakening). And we can say that you took/are taking Vo's first-year seminar class, so you know her. The degree to which you know her, and how hard you would have worked during that class, would be up to you.

                                            crapcarp - By having the rest of your Hunter cell suddenly die at the moment of your Awakening, we might be missing out on a really prime opportunity for plot hooks! I have 2 alternate suggestions for tweaking this. First, we could say that the other Hunters in your cell were all severely injured or maimed, mentally traumatized, or killed, but not all of them died. Or, I'd like you to add that your Hunter cell worked with another one in the Chicagoland area, and they've been trying to figure out what precisely happened to you after the Awakening event. Suffice to say that they're very curious about the things they've been able to figure out so far, and they're looking for you.

                                            Haberdasher - How would you feel about making Ragnar a grad student at the University of Chicago? Per Google, they have Ph.D. program in Comparative Literature, or one in English Language and Literature. You could have run into Vo and/or Oscar at some point during a campus event, possibly a LGBT-related event or guest speaker seminar.

                                            MagicSwordsman - Your character has some very unique facets to his background that I am really interested in exploring with you. First, you're the only character who seems to be in a committed relationship or with kids. You're also the only hetero member of the cabal. The way Marlin feels about the LGBT community may influence how easy it is for him to come together with the others to form a cabal. If this feels forced for you, or if you don't have any particular reason for Marlin to meet up with the others, we can also say that he has met some of them (probably Ragnar at least) at a guest author seminar hosted by the University of Chicago.

                                            My Games
                                            Mage 2E: The Supernal Suburbia (IC / OOC) - Storyteller
