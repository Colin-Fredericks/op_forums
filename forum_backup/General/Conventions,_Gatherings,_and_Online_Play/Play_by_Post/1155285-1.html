                It has been an intense night for Cassidy. The hour was late, she witnessed the butchered corpse of a priest, allowed her mind to be touched by Hell for answers and had to deal with a very complicated, explosive situation. Having Micheal covered in blood jumping upon her like a madman was more than she could have handled- she shrieked, her precious notebook falling on the floor, and recoiled into a position behind Khalil, putting him between herself and Micheal. It took her a moment to regain her composure and free herself from the infernal touch on her mind, but it was one moment too late. Suddenly, anger filled her heart- on herself for acting like a little child, on Micheal for putting her in that position, on the Dean for sending her to that damned investigation, on the Gabby for murdering people and on the priest for getting murdered. She gave an harsh, hateful look at Micheal and hissed the last sentence from her vision.

                "No more confessions he shall speak tonight"

                                        Stephanie stares unfeelingly at Julius' corpse. Her fingers reach unconsciously between the buttons of her coat and grasp the otherworldly fabric of her Lemurian armor.

                                        Then she says: "Liar. No- just a fool. Ignoramus!"

                                        She paces, extremely Not Mad.

                                        "Impossible. Meaningless scribbles. God's hooks, Cass can't even handle her own infernal blood! A vision spoken by Lucifer's own blackened lips, drawn by a whelp with milk still on her teeth- and you think it's true as Scripture! We're in a schoolhouse, for godsake- use your reason! My little-"

                                        Her voice catches. "th-the little bird couldn't knock a statue down, infernal whispers or none! She hasn't strayed that far from the nest! Listen to yourselves: a bloody corpse and a whiff of brimstone, and you go half-mad!"

                                        She fingers her knife with a trembling hand. "You think I can't keep a firm hand on my woman?"

                                        Thinking about the estrangement through the lens of the Hunt is about the first time she's spoken of it in front of the cell. Losing Gabby to demonic forces is about the worst fate she can imagine.

                                        "The killer's probably after her next, and here we are casting aspersions- enough with it! We'll track her down first and catch the devil flat-footed. Come on, then!"

                                        Steph sheathes her dagger and turns to storm out of the room. Gabby's been around tonight, she can't be that far.

                                                "We cannot stand by when the Lucifuge and its kin are under attack. Save Gabby if you can, but we must put this demon down. I had planned to to do this after you had successfully closed this investigation, but now it seems you need all of the power at your disposal to survive. Steph, Micheal, you have earned the right to unlock deeper secrets from your blood. Please bear with me, I will make the ritual as expeditious as I can."

                                                Normally unleashing a new endowment is a ritual filled with pomp for Arkham's Lucifuge. There is none of that here. The Dean gathers a crucible of sulphur and quicksilver, spills his blood in it, adds Steph and Micheal's blood before lighting the entire thing on fire. The entire room fills with the rank smell of flesh and brimstone. Your eyes water and you nearly wretch, but Steph and Micheal are left feeling the rush of power in their veins.
