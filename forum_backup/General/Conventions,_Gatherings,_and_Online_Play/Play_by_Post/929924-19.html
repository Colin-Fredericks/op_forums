    Ragnar walks over to the fallen man and kneels down beside him. He has no idea what's wrong with him. He seemed a little roughed up, but did he faint from nerves or injuries. His brows furrow as he considers the question before him, and whether he has the tools for the job. Fate and Time won't do it. I need Life. He has only touched Life once, when he opened himself to it it through his Sight. He reaches out for it again, using the same logic he did when he had used previously. Just as all things are bound to each other by the threads of fate, tugging on each other and altering the trajectory of their probabilities... all living things are connected to each other through trophic interactions, exchanging energy and chemicals through the endless cycle of life and regeneration.

    He places his hand on the man's chest. And he opens himself to magic, and turns his attention to Life, an art as fickle and mutable as Fate. He begins lightly drawing the flat of his knife over the man's body, tracing the path of his veins and arteries to and from his heart.

    Ragnar attempts to cast Analyze Life (Life 1). He has one free Reach, and uses it to reduce casting time to Instant. He uses his knife as a Path Tool yantra. He pays one Mana for a common Arcana spell.

    Gnosis 1 + Life 1+ Path Tool 1 = 3 dice -> 0 successes.


    Unfortunately, it doesn't seem to work, and the spell eludes him. Ragnar shakes his head in frustration.

    "Lynx, wanna take a look at him for me? I tried myself, but I lost the spell." He looks at the others: the younger man, Phoenix; and the Vietnamese man, Moonstone. "I'm Ragnar. The big guy over there is Lynx, and this guy here is Mathias."

        Moonstone

        Whatever nightmarish thing (or nothing) Moonstone sees while gazing slack-jawed into the future in his magic mirror, his reverie (and Mage Sight) breaks when Phoenix pulls the mirror away from his face.

        "Hey buddy?"

        He shakes his head and confusedly meets Phoenix's smile, half sitting up, still distracted. "Huh? Oh! I'm so-"

        Hard surprise smack to the face. Deadly serious look. "Don't ever do that again."

        Moonstone doesn't respond, just puts his hand on his stinging cheek and stares at Phoenix, deer-in-headlights style. He keeps forgetting that his delusions aren't the same as reality. He wasn't a comrade-in-arms to him just now; he almost got Phoenix and the others killed thanks to his utter incompetence...not that he remembers what actually triggered the Abyssal intrusion beyond the portal, only that it was unequivocally his own fault. He's been nothing but a burden. As always, he had it coming. It's like Hakim never left!

        That's the second Divination question answered, at least. Vivid fantasies well up in his mind about revenge and abandonment and violence, as is his mind's usual response to perceived betrayal on a low day, but they're just thoughts, just passing clouds. Remember the meditation. Close your eyes and count them and let them pass you by. Thoughts don't have to come true, even if you're a mage. He puts his mirror down and mumbles: "uh- sorry. glad you made it."

        He rises to his feet while Phoenix makes introductions, dusts himself off, picks up the staff again and rests on it. He's real quiet, but waves a little at Ragnar. "Well-met by daylight, proud Ragnar. You three were a godsend."

        He points the staff at Phoenix and wearily explains: "Nameless." Points to the unconscious Mr. Marlin Matheson. "Nameless." Points his own thumb at himself. "Arrow." Gestures out at Ragnar, Lynx and Mathias. "Did you also get caught in the Highstorm?"

        Rolling Int 1 + Politics 3 (Chicago Consilium) in advance of the conversation. Does "Fate mage named Mathias" remind Moonstone of any trivia or rumors? 5 dice: 1 success.
