                        Moonstone
                        You have seen these faces before and, as an Arrow Sleepwalker retainer, had to quickly learn how to navigate the political climate of the Consilium. You are surprised to see Atlas' face among the Councilors for two reasons.

                        First, until last week, he had been serving as Hierarch of this Consilium, a role usually filled by the same individual until their death except in unusual circumstances. The news you've heard has all been focused on how Cielo assumed the role of Hierarch; you didn't actually know what had happened to Atlas. Clearly, few mages in attendance of this meeting knew about this switch, either.

                        Secondly, as far as you knew, the Obrimos Councilor position was already filled before Cielo became Hierarch by a female mage known as Prism. So what become of her?


                        Ragnar
                        Although you have never seen these individuals gathered here before, let alone any mages in this large of a gathering, you guess that certain social aspects of government likely apply to this group as well. You try to mentally sift through the information presented before you like a puzzle.

                        You can tell from the hushed whispers spreading rapidly around the room that Atlas' position on this Council is surprising. You remember from Mathias' instruction on Tuesday that Cielo used political leverage to convince Atlas to step down from his position as Hierarch; you deduce that the crowd is primarily surprised to see that he hasn't stepped down very far in power. It raises questions for you about who the Councilor was in that position before, and how Atlas' relationships might be with the others on the stage.

                        Estrella and Luna certainly share an obvious physical appearance with Cielo, but more than that, they are sitting near each other and watching her with approval. Similarly, Atlas is wearing very similar attire as the other two men, and they are also sitting with their chairs somewhat closer to each other. You wonder if Atlas and the two men are part of a similar faction or maybe even cabal, just as Cielo and the other two women might be.


                        Lynx
                        You can see that the male and female Councilors on the stage seem to be ignoring each other as best they can. Their body language is superficially calm, but you see that the men and women's chairs are faced slightly away from those of the opposite sex, and their faces have hints of frowns. There might as well be an invisible wall standing right through the middle of the stage where Cielo is standing. Very likely, there is a power struggle or disagreement of some kind between these two groups.


                        Ouroboros Ophis
                        You quickly realize that you are not in your element. You are initially intrigued by the comments and whispers around you, but there is so little to base your judgment on that you're not sure how to use this information. Is Atlas liked, or disliked? Are they just surprised to see the previous Hierarch remaining in a position of power, or is there more to it? The hidden political undercurrents in the room make you feel uneasy, especially knowing that you're going to be speaking in front of them soon. You know that putting the wrong foot forward can set you up for headaches or even disaster down the road.

                        Take the Beat for the dramatic failure. You gain the following custom Condition:

                        Misinformed (Consilium). You struggle to understand the social undercurrents in a particular group, identified at the time you gain this Condition. You take -2 on Social rolls and Social Maneuvering attempts involving members of the social group related to this Condition. Whenever the Storyteller asks you to roll for a Social action or Social Maneuvering attempt pertaining to this Condition, instead of rolling you may voluntarily fail the roll and resolve this Condition.

                        Resolution: Opt to fail a Social action or Social Maneuvering attempt with someone in the identified group, as described above.

                        Beat: N/A.


                        Phoenix
                        As the Councilors and Hierarch approach, you take a moment to observe the room, taking a mental step back and really studying how everyone around you is acting.

                        First, you notice that most of the people in the room really are wearing formal attire, or at least nicer clothing. However, there are still a sizeable number of mages in the room with more casual clothes; Boomer is wearing a different outfit from the other day, rocking stilettos and a studded black leather jacket, but she's definitely not in any suit or formalwear. Still, it's a nice jacket, and Mathias and Valentine are certainly wearing formal attire. That signals to you that, at least at Consilium meetings, appearances are important to most people. A few people have given you glances, but that seems to be because they don't know who you are.

                        Second, you can tell from the body language of the Councilors that there is a clear rift between the men and the women. You overhear someone whisper, "Surprised Atlas is up there...I bet Medianoche isn't happy about that."


                        Everyone
                        Cielo stands with her hands resting in fists at her hips as she waits for the room to quiet down and everyone to find their seats. "Welcome, everyone. Thank you for coming on short notice." Her speech is crisply articulated at a smooth pace. "We have had changes in the representation on this Council. Here are your Councilors." She briefly motions to each of them in turn: Estrella, an Acanthus; Luna, a Moros; Apollo, a Mastigos; Atlas, an Obrimos; and Poseidon, a Thyrsus. "Prism, our previous Councilor representing the Path to the Aether," she explains simply, "was killed during the Deathstorm."

                        At Cielo's announcement, the room is shocked to silence.

                        After a long pause, Cielo continues, "This is likely the largest gathering we have seen in our Consilium in recent years, and perhaps one of our most important meetings. All who have been part of this society for more than a few weeks know that the Highstorms have been a Mystery explored by many for over a century. Casualties have always been a risk of these storms, but our Mystagogues have informed me that never before in this Consilium's recorded history have we lost so many in a single storm. And these from the Storm itself, not the Uratha or Kindred poachers or their servants in this city." Various mages around you grumble at the mention of these terms, but most of you do not recognize the names.

                        "I have served for some time as the Adamant Sage of the local Arrow caucus. It was the decision of this Council that a change in leadership would be wise, to provide a more direct response to this event and the anticipated reactions it may have among supernatural beings in the city. I do not take this decision lightly." Her face hardens. "I vow to act decisively, to take action as needed, to protect this Consilium from any person, organization, or being that threatens our civilization. I assure you that any practice of Abyssal magic, soul reaping, or service to the Exarchs or Seers of the Throne will be met with swift justice using lethal force."

We can't let fear dominate us or control us. Know that this Council will continue to serve you and protect you and your interests. We also will continue to strictly enforce our Consilium laws and mandates. As before, Valentine of the Shields of Dawn," she motions to him in the crowd, "and Yari of the Shadow Spears," she points to a middle-aged Asian man sitting not far from the stage, "will continue in their roles as Sentinels. They act with full support of this Council."

                            Cielo pauses and surveys the room. "Alright, let's not waste any time. We have several individuals who have business to discuss here today. Let's get to it," she says curtly. "Melody?" Cielo motions to someone in the crowd and then takes a seat, crossing her arms.

                            An African American woman who looks to be in her 30s stands up and makes her way to the center of the stage. Boomer leans over and whispers to you, "That's Melody, a théarch who serves as the Herald -- kind of like a spokesperson and ambassador for the Consilium."



                            "Hello everyone," Melody says with a wide smile. "With many of the unfortunate losses suffered during the Deathstorm, we've had a number of changes in our local cabals, many of whom requested the opportunity to update the Consilium on their membership changes. When I call your name, please stand and send a representative to the front to share your announcement..."

                            For the next several minutes, various cabals are called upon. Mages come forward and briefly share their updates. In some cases, a cabal remained and added one or two new members to replace those who were lost in the Deathstorm. For cabals that were hit particularly hard, leaving only one or two members surviving, some mages announce that they have formed an entirely new group with a new name. Melody offers each of them a warm smile, although her face is tinged with sadness as they describe their losses; she seems to wear her emotions on her sleeve.

                            After each of the cabals have shared their announcement, Melody looks down at a list in her hands and frowns curiously. She looks up at Mathias and calls out, "The Council recognizes Mathias, who says he has an announcement he would also like to share."

                            Mathias clenches his jaw but smoothly stands and walks at a steady pace to the stage. He smiles briefly to Melody and bows in respect to Cielo and the Council, then turns to face the audience. He takes a deep breath and slowly exhales before speaking. "Ladies and gentlemen of this Consilium, I stand before you today to make an announcement per conditions agreed upon as part of a Duel Arcane. As some of you know, Moonstone, previously a Sleepwalker and retainer to Hakim, Thunderbolt Guardian of the Adamantine Arrow, has newly Awakened." Mathias points out into the crowd directly at Moonstone, and his gaze remains fixed on him for as long as he stands on the stage. The announcement of Moonstone's Awakening draws some murmurs amongst the crowd, and the mages assembled here turn to look at him.

                            Mathias continues in a very even, neutral tone, in a way that is clearly rehearsed. "I hereby announce that Moonstone defeated me, fairly and per our rules of engagement, in the Duel Arcane." The whispers swell in volume, and many mages' eyes widen in surprise. A few chuckle or openly grin at Mathias' loss, but he simply keeps talking over the crowd. "I hereby announce that the late Hakim was my better as a mage, and a man. I apologize for dishonoring his memory by assaulting his favored student and dear companion." His words are nearly identical to Moonstone's exact wording of his terms.

                            Mathias bows his head at Moonstone and promptly walks down from the stage. His face is stoic, but his gait is stiff and his eyes are glaring. He returns back to his seat amidst a buzz in the crowd as the audience members gossip with each other and look at Moonstone and Mathias, as well as the rest of you sitting next to the Shields of Dawn. Mathias folds his arms and says nothing, staring pointedly ahead at the center stage.
