            Still shaking from the violence of his sudden decision to rescue Adonis, Erwin jerks back unconsciously, keeping his hands still just slightly slick with acid as far away from his boyfriend as he can reasonably get and still maintain contact. He gestures back over his shoulder into the hallway as Dr. Roman coughs and lets out low moans of pain on the floor, trying to right himself. "There's a, uh, breaker box down the hallway in the maintenance closet." He picks up a massive brown paper bag and passes it to Adonis. The bag smoulders just a little, but it seems like the acid flow has stopped for now. "I got your things." It sounded like such an inane, domestic thing to say, that Erwin had to stifle back a laugh. "Come on, let's go." He steps towards the door and takes a look as half a dozen employees in polo shirts, and a couple people in riot gear like at the Forks (possibly the same people) storming down the hall, followed by shuffling, misshapen, skittering things snarling, hissing, ragged, gasping for breath. Claws and... other things... raking at the doorway as a thing or two snuffles.

            The silver raven that is Satin flew forwards and down the hall, eyes scanning back and forth as Earth, looking every bit the generic employee, simply walked on through. The employees gawked at Mark 5, Scout and the others, however, pressing panic buttons on company wristbands and starting to make a run for the nearest intercom, but the staff trip over each other and collapse in a heap, stumbling back to their feet to either make a run to find actual help- if they can make it. Satin quorks with alarm at what she sees coming down the hall.

            "Unborn!"

                                Sneaking down the hallway, walking briskly but confidently, is Erwin in his button-down shirt and Adonis holding a lab coat tight to himself as a form of camouflage. Somewhere further up ahead, a lone alchemist in a dishevelled suit pauses, looking around, a Pandoran at their heel, the skittering, many-limbed, glistening thing letting out a couple wheezing gasps. Its flowing, waxy flesh balloons with each sucking breath, causing it to bob in place. The alchemist scans the area and makes eye contact with the Muse as he glances backwards, eyes widening in shock and just barely suppressing a shudder. "Hey! Wait!" He starts running towards him, the Pandorn loping leisurely at his side "Stop!"

                                Walking unhindered down the hall is Earth, whose flesh warps and ripples subtly every so often, a tremulous shudder like a shiver, adjusting ever so slightly as the nearby group shifts and changes, maintaining the illusion of normalcy based on the given population. The Unborn tilt their heads as they pass, but they are bound to the will of their masters, who are themselves fooled by the subtle alchemy. Satin flies overhead, close to the ceiling, certainly not where the alchemists expect to find a Created. Scout and Scarlet duck into an office, hearing rushing feet and skittering limbs slapping along tiles floors. Scarlet's face is enraptured, peering out of the window of the office door, waiting to see the mayhem unfold.

                                Meanwhile, Mark 5 cuts loose against the hopelessly outmatched employees, moving in a blur, one punch, one employee hits the floor, or flies back into the wall. The Titan's superhuman strength is overwhelming, iron mauls swung with the strength to shatter stone hurling human bodies aside with ease, leaving them in (still-breathing) heaps on the floor. Their breathing is laboured, but they are still alive. Solomon gives an appreciative nod, his gaze still ahead, studying the armoured humans and shambling Unborn racing towards them "Well, let's give them a show, shall we?"
