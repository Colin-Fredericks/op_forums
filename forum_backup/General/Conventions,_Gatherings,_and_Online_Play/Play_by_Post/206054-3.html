    Role : Ram

    Kith : Broadback

    Character Description : Nate Davis used to be a tall and slender man, with short blond hair. But that was before he met the Keeper. Now, he gained some muscles, along with goat legs covered in brown fur. His big ears are crowned with the curling horns of a ram, and he got a lot more hairy. His shaggy hair is now shoulder-length, and looks the same as the fur on his legs, coarse and brown. He's wearing a tattered and very loose-fitting Rolling Stones shirt

    Short bio : Nate, 23, was a golden boy. Born and raised in LA, he lived an easy life, his parents were kind but absent. He never had any money trouble, and was a spoiled brat. He used to take what he wanted, without any consequences. Nate is sarcastic and cynical, and can be seen as arrogant. Before being Taken, he was the lead guitarist in a small rock band called Wild Exhibit, who released two albums. He's kind of a jerk : he wasn't a stranger to cheating on women, and had just dumped his girlfriend Valerie because she told him she was pregnant. But he really doesn't care about that right now : he was the ninth person to be Taken, and has endured the Keeper's whims since.
