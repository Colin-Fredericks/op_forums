                I thought I sent this earlier but the forum may have eaten it.

                TL;DR Discord has a feature allowing you to pin messages, and its search feature is pretty robust. It definitely helps to make use of multiple IC channels if you're going to have people split off into different groups or by themselves. You can incorporate dicebots into a server (which generally are best confined to a single dice-rolling channel, with perhaps a second hidden one for secret ST rolls if you want) and one, Dicecord-CoD, is made specifically for Chronicles of Darkness rolling.

                I've been in a long-running Exalted 3e PBP game via Discord and it works really well for us. Having OOC, IC, and rolling all in the same server (with personal, hidden channels for discussion between the ST and each individual player in turn) is pretty convenient.

                Now, none of that fixes the issue of your capacity to access and post on Discord during your work hours, but I didn't want to leave you hanging any more than I already did. I will say, it might benefit y'all to have a discord just for OOC, as it helps with coordinating and that probably isn't as restricted by only being phone-accessible to you during work hours since you're not having to do big IC posts there.

                Best of luck in any case!

                                                        For you two who are already committed to the "soft reboot" we are planning, here are some additional details regarding character concepts and creation.

                                                        We will continue to use the existing OOC and IC threads for the game. It's easier than starting from scratch in a new thread, I think.

                                                        Follow normal character creation rules, with the following exceptions: Begin with Gnosis 2 instead of Gnosis 1. However, you cannot spend extra merits to begin with Gnosis 3. This essentially gives you 5 extra merit dots (if I recall correctly), but you can start with a little better control over magic without sacrificing merits to flesh out your character background. Use the merits from character creation to better flesh out and reflect your character concept. This will hopefully give you more flexibility with making your character's background match the concept you want to create, but without forcing you to start with Gnosis 1.

                                                        Feel free to suggest alternative Virtues/Vices than what are listed in the book. If you do, please list a few examples of situations that you think would qualify for satisfying the Virtue/Vice so I can make sure that I know how to reward you for following these in-game.

                                                        My ideal player group size is 4-6 characters, including the two of you. If we move to recruitment, I will not automatically add the first few people to reply; instead, I'm looking for players who appear to be thoughtful, mature, and friendly. I think these qualities are necessary to make the gaming experience better for everyone, and although I've had very few concerns about the caliber of people posting on these forums, there have been some who have given me concern or may not be a good fit for my game in my opinion.

                                                        If you have any questions/concerns, feel free to post here. I have some extra time today to help out, as I telework from home on Thursdays and it's easier for me to post during the day from my personal computer.
