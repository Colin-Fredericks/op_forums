        Though nobody was able to really see it through his armour, Pellias raised a sceptical eyebrow at the genuflecting group in front of them. Amongst the many hard-learned rules that he kept in his head, one was to never get too involved when knowing only half a situation. The Watchers that had attacked them had been obvious and simple to handle: aggressors that needed a direct show of force to avoid further confrontations.

        This proposed rescue, however, seemed a bit more sticky.

        He turned to the others and away from the crowd. "The complex in question seems less than easy to get into, and certainly not unnoticed if we did try. One of my eyes got a bit of a rude awakening when it got too close; I believe it was this 'Sable Thesis Dream' herself that poked it out of existence."

        Kneeling down, he began to trace in the dirt a rough outline of the complex with the pitcher plants that he'd seen. "With a bit of time and some surveillance, I reckon we might be able to plan around any defences. Other than the fact that it seems to be the most developed structure for quite some distance, however, I'm not sure of any concrete benefits we'd get out of trying a stunt like that."

        Standing up after finishing his diagram, he looked around for any feedback from the others.

                                                            Pellias knelt down again over his diagram and scratched his chin roughly. He let his hand down and traced over the pitcher plants. "One of my eyes got about a kilometre out before it was spotted. Remember, we're talking an immaterial globe the size of a fist or so rushing at the speed of a falcon and this sorceress was able to take it down instantly, no real warning. Damned surprising, but it does mean that she's paying attention, which means that she can't be putting as much attention elsewhere if she's worried about spotting objects coming into range"

                                                            He stood up and looked to Wren and the Enien. "She's playing a reactive game, it looks like. Glib responses to us poking her nest, full retaliation. I say we keep upping things to her expectations then: overwhelm her from a distance if we can while some of us sneak in and see what they can do from inside. If either the sorceress or her defences can be taken down, the outside team can then rush in and support the infiltration team."

                                                            Tapping his wrist, he called out to the landing team as a whole. "Transmission silence wherever possible. We can't be confident who and who isn't listening anymore, so keep it face-to-face" With that, he turned to a nearby support officer. "Did we bring the long range weapons like we did on our last field trip?"
