        (OCC: Just wanted to post some back-story. Felt the need to justify why i'm not still running away.)
        It's been a long day. The panther stopped running for the first time that day. Feeling safe from his enemies and the dangers of the world that pry on all. he relaxed his body and his form becoming what he was before Luna blessed him. A scared wrinkled old man with wispy white hair. He takes this risk sparingly less he be recognized or remembered by the wrong people. His true skin tingles at the moisture in the air having not felt in for long over a year. Secure in the canopy his tired mind drifted to thoughts untouched before now.

        I have only know servitude until Luna blessed me, but what I have done with her rare favor. Fled.

        I fled from the salve caravan, from the predators in the night, from slave hunters, even as I was offered a stable existence It was at the cost of servitude once more. To remain would be to spit in the eye of Luna herself, rejecting her gift. I am once more on the run not only now from the Guild that held me in chains but from the Master that would have kept me from them if only I live in a golden cage he defined. Escape. It has consumed my life for far to long. What remains of me if my decades of servitude and years of flight are ignored?

        His thoughts halt as he come upon such a question. A question that he never before had opportunity to think of. Relaxing his mind as he did his form, he let flow his age diminished intellect to discover the unknown within.

        My Will. None can claim to have lived so long under such harsh conditions. I, a mere mortal have persevered thou decades of what kill most within a year. Endless days of hard labor, illnesses that laid my fellows low, whips tore my body, hungry raking at the mind, sadistic overseers and crunching despair of the knowledge that there is no escape. While my will has been put towards survival thus far, what now shall I do?

        Ideal hands fiddling admits his pack, his fingers brush against the Hammer he had stolen in haste from his former master. Struck with remembrance his plan to start a project of his own, he smiled.

        I might be mortal no more but I am proof of mortal power. Moreover the Guild wrapped in the unnumbered evils of its practice stands as a paragon of the power mortals can wield. I shall purify the Guild be that it assuredly means destroying it.

        I was alone. Everything I did I did alone. I was not an overseer nor properly a slave. My lunar master cared only for himself and what might be considered a education and initiation was merely him forging a new tool for his motives. I am alone now. My mentor choose to leave me, so I remain alone in my goal to remove the Guild and empower humanity. I must find others. Mortals, Gods, Exalts whomever and whatever I can get to help I will take.

        "For the glory of Humanity for the glory of Luna!"

            Talos leads the group through the strange labyrinthine rooms and hallways of his manse, "You've probably noticed already," the Twilight says to his tourists as they cross a crystal bridge extending over hundreds of shining orichalcum gears and wheels, the same sight extending above them, "But the Manse is constantly moving." He carefully steps off the bridge and onto a nearby horizontal gear, "Everything moves according to the gears, so the actual layout of the manse is different at nearly every moment of the day." He steps off the gear and the group finds themselves in a completely new stairwall, this one descending downward. "A few former slaves I once knew used to tell legends of this place, saying that no one who entered ever left. It's pretty easy to see why: they got lost." Talos shakes his head, "Damn shame.....it's really a matter of keeping track of time...."

            The stairwell ends, opening into a large room. The group can immediately feel the heat emanating from the large shaft in the center of the room. Far below them at the bottom of the shaft was the red glow of molten lava, and from this flow rose four crystalline pillars, glowing from the lava within as it flowed up to a platform. "Thankfully, the forge is one of the few places that doesn't move in the manse." Talos says as he walks down a staircase to the platform, which had all the fixings of a masterwork smithy. As Talos stepped onto the platform, a large clockwork gear spun out from the wall towards the plaform, a few of the small automata appear at the sides of the shaft, each one bearing an orichalcum tool for blacksmithing and ready to run it to their master at a moment's notice. "I've been dying to fire this thing up, but I haven't really had a chance to build anything yet. And this is nothing compared to the workshop on the other end of the Manse!"
