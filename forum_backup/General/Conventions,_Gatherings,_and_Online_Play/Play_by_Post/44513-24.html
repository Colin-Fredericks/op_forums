        How about a survey of local opinions of different groups. I assume they do not like Fey, but are there negative opinions on Solar/Lunar anathema? What about toward ancestor ghosts?
        I suppose this would be an important thing to know.

        -Local Opinions on Various Supernatural Entities-

        Theros is a strongly Immaculate nation, and behaves as such. Anathema are openly hunted, Fae slain, and ghosts exorcised. Small cults to various individuals spring up form time to time, though they are often quashed violently by local authorities. One notable example, the Gilded Triangle, has managed to survive for several generations despite constant attempts at eradication.

        The Geherin strongly support Lunars. Because their patron goddess is supposedly a child of the moon, they view the Stewards with a certain degree of filial respect, almost as if they were beloved Aunts and Uncles. Their relationship with other Exalted is resolved on a case-by-case basis, though they tend to view them as proven Champions. This means that they will be accorded some form of respect, provided the Exalt doesn't expect the Geherin to be subservient to them. Ghosts are tolerated, so long as they are the shades of noble warriors, though the Geherin's lack of shadowlands makes any form of reliable contact between the living and the dead problematic.

        No-one other than the Ordoni know the Ordoni's relationship with the dead, Fae, or Anathema. They seem content to keep it that way.

        Novalesk has mixed views of the Anathema. On the one hand, there have purportedly been a number of Anathema in Novalesk's history, such as the Myrinne and Midea. On the other, Novalesk has dealt with a number of hostile Anathema in the past. As a whole, Anathema in Novlaesk are kept at arm's length unless they are members of one of the Gentes. Regardless, they are encouraged to not be very open about their powers, in order to keep their Immaculate neighbors at bay. The law, as decreed by Origenes at the founding of the nation, was that neither the Dead nor Divine may hold direct temporal power. The Anathema, with their powers stemming from the Celestines, are generally considered to fall under the purview of the latter.

        The Dead are treated with dignity and respect in Novalesk, though they are generally expected to just move along with their "lives" and pass into Lethe. Some exceptions exist, especially if they were respected individuals in life; the current Pyrtaneis, for example, was the Archon prior to Larissa's election.

                                                -Novalesk Military Doctrine-

                                                Novalesk military doctrine focuses around a highly-mobile light infantry model. The typical Novaleski soldier is outfitted with the following items; a short spear, straight sword, shield, linen cuirass, three light javelins, a sling and fifteen bullets. These troopers are organized into squads of thirty men, which are then grouped together into a maniple consisting of five infantry squads, and an artillerist squad that mans and constructs long-range dart-throwers and/or balistae (whose parts are scattered among the rest of the maniple for ease of movement). Novalesk has no permanent formations larger than a maniple, though the informal 'Stratos' is used whenever five or more maniples are organized into a single force.

                                                Due to cultural predilections (addressed in the Novalesk national profile) the military consists of a core of semi-professional troops.

                                                The Novalesk military doctrine, therefore, revolves around bringing their superior arms and mobility to bear in conflict. This has led to use of three major tactical doctrines, each formulated around the Novaleskis primary foes.

                                                Against heavily armored tight formations like the Ordoni phallanxes, typical doctrine is to engage in a skirmisher role, using the superior mobility to stay stay outside of melee while peppering the enemy with ranged attacks.

                                                Against conscript armies, like the Therian's, doctrine calls for the general to use their superior maneuvering capability to pepper advancing enemies at range to decrease morale and then charge into melee, relying on superior discipline.

                                                Barbarian forces are fought similar to the above, but are never charged, often due to the negligible effect on morale. Typically, the general attempts to obtain a superior position, and then to hold it against on rushing barbarians. Strategos Vasil attempted to use this tactic during the current conflict, but was rendered flat-footed by the previously hidden Fae forces.
