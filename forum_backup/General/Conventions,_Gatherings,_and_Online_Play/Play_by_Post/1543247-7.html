            Week 3

            Lots has happened! Great work fellas. Everyone take a beat for participation. lbeaumanior takes an arcane beat for a dramatic failure, and KaiserAfini takes an arcane beat for his obsession with temporal anomalies. Emerys takes an beat for discovering an arcane secret and an arcane beat for discovering a new book through magical means.

            To Recap
            • Gilad was in flight from Buenos Aires with the famous reporter Trish Takahashi and fellow Talon Galahad. Trish leaves her phone with Gilad, and then she and Galahad suddenly disappear without a trace from the plane.
            • Olorion is sent by his mentor, Alunno, to pick Galahad and his companion from the airport.
            • Helmut experiences some kind of anomaly in which the flower shop, owned by Trish's sister Nadashiko, disappears, and her husband, the Arrow Ronin Caliburn, seems to also disappear from his cell phone contacts.
            • Gilad texts Lucas, his cabal leader, and learns that from Lucas' perspective, the mission he was sent on was a disaster, and the rest of his cabal, the band Utopia, were killed by enemies. Gilad remembers events differently. He remembers Trish saving them at the last moment when they were being attacked by scelesti and a large bird that scarred his face. Lucas has never heard of Trish, which is strange since she is a famous reporter who was assigned by her newspaper in San Francisco to write a news story about Utopia. She also helped Gilad to lead a ghost child to safety in Paris, which is something Lucas surely would have remembered.
            • On the Uber ride to the airport, Olorion encounters a strange man who calls himself Blot, but he can't remember anything about the encounter. Perhaps exploring his own Oneiros will reveal the hidden memories.
            • Amir receives a message from the Heirarchs Provost, Colt, that he needs to apprehend a dangerous traitor, Gilad, who is accused of betraying the Adamantine Arrow and his own cabal to the Seers.
            • Amir yells at his boss and gets fired, along with his Sleepwalker assistant Rachel Chu.
            • Amir and Rachel go to the airport, and meet up with Helmut, who has important information to share with Amir.
            • Olorion also arrives at the airport, and meets Galahad, who has no memory of going to Buenos Aires, and was sent their to assist a mage named Amir.
            • Gilad arrives in San Francisco, and after some confused bumbling around, locates his own luggage, but does not find any luggage belonging to Trish or Galahad.
            Excited to see what happens next!
