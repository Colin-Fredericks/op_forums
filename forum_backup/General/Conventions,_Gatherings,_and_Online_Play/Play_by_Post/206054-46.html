    With the spoilers, I could see a potential case for 2e Beast fitting Nate and Jessica, what with the claims and agreements they made when they forged the Contract of Fang and Talon, but I don't think it works so well. It seems like the 2e Seeming's specifically about a willingness to engage in wild bestial acts that violate social mores that would otherwise keep one from getting out, which... doesn't seem like it quite fits what happened. A stronger case for Beast might be made from Jessica's willingness to keep stomping the holy hell out of Taurus. Perhaps also Fairest, for how consistently she's been trying to pull the Goat and Pig's spirits out of the muck and their fat out of the fire -- see leaping into action against the fish-ladies.

    I agree with Evilmarmelade that Ogre seems to make sense with that act, but since not all of the 2e Seemings we've seen seem to line up with the impression the 1e Seemings gave off... I'm inclined to wait until we see Elementals, Wizened, and Ogres before arguing for it. Particularly since I think Dave mentioned that Ogres involve "consumption" in some way?

    But yeah, I do kinda think that Beasts just don't really fit any of our three. A big part is just that the 1e and 2e Seeming narratives are pointed in precisely opposite directions, at least for Beast's: 1e's is about overcoming being thrust into animal instinct to get free, and 2e's is about thrusting oneself into animal instinct in order to get free. The latter is just not something that really fits any of our three.

    I could see an argument for Nate getting Darkling, what with some of the deals he's tried to strike being a little... traitor-y, but I dunno. Fun cue for "black sheep" jokes, I guess? Given the latest Fairest writeup, and that Max buckled down to pull off a trick that (if it works) would also get out just about everyone else... I'm amused that this little piggy could conceivably fit that mold. Even if not, the idea reminded me of the upper-left chunk of this image I saw ages ago, and so entertains regardless. ^_^

    In the end, though, I'll admit the 2e Seemings at this point strike me as maybe a little too narrow. Depending on how the last three Seemings look -- and the nature of the 7th and 8th Seemings Dave mentioned he had rolling around, for future supplements -- I could genuinely see all three characters not hitting the right notes for any of them and just leaving Seeming-less. That's kinda dissatisfying, though. :\ I'm hopeful I'll either come to view the Seemings broadly enough to ditch some of those qualifiers, in-game events will make it clearer, or the last three in the Core will really jump out.

    As far as this game goes, though, I'd just as soon either stick to 1e's take or go with 2e's in a fluffier sense. i.e. "Given how the characters took back agency in escape, which Seemings most feel right for each, possibly ignoring the 2e writeups?"
