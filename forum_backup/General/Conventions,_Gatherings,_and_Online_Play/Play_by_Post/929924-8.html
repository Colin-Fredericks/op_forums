    Active Sight: Life, Spirit, Fate (ends this post)
    Active Spells: Heightened Senses (Life 2; Potency 1, 1 week duration: +1 to Perception rolls); Body Control (Life 2; Potency 2, 1 week duration)
    Willpower: 1/5. Mana: 8/10
    Conditions/Tilts: Poor Light (Visual-based Perception rolls at -2)





    Lynx shudders at the smell, tensing with a small step backwards - but curiosity leaves him no choice, as he keeps his senses peeled on the veil. He approaches the phenomenon slowly, sniffing it deeply.

    Action: Lynx attempts a Revelation Roll, but gains no successes.

    Revelation Roll to reveal the phenomenon of the Veil: Gnosis 1 + Spirit 2 + Willpower (+3) - Opacity (-5), total dice 1.

    Revelation Roll     → 0 Successes


    He grits his teeth as he steps back from the veil, careful to keep his distance, as he gleans nothing at all. His eyes linger on the spirits instead.

    Those don't look all that friendly to me.

    "There's this...veil. Stale, and quiet, and... empty. Lifeless. There's also this swirl of colours there, trying to go two separate ways," Lynx says quietly. He then points to the spirits.

    "There are spirits around here. A little like...insects? Mandibles and glowing red eyes. They smeill rancid, and they're feeding on the hail. I don't think that's good for them. Gut tells me that, at least, but I don't really know..." Lynx shakes his head. "It doesn't feel right. I don't like it."

    He looks at Ragnar as he mentions the Sight; he notes the man's smile. "Sight?" He scratches his beard. "I think I'd describe it like a... well. I can see it, yeah, but I smell it too! Hear it, taste it, feel it on my skin! I guess it's more like Sensing rather than just Seeing. Is it different to you?"

    He observes Ragnar for a moment, then he grins. "I still appreciate it. I don't think I'd have figured it out on my own. I'll admit I'm pretty new to this..." He holds his hands out, as though touching the Wild, "To magic. Faen, but this Sight or Sense strains me over time. One moment..."

    With a slow breath, he closes his senses to the Primal Wild.

    Action: Lynx ends his Mage Sight.

                    Moonstone
                    Active Sight: None. Active Spells: None
                    Conditions: Poor Light, Abyssal Imago
                    Health: XOOOOO | Willpower: 0/8 | Mana: 3/10

                    "I just got out of the hospital." He flexes his fingers, feeling the bandages under his gloves. "I'll be okay. It hurts, but I was purging something negative from my body. But thank you for asking."

                    His thoughts are clear, but he's still mentally and physically exhausted. "The words I use- don't worry for right now. This isn't the time or place for initiation into the Mysteries. I don't know Phoenix's story yet, but I've been part of this world for a long time, and there's a common vocabulary that many people use who practice true magic in America. Lots of proper nouns in Greek and Latin and Sanskrit. I'm just now realizing how much of it was just words to me until recently, when I had an experience like yours. I doubt I know much more than you do of the Art itself. Aion knows I've never summoned a demon. But from what you said, and seeing how you work magic- they call magicians like us Mastigos. Binders and mentalists."

                    "As for the other thing..." protracted sigh, half-slump down the wall. "I want to talk to someone about it. Badly. But...I don't think I can right now. I'm sorry. My...er, mentor, I suppose, passed this week. His name is Hakim, he's a magician like us. But I couldn't work magic when he was alive. He helped me manage my illness, so..."

                    Grasping delicate hand gestures, visibly looking for words. Finally he just lays a hand on the man's shoulder and smiles. "I appreciate you working that spell for me. Thank you. Really. It's been a very difficult couple of days, trying to process everything and not being able to think as clearly. Maybe we can talk more after this storm, if you still want to listen."

                    Instinct says hug, but that doesn't seem appropriate. He just lets his hand trail back down, and looks back at the far wall with worry. "For right now...if I stay out in the hail, it's going to sap more of the magic in me. I don't know what will happen if I run out. And it wouldn't be right to leave Phoenix to his fate."

                    He glances down at the awful pile at his feet. "I could go through. You could use my blood sympathetically to see where I went. It's just following my mystical breadcrumb trail. Or cut a curl of my hair. I wouldn't ask you to dirty that lovely knife for me...you didn't see anything in the portal, did you?"
