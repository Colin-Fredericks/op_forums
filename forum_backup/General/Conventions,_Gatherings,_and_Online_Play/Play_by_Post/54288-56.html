            (Geherin)

            The collected chieftains and skalds swarm you with information, but it sums up to this;

            The Black Beast of Tarhoon is a terrifying monstrosity that can apparently move faster than sight, and is nigh impossible to see anyway. It has a cry so mournful that even the most jaded man cannot resist its sorrow, and it suffocates its prey.

            The White Boar-Giant is a huge man, over eight feet in height with skin pale as ivory, and he gives off a miasma of red-black and gold. His strength and stamina are nigh limitless; it is said that they are a boon from a Primeval boar-demon from the dawn of Creation.

            The Wizard-Master of K'ord is a Wyld sorcerer of no small skill. He resided to the Far East, on the cusp of the Middlemarches. He crafts behemoths and Oneiromantic charms, and casts them towards the center to wreak havoc.

            Moxe-Ulto was a thriving metropolis to the South-East of Geherin. It was home to an order of sorcerers who drew their magic from a powerful slumbering demon. One day the demon awoke and devoured the souls of the city's populace. The ruins gave been left empty since; many claim they are cursed.

            Bargat is a place of which the Geherin know little. Only fragmented tales of a city deep in the Wyld, inhabited by "Wing-men".

                "So this is the fabled silver-tongued Sunchild? One of your students, Feiro?" Hadad says to the Chancellor with a laugh, "you seem to be collecting the sunchildren like they're behemoths."

                "So, little Sun," Hadad says to Yi, taking a sip from his chalice, "I am a being with a great deal of resources, connections and personal power. Though I was not told the meaning of this meeting, I suspect it was for your teacher to convince me to throw in behind your little crusade. Am I correct?"

                Feiro nods, a sour look on his face.

                "Indeed," the spirit purrs, "Now. Your master wants this of me. You want information from me, and will undoubtedly need a guide. That is three things you desire of me, and I cannot think of one I would want of you. I prize my privacy more than the measly offering of worship you can provide. I doubt you have any knowledge, relics, powers or connections I would prize.

                "So tell me, Righteous One, why should I aid you?"
