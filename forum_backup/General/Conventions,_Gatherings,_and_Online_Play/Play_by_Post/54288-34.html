                                        "I will give it my all," Jensven grabs the nearest apron and ties it about himself. Shadow seems to recognize this as a sign of what is to come and curls up in a corner. "This workshop is very well stocked. It's not like the one I used back home. That was just an open air stone forge with a cover to keep the rain off the fire."

                                        The Dawn sets about gathering hammers, tongs, pokers, bellows, and other forging implements. He ensures that the anvil and table he will use are clean and gathers a fair amount of steel for the construction. A long time is spent studying the diagrams and the other golem. Jensven visualizes the process he would like to follow to craft the armored shell of the construct. He can see it form from steel ingots to actual plates and then to something that could be constructed into a machine for battle.

                                        He then imagines the warriors that wouldn't have to be in harm's way because the golems will be able to fight and the people that will be spared the predations of the Fae. The fire is stoked and the bellows bring it to a nice cherry glow. Smiling and laughing children enter his mind's eye and young lovers whisper, kiss, and nuzzle. The steel is heated and when it glows orange it is moved to the anvil. Old men smoke pipes as children sit at their feet to hear their wisdom. The hammer sings it's song, alternately hitting the glowing metal that will be a plate in a sturdy shell then the anvil and back again. Mothers and grandmothers prepare a feast for their family at a wedding of one of their offspring. All this not destroyed, all this possible because the golems were able to fight.

                                        The thoughts of these possibilities and more spur Jensven on as he works, tirelessly constructing plate after plate after plate. Hours pass and soon so does the night. Fatigue is forgotten as his hope that the hopes and dreams of the people of Novalesk be allowed to bear fruit buoys his spirits and lightens his burden. The mark of the Ascending Suns glistens on his brow as he pours essence into his work.

                                        Should Maia leave for the evening and return in the morning, she will be greeted by a cheerful Jensven, a snoozing dog, and as many golem parts as Jensven is able to make in that time.
                                        I will be spending a point of Willpower in order to put a compassion channel into the crafting. I will also spend six motes to power Craft Essence Triumphant. This gives me three successes before the roll. Intelligence (5) + Craft (Fire) (4) + Compassion (3) + A specialty in Crafting Armor (3) + What I hope is a three die stunt (3) gives me a total of eighteen dice to roll. I come up with ten successes (none for the stunt dice). Adding in the three successes from the Excellency gives me a total of thirteen successes.

                                        I will also be rolling Stamina (2) + Resistance (1) and expend another Willpower to channel Compassion (3) again. This roll of six dice gives me four successes to work through the night without falling down dead tired.

                                        Regardless of whether the stunt counts as a two or three dot stunt, I will opt for gaining Willpower from it. This leaves me six motes down on my peripheral pool, at a temporary Willpower of four, and with only one Compassion channel left for this story.

                                                        Hyuga watches as Jensven starts to work on creating the shell for the golem. Immediately, he can see him pouring his all into making these golems an realizes that this must have some sort of importance to him.

                                                        I suppose I should help. I don't know why this is so important to him, but it is nonetheless.

                                                        Not wanting to interrupt him, Hyuga quietly grabs some of the tools and heads into a nearby room to start his work. Hyuga closes his eyes for a second, remembering the exact layout of the golem and his hands immediately start to move. You will come to fruition golem body as I command now. Hyuga begins to channel his essence, letting it coalesce in his hands; a most comfortable feeling to him. His hands continue to move like a blur, picking up the tools at amazing speeds and set them down with no more than a small "tink!" sound. Noise echoes around the hall as Hyuga bends the malleable metal into shape and etches signs into the smooth side of the metal.

                                                        This makes me feel like I am at home making armor. I miss those days sometimes. Back when I had a more simple life. But here and now I have a chance to do some good. That oughta count for somethin'.

                                                        Light and sound continues to come from the room as Hyuga deftly pounds and bends the metal into the needed shapes. His eye for the details and being meticulous allows him to finish them up with ease. Metal is smoothed with his hands and various tools so that it shines brightly like the sun. Hyuga grunts and makes strange clicking noises to himself as he makes sure the bearings and joints work smoothly.
