    Jensven uses sheer will to bat aside the attack, spinning off his back foot to knock the blade just far enough off the mark to miss. The brand of the Dawn Caste blazes on his forehead as he pauses for a brief instant secure in the thought that this attack will not find it's way to him.

    The northern apprentice blacksmith is surrounded by an explosion essence as he launches a ferocious attack. The light emanating from him encompasses the entire clearing the impromptu clash of blades occupies. He drives forward off his back foot, a howl ripping its way out of his throat. The tip of his sword is aimed squarely at his opponent's abdomen as golden essence trails behind him.

    I am going to assume that I spent four essence for my last defense. It was all peripheral. I am going to combo Dipping Swallow Defense to bring my PDV back up to 5 and then crank two more motes into Melee Essence Triumphant for a total of four motes to raise my PDV to 6 and barely negate that attack. This means I have spent a total of 8 peripheral essence. Hello, blazing caste mark and bright aura.

    Jensven is through playing around. He is throwing everything he has into this attack. Melee Essence Overwhelming for seven motes to get seven more dice. This means an attack roll of Dexterity (4) + Melee (3) + 'Klaive Accuracy (3) + Stunt (1) + Melee Essence Overwhelming (7) = Seventeen plus One Dice for Thirteen plus Zero successes.

    This means I have spent a total of 15 peripheral essence. I am just this side of an iconic anima. Due to stunts I have been able to regain four of the spent motes. I now have a peripheral pool of fifteen motes.

                    09-21-2014, 12:01 AM
                    The young Dawn has trouble controlling the wave of rage that is washing over him. He does not attack, but he struggles to find words. Every muscle in his body is tense and he clenches his teeth as blood flows from his wound. Closing his eyes, he focuses first on calming himself. Jensven pictures in his mind a cold day in the evergreen forests near his home, Shadow at his side, and a light snowfall. Off in the distance a young woman can be seen. She has dark hair, almost black as night. The snow lands in it and stays there, providing a stark yet appealing contrast.

                    When he is calm enough he focuses on the wounds, on making his blood stop flowing so freely. He doesn't know how he realized this could be done the first time he tried it, but he just sat there after the defense of his home and focused on the wounds the raiders axes had caused and they closed. It took longer to heal, but he was able to expediate that as well. Both gifts of the sun. Gifts he felt utterly unworthy to have received. It was in this way that he now focused and attempted to stem the tide of blood from his gut.

                    Jensven falls to his knees and rests on his empty hand to remain in a sitting position. His 'Klaive is still clutched tightly in his right hand but rests to his side. His attempts to alleviate his suffering have failed. Shadow pads over and paws at his shoulder.

                    "I yield. I accept your offer of help. I just need to know one thing. I need to know who you are."

                    I am attempting to stop the lethal wound from bleeding with a reflexive roll of Stamina (2) + Resistance (1) = Three dice for no successes. This means I am not able to close that wound. I think I am two successes short. Keep in mind that I have Body Mending Meditation to fall back on. I don't necessarily need whoever or whatever this guy is to do it for me.

                        "I am known by many names; your mind cannot comprehend the name and titles bestowed upon me by my maker," the bell-warrior says, "But a people long dead named me Helios, the Midday Sun. They also named me Clemens, the Compassionate. Helios Clemens.That will function for a name."

                        As Helios examines Jensven's wounds, he says "you have no need to yield. The wounds were scored at identical times, and are equally severe. Besides," he continues with a chuckle, "I sheathed first." His examination evidently finished, Helios lays a hand on Jensven's wound. His touch burns like a red iron. A few moments of agonizing pain later, Helios removes his hand, taking the edge off the pain, leaving only a dull aching throb and an angry red scar. Helios's entire arm is bathed in a soft golden glow, like steam rising from his skin.

                        "That should suffice until you have time to finish healing," Helios says, "With most, I would say it would take a week or more, but one with your gifts, who knows? You may be fine by the end of the day. Even the scar will fade in time for you."

                                        (Jensven)

                                        "Doubt that I am the sun?" Helios says, raising an eyebrow,, "Is it because I am not-"

                                        Helios pauses for a moment, his form being sheathed in gilded scales, his features shifting to that of a Saurian humanoid. A massive technicolor feathered headdress materializes over his head. Twin Orichalcum Grand Daiklaives appear, floating several feet behind back, and he is wreathed in a blazing aura of sunfire. He levitates up a foot or so into the air, as if his blazing aura supports him.

                                        "-brilliant enough?"

                                        Jensven blinks, and the moment is gone, Helios standing, apparently human again. "You are correct, though. I am but a servant to the Sun. Ah... If only I could be so grossly incandescent," he says with a sigh, "By edict of the Ignis Divine, I am Herald of the Ascendant Sun, Second Lictor of the Most High. It was by His command that I came here to do battle with you and deliver this message.

                                        "REMEMBER, WHAT YOU ARE. YOU ARE MY ARMS AND SHIELD, MY BLADE AGAINST THE FOUL AND WRETCHED, MY SHIELD AGAINST THE ARMIES OF HELL AND THE OUTER CHAOS. YOU ARE THE BLADE THAT SMITE MY FOES AND THE AEGIS THAT GUARDS MY PEOPLE. A SHARD OF MY INFINITE POWER BURNS IN YOUR SOUL.

                                        "ACT AS IT."

                                        These words are clearly not Helios' own, but the words of the Sol Incarnate relayed through his Herald.

                                        "If you wish me to relay a reply, I may. Also," He says, flipping an Orichalcum disc, roughly two inches in diameter to Jensven. On one side is the eight-pointed sigil of the Unconquered Sun. On the other is the simple disc sigil of the Ascendant Sun, "It was not uncommon in better times for the Sunblessed to compete with the five Lictors to earn their favor. Those who made earned the favor of all five were granted a boon by our mutual patron. This token marks you as one who has earned mine, though had the choice been up to me, the contest would have been more in line with my own skills. If you desire to continue this challenge, I can relay that request to my siblings as well."

                                            "Despite what is said of me, I am not a Lookshian. Even so, I lived there for most of my life and worked with the Seventh Legion. I will not shame myself by complaining about my situation there, but I trust you know enough about the City that you understand how I learned humility. It was also in the city that I received tutelage. The Dragon's Chosen have a never ending need for artifices and wonders and cannot attend to all of their own needs. Such as I were trained, if never trusted, to supplement them as keepers or lore and workers of magic.

                                            My fear came in a battle, the one which led to the termination of my service with the legion. The center was overrun, our archers were pinned down, and the enemy fielded Exalted of their own. I expected to die that day, and not only on the field of battle. The night that followed and many after were more than enough to prove to me that I could no longer continue living the life I had had. Then of course came the journey. Novalesk and Lookshy are not very close as the mendicant walks.

                                            As for the choice, that I think is to blame for the new windows and skylight running around the tower." He looked mildly embarrassed. "I will have funds sent to repair that. Or... I could do it myself."

I doubt they will challenge me to a test of blades, though. I may have my work cut out for me. I assume that should I fail I can always try again later." A wolfish grin spreads on his face. "I should also like to challenge you again someday. In that form you just showed me. I promise Shadow will sit out again. We all know he's the stronger of us two."
