            I'd make a base character and play for a game or two and then spend your extra XP.

            The Investigation Charms are mostly about reading people. Our thief uses those charms to see who is worth stealing from. There is one charm for searching a room called Crafty Observation Method. It allows you to do a fifteen minute search in a few seconds.

            Athletics has some really amazing charms, too many to list. If I had to rebuild my character again, I would have favored Athletics.

            All the tracking charms are in Survival. City-Moving Secrets is a good one for your concept.
