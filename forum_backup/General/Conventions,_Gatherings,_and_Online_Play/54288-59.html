    (Jensven)

    "Oh really?" Maia laughs, "his interest in such things is..."

    Maia trails off as you hear murmuring from Cinder's furnace, "Oh yeah... Tien Yu... Slather that jam... All over my toast. You beautiful goddess that lives in my province..."

    "Well, that," Maia says waving a hand towards Cinder. She walks over towards his furnace and casually reaches in with her flesh hand, picking up the charred remains of a log, on which the fire-spirit is burning, "I know you're awake! That timing was too good! Why are you trying to set me up with him?"

    Cinder sparks to life. "Cause he's nice, and likes making things and you're not gettin' any younger," Cinder says drowsily.

    Maia tosses Cinder back into his furnace and shrugs, looking somewhat embarrassed. She shakes her head as she launches into the second question, "I don't think we'll have to worry about that. They're staying on academy grounds until the Stratos departs, and if the Fae want to get them here, they'll have to contend with several score minor demons and elementals. They would need a strike force of multiple Nobles with significant backup to destroy them, and they'd take significant losses. In which case they will ha e served their function."

        Upon learning that not one but two Solars had skipped out when there was a war ongoing Yi muttered, a brief curse about his own, presumed, misdeeds in a past life. With a slow breath he calmed himself.

        "This complicates things, though it seems every thing I learn and person I meet does the same. In case I do not see you before I leave, thank you for your efforts and for any time you are able to grant me." He gave a quick bow and departed for the tower of making Jensven was wont to frequent. He took in the presence of the forge-mistress and the Solar in a glance and dismissed any potential implications. It was none of his business.

        "Holy one," he said bowing to Jensven. "Mistress" to Maia. "I am afraid I must beg a mighty favor from the Ascending Sun. Circumstances call me to walk the path of desolation to Malfeas to seek a powerful relic. I believe that with its power the greatest strength of my grandfather Origenes can again be called to serve the nation, and even if I am wrong it will substantially empower our divine and exalted champions. However, the journey will be long and likely dangerous. Never would I claim the Sun had not given me strength enough for those tasks I must perform, but it is good to have a friend by your side when you walk to road to hell. If only to help you know when to turn back from it."

                            (Wyld)

                            You hear a tsking sound from the boat. "I aknowledge your strength," you hear Stannwald say,"you have defeated me, and will let you pass."

                            The boat moves ever closer, until it reaches the shore of the lake. It has no obvious means of propulsion, and the figure in the boat (a shapeless form draped in rough, rotting clothing) is utterly motionless.

                            Once then entire party is upon the boat (strange that you can all fit when it's only about the size of a two-man canoe), it begins to move landing you upon the shore of the island. Mist from the lake hangs low upon the ground; you can see only a couple of yards in each direction. As soon as you all step off the boat, it shoves off, seemingly on its own. It is mere moments before it and th figure within disappear from view.

                            Teleri leads on. As you walk, the stony dirt beneath your feat slowly becomes a solid piece of ivory porcelain. You cannot tell one direction from another; the fog still stands heavily upon the ground.

                            Some time later, you arrive at a chasm. You can neither discern the bottom or the other side. You see a bridge made of charred bones arching over the chasm, presumably to the other side.

                            An elfin knight stands upon the bridge. He is garbed in heavy plate armor and a tall cylindrical headdress both made of a deep blue metal. He holds before himself a heavy barbed blade of the same material.

                            What do?

                                (Academy)

                                Cinder perks up from Yi's talk of empowering the local Divinities, manifesting his spherical head form from the mass of his slowly burning furnace.

                                "Allow me to offer my services then, Champion," Cinder says in a solemn voice, "For I am one of the gods of this nation, and a magnanimous god at that. I feel honor bound to aid those who would come to my service."

                                Cinder hovers about the room, bobbing about to punctuate his points much like a human might do with their hands, "Since I began my existence as a Need Fire, it is within my power to repel minor ghosts and demons. Also, I can cast out disease and curses from animals, protecting Jensven's cow," he pauses, "No. wait. That's not right... Dog. Right?"

                                Maia purses her lips in thought. "As much as I'm loathe to admit it," she says, "Cinder has a point."

                                She smiles faintly to Jensven, "Of course. When you get back."

                                Will you allow Cinder to come with? And what do?

                                        "I am Brandt of Cobalt. By my Oaths, you may pass my bridge-" the armored figure says, thrusting his sword into the ground.                                                         He began to sing. His voice rose up and echoed against the sky, until it filled all about him in a chorus at once of many voices and of one.

                                                        Then he began to dance, and he did not hold himself back. He danced with all his might until one flesh could not contain it. From the merchant prince in the midst of his riches to the widow at her handmill the people rose up and followed him. Though no authorities remained, nothing was stolen from the places he passed; not the riches of the merchant or the last of the widows' flour, for even the thieves answered the call of the mighty one. They passed through the streets like a roaring river, until children ran across the awnings rooftops for there was no room on the ground.

                                                        "Hear O Novalesk, the Sun is Unconquered!"

                                                        So caught up in his energy the vast crowd again shouted so mightily that a hundred sheets of gilded silk caught fire in heaven. For an age the law concerning the prayers of the Sun had been forsaken, the sacred orichalc used to line the garments and pockets of those who should have known better. Now they remembered why their mandate forbid anything less than the imperishable hold the due of Sol and trembled at what it meant.

                                                        "I tell you now that the Wheel of Samsara has at last begun to turn. This age of brazen sorrows is at ending and deliverance is at hand!"

                                                        The people, cried out "A Golden Age!" and "Praise the Shining One!" It was in the midst of this riotous exuberance the representatives of the Boule came to the plaza to make their way to the Hall, summoned by the goddess who had been Archon.

                                                        At this rate I will be Archon by acclimation. Putting his cynical thought aside Yi continued.

                                                        "Now comes the Zenith of Novalesk, the noontide of its power and glory. Already the people of the forest have heard the call of unity and answered. Already the gods prophesy that silver shall be as common as stone and ceder wood like the brush of the field. Already the spirits of sickness begin to bow their heads in submission. Hunger, fear, ignorance; all are passing away. I tell you the truth, before your children are old they shall call poverty what is now regarded as wealth. They will call short the life now called blessed with age. They will call small the cities and lands of Novalesk as now they are and shall stretch out their hands to a world vaster and more wonderful than is now upon Earth."

                                                        "Now at last comes the first and final piece in this beginning. For an age the Creation has been prey to the ravages of contagion, the murderous unmaking of the raksha, and the hateful envy of demons. This nation and its god remember these things, and carry still their scars. Yet the time has come for scars to heal. I will go forth, descend into hell itself, and bring back the power that is ours by right."​

                                                        Because when nations send people to steal things they call it espionage and the agents spies. Not that I meant definitions entirely.

I must go on behalf of all of Novalesk, not merely at the behest of any who might send me. It must be the nation, and my duty to the nation, that sends me forth."
