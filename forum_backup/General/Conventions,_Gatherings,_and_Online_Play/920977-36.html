                        Several of you have made some choices or used rules that I think are in error. I know I myself had to look up rules a lot earlier today because I was rusty on a few things, but I wanted to make sure you all remembered some stuff

                        Haberdasher: First, you can't spend Reach to boost Potency like that. You can make it advanced potency, but that only helps Withstand against dispellation. Also, you can only use Concentration as a yantra on spells with a duration more than 1 turn long. Your spell only has duration of 1 turn by default. That means you would need something like a Path tool instead; however, it's a moot point because even dropping to +1 yantra instead of +2 wouldn't affect the outcome of your casting roll. You are correct that if Phoenix does not make a mundane roll this turn (such as attacking), your spell will run out of duration and expire. Finally, if you only have one free Reach, I'm assuming you spent it on instant casting?

                        Caladriu: In order to cast a touch spell in combat, you actually also need to succeed on a Dexterity + Brawl roll. -2 aura applies, but Defense does not. See chapter 5 for details. You can either keep the action and add that roll, or change your action. As with aimed spells, this roll is reflexive and takes place after rolls for spell casting and Paradox; failure means it misses and the spell fails. I will say that I think it satisfies your Naive Vice but does not count as an act of hubris; you're trying to understand and communicate with it for now, not make a deal or reason with it. I will share my thoughts on how it affects Moonstone if you decide to keep the action and succeed on the touch attack.

                        KKV: Not directly related to your post, but your header you've been copy/pasting still shows that you have 3 active spells right now. Can you clarify which are still active (and can drop any reflexively that you don't want anymore) by editing your header?

                        Crapcarp: As pointed out, concentration means your spell will end by taking a non-reflexive action, so it's not worth it for you because the spell will end before you get to use its benefit. Sounds like you will use the dedicated tool as both a tool yantra and for the Paradox reducing function, right? So yeah, casting this turn, then actually roll for casting next turn, then you can attack the turn after that. Let me know if I'm missing something there.

                        Let me know what your thoughts are about these things. Once we clear up those actions I'll find the time to put up the next post.

                                                Ok, so you've almost got it!

                                                For Potency, yes, you pick what you want it to set it to and take -2 per extra level required to get it up there. But don't forget that Potency is the primary spell factor of Exceptional Luck, not duration, so you can make it Potency 2 for free because you have Fate 2.

                                                The problem is still duration. As written, your spell still ends on his next turn. Since your roll already accounted for a -2 penalty to boost Potency that you don't need to pay, I recommend just saying that -2 was to boost duration to 2 turns. That allows you to use Concentration as a yantra on the spell (because it can only be used for spells with a duration longer than 1 turn), and the boon will still be active on his next turn if Phoenix chooses to make a roll then.

                                                To clarify in regards to the boons: If you want to pick the option of giving a dice bonus to certain actions/skills, you don't pick which rolls count, you just pick the skill; the target gets to choose when to apply the bonus. Similarly, for condition boons, you just pick which ones to grant and the target can choose when to resolve them. You get to "spend" the Potency on these options by mixing and matching what you want.

                                                So with Potency 2, you can use 1 Potency to grant +1 die bonus to 1 Weaponry roll, which can be used by the recipient any time before the spell duration ends. You can also use the other Potency to grant one beneficial condition, such as Inspired. Given all the penalties Phoenix has right now due to injuries and the aura, I would probably recommend Steadfast: he can resolve it to guarantee 1 success on a failed roll, or turn a chance die into a normal 1d10 roll. Means he can have a better shot at landing a hit.

                                                It's worth pointing out that you can also give 9-again on a number of mundane rolls equal to Potency as another boon, which don't have to be specified in advance by you.
