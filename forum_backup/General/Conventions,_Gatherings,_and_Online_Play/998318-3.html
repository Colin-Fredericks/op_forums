                Earth, Mark 5, & Adonis

                The symbols are all immediately recognizable to you, the most well-known alchemical signs for different metals. The different colours correspond to different symbols- blue for cobalt, red for mercury and phosphorus, greenish-black for copper and tin, the one resembling dried blood for iron. You know a little bit about human alchemy, so maybe these are potions? Liquified Distillations of Pyros, Transmutations held in stasis until they're imbibed. That hardly explains all the nonsensical medical jargon though. It's the clear vials that are the biggest shock. They're marked with the symbol for Vitriol.

                Earth's suspicions are confirmed when they find the symbol for Flux etched into each and every biohazard container. More of the containers have begun to flash with cyclopean red eyes, blinking with curiosity at the Created standing about them. They can hear the faint sounds of laboured breathing rising into excited gasps through the thick metal containers, the scrabbling sound of sharpened nails and... other things ...against slick steel. The containers are sealed tight, so the Pandorans within aren't at risk of getting out anytime soon.

                Adonis notices a small lab centre off to one side, complete with gloves, goggles, centrifuge, and protective clothing. It is kept meticulously clean, and appears to be used solely for taking samples and running tests on the inventory. For the first time, you notice a slight chill to the trailer, as if it is moderately refrigerated. Earth manages to find a thick spiral notebook hanging off a hook on one of the walls; it appears to be part inventory, part lab report, part journal, and part thesis. Whoever was responsible for these writings, they've been cooped up here in the trailer for a while, taking orders from someone else. Every page of the notebook is topped with a tidy letterhead marked with the same symbol painted on the trailer, and with a name in bold lettering, Amalgama LTD.

                Mark 5, on the other hand, is alert and aware, and he can hear someone coming up the street, complaining loudly on a phone "... yeah, yeah, there'll be a new truck here in an hour or so. Some furniture store was nice enough to store the trailer for me. Look, I'm not sure about these new compounds we're testing out, they're too reactive, too volatile. What? No- I got the material straight from Tucson, been with the truck the whole way, taking random samples, running tests. They. Don't. Work. Yeah? Tell that to corporate. We're losing contracts to LDI and Cheiron because even if our product is better, the side effects are too unpredictable to make them safe. Fine, whatever. We'll talk when I get this load to the office."

                OOC: Adonis gets a Beat, having Resolved the Spooked Condition by investigating further. Mark 5 gains the Steadfast Condition. Someone is coming back for the trailer, roll Dexterity + Stealth to conceal yourselves, if that's what you plan on doing.

                                    Earth, Mark 5, and Adonis

                                    The supposed Alchemist rounds the corner, fresh off the phone, and almost runs face first into the supernaturally charming Galateid. The benefits of his sanguine humour and finely tuned Azoth sets a fire in the young man, a blush unconsciously rising to his cheeks as he backpedals and tries to get around Adonis towards the trailer. He seems barely coherent at this point, just stringing words together "Wh- wait, lost? I was just, and you... uh..." He gestures back over his shoulder with a thumb "Work. I, uh, I need to get this trailer full of medical supplies back to the office, my, office. Truck will be here soon- an hour maybe? Really, really can't. Need the job to pay off student loans, you know?" He looks horrified with himself, realizing that he's babbling to an extremely compelling total stranger who just came completely out of nowhere. He turns on his heel and starts towards the trailer, hunched over as though trying to disappear from view.

                                    Earth manages to squeeze her way under the lab centre, bumping up against a door on one of the cabinets in her haste to escape. The door opens, and an old book, apparently handwritten, falls out. Notes and sketches on loose pieces of paper slip out from between the pages, covered in spidery writing and all-too familiar symbols that wake something deep in Azothic Memory. One of the loose illustrations floats its way over to her, and when it brushes her skin it's like the brush of a hand against her Azothic Radiance. This book is more than it appears, and it calls to the Torment inside her, urging her to find an outlet.

                                    OOC: The Amalgama LTD employee has only a single Door left for Social Maneuvering. Adonis' Impression is Perfect, so he can roll again immediately. Earth finds an Athanor, but what, exactly, is written within it and what secrets it contains will remain to be seen. You know, instinctively, that the Athanor is attuned to Stannum.
