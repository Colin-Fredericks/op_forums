            Shadow tilts his head at Jensven as if saying What are you talking about? We talk to each other all the time.

            "The bond between dog and their people transcends the need for words," the warrior says, nodding to Jensven, "I am Althea Solon." He is dressed in heavy bronze plate armor and carries two blades; one an unadorned two-handed sword, the other a wire-hilted sabre.

            "He is my second people," dog says, "Origenes is my first people. You are one of the Sun-dog's people?" dog sniffs Jensven. He looks over to Shadow, "Truly, you must be a mighty warrior."

            "Eryyyuun," Shadow replies with what looks like a large yawn and a flick of an ear.

            "Well, he let you keep your people."

            "Bf." Shadow retorts.

            "No false modesty," dog responds, "You must come to my dueling club. If you do not have a sabre, I can loan you one. You may bring your people, also, if you wish. Afterwards, there is a nice bakery nearby. The proprietress makes very excellent milk biscuits."

            "Arrurrurur," Shadow responds, before looking up to Jensven with a quizzical expression. You get the feeling that Shadow has deferred to you.

            How will you respond?

                                                        (Novalesk - dog-dojo)

                                                        Those small few warriors who were still fighting pause as Shadow darts about the room. They don't seem surprised. Instead, once they identify the source of the rapid motion, they sheathe their blades and an all of them begin to congregate around Dog, Solon and Jensven.

                                                        Once Shadow had returned to Jensven, Solon says, "This is Shadow, dog of the North, and Jensven, his person and a warrior of the Sun. Our master will be welcoming Shadow personally. If anyone wishes to engage Jensven in a bout, follow the normal rules of protocol. That is all."

                                                        With that, many warriors begin to return to their places on the floor, and resume their duels. They leave the central ring open, however, and dog trots over to it. Solon retrieves a pair of blunted practice sabers from a rack of blades. These two have oddly flattened hilts that suggest that they were not made for a human hand. He places them on the floor, one before dog, and one before Shadow.

                                                        Will Shadow learn the art of the blade from dog?
