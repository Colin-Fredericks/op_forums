                            I've been looking at some of the other sheets, and while the ST has the final say on the matter, I'm seeing a couple problems with 'em.

                            Dwyad: First off, there's too many dots in the Mental Skills. The 5th dot in any Attribute or Skill costs 2 dots, so all in all you put in 13 dots at creation.

                            Second, the Merit dots you purchased are a bit more expensive than what you put in your XP log(except for Quick Draw, that one's fine). See Merits cost "New dots x 2" and as misleading as "New dots" is, what they mean is that when you purchase a new dot, you have to spend XP for the entire dot value, not just the extra dot itself. For example, when purchasing the 2nd dot in Contacts, you have to spend 4 XP, not 2, because the new dot value you're getting is 2. Purchasing the 3rd dot in Allies costs 6 XP. Basically, the XP costs rise as you go up in dots. Also, you have to purchase the previous dot before you can get the next one.

                            This means that getting the 2 dots in Contacts requires 6 XP(2 + 4), not 3. 3 dots in Allies requires 12 XP(2 + 4 + 6), not 6. You've spent a total of 36 XP, sorry. Other than that, you're fine.

                            Thrythlind: First, getting an extra dot in Wyrd at creation doesn't cost 3 XP in addition to the Merit dot cost. Other than that, XP calculation is fine and dandy, so you have 3 extra XP to spend. Second, you also get a free Specialty in Athletics, Brawl, or Stealth at creation, and you start with 6 Glamour(it's rounded up, not down). Not really problems I guess, but I figured you should know anyways.

                            The only real problem I can find is in the Contracts. You only get 5 dots at creation, not 7, and 3 XP is certainly not enough to cover the extra two dots.

                            ATwannabe: I can't say because I can't see it.

                            Like I said, ST has the final say, but I thought you guys should know.
