                                        Do you mean opacity being the same for everyone, and one mage's reductions applying to other people? I'm not clear on that either, the rulebook doesn't explicitly say.

                                        In the game I run, I treat each mage as having their own fresh opacity rating, so one PC can reduce it from 4 to 3 but another PC would still have it as 4 by default. My reasoning is that if every mage to look at the mystery used the same opacity, it would presumably have opacity 0 forever once anyone "solved" it, which doesn't seem like the intent to me.

                                        Also, Opacity is described as "simply put, an abstract measure of how deep a mage must delve in order to fully understand a Mystery." What does it mean for Opacity to be reduced for multiple people in that case? Are they sharing what they've discovered so far? To me, the raw Supernal truths you get from scrutiny don't seem like something you can convey in conversation, although I could certainly see a situation where a group of mages who are actively collaborating could use a single opacity rating. That would seem to lend itself well to group play.

                                        I feel like I'm interpreting the Talmud here, haha. I think either approach would be fair and playable, this is just the reasoning I used when I was making a ruling for my own game.
