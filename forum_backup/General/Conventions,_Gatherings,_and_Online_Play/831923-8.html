                    Looking over the Goblin Vows I think maybe good idea would be pointing 2-3 typical Wyrd aspects that could be made with Court Mantle 2nd dot in Court's write-ups - they should shine on the variety of Court in question and point the differences in Courtiers.

                    Goblin Vow (• to •••••)
                    Your character has the ability to craft pledges with an aspect of the Wyrd itself, negotiating with it directly as though it was an individual. For each dot she has in this merit, she has a connection with one purview of the Wyrd. These purviews are specific subsets of larger aspects, similar to the way Skill Specializations are a narrower focus of a broader skill. “Time” is too broad of an aspect to have a
                    connection with, as is “evening”, but “high noon”, “rush hour”, “the witching hour”, and “the eleventh hour” are all acceptably narrow aspects. Similarly, “love” is too general of a purview, but “unrequited love”, “jealous love”, “broken hearts”, and “true love” are appropriate purviews.

                                        Bringing some basic character thoughts up, that may be expanded on later. I'll also do some sheet work.

                                        Daniel Hill was born to a mother from England and a father from Dubai itself. While he ended up with an English name, the family moved to the UAE when he was jut two years old and he's known that city for most of his life. (At last until his abduction). His parents were both rich and respected in the business world they inhabited and were involved in the lucrative oil trade, albeit it at the lower levels. Unfortunately, this led to Daniel becoming somewhat spoiled and arrogant as he grew up, a trait neither of his parents were ale to drum out of him. It didn't help that he was also creative and intelligent, and soon found himself with a number of promising apprenticeships for his future career.

                                        He signed the wrong contract.

                                        The Gentry who took him delighted in what It considered beauty and had acted the part of an architect in the human world. It instilled the heat of the desert in Daniel and used him s a pet, one trained to fly through the endless glass and steel it called art, flames flickering over the surfaces. Despite the pride it took in its creation, the Fae lacked the creative spark humanity possessed and rather then beautiful, its construction was a hideous mess.One day, one of its visitors told it that.

                                        Daniel managed to escape from the shattering glass and falling iron as his Master vented in fury, fleeing into the wilds at the edge of his Durance. Battered, weak and wounded, he barely made it through the Hedge alive as he clawed his way back to the world. That was some time ago now and he emerge to find his old life gone up like smoke. There was another where he used to be.

                                        Daniel now works as an archaeologist, his original dream of being an architect ruined by his Keeper. He takes a deep seated pleasure in study and learning, and also yearns to unravel all the mysteries he can, be they natural or supernatural in origin.
