                        (Yi)

                        In his free time amidst Feiro's instruction, Yi is able to speak with Larissa about being adopted into Gens Origenon. She says that the process is rather simple. You must convince one of the elders to sponsor you into the Gens. You'll legally become a member of their household, with all of the mutual obligations this entails. Basically, this boils down to convincing them netting power on the sponsorship, which, with an Essence wielder like Yi is forgone conclusion.

                        The issue with essence wielders though, is that they often have external motivations and loyalties. Most Elders will use sorcery to determine the true loyalties of prospective citizen-adoptees. This may pose an issue, as many may believe that Yi's anathema nature could throw the examination.

                        Larissa says she'll start greasing the wheels for your adoption among the Gens as a whole, but Yi's going to have to court favor with one of the elders himself. She suggests speaking to her sister, Origenon Hollidane. As an exalt herself, she'll be most resistant to Yi's Anathema magics (at least in the eyes of the rest of the Gens).
