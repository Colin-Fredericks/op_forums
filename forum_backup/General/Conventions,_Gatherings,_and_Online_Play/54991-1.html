                            A good mix between "explore the legendary Spiral and see if it matches up to the hype" and "behind enemy lines" is what I would put forward as a general idea. Spywork sounds great, but I'd also note that having a city-sized ship as home base is going to make it a challenge. I'm thinking that submarine-war fiction is actually not a bad source to look at. A shadow hunt between massive ships trying to find and destroy a single submarine that's more or less specifically hidden. As much 20,000 Leagues Under the Sea (which has the exploratory angle to boot) as Hunt for Red October.

                            I would also suggest that there be a bit more of a specific mission goal. Just a few ideas: find a place in the Spiral where a more permanent (and likely hidden) base can be set up; friendly signal sent out from the Spiral with a need to find out where it's come from; Rescue Mission: Gnosis; etc.

                                        @Jotun: I hadn't considered Sub warfare, but that's a great idea. Another thought, is despite how huge your ship will be, the Spiral is vast, and the Titans you'll be fighting, bigger still, so I think it'll work.

                                        As for a more specific mission goal, you raise a good point, so here's what I have.

                                        Gunstar Era 5063, 1/23:
                                        From: Fleet Admiral Invictus Gladians Hime, Forward Gunline Fleet Commander
                                        To: Officer Corps of the Battle Patropoli, Radical Mendicant Zekka

                                        Alright, you dogs. The Top Brass has a mission for you of utmost importance. As you are no doubt aware, attacks by the Enemy have become more frequent, and so it has become imperative to strike back and hit them where it hurts the most. We're taking back the Spiral and moving back into strike at their means of production. To do so, we need a secure base of operations, and that's where you come in. Long-range Probe deities have indicated that the Western Arm has little in the way of overt military capabilities, considering its relative distance from Us. Thus, the plan will be as such. My main line will draw their attention away from the West, by making a feint towards the South. You and the Fast Cruiser, Mercury's Delight, will then break off and continue towards the West. You need to find a piece of real estate to let us move into.

                                        Once you've discovered an ideal planet, message the Fleet, and we'll move in. Til then, be on your guard, and by the Incarnae, don't start going in grand-standing. Your's is a mission of stealth and subterfuge, not fleet action, got it?

                                        I cannot stress to you the critical nature of this mission. Should this fail, and the Enemy discover our motives, you can rest assured that everything we've been fighting for will be for naught.

                                        Is that more what you were looking for, Jotun?

                                        @Albz: I figure that you're all going to be semi-experienced members of the Fleet, so you all get 50 Exp. Does that seem fair?
