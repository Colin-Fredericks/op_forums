    [Interest Check] V20 vs. WoD vs. Dark Ages

    01-04-2014, 11:37 AM
    With the newfound ability to run play by post games right here at Onyx Path, I find myself wanting to run a game here. My schedule allows me to take on any number of concurrent games as both a player and an ST, which means I'm extraordinarily lucky that way. But I'm beginning to digress, so let me switch gears and get to the point.

    I've got a few ideas to run a game here, all of which I've got some familiarity with. They are:

    1. The Giovanni Chronicles. Starting in 15th century Italy, the characters would begin the game as mortal and end up becoming Embraced during play. The game would span centuries, with the characters dealing with not only their own immortality, but how to interact with all the other immortals they come across. And forget canon history - the characters will have the chance to write their own version of Vampire history throughout the game.

    2. The God Machine Chronicle. Set in Las Vegas, NV, the game would start with the characters first being recruited to form a cell and go through finding bits and pieces of whatever happens to be this "God Machine". The game would actually start with a story that has a perfect tie-in to the God Machine, and then would have many intertwining stories both in and out of the established God Machine.

    3. The Transylvania Chronicles. This is actually a misnomer, as I have the entire Dark Ages line plotted out by time, starting with Clash of Wills and ending with chapter 4 of The Dragon Ascendant. From medieval England, through Jerusalem, Transylvania, Russia, and into the New World, the characters will have the chance to see just how much fortitude - both the Discipline and the testicular variety - they have. Can they stand up to all of the forces that seem to plot against them time and again? Or will they fold like a cheap table, succumbing to both the beast and the sunlight?

    So, is there any interest in playing any of these here? I'll leave this thread open for at least 2 weeks to garner interest, and if there is enough interest I'll get the OOC thread set up in the new PBP forum with full rules on whichever game we end up playing there.

    Oh, and PS: Thank you Onyx Path for the PBP forum!
