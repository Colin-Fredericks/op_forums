                                Gilad was using a Finnish idiom, which he learned from his mother. Saying something costs malt, honey or stawberries means its expensive.

                                When he said saying something goes over one's dandruf, it was an idiom meaning something is too difficult for one to comprehend. Swords were almost magic items in his time, polished, beautiful, rare and deadly, not wanting people to show one off is a very confusing custom.

                                He has Swedish idioms too, but I haven't found a situation to use them yet.

                                Since we are posting in English (and I don't know Swedish), I thought it might be a fun way to capture the feel of the language.
