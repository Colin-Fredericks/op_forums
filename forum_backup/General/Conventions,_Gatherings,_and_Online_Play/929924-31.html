    Moonstone
    Rose of Sharon's face hardens again, and she clearly resists the urge to roll her eyes at your sense of humor. Askia makes no such effort to hide his irritation, and Ghost Dog just continues to stare at you blankly.

    Rose of Sharon looks around the room and shakes her head. "I thought I saw him earlier, but he appears to have left already. I will contact him directly and appeal to him at your request, out of respect for Hakim." She looks behind you at Valentine and then adds, "It seems your mentor would like a word with you. But I wanted to ask...Do you still have interest in serving the Adamantine Arrow? You would need to go through the proper initiation testing, as is only proper. You know of our mission and principles. With your new perspective of the Supernal Realms, and your previous knowledge of the Order, you certainly could be a boon."

    Feel free to continue this conversation if desired, Valentine would wait until the Knights are done with you before approaching you. Just wanted to add the next part to keep the scene moving.


    After you finish your conversation, the Knights of Alhambra say their goodbyes, and Valentine approaches you as they leave. He looks after them and comments, "They are very well respected in this community for their wisdom and contributions to the Adamantine Arrow." Valentine turns to face you. He has a slight frown, and his eyes sharply focus on you. "Until you no longer require supervision, I will be overseeing your introduction to this Consilium. I know that you have a great deal of knowledge about Awakened society and magic. Honestly, you might know even more about certain aspects of magic than I do in some domains because of your tenure with the Arrows," he admits in a self-deprecating way. He smiles warmly. "But I do want to make sure you learn everything you need to survive in this world. And perhaps, if you're open to it, I might even be able to show you a few things along the way. I might have a little more...finesse in some ways than the Knights. It's part of the reason Cielo placed me in this position."

    Valentine gently claps you on each of your shoulders. He exhales slowly. "The truth of the matter, Moonstone, is that magic is incredibly powerful -- but also can be incredibly dangerous, at least in the wrong hands or when used inappropriately. The Guardians exist to safeguard the world and each other from such dangerous uses of magic. Even the Arrows respect us for that," he smiles. "I think you know that. I think you can appreciate how important that mission is. And I even think it might appeal to you."

    Valentine looks around, then leans forward and gives you a small slip of paper. His voice becomes a whisper. "This is my personal cell phone. I would like you to keep this private between us. I don't usually give out number except in special circumstances. Don't share it with anyone. But if you need to reach me, you may use it." He leans back and speaks at his normal volume again. "Now, I would like to meet you tomorrow, perhaps over lunch? Call me and we can arrange a meeting location. Until then, I encourage you to speak with your new associates and discuss your thoughts about forming a cabal." He inclines his head and gives you an opportunity to ask any further questions before leaving.


    Binder
    Falling Blossom smiles in reply. "Well, I am glad you are receptive to the pairing. It need not be permanent, as Cielo noted, just long enough for you to learn the ins and outs of our society. But as a member of the Mysterium, I can also guide you through the initiation process and answer your questions as I am able, to help further your Supernal advancement into the ways of knowledge and truth."

    In response to your question, Falling Blossom nods and begins to take on a practiced instructive tone as she speaks. "Excellent question. We Mystagogues have various centers and repositories of tomes, grimoires, and Artifacts all around the globe. These places are called Athenaea, essentially Supernal libraries and storehouses. We can take you both to one this week and introduce you to some of our other members, should you be interested."

    When you mention initiation, Falling Blossom's smile deepens and her tone sounds eager. She seems to enjoy recruiting new mages to her Order. "I am so glad to hear that you are interested. The Mysterium is dedicated to the important task, a critical one, of collecting and safeguarding knowledge. As long as that interests you, I would be glad to serve as your formal mentor." Her voice takes on a serious tone. "The Mysterium utilizes a very traditional mentor-apprentice bond. By our customs, I am required to teach you a basic understanding about Awakened society, but also magical theory, High Speech, and how to cast rote spells. I am also held responsible for your criminal actions, if you take any," she notes with a very serious frown. Her tone clearly indicates that she expects there not to be any problems.

    "In exchange, you are expected to freely share any special or esoteric Supernal knowledge with me that you encounter or obtain, and assist me with my own studies and interests. I will have various tasks I need you to do for me periodically. As for actual initiation, we can discuss that more at an Athenaeum. I'm confident the process will not be too taxing on you, Binder," Falling Blossom waves a hand dismissively. "There are some whom we worry about because they are too eager, too impatient to use our knowledge unwisely. Such misuse would risk a great deal of danger for many. But I don't think you're like that, are you?"


    Phoenix
    The Fallen World fades to a whisper around you even as the Supernal magic in this room grows to a vibrant crescendo to your eyes and ears. Through the lens of the Prime Arcanum, the spell before you hums with powerful energy. The holy fire of the Aether shines in your eyes as you gaze into the spell. But you're distracted, and without your focus, you find yourself staring so intently that the supernatural light of the spell burns your Sight. The Truth of this Mystery not only eludes you, but it blinds you.

    Take an Arcane Beat for the optional dramatic failure. You also gain the following custom Condition:

    Supernal Blindness. Like a blind spot from staring too close to the sun, your intense gaze upon a Mystery has temporarily harmed your Sight. You must spend a Willpower point to enter your active Mage Sight, even for the first time you use it per scene; you may then maintain it for the remainder of the scene or until leaving active Mage Sight. You also suffer -2 penalties to all Revelation and Scrutiny rolls.

    Resolution: This Condition is resolved when you successfully Reveal the surface or deep information of a Mystery. Resolving this Condition grants an Arcane Beat.

    Beat: N/A.


    By the time you and Ragnar leave the sanctuary, the other Nameless mages you've met are congregating in the narthex.


    Lynx
    "The correct plural is actually 'Thyrsus'," Falling Blossom comments.

    Raven laughs. "We are Shamans, Lynx. Call us by our formal name if you prefer, but our calling is that of real magic, of life and energy and power all fused together. It's what gives the spirits their life breath, and courses through your veins, your very cells." She shakes her head at the mention of the Pentacle. "Politics, phaw! Forget that for now. You're trying to understand why ants march in formation, why birds fly in a flock. That will come later. First, I'm going to teach you why they exist."

    Raven rubs her chin pensively and adds, "It might help you to meet more Shamans, I suppose. Perhaps the Shadow Spears?" she asks, turning to Falling Blossom. The other mage nods, and Raven explains, "The Shadow Spears are an all-Thyrsus cabal. You saw Yari earlier, one of this Consilium's Sentinels. He is an Arrow, alongside our colleague and fellow Mystagogue Ascalon, and Trident of the Free Council. They are a focused lot, but may take time to meet with us this week. But your basic education must come first," she states firmly. "Talk to your companions about your possible pack, your cabal, and I will further educate you on more important matters. You won't regret it," she grins again.


    Ragnar
    Mathias nods at you and reminds you to contact him with any questions or concerns, and he says goodbye before leaving. In the sanctuary, you find yourself longing to know more about this place. It's a thirst for knowledge that you've rarely felt before, at least of this magnitude. You got only a small taste of it here, but you are hungry for more.

    Although you weren't able to learn more about the magic here, take an Arcane Beat for pursuing your Aspiration this scene.

There isn't some sort of larger Awakened nation that they answer to, despite the hopes of the Silver Ladder- although we do have Convocations every so often with the Consilia of other cities nearby, and that ghastly confederation of Shamans who live in the Shadow of Lake Michigan...I'm getting ahead of myself."

Some mages, just as some people, go without."

                                                    "Your cabal is those whom you can always count on. Some are formal, some are informal, but a cabal is a group of individual mages recognized by others. You basically tell the Consilium that these people are in a cabal, and the cabal name is X. It's not a marriage, or a lease agreement, or a deathless oath- simply the people you have chosen to stand with. Mages join a cabal for mutual support and protection in their magical lives. There's a cabal in Boystown that just shares rent on a storage unit, compares notes once a month, and shows up in force if one of them is attacked. Others are more formal and mystically powerful. I used to serve a cabal called the Knights of Alhambra, all Adamantine Arrows who draw magical power from the symbol of al-Andalus as a kingdom of enlightened warriors. They have a number of Fate-bound oaths and obligations, but they entered them willingly, and they are stronger for it. Of course, all oaths can be broken, and no cabal is permanent. The Knights chose to condemn and expel my master, Hakim, for violating their oath- even though he was a founding member and certainly the strongest of them- and I chose to stand with him. And that was that."

                                                    He disassembles CABAL and sips his coffee.

                                                    "At the most basic level, cabal members are expected to tell each other what they're working on in their magical lives, assume good faith and support each other in a crisis. If Phoenix told me that he was being threatened by a cult of necromancers, I would fight beside him first and ask why necromancers are attacking him later. If Ouroboros Ophis wanted to follow the Sympathy of some magical mystery he was studying, I would offer my help freely. And I would expect the same courtesy."

                                                    "The more a cabal pulls together, the stronger its members become. Many cabals pool their resources and share a safe house or ritual space of some kind, or a place of power like a Hallow or Demesne- sites where the Supernal is closer to the Fallen, and working magic is easier. We can look into that later. Some cabals look for a shared theme or myth or magical symbol that fascinates their members, and draw power from that shared Mystery when they incorporate that symbol into their magic. A cabal might receive a boon by swearing a Fate-bound oath, or refine their Sympathy to act more alike, or enter an agreement with a powerful spirit or ghost to sponsor them."

If we discovered its Bane, the material that especially harms it, we could destroy Alux for good."
