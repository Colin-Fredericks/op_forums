                                        So, I could jump in at a few places in my mind. Jensven would have armor-smithing on the mind. He could definitely do this in Ordon, but Geherin would seem closer to home. It wouldn't be exactly like home, but that is academic. He would avoid Theros if at all possible. There is also the option of showing up near the front lines as he would believe he could do the most good there, trying to craft/repair armor for the guys actually doing the fighting. The capitol of Novalesk is another definite possibility as the most talented smiths of the nation would naturally be there, in his opinion.

                                        So, let me list these options
                                        1. Showing up in Geherin as that is somewhat familiar with it's warlike, tribal culture
                                        2. Spending time in Ordon as that would provide the best smithing opportunities
                                        3. Heading to the front so he could directly help the combatants
                                        4. Stopping in Novalesk to try and coordinate with established smiths

                                        I am also open to suggestions. This guy is REALLY new to the whole exaltation thing. He knows only about what he can obviously do, what the Unconquered Sun showed him at exaltation, and what legends he has heard. He is still thinking in the terms that he is an apprentice armor-smith and trying to reconcile the fact that his motivation is no longer to just craft good armor, but to be the best freakin' guardian he can be. The journey of him changing his mind (his heart already has) and his perspective should be interesting to play out.

                                        Long story short, where would you guys like to see him?
