                OOC:

                Name: Richard Humphrey
                Shadow name: Gawain
                Age: 25
                Path: Obrimos
                Order: Adamantine Arrow
                Dedicated magical tool: English broadsword

                Rich Humphrey is the third son of a wealthy family from the LA area. With one of his older brothers running for Congress and the other on track to take over the family's law firm, Rich is mostly overlooked by his parents. So, when he expressed a fascination with medieval literature, they shrugged and called in some favors at UC Berkeley.

                During his Junior year, while working on his term paper on Sir Gawain and the Green Knight, he began to see the Reality behind the tale. All of human history centered on the victory and establishment of human order and reason (Camelot) over the chaos of this world (the Green Knight). After managing to cram his lofty revelation into human words, he went to turn in his paper. Luckily, the professor reminded him to write his name on it. As Rich turned to walk to his next class, the professor advised him to "Prepare."

                While possessing a mediocre physique, his passion and drive caught the eye of Caliburn of the Adamantine Arrow. Over the next four years, Gawain (as he decided to call himself) was transformed into an accomplished swordsman.
                ​
                IC:

                The sun was low by the time the grave digger put in the last shovel of dirt. The youngest of the mourners let out a small whimper and clung to her mother's skirts. Gawain put down his shovel and wiped his hands on his trousers. He was not kin to the deceased and merely being invited to the ceremony was a tremendous honor. Digging the grave was the least he could do.

                The family patriarch stood next to him. "Richard," he began with a quiet voice full of emotion. "Would you care to say a few words?"

                Gawain, or Richard as he was known to the uninitiated, swallowed nervously. This was unexpected but, in retrospect, shouldn't have been. Everything was a test under his master's tutelage. He nodded and stepped forward.

                "Ares was a noble friend like none other. He was a gentle soul and yet a fearless warrior when needed be. We are grateful for the life he shared with us. We remember all that he was and celebrate the part of him he leaves with us. You shall be missed, old man."

                The matriarch walked over and put a hand on Gawain's shoulder. "Thank you, dear. That was beautiful."

                Later that night, after the kids had been put to bed, Gawain and his master sat on the front porch nursing their beers. "Thanks for coming," Caliburn said. "The kids always love to see you. Real tear-jerker of a speech too though it's more than that flea-ridden old mutt deserved."

                "Don't speak ill of the dead," Gawain replied with a grin.

                A moment passed. Caliburn took a swig of his bear and sighed. "So, down to business. Your passport up to date?"
