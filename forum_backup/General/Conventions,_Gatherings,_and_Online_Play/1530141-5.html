                                            With Active Mage Sight Mother Time perceives that some extremely powerful magical effect surrounds the young woman. It is an aura that almost resembles the nimbus of a potent mage, but it is not a Mage aura.

                                            With Mind Mother Time perceives that Trish has a normal human mind with above average intelligence and resolve, (three dots each) and exceptional wits (four dots). She posses an exceptional emotional presence, ability to manipulate others, and composure. Further magical spells of knowing might reveal her thoughts, emotions, and moral character. It now seems extremely lucky that Bridgitte was able to so easily intimidate her.

                                            Time, combined with Fate, reveals that the pattern of time has been rewoven on several occasions in the past to change the fate of the subject.

                                            The strength of power flowing through the threads of Fate growing and twisting around the young woman is almost overwhelming to Mother Time. She has never seen anything like this before, except in the whirling Moirae that dance around the throne of the blessed prophet and his sister Ruin that she has briefly glimpsed in her dreams. The woman's aura seems to be like tidal waves of dramatically shifting Fate, or changing seasons of good and ill fortune.

                                            The young Japanese woman seems to posses a powerful destiny, though its exact nature and corresponding Bane are not readily apparent.

                                            Is this woman some sort of blasphemous abomination, or is meeting this "creature" some sort of sign from the Prophet? Should Mother Time follow her to receive guidance of the Exarch's will or should she be destroyed as soon as possible?

                                            The truly odd thing is that Mother Time does not sense any wards or veils, or mental resistance of any kind. Does this woman even realize what she is? Is she an unwitting tool of some more powerful entity?
