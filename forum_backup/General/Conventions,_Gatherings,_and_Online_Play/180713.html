    House rules:

    Even Blade rewrite

    This Terrestrial Martial Arts Style is exceptionally popular among Creation's enlightened mortals, god-blooded and other lesser supernatural denizens. Its form weapons are the slashing sword, straight sword, short sword, knife and scabbard, and their artifact equivalents, the daiklave, reaper daiklave, short daiklave and switchklave. It is elementally aspected towards Air. It may not be practiced in armour with a mobility penalty greater than -1. It is incompatible with shields.

    Death Between Heartbeats
    Cost: 2m; Mins:Martial Arts 2, Essence 1; Type: Reflexive (Step 1)
    Keywords: Combo-OK
    Duration: Tick
    Prereqs: None

    [Insert fluff here.] This Charm may be used whenever the character acts on the same tick as her enemies. Instead of resolving simultaneously, the character's action (including DV refresh) is resolved before all others. If this effect is contested by another instance of this Charm or a similar effect, all entities using such effects act simultaneously, followed by all entities which are not.

    Breath and Essence Control
    Cost: 3m; Mins: Martial Arts 3, Essence 2; Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Combo-OK
    Duration: Instant
    Prereqs: Death Between Heartbeats

    [Insert fluff here.] This Charm supplements a Join Battle action. It adds the character's Martial Arts score in dice to her Join Battle pool. In addition, it allows the character to ready any Even Blade form weapon as a reflexive action upon activation.

    Close Your Eyes And Look
    Cost: 4m; Mins: Martial Arts 3, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive (Step 1)
    Keywords: Combo-OK
    Duration: Instant
    Prereqs: Death Between Heartbeats

    [Insert fluff here.] This Charm supplements an unarmed Martial Arts attack. It doubles all DV penalties the target is currently suffering from for the purposes of resolving that attack.

    Even Blade Form
    Cost: 5m; Mins: Martial Arts 4, Essence 2; Type: Simple (Speed 6, DV -1)
    Keywords: Form-type
    Duration: Scene
    Prereqs: Close Your Eyes And Look

    [Insert fluff here.] While this Charm is active, the character never suffers an automatic unexpected attack from being surrounded in combat. In addition, she reduces her total DV penalty by half her Permanent Essence rating.

    Fiery Garda Force Attack
    Cost: 4m, 1wp; Mins: Martial Arts 4, Essence 2; Type: Supplemental
    Keywords: Combo-OK, Obvious
    Duration: Instant
    Prereqs: Even Blade Form

    [Insert fluff here.] This Charm supplements an unarmed martial arts attack, granting it piercing. Secondly it applies a penalty equal to 1/2 the character's Essence to the defender's parry DV.

    Flashing Zephyr Speed Stance
    Cost: 4m; Mins: Martial Arts 4, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive (Step 1)
    Keywords: Combo-OK, Obvious
    Duration: Action
    Prereqs: Even Blade Form

    [Insert fluff here.] This Charm alters the statistics of a wielded Even Blade form weapon. Until the character's DV refreshes, the weapon's Speed is reduced by 1, and its Accuracy and Rate are increased by 1. This may not reduce Speed below 3.

    Floating Nymph Grace Evasion
    Cost: 4m; Mins: Martial Arts 4, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive (Step 2)
    Keywords: Combo-OK, Obvious
    Duration: Tick
    Prereqs: Even Blade Form

    [Insert fluff here.] For one tick the character reduces her total parry DV penalty by her Martial Arts score.

    Gruesome Wood King Revelry Technique
    Cost: 1m; Mins: Martial Arts 4, Essence 2; Type: Reflexive (Step 10)
    Keywords: Combo-OK, Obvious
    Duration: Action
    Prereqs: Even Blade Form

    [Insert fluff here.] If the character's unarmed Martial Arts attacks successfully deal lethal damage, the character regains one mote per health level of lethal damage inflicted,and places them into an Overdrive pool. If the character does not have an Overdrive pool from another Charm, this Charm grants an overdrive pool with a capacity of 5 motes.

    The Variant of Master Azure Crane

    Loving Heart Stance
    Cost: 4m; Mins:Martial Arts 5, Essence 3; Type: Reflexive (Step 9)
    Keywords: Combo-OK, Counterattack
    Duration: Instant
    Prereqs: Fiery Garda Force Attack, Flashing Zephyr Speed Stance, Floating Nymph Grace Evasion, Gruesome Wood King Revelry Technique

    [Insert fluff here.] This Charm grants an unarmed Martial Arts counterattack upon successfully parrying an attack. The dice pool for this counterattack is equal to the character's Martial Arts score plus the number of successes by which the original attack failed.

    Loyal Dancing Lovers Technique
    Cost: 7m 1w; Mins: Martial Arts 5, Essence 3; Type: Simple (Speed 6, DV -1)
    Keywords: Combo-OK, Obvious
    Duration: Scene
    Prereqs: Loving Heart Stance

    [Insert fluff here.] While this Charm is active, the character must choose between either of the following modes, between which she may switch reflexively when her DV refreshes:
    -Sword Mode: At any time before the next time her DV refreshes, she may make a single unarmed Martial Arts attack reflexively at her full dice pool.
    - Scabbard Mode: The character’s parry DV increases by 2.

    Regardless of which mode she chooses, she reduces the total DV penalty inflicted upon her by making unarmed Martial Arts attacks by 2. For example, if she made a flurry of three attacks with -1 DV penalties, she would only suffer a total of -1 to her DV from that flurry, rather than -3.

BTW, I am working on a short backstory and wondering how to tie it in my abilities - what language do they speak in the Northwestern port cities, Seatongue, Low Realm or something else?

                              Story – in one of the windy ports of the Northwest there once was a boy called Federiko. At first he was little different than most of his peers, like most of the children there he sought excitement and wonder for his little world. In Port Cabrast, there were not that many forms of excitement, but he tried every one and wanted to invent a few more – new games, new tricks and, as he grew, new affairs. Yet of all those, his favourite excitement was to be surrounded by a garrulous group in the warm room of the tavern as tales were told of strange lands and great deeds as the wind blew outside, its icy teeth held at bay by the thick door but his howl adding to the charm of the tale. He himself was not the greatest of storytellers – though he was far from incapable – but an avid peruser. He eventually apprenticed himself in one of the small, dimly lit taverns where he served as a waiter, barkeep and at times bouncer, and in those dark evenings, he found his second passion – people. They all had stories in which they were the heroes, the villains, the distressed ones or even the onlookers, and each tale fascinated him. Some were simple, some were exotic, but each was a new world. In time, he sought to tug at the strings of those tales – prompting the protagonist to try this, say that, and hopefully get the story set to its proper path. To his credit, he found himself often succeeding, though he soon found out success wasn’t always what he imagined it to be. Well, he thought, gods willing he would have a lifetime to improve. Something inside him wanted to set things right, as if perceiving the wrongness of the tales as a jarring note.
                              Then, fate happened, and his little world was taken away from him, substituted by the promise to ply his talents on a scene unimaginably greater. He was thrilled by the opportunity at first, although he quickly became somewhat jaded with the factional baggage and hierarchy he found, in particular in the department he was assigned for, which he quickly coined several new nicknames for. On the other hand, the work he was provided with was occasionally quite enjoyable, and he found the days spent behind the huge bars at the Cerulean Lute to be fascinating beyond compare – and yet, somehow familiar. There is a sweet irony at learning how the spirit to which down-on-their-luck sailors back home is itself having a hard year, and it sometimes takes all his self-control to skew his smile into an understanding grimace. He has also made a couple of friends in Yu-Shan and back on Creation, and overall, considers his life a rather well enjoyed one. Getting his own landship, which he partly converted into an impromptu bar, is probably his greatest joy, and it is one of the reasons he so much likes spending time back in creation. He has so far avoided joining any of the major fractions at his time, preferring to keep his options open and focus on the work itself. It has not helped his career much, but he sees no reason to hurry.

                              Federiko is now the man everyone instinctively calls barkeep. He is a large, fairly calm man (well-groomed moustache) in well-made clothes, moving around with a practiced and subdued grace. He favors white and dark grey or black, but always with some blue accessory. After all, one should respect one's trade.
