        Oscar holds out his left arm. "I'll need to touch you, I think, so if you just grasp my arm..."

        Okay, deep breaths...

        He breathes in the Primal Wild again, seeing in his soul himself and his two newfound companion; he sees them as he breathes life into their collective pattern. The thrill courses through his blood, ambition rising in his act - ambition that leads him to stretch further than he has ever done with his magic.

        Then the chill grazes his soul.

        He feels... - The void? I thought... but... I had cast it away... - as it clings to the edges of his spell, threatening to taint it. He grasps his pendant with his right hand, in an attempt to find solace. A faint growl emerges in his throat, almost daring it to try. Yet its abhorrence frightens him; he exerts his will in an attempt to finish the spell faster. The spell forms and is cast, and the threat slinks away ... though in the aftermath, he stands distraught, clutching the silver lynx tightly.

        ... What was that...?

        Lynx casts Life 2 "Body Control." He casts it once Dr. Asphodel and Ragnar grasp his arm; if neither of them accept the spell, he still casts it.

        Potency: 2.
        Advanced Scale, 3 targets: Himself, Dr. Asphodel, Ragnar.
        Duration: 1 Week.
        Base Dice Pool: Life 2 "Body Control": Life (3) + Gnosis (1) + Willpower (+3) - Potency 2 (-2).
        Reaches: 2 Free Reaches: 1 spent on Instant Cast, 1 spent on Advanced Duration factor. 2 Additional Reaches: 1 spent on Advanced Scale factor, 1 spent because he has Heightened Senses active.
        Base Paradox dice pool: 2. He uses his Dedicated Tool to reduce Paradox dice by 2.
        Final Paradox dice pool: 0. If he gains Paradox, he releases as his instincts tell him not to accept it into his pattern.

        Roll for Paradox, 0 dice → 0 successes.

        Total dice pool for Life 2 "Body Control": 5 dice.

        Roll for Life 2 "Body Control" → 2 successes.

                            Moonstone
                            Active Sight: Mind/Space. Active Spells: None
                            Conditions: Divergent (active), Poor Light, Abyssal Nimbus, Abyssal Imago
                            Health: BOOOOO | Willpower: 0/8 | Mana: 5/10


                            Everything is terrible! Some of the reflections of warped postcognition that Moonstone sees in his mirror:

                            ---

                            A bruised and tired young Vietnamese woman in a hospital bed, being prompted to sign some paperwork while a nurse holds her newborn. An adolescent girl furiously shaving her head in the bathroom as her parents pound on the locked door. Watching a 9th grader threaten to do terrible things to another 9th grader, walking away and saying nothing. Pawning Mom's jewelry for drug money. A dance instructor shoving her finger in his red and puffy face and telling "Amelia" that she is lazy, ungraceful, and too fucking fat to be in this Intermediate Ballet II classroom in anything but a tree costume. Homeless in Chicago, getting the shit kicked out of him at the bus terminal. Being fired as a Wal-Mart greeter (incompetence+customer complaints, "too friendly"). Realizing that he is never, ever going to have children.

                            Improperly preparing the wards for a summoning ritual, being severely injured by the angry spirit along with the rest of the Knights, Askia refusing to heal him magically: "That scar is your idiot tax. That's what this costs you personally." Rose of Sharon rolling her eyes and waving off an incident where he embarrassed the cabal in front of a Consilium gathering: "No one was expecting better from you." Practicing with a blade in the mirror the way Ghost Dog taught him, and sternly giving himself imagined instructions: "Now remember, Moonstone, when you're on the battlefield, Hakim is what matters. You don't matter." Bowing his head obediently: "I don't matter." Talking and humming and singing to himself, alone in the sanctum, pretending he's a mage. The facial expressions at the individual moments when each of the Knights decide that he is not going to Awaken.

                            Watching Hakim court various women and laugh and smile and be happy with them. Feeling shame and physical pain at how poisonous his jealousy is. Sitting on the edge of Hakim's bed, half-dressed, ugly crying. Hakim saying: "You make it hard to love you." Hakim saying: "You aren't thinking straight right now, that's your symptoms talking." Hakim saying: "Just because I say something once doesn't mean I feel the same way forever." Hakim saying: "There need to be boundaries between us." Hakim saying: "I do not love you."

                            Most of these he remembers as he watches them, but there's one extended scene that he doesn't remember at all:
                            A closed door behind which he and Hakim are having a mild argument about some regrettable thing Hakim did that escalates quickly into Moonstone screaming and shattering picture frames. Hakim is kind and patient and increasingly scared as he fails to explain the situation and calm him down. A blade is shoved through the closed door, followed by the door being pushed open and Moonstone stalking out alone, breathing hard and clutching an ornate enchanted sword and streaming tears. He swings in a frenzy, tearing at the walls of the sanctum, slices wide and deeply cuts his own arm and yelps and falls to his knees. Ghost Dog rushes in, shouts something. Hakim stumbles through the door, clutching his abdomen, and says "Undo today." Ghost Dog chants in the High Speech, draws his own blade, and deftly cuts through Time-

                            ---

                            Moonstone (eyes wide, transfixed, panicked) suddenly hurls his mirror against the brick wall. The other magician appearing out of thin air makes him jump and push away from the wall. "NO! I am not okay! I exposed the door in the bricks, but I pushed too far, and now it's in me! It's in the mirror! Oh God, all my glass is breaking and now the shard's in my soul and I can only see ugly things!"

                            Under Mind Sight, it would be clear that his thoughts are disordered and scratchy, standing out like poison under Life Sight.

                                                        Dr. Asphodel, Lynx, and Ragnar
                                                        After preparing as much as possible for any potential threats, at least as much as you newly Awakened mages currently know how to prepare, you agree to cautiously investigate the magical door Ragnar observed down past the courtyard by a nearby building.

                                                        Between your conversation and introductions of the magical sort, a few more minutes have passed -- probably 15 since the hail started falling -- but the storm is still in full force outside. The scheduled end of today's class period passed a few minutes ago.

                                                        With Lynx's Life magic, he is able to sense that there are fewer people downstairs. Some have assumedly gone to a student lounge in the building, while others very likely left for their dorms or homes. The doorway, Ragnar senses, is leeching out of a brick wall on the far side of the building opposite the humanities building, in a small alley-like alcove. You didn't notice anyone wandering around outside, and it's still pretty dark. You may be able to get out of the building unseen if you're careful.

                                                        Trying to move this scene along without pressuring anyone into any specific action. If you do approach the Iris and do want to scrutinize, use Opacity 5 and treat it as shared Opacity for purpose of this specific Mystery (as I did with the other one in this scene).


                                                        Moonstone
                                                        ...And then you blink, and the traumatic visions in front of you are gone. All you see is the door, still giving you this hunger for more knowledge, and your shattered mirror on the floor.

                                                        You temporarily lose the Divergent condition you created. You can still do Revelation with Space magic or any other Arcanum you like at 0 Opacity. FYI, since you didn't Withstand or resist the Mind spell at the time of its casting, as far as I can see per RAW you can't later change your mind or "will it away." You either need to dispel or Binder needs to release it.



                                                        Phoenix
                                                        And the truth, as they say, shall set you free.

                                                        To your focused lens of Prime, your careful scrutiny of the door finally reveals information you nearly missed earlier. You know beyond any doubt that the hail, the storms, and the Iris are interrelated. Perhaps surprisingly to you, you can tell from the threads of magic subtly surrounding the door that no mage could have created it, because it's not actually a spell. That defies your current knowledge of the Supernal; a powerful spell could conceivably mimic the effect, but even when studying the Iris with your keen Prime Sight, you see no evidence of a nimbus reflected in, or imposed on, this doorway.

                                                        You can also see now that the Iris is...dynamic in some way. Before, you witnessed the ways that mana was pulled into the portal, and it very much reminded you of how that hail seemed to pull energy only from Moonstone. Now, you see echoes of the Abyss in Moonstone's nimbus and saw a black streak of pure negative energy break off from the door to latch onto him in some way. Before you thought that maybe the Iris went to some Abyssal realm. After all, that's what Moonstone claimed, isn't it? But with your closer look, you see that the black energy is actually superimposed on a portal that seems somehow analogous to Space magic. It's like a thin, oily film covering a pure screen.

                                                        It all clicks. The portal isn't precisely some link to some Abyssal realm. Moonstone's aura is luring, or attracting, something that resonates with him and it's hijacking the portal as a medium to make the connection.
