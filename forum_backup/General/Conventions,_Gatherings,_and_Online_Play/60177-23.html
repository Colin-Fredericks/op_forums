                        (Jarsiln)

                        The facility is approximately three cubic kilometers in size. The actual complex you are in is about three hundred meters tall, and from what you've seen and gleamed from your tentative forays into the public infonet, you could see that the construction of the manse is largely indestructible from conventional means. However, should one destroy the hearthstone room, the widespread power system feedback ought to destroy or render the entire place inoperable.

                        There have been six Gauntlets, apart from the one that's happening right now. Jarsiln has seen the last three, and the one that actually succeeded was the one right before this one.


                        (Distraction team)

                        The electric figure bows to Iereniel, and says, "Of course, Lord. The Planetary Lord is one Sable Thesis Dream, a Defiler currently employed by the Prince of Repugnance, Kurteur, Seventh Soul of Metagaos. Kurteur sought to create a temple-planet to himself, but I have heard that she has co-opted the project into some sort of Exaltation factory. After all, who would care about the deaths of such Rusemin vermin? Although....Perhaps those soldiers could help?" the Attaman says, turning to the Dragonblooded soldiers.

                        "You there, could you direct us to Sable Thesis Dream?" the Attaman asks, as the soldiers turn to Wren.

                        (Amelia)

                        Amelia lowers a nearby cable, and drags Kankos out. He wipes himself off, and he sighs and shrugs.

                        "Who am I to argue with a pretty girl? Lead the way," he says. They reach the starting point of the Gauntlet, and a hologram appears before them.

                        "Ah, so you survived. As to be expected of a Neptune Caste Titanic Exalt.But, can you survive this, I wonder?" the hologram of Sable Thesis Dream pushes her glasses up her brow, and then disappears.

                        Then, the surrounding area goes black, and both Amelia and Kankos feel hemmed in, as though in a casket barely large enough for them. Pain and fear begin to creep up their bodies, and shooting pain lances out of their skeletons, as a single thought echoes through their minds.

                        "KILL YOU"

                        They then hear a piercing feral scream. Something very bad is coming.
