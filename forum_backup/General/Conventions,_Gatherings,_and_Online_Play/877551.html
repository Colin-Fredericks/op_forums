    Hosting a Exalted 3rd Edition Skype Game

    04-29-2016, 07:29 AM
    Hello All,

    I am looking players to run a Exalted 3rd Edition Skype game both new and experienced players are welcome. All I ask is that you have a concept of the kind of Solar Exalt you want to play, have patience as we adjust to the new system and gaming on Skype as a whole. Once I have a player group established I will work with you to identify a meeting time and a gaming night. For the start I am looking to do a bi-weekly gaming session.

    The campaign will ultimately start in Nexus but from there depending on player choices and feedback the story could transition to any number of locals and focuses. So again I suggest you build the Exalt you want to play.

    If this sounds like a game you be interested in joining please post below or e-mail me and I will get back with you.

    Thank you
