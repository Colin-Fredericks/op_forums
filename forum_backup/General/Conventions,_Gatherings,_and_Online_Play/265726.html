    Back and looking to play: Skype game recruiting

    09-25-2014, 05:08 PM
    Things have been abit messy, come back to find a new forum but figure I'd just dive back in anyways and assume no new etiquette had been added that I am aware of.

    WHERE
    Game will be held over skype voice chat cause while I do enjoy a nice text based adventure I've grown more fond of talking to human beings instead of just reading what they write. Hopefully I'm not in the minority.

    WHEN
    Thinking Friday evening. I figure that would be when most people are free but if you are interested please list when you can and cannot play.

    WHAT
    Exalted 2e. Will detail rule adjustments and stuff if people are interested. The story will start basic so we can focus more on building the characters up and getting you invested. However I do eventually plan on using my Devourer campaign setting. Those of you that remember the old forum may recall this setting as the one with the unknown army wiping out existence. It originated from the wyld but those that escaped say it is completely alien to the fae.

    WHO
    All solaroids (though no broken wing crane for infernals, sorry), if you wish to play sidereal, lunar, or alchemic you can but things will be balanced for solar teir so keep that in mind.

    WHY
    Because the game will be awesome

                      I am EST, though am flexible on time. If most people was on the west coast I dont mind starting the game in what I'd consider later. I'd aim for about 8 or 9pm EST but that is flexible by a few hours.

                      Originally posted by JohnDoe244 View Post
                      Ninja'd!

                      And how broken/optimised/min-maxed a character can I make? No BWC, but any other off-limits sources? Lords of Creation? Scroll of Swallowed Darkness? Which artifact rules are we using (Core? Wonders? Codex?)? Custom manses? Custom charms? How will Player NPCs work?
                      Ok since there is interest I will get into some of this, keep in mind I haven't been around in over two years so forgive me if things have changed.

                      No Flaws or Merits
                      I like this system, I really do but its too easy to break the game to allow it for a new group

                      Errata
                      Sure its changed, but last I checked the tweaks were good enough for me to default to using it.

                      Banned Books
                      No Lords of Creation, between me not having it and it having quite a few unbalanced artifacts and charms I'm saying no on it. Scroll of Swallowed Darkness? If you really want, sure go ahead. But keep the temperment of the group in mind. If I have to I will do a mid-game ruling if the material makes people uncomfortable.

                      Artifacts
                      I tend to be stricter on individual artifacts the more standard they are. Grand daiklaive and power armor will not go over well, Resplendant recorder or perfect boots I will pretty much always give a pass on. I also dont consider "I found a manse full of stuff" to be legitimate justification on why you have 10 dots of artifacts. The rules for making them will be a modified version of the codex emphasizing the journey of its creation. In other words I want to focus on you RPing the process and care far more about that than you running all over creation for something specific.

                      Perfect Defenses
                      I go with these limitations. To dodge you must have somewhere to dodge too, to parry it must be something parry-able. Stunting can get around these limitations, in practice it just means you'll have to put some thought into it.and not blindly rely on it.

                      Stunting
                      You wont get motes for this. One die just means putting in some effort to RP. Two die is creative use of whats around you. Three die is doing something especially awesome. If you do a three die stunt in addition to the regular perks everyone will get bonus exp.

                      Homebrew
                      I do allow it but not to start, if you some how find my homebrew like Paper Crane Style or Party Cannon you can use that too

                      Min/Max
                      A degree to twinking is ok but if you are nothing but one and five dots there will be some issues. Also use logic, like I dont care how much of a military genius your dynast prodigy is there is no way your family would let you get away with zero lore and war. This is as much for the players as balance. I tend to throw out threats where "hit it until it dies" mentality will get you killed. Basic knowledge of things will give you more options and make it less likely to die from an overlooked threat. I wont fault you for having stats you really care about with five dots, but I will if your entire sheet has your character as a complete master or novice with no middle ground.

                      Thats all the stuff that comes to mind right now.

                                                      Another quick rule than a text dump.
                                                      BACKGROUNDS
                                                      Exp will not be gained or lost for backgrounds gained or lost during role playing but will be if it occurs during downtime. So if we play out a scene where your player convinces freed slaves to join him you get them at no cost. If I ask "what are you doing for the next week while your sorcerer prepares to summon a demon" and you say recruit followers at the inn that will cost exp. On the flip side if in a fight someone manages to knock your daiklave into a volcano (and assuming you dont have a way to get it back) than you dont gain exp.

                                                      Gonna re-write opening post to get this all down. Since no one expressed specific times I'll go with friday 8pm EST. This can be adjusted if another time works better.

                                                      I have a 3 player minimum for games 7 max. If 7 seems abit high its because with a new group its common for one or two people to drop early on. I know how eager people can be so we might start next week if that works for everyone.

                                                      Feel free to ask any questions, my skype is aerozord if you guys need to discuss anything.

                                                      I will also say one more thing because its come up as an issue and I want to be clear. There will be no GM transparency. You wont know the numerical results of rolls, I will not detail limitations of homebrew enemies or how to kill them, or give you any information that your player would not know. For this to work players will have to trust me to be fair and do what I feel makes for a good story. You will not always win fights, there will be times the correct course of action is to run. Followers can die, possessions destroyed, characters maimed. I might give some plot armor if its a big part of your character and played as such. For example your character intends to use his family daiklave to avenge his father, that is something I'd be less inclined to destroy even if its logical for an enemy to do so but I expect that to be played up and your character to also pass up better weapon. You need to show that specific artifact is important to the story.

                                                      Thanks!
                                                      1. Depends on how easy the conversion would be, how far along we are ect. What little I've seen of 3e (and keep in mind I have been out of the loop for awhile) is much of the stuff I tweak anyways when it comes to lore. Sadly the only real answer I can give is we'll have to wait and see when it comes out.

                                                      2. I hope this doesn't turn anyone off but, this will be a heroic story. Atleast what passes as heroic in Exalted. Few things bore me as quickly as running a game full of complete sociopaths. Thats the group as a whole. I'm ok if your character thinks slavery is ok or that violently torturing someone to death is acceptable because you need to get to the underworld ASAP to save a village, but I want death and suffering to have weight and that means the group as a whole needs to care.

                                                      This means a fairly serious game. I do expect and want some light-hearted fun too. Need to juxtapose all that drama. What I really mean is I expect players to take the game seriously and become invested. I will give fair warning I know how to poke and prod players. Last time I had a group in the underworld one of our solars ended up in the fetal position. In another I ran a player character snapped, going "its fine its fine, I'll just blow up everything and then it will be fine, ITS FINE." I like things like that, makes games memorable.

                                                      I know that wasn't what you were primarily asking but I knew I had to explain all that anyways so though this was a good time. Adult content, well one upside to voice chat is people tend not be as gratuitous with the sex. Doesn't seem to stop violence though. I have no issue with sex, but I care about player comfort levels. So while Exalted is a game where beastiality has game mechanics if it bothers people I'll step in. Same goes for other touchy subjects, rape, torture, child brutality. I do use these lightly because its a brutal setting and the intent is to unnerve you or make you angry. Though if it bothers anyone let me know. It will exist but I can just imply it. I will also always check before I make a player character a victim even if its just implied. While I'm not a fan of plot armor a PC is too close to the actual player for me to risk it.

                                                      In any case if I feel it takes away from the story I'll step in. Typically the less impact it has the more I'll gloss over it. If your character is hedonist that gets laid in every town ok fine but not going into detail about something so common. If sex is the culmination of a relationship built up over a year of real rime I can understand wanting it to be an actual scene to role play. As a GM I will also be graphic if thats the idea. Going to the home of The Lover or house Cynus or a Nexus brothel yea thats part of the setting. Likewise if you go to a Deathlord manse expect me to be detailed about the gore.

                                                      Long story short if its so common it begins to lose narrative weight I'll cut it back.

                                                      3. Voice chat. Video with that many people gets problematic and my webcam doesn't really work anymore anyways. People also tend to get more self conscious so maybe if the group is comfortable we might switch to video chat. For now, just voice.
