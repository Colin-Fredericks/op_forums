    My friends,

    I hope this letter finds you all well. I must admit, this is the first time I've heard heard of the Bellerophon Group, but I have personal experience with the Mad. During my time here in Cardiff, two Mad mages kidnapped Pisces and were planning on using her in some perverse ritual to make her give birth to one of the Supernal entities from Pandemonium, which would have killed her in the process. To save the person I love I did things that even today I regret, but even if I'm judged I would do it again to save Pisces.

    It is disturbing though to hear that so many of the Daksha have turned Mad within a short period of time, and I wonder too what the cause could be. As far as I know, both our local Daksha Janus and Thomas have been acting normal (well, as far as Daksha are capable of being normal) and we have not encountered any signs of Tulpa constructs since Pisces' kidnapping. But I have passed it along the chain, and they are being watched for anything out of the ordinary.

    This week though has not been that revealing I'm afraid. The Arrows are smarting from their rather pyrrhic victory in recapturing Jason, and are eagerly preparing themselves for their next encounter. I've had to promise to contact Gwalchmai the next time I plan to encounter Legion, as he's looking forward to regaining the honour of the Arrows. The Thyrsus' are busy with a spirit war that has started up along the high street thanks to some new shops opening and the resonance of competition has riled them up. But otherwise has been pretty quiet on the whole.

    I did have a chance to meet with Jason though. After going through several checkpoints and identity tests I managed to speak with Jason briefly. He seemed haunted by his experience, understandable considering what he went through for the past few weeks. Unfortunately, he wasn't conscious for parts of his capture, which from the sounds of it was due to spells. But he did remember a time the kidnappers spoke to someone who visited. From his description, it sounded like the man that had the Irish accent. I got a     name for him though - Buarainech    . I've looked up the obvious mythological background for the name, but he's definitely not local. Just in case, I've sent a message to the Carmarthen consilium who keeps the most complete records of mages in Wales asking if anyone goes by that shadow name, although I doubt there will be much luck with that.

    You are of course right Morrigan, that this Legion may be a powerful mage but certainly not impossible to beat and should be something that I keep in mind. It was just something to see so many of the Arrow put down so quickly, and if you happen to know the quality of the local Arrows (apart from some members like the former Kay) that is no mean feat. I will not do him the favour of underestimating him, and shall keep an eye out for him in the future. As well, you're welcome Morrigan. I hope what I said has helped.

    Regards,

    Phoenix

                I hope this letter finds you all well. This week has given me a chance to take my motorcycle out for the day and visit a place that I've always wanted to visit ever since I decided to come back to Cardiff after my Awakening - Carmarthen, the home of a legend.

One of the two largest Consilium in the UK apart from Cardiff, I received a warm welcome despite my initial worries considering the background between Carmarthen and Cardiff. To give you some background Carmarthen, despite being a medium-sized town by British standards, claimed jurisdiction over all the south of Wales for almost a hundred years. When Cardiff was awarded city status many of the mages living here wanted to separate and form their own consilium. Words eventually turned into action, and a Wizard War started. Luckily for the Pentacle in my opinion, it didn't last long as World War One put it to an end. A treaty was written and signed granting Cardiff its independence in return for being allowed access to the Coiling Stone athenaeum. And that is the history of why Cardiff is considered an independent state of sorts here in the south.

As someone who enjoys law cases, I personally found reading through some of them very interesting and look forward to going back sometime. It is worrying that so many of the Daksha have escaped under the eyes of your watchers, and you're right that trying to track one of the Mad using magic is next to impossible. I hope you can track down these people and soon before they do something.
