    Alright, so I've talked over my character with Flinty and I'm ready to show her off now.

    Maya Abernathy is the daughter of Pakistani immigrants to the USA. Her mother was a professor of sociology, her father a respected lawyer. In America, her mother became a primary school teacher, and her father became an accountant. After high school, Maya ended up studying law, and was "graduated" after burning most of her professional bridges. One of her professors, Alexander Denovo, ran an absolutely brutal internship program, one that he supplemented with Mind magic and thorough satisfaction in controlling and denying the consent of his students (especially the women). Maya Awakened after a close friend of hers, fellow law student Daphne Muir, had a complete breakdown in Denovo's firm from a combination of natural deprivation and the use of the Mind Arcanum.

    After law school (but before involvement in the Awakened community) she worked at a coffeeshop and dispensed pro bono legal advice to friends and members of the community, but managed to just barely keep her head above water. That is, until she was picked up Elayne Kevarian, senior partner at Albrecht and Ao, known to the Awakened world as Kebechet. Maya, now operating with the Shadow Name Tara, was ushered into the Awakened world, joining the Silver Ladder and being employed as a junior partner of Albrecht and Ao before becoming initiated as a member of Elayne's (and Denovo's) Legacy, the Deathless Kings, known more commonly as the Craftsmen. Now, she advocates for the living, the dead, and the Awakened, trying to resolve conflicts in a non-adversarial manner, while studying how relationships, Sympathy, and obligations influence destiny, paying off her student loans, and trying to find a cure for Daphne (whose parents she still speaks to regularly).

    Character Concept: Idealistic Lawyer Virtue: Compassionate Vice: Hasty ​Obsession: How does Sympathy impact Destiny?

    Path: Acanthus Order: Silver Ladder Legacy: Deathless Kings (Craftsmen)

    Rote Skills: Expression, Persuasion, Subterfuge, Academics

    Nimbus: Shadows and Starlight Dedicated Tool: A silver inlaid kirpan that belonged to her grandfather

    Aspirations: Settle a ghost's estate, pay off my Student Loans, find a cure for Daphne

    Mental Attributes: Intelligence 2, Wits 3, Resolve 3 Physical Attributes: Strength 1, Dexterity 3, Stamina 2

    Social Attributes: Presence 3, Manipulation 2, Composure 4* Health: 7 Speed: 9 Initiative: 7 Willpower: 7/7

    Mental Skills: Academics 3+ (Law), Investigation 1, Politics 2, Occult 2*

    Physical Skills: Athletics 1, Survival 1, Weaponry 2 (Knives)

    Social Skills: Empathy 3 (Obligations)Mystery Cult Initiation, Expression 3 (Appeals), Persuasion 3+, Subterfuge 2

    Gnosis: 2 Mana: 10/11 Arcana: Fate 2, Death 2, Time 2 Praxes: Divination, Exceptional Luck

    Rotes: Warding Gesture (Subterfuge), Postcognition (Academics), Shifting the Odds (Subterfuge)

    Merits: Order Status (Silver Ladder) 1*, High Speech*, Mystery Cult Initiation 4 (Speciality in Obligations [Empathy], Pusher, Professional Training [Law: Persuasion and Academics. Contacts: Funeral Homes, DA's Office] 2, Mentor [Elayne Kevarian: Politics, Socialize, Resources] 3), Shadow Name 1, Fighting Finesse, Trained Observer 1

    Note: Two Merit dots sacrificed for Legacy Attainments

    *= Derived from Mage Template + = Asset Skills for Professional Training
    The Deathless Kings (The Craftsmen)



    Originating in Eastern Europe at the end of the Reformation in the 17th Century, this Legacy was founded by a doctor of law known as Maester Gerhardt. He had many theories about the nature of the human soul, writing a treatise known as Die Thaumas, which posited that the nature of Sympathy, Destiny, magic, and even divinity, was that of systems in relationship with one another. Those who could learn the laws of that divine system could learn to manipulate them, and those with sufficiently powerful obligations could extend their lives well beyond a human lifetime, ending their lives only when they are good and ready and have planned a suitable afterlife, whether in the Underworld, using their own corpse, or even stranger means.

    Modern Craftsmen seek to continue Gerhardt's work, and also mediate between the living, the dead, the Awakened, and others, using their Attainments to forge enduring agreements between multiple parties. Ghosts and the mysteries of death and the Underworld are of particular interest to them, since ghosts seem to be defined by their obligations, their Anchors, and the things that they have left behind. By making and enforcing agreements and Oaths, they can bless the well-intentioned, punish the guilty, and maybe (just maybe) keep someone alive through their debts to others.

    Origins
    Parentage: Acanthus, Silver Ladder, Seers of the Throne
    Background: The Deathless Kings draw from careers based in community and interpersonal relationships. Lawyers, social workers, educators, academics. Those who have built a web of obligations between themselves and others who feel the need to give back.
    Appearance: Craftsmen prefer to resemble professional mediators. Suits and the fashion favoured by professional academia are the order of the day, but whatever they need to look authoritative and in control is the order of the day.

    Doctrine
    Prerequisites:     Death 2, Persuasion 2, and two dots of any of the following skills; Academics, Expression, Empathy, Investigation, Subterfuge
    Initiation: Prospective members of the Legacy are chosen by a mentor, hired for a single mediation "case". The apprentice must perform to the standards of their mentor, resolving the case successfully and preferably in favour of their client. If they have performed to the satisfaction of their mentor, and can successfully defend themselves under the scrutiny of one or more senior members of the Legacy, then they have become a Craftsman.
    Organization: The Deathless Kings generally organize themselves into Mystery Cult "firms", meant to professionally mediate and seek out potential students among the ranks of their interns, employees, and competition. Firms are run by senior students, and the most prestigious firms are run from the shadows by some of the original students of Gerhardt himself. They generally operate across an entire state or similarly sized region, senior partners travelling where they may, and having their pick of junior members to call to bring with them on assignment.
    Theory: Obligations and relationships tie us to the world, shape our personality, affect our lives and afterlives, and can even alter our Destinies. It is best, therefore, to cultivate advantageous relationships and enforce them through Oaths and Vows. Transactional relationships are the order of the day, and by taking control of them, you can take control of your place in the cosmos.

    Note: All Attainments of the Craftsmen can ignore Withstand if the target has broken an agreement with them, and reduces Withstand by one if the target is upholding an agreement with them.

    First: Measure the Soul
    Prerequisite: Initiation
    A Craftsman would be negligent to not check a prospective client (or their opposition) for the obligations that weight heaviest on them. This Attainment mimics the spell "Soul Marks" with Reach allocated to Instant casting and Sensory Range.
    Optional: Fate 1
    The Craftsman can expand their view of the web of agreements and connections in which their client (or opponent) sits at the centre. This Attainment mimics the spell "Interconnections" with Reach allocated to Instant casting and detecting possession, supernatural mind control, and alterations of destiny.

    Second: Secure the Client
    Prerequisite: Death 2, Persuasion 3
    Those most in need of a Craftsman's services are the dead, as they are those with the least say in their obligations, tied roughly to the unfinished business symbolized by their Anchors. This Attainment mimics the spell "Speak with the Dead" with Reach allocated to Instant use, Advanced Duration, and communicating with Rank 2+ Ghosts regardless of language.
    Optional: Fate 2
    Having assessed the needs of a client, a Craftsman can draft an agreement. Over a scene of negotiation between the relevant parties involved in the process, the Craftsman at least ensures that they can know whether their mediation resulted in an agreement of good faith. This Attainment mimics the spell "Oaths Fulfilled", with Reach allocated to Advanced Duration, seeing a brief vision of the fulfilled agreement, and tracking the subjects until the Duration elapses.

                                                I went with a Mastigos in the end, albeit one with a touch of Death about him. (Unfinished) Sheet here. https://www.myth-weavers.com/sheet.html#id=1654900

                                                There is more detail regarding backstory in the sheet, but I will post the cliffnotes below.

                                                Veles was already in his 30's when he Awakened, but looking back he thinks he spent most of the previous 20 years trying to escape an inevitable trip to Hell. He certainly always seemed to be on the run from something. From an early age, he would hear voices no one else could, and occasionally glimpse horrors no one else could see. He soon learned to keep his uncanny observations to himself, struggling in school and becoming something of a loner.

                                                Perhaps as a result Martin has been mixed up in organised crime for most of his life, largely specialising in housebreaking, car-jacking and occasional drug smuggling. He Awakened and came to the Guardians attention at roughly the same time, when he was unwittingly hired by a Seer to steal a Grimoire from a Guardian storehouse. Against the odds he succeeded, and it was only when the old book started to to talk to him that things started to go sideways. The entity trapped inside the book tried to possess the thief, intent on devouring his soul and wearing his flesh like a new suit. In the midst of the spiritual confrontation, Martin made a commitment to never again run from his Demons and finally Awakened.

                                                Damascus, the Guardian in charge of the storehouse, tracked him down in time to retrieve the Grimoire and save the newly Awakened Martin. He took the man in, and taught him what he knew of Magic.

                                                Taking on the Shadow name Veles after the dark Slavic god of the underworld, Martin threw himself into his new life. His skills as a housebreaker now serve the guardians, and he has successfully retrieved all manner of relics and documents for the Order in the last few years. However he now finds himself in a strange place, a middle aged man with no real 'normal' life. He has few friends, no sleeper job, and lives in a rented one room flat. While his Obsession with magic is as strong as ever, he has made the conscious decision to try and build a more solid foundation for himself. After consulting with his Order colleagues, giving up the live of a Solitaire and petitioning to join a Cabal seems like a good first step.
