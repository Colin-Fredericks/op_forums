    Gauging Interest: (Chronicles of) Dark Tournament

    05-12-2016, 01:39 PM
    So, I've been revisiting an old favorite anime, YuYu Hakusho. And I just can't get the idea out of my head of a massive Chronicles of Darkness crossover PvP PbP tournament in the style of so many Shonen anime. I realize it's pure silly nonsense, but I eat that shit up. I can help it. So, I figured, what the hell? There have got to be at least a few folks out there as massively dorky as me who would find this kind of madness appealing. So what say you? Anybody else down for some good ol' over-the-top senseless PvP violence?

    Going by Willow now, or Wil for short. She/Her/Hers.

                      Something like this could theoretically involve a *lot* of players, but I also figure if we can get at least 5 or 6 players, maybe willing to run multiple characters, that could work too.

                      Something that will have to be considered as well is what edition to use. Personally, I much prefer the second edition system. On the other hand, 1st edition has a lot more published options for things like Fighting Styles and Z-Splats. Either way there are a few templates that wouldn't be available without some conversion work, though. That's a bridge we can cross when we come to it, once we've got a better idea what the player base is going to look like, but it's something to consider.
