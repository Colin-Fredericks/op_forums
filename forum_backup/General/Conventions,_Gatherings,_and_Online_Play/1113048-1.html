                                                        Briar Rabbit is likely what most people think of when they think about the Acanthus. Carefree, foolish, spontaneous, yet as prone to cruel barbs and malevolent grudges as benign aid. She's also fairly foolish. Serving as the Daoine Maithe for the Guardians of the Veil, Briar is a skilled manipulator both mundane and mystical. Her preferences are in the labyrinth, where she ensures that only the worthy get to awaken, while the less deserving remain trapped in the honey pot. She advertises herself as a career agent, whose able to get you that guaranteed well paying gig and she can. That gig can be as much blessing as a curse, however, as you may be stuck in the well paying bar gig with no room for supernal growth just as easily as she can weave the web that causes the crisis of faith that sparks the flash of genius that leads to awakening.
