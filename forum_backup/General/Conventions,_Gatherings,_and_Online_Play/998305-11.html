        He fulfilled the Role of Pilgrim upon surviving the journey to the city, where it is rumored that Athanors and other secrets of the Pilgrimage could be found. It was a Milestone because he didn't complete the journey alone, upon leaving his Genitor and wandering through the wilderness for some time he came upon an Athanor that would allow him to adopt his current Refinement. He only discovered how after he meet the first other Promethean besides his Mentor, a much wiser Created who had been looking for the same Athanor as part of their Pilgrimage. Together they formed an alliance and together they became Originists, and while his newfound friend went their separate ways about a month ago, he learned invaluable lessons about the world and Prometheans from him, and tries to keep in contact as often as possible. By reaching the city with his friend's help he learned that by the Great Work can only be achieved by working together with other Pilgrims, so he now seeks more like him within the city. He doesn't realize it yet, but he's already adopted the role of Chronicler, seeking to learn more from other Pilgrims.

        As for Refinements, I feel that Aes would be the next possible step along his Pilgrimage. He's already learned the importance of cooperation and collaboration, plus his residual memories will find confront as being part of a group who share a common mission. Depending on how the story goes he might seek Aurum next though, as he need to definitely learn more about humans themselves.
