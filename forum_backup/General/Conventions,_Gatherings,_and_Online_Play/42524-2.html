                                        Originally posted by JimB View Post
                                        Was there ever actually a Charm to declare any random object to be a Throne Shadow form weapon, or am I making that up?
                                        The capstone makes any attack from your shadow fingers count as Form Weapons (make all members of circle Shadow Fingers+Horrific Wreath+World as Weapon Mastery=Profit).

                                        There's a charm that allows you to add a Form Weapon to any style you've mastered- its a Charm called Master's Hand Envisioned Anew in Dreams of the First age 2, p 157. The only errata on it is the clarification that the new form weapons must make sense for the style. The example they give is that a crossbow would be a valid addition to Wood Dragon, but not Snake.
