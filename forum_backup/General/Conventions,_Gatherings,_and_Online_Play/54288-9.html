    Okay a brief synopsis of what you could expect going to the capital:

    The capital city of Novalesk is home to both the Boule, the legislative body of the nation. Seats are granted to Gentes based off of their proportion of the overall population. Additionally, the capital is home to several (nominally) non-partisan entities, such as the Strategoi (the overall military commanders), the Academy of Sorcerous Wisdom (a state-sponsored school of thaumaturgy and sorcery), and the Temple of Origenes (Temple to the City-Father, and headquarters of the nation's administrative bureaucracy).

    Going to the capital now will get you access to representatives of all Gentes, and other important people besides. However, getting to actually speak with them and getting them to work with you will be difficult without a local sponsor. Getting sponsored by Gens Ptreas would open many doors to you, but close others.

    So, its a trade-off; have more time to muck around in the power-players in general, or spend some time now to get a specific sponsor, which will make navigating those social circles easier?

                    Alright. Orchid waves as she leaves, shouting that she'll look for you once she's back in town. Once you regain contact with Tirien's footman, he tells you that the House elders generally hold audiences and interviews in the mid-afternoon of each non-Holy-day. This practice was established to give individual members of the Gens a formal method to contact their leadership, to voice their concerns, opinions, and generally keep the elders in touch with their extended family. The only people who are expressly forbidden from requesting an interview are the members of other Gentes; many a foreign merchant or envoy has made use of the practice in the past. You have enough time to grab a hot meal and get the road-dust off you at the public bath-house before interviews start. The large hall which Tirien's lackey points you towards is packed with people.

                    As you enter, A tall, balding middle-aged man, who is holding a large slate asks for your names, and reason for wishing to see the elders. How will you respond?

                                                    "A Legion, huh? That's all?" the old man says, his voice dripping with sarcasm, "If I had a legion, this whole mess would have been solved already."

                                                    He shakes his head as if to clear it. "Perhaps it would be simpler if I explained our history. Our nation was founded in the days following the great contagion. Our ancestors lived in a kingdom to the east, in what is now the Wyld. The population was ravaged by the Contagion, to the point that the Fae simply ignored the measly band of survivors the the environmental terrors of the Wyld. So we began travelling West. So many of us fell. Of the hundreds of thousands who once lived in our kingdom, barely a hundred survived.

                                                    "But one of the survivors was Beloved of the gods. Her name was Myrinne, and she was a architect, scholar and leader without peer. Our histories say that she was blessed by the hand of the Sun, gifted powers to save our people. Within twenty years under her leadership, the ragged band of survivors had built the town of Novalesk, and it was a prosperous town. Nearly fifty years after the contagion, she traveled downriver towards Nexus, and was never seen again. But she bore three children, each with one of her gifts. Nikoloi, the eldest, a skilled scholar and sorcerer. Zopyrus, the younger, a skilled diplomat and leader of men. And Ptreas, the youngest, a skilled worker of stone and earth. The three eldest Gentes of Novalesk are founded on these bloodlines, and took the names of our founders in imitation of the families of the Dragon-blooded.

                                                    "Despite its age, my Gens has a mere eleven thousand members, giving us only six of the fifty votes in the Boule. It would depopulate our entire family to build a legion. Nikoloi and Zophyrus, are significantly better, but even they would be hard pressed to put an entire legion into the field individually. Which is just as well, as the two are a step away from being at each others' throats at the moment. We could muster a measly five-hundred trained spearmen. Double that if we convinced our cadet families to contribute. But I still haven't heard anything from you or your lackeys that makes me feel like I can trust you, Dynast."

                                                        Tirien blinked. These people had lineages as esteemed as her own, and no less storied. She considered her words carefully, and then bowed.

                                                        "I apologize deeply, Ser. I was unaware of the storied lineage that your house commands, as it seems we are cousins of a sort; my clan being formed in that selfsame catastrophe. To make amends, I will be perfectly honest with you; I have become disillusioned with the Realm. The Great Houses, once bastions of morality and integrity of Law, are now slack, fattened with excess and debauchery. However, I am a thinker, and cannot sit still, and so I have sought a nation I could make great with my abilities. I believe that Novalesk is this nation, and so, I would seek to defend her. Thanks to little Dero here, I have sought you out, as your clan's industry has impressed me. I believe, together, we could not only defend Novalesk, but also make it greater than before. I envision that Novalesk could become a power, not only in the River Province, but also making her weight known across the East and even to the Realm. And above all of this, Gens Pteras could be at the helm, if we act quickly and assume command of the defensive efforts. So, what do you say, Ser, will you trust a woman who seeks nothing more than what you do, but also to make your nation and your clan great?"
