                                        I think Scott gravitated to the Children of Gaia because they have an outlook that makes him comfortable. He grew up in an out-of-state "cult compound"-like environment that included more than a few really macho guys, with the Silver Fang pack being composed of members of his large family. Scott never really fit into that mold though. He's not much for hunting and seems to lack a lot of aggression, with more of a caretaker mentality. Scott would prefer a peaceful solution to problems, despite the fact that he understand there are times for fighting. The Children of Gaia are also the sort of tribe to take in Garou from other tribes, and Scott would be drawn to them at least on an unconscious level simply because he wants to be a part of that nurturing environment, instead of the strange way he grew up.

                                        If I were to do Stargazers, I'd have to change some aspects to Scott. Currently, Scott is more of a social character. He fits as the "heart" of a group. Not the brains, brawn, or big personality type. This mindset is probably what is most similar to Geoff, rather than merely tribal identity. As a Stargazer, Scott would have to be a bit more of an intellectual than he currently is. In the setting, only a small number of the Stargazers remained after they left for Asia, with the promise that they would send any new Garou born to them to Asia as well. This wouldn't fit for Scott, since he is of Silver Fang lineage, not Stargazer. So what I think would be most likely is that an elder member of the Stargazers, who is respected by the local Garou Nation, intends on either departing for Asia, or even departing the material world for a part of the Umbra so deep he does not plan to return. He'd be the last member of his pack alive, perhaps over a century old, but does not want to deprive the Garou Nation of the bonds he's formed with various spirits, other shapechangers, or strange beings that he has come to understand.

                                        In this, he would introduce Scott to the Stargazers. While a lot of this seems to be a stretch on Scott's concept, one aspect of him is that he is a prospective author, and has quite an imagination. Instead of being brought into a community of Children of Gaia and Fianna as I had originally intended, he could instead go on spirit voyages and faerie adventures on what to him feels like a strange journey to enlightenment and out-of-this-world fiction. This can fit with Scott because he has a big heart that can help bridge gaps that might exist between the Garou Nation and potential non-Garou allies. He's not the sort to discriminate or feel superiority for being a werewolf. His teacher would be staying for this group ceremony, at which point he intends on departing forever. That would be a big source of anxiety for Scott, since his teacher is all he has left at this point, and adds apprehension over what his pack will look like. In this version, Scott would know things. Left a mysterious journal, or taught a lot of important lessons, or having memorized cryptic sayings or something. I considered that his Stargazer mentor would of been called the "Faewalker", if he had perhaps visited the land of the fae.

                                        Sorry for the large post. It was as largely exploratory, but I want to know what everyone else thinks. Are Scott and Geoff's concepts/personalities different enough that we can have meaningful interaction without stepping on toes, or does the spiritual and mystic side add something better to the game?
