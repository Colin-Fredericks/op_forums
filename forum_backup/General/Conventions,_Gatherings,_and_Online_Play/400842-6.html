        As for Belkra's earlier question about the Tyrant, I recognize that you and Exthalion are probably going to use the Bishop, but I'd like to add this as well, just so you all can see it.

        The Glacial Tyrant of Unfettered Disaster is an anomaly among the ranks of the Deathlords. She has a penchant for pomp, high technology and overwhelming force, but she has been silent for several centuries. She loves massive super-weapons the likes of which have not been seen since the First Age, but the only traces of her seem to be elite, highly trained teams of ghosts.

        Appearing as a short, redheaded woman with a trio of scars down her face and an aquiline nose, the Tyrant isn't what one would call conventionally attractive, but she has a unique charisma that makes most deathknights amenible to her. Indeed, she gives all who wish to join her cause a thorough education and understanding of military tactics and strategy, with her deathknights being drilled in gunshoza and airship tactics, despite neither of those items being in regular use in the Age of Sorrows. That being said, once training is over and the grandiose ranks are given, the Tyrant is extraordinarily lenient in the activities of her deathknights, allowing them free reign and great use of resources. However, one thing she does not abide by is failure, which prompts unusual and cruel torments for the lapse in trust the deathknight has earned.

        While the engine of war the Tyrant has grown is steady in production, the Tower of Unending Ice, her personal forge-fortress, has not been up to her expectations for some time, with the lack of a steady stream of soulsteel impeding her efforts to making the weapons she desires. While she has come up with several solutions, like chipping frozen Hekatonkhires out of the glaciers of frozen prayer around her forge and running plasmic garment racketeering rings to ensare gullible ghosts into her clutches, even this is not enough for the megalomaniacal military genius.
