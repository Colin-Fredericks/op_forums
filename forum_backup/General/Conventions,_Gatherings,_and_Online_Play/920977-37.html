                                A few miscellaneous things here...lots of bookkeeping needed for this fight, haha.

                                I reviewed the Body Control spell and forgot that it adds to initiative. That means Ragnar and Lynx should be at +2 initiative until the spell ends.

                                Ragnar, I forgot to let you know that you should lose your bashing damage. It's healed by now, that was from inside the portal and before you took the train here.

                                Picking up items is an instant action, yes. Feels kind of costly to me, but there are several ways to get around it (merit or probably an improvised spell).

                                I realized in re-reading boons/hexes that it references Potency of the spell versus Potency of the boon/hex. Anything that references spell potency is based on the full potency, which can be allocated to different bonuses such that the total potency of the boon equals the spell's potency. I posted in the general Mage forum here asking for clarification, but I believe that means you should get a +1 bonus to Brawl on 2 rolls during the spell's duration. This is because Ragnar cast a Potency 2 spell and granted two types of boons: Skill bonus (Potency 1 boon) and condition (Potency 1 boon).

                                Lynx, because you waited on characters to listen to you but neither followed your advice, it looks like you lose your instant action for this round. Unless someone remembers whether he can just delay to a certain point in initiative and go again now?
