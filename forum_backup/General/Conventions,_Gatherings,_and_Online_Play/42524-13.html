                    OK, here's what I'm thinking right now. This is still very much a work in progress at this stage, so I apologize if it has something of a stream of consciousness feel to it. Secrets Caste Sidereal, probably Gold Faction but maybe independent (still up in the air on that one). Assigned to the Convention of Wood (obviously) and the Convention on the Wyld (a Subconvention of the Convention of War, if memory serves). She's from the Confederation of Rivers, probably Nexus (though I'm still up in the air on that one), but she wasn't born in the city. She was born in a small river village at the eastern edge of the Confederation. Principle agricultural product Fish Sticks. Or maybe it was a disputed area within the Hundred Kingdoms. Dither way, small fishing village, with no particular government clearly protecting it. When he was... let's say 6 or 7-ish... the village was raided by slavers. Everyone who wasn't killed was captured by the slavers. His mom was killed defending the village, while his dad and older sibling(s) were captured, along with my character. The slaver's brought them to a freehold they had a deal with to sell them to the Fae. I can make this up, but it might be best if the ST chose a freehold or other group of Fae that have some significance to the plot. Anyway, yadda yadda yadda, she was forced to watch while Dad, older siblings and other villagers had their brains eaten. She managed to escape (or possibly was allowed to escape- whatever the ST would prefer), and made her way eventually to Nexus (or whatever major city we decide to go with). Maybe she made off with a trinket or something from the Fae if we can come up with something interesting. Either way, now we have little kid living with no family on the streets of a major city. He did what he had to, stealing, begging, selling his body, killing other streetdwellers, whatever was necessary to survive. She got pretty good at the stealing, and one day in her early teens she came across some valuable information in a mansion she was robbing. She sold it for way more than the take from the valuables she stole, and decided she was in the wrong business. She stopped stealing money and jewelry (mostly) and started stealing secrets. Over the years, he (or rather the alias he assumed, a suitably mysterious and dramatic name that I haven't come up with yet) became one of the most powerful and respected information brokers in the city. He never lost his hatred of the Fae, however, or an even deeper hatred of slavers who dealt with them (note that he isn't totally opposed to slavery, just to the selling of slaves to the Fae), and through careful manipulation of who came into what information did great harm to slavers who dealt with them without betraying himself. Direct harmto the Fae was, of course, beyond him. Anyway, time went on, and eventually he Exalted (not sure of the circumstances yet). The Sidereals picked him up and brought him to Yu-Shan, as per usual. As a Chosen of Secrets, the Bronze Faction had her marked off for recruitment, but during the course of her studies, particularly of history, she saw how effective the Solars had been against the Fae and was instead drawn to the Gold Faction. Or she resented the control either faction would have tried to exert over her and chose to avoid both. Still up in the air on that one. Either way, she completed her training, and was assigned to the Convention of Wood, posted primarily in the River Province, and frequently sent on missions involving the criminal underground and street culture, with which she was well associated. Sometimes he was tapped for espionage missions unrelated to these assignments because of his skill. Her favored technique is finding ways to blackmail her targets into doing what destiny needs them to do. In any case, he also requested and was granted assignment to the Special Convention on the Wyld, and has proven himself on several missions, earning the favor of the director of the Convention (who might be his mentor/sifu, now that I think on it). Her most recent assignment is to investigate the Fae invasion force(s) facing Novalesk, provide assistance repelling it, including providing information to the nation's exalted defenders at her discretion, and to determine if a stronger divine response to the situation may be warranted. She suspects that the assignment of a sidereal from her convention to such a broad mission where solar exalted are involved was politically motivated, resulting from the manipulation of the gold faction (either to get one of their own assigned if she's a member, or to prevent a bronze agent from being assigned if she's independent) and the local deity, Origenes, who obviously has a vested interest in gaining help to protect his purview. How does that sound?
