                                        You follow Solon and Alopekis to the bakery the dog-god had mentioned. It is a small but prosperous establishment, run by a young woman in her late twenties or early thirties. She flirts shamelessly with Solon, who is rather flustered by the ordeal. Nevertheless, he purchased a large basket of freshly baked fluffy biscuits.

                                        Solon takes the basket, and follows Alopekis to a secluded, mildly wooded park. It s surrounded on all sides by large buildings; there is no way that you would have found it without having been led there. Solon, still tired from his fight with Jensven, quickly collapses onto a large, flat stone that his been carved into a rough approximation of a bench.

                                        Solon feeds Alopekis and Shadow the biscuits, both of whom wolf them down with relish.

                                        You now have an opportunity to talk to Alopekis and Solon. Otherwise, we can move on so Jensven and Yi can go on thier Malfean Safari.

                                            (Wyld)

                                            At mention of Aurem, the workers all cease their labors and singing. A shrill buzz echoes from the throat of the Worker Amelia addressed. Several others join in in an ascending harmony that fills you with apprehension. Those with hammers begin pounding with a heavy staccato beat. The remaining workers start a descending baritone glissando as the figure on the ruby throne descends.

                                            As it approaches, you see the figure is of the same build, proportions and cast as the workers, but stands over eight feet tall. He is draped in a purple silk toga, and is seemingly made of gold. A too-large toothy grin sits upon his face.

                                            "I am Aurem, knight of Yttrus," he says, "what wishes to thou of me?"
