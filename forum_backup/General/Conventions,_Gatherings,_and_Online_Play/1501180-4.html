                To ST-answer your questions on the Simple Questions thread -

                1. Prime would be fine for detection, but all it does is show you murky outlines of things in twilight. No way to tell which frequency, and not much way of telling what they are beyond guessing based on the vague silhouettes. Easy if you're distinguishing a rat-spirit from a ghost, difficult if trying to distinguish between a murder victim and a murder spirit.

                2. Ephemeral Enchantment is about your limit for Banes in Prime - you can designate an object and a being to be Bane and vulnerable party, but it requires targeting both subjects and a good bit of Reach.

                Tesslin's Holllow is named after the old owner, and is refering to the hollowed-out bowl of rock that eventually became the quarry lake. The Hollow forming there was natural, like the majority of Hallows in Little Atlantis. Cloudchaser can give a little history on it, Prospect Yard, or Hyleen Cottage. Njord's Draught's history is pretty simple - "saloon that's been around since the town was founded, changing hands every few decades and being rebuilt each time".
