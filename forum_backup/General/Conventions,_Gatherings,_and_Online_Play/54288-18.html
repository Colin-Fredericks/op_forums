                                "Honored Strategos," Yi nodded his head, "As the honored scion of House Ledaal said, I have this very day received the blessing and backing of Origenes to act with all energy and diligence for the preservation and its future prosperity. I have also made arrangements for the Academy to begin full scale production of enchantments to supplement your armed forces which will be covered by the funds of the temple. They have requested that your office dispatch notice of your intention to receive these supplies.

                                I wish to make something clear to you. I arrived in the city this morning a vagrant priest. This evening I have spoken with the city god in his sanctum, convinced the Chancellor to risk his political neutrality, and have entered the halls of power while still an unknown and a foreigner. I mean no boasting in this, for there is none of it worthy of celebration. Rather let it be demonstration that I accomplish what I say and set out to do. Tomorrow I will convince Gens Ptreas and Spyrus to place their household troops under your command.

                                What I require of you is to raise new levies to supplement these troops and to equip these forces with the Academy's thaumaturgical war material. However, discussions this day with gods and men have enlightened me to the tremendous inability of those in power to do what they wish or believe best. If there is someone else I must speak to to persuade you to authorize these measures I will see to that as well. Moreover, if there is any glaring flaw in this course of action I would be delighted to hear of it so that I may see to rectifying it."

                                    04-12-2014, 12:39 AM
                                    "Your dynastic friend has already brought me the support of Gens Ptreas," Eugene says, "Though the support of the Temple and the Academy help greatly." Turning to one of his adjuncts, he says, "Inform the Academy that we will will take whatever order the Lookshan priest made."

                                    As the adjunct departs, the Strategos continues, "In regards to your plan. One, the system is not as broken as you've likely been led to believe. Undoubtedly the Chancellor and Origenes have regaled you with how terribly the current system locks them out of politics. In theory, they have little direct power over the Boule or the Gentes, yes. But centuries of precedent give them unfettered access to the highest rungs of government. It's just that there's not much of a government to have access to at the moment.

                                    "But, more importantly, ask yourself this question: why have neither faction in the current succession crisis resorted to violence? Because of me and my troops. The two factions' forces are matched evenly enough to make the result of any battle unpredictable. And they know that any force multipliers they have would pale in comparison to Capital forces and the Unaligned Gentes' mobilizing in support of the attacked.

                                    "So, as much as I'd love to lead a legion against the Fae, your plan would cause the pot to boil over, so to speak. Both sides would see a golden opportunity to take the other out and quickly install their chosen regime."
