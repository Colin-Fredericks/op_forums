        Evening- Friday November 4th

        Earth

        The Named surprises everyone by being astonishingly eloquent. She speaks, and the words pour out of her. It's a kind of fluid poetry, sharing her experiences, sharing the lore that she's accumulated. Earth doesn't even need Dead Girl's journals for this, it's all her. She can hear the Ramble whispering and muttering and interjecting in her stories. A few people pipe up about the Unfleshed, how there's more of them in Korea and Japan than anywhere else, or talk about a friend of a friend who went to Phoenix and didn't come out. They talk about a city out in California where mages have gotten ideas about the Divine Fire, and where Pandorans haunt a subway station that is absolutely coated in Pilgrim Marks and written Rambles of all description. A couple curse loudly when it comes to the Calavera, especially fellow Golems. It's a cautionary tale passed around the Lineage about what goes wrong when you put a body down in the ground and expect a person to come climbing back out. There's better ways to do it, if you just have the words.

        A blue skinned Nepri in worn canvas robes and a thick trench coat shakes their head. Their chest is bound tight, like a mummy, and you aren't sure you want to know why. They shake their head and stroke a long, curly square beard and laugh. "What is it about Golems and words?" You can feel the Galataeids behind you staring into your back and you don't know what they are thinking. They thinking the Freak dragged a fellow Muse down with her? Thinking that a fellow Pilgrim is dead because of her? Someone speaks up.

        "Haven't heard anything about anyone calling themselves Dead Girl, but it's hard to keep track of the stories, sometimes. You said she likes wings, messing with people's heads and making Firestorms? Would be easy enough to get her confused with the nasty sort of Messenger...or get a Messenger confused with her, but we'll keep an ear to the ground sister. You keep walking your trail until you figure out where it ends." There are murmurs of agreement throughout the Ramble.

        Somebody else steps forwards, a Wretched with loose stitches and peeling skin, to start talking. "Well you know what they say about the Messengers, lightning never strikes the same place twice. 'Course that's straight up bullshit, or else we wouldn't all be here!" There's a lopsided smile with a drooping cheek and a ripple of laughter. "I've heard of a place where the angels are thick on the ground, where even humans go to get a look at 'em. Met this Pilgrim on my way through Oregon and they said-" And the story goes on.

        Earth isn't paying too much attention, because the Riven with mist in their wake, ribbons on their arms, and keys on their wrists is heading towards her. They don't seem wholly...real. Like they're stepping on different ground as they head towards her and seat themselves on the ground. They extend a hand, worn and cracked with work, ink and blood and dirt caught under fingernails, and offer a small, crooked smile. "They call me Satin. How long you been in town, Earth-Who-Lives-Again?"

                    Evening- Friday November 4th

                    Scout

                    The Nepri wandered through the crowd, picking up the latest rumours of the strange and the macabre. Some of the local Pilgrims told stories about the tunnels under the Exchange district- not the walkways, those everyone knows about, no, the old tunnels, the ways people moved stock, mail, and bodies from building to building in the days before air conditioning and the internet when they had absolutely no desire to brave the horrendous weather outside. They're mostly abandoned, these days. Kept under lock and key, abandoned, forgotten, but what's any of that to the Night People? They say vampires rest in those tunnels the way they use catacombs, like they use the Prohibition smuggling tunnels in Moosejaw. They also say there are Pilgrim Marks and Athanors... if you want to brave the licks and the Unborn.

                    They're talking about the old cathedral, the one across the river. It burned down twice in less than a century, and Winnipeg has always had a thing for alchemy. There are rumours that it did (might still) have Athanors inscribed into the walls, alchemical secrets in the masonry. There are rumours that at least one of the fires was a Firestorm. Whatever really happened, the city isn't likely to forget it any time soon.

                    ​Everyone knows that the Red River and the Assiniboine are the lifeblood of the city. Their meeting at the Forks is the reason the city was a centre of trade amongst the First Nations people for hundreds of years. What most people don't know is that the Rivers have serious weight. Some say that Underworld Rivers follow the real ones, and that if you swim deep enough in the Red River you'll start swimming towards the surface of the Underworld. They also say it isn't the only Invisible Realm to have a River in the same place.

                    OOC: Scout gets Story Hooks equal to successes. These are things to pursue if you want to, or not. It's urban legends and tall tales, with various degrees of truth.

                    Mark 5

                    He speaks well enough, for a Titan. The stories about qashmallim and Firestorms were getting old, anyways. No telling what either of those things was liable to do, so why waste time trying to figure it out? Besides, Firestorms were more like natural consequences of alchemical buildup, and the Created were careful not to create Wastelands in Winnipeg. The weather was volatile enough without adding the Divine Fire to the mix. Someone over in the group of Golems called out "Mudbreaker? She's long gone son- went north, chasing the Aurora. Everyone goes chasing the Aurora once or twice in their Pilgrimage, but she's wild for it. Getting too dangerous up north these days, though. Lots of skin-changers gathering with some kind of mad moon spirit." There were nods and murmurs of agreement. A couple of the Created here had met a death under the teeth and talons of werewolves.

                    The blue-skinned Osirian in the trenchcoat points at Mark 5 and shakes his head "Don't you go messing with alchemists kid, they'll suck your humours dry to get what they want. Pyros works miracles for them same as us. They don't know we're here, and if they do, they're leaving us alone, which is just fine with me. Lead with your head, not with your fists. Start asking yourself why they're doing what they're doing, instead of focusing on what they're doing."

                                            "This is a proof- even Centimani are not always our enemy, for they can be Redeemed. Protect yourself, but know- the centimani, those fallen to Flux, they are our brothers and sisters, as much as you are mine. They may walk the path again one day, as Earth Who Lives Again does. They need only guidance to find the way. Guidance, like Earth has had"
                                            Why did Scout wave at him, Mark 5 wondered. He didn't guide Earth - the Principle did. He was just lending a hand. Then, something hit him: If Earth could be redeemed, all Centimani potentially could... which was his hope, in a way. So far, so good. But how could he judge them, then? How could he end them, if they carried the potential to change? Was he truly Good if he destroyed Evil which could be made Good? Which could become good by itself?
                                            ... was this something about Intention?
                                            Mark 5's head hurt. This was so confusing.

                                            "Amalgama, they are not our brothers and sisters. But they are humans- those who we would have for brothers and sisters after our New Dawn. I myself am afraid of Alchemists. To have my vitriol taken and be dissected like a government project. But we must not let fear rule us- we should not hurt these people. Only their company, and the true Alchemists within. And with that, you have my full support."
                                            Mark 5 felt like he missed something important here, again. Was it intention, again? Why not fight back against injustice?
                                            Thankfully, the Scout was less averse to his mindset than Regret: he said there is an acceptable target. Why is one part acceptable, the other isn't, though?
                                            His head starting hurting again.
                                            In either case, he was relieved: not only did someone offer his help, this someone apparently understood something about this confusing topic of Intention. Maybe he could learn something himself. He nodded at Scout as a sign of thanks.
