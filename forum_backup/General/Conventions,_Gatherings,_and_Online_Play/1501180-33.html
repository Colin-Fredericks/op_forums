    Absolutely agree about the light pollution thing. IRL it makes me sad when I can't see stars at night, and IC I think Philly felt the same thing returning to Little A from her journey in the mountains. Not sure exactly how far out from Old Town the Heights is, but even a little bit of light pollution can often spoil a night sky. Would the cabal need space magic for that, or maybe forces could enhance the illumination of stars?

    Depending on the size of the property, I don't see why the cabal couldn't plant all the different kinds of trees Kaiser mentions. Also remember that according to Cauthon's description many of the other houses in the neighborhood are abandoned and falling down, so sleepers might not notice if the cabal made changes to other properties around Hyleen Cottage.

    Centering a whole arc of a chronicle on a cabal's sanctuary might be unusual, but I think it could be fun. Lots of mysteries to explore, and wisdom choices to make: whether to try and get Pandora's soul stone back and use it; whether to get rid of the summoning circle in the attic, and so on. Also there is a political aspect to it. If we start altering ley lines or making significant changes to the Shadow landscape we might come into conflict with the other cabals or even the Uratha pack. Do we know whether they are primarily Bone Shadows or Hunters in Darkness?

    The Astral angle is interesting. Mind is Phily's inferior Arcanum, so she might have misgivings about accessing her Astral Path. I feel like what I know about her already, she probably has some scary Goetia, and I think Proximi probably have really weird interactions with the Astral (think about Alia in the Dune books). BtP is the CoD that I know the least about, so maybe something with the Primordial Dream would be interesting, and it might give Philly insight into her path. And of course it is interesting to explore the similarities and differences between the Anima Mundi and the Shadow.

                                            I think the baseline Curse needs to be less restrictive. It must matter, but not shut down their ability to act outside their niche. The Merovingians, for example, lose 10-again on all physical rolls and each 1 cancels a success. The Sisters of the Mountain suffer from permanent lethal damage and must sleep in soil. The Priamid can never convey the predictions they have seen and this cannot be bypassed by magic. Maybe say they must always sleep at sea, making them stand in buckets of water, sleep in a bathtub or use any other methods to carry the sea with them. That gives them a logistical challenge and a targetable weakness, but its something the cabal can normally manage.

                                            Blessings wise, they have 3 Arcana to choose from, 2 from their parent Path. 30 Arcana dots to compose the possible spell list, which are then bought like merit dots. Transform Life is a solid choice, so is something like Speak with Animals. Its all a matter of how the spells build on their theme. If you were to express the theme in one phrase, with no pauses or commas, what would it be ?​
