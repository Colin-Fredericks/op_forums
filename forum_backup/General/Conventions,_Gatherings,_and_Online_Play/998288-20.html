                                                            Ok, that's the Tepets! Partially in parallel (waiting until she confirms the meeting with Tepet, but before actually occurring), it's time to start setting up a meeting with Cynis! To Cynis Belar (MoEP: DB pg. 32):
                                                            The Slave Coast rebellion is can turn to profit rather than disaster. Are you willing to attempt the register of account rather than the iron boot?

                                                            I have in my hand the keys to Hohenzon's ivory gates. Do not let the opium-daze of the North distract you from what is truly valuable.
                                                            - Mnemon Aisa

                                                            She's going to offer Cynis the same deal as Tepet agreed to - give up the Slave Coast, and Tepet will transfer control of Hohenzon to their forces. Aisa's fee and interest in mediating this will be one of the Hohenzon's wood lots, famed for their fast-growing Ash trees. One of her big points is that Tepet is desperate, making them dangerous - better to profit from a sure exchange (unequal in their favor!) than risk the irrationality of a desperate house.

                                                            Aisa's willing to give up the land, or accept a smaller fee of some other sort, but opening with it gives her more room to be negotiated down without having to chip in further resources or promises of her own.
