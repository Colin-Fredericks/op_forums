            Solon taps his bearded chin as he looks the figurine over, turning it slowly around to see it from all angles," This is quite good, young man. I have not seen the likes of which in a long, long time. Sad to say, though, that there are no goldsmiths in Flotsam. There's no real demand for dedicated goldwork, at least no on a regular basis. You'd have to go to Dhaam-Bel. for that sort of thing. Still, this is fine work, and...Hm..." he places the figurine down on the counter, and pulls out a rack with a finely crafted steel cuirass and scale skirt suit of armor with matching short blade and shield. The style is foreign and unusual to Scholar, but Solon beams as he pats it lovingly, "I don't have the coin for such a treasure, but tell you what? I'll give you this set here for the figurine. You'd look quite sharp in this, and you'd be sure to catch the eye of a fine young lady, eh? Well, what do you say, young man?"

                Essen coughs as she straightens up," The Will of Cobra is the philosophy of Outer Sanctuary, to continue the Endless War against the Realm and its catspaws until the right and proper government of Creation can be reestablished. Cobra was the Mother of that land, an outlaw who fled a terrestrial paradise on the central continent now lost to the ages, and sought her entire life to reclaim it for those who allied themselves with her. Though she passed on, stolen by Heaven, her Will continues, carved into the very stones of her land. That is why the people of Outer Sanctuary continue to fight even when she has been gone for almost a thousand summers. My teacher taught me that she had envisioned a means by which regardless of their power or their magic, that the people of Outer Sanctuary could halt the Realm's ambitions, and perhaps even turn the tables on them and become the dominant power in Creation, but he remained tight-lipped as to what that was. Why are you asking, though? Do you wish to become Sons of Cobra yourself?"

                Well? What how will you respond?

                                                        Scholar takes the sword, armor, and shield in turn and pulls them close so he can examine them in fine detail despite his weak eyes. Three times he squints and runs his sight along the shape of the item in his hand. He studies each in turn and learns very little as he doesn't know beyond a trifling amount what to look for. He doesn't have the experience in appraisal that the blacksmith has. This is true for all save the sword. He is an expert in bladed weapons, taught by his master who was not only a great warrior but a great blacksmith also, with fiery spirits of knowledge adding their capabilities to his massive reservoir of fighting knowledge. He understands how much the blade is worth. But then, the entire set is worth nothing to him as it is not what he needs now. He needs raw material to create personal items to sell and replenish his dwindling supplies.

                                                        "Kind sir these look like wonderful pieces, but they are worthless to me. I need raw materials. I need iron and coal and the trashy resins that are left when you smelt iron. I would trade this figurine for all of the raw materials I can get to be delivered to the ship I'm on. I can direct the delivery."
