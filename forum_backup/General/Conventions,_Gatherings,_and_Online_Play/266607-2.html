            Okay! It's a bit after Friday, but I haven't gone to sleep yet, so it's still the same day to me!

            Thanks to everyone who expressed interest! My cast of players will be:

            Crapcarp,
            Thrythlind,
            Dwyad,
            and A Twannabe!

            You don't have to have chosen a character yet - I figured we'd work out setting and playstyle together before we set out characters, and it might be useful to have the flexibility to shift concepts if needed.

            So! My four players: What sort of game are you looking for? Machiavellian politics? Brutal war? Deep enigmas and occult investigation? Are there any themes you'd like to cover?

            When left to my own devices I usually lean toward investigation and action, though I'm willing to go in another direction if that's what the group prefers. My standard tone for Lost is darkly comic, with an edge of desperation. As for setting, I'm thinking probably a medium-to-large freehold in Chicago (though if you guys have ideas for an evocative location you'd prefer, I'm glad to consider it!).

            Basically, I like to let the players have a hand in building the game's world, just a bit. We'll all be playing in the sandbox, after all! If you have a fun idea for a setpiece, location, NPC, or potential conflict down the line, let me know. Maybe I can work it in!

            Characters will be base creation + 20 XP. Dropping Clarity for XP is allowed at 5 XP per drop, but only up to two times. If you choose to do so, we'll roll for derangements.

            I *love* characters to have backstories - especially ones that are rich in setting details, character establishment, and plot seeds to mine. Submitting a background with your character will be worth a minimum of 5 XP, up to 10 XP for an exceptional one. Exceptional, in this case, involves quality, rather than quantity. If your one paragraph gives me more to play with and is better-written than another guy's novel, you'll get more XP from it. If you don't feel up to writing a narrative, I can accept timelines or bullet points, too, as long as they're easy to read and get the important bits across.

            If anyone needs help building or fleshing out their sheet, I'm happy to do so - and I'm also glad to look over a sheet post-creation for XP efficiency, if you like, so you can have all the Cool Stuff you can afford.

            Regarding house rules, we're using a 2 dot in-seeming/3 dot out-of-seeming merit for Dual Kith. I'm afraid that I'm not incorporating Talecrafting into the game, as the addiction rules can just be too disruptive in play. The first dot of Court Mantle for any of the Seasonal Courts is free, though the rest cost as usual. If there's any homebrewed material you want to use, let me look it over first. I might allow it, I might allow a modified version, or I might deny it, depending on balance and suitability.

            I don't require training time or anything like that, but I generally prefer that XP spends in game make some sense - if you've been spending the last six months straight in Autumn's occult archives trying to find the meaning of that strange symbol that's appearing everywhere, you'll have to come up with some justification on how you've been training to boost Strength. I'm usually more lenient on lower levels than on higher ones.

            A note for my ST style: I take a two-tiered path when it comes to ST Veto. The first time I say "no," to an idea or request, it means "not at first blush, but please lay out your arguments for why I should say yes." The second time I say "no," it means "Not now and probably not ever, but if that changes I'll let you know." I'd rather not hit a third time, if we can avoid it.

            I'm sure there's more than one thing I'm forgetting, so ask any questions you have! Also, let me know if you'd rather play on these forums or on a Google Doc. I'm *leaning* toward Google Doc as I don't have access to these forums at work, but I'd rather not force it if folks can't use that.

                    Everybody can contribute to the document, basically.

                    As for me, I like redemption and recovery stories. I tend to like to see my characters start as broken in someway with the game or story one of them getting progressively better and more accepting of themselves and/or whatever caused them to be broken in the first place (accepting or defiant, depending). I also prefer Dark is Not Evil, Holy Dark, Good is Not Nice and other such concepts. I have never played an evil character, generally don't feel comfortable with it, but I have played many characters whose morality is far more old world than modern world, which many people would think is the same thing as psychopathic or evil. I also like "Earn your happy ending", rather than "always a happy ending."
