            Right, so it looks like the UK is it.

            I am thinking the Midlands, covering an area roughly corresponding with northern mercia (derby, leistershire, Stafford, and Nottinghamshire mostly, perhaps pushing south as far as Birmingham and north as far as Sheffield). It's a pretty big area in some respects, but depending on the characters we will probably focus on a smaller section of it and it is necessary to have a large enough population to support a decent number of mages.

            So that you can start to think about where you fit in, I see a number of groups having interests in the region:

            Society of Ether:
            The industrial revolution was born in rural Derbyshire, and as Iteration x withdrew from the mills and factories the society of ether moved in to fill the vacuum - drawing inspiration from whole industries consigned to history inn a place that was once brimming with inventive energy. Their main British chantry resides at arkwrights mill in Derbyshire where it all started and they maintain several factories, mills, and mines across the area all converted to experiment with forgotten sciences.

            Mercurial Elite:
            Bletchley park to the south is somewhat of a mecca for the adepts, but unfortunately it is still in technocratic hands. As a result they are somewhat militant in the British isles, and many still raid the southern technocrat wherever possible before regrouping in the relative safety of the Midlands.

            Order of Hermes:
            Before the rise of the order of reason, the hermetic held great strength in Britain and they still maintain several changes and strongholds. The main seat of their power lies further north, but a handful of minor changes dot the region.

            Verbena:
            As the technocracies power in Britain dwindled, the verbena emerged from hiding and underwent something of a resurgence. They maintain a number of sacred sites and stone circles across the area.

            Celestial chorus:
            The nwo has subverted large parts of the faithful in England to effectively worship the state. Nevertheless, the chorus has regained power to the north, and they maintain a presence in the region.

            Sahajiya:
            lastly, and most importantly. A small chantry exists in Nottinghamshire, run by the remnants of a helfire club on the ancestral estates of Lord Byron himself. Depending on your opinion of the code of ananda you may not have much to do with them (at least not by choice) but you probably know of them by reputation if nothing else.
            Then there is you.

            if anyone has any ideas they definitely want to see (such as cool ideas for locations on either side of the gauntlet) or definately dont want to see then feel free to throw them into the pot.

            assuming everyone is happy with the location however, let's start to think about characters.

            For each character I need:

            high concept: if they were a film character, how would you describe them?
            how do they fit in the sleeping world? Native? Migrant? (From abroad or elsewhere in the uk) job?
            how do they fit in the awakened world? Probably overlapping with their high concept.
            Paradigm: probably the most interesting part of mage, so let's start thinking about it early.

            You can also start thinking about the mechanical aspects of your character if you wish. We are using the standard character creation rules from mage20 with a couple of exceptions:

            1) the only ecstatic chantry in the region is the helfire club mentioned (probably a 1 or 2 point chantry) so if you want to belong to a larger chantry it either needs to be that, or be representatives of a more distant chantry (possibly with a portal leading to the region for easy access). Otherwise, if you want a chantry you are going to have to build it yourselves.
            2) Nodes are a commodity that is hard to come by and harder to keep. If you have access to one you need to explain how you gained it, and how you are managing to keep hold of it.

            It is also worth starting to consider links between your characters. Everybody should know at least one other character.

                                My character's concept will be the prodigal son (at least in the contex of the chronicle, all in all he is becoming the wandering sage). He left the UK for a trip to india before starting college and never came back - until now. He spent his years wandering the wild places of the earth, directed only by his gut instinct and sense of curiosity. He learned from many teachers on his way and learned much about the world most city dwellers never will, though his wisdom is more founded in doing and being than in knowing. Now he has decided to return to his roots in the UK and finds his home a foreign world.

                                In the sleeping world he is struggling. He doesn't have a job or any money and the thought of confronting his family and erstwhile friend is somewhat daunting. The sheer press of humanity in cities like London feels allmost violently opressive after his time adrift in the wilderness. He's a UK national, but his passport got lost ages ago, so he doesn't have any ID for the moment.

                                In he awakened world... he fits everywhere and nowhere. In his travels he studied under many teachers, he studied meditation in a monestary in Khadmandu, trained martial arts with tibetan monks in the himalayas, danced to exhaustion in the deserts surrounded by Djin, drank the strange, hallucigenic juices of various unknown plants in the rainforest and lived with a witch in rural greece for a season. He doesn't know anyone locally, but he probably knows someone who knows someone they know around a few corners and has various teachers and connections to wizards worldwide with affiliations to various traditions and crafts.

                                Paradigm: The world is an illusion and only by moving pas thinking can we pierce the veil of deception and discover the true unity of being. This is magic. Unlike many urban Ecstatics he touches upon this tracendent state of mind not through excessive assault of the senses, but through discipline and moderation. Yoga, Tai-Chi and Wushu focus his body and mind, meditation helps him to loose sight of the self and careful deprivation of the senses sensitivizes him to the point where a single kiss or brush of silk against his skin can push his mind from meditative calm to a state more divine.

                                Mechanically speaking he will focus on the Time and Correspondence spheres, piercing the very foundation of the illusion surrounding him. In play I would like to explore learning the mind sphere, which will be quite a journey as I plan on giving him a budding demense wherein he comes into contact with alternative versions of himself that have lived their life differently in some way (he thinks these are time related visions.... and perhaps they are?). He also has the Dream background maxed out, which guided him on his journeys and was one of the reason his many mentors decided to tutor him. Speaking of mentors I also plan on taking high levels of the mentor background to signify a whole slew of mentors of different skill levels ranging of ancient and wise to a kitchen witch he dated for a few months. None of them are local, but they present potential inroads with pretty much any sorcerous society outside the TU and represent a vast reportoire of mystic knowledge.

                                Houlio if the CoE is absolutly not to your taste, perhaps look into one of the "adjacent" paradigms. Ahl-I-Batin, Euthanaoi, Akashayana, etc.
