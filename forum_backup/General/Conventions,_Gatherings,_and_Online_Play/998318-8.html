        Friday November 4th 2016 - Evening

        Earth

        Pyros surges through your veins, recharging in the chilled embrace of the soil. Things whisper to you through the ground, memories that aren't your own, fingers brushing along your skin. Nostalgic, desperate, frustrated, resentful. You aren't sure how much is dream, and how much reality. As usual, your dreamscape is... difficult to describe. It's from a place before language, a place that is all vibration and sensation and endless fields of flame. Your Azoth surges in sympathy, opening to the very source of the Flame Immortal. You get the idea that it is trying, desperately, to communicate with you, but it can't quite reach you.

        You spend most of your morning, and a good part of the afternoon, wandering from the community garden, to the Exchange, to the West End, and back downtown towards Adonis' apartment. Pilgrim Marks are such a varied form of communication, a mixture of simplistic ideograms and complex alchemical ciphers. They're a patchwork of language, English blended with Church Latin, street maps from above blended with transmutation equations. It's base lead and refined gold all in one.

        You pick your way through delayed conversations, Created sharing progress, breakthroughs, triumphs, and sorrows- words that might be taken for crude graffiti tags serving as key and identifier for the equations that comprised the conversation. However, every Pilgrim Mark you find makes reference to the same thing, a weekly Friday night meeting at the Oodena Celebration Circle.

        Adonis

        The Muse wanders the streets, almost aimlessly. He's still shaken from reading the Athanor the night before; what will Torment be like for him, when he finally falls into it? He doesn't really connect with anyone in his social circle, or through polite conversation on the street. He ducks into a coffee shop and looms over a mug and a pastry. Eventually, he passes the National Research Council of Canada and the Amalgama LTD office beside it, the gilt-edged black symbol gleaming in the sunlight on the side of a lilac metal building.

        Is Erwin in at the office today? Could he go in and just... see him? Humans do that, don't they? Visit boyfriends at work? He wonders what he might do to Erwin, if he came across him in Torment?

        The Galataeid is thrown off his game, wasting the rest of his morning, needing a bit of time to himself. He meets up with Earth and Mark 5 after lunch.

        OOC: Adonis gains a Beat, a point of Willpower, and three points of Pyros

Who knows what the local Created are like. Perhaps they are amassing to figure out what to do with Earth. Perhaps they are terrorizing the humans around them. People speak in hushed whispers, thinking that these scarred and somewhat fearsome wanderers might be some kind of new gang, what, with their distinctive looks and peculiar tags.

                                The Forks

                                Evening- Friday November 4th

                                You spend most of your afternoon wandering the Forks, walking along the pathways that border the mighty Red River. At a viewpoint gazing across to the other side, flanked by worn stone bison, you can see the weather-beaten edifice of the St. Boniface Cathedral, the only thing left standing of the original building after a catastrophic fire in 1968, the second such fire to destroy the place. In the summer, the river rises up to cover the walkways that are supposed to allow safe travel at the water's edge, submerging the works of men, indifferent to the passage of humanity. You saw a marker, under a permanent tent, outside the Market, that shows where the water rose in a massive flood in the 50s. The Red River brought people to Winnipeg, and it could rise up and take them away. It was a sleeping giant the people in the city were keenly aware of. All too often, the Red would give up bodies. Red ribbons with bleeding black ink are tied to bridge railings, torn at by the wind, commemorating the forgotten, the invisible.

                                As night begins to fall, humanity begins to clear out. You find the Oodena Celebration Circle... and you wait.

                                The constant clicking of the weathervane pays witness to the wind as the lamplight glows in the twilight. You sit on the grassy hillside, gazing down at an ancient horizon that was elevated over millennia by the silt that gave the city its name. Winnipeg. Muddy Waters. The giant metal armatures are empty, their tiny peepholes and summoning circles void of the stars that will, in time, fill them. As the wind picks up, the aeolian harp built into the Northeast armature begins to moan and sing. The light fades, the tuneless song increases in volume.

                                The sound speaks to something deep within both Adonis and Earth, a thing from before words, a Divine Breath.

                                Like a lighthouse calling ships home, the light of the winter fire pit and the tuneless harp call to the Created.

                                They come.

                                They come in tattered rags and patchwork finery, they come at their best, they come as they are, and are those things altogether different? There are more Created gathered here at once than you have ever even heard of. Almost two dozen come strolling down the shallow steps and shambling across the rolling hills. Sturdy folk with clay in their hair and tattoos on their skin feel the stones carved with an outline of a Mesopotamian lion, the herald of the Harvest, and of Tammuz. Gaunt people with missing features and complexions of grey, black, green, and blue gather by the carving of Aset while a group of almost jaundiced individuals, their skins a riot of colours and textures, stand by the outline of Orion, tension like a thunderhead hanging over them. The beautiful, fitting every possible description of the word, lounge on the grass, while strange, damaged people leaking shadow and smoke, their faces and bodies a whirlwind of symbols, sit beneath the lyre.

                                They seem to gather by Lineage out of habit, more than anything, as if they come to their shared symbols through Memory instead of conscious thought. The crowd slowly mixes, as the patchwork, premature people seek out those they recognize, avoid those they dislike, and ramble about the group. The fire pit down at the lowest point, in the centre of an Azothic compass, is conspicuously empty. There is no tradition, no law, for gatherings such as this. The Created must build it like they do everything else.

                                When someone is ready, they will go down and speak.

                                                        Oh- it looks like Adonis' speech went...really well. They're not even raising their pitchforks. She's visibly dumbstruck. When the Muse comes back, she mumbles something to him: "Hey, uh...I thought that wouldn't go so good. You really went out on a limb for me. I won't forget you did that."

                                                        The Riven with the ribbons and keys and wild hair is absolutely fascinating to her. Earth can't take her eyes off them, except when she occasionally gets self-conscious and stares at her feet. Many eyes are on her, and her tattoo itches: 'A MAN OF WORDS AND NOT OF DEEDS IS LIKE A GARDEN FULL OF WEEDS.' After yesterday, she can think of worse things to be than a garden full of weeds. She takes a breath and strides forward into the circle.

                                                        "Uh...hey. I'm Earth."

                                                        A flash of memory from her stolen body: people sitting on folding chairs at an AA meeting, repeating "hi, earth." The memory makes her laugh a little. She has a surprisingly nice laugh, like a hiccup. It breaks the ice. As she speaks, her Inscription glows bright on her bare bicep and sparks with Pyros.

                                                        "That's all true. I come from Phoenix, and my creator always told me to stay out of the city. The cops know what to look for, and they try to kill us if they see us. The one who made me- she's called Heartsblood, eight foot tall, hard as granite, devil with a hammer- she'd lurch around in the desert looking for fights, and even she stayed out of Phoenix. See, she was looking for a worthy challenge, or some Titan crap like that...

                                                        ...there's an Unfleshed called Waking Cloud who runs vision quests out in the desert for rich humans from the tech industry, Burning Man types. I think he's looking for someone with the expertise to help him program his progeny...

                                                        ...weird stuff out in Tucson. I guess the old mission there is haunted, and there's a secret cabal of magicians doing rituals with the bells. Heartsblood always said to watch my back around there, they're as dangerous as alchemists...

                                                        ...you need to watch out for this Pandoran, the Calavera. It looks like a fancy skull when it's dormant and shows up in traveling shows in the Southwest and Mexico- stupid humans- and it always wakes up on the Days of the Dead. I can tell you all about Pandorans..."

                                                        That kind of stuff. Traveler's tips for the Southwest, people she met down there, places to see or avoid. It feels good to be useful to the crowd, hoping that someone might derive some use out of her experiences. She doesn't talk much about being Centimani, only in passing. As the glow from her Inscription fades, she adds one more thing:

                                                        "I got a question. Earth-Dies-Screaming used to travel with Dead Girl. She's a Galateid, traveled all over the place, used to follow Gold and Bronze and have a throng on the East Coast...a lot of the time, she had these big beautiful wings. Knew a secret Flux way to call down Firestorms. Liked to get inside peoples' heads. Twist them.

                                                        Do any of you know that name?"

                                                        Making a good impression, practicing her storytelling, and seeking information about Dead Girl. Especially want to impress the Ulgan with the ribbons and keys. Presence 3 + Expression 1, Willpower 3, spending 1 Pyros to add +3 Expression from Inscription. 10 dice: 6 successes!

                                                            Centimani...Centimani, Pandoran, Flux, Pyros, Azoth, Qashmal, Elpis, Vitriol, Alchemy, the Great Work, words, words, words that Regret has heard over and over again but had very little context for. Prime drilled them into his head when he was created. Frank explained them in a way he could better understand as they traveled to Winnipeg, but like any word describing what they represent is very different from knowing what they represent. And while Regret certainly didn't know what those words truly represented, some part of him DID...and that was both a comforting and terrifying feeling.

                                                            Amongst the other patchwork people like himself, Prime & Frank called them the Frankenstein Lineage, he listened to stories of comings and goings. Of lessons learned and lost. Of the Great Work. Of the world. Of Prometheans and Humetheans...no wait, that was wrong...of human-theans...of the ones who have homes. Yes. That was it. People made of those who could never go home, who had no home of their own trying to become people who have homes. People like himself, trying to become people like the ones who make up his body. "Atta boy Regy, language is a filthy whore anyway", he remembered Frank saying as he helped him practice speaking. His hands, mouth, and tongue was the artist who died in battle, and while they often remembered how best to say any given thing Regret himself was still trying to master their intricacies.

                                                            It was nice to be around people like himself and Prime, who didn't act like Prime. It was also nice to listen to their stories too. It was also nice to listen to people who were only sort of like himself and Prime, and for now it was nice to be around them too. He wondered what he would do after the gathering was over though. He came to Winnipeg to learn more about what being a Promethean meant, and while listening to the others did help him greatly he knew this many of his kind couldn't stay in one place for longer than an evening at best. He knew this partly because Frank had warned him about this, and partly because The Fire knew this. He needed to find another Frank, someone who he could learn from but wasn't like Prime. He needed someone who could see their own mistakes. Someone who knew sorrow. Someone who knew beyond themselves what it was like to loose something.

                                                            Centimani...former-Centimani...someone who was once the Enemy, but is no longer the Enemy. That's a start.

In truth, it's a mixture of determination and hope.
