    "Just call Valentine. I'm running on empty."

    "Alright." Ragnar fishes his phone and starts scrolling for Valentine's number. ​So if Moros can travel to the... Underworld?... Then what about me?

    "If I'd known Ragnar would be in danger- well, I did know, but I thought I would be able to help, but I'm not nearly as puissant with Space and Sympathy as I deluded myself into believing- oh, this 'lesson' was a disaster."

    "Not necessarily," muses Ragnar. "I managed to successfully cast a Space spell. First time I've ever done that. And I learned a couple of important things about fighting with magic: I can cast as long as my Mage Sight is intact, or if I can hear. And even if I can't injure somebody with magic yet, I can still make life difficult for them." Life-and-death combat might not have been the ideal place to learn that, but hey. Learning on the fly.

    "Did either of you know who that magician was, or what he wanted? I've seen him bedeviling my master, Hakim, in the last few weeks- coming to his home, even."

    Ragnar's finger hovers over his phone, poised to call Valentine. Well, well. Isn't that interesting? Oh-so-perfect Hakim hanging out with a Mage hooligan. Let's just file that one away for later... Moonstone might think that this lowlife was "bedeviling" Hakim, but for weeks? Without getting kicked out or reduced to a smoldering pile of ashes? That sounded less like "annoyance" and more like "accomplice." But why?

    All of this crosses Ragnar's mind in a split second, but he calls Valentine like nothing's wrong. "Valentine, it's Ragnar. We have a bit of a situation on our hands. I got attacked by a mage. I didn't recognize him from the Consilium. Moonstone and Phoenix helped me fend him off, but he disappeared through some sort of portal with a Death spell. He looks like one of the hipster types hanging around UChi. Seems like a Moros based on the spells he was casting. I tagged him with a couple Fate spells, they're still active on him. If that would be any help in tracking him. Can you find us on your own, or do you need an address?"

        Ouroboros Ophis
        Dante nods along eagerly as you discuss your findings. "Well done, Ophis!" he replies with a warm smile. "You are closer to the mark on several of those hypotheses than you know. I won't spoil the lesson by filling in all the gaps -- that would defeat the process of learning through effort."

        Dante further explains, "In laypeople's terms, yes, they are vampires. And they are incredibly powerful, dangerous, and knowledgeable. They call themselves Kindred, at least among more of their civilized and social kind. Because they are no longer living, but not technically dead, they can be studied and interacted with the Death arcanum in very unique ways.

        "And I would like to challenge you to study more of them,"         Dante says with a smile. "Learn from them. Consider how you can protect yourself from them. But study their culture and their behavior, too, when the opportunity arises," he recommends. "Mrs. Vial is indeed a very knowledgeable informant. Powerful and dangerous, yes, but also knowledgeable."

        Dante nods at your question. "Yes! The gates to the Underworld. They form naturally around or in cemeteries, and in some other less common locales. Ghosts use them to pass Beyond. A Moros Mage is truly dangerous near the presence of ghosts, but especially near one of these portals, as you can summon a specter much more alert and powerful than the kinds you have seen. Be very careful not to enter one unless you know exactly what you are doing," he cautions you. "The dead do not take likely to the presence of the living."

        For a time, Dante turns philosophical and lectures you on his theories about the Underworld and the nature of death versus undeath. You have some difficulty following all of his points, but you find that you are thirsty for more supernatural knowledge.

        Through your conversation with Dante and efforts to study the Kindred, take an Arcane Beat and permanently add your bonus dot to the Occult skill that comes from joining an Order.

        Ragnar, Moonstone, and Phoenix
        Within moments of dialing Valentine's number, you feel a swirl of energy hum around you on your Periphery, and the Mage walks around the corner and stares at each of you in turn. "Tell me everything," he says with a quiet intensity. He seems concerned.

        As you briefly discuss the attack, Valentine surveys the scene. He frowns when you mention that the man was not seen at the Consilium meeting, and nods when you suggest he is a Moros Mage. "A very good guess, considering everything you've seen today. He probably entered Twilight," Valentiner explains. "It's a state of being, rather than a location per se, usually experienced by ghosts and other ephemeral beings when they are in our world.".

        Valentine briefly studies each of you with an expression that suggests he is looking around you rather than at you, likely with his Mage Sight. "You said you were able to cast some spells on him?" he asks Ragnar. He smiles and continues, "Good. We'll be able to use that to track him. Let me see..."

        Each of you feels a faint tremble in your skin, in the air around you, as Valentine casts another spell. But as soon as he does, the smile slips off his face and his expression darkens. He quickly pulls you all close, close enough for physical contact, and you faintly hear him swear under his breath. A wave of energy surges through all of you like an electric current, you feel a brief flash of nausea and a lurching sensation, and then...

        ...You blink, and you're standing a few blocks away from the church from this morning. You're in an alley between two buildings. Valentine quickly casts another spell, rapidly flicking his fingers and spinning his wrists in an odd fashion as he does so. He takes a deep breath and relaxes visibly, then looks at each of you.

        "The sympathetic threads connecting you to him were gone. Frayed. Intentionally cut. That requires Adept mastery of Space. The chances of a Moros Mage having that kind of training are very slim, or he would have had no difficulty capturing or killing you all in moments." He lets that sink in before he continues, "That means he is not working alone. I brought us somewhere else quickly and cut all of your ties to that location, which will make it harder for them to find you."

        Valentine crosses his arms and sighs. "I don't know how or why he came across your path, but I don't like it. Keep an eye out for him." To Moonstone, he nods and says, "I'll see you tomorrow."

        You each gain an Arcane Beat for your supernatural encounter with another Mage and learning about Twilight.


        Binder and Moonstone
        Later that night, as agreed upon, Moonstone makes his way to Binder's bookstore in La Grange. He finds the front door unlocked as Binder lets him in.

        It's not long before you both hear footsteps and see a lean, scruffy-looking Caucasian man in his mid-30s saunter up to the store. He's wearing a black hoodie with a symbol of a white spade in its center, and a baseball hat cocked at an angle. He takes a long drag from a cigarette and tries to flick it to the ground, but it gets caught on his sleeve in the process. He yelps in surprise and quickly pats his sweatshirt to get it to the ground, muttering "OwowowOW!" in the process.



        Spade stumbles through the front door and turns it into a sort of half-flourish, half-bow. "Tada!" he says. He looks between the two of you and then points at Moonstone. "You. It's gotta be you, right? Moonstone? The guy who wants to summon a nightmarish plaything and make a pet out of it, yeah? I kid, I kid. I'm Spade," he points a thumb towards the symbol on his hoodie. "Case it wasn't obvious. So. This your place?" He looks around curiously. "Could use a little more privacy."


        Phoenix
        Let me know what you would do on Saturday, if anything, before the following begins.


        On Sunday as instructed by Boomer, you read your slip of paper with the address she gave you and plug it into your phone to follow directions to the meeting site. When you arrive, you find yourself at a run-down gym in such seemingly poor condition that you're not initially sure you're in the right place. Half of the lights in its neon sign are out, but it reads "Striker Fitness." No vehicles are in the small, unpaved parking lot.

        You walk up to the front door and see that it's not locked. The door leads you down a narrow hallway to an old stairwell heading underground. There's an unlocked padlock on a chain around a set of double doors, which are cracked partially open. You hear echoes from inside.

        When you open the door, you see a very different setup then what you had envisioned. A large boxing ring stands in pristine condition at the center of the room. Every wall has training dummies, bullseye targets, or racks with all types of melee weapons, from katanas and knives to staffs and spears. On the far wall, there is a long firing range with a shelf filled with pistols, shotguns, and rifles.

        Boomer stands in the center of the boxing ring and turns to face you with a boyish grin. She's wearing gym shorts and a tank top, and her bright pink mohawk is pulled back into a makeshift ponytail. "Glad you could make it," she greets you. She motions for you to join her in the ring. "If you don't have any exercise gear with you, there's a locker room around the corner with some spare gear. Get comfortable and then get up here."

        Once you're ready, Boomer explains, "Today, I'm going to teach you something that will help you defend yourself and your cabal. It's a trick of magic that can help you cast a spell more efficiently and potently. It's called a rote. Usually, each Order will pass some along to new initiates. Although you're not officially part of the Arrows yet, I'd like to think that you will consider petitioning. And as your mentor, I can choose information to pass along, and I think learning this will help you, regardless of whether or not you try to join our ranks."

        Boomer starts the lesson by engaging you in a warm-up spar. For being slightly shorter and leaner than you, she is surprisingly agile, but she also packs a pretty good punch.

        Roll Strength + Brawl for using the Down and Dirty combat rules.


        After warming up, Boomer spends the better part of an hour teaching you a series of fast, complex hand and wrist motions. She explains that the motions are used to encode the Imago for the spell, like a physical mnemonic device, to allow you to access the spell effect rapidly and effectively. As you perfect the technique, you feel the motions becoming more than just a series of movements, and as she explained, you start to find that the particular spell she's teaching you comes quickly and efficiently into existence.

        After practicing, she nods and walks you over to a practice dummy. "This spell will let you shape the kinetic energy around your attacks or weapons, even firearms, to make them strike faster and harder. With a single well-aimed strike, you could do as much damage as a bullet," she explains. "Try to cast the rote like I showed you, and then punch this fucker with all you've got."

        You gain access to your Kinetic Blow rote. Cast your spell, and then make an unarmed attack roll.

        Let me know what you'd like to do for the rest of Saturday.

He's pleased to meet up with you today and get your help on some transcription for some research projects he's working on. The professor has been reviewing some old items recovered from various sources, some from archaeological excavations and some from auctions or donations, and he can work faster with your support.

        The items he's working on today include some very old documents, some kind of papyrus document that was donated by a patron to the university who supposedly obtained it from the archives at someone's estate sale. It's the kind of chain of transfer that makes it hard for him to verify authenticity of the item, so he's speaking out loud while you quickly type away his words. "I've tried dictation software and it never seems to work nearly as well as you," he says with a smile.

        After reviewing the papyrus document, he moves on to some old books with symbols neither of you recognize. But while you're working, your eye catches the symbols on one of the books; although you've never seen it before, you have an immediate gut reaction to seeing it, like a feeling of deja vu. Somehow you instinctively know that they are written in some runic form of High Speech.

        Let me know what, if anything, you would do here or if you would study any of the objects further.

What I don't understand is what he was even trying to do. He could have incapacitated you quickly if he had opted for a surprise attack..."

        After dinner, Mathias surprises you by leading you down a hallway in the back of the restaurant to a private room. "The owner of this establishment is one of our Order contacts," he explains. "We can have some privacy here. I want to teach you something that will help you prepare your spells in a different way, to create traps or contingency plans that can harm your foes or help you and your allies at just the right moment."

He explains the arcane theory behind its purpose and allows you to practice. He expects and requires perfection, but he also rewards you with praise as you successfully cast the spell using his technique. He also explains how you can set spells using an Imago in such a way that they end after a condition is met.

        "I think you've got the gist of it now," Mathias says with a smile. "Go ahead, try something. Cast a spell that will trigger when you clap your hands."

            Ragnar jerks in surprise as Valentine pops in right in front of him. He stammers a little at first, but eventually gets control of himself and recounts what happened. He smiles when Valentine confirms that his spells could link him to the mage, but his stomach turns a little when Valentine's mood turns tense. Something's wrong. He gets a brief whiff of a cologne that smells ridiculously expensive as Valentine grabs him.

            "The sympathetic threads connecting you to him were gone. Frayed. Intentionally cut. That requires Adept mastery of Space. The chances of a Moros Mage having that kind of training are very slim, or he would have had no difficulty capturing or killing you all in moments. That means he is not working alone. I brought us somewhere else quickly and cut all of your ties to that location, which will make it harder for them to find you."

            With that, Ragnar dismisses his spells out of fear.
            For the rest of the day, Ragnar returns to his apartment. He tries to keep the bruises and the scrapes on his leg out of Patty's view. He gratefully falls asleep, his mind churning with thoughts about the attack, and how Hakim and Moonstone factor in to it...
            Sunday morning, he meets with Dr. Langley. The older man's compliment makes Ragnar smile with a pride. He cracks his fingers and his fingers hover over the keyboard. Then a thought hits him. Wait... why not give myself every advantage I can get?

            He quickly begins weaving an Imago, invoking his Praxis.

            Ragnar will attempt to cast his Exceptional Luck Praxis on himself. He has one free Reach, which he will spend on Instant Casting. Gnosis 1 + Fate 2 + Willpower 3 = 6 dice-> 2 successes. He'll take a Boon that grants him the 8-again quality on his roll to transcribe.


            Ragnar will attempt to transcribe Dr. Langley's musings. 4 Intelligence + Academics 3 = 7 dice -> 4 successes.


            His fingers fly across the board, without a single mistake. Magic is a guy's best friend...

            The manuscript, though... he doesn't understand what the words say, but he knows it's High Speech. Let's try and copy this down... He briefly examines the manuscript with Fate and Time Sight, and then takes pictures of the manuscript. "Let me see if I can dig anything up on my own."
            At dinner with Mathias, Ragnar continues to ponder the riddle of the mystery Moros.

            "I'm sorry I couldn't get there quickly to help, but thankfully Valentine was able to assist you and your cabal. What I don't understand is what he was even trying to do. He could have incapacitated you quickly if he had opted for a surprise attack..."

            With that, the pieces click into place. An image pops into Ragnar's mind of himself standing on a chessboard, and a hand reaching out of the shadows to place the Moros before him. They fight until Phoenix and Moonstone join him, and then the hand reaches out to pull the Moros back into the shadows. He looks up from his meal with a look of comprehension.

            "That's because that wasn't his goal. Whoever is pulling the strings... that Moros, whoever he is, he didn't show up with the intention to kill me outright. It's like a shark when it bites a surfer- it's not necessarily hungry, it wants to see if this weird thing is worth eating. Or pack hunters trying to see if an animal is strong enough to defend itself. When Phoenix and Moonstone showed up, he ran."

            After Mathias responds, Ragnar sits in silence for a few moments. Then he clears his throat. "So... the Silver Ladder. You mentioned the... Lex Magica, right? Law of Magic. What differentiates the théarchs from the Guardians, then? Is it like the difference between lawyers and police? The Arrows I get, I think: magic comes through competition, struggle. Not wrong per se, but... I don't think I'm cut out for it. The Free Council... do we get along with them? This use of technology to help with casting spells that they do, it sounds... unorthodox, maybe? Potentially useful, though."

            He fiddles with his fork as he considers how to phrase the next question. "Yesterday, there were... people who enjoyed what happened. I could see them smirking, laughing. Even those guys with Cielo. Poseidon, Atlas, Apollo. I'm not sure Moonstone made friends with the ladies though. Luna seemed neutral, but I could tell Cielo wasn't exactly pleased. Estrella looked pissed. Melody too. So what is Poseidon's deal? He's a Guardian, right?"

            He doesn't mention Moonstone's revelation about the mystery mage and Hakim; he figures he owes it to Moonstone to gather more information before blindly implicating him. What he does do, however, is show Mathias the pictures he took. "I was helping a professor at UChi with some research this morning. He had this manuscript that he couldn't decipher. I couldn't read it, but I knew it was something in High Speech. Can you tell what it says?"

            Practicing the strange hand gestures for the Rote takes time, but eventually he gets it down. "So you're saying that this will help me avoid Paradox? Sweet." Wait a second. Hand gestures to cast a spell? He looks to Mathias with a curious look, his hands continuing to practice the Rote. "Say... Gary Gygax wasn't a Mage by any chance, was he? Because this is some straight-up D&D stuff."

            "I think you've got the gist of it now. Go ahead, try something. Cast a spell that will trigger when you clap your hands."

            Ragnar stands in the middle of the room, considering his options. Finally, he hits on a simple but practical idea. With his mind, he begins weaving together the Imago of his Praxis. "Sorry in advance."

            Ragnar will attempt to cast his Exceptional Luck Praxis. The target will be Mathias, in an attempt to inflict Arm Wrack on both arms. He has one free Reach, which he spends on Instant Casting. Gnosis 1 + Fate 2 = 3 dice -> 1 success.


            The Imago formed and affecting Mathias, Ragnar immediately begins working the Rote, overlaying the Imago of the Time Spell over his Praxis.

            Ragnar will attempt to cast his Hung Spell Rote. He has four free Reach, one of which he will spend for Instant Casting, another one for Sensory Range. Gnosis 1 + Time 2 = 3 dice-> 1 success.


            He waits a few seconds to check that the spell is working, and then claps his hands, restoring Mathias' arms back to working condition.

            "Something like that, I'm guessing?"

But this tass I'm hearing about isn't stable. You can harvest it, but it's not sticking around, so no one I know is really interested in checking it out. If it's not coming from a hallow, and you can't keep the tass to store it for later, it doesn't have much value to most mages except as a quick recharge. But I know your group was looking for mana, so I thought of you."
