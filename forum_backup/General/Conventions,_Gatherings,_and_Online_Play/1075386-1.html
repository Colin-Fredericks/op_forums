                For the MythicGM thing, the more stuff you create to be input into it in terms of NPCs and possible plot threads the better it will function. It also requires the people using it to be willing to jump in and interpret its results. I can promise that eventually, it will hit everything you want it to because that's it's function, and with full player participation it will do it in ways that should satisfy the participants. A good ST is a person with a compelling vision, but on the other hand a group of people willing to brainstorm an idea is a viable development method for a reason. You're essentially crowd sourcing your story that -you- want instead of putting the whole burden on one person.
