                                                Originally posted by p. 119
                                                To reflect this, the number of Yantras she can apply to a given spellcasting pool is limited by her Gnosis. Matters primarily for Withstanding traits and counterspelling, and also for any spell that says it's better with higher potency.

                                                    "Potency is a measure of the spell’s power. It determines the extent of the effect of a spell. For example, attack spells use Potency for how much damage is applied."
                                                    Damage done, number of questions asked while Divining or using Supernal Vision, how many dots you add to your attributes when casting Honing the Form - these are all determined by Potency. Potency is also used to overcome a target's Withstand, if the target resists, and Withstand reduces potency on a 1-for-1 basis, so Potency 4 versus Withstand 3 means the spell takes effect at Potency 1.

                                                    As for Reach in primary factor, the tables on p. 326-327 shows what Reaches do to Spell Factors as a whole. For Duration, it changes the spell's duration from Transitory to Prolonged. For Casting Time, it changes from Ritual to Instant. For range, it changes from Touch to Sensory. For Potency, Reaching for Advanced spell factor gives the spell +2 to Withstand dispellation.

                                                    The values from Boons and Hexes are affected by Potency, and the benefits last for Duration, but as far as PSF goes, the Reach from exceptional success is, from how I understand it, applied only in regards of the tables on p. 326. If I am mistaken, feel free to correct me.

                                                    "After taking penalties as desired to advance spell factors that allow it, increase the spell’s primary spell factor by Arcanum dots in primary Arcanum –1"
                                                    Potency is only based on Arcanum rating if it's the PSF.
