        As soon as he was a way beyond the doors of the Boule Yi began to smile.

        There are ways that could have gone better, and most of them require ancient prophecies or someone else putting in a lot of legwork. Ordon is closed to outsiders, a mystery to their neighbors and friends, and now a path is opened to me. I would give thanks to happy chance if I thought she actually had anything to do with it or Heaven were it not what we make of it..

        With a laugh he continued on his way. Before he returned to the temple he made for a park he had seen. To each person he passed he said, "I go to teach the Way, and the will of Heaven. Will you follow me?" It did not matter if they were beggars clad in rags or nobility in their palanquins.

        When they had reach the park and all were seated as they were he began to speak.

        "Those who do wrong are not struck dead by lightning from the sky. Murders are not themselves murdered, thieves are not stolen from, hard work is not repayed with success, and in ten-thousand ways there is no justice. Some have said that those who go unpunished shall be punished in their next birth and those unrewarded shall be rewarded in their next birth. Yet their teachings are incomplete or confounded by worlds and times. If we do not know about life, on what ground do we stand concerning death?

        I tell you know the most hideous and terrible secret of the Creation, there is no good order save that which we make. There is no rightness in the world intrinsic too it by which it may be judged. There is no justice, there is just us.

        Now I tell you something more hideous because it is not secret. Everything is our fault. If the nation is good we deserve the credit. If times our wicked we have no one to blame. You cannot rely on gods or kings nor curse demons or fairies. If we are wiped out it is because we did not have the strength to defend ourselves or make use of that strength.

        By no doctrine under heaven can I make you greater than this. Not the Empress on her throne can claim to be the better of the urchin on the street in this.

        So answer me, in light of all this, what is the good?"

        In such way did begin to teach.
