    Resolve 4+Composure 4, -2 dice for being in Active Sight and rolling something unrelated to magic: 6 successes!


    Moonstone is in his element. He is achingly, painfully sane. He could look through the door, couldn't he? Just one step and he'd see beyond the wall. But- no. Not without testing the water. That would be Unwise. He hears himself saying to any minds listening: "I can see it. I'm in control. I'm going to look now."

    He reaches into his messenger bag and pulls out a cracked hand mirror. It's cold and real and not at all comforting in his hands, but the sharpness is grounding. It reminds him what's real and what's fake. He turns and stands with his back to the wall, ignoring the black hail clattering against Phoenix's shield, and he looks back through his mirror like a sharpshooter lining up a trick shot.

    Let's do this. Entering Focused Sight with Space and spending 1 WP to initiate Scrutiny, which puts me at 4. Gnosis 1+Space 2: 0 successes this turn. I have 2 more free rolls before failures risk polluting the Mystery.

                                                            Binder, Moonstone, and Phoenix
                                                            Each of you pours your soul into your attempt to tear into this Mystery. The very air crackles around you from the intensity of your scrutiny. It's like Schrodinger's cat, but magnified: the very act of observing this particular Iris, with all three mages resonating in concert, changes its quality.

                                                            You feel an incredible hunger for knowledge, an obsession for understanding the secret lying right before your eyes and your Sight. But it's not enough; in turn, you each find yourself falling short of the epiphany you crave, despite your desperate attempts to claw at the Supernal truth. Your efforts to do so are incredibly taxing, threatening to crack your sanity.

                                                            You long to continue. You lust for that knowledge, hanging just beyond your fingertips. If you just strain a little further...But is it wise to do so? Does it matter?

                                                            I'm giving each of you an Arcane Beat for the drama of this scene, and for your efforts to scrutinize the Mystery.


                                                            Moonstone
                                                            The strain on your mind, on your very sanity, temporarily blinds you from the gravity of what you are attempting. You overextend yourself, but you barely notice.

                                                            The Abyss does.

                                                            You angle the mirror to find the perfect angle, to line up your metaphysical strike against the Iris with Time. When you do, something moves from the door. A cloud of energy black as night seems to break off from the door, swirl around you, and infect the cloud of magic hanging around you. Cold and clammy tendrils wrap around your head and pierce into your mind, and refuse to let go. As you study the mirror, instead of your reflection, you see countless scenes flash by that you can't take your eyes away from -- scenes of your failures, your bone-deep shame, of pitiful weakness.

                                                            The whispers, the quiet laugh you heard lingering in the air around the Iris, turn to moans and screams that you can't shut out.

                                                            You gain the Abyssal Imago condition (p. 117). For the next day or until the condition resolves, you must spend at least 3 reach on each spell you cast or it automatically fails and provokes an enhanced Paradox roll.

                                                            You would also instinctively know that you can scour either or both Abyssal condition from your pattern by taking 1 resistant lethal damage per condition. Otherwise, you can gain an Arcane Beat if you let the condition resolve by lapsing.



                                                            Binder and Phoenix
                                                            While you attempt to scrutinize the Iris before you, you watch the black energy spin off and infect Moonstone further. His nimbus grows darker, mirroring the pitch black energy swirling around the Iris, and "glows" with an unholy anti-light as he attempts and fails to cast Time magic.
