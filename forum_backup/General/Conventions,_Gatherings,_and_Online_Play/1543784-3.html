                                                Gilad climbing up the baggage carousel seems to have snapped the people around him from their bored stupor, who call him down. Between gestures and the odd word he recognizes, they are able to communicate just enough so he can retrieve his luggage. Unfortunately, the gathering of people draws a lot of attention and, as he begins to follow the crowd to the next waypoint, he feels he is being followed.

                                                Remembering a few lessons from the village hunters, Gilad attempts to blend into the crowd, using pillars to break line of sight. When he still feels someone chasing him, he pulls out his incomplete music sheet. He visualizes it as his own personal song in the greater chorus of nature, starting to hear the sounds of his hometown as a distant echo, forming into musical notes that imprint an Imago onto the paper. He folds the sheet, hiding the melody and starts hearing it start to fade. Before it can, sudden notes of discord start playing, trying to draw more attention to it, someone is trying to interfere with his spellwork.
