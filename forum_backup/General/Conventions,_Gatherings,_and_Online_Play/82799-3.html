                            I am at work right now, but I plan to get my sheet up in the morning when I get home. Darksol brings up an interesting point about virtues. The default Azlanti virtues are Conviction, Courage, Duty and Loyalty. I plan to focus on Conviction, and to a lesser extent on Loyalty. Duty, however, is somewhat antithetical to my character's focus. If I were smart, I'd replace it with something more condusive like Endurance or Vengeance. However, I prefer not to change a Scion's virtues from those of the Pantheon. I see virtues in Scion as a supernatural thing, a part of their Legend and divine nature. After all, if they were not, very Tom, Dick and Mortal would have them (as in, say, Exalted 2E, where nearly every sapient being has the virtues Compassion, Conviction, Temperance and Valor). I view the virtues as part and parcel of being in a particular pantheon; so, I do not change them, even when it would cause me inconvenience. My character is a violent criminal, and he makes no apology for that fact. However, he is also a Scion of the Azlanti, and so feels some small shred of Duty telling him what he is doing is, at least to some extent, wrong. This single dot of Duty can also be a seed. If, during the course of the chronicle (perhaps through the influence of the other PCs), my character does start to feel a stronger sense of duty, that seed might grow. I prefer to leave that potential intact.
