                            Hemophage, your character looks like a social monster (which is a good thing), but I should warn you that the ST likes to throw oddball rolls out. For example, there were times where we had to roll outside of our favored/castes abilities (Mayu has had to roll sail, melee, and integrity a few times). I'm not saying that you need to put something in every ability or you can't have five dots in an ability, but spreading your dots around a bit more would make your character's life easier.

                            Also, don't underestimate the power of the 2nd Excellency Charm. It's probably my most used charm in the game.

                                                            If it's a short session like that, my vote would be to wait until the following week.

                                                            I was mentioning that I wanted to make some custom charms, so Aeroz suggested I post it to see what we could come up with. I'd like something that helps with flurries. There is an existing magical flurry charm (Iron Whirlwind - 5m, 1wp) which gives you Dex+1 attacks, ignoring the weapon's natural Rate and all flurry penalties. I read this as something you use with your Rate 2 Grand Daiklave more than anything else. It's more about the extra attacks in a round, then the reduction in penalties thought I'm sure those help.

                                                            I'd like something that lets me make attacks within the Rate of the weapons but without the penalties for Flurry. So, the Grand Daiklave would only get 2 attacks, but my paired Short Daiklaves would get 6 (Rate 3 + Rate 3). I was thinking it would reduce the flurry penalty for 1m per flurry die penalty negated. The reduction would apply to every attack in the flurry. Limit Dex+1 motes can be spent.

                                                            Examples: Grand Daiklave, Rate 2: pay 2m and make a two attack flurry.
                                                            • First attack is at your base pool (-2 for the flurry, +2 for the charm).
                                                            • Second attack at base pool -1 (-3 for the flurry, +1 for the charm)

                                                            Example: Daiklave, Rate 4: Pay 5m and make a 3 Flurry attack.
                                                            • First attack: full pool (-3 for flurry, +5 for charm).
                                                            • Second attack: full pool (-4 for flurry, +5 for charm).
                                                            • Third attack: full pool (-5 for flurry, +5 for charm)

                                                            Example: Paired Short Daiklave, Rate 6: Pay 6m and make a 6 flurry attack.
                                                            • First attack: full pool (-6 Flurry, +6 charm)
                                                            • Second attack: pool-1 (-7 for Flurry, +6 for charm)
                                                            • Third attack: pool-2 (-8 for Flurry, +6 for charm)
                                                            • Fourth attack: pool-3
                                                            • Fifth attack: pool-4
                                                            • Sixth attack: pool-5 (-11 for Flurry, +6 for charm)

                                                            Thoughts?
