    [Recruitment] Chained In Nightmares - Homebrew Exalted 2.5 Play-By-Post

    03-21-2014, 07:15 PM
    This game is a playtest for my supplement Masters of the Industrial Elements, so apologies to those expecting a game about Exalts. This game will feature the Stonebearers, mortals who have risked their lives to wrestle the power of an Autochthonian Elemental into submission. Specifically, this game will follow the adventures of a group of Stonebearers from the Thundering Falls Tribe, a once-prosperous tribe who now find themselves struggling to survive at the edges of a massive blight zone. I'm looking for around six players for this game, but I may take more or less, depending on the quality of submissions.

    Some information about me: I'm a very sandboxy Storyteller. I'm fairly sure that any plot I think of will be derailed within a scene or two and that you will have ideas that are better than what I was thinking of anyway and I'm fine with that. I am also somewhat bad at dangling plot hooks. Combined, this means that my players should expect to come up with their own course of action from time to time and should feel free to improvise something.

    Prospective players should create starting Domini characters as per the default rules. If you want to use gear from outside of core or the MoIE book, including items from MoEP:Alchemicals, or custom items, please check with me before counting on them. I'd love to work out a custom Binding Stone or other artifact with you and I'm probably going to say yes to most things, but Monofilament Scourges and Gyroscopic Chakrams probably won't make it in due to the Taken's low-tech aesthetics. Your character should have a backstory that covers what s/he does in the tribe and why s/he decided to risk his/her life to become a Dominus. Don't feel the need to write a novel - the best backstories are long enough to cover the important stuff and short enough to keep it interesting. If you have in mind any cool things you would like your character to be able to do or achieve in the course of play, feel free to include those.

    Players should feel free to raise any complaints they want about any of my homebrew or my Storytelling. I won't hold it against you and this is a playtest - finding problems, hiccups, and inconsistencies is the point.

    I won't be making decisions about who is in for a couple of weeks. I'd like people to not feel the need to rush.

                                  1. A veteran guard is certainly possible. What do you want out of him as a character?
                                  2. If you want some quick and easy stuff, look over 2 dot hearthstones. If you want to make something up, just run it by me. The lack of an artifact is a tricky spot. How tightly spread is your background dots?

                                  4. That one has come up a few times and I blame myself for being unclear. Attacks are almost always made with Dexterity, so I didn't think to mention it. The weapon stat line, though, has the accuracy as +Essence, not +Aspect. So an attack with (Aspect) Bolt Attack would be Dexterity + Aspect + Essence. When comparing (Aspect) Bolt Attack to (Elemental) Bolt Attack, Stonebearers do seem to have the better Charm. This is because of a combination of factors - the basics of the Stonebearer version is pulled from a God-Blooded mutation in Scroll of Heroes, Domini have smaller Essence pools (they start at 11ish) than Dragon-Blooded and so are paying a lot proportionally to use the Charm, and the Charm hasn't really been playtested yet.

                                                      Alright.

                                                      I like the backstory for the most part. The only bit out of place is the bit about the surgeon - the surgeon has no direct influence over a Dominus' chance of success in mastering an elemental. Also, 8+ Willpower is certainly common in char-op, but your character's willpower is exceptionally high from an IC stand point.

                                                      Mechanics-wise, I have some concerns.

                                                      First, are you aware that you can probably get the same Ability list as you have for 3 bonus points cheaper? Use freebie dots for Investigation, Survival, and Thrown and then buy your Resistance or Integrity dots with bonus points for half the price.

                                                      Second, you could change your Specialty in Inspiring Troops to Inspiring Others if you wanted and you probably should change Countering Stealth to something a little more specific, like Ambushes.

                                                      Third, are you sure you only want Dexterity 2? It's sort of the god-stat of combat. I can see why you have Stamina 4, but you could probably drop a dot of Strength without feeling it too much.

                                                      Fourth, are you sure you want both Charisma 3, Manipulation 3, and Appearance 1? Charisma and Manipulation are hard to leverage at the same time and Appearance is pretty key in social MDV values.

                                                      Fifth, are you sure you want to use a Focus Talent purchase on Resisting Poison? It's one of the only ways to increase your Excellency cap (right now, you can buy 1 die) and you only get three of them per Excellency. I can certainly see resisting poison as useful, especially given the local environment, but I feel I should double check that you've considered the ramifications.

                                                      Sixth, I think you could do with Resources 1 as you are not literally penniless and you would have great use for a jade hearthstone amulet or artifact weapon

                                                      Seventh, I think you should change your Intimacy from Great Maker to Machine King (the Taken's term for him) and should drop the Intimacies to the Divine Ministers and to the Octet. While I mentioned them heavily, I don't think that the Taken are aware of the Divine Ministers or the Octet as such. The Taken, as far as my conception of them goes, are aware that the Machine King has his faithful mortal servants (they term them "slaves") but are not aware of what their current organization is. They also probably have lumped the Divine Ministers in with the rest of the exmachina as the spawn of the Machine King.

                                                      On the whole, I think you are looking pretty solid. I don't think I'll have much trouble motivating your character with the plot I have in mind. Don't feel you have to change everything I pointed out - most of it is just me making sure you are aware of the mechanical ramifications of your choices.
