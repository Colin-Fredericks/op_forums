        I want to make it clear that Mathias' actions after the fight with Alux were fueled by his backstory and personal demons, which were hinted at but not fully divulged in this scene. But even with that history, he and the other Shields of Dawn have the temperaments that oppose pointless violence or brute forced methods.

        The reality though is that some other mages in this game setting could easily enjoy using their superior Arcana mastery to ruin Moonstone's day in a way that might evoke similar feelings of being threatened or controlled against his will -- things like being violently blasted away with magic, mind controlled, or humiliated by other magical means. This to me is an illustration of how access to real power can corrupt people, which is a core theme in this game. Do you think that might be a problem for you as well? Or are you asking that we avoid or minimize use of graphic physical violence related to acts of forced submission specifically?

        I also want to make sure that as a ST that I'm allowing each of our players to get the spotlight and feel successful and satisfied with their characters. I have no issue with PCs attacking or RPing through conflict together, especially in heated situations like what has happened here. Sometimes that leads to people arguing over which PC would be able to get the upper hand, and in my opinion, the most fair way to resolve that is to use the game mechanics (like attack/defense stats and rolls) inherent in this system. This is why Moonstone was able to evade Phoenix's attack earlier even though crapcarp wanted him to hit you, and why I agree that Moonstone should be able to escape his grab again now. Rules help arbitrate these conflicts. Do you have an issue with that approach to the game, or was it more specifically about the narrative used in his post when he tried to grab Moonstone (or something else altogether)?

        Basically I just need to know that if Phoenix or any NPC want to (and have the capacity to) get comeuppance against Moonstone that we're on the same page about it, because this issue is likely to come up again. Power and control are parts of this game system.

            I didn't mean to make it a big problem, let me be a little more clear. I pretty much don't have an issue with things NPCs do, because that's more easily resolved. I do sort of prefer explicitly magical/supernatural/fantastical threats (being trapped in a Space ward, banished to the Shadow, attacked by dude with flaming sword, pursued by demon monster, Mind control in small doses, etc) over totally mundane and grounded harm (being mugged or beaten up in a dark alley, using slurs, etc). Like in my home game, there was initially a focus on racialized gang violence in the background, until the group decided that wasn't fun and we replaced those elements with more fantastic Jet Set Radio/The Warriors style gangs.

            But the actual issue isn't so much the fictional content as that it was coming from a PC rather than an NPC. I can try to solve or just endure something coming from an NPC in a variety of ways, but I don't want there to be a status quo of open hostility and fighting with another PC where we're both going to be working together. That and the language used for Phoenix's narration about Moonstone read to me as kind of over-the-top cruel and demeaning in a way that was a little too close to home. Like, because of the fact that we're in a group together, Moonstone isn't just going to bug out and leave if there's an issue with another PC, and what's unfun for me is being in a position where there's a problem that might cause OOC tension if it were resolved with game rules. I just want everybody to be friends!

            That's all! Again, not meaning to make it a huge concern, it's just something that had been bothering me for a little while that I decided to state openly instead of ignoring. Thanks for being accommodating!

                            Moonstone's plan for the next few days: recover from his resistant Lethal damage at an Arrow sanctum, and hopefully get in touch with Hakim's former cabal just to say that he's Awakened, he might have a new cabal, and he'd like to be in touch with them going forward.

                            Go to Rumors Nightclub, or some other semi-classy space that does cabaret and burlesque shows, and start talking to an organizer about dancing as part of a showcase. Ideally this would be Social Maneuvering to impress the lady/dude/person in charge and get on the list.

                            Get the name of an Adept of Mind who might be willing to do some goetic summoning and Familiar binding for him, preferably one who respects the name of Hakim.

                            Spend some time out around town collecting good sacraments for spellcasting, hopefully stuff suitable for a +2 Sacrament yantra. Old watches for Time, transit maps of the area for Space, crosswords and dream journals for Mind, etc. I was thinking I could make a Skill roll now and then treat my total successes as a pool that I can spend in the future to have the perfect sacrament on hand for a given spell.

                            Also there's some specific spells I want to cast, possibly ritual cast some of them as long as we're hanging out at a proper sanctum.
                            • Divination to learn when and where Alux will return from hibernation. There's also a Time/Space spell from Banishers that I want to adapt which lets you learn the perfect time and place to attack someone, and gives bonuses/penalties if you do so (maybe modeled on Fate boons/hexes.)
                            • Attempt Postcognition to see what happened in the scene Moonstone and Phoenix spent in the otherworld. I assume this will be more complicated than that because the portal exists in a weird place relative to Time, but I want to see what happens. Also try Perfect Recall for a similar effect.
                            • Dream Reaching to see what Mathias is dreaming about, just from sheer voyeuristic curiosity and looking for insight into things the prophet sees on the horizon.
                            • Correspondence as a ritual casting to further his Obsession. I want to boost Potency as high as possible and cast it on Hakim with the Reach options to follow a trail and see a specific type of connection (those related to the Deathstorm, if they exist). Moonstone has three hypotheses right now: either all victims of the Deathstorm have Sympathy to one another directly, or they each individually have Sympathy to a single person/entity/place/thing that embodies the storm, or there is no shared Sympathy and it's a null result.
                            • Attempt to use Focused Mage Sight during Postcognition to scrutinize/reveal Alux. I'm thinking of targeting it during the 3 turns where it was manifested in the alley, and seeing what I can uncover.
                            • Do some experimentation with Sympathy, and learn whether or not he can effectively create a Symbolic-level sympathy yantra by acting as/impersonating someone (Manipulation+Expression) and recording the result. The image of Moonstone spending an hour alone, repeatedly trying to imitate someone's voice and the way they move, is deeply weird and unsettling and appealingly Mage-y to me.
