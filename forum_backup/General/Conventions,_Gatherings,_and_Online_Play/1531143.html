      Chapter 1
      It is the stroke of midnight, January 1st, 2016. The chimes of Big Ben ring out. The sky over London lights up with fireworks launched from the river and the Eye of London.

      Boris Johnson is Mayor of London. Elizabeth II is Queen of England, the longest reigning monarch in modern history. David Cameron has been re-elected Prime Minister and promised to hold a referendum on the United Kingdom remaining in the European Union. Two months ago, multiple terrorist attacks in Paris killed 130 people. A massive earthquake recently shook Afghanistan and Pakistan, causing 398 deaths.

      Across the pond, Barack Obama is president of the United States, the first reusable rocket has been launched into earth orbit, and a prominent tech company building an Artificial Intelligence has been founded. No one has heard of SARS-CoV-2, but there is an epidemic of the Zika virus in South America. Rio de Janeiro is planning to host the Summer Olympics in the Summer.

      Several skyscrapers in London are illuminated in blue in honor of UNICEF's pledge to help the children of the Syrian Civil War. The Spire, an elongated pyramid of steal and glass, the tallest building in Western Europe, towers over the city.

              A few days later Mekhaine approaches the Spire. Even from a block away the supernal energy threatens to overwhelm his unseen sense.

              There is a large man in a big coat and hat standing by the door.
              "Can't go in there yet sir. Not open to the public yet."

              Mekhaine explains that he has an appointment and the door man confirms by walkie-talkie. He enters the lobby which has rich carpeting, wood paneling, and magnificent crystal chandeliers in a futuristic style. A bell man shows him to the elevator to go up to the Hotel level, 50 stories up. The elevator is so fast it seems like it adds an extra G.

              The restaurant has a magnificent view of the city, looking down on the Tower Bridge and the Eye, as the sun has begun to set. Magic hour as a photographer might say.

              "Right this way, sir. Mr. Amjad is waiting for you at your table."

                  02-23-2024, 10:03 PM
                  Sitting at her blocky laptop, worn from use, the Mage known to some as Mother Time stared blankly at the lit up screen. Eventually, with a sigh she closed the lid on the device and put it to the side. "Enough of this, I can't waste any more time with useless devices." If she was going to find the right office she needed to look up the records for the Walworth Estate, she needed something more precise. And nothing was more precise than the Prophet's reckoning.

                  She pulled out a map of London, unraveling it across the surface of a conference table in her office. Placing whatever books she had on hand to hold down the corners, she stared at the flat diagram of the city. She pulled out her silver pocket watch, her most precious of tools, and speaking in a tongue few could understand, began to chant. She opened her pocket watch's lid, and then moved it slowly in a pendulum motion. Each motion was accompanied by millimeters of southern movement, the watch passing above every inch of the map, scanning London for where precisely she was needed.

                  After almost 10 seconds of chanting and swinging, she was beginning to lose hope on finding the right place. Until-

                  -CLINK-

                  -the watch's lid closed the moment it passed over a particular office building. Finally, just what she was looking for!

                                      The clerk seemed surprised.

                                      "You want to do research in the housing archives? Two in one day! We don't normally get this many visitors. There's another woman already in the archive stacks. Sign the visitor book here mame".

                                      Briggett notices the name above hers. "Trish Takehashi" in nearly perfect cursive penmanship.

                                      Briggett goes into the archives where there are rows and rows of dust shelves filled with papers. Some of them are bound and cataloged, but some papers seem to have been piled up in boxes in a disorganized fashion.

                                      In the back stacks, in a section that seems to contain material from when the Walworth estate was first built, Briggett sees a tall, slim, Asian woman. She is young, and could almost be confused for one of Briggett's senior students where it not for her smart, fashionable outfit. She is reaching for a box on the top shelf then puts it down to begin sorting through it. She looks up and sees Briggett and smiles. She has very striking looks.

                                      "Hello! I didn't expect anyone else back here but myself." She looks down at herself and notices that she has dust all over, and brushes some off. She straightens up into a formal pose and bows slightly and then offers her hand. "My name is Trish. How do you do?

                                      Her English is good though maybe a little formal, clearly learned as a second language. Her accent is half way between American and British, and sounds a little bit like an old Hollywood moviestar. It reminds Briggett a little of Audrey Hepburn.

                                          The Mage known as Berith eased into his desk, strewn in papers in an eclectic form of organization between plans, budgets, and correspondences from his colleagues. The Moros closed his eyes, mulling over a number of vectors in which he could explore. While his welcoming to higher knowledge had propelled his career from one of just idle service and into something more active and profitable, the roads were not always the most clear.

                                          But, Thomas Haytham was Berith, and Berith could put much better use to what Thomas had than he could do alone before. So, first on his docket... was figuring out his next move. Berith, a great Duke of Hell, who knew things and could tell them not only of the Past, and Present, but also the future. He closed his eyes, his mind playing the images of possibility on a reel like film. He followed the mental routes he could take and explored them to ultimately two questions:

                                          "Who might I speak to, about local projects, that would have information useful to the Ministry?"

                                          And

                                          "where will this search for useful information take me?" The symbols for finality that lingered naturally to his path now welcomed to the queries.

                                                  Berith let a low chuckle slip from his lips. Excitement, and apprehension, mingled in the pit of his stomach. The presence of those on high never not unsettled him. Then, well, disappointment slipped into the mix as he took up the cup of tea. Rolling it slowly to inspect its angles. Opening a window, he'd dump the frozen solution out with a plop into the grass below. Thomas resumed as himself, leaving the cup in his office as he left for the day for far more interesting things.

                                                  Berith, now approaching the office building took a moment to peer through the windows and take stock of the applicants. He wore a business friendly smile, and he twisted the ring on his finger for luck before pressing inside. The Prophet's scion, and the servant of the Fox Riddler. Who could they be?

                                                  Quickly, Thomas approached the desk, his hands set flat and gentle on thr top. "Hello, dove," he greeted pleasantly. "I'm looking to do some research on just a couple of things, and maybe meet with a couple of colleagues here. Sign in and go okay?"

                                                  Already, Thomas' attention was shifting to see who had arrived today while he fished up a pen, his gaze flicking back up with a smile.

                                                      I deal in one of the most valuable commodities of them all, information, and I have a standing deal with a contact in Panopticon. I keep an eye out on anything, or anyone, they are interested in and they supply me with information in exchange.
