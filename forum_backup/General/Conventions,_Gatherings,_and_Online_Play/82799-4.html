            About XP. I will reward much more than suggested in the book. I tried different methods to make my players spend their XP but it was once too restricted (they could only spend points depending on what happened) or too conventional (very little XP). I even offered lots of XP but it didn't help them what was best to buy... (first-time players probably).
            I'll be trying a new system which would look like the video game one. You won't have to wait until the end of the chapter/storyline before earning XP. Every time something is worth to be getting my attention and helps the Scion to progress or helps the storyline to evolve, I'll grant XP through PM. You might spend them whenever you feel like you need an extra dot or Boon. It might ease the tasks but we're not playing to frustrate the players all the time.

            I don't think I'll be changing the way I'll grant XP. If you disagree with how to spend them, however, we may proceed differently (as the book suggests).
