                      You can have any set of rolls for each milestone. For example, you may have a milestone focused in they taking a off road trip to avoid a road block that and up with they needing to spend the night in the woods (survival as skill) and another of them driving through a city avoiding the police (using Larceny) and another scene that is basically a road trip with some obstacles (requiring pilot).

                      Personally, unless the story is focused in the trip itself, going by car will be extremely boring, as they will need to sleep, refil the tank, take some time to eat. So you can have the many different scenes of a trip movie or can just jump to the end, as it’s not going to be THAT much of an action most time.

                          This is a cinematic game trying to emulate action movies.

                          In an action movie like Mission Impossible would you see scenes of people travelling for hours on end from one continent to the next? No, that would be boring. You just skip right to the new place (with a nice overhead shot and a text saying where we are now) to get to the action.

                          If I had the characters go for an international continent chase that takes days then chase rules are entirerly the wrong approach. Use the Milestone system instead with maybe 3-5 rolls. These are just some examples:
                          1) Technology + Wits to have the hacker trace their quarry through the technology they use.
                          2) Academics/Enigmas + Intellect to figure out the best route to take to avoid notice.
                          3) Persuasion + Manipulation to have the face character call in a few favors to slow their quarry down.
                          4) Integrity + Composure to make straight-faced lies when the corrupt police tries to slow the characters down.
                          5) Drive + Wits to stalk the quarry discretely.

                          Any failures during these rolls will accumulate Complications such as:
                          - The characters strain themselves to get there on time and are hungry and tired when they reach their quarry.
                          - The characters draw too much attention to themselves and get on the news.
                          - Some corrupt police are sent to deal with the characters.
                          - The quarry becomes well aware that they are followed and organize an ambush or gather more allies.

                          Once the characters get to their quarry THEN you can break out chase rules.

                                  My suggestion is you give a certain number of “free successes” to start with, representing the head start the characters have over the pursuers.

                                  Each milestone the characters spend their successes to do their regular actions and the extra successes they can spend to gain advantage (add to the pool), representing they did their chores under the expected time and so they hit the road faster. The pursuers do the same in their turn, except they subtract those successes from the pool (with the characters not knowing how well they are doing over the pursuers).

                                  If the poll reach 0, the pursuers reached the characters at some point, and the real action will start.

                                  Let’s say they reach and there was a big fight in the road, with shots between vehicles. If the characters break all the pursuers vehicles they will be left behind and the characters gain a new pool of successes representing the pursuers fixing their vehicles.

                                              It depends on you as a SG want the, to be reached or not. I think you are over complicating a decision you could do without rolling dice.

                                              Honestly, I personally would do as I want, and the players would have to deal with it. The idea of adding scenes to the story on the fly can have some unwanted results. It’s not like you are opening new ways, it’s more kind of needing to plan for additional scenes that probably won’t happen (or probably will).

                                              If your pursuers are as good as the characters, giving 6 successes will make them never reach them, if they are better, the result is the opposite, they will reach sooner or latter, and it’s a LONG trip to think as a chase.

                                              You don’t even know it they will fly or drive. If they drive, and the pursuers know when’re they are going, they would fly and reach the destination before (days before), and if they fly, there is not much open space to roll, as they will be in the plane, pretty high and out of reach.
