                        The main developers of #SPUltra describe why the new edition was created. They admit that the distinction between Complication and Difficulty was not clear in the current books. They admit that Advantage is a better term for the earlier Scale - And they say that they should not have inserted a table with examples of Scale, e.g. that the Empire State Building has Size Scale 5. Personally, I disagree with this, because how is the SGC supposed to assess the size or power of something when the player comes with, I don't know, a Scale 6 character? 🤔
                        The new Injury system gives BONUSES to characters - it is taken from They Came From, but also from 7th Sea. What Devs sells is that the Positive Death Spiral makes players want to get hurt a little to get bonuses - but not enough to be on the brink of death. And that it fits the genres Storypath wants to emulate. 🤔
