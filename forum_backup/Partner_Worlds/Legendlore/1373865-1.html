        Today at Kickstarter Legendlore shared a new preview of the text - character Classes. On March 18th Backgrounds (and yet there are, apart from Legend!) and Feats will be available, and March 20th will be Magic and Spells in Legendlore. Each Class can give modifiers instead of Attributes from Peoples' cultures. And now the details of the news from Classes - we get two new ones, Alchemist and Gunslinger. Alchemists are magicians who want magic like science and shut it in a bottle, to help everyone. Gunslingers are a new type of warriors who fight with limited firearms brought by Visitors to the Realm.
        ​
        The other classic classes received new subclasses - Barbarian got Woad Painted, who draw power from runes and tattoos - and Ocean Raider who are savage warriors on ships. Bard receives looking for Bakhurun ​​who also deal with runes and symbols. Cleric gets the Eye of Othaaris - priests of light, fire and observation. We also have Druid of Fantasia, who receive more animal features on a daily basis, like horns, hooves, etc. giving small bonuses. The warriors get O'koth Ranger, who look like a fantasy version of commandos - and White Unicorn, a female order of warriors who defend others. Monk gets Fist of Othaaris who have burning hands and use several of Cleric’s spells. Way of the Broken Sword for a tradition dealing with pacifism and defending others through defensive powers. Paladin gets the Oath of Fealty, which focuses on defending his country, above all. Ranger gets nothing. Rogue has Hidden Shadow, who are the killers who boast that the best of them will kill a dozen people in the light of day. Sorcerer gets Sourceborn, who are connected directly to the source of full magic in the Land - and thus repel the menthruac (???) effect, and then change Warlock receives Parons as the Demon of the Shadow Lands, which is the 'evil demon' here - but also Last Unicorn, which is a force of good, and lets you act with slow moves. The last is the Wizard who receives Arcane Artificer who binds with an object from Earth and they use their magic through him. Then they can repair objects from Earth or even create impossible objects from outside the Land.

        glamourweaver, you will be interested in this.
