          A) Like all our other forums, we create them as needed. There's no requirement that says we have to keep everything consigned to a single forum forever.

          B) Pugmire and Cavaliers of Mars are creator-owned properties. While Onyx Path is publishing them, they are not Onyx Path games, so "Onyx Path Originals" would be very misleading. Perhaps misleading in a legally actionable sense.

          Right now, Onyx Path owns the Trinity Continuum, Scion, and co-owns the Scarred Lands. Pugmire is owned by Eddy Webb's Pugsteady, and Cavaliers is owned by Rose Bailey.
