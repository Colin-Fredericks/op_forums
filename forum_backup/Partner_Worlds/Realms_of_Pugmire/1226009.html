    What Unknown Uplifted Races do you think there?

    06-08-2018, 10:44 PM
    So we already have an idea of three other uplifted races that has been mentioned in the material. Rats, Badgers, and Lizards. We also have a small inkling on how they are like on a societal level as well or at least we have ideas. That said these three certainly aren't the last of the uplifted races we can see in the future, but it's beginning. So here's a question. What other animals do you think will show up in the world of pugmire, and to add a little bit more to that. How do you envision there society to appear like. Whether super detailed or through stereotypes by the other races. Just thought this be a fun thing to ask.

                              I would really love it if the currently defined Uplifted species were to get their own books. I feel like I'm getting kind of worried that it won't happen. Rats with their Science! and pseudo psionics on one hand and Italian crime families on the other are exciting to me. I'm getting an almost Arabian Nights vibe from the Lizard NPCs we're seeing, and there's so much to explore there. And then there's the Mustelids we seem to naturally think of as the 'weasel family' that we're calling Badgers for some reason. At first blush they seem like the Orcs of the setting but I'd almost give them a pre-Roman Celt feel. I feel like Rats would almost have the best chance to be the next thing just because they don't necessarily need a *new* slice of the map laid out for them. On the other hand Badgers actually already have the six 'divisions' already laid out from the Pugmire book. Still, I'd love for some Lizard action.

                              If I had to guess a future unreferenced Uplifted species I'd have to guess Birds. Feels like Apes are right out, but Birds seem to make sense and you could get some great divisions within them, from your Songbirds to your Raptors to your Mimics to your Carrion-eaters.

                                                          Now that's all well and good and perfectly fine, and maybe in 10 years we'll have quite a few source books on other uplifts, and there will still be no reason not to have some custom uplifts at your table cause that's the game you want

                                                          I think it would be nice to know more about how the uplifts were chosen, but no need to put too fine a point of the mystery of the world either. not for some time at least. It could be some scientist or group knew mankind was on it's way out and had little time to preserve..., well, not "humanity" but intelligent life on earth and quick and dirty, started the Uplift of what was at hand, cats, rats, and dogs, and it took some number of generations and that's about all there is, but with some fallout that generated the monsters too.
