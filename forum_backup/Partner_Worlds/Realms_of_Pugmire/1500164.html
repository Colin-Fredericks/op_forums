                          Biggest rules change I see in the RoP 2E Preview 1 are changes to Attributes. Other changes are seems to be lesser than those. ( At least in Character Creation chapter )

                          Originally posted by Preview 1, p. 35'
                          Each attribute ranges from -3 to +5. They are as follows:
                          • Agility: Physical coordination and dexterity.
                          • Might: Brute strength and the ability to apply physical force.
                          • Vitality: Overall hardiness and resistance to pain.
                          • Cunning: Wit, wisdom, and common sense.
                          • Intellect: Mental skill and knowledge.
                          • Presence: Social awareness and personality.

                              Design Dairy from Eddy - Converting from Realms of Pugmire 1E to RoP2E - meaning for Pugmire, Monarchies of Mau, Squeaks in the Deep - is VERY simple. Major changes from 1E:
                              • Attributes are now only modifiers -3 to +5 - no full stats from 3 to 20
                              • All Species can choose all Callings if the Guide agrees - Cat Artisan, Dog Minister, etc.
                              • More Skills are pinned to Charisma / Presence - thus this Attribute becomes not only the 'beauty' of the character, but the understanding of social interactions - as well as resistances to them.

                                  Podcast from the devs of Onyx Path about the OGL drama in the RPG industry - and how it affected the writing of Realms of Pugmire 2E (TLDR: Not so much taken over by RoP. 😎 )

                                  Podcast Highlights:
                                  • 20:15 - The history of OGL stems from the fight against piracy in D&D in 2000 - but it started with the beginning of D&D in 1974
                                  • 29:00 - As Realms of Pugmire was written - why OGL was removed in 2E - and why it doesn't actually change anything in the game and can safely leave OGL, releasing its Onyx20 mechanic (inspired by 5E).
                                  • 36:20 – History of a (not) lawsuit from Vampire: the Masquerade against an attempt to copy the entire brand to another game
                                  • Bonus - Everything UNTIL 20:15 - Discussion about the 90s in culture and RPG.
