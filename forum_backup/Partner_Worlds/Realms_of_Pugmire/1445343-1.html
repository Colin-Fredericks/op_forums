    Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post
    So with Rodents in this book, are “Badgers” the only canonical uplifted species group not to get player character coverage?

    To answer your question without answering your question, I quote the Canis Minor FAQ:

    Pugsteady and Onyx Path reserve the right to product official products featuring rules for playing all mentioned species (including dogs, cats, rats, mice, lizards, geckos, turtles, snakes, birds, badgers, otters, polecats, weasels, ferrets, wolverines, and rabbits).

    There have been official player character rules for dog (Pugmire), cats (Monarchies of Mau), rats and mice (Squeaks in the Deep), lizards, geckos, turtles, snakes, and birds (Pirates of Pugmire). That leaves badgers, otters, polecats, weasels, ferrets, wolverines, and rabbits.

    Make of that what you will.
