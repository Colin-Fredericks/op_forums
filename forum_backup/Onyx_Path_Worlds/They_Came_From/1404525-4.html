                            ​The Came From Beneath The Streets

                            Urban heroes - modern or from the late 70s to early 90s - dealing with monsters from down in the sewers, subway tunnels, and lost catacombs. Giant albino alligators, homeless cannibal mutants, giant insects that masquerade as people, weird cults, deformed vampires, were-rats, ghosts, urbanized fairy folk, and other crazy things.

                            Either as its own thing or as an expansion for Beyond the Grave.

                            See films such as Aligator (1980), CHUD (1984), Mimic (1997), IT (2017/2019), Blade 2 (2002), and The Blob (1988).

                                They Came From the Terrible Present

                                This time, the players are chronauts trying to fix "terrible events that happened in the past". Traveling back in time to, for instance, avoid the World Wars, the spreading of deadly diseases, or even to give the right information/inspiration to certain scientists/artists to further advance their areas. Maybe we can have people going back in time for selfish reasons (like, to avoid their past self to meet their now-ex and skip and entire bad chapter in their lives). Maybe the players are trying to refix something that somebody else tried to fix, but actually made things worst in the present day.

                                        They Came From The Grindhouse. The PCs are the protagonists of sleazy 70s exploitation flix. Inner city street hustlers, women in prison, redneck racecar drivers, and other assorted weirdos.

                                        Obviously this has potential for crossover with Classified (some of the stranger ends of the Bondsploitation genre) and Beyond the Grave (stuff like Texas Chainsaw Massacre and other grindhouse horror), as well as expansion into things like Japanese yakuza and delinquent films.
