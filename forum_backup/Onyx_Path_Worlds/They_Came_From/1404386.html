                              Yesterday I run the my own ‘Dinner at Karl’s’ story with group of friends and my life partner. It was quickly made hodge podge of old manor, mysterious invite, forcing cake/brains to eat by guests, invisible threats and satanic rituals. 😂 Very absurdist and run ad hock. We set to use the Cinematics and Quips systems, so Players got twice the number of them Rewrites on the start and 7 Cinematics to choose from list, instead of general 5 by core rules. I also award good role play and sticking to convention of ‘bad horror movie’ by new Rewrites, instead Experience – as it was one-shot. Players by the end used most of them, but we felt they do not really need to generate them – they spent like about 10 Rewrites, but get back about 5 of them. My partner was most generating one the Rewrites by roleplaying and humor ( he was just sooo good at this 😉 ).

                              The other ‘problem’ ( kinda? ) we found was with no way to make new Quips in play – partner made great one-liners that where other than those from his sheet – and even deliver truly Award-Winning scene about it. ( With with water heaters, electrocution and satanic cultists 😁 ) I rolled with making it Award-Winning as it surly would be in trailer or Oscar nomination ceremony – but by the rules, he could not use it – as there where no the Quips from his sheet. 😞

                              After all – great one-shot system, maybe run it once again on Halloween – only Quips and Cinematics cards are MUST HAVE! I solved this by printing lists of Quips and Cinematics from manuscript preview - and players drew Quips by tossing a pen on them - but the cards of these powers would have made everything MUCH easier, in my opinion.
