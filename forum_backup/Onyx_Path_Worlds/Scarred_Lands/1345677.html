    Yugman's Guide to Ghelspad

    11-03-2019, 08:49 PM
    As announced at http://theonyxpath.com/onyx-path-pub...red-lands-rpg/
    Yugman’s Guide to Ghelspad offers new insight into heroes and villains, as well as the tools they use to rise to power, through the eyes of Yugman the Sage. This release will contain:
    • 21 new Social Backgrounds expanding the sects & societies introduced in the Scarred Lands Player’s Guide
    • 23 new subclasses, with each base class represented
    • 6 new Races including Broadreach dwarves, and a revised Hollow Legionnaire
    • New Spells, True Rituals, Magic Items and more!

    Yugman’s Guide to Ghelspad is written by Chris Bissette (Loot the Room, Breaker of Chains), Oliver Clegg (Ravenloft: City of Eyes, Weekend at Strahd’s), Celeste Conowitch (Blood Sea: The Crimson Abyss 5e, Venture Maidens), Beatriz T. Dias (The Fall of Durgan’s Rest, Off to a Weird Start), Lewis Harris (Destiny 2, Hunter: The Reckoning – Wayward), Jeremy Hochhalter (Creature Codex for 5th Edition, A Titanic Feast), Megan Mackie (Legendlore RPG), Jessica Ross (Sundered Sisters, Uncaged Volume I), and Christopher Walz (Morty Corgi’s Scroll of Mysteries, The Midnight Revelry).

    Let's talk about it!

                                  Let's talk about it!
                                  So, now that we have 5 of the 6 volumes out, let's figure what is missing and what will be in part 6 ^^

                                  Part 1
                                  3 races (broadreach dwarf, half-elf, hollow legionnaire), not sure if the dwarf or the hollow legionnaire count as "new"
                                  5 new subclasses (cleric, druid, fighter x2, warlock)
                                  Spells
                                  Eldricht invocation

                                  Part 2
                                  6 social background
                                  2 races (triton, scrag)
                                  2 subclasses (rogue x2)
                                  Spells
                                  Blood sea alchemy

                                  Part 3
                                  3 social background
                                  3 subclasses (sorcerer, wizard x2)
                                  Spells

                                  Part 4
                                  6 social background
                                  2 subclasses (paladin, rogue)
                                  Equipment
                                  Magic items
                                  Spells

                                  Part 5
                                  6 social background
                                  3 subclasses (barbarian, fighter, monk)
                                  2 races (half-elf, minotaur)

                                  So in total we have already
                                  21/21 social background
                                  15/23 subclasses (no bard or ranger subclass)
                                  7/6 races (or maybe 5 or 6 depending what counts as new)
                                  Spells, Magic items, and more (no true rituals)

                                  So, unless I can't count, volume 6 will mostly be subclasses with a few true rituals, with at least 1 for the bard and 1 for the ranger, and maybe other miscalneous materials, and a tiny possibility of having 1 new race (quite unlikely though). Given that there are no rangers in the SLPG, I expect that this one will contain more than 1 ranger subclass ^^
