    Scarred Lands Kickstarter! (Pathfinder and 5th Edition)

    01-05-2016, 04:46 PM
    Update: The Kickstarter is over, but you can still pre-order.

    The Kickstarter for a new edition of Scarred Lands is now live!

    Born at the advent of 3e, the Scarred Lands fantasy roleplaying setting returns in not just one, but two new forms! More than a decade after the setting was created, we want to share this classic setting with fans of both Pathfinder and 5th Edition by creating two different versions of the core book.

    We want to put this new version of the Scarred Lands into the hands of the thousands of players of the original 3e OGL version, but we also want to introduce new players to this large and detailed world. We hope to accomplish this directly to those backing our project and also by funding an offset press print run of the two versions of the book to sell to game stores.

              I have a worry regarding the 5th edition version of Scarred Lands products, and I'm not sure where to express it to give it best chances of getting noticed by those it would matter if noticed it:

              The DCs for skill checks (well, 5th edition calls them ability checks, but that is besides the point) shown in the preview image that shows a sample of the 5th edition layout of Gauntlet of Spiragos aren't correct unless the intent is to have 5th edition characters have less of a chance of succeeding at them than Pathfinder characters would have (since the DCs are the same in both version samples, even though 5th edition characters add almost universally lower modifiers to die rolls).

              Hopefully this is just a product of grabbing a sample that happens not to be finished, not an indication that the subtle but important differences between Pathfinder and 5th edition D&D are being overlooked.

              Not so noble anymore.
