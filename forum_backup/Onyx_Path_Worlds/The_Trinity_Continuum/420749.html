    Novas vs Psions, balance and setting

    02-26-2015, 06:43 PM
    Hi, sorry if this is an old idea, a bad one or just out of place.

    I stumbled upon an old discussion on rpg.net which talked a bit about how psions could possibly stand up to aberrants/novas. There was some talk about psions somehow making each other stronger in larger groups, but most people wanted to keep the groups small (player group sized) and felt huge amounts of psions making mincemeat of aberrants wasn't a good idea.

    This clicked in my mind with the concept of psionic/subquantum powers interferring with nova/quantum ones. The result was the idea that each psi order could have the potential to interfere with a particular aspect of aberrant powers - say, a biokinetic/norca being able to prevent a nova from shapeshifting, an esper/ISRA to prevent a nova from using extrasensory perception, et cetera. That way, a small group consisting of members of most or all psi orders would have the potential of "shutting down" and defeating any one aberrant. One on one, unless the aberrant is very one-dimensional and encounters just the wrong psion, the aberrant is highly likely to win. Also, this creates an impetus for player groups/small diverse groups from various orders, possibly under the aegis of Aeon Trinity to help smooth over inter-order hostilities.

    Just my five cents, be seeing y'all.

      Eh, balance kinda bugs me.

      In the original universe Novas and Aberrants are not the same thing. Aberrants, in Aeon, are twisted and broadly weaker decedents of what were once Novas. I could see some form of rules for a team of psions being able to resist Aberrant powers to a very limited degree, but I still want the Aberrant to have the edge against a team - it should be very hard to put down an Aberrant IMO.

                      I have always read 'the group effect' of Psions as meaning the doyen didn't want any individual (even the proxies) to singularly wield the true nature of their gift. The Prometheus Chamber is their 'soft-power' approach to the problem - disperse enough of their 'memes' into the awakened that the ideas join the human zeitgeist. So rather than single Psions having any effect on individual aberrants, what's happening is more like a greater diffusion of Psions (the sub-quantum particle) through the quantum medium and that's what makes high-taint aberrants in the 2120s less powerful than they expect (but still seriously powerful) to be. At least, in my own understanding. Tactical suppression as propaganda, mostly.
                      -shrug-
                      So system wise, i've rarely had reason to mess with how it was detailed. I never used Quantum/Psi suppression rules in any low number Psion encounter, when necessary to convey an atmosphere of Psionic suppression, I just improvised (ST fiat) weakened results (flight into clumsy high jumps, melee-range & weakened Q-Bolt). I shoe-horned another escaped Huang-Marr Biorg (EK with teleporting auxiliary) into a parallel campaign from Darkness Revealed, with that foe i went with some very loaded house rules for the Biorgs surgically enhanced 'suppression field' - basically rendering the Aberrant PCs powerless inside of a stated range - as long as the enemy was aware of them.

                      In my own personal experience, running many Nova characters through a 2120 setting, i've rarely really been bothered by any imbalance between the two as enemies. It's only the mixed inter-party balance that seemed problematic for me - using the Adventure! rules later on made it much more tenable - but lost the sheer volume of choice in character generation that the Aberrant style offered.
                      Enforcing the low-Quantum novas from Asia Ascendant are the best way of crossing the styles over. These poor novas, erupted in the psionically cleansed 2120s (increasingly so after process 418), can be very human seeming (at first, at least) and, without combat specialization, are prime candidates for single PC beat downs (i think judge dredd vs mostly incompetent young spandex heroes/villains could be a fun role to play) by combat specialized Psions, as are a number of low-level sub-aberrants who mostly don't actually have access to true quantum power. Sub-aberrants could be made fairly pitiful and a decent combat party could see a number of kills each turn fresh out of character generation. But verifiable, experienced/notorious, quantum-tainted space-aberrants should scale between extremely dangerous (Anders Nash, Warfire) to plot device wielding deus ex machina (Apollo Milliken, all Prime Threats).

                      But the idea of various Prometheus Chambers being used for specific suppressions is good, enough so that i'd like to use it at least - opposed to the general hand-wavery that noetic suppression of quantum taint as strictly background details. The one-for-one suppression rate leaves one side entirely powerless a good deal of the time and does get in the way of Aberrants being the scourge of known space - at least when using pure Aberrant-system nova generations (with increased taint levels in most cases). The few times an unsupported Psion or group encounter a decently powerful Aberrant - what could be a gripping escape/conflict becomes instead an anti-climax if a single full set of aptitude Psions with mid-level psi scores can significantly decrease the target's threat level.
                      Each aptitude having its own specialty in the area is a delicious team-making element, like PK's cancelling out Mega-physical with ease, while EK's sort out the Q-Bolts and shields with cheap pulses. Some writings suggest the now defunct Chibs were the crown jewel of tactical suppression - with the unofficial supplement India Underground indicating they had routinely made Aberrant captures before their purge. But even there the system lacked the flair that a well thought out, aptitude approach could bring to the topic - maybe with simple sub-effects to each listed power when applied for taint suppression value. Just be cautioned to not go overboard, any climactic Aberrant encounter that doesn't lead players to seriously questioning their resolve isn't getting your money's worth out of the setting, as i understand it.
                      The real-deal Aberrants of 2120 are boss-fights, often with spectacular set pieces and dramatic outcomes.
                      I'd assume that beyond a certain level of permanent Quantum (or maybe Taint?), the Aberrant ought be immune to any small group suppression - simply to maintain that aspect of the setting balance. But considering some of the known intentions of the Doyen in distributing the Prometheus Chambers, some fun little detail like this for lower level taint encounters could be useful in penetrating both the Doyen war on taint and Aeon's message of unity into the system. Simply cancelling out the Aberrants powers makes it frustrating to play as the suppressed Aberrant, but messing the effects up and making them unpredictable is less so. Finally, nothing should really be able to cancel out an Aberrants permanent physical alterations, except perhaps forced Dormancy, which i think comes from India Underground(?).

Novas are no less powerful when Psions are around and Psions do not have powers which are specifically able to overcome Nova's defences, but Psions in large groups are able to direct forces which can contest Novas and when this is combined with judicious use of advanced technology and tactics, it means that Novas can be defeated by sufficient numbers of Psions working in concert.
                                                      Pretty much my take in general - Tactical Psionic Supression of Taint is propaganda, a fairy tale for the un-dipped.
                                                      It shows up in popular media and recruitment pushes, but is never talked about seriously on a battlefield - maybe the Chibs made that different, but with India Underground being unofficial it can be hard to tell what the original intentions were. I made some concessions toward localized sub-quantum fields - but i felt that these were more a product of Doyen planning/manipulation than of conscious human agency. My suggestions to 'from' above are how i might imagine it works on Strike Force Psion - in some strikingly co-ordinated, visually 'enhanced' display for audiences of potential recruits.
                                                      India Underground makes mention of Chitra-Bhanu lead aberrant captures that require intense inter-order co-operation - but never expanded on the idea in clear rules (as far as i remember). So, i guess you could synthesize a system based on something like the 'Noetic Crystallizers' from that source (India Underground page 61 by my reader's count), because siphoning off quantum pool is a great way to finish - but the problem is how to start without a Chib as i understand.
                                                      I would probably make concessions to Quantum-Powers in non-freeform psi in any new edition of rules if it were up to me - because, damn, that one-on-one is just embarrassing - if it was between two PCs, one is coming away with lowered confidence (and possibly a bad experience in what should be fun) and we can predictably guess which one it will be. Not too much, mind, aberrants are the terrors of known space and besides some frustration a well rounded Quantum wielder should be able to adapt, but something to buy a turn or make it easier to target something else would be better than the nothing they currently have for most Psions. Maybe a seperate dueling system would work, like psionic combat in D&D psionics, but it would have to include a lot of other stuff, so i won't be personally advocating that idea any further lest that work fall on me.
                                                      -distancing self from dueling system-
                                                      When you say "advanced technology and tactics", i'm reading "orbital weapons platforms and 1000-to-1 ratio of manpower and resources or greater". Even century old, ten quantum war-dogs are going to run low on quantum eventually - you corner them at the right moment and it's just a question of how much firepower to bring to bear. Well, with some adjustment to soaking mechanics at least...

                                                      Even in my own experience no concessions further than allowing a turn of damage to be calculated as a single soakable total for included attacks (hand-waved using 2120 computer gunnery, potentially with some clairsentient-telepathic-networking if needed) are necessary for the heavier attack vehicles to out-damage even the most loaded 2008-style Nova build. But that concession has needed to come up a few times at the highest levels i ever let it get to (a few NPC Q6-8 veterans of the aberrant war specialized in combat who could sometimes soak any single weapon in the 2120 setting with no damage, literally game-breaking, but vital elements of a few set pieces). I also found myself switching the order of damage and damage add in the soaking process when the Aberrants were defending against vehicular scaled attacks, but even with all that in their favour individual vessels were still glass cannons serving as pawns and requiring a well-funded, -concerted and -planned effort against real threats. Mostly those NPCs were capable of taking on whole armies and living to fight another day in almost all cases - when the PCs started to beleivably challenge them, i knew it was time to pull the plug. The system wasn't written for cross-overs. But the stories were all there, lined up and ours for the taking - which kept things rolling long enough to get to that stage and i could have kept going if the framework was there. The scales were wasteful and the rules were broken, but the setting thrived under the inverted perspective and little effort was needed on my side to find space or motivations for 2120 aberrants - enough that i tried again later with more realistic expectations and better understanding of the complete setting. I'd even give it a third try, if the players wished.

                                                      But in one on one PHYSICAL combat with Psions? No. Even the high hopes i placed on a custom-Varg fighter-ace with the house-rules above were dashed out in a few scant rounds. A single psion against a party of (relatively) low-powered novas became a pet project, for a while then.
                                                      When eventually i went nuclear on the house-rules for Huang-Marr biorgs (free-form psi based Mega-Psi, no aptitude restrictions, fully replenished doubled psi pool each turn, psionic version of Mega-Wit's quickness, and so on...) the 2120 'Novas' finally had to think out of the box and work together though, enough that i was already planning for another couple of escaped biorgs breaking out of canon to show up later on - i flirted with one being skilled in QK - which might have been the measure to really put it over the edge into nightmare material for Novas. But that was fighting fire with fire and even i admit it was extreme and unhealthy for the system as written - but made somehow necessary by the ease the PCs dispatched the canon threats to aberrant invaders with. If i had lined up all the Novastorm IACruisers in a single place at once, a fresh <100xp team might have been able to take them all out in a single well-planned attack of their own - which just begged for some re-balancing. But mostly, i just made sure no-one was ever dumb enough to make such a rendezvous publicly available intel - same for the Leviathans, but even more so.
                                                      It should probably be noted that this was in a 2120s Tainted Nova campaign, where these conflicts took place much more regularly than in any well-paced Trinity game. Further, the Nova PCs who were capable of this level of Quantum are explicitly rare - the elder aberrants who smuggled them off earth and outwards gathered less than seven in an unstated number of years. Mostly the newer 'Novas' of 2120 are low-quantum, stooges or thugs for the 4th legion and other extremists to pick on for media events, in my head-canon.

                                                      Look at me, still going, and we haven't even touched on the more subtle Quantum effects either. That's a much more nasty can of worms, leaving you wondering how many humans still actually possess free-will in 2120 between the Doyen, Telepaths and Q-powered remnants. I touched on it when i mentioned how i ran the suppression field around the Qin embassy - but that was a pretty specialized scene where the characters knew they were probably getting in too deep. I was quietly doing everything i could to sabotage their (understandably) growing confidence over psion/human resistance, playing to their expectations was predictable but healthy for the tipping balance. Which is why i hope to find some useful, but relatively unobstusive game-mechanic in this thread.
                                                      -shrug-
                                                      If there was a framework for the Onyx Path edition we could be using that instead - is there somewhere i can get a light version of the system for discussion or be there a pay wall to negotiate?
