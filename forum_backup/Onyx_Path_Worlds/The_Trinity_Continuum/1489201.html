      Max just returned to the 'present' timeline of 1923, after jumping 200 years in the future - to year circa 2123, i.e. Aeon Era. He is Daredevil, instinctively using his few Gifts. For now, he is just more or less semi-famous explorer and adventurer.

      How do we made it - details below in topic posts...

      Let's make it by the Adventure! ( and others ) books

      Step One: Concept ✅
      Choose your character’s name, background, and Aspirations.

      This can we pass, as we know who Max Mercer is. 😎


      Step Two: Paths ✅
      Choose your character’s Origin, Role, and Society Paths.Gain Skill dots and Edge dots from each Path.

      Here we need to use some information’s and options from Aether Kickstarter preview, to determine past of the Max. From Aether book we know that Max parents, Richard and Anne Mercers, were field researchers for Esoteric Order of Aeons, ‘secret’ society of Victorian scientific investigators into wonders and horrors of Aether energy ( what later be known as Flux or mix of Telluric Energies, evidence are not conclusive ). This implies that Origin for Max is Field Researcher from Adventure! – he is literal poster boy of this particular Origin. I think about Role for him as Show Off – he seems to this kind of guy, from all the lore I read about him to this day. For now he is part of Esoteric Order of Aeons that he will reform in to ‘modern’ Aeon Society, aimed more on utopian and humanitarian work in coming years.

      Max Paths

      Origin - Field Researcher ( from Adventure! book )
      Example Connections: Academics, Foreign Friends, Grad Students, Librarians, Travel Guides
      Skills: Culture, Enigmas, Humanities or Science, Survival
      Edges: Direction Sense, Fame, Hardy, Library, Patron, Polyglot, Speed Reading
      Daredevil Gift Keywords: Attribute (Intellect), Skill (Enigmas, Humanities)


      Role - Show Off ( from Adventure! book )
      Example Connections: Agents, Performers, Reporters, Star-Struck Fans
      Skills: Command, Persuasion, and one Skill that reflects your character’s favorite method of
      showing off — a trick shot artist could choose Aim, for example, while a barnstorming aviator could choose Pilot. / Close Combat? Max in fiction is known to 'hit hard the classical enemies of pulp'./
      Edges: Adrenaline Spike, Artistic Talent, Dashing Presence, Fame, Fast Draw, Hair Trigger Reflexes, Outsized Personality, Striking
      Daredevil Gift Keywords: Attribute (Presence), Skill (Persuasion, Variable Skill)


      Society - Esoteric Order of Aeons ( from Aether book )
      Example Connections: Adventurers’ Maps, Inventors, Newspaper Office, Scientists
      Skills: Culture, Enigmas, Persuasion, Technology
      Edges: Adrenaline Spike, Aura of Innocence, Hair Trigger Reflexes, Inventive, Keen Sense, Library,
      Polyglot, Speed Reading, Voice of Reason, Work the Room
      Daredevils('Squires') Gift Keywords: Attribute (Cunning), Skill (Enigmas, Persuasion)


      Max Skills and Edges ( from Paths )
      Skills
      Field Researcher – Culture 1, Enigmas 1, Science 1
      Show Off – Command 1, Persuasion 1, Survival 1
      Esoteric Order of Aeons – Culture 1, Enigmas 1, Technology 1

      Max Paths Skills Sum-up – Command 1, Culture 2, Enigmas 2, Persuasion 1, Survival 1, Science 1, Technology 1

      Edges
      Field Researcher – Direction Sense, Fame, Hardy, Library, Patron, Polyglot, Speed Reading
      Show Off – Adrenaline Spike, Artistic Talent, Dashing Presence, Fame, Fast Draw, Hair Trigger Reflexes, Outsized Personality, Striking
      Esoteric Order of Aeons – Adrenaline Spike, Aura of Innocence, Hair Trigger Reflexes, Inventive, Keen Sense, Library, Polyglot, Speed Reading, Voice of Reason, Work the Room

      Max Paths Edges Sum-up – Adrenaline Spike 2, Direction Sense 1, Fame 1, Polyglot 1 ( Adventure! or Aether ) Outsized Personality 1 ( Adventure! )



      Step Three: Skills, Skill Tricks, and Specialties ✅
      Assign six additional Skill dots, and pick Specialties for any Skills with a high enough rating, and gain one Skill Trick in a Skill rated three or higher.

      Extra Skill dots - Athletics 1, Close Combat 1, Humanities 2, Pilot 1, Science 1

      Max starting Skills final spread – Athletics 1, Close Combat 1, Command 1, Culture 2, Enigmas 2, Humanities 2, Persuasion 1, Pilot 1, Survival 1, Science 2, Technology 1

      Step Four: Attributes ✅
      Assign your Attribute dots and select your character’s favored approach.

      First we choose priority on Mental, Physical and Social Arenas. Describing him as "multi-millionaire philanthropist - businessman, entrepreneur, explorer, and physicist" seems to point him as Social first, Physical second and Mental third character. It means Social 6 dots, Physical 4 and Mental 2 – at least on start. Starting with 1 dot in each Attribute, I would see dots first spread as this:

      Presence 3 Manipulation 3 Composure 3
      Might 2 Dexterity 2 Stamina 3
      Intellect 2 Cunning 1 Resolve 2

      Bombastic and outgoing Max seems to me he should be at Forceful Approach. This means….

      Final Max starting Attributes spread:
      Presence 4 Manipulation 3 Composure 3
      Might 3 Dexterity 2 Stamina 3
      Intellect 3 Cunning 1 Resolve 2

      Step Five: Moment of Inspiration ✅
      Decide how the moment of Inspiration occurred, and whether the character became a daredevil, stalwart, or mesmerist.

      Hammersmith Incident in 1922 is Moment of Inspiration for Max, setting him as (super)Daredevil. It works perfectly with Exposure To Flux option from corebook, so we chose +1 dot to any Physical Attribute. Max felt to me more ‘slick’ than mighty character – so we go with +1 for Dexterity.

      Originally posted by Trinity Core 2E Rulebook
      EXPOSURE TO FLUX
      The character was exposed to a high level of flux. The character may have been born in the middle of a flux storm, his infant body absorbing the swirling energies in the air. The character might be exposed to flux later in life during a presentation of some advanced technology, or during some kind of archeological survey.
      Associated Attribute: Any Physical
      Max Attributes after Moment of Inspiration:
      Presence 4 Manipulation 3 Composure 3
      Might 3 Dexterity 3 Stamina 3
      Intellect 3 Cunning 1 Resolve 2

      Step Six: Add Advantages
      Assign three points between the Facets. Calculate your Inspiration pool. Gain four Gifts.

      Step Seven: Final Touches
      Calculate Health and Defense ratings, as detailed on p.38 of the Trinity Continuum Core. Assign one bonus dot to any Attribute, and spend four dots of Edges


      Max Attributes after Bonus Dot ( got Cunning +1 )
      Presence 4 Manipulation 3 Composure 3
      Might 3 Dexterity 3 Stamina 3
      Intellect 3 Cunning 2 Resolve 2

      Max secondary stats
      Health – Got +1 Injured level from Stamina 3
      Defense 1 ( Defense 2 with Static Defence House Rule )


      Step Eight: Advancement to Veteran (50XP)✅

      Here are my 50XP investments options for Max:
      • 10 XP - Skills: Athletics +2 ( to 3 dots )
      • 10 XP - Skills: Science +2 ( to 4 dots ) – Literal time travel 200 years in future makes you great in Physics. 😎
      • 10 XP - Skills: Close Combat +2 ( to 3 dots ) – Max is shown on covers and illustration with punching bad guys, a lot. 😉
      • 3 XP - Edge: Wealth +1 ( to 5 dots ) – Time travel let’s you invest in better future profits 😎
      • 2 XP - Edge: Hardy 1 dot – Max is world known explorer, even in jungle on Adventure! book cover
      • 2 XP - Edge: Hair Trigger Reflexes 1 dot
      • 2 XP - Edge: Striking 2 dot
      • 3 XP - Edge: Useful Esoterica 1 dots – He travels A LOT.

          Max Mercer is described in Adventure! as a "multi-millionaire philanthropist", so he needs Wealth 5, but wouldn't count as Loaded, since in the '20s that'd mean he's worth at least $100 million.

          He's also described as a, "businessman, entrepreneur, explorer, and physicist." As an explorer, he should have at least one dot in Survival and possibly Pilot. As a physicist, he should have at least one dot in Science.

          It goes on to state he, "earned a double major in chemistry and philosophy from Harvard, but earned his Master’s degree in sociology." His knowledge of chemistry in addition to physics should boost Science to two or three dots. His degrees in philosophy and sociology would be worth multiple dots in Humanities.

          The manuscript also says, "Max Mercer, wunderkind physicist Michael Donighal, and Hammersmith’s laboratory assistant, Sara Kaur, were exposed to the most intense and direct dosages of telluric energy released in the explosion. This direct exposure at the source of the telluric engine unlocked incredible potential in these three, making them the most powerful and unusual Inspired this age would know. A daredevil, stalwart, and mesmerist, respectively, the abilities of this trio represent the theoretical pinnacle of what ispossible through each of these paths of Inspiration."

          This tells us he isn't a starting character. He likely came out of the Hammersmith event with more power than any other daredevil would achieve over the course of their adventuring careers.           He likely started with a very high Inspiration score. I'm pretty sure I also saw an author's post on the forum that the big three (Mercer, Donighal, Kaur) should all be considered effectively unaging, possessed of bonus health levels, and able to heal quickly between scenes to represent how hard it is to actually kill them.
