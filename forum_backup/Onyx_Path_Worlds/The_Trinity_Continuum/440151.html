      Me first, Pellaura was settled in the 1920's Doctor Aubade (a powerful Stalwart) created the electro-etheric bridge. Between 1930 and 1940 (when Doctor Aubade was murdered) about 25,000 people crossed over to the planet Pellaura (the clearest air). The population has grown steadily since then. Stalwarts, Daredevils, and Messerists, are common throughout the population of around 1.5 million people. Doctor Aubade had founded a functional university on the planet, so most of the knowledge Aubade got across to Pellaura has been preserved and/or built upon.

      Due to the small population the ecconomy isn't as strong as on the Earth nor has technology advanced as far. However Pellaura is a stable democracy.

      If discovered by Psions or Sane Novas Pellaura should be friendly and open. High taint Novas will be rejected and fought.

          Amiranth
          In 1949 the earth's scientific community did not yet seriously consider aiming their radio receivers upwards to listen for alien signals, but when a raid on the Rational Expermentation Group turned up a woman who claimed to have come from another world, Aeon was forced to reconsider the consensus. 'Princess Kiyali' as she called herself appeared little different than any other human, she could eat our food, breathe our air and had no unusual anatomical features apart from slightly pointed ears, orange colored eyes and a few ridges across her shoulders and back which could easily have been simulated with makeup - she apparently had no problem understanding english either. Nobody at the time ever really questioned this.
          Despite contributing to a few Aeon investigations, the princess' ultimate goals were always to return to her homeworld 'Amiranth' and after one of Dixon and Wexler's ill-fated attempts at reconcilliation the Machinatrix' discovery of that planet and subsequent plans to conquer it were revealed. The R.E.G. had used a complex meme-seeking dimensional transit system to reach the planet - where they had forcefully kidnapped the princess and absconded with her. When the reconcilliation (inevitably) broke down, Dixon proposed a counter-attack on the R.E.G. plot - a plan that was immediately siezed upon by a maturing 'Danger Ace'.
          In her time on earth, Kiyali and Ace had begun a fledgeling romance and the passionate airborne daredevil had vowed to work towards getting her home. So when the attack on the R.E.G.s dimensional transit facility saw the precious target device sabotaged and left for scrap by Wexler in 1950, Ace and Dixon began working on alternate methods. By 1952, the first (and perhaps only) Aeon 'Starjammer' vessel launched from facilities that would eventually be sold to the US governement for their own space-explorations a decade later. Despite the events being covered in depth in the Adventure! periodicals and seperately in Sarah Gettel's own publications - the event has generally been dismissed as a hoax or pure fiction by future generations - an impression only entrenched by the closing of ranks and secrecy that the society started embracing in later decades.
          The '52 Starjammer voyage saw an initial crew of fifty set off into the voids, using the (still) obscenely advanced 'Dixon-Drives' the Starjammer took less than a year to reach the oceanic world of Amiranth where they were welcomed as heroes when Princess Kiyali emerged from it's steaming hull to thunderous alien cheers. Amiranth had been subject to a long series of world wars over the disappearance of the 'Julco' Nations princess, only exacerbated by the R.E.G.s manipulations in the interim and the adventure did not end there for Kiyali and Danger Ace.
          ...
          In the early sixties, the elderly professor Dixon announced to the society that he had started to receive incoming signals from the Starjammer and that it's return was imminent. In the face of the cold-war and growing zietgiest around alien encounters, the society made arrangements for the landing in complete secrecy - seeing the vessel arrive home on an equatorial island nation in '64. There had been extensive modifications to the initial design and was now only piloted by 'Queen' Kiyali and 'King' Ace of Julco, Amiranth. They had brought diplomats and scientists interested in opening channels to earth, but due to the world atmousphere and declining credibility of the society - this first contact has gone virtually unnoticed by history.
          Leaving a number of Amiranthians on earth, the Starjammer then contiued back to Amiranth with a load of earthlings - who had begun settling Amiranth's Julco nation (among others, in lesser numbers). Amiranthian age-retardation technology had arrested Danger Ace in his fifties, and in the several trips they made back and forth between 1964 and 1969 he was never seen to age further. By the end of this period it was considered simply too dangerous for the Starjammer to be detected and the process entered a long period of hibernation through the remaining cold-war era.
          When the cold-war had ended, the society had changed and there was no attempt to communicate the all-clear to Amiranth. This only added to the confusion in 2039 when an interstellar invasion by an Amiranthian enemy (the Kulmorians) was defeated by world Novas, but dismissed as a Sphinxes trick when the species inside the vessels were found to be almost genetically identical to humans. Leaving the event lost in the strange narrative of the era. The Kulmorians soon thereafter surrendered to Amiranth, mistakenly fearing the emergence of Amiranthian Novas - much to the confusion of Amiranth, isolated from earth at that stage.
          Indeed, it wasn't until the rejuvination of Aeon after the Aberrant war that Amiranth was finally contacted again. Now a carefully guarded secret, the King and Queen of Julco began making quiet trips back to confer with their surviving allies - even taking a ponderously selected few back with them. But, the arguement of how to introduce an alien (but suspiciously earthlike) species to the world was cut short when first contact with the Qin occured in 2107. The orders had made such a spectacle of it all that the carefully seeded ideas of any previous contact were lost in the publicity and attention now reserved for the beautific psion discovery.
          ...
          Amiranth itself is a mostly oceanic world, with archipelagos and islands no bigger than our own Japan seperated by oceans subject to much less dramatic tides than earth due to Amiranth's only moon being a captured asteroid no bigger than Pallas. The life on Amiranth is mostly amphibious bird-like creatures that have very little genetically in common with the sentient species of the world and don't even have the right nutrients for eating. But in the (mostly) benevolent dictatorships of Amiranth, earthlike animals (but with the subtle ridges, pointed ears and variant eye colors mentioned above) are raised alongside by their alien(?) masters.
          Amiranth has been aware of earth for nearly a century, they are also aware of a few other alien worlds in their local area with similar curiousities (like the Kulmorians). They have never actually encounted Novas or Aberrants and despite being warned by earth of the dangers - may find themselves taken in by expelled Quantum visitors. Amiranth is a hopeful world, if a bit naive and unquestioning. Whether it is ever actually revealed to the general public is a question still asked by those in the society with sufficient clearance to go poking through the old periodicals, but for the most part the debate has been viewed as academic and been further relegated into future discussion.
          The potential study of Noetics in regard to Amiranth are particularly interesting, it is possible the whole world (and its earthlike neighbours) was at least partially created by the Hammersmith event (or an even earlier telluric event). The specifics of this of might help shed light on some of the still unknown elements of those events. Why did Amiranth stick around when many inspired locations on earth disappeared? Did the history of the Amiranthians occur or was it retroactively emplaced by the event? How far through space and time do these events actually reach?

          Rāstē kā ṭhaharāva
          (Hindi for Stopover, Pronounced Ras-tee Kah Teh-ha-rav)
          Once mere teachers now expelled from earth and cruelly admonished by the future Nihonjin remnants, the subcontinental collection of fleeing Novas called the Avaśēṣa (Hindi translation of 'Remnants', Pronounced Av-shees) were desperate when they used a group Warping effort to relocate their entire local Rashoud facility (along with no small amount of terrain), at first into Antarctica, where they made the facility durable enough to carry passengers in vacuum before departing their home-world under the increasingly resigned threats of the Chinese Ultimatuum. Despite many knowing better, the Avaśēṣa allowed a number of human family members and allies to accompany them on this voyage.
          The warper novas who carried the facility over the light-years toward Gliese 581 paid dearly for the desperate feat, taking on taint in terrible amounts to complete the task by 2063. Once there, the Avaśēṣa were not surprised to find Novas already present in the cluster frantically searching for a new home. Gliese 581 had been a wash, the planets of that system were gas giants alongside a single super-Venus so toxic even Novas could not seriously contemplate settling it, at least in the short term. But once the Avaśēṣa made contact with the surveyors already present in the system, they discovered that none of the known Gliese exoplanets were immediately habitable, except for a small arctic zone on an inner orbiting super-Earth around the '832' star.
          Due to the rapidly deteriorating form of the Rashoud Facility, one of the Avaśēṣa warpers (Navdeep Khatters Attwal) accompanied a surveyor to 832's harsh world to negotiate placing the facility there, at least temporarily. Navdeep returned with stories of a world ruled by radically tainted and unpredictable space-adapted Novas who were attempting to create lava-tunnel facilities with destructive powers - to be air-locked at some unspecified future date. The gravity was intense and the atmosphere was a thick stew of methane, but due to the gravity had remained intact despite the planet's proximity to its dim dwarf star.
          The novas there had been suspicious and even under much accumulated taint Navdeep counselled the Avaśēṣa to avoid making arrangements with them for risk of betrayal and war. So instead, the travellers chose to place their progressively crumbling infrastructure onto a frigid ice moon of 581b. It was in 2064 when the cold, dark world of Rāstē kā ṭhaharāva (Stopover) was baptised by its Nova colonists. The facility immediately began extracting oxygen from the water ice plain where it had taken root - the intention being to make repairs, resupply and begin the journey to Cygni.
          In 2081, Navdeep Attwal defected to the 832 Novas for implacable reasons - leaving the facility stranded without the raw power to Warp its bulk. The remaining 38 Novas and five dozen or so baseline family members did what they could stave off depression and madness in their alien isolation, but when the second generation of occupants were born malformed and diseased from nutritional deficiencies and radiation damage - the facility began to rot from within.
          Hopeless and desperate, the lonely colonists of Rāstē kā ṭhaharāva began sending pleas for help back to their home world in 2106 via laser signals. The Novas who facilitated the task agreed to stay upon their dark, frozen rock if only something could be done to save the humans they had unknowingly sentenced to lives of madness and disease. The first of these transmissions begin arriving at the tail end of 2121.
          Rāstē kā ṭhaharāva is an unforgivingly harsh environment - only made survivable through liberal use of Quantum innovations and necessarily advanced technical solutions. All food and liquid coming from the dark moon has been synthesized out of nutrients either drawn from the scant elements present or entirely created by Novas - while this worked for a while, the long term deficiencies are now turning the 2nd generation humans amongst them into twisted, malnourished shadows.
          Rāstē kā ṭhaharāva has lunar-level gravity and no atmosphere to speak of, due to the distance from the dwarf 581, the days are little different from the nights - both illusions anyway, due to the moon being tidally locked to 581b. There is no economy of government to speak of and technology is thought of in terms of necessity, not development. If earth forces were to respond to the distress signals from Rāstē kā ṭhaharāva, the chances of that effort being intercepted by the mad aberrants of Gliese 832 would be high and the subsequent risks; severe.

          Delta Pavonis C
          (Stop me if you've heard this one before.)
          Prior to the Esperanza disaster, Clear surveys had been struggling to lock onto a curious sensation sometimes referred to as the local void. During the time since the Upeo wa Macho's disappearance, ISRA deep scans were finally able to properly trace the void back to its source, the aging local star, Delta Pavonis. At a mere 20 light years away, the scans were confusingly suggesting that a habitable planet was to be found around this star - but the strange bluriness of the void was too difficult to pierce any further.
          When the teleporters began returning during the war against the Chromatics, some of the old far-jumpers began reconnecting with trusted clairsentient scanners and the meme was passed. By the conclusion of the Chrome-Prime invasion, plans for a survey-vessel to visit Delta Pavonis were already in the works and initial observations began at the beginning of 2122. At first the third planet from the star bore remarkable similarities to Earth, but lacking any visible water.
          Indeed, as time passed it became clear that the unlucky world had been subject to some kind of extreme stellar event and scoured of anything but razor fine dust. Despite this sad turn, the planet had comfortable gravity and a promising balance of chemicals in its trace atmosphere. Colonization plans began almost immediately with the first materials being teleported on-world before 2123. The curiosity of the clairsentient void continued to plague Noeticists and clears alike, why had more distant worlds been easier to locate?
          In the early years of the 2130s, xeno-ruins begin turning up on the clear-thwarting world, a sentient species had occupied the world up until a few million years ago. All evidence pointed to the life on the planet being incinerated by the intense event that left the world sterilized - but the void effect remained in place. By the 30s however, knowledge of the Doyen was becoming more widespread and the void was right up their alley.
          What had happened on the dry desolate world that turned it from a haven of sentience into the dustbowl it was today and why were the Doyen going to such measures to keep it from humanity?
          P.S. Yeah, Delta Pavonis C is a direct rip-off of Resurgam from Alastair Reynolds brilliant Revelation Space series. But if the Zepps are still out there (nothing official, but i have heard it suggested) then Trinity is seriously lacking in the xeno-archaeology department. Drakes Equation and the Fermi Paradox desperately require addressing! I will not apologize!

          Pacis Aurae
          Mostly driven insane under the burden of Taint and history, the many haralded names of the Aberrant era were lost to time and tragedy in the intervening years. But of the few names still mentioned alongside the likes of Divis Mal, mostly due to surviving promotional tracts and reports from the time deeply secured in the bowels of Project Rewrite is that of Shelby 'Caestus Pax' Eisenfaust.
          Eisenfaust had been an administrator and politician of the era, gifted with one of the most significant initial power levels of the early era, which he had put into the toolbox of civil services and PR he so masterfully employed. While details on the change aren't reliably documented (yet), the image-aware aberrant became commonly known as Malleus Pax to the populace after some sort of 'accidental' atrocity he performed in St. Petersburg sometime before the war sometimes referred to as the 'silent peace'.
          While little physical evidence of this atrocity remain today with St. Petersburg bearing no tell-tale reminders beyond a few bronze plaques about the Kalininsky district, Eisenfaust found it difficult to rebuild credibility to following the silent peace event. With the general loss of confidence in Eisenfaust and his celebrated team of international troubleshooters, it was not until the event of 2049 and subsequent aberrant war when he and his fellows renewed themselves in a series of high profile battles.
          In this period the palaces of aberrant creation developed in Ethiopia were subject to horrendous strikes and an off-world warping effort later mimicked by the Nihonjin remnants had begun. An innocuous staging grounds on Mars' Mare Tyrrhenum was produced, innovated to boost warping capabilities closer to the distances capable by psionic 'attunement' based ranging. The scientists of Addis Abbaba had made tentative surveys of a world they called 'Tomorrow' circling the aging 24 G. Virginis star.
          ...
          Before the ultimatuum was issued, Eisenfaust had become a tainted shadow of his earlier self, but never stopped looking for the public's approval, either due to some calculated scheme or a perfectly human need for recognition. Regardless of his increasingly alien motivations, Eisenfaust was instrumental in removing a number of entrenched elements during the ultimatuum including the equally noteworthy church of Michael and a number of middle-eastern war-mongers.
          As the Chinese doomsday clock approached midnight, Eisenfaust used his hard-won credibility to encourage aberrants and human sympathizers to accompany them on their journey to the discovered world. An invitation that saw a response unimaginable by the people of 2120 - hundreds of thousands, perhaps millions pleaded to be taken with the popular leader to his new home. With his characteristically inhuman efficiency, Eisenfaust processed and transported many of the accepted petitioners in less than a week and finally departed the home system with a highly restricted final address that was echoed in the words of Apollo Milliken over half a century later.
          The final address gave stellar co-ordinates and radio frequencies upon which the exiles could be contacted, should their assistance ever be required. But due to the tight regulations over the broadcast and political dangers, this message has never been viewed unedited by more than two dozen individuals since, mostly high ranking Aeon officials. Any public revelation of Malleus Pax's final address in the 2120s could be as damaging as the hauntingly familiar words from Eden carried by the Upeo wa Macho upon their return.
          ...
          Tomorrow itself was not to last however, a gravitic disruption caused by the systems outer cold-Jupiter saw an inward rain of comets in the early 2070s leaving Tomorrow deeply at risk of catastrophic impact. The heavily populated, new world was faced with a sudden need to relocate amidst this disturbance and the increasingly unpredictable fluctuations in the sub-giant 24 G. Virginis. Tackling individual threats as they occured, the high population of aberrants were able to buy the colony time to formulate a bold plan to relocate the planet itself.
          The 2081 exodus from Virginis was an incredible feat, salvaged from certain disaster when miscalculations lead to Tomorrow being fractured in the process. Due to the resources available to the aberrant colonists, the project still managed to relocate the planet into the dual system of Porrima, just over ten light years distant. But by then even more drastic measures were required.
          The 2080s were a time of wonderous super-science and physics-bending solutions for the fatally wounded world of Tomorrow - the sheer scale of which could hardly be comprehended even by many of the super-intellects contributing to the solution. Eventually taking the form of suspending the fragments of the world in the breathable atmousphere of a petite oxygen rich gas giant, eventually named Pacis Aurae by the miraculously survived occupants, in honour of the great deeds performed by Malleus Pax in the effort.
          The effort, however had been the final straw to break the back of Eisenfaust, plunging him deep into tainted madness and mutation. Only through the efforts of aberrant medicine and technology were the scientists of Pacis Aurae able to save what little they could of their leader. The mighty node was painstakingly extracted from the vestigal remains of Malleus Pax, reportedly under his own insistence and preserved in complex innovations allowing the mighty tainted power stabilizing the 'islands' of Pacis Aurae at the breathable altitudes to be maintained indefinitely.
          ...
          In 2120, the floating landmasses of Pacis Aurae can vary greatly, some larger islands have been engineered for earth-like scenery and ecologies and can seem utopian compared to other extra-solar colonies. Other smaller 'islands' have been subject to full conversion into mighty, moon sized habitats, not unlike the Nihonjin arcology. All landmasses have been cautiously shielded from the radiation and magnetic field of their host and can even be drawn together for short times agreed to by the mostly machine-emulated Eisenfaust. The population of Pacis Aurae has burgeoned up to 2.5 million in the years since, with a number of new eruptions even occurring in the tainted medium.
          Careful attempts to monitor the deteriorating 24 G. Virginis for potential communications from earth have continued, but any travellers physically visiting that system will be able to use astrophysical simulations to find asteroid traces of Tomorrow in the orbit it should be in. Although the chaotic storm of comets and disrupted orbits of the three remaining planets tell of a system collapsing toward its expanding star in an inevitable Newtonian catastrophe.
          Visitors to Porrima however, will find a cautious welcome by the inhabitants of Pacis Aurae. Even extended to the highly tainted, out of respect for the deeds of Shelby Eisenfaust. Any anti-aberrant bigotry could be seen as offensive and even aggressive to the inhabitants, who have found reliable medical processes of softening the decay into degenerate aberrancy. Any psion visiting the system will likely start receiving visions from the Doyen on how to best sabotage and destroy the world for its continued supply of heavy taint into the galaxy.

              Bolthole-> A powerful Nova calling himself The Other Guy (Short for THE OTHER RED-HEADED, GREEN-EYED, IRISH AMERICAN, GAY GUY, WITH A BEEF!) set up extrasolar colonies that could be gotten to by Warp portals. The Other Guy seems to have set up several different worlds, but no one seems to know how many there were. All of these worlds started out being called BOLTHOLE, and took in refugees. All of these worlds had been made "Earthlike" by The Other Guy. All of these worlds have a sea to land ratio higher than Earth's(Earth 3 parts water to one part land, all of the Boltholes at least 4 parts water to one part land) but more arable land over all. Deserts are rare, continents are small, the Temperate Zones generally dominate the land mass, and the planetary Oceans have good circulation.


              Each of the known BOLTHOLES are well populated, the population tell of escaping the "Mad Novas" in the mid 21st century and coming to this place of safety. Their were always suppies prepared and some useful infrastructure. Each world had a library/university set up to preserve knowledge and train the refugees in needed skills. Novas have since appeared in all of these populations, they are generally low-taint, low-powered, and low Quantum. There are some second generation Novas on each world. There are known to be Chinese, Anglo-phone mainly American, African, Arab, Indian/South Asian, Indonesian, Latin American, and European, "Boltholes" at least. There is a rumored "Bolthole Central" where The Other Guy took low taint Novas and those tainted Novas he found honorable.


              I'd have the PCs find one of these worlds, start looking for the others, and hear rumors that "The Other Guy" is still alive, somewhere out there checking up on his refugee refuges, and at least as old as Divis Mal. An ancient Nova smart enough to keep hidden throughout the Nova years and powerful enough to reshape worlds to his whims. The few out of control Novas on these Bolthole worlds were taken away by The Other Guy. Each of these Novas visted their homes and family later on. Each claimed that The Other Guy delt with their Taint and "healed them" however The Other Guy aways got the authorities to let him take the troubled Nova away to garrentee everybody's safety. Most of these "Troubled Novas" wrote to their families and sent gifts for years after they were taken away.


              Bonus: Each world has vast reserves of information. Any and all information that existed into the middle of the 21st century was brought to Universities on these worlds. The locals know what they have and will fight to preserve their greatest treasure. Meanwhile, the Aeon Trinity needs these Libraries purged!
