                                            04-16-2016, 07:21 PM
                                            I could see a Quantum “boomlet” in the mid-50s, a side effect of Dr. Primorius' defeat at the end of WWII and a preview of sorts of the later Aberrant era. To the extent that they draw inspiration from superhero comics, the heroes of this era would draw their inspiration from the likes of X-Men and Doom Patrol: they're outsiders with problematic powers in a society that hates and fears them — sometimes rightfully so. More generally, it goes with the 50s notion that atomic mutations are weird and dangerous. These “Atomic Mutants” exist at a stage between Adventure!'s Stalwarts (who, in Aberrant terms, are “Stealth Novas”) and Aberrant's Novas, with their defining characteristic being that their Quantum Powers tend to be low-level and Taint-ridden.

                                            In the bigger picture, Primorius learns some valuable lessons here that he implements when he triggers the Aberrant era. Namely, the trigger for the Atomic era is effectively a “dirty nuke” that results in excessive Taint; this lets him build a “clean nuke” to trigger the Aberrant era which, while not eliminating Taint, makes it something much more manageable.

                                            This is also the first era where alien incursions are a thing, with the Roswell incident and alien infiltrators who either replace or possess humans. I'm not up on my Æon lore; but I don't think this would be any of those aliens — which would raise the question of what happened to them?

                                            My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
