    New Alliance: Libra

    08-27-2022, 03:46 PM
    Hello everyone,

    One thing that I missed from the trinity continuum is more organizations that fall in the morally gray area, with that in mind I created Libra. I'm posting it here to see what you think about it and what do you think needs changing. Feel free to use it. Thanks in advance.
    Libra
    “A world in balance is a safe world”



    History

    The end of the Cold War shook the world as the old order crumbled. Some proudly proclaimed that this would be the end of history and that the world would walk towards a new order based on democracy and capitalism, but others knew that it wouldn’t be so simple. Those with a more “realistic” perspective understood that the new era would bring a lot of chaos and uncertainty till it reached a new power balance.
    So it was that in 1990, three special individuals gathered in a café in Paris to discuss the future. Their names were Joana Willhem, a CIA operative expert in diplomacy and international politics; Philip Doring, an analyst and computer wizard of the MI6; and last Pavel Volkov, an ex-KGB field agent with contacts in almost every criminal organization in the world.
    These three individuals lived through some of the worst moments of the Cold War. The first time they met, after a fortuitous and tense encounter in an operation gone wrong, they decided to join forces to ensure that nuclear annihilation didn’t become a reality. The operation cost them dearly, and they had to cross lines they thought they never would, but when the other option is world annihilation, no price is too high, and so they made an oath to do whatever it took to make sure there would always be a tomorrow.
    In the following years and till the end of the Cold War, they created an informal network with like-minded individuals with the objective of making sure that the Cold War would never go hot. Few know just how many close calls were prevented thanks to the efforts of these anonymous agents. The network never had an official name, but Joana liked to call it Libra on account of them keeping balance between world powers.
    Such a global conspiracy, no matter how informal and well-intentioned, would have never worked if not for one thing: Joana, Philip and Pavel were talents. Joana was a master understanding relationships between organizations, Philip was the same but with information, and Pavel’s strong point was people. Together, they knew just how, when and where to act to get the best results, and who would be a good fit for their network.
    At their meeting in Paris, they agreed that although the Cold War had ended, their objective did not. Their experience and insight warned them about the rise of the information age, the increasing power of corporations, and the many consequences that the USA and USSR created due to their machinations. More political instability was on the way, and many of the greatest dangers of the future wouldn’t come wrapped in flags, the enemy could come from anywhere.
    It was then that they used their network to create three independent organizations that nevertheless would work together in the shadows: Joanna founded Libra’s relations incorporated, a law firm specialized in international law, Philip founded Aegis Soft, a cybersecurity firm, and Pavel founded the Volkov society, a criminal organization. They also created a think tank toguether called the I.W.P.S (Institute fro world peace and stability) a foundation and think tank to atrackt like minded individuals with influence and power. Working together, they’ve raised to the top of their respective fields, with none the wiser as to the secret of their collaboration, except for their respective inner circle and most trusted agents.

    Recruitment

    It’s important to understand that Libra has basically two types of agents, those who work for the three organizations and don’t know about Libra, and a select group of freelancers that know and carry the most important missions. Regardless, Libra as a whole values quality over quantity, so whenever possible they try to hire the best of the best, which isn’t a problem taking into account their vast list of contacts and their deep pockets. If necessary, libra can arrange for further training of its agents, but it prefers to have the agents learn from each other.
    The only thing that changes between the two groups is that for the second, they also look for a very specific type of people: those who believe that sometimes the ends justify the means. Special emphasis is made on sometimes, Libra needs agents willing to do whatever it takes to ensure that the world doesn’t destroy itself, but that can’t come at the cost of creating a bigger problem. They have to be able to walk on the edge without falling to either side.
    Curiously, Joana, Pavel and Philip seem to know who is a talent, and they always tend to end in the groups with the most important missions. This is not a conscious choice from their part, they just know that some people have “something” that makes them unique and pull out the impossible. They have created something similar like the project tantalus of AEON, but beyond the ocasional inspired science apparatus and some anomalous results, they haven't found anything concluding regarding Talents.

    Organization and structure

    Just like the agents they recruit, the organization is divided in two. On one hand you have the three organizations which behave like you would expect from organizations of their type. Although they do work towards Libra’s ultimate goal, they do so indirectly. Their main use is to gather resources, contacts and intel for the main objective. These are then collected and used to create the missions for the freelancers by a team of analysts.
    O    n the other hand, the second part of Libra is organized in separate cells to ensure maximum security. Nobody, not even the three at the top, know the exact number of cells. These cells are composed of freelancers who live regular lives till they’re activated, at which moment they must drop everything and prepare for the mission.
    The agents have absolute freedom to handle the task as they see fit, as long as they’re discreet and don’t make a mess. Preparation is key since once the mission starts, the agents are on their own, and they can only contact the highers ups in Libra to either communicate the success, or failure, of the mission.
    This is why it’s expected, and encouraged, for each agent and cell to create their own network of contacts and allies. Libra will always provide the cells with all the money, intel and contacts for specialists they ask for through anonymous channels, but anything else risks creating a trail, something that Libra cannot afford. Deniability is at the order of the day.

    Goals and methods

    The main Goal of Libra is to maintain a balance between the major powers of the world, be they governments, corporations, criminal syndicates or any other type of organizations. This balance is key to ensure cooperation, prosperity, and most important of all, prevent the world from destroying itself. In order to do this, Libra uses their intel network to keep an eye in the critical nodes that make this balance possible and eliminate anything that threatens them.
    Libra believes in action more than reaction, so if their analysts point to something as a potential threat in the future, they will make sure it never becomes a problem in the first place.     Having lived with the lingering fear of a nuclear apocalypse, Libra is willing to go to any extremes to make sure there will be a tomorrow. Assassination, blackmail, kidnapping, torture bribery and anything else is on the table, as long as it’s deemed necessary. Again, agents are expected to use their freedom with common sense and with the knowledge that these methods have serious consequences if abused.
    A typical team of Libra is formed by experts in different fields, both legal and illegal, but it usually has an analyst to study the target, a hacker to obtain information and someone with black ops experience. The idea is to have as many perspectives as possible, so they can come up with the best approach to the mission. Regardless of the approach, the goal is the same: be precise, be fast, be invisible, and don’t make a mess.

    Advantages

    Libra has three main advantages: the first one is that, if a line needs to be crossed to get the job done, then cross it. Cutting the head of the snake on time can save a lot of lives, or maybe the whole world. Then again, always exercise judgement and precaution before doing it. Extreme methods must be applied in extreme circumstances, less they create more problems than they solve.
    The second one is a list with the largest networks of contacts for professionals in the fields of security, crime, IT, law and many others. It has been carefully cultivated over decades to ensure that only the best make it on that list. If one cell needs an expert in a particular field, Libra can either give them the contact or point them in the right direction.
    The third and final is that Libra exists in multiple levels of society. Their reach extends to both the world of law and the world of crime, and they know how to make both work in harmony. This gives agents multiple ways to approach their target, and if they get in trouble with one world, they can still rely on the other to get out.

    Why Libra?

    Libra exists to explore the places where the world is morally gray and how sometimes an evil act is necessary to prevent an even bigger evil. The question at the center of Libra is this, how far are the agents willing to go? Is it possible to walk a middle ground without falling too far in the dark? Can the same be said of the organization?

    Path Concepts

    Ex-government agent, army veteran, unscrupulous lawyer, professional thug, assassin with a conscience, mischievous hacker,
    Connections: FBI Agent, Safe House Owner, Weapons Dealer, Smuggler, Gang leader
    Skills: Empathy, Larceny, Integrity, Aim Edges: Always Prepared, Covert, skilled liar, Small Unit Tactics, Sniper, Alternate Identity
    Gifts: Skill (Aim, Larceny, Empathy)

    Opinions on other alliances

    AEON Trinity: "Few organizations have achieved so much in the name of world peace as AEON, and yet our multiple failures to infiltrate their ranks have shown that they're hiding as many secrets as we do. We must decide if we should risk antagonizing them just to find out the truth."
    9: "This world has always been filled with unexplained phenomena and dangers that nobody could imagine, it has remained a blind angle for us till 9 arrived. So far they have conducted themselves admirably, but we must be vigilant in case they're tempted to use the very things they fight against."
    Archangel: "We like to think about archangel as our younger sibling. We would like to welcome them into our fold, but their rigid morals makes this unlikely. We'll keep collaborating and waiting for the day they finally grow up."
    Pharao's lightkeepers: REDACTED
    Assassin's organizations: "A paradox, on one hand they're our greatest allies in the fight for world peace, on the other they could throw the world into chaos easily if the choose so. We must remain their allies and do our best to make sure that they never realize just how much power they have."

    Libra in the Nova Age

    Taking into account the obsession of Libra with balance and an stable world, the appeareance of Novas sent the organization into a panic Few people know this, but Libra almost activated their "Total balance protocol". Their doomsday plan consisting on a purge on a massive scale of any "disruptive" elements via assasination, and then using the organizations resources to regain balance. Thankfully, internal division prevented the implemtation of the protocol, which gave the world crime task force of project utopia enough time to destroy a good number of their cells and almost expose them to the world. Crippled, divided and on the run, Libra went back to its roots as an informal network while each of their organizations went their separate ways. From then till the end of the Aberrant war, Libra would conduct their operations by supporting the Directive, Aeon Trinity and Project Utopia. That said, in a vault hidden inside one of the founders safe houses, there's a hardrive with the necessary codes to start the "Total Balance Protocol", which was designed to stay valid even after years or decades, who knows what kind of damage it could bring on the wrong hands...
