    Actually: before you determine what Super-Science looks like, you first need to look at what “ordinary” science looks like. And the first thing I'd do there would be to take all of the biotech that's proprietary to the Psi Orders in Prometheus Unbound and make them broadly available.

    I would further expect the Psi Orders themselves to be lost to the past, replaced by newer organizations that fill similar roles without the Psi-specific focus: they should cater to paragons, mentats, and augments, not just (or even primarily) mentats. Part of this comes from the fact that ubiquitous “Ambrosia” renders Prometheus Chambers (and the Nakamura Process) obsolete: anyone who has the potential to be a mentat or augment is one, and long before the various Allegiances have anything to say about it.

    The second thing I'd do for technology would be to look at the various limitations that exist for TC: Aeon​​​ technology, and ask if those limitations should be mitigated or even eliminated over the next 900 years. Should the Jump Rings become more like Star Trek's Transporters? Should Medical Symbionts become noteworthy only in their rare absence? Should IADs still be restricted to single Aptitudes? Should robots still be restricted to SI? Has Quantum-based technology allowed for the development of hardtech equivalents of biotech innovations?

    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

But yeah; I could see ubiquitous nanotech in the Space Opera era leading to the distinctions becoming fuzzy at the low end of the power scale, such that the distinctions between the different categories of Gifts become more guidelines than actual rules. Something like: the Talent equivalent in this setting chooses one category of Gift (Quantum, Psi, or Flux) as a favored category, which he can develop more easily than the others; but, e.g., a “Flux Inspired” would still be able to pick up Quantum and Psi Gifts with a little extra work.
