    Could the Star Trek Universe be the future of the TC timeline after Aeon?

    06-21-2020, 04:43 AM
    A friend of mine really likes Star Trek and he was asking me this.

    I guess the question is would it work both historically and thematically. Obviously you would have to change a few of the alien races and some of the technology, but a lot of that could be accounted for by the 250 year gap between Aeon and Star Trek. Also, you would have to either add psions to the Star Trek universe or explain what happened to them.

    What do you guys think?

          ...Think the Storypath game engine can be used to run Star Trek. So yes it can be done.
          But I hesitate to try and combine the lore.

          Putting aside the internal inconsistencies with the Star Trek's own cannon, there are a large number of errors that arise trying to bolt the two together. Ironically the broadstrokes are surprisingly close in places. The Aberrant War can replace the Eugenics War, as an example (though there is a thirty year difference) or WW3. Heck, Aeon itself is set only a little before Star Trek Enterprise is set. So you can treat it as a prequel of sorts
          Psions then, are easy to remove; 2063 (first contact) happens well before the Promethean Chambers are developed and Vulcans have a natural Psi rating (mind meld = Telepathy aptitude), albeit one capped low, which would lower the immediate concerns of the Doyen. (It would make them more paranoid about moving against humanity openly, more likely to use other species as proxies.) Even if that doesn't work, someone could find out about the secret issues with the chambers and blow them before Cpt. Archer was born, let alone anyone else. Star Trek is filled more with Talents anyway.
          A bigger issue is the FTL aspects; Star Trek Warp engines work very much like Trinity Q-Tech Warp engines. And almost none of Star Trek's ship are bio-organic. Discovery's spore drive is the closet to the Leviathan jump drive...but it is still way off.

          Still it does depend on which aspects are more important to you and your friend. The game can be used to run Star Trek-like adventures already.

          Thoughts ripple out, birthing others

                      What Bunyip says here is correct. I can and would totally run a Star Trek game with the Æon rules, but setting-wise there are too many differences.

                      That said...

                      ----
                      The Aberrant War left Earth devastated. Even after the Aberrants had been forced off-planet by the Chinese Ultimatum in 2061, it took decades for the nations of the world to recover.

                      Possibly inspired by the Aberrants' use of space warps to travel distances, entrepreneur and scientist Zephram Cochrane developed a prototype FTL engine he called "warp drive" from a rural Montana facility in 2063. Although initially motivated by greed, he quickly realized the potential ramifications for humanity. The recovery of Earth had begun.

                      While warp drive was still useful for Earth's immediate neighbourhood, it still took several weeks-to-months to travel longer distances. While this was still a vast improvement over the centuries such travels would have taken previously, it still left interstellar travel, exploration, and colonization largely constrained to the closest handful of star systems. No one could break the vaunted warp five barrier.

                      Then the psions appeared.

                      In the early 2100s, psions of all stripes manifested to combat the returned Aberrants. These included teleporters, who had been given a set of psi coordinates for more distant systems ripe for colonization. While this made instant travel possible, it was only to a small set of pre-defined worlds, or anywhere else the psion had personally seen. Even after the advent of the Leviathan-class jumpships in 2119, the relative cost and scarcity meant most freighters and other private craft were still limited to low warp speeds.

                      Fortunately, a research breakthrough led to the development of the warp 5 engine, the first capable of hitting warp 5. This led to a new wave of exploration from the new Earth Starfleet, spearheaded by the first warp 5 ship, the Enterprise, in 2150. Distances further than 100 LY or so were still effectively beyond reach, but Earth's immediate neighbourhood grew, worlds were colonized, and new friends were made among the stars.

                      This new exploration was nearly cut short before it began thanks to the discovery of Prothean ruins and element zero ("eezo") on Mars in 2148, allowing FTL travel at much lower costs in both energy and material than a standard warp drive, as well as granting access to the newly-discovered mass relay system. An eezo-equipped ship could travel to the other side of the galaxy in a matter of hours using the mass relay network. Hundreds of worlds and a dozen new species and civilizations opened up before the human Systems Alliance. Exposure to eezo also seemed to develop psi abilities, now called "biotics," in affected humans, although teleportation remained rare.

                      Although few initially believed Commander Shepard's initial reports of the ancient alien Reapers, Shepard's conversation with the Reaper known as Sovereign in 2183 later sent chills down humanity's spine: the Reapers had created the mass relays and mass effect technology, intentionally shaping the direction of civilizations who used it, making them suitable for harvest. Although the Reapers were defeated by Shepard and the united Citadel races in 2186, humanity felt ill-at-ease at having their technological and societal evolution dictated to them by the Reapers. More resources were poured into warp technology, leading to further developments in that arena, and faster and more powerful warp drives, and the launch of an all new Enterprise.

                      With the mass relay network destroyed, it's uncertain if humanity will ever decide to reinvestigate eezo and reconnect with the Citadel races. Some are wondering if teleportation psi powers will ever be developed further to render the "arms race" of space travel moot.

                              Just a few rules to set the base and you can run Star Trek using TC rules easily.
                              If you use Superiors instead of Novas, you can have the Eugenic War taking place in place of Aberrant War.
                              The great inventors (Cochrane included) could be Talents that can create Inspired tech based on Nova Powers (warp drive for example).
                              Psiads may be common among other races (Vulcans and Betazoids, for example) usually running around one or 2 modes.

                              House Rules - The Bundle - House Rules for Everyone
                              Powered Inspired - Powered Characters using Inspiration and Gifts
                              Fists and Tomes - Inspired Martial Arts and Mysticism for Talents
                              Supertech - Super Science in the hands of Talents
