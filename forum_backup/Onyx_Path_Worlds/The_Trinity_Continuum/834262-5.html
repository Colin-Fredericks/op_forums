                            I'm still holding out for the 1970s era that was teased at in the original sourcebook timelines. The age of the Shaft novels and movies, Stephen King's Carrie, The Six Million Dollar Man and The Bionic Woman, Jaws, The Stepford Wives, The Boys From Brazil, Enter the Dragon, Alice Cooper, The Omen, The Andromeda Strain, David Bowie's Ziggy Stardust and Thin White Duke periods, and the highly underrated work by Kurt Russell in the trilogy of The Computer Wore Tennis Shoes, Now You See Him Now You Don't and The Strongest Man in the World.
