        Wow all. This is great.

        I'm also going to say, all Novas are sterile as matter of course as being not enjoyable. At all.

        I like the idea of the potential of say an all nova species or society. A potential gone if everyone is sterile.

        As well it removes the Incredibles like story of a family and it's dynamics.

        That said:

        Originally posted by Bunyip View Post
        I also have no problem with the sterility conspiracy being a conspiracy for players to solve. But you need to make it bigger, not smaller, so that it takes nova-level characters to unpick it and destroy it. (Or subvert it to their own ends, if that’s what the players want.)

        It needs to be bigger than Utopia giving people who walk through their door an injection, and that’s it. That’s simple and easy to solve. It needs to be spread wider through the ecosystem - in the water, in the food, in cosmetics, in clothing. The conspiracy behind it has seeded it everywhere, so much everywhere, that it’s difficult to tell if it is a conspiracy or whether it’s the natural world being intrinsically against novas.

        It doesn’t need to be hard to discover. One of the 1e problems was that it was laughably easy to detect for any nova with a certain set of skills or powers, and only moderately difficult to discover for novas without those abilities. Make it easy to discover - that’s not the challenge. As per above, the challenge is discovering whether anyone is actually behind it (they are), and convincing others that it’s not just natural.

        Make it difficult to cure. Various novas should have been able to easily shake off the sterilization, but make it more of a challenging quest in 2e. The way to do this is relatively simple - make the affliction psi based*. The conspiracy doesn’t know why this special compound is so anti nova (and harmless to normal humans), but they know that it is. This gives story hooks and a trail of evidence that goes way back, to some group who studied stalwarts and Mesmerists, and noticed a tiny but statistically significant adversarial aspect to how their powers worked. Maybe this original group had no negative implications - maybe they just studied. But somewhere along the way, when novas appeared, someone else got hold of their research and synthesised the chemical and worked out how to make it global.

        (Maybe some lost, solitary Doyen was stranded on Earth, couldn’t escape out of the barrier, and used its psi powers to modify Eath chemistry and organisms to produce this chemical as a terrified form of self Defence.)

        A conspiracy is fine, IMO, but it needs to be something to be defeated. In the 1e games I ran, this was certainly the case, but it was too easy to discover and defeat.

        * But such tiny amounts of psi that it does nothing to hinder the novas powers, but is incredibly hard for anything quantum-based to remove.
        This! Very much this.

        Having it be the Doyen begins the scenes is a perfect the in. A perfect "hey all so the settings are linked" nod. Not too in your face just something cool.

        May be part of the problem is that the Galtea explosion had some effects on the environment and the Doyen used that, invisibly of course, to hide the manipulations.

        So like publically a lot of 1st gen Novas are sterile, but not all. The secrets chapter could say "this can be am accident, or a conspiracy. If it's an accident then is mostly die to x, such as the radiation put out by the explosion. If it's a conspiracy here are two possibilities - human or alien - both involve psi.

        But yeah having all quantum using people naturally sterile seems to limit stories and campaigns more then it helps.

                                While it is definitely possible that there could be a conspiracy to prevent Novas from procreating and possibly replacing humanity... I think there could be another possibility that may be a contributing factor. While the ability to manipulate Quantum (or even Psi) is genetic in nature, we do not know how dominant these genes are (they seem to be very recessive) and often seem to require an outside event to fully activate. The number of Novas even during the Nova Age was surprisingly small... in the hundreds world wide, and we have no idea how likely the genes that gave them their fantastic powers would be passed on to any children they might have... and even if they were, whether those children would manifest any powers at all.

                                It is very possible that what might appear to be a conspiracy to sterilize all Novas to prevent them from having any children may merely be research to see how viable Novas (and Aberrants) are as a species. It is possible that their Nova nature naturally makes procreation (even with another Nova) difficult... the further the Nova (Aberrant) is from being human (with various mutations), the less likely that Nova would be able to procreate. Even those who are able to have children may not pass on the right genetics for the child to develop Nova powers... and even if they could, the conditions to bring out those powers may never happen. In short... the chances for two Novas being able to have a child together may be infinitesimally small... and the chance of that child developing Quantum Powers may be even smaller still... all without a dedicated conspiracy to prevent Novas from having children.

                                Of course this doesn't mean there are not individuals who are so paranoid that even a 1 in a billion chance of Novas producing a Nova child is too great a risk... and will want to eliminate ANY chance of a 2nd Generation of Novas. Likewise it is also very possible that a sufficiently intelligent Nova could find a way to increase the chances of producing children (including those who can become Novas themselves)... something that would likely make those who fear Novas even more determined to prevent them from ever having children. All I am saying is that Quantum Power expression is one of the rarest forms of power in the Trinity Continuum... and it is hard to be sure if the low number is the result of simple nature or a deliberate conspiracy.

                                It may just be that the Quantum Powers that make Nova's different... also makes procreation with others (baseline humans or other Novas) equally difficult. Just because all other aspects of Nova biology seem enhanced doesn't mean that they should be super fertile too. An active M-R node may naturally have the unintended side effect of reducing the viability of the Nova to produce children... or it may be that the Quantum Powers they use does the same thing... especially for those Novas that become noticeably inhuman in appearance.

                                Both are viable options for why the Nova population remains surprisingly small... and are not mutually exclusive. In one timeline, it is possible that there is a conspiracy to render all Novas Sterile... while in another, it is merely their own unique genetics that does so... while in a third both contribute to keep Nova populations down... and a fourth timeline, neither is a factor and Novas become a new species of humanity (for good or ill). All things are possible within the Continuum.

                                There are three types of people in the world... those who can count and those who can't.
                                I reject your reality and substitute my own!
