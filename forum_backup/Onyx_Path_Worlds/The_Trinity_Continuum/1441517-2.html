            Primoris is not Nazi in any way, and I don’t think he is really Supremacist until much later (somewhere around Aberrant era start).                                                             I have to say that I feel terrible for Max. I mean, just think about his position. For all his power he has basically been reduced to be his timeline's janitor, quietly cleaning up messes and doing little things to try and improve it, but not change it in any significant way, at least not without risking creating another timeline (and possible another evil Max). Then to make matters worse he's been hunted by a future version of himself that became his opposite, and he's much more powerful, talking about a blow to your moral (he could be staring at his future for all we know). Finally, he's completely alone with no one he can trust or ask for help, or just talk. So knowing all this, how does he do it? How does he retain any hope whatsoever? Has he seen a future where perhaps, just perhaps, everything turns alright? I hope that the supplement of timelines gives us more information.
