            Originally posted by Bhurano View Post
            Here goes:
            Shameless Lying Smile the Talent Gift on page 214 in the Core Book... how does it work exactly?
            The description is easy enough, but the potential implications are another thing.

            So, here's the rub:
            How far goes that talent?
            Can a Talent put suggestions into people that are potentially far reaching and life altering or not?
            Can he spin stupid stuff like "you drank truth serum and have to answer honest to god"?

            I mean, could be pretty useful in the hands of a creative player, while in the hands of another it's just adding dice and complications.
            If for instance are suggestions are a possibility there is the option to turn a person into a manchurian candidate or a sleeper agent.
            Sure that would take a bit of time and cost Inspiration, but that's hardly a barrier.
            It's lying, not mind control. Even in the hands of a creative player, it's still just dice and Complications. Having a bonus to lie doesn't mean the huge lies don't have their own Difficulties and Complications to overcome.

            The Talent with this can lie through their teeth and seem 100% convincing, but that doesn't make it true. And the more easily disprovable the lie, the harder it's going to be for the Talent to sustain. Even if a target is completely convinced they're still going to react in their own ways that might not be precisely what the Talent intends. And the final limitation - it lasts for a scene, after which the target might still believe the lies but they're more easily disprovable.

            Also, lying usually comes under the Influence systems, which specifically allow the victim to reject the Influence if they want. This will give the Talent Consolation, but it's not going to make automatic sleeper agents.

            Basically, if the Talent's lies are fun and advance the game, regardless of how influential they are, go with it. If they're too far, they won't work.

            Writer. Developer. World of Darkness | Chronicles of Darkness | The Trinity Continuum
