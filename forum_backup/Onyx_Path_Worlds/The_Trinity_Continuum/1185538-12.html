                    Originally posted by Bunyip View Post

                    You’ve misinterpreted that. Bhurano was a psiad - the Doyen forced her to become a psion so they could study QK and control her.
                    Not so much a misinterpretation as a choice not to make an assumption that wasn't specifically stated. Yes, it was said she gained vast psychic potential, but it was never actually stated that she was able to reach any notable level of it before the Doyen contacted her an the other would-be Proxies. Yes, there are a number of months between the setting off of the noetic device in early 2104 and the Doyen creating the 8 Proxies late that year, but would this be enough time for her to develop any of her psychic gifts beyond the most basic levels (even with a Psi rating of 3 or more) before the Doyen castrated that development to control her power? Maybe... but we may never know for sure how far she had progressed before she was possessed by the Doyen.

                    Rereading the section, it does say that the noetic device she set off to reduce the various corrupted lands did indeed have the effect of creating hundreds of new Talents and Psiads around the world (and hundreds of thousands of Latents), so maybe a new event isn't needed. Why none of these individuals have surfaced is a question that will need to be answered when the Psiad rules come out. Chances are that many of them have been diagnosed as "Latents" and been subjected to the same awakening that creates Psions... similar to what happened to Proxy Bhurano (where her Psiad potential has been effectively castrated). As for what happens to those who decide they don't want to join a Psi-Order... it would depend on just how paranoid the Doyen are (and how closely they are paying attention to the identifying of "Latents").

                    Judging from their fear of Proxy Bhurano, chances are that they would be very concerned about any other naturally occuring Psiad emerging from humanity. If such an individual can be identified as more than just a mere Latent, chances are that the Doyen will arrange for some sort of surveillance of the individual, to see if they develop any powers at a later date. How long this surveillance lasts will depend on how determined the Doyen are to control any such potential threats (likely for a few years at least, given how paranoid these aliens seem to be). If the would-be Psiad begins to show signs of developing their powers outside of the Psi-Orders, I can see the Doyen responding in three ways.

                    1) Manipulate events around the Psiad that sees them put some place where they can be monitored more carefully. This can be anything from having them arrested for a crime, to having them committed... or even getting a dream job (or even finding a "soul" mate) where they can be watched closely by the Doyen or their agents very closely.

                    2) Arrange a situation that would make the Psiad reconsider joining one of the Psi-Orders (either by choice or by lack of any other choice) where their potential power can be curbed (forced to become a Psion with all the limitations that come with that).

                    3) Arrange an event that will eliminate the potential threat the Psiad might pose to the Doyen... though it seems unlikely the Doyen would lower themselves to anything this direct (too cowardly to eliminate a Psion directly as the risk of being discovered by the Psiad and possibly destroyed is too great, even if it is fairly slim).

                    All of these could contribute to how rare identifiable Psiads seem to be. Of course we will know more once the Psiad rules are available.

                    There are three types of people in the world... those who can count and those who can't.
                    I reject your reality and substitute my own!
