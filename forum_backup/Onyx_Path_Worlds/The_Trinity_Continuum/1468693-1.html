                                            With finally getting TC Aeon 2E corebook from sale, I can finally finalise my Wyrd’s Darker Trinity Timeline. I use here a lot of timeline quotes from Anima and Aeon books, for fuller look on events.

                                            Wyrd’s Darker Trinity Timeline

                                            Up to 2025 - History run as in real world ( beside Trinity shenanigans )

                                            2018 - We DID NOT get Galatea Explosion.

                                            2019-2022 - Pandemic of COVID19.

                                            2022-2024 - Russia-Ukraine war. In the end, Russian Federation lost. Ukraine is accessing to European Union and NATO. War ended on May 9th, on Russian Victory Day, greatest Federation national holiday. Russia humiliation starts process that will end de facto annexed into China, in coming decades. ( By the time of Anima era. )

                                            2025, May 1st - Tokyo Incident. Variant of N-Day where Davis Mal did not broke Doyen Barrier - only made 'holes' in it. Novas start to show up, but are much, much less populate than in canon Aberrant era.

                                            June of 2025 - United Nations Security Council secretly start Project Proteus - Assassins network across their Societies, that are ( unknowingly ) send to kill this few Novas that already Erupted.

                                            2030s - 2050s - Nova Age? They start in hiding, unsure of their powers and hunted by Proteus. In year 2030, first Novas are proud enough to stand in the light and talk with media and their outcast family, without maniacal laugh – Queer Nova Alliance. On the other hand, from the Tokyo Incident forward, antisocial superhumans are pointed as Aberrants by governments agencies, like 9 or Alert Status 1 ( See Trinity Continuum Core Rulebook ) Term leaks to press in few cases, living from now on it’s own in Internet.

                                            In those decades, Earth is divided on subject of Novas. Some points their humanity, under all the powers. Individuals invent hyperfusion and superconducting batteries, giving humanity unlimited cheap energy in some instances – but it’s always not enough. Even with reversing global climate change projects and famous ‘blooming Sahara Desert’ – worst enemies were other Novas, more ‘criminally minded’. And there are also baselines humans pointing not every superhuman is knight in the shining armour. There are those that steal and kill others simply cause they can. There are those taken by Davis Mal the Teragen movement. And there were simply Nova psychopats – those become Aberrants.

                                            During the height of this time, some of superhumans built research bases on several asteroids and planets within the solar system and explored the stars. China’s controlled Nova led an exploration team to Khantze Lu Ge in 2040 and founded several other extrasolar colonies. In addition, several groups of humans paid space-traveling Novas to help them establish colonies on habitable extrasolar planets. A few of these colonies were founded in secret and records of most others were lost during the Internet Crash in 2067.

                                            No wonders of Nova Age?

                                            So with Tokyo Incident and creation of Project Proteus in 2025, did Nova Age really started before Aberrant Wars? Yes and no. With tumultuous times and society divided on Novas subject, some of them reached the heights of canonical Trinity Continuum. But also they were hindered by they more malice inducing brethren. Many project of official Nova Age were devised in this timeline, also, but most of the time were not so widespread and much more individual projects – until baseline humanity took those techs in their own hands. Particular example is OpNet.

                                            No OpNet… yet

                                            In this variant timeline, Great Crash of 2067 destroys Internet – 10G to be precise – not OpNet. The later in this version is Nova tech developed in 2049, and beside great speeds and uniform laying ( that 10G also had ) OpNet let to send full senses across it. True virtual environment immersive simulation! But as it was Novas invention – it was very distrusted outside of superhumans supports and cults. When Aberrant Wars started in 2056 - network do not have time to propagate around the world. Instead, in this variant timeline, OpNet is introduced to whole world in times of Great Reconstructions after Wars, in 2070s – of course, ‘Aberrant protected’ in Gen 2 of tech. GLASS implant is making this network full virtual senses experience from start, hyping and propagating around the planet and Sol system.

                                            2025 - 2105 — Japan — After Tokyo Incident in 2025, Japan close it’s borders for few months. In coming years, Japan becomes more and more isolation. Rumors say that Novas started there and government is exterminating with their sources. In 2053, Japan finally closes its borders and changes its name to match the one it uses internally — Nippon. Until 2105, no outsiders are allowed to step onto Nihonjin soil, and all contact with the outside world comes through the large offshore floating settlement of Makaro-Shima.

                                            2079 — In response to declining Church membership and empty parishes resulting from the Urban Schism, the papal bull Qua dispensatione animam (“The Dispensation of Souls”) from Pope Innocent XV states that transubstantiation can occur via digital projection. So long as the sensation of consuming bread and wine are experienced by a real priest, any Catholic sharing that sensory experience may enjoy communion with God and receive transmission of the sacrament over OpNet. The Anglican and Orthodox Churches follow suit, as do other denominations of Christianity. At the same time, the pope elevates Friar Byrne Draper to bishop and sets him to lead the ecclesiastical province of Cascade in once Canada, with the expansive mandate to hold Mass for worldwide audiences projecting into the city’s municipal servers.

                                            Rest of events in this timeline is the same as in the official canonical timeline. 😎
