                                                            From what I can tell the setting for this is a single city (Cascade) within a single nation (the FSA). Unlike Adventure, Aberrant or Aeon, you don't have a whole world or possibly multiple worlds (aside from the one in the computer). Within that setting, sure, if you want to play some small-business people or a street gang or whatever, that's fine. However, the plot (not really metaplot) is still going to be happening and some of it will eventually happen to your characters. At that point they can either choose to try to do something about it or just let it keep happening to them.

                                                            So, yes, unlike the other mainline games this is a setting with a small scope and more limited focus.
