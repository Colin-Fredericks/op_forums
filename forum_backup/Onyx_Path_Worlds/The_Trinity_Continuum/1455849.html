    A Technique Equivalent For Mega-Attributes

    07-31-2021, 09:47 AM
    So, on a thematic level, Mega-Attributes are one of the things that really separates Aberrant from other supers games. Sure, being superhuman is a common theme, but it being separate from powers and quite as all-encompassing as Mega-Attributes is fairly unique, and the way that NPC Mega-Attributes were handled in 1E (ie: it was a rare Nova that wasn't shown with several), created a very specific and unique dynamic. 'Dark Stars' with low ratings in all the Mega-attributes were and are also fairly unique and interesting conceptually.

    On a mechanical level in 2E, however, there are some cost issues with implementing this dynamic. Specifically, Powers are actually much cheaper than Mega-Attributes (due to how Technique works), and some can even grant Mega-Attributes...and thus the best way to build, say, a Brick is the Transformation power, not actually buying Mega-Attributes, while the best way to 'have 1 dot of every Mega-Attribute' is actually Boost and Variable or Extra Attribute. That's counterintuitive for new players and not super fun, IMO.

    In fact, I can build a Nova with Quantum 4, Boost 4 (All Attributes, Subtle, Maintained, Ramp Up -3...activated when you wake up in the morning and left on all day) and, as a Technique, Transformation 5 (Maintained, Subtle, Extra Attributes 3) or thereabouts who walks around with straight 5s in all Attributes, and at least two dots in every single Mega-Attribute (and 5 in all the physical ones, plus Toughness 5), as a starting character. That's exploitive and should generally be vetoed by the SG, but a biokinetic, say, with Boost (Might, Dex, and Stm) and a Technique for Transformation (as well as others for Quantum Attack and Healing), on the other hand, is a pretty reasonable concept and runs into the same problem of 'better at Mega-Attributes than people who actually bought Mega-Attributes'. Which is a problem.

    So, I've added the following Mega-Edge to my games as a House Rule:

    Polymath (O – OOOOO):
    Prerequisites: Quantum equal to dots, one Mega-Attribute at 2+

    Select one Mega-Attribute. For every dot in this Edge receive additional dots in Mega-Attributes and Mega-Edges equal to that Mega-Attribute's rating. These points may not be used to purchase the Technique or Q-Tech Mega-Edges or to increase any Mega-Attribute or Mega-Edge above the rating of the original Mega-Attribute. A character may only ever have this Edge once, though if another Mega-Attribute ever equals their rating in the Mega-Attribute it effects, they may switch it to be based on that Mega-Attribute instead. Since this Mega-Edge has no mechanical effect beyond changing costs, it should not generally be listed on NPCs.


    Technically, the above mentioned Boost/Transformation combo remains more cost effective than this, but this lets you actually take advantage of Mega-Edges and the difference is a lot less glaring, and many more concepts can and should have access to it (the Boost/Transformation combo does also have real downsides while Polymath just doesn't). It does necessitate one high Mega-Attribute even on a thematically 'Dark Star' Nova, but both Cunning and Composure (especially Composure) actually work well for that as 'subtle' Mega-Attributes. I highly recommend something like this for any game wanting to focus on Mega-Attributes, especially those with relatively new players.

      I use a similar idea, but just giving more options to Technique Mega Edge (but making one for it is a better option).

      In my idea you add the technique to a Mega Attribute and gain the MAtt rank times the technique mega edge dots as dots of Mega Edges related to that MAtt (Technique is not a valid one).

      So if you get Technique to Mega Might, you can get Mega Lifting, Mega Crush, etc, but not any Mega Cunning mega edge for example, or any dot in Mega Dexterity.

      A bit less versatile than yours, but I honestly prefer that way.

          Personally, I think that the problem is that Technique is too powerful: you get a whole other power for the cost of just one dot in a Mega-Edge, and the only downside is that if you want both active at once, you need to split the activation dice pool. That feels more like it should be handled by requiring every power separately, then applying a negative tag to each power in a suite to represent the shared activation dice pool: say, -1 to each if two powers are in the suite, -2 to each if three powers are in the suite, and so on. (This escalating discount is based on the idea that the more powers you try to activate, the thinner you have to spread your successes.)

          The Technique approach is only priced reasonably if the powers in the suite cannot be active at the same time: you'd basically be buying one power, and supplementing it with the ability to swap it out for something else as needed. So you pay the full price for one power, plus one Mega-Edge dot for each alternative way to use it.

              Personally, I think it’s the one thing that allows Novas to follow other splats in the XP cost benefit.

              At first a Nova gains a bunch of extra,XP, but everything a nova can buy is really expensive. You may think it’s about making it tempting to buy it corrupted, but at the long run (when you already got all the transformations you want and are already afraid of transcendence), any modification in the powers costs a lot of XP.

              For example, Psion/Psiad powers costs 6-8 so per dot, but you gain an improvement on the previous levels (as per dot table) and an entire new power (at least on a cheaper way to use it), while a Nova would need to spend 1 “Nova Dot” in the range, 1 duration tag, and another in the new effect, also, the nova dot costs twice the cost of the Psion equivalent, in other words, 6 times more to get the same effect.

              In other words, Novas start powerful, but after 150 more XP, the Psion will have 15+ New mode dots (considering they spend some in psi and other stuff), while a nova will have spent most of the same XP in just 5 or 6 tags and a 3 or 4 ranks in powers (and maybe +1 or 2 quantum), and the 15+ dots in modes will grant lots of advantages beside the powers themselves.
