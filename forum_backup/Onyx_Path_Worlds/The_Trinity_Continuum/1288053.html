    Ideally how do you want Reproduction to be Handled

    02-14-2019, 04:23 PM
    So since we don’t have all the answers yet in books let’s discuss this.

    Talents are basically human and their children most likely don’t inherit Talent status, but you may go with the trop that Talented and Heroic people have Talented and Heroic children. But basically up to the SG to decide if Professor Jones Kid grows up to be Indiana Jones or Shiloh Lebouf.

    Psion if I recall have the issue with them being to new to have much Data. Correct me if I’m wrong but didn’t the Doyen design the Prometheus Chambers so they remove the potential of the subjects bloodline from having Psiad kids and instead their kids are Latents?

    What about Psiads though? I think I would have them have a higher chance of having Psiad children especially if they have kids with another Psiad, this gives me the option to have bloodlines of Psychics/Mystics/Witches hidden around the world. Not sure how likely I should make Psiadhood be passed onto the next generation but I do like it being say something that may be dormant in the blood for generations, kind of like a recessive trait.

    I forget was Latents a result of Bhurano’s Noetic Wave or were they something the Doyen planned?

    Aberrants now are the meat and Potatoes of the discussion! Now let’s ignore them being sterile and consider if that is the case it’s because of a conspiracy trying to sterilize Novas to contain them, but they aren’t too wide spread. But what do we want for Nova Breeding? I take Marvel as my biggest inspiration. The Earth X TrIlogy brought up the idea that Inhumans can be long lived but virtually infertile, but Attilan curbed this due to carefully arranged marriages to maximize fertility. And Early Marvel has a proclivity towards mutants producing Mutant children virtually all the time, with a handful of anomalies like Graydon Creed being the human child of Mystique and Sabertooth. They also brought up the idea that mutated superhumans have a proclivity towards their children being Born Mutants, like Franklin Richards and Spider-Man’s family. In an early 90s late 80s Spidey comic he talks with Hank McCoy about what would happen if he had a kid and Hank brought up that the child could be a mutant, and I believe Spidergirl from the MC2 timeline is a mutant but with her pappy’s powers.

    So maybe the higher the Quantum the less fertile you are through conventional means? But Aberrants with low Quantum and High Corruption are more fertile like the Aeons Space Aberrants. This also kind of goes with how the Eternals were handled by Marvel, Earth Eternals were immortal but static in Age but when a group left to populate Titan they seemed to have no problem Breeding, but there may be an implication that Science helped them, with Mentor and his Father Chronos regarded as the Fathers of Titans population. They also seemed to be over all weaker with successive generations, with Thanos being a mutant, and having experimented on himself to Enhance his body and mind. His brother Eros seems to be more potent then most Titans but his father was Mentor. The average Titan seemed to probably be superhuman but much less potent and more human then then the small number of Immortal Eternals on Earth.

    And then we have Mutants. They often breed in bloodlines that shares similar powers, and they stayed potent if not getting more potent with each generation, especially if they mate with another mutant rather the. Human. Like Beaks kids had a mix between his mutation and his Wife’s. He seemed to be more a start of a new species, which a lot of Mutants seem to be.

    Which the Earth X Trilogy back. They viewed the process of mutation as evolving towards a generalized shared mutation like the Skrulls and shapeshifting. And they viewed Shapeshifting as reaching a peak of both homogeneity and vast potential. The series has it that the Celestials built a failsafe in their experiments so that when they start reaching that peak of mutation they start becoming more psychoreactive, so they don’t rival the Celestials they start being shaped mind and body by the expectations of another. So the Skrulls were peaceful and were approaching this phase when the Kree stole their tech and started a million year war, which shaped the Skrulls by their prejudices and expections changing them from a peaceful civilization to A warmongering rival civilization. Reed was of the Skrulls stage of mutation, even having a form of shape mastery, and his son Franklin was at that peek stage of Mutation and had Godlike powers to rival the Celestials but he was effected by the expectations of his family, till eventually when the Universe needed a new Galactus he was convinced he was Galactus, and that Galactus wasn’t Heartless.

    So a big thing I want for Aberrants is to have them effected by their Eruptions. The books have it Novas powers are random and subconscious derived. I want to reign this in a bit and give it structure. So those who erupt spontaneously are like Mutants and Inhumans, their powers could be anything, but they are effected by their parents powerset, perhaps the parents genes but also subconscious expections about their progeny. So Novas have kids similar to them, with powers at least somewhat related.

    But I want other methods of Eruption to have somewhat more stable results. Like Gamma radiation making super strong monsters, or specifically Hulks if related to one or getting a transfusion from one being a method of eruption.

    But if Say Dr. Connors makes a lizard serum it creates lizard Novas rather then a random eruption. So Superiors are similar to the Weapon Plus/ Project Rebirth, having a stabilizes somewhat controlled method of eruptions. Thoughts? Suggestions?

      Oh man, so much to read, so many references, I got a little lost, so let’s beggin by Talents.

      Talents are, as I understand, regular humans exposed to Flux, all but one of the “eruptions” of talents are related to events and not birth (one is you were not supposed to be born, but you did). A son of a Talent is a Talent in potential as all the other humans.

      Psiads and Novas are a weird situation, as there are two main theories around them.
      The first one is a person is born with a gene that say if can became superhuman, and it is unlocked by psi or by quantum. So a child of a psion could became a Nova, as long it is exposed to quantum and not to psi, and a child of a nova could became a psiad if exposed to psi and not to quantum.
      Second Novas and Psiads are 2 totally new subspecies os humans, they can reproduce with humans and one with each other, but it will be a potential nova or a potential psiad since birth, unlocking if exposed to the proper condition.
      Both theories focus on fact that the eruption or psi awakening are dependent on external energy source. So a potential nova can erupt before birth if exposed to enough quantum (for example being inside of a nova), probably the same can happen o a child inside a psion, in this case being born as a psiad, possibly even a psion, as the chamber changes the DNA of the psion.

      About powers from novas. Novas don’t have a specific power, they know how to use a specific power, their real power is unlimited, it means a nova erupting before developed any conscious have no limit to their powers. That’s why second generation novas are powerful, they are born with power and their power is unlimited by definition. Of course there is a chance of the baby nova replicate their parents powers, as we replicate our parents cultural and social behaviour, but it’s not about genetics, but about learning what is possible.

                                                          Also I’m still defining some of my odd eruption theory so I have a question how would the setting handle the Ironfist and Immortal Weopon of the Capital Cities of Heaven. So basically the Ironfist is. Special refined Chi, and usually you inherit it after another Iron Fist dies, but also if one retires or goes AWOL like Orson Randal.

                                                          So the Iron Fist allows superhuman feats of skill, speed and strength and it’s eponymous Ironfist, which is gather a your qi in a concentrated aura around your fist or another extremity making it indestructible and explosive. Later he learned other Ironfists developed methods to channel the Ironfist into weapons like swords, staves, or arrows and bullets.

                                                          So it sounds like it could be either an expression of Psi or Quantum, it’s a bit out their for Flux. But to inherit it you have to prove worthy by beating the Shou-Lao the Undying, a dragon that seemed to be a living incarnation of the Ironfist, that steward the Energy and keeps the keys to its power in its very heart. If you plunge your fist into Shou-Lao’s heart your chest is branded with the Dragons symbol and you posses the Iron Fist and become the Immortal Weapon of Kun Lun. Now Kun Lun and the Iron Fist are a perfect Example or something that what come about with the Z-Wave, but those faded, though I may make an exception and say it stayed or somehow rewrote history. Kun Lun could be an Ancient City of Immortals, but would Qi Masters Fit better as Psi or Quantum users? A lot of the Iron Fist has Psi powers but defined in a narrow but potent way, so what do you think of a “Psion” (nonPrometheus Psion) whose Psi Aptitude’s are channeled through Martial Arts and Meditation, and which takes the form of say an inherited Qi, so like you embody the Qi of a Noetic being that seems to be a Dragon. Shou-Lao in this case seems to be a dragon which could be one of those Ascended Noetic entities who dips into the mortal reality to steward humanity using the Ironfist.

                                                          Or Kun Lun could be a Quantum City, held in a pocket Dimension using Quantum, some of the people there use low level Quantum powers, but if you prove worthy by besting Shou Lao, who may be an Ascended Quantum Being or maybe a Construct/Avatar/Familiar of such a Being, you erupt with a specific Quantum signature of the Iron Fist? While his powers could be reflected with Psi it could also quite easily I suppose be Nova powers.

                                                          Or should I do a mix of Both. Kun Lun is an otherworldly pocket dimension, and it most beings who use Qi are actually Psiads, with Talents mixed in for those who don’t manifest vulgar Qi displays. But at the heart of this Psi society is a Being of Pure Quantum. Shou-Lao holds all the Quantum of Kun Lun in his body, this helps prevent corruption in the city’s Qi and keeps everyone’s Qi healthy since Quantum and Psi are antithetical to each other. The one who becomes the Ironfist however erupt using the Quantum of Shou-Lao so they can do things most of the other Qi users can’t do as they posses the Singularly potent Golden Qi of Shou-Lao the Undying, but Golden Qi is actually Quantum and the subtle Qi other masters use is Psi, and Flux.
