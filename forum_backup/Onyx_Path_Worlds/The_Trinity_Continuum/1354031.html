    PC Novas in Aeon Trinity

    12-09-2019, 09:54 PM
    For the record, this is my take for my game. Others are perfectly free to alter, discard or ignore anything here for their own games. In deference to past custom with fam-made materials I will refer to book page numbers and otherwise be a little oblique to avoid copyright infringements.

    First, build the character as a human per the character creation rules outline in the Aeon Core pp. 34-38, though in this case Step 6 is eliminated. Note that the only two Nova-specific Edges are Attunement and Dormancy (Aberrant KS Manuscript Preview 2 pp 12-13). Eufiber is no longer a thing in Aeon.

    The Origin and Role Paths may be completed normal, but the Society Path needs a bit of clarification. For the most part the Society Paths of previous era have ceased to exist and it's unlikely (at best) that a Nova in the Aeon era would be taking most of the Psi Order Paths. Of the official Paths remain, some possibilities include the Freeland "Psion" (change "psi powers" to quantum powers), (replace Enhanced Attunement and Favored Mode with Attunement and Dormancy); The Nihonjin Agent (in addition to any other Cyberware, the Nova version of this guy gets a free, remotely activated Kill Switch (Aeon Aexpansion pp. 21). Another option might be as with Aeon Society Minerva Aeon pp 191, though you'll get a remote Kill Switch from them, too. A final option could be as part of the hidden Chitra Bhanu switching Attunement for Enhanced Attunement and Dormancy for Enhanced Psi Cloak. While these folks probably won't put an acid bomb in your brain, presumably pissing off S.K. Bhurano would have some deeply unpleasant consequences for your Nova character.

    After this apply a modified version of the Nova Template (Aberrant KS Manuscript Preview 2 pp 3-4) with the following changes: Along with a point of Quantum, they also receive a point of permanent Transcendence. They are still considered to be a Tier II character. Should they raise their Quantum to 6, they are considered to be a Tier III character at that point. While they can accept the Favored Attribute point, they may also choose to exchange it for an additional point of Quantum (along with a second point of Transcendence) and a point of either Attunement or Dormancy. Instead of a dot of Fame, they may choose a dot of Covert is that hasn't reach 2 dots yet. If both Covert and Alternate Identity are at 2 dots, the Nova may instead choose another Dot of Attunement, Dormancy or a dot chosen from one of his Paths).

    Instead of the normal XP, the nova gets 75 XP to spend on various abilities, at least 50 of which must be spent on Nova-specific traits (Quantum, Mega-Attributes, Attunement/Dormancy, etc) using the cost table (Aberrant KS Preview 2 pp. 4. Quantum may not be raised higher than 3 at this point. Quantum is also considered to be bought at half the normal cost, but each additional point of Quantum comes with an additional point of Transcendence (Should a character raise his Quantum to 4, they'll get 2 permanent points of Transcendence (1 for going to Quantum 4, the other for buying at half cost). Powers, Mega-Edges, Tags, etc can be found in Aberrant KS Preview 3 pp. 18-75. As usual, anyone (foolishly?) wishing to purchase a Quantum-based Trait (not including a Nova-specific Edges like Attunement or Dormancy) may do so at half the XP per Dot/Tag level, etc. For example, buying the "Quantum Attack" power with the "Aggravated (2)" Tag this way would give you 3 additional points of permanent Transcendence.

    Note that per changes in the Forums, the power tag "Technique" no longer exists. Instead, one may buy the Mega-Edge: Technique which otherwise obeys the rules and restrictions for Power Suites and Tags outlined at Aberrant KS Preview 3 pp 41-42.

    In addition to all of this, Aeon Nova apply a modified version of the "Horror" Tone at Aberrant KS Preview 3 pp 14. Along with what's listed there the Threshold for gaining Transformations (detailed in Aberrant KS Preview pp is 2 lower. So, along with their first, a Nova gains low-level transformations at Transcendence 2 and 3, Medium-level once at Transference 4 and 5 and high level ones for 6 and above. Note that they gain a Transformation for each dot of Transcendence they get. A nova with Transcendence 3 who buys Quantum 4 raises to Transcendence 5 and this gets two mid-level transformations.

    Reading through the above, it would be easy to assume that any Aeon Nova is doomed to swift, inevitable descent into becoming a twisted monstrosity. That is far from wrong. However, there is hope. Novas of this time period have potentially access to avenue that their previous counterparts did not with the advent of the Chitra Bhanu.

    In addition to the technique described for shedding temporary Flux in Aberrant KS Preview 2 pp. 7-8, a Nova may shed Flux more quickly with the help of a Quantakinetic. First, the nova must expend his Quantum down to 0. Then they must "Dorm down per Dormancy (and thus have only normal human Health level) Next, a QK must use Drain on Aeon pp. 243. For these purposes, Corruption is effectively Quantum points. As this occurs, the Nova suffers injury (and pain) per Burning Out in Aberrant KS Preview 3 pp 20. Odd numbers of Quantum are rounded up to the next injury level (so a QK Draining 5 Quantum points would inflict Injury -1 damage on a fully healthy Nova. Finally, the Nova makes the Quantum+Composure with each success removing one Flux point for every two full Quantum points taking by the Drain with failure/botches having their usual effects. After all this, the Nova's Quantum template is something of a mess and he needs to heal his injuries with time, rest and medical care. Attempting to speed the healing with Psi or Quantum powers applies a +2 Complication for each injury level. Failure aggravates the injuries and causes the Nova to lose an additional injury level. A Botch aggravates the injury as above and also causes the Nova to gain temporary Flux equal to the higher of his Quantum or Permanent Transcendence.

          I'd generally agree in terms of Nihonjin with the idea of the Nova agent being some kind of exception/experiment or something.

          Eden Novas would be rolled up in the normal way as Novas in the main Aberrant book (150 XP, Tier 3, etc) but with Origins, Roles and Society Paths unique to Eden.

          The above template is meant to roll up Novas from Earth and human colonies who did not descend into total Transcendence/Corruption at the moment of eruption.

          My take is that in the Aeon era true Nova eruption are very rare and occur when a baseline-latent is exposed to some source of Corruption/Quantum. Between the end of the Aberrant War and 2104 these eruption produced low-powered (Tier II, Quantum 1-3) Novas that were consumed with Transference and usually hunted down and killed though frequently with high casualties among the hunters. After 2104 and Bhuarno's noetic "bomb," there was a chance for these Novas to emerge from their eruption with their sanity and humanity somewhat intact. But if they Hope to maintain their humanity, they'll have to be willing to Sacrifice for the good of others to build a Unity with the rest of the human race.

          In the Aberrant era Novas were rock stars, celebrities, modern day gods and goddesses (some of whom built literal cults that survive to this day). For Aeon Novas the watchwords are caution, restraint and temperance. Dormancy is almost a requirement for them and they'll tend to spend much of their time "dormed down" using their nova power only at times of great need or when they won't get caught. The Aeon Nova experience should be part X-Men (fighting for a world that fears and hates them) part The Incredible Hulk 80s TV show (Bill Bixby usually spend most of the show trying to deal with a situation until finally getting pushed too at which point a green-painted Lou Ferrigno smashes things and throws guys through plywood walls.

          While a party of Novas is possible, figure most of the time an Aeon Novas will end up as part of a group of Psions, Talents and others as someone able to deploy unique abilities that potentially have a terrible price. The Nova and his friends will need to keep his nature a secret - if not from other organizations then from the general public. At least until some suitably dramatic moment of revelation and, perhaps, acceptance.

                                  One thing I'll include is a way to reduce Permanent Transcendence. In conjunction with a QK using Transference, a Nova needs to spend double the XP to buy a point of Quantum at the same level. Once that first, highest level of Transcendence has been removed, the Nova may continue to buy Transcendence down to a minimum of 1. So, if a Nova has Transcendence 6 to begin with, the Nova needs to drop 64 xp to reduce it to 5 and 32 more to reduce it to 4, etc. Obviously it's best to lose Transcendence before it gets higher than 5 and becomes entrenched.

                                  While Talents and Psions can fall back on their powers, Nova need to develop their "human" skills/attributes to be able to take of themselves without quickly falling back on their powers. Thus they can focus on shedding Flux quickly when it does occur.
