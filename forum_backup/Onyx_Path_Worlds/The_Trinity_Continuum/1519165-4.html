                            Steve Jackson Games has a setting called GURPS Technomancer, where the premise is that Oppenheimer's quotation during the atomic bomb test accidentally completed a mystical ritual and turned the detonation into a dimensional gateway, letting magic flow into the world. I could see something very similar with “Atomic!”, except somewhat intended: while mostly forgotten, the age of Aether wasn't completely forgotten; and the Trinity Continuum counterpart to the Manhattan Project almost certainly included Talents and maybe even one or two Stalwarts; if any of them remembered the Aethergates, they might have co-opted the atomic bomb project to attempt to create a new Aethergate.

                            The result likely wouldn't have been as dramatic as what happened in GURPS Technomancer; but I could definitely see it leading to a subsequent Aether-like Atomic Age. Only in the Atomic Age, it probably wouldn't be called Aether; instead, it would be viewed as some sort of nuclear by-product.

                            My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
