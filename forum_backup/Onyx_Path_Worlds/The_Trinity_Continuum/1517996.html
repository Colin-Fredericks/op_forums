    [TC Aeon 2E fan] Europe in 2120s Redone

    07-14-2023, 09:31 PM
    I listened lately great OpCast episode on 1E book Shattered Europe – and how it’s ‘unrealistic’ in context of continent history of 2023. As I already did my more grounded take on general TC timeline, I thought I give a try, even as my loose campaign notes and maybe fan discussion starter. 😎

    Just one note – I’m just Polish guy in mid 30s, interested in history and politics of Europe, from times of our accession to EU in 2004. If you are particular countries citizen – feel free to correct me. But remember – it’s just our thought experiment to build cool, but much more realistic setting for Aeon on Old World. 😉

    Let’s start with countries most close to my heart, as I live in eastern Poland ( City of Lublin ), near Ukraine border…

    Ukraine

    Original take on country even in Aeon 2E corebook is looking real bad after Russia attack on Ukraine in 2022. We know that Ukraine simply cannot be territorial power hungry conqueror corebook is pointing it. So what it should be in new take?

    I would propose a poster child of prodemocratic changes, mixed with the granary of Europe ( which already it is ). To me Ukraine feels very much like Poland through the 1990s, where we westernize as Poles. Kyiv, NATO and EU, all points that country can access those structures after Russian war. Ukraine in 2022 marks possible recognition of same-sex unions, pressed by both war and LGBT+ activists. Main source of change comes from anti-Putin rhetoric – that Russia is one of most anti-LGBT+ countries in the world and that Federation in the war are openly declaring to ‘cleanse Ukraine from western influences’, for example, so called ‘denazification’ of Ukraine. ( Oh, irony is that President Zelensky is Ukrainian Jew himself – good joke Putin with this ‘nazification’ of government. )

    Going with Trinity themes of Hope and Unity, all those points to me that if Ukraine won with Russia – for example, in 2024 – it would straight go for EU and NATO in few years. It would probably westernize in decade or two. In this state of being, I would not be surprised for Kyiv to grants it’s citizens equal LGBT+ rights quicker than Poland, e.g. ( See notes on my homeland below. )

    The only question left is how country would answer Aberrant War? As with Poland, I suspect much of the War would miss Ukraine. It’s already beaten land by Russian war. They are rebuilding, reforming, probably doing more work they can afford to help countries in need after conflict. However, Ukraine still is much more poster child for EU values and success story in coming century – than proper expansionist we see in Aeon 2E corebook. They are a bit contrasted with Poland that Ukraine is mostly left to itself and sustaining EU dogmas as much more watcher – where Poland is active player in politics, troublemaker, probably to determent whole Union.

    Poland

    What’s weird, I totally agree with vision of Poland in Aeon 2E corebook. We already are lead by semi-fascists party from 2016 – PiS, ‘Prawo i Sprawiedliwość’, I hope we will change them in 2023 elections, finally – and this nationalistic sentiment is obvious to us in 2020s. Making Poland industry behemoth does makes sense, as country is leading in hours worked per year, even before USA in 2020s. Hard working Poles with nationalistic sentiments – and still big relations with Catholic Church, are probable future in Aeon era. ( I just hope we finally stir out of this course, on this year elections. ) Optimistic part of this vision is that Poland in 2120s is still member of European Union and by Esperanza catastrophe, is becoming more and more local EU leader.

    Vatican ( ‘Holy See’ )

    As raised as Catholic ( even if being now much more Agnostic personally ) I cannot imagine moving papal capitol to other place on Earth from Rome. It’s heart of Catholicism for over 1500 years. However, I can easily imagine that 2120s pope is having ‘summer residence’ on Luna – and that Moon base is de facto second seat of power in Earths near orbit. Even in Luna Rising 1E book, Basicilica of Ascension is pointed as greatest point of Churches vanity and hubris in canon timeline. ( Luna Rising, page 94 ) Or even that majority of popes in 22nd century comes from Sudoamerica or UAN countries, as there is already majority of cardinals from those places in our real 2020s.

    If we want to be really controversial about Catholicism in 2120s, I would make newest pope a women. Catholicism is one of most doctrinal churches in the world. Already in 2020s there are talks about priesthood of women – and it’s points that in coming decades, women can really takes power in Catholic Church. Having newest pope being first women would really show changes in this organisation. ( To contrast, I would probably in protestant churches already accepting women cleary in 2020s to push for inclusion of trans and non-binary people in 2120s to still point that Vatican, even if reformed, is still much behind existing times. As it was almost always the case. )

      Germany is facing a demographic challenge in the coming decade. They are going to have more retirees than workers. That will seriously disrupt them.

      Egypt is going to effect Europe big time. The Nile Delta is in crisis. First the Aswan Dam has cut off the silt flow to the Delta eliminating the source of the delta's soil renewal. The Aswan Dam has also reduced the flow of the Nile in Lower Egypt for several reasons. The flow of river water through a delta protects it from erosion. With less protection from erosion and less new soil the Nile Delta is eroding quickly. Add sea level rise to that and Egypt is losing its best farmland. That means between twenty to fifty million Egyptians will need to leave Egypt or starve.

      I don't see Europe, or anyplace else, taking in tens of millions of refugees in anything like todays political climate. The fallout of such a crisis will be a big issue.

      Ireland never gets a mention in the books. It should get attention. If it's under British jack boots, say so. If it is an independent nation, its location would make it important to multiple factions. Ireland needs at least a couple of paragraphs.
