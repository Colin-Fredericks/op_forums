      Sure, though I'll spare you the ones I've written that are too specific to my game world.

      Origin Paths
      Former Experimental Subject
      Skills: Culture, Humanities, Science, Technology
      Edges: Ambidextrous, Artifact, Covert, Hair Trigger Reflexes, Hardy, Striking
      Gifts: Stamina, Culture, Technology
      (must take 2 dots in Covert at first dot in path)

      International Traveler
      Skills: Athletics, Culture, Humanities, Survival
      Edges: Alternate Identity, Dual Citizenship, Fame, Free Running, Safe House, Wealth
      Gifts: Presence, Culture, Humanities

      Role Paths

      Treasure Hunter
      Skills: Culture, Enigmas, Humanities, Larceny
      Edges: Dual Citizenship, Artifact, Weak Spots, Speed Reading, Adrenaline Spike, Library, Direction Sense, Always Prepared
      Gifts: Cunning, Enigmas, Larceny

      Research Specialist
      Skills: Enigmas, Humanities, Science, Technology
      Edges: Library, Patron, Weak Spots, Photographic Memory, Speed Reading, Artistic Talent, Keen Sense
      Gifts: Intellect, Enigmas, Humanities

      Society Paths

      US Army Ranger
      Skills: Aim, Athletics, Close Combat, Survival
      Edges: Always Prepared, Hardy, Iron Will, Small Unit Tactics, Breath Control, Hair Trigger Reflexes, Tough Cookie
      Gifts: Aim, Close Combat, Survival

      Freelance Reporter
      Skills: Culture, Humanities, Integrity, Persuasion
      Edges: Dual Citizenship, Photographic Memory, Library, Speed Reading, Skilled Liar, Safe House, Keen Sense, Weak Spots, Fame
      Gifts: Empathy, Humanities, Persuasion

      Tech Industry Insider
      Skills: Culture, Integrity, Persuasion, Technology
      Edges: Fame, Lightning Calculator, Patron, Skilled Liar, Wealth
      Gifts: Culture, Persuasion, Technology

      Hacker Collective
      Skills: Enigmas, Integrity, Persuasion, Technology
      Edges: Forgery (2), Darkest Web (2), Conspirators (3), Bolthole (3), Safehouse, Wealth (5)
      Gifts: Enigmas, Persuasion, Technology

      Professional Thief
      Skills: Athletics, Close Combat, Integrity, Larceny
      Edges: Always Prepared, Polyglot, Criminal Contact (2), Fame
      Gifts: Athletics, Integrity, Larceny

      They're really easy to do as you need them. I hope these give you some ideas. They should all be legal, but I don't promise there aren't errors.

                          Because I had some time on my hands, a few Origin paths. Some of these are mild variations of the existing ones, but others are new.

                          Rural (Origin)
                          You grew up far from the urban world, be it a family farm, ranch lands, or some other isolated homestead. Neighbors were few and far between, but your family was likely close knit. You know how to take care of yourself and your things.
                          Connections: Members of your extended family, the County sheriff, Animal veterinarians, Local militia,
                          Skills: Aim, Athletics, Integrity, Survival
                          Edges: Always Prepared, Animal Ken, Danger Sense, Direction Sense, Hardy, Keen Sense, Sniper, Swift, Tough Cookie

                          Small Town (Origin)
                          You come from a small town or village, a place where everyone knows each other (for better or worse), going back a long ways.
                          Connections: Town government, Local librarian, Former neighbors, Town newspaper, Family doctor
                          Skills: Empathy, Humanities, Persuasion, Technology
                          Edges: Artistic Talent, Big Hearted, Iron Will, Ms. Fix-It, Skilled Liar, Swift, Tough Cookie, Weak Spots

                          Working Class (Origin)
                          From a proud line of factory workers, miners, or other skilled labor, you grew up respecting hard work and people who do it. Both home and co-workers formed the backbone of your life.
                          Connections: Family members, Labor and trade unions, Local store owners, Labor activist lawyers
                          Skills: Command, Culture, Empathy, Technology
                          Edges: Artistic Talent, Big Hearted, Iron Will, Ms. Fix-It, Patron, Tough Cookie, Weak Spots

                          Child of the Streets (Origin)
                          Be it as a runaway, a product of neglectful parents, or some other reason, you made your way through the meaner corners of the city while growing up. What you might lack in formal education, you make up for with street smarts and tenacity.
                          Connections: Homeless shelter, Street gang, Former mentor, Police officer, Medical clinic
                          Skills: Athletics, Enigmas, Larceny, Survival
                          Edges: Adrenaline Spike, Alternate Identity, Danger Sense, Free Running, Hair Trigger Reflexes, Skilled Liar, Small Unit Tactics, Tough Cookie

                          Suburbia (Origin)
                          While not necessarily the white picket fences and green lawns of stereotypes, you grew up in a fairly descent neighborhood with a financially stable family.
                          Connections: Life-long best friend, Former teacher, Family member, Former classmates with interesting jobs
                          Skills: Culture, Empathy, Humanities, Technology
                          Edges: Artistic Talent, Big Hearted, Library, Patron, Wealth

                          Life of Privilege (Origin)
                          You were born among the elite and raised that way, with the best schools and best toys. Outside of the US, this can easily include a nation's nobility or other heirs to power.
                          Connections: Family business interests, School alumni, Political groups with ties to your family, Family lawyer or financial manager,
                          Skills: Command, Culture, Integrity, Persuasion
                          Edges: Fame, Patron, Skilled Liar, Striking, Wealth


                          More later.

                              And more.

                              Latch Key Kid (Origin)
                              Because of either a single parent or both parents working, you mainly learned to raise yourself (and perhaps your siblings) from a young age. While still having many of the benefits of a stable home, you were able to take advantage of your freedom to live an interesting life.
                              Connections: Landlord, Local busybody, Life-long Best friend, Siblings, Neighbor who kept an eye on you, Local oddball
                              Skills: Integrity, Larceny, Persuasion, Survival
                              Edges: Always Prepared, Animal Ken, Big Hearted, Iron Will, Hardy, Ms. Fix It, Skilled Liar, any one Sense edge

                              Underdog (Origin)
                              You were born behind the eight ball, so to speak. Be it chronic illness, being the runt of the litter, or just constant bad luck, you always had to work overtime to get what you wanted and be successful.
                              Connections: Social worker, Local priest, Former trainer or teacher
                              Skills: Athletics OR Close Combat, Culture, Empathy, Integrity
                              Edges: Adrenaline Spike, Big Hearted, Hair Trigger Reflexes, Iron Will, Patron, Swift, Weak Spots, Free Running OR Martial Arts

                              Prodigy (Origin)
                              A born genius in your field, your special talents were encouraged and cultivated from a young age.
                              Connections: Prominent think tank or lab, Former teacher, Other luminaries in your chosen field
                              Skills: Enigmas, Medicine, Science, Technology
                              Edges: Covert OR Fame, Library, Lightning Calculator, Patron, Photographic Memory, Speed Reading

                              Bookworm (Origin)
                              You grew up with a love of books and the worlds they opened up to you.
                              Connections: Famous author, Professional researcher, Rare book collector
                              Skills: Culture, Enigmas, Humanities, Science
                              Edges: Artistic Talent, Iron Will, Library, Lightning Calculator, Photographic Memory, Speed Reading

                              Jock (Origin)
                              Sports and athletics have always been your thing.
                              Connections: Former teammates, Sports doctor, Former coach
                              Skills: Athletics (required), Command, Culture, Medicine
                              Edges: Adrenaline Spike, Big Hearted, Hair Trigger Reflexes, Iron Will, Small Unit Tactics, Swift, Tough Cookie, Free Running OR Martial Arts

                                  Once more into the breach.

                                  Nomadic (Origin)
                                  You grew up moving from place to place, perhaps as a military brat or as a member of a traditional nomadic culture. Either way, you've been a lot of different places and no how to adjust to new circumstances.
                                  Connections: Extended family members, Fellow travelers, Smugglers,
                                  Skills: Culture, Larceny, Pilot, Survival
                                  Edges: Covert, Hair Trigger Reflexes, Iron Will, Patron, Skilled Liar, Tough Cookie, any one Sense edge

                                  Primitive (Origin)
                                  You come from a pre-industrial culture apart from much of the modern world. This includes certain religious and other sects who shun modern technology.
                                  Connections: Fellow tribe members, Government agencies dealing with your people, Anthropologist
                                  Skills: Aim, Integrity, Medicine, Survival
                                  Edges: Animal Ken, Breath Control, Covert, Danger Sense, Direction Sense, Hardy, Keen Sense, Small Unit Tactics, Swift

                                  War Torn (Origin)
                                  You grew up in a war zone or in a region very recently impacted by conflict, and learned at an early age how to survive and potentially fight back.
                                  Connections: Refugees, Non-Government Agencies, Soldiers, Black Market
                                  Skills: Aim, Close Combat, Medicine, Survival
                                  Edges: Adrenaline Spike, Always Prepared, Covert, Danger Sense, Hair Trigger Reflexes, Safe House, Small Unit Tactics OR Cool Under Fire, any one Style edge

                                  Monastery (Origin)
                                  Raised among a religious cloister. While this is mainly meant for various Eastern traditions, it can just as easily cover certain Western orders as well.
                                  Connections: Monastery leader, Fellow monks/nuns, Religious scholars
                                  Skills: Athletics, Close Combat, Enigmas, Integrity
                                  Edges: Breath Control, Covert, Iron Will, Keen Sense, any one Martial Arts edge, Swift, Tough Cookie, Weak Spots

                                  Ward (Origin)
                                  You were brought up by someone other than your family, be it a state agency, wealthy billionaire, or other surrogate.
                                  Connections: Former guardian, Social worker, Lawyer, Fellow wards
                                  Skills: Athletics, Empathy, Integrity, Larceny
                                  Edges: Alternate Identity, Danger Sense, Patron, Safe House, Skilled Liar, Wealth, Weak Spots

                                  Cult (Origin)
                                  You were raised (indoctrinated) by a group at odds with normal society, be it for religious or political reasons, and trained to carry out their doctrine and goals. You may or may not still be a member of said group, depending on circumstances.
                                  Connections: Cult leader, Former cult members, Deprogrammer,
                                  Skills: Aim, Integrity, Persuasion, Survival
                                  Edges: Alternate Identity, Covert, Hardy OR Iron Will, Skilled Liar, Small Unit Tactics, any one Style edge

                                  Alien (Origin)
                                  You are ... not of this world. Possibly from another world, another time, or another reality, or perhaps just so odd that you have never fit in with any normal society.
                                  Connections: Fringe scientist, Conspiracy theorist, Rogue government agent
                                  Skills: Empathy, Enigmas, Integrity, Science OR Technology
                                  Edges: Alternate Identity, Covert, Keen Sense, Striking, Artifact, Superior Trait

                                  And that's it for Origins. Next is Roles.

                                  What is tolerance? It is the consequence of humanity. We are all formed of frailty and error; let us pardon reciprocally each other's folly. That is the first law of nature.
                                  Voltaire, "Tolerance" (1764)

                                      Some alternate and/or new Role paths.

                                      Ace (Role)
                                      Be it land, sea, or air, you are a master pilot at one with your chosen vehicle (or perhaps any vehicle).
                                      Connections: Fellow pilots, Former clients, Mechanic, Airport/Garage/Pier owner, Vehicle manufacturer
                                      Skills: Aim, Command, Pilot (required), Technology
                                      Edges: Adrenaline Spike, Ambidextrous, Always Prepared, Cool Under Fire, Direction Sense, Hair Trigger Reflexes, Lightning Calculator, Ms. Fix It, Patron

                                      Survivalist (Role)
                                      You can survive and even thrive in the harshest of conditions with little or no support.
                                      Connections: Fellow outdoorsmen, Cryptozoologist, Conspiracy theorist, Native guide, Gear manufacturer
                                      Skills: Medicine, Survival, any two of Aim, Athletics OR Close Combat
                                      Edges: Always Prepared, Animal Ken, Covert, Direction Sense, Hardy, Iron Will, Keen Sense, Swift

                                      Everyperson (Role)
                                      Just your average, everyday normal person.
                                      Connections: Neighbors, Coworkers, Family members,
                                      Skills: Culture, Empathy, Integrity, Humanities OR Technology
                                      Edges: Always Prepared, Animal Ken, Big Hearted, Covert, Cool Under Fire, Direction Sense, Swift, Weak Points, any 3 point edge

                                      Occultist (Role)
                                      You delve into the mysteries of magic, the paranormal, and other areas beyond everyday life.
                                      Connections: Fellow seekers, Parapsychology group, Anthropologist, Professional psychic/shaman
                                      Skills: Culture, Enigmas, Integrity, Command OR Persuasion
                                      Edges: Alternate Identity, Breath Control, Covert OR Fame, Danger Sense, Iron Will, Library, Safe House,

                                      Traveler (Role)
                                      An old hand at international travel, you've been nearly everywhere and can easily find your footing in any new location.
                                      Connections: Pilot, Ship captain, Jet Setters, International journalists, Local guide, Diplomat,
                                      Skills: Culture, Empathy, Persuasion, Pilot OR Survival
                                      Edges: Alternate Identity, Always Prepared, Animal Ken, Direction Sense, Hardy, Safe House, Wealth

                                      Pillar (Role)
                                      Be it as assistant, sidekick, or any other role, you are the rock your group can always rely on.
                                      Connections: Household or other support staff, Lawyer, Doctor, Martial arts instructor, Lifelong Friend,
                                      Skills: Athletics, Empathy, Integrity, any ONE of Close Combat, Medicine, OR Pilot
                                      Edges: Adrenaline Spike, Always Prepared, Danger Sense, Big Hearted, Patron, Martial Arts, Safe House, Swift, any ONE of Cool Under Fire, Ms Fix It, OR Skilled Liar

                                      What is tolerance? It is the consequence of humanity. We are all formed of frailty and error; let us pardon reciprocally each other's folly. That is the first law of nature.
                                      Voltaire, "Tolerance" (1764)
