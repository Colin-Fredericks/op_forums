            I’ve been trying to think of a succinct way of explaining it without getting bogged down in too much detail.

            A latent is what happens when you have a human with the potential to develop psionic power, but who has no outlet for that power. The potential builds to an extent, but eventually stops. It’s an evolutionary dead end (psionically), but someone has given humanity hacks to get around it.

            A psiad is what happens when you have a human with the potential to develop psionic power, *and* the means to express that power. As the power builds, they learn to express it. They don’t become latents.

                        That's... very different then what they were in 1e... and I'll be honest... I'm not sure if I like the change or not. I'm going to need to think about this for a bit.
                        IIRC the only thing that 1st Edition had to say on the matter was the following text, from the APG, p. 58: A nova doesn't develop psi talent; he grows an M-R node. Conversely, a psych won't develop a node; he possesses latency, which may later develop into psychic power.

                        That definitely implies that you first become a latent, and can then become either a psion or a psiad. However, just like Trinity 1e used Taint interchangeably for the two traits Aberrant 1e called Taint and Quantum, any discussion of psychic capability in an Aberrant book should be taken with a grain of salt. In the context of Aberrant, the details are irrelevant.

                        So technically it's a change from how it was presented in 1e, but it's not what I would personally call "very different." We're just tweaking the terminology a bit.

                        This is hardly the first change we've made from 1e (just wait until you see the Setting Secrets chapter), and it certainly won't be the last. But, as we keep pointing out, this is a continuum: if you don't like our answer, you can use your own, and that's fine by us.

                                    Originally posted by TheSarcasticSage View Post
                                    That's... very different then what they were in 1e... and I'll be honest... I'm not sure if I like the change or not. I'm going to need to think about this for a bit.
                                    It's a nuanced change, but not a significantly different one, and I can see how it could work conceptually.

                                    Here's a (possibly terrible) analogy: think of a chicken egg.

                                    The Psaid chicken egg matures, grows, and eventually the chick inside gains some sense of mobility and awareness. At that point, it breaks the shell and becomes a Psiad Chick. It's young, it's weak, but it has the potential to some day slowly grow to become a Psiad Chicken.

                                    The Psion chicken egg also matures, grows, and gains some sense of mobility and awareness. However, on its own it lacks the strength to break the shell. So there's a chick inside, but it'll never develop, or grow, or erupt on its own. It might occasionally wiggle around; maybe it even learns to roll a little. But that's it. It is, then, a Latent Chicken.

                                    What the Doyen did was find all of those Latent Chickens, and give them a dose of Psi-Chicken Gene Therapy. (PCGT; powerful stuff!) That genetic bump force-grew their legs, their wings, and effectively *punched* them out of the shell. The resulting freaky chicken is a Psion.

                                    The reason why starting Psions have stronger powers, potential, and even Psi than Psiad is that the PCGT specifically works like Psi steroids; it bulks up their abilities and powers, at the cost of, uh, "shriveling" some other very important things.

                                            Going back to the original post, I'm considering the possibility of a fifth option: Psiads resemble Mesmerists more than they do Psions. That is, Psiads pick up individual psi powers in a haphazard manner that's more like how Talents select their Gifts than how Psions put dots into Modes: you can, say, pick up Sensory Manipulation without having to pick up Orientation or Dowsing first.

                                            During character creation, a psiad gets more dots to spend on powers than a psion does (say, ten instead of four); but the powers are more expensive: it takes one more dot than the power's rating to pick it up. So a basic power costs one dot, a one-dot power costs two dots, a two-dot power costs three dots, and a three-dot power costs four dots.

                                            While the Prometheus Chamber amplifies a psion's power, it also structures and restricts it, limiting the psion to the Modes of one Aptitude but allowing him to advance beyond three dots within those Modes. Psiads don't have that restriction, and his choices aren't limited to what Modes provide. [This is to allow for the importing of Psychic Knacks from Adventure! that don't match up with any of the powers available to psions.] Their choices aren't totally unfettered, though: you still need Psi 3+ in order to pick up Quantakinetic or Teleport powers, and there may be further restrictions on which Aptitudes are readily available to the psiad.

                                            My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                                                        Ok, lets take a moment and go with the egg analogy. An unfertilized egg would be a Baseline or Talent. The things that breaks the analogy, are 1) Without an Eruption Event, perfectly viable "Eggs" to use this example, will never reach their potential "active" selves, 2) Until Eruption, both viable, and non-viable fertilized "eggs" will show as Latent to testing, because until they erupt, by definition, they are latent. It's what they word means... "lying dormant or hidden until circumstances are suitable for development or manifestation." 3) Significant exposure to PSI/Quantum energies can and do trigger "Latents" into becoming full fledged members of the energy type (Psiad/Nova). Therefore there really is no such thing as non-viable fertilized eggs in this example, there are just some that require more "oomph" to trigger then others. However I agree that right now in Aeon, some peoples potential may be so miniscule that they require spiritual castration (Prometheus Chamber) to trigger (Psions), but, in theory, if the circumstances were right (Eruption Event), including "enough" of the right energy (PSI/Quantum) with the right (PSI/Quantum) genetic marker, anyone could trigger and go full active.

                                                        tl:dr - In 1e, the Nova/PSI genetic markers were the exact same until triggered, and in order to get a full Nova/Psiad, all you needed were 1) the right genetic marker (ie. If you are missing #1 you are a baseline or Talent, if you are missing either #' 2 and/or 3, then you are a latent.
