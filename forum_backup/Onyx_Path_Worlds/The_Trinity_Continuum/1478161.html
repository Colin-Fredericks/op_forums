    Character Creation for TC 2E

    04-06-2022, 03:09 PM
    So I'm preparing to run TC multiera chronicle - one story in each TC era. However, I did not know mechanics of TC, for now. I have run Scion 2E and They Came From a few years now, but I suspect some difference from TC will come around. Best way for me to learn mechanics to new system is to create characters - so here is topic for Character Creations in TC. 😎

    I will start with my Adventure! example NPC - Thule member occultists I want to send against my players. Yes, I know most of Storypath antagonists are made by using templates - but I like make major story antagonists like PCs characters. It gives them depth and makes me learn mechanics sorta like players will in play.

    Let's start with...

    Ferdinand 'Franz' Herz - Adventure! era Villain
    Concept: It's early 1923. Franz was lower class nobleman in times of German Empire ( till 1918 ), He was entrenched in Völkisch movement occultism from his youth. Got rather good, but mainly lonely life with unloving parents and large 'duties to his homeland'. He still has resentment for crumbling of First Reich in Great War and more and more with Weimar Republic, as forced country made by WW 1 victors, on ashes of once Empire,

    He joined Thule Society in early 1918, to explore human capabilities and 'make Germany great again'. Quickly disillusioned with Republic and Thule itself - at start of 1920s, he traveled world to find the Übermensch possible source - he came to Far East. He is now fascinated by the rumors of people having Sight into the Future. And he is sensing in his dreams future problems with one word in his mind - Aeon...

    Franz Aspirations:
    Short – Find ‘Future Sight’ relic ( to create Übermensch with it )
    Short – Show ‘Aryan superiority’ over others
    Long – Find the miracle for Übermensch ( now probably something like Clairsentience of ISRA from TC Aeon )

    Let's deep dive into rules then...

    Character Creation Summary

    Step One: Concept
    Checked ✅
    To Aspirations I will return later on...

    Step Two: Paths
    Checked ✅
    Choose your character’s Origin, Role, and Society Paths. Gain 3 Skill dots and 2 Edge dots from each Path.

    Franz has:
    Origin Path - Occultist
    Role Path - Mastermind
    Society Path - Thule ( See below )

    Franz Paths Edges

    Origin Path - Occultist - Iron Will (*), Polyglot (*)
    Role Path - Mastermind - Outsized Personality (*), Photographic Memory (*)
    Society - Thule Society - Useful Esoterica (*), Library (*)

    Step Three: Skills, Skill Tricks, and Specialties
    Checked ✅
    Assign six additional Skill dots, and pick Specialties for any Skills with a high enough rating, and gain one Skill Trick in a Skill rated three or higher.

    Franz has Skills:
    Aim 1, Athletics 1, Command 1, Culture 3, Enigmas 2, Empathy 3, Humanities 2, Integrity 2.

    Step Four: Attributes
    Checked ✅
    Assign your Attribute dots and select your character’s favored approach.

    Intellect 3 Cunning 4 Resolve 3
    Might 1 Dexterity 3 Stamina 2
    Presence 2 Manipulation 4 Composure 2

    Step Five: Moment of Inspiration
    Checked ✅
    Decide how the moment of Inspiration occurred, and whether the character became a daredevil, stalwart, or mesmerist.

    Moment - Induced Inspiration ( from TC Adventure! )
    Franz was experimenting with dangerous occult techniques to break the barrier that separate him from his true potential as ‘Uber(Manch)Mesmerist’! 😮
    Associated Attribute: Resolve

    Franz Attributes now…
    Intellect 3 Cunning 4 Resolve 4
    Might 1 Dexterity 3 Stamina 2
    Presence 2 Manipulation 4 Composure 2

    Step Six: Add Advantages
    Facets, Gifts, etc.

    Step Seven: Final Touches
    Calculate Health and Defense ratings, as detailed on p.38 of the Trinity Continuum Core.

    Assign one bonus dot to any Attribute, and spend four dots of Edges — these Edges can come from outside your character’s Paths, but she must meet any other requirements normally.

    Facets and Inspiration

          Thanks for the title... maybe I can get some scale in my future actions using it... haha

          While TC has a more "solid set" of Paths, it doesnt mean you are locked on them, so, if you are creating a character that is halfway between role paths, there is nothing that forbides you from creating your own paths, so you can build a path that is a mix of Mastermind and a Fixer. In your case specifically, I would go for his more active aspect, that is Mastermind, and maybe use the Fixer idea as part of the Occultist origin (you must do a lot of things to survive as an Occultist).

          Now, for your society, I like the Aeon's Evil Twin idea, mirroring the entire society path. Daredevils gifts keywords are skills, attributes and luck, and Luck is not an option for path keywords, so pick 3 skills (from the 4 above) and you are done, my suggestion, Culture, Enigmas and Humanities.

                                      Maybe you got it, but didn’t use, but you have 3 dots to spend on each path, not 3 skills with 1 dot in each, for example, you could go for Occultist giving Humanity 3. You spend it evenly, what is not bad, I just want to make it clear you have more options.

                                      Enigmas is more about mathematics, codes and logic, while myths and legends would be more related to culture (as I see). Humanities, at least for me, is more about human behavior, psychology, things like that, while Culture is more focused on the general knowledge related to beliefs, legends, traditions and popular knowledge.

                                      In other words, you probably would need to focus on Culture to get a general knowledge about occultism, but you may find some occultist aspects in other skills, like a lot of Egyptian and Greek traditions are related to the principles of mathematics (geometry, mainly), so a character with high Enigmas may know a lot about ancient Greeks and Egyptians tricks. It may happen with any skill actually, Enigmas related to the story of numbers, numerology, secret codes, etc, Science may have some information related to astrology and seasonal traditions, Humanities may have informations about how people behave in certain places due to their traditions, etc.

                                      The small number of skills in Storypath is a very interesting thing that gives a lot of space to play. I really love it.

                                              Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                                              2. Do I get it right that Enigmas Skill can work for semi-Occult Skill in Trinity? The knowledge of myths and legends would be in the Humanites Skill - but 'I understand that three turning in the left and pulling this light source summons this being' - would be much more Enigmas Skill?
                                              In addition to math and codes, Enigmas is good for solving riddles and puzzles, so it could be useful for some sorts of occult rolls, since many mystic texts are likely written in purposefully obscure ways to keep the knowledge out of the hands of the uninitiated. On the other hand, if you're dealing with a famous riddle (such as the riddle of the sphinx), you could roll Humanities, Culture, or Enigmas, since they'd all apply. As a rule of thumb, I'd say that any roll to remember a fact you've read would benefit from Humanities or Culture, while a roll to figure out something new would use Enigmas.
