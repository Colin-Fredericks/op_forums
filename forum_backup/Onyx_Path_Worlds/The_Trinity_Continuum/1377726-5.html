                            Originally posted by pawsplay View Post
                            So where in the rules does it say how to stat up weapons for characters without using the Wealth tag?
                            It doesn’t. But building weapons with Wealth dot costs, and acquiring weapons via Paths, are two different things that don’t have to be the same. Or to put it another way, you can make a weapon that costs 3 dots. A character with Wealth 3 can purchase that weapon. Another character with a relevant Path can use the Path to gain acquire the weapon without needing to purchase it. Once the game starts, you’re using Path Access to get the weapon. If you want, you could apply a Difficulty or Complication to the roll, to represent the high cost or rarity of the weapon/item you’re trying to acquire. But before the game starts, there are no rolls and you don’t need to test whether you get access - you just get things that make sense for your Path.

                            I’m trying to approach this from the point of view that you’re genuinely confused, but your attitude comes across as being deliberately ignorant.
