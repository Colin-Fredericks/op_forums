                                            The Skills need to be standardized. Firearms as Aim only works until the Deva show up and want to use a bow with Athletics because that is their Pantheon Path.

                                            The Scale chart is slightly different between the two games (off by one in direct comparisons). When directly importing antagonists, update their stats accordingly.

                                            Followers appear on both the Edge and Birthright lists and work the same way, which is an issue. That said, Birthrights have been hinted at growing in power as you rise in tier so...

                                            The interaction between the Wealth edge and the Prosperity Purview needs to be considered if not addressed.

                                            Crafting has minor differences, but it is easy saying baselines and Inspired can't make magic items above tier one and Legendary beings can only venture as far as Advanced Science (not Inspired or Powered).

                                            Scions and Denizens can generate their own Scale with their Titles and Deeds. While this won't be a common occurrence, it does mean they can overcome obstacles unexpectedly compared to Trinity characters of the same tier.

                                            As for the concerns about the limited shelf life of Scion before they ascend, I'd argue you could just not let them move up a tier until it is less of an issue. By all means, give them the exp for completing deeds but Legend basically goes up via Fiat anyway. There is not a lot of 'sideways' growth in Scion but Edges and the ability to purchase hew Paths in Trinity would help a lot with that.

                                            Thoughts ripple out, birthing others

                                                The thing about scale and powers in Scion and Trinity is not about the frequency but about flexibility.

                                                Scion is flexible, basically if it’s sounds good it works as desired, because that’s how myths work. Booms, miracles and uses of titles are ABSOLUTELY flexible, just pick the proper point of view or motif and Bang, it works. Novas CAN do that as power stunts... well, novas can do anything... but they are on Demigod Tier, so it’s expected from them.

                                                Psions in the other hand, are very focused on their powers, it’s not like they are completely restrained, but they can’t go too far away from the center point, even combining modes. Talents are flexible in the dramatic edition, more restricted by the gifts, but they lack the scale capacities of heroes, at least comparing to L3-4 heroes.

                                                Honestly I really want to see what Ian and the devs will bring in Aegis, it’s probably the closest we are going to get from a cross over...
