    Less Plot, More Choices, Please?

    11-02-2013, 01:03 AM
    I really hope that Trinity.2 is going to be less plot-based than Trinity itself was. Part of that means it'd be really awesome to have all nine Psi Orders in the corebook, fully playable. Then if you want to have optional metaplot stuff like the loss of contact with the Extrasolar Colonies, the destruction of the Chibs and the disappearance of the Upeo, that's awesome! But please, let the Storytellers decide what material they do and don't want in their games instead of writing everything into the books.

    With each setting being distinct in time, a leap of almost 100 years between Adventure!, Aberrant and Aeon, obviously there are going to be some setting assumptions. The Age of Adventure! evolved into the Nova Age and the Novas went insane and ushered in the post-apocalyptic-new-world of the Psions. That's cool. It'd just be nice for the settings to stay pretty static instead of having things like the Upeo returning in the "middle" of the gameline. It's even weird that there is a "middle" of the game line. Like it's a novel and you have to buy all the "ploty" books to understand the "real" setting. Can we just cram all the default assumptions into the cores, have as many playable options as possible then release some of those awesome and badass tiny-tiny books like the Field Reports that have "metaplot" updates? I'd be all for a book about how to run the Chitra Bhanu Purge, or the Disappearance of the Teleporters. I just would prefer that to be optional stuff. I get that it adds tension to the plot.. but I'd rather be the one adding tension to the plot that I made for my players instead of following the plot the writers are creating for everyone.

    I'm a professor! Why is no one listening to me?!

              Groups that aren't playable at first and become playable later exist for 2 reasons within an RPG.
              1. To sell their inevitable splat book that details them and their powers.
              2. because the writers haven't found a worth while way to use said groups correctly within the established setting books.

              I'd like to believe that Ian is going to run this ship correctly. part of running it correctly is making it have some ability to make profit. that means, on some level, selling splat books. I dunno what the Roi for splats with just adventure material in them..but I'm sure it's low enough that most of those books sell on drive thru for less than 10 bucks each. If they hold back a group that might be playable at some point, then people who want to play that group will purchase the books, and thus more money for both the game line, and the company, more perceived interest, and more books get written.

              It seems unfair maybe, to not have every group in the entire game available from the start, but it's been that way for years within the RPG industry, and I'm not sure why we're holding OP/WW to any sort of different standard.

              Hey man, be thankful he's not making the Aeonverse D20..

                                                          I sort of had the understanding that the backstory of the new Trinity Continuum would not be exactly the same as the old. Perhaps not as different as say CWoD and NWoD, but different none the less.

                                                          Either way since I've sort of gotten the impression that part of the goal with the new Trinity line is to create a setting where you can run games at pretty much ANY point in the timeline of the universe, I would hope that if certain groups have been purged in the default time setting (Chitra Bhanu got wiped out say) that the rules crunch necessary to use them would still be included in the GM section or as an appendix in the back of the book.

                                                          This was something that really bothered me in Æon since it was later heavily implied (at least to my reading) that pretty much ALL the orders saved/hide a couple Quantakinetics after the purge, which meant that there were at least a few left for use as plot characters, but no rules for their powers...


                                                          All other things aside, one of my favorite parts of NWoD with I hope will be carried over to Trinity Continuum, is the persistence of the "Here's a major plot things, now here are Three viable options, no single one is the absolute correct canon answer nor ever will be." The VII supplement for V:tR is my ideal for all things dealing with stuff that in CWoD would have been part of the Meta Plot.


                                                          Something like a Chitra Bhanu source book like this.
                                                          Chapter 1: Option, the Chibs are all dead. Details of the purge. Plot element: the order heads keep this very hush hush, but the Prometheus Chamber of an order is tied metaphysically to EVERY psion empowered by it. The Chibs are all dead because the other Proxies siezed the Chitra chamber and created a feedback overload that literally cooked all the Quantakinetics brains in the skulls. Further details on Prometheus chambers, further details on fall out from genociding an order, plot seeds for "Oh crud, someone stole the Legion Chamber and now I have a headache"

                                                          Chapter 2: Option, the Chibs were purged, BUT a small number survived, both escapees who were away on secret assignments, and a few kept by other orders as pets/researchers/weapons of last resort. Details on Quantakinetics as a set of powers that basically all revolve around "Interfere with OR bolster Nova powers". Chibs of this type are only useful in a situation specifically involving direct contact with Novas.

                                                          Chapter 3: Option, the purge happened, but it went bad and 90% of the Chibs escaped. The Proxy EITHER managed to escape with the Prometheus chamber OR the Chamber was captured by the other orders but STOLEN BACK by the aid of a rogue teleporter. Details on Quantakinetics as a set of power that allow Chibs to MIMIC Nova Powers. Chibs of this type are, with a bit of effort, able to manifest Mega-stats, some quantum powers, and other nova abilities for short times. They also, however, generate Taint when using Nova powers. Perhaps MORE than an actual nova. These Chibs are scared, angry, on the run, and just about ready and able to visit their wrath on the world for their betrayal. And they might just decide that Novas aren't so bad after all...
