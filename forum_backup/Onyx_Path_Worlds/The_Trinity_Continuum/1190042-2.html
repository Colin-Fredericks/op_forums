        I like the idea of having characters encounter a Max Mercer who doesn't have control of when he'll shift, and so mostly just hands off information for others to do good with, or ties to learn as much as he can to use after his next shift - but also sometimes meet a more zen Max that appears to have perfect awareness of his movement in time (if not true control, his perspective of causality may have lifted the illusion of control...) and is more enigmatic and less direct in his interventions.

Buddha of Time Max is after he finally makes peace with his relationship with time and achieves understanding of his own causality (Tier 4 paramorph).

                                        Using the whole humans going/building a dyson sphere led me to this idea.

                                        Basically in the modern day Earth there is an Inspired scientist who announces the establishment of a project to build a rocket carrier designed to serve as a method of transport for a collection of scientists, explorers, and colonists to a new world beyond Earth's solar system. Some of the lowkey funding for this operation is done by the Aeon Society, though that is more of a nod nod wink wink.

                                        The rocket carrier is populated by say a few thousand people in suspension - which uses Inspired developed technology - and blasts off as predicted. Something happens, an explosion occurs on the quantum level and the rocket is lost, believed to be destroyed. Earth gets bathed in quantum energies and untold numbers of novas appear.Of course that is just a bit of side information, not the major point of the campaign.

                                        The ship itself survived the explosion, and being bathed in the Telluric (which I know isn't its own energy but its a good word to describe how the ship got hit by both quantum and subquantum/noetic energy( and actually began traveling through a warp tunnel/ Hours, maybe a day or so later, it came out in the orbit of a dyson sphere, though it quickly lost orbit and crashed on the planet. Thanks to the Inspired sciences that were part of its design it survived more or less intact, though its engines are damaged to unusability. Once matters stabilize the command crew wakes up the first-in crew (which is the PCs) and operations are launched to try and figure out what is going on in this alien world. Thankfully the Inspired scientist is still around and whole he declares he doesn't have the resources to rebuild the ship he can, if given proper materials, create new and innovative tools and equipment and even buildings.

                                        In time the PCs, and the command crew and any colonists that are awaken, all begin to exhibit signs of change,of awakenings. This will lead to the formations of Talents, Stalwarts, and Mesmerists - though names can be changed as needed with the note that eventually some will become Novas and Psiads.

                                        The campaign is exploration and survey at first, but also can contain resource gathering and society building since the few thousand people in suspension need a place to live and work when awoken. Thankfully the dyson sphere they landed on has a Earth like atmosphere and copious amounts of plant and animal life, as well as numerous other mysterious - like who built it and where are they now.
