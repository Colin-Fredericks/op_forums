    Evolved psiads.

    10-14-2021, 11:14 PM
    Let's assume that a doyen for some reason created a prometheus chamber to just generally enhance the psionic capability of humanity, a psiad based chamber, applying the same boost psions got. Or, it you want, it was a crazy alien gadget, or maybe even the bad cheese someone ate last night.

    But ultimately, what would "evolved" psiads look like? how would they differ from psions, if both were in the same setting?

    My first thought is that they still don't get the easy ways to combine powers psions get, so that even if they can access higher tiers of power than "natural" psiads, they still run into issues compared to a group of psions working together. Another possibility might be higher limit in terms of dot cap for various psionic modes.

    What dost thou think?

              If it was I who would describe the next step of psiad evolution, I would allow Psiads to reach Psi 7 and 5 dots in every mode.

              Than, you have Favored Aptitude and Strong Mode that allow you to reach 6 dot in a single mode.

              I would make new Psionic Edges, one that allow you to increase by 1 dot all the modes of their favorite aptitude (what would allow you to reach 7 in a mode with Strong Mode), one that align the amount of psi points the psiad get with what psions get, and 1 that allow the psiad to empower each other like psions do (considering the psiad as a Psion of the same aptitude as their Favorite Aptitude)

              That set of small steps would put a psiad in the same level as a Doyen (7 in one aptitude and 5 in all the others)

              Edit: I would not change the XP cost of psiads. Psion are genetically enchanted to become masters in their aptitude, what is not the case for psiads.

              Additionally, I would make all children of psions be automatically psiads capable of reaching psi 7, but the slow evolution would point them to enter the chamber to empower themselves (make it easier to evolve), as no one really knows how far they can go without a chamber.

                                              The first issue I see in this scenario, is that aside from the Doyen, in-universe, there are very few people who could envision this, let alone achieve it, and the Doyens have a marked interest in making sure it never ever happens, and have consistently hobbled the scientific and psi development of other alien races to an even greater degree than they've done to humans.

                                              So you'd need access to high-level noetic science that was never influenced by the Doyen (like the one that possessed poor Sara Kaur precisely to make sure humans never uncovered something that might make them too powerful). The ideal moment to escape Doyen sabotage to achieve this is paradoxically before the Aeon era, since Doyen oversight might have been lesser, but the scientific knowledge to do it would have been unavailable and would have to be achieved from the ground up, which would take a *long* time (or that knowledge could be discreetly stolen from other advanced psi aliens with a clairvoyance/teleportation/telepathy combo).

                                              If your goal is a super-psiad, I don't think by the RAW any power achievable by an "unevolved" psiad could conceivably "pop the locks", because unlike psions, psiads don't actually have any locks, they're just limited by their own unrefined potential. So the issue is how to "evolve" a psiad, which is markedly different from unlocking something that's already there. It would require superscience and given that it took supergenius Sara Kaur almost 2 centuries to just unlock QK, I don't know what it would take to effectively compress millenias of human evolution, but it should be beyond the current scope of humanity. Maybe a super-intelligent nova could figure it out, but why would they bother trying to ?

                                              If your goal is a super-psion, assuming regular psions know to and manage to escape Doyen surveillance somehow, I expect that it's a matter of regrowing what psionic pathways the Doyen amputated with their regular prometheus chambers. So I would say, you'd need psion scientists working in concert figuring it out. At least one (but most likely several) geneticist and noeticist with clairsentience on steroids using Omniscience to observe alien and human DNA strands and figure out the subquantum interactions between psi and human biology necessary to boost human psi without limiting its versatility. And then getting around whatever genetic boobytraps the Doyen likely arranged to keep humans from doing just that.

                                              Once he figures out what to do, he either needs a telepath to convey to a proxy-like VK the super-sophisticated alterations that should be made to a psion's template to implement the "jailbreak codes" to his full abilities, or more likely he needs brilliant EK and VK engineers to help him build a bioware "jailbreaking" chamber.

                                              And since that guy (or those guys) is not a Doyen relying on millenias of knowledge of supernoetics, even with Intelligence 6 or 7 it might take him a while to achieve limited results (a psion with 2 main aptitudes (3 if you count QK) and auxiliary modes up to 3 or 4). Anything beyond that would for me be grossly excessive, because psi transcendance should still be out of reach for humanity. I don't think we currently know with any accuracy where the limit stands between "the Doyen didn't want humanity too powerful" and "there's a limit to how much we can overclock these primitive lifeforms even if we graft on them some of our DNA".

                                              As always, YMMV
