    [Aberrant] Understanding Scale through specific examples

    05-26-2021, 11:56 AM
    As I'm familiarizing myself more with Storypath in general and Aberrant in particular, I thought it might be beneficial to have a thread where folks can post examples of their understanding of Scale interactions. I know these have been tricky for people to wrap their head around, myself included, so having some more concrete use cases to discuss could help.

    Let's say we have a nova with Toughness 3, giving them Soft Armor (1) and Scale 3 durability, with Defense 1. For purposes of this example, the nova won't roll a Resilience attribute to defend.

    The example soldier from page 261 of the Aberrant book shoots at the nova. They have a Combat pool of 7, so on average they will roll 2.1 successes; we'll round down to 2. With their Enhancement 1 from their equipment, they have a total of 3 successes.

    They spend 1 success to hit a Defense of 1, leaving 2 successes to purchase Stunts. Normally, they could spend 1 of these to overcome the Soft Armor (1) for Inflict Damage, and the second for Pin Down or something else. However, in this case the nova has Scale 3 durability, against the Scale 2 damage inflicted by the rifle the soldier is using. 1 difference in Scale adds +2 Difficulty, so this would increase the Difficulty of the Inflict Damage stunt to 3, one more success than the soldier has available, so the nova takes the hit with no consequences.

    Is that all accurate? This is based on an average result from the soldier, so if they got lucky and rolled 3+ successes on 7 dice, they could still injure the nova. Then again, the nova would presumably roll for Defensive Stunts, decreasing the number of successes the soldier would have available to purchase Inflict Damage. (Aside: seems odd that you would roll Resilience instead of Finesse for purposes of defense/dodging.)

    Thanks in advance for any feedback!

      Yes, it’s accurate.

      Its not a regular rifle that have Scale 2 damage, usually they are scale 1, but with the Heavy Weapon tag it can be 2. With a regular rifle the soldier would need 5 successes (beside the defense) to cause damage, 1 from soft armor plus 4 from 2 scales above.

      Now, the pin down stunt you suggested, is a bit complex with the scale gap. While it don’t say anything about scale, you would not be able to pin down a virtually bullet proof nova, so I would allow to use this stunt only if you spend 2 extra successes per scale to break the scale difference, but it’s not RAW.

      If you want to improve the soldier chances, you may use the Heavy weapon (you were using before) to increase the savage by 1, and the Two handed tag, that add 1 extra enhancement to damage. The Emptying the Magazine stunt can also be used for extra 2 enhancements, but will force a reload next round.

          Okay, let's put it all together!

          Nova - Toughness 3, Stamina 3, Defense 1

          Soldier - Combat Pool 7, using a rifle with the Automatic, Heavy Weapon and Two-Handed Qualities (+1 Enhancement, +1 Scale, +1 Enhancement to Inflict Damage).

          The soldier rolls 2 successes on their pool, plus 2 Enhancement from the rifle, one of which must go toward Inflict Damage, for a total of 4 successes. They also use the Emptying the Magazine Stunt, adding 2 more Enhancement, for a total of 6 successes.

          The nova rolls Stamina 3 and gets 1 success, making their Defense 2.

          The soldier spends 2 successes to hit the nova, leaving 4 successes. The Heavy Weapon rifle has Scale 2 power against the nova's Scale 3 durability, increasing the cost of the Inflict Damage stunt from 1 to 3. The soldier spends 3 successes and successfully inflicts a Bruised Injury Condition on the nova. Of course, they now have to reload, and presumably have an irritated superhuman bearing down on them...

          EDIT TO ADD: The Heavy Weapon tag does a lot of...heavy lifting here. Using the standard Assault Rifle from Trinity core, the soldier loses out on +1 Enhancement and +1 Scale. In that scenario, on an average roll of 2 successes, +1 Enhancement to Inflict Damage from Two-Handed and +2 Enhancement from Emptying the Magazine, they're down to 5 successes. Spending 2 to overcome the nova's Defense leaves them with 3. However, since it's now only a Scale 1 attack, their cost to purchase Inflict Damage is 5 successes, so they are out of luck.

                                          Excess durability scale gives you +2 soft armor per excess durability scale level against an attack.

                                          Toughness 3 gives you 1 soft armor +2 durability scale for a total of 3. Yes, mega-might 3 gives you 4 power scale. You're down 1 net success. This is a good thing. You always want attacks to be at least slightly more powerful than defense, because otherwise it makes combats drag on long.
