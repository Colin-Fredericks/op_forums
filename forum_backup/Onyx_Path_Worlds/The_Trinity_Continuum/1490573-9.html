            I'm not sure if there's an official view, but this is also a perfectly valid approach. My take is based on the societies letting paranoia and distrust of the Novas be the default attitude. In another version, greed and opportunism wins over from the beginning which means societies work with, and even use, Novas. The problem is, as you said, that Novas are larger than life and the shadow struggles to contain them. Eventually, the shadow ruptures, societies are exposed to the world, and like a piece of bloody meat in a piranha tank law enforcement and rival criminal organizations turn them to shreds. It would take years or even decades after the aberrant war for a new shadow to establish itself.

Maybe with some help of the core of Aeon Society, that have the knowledge of the different kinds of Inspired, or studding how Novas powers work they may find ways to differentiate what is a real Nova and what is not, the assassins can identify themselves and fight Novas certain they are not ones (and the following time line you described, that is now ours).

                It's kind of tricky to think about it, but people don't know how their powers work and dont know how other people powers work, so, unless you deal with many different powered people and have some kind of sense of source (Talents and Assassins may have it and all Psiads have it, but they may still lack the experience to differentiate them), there is no way to tell who is what.
