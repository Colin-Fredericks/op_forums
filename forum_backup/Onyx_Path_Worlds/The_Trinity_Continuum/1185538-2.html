            No one in the Æon era really understands any more about Talents than they do in the modern era -- and given the presence of psions, most people are more concerned with figuring the ins and outs of how psi and the subquantum universe work.

            Powerful clairsentients can sometimes detect an extra "oomph" around Talents, due to their bending the strings of Fate (or perhaps Fate bends the Talent). But even then it's not enough to definitively say anything is particularly different about them. The Æon Trinity, however, remains one of the most popular employers of Talents, whether by design or by happenstance. They probably know most about the phenomenon, if it is one, but even then they don't know much. It's entirely likely that Max remains the sole identifiable paramorph.

            Nihonjin Superiors are a weird case: they may be the 22nd century equivalent of stalwarts -- novas erupted and locked at a low ("safe") level of power. Or they may be a particular flavour of paramorph that doesn't usually fall under the header of "Talent." Either way, Nakamura-san and his contemporaries are barely scratching the surface: they don't know about Talents beyond the bounds of their own research, and don't have a handle on the larger implications of their work beyond finding a safe alternative to eruption.

                        I think you're being held back a bit by 1e thinking, Shadowstripe.

                        In 1e, proxies and psions were technically different things: the processes used to create them were different. Psions were latents that basically got a genetic spit-shine to let them do their thing better, while Proxies got mixed with bits of Doyen DNA in order to do things better than any human was capable, Prometheus Chamber or not. Technically speaking, they were hybrids, although they probably wouldn't want to quantify it like that.

                        That's no longer the case.

                        In the new edition, proxies and psions are fundamentally the same thing. Any psion, through enough dedication to their noetic abilities, can rise to proxy-level. The proxies themselves just got to skip that middle bit and got awakened to their full potential all at once. There's no in-setting justification needed for "people getting better at what they do."

                        In the years since the Sydney attack, there are few, if any, other psions who have risen to their level. Maybe someone like Ellayne Cassel, or a couple of other "number twos" who have been with their orders for a long time.

                        But the capability is there, if PCs want to claim it.

                                                        To be honest, i was expecting as much (that many of the behind the scenes details would be in that chapter and that it wouldn't be discussed until it was released). I tend to assume that things between Editions of a game will generally be similar until the newest edition says otherwise (or someone in the know of the latest lore/information reveals such).

                                                        In 1st Ed, Proxies were more than mere human thanks to the Doyen... thus could achieve higher levels of power than human Psions. Now it appears in 2nd Ed, that the Proxies merely became advanced Psions (again, likely because of the Doyen if they are still working behind the scenes) and that all Human Psions have the potential to reach this same level of power (though without the Doyen advancing their abilities like with the Proxies, it will take longer to unlock this potential).

                                                        I am hoping that we will get rules for the rare Psiads who unlock their potential on their own.

                                                        For now, I think i have a few ideas I can work with for creating them until official rules are provided. First, since Psiads do not use Prometheus Chambers to unlock their potential, they are not limited to a single Psi Aptitude... meaning they can learn any and all the various psi abilities given time. However, this also means they don't favor any particular Aptitude either and treat all modes within a given Aptitude as Auxiliary Modes when it comes to purchasing them (thus requiring more experience to master any one Aptitude compared to a Psion, but can master more than one). Additionally, since they don't use the Prometheus Chambers, they don't get the bonuses the Chambers provide Psions (the bonus Attribute dot or raising their initial Psi rating to 3 from 2). This means that all Psiads will start a little weaker than Psions (they start at Psi 2 and have one less attribute dot compared to Psions), but can become significantly more powerful over time with their ability to master multiple Aptitudes if they so choose. Since they start at Psi 2, they can not learn Quantakinesis or Teleportation modes to begin with (but can pick them up once they raise their Psi to 3). Of course, like any Psion, they run the risk of psionic dysfunction if a mode in any Aptitude they know is 3 dots higher than the next highest mode in that Aptitude (which can be a problem if they are learning multiple Aptitudes at once... though each Aptitude is treated separately).

                                                        I wonder how close these rough ideas will be to the official rules when they are provided (in the Setting Secrets chapter perhaps... or a future product maybe?)... if we ever get them that is. For now these are just speculations and may or may not be present in my games (provided no official rules for Psiads are provided). I wonder if anyone has any ideas on how the established Psi-Orders would react to a Psiad being discovered who has developed their powers independently from their teachings. I love speculating in these areas... as they lead to all sorts of interesting questions and such, but perhaps it is best left for its own thread.

                                                        In the meantime, looking forward to the next backer manuscript reveal and to see what other stretch goals will be unlocked.
