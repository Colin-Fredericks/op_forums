    Making a 4th Force?

    03-10-2019, 07:54 AM
    So the way I see it Quantum is like the Wellspring of Universe. It is the substrata from which new universes may be formed.

    Psi however seems to represent mastery of the fundamental forces, psychics seem to master the facets of reality as is, whether thought, space, kinetic force, energy ect. Mastering it allows you to ascend to the subtle realms beyond this reality.

    And Flux is the power of tapping into the Other. It is the power of the inbetween of the Multiverse summoning potential and causality and probability. Mastering it involves ascending into a new perspective of Reality, so being one with time (past/present/future/maybes)

    So what about the idea of Quantum Gods, or a combination of Quantum Psi and Flux Ascended Beings creating another force, like a 4th or even 5th Force.

    Even with it not being Canon I can see myself using the idea for homebrew games.

    So with Flux we can access alternate reality’s, worlds that use different rules and different Forces. So say a Flux God Creates the concept of the other/elsewhere/beyond, creating essentially a “cheat Potential”. Then Quantum Gods create a Universe if Power, new rules and fundamental Forces. Then Psi gods in Coordination with the Quantum gods anchors a method to tap into this otherforce, maybe it’s a tool like the Green Lantern Rings, or a backside code in our universe such as magical rituals and strange geometries which seems like magic. Or maybe it’s by physically merging the force into a being of this world, like The Flash and the Speed Force, Captain Marvel and Shazam, the Silver Surfer and the Power Cosmic or Jean Grey and the Phoenix.


    This is just a thought exercise. You could use it to make one off phenomena or mysteries. Or a Unique impossible being. Or more fun to introduce new potential into the setting. Beings that don’t fit the solid stone rules of the Universe.


    Thoughts? These are just the most ambiguous abstract explanations of events. For instance maybe a wish Force is made so that Ascended Beings could exert their influence in the Universe they left behind, for good or ill and without disrupting the Universal balance of Quantum and Psi.

    Maybe it’s for making a singular Godbeibg who can exist in the Universe like a Celestial or Galactus. Or maybe you can use it to make an otherworldly Force to explain weaker but more omnipresent phenomena like Ritualists or Sorcerers, or even the strange Infrastructure of the Machine God.

    Or even making Exalts in Trinity. Or Mages, both styles. So Ascension mages have Avatars which are these shards of the “Other” which translates belief and will into change. While Awakening Mages Tap into the Supernal, a reality of Truths which you can call upon to change reality.

    Again this can be simplified by just saying a Single Quantum Being Created this Power, like Franklin Richards Creating another World, or it can be As complex as you want to cheat the universe.

    Thoughts?
