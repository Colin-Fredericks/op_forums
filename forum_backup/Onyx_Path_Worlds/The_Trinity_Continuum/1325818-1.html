            I love your sheet and I have some corrections / suggestions.

            - Defense score is 1, the resistance attribute is rolled when you take defensive actions. The sheet in the book has this badly noted too.

            - Quantum Points would benefit from a Max / Current spaces

            - The first couple lines for the Injury Conditions extends into the modifier.

            - The placement of the Hard Armor might be better centered or stretched to fill the width.

            - As the Flux trait is a temporary one, boxes would be better as dots usually indicate you can roll a trait. (Unless I missed somewhere that Flux gets rolled)

            - The colons after Skills are unneeded and I’m unsure what the boxes are for.

            - Moment of Inspiration, might be better as Moment of Eruption, as Inspiration is a Talents thing

            - The space for Quantum powers is cramped, not sure the best way to help with that, but I believe the “Min” space is unneeded except when raising the power.
