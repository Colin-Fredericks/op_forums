    Trinity Continuum crossovers

    11-04-2013, 03:08 AM
    So: the Trinity Continuum core book will include a modern-day setting featuring Talents; Æon will feature a century-from-now setting featuring psions. Presumably, Aberrant will be a day-after-tomorrow setting featuring Novas. Will these games be designed to be compatible with each other, system-wise? That is, if I want to include Talents in the Aberrant or Æon settings, will I be able to use the Talents rules from the core book as is? If I want to include psychics in the Aberrant setting, will the rules suggest hacking the Aberrant mechanics to represent psi-powered characters, or will it point to Æon's rules for psions, possibly with some tweaks to represent how psis of that era differ from psis of the later era?

    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

          As Telgar said, they were compatible, perhaps not cleanly so though.

          On a semi-related note - If we really have to go down the route of mixing things up so the era and power type are essentially interchangeable, I'd like to at least see that done without compromising the tone and themes of the different periods. Aberrants (end of the War era and out to 2120s) should be much more powerful than, say Psions. If the history is relatively intact in the new edition then the War was won only through the threat of a scorched earth policy, not because humanity could actually 'win' (or even come close to it). in 2102 humanity is (rightly) terrified that Psions won't be up to the challenge of taking down the Colony or Mal and the hordes of space crazy abbies.

          One of the things that drew me to Trinity was that the bad guys were such a potentially overwhelming threat. Then again, I intensely dislike crunchy overpowered PC's in a game, probably one of the reasons I'm not that big a fan of Aberrant as it was published. I suspect from what's been said so far, there will be a measure of interchangeability between the three (and potentially other) periods - that makes sense commercially let alone mechanically.

          I just hope it can be done without having to spend too much time undoing such rule for those of us that, for example, want to avoid A! and Aberrant era powers showing up in Trinity. I have a hunch the dev's will figure out a middle ground. This topic seems to come up frequently enough I doubt the devs aren't aware of various desires to mix, and keep distinct, the different periods/powers.

                              I like the idea of the three line working better together, but I've always crossed classes from the three lines with each other. I do think that the Continuum would be served by a more consistent rule set, and clearly defined rules for how things interact. I never liked building Abberants for Trinity by giving them versions of Psion powers. I've always used Aberrant rules for the Abberants in my Trinity games. But, if the system were designed from the beginning to support that, I'd be pleased as punch. So, I'm excited to see where Continuum 2.0 takes us. I feel the design of Adventure! was sublime, and those rules should be ported to the new system as directly as possible. The other two lines are where we hit a few issues.

                              I don't have a problem with Aberrant characters being crazy powerful, but I want the feel to be right. I never felt that Taint was half as big a threat as the setting material makes it out to be. Nova characters should feel like a supercharged train engine that will go out of control and go off the rails at any moment. Novas should have to struggle to maintain control and avoid going taint mad. On the other had Psions should feel like the undisputed masters of their given spheres of influence. This doesn't require that Psions beable to out damage Novas/Aberrants with simular power. While Novas/Aberrants should be brute force like a hacksaw, Psions should embody precision like a scalpel.

                              But that's just my take.

                                      My initial comment was based on inconsistencies that existed among the various gamelines in the Trinity Universe. Consider how each line handled psychic characters: Trinity had its Psi, Aptitudes, and Modes, and an assumption that baseline humans have Psi 1. Aberrant assumed that psychic characters would use reskinned Quantum Powers with some minor rules tweaks mostly concerning Taint. Adventure!’s mesmerists used an ill-defined Inspiration trait and Psychic Knacks. The result was that the three games’ respective versions of psychics felt like three radically different kinds pf things, not merely different stages of power with a few minor variations in some of the specifics.

                                      What I’m hoping to see in the Trinity Continuum is a standard framework for handling psychic characters, with the different eras varying in terms of, say, which Techniques an Adventure!-era mesmerist has access to and/or how readily available Noetic Energy is. Different dials and switches on a common “Noetic Powers” engine, rather that completely different engines. I want Æon-era Aberrants to use the same rules as Aberrant-era Novas, tweaked to allow for how much more serious of a problem Taint is in the Æon era, and I want Adventure!’s stalwarts to be mechanically identical to Aberrant’s “stealth Novas”. I want Talents to work the same in all eras: baseline humans who are remarkably capable.
