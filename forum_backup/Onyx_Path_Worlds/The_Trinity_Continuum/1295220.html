    At what point in Evolution do we Determine Quantum/Psi/Flux Predilection?

    03-09-2019, 02:04 AM
    So Aeon talks about species having predilections towards usually Quantum or Psi. Humans are of the rarest type of sentient life in that they have potential for both. They also talk about Flux but they don’t really go into detail about it too much, I like to think Most Species have Flux potential but once they embrace Psi or Quantum it disappears because they went all in and you can’t master two type of “energies”.

    But they mention species favoring Quantum or Psi, with Psi being much more common then Quantum. Psi does seem like a more standard evolution. But Those who tap into Quantum can be bonkers Nutzo! But not everyone is Nova Age Eximorphs, I believe the Bullseye civilization was a lower level Quantum civilization, however maybe this could be explained with Quantum being more defused because it was stable and omnipresent rather then a 1 in a million eruption of super powers. Maybe they were more Stalwart level. Quantum seems more fantastical/impossible levels of sci fi.



    But at what point in evolution does predilection occur? Some parts of the books indicate it’s at the sapience/intelligence stage. But with the nature of some Psi, like with the Chromatics, they seem very natural evolutions that could have been developed at low levels early on before tool use or culture was developed, Hell species learn to give organic ships Jumping Psi. Which raises the question for me, you then don’t need someone that was gene modified in a Prometheus chamber, since you can grow Jump ships you essentially have simple life with Psi powers. And sub-Aberrant Mutants can be animals and they are a quantum Mutation.

    Meanwhile the Coalition was able to reverse engineer Quantum using slaves from vivisecting relatively a weak Nova. And due to the Nature of Eruption they were able to create Slavs with a diverse array of powers.

    So then I like to think maybe the most satisfactory solution is early on in evolution when a planet first gets life could be when they develop along the path of Psi or Quantum. Otherwise it could be at the stage of intellect, but then that opens up a can of worms because what about sub-intelligent life, like Jump ships, or at least primitive life like say I don’t know elk. If it was that simple then a planet could have one form of life develop Psi or Quantum and all they have to do to access the other is evolve a different life form. But due to the push and pull causality of Psi and Quantum it seems natural that planets naturally have life synchronize towards one or the other planetwide. They naturally adapt to the flow the planet’s life goes with early on, or in the rare case they develop a capacity towards both so they was and wane like Earth. Maybe Earths life was exposed to Psi and Quantum Early On, or they inherited the dual capacity from some very specific chaps.

    And what about Artificial Intelligence/Lifeforms? IF we can have Psi tech and Quantum tech I suppose that means we can have Psi or Quantum Robots. Though again at what point can we have both, like say by having a Quantum Robot can it not have Psitech Jump drives? Probably not, but what about a Psitech gun, probably yeah? What point is the line?

    I like the idea that often AI, that is non Organic AI especially, are perhaps predisposed towards Flux! It goes well with the idea of many Sci Fi Stories about the evolution of AI linked towards a fluke of intelligence, as well as AI’s link towards Probability and what not.


    Thoughts? Like Chromatics have other instinctual Psi using lifeforms on their home world right? Or are they distinct? And do you think say if Chromatics left or went Extinct but life still Thrived on their world the next high form of life could develop Quantum use, or do you think the planets natural life is locked at Psi at this point.

                  I think the disposition to Psi and Quantum is related to the life in the planet as a whole, not just a species. What happens is it is necessary to be intelligent enought to unlock this capacity, maybe humans were not ready yet as a species, but the ones that where (or made capable) build the machines that accelerated the evolution. That would explain why animals are capable of became quantum monsters while psions usually die before corrupt.

                  The coalition uses high technology to mutate their members, so, they copy, what for them is an alien, DNA to simulate the natural evolution, injecting themselves with the genetic capacity of working Quantum. So they are not naturally quantum nor are mutated by the aberrant power, they are genetically manipulated to became quantum active, what I think is basically what the Doyen did to psions but on the psi side.

                  About AI, well, an AI is not biological and need to rebuild their bodies to evolve, so... no natural tendency. They can build hightech bodies, they can build biotech/psi bodies and they can build nova tech bodies, and even combine them in the same body but not to create one single effect, as long they can reverse engineer them.
                  Can they build novatech from nothing? No. Psitech? No. Advanced tech? No. Hightech? Yes, it’s the natural way for them, probably faster then we can do.
                  Can they copy an Quantum battery that allow baselines to use novatech? I would say yes, but it’s up to you.
