                                                    Mr. G and I have collaborated before on some game sheets. I'm sure we will again.

                                                    And I can't really take any credit for the original design of this sheet. As I mentioned before, it's something I found on the Internet when the Aeonverse was still being published, printed for shits and giggles and managed to keep in my Trinity books for the last 15 years. (I lost the actual PDF file years ago.) The original source of this Trinity starship sheet is long gone, I'm sure.

                                                    My only misgiving about these sheets (and it's a small one) is that the Tech dropdown menu couldn't include the Greek letters Omega and Psi to denote hardtech and biotech. Mr. Gone tried, but the Adobe tool he was using just wouldn't accept the Greek letters.

                                                    Whoever originally designed these sheets seemed to know what they were doing, since it looked so complete to me when I first saw it. I'm glad there's nothing missing. I just hope the Structural levels are mostly correct for each ship type. And if they're not, people can always take the freighter sheet and black out the SLs they don't need, easy peasy.
