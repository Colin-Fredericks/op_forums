          Originally posted by Shepherdboi View Post
          Your assumption is that psiads and novas are somehow "evolved" versus mesmerists and stalwarts, and this isn't really true. Mesmerists and stalwarts are the way they are because the Doyen blanketed the Earth in an effect which suppressed the growth of psi and quantum powers.

          As for the future of flux powers, presumably something akin to magogs, but without the Deviations.
          I disagree. Magogs are the result of Aether, which is a byproduct of the interaction between Quantum and Flux; it is not the future of those who use Flux. In particular, Magogs are more akin to Novas than they are to Talents: they're all about boosting raw physical capacity to inhuman levels. As a “future of Flux users”, it's singularly unimaginative: “let's make enhanced Flux users just like Novas”.

          Instead, the future of those who use Flux is more along the lines of Assassins: a shift away from subconscious manipulations expressing themselves as lucky breaks and towards more intentional expressions of competence, to the extent that the competence can reach absurd and even impossible levels:
          Masteries are special abilities that an Assassin learns as tools of her craft. An Assassin doesn't just bend the likelihoods and probabilities around her, she cows them to her whim and breaks them. … Like Talents, she uses her Inspiration to do this, drawing on the Flux of the Continuum, but unlike Talents, an Assassin has control over her use. This is a deliberate act, one that would appear in Daylight as a fantastic display of unbelievable magnitude…

          TC: Assassins p.127.

          ​​​​​More generally, I would say that “evolved Talents” would tend to bear a not inconsiderable resemblance to Exalted. Which isn't surprising, because both Assassins and Exalted draw inspiration from wuxia.

          And to the extent that they start developing outright powers, as opposed to impossible feats of skill, I expect those powers to be related to space and time: teleportation, “stepping sideways” into the Mists, conjuring items to them or banishing opponents to other times, places, or worlds, creating portals; maybe even distorting distances or durations or manipulating gravity. That sort of thing.

          But physical mutations that lead to the body doing inhuman things, a la Magogs? That's what Quantum does; not Flux.

          My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

              I guess the use of Flux Assassins and Polyphemus have show us that Flux is amazingly versatile on the directions it can go.

              My guess is next tier Flux users have strong connections to all Dimensions (Time, Space and Cross-Time), so seeing the future, traveling to other dimensions, teleportation, distorting space, distorting time, accessing the knowledge of their other-selves, bringing the physical traits of their other selves, changing characteristics of objects to what it could have been, etc etc.

              They probably can do other things, same way a Psi user can alter their body properties to a limited extension, while the Quantum user is the true master of their body, a Flux user probably can do other things that at first are not obvious linked to Flux, much like an assassin can cut a wall to reach the target, or a talent can come back from death, I would say the possibilities are infinite but at a limited power.

                          My interpretation of Aether, as I understood from the book, is that it’s not PURE flux, it’s Flux forced out of its natural state with external source of energy, that is Quantum for the Aether era. The amount of Aether during that period is due the Martian attacks, but it can be made in smaller amounts using other methods, and people have been doing it for years.

                          It doesn’t mean Aether is Flux, it is mostly Flux, but not the natural Flux. So what Magogs can do is MOSTLY what is possible to do with Flux, but as it’s not in it’s natural state, I would say they can do what they do in the scale they do due to the large amount of Aether available in the time, while they would be largely limited if they had a normal level of flux.

                          So, Gogs and Magogs are flux users, but they are not really a talent next step, they are what you can become for overplaying with Flux.

                          To complete my point, a Nova the way they are in Aberrant is not the natural evolution of quantum users, but a forced version of it. Probably natural Novas would be closer to what Mal is in Adventure Era, less prone to mutations.


                          Edit: From the manuscript of players guide:
                          Aether is not the same as flux, yet this period is another obstacle to be surmounted by those who would travel past it in time. Rifts to this era may open into the labs of Aethernaut geniuses, leaving the characters stumbling out of glowing green portals. Also, the differences between Aether and flux can cause time travel using Aether — or even taking place too close to a significant concentration of Aether — to operate in unexpected and problematic ways, risking stranding the time travelers in the past. This era is covered in detail in Trinity Continuum: Aether.
