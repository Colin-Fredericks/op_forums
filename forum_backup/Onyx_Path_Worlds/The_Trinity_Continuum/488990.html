              I just scanned my copy even though it's already partially filled in. I wrote up stats for a Leviathan-class jumpship named Chicago. (Not a canon name for a jumpship obviously, but I wanted something different from all the Hindu and African names like Svaha and Shaka.)

              The question is, how do I get it to you guys? Also, I remember there being 3 versions of this starship sheet: one for hybrids, one for frigates and one for freighters, with the only real difference between the versions was the number of Structural Levels for each type of ship. None of the 3 versions was labeled as such on their sheet. Indeed, the sheet doesn't even have a title saying it's a startship sheet or even that it's related to Trinity at all. Aside from that, it seems to have all the needed stats for a Trinity starship.

                                                  MOVED FROM PREVIOUS POST: Ok, rules for Structural Levels for each type of ship: Trinity Technology Manual, p.135 says that there are 3 types: Hybrids with 8 SLs; Frigates with 12 SLs; and Freighters with 15 SLs

                                                  So where did the Cruiser and Scout types come from? Or did White Wolf goof and forget that these two types mentioned in the color section needed to be included in the rules section?

                                                  Just how many types of spaceships are there and how many Structural Levels do they have? Mr. Gone's scale from smallest to largest is:

                                                  Fighter(4 SLs) > Hybrid(8 SLs) > Scout(12 SLs) > Frigate(15 SLs) > Cruiser(20 SLs)

                                                  Mr. G, where did you come up with these numbers? I didn't see them in either the corebook or the Technology Manual. And where do freighters and/or jumpships fit into this scale?

                                                  Ok, finished reading through the color section of Technology Manual and Gone's scale is roughly correct: Scouts are referred to as "small frigates" and cruisers are indeed larger than standard frigates. And what's more, cruisers are designed to attach to Leviathan-class jumpships, so it looks like we need an additional sheet for freighters/jumpships at the very least. Just how many Structural Levels will a jumpship have if it's designed to carry something as big as several cruisers? Something like 30 Structural Levels? Remember, Leviathans are 1.2 kilometers long and almost as wide....

                                                  EDIT: Just sent off another email to Mr. Gone with my concerns. He and I are conversing on how to make the sheets prettier as well as trying to figure out how many SLs the new freighter sheet is going to need. CHILL, we should have new sheets for you pretty soon. ("Soon" of course meaning "whenever Mr. Gone gets it done.")

                                                  Stay tuned.

                                                          04-25-2015, 05:42 PM
                                                          Just exchanged a few emails with Mr. Gone about my suggestions, including for the freighter sheet. Stay tuned.

                                                          EDIT: Mr. Gone just sent me his first draft and it looks AMAZING. I just sent off a couple of suggestions to Mr. G to touch it up, including making the lines typable/interactive and making sure all the sheets were horizontally oriented (to make them easier to type on, natch). They should be done soon and then we'll send them off to CHILL for inclusion in the EON Archive.

                                                          Now for the "bad" news. The Structural Levels on these sheets are totally untested, so I have no idea how they'll perform in playtesting. I'm hoping the guy who originally made these sheets (whoever he was) knew what he was doing when he assigned the SLs on his sheets. I tried my best to assign (what I thought would be) a reasonable amount of SLs for the freighter sheet. Considering that the Leviathan is so huge (1.2 km long!) I guestimated that it has about 32 SLs, compared to a cruiser's 20 and a frigate's 15. And since a Leviathan can carry 4 cruisers/frigates (as well as 12 hybrids), I figured it needed to be appropriately huge.

                                                          If playtesters using these sheets find that I gave the freighter sheet too many SLs (or if the SLs on all of the sheets need to be reworked entirely), please let me or Mr. Gone know.
