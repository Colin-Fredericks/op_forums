                            It's worth noting that there are two directions one could go with high-powered Talents:

                            • Play up the timeline manipulation and give them conscious probability manipulation, time travel, or dimension hopping abilities.

                            • amp up their Gifts, especially the ones related to attributes and skills.

                            The latter option gets into similar conceptual territory as Exalted: channel an empowering energy (Essence in Exalted; parachronic Flux in TC) to enhance your competence and pull off stunts that range from extraordinary to flat-out impossible.

                            You could even do both, with higher-level Gifts being the TC counterpart to Charm Trees (or whatever Exalted calls them) and the conscious parachronic powers being the TC counterpart to Exalted's Sorcery.
