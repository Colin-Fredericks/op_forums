    Trinity: Atlantis (Era Setting)

    02-20-2018, 09:36 PM
    Since creating new time periods for the Trinity Continuum seems to be popular, I have decided to do the same... but I will go to the distant past to explore a possible origin for the timeline.

    I am tentatively calling this era Trinity: Atlantis, after the mythical ancient civilization that predates all known civilizations that have been proven to exist... possibly the very first civilization... so what better place to put the origin of Talents, Psiads/Psions and Novas/Aberrants? Also since these eras also need to have a name that starts with an "A"... it seemed perfect.

    Kidding aside, this era will actually be about all the various mythical civilizations, which I will have as City-States of this First Civilization... where the discovery of unique resources (advancing a number of the citizens to Human+ status) advanced them considerably compared to other human tribes of the time (between 50,000 to 25,000 BCE). For the moment, I think these resources would be unusual crystals that resonate with different kinds of Flux (dimensional, quantum and psi/sub-quantum), inspiring members of the First Civilization to become the equivalent of Tier 2 beings (Daredevils, Mesmerists and Stalwarts to use Adventure! terms and power levels). This gives them the advantage to be come a powerful and prosperous civilization much quicker than other human tribes of the time.

    This would allow me to customize what power level the game would take... perhaps it is the early development of these city-states, where the those with unusual abilities are still fairly grounded in what humans can normally do (Tier 2) but with a few advantages... or progress things to where the City-States are fairly developed and the civilization and are beginning to explore the rest of the world... where they find that the crystals that empowered them are not as unique as they believe... and is causing a lot of problems for other human tribes, but fortunately, their exposure to these same crystals (over the last few decades to centuries or even millennia) has also improved them to deal with these greater threats (Tier 3 power levels). Then of course, we know that these city-states were eventually destroyed... but how this came about is not entirely explicit. Perhaps they couldn't control the powers they were developing (much like the Aberrants in the future)... or they encountered something so powerful that only their ultimate sacrifice could preserve the world for the rest of humanity... either way this would likely see Tier 4+ power levels.

    Atlantis and similar civilizations were known to be incredibly advanced... allowing for the development of super-devices similar to modern technology (though more likely to appear more steampunk than what we have... similar but with a unique difference... and likely use the flux crystals as power sources). This could even see the genre using the powers of Psiads and Novas more like magic than technology (even dealing with extra-dimensional entities similar to those written about by Lovecraft)... and inspiring tales of gods, monsters and beings that will be remembered by other civilizations they will eventually encounter (on Earth and beyond).

    Even after their disappearance, the artifacts and remnants of their civilizations will likely remain to be found by future generations... even with pockets of survivors existing in lost lands out of time with the rest of the world... giving rise to yet more legends that future generations will discover (and possibly exploit).

    As with all other eras in the Trinity Continuum... the possibilities for this era setting are nearly limitless... from exploration and discovery to gritty wars between the city-states (or even other nations that are being corrupted by other forces). Perhaps one or more of these city-states decided to become conquerors rather than explorers... or forms an alliance with extra-dimensional forces/aliens... or accidentally unleashes some form of advanced technology that was never meant to be used (a doomsday deterrent created but not meant to be used). Being so far back in history means that the impact on later eras will likely be minimal... any remnants will have faded by the time known civilizations arose and be relegated to myth and legend.

    So many possibilities to explore... so little time to prepare.

          It is still a fairly rough idea of where I would like to take they idea... a lot of details need to be ironed out (plus I would likely need to wait until the Aberrant and Adventure! books are released for the Trinity Continuum... to include any character features from those settings).

          One of the things that will need to be decided is where the heart of the Civilization that these City-States are part of will be found. Will it be on a lost continent (well, a continent that will eventually become lost), be in a place that currently does exist (but not in the way it once did when these City-States thrived) or some where else entirely (a pocket dimension tentatively connected with Earth or deep within the Earth's crust)? To be honest... any or even all of these are possible. I am personally drawn to the idea of a land that currently exists but was very different 50,000 years ago... likely due to the presence of the very flux crystal resource that will be the main source of power for many of the City-States of the setting. Perhaps the presence of these crystals allows for a thriving ecosystem in a place that would normally be uninhabitable for most life? Some place like Antarctica (with a Savage Land type inspiration).

          Beyond this is determining what the rest of the world could be like. Many places such as North and South America have yet to be populated by early human tribes in this time period... which could mean that they are completely empty of sentient life, but where is the fun in that? Maybe there are remnants of even older non-human civilizations in these places... which may or may not be willing to share the Earth with this new ape-like species called Mankind. It is possible that extra-dimensional entities have a foothold in our reality in these places (and other distant lands yet to be reached by humanity) and it will be the explorers from these advanced City-States that first discovers them and the threat they pose to Earth and Humanity... leading to confrontations and wars that shape both the City-States and pave the way for other tribes to eventually find these lands (now freed from hostile forces) to be settled. It is even possible that explorers from the City-States could discover alternate realities or even holes in time to future eras... where their actions in the past (from the new time's point of view) are legends and discover the fate of their civilization... or people from the future discovering such holes in time and coming to the distant past and learning places like Atlantis, Lemuria and Mu actually existed.

          Depending when in this era one wishes to develop... the sorts of antagonists can vary greatly... from the more survival of early tribes against mega-fauna predators... to dealing with other tribes as advanced as theirs or even alien entities (extra-dimensional or even extraterrestrial). The unique and unpredictable gifts granted by the flux crystals can change anyone or anything into something beneficial or detrimental to such civilizations... allowing for an unlimited potential for stories and adventures.

          Ultimately, the possibilities for such a setting are nearly unlimited... any and all the above are possible, even likely (even at the same time with the various realities of the continuum colliding with each other to one degree or another). Hard to decide which direction to take such a setting initially. Thankfully there is still plenty of time before rules for Novas, Aberrants and other characters are released to work out any such details.

                          To be honest, I have considered this possibility... that time travelers from the future (Novas or Aberrants most likely) could be encountered. Whether it is a large number of such individuals or just a few, and whether they are benevolent or malicious in their desires... remains to be decided. However, having beings from the future becoming the origin of the human+ potential, while ironic, is just a little too paradoxical for me.

                          We know that humanity somehow developed the potential to use various forms of flux )whether it is from dimensional events, quantum events or sub-quantum/psi events)... but to learn that such potential only entered the human genome because travelers from the future who have that potential came to the past and gave it to humanity begs the question... where did that potential originate... in the past or in the future? Such thinking tends to give me a headache.

                          No, i don't want a setting that relies so heavily on future settings for it to exist... as part of the premise is that that the future isn't fixed... but is constantly shifting... in Flux, if you pardon the pun. Yes, time travel is an inherent part of the Continuum setting, but to what extent remains to be seen. Creating a setting that relies on time travel to even exist is... well... not what i want. Does that mean there won't be travelers from the future? Of course not... but it does mean that I intend to be cautious on how much such things will be used.

                          As for what I see as the initial cause for this potential being present on Earth... for that we would have to go back almost 65 million years before the modern setting, to when the Earth was struck by a large asteroid that ended one age and began a new one. The Cretaceous era was ended when a massive asteroid collided with the Earth, releasing a 100 terraton explosion off the coast of what is now Central America. While this is not the first time such an asteroid has hit the Earth, this particular asteroid was of an unusual composition, resulting in a massive flux event that affected the world on both a quantum and sub-quantum level, severely weakening the barrier between dimensions and realities. The shock wave from this event not only changed the Earth, but was felt on a multiversal level, even causing other realities to bleed into Earth to one extent or another.

                          The debris from this impact would be spread across the world where it would eventually settle, affecting all life that would develop from that moment on. The largest particles would cause lesser impact sites, further weakening the dimensional barriers in those locations and causing unusual formations (mostly crystals) that would absorb many of the new energies (flux) that were released into the world. Life that was near these crystals would develop in amazing and different ways. Over the next few million years, additional (but smaller) impacts would have similar effects, with the result that the barrier between dimensions on Earth would never fully heal as every new impact shocked the still weakened barrier and even punched holes in it.

                          Eventually, the flux energy absorbed and radiated by the crystals formed in such events would have profound effects on any species that remained near them for extended periods of time... including early hominids. Whether or not these crystals had any effect on human evolution or if human evolution allowed them to access the energy within the crystals to a greater degree than other life forms may never be known. Some tribes avoided areas where such crystals were abundant... seeing them as unnatural or haunted... while other tribes sought to understand and use the crystals to their advantage. The First Civilization was one of the latter groups... and their discoveries with the crystals would have long lasting effects for all of humanity.

                              I am thinking I haven't explained well how the Cretaceous era impact affected Earth and the dimensional barriers between it and the rest of the Continuum.

                              Okay... we know much of what happened when the Asteroid hit the Earth... it caused a massive amount of physical damage that resulted in the extinction of the dinosaurs (or at the very least was a contributing factor to it) and likely resulted in a nuclear winter-type event that had a serious on all ecosystems world wide.

                              As for the effect on the dimensional barriers around Earth... imagine the barrier as a large pane of glass. This impact (due to the composition of the asteroid... possibly with a dark matter or exotic matter core) fractured that glass, causing "cracks" to form throughout the barrier. The site where the impact happened is the most fractured, but the "cracks" spread far... and the smaller impacts that followed (both from this and other asteroids hitting the Earth with its weakened barrier) caused even more cracks to form, fracturing the barrier more. Some of the later impacts even punched through the barrier, creating small holes that things could get through (only energy could make it through a crack... like rain through a leaky window), but over all the barrier between Earth and all the alternate realities and dimensions remains intact... weakened but not shattered.

                              This will be how flux events in other dimensions can affect people in this dimension... as the energy from those events passes through a crack between the originating dimension and Earth.

                              Beyond this, the Cretaceous impact also caused unique crystals to be formed around the world (wherever the dust from the impact site settled where the proper conditions for them to form into crystals). Due to the unique composition of this asteroid, these crystals will have fragments of that composition within them... allowing them to absorb the various forms of Flux that bleed through the cracks in the dimensional barrier... and then radiate that energy back into the environment, causing anything near by to be altered by the Flux. The crystals will also absorb and radiate Flux generated on Earth, though for much of history, such events are fairly rare on Earth.

                              Impacts that happened after this would again spread smaller amounts of the rare chemicals that create these crystals... which again start to form where appropriate and absorb the energies that bleed through the dimensional barrier. Some of these crystals (both from the initial impact and those that followed) would affect any life near by... altering and enhancing them... speeding the evolution of some species... or creating aberrations that would eventually give rise to monstrous beings that have been long forgotten by the time humanity finally migrates out of Africa.

                              Who knows how many non-human sentient species arose in the millions of years since the first impact... and what eventually happened to them? Perhaps some remnant of them survived to watch as the first human tribes migrated to new lands and discovered the flux crystals... and wondered what this new species would do once it learned what the crystals could do. The tribe that will eventually be remembered as Atlantis will be one of the first to learn the secrets of these crystals... but not the only one. As one of the members of these early civilizations, it will be the players who will learn to harness the powers of these crystals and discover the secrets they hold... for better or for worse... and the fate of humanity around the world may depend on what they decide to do with that knowledge.

                                  Looking through the other settings for the Trinity Continuum, we see the Sci-Fi setting of Aeon... the Superhero-esque setting of Aberrant and even the Pulp Adventure of Adventure! Even the proposed setting of Anima will deal with the darker, cyber-punk aspects of sci-fi.

                                  What we don't currently have is a Fantasy-type setting in this continuum. This is where I think Atlantis will come into its own... presenting the powers of Talents, Novas and Psiads through a fantasy-type lens.

                                  Let's face the fact that the abilities of these three groups really do seem almost magical... even if they are based in scientific theories that are not completely understood. To modern people, a Television is just a piece of technology that is fairly common, but to someone even 100 years ago, it would seem almost magical if they saw it (not to mention what can be done with special effects in movies today). As has been said in other settings in different ways... "Magic is merely science we don't understand... yet." And it is in this way that I will try to present the abilities of these types of individuals in a more mystical manner.

                                  Of course, Fantasy settings run a large range from the barely noticeable magics one sees in stories like Tolkien's Middle Earth, to the fairly common use of magic (though still hidden from mundane eyes) one sees in stories like Harry Potter. There are are even stories where magic is so common that almost every person in the setting has some, even if it is a very small amount, like in Piers Anthony's Xanth series. My Atlantis will need to be flexible enough to handle all of these... and more.

                                  Low levels of magic... the Low Fantasy type settings would likely be set in the early days of cities like Atlantis... when the discovery of Flux Crystals (the main source for the powers players will have) is still new and not well understood. This will likely involve discovering caches of these crystals to help give players their extraordinary abilities through "magical" equipment... the crystals acting as both a power source and a focus for the characters will to use that power. It is only with long term exposure to such crystals and the energies that are contained within them that players may discover that they have changed enough to power their "magic" without the need of the crystals. There would also be an element of survival against other things that are attracted to the crystals... some which may not even be from Earth.

                                  The standard levels of magic, or Standard Fantasy will still see a lot of magical object, but also the appearance of the first "wizards" and "sorcerers" who no longer need the Flux Crystals to perform their magics... but still use them to achieve even higher levels of power. This is where we start to see truly impressive creations of magical engineering... such as flying ships and autonomous Golems... though these are not every day occurrences. It is when the boundaries of what is known of "magic" are pushed... but one also has to be careful, least something push back. While cities like Atlantis are likely to be hubs of magical discoveries... there are still other places where such things are rare... and even the "advanced" cities may be more advanced in some areas while not as advanced in others as other cities are. This is settings like the various worlds of Dungeons and Dragons and most other Fantasy RPGs.

                                  High Fantasy settings, where magical items are as common as technology is for us today... are surprisingly rare. This would see cities like Atlantis at the height of their power... and likely at the height of their hubris... where those in the setting's main hubs of civilization think that they are so powerful and so superior that nothing could possibly topple their prosperity... at least until something does. It could be war... either from within or from without... against other equally powerful cities... or from something far beyond what they have yet experienced. Perhaps their use and exploitation of Flux Crystals or the dimensional barriers has attracted the attention of something(s) so alien and powerful that even the might of cities like Atlantis are nearly powerless against it (something like the Elder Gods of Lovecraftian lore). Maybe one or more of these cities thought themselves the equal of such beings or wanted to control such beasts... never realizing how powerful they truly were until it was too late. Great power often comes with great responsibilities... and great heroes often make great sacrifices. The end of an age of High Fantasy is often one of catastrophy and ruin... where the heights that had once been reached will never be reached again (or at least not for a very long time)... though there may be some remnants of such an age left behind for others to pick up and try to rebuild what was lost.

                                  The end of the mythic age of Atlantis may be one of tragedy... but it will be the players who will decide if it is though divine punishment for their hubris... or of noble sacrifice for the preservation of their world. Either way, the tales of their adventures in this age of myth will become legend.

                                          The first main era for Atlantis will be just a few generations after the early tribes that will found these early cities have settled in a new land (a century or two). Like many of the early human tribes, they were nomadic hunter-gathers... but where other tribes feared and mistrusted the strange crystals and the unusual things that often accompanied them, this tribe sought to understand the crystals. As they gathered more, exceptional individuals began to rise among the tribe... some as mystics... others as great warriors... and a few as daring inventors. Eventually however, they were eventually forced to move on... though for what reason, none can remember.

                                          Their mystics lead them to a new and unspoiled land untouched by any other tribe of man... by listening to the call of the crystals that they had gathered. The crystals guided them through the dimensional fissures that crossed the world... to a new land far from any they had previously known. While there were still many dangers in this unknown land, there was also great opportunity too... and a vast supply of the very crystals that had allowed them to prosper in their old homeland. The tribe spread out, settling many villages over the years as they quickly progressed from a hunter-gatherer to a more agrarian society. The early villages have grown into small fortified cities (the land is still fairly dangerous), but the ingenuity of the Alyn people has allowed them to incorporate the mystic crystals that they have found into simple tools, weapons and armor, allowing them to tame more of this wild land and begin building a civilization.

                                          Maybe your character will be a daring hero who wields such "mystic" weapons and armor in defense of their village... or a cunning rogue from another city seeking out weakness in another. Perhaps you are a mystic who listens to the songs of the crystals to gain insight into the world... and see where danger may soon come from. Maybe you are one of the truly exceptional who has been gifted by the crystals with abilities never before seen (but somewhat matched by the powers of the mystics and inventions of the heroes). Regardless, Alyn is but one city-state and the land of Anaria is vast with many secrets still hidden within... and who knows what lies beyond the seas in other lands?

                                          This will generally be a low fantasy type era... where few have anything more that skill and a blade to see them through. While a few will have apparently "mystic"power, most will need an outside power source (Flux Crystals) to fuel those powers regularly and reliably. This isn't to say that such individuals are powerless without them... but rather that it takes a lot of effort to do so without the crystals. The Flux Crystals are the main source of Inspiration and power in this early era, and act as an outside reserve of power for the characters, supplementing their own minor pools of similar powers (humanity has yet to truly adapt to the use of the power on a genetic level, thus their own stores of the various forms of Flux will be fairly small). While these crystals can be drained of their power reserves, they will also regenerate those reserves over time... thus replacing them isn't a concern. Most exceptional individuals (such as the characters) may have a fair collection of crystals they can draw upon... though how they do so will vary.

                                          Heroes (Talents) will tend to use crystal empowered tools and equipment, channeling the Flux of the crystals into extraordinary skill and surprising luck. Mystics (Psiads) will tend to wear the crystals (in elaborate jewelry or adorned to ritualistic instruments like wands and staves), listening to their songs and gaining remarkable insights into the world around them. Champions (Stalwarts) have developed a means of empowering themselves through crushing the crystals and applying it to their bodies (as ritualistically applied warpaint or even as tattoos) that can grant them truly remarkable abilities (for as long as the paint lasts or when they empower their tattoos). Overall numbers of these remarkable individuals is fairly low... with one Hero able to channel the Power of the Crystals for ever 10 villagers who can not... one Mystic able to hear the Song of the Crystals for ever 10 Heroes and one Champion able to receive the Gift of the Crystals for every 10 Mystics (on average, at least in Alyn). Why only some people are affected by the crystals to these degrees is just one of the mysteries they still possess.

                                          The adventures of these individuals will mostly involve taming the wilds around the various city-states (Alyn being the one I will focus upon mostly throughout my development of this setting) and making contact with the other cities that have also risen in this new land (known as Anaria). There will also be others inhabiting the land in remote areas... some of which have been secretly watching the human tribes as they established their communities... and waiting to see how they will use the powers of the crystals. Some of these Watchers seek to test the newcomers... to see if they are worthy of learning deeper secrets... while others may decide to use their own knowledge of such secrets to control or exploit these emerging cities. A few may even come to see these humans as a threat and will unleash unspeakable monstrosities upon the early city-states to wipe the land clean of these "invaders". As for who and what these Watchers are... that remains to be discovered.

                                              The Second Era of Atlantis will be about 1,000 years or so after the First. The City of Alyn has grown to become the Capitol of the Nation of Anaria. Other City-States have also grown and have developed their own character and culture as well... but deffer to Alyn as the first among equals (for the most part). The land of Anaria has been tamed for the most part (still pockets of true wilderness here and there), but for the most part, the secrets of the hand have been uncovered (if not completely understood just yet).

                                              The Flux Crystals that once empowered the exceptional of Alyn (and other city-states) have now become fairly common... used to create a sort of magical technology to help spread civilization through the city and the land. Most of it merely enhances the otherwise mundane tools used by most people, but the most advanced uses are truly miraculous inventions that defy belief. Comparably, much of this technology would be equivalent to what would be found during the mid 19th to early 20th centuries in terms of what sort of common technologies would be available (though powered by Flux Crystals rather than coal, steam or oil). Society is progressing from an agrarian society to a more industrialized one (though it is "magic" fueled industry).

                                              Having survived the tests of the Watchers (and some attempts by a few to conquer or destroy their emerging society), the people of Anaria have gained a deeper understanding of the powers the crystals channel... and are now turning their attention to the world beyond the seas. Have other human tribes progressed as much as they have? Are their other civilizations out there that use the crystals as they have? Some of the Watchers mentioned that Anaria was not the only land that was under observation... and perhaps now it is time for the Anarians to seek out new lands and new adventures... but to do so they will need a new breed of adventurers to brave these new wilds.

                                              As the understanding of the Flux Crystals have grown... so to have the methods by which exceptional individuals draw power from them. No longer are such individuals merely drawing on the power from the crystals, but now use the crystals to enhance and channel their own power. While Heroes still focus their power through extraordinary skill and luck (and use crystal empowered equipment), but they can do far more with them than before and can enhance nearly any skill with the use of a few crystal foci on even mundane equipment. Mystics no longer need to wear or have the crystals close to them to hear their song... and can more easily draw upon their own mental reserves of energy, though the crystals can still enhance their abilities to higher levels. Champions are possibly the most changed as their empowerment by the crystals has become almost permanent... drawing upon their own internal power rather than needing to be recharged from time to time (they can still exhaust their own formidable reserves and recharging can help, but they will recover in time and may not be near a means of recharge). Power level wise, these exceptional individuals will be high Tier 2 to low Tier 3 levels of power... about what you would expect from Proxies and average Novas... and will show some impressive abilities that will easily be viewed as "magical" by others.

                                              With such levels of power will come a greater amount of responsibility as these individuals will likely be envoys to distant lands where they will explore and tame... possibly helping out less advanced cultures or fighting more aggressive ones (which may or may not be as advanced as they are). New dangers also await... as are other Watchers that oversee these new lands. With greater levels of power at their command, the players may even accidentally release new threats that had once been contained through their actions (either because of curiosity or through ignorance of what they are doing).                                                   The Last Era for Atlantis will deal with the final fate of Atlantis and many of the other mythical nations that arose in this time... almost all of which will have long since vanished into legend by the time ancient civilizations known to the modern word were first forming. This could be anywhere from mere centuries to even thousands of years after the 2nd Era, but most likely happened before the last major ice age that shaped much of the world we know today.

                                                  The City of Alyn (Atlantis) and many other city-states have grown powerful from the various forms of Flux Crystal-based technology that has been developed. Achievement-wise, this technology would likely be able to create equivalent devices that are available to late 20th to the early 22nd century (including the Nova and Bio-tech devices seen in Aberrant and Aeon). While such technology would be readily available to cities like Alyn (with the same levels of access that people have to such things today... your average person can't access a nuclear weapon, so the average citizen of Alyn isn't likely to have a death-ray capable of leveling nations), most of the similarly advanced nations of this time will have seen the disastrous consequences of allowing such technology to fall into the hands of less developed nations (some likely destroyed themselves, while others have potentially become a serious threat to the developed nations that needs to be dealt with delicately).

                                                  On a larger scale, the Watchers that were influential in the previous settings have vanished. Whether they have all been killed (or merely died) or left for other worlds remains a mystery, but the Earth is now the sole responsibility of Humanity... to defend or exploit as they so desire.

                                                  Power-wise, the Heroes, Mystics and Champions will mostly be in the Tier 3 range... with their mastery of their powers from the last era having grown to match this level. While Heroes will still mostly focus on skills and probability manipulations (maybe even some time manipulation as well). Mystics are likely gaining mastery of their noetic powers, as are Champions. Some of the these individuals are likely showing evidence of instability to some degree or another (some Champions showing the effects of Taint/Corruption, some Mystics showing Psychic Dysfunction, while some Talents see the potential for their powers to be used for their personal gain). This means that many of the threats that will be faced in this setting will come from other beings similar to the Characters... possibly even from NPCs trying to reach Tier 4 levels of power to rule over the Earth as Sorcerer or God-Kings.

                                                  Of course these Errants (Inspired individuals that seek power for their own gain regardless of who has to suffer for them to attain it) will not be the only threat. Other threats from rogue nations (as advanced as Alyn or from less advanced nations that have gained access to dangerous Flux-Crystal technology/knowledge) as well as from other realities and worlds could also plague the chaacters of this age. While characters may have faced off against Tier 3 entities in the 2nd Era, they will seem like nothing compared to Tier 4 level threats that could have taken notice of Earth in this 3rd Era of Atlantis. Remember, while the fractures in the dimensional barriers allow energy to reach Earth, they will also allow the energy released from the use of the Flux Crystals to flow in the other direction as well. It is very possible that the very technology that allowed Alyn and other nations to become so advanced has attracted the attention of beings that could be the downfall of this mythic age.

                                                  In the end, these will likely be the last battles that the people of Alyn and the nation of Anaria will fight... and the legends they will inspire will last for ages to come. The final fate of Atlantis may be in the characters' hands, but their legacy will live on in the Trinity Continuum.
