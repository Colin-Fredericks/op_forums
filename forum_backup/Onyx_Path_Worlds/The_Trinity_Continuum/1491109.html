    More Power! Psions and Novas under the Talent rules

    08-23-2022, 02:40 PM
    So, I purchased Mateus Luz's excellent House Rules: Quantum Nexus supplement which, among other things, provides guidelines as to how to use the rules for Novas to model stalwarts, Superiors, Sub-Aberrants, and psiads; and it made me wonder if something similar could be done with the rules for Talents. I know that mesmerists and stalwarts already do so; and I'd almost certainly use that as my starting point for this project.

    But how would you modify that to provide for the equivalent of Novas and psions? In the former case, would it be enough to remove the cap on Scale and maybe add a handful of Dynamic Gifts to cover Nova powers that can't be modeled with the existing Dynamic Gifts? For psions, would it mainly be a case of restructuring and extending the Psychic Gifts (e.g., splitting the Biological, Cognitive, and Energetic categories to into subcategories corresponding to the Aptitudes, and adding some more potent Gifts to represent powers available to psions but not psiads; and then restricting each psion to one subcategory), or would there need to be some underlying structural changes as well?

    The idea behind this approach is to allow for the inclusion of Novas and psions in games that otherwise use just the Talents rules, without having to purchase and use the rules from TC:Æon or TC:Aberrant to do so. But would the amount of additional material needed to make this work make it a moot point?

    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

          On a quick think about the subject:
          - Aberrant system is very flexible to be completely turned into a set of gifts without a monstruous effort, but a possible idea is to have a few sets of powers with a "Gift Tree" that may direct those powers to certain concepts, like the set presented in Adventure! with the Cold thematic effects and the Heat thematic effects. It would make FAR simpler to build a Nova, but would make them much more restricted in the options of fluffy.
          - Maxing out and Power Stunts become more like Dramatic Editing and less "success for a dot"
          - I would not go in the direction of Transcendence, but transformations could be Conditions that when come to play grant Inspiration instead of Moment, allow them to use more powers more often if they are weird.
          - Psi powers are far more simple to simulate as they are pretty limited in fluffy, but the way they work as skills that can do a lot is hard to model as . I would maybe make something like each mode of an aptitude be based in a facet and they gain gifts that evolve according to the facet grow instead of many gifts that evolve.

          On a alternative approach that can work for both templates, the character may gain gifts that allow them to gain dots in skills and with those skills do powers by paying Inspiration, like the Psi Modes.

                      The Magog rules from Aether already let you play nova level characters using Gifts.

                      There are several Magog Gifts that boost a type of Scale by one of your Facets (ie. Endurance of Steel boosts Durability Scale by your Destructive Facet); two are Destructive, three are Intuitive, and two are Reflective. Stick one dot in each Facet and choose the proper Gifts to gain Scale 2 in any areas of your choice or stick three dots in a single Facet to gain Scale 4 to a more limited range of Gifts.

                      The Paragon Gift can be taken up to nine times (once per Attribute), and each purchase boosts a type of Scale. Multiple purchases let you boost your Speed, Durability, and Leadership Scales by 2. This probably stacks with the previous Gifts and may stack with edges like Swift and Strongman. You could get a starting character with Scale 6 Durability or Leadership, or Scale 7 Power or Speed. Investing 10 xp in the same Facet (two sessions worth of xp) gets you to Scale 8 or 9. This is far more power than a beginning nova.

                              Following what I had previously written about using Inspiration as the only measure of how you interact with your source for Psi and Quantum.

                              It was Inspiration All Along
                              Inspiration, as presented in Trinity Continuum Core Rulebook has many interesting impacts over the game that are lost in the way Psi and Quantum works. It’s possible to switch back that, turning Inspiration in the measure of the character control over their powers, no matter what power source it is. There are a few changes in the way it works, as it impacts all aspects previously related to Psi and Quantum, the prerequisites in certain powers and aptitudes, and the way you spend Inspiration to use powers.

                              Psychic Inspiration
                              Inspiration replaces Psi as the measure of control over psi a character have. It replaces the use of Psi whenever it affects difficulties to affect the character, to define the Formatting Tolerance of the character and to define travel time using Teleportation modes.

                              Psion Template and Advancement
                              The Psion template remains exactly the same except for, instead of receiving Psi 2, the Psion gains 3 dots to distribute among the 3 facets. During step 6 of character creation, the Psion can exchange the Attribute dot by a Facet dot. Increasing each facet by 1 dot costs 10 experience, but the Psion can only have up to 4 dots in each Facet. Inspiration Effects Psi have many impacts in the powers of a Psion that are all attributed to specific facets, following the tables below.
                              When the Psion achieves Inspiration 6, the mastery they have over Psi changes their target number 7, allow them to gain the 6th dot in their aptitude modes, 2nd dot in auxiliary modes and 6th dot in all attributes (without the Superior Trait Edge), adds 1 dice to any facet roll and regains two points of Inspiration at the start of each new scene. Achieving Inspiration 7 allows the Psion to gain 7th dot powers of their aptitudes, increases the Size and Combat scale by 1, the Attunement range by 1 range band (or 10 times for Extreme) and 2 dice to any facet roll (not cumulative with psi 6).

                              Inspiration ... Primary ... Auxiliary ... TN
                              3 .................... 5 ............ 1 .............. 8
                              4 .................... 5 ............ 1 .............. 8
                              5 .................... 5 ............ 1 .............. 8
                              6 .................... 6 ............ 2 .............. 7
                              7 .................... 7 ............ 2 .............. 7

                              Quantakinesis and Teleportation
                              Quantakinesis and Teleportation have a minimum Inspiration of 4 instead of Psi 3. It’s won’t force increasing their Psi by exchanging an Attribute dot for Psi as before, only requiring a specific distribution of facet dots. Facet Effects Psi have many impacts in the powers of a Psion that are all attributed to specific facets, following the tables below.
                              Destructive ... Size
                              0 ..................... 0
                              1 ..................... 1
                              2 ..................... 1
                              3 ..................... 2
                              4 ..................... 2
                              Intuitive ... Range ... Attunement Range
                              0 ............. Close ..... Short
                              1 ............. Short ...... Medium
                              2 ............. Short ...... Long
                              3 ............. Medium .. Extreme (10 kilometer)
                              4 ............. Long ....... Extreme (100 kilometer)
                              Reflective ... Duration
                              0 ................. Rounds
                              1 ................. Rounds
                              2 ................. Minutes
                              3 ................. Hours
                              4 ................. Days

                              Power Dice Pools
                              All powers that dice pools were Psi + Mode become Facet + Mode, and the chosen facet is defined by the approach intended with the power, Destructive for Force approach, Intuitive for Finesse approach and Reflective for Resilience approach. It includes regular uses of power and when power reaching.

                              Spending and Recovering Inspiration
                              A Psion may spend Inspiration the same way as a talent, except for not being able to Dramatic Edition (optionally the Storyguide may make it possible). The same way, a Psion also recovers Inspiration as a Talent, except Psions require exposure to psi to recover, instead of flux. Psions can also recover Inspiration equal to their Reflective facet by spending a scene meditating.
                              Psi powers require spending 1 Inspiration for every psi of its cost, including additional psi to expand effects and reaching higher mode dots. When using powers of lower mode dots, each mode dot above it reduces the cost by 1 point, in addition to other effects, to a minimum of 0 Inspiration. Some powers suffer small changes to better fit the system.
                              Drain (Quantakinesis, Fundamentals 3): The psion takes one Inspiration or Corruption from her target per success, adding Inspiration to her pool or the pool of any single psion within his Range at a rate of one Inspiration point per 2 Inspiration or Corruption drained.

                              Empowering
                              Combining powers and super science that increase Psi instead increase an specific facet, if it usually limited to Psi 5, it may increase the Facet up to 3, if it was limited to Psi 7, it may increase the Facet up to 4. Some powers suffer small changes to better fit the system.
                              Empowerment (Vitakinesis, Augendis 3): Instead of increasing separated aspects of the patient psi, the Vitakinetic increases one of the facets by 1 per 3 successes up to 4.

                              Quantum Powered Inspiration
                              Inspiration replaces Quantum as the measure of control over quantum a character have. It replaces the use of Quantum whenever it affects difficulties to affect the character, to define the maximum number of Q-Tech that can be attuned and to define travel time using Warp and Teleportation.

                              Nova Template and Advancement
                              The Nova template remains the same except for, instead of receiving Quantum 1, the Nova gains 1 dot to set to any of the 3 facets. Each Facet works as an independent Nova Trait, costing 12 experience, and can be bought by half the cost if the nova also takes a point of Transcendence at that time.

                              Spending and Recovering Inspiration
                              A Nova may spend Inspiration the same way as a talent, except for not being able to Dramatic Edition (optionally the Storyguide may make it possible). The same way, a Nova also recovers Inspiration as a Talent, except novas require exposure to quantum to recover, instead of flux.
                              A Nova may also use a reflexive action to gain 1 Flux and recover immediately their Destructive Facet Inspiration. The cost of each Quantum Power or Mega-Edge is reduced by half the related Facet (round up), to a minimum of 0.
                              Some powers suffer small changes to better fit the system.
                              Absorption: The power works as described, except that, instead of recovering Quantum Points, the character receives 1 point of Inspiration for every 2 successes. Check Overcharge bellow for more information about the excess of Inspiration.
                              Quantum Leech: The power works as described, except that, instead of taking the target’s Quantum Points, the character takes 1 point of Inspiration for every 2 successes and adds to their own. Check Overcharge bellow for more information about the excess of Inspiration.
                              Vampiric Tag: If the Nova has the quantum absorbing option, each injury caused to the target the nova regains 1 Inspiration.

                              Overcharge
                              Some Novas can absorb energies to recharge their Inspiration. The rules for Overcharge don’t change, except for trading Quantum Points for Inspiration.
                              A nova with more than their normal amount of Inspiration acquires either the Aberrant Eyes or Anima Aura transformation, as their nova nature literally shines out.
                              When they have 3 or more above their normal amount of Inspiration, they acquire both previous transformations as well as the Energy Bleed transformation, giving off random sparks or flashes of energy.
                              At 5 or more above their normal, the nova acquires the Energy Emissions transformation, barely able to contain the sheer amount of power. For each minute the nova remains at this level of Inspiration, they also suffer either an Injury Condition or acquire a point of Flux, as the nova’s player chooses, until their Inspiration total drops below 5 above normal.

                              Burn Out
                              For every Inspiration a nova expends after reaching zero Inspiration, the nova takes an Injury Condition. This is considered lethal damage, and novas can be Taken Out by this damage (commonly known as “burning out”). The nova suffers the Injury Condition after taking the action where they spend the Inspiration.

                              Power Dice Pools
                              All powers that dice pools wereL Quantum + Power become Facet + Power, and the chosen facet is defined by the approach intended with the power, Destructive for Force approach, Intuitive for Finesse approach and Reflective for Resilience approach. It includes regular uses of power and when power stunting.
                              When Novas reaches Inspiration 6, the mastery they have over Quantum grants them 1 enhancement in all Facet + Power Rolls, including power stunts.
                              At Inspiration 7 it increases to 2 enhancements, at Inspiration 8 to 3 enhancements and at 9 to 4 enhancements.

                              Quantum Minimum and Prerequisites
                              Quantum cease to be the reference of the character power, giving its place to individual Facets, affecting all Mega-Attributes and Quantum Powers. Force Mega-Attributes have Facet Minimum (like Quantum Minimum) equal to Destructive Dots, Finesse Mega-Attributes have Facet Minimum (like Quantum Minimum) equal to Intuitive Dots and Resilience Mega-Attributes have Facet Minimum (like Quantum Minimum) equal to Reflective Dots. Quantum powers, instead of Quantum Minimum, have a Facet Minimum, with the related facet defined according to the following list.
                              For example, to have dots in Flight, the Nova must have at least the same number of dots in Intuitive Facet (previously Quantum Minimum was Dots), and to have dots in Quantum Imprint, the character must have at least 4 dots in Destructive facet (previously Quantum Minimum was 4).
                              Power ............................... Facet Minimum
                              Absorption ........................ Reflective 2
                              Boost ................................ Destructive Dots
                              Cloak ................................ Intuitive 2
                              Density ............................. Reflective Dots
                              Elemental Mastery ........... Intuitive 2
                              Environmental Anima ....... Destructive Dots
                              Flight ................................ Intuitive Dots
                              Growth ............................. Reflective Dots
                              Healing ............................ Destructive 2
                              Illusion ............................. Intuitive 2
                              Molecular Chameleon ..... Reflective 4
                              Molecular Manipulation ... Intuitive 3
                              Morph .............................. Destructive Dots -3
                              Phasing ........................... Intuitive 2
                              Plasticity .......................... Reflective 2
                              Quantum Agent ............... Destructive Dots
                              Quantum Anima .............. Intuitive Dots
                              Quantum Attack .............. Destructive Dots
                              Quantum Aura ................ Destructive Dots
                              Quantum Construct ........ Intuitive Dots +1
                              Quantum Deflection ........ Reflective Dots
                              Quantum Disruption ........ Reflective 3
                              Quantum Field ................ Reflective Dots
                              Quantum Imprint ............. Destructive 4
                              Quantum Leech .............. Destructive 2
                              Remote Perception ......... Intuitive 3
                              Shrinking ........................ Reflective Dots -1
                              Shroud ........................... Reflective 2
                              Teleport .......................... Intuitive Dots +1
                              Transformation ............... Destructive 4
                              Transmutation ................ Destructive 4
                              Warp .............................. Intuitive Dots +2
                              Technique Mega-Edge prerequisites become Intuitive facet equal to Edge Dots, and Toughness Mega-Edge prerequisites become Reflective facet equal to Edge Dots. Inspiration is also the maximum cost of any tag the character may have in their powers.
                              When Novas reaches Inspiration 6, they reduce by 1 the Facet Minimum of all their Quantum Powers and Mega-Attributes dots by 1, as well as the prerequisites in Facets. For example, if a Nova has Destructive 3 and Intuitive 5 (Inspiration 6), the character may have Quantum Attack 4, Mega Dexterity 6 and Toughness 1.
                              At Inspiration 7 the Facet Minimum and Prerequisites are reduced by 2, at Inspiration 8 by 3 and at 9 by 4.

                              Transcendence
                              Without Quantum trait, a Nova increase Transcendence by 1 when reach 4 and 5 in each of the Facets.

                              Advancement and Maxing Out
                              Novas can’t max out Inspiration itself, instead they max out each Facet independently. A Nova just need to max out a Facet once before increasing it, as for every other nova Trait. The Nova spend 1 Inspiration and gains 1 Flux, rolls Inspiration and each success can grant a single nova trait. Spending 1 Inspiration

                              Super Science
                              When building Q-tech, a nova inventor must meet the Facet minimums for any power she builds into a device, but she need not possess the power herself. When attuning Q-tech, a nova must meet the Facet minimums for any power built into the device. Attuning to a Q-Tech costs 1 Inspiration, but it is not imbued to the item nor can’t be recovered. When the Attunement is broken the Nova don’t receive back any Inspiration. Activating an attuned Q-Tech also don’t requires committing Inspiration, instead it requires the character pay 1 Inspiration if the Q-Tech have any Intrinsic power (the spent Inspiration can be recovered as usual) when first activating it, plus the cost of any other power when using them. It is possible for novas who are not attuned to a Q-tech device to gain some limited use from it. The player rolls the nova’s highest Facet, and on a success can use the device for one Scene before it shuts down

                                          Power Dice Pools
                                          All powers that dice pools were Psi + Mode become Facet + Mode, and the chosen facet is defined by the approach intended with the power, Destructive for Force approach, Intuitive for Finesse approach and Reflective for Resilience approach. It includes regular uses of power and when power reaching.

                                          Spending and Recovering Inspiration
                                          A Psion may spend Inspiration the same way as a talent, except for not being able to Dramatic Edition (optionally the Storyguide may make it possible). The same way, a Psion also recovers Inspiration as a Talent, except Psions require exposure to psi to recover, instead of flux. Psions can also recover Inspiration equal to their Reflective facet by spending a scene meditating.
                                          Psi powers require spending 1 Inspiration for every psi of its cost, including additional psi to expand effects and reaching higher mode dots. When using powers of lower mode dots, each mode dot above it reduces the cost by 1 point, in addition to other effects, to a minimum of 0 Inspiration. Some powers suffer small changes to better fit the system.
                                          Drain (Quantakinesis, Fundamentals 3): The psion takes one Inspiration or Corruption from her target per success, adding Inspiration to her pool or the pool of any single psion within his Range at a rate of one Inspiration point per 2 Inspiration or Corruption drained.

                                          Empowering
                                          Combining powers and super science that increase Psi instead increase an specific facet, if it usually limited to Psi 5, it may increase the Facet up to 3, if it was limited to Psi 7, it may increase the Facet up to 4. Some powers suffer small changes to better fit the system.
                                          Empowerment (Vitakinesis, Augendis 3): Instead of increasing separated aspects of the patient psi, the Vitakinetic increases one of the facets by 1 per 3 successes up to 4.

                                          Quantum Powered Inspiration
                                          Inspiration replaces Quantum as the measure of control over quantum a character have. It replaces the use of Quantum whenever it affects difficulties to affect the character, to define the maximum number of Q-Tech that can be attuned and to define travel time using Warp and Teleportation.

                                          Nova Template and Advancement
                                          The Nova template remains the same except for, instead of receiving Quantum 1, the Nova gains 1 dot to set to any of the 3 facets. Each Facet works as an independent Nova Trait, costing 12 experience, and can be bought by half the cost if the nova also takes a point of Transcendence at that time.

                                          Spending and Recovering Inspiration
                                          A Nova may spend Inspiration the same way as a talent, except for not being able to Dramatic Edition (optionally the Storyguide may make it possible). The same way, a Nova also recovers Inspiration as a Talent, except novas require exposure to quantum to recover, instead of flux.
                                          A Nova may also use a reflexive action to gain 1 Flux and recover immediately their Destructive Facet Inspiration. The cost of each Quantum Power or Mega-Edge is reduced by half the related Facet (round up), to a minimum of 0.
                                          Some powers suffer small changes to better fit the system.
                                          Absorption: The power works as described, except that, instead of recovering Quantum Points, the character receives 1 point of Inspiration for every 2 successes. Check Overcharge bellow for more information about the excess of Inspiration.
                                          Quantum Leech: The power works as described, except that, instead of taking the target’s Quantum Points, the character takes 1 point of Inspiration for every 2 successes and adds to their own. Check Overcharge bellow for more information about the excess of Inspiration.
                                          Vampiric Tag: If the Nova has the quantum absorbing option, each injury caused to the target the nova regains 1 Inspiration.

                                          Overcharge
                                          Some Novas can absorb energies to recharge their Inspiration. The rules for Overcharge don’t change, except for trading Quantum Points for Inspiration.
                                          A nova with more than their normal amount of Inspiration acquires either the Aberrant Eyes or Anima Aura transformation, as their nova nature literally shines out.
                                          When they have 3 or more above their normal amount of Inspiration, they acquire both previous transformations as well as the Energy Bleed transformation, giving off random sparks or flashes of energy.
                                          At 5 or more above their normal, the nova acquires the Energy Emissions transformation, barely able to contain the sheer amount of power. For each minute the nova remains at this level of Inspiration, they also suffer either an Injury Condition or acquire a point of Flux, as the nova’s player chooses, until their Inspiration total drops below 5 above normal.

                                          Burn Out
                                          For every Inspiration a nova expends after reaching zero Inspiration, the nova takes an Injury Condition. This is considered lethal damage, and novas can be Taken Out by this damage (commonly known as “burning out”). The nova suffers the Injury Condition after taking the action where they spend the Inspiration.

                                          Power Dice Pools
                                          All powers that dice pools wereL Quantum + Power become Facet + Power, and the chosen facet is defined by the approach intended with the power, Destructive for Force approach, Intuitive for Finesse approach and Reflective for Resilience approach. It includes regular uses of power and when power stunting.
                                          When Novas reaches Inspiration 6, the mastery they have over Quantum grants them 1 enhancement in all Facet + Power Rolls, including power stunts.
                                          At Inspiration 7 it increases to 2 enhancements, at Inspiration 8 to 3 enhancements and at 9 to 4 enhancements.

                                          Quantum Minimum and Prerequisites
                                          Quantum cease to be the reference of the character power, giving its place to individual Facets, affecting all Mega-Attributes and Quantum Powers. Force Mega-Attributes have Facet Minimum (like Quantum Minimum) equal to Destructive Dots, Finesse Mega-Attributes have Facet Minimum (like Quantum Minimum) equal to Intuitive Dots and Resilience Mega-Attributes have Facet Minimum (like Quantum Minimum) equal to Reflective Dots. Quantum powers, instead of Quantum Minimum, have a Facet Minimum, with the related facet defined according to the following list.

                                          Super Science
                                          When building Q-tech, a nova inventor must meet the Facet minimums for any power she builds into a device, but she need not possess the power herself. When attuning Q-tech, a nova must meet the Facet minimums for any power built into the device. Attuning to a Q-Tech costs 1 Inspiration, but it is not imbued to the item nor can’t be recovered. When the Attunement is broken the Nova don’t receive back any Inspiration. Activating an attuned Q-Tech also don’t requires committing Inspiration, instead it requires the character pay 1 Inspiration if the Q-Tech have any Intrinsic power (the spent Inspiration can be recovered as usual) when first activating it, plus the cost of any other power when using them. It is possible for novas who are not attuned to a Q-tech device to gain some limited use from it. The player rolls the nova’s highest Facet, and on a success can use the device for one Scene before it shuts down
