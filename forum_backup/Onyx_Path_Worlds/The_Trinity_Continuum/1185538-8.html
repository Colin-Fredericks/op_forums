                    Originally posted by Lian View Post
                    So a biokin with clone what happens if the origional dies?
                    We’ve deliberately left that undefined. I’d suggest going one of the following four ways, depending on your campaign’s needs:

                    1) All clones suddenly cease to exist.
                    2) All clones continue to survive, and live until they die. None can ever develop the Clone power.
                    3) All clones continue to survive, and the eldest clone gains the potential to develop the Clone power. (Note, I say potential to develop. It’s not automatic, and if the biokinetic’s eldest clone was deliberately created as much less powerful than the proxy, it’s going to have a lot of power-development to do.)
                    4) All clones continue to survive, and the last existing clone gains the potential to develop the Clone power. Battle Royale with a bunch of duplicates who suddenly have a motive to kill one another, and whose biokinetic powers explicitly *do* affect each other. This could be delightfully inventive and nasty.

                    Personally, I’d probably go with option 3, but needs may vary.
