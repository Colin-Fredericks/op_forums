      1- Doubt, but...

                          Originally posted by Penelope View Post
                          Random but really important question

                          If the Doyen Shield exists around Earth between the Hammersmith event and N-Day, does that mean there are stalwarts and mesmerists in the core setting (as well as Talents)? If so, can they still use Dramatic Editing?

                          Thanks 😊
                          My understanding is that they're still around; but they've become rare. This is due to the ambient energies fading away over time. As for Dramatic Editing: I'm unsure. The fasting away of the telluric energies has left the Talents without the Dramatic Editing discount that the daredevils had during the Adventure era; it's possible that the ability of stalwarts and mesmerists to engage in similar Dramatic Editing has all but disappeared; or it may still be around, at the same +1 Inspiration cost (or possibly even higher). What they should definitely keep are their own unique forms of Dramatic Editing (the ability to change up their Psychic Gifts in the case of mesmerists, and the ability to exceed their limits in the case of stalwarts).

                          If another Event takes place while the Doyen barrier is up, you get a resurgence of the Adventure era, but on a smaller scale: more localized, and a shorter duration. But where it happens and while it lasts, you get a boom: if it's a Hammersmith-style Event, you get a resurgence of daredevils, stalwarts, and mesmerists; if it's a precursor to Divas Mal's Event, you get a return of stalwarts, possibly with some sort of upgrade making them a bit more like Novas; and if you get a precursor to SK's Event, you get a return of mesmerists, possibly with some sort of upgrade making them a bit more like psiads.

                                  Originally posted by shadowninja View Post
                                  I have a few questions about Stalwarts (from Adventure! setting):
                                  1 - Its possible they exist in Æon Trinity era?
                                  1 - Not naturally, as noted several times above. If you really want stalwarts to exist in Æon, you'll need an appropriate MacGuffin. Maybe some Aberrant War era nova wanted to protect the human populace of their town from others of his kind, so built a device to generate a quantum energy suppression zone (avoiding the worst of the effect themself by generally staying inside the 'eye' of the effect or just dorming down to interact with the townsfolk).

                                  The device's creator transcended years ago, so the thing is slowly breaking down without proper maintenance; sending out jolts of quantum energy immediately followed by a renewed suppressive effect. This results in people walking near the device occasionally turning into stalwarts.

                                  One the one hand, the proper way to handle inexplicable quantum tech is to haul it off and destroy it. On the other hand, a device that suppresses nova powers over an area the size of a town is priceless. On the - most worrying - third hand, the device either failing or being removed may result in the town's stalwarts turning into novas, who then risk turning into aberrants.
