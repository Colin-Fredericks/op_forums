                                                            Originally posted by Demigod Beast View Post
                                                            Oh! Ok! Cool! I’ll definitely pick up TC Anima, when it comes out.

                                                            A question, though...

                                                            I noticed Talents are the ‘core’ character type, and they manipulate, luck, probability and chance to do some really awesome stuff. TC Aeon mentions Talents quite a lot. Is TC Anima, TC Aberrant, TC Assassins and TC Aether the same way, making Talents the only character-type playable in every time period?
                                                            I presume Talents are the default character type because:

                                                            1). An RPG's core book needs character creation rules.
                                                            2). Playing (mildly) superhuman characters is seen as cooler than playing a baseline human.
                                                            3). Daredevils were part of the original Adventure! setting.
                                                            4). The Talent mechanics are simple enough to cram into the core book without taking up much space.

                                                            As a comparison, the Talent rules take up about 30 pages, Nova rules take up 50+ pages, and Psi rules take up 60+ pages. Using Talents gets you an interesting character at a lower page count, which make the core book cheaper to print.

                                                            Talents also tend to be significantly weaker than most other character types, so the default assumption is that Aberrant players will probably play Novas and Aeon players will probably play Psions. Storyguides should be careful when mixing character types, as this can lead to some players almost completely dominating game sessions while others are reduced to punching out minor antagonists in the background. Even the Gift from the Aberrant book The Bigger They Are only lets a Talent compete with a Nova for 3-10 rounds, after which they're tapped out of Inspiration, while the Nova Antagonist will never run out of Mega-Might.
