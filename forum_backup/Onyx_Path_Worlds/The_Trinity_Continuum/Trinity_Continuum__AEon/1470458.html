    Why is Nippon Frightened of Novas?

    12-14-2021, 03:04 PM
    Nippon devloped a test to detect the potential for Nova (or unknown to them, Psionic) potential, and from this eventually sprang the Superior treatment.

    But... It strikes me that of all the nations, Nippon should have the most positive attitude towards Novas, especially since they canonically developed tests that allowed them to determine if a Nova would fall to madness. Because of this, Up until 2104, Nippon's government worked with dozens of sane novas, who played an immense role in ensuring the survival of the nation--and then, with the fall of the quantum barrier, died attempted to protect Earth from their maddened counterparts.

    So given the existence of tests to determine a Nova's sanity, and a test for Nova potential, why woudln't Nippon try to arrange for at least a few Nova eruptions? A superior is a powerful asset, but even so, Nova's, especially megaintellitent ones, had a great deal to offer Nippon.

      The test in TC is not about “turning mad”, it’s about being capable of erupt and be dedicated to the country.

      I have the feeling that, in TC, Aberrant War stories are told by the humans only and that not all the things that are narrated are really the way they narrated. I mean, novas did a lot of bad things, and killed many, but the war was fought by humans, Aberrants were not a team fighting a war, they were a bunch of individuals (as powerful as nations, to be fair) fighting for their lives and what they believed were their rights.

      Anyway, Nihon didn’t suffer from the war as the rest of the world did, but they know that once they have novas, they would be targeted by all other nations. The tests can’t detect capacity of going mad, only if they are capable of erupt and if they follow a strict psychological profile. Eruptions can be dangerous, even more living in closed archeologists, so just let a nova erupt may be risky. So they control the eruptions and still keep some of the nova advantages in the form of superiors.

      Thats not a bad deal.

                  Yeah--I mean, the Trinity core book mentions that by 2049, they had some method of testing novas for stability. not just potential, but whether or not a nova would go insane. So, it would seem to make sense to allow other novas to erupt and then test them. The later genetic test was just for whether or not you had nova potential.

                  Here's the text:

                  By 2049, tests allowed Nihonjin
                  scientists to discover which novas were most likely
                  to become dangerously unstable. Since there was no
                  way to prevent or reverse this growing madness, the
                  Nihonjin government attempted to exile or secretly
                  execute any nova who failed these tests.
                  When it closed its borders in 2053, the Nihonjin
                  government allowed more than three dozen sane and
                  loyal novas to remain. Tests revealed that these novas
                  would not become dangerously insane,


                  Now of course what the tests were and how foolproof they were is left open. It's possible that say, they had a 10 percent "fail" rate and after a few examples of "and our sane nova is now talking in third person." They may have decided to not risk it, though that does seem odd, since well, almost three dozen supergenius novas would seem to open up a lot of options for coming up with something.
