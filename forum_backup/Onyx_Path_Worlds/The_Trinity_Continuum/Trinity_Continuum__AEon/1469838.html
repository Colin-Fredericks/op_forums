      Not totally right.

      Doyens start observing Earth sometime before Adventure! Era (possibly related to Aether).

      In 1922 there is the Hammersmith event that started officially Adventure! Era, the observing Doyens raise a barrier that made it hard to handle Quantum and Psi inside and limited “teluric events” to a local level, preventing things like Hammersmith from happening and affecting the entire planet and more. The barrier has this side effect that increase the general “weirdness level”, making Flux Manipulation (Talent stuff) easier.

      In 2018, after more than a few trying, Divis Mal cause a Quantum event big enough to break the Barrier, and at same level increase the “background Quantum level” to a level that cause people to start erupting all around the world. As a side effect, it keeps Doyens away for a while, and Earth capable of defending from flying meteors and other weapons of the Doyens.

      Mal pumps a bit more Quantum during his time on Earth, keeping the Quantum level high enoug for a few more years until Ultimatum, when Novas leave Earth. The Quantum level slowly goes down, but the war left many Quantum flux zones, places that had their “quantum stability” broken to the point it causes mutations and general death to all inside.

      In 2104 Sarah Bhurano cause a new event, now using the knowledge she gathered over the last 200 years, reducing the general Quantum level, reducing quantum flux zones and creating a bunch of Psiads and latents (people capable of gather psi, but not use it). As a side effect, it unlocks Quantakinesis to all human Psiads.

      Doyens that where around noticed this event and has the idea of using humans to hunt the Aberrants that are all around with their lethal Quantum powers, and pick some humans and boost their psi use to create the first Psions, the Proxies, one of them Bhurano, that becomes the first Quantakinetic. They also give the humans the Prometheus chambers, to create Psions from the latents.

          Additionally, we know a bit more about Doyens

          They were a biological Psi user species once, that were so paranoid that they tried to ascend to real immortality in a forced way, becoming locked in a “mid term”, a psi powered bobble of plasma. They start hunting Quantum user races, as they can kill doyens by using their powers, while psi is not effective in it (except for Quantakinesis, but it’s a human thing).

          We also know they are not very United, and there are a limited number of them, as they can’t reproduce anymore, and previous attempts created monstrous offsprings (to their eyes). They work in groups and there are some of them that are absolutely powerful, but they are individually limited, so they need to help each other, even the most powerful among them, to properly kill races and destroy planets. So they use other races to their long term plans, evolving them to a level they can still control, but limiting their further evolution, like they did to the Qin and Chromatics.

          When the Quantakinetic powers start to be understood by the doyen, they got afraid and decided to kill all humans, not only the Aberrants (to be fair there were Doyens working in that for a while, and the Doyen Bering int he use of humans to kill humans just changed their mind).

          Eventually they would launch a war effort to kill humans, but that is not part of the timeline yet.
