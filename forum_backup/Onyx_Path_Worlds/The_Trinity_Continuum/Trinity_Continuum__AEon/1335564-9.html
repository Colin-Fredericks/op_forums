                                                    They’re not going to like it if humanity ever reaches Psi 8.

                                                    To be honest, I was thinking along the same lines... Psions cap out at Psi 7 because that is the limit of the technology the Doyen have provided them with (though I wasn't entirely sure if that was the limit of Doyen tech since such a paranoid species would very likely hoard the best for themselves to maintain some superior power over those they are trying to dominate). Given this (unofficial) position, it does seem reasonable that Psiads are caped at Psi 6 since humanity has yet to reach the level to unlock Psi 7 naturally (Psions can reach it through the unnatural means provided by the Prometheus Chambers, but that technology is only available to the Psi Orders.

                                                    In time, should the Psi Orders allow access to their Prometheus Chambers, it may become possible for them to be altered in such a way to allow Psiads to attain similar levels... but that would likely require decades of research at the very least (probably even centuries at this point). Ironically... the technology that they Doyen have already provided humanity might be the key that allows humanity to become the very threat they had hoped to prevent.

                                                    Personally though, I think that in my games I will alter the maximum dots that can be put into each mode for Psiads a bit... with them being able to advance to 4/2 (Primary/Secondary) at Psi 4, 4/3 at Psi 5 and 5/3 at Psi 6. By the time my players reach this level and start to take advantage of this, I will be able to judge just how powerful it makes them compared to Psions. Just to be clear, Psiads will not be able to get the 6th or 7th dot in any Primary Aptitude... either through stunting successes to perform at a higher level or through edges like Strong Mode. Psiads just aren't evolved enough to gain these levels of Psi abilities, even at Psi 6. I will also have a few other requirements for them to meet before they can reach such levels of Psi too... so it will likely take at least a year of weekly sessions (at 5 EXP per session) to reach this level of power. Hopefully that will be more than enough time for me to see how this balances out overall.
