      The Aeon/Trinity era has a number of alien species.

      - The Chromatics are sort of giant frog/dog things that use light to communicate via light patches on their skin and projected holographic shapes. They're sentient but relatively primitive, uplifted to serve as shock troops.
      - The Qin wear human-appearing biosuits but are actually closer to large slugs. They're masters of biotech and are mostly humanity's allies.
      - The Coalition are technically a single species, although they appear as a half-dozen different forms or more. The leaders basically collect DNA and then use the sequences they want to create specific servant races.
