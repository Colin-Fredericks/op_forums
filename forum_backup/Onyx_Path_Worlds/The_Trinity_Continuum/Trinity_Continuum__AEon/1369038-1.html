                                    Great first impression. Most pages are plain text (I like it!) but the art present is awesome. Honestly I didn’t read much, but what I read was great.

                                    I went thru the planets really fast, stoped for the aliens (Listeners are great, added a lot to the alien concepts I had).
                                    Technology chapter is good, a bit of everything (weapons, armors, vehicles and general equipment) including alien artifacts (I wasn’t expecting it, what is a great addition).
                                    Then we have the Qin... no words, I hope we can have at least one book in the nexus with a great description of the alien race as this chapter. First of all, it’s an entirely new template, what is what I was expecting, but they went thru edges and paths that are specifics to Qin. There are descriptions of a few Qin interstellar colonies, Qin tech (including Biosuits and lots of customization options) and something I was decently not expecting, a guide to play a Qin, not only on what they do and don’t, but the way they see the universe, the way the castes work, their reproduction, their beliefs.

                                    Thanks again to the devs!

                                                    Just bought Distant Worlds and began my journey through it. First thought, I can't wait till there is a physical book option as I want it on my shelf. Second thought, ooh, so many new shinies!

                                                    Third thought, Eden is cool. Fourth, the edges and such are very interesting. Fifth thought, the Qin are very cool. Sixth, Bullseye makes me mad. My seventh thought was that I need to stop jumping around and head to the beginning and read the actual book from start to finish. It is at this point that I am now at it. hehe

                                                    But yeah, Eden is awesome. I love that world and the novas that are on it. So to see it fleshed out a bit more in fascinating detail was really cool.

                                                    On other topics I will comment more in detail when I finish reading it but I need to mention something. Reading the stuff on Bullseye makes me mad. Not in a "I can't believe the authors did this" way and more in the "I want my characters to destroy this enemy plot soooooooooooooooooooooo much!" way. Which of course makes me angry in setting but happy out of setting. hehe

                                                    All in all, at first glance, this is a really cool book, a fitting addition to what we have so far, and the writers and devs and all the participants in the project are awesome for doing it.

                                                    Oh, I want to give a shout out to the artists. The pics in the book are cool.
