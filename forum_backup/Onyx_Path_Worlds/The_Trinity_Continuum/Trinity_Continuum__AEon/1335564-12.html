                                Originally posted by muon View Post
                                When the truth about the Doyen and the Proxies is revealed, what will Sandoval Gutierrez and Monolith do, considering he was right about them? Would he accept psiads if the difference between them and psions was shown to him?
                                Well, on the former, I think it'd depend on the context. In general, it likely means he cleaves even harder to his paranoia and determination to bring down psionic influence, dialling up Monolith's worst traits to 11 - but if the truth came out publicly and in a way that hugely damaged the Psi Orders' influence, I could see him assessing whether or not to go public himself in an attempt to rally people against the enemy. Whether that'd work out well is anyone's guess.

                                On the latter, Sandoval believes that the way forward for humanity is via neither psi nor nova, so psiads go against his vision of a future new order. They may not reflect the direct work of the conspiracy he believes exists, but even if convinced they're a natural manifestation of psi power, he can't really control them either. As such, I imagine he'd view them as a nuisance, a rogue element to be gotten rid of - or, actually, thinking about it, if he discovers psiads before getting proof of the Doyen etc, he'd likely want to get psiads into Monolith labs where they can be experimented upon to figure out how the 'conspiracy' managed to create psions in the first place.

                                - Chris Allen, Aberrant Line Developer, Freelance Writer

                                                            So re-reading (xsome number) Aeon Aexpansion has me realizing something. One of the main reasons I like Talents, Superiors, Psiads (and Psions) is that there is nothing negative in their powers. Their isn't a morality stat or a forced weakness or a great curse, or anything like that with the reason of just because. They are what they are. Their powers aren't a corrupting influence.

                                                            I guess, going one thought further, one of the things that make me sad about Novas is that they do have such a forced weakness, a corrupting power. The one group in the whole setting that does. Which yes, I realize some will say is part of the theme, but to me that lessons the fun of them. Which is also I guess why for all that I love certain aspects of Aberrant I have been turning my attention more to the other groups, Superiors and Psiads most especially but also Talents, which are marvelous.
