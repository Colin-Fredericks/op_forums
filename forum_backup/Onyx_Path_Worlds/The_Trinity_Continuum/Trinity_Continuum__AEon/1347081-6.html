                    I wouldn't use a sci-fi show as a reference for believability.

                    That said, the smarter play by would-be tyrants wouldn't be to take away the vote. It would be to keep the vote to help keep the people pacified (because they're less likely to revolt if they feel like they have a say in the political process), but to manage it by establishing full (though probably covert) control over who can run, and by severely limiting the power of elected offices and moving all of the real decision-making power out of the hands of the elected officials and into the hands of a largely behind the scenes bureaucracy that's directly answerable only to said would-be tyrant. That is, arrange for a takeover of the government in a way that the public doesn't realize that they've been shut out: let them have the illusion that they have a voice in the government.

                    Basically, a brutal police state strikes me as more likely as a “Plan B”, to be implemented if something happens to disrupt the aforementioned “Plan A”. Something like someone from the outside getting elected before our would-be tyrant has locked down his control over who can run, who starts reclaiming power from the bureaucracy that's implicitly but not explicitly been handed over to them, and who starts putting people into the bureaucracy who share his agenda of dismantling the “shadow government” aspects of it and feel no loyalty to the would-be tyrants.

                    If something like that were to happen, the would-be tyrants might panic and decide that more overt actions need to be taken. Even then, I figure they'd start by trying to get their “shadow government” plan back on track by doing whatever it takes to resist and ultimately oust the disruptor to minimize the damage he does, then set to work on repairing the damage and putting in additional safeguards to make sure it never happens again. If all of that fails (say, if the resistance isn't effective and the attempt to oust the disruptor fails), then getting people scared and playing on their fear to establish a more overtly tyrannical government would be a way to go.

                    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
