                            Okay. After all the debates, the shifting of interpretations (including by me), and Eddy’s post that the devs will go take another look at clarifying exactly how Scale works, here’s how I’m going to implement it in my games. This might not be precisely how the final official version will look, but I think it’s a sensible approach.

                            1. As a blanket rule, psions don’t apply Scale in most situations. When up against humans or human-type problems, they just use the normal (non-Scale enhanced) rules.
                            2. When psions are in situations where they’re competing against a lower Tier and their Aptitude would give them an edge, they can benefit from Scale. This is more arbitrary and subject to SG approval, but Biokinetics and Vitakinetics might get Scale when engaged in physical contests, Telepaths might get Scale in mental contests etc. This is used to help demonstrate the slight boost psions got from the Prometheus Chambers and the subconscious application of their powers.
                            3. When psions apply their powers, they gain the benefits of Scale for Inflict Damage purposes. They don’t gain Scale for their psi activation roll, or Scale Enhancement in applying their powers against lower Tiers.

                            Anything else I’ve missed? I can edit this post.

                                    Hi everybody, I’m new here, but I’ve been following for a while this discution.
                                    As I see, it says on page 74 from TC core “These relative Scales apply when a character is utilizing her greatest powers against others”. As I understand, the tiers apply only to powers as discribed on the power. For exemple, the Bk fighting a human don’t get any advantage from his powers unless he is using a power, and in this case only the advantages described on the powers.
                                    The only thing I would house rulle is the Inflict Damage stunt using powers. Let’s use the same bk using his powers to create a claw, if he fights a human he can use this stunt twice, if he fights a psion or Aberrant with no Defencive powers (any power that increase the difficult of inflict injury stunt), he can inflict injuries as if the other guy is human. If the other guy is using any kind of defencive power he can inflict injury only once. The same as an tier 3 Aberrant, attacking a human he can inflict injury 3 times, attacking the bk with no power active he can use 3 times, and if attack the bk with an armour power on, only twice.
                                    Super science armour and weapons count as powers for that situation, advanced SS as tier 1, inspired SS as tier 2 and powered SS as tier 3.
                                    I would apply this rules to any similar conflict that needs a variable number of successes to cause a condition or complication that can be applied multiple times, like mental injuries or some social injury (taunt, and things like that).
