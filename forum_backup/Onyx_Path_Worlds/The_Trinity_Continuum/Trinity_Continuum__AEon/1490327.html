                          There's a section in one of the chapters about Unwinding Time, where Æther-related events can retroactively be removed from the timeline — partially, at least. It's not just an alteration of memories; people who were killed by the Unwound Event are usually alive once it has been Unwound. But it's also not a “fix everything” button, as the people involved in the Unwound incident still remember that something happened; just not precisely what.

                          So they can Unwind the destruction of Paris, and the residents who were killed by it are back; but they're aware that something bad happened, even if they can't put their finger on just what that something is.

                                      Rules for Psiads can be found in the Aeon Expansion.

                                      Psiads are more 'broad' than the focused Psions. They can use more Modes, but are not as powerful as Psions. (I think 3 dots was the max?)

                                      Bhurano goes under a different name in Abberant, mostly doing science stuff (investigating Quantum, etc.).

                                      Anima takes place between Abberant and Aeon. It's pretty stand-alone. Yes, you can play it as a game-within-a-game - LitRPG is one of the main inspirations. You don't HAVE to, though - a pure cyberpunk, no MMO game is certainly possible.

                                      Assassins takes place in the same undefined 'Present' as the Core setting - the other side of the coin, so to speak. Character types? You're an assassin - you kill people. The only real differentiation is your reason for killing people and your methods for doing so. If you've seen John Wick then you basically know what this is.
