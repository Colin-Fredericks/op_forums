    I will bring up a question. Aeon needed one post-apocalyptic and one Mega-Coorp setting.

    If you put Africa, Australia or South America as Apokaliptic, there would be no reason to make a character from this places. Almost the same happen wen you talk about facist SA, Africa or Australia, you have a place that is basically immigrant or black people (not African America, they are just African), so fascism here would be against the locals, not against foreigners (it’s like this now, so...)

    China is the Grovernment centered country, and Japan is prety much cyberpunk now, just add some urban samurai and you got the idea.

    If you prefer Europe as facist, what makes sense as Italy, Spain, Germany, Portugal, and most of the Eastern Europe were facist at some point of XX century.

    US would be a great apocalyptic setting, as it’s larger, have proportionally less population and would be the main target of most Aberrant (we already discussed it before). What do you think about it?

                                        Eh. It doesn't actually address any of the issues I have with the FSA; it just swaps out one catastrophe (the Blight) with another (the missing arcologies) while leaving the underlying assumptions intact (namely, that the military, corporations, and Christianity are “one bad day” away from establishing a fascist state).
