    Originally posted by IanWatson View Post
    It's suggested:
    I said that nothing in their chapter directly tied them to Quantum, and nothing does. In the TC Core, and in the AEon book the word "Inspired" in the sentence you quoted, while often used generally to refer to people with powers, it is also used to refer to Talents and their power source in particular. (ie. the Setting Secrets chapter of the TC:AEon Core) Therefore by that same logic the sentence you quoted is also a suggestion to the reader that these are in fact the artificially created version of Talents, which is far better supported in the writing then Superiors using Quantum is.

    Also, if we are supposed to infer that Superiors use Quantum because of the use of the word stalwart:

    A) Why was it decided to use a lower case adjective that means "loyal and/or reliable" where in it is describing beings that are usually thoroughly vetted and heavily trained government operatives to infer Quantum use instead of the upper case proper noun?

    and

    B) Why was it also chosen to do this by using the word stalwart exactly once in the thousands of words of text in the chapter, with no other facts, clues, or hints given to this relationship to Quantum?

    Even though:

    1) Superiors have no Quantum score

    2) Superiors have no Quantum points

    3) In point of fact Superiors have no Power Source at all (Save for a PSI score of 1, which all non-quantum users have by nature of being alive)

    4) Both artificially and naturally created PSI users are the same Tier, have the same Power Stat (PSI), have PSI points (and those points do the same things), and use very similar mechanics for their powers (PSI+Mode). While none of these facts are true when comparing Superiors to Novas.

    5) The fact that a Superiors "powers" find themselves mirroring a Talents Gifts far more closely then anything from the Aberrant book. (You can go and check, and while they are hardly 1 for 1, the similarities are striking)

    6) Artificially created PSI users, though more limited/focused, are more powerful in that focus then naturally occurring PSI users. Therefore if the artificially created Quantum users, who in theory are supposed to "mirror" the artificially created PSI users (in some peoples minds anyway), and therefore be the limited/focused versions of Novas... well... shouldn't that theoretical group be more powerful within that limited focus then the naturally occurring Quantum users the way that Psions are? (Think a light bulb vs a laser, both are putting out light, but the one with more focus produces increased results within it's area of effect)

    7) They are able to format, create, and even invent new Noetic Biotech with the same "Advanced Science only" restriction as Talents. A feat that requires the individual to have a PSI rating of at least 1 per the AEon Core.

    After all of the above, was it really expected that one single adjective, in one single place, would suggest to the reader that Superiors were using Quantum for anything?

    In the end canon will be what ever it will be, but I personally feel that the Source of Superiors abilities are much better suited to be: A) The artificially created version of Talents, or B) Something new that is the result of humanities unique relationship to both Quantum, PSI, and Inspiration, or C) Undefined.

    I know that: 1) You're the TC Lead, and therefore have to look out for the integrity of the game line as a whole 2) Ultimately you have the final say on the nature of Superiors, and 3) What ever you say goes, but I hope that you will consider my reasons, or at the very least leave their Quantum ties as nothing more then the vague one word hint that it currently is.

        Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post
        The fact that they’re clearly modeled on Novas and 1e Stalwarts expression of superhumanity thematically through their attributes.
        And they did this by mirroring and tweaking the effects of many Talents Gifts instead of actually doing anything directly Quantum related... right...

        Psi may be involved as a diegetic excuse for why they can use Noetic tech (a decision that strikes me as balance motivated for the Aeon Superiors’ benefit, rather than thematics or setting motivated) but they’re obviously modeled on being Tier 2 artificially created counterparts to Novas based on literally all their power motifs.
        Their power motifs are just like Novas, except they're attribute category focused instead of being specific attribute focused, oh, and they have no access to any way to customize their powers.

        They also have no way to effect the world beyond themselves, unlike Novas, and there's no Flux, which Novas have, and no Maxing to get powers you haven't purchased, or better version of the powers you already have, and even in the rare times you get a point of Scale (seriously, can things get any further from Novas "power motifs" then the inability to easily gain even a single point of Scale in any area that isn't speed or lifting related?*) there's no Collateral Pool to worry about.

        There's no Quantum Minimums to worry about, and no spending points to activate anything. (because you have no points to spend)

        They also lack power themes that you build your powers and abilities around, and lets not forget the fact that all a Superiors Powers are Innocuous by default (Unlike Novas that have to spend 24xp on every power and edge to have the Innocuous Tag), and they have powers that do things that Novas have no direct way to do**...

        ... yeah... except for those few things, literally all their power motifs are just like Novas...



        Regardless of any official word from on high as to their vague connection to Quantum, maybe you should take a step back and compare them with their most direct counterpart, the Talents***, rather then trying to sell people on their non-existent "power motif" ties to Novas.



        *Power Lifter, Speedster, and Swimming Champ are the only way to get Scale in the currently published Power list for Superiors. There are more ways to get Scale outside of their Powers (edges, skill tricks, & gear), which anyone can get, then within it. Above all else this is what screams "Not Quantum/Nova Related at All" to me.

        **I'll admit that they things that Superiors do which Novas don't do directly are mostly covering things that Novas don't need to because they can just steam roll around or ignore the issue addressed by the Power thru superior Scale.

        ***Even if the Superiors are Quantum users, their most direct counterpoints are Talents: 1) They're both Tier 2 and stuck there, 2) Their Gifts and Powers have a good amount of overlap, and 3) The main difference is the Talent has outward focused abilities (Gifts/Dramatic Editing), and the Superior has inward focused Powers.

        In fact, the Gift list is a great place to mine for ideas when homebrewing new Powers. (Not to directly copy, but to give an idea of the appropriate power level, and to look for where they might possibly have similar effects)

                            I'll say this: the rules for Talents were developed in the Core book, which everyone is assumed to have; so Talents and their Gifts are almost certainly going to be handled consistently over the entire TC line. The rules for Psi and Aptitudes were developed in Æon, which was developed in tandem to the Core book; there were no prior books that included people with noetic powers. So here, too, chances are high that we're going to get a single, consistent set of rules for psionic characters throughout the entire line (though the jury's still out on how Adventure! will handle it, and I still have my hopes that we'll get something for psiads in the Nova Age that doesn't require picking up TC:Æ to use).

                            But Aberrant powers are already an anomaly in that regard, as both TC:Æ and TC:Ab have published rules for them and those rules are substantially different. Yes, there are arguments that Aberrants have changed over the century since their heyday; but the changes are still there, and are substantial. So there's a fracturing of the rules where superhumans are concerned that simple isn't there (yet?) for Talents or “noetics”.
