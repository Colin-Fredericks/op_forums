    [AEON] What's going on on Venus?

    12-14-2021, 07:34 AM
    Or rather: is anything going on there at all?
    In Aeon, the colonisation of our home solar system had fallen behind somewhat, because thanks to the possibility of interstellar space travel, humanity could more or less cherry-pick planets that could be colonised. That is why the population of Mars at the beginning of the 22nd century is comparatively low.
    Now Mars, asteroids and the moons of the outer planets are not the only worthwhile targets. There is also Venus! And it does indeed offer some potential! Here are a few interesting YouTube videos on the subject:

    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l7A0O_4J5LE


    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gJ5KV3rzuag


    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uCYyog_CD_c



    This raises the question of what the colonisation of Venus looks like in Aeon. Is there any more detailed material on this somewhere? And if not: What could be settled on Venus? Are there a few lonely research stations floating high above the surface? Are there cloud cities with several thousand inhabitants? Or is Venus, apart from a few scientific missions, simply still completely untouched?

              Venus would be a fantastic for colonization. Assuming you could deal with its atmosphere first. I've always liked the idea of a terraformed Venus because honestly if humans could do that it would be way more ideal than Mars as a human colony. For Aeon, I'd think the limiting factor would be cost and lack of appropriate tech. Novas working together I'm sure could do it, but without them it would likely be a very very costly and difficult mission. There is mention of new terraforming tech gifted by the Qin but that strikes me as more for creating ecosystems than altering facets of a planet like one as dense, poisonous, and hot as that. An interesting story hook maybe is that an old Daedalus League technology that was meant to spend decades altering a the planet's atmosphere has had results that recent scientists have noticed and hope to exploit. You could make a story out of investigating, making sure there aren't unknown dangers from the technology or monstrous subaberrant mutants that are there. Or they could stumble upon a silicon based life form that requires the ultra dense carbon dioxide atmosphere to breath and the changing atmosphere is killing them

                                      Seeding the upper clouds of Venus with life forms that slowly terraform the planet would be a thing. An automated aerostat, long abandoned by humanity, would make a great "haunted house" setting.

                                      The very size that would be required for aerostats would fit the haunted house genre (either haunted with ghosts, memories, or human failings). Small houses rarely get ghost stories, ghosts seem to need large amounts of room to haunt. So we humans place our ghosts in big mostly empty buildings. An empty aerostat would attract ghost stories just like any other large abandoned structure.

An aerostat is more or less a stationary zeppelin and not a massive space station. Therefore, the whole installation could be characterised by an extremely lightweight construction.And instead of triggering the characters with their claustrophobia, as is usual in space horror, you trigger them with their fear of heights. Only a wafer-thin membrane separates them from the toxic Venusian atmosphere, below them is nothing but a void of swirling clouds and with every gust of wind the entire facility groans and creaks. Ooooh yes...
