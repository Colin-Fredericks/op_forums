    To avoid the Aberrant War:

    Step 1: DELETE PROTEUS. This will simultaneously delete the Aberrant faction as they have nothing to react against.

    Step 2: BLACKLIST any nova attempting to join the Teragen pantheon other than Orzaiz, Fong, Scripture, Mathematician, Bounty, and Apothecary, PERIOD. Watch in awe as it transforms into a scientific think-tank.

    Step 3: Unchain the UN from Utopia, MAKE them stand on their own. Utopia can be the nonprofit organization it pretends to be instead of a shadow-power.

    Step 4: Divide the Directive into like-minded intel agencies that have jurisdiction ONLY in their own nations, then point them at the megasyndicate and everything like it.

    Step 5: Focus MORE attention on the Daedalus League and their extrasolar explorations so mankind's collective mind is distracted by SPACE.

    Step 6: Profit!

    Backup plan 5a: Stumble across the Chromatics and trigger interstellar war. All human species and ethnicities are now united in defending against the aliens (literally the only way that works...EVER).

    Backup plan 5b: Flood the world with data on the two other species humans evolve into (psiads and whatever paramorphs are), the resulting confusion staves off war for decades till the frogs invade. See backup plan 5a.

                It is assumed that the plan must be implemented as of 1998 at the very latest, though you are right I probably should have specified.

                Yes, the Chromatics will get steamrolled, but that's not the point. The very fact of a potential threat from space creates a lingering paranoia in the human mind that will have them watching the stars like hawks and stockpiling any weapon they can find. If one alien race has beef with Earth others might, and that's all the flimsy justification the human mind needs to prep for war. The Doyen (the psi race you were talking about) would just find a new patsy to use against Earth if the Chromatics fail, so all the wartime buildup would be justified. Hell, additional funding would go towards colonization so we could setup numerous early warning systems (and military garrisons) all through the solar system.

                Trinity-era Earth is just as factionalized and divided as Earth has ever been, if AEON really want's to unify mankind, they need to sell the threat from outside (i.e. SPACE).

                    Step 2: Good luck Blacklisting Divis Mal from his own organization, but whatever. Fine, they're blacklisted. So, they'll form their own, more violent organizations without cooler heads to reign them in.

                    Step 3: So we'll have something like a cross between the Red Cross and Charles Xavier's School for the Gifted. And meanwhile the individual members of the UN will have a worldwide Nova arms race because there will be no UN-backed Utopia to stop them.

Sure things are tough in 2120+ but there's plenty of hope as well. It's been a long time ago, but I recall posting an adventure outline on the old White Wolf board that dealt with the AEON group of the previous published adventures going on a diplomatic voyage to Eden to help negotiate an alliance with them against the Coalition. Bruce Baugh commented that this idea was very close to where Trinity would have gone next. The arc of the future bends toward hope. Studying the past is good, Trying to changing is either impossible or a really, really bad idea. Aeon is a continuum. Disrupt one part of that continuum and you're likely to get something much different and much worse than what you wanted.

                                    Originally posted by Darksol-aeternium View Post

If you really want to stop everything before it starts KILL MAX MERCER BEFORE HE STARTS HOPPING THROUGH TIME.                                     Letting AEON and all it's affiliates and subdivisions get away with genocide, and then allowing them a few hundred years for them to come to their senses, sounds like letting the nazis off with a warning if they say "sorry, we messed up!"
                                    I'm not quite sure where killing Max or getting rid of the Aeon society factors in. First off, Max started hopping through time during the original Hammersmith explosion. The same one that empowered Michael Donighal (aka Doctor Primoris, aka Divis Mal) in the first place. Second, even if you could stop that, there actually isn't a whole lot of evidence of Max actually doing that much time travel-related meddling. The Aeon Society is not an organization of murderers or wicked schemers undermining society with steepled fingers and cackling grins in dark, smoky rooms. Proteus was a perversion of the Aeon Society's philosophies and resources and it got way out of control. And while I'm not saying it all magically balances out, it's largely due to Aeon's existence that there was an infrastructure geared towards encouraging novas to improve the world in the first place (or at keeping them from destroying the world by accident in the first couple of years). In my personal opinion the world (as presented in the setting) is better with the Aeon Society than without it.

                                    Also, the Galatea Explosion wasn't any sort of camouflage. A red herring of sorts, sure, but an unintentional one. It was a side-effect of Divis Mal's attempt to elevate humanity. (It was said back when the game line was still originally running that the Galatea Explosion was in fact the first major nova eruption after Divis Mal did his thing.) And again, killing Max Mercer wouldn't prevent the appearance of the novas. (I'd have to dig out my books and check the timeline, but I think it was the Aeon Society that stopped Divis Mal from actually trying to conquer the world back in the 40's)

                                    If there's really is a single "Go back in time and kill this guy and it's all better" lynchpin, it's Hammersmith more than anyone else. The only way to really stop a war between any metahuman faction and anyone else (whether it's another faction or the rest of humanity) is to take the superpowers out of the equation.

                                    The Nazi comparison, not even taking Godwin's Law into account, doesn't really hold water here. Declaring all of the Aeon Society to be responsible for the Aberrant War (many aspects of which were outside even Proteus' control) is like declaring all Germans -- including citizens and the resistance groups -- responsible for the Holocaust as well as the Nazis. Aeon as a whole is not responsible for Proteus' manipulations, and there's evidence in the books that there are a lot of individuals in the Society who would not have approved of Proteus' actions.

                                    Finally, given the damage that was done to the world in the Aberrant War, I don't think it's fair to say that anyone 'got away' with anything.

                                        For a bit in the mid 2000s I was simultaneously in two different Aberrant games, which was a little odd to say the least. There was substantial player overlap but different STs who had very different takes on how things ran. I had to leave before both games finished, sadly.

                                        In one the Aberrant War was something one of the factions of novas was trying to start early, as they could see the future and feared it greatly! I was playing a psiad who worked for the AEon Society, had precog, Meta-Wits, and similar kinds of traits, including the ability to stealth against Novas. Things were definitely on an ugly trajectory. For instance, a nova erupting in Kampala, Uganda, went off with the force of a small nuke. Said nova was later deep-sixed by Proteus. In this chronicle Divis Mal wasn't Doctor Primorus. The latter was an old mad scientist from the Adventure! days. We also ran into Danger Ace, who was old.

                                        The other one was quite different. That had old Novas hiding out in space by Alpha Centauri. My character in that game was a flyer who bought up some heavy duty transportation powers, including the portal one. (The character's basis was as a Farsi who was slowly becoming a Zoroastrian angel, or deva, depending on how things played.) I was the first Nova in space, the first Nova to go to Alpha Centauri, etc. (At least as far as anyone knew....) After I left the game my character ended up becoming the equivalent of the Silver Surfer as the herald for the old novas.

                                        Regardless, I have to say that humanity's historical record on stopping wars is pretty darn poor. Add in Novas and a general cult of Celebrity and I just don't buy it. The Aberrant War probably came out about as well as things possibly could!
