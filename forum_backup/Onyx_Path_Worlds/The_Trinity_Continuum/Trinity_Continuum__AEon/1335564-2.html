    Put another way, Superiors are “artificial Novas” in the same way that Psions are “artificial Psiads” and, like Psions, they have their potential range of abilities greatly restricted compared to their natural counterparts: a Superior only gets inwardly-directed powers (basically, a subset of TC:Ab's Mega-Edges; no Quantum Powers) and have to focus on physical, mental, or social abilities (though not to the exclusion of the other two); and they don't get Mega-Attributes. So in their case, the tradeoff isn't diversity for power as it is with Psions. Rather, it's diversity for stability: Superiors don't ever have to worry about Flux or Transcendence or their TC:Æ equivalent.

    In fact, that's the whole point of creating a Superior: it eliminates the possibility the he or she might ever spontaneously Erupt and become an Aberrant monster.

                    When it comes to Aeon Aexpansion the hype is real and the result is glorious.

                    --

                    On the restriction on Psiads with higher Mode dots I totally get it, I even approve of it on a setting level. That said if I was to run a game with Psiads and we got to the point where my players had Psi 6 I would basically have a campaign event occur that would let them gain higher mode levels at higher Psi ranks. This could be something adventure wise or it could be sneaking into a Psi Order and use a Prometheus Chamber that they recalibrated slightly so that their genetics could be enhanced.

                    BUT, all that said, as an official rule I cannot say I have any issue with the whole limiting how high a Psiad can go considering how wide they can go.

                    The whole QK being a natural human power is just so BRILLIANT. Like woah, how cool.

                    ---

                    So Superiors have no powers that effect the world outside their own being but it makes me wonder if a Power that relates to say pheremones or whatever could be applicable. Its natural and self-generated and so it still fits the theme.

                    As an aside I wonder how much of the Superior design we will see when it comes to Stalwarts. While I would totally enjoy a slight broadening of Stalwart power theme I have to say I REALLY (like REALLY REALLY) hope that the whole idea of powers being specific things with multiple dot ratings is a thing. I LOVE that.

                    For many reasons, one of which is the idea that a character might start off with say 1 dot in a power and that as the campaign progresses that goes to 2, then 3, and then 4, and then 5 near the end. Its the same power but it just gets progressively more developed and potent. I LOVE that concept.

                    ---

                    Oh and I love the powers that Superiors have that basically lets them go into the wilderness, use various materials around them, and built some tools that let them build other tools that would let them build civilization. That's a brilliant thing to me.

                    --

                    Here is another question - Do you think Superiors would have access to powers that let them increase Scale for actions. Their powers, though constrained and limited, are somewhat proto-Nova and it would be nifty to see certain small effects related to what they could have become.
