                                                From what I read about Psiads, they only get 3 dots to divide between the 6 modes of their two Primary Aptitudes at character generation. The reason Psions get a 4th dot is because of the improvements the Prometheus Chambers provide. This is the same reason why Psiads do not get a bonus Attribute dot for their preferred Approach (and thus can't sacrifice it to start the game with Psi 3 like Psions can).

                                                One thing I am not clear on is how Quantakinesis works with Psiads. I know that once they reach Psi 3, they treat it as a 3rd Primary Aptitude, but it isn't clear if they can purchase Quantakinetic modes before then (though it is clearly stated that they can not choose Teleportation modes until Psi 3). Since Psions who specialize in either Quantakinesis or Teleportation need to be Psi 3 to do so, I am inclined towards the idea that Quantakinetic modes can't be obtained until Psi 3 for Psiads, just like Teleportation modes. I think this makes the most sense overall to be honest.

                                                As for the rest of the PDF, I am still working my way through much of it. Still, I am looking forward to getting a physical copy of this product once a PoD is available (PDFs are okay, but my computer isn't really portable to my games).
