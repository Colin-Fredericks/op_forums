                      Originally posted by tragedyjones View Post

                      Would you be so kind as to share what an example of "illegal nova age tech" would look like?
                      Brain implants in the vein of Matrix or Johnny Mnemonic. Artificial Sentience. Unrestricted nanotech.

                      Most Q-tech is irrelevant without a source of quantum, so without novas, they're effectively useless. Still, Æon would like to keep their hands on it, just in case.

                      A lot of it is hard to quantify. Trinity 1e had a story about early Qin diplomacy: they gave their human hosts a piece of biotech. Humans studied the intricacies of it for weeks before going back to the Qin and asking what it was? The Qin looked confused and said "a vase."

                      From the Qin, our allies, it's an amusing story of clashing cultures, and also serves to demonstrate their mastery of biotech over humans' capabilities. If that same item came from an Aberrant? Well, fuck. Put it in isolation. Sure, it might just be a vase, but who knows? It's a ticking time bomb. Maybe it'll explode. Maybe it'll infect everyone who touches it with some weird corruption virus. Maybe it'll remain inert for the first 1000 touches and then activate. There's no way to know. Aberrant powers don't obey the laws of physics. Best stick it in a deep dark hole and try to forget about it.

                                      Not only that, but by the Aberrant war, it was very likely a lot of teh nova inventors were mega-int 5, possibly more (I don't know if there's still the intent to allow high quantum novas to break that cap a'la first edition or if it's a hard cap). Mega int five... is just beyond anything you can imagine. Even perfectly ordinary tech, stuff that doesn't incorporate nova power, could be dangerous and incomprehensible.

                                      My take on why it's banned comes from a Vernor Vinge novel--a fire upon the deep. It starts with an expedition finding a data vault of an ascended power. And they're very careful. They evaluate every step in the menu driven design process. But the problem is, compared to what created the archive, they're smart apes. And before long, without knowing it, the entire system is compromised, with their systems feeding them the data the creature wants them to see--until it's too late to do anything.

                                      That, I expect if the fear of a lot of advanced nova tech. And given the challenges of the world, you can bet the ban wsan't casually arrived at. Aeon and the nations of the earth probably got burned a lot before they finally decided: we can't risk it.
