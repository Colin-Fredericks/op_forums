                                      01-29-2020, 05:46 PM
                                      Quality for weapons and soft armor for armor are worded diffidently. The quality tag talks about increasing the default enhancement while soft armor just says it increases the difficulty to buy the injury stunt. Soft armor isn’t the general term for increases to the difficulty to buy the injury stunt, it’s just a source of such a modification. The soft armor tag isn’t increasing your soft armor.

                                      If you look at the weapon stats in the core you can see that everything has +1 enhancement even without the quality tag (Unfortunately one of the 2 examples of the quality tag either has the wrong quality tag or enhancement). Contrast that with the armor where every piece of armor has the soft tag. There isn’t a single piece of armor that doesn’t possess the soft tag. Even the most basic, least helpful piece of armor has soft 1. It doesn’t make sense for it to be 1+soft 1 from a game design perspective. That effectively makes all armor a plus 2 difficulty to the injury stunt. They could have just started the difficulty at 1 and added to it, or made the base rule that all armor starts at soft armor 2.
