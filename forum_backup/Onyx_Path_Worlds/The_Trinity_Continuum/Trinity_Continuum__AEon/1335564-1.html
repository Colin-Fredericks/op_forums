                                                            Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                                                            As someone maybe interested in this product - Can someone run down me general view on how Psions, Psiads and Superiors are different from each other? It can only be in general lore description, but pointing some broad mechanics differences would also help.

                                                            Psiads - These are individuals who have naturally come into their noetic/psychic abilities. Because of this they generally have a lower power maximum while making it up by being able to learn powers from all psychic Aptitudes. They are what one should be when accessing noetic power.

                                                            Psions - These are individuals who have gained access to their psychic/noetic powers thanks to an artificial process known as the Prometheus Effect. They gain greater power maximums at the restriction of only knowing one Aptitude of psychic power. They are artificial in that the technology that makes them available is alien.

                                                            Both Psiads and Psions use the same listing of Psychic Aptitudes and Modes so in this they are the same. The main difference is, as stated, Psiads are not restricted to a single Aptitude while Psions are. That said at the current standpoint of human evolution Psiads are limited to Aptitude 4 in primary and 3 in secondary while Psions can go up much higher in their chosen Aptitude.

                                                            ---

                                                            Superiors - Are individuals associated with Quantum energies given manifestation through a Nippon-made but Aeon-gained technological process. Their powers are organized as either Physical, Mental, or Social and are much lower powered then their Nova super brethren. Their powers control the self but are limited in being able to control others or the world. This limitation is in some ways artificial, though that said it is intrinsic to being a Superior.

                                                            Psiads and Psions use Noetic energy while Superiors (and Novas) use Quantum energy.

                                                            I hope this helps.
