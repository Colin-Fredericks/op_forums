                                            I don't know about Scion, but I think that psions are like every other human or talent except for their psionic powers. So they can blast things using their minds, ok, but automatically making them better at anything (even hair cutting competitions) compared to a normal just because of innate scale doesn't seem right to me (*). Same counts for novas IMHO. The mega attributes of 1e could easily be translated into scale, but if not boosted somehow even a nova wouldn't be any better than a normal human.

                                            I think it'd be better if powers, gifts, equipment etc. would explicitly state that it provides scale X.

                                            (*) = That said, I could imagine that proxies and aberrants and are a different matter. But I have to think about it before going deeper down this route.
