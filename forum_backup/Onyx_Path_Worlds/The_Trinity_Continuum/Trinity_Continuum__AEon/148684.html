    What I want to see in a new Aeon Trinity reboot.

    05-25-2014, 10:39 AM
    Hi there. Just stumbled over the old rpg.net thread http://forum.rpg.net/showthread.php?...ou-want-to-see and thought I' add my personal wishes. So why not do it here.

    REGARDING APTITUDES:
    Being dunked in a Prometheus Chamber limits a psion to one aptitude. I like this for gaming reasons: There will be no super-duper-100-aptitudes-kick-ass psions, which forces the players to work together to solve a problem. Sort of a scissor-paper-rock situation.

    Having said that, I would like some mechanics for psions which are NOT limited to one aptitude (I don’t mean auxiliary modes here).

    Also, full rules for Quantakinetics please.

    Finally, a understandable and easy-to-use freeform rules would be great. The one in the Players Guide was a mess. I just couldn’t get all the different tables in my head. A system which needs constant table consulting is unuseable.

    REGARDING TELEPORTATION:
    I always disliked the time-delay aspect of theTranslocation mode, especially since Transmassion was instantenous. I’d house rule it at least to be quicker with each dot in Translocation.

    Also, the familiarity rule is kind of draconic. I understand that these restrictions try to prevent Teleportation from being overpowered, but it still doesn’t… feel right (especially after reading the Jumper series from Steven Gould).

    REGARDING BIOKINESIS:
    I never understood why Biokinetics weren’t able to heal themselves. Yeah yeah, that’s the domain of the Vitakinetics, but still. They can grow additional limbs and organs, so why not close their wounds. Does. Not. Make. Sense. (I know that the free mode rules allowed this, but Terra Verde didn’t include them anymore.)

    REGARDING TIMETABLES/SETTINGS:
    I like the idea about the different time setting between Adventure!, Aberrant and Trinity. But why are the rules (about Daredevils, Psions, Novas) bound to their setting? How about the rules to support e.g.Psions in the pulp era, and even completely new settings, like Steven Otte’s Milleniums Edge 2997?

    CROSSOVERS:
    Support for all splats (Daredevils, Psions, Novas) to be played in crossover mode. I suppose that a Nova will always outpower your average Psion, but what the heck.

    ALIENS:
    I’d like rules for aliens (Chromatics, Qin) to be playable as PCs, especially when playing in the far, far future.

    SUPPLEMENTS:
    Will there be any more supplements, like the Trinity Order/Region books? I always missed that Africa didn’t get their share of love.

    AND... ACTION!:
    Maybe this doesn't belong here. But I didn't like the sparse Psi points, resulting in the PCs to use their powers maybe three times a day or so. I'd give the players the Merit Mode Natural (7 points) for free for one mode. Results in a bit more ... cinematic action. Let's see how the reboot handles this.

                                          What I'd like to see... in no particular order... (warning, some of these are petty; I make no apology for my nitpicking! This is how I show my love!)

                                          1. Some clarity over the distinction between psionically-active "biotech" and plain ol' biotechnology. There were hints of this in the original edition; biotech "Enamel" knives had no Formatted Tolerance rating because they were, ostensibly, just really hard organic enamel in the shape of a knife. I loved the idea that true psitech could become parasitic, so characters had to manage their Tolerance ratings.

                                          2. Better story-mechanics transparency. The original Trinity (Aeon) book fell flat on a few measures. The ones that stood out to me were: a) biokinetics boasted they had need for vitakinetics in story (maybe they were lying!), but had no healing powers in their power suite; b) clairsentients were described as precognitive, but had no power that could see the future; c) psions generally were cited as being particularly effective against Aberrants, but mechanically few of their powers gave them an edge over Aberrants greater than a neutral with sufficient tech. Various supplements addressed these discrepancies: the ISRA book gave clears precognition, the Players Guide freeform psi allowed for biokinetic healing, and somewhere in canon they suggested that psi and taint/quantum cancelled each other out, and that you could represent this by having psi and quantum powers concurrently fizzle giving psion strike teams a distinctive edge. It would be preferable to have this transparency up-front.

                                          (Addendum: the biokinetics were the ne plus ultra of infiltration in the fiction, but how did they get around the fact that every single psion (and some devices) could detect them by using Attunement? Not to mention the Clairsentient powers that could thwart them. I can imagine some ways they could do it, but I'd like the glaringly obvious disparity to be addressed).

                                          3. Fewer rolls. Trinity was an advance in the Storyteller system by having Armor/Soak automatically deducted from damage, instead of rolled. (They also were advanced in having a static 'difficulty number' of 7. Ah binary distributions.) You still had to roll attack, dodge/parry, and damage as separate gobs of d10s. Also initiative rolls. We can simplify. We can use more static mods!

                                          4. Change or eliminate Qualities. I hate reroll mechanics. They throw off my precious binary distributions!

                                          5. Front and center clarity that every psi Order has members from (mostly!) every Aptitude. This was established fairly early on but the idea that each Order was monolithic stuck in the heads of many players. Diversity of options is attractive!

                                          6. Having the South American psi group structured as a criminal family/ drug cartel made no sense and was a little offensive. As a biotech company crossed with a transhuman project? Much more interesting. NORCA was all these things, but I hope for an emphasis on the latter.

                                          7. The UAN was presented as one of the most prominent geopolitical powers, if not THE most. Yet they were barely mentioned in the fiction. I guess being peaceful and affluent is boring and not conducive to fiction, but I'd love to see more African cultural influences on the setting as a whole. Maybe more Swahili slang in space.

                                          8. Related to (7) above: the Chinese made first contact with the first alien species, so they got called the "Qin." The Karroo miners, a subculture of the UAN, made first contact with the second alien species so they got named... the "Chromatics"? Why not use an African name? (I did in my game.)

                                          9. The Coalition as rape aliens was poorly executed. The rape made no sense, the biology made no sense, and the subgroups looked like muppets (not sure that last one follows). Their being genestealers with advanced technology was great, and I loved the reveal that their socialization was pheremone based (just as Qin were telepathy-based and the Chromatics were light pattern-based; it contrasted with humans as linguistically-based). If you must have rape aliens, I prefer space wasp (xenomorphs!) or succubus models.

                                          10. If you can, avoid or give context to headscratcher statements like "Unless you want me to carve a pair of these into you, I'd turn the camera off," (Trinity, p.57) or "your gender [male] can't have children." (Shattered Europe, p.133). Did I mention that I nitpick?

                                          11. Give additional clarity to the technologies that are ubiquitous in the future. It took me quite a while to figure out that hover vehicles used ducted fans rather than some kind of anti-gravity. (Maybe that was just me being slow.) I am being a little unfair, fusion rockets and computer agents were fairly well-detailed in the main book and many other technologies were explained well in the tech manual. And I admire the tech manual for giving equal room to peaceful tech as they did to standard RPG adventurer murder-hobo weapon tech.

                                          12. Eliminate or change Psionic Backlash. This is a holdover of White Wolf games where the better a character got at some supernatural stat, the worse off he was mechanically in some way. (Rage in Werewolf: the Apocalypse is a good parallel example). Psionic Backlash is used arbitrarily and punitively in the Darkness Revealed series of adventures; page 91 in Passage Through Shadow calls for "All Players roll Psi to resist backlash" due to the arrival of a Chromatic jumpship. This is contrary to the corebook rule that backlash events be "within the range of your character's Attunement," which may be as little as five meters. It was, in effect, instructing the gamemaster to cheat to fuck over the PCs. (Not that I'm wholly innocent of that, but still, bad form). Also, Psionic Backlash raises the question how powerful Clears aren't taking Bashing damage constantly; I think somewhere it is mentioned this is one reason why Otha is such a recluse, but that sucks, and sucks especially for PCs.

                                          13. Clarify psi prints (Trinity, p.194). Can any psion sense them? Is detecting them really a prerequisite to the usage of some powers? It is hardly mentioned anywhere else in the rules or the fiction, how does it all fit? (In my game, any psion could do it, but only Clears and Telepaths were told about it during training).

                                          Whew, I've been waiting 15 years to get some of these off my chest. Thanks for reading my rant!

                                                  Wow - I don't know how I missed that Trinity was coming back, but I saw the poster release and followed up and now I am super excited that both my favorite old White Wolf games are getting a new coat of paint and are hitting the road again. I have been following the Mage posts excitedly and now I have something else to follow with baited breath as well.

                                                  I will be honest though, and admit that I haven't cracked open a Trinity book in close to 10 years, so I pulled out my old spiral bound copy, checked to make sure no pages had fallen out, and started reading the colored setting section. I don't know how much of the setting revisions have already been completed and firmed up, but I did walk away with a list of things I can hope get a good looking over.

                                                  Caveat to everything that follows - It has been a long time since I have read the various source books, so some of these may have been addressed in the original setting, others are simply my "wish list" opinions.

                                                  1) It sounds like instead of a strict Trinity Timeline we are getting the Trinity Continuum, which in the words of the Doctor, will have all sorts of timey-whimey oddness. If this lets the 20's era have lost worlds and hollow earths while explaining why they don't exist in the now or future I am completely cool with this. I am personally hoping that the various Telluric energy events brings them into and out of alignments with the various eras.

                                                  2) Please no references to 70's "super-normal events' and "unusual individuals." I am cool with the introduction of other eras, but I could never buy that people alive during both the 70's and the Aberrant era didn't put two and two together. Isolated Inspired throughout history is cool though.

                                                  3) Which leads me to Project Rewrite - I am cool with a huge amount of data being lost, but the entire idea that Aeon Trinity is the only organization controlling the rebuilding of a knowledge database for the world is nuts.

                                                  4) Aeon Trinity, while I love the place it has in the history of the setting, could it please be dialed back some. It is described as having "hundreds of thousands of agents." the entire US government has 400K+ employees. The UN has about 50K staff and about 80K peacekeepers with a combined 2 year budget of around 12-13 billion for both. I'll admit that Aeon Trinity is described as doing a lot, but is it really a 200K+ organization? How is it financed? The US does it through taxation, the UN through member nations contributions. Where is Aeon Trinity raking in the money to finance everything?

                                                  5) Hopefully the setting reboot will deal with technology that has become ubiquitous over the last 16 odd years. Some tech I would really like to see address
                                                  A) Drones - are they being used in the future, why use psions when you can drone strike an Abberant? I personally would love it if the future is drone phobic because too many Aberrant found ways to control them and use them against their operators. With commercial applications of drones being developed right now as well, if drones are still around are they really being used to deliver pizza and beer to college dormrooms everywhere in 2120?
                                                  B) Social Media - I think I read already that wireless communication is back, so it sounds like the Comm Crunch is gone or modified. If that is the case, then I am down with that. Communication technologies, social media, surveillance concerns are all far more interesting to me as social pressures than nobody being able to figure out how to build secure decentralized communication systems with redundant back ups just to provide a tax source. Considering how social media dependent parts of society is now, it would be iteresting to see how the loss of the OpNet and then its return affect it. Also with various social media driven events like various Arab Spring protests, It would be interesting to see how social media affects the battlefield of 2120. A team of heroes could actually be formed when they all get an app alert of threat in their area and respond.
                                                  C) 3-D Printing - Its already possible to make working plastic guns at home. Advance the technology and add in Hyperfusion power sources and we begin to verge on post consumer economies. Blue prints and raw resources could become key economic drivers.

                                                  6) As a Texan, I would ask that the Tex-Mex district be renamed if it exists in the Trinity Continuum. Tex-Mex is a style of food.

                                                  7) I know I already saw a comment that lay out style would be updated, but just my two cents - please no weird squashed face hologram images for the various personalities in the setting. I didn't like those graphics 16 years ago and they haven't gotten any better with time.

                                                  8) I doubt this would every happen, since its so key to the setting, but could we please not destroy the Louvre. If I had the cash I could easily spend a month in Paris and visit the Louvre every day. I've visited museums on 5 continents in it is one of my favorites. I know that Apocalyptic settings always like to destroy France/Paris. it's so cliche at this point that I just roll my eyes when I see it happen. I like the idea of a shattered Europe, I would just like the space station to hit a different region. My personal preference would be Rome. For some reason I really like the idea of a Catholic Church without a seat of power and the newly elected Pope being a Dali Lama sort of international pilgrim.

                                                  9) Religion, I know it can be a touchy subject, but if the setting continues to have religiously motivated Aberrants decimate huge portions of the globe, I would really like to know how 20th century religions shake out in 2120. About 16% of the worlds population is already non-religious, and that jumps up to as much as 25% in western industrial nations like the UK. Super powered beings, super powered religious terrorists, world war, psions, aliens all should have a profound effects. The setting gave some brief mentions to this issues 16 years ago, and hope we see the evolution of religions explored in the reboot.

                                                  10) If Scotland votes to split from the UK on Thursday, I hope that makes it into the new setting as well. Of course, since they will be depending on the North Sea oil fields to help pay for their new economy, it will suck for them when hyperfusion is developed and crashes oil based economies.

                                                  Ok - that's enough for now. Time to randomly crack open one of the other source books and reread it.
