                              To purchase a seven-dot power, characters must have seven dots of Psi and six dots in at least one Mode.
                              Aeon, page 209

                              Expanding on that.
                              To get the 6th dot in Psi the character need one mode at 5 dots and another at 3 dots.
                              So the least the character may have to get the 7th dot is Psi 7, 6 dots in one mode, 3 in another and 0 in the third mode.
