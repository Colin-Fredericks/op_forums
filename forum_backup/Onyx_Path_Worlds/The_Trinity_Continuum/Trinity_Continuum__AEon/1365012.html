                  People go after latency tests, usually in the order they want to join or Aeon Trinity. If they have positive results they can ask for the order for a different chamber or the order chamber (in game terms, you just pick any one).

                  The generic marking for Novas, Psiads (including psions) and Talents is virtually the same, and it don’t differentiate if it’s active or not yet, so a latent a Psion and a nova will have the same result in the test. The method to detect latency is not genetic, is noetic, and it differentiate the kind and if it’s active or not.

                              Genetically, eximorphs (quantum-users) and psychomorphs (psi-users) come from the same basic genetic sequence, but Talents can't be pinned down so easily. Sometimes -- like during the Nakamura process -- the process that generates an eximorph might instead give rise to a Talent instead, which may suggest a genetic link. But you'll find just as many (if not more) Talents who don't have that particular sequence.

                              Otherwise yes, there's a genetic switch or fork, and a number of factors could cause that switch to flip either to quantum or psi. It's more complicated than that, obviously: there are two general noetic branches: psiads and latents. Latents would be a sort of evolutionary dead-end for noetic ability, except for the Prometheus Chambers. You may think it curious that the Doyen built the Chambers to work on latents instead of psiads, but latents are probably more numerous.

                              Did you know biologists estimate that the eye has evolved over 50 times? I wonder what latents would look like in the fullness of time...

                              And of course these methods aren't foolproof. Dormed-down novas, quantakinetics, and psiads may not register, depending on the quality of the detector and the method used.
