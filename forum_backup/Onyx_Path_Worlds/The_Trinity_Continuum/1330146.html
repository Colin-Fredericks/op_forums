    Starcraft in Storypath

    08-09-2019, 06:25 PM
    STARCRAFT Creating Characters
    On StarCraft there are no special kinds of humans, characters with psionic powers are not entirely special like psions, they are regular Terrans that happens to have higher psi trait from birth.

    Terran Characters
    Terrans are regular humans, and created as such, no matter the psionic capacity to start, but later the psionic capacities can be developed.
    Terrans are the standard human, having no template related, yet a few of those can gain Psi above • using the Psionic Capacity Edge, as described in the Edge.
    A few rare Terrans are Heroic, considered Tier II characters, and capable of developing some tricks beyond those of regular humans, Talents and Psiads.

    Talents
    Talents have the Talent template, as described in Core Rulebook, but there are no Allegiances, so, the character will pick 3 skills from the Society Path list and use them as Skills from that Society path.
    No one will tell there is any difference between an Inspired and a common person. In fact, all the effects of all the gifts are due extreme luck and skill, and there is no Flux in Starcraft.

    Psiads
    Psiads have the Psiad template, as described in Æon Æxpansion, but all of them have Clairsentience and Telepathy as Primary Aptitudes and can’t raise their psi above 5.
    While rare, all aptitudes are available, except Quantakinesis, that is limited to the base powers, as described for pre-2104 Psiads.

    Protoss Characters
    Protoss are all created as regular characters, with a few changes.
    Protoss divide themselves in 3 great groups, that are represented by the character Origin, Khalai, Nerazim and Tal’Darin, each one with their own cultural backgrounds and structure.
    During the Step 5, receives the Protoss Template, described bellow.

    Protoss Template
    Protoss are humanoid, meaning they have a pair of legs, a pair of arms, a head and all that linked to a torso, while a taller than a human, their light structure don’t make them more resistant or strong than a regular human, at least about physical impacts and muscular strength.
    The first trait noticeable in Protoss body is the complete lack of a mount, nose or eyers, as their skin, while though is used to absorb water, air, nutrients and light directly form the environment, that they use to do photosynthesis. Protoss skin have the Enviromental 1 (pressure) tag, capable of maintain the Protoss for up to 16 hours per day in any kind of atmosphere, including the vacuum of space, but the protoss need to breath, drink and be exposed to light for the rest of the day, every day.
    Protoss are a very powerful psychic race, using this power for thousands of years, this power is part not only of their society, but also their technology. All Protoss are Tier II characters, starting with Psi ••, 1 dot in Mindshare mode of Telepathy and 3 dots in any Electrokinesis or Telepathy modes, their primary aptitudes. While most of the population are trained only those 2 aptitudes, Protoss can develop any modes of Clairsentience, Psychokinesis and Vitakinesis, and, with Psi •••+, Teleportation, as secondary aptitudes.
    When the Protoss have 5 dots in at least one mode, and at least 10 dots among all his modes, its possible to raise Psi to 6.
    The maximum dots in the Primary and Secondary modes are defined by the Psi. All other characteristics works as described for psions (Trinity Continuum: Æon, Chapter Seven).

    Khalai (Protoss Origin Path)
    Khalai Protoss are the Protoss connected to the Khala, a telepathic network, working exactly as the first dot of Mindshare mode, but maintained unconsciously by all Khalai Protoss and connecting every single one of them as long they are on reach of each other and keep their Neural Cords intact.
    The usual reach is not enough to cover the entire sector, so there are several Psi Amplifiers that link to each other and to the Khalai Protoss in its reach, allowing extremely long range almost permanent telepathic connection.
    Example Connections: The Conclave, a Warpsmith, a Templar mentor, a High Templar.
    Skills: Culture, Empathy, Humanities, Technology
    Edges: Adrenaline Spike, Danger Sense, Fast Draw, Forceful Martial Arts, Iron Will, Patron, Small Unit Tactics.

    Tal'Darin (Protoss Origin Path)
    Tal'Darin Protoss are a small group of Protoss that disconnected themselves from the Khala and kept their Neural Cords intact. They used Void Power to block their connection and established their own network using that. All Tal’Darin have the Void User Edge.
    Example Connections: The Ascendents, an Artifact Hunter.
    Skills: Command, Enigmas, Integrity, Technology
    Edges: Alternate Identity, Any One Style Edge, Armor Expert, Breath Control, Fast Draw, Hair-Trigger Reflexes, Small Unit Tactics, Trick Shooter, Weak Spots

    Nerazim (Protoss Origin Path)
    Nerazim Protoss are a group of Protoss that severed their Neural Cords to get free from the Khala. It caused a civil war that ended with the exodus of the Nerazim. They dont have a similar permanent network but are capable of establish one as the power described.
    Some of the Nerazim became Dark Templar, capable of using the void to several effects, but not all Nerazim are trained in those arts. Only the Dark Templar have the Void User Edge among the Nerazim.
    Example Connections: A Prelate, the Matriarch, a Dark Templar, a void Scholar.
    Skills: Enigmas, Integrity, Larceny, Survival
    Edges: Always Prepared, Animal Ken, Danger Sense, Direction Sene, Hardy, Iron Will, Keen Senses, Swift

    Zerg Characters
    There are 2 kinds of playable Zergs, Infested Terrans and Primal Zergs. In both cases, they are created as regular character and receives the Zerg template. Other Zergs are feral in essence, not intelligent enough to be interesting characters when not members of a Hive Mind. It is still possible to play a Zerg Master, like a Queen or a Cerebrate, creating them as Primal Zergs and adding the Hive Mind Hub quality, as well as raising the Psi as much as possible to avoid being dominated by other hives.
    Zerg Characters are, usually, Tier I Characters, but a Tier II Terran that becomes infested will become a Tier II Zerg from beginning (evolution 3).
    Primal Zergs are created as regular characters, but due to their savage origin, they have some restrictions on the Origin and Role Path selection, Origin are limited to Military Brat or Survivalist, while role is limited to Charismatic Leader, Combat Specialists and Sneak.

    Zerg Template
    Zergs are creatures born Zerg or infested by an Zerg virus, including the Primal Zergs and the Infested creatures.
    Zergs have a deep control over their bodies development, being able to evolve their bodies thru genetic manipulation instinctively. All Zergs have an Evolution value that goes from 0 to 6 and grants greater capacities to evolve their bodies. The zergs listed on Zergs session are all already adjusted by this template, and have Evolution 0. Evolution 3 to 5 Zergs are Tier 2 character and Evolution 6 are Tier 3 characters.
    The Evolution value starts as 0, or the Evolution value of the last Hive the feral Zerg was part of, and can be raised by gathering essence, each new rank costing 25 essence. A Zerg can only absorb essence by devouring creatures, and the amount absorbed is defined by the devoured creature tier, 1 per tier per creature. Tier 2 Zergs receive only half of the essence per devoured creature and Tier 3 Zergs don't receive any essence anymore.
    The Zerg receives 3 + Evolution value as Anomaly Powers, as described for Antagonists (Trinity Continuum Core Rulebook, page 139), there are a few changes and additions to the powers, described in Zerg Powers, bellow.
    All Zergs have Psi • (or its original value for Infested), and can raise it up to 5 (except for Infested that had a higher limit before infestation). They all have access to Biokinesis and Vitakinesis modes, while it's possible, mainly among the Hive Mind Hubs, to develop any other aptitude modes as secondary, including Quantakinesis, as auxiliary modes.
    The maximum dots in the Biokinesis, Vitakinesis and auxiliary modes are defined by the Psi. All other characteristics works as described for psions (Trinity Continuum: Æon, Chapter Seven).

    Zerg Powers
    Following, there are a few changes some anomaly powers receive when dealing with Zergs.
    Enhanced Attack — This power grants 2 additional tag points, that can be used to buy 2 1-point tags or 1 2-points tag and requires Natural Weapon or Spitter Powers.
    Extraordinary Speed — This power can be bought multiple times, and staking the scale increase. Any zerg capable of flying at speed 6 can reach orbit and have a Space Speed Scale of 3, that can be increased with this anomaly power cumulatively.
    Heightened Reflexes — It adds 1 enhancement to the defense roll.
    Hyper Intelligent — This power grants the zerg 5 Skill dots that can be added to any of the skills.
    Spitter — This power can be added a second time to increase the range to Medium and a third to increase to Long.
    Additionally, Zergs can also have the following Anomaly Powers:
    Gigantic — The Zerg is larger than those of its original species. Each time this power is chosen, the zerg becomes one scale larger, what results in scale when causing damage with its weapons, when resisting against direct damage and when moving or handling objects. This power can be chosen once for Tier 2 Zergs and twice for tier 3 Zergs.
    Hive Mind Hub — The Zerg is capable of extend the reach of a Hive Mind to all non-primal zergs within its Psi attunement range. This power can be chosen a second time, multiplying it attunement range by 500. If not in range of any other Hive Mind Hub of higher Psi, the zerg becomes the Hive Master extending its will to all zergs of lower Psi at range including other Hive Mind Hubs of lower Psi.
    Self Sustained — The Zerg is resistant to environmental conditions of a certain aspect. Usually the first time this power is chosen, it’s to turn the zerg immune to the effects of atmospheric presure (including vacuum of space), but it can turn the zerg immune to one form of environmental damage, such as Radiation, Heat or Cold, for each time this power is chosen.

    Zerg Hive Mind
    Almost all Zergs found around the Koprulo Sector are part, or at least were part, of a Hive, gaining some advantages due to the Hive Mind and the control the Hive have over the zergs genetics. Hive Mind is persistent condition all zergs suffer when becomes a member of a Hive Mind.

    Hive Mind
    The Zerg is part of a Hive Mind, taking advantage of its Evolution value, but being restricted when not following its interests.
    System: Anytime a zerg tries to act against the Hive Mind, or going against it will, the zerg will suffer a complication 3 in the action, that if not bought will make the action impossible. When the actions is following the Hive Mind will or interests in general, the complication is ignored.
    Resolution: The effect lasts for as long the zerg is at a Hive Mind Hub attunement range. If broken, the zergs are free to act at their own, what usually turn them into feral zergs.

    New Edges
    Psionic Capacity (•• or •••)
    Prerequisite: Terran, not Heroic.
    The character can use experience to increase their Psi Psi above 1, to a max equal of this Edge cost.
    Psi costs 20 experience per dot, all modes of Clairsentience and Telepathy are considered Secondary modes, costing 10 experience per dot. All the other aptitudes (except Quantakinesis, that is not available) are considered Auxiliary, limited to the Base powers, that cost 10 experience.
    The maximum dots in the Secondary and Auxiliary modes are defined by the Psi, as well as the Attunement Range and Psi Points. All other characteristics works as described for psions (Trinity Continuum: Æon, Chapter Seven).

    Void User (••)
    Prerequisite: Protoss, Psi •••+
    The character have learned to connect their mind to the Void, allowing to access its powers. The Zerg gains access to Quantakinesis as a secondary aptitude. The character gains also a permanent Noetic Connection to the void itself, what allows it to teleport or open portals to the Void, and also to deposit objects in or retrieve those from the void.

    Psionic Powers
    Terrans use a Psionic Index to measure the psionic capacity of any creature, ranging from 1 to 10 for humans, with some non-humans reaching higher values. The PI scale is a direct translation of the Psi of the characters in Starcraft, Psi equaling to ½ of the character PI (round down, 1 minimum).
    The psi rank of the character the same way as it does for a Psiad, limiting the maximum rank, Psi points and Attunement Range. A character have access to all the characteristics from their Psi rank and all ranks below.
    These rules includes all the psionic beings, including Zergs, Protoss and Xel’naga.

    The Void
    The Void is an alternative power source for psionic creatures to use their powers. The void allows a stronger connection to the structure of the universe, making it possible to learn some powers easily. The Tal’Darin and Nerazim Protoss are the most common users of the void, but Amon and some other Xel’naga are the real masters of it, capable of opening passages through to the void with easy.
