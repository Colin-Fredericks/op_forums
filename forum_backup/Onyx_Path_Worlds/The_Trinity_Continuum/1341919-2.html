                                Are you familiar with the Conspiracy X setting? It's a setting with a very “X Files” vibe to it, including the protagonists being agents of a government organization whose mission statement is to track down and contain all the weirdness that's lurking in the shadows. That weirdness includes, among other things, three types of aliens: one that's effectively “the Greys” (a telepathic species that has it out for humans because, from a psionic standpoint, we're walking pollutants who are dumping raw psychic energy wherever we go), the Atlanteans (nanotech-wielding Nordics who serve as humanity's “benefactors”, but from a position of arrogance where their idea of what's best for us doesn't necessarily have anything to do with what's actually best for us), and a reptilian species that's essentially in a sort of “Planet of the Apes” predicament: their homeworld is ancient Earth (as in “when dinosaurs ruled the Earth” ancient), and their space fleet got thrown eons into their future by an encounter with a Singularity; now they're trying to retake the planet that was their homeworld from the mammals that evolved and replaced them during their long absence).

                                Because Conspiracy X has done the “survivors from the age of the dinosaurs” thing, I'm not inclined to repeat it. As well, if they've survived for millions of years, there's no reason to think that they'd suddenly be on their last legs right when the Antediluvian Age begins. One way or another, there should be a connection between the event that kicks off the Antediluvian Age and the reptilians; and my preference is for their arrival to be the trigger for the Event (the alternative being that the Event was the trigger for their sudden near-extinction).

                                I'm also trying to avoid the idea of “aliens are furries” (or in this case, “scaleys”?): specifically, I don't want any of the non-human species in the setting to be humans versions of specific known Terran species, be they cats, dogs, birds, or triceratops. Either they're not from Earth (in which case, why should they resemble Earth species?) or they're from Earth's distant past (in which case when and why did the surviving species of dinosaurs evolve into humanoids?).

                                That said, what I'm thinking involves the reptilians being so diverse that if you want to introduce some dinosaur-themed reptilians, you can easily do so; it's just that they're not limited to that.

                                My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                                            Ai! Ai! Necromancy fhtagn!

                                            Because of how my brain works I lose and gain interest in things at random intervals. I woke up thinking about this idea. And honestly? I think I would really morph it into DnD-by-way-of-Storypath. Probably end up calling it Ancient, but keeping the same premise. Ancient earth with fantasy elements, and a side order of demihumans. I will admit part of my draw to this is to find a system that fits my own personal worldbuilding, so a lot of this includes that. Earlier there were some questions about how some of them mechanical changes would be done. (e.g. races, and all) I have some ideas. To begin let's start with;

                                            ORIGIN PATH
                                            A fitting start to the discussion, if I do say so myself. For this hack (Era?) my proposal would be that you split Origin path into Ancestry and Culture. It would stay as Origin path, but you would lose some flexibility to be able to fit in species and culture. So, for instance, say you want to play an Atlantean human you would, then, make your Origin path Man (or woman) of Atlantis. Mechanically, I would propose that you Ancestry has a choice of a 1 point Attribute increase (maybe with a corresponding -1?) like a Psion's stat increase from the Prometheus Chamber. Humans are the odd one out, they don't have an Ancestral path, but they don't split their starting points between Ancestral skills and Edges, and their Culture skills or edges. A nice way to represent that humans are way more community-minded than most other Ancestries.

                                            So what are my proposed Ancestries?

                                            Elves
                                            The Forest People, The Fair Folk, Aen'shae
                                            Called the aen'shae in their own tongue, the elves are a long lived, graceful species that is older than humanity, but shares a common ancestry. I want to incorporate some of the more 'fey' aspects into them. They are more diverse ranging from human coloration to very inhuman coloration. They are taller with and slender, but just as strong and tough as most human due to their size, and having different musculature. Their eyes have a tapetum lucidum like a cat, giving them better night vision but also giving them that shimmer at night.

                                            Dwarves
                                            The Mountain Folk, Dvergar, The Stone Men
                                            For dwarves I really think I would put 'technology' as their main point. At one point in time, I really thought about making dwarves as Neanderthal. 5'8'' with bigger muscles with a more defined brow-ridge; if you gave a Neanderthal a long beard it would be a dwarf. That is basically my thoughts on how the dwarves would look. Now if one can square living on top of, and in mountains with more advanced tech than anyone else with Neaderthal then it'd be a doable proposition. I am still in thought about it.


                                            Reptilians
                                            The Dragon Folk, Snake-Men, The Na'aga
                                            With here I kind of went a combined approach. I envision the Reptilians to have a host of different phenotypes, and a caste system. Their society is run by dragons. As in, serpentine, intellegent, quantum-infused reptiles that breath elemental quantum energy and are generally badasses. So you common Reptilian will be very much like a Conan-esque Snakeman. While other higher-caste will be more draconic, looking like a DnD Dragonborn. But they run the gamut between both types, some of more Lizardmen-esque, some are dinosaur people. Their society doesn't discriminate (unless you are more draconic, then you are higher caste). I think that the physiological differences could be mechanically simulated as Edges.

                                            Beastfolk
                                            This one is one I have a lot of internal debate about. Anthropomorphic animals are one of those popular things that I don't usually play, but that I do like. The inspiration here comes from, partly, Exalted with their Beastmen. I would, honestly, go for that flavor. Genetically modified and uplifted Beastfolk who have been abandoned, and who developed into an early societal model. Live in small tribes, some of them raid civilized towns, others do not. A mixed bag really.

                                            More could be added, but those are the five Ancestries (including Humans) that I would start with.

                                            PROFESSION PATH
                                            This one changes the least. It does the same thing, and has the same mechanical effects. The most change here is probably going to be a new list of example Professions that is more in-line with the setting.


                                            POWER PATH
                                            This one takes the place of the third Path for all templates. It is really 'WHAT is my template, and how did I learn it?' For example, a Psion in this setting would have a Power Path of 'Psionics: Disciple of the Urdem Temple' meaning that your power source is Psionics, and you learned it from meditative practices in the Urdem Temple. The power sources will map pretty well to the templates.

                                            Heroism
                                            Talents, and the Inspired. This is more of a simple rename, with some additions of higher level Gifts. Specifically, some gifts will require Heroism of 6+, and will be higher tiered.

                                            Psionics
                                            This one has the least amount of changes from baseline Aeon. I thought about renaming it 'Mysticism' but decided against it. Psions can be people who awakened on their own and have strange powers, or they can be part of schools and traditions who take in Gifted people and train them. Ala Jedi. Since everyone is technically a Psiad, I figure that one can do away with Aptitudes and focus on Modes, which will be renamed Disciplines.

                                            Quantum
                                            I haven't come up with a satisfactory name for Quantum. I am leaning towards 'Divinity', but that's still in the air. This one stays pretty much similar. It isn't made to play with the others starting out, but Superiors, otherwise known as Paragons, are Tier 2 and can be played. The only difference is I would add a power stat. This power source will basically adapt a lot of Scion also.

                                            Eldritch
                                            This one is new. It includes Arcane, Divine, and Primal power sources. They all run on similar rules, differentiated by where one is pull power. The Sorcerer template covers these three Power sources. The Sorcerer is a ritual casting template. Each of them have Rituals (maybe piggybacking off of Super-Science system?) and a Tolerance rating (name pending) which says how many effects they can have hanging. This is how one gets slot casting. All rituals can be cast if given enough time, this power source is not about personal power. It's about how to pull power from external sources. Hermetic sorcery is about using laws and knowledge to pull power from multiversal and eldritch beings. Faith casting rituals are powered by their deity, and a shaman is gifted power from Spirits. For example, a Atlantean wizard may know a Ritual that allows them to summon fire from an eldritch source, maybe of hypothetical places of pure fire, or maybe the concept of fire. Now they can spend a minute summoning and crafting the fireball through ritual, or they can hang it onto themselves and finish the casting with a code phrase or word. If it is hung it counts against Tolerance, and they can use an action to activate it later. Viola! Write a bunch of ritual, and note if they can be hung or not, and one is good to go.

                                            Honestly, I have thought to simply name the Power Stat for Sorcerers as Spell Tolerance. A higher power Sorcerer is really decided by how many spell they can attach to their metaphysical selves, but a better Sorcerer is really about the amount of Spells you know. One can follow the other, but it doesn't necessarily have too. You know? It's a little too 'game-y', and going with something like Gnosis would fit better with the other power stat names, but the major benefit of the power stat will be having higher tolerance to hang spells.

                                            Now, to be a Sorcerer one has to be Gifted, which means they need to be a Latent, so it's not unusual to be both a Psychic and a Sorcerer. Which brings us to the next major change...

                                            Power Stats and Levels
                                            This is probably the biggest change that will be made here. I want to unify all the power stats and tiers under a universal rule. This system is Levels (or maybe Power Level). Your Level will determine your Tier. Unlike in regular Trinity you don't buy your Power Stat. You gain your power stat depending on how much XP you have spent and it automatically increases. There has to be some changes here, also. For instance Psi starts at 1. A Mundane has a Psi of 0, not 1, in this system. So you can take Aeon and subtract 1 from any requisite Psi and get the Psi (or maybe Essence) score in Ancient. In short, when you gain a Level you get one point to put into a Power Stat. So you can increase Psi from 1 to 2, and so on and so forth.

                                            This also allows multiclassing, or multi-power source characters. The main benefit I see here is when a character reaches the logical end that a player sees for the character down that path they have a few avenues to still level up in. For instance, a character that had a psychic awakening and starts the game like that, who finds a conclave of sorcerers who offer to train them can become a Psi 1 Psion, and a Gnosis 1 Sorcerer. They are a level 2 character, just the same as if they were Psi 2. Your tier rating really only cares about your total levels, which means it looks something like this;

                                            Mundane Tier; Tier 1, Level 0
                                            Baselines. Mundanes. Muggles. This is where un-templated characters go. They are Tier 1 and usually in the role of minions.

                                            Heroic Tier; Tier 2, Level 1-5
                                            And here is when true power is first wielded. All characters are local movers and shakers able to do great things, but they are no where near the apex of their power.

                                            Paragon Tier; Tier 3, Level 6-10
                                            Proxy tier, or Nova tier. This is where players will see sight of demigodhood if they have that path before them. This is where Novas start which is also why I think Superiors will have a power stat ranging from 1-5. When they get to level six they will transform into a Quantum 1 Nova (or demigod). This is also where other characters can pursue godhood and multiclass into Quantum 1.

                                            Epic Tier; Tier 4, Level 11-15
                                            This is the God tier where characters are interacting with beings who transcend just one universe. It is also, mainly, the frontier of Quantum 6+ Novas at this moment. Other characters can get here by multiclassing, or trading out power stat for Quantum. So a Heroism 10, Quantum 1 character would exist here also.

                                            Anyways, that is my current thoughts. Any critique or thoughts are always welcome.
