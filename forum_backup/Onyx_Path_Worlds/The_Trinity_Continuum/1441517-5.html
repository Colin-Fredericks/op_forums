        As amusing as revisiting the "Divis mal has arms !!1!" thing in 2022 in a "Max Mercer has legs!!1!" version might be, until we get a canon detailed genealogy of the Mercer clan, I'm far more interested and confused by Max Mercer's personal timeline.

        We know that he started "time skipping" immediately after the Hammersmith incident while evacuating the wounded. Max Mercer tried to control the skips, and accidentally traveled to the future, initially "as a helpless observer", and met MirrorMercer when he reached the Aeon era. His evil twin would have crushed him, but thanks to Hope and the Power of Hope/Friendship/ChasteLove, Max found his way back to Michael in his own time period and escaped. So far, Max has time traveled "as a helpless observer". He's just seen the future, but hasn't interfered with it yet.

        Then Michael nurses him back to health and Max founds the Aeon Society hoping to course-correct the future he's seen. It doesn't seem to be working much and he retires from public life in the 1950s. At this point, Max now mastered his abilities enough that he is no longer "a helpless observer" when he travels in time, but he can't fix anything he foresees. When he tries to change the past to fix a future problem, he just creates a parallel universe where the problem is fixed, but the problem remains unchanged in the Original Timeline. I imagine that first half of the 20th century is when Max did the bulk of his time travel and created a gajillion alternate timelines.

        We know that Max and S.K. Bhurano compared notes about what they saw of the future, but I find myself wondering if as a clairsentient, she's bound by the same limitations Max is, or if she can course-correct the original timeline without the difficulties Max has. If so, it would be kind of a shame that she spent 200 years getting every Ph.D. ever instead of changing what Max couldn't.

        Smaller events do not create alternate timelines, but their effects are hard to predict. In theory, Max could still make the big changes to the original timeline by tipping the right domino at the right point in time, which I'm assuming is what he has been trying to do with limited success since the 1950s. I imagine that the only reason he remains involved with the Aeon Society is to keep it from veering too far off course and to maintain, through Aeon's far-reaching connections, access to whomever he needs to influence to make his targeted changes to the original timeline. His success remains a matter of debate : was he generally useless or would things have been much worse without him interfering discreetly here and there ? (Also, the targeted way that he's supposed to act to change the original timeline made me wonder if Lodestar/Lightkeepers would not be his scalpel answer to Aeon's hammer. Max Mercer as Pharos could be a fun twist.)

        tl;dr my Max Mercer Theory is that Max is Pharos.
