    I don't get why he has to be alone though. I mean he has at least one if not two other really super-powerful individuals he could talk to about what he saw.                                 Time travel is such a doozy. lol

                                So, from what we know, small changes can adjust the timeline while big changes lead to a fork to a different timeline. I would think a million little changes could lead to a major shift in the actual timeline, but that would require lots of micromanagement and such. This leads me to wondering if Max shouldn't create a temporal version of Aeon, maybe have their be some sort of super-item using telluric energies to let one manipulate the time stream in interesting ways.

                                In this I have to say I am glad that time travel powers are at this time not something anyone can do. Right now, canon wise, all we have is one person who can shift his time from present to past to future and comes in, makes a comment, stays a while, then leaves. If lots of people do it then we need to have mechanics for it - and that is a book and a half itself.

                                So much potential here though, so much fun potential.
