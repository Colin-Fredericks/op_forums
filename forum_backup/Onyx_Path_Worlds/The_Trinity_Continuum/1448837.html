    [Æon] Winged Flight

    05-18-2021, 06:39 AM
    I'm kinda hoping for an official answer if a Dev is wandering about but I'll also accept something unofficial. So at character creation I took body mod wings because well... I'm doing a dragon girl. Since it only allows flight scale 1 I doubled down and took Q Flight figuring that it would stack and if not I'd at least be able to increase flight speed later. This has led to quite a bit of discussion in the group regarding my flight speed and if I even NEED Q flight. The 2 solutions we came up with were

    - Let Q Flight and winged flight stack as long as Q flight is on putting me at speed scale 2 at base.
    - Drop Q flight for mega dex and then get mega-speed and power stunt it as working with wings which would put me at speed scale 1 still but at least let me increase it
    - build some Iron Man like boosters to allow a speed increase until I can PROPERLY increase the speed.

    In the end we decided that Q Flight and winged flight can stack for speed scale. How should this situation be handled cause I'm feeling a little burned on the winged character concept cause as far as I can tell winged flight is never meant to be GOOD

                      Originally posted by Shepherdboi View Post
                      You can buy the first level of Flight with a mid-level transformation (physiological shift: wings) to cut the xp cost by six. Further levels would require you to pay full price unless you keep stacking on more transformations.
                      It has been awhile, but I had ideas for both an expanded explanation of my original suggestion and a hilarious - and almost certainly unintended - alternate method for gaining a winged flight speed.

                      Method 1:

                      Buy the first level of Flight with a mid-level transformation (physiological shift: wings) to cut the xp cost by six. Buy a second level of Flight and counter the cost via a variant of the Restricted tag to keep your entire Flight power from working unless you're able to spread/flap your wings. You now have Flight 2 and a permanent set of wings for only 6 xp, but need to expend Quantum to fly (unless you buy the Reduced Cost tag twice for 48 xp), and can have your power neutralized (although you still keep the wings).

                      Method 2:

                      This is the entire reason I bothered posting. Purchase a dot of the Eufiber edge.
                      Lastly, energized eufiber becomes highly resistant to damage, providing its wearer with armor with the Innocuous and Soft 1 tags, so long as the eufiber contains at least one Quantum point. Add additional armor tags equal to the number of dots in this Edge.
                      The edge provides no limits on which armor tags can be purchased. Here is a fun one from page 269 of Aeon:
                      Propulsion (1): This armor has some motive power allowing it to move in a single medium, like water, land, air, or space. The armor allows the wearer to move at Speed Scale 2 in the medium specified, or in space at Space Speed [1] (p. 195). Multiple uses of this tag allow the armor to move in multiple environments.
                      Yes, RAW does seem to let you spend 3 xp to take a level of the Eufiber edge, granting you cloth wings with Speed Scale 2 in addition to attuned shape changing clothes, a Quantum battery, and a point of Soft, Innocuous armor. I'm not even sure Quantum Disruption could prevent this flight, since it doesn't involve a Power, Mega-Edge, or Mega-Attribute. So apparently Eufiber is indeed the greatest Edge ever.
