                  Is Natalya 'Swarm Queen' Dornova still around in this edition? She intrigued me in that her powers were a complete mystery, and as the one member of the Teragen 'Pantheon' to be said to be at the famous meeting, and not show up in the 'Last Supper' picture or get a tiny write-up blurb of her own. Homonculos to become a living swarm? Control others through mega-socials to be 'queen bee?' Big scary alien-queen insectoid form and /or quantum clone? Quantum summoning to create 'swarms?' All of the above?
