        I can definitely see a Fall of the United States scenario, and the rise of a totalitarian state in its wake. What doesn't ring true to me about TC:Æon's FSA is the choice of villain. I can even buy a military takeover backed by corporate interests and supported by a form of Christianity. But in all three cases, there's a subtext that these are natural developments for these groups given the circumstances, rather than aberrations.

        I don't buy the notion that social or religious conservatives would like or stand for these developments, let alone being the driving force behind them. Which is why my approach is to introduce specific personalities that have subverted the groups in question (e.g., my reference to Reverend Nehemiah Scudder) as well as prominent inclusion of the non-subverted parts of these groups in the resistance against the FSA. (It's also why I can't take seriously shows like the Handmaiden's Tale or Revolution; they strike me as dealing with caricatures of conservatives rather than anything resembling the real thing.)
