    HIgh Tier Play

    02-16-2018, 07:20 PM
    In the latest preview we are given rules for how Psions (Tier 2 Characters) can become Proxies (Tier 3 Characters). Extrapolating from these rules, if I as a SG, wanted to create my own little world, where Tier 4 Psychics are available, what should it require (Must have (x) at (y)), and what should it allow (May now raise (x) to (x+y), etc)? If you were in a game such as this, as a player, or as a SG, would you prefer Depth of power (More, PSI, Higher ratings of Aptitudes, etc), breadth (Higher ratings on Auxiliary Modes, etc), or some combination of the two?

                              My personal view is that there’s nothing stopping higher Tiers existing under the Trinity Continuum concept. I have a few ideas towards that way, myself. However, it’s way too early to begin building for higher Tiers when we haven’t really finished rounding out what’s possible at Tier 3 (novas), and have only scratched the surface for some antagonist builds at Tier 4 (Aberrants).

                              In my own personal head space, humanity is destined for higher Tiers. However, Quantum powers tend to get their faster by their very nature (burn bright, burn quick), but this speed brings dangers and pitfalls along the way. Subquantum powers are the slower and safer way to reach these higher Tier levels, but when I say slower, I believe humanity was still several hundred to a thousand years off Psiads getting to Tier 3 (based on 1E Psiad builds, and assuming that level of power fits into Tier 2), let along humanity naturally growing into Tier 4+ via that route. THe Prometheus effect allowed us to skip quite a few speed bumps, but the trade off was that it’s a form of Doyen control and psions (and Proxies) can and will never raise higher than Tier 3.

                              That’s entirely my own speculative thoughts on the matter, not endorsed or followed by OPP at all.

                              Edit to add: I don’t know how Talents fit into this head canon, or whether they can ever move to higher Tiers.
