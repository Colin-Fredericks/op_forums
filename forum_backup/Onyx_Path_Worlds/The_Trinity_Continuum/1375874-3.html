            Three more things to watch to inspire Adventure stories:

            Sky Captain and The World of Tomorrow-Jude Law plays a heroic aviator and Gwenneth Paltrow plays a reporter who team up to investigate the scheme of a mad scientist to destroy the world.

            The Fabulous World of Jules Verne-A protege scientist investigate the kidnapping of mentor. It was a Czech movie with art house special effects that made old fashioned line illustration of Victorian science fiction novels come to life. Perhaps you could be mesmerized like I was, or not.

            Le mysterieux docteur Cornelius-A French TV miniseries with an over the top evil plastic surgeon who leads the crime organization the Red Hand to prey upon the rich families, and some of those rich families fight back.

                                                The point is, their society is flawed but so is ours, and there is no need to change it at all, that is what give the setting a flavor. There is no way to create a powerful woman that fight against sexism and for women rights if the society was not sexist, that’s is no way to create a Martin Luther King Jr without racism.

                                                If you don’t want racist heroes ok, make them non racists or even anti-racists (actively fighting racism), change Quartermain to be less superior to their African friends, but if there was no systemic racism in the 1800s, Africa would never be explored by the Europeans (explored both in the discovering aspect and exploring the natural resources and people), and the merits of UAN would be none.

                                                A society that point racists as correct is the background to build many heroes fighting for the right causes. A racist hero from a book can be the villain of your campaign, showing how bad a National Hero is is the kind of job for a great group of heroes, member of a inclusive and equalitarian Aeon Society. Safari Jackson was openly sexist, yet he was changed by Crackshot capacities, it’s never too late for a redemption ark for an old Hero, even if it’s a pulpit domain character.

                                                Of course, if you want a world with no prejudice, feel free to adjust. I am just pointing the advantages of a racist/sexist setting dot the game (it doesn’t apply to real life, there is no advantages to have a racist/sexist society in real life).
