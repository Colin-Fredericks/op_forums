    Which Historical Eras?

    01-19-2020, 05:40 PM
    We're clearly getting Adventure! first. (Let the heavens be praised!!!!!) But what other historical Eras do we want?

    In my case I'd want...

    1) 17th through 18th century Asia. Clearly to vast a subject, I'd expect a core book on Qing China and then branching out to Japan, India, Indonesia, et al. Wuxia fighting rules would support Adventure! and the Core setting.

    2) Age of Sail, the Elizabethan Sea Dogs, the Sea Beggers, the early transoceanic navigation, and PIRATES!!! Lots of swashbuckling, conspiracy, Hermetic Occultists who touched the flux and thing it's the Azoth! Witch-hunts, colonial rebellion, the 15' , the '45, and more.

    3) The Classical world.

    4) The Arabian Nights.

    5) The Age of Heroes. Everything from the Tain, to the Homeric epics, to the Mahabharata, and points east!
