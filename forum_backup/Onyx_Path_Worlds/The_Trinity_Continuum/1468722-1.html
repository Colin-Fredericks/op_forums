    So, another update. Remember how last time I said I would do lore last?

    Welp, I lied. Apparently charms were more difficult to write up than I thought, so I instead turned my creative efforts towards the "background" behind the Exalted and Essence (known sometimes here as "Essential Energy," but generally just referred to by its smallest unit, Motes.) The list of origins I generated for the Exalted are non-exhaustive, feel free to come up with some stuff yourselves if you feel what I've added wasn't enough. What I'm looking to do is to embrace the spirit of the Trinity Continuum by having as many possible origins for the Exalted as possible, with most (if not all) of them being true. Hence why you could potentially have a Dragon-Blooded immigrant from Creation, the latest scion of a Sun God bloodline and someone enhanced with luck-altering nanomachines all still be "Exalted."

    In addition, in my eyes Essence has a complicated relationship with the other types of Telluric Energy (Flux, Quantum and Noetic). On the one hand, unlike the other three Essence can genuinely do anything except for resurrection of the soul and time travel, there's nothing besides that which is beyond it's grasp. On the other hand, Essence has a very difficult time bonding together because the Telluric Energies are just so much smaller and intrusive, preventing most locales besides Creation from having any massive Essence-fueled structures or effects (No Sorcerous Workings, essentially). Thus, Essence is an omniapplicable energy source which cannot be used for large infrastructure or permanent transmutation because of the presence of competing forces. You could say it's my way of balancing it all out, but if you want to just disregard that bit of lore, feel free.

    As always, I welcome you to ask questions and post comments. I will try to focus on charms more now, but I think it's going to take me even longer than I suspected to even have the basic arrays done. Hopefully I'll have something done and ready by the time the new year rolls in, but I wouldn't hold my breath if I were you.

        You might want to riff off of Burn Legend, one of the chapters in the Exalted 2e supplement Shards of the Exalted Dream:
        Set in the modern world, Burn Legend is a manga-inspired take on Exalted. In this shard, the Exalted do not exist as heroes chosen by the gods to act as their political and spiritual representatives. Rather, Burn Legend focuses on martial artists the world over, who are able to channel and use ki — the stream of life energy flowing through all things — to emphasize their martial prowess. Here, martial artists do not need the power of the Gods to exhibit extraordinary powers. There might come from arduous practice of ancient techniques honed over centuries, and the perfection of physical form. Emphasis lies on the heart of the fighter, the mentality he uses to get through the day, to train until his next big fight, and his raisins for doing so. The exalted do not exist as a quantifiable group of entities, but rather as an outgrowth of Martial arts. In this shard, the term “Exalted” has no special meaning. Rather, the exalted are the strongest of those who can attune to the spirit energy around them, moving it with their ki. By this connection, and through unlocking ancient and terrible — sometimes otherworldly — martial arts, these humans take the next step, becoming greater and more powerful than any legend. It is said that in the moment a fighter reaches this peak, this nirvana, is Aura bursts into spirit flames. He steps into a world live with ancient spirits and new powers, and sees the ki of all things winding through the world and separating God's from men. Only then is the true path of battle open to him.
        As I said, I'm thinking in terms of riffing off of this. That is, while Burn Legend as written is martial arts centric, what you're doing could and would extend well beyond that, with martial arts being but one manifestation of Essence — what Burn Legend calls ki.

        The factions described in Burn Legend are the Akuma, the Yamajin (Infernal Exalted), the Tennin (Sidereal Exalted), the Ryuujin (Dragon-Blooded), the Okami (Lunar Exalted), the Shinigami (Abyssal Exalted), and the Mugen (Solar Exalted). As written, Burn Legend has no place for Getimians, Liminals, Alchemicals, or Exigents; though as I said before, I'm recommending Burn Legend as a starting point; adding something like these Exalts wouldn't be hard.

        Anyway, I would recommend reading the entries that I just mentioned to get a sense of how the various kinds of Exalts can be reinterpreted in the context of the modern world.

        My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                    So, after so many months taking a break to give my mind space to breathe, I finally have the system for charms complete and actualized! For version 0.5, I have detailed charm families for Aim, Athletics, Close Combat, Integrity and Medicine, with the rest soon to be finished/worked on. I've also added some of the Exigent types present in the Exigents book as potential options. My plan for right now is to finish up all of the Universal Charms, and then start doing each Exalt type's charms one by one. For those curious, here are rules and some examples for what Universal Charms at least look like:

                    Charms

                    Charms come in 2 general Categories, Universal and Specific. Universal Charms can be learned by any Exalt type, while Specific Charms can only be learned by 1 specific Exalt-type. They are organized into 3 levels of power: Mortal, Immortal and Exalted. Mortal Charms can be learned at any Essence level, and require around 1-2 dots in a Skill, or 2-3 dots in an Attribute (Universal Charms have both as potential requirements, depending on whether an Exalt focuses on Skills or Attributes). Immortal Charms require at least Essence 3, and 3-4 dots of either a Skill or an Attribute. Exalted Charms need Essence 5, and either 5 dots in a Skill, or 4-5 dots in an Attribute.

                    Universal Charms are easier for certain types of Exalted to learn. Where most Exalted would have a tough time flying under their own power, Lunar Exalted can do so merely by growing wings. Infernal Exalted on the other hand easily run like the wind where others might struggle to sprint. These Exalted have a "Leeway", which allows them to treat the Essence requirement as if it were 2 levels of lower. Thus, an Immortal Charm with an applicable leeway can be learned at Essence 1, and an Exalted Charm with the same could be learned at Essence 3. Solar and Abyssal Exalted do not have listed Leeways, and instead treat all Caste Skills as having an applicable Leeway due to their sheer talent.

                    Examples:
                    • Trick Arrow Shot (Mortal)
                      • Requires: Aim 2 or Dexterity 2
                      • The Exalt can grapple targets using ranged weaponry out to (Essence/2) range bands away. Maintaining this grapple requires continually firing arrows at that target every turn. By spending 1 Mote, the Exalt can reduce the effective Size scale of a target for this purpose by (Essence) for 1 turn.
                    • Feather Foot Style (Mortal)
                      • Requires: Athletics 2 or Dexterity 3
                      • Invest 1 Mote for the scene. The Exalt can walk across liquid surfaces without falling through, and can run up walls and ceilings, so long as they do not stop moving. They also reduce the damage rating of dangerous surfaces they walk across (such as lava) by (Essence).
                    • Exalted Sword Flash (Exalted)
                      • Requires: Close Combat 5 or Dexterity 5
                      • Leeway: Dragon-Blooded, Getimian
                      • Invest 2 Motes for the scene. For the duration, the Exalt’s close combat attacks are treated as being indirect damage with a damage rating of (Power Scale + 1) which affect all enemies within short range. Durability Scale only reduces the damage on a 1-to-1 basis, and is treated as being (Essence/2, min 0) lower for this purpose.
                    • Spirit-Slaying Stance (Immortal)
                      • Requires: Enigmas 3 or Cunning 3
                      • Leeway: All Celestial Exalted
                      • Spend 1 Mote when taking out an entity in battle with lethal or greater damage. The entity is guaranteed to die, any power that would prevent their death or resurrect them is first rolled off with the target’s Stamina + Integrity vs the Exalt’s (Cunning or Resolve) + (Close Combat, Enigmas or Ranged Combat). The Exalt gains +1 Scale per difference in Tier between themselves and the target for the roll-off. On a success, the target can activate that power, otherwise they die.
                    • Ox-Body Technique (Mortal)
                      • Requires: Integrity 1 or Stamina 2
                      • The Exalt permanently gains 1 Hard Armor. In addition, increase the amount of Health levels provided at Stamina 3 and 5 by 1 each.
                      • Special: This charm can be purchased again at Essence 3 as an Immortal Charm requiring (Integrity 3 or Stamina 3), and again at Essence 5 as an Exalted Charm requiring (Integrity 5 or Stamina 4). Liminal and Lunar Exalted have leeway for these repurchases.
                    • Divine Lifespan Blessing (Exalted)
                      • Requires: Medicine 5 or Intelligence 5
                      • Leeway: Getimian
                      • Spend 2 Motes while treating a character. That character reduces their physical age by up to ((Essence - Target’s Tier) x 10) years, to a minimum of 18. By investing an additional Mote, that character no longer ages so long as the Mote remains invested. Once it is withdrawn, the character rapidly ages to match their current physical age (after the initial reduction). Every time this charm is used again on the same character by any Exalt, increase the initial cost by 1 Mote. Exalted of the same Essence or greater than the Exalt cannot benefit from this charm due to their greater metaphysical weight, and Sidereal Exalted cannot benefit at all. Abyssal Exalted receive no benefit as they disregard aging already.

                    Admittedly, I did draw on Exalted Essence a lot for inspiration here, but I tried to have some original things as well.​

                        Apologies for posting again so soon, but Inspiration struck me as it were, and I busted out some Super-Science rules in about an hour. Here's a preview for those curious:


                        Exalted Crafting

                        The Exalted replace the Super-Science table used in the TC Core Rulebook with the following below. By default, only items of Ranks 1-5 can be constructed, once an Exalt has reached Essence 6 or higher, they unlock 1 Rank per dot of Essence above 5. Mental Scale is an exception: For every point of Mental Scale above 1 the Exalt possesses, they can either treat the Rank of an item they are working on as 1 lower for the purposes of time and materials, OR unlock the next-highest Rank of project.

                        Rank Max Enhance Maximum Edge Dots Max Evo Level Max Tag Time Between
                        1 2 1 dot of M/P/S Edge None 1 1 Session
                        2 3 3 dots of M/P/S Edges None 2 2 Sessions
                        3 4 Up to 5 dots of Mental, Physical or Social Edges OR up to 3 dots of Style Edges OR 1 Defined Evocation​ Mortal 3 3 Sessions
                        4 5 Up to 9 dots; No more than 2 Evocations (Loose Evocations cost 2 dots, Defined Evocations cost 3 dots)​ Mortal 4 1 Story Arc
                        5 6 Up to 11 dots; No more than 3 Evocations​ Mortal 5 2 Story Arcs

                        8 9 Up to 25 dots; At least 2 Evocations and no more than 9.​ Exalted 8 2 Chronicles
                        9 10 Up to 30 dots; At least 3 Evocations and no more than 11.​ Exalted 9 3 Chronicles
                        Evocations are custom Charms which fits the item’s materials, purpose and legend. Uniquely among Super-Science items, Exalted “Artifacts” as they are known have a degree of customization for their user. They can create wildly different power sets depending on the user, while still holding true to their usual themes. These “Loose” Evocations require the user to purchase them with XP as if they were Charms of the appropriate level, but are otherwise customizable to match the user, so long as it still suits the item’s themes. However, they cannot be utilized by anyone other than Essence wielders.

                        Alternatively, “Defined” Evocations must be defined by the creator at the moment of creation, at least in terms of utility, but do not need to be learned by the user to wield. They must still fit the item’s themes. In addition, baselines and other characters who wield Artifacts can use these Defined Evocations themselves, substituting Momentum, Inspiration, Quantum Points, Psi Points or even Injury Conditions for Essence.
                        ​
                        An Artifact can have at most half of their Evocations be Defined (rounded up). At least half of their Loose Evocations (rounded up) must be Mortal Level. Rank 6+ Artifacts are always storied items, just by their construction if not their deeds. Treat them as having (Rank - 5) dots of the Fame Edge. Rank 9 Artifacts have the Living Legend Enhanced Edge instead.

                        The Exalted in Creation define Artifacts as being of 3 levels: Talismans, which most mortal sorcerers can content themselves with creating, Lesser Wonders, which most so-called Artifacts truly are, and True Artifacts, which have legendary backgrounds all on their own. These roughly correspond to Ranks 1-2, 3-5 and 6-9. They notably do NOT line up with the general Super-Science categories and are mostly descriptive. Treat “Advanced Science” as a 1-dot Edge granting +1 Scale, “Inspired Science” as a 3-dot Edge granting +2 Scale instead, and “Powered Science” as a 5-dot Edge granting +3 Scale instead, each of which requires the prior. The Scale provided this way only affects 1 type of Scale, multiple varieties require multiple purchases.​

                            Back again with a new update, this time regarding Martial Arts, and with it comes a question that will be presented later. I also have the "basics" of Spirits taken care of, though that will likely need to be adjusted with time as well.

                            Starting with the latter, Spirits are sentient Essence constructs which only function effectively in High-Essence Environments (essentially, Creation, rather than Aeon or TC:Core's setting). Outside of such environments, Spirits must either spend 1 Mote or suffer 1 Aggravated Injury Condition each scene as their body cannibalizes its internal power structure for fuel. This can be resolved through the use of powers or Super-Science, but is otherwise unavoidable.

                            Most spirits, with the exception of elementals, are "dematerialized characters". Dematerialized characters are entities which do not have a material presence in reality, such as, again, most spirits, Psions using astral projection, Novas using Immaterial Phasing, or other such entities. Dematerialized characters cannot normally be interacted with physically, though they can psychically unless stated otherwise. The Exalted however are specialists in dealing with dematerialized characters: Spirit-Cutting Glance (which requires either Enigma 1 or Cunning 2) allows them to see, touch and attack such entities, Spirit-Grounding Shout makes them material and affected by heavy gravity, Spirit-Slaying Stance can permanently destroy them and Spirit-Repelling Diagram allows the Exalt to create barriers which stop their entry. These and more are all universal charms the Exalted host have access to, with some like Lunars and Liminals taking this expertise to greater heights.

                            And now, we get to Martial Arts. Martial Arts, or rather Mystic Martial Arts, is the privilege and specialty of the Exalted. These styles of combat call upon spiritual themes in order to extract heightened performance. While only the Exalted can Master such arts, they CAN be taught to non-Exalted, albeit very inefficiently. How this is done is as follows:

                            The Exalted naturally have access to Martial Arts so long as they have at least 1 dot in Close Combat. After that, they can purchase Martial Arts Charms. MA Charms have 3 requirements, of which only 2 are needed to actually learn: A minimum rating in Close Combat, a minimum rating in a style-chosen Attribute, and a minimum rating in some other Skill. Technically speaking, you don't even need Close Combat beyond the first dot to access these arts: A Righteous Devil has little use for hand-to-hand combat when they can just shoot everything with Ranged Combat and judge their targets with Resolve. You can also substitute the selected Attribute and Skill for the normal stats when making unarmed attacks, or with attacks that have the right weapon tags.

                            Just like Universal Charms, certain styles also have Leeways for a given Exalt type. If the Exalt matches the Leeway, they treat the Essence Requirement of charms as being 2 lower. The Immaculate Dragon Styles for example are uniquely suited to the Dragon-Blooded, and Solars are greatly suited towards Righteous Devil. Sidereals are the only Exalt type to have a universal Leeway for Martial Arts, outside of Solars and Abyssals with the right Caste Skills.

                            Every MA Charm also has a Mastery benefit, which either enhances the original effect or provides an alternative use. Exalted that have all 3 requirements fulfilled unlock this Mastery effect. Alternatively, having a Leeway automatically unlocks it as well. In either case, Mastery of a Martial Arts Charm is necessary in order to teach the style to non-Exalted.

                            Non-Exalted must purchase some form of "initiation" into the Martial Arts to use them. Spirits just have to buy a certain charm once (or twice to unlock Mastery effects). Those who cannot use Essence must employ more roundabout means, and also suffer a +1 dot requirement to all MA Charms (max Attribute rating 6, max Skill rating 5). These are as follows:
                            • Talents and other Inspired must learn the Close Combat Skill Gift “Chakra Alignment” which requires Close Combat 2 or more. Talents gain Mastery by repurchasing Charms again at 1 level higher (Mortal -> Immortal -> Exalted), which requires additional instruction and all 3 traits fulfilled. They treat the required Essence as equivalent to (Inspiration/2, rounded down), and 1/2 points of Inspiration equals 1 invested/spent Mote.​
                            • Psiads and Psions must develop a special 1-dot Mode called "Motonics", which uses their least favored XP Cost. They unlock the Mastery effect by purchasing the 2nd-dot of Motonics and having the correct traits. They treat their (Psi Rating - 1) as equivalent to Essence, and 2/3 Psi Points equals 1 invested/spent Mote.
                            • Novas must learn a special Mega-Edge called “Mega-Enlightenment”. They treat their Quantum Rating as equivalent to Essence, and can add their Mega-Attribute dots to their Attribute dots to meet the requirements. They unlock Mastery by purchasing the Mega-Edge a 2nd time, and 3/5 Quantum Points equals 1 invested/spent Mote.
                            • Baselines by default cannot learn Martial Arts. However, they can pay 15 XP to learn “Chakra Alignment”, which provides sufficient Inspiration to immediately transform them into a Talent. After that, they use the same rules as above.
                            There's also Sidereal Martial Arts, but I'm still working through that at the moment.

                            Now, for the question I mentioned earlier. I forgot until I was finished with this whole ruleset for Martial Arts that Style Edges are a thing. Since those do represent mortal knowledge of Martial Arts, I was wondering: Should I scrap the special powers for initiating into Martial Arts in favor of a Style Edge that does the same thing?

                                        New Update (v.0.7.5, close to completion): Sorcery

                                        Yes, I did say before that I had no intentions of adding sorcery into the mix. That was before I realized that the Super-Science rules could easily be adapted to Workings as well. So, here are my rules for Sorcery thus far. Note that I have no idea what to do with spells thus far and am still trying to come up with something. I could do the usual learned formulas, or maybe I could do something like power stunts for Novas. Regardless, I DO have rules for Sorcery in general as well as workings.

                                        Sorcery

                                        Sorcery is the art of altering reality to fit the will-worker's desire. It is divided into 3 Circles of Proficiency (First, Second, Third) and 3 Categories of specialization (Miraculous, Necromantic and Prophetic). The 3 categories correlate to standard-Sorcery, Necromancy and Astrology in Exalted proper (see the document for more details). To begin using Sorcery, one must purchase the Sorcerous Initiation Charm (Mortal), and then later repurchase it as an Immortal and Exalted Charm for the 2nd and 3rd initiations. These Initiation charms have no Leeways, not even for Solars or Abyssals.

                                        The first time the Exalt is initiated, they unlock the 1st Circle of a given category corresponding to their Exalt Type (Solars unlock the First Circle of Miraculous Sorcery, and Sidereals First Circle Prophetic Sorcery). When they are initiated a second time, they improve their first category to Second Circle, and unlock a second category of their choice to First Circle. The third time, the first category reaches Third Circle, the second Second Circle, the third First Circle.

                                        However, not all Exalted can reach the Third Circle for any given category. Solars and Infernals can do so for Miraculous Sorcery, Abyssals for Necromantic Sorcery and Sidereals for Prophetic Sorcery. Everyone else, when they initiate for the third time, instead treat the Essence Density of the area they are in as 1 level higher for the purposes of Sorcery (thus making them more efficient at the cost of potential). The non-Third Circle types also have two choices for their primary category instead of being stuck with one.
                                        Exalt Type Primary Category Max Circle Rating
                                        Solar Miraculous Third
                                        Dragon-Blooded Miraculous/Necromantic Second
                                        Lunar Miraculous/Necromantic Second
                                        Sidereal Prophetic Third
                                        Abyssal Necromantic Third
                                        Infernal Miraculous Third
                                        Alchemical Miraculous/Prophetic Second
                                        Liminal Necromantic/Prophetic Second
                                        Getimian Necromantic/Prophetic Second
                                        Sorcerous Workings work just like Super-Science projects. They are different from artifacts in that they alter reality in order to achieve a given outcome. Due to their heavy reliance on Essence Density, Sorcerous Workings are more limited in dimensions outside of Creation. The less Essence there is, the less powerful a Working can be and the shorter it's lifespan. A Working with a “Forever” Duration lasts forever: The Exalt does not need to supply Essence to sustain it, and it cannot be undone except with another Working (or similar effort) to alter it back into how it used to be. “Continuous (Year)” means that the Working lasts for (Essence) years, and after that dissipates unless the Exalt spends (Circle Used) Motes to extend its duration by another year. Maintained means that the Exalt must maintain the initial Mote investment, otherwise the Working fades immediately.​
                                        ​
                                        Essence Density Example Location Maximum Working Circle Maximum Working Rank Duration
                                        High Creation Third 9 Forever
                                        Medium TC - Core Second 6 Continuous (Year)
                                        Low TC - Aberrant First 3 Maintained
                                        To begin a Working, the Exalt must designate the Rank of the project, which is limited by their initiated Circle and Essence Density: First Circle for Rank 1-3, Second Circle for Rank 4-6, Third Circle for Rank 6-9. Then, they must Invest Motes equal to the corresponding Circle. After that, things work as they would for a Super-Science project. Flaws work the same as with Super-Science, as do Crafting Stunts.​

                                        One important thing to note is that Ranks within each Circle are not inherently more “powerful” than another. Rather, the higher a Working’s Rank within a given Circle, the more accurate it becomes to their desires. A Rank 1 and Rank 2 Working can both bless a wheat field to become immune to disease, but the Rank 2 Working does so by fortifying their genetic code as the Sorcerer wished, while the Rank 1 Working did so by creating symbiotic fungi that protect the wheat at the cost of making it taste weird. Both accomplished the task, but the Rank 2 Working did so to specification. Rank 3/6/9 Workings can also include contingencies, adapting the Working to changing circumstances.

                                        Specification Levels:
                                        1. None: There is no focus as to how the goal is accomplished, just that it is.
                                        2. Contained: The Working is accomplished through some general method: A “biological” method differs wildly from an “engineering” method. Rank 1/4/7
                                        3. Focused: The Working is performed exactly as the Sorcerer desires. Rank 2/5/8
                                        4. Adapting: The Working can adapt to changing circumstances beyond its normal parameters, to a point. Rank 3/6/9
                                        ​

                                                            Alright! I have put the pedal to the medal before taking a small personal vacation, and have come up with the revised ruleset and examples of Martial Arts:

                                                            While anyone can learn and practice martial arts, the Exalted are the greatest martial artists in the cosmos. No entity or species (save maybe the Celestial Incarnae and the greatest of demons) can ever exceed them in prowess in this regard. They are also the greatest instructors in martial arts, and this can be seen in their creation of several styles which non-Exalted can use, which nonetheless are superior to the normal fare. Still, there are some styles, referred to as the Lotus (or in the First Age, the “Sidereal”) Styles, which only the Exalted can learn, barring one or two exceptions. In this section, we will cover both examples.

                                                            There are 3 effective tiers of martial arts, which roughly correspond to character Tiers.
                                                            1. Standard Style Edges, like Precise Martial Arts (see TC: Core). These can be learned by anyone, even baselines, in order to gain the benefits. They represent the basic training in martial arts like blocking and practicing strikes. Tier 2 or higher characters must have fully mastered a given Style Edge in order to advance, but the Exalt can skip this stage if they desire. Any points invested are purely for the benefits of the Edge.
                                                            2. Mystic Martial Arts are special Style Edges which can only be learned by Tier 2+ characters. They represent the “normal” Martial Arts of Exalted proper, such as Snake or Tiger Style. Exalted can learn these styles with no requirements, and indeed must master at least 1 Mystic Style in order to advance. Other Tier 2+ characters can only learn them by mastering a Standard Style, and cannot advance any further barring Storyguide fiat.
                                                            3. Lotus Martial Arts are not Style Edges, but instead a line of charms from Mortal to Exalted. To begin learning these Charms, the Exalted must be Tier 3, and must have a Mystic Style fully mastered which corresponds to the Lotus Style they are attempting to learn. Lotus Martial Arts are purchased in a vague order: Before you purchase the Immortal level charms, you must first purchase all of the Mortal level charms, and the same with the Exalted level charms. Unlike standard charms, Mortal level charms require Essence 3, Immortal Essence 5 and Exalted Essence 7 (which is likely impossible outside of Creation). Leeways exist for entire styles, which reduce the Essence requirement of every charm in that style by 2 points. Sidereal and Getimian Exalted have a universal leeway for Lotus Martial Arts. Leeways are important for another reason: Only someone with a Leeway who has fully mastered a Lotus Martial Art can instruct others in its ways.


                                                            Standard Styles

                                                            Gunslinger (o to ooo)
                                                            Prerequisite: Cunning 3, Aim 2 and an appropriate Path

                                                            Rather than merely focusing on precision, the character also tries their best to fire at a target as quickly as possible without sacrificing efficacy. Your character cannot use these maneuvers with shotguns and other projectile launchers that only fire once before needing to reload.

                                                            (o) Steady Hand: Increase the amount of successes needed to disarm the character’s gun by (Gunslinger).

                                                            (oo) Quick Draw: While wielding a pistol or other small gun in one hand, treat the character as having Speed Scale 2 for the purposes of initiative. With all other applicable weapons, gain 1e for initiative instead.

                                                            (ooo) Gun Fu: The character can fire applicable guns in close range with only +1 Difficulty.​
                                                            ​

                                                            Mystic Styles

                                                            The Mystic Styles are structured as follows:

                                                            (o) The prerequisite style gains a small permanent bonus. Exalted gain an additional benefit they must spend or invest Motes for. This way, both Exalted and non-Exalted characters gain a benefit for investing.
                                                            (oo) Some sort of activateable ability. Exalted can use Essence to do it reflexively, while other characters can take a simple action.
                                                            (ooo) The Form of the style. A given character can only be in one Martial Arts form at a time, whether that be Mystic or Lotus Martial Arts. Entering the form takes an action, or investing 1 Mote for Exalted. It lasts for the scene, or until switched to another form. While in the form, the character gains several passive benefits which fit with the style’s overall themes.
                                                            (oooo) As with the 2 dot option, generally more powerful.
                                                            (ooooo) The capstone of the style, it can be an enhancement for the Form or a powerful technique akin to the 2 and 4 dot benefits.
                                                            ​

                                                            Righteous Devil Style (o to ooooo)
                                                            Requires: Gunslinger ooo OR Exalted; Aim ooo, Resolve ooo

                                                            Righteous Devil Style is the art of the gunslinger and the firebrand, the lone figure that pursues justice for crimes committed. Part of the instruction in this style involves morality, either in some defined code or a loose philosophy, but it always involves some form of ethics. Righteous Devil Style techniques can be used with pistols, rifles and weapons with the Incendiary tag.

                                                            (o) Kiss of Sun Concentration: Weapons with the Incendiary tag qualify for the benefits of Gunslinger, no matter their size. Exalted wielding weapons with this tag can spend 1e to grant their weapon the Explosive (Ranged) tag for the turn.

                                                            (ooo) Righteous Devil Form: Take a simple action or invest 1 Mote for the scene. When this Form is activated, the character reflexively rolls (Presence + Persuasion) to create an atmosphere of fear, shame, guilt or remorse. They reduce the Difficulty to do so by 1 per Tier above the highest Tiered enemy. Enemies within the scene suffer a Difficulty increase to all rolls to retreat or hide equal to the generated Atmosphere level. Finally, guns wielded by the character gain the Melee tag, allowing them to be used with the Close Combat skill in close range.​
                                                            ​
                                                            (ooooo) Caress of 1000 Hells: After spending a simple action and taking no other actions that turn (or by spending 2 motes with a simple action), the character can on their next turn roll Presence + (Aim or Persuasion) against a Difficulty of the target’s Resolve. On a success, the target faces a choice: They can either suffer a Persistent Injury Condition in their worst available Injury box, or the target must immediately form a Bond of remorse towards an individual or group which they have wronged. They are compelled to do something to make up for this crime, as if the Exalt had successfully persuaded them to do so.​


                                                            Lotus Styles

                                                            Most Lotus Styles have origins within Creation, and thus are more difficult to find a teacher for outside of its boundaries. This doesn’t mean that it's impossible, simply that the Exalt might need to find some other, more esoteric means of instruction like an enchanted technique manual.
                                                            ​

                                                            Prismatic Arrangement of Creation Style
                                                            Requires: ANY Mystic Style ooooo; Culture oo, Science oo
                                                            Leeway: All Exalted
                                                            Origin: Creation

                                                            Prismatic Arrangement of Creation Style is a unique creation, in that it is designed from the ground up to be easy to access by non-Sidereals. Originally, it was thought that its connection to 4 of the 5 Magical Materials is what allowed for its universal ease. However, this was soon proven wrong when the Liminal Exalted, which favored the last remaining material “soulsteel,” were able to learn the style themselves. The emerging Abyssal Exalted, also able to wield it, suggested that the magical materials were not the true connection point. New theories suggest it may be a result of the Charm “Ways of Exaltation,” but even this is hypothetical at best.

                                                            Regardless, Prismatic Arrangement of Creation Style is easy to learn and offers many applications outside of combat. The toolbelt of options it provides means that over half of the Sidereals employed by the Bureau of Destiny know at least 1 charm in the style.
                                                            ​
                                                            • God Ways (Mortal)
                                                              • Invest 1 Mote. While active, the Exalt dematerializes and possesses an object within close range, up to Size Scale 2. By default, the Exalt cannot move or act outwardly while possessing an object, but by spending 2 Motes, they can regain mobility and act for a scene using their normal Scale - 1 (except for Size or Mental Scale). Destroying the object does not harm the Exalt and merely exorcizes them, unless magic that targets dematerialized characters is used.
                                                            • Four Magical Materials Form (Immortal)
                                                              • Invest 3 Motes for the scene. For the duration, the Exalt treats their body as if it were an Artifact of Rank (Essence). They automatically gain the “Advanced Science (Power)” tag, and can assign the remaining dots for more Edges. They can also use these dots to develop Evocations for this Form as if they were an Artifact. In addition, they become immune to all effects that would immobilize or cripple the Exalt’s limbs. Finally, the Exalt gains the ability to use Powered Super Science items for other energy sources (Flux, Noetic, Quantum or Aether).
                                                            • Prismatic Arrangement of Creation Form (Exalted)
                                                              • This Charm is not a true Form charm, so much as it is a culmination in the concept of “Forms.” When learned, the Exalt gains the ability to maintain 2 Martial Arts Forms at once. By investing an additional 2 motes, the Exalt can maintain a third Form on top of this, and reduces the mote cost of Mystic Forms and this style’s Forms by 1 each (min 0).
