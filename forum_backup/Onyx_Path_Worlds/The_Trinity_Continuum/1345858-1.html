    Originally posted by Zeea View Post
    Also, does anyone else think its funny that the most common running theme of the Trinity setting is "okay, bad idea, don't try that again!" I'm not sure if any human decisions ended the Adventure! era, but Aberrant ends with "okay, those were a bad idea" and Anima ends with, "okay, brain tech was a bad idea" and Aeon is already in the middle of "maybe psions are actually a bad idea" (with the Quantakinetic purge and the shift away from relying on teleporters.)
    Hmm… kind of. From the future history we're given in the back of TC:Æ, it's eventually discovered that psions are the result of malicious alien meddling, with psiads being humanity's true psionic heritage. But there's not really a backlash against psions: if anything, they tend to be seen as fellow victims of the Doyen manipulations: after all, the Prometheus Chambers artificially limited their potential.

    Originally posted by Mateus Luz View Post
    Personally, the 3 question marks Ian left I would make:
    The first around the Discovery/Colonisation of the American Continent, El Dorado stuff (Victorian and pulp are really connected, Victorian being the inspiration to pulp)
    Nitpick: Discovery and colonization of the American Continents was late 1400s to early 1700s; the Victorian era was the mid-to-late 1800s. They're distinct eras.

    Originally posted by Mateus Luz View Post
    The second I would put on a 70’s 80’s style, with the surge of low level novas Psiads and talents working as super-soldiers and super-spies on the Cold War (noir is basically pulp but darker).
    Recall that during this time, the Doyen had erected a barrier around the Earth that suppressed the likes of Hammersmith Events; when Divas Mal triggered the Galatea Event, a case could be made that it was as potent as it was because it needed to be in order to shatter the Doyen barrier. But that does mean that the Cold War would have been a period of waning power where Stalwarts and Mesmerists are concerned: it would likely have been an all-Talents era. That said, I could also see something triggered by Roswell, with the trigger being the crash-landed of an alien spacecraft whose detonated drive system serves like a localized Hammersmith Event.

    Originally posted by Mateus Luz View Post
    The third I would make a Space Opera on Star Trek TOS style, finding ancient gods living in space and sexy aliens fighting in colosseum.
    I tend to lean more toward classic Legion of Superheroes (for that era's Quantum-powered types) or Lensman (for that era's Psi-powered types), supplemented by a small selection of aliens and robots. In particular, the humanoid “aliens” (the ones who seem mostly human save for a few cosmetic differences like pigmentation or bumpy foreheads) would mostly have started out as Æon or post-Æon Aberrant cults before developing into stable parahuman space cultures over the intervening centuries.
