    I'm curious about your interest in Kickstarter highest reward levels

    01-26-2014, 06:40 PM
    I understand that any Aeon/ Trinity Kickstarters if they happen are way off and may not follow the format of what has been done already as OPP evolves the Kickstarters necessarily as they go. However, I'm curious on your thoughts of the higher level reward tiers in OPP Kickstarters.

    I was skimming through the list of OPP Kickstarters and noticed that the highest level rewards are pretty much left unsold. Out of the 9 KS's I think OPP has done the highest level has been sold on only two of them - and I think I remember hearing that one of those was one of those annoying scam purchases.

    Although the details have evolved over the 9 KS's, generally you get a mix of the core deluxe edition, crediting in the book, wallpaper, perhaps a screen, some other PDFs, recently a discount on POD versions, inclusion in the development process in some way, and usually a game session run by the developer in person or online - for a price of between $2k to $3k or so.

    Looking at these I personally prefer actual non-digital product over, say, a game session run by the dev or inclusion in the design process in some way. So, while I could spring for the higher tier to support my fave game it'd be hard to justify as I can probably get all that really interests me at a much, much lower priced tier. But, I know I could be tempted to shell out more.

    So now I'm curious what others think of what's on offer in those highest reward tiers.

    What would it take to make you take the plunge? Are the actual items in the highest level reward level you've seen on the 9 Kickstarters appealing to you? Is the price too high to justify? Are you planning on going for it anyway?


    Ad Astra

I say this as a photographer and graphic designer, where all my personal art is non-functional. I lurve me some functional art and generally find myself much more willing to shell out for an awesome t-shirt as opposed to an awesome poster. I only have so many walls, you know? Bookshelves take up a lot of area!

                      There are issues, Jeebus knows the Artisan Dice guys did a Kickstarter last year and their materials due to be delivered in May of last year were still being delivered in January of this year. However, Kickstarter backers tend to understand stuff like that and theirs is kind of an extreme case from what I've seen around Kickstarter. High-level backers tend to be few in number, meaning you can do low-run and keep the stock pretty small. There isn't much holding of stock since you can place the order once the Kickstarter succeeds, then receive and ship out the items as soon as they're produced, with minimal holding between receiving and shipping. Maybe you can talk to some other Kickstarter-ers that've done that? Like the Grimrock folks and the Artisan Dice guys, for example.

                      Depending on what companies or artists you deal with to make the prints/posters/shirts/dice/beer-coozies, they might be really happy to work with you on logistics. Especially smaller artists that are interested in working on an RPG project, or might love the Trinityverse already. Just some brainings to cogitate.
