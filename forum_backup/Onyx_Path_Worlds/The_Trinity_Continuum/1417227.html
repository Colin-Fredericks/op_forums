    Modern Psiad Idea Dicussion

    10-07-2020, 09:07 PM
    Hey all, so I am in a Trinity mood. Well, really, I am in an Adventure! mood but as that is not out in the Storypath I figure discussing Aeon might be the next best thing.

    My idea is. Its modern day. In a version of the Continuum without Novas. Aeon Society can exist, though it doesn't.

    You, or a personification or a character, have become a Psiad. As far as you can tell you are alone - though this might not be true, other Psiads might exist as well.

    (As a note I picked Psiad rather than Psion because a) I wanted spontaneous creation to be the way you gain power, and b) I want access to multiple Aptitudes.)

    The question becomes what do you do. How do you use your newly gained powers. And what sort of Powers would you want.

    Me, my two primary Aptitudes would be Telepthy and either Electrokinesis or Psychokinesis, not sure. I will say this, I would definitely spread things out so that I have at least one, if not two, dots in every Mode possible. I might go deep in my main field but I would also go wide, very wide.

    I figure there would be a lot of hidden exploration of what I can do, once the whole 'wow, do I really have these powers' moment faded away. Afterward my first action would be to find a way to 'legally' get myself money - this is necessary as money unfortunately is needed to become secure in our world, and I think being secure with powers would be very useful. I figure something with creating and running a corporation might be useful as a way to gain lots of money relatively quickly.

    My other main thing is that unless my hand is forced I would keep my new state a secret. Maybe tell my parents, though that depends on the character's relationship with them, same with a significant other.

    I would also be on the look out for others, not just people with a similar ability but also governments and organizations that might know something about my state. I do have to admit I am not entirely sure what I would do if I ran in the Aeon Society, I gotta think more on this.

    Feel free to provide your own ideas. Additionally, feel free to vary the setting itself. Like if you want x to exist but I made it seem not there, then add x to your version.

    Oh, as a thought, Talents probably do exist. They are normal enough to have moved under the radar and such.

    So yeah, I hope others participate in this thought train as well.

      Basic paranoia from a lifetime of pop culture means I tell nobody, out of fear of getting the wrong sort of attention. Having legitimate psychic powers is the most interesting thing to ever happen to me, so my current interests atrophy while I spend all my time exploring my psychic potential. Powers that can be practiced alone, and in a non-destructive way would get the most practice, so likely possibilities:

      Biokinesis: Psychomorphing and Transmogrify. Adaptation is hard to learn without getting poisoned/sick.
      Clairsentience: Psychometry and Psycholocation. Psychocognition is difficult to learn if you're never in danger, and you don't want to put yourself in danger.
      Electrokinesis: Any can be learned at low levels. Trying to zap things with lasers/electricity is unwise.
      Psychokinesis: Thermokinesis 1/Telekinesis. Are you likely to learn Perikinesis without being attacked? A tennis ball launcher may help, but THEY may see you practice.
      Quantakinesis: Probably locked. Energetics and Transmutation could be learned, but you can't learn to counter powers without other powers to counter.
      Telepathy: Rapport is easy enough. Hard to learn Mind Shield without Telepaths to shield against. Mindshare requires another person to train with.
      Teleportation: All three can be learned alone with little danger of exposure, and are great for escaping from THEM.
      Vitakinesis: Hard to learn Iatrosis without injuring yourself or becoming known as a faith healer. Similar problems with Algesis. Practice Augendis on yourself.

                  The ideas here are really cool. Glad to read them.

                  So I wonder if others would actually announce themselves and their new powers. hehe

                  As the powers go here are some more of my thoughts.

                  Biokinesis is not exactly something that will let one easily make money, at least not if they wish to keep their secret. But it could be useful to try and hide oneself a bit more, that and maybe improve their physical and mental abilities.

                  Clairsentience is very useful in knowing what to do to profit and advance. All three modes have their advantages, like Psycholocation Dowsing being used to find Gold or something to make more money and Psychocognition being used to find the best path forward. Also it can just be fun, I mean imagining standing outside and using Sensor Manipulation to see Mars. So cool.

                  Eletrokinesis might be one of the 'easier' powers to make profit from. You work with technology and machines and computers. In some ways you can be like Tony Stakr and invent cool new techs.

                  Psychokinesis is not so useful if you stay at home and remain safe, though Telekinesis is always useful as it can be fun to get stuff by reaching out to it rather than moving to get it. Remote Hands is a two dot power and it requires no Psi, so its easy to use.

                  Quantakinesis is offiically not something one can get, but if we are going with the idea of Psiads manifesting in the modern day then I see no reason to prohibit that aptitude from use. Which could lead to all sorts of fun uses when it starts to develop.

                  Telepathy has a lot of the easy methods to gain power and such, especially in a world that doesn't exactly have knowledge that one needs to hide their very thoughts.

                  Teleportation is hard to get but once its gotten there is much potential in not having to move about through space the normal way. One could pick a couple of stops to act as stepping stones and so you can go where you need to go easily and quickly.

                  Vitakinesis is useful on the whole healing others part, and also being better and healthier yourself (the whole living forever thing helps too) but its hard to hide having this abilty if you want to use it to help others.

                  Going over the Aptitudes I do really think my initial idea of Telepathy and Electrokinesis would be my go to primary. I would probably develop Clair to as high as I can because its useful, same with Telekinesis. BK would be useful to get to make myself healthier but I wouldn't foucus on it, sake with VK. QK would tried to be gotten and so would Teleportation.
