    [Æon] How much do people in the Trinity Era know about the Nova Era?

    11-02-2020, 01:14 AM
    In 1E there had been a concerted effort to remove any information about "good" novas, to give everyone the impression that the only good Aberrant was a dead one.

    But in 2E, rewrite seems to be much more about keeping the nova derived plans for Grey Goo off of the public archives.

    So in that case, how much is publicly known about the Nova era? Do people know about the Fireman and the era of good times before everything went to hell?

                                          Aeon era people know that novas were a thing, they weren’t all bad, they did a lot of good, developed technologies, cleaned up the world, helped out.

                                          But Aeon era people also know that Quantum eruption inevitably leads to mutation and madness, coupled with rapid development of near unstoppable power. Aeon era people know that it doesn’t matter how much you loved the person who erupted, they’ll go nuts and murder you and probably your city. Odds are they already did so by erupting. Aeon era people know that the energies the Aberrants draw on and use are long-term incompatible with normal humans, and if you let them live you’ll mutate until you’re dead or some kind of monster under their control.

                                          Aeon era people know that as difficult as it is to know that these people were once human, they’re not anymore and need to be exterminated before they destroy humanity.

                                          The colony on Eden flies against all this knowledge, which is why its existence is so shocking and confusing. The Aberrants everywhere else (Khantze Lu Ge etc.) absolutely conform to this. So now those in the know are trying to work out why Eden is different, while fully expecting them to turn out to be monsters at any moment.
