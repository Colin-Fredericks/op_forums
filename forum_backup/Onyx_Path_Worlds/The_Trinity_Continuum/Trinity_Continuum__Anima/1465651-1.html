                            Ok, there's something that I want to point out that has bothered me.

                            SPOILERS

                            So am I the only one a bit disappointed at the fate of the directive? I mean, from the largest intelligence service in the world that dealt with strange phenomena, then to the Directive to protect humanity from novas to...a minor security company focused on protecting the interests of abusive corporations...what?
                            Here's the thing, I'm not disappointed at the what, but how they ended there. It was basically the world leaders saying, "Hey, we don't need you anymore, so we're cutting funding, also we need the money to rebuild" Sure, it makes sense, but isn't that a bit underwhelming?
                            I can accept the nations of the world taking away the funding and resources as a punishment for the aberrant war, after all the Directive was created precisely to prevent something like that, and they failed, hard. That left the Directive bitter and lost, with not choice but to become mercenaries as a way to survive, and also working for the corps was a perfect way to get back at the governments that punished them.
                            I could also accept a more heroic approach, where the Directive fought heroically to prevent and minimize the aberrant war. Even thought they failed in the end, things could have been much worse without their help. The thing is that they suffered such catastrophic losses that the world nations decided to just abandon them, since it was easier than trying to rebuild the organization. Again, they reformed into a merc company for the same reasons as before, survival and revenge.
                            It's just a minor thing. Again, I don't mind seeing one of the major alliances fall from grace, I just expected something more from such an important organization in the history of the trinity continuum. What do you think? Would you like to share other takes?
