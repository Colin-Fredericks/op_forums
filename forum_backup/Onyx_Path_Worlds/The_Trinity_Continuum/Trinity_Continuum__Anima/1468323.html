                                              That said, I live in Canada. Ordering in Canada works about the same way it does in the US. I have never had difficulty getting any of the books I work on.
                                              • If you get in early enough, you can pledge on Kickstarter. Shipping's a bear, but you're guaranteed your book, and you often get a lot of supplementary PDF material "free" which balances it out.
                                              • If you miss the Kickstarter, you can preorder on BackerKit. Same deal but without the freebies.
                                              • Post-campaign, the Kickstarted books can be ordered from Studio2 or Indie Press Revolution, either by you or by your local game store. IIRC Studio 2 ships through Alliance, so for most stores it can be made part of their standard order without much additional shipping cost.
                                              • You can get PDF or print-on-demand from DriveThruRPG. DTRPG's got a printer in Markham or thereabouts, so it's domestic shipping.
                                              It's the same sources that Americans have access to, except the shipping's higher on the traditionally-printed books.
