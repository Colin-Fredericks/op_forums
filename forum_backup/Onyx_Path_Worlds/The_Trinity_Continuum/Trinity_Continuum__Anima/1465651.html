                  Trinity Anima Kickstarter is starting on November 18th! Here's how the creators sum up what he is! (Sounds like more digestible postpunk, even )

                  What is Trinity Continuum: Anima?
                  * A brand-new Trinity Continuum game. Unlike TC: Æon, TC: Aberrant, and TC: Adventure!, this isn’t the second edition of a previously made game. This is a completely new setting in the year 2084, sitting between TC: Æon and TC: Aberrant.
                  * A new take on cyberpunk. Unlike a hundred gritty, worn-down cyberpunk dystopias, the city of Cascade is shiny and new. Characters will still fight a fascist government and unethical corporations, but they do so in a gleaming city full of potential and hope.
                  * A video game in an RPG. This isn’t just a tabletop adaptation of a popular video game; this is a new system that allows the characters of TC: Anima to themselves play in a fully immersive MMO. The rules are designed to make the experience feel like playing in a video game.
                  * A modern take on cyberware. No loss of “humanity” or “essence” happens in TC: Anima. Nearly every character has cyberware, either as a brain implant or as assistive devices after a devastating war. Using cyberware doesn’t make you less of a person, but it does open new dramatic possibilities.
                  * A mystery you can dig into right away. Streamers of the popular video game Terra Surge are disappearing. Why? That question is something that can draw the player characters in immediately, or Storyguides can focus on other parts of the setting to create their own conflicts and intrigues.

                                                      Originally posted by Planguy View Post
                                                      Huh. Is this going to be like Orpheus in the old world of darkness? Like a game that is kind of like a miniseries with a really focused story line?
                                                      Like every TC game line, we'll have as many supplements as we think we can fund and get away with. Other game lines thus far had 20+ years of support from an existing fanbase, so they were comparatively an easy sell. Anima is new, so the degree of support will depend on how well it performs.

                                                      I mean according to the timeline in 2086 the Shanghai Accords probably results in Terra Surge and the Glass implant getting outlawed. I'm guessing the player characters of Anima end up taking the whole thing down?
                                                      I have previously strongly suggested that the events of Anima are what leads to the Shanghai Accords. How that plays out (or not) is up to you.

                                                      Seems like an interesting idea but fairly limited in scope. Not really the galaxy spanning adventure you can take part in Aeon.
                                                      Well, we already have a galaxy-spanning game, which is Æon. If that's what you want, it's already there. The whole point of having different genres for each game line in the Continuum is that we can do different things with them. A lot of cyberpunk games and settings are focused on the event of a single city. That doesn't mean that there aren't endless stories to tell. Blade Runner's LA, Altered Carbon's Bay City, Remember Me's Neo-Paris, the City of Mirror's Edge, Ghost in the Shell's Niihama.
