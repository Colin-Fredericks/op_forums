                                                        Gotta remember there is no Psi based detectors in the Anima book. Psion's don't appear till 20 years after Glass is outlawed by the Shanghai Accords. No Psion's or Biotech means that any Aberrant Detectors are either based on Nova-tech and as such not trusted (for the most part) or crude and inexact. We don't have a write up of a Aberrant detector yet but I would image what they are detecting is Quantum Flux rather than just Quantum. If it was that easy to detect Quantum in a human body Dark Star Nova's wouldn't be a thing. However that is just my opinion as there is no official word on it.

                                                        Quantum Flux diseases are caused by Quantum Flux, not just Quantum so equating the power of a thing to whether or not it causes a Quantum Flux diseases doesn't make sense. Aberrant powers cause Quantum Flux disease due to them having a huge amount of Quantum Flux that infects things they use their powers on. Normal Nova Powers have little to no Quantum Flux and as such don't cause any Quantum Flux disease at all, otherwise the colony on Eden would be riddled with Quantum Flux diseases due to the pervasive Nova Power use, and I don't think it's even mentioned.

                                                        Psi and Quantum don't play nice together. As the brain is a fairly delicate organ it would stand to reason that possession is not a brute force attack but rather a delicate processes that uses a small amount of Psi on a very tiny scale to take over a person. Therefore warping a brain in a weak Quantum field could disrupt a Psi based attempt to take it over, due to them interfering with each other.
