    The way you put it, it seems you expect the TC to be at odds with the setting that brought most of us to the table. While i'm open minded about a few healthy revisions/changes, what on earth could i actually discuss here if not for the information i already have access to. Is 2120 really going to change so much that previously accessible concepts may no longer be accessible? I certainly hope not, versatility is perhaps the greatest strength of the original 'three pillar' settings.
    I'm not saying it doesn't appeal, on the contrary, i've personally run a few 2120s tech based games with Psionics playing only a background role and the material supports that, if Anima is decent i may find myself entirely converted to this period/timeline (take that, Doyen). But to ensconce "more tech + less psionics" as a primary seperation of an alternate setting just seems to dilute the Sci-Fi potential of Aeon (as opposed to the comic potential of Aberrant and pulp potential of Adventure). I want the tech, but what is so momentous that it couldn't just be a supplement for Aeon? There are neutrals in 2120 and a group of all neutrals should still be an interesting scenario in any era, not just an alternate setting. The tech is just restricted, superscience in Aeon should be free to shatter those legal restrictions and face the consequences - so why seperate the two?
    Prior to OPP, the main unifying factor (apart from inspiration) for the series was its timeline, i understand that this is going to be more or less obsolete in the time-travel-ready TC. But until i see more 'structure of time' details should i just ignore sequential history as detailed in the original books?
    Again, don't get me wrong, i like the notion and i'm interested to hear more - but the distinctions aren't as clear as i'd like. I just don't want to see the setting diminish the possibilities of Aeon, by sequestering it's own slice of the sci-fi pie. I want to see Aeon reach it's potential, i like Anima, but not if it comes at the expense of era versatility.
    -shrug-
