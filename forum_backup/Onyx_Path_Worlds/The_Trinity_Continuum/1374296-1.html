                                                Re: Rifts

                                                I have a great fondness for the Rifts Setting. Palladium Games (mostly Rifts and TMNT) were the systems that got me into gaming. That being said, even back then I noticed quite a few ..balance issues...with the system. Converting the setting to other systems is something that comes up occasionally at my game table. This topic inspired me to start brainstorming some conversion ideas. Here is what I have thus far - I will post my ideas as they develop

                                                TC: Rifts

                                                My main resources for Rifts information for these conversions are the Rifts: Ultimate Edition, and Savage Worlds:Rifts (Game Master's Handbook and Tomorrow Legion Player's Guide)

                                                Tiers

                                                Tier 1: Mundane
                                                Tier 2: Augmented (Borgs, Cyber-Knight, Crazy, Juicer), D-Bee, MARS (Mercenaries, Adventurers, Rogues, Scholars), Mutant (Dog-Boy, Psi-Stalker), Psionics (Burster, Mind Melter), Practitioner of Magic (Line Walker, Mystic, Shifter, Techno-Wizard)
                                                Tier 3: Dragon Hatchling

                                                Paths

                                                Origins Path: Adventurer, City Rat (Street Rat), Citizen (surburbia), Frontiersman (Survivalist), Grunt (Military Brat), Privileged, Vagabond

                                                Role Path: Body Fixer (Medic), Charismatic Leader, Operator (Tech Expert), Robot Pilot (Pilot), Rogue (Sneak), Scholar (Detective), Scout (Sneak), Solider (Combat Specialist), Warrior (Combat Specialist)

                                                Society Path: Coalition States, Colorado Baronies, Federation of Magic, Free Quebec, Lazlo, Northern Gun, Pecos Empire, Tolkeen Refugee, Tomorrow Legion

                                                Power Armor & Robot Armor
                                                See VARGs (Æon Æxpansion, pg. 45-59)

                                                Scale
                                                Storypath Rifts uses Extreme Scale and the standardized scale qualifiers of Durability, Leadership, Might, Mental, Power, Size, and Speed. (TC: Aberrant, pg. XX)

                                                Size
                                                Storypath Rifts uses rules for Negative Size. (TC: Aberrant, pg. XX)

                                                Collateral Damage
                                                Storypath Rifts uses the Collateral Damage rules (TC: Aberrant, pg. XX) to represent the destructive capabilities of high yield (Mega-Damage) weapons, magic, and psionics.

                                                Templates
                                                Borg (Full Cyborg), Crazy, Cyber-Knight, D-Bee, Dog-Boy, Glitter Boy Pilot, Headhunter (Partial Cyborg), Juicer, MARS, Practitioner of Magic (Elementalist, Line Walker, Mystic, Shifter, Techno-Wizard), Psychic (Burster, Mind Melter), Psi-Stalker
                                                Last edited by Reighnhell; 03-15-2020, 11:15 AM. Reason: updated notes

                                                The Artificer's Workshop - A collection of Exalted Artifacts
                                                Curios, Relics, and Tomes - A collection of Relics (Cursed and Otherwise)
                                                The Horror Lab - A collection of Beasts, Monsters and less definable things.
                                                Strange Places - A collection of Dark, Mysterious, and Wondrous Locations
                                                Twilight Menagerie - A collection of Ephemeral Entities

                                                            Here is my quick skill conversion, open to feedback.

                                                            COMMUNICATIONS = ?
                                                            COWBOY = ?
                                                            DOMESTIC = Culture
                                                            ELECTRICAL = Technology
                                                            ESPIONAGE = Larceny
                                                            HORSEMANSHIP = ?
                                                            MECHANICAL = Technology
                                                            MEDICAL = Medical
                                                            MILITARY = Variety Review
                                                            PHYSICAL = Athletics or Close Combat
                                                            PILOT = Pilot
                                                            ROGUE = Larceny
                                                            SCIENCE = Science
                                                            TECHNICAL = Technology
                                                            Ancient Weapon Proficiencies =
                                                            * Atheletics for Thrown and Bows (my house rule)
