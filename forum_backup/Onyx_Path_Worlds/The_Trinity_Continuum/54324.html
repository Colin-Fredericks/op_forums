      In Aberrant 1e, the explosion of the space station Galatea in 1999 triggered the eruption of novas (the setting's term for superhumans) worldwide. The game's starting point is set 9 years after that, the novas' presence already having shaped societies considerably.

      (Well, that's what everyone thinks happened. The truth is, the Galatea explosion was a side-effect. A certain Doctor Primoris manipulated the fundamental forces of the universe to create more superhumans like himself; he's better known in this era as Divas Mal.)

      You're one of the novas, a celebrity, a superstar, someone with the potential to change the world. What are you going to do with that power?

      ---

      Canonically, over time, the novas became drunk on their power, grew corrupted, twisted, insane, renamed Aberrants. Eventually, things came to a head in the Aberrant War, humanity fighting those they had once called heroes. The war ended with the Aberrants departing for deep space, having been given the choice of exile or destruction of the planet.

      ---

      Aeon / Trinity is set in the 2120s. The world is recovering from the Aberrant War, some places better than others. Now, however... the Aberrants are back, to reclaim their homeworld.

      You're a psion, someone who's been gifted with extraordinary psychic abilities to stand against the threat of the Aberrants, and to help put the world back together.

      Want a sci-fi genre? The 2120s can provide. Cyberpunk? North America. After the end? Europe. Military sci-fi? Australia. Etc.

              The links between the games are loose, and optional depending on what kind of game you want to run.

              Some elements from Adventure! are "prequel" appearances for Aberrant and Trinity - Max Mercer and Michael Donighal / Dr. Primoris / Divis Mal are among the key figures of the setting, but Mal is the only one "on stage" in Aberrant.

              A lot of the weirder pulp action stuff in Adventure! fades out as the Z-waves go (so there are no lost cities or intelligent apes in Aberrant as written, for example) because each game is essentially a different genre or set of genres.

              2008 and 2120 are the canon future of the setting, but not necessarily the only one. After all, Max is a time traveller...

              Craig Oxbrow
              The Trinity Continuum freelancer

                      Originally posted by BrilliantRain View Post
                      What's actually the difference between an Aberrant and a Psion? Do Psions get get the manipulate the mind type mentalism powers, while Aberrants get more physical transformations and direct manipulation of the world?
                      Short answer: They manipulate different forces but can accomplish many of the same effects in those ways. Psions manipulate psi, and Novas/Aberrants manipulate quantum.

                      Long answer: Both have ways of interacting with the world, but Nova/Aberrant powers do tend to be a lot more direct with a good deal of sheer bang for one's buck, power-wise. They operate at something of a cost, as Novas/Aberrants are prone to mutations and insanity as their power grows and they channel more and more of it. Psions, however, seem to be more common but are generally more low-key in terms of raw strength but their abilities come at at much lower risk to the user. Novas/Aberrants can put out a lot more raw power, actually transforming energy and physical matter at the molecular level and what have you, but Psions have much more subtle advantages. A Psion clairvoyant or teleporter, for instance, is limited more by the ability to conceptualize the target than by range. Psions also have access to biotech, which is more compatible with their bodies and abilities than it is with Novas/Aberrants (I believe Aberrants can't use biotech at all, as their bodies are attuned to quantum energies rather than psionic ones). The mechanism for this is never really explained, but when you get psi and quantum energies in the lower, more balanced levels seen in Adventure! you get the Z-wave effect but if things lean too much in one direction they start contrasting and interfering with each other.

                      To put it in Adventure! terms, Stalwarts are the precursors to the Novas eventually activated in the late 90's. Mesmerists are precursors to the Psions. Both types are capable of a lot of overlap in terms of basic abilities (which is why there is a common house rule for Adventure! that Stalwarts and Mesmerists can purchase each others' Knacks as if they were a level higher).

                      Christopher Shaffer, freelancer. Game line contributions: W20, Night Horrors, TC: Æon, Scion (details here)

                              A Not-So Scientific Treatise on the Comparisons of Daredevils/Mesmerists/Stalwarts, Novas/Aberrants, and Psiads/Psions

                              (TL;DR toward the bottom, since this turned out to be very long)

                              So, I’ve noticed that some people occasionally have difficulty understanding the difference between the “lesser” Inspired, Novas/Aberrants, and Psiads/Psions. This is totally understandable, since the VAST majority of information provided on the nature of the Inspired is provided in the context of in-world and in-character discoveries and knowledge -- and the inhabitants of the Aeonverse don’t understand Inspiration that much!

                              To try to remedy this dearth of data, I’m going to give a few analogies that I’ve occasionally used to help my players understand the similarities and differences between people who use Telluric, Quantum, and Noetic energies. Like any analogy, these aren’t PERFECT (they get muddy if you try to think about how a Mesmerist summons up fire or ice, things like that), but they do a fairly good job on the whole of it.

                              For the first analogy, let’s imagine a very basic problem: there’s a big-*** boulder that needs to be moved from Point A to Point B. The distance between the two points is arbitrary (far enough that it would cause problems) and the exact weight/mass of the boulder is arbitrary (large enough that it would cause problems). Under normal circumstances, an average human being would find that this boulder is far too large to move without mechanical aid -- they’d need to bring in construction equipment or the like to move it. Now, in addition to our normal human (or “baseline”, if you will), let’s imagine five additional people: a Daredevil/Talent, a Mesmerist, a Stalwart, a Nova/Aberrant, and a Psiad/Psion. These five people each have some trick which allows them to move the boulder without relying on heavy machinery. Egads! How is this possible?

                              If our normal human were to observe these five individuals, all five appear to do identical things: they walk up to the boulder, push on it with their arms, and the boulder moves. From an objective, universal Point Of View, however, these people are doing VERY different things. Let’s begin with the analogy proper!

                              Imagine, if you will, that the universe is a computer -- identical in many respects to the desktop on which you are probably browsing this forum. We can roughly divide this universal computer into three parts:

                              --- the INPUT (the keyboard, mouse, joystick, trackpad, Wacom, Leap Motion, whathaveyou) which accepts input and data for processing and manipulation; for our universe, this would represent any actions a person performs or any natural event which occurs
                              --- the OPERATING SYSTEM (Windows, Mac OS, Linux, any specific programs you’re using) which tell the computer how to interpret and manipulate data; for our universe, this would be Newtonian Physics, Quantum Mechanics, and other Laws of Nature, which determine how things interact and how Cause leads to Effect
                              --- the OUTPUT (monitor, speakers, etc) which takes the processed input and data, and displays the results; for our universe, this would be anything which we observe as having resulted from an action or event

                              In this analogy, the act of starting a fire would be as follows: a heat source is the INPUT, the OPERATING SYSTEM (Laws of Nature) say that the energy in the pile of wood is now high enough to spontaneously combust with oxygen, and the OUTPUT is a now-burning pile of wood.

                              Let’s return to our five Inspired individuals (and our single confused and probably awed normal human). Despite the fact that they all managed the same feat, they arrived at it in vastly different manners. Breaking them down one-by-one, we can see how each of them manipulate the universe to pull off superhuman feats. Daredevils/Talents, Mesmerists, and Stalwarts all have abilities which increase their capabilities (mechanically, this is usually represented by increase the number of dice or successes for certain tasks). Novas/Aberrants and Psiads/Psions have more obviously supernatural powers.

                              --- The Daredevil/Talent has an incredible amount of training and luck, granting him better chances and skill at moving the rock (with Ability Mastery: Might and a point of Inspiration to double his dice pool, he gets radically more successes than our normal human)
                              --- The Mesmerist has an almost mind-over-matter control of his body’s natural processes, and can grant himself hysterical strength (with the right Knack, he might be able to add Willpower to feats of raw strength for a short while if he pushes himself)
                              --- The Stalwart is a quintessential strong-man, and his muscles are powerful enough to put The Governator to shame (with the right Knack, he could add his Destructive Facet to any feats of strength)

                              Looking at these three, they all have a similar theme. Can you notice it? In order to perform a feat that most humans couldn’t manage, they all manipulated some aspect of the universe -- for these “lesser” Inspired, they lack the raw power to change most things, but there is one thing they can manipulate: the INPUT. They can all change the raw INPUT into the universe before that information/cause/effort is processed (represented by the fact that Telluric Energy is pretty good at adding dice to their dice pools for actions). A higher INPUT (effort) means that the OPERATING SYSTEM (universe) spits out an improved OUTPUT (result), and the boulder moves. In order to get the right OUTPUT on the “screen” of the universe, they worked harder to put in more INPUT through the keyboard and mouse, giving the OPERATING SYSTEM more to work with.

                              How about the Psiad/Psion?

                              --- The Psiad/Psion has a significant leg-up from our previous Inspired; they can manipulate Noetic Energy. Using Noetic Energy, our Psiad/Psion changes how the boulder’s matter interacts with the universe via the Higgs mechanism; in doing so, the gravitational and inertial mass of the boulder is changed, making it weigh less, which makes it easy enough for our Psiad/Psion’s normal muscles to move it.

                              Now we’ve got something different on our hands. In this instance, we can see that the Psiad/Psion is not really that much stronger than most people, but they still moved the boulder. They didn’t make themselves stronger, but they didn’t actually make the boulder lighter -- they didn’t change the informational content of the boulder; instead, they changed how the universe INTERPRETED the informational content of the boulder. They used Noetic Energy to affect the OPERATING SYSTEM of the universe, temporarily changing how physics and the Laws of Nature worked. They kept the INPUT the same, but changed how the OPERATING SYSTEM used that INPUT in order to affect the OUTPUT indirectly. Obviously, this gives Psiads/Psions a great benefit; they can perform an enormous variety of actions that would be beyond the reach of even the most capable human, by stretching and pulling how the universe operates, allowing the impossible to temporarily become possible. In order to get the right OUTPUT on the “screen” of the universe, they did not change how much INPUT they gave through the keyboard or mouse, but instead changed how the OPERATING SYSTEM used what little they could give it.

                              Finally, let’s take a look at the Nova/Aberrant.

                              --- The Nova/Aberrant can manipulate Quantum Energy. Using Quantum Energy, our Nova/Aberrant does not increase their muscle mass, or supplement their training, or alter the boulder’s mass. Instead, they push the massive boulder with (more-or-less) normal human muscles, and the boulder just MOVES.

                              What the frack? How did that work? Our particular Nova/Aberrant doesn’t have superdense muscles or Olympian training, so they’re technically just as strong as our normal human. They didn’t change the mass of the boulder, either, so they’re pushing with normal human muscles on a (literally) massive boulder. But it still moved! Basic kinematics (the OPERATING SYSTEM) tells us that shouldn’t have happened with such relatively little force (the INPUT). It worked, because our Nova/Aberrant’s manipulation of Quantum Energies simply MADE IT MOVE -- they changed the OUTPUT directly, without manipulating either the INPUT or the OPERATING SYSTEM. Novas break the rules -- really, they do. They need not concern themselves with what they themselves are capable of doing (the INPUT) or what the universe is normally capable of doing (the OPERATING SYSTEM). In order to get the right OUTPUT on the “screen” of the universe, they just straight-up ignored their regular INPUT through the keyboard and mouse, and the OPERATING SYSTEM of the computer -- all they do is will the right pixels to light up and vibrations for the speakers, and they make whatever they want happen. Terrifying, really, when you think about it.

                              (TL;DR begins here) For a much more simplified version of this, we can just boil all of that down to a mathematical equation: it has an input/variable (the effort you put into an action), a constant (the physical laws of the universe), and the output/result of the equation (what you see as the result of your actions).

                              For simplicity’s sake, let’s say it is (2)*X = Y for our purposes. For whatever superhuman feat you need to perform, Y MUST equal 8, for completely arbitrary purposes. To do whatever it is you want to do, you need to put in 4 units of… whatever. Less than 4, nothing happens -- you don’t measure up to what the universe says is necessary. Normally, however, a human being is only capable of putting in 2 units of whatever. Uh-oh! How do our Inspired examples fix this?

                              Our Daredevil and his friends use Telluric Energy to manipulate the input they can give -- normally, the most a human can make X equal is 2, but the Daredevil can get a bit more oomph out of himself, and he can make X equal 4. Plug this in, and we get the following:

                              (2)*4 = 8

                              Fantastic! It works.

                              Our Psiad/Psion can’t do more than the average human -- their X can only be as high as 2 -- but they CAN use Noetic Energy to change the universal constants involved, so they change the constant from (2) to (4). Plug this in, and we get the following:

                              (4)*2 = 8

                              Wunderbar! We can still make it work.

                              Now, the Nova/Aberrant. They don’t put in more effort (X = 2), and they don’t change the universal constants [(2) will still equal (2)], but they can directly manipulate the output Y and simply overwrite what the universe SAYS it should be with what they WANT it to be. So, we get the following:

                              (2)*2 = 8

                              This makes absolutely no sense, which is exactly why Novas and Aberrants terrify people in the Aeonverse -- they can do, literally, ANYTHING THEY WANT. They are in no shape or form inhibited by human limitations or universal constants. Law of Conservation of Energy? Only for wusses. Speed of Light? More of a suggestion, really.

                              So. That was much longer than I planned. I hope that makes sense.

                                  I suppose I should also point out that, according to many sources, "Telluric Energy" isn't actually a real energy at all; Mesmerists and Stalwarts don't both manipulate "Telluric Energy". Instead, it is suggested that Mesmerists manipulate low-level Noetic Energy, and Stalwarts manipulate low-level Quantum Energy. In my game, I noted the the problems that this could cause -- namely, there are instances in which the energies manipulated by both Mesmerists and Stalwarts could be used to the same goal, such as having a person of each type of Inspiration fueling the same Telluric Battery. If they really were combining both Noetic and Quantum Energies, they should be canceling each other out (or otherwise reaction/neutralizing) instead of compounding their effects.

                                  To that end, I use the interpretation which states that Telluric Energy is distinct from Noetic and Quantum Energies, and it is the same energy used by Mesmerists, Stalwarts, and (passively) by Daredevils.
