                                                        Honestly, the scale system kept the low quantum novas pretty weak, compared to the old Mega Atributes and unlimited dots in the powers once the quantum minimum is reached.

                                                        For example, Mega Speed 1 make you capable a hold the speed of an athlete for an entire marathon, probably finishing it in under an hour, it's on pulp range. Mega Might allow you to lift 3 or 4 times the usual limit of your strength, its far from a ton unless you are a professional weight lifting athlete, very pulp too.

                                                        Another thing that I must bring is the fact that Talents and Psions can work together with no issue in Aeon, and its possible to add a talent on Aberrant and still don't miss much of the game. Unless you are planning to add Dramatic Edition to the Stalwart and Mesmerist reach (what don't sound interesting from the "each kind have their own powers") you don't need to remove any of the Q2 powers (maybe shrink, this power is too effective for a low quantum character) to keep it Pulp-ish.

                                                        A few Dynamic Knacks that were not very Pulp-ish in Adventure and still are there (not even touching the Psychic power): Single Bound (lvl 1: 3 times distance and hight jump), Man of all seasons (lvl 1: -30 to 150 C without risks), Blazing Speed (2: entire extra action in the round), Body of Bronze (3: you turn into metal), Man of Many Faces (3: you can change your aparente), Reptilian Regeneration (3: recover lost body parts).

                                                        My players, when we anted something really pulp, used to use only Deredevils, because Mesmerists and Stalwarts were a bit out of the genre we desired.

                                                        Well, that's my opinion, I always wanted to make a cross from Adventure to Aberrant without changing character sheets, but it was never possible. I hope they keep the possibilities.
