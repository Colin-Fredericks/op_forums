                                                        Psi 6 is a limitation for psiads because humanity isn’t particularly psionically ‘refined’ at the moment, with only a couple of centuries of psionic evolution. Psi 7 comes from thousands of years of evolution and practice in a species using their powers. Or, you know, from a race millions of years older and more advanced genetically engineering your species to be able to reach those higher limits.

                                                        Though it may not look it, because you have psions to compare to, being able to naturally reach Psi 6 is pretty impressive on the psionic creatures evolutionary scale. Qin and Chromatics can’t do it - or have the breadth of Aptitudes that humanity has - and they’ve been evolving Psi for thousands of years.

                                                        Getting to Psi 7 (and beyond) is something for humanity to grow into over the rest of its evolution. Or for them to use their ingenuity as they normally do and reverse engineer those biotech pods the aliens gave them and figure out how to make some that enhance all Aptitudes and give higher power boosts.

                                                        These things are story hooks and campaign goals. There’s both in-universe and out-of-universe reasons for different character types to have different limitations and challenges.
