                                Originally posted by wyrdhamster View Post
                                I think we got out of topic main idea - How to make TC Modern -> Anima -> Aeon setting transitions without Aberrant era in it?
                                There have been some good answers already for the middle part, but I think I've got a good explanation for the final part.

                                Even without an Aberrant era, the Hammersmith event and Adventure! era would have happened. That means the Doyen still show up to stick Earth in a power dampening bubble. Have a Doyen investigator check up on the world in the aftermath of the Anima era and get ambushed by Dr. Bhurano and a small team of prepared psiads. If Divis Mal and Max Mercer knew about the Doyen, there is no reason she couldn't have found out about their existence as well.

                                Incapacitating attacks and psi-tech restraining techniques coupled with telepathic interrogation would reveal that humanity is slated for eventual extermination due to our capacity to produce stalwarts. Digging for technical knowledge could reveal intel on how the Doyen once artificially empowered themselves, and this info could be used to similarly enhance humans, although limitations in the experimental technology would mean each prototype device would only empower one Aptitude, while mostly burning out the subject's capacity to develop other Aptitudes.

                                Humanity is now prepping for a space war with the Doyen and the alien species under their control. This gives you Aeon with no Aberrant War.
