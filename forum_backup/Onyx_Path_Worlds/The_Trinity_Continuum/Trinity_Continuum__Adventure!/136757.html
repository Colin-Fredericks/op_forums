                                              05-15-2014, 02:51 PM
                                              The botch -> failure, failure -> success thing could work, but there aren't really hard and fast grades of success in the system Adventure! uses. For example, in NWoD, getting between 1 and 4 success is rated a "success", and getting 5 or more is an "exceptional success". That system doesn't exist for Adventure!. The Storyteller is encouraged to have better results for more successes, but that's mostly a storytelling element and not a mechanical one.

                                              Also, keep in mind that in Adventure!, 10s do not explode all the time. That's a bonus given for the right circumstances; usually, one does not receive any exploding dice. When you have 4 or more dots in an Attribute, the character picks up a quality associated with that Attribute (essentially an adjective, or a one- or two-word phrase describing what that character specializes in). For example, I'd have a character with Dexterity 4 (Quick Reflexes). If I normally roll a Dexterity-based dice pool, I do not reroll 10s -- but if that Dexterity-based roll is the result of an action where having "quick reflexes" is a good bonus, then I am allowed to reroll those 10s.
