    What do you think of these ideas for Aberrant?

    10-31-2019, 09:35 PM
    1. Replacing the character of Alejandra from the original book with Ariana Grande. Other than the fact that Alejandra was really virginal and Ariana isn’t, they have a lot of similarities.

    2. Creating a clandestine romance between Slider and Andre Corbin. I know the original book says they were just friends, but you don’t go to the extraordinary lengths Corbin did for someone who’s “just a friend.” And they would probably keep it a secret because she’s in Team Tomorrow and he’s in Team Tonight (plus they have the whole good girl-bad boy thing going on).

    I don’t think either of these ideas directly contradicts anything in the new Aberrant book. What do you guys think?

                                              Originally posted by Penelope View Post
                                              True...all but one are guys so they might not really care if Corbin and Slider are hot...
                                              As a straight white male, I'll tell you right now that Beyoncé and Jay Z are hot. So is Chadwick Boseman. If Beyonce dumped Jay Z and got together with Boseman (or Jay Z dumped her and got with Boseman), however titillating it might be in terms of gossip, etc, it would be absolutely irrelevant to my life. The only way it becomes relevant to my life is if Beyoncé dumps them both to start dating me.

                                              Right now the love lives of Corbin and Slider seem to be background gossip. Your players are not going to care about the love/sex lives of your celebrity NPCs. Unless it matters to them. If one of your PCs is the "official love interest" to Slider and stands to lose money or be embarrassed when Slider and Corbin get caught fooling around, it will matter. If another PC has Corbin (or Slider) as one of his Path Contacts and Corbin/Slider needs a "huge favor" it will again matter. Or, hell, one or both of your players are playing Slider and/or Corbin as their characters. Then it will damn sure matter to them.

                                              Players care about the stuff they're involved with. If "Nova Tony Stark" develops some cool bit of Q-Tech, big whoop. If Nova Tony Stark hires the characters to guard that piece of Q-Tech from theft and/or retrieve it when it eventually gets stolen (or "Nova Justin Hammer" hires them to swipe it), then it matters to them because it's part of their story.
