            Inspired Psions
            Psions are artificial Mesmerists, created to fight against the powerful Aberrants, their Gifts is limited to certain focus, yet they are more powerful, exchanging versatility for potency.
            Psions have access to psychic gifts of a single Aptitude, defined by the chamber turned them into Psions, Biokinesis, Clairsentience, Electrokinesis, Psychokinesis, Quantakinesis, Telepathy, Teleportation and Vitakinesis. Each Aptitude has a list of possible gifts divided in 3 “tiers”, Auxiliary, Standard and Advanced, all Psions consider their Aptitude Gifts as Path Gifts, and can learn and reach Gifts of all 3 tiers, as long all prerequisites are met. They can also learn Auxiliary tier gifts of any other aptitude, not treated as Path Gifts.
            Psions, as well as Psiads, can combine their powers by touching each other and all assisting the primary Psion spending Inspiration and rolling Inspiration, the successes are transferred to the primary Psion roll. It happens Psions are far superior at doing so, granting advantages related to their aptitudes when combining their powers:
            Biokinesis: Double the number of successes the primary psion has available to spend, after accounting for Difficulty or Complications.
            Clairsentience: Gifts can be used at Inspiration range bands from the clairsentient. In addition, gifts that can reach beyond that, have a maximum range of Inspiration x 10 light years.
            Electrokinesis: Multiply the duration of the gifts’s effect by the electrokinetic’s Inspiration trait. If the effect normally lasts an instant — such as an attack — the effect can be used again the next round without additional cost.
            Psychokinesis: Add the psychokinetic’s Inspiration trait as Enhancement to powers used on objects and devices.
            Quantakinesis: Double the primary psion’s facets when calculating dice pools and effects.
            Telepathy: Allow the primary psion to affect a number of targets equal to the telepath’s Inspiration with each use of a gift that can normally only affect a single target other than the psion.
            Teleportation: Increase the power’s area of effect by one range band. Gifts without an area of effect gain one equal to short.
            Vitakinesis: Add the vitakinetic’s Inspiration trait as Enhancement to powers on living creatures.
            When a Psion achieve Inspiration 6, her psychic powers expand beyond the levels of regular Psions, and she become capable of learning Psychic Excellences, much similar to the Excellences of Talents, but focused in their Aptitudes instead of Skills. Once learned, Psychic Excellence reduce the target number of any dice pool to 7 (from 8). When the Psion reach 5 dots in a facet, the effects of Excellence improve, granting Aptitude, Intention and Luck effects to it. The effects stack, and a Inspiration 10 Psion gains the 3 effects at same time in all uses of their gifts.
            Aptitude: at Reflective Facet 5 the Psion can use any Gift of the Aptitude spending 1 less Inspiration.
            Intention: at Destructive Facet 5 the Psion add 1 Scale to all the Aptitude Gifts effects.
            Luck: at Intuitive Facet 5 the Psion gains 9-again to all the rolls using the skill.

            Inspired Nova
            Novas are much similar to Stalwarts, but they are more powerful, far more powerful.
            All Novas have a unconscious control over reality, making them more capable than expected, reducing the target number of all dice pool to 7 (from 8).
            Novas have access to dynamic gifts, similarly to Stalwarts, but they can reach higher limits of power, not available to regular Stalwarts. Additionally, most Novas eruptions take them to levels of power far above the usual for Stalwarts, granting a higher number of gifts and possibly facet dots, but that values should be defined by the Storyguide, as it may unbalance the game if other templates are available.
            Nova powers can be far more powerful than Stalwart ones, as they learn Dynamic Excellences, much similar to the Excellences of Talents, but focused in Dynamic Gifts with specific Attribute keywords or the Power key word instead of Skills. Once learned, Dynamic Excellence increases to the effect of all gifts with the proper keyword, granting half of the related Facet as scale, always rounding up. Force attributes are related to Destructive Facet, Finesse attributes to Intuitive Facet and Resilience attributes to Reflective facet, while Power gifts always use the lowest of the 3 facets.
            When a Nova achieve Inspiration 6, their power increases exponentially, all effects based in Facet start to use Facet +1, including the effects of Dynamic Excellences. Each dot of Inspiration from that point on increase the effects of Facets by 1, up to Facet +5 at Inspiration 10.
            Novas have one additional trait, Flux, that measures overuse of their powers, going from 0 to 10.
            Whenever the Nova would receive the Telluric Burnout condition, the Nova instead gains 1 point of Flux.
            If a Nova spends an entire scene interacting with humans without using any of their powers, the Nova lose 1 Flux.
            If the Flux ever reach 10, the Nova lose all Quantum Flux and gains a Transformation, that change the Nova’s appearance, physiology or psychology, making the Nova feel less human To them selves or the others.

            A few comments:
            -Need to work in a list of Gifts per Aptitude and Tier
            -Need to create the advanced gifts of each aptitude (read 4-5 mode dots)
            -Need to create the quantakinesis gifts (there are too few of them)
            -House Rules: Inspiration will be an Adventure! CC, so it may take some time…

                ​​​​​Biological Gifts
                My initial thought was that Biological Gifts should get subdivided between Biokinesis and Vitakinesis. But far too many of them as written tend to have more in common with Clairsentience or Telepathy; so I ended up dividing these more ways:

                Biokinesis:
                Adaptation: Darwin's Razor
                Psychomorphing: Density, Made of Rubber, Mind Over Matter, Reflex Booster, Stiff as a Board, Supreme Flexibility, Telluric Enhancement, Traversing the Impassable, Treading Death's Path, Walking on the Water
                Transmogrify: Face of Clay

                Vitakinesis:
                Iatrosis: Ascetic Trance, Command the Many Meridians, Push the Pain Away, Read the Wounds, Touch of Life
                Augendis: Human Battery
                Algesis: Pain Amplification, Phantom Pain, Slow, Stop

                Clairsentience:
                Psychometry: Dead Minds Tell Some Tales
                Psycholocation: Find the Weak Link, Over Your Shoulder, Sympathetic Link, Unmoored Perceptions
                Psychocognition: Counter-Gambit, Three Moves Ahead

                Telepathy:
                Rapport: All Creatures Great and Small, Check!, Keep Your Friends Close
                Psychbending: Brain Jamming, Over There!, Refuge in Banality

                Quantakinesis:
                Fundamentals:Telluric Resonance

                Mindshare: Direct Voice, Dream Diver, Dream Transmission, Hive Mind, Mental Crucible, Oneiric Network, Palace of the Mind, Sense Minds, Thoughtography, Veil of the Unseen

                Telekinesis: Adrift in Etheric Currents, Awash in the Etheric, Fist of the Mind, Halt Flight, Intensifying Grip, Javelin of Will, Levitation, Manipulation Without Touch, Mental Burst, Mindhammer, Psychic Accuracy, Psychic Hand, Seize the Puppet Strings, Strength of the Mind, Teledigitation, Telekinetic Summoning
                Perikinesis: Force Cage, Psychic Conjuration

                Telepathy:

                Psychbending: Astral Projection, Astral Transference, Brainstorm, Enervating Grasp, Pareidola's Price, Stunning Pulse

                Teleport:
                Translocation: Vanishing Act
                Transmassion: Sleight of Will
                Transportal: Doorway

                Quantakinesis:
                Fundamentals: Power Transfer

                ​​​​​​Clairsentience:
                Psycholocation: Telepresent Relay, Tremor Sense

                Conclusions
                Just about every Mode of every Aptitude has representation here. The exceptions are EK's Electromanipulation and Quantakinesis's Energetics and Transmutation. I would put any new Gifts based on these Modes under the Energetic Keyword: Electromanipulation and Energetics for obvious reasons, Transmutation because it's the least bad choice. Given that the Teleportation Gifts have the Energetic keyword, that's not much of a stretch at all.

                Other areas where Adventure's Gifts are particularly weak are Quantakinesis's Fundamentals and PK's Perikinesis.

                                Quick update on my ideas for Novas as empowered Stalwarts.

                                Stalwarts have basically 2 different kinds of Gifts, Attribute Gifts and Power Gifts.
                                Novas have access to the same gifts, but they can have some of those gifts more than once.
                                - Most Attribute Gifts can only be taken once, except Scion of Humanity (see bellow)
                                - Most Power Gifts can only be taken once, except a few (see bellow).

                                Scion of Humanity can be taken multiple times, no more than 1 per dot in the related Facet, split the followin way:
                                - Might: once per dot in Destructive Facet
                                - Cunning or Dexterity: once per dot in Intuitive Facet
                                - Composure or Intelect: once per in Reflective Facet
                                - Presence or Manipulation: once per dot in Intuitive Facet
                                - Stamina or Resolve: once per dot in Reflective Facet
                                So if you have Intuitive 2, you can pick Scion of Humanity twice for Dexterity or twice Cunning or once for Dexterity and once for Cunning.

                                Power Gifts that has no prerequisites in other gifts can be taken a second time onde the character has raised all facets to 2 or more, and again when all facets are 3 or more and so on.
                                The number of times the gift is taken defines the scale of the effect of the chosen gift and all the related other power gifts (gifts with prerequisete in the gift or prerequisite in the gifts with prerequeisite in that gift and so on).
                                So if you have a Gift that let you make your hand hot, and pick it twice, the heat scale of your hand will be 2, so cause damage scale 2. If that gift let you pick a gift that let you shoot fire from your hands, that fire will have power scale 2, and if a third gift (with prerequisite in the second gift) let you fly with the fire jets you throw in the ground, the flight speed will be scale 2.

                                That put your nova on the 1-5 scale, but Novas can go beyond.
                                When a Nova reach Inspiration 6, all facets are treated as 1 higher than original to define how many times the gifts can be taken.
                                At Inspiration 7 it increase by 1 (to +2), Inspiration 8 by 1 again (+3) and so on until Inspiration 10 that increase all to 10 (5 from the facet +5)
                                So lets say a Nova has facets D0 I3 R4 (Inspiration 5), if he increase Reflective Facet to 5 the Inspiration is 6. and so, to define the number of time the Nova can take the relates gifts is as if the facets were D1 I4 R6.
