                                    Subsequent to the Iprelia debacle, Radoslav and Wolfgang have been transferred to another unit led by Sir Steven Montague under Blake Carter (the UK representative to ESPER). The team members (less Radoslav and Wolfgang) is as follows:

                                    Sir Steven Montague: an English nobleman with extensive political influence, resources independent of ESPER and the ability to teleport. Walks with a cane and missing an eye due to an altercation with a serial killer in his past, which motivated him to join ESPER to try to use his abilities, nova and otherwise, to help Mother England. He is the leader of the team. It is a minor point of pride that he has resisted the physical and mental warping that novas tend to be prone to.

                                    Elizabeth Ravenhurst (Morgan): the daughter of a wealthy English baron, little is known about Elizabeth (or Morgan, as she prefers to be called) apart from her strong interest in the occult and her ability to summon demon-like entities and fly.

                                    Edouard Rolin (Eclair): a Frenchman with an unclear connection to the French military. With the ability to turn invisible, paired with his incredible running speed and knowledge of computers, Eouard is an exceptional scout and reconnaissance expert.

                                    For those playing the home game, Sir Montague is played by Hyde's player, and Morgan is played by Zoya's player. Edouard is a player new to this group and game, but I've played with him before.


                                    Rather than the traditional meeting, Morgan elected to greet her new team-mates with a lavish and well-attended party, which for reasons that only make sense to her opened with an invisible Edouard jumping out of a cake wearing a non-invisible bikini. Odd opening aside, the party went well, Radoslav putting people off and Wolfgangs compulsive flirting with every woman who he spoke to (disregarding marital status or spousal presence). Morgan and Sir Montague spent much of the time playing damage control for these actions.

                                    ESPER has been suffering serious consequences after being discovered interfering with Iprelia, with an estimated €750 million demanded in reparations. While this is an extraordinarily strong negative sign as to ESPER's long-term viability (and thus placing strain on the tenuous peace it represents between the historically conflict-ridden member states), the European Parliament has elected to maintain it, given its sheer promise of a hundred-odd novas working for the European public good, as well as the possible negative consequences should it fail.

                                    Aguda, for his part, has been working these events to maximum advantage. He has been airing the video on Iprelia public television regularly, as well as continuing his denouncements of foreign interference. All visas or official permissions for foreigners to be in Iprelia have been revoked, and any foreigner found within the borders of the country without official permission are imprisoned.

                                    Always desirous of a challenge, as well as concerned about the impact this has on England, Sir Montague has managed the in-itself impressive task of convincing his superiors to attempt to rectify the situation through discrediting Aguda. For ESPER, this represents a high-risk, high-reward venture: if it works, it will more than restore any lost confidence in its abilities from Parliament, as well as accomplishing the original mission. It is a testament to Sir Montague's influence and diplomatic skill that they agreed to it.

                                    Sir Montague's plane took off, with Radoslav, Morgan and Wolfgang getting off prior to landing, with instructions to find a local group of rebels, deliver weapons, and encourage them to start making strikes more strategic than nabbing the odd goat from outlying villages. With Radoslav's and Wolfgang's military background, it did not take long before they located one of the rebels hideouts in some hills in the western part of the country. With their usual tact, Radoslav walked directly into the base and invited the armed gunmen to shoot him, trusting in his armour and enhanced toughness to take the hit. The rebels, awed at the sight of this power, eagerly agreed to his plan to strike at a nearby fueling station for patrolling Jeeps, particularly with their increased arms.

                                    In the meantime, Sir Montague's plane was met with an armed response, with the soldiers demanding to either see official permission, to take them into custody, or for the foreigners to leave. Through an amazing display of sheer charisma, Sir Montague convinced the commanding officer to deliver him and his 'honour guard' (he had recruited some mercenaries prior to leaving Berlin) to the Presidential Palace. While his men were forced to wait outside, Sir Montague continued his patented 'act like you're supposed to be there' school of diplomacy and managed to meet Aguda and Attah, with proposals with trade agreement in Attah's office.

                                    The negotiations, as they were, did not go well. Montague presented two trade agreements, good for the next six months, which Aguda rejected after only glancing at them. Aguda made it clear that he did not respond well to either bribery attempts or fancy political speech, bluntly declaring that he had no need for such an agreement and anticipated his bargaining position to strengthen yet further when other countries rallied to his cause of unity against foreign exploitation, and that he intended to use the reparations money to fund his plans of a unified Africa.

                                    Sir Montague left the agreements with Aguda 'for your perusal' and retired to the quarters provided to him in the palace.

                                    During this time, Edouard/Eclair scouted the palace and particularly the files in Aguda's computer and hard-copies. Being written almost entirely in Afrikaans, he was unable to make sense of them, but he made copies of the computer files and photographed what he could of the hard files.

                                    The teams plans remain unclear, as does the extent of their comprehension of the potential consequences should they fail...

                                                Settle in folks, this is a big 'un. Before I start, the players were in the drivers seat the whole time, so all credit goes directly to them. I was just trying to keep up.

                                                After delivering the trade proposals to Aguda, Sir Montague was 'escorted' back to his 'quarters'. A couple of hours later, General Attah, Aguda's right-hand-man, arrived to try to work out an approach to handle the situation. While Attah had done his best to direct Aguda's extreme tendencies, Aguda's xenophobia and extremism had him concerned about the long-term viability of Aguda as a leader. While Aguda is clever in his way, and his end goals are noble, he was a product of his often brutal environment, and he is often enraged by the exploitation and callousness by foreign interests with regard to his people. Sir Montague's approach, attempting to apply a band-aid to the problem, backed up with fancy speech and patronising, was quite frankly the worst possible approach; dismissing Aguda's concerns as being purely financial.

                                                This discussion turned into an all-night talk, during which Sir Montague and Attah bonded somewhat.

                                                Meanwhile, Radoslav and Wolfgang, along with the rebels attacked a patrol of soldiers and seized two Jeeps, quickly followed by a raid on a nearby fueling station. After this, Wolfgang instructed a rebel to broadcast a message over the radio there - which included, not to Wolfgangs knowledge due to the language barrier, bragging of their powerful leaders.

                                                When word of these attacks reached the city, Aguda called another press conference, where he made two announcements: the first was the formation of a North African Union, intended to expel foreign exploitation and influence and allowing the countries to take their place as powerful, respected countries in their own right. The second was a challenge to all of the rebel groups in the area to a showdown: to strike at Aguda rather than cowardly at his people, he would be meeting in three days at a location outside of the city. If they were to show up, he would kill them. If they do not, they would be exposed as craven, only attacking those who cannot defend themselves.

                                                During the intervening three days, Wolfgang scouted the announced location of the showdown, and noticed that they were building an improvised bunker (insofar as one can), presumably for the press to view the fights from. For reasons only clear to Wolfgang, he killed the builders and destroyed what progress had been made. After the bunker had been constructed the second time, he proceeded to mine the area with remote-controlled explosives, in order to kill the press after the showdown.

                                                During this time, Sir Montague and Attah grew closer over attempting to come up with an acceptable proposal.

                                                The day arrived. Aguda arrived at the designated area roughly half an hour before sunrise, with the press, Sir Montague and Attah with him, setting up in the bunker. Somehow a sweep failed to show up the explosives; Wolfgang had hidden them too well.

                                                At sunrise, Aguda uttered his challenge again, and the rebels attacked emboldened by their leaders. Aguda was not impressed, and killed three with a single blast of fire from his hand (roughly a fifth their number). This was followed by two rocket blasts from Wolfgang and Radoslav, which were no more effective than the rebel guns. Radoslav then began to rain fire upon the area, following it up with fire from his assault rifle, while Wolfgang attempted to simply wear down Aguda through sheer volume of weapon fire. Neither were effective at accomplishing much more than irritating Aguda. So far, it seemed the battle to be at a stalemate.

                                                The tide turned when Morgan, the soft-spoken British noblewoman, revealed unknown destructive capabilities, channelling mystical energy to tear Aguda apart, while using her ability to alter her body's density to move through the Earth so as to avoid retaliatory attacks. Between her ability to avoid being targeted, as well as Wolfgang and Radoslav's distraction, Aguda slowly but surely began to wear down.

                                                With increasing desperation, Aguda attempted to physically attack Radoslav, but between Radoslav's inherent toughness and the wreath of flame around him somehow absorbing the brunt of Agudas blows, Aguda was unable to significantly hurt him.

                                                Meanwhile, Sir Montague began to suggest to Attah that the attacking novas were from the US. Between the distance, dust and flames, getting a clear look at the attackers was near-impossible, and Sir Montague's influence, coupled with Attah's heightened emotional state at seeing his friend be so badly injured while being unable to help him, began to show its effects.

                                                Finally, after an enormous and exhausting battle, Morgan's strikes became too much, and Aguda was defeated, falling on the battlefield. Using his immense force of personality, Sir Montague managed to convince Attah that, as the de facto leader of the nation, he needed to leave the area, lest he fall prey to the US attackers. After this, Radoslav detonated the explosives, and killed the rebels, removing all witnesses to their involvement.

                                                Sir Montague and Attah left the field of battle, and returned to the city, announcing Aguda's death at the hands of the US attackers, and the formation of the North African Union in order to continue Aguda's dream of a unified, powerful, Africa. Over the next two months, given Sir Montague's immense skill, as well as proximity and influence with Attah, in combination with the information-gathering abilities of Edouard and brutality of Radoslav and Wolfgang, the group managed to control the violent outbursts that inevitably follow such an event. By visibly working with the neighbouring countries of Mali and Niger, the xenophobia they felt at seeing their best chance killed by foreign forces was channelled towards a (from ESPER's perspective) non-threatening target; the US. The US, of course, denied any involvement, but Attah's personal testimony was seen as immensely damning. They even managed to convince the UN that the US was behind the killing of the original ESPER operatives, who were seeking out anti-nova terrorist groups (officially forgiven by Iprelia); pre-prepared supporting intelligence reports managed to swing the tide. As a result, the expected reparations ESPER was expected to pay were forgiven, and redirected to the US. The US is still attempting to fight the accusations.

                                                While the NAU is still extremely nascent, with internal conflicts simply being too in-grained and established to simply be wiped away, it is now appearing to be a possibility. Trade has been established, the area is, for the first time, well on its way to long-term stability, and ESPER is cleared of any wrongdoing from the original mission.

                                                As first official missions led goes, Sir Montague would rate this as 'acceptable'.

                                                            After the last one, this is a comparatively simple and relaxed mission, at least so far. Albeit one with a lot of background explanation required.

                                                            Not all member states of ESPER are members of the EU, although it is commonly viewed as a first step towards eventually joining. One such state is Estonia; although reluctant to join after their struggles to regain their independence from the USSR, the eruption of Anton Saar, a nova with minor telekinetic abilities and the ability to cure any disease (as well as an involuntary full-body glow), has made ESPERs scientific resources invaluable in their attempts to understand and replicate his power. Of course, all of this has become largely moot, as Anton Saar is now dead from an explosion in his apartment building.

                                                            Understandably upset by this, Estonia has requested ESPER's aide and advice in dealing with the anti-nova cell responsible; heavily suspected to be IEE, or Humanity First Front. A reformation of VET, or Free Estonia Against Tyranny, a militia that carried out several violent acts while trying to secure Estonia's freedom from the USSR that went underground since that occurred. The membership of IEE includes a large number of the same people as was in VET, however, which means a small but militant, military-grade trained group prone to extremism. Up until now, although individual members have been convicted of assault of government officials, vandalism and other crimes, the individuals have always maintained that they acted alone, and the leader of the group, Jakob Nigul, officially decries any illegal act against Estonia's legal government. However, he is also a strident opponent of the perceived 'special treatment' that novas receive: the contributions they can make for society are often weighed against their crimes (see: Wolfgang and Radoslav). The group also fears novas 'taking over' from humanity, and remaking society in their image, with humanity being a slave race, ignored, or outright exterminated.

                                                            The explosion that claimed the life of Anton Saar occurred at 3:47am on a Tuesday night. His apartment building (in which his apartment was on the third and highest floor) was swept daily for explosives, and was overall one of the more secure buildings in Tallinn. However, somehow an explosive device found its way into his bedroom and detonated. The explosion itself was serious but likely non-fatal; the ball-bearings it released, however, made sure even nova toughness was insufficient to survive. The Estonian police have done their best, but they have been unable to find the proof they need to finally put this group away.

                                                            Wolfgang and Radoslav are ESPER's official response; with instructions to act as tactical advisors and, if needed, assistance should it devolve into a fight. Unofficially, their task is to distract the general public and the perpetrators from Sir Montague, Morgan and Edouard's attempts to find the proof Estonia needs.

                                                            But not the proof it deserves.
