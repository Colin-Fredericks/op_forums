                        Originally posted by Nihilist View Post
                        The title calls this "1st Assignment", what happened to the 2nd? Did you have plans you could share? Colin seemed suspiciously poignant at the end there, for a character that had little actual role in the drama...
                        I did plan several other Assignments, but none of them got fleshed out anywhere nearly as much as Frantically French. I did plan on having Colin show up as a re-occurring NPC in some of the other Assignments.

                        Originally posted by Nihilist View Post
                        The party composition seems to be locked in by the narrative, yet the story doesn't really take advantage of that expressly. Did you intend for each member to actually have been dunked by their respective orders? Was the grouping one of psionic or political consideration? Is there any reason why another group couldn't take part in the adventure? Would you say it could even be headed up by organizations other than Aeon, with a bit of re-tailoring?
                        The approach of the party composition was intended to be idealistic from a story perspective as well as from a game dynamic one. Trinity had a theme of unity, which I thought could be represented well by a group of psions, one from each of the Orders, working together to succeed in aiding humanity, hence the beginning narrative in the Assignment I wrote. The Assignment can be re-tooled to easily handle any mash up of psions or even normal humans for that matter. By the way, did Trinity have a term for normal humans like Aberrant did, I can't remember?

                        Originally posted by Nihilist View Post
                        Why use Hector Ramirez? Was this eventually going to run into Darkness Revealed or in the midst of it (Passage Through Shadow may have room before the trial, maybe after with some adjustments...)?
                        I did plan on running all of the printed material as well. I figured it would be best to introduce Hector right from the start to enrich the published material down the road.

                        Originally posted by Nihilist View Post
                        Did you refer to the UK Sourcebook "In his Majesty's Service" for your visit to London Arcology? (nice placement of the beanstalk, by the way...)
                        Did you avoid geographic details for any reason? The Thames doesn't get mentioned at the point of departure, the arrival location is an unnamed beach, when Calais, Boulogne and Dunkirk could be used. Why do they need to do this quiet insertion as opposed to a more grand introduction to ruined France?
                        Were you tempted to use French Flu at any point? Or any contemporary French locales as ruins?
                        In His Majesty's Service was not written at the time I wrote and edited Frantically French, though I would have definitely tried to use material from it for my Assignment. I did try to incorporate as much of the published material as I could because I like practically all of it. Mining as much detail as possible really makes the game more of a reality for players. However, I think it is possible to include too much. Really, I just read the books, jotted down enough notes to get started and then began writing. If I didn't include something from the published material it was either because I didn't feel it was necessary, simply forgot about what I read, or I felt I already had enough material. Though I could easily see myself adjusting the Assignment with the ideas you mentioned. As for the quiet insertion, I think that fit the approach Aeon was trying to take in the Assignment. They wanted to try out something new with this team dynamic of a psion from each Order and publicize the heck out of it...if it worked. No need to make it a press release until they were confident it was a success.

                        Originally posted by Nihilist View Post
                        How long does it generally take you to run the scenario and how would you award experience for it?
                        Unfortunately, I never did run Frantically French, nor have I run a Trinity game. I would hope the game would take two to three sessions, each four to five hours long, to complete. As for experience, I had planned a ton of house rules, especially when it came to character advancement. I had anticipated a run of three Assignments in Europe before reaching a milestone and moving on to a different setting. Perhaps that gives a better picture for how much experience to dole out.

                        Again, thanks for the interest and I hope Frantically French lives on in other games!
