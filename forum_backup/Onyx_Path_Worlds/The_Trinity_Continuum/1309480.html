    Campaign Idea Thread

    05-02-2019, 02:26 PM
    Hey all, so I was thinking about potential campaign ideas and that led me to deciding to make this thread. A thread for potential campaign ideas, short or long, detailed or 'simple', standard world or alternate world idea.

    My first idea is a post apocalyptic world hopping game. The World we start on is a destroyed, apocalyptic wasteland home to only one surviving city of a few thousand people. Everyone knows there is no way for them to continue to go on, no way to rebuild a world in such a state of monstrous total devastation. Which is why scientists came up with a way to basically change / undo the devastation, a plan that involves sending teams of special agents to various places and times throughout the worlds of reality in order to collect certain Seeds of Potential. When enough of them are gathered a process can be done that would basically change the world, rebuild it.

    Another idea involves a mission to mars, with the players being a part of the scientific or colonist body. On Mars they discover something ancient that points to a great mystery. Accidentally accessing said artifact sends out a blast of energy which doesn't seem to do anything at first but in time will see the awakening of the Inspired and such. First phase is events on Mars then second phase is events on an Earth that is beginning to change.

    A new idea that I just had is let's go traditional. The players are part of a society, probably the Aeon Society, which works to protect the world from threats of a more mysterious nature, A theft in a museum, an assassination of a political leader, and an arrival of a mysterious letter reveal the return of some great foe. The players are the ones tasked with investigating the mystery.

    Feel free to comment on these ideas while also posting your own campaign ideas.

                      The idea of having a game that starts in one era and then jumps upwards a few years each adventure really seems nifty. You say 70s/80s but imagine even earlier, like around the Great Depression, or the World War II, and then the aftermath, and all that.                       I could see a prologue which is set NOW and the group are either reminiscing or something which then leads to a fade to the past.

                      OR it is NOW and something they are working on now requires them to revisit their past cases which suddenly becomes all linked together in a way they hadn't realized they were. That could be fun.

                      I could even see having them create two character sheets. One, their main one, starts off as a basic character with all the normal things. The second, the 'present' them starts with hundreds or something worth of extra XP. The cool thing is that as adventures go in time they become more and more the people of the present.

                      Just had another idea.

                      Events are going normal when a pulse of mysterious energy comes from somewhere in the Atlantic Ocean. This leads to storms and tidal waves in the east coast of the Americas and the western coasts of Europe and Africa. But it also leads to NATO dispatching a group of ships to the theorized epicenter of said pulse. They discover an Anomaly, one whose appearance I am not entire sure of (so it could be an orb of spinning light or it could be a fissure and crack), and so focus study on it. Events occur and a ship is transported through the anomaly, which leads them to another World (one that is clearly the moon of a massive gas giant, in order to give it a foreign field.). A bit of exploration and they discover a nearby massive continent, For irony the location is the Atla Reefs and the ship that is transported is the USS Atlantis which leads to the continent being called Atlantis by the initial scouts. Within a year a treaty is signed between the nations of the world which lead to the start of an initial set of base building on the continent of the new world. The original explorer, a former Captain in the US Navy, is given permission to settle on a small island within a central river near the center of the continent. The players are a team of scouts and explorers based at his new settlement but who roam beyond it. The game could also have some city building with the players directing the growth of the colony. The new world could have megafauna and megaflora as well as special resources and unique anomaly sites. I could see the players becoming Talents due to nature of their lives int he new world.

                      But that is not all.

                      An older idea I had is an Inspired businessman built a giant arcology tower and populated it with millions of people within its spiraling open spaced interior. After a series of bizarre events on Earth he activates a prototype engine and jumps, an act which sees the tower and all inside it traveling between the worlds of reality. They will spend a day or so in each world before jumping again. The players are a team of first-in scouts who exit the tower (the safe haven) and explore the nearby countryside. Obviously at some point something should strand them on a world which leads to an adventure which leads to them gaining jump capability which leads them back to the arcology.

                      Furthermore.

                      I bit of watching Surviving Mars on YouTube has me really thinking about a Mars-based Trinity game. (Power groups could be Talents but I wouldn't mind there being the other two types of Inspired, when we get them.) Really thinking about it. I could see it having a bit of base building in addition to some sort of mystery that the players need to solve and that would make it quite a fun game. I could also see there being a storyline about what happens when there is loss of contact with the Earth.

                      Soooooo would like to think more about an adventure on Mars!!
