                Potential can be energy, but the same way Psi is out of the reach of Quantum, Potential is out of reach of Psi because it is not in the universe, but in the between universes.
                Novas accumulate quantum energy in their bodies, and it distorts their bodies in the process in the form of Quantum Flux.
                Psi has no issue, as the energy is stored in the sun quantum space, in the Psionic lattice that describes the psion self.
                Potential is stored out of the Talent body, in the between of the many versions of himself, so out of the universe.

                Maybe a Quantakinetic or virtually any super human can drain Potential of a Talent, but only after the potential enters the universe, otherwise it don’t exist in that reality to start with. The problem is, and that’s why I think potential is a great name, once it is in the universe, it’s not potential any more, it becomes... part of the reality, the result of the gift or dramatic edition.

                    I have always thought about the relationship between Talent-flux (Potential) with the universe and its relationship with the other two forces (Psi and Quantum). After reading and rereading I found this here:
                    "Inspiration is the flux that surrounds a Talent, fills her, and lets her tap into energy that allows her to perform extraordinary feats." Trinity Continuum Core Pag. 151
                    In the same way that a nova is a Quantum-flux battery. A Talent is a Talent-Flux battery.
                    A Psicomorph would be a bender of the material reality template.

                    In my opinion, Talent-Flux does exist between worlds, but in flux-events it leaks into our reality, and these leaks inspire Talent and allow his connections to other universes.

                                                            I think I get it. In Mateus' Luz 1st paradigm (I'll call it "Potential Paradigm"), Potential is not an energy per se, but an actual "difference of potential" in the Physical Science sense (such as "10 Volts" between two different poles of a wire) that does not exist inside the universe (it's merely the measure of difference between "our universe" and "another"); in Jonnes' paradigm (I'll call it "Energy Paradigm") Talent-Flux is akin to some kind of material energy (such as "10 Coulombs") that does exist inside the Talent's body. In Mateus' 2nd paradigm (I'll call it "Excelsior Paradigm") is an alternate version of the Potential Paradigm: in this version, the Excelsior's body does contain actual Potential Energy (such as a "10 Volts" battery).

                                                            I think both (or "three-both"?) solve the problem about Paramorph metaphysics, but I think the choosing criteria of which one to use should be which paradigm explains perfectly everything we see in-game and in-systems. If none can be perfect, then we should list the Pros and Cons of each, and then make an informed decision about which one to use as "truth".

                                                            I think the Potential Paradigm is very elegant and my instincts tell me it fits the world perfectly, but since I did not think about all of the emergent consequences of it, I cannot say (yet) that I'll use it.

                                                            The Energy Paradigm's main good points, for me, is that it has a high usability (I'm not sure average Joe grasps what "Potential" means in physics), and if I'm not mistaken, is also the one the official books use.

                                                            I don't know if I like the "Detectable/Stealable" energy feature of the Excelsior Paradigm, but my gut feeling tells me it's not a good choice - but I can be wrong, I should think much more about it.

                                                            Despite all that, congratulations to Mateus and Jonnes, your thoughts are really interesting!!!
                                                            (Oh, and I bought your Fists of Flux book Mateus, really liked the Excelsior concept. Reminded me a lot of MCU's Doctor Strange!)
