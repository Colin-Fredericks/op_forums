    Wold Newton

    02-15-2019, 04:00 PM
    In Contorting Your Continuum, we have the following news report:
    December 13, 1795: EXTRAORDINARY STONE Falls from Atmosphere – Lands at Wold Newton Cottage. Proof of Existence of Extra-Terrestrial Matter?

    For those of you who aren't aware, this is a reference to the Wold Newton Family, a literary concept that is used to tie together many Victorian-age adventurers such as Doc Savage, Tarzan, Sherlock Holmes, Phileus Fogg, the unnamed protagonist of H.G.Wells' the Time Machine, and many, many others. It appears that Ian is hinting that the Wold Newton event and its consequences are part of the Trinity Continuum: the meteorite that crashed in Wold Newton in 1795 serves as a sort of minor Hammersmith Event that sets off a Victorian Age era of adventurers.

    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
