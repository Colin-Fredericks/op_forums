        The thing about such plans (from an inworld perspective) is that its hard for scientists to fully take into consideration all the potential methods that an active Psion can use to resist the effects either singularly or in tandem with others in cooperation. Which to me is where the fun is - such plans rarely if ever can truly be kept secret because something will let it out. Which when that happens means Aeon or the Orders will gather personnel and resources to stop the attempt.

        Which is why I think it would be really cool if something happens where a person is given the drug and Erupts as either a Nova or Psiad, leading to damage in the area and a growing sense among the neutrals that maybe they should show restraint on giving these drugs to everyone.
