                                                            p.60 Fast Draw "Drawing or holstering that weapon is considered a reflexive action."
                                                            p.99 "Reflexive actions in combat represent activities that take little to no time to perform. Actions such as looking around, retrieving objects from a holster...are all considered reflexive actions"
                                                            Based on the examples of reflexive actions on p.99, the Fast Draw edge provides no actual benefit.

                                                            p.99 "When it is a character’s turn to act, they can take any number of different actions (equal to their Cunning). Actions fall into three broad categories: reflexive,ordinary, and mixed."
                                                            Does this mean that a character with cunning three can draw a weapon (reflexive action) shoot two targets while lobbing a grenade (mixed action, limited to 3 per this phrase on p.101 "The limit
                                                            to the number of mixed actions a player can take as part of a single action roll is equal to his Cunning.") and then dive behind cover (defensive action)?
                                                            Thereby totaling six actions? Or does the limit on "different actions" on p.99 include multiple actions taken as part of a mixed action?

                                                            p.100 Initiative
                                                            "That character gets the Focus, acts, and chooses who from their team will get Focus next." the should be removed.

                                                            p.101 Attacking
                                                            "Attack actions have a Difficulty of 1, which may be modified by the target’s Dodge Stunt."
                                                            Shouldn't this state that Attack actions have a difficulty equal to the target's Defense rating? Otherwise this seems to imply you need 1 base success + 1 attack action success + 1 target defense success. Then modified by stunts, and so forth. on p.102 - close combat strike it states "Hit: Overcome the opponent's Defense" which is much more clear.

                                                            p.101 Attacking - Example:
                                                            "After he declares the attacks, his opponents roll for defending" Should be roll for defense.

                                                            p.102 Close combat strike - Inflict damage
                                                            Difficulty is listed as "Opponent's Armor" instead of "1 success + opponent's Armor" as is listed for Shoot and Thrown Attack

                                                            p.103 Defense "Penalties to Defense from sources such as Injury Conditions can never bring the character’s Defense below 1."
                                                            p.105 Bruised "Bruised Ribs, -1 Defense"
                                                            p.105 Injured "Cracked Ribs, -2 Defense"
                                                            Seems completely contradictory, that you can have these penalties and still not have your Defense reduced to 0. Also, wouldn't an unconscious/unaware/paralyzed person have a Defense of 0?

                                                            p. 104 Condition Levels
                                                            "Any time the character takes damage, he must fill in an Injury Condition box. His player must choose to take a Bruised, Injured, or Maimed Condition, if available. If none are available, he is Taken Out."
                                                            p.105 Taken Out
                                                            "When a character is all out of Condition boxes, he is Taken Out."
                                                            The statement on 104 implies that the character is taken out when they are Maimed and take one more Injury Condition.
                                                            The statement on 105 imples that the character is taken out when they become Maimed.

                                                            p.105 Taken Out
                                                            "A player can choose to fill in the Taken Out box at any time, rather than take potentially long-lasting Injury Complications."
                                                            Neither the Core nor Aeon sheets provided with these pdfs have a "Taken Out Box"


                                                            p. 109 Damage Examples
                                                            Falling damage is not correctly formatted to table.

                                                            p.111-112 Sample Combat
                                                            "Ariadne has a Close Combat of 4 and a Might of 3, so her total dice pool for this action is 7."
                                                            "Colton’s player rolls his Aim (5) + Cunning (4)"
                                                            please standardize format.

                                                            Sample Combat mentions Arena Rating several times, but this does not appear anywhere else in the book.
