                    Originally posted by Shepherdboi View Post

Given that they wouldn't know of the government's secret Novas, they'd have done everything possible to keep hidden for fear of being executed.

                    Additionally, if everyone was tested for low-taint potential before they disappeared into 'uninhabited' arcology areas, they'd likely be a low-taint Nova.
                    As far as we know, there's no way to test for low flux/taint novas. Really there's no way to test for novas at all. There's a test that reveals that one can potentially be a Nova (or Psion/Psiad/Talent/Superior) but until that genetic die is "rolled" or they get dunked or made a Superior, there's no way to note what the person will become.

                    Another point is that not everyone is tested. Figure if your background is that you're a loyal Nihonjin citizen living a normal life in the arcology, you can only become a Superior or a Talent because you'll be tested, noted and, assuming you pass the other tests of your loyalty/personality, undergo the Nakamura Process which either turns you into a Superior or "fails" and turns you into a Talent. The older generation of the "disappeared" mostly got tested and got the retrovirus, which stopped any chance of eruption, becoming a Psion/Psiad, or being a Talent. But the retrovirus doesn't necessarily eliminate genes for Quantum/Psi/Inspiration, it just prevents them from being expressed. Otherwise there wouldn't have been any Superiors because the genetic code for them would have long since been wiped out.

                    The "disappeared" are having children. The percentage of them having children is generally higher than those living "straight lives" in the arcology. The children they have aren't being tested, except perhaps by the Akiyama group, which has limited resources at best. It's weird to think about, but in some ways the best place to start a group made up of Psiads, Superiors, Talents and Novas would be in the fringe areas of Nippon. Also, prior to closing itself off from the world, Nippon accepted millions of refugees, so character from Nippon, wouldn't necessarily have to be Japanese Asian in appearance or even have a Japanese name (though figure many of those refugees and their families would likely have done so to fit in better).
