    Biokinesis - Psychomorphing - Enhance (p. 214-215) - Fluff says characters can raise physical Attributes to ‘maximum human levels’, which Trinity Core defines as 6 for Talents and other special characters (TC p. 37), but Enhance’s system then limits boosting Attributes to a max of 5 dots in each Attiribute.

    (Edit to add: TC p. 54 says “Attributes are generally rated from 1-5. Some characters may have the ability to raise their normal maximum to 6, though this requires an Edge“, which is consistent with AEon but possibly inconsistent with p. 37.)

    Question 1 - is ‘maximum human limit’ 5 dots or 6 as per TC Core? Follow-on - p.125 of Aeon says Proxies can raise Attributes to 6 dots (or 7 with Superior Attribute Edge, which p.125 erroneously calls the Extraordinary Attribute Edge - needs to be changed.)

    Question 2 - Other boosting powers (Enhancement in Electromanipulation, and dots 2-4 of Augendis) allow Attributes to be raised to 7 dots. Is Biokinesis’s limit deliberately lower?

    Question 3 - Do Proxies or other characters with the Superior Attribute Edge have access to higher than 5 dots from Enhance, which should be specified in text?

                Alertness p. 225 - consider changing the increased difficulty this power gives to the Inflict Damage stunt to Mode Dots/2, rather than +1 per success spent. As the psion gains greater dice pools and benefits from Enhancement with greater Dots in the Mode (with this being the first dot power), this could easily add huge degrees of difficulty to inflicting damage for a zero cost power.

                Edit: On further reflection of this power, a better effect might be that the Clear adds Mode Dots to her Defense, rather than just adding Difficulty to the Inflict Damage stunt. This makes more narrative sense as the clairsentient is then more protected from the attack itself - which is what her precognitive sight would be looking for - rather than the outcome. Under the current wording the Clear is just as susceptible to attacks but more resistant to damage, but still vulnerable to grappling, being disarmed, being blinded, being knocked down, being shoved, not seeing that the attacker’s move is a feint etc.

                I’m suggesting Mode Dots rather than Mode Dots/2 because I still think it makes sense for powerful precogs to be *very* hard to hit. Otha Herzog should have +7 Defense if he wants it. A 5 dot precognitive Clear should see you coming from miles away.
