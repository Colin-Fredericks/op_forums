    If/When I get a chance to run Aberrant 2ed, if my PCs do long range space travel, the times involved would simply have to be taking into account. It's no worse than having a party insist on splitting up over interstellar (or even intrastellar) distances in a game like Traveller.

                            Yeah, I really consider this the silliest of complaints. Should Storypath also be bound to the other bad decisions of the first edition? Should all the Mega-attributes just give loads of dice? Should we... well, I'm being silly. The answer, obviously, is "no".

                            The writers of 1e Aberrant ignored what was supposed to be true for novas in favor of adding more 'standard' superhero powers. Note you can still often achieve the effects of those powers; If my mega-intellect can predict a baseline's actions super-accurately because I am running a very accurate mental simulation of them in my head, does it matter that it isn't "really" mind-reading?

                            It's also the easiest thing in the world to 'hack'. We have XP costs for psions. Novas get a load of XP. If you really, really don't care about the whole continuum thing... just let Novas buy psion stuff like they buy everything else. Use Quantum in place of Psi if you don't want two power stats.

                            Similarly, space travel. Frankly, "it takes me awhile to get to the next star" is still a hell of a lot better than what we have in the actual timeline where Alpha Centauri is several human lifetimes away, but it might be understandable if baseline humanity might get nervous about setting up colonies whose only transportation is a handful of novas.
