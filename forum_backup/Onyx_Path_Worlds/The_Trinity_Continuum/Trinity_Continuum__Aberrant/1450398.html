    [Aberrant] Mega-Attributes, Power Tags, and Negative Power Tags

    05-31-2021, 01:33 AM
    So as I was reading through the section on power tags, I noticed it mentioned that they can sometimes apply to Mega-Attributes and Mega-Edges as well as Quantum powers. There aren't very many negative power tags, but I was curious how those might work.

    Ramp up, for instance. Would that require a certain amount of rounds every use? Mega-Attributes don't have a set duration, so are they simply inapplicable? What about reduced duration, or Restricted?

      The book says they can apply as appropriate for the Qunatum ability, and the SG has final say. Therefore, I'd suggest the player and SG need to discuss how (or if) any given tag applies.

      For example, I could see Ramp Up applying to a Mega-Attribute, requiring however many rounds before it can be used, and then maybe remaining active as long as the character uses it each round. Or perhaps lasting Qunatum rounds. Whatever works. For Reduced Duration, assume that Mega-Attributes begin with something like Maintained duration, and step down from there. The nova may need to Concentrate (suffering the Complication), or impose a Quantum point cost to use the Mega-Attribute.

                  Meanwhile, another consideration might be a take on Restriction called "Alteration" or something: Whenever the Nova uses this power/Mega-Attribute, the Nova suffers the effect of Transformation "whatever" during that time. Figure for a -1 you'd take a Mid-level Transformation like...

                  Sheriff can produce a Force Field but only as long as she's wearing the toy police badge her Dad gave her when she was 8 - and the badge is not protected by the Force Field. If somebody takes it or destroys it, she can't use her Force Field until she either reclaims the badge, picks another talisman (something requiring at least a scene AKA not something she can do in the middle of a fight) or pays the extra XP cost of her power without the cost break provided by the badge - AKA she gets over the weird psychological block (which is, again, not something she can do in the middle of a fight - unless she already has the necessary XP and the GM okays it).

                  For a -2 you'd get a High-level Transformation like... Whenever Storm-lord unleashes Lightning (Quantum Attack) lightning rains down around him creating the Environmental Hazard Energy Emission in a Close area.

                  In either case, Transformation effects lasts for either the duration of the Power or for Quantum rounds, whichever is greater. So if Storm-lord has a Quantum of 3 and fires two blasts in a two-round fight, Lightning will continue to rain down around him for an additional four rounds.

                  Figure GMs should carefully consider what kind of Transformations work like this and make sure they're thing that would be real disadvantages. Like Permanent Power Force Field (effectively only while using Force Field) probably wouldn't be much of a disadvantage. Meanwhile Severe Psychological Disorder (Berserk Rage) attached to Mega-Strength would be appropriate but maybe a little more trouble for the party/game as a whole than it would be worth.
