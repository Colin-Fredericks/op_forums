                        You can have a Nova that spent all the 150 XP in skills, attributes and other mundane things, what would make them the ultimate DarkStar, not even themselves understanding or seeing themselves as Novas, they would still pop in a quantum detector, and would be able to use Qtech (not much, too little juice to power them).

                        But that is the amazing part of what is a Nova and what is not, from the general population understanding, a Talent with high inspiration can be seen as a Nova for ALL that luck, or all the amazing skills they show even with no proper power. Same for a Psiad, they have powers so they must be a Nova, nobody care if they don’t show in quantum detectors, as far as we know popping in quantum detectors may just mean they can use their powers while dormant or that they have a stealth power that protect them from being detected, or even they have a weird signature that is out of tune with the detector. Using that criteria, you can easily recreate all the Avengers and even more as Novas. The system is even more flexible, you can create Dr Strange, Goku, Lex Luthor, even Machine Man as Novas, but they are not, none of them are.

                        So, you can play a game of Aberrant where everyone is a Nova, no matter what is the character theme, in this game Batman is a Nova, same way as Superman. Or you can have a game where Supers are Novas and non supers are Talents. It’s a matter of what you feel would be appropriate.

                        There is one single problem in the first option, if you are a Nova, you pop in quantum detectors, so anyone can differentiate a Nova from a baseline (you can have novas that don’t show in the quantum detector, but not baselines that show). In universe, Black Widow, Hawkeye, Batman, Green Arrow, Kingpin, etc etc are not Supers, they don’t have powers, they are not mutant, they are not aliens, they have no source of powers, they are humans very well trained, very competent and prepared to all situations, in other words, they are Talents.

                        But taking the same criteria, Tony Stark is human, not generally human, completely human, he is a genius, capable of inventing technology that others can’t, but his tech is not reality bending, it’s just absurdly advanced. His armor is not powered by him, in the best description he was powered by the same source as the armor. His inventions have their own source of power (it’s the main reason why it’s so hard to replicate his tech, it consumes too much energy and is still compact), so Rescue, Warmachine and many others have used some of his armors with no difficulties. You can say those armors are Nova tech versions, but they are just old designs or adaptations of old designs.

                        In truth, anovas and Talrnts are setting specifics, that can be expanded to fit other concepts, but not entirely, Superman can be portraited as a Nova in TC, but is he quantum powered or is it a very specific set of Psionic powers? If you read the old description of the X-men powers they are ALL Psionic powers somehow, Colossus have a Psionic capacity of turning his flesh into organic metal, Cyclops blast is made of Psionic energy, etc. Does it make them Psiads? Not really, they fit bette as Novas.

                        My preference is, if you want to play a human play as a Talent, if not play as a Nova. But I want to play as a Psiad! Sure, you can, there are a few hundred of them in Earth, so why not. Same apply to all NPCs. Being a Nova have some impacts in setting, even if you are a DarkStar, and so it is important to pick wisely.

                            Note that Foresight is specifically limited to Dramatic Editing that represents retroactively having planned ahead; that's why it's tied to Mega-Intellect and Mega-Cunning. Talent Dramatic Editing has no such restriction; they can go with any character concept and attribute spread and still get full access to Dramatic Editing. In order for a Nova with Foresight to keep up with a Talent on Dramatic Editing beyond starting characters, the Nova always needs literally inhuman levels of Mega-Intellect and/or Mega-Cunning: Talents always start with a minimum Inspiration of 3 (the one point that all Talents automatically have, plus two or three more points from their Facets), meaning that a Nova with Foresight would need at least one of Mega-Intellect or Mega-Cunning to be at least two — which makes it somewhat difficult to consider that Nova to be a Darkstar. And this problem only intensifies the more the Talent's Facets are raised. And Foresight isn't conceptually appropriate to many Mega-Intellect/Mega-Cunning Novas, either: Tony Stark is very much a Mega-Intellect Nova, possibly with as many as five dots in it; but he's not much on the “planning ahead” thing, and there's no way I'd give him Foresight.

                            And if it's just a matter of experience that puts the Talent at a disadvantage, just give the Talent more experience. There's precedent for that, in that the likes of Batman and Black Widow tend to have undergone some form of intensive training to get to the level they're at. Meanwhile, don't forget that Talents can do things with their Inspiration that Novas can't (easily) do: a Talent can increase the Intensity Scale of any action just by spending a point of Inspiration; a Nova needs to acquire a point of Flux to do the equivalent, with all the hassles that come with that; and he has to find some way to justify it through a Nova trait: Novas can't Max Out Skills or Edges.
