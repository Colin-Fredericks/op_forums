    Advice to adapt concept to Aberrant 2e

    03-29-2022, 06:57 PM
    I am an old fan of Aberrant 1e, even if I was well aware of its flaws. I got the new version of the Trinity Continuum, even if I did not yet get to do much with it. I am only interested in Aberrant and playing a nova, and got the rest of the line as a completist reference for it (e.g. I know Aeon is very important to get the whole picture as it concerns the metaphysics and the aliens). My opinion of Aeon might only change if you get rid of the power specialization system. What the Doyen did to cripple the potential of psions poisoned the game for me.

    I recently got more interested to do a little character creation and solo play experimentation with Aberrant 2e, although I am very unfamiliar with the new system. Hence I am interested to get advice and hints on how to adapt a preferred character concept of mine to the new system.

    I have no special preference as it concerns the mundane background of the character, since they are supposed to turn their back on that part of their life once they became a nova. We may pick whatever seems best here. Character wholeheartedly supports the Teragen viewpoint and is an eager follower of Teras, even if they mostly lack interest in the power plays of the Teragen cliques. They support the Aberrant stance as it concerns dealing with baselines that try to harm, abuse, or exploit novas. They keep some serious attachment to a conventionally attractive humanoid form and/or find it convenient in practical terms. Therefore, they are going to try and guide their personal evolution towards a kind of Transcendence that makes them not too monstrous or does not cripple their agency.with especially burdensome Transfigurations too much. In their opinion, picking unnecessary Flux is slacking or cutting corners with self-development, and limiting long-term options.

    They find the Elite lifestyle rewarding and convenient for anything their personal abilities do not cover, so they strive to balance that with their commitment to the Teragen. They do not object to doing freelance services for baselines, as long as it happens on their terms. They have no problem with using lethal force against baselines for material benefits, although they balk on doing the same to novas, unless for self-defense or against traitors of the One Race or rogue novas. Engaging in combat that is not likely to endanger a nova's life is fine and good sport. They define traitors as novas that harm or abuse their kind for the sole purpose of baselines' welfare. They understand and justify the need to stop rogue novas that make themselves a threat for their own kind or the world at large. They have no problem with using their abilities to improve the world on their own terms and in ways that ultimately benefit novas. However, they violently object to an ideology and practice of nova subservience and expected or worse mandatory service to baselines. Yet another faction they are seriously interested in is the Daedalus League, because they sense that the future of novas is among the stars. Broadly speaking, they would try to balance their involvement and commitment with their three preferred factions. For similar reasons as the Elite lifestyle, they might not be averse to indulging in occasional dabbling with NWE performance, although they are not likely to make that one of their main callings.

    As it concerns their preferred power set, core elements include enhanced strength, near.invulnerablity, immortality, immunity to baseline frailties and bodily needs, regeneration, enhanced reflexes, not-so-specialized elementalism, telekinesis, healing the self and others, matter manipulation, and shapeshifting. It is necessary for the character to master at least electricity and heat/fire manipulation in equal measure. Other elements are more than welcome, although in many cases a combo of the above with telekinesis may suffice. Some serious degree of enhanced intelligence, mental speed, and enhanced senses is also more than welcome. The PC is not much interested in making gadgeteering one of their primary callings, although they find brainpower very useful and like to be able to understand and tinker with advanced tech if necessary. The more developed these abilities, the better. They should also be very attractive and have an indomitable will, even if they are not interested in doing much social manipulation themselves. The character is supposed to have potential to be one of the most powerful novas in the world, and be at least halfway to fulfilling it, so the higher the Quantum, the better. Xp pool should be as big as potentially allowed by the game, and possibly bigger. Yes, I know, all novas have boundless potential to be gods in the long term, but everything is relative.

      Well, you want the complete kit of overpowerness…

      My suggestion to you is go for Q5
      Pick Transformation with a technique in Healing for the phisical mega attributes + Shapeshift + Healing and call it control over biological functions
      Pick Quantum Anima for telekinesis and a technique for Quantum Attack (with the incendiary attack for fire and electricity or stun for electricity) calling it control telekinesis and use fire and electricity as controling the atoms of the target to create heat and static electricity.
      If you want to be tough all the time, add Mega Stamina with toughness and Adaptation or Immunity (or both)
      If you want some mega mental, I would go for Mega Cunning, it will give some understanding of super science but not mega crafting, while also give enhanced reflexes in general.

      How you will balance it all than it’s up to you, as you have a lot of things to spend XP and it’s too personal…
