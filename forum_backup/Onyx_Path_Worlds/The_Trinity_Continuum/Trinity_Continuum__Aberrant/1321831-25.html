                                    Quick clarification: obviously, TC:Aberrant is “it's own game” first and foremost; but part of its identity is that it's part of the Trinity Continuum — which means that it's about psiads and Talents as well as Novas, and the Nova rules have to take that into account.

                                    When I said “it's first and foremost part of the TC”, I was contrasting that with “it's a do-whatever-you-want-with-it superhero game”. Which is not to say that you can't bend it to be more all-purpose than it is; you can, and it's not even all that hard to do. And some of that is even supported in the book with its Tones and Subgenres.

                                    But don't expect the writers to rewrite it as the Storypath edition of Mutants and Masterminds. Whether or not it “should” be that, it isn't going to be; and arguments that it should be aren't constructive.

                                    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                                        My one addition to this discussion is that while Psiads have a place in the current Aberrant setting Psions do not. For this reason I don't think there is anything wrong with the idea of saying Psiads should be able to stand toe to toe with Novas in an Aberrant setting game but its a different can of worms to say Psions should stand toe to toe with a Nova game.

                                        Here is the thing this point does change things, because Psiads are able to learn multiple dots in multiple Modes of multiple Aptitudes. This is different from Psions who are stuck at one Aptitude and one dot in other modes. This means a Psiad can reach power levels on an individual level potentially much higher than what Psions can reach, without them breaking the Doyn blocks and becoming something else.

                                        As another thing there really shouldn't be an automatic assumption that Novas will have to handle Psions in the Psion home turf, that is the Aeon game. The Continuum shouldn't mean that the Nova Age automatically ends in fire and then comes the Psion Age.
