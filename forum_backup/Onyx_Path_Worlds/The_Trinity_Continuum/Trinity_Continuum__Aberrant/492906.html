    If Galatea happened today.

    04-26-2015, 05:22 PM
    So I've been trying to put a story arc together to run some friends of mine though. I really want do a Day 0 sort of scenario, those initial groups of novas who erupted the day of or right after the Galatea explosion, but I really didn't want to deal with the keeping up with the old dates. I didn't initially really put much thought into what that meant, but now I'm sitting here thinking: We have a way different tech base that we did in 1998. Programs like 23andMe that groups like the Aeon society could mine for potential novas even before the Galatea happens. We live in a world were almost every one has a camera in there pocket. Nova eruptions will be posted on youtube and vines.

    Anyone else want to help me add to this?

      data analysis. the use of big data and analytics would make it childs play to spot many newly erupted novas, even if they stayed hidden. because it would represent a change in patterns, and the various agencies would quickly build models to predict hidden novas with some percentage of accuracy and significance. if your cell phone moved too fast while on, thats a flag (but that could be other things). sudden changes in pattern right at the event may be other flags. add up enough flags and you know where to look. a sudden absence of data is also a flag, moreso if data was removed.

      at this point, op!net would be 'different' than the internet to avoid the current FCC regulation rather than any other reason. possibly engineered into a legal blind spot on purpose. eufiber would exist in about 3 years, so you have to wait for it a bit, but its probably a massive bandwidth increase.

      some nova would find all the backdoor hacks in our current OS that the required, and either abuse them or reveal them. or maybe both would happen. another nova would make a new secure OS and everyone would migrate to that platform. it would be a massive leaks situation for everyone.

                                  Extrapolating from world populations...
                                  It make take up to three days for the first eruption to occur, when it does, the first eruption occurs in China and is female and is also somehow christian (statistics by themselves don't always make perfect sense), but the first nova caught on camera is in urban India, likely in a slum due to population density. By the time the US has its first Nova, India and China have four apiece, then Indonesia, etc... Novas begin leaving poorer countries for richer ones immediately. Within the first week, we not only have public novas but also novas who post videos of themselves defying physics.
                                  After the first month India and China, with ten native Novas each, are likely interacting with at least one of their Novas - whether this is peaceful or violent in each case will determine a lot. Given China's treatment of Novas in the material, it might be safe to assume their first contact doesn't go well - it would be concealed, uncovered, reported and then promptly forgotten by the next news cycle. The first videos of Nova eruptions are carefully edited phone recordings out of Chinese media - but soon after, India sees recorded eruptions going up onto the internet unedited. Given their advanced mental agility, some of these Novas are hitting the ground running with the ability to connect with each other over global distances - nascent Nova institutions begin to form before human governments can shift their bulk to adapt to the rapidly changing landscape.
                                  After ninety seven days, the world has one hundred novas. Both China and India have contributed thirty or more, the US has seen seven, many countries are seeing their own emerge. An arms race between India and China over who first wields the power of a nova on the battlefield begins. The first acts of Nova terror begin as oppressed minorities strike out against tyrants - likely bringing the world to the brink of revolution within the first four months as old defence structures are rendered mostly useless in the face of lone Nova terrorists/freedom fighters. Nova exclusive circles are found online, but go above the heads of most baselines.
                                  After the first year (if the war hasn't already started, likely in Kashmir or somewhere over the Himalayas if it has), India and China both boast generation of over a hundred native novas - the US has produced 28, but likely has a great number of Indian and Chinese Novas working either in the US or for an organization from the US. Companies like Devries and Kurotek are given incentive enough to get started as soon as the concept pops into their creators mind. Early nova dabbling in science, art and war are outperforming and re-imagining the human spirit in ways that leave the world almost completely in their thrall. Nobody watches baseline videos when Novas are free to create their own content online - youtube-like nova-made video is shown nearly 24/7 on many tv stations. Nova discussions of Opnet conversion are blown into a doomsday scenario by respected baselines unable to understand the technology.
                                  By the end of the first decade, consult the following list for roughly predicted minimum nova populations by country of origin:
                                  China 1221
                                  India 1160
                                  US 284
                                  Indonesia 224
                                  Brazil 181
                                  Pakistan 173
                                  Nigeria 158
                                  Bangladesh 141
                                  Russia 128
                                  Japan 113

                                  Basically it happens much like it does in the book still, but all apologies to the US, they don't get to go first this time.
