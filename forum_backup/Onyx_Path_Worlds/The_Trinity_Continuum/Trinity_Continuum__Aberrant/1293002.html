    Earlier Novas

    03-01-2019, 09:27 AM
    Basically, this is about events causing an eruption of Novas earlier in history and the worlds that would make. Example: a meteor composed of an exotic element explodes in Earth's atmosphere in 1,200BCE causing a worldwide outbreak of Novas. Or an Alchemist in London in 1575 (a ten point Nova himself, but he doesn't know it) comes up with a drug to unleash "spiritual powers" (turn on your node, but he wouldn't think that way). Elizabeth is a powerful Nova and Europe is being transformed. Or Mal sets off the whole Nova thing in 1938. Superman battles Ubermensch at the Normandy beaches.

    However, you slice it is a wildly different game. What would you do and how?

      Depends on how early you're talking here. Early enough and; European colonialism ceases to be a thing. The North Atlantic slave trade never happens. The empires of Central and South America never fall. Egypt repels all invaders. Persia, Rome, and Carthage become the new (old) world super-powers. If it's early enough, so much of history gets re-written that it's no longer relevant.
      If you go later than that, like in your 1930s for example; WW2 either lasts far longer or is vanishingly brief thanks to there being novas here and there. Axis powers would find to their dismay that it is harder to gestate a full-grown nova than it is to build armies of mooks, and the countries with the most nova population would essentially swarm them unless they found new axis members. Allies would then fall to rancor over dominance faster than usual since there would be more power to go around, more pride, and less good will. Areas with a lot of nova population that weren't originally part of that struggle would now be players in the game, which lends more evidence to a longer game. Trench warfare was already harrowing for those in that era, anti-nova warfare would be shattering to whole cultures/generations. Between kamikaze nova soldiers laying waste to huge areas, and others racking up preposterous amounts of taint, WW2 would mutate into the Aberrant War pretty fast.

                      Originally posted by Astromancer View Post

                      That depends on how hard European diseases hit Native American societies. Five out of six Native Americans died by disease between 1492 and 1525. Just having Novas wouldn't guarantee much.
                      There's also the question of one culture or state's Novas cancelling out the presence of another. If the only superhuman in 1942 is the Golden Age Superman, then it's conceivable that WW2 could be over by 1943. But if there is some sort of German Ubermench one the other side, then the two may just continuously clash inconclusively over and over as the war more or less plays out as it did in real history. (Alternately, said clashes might result in so much collateral damage that by 1945, much of Europe is a post-apocalyptic wasteland even worse than it was in the real world.) The same thing could play out in any conflict, from the Punic Wars to the Conquistadors to modern day.

                      I think the ultimate question is "What sort of setting are you looking to create?" The real world but with some superhuman elements (sort of like the Marvel and DC settings)? Secret conspiratorial history? Alternate history? Science fantasy? Dystopian apocalypse? Then fill in the backstory accordingly.

                                      I am not saying it became Scion per se, it’s about the theme and interaction. I have some restricted views of what the games are, not the rules but the settings, and that may be what is confusing my explanation.

                                      Trinity is not about SciFi, it’s about unity of the people against the evil of the past. You can substitute the Aberrants from trinity by nuclear weapons or chemical weapons in the hands of people unprepared for the responsibility without changing the theme of the game. Take the psions out and put military with new more controled but not so powerful weapons and you have it. Even a wrath of khan stile idea, they don’t need to be infinitely powerful, they just need to be a thread to humanity.

                                      Adventure is about exploration and doing the right thing hoping to get a better future. You can put the theme of Adventure in the Expance or Star Trek with no difficulty, as it is not about pulps heroes, it’s about heroes overcoming danger to make a better future.

                                      Than my view of Aberrant, it’s probably the most difficult of explain as it was the one I played most, but it’s about people interacting with people, but some of them were given powers beyond the usual and they were not prepared. It’s more or less the same as putting random people to govern countries and see what happen when they discover they have an army to control. It’s about people unprepared for doing a job that can cause disasters. It’s about people who won the lottery and got an amount of money could make the world a better place, but want to spend all with themselves. And it’s about sacrifice of the self to help the others, learning about the nature of the power and start using it for good.

                                      Scion is not about power, Scion is about Myth, it’s about birthright, family traditions and changing of point of view. It’s about the insignificant job you have to do why’ll the true powers are up there and don’t help you, until you get to the top and see they have their own problems. It IS about gods and ascension, it’s about worth and values.

                                      When I say you go too far you turn into Scion, I mean you stop thinking about sacrificing to became a better person, and start thinking about building a myth of yourself. The moral is different, there is no caring about the future of humanity, you are no human, you are above and beyond. In aberrant the “gods” are the villains, they became the Aberrants from trinity, you don’t want to be one of them, you want to stay human somehow, in Scion you want to be the god, you want to kill people for saying they are better than you, you don’t care if the tsunami you made will destroy an island population, as long the stupid mortal that didn’t made a proper offer die.

                                      That’s it, and it’s completely off topic. So let’s get back to earlier novas...
