                                                        Warning: I haven't read the thread yet; so my comments may be out of whack.

                                                        I see there being two basis strains of eximorphs: the Enhanced, of which Superiors are a Tier 2 example and Dark Stars are a Tier 3 example; and the Empowered, of which Novas are the Tier 3 example. The distinction is that the Enhanced are limited to Mega-Edges and (at Tier 3) Mega-Attributes, while the Empowered also have access to Quantum Powers.

                                                        Now, Superiors don't actually have access to Mega-Edges; what they have are lower-powered conceptual counterparts (the Superior powers). But they are conceptual counterparts: both are built around the premise that they enhance the character's existing abilities in some way: they're directed inward. From here on, I'll use “Augmentation” to refer to any inward-focused power, whether it's a Superior Power, a Mega-Edge, or a Mega-Attribute. By contrast, Quantum Powers are about affecting the world around the Empowered; they're directed outward. And the powers that Tier 2 Empowered have access to that Tier 2 Enhanced don't should likewise be directed outward.

                                                        At Tier 2, I'd consider the possibility of saying that you're either Enhanced or Empowered, but not both; this would be in contrast to Novas being both. On the other hand, an argument could be made that Augmentations are the birthright of all eximorphs, and that Tier 2 Empowered ought to have access to both: in particular, the biggest difference between eximorphs and psychomorphs is that the former has access to Augmentations while everything that the latter gets is more akin to Quantum Powers.

                                                        Either way, the superhuman abilities that the Tier 2 Empowered have that the Enhanced don't should arguably be patterned loosely off of Psion Aptitudes — not in terms of how they're organized (I'm not saying “eight Aptitudes with three Modes of chains of five powers each”), but rather in terms of how much a Tier 2 Empowered's powers ought to be able to do.

                                                        My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
