    Making a pilotable body to remote control- How do?

    03-11-2022, 04:33 AM
    I've been working on an Aberrant concept and am struggling on how to make it work in a way that will both match the idea and also not entirely suck.

    The core concept is a Nova reduced down to a immobile, small object due to Transformation reshaping their body. I thought it'd be an interesting concept that much of their power set was based on recreating a body for them to control and perceive through, since they can no longer do so with their own body. Potentially utilizing Bestow effects to grant the illusion that the artificial body was the actual Nova while they did their best to conceal their true nature.

    Problem is, the best power that seems to match that concept is Quantum Agent, but from everything I can tell, the Agent created is pretty weak. I thought maybe Quantum Construct could do it, but I'm not entirely sure that is how that power can work.

    Or maybe it'd best be done by a high level Q-tech item, to help get around the powers being less effective at low levels?

    I'm not really sure, but I'd appreciate any advice or ideas.

                  The Adventure! era villain known as the Ubiquitous Dragon could apparently make permanent Quantum Agents, and swap his mind between those bodies to run his global crime empire. He obviously had some unique Tags applied to the Power.

                  You could also go the route of Bloat from the Wild Cards novels, and limit your constructs so they can't travel too far from your real body. This keeps you from becoming effectively unkillable because there is no reasonable way for enemies to find your true body.

                                  For your original concept, I'd write up the character as if he was the “piloted body”, then define a unique Transformation a la Puella Magi Madoka Magica: a High-Level variation of Power Loss, where the character is rendered immobile and effectively unconscious if separated from his “talisman” — with the dirty secret being that he is the “talisman”.

                                  Another possibility would be a creative use of Transformation (the Quantum Power) by inventing a pair of Negative Power Tag for it: an “inflexible” tag that only allows you to switch between two configurations, one representing your actual form, and the other representing your robot body; and another that specifies that both forms technically exist at all times, but you can only control the piloted body when you're “wearing” it.

                                          A Varg would use it's size scale as might scale in any instance where lifting, punching, etc would be involved. I think where you may run into trouble with your concept is that the Nova would probably loose access to their other powers while their conciousness was transmitted into another body. Most of the ways I can think of would end up that way. The Q Agent power would give the external body some powers (but probably less than a full character), but would require a high Quantum rating. I suspect the reason this build is difficult to do is because allowing a PC full access to all of their power while in the field with no actual personal danger is a little OP.

                                                  The roll would be a piloting roll. So you could either use pilot (your ability to use the controls to make the bot do what you want), or if it is a more direct mental connection you might use close combat or athletics depending on what you are doing. In the latter case I'd probably use the Mental Attribute (so Intellect when might would be involved and Cunning for Dex).
