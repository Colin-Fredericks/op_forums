                        The Q6+ powers in 1e APG we’re a mix of things that are perfectly doable in TC.

                        Most of the powers are only other powers with scale. Some are just boring uses of the power to change a planet, basically a bunch of rules to do things narratively.

                        Quantum Authority and Mastery are a bunch of random powers that were connected by “Quantum”. They are techniques with bizarre explanations of how they are connected, like Illusion that work on your quantum signature and your powers, remote perception with extra steps, bestowing powers to people, etc. Turning people into Novas is another thing that falls in the next group of powers (narrative powers and plot hooks left in the hands of players).

                        The remaining powers are plot hooks that make no sense to give to the players, Cross time travel, Time Travel, Universe Creation and Plank Scale. If you really want your character to have this kind of power in my game, it’s better you become the GM, because it’s going to be really hard to GM on a player that can undo whatever the GM want to happen:
                        GM: “ok, there is a neutron star coming to the general direction of Earth and it will eventually hit it. It is to big and heavy to be moved”.
                        Player of a Cross time Traveler: “I take us to a universe where this star will not bother Earth”.
                        GM: “What about the people you love in your universe?”
                        CTT: “They are in the new universe we moved into too, so we won’t miss, also there are infinite universes where it will happen, saving one will not matter in the long run”
                        Anyway, these powers can be done by power stunt easily, because you are not going to do this kind of thing very often, at least not without taking the GM chair, so using it once in a while with a small chance of failing is not that big deal, and you are not going to spend XP in powers that you won’t use very often.
