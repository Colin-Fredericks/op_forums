    [Aberrant] What sorts of new consumer technology exists in 2028?

    12-01-2020, 09:28 AM
    Trinity Continuum: Aberrant is not our future, but we can definitely make some extrapolations. Novas have changed the world and technology, but what does that mean to the average person? Here are the ideas I have and evidence from the text so far.

    Advanced Drones - Slider is given one of these by her teammates.
    Faster, better networks
    Further expanding of social media and online platforms
    Flying cars (not really consumer but)

    What else is different? Obviously things are better, but what is going to be commonplace then that isn't now. Has AR become widespread yet? Are drones delivering packages directly to you?
