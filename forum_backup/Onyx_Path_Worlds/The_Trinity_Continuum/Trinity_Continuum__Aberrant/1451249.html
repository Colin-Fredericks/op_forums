    [Aberrant] Crafting a cure, and other ideas

    06-07-2021, 04:21 PM
    I've been trying to figure out how to model one of the core tech cures mentioned in Aberrant 2e, but the rules seem geared towards hardware rather than vaccines, software, etc.

    The only method that comes to my mind at the moment is crafting a serum that has Immunity 1-5 with the Intrinsic tag. Would I also need to include some degree of M-Sta to utilize this mega edge? I'm also thinking the serum would need to have the Bestow tag to indicate that it imparts the Immunity to the patient (rather than having the edge be a property that protects the serum)?

    I've found the crafting rules pretty difficult to follow; if anyone has any tips or examples, I would be grateful.

      I tend think you handle those without rules since its not a technical device. You set a difficulty, milestones and what not like normal, but there no utilitarian product to hold in your hand to use. Its not as if you will use the cure for cancer in combat or to solve a riddle. In general super science is for crafting items but I think it can serve for other things. The Adventure manuscript touches on this briefly.

              for curing: I would basically use the Heal power with some negative tags restricting it to a certain disease/ adding in an amount of time for it to work. Make it a single use item (flaw). Then you just have to "invent" it 3 times. That it is a small injectable treatment etc.

              For vaccines: I would make it Immunity mege-edge with Impose, Intrinsic, and several negative tags similar to the cure as well as the single use item flaw.

              If the players are crafty they could then use this template to start making semi-super humans based off of similar logic to start crafting a way for them to get "free" powers/mega-attributes/mega-edges. just make sure the line is clearly discussed. It is relatively easy for a mega inventor to make a nova-tech injection or pill that would give them permanent Mega-attribute 5 ...This can be repeated for several other powers/mega-attributes/mega-edges.

              Intrinsic Transform 5 (maybe with some edges) as novatech is not difficult to accomplish for a good inventor.

                  Originally posted by Timecrafter View Post
                  Trinity Core, p.93, "compounds"?
                  Crafting is not about technical things. The rules can be used for creating a plant that can make someone smarter. You Craft the plant that uses the Bestow Tag on Mega-Intellect 1.

                  Sure, they do talk about mechanical devices but there is, literally, nothing that says you are limited to that.

                  In Adventure!, they gave a sample device which is a mathematical formula that lets you affect an organization.

                  You can make a drink that gives you super powers for an hour.

                  The Crafting rules allow you to create an item to do something more than you, or your allies, can do normally.
