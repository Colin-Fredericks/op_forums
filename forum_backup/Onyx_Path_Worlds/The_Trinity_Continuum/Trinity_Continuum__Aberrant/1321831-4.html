                                                        I have to admit, while I can deal with it in the specific genre context of Adventure!, how much “Guardian Ethos” is at work with the Aeon Society bugs me. Maybe I just have too much LRE in me, but the idea of rich philanthropists being the big heroes of the setting always make my eyes roll.

                                                        For the Trinity Core setting, I reskinned the Global Cartography initiative to make Aeon Society academic explorerers/researchers, who in turn provide data to their sister organizations Triton (who seek to apply the information systemically to do long term good) and Neptune (who seek to apply the information acutely to do good where it is imminently needed) - both of which are established NGOs that do get philanthropic funding, but those donors aren’t their members.
