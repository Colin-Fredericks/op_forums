            The increase in scale with bare hand punches is a big question mark to me to, you can have both approaches, and I think it’s a SG option.
            Honestly I would make MMight not increase scale of punches, giving you 2 options to do so while fighting, using large objects as weapons (as you can lift them easily) or have a Quantum attack that works as your “bare hand punch”.
            This option balance MMight to the other physical MAtts (none giving scale in combat).
            That also gives you an explanation of why using cars and posts to hit the enemy is pretty common in comic combats, while punch would have the same result by the rules.

            Now on weapon/armor scale.
            You CAN use weapons and armors giving scale as described in the table or can consider them all scale 1 as standard, it’s up to the SG. In Aberrant specifically, we deal with a lot of scale and giving scale to guns would have not that much impact in the game, but in a Core game it would have a lot.
            First, it would make guns extremely lethal (like in real life), possibly making any attack against an unarmored target almost a instant take out.
            It would make most characters afraid of jumping into gunshots (like in real life), and fights, once you are armed, would be rare and fast.
            You would also need to add scale to other big weapons, like swords and axes, as those weapons do as much damage as a gun, but small knives would be still scale 1 weapons.
            My approach on this is, ignore the table and follow the rules described in Core, all weapons are scale 1 unless they have tags that increase it.

            Lastly, Qtech and Nova tech weapons and armors use super science rules to be built, and are built using powers and regular tags, so you CAN build an scale 9 armor, but it would be a rank 9 item and you would also need Q9 to use it, something that is not present in Aberrant era (at least not at the standard Aberrant game).

                                                            Well, I don’t really care much about the laws of physics when dealing with Novas, but I do care about how the powers work in game and how they may be balanced or not when the players have to deal with a lack of resources while others just don’t use their quantum at all, or the fact one of them is one scale above all the time.

                                                            But ok, I will give another example this time on the “not following physics rules”.

                                                            Let’s describe Mega Might as touch range telekinesis, it’s not really it, as there is still the need to grab, but it work pretty similar.

                                                            The nova touch the target and move it using only his telekinetic power that he basically can’t project further than touch, but once touching he can lift a huge amount of material with no physical effort.

                                                            Usually, characters that can lift objects with telekinesis are not automatically capable of using it as a weapon, so it’s hard to them to accelerate it fast enough to cause damage, you need a tag that allows you to do so, the attack tag. You can also question the capacity of destroying the object using telekinesis, that is basically what a Mega Might user can do by crushing it with his hands, not really causing impact but smashing by continuous pressure.

                                                            Now back to the touch telekinesis, you can say when the nova moves his fist and touch the target he immediately discharge a huge telekinetic force over the target, that would be the attack, it can be part of Mega Might Mega Attribute, but it’s a tag to other kinds of telekinesis, so it should be a tag (or Mega Edge, or independent power) to Mega Might too.

                                                            You can always say a nova don’t follow the physics laws, and so, a Mega Speed character should be able to do a lot of damage easily just because he is as fast as a bullet and the size of a person, even an automatic sonic boom could be a side effect of running at Speed scale 6+, but no one question it would be unbalanced. It’s a paradigm, you think mega might should cause damage, so you don’t question it, I think it’s about constant pressure and lifting, and so high impact is not a immediate effect.
