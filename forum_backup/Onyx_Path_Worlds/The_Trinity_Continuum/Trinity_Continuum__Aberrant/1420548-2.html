        A comment on Scale on Attack and Defense:

        No power cause scale on attacks or defense, except Speed and only if the target is moving, not during a close combat fight. The scales apply only to damage, and durability don’t make the targets more capable or dodging.

                                                        Personally, I tend to view the relationship between Attribute and Mega-Attribute with the Mega-Attribute being dominant and the regular Attribute representing a fine-tuning of your Mega-Attribute: someone with Mega-Might 1 and Might 1 is at the lower end of what Mega-Might 1 can do, while someone with Mega-Might 1 and Might 5 is at the upper end of Mega-Might 1. And instead of saying that you can't have a Mega-Attribute higher than its corresponding Attribute, I view it as you can't have an Attribute lower than its corresponding Mega-Attribute: someone with Might 1 is too weak to handle the rigors associated with Mega-Might 2. Mathematically, the two approaches are equivalent; but conceptually, this alternative interpretation makes more sense to me.

                                                            (Will comment on other things once I take them in)

                                                            I will say this though I think that all Mega-Attributes should effect Scale in some main way. The idea that Mega-Cunning only effects scale with Initiative seems to be lacking in putting the full power behind Cunning. Resolve and Dexterity don't effect scale at all. How is that logical?

                                                            That said, I did just had a thought that there might be a benefit to having an eight category of Scale - Control. It would represent doing things precisely and with super-finesse. It could be quite useful with say certain elements of Dexterity and such things.

                                                            The fact that a Mega-Attribute costs 12 XP and in some cases doesn't seem to do anything practical seems lacking to me. Everything I buy should do something, should give me something. Even if its simply another dice to roll in a pool. I mean I get why Mega-Attributes don't give extra dice, but then it should naturally give Enhancement for everything the base attribute does. This, I think, is where a big part of my problems come from.

                                                            I mean if my character with Mega-Attributes of 1 in all nine attributes automatically gained a +1 scale and/or 2 Enhancements to whatever he did, then I would feel him being potent without being game breaking. He would feel superhuman. He would feel like he is on the path of having the power of a god, which is the tag of the game.

                                                            But in a number of cases, Mega-Cunning for example, he doesn't. When he rolls say Cunning + Empathy his Mega-Cunning doesn't do anything.

                                                            ---

                                                            Oh, so I said this in another post but the way that Hypnotic Presence works seems designed with too high a chance of failure for me. Firstly, its dice pool is Quantum + Mega-Manipulation. Its not roll your normal dice pool and get Mega-Manipulation in Enhancement but straight up Quantum + Mega-Manipulation. That is not a really easy to use power. Especially considering the success costs for the difficulties of commands.
