              For 2E, I'd probably go with Transformation 5, which requires Quantum 4. Give the power a 1-point tag to drop Dr. Banner's Intellect, Presence, and Manipulation to 1, while raising his Might and Stamina to 5. Give him a -1 tag to make the change based on getting angry/calm. Give it a -1?/-2? tag to limit him to a single alternate form (he can Max Out to briefly overcome the limitation and become a variant Hulk). Add a +1 or +2 Duration tag to make the power last minutes or hours. Finish by giving the power the +1 Extra Traits tag as many times as you have XP to spare. The base power will give him 5 dots from a laundry list of Hulk-appropriate traits, and each purchase of the Extra Traits tag will give him 5 more. Taking the tag the max of 5 times gives him 30 dots to spread around his Hulk form, which should be plenty.

              He'll likely need Mega-Might and Mega-Stamina 5, as well as a few levels of Durability. Most of the Might based Mega-Edges would be appropriate as well, as are a good chunk of the Stamina Mega-Edges. The Hulk is technically highly acrobatic, but this never seems to come up, so just give him Mega-Dexterity 1. He has super keen senses (he can perceive astral forms), so add on a sensory Mega-Edge. I don't think he actually needs to take the Growth power, since Transformation presumably covers him becoming really big.

              Raising Quantum to 4 should be 48 points.
              Transformation 5 would be 60 points.
              Five Extra Trait tags cost 60 points.
              Assume the single form limitation and Duration enhancement tags cancel out.
              Swapping mental/social stats for physical and limiting the change to an anger trigger cancel out.
              End total is 168 points.

                      Thinking things through further, the Transformation power wouldn't let me bypass the Quantum limit on Mega-Stats. It also doesn't allow you to take physical Mega-Edges, despite letting you take the Body Modification, Durability, and sensory Mega-Edges. I'm ignoring that bit and letting Dr. Banner take physical Mega-Edges via Transformation. Here is Dr. Bruce Banner as a (mostly) legal starting character:

                      Attributes-
                      Might: 2
                      Dexterity: 2
                      Stamina: 2
                      Intellect: 5
                      Cunning: 3
                      Resolve: 3
                      Presence: 2
                      Manipulation: 2
                      Composure: 4


                      Paths-
                      Origin Path: Suburbia
                      Role Path: Technology Expert
                      Society Path: Freelance Nova (adapted from Freelance Psion)


                      Skills-
                      Close Combat: 1
                      Enigmas: 1
                      Humanities: 1
                      Integrity: 1
                      Larceny: 1
                      Medicine: 1
                      Pilot: 1
                      Science: 5 (Specialty: Radiation)
                      Survival: 1
                      Technology: 2
                      Skill Trick: R&D Expert

                      Edges-
                      Fame or Alternate Identity (from being a Nova)
                      Big Hearted
                      Patron (1)
                      Lightning Calculator
                      Covert (1)
                      Danger Sense

                      Quantum: 4 (48 xp)
                      Transformation: 5, -2 tag (only turns into the Hulk), -1 tag (only when angry), +1 tag (Attribute swap), +2 tag (Duration), +3 tag (Extra Traits). (96 xp)
                      6 xp left over

                      Hulk-
                      Attributes-
                      Might: 5
                      Dexterity: 2
                      Stamina: 5
                      Intellect: 1
                      Cunning: 3
                      Resolve: 3
                      Presence: 1
                      Manipulation: 1
                      Composure: 4

                      Mega-Might: 4
                      Mega-Crush (1), Mega-Lifting (1), Quantum Leap (1), Shockwave (1), Thunderclap (1)
                      Mega-Dexterity: 1
                      Mega-Stamina: 3
                      Dense Flesh (2), Regeneration (1)
                      Durability: 3
                      Detection (astral beings): 1 [taken despite lacking Mega-Cunning, as per the Transformation power's text allowing you to take sensory Mega-Edges without letting you take Mega-Cunning]

                      With more xp, go for Quantum +1, Mega-Might +1, Mega-Stamina +2, Durability +2, Mega-Speed (1), Rapid Strike (1), Immunity (1), and Dense Flesh +3. Dr. Banner could eventually get Mega-Intellect and related crafting Mega-Edges.
