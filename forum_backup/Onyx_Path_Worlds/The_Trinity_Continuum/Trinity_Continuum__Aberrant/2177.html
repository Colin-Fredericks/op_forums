                  This may be cheating.


                  My favorite Abberant game was not, technically speaking, only an Abberant game. In the main Abberant game, the players were a gang of Project Utopia investigators, who were gradually drawn into a Teragen plot to recreate the Hammersmith Device (which was based on alien technology, as it turned out) in order to forcibly empower a lot more novas. The players were opposed, because they had discovered the existence of Taint and were terrified of the notion of billions of people going full nova before a way to actually stop them from going equally insane had been developed. They did not think the Teragen's "become more inhuman" plan was a solid one.

                  Along the way, they kept running into psychically-infused journals kept by a team of Aeon Society adventurers, and whenever they did, we stopped playing Abberant and started playing Adventure. The players swapped over to their Adventure characters, and ran through one of the past situations. We had an agreement that players wouldn't create time paradoxes this way, or if they did, it would be due to the journal writer getting some of the details a little bit wrong.

                  "Remember - popcorn can feel pain."

                      I've got one of those best/worse stories to tell. My old GM used to take every game and turn it up a notch. He even did it with 1st edition Exalted but that is not the topic.                               I've got two favourite Aberrant games, both kinda still ongoing although on hiatus at the moment, 1st is a solo game ST'd by my wife and the other is actually the first Aberrant game my wife started god knows how many years ago, right after I bought her the collectors edition of Aberrant. The latter has mostly had the same cast although some players have, due to moving away and life in general, had long breaks in it.

                              For the solo game I played my first, and only to date, amnesiac with my original character sheet being a blank A4. I awoke from an alley after obviously been thrown down from the top story of a 4 story building, evidence supported that I had been thrown through a window across the alley to hit the neighboring building and then fell on the ground. Of course I only found most specifics after the fact as I was busy escaping my attackers, presumably at least, and finding some help as I was injured. Long, long story short very slowly I started to piece together who I was a bit awkwardly as the person I mistook as my wife was my mistress and it took me a while to find my wife and child. I also seemed to be a doctor working for a pharmaceutical company, virologist to be specific, working in research for biological weaponry and human enhancements through microbes. All of this had my morals rattling, mistress, family, totally unethical research using human subjects, mostly of the unwilling kind. My main project seemed to center around a girl in her teens who was kept in high security facility under lock and key and heavily medicated as she had manifested great psychic potential. I came to feel sorry for her and started in secret to seek ways to help her.
                              While doing all this, still unsure of many, many odd facts about myself emerging here and there, like why did I speak perfect and fluent Russian etc. After months, may even be a year, of play I thought I had a pretty good picture of whom I was. Reconciling my relationships to my family, mistress and the young nova whom I first met right after my amnesia and whom had helped me get here. Again long story short I got a divorce from my wife but still kept probably better relations with her than I had before my amnesia as well as became a lot closer to my daughter while I all but completely broke up with my mistress, managing again to have amiable relation with her after wards, and for a short time dated my saviour, who got pregnant and incidentally gave birth in a matter of weeks to my second daughter, a child now of two Novas and so of seemingly great potential but alas that relationship couldn't last for long either. But again I managed a clean break up.
                              In the midst of all this my apparent first assailants managed to get on my trail again and in a shocking revelation I managed to find out that they were not really attacking me all, in fact the one I had for all this time thought to be the bog bad of the chronicle was my own brother. This revelation lead me to find out that the identity I thought was mine was actually a cover identity that I had taken to infiltrate the pharmaceutical company. I had stolen my own research from there and infected myself with it during my escape, so that it couldn't be taken from me.

                              My powers during this chronicle have been the ability to control microbes, viral and bacterial, regenerate, some mega-stamina and intellect as well as perception. The Disease power with re-lapse addition and my whole body is actually a giant viral colony hidden underneath my skin. All this has meant that for most of the chronicle I have been struggling to keep my viral powers in check so as to not infect everyone around me, which has been especially hard in cases I have been wounded as that has splattered the infecting agents around the scene. For all I know I might be one of the deadliest Novas in my ST's world able to cause countless casualties from the diseases that fester within me should I merely loose control or just forgot to clean the scene where I was wounded drawing all the escaped microbes, and likely most microbes in the vicinity, back to my body. Each new disease that I draw into me adds to and strengthens the infection I carry.

                              Well I didn't manage to make that story short after all, but at least it's considerably shorter.

                              The other major game for me is the first Aberrant I have ever played in. My character there was from a religious family even though he himself was not overly religious and had moved into London for college. Pretty average student in general until he got into an accident that he survived by teleporting away and sprouting angelic wings along with an appearance to match. This character has done a lot of self searching since after he became such an obvious angel he couldn't really go to classes or even leave the apartment except by teleporting out and taking wing. So he has gone from an angsty rebellious young angel to a capable leader and a future Marvel of the Teragen. In this game we've had unfortunate attention of the Directive, I still know pretty much jack about them though, and my fellow player and his best friend (an NPC) were both at the start of the game prisoners and research subjects of an organization, presumably the Directive, and this has colored much of our perception about Utopia etc. The M-R facilities and the help and research received there sounded a bit too close for comfort of the place they had barely escaped so we have had rather negative thoughts about Utopia all along. The fact that my Angel desires to master his powers and enjoy his life as what he has become instead of fighting others battles the Terats vision of personal growth and discovery suits him fantastically. Especially after they explained Taint and Chrysalis to him, both pretty alien concepts to my char as he literally has from several years of playing managed to accumulate I think 1-3 temporary taint all together. Still the ability to be in control of your own evolution and the chance to turn Taint into something productive and empowering instead of instabilizing and sanity shattering kinda sounds sensible.

                              I wont go into more detail about the latter game as it is still ongoing and likely to continue and besides this post is long enough as it is.

                                  A few years ago, as an experiment, I played a Process 418 Nova with Pretercognition and Temporal Manipulation. He was a Blue Circle transport thief from Australia who fled to Luna after he erupted. While there he got pulled into the Darkness Revealed story where he pretended to be Clairsentient. Eventually he was outed to the other PCs who kept him around because he was pretty useful and nobody would have believed they didn't notice him earlier. Ditto Aeon. Ultimately he went through the Ascent into Light story where the PC group makes its way to the UN to alert humanity to the coming Chromatic incursion.

                                  Kevin wasn't stupid, but he was direct, a little provincial and had the Big Mouth flaw (which occasionally resulted in lost Willpower and a well-chewed tongue). In any case, right after we teleported down to the midst of the UN hearings, our poised, charismatic executive did not get to calmly explain the impending invasion before Kevin blurted out "Invisible alien frog people are comin' to kill us all!"

                                      In the (currently suspended) homegame, my character Abyss (a pre-terat) had a personality flaw he’d acquired after his eruption that removed all social skill or restraint. I never took this as an actual flaw or an aberration, but people just assumed IC that this was a taint-relate issue. Anyway, during a night when he and the other characters were hanging out, one player’s character, Lilith (who erupted with severe goddess complex) made the 5th of her several (and increasingly less subtle) passes at my character. Each time this occurred, I would make a roll to see if he could tell what she was trying, and for the 5th time IN A ROW I completely botched! Totally missing the point of her advances I carried on the conversation as if she were talking about something mundane.
                                      At this point another player (as Leon, who was completely sane) attempted to “help” Lilith by advising her to phrase her advances in terms of an anthropological experiment where they could “observe the baselines in their natural habitat.” When she attempted this I actually rolled 2 successes and replied, “You wanna to go have sex? Why didn’t you just say so?” They stopped me before I could drop trough in public and sheer ridiculousness ensued.
