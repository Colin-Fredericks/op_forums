    Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
    Frankly, Mega-Intelligence is probably the aspect of the setting that gives the most credence to the Null Manifesto: I'll argue up one side of the street and down the other that a Nova that “merely” has Quantum Powers is still essentially human from a societal standpoint, and ought to fall under the same rules and regulations as everyone else — with the possible exception of laws that take into account the fact that many of them are lethal weapons. Physical Mega-Attributes are no different.

    But when you start getting into Social and Mental Mega-Attributes, you start getting into a class of novas that arguably don't think or behave like humans anymore. If they're capable of designing whole new governmental systems for a society that work brilliantly but that baselines can't comprehend (a la the Nova that's going to restructure China at some point in the near future), are they still something that's subject to a political system that was designed with baselines in mind? If Mega-Intelligent philosophers are capable of seeing and compensating for the unintended consequences that tend to stymie baseline philosophers, a case could be made for putting them in charge as the philosopher-kings of Plato's Republic; and a key principle of American Declaration of Independence (“We hold these truths to be self-evident, that all men are created equal”) comes into doubt.

    It's a very disturbing notion.
    That’s where the Transcendence get you. You can pretend you have super strength using an Ironman suit, or fire quantum blasts with a Q-gun, but you can’t change the way you think. It’s not “he have 15 phds” it’s more like “he can say what you are thinking by the micro expressions in your face, before you even understand your thought” or “she is reading the internet... all internet... give her a minute”

        I'm with you on that, those are the biggest reasons for a nova to accept the Null Manifesto. Quantum powers potentially could eventually be copied with science, same with the physical mega-attributes. But the others are much much harder. The only way I can think of scientifically for a baseline to get Mega-Intelligence would be fusing with an AI, a step that quite a few people I've talked to say would mean they aren't human anymore.

        Related to this I really wonder what the Transhumanism community is doing in all of this, especially ones that are actively working on technology, even more so ones that are working on technology that isn't mentioned in the timeline yet like life extension (for example, the SENS foundation led by Aubrey de Grey). It's entirely possible that none of them erupt, especially with only 2000 novas worldwide. Personally I think they would end up as something similar but not the same as the Teragen, namely pushing for research into making it possible for baseline humans to become novas voluntarily.

        Going back to a further post, I am also highly excited for a Daedalus League campaign. Personally I'm a fan of Molecular Manipulation type powers, perhaps a nova with that that can either move through space on their own or that has an ally with Warp or similar. Then they work on terraforming a planet, as the best place to start is in making an appropriate atmosphere, which is just molecules.

        Tangent time. Personally I've always seen a lot of overlap between certain types of powers, the big difference being in how you use them. Molecular Manipulation and any power that targets a single type or class of molecule, Gravity Control along with Time Manipulation and Spatial Mastery (as gravity affects space/time and all three are closely related as shown in General Relativity), and so on.
