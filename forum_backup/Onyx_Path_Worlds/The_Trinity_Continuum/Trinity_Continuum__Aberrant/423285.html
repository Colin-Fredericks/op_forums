      I don't know what you mean by a "node brain".
      So in this scenario, a psionic survey team is contacted, star-trek style by a mysterious entity of great power and seemingly benevolent, they are going to let it on board and team up with it? Exactly how many of these psions paid any attention to Aberrant hysteria or adventure tropes growing up in the late 2100's? Because the whole thing could fall apart right there. Expect more incoming fire than incoming replies.
      But, i can't talk, i tried this once before myself and so i must warn you strongly against it - if you are a player coming into another's game you should warn them too. But, no testimony i give is going to better the mechanism to prove it to yourself.
      First and most important question is; why do this as opposed to adding a character better balanced for the system? The Stalwart of Adventure! would be a better ruleset to utilize, if you have to do it this way, simply for balance. I don't know what would be needed to survive in deep-space like you suggest, but a decently crafted pod might sustain a mighty stalwart for quite some time in the void - Captain America style. If you can do that, do that instead - the rules will release a sigh of relief. If you can create a character free of the need of Quantum distinction from the rest of the party, do that first - our characters are roles, not power-sets.
      Second question is; where did this 'Nova' come from? Why are they in deep space to be encountered there and how long have they been there for? If the answer to the last question is greater than a year or so, they will need social and mental adaptions for the isolation and sensory deprivation. You want Mega Charisma's Autonomy (Forceful Personalities p90), something like Mega-Wit's Unfazeable from Brainwaves (p52), whatever your ST wants you to take for physically surviving in space - if longer than a year, maximized mega-stamina might be needed to hold your breath (or just not breathe) for long enough. So, maybe a physical alteration, or some inexpensive way to fend off the temperature extremes like Armor. If you were under propulsion in space, you'll need to explain that - flight will do. If you were headed somewhere through space, then some level of mega-perception and maybe mega-intelligence would keep you on course - but even the shortest distances would be a nightmare journey without machine instruments to discern distances and time.
      Again, the ST will have the final say on what you need to survive their vision of vacuum - maybe add Sensory Shield into the mix or something i'm not thinking of. Oh, and of course Taint Resistance (Player's Guide p76). You need it or you are guaranteed to be a lost cause before you begin, with Taint equal to your starting Quantum - but i think it's bundled for the suggestion below.
      The best balance for 2008 era Novas in 2120 are the 'Asia Ascendant' web-supplement's Low Quantum versions on page 72, but rather than me repeat that here, go out and give it a hit where-ever it's posted these days. Follow those rules, i can't emphasize this enough - beyond that lies ruin and despair for the game balance. But stick to the low Quantum rules and be prepared to be hunted until the end of your days for showing your face and you can get a few games worth of tolerable cross-over, i'm sure. It's worth noting that the Asia Ascendant rules still aren't as preferable as the Stalwart system from Adventure!. But they avoid inspiration points if that's the desired result.
      I'm not looking at the prices or doing any math, but i'll bet you can't afford the character with those resources. This is intentional - the kind of Nova you seem to be talking about is a league above the level of Nova that can safely cross-over with a party of Chamber Psions without scarring their sense of self-value. Your mileage may vary as the player of said Nova, obviously, but it would be a cruel trick to pull on an unsuspecting ST. I did it to myself and still consider it a cruel trick.

      If the nova in question wasn't a player, this scenario would be rather more appealing as an adventure for the psions. If you are their first space aberrant and you come to the airlock with a smile and a hard-luck story - you may irrepairably damage the universe (read: 2120 setting) for them in the process. What dividends can you yield that surpass an encounter with one of the possibilities "free-floating space nova" could entail? Think about this, because it's a dramatic departure from likely player expectations if i have the details correct. If you can spin a fun tale on the way in, it could be forgiveable, if still jarring - but you have to work with the ST for that.
