        Originally posted by IanWatson View Post
        Canonically, novas with psychic powers exist, they're just one of the following:
        • Novas with &quot;psychic powers&quot;: They think they're using psychic powers, but are actually using Mega-Mental predictive Edges, telecommunication, or Mega-Social manipulative Edges.
        • &quot;Novas&quot; with psychic powers: They're actually psiads, but no one yet has the knowledge to distinguish them from novas, so they get lumped in.

        Even if Project Pandora technically understands that psiads are a different thing, even they'd be hard-pressed to distinguish between the above two groups. Except maybe one of them sets off quantum detectors and the other doesn't. Outside of Pandora, few people know, and few people care. If you have powers, you're a nova.

        (Although imagine if your team got taken captive and outfitted with quantum-nullifying collars, having the actual psychic still able to use her powers would be quite the ace up your sleeve.)

        If you don't like that setup, it's easy enough to do one of the following:
        • Reskin the existing powers. The powers that say you can predict what someone's thinking based on an understanding of their psychology and acute observation of microexpressions? Just pretend it says they're actually reading the target's mind. Done.
        • A lot of psychic powers already exist in Aeon. Just buy them as nova powers instead.

                            So a bit late to the party here, but there's a fairly interesting change between 1e and 2e I noticed in the manuscripts that I don't think anyone here's mentioned.

                            Ceastus Pax is a completely different character now.

                            Literally.

                            In Aberrant 1e, he was named Shelby Eisenfaust, Erupted in the first year of N-Day, and was your standard Glory Hog Dickbag Superman.

                            In Aberrant 2e, his name is Sam Williams and he Erupted forty years prior to N-Day. The two paragraph blurb we got for him is that he Erupted in a car accident and carried around some survivor's guilt for living when his family didn't, particularly as his new powers couldn't save them. He's also got more than a little guilt over sitting on his powers and doing nothing with them, particularly when he was "brought up to believe that power brings responsibility."


                            As a change, this is something I definitely approve of. "What if Superman was an asshole?" is a question that's been asked and answered and it's more than a bit tiring to see at this point.
