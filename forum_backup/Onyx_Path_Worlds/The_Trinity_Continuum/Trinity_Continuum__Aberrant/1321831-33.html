                    My opinion about the way they divided the 3 Social Attributes:
                    Presence is first impact and direct effort, the target do things for you only because you told to. Sargent PS use to give orders, a Models to ask for favours, things like that.
                    Manipulation is about convincing the target that it’s a good thing. Conman tell people to guide them money because they will get money back, Lawers convince the judge the client is guilt or not.
                    Composure is about be cool, resist temptations, convince people after long discussions, stubbornness. Guards don’t move after bribes, Leaders hold followers after years of propaganda against them.

                    The 3 original ones were scrambled a little and reshaped on the new 3 ones.
                    Appearance went basically to Presence, as the thing you use to go straight to business in social situations, a little part went to the “Coolness” of Composure and a bit to the idea of distracting people of Manipulation.
                    Manipulation basically keep it to itself, except for the direct impact that went to Presence and some timing to
                    Charisma is decided basically among the 3 as it was about talking, listening and appearing interested.

                                            Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
                                            The TC Scale rules were designed specifically to reflect the cinematic reality that lets things like Tony going up against the Winter Soldier without innate Mega-Strength to happen — especially since Tony was wielding his armor's gauntlet, which according to the logic of cinematic reality (as opposed to the ruthless logic of realistic physics) should give him access to an least a measure of his armor's Mega-Strength. Ditto with his armor experiments in the first movie: his survival of accidents that realistically should have killed him aren't evidence of Mega-Stamina; they're just good old-fashioned cinematic damage. Extremis potentially changes things; but not as much as one might think. I would argue that it granted him the Mega-Edge that lets you interface directly with machines, a healing factor, and maybe a dot each of Mega-Intelligence and Mega-Cunning (which also would have been his first dot in Mega-Cunning).

                                            But more importantly, those Mega-Attributes and Mega-Edges would arguably be Q-Tech, not Nova powers, where the distinction matters. Tony could have applied the Extremis process to someone else according to the rules given in TC:Ab's Technology chapter, even to a Talent or baseline (though that would have required him to put the effort into shifting it from Q-Tech to Nova Tech, and the baseline would have had to cope with Flux); you can't really do that with Nova powers.

                                            I'm also a Tony Stark fanboy. But to me, what makes him awesome isn't that he's a “dark star” or “stealth Nova”, but rather it's that he showcases just how much technology can do. In Aberrant terms, what makes him cool is that he has just one (very subtle) superhuman ability (Mega-Intelligence, and maybe one or two related Mega-Edges), but he leverages the heck out of them until he's among the most powerful heroes on Earth.

My Stark would have at least a dot of Mega-Stamina and some defensive Mega-edges. Not to mention his contributions in rounds 1 and 3 vs Thanos...

                                            Also, one of my happiest surprises when digging through the manuscripts, my likely happiest finding was that Novas - while way more powerful - weren't just better than everyone at everything. Kind of have a solar Exalted feel now. I was making one of my old fallbacks that I like to create in any new system - a slick gambler and prestidigitator called Johnny Prestige and LO AND BEHOLD he had to use mega-edges to do the things that luck, telekinesis, domination and premonition did last time around. It's made such a more interesting character. Very excited to build some of my old favorites, try a few intellectual property ports (Hola Hancock and Alex Mercer from Prototype) and some setting specific new friends.
