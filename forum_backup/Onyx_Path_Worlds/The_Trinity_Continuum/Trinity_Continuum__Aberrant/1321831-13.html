                                    While I do like novas who basically look human I do like them to have some sort of "what?" features - features that usually come from something that is mechanically an 1e Aberration or I guess a 2e Transformation.

                                    My thought is that maybe either there can be a mechanical way to get a Transformation without the resulting Flux OR maybe if a player chooses a Transformation and then later gets a Flux-imposed one the one he already has retroactively counts. For mechanical reasons rather than purely fluff ones.

                                    I kind of like the idea that Transformations can be bought and when they are bought they provide some benefit, small though it may be, as compared to when its a Flux infused transformation, which is almost entirely negative.

                                    Another way to go about this might be to have a sort of power category that gives some small effects that can be purchased, with these effects leading to mechanical benefits similar to Transformations.

                                                        I was thinking of two potential timeline points.

                                                        One is that the the Daedalus League works with the UN/NASA/EU in the removal of much of Earth's orbiting space debris. The materials gathered in this operation are utilized in the expansion of the first Earth Orbital Station, which in time comes to be known as Clarke Station.

                                                        The second is about a nova with nature powers. A nova with nature powers goes on a grand spree of reforestation throughout North America. That is, to the amusement of some and the annoyance of others, wherever he goes hundreds of thousands of trees soon follow - taking root and growing to maturity overnight. By the time he basically stops the forests of America have regrown to levels not seen since the late 1600s. He is called an eco-terrorist by some and an ally to nature by others. At the end he disappears just as quietly as he appeared.
