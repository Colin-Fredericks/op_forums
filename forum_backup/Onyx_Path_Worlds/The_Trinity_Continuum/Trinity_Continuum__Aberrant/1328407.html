                  One point the devs made in an interview was that, if someone finds out about the Medusa Protocols and makes that public, there’s plenty of ammo there to indicate that Utopia is totally morally compromised, especially if you only get bits and pieces of the info.

                  Someone who got some partial information off a deeply hidden Utopia server might even think that there actually WAS a sterilization plot or that Utopia was going to just kill all the Novas the moment they step out of line, instead of try to contain and then rehabilitate them.

                  Personally, I’m inclined to think that one of the few things that might totally derail the Aberrant War, instead of just pushing it a few years into the future, is finding a way to help Burano invent Quantakinesis much, much earlier. That might actually help reduce Flux and even Transcendence, which could help keep Novas from going Aberrant, negate their powers, and might even end up with some kind of drug you could take to stop becoming a disinterested god.

                  Granted Divis Mal probably wouldn’t like it, but, honestly, he lost control of this situation about ten years ago.

                      Originally posted by DeathChipmunk View Post
                      Am I right that the whole "nobles are sterile" storyline has been dropped in 2e? Not a thing at all? Seems so to me, but want to confirm.
                      Correct.

                      By making the Aeon Society the good guys, that had a cascading effect on the setting:

                      Aeon are good -> no nova sterilization conspiracy -> Slider doesn't get assassinated -> Corbin and Rousseau don't form the Aberrants -> T2N happens instead -> Aberrants form as a sub-group of Teragen

                      Deciding that the Fireman was going to be our first big example of Sacrifice was a second big change, but didn't have the same ripple effect.
