But there are more than a few nations whose leaders are less than rational. I could definitely see places like Pakistan, Iran and Saudi Arabia (countries not known for respecting minority or equal rights) or transnational religio-political movements like ISIS turning against novas en masse if the religious leaders of those countries or organizations decided that novas were "servants of the Devil." The practical value of the novas wouldn't be able to outweigh the religious fanatical desire of "purging the Devil's minions" in these places. Drazosh is quite right that countries with rational leaders would bend over backwards to accommodate their novas. My point is that there are more than a few countries whose religious fanatics would demand that the novas should at the least be kicked out or at worst captured and executed "to fulfill God's will" and never mind the immense benefits they could bring to their nations. Even if the leaders of such countries saw the benefits of having novas under their sway, if the majority of the population was fanatically opposed to novas, that opposition would make accommodating novas politically untenable.

                                    I was kind of surprised to see the numbers for religious populations in 2015 - i thought there was more secularism out there honestly. So there will definitely be faithful religious Novas, perhaps even more reliably than patriotic nationalist Novas. But one would hope that both faiths make enough noise about peace for Nova adherrants to not go medieval on us. I think any faith claiming the Nova standing in the aisle is a false idol, while holding up their own ancient human traditions may find themselves in a race they can't win - i see new dogma and tenets being a fascinating avenue of debate amongst theologists post n-day, perhaps even hearing a bit from newly erupted in the exploration.
                                    -shrug-

                                    While certain outliers will exist, nearly all modern nations will try to woo their Novas to stay rather than provoke them. At least one serious revolution behind a Nova figurehead would be happening in many of the nastiest tyrannies about the world. Some tension may even be created solely by Novas out for the 'lolz' - humiliating whole baseline authorities with seemingly little effort. But after a single month, Aeon steps in to negotiate for the UN - probably quicker in the modern speed of news cycles - after a month no more than 80 or so eruptions have occured across the world - less if Aeon acts quicker. I could see a few countries executing a number of Nova citizens in military actions against them - but this won't hold back the tide for long, driving new eruptees underground and out of sight if they decide to stay at all.
                                    If anywhere gets badly hit by N-Day, it might very well be the US - the country has really made a mess of its international standing over the past decade or so. An international consortium of Novas might take action against their perceived oppressors - while some of these conglomerates might sanction violence, i feel a large enough group would tend toward peaceful restructuring of global US hegemony, at least at first. Eventually there will almost certainly be a post N-Day attack on the US - but for all their power and money, they simply can't risk the global community of Novas getting any more fed up with them and will be hesitant to engage their (still) fearsome hard power.
                                    The US isn't entirely alone here, but the disapproval ratings they face in these last few years is kind of unprecedented - France, Russia and Britain may also see blow-back parrallel to the US - due to their colonial ways. The trend of international recruits making their way into ISIS/ISIL could easily parrallel a rise in violent nova activism against US interests from across the world. Unlike ISIS/ISIL, the beneficiary nation(s) to all these Novas would probably prosper - it could be a stronger clarion call than that of the Zurich Accord if left long enough.
                                    Frighteningly, this kind of accumulation of disenfranchised Novas into the middle east seems to me to have been one of the ultimate causes of the aberrant war in 2049, but for it to already begin in 2015 without the early optimistic years of 1998-2008 to set a solid foundation from. The risk of escalation is much more immediate and dire if this occurs.
                                    -shiver-
                                    Elsewhere, China will almost certainly see the first Nova joining the Peoples Army, simply based on the numbers involved. I can't recall what happens to it in the canon setting, but Taiwan will probably see the first national nova military (soft power) action within the first few years - leaving China one-up against the US in the post N-Day pacific theatre. Soon afterwards, the Himalayas will see tensions between China and India as India starts operations in Kashmir - all soft power endeavours in the early days - made very effective by Nova contributions. The entire power structure of the world is going to shift to China and India quickly - because there is no chance of any other country matching their rates of new Novas and the global nova sentiment against US hard power preventing the US from effectively containing the eastern powers.
