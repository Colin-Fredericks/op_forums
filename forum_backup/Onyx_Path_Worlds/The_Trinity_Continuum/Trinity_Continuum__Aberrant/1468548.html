                                                      You reflexively spend Quantum points to Regenerate so a Quantum free zone wouldn't stop them unless it also inhibited a Nova from spending points.

                                                      Quantum Drain would allow you to take all of their Quantum points which would stop the Regen.

                                                      Quantum Shutdown is a trickier thing though. It says it will shutdown quantum powers, but Regeneration isn't a power it's a Mega-Edge. While it costs Quantum to use it isn't in the powers section.
                                                      I have no idea as to the official ruling on that so it's either post in Ask the Devs and see if one of them knows or leave it up to the Storyteller.
                                                      In my game I would say no, Quantum Shutdown wouldn't stop Regeneration but that is just my personal opinion on it.

                                                      However the easiest way to kill a Nova with Regeneration is keep hitting them. Once they run out of Quantum points they stop Regenerating and die. The method to recover Quantum points require a scene change to work so as long as any damage is being applied they will run out of points sooner or later.
