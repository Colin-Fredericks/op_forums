    Alternate N-Day Events

    09-12-2019, 04:12 PM
    So I may mess with things going for an alternate version of N-Day causing Novas to erupt world wide.

    For example a big one people mention a lot here is the CERN activation causing eruptions.

    What other ones would you propose and any comments on any mentioned here are welcome. Its a discussion and brainstorm thread.

    So I like the idea of a big mysterious Solar Flare EMPs the Earth into a big blackout, it doesn't cause the dark age but it causes a lot of devastation and probably a recession. And the catch is something caused the Quantum Solar Flare, so either secret Earther Mad Scientists/Conspiracies (like Doctor Primoris) or Aliens shoot a device at the Sun and Novas are the result, and maybe even life on Mars...

    Time Travel caused N-Day, either the present activating a time travel device or the Future or Past touching the present causing a Quantum Backlash.

    Then, of course, a secret project, maybe during World War II or earlier, activated a sleeper Gene that cause Nova eruptions after a few generations. Maybe even the first batches of Nuclear weapons bathed portions of the world in ambient Quantum that was benign at first but once those exposed to it had children with others exposed to it they became potential novas in later generations. The ambient quantum could have been more widespread than anyone imagined so maybe the Manhattan project resulted in undetectable Quantum saturation reaching from Alaska to Central America, and Japan and Bikini Atoll saturated Asia and the Pacific. This could mean Europe, Africa and South America at first has the least amount of Novas Erupting as they spread from North America and the Pacific.

    Other Ideas?

                      I really like the idea of a science project (be it the activation of a particle accelerator or the use of a nuclear weapon) could set off N-Day. The whole finding of something mysterious underground and the activation of it also interests me. I also really like the idea that an asteroid full of Quantum-imbued materials (Quantinite, hehe, or Quantium) and energies passing by and causing N-Day.

                      (The fact that I not only enjoy but approve of variant ideas that take N-Day out from the hands of Divis Mal is interesting t o me as I really like the character and his power levels. hehe)

                      One of the thoughts I had was to take the whole history of the modern day is based on the presence of an Inspired past. So human presence in space is a bit more nuanced then the public knows about. That is,a number of countries have secret ground to space shuttles (maybe built on Inspired tech and so they can repair it but not replace it) and a few orbital stations of the rotating torus shape. The point of this is that there is a destroy the world organization who managed to commandeer both a former Soviet space shuttle and space station that fell to disuse with the collapse of said nation. Over the last few decades they have been working on a project that when activated would basically destroy everything. Thankfully intelligence came in informing the UN and member countries of the project and the attempt. So a multinational special forces team is dispatched to said location to stop them. Unfortunately they can't totally stop the operation but what they can do is shift the energies outputted by said machine so that instead of destroying all things it simply passes through 'harmlessly'. The operation is a success and while much is kept hush hush some information about it gets out, leading to fame for some.

                      A few years later, 2018, and the energies unleashed by the machine interact with something on Earth which causes a massive burst of Quantum radiation that starts N-Day.

                      Another thought for this is that when the energy wave erupts it causes the presence of Inspired upon the Earth but then a few years later it interacts with something that leads to the presence of full blown Novas. The idea that the world has low powered 'heroes' and fully super powered 'heroes' is brilliant to me.

                      A related idea is that some or all of the player novas could have been a part of the team that was sent to the orbital installation held by the terrorists. Their presence at the event could supercharge them - which could explain why they gain powers at a much faster rate then most others and such.

                                      I'm rather partial to this idea, that the eruption of each Nova causes further eruptions in those who have the potential for it. Further, that the very presence of Novas increases the likelihood of more Novas coming into existence.

                                      This process could play out imperceptibly over time, as the presence of individual Novas steadily increase the Quantum saturation in the environment. In the Adventure! era, Novas are rare, but Stalwarts less so. Their modest output of Quantum still contributes, until full blown Novas begin to emerge, in small numbers at first. The most likely to erupt are those closest to existing Novas (or proto-Novas), their proximity causing them to develop power first. (This, incidentally, also neatly explains why superpowered beings always seem to be in close proximity to each other; the effects of Quantum make it almost inevitable).

                                      Through the 60s, 70s, and 80s, Novas start to pop up more and more, to the extent that they cease being mere rumors. But their numbers are low enough - and the Novas reclusive enough from official scrutiny or study - that the cause and mechanics of them aren't understood.

                                      Come the 21st century, and Quantum saturation reaches a tipping point. Suddenly, the background Quantum is high enough - and absorption entrenched enough over years and generations - that Novas erupt in the hundreds, and then the thousands. The more erupts, the more those eruptions cascade into other eruptions. A quantum domino effect, aided by the degree to which both the environment and population have already been primed to Quantum energies.
