                            I think you really need to decide whether you want to do a story or an adventure, because they aren't the same thing. You can do a story. Just write your bit of fan-fiction about Corbin, Slider and Butt-Floss Girl in their love/lust triangle set in the Nova age. Then publish it on fanfiction.net and you'll probably get some readers/fans.

                            But if you want to do an adventure, you need to make sure you've set plenty of hooks for your players. You set initial conditions and then react to what your player do with them. You'll need to be prepared for your players to do stuff you didn't want or expect. Maybe Slider gets kicked off T2M. Or Corbin gets arrested. Or Slider, Corbin and Butt-Floss Girl get killed because the PCs "solve" the issue by going full "murder hobo."
