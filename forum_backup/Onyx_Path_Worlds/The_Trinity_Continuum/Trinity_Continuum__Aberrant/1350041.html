    Superhero Conversions

    11-21-2019, 01:15 PM
    In an effort to keep myself fresh with the System, indulge my inner geek, and create new Paths, I am working on a number of conversions of pre-existing heroes to Trinity Continuum. I will mostly be doing Novas, but may perhaps get around to some Talents. Many of these will have Custom Paths that I will, eventually, try and compile.

    To start will be characters from vastly different ends of the power spectrum while both being decidedly superhuman.
    Jessica Jones (Marvel Cinematic Universe)
    Origin: Suburbia
    Role: Detective:
    Society: Alias Investigations
    Skills: Athletics 1, Close Combat 1, Culture 1, Empathy 3 (Lie Detection), Enigmas 4 (ST:Connecting the Dots, Patterns), Integrity 1, Larceny 3 (Sleight of Hand), Persuasion 1, Technology 1
    Attributes: Intellect 4, Might 2, Presence 3, Cunning 4, Dexterity 2, Manipulation 2, Resolve 3, Stamina 2, Composure 3 (Favored Approach: Might)
    Edges: Patron 2 (Trish Walker), Photographic Memory 2, Wealth 1, Swift, Fame 1 (Nova Template)
    Quantum: 2
    Transcendence: 1
    Mega-Attributes: Mega-Might 2, Mega-Stamina 2
    Mega-Edges: Dense Flesh 1
    Quantum Powers: Flight 1 (Restricted: Upwards only)
    XP Cost: 74
    Contacts: Trish Walker (Patron), Jeri Hogarth, Malcom Ducasse

    Superman (Clark Kent/Kal-El) (DC Extended Universe)
    Origin: Farm Life
    Role: Paragon of Virtue
    Society: Daily Planet, Justice League
    Skills: Aim 1, Athletics 1, Close Combat 2, Command 1, Culture 1, Humanities 3, Integrity 4, Persuasion 3, Survival 1
    Attributes: Intellect 2, Might 2, Presence 4, Cunning 1, Dexterity 2, Manipulation 2, Resolve 4, Stamina 4, Composure 4 (Favored Approach: Resistance)
    Edges: Adrenaline Spike, Alternate Identity 2, Artistic Talent 1, Big Hearted, Danger Sense, Fame 2, Wealth 1
    Quantum: 8
    Transcendence: 6
    Transformations: Feeding Requirement (Solar Radiation), Inhuman Beauty, Vulnerability (Kryptonite Radiation)
    Mega-Attributes: Mega-Might 8, Mega-Dexterity 4, Mega-Stamina 8, Mega-Presence 2, Mega-Composure 2
    Mega-Edges: Adaptation, Calming Composure, Durability 5 (Environmental), Dense Flesh 5, Immunity, Mega-Hearing, Mega-Scent, Mega-Vision, Mega-Speed, Spectrum Vision, Regeneration
    Quantum Powers: Flight 6, Quantum Attack 5 (Heat Vision - Range 2, Beam, Destructive), Quantum Attack 4 (Freeze Breath - Range 2, Entangle 2)
    XP Cost: 859
    Contacts: Lana Lang, Emil Hamilton, Lois Lane, Bruce Wayne/Batman
