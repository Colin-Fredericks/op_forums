        From memory of posts made 10 years ago or so, the bit in the APG about vampiric eufibre was put in with the Colony in mind. The Colony started out as the vampiric eufibre of several powerful elites. While eufibre is normally a mindless organism, it does take on the wearers quantum signature and when enough mental mega-atttributes get imprinted...during some battle, enough of the eufibre got in physical contact to cross a threshold. And the Colony coalesced. A literal colony of vampiric eufibre.

        That is what I remember us being told. Whether it was made up on the spot or planned since the APG, I don't know. It has some interesting implications:

        The Colony is not human. The Colony was never human. Aberrants mostly started out as humans and have human memories and concerns. Even second-generation Aberrants have humanity in its very recent ancestry. The Colony is no closer to humanity than the bacteria that lives in your guts.

        The Colony is not an aberrant. The Colony was never a nova/aberrant.

        The Colony was begotten in pure violence, not sex of any kind.

        The Colony started out very, very powerful with the quantum signatures of several powerful elites.

        The Colony may be an exception to the rule about attributes and mega-attributes. It may have only mega-attributes, making for a cold and different mind indeed.

        And presumably, the Colony still needs to feed....

                                                The Aeon era story has little room for Divis Mal to drop by, i know it well.
                                                But in the peculiar instance of playing tainted novas in 2120, i had the room, if i needed it. Plus, the fear of Divis Mal's return can serve as a motivator for Psions - his art and background in the original books leave such an impression. When i first started playing, i had a couple of players who would have loved to quip one-liners and empty clips into that fiend, however wasteful that would have been as a use of the character. My brother still runs him as a villain, however conflicted and sympathetic, but since i never ran Aberrant or Adventure - i suppose i just never got a chance to really explore the character as deeply as i might have liked to - so sequestering him out of 2120 and 'replacing' him with the colony never appealed to me.
                                                It could have felt natural for a 'Battleground' type of strategy-driven campaign to roll over KLG, invade Chrome-Prime and finally chase down the Colony prior to the Coalition Ark arriving, then move onto the Prime Threats, eventually including Mal. Mal is a kind of end-boss, to those who like to play that way.
                                                -shrug-
                                                The thoughts of how others have used/interpreted the character during his active eras have made this thread a very interesting read and has just aggravated the soreness i felt losing the character from 2120. Sure he's powerful, but the source and this thread make a good case for him being mostly harmless outside of a few rare demonstrations and too alien by the 2120s to concern himself with human pettiness. Post-humanism taken through to godlike extremes has a place in Sci-Fi, what was once Mal may have another new identity by then - only peripherally occupied by his roots in humanity at best. But, the universe is a big place, no need to remove the character entirely - so i was happy his ultimate fate was never really confirmed anywhere important.
