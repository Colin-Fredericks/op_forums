    One of my favorite Golden Age characters I made for Mutants & Masterminds was The Thunderbolt of Liberty, a Captain Marvel/SHAZAM homage who was a young boy from Philadelphia who'd found Franklin's key in an antique store and could use it to turn into an adult superhero by saying the magic phrase "With Liberty and Justice For All". This quickly developed into an elaborate backstory, with both Revolutionary War and Civil War versions of the character, and a series of inheritors that included a Western gunslinger, a Gaslight Era detective, and a WW1 flying ace, as well as a 1970s Brother Thunder and a modern day libertarian version who bordered on being Doctor Manhattan levels of ludicrously overpowered.

                        A mainstay of my various Mutants & Masterminds settings has been the UK's Project: Unicorn, a government/crown agency that originated with the much older Unicorn Chamber, tasked with dealing with paranormal, mystical and other mysterious happenings. (With WW2, they have a splinter group, Project: Lion, which is much more public and deals with the realm's superhuman community.) Since the Pulp Age, Unicorn has been lead by the mysterious "M", who is either Mycroft Holmes as a "brain in a jar" or Merlin still in his Crystal Cave, depending on my mood at the time. I use a more horror/dark fantasy version of Unicorn in the World of Darkness as the UK's counterpart to FBI-SAD and Task Force Zero. (At some point, I wrote them up as a Hunter: The Vigil conspiracy, complete with their unique Encryption powers, which allowed them to decipher puzzles, encrypt their own minds, and even cloak their existence from supernatural beings. I wish I could find my copy of it.)

                        As far as Britain's secret history, I also had Merlin's own secret circle of knights and champions, which included The Green Knight, Sir Ironside, the displaced 19th century time-traveler Sir MacHinery, and what was essentially the Demon Etrigan, among others.

                        I kept meaning to come up with an Elizabethan period group made up of the "real" inspirations for various Shakespeare characters (the exiled wizard, the haunted prince, the crossdressing swordswoman, the wise fool, etc.), but never managed to get around to it.

                                                Let’s talk about Aberrant 1e, as there is nothing about 2e yet.

                                                On 1e there is a power called Quantum Imprint that basically let you copy the powers of any other Nova. There is also Quantum Vampire that allows you to Steal the power for some time. Combine it to building tech with your powers and you have it. Also, quantum imprint don’t need much of touch to copy, in fact a drop of blood with the proper extra (and a flaw to keep it on 3rd level) is enough (in thesis you could copy by sensing his quantum signature). The system for power creation is quite flexible so you can grow any power as you wish if you have enough quantum to accumulate extras...

                                                About Brood, quite complicate situation, as in Marvel they are all interchangeable, a mutant (rogue) can copy a extraterrestrial power (miss marvel), so why not copy psychic powers or the throwing power of bullseye... but Aberrant by default is different.

                                                Captain America is a Stalwart by definition. He is on physical pick of everything and is quite immortal... not to mention references to his intellect and charisma. It’s all on reach for Stalwarts. He is a result of a super science experiment that turn regular people into something above, it’s not questionable if he is luck or anything, he is superior to a regular person and nobody can question it, so no Talent Cap.

                                                Aberrants on 1e started with 40 nova points, that could be used to buy basically everything, from powers and quantum (quite expensive) to attributes and abilities (now known as skills), what was something like 3 dots per Nova point for attributes and 5 for abilities. You can gat about 200 skill points or 120 attribute points, of course you can’t put any thing about 5, so you can mix and Mach and full basically all the dots on neutral sheet and keep a few points for backgrounds and specialities.
