    *Given that I don;t have the Aeon supplement for TC that has all the Psion rules, and working off of my knowledge of 1e Trinity rules.

    Thematically I see why they gave Psions/Psiads a preference for telepathy-based powers as they affect a consciousness which is itself a Noetic construct. Obviously, people who use Psi energy would have an easier time influencing such things as people's minds. However, that shouldn't mean that such powers should be barred forever from Novas. I can definitely see ways for novas to have a harder time with the whole thing; like having to buy multiple discreet powers instead of one single suite, needing higher quantum minimums to use any of them, making them more difficult rolls, etc. Since a nova is trying to brute-force hack a mind with quantum whereas a Psiad can affect the underlying system of "consciousness" it makes sense for it to be harder, but not off the table entirely.

    The clairsentience thing I can't justify as easily. Both sides are using two different methods and achieving the same results.

    And it raises some odd questions. Like If novas can't pull telepathy due to psi/quantum interactions, then why can electrokinetics manipulate electromagnetic radiation (an obvious quantum expression)?

                            I'd be careful in saying the psions are better than novas; after reading through the Aeon book, psions just can't do the kind of EXTREEEEEEME POWA a nova can until they're proxy-level.

                            It would be fair to say that psi and quantum powers have different areas of expertise, and the Trinity Continuum is writing a book about characters in that fiction as opposed to releasing generic superhero rules. Of the two 'loud and obvious' power types (Talents being more subtle and generally less powerful), Psions are very focused, whereas a nova can have as random a combination of powers as they like. (Literally, if they're directing their evolution via Chrysalis.)

                            How different Psiads will be, I don't know. But that's the comparison we can make right now.
