    One thing Impernious didn’t add, and make sense not adding, is a Mega-Edge as the Agility we made.

    Mega-Speed is enough to do the effect, you are that fast, not just because you move your legs, but because you can react super fast. In this case, the “running to get enhancement” should be eliminated completely.

    A Psion flying at speed 6 is fast, but he can’t dodge bullets, he just is accelerating really fast in one direction, same for a Jet Fighter, but a Speedster need to react super fast to do their trick, as they can run between people and dodge buildings coming at speed 6 in their directions.

                    RAW, you would need some careful applications of your increased movement and cover. Kind of a hitch in a turn based system that if you use your extreme movement and are in the open between turns you are functionally standing still when someone else has their turn, even if on your turns you were running at Super-sonic speed. Could probably stand to be a special use case under the current super-speed edge, or an additional MEdge. I'm thinking instead of just adding enhancement or scale or soft armor it would be neat to add a Complication to attacks equal to Speed Scale. Flexes a different part of the system, and could potentially dovetail nicely into Mega-Dex's use case of cancelling Complications. Or maybe if you have been moving a certain number of rangebands a turn you effectively have various levels of cover.

                    The specified rolls and usage are there for a reason, and there is an overarching principle of the rules being flexible, but if you are constantly having to bend the rules for something, there should probably just be a change to the rules.

                    I could also see this being an opportunity to use marvels, I mean Power Stunts, but again, if you keep finding yourself having to make the same exception over and over again you probably need a new rule.
