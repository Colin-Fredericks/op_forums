                                            I think Mateus is probably right on this. I expect that it's Technique (1), with no mention in its description of being able to be applied more than once (unlike, say, the Area power tag), to get around the problem of limiting the number of Techniques in a Power Suite by Quantum.

                                            So, then, how does Technique work?

                                            "The Technique power tag (p. XX) enables a nova to use one Quantum Power to duplicate the effects of another, ...." So, the Technique tag is placed on a power, and that power can optionally pretend to be a different power (or a different version of the same power). That's simple enough. The part that gets a little weird is that the Technique itself must also "include the Technique tag to be part of a Power Suite (p. XX) for that power."

                                            So, it appears that Power Suites work as follows:
                                            (Assume this character has already bought Quantum 3)
                                            Elemental Mastery (kinetic force) Power Suite
                                            -base power: Quantum Anima 3, free tags[Scale, Manipulation, Strike], Technique (1) [48 points]
                                            --Technique of Quantum Anima: Quantum Attack 3, free tags[Range 2, Beam], Technique (1) [0 points, as this is just the power that Quantum Anima can emulate via the Technique tag]
                                            ---Technique of Quantum Attack: Quantum Field 3, free tags[Complete (3), Environmental (1), Soft Armor (2)], Technique (1) [0 points, as it's just what Quantum Attack can emulate]
                                            ----Technique of Quantum Field: Flight 3, Technique (1) [0 points]
                                            -----Technique of Flight: Environmental Anima 3, Technique (1) [0 points]

                                            And so on, for a total cost of 48 points regardless of how many Techniques are in the suite. That is an amazing deal, and I'm not sure that it's what was intended. My expectation is that the cost of my original interpretation is closer to the mark.

                                            Also, why does the last power in the suite have to take the Technique power tag? I don't know, and think it shouldn't. Otherwise, I'm owed an infinite number of Techniques! I expect that the wording of the Technique tag will be cleaned up to deal with that problem.

                                            Finally, what, exactly, does the restriction, "The Technique cannot have more dots or power tags than the original power...," mean? Does that mean that the ranks in those power tags don't matter, allowing something like:
                                            Quantum Attack 3, free tags[Scale (1), Range (1), Beam (1)], Subtle (1), Technique (1)
                                            -Technique of Quantum Attack: Quantum Attack 3, free tags[Scale (1), Range (1), Beam (1)], Reduced Cost (4), Technique (1)

                                            Or, does it mean that the Technique can only have as many points of power tags as the original? I'm assuming that that's the actual intention, but I could be wrong.
