                    My house rules so far are:
                    Novas Q1 or 2 are Tier 2 characters, from Q3 are Tier 3, and Q6 Tier 4
                    Novas Tier 3 can have up to 6 dots in Attributes and 7 with Superior Trait Edge.
                    Novas Tier 4 can have up to 7 dots in Attributes and 8 with Superior Trait Edge.
                    Mega-Attributes are limited only to the Quantum value and not to baseline Attributes.
                    Transcendence 10 don’t mean you are out of the game, but you get 2 additional severe Transformations and are treated as permanent quantum instability (unless you have que quantum Stability Mega-Edge)
