                                                    Originally posted by Slagheap View Post
                                                    I liked that the most recent manuscript update included blurbs about some of the Alligences from the TC Core and how they change with the Nova era.
                                                    Thanks! I was happy we managed to get those in there. Given how close the core setting and Aberrant are, a lot of those Allegiance are probably still active, and we wanted to provide some additional utility. I know some people will be tempted to just use the core rules and ignore the rest as irrelevant for their Aberrant games, so we wanted to show there was more in there that they could use.

                                                    I hope we get a stretch goal that covers them in more detail, and also includes the ones they didn't cover, like Nine.
                                                    If you read the core's writeup on 9, you'll see that they're considering the possibility of working under the UN under the auspices of Directive 9. And Aberrant's Directive sprang from the UN's Directive 9. You've already heard about what happened to 9.
