    A Trinity Setting with Novas, Psions, and Talents?

    12-15-2021, 02:07 AM
    I wonder if we'll ever get a setting with all three types in play, not as hostile factions, but as allies. Because it's been mentioned in numerous places that on of humanity's big special "hat" is the fact that alone of all the groups out there--mankind can simultaneously develop nova, psi, and talented individuals--and yet at least for now, each setting focuses on one single type, with the others more or less sidelined.

    So do you think there will ever be a published setting with all the groups available as protagonists?

      If such a setting does get published - I don't think it's going to come from Onyx Path. They've already got MANY irons in the fire, not many devs/testers/editors to work with, and a backlog of things they've already committed to publishing. That said, I can easily imagine a third-party doing so through their DriveThru community program (can't recall the name, but a few minor releases have come out using it already).

      Personally, I ignore the setting material and use the rules to play in my own worlds. I have one where it's Talents & Psions in a space opera setting very reminiscent of Mass Effect. I have another I'm playing with my sons which is basically The Boys (comic, not TV series) with a very basic make-over that allows Aberrants & Talents to work side-by-side. The Aberrants start at 75pt's of XP instead of 150pts in character generation to give the group a relatively level starting point.
