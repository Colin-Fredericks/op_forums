    Everyone posting here is clearly invested in the setting - in the years post-cancellation many of us took the system under-wing and used it as our own.     Originally posted by Teulisch View Post
    i dont think strength is the problem people think it is. the reason mega-strength is so potent, is that it has to be in order to keep up with an equal investment in quantum bolt+quantum, which does the damage of an artillery strike at the high end. balance is about comparing alternatives for same cost.

    and on the defense side, for an equal investment you could get enough soak to withstand the average attack- and still die in one hit to a very high roll. but then a high-rolling force field could survive even that with the right dice pool.

    i think we need a new mega-stamina enhancement- spend 1 quantum point, and that lethal hit simply incapacitates you, just short of death. then some mechanism of how long your out based on how excessive the damage was. it would be very theme-appropriate, useful for keeping important characters alive, and also leave the character helpless for some time. this would fit well with some of the other things novas can do anyway, and not make anyone better at winning a fight. does make murdering someone more difficult, but not impossible.
    Good point that M-Strength is balanced against Q-bolt, the cost is much more comparable there. I left out any rolling defences in the numbers above because of the variables i would then need to account for - but it was there in the back of my mind.
    I'd like to see some kind of safe-guard against casual, accidental murder at higher levels - if it happens all the time it loses its impact as a dramatic hook. A simple M-Stamina enhancement could fill that need. Good idea. Want to expand on how it would work, since it's your concept?

        yeah, I can think of a few ways to make it better....

        Unkillable (mega-stamina enhancement)

        if an attack does damage that would kill you, this enhancement spends one quantum point to prevent you from dropping below incapacitated either for the scene, or until you are healed above incapacitated. while in this state, you appear dead to outside observers, but your node is still active and node activity may still be observed. you will remain in this state until you can heal to crippled on your own (see p. 254), or are healed by someone else. if you take damage again during this time that would kill you, this enhancement will spend one quantum point to keep you alive for the scene. you cannot regain quantum pool while incapacitated in this manner. this enhancement is passive, and will not function if you are dormned down. no other powers may be activated while incapacitated. if you spend your last quantum to stay alive, you may require medical help to survive. each time you are attacked such that this enhancement must spend quantum to keep you alive, the time to heal starts over. each time this enhancement activates, gain one temporary taint.
        once a nova will this enhancement heals to crippled, he may start gaining quantum pool again and will appear alive again, but will not awaken until healed to maimed, although regeneration may be used once quantum pool is full. injuries that require the use of this power will leave scars, which may be healed with regeneration.

        special note- aggravated damage will only be survived if you have a way to soak agg damage, and this enhancement will automatically activate the appropriate enhancement or power if needed. healing from aggravated damage will take the full 5 months. if you cannot soak agg, then you are simply dead, and your body destroyed.

        so, this keeps you alive, and you have at least 3x healing rate from mega-stamina. you will likely be out of the action for a couple of months. The survival hibernation of this enhancement is similar to chrysalis in many ways. while it makes you difficult to kill, you may still die if they keep hitting your corpse, even if it takes a couple days of effort to do it. yes, this enhancement can survive a direct hit with a nuclear warhead, but you will need adaptability to survive the radiation that follows. as taint gain from repeated attacks can result in new aberrations, this may be a large clue to others that you are not dead. such aberrations will likely be quantum related.

                                The word balance is thrown about a lot. With Aberrant, things were lop-sided as all get out. However, bear in mind that balance should not be white-room balance. For example, it has been noted in this thread that the setting also helps to balance out the outlandish use of Mega-Str to rip people apart and then use their femurs as drumsticks. (Insert Nathan Explosion meme here) When you have someone turning people into smears and it gets out, then the universe tends to start putting obstacles in your way that have nothing to do with normal powers.
                                In combat, everyone has their own strengths and weaknesses. Some things are OBVIOUS such as those who go Mega-Strength 5 and then enhancing it and adding to it as much as possible. Others are the people who get a ton of Mega-Social and enhancements. They can wreck a setting fast in practice. The math bears out that defense can easily be obliterated but it all depends on how you attack and defend. Density Decrease + Mega- Socials = Charming Ghost who makes you hit yourself until you die.
