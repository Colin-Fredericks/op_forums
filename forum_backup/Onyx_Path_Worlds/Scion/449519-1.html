                                        It's also an issue with ancient mythological pantheons as well. The Persian, mostly pre-Zoorastrian pantheon, the Yazatas is effectively the Asuras or "somewhat bad, sometime enemies" opposition gods of the Devas of Hindu mythology. Likewise, to the Yazatas are effectively their opposition, the deevils. Plus here are insane amounts of cross-over between the two with gods that are almost completely the same between the two. The Yazata book discussed the situation lightly, but defaulted to the Scion "standard" of Pantheon vs. Titan default instead of Pantheon vs. Pantheon.

                                        It also happens within a culture. The Sumerian gods are divided into two groups the Annuna (Annunaki) and the Igigi who are partially, kinda, a little opposed. Or the Norse, where the Vanir and Aesir are buddy-buddy now, but they used to be not very friendly at all.
