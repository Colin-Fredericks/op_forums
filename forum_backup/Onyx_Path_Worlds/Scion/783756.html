    Building Campaign without ideas

    12-10-2015, 07:51 PM
    Hey guys, so my gaming group and I are in full swing of a campaign, and my turn to GM is up next when we finish. We'll be playing a scion campaign, and I have a general idea of a plot/quest, but I need some help fleshing it out and coming up with some subplots.

    Here's the main idea:
    In sunny California, children have been disappearing. It's been going on for years, and the players' band has finally caught wind and made a startling discovery. Most, if not all, of these missing children have been scions! Unvisited and unclaimed, or else convinced to turn away their divine parent, all of the children have no remaining connection to their godly parent.
    The band gets a hint that a child in Lemoore, California has been targeted, and set out to investigate. They roll into town to find that the local high school was evacuated after a shootout between to rival "parties". The school was set ablaze in the commotion, and is burning to the ground as the band arrives.

    Character-wise, I have set up:
    The bad guys:
    Sharptongue, evil mastermind and scion of Coyote. He has all manner of mundane followers and "believers" (I've decided he's a charismatic psychopath) who are militant and more than ready to do his bidding.
    Bryan O'Leary, brainwashed(?) 16 year old right hand man and lover of Shartongue, scion of Manannán Mac Lir.
    Kenneth Graves, personal body guard of Sharptongue and scion of a war God from an original pantheon we called the Radi'Solus.


    Additionally, I've decided that the shootout was the result of a different band of scions, who were onto Sharptongue, trying to stop his men and failing. The band was massacred except for one wounded member trapped inside the burning school, brought to the players' attention with the dying warning of another scion's last breath.
    This would be Andre Syccarus, scion of Hephaestus, blacksmith, and virtual noncombatant.

    What I need help with is a deeper understanding of my story. Why does Sharptongue kidnap dozens of unclaimed scions over the years? What are his plans?
    Should there be some subplots going on? Perhaps the power of all of this divinity, even unclaimed, has attracted all manner of supernatural happenings?

    I'm just really stuck here, guys. Any help or ideas you could give me would be hugely appreciated.
    Thanks!

                      Okay, so upon talking with my friend who is a more seasoned GM than I, I've come up with a fairly solid plan.
                      Sharptongue is trying to resurrect the Anaye, (the Navajo equivalent of Titans, except their mythology actually involves their destruction). He has far reaching plans to do so, including the sacrifice of a whole lot of people. Additionally, he's trying to raise up an army for their arrival. This is where the unclaimed scions come in.
                      He is holding them captive, with the intent of force-feeding them human flesh, which is an invitation for the spirit of a Wendigo to invade their body. He has tried to do this with Visited scions, but the ichor in their veins is too powerful, resulting in their death, rather than transformation. The unclaimed children, however, are still more than mortal and when transformed become horrifyingly powerful demons, even more so than a traditional Wendigo.
                      He has succeeded on this front before, creating a more powerful Wendigo that was susceptible to his Epic Manipulation. This creature was killed by a traveling Band, however (which is why Andre's Band was there, they tracked down his operation).

                      His base of operation is in Lemoore, CA. It's a small town I know a decent amount about, and has all the story elements I need readily available. He has it outfitted with several titanspawn, children of the Anaye that serve Sharptongue only so that he will resurrect their Titan parents.

                      The players' goal is twofold.
                      1) Find and save the children (possibly recovering the stolen birthrights of those that received visitations in the past but are gone now)
                      2) Discover Sharptongue's real plans and put an immediate (if not permanent, which would allow me to set up a sequel down the line) stop to them.

                      To this end I've given lots of variability in how they can do so. Bryan O'Leary has a Child Geas, but has been tricked by Sharptongue into believing the children are better off without their divine parentage. If Sharptongue's real plans and methods were revealed, Bryan would be forced to switch sides and save the children.
                      Additionally, I've decided Sharptongue has mortal "spies" working for him throughout the town giving the players a chance to gain insight through social skills, if they decide to go that route.

                      Does this sound reasonable, ST-wise? I would love your guys' continued feedback, it's really helpful. Any ideas on how to build on the idea (especially for sequels down the line) would be great, and any flaws or questions you could bring up would also be appreciated.

                      Thanks guys!
