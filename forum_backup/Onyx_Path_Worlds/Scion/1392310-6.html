                    Thank you Mateus! That's a really interesting list!

                    Edit: Okay, I guess I'll leave my thoughts.

                    The Loa: That's a good list. I'm pleased that the Barons are a group of deities rather than a single one this time around. I would honestly have liked to see Ezili handled the same way though, and I am a little disappointed not to see Maman Brigitte. Oh well. She shouldn't be too hard to homebrew, though.

                    ​The Nemetondevos: Seems like a good list. I know a lot of what I think I know about these guys is based on outdated information, however, so I'm really interested in Watcher's opinion, if they have the book. Is Cernunnos at the very least a deity of commerce and not of the hunt? I'm glad to see that "Teutates" isn't on there, as that term gets applied to a single Gaulish god way too often.

                    Yazatas: Honestly, I don't really know enough about the Yazatas to have an opinion. I have read the 1e write-up of them, but given that as Samudra put it, actual practitioners of Zoroastrianconsider it to be so bad as to be heretical even when the religion doesn't have a concept of heresy, I think it would be best if I just assumed I lack knowledge of them completely. That being said, the fact that there's fourteen of them is cool, as it means all the big ones are probably covered at least.

                    As for the miscellaneous deities, it's an interesting list with a few I didn't honestly expect. Hecate was definitely the obvious choice for the Theoi, but beyond that I'm surprised by most of them. I really would have put my money on Ganga for the Deva, for instance. Not that there's anything wrong with Samudra's homebrew write-up and I'll happily use it, but I more or less assumed she was the most important one not yet present. Upuat would not have been my first guess for the Netjer, either, though to be honest I can easily name two dozen Netjer I'd like to see thrown in and would have been happy with any of them. Thanks to my Egypt phase. You know, we all had one.

                    Very pleasantly surprised to see Xolotl. He's a personal favorite of mine, and I'd honestly love to play a Scion of him at some point. I'm really interested in playing a Teotl scion, and Xolotl would be a really intriguing choice, being a protector of the deformed, disabled, and generally ostracized. Plus he looks so damn cool and I'd love to have my character get gradually more monstrous as their legend increases, and he seems like a good patron for that idea.

                    I take it Columbia is either unaligned or a spun-off Theoi?

                                                        Originally posted by Kyman201 View Post
                                                        See this is why I'd flat-out ban putting Boons on Relics. It just feels like it's trying to game the system. You want to do Marvels through a Relic? Put Purviews on it and make a Marvel with it. And if it's just "Get a Boon from a different Purview" for one dot then especially banned, because there's no way I'm letting Boons cost less than a flipping Knack.

                                                        I've always charged boons as 2 dots - very slightly modified - and they cost the legend as though they'd been marveled. This was based off of the Icarian Wings. It allows 24 hours of flight (pretty sure, book's not on hand) for spending 1 legend (as though you had marveled the sky boon). In this way you can add unique gifts to items that you don't necessarily want to give access to a purview - such as a teleporting sword or a flying boat. But based on other relics I still think it should be 2 dots, and charge the legend of a marvel. So that teleporting sword? 2 legend to teleport with it.
