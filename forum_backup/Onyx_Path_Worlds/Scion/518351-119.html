                                Originally posted by Maitrecorbo View Post
                                I am unsure if i am phrasing my question correctly so bear with me a bit.

                                How do you envision playing a scion say from the algonquin pantheon who is not from that particular culture?? The same question could be said with a scion born in japan who is not from the kami pantheon.
                                It is something i feel would be interesting to explore but i am unsure on how to do it well.
                                Would that be something offensive in your opinion?
                                Obviously not on the Dev team, but this exact topic is actually a pretty common theme in Scion. Cultural differences are a huge note of a lot of Scion games, and cultural differences are incredibly significant even across time. A modern Algonquin Scion will not find themselves exactly at home immediately with the Algonquin Pantheon, there may even be significant points where they disagree. The same for someone born in Ireland with the Tuatha. Cultures shift over time to the extent that just being in the same region as your Pantheon doesn't mean you will feel familiar with that culture. As L.P. Hartley said, "The past is a foreign country, they do things differently there." On top of this, most cultures of the Pantheons do not exist any more. The Theoi, Tuatha, Aesir, Teotl, and Netjer all lost their original cultures.

                                A Japanese person who is not the Scion of one of the Kami would also be a reasonable point. The Gods do not (some exceptions, the Kami in general are probably unlikely to have Scions outside Japan) really care about borders. Especially the traveling Gods. Odin, Manannan, Hermes, Eshu, rambling along through the mortal world. More space for culture clashes, which is a amazing sub-theme of Scion.

                                To do it well, just approach things with accuracy in mind. The easiest example that I can give is a Scion of the Teotl. Almost no modern person would be down with participating in the human sacrifices. However, have their Divine Parent explain that the blood is to ensure the Teotl can continue to protect and provide for humanity (Except for Tezcat who's a coin flip if he's helping or not) and that the sacrificed all get access to the nice afterlives unlike most other people. The Scion can chose to disagree, attempt to bridge a gap, or ect. Same for a Teotl Scion who is Homosexual, and finds themselves in the crosshairs of the Pantheon (or an Aesir Scion, or a Netjer Scion, ect) and have to deal with these issues of cultural clashing.

                                Edit: Know what, I totally hadn't considered what Atamajakki said below me. I was just thinking about it from a "would this make sense" angle. If someone is being a racist idiot, playing offensive stereotypes, or essentially other branches of being a twat, I wouldn't even want them in my play group. I do think that it's totally reasonable to be a Scion of Odin who is Moroccan for example, a Irish son of Sun Wukong, or an Iraqui son of one of the Incan Gods, as long as you're not being a racist idiot about it. Pretty much, to do it best, don't do it badly, and don't be a racist.
