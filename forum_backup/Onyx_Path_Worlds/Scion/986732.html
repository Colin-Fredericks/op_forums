                                                          I think an important thing to keep in mind when presenting unique individuals is that they can serve the dual purpose of providing a guide on the general thing (in this case, Frost Giants) while also showing an example of how these creatures can be unique. Particularly in a game where stats are not necessarily as important (These creatures might just be presented as Antagonists using quicker rules rather than full characters) it might actually be more valuable to present a unique version because it presents both guidance on the general phenomenon and how to take it a step further.
