    Little errata for the Bogovi write-up, on Underworld: Peklo – The Underworld
    When one mentions the Underworld, they usually think of two separate and diametrically opposed places: heaven or hell. Peklo, however, is both. How it appears to any singular spirit depends on how they carried out their lives. Those who lived in accordance to their nature and upheld their honor and duty find themselves in Raj – a warm and lush, heavenly paradise where they want for nothing. Those who failed to uphold their honor and duty, however, experience not fire and brimstone as one would equate a hellish landscape; Nav is a dreary, frozen wasteland which falls under Veles’ personal dominion    .

    In text about Peklo ( Piekło, in Polish, I assume ), it mention Nav, realm of spirit, as description of Peklo itself. So Nav should be changed for Peklo here.
