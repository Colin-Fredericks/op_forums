    Awesome stuff! All good news so far. Clarification on the first question though. I was actually referring to character creation. When you chose your birthright options and built your custom starting relics (by spending points) you could create some pretty interesting stuff. The system got a extra chart in the companion guide (I think that was the one) that helped with assigning point values to certain benefits. Overall it seemed like the intention was that you could easily make up stuff from scratch. Long story short, one guy in my party had a ironman like suit with alot of logistics to it so he could summon it to him alongside his weapon and my other buddy had a amulet that let him be a sort of D&D wizard. Awesome stuff in the end but overall I had a hard time balancing it all to work smoothly. Sure, my bad for trying to do full on custom relics but it did seem like that was the hinted intent.

    I guess long question short, are we going to get another chart with point costs for certain benefits that will make unique birthright/relic creation easier? A bigger or more encompassing list? I think back to Mutants and Masterminds and how they allowed you to essentially build everything from a list of components.

    In the end, id rather see it (a) just become less open but more refined and balanced or (b) completely open easyish to create about anything akin to Mutants and Masterminds or other point based system.

    In all honesty, id probably learn more towards wanting A personally. Sure, tons of open options are great... but ultimately that tends to create power disparity when some people want flavorful items and others want nuclear warheads attached to weapons essentially. There is always going to be power disparity in a open progress dot based game but any way we could lessen that would be great.

    Which leads me into what should be my final random question.

    - Any easier magic blasty options? Seemed like purviews didn't have many direct elemental blasts (atleast early) that someone could build a character off of. Part of the reason I had to design an entire mana/magic blasting relic.

    Thanks alot Neall! Really love the level of interaction you are having with the fans during development.

                    Hmm...I guess it depends a bit on exactly what these purviews represent. For instance, do the moons Phobos and Deimos have any mythical associations to them that are similar to the ones given to our own moon? Where they even recognized as other moons? Or where Phobos and Deimos the moons just sharing the characterization already present in Phobos and Deimos, with nothing of significant being added from being represented by celestial bodies?

                    Personally, I probably would not give Herakles a Stars Purview. Too broad of a powerset* for someone who is just represented in a constellation and with, as far as I know, no mythical associations with powers from or over the stars in general. In the same way, I don't think, say, Mars, should have some sort of planetary purview just because he is represented by a Planet.


                    It probably makes sense that such Fate Bindings might occur, but I think in many cases a Purview might not be the best way to represent it. Gods have some sort of "seat/place of power" mechanic this time around, right? A place where they are stronger? Maybe such bindings can give rise to such a place in some form? Like, Ares has his place in Olympus, and a lesser seat of power on the red planet?

                    Maybe specific Mantles? I'm still not sure exactly what those do and how far a god changes in different Mantles, but there might be something there to allow for the less common interpretations of a given divinity to be expressed.


                    *Of course, that can change depending on how far one can personalize and specialize Purviews in second edition. But it still seems far fetched to me to present Hercules with any sort of broad Stars associations.

                                    That is a weird question. I'd say no - Scion takes lengths to stop gods from feeling like comic book superheroes, and while humans can attain a sort of folk deification, actual apotheosis or Visitation isn't possible without a divine spark.

                                    That said, I'd love to do a techno-data Shard later, where gods are Big Data, pantheons are proprietary software engines on quantum platforms, and Scions are their analog interfaces. In that case, yeah, a god or Scion could be remade through human ingenuity.
