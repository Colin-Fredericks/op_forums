    It is a pretty fine line, but I come down mostly on the end of gods being shaped by sincere human belief. Thor is not affected by Marvel, because nobody (or an incredibly tiny number of people) actually believe that that version is a deity. In modern terms, it pretty much means that if we take the world as it is right now, really only the Deva are being actively influenced by human belief. However, if the "Masquerade" is broken, things may change. Pop culture and religion are not the same thing, and as much as Scion deals with mythology as a thing that 'happened in the past' it also is a game about religion.

    Games are a way for us to explore what it means to be human, or inhuman, as the case may be. Let's all have a good time playing them.

                            I see two small problems with powers that are too flexible. Firstly, it leads to situations like the Water Purview, which effectively has the exact same Boon repeated thrice with a few more extras attached each time. This makes it a bit boring in my opinion. The other thing I dislike about them is that they end up making older powers feel redundant. Human Clay, in Health, can effectively do anything the Boons before it can do, and more. These are, of course, more personal gripes than any actual criticism of the mechanics.

                            However, one thing I would very much like to see is Avatars made more impactful. As of now, there amazing one trick ponies, but I would like to see attaining the Avatar of a Purview do more than just add to your list of associations. I would like it if they gave passive bonuses to Boon use in that Purview, because as it stands, any random Legend 12 God who picks up a Water Boon is as good at it as a dedicated Water God like Poseidon or Sarasvati.

                                        Good analysis, Griffinguy. I wish I had the time to attempt something similar.

                                        I posted this before when a similar topic came up, but it seems relevant.

                                        (I apologize if anyone already posted something like this. The forum and my computer still don't get along, meaning I can read only the first and last pages of a thread.)

                                        I’d recommend getting rid of the “Special Purview” category. I know from experience that players have enough trouble keeping straight the difference between buying PSPs a dot at a time and buying APP Boons singly. Special Purviews are a needless complication. It is also objectively stupid, or at least a waste of XP, to buy more than one dot in either Mystery or Prophecy.

                                        Instead, I would create a new APP called Oracle. Mystery and Prophecy would be single Boons within the APP (and likely rewritten a bit). Oracle would also subsume all the divinatory spells from Magic, such as Ariadne’s Thread or Unlidded Eye.

                                        Remaining spells would go in other new APPs. The probability-controlling spells such as Bona Fortuna and Evil Eye would become Boons in the Purview of Fate – or more accurately, Fate and Chance, as all the probability-related Boons from Chaos (such as Eye of the Storm and Hornet’s Nest) would go here as well.

                                        Chaos is redefined as the Purview of madness, illusion, and generally breaking or warping consciousness, perception and (at higher levels) the rules of reality. IMO this makes it more representative for Gods of frenzy and intoxication such as Dionysus, or deception such as Loki. The new Chaos can swallow the Illusion Purview, if you like.

                                        There can still be a Magic Purview, but this APP deals more with direct manipulation of Legend, transformation and the interactions between Legendary creatures and mundane reality. Examples would be Legendary Surge, Blood Mead (a transformation, transferring Abilities between characters) or Legendary Beacon (a proposed Boon that simply sends out a signal to every creature of Legend in a wide area, letting them know another creature of Legend wants to meet them). Maybe Boons for sympathetic magic, too, though this could create overlap with other Purviews. (But I think it’s okay if some Boons can fit in more than one Purview.)

                                        [EDIT: In view of Telgar's analysis, I am not even sure there should be a Purview called "Magic."]

                                        I’d also add an APP of Craft or Artifice, for reasons that should be obvious to anyone who knows their mythology.

                                        As it stands, Fertility deals with plants. Period. Can we just call it the Plant Purview?

                                        I have no serious problems with any of the other APPs, though plenty of Boons should be revised or replaced. [EDIT: Some Purviews, such as Moon, will probably have to remain grab-bags of oddball Boons, just because they don't lend themselves to easy, coherent sets of powers the way, say, Fire, Sky, or Guardian do. I can live with it.]

                                        Dean Shomshak

                                                    I strongly support the suggestion to disentangle Epic Attributes from Purviews, and that includes removing attributes from the dicepools for them - it just pigeonholes a god of Death into Charisma, even if it's bloody Hel.

                                                    I have a point pertaining to that that hasn't been made yet:

                                                    It has to be decided of purviews should be focused on a specific purpose or flexible. What I mean to say is: In the current system, as a god of Fertility, your combat and social utility is basically nil. As a god of Frost, you have a respectable amount of combat boons, but just one social power and little that is useful in shaping the world at large to your purposes - even if freezing a field of crops isn't a god-level power, it just isn't there earlier. It has been theorized by many that the official boons are just examples and you could write combat boons for Fertility or more social boons for Frost (I think GBN did just that).

                                                    Both perspectives are acceptable, but I suggest that the decision should be made once and for all, and then executed with utmost consequence. If my divine purview of Death is to be useful to me in combat, social confrontation, and manipulating the setting at large, then this has to be built in from the start, with boons to support all of these purposes at all levels as is appropriate for the Purview.
                                                    If, on the other hand, purviews should inherently support only one or two areas of application, that should also be done with consequence, and there should be NO social boon for the Fire purview, and useful Boons for both combat and elemental shaping at any level.

                                                    The current situation - with some purviews having a clear vision in their design and some just being all over the place of possible uses - makes it very hard for players to decide on just what your character should also be good at if he is to be a future Sun god. Either purviews are useful for all areas of expertise, or they should come with a clear focus to build a character around.
