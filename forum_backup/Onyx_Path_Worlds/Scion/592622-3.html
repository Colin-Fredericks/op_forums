    Yeah, Magic is kind of infamous for having powers that should be in other Purviews. Mostly because the RAW Fatebinding system was so... ineffective, Magic couldn't really interact like it as you would expect the Purview would, so it stole a bunch of stuff from other Purview's areas of influence. Let's see if I can make a list...

    Scion Adoption Rite should be a Justice power. Complex systems of adoption (or similar things) are present in many ancient cultures and have nothing at all to do with Fate. In the very least, if Magic can do this, Justice should be allowed to do it as well. But, in my mind, it should just be for Justice.

    Transient Visitation should be Psychopomp or Illusion. Magic has nothing to do with dreams. Sure, you're giving a Visitation through dreams, but Morpheus for example should be able to do that without buying up a bunch of Magic and instead just use his existing dream powers.

    Illwind Cures from Ragnarok should be in Health since it's causing someone's body to do something. If it is to be kept in Magic it should just cause people to keep making or causing minor Social faux pas which would be in the realm of Fate.

    Demand a Labor is just Justice again. "Do XYZ for me because I'm King/Queen/A God." Magic has nothing to do with the functioning of this unless Fate is shoehorned in. It doesn't even compel someone to do things as part of their Fate. It's just a Justice Boon.

    Peaceful Meeting itself works just fine at the same rank as it is in Magic, just make it a Justice Boon. Maybe swap the roll to Charisma + Academics/Occult/Command.

    Originally posted by simison View Post
    And, I was considering two last, huge, house-rules. We all know how the auto-successes start multiplying the more god-like a scion gets. Well, what if instead of it multiplying like it does, it always sticks to sequential order? Instead of 1-2-4-7-11, it'd be 1-2-3-4-5 and so on. Because I've heard that god-level games get ridiculously unbalanced.
    If you want to alter how Epics work, you need to go through and edit how many powers (Bona Fortuna, Animal Aspect, and that one Animal Boon where you eat animal bits to gain dice IIRC) to balance them with this new scale. Furthermore, you need to nerf how armor, and weapons work, along with Untouchable Opponent (As now it would work exactly as it did pre-previous nerf, and Body Armor) and you would need to nerf Self-Healing and Regeneration least they just become the staple of combat. Do Not Want from Appearance too IIRC would make everything a bit wonky.

    Additionally, all Deities become incredibly weak. Here, I'll give you a breakdown of... The Dagda (legend 12) and CuChulain (Legend 4) fighting.

    The Dagda's Dodge at Legend 12: 12 (10 Athletics + 5 Dexterity = 15 / 2 = 8 [rounded up]. + Epic Dexterity 4 = 12)
    The Dagda's Lethal Soak at Legend 12: 20 (10 Stamina = 10. + Epic Stamina 10 = 20.)
    The Dagda's Agg Soak at Legend 12: 10 (Epic Stamina 10)
    The Dagda's Hit with Club at Legend 12: 14 dice + 4 Autos = 11 average. (10 Melee + 5 Dexterity -1 club (Using Quauhololli template) = 14. + Epic Dexterity 4)
    The Dagda's Damage with Club at Legend 12: 27 dice + 10 Autos = 24 average. (10 Melee + 10 Strength + 7 Club (Using Quauhololli template) = 27 dice. +10 Epic Strength Autos)

    CuChulain's Parry DV at Legend 4: 9 (5 Melee + 5 Dexterity + 1 Mundane Spear [Naginata template] = 11 / 2 = 6 [rounded up] + 3 = 9.)
    CuChulain's Lethal Soak at Legend 4: 8 (5 Stamina = 5. + Epic Stamina 3 =8)
    CuChulain's Agg Soak at Legend 4: 3 (3 Epic Stamina)
    CuChulain's Hit with mundane Spear at Legend 4: 11 dice + 3 autos = 9 average (5 Melee + 5 Dexterity + 1 Mundane Spear [Naginata template] = 11. +3 Automatic Successes from Epic Dex 3)
    CuChulain's Damage with mundane Spear at Legend 4: 14 dice + 3 autos = 10 average (5 Melee + 5 Strength + 4 Mundane Spear [Naginata template] = 14. +3 Automatic Successes from Epic Strength 3)

    What we can see with this is, without using any buffs, a Legend 12 primarily Physical God and a Legend 4 primarily Physical Scion are incredibly close in several areas. The sheer power of weapons adding some dice is also apparent. The Dagda would only need a slightly below average roll (-3 successes from average) and be unable to hit CuChulain who is 8 Legend below him for example. Add in various other dice adders, armor, and Stunts and a Hero could easily throw down with a God who doesn't have Animal for example. (In total, Animal could be giving someone +30 extra dice on any of these rolls between Animal Aspect and the eating one I think at Legend 12, which makes Animal even more powerful.

    It also really nerfs Socials too in a round about way. I can't remember the exact details of JSR's social resist stuff, but it would make things even more funky than they start with.

    So, feel free to make that change, but you'll have to change a whole lot of things. Additionally, your players will be far less distinct from each other (Someone who has 2 Epic Dex and someone with 5 Epic Dex are only 3 Successes apart) and they will be comparatively way more powerful. Also, actually, Mortals become hugely powerful. I think some of the explosives in Companion could easily kill The Dagda up there.

    God Level games are crazy unbalanced, but that's sort of the point. Scion is essentially a game of hyper-specialization. Honestly, God level stuff is more problematic because of Avatars, Ultimates, and Pantheon Politics.

            Originally posted by simison View Post
            I'd imagine that reducing the number of Legend 12 deities would help reign in the Avatar & Ultimate problems. How does Pantheon Politics screw things up? I would think that would add an awesome avenue of storytelling.
            Reducing the number of Legend 12 Gods actually probably makes Avatars and Ultimates problematic in a different way. Essentially, think of Avatars and Ultimates as Nukes. No one uses Nukes because then everyone would be using Nukes. So, having a large number of figures with Avatars/Ultimates actually prevents people from using them. Reducing this number would reduce the amount of 'policing' that could happen. While you would be dealing with less nukes, the people with the nukes would be more likely to use them.

            Pantheon Politics is the key feature of God. In fact, Pantheon Politics is essentially the entire God Tier. The way the game is designed with Avatars and Ultimates means everything is in a perpetual state of cold war enforced by MAD. So, when no one can act against someone else, be they a Titan, another Pantheon, or another God, everything becomes super diplomatic. Talking becomes way, way, way more relevent than hitting stuff.

            If you want to get something done, you can't take it unless you want everything to go to hell in a hand cart. So, let's use Odin wanting Cronus' Sickle for some reason. Odin could do three things to get it. He could a) steal it by force or trickery, b) take it by conquest, or c) ask for it.

            If a) Odin is blocked by one of the Ultimate Perception Theoi without even using their Ultimate Perception. There is only one Odin, and there are several Theoi with this, so statistically, one will notice. (not even counting Arete which means they'd actually all notice due to having a way bigger dice pool) Odin is now caught red handed. Zeus smacks Odin with Justice because he broke one of Zeus' laws.

            Now, back in Asgard, the Aesir are torn. They are loyal to Odin, but Odin did break the law. So, the Aesir now have to decide to either use force, trickery, or diplomacy to get Odin back. Force and Trickery are both contested by the Theoi who are equally powerful. This can get out of hand, someone uses an Avatar, and suddenly you have upwards of thirty Avatars going off and the allied Greek Titans are showing up to throw down to help their younger cousins. Gods Die. This is a bad thing.

            If b) Odin rolls up with his army and the other Martial Gods. The Theoi knew about this ahead of time because of their All-Seeing figures, but also their Prophecy people. Odin's army drops into Olympus via the Rainbow Bridge, and Odin throws his spear on his side meaning they will win. But, wait, hold on, Nike is hanging out with Zeus, so that means the Theoi will win. Paradox. (I'd probably rule in favor of the Theoi there since they're in a Theoi dominated space) Zeus hurls his Thunderbolt, Thor throws his Hammer, Athena runs rampant as Apollo and Artemis rip things up. Odin fights Ares (and probably wins because Ares is a terrible fighter) and someone dies. If Major God, Avatars go off. If Minor God, the Theoi or Aesir have Vengeance or Loyalty, statistically, someone fails a roll, and an Avatar goes off. Back where we were before, we now have upwards of 30 Avatars throwing down, all the Minor Gods are instantly obliterated unless someone is specifically trying to protect them (and even then, if people gang up on them, they will fail even in their Avatar state) and the Theoi allied Titans show up to throw down.

            Gods Die. This is a bad thing.

            And then, c) Odin goes to Olympus as a honored guest protected under Hospitality. Odin explains why he needs Cronus' Sickle, and Zeus thinks about it. While Odin has Ultimate Manipulation and can get Zeus to agree with him, Zeus has Ultimate Charisma so Odin doesn't want to rip Zeus off. Zeus agrees that Odin can use the Sickle for some time, and Odin offers to give Zeus his spear as collateral. Zeus thinks that's a lovely offer, and both of them Pantheon Head it up and complain about things. Odin and Zeus both decide to seal this pact between them (As they now both are best buds due to both of their Epic Socials) and Odin sends Hugin and Munin out to fetch some Scion of theirs. Zeus yells for Hermes until he shows up, and sends Hermes off to gather up one of their Scions.

            Both Scions get dragged, confused, to Olympus to see two probably a bit tipsy Pantheon Heads (who have been arguing with each other about the superiority of beer or wine) who tell them they're getting married. Hera is pulled up from somewhere (as Zeus is the host, their traditions) and the two Scions are married to each other to seal the pact despite any of their comments otherwise.

            Now the Pantheons are allied, no one died, the two Pantheon Heads now expect babies soon to intertwine their lines, and Odin got what he wanted.

            Fighting is a terrible, terrible idea at God, talking things out is king flatly due to the MAD that Gods embody. That is how Pantheon Diplomacy makes God Games super complicated. Now, what I described above in section c) isn't even as complicated as it could be, since now there's fallout to the Aesir running around with a Primordial Neutering weapon, and the Greeks are playing with something that guarentees their victory. (which they can double up on with Nike) Some Gods may be insulted their Scions were not picked for the marriage, some hearts may be broken from the marriage, the couple may hate each other but forced to keep up appearances least the Theoi sick Eros on them to obliterate their opinions and make them whole heartedly in love with each other. ect.

            Edit: And, just to further explain why no one wants outright conflict, everyone's Ultimate Intelligence Gods and Goddesses can explain that this will be the outcome of Pantheons clashing. The same is true even for antagonistic Titans. If the Titan is ever out of the Titanrealm, the Pantheon is instantly aware due to all-seeing figues. Someone uses The Way, and the entire Pantheon ganks the lone Titan. So, Titans are themselves forced to use diplomacy (Except for the more bestial ones like Apep who are not really concerned with talking and more concerned about eating that tasty tasty sun)

                    Originally posted by simison View Post
                    Those poor Scions.

                    Hm, yeah. I pity the War gods who must feel so useless in these situations, forced to do nothing for ages before they have a real chance to fight.

                    It does sound fascinating as a huge Social game, but, given the limitations, has anyone ever thought of just dropping Avatars and Ultimates?
                    That sort of thing's the standard fate of Scions really. Getting married off for alliances is pretty standard in the ancient world for the children of royalty/leadership, with the kiddos of Gods and Goddesses, it's even more common.

                    War Gods do seem to have a harder time, but what's good about them is that when something does happen, when the terrible happens, the War Gods are hands down the greatest. They can end wars by demanding champion combat, outmanover anyone, even lay Titans low. The War Gods are a brilliant trump card. They may rarely get involved, but when they do the world shakes.

                    But, removing Avatars and Ultimates brings a host of problems with it. Firstly, there's a lot of stuff that's not possible RAW IIRC without Avatars. Bringing the dead back to life for example can't be done without an Avatar or Ultimate. A huge amount of myths don't really make sense as well, since Creation Myths pretty much require Avatars to have happened. What did you do, use Create Earth about five thousand times? Then spend several billion years using Shaping? That's just to build the foundation. Or you can pop The Maker and forge a continent. The Deluge myths can't really work without The Flood too, or when The Dagda held the sun in place for nine months, it's hard to do that without Ultimate Strength. Tezcatlipoca knocking the sun from the sky as a colossal jaguar is difficult without The Beast, ect. The Narrative function of Avatars allows very easy solution to these problems.

                    On top of those problems comes the fact you need to find a way to award the Legend 12 Gods and Goddesses something that the Legend 11 Deities don't have. A Legend 11 Goddess can have Rank 10 Epics, the same as a Legend 12 Goddess. What makes the Legend 12 Goddess more powerful is their capacity to use Avatars and Ultimates.

                    And then, if Titans don't have Ultimates or Purview Avatars, a lot of their threat is removed. You can just beat them down without any real difficulty. But, if they retain Ultimates and Avatars, the Pantheons have no capacity to compete with them. A Titan of Death could just go, "You all die" to a Pantheon, and without access to Avatars or Ultimates themselves, they have no capacity to resist this.

                    A lot of new storytellers are freaked out by the sheer power of Avatars and Ultimates, but they fill a good nieche in the story, and makes the Major Gods terrifying in power. It also helps create the MAD system. (Also, without Avatars, whichever Pantheon has more Gods could steamroll other Pantheons. The Deva, the Shen, and the Theoi are good examples of this. They have so many Legend 9 - 10 figures that they could crush the Aesir, Tuatha, Orisha, Teotl, without any of those Pantheons really able to stop them.)

                        The other thing is, this is a game about GODS. If you nerf them so that they can't pull Ultimates and Avatars, or cut down the automatic successes, you remove what makes them Godly. At the start of the game, he's America's Shining Son, the Hero Saved the Big Apple... Capitalize on that with a few Social Knacks and some bush league Boons...

                            You can think small if you want to and have characters who are neurotic mechanics with daddy issues, but you don't HAVE to do that. After all, not every mythic child of a god/mortal cross has to recapitulate a rags-to-riches story. That's just one mythic path... For every Theseus who has to live in poverty the true ID of his sorta-father is revealed (since he's variously Aegus, Zeus or Poseidon's son...), there'a s Heracles strangling magic serpent demons in his crib....

                                            Another session tonight. I thought it went really well, which is a far cry from my usual anxiety after a night.

                                            Quick summary: The Band met at the diner, except for Vjun, whose player was absent this night. We had him hang out outside with the animals since they weren't allowed inside. The Band had food and got to know each other a little bit. They agreed that they would meet together twenty minutes before the meeting half a block away. My goal for the diner scene and the in-between time was to emphasize how they were supernatural now. Scions with Cat's Grace noticed how they could walk on ice without issue. Leithe's new powerful hearing allowed her to catch mistakes and played better than ever. Elias had a moment where his glass eye briefly saw in electromagnetic waves, alerting him to his new Perception knacks. Jamie's Frost Immunity (which her player just bought) discovered the cold no longer bothered her. Katya spoke with Amanda, the young woman who was stabbed to death in Central Park a few days ago and is demanding vengeance.

                                            At the meeting point, they gathered and then headed for the apartment for the actual meeting. Now, the Band had put Elias at the rear to keep an eye out. So, I had the idea that one of the Dark Alf was watching the Band to give Elias a chance to roll. I intended for him to merely notice eyes on him without seeing the Alf. I jokingly thought if he made 10 successes, I'd actually give him a glimpse. To my shock, he rolled 11. So, I gave him a peak at the Alf, who was completely clothed from the sun. Then, to add to my surprise, Elias used his ability to see infrared at him.

                                            Now, I said there was a little more heat than normal around the Alf's head, a subtle hint about the lengthened ears. Then, I said that the Alf had less body heat than a human's. I thought that this might be the case since Dark Alfs are subterranean creatures and, thus, they operate with less body heat than the average human.

                                            From there, they went into the meeting. The first half went as expected. They chit-chatted with the two cultists until the leader, William, showed up. He gave a friendly greeting and moved into a speech about how the Band was here for 'change' and that his group/cult was trying to stop the End Times. He explained that he was under divine orders, charged by an angel. They needed to gather the bodies to burn them. To prove himself, he used a ring that was enchanted to have a single fire creation spell. He activated it with the Old Norse word for fire and then deactivated it. After all of that, he offered an official invitation to join him.

                                            The Band asked for a moment to think, which the cult obliged, leaving them alone in the apartment for a few minutes. During this time, the Band decided to continue playing along, but any actual evil actions were off the table. So, Lad goes out and tells the cult to come back in. And, this is the moment where everything was nearly derailed. Jamie decides to do two things. One, she uses the Unlidded Eye to check out Will's ring. She successfully identifies the ring as a simple relic with the single fire spell, and that all three cultists were mortals. That was fine. The second thing she does is use Overt Order: "Tell me what deity you serve."

                                            Will answers, "There is no deity."

                                            Confusion abounds with the other two cultists, while the Band immediately jumps on Will's admission. The cult leader immediately defends himself by accusing Jamie of tricks. After a moment of arguing, Will decides to get out and takes his cultists, Ernest & Christine, with him. Lad almost starts combat by physically trying to stop them from leaving. However, Leithe halts them with a combination of Charmer and some comforting words. After successfully preventing them from running out, Leithe fumbles for a bit (her player's anxiety was getting the better of her), but Lad swoops in with Center of Attention. Christine, the female cultist, is hooked, while Earnest and Will are slowly being eased back down. At this point, Will still wants some explanation for what Jamie did to him and why Jamie 'Moriarty' is working with a hero cop. It's here that Lad explains that he doesn't know about the power, but that Jamie is trustworthy because she is secretly a double agent for the cops. Lad wins the roll, so the cultists believe him. (I totally want that to become part of Jamie's legend.) Jamie slides behind them without being noticed and guards the door. By this point, the cultists have been calmed down enough to resume business. The Band accepts the invitation, and Will makes arrangements to add them to guarding the bodies at the Pier, though not to let them deliver the bodies, and to bring in more bodies.

                                            The session ends there.
