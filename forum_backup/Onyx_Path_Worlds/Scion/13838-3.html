Even though we were underground, I felt like there was plenty of space, so I didn’t start to feel claustrophobic even when we were forced to follow him into tight maintenance hallways. Intellectually, I knew I was getting in over my head, but my curiosity got the best of me, as it always does, so like Alice I kept following the white rabbit until he disappeared down the rabbit hole. Or, through a wall in this case.

The trolls said that the hideout was a place of learning—a laboratory—and they knew they were no match for us, but before Magnus could intimidate them into telling us where Ulfa was, several massive fire giants and a couple of flaming dragons came crashing through one of the concrete walls in a wave of red hot flame. As the fire engulfed me, I screamed in terror until I remembered that I’m immune to flames thanks to my mastery of elemental fire, all thanks to the sunglasses my father gave me a few weeks ago. Fu Xi’s gift could not have come at a better time!

                                                        Rajani and Andy dove out of the pump room to avoid the flames that kept swirling through it. Zane bolstered me, and it’s a good thing he did, because when the fire giants attacked, they were empowered with the raging force of a fiery furnace and the speed of an explosion. I couldn’t quite avoid getting hit, and a forge-hot blow from one giant’s hammer sent me flying across the room. Zane got hit too, but he just disappeared into his shadow and then appeared from mine, catching me as I slammed into the wall. He may be a rapscallion, but he’s a chivalrous rapscallion! And if he hadn’t bolstered me against harm in the first place, I might have died! As it was, I was still injured a bit, but not badly.

I didn’t have nearly enough room to move around in the pump room, though, and I was fighting against my claustrophobia the whole time. Magnus didn’t care about any of that, and he tore big chunks out of the walls and machinery as he swung his axe around. Edward struggled to hurt the giants, but he was able to keep the dragons occupied long enough for us to finish the giants and help out. Edward and Magnus killed one dragon, and Zane caved in the other dragon’s skull after I shot it.

I didn’t know if it would work, but with a bit of concentration, I was able to cancel the flames. The room was still unpleasantly hot, but at least it was bearable again. The heat didn’t do a thing for my claustrophobia, though, so I grabbed Zane’s hand again. It helped calm my nerves a bit. I wanted to leave right then, but Magnus wanted answers, so he pounded deeper into the hideout and the rest of us had no choice but to follow.

                                                        We found a sort of research center and lab. The search for clues was . . . destructive. I was distracted anyway, but then Edward went around smashing things up and turning over file cabinets and tables. I was going through a file of papers when he snatched it from me, tore it in two, and tossed the shreds into the air. I think he got “ransack” and “search” confused in his drug-addled brain. Oh well, that’s Edward for you.

                                                        Magnus soon found something good in some of the lab’s records, though. Ulfa has a few lairs. We already ruined one a few months ago when we foiled her plans the first time. The other one is a fortress somewhere else in the swamp, which is more-or-less what my prophecy predicted. There was also the National Geological Association building, but that’s been cleared out by now. So Ulfa must be in the swamp fortress. Unfortunately, the standing orders we found say that if we get too close to her swamp fortress, she going to dispatch fire giants to cause a distraction, probably sending them to kill Hert.

                                                        We also found out more about the mutated shrikes. They were seriously messed up and gross-looking. Apparently, the trolls were injecting them with troll blood in an attempt to mutate them into something more powerful. I wonder if it also made them more vulnerable to sunlight? We kept the experiment records, so I should look them over later to see if they have any data on that. It might be useless information now, though. For all we know, the shrikes in the lab were the only ones they had mutated. At the time, though, I just wanted to get out of the tight, subterranean tunnels. At the very least, I wanted to gt back out onto the subway platform, because that’s spacious and familiar enough that it doesn’t bother me. Since we had learned all we could in the lab, Magnus agreed that we should get out of there, and so now we are thankfully back under the fresh air of an open sky!

                                                        One thing’s for sure, though. The redhead who led us to the enemy hideout had to be Loki, which means he probably IS the trickster from my prophecy, which is what I suspected all along. I still can’t figure out what his game is, but he doesn’t seem to be antagonizing us, so that’s good, right?
