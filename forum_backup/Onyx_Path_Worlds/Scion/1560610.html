    Thoughts for 3rd Edition

    06-14-2026, 02:40 AM
    After hearing that a 3rd edition is in the works, I thought I would throw out my thoughts on what might make this launch a better one from my perspective as a customer. I don't pretend to speak for anyone else, this is just me riffing.

    1. Release the three core books first, and THEN start adding Companions, Dragons, Pantheons, and so on.This will eliminate the two biggest problems I had with the current edition. First, you couldn't play all the way from Origin to God until 8 years had passed and all the books were out. And second, every expansion was released when different core books were out, so they didn't contain all the rules needed for playing at higher power levels, and those had to be added on later.

    2. Come up with a game world that fits the game premise. The current "our world, but not" makes absolutely no sense, and requires everyone to come up with their own take before they can even start up a campaign.

    3. Include more and better examples of character creation, skill use, and combat for newcomers to the game. Hopefully, having it fit into the fully developed OP system will make things go more smoothly, but it still helps to have clear directions on how to do things.

    There might be more, but those are the biggest issues I hope will be addressed. Any other thoughts?

                                                      Honestly, I want the supplements to be planned out better. A lot of the supplements always felt half-done, such as how non-human species and sorcerers had almost no info on how higher tiers differed, or even how you reached them.

                                                      Meanwhile, titanic scions had their information and mechanics spread over about 3-4 books, each of which changed things, creating what was mostly a confusing mess where you needed 5 books just for a hero-tier character.

                                                      Generally speaking, I really don't like how common it is to find multiple books reprinting the same mechanics, but now updated with different rules. I'm not sure how many crafting rules iterations we've had, but I'm pretty sure it's too many.

                                                      Finally, I really want more support for Scions who focus more on magic, especially for combat. You can sort of use weapon tags to hack together magic wands and such, but otherwise you're mostly stuck spending a legend to gain a basic magic attack for a scene. In general Scion really does not seem to want you to be magical in the lower levels, beyond your two boons and the occasional marvel.
