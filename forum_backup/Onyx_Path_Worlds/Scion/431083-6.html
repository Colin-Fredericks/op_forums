    121) The Titans have done something they weren't supposed to do; they got smart! After repeatedly throwing waves of her children at Mount Olympus, Gaia has decided that if she can't attack the Overworld directly, then she shall do so indirectly. The prime avatar of Terra has begun to divert her forces to the World in hopes of destroying the mundane counterpart of the Theoi's home. Nobody is sure what will happen to the home of the Gods if that happens, but they don't intend to wait around and find out!


    122) When recounting the deeds of heroic figures, their more shady activities often get swept under the rug and fade from the public's collective knowledge. You have just heard rumors of a fellow Scion who seems to have let their heritage go to their heads and is behaving in a self aggrandizing manner which leads to innocent people getting hurt to satisfy their ego. Yet despite what you've heard, nobody seems to be prosecuting them; in fact, they're actually quite popular, despite their selfish behavior. And now you're starting to have some doubts yourself. Do you want to follow their example and lord your divine heritage over lesser mortals, or do you want to teach this Scion what it really means to be a hero?

        123) To the Ancient Egyptians, the time before time was an endless void-ocean without shape or light until Ra hatched from his egg (or lotus, depending) to create the sun and bring forth dry land. This abyssal sea was represented by Nu, a frog-headed deity of the oldest Egyptian pantheon that was later forgotten.

        The thing is, Nu never died. It's a Titan, upset by all the light and sound and chaos of existence, and the time has finally come to make its move and return the world to nothing. Nu has servants and cultists searching for weapons of apocalyptic scope, but not even a nuclear holocaust erases things completely enough for its taste; it has placed its eyes squarely on the physics experiments conducted at the Large Hadron Collider and elsewhere, hoping to create a black hole that will engulf the World and return everything to the primordial nothingness.

        Nu is the oldest being, and one of the most patient; it has had thousands of years to build up a conspiracy of servants, and it falls to your band to stop the actually end of the universe.

            124) Akhetaten's greatest strength is that it only has one Avatar, allowing it to marshal it's full army towards a singular purpose, without having to worry about conflicting ideas. But one Scion has come up with an extremely bold plan to change this uniformed cohesion by introducing a new Avatar for the Titan of Light. A sleeper agent who will combat Aten's forces from the inside out, and hopefully stem the tide of suicide bombers being sent into the Gods' territories. Of course, the Scion has generously volunteered to sacrifice himself to Akhetaten so that he may become the new Avatar, but his plans are met with great suspicion by the established pantheons. On one hand, not all Titans were considered dangerous enough to need to be sealed away, and it's possible that this Scion may prove to be one such example. But if something goes wrong with this plan, then the Gods will have one more enemy to worry about!

            The Scion does not seem to be the fool, however. They claim to have devised a ritual that will make the joining process go a lot smoother than it usually does. But when the players are petitioned by both sides of the argument, they must decide whether to help the Scion perform their ritual, or to disrupt its procedure and halt their ambitious plan.
