                                          Originally posted by Storyteller323 View Post
                                          Also question, say I wanted to play a draconic brawler who throws cars and rips monsters apart with his bare hands, would there be any super strength style powers inheritors have that could assist that?
                                          In the prerelease manuscript, it says that you have four ways to increase Scale.
                                          1. If you are acting in coordination with your Calling, and if you have a correct Knack, you can increase your Scale by 1/2 of your Inheritance [replaces Legend] rounded up.
                                          2. If the action matches a Deed Name, you get an extra +1.
                                          3. Activate a purchased Transformation knack reflexively. Your body changes in some way.
                                          4. Imbue one Inheritance point [replaces Legend] and take a Simple action. You gain +1 Scale (doesn't stack with number 1 above; the higher value wins, so at Legend 3 it would increase) and turn into a Dragon. Whatever you want your Dragon to have, it has (claws, wings, etc). If your form has nonhuman features such as wings, you get a Complication to use it unless you've purchased a Transformation Knack.


                                          Example from the book: "an Inheritance 4 Heir with the Iron-Fisted Rule Knack for her Judge Calling takes an action to punish someone and her Deed Name is The Writhing Punisher of Prey, she can invoke her Knack of Scale to gain +3 Scale for the action."

                                          So I create a Dragon Heir named Elspeth Níðhöggrsdottir (Lindwurm Flight). I choose the Callings Destroyer,Predator,and Nomad. As a starting character, Elspeth gets 2 Draconic Knacks. She takes one from Scale and one from Transformation.
                                          For the Scale knack, I choose Destroyer: "Unstoppable Force." Elspeth Scale equal to half her Inheritance (rounded up) when fighting a massive force.
                                          For the Transformation Knack I take "Massive Beast Shape." Elspeth gains +1 Size and the same Scale for Feats of Strength or Stamina.
                                          I give her the Deed Name "Wolfsbane".

                                          When confronting an army of werewolf Titanspawn, Elspeth has two options:
                                          Human-mode:
                                          Elspeth can gain +1 Scale (half Inheritance rounded up) as a Feat of Scale because of Unstoppable Force. She can spend 1 momentum to invoke her deed name, adding +1 because she's fighting wolves. She can also gain an additional +1 Scale for strength and stamina actions by activating Massive Beast Shape. When she does this, she physically grows even bigger. That's +3 Scale total for a single action against wolves.

                                          Dragon-mode:
                                          She can also imbue 1 Inheritance to transform into a dragon for a scene instead. This gives her +1 Size and +2 physical Scale. She gains another +1 for fighting wolves. That's a total of +3 Scale, for the scene. The only flaw is that you look like a dragon.

                                                  Originally posted by Storyteller323 View Post

                                                  I am sorry, I do not wish to sound rude but I am a bit rusty with the game, how do feats of scale equal mimicking Epic Strength?
                                                  This is a Dragon. They don't have access to Purviews or Boons; All Dragon magic is Knacks (three flavors: Calling Knacks like Scions, but also Draconic Knacks: Scale Knacks that allow you to gain Scale for an action, and Transformation Knacks that let your dragon form to have draconic powers) or Rituals.

                                                  So the Epic Strength Innate isn't something that a Dragon can get, except by purchasing as a Transformation Knack.
