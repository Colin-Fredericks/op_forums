    Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post
    Has it been discussed yet whether the new edition Nemetondevos are going to follow the French version and give them a PSP based around being “post-Fate” because they already died and their legends are gone?

    It was a clever way to handle how little we know about Gaulish myth - making that the premise around which the Pantheon was built.

    My guess for their predestruction existence is that Geas (possibly under different related names) was a Pan-Celtic PSP, the way the Orisha and the Loa share the same PSP under different names. I suppose, if the Nemetondevos have a PSP that doesn’t fit what it would have been in the ancient world, we’ll see if the Plant Don get Geas (seems plausible) - that would be a strong point in the Pan-Celtic theory’s favor.
    Last Neall said, a chunk of the Nemetondevos are in the cycle of reincarnation (ie, Incarnate candidates), while the others are sitting in their Underworld, really upset.

    The last he said on reincarnation, meanwhile, it involves partially rebuilding your Mantle from the ground up - I think the idea is there's a good part of your Mantle still there, something that embodies the archetypes and power of your previous incarnation, but you have a certain degree of leeway in remaking it.

    So... not all the Nemetondevos are dead, and even the ones who are still have a fair part of themselves in Fate. My hunch is that, essentially, this time round how little we know of the Nemetondevos will be the basis for their Incarnates' play experience - the players get to (re)define what the gods of Gaul are now.
