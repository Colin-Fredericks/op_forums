        Originally posted by varygoode View Post
        So it looks like you guys are throwing out Dying Boxes, is that right? I see bashing and lethal are aesthetic choices for players, while filling your Health Boxes with Aggravated Damage up to your incap box is what kills you. Why this change?
        No single overwhelming reason inspired the change, but a large number of small reasons that began to add up!
        • Dying Boxes were a strange mechanic that showed up almost nowhere else.
        • Dying Boxes were equal to your mundane stamina only, which means someone with Stamina 5 had the same amount as someone with Stamina 5 / Epic Stamina 5, which did not make sense.
        • Dying boxes were mysteriously immune to bashing damage, even if it was a mighty giant smashing you with a club twenty times the size of your body.
        • Dying boxes were mysteriously immune to the attack that put you into the dying state. You could take ten billion damage in one attack and it would still only reduce you to dying. This is actually kind of awesome in a way, but also makes no sense.
        • Dying boxes were healed whenever anyone stabilized you, and there was no limit to how many times someone could be brought back and forth between incapacitated and dying. The difficulty to stabilize becomes trivial at demigod.
        • Dying boxes were lost at a rate of one every action, but the roll to stop bleeding is reflexive and trivial, which created a strange rules conflict.
        These are just a few of the issues with Dying Boxes. While any one or two of them could easily be fixed, you had to be a rules lawyer to keep track of them all!

        Changing the point of death to 'Full Aggravated' goes a long way towards cleaning up and addressing many of these issues. The rule is much smoother to use and easier to remember; it uses established mechanics that players are familiar with; you can actually be killed by someone smashing you into paste; people can be killed by a single overwhelming attack; bashing and lethal damage actually have a harder time killing you now, you cannot be infinitely stabilized, etc.

        Don't forget that this works in tandem with the new rules on aggravated damage only reducing your Soak DV by 1/2!

                    Originally posted by varygoode View Post
                    I feel that everything you said should remarkably affect what Damage Conversion, Divine Damage Conversion, and Unbreakable do as Knacks. How can we House Rule them to make them, well, useful again (especially Damage Conversion)?
                    Unbreakable is already an incredibly useful knack, since there is no limit on the amount of legend you can invest towards soaking something if you want to be extremely epic about it. It also has functionality above Self-Healing because it naturally mitigates "Bad Touch" effects, such as poisons and drugs getting into your bloodstream, magical injuries that resist being healed, some horrible creature laying eggs in you, or other dreadful consequences that taking damage might cause.

                    Divine Damage Conversion also remains powerful, because aggravated damage is twice as effective as lethal damage, and three times as effective as bashing damage at making you dead. Just reducing aggravated down to lethal means you are going to be able to take twice as much damage, unless you are right at the point where any lethal you take is spilling over into aggravated anyway. Of course, reducing the damage from aggravated to lethal means the attack no longer causes you to suffer 1/2 Soak DV. This is not a big deal if you are also heavily invested in Epic Dexterity, but can mean an enormous difference to someone with Epic Stamina as their primary defense.

                    Damage Conversion does need to be investigated. While it does a good job making your enemies have to deal twice as much lethal damage to take you down, once you get to the point where bashing damage is spilling over into lethal it ceases to be useful. It also does not perform double duty protecting you from having your Soak DV reduced the way that Divine Damage Conversion does. Damage Conversion is still completely playable, but it could use a splash of something to make it special.
