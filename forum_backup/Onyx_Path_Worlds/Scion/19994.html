    11-28-2013, 08:56 PM
    I get the weirdest inspiration sometimes. I was playing Arkham Origins and then it hit me: Use some sci-fi to have mortal humans do battle with Scions.

    I know that Scion is supposed to be modern fantasy but I like to mix things up some to keep the players guessing. It keeps the game refreshing for me and also for my players since they have no idea what I will toss at them next.

    Any ideas for what kind of tech/tactics can be used to combat Epic Social/Mental Knacks?

      This depends on what Legend your Scions are. After Legend 5 or so, mortals just stand absolutely zero chance of hurting a Scion. Once they're applying +7 auto-successes to their rolls, Scions just don't care much about getting shot by a tank or whatever mortals can throw at them. Hero-level Scions, however, are not as lucky. They can massively out-class mortals, but in big groups or with heavy combat equipment (like tanks), mortals do stand a decent chance of hurting a Hero.

      Now, if you want to get some weird sci-fi technology involved, then that works. Just have it basically apply Epics to the mortals, or give them psychic powers to mimic supernatural abilities, or whatever. Scifi tech is basically ST's choice because there are no rules for it. You have to make up what it does anyway, so just have it do whatever you want to challenge your Scions with. Just remember they could get their hands on it, and if it provides such nice bonuses for mortals, it'll be utter bonkers in the hands of a Scion.

                      If you are using RAW then mortals are exactly as dangerous as the Storyteller wants them to be. Hero Page 180 gives you access to Full Teamwork and Limited Teamwork, which can be applied to a wide range of actions. You could declare that a large mob of mortals provide limited teamwork on their mental resistance roll because of mob mentality. You could declare that a nationwide manhunt for the scions provide limited teamwork on their investigation or survival rolls to find the band. You could declare that a hundred archers are providing limited teamwork on their archery attacks.

                      Since you are the Storyteller, you decide when mortals become 'redundant'. You could decide that every 10 mortals adds +1 limited teamwork, or every 100 mortals adds +1 limited teamwork, or even every 1000 mortals adds +1 limited teamwork. The rule gives you ultimate flexibility for making mortals dangerous.

                                                  I may have done this in an incredibly dumb way yesterday, but I started trying to think about nuke damage in the context of other weapons that have already been written up. As the highest-damage melee weapon in Core, I chose the Labrys as my unit (8L).

                                                  Now, before trying to scale these things, it's necessary to make particular assumptions. There are three ways that damage dice could scale: roughly linearly, where as you increase the destructiveness you increase the dice by a roughly equal amount (twice as destructive is twice as many dice), roughly logarithmically, where as you increase in destructiveness dice increase but gradually level off, and roughly exponentially, where if you, say, double the destructiveness you'd roll four times as many dice.

                                                  I assume that dice should scale linearly, even though some things in Scion (eg epics) don't. And please note that all these figures are ballpark, and yield figures, where relevant, are sourced from wikipedia which tends to be relatively reliable on such things. I'm totally down for people to critique my numbers, because I did these up on a train yesterday and they may not be totally accurate

                                                  Mk 84 Standard bomb (yield roughly 1 ton of TNT "T", the standard unit of explosive yield): roughly 10 labrys, for on the order of 80 dice of damage, averaging to around 40 levels of lethal damage

                                                  Tomahawk cruise missile (2T): twice a Mk84, for 160 dice or 80 levels on average

                                                  now that you've seen my scaling for explosives, let's go nuclear.
                                                  Little Boy (Hiroshima bomb): 16,000T
                                                  Mk 83 (common US fusion weapon): 1,600,000
                                                  Tsar Bomba (largest fusion weapon ever detonated): 58,000,000T

                                                  So even if you say that one ton of TNT does only one die of lethal, if you nuke your players with a Mk 83, you roll 1.6 million dice, for an average of 800,000 damage.

                                                  And the only core knack that'd let you soak that is Skin Shedding. There isn't anything else I can think of that comes even remotely close to that. You need an Exalted-style perfect dodge or perfect soak.

                                                  Games are a way for us to explore what it means to be human, or inhuman, as the case may be. Let's all have a good time playing them.
