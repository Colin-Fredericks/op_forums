    Originally posted by Kyman201 View Post
    From what it looks like... It depends.

    Crafting isn't Exalted's system of &quot;Okay roll and get an improbable number of successes&quot;, it's more &quot;Get the stuff you need to MAKE it&quot;.

    Using my read of the system, it may be that there's like, an Athletics check to represent a day spent in your forge, perhaps you can use Culture or Persuasion to speak to a smith in order to get a design, perhaps you spend a scene getting the right ingredients (and may require Subterfuge to spot whether the guy is trying to sell crap steel to you).
    I expect we haven’t seen full details on crafting yet since generally (especially at Origins level in a modern setting) mundane crafting isn’t going to create much nuance in products’ capabilities. The main distinction will just be artistry, but with modern tools, your mundane ability to craft a sword or build a gun isn’t going to make a huge game relevant distinction in final product once you bring modern technology in production to bare.

    HOWEVER, once the Forge purview comes into play and we’re talking about the power of magical products of crafting... that will be a whole other story.
