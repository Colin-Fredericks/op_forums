    The Scion "Hunger Games/Tri-Wizard Cup" Tournament...

    12-30-2013, 11:47 AM
    Okay, so since I've been getting so much great feedback on my Birthrights Suggestions thread, I thought I'd ask for some advice on how to flesh out the storyline I'm working with for this game.

    I already mentioned the basic plot of the story in the Suggestions thread, but here it is in summary.

    This story is set in a World where the Titans have not yet escaped. This is more of a "peacetime" setting. Scions exist in large numbers. It's more of a Percy Jackson kind of world. Scions from all over the world learn about their powers and responsibilities at a special school maintained by their respective pantheons. Every year, all the schools choose a single Scion as their champion and these champions compete against the champions of the other schools in a competition that closely resembles the Hunger Games. Unlike the Hunger Games, however, Scions are there on a voluntary basis, and killing every other Scion is not the goal of the contest. Like the Hunger Games, the victorious champion is richly rewarded by his/her pantheon. The Gods themselves don't really gain anything except for bragging rights when their champion wins, not to mention a lot of gods probably do a fair bit of wagering on the outcome.

    The Game resembles the Hunger Games in the sense of the Gods create an isolated, challenging environment for the champions, full of traps, captured Titanspawn, and other hazards in which the Scions will compete. As I said, killing is NOT the central goal of the Game. If it was, then only the combat-oriented Scions would ever get chosen to compete. You just don't send a Scion of Aphrodite into a "to the death" battle against Scions of Tyr, Hachiman, Horus, etc. No the idea behind this Game is more of a "Capture the Flag" kind of deal. Somewhere on the island, there is a "Golden MacGuffin" and victory is achieved by finding the Golden MacGuffin, acquiring it, and bringing it back to your pantheon's base. This allows for a wide range of possible victory scenarios that allow Scions of every stripe to successfully compete.

    Now, Scions can and do die in this competition. As I said, the environment is designed to be hostile to the champions. Scions can be killed by deadly traps, or eaten by monsters, or killed by some environmental mishap. Also? While killing your competition isn't a victory scenario, killing isn't EXPLICITLY forbidden in the rules. Scions CAN kill each other, it just isn't a guarantee of victory to do so. One obvious thing to be mindful of is the Teotl Scion. He/she always has the option to sacrifice an opposing Scion for a quick Legend recharge. If you do kill an opposing Scion, you will have to deal with the fact that that Scion's divine parent (not to mention his/her entire pantheon) is going to be PISSED at you. Not to mention, once word gets around to the other champions that you are killing your competition, what do you think they're going to do if THEY encounter you? They're probably going to decide that they need to kill you before you kill them. So killing is permitted, but not a great idea. There are already several ways you can end up dead in this competition as it is.

    Also like the Hunger Games, Scions can improve their chances for victory by attempting to win "sponsors" from the Gods. The more the Gods like you, the more likely they are to aid you. The rules forbid the Gods from intervening TOO directly, but they can do things to help you out. No, Zeus can't strike the Aesir champion with a bolt of lightning, but he CAN summon up a sudden thunderstorm to make climbing a mountain much more hazardous for him. No, Tyr can't personally come down and beat the crap out of the Scion of Horus, but he COULD dispatch a squad of einherjar to support the Aesir champion. The Scion of Odin is running short of Legend points? Poof! One of Idun's golden apples appears next to him. The Scion of Horus has the Golden MacGuffin and is making his way back to the Netjer's base, but the Scion of Zeus is in hot pursuit? Bam! Geb raises an earth wall to slow him down. Stuff like that.

    Obviously this aid may not necessarily be free. If a god aids you (especially if you win because of it) he/she can easily approach you later and demand a favor for the assist. Some gods may help without thought of recompense, but others won't. Also? Gods CAN aid champions from outside their pantheon. So the Scion of Nuada thought that timely assist came from the Dagda? Nope. Turns out it was Set.....and now he/she owes this foreign god a debt.

    All Scions in this competition are starting out at Legend 5 to allow for some good use of Boons and Knacks to keep things interesting. Each pantheon has one champion only. The competition does not end until one Scion brings the Golden MacGuffin back to his/her base. Yes, this means that a sneaky Scion may just remain in hiding until he/she sees one of the other champions nearing his/her base with the MacGuffin, bash that champion over the head, snatch the MacGuffin and run like Hel (see what I did there ;P) back to his/her OWN base. Also like the Hunger Games, champions can form alliances with each other at will. The obvious understanding being that these alliances are short term. Champions can break their alliances at any time, whether by simply agreeing to go their separate ways or by suddenly turning on each other at a critical moment.

    So, that's the basic premise. My players are already working on their own characters. I will be working on various NPCs to represent the other champions from pantheons that no one claimed. I don't have a specific plan for who should win and who should lose. I just want my players to have some fun knowing that they can do basically ANYTHING to attempt to win this thing. Including fighting each other and/or backstabbing each other if they wish. I'm also thinking I'm going to want to draw up a map of the island so I can decide what they encounter and when.

    Do any of you have any suggestions that I may not have thought of yet? Any rules I may want to add to the competition? Any advice on NPCs I might want to design? Any specific ideas for challenges to put in front of the champions? Any advice on what the "Golden MacGuffin" should actually be? Any advice on stories that may spin off from this Game?

                                  Okay, so I've managed to hammer out most of the details for this Game, and now I'm doing some thinking about what to do for a follow-up. I've been trying to contemplate what kind of stories could spin off from the Game, and I think I've got something. Tell me what you think.

                                  Okay, so I've been playing with the idea of having the competition of the Games kind of spill over into a potentially larger inter-Pantheon conflict. My idea here is a bit of a variation on how the Trojan War got started. So, as I said from the beginning, the victorious Scion in these Games is richly rewarded by the Gods. I'm thinking the Games are celebrated by a victory feast wherein the other Champions and other Pantheons are expected to pay at least a little homage to the Scion (and by extension, his/her Pantheon) who actually won the competition. At some point during the feast, the Victor (may as well use Hunger Games terms, since it inspired this idea) enjoys a special treat........a golden apple.

                                  As soon as the Victor eats the apple, several Gods and Goddesses pretty much instantaneously falls in love with the Victor. Who specifically is affected will be customized according to whoever it is who actually wins. The important thing is that several Gods and Goddesses (all of them from other Pantheons) suddenly want this Victor in the worst way. Fighting erupts. Eventually one of the affected Gods/Goddesses manages to grab the Victor and run off with him/her. A huge scandal erupts. The other affected Gods want to reclaim the Victor for themselves. The unaffected Gods are still outraged by this massive breach of etiquette. Tensions start to build. Pantheons start preparing for war. My player's Band are charged with the task of trying to defuse the situation. They can either try to help calm tensions, they can attempt to rescue the Victor (which may involve a directive from an affected divine parent to return the Victor to him/her), or they can investigate the root cause of this situation.

                                  Now, I know this sounds like it's been done before...and that's the point. Sooner or later, someone comes to his/her senses and goes "Hey, wait a minute! A golden apple comes into play and suddenly we're all at each other's throats over one person? Why does this seem so familiar? ERIS!"

                                  Now, I'm still trying to iron out the particulars, but I don't want it to be THAT obvious. I kind of like the idea that Eris may actually have nothing to do with this incident. The idea of Eris being framed is kind of amusing to me. So, for a twist, I thought it would be fun to reveal that someone else may have set this up. Perhaps more than one "someone else." Or, if Eris is, in fact, involved, then she isn't operating alone and there's some larger effort underway here. What exactly that is, I can't really say right now.

                                  So, what do you all think? How would you run something like this? What advice do you have?
