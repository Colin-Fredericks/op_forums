                        Fun fact: Hitler loathed Himmler's fascination with the occult, as he felt it discredited his cause and valuable resources that could go to the military were instead spent on expedition to try and find proof of sublime energy beings in Tibet. The average German didn't care for any of this and neither did the average Nazi, with only Himmler and his men in the Ahnenerbe bothering with the crazier fringe stuff, and even then less so as the war went on. The most you can really get out of all this for Scion is some German interest into Norse/Germanic runic magic unless you want to go full pulp and have your Scions fighting in the war.
