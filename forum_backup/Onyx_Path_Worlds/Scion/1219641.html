                  The Aesir and the Tuatha are always up for a good scrap, so they'd probably love being involved. The Orisha seem to really appreciate good fighters and athletes, so I could see them being interested. The Teotl love a good fight too, and this kind of entertainment would be somewhat similar to ritual forms of combat they practiced back in the day, just without the whole "sacrifice the losing team at the end" thing. The Manitou don't seem to be a particularly warlike Pantheon, but as I understand it, a lot of Native American tribes were quite fond of forms of warfare that didn't necessarily result in death. The Shen and the Kami are both quite fond of displays of martial arts prowess. I could see Scions of Takemikazuchi being quite fond of bringing sumo wrestling to the sport. I'm sure the Netjer and the Deva would have their own warriors who'd like to be involved too.

                  You can pretty much make a case that just about any of the Pantheons would have at least some interest in being involved. Pantheon pride, if nothing else.

                                              (Here is a link to what part of wrestling i am about to talk about in case you don't know what it is. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manage...onal_wrestling))

                                              Another idea for this promotion is that there are 10 managers, five heel and five face. The heels managers often play dirty to help their clients win, regardless if their opponents a regular face or a mega-face. Regardless of alignment, if a manager is given a mega wrestler as a client, there is a good chance that they will be under direct pressure to make them winners, mostly from fear of getting struck down by that scion's parent if they fail, especially if they are Theoi. But, a heel manager has a even bigger risk, pissing off the Scion themselves. Because, Heel managers often interfere in matches to help their clients win when the official is not looking. If the Scion catches them or if the scion wins, you can fill in the blanks what the scion can do to them. Though, i do wonder what kind of non-lethal temporary punishments Scions from different pantheons can dish up for a interfering manager, besides a beating?
