            I mean I am too fond of the Loa to make Monotheism just Fate or an unknowable. The truth is many Polytheistic religions have inclinations toward a larger being. So Monotheism's definitely a Real thing when I play, but that's the thing.

            I kind of play them as a God of Gods and while mortals can get glimpses and signs of a bigger god, they often misunderstand or misconstrue. I mean look how varied Egyptian Gods are over a period of years and cultures. Now add the entire history of the Earth and all the cultures there-of. People misinterpret God all the time.

            Monotheism is worshiping that source, believing the other gods to be the equivalent of powerful spirits/demons/Angels/ and even forms God takes similar when a god reincarnates into a human, but with God they take the form of a "new" god, giving them individuality and sentience when they do so. Some Pantheons even support this theory, believing themselves to be powerful entities in service to a larger one. Others do not.

            In any case, there is enough evidence that being a Monotheist is not impossible or delusional. God works in mysterious ways, and the idea of a "King of Kings" is not lost on even a scion. Your parents are strong and powerful, no doubt, but no stronger than a D'jinn or an Angel, or a powerful Spirit. Of course that doesn't mean anything else about their powers. And you can still help/fight/serve as a scion. It just means your prayers go to someone else, someone more powerful than all of them combined. It's a hell of a lot less personal and sometimes you doubt if He's listening, but few gods would ever call it crazy.

                                                        Can you get on board the notion that various Pantheons such as the Orisha or the Devas have a Primordial in their ranks, serving as a “Greater God”? Not all of them, to be sure; but many of them.

                                                        If so, how about saying that monotheistic faiths in the World tend to be backed by suitable Primordials who aren't members of Pantheons? This sets the truth of the monotheistic faiths as being more henotheistic than monotheistic, as the existence of other gods is objectively true; but that still allows the Hebrew and Christian God to say “you shall have no other gods before me”.

                                                        This notion hinges on what Primordials are like. If they're as mysterious to the Gods as the Gods are to mortals, then it's possible that all Primordials are in fact different aspects of one being in a manner similar to mantling — or at least all Primordials who serve as the Godheads of monotheistic faiths and/or the “Greater Gods” of Pantheons who have such a thing. And mysterious Primordials are unlikely be active participants in Divine politics; if they were, they probably wouldn't be mysterious. So even if they technically have phenomenal cosmic power well beyond even the gods, Pantheons with a Primordial leader aren't going to have much more than an edge over Pantheons without one.

                                                        This doesn't put Christianity at the center of Scion. Heck, it doesn't even put monotheistic faiths in a better position than polytheistic faiths, metaphysically speaking. It also doesn't make Christianity or Judaism any more true than any pantheistic faith; but its big advantage over other ways of handling the subject is that it doesn't make them any less true either.

                                                        Frankly, the introduction of Primordials and Chosen Scions have gone a long way toward making Scion more accepting of non-pantheistic mythologies, monotheism included.

                                                        My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
