    Monotheistic: is it possible?

    11-14-2015, 08:24 AM
    Does anybody have any thoughts on the possibility that a Scion who rises to godhood abandons their ties to a pantheon completely and establishes themselves as the soul deity of their cult? I understand this might be a bit complicated as it would effectively mean they're defending their overworld and underworld singlehandedly against the Titans, but could it be done, regardless? Scions of such a god might be viewed as saints or prophets by their parent's followers, but the belief in true divinity would fall to their parent alone.

    I know that Scion focuses more on the polytheistic religions. But in a world where gods openly clash with one another, I find it unlikely that there hasn't been at least one cult stepping forward who denounces the others as false gods or attributes them to being avatars of their lord.
