                                            09-07-2016, 10:36 AM
                                            Originally posted by Dr. Quantum View Post
                                            Will it be possible to pick a Primordial or a Titan as the divine parent at character creation?
                                            No.

                                            There are some deities who might otherwise be classed as Primordials (they represent massive concepts, have world-bodies, manifest only in the Overworld) who make Scions - the Goddess Danu, Kitchi-Manitou, among others.

                                            Titans do have children, but I really want to introduce separate Callings and Knacks that cause physical deformities for their Scions, and that just won't be in the core.
