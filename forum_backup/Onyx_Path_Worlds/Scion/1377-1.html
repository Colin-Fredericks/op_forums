                                                    Brythonic Pantheon
                                                    Apologies if this is posted in the wrong place...
                                                    I’ve been trying to come up with a fair and useable British pantheon for ‘modern’ scion purposes. I’ll post what I have so far: if anyone has any ideas for amendments or to fill in the gaps, please let me know and I’ll add in your ideas.
                                                    The list I have come up with incorporates deities that have been worshipped since Roman times, up to the present day. I don’t want to include those deities that can be seen as aspects of existing Scion Gods/Goddesses, particularly the Dodekatheon, so derivatives of Roman Gods such as Mars are out as they tie in with Ares. So far, the Gods I have listed are based on Wiccan, Roman, Norman and to a certain extent Celtic deities. Saxon Gods are out as they are derivatives of the Aesir.
                                                    I have deliberately omitted semi-mythical characters such as Boudica, King Arthur and Robin Hood as they were never worshipped as Gods and (for gaming purposes) are more likely to have been Scions themselves.
                                                    So far the Pantheon look like this;
                                                    Ankou; God of Death, the Grave Guardian.
                                                    Animal (Owl), Civitas, Death, Epic Appearance, Guardian, Prophecy, Psychopomp
                                                    Control, ?
                                                    Andraste; Goddess of war and victory.
                                                    Animal (Hare), Civitas, Epic Charisma, Epic Strength, Epic Stamina, War
                                                    Command, Control, Fortitude, Integrity, Melee, Politics
                                                    Aradia; the Mother Goddess, the Moon Goddess, the Triple Goddess, chief of the Pantheon.
                                                    Civitas, Earth, Epic Intelligence, Epic Stamina, Epic Strength, Fertility, Guardian, Magic, Moon
                                                    Academics, Art, Athletics, Craft, Fortitude, Presence
                                                    Britannia; the Avatar of Britain, Goddess of valour, leadership, and the sea.
                                                    Animal (Lion), Civitas, Epic Charisma, Epic Intelligence, Guardian, Justice, Water
                                                    Academics, Awareness, Command, Integrity, Politics, Presence
                                                    Cernunnos; the Horned God, the Green Man, Lord of the Hunt. Consort of Aradia.
                                                    Animal (Stag, Dog), Civitas, Death, Epic Appearance, Epic Perception, Epic Stamina, Epic Strength, Fertility, Psychopomp, Sun
                                                    Animal Ken, Athletics, Fortitude, Marksmanship, Presence, Survival
                                                    Sulis; Goddess of health, springs, curses and blessings.
                                                    Animal (Snake), Civitas, Guardian, Health, Magic, Water
                                                    Medicine, ?
                                                    Thoughts? Once the Pantheon is complete I'll create a pdf.
