      Oh yeah, that's totally viable. There is an absolutely monstrous amount of stuff that is viable as a Relic for Abrahamic themed stuff, especially if you pick up Christian relics which could be Relics. (fun with homographs!)

      I know Medieval Christian stuff the best due to my work on the conversion of Ireland, so I can give a bunch of suggestions from there to start with.
      • The Bachal Isu is the Bishop's Crozier of Saint Patrick, purportedly originally the staff of Jesus. Given to Patrick when he arrived on a mysterious Isle of Youth by an elderly old man who resided there, a figure I point out is interestingly incredibly similar to Manannan. (Elderly man, lives in otherworldly isles, perpetual youth a common feature, has a habit of handing out treasures) The Bachal Isu is a powerful tool for a Scion, any oaths sworn upon the staff being enforced through divine will, and was used to topple the twelve idols of Crom Cruach as well as the chief golden idol itself. The Bachal Isu was reportedly destroyed by the 16th century Bishop of Dublin as the staff had become a center of local superstition, with the staff being used to swear oaths by the local lay population, and was regarded as more significant to swear an oath upon the staff than the bible itself.
        • Channels Justice, has a Unique Power based off swearing and binding Oaths to the staff itself.
      • The Cloak of Brigid was used by the Saint to trick a local Ri out of his land to construct a monastery. The Saint extracted an oath from the Ri that she would be granted as much land as her cloak could cover for a monastery, before having some of her servants take up the corners of her cloak and start running towards the horizon as the cloak began to unfold infinitely, with the Ri quickly granting a sizable parsel of land rather than accidentally open-ended previous promise.
        • Channels Earth, can be unfolded infinately.
      • The Book of Columba is the greatest sin of arrogance that Saint Columba committed. When a guest at another's home, Columba was shown a new book his host had just had created. Columba became obsessed with the book, and began to sneak out of his room every night to copy the book for himself. When he completed this very illegal copy of the book, and was caught, Saint Columba refused to give up the book even when a local Ri ruled that, according to the presedent that the calves of a cow are owned by the cow's owner, Columba had to hand over the book to the owner of the original. Saint Columba would leverage his political alliances into waging war over this illegal book of his, resulting in many deaths, Columba's side losing, and Columba being exiled from Ireland.
        • Channels Chaos, and War.
      • Columba's Clods were used by Saint Columba to twist the words of his oath to never return to Ireland. Strapping clods of turf from Iona to his feet, Saint Columba rationalized that as he was not stepping foot on Ireland, technically, he could return to Ireland for a time to visit and move through the northen courts of Ulster. While exceptionally Irish, functioning within the system of word play and twisting oaths, the degree to which other Abrahamic figures would accept this is... highly dubious.
        • Channels Chaos, and Justice. Has a Unique Power that allows a social attack against others based on the user's Charisma to compel others to accept oath twisting as long as it's never strictly breaking the wording of the original oath.
      • The Charts of Brendan were not actually of Saint Brendan's own devising, as charting the open ocean was not entirely within the skill of seventh century Irish monks. Later Monks were able to construct these charts based off Saint Brendan's voyage, a complex series of maps which are both for physical traversal as well as maps for spiritual travel within one's self. It appears to follow either the physical or spiritual maps, you must be following both paths at once, the journey a physical and spiritual pilgrimage over Otherworldly seas. A Scion who follows these maps will surely find their way to the mysterious Isle of the Saints, or could swing by and visit Judas on his day off from Hell, but these places are not part of the Tuatha's cosmology, just the distant reaches of Christianity's world cosmology instead.
        • Channels Psychopomp. Allows a Scion to follow in the footsteps of Saint Brendan, though there may be cosmological conflict at points. The travel of Saint Brendan is highly suspiciously similar to the Voyage of Bran, a pagan narrative from two hundred years earlier.

              The Spear of Destiny is a tricky thing to stat out, it is very similar to the Spear of Lugh in that it is a tool of Victory. Essentially, whomsoever wields the spear is sure to be victorious in war. (Technically different from Lugh's spear where the wielder will win any fight with another, it's more like Gungnir I suppose than The Spear of Lugh.) The tricky thing about this is that it is crazy imbalanced, obviously. It is essentially The General, the Avatar of the War Purview, folded up in an Relic. If your group is playing by RAW, there's sadly no easy way to represent it reasonably. However, if you are playing with Relics which reach higher than 5 Dots, you could do something like have a Hero-tier version that channels War and provides buffs to others, scales up at Demigod, and at God is just a pure victory guarentee.

              If you are looking for something more Old Testament-y, the rock Cain used for the first murder is a pretty good Relic. Channels Death, has some benefits when attacking someone you are legally not supposed to be killing. If you are specifically looking for something more Socially inclined, I'd suggest looking for weapons which can be a sign of office rather than a weapon in of themselves. A Bishop's Crozier, that sort of thing. There are not a ton of things like that floating around sadly, for the Old or New Testament. You could check out the Staff of Moses, but that might be pushing it a bit on what qualifies as a weapon.

                                  I've been trying to work on Birthright Relic for a character based on Druids performing human sacrifices (not interested in hearing the historical accuracy of such practices as they are beside the point).

                                  ​As a druidic character, this scion would naturally be part of the Tuatha... specifically a daughter of the Morrigan (a dark goddess that could conceivably tolerate such practices... even encourage them). In this character's specific case, to perform miracles for others, she will ask for a price... not out of greed as many would think... but to judge how far people are willing to go to get what they want... and because changing the will of nature will always have consequences. The price she asks for is as much a test as it is the actual cost for performing the miracle/boon.

                                  ​In 1E, I created an obsidian dagger for her to use that mimicked the Aztec Pantheon boon that allowed one to convert health levels inflicted on others into legend points to power Boons. This was a level 4 Relic that mimicked the effects of a level 4 Boon... which is well within the rules. Unfortunately there were those on this forum at the time that felt that such a relic was overpowered... especially for a Heroic level Scion and out of theme for a Tuatha Scion (they were also quite vocal that Pantheon Specific Purview Boons were NOT allowed to be mimicked by Relics). They argued that human sacrifice was specific to the Aztec Pantheon... and only they were allowed such benefits.

                                  ​Needless to say, I found this reasoning both limiting and quite ridiculous. Many cultures and religions practiced human sacrifice to some degree... it may not be as wide spread or as culturally acceptable as it was with the Aztec culture... but it still occurred, thus it isn't exclusive to the Aztec Pantheon alone. Another suggestion that was made at the time was that PSP Boons had to be bought in order (unlike other Purviews), thus it was felt that a Relic that mimicked the powers needed to buy the 1, 2 and 3 dot boons before being allowed to have the 4 dot boon... and since Relics only went to 5 dots (and one dot buys 1 dot of boons), it was argued that it was mechanically impossible to get the 4 dot Aztec PSP boon (you would need a 10 dot Relic to do so).

                                  It was also suggested that the blood sacrifice rules for magic be used instead... rules that were much more restrictive. The reasoning behind this was simply to preserve the Aztec monopoly on human sacrifice. The problem is that one doesn't need a Relic to perform such blood magic... which defeats the point of having a powerful Relic to help with such things.

                                  ​I wanted a relic for this character that would be as much of a burden/responsibility as it was a benefit. It would be a huge temptation for her to become the instrument of vengeance her mother intended her to be... and would require a monumental strength of will to use it only when absolutely needed... due to the huge cost in using it. The 4 dot Aztec PSP Boon would give her a very readily handy power source to draw from... at the cost of her humanity... a constant temptation to become a bloodthirsty villain slaying everyone in sight (which I believed many feared was the intent of the character, thus the huge opposition I originally had to the character on this forum)... rather than a druid that is constantly seeking to balance the needs with the power one draws forth. For this character, nature is about balance... to change that balance can have consequences... and thus to change things, a price has to be met to keep things in balance. That was to be the focus of the character... but many saw the potential for abuse (and I won't lie, there was definitely that potential) and refused to see the greater potential for character development.

                                  ​Now, with the new Edition coming out, I hope to be able to rework this Relic and Character concept into something that is just as evocative and intriguing... a Scion that is walking a fine line between light and dark... with a Relic that is as much a burden to them as it is a powerful gift... A Scion that many could see as both a hero and a villain... depending on their point of view. This Scion was always meant to explore the shades of grey between the Light and the Dark... because the world isn't just black and white.

                                  There are three types of people in the world... those who can count and those who can't.
                                  I reject your reality and substitute my own!

                                          When I discovered Scion, I had a lot of fun making relics. My first character was a Scion of Hel, so I've got a few things for her and some other frost giants of the Aesir.

                                          First, I made a black mirror with the Death purview. It's called "Sanningen" meaning "truth" in Swedish, and it reveals shape shifters as well as the supernatural origins of other Scions. A Scion of Hel would have a half rotten face in the mirror, while a child of Loki would have fire for hair, and the list goes on. It's like a "scent of the divine" but visual.
                                          He also dealt constant agg damage to anything he touched with his left arm, so I made fur gloves from the goddess Skadi that contained the death touch while he wore the gloves, as well as giving access to the Frost purview.

                                          I made a few other relics for other death gods (the game was heavily focused on death gods, despite my character being the only child of a death god in the entire band) like Baron Samedi's death camera, that allowed access to the Death purview and if a Scion took a picture of a corpse they could see their death being replayed. Hades had a gemstone bracelet that summoned valuable gems out of the ground and had the Earth purview. Xipe Totec had a flaying knife that would give the user the Death purview as well as bonus dice to intimidation rolls.

                                          Hopefully these give you ideas for your game, I hope it all goes well.
