                                                    Okay, so I met with both of my friends who are playing the Ishtar and Tsuki-Yomi Scions. We haven't completely ironed out everything, but some progress has been made.

                                                    For the Ishtar Scion? Telgar, you'll be happy to know that he loved the idea of the beard bling for his Health relics. He's not certain he plans to do the whole "Babylonian goat beard" look just yet, but he said even if he decides to go clean-shaven, those beard ornaments would work just as well in one's hair if it's long enough. But one way or the other, he thinks the beard bling idea is really cool. Especially the idea of it being made from the same milk pails Tammuz brought to Ishtar.

                                                    As for the Fire Purview? He's thinking he may not even NEED a relic in the strictest sense. He is thinking about using the Me Purview for his Fire Boons. The first dot of Me, of course, makes it possible to wield the given Purview without the need for a relic. Failing that? He is considering letting his Fire relic be a weapon. He's definitely intrigued by the Babylonian mace designs we looked up, so he's thinkin about a mace. On the other hand, he pointed out that a mace seems to suggest "Earth" rather than "Fire" to him. To that end, he looked into some Mesopotamian sword designs and he was quite intrigued that the Mesopotamians also used a sword very similar to the khopesh. So he's considering that as well.

                                                    For Illusion? He's still not quite sure. He's considering just going with a ring or something. More information is needed.

                                                    For Chaos? He's thinking about either another ring, or perhaps some kind of amulet.

                                                    As far as Creatures or Guides or Followers? He was very intrigued by the Zu bird. He was also quite intrigued by the Lamashtu concept. The idea of having his own pet succubus is definitely interesting to him.

                                                    For the Tsuki-Yomi Scion? As usual, this player is waffling a bit. He's now considering switching to the Celestial Bureaucracy and going with Nuwa (she also has a lot of the powers he's looking for.) Nonetheless, he did discover that the Japanese pretty much use the same Astrology system as the Chinese, so for his Stars relic, he's considering going with this really cool feng shui compass he found online. He thinks it may even serve as his Psychopomp relic as well.

                                                    So that's pretty much the latest.
