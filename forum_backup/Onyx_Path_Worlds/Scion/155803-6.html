Once all your health boxes are full of lethal damage, any further lethal damage inflicts aggravated damage instead. When all your health boxes are full of aggravated damage, you are officially dead."

            Hey everyone,

            We just need to let everyone know that Gotham By Night has asked that we take down their Artistry, Magic, Prophecy, Sky, and Thunder Purviews, and their Dvoeverie, Heku, Me, and Ori.

            We had been given permission to use their works, but because of some legal complications, they have asked us if we mind taking those things down. We entirely understand where Gotham By Night is coming from, and are happy to comply with their wishes.

            Because of this, those Purviews and Pantheon Specific Purviews will be missing from our site for a little while, which is something we do apologize for. We are entirely refocusing ourselves in order to try and resolve this, pausing our work on Death, War, and additional Pantheons for the time being in order to fill in these openings.

            Me is going to be finished first, because we already have a mountain of research material on that pantheon, and the result will probably look similar to the old Me. I am going to spend my time working on doing research for the Orisha and the Bogovi so we can develop Pantheon Specific Purviews for the both of them. Aynie is going to begin to work on creative designs for a new Sky and Thunder.

            We are very sorry for any inconvenience this causes anyone running any games with our rules. We hope to get things back on track as soon as we are able. As always, if anyone has any questions, or suggestions, just post them here.

                                                Oh, it is not bothersome in the slightest, don't worry about that.

                                                Things are going slightly slowly at the moment since we are working on multiple fronts, but things are coming along. The three Purviews we are focused on at the moment are Storms (Thunder, Lightning, and Rain), Me (The PsP of the Anunna), and the PsP of the Bogovi which we have not worked on a name yet for.

                                                For Me, we are going for the same general 'feel' of Purview as before. It will be a Purview improving other Purviews, making the wielder of the Me of Flames greater at wielding the Fire Purview, and so on.

                                                For Storms, we are attempting to create a balance between the offensive thunder and lightning, with environmental effects, and rain powers. Rain was so, so incredibly important in the ancient world, and thus mythologically, but it is difficult to create powers using rain that players would be interested in.

                                                For the as of yet unnamed PsP of the Bogovi, the research I have done has lead me to decide that a PsP based around a personal cult would work best. Having a priest, a horse, and many other things that the Bogovi are very much not supposed to have, but have anyways. For, despite how much the Bogovi love their laws, not a single one of them actually follows them when it is inconvenient for them. So, everyone has a horse they ride in secret and have it kept by specific people in a specific location when they are not riding it in private, away from the gaze of their 'Over-King'.

                                                One of our trio has had some computer issues, which has slowed things. But, we are still working away to try to recover and get back to as we were before.

                                                Just as a side, when we get around to it, the Tuatha's Antagonist Titan is going to be the Titan of War. But, that won't be for a long time yet.

                                                As always, if anyone's got any questions or comments, feel free! Always happy to answer questions.

                                                        Originally posted by bigwhitt View Post
                                                        I don't know if anyone has said any thing about this, I;ve searched through the posts but cant find anything. I have always enjoyed playing my Scion character as a type of Palladin. One whose able to protect and heal. I enjoy him as a Tuatha, normally a scion of Lugh. However I noticed Lugh does not have the Guardian or Health Purviews in this edition. I noticed that none of the Tuatha have these traits. However, almost all of them have the Purview of War. This seems to unbalance the Pantheon. Is there a reason for this or is it something that can change?
                                                        The changes to the Associated Powers and Associated Abilities of the Pantheons were done originally by John and Anne at Gotham by Night, but they have all been made to attempt to bring the profiles of these Gods better in line with their Mythological counterparts than RAW managed to do. So, there were some rather drastic changes to the Tuatha. But, I adore Irish Mythology and can explain every change you would like for them.

                                                        So, the reason Lugh no longer has Guardian is there is not a single myth that I know of where Lugh is in the role of a guardian, or a protector. Lugh is not a warden on the walls, or a God who dispensed blessings to safeguard others. Additionally, I don't know of any cult worship of Lugh that identifies him as such a Deity. I have no idea why he was given it in RAW, but Lugh is not, and has never been (Historically) a Guardian Deity.

                                                        Now, the reason they gave Lugh Health is that he once healed CuChulain when he was fighting at the ford. Also, sometimes he introduces himself as a physician or doctor when requesting entrance to Tara. But, neither of these are sufficient reasons to give Lugh Health. A single account of him preforming it, and him boasting about his ability to be a doctor don't particularly compare with Apollo slaughtering the Greek Army with plagues, or Mokosh bringing humanity into existence. Additionally, once again, we don't see any cult presence for Lugh as a healer or plague bringer.

                                                        Now, the reason for the Tuatha having many members who have War is because they are composed of many War Deities. It's just how it has turned out for them. They are an incredibly war centered Pantheon.

                                                        Lugh has War for he is the Champion of the Tuatha de Danann. He is their war leader, and warrior without peer. Wielding his great blazing spear and his sling.

                                                        Nuada is the greatest King that has ever ruled Ireland, and has lead the Tuatha successfully in war against the forces of their foes many times. He lead his people against the Fir Bolg when they first invaded Ireland alongside The Dagda and Ogma.

                                                        Ogma has War since he was the Champion of the Tuatha before Lugh, and lead the armies of the Gods against the Fir Bolg as I said. Additionally, we have evidence of Ogma being connected to the idea of 'eloquence', and getting armies ready for battle beforehand by speaking praises and other great oral works to them.

                                                        The Dagda is a colossal man, wielding his great club and draging his other 'club' behind himself as he trudges through the battlefields. He has lead the Tuatha in warfare, and is written as a great warrior. Though, very importantly for the Irish, not a Champion.

                                                        And, lastly, The Morrigan herself is a Goddess of battlefield frenzy, death, and destruction. Her crows pick out the eyes of the dead, and she flies over battlefields to strike blind fury into warriors. Additionally, she has brought the Tuatha secret knowledge on how to defeat their enemies in battle before, and has almost single handedly won battles for them.

                                                        I do not understand though how this "Unbalances" the Pantheon though, would you mind terribly explaining what you mean so I can help work things out?

                                                        But, in sum. The changes that we use to the God profiles are to keep them more in line with their mythological and and historical realities. To best represent the Gods of cultures long past through their own eyes as best we can. RAW did not do a spectacular job with this sort of thing in our mind, as can be seen through the large changes.

                                                        EDIT: Oh, I do want to add that the Tuatha represented are only the Major Gods (Legend 12 Gods). The Tuatha have three very skilled Health Gods (Dian Cecht, Airmed, Miach) though their powers, legends, and worship don't indicate that they would be on equal footing as the Tuatha we have listed. I am not sure for Guardian though, I know of no figures who serve as a protector in Irish mythology. But, all the more room for a new Scion to fill a void!
