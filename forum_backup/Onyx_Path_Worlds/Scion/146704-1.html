    In that spirit, here is another line of argumentation. If Scion:God had not included Avatar as a Background, would we be even having this conversation?                         This became so much more than I expected. Thank for everyone who offered their opinion.

                        I approached my players Saturday and they all agree that it should simply come with being a god. So, in that way, problem solved.

                        I agree though, that it is ultimately an issue of fate bonds and fateful aura when talking about the ramification of Avatar use. The way I see Fateful Aura being balanced is this – if you're a big enough deal to the cosmos or even just the overworld (implying a high Legend) then your Fateful Aura has created a sort of divine expectation of you from other entities. Thing happen and you've always dealt with them before.

                        Your Fateful Aura is like a divine gravity in the vacuum of existence. Once a fated occurrence is triggered and first pulled toward you – it doesn't stop coming at you. So whether you're Legend 12 and sitting in the overworld or you're Legend 3 and cavorting with mortals down in their realm, that event is still going to happen. If you're refusing to deal with it then someone else likely will, and the resulting fatebonds to them will not go over lightly when they find out what's going on. This is why Justice Gods get super-serious with other gods shirking their "responsibilities." It's why Order, Duty, Loyalty and to a certain extent Harmony and Piety are the course-correcting Virtues that they are. Sure a God could hypothetically try to stay in Avatar form, but something is going to prompt them back into their true form eventually.

                        All Avatar should be expected to do IMO is mitigate fatebonds. Gods are people too, and they're prone to have fun with their abilities when not embroiled in the most serious of Fateful Aura events. Avatar is usually how Gods get to do that and not make the players suffer as a result.
