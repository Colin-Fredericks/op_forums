    Animal Purview (Ape)= Human?

    08-08-2017, 04:26 PM
    I know this is a matter of viewpoint and is mainly anthropological in nature, but would you consider Animal purview (Ape) to include humans? Personally having taken several anthropology courses, I often think of humans as just a highly evolved ape species. I mean chimpanzee DNA is is only different from human by less than one percent. So personally I would include humans as a great ape. Would you consider this acceptable in your campaign? I would consider communication type boons to ensure a lack of mixed signals or misunderstandings, allowing the scion to communicate on a deeper intuitive level with human beings.

              I would potentially allow a Scion intentionally pushing to build that sort of thing into their personal Legend to use Animal (Apes) to influence humans. However, that comes with two caveats. Firstly, just like Lula mentioned, I'd allow it to fiddle with the animal side of humans as long as it didn't start messing with other Purviews areas. Secondly, I wouldn't allow just anyone to use the Animal Purview to do this.

              Purviews don't deal with the scientific, exact thing. They are a bit more conceptual than that, and can get very culture-specific. The best example of this I think is darkness. Natural, scientific darkness from our reality, and the Darkness of the Divine of Scion are very different things. Darkness is almost tangible in myths, it almost has physical mass, and it exists independently of light. In our reality, darkness is an absence of light, where as in the reality of Scion, Darkness is something that can exist independently. A colossal snake embodying darkness can swallow the sun, night is draped like a blanket on the land, night is a horse, that sort of thing.

              So, for most Pantheons (I don't know any Pantheon that made humanity out of apes, but there might be one somewhere) I would rule the Animal Purview doesn't effect humans. Humans, in their cosmology, are not related to apes. They are made of clay, ants, the bones of giants, and many other things. I might allow someone with an appropriate Purview mess with humans if they had an appropriate Purview and were somewhere where their Pantheon's cosmology was 'dominant,' like their Overworld, or somewhere on earth that is incredibly influenced by them. So, I might allow an Anunna God or Goddess fiddle with humans physical shape if they had the Earth Purview for example.

              If a Scion wanted to weave the modern, real-world concept of humans sharing a common ancestor with modern-day apes, become a Deity of Evolution, I would probably allow them to have a bit of a capacity to influence humans with the Animal Purview. I'd need them to establish it in-character though first, make it a part of their legend, weave it into their Pantheon's story. That sort of thing.

              Edit: I probably wouldn't allow Animal to influence languages or cultural things. It could do body language, but I put actual language as a technological development. Understanding humans doesn't let you understand the Hittites specifically, you just get the very simple, baseline of 'human,' without any of their cultural or technological developments. Thinking about this, it sounds a bit like the 'Wild' Purview the 2E Devs have been talking about.

                          Science is really interesting in Scion. The biggest thing you need to do is sit down with your storyteller and figure out how their cosmology works. Some believe that all myths are true. Others pick and choose between primary myths, and select others to be nothing more than stories or misunderstandings. A few change their cosmology between every game just to mix things up and explore new and interesting stories.

                          Once you figure out the general idea behind cosmology in your particular game, you can talk with your storyteller about how you want to play a scientist character. Discuss the kinds of mysteries you want to answer. Science is the systematic study of the structure and behavior of the physical and natural world through observation and experimentation. So what happens when your pantheon (for example) created Humanity out of Clay, and they can objectively prove it to you? In this version, Humanity has more in common with the Earth purview than Animal (Apes).

                          But better yet, what does your scientist conclude when they stand before the gods of a different pantheon and they create Humanity by bleeding upon some ground up bones? What happens when your scientist is given two conflicting stories that can both be proven? Does your scientist settle upon the answer being subjective reality warping? Or is there some other, greater, hidden universal truth that your storyteller has placed into the setting for your scientist to discover?

                          Science can be a lot of fun in Scion, but it often takes you places you never expected.

                                  In the real world, we tend to chalk mythology up to being allegory and metaphor, but in the world of Scion, mythology is taken in a very literal sense. Meaning it's difficult to tell what can and can't happen when it comes to supernatural powers.
