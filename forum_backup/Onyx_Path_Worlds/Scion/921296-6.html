                            Phoebe Finbar
                            Divine Parent: Poseidon
                            Callings: Leader (Favored), Sage, Warrior

                            Origin Path: Haunted Misfit (raised in a single parent household, spent most of her life running away from home chasing dreams and stories; Asset Skills: Occult, Survival)
                            Role Path: "Doesn't that make you angry? Don't you care?" (After returning from Greece, dedicates herself to activism and stirring the hearts of people; Asset Skills: Leadership, Persuasion)
                            Relationship Path: "You were Fated for more than childish dreams." (Is opposed to her father's plans for her, and is willing to fight to get her way; Asset Skills: Close-combat, Athletics)

                            Born in Ocean Beach, California. Phoebe was the only child to a single mother. As costs in California continued to grow out of hand, they eventually settled in the East County area of San Diego, the Alpine/Descanso area, so right up next to the Cleveland National Forest. It was an area that still had a bit of an 'old west' vibe, with lots of restaurants evoking the imagery, quite a few horse ranches. She spent a lot of time dealing with people riding them down hiking trails, or through the areas where she'd usually play, and got acquainted with the riders and their horses. Especially the horses. Things like The Firebringer Trilogy and The Last Unicorn were incredibly important to her when she was young.

                            She and her mother didn't get along. Phoebe often ran away from home, scrounging up bus fair to make it at least as far as the coast. For reasons she couldn't express, the ocean had a soothing effect on her, especially the sensation of wave after wave hitting her.

                            Growing up in a conservative community with exposure and access to liberal ideas embittered her quickly, and her mother, being of Irish descent with strong values, was not enthusiastic about her daughter's interest in pagan gods, books of magic, or the odd dreams she'd complain about. In one instance she went so far as to burn all of her daughter's books, on another occasion, she personally tried to perform an exorcism on her daughter.

                            Life as a teenager wasn't much better. More and more she began to realize that a lot of the truths she'd been fed as a girl, the things she'd taken for granted as true, weren't. Her mother never bothered saving money for her college, and claimed that she should just 'get a scholarship' or 'get a grant'. The recession hit, and small towns were hit hard. She watched as one by one, stores began to close down. Any hopes of employment in her home town dwindled to waitressing at the same steakhouse her mother worked at. Indeed, her mother was increasingly insistent that Phoebe get to work to help pay the bills. Phoebe spiraled into a passion devouring depression, and as a desperate act, stole what money her mother had managed to save in order to fly to Greece. Corinth, more specifically.

                            She'd had enough of humanity and made for the Fountain of Pirene. Heartbroken, she arrived only to find that the great spring of Pegasus had completely dried up. She leaped down to get a better look, and the moment her feet hit the ground, it began to shake. Water bubbled up, sweeping the dirt and dust away from the ruins. Phoebe drank from the fountain, uncertain of what to expect, but what happened is not something she ever imagined.

                            She expected some form of transformation, like all the Greek myths, maybe even into a winged horse, like Pegasus himself, or even just a regular one. Anything would be preferable to human life with human problems, really. Instead, she was visited by her father, Poseidon. He laid out in no uncertain terms that she is his daughter, that she was to put aside childish dreams and fantasies, that Fate had a greater part for her to play in service to her divine family.

                            Phoebe balked at that, recalling the story of Pegasus, broken by a golden bridle, of noble Areion, who in legend became little more than a king's favored mount. And her—she'd been completely ignored until her existence was convenient for him. It was in that moment she realized: if Poseidon was her father, then those two horses of legend were her brothers, her actual siblings.

                            Phoebe turned her back on her father, only grudgingly accepting his commands, if at all, and using what few brithrights he bestowed sparingly. After returning home, her mother confronted her about the money, then threw her out. But to Phoebe's astonishment, she learned that she'd become quite a bit more sturdy since her trip to the fountain. She still needed food, shelter, rest, but she could accomplish a lot more than her friends on a lot less. She took to the road, crashing on couches of friends and anyone of like mind.

                            Through blogs and videos, her legend grew, especially when odd things start to come out of the woodwork wherever she goes.

                            Purview wise she focuses on Metamorphosis, Beast (Horses) and Epic Strength, as she grows into her Ichor, she starts becoming increasingly dissatisfied with humanity, feeling the call of her equine kin more keenly. She spends increasingly more time as a horse - or rather, something a bit different. She becomes to horses what humans are to chimpanzees, and adopts a new overarching calling - to steal the fire of the gods and use it to uplift horses to the same level as humanity itself. (In our games, we tend to use the theft of fire myth as a parallel to Sophia breathing divine sparks into humanity)

                            Stat wise, Phoebe is more Physical/Social, hot-blooded and quick to solve her problems by punching them (or kicking, as the case may be). Of note is Hermes, who is genuinely amused by her motivations and dreams, and takes it upon himself to place her in situations where her strength won't be of much use. She has some involvement with the Netjer through a bandmate, and has a scion of Dagda as a bit of a brother figure, with her Irish blood drawing some interest by unrelated gods, notably Macha and Fand—who is always willing to explain to Phoebe that it's perfectly okay to abduct guys she likes and turn them into stallions. Where's the harm in that?

                            Plus some potential involvement with Enbarr and Sleipnir, because horses with divine bloodties unite, right?

                            Antagonist wise, the greatest threat is Athena. While Poseidon was responsible for creating horses, Athena is largely given credit for helping humans to domesticate and tame them, she's also the one who gave Bellerophon the golden bridle, which was necessary in order to break/tame Pegasus, Phoebe's older sibling. In the context of the game there is an organization forming that rounds up scions and puts them to work, known as Aeterna. It's run by a Scion of Horus, with one of Athena's daughter's near the top of the power structure, and constantly attempts to get in Phoebe's way. There's also a scion of Dian Cecht to round out their group.

                            There's no chance Zeus would stomach the Hubris of a someone less than a god stealing the flame of the gods, but for now Phoebe is beneath his notice, so Athena contents herself with tormenting her cousin through scions of her own. It'll be interesting to see how far she gets in her ambitions.

                                    Ula Stone
                                    Divine Parent: Skadi
                                    Callings: Sage (Favored), Healer, Lover

                                    Origin Path: Carved from basalt. (Created by Skadi's husband, Njord, by carving her from basalt, with waves and seafoam for hair; Asset Skills: Athletics, Survival)
                                    Role Path: "High wind warning for the rest of the week, heavy snows." (Her weather forecasts are always right; Asset Skills: Culture, Science)
                                    Relationship Path: "You have all of my grace, and his kindness." (Kind-hearted daughter of Skadi.; Asset Skills: Medicine, Stealth)

                                    In the time after Skadi joined the Aesir in Asgard, demanding her father's lands as well as weregild, she also demanded a husband. In response Odin blindfolded her, and bade her choose from the unmarried men of Asgard by feeling their feet. She chose the one with the finest feet, hoping to win the beautiful Baldur, but it was Njord, the Vanir Sea-King. He was good-looking and kind enough, so they married. Skadi's homeland, Thyrmheim in Jotunheim, and her inherited lands in Asgard, were in the mountains; but Njord could not bring himself to dwell long away from the sea. His home in Asgard, Noatun, and his Vanaheim home on the other side of the ocean, on the other hand, made Skadi uncomfortable; she complained about the noise of the sea-birds and the constant sound of the tide.

                                    As his bride's displeasure continued to mount, Njord led his daughter Freya to the shores of Noatun, there he carved a small girl from dark basalt, and fixed sea foam for her hair. Bidding his daughter to work her seidr magic, to sing a spell around the work of art, Njord took and applied a drop of his wife's blood, and the creation came to life - a daughter to his bride in spirit, if not by birth. The girl was a fine thing, with dark brown skin and hair the same shining white of the sea foam used to form it. When he brought his creation home to his wife, she was furious. Fidelity, responsibility, and oaths are all of utmost importance to Skadi, and here her husband had assigned to her the greatest responsibility of all, without her consent. It was the final straw in their relationship, and she left Njord's hall forever for her mountain home.

                                    Heartbroken, but fearing for the child's safety (he knew well the Aesir and their track record toward Jotuns) Njord called up Mani the moon god, and bid him take the child in his chariot to a place where she could grow in safety, for Mani is often known for his stewardship of abused and forsaken children. Mani did as he was asked, and a small child was left, appearing somewhat mysteriously at a designated drop off point, outside a fire station in Maine. When the men found her, there was only the basket, a small girl, and a pendant reading 'Ula'.

                                    Ula Stone grew up in the system, and she hated the sea. Whenever she stared at it for any appreciable length of time, a longing of the heart she couldn't explain would steal over her, one that could darken her thoughts for days. She had a quick mind and a kind soul, however, and when she graduated she moved as soon as she was able, traveling south to upstate New York. She settled in a small town within the Adirondack mountain range, and found what work she could. As a way of connecting with a community where most families had existed for generations - had known each other for generations, Ula started a community blog, chronicling events and whatever news in the area she could scrounge up. On a whim, she began doing astrology reports and weather readings, but an odd pattern began to emerge - where it came to the weather she was always, 100%, without fail correct. More locals began to follow her blog, placing bets on when the streak would come to an end. Old men and women would joke that their knees would ache whenever Ula walked by. Before long, Ula began to spread her net beyond the town, first to the mountains surrounding them, and then to much of New York itself. The community even took her and put her on local television, where she'd broadcast her weather reports an hour each night. Ula became a curiosity, going so far as to put together a late night talk show for all the night owls.

                                    It was a year plagued by storms, New York city was racked by hurricane winds, flooding, and record snow fall, the mountains weren't much better. Several hikers and vacationers had gotten stranded, separated from town by storms Ula had failed to predict - the streak was over. Rounding up men and women from her community, Ula built a party to go in search of the missing people, but over the course of the expedition, she became separated from the group.

                                    Time passed, and while others might have succumbed to hypothermia, Ula felt - fine, really. The wind and the winter cold was harsh, but bearable. She became more concerned when she found the search party - or what was left of them. Many of them had been ripped open, their blood frozen solid inside of them. Something was hunting them through the woods.

                                    It was only by Skadi's intervention that Ula survived, she took the creation that was meant to be her daughter in, explained the world to her. She told her that the bridge she'd hoped to build between the Aesir and the Jotuns was crumbling, and that more and more, the world would not be a safe place. Skadi begged her daughter's forgiveness, and charged her with stopping the chaotic actions of her kin, before they do something that can't be taken back.

                                                            This is a translation from a Gurps game my sister once ran, it was similar to Scion, but focused more on initiating into various magical traditions and mystery cults.

                                                            Isabella (Citlalmina) Heladio
                                                            Divine Parent: Ixchel
                                                            Callings: Leader (Favored), Hunter, Warrior

                                                            Origin Path: Born of Night, taken in by the Moon. (Descendant of a long line of werejaguars which can trace their origins back to Tezcatlipoca, adopted by Ixchel; Asset Skills: Close-Combat, Survival)
                                                            Role Path: "I will restore the old empire, and banish the bloodlust that has consumed the gods." (Ambition is to return to Mexico and return it to the glory it enjoyed during the height of the Mayan Empire, banishing the influences of Catholicism and the Spanish; Asset Skills: Culture, Leadership)
                                                            Relationship Path: "The path of your father is seductive, I chose you for your strength." (Adopted scion of Ixchel, Jaguar goddess of war, childbirth, and medicine; Asset Skills: Athletics, Medicine)

                                                            Isabella Heladio belongs to a long line of Jaguar priestesses out of Mexico. The gods of the Maya sought a means to change the Fate of their people, performing complex often dark rituals from the dawn of their pantheon's existence. The magic they worked changed them, turning them into something new, and ultimately more savage and primal than their previous incarnations. In the end, they could not change Fate, and their empire was all but lost by the time the Spanish arrived. The Aztecs, and what remained of the Maya, were clever and resourceful, keeping their lineages pure in whatever ways they could, hiding their very languages under the noses of the Spanish. Isabella's grandmother was the first to leave Mexica, settling in Brooklyn. She had two daughters, both of them possessed of the same gifts she possessed, but her youngest involved herself with the occult, and same sorts of gangs she had hoped to spare them from by leaving their homeland. In an effort to alter her daughter's course, they both perished, leaving Helena, Isabella's mother, alone.

                                                            Helena swore off her inhuman nature, performing a ritual to cast the jaguar spirit from her, body and soul. Afterward she swore off the occult and chose to acknowledge only her Spanish heritage. She moved to Manhattan, met a man named Francisco, a doctor from Spain, and they soon married. They had two children, Isabella and Adrian, of which Isabella was the oldest. Both of them were curious about their heritage and ancestry, their mother's defensiveness only encouraged them. Isabella committed herself to study, using iron determination in place of a quick mind, and eventually was accepted into Columbia university. Adrian, still in High School, took a different approach, one eerily reminiscent of Helena's own sister. Isabella's life was relatively normal, until her brother got himself involved with a gang called 'Las Cinco Muertes'. One day, when picking Adrian up from school, she confronted them and their leader, ordered them to leave her brother out of whatever schemes they were up to. A fight broke out with her primal heritage more than carrying her through, until one of the leader's henchman shot her.

                                                            Her dreams in the hospital were clouded, like smoke trapped in obsidian. In her dreams she experienced the thrill of the hunt, of running on all fours, of sacred blood rites and sacred pools deep underground. After she left the hospital, life was quite a bit different. She became increasingly distant from her family, exploring magic and her newfound shapeshifting powers, she didn't notice things were going south until the cult abducted her brother.

                                                            The leader of Las Cinco Muertes, 'the five deaths', was a man calling himself 'Mecatl', with a strange accent and even stranger ambitions. He gathered to himself four Lieutenants, and initiated each of them into his cult by persuading them to commit specific highly ritualized murders: Javier, death by hanging. Thomas, death by poison. Demian, death by drowning. Claudio, death by burning. And Mecatl himself, whose favored method was ritually flaying his enemies alive. What's more, he represented the last in a line of Mayan Kings, and murdered his elder brother in order to seize a birthright - the Bracelet of Kings, a relic that the city states of the Mayan Empire spent its final years warring over. It possessed the power to open various existent gates to the Overworld. Mecatl's ambition was to use his cult, the bracelet, and a daughter of Tezcatlipoca, to open the gates to Xibalba, so that he might ride forth as a god among men, in service to the Xibalbans.

                                                            But Isabella, or Citlalmina, as she came to call herself, was far too stubborn to serve as a puppet. Adrian, however, was of weaker will, and in a far more vulnerable place. 'His' own spirit began to stir, not long after her sister's, with Thomas's help, he began to dose Adrian with hormones and drugs without their knowing. The more her body transitioned, the more adamant her primal spirit became, infecting her thoughts and influencing her dreams. Horrified and confused, Adrian returned to the only group that had offered her answers in the past. Mecatl took her in, promising to help Adrian 'prove he's a man'. The gang tormented her, demeaning her even more so than other women in a dark exploration of how hispanic gangs often respond to queer behavior. Adrian was little more than an animal to them.

                                                            Finally, Mecatl led Adrian into the Overworld, to the gates of Xibalba, and offered him the chance to prove once and for all whether she was a man, or wild beast. He began a ritual, and try as Adrian might, she couldn't resist joining in, her jaguar spirit asserting full control as she changed for the first time. The gate to Xibalba began to open, and in a moment of realization and horror, she threw herself against it, forcing it shut.

                                                            Mecatl was furious, cursing her for nothing but a wild animal. With the spirits he had enslaved, he captured and imprisoned Adrian, throwing her into some old temple to a forgotten god.

                                                            With the help of Hypatia (who managed to survive Alexandria by making a deal with the goddess Bastet, a deal which offered her some degree of immortality) Isabella managed to track down Mecatl's lieutenants, and with the help of her friends, she killed each of them, save Demian who had already turned away from the cult after all he'd been forced to do to Adrian, and Thomas, who Mecatl had betrayed by using his own magically enhanced cocktail of hormones and drugs against him. Isabella confronted her mother, forcing her to accept the spirit she'd struggled to cut from herself, and set out to stop Mecatl once and for all.

                                                            A skeletal jaguar spirit, a shade sent by Tezcatlipoca himself, had already warned Mecatl of their arrival, and when Isabella found him he was garbed in full regalia, his birthright of flayed skin (his brother's) proudly on display. Isabella fought him, while he and the spirit tempted her in the voice of her divine father. In the end she only barely managed to kill him, and then only by the help of her friends. The gate to Xibalba closed completely, sealing itself, and she claimed the Bracelet of Kings for herself.

                                                            Ixchel came to her then, adopting her as her own and praising her for her resistance to her father's manipulations, a presence she still feels in her dreams, speaking to her through a smokey obsidian mirror she can never long be rid of. Isabella left Manhattan for Mexico, armed with the Bracelet and a few other birthrights, she hopes to seize power and bring about sweeping reforms that can make Mexica a place of science, academia, and art.

                                                            Adrianna (Ichtaca) Heladio
                                                            Divine Parent: Xochiquetzal
                                                            Callings: Lover (Favored), Hunter, Guardian

                                                            Origin Path: Jaguar spirit trapped in a human body. (Exhibited transgender tendencies from an early age, forced to conceal it due to cultural norms and shame; Asset Skills: Empathy, Subterfuge)
                                                            Role Path: "I will not live in my sister's shadow, and inspire Legend in my own right." (Hopes to restore respect for women, and with the help of fertility rites, reestablish her people ; Asset Skills: Culture, Medicine)
                                                            Relationship Path: "You are the moon to your sister's sun, just as radiant, but easier on the eyes." (Adopted scion of Xochiquetzal, goddess of women, beauty, and female sexual power; Asset Skills: Occult, Persuasion)

                                                            Isabella's younger sister, growing up in Hispanic and Latino communities, even with a home in upscale Manhattan to retreat to, did not experience much support for her 'odd behaviors' from an early age. She quickly learned to cover these up, burying them in shame and self-disappointment. She instead flung herself into proving her worth as a man, something she had a hard time accomplishing when her older sister was seemingly more of a man than she could ever be. When she got into fights—and she did, often—she always won, Adrian usually needed her to come to the rescue. Things were going to be different with Las Cinco Muertes, she felt accepted. She had her own way to connect to their roots, and her sister stepping in to interfere only pushed her in deeper.

                                                            Of course, that all went to hell when her body started changing with seemingly no rhyme or reason. When feelings began to build around herself and one of Mecatl's lieutenants, a soft spoken boy named Demian. On more than one occasion he'd stopped just short of asking her to come away with him, to leave Mecatl and his cult behind, until they found some place they could be safe. After Mecatl imprisoned Ichtaca, Demian sought out Isabella and her friends, and was instrumental in helping her locate Mecatl in the spirit world.

                                                            While trapped inside an old abandoned temple, Ichtaca met the goddess Xochiquetzal, who claimed precious little worship or acknowledgement in the modern age. She took Ichtaca in, comforting her until such a time as she was able to escape, and help her sister seek revenge on Mecatl.

                                                            Mecatl
                                                            Divine Parent: Xipe Totec
                                                            Callings: Leader (Favored), Warrior, Liminal

                                                            Origin Path: Last king of the Maya (The last in the bloodline, grew up on a farm with his grandfather and brother. On his Grandfather's death, murdered his brother to claim his birthright; Asset Skills: Close-Combat, Occult)
                                                            Role Path: "I will ride into Xibalaba and become as a god." (Wants to give the Xibalbans free reign in return for apotheosis; Asset Skills: Leadership, Persuasion)
                                                            Relationship Path: "Fail me, and perish." (Servant to Xipe Totec and Tezcatlipoca; Asset Skills: Academics, Athletics)

                                                            Mecatl's older brother claimed he would seek out the lost line of jaguar priests, and take one as a bride, relying on her advice and assistance in order to build a new kingdom in Mexica. Disgusted by the idea of his brother bedding with a wild beast, Mecatl sought the attention of spirits, and found one willing to listen. The spirit seemed just as disgruntled as he, and was willing to assist. It acted as an intermediary between Mecatl and the gods, teaching the boy a ritual by which he might usurp his brother's birthright. On the day of their grandfather's death, Mecatl attacked his brother with a heavy stone, cracking open the skull. From there, he flayed his brother alive, taking the skin as a birthright, as well as their grandfather's relic: The Bracelet of Kings.

                                                            His dreams and the spirits spoke to him, leading him all the way to New York, even if he had no intention of sleeping with a wild animal, he'd need the assistance of one to accomplish what he'd set out to do.
