                    Here's another question: do the Gods take their form from the human culture they are most associated with, or vice versa? In other words, do the Aztec Gods look as they do because of human influence, or do the Aztecs take their dress, pyramids, music, etc., from their Gods ? Either way, with required worship or not, this implies a strong tie between culture and deities, another reason why I see the Scion world as very different from ours; there's too many reasons to feel that the faiths of other lands would be as strong and vibrant as Hinduism and other eastern faiths are in the "real" world. Perhaps religion in the Scion Europe is dominated by other faiths (Aesir, Bogovi, Tuath de Danaan), with that "upstart faith of the Romans (Christianity)" in the shadows? Why presume that things are essentially the same as in reality, with only little pockets of the thing that make Scion what it is?
