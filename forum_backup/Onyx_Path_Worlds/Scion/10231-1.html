        I actually had something similar in a game I ran a long time ago. It was a fairly normal research and development corporation with the exception of the mysterious CEO. To start with the corporation hired the band separately for a investigation that need their various "specialties" . After meeting the CEO Arty Fowl he sends them globe trotting as a band gathering various artifacts to help the war against the Titans. After a very long time they discover that Arty was actually a scion of Loki who died on a mission from his father in a cave in the deserts outside of Vegas. It then got even more complected when they realize it is still the real Arty except its only his ghost given a semblance of life by one of the Titans. Oh and his ghost is tied to his skull which is now a crazy weird relic that keeps him alive. Not really what you were looking for but I hope it might have given you some ideas.
