    Originally posted by Samudra View Post

    The sample Beauty boon was in the Shen Preview:

    Visage Great And Terrible : The line between beauty and monstrosity is drawn by your hand. Your very appearance instills your enemies with the absolute terror of confronting your divinity.

    It also contained possibly my favourite Spoiler from any Preview... that Gods who build their Legends appropriately can channel and learn Boons from one Purview through another... Kali is given as an example of a God who has that Boon but as a Death Boon, since Kali's fearsome appearance is a result of her association with Endings and Absolute Freedom. Which is generally the case for Ugly Gods... there are many Gods of Beauty, but while there are many Ugly Gods I don't think we have much in the way of Gods of Ugliness... Mythology tends to treat Beauty as an actual Purview of Divine influence and Ugliness as a side effect of being associated with some other unsavory Purview.
    I have been thinking about this a lot, and think that the choice to represent the Deities who would have previously had Negative Epic Appearance as accessing a single Boon in the Beauty Purview through another Purview is a bit odd.

    Firstly, this means you can have no Titans of this concept. You can have Titans of Beauty, but you can't have Titans of being Fucking Horrific Monsters. Balor could be represented as one of these Titans, though I would more often give him War, but I can see this non-existent Nega-Beauty Purview being used for him. This option is sealed off from the official game due to regulating the Disgusting, Frightening, and Horrific to accessing this concept through other Purviews.

    Secondly, it's a stumbling block to players who want to play the Horrific. You will need to bind one of your other Purviews to Visage of the Great and Terrible, and, while you have to go through this hoop, the other player who is just playing the Beautiful can do exactly what you do, except they don't need to jump through this hoop. The Beautiful PC could be Ugly more easily than the character who was designed around that notion. Furthermore, we obviously don't know how this will work, if there are other Boons for being Scary or if it is limited to VotGaT, but unless the Beauty Purview has a lot of Boons for being Not-Beautiful, the players who want to play Scary are more limited. You can be Beautiful, but you can't be Scary, unless the Purview contains a lot of Scary Boons,in which case I guess the question is why don't just make it a Dual-Purview like Health was in 1e.

    Thirdly, just as Ugliness is often a side effect of being associated with some unsavory Purview, being Beautiful is often due to being associated with a savory Purview. The Beautiful Sun, Amaterasu, Apollo, Dazhbog, that lot, are all Beautiful due to their association with the Sun. (Technically Apollo is probably Beautiful because of his links with male youth, but you know, Greeks) The Alluring Moon Deity, Sin, Chors, Selene (technically Titan) is similar. The Sexy Sex (And Farming) Deities, Freyr, Freya, Eiru, Ishtar, Eros, Aphrodite, Dionysus, Xochiquetzal, The Morrigan. All of them could be accessing the Beauty Purview through Sun, Moon, Fertility, and probably Passion (Love) as much as The Dagda is using Visage though his own Fertility, or Hel is using it through her Death.

    But obviously, that's insane. Of course Apollo has Beauty, of course Ishtar has it, and Freya, and Eros, and Aphrodite. These are all the unquestionably beautiful, but, their beauty is an expression of their other Divine features. Just as much as these figures deserve to have the Beauty Purview, so does The Dagda deserve to be Comically Gross, or Hel deserves to be Disgusting, or Mictlantecuhitli deserves to be Horrifying. It's tricky.

                                    Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post
                                    A lot of the gods specifically noted for their beauty (relative to godly norms) are also associated with youth & beauty as dominions, often in conflation with spring/summer or dawn.

                                    Uzume for the Kami; Aengus and Aine for the Tuatha; Baldr for the Aesir; and Xochipilli and Xochiquetzal in the Teotl all immediately spring to mind.
                                    I can't speak authoritatively on the others, but I can say that Aengus and Aine are not Deities of Beauty or Youth. The idea that Aengus The Macc Oc is a Youth Deity is a misunderstanding of his epithet, "The Young Son," which is a reference to how he is conceived and born in a single day. Aengus is very much presented as an adult, taking part in Cath Maige Tuired, as well as having his own foster-children. The idea he is a Love or Youth Deity is a Victorian imposition on him. Áine is just a regionalized Soverignty Goddess, their beauty, youth (and horrific appearance, and age) are reflections of local fertility, and part of bravery tests they impose on potential kings. The idea she is related to seasonal shift is also just a breakdown of her function as a Soverignty Goddess.

                                    There are individuals who I would flag as being particularly attractive, mostly individuals who are able to provoke Wasting Sickness in others, wracking other people with physical sicknesses from 'withdrawal' of their presence. However, this feels like it might be better represented by Passion.
