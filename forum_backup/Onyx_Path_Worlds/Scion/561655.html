                  Yeah, it works like this:

                  Everybody rolls for initiative. Director rolls for director characters/groups.

                  If the highest roll is a director character, that character goes first. If the highest roll is a player, that character goes first.

                  Then it alternates between player and director. So it would be Player 1 -> Director Character -> Player 2 -> Director Character and so on. If the players are all done before all the director characters are finished, the remaining director characters go. If all the director characters have gone, then all the players get a go.

                  Players determine order by just saying who is next. The first person is whoever rolled highest, but after that it's who that player says, then the next person, etcetera. There is no tick wheel, no ticks, and no tick cost on actions. Tick order is completely irrelevant. You just kinda do your thing, if possible. So except for determining the first player, "tick order" is meaningless on the PC side.

                  For director characters, they go in tick order/initiative order as rolled, from highest to lowest.

                  When everyone is gone, you have a new round. The last player in the previous round is the first player to act (LIFO), and the first director character in the previous round is the first director character to act (FIFO). If the last person to act last round was a director character/group/side, then the players act first this round. If the last person to act last round was a player/the player side, then the director character acts first.

                  That it. It's one roll, made at the start of the fight, and mostly matters for who goes very first, who starts with focus, and order of director character actions.

                                                      Originally posted by ArthurHansen View Post
                                                      Hmm. This system doesn't really lend itself to ambushes and seems to discard 'slow lumbering monsters' going last, because the other side immediately goes after the first your first person.

                                                      Even worse, it doesn't seem to allow for multi-sided combats easily. I also don't see any system for 'joining' into the combat late.
                                                      1) You wouldn't see who acts first in an ambush because the ambushers are the only people aware that an action scene is starting. If they conceal their intentions, then initiative is irrelevant.

                                                      2) "Slow, lumbering monsters" don't move as fast, so they're going to take time to get to you in any event.

                                                      3) In the rare event of multiple PC sides, the system is the same--the handoff just isn't cooperative. Yet everybody *does* get to go, and if you decide to make the other side wait to be last . . . they go first next time. You could split them into two groups and do separate handoffs if you like, but I think the former option is a more interesting strategic choice. WRT NPCs, the Director slots them in whether they're allied or not. As noted above, new combatants get picked or rolled in.
