    Ideas of Chosen Scions

    04-22-2019, 12:12 AM
    Games 2E gives us broadening of Types of Scions. Born are most popular and easiest to understand. Created are more or less mythical in the straightest sense. And Incarnate are most enigmatic.

    Chosen Scions, on the other hand, looks as rather plain – but do we really got idea who can they be, really? This topic is to give us all concepts who Chosen can be.

    I will edit opening list with coming ideas. You can also point your own Examples, I will add them to list.

    Chosen Scions ideas:
    • Artist – You are creator that God is inspiring in it’s dreams. Examples: Rock star under guidance of Apollo.
    • Disciple – You are studying one of Incarnation’s Callings under the God. Examples: Con-man learning how to cheat from the Loki itself.
    • Lover – You are, conscious or not, romantic lover of one of Incarnation of the God, giving you powers. Examples: Paramour of Aphrodite.
    • Priest – You dedicated your life to worship and myth of you God. You lead the rituals and masses to it’s name. Examples: Shinto priest of Amaterasu.
    • Warrior – You fight for your God, conscious of it or not. You can be real world berserker in older times – or bodyguard looking over VIP in nowadays. Examples: Mercenary under sacred contract with Odin.

      You're thinking far too small. The potential backgrounds for a Chosen Scion are limited only by one's own imagination. Hell, from collected myths around the world, CHOSEN would actually be the most mythically common Genesis.

      Imagine, if you will...
      • A child in Ireland, given into the care of Ogma under the old Fostering system, taught in the laws of the Tuatha and in their forms of combat. Now the Chosen wanders on his own, serving as Judge and Jury in the World.
      • A revolutionary fighting against the corrupt government system in parts of India, and during a demonstration she sees the skull necklace and arm-girdle of Kali encouraging her to violently overthrow this bullshit.
      • A child, not born of any God, but raised to be a fighter from the time they could hold a sword. Trained in what they learned was a Terra Incognita, she learned to master every melee weapon possible, and was taught how to fight countless monsters of myth. The whole time she was told "One day, you'll be a Hero who saves people". Until that day arrived, when Athena blessed them.
      • We have a troubled youth here, one who grew up on the streets of the city, running with gangs and committing petty crimes. However, as he grew older, he realized he was on the path to self destruction, and did his level best to turn his life around, AND to help improve his community. At the opening of the Youth Center he managed to help build, he's greeted by a jolly man at the snack bar loading up on sweets, who reveals himself to be Ganesha, who had set this child up with all kinds of difficulties in his life but by gum this kid overcame them.
      To say nothing of what kind of options you have if you decide to play a Denizen.
      • A Jotnar who's decided 'Nuts to this Ragnarok bullshit' and is trying to improve his own temperamental nature through the teachings of the Buddha.
      • A kitsune who, being a shithead like most kitsune are, gets dragged in front of Inari and told "Go out and do good deeds or else you're gonna get trapped inside a rock for a century and a day"
      • A Satyr who, during a wild party, realizes that the guy on the dance floor encouraging him to keep doing shots was Dionysus, who's gonna teach this goatman how to REALLY party.

      Disclaimer: In favor of fun and enjoyment, but may speak up to warn you that you're gonna step on a metaphorical land mine
