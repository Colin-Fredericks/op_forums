                    Originally posted by Darksol-aeternium View Post
                    The sidebars you are looking at in God on pg 206 and 209 do indeed mention titanspawn scions or Dark scions. It presents them as an optional ploy by the titans to create servitors that are as powerful and mobile as those of the gods. The only details you get are that they; A) don't get birthrights (instead using titanspawn trophies as relics, with nothing about guides or followers mentioned), B) get slapped with some unusual twist (which I interpreted as physical mutation or quirk) whenever they learn boons, and C) can upgrade their legend whenever they want by replacing a previous virtue with a dark virtue. It can also be extrapolated that they D) have no PSP.

                    I actually played one in a home game, the character ended up working for his titan, but since that titan was one of the handful of indifferent/neutral ones he was allowed to cooperate with godly scions against that titan's rivals/enemies/annoyances.

                    Another detail they never get into is what a scion of an allied titan would be like. Examples of allied titans: Helios, Thanatos, Hypnos (who apparently crashes in Hades's underworld), Rhea (Zeus's mom), Chronos, Oceanus (whose daughter married Poseidon). These are titans who never got slapped into tarterus because they aren't titan-avatars, but they ARE titans.
                    The "sidebar" only mentions an accidental visitation by a Titan, it makes me wonder if an abandoned titanspawn adopted by a god would pattern after the god and progress normally, be forced to progress as titanspawn only, or if it would always be some kind of hybrid.


                    In another game system I had a centaur Nightbane that I never really got to play (the GM only did the lead in scenario and abandoned the campaign for another one) and I am curious if a sion with some centaur blood and maybe a dark side that had to be resisted would work in Sion without distorting the background of the game to accommodate it. I was thinking of the character starting out as the usual human scion in form and taking on more centaur traits as she gains power and probably having a device (relic or boon or whatever is appropriate) to (at least eventually if it is not possible at Hero level) toggle between four and six limbs (or seven if the tail would be considered a limb...) and the "base form" eventually switching from human to centaur. The suspicion the character might be saddled with having been originally titanspawn-scion would fit in with the slightly pariah air that a Nightbane normally has.

                                It's not a hard-and-fast distinction, and it probably shouldn't be. The gods are themselves a particular type of Titanspawn if you take the word literally, which means Scions are also Titanspawn in a sense. It's just the the word is normally used to designate beings that were born of the Titans that aren't on the side of the gods. They're monstrous or inimical to the order of the cosmos or otherwise hostile to gods and their Scions, so they get stuck with the Othering term. They're the black sheep of the family, so to speak (but family nonetheless).
