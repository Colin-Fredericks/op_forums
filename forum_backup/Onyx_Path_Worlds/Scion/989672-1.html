                                    I'd like to double-plus on this.

                                    Think about it for a minute - Egypt has been the byword for magic throughout civilization, rivaled only dimly by Babylon and Persia. Daily magical ritual was a constant practice of the Egyptian household. They have not one, not two, but three or more gods explicitly devoted to magic (Thoth, Seshat, Isis - and that's not even counting the personified god of magic itself, Heka, or their companions Hu (magical utterance) and Sia (magical knowledge)).

                                    Deception having any part of the Egyptian gods is doubly absurd. Maybe if someone's daddy is Set, sure, but barring that, I really don't think there's an excuse here.
