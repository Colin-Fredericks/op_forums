                            At a minimum? An alternative to playing literal human/god half-breeds, so that all of the pantheons that don’t feature promiscuous gods can still join in the fun without having to shoehorn themselves into that model. Also useful would be to ditch the “Titans are EVIL!” schtick; you could get a lot of mileage out of having the Titans pick from the same set of Virtues that the Gods do.

                            Another option would be to blur the distinction between Gods and Titans: e.g., allow (but do not require) a Pantheon to have a “Greater God” that is analogous to a Greater Titan, which manifests godlike Avatars within the Pantheon’s Godrealm. In effect, that Pantheon’s “Godrealm” would be a Greater Titan. This would allow for more henotheistic models for Pantheons, with the option of a Titan-like “Greater God” presiding over the rest of the Pantheon.

                            Likewise, play up Lesser Immortals as potential denizens of Godrealms, possibly with the option to achieve full-on God status; this would allow for various “Little Gods” options such as kami. The main line I wouldn’t cross would be the notion that Gods are somehow created by human beliefs: I’d save that for a Legendary Beings “Shard”.

                            My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                                    As this topic actually came up in a quasi-ST brainstorming session between Watcher, Iry, and myself, we had a sitdown for thinking how you could put monotheistic Christianity into Scion RAW.

                                    It could work as Yahweh being a god who splintered from the originial Caanite pantheon; (or his own entirely) but instead of taking the Israelite people into their own nation, he created them from Adam and Eve (since all Creation Myths are true) maybe created them in his own Terra Incognitae/personal sanctum. Bible actually happens. Adam and Eve break His own laws and are kicked out into the real world, have their own children, yadda yadda.

                                    Since Yahweh formed his own new pantheon, he makes a new PSP and this PSP allows him to adopt (or even a new power/relic, or a condition worked into the mix when he created Adam and Eve) but either way, he can take anyone born of Adam's lineage into a Scion which would explain Saints; and Jesus himself is an actual born Scion through Divine intervention and sacrificing himself did something (maybe banishing away the Titanic stain that happened when Adam and Eve got cast out of Yahweh's paradise)

                                    Because Angels are not humans, and they're not Gods; they're Legendary Creatures (Legendary Beings) with their own ranking hierarchies and whatnot. They themselves cannot sire Scions, but they have the power to Visit scions on Yahweh's behalf (because that's also RAW scion).

                                    At least, this is a way that works for me to make both sides have their own respectful histories, mythologies, and understandings side-by-side. It is soooo not perfect, but I dunno; it's better than flat out "nos" like I have been doing.
