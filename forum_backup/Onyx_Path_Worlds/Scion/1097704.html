                                  Indeed, I'm really excited for the possibility of playing a Scion level character who's not strictly human.


                                  But the way I see it, the difference between Titans and Gods is their intentions.

                                  Titans have no great plan; they flex their power simply because they have the freedom to do so. They just want to do whatever makes them happy at the time.

                                  Gods, on the other hand, use their powers for some sort of purpose, interacting with society and guiding it towards whatever goal they have planned, be it for good or ill.


                                  In other words, you have the Chaos vs Order dichotomy in regards to which is which.
