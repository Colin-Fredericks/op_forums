                            Or, much like the woman that run the local Chinese take out, it could be both, she looks and sounds like the stereo type but like all PEOPLE is much much more. Our take out lady is the stereotypical angry old Chinese lady, except that when you get to know her she's just caustic because it amuses her and she's usually annoyed at her son (the cook) who she thinks is too lazy (he's not but you know moms) and very much enjoys her customers despite fighting through the language barrier (English is a tough nut to crack when you're raised speaking mandarin... or any other Asian language for that matter).

                            My point is she can be a morally gray, highly intelligent assassin, spy and strategist and still be a gossipy old Vietnamese woman (seriously, many Vietnamese women fought in a pretty big international scuffle not so long ago and gossip is a GREAT way to get information) who takes great pride in her skill with an emery board (and a perverse joy out of insulting her daughter who's too busy going to college to be smart and find a man and settle down).

                            ​sorry for being a bit ranty, but I enjoy playing older characters BECAUSE of that weird "sweet old backward thinking".
