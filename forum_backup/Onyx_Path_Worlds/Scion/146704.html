Where I am sure the person would have dismissed their Epic Appearance in an instant? There is the rape of Demeter, Selene and Endymion, Eos and the multitude of people she molests, Zeus and Ganymede, Medusa and Poseidon, Hermes and Aglaurus, and quite a few more.                                           But, in the scene with Zeus and Semele, it is very specifically mentioned that Zeus is hiding his true divine form from her, as to prevent the obliteration (as is mechanically represented through Gotham By Night's Divine Splendor) that would result if she saw his true form. This isn't that Zeus is toning down his true appearance, but he is concealing his true form from her.

                                          Additionally, the issues of being overwhelmed by your Epic Perception are already addressed through Parallel Attention, which helps a Scion manage the utterly overwhelming barrage of sensory input that they receive.

                                          While the issue of having a world made of tissue paper is not addressed for Epic Strength RAW, I have seen Storytellers handle it two ways. One of which is requiring a Knack to prevent harming others, or risk accidentally, for example, maiming a Significant Other in bed. While, I have seen others rule that since we are capable of controlling our strength, Gods can control the extent to which they wield their Epic Strength. Which is quite unlike Epic Appearance, for while someone can control the amount of force they put into objects, they can't control how beautiful they are.

                                          And yes, those are pretty much perfect examples of how having incredibly high Epic Manipulation and Charisma works at high Legends when facing beings with low resistances, such as mortals. If Baldur walked down the streets of Reykjavik as a Legend 12 God, all the mortals who saw him would love him for eternity, the love they could share with each other being as pale as a piece of gravel compared to the majesty of the Sun itself.

                                          But! Ultimately, this is up to the individual Storyteller. While I am not one myself, Iry, Aynie, Telgar, John, and Anne all use some different pieces of this as an ever present reality for the Scions, and the Gods. Though, every single storyteller can obviously run the game in whatever way they wish, not a single one of us uses RAW Scion in its entirety, everyone is entirely welcome to interpret the rules and the universe as they wish, and no one is any more correct than anyone else for it.

                                              Your example in no way implies a use of Avatar. It is either using My Eyes Are Up Here or Zeus controlling his Appearance. Having people reduce their Legend is a ham-fisted, overbearing and, frankly, stupid-for-the-god-in-question way of doing it. Why? Want to kill or control Zeus, wait until he goes to see his lady-friend. He just doesn't measure up. He's toast.

                                              Gods who make people melt are usually examples of gods being careless or possessing hubris. A divine "oops" situation.

                                              In actual Avatar-as-they-are-handled-in-myth, which all of the people mentioned use as their ultimate reason for handling things one way or the other, a god separates a part of him or herself off as a semi-independant entity that is still them. Vishnu doesn't change into Krishna. Krishna is running around doing his fun stuff and there is still a Vishnu. That's Avatar. (a word that gets heavily abused and over-used in Scion anyways. It is bad design to have the same word mean multiple different things.) Anne does handle this concept pretty well with her Mayan PSP.

                                              And, having said that, I can also advocate getting rid of it as a difficult, clunky and unwieldy mechanism that doesn't reflect the concept it's trying to embody in pretty much any way, shape or form. I don't agree with the way some of those people (John, Anne, Telgar) use Avatar to explain away things in myth that don't fit their conceptions. Specific example. the Trojan War. Telgar and I have had intense discussion about whether Diomedes could actually really wound Ares and Aphrodite (who, by the way interacted with mortals all the time, with them knowing she was Aphrodite without them just melting at how insanely beautiful she was, or becoming her devoted slaves.) (Note: These occurred BEFORE Zeus banned the gods from interfering.) Anne, John and Telgar say those have to be Avatars to be defeated by mere mortals. I say Diomedes was a kick-ass mortal (with a fair bit of divine ancestry more than 3 generations back) who did catch a couple of petty, willfull, condescending and ignorant gods off guard and do the unthinkable. Anne/John/Telgar all maintain that had to be a use of Avatar because otherwise no mortal can ever do anything to a god ever. I don't agree with them on the relative potential coolness of mortals and/or the concept of divine beings being nigh omnipotent but just fucking up from time to time in this particular way as they do in so many others.

                                              We don't even have to agree to disagree. Pundits on the subject certainly don't. I think I'm right and they're wrong. They, the opposite. We each get to advocate our positions and we never ever have to come to a compromise. Doesn't stop me from discussing and hashing out things Hittite with Anne. Doesn't stop me from being in Telgar's game, while enjoying it thoroughly and and completely disagreeing with him on the use or existence of the Avatar background.

                                              By my prefence, I'd handle the concept of a couple of ways. Not making the gods have powers over which they have no control. That seems pretty nice. I like the fact that I, as a player, and the people in my game are going to have to invest in something separate to take care of something that makes them unwieldy and more difficult to play. If not that, then have the gods running amok tearing holes in the world and the fabric of civilization by accident unless they invest in something much less dear than a build point at apotheosis and make it a set of Knacks like My Eyes Are Up Here. Make the concept of Avatar something that is and make it pantheon appropriate, meaning part of the PSP for pantheons that are into that sort of thing (Devas and Mayan gods LEAP to mind). Otherwise make it part of a Purview about interacting with mortals that everyone can buy and letting players decide if they want to purchase relevant Boons from a robust "Boon Cloud". (I prefer the former).

                                              Hopefully Scion 2.0 will either eliminate Avatar as is or integrate into the character substrate at apotheosis in a more elegant and appropriate way.

                                                          So, Avatar, no. Current Scion game is about to hit Demigod, so that's WAAAAAY off and it's not something I really care for in any case.

                                                          Enech? Well.... that's a different story. I don't like it on it's face per Irish mythology. I love Irish mythology, so I am somewhat motivated to replace it with something else. I am also playing a Tuatha De Scion right now, so I am having to confront it squarely as we were NOT using it as written. So I worked with Telgar to come up with some alternative. Which is currently being playtested and going... okay. Compared to the rest of the group, the replacement is not super-snazzy or as useful as revised Jontunblut or Arete or Cheval. Enech had more visceral punch but it's more like a badly made sledgehammer than a well-thrown haymaker.
