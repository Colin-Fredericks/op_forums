                            The advantage is flexibility. Zeus can pick up Animal Form by itself without needing to buy any other Animal Boons. Tlaloc can buy Rain of Fire without buying any other Fire Boons. It's not just about skipping around inside your Purviews, it's about being able to dip into other Purviews to represent situations in mythology (and they're everywhere) where a God steps outside their Associated Purviews in a single, very specific way. Not enough to suggest they have a whole Purview, or even half a Purview, but they do one, single specific thing. Sometimes over and over.

                            It doesn't put any pressure on players to do things "the right way" and buy their Boons "in order" even if they don't want to. Maybe with all the possible choices, there just isn't anything in Fire that they want before Demigod, but then at Demigod they're like "woo!! Firebaaaalls!!!" and start buying some stuff. Maybe they're angling for a God of Ice and want to get some Boons to control diamonds because they see a cool crossover there, but they don't want to be an Earth God as well as a Frost God.

                            Clouds let you do things your way, instead of following a narrow path. No matter how many options you can get in a linear system, you have rails to follow. There's nothing you can do in a linear system that you can't do in a cloud system, but tons of things you can do in clouds that you can't do on a line. I see only benefits to Clouds, no downsides.

                            I'm a professor! Why is no one listening to me?!

                                                        Originally posted by Aynie View Post
                                                        A Hero should not suddenly know how to call down lightning and turn into the clouds themselves;
                                                        I absolutely agree with this part. There needs to be a clear division between what each Legend point is capable of, but that's already built into the system. A Cloud Power System does not negate the Legend progression. Even in a system like Werewolf, just-Changed cubs can't buy Rank 5 Gifts. They have to prove themselves ready by advancing in Rank. In Scion, the same is true. You can't buy level 10 Boons without having Legend 11 to prove you're ready to handle that much power. So no matter how freeform a system gets, you won't have Heroes using God-powers. Even a system like Mage Spheres has that covered, since you'd need Godly rating in your Spheres to do God effects.

                                                        Originally posted by Aynie View Post
                                                        they should start out with maybe learning not to get crushed if they step off a high ledge.
                                                        I absolutely disagree with this part. For such a linear progression from 1 to 2 to 3, etc, to make sense to me, the requires a consistency and really logical transition from one Boon to the next. Scion already doesn't have that. There's no connection between Life-Giving Rays (Sun 3) and Sun Chariot (Sun 7). If you have to know how to avoid falling damage in order to electrify your weapon, I want a real, visible connection between the first and the second. I don't see that. I can't see why someone needs to know how to fall better to be good at zapping stuff. If someone wants to zap stuff, all they need to know is how to zap stuff. Falling isn't involved.

                                                        I can see where you're coming from, though. The basic concept that comes from a lot of folks that like linear Purviews is that each Boon in and of itself isn't really important. It's not that you need to know how to fall better to learn how to zap stuff. The idea is that you're investing in a Purview and gaining knowledge of how to master that specific type of magic, no matter what you do with it. I understand that, I just don't think it has enough benefits to outweigh the coolness and freedom of a cloud-based system. The only thing a linear system provides (and Scion1 doesn't even provide this) is that it's easier to go in order because you're incentivized to do things the "right" way by lower XP costs or more dice or something. If you go in order, you get a cookie. I think the cookie isn't necessary, and everyone should be free to choose to buy only the powers they like. No one should ever have to "settle" for a power they aren't really enthusiastic about just to fill out some arbitrary set. If you're not super-duper-excited about any level 2 Sky Boons? Don't buy one. Ever. And you don't get any penalty at all.

                                                        I'm a professor! Why is no one listening to me?!
