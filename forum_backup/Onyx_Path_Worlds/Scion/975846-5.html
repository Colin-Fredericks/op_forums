        Oh, that makes sense.

        I more meant that is how someone who is monotheistic and a scion can believe without seeming insane. I agreed with others that they would seem insane and I think where we disagree is the part about Monotheism. If One God is only as powerful as one God from another pantheon who have an alliance than Monotheism just doesn't seem likely to spread. God faces off Jupiter while Mars/Neptune/Juno/etc get him from behind.

        On top of all that, any pantheon would have killed them just based on how much damage evangelicalism did to so many faiths. If Polytheism is True and Monotheism exists as a power in the world, than monotheism has to have weight. My idea would be Monotheism is more powerful (I mean look how much it has ravaged many Polytheistic faiths and seems dedicated to doing so, it doesn't do so as much here but still there's a reason it's number 1) but God rarely blesses anyone and mostly, does not answer prayers.

        Also, Monotheism has a place in so many mythologies, it seems a shame to call them all wrong. Say this God is a force that gets reinterpreted every way. Mortals made a faith that made them feel other gods weren't necessary trying to worship directly to this entity, but most Gods who do Worship this Deity feel they got it all wrong. In some ways I'm saying that Mythologies who have a monotheistic bent are right while the Abrahamic faiths are not. Still, by worshiping them Directly there is some protection that they get even the gods don't understand.

        In essence; the only way I see Monotheism still around without being declared the supreme religion with a supreme deity that actively stomps on the other gods, is if something special holds it back.

        Here's an idea, Monotheism are sorcerors stealing power from an almost Pantheistic deity, they first tied it to El, but the reason the religion spreads so far is God is everywhere and part of so many religions. All it takes is sincere belief in a one true God as a motif for these guys to steal the power. Without that extra buffer (the gods) Man kind of ran amuck without much to stop them. That doesn't mean these sorcerors are cruel or evil or do any of this on purpose, although some do. They appealed to a Deity, one even some gods had trouble fully understanding, and with that power spread their beliefs. Thus the very strong emphasis on faith. God still is very much real as a Christian/Muslim/Judaic figure since, y'know what it is as an almost Pantheistic god, but that isn't the only way to worship them, or even the one it is most active as. You believe in a supreme Deity that is this God, but whether Hindu, Persian, or Abrahamic they are a lot more complicated than meets the eye.

            Originally posted by vonpenguin View Post
            How regular is regular though? 10 or so a generation? Which becomes a much smaller ratio as population grows, and similarly, less mythical events "happen". And even then most scions never reach demi-god level, they instead become our De Vincis, our Hannibals, or our Beethovens. They are on the winning side of battles like Agincourt or New Orleans 1812. They are impressive and might alter events, but only a few will be known by more than a few hundred people. The core element here is that while there aren’t any hard rules about not showing yourself, most tend to avoid the nasty fate entanglements, most periods of history don’t have many, and most of those probably fade into folktales and urban legends. I think it is fairly easy to recontextualise events as “there was one at the one historical event” well “maybe opposing ones canceled each other out”, “Pantheon X didn’t want to provoke a war with Pantheon y”, “No one believed the witnesses”, amd other perfectly believable excuses. If we assume that the cold war Neil has talked about has only been ramping up over the past couple decades than history probably wouldn’t be massively changed.
            See, I can't see even ten in a generation going unnoticed. They're going to be at the forefront of every major event just by dint of Fate making them go interesting places and that can't go unnoticed. Remember: Hannibal nearly brought Rome to its knees; Caesar destroyed an entire Pantheon; Christ and Mohammad both founded religious traditions that, at present, contain about half the entire populations of the planet.

            Imagine ten people like that every generation, with Fate bending to place them in just as grand a spotlight. How would our world survive it?

            This is without even entering into the sociocultural effects. I can't imagine there being a Reformation that split the Catholic and Protestant churches in a world that has far, far more polytheistic cults that are just as popular as the monotheistic ones. Monotheism won out over polytheism in our modern world but there's no reason for it to in Scion and quite possibly a large number of reason for it not to, as there are way more gods in a polytheistic pantheon than a monotheistic one. That's a lot more hands out there sprinkling the ichor.

            We are talking a world that might look a bit like our own, but only because a lot of the national boundaries we draw form from language and geographical barriers.

            The easiest answer to this is the most boring; to assume that there aren't any Scions we're unaware of in the background, bending the flow of history to their will, but rather that any given famous person of any import is in some way gifted with divine blood. Personally, though, while I would be truly enamoured with the French Cult of Reason managing to conjure a goddess who blessed Thomas "A violent uprising every couple of decades is probably good for the country" Jefferson, I don't like that idea for the same reason I don't like Big Man History; it's too simplistic and deprives the setting of feeling grand and mythical.

                        The thing is, "Fight off thirty people singlehandedly" isn't something a Scion just does once in their entire life. It's something they do over and over and over again. It's something Fate will make them do over and over again because Fate wants them to be as balls-out interesting as possible, and it's something their divine patrons are going to keep poking them to do. "Live in interesting times" is a curse written into their very ichor.

                        A Scion isn't going to be the sort of person who fights off thirty insurgent fighters in the desert and then goes back to an otherwise modestly impressive but mundane military career. They're the sort of person who keeps doing even more ludicrous stuff after that, over, and over, and over, because they are protagonists in the story. They're always at its centre. Scions don't get to do one big thing then retire; they've got to keep doing bigger and bigger things because that's how legends work.

                        For a Scion, the Audie Murphy style "Climb inside a burning gun wreck and fire it into a wall of tanks shooting at you with blood dripping across your eyes until you've driven them back, then walk away from the wreck before it explodes" moment is just the beginning. After that, the Scion pretty much has to singlehandedly bash Hitler's head in with the giant concrete swastika torn from above the Reichstag.

                        That's the take I've always seen in it, at least. And given that the example art shows a fire giant burning a city before a Scion comes riding a tidal wave to deliver a smackdown, I don't see how you couldn't have them bedecking the history books.

                        "That time D.C. was about to be obliterated by a giant until Teddy Roosevelt rode a giant moose straight into its gut" is one of those things history remembers, y'know?

                                        I would err on the side of Axelgear here, Heroic Scions, at least in 1e, are incredibly powerful. Through our play group's experiences in 1e, at Hero, Scions have accomplished the following off the top of my head.
                                        • Leveled downtown Paris.
                                        • Beat down a Legend 4 Heimdall at Legend 2. (Extraordinary dice rolls)
                                        • Built and detonated Epic Int enhanced Nukes along a fault line, sinking the entirety of the British Isles, devastating the world economy and thrusting the world into chaos.
                                        • Saved downtown New York by beating a Gorgon out on Epic Appearance, preventing anyone from looking at her.
                                        • Talked down an enraged Ba'al and Yam before they threw down inside an urban center. Were manifestation as themselves, a colossal sea serpent for Yam's form.
                                        • Surviving a massive explosion point blank without dodging that managed to almost biscect The Nuckelavee.
                                        On a more conceptual level, I'll break down this a bit more into Physical, Social, and Mental Scions.

                                        Physical Scions in 1e could survive almost anything the mortal world threw at them. Armed with the Remington template, the best mortal (5 in Dexterity and Firearms. Remington +3) rolls 13 dice to hit. On average this will be 6.5 Successes, so, let's say 7 to be nice to them. The best Physical Scion at Legend 2 will defend against this with their Dodge Defense Value which is 8. (5 Dexterity, 5 Athletics, Epic Dexterity 1, and a basic Shield) So, on average the Mortal fails to hit by 1 and does nothing to the Scion. For this, let's say they got lucky and instead got 9 Successes, two above average, to hit with a Threshold of 1.

                                        Now the Mortal rolls Damage. They have 14 Dice (1 Threshold, 1 Base, 7 Remitting template damage, 5 Perception [This is technically a houserule, the only thing that adds to Damage is strength. But, this makes guns useless as they don't get an attribute. So, we'll give them Perception for damage.]) which will on average be 7 Successes. The Scion has the hide of a Nemean Lion since they are Theoi and rocking their Pantheon Pride. Therefore, the Scion has 12 Soak. (5 Stamina / 2 + Armor [8 Lethal], + Epic Stamina 1), and takes 0 damage.

                                        If this Scion were putting effort in, they can also chose to downgrade any Lethal Damage to Bashing for 1 Legend a piece, in which case their Bashing Soak is 13. Furthermore, once a Scene they can use Sophalistic Well Being and ignore any attack they were not directly aware of. Shoot them in the back of the head from point blank, shoot them with a battleship mounted railgun, they won't take a single point of damage. Additionally, they can use the Knacks Untouchable Opponent (with the nerf in place to prevent it from breaking everything) and Body Armor to bump their DDV and Soak up by 1 each.

                                        The Scion's values also improve as they get higher Legend to the following with these bonuses. Legend 3: DDV = 11 Soak = 13. Legend 4: DDV = 14, Soak = 16. Additionally, their ability to use their Knacks increases as their Legend pool becomes exponentially larger.

                                        Social is harder to look at with numbers, so, let's look at Knacks. Epic Charisma lets you make one person for 1 legend point totally invested in helping you, totally absolve yourself from blame (and also push this blame onto anyone else). Epic Manipulation make one person believe anything you say at all unless there is pure undeniable proof otherwise, as well as force one person to take one action immediately. Epic Appearance allows you to be so frightening you increase your DDV substantially.

                                        For a little focused study, we will look at the Manipulation Knack Trendsetter. You will be using this to cause a massive panic crash in the New York Stock Exchange, while you yourself profit infinitely through investments. This is a Manipulation + Presence roll, and let's say Legend 4 for this example, still Theoi. They have a pool of 14 dice and +4 Automatic Successes. On Average, you will get 11 Successes, which is 6 Successes higher than required to cause the entire population of New York to follow your trend. (which is making some horrific financial decision) The Social Scion is now vastly wealthy, and has brutalized the world economy for 2 Legend.

                                        Mental is even weirder to play with. But, first, let's look at the best possible mortal trying to invent something. They have 15 Dice (5 Int +5 Skill +5 Tool) to throw down, average of 8 rounded up. A Legend 4 Scion, still Theoi, gets to throw down 19 Dice and +4 Automatic Successes is 14 Successes. Add to this the Epic Intelligence Knack Math Genius, and Cypher, this Scion is also able to, making no rolls, solve every one of the Millennium Prize Problems in a matter of seconds, revolutionizing, like, everything. Also becoming a multi millionaire, but that's small bucks compared to our Epic Manipulation Scion above. Then with Cypher, you can rip through any form of computer security with no rolls in the blink of an eye, and make your own computer security that no mortal can ever solve ever in a million years ever.

                                        Then the Epic Perception Scions can also see really really far, but, that's not really dramatic.

                                        But, right. Hero Scions, at least in 1e, were world shakingly powerful. If you tried, you could bend the world around you. If 2e follows in a similar power scale, then a lot of Scions running around is probably not what's been going on. The kiddos are probably pretty rare. Especially because of the following: Scions are big risks. The Divine are investing a lot of time and effort into you, having Birthrights made, bound to you, and if you are a born Scion such all of 1e, having a child which they may have social obligations to. And then this Scion has a will of its own, it could try to end the world, or kill you, or rage against the heavens siding with Titans, or just be a problem. Scions are good things to keep low numbers of, they are such a hassle.
