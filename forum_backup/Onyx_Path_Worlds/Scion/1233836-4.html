                    Just logging in to present what my sleep-deprived brain has locked onto.

                    On the Ready Made Characters, there are some problems with their Specialties, but the rules for Specialties don't agree. On page 59, the sidebar at the top of the page says "...once a character reaches four or more dots in a Skill they may choose a Specialty focus..." On page 97 the text reads, "For every Skill that ends up with three or more dots, choose a Specialty.

                    This throws some confusion on the Ready Made Characters that could use some clarification: Eric Donner has Pilot 3 (Driving Cars). Emanuel Montero has Academics 2 (Aztech History), Empathy 3 (Sympathy) and an errant, 'M' next to Technology. Hassan al-Hakim has Empathy rated at zero dots with Bedside Manner as a Specialty, and the rules don't tell us how that would work (presumably boosting success from just the Attribute). Rashmi Battacharya has Culture 3 (the Hinduisms - which should be Hindi Tenets or Hindi Dharmas according to several associates), as well as Firearms 3 (Rifles). All three of Rhiannon Jernigan's Specialties are attached to Skills rated 3.

                    On pg 123, formatting gaff on the Brutal (1) entry: entry is not bold, nor does it conform to the alternating greyscale bands and subsequently throws off the rest of the table.

                    On pg 151, the "Sorcery Without Heroism" sidebar, parenthetical reference: "(whether directly from a God, stealing it from a relic or artifact, or draining it from a mystical sight)" I feel this is intended to be 'site,' unless the sorcerer is stealing essence from a prophetic vision or other divination; if this is the case, it needs to be clearer.

                    On pg 163, Virtuous Condition: " Momentum: Every time your Virtue rage causes trouble, add another point of Momentum into thepool." I think 'rage' is meant to be 'range,' as in the two polarized Virtues embraced by your culture/pantheon.
