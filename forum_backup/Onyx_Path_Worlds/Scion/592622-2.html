                                        Originally posted by simison View Post
                                        Are there any other house rules (John's or otherwise) that people think are necessary? I'm thinking of adding their Relic and Knack house rules, but nothing else comes to mind.
                                        Oh man, okay, yeah. So, first off, grab the Knacks and Boons. RAW has really unbalanced stuff, and some of it is exceptionally game breaking (Untouchable Opponent becomes almost statistically impossible to counter IIRC) RAW Jotunblut is very... it doesn't work brilliantly the moment you get beyond Hero, and JSR has a pretty decent one for the Physical characters. It's unfortunately not as good for the Social or Mental ones however.

                                        I'd also say you'll want to use the houserules for Birthrights. Creatures, and Followers both don't really... work if you don't do that. A 5 Dot Creature will be pretty nice at Hero, and then at Demigod die like a leaf in a storm from Typhon's wings. Furthermore, the 1 - Star Dot Relics make the world more... sensible. Like, Thor's Hammer, The Spear of Victory, and Smoking Bolt couldn't possibly be 5 Dot Relics. Those are within the reach of a Legend 2 Scion, and are thereby balanced for the Heroes. But, this means at Demigod and God they become increasingly weak since they can't scale up any more in RAW which doesn't make sense. Thor without his hammer in myth is dangerously weakened, RAW Thor without his hammer is mildly inconvenienced and gains very minimal benefit from wielding it.

                                        It also lets you actually have mythic-accurate Relics in the game. For example, The Dagda's Club can kill nine people per swing it is so huge (it's also so big it needs wheels on it) which is a really substantial power depending on how you stat it. If it allows him to essentially Cleve attack 9 people at a time, that's utterly insanely powerful, but accurate to the myth. (One of) Lugh's Spear(s) gives the wielder Victory, whoever wields it becoming incapable of losing a fight, which is equally insanely powerful. Manannan's Sword is always lethal even if it only inflicts the smallest scratch (it also can cut through anything, forces people to tell the truth, and commands the winds) which is frighteningly almost broken. (The Tuatha have a crazy list of incredibly powerful Relics, I could go on for ages) Odin's Spear is a good Norse example of a Star Dot Relic, as it's like Lugh's Spear I just mentioned except is for external use. None of these Relics can actually work with the 1 - 5 scale in RAW, so I'd advise swapping to the 1 - Star system.

                                        Let's see, what else. Some people allow characters to swap one of their Virtues out for another, but since you're past Chargen that may be a bit difficult. Changing how you use Boons is also pretty popular. Moving from an Epic + Ability to Purview Rank + Ability using the number of Dots in the Purview to give Autos on the Epic Attribute Scale. (This prevents things like all Fire Gods needing to be Manipulative unless they're content with being terrible with their Purview, ect)

                                        If you want the houserules I use, they can be found here. They are not complete, with the three of us working on the rules locked in a battle dancing between nasty sickness, and University, but the core systems are present. I've not been knocked down for the count this summer yet though! It's normally one of our specters slowing things. You'd need to supplement some Purviews and the like from other sources like JSR or the like.
