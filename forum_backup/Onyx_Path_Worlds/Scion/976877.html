    09-26-2016, 10:08 PM
    As it says on the tin.

    Its the new stretch goal (well okay there a stretch goal at $55,000, but they've nearly reached it already, its for LARPING.

    So fans of Atlantis rejoice its the example Pantheon for Fictional make your own Pantheon rules, so its kind of a two for one deal.

    Personally exploring the possiblities could be fun. I look forward to it, and I think reaching the goal is a safe bet. It could be used to do Monotheistic "pantheons" as well, as someone else here pointed out.

    PS if I ever do a Mage game, I'll likely borrow the Atlantis Pantheon.

    What Fictional Pantheons would you do for example?
