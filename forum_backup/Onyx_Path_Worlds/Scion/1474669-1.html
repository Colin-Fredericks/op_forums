    Eh. I still say that a person needs to somehow be connected to a particular mythology before their soul ends up in its Underworld. Plus, the idea that it only ends up in one leads to tension and drama, which is good for storytelling; the idea that your soul ends up in all of them is kind of like the notion of Quantum Immortality: if every possible fate is going to be realized, then what's the point of striving for one fate or trying to avoid another?
