    Scion 2nd Mass Combat System

    04-12-2019, 04:37 AM
    I was trying to make a system for battles/wars too great for our usual combat system. I took inspiration in the rules from Exalted 3rd and L5R basically, but got stuck in some things (discussed by the text). They are far from essential by now, but I suppose at Demigod/God levels, they can be handy. Or, maybe at least Scions of War can find funny... Needless to say, nothing here is written in stone, and I really would like some help around.

    Battle Groups
    Battle Groups are the main components when two groups/armies face each other. Even the most savage, brutish and uncult warlord, wanting or not, divides its forces in these groups. It's entirelly possible (sometimes, even wise) to have an army composed by different types of Battle Groups (frontliners, cavalry, aerial support, artillery, etc.).
    Each Battle Group is composed by the following Attributes. Treat each one as a "medium" for the Battle Group:
    Size: How many members there are in this unit. (1-5)
    Might: The offensive technological/magical power. (0-2)
    Endurance: The general resistance (simillar to Defense, from the normal combat rules)
    Tags: Special Qualities for the Battle Group. Maybe its role in the combat, or even Special Power for it.

    Size are the main Attribute of Battle Groups, and obviously are oriented by the quantity of how many people we have in each group. The Size level represents how many dice they roll for an attack in a Mass Combat Turn.
    • 1 = One single combatant (not using the word "person" here. Giant reasons) <Maybe more usefull with Scale>
    • 2 = around 10 people
    • 3 = around 100 people
    • 4 = around 300 people
    • 5 = around 1,000 people.
    It's possible to have a Size 0 Battle Group, meaning probably the group has been defeated. It's reccomended to have groups with more than 1,000 people being divided in more Battle Groups.

    Might is a short term for many other variables. The sheer offensive power is a way to understand it, but it also means the training level and equipment of this Battle Group.
    • 0 = Barely trained, poorly equiped
    • 1 = Regular forces, regular equipment (+1e to Attack Mass Combat Rolls)
    • 2 = Elite forces, elite/legendary equipment (+2e to Attack Mass Combat Rolls)

    Endurance acts as Defense would work for a normal character. It's normally 1, or 2 if the Battle Group has any effective amor/shield to defend against what they are facing at the present moment. Ancient armors and shields may not be usually effective against present day firearms. Or maybe the other army is composed by supernal creatures that may ignore bulletproof vests.
    Damage suffered by one Battle Group reduces the target Battle Group's Size. When its Size reachs 0, it can not act anymore, but a retreat order may take it before that, when it get obvious that its Size is too small to keep being relevant.

    The Mass Combat Turn
    At the start of a Mass Combat, both sides must first declare their forces. Not necessarily in a map, but both sides must be aware of how many and how different Battle Groups they are facing. Reinforcements may arrive, in according to the narrative, but at the moment, that's what each side has. At this moment, the players can assing their characters to a Battle Group at their will, leading them in where they fight better. One of the players from each army (or the SG, if this is simply the group fighting bad guys) is selected as a Commander. If a Commander had the opportunity to make previous plans for this battle (meaning, they are not under a surprise attack), they can make a roll for Strategy (that depends on how he/she is gonna make this plan. If he/she will study maps and reports of the terrain for hours, that would be Survival + [Mental]. If he's trying to remember famous battles won there, Culture + [Mental]. If he's trying to conceive some master ruse, Subterfuge + [Mental]). Any successes obtained are kept at the Strategy Pool of each army. If the Commander agrees, a Leader of a Battle Group can spend a success from the Strategy pool to get an Enhancement in any roll his/her Battle Group needs.

    The Battle Groups of Both sides then make Orders in turns. To decide wich side acts first, the Commanders of both sides each make a Leadership + Intellect roll, with the most successfull acting first. The first Commander then chooses a Battle Group to act first, and then enemy Commander and so on. Each Success in this roll is kept in the Order Pool for that army. Battle Groups can, at their Commander turn, move or attack by themselves as an action in their Mass Combat Turn. Anything that is not move or attack, requires an Order to be spent. Regroup, use any Power of a Leader or special soldier from any Battle Group is an Order.

    The Mass Combat ends when one of the armies has not any Battle Groups able to fight, or when one side declares surrender. Acceptance of surrender depends of the enemy Commander, though.

    Orders to Battle Groups:
    • Regroup: The Leader of this Battle Group makes a Persuasion + [Social] roll. If any sucesses, the Size of the Battle Group recovers 1. (Troops coming from others Battle Groups already defeated, soldiers deciding not to flee, healing, etc.)
    • Use a Power: Boons, Knacks or Relics special powers. Legendary Creatures/Denizen Special Abilities.
    • "Stand Your Ground!": The Battle Group receives +1 Endurance while mantain its position.
    • Anything Special: Demoralize the enemy, inspire your troops, challenge an enemy Leader to a duel... Feel free to get creative.

    If by any reason the Battle Group of the Commander is took out of the combat (defeated, retreat, Commander knocked uncounscious, etc.), his army can not make use of Strategy and Order pools.

    Tags for Battle Groups:
    • Ranged: If there are no enemy Battle Groups engaged with this Battle Group, they can attack an enemy Battle Group.
    • Armored: Treat this Battle Group as having Armor (Heavy). (It has a "Size Damage box" that can doesn't affect its real Size)
    • Brutal: The successes necessary for a Critical Stunt are reduced by 1.
    • Special Mobility: The Battle Group may move not normal distances as a move action. Ex.: Cavalry, aerial support, subterranean movement troops, etc.
    • Toxic: And enemy Battle Group damaged by this Battle Group suffers the Poisoned Condition (similar to the one from normal combat rules). For a number of Mass Combat Turns equal to the margin of succes for this attack, the Battle Group suffers -1 Size.

    Other Tags/Flairs are welcome, but the core books already offer many that can be used here, as Incorporeality, In/Vulnerability, Inspiration, etc.

      My idea when conceiving this system was to make general and diverse rules as possible. Appliable to WWI soldiers firing from trenches, Spartan troops "fighting at shade", huge dragons raining fire from the skies, aerial bombards, archers firing from tall towers to troops under them, samurai armies colliding in melee charges, or even scenes where one single Legendary Elite Badass soldier hacks tens of enemies with a single blow (Dinasty Warriors says that it's possible)... All of that.

      They are still pretty simple. Even with the idea already in my mind at some time, I just wrote these rules directly to the forum at 5 AM, and as such, we have not so many options about Orders or Tags. I would really appreciate some help with these.

      I don't think the Order pool is a good idea at this point. It works fine when it works (all the sides have many Successes in that pool to spend later), but my worries are when one Commander simply hasn't any and then... "We walk and we attack, guys. That is it". Maybe having a minimal of Orders depending of the number of different Battle Groups he has at his command would be a good idea... Kind of thing that needs testing imo.

      Maybe the "Use a Power" needs specifying that for that power to be effective in a Mass Combat. Feats of Scale sound like a good idea. But Powers like Bolt from the Blue seems perfectly usable in large scale by themselves, so... Also needs testing.

                      I was re-thinking all that. I really would like the system to allow the "Battle Group of a single man". Not just for "Dinasty Warrior-like warriors", but because technically, a single and enormous dragon, warmachine, giant, warship, avatar of a God, etc. would be, by definition, a Size 1 Battle Group. Simply say that "That depends" would let the Size system open to an undesirable relativity. Maybe to forsake the idea of "Size" and ponder that (or simply the attack) by another variable more relevant to this would make more sense...

                      This is basically my big problem with the Size 1 Battle Group. It has only one die to attack and vanishes after one damage (ok, we can say that to inflict this one damage is pretty hard, considering Endurance/Armor/whatever), but that doesn't sound in accord to the epic that "kill a giant dragon" should have. Maybe remodel Endurance as more like a Health system can do that.

                      So... Instead we make Attack in accord to Size, how would be if we create another Attribute in the Battle Group in for Attacks? (Let's call it - in a surge of creativity - by 'Attack'). It's the same as the Attack Roll for the single character (Size 1 Battle Group), or, in case we have an army of Centaurs, their medium value of Attack Rolls. I think it works because: "We need to make a Battle Group made of [insert Denizen here]. What is their Attack value? Their Attack Roll. And that is it". Maybe a larger group can apply Scale too, when attacking a lesser group...

                      PS.: I was playing Shogun Total War 2 yesterday, and didn't realize how this system is similar to their system of "units". I think it's good. I love that game.
