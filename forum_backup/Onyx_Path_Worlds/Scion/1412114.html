                      The Nemetondevos were destroyed, with rare exception. A few survived in Britain until Rome conquered it a century later. Several lived on in different Mantles as members of neighboring pantheons or Titans craving vengeance. - Mysteries of the World, pg 38
                      Gods of the Nemetondevos surviving as different Mantles, some of them Titanic, shouldn't come is a major surprise. Irish Cernunnos or Belenus (who says in his write-up that he grew in power under Roman rule) vs. Belenos (the fallen Nemetondevos) shouldn't be treated as any different than the Irish Gobannos that was already presented in the Nemetondevos write-up.

                      Personally, I'm still not 100% sold on the Nidhoggr the Titan vs Níðhöggr the Dragon write-up, but I'm willing to withhold judgement on it until I can get my hands on Scion: Dragon.
