    So would Sedna be part of the Inuit or Algonquin pantheon?
    Sedna is an Inuit goddess, belonging in particular to the communities inhabitating the eastern shores of Canada, and Greenland. The set of deities revered by the different Inuit communities is quite environment-specific, so a sea goddess like Sedna is virtually unknown in the more landlocked areas. While both are thus artificial labels for internally quite diverse cultures, Inuit and Algonquian peoples are in no way related. The Inuit, in fact, are not even Native Americans in the traditional sense, belonging (as other Arctic and Northwestern peoples of America) to a different, much later, and genetically and culturally unrelated wave of migration than the rest of the American peoples.

                                        Jumalat would be the actual plural (Jumala just means "God" and is the word Finnish translations of the Bible use) - and yeah, the Finnish gods should be a definite possibility! They're pretty much the primary unconfirmed European Pantheon we could hope to see.

                                        The big blank on the map though are other African Pantheons besides the Netjer and Orisha. Possibilities that come to mind are: Igbo, Dinka, Mbuti, Khoisan, and Zulu (or a broader Bantu Pantheon, but if you're going to focus on a specific Bantu culture I'd go with Zulu). Ashanti is possible just to get Anansi, but there aren't a lot of other active figures to draw on - I'm kind of inclined just to make Anansi a roving trickster figure who doesn't act as part of a greater Pantheon, as Nyame and Asase Ye that bore him are just names of the distant Sky and Earth Primordials.
