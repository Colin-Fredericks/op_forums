                                                  Obviously I wasn't using the actual mythos, but the setting itself. The fact that many of the "titans" weren't progenitors but just gods associated with not bowing to the established Pantheons is obvious. I am only working within the games mythology to construct the theories, not those of the actual myths. That would be like arguing that Werewolf the Frosaken/Apocalypse are idiots for using Silver as the werewolves weakness as te in game mythology says it is a curse associated with the spirit Luna (rather than the general mythology that silver is a pure and sacred metal that works on nearly every evil undead thing... and it not becoming the werewolves specific weakness wasn't till the 20th century). Yes, in our mythology, Prometheus was actually a good guy. The game even acknowledges that, before giving "the true story" as per what the game mythology calls for. Considering that the OP is asking about the Titans and God's in the setting of the game, going beyond that to argue the nature of the source mythology is just misleading the topic. So, for the sake of this Topic, per the Scion mythos, the Titans are all right bastards and the Gods were humanities abusive protectors (I am not saying they were good guardians, just that they kept the titans from killing us).

                                                  As for the issue of Law and Justice, that is why I referred to the abstract of Justice, not its mundane form. The abstract would reflect the concept of identifying, fairly trying and rewarding/punishing actions. And most cultures quickly developed thesee rudimentary concepts, even if they chose to then ignore them at occasions or apply them only to specific individuals. Hence Logos being the Titan, remaining removed from humanities ideas and focusing on the Ideal of Justice, not the cultural (and as you pointed out, the gods can be jerks. Many abuses of Justice were justified as religiously necessary, from defied kings/kings who ruled by divine right, to human sacrafice, even in this games setting).

                                                  Finally, per the Titans, Justice is a counter to Chaos (the titan of chaos has less resistance to harm from justice based boons), indicating it might also reflect the primordial force of Order, with Justice being how it manifests within humanity.
