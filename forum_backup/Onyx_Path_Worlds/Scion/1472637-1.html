                                I disagree that the world would be the same as reality. Perhaps there are people in our world who have polytheistic faiths, but the Scion world is one where even the mostly-forgotten ir inactive ones are still active, because their Gods are also active. When was the last time you heard of a great superhuman hero doing some larger-than-life deed in the real world? Or saw tales that Mama Coca or Odin or Rangda had actually been seen? Why take a fantastic premise and bury it under "everything is the same"? Obviously, you can play it however you want, but it seems a shame to conceal the very fantastic elements that make Scion what it is.

                                        Comic books would have more mythic themes as would most popular literature. Mythic themes are already everywhere in popular culture. Heck I hear there's an RPG were you play Scions of the Gods ;-)

                                        But in universe, it would be vastly more common and not as limited to the Greeks with rare nods from the Norse and others. During WWI, there was a movement in America to write new operas based on Native American myths and themes. The only remnant of this movement is that Duh- da-da-da-duh they play to signal the presence of Native Americans in old Westerns ( that hook was probably composed by a Native American Opera singer).

                                        In the world, Native American deities of Beauty and Craft(Music) would have blessed these opera composers in order to both gain legend and respect. Getting their story out would be important.
