                    Originally posted by Iry View Post
                    In the spirit of getting the thread back on track, could I convince Telgar to give us some fun examples of what he imagined for Ability Knacks? The closest I am imagining is the kind of ability charms present in the Exalted system.
                    Actually, most of the current Knacks are pretty easy to imagine as Ability Knacks. The only problem is that because of issues with the current Ability system, some Abilities are outrageously over-represented. Presence, for example, which is just "Social Stuff" with no real modifiers. All the other Social Abilities (Empathy, Politics, Command) are just Presence with conditions put on them. Most of the Stamina Knacks end up in Fortitude because Fortitude is just Stamina-the-Ability. That kinda thing.

                    But for some of the more esoteric Abilities? A Control Knack that lets you accelerate whatever you're driving twice as fast as it could normally go, eventually leading up a short tree to the point that you can drive your car in the sky, or fly your plane underwater. An Animal Ken Knack that lets you calm wild beasts just with your presence, or even attract wild animals to you like Snow White. A Medicine Knack that lets you instantly diagnose diseases

                    This does bring up the issue of where the line falls between some Purviews and some Abilities. What's a Medicine Knack vs a Health Boon? Admittedly, this is a challenge. However I think it's a good challenge since it encourages the writers of Purviews to come up with strong, effective powers that cannot be replicated with an Epic-backed Ability roll. Even level 1 Health Boons should do things that you cannot do with a Perception + Medicine roll.

                                        No matter how sexy you are, you can't shift your shape. No matter how intelligent you are, you can't control computers with your brain. No matter how strong you are, you can't magically protect your tools from harm. Knacks are magical extensions of your Attributes in the current system. Ability Knacks would be magical extensions of your Abilities. You're such an amazing driver you can drive anywhere, even places cars can't normally go, like the sky.

                                        Part of the issue of having multiple power systems (Scion has at least two: Knacks and Boons) is that unless you have very clear lines, there's overlap. I don't necessarily see that as a terrible thing, as long as there are some things that Boons can do and only Boons can do. There should also be some things that Feats (sure, lets give Ability Knacks their own name) can do and only Feats can do. Same for Knacks. There's never going to be a Knack or Feat that throws lightning or banishes someone to the Netherworld. But there could be one that lets you see and command ghosts, or bark orders to animals.

                                        As long as there are specialties for each type of power that they're the best at, or the only way to get, I'm pretty alright. Purviews need to kinda loosen up anyway. Health is just "magical healing" but in Scion, everything is magical. Boons don't get to coast along on being "magic" because Epics are magic, Virtues are magic, Birthrights are magic and, yes, Feats will be magic. Boons need to define a good place for themselves, but I don't think that's impossible.

                                        I'm a professor! Why is no one listening to me?!

                                            I'm not saying Feats shouldn't get into overtly magical displays... I mentioned driving a car with your mind, and being able drive so fast your car doesn't sink.

                                            And yes, some overlap is inevitable. If you can drive your car on the surface of water, you can probably also have a Boon that lets you walk on water that also works for vehicles or other people with enough juice behind it.

                                            At a certain point there's gotta be a line though. It seems easier to keep Feats a bit more focused. "I'm such an awesome driver I can make my car do anything," doesn't exactly leave transportation or travel Boons much space to define themselves. Can I be such an awesome driver people can't help but want to get in bed with me? Can I be such an awesome driver I can drive into the Underworld? I can be such an awesome driver that my car is a better weapon and armor then everything Vulcan's ever made combined?

                                            I have no idea where the line properly rests, but my inclination is to be a bit more focused on Feats letting you do things that are extensions of the linked Abilities taken up to 11 rather than stemming too much into being magic tricks that happen to be arranged around an Ability.
