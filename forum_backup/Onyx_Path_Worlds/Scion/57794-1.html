                                                            True as far as you take it, but EDex covers both attack and defensive capabilities, while EStam is just defensive and EStr is just offensive. (Obviously this is somewhat academic, as you point out you won't have just EDex or EStam/EStr, you can easily have both, but let's keep it simple to illustrate the point.)

                                                            But more to the point, if your opponent has EDex, which many antagonists do to one degree or another, and you don't, the balance between your attack pool and their DV is immediately swung heavily to their side. So you won't hit them most of the time, and unless you're wielding a powerful weapon probably won't hit them very hard, which results in, as you point out, the slap-fight scenario. Which is just slightly less than epic. EStam accentuates the issue, to be sure, but that can be wiped out by a sufficiently powerful weapon, like a machete or similar. The only way to cancel out the benefit of EDex is to have EDex yourself (assuming the opponent is reasonably set up apart from EDex: reasonable Dex score, few points in Athletics and similar).

                                                            Which, given opponents have it, then it makes characters need it, or their survivability is that much less. And nobody likes their character to die, particularly if it's because you built him for character reasons and didn't come up with a hyper-lithe co-ordinated hero.
