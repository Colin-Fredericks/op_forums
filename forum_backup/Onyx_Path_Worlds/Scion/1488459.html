    [Sc 2E] How to rise Legend?

    07-30-2022, 10:31 AM
    I know that character get new point of Legend when they each fullfill their Short, Long and Band Deed. Band Deed is mostly chronicle/series arc played out. But I still have question WHEN rising of Legend happens.

    My players have as Band Deed 'Defeat Evil Organisation'. Now, when this Band Deed is resolved? Characters killed one of higher officers of organisation, they now hunt for the CEO of it - and then, on the billionaire patron of it. They literally blown up office of Evil Org.

    So my questions are those:

    1. Players Characters resolve Band Deed when...
    A. They blown up office of Evil Org - cause public face of it is 'dead'.
    B. When they kill CEO of it - as the leader of Org, he is responsible and 'symbol' of Org.
    C. When they kill billionaire patron - as he is de facto inspiration for Org, it's grand master to Org being only his chess piece.

    2. HOW raise of Legend max points looks like for PCs? They are strike by divine lighting and like He-Man, say 'I have a Power!' in one second? Or they got next small Visitation like vision in their sleep? Something other happens? 🤔

          Originally posted by Possessed View Post
          Considering that Legend is about, well the legend of the characters, I would say it raises when the word of their deed spreads. As the news proclaim the organisation has fallen or the word otherwise spreads. So it is a slow raise of Power as the characters reputation spreads.
          I kind of see this in reverse. IIRC the books say that a Scion's fame increases in reflection of her rising legend, rather than the other way around. So the completion of the deed itself causes the rise of legend, but it would be fitting for a storyguide to narrate it as the records of history or social media spreading the word. Perhaps there's some dramatic license taken by the narrations--did the Scion really walk out of the explosion unscathed? Did they *really* plant the CEO in the pavement like a sapling and carve their epitaph in the erupted pavement like a headstone? A PC's Legend score, like so much of the character sheet, is a mechanical and concrete abstraction that doesn't cleanly reflect the gradual growth of a character's myth.

          It was probably big and memorable at the time. It'll almost certainly change in the retelling. It'll get bigger. Louder. In years to come people will get details wrong as they see themselves reflected in the PC's deeds. Does it matter? Not really.

          Originally posted by Possessed View Post

          For what it is worth, I absolutely agree with this. The Billionaire Backer is definitely worth milking a little longer and being the target of a later deed, if not a recurring figure. I'm not saying it has to wait until the literal moment of its ending; it's kind of silly to have a Scion in line for a hot dog or whatever when suddenly their Legend goes up because somewhere the board of directors agreed to formally dissolve the company. But something big and narratively significant where you, as storyguide, feel comfortable calling their victory.

                                      I feel like this is a bit of a chicken and the egg sort of thing.

                                      In my mind, the chain of events goes like this:

                                      Scion and/or Band do Deeds. Deed makes legend go up. Legend makes the world talk about you and your Deeds. When the deed is accomplished is when you make the impact of yourself in the undercurrent of myth in the world, and this is when your legend rises

                                      If you did it in secret, then it is still *known*, as far as I care. People don't have evidence, there's not a court around that could convict you. But the people who know about you, and know about EvilCo and that it's done for, just have an instinctive feeling that you were involved. A couple people you helped out two arcs ago hear that EvilCo fell on TV, and they look to each other. "How much you want to bet Bob Scion had something to do with that?" One asks the other, and they both laugh and nod, because that's so obviously *you* even when they didn't even know you had a grudge against EvilCo in the first place.

                                      If I haven't made it clear, I don't feel like ascending in legend is flashy in general. If you're ascending to a new state of being, perhaps--from Hero to Demigod, probably, and almost certainly from Demigod to full-fledged divinity. But in general, a Scion feels a sort of frisson, and a sensation that this thing they've been trying to do is now *done.*

                                              I think there's a lot of really interesting subtleties to be considered about what the collective unconscious must be in the game and the relationship Fate has with it. To be consistent with our out of game appreciation of mythology there's quite clearly got to be a lot of stuff that makes it to the collective unconscious that never turns into the specific deeds of the hero being recounted until well after the hero has obviously leveled up if at all...myth is full of people showing up out of nowhere faster, stronger, smarter than ever before to take revenge. In the game obviously they would need to have been getting more legend by deeds...but equally obviously the story of their deeds, which must have been spectacular, would have not have spread as far as needed to actually warn the target.

                                              I mean c'mon ... Lugh shows up for the Tuatha seemingly out of nowhere. One moment he's being passed around in fosterage...then next we hear his face shines like the sun and Balor is in real trouble. While one can reasonably say maybe some of his deeds were known but no-one had connected them to the prophecy, or have since been forgotten, the impression of the situation the stories give us isn't of a steadily building public awareness of Lugh becoming stronger and stronger.

                                              I like the idea of sometimes Fate having to catch up to the hero. The hero can, if clever enough, sneak off and do a great deed in private, harvest the benefits and seemingly stay a private person. Maybe Fate will let that deed stay private. But ripples spread through the collective unconscious. Why did those serial killings stop? What happened to that guy who used to drive a truck round the small town, who was he anyway? Fate isn't going to let the hero just get away with having any impact without consequences. Legend goes up. Fatebinding becomes more likely. If there are no people round to bind to then fate might bind you to places, things, ideas, story elements from your parents past. If you killed someone without legal basis maybe justice gods just keep crossing your path. You keep doing things to keep your secret and those things become public. Fate catches up.

                                              So obviously Legend increase outside of major milestones can be a subtle event to our conscious appreciation of the hero. Fate never fails to notice. Fate always catches up with the hero
