                                                            How I'm inclined to divide things at my table...

                                                            Aset as the queen, goddess of magic, goddess of family bonds (including dynastic authority), and uplifter of the downtrodden; she is daughter of Keb and Nut, wife of Azar, and mother of Heru.

                                                            Hathor/Sekhmet as the fertility figure and war goddess of creation and destruction, not unlike Ishtar, but more divided in her duality, actually split between two mantles; daughter of Neith and Khnum (who is himself the Evening Mantle of Re, but like Khepri [dawn] separate enough to operate as his own god) wife of Heru, and mother of the four sons of Heru

                                                            Bastet as the agent of Re in the darkness. While Sekhmet is his glorious warrior "Eye" in daylight, Bastet is the "Eye" that stalks the night and protects the will of Re where his light does not shine. She is more guardian and assassin than the direct warrior Sekhmet. Her exact origins are ambiguous, as some recall her as a mantle of Sekhmet, and being more lion than the cat she is now. Perhaps a Scion grew powerful enough to take one of their mother's names for themself?
