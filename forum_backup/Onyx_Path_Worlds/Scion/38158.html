    Seeking some Birthright suggestions....

    12-28-2013, 12:36 AM
    Okay, so my friends and I are preparing for our newest Scion game and we just had our first preliminary meeting to select pantheons, divine parents, and Purview specialties. Two of my players are seeking advice on possible Birthrights for their Scions.

    The first Scion is a Scion of Ishtar. We are using Gotham By Night's systems for this game, for the record. Now this player needs the most help, since unfortunately none of us are too familiar with Ancient Babylon. What kind of relics are most appropriate for Annuna Scions? What are some signature Babylonian relics, weapons, etc?

    To give you an idea about this character? He's about eighteen years old, and like Ishtar, he's focusing on his Social Attributes. He's a seductive type who likes to manipulate people. He's also capable in the Physical field as well, as our game will be fairly combat heavy. For his Purviews? Everyone selected four. His four specialties are Fire, Chaos, Health, and Illusion.

    What do you think are appropriate relics for such a character/power set?

    The second character isn't as bad off. The player has several relic ideas already, but two Purviews escape him right now. He's playing a Scion of Tsuki-Yomi. and the two Purviews with which he's having trouble are Psychopomp and Stars. What would be an appropriate relic(s) for a Japanese Scion with these two powers?

    Also, as we are using Gotham By Night's Stars Purview, he's already noticed that Boon they have where you can call down the constellations to aid you. Does anyone know any books and/or web sites that detail Japanese Astrology? Where can one find information on Japanese constellations?

    Thanks in advance for any help.

      For Babylon, you're looking at cylinder seals, cuneiform tablets and ceremonial headdresses. All very, very important to ancient Babylon. He's a man, and he's a manly man (seductive, yes?) so he obviously has a badass Babylon Beard, yes? He could easily invest in a Health Relic that takes the form of beard decorations. Seriously, beards and decorating them was a big deal in Babylonian society. Also, it looks badass. The Babylonians also loved them some bracelets, armbands and rings. They also used ceremonial weapons like maces, which always does a great job doubling as a phallic symbol. Sometimes Ishtar is represented as a winged woman, you might do something with his mom's feathers.

      I can't really offer much for the Kami player. Never been that interested in the Asian religions.

              Exactly like Medieval maces, no. Generally the same shape, yes. In thousands of years, there hasn't been a lot of evolution in the most efficient way to crush someone's head with a heavy bit of metal. It's pretty easy to find some examples, though. A thorough Googling for "Mesopotamian Mace", "Babylonian Mace", "Assyrian Mace" and suchnot will get you some results. Here's a sample: Mesopotamian Mace at Christie's for Auction.

              The art by TerriblyUncreative that went into the GBN Anunna supplement shows the beard ornaments of Mesopotamian men pretty well. If you watched The Hobbit, you saw the Dwarf King rocking some pretty snazzy beard-bling of his own. I don't mention the beard-bling just because it's cool, it shows up regularly in artistic depictions of powerful male figures. If you want Birthright ideas for an ancient culture, their art is the best place to go. Just try some brief image searches for Mesopotamian Art, or Babylonian Art. You'll get some great ideas very quickly.


              EDIT: Don't miss checking out Babylon Batteries. Modern science says they're just boring old vessels for sacred texts, but they could totally have been the first-ever electrochemical battery!! Obviously, Scion goes with the cooler of the two ideas. Sounds like a good channel for Sky or Fire to me!

                              I should point out that in this particular story? The Titan War hasn't actually started yet. It may not start at all. With this story, I'm going for more of a Percy Jackson kind of feel. The players are young Scions who attend special schools maintained by their respective pantheons. The game will actually begin with kind of a "Tri-Wizard Cup Tournament" kind of competition between their respective schools. Think "Hunger Games" but without the unwilling participants and the murder. The goal isn't to kill everyone else in the contest. It's more of a "Capture the Flag" kind of game. The gods create a challenging environment for the Scions, who have to locate some important object, get it, and return it successfully to their particular pantheon's camp. This allows for Scions of all stripes to have a decent shot at winning. Yeah, the combat monsters will just try to fight their way to victory, but the more cerebral Scions can try to win through brains over brawn and Social players can play the manipulation game to get what they want.                               Oh, and the players are actually starting out at Legend 5, using the rules found in the Scion: Companion. Also, like the Hunger Games, Scions can expect aid from the gods, much like Tributes could win aid from Sponsors. The Scion of Odin is running low on Legend? Poof! One of Idun's Golden Apples appears nearby him. The Scion of Horus is being pursued by the Scion of Zeus, in an attempt to capture the object from him and claim victory for the Theoi instead? Boom!                               So, with everyone starting out at Legend 5, perhaps the Tsuki-Yomi Scion got his Stars relic as a Trophy? I'll keep working on a more intriguing plot hook, but that may end up being something of a fallback.

                                                  Could you go into a bit more detail about who the Scion of Tsuki-yomi is supposed to be and what he does? Perhaps elaborate on what kind of Birthrights he already has in mind?

                                                  Regarding Stars, I'd recommend some kind of Japanese clothing woven by Orihime. She's more interesting than Ama-tsu-mikaboshi because she is more fleshed out. Furthermore, she can be connected to the Chinese shen for inter-pantheon shenanigans, though Orihime and Zhinu are different enough that the pair can also be kept as separate but similar figures. If he wants to relate his usage of the Stars purview to Japanese astrology, other possibilities include but are not limited to the implements used by onmyoji and other Shinto priests. Ofuda seem a bit too ephemeral to me (not to mention cliched), but a ritual baton, a wooden wand, or even a mobile shrine carried in processions might be suited to his tastes.

                                                  If none these options appeal to him, stick a piece of something appropriate in a brocade bag, hang it with a bit of string, and call it an omamori.

                                                  Regarding Psychopomp, it depends on a lot on what he wants to do with the Scion. If he wants to focus on the bit about guiding the souls of the deceased, toss him one of those paper lanterns that can be carried on a stick. Alternatively, if he wants to be a messenger of the gods, I'd recommend a sashimono, which has very strong military implications. Of course, you could also recommend a simple pair of straw sandals, but that strikes me as a bit boring on its own.
