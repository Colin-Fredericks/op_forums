    Scion 2: A Developer's Note

    11-20-2013, 04:50 PM
    Hello, all. I'm throwing down a little introduction and update for folks here. It'll be similar to the one I had on the old forums, most likely. I'm a pretty predictable guy.

    My name is Joseph Carriker, and I'm the developer-type for the new Scion line. I'm an avid mythology nerd and amateur folklorist, a practicing polytheist and did some writing on the old Scion (in particular, in Scion: Demigod, I designed the Touchstones and the myriad Underworlds there). I've written for a bunch of New World of Darkness (including having been developer for Vampire: the Requiem for a while), a bunch of Exalted Second Edition, and do plenty of other freelancing in addition to all that.

    At current, we're putting the finishing touches on a baseline system for the new Scion. Rather than simply pick up the old system, we decided to build one from the ground up, to better suit our needs for issues of things like scaling and player-driven narratives. That can be a pretty exhaustive process, even when the developer *doesn't* have to deal with recent spinal surgeries. I did, though, back at the beginning of October, so you've all got my deepest apologies: I'm the reason things are running a little behind where we'd like them. That's also the reason I've been a presence here in name spoken by others only.

    At this point, though, we're about to enter a "hammering on the systems" phase of the design, and to get folks writing on both systems and non-systems portions of both Scion: Origins and Scion: Hero, so that's all about to pick up like crazy. I'm not a big community development type, I'll admit. I admire my fellow developers who can lay out everything they're working on as they're working on it. I'm just a bit more of a hermit when it comes to my creative process, I fear.

    That said, do keep an eye out for more of these developer's notes. I'm going to use them as a means of communicating some of the new or improved elements of our game, and get some conversation going about them. I'm also here to answer any questions you might have, but I'll be super up-front with you right now: I probably don't have the answers to anything but the most general of questions at this time.

    I will say this, though: "Hero" is going to include ten full pantheons, and my goal is to have a handful more each in both "Demigod" and "God". That may fall by the wayside, depending on the needs of the books themselves, but I like a world full of possibilities, and I think that's a great way to start.

    Regards,
    Joseph D. Carriker

                                                      Originally posted by Shining Dragon View Post
                                                      Is the system still recognizable as the Storyteller system?
                                                      Depends what you mean. The system isn't Storyteller (any of the many, many versions of it), or Storytelling (either version). The rules team includes people who've worked on Trinity 1st, cWoD Storyteller Revised, the *20 version of Storyteller from V20, W20, and M20, Storytelling, GMC Storytelling from God-Machine, Demon, Strix, Idigam, and Fallen World... You can recognise elements drawn from all of them, and elements that are completely new.

                                                      It uses d10s, and the character sheet has dots on it.


                                                      For reference:

                                                      Storyteller - the original engine, used from Vampire: The Masquerade to Wraith: The Great War, with slight variations like Werewolf's original combat system along the way.

                                                      Storyteller (Trinity) - the fixed-target number, skills linked to attributes version used in the Trinity, Aberrent, and Adventure 1st editions.

                                                      Storyteller Revised - Vampire: The Masquerade Revised through to Orpheus, which imported some design from Trinity.

                                                      Storyteller (Exalted) - the 1st edition of Exalted, technically based on Trinity's system, not the cWoD's. Includes a 1.5 version in the players guide that changes the combat rules.

                                                      Storyteller (Ex2) - the system Exalted 2nd and Scion use, with tick-based combat.

                                                      Storyteller (Ex3) - the system for Exalted's new edition.

                                                      Storytelling - the nWoD system, different from any of the above. Fixed(ish) TNs, one success is a success, Power/Finesse/Resistance stats.

                                                      Storytelling (GMC) - the updated nWoD system from Demon onwards, and the Chronicle Books.



                                                      So... Which Storyteller do you mean?

                                                      Dave Brookshaw
