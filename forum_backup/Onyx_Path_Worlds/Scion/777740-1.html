I suppose it could have dropped after he left the Dodekatheon, but unlike them, he stayed behind in the world and took the form of Satan, so he's had thousands of years to rebuild his power.
                                                            Pan's backstory in Demigod is also highly flawed. The origin of his Satanic nature rightfully belongs to a more obscure mythical figure. The satyr Marsyas was the one who challenged Apollo to the contest, only to be flayed for his hubris. I have generally concluded that Pan's name was assigned to the role of Marsyas in Scion: Demigod so that they could have a more famous mythical figure covered rather than giving such a position to a comparatively minor one. In my games, though I have sometimes used it as written, I generally treat Pan as a full god in his own right (with Purviews such as Animal (Goat), Chaos, and Fertility favored) with his own Scions running around.
