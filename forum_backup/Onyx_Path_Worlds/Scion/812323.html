    Idea for a Hero Game

    01-19-2016, 10:51 PM
    So I've had this idea for a story hook that starts out at Hero level and possibly extends to Demigod / God level depending on the events play out. The main plot is that most of the villains aren't actually villains. They're just misunderstood or they're misguided in their attempts to do the right thing.

    Below is what I came up with for the main villains of the story hook, and I'd like to know what you guys think.

    Please keep in mind, however, that these are very rough outlines of their characters. Their character sheets will need to be fleshed out, but this is the general concept of what I'm thinking of for their ideas.


    ------------------------------------------

    The Terrans: Not all Scions chose to ally themselves with the Gods to fight off the Titans. One group calling itself The Terrans is a Band who have allied themselves with Terra, the Greater Titan of the World. Yet despite their affiliations, The Terrans are not inherently malicious or cruel. In fact, their leader plans to save humanity from Terra's wrath, as opposed to wiping them all out. Unfortunately, while their intentions may be altruistic, they are ultimately misguided.

    The Band's name comes from an alternate name for humanity, which is commonly seen in science fiction settings. The Band's leader, Rutfol, thought it was an appropriate name to show his alliance with Terra, and so he took it for his own.

    Out of all the Terrans, Rutfol is the only member who is wholly comitted to working with Terra. The rest of the Band has either been bribed in some fashion, or possess alterior motives of their own. It is possible that with the right incentives or encouragement, The Terrans can be convinced to disband and switch sides in the war. Although their parents will likely not be happy with their actions up to that point.

    Unbeknownst to The Terrans, Rutfol is not the only one with a master plan. Another among them has been sent under cover to interfere with his efforts to sabotage his work right under his nose!


    -------------------------------


    Rutfol: Scion of Loki

    Calling: Mad Doctor

    Nature: Visionary

    Legend: 4

    Details: The Norse God of mischief is known for producing many Scions who side with the Titans in the war against the Gods. Rutfol is one such Scion, having thrown his lot in with Terra, the Greater Titan of the World. Unlike his half siblings, however, Rutfol has yet to be hunted down by his angry relatives and continues to remain active in the war. In fact, he has remained active so long, that some believe he is on the cusp of becoming a Demigod, which will make him an even greater threat than ever if he is not stopped.

    Receiving his visitation late in life (he was 56 years old at the time), Rutfol is no warrior. Indeed, he is a man of science, using his brain to overcome problems now that his aging muscles are no longer up to the task. His black, silver streaked hair is kept short and combed back in a wavy style while circle rimmed glasses rest upon his face. Like the stereotypical scientist, he is usually seen in white garments as a notification that he is working on his experiments.

    Rutfol is the current leader of the Terrans by virtue of him being the oldest and presumably smartest member of the Band. When the others hear that he is close to ascending to the next level and already has a master plan, they decided to go with the flow, rather than fight for supremacy. If Rutfol is taken out of commission, however, this might change as they would be left to their own devices.

    Despite the fact that he works for Terra, Rutfol's plan is not human extinction as many would expect it to be. He wants to "evolve" humanity into a state where it can survive in Terra's realm without having to rely on technology to sustain itself. He even has a master plan already in mind that he has been working on, but progress is going slow.

    Rutfol's grand plan is a project he code names the Chimera Cloud. This cloud is intended to be an airborne mutation of the therianthrope virus, which he will then release into earth's atmosphere to spread across the entire population of earth. In addition to the minor difficulties that comes with engineering a supernatural plague, his biggest challenges are finding a way to include multiple strains without compromising the cloud, and making sure the victims remain loyal to him in case Terra decides he's expendable afterward.

    Rutfol carries two birthrights, one given to him by Loki upon his visitation and one given to him by Terra upon his defection. Loki granted him a Mossberg called "Disaster" which allows him to channel the Chaos and Magic purviews. Terra granted him a polar bear fur coat which allows him to access the Animal and Health purviews and is considered to be Hide / Leather on the armor chart. Due to its color and design, it looks like a makeshift lab coat.


    Powers: Epic Intelligence, Epic Wits, Epic Manipulation, Animal, Chaos, Health, Magic, Jotunblut


    Skills: Occult, Empathy, Animal Ken, Marksmanship, Craft, Medicine


    Birthrights: Disaster (Mossberg - Chaos, Magic) 2, Bear Coat (Leather / Hide, Animal, Health) 3


    Role Playing Hints: You genuinely believe that mankind can co-exist with Terra if you complete your master plan. If the human race is transformed into chimerical beasts, they can be safely absorbed into Terra without having to destroy them. This transformation will also sever humanity's dependency on technology to survive, allowing them to live in harmony with nature instead of exploiting it. Your assistant Eliza tries hard to get your approval, but she's not a super genius like you. She is attractive though and you might be willing to give her some more attention if you could only pry yourself away from your work.

    Your ancestors may be Norse, but you are no warrior. The shotgun you carry around is for self defense should anybody attack you or break into your lab. Instead you prefer to outwit your enemies. Luring them into traps and utilizing chemical warfare to disrupt and destroy their bodily functions.

    Simon Ash: Scion of Ares

    Calling: Strongest Warrior

    Nature: Bravo

    Legend: 4

    Details: The member of The Terrans who's goals are most at odds with the rest of the Band. Simon doesn't give a crap about Rutfol's plan to save humanity, he's here because he has something to prove; both to himself and to his father. Prior to his visitation, Simon was part of an underground fight club and miraculously ruled as their undefeated champion. But one night his jealous competitors accused him of cheating and pulled a gun on him. Armed with nothing but his fists to protect him, Simon was gunned down and left for dead, which is when Ares stepped out from the shadows to ensure his son would not die because of a weakling's cowardice.

    Simon is actually part of an experiment that Ares has been testing. The God of War is curious to know whether his children are truly deserving to be called warriors, or if they have become too reliant on their supernatural powers. As part of this experiment, Ares has forbidden Simon to learn any Purviews with the exception of Arete. Thus, Simon has had to overcome problems with the aid of his Epic Attributes and whatever tools he has at his disposal. Time will tell whether this makes him a weaker Scion, or one of the deadliest in the modern World

    Simon is constantly ready for a fight and looks the part too. He is tan skinned, broad shouldered with thick muscles and bares a multitude of scars on his upper body, he appears to be in his mid to late 30's in age. His hair is dark brown, but he keeps it shaved to stubble so his enemies can't grab it in a fight. Instead of a shirt, he wears a chest harness which carries his Birthrights strapped to it. He also wears a pair of cargo pants, the multiple pockets of which can be used to store other supplies he may need.

    Simon's induction into The Terrans is actually part of an ongoing test. He wishes to prove himself as the greatest warrior by defeating Scions who rely too heavily on their vaunted magic in battle. So far, he seems to be proving his point, as Scions who have attempted to wield Purviews against him come back dead or wounded! If Simon was convinced that he has proven himself worthy to his father, he may decide to take up arms against the Titans as the equal of any Scion beside him.

    Aside from the powers inherent to his divine blood, the only presents Ares bestowed upon him during his visitation were his silver Labrys and Suctum. The Labrys (Strength) adds +2L to the regular template and inflicts aggravated damage on opponents weak to silver. The Scutum (Resolve) adds +1B and +2Defense to the regular template.


    Powers: Epic Strength, Epic Stamina, Epic Wits, Epic Perception, Arete


    Skills: Brawl, Craft, Fortitude, Melee, Presence, Control


    Birthrights: Strength (Labrys - +2L, Silver Weapon) 3, Resolve (Scutum - +1B, +2Def) 3


    Role Playing Hints: Magic is for weaklings and cowards. Or at least that's what you've come to understand since your father has forbidden you to use the powers wielded by so many other Scions. While they hurl lightning bolts or conjure fire balls, you rush in screaming with an axe. You've lost count of how many would be Heroes you've defeated while working for Rutfol, but you've been at this test for a while now. You're beginning to suspect that you may have proven your point, but Ares has yet to confirm or deny your suspicions, so for now you continue to work for The Terrans until you get some good news.

    Close quarters combat is your specialty, you won many fight club tournaments before your visitation and now your Scion powers are focused entirely around Epic Attributes. You're durable enough to tank whatever your enemies throw at you, but if you can't close the distance, they may eventually wear you down.

    Eliza Gaetur: Scion of Sobek

    Calling: Research Assistant

    Nature: Pedagouge

    Legend: 3

    Details: Eliza is the beautiful, young research assistant to Rutfol. Unfortunately, she is nowhere near Rutfol's level of intellect; her father being more focused with brawn than brain. As far as mortals go, she is pretty smart, but Rutfol's experiments require a supernatural level of intelligence, which prevents him from taking her seriously. Many of her duties include security detail such as cleaning up the lab, securing the specimens, and preventing escapees from leaving the facility. She also helps with administering clinical trials when Rutfol needs the extra hands.

    While she may not possess the divine gifted brilliance of Rutfol, she is still a fully qualified biologist and has been working hard to prove herself to be a worthy partner in his research. In fact, she has been trying so hard that she accidentally developed feelings towards Rutfol against her better judgement. For now, her crush on him keeps her around, but if she decided that Rutfol was a lost cause, she may give up and seek employers more appreciative of her talents.

    Eliza is a young woman approaching her early 30's and is rather attractive to look upon. Her long hair is tied in a bun to keep it from dangling around her research and is a shade of mottled black that almost seems green in the right light. She doesn't wear glasses, but she does have a form fitting lab coat with a number of glass vials tucked into the pockets.

    As part of her visitation, Eliza was granted a necklace made of predator teeth which allows her to channel the Animal and Water purviews. She also wears a cactus flower hairpin that allows her to access the Fertility purview. After joining The Terrans, Eliza also carries a set of chemistry vials in her coat.


    Powers: Epic Stamina, Epic Appearance, Epic Strength, Animal (Alligator), Fertility, Heku, Water


    Skills: Stealth, Awareness, Brawl, Medicine, Animal Ken, Occult


    Birthrights: Tooth Necklace (Animal, Water) 2, Flower Hairpin (Fertility) 1, Experimental Serums (Unique Power - 5 uses) 5.


    Note: These vials she carries are actually experimental samples of the Chimera Cloud and can transform mortals (ordinary humans) into therianthropes upon exposure. If she uses these vials, they will be replenished after 24 hours. The strain of which species of therianthrope is created is determined by the Story Teller.


    Role Playing Hints: Despite what Rutfol might think, you are a real doctor and have been trying to prove that to him with every stage of the cloud's development. The frustration of him treating you like a common nurse, combined with your growing sexual attraction towards him has begun to negatively affect your work. It won't be long before you're forced to make a choice between taking a more affirmative approach to getting his attention, or seeking other means of employment.

    Some might understandably be curious how you could support a man who wants to turn the entire world into chimerical monster. To which you calmly respond that most of your pantheon are half animals themselves, so it doesn't seem like such a big deal.

      Kyle Junior: Scion of Hera and Gaia

      Calling: Group Organizer

      Nature: Survivor

      Legend: 3

      Details: The youngest member member of The Terrans, Kyle stands out among his colleagues. While the others sided with the Titans out of some desire for power or goal, Kyle joined up out of gratitude. He was born as a Scion of Hera, but never received a proper visitation because Zeus was furious when he learned of Kyle's lineage.

      During the night Hera was supposed to reveal the truth to him, Zeus conjured a mighty thunder storm which burned Kyle's house down, killing his mortal parents and leaving him with severe burns across 70% of his body. Miraculously, Kyle managed to escape without brain damage and recovered most of his primary motor functions. He was later visited in a dream by Gaia who revealed she had protected him from Zeus' fury and offered to provide him further assistance if he would aid her in return. Kyle accepted and awoke in the hospital bed, along with his Scion powers.

      In appearance, Kyle looks like a disfigured teenager between the ages of 15 to 16 years old. About 70% of his naked body feels like dried leather because of the burns he suffered. He prefers to wear baggy, light weight clothing to avoid irritating his skin. He is rather proud of the fact he still has a full head of dark hair, which he grows down to the base of his neck. The burns include the left side of his face and most of his mouth. The right side of his face from the nose up to the forehead is normal, however. Despite his gruesome disfigurement, Kyle does not appear to have any impairments when it comes to physical activities.

      Despite his age, Kyle is the most coordinated of The Terrans and has been placed in charge of commanding the Band's servants. He rarely enters combat by himself, instead being accompanied by a squad of minions which he organizes into attack teams when necessary. Simon jokingly refers to him as the "ant king" because of his armor and leadership skills.

      It is impossible to convince Kyle to serve the Dodekatheon. They tried to kill him over something that wasn't his fault and it was the avatar Gaia who saved him from their wrath. However, clever or wise Scions may be able to convince Kyle to go rogue if they can find a way to disrupt his feelings of gratitude towards his adoptive patron.

      Gaia bestowed Kyle with two birthrights that have served him quite well up to this point. The first is a suit of chitinous armor that seems to have been fashioned out of some kind of giant insect carapace. It allows him to channel both the Animal and the Earth purviews when he's wearing it. The second birthright is a whip that has been woven together from thorny briers and inflicts lethal damage as a flexible weapon template. It also allows him to channel the Fertility purview!


      Powers: Epic Appearance, Epic Stamina, Epic Strength, Animal (Ants), Earth, Fertility, Arete


      Skills: Animal Ken, Empathy, Awareness, Presence, Command, Melee


      Birthrights: Chiton Armor (Lamellar - Animal, Earth) 3, Thorned Whip (Flexible Weapon - Fertility) 2, Mercenaries (20 Followers) 5.


      Role Playing Hints: You hate (and fear) the Dodekatheon, they tried to kill you as a child and they never take responsibility for their actions unless somebody forces them to do so. Your life wasn't saved by the Gods, it was saved by Gaia; a Titan. It is clear to you who's side of the war you should be on. Or at least it was in the beginning, the Scions who failed to stop your Band in the past kept talking about how Titans were master manipulators. Despite the gratitude you bare towards Gaia, the seeds of doubt have been planted and you can't help but wonder if she sees you as some pawn to be discarded later.

      Like an ant colony, you prefer to hide your true numbers until you feel threatened. You might keep one of your Mercenary squads in the open to maintain a security presence, but the others are usually hiding, waiting for your signal when they can swarm in from all directions, trapping your enemies in the middle.

      Heather Mukherjee: Scion of Shiva

      Calling: Doom Bringer

      Nature: Fanatic

      Legend: 3

      Details: If Simon represents an immediate danger to enemies of The Terrans, Heather represents a long term danger. Heather believes that the World is supposed to be destroyed; that it should have been destroyed a long time ago. But this ongoing war between the Gods and Titans is prolonging the inevitable and thus interfering with the cycle of death and rebirth. Her father is supposed to bring about the end of the world according to Hindu mythology; so as his daughter, she seeks to help him in his destined role.

      If humanity's next stage in the cycle is to be reborn as Chimeras, then that is how it must be. Heather will gladly aid Rutfol in his plan to carry humanity to into it's next stage of existence. However, should Heather be convinced that her actions bring harm to the natural cycle, her fanaticism may cause her to change course, if not suffer a crisis of faith.

      Heather is a half Indian, half American immigrant with long dark hair and a lightly tanned complexion who appears to be in her mid 20's. She dresses in traditional Hindu clothing, consisting of light, breezy garments with an abundance of jewelry. Unfortunately she is too poor to afford real jewels and so she makes up for this with painted beads and tacky plastic imitations.

      Upon her visitation, Heather was granted three birthrights, her only real pieces of jewelry at the moment. The first is a silver chain with a tear drop shaped ruby that she wears around her head, which allows her to channel the Fire purview. The second is an onyx encrusted ring that she wears on her left hand which allows her to channel the Darkness purview. Lastly, she wields a crescent dagger in her right hand which adds +1L and +1 Deffense to the Khopesh template and allows her to channel the War purview.


      Powers: Epic Dexterity, Epic Stamina, Fire, Darkness, Samsara, War


      Abilities: Melee, Athletics, Fortitude, Survival, Presence, Stealth


      Birthrights: Ruby Circlet (Fire) 1, Onyx Ring (Darkness) 1, Crescent Dagger (Khopesh - +1L, +1Def, War) 3


      Role Playing Hints: No matter what anyone says, you know in your heart that you are not evil. Fate itself has foretold not one, but several prophecies in which the World is destroyed, only to be reborn later as a fresh, clean start. This war between Gods and Titans serves no purpose, it merely delays the natural cycle of birth, death and rebirth.

      Only direct instructions from Shiva himself could make you pause in your attempts to bring about the end of the world, but even he would need to explain why he neglects to fulfill his destiny before you would consider altering your path in life. Fortunately for you, it seems he is content to sit back and watch this play out, which must mean you're doing something right if he chooses not to intervene.

      Veronica: Scion of Pan

      Calling: Drug Dealer

      Nature: Trickster

      Legend: 3

      Details: A secret agent for the corrupt God of Love, The Terrans know that Veronica is a Scion, but they don't know her real motivations for joining their Band. In truth, Veronica's father is quite happy with the idea of turning humanity into a bunch of animals. He just has one little idea he wants to tweak into the mix. Once the Chimera Cloud is finally complete, Veronica will sabotage the master sample by mixing in an aphrodisiac of her father's own creation. This will corrupt the Chimera Cloud, driving its victims insane with primal lust! An entire planet full of mindless, sex crazed animals; what more could a satyr ask for?

      Until she is ready to move, Veronica takes the role of lab assistant to Rutfol and Eliza. Rutfol mixes up the drugs, Eliza studies the results and Veronica administers the doses. Neither of her "fellow doctors" have considered that putting Veronica in charge of distribution gives her plenty of chances to contaminate their work!

      In appearance, Veronica is the most attractive member of The Terrans and shamelessly flaunts her sex appeal for all to see. She appears to be in her early 20's, marking her as more of a nurse than a doctor. Her ample breasts threaten to pop the buttons of her form fitting lab coat while the mini skirt and thigh high stockings give her the sexy nurse look only seen in kinky pornos. Her blond hair is so brightly shaded there is no way it could naturally be that color, while her pretty white skin is smooth as silk and pillowy soft to the touch.

      As is expected of one of Pan's children, Veronica is gripped with sexual addiction. Promiscuous and near omnisexual, she is completely willing to use her body as a bargaining chip to get what she wants, whether that be personal favors, some manner of payment, or just for the fun of it. She's not too picky about who or what she shares a bed with, either.

      Veronica's Birthrights are strange things dreamt up by the mind of a God too crazy to live among the Dodekatheon. She wears a black dog collar which allows her to access the Animal purview, but is also cursed. Only Pan has the power to freely remove it without using some kind of spell. It also compels the wearer (in this case, her) to obey any commands given to them by Pan.

      She also carries a syringe that allows her to access the Health purview and a bouncy ball that allows her to use the Chaos purview. The ball has 2 unique powers of its own. First, when thrown as a weapon, the ball inflicts the same amount of damage as a heavy unarmed attack. Second, by spending a point of legend just before throwing it, the ball will always bounce its way back to the pitcher's hand!



      Powers: Epic Manipulation, Epic Appearance, Epic Stamina, Health, Arete, Chaos, Animal (Dog)


      Skills: Presence, Medicine, Animal Ken, Stealth, Empathy, Thrown


      Birthrights: Dog Collar (Animal, Unique Power) 2, Syringe (Health) 1, Bouncy Ball (Chaos, 2 Unique Powers) 3


      Role Playing Hints: None of the others in your Band know of your true motivations for joining their group. You would like to keep it this way for as long as possible, as you have no doubt they wouldn't understand if you told them. So for now, you play the role of lab assistant, helping Rutfol and Eliza test out their new drugs while keeping a very close watch on their development. Once the Chimera Cloud is finished, you may only have a few days to act before Rutfol carries out his master plan to release the master sample.

      You're a lover, not a fighter. Your first response to dealing with a problem is to make your enemy think you're not a threat. Entice the enemy into lowering their guard, then stab a giant needle in their heart when they think they've won! If seduction isn't working, however, you're not above stirring up the hornet's nest and slipping through while everybody is distracted.

          Sounds like an interesting idea Nyrufa, there really are a lot of neat things you could do with them, especially depending on just what Pantheons and Divine Parents the players of this game end up being.

          With Rutfol, you could do something interesting with how he is reconciling his plan with Ragnarok. Within his cosmology, all of his works are Fated to be undone, as there is a very strict list on 'who is getting through this alive', and there is specific mention of only two human survivors (Lif and Lífþrasir) who climb down from the branches of the tree. Since this means that within his own cosmology, Rutfol is fated to fail this (as at least two humans are going to survive and rebuild the species) it gives him a huge incentive to fight tooth and nail against the coming of Ragnarok. He might even want to try to specifically prevent any lycanthropy within his strains due to avoiding the fulfillment of the 'Wolf Time' part of the Prophecy.

          Now Simon, there is someone you have a fun opportunity to play Trial with. Killing Scions is a hugely terrible idea, most cultures represented in Scion have very big concepts or even laws about how to deal with someone murdering a child of yours (even if it wasn't technically 'murder' as we would understand it) which in some cases could even get very powerful folks coming at him like a bull in a china shop. Maybe make a list of the Scions he has killed and then check out the cultural reactions from those Divine Parents to the murder of their child? For example, if he killed one of the children of the Tuatha or Aesir, they could demand an honor price (Log n-Enech for the Irish, Wergild for the Norse) from Ares, or if Ares refuses, Zeus even. That's even assuming a specific Deity doesn't decide to ignore their laws and try to come down onto Simon like a ton of bricks. Less... directly violent Gods or Goddesses might instead post a bounty for Simon (the Irish might want his head, other Pantheons might want him alive for trial or torture). You also could explore just what counts as 'magic' in Scion. While hurling lightning bolts is defiantly a use of Levin' Fury, Zeus' lightning (and several other Deities) has their lightning forged for them, and they physically wield it like a weapon which may cast a degree of confusion with Simon.

          Eliza seems a little flat at the moment, but just as you said, it's a rough draft. Now, since it looks like her primary motivation is getting validation from Rutfol, that gives you a place to start. If a member of the Band were to catch her eye (Rutfol only has Epic Manipulation, a Scion with Epic Appearance or Epic Charisma could pretty easily contend against him, it only really being a contest if he is intentionally trying to string her along romantically.) she could go turncoat on him. The evil doctor's assistant falling for a hero and turning on him is... sort of a, well, overused trope however. I don't know if these Scions have lost their Virtues yet, but Eliza might have to end up fighting her Harmony tooth and nail over this plan as well, which could help explain why she is seeking validation / support (would be at low willpower).

          Kyle presents an exceptionally interesting opportunity to deal with who his father is. Since he isn't a PC, you could fiddle with the normal "must have a mortal parent" condition of Scions, the Greeks certainly have had Heroes who were not Divine and the children of Gods who were rather, well, weak or even mortal. Since Hera does love Zeus in her own way, whoever this father was had to be an exceptionally special person. His father might have actually been the one to save him as well, and Gaia took the credit (she is presented as being a bit opportunistic, such as leaping to help Rhea when Cronus had imprisoned the Cyclopes and the Hundred Handed Ones). I'd suggest a God who has Epic Charisma, and Epic Appearance who has a political reason they couldn't directly claim Kyle (I don't know if you are using the Bogovi, but Dzahbog could be a good option. The Bogovi have serious laws against illegally conceived children.) and he has been protecting his son as best he can since. The Band could even be tasked to bring Kyle back so his super-duper-high Epic Socials parent could explain the situation.

          Heather is my favorite just because there is a fascinating way to 'defeat' her as an antagonist. A Scion, from the Hindu Pantheon or not, could clash with her in a theological argument over the nature of the cycle of the rebirth of the world. Big points could be her believing to know better than the Triumvirate which is a pretty big "don't do that." for almost any Pantheon. They just know better than you. If she is so devout, she would need to accept that they know better than her, or be 'heretical'. A very fun thing to allow a Mental Scion to clash with her and take one home for the team.

          Veronica is sort of tricky. She's lucky none of the Terrans have Mystery, or she would be out of luck with this betrayal plot. Pan's idea does seem reasonably in character for his presentation in Scion: God (I think that's where he is? Or Demigod?). No real ideas for her, sorry on that front. Seems a bit two dimensional at the moment, which is totally reasonable for a rough draft. Maybe add in some deeper motivation? I think her big risk is someone just changing her mind about the whole situation, it would tie in with her devil may care presentation.

                  Alright, I came up with some ideas / notes for things to edit about the character concepts in the future.


                  1: Exchange Rutfol's Magic purview (on his shotgun) for Fire.

                  Reason: Magic doesn't seem like an appropriate purview to be tied to a weapon named "Disaster." Also, Rutfol is supposed to be a doctor, not a sorcerer.



                  2: Make Veronica's syringe double as a melee weapon with piercing quality. Possibly modified from the knife template.

                  Reason: Given Veronica's approach to dealing with enemies, the Syringe seems like it would be good for a concealed weapon style Relic.



                  3: Clean up the character backstories. Provide explanations for how they came to join The Terrans for those missing one.

                  Reason: Should be obvious.


                  4: Give Kyle one or two more Epic Attributes. Possibly Wits and Perception

                  Reason: Kyle is supposed to be the organizer of the group and is the one in charge of minions. Also, he has an insect theme and is currently lacking in terms of hive mind mentality.
