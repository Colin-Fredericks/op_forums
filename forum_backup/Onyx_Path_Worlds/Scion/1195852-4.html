                                            Originally posted by Gnomeborg View Post
                                            I'm also still confused about EXP costs, because the chart in Origin makes everything seem astronomically over-priced.
                                            Really? I had the opposite reaction.

                                            Players should be expecting 4XP per session, minimum (1 for showing up, 1 for achieving their Short-term Deed, 1 for everyone achieving their Short-term Deed, 1 for spending half the Momentum in the pool). That's all entirely within the player's control - They set their Deeds, they control their Momentum, etc. (and you can have multiple Short-term Deeds, so you can hedge your bets).

                                            And then in any "significant" session for your character, you're going to be picking up some of the extras (Long-term Deed, story milestone, story end, etc.). Assume one of those every 2 or 3 sessions, on average?

                                            That means you're looking at putting up an Attribute every other session, or picking up a 4 dot Relic in 4 sessions (That's a Relic that grants you two separate Purviews, or grants you a general +2 Enhancements. To put it another way, the equivalent of 6 extra dice.). It means that in 2 or 3 sessions, you can grow your single Follower straight past a group (5-10 individuals) into a mob (20-50 people). You're never more than about 5 sessions from having the Golden Stag of Artemis, or from owning Tyrfing.

                                            And remember, this is just one of your advancement methods. While you're spending experience to pick up Birthrights, or to max out your Skills and Attributes, you're also completing Deeds. And it's that which triggers Legend increasing, and controls Callings and Boons (I.e., where you actually get your big, flashy power).
