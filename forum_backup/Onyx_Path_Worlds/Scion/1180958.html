    Enfant Terrible: The Scion Origin PC thread!

    01-30-2018, 09:28 PM
    Ho ho ho! Scion: Origin is out, so lets make some Characters!

    (Note: Scion: Origin is the most foundational tome of the line, with Scion: Hero, Scion: Demigod and Scion: god going on top. Hence, characters made with Scion: Origin are basically Mortal+ and include Pre-visitation Scions, supernaturally empowered mortals or minor supernatural figures/supernatural figures at the beginning of their journey to divinity, like One tailed Kitsune)

    Since this is the first character, lets have a quick run through character creation.

    Step 1: Concept. - Every game has this, who is your character and what spark of life animates them?

    Hmm, something striking then, a splashy intro:

    Guo Er: Prince of the Walled City of Kowloon!


    Step 2: Paths. - You were someone before, who was that? How did you get here?

    Each path you choose allows you to pick three Abilities (by the way, this is the main source of your skills in game, so pick wisely), and depending on whether your Path is Primary, Secondary or Tertiary (denoting how important they are to you as a person), you get 3/2/1 points in the Abilities you picked (max 5 points in any ability, extra points can be put where you please)


    Origin Path: Beijing Opera Rafters Rat (Secondary)

    Abilities: Close Combat, Athletics, Persuasion (2 points in each)

    Hey, our rooftop rugrat is back.

    The little monkey, always copying our "Monkey King" rehearsals...

    Oi! Can the chatter you two, he makes the movements more accurately than you useless gossips ever could, all with no training! You shame your ancestors! Get going backstage!

    ...

    *sigh* If we had been a bit faster, we would have had you, Young Master Prodigy. But you just had to go catch the eye of the Sect Elder didn't you...


    Role Path: Rising star of the Sect (Primary)

    Abilities: Close Combat, Academics, Empathy (3 points in each)

    Fùnyìhng Everyone! Welcome to the wonders of HK! I'm your host Melissa Ying, and tonight, one of Hong Kong's unsung heroes, the Sage of the Walled City!

    For most of the week, he's Guo Er, Esquire, son of Guo Men - the influential community organizer and head of the mysterious and extensive Mendicant Brotherhood.

    A gifted child, Guo Er rose quickly through the ranks of the Brotherhood, and today he is treasurer, legal advisor and chronicler of the august charitable and patriotic organization, but two days a week, as tradition demands, he dons rags and goes out into the walled city to beg for alms, living the life of a beggar.

    Only, not being content to be almost running one of Hong Kong's oldest and most benevolent charitable bodies, Guo Er uses this chance to do even more good! Look here! A serpentine line has formed around the block to where he hands out his pro-bono legal counsel to the poor and destitute of he walled city!

    Lets just cut to the head of the queue...

    Alms for the hungry...

    Master Guo, what drives you to do this manner of charity? You could be one of the most influential men of the city under 30 if you wanted?

    Ma'am, I'm a simple beggar, no "Master" as you call me. And no offence I'd be more than happy to talk to you, and maybe get to know you better later over lunch (your treat), but Widow Lan here has already paid me in boiled eggs and I really have to resolve this "issue" she has with some loansharks, so maybe later? *wink*

    Oh... oh my. Well there you have it viewers! The future of the Mendicant Brotherhood is in good hands!... anyone around here have a pen and some paper? I've errrmm, got to write my phone number down...

      Step 4 (Step 2 and 3 were the origin paths and skills): Attributes

      There are 3 "arenas" (areas of ability) - Physical, Mental and Social

      Each arena has three "Approaches" - Force, Finesse and Resilience. How you solve problems

      How it works out in practice is like this:

      Physical Arena
      Might (Force)
      Dexterity (Finesse)
      Stamina (Resilience)

      Mental Arena
      Intellect (Force)
      Cunning (Finesse)
      Resolve (Resilience)

      Social Arena
      Presence (Force)
      Manipulation (Finesse)
      Composure (Resilience)

      Firstly all Attributes have a dot in them for free (no zero dot attributes), then you decide on your Character's main approach style, is he/she/ze forceful in everything? A cunning manipulator? Or a rock, unmoved by the waves? Your character's approach gets a bonus two dots.

      So, being the little monkey that he is, Guo Er favors finesse, so his attributes are (for now):

      Physical Arena
      Might (Force) O
      Dexterity (Finesse) OOO
      Stamina (Resilience) O

      Mental Arena
      Intellect (Force) O
      Cunning (Finesse) OOO
      Resolve (Resilience) O

      Social Arena
      Presence (Force) O
      Manipulation (Finesse) OOO
      Composure (Resilience) O

      Now you get to pick Primary (6 points) Secondary (4 points) Tertiary (2 Points) once again! Guo Er is a Social Primary, Physical Secondary, Mental Tertiary character.

      Physical Arena
      Might (Force) O
      Dexterity (Finesse) OOOOO
      Stamina (Resilience) OOO

      Having 3 points in Stamina gets you extra health levels later. 1 dot is human average. 5 is Human maximum.

      Mental Arena
      Intellect (Force) OO
      Cunning (Finesse) OOO
      Resolve (Resilience) OO

      Social Arena
      Presence (Force) OO
      Manipulation (Finesse) OOOOO
      Composure (Resilience) OOOO

      Step 5: Calling and Knacks

      This is basically a declaration of what kind of character you're going to be. Your superpowers (such as they are at the Origin level) come from here, and if you are a Pre-visitation Scion, you have to pick one that matches your Sire's character. (Loki gets trickster).

      Being a Scion of XXX XXXXXX of the Shen (its obvious, but a no prize for those of you who guess), Guo Er can pick from the Trickster (self evident), Liminal (you stand at the boundary of all things) and Warrior (your calling is conflict) Callings.

      Being a sort that is at the nexus of things, Guo Er choose the Liminal Calling. He will be the One who Stands in the Gap.

      He gets to pick three Knacks! (Supernatural Powers)

      Note: I know you should only get 1, but you get to pick up 2 later (or your Birthright items which we don't have any idea about so I picked Knacks).

      So I picked:

      Flatlander: In combat you get to do the following if you roll well enough:

      Glimpse the other Side - Send foes... Elsewhere... for a turn. Where? gods only know.
      Stutter Time - Opponents act in a turn slower.
      Bend Space - Reduce the Range of a target (Stopping bullets matrix style!)


      Neither the Minute or the Hour: Make a dire pronouncement (that can be complete Blarney if you want) about a target's death, your target suffers a +1 difficulty to all actions for 1 scene

      Unobtrusive Visitor: Wherever you are, you belong there, and it is right that you are there, listening to everything everyone says.

      Step 6: Odds and Ends.


      You also get an extra Attribute Point and 5 extra Skill points, I chose these:

      Physical Arena
      Might (Force) O
      Dexterity (Finesse) OOOOO
      Stamina (Resilience) OOO

      Mental Arena
      Intellect (Force) OO
      Cunning (Finesse) OOO
      Resolve (Resilience) OO

      Social Arena
      Presence (Force) OO
      Manipulation (Finesse) OOOOO
      Composure (Resilience) OOOOO (Extra point went here, Defence in combat is apparently based on Resilience Attributes, so I thought it a good investment)

      Skills
      Close Combat OOOOO (Dog Beating Staff Technique)
      Academics OOOOO (Law)
      Persuasion OOOOO (Lets make a deal)
      Empathy OOO (Knowing Exactly what people want)
      Athletics OOO (Footraces)
      Leadership O
      Subterfuge O

      Knacks
      Flatlander
      Unobtrusive visitor
      Neither the minute nor the hour

      And phew, Guo Er is done! Next maybe I'll do a non Pre-visitation Scion character.

      Things to ponder:

      1. How do you decide what gear you have?
      2. Can you close combat with Dexterity? (Most people say yes, some on the Onyx Path say no)
      3. I suppose you can just nick the Knacks of the supernatural creatures?
      4. Did I get everything right?
