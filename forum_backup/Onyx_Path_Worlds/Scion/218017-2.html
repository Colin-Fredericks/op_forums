            This vague relationship between mortal man and the gods/titans is one of the things that needed to be better thought about in this game series. I totally agree with Telgar and Ajax; god created man so god could not possibly NEED man for anything, yet for god to bother creating man there has to be some kind of side-benefit that makes it worth the trouble of creating an intelligent species that can screw up the environment around it. I created a mechanic on my last home game that granted a bonus to PCs who had some kind of active cult dedicated to their worship, since otherwise mankind as a whole was largely an obstacle/annoyance. But it should be noted that this mechanic isn't absolutely necessary and shouldn't be. Having a bonus is great but the gods should be able to stand on their own power.

            Incidentally the supplement "We are but Mere Mortals" from biblioteque interdite further muddies the issue further by suggesting that mankind is a spontaneous outbreak from the early days when the titans ruled over nature and the gods did not exist. It describes the initial worship of vague elemental powers (i.e. titans) who ultimately did not care whatsoever as long as man knew his place.

                    The issue with having mortals granting mechanical benefit to gods can be summed up in one word: Devas. When you have one pantheon with close to a billion active worshippers, orders of magnitude above most, if not all, of the others (more, even, than their likely historical peaks in terms of sheer number of worshippers, but I may be wrong there), you can create major dysfunction in terms of your game balance. Suddenly making a Deva character becomes the mechanically best choice.

                    I'm not saying that having mortal worship have an effect on the gods is a bad thing. It isn't. But in order to both conform to the state of reality and also create a fun gaming experience, it has to be treated carefully. I'll be the first to admit that I don't know what the right choice about this is.

                    Games are a way for us to explore what it means to be human, or inhuman, as the case may be. Let's all have a good time playing them.
