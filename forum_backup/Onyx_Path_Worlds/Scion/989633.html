                                                  The core 12 I'm inclined to go with at my table (with Ma'at as a distant but honored primordial figure rather than a ruler)

                                                  Re (with his primary Mantles being the scarab Khepri for the dawn, Atum-Re the Creator for noonday sun, Khnum the shaper for dusk, and the unseen Amun-Re at night)

                                                  Azar

                                                  Aset (she is not petty, she is however Machievellian, and is the political shadow behind the thrones of Re, Azar, and Heru simultaneously)

                                                  Sutekh

                                                  Heru

                                                      For religions, I'd include more specific cults rather than the broad brush of Kemeticism.

                                                      Cult of Isis is most widespread, related closely to the Eleusinian Mysteries, with the promise of the blessed afterlife in Aaru and built around the story of Aset's resurrection of Azar.

                                                      Cult of Re is more Egypt-centric, and honors Sutekh much more than the Cult of Isis that tends to make him more of a satan figure - this religion is much more focused on the conflict between Re and Apep as the center of their faith.

                                                      Finally the Cult of the Hehu (Ogdoad) are esoteric fringe weirdos in the eyes of the mainstream Netjer worshipers, but Djheuty himself finds the subject matter of arcane studies a matter of great interest.
