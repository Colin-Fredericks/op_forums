                                              I feel like a line should be drawn between works of fiction being written in modern times that are based on old myths but play with the assumptions those myths rely on and actual mythology as it stands from a scholastic/secular(?) perspective While modern works of fiction make for great inspiration for a game, the corebook doesn't seem like the place to use them, since the corebook is supposed to be more universal.

                                              The Minotaur was the product of Zeus and one of his many lovers, whom he seduced while in the form of a bull. This lady was a princess, and her father locked him (the minotaur) in the Labyrinth because it was a threat to the people / because he was ashamed of what his daughter had given birth to, depending on the tale. So while creating a bull-headed scion to run in your game would be cool, and it would be awesome if things like that were nodded to in the corebook, actually having the Minotaur as a god from which to play Scions would just be kind of odd, especially given as he only appears, really, in that one legend.

                                              On the topic of Cerberus, again, though -- since I can't find any evidence of that story anywhere, I'm going to go out on a limb here and presume it was pretty localized, or even fictional. I have found a series of books called "the Wrath of Kerberus", but that doesn't seem to be what you're referring to be any stretch, and if I remember correctly, the way I hear it, Cerberus has been in Hades' care since he was born, being a terrible hound who's spit created wolfsbane; he was taken to the underworld where there wasn't anyone to inadvertently kill because everyone was already dead. However, again, this story could make for interesting campaign fodder -- perhaps he escapes the underworld for some reason (even well-behaved dogs get mischievous sometimes, right?), and meets a young girl who can talk to animals; meanwhile, no one's there to stop the souls of the dead from escaping. . . . and Cerberus proves himself worthy of godhood when he rounds up the dead he's let escape and brings them back to the underworld, returning to a lonely life out of duty or tragedy.

                                              However, while it would make a good story, it would not make for a very good core book.

                                              Pan and Eris, however, are both very good candidates for possible deities, though they'd be lower Legend.
