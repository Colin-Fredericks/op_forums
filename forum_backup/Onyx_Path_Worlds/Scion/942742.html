    Home Brewed Titans

    08-03-2016, 12:00 PM
    So I've been wanting to create my own batch of Titans / Avatars for a while, but was afraid that my lack of extensive knowledge on mythology would get in the way of that. But, I finally decided to just go ahead and give it a shot by showing off some Avatar ideas I came up with. Starting with The Dark Emperor, a Chinese avatar of Tusko-no-Kumi


    ----------------------------------------------


    The Dark Emperor: Was not always the way he is now known. Once, he was a Scion worthy of praise, a direct child of Guan Yu who managed to achieve divinity and became known throughout the Chinese pantheon as a God of Order and Stability. Perhaps, he would have gone on to do greater things, had he not accepted a missive to launch an attack on Soku-no-Kumi, the Greater Titan of Darkness.

    Assembling a team of lesser deities, The Harmonious Prince (as he was known at the time) infiltrated the Titan with intentions of subduing Huehueteotl. Sadly, they were unprepared for the journey and suffered many casualties just trying to even reach the avatar of blindness. The Harmonious Prince lost the last of his subordinates in the ensuing battle, along with his senses as the Titan's crows feasted upon his eyes. But rather than kill the petulant God, he chose to abandon him to fumble around in the dark.

    A normal victim would have died under such conditions, but The Harmonious Prince was a God, and a powerful one at that. For weeks, he fumbled around, desperately trying to find a way out of the darkness, until Soku-no-Kumi finally consumed him, assimilating the God into it's being. Rather than perish to the Titan's hunger, he was reborn as The Dark Emperor, an Avatar of Uncertainty.

    If Huehueteotl represents the physical loss of one's senses, then The Dark Emperor represents their metaphysical loss. He is the emotional darkness that brings despair and confusion to our lives. His long term goal is to corrupt all nations of the world, starting with China, into a state of civil unrest. A world where he rules the people through tyrannical brute force, rather than structured order. In this world, the common people will grow fearful and distrustful of their government. Greed will overcome charity, and the human race will favor the dark virtues of the Titans over those of the Gods.

    Like Erebus, The Dark Emperor is no friend of Mikaboshi. His dream of ruling humanity directly conflicts with Mikaboshi's dream of destroying it. Unlike Erebus, however, The Dark Emperor did not simply lay down and allow his fellow Titan to imprison him. When Mikaboshi sent his assassins to deal with the upstart Avatar, The Dark Emperor subsumed their minds and their loyalty, turning them into his own agents. Since then, Mikaboshi has kept The Dark Emperor under 24 hour surveillance for any sign of a counter attack.

    In appearance, The Dark Emperor takes the form of a strongly built man of Chinese heritage. His hair is jet black and grows up to three feet long, but he likes to keep it bundled up to appear shorter. His facial hair is a Fu Man Chu, and his clothing is a set of traditional royal robes. Most of the robes are as black as his hair, but the stylized image of a jade dragon is wrapped around the design. Starting at his ankles and finally coming to rest on his left shoulder. The purpose of this brilliantly colored dragon is both to mock the Celestial Bureaucracy's current government, and to remind The Dark Emperor of the God he once was.


    Despite the failed assassination plot against him, The Dark Emperor is not so arrogant as to believe he is invincible. He knows that in a full on conflict, Mikaboshi and Huehueteotl would most likely destroy him. So to overcome this, The Dark Emperor has made some alliances of his own. More specifically, he's made alliances with other Titans, giving him potential resources that the rest of Tsuko-no-Kumi's Avatars do not possess! At present, The Dark Emperor has healthy relations with the Avatars Ran, Prometheus and Jord.

    -------------------------------------

    Creatures: Among his servants, The Dark Emperor has stolen some of Mikaboshi's shinobi agents. Having corrupted their loyalty, The Dark Emperor utilizes these silent killers to dispose of political figures in society, resulting in the spread of panic and ignorance. He also commands Nemean beasts that terrorize the outskirts of society, keeping people isolated from the outside world and causing society to stagnate as a result.

    But his most favorite pet is one of his own creation. The Dark Imperial Dragon, as he has taken to naming it, serves as The Dark Emperor's mascot, and is used for the purpose of confronting Gods, should they be encountered in battle. It is an enormous, serpentine dragon measuring a mile in length. The dragon has jade colored scales across it's primary body, while the dragon's underside is as dark as the shadows that have corrupted its master. It possesses the ability to speak human languages, but rarely chooses to do so when it is given the order to kill.

    The Dark Imperial Dragon has the same traits as the Great Coatl, but replaces it's normal Boon selection with the following assortment: All Darkness Boons of Legend 10 and lower, Sky's Grace, Wind's Freedom, Eye of the Storm, Hornet's Nest, Paralyzing Confusion.

    The weakest of The Dark Emperor's servants are his Dark Imperial Army. Although these soldiers only have traits comparable to mortal Mercenaries, there are thousands of them patrolling his territory, allowing them to make up for in numbers what they lack in manpower.


    ---------------------------------------------

    The Dark Empire: After being absorbed by Soku-no-Kumi, The Dark Emperor began to carve out his own territory within the Titan. The so called Dark Empire is a shadowy kingdom which the Titan rules with an iron fist. It serves as the epitome of a corrupt and ruthless government, just like the one the emperor plans for earth. The inhabitants of the city are kept in a perpetual state of ignorance, having access only to whatever knowledge that The Dark Emperor chooses to share with them. Towering walls surround the empire's borders, which are patrolled at all times by servants of The Dark Emperor. Anybody who does not have proper authorization to pass through is to be killed on sight, regardless of which side of the war they stand on. This extreme security measure wards against raids by the Gods, as well as subterfuge from his fellow Avatars.

    In its present form, The Dark Empire spans 1,000 square miles in size. But as with all empires, it greedily seeks to expand its territory, usually doing so with a display of force. Since the empire was founded, The Dark Emperor has been conducting the occasional skirmishes on "foreign soil" to assimilate more land and resources into his growing empire. An action which infuriates the formless Mikaboshi to no end.

    At the heart of the empire is the Imperial Citadel, where The Dark Emperor maintains his court. The citadel itself is broken up into three districts, with each district being separated by a wall. The outer most district is the peasant's district, where the working class reside. It is perhaps the largest district, spanning a length of 100 square miles. It is also considered barely more than a slum, as the peasant folk live in squalor, scraping to get by on rations and poverty. Crimes of theft, destruction of property and sexual assault usually go unreported in the peasant's district. Mostly due to perpetrators responsible being the very soldiers assigned to keep the peace!

    The middle district is the military district, where the bulk of The Dark Emperor's army resides. It measures 50 square miles in size, giving plenty of room for The Dark Emperor to train his troops for war. From here, they are able to quickly access the other two districts in case there is trouble. The military district is better maintained than the peasant district, but it is a very hostile environment. Soldiers heavily abuse whatever authority they possess over those found wandering through the district, with higher ranking officers being the cruelest of the bunch.

    The inner most district is the noble's district, where The Dark Emperor and his most valuable allies reside in the finest luxury the empire can afford. It is the smallest of the city districts, measuring only 10 square miles in size, but nearly all of the empire's financial resources goes to keeping the morally bankrupt nobles happy. This district is also patrolled by The Dark Emperor's most elite troops, such as his beloved Dark Imperial Dragon. Commoners are forbidden to step foot in this district unless they are performing work for the nobles. Those caught trespassing are dragged through the city streets and publicly executed for their insolence.


    ---------------------------------------

    The Pit of Despair: although The Dark Emperor has many ways in which to deal with his enemies, there exists one form of punishment that he favors when he wants his enemy to suffer, rather than die. The Pit of Despair is a sub-realm within The Dark Empire that contains an allegedly bottomless pit. The pit serves as an allegory for depression, as its victims plunge deeper into darkness, while climbing back out is both long and difficult.

    Contrary to popular opinion, however, the pit isn't as bottomless as The Dark Emperor would like people to think. Approximately 10 miles down into the pit is an Axis Mundi through which victims enter The Drowned Road. More specifically, it deposits its victims in the avatar Ran's territory, where they are quickly drowned to death and added to her list of zombie slaves.

    The primary use of the Pit of Despair is to maintain peaceful relations with Ran by offering her tribute for her services. The secondary use of the pit is to allow Ran to send undead reinforcements to aid The Dark Emperor's troops. However, the emperor has neglected to call upon this side of the contract. He prefers to keep the pit as a trump card for the day when his citadel is in danger of falling to the enemy outside the gates. When that day comes, he will unleash a horde of fearless, zombie troops to devour the enemy forces.

    The Pit of Despair typically has no guards. In fact, the pit is usually left alone, unless The Dark Emperor is planning to throw somebody into it. While this means that the Pit of Despair is a potentially unguarded Axis Mundi, the fact that it is connected with The Drowned Road means that few enemies would consider using it as a means to infiltrate the empire.


    ------------------------------------------


    The Sweat Shops: another sub-realm of the empire dedicated to maintaining The Dark Emperor's alliances is known as the Sweat Shops. It is here that commoners and peasant folk are forced to conduct manual labor for the glory of the emperor. The influence of Prometheus drives the empire's citizens to devote what little energy they have to manufacturing the resources and materials needed to keep the army strong and the nobility comfortable.

    Minimum wage is worthy of its namesake; entry level workers make barely enough money an hour to pay for a single meal, forcing them to conserve their money for only the absolute essentials. The Sweat Shops reward hard working obedience, and punish signs of laziness or flaws. As a result, the workers are willing to stab each other in the back for the chance at a better position, where they can receive better pay and living arrangements.

    Like the Pit of Despair, The Sweat Shops also serve as an Axis Mundi through which to access one of The Dark Emperor's Titan allies. The flames that burn in the empire's factories allow passage into Muspelheim by spending a point of Legend and climbing inside. However, travelers will obviously need some form of protection in order to survive the trip unharmed. The threat of burning to death also keeps the disgruntled workers from using the Axis Mundi to escape The Dark Empire. The purpose of the flames is to allow Prometheus' influence to seep into the Sweat Shops, and keep the workers active.

    -------------------------------------------

    The Brothel Houses: yet another sub-realm of the empire that is dedicated to maintaining The Dark Emperor's alliance with his fellow Titans, the Brothel Houses are conducted with help from Jord's influence. Empires thrive on expansion, and expansion requires people.

    To meet this requirement, The Dark Emperor has constructed the Brothel Houses, where imperial citizens go to have sex. The influence of Jord seeps into the brothel's environment, greatly increasing the lust of those who venture here. Every child conceived in the Brothel Houses means another soldier or laborer for the empire to use.

    While Jord's influence keeps the brothel's workers in a constant state of arousal, most of them are not willing participants. After all, they are called the Brothel Houses, which means the circumstances for their being here are not pretty. Some of the workers are convicted criminals who were sentenced to work in the brothels as punishment for their crimes. Others were once free men and women who were abducted from their previous lives and sold into slavery.

    Unlike the last two sub-realms, the Brothel Houses do not have an Axis Mundi through which to travel into Jord's realm. Instead, Jord has charitably donated a large number of relic fertility statues that allow her sexual influence to permeate the environment. The Dark Emperor has carefully placed these statues all over the sub-realm so that inhabitants are never more than 20 feet away from one, making it almost impossible to escape their corrupting influence.

    While the Brothel Houses do have a regiment of guards to ensure the workers keep doing their jobs, the biggest threat that visitors face is an attack on their senses, rather than their bodies. The fertility statues that span the Brothel Houses are relics carved by Jord, herself and infused with her power. Merely standing in their presence incites feelings of lust in those who lack the strength to resist their compulsion. Should they give into its power, victims will feel compelled to satisfy themselves at the earliest possible convenience. The statues also guarantee that unprotected sex will result in pregnancy for women who are not already with child.

    Resisting the power of Jord's statues requires a successful Legend + Willpower roll at a difficulty of 8, and this roll must be performed every hour one spends inside the Brothel Houses. Gods and high level Demigods shouldn't have too much to worry about, but mortals and lesser immortals are almost assured to give into compulsion. The guards who patrol the Brothel Houses are considered immune to the statue's charms, allowing them to maintain a presence without being compromised.

              Gloria: the Titan of Light is not known for its over abundance of Avatars. The tyrannical Aten makes a point to destroy his rivals before they gain enough momentum to become a threat. But this new Avatar is more clever than his former rivals and has started using non violent protests as a means of converting worshipers to her cause!

              Gloria, otherwise known as The Light of Purity is a Greco-Roman Avatar of serenity and innocence. To a casual inspection, she appears to be a completely benign avatar who shows no interest in forcefully conquering anybody. But a more in depth exploration will realize that she poses a threat in her own way. Gloria's worshipers are every bit as loyal to her as Aten's are to his. Probably even more so, because she allows them to retain the illusion of free will!

              The prismatic light that radiates off Gloria is not the retina searing flare of Aten. It is a gentle, soothing light that calms the mind and warms the body. It is a compassionate light that offers to ease one's troubles if they would only open themselves up to her. Those who become exposed to this light, develop feelings of plutonic love and deep respect for Gloria, while keeping their personalities intact.

              In the past, the Gods either couldn't or wouldn't interfere with Aten's schemes to destroy his newborn rivals. But now they face a moral quandry; Gloria is still twisting the minds of her followers to her own ends, but at the same time, she is an Avatar of peace and love. The consequences that her death would have upon the World are unknown, but sure to be catastrophic for humanity.

              Realizing this, the Gods chose the only option they felt they had, they offered Gloria an alliance. If she agrees to keep them posted about Aten's movements, they will do what they can to protect her from his wrath. It is an alliance that has so far born fruit, as the Pesedjet have been making great progress in repelling Aten's troops from their borders. The terms of this agreement make no mention of what is to be done about Gloria after the Gods win the war, however, and some believe that Gloria will find herself locked up with Aten once the Gods have no more use for her.

              Gloria takes the form of a young, innocent woman in her early 20's who manages to appear beautiful without being sexualized. Her shoulder length, curly hair is as golden as the sun's rays, while her skin is flawlessly smooth and soft. Her eyes shine like tiny, blue stars and her voice is friendly and welcoming. She garbs herself in modest greco-roman dresses, and her body shimmers with a rainbow hued silhouette of light.

              Gloria is almost always in state of peaceful tranquility. It is rare for her to get upset with others, and when she finally does, she is passive aggressive about it. Her worshipers, on the other hand, may be less forgiving towards one who went to such great lengths to offend their mistress. Of course, they would never be so violent as to attack somebody in her presence, that would go against everything Gloria stands for! No, they prefer to wait until she isn't looking before carrying out their revenge, telling themselves that they are protecting their mistress' purity by acting where she can not.

              Scholars of Judeo Christian religions have noticed a stark comparison between the rise of Gloria and the authority of Aten in regards to the biblical depiction of God. Namely, Aten appears similar to the old testament, which depicts the Lord as a merciless tyrant. While Gloria appears similar to the new testament version in which the Lord is more compassionate and forgiving.

              Attempts to bring up this correlation in Aten's presence, result in the immediate detonation of any servant foolish enough to insinuate he's being replaced!

              ----------------------------------------------------------

              Creatures: Those who follow Gloria are every bit as loyal to her as Aten's brainwashed soldiers are to him. The difference is that they don't understand they've been brainwashed, and believe they follow her of their own free will.

              Many of the Titan's followers are former servants of Aten who have been converted to The Light of Purity after she cleansed his cruel brainwashing from their minds. Happy that they will no longer explode on command, they have come to see Gloria as a preferable alternative, and have chosen to rebel against Aten's tyranny.

              More unique servants of Gloria include the Serene Monks, who serve as her personal attendants and bodyguards. While normally a peaceful sort, each individual Monk is as dangerous as a Valkyrie when physically confronted, and carries at least one relic that allows them to channel the Sun Purview as a gift from their mistress. Gloria never goes anywhere in public without at least ten Serene Monks within shouting distance of her location, ready to come whenever she needs them to.

              The Light of Purity also has access to her own spy network of "Little Birds" who keep her informed about everything that goes on in her territory. These creatures are roughly the size of humming birds and have brilliant, rainbow hued feathers. Their sing-song chirping is actually a coded language that even Scions who have the ability to communicate with birds would be unable to translate properly. Only Gloria has the knowledge of how to do so, making it impossible to decrypt without her guidance. Aside from being able to communicate with their mistress, Little Birds have the same traits as typical song birds, and can be destroyed with minimal effort even by mortal attempts. Doing so, however, is certain to attract Gloria's attention.

              Gloria's mortal cult is comprised of non violent protestors, missionaries, hippies and humanitarians that seek to bring others into her service without bloodshed or threats. The Light of Purity has given her religious leaders a spell that can channel her enchanting light into their shrines, converting the hearts and minds of those who gather there. The light does not remain in the shrine for very long, which is why the ritual is used for the conversion of new members, instead of keeping it active 24 hours a day.


              But above all, Gloria's most powerful servant is her typhonian gorilla, Light Bringer, who's name is engraved upon a gold plated collar worn around his neck. Large enough to make King Kong feel inadequate, Light Bringer is capable of exchanging blows (as well as wits) against even high ranking Gods and Titanspawn. Light Bringer is the only known servant that Gloria openly permits to engage in violence and rumors persist that he may actually be a conduit through which Gloria channels her more destructive powers.

              Light Bringer is a Gorilla with the Typhonian template and 11 Legend. In addition to his physical enhancements, Gloria has also endowed him with 10 levels of Epic Intelligence. This makes him immensely more cunning than standard typhons, and great care should be taken by those who insist on fighting him. Lastly, Gloria has granted Light Bringer access to all Sun Boons of Legend 10 and lower, which he is capable of channeling through the engraved collar worn about his neck.

              It is unknown what consequences Light Bringer's death would have on Gloria, but the general consensus is that the loss of such a conduit will either sever her destructive capabilities, or will allow Gloria to wield them directly. It is a gamble that the Gods have chosen not to risk at this time, and so they leave Light Bringer alone until they have a more clear understanding of the outcome.


              ----------------------------------------------

              Gardens of Paradise: Although it took some effort, Gloria has managed to establish a base of operations in the sun scorched realm of Akhetaten. Instead of being engulfed in the relentless light of Aten, the Gardens of Paradise are illuminated by the gentle glow of Gloria's radiance. As such, it is one of the few places in Akhetaten which visitors can safely function without eye protection. The realm spans a length of roughly 1,400 miles in radius and is completely surrounded by a near impenetrable wall of protective hedges.

              True to their name, the Gardens are a paradise, where food grows in abundance, water is purified, the animals are docile, and the weather is always perfectly comfortable. Within the boundaries of the Gardens of Paradise, Gloria's followers have want for nothing; all their daily needs are met.

              Upon their construction, Gloria had the Gardens of Paradise blessed with some of the most powerful Health Boons she could find. As long as they remain inside the boundaries of the Gardens, Gloria's servants are functionally immortal. Neither disease nor age may affect them, their wounds heal almost instantly, and they are incapable of feeling pain. All of these protections vanish upon stepping outside the Gardens, however, which makes the otherwise perfect paradise into a gilded cage.

              Of course, these protections also mean that Gloria is invincible when confronted inside her own realm. Those who wish to do battle against her forces (or even Gloria herself) must either convince them to step outside the Gardens, or force them to come outside. Gloria is fully aware of this near invincibility, and leaves the confines of her Gardens only rarely. It is one reason why she invented the spell she gave to her cultists, so that she could convert new worshipers without having to leave her protective environment.

              There is only one publicly known entrance to the Gardens of Paradise, which is a marble archway surrounded on both sides by a hedge wall. This archway is guarded by Light Bringer, who's typhonian size makes him tall enough to spot enemies over the horizon, and who's divine intelligence gives him more than enough time to prepare for their arrival.

              The secrets entrances to the Gardens of Paradise are a series of Axis Mundi that are known only to those who have accepted Gloria's light into their hearts, or have extracted the knowledge through forceful interrogation. Gloria's followers would never voluntarily surrender that information, for fear of putting their mistress' security at risk.

              The Gardens of Paradise can be accessed through the use of any hedge maze garden scattered throughout the World. The user merely has to travel through the garden while spending a point of legend and meditating upon the purity of Gloria's light. Instead of stepping out of the garden's normal exit, they will find themselves deposited inside the Gardens of Paradise. Only a true convert of Gloria would think to perform this ritual, and so the Axis Mundi is largely unguarded. However, the Worldly owners of such mazes have been known to put up their own security measures (or tests of character) in order to keep unwanted visitors off their property.
