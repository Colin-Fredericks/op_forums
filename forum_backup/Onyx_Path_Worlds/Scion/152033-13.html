                    Your fake "Coyote"? That's the Lakota "Coyote" - Mika, the Aztec "Coyote" - Huehuecoyotl, the Hopi "Coyote" - Istaqa, the Navajo "Coyote - Attse0Haske, the Crow "Coyote" - Abka-Atadia, etc., etc. ETC. all mashed together and they are NOT the same guy (or, in some cases, gal).                     There is pandering and there is pandering. Pandering to the lowest common denominator is actually fine. Sad. But fine.

                    Pandering to culturally damaging stereotypes is completely different. Witness the difference between the way people are treating the debate over the use of the name "Redskins" for a football team. Witness the fact that, even though Native Americans have a legitimate case for objecting to other Native American themed team names (Chiefs, Braves, Seminoles, Etc.), there is not nearly as much support for it? I'm not saying what you are doing is tantamount to using the term "Redskin" to refer to a football team (Which IS as bad as people are saying it is. It's not being over-sensitive. If I suggested using the equivalent terms for gay people, Black people, Jewish people, etc. as team names, I would, quite rightly, be banned from this board.) but it definitely falls into a lesser degree of the same phenomenon.

                    Why do it?

                                    I do want to comment here for a sec. The "Senate" of the Native North American Gods is not a Pantheon in Butterflies and Hurricanes. It is an organisation of Pantheons. They don't have a single PsP, they are not a single Pantheon. All they are is a way in which the hundreds of Pantheons can be interacted with. There are similar organisations for the Pantheons of North Africa and the Middle East, and an additional one of the lands the Theoi have conquered recently.

                                    So, it's not a single Pantheon by any stretch. Just as the Grecian Confederacy is not a single Pantheon (Greeks, Irish [Long story], Not-Slaughtered Gauls (Very few), Romans, Etruscans [Present in theory], Iberians [Again, in theory], and many others). Not all Native North American Pantheons are involved in the "Senate", for example, the Inuit have refused to cooperate with anyone, and the various Cree Gods are keeping an eye on the Eastern Northern border for the Greeks (Native "Senate" is in bed with the Greeks as a series of Client Kingdoms due to truly colossal social rolls).

                                    Sorry for the confusion. But, just wanted to clarify it. It is a Pan-Pantheon Senate, while the Greeks have a Trans-Pantheon Kingdom, and the North Africans + Middle East has an alliance. They are are individual Pantheons, distinct, and possessing their own PsPs. There are multiple "Coyote's" (Who are not all named Coyote) just as there are many "Lughs" (Not all named Lugh), and so on.
