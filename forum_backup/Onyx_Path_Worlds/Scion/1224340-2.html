        Originally posted by Achilles View Post
        I have a question about things like how do you deal with things that Art, Crafts, Performance and Expression used to handle? I've looked and I can't find anything for what roll you would use to dance, cook a meal, paint a masterpiece, create a song, play an instrument.

        I see in both Academics and Culture that you are exposed to these things and may even study them but being an art critic isn't the same as being able make art.
        Things like that are handled through the crafting system and your Paths... It's designed to mostly do away with having to roll things to make stuff... If you have the appropriate Path to paint, and the Skill to represent your knowledge (Culture for formal techniques, Persuasion or Empathy for more free spirited work, Integrity for meditative pieces) you can make the art. The things you actually need to worry about are things like procuring the assets, booking a stage... Etc, that is, things that actually cause Things to happen in game, instead of having your crafter be holed up in his studio painting while others are having adventures, as such tends to not be the best activity in a cooperative game.

                                                Okay, let's take a Kitsune as an example. At Origin-Tier, you have space for one Knack, which can be a Kitsune Knack.

                                                If they awaken to their Legend, through deeds or through a Visitation, they have the option to instead of one of 11 standard Callings, this Kitsune can replace one of their three Callings with an effective Kitsune Calling, which can be leveled and ranked like other Callings, and the Knack Slots this Kitsune Calling provides can be used for Kitsune Knacks.

                                                Also, on the Hero book it says that the Denizen dont have boons for free and need to use Marvels to use any power. Does that means that they begin with boon points but still can buy with exp or they cant access it? If I'm right, dont it make them vastly weaker than their Scion counterparts?
                                                Now I believe it works like this... If a Denizen gains Legend through means other than a Visitation, just growing as a Satyr or a Naga or what have you, then they effectively only have the one Calling, that matches their type, ranked at 5. I believe they can also pick one logical Purview to have access to... But they don't get Boons, and instead use their Legend to power Marvels.

                                                Now a thing to note here: You can't buy Boons in 2e. The number is fixed.

                                                And that will make many Denizens somewhat weaker than Scions. They're still viable and many can do specific tricks, but you can also be a Denizen Scion if you're worried about power. Inari, for instance, is the patron of all Kitsune, A Naga may get Chosen by one of the Deva, and I imagine there's a fair number of Amazon Scions who get Chosen by Athena or Artemis.

                                                Hell, there's a sidebar mentioning that the world is becoming increasingly mixed, culturally. A Satyr can become a Scion of a member of the Kami, or the Shen could draft a werewolf.
