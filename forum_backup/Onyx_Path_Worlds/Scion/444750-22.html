        Fun, okay, I like this idea! Let's see...

        The Etruscans pretty clearly had some very different ideas of Divinity to the Latins to the south of them. The idea of Deities who are less Anthropomorphic, and the Haruspication are really interesting, but we just know so absolute little about them due to having limited written records, and then not having the ability to translate all of their language adding to the problems.

        I am going to make a single explanation for various Celtic groups. I would love to have a flourishing collection of Celtic Pantheons, but, we have scant to no records for most of them. The Manx, the Picts, the Cornish, and the Iberians are all not really capable of being a Pantheon, we just know next to nothing about what their religious systems, or sometimes anything about them anymore. Also, while I love the Celts, it would add to the Eurocentric bloat.

        The Basque Pantheon interests me as a non-PIE European culture. However, things are so incredibly spotty that I am unsure they could really be written up. Since we know nothing about them, their Pantheon plot would almost surely get tied up arguing with the Proto-Indo-European peoples around them, and for a reason I am about to get into, I think avoiding that topic would save an enormous amount of headache. Also more Eurocentric bloat.

        The Proto-Indo-European Pantheon is something I have seen come up very, very rarely. We know pretty much nothing about them, so including them would be almost entirely fiction to start with which isn't really up my personal alley. However, for everyone else, the PIE Pantheon would be an utter mess for everything. It would start getting into the nitty gritty of where a ton of Pantheons have their Genesis from, gets into the tricky realm of how Gods come to be, starts to challenge the All-Myths-Are-True system in a weird way. I would avoid proto-Pantheons like a plague just because they cause so many issues, even just by acknowledging they exist. They just... complicate everything by their very existence. Further, it would add to the Eurocentric bloat, at least in part. And furthermore would probably get into some metaplot mainly involving their progeny Pantheons, which with the existing Eurocentric bloat means that it would involve a lot of European Pantheons at the exclusion of most of the world with PIE cultures.

            I would love to see some form of Slavic Pantheon as that is a very large region of the world unrepresented [Plus fuck I'd like to see what twisted shit would come out of whoever made Baba Yaga without that stupid ass "Nationality" bit to her]

            With the Polynesian Pantheon yes it is true that each group have their on distinctive narrative, but each of the regions all form from the same group. Just as with how Greece and Rome view the gods different [Rome saw Mars as a Strategic Military Genius whereas in Greece he was a Savage Brute who lusted for nothing more than battlefield carnage] they have enough similarities that they can be unified under a single pantheon. Will it be perfect? No not really. What it will do is save us from far to much redundancy in the aspectst that are similar. Something has been said [don't ask where, I forget] about gods who take on various persona's, such as The Morrigan who can either be 3 faces of the same god, or 3 Goddesses all called The Morrigan. They would all be the same gods, just varying by how each group views them in their narrrative.

            If we're going to narrow it down to a single Native American group for which to have a Pantheon than i would suggest either the Navajo or Cherokee as they were the two most numerous tribes in the United States.
