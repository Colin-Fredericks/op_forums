                                        I think we might be disagreeing on exactly what the legend difference means in terms of raw power. I totally agree that Odin's personality and suite of influence lends itself to playing the political game and functioning as a Germanic King, even If Thor might kick his ass in a white room brawl (Odin is more about leading troops than being personally a badass warrior) or that Freyr might out talk him and have more straight respect and admiration, Odin has the ability to beat them in different ways and the will to do so. Odin is basically the God of the Xanatos Gambit.

                                        My argument is that this is more a matter of characterization and attribute choices than it is a true measure of raw power. Thor, Freyr and Odin are each the most prominent and powerful examples of the things they each specialize in (Roughly Physical, Social and Mental). That's why all three should be legend 12, they are comparable to each other even if in the situation of "Who gets to lead the pantheon" Odin ends up winning. There is much more of a power differential between say, Freyr and Freya (Freyr is canonically more powerful even though they are basically divine twins and share almost entirely the same suite of powers and influence) or Thor and Tyr (Thor just wins). Putting the Triumvirate at legend 12 establishes them as roughly of equal levels of power and the others as being roughly less powerful overall. (Not to mention opening some very interesting possible stories and internal tension).

                                        Putting Thor and Freyr at Legend 11 puts them both at roughly the same power level as say Tyr or Heimdall, when both are quite superior and more comparable to Odin in what they actually do do.

                                                            Originally posted by marin View Post

                                                            Will any of the pantheons be getting a name change like the Theoi?
                                                            Yes. Chinese, Egyptian, Aztec, the Loa (kinda-sorta), to name a few.

                                                            I don't think anyone else is changing their name, and there are other names and groupings within there. The Dodekatheon, for example, is now more properly the twelve greatest gods of Olympus proper (which the book will detail, +1 for Hades, and maybe +another for Persephone, since those two never visit).

                                                            Lots of pantheons have specifically-named groupings of three with mythical significance, or sub-pantheons and groupings contained within them. The Theoi up there contain two Triads, the (Greek names ahead) Olympioi, the Halioi, the Khthonioi, and associated sub-groupings. That's not accounting for allied Titans, where the politics get interesting.
