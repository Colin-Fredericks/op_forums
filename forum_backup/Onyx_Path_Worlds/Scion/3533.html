              Originally posted by Egg View Post
              Can Scions of Vodoun be other ethnicities than African?
              Definitely. Especially if we go with the pantheon of Vodoun and not the original African religions. New Orleans alone has thousands of people from many differing ethnic backgrounds who follow the beliefs of Vodoun. Scion has no rules against the culture or heritage of a character. It would be a little easier to explain background-wise, but if you've got a good reason as to say ... a girl from Alaska is Visited as a child of the Baron in New Orleans, go for it.

              Originally posted by Egg View Post
              Can Scions of Amatsukami be other ethnicities than Japanese?
              Same answer as above, but with a twist. The Japanese are very xenophobic. One can only assume this view on outside cultures and races mirrored in their gods. While I see no problem in a scion being sired on, say, an American military wife in Okinawa; that child would be considered at best a half-breed in the gods' eyes and might face a lot of hardships being recognized and gaining status within the pantheon itself.

                  Most pantheons are at least somewhat xenophobic, so it's far more likely that the Scions in question are going to be similar in ethnicity to the peoples who worshipped them. That being said, as Aynie points out, there's no reason a Scion of a pantheon CAN'T be of a different ethnicity, but doing so is going to probably cause some issues, or at least weird looks. It depends on the pantheon in general, and also individual gods. The Aztecs were pretty racist, but Quetzalcoatl, being a god worshipped from Arizona to Nicaragua, is likely to be less concerned about a Scion being some other race.

                  Also to note is that with enough Appearance Knacks, the Scion can be any ethnicity they want. And Human Clay has the potential craziness of asking a Health God to actually change their ethnicity

                  I kind of want to have a game where every member of the Band is a different ethnicity than their pantheon. Chinese Loa. Mexican Kami. Samoan Aesir. Tons of possibilities here

                                                      Ethnicity is never a concern mechanically speaking, it is only a concern for roleplaying reasons. Some gods may be 'enlightened' with modern first world values, but you can always expect some gods to cling to their bronze age mentality with the passion of a thousand burning stars. That means racism, elitism, nepotism, cronyism, jingoism and a bunch of other 'isms that have the potential to spice up your game and make life a roller coaster for your players.

                                                      But part of the fun of choosing an outlier is overcoming or embracing those negative expectations.
