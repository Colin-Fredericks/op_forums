    Originally posted by Telgar View Post
    That's because of a fundamental difference between Scion and "Fantasy" RPGs. In most Fantasy games, "Magic" is a thing that only SOME characters do. In Scion, ALL characters have magic, do magic and are magic. You want to throw fireballs? Learn Fire. Raise Zombies? Death. Summon beasts? Animal. Waterbend like Katara? Water. Smite people with lightning? Sky.

    You can do everything a typical fantasy wizard can do, but it's not in one "Wizard" set of powers. Everyone in Scion is a wizard. You can't specialize in magic, except by specializing in Purviews. Admittedly, the system does make it hard to bypass Epic Attributes because they are hands-down BETTER than Purviews and are, in fact, REQUIRED to use Purviews on-level. So, there are some flaws (big ones, tons of them), but not being able to play a magician isn't one.
    In general, then, it sounds like something that Purviews should do is be able to compete with Epic Attributes?

                                        Off the top of my head...

                                        Why a Time purview:
                                        -There ARE Time gods, and titans (yes they’re rare, but they do exist). And such beings would have to be something other than gods who do nothing but chronicle everything.
                                        -Time is an elemental force of the universe as much as Fire, Death, or Light. And it exists beyond the mere concept of seasons.
                                        -About half the powers under the Stars purview do not belong there.

                                        What would it do?
                                        -Transfer: Perfect Timing, Sense Age, Still Object, Twist Time, and Frozen Moment to this new purview
                                        -Split: Twist time into slow and haste as opposed to both at once.
                                        -Add the following effects:
                                        Hindsight (Prophecy deals with the future, Mystery deals with the present, this would see into the past)
                                        Preserve (remove something/someone from the process of decay. There are stories of those cursed with immortality, so here you go…)
                                        Erode (do the opposite, see Hades in the recent Clash of the Titans remake), Reset (undo last action)
                                        Repeat(repeat last event)
