                            Name: Sophia Kallis
                            Divine Parent: Athena
                            Callings: Creator, Sage (Favored), Hunter

                            Origin Path: Child prodigy of the science fair circuit. (Hailed as gifted from a young age, was quickly drawn to a love of science and explored this passion by entering and winning many science fairs and competitions.)
                            Role Path : "There's never been a case I couldn't solve!" ( After solving the theft of a rival science project she was framed for at a competition, became interested in solving crimes and eventually became a well known private investigator and police consultant.)
                            Relationship Path: "I've always been proud of your craftiness." ( Daughter of Athena tasked with investigating potential threats to the Theoi.)

                            From a young age, it was clear Sophia was intelligent and inquisitive. Raised by a single father who was a crime scene technician, as soon as she could read she began to pour through any books she could get her hands on. She especially enjoyed her father's old college science textbooks. Recognizing a budding passion in his daughter, he encouraged her to conduct experiments and began entering her into science fairs and other competitions. Sophia quickly became known as a prodigy, always placing in the top three of every contest she entered and winning first more often than not.

                            When she was 13, a new passion began to grow as she became fixated with mystery novels. She loved imagining herself as a great detective solving mysteries and catching criminals. Sophia's two passions unexpectedly collided a year later when she entered a prestigious national science fair. The talk of the fair was that the grand prize was going to be a close contest between Sophia's entry and that of another child prodigy. Yet when the boy's project mysteriously disappeared, only to be found destroyed under Sophia's table, it seemed like she would be disqualified. However, in light of her protests that she was innocent the head judge, a older man in a wheelchair who looked strangely familiar to Sophia, told her to solve the case and find the real culprit. As she examined the crime scene, she found that the clues just jumped out to her, and she was quickly able to identify the culprit as the mother of another contestant who was the popular pick for third place.

                            From that day forward, she knew she wanted to be a real detective. She worked incredibly hard all through high school and college, eventually earning her private investigator's license at the age of 20. The morning after, she was visited by two people. One was the wheelchair bound judge who had given her the chance to prove her innocence and the other was a woman who bore a striking resemblance to Sophia. The woman was her mother, Athena, and the man was her uncle Hephaestus. Athena explained that she had sent Hephaestus to that science fair and set up the sabotage as a way to test Sophia and nudge her into her destiny. Athena explained that Olympus had many enemies who lurked in the shadows and plotted their downfall in secret. The gods were in need of a bright young investigator who could learn about these threats and bring them into the light to meet justice. Her uncle then presented her with a gift, a pocket knife that he referred to as an "Olympus Army Knife" that he explained would always have the right tool for the job. Armed with only her wits and her knife, Sophia Kallis quickly embraced her destiny to become the investigator for the gods!

                            Notes: Sophia was my first Scion character. She's inspired by my love of shows like McGyver, Monk, and Psych. The idea is that she combines the hyper perceptive observational abilities of characters like Sherlock Holmes or Shawn Spencer with McGyver's scientific knowledge and ability to improvise gadgets from materials found in the field. Her eventual destiny is to become Goddess of Investigation, watching over detectives and scientists alike.

                                            Redwood is unlike the rest of her satyr kin, wholly uninterested in drunken revelry and sexual escapes her entire life; when another satyr tried to force himself upon her, she blinded him in one eye and turned her back on the Dionysian troupe forever. She wandered the wild places of the world and grew angry at man's encroachment upon it, but hers was the steely anger of a woman wronged, not the raving fury of the Maenads she had left behind, and so she took up the role of a primal avenger, watching from the forest and acting without mercy on any who tried to harm the natural world. Artemis appeared to her in one of Northern California's mighty oceans of trees and appointed her their guardian, gifting her with a divine bow and the name she carries to this day.

                                            Over the years, Redwood has seen her hatred of humanity cool, softened by the reverence afforded to the mighty trees by countless tourists, rangers, and local hikers, but she does not forgive any perceived sleight against her home or the flora and fauna that share it with her. Her disgust towards to the mindless excesses of her kin has only grown with years and distance, and she's sworn to kill the man she blinded if she ever sees him again. A part of her deeply resents Dionysus himself for (in her eyes) leading the rest of her people astray to waste the ages in mindless vice.

                                                Name: Hitomi Tsukino (She's Japanese American, so name is in Western order)
                                                Divine Parent: Tsukiyomi
                                                Callings: Trickster (Favored), Liminal, Warrior? (Not sure on the third one)

                                                Origin Path: Always the class clown. (Middle child in family of workaholic parents. Acted out with pranks and jokes for attention.)
                                                Role Path: Break-in artist turned security expert. (Began breaking into places for the adrenaline rush of it, got caught by a security company in the middle of a demo, were so impressed by her skills they hired her to test their services)
                                                Relationship Path: "Would it kill you to show a little affection?" (Daughter of Tsukiyomi dissatisfied with her father's distant nature)

                                                Growing up as the middle child and only daughter of her family, Hitomi was used to being invisible. Her mother was a world famous astronomer and her father was an aspiring astronaut turned founder of a private company trying to achieve space travel after a heart condition kept him out. As such, they were never home, always working. What time they were home they spent doting on Hitomi's baseball ace older brother or her math genius younger brother. So, like most kids she began to act out. She became the class clown, telling jokes and pulling pranks to try and earn her parents' attention. Yet no matter what she did, her parents rarely seemed to have time for her. The only thing she found solace in was the strangely peaceful feeling of belonging she got when she looked at the moon.

                                                Eventually, as she got older she figured if people were going to treat her as invisible she'd take advantage of it. She began sneaking around, going places behind her parents' backs. She found such a rush in sneaking around she even began breaking into places. She never really felt the need to take anything, at least not anything important or valuable. Maybe a glass of milk from the neighbor's fridge or a pen from a teacher's desk, petty souvenirs. Really, the thrill for her was all in the actual sneaking in and out, and she was really good at it. So she kept on doing it and doing it, very rarely getting caught.

                                                It all paid off after she graduated high school. She moved out on her own, crashing with some friends as she pondered her future. One day, on a whim she decided to break into an office building. After a few days of casing the place, she made her move. Parkouring her way into a second floor window, things were going smoothly for a while as she avoided people, slunk through blind spots in security cameras, and stole a sandwich from a breakroom fridge. As she sat there in an empty room eating it, she was surprised when a large group of people entered. Turns out that a security company looking for a contract was showing off their services to the board members of the company that owned the building. The building owners were furious and were going to call the police when the security consultant stopped them. He grilled Hitomi heavily on how she had gotten there, and why she had broken in. When her explanation revealed several vulnerabilities in their security net, and she explained that she wasn't going to take anything she just wanted to break in for fun, the security consultant saw an opportunity. He offered her a job. She would try to break into places this company protected to help highlight weak points in their security. Hitomi was delighted, and she accepted the job immediately.

                                                A few years later, Hitomi got an unusual request from the company she worked for. A hotel nearby was hosting an important person, although details were scarce on who. Her boss wanted her to see if she could sneak in, find the person's room and see if she could make it inside. Apparently the hotel was concerned about assassins. Figuring a job was a job, Hitomi went about preparing her break in, and on the night of her job a full moon shone brightly on her successful attempt. She was surprised to find that this important person was a seemingly plain Japanese salaryman. Just as she was about to leave, figuring she had the wrong room, the man called her out by name even though his back was turned to her. With a shimmer of moonlight his clothing changed into ornate robes. He explained that he was Tsukiyomi, and that he was her true father, conceived when he fooled Hitomi's mother by taking the form of her mortal father. He then continued by saying that this job was a test to make sure Hitomi was truly skilled enough to justify him putting effort into developing her potential. He presented her with a mask, carved into the shape of a rabbit's face, and told her he expected much from her.

                                                Hitomi is a little unsure of her life now. While she loves her new abilities and the new places they let her sneak into, she's let down that she has yet another parent that seems to barely notice her. She's now determined that if nothing else, she's going to earn the respect of her father and make him pay attention to her.

                                                Notes: My second Scion character. I was looking at the purviews of Tsukiyomi in 1st Ed and their boons when I realized what a great cat burglar a Scion of Tsukiyomi would make. Yet as I built the character I realized she wasn't a thief and instead she became this adrenaline junkie break in artist. If you're wondering about her mask, it's supposed to look like the ANBU masks from Naruto. She has a rabbit motif, and her calling card when she breaks into somewhere is a little oragami bunny. I kind of had the idea she could become the Goddess of the Obscuring Darkness, darkness that hides people and protects them, as a way to do a good deity of darkness. I'm sure she'd get thieves that would pray to her, but I imagine she's also the goddess who would protect say, that poor abused wife who finally had enough and who is running and hiding and Hitomi would send her shadows to hide her from her abuser.
