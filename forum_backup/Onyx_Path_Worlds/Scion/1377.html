    Fan-Written Scion Material

    11-02-2013, 07:38 PM
    Fan-Favorite Non-Canon PDFs and Other Fun Files

    Charts and References
    Scents of the Divine Index - A compiled list of all canon and many non-canon Scents for easy reference. (by Telgar)
    Associated Powers Index - A big list of all the Epic Attributes and Purviews along with which deities are Associated with each one. Canon-only. (by Telgar)
    Divine Nouns - A list of how to refer to singular and plural deities of the various Pantheons. (by Telgar)
    Knack Charts - Flowchart of all the canon Knacks. (by Telgar)
    Expanded Quick-Reference Chart - Tables listing the Associated and Favored traits of most of the non-canon Gods listed in this thread. (by Telgar)
    Fan-Written PSP Quick-Reference Chart - Quick-refernece for fan-written PSP's. (by Telgar)
    Titanic Templates - Cliff-Notes of all 10 official + 10 fan-written Titan Templates (by Telgar)
    Scion Armory (link broken?) - Illustrated table of various modern and historical weapons from the Scion Corebook. (by Kalle)

    Extra Gods
    •Additional Gods - extra gods for all Pantheons (by Demonchilde)
    •Additional Gods 2 - Compiled list containing (most of) the gods written up on the Official Scion Wiki. (multi-author document)
    •Additional Gods 3 - Collection of Gods from several different Pantheons. (by Sorcerymon)
    •Additional Canon Gods - A compiled list of the extra canon Gods that are scattered around the various books. (by Telgar)
    •Combined Additional Gods - This document combines all the above documents and includes a few new Gods. However, duplicate Gods were removed. (multi-author document)

    New Pantheons and sub-Pantheons
    •The Atua - Maori / Polynesian Pantheon (by Ptah1888 and Pashupatastra)
    •The Vanguard of Avalon - Arthurian Pantheon (by BlaineTog)
    •The Vairgit - Arctic Pantheon (by TerriblyUncreative)
    •The Rus - Slavic Pantheon (by Demonchilde)
    •The Anunna - Mesopotamian Pantheon (by Demonchilde)
    •The Manitou - Native American Pantheon (by Demonchilde)
    •Sub-Pantheons - Several small groups of Gods that fall under the political aegis of larger Pantheons. (by Demonchilde)
    •The Vanir - Norse sub-Pantheon (by Telgar)
    •The Aisar - Etruscan sub-Pantheon of the Dodekatheon (by Telgar)

    New or Revised Purviews
    •Alternate Arete - A single-purchase version of Arete. (by ArthurH3535)
    •Alternate Prophecy - a conversion of Prophecy into an All-Purpose Purview, with distinct Boons for each level. (by R_B_Bergstrom)
    •Emotion Purview - an All-Purpose Purview to control emotions, differentiated like Animal, by specific manifestation. (by Brent-not-Broken)
    •Atman - a replacement PSP for the Devas. (by Brent-not-Broken)
    •Jotunblut - a revised Jotunblut, focusing on gaining giant-like traits and power. (by Brent-not-Broken)
    •Tsukumo-Gami - a revised PSP for the Amatsukami. (by Brent-not-Broken)
    •Forge Purview - A Purview for crafters and artificers of all kinds! (by Telgar)
    •Slavic Pantheon-Specific Purview: Prav - Prav empowers a Slavic Scion when he follows his Virtues. (by Telgar)
    •Sound Purview Revised - A Purview for singers, storytellers, screamers and speech-makers. (by Telgar)
    •Revised Animal - A version of Animal that is suitable for hunting Gods and has no Animal (Species) divisions. (by Iry)
    •Roman Pantheon-Specific Purview: Civitas - The Roman Gods are at their strongest when working together for the common good.
    •Gaulish Pantheon-Specific Purview: Deuogdonio - Gaulish sacrifices can harvest strength from defeated foes.
    •Etruscan Pantheon-Specific Purview: Nethsrac - Gods of the Etruscans make their will known through visions and portents.

    Pantheon-Specific Resources
    •Aztec Resource Double Whammy - A combination PDF that has both Brent-Not-Broken's Atzlanti Toolkit and Griffinguy24's Nagual Animal Companion resources! (by Brent-not-Broken and Griffinguy24)

    Antagonists and Titans
    •Expanded Titans - New Titan Avatars, creatures and other assorted information. (by Pashupatastra)
    •Munera, Titan of Decadence - A wiki-page detailing the Titan of Decadence and its Avatars. (by Kizuna Wave)
    Last edited by Telgar; 09-22-2015, 12:31 PM.
