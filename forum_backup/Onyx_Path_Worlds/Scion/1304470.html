    Timeline of the Scion KS Printing

    04-12-2019, 02:24 PM
    Hi all,

    This is a timeline of events in the last 8 months in creation and publication of Scion 2nd Edition. The dates listed were taken from Kickstarter Updates, Monday Meeting Progress Notes, Onyx Path forums (specifically the errata collection dates), and my DriveThru library entries.

    At first blush, one might assume this is a passive aggressive comment on delays experienced in the Scion 2nd Edition project. That’s not accurate, but I can see where one might make that assumption. I’ve been fine with the time required to get the game in the best shape possible. I did put this together however, because of the lost errata situation. If you’re unfamiliar with it, Rich Thomas provides an overview in the Monday Meeting notes from April 8th, 2019.

    Rich has said he’ll share what he knows once he has some definitive information, and I have faith he will. Doing my own due diligence as a Kickstarter backer, I started following one of his long standing recommendations and “read the Progress Notes.” In addition, I started reading community questions and the direct communications from Onyx Path staff in the Monday Meeting comments.

    It’s not difficult to get confused. Not because the information or the notes are confusing in of themselves, but I’m looking at roughly 8 months. There are a lot of common terms that mean different things depending on the context. For example terms like proof and print can be nouns, verbs, and apply to entirely different separate stages in this whole big process. The term “files” can pertain to the Prestige print run, the POD print run, the Advance Backer PDF, or the Post-errata PDF. Add to that, these events all run and bleed into each other over months in weekly installments.

    So I broke it into a timeline. That way when Rich does have some information he can share, I can put it context. Or a continuum if you like. And I’m sharing it with the community. I DO NOT want to foist any conclusions on anyone. This post is not an allegation or an indictment. Do I have questions? Definitely and hopefully this will help me (and you) ask BETTER questions. The community will draw it’s own conclusions as is proper.

    Anyway, I strived to only provide accurate information. I DO interpret what I think the Progress Notes are meant to communicate, but I did so in good faith. If I have gotten anything wrong, I welcome correction from an informed source. I’m not trying to mislead anyone.

    Let me offer a few common definitions for clarity. It helps to be specific:
    Prestige hardcover: The book store quality version of the book that backers will receive from pledging for a hard copy in the Kickstarter. It went through a separate proofing process than the POD version. If it’s not POD, it’s Prestige.

    POD or POD hard copy: Either the digital file used for a Print-on-Demand hard copy or an physical book created on spec by DriveThru. The release of final backer PDF has been tied directly to this digital file. If it’s not Prestige, it’s POD.

    Advance PDF: A PDF shared with the community that is not expected to perfect or final. They’re both previews to backers and a tool for the community to help identify errata.

    Post-errata PDF: This is either the finished PDF with all the corrections (that backers are waiting on, as of this writing), or… it’s the PDF released after April 2nd which was intended to final.


    Without further ado.

    July 9th, 2018 The Origin Advance PDF is made available to Backers. This was Pre-Errata and not expected to be finished or final, and may have had missing art. That said, it had a preliminary layout.

    July 10th to August 19th, 2018 This is the period open for backers to assist with errata collection for Origin. The thread is locked on the 19th.

    October 17th, 2018 The Hero Advance PDF is made available to Backers. This was Pre-Errata and not expected to be finished or final, and may have had missing art. That said, it had a preliminary layout.

    October 18th to November 11th, 2018 This is the period open for backers to assist with errata collection for Hero. The thread is locked on the 11th.

    February 11th, 2019 The Monday Meeting notes reflect that Origin and Hero have the following status – Putting printer files together, sending to press this week. This refers to the Prestige Hard Cover. Spelling it out a little more, this is when Onyx Path was preparing their digital file to be sent to sent to the Prestige printer, so that printer can create and return a prestige proof copy.

    February 18th, 2019 The status or Origin and Hero changes to At the printer. What this is telling us is that the Prestige Printer has Onyx Path’s digital file and is creating a proof of the Prestige hard copy, which will be returned to Onyx Path for them to inspect.

    February 25th, 2019 The status of Origin and Hero upgraded to Getting printer proofs this week. At this point, Onyx Path was expecting the Prestige printer proof to arrive.

    March 4th, 2019 The status of Origin and Hero upgraded to Printer proofs returned. BackerKit site orders getting locked Weds. This means that the prestige printer proof was reviewed and sent back to the prestige printer.


    March 11th, 2019 The status of Origin and Hero changes to ”Printing. PoD proof ordered.” This update communicates two different things on one bullet point. First, an actual print order for the Prestige hard covers has been issued or released. Secondly, Onyx Path sent DriveThru a digital file, so that DriveThru can produce a POD proof for Onyx Path to examine.

    Again, for emphasis, this the point at which the Prestige had copies were released to print.

    My only comment: At this point, backers begin to ask weekly for a post-errata PDF, because they’re reading that the Prestige hard covers have been released for fabrication. The response every week is that the post-errata PDF is being held until the Proof copy from DriveThru has been received, examined, and approved. Pointing this out might constitute an editorial on my part, but it’s also what happened.

    March 18th, 2019 The status of Origin and Hero remain at ”Printing. PoD proof ordered.” Same as the previous update, we can assume they’re waiting to receive the POD proof from DriveThru to arrive.


    March 25th, 2019 the status of Origin and Hero are changed to Printing wrapping up this week. I believe this status on applies to the Prestige hard copy printing and that DriveThru’s POD proof is still ongoing.

    April 1st, 2019 the status of Origin and Hero change to ”Shipping from printer to fulfillment shipper this week.” This pertains to the Prestige hardcopies, but Rich addresses the POD in comment I’ll quote below.

    To put a capstone on this timeline, this is a quote by Rich from the April 1st Monday Meeting comments.

    Originally posted by Rich Thomas on April 2nd
    I haven’t notified backers in an Update yet, as I need to also set up the PoD coupons, but the final PDFs are already up today. You can download the updated ones in your DTRPG library. If you’re not a backer, Scion will be available to the public on DTRPG around the same time as stores will have the physical books.
    April 2nd, 2019 There’s no status update on this date, but what Onyx Path believed to be the Post-Errata PDF (otherwise known as the “Final PDF”) was updated on DriveThru. This is the first opportunity that backers to see what they expect to be the full and corrected rule set.

    During the next week Backers discover that not all the errata appears in the “Final PDF.” Neall Price becomes aware of the situation as so does Onyx Path salaried staff. Neall begins to the review the “Final PDF” in detail to determine what made it into the file and what didn’t.

    April 8th, 2019 The status of Origin and Hero is Shipping from printer to fulfillment shipper this week. In the body of that day’s blog entry, Rich made a more detailed report explaining the situation.

    Originally posted by Rich Thomas on April 8th, 2019[/quote
    Today, though, we were less happy with having to get together during the meeting and talk about how we need to figure out what broke in our proofing process with our Scion 2e core books.

    I’ll talk more about this when we get all the information in, but it seems pretty sure that a number of errata fixes that Neall input did not “stick”, but remained as errors. He is currently, painstakingly, going back through his errata notes and reconstructing what changes need to be made.

    The last I checked, he hasn’t found a pattern for why some changes stayed, and others did not, nor why this happened with these two books, but not on the dozen-plus books that have previously used the same proofing process. While I’d like to know that, and will, our in-house devs have already taken over the errata input phase so that our book developers only have to notate the PDF.

    This will add another stage, but will also give us an additional chance to identify whether errata that we gathered is actually in a book.
    We’ll be able to tell better once Neall finishes his pass, but we’re not seeing anything that breaks the game or makes it unplayable. Certainly, the rules as they stand have worked for the many Scion 2e groups who are already playing the game, including the fraction of Actual Play groups we can highlight each week in our Media section below.

    But it is extremely frustrating for us, and Neall, but also for all of the Scion community that stepped up to pore through the Backer PDF and send us what they found. Sometimes, in publishing, despite all your safeguards, stuff slips in. Here’s a painful example of a process that worked…until it didn’t.
    Rich followed up after a question in the comments with the following statement.

    Originally posted by Rich Thomas, April 8th, 2019
    Folks, just to put this upfront: if I intended right now to go into details of what may or may not have been affected by the errata problem, or details of our proofing process, beyond what I’ve written about here or in previous MMN blogs, I would have gone into those details already. Not trying to be cute, we really do need to determine the extent of the issue before we can solve any problems.
