    Creating Monsters: How do they do it?

    12-27-2015, 08:31 PM
    So anybody who knows anything about mythology knows that it is full of stories about Gods turning people into monsters (such as Athena cursing Medusa), but I don't see any specifics on how to do this in the Scion universe. There are references to characters who have done so, but nothing specific on how. The closest I can find are the Animal and Jotunblut purviews that allow you to make Chimeras and Giants at really high levels.

    In Demigod, there is one Scion (Ixion) who is responsible for the creation of many Titanspawn, but again, it doesn't say how he did so.


    So if I wanted to play a Scion who goes around turning people into monsters, how would I do it in game?

                                  There is a 10 dot Health boon that lets you create new humans from a blood/tissue sample, if you have samples from 2 or more people, the results will be a combination of the donor's traits. If you have the 10 dot Animal Boon, you can create your own Chimaera(gryphons and such); Fertility has "Impossible Hybrid", that lets you combine any 2 types of plant; both of these can be used in combination with the 10 Health boon to make Human/Animal or Human/Plant creatures, and finally, Chaos has "Unintended Purpose"; that can fuse machines in place of body parts; if you have all 4, you can make some pretty crazy shit!
