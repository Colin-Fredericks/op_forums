                        142) Your pantheon's monarch/chief/parent-and-progenitor has asked you (and, by extension, your Band) to play matchmaker for the Scions of two other deities from your pantheon. Specifically, two deities with a history of enmity/estrangement between them. (Naturally, this works best if at least one of those Scions is already in your player group.)

                        According to the requestee, dark days are coming, for the World in general, and your pantheon specifically. The theory is that this marriage will begin healing the metaphysical breach between the feuding gods, and help your pantheon with some much-needed unity. Of course, the good making this request isn't always known for clarity or sanity (monarch/chief/parent-and-progenitor deities often aren't.) But they might just be right this time...

                        (This is based on a storyline my own gaming group began to play out, between my Scion of Tsuki-yomi and another player's Scion of Amaterasu. Sadly, that player quit the game almost immediately thereafter, so it went nowhere. Hopefully, you'll have better luck...)

                                    143) Some say that we are fated to become our parents. And in recent months, Scions are starting to experience that fated-ness (with a capital "F"). Those who try to be different than their divine parents (kind in comparison to cruel, altruistic in comparison to self-absorbed, et cetera) find themselves drawn, like moths to a flame, to that same kind of behavior. Ditto for their powers: woe betide the Scion of a fire god who tries to use his Water purview.

                                    Your Band is relatively unaffected so far, and so you lot have been "requested" to get to the bottom of this. And fast, before you find yourselves reliving your parents' mistakes indefinitely.

                                        144) When it comes to family feuds, it seems Fate has a hard on for them. Almost immediately after your visitation, you received a Fate Bond that has existed since time beyond mortal reckoning. Your divine bloodline, and that of another deity, are destined to oppose each other until the end of time. It just so happens that another Scion received their visitation around the same time you did, and now they find themselves compelled to challenge you at every opportunity. Regardless of the motives or cause, they just feel compelled to stand against your side of the argument. Maybe that wouldn't be as terrible as it sounds, if they didn't have a plan to break the fate bond. It's a simple process of killing the rival bloodline before they produce the next generation to perpetuate the cycle. That's right, your new rival isn't content to just sit back and act as a foil to your schemes in some grand tapestry, they're determined to make their own destiny, but they need you out of the way to do it.

                                        Luckily for you, two can play at that game!

                                                    146) Competition and oneupsmanship are nothing new to the gods.

                                                    The latest fad: street racing!

                                                    Perhaps Thor is a bit too enamored of Days of Thunder, or Indra finds common ground with movies that are fast and furious. But neither deities nor their offspring are immune to adrenaline rushes or counting coup at each other's expense, and the chosen racetracks aren't hairpin curves on mountain roads.
