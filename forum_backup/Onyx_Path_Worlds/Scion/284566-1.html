        Alright, well this is still clearly a work in progress. Yes, I know I don't have the dice mechanics written up, these are just descriptions of what the powers are supposed to do. I'm having a bit of difficulty thinking up a level 7 and 8 ability that seems proper for such a Purview. Any suggestions?



        Possession: For as long as the demons have known about humanity, they have had one goal in mind, to corrupt and enslave the population. After thousands of years of practice, they have refined corruption to an art form.

        Domination over the mind and body of another is the exclusive purview of the Great Evils, and they call this art Possession! An early practitioner of Possession is limited to subtle whispers of temptation and sin. But a true master is able to hijack a victim's body, transferring their essence into the host and controlling them from the inside out. Unless the demon chooses to voluntarily leave, it usually requires an act of divine intervention to force them out. Because of this, Possession is a powerful and terrifying Purview!

        Possession gives its wielders the power to invoke emotional responses, weaken inhibitions, control the dream world, and manipulate flesh and bone like a puppeteer. Prolonged application of Possession can see a victim's willpower almost totally destroyed, as they become enslaved to the demons who torments them in such a manner. Possession costs 1 point of Legend equal to its level when attempting to perform it.


        --------------------------

        Level 1 - Fell Whispers: By focusing their gaze upon someone and speaking in a whisper, the Scion's words are carried to the subject. They do not actually have to be within earshot, they simply have to be within their line of sight at the time. While Fell Whispers is unlikely to invoke any kind of reaction on its first use, repeatedly whispering the same phrases over several days can wear down the subject's mind as they begin to question their sanity.


        Level 2 - Brooding Trance: At this level of mastery, the Scion is able to shut out distractions the victim might experience when whispering to them. Their vision begins to dim for things which are not the object of the Scion's discussion, and background noises are muffled and dull. It becomes very difficult to focus on anything that the Scion does not wish you to focus on!


        Level 3 - Whispers of Temptation: No longer is the Scion limited to simply having a one way conversation with a subject. They can now implant hypnotic suggestions when they transmit their whispers to another subject. When using Whispers of Temptation, the Scion utters a sentence, which (if successful), the subject will come to believe are their own thoughts. The appropriate legend cost must be spend for each sentence the Scion attempts to speak in such a manner, so the more complicated the suggestion is, the more expensive it will be.


        Level 4 - Touch of Corruption: The Scion no longer needs to speak to a person to coerce them. When Touch of Corruption is activated, the Scion chooses one vice, and then the next person they lay their hands upon becomes the subject for the spell. If the spell succeeds, the designated vice becomes a nagging obsession, constantly urging the subject to partake of it. The spell wears off once they indulge in the designated vice, but at the start of each turn, they may attempt to break the compulsion through willpower and concentration. If they succeed, Touch of Corruption ends without them having to indulge in it.


        Level 5 - All in My Head: Glaring at people while you mutter under your breath is probably a good way to attract unwanted attention. But with this level of mastery, the Scion can become undetectable by mundane attempts. While this boon is activated, the Scion can perform levels 1-4 of Possession without being detected except by mystical investigations. Their body fades out of sight, and their voice appears to come from all random direction for the intended target, making it nearly impossible to tell their origin point.


        Level 6 -


        Level 7 -


        Level 8 - Puppet Master: A nightmarish and disgusting use of Possession allows the Scion to work a victim like a marionette. Using their hands to conduct the victim, the subject's body moves in a jerky, limp fashion as if it is supported by invisible strings. If the subject attempts to fight against this power (which they most certainly will), it can be a very unnerving sight with the body struggling over which master it listens to. The subject can fight against Puppet Master's effects, but their strength must be comparable to the user, or their muscles will be overpowered by the hellish force controlling them.


        Level 9 - Body Snatcher: No longer does the Scion have to restrict themselves to honeyed words and macabre puppet shows. Their ability to control another person is so great, they can shunt themselves into the victim's body, taking control over its actions completely. They can speak in either the host's voice or using their own. Often at times, they switch between the two as they find it amusing. The subject's mind is still conscious and aware of what it is doing, but has no power to resist once it has been possessed. Many demons receive ecstatic levels of pleasure from listening to their host scream and beg inside their own minds, watching the demon perform blasphemous actions with their body and being unable to stop them. Unless the Scion voluntarily chooses to leave the body, they must be forced out through some manner of divine intervention, such as an exorcism.

        Body Snatcher can not be used on Gods, Titans, or inorganic lifeforms. It can be used on Heroes and Demi Gods who's legend is at least 2 stages lower than the user.


        Level 10 - Soul Contract: The highest form of possession that a Scion of the Great Evils can manifest is the ability to bargain with a victim's soul. With the use of this boon, the Scion forms some manner of deal with the subject, and upon upholding their end of the deal, ownership of the victim's soul is transferred to the Scion who formed the contract. Once the contract has been carried out, the victim's body is spontaneously branded with a mark that is unique to the Scion as a permanent reminder that they are now their slave, both in life and in death. The branding process is very painful, as it is burned into their flesh and, indeed, their very souls. The catch is that the demon must follow through on their end of the deal before the victim dies, or the contract is null and void. Therefor, Scions should not agree to contracts they can not complete, and they should see to fulfilling the contract as quickly as possible.

            At first glance, you forgot to give a couple of the Evils favored epic attributes. That's going to hurt (cripple) their scions. Scion-equivalents for the Heavenly Host would be cool. You problably could even use the same PSP with little change, just re-fluffed as angelic advice/aid. I however take issue with your list of 'relics' like the Worldstone or Horadric Cube. One of them wasn't even made by the gods, and another of your examples is a TOWN. And not using dark virtues for evil scions? Really?

            While a lot of settings could have powerful entities that could be statted out and used in Scion, it doesn't really work with Diablo. The setting and themes are just too different. The Scion purview of 'magic' uses subtle fate-based effects, which are practically unseen in Diablo. While Sky and Fire may let you toss lightning and fire, there's a much, much wider assortment of ways to bring the magical hurt in Diablo than the Purview system can provide.

            What about mortal magic? Can Scions study that?

            What about relics? What's the difference between a legendary god-forged artifact and an item produced by any number of mortal mage-smiths?

            While 'manipulative bastard child of Andariel' is a fun concept I'd like to play some day, I really don't think you can just shoehorn Diablo into the Scion framework and call it a day. Admittedly, Tathamet and Anu are already Titan-equivalents. The Archangels and the Evils could be their avatars. Purviews are just schools of magic scions have an instinctive affinity with. But I think you've got a lot more work to do if you want to reconcile the two settings so that one can be played with the other.
