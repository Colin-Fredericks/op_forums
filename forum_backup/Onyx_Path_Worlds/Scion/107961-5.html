                    Maybe it's the min-maxer in me... maybe it's the fact that I've been a Storyteller for practically my entire gaming career... but it seems to me that if you have a problem with Order, follow the KISS (Keep It Simple Stupid) method:

                    You have 5 virtue points to spend at character creation. If you have Order, keep it at one dot and fail away at the roll. If you're rolling rather lucky (or unlucky in some cases), spend the willpower. OR... Hero has a wonderful sidebar detailing the possibility of taking alternate virtues. So long as your Storyteller is willing, go with a different virtue that could work instead. Vengeance or Intellect or Courage... seriously It's not that complicated. Maybe it will cause some strife with your parent pantheon. That's good roleplay value. And it might get you in better with pantheons that have the virtue you took instead.

                    Otherwise, I have no need of putting in my two cents worth. Plenty of people have already explained to death what Order is. If there was something I thought I could add, it'd be along the lines of "Order is meant to prevent chaos and anarchy, whilst Duty is the drive to follow." Order can be subjective. If a criminal is getting away with something like murder because the law's too lenient (not because of a loophole or anything), then Order claims he needs to be punished. It's the overlap of Duty and enforcing Order that seems to be the big argument overall. To keep it simple for me, I view Order as the knowledge and Duty as the action. While Order requires some acts, it relies on the basis of knowing that what you're doing is right, whereas Duty relies on (hopefully) believing that what you're doing is right.

                        The only way someone can "get away" with murder as far as Order is concerned is if they committed that crime and were not punished for it. As long as there is no violation of the system, that person did not commit murder, they lawfully ended the life of another. Which means, under the legal systems most of us live under, a character laboring under Order cannot kill someone they know killed another until that person is actually found guilty by an appropriate authority and then said authority gets to designate the lawful punishment.

                        No matter how much you KNOW he did it, you're going to have to let the system do it's job. Or fail a roll/spend a WP.

                        Living with the ugly side of Virtues is part of the time. You're right. Take a 1 or switch it out (if you're allowed and are willing to accept whatever your Storyteller intuits the penalties might or might not be for doing so).

                        Even 1's can suck. I had a character roll on Intellect 1 and make the roll and he had to go with a plan that everyone else had abandoned. Since they abandoned it for no plan at all.

                                    Originally posted by Allan53 View Post

                                    While you literally have a guillotine hanging over your neck forcing you to take Virtues you don't like? Shouldn't all Virtues only be taken because they suit your character?
                                    That is a question with lots of differing opinions in the Scion fandom (which, honestly, can be said for any question in the Scion fandom). It depends on if you think Virtues are a representation of a character's deeply held beliefs that are enhanced by ichor, or if they are a representation of the Pantheon's deeply held beliefs and are imposed by ichor. If the former, then Scions should definitely only pick the Virtues they feel their character should have. If you hold the latter view, on the other hand, then Virtues are more akin to control chips in your brain and you don't have any more choice than a Vampire has a 'choice' to not burn in the sun. I have seen most people go with a compromise of Three Pantheon Plus One Character Virtues, with or without XP penalty on your PSP.
