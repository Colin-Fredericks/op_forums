    What if a group of Lesser Immortals wanted to be a full fledged Pantheon?

    03-22-2015, 04:24 PM
    Basic idea, a group of lesser Immortals and disaffected scions have come together. They've set them selves up as a pantheon, they intend to strive
    against the Titans, and they've managed to gain the use of the Old Atlantean PSP as their own. (Their virtues are Conviction, Expression, Intelect, and Valor). They've shown they aren't Titan corrupted and Fate seems to say they aren't to be attacked or slain without just reason.

    The Pantheon has been clever and gotten a very talented author (also a lesser Imortal) to write them up as Science fiction Characters. The pantheon of an interstellar empire! They've gotten a series of really good novels written which tell the Mythology of their fictional empire. They depict a fictional religion drawn out of the best/noblest ideals of Buddhism, Chritianity, Bahai, and Daoism. They've also gotten a series of graphic novels printed and have gotten some good movies made as well. They are very real and interesting to their fanbase. And they are intending to produce more films, books, graphic novels, a TV series on basic cable, and record albums.

    Reactions?

                              Seems like a God with Epic Charisma 9 and a softly spoken word during a TV interview wouldn't ever need a Homer. People will tell stories about gods doing godly things. We're tale-telling critters. Look at the things we care about so much right now without gods: the Kardashians, what we just ate, sports, movies, RPGs... What would people be talking about if actual GODS were striding around doing godly things. What the gods DID. Not what some dude wrote about them in a comic book.

                              The Greek Gods didn't need Homer. In fact, if anything, Homer and Snurri and their lot really cramped the gods' style. Homer wasn't an embedded reporter during the Trojan War, nor was he a crewman of Odysseus's (luckily for him). He sang about things AFTER they were long over. Based on the tales passed down from the long ago. When the gods weren't out being super impressive anymore and were cooling their heels back on Olympus avoiding any more Fatebinding.

                              If the group is out and about doing godly things, people will probably be reporting it, showing it on TV, talking about it on Twitter and Facebook, Instagraming away, writing fan fic, etc. etc. but that will all pale before the deeds proper. Gods don't need press agents all that much. What image massaging they might want can be accomplished effortlessly with Epic Socials and/or a few relevant Knacks.
