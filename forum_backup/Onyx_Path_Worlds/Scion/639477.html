    The Anga - The Guarani Gods

    08-25-2015, 05:56 PM
    (It's about bloody time...)

    So, some insight as to how I'm operating here here: I wanted to do this as a big Companion/JSR-style additional fan-made pantheon, but I am way too perfectionist and busy to make the effort. And when Neall mentioned the possibility of using these guys for stretch goals in 2e, well, I felt that my hand was forced and that I should probably post what I have.

    There were difficulties in making this, because while I strive for being as mythically accurate as possible, Amazonian gods have myriad issues. A problem I've personally faced as a monolingual person is that many studies of these myths are in Spanish or Portuguese, or untranslated ethnographies done by French and German anthropologists. As such, there's a dearth of material on them. Another problem is the fact that, like the Aztecs, Inca, and Maya religions, Guarani religions were suppressed by conquering Spaniards, so a lot of the history of them was written by Catholics whose primary mission was to get them to dump their old religions (and many others were written by structuralist anthropologists. I still debate as to which one was worse).

    And, the most important thing is that while many of these figures are present in religions throughout a number of places in South America, different individual tribes do different things with them and sometimes it's difficult to figure out what's what. As such I've decided to lump together a lot of gods together and be very permissive as to what powers they might have, as my personal choice. Just keep that in mind while reading this.

    So, for some more background, Guarani is a term for a language and cultural group that's spread throughout the forests of southern Brazil, Paraguay, Bolivia, and northern Argentina (the Guarani language is actually one of Paraguay's official languages, and a lot of info about their myths comes from there). They share a lot of words, cultural characteristics, and mythical figures with the Tupi tribes of northern Brazil, but the two cultures are rather distinct. There are numerous tribes considered "Guarani", each with populations ranging from the thousands to the dozens.

    The word "Anga" means "spirit". Most translations for "god" in Guarani refer to the god Tupa, who's on the roster and is often conflated with the Christian god, or "Nandejara" or "Our Lords/Owners", which is a Catholic term transliterated into the Guarani language. So, Anga is what I'm using.

    The Anga all have the Virtues Duty, Endurance, Harmony, and Vengeance

    In a similar vein to how the tribes are divided and in danger of extinction, a major theme I consider for playing with or as the Anga is that these gods are both not particularly united on the front of the Titan War, and are skating the edge of oblivion. I focused their bios on their past, but in the world of Scion, the future doesn't look good for them.

    And with all that out of the way, here's the roster of the Legend 12 heavy hitters of the Anga...

    Arasy –
    AKA: Jasy, Nandesy
    The goddess of the moon and wife of Tupa, Arasy is a creator equal to her husband. In addition to creating the world and the people alongside Tupa, she is also thought of as the creator of all plants and the goddess of childbirth.

    While typically seen as loving, Arasy is no pushover, and she can be quite vengeful. When Tau, the spirit of evil, kidnapped the mortal woman Kerana and raped her, Arasy cursed Tau that all his children would be monsters. This resulted in the birth of seven monstrous children that were sealed away with the Titans long ago.

    Abilities: Awareness, Craft, Empathy, Integrity, Medicine, Presence
    Powers: Earth, Epic Appearance, Health, Fertility, Justice, Moon, Stars

    Guaracy –
    AKA: Karai*, Kuarahy, Torushompek
    Brother to Tupa and Jakaira, father of Jurupari, Guaracy is the god of the sun, as well as the god of fire. He is the creator of all animals, and a superlative hunter. He is also known for being a shaman, particularly a prophet who once danced for so long that he was taken bodily into the heavens, still dancing, mind you, for his dedication.

    Abilities: Animal Ken, Athletics, Integrity, Marksmanship, Occult, Survival
    Powers: Animal (Jaguar, Macaw), Epic Perception, Epic Stamina, Fire, Prophecy, Sun

    *Fun Fact: In addition to the god, the Guarani word “karai” can also mean a prophet, or it can refer to any non-Guarani person. This adds another layer of difficulty to researching him!

    Jakaira –
    AKA: Peruda
    Jakaira is brother to Tupa and Guaracy, and holds ownership over the good winds, springtime, and mist. He is represented by haze and fog, as well as the tobacco smoke used by the Guarani people in their religious ceremonies. Few gods are seen as positively as him, he is known as “big-hearted”. He’s the one who taught the various tribes about shamanism and the use of medicinal plants, and is seen as the giver of good fortune, as well as the father of sorcery.

    Abilities: Empathy, Investigation, Medicine, Occult, Stealth, Survival
    Powers: Epic Charisma, Fertility, Health, Magic, Sky

    Jurupari –
    AKA: Yurupari, Kuwai
    Jurupari is perhaps the most dangerous of the Anga. Jesuit missionaries conflated him with Satan, and he fits the bill quite well. The son of Guaracy, born of the virgin star goddess Ceucy, he was born to take power, both magical and political, from women to be transferred to men. He is the spirit of the palm trees, the shapeshifting bogeyman who hunts the forest and kills those who subvert cultural norms, but he is also the giver of music and dances, and the patron of males and warriors. His cult and the ceremonies associated with them are some of the most widespread throughout the forests of South America. He is strict and unyielding regarding social taboos, going so far as to kill his own mother via poison and hurl her body into the sky, forming the Pleiades.

    Jurupari is seen in the World more than any other of the Anga, often in his hideous, misshapen and hairy true form, but he can shapeshift into any form, human, animal, or monstrous. His stated goal is to find a woman worthy of his father, which is supposed to bring about true equality between the sexes. The fact that Jurupari is a strong contender for the most misogynistic god in history means that it’s unlikely that this would ever happen.

    Abilities: Animal Ken, Art, Brawl, Fortitude, Presence, Survival
    Powers: Animal (Fish), Epic Appearance, Epic Stamina, Fertility, Justice, War

    Tupa –
    AKA: Maira, Monan, Nanderuvucu, Tupan
    Tupa, the thunder god, is the most beloved and important of all the Anga. He resides in the sun, and traversed the heavens to woo his wife, Arasy. For their honeymoon, they created the earth and humanity. He is the keeper of the waters, and has sent floods to overturn his own creation. He has taught humanity the ways of civilization, and is so beloved that his name is often used to refer to the Christian God.

    Tupa, for all his power and prestige, prefers to spend his days lounging around in his hammock. He makes brief forays into the World on business, often teaching people and inspiring them to action. Worship of him is widespread, but he is often seen in different ways by different tribes. Some say he is the father of all the gods, some say he is but one of them. Some separate his different aliases into many figures, others claim they are all one. Tupa, for his part, tends not to care about those petty distinctions that humans make.

    Abilities: Academics, Art, Craft, Politics, Presence, Science
    Powers: Earth, Epic Charisma, Epic Intelligence, Health, Sky, Sun, Water

    TUNE IN NEXT TIME FOR: Non-Legend 12 Anga, Legend 12 Amazonian Gods who AREN'T part of the Anga, and the primary antagonists, the Titan Tau and his Seven Monstrous Children!
