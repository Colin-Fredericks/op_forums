    Heres my 2 cents.
    In my Scion game this how I did it. I borrowed the Innomine write up on Angels and Devils and used them. In my cosmology I have God as the super powerfully God from the Judeo-Christian-Muslim. But HE/She/It is so distant from the game. God created the Titans and they created gods. God created the Angels to help the Titans run the Universe (Since Titans are embodiments of nature and concepts), while god went off to "Work in mysterious ways." Man makes his appearance in world. gods are created by Titans. Angels have internal conflict (whole Lucifier fall) and can't restrain the Titans from giving there nature and wreaking the world. The gods rise up and imprison the Titans. Angels defeat rebels and cast them in their own prison (Hell) Titans get looses, devils never left. So angels and gods have to fight them.

    So what the God in my game represent. The GM. He ultimate authority, but is so distance that his role in daly affairs is near nill. Even his own guys the angels (who I treat just like another pantheon) are not in the loop what HE/SHE/It is up to.

    Of course that how I did it. My group is not very religious so they don't really care. But for any other GM, like it been said before, know you players and work with their sensibilities on the subject.

All the options would be simplified for modern people. I was raised roman catholic and did what I thought was a lot of research to finally settle on my view of religion but you all are really beating a dead horse. IN MY VERSION of scion I explain away Christianity with the fact that prayers to "GOD" can be answered by any god in the vicinity. If you specify a named god then the prayer finds its real recipient. Most people I know (and when I say this I allude to the greater majority not the minorities which is what the writers aim will be) don't ask a named god for special treatment unless they are not one of the monotheistic religions. Now on to the simplified versions. God is a single deity pantheon with a army of angels as low level gods or demi gods who enforce his will and specialize in certain previews so he can have Sundays off. Next choice, God is a titan of order who sleeps eternally so as not to distub his creation and dreams of his followers prayers. In this angels are GOD LEVEL creatures of myth, saints are demi gods, and the prophets of the one true god are scions of various legend level. In these scenarios several things can hold true. There is no evil titan, there are only the equivalents of titan spawn where Lucifer and Lilith take head positions and various demons and fallen angels underneath them. (it is also feasible that they my have scions as well since they are GOD LEVEL creatures). Finally we cover the whole scions of god or scions of angels coming to ...um... fruition for lack of a better term by creating a new stamina knack OR fertility boon called "Immaculate Conception" where any willing subject can receive the divine grace of carrying a scion of God (as all angels are only messengers of god carrying out his will).

            For the record I have no interest in playing a Scion of the Abrahamic Faith but it is not for the same reason as others. First and foremost the game is based on earlier beliefs so we can toss out the notion that the Abrahamic Faith is a monotheistic faith, it was a Monolatry. In other words they didn't believe in just the one god, they just believe that only their one god should be worshiped as only he aided his people and therefore only he should receive their prayer. For more information on this, this is the quick and dirty of it http://booksnthoughts.com/ancient-je...-of-many-gods/ since some folks don't much care to go into in depth reading for a game.

            The above statement and relation of information relating to an older (And fitting more closely to the era in which the other pantheons came from) version of the faith, it would not cause conflict with existing Pantheons as Yahweh's existence does not contradict their own existence nor does it identify him as hostile towards the other gods, only that he is a jealous god (And really...aren't they all?) who is equally on par as a Legend 12 god, just like the other gods.

            My objection to it is that without giving a huge overhaul to the Abrahamic Faith, making angles into gods and what not, you have only a singular god for which you can be the scion of and this can be very boring without having variety.

            The other issue is that of Aten (And the much longer name associated that I can't remember how to spell) and the fact that in every single aspect he is essentially the same being, and to introduce the Abrahamic God, is to negate a Titan and Titan Avatar of another Pantheon, and that doesn't really sit all to kosher with me.
