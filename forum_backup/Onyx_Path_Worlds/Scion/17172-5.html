        Originally posted by Telgar View Post
        See, there are no "Purview Dots" and there's no way to "buy" a Purview by itself. The only things in a Purview are the Boons. No one has "Health 4" or "Fire 8". They're not Disciplines, or Spheres, or Arcanoi or Abilities or whatever. They're categories. The problem is that since there's only one example of each power per level, they don't feel like categories, they feel like Disciplines, Spheres, Pillars, Arcanoi, etc.

        You aren't buying "Health 4", you're buying Cradlesong, a Rank 4 Health Boon. Of which there should be at least three.
        Exactly. And the fact that you have to explain this is the core of the problem. That’s why I was suggesting the removal of anything resembling “Health ••••” from the text, and replacing it with some sort of purchasing requirement. Compare the following:
        CRADLESONG (HEALTH ••••)
        Dice Pool: Manipulation + Medicine
        Cost: 1 Legend (or 1 Legend + 1 Willpower)

        By singing or playing some sweet lullaby, the Scion…
        and:
        CRADLESONG
        Purviews: Health
        Legend Minimum: 4
        Dice Pool: Manipulation + Medicine
        Cost: 1 Legend (or 1 Legend + 1 Willpower)

        By singing or playing some sweet lullaby, the Scion…
        Mechanically, these two entries are identical. But the former looks and feels like one entry in a chain of powers, while the latter doesn’t. Note also that the latter gives you an easy way to specify Boons that are shared by multiple Purviews: I phrased “Purviews” in the plural deliberately.

        Really, there are only three Purviews for which this wouldn’t work: Arete, Mystery, and Prophecy. The common factor? None of those Purviews grant discrete Boons; they just give you more dice. And frankly, “more dice” is boring if that’s all you get. I’ve mentioned elsewhere that I’d like Arete to more closely resemble Epic Attributes, with each pillar of Arete that you purchase coming with Knack-like traits that define particular uses of the Ability where you’re especially capable. I could see a similar approach being used with Mystery and Prophecy.

        Note that the above helps break free of the “chain of powers” mentality without defining multiple Boons per level, which might be an issue given word count constraints. My suggestion of replacing the “Minimum Legend” requirement with a power tier gives another way to break out of that mentality: if Cradlesong, Control Aging, Restore/Wither, and Holy Font/Epidemic are all classed as “Demigod Boons” instead of requiring four, five, six, and seven dots of Legend respectively, the “Chain of Boons” mindset gets shattered beyond all hope of recovery.

        My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                To elaborate a bit on my earlier “limited freeform” comments: something else I’d like to see changed in 2e has to do with your associated Purviews. In particular, I’d like to see a change to the rules for determining your associated Purviews when you achieve godhood. Instead of one’s associated Purviews being determined by which “Avatar expressions” the Scion has achieved, I’d rather see it work the other way around: upon becoming a god, the character gains the ability to designate one or more of the Purviews as being associated with him; and once he achieves maximum Legend, he gains access to the Avatar expressions of these Purviews.

                Obviously, some constraints would need to be put in place as to which Purviews can be associated (and possibly how many can be associated); but if you’re seeking to get away from the “Linear Boons” mentality, you’re going to want to do that anyway. My point is that you shouldn’t have to wait until maximum Legend to define your own associated Purviews: it should become possible as soon as you’re a god.

                This ties in with my “limited freeform” comment because the Avatar expression is essentially freeform access to the Purview turned up to eleven. My thought is that once a god has associated a Purview with himself, he gets freeform access to its Boons, in some manner that invalidates neither his choice to purchase Boons nor the benefits of the Avatar expression of that Purview.

                My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
