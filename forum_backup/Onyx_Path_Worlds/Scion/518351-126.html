                                                    They're pretty much what the KS blurb says, gods dedicated to an area, who oversee and protect it; e.g., Athena is patron of Athens. According to Wikipedia, it was a big thing in Rome's sphere of influence, with each town/city having its own tutelary deity. The Theoi, Devas, Shen and Kami are among the pantheons known to practice it.
