                                                      I guess my issue is virginity always felt very extraneous to Athena's purview. As soon as she got out of the Classical world and realized there was no need to "lose one's reason in the grips of Eros," she pretty much decided her virginity was as useful as tits on a canoe, and was actively impinging on that whole wisdom thing. She's not some sultry sexpot or anything, she just has sex when she wants to, and doesn't when she doesn't. It's not a part of what she represents, and ancient Greeks worshiping her for it, to her, is a bunch of ancient Greeks missing the point of who she is.

                                                      I understand the appeal of a more traditional Athena, or one who chooses to abstain because she has better things to do. I simply prefer one who doesn't feel compelled to tie her sexuality in with her divinity.
