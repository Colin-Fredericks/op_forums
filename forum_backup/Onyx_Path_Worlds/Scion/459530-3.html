            It was a poorly worded sentence, but he was not trying to suggest that those ancient cultures have a simplistic outlook on the passage of time, only to suggest that our widespread idea of space-time as a malleable and bendable thing is pretty modern.

            As he mentioned, it was Industrial Time that contributed heavily to our modern time-discipline; not in depth of complexity but width of exposure. From that we began to see a blossom of people toying with the idea of time (both scientifically and in literature) which leads us to our current conception.

For example, the time bubbles of Tir Na nOg (which he mentioned), the Deva heavens moving at a ratio of 1 Day : 100 Years, Taro losing years visiting Ryujin, Kakudmi losing millions of years visiting Brahma, Rip Van Winkle, King Arthur, Geroldseck, the three Tells, etc.

                        Originally posted by Astoril View Post
                        Sorry to break the star momentum but I have a question about character creation beginning at legend 2 (case never happenned since we always started character legend 3 before). It is said that you can buy epic and purview up to rank 2 at character creation but can you use those rank 2 epic/boon/knack from it while you are still legend 2 or do you have to wait until you go legend 3 with character advancement ?
                        Yes, you absolutely can use Rank 2 Epics and Purviews even at Legend 2. But the option to get Rank 2 Epics and Purviews is only available at character creation -- so once gameplay starts you, cannot buy Rank 2 again until you reach Legend 3.

                        For example -- Mokosits could start the game with Epic Charisa 2 and Epic Perception 1, but he cannot buy Epic Perception 2 until he earns Legend 3. This was done to give new characters more options and diversity.

                                    At the moment, our rendition of the Illusion Purview is including the idea of Dreams. Though it is prone to change until we release it, this helps widen the breath of the Illusion Purview slightly without grossly deviating from the mechanical basis of the Purview. On top of being able to interact with the dreams of others, a Scion who is walking the seldom used path of a Dream Deity could beset others with daydreams altering their perceptions in the waking world to provide a Dream figure a capacity to wield their Dream powers in a normal waking Scene rather than limiting them purely to instances of sleep.

                                    But, of course, this could all change. We currently have a character using Illusion which is giving us a chance to run it through the gauntlet.                                                     Originally posted by Corax View Post
                                                    I would like to know a bit about Titan-God relations, both metaphysical and political. Like are they in an out right war or just two different classes of beings that see things differently? How awkward are family reunions?
                                                    Sure, I can totally talk about that! Or answer any other questions anyone has.

                                                    Okay, right, so. I can only talk for myself here, how I run Titans. I know Aynie, and Iry both run them differently, mostly because we have different structures. For example, I have one cosmological metaphysical mess based on rubber sheets and lead balls, while Iry, and Aynie both have their own with their own metaphors. They both are less needlessly complicated than mine, I have had a lot of time to think about this entirely unnecessary topic though which has resulted in mine being a bit arcane.

                                                    However, we have established a common way to note who is a Titan and who is not. Ultimately, the details can vary slightly Pantheon to Pantheon, just like who is a Major God varies slightly. Distance, either from the Pantheon themselves, or from the mortal religious system is the first big note. If someone has a large amount of worship, and 'hang around' their Pantheon a lot, they probably are not a Titan (there are exceptions to this, but being a-social or anti-social are big Titan markers). Being very powerful is a second feature. If you can single-handedly fight the entire Pantheon, or destroy reality absent-mindedly, you are probably a Titan. If you represent a very core cosmological feature, such as being the physical personification of Order, you are probably a Titan (such as Ma'at). Being imprisoned, literally or not, also is a big feature of Titanhood.

                                                    You do not have to be antagonistic to be a Titan, some Titans are friendly, some Lesser Gods are Evil. However, being Antagonistic does tend to make you more Titan-y, especially if you are cast against the well being of reality (such as Apep).

                                                    Now, with that disclaimer, let's talk about my Titans.

                                                    The line between Titan and God is not recognized in-universe for two reasons. Firstly Titan is a Greek concept, and secondly, even the Greeks break the Scion term Titan into two categories, Titan and Primordial. Further, it is more of a spectrum than a binary situation. Ra is exceptionally Titan-like, for example. The only reason Ra would not be represented as a Titan in our games is because Ra is very close to his Pantheon, and his culture's religious system. Some Pantheons have other terms they use, such as the Hittite or Anatolian Gods having the Primeval Gods, but many Pantheons, such as the Teotl, Tuatha, or Aesir, do not.

                                                    Titans tend to have a different outlook on things than Gods do as well, just as you noted. Since most Titans are a-social or anti-social, they normally have very different outlooks on life than Gods do. For example, Bres is anti-social, he resists his duty as king of the tuath, and attempts to conquer his own people when they entirely legitimately depose him and instate a new king in his place. Bres thinks only about Bres, he wants to be king again, with every fiber of his being. He wants to rule like a petty tyrant, forcing people to bow to his rule, and do whatever he wants. On the other side of the coin, Lir is a-social, he withdrew from the world first in anger he was not chosen to rule the tuath, and then withdrew even further when stuck with the overwhelming sorrow of the events of The Children of Lir. Lir can't function much as a person, he is only sorrow for the time being. Hedammu from the Anatolian Pantheon wants to devour everything, having no real capacity to even understand beauty short of knowing he wants to eat it. Namazu from the Kami does none of these things, not really a 'person,' more a terrifyingly powerful animal.

                                                    Due to these various different perspectives, Titans often clash with their Pantheons. Though, most of them are 'dealt with' for the time being, up until the story begins and Scions almost inevitably break things. When the Titans become active again, they begin following their own agendas. Balor returns to his unnamed home somewhere near the Isle of the Glass Tower, and sends out his raiders to raid or institute taxes as they find new peoples. He would likely direct them to Ireland to start with, and then meander around the Otherworldly Islands, possibly down around the european coastline looking to enrich himself. Cronus escapes Tartarus and is picked up by Prometheus who was already on the lamb from Zeus and the pair (along with probably many other Titans) start causing trouble, or trying to find, help, or bring into being The Son of Zeus. Typhon would just b-line Olympus since he is almost biologically programmed to go try to overthrow Zeus (even though he never can, Fate already has a choice for that act, and it's not Typhon). Ran would continue a good relationship with the Aesir, feasts being held, all the while huge oil tankers in the North Sea would be dragged down into the depths, propellers caught on her net.

                                                    However, it is not always bad, not always bloodshed, or world-ending violence. Politics is important, not everything is a fight.

                                                    Just as I said, Ran has an entirely reasonable relationship with the Aesir. The Irish are related to many of their Titans, and both function within the same cultural system, so a Scion of Manannan (specifically chosen because they have no Fomori blood ties, someone with blood relations would be invited in with cheers as long as they had not proven themselves an enemy to the Fomori already) who was a Bard would be invited into Balor's home, just as the Tuatha would invite a Fomori Bard into their own homes. Hecate, Helios, Eos, and Selene are all Titans who are allied with the Theoi, and while they may be... difficult to talk to, being mad, or frightfully powerful, they would be welcome on Olympus.

                                                    The biggest, and most difficult thing for the Pantheons most of the time, as you said, is very awkward family gatherings. It pretty much all comes down to individual relationships. A Tuatha family gathering would involve a lot of shouting, wild accusations, and people getting handsy under the table. But, really, that's not out of the ordinary for the Tuatha. People would die, but, again, normal. It might not even be a Fomori v. Tuatha thing, Lugh and Aengus might just start trying to stab each other to death. There is actually a perpetual family gathering for the Hittite Pantheon, with the Primeval Gods not exactly sealed away, but permanent guests of Allani, the Sun Goddess of the Underworld. Feasts are held, everyone is seemingly alright with the situation.

                                                    However, while I do love politics, it wouldn't last that long for some Titans. If Izanami escaped, things would get bad fast. If Fujin and Raijin's conversion lapsed, things would devolve into violence quickly. If Hahhima returned, the Thousand Gods of Hatti would be in a panic to track down The Sun God again before all of creation froze solid.

                                                    There is one last note that I would make. Titans are powerful, very, very powerful. Normally however this is quite narrow. For example, Bres is beautiful. Bres is more beautiful than a Legend 12 Epic Appearance Deity. Just looking at Bres is not a great idea. Celtic Mythology loves love at first sight (and loves even more love before I even knew you existed) so you might find yourself in love with him, and in a Wasting Sickness. However, Bres is not very physically strong, or smart. In my Titan lists, they have a few Purviews and Epic Attributes listed. Those are ones they should be on par with a Legend 12 God in, if not better. The one that is bolded, in that specific Purview or Epic Attribute, they are leaps and bounds beyond Gods in most cases.

                                                    I think I rambled on here for about an hour, I don't know if I addressed your question exactly. Let me know if you want any more specific details Corax! If you want to know about a specific Pantheon, or specific Titan, I could go into the nitty gritty. And if you have any different questions Havoc, just let me know!
