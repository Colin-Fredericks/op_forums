                        (sorry, no page numbers; I'm reading the pdf on my Kindle right now)

                        Related to powers, Relics can do two things:
                        a) Give you access to a Purview (through the Relic's Motif)
                        b) Give you access to a custom Knack that emulates a Boon

                        If a Relic grants access to a Purview which the character already has as Innate, using the Relic reduces relevant Complications by 1 per dot of the Relic's rating.

                        So if a Scion of Eshu has the Twofold Guise (grants Deception), Assess Attitude rolls from other characters are always at +3 Complication when he is wearing it.
                        He can learn and use False History, convincing someone that their memory is false, if they look at it from another perspective. This roll is a Clash, Manipulation+Legend vs. his target's Resolve+Legend.

                        Until a scion of Anansi comes along and steals his hat. He immediately loses access to the Purview, but he still knows the Boon. If he manages to steal the Scion of Anansi's spider pendant later (a great short term goal), he gets access to the Boon again, but he has to spend Momentum equal to the pendant's rating when he uses it (until it becomes Fatebound to him).

                        A different Scion of Eshu could have a different item (let's say it's a pocketwatch) that gives no Purview access, but it lets him hypnotize a target and rewrite memories (basically, False History as a custom Relic Knack). The roll would be different - instead of using the character's stats, I would rule that the roll would be a value related to the power of the relic, to represent the power imbued in the Relic during its creation. When this gets stolen, he loses the power completely. If it were to get destroyed, he would have to quest for another relic with the same properties. This is a great long term goal.

                                        I feel like it really depends on how much the "learn X boon Y purview instead" knack costs compared to access to a purview, unfortunately, all three of the relics with the knack have multiple variable features, making deducing the exact cost difficult. If the learned boon is lost without the relic, than it should cost LESS than raw access to a purview, because ultimately you are gaining access to a single boon, rather than an entire purview's worth of boons... and a whole new type of marvel... and a motif. Alternatively, IF you DO NOT lose the boon when losing the relic, then it should cost MORE, because while it is still fewer total boons... you are getting to pick and choose, and getting the potential for inherent access to a boon, which is scarce at Hero tier.

                                        Like I said... it's unfortunate that this particular unique knack is not super easy to deduce directly, because of the multiple variables... however a RANGE of potential values can be determined

                                        Tezcatlipoca only has two variables that don't have clearly defined point values: Blurt it Out as a Fortune Boon and Remote Viewing

                                        For Tezcatlipoca's Mirror, the MAXIMUM cost is
                                        2(Fortune Purview)+X(Blurt it Out as Fortune)+1(Remote Viewing Knack)-3(Tension Pool On Each Use)=3(Listed Price)

                                        X=3

                                        While for the same relic the MINIMUM cost is
                                        2(Fortune Purview)+Y(Blurt it Out as Fortune)+3(Remote Viewing Knack)-3(Tension Pool On Each Use)=3(Listed Price)
                                        Y=1

                                        HOWEVER, this one's description for the USE of the boon implies that it requires the relic to work, because the information is displayed on the mirror itself. So, either they ALL require the relic to work, or this relic uses the cheaper version that DOES require the relic to work. Either way, we can deduce that the version that DOES require the relic to work, which MAY be the ONLY version, costs somewhere between 1 and 3 dots.... the thing is... it really should cost Equal-to-or-less-than just plain access to a new purview, and straight up remote viewing is MUCH more valuable than 1-dot, so that pretty much narrows it down to 2 or 1

                                        Designated Constellation is a bit more complicated, because it has THREE undefined variables: Muse's Kiss as a Stars Boon, The ability to send a message through the constellation, AND the flaw that you can only use the relic when it is in the sky visibly.

                                        For Designated Constellation, the MAXIMUM cost is
                                        4(Fortune and Stars Purview)+X(Muse's Kiss as Stars)+1(Send Message)-5(Only Usable When Visible)=4(Listed Price)
                                        X=4

                                        Now, I'm working under the assumption that sending a message can't possibly be more expensive than acess to a whole purview, and losing access to the relic for at-least all daylight must be worth at-least 3
                                        Under those those assumptions, for Designated Constellation, the MINIMUM cost is
                                        4(Fortune and Stars Purview)+y(Muse's Kiss as Stars)+2(Send Message)-3(Only Usable When Visible)=4(Listed Price)
                                        y=1

                                        So, whichever version of the knack that Designated constellation uses must be valued between 1 and 4

                                        Now, The Flaming Serpent has a whopping 4 not clearly costed variables: Blinding Glory as a Fire Boon, Shooting Aggrivated fire bolts that can get ENHANCEMENT rather than bonus-dice from momentum, the ability to add the Shockwave tag to Heaven's fire, and the flaw of not being able to back down from a fight once using the boon. Honestly, I'm not even going to wager a guess at the value within that relic.


                                        My best tummy-feels estimation based on what information I DO have tells me that
                                        A: the version with which you DO lose the boon without the relic should probably cost 1
                                        B: the version with which you do NOT lose the boon should probably cost 3 or 4... if it even exists at all
