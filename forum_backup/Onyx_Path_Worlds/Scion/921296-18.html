    I had an idea for an antagonist that I've kicked around for a while now. A Scion of Shiva who's kind of an angry extremist. It started with just the officially renounced but still often practiced caste system, which he was born on the low end of, and his method of resolving this issue by targeting people who he sees as enforcing it and executing them in some public way. After one too many such executions (and his habit of using an axe, like some barbarian, and hardly ever a ranged weapon) he got exiled from India, but he's broadened his scope to generally trying to tear down corrupt systems and kill tyrants in whatever form he sees them. He's grown more cunning and more adept at deception over the years, but his plans all end the same way: Lots of blood and fire.

                        These characters were not naturally born Scions, but rather chosen for the role as a reward for meeting other criteria.


                        -------------------------------


                        A modern descendant of Chiron, who's chaste and pious life style is virtually unheard of among her fellow Centaurs. Having been pleased by her purity, Artemis chooses to bestow her favor upon her. Under her patron's continued guidance, the young Centaur goes on to become a virgin Goddess of Innocence. Her cultists prefer to live in secluded communities, where they can avoid the coruptive influences of the outside world.



                        An obsessive historian, who will brave any danger if it leads to uncovering the secrets of the past. Such dedication to studying ancient lore earns him the favor of Thoth, who leads him on the path to becoming a God of Lost Knowledge. If you're looking for some obscure artifact or historical document from a bygone era, then chances are he has the original copy stashed away on his barge somewhere.



                        A traveling martial artist, who's fighting style is adapted from techniques he learned while studying under monsters. Impressed by his fearless dedication to the art of combat, he earns the favor of Nezha, who points him in the direction of increasingly more difficult opponents. Ultimately, his training leads to him becoming a demonic God of Battle, who's followers include those who wish to learn more than just 'self defense' techniques.

                                                    Possibly my favorite PC:

                                                    I once played a scion of a death God that had a set of followers/creatures in the form of zombies tattooed into the skin of his back. He had the ability to summon the zombies at will, but they didn't follow more than the most basic of orders and couldn't distinguish between friend and foe, instead attacking anyone nearby without the Death purview.
                                                    In his first quest he vowed to avenge the death of a small Mexican girl, whose ghost followed him throughout the quest. Hilariously, he spoke no Spanish and she didn't know English while she was alive, so all communication was through over-the-top pantomime. He actually did avenge her, destroying the Death cult on the Mexican coast that was brutally sacrificing innocent mortal children, and rather than move to her own Afterlife, she chose to stay at his side, becoming a faithful Fatebound companion.
                                                    Most of his life was devoted to avenging the restless dead this way, putting to rest souls and ghosts the world over. He became particularly strong in the Justice and Guardian purviews, which supplemented his Death boons and his Epic Strength and Stamina surprisingly well. His companion, the ghost girl, grew in power along with him, gaining the ability to channel the effects of his Death boons for him, as well as the ability to restrain and even dominate weaker spirits.
                                                    He and his band were the most successful my group ever played, and we actually got them to Apotheosis, but stopped there because the system was so broken at that level. My character actually ended up becoming the God of Avenging the Innocent, with maxed out Death, Justice, and Guardian purviews.

                                                    I know this is a full character and not a concept, but I wanted to share, because of the different and unusual ideas and concepts that went into him. I helped my ST with the actual mechanics of most of his powers, so let me know if you're curious!
