    2E Confussion about inverosimile setting

    04-09-2022, 09:00 AM
    Greetings,


    I have read the mechanical details on the Purviews and Knacks sections of Scion, but I am really at fault for barely skimming the world lore. I ask the forums to explain to me:
    1. Are the gods publicly known and revered?
      I keep finding random paragraphs about secrecy and history being lost (how if you can ask your gods about things that happened?) but also that traffic controllers need to coordinate heaven residences and Chinese air traffic, that Lanka exist and government tried to invade, that some creatures have a public social network, etc.
    2. How does the setting explain the fact that history could have not gone the same if gods exist?
      This is the part that seems the least consistent to me. Some nations would not exist at all, some buildings, monuments, people, technology would have arrived (if at all) through different channels.
    3. What role, if any does monotheism play on the setting?
      I see the Americas were conquered by the Spaniards... But some of the things the Spaniard did (e.g. suppressing "heathen" religions) would make no sense if the native deities exist and intervene. The mere existence of SPAIN as a nation will make little sense if the Theoi were active in the Roman Empire.

      Christianity-influenced thought played a large role in European science during centuries, some historical and social issues are Christianity derived: homosexuality decried as a sin, having as many children as you physically could, the Crusades and the partition of Arab territories, "evangelizing" the Americas, modern day Americans self image and voting patterns, etc.

    Essentially, I find the mechanical part interesting, but the setting as a whole lacking verisimilitude. It requires some heavy mental gymnastics to pretend the deities exist in some form and intervene at some level, while having a world that looks like current day Earth plus Scions.

    Again, this might be me misinterpreting and there might be a set of good explanations, that I am missing, but I have yet to find those.

    Thanks for your time and attention,

                                              As I understand it, “Sky Father” isn't a Mantle; it's a Title. “Zeus” is a Mantle.

                                              The difference, as I understand it, is that a Mantle is a god's position within a given Pantheon; when you take a Mantle, you become part of that Pantheon, and whoever had that Mantle before doesn't have it anymore. By contrast, a Title is more of a property of a Mantle: take the Mantle, and you get all of its Titles. On the other hand, while this makes a given Title unique within a Pantheon, a Title isn't unique to a Pantheon: multiple Pantheons can have their own Mantles that include a “Sky Father”, with no conflict.

                                              But yes, a lot of religious conflict takes the form of appropriating Mantles: “your goddess Brigid is actually our Saint Brigitte”, or some such. Mind you, this isn't unique to Christianity or Islam; the Shen, for instance, exist largely as they do due to extensive appropriation of local gods into a “greater whole”. Indeed, it might be said that the Shen are to China as Christianity is to Europe and Islam is to the Middle East, and not be too far off the mark.

                                              Which brings up another point: the Abrahamic faiths are dominant… in the West and in the Middle East. But how dominant are they elsewhere? I'm pretty sure that the Shen, Kami, and Deva's respective religions haven't been crushed under the boot of the Abrahamic faiths; and the Orisha might be in a similar position, for all I know (as opposed to the Loa, who wouldn't exist without Christianity, and are arguably a subversion of the aforementioned appropriations).
