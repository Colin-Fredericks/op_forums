                79) Cool guys don't look at explosions, they create them! There is a Scion who has ambitions on becoming a god of war some day, but believes such things as swordplay and honorable accord are the byproducts of ancient civilization and no longer relevant in today's society. In the modern world, warfare is all about destruction on a massive scale. The deadlier the weapon, the more valuable it becomes. Their approach to the Titan war favors the Scorched Earth strategy, which does seem to eliminate the targets, but causes substantial damage to the environment in the process. The gods are getting nervous, if this scion manages to ascend to divinity, their destructive tactics could lead to widespread damage of the Overworld. Destroying the heavens just to keep them out of the Titan's hands is not an option many would agree with and so they send you to negotiate a more humane approach to combat. If they prove uncooperative to such a bargain, you have authority to respond appropriately to this god blooded terrorist.

                    80) Bad news, Loki has a new grandson. Worse news, his father is Fenrir who you're pretty sure was still tied up last time anybody checked. The Scion has proven to be every bit as strong as you might expect from such a heritage, which would normally make them a worthy ally in the fight against the Titans. Except that like the rest of the Aesir, they desire a way to avoid the trappings of Fate. In this particular case, they plan to assassinate Vidar and thus prevent the death of Fenrir in the time of Ragnarok!

                    Is this Scion acting alone, or do they have allies to facilitate their plan? But more importantly, how did Fenrir conceive a Scion without anybody noticing and does Loki have some hand in this? Somebody had to give them their visitation and the list of suspects is quite small!

                                83) Eccentric, it's how the upper class refer to one of their own who suffers from mental instability. So when the chief of your respective pantheon calls you up and says your 'eccentric' cousin is off their meds, it can't be a good time for anybody involved. Over the last several days, there have been reports of raining frogs, bloody fountains, an epidemic of lice and wild animals wandering into town attacking people. Every day your cousin appears before the mayor and orders him to release his slaves, which the mayor publicly denies having any idea what he's talking about. The story is popular enough that you can tell how this is going to play out if left unchecked. You decide to intervene, but is your cousin really just in need of help, or is there more going on in the boardroom than the mayor reveals?


                                84) Pranking a prankster is always fun. But somebody has decided to steal all of Sun Wukong's private banana stash, leaving a sign explaining they've been requisitioned by "King K Rool." Needless to say, the monkey king is not pleased at somebody stealing from his personal belongings, and has asked for your help in tracking down the culprit. The first step is figuring out why the Sun Wukong's realm is seeing an influx of crocodiles instead of primates.


                                85) A recently slain Scion of Hades was improperly buried without coin to pay for Charon's passage. Rather than be stranded on the other side of the River Styx for eternity, they turned around and walked back to the world of the living, bringing a host of lingering spirits with them. Now, the city is overrun with ghosts and specters looking to celebrate their come back, or having difficulty adjusting to the modern era. The city is in chaos and the mortals are terrified out of their minds. The Dodekatheon needs a band of Heroes to bust these ghosts and send the Scion back to the underworld once and for all!

                                        87) If you want to become truly legendary, start by proving you're the greatest among your peers. A Scion has declared a world wide tournament to prove who is the greatest fighter on the planet. The tournament is reserved for battle oriented Heroes and the rules appear simple enough. The fight continues until one opponent surrenders or is unable to continue the fight. The prize for winning each match is a noticeable increase to one's popularity, which is always good to have. But the grand prize for winning the whole tournament is elevation to the rank of Demigod!

                                        Warrior Scions all over the world have registered to compete, eager to rise through the ranks in the only way they feel comfortable, by beating the crap out of their rivals!
