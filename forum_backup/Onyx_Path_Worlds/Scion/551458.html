                          Trying to judge the differences between the Tiers with just this is a bit impossible from everything the devs have said. Even then, you're not really being fair to how even these base mechanics impact conflicts between Tiers.

                          We know the TN changes with Tier. Anyone experienced with the cWOD can tell you how major this is. A character rolling against a TN of 8 vs. a TN of 6 is at a massive disadvantage. Consider for a moment that 6 dice at TN 6 is better than 10 dice at TN 8 with the mechanics we know about so far without taking into account all the other widgets and dials in the game. A maxed out mundane needs more than dice if they want to go up against a higher tier PC that's only a bit above average in the same area.

                          So without any of the many, many other things that could impact a contest between two characters, high tiers have massive advantages even without major differences in dice. In my mind, that's a good thing. Having to have massive dice pools, or manage huge numbers of extra successes to differential tiers was extremely clunky in comparison.

                          The fact that Granny might be able to hit Atlas also doesn't mean she can actually effect Atlas. This isn't the nWOD where successful attack rolls with a shotgun mean a minimum of 4L damage. Granny still had to do with with the Difficulty of the roll, any Complications, and putting what Effects she manages to have after that into play.

                          Tiers are SFX on the basic dice mechanics and Attributes. They're also going to be a lot more. Remember we're being teased with a core system that Scion will be one game line built off of, not the new Scion system in full. Even with the Sardonyx core system fully spoiler where we get to see things like Scale, Intensity, and the new combat systems, we still aren't looking at the specifics that Scion uses.

                          ---------

                          Can we also not push for some sort of weird quasi-edition war regarding the nWOD? If you want to discuss the pros and cons of the Sardonyx Attribute spreads, lets do that. Not just bash the nWOD and claim that's a critique of what Sardonyx is doing.
