    Berserk Conversion: The Idea of Evil

    06-05-2016, 11:23 PM
    The Idea of Evil:

    "Greater Titan of Suffering"

    Forbidden Purviews: Sun, Order, Guardian

    Details: A great deal of mystery surrounds the being known as The Idea of Evil. Even the very nature of its existence is debatable. Some believers speculate that it may be a corrupted God, while others propose the idea that it is something else completely. To hear The Idea of Evil explain its own origins, it claims that it was given life by the Law of Causality; the belief that everything happens for a reason. In ancient times, people could not accept the idea of misfortune happening without cause, something had to exist that was actively making people suffer. And from this belief, The Idea of Evil came to be; an immensely powerful titan who's purpose was to give a reason for mankind's suffering.

    If The Idea of Evil were to be slain, it would usher in chaos throughout the multiverse. Unprovoked feelings of hatred and sorrow would fill the hearts of men and women, driving them to spontaneous acts of despair, envy and bloodlust. For the sake of everyone's sanity, The Idea of Evil must be preserved, no matter how much its victims may wish to do otherwise.


    The Idea of Evil is a patient and calculating being, which leads further credence to the idea that it may not truly be a titan. It is a consummate wielder of the Prophecy purview, allowing it to see events hundreds of years before they happen. At times, The Idea of Evil has actively worked to make these events come to pass. Even going so far as to orchestrate selective breeding among mortals, so that one day a descendant with the proper qualifications to fulfill the prophecy will be born into the World. With its ability to see the future, it can detect threats before they even manifest, and adjust its plans accordingly. Because of this, it is very rare that The Idea of Evil makes a mistake, and those capable of defying it are truly legendary.


    Unlike the rest of its peers, The Idea of Evil does not typically create its own minions. Instead, it has mass produced a Relic known as the Behelit, which allows mortals to become demons (called Apostles) in exchange for sacrificial offerings. It is unknown how many demons The Idea of Evil has created in this manner, but estimates put the number somewhere in the thousands.

    The Idea of Evil's realm is an abyssal plane who's landscape is formed out of the corpses of the damned, crammed together so that only their faces remain visible. Even more disturbing is the fact that some of these faces are arranged in such a fashion as to resemble human body parts, such as giant heads or outstretched arms. Despite resembling festering corpses, the heads are still functional, wailing in agony and gnashing their teeth. Merely gazing upon this hellish sight is enough to send ordinary mortals into delirious insanity.

    The Idea of Evil itself takes the form of a gigantic black heart large enough to blot out the sky with its scale. It is capable of levitation and communicates through telepathy with its servants. This form, however, is rarely seen in person, and it instead shows itself through the use of its avatars.


    -----------------------------------------------

    Avatars of Suffering: The God Hand

    Details: Only a single human every 216 years is chosen to possess the crimson Behelit. Those who are worthy of this honor become more than simple demons, they become avatars for The Idea of Evil's power. Known to others as The God Hand, they possess abilities that rival the beings for which they are named after.

    The God Hand go by a variety of identities depending on who you're talking to. In some communities, they are considered to be a form of demon, while others claim them to be a form of angel. However, there are also cults and religious institutions who worship them as pagan deities, performing any number of profane rituals to satisfy them. Whatever it is they are, the God Hand serve as The Idea of Evil's most powerful creations.

    The full power of a God Hand member is almost impossible to calculate. Described as being "reality warpers" on more than one occasion, The God Hand exist on the astral plane. As such, they can not be permanently slain by attacks which exist in the physical realm. Only a weapon capable of affecting the astral realm can inflict permanent harm upon the God Hand, but even then, it would be a legendary battle for the ages.

    At present, there exists 5 members of The God Hand, one for each finger, plus the thumb.



    Void: Presumably the first of The God Hand, Void is often the one who speaks about Prophecy and Fate, leading others to believe that he is their leader. He is also the one who places the Brand of Sacrifice upon those offered up by a Behelit user. He is a being of immense height, standing almost 10 times taller than a normal man, but is also horribly withered to the point of atrophy. His exposed brain comprises his entire cranium while his face is pulled into a skeletal snarl. His eyes are sewn shut and he speaks telepathically. He is draped in a long, black cloak that covers him from the neck downward.

    As an avatar of suffering, Void appears to be the primary avatar for The Idea of Evil. He is often seen presiding over religious ceremonies and professing The Idea of Evil's machinations to its demonic servants. He has also demonstrated advanced skill with psychopomp, being capable of teleportation and dimensional rifts.


    Lady Slan: The only current female member of The God Hand. Lady Slan resembles a seductively beautiful woman with flowing green hair and wearing a corset as her only form of clothing. She also possesses a pair of great, black wings which can shift between angelic or bat-like on her whim. These wings are strong enough to produce hurricane force winds upon flapping them.

    As an avatar of suffering, Lady Slan appears to represent corruption. She is a euphoric entity who derives great pleasure from the emotional torment of lesser beings, like some form of psychic vampirism. She is also greatly popular among both male and female demons, who excitedly chant her name when she appears before them.


    Ubik: The most excitable and chatty member of The God Hand. Ubik is described as having pale blue skin and resembles a fat man of dwarfish stature. He wears a pair of spectacles which have been sewn into his skin, permanently hiding his eyes behind black tinted lenses. Ubik does not appear to have normal legs, but instead has a multitude of fin-like appendages hanging down from his waist. Despite this, he has no trouble getting around, as he appears to be capable of flying for indefinite periods of time.

    As an avatar of suffering, Ubik represents desperation. He appears to be in charge of recruiting demons for The Idea of Evil, visiting mortals in disguise and presenting them with their Behelit. After doing so, he uses his powers to subtly encourage the mortal to perform the sacrifice and join the ranks of evil.


    Conrad: Among his fellow God Hand members, Conrad is perhaps the least pleasant to be around. He is described as being a hideously fat man with a body like that of a mealworm. His skin is bright red, and his face is perpetually contorted into an expression with clenched eyes and puckered lips. His clothing is nightmarish to behold, as it appears to be sewn together from human faces, their expressions frozen into states of agony. His voice is high pitched and sounds faintly melodic. He's not much of a talker, but when he chooses to communicate, he does so by screaming everything he says. His voice can reach volumes high enough to cause pain to unprotected mortal ears.

    As an avatar of suffering, Conrad represents disease and filth. He has an affinity for rats, and uses them to spread plagues and pestilence across the World. At times, he has also been known to manifest himself as a giant swarm of rats, instead of appearing in his more humanoid form.


    Femto: The newest and youngest member of the God Hand, Femto was the last mortal to possess the crimson Behelit. Events of his life correlate him to being a Christ-like figure. He was born a peasant, but rose to greatness, only to be cast down again and brutally tortured by a wrathful king. Yet he emerged from his ordeal to take his place at God's (The Idea of Evil's) side. Femto resembles a human male with androgynous beauty wearing a suit of black armor that covers him from head to toe. The helmet is shaped like the head of a hawk, with his face resting inside its beak. His feet are shaped like talons, and he wears a black cape that can function like a pair of wings to grant him flight.

    As an avatar of suffering, Femto represents longing (greed, ambition, lust). Since he was mortal, he has always wanted to rule his own kingdom and he is willing to sacrifice anything (or anyone) to achieve that dream. Although he may feel remorse for his actions, he will never apologize for them; believing that the moment he does so, he will lose the strength to pursue his dreams.


    -------------------------------------------------------


    Behelit (Relic 5): A Behelit (pronounced Bay-lit) is a mass produced relic that is exclusively unique to The Idea of Evil and its followers. The Behelit is described as having a texture similar to that of a potato, and is actually edible. But anyone who tries to eat one will suffer crippling indigestion for the rest of their life. Behelits are commonly green, brown or yellow in color. However, there also exists a single red Behelit, which is known as the Egg of the King. Only a single mortal every 216 years is worthy to possess this particular Behelit.

    Each Behelit is destined for a specific owner, and once they come into physical possession of it, they are immediately Fate Bound. Once this happens, they can not be permanently seperated from the Behelit until they have invoked its power. If they try to do so, the Behelit will mysteriously find its way back to them.

    The Behelit's power allows a human to become a demon (Apostle) in service to The Idea of Evil. But in order to invoke this power, the human must sacrifice at least one person to whom they have developed an emotional attachment. The emotion can be positive or negative, but it must be strong enough to qualify as a bond; moments of passion (such as anger) are not enough to achieve the transformation. When activated, the Behelit's facial features assemble into a proper face, at which point it will scream in agony and begin crying blood. This grants the Behelit's user an audience with the God Hand. Once the ritual is performed and they become a demon, the Behelit is no longer bound to them, and may pass into the possession of another destined owner.

    The point of the sacrificial ritual is to harden the Apostle's heart upon becoming a demon, but this is not guaranteed to take effect. Some Apostles such as The Count retain their human emotions, even after they sacrifice their loved ones.

    Assuming they receive the proper Behelit, any mortal can use them to become a demon. This includes Scions, but only if they are Heroes or Demigods. Scions who ascend to Godhood are no longer mortals and thus can not activate its power.

    An important secret about the Behelit is that it is not limited to only a single use. Indeed, an Apostle can use their Behelit additional times in order to grow even more powerful than they already are. However, this is considered to be difficult to achieve. Most Apostles don't have the ability to form emotional bonds with others once they use the Behelit the first time, and with the relic no longer bound to them by Fate, it is harder for them to keep in their possession.


    ------------------------------------------


    Apostle Template: When a mortal uses a Behelit to sacrifice somebody they have an emotional bond with, they become an Apostle in service to The Idea of Evil. Apostles are considered to be a form of demon and are commonly referred to as such. However, they are not a unique species, but rather they are a template of supernatural being.

    Every Apostle is unique in their own way. Their demonic state is meant to be a spiritual reflection of their inner self, no two Apostles look the same. This gives Story Tellers freedom to custom design their own demons when running a campaign in which Apostles will be present. However, because it is a template, there are some features which all Apostles share with one another.


    1. Upon becoming an Apostle, the human automatically gains 2 dots of Legend. A minuscule increase for ordinary humans, but a giant improvement for those who are already Legendary. In some cases, this increase can push Scions to their next stage of development!


    2. Apostles have a limited ability to shapeshift, being able to change between their true demonic form, and a more subtle human disguise. While in human form, they still retain some unnatural features to their body, but these can be obfuscated with proper effort.


    3. Upon becoming an Apostle, the human gains 2 normal ranks and 1 Epic rank in any two attributes of their choice. Common choices include Strength, Stamina and Appearance. The increase to Epic attribute also comes with a free Knack.


    4. When an Apostle changes into their demon form, all their Appearance modifies become Negative Appearance, regardless of how attractive they look in human form.


    5. Apostles have supernatural longevity, allowing them to live forever unless they suffer critical damage.


    6. The sacrificial offering needed to become an Apostle replaces at least two of their Virtues with Dark Virtues. The other two virtues have a 50% chance to change as well. Story Tellers and players can decide this by flipping a coin, rolling a die, or whatever other method of random chance they desire.


    ----------------------------------------------


    Brand of Sacrifice (Relic 3): This cursed relic is unique to those who have fallen victim to The Idea of Evil's servants. It is bestowed upon those who are offered up as a sacrifice by those wishing to become Apostles. The brand is a three pronged fork in which two of the prongs cross through the middle of the center prong before splitting upward. Most who receive the Brand of Sacrifice are killed shortly afterward, but a rare few Legendary victims manage to escape and fight back.

    However, the Brand of Sacrifice is a cursed relic, and those who carry it are forever damned. Once branded, every piece of the victim down to their very last drop of blood is property for the forces of evil. Every night from sunset to sunrise, evil is drawn to them like a beacon. If they can not survive until dawn, they will be be tortured, eaten, raped, killed, or whatever other atrocities their assailants have planned for them.

    But that's not the worst of it; those who carry the Brand of Sacrifice are condemned even beyond death. Upon their demise, their souls are dragged into hell to become part of the abyssal realm of The Idea of Evil. A most tragic fate for those who associate with Apostles.

      Historical Apostles: The following is a list of some Apostles who have appeared in human history.


      Nosferatu Zodd: One of the strongest Apostles ever known, he used a Behelit because he wanted to become the strongest warrior in the world. Once he got his wish, however, he soon realized that fighting had grown boring with no worthy opponents to face him. Mortals gave him the title "Nosferatu" because of his alleged immortality. Legends say he had lived for over 300 years on the battlefield. Zodd showed great respect towards any fighter capable of challenging him, but was quickly enraged by those too weak to fight. After Femto begins to recreate the mercenary band he sacrificed, Zodd became one of his highest ranking officers.

      In his human form, Zodd possessed a gigantic figure, standing half again the size of most grown men. He also had bright red eyes and sharp teeth. He was substantially larger in his demon form, growing nearly five times that of his human disguise. His demon form resembled an anthropomorphic lion with red fur, thick horns, cloven hooves and a pair of retractable wings capable of sustaining him in flight.


      The Count: A deeply religious man of noble standing, The Count would regularly take part in crusades to wipe out heretics from his lands. One day, however, he returned from his crusades to find his own wife participating in a ritualistic orgy in front of a pagan statue. In a blind rage, he slaughtered the entire cult; only managing to stop himself before delivering the killing blow to his wife. His grief caused him to accidentally invoke his Behelit and sacrifice his wife to The God Hand, becoming an Apostle against his will. Even after he became a demon, he continued with his crusades, although was far more vicious in doing so. When confronted by a wandering Hero, The Count attempted to use his Behelit again to gain the power to defeat him, but was told by the God Hand that the only thing he had left to sacrifice was his own daughter. The Count was unwilling to sacrifice her to save himself, resulting in his death at the hands of his enemy.

      The Count's human form was a bald headed, obese man dressed in the noble finery of his era. His demon form was that of a gigantic slug with a second mouth underneath his head. His head still appeared like that of his human form, but had a pair of whip-like tentacles growing from it.


      Rosine: A little girl who became obsessed with elves after hearing fairy tales about them. She adored them so much, she wished to become one, and the God Hand offered her the chance to do so. After sacrificing her own parents, The God Hand turned her into a twisted mockery of what she imagined elves to be.

      In her human form, Rosine's appearance resembles that of a fairy. She has the body of a young girl in the early stages of puberty. A large pair of gossamer wings grows from her head and appears to have fused with her ears. A pair of antennae grow from her forehead, while her eyes are insect-like, being a solid purple with no pupils or irises. She also chooses to remain completely nude, even in public. While in human form, her scalp, biceps and upper back also display bug-like fur instead of human hair. When in her demon form, Rosine transforms into a gigantic moth with her torso grafted in place of the creature's head.


      Egg-Shaped Apostle: Born hideously deformed, the Egg-Shaped Apostle was vilified by the rest of humanity his entire life. He is unique among the Apostles because he chose to sacrifice himself to the God Hand in order to become a demon. In his own words, he would "sacrifice his entire world" so that the next one could be born. He eventually died acting as a catalyst for Femto to be reborn into the physical world.

      While in their demon form, the Egg-Shaped Apostle resembled a giant crimson Behelit with a pair of sinewy legs and tentacles for arms. Their human form, if they even had one anymore, remains unknown.


      Irvine: A master archer who served in Femto's new mercenary band after he became one of the God Hand. His skill with a bow was so great that he could fire multiple arrows simultaneously without losing accuracy. His bow was also capable of firing arrows with enough force to decapitate his victims. The circumstances for him becoming a demon are unknown.

      While in human form, irvine appears almost completely human with the exception of having solid white eyes and carrying around a bow with a large eye on the hand guard. In his demon form, he has a human torso grafted to the hind quarters of a giant wolf. The wolf has a pair of large horns which acts as his new bow, firing arrows the size of spears as he races across the field of battle. The eye on his bow appears on the wolf's forehead, indicating that his bow may be part of his body.


      Ganishka: A mercilessly tyrannical king who was nearly assassinated by his own son in an attempt to stage a coup. Heavily wounded and poisoned, Ganishka managed to use a Behelit to become an Apostle, sacrificing his traitorous son in the process. His desire for becoming a demon was that he would never again feel pain inflicted upon him by mortal hands. After becoming a demon, Ganishka openly rebels against the God Hand, using his new demonic powers to satisfy his personal ambitions.

      In his human form, Ganishka resembles a bearded man of Turkish or Arabian descent with light blue irides and blackened sclerae. His mouth is unusually wide and has sharp teeth. In his demon form, Ganishka transforms into a giant made out of living fog. While in this state, his gaseous body became invulnerable to physical attacks, giving him a form of invincibility similar to that of the God Hand!


      Grunbeld: A legendary knight who is said to have singlehandedly defended a country against 3,000 soldiers. He eventually became a commanding officer in Femto's recreated mercenary band. The circumstances for him becoming a demon remain unknown.

      In his human form, Grenbeld appears to be a large man who dresses in armor depicting the image of a dragon. His weapons of choice included a war hammer and a shield which was capable of expelling flames at his command. In his demon form, he resembles a European dragon covered in stalactites with his human face grafted to the front of the dragon's nose. The scales of his dragon form were said to be strong as steel, making him extremely difficult to injure. While in his demon form, he was also capable of exhaling fire, similar to the shield he carried in human form.



      Snake Baron: A sadistic nobleman who used his Behelit in order to conquer a town and usurp it's Lord Mayor. He then proceeded to eat the prisoners that were held captive in the dungeons. The Snake Baron was eventually killed by a wandering Hero who shot him in the neck with a canon, sliced his torso in two, fired a crossbow repeatedly into his face, and finally buried him under a pile of flaming rubble!

      In his human form, the Snake Baron resembled a man with pale white skin, serpentine eyes and sharp teeth wearing a suit of armor baring the image of a cobra. In his demon form, he took on the shape of a giant, anthropomorphic cobra with claws on the ends of his hands and feet. His human face was fused with the inside of the snake's maw and a forked tongue flailed in and out of his gaping mouth. While in demon form, he appeared to gain the power to speak telepathically.



      Wild: Once a feeble old man who despaired over the loss of his youth and vigor, Wild desired a second chance at life so that he could once again enjoy all the worldly pleasures it had to offer. With the aid of The God Hand, he got his wish, transforming him from a decrepit mortal into a mighty barbarian with the strength to take on an entire army of soldiers! He was later killed in battle by Zodd, who casually proceeded to rip him in half.

      In his human form, Wild appears similar to Zodd in that he is a large man with powerful muscles and glowing eyes. His demon form is much different, however, appearing as a gigantic ape-like creature. An enormous mouth is located in the center of his chest, and the torso of his human form is grafted in place of where the creature's head should be. While in his demon form, he gains three additional eyes, two of which are located in the front of his shoulders, and the third being in the abdomen of his human torso.



      The Female Apostle: This unidentified woman who has since come to be known simply as "the female apostle" exhibited succubi-like tendencies and derived great pleasure from killing men while performing sexual acts with them. She was said to be extremely vain about her appearance, to the point where she believed no man on earth could resist her charms. She was later slain by a wandering Hero when she tried to employ her usual feeding habits on him.

      In her human form, The Female Apostle took the shape of an extremely beautiful woman who had apparently forsworn the idea of wearing clothing of any kind. Men who gazed upon her often became entranced by her physical beauty and happily submitted to her advances. Her demon form was a completely different story, and was said to be extremely hideous to behold.

                      Dragon Slayer (Relic 3): 1 Unique Ability

                      Accuracy: +2

                      Damage: 7L

                      Defense: +1

                      Speed: 6

                      Details: Forged by a Legendary smith in the medieval ages, the Dragon Slayer was specifically made to kill dragons. Unfortunately, the 6 foot long, 400 pound sword was laughed off by soldiers as being impossible to wield until a Hero known as the Black Swordsman came to his shop and purchased it. During the Black Swordsman's rampage, the sword was bathed in the blood of over a thousand demons, and it now exists on both the physical and astral planes. This gives Dragon Slayer the ability to harm those can become intangible to physical attacks, such as ghosts, elementals, or even Gods!


                      -------------------------------------

                      False Behelit (Relic 5): 1 Unique Ability

                      Details: Created by the demon, Ganishka after he rebelled against the God Hand, the False Behelit allows a mortal to artificially become a demon without having to rely on the God Hand to do so. False Behelits are significantly larger than regular Behelits, and the person attempting to use them places themselves inside like a cocoon. Once they are properly sealed, they will begin the metamorphosis and emerge from the relic as a demon with the Apostle template.

                      The drawbacks to using a False Behelit is that the user is helpless while the transformation is taking place. It also requires 24 hours of gestation in order to fully become a demon. If the process is interrupted before then, they will emerge partially formed and very likely die.


                      -------------------------------------------

                      Behelit Sword (Relic 3): 1 unique ability

                      Accuracy: +2

                      Damage: 5L

                      Defense: +2

                      Speed: 4

                      Details: Forged inside the stomach of the legendary Skull Knight, the Behelit Sword is made from a collection of Behelits fused together. The sword itself has human eyes, noses and mouths scattered along its surface and the weapon is a sickly red color like that of bloody flesh. In order to create this weapon, the Skull Knight collected and swallowed the Behelits of slain Apostles so that they could not be used to turn more humans into demons.

                      Like the Dragon Slayer, the Behelit Sword has the power to affect the astral plane, causing damage to enemies who can become intangible to physical blows. However, the sword is small and light enough to be used with one hand, allowing for greater dexterity, at the cost of offensive power.


                      --------------------------------------------------

                      Berserker Armor (Relic 5): +5 Attributes

                      Soak: +2L, +3B

                      Mobility Penalty: 0

                      Fatigue: 2

                      Tags: -

                      Details: Even the mighty Apostles have reason to fear a Hero that wears the Berserker Armor, for at least two such individuals have terrorized them throughout history. The Berserker Armor is a suit of plate mail armor endowed with supernatural power. It draws out the wearer's inner monster, sending them into a berserker fury, for which the armor is named after. The armor will even shapeshift itself to cosmetically resemble the wearer's inner monster, further increasing the intimidation factor it provides.

                      When activating the Berserker Armor's power, the wearer gains a temporary increase of +2 Epic Strength, +2 Epic Stamina, and +1 Epic Dexterity. For as long as the armor's power is activated, the wearer automatically enters a Berserker Fury, similar to the Courage Extremity version.

                      In addition to the increase in physical performance, the Berserker Armor is also said to have greater abilities which have been forgotten over time. One legend tells of a power hungry king who was unable to suppress the armor's thirst for bloodshed. Upon his death, he became one with his inner monster, being reborn into the World as the legendary Skull Knight.
