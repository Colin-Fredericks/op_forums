    Idiocracy : Scion Campaign In Idiocracy (film setting)

    11-09-2013, 01:59 PM
    This is centuries after the movie Idiocracy, where everyone on the planet is sufficiently below average intelligence enough to be considered "Very Slow" by modern Humans. Civilization is greatly different than our modern world. First of all, there is now a Pantheon of absurdity. Pantheon(s) of different gods and goddesses of mostly purely fictional characters that became real gods after Fate made it so. For example, Captain America is now a fully fledged deity. So is Optimus Prime. The difference between this world and Idiocracy is that, 3,000 years after the Movie, most people are still below average in intelligence, but not stupid. Furthermore, there are some elite civilizations in the world that actually have mediocre intelligence as an average thing in society. Civilizations have actually been able to increase in quality for many years, and rather than the population being very Anti-Intellectual, civilization has progressed to the point where they actually admire intelligence, but most people simply aren't intelligent themselves.

    One of the world's most intelligent people, President Sharpy Godwynne currently has an intelligence quotient of 150 points by today's standards. (Bare bones genius) America consists of all of it's continental territory, now that Humans had finally become intelligent enough to sustain enough of it's former civilization a thousand and five hundred years ago. (The average I.Q is barely sustainable for a normal person by real world standards, but the "elite" can be quite intelligent.) The Declaration of Independence and U.S Constitution has been miraculously preserved and replicated, and has been rediscovered and put into practice, allowing America to regain it's position as a highly advanced political entity. Most of the world is not so lucky, and the U.S raises it's nose at most of the world. However, because of this, much of the world hasn't been paid attention to.

    Scions have the advantage of being at least average intelligence at the start, rather than possessing low intelligence. Bare in mind most Humans have only 1 dot of intelligence. With that in mind, in America, many places are still very weird because of the lack of intelligence. There are some places that are just "bizarre".

    The Gods
    In secure computer databases, 2,100 years ago, fictional characters from our current time and earlier were discovered. Luckily, the world's elite intellect, at the time who were smart, hid the knowledge and grew cults out of them. However, because of the funny way Fate works, these characters eventually became actual immortal beings referred to as gods. The gods are well aware at how absolutely weird Humans are, and they are humiliated about their origins.

    The Myth

    Arrival Of The Gods
    The gods came from a Galaxy far far away, from a long long time ago. Captain Picard brought the gods from the stars with him above the world. He took some of the stars with him, and he placed some in the sky to keep the night from being dark. The Pony Princess Celestia brought the Sun with her to keep the world full of light during the day, and Farnsworth helped to make the world with other gods. Below the Earth, Super Mario keeps a brilliant garden of Fire Flowers, which keeps the word hot down below, and he set of magical pipes to transport into other worlds. One day, after she broke a nail, Lady Gaga went even more insane than usual, and that is what caused the world to become to be full of Chaos. Judge Judy tried to kill Lady Gaga for screaming in her ear, but Professor Farnsworth fought with her by piloting a Mecha Armor. . . Other gods got together to protect Lady Gaga and Farnsworth from dying, because no one knew what would happen if a deity passed on. After a brief struggle with the others, Judge Judy was trapped on a Hive World, where she collects the souls of certain Evil dead, and sometimes transforms them into spiders.

    Dr. Oz taught Mankind the science of healing, and created magical healing pools of water throughout the world. Finally, Xena brought death on the world after getting drunk. Dr. Oz face palmed and the two gods fought. Dr. Oz almost died but Superman intervened knocked her in the head until she was sober.
