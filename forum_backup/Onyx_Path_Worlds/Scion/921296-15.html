                        (Holy Thread Necro, Batman!)

                        ---

                        People come to clubs to get away from the outside. From their lives, from their troubles.

                        I work to help them with this. They call me DJ Ember, and not to toot my own horn, I'm pretty damn good. I keep the beats pumping, the dancers moving, make sure that there's enough water for those who need to take care of themselves... They're my crowd and fans. I need them, they need me.

                        But SOME people don't play by the rules. People getting rowdy on the dance floor, people getting grabby, people starting fights... THAT isn't something that flies in my clubs. We have bouncers for that, thankfully.

                        But sometimes, it gets even worse. Sometimes creatures that many people like to pretend don't exist slip in. If they come to dance, I leave them be. Music is for everyone, after all.

                        But if any of them want to try and make victims out of the people here? That's when things get messy. And I'm not a girl who's afraid of getting her hands dirty.

                        My name is Ash, Scion of Xiochipilli. The club is my sanctuary and my temple. And its patrons are under my protection.

                        ---

                        So yeah, Ash is a Scion of Xiochipilli... And a DJ. Obviously her Innate Purview is Artistry (Music), and I believe her Callings would be Guardian, Trickster, and Liminal. Though Trickster may be replaced with Lover, I'll have to see when the books come out.

                                    I'd like some help with my concept, actually! The loose idea is to create a son of Sekhmet who, very eventually, becomes the god Maahes. Maahes was a real god worshipped in Egypt, specifically in the cult center of Leontopolis. He was considered a son of Sekhmet, and considered the god of knives, Killers, deserts, and of the wind. Basically, purview wise, he's Sekhmet + Sky.

                                    The first thing I need to figure out is whether to make him an incarnation, or to do what Scion Ragnarok did and say that while Maahes exists conceptually in real history, he's new to the World as an actual God, and this is how his story starts (as Ragnarok did with Hermod and Vali).

                                    The other debate I'm having is that, while I really like the idea of his power set being Sekhmet but also with wind, I recently saw pictures of a rare melanistic, IE totally black lion, and now my brain is filled with an alternative power set more inspired by the likes of Amun such as Sky, Darkness, and Stars. I'm also imagining a bunch of really cool divine epithets such as The Lion of Night (thanks, GRRM), He Whose Mane is the Stars, and The Lord of Knives. That last one is historical to the real Maahes by the way.

                                    TL;DR: Son of Sekhmet, but I can't figure out what power set. Please help.
