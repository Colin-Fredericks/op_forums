                                The current approach, where the Devas and Yazatas are both Pantheons of Gods and where the demonization of each by the other is strictly a matter of propaganda, is a good, valid way to handle things. What I’m talking about, though, is a hypothetical situation: what if Scion were to declare that the official position of the game was that the Yazatas were a Pantheon of Gods while the Devas were “merely” demonic Titanspawn? That official declaration that the Devas are not gods wouldn’t fly with the fans.

                                But that’s exactly the sort of thing that’s being suggested here: either ignore the Abrahamic faiths outright (which implicitly declares them to be essentially false) or present them primarily as a human invention — a massive game of make-believe that’s backed by a handful of opportunistic gods — which, again, declares them to be essentially false. Why not declare them to be essentially true, save only for that troublesome “strong monotheism” thing that could be downgraded to henotheism to accommodate other gods? Why the hostility to this particular set of religion-based mythologies, when nearly every other religion-based mythology is endorsed? Not necessarily as a Pantheon, mind you (although Abrahamic mythology includes the notion of Nephilim, which could be equated with Scions if you equate angels with gods); but also not as inherently malevolent Titanspawn (the operating term being “malevolent”).

                                My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
