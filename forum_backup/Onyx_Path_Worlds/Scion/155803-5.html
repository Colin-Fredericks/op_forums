    Scion also has absolutely no rules for what twenty successes on an Intelligence + Medicine roll even mean. And what the hell a player would get out of taking Arete (Medicine).     This is a hallmark of the World of Darkness system in general. The difficulties are established by the storyteller in an organic way that is usually based on percentiles (whether the storytellers are consciously aware of this or not).

    For example, you sit down and look at your group composition. In one group, your best fighters may be able to hit DV 20 about 40% of the time. You may decide that is a perfectly fine percentage, or you may decide that you prefer your players to have a higher rate of success, so you reduce DV down to 16, which gives your best fighters an estimated percentage of 60%. Now that you have a baseline, you tailor the enemies around those numbers based on how you want the enemies to perform. Maybe this next fight has one enemy that is very hard to hit, but also some little enemies that are weaker so that your second tier and third tier fighters can participate.

    In another group, your best fighters may be much more powerful, and require higher difficulties for you to maintain the same percentages. Or maybe you want different percentages for this group. It is not even the case that everything should even have perfect balancing. A group that completely neglects combat should have a few combat encounters that are appropriate challenges for them, but they should also be threatened once in a while with combats that are far above their head. The onus is on them to come up with a non-combat solution to those situations or they will be stomped. All the while your storyteller is judging the percentages and adjusting them as appropriate to meet the increasing power of the players.

                        Happy Father’s Day!
                        Relic Page

                        Pantheon Page


                        Firstly, the Relic page has been updated with four potential Relics per Pantheon, and the page with all of our example Relics are not located on the sub-page of the primary Relic page.

                        We also have our Divine Pronunciation pages up, thanks to Anne Ruthven giving us permission to repost her wonderful guide from the JSR website. We split them into two pages just to cut down on loading time, but it can be a bit of a slow load regardless - awesome things take a second or two!

                        But, now on to what I assume people are far more interested in, a quick review of our plans in regards to the Epic Attributes and Purviews. Now, all of these are prone to change, though since they are the basic conceptual ideas of what we want the Purviews to do, I doubt they will change that much at all.

                        Epic Attributes
                        ​Epic Charisma - We hope to include a greater spread of powers in Epic Charisma so that all of the major forms of Charisma can be represented in powers. Currently, the charming Scions have the majority of the powers in Charisma, with some given to those who lead. We hope to expand this so the bold leaders, the kind charmers, and those who inspire great emotions, such as love, but also fear, hate, and so on are represented.

                        Epic Manipulation - We are fairly happy with Epic Manipulation at the moment, but of course we will have to rebalance things to better fit inside Scion 2.0. One of the areas that Epic Manipulation is lacking powers in successfully pulling off the social half of a disguise, and since it is such a common theme in mythology, it is likely to gain a knack or two.

                        Epic Appearance - Epic Appearance sits in a continually weird spot. It has a lot of cross over with Epic Manipulation, and Epic Charisma mechanically, but it has some very cool Knacks. These Knacks will be rebalanced, and, of course, some new ones will likely be added. Additionally,we have been toying with the idea of Epic Appearance possibly augmenting your Charisma or Manipulation somehow, though that is very much a vague idea that may not come to pass.

                        Epic Perception - The three mental Attributes are fairly strong at the moment, but Epic Perception needs an injection of knacks that allow you to sense even more things, such as ghosts (hopefully in a way that does not step on the toes of Death Senses), legend use (hopefully in a way that does not step on the toes of Unlidded Eye), and peering across dimensions (to see terra-incognita, or gaze down from your overworld at the mortal realm).

                        Epic Intelligence - Epic Intelligence has some amazing powers, and though we are going to have to rebalance everything, there likely won't be much change. It would be nice to find a way to make Axiom more appealing though, since in all my five years of playing Scion, I have never seen it used once.

                        Epic Wits - Epic Wits is filled with dice adders, which is something we are going to have to address, though the basic premise of these Knacks is going to be kept. There is not much needed to be added to Epic Wits in our mind, though we are sure we will come up with some nice little things to slip in there.

                        Purviews
                        Darkness - Darkness is going to encompass the powers of night, fear, along with common darkness in all of its forms. We hope to have Darkness represent the idea of what exists outside the campfire's light, what exists outside the walls of the cities. The dark places of the world where humanity has not reached yet.

                        Death - Death is fairly good already, though we are going to rejig the order of a lot of the Boons. The ghost based Boons are going to be lower tier, for high tier Demigods don't really interact with ghosts on an incredibly meaningful level from what we have seen, and it is more common at Hero. We hope to make the ghost based powers, additionally, capable of being used more often.

                        Earth - Earth is fairly good at representing the powers of Earth Deities, though we want to include some powers of wealth under Earth, and giving it a power for becoming the land itself is also likely going to be present, for it is a very common theme for Earth Deities.

                        Fertility - RAW Fertility is going to take serious investigation, because it is probably the purview that is most overwhelmingly worshipped by mortals in our estimation. And yet, most of the mythologically resonant ideas contribute more towards agriculture than something that can be used by a heroic scion in the heat of a moment. Additionally, there will probably be a power somewhere for the constant theme connecting Fertility and sexuality.

                        Fire - RAW Fire is all about the all consuming fire, and, while this is a mythological theme, it ignores those who inspire through fire, those who are the hearth and home, and also giving representation of great passion.

                        Frost - Frost has some problems with overall theme asides from 'snow, ice, and cold stuff' RAW. So, we hope to, while keeping much of the same powers, bring themes of destroying winter, sapping emotions, and such things. We hope to have Frost run counter to Fertility, destroying life, causing famines, and representing the fear that winter brought for many ancient peoples.

                        Guardian - RAW Guardian acomplishes its goals, but, we want to include more powers for beings who are not directly martial protectors. Those who watch over children, those who guard specific buildings, and the such. While there will be powers for the martial defenders, such as Thor, we hope that there will also be powers for people such as Hera who protect children and mothers.

                        Health - Powers of plague, and powers of healing are represented in Health already. But, we wish to further the powers of bringing life if we can find places for them. Giving people children, striking people impotent or barren, and such powers we hope to include.

                        Illusion - Powers of tricks, and lies are already represented with Illusion, but we want to include powers for the dream Deities, though few they are.

                        Justice - Justice is a deceptively powerful Purview, and we would like to continue that theme. While it should only be able to bring justice to those who have broken a law, we also want to include powers of rulership, for those who are the one true king, and such.

                        Moon - Madness, and serenity are the main themes of Moon we want to bring, along with some minor trends of secrets. Moon, being one of the three Celestial Purviews with so many different mythological themes across various cultures, is rather difficult to write for, and it will likely be one of the last we complete.

                        Psychopomp - RAW Psychopomp has a few glaring issues. Firstly, they are incapable of instant travel until Legend 10, and additionally, they just become travel batteries for their Band from what we have seen. We want to fix this issue by giving earlier quickened transportation, creating means of travel, hampering others travel, and such things are how we wish to address issue number one. The second issue has several things in the playtest stage, so we are eager to see how they turn out.

                        Sky - The vault of the Sky, breath, and so on are what we want to accomplish with Sky. GBN's current revision is very good, and we are probably going to draw a lot of inspiration from them. But, it will not be a word for word copy of it.

                        Stars - Oh Stars, you are so incredibly difficult. Stars is very likely going to take quite a long time to write, since there is a surprising lack of consistency through the ancient world about Stars. But, being eternal and unchanging is fairly common, in addition to navigation themes.

                        Sun - RAW Sun descends into the modern idea of the Sun very quickly, which is something we want to avoid very much. Dawn bringing hope, light fighting the darkness, and blistering, hateful heat are what we want to evoke with our Sun, and hopefully we will.

                        Thunder - Storms, thunder, and lightning are the themes of Thunder. GBN very recently created the Purview, and we hope to continue the theme of barely controlled power that the Purview currently has. But, there, of course, are going to be some minor changes.

                        War - We want to streamline war along two primary themes. The general, who engages in tactics and logistics. And the champion, who inspires his allies through duels and single combat. While cultural powers like Raistrad are being moved to Guides and Relics, War is picking up a few powers to cause friendly fire accidents and the ability to fill everyone with fury and bloodlust.

                                        I like your work, especially the retainer part. Such an easy and amazing way to handle this birthright

                                        I noticed in your combat section that you are using dexterity in a lot of trait or derived trait, more than in RAW I think. Is it intended that dexterity is the easyest way to make a combat oriented character ? As I see it, it's dexterity VS strength + stamina. Is dexterity becoming a bit too powerful ?
                                        RAW Scion had a colossal dependence on Epic Dexterity for fighters, and it is something we have tried to stray away from. While it is technically easiest to just take Epic Dexterity, and use Dodge DV and use it for attacking, you don't really come out totally on top. Epic Dexterity is one of the less powerful both defensive, and offensive powers, but its strength comes from the fact that it is both, and gives you a lot of other features.

                                        On the other hand, Epic Strength, and Epic Stamina, both being far more 'focused' than Epic Dexterity, can bring more to the table in their focused areas. Epic Strength brings a number of ways to deal with armor, and brings Aggravated Damage to the table, something which Epic Dexterity does not possess. Epic Stamina brings extra health boxes (deceptively helpful) and many different ways to, in broad terms, not to die.

                                        Additionally, if someone wishes to, they can use Epic Appearance as a physical defensive value through Game Face or Do Not Want. We have not totally revised the Epic Socials yet, so they may not be all fixed up properly.

                                        So, while someone can just flat out use Epic Dexterity, it is very much not required. It also must be remembered that you can't use a Knack from Epic Strength when making a Dexterity based attack, and vice versa. So, someone using Epic Dexterity as their primary mode of attack can't use Epic Strength's Aggravated attacks unless they are willing to use their lower Epic Strength to attack with. Additionally, someone with Epic Dexterity may not used the enhanced range powers of Epic Perception when attacking with their Epic Dexterity. So, while Epic Dexterity can be used for both attack and defense, it is defiantly not the king of either.

                                        I hope I explained that well. I am not the mechanics person, so if I have done a poor job, you will see Iry here in a while putting some corrections in. But, I am very glad you are enjoying out system. Always feel free to ask any questions, or make any comments!

                                                        Originally posted by Astoril View Post
                                                        Ok I'm convinced but you seem to say that aggravated damages are a big deal but the only reference I found is that it halves your soak DV. Is there another difference between aggravated and lethal damage ?
                                                        Aggravated damage is horrific to take for two reasons. Firstly, if you are someone who relies on Soak DV, you are only able to use half of it to resist people. (Dodge DV can be similarly slashed through grappling.) But, secondly, as Griffinguy said, it is easier to just outright kill someone with Aggravated damage for the fact that the moment someone is filled with Aggravated, they perish. With Lethal damage, one has to still fill their health boxes with Aggravated Damage by having the Lethal Damage wrap over into Aggravated if you want to outright kill someone.

                                                        Additionally, some Knacks, such as Self-Healing and Damage Conversion have to use their higher cost counterparts, Regeneration and Divine Damage Conversion to try to resist Aggravated damage, which some beings may not have.

                                                        So, overall, if you want to just flat out slaughter someone, use Aggravated damage. Be it from Fire (Dragon's Breath), or Epic Strength, or some of the other Purviews we have not yet revised (I am sure Thunder will have some, and likely War), if you want to destroy someone entirely, Aggravated is the way to go.

                                                        But, hey. If you are an Epic Perception Marksman, you can shoot a target pretty much no matter where they are through combining Clairvoyance and Divine Marksman, and it is far harder to hide from them. So, don't think that only Epic Strength is viable for total offensive combatants. I would be more afraid of offending Apollo than Ares.
