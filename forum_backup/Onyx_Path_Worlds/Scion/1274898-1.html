                                            Maybe Belief and Worship is a rare commodity in your chronicle, and every region's Worship is different. The Catholic Church has been the single pipeline for Belief for the past 2.000 years. Any threat which appeared was dealt with by Sorcerers within the Church. Now, things are changing.

                                            The Internet is bringing ideas all over the globe, and neopagan communities are growing. LGBT+ communities, told they have no place in the Church, seek out other assurances of a favorable Afterlife. Police Officers begin to wear a badge with the Tiwaz (Norse God of Justice) rune on the back, "as extra insurance," and it works, as long as they're not corrupt. For the other Pantheons, even this small drop of Worship is enough to attract their notice. The gods reward drops of homage with more favor, sending their Scion offspring or Visiting near-strangers.

                                            Option 2: The Church is powerful
                                            Maybe the Catholic Church has worked as a centralizing point, and the Gods franchise belief and guidance out to the Catholic Church. So, rather than Zeus getting bugged for assistance with the weather from all over, he's able to focus on war with the Titans, and the Church routes prayers as necessary ("Aesclepius [Greek god of Healing] is busy this week. Route the St. Luke [Patron Saint of Doctors] prayers to Chicomecoatl [Teotl goddess of healing]"). In return, the Gods provide some blessings and fortune.

                                            Option 3: The Church is secret
                                            Maybe the Catholic Church has spent centuries collecting Relics and harvesting Legend from those Relics. Picture catacombs under the Vatican, with relics connected together with pipes of Holy Water. This Legend Extraction process has been the work of the Church since Peter, the first Scion of Jesus, defeated a low-level Scion of Zeus. He took a trophy, and it was his refusal to divulge its location which prompted his execution.

                                            Now, the Throne of Peter grants the Wearer of the Mitre the ability to view events from an Eternal perspective. However, recently the balance between Godly and Titanic Relics within the Collection has shifted. The Holy See has been tainted, and the Church's perspective has become flawed.

                                                If I can put my two cents on the whole conversation

                                                People thought it was silly that the Gods keep hidden because it made no sense that they would, considering their character on the mythologies, and that it was, essentially, an Idea brought from Old World of Darkness. I played a lot of Old World of Darkness, and that doesnt make a lot of sense there either, but still.

                                                The idea of now all Gods are known to everybody is to better simulate how mythological stories actually play out. If you want to play differently, go for it, but it is up to you.

                                                I dont think the idea of "Consensus makes real" (which is "Mage the Ascension" if you ever played) works with "All myths are true" because, if no one believed in a Myth, it would stop being real, therefore it became untrue that they exist, therefore, the Myth is untrue. Same goes for if a prophecy, like Ragnarock, came without happening as predicted. Honestly, I am not a great fan of the "Belief alters reality and make it real", so I prefer the Classic Scion 2Ed, but, as Watcher pointed out, you can play however you want.

                                                Just take caution when saying things like: "Christianity is the religion of hope, Old Gods are of war and conflict". Not only this can be perceived as disrespectful to ancient cultures but directly offensive to Neo-Pagans of all kinds.
