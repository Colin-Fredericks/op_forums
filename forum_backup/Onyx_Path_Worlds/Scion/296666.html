    Scion Origin Stories?

    10-30-2014, 02:49 AM
    Anyone got any good ideas for a Visitation that new Scions could receive? I came up with 2 and may have more later depending on ideas. Here's an origin story for a child of Ares and Odin.




    Winning the world heavy weight championship was supposed to be the happiest moment of your life. Perhaps it would have been if it wasn't for Him. You thought they were just an obsessed fan when they said you were a child of the gods, but you promptly called security when they began insisting they were your father. You stepped into the ring, thinking they were just some random stalker. But when your first punch took the champion's head clean off, your boxing career was destroyed instantly!

    You are a child of Ares, god of war, and you are one of the greatest boxers in the world. Only problem is that now mortals crumble like tissue paper under your blows. That's okay, though, you've got plenty of Titan Spawn who think they have a shot at being a contender!



    The car accident was going to kill you, you knew that as you lay in your hospital bed and waited for the last of your strength to fade away. But late at night, after visiting hours, an old man with an eye patch came to you and gave you a lengthy tongue lashing about how you were being a lazy bastard and did not deserve to join the others in Valhalla. Thrusting a weapon into your hand, the man said if he caught you slacking off again, he'd beat the hell out of you. Then he vanished suddenly, and you came to realize that your wounds were healed completely!

    You are a child of Odin, a sibling to Thor, and apparently regarded as the "lazy one" of the family. You have to work extra hard to prove you're not weak and cowardly, and hopefully earn your way into your father's good graces.

                      •You always wondered who your real parents were. Mom and Dad found you on top of a freshly filled grave on the first day of spring, and you've always wondered. Growing up the child of a mortician, you've always been a bit odd, and figured you'd go into the family business simply for lack of a better option. Then one day, the dead started climbing out of their plots. When the strange man and woman came out of nowhere with swords and started chopping things up, you really were too busy fighting for your life to care. When, after the fight, they claimed to be your parrnts, you started paying attention. They were sorry to give you up, but Hades and Persephone were not exactly the best choices to give you a normal childhood. Now you wander the country, putting the unquiet dead to rest. It's the family business, after all.

                      •It was just you and your mom growing up. She was sick most of the time, so you tried to be good. Really, you did! But then you'd see some bully or thug causing people grief, strutting around like they owned everything, and you'd get so mad, and next thing you know, his pants are full of glue and you're in the principal's office. When you weren't growing into a vigilante, you spent you time in the library, reading of Odin, Thor, Loki, and the other lords of Midgard. Then mom got sick. Sicker than normal. You worked hard to pay the bills and pick up the slack, but she just grew worse and worse over the years. As she told you about your dad, how wonderful and perfect he was and how she wished she knew what made him grow away, you grew to hate your sire. Then, one day, she finally died. No other relatives, no friends, no one. You were her only tie to the world, and her yours. That day, your 21st birthday, you went to get drunk for the first(and last) time in your life. The old man behind the counter listened, felt pity, and offered you a drink. Next thing you know, it's morning, you're on the other side of the state, your body is covered in runic tattoos, and the old man is introducing himself as your grandfather, or, as you probably better know him, Loki Laufreyson. Your dad was a Scion like you, but he decided to desert the Titan War and go do whatever he wanted. You aren't going to be like him, and the war may be the only toe you have to the World. Regardless of the reason, there 's a lot of bullies strutting their stuff that only you can punish. Time to get out the glue...

                      •Growing up son of an unwed mother in the middle of the 20th century wasn't easy, but you managed. You learned to tune people out, to stop caring. You went into medicine more for the money than the chance to do a good thing. You grew old, cold and alone, and you liked it that way. Then, one day, a few weeks before you were going to retire, a man a little older than you came in with a patient. You were shocked and a little nauseated when you saw what his wounds looked like, and you weren't sure what animal could have mauled him like that. But, under the watchful, judging eye of the man, you fixed him up. That's when the Titanspawn lept into your clinic, eager to finish the job. The old man threw you a sword, and you proved yourself a natural as the monster tried in vain to not be chopped to bits. When the old man told you he was Dian Cécht of celtic lore, and you father, you were too weirded out to care. He told you about the Titans and the war, and asked if you wanted to fight. You wanted to tell him to go away, but a part of you, the Compassionate part you thought died a long time ago, reminded you of the boy you had just saved. You may have been a bitter old man, but you swore an oath, and you won't let anyone get hurt like that while you still breath.

                                              Return of the King: Your father took his Egyptian heritage quite seriously and was adamant that you came from the bloodline of ancient pharaohs. When he died, he even went so far as to commission an elaborate tomb be constructed for his final resting place. While you and the rest of your family went to mourn him, the tomb was sealed up behind you, as is royal burial custom! It was then you received your visitation from Osiris, who explained you were, indeed, descended from his blood. He gave you a choice; be entombed forever with your father, or embrace your heritage as the next Pharaoh. Leaving your relatives to starve to death may have been hard upon your conscience, but you'll see them again in the next world. Besides, Osiris didn't sound pleased about his empire crumbling to ruins, so you've got some work to do!



                                              Afro Samurai: It was practically unheard of, the Amatsukami are usually xenophobic towards other ethnicities. But Hachiman, the Lord of War, was impressed by your village's ability to fend off tribal warriors and animal poachers alike. You're fairly certain Hachiman only adopted you into the family, but it doesn't matter now. Your "father" has elevated you from being a simple village protector. Can you overcome the culture shock and make a name for yourself as a true warrior, or will this adoption spark a war between the Amatsukami and the Loa?



                                              Sacrificial Victims: You thought you were going insane, degrading into a serial killer. Who knows how much money you blew on counseling and medication before you finally stopped caring? You cannibalized two dozen people before the police finally caught you. Sentenced to death, you were awaiting the gas in the chamber before he game to you. Miclantecuhtli, the Aztec god of the dead. He gives you a choice, either suffocate to death in the execution chamber, or embrace your destiny as a bringer of death and despair. You had to laugh at the offer when it was presented. Many psychopaths have claimed theirs was a divine calling, but you can't think of any who had the opportunity to prove it!

                                                          Setting It Straight: Unlike the rest of the modern society who get their mythology teachings from movies and television series, you actually picked up a book once in a while. Unfortunately, it seemed all your protesting did was fall on deaf ears, as more interpretations continued to support the popular belief of today over the actual version of the story. Then one day, somebody stopped to listen, interested in hearing your side of the tale. They smiled and nodded with interest about various points and stories you recounted about Zeus and Hades' deeds. You explained that Hades was never tricked into getting the underworld, he chose to rule it and he was quite happy with his station.
