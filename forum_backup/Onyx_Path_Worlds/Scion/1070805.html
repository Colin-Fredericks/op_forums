                                              Tower Over Mortals: (Health 4)

                                              Many deities are said to have the ability to expand their size, becoming colossal sized figures who can squash the likes of mortals beneath their thumbs. Those who possess this boon can draw upon this awe inspiring power for themselves. For the rest of the scene, the user adds their Legend to their Size. For each level of Size increase, they gain 1 temporary dot of Strength or Stamina, chosen at the time of the boon's activation.

                                                  BREAKING THE CHAIN ●●●●● ●●●●
                                                  Cost: 20 Legend, and 4 Willpower per use
                                                  Dice Pool: Chaos + Command (vs MDV)
                                                  Type: Miscellaneous
                                                  The Great Chain of Being suggests that all things in the world follow a strict hierarchical structure, and every single link upon that structure has the positive qualities of every lower link upon the great chain. The god with this boon may break down the chain of being, which allows chaos to slowly saturate a domain that stretches ten miles for every (Chaos Boon) she possesses. Within this realm of chaos, the natural order of the world ceases to be true and strange things begin to happen everywhere within her realm. She may also dictate one new rule that is absolute within her domain for every six (Chaos Boons) she possesses. She may declare that all dogs are brilliant chess masters, all weapons only tickle when they strike someone else, or everybody gains flight in complete defiance of the natural order. This boon lasts until she activates it again.

                                                  FANTASTIC CHIMERA ●●●●● ●●●●●
                                                  Cost: 25 Legend per use
                                                  Dice Pool: None
                                                  Type: Miscellaneous
                                                  Creatures of legend range from the rapacious centaur, to the inquisitive sphinx. So complete is the Scion’s mastery over the animal form that she may instantly create a beast with completely fantastic qualities. This creature can be entirely in defiance of physics, allowing fire breathing dragons, shapeshifting jorogumo, or golden boars that return to life every morning after being eaten. Once created, the creature is permanent unless killed.
                                                  The exact abilities of any given fantastic chimera must be agreed upon by the player and Storyteller, but fate has a habit of weaving the most powerful of chimera into her story in unexpected ways. Unless the Scion uses Nemean Creature or Typhonian Creature, the Fantastic Chimera will have no epic attributes to power its supernatural abilities.

                                                  PAST THE FARTHEST REACHES ●●●●● ●●●●●
                                                  Cost: 22 Legend and 2 Willpower per use
                                                  Dice Pool: Stars + Occult (vs PsP + Integrity)
                                                  Type: Miscellaneous
                                                  In the distant dark stand the Star Gods and Goddesses. A departure, physically, or figuratively, from their fellow Deities. From Radegast’s detachment from the politics and lawbreaking of the Bogovi, to Ishtar’s disruption of society, to the Four Hundred South Stars diametrically opposed to Huitzilopochtli, or Nut irrevocably separated from her lover Geb, Star Deities are often separate in their own way. Detached from their Pantheons, existing on the peripheries, sometimes socially, through transience of behavior, or physically, through chains or banishment. To these Celestial Deities, this is often something they accept (except for Nut), this withdrawal. The Four Hundred South Stars oppose the Teotl, and Radegast has his own business to attend to (presumably). However, other Deities may not be familiar with this isolation, and from this, comes one of the most terrible skills of a Star Deity.
                                                  By activating this Boon, the user immediately has a contested (Stars + Occult) roll facing off against a (PsP + Integrity) roll from their target. If the target can resist this power, they suffer no penalty. But if they fail, they suffer the terrible fate of being metaphysically ostracized from their own pantheon. Their Pantheon Specific Purview temporarily fades from their grasp, and leaves them alone, as if they belonged to no one. The Theoi’s great skill fades, the rightful ownership of Mes vanishes, the all consuming Fates of the Aesir flicker, and the like.
                                                  This effect lasts for one day per Threshold Success.

                                                  LIFE ETERNAL ●●●●● ●●●●●
                                                  Cost: None
                                                  Dice Pool: None
                                                  Type: Persistent
                                                  There is no end to life. The world dies in the Autumn, and in the Spring it returns, just as strong as it was before the death knell descended on the world. With this power, the Scion may echo this great pattern, but at a grave cost. At the moment of their death, the Fertility God counts up their unspent Agrarian points and Wilderness points. The effects of this boon are based upon whichever one is higher.
                                                  Agrarian: When a God with this Boon dies, they may chose to resurrect at the end of the scene in a beautiful blossom of wheat, grapes, or some other display of organized agriculture. Once they have used this boon, they must wait until the next Spring to benefit from it again (if it is currently Spring, they must wait the full cycle). Only powerful uses of Death, or similar high ranking powers that thematically counter spring can be used to prevent this fertile rebirth.
                                                  Wilderness: The young buck defeats his father, the old king of the forest. The King is dead, long live the King. Then the king’s son, a young buck, defeats his father, the king of the forest, and becomes king. The King is dead, long live the King. When a God with this Boon dies they do not simply return to life from the Underworld like their Agrarian siblings. Over the course of the scene they begin to possess one of their natural children, until the child's personality, Legend, and powers are overwhelmed by the Deity at the end of the scene. The God may have dodged their death, but it costs them the valuable resource of one of their own blood.

                                                      Living Relic (Health 9)

                                                      A goose that lays golden eggs, a dragon with a sword in its tail, a rabbit with a ruby embedded in the forehead; there are plenty of legends that tell of creatures who used their own bodies as containers for artifacts of power, but most people don't know how it happened. In truth, the Gods placed them there, either as a test of worthiness for those who would claim them, or as a means of keeping them protected. With this Boon, the user can physically merge a relic into a living creature's biology without causing any negative side effects. The merged creature gains the ability to use the relic's powers as if they were its own, but loses these abilities if the relic is extracted from their body.
