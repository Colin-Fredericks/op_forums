    You haven't exactly led what one may call a charmed life.

    Oh, that's not to say you've been living like a Dickens character. You've got a loving family. Your Mom passed away when you were a baby and your dad apparently never even showed up for the funeral, much less to take care of you, so you were raised by your Grandparents, who put their all into raising you. But you were still always getting in trouble. When you were a kid, it was because you had a big mouth and were too clever by half and were always at the head of your friends, leading them into one adventure or another. When you were older, it tended to be because plenty of guys didn't like the way their sisters or girlfriends looked at you. Your mouth didn't help either. People loved you or hated you, but rarely did they not have an opinion.

    Still, for every good thing that happened, trouble never seemed far behind.

    For instance, you finally scored a date with the girl you've had your eyes on for a while and your grandmother passed away the next day. And with her gone, all the fire and life in your Grandfather seem to have gone with her.

    A few weeks later, you were out on a walk hoping the night air would help clear your head of the depression and frustration when you came across a woman being attacked by what looked at first glance like a guy in a trench coat. Well, that sort of shit doesn't fly when you're around and you charged him. Very quickly, you found out that he was much bigger than you thought.....and had one eye.

    You didn't mean he was missing an eye. You meant "He had a giant fucking eyeball in the middle of his forehead."

    He was also hella stronger than you and spent a good while demonstrating that on your poor body. For a brief moment, you thought you were gonna die and be with Grams again. Then you took a look at the woman you came to save and decided that there wasn't a chance in hell you were dying like this. Strength surged through you in a hot wave and you felt your body ripple and expand and an instant later, you were a giant bear.

    Turns out, Bear > Cyclops.

    After you turned back to normal, the woman, not a single hair so much as ruffled comes over and takes you aside for a talk.

    She explains that you're a Scion, a child of the gods, like out of one of the old stories. She tells about the monsters and magic of the world. Then she tells you that you have your father's eyes.

    Now, normally you wouldn't exactly be terribly interested in who your dad was, except maybe to know what he looks like so you could punch him in the face one day, but after learning that he's apparently a god it's sorta hard not to be interested. Besides, you couldn't really think of any gods who turned into bears off the top of your head.

    "Most of your father's demigod children inherit his great strength or at times, his thunder." She said. "But Zeus is also a skilled shapeshifter and you seem to take after him in that regard."

    "Zeus...King of the Gods Zeus?" You ask "Father of Hercules Zeus?"

    "Yes, that Zeus." The woman says distastefully, her eyebrow twitching briefly at the mention of Hercules.

    A sudden horrible premonition cuts through the fog of confusion you've been in since the whole bear thing and you hesitantly ask the woman who she is.

    "It's about time you asked." she said. "I am Hera, Queen of the Gods."

    Oh. Shit.

                                The Stars: You have always been fascinated by them, in a sort of wary, cautious sort of way. You always felt like there was something more to them. While earning your PhD in Astronomy, specializing in the dead stars known as black holes, you read everything you could on stars, from ancient mythology to the writings of H.P. Lovecraft. Astronomy documentaries were the only thing you ever watched on TV, but nothing seemed to satisfy your curiosity. Then, on the night of your graduation, drinking cocktails out of beakers passed out by the chem graduates, you were approached by a man with a slight limp and a leopard spotted tie.

                                "I must admit, this is certainly not the path I expected of you when I sired you. Still, you could be a useful warrior against the tzitzimimeh."

                                You knew that name. It was the name of the star demons in Aztec mythology that continually tried to eat the sun. With a growing apprehension, you cautiously ask the man who he is.

                                With a fang filled grin, the man simply responds: "Your father, the Black Tezcatlipoca."

                                ...you really shouldn't have drank that half-pint of Four Horsemen.

                                                            And you were mad. All the time. Like an anger towards everyone and everything just made you want to lash out at whatever (or whoever) was closest. You lived in an orphanage almost your whole life, since foster homes never worked out with your hostile disposition. You even went to juvie once for putting another foster kid in the hospital.
                                                            The ones you were mad at most though were your parents. Your mom, for up and dying when you were only two years old, and your dear old dad for leaving for the army and never coming home. Honorable discharge and all, he just... left. No call, no postcards, no notes on birthdays, just gone.
                                                            And so, you've been mad. You aren't exactly the smartest in the bunch, either, since sitting in a classroom makes your blood boil and the only class you've ever passed in high school was gym. You joined the wrestling team and excelled, you'd even have gone all state, if you hadn't been dropped for bad grades. You were stronger and better than any wrestler on the team. Or anyone else's, for that matter. You ended up flunking out, and never heading back home to the orphanage after.
                                                            Which is why you were wandering around, freshly 18, not a dime to your name, in an alley when a sketchy dude with black eyes offered you a place in an underground fight. You took him up, and made a couple hundred bucks for beating a guy into the ground. Now that's a line of work you could get behind.
