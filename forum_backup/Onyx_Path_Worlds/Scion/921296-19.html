    Though on a serious note for this thread. I had a character concept in mind for one for 1e. Yeah I know "Boo! Get off the stage! 1e sucks!" Yeah I don't care.

    My concept was for scion of Izanagi (yeah I know he's not a god anymore blah blah blah once more I don't care) who is meant to end up wandering the Earth as a guardian and warrior in the name of justice, protecting the innocent and writing wrongs done by others. Eventually leading him to becoming known among the Amatsukami as "Wanding God of Justice. A god who wanders the world protecting innocents, punishing the guilty. The idea that when when his sword falls no innocent blood is shed. Yet none who watch the sword fall are ever the same again. They leave changed in one form or another. Either they passed the test and the sword itself passed through them, or they failed the test to one degree or another. For the truly heinous of crimes there is but one punishment, death. Others leave marks on the body. As a warning. A warning that though they escaped death once before on his blade. They had best be careful lest they face the blade a second time and fail. But yet with all this talk of death and blood letting in all honesty he would much rather not. He'd much rather sit, talk and enjoy a cup of tea. Laugh and smile. To play with children.

            Here are three competing Gods of the Internet. Each one warring aganist the other to be the sole bearer of the Mantle.

            A Scion of Lakshmi who is the God of the Internet as a Commodity. The Internet is just another technology, and like all the technologies and innovations that have come before it exists to be a commodity, provided by ISP's and given to the consumers. At a price. Sure people may complain that the Internet, like information, should be free for all to use, but this God knows better. Nothing is free. Everything has a cost and to deny that reality is the height of childishness. Thus, this well-dressed and always professional God of the Internet does its best to provide the best Internet service possible for those willing to pay for it. It's only natural. You don't expect the same quality of food from a fast food place that you would expect from a five star restaurant do you? Of course not. Quality costs money, and in this God's case worship as well. This God is usually male, but is willing to be female if The Chart says people are more comfortable around a female figure. In the end, making people feel more comfortable is the end goal because when they feel like they can trust you than they're far more willing to buy your product and accept your services.

            Portfolio: God of the Internet, E-Commerce, Profit, and ISP's.

            A Scion of Ares who is the God of the Internet as a Weapon. The Internet is a tool, and better yet it is a weapon. The ultimate weapon in the modern day. With it careers can be ruined, movements ignited, lies and truths spread globally and governments toppled. More importantly though, the Internet is a battleground for an endless war. Call it the Culture War if you want, call it trolling, call it flaming, call it whatever, to this God its just plain beautiful. Humans aren't meant to passively accept the Status Quo of the Present. A real human is meant to topple whatever structure they deem unacceptable. Doesn't matter if you replace that structure with something new or something old or something good or something bad. Social Justice, the Dark Enlightment, the Alt-Right, Black Lives Matter, this God doesn't care about the ideology, so as long as its dogmas are used to smash the other side. Punch up, punch down, but whatever you do always keep punching and fighting for what you believe in. Compromise is for the weak. Be pure and never given into the Enemy, to do so is worse than dying. This God demands that you expose the Truth. It doesn't actually matter if you're Truth is actually true, in fact, its better if isn't. People will come to debunk you or shallow your lies and when they meet each in the comments another glorious fight can break out. This God appears in whatever form will best piss off its opponents. Gender fluid or hypermasculine, young or old, black or white, it doesn't matter. So as long as it brings hate and fear into someone's eyes.

            Portfolio: God of the Internet, Trolling, Hacking, Conspiracies, Fake News and the Culture War.

            A Scion of Loki who is the God of the Internet as Transformation. The Internet is the liberator of the human spirit. With it there is a new possibility of transformation and enchantment. Virtual reality, argumented reality, cybernetics, AI. While many of these transformations and technologies are just the stuff of science fiction currently the possibility and idea is out there and this God strives for its followers to make them a reality. Any fears of conseqences be damned. Anything is better than being in meatspace and not being able to reshape reality with just your knowledge of coding. This God sees the Internet as the way to transform all of humanity and turn them into something kin to the Gods themselves. Perhaps even something greater. So, it demands that its followers go the extra mile and be the test cases for this upcoming Cyber Age. Resistance is futile. Progressive is coming. So create new VR technologies, support biorobotics research and never be at peace with what Mother Nature left you with. This God is always changing its form. Sometimes masculine, sometimes feminine but usually neither and something completely alien and off the map.

            Portfolio: God of the Internet, Virtual Reality, Cybernetics, Robotics and the New Flesh.

                            My blog currently hosts about 18 character concepts including two scions actively being played, a character made to analyze the creation and advancement in scion (and which I screwed up and need to redo), and 15 theoretical characters in Origin and Hero versions. As with my page of theoretical D&D 5e builds most of the idea is to look into how to build certain character concepts. I should probably go in and add some reasoning and analysis for choices later before I get into high numbers. A lot of these are characters I'd like to build or based on past characters of mine, one character has two variations in her build based on different connected gods. So I suppose that's only 17 concepts in total then.

                            A daughter of incarnations of Sif and Thor who is very eager to be a real scion. Daredevil media darling with her own YouTube channel.

                            A 18 year old runaway turned tomb raider who has found herself bound to an incarnation of Inari. Said incarnation hasn't noted that they were intrigued by the character because they could tell she was the child of another of Inari's incarnations.

                            A child of Benten who was a marine martial arts instructor who turned Japanese cinema stunt coordinator and then actress taken into service by Amaterasu as an agent.

                            A Hollywood agent, voice actress, and sometimes actress who looks like a living corpse because the producer of a previous movie she was part of toyed with Netjer magics and got her cursed. Taken on as a scion of Wesir sort of to return balance and justice to her due to the misplaced curse.

                            A girl who survived an attack by a demon that killed her grandmother and now can talk to and command animals as well as a purpose to protect humanity and nature from demonic mockeries of life.

                            A harpy trying very hard to not be a harpy and get taken on by a kami because "Tengu are cool ninja" attracting the eye of Frigg due to the sheer audacity of trying to get a pantheon to take her up.

                            A 19th century vampire dug up and set into the world as part of some sort of mysterious long play strategy by Kali. (mythology of this one is she became a vampire due to having been forgotten by everybody including herself and her killer. which I pulled from a webcomic admittedly, but unconsecrated murder victims I believe are a potential source of vampires)

                            A woman from a cult who infiltrates businesses as secretaries and use a combination of espionage and seduction to secure control of the companies and use those resources to fund heroes. Some of the branches have....questionable recruitment practices.

                            A Korean-Japanese woman working as a stage magician in Las Vegas who is a child of Susano'o and refers to herself as The Trickster Fox due to using animal-fables in her stage show along with four friends of hers who took on identities as Dog (a police officer), Tiger (a tough), Mouse (a flirt), and Rabbit (a housewife). Unbeknownst to them, the scion does have kitsune blood (though this is a 1e rebuild so that blood never manifested much) and the four friends were all denizens as well (including a fifth Raven girl who just did research and didn't perform).

                            A homonculus who wasn't aware that she was one, created by a sorcerer in a fit of grief and guilt after the death of his wife and brought to life by the grace of a merciful god (Ganesha in his Kami guise).

                            A gender fluid alligator farmer and scion of sobek who swings from romance to heroism on the drop of a dime.

                            An unethical Canadian archeologist who was boasting about her methods of circumventing archeology regulations within earshot of a drunken Egyptian woman at the bar and now finds herself press-ganged into hunting monsters.

                            A daughter of Yama who is a grief consular and occasional exorcist and is preparing herself for a job in her father's operation later. Fascinated with the idea of true beauty being judged based on how "useful" a person is to others and The World at large.

                            A meteorologist post-grad who ended up changed into a satyr and a fling with Zeus after getting lost and stumbling into a Satyr kegger. Most of this is rebuild of a silly CoH character from back in the day. (also, yes, any theoretical I've made so far that is a denizen is also a scion). Now she has an academic advisor who is an incarnate of Hera that is definitely trying to kill her. They have a truce, the incarnate doesn't touch the woman's child or parents and the satyr keeps going on her suicide missions.

                            A child of an incarnate of Ares whose father was killed and she took up his knives, a pair of cursed knives with violent personalities embedded from a very obsessively focused US Marshal from the old west and a bloodthirsty soldier from the WWI trenches. She has to manage the rage from the knives and her developing scion nature.

                            A child from an Italian coven that has been using magic and incest to keep the presence of divine blood in their veins strong. She ran away as soon as she could and became first a circus performer and then a high-class thief. Has magic-eating squirrels. Rebuild of an NPC from a 1e campaign.

                            Think that's all of them right now.
