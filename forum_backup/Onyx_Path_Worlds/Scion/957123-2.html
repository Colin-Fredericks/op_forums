                                                Perhaps. But that's like saying a character's stat block is a great way to describe them. It's a way to describe them, but it's ultimately flat and is going to take a lot of work to bring to life. And by stat block I mean JUST the crunchy number-y bits, no description block. No history write up. Particularly nothing that relates to character as a person relating to other persons, places and things.

                                                You hand me a sheet of paper with an artist's picture of the good Baron, his veve and a truncated write-up like a profile from a dating website, it's NOT the same as knowing all the stories about him from mythology. Actually, thinking about it, I'd LOVE to see Baron Samedi's e-Harmony dating profile. That would make him a person who wants things. This is more like just seeing the D&D tagline (Name, Deity of?, Gender, Color, Animal, etc. in a table format.) and then just slopping all over the place with those really broad brushstrokes.

                                                Hephaestus's identity is the "God of the Forge" and that's how the players are going to connect with him? How does that make him different from Goibniu, Ogun, Volund, any other smith deity or, for that matter, a Norse dwarf? It's his relationship with his mother, his wife, her lover and his half-sister. It's the stuff he's made. It's his stories.
