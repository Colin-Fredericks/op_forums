    Purview Ideas?

    10-18-2014, 04:21 PM
    So I was wondering if there were any other types of Purviews that people have come up with that aren't included in the normal set. I thought up of a possibility for Volcanoes and Emotions, and would have done more, but the Order idea seemed too close to Justice, and the Forge idea didn't seem worth it. I couldn't think of 10 powers between them, but I did come up with a basic premise and an Avatar State (hah!) for the two of them.




    Volcano Purview: Those who wield the awesome power of the volcano are some of the most fearsome gods in the realms. Magma courses beneath the earth like blood through the veins, and they can harness this magma for their own ends. When they invoke this purview, the ground explodes like a minefield, volcanic ash threatens to blot out the sun, super heated rock is rained down from the sky, and the landscape is decimated almost beyond repair. Such is the terrible might of the Volcano gods, who channel the destructive rage of the planet itself.


    Avatar of the Volcano (The Cataclysm): Rare is the need to invoke this great being, but when it happens, it is like the apocalypse has come to earth. For one scene, the Scion becomes the blood of the land, a vast network of lava flowing beneath the planet's crust. At their will, this lava surges to the surface in an explosive display, shattering the earth beneath the enemy's feet and spraying them with a geyser of molten rock Which can sear even a Titan's flesh if they are caught by it. Clouds of ash fill the air, blotting out most forms of light, as well as choking those who inhale, and threatening to encase them in mummified prisons. Fire rains from the sky like meteors and the landscape will never be the same without further divine intervention!



    Emotion Purview: Gods of love, hatred, terror, greed and more have been heard of throughout history. The Emotion Purview is the ability to ignite and control the desires of those around you. Scions who wield such power are able to feed upon the emotional energy of those around them, or magnify it to levels that are nearly impossible to control. More than a few evil deities have used this purview to drive mortals to sin, but good deities have also used it to bolster their resolve against temptations.

    Avatar of Emotion (The Bacchanal): For one scene, the scion becomes the very embodiment of emotion. Their body dissolves into a cloud of narcotic, pink mist which permeates the area, intoxicating anyone who inhales it. The Bacchanal drives those assembled into a maelstrom of emotional outbursts, causing them to lose their senses as they hurl themselves into whatever vices grip them at the moment with primal abandon. Even the Titans may forget what they are doing and lose themselves to an emotional outburst, giving their enemies time to strike while they are distracted. Or perhaps the Titans will even turn on each other if they have repressed aggression towards their fellows! The Bacchanal is able to manipulate the mist at their will, so those who they do not wish to intoxicate may pass safely through. After the scene ends, the vict    ims have no memory of what transpired, as if they were lost in a trance... or just black out drunk.

      Expansion Purview: In your life time, if you've paid any attention to legends and fantasy, you've probably heard of characters who were "giants of men" or "larger than life" when describing them. The Expansion Purview allows its wielders the gift of exaggerated size and reach. A wielder of Expansion can strike foes at a distance, by extending the range at which their weapons inflict damage, resulting in legends of swordsmen who cleaved a mountain in two with one swing of their blades. They can achieve mythical proportions, crushing legions of enemies under their boots, or smash through their enemy fortresses with the fist of an angry god. Few beings are as imposing as a high level master of Expansion!


      Avatar of Expansion (The World Wrecker): The Scion becomes the embodiment of expansion for one scene. They grow to impossible scale, their head towering over the clouds. Their blows can shatter islands and split continents, and if they rage out of control, they endanger the planet with their fury. The blows from The World Wrecker do not merely end upon initial landing, but continue forward up to a number of miles equal to the user's Legend. As the World Wrecker, they can pulverize mountain ranges, gouge canyons into the earth, their footsteps leave behind craters. In the events following a battle in which The World Wrecker has participated, the field of battle becomes the sight of a natural monument or a world wonder!

                                      This was a lovely entry written by Anne:
                                      Pele
                                      Of all the goddesses of Hawaii, temperamental and fiery Pele is the most famous. The volcano goddess is a figure of enormous passions and even larger tantrums, and her stormy love affairs, violent sibling quarrels and boiling disastrous eruptions have been favorite stories of her people for centuries, retold with careful respect beneath the smoking craters of the mountains she calls home. Few even among the gods dare to anger Pele, who responds to any and all insults and affronts (even imagined ones) with immediate force and extreme prejudice.

                                      Pele is unignorable; when she enters the World, it is as a destructive, unconquerable force, upsetting, overturning and changing everything and everyone she comes into contact with. People are polarized towards her or left as debris in her wake, while all the events and situations immediately gain her as their central focus. She frequently appears as a firebrand marching in feminist rallies, a rebel leader stirring up riots against corrupt governments on the islands or a world champion surfer soaking up her fans' wild adoration.

                                      Scions of Pele are as passionate as their volatile mother, quick to love and even quicker to anger. They usually pursue what they want with gusto and seldom suffer insults or attacks on themselves without meting out appropriate retribution. despite their undeniable temper control problems, they can be fierce warriors in the defense of a cause they believe in and never back down, even when others might decide to cut their losses.

                                      Associated Powers: Chaos, Fire, Epic Charisma, Epic Perception.
                                      Abilities: Art, Athletics, Awareness, Control, Presence, Survival.
