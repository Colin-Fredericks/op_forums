An honor guard of ten frost giants stood like stone pillars in a semicircle around Sigrid. Magnus clutched his axe and started to prepare himself for the fight as Sigrid challenged him to battle. She said that she had come to face Magnus and determine whose fate would continue and whose would be snuffed out like an ember in snow. I didn’t really get all the posturing, but Sigrid seemed satisfied, and the giants stood down.

In short, don’t do it, or Zane will kick your ass. Sadly, the giant I shot was beyond help. I feel a little guilty about that, and wish I had thought of the whirlwinds sooner.
