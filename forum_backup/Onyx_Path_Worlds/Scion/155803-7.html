        Originally posted by Watcher View Post

        The changes to the Associated Powers and Associated Abilities of the Pantheons were done originally by John and Anne at Gotham by Night, but they have all been made to attempt to bring the profiles of these Gods better in line with their Mythological counterparts than RAW managed to do. So, there were some rather drastic changes to the Tuatha. But, I adore Irish Mythology and can explain every change you would like for them.

        So, the reason Lugh no longer has Guardian is there is not a single myth that I know of where Lugh is in the role of a guardian, or a protector. Lugh is not a warden on the walls, or a God who dispensed blessings to safeguard others. Additionally, I don't know of any cult worship of Lugh that identifies him as such a Deity. I have no idea why he was given it in RAW, but Lugh is not, and has never been (Historically) a Guardian Deity.

        Now, the reason they gave Lugh Health is that he once healed CuChulain when he was fighting at the ford. Also, sometimes he introduces himself as a physician or doctor when requesting entrance to Tara. But, neither of these are sufficient reasons to give Lugh Health. A single account of him preforming it, and him boasting about his ability to be a doctor don't particularly compare with Apollo slaughtering the Greek Army with plagues, or Mokosh bringing humanity into existence. Additionally, once again, we don't see any cult presence for Lugh as a healer or plague bringer.

        Now, the reason for the Tuatha having many members who have War is because they are composed of many War Deities. It's just how it has turned out for them. They are an incredibly war centered Pantheon.

        Lugh has War for he is the Champion of the Tuatha de Danann. He is their war leader, and warrior without peer. Wielding his great blazing spear and his sling.

        Nuada is the greatest King that has ever ruled Ireland, and has lead the Tuatha successfully in war against the forces of their foes many times. He lead his people against the Fir Bolg when they first invaded Ireland alongside The Dagda and Ogma.

        Ogma has War since he was the Champion of the Tuatha before Lugh, and lead the armies of the Gods against the Fir Bolg as I said. Additionally, we have evidence of Ogma being connected to the idea of 'eloquence', and getting armies ready for battle beforehand by speaking praises and other great oral works to them.

        The Dagda is a colossal man, wielding his great club and draging his other 'club' behind himself as he trudges through the battlefields. He has lead the Tuatha in warfare, and is written as a great warrior. Though, very importantly for the Irish, not a Champion.

        And, lastly, The Morrigan herself is a Goddess of battlefield frenzy, death, and destruction. Her crows pick out the eyes of the dead, and she flies over battlefields to strike blind fury into warriors. Additionally, she has brought the Tuatha secret knowledge on how to defeat their enemies in battle before, and has almost single handedly won battles for them.

        I do not understand though how this "Unbalances" the Pantheon though, would you mind terribly explaining what you mean so I can help work things out?

        But, in sum. The changes that we use to the God profiles are to keep them more in line with their mythological and and historical realities. To best represent the Gods of cultures long past through their own eyes as best we can. RAW did not do a spectacular job with this sort of thing in our mind, as can be seen through the large changes.

        EDIT: Oh, I do want to add that the Tuatha represented are only the Major Gods (Legend 12 Gods). The Tuatha have three very skilled Health Gods (Dian Cecht, Airmed, Miach) though their powers, legends, and worship don't indicate that they would be on equal footing as the Tuatha we have listed. I am not sure for Guardian though, I know of no figures who serve as a protector in Irish mythology. But, all the more room for a new Scion to fill a void!
        Using John & Anne logic, none of those gods should have the War Purview as all of them were defeated by someone else. Meaning they don't have the War Avatar, since that would render them undefeatable or produce a stalemate in head to head fighting.

        I'll buy the Morrigan though. No one ever defeated her.

        It also looks like a lot of these viewpoints are pretty much based on just the Book of Invasions and the Tain. There are a lot of other stories out there. Admittedly, much harder to get at, where things other than war are dealt with (lots of marking the edges of fields, throwing things, political maneuvering, etc.). It's easy to think a pantheon is war oriented if the sources used are two battles. It would be like setting up the Greek gods just using the Iliad.

        Lugh probably should have War. But then, as Samildanach, you could give him ANYTHING, but he did fight and win a war quite handily. Since War is about tactics and strategy and not about combat prowess in most cases, then it's not a lock.

        Nuada, I'll buy 100%. He's a War god. One that got beat and mutilated, but still, he does the necessary deeds.

        Ogma. He gets to be Champion and he fights well, but a "leader" type? No. He's got hella Strength. But not War. (Champion means "the guy who goes out and takes the 1 on 1 challenge. Not the general).

        The Dagda. No. He was King at some point, and he can fight damn good, but there is nothing to indicate War. More plowing and brewing and laughing.

        That would leave two with War as Associated..... Nuada and the Morrigan. Possibly 3, with Lugh.


        Ogma

                        Originally posted by bigwhitt View Post
                        Hello, thank you for the explanation. The reason that I thought it might unbalance them was because in the original game it seemed that each pantheon represented the entire set of purviews. If you wanted to create a scion who definitely had the Health purview there was at least one God who had it, allowing you to choose any pantheon you wanted because it matched your character concept. With Tuatha not having a god with Guardian or Health in the major gods that are listed I though it might unbalance the overall purview percentages represented in each pantheon.
                        When John and Anne began to revise pantheons, they sat down with as many primary documents they could get their hands on and tried to create associations that were as historically accurate as possible. What they discovered is that real pantheons have no sense of game balance! Some had no animal gods or fire gods among their most powerful members, while others had no health gods or prophecy gods. Often a pantheon might actually have a god who can do those things, but that god may not have been Legend 12. Other times there may be gods who use that purview, but they only dabble and would not have every dot of a purview.

                        For the most part, we are going to be sticking with the massive amount of research that John and Anne have done because we also value historical accuracy. If a pantheon has too many of one thing, or seems to have nobody with another, then that is the way they really were (to the extent that we can research). It is a great opportunity for a new Scion to rise up and become a god of those things, or wrestle with the political atmosphere of being one god among many who have those things!
                        Originally posted by bigwhitt View Post
                        Also, I happened to notice that the experience rules do not take into account a God's Associated powers or associated abilities for a reduction in experience cost Am I missing it somewhere?
                        Yes, we firmly believe that experience points are an OOC mechanic. We have removed every single knack and boon that changes the amount of experience you have to spend. Once per game, you may activate a knack or boon from one of your Associated Attributes or Purviews and the base legend and willpower cost becomes free. You must still pay for any legend and willpower cost beyond the base.

                                                    Originally posted by Shadowflame View Post
                                                    A small issue of a sort...I don't like seeing the word "fertility" used for what is essentially "Plant."
                                                    Well, in the ancient world, the concept of the green and growing plant life was, incredibly commonly, tied hand in hand with the idea of sex and fertility of specific people, or all people. So, for that reason, we have kept the name of "Fertility" for the Purview controlling plant life, and all things green and growing. Fertility is a Purview for the fertility of the land, and commonly the fertility of the Deities or Titans who possess it.

                                                    For example, Freyr is a God of Fertility, bringing growth and new things to the world. And, if you look into his cult statues, he is frequently depicted with colossally exaggerated genitals. This is also seen in Goddesses, such as Oshun, and many others, through exaggerated hips and breasts. One of the (suspected) earliest examples of this are the many different statuettes that have been referred to as "Venuses" by Archeologists and Anthropologists.

                                                    Because of this, the later levels of Fertility are planned to have some powers related to sex. The one I suspect will be most definitely included is one directly linking the fertility of a specific geographic location (Be it the size of Mesopotamia to the size of Ulster) with the fertility of the God or Goddess in question.

                                                    I hope that makes this make more sense Shadowflame, but you are welcome to change the name for your own game if you still don't like it. I suspect we will keep it as Fertility though.
