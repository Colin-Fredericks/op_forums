    On the ww2 specific deities, i have used them as hooks for a bunch of stuff in my games, but as most people might guess, considerably tweaked/deconstructed them, working from the idea of them as false new pantheons created by a number of deities, from both within the "Axis pantheons" and outside it, to serve as a misdirection tool in their campaign of attrition against the aforementioned trio. Columbia, Brittania and Marianne were Athena under a series of disguises, Uncle Sam was actually a rescued and amnesiac Zeus, Baba Yaga & Rodina Mat as "rogue" Vanir goddesses (the hag in particular being Elli, Goddess of Old Age, from Scion: Ragnarok), some were actually demigod-level scions and not real deities, while Madame Guillotine and the Citizen were actually dangerously smooth and stealthy avatars of a mostly unknown Titan related to emotions, social dynamics, oppresion, tirany and oppresion.

    Fun times overall.

    On the matter of goddess virginity, i would point out the case of Artemis, whose vow seems to be tied to her role in helping with her brother's Apollo birth in myth and also her relationship with Orion that ended most tragically.
    Meanwhile, Athena's own vow does seem to have an element of imposition from Zeus part, but also the possibility that the woman that she is exists as a replacement of sort by Zeus of the man that should have been, what in itself raises all sorts of interesting issues. Beside this, there's the tale of Athena, Hephaestus and Erichtheus/Erichthonius, that in some of the archaic versions not only ends differently but has a far from unwilling Athena.

        The oldest version romanticizing Artemis and Orion's relationship is from a Latin source (Hyginus). There isn't a shred of that in the Greek. Hesiod's version, which Homer is consistent with, has Orion killed by Gaea (via a giant Scorpion) for his boast he would hunt and slay every animal on earth. No Apollo & Artemis drama at all (he did have the honor of hunting with Artemis, but nothing romanticizes it).

        i am positive as possible, dealing with a culture that didn't have words for the concept, that Artemis' disinterest in men, and preference for the company of women, was originally understood to be exactly what it sounds like.

                                    I'd actually thought of some ways to fix the Yankee Pantheon.

                                    Uncle Sam isn't The Uncle Sam. He's a Scion who took "embodiment of the ideals of the United States of America" as one of his mantles, and Uncle Sam just happens to be a convenient role to assume for that mantle.

                                    Likewise, Rosie the Riveter isn't really Rosie the Riveter. She is a scion turned God of Female Empowerment, Women's rights, and women who work who took Rosie as one of her Mantles.

                                    Drop Betsy Ross. The real Betsy Ross didn't even sew the flag they say she sewed. She really only seems to be their as a excuse to have a god of "Mom and Apple Pie." If you want that, replace her with a Scion turned Goddess of Mom and Apple Pie who calls herself "Mom" and drop the Betsy Ross connection entirely.

                                    Br'er Rabbit is a combination of trickster characters from African and Cherokee Folklore. If he is a god in his own right, as opposed to one of those tricksters mantling the other or just a diferant name for one of them,then he belongs in one or both of those pantheons, either instead of or in addition to the Yankees.

                                    Columbia is fine as a scion or grand scion of Athena who was either adopted by another Yankee or joined it as a god.

                                    That leaves us with Pecos Bill, Paul Bunyan, John Henry, and Johnny Appleseed. These are either characters from Tall Tales, or they're real people who inspired tall tales. They're all scions, the Tall Tales were their heroic journeys which then inspired their legends, and they all became gods towards the end of the story's/their lives.

                                    I'd also make it very, very clear that the Yankee's a young Pantheon, even younger than the nation that they're gods of. Hell, maybe even use them as an example of a bunch of Scions getting together to form their own pantheon when it's time to write the rules for that?

                                    Industry is no longer the Panthein Specific Purview, though industrial Elements might be in the new one. Instead, Industry is an application of crafting purviews...I still like the "Divine Nuke" Boon though. That'd need to be reworked and incorporated somehow.

                                    The Allied Pantheons could work similarly, but break the up into individual Pantheons instead all being the same, and for Fucks sake, where is King Arthur?
