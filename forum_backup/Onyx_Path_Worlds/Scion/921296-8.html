    Originally posted by Zeldafan42 View Post
    Honestly, Hitomi's backstory is something that's a work in progress. The key points are mostly to establish her as that "invisible middle child " who acts out for attention.     While details can give a person life, there's such a thing as conservation of detail too - your parents are world-class individuals. That should be a fundamental part of your character concept both before and after her Visitation. Details like 'world-class' stick out and demand attention, they aren't just ho-hum background notes.

    Originally posted by Zeldafan42 View Post
    The security consultant thing was actually a suggestion a friend made.     While this isn't inherently a bad thing, I think her motivation is a bit weak. Also she got her ass caught, she can't be all that good. Maybe if the contractor showed up on the park bench next to her one day or something.

    Originally posted by Zeldafan42 View Post
    As for her conception, I was thinking more of that one time Zeus did it in Greek myth, but yeah it's definitely a squicky thing even if it is an established thing in myth. I know my playgroup wouldn't find issue with it, but that's definitely a detail I'd change if I was dealing with anyone else. I'm not the type to normally include rape in my characters' back stories. I really was just going for something I saw once in a myth.
    Your character probably ought to be enraged on her behalf. She might become paranoid at the thought of someone doing that to her. How can she ever look at a boyfriend again without wondering if it's a god pretending to be him, come to seed her with his next scion? Certainly she should resent her father - it might even make her appreciate the man she thought was her sire and make sure that he understands that she appreciates how he's been such a good father to her, because Blood Isn't Everything.

    It's a violating thought that should be a bigger note for your character if you include it. You and I both know how bodily autonomy isn't a joke.

    Originally posted by Zeldafan42 View Post
    And I'll defend my use of a visual nod to Naruto by saying that the writing may be atrocious but despite the plot holes, obvious retcons between parts one and two, and the poorly written main characters...the setting was interesting, some of the supporting cast was enjoyable, and part one actually had a decent story arc going before he decided to toss out all the decent parts with the time skip. Besides, if you tell people your character is wearing an animal like mask and want a decent reference point, there's worse aesthetics you could go with than an ANBU mask. At least they have a nice simple design to them.
    It's not that Naruto is bad, though it is, it's that it borders on weeaboo to include it. Even if you're Japanese American, it's kind of a - warning sign.

    I'd suggest making reference to Japanese animal masks without the anime reference included. There's a rich and diverse history there.

                                                            I've got a background for a character concept, hopefully one of many that I'll be able to use in a game or as fodder for fiction material. I've got a few ideas, I'm just short information on pantheons to start it with.
                                                            ----------------------------------------------
                                                            Mark Rodach: Son of Brigid

                                                            Background: Mark grew up in a home filled with opposites. His father worked as a mechanical engineer by day and as an artist by night. Brought up to use his head and his heart in his decisions, he always took the hardest classes, went for the biggest roles, and spoke up for the greatest causes. He always knew the value of honor, and standing by the things he believed in, and wished to inspire others much like his father. Growing up in a world with YouTube, he began by showcasing his talents there, from Artistry, Performance, and Music, to Magic, Mechanics, and building great big things. Throughout high school, he gained popularity and tasked himself with continuing to create and have fun with doing YouTube. Mark reveled in the ability to captivate the masses through the myriads of content on his YouTube channel.

                                                            As he has been going to college, he always finds new ways to prove his ability, and create new content for his peers. Mark uses his skills, his art, and his voice as a YouTuber to forward good causes. He speaks up and speaks out for people who are downtrodden, by staging protests, running flash mobs, painting murals, and giving campus security a hell of a time, all while honoring his commitment to his channel. Mark performs weekly at a favorite bar of his with some friends in a band and does little bits of performance whenever, whether it be street magic or just busking with a guitar, to keep his skills flush. Mark always knew he was meant to inspire, and yet he feels there’s always something missing to his performance, like some small ember that’s struggling to alight.

                                                            That changed in that fateful show. Mark was up on stage, getting ready with his bandmates, beers in hand, and a set list between them. They had heard a record label might be coming to visit through some of Mark’s YouTube friends. They were really nervous, and had been deliberating over the set list for the last week. Mark peeked up from the huddle, and locked eyes with a redhead that to him looked like she had fire in her eyes. He knew, in that moment, that this show was going to be one of their best. He knew that the ember inside him had just caught flame.
                                                            After the show, Mark found the redhead at the bar and went to thank her. The woman grabbed a couple of beers and took him aside, revealing herself as his mother, Brigid. “No need to thank me,” she said, “I just lit the match. Now use your fire to light up the world.”

                                                            Description: Mark has a tall and slim build; wiry, lightly toned muscle, with short strawberry blonde hair styled with an undercut that highlights his front man face, and the shocking blue eyes. He has tattoos around his arms and back, mostly of his own design, meant to evoke interest and other varied emotions. When he’s not filming for YouTube, performing with his band, or causing general art based mischief, he can be found driving around town with his friends in a rebuilt 1970 Chevy Chevelle. His wardrobe consists of general hipster/artist fare, with special duds for shows and dates.
                                                            ----------------------------------------------
                                                            Basically, I see him as being someone who uses his skills to inspire and forge new paths.
                                                            I'll be writing up his actual visitation fiction later, as I know how that goes, there's just a bit of background that i'm not terribly sure about.

                                                            I've also got a Scion of Hestia sitting in my list of things to write-up, both background and visitation fiction.
                                                            Enjoy!
