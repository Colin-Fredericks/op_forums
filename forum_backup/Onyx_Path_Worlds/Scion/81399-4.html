                            There are definitely some powers that require your target to understand the language you are speaking, so that they can follow your commands to the letter. However, epic socials are generally going to transcend the need for many senses. This is especially true if you have epic perception of your own (and if you do not, that is a whole different set of problems).

                            Even if you cannot hear, you can still see their body language, feel the vibrations of their words against your skin, and taste their emotional state in the air. Even if you cannot see, you can still smell the chemical changes in their body, and hear the whisper of the wind whenever they move their body. If you have multiple permanent handicaps at once then your storyteller may give you some bonuses to resisting those powers, in exchange for all the penalties you are going to be suffering in your daily adventuring. Otherwise whatever happened probably counts as a stunt.

                            Basically, when Odysseus' men plugged their ears to row past the Sirens, they were performing a stunt against a difficulty appropriate to their prowess.
