    As settings go, it would be fairly ridiculous to grant Christianity primacy over other religions and pantheons in Scion. One of the most baseline assumptions of the Christian faith is downright wrong: That there is only one true god.

    Does that mean that nothing is there? No, of course not. Does it mean that anyone is truly the King of Kings? Probably not. Does it mean that anyone is the God of Gods? Oh hell no. Does that mean no one can believe these things? Of course not. People believe all kinds of things, even when they're not true.
