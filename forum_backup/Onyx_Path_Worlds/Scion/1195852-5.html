                        So here is a good question. What are the skills for 2e? Or abilities if you are going by 1e's rules.
                        Assuming you don't have the Manuscript so the snarky answer of "They start on Origins, Page 60" won't help.
                        • Academics - Book Learning, particularly the Humanities like legal, political, and bureaucratic systems. Also contributes to how many languages you can be fluent in.
                        • Athletics - Physical training and conditioning. Determines how much you can lift, and also is used for making attacks with bows and javelins.
                        • Culture - Represents your understanding of different societies and cultures. Encompasses understanding of art, literature, and religious practices from around the world.
                        • Close Combat - Used for hitting people who are in your face. Due to the mutable nature of the skills, can also be used for knowing about fighting styles.
                        • Empathy - Social Awareness. Helps you understand other people.
                        • Firearms - For all your gun-based needs. Mostly for shooting, but could also be used to represent how well you know your guns.
                        • Integrity - Your ability to resist emotional influence. Often used to resist mind control effects.
                        • Leadership - Your ability to lead and coordinate groups. If you're taking the position of say, a project director, you'll need this.
                        • Medicine - For figuring out how someone's hurting and how to fix it.
                        • Occult - Your knowledge of the supernatural, and the spooky scary things. Good for monsters, rituals, and knowing the histories of the various pantheons.
                        • Persuasion - How easily you can influence people, one-on-one.
                        • Pilot - Your ability to make an object of transportation work, whether it be a car, a motorcycle, a horse, or a jet.
                        • Science - Your understanding of the Scientific Method and your ability to parse out evidence from observations. Note that you can use this with Occultic stuff, such as using tissue tests to see what the weakness of this monster may be.
                        • Subterfuge - Ability to deceive others, hide, and create disguises. If you're doing stuff the cops wouldn't approve of and it's not murder, you'll be using THIS a lot.
                        • Survival - Your knowledge of the general outdoors. Navigation, scavenging, putting together simple tools from resources around, taming local wildlife... D&D Ranger Skills.
                        • Technology - Your understanding of modern devices. Equally good for computers and other electronics, or fixing giant machines. Note that this skill may differ in era-specific games.

                                            Originally posted by Sessha View Post
                                            Also how did they change the Epic Attributes in this system? Is that still like it was in the old system where your parent deity gave you certain affinities but you were free to pick and choose which ones you wanted to do?
                                            Epic Attributes don't really exist, at least not in the way they used to... The three Epic Physicals are Purviews now, Epic Appearance is now the Beauty Purview, and most of the powers that would have been in the other Attributes are spread across the eleven Callings, which describe essentially the kind of God or mythic archetype of a Hero you're aiming to be... These are Creator, Guardian, Healer, Hunter, Judge, Leader, Liminal, Lover, Sage, Trickster and Warrior.

                                            Each God has a list of three Callings. Each Scion must share at least one Calling with their parent starting out. You also get any single one of your parent's Purviews to channel innately (along with the signature pantheon Purview of course) that you don't need birthrights to channel. That's it. You no longer get any XP discounts, so you're completely free to follow any Purview you choose without penalty.

                                                    Originally posted by Sessha View Post
                                                    So how did the change the Pantheon Specific Purviews for the new system?
                                                    They function just like normal Purviews... They each have a passive power that you get just for having access to them (and you always have access to your PSP) and beyond that you have Boons in them that you can use as normal. They can also be used to work Marvels (free form Purview magic) just like all Purviews can.

                                                    Some of the PSPs have changed themes since 1e though... The Aesir now have Wyrd, their own brand of Fate magic instead of Jotunblut; the Theoi have a penchant for shape shifting through their Purview of Metamorphosis; the Teotl PSP has evolved into being about both accepting AND repaying sacrifice, and is called Nextlahualli, Repayment of Debt; the Deva focus on the pathways of union with the divine that is Yoga; the Shen now focus on the Bureaucracy of Heaven through their Purview of Tianming; and the Manitou, being new this edition, are rocking the dream quests and spirits of Dodaem.

                                                    The Orisha still have Possession as a PSP; the Kami still have Eight Million Kami, which has had its name changed but is doing the same thing as the 1e Purview; the Netjer are still working with the soul and name magic of Heku; the Tuatha, finally, are channeling Geasa and Bardic Knowledge through their PSP of Geasa.
