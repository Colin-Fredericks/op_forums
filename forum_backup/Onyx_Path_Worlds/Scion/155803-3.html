                            Originally posted by varygoode View Post
                            ​So, is weapon accuracy no longer a thing? Is it absorbed into weapon damage? Or is something happening that you guys didn't put up?
                            Weapon Accuracy is no longer a thing in Scion 2.0. You can use the higher of the weapon’s Damage or Accuracy as you see fit. We are going to revise the weapons at some point, but that is a distant project that will come after everything else has been revised.
                            Originally posted by varygoode View Post
                            ​Do you guys do different things besides stunts to regain Willpower? Is there a timing, like with healing, or some other mechanic? How do you do it?
                            Just like normal Scion, everyone regains all their willpower at the end of the story. You can also regain willpower through stunts, knacks, and boons that grant willpower. Even non-player characters are gaining small stunts and regaining willpower at the end of every scene or whenever they accomplish goals.
                            Originally posted by varygoode View Post
                            I see you do stunting a bit differently, but it seems kind of confusing. Do you no longer give dice for stunts at all? Do players have to wait until the end of the scene to hear how their stunts went? Please explain how you're doing it and what you've changed from RAW, if you don't mind.
                            It’s actually a lot more simple! You award a big pile of stunt at the end of every scene, and whenever the band accomplishes something awesome within a scene. For a Legend 2 band the average would be 6 Stunt, but you can easily award more if your players are describing things in a really impressive way, or if you just want a higher powered game.

                            Stunts no longer award dice, mostly because it very quickly becomes a trivial bonus and one more thing everyone needs to add when they are figuring out how many dice they need to roll. A lot of people use cheat sheets for that kind of thing, but it still slows the game down a little bit.

                            More importantly, awarding everything at once helps the players to stay in character instead of breaking the flow of the game and asking how many stunt dice they get. It also helps to balance things out between outgoing players who frequently ask for stunts and shy players who have a really hard time asking for stunts.
                            Originally posted by varygoode View Post
                            Also, and this can be a separate response (because the three sections before this are of utmost importance to me), what are you all thinking on the Dying Boxes issue now that John replied on Onyx Path?
                            We are still thinking the same thing. The entire mechanic is a leaky boat that should simply be replaced with a shiny new boat instead of a bunch of patches.

                            Apparently John already allows damage to wrap around into aggravated, so he is using the same 'shiny boat' in combination with dying boxes, except that he allows his players to choose if damage goes into dying boxes or aggravated boxes.We strongly feel that our greater aggravated soak and balance of dice adders makes that kind of juggling unnecessary, and the rule already has an extremely strong track record because it is the same thing they use for New World of Darkness.

                            In some sense, keeping Dying Boxes may not even make a difference in your game. The storyteller may simply increase or decrease the power of your enemies to match it. So it ultimately comes down to doing whatever your players think is the most fun!
