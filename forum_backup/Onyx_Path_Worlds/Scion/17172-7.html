                                    There’s also the page count concern: the books are going to be crowded enough as is without doubling or tripling the size of the Boons section. It’s why I prefer formulations of a “cloud of Boons” that don’t require a massive inflation of the number of Boons.
                                    Originally posted by Telgar View Post
                                    The whole Epics-in-Purviews is actually part of a much larger discussion about how Epic Attributes work. They're really two distinct systems: Knacks and Epics. I'd actually love to see Knacks moved over to Abilities and divorced totally from Epic Attributes. That gives Scions three kinds of powers: Knacks, Epics and Boons. They can choose to be magical (Boons), superhumanly powerful (Epics) or superhumanly talented (Knacks). In this way, it kinda mimics the Trinity system of Psions, Aberrants and Talents. You can choose which aspect of your character you want to focus on, or dabble in all three! It's utterly flexible and removes a lot of the issues the original system had.
                                    While I agree with this in theory, I have a few problems with some of the specifics. In particular, completely divorcing Knacks from Epics strips out all of the flavor from the Epic Attributes: they become mere dice-adders. And Scion needs less in the way of game systems that merely add dice, not more.

                                    That said, I’m not opposed to revising the relationship between Epics and Knacks: Knacks should be about new or specialized uses of your Attributes — IMHO, a Knack should be useful even without a single dot in the associated Attribute’s Epic. OTOH, if Epics are strictly dice adders, then there should be Knacks whose effectiveness scales up with the Epics, providing that there’s no attribute roll involved. If such a roll is involved, then the Epic is already factored in through that roll.

                                    I also like the idea of something Knack-like for Abilities — that is, something that adds new or specialized uses to your Abilities. In the past, I’ve called them “feats”; but that’s semantics. There’s also the issue of Arête to consider, though: either these feats should be integrated into the Dodekatheon PSP, or the Dodekatheon needs a new PSP. My preference is the former; but if you could show me an alternative PSP for the Greek Pantheon that suits them as well as Arête does, I might change my mind.

                                    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
