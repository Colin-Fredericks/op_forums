    [1e]Road to Route 51

    06-22-2020, 09:09 AM
    Hey folks!
    I'm running a game of 1e and figured I'd share what i've got for villains so far for my group and how I expect the stories to progress. I'm always open for feedback or ideas of course(especially ideas for Tiana) so don't hold back. I do realise that these villains have stuff that isn't official or isn't mechanically viable, but the players understand that as well; hence a section called "The rules of fate" XD

    Name: Hasani Ibn Qualavu
    Moniker: The Guitarist
    Parent: Kalfu(Loa)
    Hasani was obsessed with the band "Powerman 5000"(PM5K) long before his visitation, and upon being given his birthrights, began to test what he could do with his power and the songs of the band. Eventually, amidst all the chaos his song testing was causing, Hasani began researching other Scions of his calibre. To do so, he collated strange or suspicious news stories from around the world over the past several months and searched for any that had patterns that seemed to match a PM5K song, managed to find two. Became aware of the PCs tracking him and attempted to scare them away by ramping up his use of his abilities. When they simply pressed harder, he fought back long enough to cripple the PCs so he could flee. The PCs were able to find his research after he fled, including a note simply stating "Supernova goes pop ~ Berlin, Germany" and "When worlds collide ~ New Orleans, Louisiana"
    The rules of fate: What hasani can do is not rules legal. The closest thing is the knack "Instant Hypnosis", but he doesn't have it. Mechanically, as far as the players understand, if Hasani sings a song to a Mortal; that mortal develops 1 aggravated damage(he can repeat the song or add more to stack them). As long as the mortal has this aggravated damage, they are subject to the effect of the song sung to them. The players have also figured out that the more complex a song(i.e. the more unique lines), the most things he can do with it. But complex songs are weaker, with the simple songs(those tending toward just being a chorus) providing stronger effects but being very limited in their capacity.
    Future plans: Hasani will become a victim of his own abuse of fate. His life will at all opportunities conform to whatever PM5K song suits. As such, he can later be exploited and forced to do certain things if the PCs actions with relation to him conform to a particular song(effectively, he becomes fatebound to a discography). [PLAN]He'll die/be beaten at sunset on Route 51 in america, after singing the song "no white flags" for the first and only time.

    Known:

    A Is For Apathy: 1 victim, repeated applications of the song resulted in a situation reminiscent of the "Sloth" victim in the movie Se7en
    Die on Your Feet: Used in conjunction with "Sid Vicious in a dress". If the man subject to the song stands from his knees, his wife(subject to this and "Sid vicious-") would attempt to stab him to death. Otherwise he must simply endure a beating by his wife
    Heroes And Villians: No defined ability yet. Was used in the fight with the PCs. Did serve to carry clues that the Swat teams infiltrating the building with the PCs were working with the enemy
    How to Be a Human: Intended to be a debuff. Preferably something that makes the PCs feel more human. Interested in ideas
    I Want to Kill You: His most used song. Makes mortals attempt to kill the first coworker they see. Attacks are at full intention and always preceeded by the statement "And you want to kill me too"
    No White Flags: End of the road. The players already know this is a song in the band's discography and are concerned about what he can do with it. In truth the plan is that he will be forced by fate to sing it, and constrained by it to be unable to flee from the fight; leading to his demise.
    Nobody's Real: Was used over time to create general paranoia in a low income area of Cairo, coming to a head in a street brawl/riot of innocent civilians
    Sid Vicious In a Dress: Turns women(and women only) into sadistic, cruel, drug/alcohol fueled psychoes. Early attempts use "Beat cop" stats, later attempts use "Harpy" stats
    Supernova Goes Pop: Used to find "Supernova", explains the entire plot of the story arc introducing supernova, hints at Supernova's resurrection. Will later be used to Buff Supernova during combat
    When Worlds Collide: Used to find Tiana. Other abilities/uses unknown yet


    Name: Unknown
    Monker: Supernova
    Parent: Huitzilopochtli(Atzlanti)
    The Supernova is the title given to this 7'6" source of destruction. In addition to being an immense combat powerhouse with ridiculous soak, the Supernova has the ability to regenerate legend and willpower while on a rampage. He's become locked into following the path of the Hungarian Conquest of Europe(954ad) through germany, through some unknown mechanism. The PCs were barely able to stop his rampage, apparently killing him before his Jaguar companion grabbed his wrist and both vanished. Most of his rampage was disguised by police and the german government as "terrorist attacks", with many witnesses reporting the sounds of explosions(an interpretation of the sound of his punches) and some mortal minds remembering him as a tank in hindsight due to the impossibility of what they were seeing.
    The rules of fate: The supernova has a 3p character option; Nagual birthright. His Jaguar is a separate entity that shares his soul.
    Future Plans: Through use of the song "Supernova goes pop", Hasani will be able to bring the Supernova back to life by using [abstracted] voodoo magic to tie his soul to a new vessel. This feat would normally not be possible, as that kind of magic can only move a soul, not bring it back from the afterlife. But as the Nagual Jaguar bears his soul, it can be rebound to his corpse and the body can repair supernaturally. Hasani will however tip the corpse into a vat of combined molten plastic and steel(specifically playing to the line "and now I'm made of plastic, wire, and steel"). The liquids will not destroy the body before it heals, and will instead result in a monstrous mortal-ichor-plastic/metal amalgamation. I don't yet know if i'll add any mechanical effect to this change. Supernova's ultimate demise is not yet known.

    Name: Tiana Facis
    Moniker: -
    Parent: ? Currently unsure if I want Tiana to be a Scion of an egyptian deity, or be a medusa.
    A waitress in New Orleans, Tiana became disillusioned with the world when her father left the family unexpectantly four years ago. Having to sell the restaraunt she had helped him buy in order to pay off the debts, Tiana has grown angrier and angrier to the point that she now spends her off time directing poor hunters into dangerous areas, or escorting tourists to their deaths. She's also a frequent force driving social unrest in her area, resulting in several riots and a large amount of collateral deaths.
    Future plans: The only thing I know for certain, is that Tiana will play the friendly patron role to the PCs in New Orleans as they attempt to find the dangerous entity that Hasani is attempting to recruit in the area. If given the option, she will lead the PCs into dangerous areas and feign her own death/attack in order to get the PCs hurt of killed.
