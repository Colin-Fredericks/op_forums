    Rebalanced Gods, Sort Of [Homebrew]

    11-30-2013, 03:17 PM
    While there are many, many criticisms I would level against Scion, one of the most annoyingly obvious ones is the rather extreme variation in the number of purviews. Just take a look at Odin. So in a fit of boredom, I went through and rebalanced the gods. Each god gets three Epic Attributes and three Purviews, not counting their pantheon one. "Epics" have been omitted so I didn't have to type it a million times. When I used purviews outside of the core book, I put a core replacement in parentheses next to it.

    Due to the way the Pantheons work out unevenly (mythology is not particularly neat), I would suggest sticking in a "Favored Boon" houserule if you use these, letting each player pick one Epic Attribute and one Purview to treat as associated.

    Aesir (Norse)

    Pantheon Purview: Jotunblut

    Baldur: Appearance, Charisma, Stamina, Guardian, Justice, Sun

    Freya: Appearance, Charisma, Strength, Death, Health, War

    Freyr: Appearance, Epic Charisma, Epic Dexterity, Fertility, Health, Sun

    Frigg: Charisma, Intelligence, Wits, Magic, Prophecy, Sky

    Heimdall: Perception, Stamina, Intelligence, Guardian, Justice, Prophecy

    Hel: Appearance, Manipulation, Stamina, Darkness, Death, Justice

    Loki: Intelligence, Wits, Manipulation, Fire, Illusion (Chaos), Magic

    Odin: Intelligence, Manipulation, Wits, Death, Magic, War

    Sif: Appearance, Charisma, Stamina, Earth, Fertility, Mystery

    Thor: Strength, Stamina, Charisma, Guardian, Fertility, Sky

    Tyr: Charisma, Stamina, Perception, Justice, Stars (Guardian), War

    Vidar: Stamina, Strength, Wits, Justice, Guardian, War

    Amatsukami (Japanese)

    Pantheon Purview: Tsukumo-Gami

    Amaterasu: Appearance, Charisma, Strength, Guardian, Fertility, Sun

    Hachiman: Charisma, Dexterity, Wits, Animal (Dove), Guardian, War

    Izanagi: Charisma, Strength, Intelligence, Health, Psychopomp, Sky

    Izanami: Appearance, Charisma, Wits, Darkness, Death, Earth

    Raiden: Appearance, Dexterity, Strength, Guardian, Sky, War

    Susano-o: Strength, Dexterity, Wits, Chaos, Sky, Water

    Tsuki-yomi: Charisma, Perception, Stamina, Darkness, Moon, Psychopomp

    Atzlanti (Aztec)

    Pantheon Purview: Itztli

    Huitzilopochtli: Charisma, Stamina, Strength, Animal (Hummingbird), Sun, War

    Miclantecuhtli: Appearance, Charisma, Perception, Animal (Dog), Death, Guardian

    Quetzalcoátl: Charisma, Intelligence, Wits, Fertility, Sky, Water

    Tezcatlipoca: Dexterity, Manipulation, Wits, Darkness, Mystery, War

    Tlaloc: Appearance, Strength, Charisma, Earth, Fertility, Water

    Tlazoltéotl: Appearance, Charisma, Manipulation, Death, Earth, Justice

    Xipe Totec: Stamina, Appearance, Charisma, Death, Fertility, Health

    Dodekatheon (Greek)

    Pantheon Purview: Arete

    Aphrodite: Appearance, Manipulation, Charisma, Fertility, Health, Water

    Apollo: Dexterity, Appearance, Charisma Health, Prophecy, Sun

    Ares: Stamina, Strength, Wits, Chaos, Guardian, War

    Artemis: Dexterity, Perception, Appearance, Animal (Deer), Health, Moon

    Athena: Dexterity, Intelligence, Perception, Animal (Owl), Justice, War

    Dionysus: Charisma, Stamina, Manipulation, Chaos, Fertility, Mystery

    Hades: Charisma, Manipulation, Perception, Darkness, Death, Earth

    Hephaestus: Strength, Stamina, Intelligence, Earth, Fire, Illusion (Psychopomp)

    Hera: Stamina, Charisma, Manipulation, Animal (Peacock), Health, Magic

    Hermes: Dexterity, Intelligence, Wits, Illusion (Death), Magic, Psychopomp

    Poseidon: Strength, Charisma, Manipulation, Animal (Horse), Earth, Water

    Zeus: Charisma, Manipulation, Strength, Animal (Eagle), Justice, Sky

    Loa (Voodoo)

    Pantheon Purview: Cheval

    Baron Samedi: Charisma, Manipulation, Dexterity, Death, Healing, Psychopomp

    Damballa: Charisma, Intelligence, Wits, Animal (Snake), Health, Sky

    Erzulie: Appearance, Charisma, Strength, Guardian, Justice, War

    Kalfu: Charisma, Manipulation, Strength, Darkness, Magic, Moon

    Legba: Charisma, Wits, Perception, Mystery, Prophecy, Psychopomp

    Ogoun: Manipulation, Strength, Intelligence, Earth, Fire, War

    Shango: Charisma, Strength, Stamina, Justice, Prophecy, Sky

    Pesedjet (Egypt)

    Pantheon Purview: Heku

    Anubis: Perception, Intelligence, Manipulation, Animal (Jackal), Death, Justice

    Atum-Re: Charisma, Manipulation, Perception, Animal (Falcon), Earth, Sun

    Bastet: Dexterity, Charisma, Wits, Animal (Cat), Moon, Sun

    Geb: Perception, Stamina, Wits, Earth, Fertility, Justice

    Horus: Strength, Dexterity, Charisma, Guardian, Justice, Sky

    Isis: Charisma, Manipulation, Intelligence, Health, Magic, Stars (Death)

    Osiris: Charisma, Manipulation, Perception, Death, Fertility, Justice

    Ptah: Intelligence, Charisma, Strength, Earth, Fire, Psychopomp

    Set: Strength, Manipulation, Wits, Chaos, Guardian, War

    Sobek: Strength, Stamina, Perception, Animal (Crocodile), Fertility, Water

    Thoth: Intelligence, Wits, Charisma, Justice, Magic, Moon

    -----

    I'm probably going to run through and do the Companion pantheons and the Yazata later, but that's enough for today. If you have any criticisms or just want to ask why I chose something, I will happily nerd out about mythology with you.

              *reads post.*

              *facepalms*

              *double-facepalms*

              *grows additional arms, Hindu-god-style to effectively INFINITE facepalms*

              Where do I even start?

              I can agree with you that a number of the powers given to gods of the various pantheons are ridiculous or unbalanced. However, I will start by saying that I think that giving all gods an equal number of Epics and Purviews is not a good solution. It forces you to grant gods powers they should not have and take away powers they definitely should.

              If you insist upon the three Epics, three Purviews rule, how about this? The player gets 3 Epics and 3 Purviews favored, no matter what. If their parent has more, they have to pare down to the ones they prefer. If their parent has less, they can fill in.

              And, in particular, I'm going to focus on your treatment of the Aztec Gods, since they're my favorites and my personal pet peeve.

              Originally posted by Mr.Bookworm View Post

              Atzlanti (Aztec)

              Pantheon Purview: Itztli

              Huitzilopochtli: Charisma, Stamina, Strength, Animal (Hummingbird), Sun, War

              Miclantecuhtli: Appearance, Charisma, Perception, Animal (Dog), Death, Guardian

              Quetzalcoátl: Charisma, Intelligence, Wits, Fertility, Sky, Water

              Tezcatlipoca: Dexterity, Manipulation, Wits, Darkness, Mystery, War

              Tlaloc: Appearance, Strength, Charisma, Earth, Fertility, Water

              Tlazoltéotl: Appearance, Charisma, Manipulation, Death, Earth, Justice

              Xipe Totec: Stamina, Appearance, Charisma, Death, Fertility, Health
              It seems to me that you just picked random purviews given to the gods in the original book. Now, the original book screwed up the Aztecs like you wouldn't believe, but this is not an improvement by any stretch of the imagination.

              I will give you some credit: In your restrictive way, both Huitzilopochtli and Xipe Totec have powers that fit. Partly because those gods are the most straightforward. The pwoers you give Xipe are the ones I stick with (even though Death and Health are weaker connotations for Our Lord the Flayed One), and despite paring down Death and Guardian (and keeping Stamina, which to be fair isn't that bad), all of Huitzilopochtli's fit him. I will not be as kind to your other choices...

              Mictlantecuhtli has Animal, but he's not associated with dogs. That's Xolotl, the Aztec Psychopomp. His totem animals are many, including bats, owls, and spiders, all animals associated with death and who served Mictlantecuhtli in the underworld. Perception doesn't fit at all, his main myth involved him not sensing that the bones he was guarding were missing until Quetzalcoatl was halfway through the underworld. That also means that he's not exactly the best Guardian. And Charisma's on the fence, I'd say yes due to his scary presence and the Day of the Dead celebrations, but it's a toughie.

              Quetzalcoatl should not have Water. Ever. This was a bad decision in the RAW and it's worse here, what with paring down all his powers (Earth, Justice, and Water all do not fit Q at ALL, and a number of other RAW powers are shaky). Charisma is also iffy, given that his time ruling Tula involved his influence slowly being taken away from him by Tezcatlipoca. Adding Wits was a good idea, but not adding Stamina, given both his ascetic nature and tendency to die and be reborn, is not. Sky is a default power of his, as a wind god, but Fertility is a weaker association (giving people agriculture, as well as shaping people by grinding bones and his own blood like cornmeal and water to make tortillas). Stronger associations are Animal (both the resplendant quetzal and the rattlesnake are his sacred animals), Health (with his creator-diety nature), Psychopomp (getting through and eventually escaping the underworld, plus using the wind to propel the sun across the sky in one myth cycle) and Stars (he is the planet Venus).

              Now we have Tezcatlipoca. You kept Dexterity. The. STUPIDEST. Association. EVER given to an Aztec god in the whole game. Second only to the tie-decision of giving Quetzalcoatl Earth and Water. Tezcatlipoca is troublesome because the Aztecs placed him on a level close to omnipotence, but his power was not physical in any way (there are no myths showing physical prowess in anything). You shot yourself in the fiit giving Tezcat only three purviews, since he does a LOT in the religion and has a lot of powers, but Mystery and War are two of the weakest associations for him. When I was trying to remake the Aztecs from the ground up, the starting purviews I gave Tez were Animal (Jaguar), Darkness, and Magic, because those three are his strongest associations. AND HE HAS A LOT OF STRONG ASSOCIATIONS! Some given in the raw, like Moon and Sun, don't make sense. However, some powers like Chaos and Earth fit Tezcat very well. Charisma is a big one! That's how he can do horrible thing after horrible thing

              Tlaloc is a sky god. He is a god of rain, clouds, and thunder. Rain god does not equal water god. Mythic associations with water are present, but not to a great extent (his wife, Chalchiuhtlicue, was the goddess of bodies of water). Rain in Scion is clearly the domain of Sky, not Water. The Boon paths make that clear. As for Strength and Charisma... urgh. Neither really fit him. MAYBE Strength if you go by the Mayan Chac myths, since RAW makes Chac and Tlaloc the same dude (he isn't, and some STs do separate the two, even though I personally think for game purposes, you might as well make them the same dude), but Charisma? No.

              Tlazolteotl... have to be honest, you giving her Death made me make an enraged sound that startled my dogs and made me have to take a few deep breaths. Tlazolteotl isn't a Death goddess. Period. It's not something she does at all. Kudos on adding Justice, that works. But seriously, Death? Health works for her, what with childbirth connotations and the fact that she's associated with both STDs and healing steam baths (Hell, the latter makes her connection to Water stronger! but seriously, she shouldn't get Water just for that). And Charisma? Appearance works, since she's gorgeous, and Manipulation sort of works given her tricking people into sleeping with her, but she was never beloved by the pantheon the way others are. If anything, give her Perception, what with her abilities to find wrongdoers and suss out sin and punish it.

              And this is just one pantheon. This sort of thing is found throughout your post. I just needed to focus so that this didn't turn 50 pages long.

              If you want a better version of the various pantheons, go here: http://www.gothambynight.com/scion/pantheons.htm Some (the Kami, the Shen) haven't been redone, and I disagree with some of their choices, but they put a ton of effort and research into this. Check them out. They do good work.

                          My solution was to simply allow favored Epics and Purviews to grant one free channel of a knack or boon per game. Not one for each favored, but one to be divided between all favored. This creates a situation where your players can emulate their god parent in a visible and dramatic way, but choosing gods with numerous associations only gives your players more options. Not more raw power. It has proven very fun and effective across many playtests.

                          But no matter what you choose to do, I definitely recommend getting rid of the XP discount associated with favored Epics and Purviews. It encourages people to make choices entirely based on how many favored they can wrangle, and XP is primarily an OOC benefit not an IC benefit.
