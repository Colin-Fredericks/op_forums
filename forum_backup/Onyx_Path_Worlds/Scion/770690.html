    Some Relic and Birthright Ideas

    11-21-2015, 09:29 AM
    Sadly, I can't find a section in Scion Hero that provides a guide on how to create birthrights. Somebody correct me if I'm wrong, but I think that might be listed in Companion?

    Anyways, I found a website that gives a pretty easy run down of how to make some birthrights and came up with some of my own. If any of you have your own, feel free to share!


    NOTE: The number located in () marks the rank / cost of the birthright based on this chart


    Philosopher's Stone (2): A lapis colored gemstone embedded into a ring that is stylized with the alchemical symbol of a circle inside a square, inside a triangle, inside another circle. It grants access to the Health and Earth purviews.


    Dragon Needle (3): Said to be sharp enough to pierce dragon scales, The hilt of the sword is fashioned in the image of an oriental dragon with the blade protruding from its mouth. It adds +2L to the Wakizashi template and allows the wielder to channel the Fire purview.


    Elixir of Immortality (4): At first glance, this appears to be an ornate drinking gourd, fashioned out of pure white material with the kanji for "life" stenciled into the sides. This relic allows the wielder to channel the Guardian and Health purviews. It also has the power to regenerate anything placed inside it (commonly liquor) over night!


    Iron Stick (5): Made from a chipped piece of Sun Wukong's iron staff, this relic adds +4B to the Bo template and has the power to extend (as well as retract) in length, allowing it to strike targets up to an additional Legend x5 yards away!


    Gate Crasher (4): A kite shield made from volcanic rock that holds the power to bust open the gates to the Underworld. It allows the wielder access to the Death purview. It can also bring the dead back to life by allowing the wielder to journey to the Underworld on a quest, provided the subject's legend is smaller than the one attempting to bring them back.


    Mischief Maker (3): Made from a fragment of Loki's sword its design resembles that of a desert eagle, but is roughly half its normal size, making it appear less dangerous. Adds 2L to the Desert Eagle template. It also allows the wielder to channel the Chaos purview.


    Heart of the Forest (4): Commissioned by Amaterasu and forged by woodland kami, it is said to have the power to restore the world's natural beauty. The shaft is carved from sturdy oak and bound in vines. The head piece of the staff is a slightly oversize rose bud which blooms when its power is activated. It adds +2B to the Bo template and allows the wielder access to the Fertility and Sun purviews.

                                              Spartan MK4 Exosuit (4 Attributes, 1 Purview): Hephaestus is responsible for the creation of many tools and weapons that allowed the Dodekatheon to face down impossible odds and emerge victorious. As the age of man became ever more advanced, so too did the smith god's creations. The Spartan MK4 Exosuit is mechanical suit of armor which augments the wearer's physical performance, turning them into the perfect warrior, or at least as close to perfect as the wearer can manage. The machinery powering the suit enhances the wearer's Strength by 2 and improves their Dexterity and Stamina by 1 each. It also enables the wearer to channel the War purview. Aside from its affects on one's physical enhancements, the Spartan MK4 Exosuit affords the same protection as a suit of Riot Gear on the armor chart.


                                              Oracle's Lamp (2 Attributes, 1 Purview): Incense has often been associated with rituals of meditation and prayer. At times, it has also been said to cause visions and hallucinations in those who are exposed to its aeromic smoke. This relic is a lamp commonly used in such meditative rituals, the style of which can vary between cultures. When incense is placed inside of it and burned, it allows the Scion to channel the Prophecy purview. It also increases their Intelligence by 2, clearing their minds and allowing the visions to come through more clearly.


                                              Shears of Fate (2 purviews, 1 unique ability): In Greek mythology, the tapestry of fate is administered to by three sisters who use a pair of scissors to cut the thread that signals the end of one's time in this world. This relic is believed to be a replica of the very shears used by the three sisters, now repurposed for Scion use. Having directly influenced the weave of Fate for time beyond measure, the Shears of Fate hold great power for those with the courage to wield them. The scissors grant the wielder access to the Prophecy and Death purviews. Additionally, Fate does not appreciate the unworthy meddling in its affairs. If the relic is stolen, the thief faces a terrible curse. Once per week, a randomly selected loved one will die until such time as the shears are returned to worthy hands, preferably the rightful owner.

                                                      My only real critique is the Spartan Armor.
                                                      Having a birthright that bumps up your attributes as long as it's on you, without cost, let alone by 4 whole points, is seriously overpowered. Boosting your attributes tends to cost 2 birthright points for every 1 point in an attribute, so even a 5 point birthright can only boost a maximum of 2 attribute points without some sort of massive deficit. Something of that power level needs to have a drawback, or else it's practically unfair to everybody else in the campaign.
                                                      Unless, of course, everybody in the campaign is equipped with the armor, which rather defeats the purpose.

                                                      My suggestion, since I don't want you to think the armor is a bad idea, would be to 1) Reduce the bonus from the armor. Maybe it boosts two Physical attributes by a single point and you get to choose which ones, because the suits are specialized? That would keep it a 5 point birthright.
                                                      Or 2) Give it some sort of drawback or downside. Such as the mechanics are solely solar powered, meaning the suit works to aid your attributes only in direct sunlight, but becomes mere heavy armor when indoors or at night or in too dark of shade. Or else it has a finite power source, requiring some sort of mythical (or an abundance of mundane) fuel the character must try to maintain a steady supply of. This also adds to the backstory of the relic, making it seem more genuinely mythical.
                                                      And remember, these are (in this world) actual artifacts of mythology and ancient history. Creating a unique and diverse backstory them is half the fun!!

                                                      All that being said, it is your campaign after all, and you get to decide how birthrights work and whether they fit your desired realm. These are just suggestions for ease of play and happy, cohesive player interaction.
                                                      I really like your other relics as well (especially the penitent blade, I might steal that for an upcoming campaign), and I hope you keep posting them!
