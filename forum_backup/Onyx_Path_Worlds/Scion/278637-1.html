                            Story Ideas



                            Hero: By whatever means it happened, you became awakened to the spiritual world, and learned you were conceived by a Totem. But your spirit is out of balance, and you have strayed too far from the natural world. They have sent you a guide to lead you on the proper path, and to even greater rewards, should you stay the course. Maybe you'll follow in the footsteps of your parent. Maybe you'll carve your own path towards spirituality. The choice is up to you, and you're spirit guide will show you the way towards your destiny.


                            Demi God: Something has happened, the spirit world is in chaos... more so than usual. The Totems are gone and nature has no guardians to lead it. Then you find out why, the gods and goddesses of the human pantheons have abducted the Totems and locked them in cages like wild beasts. Without Widow to grant permission, souls can not travel through Widow's Web, and are trapped in her webs, awaiting judgement in the realm between heaven and hell. Even Serpent wasn't able to escape their prejudice, much to your amusement! This blatant disrespect can not be tolerated, but you know the consequences of challenging a full god in your current state. So it's time to stage a jail break, instead!


                            God: The Totems are free and now they count you among their number. No longer are you a wandering shaman or a vision quester, you are one with the animal pantheon. Will you forgive the other gods for their disrespect and focus on the Titans, or will you show them what wild animals do when you intrude upon their lair?


                            Ragnarok: Set into the distant future, mankind has grown callous and arrogant. Having been pushed to desperation by the Titans and the warring gods, they seek to create more effective weapons and defenses. Of course no man made weapon is going to put a dent in the Gods, but that doesn't stop them from polluting the earth and torturing animals in their attempts to prove otherwise. The Totems feel the spirit world crying out for mercy, and they know it's pain for it is mirrored within them. As their habitats are destroyed and their animals slaughtered, the Totems feel their power dwindling a little more. Nature is losing its power in the face of military expansion, and the Totems which govern it are gradually dying. If the Totemic Pantheon can not restore the balance of nature and heal the land, they will die, and so will the spirit world with it. These are the End Times!

                                                    Here's what I got so far.



                                                    Shamanism: In the old religions, people who claimed to have a connection with the spirit world and could communicate with it were called Shamans. The power of Shamanism is the Pantheon specific Purview for the Totemic Pantheons. By using artistic depictions of spirits, the user is able to perform various spells and rituals. Sometimes these depictions are in the form of carved statues and figurines. Other time, they take the form of ritual tattoos and body paint. Whatever the case may be, a user of Shamanism is never truly alone, so long as the spirits are there to answer his or her call.


                                                    Level 1 - Totem Figurine: The user carves an animal statue that is small enough to fit in someone's palm. Anyone who is in physical possession of the figurine gains +1 to an epic attribute that would be associated with that animal. Such as Epic Strength for a bear, or Epic Dexterity for a rabbit. Although a person can own multiple figurines, they can only gain a benefit once from each animal. So carving 5 bear figurines is just a waste if you plan to use them all yourself.


                                                    Level 2 -


                                                    Level 3 - Spiritual Absorption: This somewhat gross boon allows the user to gain bestial talent in various skills by consuming an associated body part of an animal. Eating the legs of a rabbit might allow them to run faster or jump higher, consuming the ears of a dog may improve their audio capacity, or eating the claws of a cat may give them unusually sharp fingernails. The effects to Spiritual Absorption last for 24 hours, at which point, they must consume the body part again to renew it's effects. Also... the body parts must be eaten raw...


                                                    Level 4


                                                    Level 5 - Vision Quest: The user of this boon gains the ability to partially enter the spirit world. Their body remains in a trance-like state in the physical world, but their mind and spirit travel in another plane of existence. At the completion of a Vision Quest, the shaman often receives a prophetic warning of things to come. Novice Vision Questers who are not used to the way spirits communicate may be confused by what the visions mean, but those with the wisdom and experience of multiple visits are better skilled at deciphering them.


                                                    Level 6 - Totem Pole: This boon allows the user to carve a large totem that bares the image of 4 different animal gods or goddesses. It is a more powerful version of Totem Figurine in the sense that it provides it's gifts across a wide area, and not just to whoever is touching it at the time. Totem Pole gives a +1 to an Epic Attribute associated with each of the animals it depicts. It's effective range is 1 mile equal to the amount of Legend used to create it, and it only benefits those who the creator acknowledges as allies. Unfortunately, the Totem Pole must be recharged once a month in order to maintain it's benefits.


                                                    Level 7 -


                                                    Level 8 - Tattoo Conjuring: With this boon, the Shaman has the ability to use their own body as a summoning catalyst for various animals. By spending Legend, they can transform a tattoo into a real animal, which will separate from their body and obey their will to the best of it's ability. When the power is disabled, the animal once again merges with the user and becomes a regular tattoo again. The user can only animate tattoos based on animals in this manner, and the animal will be life size while it is seperated. Because there is a limited amount of space on someone's body, the user should be careful about choosing which animals they want at their disposal.

                                                            General Relationships: The Totemic Pantheon has an interesting relationship with the world. At first glance, many would tell you the world is chaotic and unpredictable. That only the fittest survive in the wilds, and while this may be true, it is not all that the Totems represent. They find harmony in the natural order of things. The food chain is a form of social hierarchy, with some animals working together to keep the world in balance. Destruction is a necessary act, for with its passing comes the chance for new things to grow and evolve. By facing destruction, the next generation can learn to adapt and survive until the next tragedy befalls them and the cycle continues.

                                                            It is difficult for outsiders to comprehend this philosophy, seeing a pantheon that espouses chaos while at the same time warring against the Titan of Chaos itself. The Totems claim that unrestrained chaos only brings destruction, causing the ecosystem to be thrown out of balance. Unbalanced food ratio causes starvation or possibly over eating, over population causes one species to grow too powerful, and environmental disasters kill thousands. If the chaos is skillfully orchestrated, the Totems believe it can benefit those who live within it. If the chaos runs rampant, it will only bring the doom of those affected by it.

                                                            While Canis Lupus has amassed the power needed to impose a form of society among the other Totems, his rule as Overgod is shaky at best. Much like the animal kingdom, the Totems are prone to infighting and preying upon one another for their own gain. The fact that Hart is another Totem who has garnered respect among his peers, while both Lion and Serpent have plans to steal the throne indicates that Canis has several other candidates who might replace him if he lets things fall to pieces.


                                                            The Totemic Pantheon is unpopular with many of the other Pantheons of earth. While there are many gods and goddesses who have taken the form of animals in the past, they have chosen to live by human ways, and these transformations were only temporary. The Totems, however, choose to live by the ways of animals, living in paradoxical harmony with nature, and this offends their fellow deities. What sort of divine being would cast aside his manhood to live in a cave eating raw deer carcasses? Scions who are raised in human society and voluntarily choose to join such a life style are also looked upon with disdain by those who feel themselves more civilized and respectable.


                                                            Living as beasts and following the laws of nature over civility, the Totems can be mistaken as disrespectful and savage. They do not recognize human laws or customs, only those of the spirit world. And this has lead to violence and rebellion when they encounter humans that refuse to properly respect the ways of nature. These outbursts are especially dangerous when they encounters beings of similar power who support humanity's disrespect. In fact, there have been cases in which the other Pantheons view the Totems as a threat to thousands of years of progress. In their eyes, if they allowed humans to go back to worshiping the beasts of the land, it could result in devolving society back to the stone age, and that simply will not do.


                                                            There are times when the Totems have blended into human society before, such as when they are looking to produce Scions. Only the more perverted or fanatical cults are willing to mate with animals directly. Most would prefer a human lover at the time, and so the Totems adopt human disguises. Considering sexual activity to be perfectly natural, they are far lest modest about such things, and have trouble understanding why humans are so sensitive about the subject. They have also used their disguises as a means of keeping a watch over the human population. By doing so, they can monitor whether or not they are posing a threat to the spirits, and hopefully move to stop it before it goes too far. Regardless, they still feel most comfortable in the wilds, and spend most of their time in animal form.


                                                            The Totems are not mindless animals, however. Even the pantheon's founders were born and raised human. They did not decide to become animals completely. They embody the spirituality of what that animal represents, but they still retain their sentient minds. This has caused many foes in the past to underestimate them, being caught by surprise when what they think are wild beasts demonstrate keen insight and strategy.


                                                            Scions of the Pantheon can expect two kinds of discrimination depending on their origins. Those who are natively born into the pantheon, can expect to be treated like animals by the more prejudice oriented Scions. Those who are adopted into the pantheon can expect other forms of ridicule, with such comments like "Were you raised by wolves!?" to be a common response to their actions.


                                                            The beast headed gods of Egypt have found an accord with the Totems, however, and are perhaps the only Pantheon the Totems can reliably call an ally. While their Egyptian counterparts are not as focused on their animal characteristics, they do have a close enough relation to animals that they don't feel complete disdain for the Totems.


                                                            ------------------------------------


                                                            Scion Relationships: During their visitation, most Scions find that their parent is rather alien and difficult to comprehend. As spiritual beings, they are removed from the limits of physical and scientific understanding. During their Hero years, many Scions of this pantheon have been known to struggle with understanding the will of the gods as they try to find the correct path towards spiritual harmony. In an effort to gain a deeper level of understanding, Totem Heroes are driven to commune with the wilds, partaking in ceremonies and rituals that strengthen one's connection to the spirit world. Once they obtain the ability, they may also spend extended periods of time on a Vision Quest, learning to decipher the meaning behind the Totem's cryptic messages.

                                                            As they later progress to Demi Gods, they find their parents are easier to understand. They now have a firm concept of what it means to be a spiritual entity, and following the proper path is easier to recognize. They are also more in touch with their inner beast and see the world with a less human perspective and clouded judgement. Now that they have learned how to understand the spirit world, they turn their focus to harmonizing with their animal. Demi God is the stage where most Scions of this pantheon start to lose touch with human society.

                                                            Upon finally ascending to godhood, the Scions of the Totems achieve a state of enlightenment. Now as fully spiritual beings, they are freed of the restrictive preconceptions of humanity and the physical world. But with this enlightenment also comes its own share of problems, for now that they are detached from human civilization, they find associating with it to be more difficult. Additionally, their parent's enemies now turn their attention on them, considering them as potential rivals or possibly allies they can turn against their parent. Ascending into the ranks of the Totems thrusts the Scion into the chaotic power plays and infighting of the pantheon.


                                                            A Scion of the Totems can choose to follow in their parent's foot steps, adhering to their teachings and philosophy. Doing so often causes the Scion to take on the traits of a species related to their parent. However, if the Scion has the will to follow their own path to ascension, they have been known to take on the characteristics of completely different animals. Doing this broadens the ranks of what the Pantheon has to offer, but adding a new animal spirit is considered to be a difficult quest, for it includes forming their own philosophy and guidelines in which to follow. Without a parent to guide them, Scions who choose this path often take longer to reach godhood than one who follows another's example.



                                                            When it comes to parenting, Serpent is easily the worst out of the Totems. As the god of cruelty and black magic, his Scions are known to exhibit signs of racism, sadism, blood lust, sexism, power hungry, and dementia. They often develop the features of various reptiles including snakes and lizards. Scions who choose to shun their dark father and seek a more noble invoke the wrath of Serpent who considers them unworthy of his blood. Not only this, but the other Totems and their children view them as evil and untrustworthy simply for carrying the dark essence of Serpent within them. During their mortal years, Serpent plans and schemes different ways in which to make his rebellious offspring fall. Those who die in their quests are claimed by Serpent in the afterlife as slaves, where they are tormented by his more loyal followers, as a constant warning to any other Scions who would dare refuse his teachings. Those who manage to succeed in reaching godhood, however, are considered to be purified of Serpent's tainted essence, and they have every reason to hate the snake god. Serpent is usually furious that not only has one of his children escaped his grasp, but they have now been added to his ever growing list of enemies.



                                                            -------------------------------------


                                                            Mortal Relationships: Only the cults dedicated to worshiping the Totems appear to have the wisdom necessary to comprehend their actions. The worship ceremonies they conduct harken back to the days of tribal rituals and involve the use of body paint, masks, frantic dancing, partaking of drugs, a great hunt, or offering sacrifices. Totems visit their worshipers in the forms of animals, man-beasts, or even human form wearing the pelt of the animal they represent.

                                                            The priests of their cults bare the title of Shaman, and are recognized as those who have a deep connection to the spirit world. In the Totemic religion, nobody can just decide to become a Shaman. Instead, the Totems hand pick their priests, visiting them in their dreams and communicating their desires, which the Shaman then passes along to their cult.

                                                            Siring within their cult is the easiest way for a Totem to produce a Scion. Their followers consider any such child to have the potential to become a powerful Shaman, and this belief is reinforced by the Scion manifesting their divine powers. These cults have been known to offer up virgins of either sex in a mating ritual, with the hopes that their Totem will see fit to bless them with such a child. However, while their cults may be the easiest way, it is not the only way they seek.

                                                            If the mortal world proves to be overstepping its bounds, the Totems have been known to breed with the more civilized portion of society. They do this because they hope that by raising the child in human dominated society, they can become an arbiter for both realms, and figure out a way to solve the problem that doesn't result in open warfare. Sadly, being raised by humans means they grow addicted to human comforts, and their ability to view the spiritual side of the argument is diminished from those raised within the cult.


                                                            The largest collection of Totem worshipers belong to tribal communities. In the west, this includes the ranks of the Native American tribes. However, while they are the more traditional cultists, they are not their only source of worship. Traveling hippies and reclusive druids have also been known to pay homage to the Totem spirits, as do medicine men and crystal healers. Overall, anyone who feels a deep connection to the land and its creatures can potentially be a Totem worshiper.
