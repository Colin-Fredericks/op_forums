        Originally posted by evilnerf View Post
        A cool project for someone more up on their mythology than me would be to find all of the places that would have tutelary gods.
        It would be a colossal list honestly. I believe the major Deva all have sacred cities that function as a cult center, almost all of the major Kami have been claimed as familial tutelary deities (instead of the patrons of a city, tutelary deities can be expressed as the patrons of specific families), and as Gnomish American has mentioned, a similar situation to the Deva existed for the Netjer in Egypt. From what we know of the Canaanites, each of their cities would have a major patron, similarly for the Mesopotamians (such as Babylon with Marduk, or Ur with Sin the Moon God).

        Tutelary Deities are incredibly popular. Well, actually, that's a bad way to explain it. Popular Deities tend to become Tutelary Deities, as specific regions, families, or nations want the patronage of specific figures.

        Edit: I am being lazy and not doing readings. So, here's a handful of Tutelary Deities off the top of my head. Marduk with Babylon, Sin with Ur, Ishtar with Uruk, Athena with Athens, Juno, Jupiter, and Minerva with Rome, Navan Fort (Emain Macha) possibly with Macha, Amaterasu for the Japanese Imperial Line, Hachiman for the Minamoto family, Susano-O and Okuninushi with the priestly line of Izumo, and Ame-no-Koyane along with Takemikazuchi with the Fujiwara family.

                        Originally posted by Dave Brookshaw View Post

                        IIRC, Demigod.
                        Yeah; from what I recall, the idea is that Heroes rely on Divine gear (i.e., Previews are channeled through Relics); Demigods start treating Purviews as powers; and Gods gain the ability to improvise new uses for their Purviews on the fly (i.e., marvels). You have the same Purviews at all tiers; but how they manifest depend on the tier.

                        Conversely, the new ways of accessing Purviews that are unlocked when you attain a new tier does not render the older ways obsolete: Thor still has and uses his hammer, despite having both a Demigod's ability to call on particular Boons directly and a God's ability to work marvels.
