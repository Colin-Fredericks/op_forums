    What book topic would you like to see in Scion 2E?

    01-11-2014, 02:29 AM
    So far we know the outline for the four main books (though I hope that OP will have example pantheons that could be used in Scion. I would love to see Donnie, Horace, Bridgette, Himiko, Aaron, and Eric back again.) What kind of books would be helpful in expanding the Scion universe for your game? Any wish-fulfilling thoughts on topics?

    The two book topics that I would like to see is:

    1. A book of Gods and Goddesses. There are other gods and goddesses in the pantheons that also can be used as divine parents for Scions. In the Pesdajet, Maat wasn't only a concept, she was also a Goddess of Order and wife to Thoth. There was also deities that were listed in the pantheon that were relegated to side blurbs like Anubis's son Wepawer and Horus's children, and weren't included like the hippo goddess of childbirth (I forget her name) and Bes, the dwarf god. There was a ton of Greek and Roman gods as well, like Morpheous (see thread on new purviews) and Terminus (Roman God of Paths), Eris (Goddess of Discord), and Nemesis (Goddess of Vengence). And who knows who was left out of First Edition that was part of a certain pantheon. A book detailing the minor gods and goddesses will expand on the flavor of Scions in general.

    2. A book of other worlds or realities. Just like how the WoD has the shard book "Mirrors", Scion could have a book detailing of other worlds that Scions can visit or other realities where things happened differently (kind of like a Scion version of the show Sliders.) Imagine if Zeus and the other deities were the ones locked in Tarturus as Titans and the Titans were the Olympian gods and goddesses. You could run into a version of yourself who's a Scion of Chronus, who can use the Time Purview. Imagine going into a reality where the pantheons war against each other to be dominate in a Pantheon War. James Lovegrove wrote about this in his godpunk novels Age of Ra, and Age of Aztec. Onyx Path could call these realities Twists (insert your "What a Twist!" joke here), because in the grand scheme of Fate, these different realities are essentially Twists of Fate.

    Any ideas for Onyx Path to use in future books?

                              1) Book of Pantheons: One per Pantheon. This gives a brief history of the pantheon, splats for the major and minor players, heroes, and creatures of that pantheon. Unique advantages and disadvantages for being a member of the pantheon. Secrets, relics, artifacts. Duties, rules, expectations. This could also show 'sub pantheons', smaller cliques within the larger pantheon that you can join. I think one book might want to go into depth about making your own clique, and how to eventually get this made into its own pantheon.

                              1a) Actually, to expand on it some: I would love to see the codes for each pantheon. For example, I have a list of the laws of Ma'at - these are the things you say to Anubis as your soul is being weighed against a feather - and this pretty much defines what is expected from follows of the faith. I've also got the 'code' for the Greeks, and for the Romans, and of course, there's Bushido for the Japanese faith. I'm sure we can find similar things for other fails as well. That way, if you're playing someone from the Egyptian Pantheon, you'll be well aware of what your conduct is expected to be.

                              2) Terra Incognita: One per Pantheon. The realms that exist, what's expected there, what you're going to encounter, how it plays with your abilities. It should also provide the usual - additional creatures, heroes, monsters, artifacts, relics. Perhaps some abilities you can only get if you go there and do the proper quests / find the proper trainer. Discussing how to get around, what touchstones are and how they work, and ultimately, how to make your own.

                              3) I want to say 'a book of powers', but it goes a bit beyond that. I'd rather see, scattered through the various books, how to develop powers, make your own, alternate paths if such a thing will still be viable. I would also love to see something like V:tR's Devotions. What happens if you mix power X with power Y? What kind of effect will you get? I want to see the abilities of the scions be more fluid and less 'if you have Fire X, it does Y', with no room to wiggle.

                              4) Balance between Offence and Defence. I really really don't want to see 'One Attribute to Rule Them All'. (I'm looking at you, Dexterity). I really want to see a balance between Attributes, so that yes, if you do focus on one, you'll rock in that field, but that doesn't mean you'll rock in the other field. For example, Dexterity may make me rock in throwing and ranged combat, but it should not mean that I'll automatically do craptonnes of damage with every hit.

                              5) I absolutely want to see something concrete said about the setting itself. Is the 'canon' setting one where the gods have a lot more worship on Earth? Are they all secret cults? Is it more a mirror of our world until the scions peel back the layers? If an ogre goes rampaging through the streets, and a scion hurls a care at him, will people see exactly that, or will they try to explain it in a manner that makes sense for the mundane world, ala Percy Jackson? Will they freak out, or take pictures? Will the newspapers scream about terrorist attacks and be amazed, or will they say 'a demigod battles an ogre in the streets of Kanata today'..? I know people will want to run their own settings, of course, but I want to know what the 'base 0' setting is. Even V:tR has a base 0 that people can agree on.
