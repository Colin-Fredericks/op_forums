    [Fiction Thread] My Little Reaper

    03-04-2016, 12:47 AM
    Story Details: Being the son of a God isn't always easy, especially when you're the son of one of the gloomiest deities in mythology. Join Cooper Cross as he takes on the role of Hades' bounty hunter and takes on some of the most wanted escapees from the Underworld!

    While the purpose of this thread is to include chapters for the story, I do appreciate constructive feed back from readers to help improve the story's quality and content.


    -----------------------------------


    The year was 2,000 and shit was going crazy. While the World was counting down to the start of the new millenium, the Gods were running damage control as somebody decided to pull the biggest jail break in history. The gates of Tartarus had been blown open, the various Underworlds were scattered through the void, and the heavens were under siege by creatures that haven't walked the world in thousands of years.


    The Titans were free, and they were raging pissed! The Gods of Olympus were caught with their metaphorical pants down, but they adjusted quickly to the break out. Hephaestus arrived with two wagon loads of lightning bolts just as Zeus ran out of ammo blasting a hundred handed giant off his mountain.


    The lame God of the Forge didn't even wait for Zeus's acknowledgement as he hobbled at top speed back to his work shop. The amazons were in dire need of more arrows and some fresh spears, and Ares was still waiting on his sword to be sharpened!


    Hades stood atop the mountain with bandages covering his wounds. As the Greek lord of the underworld, he had been at ground zero during the Titan attack and was messed up pretty badly in that fight. But as a God, he recovered quickly, and he held his pitch fork over head to issue a challenge to the forces below.


    Beside his elder brother, Poseidon brandished his trident and unleashed an earth quake that shook some of the mountain loose, sending tons of rubble falling down upon the Titan's armies. In days long past, the three brothers who governed the heavens, the sea and the dead were powerful enough to quell these invaders with relative ease. But they had not made their presence known to mortals for a very long time, and their power had waned since then. Zeus, Poseidon and Hades were showing the faintest signs of exhaustion from this surprise attack.


    "I believe we will need some reinforcements for this battle." said Athena, one of the wisest of all Olympians, if not the wisest. She stood in full battle gear while issuing coordinated tactics to the Olympians and their servants.


    "This isn't a battle, it's a war!" exclaimed Ares who was brandishing two Spartan swords and flung himself off the side of the mountain to engage the armies below. Athena looked away in disgust as her brother did what he was best at and started repopulating the Underworld.



    "For once, you may be correct." Athena said under her breath before calling out. "Hermes, it's time to call in the contingency plan!" it was mere seconds before the messenger of the Gods appeared before her.


    The Goddess of wisdom and battle presented Hermes with a golden scroll wrapped in blue silk and told him to visit the names listed there as fast as possible. The fleet footed God scoffed at the request as if she were calling his abilities into question, and then zoomed off so fast, he seemed to vanish into thin air.


    ------------------------------------


    The year is now 2016, and the new millenium was not what people hoped it would be. In fact, a lot of the world had gone to shit in the last 16 years, and showed no signs of recovering any time soon.


    Most of the world chalked it up to random acts of nature, or humans being stupid. But the truth was much darker. There was a war going on between supernatural forces, and fallout was leaking into the mortal world.


    And thus we come to Cooper Cross, an American boy of 16 years who was sitting through class, trying not to fall asleep. He was not the most enthusiastic about school, especially when the teachers droned on in their lectures with not an ounce of passion in their voice. It was as if they were suddenly regretting their life choices in mid lecture!


    But there was light at the end of the tunnel for Cooper, as spring break was only a week away. Soon he would be free of this dark, dreary place and partying it up somewhere more sunny and warm. Or at least, that was his intention, but it seemed that fate had other plans in store for him.


    As mentioned earlier, Cooper was 16 years old. A white boy with short black hair that curled near the ends. His eyes were an unusually bright shade of green, almost like emeralds which some people complimented him on the first few times they noticed. Being a member of the track running team, he had an athletic build, but wasn't as buff as some other jocks. His muscles were more sleek and elegant, designed to sprint long distances and jump over obstacles rather than plow through them.


    So when the final bell rang, Cooper was one of the first out the door, a bit of a spring in his step as he counted down yet another day before his vacation. Most days, he would usually ride the bus home, but he had decided to try staying in shape this week, so he was going to jog his way home. Besides, he only lived a 10 minute drive away; how far of a run could that be?


    As it turns out, mechanical vehicles travel a lost faster than human legs, and it was almost half an hour before Cooper was sweating from his decision to run home. He was going to need to take a shower as soon as he walked in the door at this rate. He decided to stop for a rest in the nearby park and took a seat on one of the benches. After a few moments, he laid down with an arm crossed over his face to block the sun out.


    "Just a few minutes..." he said, closing his eyes. But he accidentally drifted off to sleep without realizing it, and that was when the nightmare began...


    The next thing Cooper was aware of, he was standing on a piece of volcanic earth floating in the air. The sound was filled with wailing screams and moans as fires and sulfuric gas churned around in the background. Looking over the side of his floating platform, he saw people under attack by monsters which he immediately considered to be demons, given the circumstances.


    "Am I in... hell?" he asked to nobody in particular, and nearly jumped out of his skin when somebody answered him only a few inches away.


    "Not quite, but there's hardly a difference at this point." said a gruff, angry voice told him as Cooper quickly spun around to see a man standing over him. A man dressed in the style of ancient Greece. A man with gray skin and a very displeased grimmace on his face.


    "My name is Hades, and this is my realm." he introduced himself, "It had been plunged into chaos since the Titans broke free of this prison and I am no longer capable of maintaining the order by myself." Cooper looked at him in utter confusion before passing another glance off the side of the platform. He watched as a woman was torn from her clothing by a surly beast while a man beside her was eaten alive for trying to defend her.


    "You're the God of the Underworld, aren't you?" Cooper finally asked, turning back to the gray skinned man. "What do you want with me?" he followed up, wondering why he was having a dream like this.


    "I rule one possible afterlife, yes." Hades answered, "and I don't have a lot of time, so I'll be blunt. You're my son and I need you to help me get things back into working order." Cooper balked at that response, but the God of the Underworld was not finished.


    "I'm in need of a bounty hunter, you see. Some very nasty things escaped from this place and there are lost souls who have yet to find their way into my realm." he paused and gave Cooper time to absorb this knowledge. "Your job is to hunt them down and escort them back here. If they put up a struggle... well, just use a little police brutality."


    "But how am I-" Cooper began to protest before he got an extremely blunt and unapologetic quip from the God claiming to be his father.


    "Your parents are dead, take this, get to work." to which Hades then tossed him a kama (one handed scythe) Cooper had just enough time to grab it out of the air before he snapped out of his dream, sitting up on the bench.


    It took a couple moments for him to regain his senses, but once he did, Cooper saw he was holding his hand up in the air with a kama clutched in his fist. He starred at it in amazement, noticing that the weapon seemed to have been fashioned out of human bone, instead of wood or metal. Suddenly he recalled something far more important that Hades told him.


    His parents were dead...


    "Fuck!" Cooper shouted, as he climbed off the bench. He had been asleep for longer than he thought. At least a couple hours had gone by at this point. He took off in full sprint, hoping to make it back home as quickly as possible, and paying no heed to how he may have looked running through the streets with a deadly weapon in his hands.


    Maybe it was the surge of adrenaline, but Cooper reached his house much faster than expected, and didn't even feel winded by his full on sprint. As he raced up the drive way, he saw a blood splattered hand print on the front door with the door knob missing. A big hole was blown in the door like somebody had shot the knob off. Without thinking, Cooper barged through the door, brandishing the kama and shouted just shy of screeching.

    "Mom, Dad, where are you!?"


    End of Chapter 1.

      The house was a nightmare, with blood trails splashed on the walls and floor. It looked like whatever was responsible for the scene had been literally dragged through the house. Cooper didn't hear signs of a struggle, which he hoped meant whoever the attacker was had been long gone. But as he finally arrived at his parent's bedroom, he found their blood soaked bodies strewn over their bed. Cooper was already screaming too much to notice the message written on the wall in their blood.

      The one reading "Death to Olympus!"

      Still screaming, Cooper nearly jumped out of his skin when he felt a hand placed on his shoulder and quickly spun around to see a rather handsome faced man holding a scroll case in his other hand. He managed to regain his composure long enough to demand the man's identity, to which he answered curtly that he was Hermes.


      "The messenger of the Gods?" Cooper asked rhetorically, still tense about finding his parents's remains. He passed them another look before reflexively turning his gaze back upon the man in the room.


      "Only for the Dodekatheon, but yes. That's me." Hermes answered before gently waving the scroll, "And I have orders to give you this." he said, extending his hand to offer the scroll to the boy who hesitantly took it.

      "It's Hades' wanted list. It has information on all those who escaped from Tartarus. You're supposed to bring them back." Hermes explained, "It's also self updating, so you don't have to worry about filling out paperwork."


      Cooper looked at the scroll in his hand which from what he could tell was fashioned out of leathery skin with a pair of bones attached to the ends of it. As he unfurled the scroll, he saw a fully detailed drawing of a cyclops, along with a list of crimes and potential whereabouts written beside it. The thing that stuck to Cooper most of all, however, was the latest crime on the list. The murder of Mr. and Mrs. Cross, Cooper's parents!


      "This is the guy who killed them!" Cooper exclaimed, glaring daggers at the scroll. He only managed to look away when a cough from Hermes drew his attention.


      "Your father didn't have time to explain how that works..." Hermes said, pointing to the bone kama that Cooper didn't remember fastening to his waist in all the confusion. When he noticed the boy's curiosity, he went on.

      "Alright, here's the deal." Hermes began, "As long as you're carrying that thing, you can channel the powers of death itself. That's good because you're going to need them if you plan to round up Hades' escapees. It's not that impressive right now, but show your father that you're worthy and he may see about upgrading it." the fleet footed god smiled at that last part.


      Cooper Cross reached down with one hand and plucked the weapon from his hip as he inspected it. The handle still looked like a human shin bone while the blade was forged out of some kind of metal that was so black, it was like staring into a shadow. No light reflected off its surface, and its very presence began to make Cooper nervous before he fastened it to his hip again, the weapon locking into place almost of its own accord.

      "With your weapon, you can detect the presence of ghosts." Hermes explained, "And you can ease the suffering of those who are barely clinging to life. But that is all the information I have for you now. I have other deliveries to make." and before Cooper could protest, a set of wings emerged from Hermes' feet and he vanished in the blink of an eye, leaving Cooper as the only one in the room.

      The young boy blinked a couple times, allowing this information to sink in before once again looking at the scroll he had been given and the culprit behind his family's murder. He zeroed in on the target's location and recognized the address. He wasn't far away, Cooper could still catch him if he left now!

      So that's what he did, rolling up the scroll and slipping it into his pocket before racing out of the house in persuit of vengeance!


      ----------------------------------------------


      The address was for a new youth center that had been constructed to help orphaned and abandoned children overcome the difficulties in their lives. Or at least that was what the rest of the city had been lead to believe. Truth be told, nobody could actually recall any of the kids being assigned to proper homes. Once they attended the youth center, they just kind of vanished from public notice.

      Cooper Cross was about to find out why...

      The sun had already set by the time Cooper arrived near the parking lot, instinctively hiding in the bushes to remain undetected in case of an ambush. Although it night time, the parking lot had a couple street lamps that illuminated the area, allowing Cooper to see three guards patrolling the area. They looked like older kids who should have been in highschool by now, they also looked ready to pick a fight at any moment judging by their aggressive body language.


      "Why the fuck do I have to be on guard duty?" demanded one of the guards loudly as he passed under the lamp post, "I was supposed to be part of the ritual this time!" in his frustration, he punched the lamp post with his bare fist. Instead of recoiling in pain like a normal human, he pulled his hand away to reveal a small dent banged into the metal pole!


      "Somebody's blue balling!" taunted one of the other guards, causing the third to laugh at the mockery of the first. The first guard was not at all pleased with that and snarled at them.


      "Fuck you! It was my turn and he fucking knows it!" he bellowed, moving away from the lamp post to continue his patrol.


      "Well he's the one supplying us with the blood, so he's the one that calls the shots." said the third guard.


      "Blood?" Cooper asked himself, "This better not be a nest of vampires, I swear to... whoever!" He unfurled the scroll again and looked at it. His target was definitely a cyclops. Rolling the scroll back up again, he kept low to the shadows and crept his way to the youth center's doors.


      He almost didn't realize that he was suddenly quite skilled at remaining undetected. He didn't know why, but the knowledge just seemed to come to him by instinct, rather than formal training. Within a couple minutes, he was inside the facility, leaving the disgruntled guard behind to deal with his embarrassing partners.

      Once inside the youth center, he began to hear a rather boistrous commotion from further down the hallway. Still attempting to remain stealthy, Cooper carefully walked down the hall to peer around a door frame to what looked like a class room of some kind. Except there were no desks and chair, leaving the room quite barren and spacious.

      Inside, Cooper saw about half a dozen other teenagers in the room. They were paired off with a much younger boy kneeling between them. Or rather being forced to kneel as the teens on each side of them were holding their arms back and their heads forward. At the front of the class room was an enormous man nearly as wide as the door frame and with a single, massive eye in the center of his forehead. That was his target, and Cooper immediately grabbed his weapon in anger, ready to attack before he head shouts coming from the opposite side of the hall that sounded like people calling for help.

      Forcing his rage back for now, Cooper made his way to the other side to be greeted with a shocking sight. From the looks of things, it appeared to be the girl's bunk house as it was full of beds with young girls laying in them. Except after looking closely, Cooper realized they weren't laying in bed voluntarily, but had actually been shackled to them.

      Their clothes were torn, their hair was out of place and they had several bruises. Cooper already knew what their situation was even before he noticed the single male in the room forcing himself on one of the girls in a fit of lust. She had been the one screaming for help and looked to have less injuries than the other girls. That could only mean that she was new.

      Flinging the door open, Cooper stormed in to the bunk house with his bone kama at the ready as he issued a challenge.


      "Get off her!" he demanded, leveling the weapon at the rapist who looked to be one of the teenage boys. He turned angrily to Cooper who finally realized he was the guard from outside. he had probably left his post after getting fed up with the others.


      "Who the fuck are you!?" the guard shouted as Cooper charged at him and swung his blade. The angry teen tried to block it with his arm, but the blade sank through to the bone, nearly severing his arm in one strike.

      Blood sprayed from the wound as the teen clutched his arm, falling to the ground and going into shock. He bellowed loudly and Cooper knew that trouble was coming his way just as the guard passed out from blood loss.


      "The blood, it's his blood!" exclaimed one of the girls frantically. Cooper thought she was freaking out over what he had done, but she quickly began to correct him.


      "The cyclops!" she urged, "He forces the boys to drink his blood, it turns them into that!" she explained. Suddenly, Cooper remembered the three captive boys from the other room who had been forced to kneel before his target.


      "Oh shit..." Cooper muttered before the bunk house doors were torn off their hinges and about nine big angry teens stormed into the place. Behind them, the cyclops waded his way through, glaring at Cooper standing over the injured boy.


      "Well what do we have here?" he asked with a grin that practically oozed malice and sadism...

      End of Chapter 2.

          "Children?" Cooper asked to nobody in particular. The word seemed to imply that he wasn't the only one hunting this monster. His suspicions were confirmed with one of the girls called promised to help him fight if he could free her. Cooper noticed it was the same girl who had warned him about the cyclops blood from before.

          Out of all the girls collected, she looked the most beaten up and bedraggled. Whoever she was, she had suffered the most abuse at the hands of her captors. She also couldn't have been more than a year or two older than Cooper was. Her golden blond hair was matted and frayed while her clothes were stripped to practically ribons Yet despite the physical injuries, the look in her eyes suggested she was still ready to crack some skulls. All she needed was somebody to free her from her restraints.


          "Quiet, whore!" the one eyed Giant shouted, but Cooper was already on the move as he dove for the bed, raising his bone kama and striking the blade against the chain that had been shackled to her neck.

          After the sudden burst of adrenaline, Cooper expected the chain to require a few more strikes, but when he saw the links begin to snap like buttons, he stepped back as the girl tore her collar off like it was made of paper and jumped out of bed.


          "Kill them both!" the cyclops shouted as the nine boys charged at them. Cooper was about to swing his weapon when a battle cry from his unknown ally caught him by surprise.


          With one punch, she lifted a boy off the ground and sent him hurdling through the air to crash into a couple of the others, knocking all three of them to the floor. Cooper managed to suppress his awe long enough to slash another boy across the chest, sending him toppling to the floor as he clutched his wound.


          "Of all the scions you could have tortured, you idiots picked a daughter of Zeus!" the girl chastised, lifting another couple of boys off the ground by their throats and slamming their heads together before dropping them like rag dolls to the floor.


          Six of the boys had been taken down now, with Cooper being fairly certain he was inflicting lethal injuries. The ones taken out by his ally were probably just unconscious from the beat down they were receiving. Regardless, they now had more elbow room as they advanced towards the cyclops who was retreating back down the hall.


          "He's getting away!" Cooper exclaimed, burying half his blade into his enemy's skull while the last two boys were back handed against the wall and collapsed. At last, all the teens had been dealt with.


          "No, he's making space. He knows he can't fight us in these hallways." the incredibly strong girl corrected him. "We can rescue the girls later. Right now I want to rip that eye out so he can watch me kill him!"

          Cooper looked around at the captive girls and then the augmented boys sprawled around the room. It didn't look like they were going to cause any further trouble, so he nodded and promised to come back for them later.


          "I'm Cooper, by the way." he introduced himself to his ally. She nodded and introduced herself as Samantha, the two of them shaking hands as a sign of respect for each other's skills.


          "Child of Hades, was it? I guess we're cousins then." Samantha mused before the two of them turned and began chasing the cyclops who had succeeded in making his way to the parking lot. the double doors being flung wide open for him.


          Cooper and Samantha had just barely crossed the threshold of the doorway when a car was sent flying towards their location. Samantha shoved Cooper off to the side before the vehicle slammed into her and sent her flying back into the building, rolling across the floor.

          "You know." said the Cyclops loudly, getting Cooper's attention, "It took five of my thralls to catch her off guard last time. But you..." he paused long enough to lift another car over his head, "You don't seem to have that same level of strength."


          With that, he hurled the car again, Cooper having just barely managed to roll out of the way before it struck his location and bounced along the pavement. He finally climbed to his feet again and brandished his weapon, really wishing he could do more than just sea ghosts and provide mercy killings right now.


          "You killed my parents!" he exclaimed angrily, charging the cyclops with the intend to eviscerate him. Super strength or not, he was going to kill this thing even if he had to go down with it.


          "That doesn't make you a super hero!" the cyclops retorted, responding to Cooper's charge with one of his own, the two of them racing for a head on clash. As they approached, the cyclops reached a hand behind his back and drew forth a weapon. Cooper had just barely enough time to recognize it as being Roman in style before they clashed.

          Cooper's blade managed to parry the weapon and prevent it from cleaving his head off, but the force of the giant's strength still knocked him off his feet as he rolled backward. He looked up from his prone stance on the ground to see the cyclops leering at him as he held his own weapon up to see.

          "I took this from the girl. I'll be taking yours soon enough." the cyclops threatened. The weapon he was holding looked to be a spatha, the same kind of sword used by the Roman legion thousands of years ago. The short length of the blade made the ancient sword look more like a dagger in the giant's meaty fist.


          "I don't think so!" came Samantha's voice just before a busted up car dropped directly on top of the cyclops. He gave a muffled grunt of surprise as the weapon was knocked from his grip and went sailing through the air. Cooper followed it with his eyes to see Samantha catch it by the handle, seemingly not intimidated by the possibility of slicing her hand off.


          "Ah, it feels good to have this back!" she cooed, admiring the weapon. By this time Cooper had forced himself back to his feet. His body was starting to feel sore, but with Samantha helping him, they might be able to pull this off.


          "Thanks for the save." Cooper muttered, gripping his weapon firmly as he moved to stand next to the daughter of Zeus. "This is kind of my first night on the job." he added as the crashed vehicle began to shutter and rise. Cooper noticed it had been the same vehicle that had almost flattened them earlier.


          "Been a couple months for me." Samantha replied, "Just follow my lead and we'll get through this." and with that, she leveled her sword at the cyclops who was now holding the car above his head with one arm and looking severely enraged as blood pooled from multiple wounds on his body.


          "Damn you... Olympians." the cyclops fumed as blood was leaking from multiple wounds on his body. It seemed even he wasn't strong enough to withstand a couple tons of steel falling on his head without some form of injury.


          "Cerberus is going to use you for a chew toy!" Cooper taunted before the two children of the gods attacked. Cooper's kama sank to the hilt in the cyclops' eye, while Samantha's sword cleaved his stomach open. With a death roar, the cyclops tossed the car away like it was a paper ball and fell to the ground clutching his newest injuries.


          After a few seconds of spasming on the pavement, the giant began to dissolve before their very eyes, leaving nothing behind but an over sized heart. The young child of Hades was surprised, but decided to reserve his questions for later. It was hardly the most absurd thing he had seen in the last 24 hours.

          Suddenly, Cooper's scroll began to glow in his pocket, reflexively pulling it out to unroll it as Samantha looked on in curiosity. A couple seconds after unrolling the scroll, Cooper saw the bounty information of the cyclops vanish in a plume of flame which quickly dissipated to reveal a new target's information. This one appearing much more human than the previous one. He rolled the scroll back up and pocketed it again as he turned his attention back towards Samantha who was absently wiping the blood off her sword.


          "I'd probably be dead without your help tonight." Cooper said, "thanks for saving my life." he fastened his bone kama to his waist again, still unclear how the weapon just seemed to lock into place on a whim.


          "Yeah, well you saved me from becoming a sex slave. So I'd say we're even." Samantha replied, "Cyclopes are master craftsmen and those chains were specifically designed for me. I couldn't break them until you dispelled the enchantment." she explained, obviously predicting Cooper's question on how they managed to keep her prisoner in the first place.


          "He said he caught you off guard?" her cousin asked, eliciting a nod from Samantha. He let the follow up conversation hang in the air as he finally remembered that Samantha was standing in the parking lot with barely any clothing on. Her shirt and pants were strips of cloth at this point, while her shoes were missing completely.


          "Maybe we should find you a change of clothes?" Cooper suggested rhetorically. For a moment, he almost let his hormones take over before a voice in the back of his mind reminded him that they were essentially cousins as far as Greek mythology was concerned.


          "I've got a safe house." Samantha nodded, "Let's free the girls then make our way there. If we hurry, we can make it before sunrise!" the two heroes turned and headed back inside the youth center, eager to put this night behind them once and for all.

                          "Somehow, I get the feeling my capture was Hera's doing." Samantha said as she sat down on a leather couch with a green towel wrapped around her head. After the shower she had taken to clean herself up, she was now wearing a new outfit.

                          It consisted of a black tank top and jeans with knee high boots that were fastened with buckles instead of laces. Cooper noticed that at this point, she looked a lot more Gothic than Greek. But realized that she may have had good reason to be so.

                          "Hera doesn't like your kind very much, does she?" he asked, keeping in mind that she was a child of Zeus. Such children did not enjoy happy lives when their vengeful step mother learned of their existence.


                          "You studied Greek mythology?" Samantha asked, scrubbing the last of the wetness out of her hair and removing the towel to let her hair fall down. It looked out of place with the rest of her dark outfit, but she didn't seem to mind it too much as she looked at Cooper sitting across from her in an arm chair.


                          "Only the parts that are common knowledge." Cooper answered, beginning to scan around the safe house with casual glances. It was barely more than an apartment, but was surprisingly well furnished and comfortable. He had to wonder if she didn't have some kind of benefactor to set the place up for her.


                          "I did pretty good for my first night, wouldn't you think?" Cooper asked, thinking back to the battle with the cyclops. He had never been in a serious fight before in his life, and yet somehow, he managed to hold his own against the giant and his gang of angry teenagers.


                          "You should be dead by now." Samantha answered sternly, causing her cousin to blink at her in surprise. "You have some talent, I'll give you that. But that only takes you so far, you need proper training if you're going to survive as one of us." she went on.


                          "You seem to have the hang of it for somebody who's only been at it a couple months. How hard can it be?" Cooper replied confidently as he crossed his arms and leaned back in the chair, propping his feet up on the coffee table.


                          "My mother is part of a cult." Samantha quipped, "I've been raised from birth to take on this responsibility. You on the other hand seem like you just got drafted off the streets!" when she noticed the expression on Cooper's face, she realized how right she was.

                          Samantha was mortified that her life had been saved by a complete green horn, but hid it behind a stoic expression of contempt as she rose from the couch and pointed to a nearby closet.

                          "Alright," she said, "since you saved me, it's only fair I return the favor. There's a spare blanket in the closet. Your training starts tomorrow." and with that, she headed off to her bedroom, leaving Cooper with the leather couch to sleep on.


                          --------------------------------------------------


                          Much to nobody's surprise, Samantha had been absolutely correct. Cooper's movements were sloppy and disorganized, as she demonstrated by repeatedly kicking his ass in hand to hand combat. Boxing had been a disaster and there was no way Cooper was ready to clash blades with her. So instead, she decided to tone it down a bit and practice wrestling him into submission.

                          "Wow, that was... not what I was hoping for." Samantha chastised as she stood with her hands on her hips looking down at the bruised and bloodied Cooper who was glad nobody else was around to witness his public humiliation.

                          The two of them had decided to borrow a training room at the local gym. But by some trick of fate, the gym was relatively empty at this time, allowing the two scions to spar together without having to worry about witnesses.


                          "They really did just pick you off the street, didn't they?" asked Samantha as she helped her cousin to his feet and handed him a towel. They had been at it for hours and not once had he even so much as scored a draw with her.


                          "You know, you shouldn't question the god of the underworld, Samantha. It's not healthy!" said a voice that caused the two of them to jump in surprise before turning to see Hermes standing behind them. He was leaning against one of the lockers as if he had been waiting on them to finish.


                          "Lord Hermes!" Samantha gasped before bowing to the messenger of Olympus. Cooper followed suit, believing it was the proper thing to do, given the circumstances. In response, the god merely shrugged before pointing to Cooper, claiming to have yet another message to deliver.


                          "Your father, Hades..." he began, "Was unimpressed with how you handled the cyclops. He wanted a quick in and out job and you turned it into a massacre!" he paused and gave the inexperienced scion a mischievous grin before continuing.

                          "Hades didn't choose you to be a warrior, he chose you to be a hunter. You find your target, you kill them, you move on. None of those teenagers were on the bounty list and yet you started hacking away!" he crossed his arms and scoffed as if remembering the event first hand, "Very unprofessional of you." he finished.


                          "So I was just supposed to leave the girls prisoner forever?" Cooper asked in surprise. Hermes merely shrugged in response saying he was just the messenger and only relaying what he had been told. Samantha was just as, if not more, curious to hear what Cooper should have done in that situation, but wasn't about to start calling out the gods on their bullshit right now.


                          "Anyways, it's obvious that you aren't ready for a serious confrontation just yet, so Hades wanted me to give you this." he said before holding out a hand to reveal a shiny gold coin between his fingers.


                          "This coin will allow you to summon allies to assist you in your hunts, should the need arise. Make sure you don't accidentally spend it!" he explained before flipping the coin with his thumb. It went sailing through the air and landed in Cooper's outstretched hand perfectly. But before it reached him, Hermes was already gone again, leaving the two of them alone in the training room.


                          "You screw up a hunt and still get paid for it?" Samantha asked in disbelief, "Hades must have really big plans for you..." she couldn't hide the twinge of jealousy in her voice as she began to grab her bag. She hadn't even broken a sweat in her matches with Cooper, so she didn't need to wipe off.


                          "I'm not sure if I should be excited or terrified." Cooper said absently as he inspected the coin in his palm. It looked like it was from the roman empire, yet was as shiny as if it had been freshly minted. Slipping it into his bag where he stored his bone kama, he and Samantha made their way to the showers to wash off after a hard day's training.


                          End of Chapter 4
