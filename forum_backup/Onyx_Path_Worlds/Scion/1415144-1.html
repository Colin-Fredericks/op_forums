                        My thoughts on having fewer Callings is simply a matter of experience is all. You're not 'less powerful because you're a kid' so much as you have had less experience and time in the world to have settled deeply into your calling/s.                                 Counterpoint: Assuming you're a grown-ass adult at Origin, you have one (1) Calling Dot.

                                Then you get your Visitation, and you suddenly jump to five (5) Calling Dots. All at once, gain 4 Calling Dots. At least two of them in Callings you didn't have BEFORE the Visitation.

                                Would a Kid Character as an Origin Pre-Visitation Scion get zero Calling Dots and no Knacks because they're a kid?

                                Experience doesn't have much to do with it. It doesn't make any sense to me.

                                But granted, this is all abstraction. I bet nobody in-universe could name their Callings because Callings are an abstraction that I don't like to apply in-universe.

                                Remember, CuChulainn was defending Ulster at what, 13? Tell me he had fewer Calling Dots. His Hero Tricks were fucking bonkers and the kid was using crazy numbers of Knacks.

                                Classic myth didn't give a shit about if the Magic Hero was a kid or if they were an older adult. It's an arbitrary restriction. And I don't know about you, but I don't see many Scion games lasting long enough in-universe for the kids to not be kids anymore. So in practice you're locking away those extra Calling Dots and most players won't ever regain them.

                                No, going "You're a kid so you have fewer Calling dots" is not a good mechanic. It restricts and punishes for wanting to be different, and what does it add? Fuck all. Making things worse for no reason is not good game design.

                                Edit: When proposing a mechanic, ask yourself "Does this make sense within the game?" and "Does this actually make the game better?" The proposed "Kids have fewer Calling dots because they're kids" doesn't make sense because it applies some arbitrary restriction where there's no indication of it elsewhere, and it doesn't actually improve the game in my mind, because it's telling me to be less effective Just Because, and no I don't get anything to compensate, and you're basically going to be hobbled for most game lengths.

                                    For some reason, I feel that a key part of the kid genre - or maybe at least one of the potential focuses for such a game, I guess - is self discovery, including figuring out who you are and what you want to be. Including the "oh, cool! I can do that now!" moment of discovering new powers. On the other hand, I can certainly see the appeal of just starting with "we are the most awesome kids to have ever awesomed!" and just running with it.

                                    But I admit that I also have this tendency to want to have the transition from Origin to Hero be played out similarly to the way the transition from Hero to Demigod would be. At least if I were going to start at Origin level. If I were starting a game at Hero level, I'd just have that be blue-booked/included in prelude and/or character backstory.

                                    I think maybe the issue of Callings comes down to the question of Are these kid characters just now emerging into the world of Heroes or are they already Heroes? If the former, these I think maybe it might be appropriate to have at least the first story arc involve the characters coming into their new Callings. But if the latter, then they already have them all.

                                    So, in this context, I think the Callings would depend on how everyone agreed they wanted to start the game out.

                                    But that things like not having a Favored Approach and/or Role at certain ages, as well as gaining Momentum when adults don't take you seriously because you're "just a kid" are probably ideas that would work well regardless of if the character is already a Hero or not. (Maybe even for Demigods? I have no idea about that one.)
