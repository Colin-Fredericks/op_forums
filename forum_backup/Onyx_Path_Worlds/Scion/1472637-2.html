                Then why play Scion? It's a game that is based on the premise that the older, polytheistic faiths are still vital and important in the world, and that their deities still actively grant power and influence to their Scions. That is pretty clearly not how it works in our own world (local legends of heroes and claims of "faith healers" aside). There hasn't been a tale of a Heracles, a Cu Cullain, or a Jesus in millennia. The game is about a world where the Aztec faith wasn't annihilated, where the Norse Gods are still commonplace in their lands, where the First Nations still have their religions. It's about heroes and myths, not our own mundane reality. It loses something if it's just "reality but with Scions of God doing miracles." YMMV, of course.

                                    Answering your question.

                                    Just because its not a world full of mythical beings walking around all the time, it doens't mean its not a magical world. I like to think Heroes are not just powerful, they are unique, there are not that many of them and what they do is important,, even if they dont become gods, they are still known (as Heroes or as Urban Legends).

                                    A pretty mundane world, like ours, can be an awesome place to play as a Hero because they are unique.
                                    - They can be a select group of unique individuals fighting to bring back myths to a mundane world.
                                    - They can be the front end of a fight against a Titan that is removing all gods from history to become the only single God.
                                    - They can be the reincarnation of the old gods (like the Nemetondevos) after the pantheons were destroyed by God.
                                    - They can be all new gods after all the old ones disappeared (Part Time Gods idea)

                                    There are MANY different themes that can be played with Scion. The system is absolutely flexible and can cover all the way from a mundane to a very mythical.
