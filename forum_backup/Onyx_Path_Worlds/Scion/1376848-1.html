            Originally posted by Dataweaver View Post
            Given that this is a game, I think avoiding disrespect is good enough. You can go overboard on being respectful, to the point that it starts interfering with the game.
            I'm not an expert on, well, anything, but I can at least try to use the bare minimum of respect. And I don't think that in any way interferes with running a game or playing in one.

            I mean I'll agree that getting too focused on the details and worrying about the smallest details may not be productive to every game group, but y'know... We can TRY for basic decency.

            Like, the original idea... At its core, it's going "We know this culture existed but very little else so why not just make up a Pantheon?" and my problem with that is... If you're going to make things up completely? To the point where you have no idea if there's even any tenuous connective tissue? Why bother tying them to that culture?

            Like, if you have information here and there, but have to use conjecture and supposition to fill in some blanks, then sure. But if all we have is a name of a God (and they may not even be a God because we don't have any additional context) so you're making up who they are, what they did, what their practices were, what relationships they have with other Possible Gods (half of whom you made up because we don't even have THOSE names in any records)... Like, if you're making up 99% of a Pantheon, you might as well just commit to your own original creation. I'm sure the original Gods that are being made up are absolutely fine, and heck, may even be great fun to read about.

            I'm not going to slap Alejandro the Savior, my original character, into the Teotl as a God and insist that he was worshipped in the mid 1200s by the Aztec people*. Because they probably didn't. Yes, it's my table, and yes, it's my history of my World, but... Kali, Krishna, and Christ, I can fucking try to treat this with a modicum of respect. And in my mind, part of that Respect is "Stick to what I know is Important, find out if I don't know what's important".

            Also, what Manus noted. It's kind of like claiming "Well it doesn't matter if I insult Jim when he's not in the room".

            *Now if I'm doing something mad like, my players have agreed to a Historical Eras game and one of the PCs achieved Apotheosis in the early 13th century then sure I'll acknowledge them if a game takes place further down in the same timeline but by THIS point we've careened off into Full Historical AU Fanfiction

            Disclaimer: In favor of fun and enjoyment, but may speak up to warn you that you're gonna step on a metaphorical land mine

                        There actually is quite a lot of material available for the Nemetondevos! They have often been presented as this sort of height of a challenge due to lack of materials, but there is actually quite a lot through archeology and conjoined studies with Classical studies.

                        The big issue is that the material that is available for them tends to be rather modern and academic. Such as the recent study that shows that Cernunnos most likely had nothing to do with animals or wilderness stuff but instead was a patron of merchants and wealth. So, totally a ton of material, could make a full like 'Pantheon' for them for sure as long as Underworld and Overworld being vague and uncertain is cool.

                        I honestly was pretty sure the Nemetondevos were a lost cause until I did a bunch of research on my end. The resources are not at all easily accessible as they are all very recent innovations, but there's totally enough material to do a very good job with them! Totally a minimal amount of making stuff up is necessary. It is really heartwarming after having heard of them as an impossible task for the last decade.
