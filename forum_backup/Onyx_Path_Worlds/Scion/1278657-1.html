                    Originally posted by Wannabe Demon Lord View Post
                    The Caribbean also has overlapping pantheons, with the indigenous Taino Zemis and the later Loa.
                    Sorry, kinda missed your earlier post. So, the Zemí have a very positive relationship with all of the coastal African pantheons that ended up losing people to the Atlantic Slave Trade. These enslaved people often ended up fleeing into the woods and there joining forces with indigenous rebels to fight against the Spanish colonisers together. You have to keep in mind, though, that the Zemí are incredibly bad shape, and thus don't have a lot of weight to throw around in an alliance these days. But they do try to take political stances, and thus have clear pro-African, pro-American, and anti-European stance in their "international" politics. The African pantheons we have so far, the Orisha and the Abosom, are actually both not that close with the Zemí - the Orisha because they only arrived after most of Taíno culture, and the Abosom because little of their lore was actually carried over into Jamaica. Their closest African allies are probably the Basimbi of the Kongo people, because that was the main source of slaves for the Spanish colonies while the Zemí were still more or less around. As for the Loa, I find it very hard to take any stance on them, seeing as they are a mixture of so many different elements. A lot of Taíno elements in there, in fact. I couldn't imagine writing them, and so I don't have a very clear idea of how they'd behave either, but I assume they share the general friendship between Zemí and African-descended pantheons.

                    As for the relative niceness level of the Zemí, they are tough little cookies. They love their people and depend on them to survive, but they also believe in very strict ... uh ... parenting or something. If not worshipped enough, they will abandon their worshippers, support their enemies, or just hurricane them with extreme prejudice. They are not as horrible as the Shiunesh and not as sexy as the Theoi, some of them are downright friendly, but they also have their absolute psychopaths. So they are not exactly a cuddly pantheon. Also understandably prejudiced against White people, at least the way I'm writing them.

                                                            Yes and no.
