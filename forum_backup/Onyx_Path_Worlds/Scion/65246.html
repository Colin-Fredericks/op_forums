    What does Epic Attributes represent? (Both editions I suppose)

    02-04-2014, 08:19 PM
    What does Epic Attributes actually represent? What do they do to your character?

    Are they like Aberrants Mega-Attributes? Where even only one dot is supposed to represent a greater ability than any normal human being can ever possess. Not that this was supported by Aberrants rules, a normal human with attribute 5 consistently outdid an aberrant with attribute 2 and Mega-Attribute 1 (except for strength, who was the odd man out).

    Or are they more like 'your ability is raised with (automatic successes)x2', so that a scion with attribute 2 and Epic Attribute 2 basically has 'attribute 6'?

    Or do they represent some sort of influence of events, that the world somehow bends to make things easier for you?

    Or can you chose any of the above to be true for your character?

    So if your scion has appearance 2 and Epic Appearance 1 (the attractive variant), does this make him/her more attractive than any mortal can be? Or 'just' pretty dang attractive (the equivalent of appearance 4) or just a normal looking guy/gal who somehow has more 'luck' with appearance rolls.

      Couching this in the classic "Your mileage may vary" terms, but Epic attributes are just that.

      Having even a single rank will, under normal circumstances, prevent botches and guarantee success at most tasks (hooray auto-successes).
      A scion with mediocre attributes but a single Epic may be outstripped by a mortal with 5 dots in the same attribute and whatever ability you are using. That's simple math. It also reflects the early game - you are just a touch better than mortal men. What Epics give you that (arguably) Mega-Attributes do not, is that they open the door to knacks.

                          I only have passing familiarity with Scion, but the way that I always interpreted Epic Attributes?

                          They are mythic.

                          What do I mean by that? Well, they fulfill some aspect of your character's personal narrative as dictated by his Fate and Ichor. Your character is epically strong because his legend says that he is. Your character is epically intelligent because her legend says that she is. Whatever her intended role in the grand scheme of the story of the gods and their children and their enemies determines what Epic Attributes they develop.

                          What does that mean on a personal, sensation level? I, personally, would write it as a sense of overwhelming rightness. Not talent per se, where you have a moment of inspiration and wow yourself, but whenever you tap into your Epic Attributes, things are just right. You are where you need to be in the world. If you're epically strong, then you just feel right when you're lifting or breaking something. If you're epically smart, then you just feel right when you're puzzling out a problem or working out a mystery.

                          And so forth.
