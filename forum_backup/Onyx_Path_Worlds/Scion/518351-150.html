                                                    I've said this before, but it's a game about a fictional world where the polytheistic faiths of many cultures are all real and demonstrably involved in the lives of their faithful. It isn't a commentary on Christianity, Judaism, Islam, Jainism, Sikhism, or any of our real-world faiths (including those who still worship those same polytheistic Pantheons).                                                             The easiest solution to the monotheism issue is that a monotheistic tradition can simply claim there's a single overwhelming cosmic Godhead that is far above all the pantheons claiming to be gods. A particular god in a monotheistic tradition (e.g. El) can contend (s)he is the voice of the Godhead, or an incarnation of the Godhead, or something along those lines. That god becomes the (possibly unreliable) representative of the Godhead, and can be "good", "bad" or whatever.

                                                            After all, the gods of Scion really aren't in the same class as the truly transcendent gods of real-world monotheistic traditions. Scion's gods are powerful, but they aren't omniscient or omnipotent. So any player, or character, or NPC god can easily believe that there's something infinitely superior to the "gods" that appear in the game. You can then proceed to play the game on its own terms. ---Jim
