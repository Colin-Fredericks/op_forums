                      This ‘Ladon’ is the greater Heir of the legendary dragon from Garden of the Hesperides, that guarded Golden Apples of Hera in there. This Heir is now curator of one of major art museums, where he guards most of it’s collection – especially Theoi Relics he is very fond off.

                      o – Material from Origin book
                      h – Material from Hero book

                      Flairs: ?

                                          Well... most of the famous dragons were killed by or defeated Gods and Demigods, so real Wyrms, the big ones, are just too big to deal with on heroic level, at least in direct combat.

                                          If you want to interact with it, maybe you should use the Goliath rules from Demigod (Demigod big manuscript page 257), much like I would do to face a god. Make it scale 6+ and define how much the dragon is trying to kill you actively (in opposition to kill you by mistake when step in a building or something with you inside).

                                          This system keep the fight to last longer, even several sessions, while the characters have a chance to die, it’s not instakill. It definitely worth the reading!
