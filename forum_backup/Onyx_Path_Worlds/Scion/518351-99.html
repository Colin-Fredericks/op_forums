                                Yeah, I wouldn't want to like, be dismissive of possession, it's just I see it more in talks of Umbanda and Vodoun then native Yoruba traditions where it still happens but it's much more limited to those specifically initiated as opposed to Umbanda where anyone can become possessed, and Vodoun which kind of lies between the two. It's still totally culturally appropriate, so uhh, not trying to be a downer or something .

                                As for Ase, and making it as a concept, well I guess to come up with one would largely depend on what separates an Innate power from a Boon. Cause with ashe it's in many ways absolute but limited. In that, since it's in many ways the "ability to make things happen" in combination with "command and authority". Yet, to make use of certain ashe you have to be initiated. Yet, it's also transferrable to some extent. One interesting boon could maybe be the ability to transfer your ashe to someone. Which mechanically could look like, you taking some sort of object and spending Legend(or whatever you do to activate a Knack or Boon or what have you) and then make your roll. The result becomes how much ashe you placed into it, and then you could give that to someone, and they could unleash that singular use of it. While another Boon could maybe be like, the ability to take the ashe from another thing, and by doing so you can assume one trait from it or at an equal expenditure of Legend a certain amount of dice that thing has. Or you could find a way to send out your ashe to possess someone.

                                While, for an Innate power, I don't know, I'd have to think longer on that.

                                                Originally posted by KingCarnival View Post
                                                So PSPs are still one Boon per legend level then
                                                Every Purview has three sample Boons; every PSP has two sample Boons. They're not the only possible Boons, though. As of playtest, you only get a limited number of Boons at your level of Legend, 5 Boons total at Legend 4 (so they can max out one Purview and their PSP, or potentially have 5 different Purviews. Better have relics for all those Purviews.)

                                                PSPs and relics give motifs, or methods of using the power of Purviews.

                                                A Scion may channel one Purview of their divine parent without need of a relic. Someone mentioned that Eric Donner is often fixing up his car with Forge while he uses Giantsbane to channel Sky, and I think that's a good example.
