            The Expendables Shard: In a world where any deity is capable of producing a Scion, you play the children of lesser immortals. Not as popularly recognized as the major pantheons, you are considered B-List cannon fodder in the war against the Titans.

            Note: Sort of a comedy-action setting in which we get to see how hard the little guys work to support the big stars in the Titan war.



            Berserk Shard: In a world where humanity is subjugated and tortured at the whims of immortals, you have a chance to rise above lesser men and take your place at the top of society. But be warned, the consequences for failing are infinitely worse than being a slave.

            Note: For those who haven't read / watched Berserk, be advised the setting deals with some pretty extreme themes including, but not limited to rape, hell, psychological trauma, religious fanaticism, human sacrifice, class warfare, gender inequality, and the inquisition itself!




            It's all Wrong Shard: The pantheons have been rewritten to fit the popular modern depictions of their mythology. Some Gods are better people because of it, and some are much, much worse!

            Note: An alternate setting in which we follow the bastardized interpretations of the classical mythology as told from a modern perspective.

                        Rebel Alliance Shard: The Overworld war is done, the Gods failed and humanity is once again subjugated under the rule of the Titans, as they were eons ago. But even though the Gods have fallen, there is still hope; there are still Scions! The lines between pantheons and cultures have been erased, and rather than squabbling among each other, the Scions have banded together under a singular army with the intention of succeeding where the Gods failed. Some say that without the aid of the Gods, the rebels are facing an uphill battle, but others believe that unifying the pantheons may be our one hope of victory in this post-apocalyptic scenario!
