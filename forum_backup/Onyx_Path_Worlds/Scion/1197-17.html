Valour has out of combat applications, such as helping an old lady cross the street or help a cat out of a tree. It is the general 'friendly neighbourhood superhero' Virtue, and thus quite compatible with Courage.

                                            There are many religions that hold 'loving thy neighbour' as an ideal. There are no mythologies that do so. I will echo the feelings of Dean Shomshak on another thread. While deciding the Virtue set of the Shen, he realized that, while the culture and religion strongly favoured Order, none of the great heroes of Chinese Myth ever give a shit about laws. So Order is not a fit Virtue even if the religion came up with Legalism. Similarly, Compassion is an ideal for many religions. For their Gods? Not so much. Even the Pantheons that are heavily invested in, and deeply care about, protecting their peoples, like the Aztec and Hindu, this is best represented by other Virtues. In both these cases, the Gods have a Duty to protect their worshippers (and yes, I know that the Deva in Companion don't have Duty. That is but one part of that chapter that needs serious revamping).

                                            There is room for compassion (small c) in Scion. Very much so. But to create a new Virtue that has only one Pantheon as an adherent? That smacks of Special Snowflake Syndrome. Of course, if we're still discussing the Abrahamic Pantheon as an optional 'shard' like supplement, then go for it. Introducing one-off Virtues is just one of the things that optional supplements are there for.
