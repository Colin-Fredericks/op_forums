    Building a Pantheon AND chosing a leader IN-GAME

    01-16-2015, 07:13 PM
    Hi all, I've been lurking on these forums for a while now but never found answer to one question:

    As a V:TR, WoD, Exalted, Scion player and ST I know that sometimes there's no direct answers in-book and you must come up with a fair answers of your own but one one them as seemed to me too important for not asking on these forums first.

    I am currently running a long date game of players who actually became Gods quite a long time ago. They've builded their sanctums in their own overworld, their cults and managed to become quite important gods over time (Legend 11-12). They're now thinking of creating their own Pantheon, quitting respectively their previous ones.

    The question is now: how can they do that? How can they build their own pantheon purview and share it between them and their offsprings? How can they, lore speaking, acquire that type of knowledge?

    Thank you and sorry for my bad english

                          I'd do the Virtues the way PhGraves suggested... just total all the dots and the top 4 are the pantheon Virtues. You might want to allow the players to shift over to those Virtues where they don't match up on a gradual basis somehow, if they so wish. It's not like every member of a pantheon HAS to share all of the pantheon Virtues, but the pantheon will be more cohesive if the majority of the characters have all 4 and the others have at least 2.

                          So you'll need a mechanism for that shift.

                          As for the PSP. You haven't given us any hard information about what these guys all share as a focus. You HAVE to come up with that. It doesn't have to be in place, but it's got to be something that they would all aspire to and would characterize them in the same way that Arete characterizes the Greek focus on excellence in one's chose purview, that Iztli (in spirit at least) exemplifies the importance of blood sacrifice to the Aztecs. Probably not in the same way that Jotunblut ties the Norse together as that is ancestral in character and the characters don't share that (unless, weirdly, they find out that they do....?) (And Jotunblut sucks anyways).

                          As for the mechanics, I would have them, maybe, after deciding where they want to head with it, come up with the first dots (levels 1-3). I would make them purchase the first dot at the same time and have it be something epic that they all do - Maybe have some kind of blood/ichor brotherhood pact thing? sacrifice one of their number and then divide him/her up somehow? (And hoepfully have some members who can enact some Avatars for Heal and Death to resurrect the sacrificee...) Maybe they all jump into a bonefire together? There's lots of this stuff in mythology. Since they are all jumping ship from other pantheons, maybe something full of conflict where they are standing off or stealing stuff from their parents/parental pantheon (recasting their parents as Titans in the generational conflict). Or even some "plot coupon" collection for the above... (winthe wood from Ygdrasil in the three part contest with the eagle/squirrel, the dragon and the Norns, ignite it with Zeus's stolen thunderbolt, have the Azlanti cut out each one's heart, cook it a little in the fire before returning it, etc. etc.)

                          Mechanically, maybe each dot they buy in their new PSP takes out the highest dot in their old one? So they become less and less what they were and more and more what they will be?

                          That's my thought salad for that....

                          EDIT: Maybe give the chance to shift a Virtue over at the Hero/Demigod and Demigod/God breakpoints as they are buying the new PSP dots? Probably from whatever value it had to 1 dot in the new one.

                                                  Been thinking about it. I am kinda gonna go back on what I have been saying. The PSP should be something intrinsic to the identity of the pantheon as an intrinsic group. Not so much what they do but about who they are. And a bunch of buddies doesn't really have that kinda connection. Maybe being a pantheon really does have something to do with being a true family. Maybe they can't start their PSP until they start popping out little Scions of their own?

                                                  OR.... maybe a PSP has as much or more to do with the Fatebinding wrecked on the group by a mortal following? Do these guys have worshipers? Maybe they HAVE to have them to really become a pantheon.

                                                  A pantheon may be more than a collection of like-minded individuals, thought that might be essential at the start... the Greek sextet of sibs defined by their opposition to their dad and his brothers and sisters... the various Maori brothers (excluding the oldest) trying to get some breathing space between mom and dad.... Odin and his brothers against Ymir, then, apparently, wife-hunting amongst the Vanir.... A lot of those conflicts seem to follow the intergenerational model implicit in the setting. It may also have to include the group becoming an actual family (if it didn't start that way). And it seems like it might actually need the mortals to weigh in on establishing the identity.
