    First time homecrafting an adversary and would like help.

    05-09-2022, 06:58 PM
    Hello! For the campaign I am running, I wanted to make an adversary who could be sort of a spylike reoccuring mysterious figure through the campaign at least at origin level. Nobody is quite sure what her motivations are, and they do not seem consistent so one adventure she could be ambushing the party, the next she's seducing one of them to steal a mcguffin, the next she is fighting alongside them. I wanted her to have a sort of spyish edge so I figured I would flavor her as an Heir, since the dragon book outright mentions spy stuff as how a typical campaign would run. As such I figured I would make her a Draq heir of Leviathan. I just finished the stats and want to know if she seems too weak or too powerful. In addition I would like suggestions thinking of a backstory and motivations, so far I just know she is the daughter of an Apache woman and an Arab man (Which is where her Draq blood comes from).           I gave you the benefit of the doubt, scrolling through some really... exaggerated and unnecessary images (After page 2 I thought you were just trying a really weird troll to make people look at the art) to find the statblock. So:

          The Dragon Kickstarter preview offers "Rogue Agent" as a Dragon Archetype, matching Villain. It also offers a few Qualities that work for that tier:
          One Step Ahead: +2 Speed Scale when escaping/being chased (That means +4 enhancements on the roll)
          Shattering Transformation: Spend 1 tension to gain +2e to the Primary pool, +1 Size and Force Scale, Hard Armor 3
          Flair:
          Step Sideways: Spend 1 tension after the Antagonist suffers damage. The Antagonist disappears from the scene. Tracking requires a Clash of Wills. The Antagonist may return later in the same scene.

          But if you stick with Origin stuff, here's what I see:
          1. The character isn't described as a combat wombat, but everything you've chosen is combat related. Look through the Social Flairs and decide if any of those work for the character, or at least keep them in mind.
          2. At very least, you should add Red Herring as a Flair, in case the players come looking.
          3. The primary/secondary pools are defined with Skills, in a way that limits her. Instead of Skills specifically, stat her out with "Attack from the Shadows" and "Clashes of Will" Primary and "Mysterious Ally" Secondary, or something like that.
          4. The Drive is really specific to the Band. Maybe instead, she is working on her own agenda each time. Think of her Drive as her current Draconic Deed. Maybe the Drive the first time is "delay the Band so they miss their meeting with the Scion of Apollo". Next time it's "Take the idol before the band does" and the time after that it's "Give the counterfeit statue to the band."

          Remember that Dragon Heirs are often given tasks that they don't understand, because their Handler's agenda is hidden. Changing the Drive each time they meet illustrates that, and gives you a specific thing for her to do.

          Don't plan to move her from Mysterious Ally to Adversary quickly. Let them get used to seeing her (or evidence of her) around, like she's Moriarty or Lupin. Give her Thousand Faces so they never know who she is, but they keep seeing evidence of her passing (I'm picturing something like wingprints in dust, or talonmarks in a doorway). As long as she stays on the same Tier as the players, she has a good chance of defending against tracking Knacks and other magics, until her Handler's plans come to a peak, and they find themselves in direct opposition to her. But she's also the one who rescued the daughter of Obatala in Tartarus. She was the Canary who died to help us take down the Troll and moved the Scion of Freyr closer to his Wyrd.

              Could this adversary be a Scion of Nemesis? I realize you were thinking dragon, but that could be a miss direction. Where Nemesis is about justice for those who have avoided earthly justice, this Scion of Nemesis could be about testing those seeking power. Nemesis brought punishment, her sister gave rewards for no reason. This Scion could be about heading off trouble by getting rid of the unworthy before they can create destruction and misery.
