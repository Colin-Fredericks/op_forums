                            I'm going to start by throwing out some words of caution/advise to folks as someone active in the DM's Guild:

                            The DM's Guild was the first Community Content program I saw with DTRPG but several others quickly popped up in the following weeks. None of them were for systems I had any investment in so I didn't look them over but I wouldn't be surprised if each one has some very specific and distinct differences regarding what can and can't be published. A good example of this would be custom pantheons. I saw someone mention that they wanted to convert Gaia, Luna, and Helios from Werewolf. I like the idea but I will be surprised if it's allowable as something that can be sold. As I understand it, OPP doesn't own any of the World of Darkness (which was why everything had to go through CCP and then got bogged down when Paradox picked up the rights). If Paradox finds any World of Darkness conversions and Rich hasn't gotten the Community permission to use it, DTRPG will pull it and Paradox could have grounds for legal action.

                            Rich said that this is the first time he's done something like this and will need some time to iron out the details.

                            Touching on the point about DTRPG pulling titles, I've personally had a few titles pulled and received at least one polite but strongly worded e-mail about what I did and how it better not happen again. I've seen other titles get pulled as well, though I don't know what, if anything, was said to the authors (at launch, the DMG was exclusively Forgotten Realms and people were posting non-Realms material).

                            The DMG operates with a nice 50% royalty for the authors and then DTRPG and Wizards split the remaining 50%. Wizards has a much larger player base (at least, I assume it does given the weekly Adventurer's League games at a lot of game stores) and also the backing of Hasbro. Rich doesn't have that kind of financial safety net so I'm expecting much smaller royalties (though I'd love for him to prove me wrong here).

                            I'm certainly not trying to discourage anyone. Writing and idea cultivation is one of the best parts of TRPGs. I'm hoping folks avoid the errors I've made (or seen others make.)

                            "The Man and the Beast are having an amiable picnic in that Savage’s head. It’s rather disturbing." - Blood and Smoke: the Strix Chronicle, pg. 22
