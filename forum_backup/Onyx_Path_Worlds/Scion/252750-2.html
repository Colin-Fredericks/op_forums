        ...And how exactly is that constructive? Calling a moderator in every time someone posts something with as much cultural sensitivity as a 40's comic book? It's better to toss a warning at them with no explanation of why that topic is sensitive, of why their idea is offensive?

        No one is being a Forum Vigilante, valiantly attacking the mean, mean racists. I haven't even seen anyone (recently) being intentionally racist. We're talking about deep-seated cultural biases that are remarkably hard to deal with. About things like the perception that all Native Americans are the same, but tiny and virtually undocumented groups of Europeans deserve special attention. About the perception of Mayans and Aztecs as virtually identical bloodthirsty savage tribes that sacrificed people willy-nilly like some B-movie horror villain.

        No one deserves to be suspended or reported for that. But they also don't need to continue spreading such ideas. That's when people have stepped in and said "Hey, woah, woah. You're going about this all wrong and it's pretty offensive. Here's why, and how you could do it without the racism!"

        It happens a lot on the Scion forum, because Scion deals with a lot of marginalized, poorly represented groups and periods of history. And no, it is bloody well not reasonable to expect everyone to offer their insight with a smile and polite diction. As long as no personal attacks are involved and no one is insulted, belittled or denigrated, how is that different from any interaction.. uh, ever?

        Scenario 1) "I wanna play with superheroes!" No one is harmed by this, at all. Ever. There is no need to respond if you disagree with the premise.
        Scenario 2) "I wanna play with cereal mascots!" No one is harmed by this, at all. Ever. There is no need to respond if you disagree with the premise.
        Scenario 3) "I wanna play with HP Lovecraft Gods!" No one is harmed by this, at all. Ever. There is no need to respond if you disagree with the premise.
        Scenario 4) "I wanna play with Coyote and Brer Rabbit!" This brings up a bunch of loaded, harmful stereotypes. It needs to be addressed, because not addressing it allows ignorance and casual, passive forms of racism to proliferate. There is a need to respond, especially if you disagree.

        Is the correct response to say "You're an idiot, those are racist!" with no explanation and personal attacks? No. Is the correct response to offer information and explanation as to why those things are racist and insensitive? Yes. That's being a good person who wants to see improvement in the world and in cultural perceptions.


        It is a very poor choice to change the purpose of a discussion board from "Hey, what does everyone think of this idea?" to "Hey, what does everyone who already agrees with me think of this idea?"

        Personal attacks and bullying have always been things that the moderators and administrators have been on the lookout for. Now they're also going to have the Hurt Feelings Patrol? Getting your feelings hurt because people disagree with you, even dismissively or angrily, is not at all the same as being the target of bullying or personal abuse.

        I'm a professor! Why is no one listening to me?!
