        Originally posted by Aquatica1000 View Post
        I still don't fully understand Asset skills. Are they the only skills the scions can have, or just skills that the scion has an advantage on? Neither books are very clear about them, and the guide section mentions that Guides don't grant the +1 given to stunts for normal Asset skills, a rule that I also can't find, so I'm very confused atm.
        Each Pantheon has two Asset Skills. Each Path that you choose has three Skills associated with it... for your Pantheon Path, two of those three Skills must me your Pantheon's Asset Skills... this means that you will always start with at least a single dot in each Skill, even if the Path is tertiary. They don't have any advantage inherently, at least none that all the other Skills bound to your Paths don't have. Also, Guide Asset Skills section doesn't say anything about getting +1 to Stunts, merely the fact that you will automatically start with 1 dot in each... the Guide's Asset Skills are the skills through whose rolls you can access the Guide's Stunt.
