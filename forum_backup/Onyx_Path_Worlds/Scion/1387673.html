    University degrees in The World

    05-12-2020, 05:50 AM
    Just for fun I am brainstorming what different types of scientific disciplines there would be in The World that do not exist in real life and presenting them as a study counselor at university would.

    Theology
    This is required study for any priest of any kind that wishes to have an official clerical license. As opposed to Religious Studies which is a theoretical academic field, Theology is the study of practical applications of divine sorcery, encompassing diverse subjects such as conversation therapy to ascertain the health of the person's soul, immaculate knowledge of best practices for rituals, ceremonies and sacrifices as well as temple dogma and religious law. Theologists almost always become priests after graduation and some go on into academica. Almost no one experiences difficulty finding jobs anywhere they might go. A minor in Theology is a good investment for many other types of studies, such as lawyers, performers, technologists and psychologists.

    Divination
    Divination has always been a solid choice for the mathematically minded students. This field of study deals first and foremost with the divination of a person's traits and personality (often called Astrology) as well as estimating how the future will turn out (sometimes called Zodiology or Horoscopology). It is, however, not an easy study as it not only requires great mathematical skills but also occult understanding and the ability to make intuitive leaps to know what is or is not a valid estimation of the future. AI is projected to be able to crunch the heavy numbers involved but Diviners themselves are still in needed and in very high demand all across the labor market. Middling Diviners can still count on comfortable jobs in the corporate world while high-end Diviners look forward to high positions in governments and big corporations.

    Thanatology
    More known for the old term Necromancy (which is considered as a demeaning and outdated term), Thanatology is the study of ghosts, the underworld, ectoplasm and the afterlife in general. It is a niche field of study which sometimes experiences highs and lows in popularity. Thanatologists are by necessity investigative types who research family histories and consult with the spirits of the deceased to find the most efficient way to appease, release or, in worst case, exorcise the spirit. Thanatology majors can expect a labor market currently in balance with plenty of job opportunities.

    Geometry
    The old term Geomancy has been commonly phased out in many universities but the same principles apply: using mathematical models to calculate the flow of energy in a room or across for maximum effect. Beginners courses in Geometry are required for architecture students as well as designers. It is also an appreciated minor for many professions such as farmers, business majors, therapists or even IT-specialists. Geometricians are highly valued almost anywhere and can look forward to good career opportunities. The prerequisites are high with a solid grasp of mathematical and scientific principles necessary.

    Thaumatology
    This is still a very popular field of study in many universities but job opportunities are unfortunately slim as sorcery is not as popular as it was a century or even 50 years ago. Many sorcery disciplines are based on ethically problematic practices such as blood sacrifice, manipulation of a person's destiny or even outright siphoning of divine energies. What's worse is that even if effective, many studies have shown that even accomplished sorcerers are prone to have fatal backfires and unforeseen consequences which has caused many countries and insurance companies to heavily regulate the use of sorcery. Many actors in the corporate world prefer the reproducibility, usability and generally much lower risk of technology for most tasks where sorcerers used to work. An undergraduate is therefore advised to consider technological disciplines instead.

                                              Some more fun university degrees, this time Master Degrees:

                                              Eschatology Master's Degree
                                              The name has inspired many jokes among first year students, but students of this discipline take it highly seriously. Eschatologists study all the catastrophes, omens, apocalypses and so on that are present in all religions and try to verify if such events are coming to pass or no. That hail storm in the middle of summer is usually just a freak meteorological phenomenon, but it might just be a sign of some coming apocalypse or catastrophe on the way if all the conditions are true. Forewarned is forearmed and some governments value the counsel of Eschatologists but just as many do not. As such, a Masters degree in Eschatology is not a surefire road to safe employment. Eschatology students almost always have a background in Religious Studies but History and Theology are also common.

                                              Enochian Studies Master's Degree
                                              This is the study of enochian spirit magic as well as angel hierarchies and categories of celestial beings. It claims a long tradition from early Christian Angelology and the principles set forth by John Dee but the modern formalised version is much more recent. Some accuse this of being more of a Judeo-Christian sect than a Master's Degree as it places a lot of importance into the power of angelic beings and the potential benefits of supplicating and appeasing them. Many also proclaim that the appearances and utterances of angels signify great events and omens. Some Enochian students also claim to have mastery of Enochian, the supposed language of Angels.

                                              Animology Master's Degree
                                              Sometimes called Neurotheology, sometimes Animancy. This is the study of the soul and all aspects of it. It is a highly interdisciplinary study encompassing everything from concrete neurology to philosophy. The goal of most animologists is to find scientific and accurate ways to gauge the health of a person's soul in order to prescribe the most effective spiritual remedies. Many mock these attempts and ambitions as it is commonly taken for granted that priests of various religions already have this knowledge and the answers. The ambitions of animologists however would be to seek the underpinnings of the soul and how sin works on it as well as to more effectively find spiritual remedies and separate the fact from fiction across religious dogmas.
