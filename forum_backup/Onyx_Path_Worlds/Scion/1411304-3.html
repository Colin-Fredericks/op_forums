                                                        I did say I would write this. Fair note, I'm only using one source and the summary version on there.:

                                                        Sutoku-tenno, the Demon Emperor

                                                        aka: Yamato Akihito

                                                        While there are three Great Evil Yokai of Japan, each one a powerful human who somehow became a titanspawn yokai, and from there a true Titan, Sutoku is by far the most amenable to creating Scions, for the same reason he bares the unique honor among his peers of being enshrined as a Kami in his beloved Kyoto. Frankly, after everything he went through, the fact that he was still coherent enough to focus his rage on the imperial politics he was born at the apex of seemingly purely so that all ostensibly beneath him could more efficiently punch him in a very sensitive spot is commendable. Before he became the most powerful tengu ever recorded, he was indeed Emperor of Japan, and had a beloved and doting father that wanted nothing more than for his son to be a good ruler - which was a problem, because his biological father was, for all Sutoku can tell (and in truth, wants to believe) the retired Emperor Shirakawa, rather than the reigning Emperor Toba, a fact with his ostensible legal father never forgave him for. More than that, the unfortunate Prince Akihito was sworn in at the tender age of five, following the tradition of the court, and allowing Toba enough power and influence to unleash profound abuse on the child-Emperor until he was forced to retire early - and hand off the reins to Toba's actual son, not coincidentally - just to get away from it all, which Toba then happily exploited to turn Sutoku into a powerless pariah in a gilded cage, unable to even advise his younger half-brother or enjoy life away from the stifling nature of the pre-shogunate imperial court.

                                                        Of course, what Toba didn't expect was that Sutoku was always the healthier and more romantically inclined of the brothers, so when Emperor Konoe died, Sutoku had his own backup heir coincidentally ready to inherit and maybe actually perform the duties of a retired emperor now that he had some experience and distance from his oppressor. Unfortunately for him, Toba had not been idle, stocking the court with his supporters and sycophants, who decided that infancy was not nearly enough of a disqualifying trait to pass the throne to Toba's fourth son, something Sutoku and the many allies he had made in his initial exile did not buy to the tune of a full-scale rebellion soon after Toba's death.

                                                        What happened next, however, is what truly set Sutoku on his path to Titanic vengeance; the forces of the court, fearing that Sutoku's charisma would lead to further rebellions or worse, a valid claim, proceeded to execute not only the rebels, but their entire families for their disobedience in order to intimidate the remainder into mute obedience. While even they could not convince the executioners to harm a descendant of Amaterasu, they certainly could inform him of their fates, before all but forcing him to become a monk in a far-off monastery. When he came offering lovingly copied and decorated manuscripts to make Kyoto beautiful with and as an offering of peace, the court decreed that, since they were obviously cursed due to the person who made them, they should be destroyed, and sent back to him as ashes.

                                                        Needless to say, Sutoku decided at this point that cursing the court was actually a decent suggestion. Especially the part about biting off his own tongue and making a prayer to Izanami in blood to grant him the capacity to pursue his vengeance against the system that had heaped misery and indignity upon him his whole life. She was listening, and seeing a bit of herself in the tragic emperor, granted his wish.

                                                        While it was decreed by the new emperor that Sutoku would be unmourned for having been such a criminal, any attempts to dishonor his corpse proved fruitless when decay simply did not affect him; except for his de-tongued state, the cursed monk appeared to be peacefully sleeping a month later when brought to the crematorium. There, a storm that drew blood from stone heralded his arrival, and when he finally was cremated, ash the color of thunderclouds drifted in defiance of all weather to Kyoto. In less than twenty years, the entire line of Toba was rendered powerless by natural disasters and sudden rebellions inspired by a strange, regal tengu with hair like an onryo's - followed soon after by the very court itself, beginning the age of the shogun and the emperor becoming a figurehead - which much less drama attached to his life, giving him a chance for a vaguely content one.

                                                        Sutoku now is a personification of the natural disasters that force traditions to adapt or die, and occupies an ambivalent spot among the Kami. On the one hand, he did more or less singlehandedly reduce the power of the emperor to a figurehead for much of Japan's existence, and only through honoring him as a Kami did the Meiji Restoration ensure that the forces of the emperor didn't singlehandedly die by sudden typhoons. At the same time, he also is a crusader against the corruption and greed that destroyed his mortal life, and he was one of the first to realize that Japan's imperial ambitions in the leadup to WWII would doom it if not curtailed. While he despises Amaterasu for what he sees as the dereliction of her duty to protect her descendants, he has little anger towards the rest of the Kami; he just finds their Titans agree with his tempestuous demeanor more, especially Fujin, who understands the nihilistic rage and sorrow he felt in the last days of his mortal life. He has no particular desire to rule again, either; he always preferred life in exile and his religious life more. Rather, he is a cheerfully radical political philosopher who falls in love with any movement that promises to liberate both the weak and the powerful from the burden of institutional rulership; he will support communist revolutionaries and libertarian uprisings in the same breath without noticing a difference, or caring. For the most part though, he seeks the faults in power structures to unleash disasters upon, eager to see if the state will thrive in the face of adversity or be torn down to build a better one. He is especially proud of the destruction of the Chernobyl reactor, and how it led to the end of the Soviet Union and the possible permanent destruction of all states that could be reformed forever.

                                                        When he walks in the World, Sutoku is fully aware of how he often appears as the epitome of the troubled but handsome Byronic hero; there are more than a couple dating sims he has licensed out his favored faces and writing advice to, though his fury is spectacular should he realize his face belongs to a romantic abuser - it reminds him far too much of his past. It should come as no surprise that he has a significant portion of Born Scions, but he actually prefers Chosen for the simple reason that he has vowed to himself to never be Toba; he does not trust himself to be able to dote on too many families at once, so he only has a couple children to focus on until their adulthood at once. Naturally, the people he Chooses tend to be countercultural leaders; anarchist philosophers, artistic teachers, and master survivalists. Even more naturally, all of them are angry enough at the status quo to be willing to blow it up, secure in the belief that whatever comes next is an improvement by default.

                                                        Virtues: Sincerity, Fecundity

                                                        Callings: Adversary, Destroyer, Leader

                                                        Purviews: Artistry (Writing), Beasts (Corvids), Chaos, Death, Fortune, Passion (Cold Rage), Order, Sky

                                                        EDIT: Not sure if he should be a Monster instead of an Adversary, though. He's way too direct and is very much about letting you know he's there. Leader is staying, though; his major positive quality is that he is honestly a fair and even-keeled politician when he's calm. It's what makes him a patron of rebels.

                                                        Still on his associations, but the major thing is that he seeks to unseat Amaterasu in the possibly accurate belief she could have saved him from his stepfather but didn't, and is happy to work with other Titans and Gods to embarrass her. He's not nearly so eager to deal with Titans who view their Scions as tools (which is why he hasn't allied with Apep into the Sun Hater Club), and his Scions are fewer compared to other Titans, but doted on; he doesn't Visit you unless he likes you, and so he encourages other potential Titans; the more storms to change the world, the better.
