    Divine Lineage

    12-05-2013, 10:03 AM
    So, the Scion rules say that the children of a deity are Scions of that deity. The children of Scions of a deity are also Scions of that deity. (p. 60 & 79 of scion god book)

    "Until he becomes a God, his own divine parent’s predispositions are passed on to any children, and they may become Scions of their divine grandparent, if they receive an appropriate Visitation"

    Two questions:
    1. The wording is only for a single generation - Scions of the grandparent. Does the power of Ichor extend throughout multiple generations? if parent and grandparen were Scions, but never ascended, can the child become a Scion of the divine great grandparent?
    2. What happens when 2 scions with different divine parents have a kid?

      1) Usually in mythology, heroes are chosen from either children or grandchildren of gods. I'd think it would be pretty rare for power to translate any further unless the direct child of the god is uplifted.
      Think in terms of God > demigod > Hero. That's three generations. The game starts as God-to-Hero transfer.
      2) Roll Legend for whose divine parentage is stronger in the grandchild? Have the grandparents compete in the Overworld for 'dips'? Or have both grandparents go 'dah that's so adorable' and be co-pilots? Your call. But try to find commonalities with that last one. Maybe limit it to five Power Thingies.
