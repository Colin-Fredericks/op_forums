        Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post

        Aesir: Bragi, Idunn, Ullr, and Vidar
        Yeah, I can see this, but I can't help wondering if Hodr should get in before Bragi

        Deva: Ganga, Chandra, and Brahma (yes I know he's currently a Primordial, but he was more active in the World as a God in the past, so i think it would have value to write-him-up as reflected in those traditions for those who want to use him that way)
        Well, good news is that Brahma has Callings and Purviews in the Origin appendix, so I'm assuming he'll be in the core book. The other two seem the best inclusions

        Kami: Oyamatsumi and Konohanasakuya-hime
        This has been covered extensively, and I don't have much to add except that I'm still not convinced that Ryujin is Ao Guang.

        Manitou/Orenda: Kitchi-Odjig, Hawenneyu, Doyadastethe, Akonwara, Doendzowes, Aireskoi, Hinon
        I... I really don't like that Anishinaabaek and Haudenosaunee myth has been conflated like this. I really don't. But since that's what we have here, this list is about as good as could be expected, save that I perhaps would have Onatah in place of her father, as I haven't seen much in the way of actual myths for the latter (though even then, I'd see them both over Tawiscara, who I really think should be a Titan)

        Netjer: Neber-Het, Bes, Neith, Serquet, and Renenutet
        Neber-het, Bes, and probably Neith and Serqet seem fine, but I've always had a soft spot for Anhur. I could even see Kebauet if they want to really cover what was possible in 1e, or Bastet (who is explicitly not Bast here, and was rather important in her own right).

        Orisha: Oba and Aganju
        I really think Erinle is an obvious choice for the Orisha, very popular and with an interesting water/earth duality. I've seen some takes where there's a gender difference there, but I'm not able to find anything reliable right now on that front. Either way, I think they'd be a more interesting choice than Oba.

        Shen: Yao, Shun, Yu, and Xiwangmu (as with Brahma)
        Hmmm, one short here. Also, more good news, Xiwangmu also has stuff already set out in Origins. Otherwise, I can't really agree with this. I understand wanting more of the Five Emperors present, but I think there are too many more varied, interesting, and/or popular deities in the pantheon that could be included, like Caishen, Wenchang Wang, Mazu, and Houyi (which the full Hero text confirms is totally someone who you could be a Scion of).

        Teotl: Tlazolteotl, Camaxtli, Mayahuel, and Xolotl
        I really think Xiuhtecuhtli is someone who should be in here. Tlazolteotl and Mixcoatl are too important to leave out, I think, and while I think Xolotl is more prominent than Mayahuel, that puts a really big gender gap there. Hmmm.

        Theoi: Hecate and Pan
        I just don't buy Pan. He's prominent, yes, but he dies, and that's just not something the Theoi can do. I don't think he topped Legend 8. My vote would be for Tyche, whose cult was surprisingly widespread all things considered.

        (and I'm still working on the Tuatha - all I currently have for them is Cailleach, which still leaves me with three more to choose)
        Yeah, Tuatha are hard. I love Watcher's Midir, and Fand is probably workable, but even after that the best I can think of would be the likes of Bobd Derg, Cian, or Niamh, and those aren't particularly strong choices all things considered.

                    I honestly don't see much of a consistent "Othering" theme with the Titans. Yes, Tawiscara is selfish and spiteful like humans, in the same sense that Cronus is paranoid and jealous like humans, or that Bres is a hot asshole who just wants to keep his job despite sucking at it.
