                                                            Originally posted by finch181 View Post
                                                            how unfortunate. But aren't titans evil? I don't remember because I haven't read anything about scion in ages.
                                                            Scion 1e does make it look like all Titans are evil (the fact that every last one of them has all four Dark Virtues does NOT help), but I sincerely hope that 2e takes a step away from this approach. What Titans ultimately are, is powerful. They are embodiments of aspects of the fundamental building blocks of the World. All the Water Titans in the book, for eg. are evil, but the Greater Titan of Water is supposed to be the underlying principle of the element of water in the World, so it stands to reason that somewhere in there is a Titan Avatar that represents water's helpful qualities...Mizuhame, the direct counterpart to her brother Kagutsuchi, could be one.
                                                            Similarly, you could play Ahura Mazda as an Avatar of an Order Titan, representing rightful Law, and give him just Zealotry (to represent his somewhat less than flexible view towards morality). Given his general nature, he might even have volunteered to be bound to Tartarus so that the rest of the more dangerous Order Titans can be contained (and honestly, 1e does paint Order as the one Titan on the side of the Gods, but that is equally as uninspired an approach as 'all other Titans are evil'. There are plenty of less savoury sides to Order as well).
