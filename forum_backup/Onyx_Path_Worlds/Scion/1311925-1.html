                                                        The challenge with this is individual Pantheons don't work great with that paradigm. It works pretty solidly with the Theoi, but for instance it breaks down with the Túatha Dé Danann.

                                                        Just going from Hero, we can look at their lineup and see where this more Theoi-centric take on Primordial-Titan-God breaks down.

                                                        Danu isn't anything, she is just an ancestral figure to the Pantheon. She isn't what she represents because she doesn't represent anything, she is just an old, probably dead or otherwise permanently 'off screen' figure whose entire claim to fame is that she is the shared ancestor of the multiple tribes that make up the Pantheon.

                                                        Titans are harder to do for the Túatha Dé as I disagree with all of their assigned Purviews, but for instance, Balor. Balor is a Chaos Titan, but, is he motivated by Chaos? No, he is a king in his own right, he is a source of stability and power and wealth for his people, he is just one of multiple Fomori kings who attempts to dominate Ireland. His Purview is the result of his actions, rather than the motivation for his actions. (As a note, I would make Balor probably a War or Epic Strength Titan. In these cases, he still isn't being governed by the Purviews, they're just representations of what he does. Conquest or Might, as the 'evil' eye can also be translated as Piercing Eye)

                                                        For Gods, the Túatha Dé aren't responsible for anything, they don't represent anything, they're just people who stand around and have supernatural heights of talent and learning in specific professions. Dían Cécht doesn't represent physicians and isn't responsible for them short of his name showing up in one medical prayer (but so does Goibniu so it doesn't seem to be based on Dían Cécht being a physician) in the St. Gall gospels.

                                                        Ultimately, what is a Primordial, Titan, and God is loosely defined and varies Pantheon to Pantheon, as if everyone had to use the Theoi model, not a single being in the Túatha Dé Danann wouldn't qualify for any of the Primordial-Titan-God ranks.
