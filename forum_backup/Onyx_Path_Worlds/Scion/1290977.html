    Combat, mostly Defense.

    02-22-2019, 11:06 PM
    So just last session me and my group had some combat and we're slightly confused as to how defense works. Do you roll only your resistance attribute? This felt like it resulted in very weak defense pools even for quite competent characters. Yet setting the highest of your resistance attributes as a flat value felt similarly too high, and we weren't quite sure, based on reading the book, which was correct.

    So how should this be handled or done?

      You roll resistance attribute and the successes are added to you defence. But it’s not all that you use, you can use a shield, that add to your defence, you can have armor, that force attackers to spend successes to hurt you and may have some knacks or booms that add to your defence or armor.

                                          Originally posted by Graylion View Post
                                          Can anyone give me an example of the defensive stunts being used?

                                          Thank you
                                          Another Stunt:

                                          Frankie "Bullets" Malone, Chicago Hitman, opens fire with his tommy gun and gets three successes on the attack. Andromeda, Scion of Loki, decides to get her head down. When she rolls Composure, she gets two successes. She spends one of them to use the "Dive For Cover" Stunt to scramble behind the bar's counter, which the SG rules makes for Light Cover.

                                          Light cover, as per the Origin book, can absorb four damage before breaking. Assuming Andromeda's player pumps up their defense to 2 from the remaining success, the enemy has 1 threshold success. The Inflict Injury stunt that Malone gets for 0s is applied to the cover, so it can take three more Injuries before breaking. The SG also applies the Pin Stunt with the last Success, so Andromeda's player will need to buy off a level 2 complication when returning fire on the next round, or else will take an automatic injury.
