                                                            They're not varients on Storyteller; the shared system for Scion and Trinity (and who knows? Maybe more games in the far-off future) are our own system rather than one we've licensed from CCP. It uses d10 dice pools and has dots on the character sheet; you can tell it's us, it's just a bigger departure than, say, Exalted is from cWoD. A bit more different than Storytelling (nWoD's system) is from Storyteller.

                                                            And yes, Trinity and Scion share it, to the extent that the nWoD games all share Storytelling but build different special mechanics into the core stats, dice rolls, and mechanics of the game.
