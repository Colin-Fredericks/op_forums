                                Recommended for established Scions (Legend 4+):

                                150) It's as true for the divine world as for the mundane one: the reward for a job well done is more work, usually harder and less pleasant. So, when your pantheon's head asks you (as an up-and-comer) to show his newest Visited Scion the ropes, you already know that "honor" is a dubious one.

                                It gets worse.

                                You see, you already know this new Scion. It's your cousin, a spoiled brat and troublemaker whom you've always had to bail out (and who's never had an ounce of gratitude for it). And finding out they're half-God hasn't done a damned thing for developing their humility or willingness to work with people.

                                So. If something happens to this jerk, you're gonna get it from both your mundane and divine families. Your requirement to watch after them is likely to try your Bandmates' patience. And your cousin wouldn't follow directions even if Zeus zapped them directly into their brain.

                                Better ask your Band's resident brain to gin up some celestial-strength ibuprofen for you. You're gonna need it.

                                                154) Two schools of thought have emerged among the Scions of the World. One side claims that it is their duty to protect and serve, while the other claims it is their right to conquer and rule. Fate, it seems, has just pulled an X-Men, and a vast number of Scions are being drawn to one faction or the other as they decide how they wish to use their divine powers. Neither school has thrown in with the Titans, as they both still aid the side of the Gods, but their ideologies on what it means to be a Scion are mutually exclusive to each other. They can not both get what they want, and so they have turned on each other in an effort to convert or dispose of as many members of the opposite side as possible, before this escalates into full blown war.

                                                And as newly visited Scions, you are wild cards in this debate. They will come to recruit you soon, and at least one of them isn't likely to accept 'no' for an answer.
