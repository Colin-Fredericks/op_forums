        A quick mechanics question on the preview, regarding Defence.

        Defending
        Defending creates the basic difficulty an attack must beat in order to successfully hit its target, requiring the defending character to roll the highest of her Resistance attributes. Apply the successes on this roll to generate Defensive Stunts. Defensive Stunts persist until the end of the round.

        Defensive Stunts
        Dodge: Unlike most Stunts, Dodge generates successes, which an opponent must overcome in order to inflict an Injury. If this roll generates no successes, the base Difficulty to hit a character is 1.
        It looks like what should happen is the following. Someone declares an attack, and I roll Defence. If I roll 0 Successes, my Defence is 1 (I.e. this roll generates no successes, so base Difficulty is 1). If I roll 1 Success, my Defence is still one (Dodge generates 1 success which the opponent must overcome). If I roll 2 successes, my Defence becomes 2, etc.

        This means that, generally, most characters will sit at one Defence, because that's the average for a 3 dice pool.

        My instincts (And the Trinity Core previews) make me think that Dodge should be additional Defence, rather than substituting for a base Defence, but that doesn't seem to be what's actually written here.

                                I think any would be possible, based on the character build.

                                With purely Athletics, observers would be conscious of the skill that the dancer showed.
                                Culture would represent understanding the background of the dance.
                                Empathy would represent a dancer who communicated feeling through dance ("Her Odette (from Swan Lake) was so beautiful, and so sad!")
                                Persuasion would represent a dancer who compelled people to act through dance (I'm picturing interpretive dance here).

                                You could also flip the narrative in multiple ways, depending on Specialties.

                                Athletics (Dance) could allow a Physical + Empathy roll in the Intrigue system, either alone, or to add Enhancements to someone with a high Presence score.
                                Empathy (Dance) could allow a Social + Athletics roll for a Dive for Cover action (Usually Physical + Athletics), where the dive was so graceful it distracted the attacker.
