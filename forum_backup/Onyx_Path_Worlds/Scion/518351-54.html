                                        In Re 1: Unless it's just outright pandering to cultural stereotyping, it would be pretty hard to shoehorn in such a god, assuming you mean the post-Contact mish-mash of many different deities into one "Raven", despite the fact that those mythologies referring to divine ravens come from many different groups and are irreconcilable. There are people out there that have done a fair bit of close-in work on this and I'll defer to them to see if they can dig up something vaguely Raven-like from the relevant materials.
