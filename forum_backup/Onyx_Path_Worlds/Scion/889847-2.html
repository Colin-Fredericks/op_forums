            Originally posted by Anteros View Post

            Even the weaving was more a Roman than Greek thing.
            The authors (Ovid and Virgil) were Roman, but that doesn't make the material Roman in the slightest. Both were working from Hellenistic sources (which are largely gone - the burning of the Library of Alexandria, the Dark Ages, et al.) and weren't creating this stuff from whole cloth (an inadvertent pun). In fact, they WOULDN'T have. This isn't creative writing, it's an effort to take extant (Greek) stories and bring them together in a way that makes the author's point. The fact that the story of Athena and Arachne is takes place in Greek locations, Greek names (it's not Arananea, it's Arachne and even though he says "Minerva" he says "Pallas Minerva", and Pallas is a Greek epithet) and involves Greek themes (like Athena's choice of topic for her tapestry) indicates this is not a Roman story.
