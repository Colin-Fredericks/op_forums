                                                      I honestly would not bet on the “Wrong Upload”. Most of the problems are on the way it is written and contradictions between pages on the same subject.
                                                      There is a few formatting mistakes, much less than the backers preview, but still some. Some game terms that were removed are still present, like Saints being called godly or something like that...

                                                      My opinion didn’t change about the way the 2 books don’t work fine together, Hero have too many text that is in Origin, Heroic Knacks, description of the denizens, some qualities. I do like the idea of one book to rule than all, but Hero doesn’t, neither does Origin, so they could focus on what’s new and not copy paste of so many texts. Copying text could cause contraditiction as one is corrected and the other no (as it happened to Kitsune and some of the permanent conditions of the denizens).

                                                      It’s annoying go thru 3 books as we needed to do to build a god (or even a Hero with some exotic knacks) on 1e, but it’s the best way to keep the books working fine together.

                                                      Now the good part, I liked a lot the new arts, the 2 old Shen and the young one playing risk made me laugh more than I would like to admit, not a big deal, but... The new “standardised” experience cost make the use of Edges and skill tricks possible, as it really annoyed me they are not available, Origin and Heroes need the more human side to be ready...

                                                      The way they described the scales is much much much more clear than before, and Tiers are described apart of it, making it clear that they are not the same thing. I didn’t read thru everything, actually Inwent straight to the points that were not clear before, so many thing that were clear in my mind (it doesn’t mean it was working before, just that I had a proper image of it)...
                                                      In the next few days I intend to go thru Origin properly, and latter Hero... until there I will go easy on the critics...
