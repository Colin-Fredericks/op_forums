    Great Evils Pantheon (Diablo Conversion)

    10-16-2014, 11:40 PM
    The following pantheon is a conversion of Diablo into the Scion universe.



    The Great Evils: most pantheons who clash against the titan spawn are good people. At least as far as ancient pagan religions can be considered good in the modern day. But this particular religion is outright malicious, destructive, and evil. It seeks to corrupt humanity wherever it goes, and destroy those who will not convert. Sometimes they do this covertly, disguising themselves in mortal forms and influencing their subjects from the inside out. Other times they are far more blatant, storming the mortal plane with armies of demons and conquering the land in waves. To even call them a pantheon is done so in the loosest sense of the word, for their history is rife with infighting and betrayal. They are called The Great Evils, and they are the demon lords of Hell!

    Ascension for a Scion is extraordinarily difficult when attempting to join this pantheon. Even their own siblings want them dead so they can increase their own chances to steal the throne. A Scion of the Great Evils must be constantly on guard at all times, so it values those who have the strength to crush their inferiors, and the cunning to outwit their rivals. If you have two or more Scions from this pantheon in your band, it will not be long before they start undermining each other in the quest for ascension!


    The Great Evils were once ruled over by a trio of brothers known as the Prime Evils, but when their subordinates (the Lesser Evils) banded together in a truce, they managed to overthrow them for a time, but eventually the Prime Evils managed to reclaim their former stations. These Prime Evils are Diablo, Mephisto, and Baal. The original Lesser Evils are comprised of Duriel, Andariel, Belial, and Azmodan. Any Scions that rise to the level of godhood become additions to the Lesser Evils, but for the purpose of saving time, we will only mention the history of the original 4 in this back story.


    The origin of this pantheon (if it can be called such) states that at the beginning of time, there was a being called Anu. This Anu wished to purify itself of the darkness within and separated its dark half into another entity. This entity became a seven headed creature by the name of Tathamet, who is believed to have been a dragon. As you can probably guess, Tathamet soon turned upon Anu, and the two of them fought bitterly for untold eons, culminating in the two entities being destroyed in the process.

    Tathamet's body is said to have turned into the Burning Hells, and each of its seaven heads became a demon lord who would become the rulers of hell. The three dominant heads became the Prime Evils, while the remaining heads became the Lesser Evils. It was the Prime Evils who established themselves as the leading power of Hell, for they were the first of the Evils of Hell, and also the most powerful of them all. Originally they ruled the Burning Hells by fueling each others' power, as terror leads to hate, and hate leads to destruction. Unfortunately this alliance was not built to last, and the three brothers had a falling out when Mephisto decided to play his siblings against one another to keep them distracted.


    The corpse of Anu is said to have become the High Heavens and the Angiris Council. Becoming the forces of light, they clashed with the Great Evils on multiple occasions, and although the Prime Evils won many victories against the Angels, they were unsuccessful in breaching the Diamond Gates and claiming Heaven entirely. This war was known as the Great Conflict, but it came to an end with The Sin War.

    The Sin War was a time in which the Worldstone mysteriously disappeared, but it was eventually discovered that it had been used to create Sanctuary, which was supposed to a place where Angels and Demons could live together in peace. Sanctuary would later become recognized as Earth, and the first humans (called the Nephalem) were produced from the unity of Angels and Demons. The Prime Evils sensed that the Nephalem had more power than either Demon or Angel and could make for powerful allies if they could be manipulated, so they founded the Triune, adopting the namesakes of Dialon (Spirit of Determination), Mefis (Spirit of Love), and Bala (Spirit of Creation). The rogue angel Inarius sensed their influence and founded his own religion in Sanctuary, resulting in the Sin War. This war eventually resulted in a truce between Heaven and Hell. The Evils would leave Sanctuary alone, and in exchange they got to keep Inarius as their prisoner. Additionally, the Heavenly Host declared that human memory of their origins and The Sin War was to be erased, so that they would be able to start fresh, and free of the corruptive influences the demons had imposed.


    What came next was known as the Dark Exile. The Prime Evils had kept the existence of humanity a secret from the Lesser Evils, and upon their return to Hell, they discovered their subordinates had grown frustrated with their fixation on humans and supposedly having abandoned the Great Conflict. What followed was a series of events that resulted in the Prime Evils being dethroned by the lesser Evils, and the war was lead by Azmodan and Belial. Although they were dethroned, the Prime Evils destroyed roughly a third of the rebel forces. Separated from their physical bodies, the Lesser Evils cast the Primes into the mortal world, believing that the armies of heaven would be forced to redirect their attention, and give Hell the chance to sieze victory.

    Unbeknownst to anyone, the Dark Exile was a ploy all along, constructed by the Prime Evils themselves. A fallen angel named Izual told them of the existence of the Soulstones, and how they might be corrupted. By corrupting Soulstones, they would in turn be able to corrupt the Worldstone, and through it corrupt all of humanity. The Prime Evils were imprisoned in the Soulstones, but it was to their own long term benefit, for while they were sealed away inside, they would gradually corrupt their own prisons! By embedding these stones inside a human body, they could possess the vessel, and continue to operate within the mortal world, although their human bodies eventually broke down from the terrible power of the demons sealed within them.


    It would be hundreds of years later before the Prime Evils would return to the world, but Diablo was the first to be released, and would proceed to free his brothers, starting with Baal and then moving to Mephisto. Upon their release, they promptly reclaimed their thrones, and now seek to reignite the Great Conflict, starting a second war between Heaven and Hell!


    The Great Evils view humanity as a powerful ally, but only if they can ensure they remain the ones in control. To accomplish this, they spread their demonic taint, corrupting the souls of men and forcing them to submit. Their eternal foe is the Heavenly Host, and they shall not be sated until they have breached the gates of heaven, and taken the realm for their own.



    -------------------------------------------------


    Story Ideas



    Hero: As a newly awakened hell spawn, you carry on your demonic parent's will to corrupt the mortals of the realm. Spreading chaos and misery, your legend begins to grow with each infamous deed you accomplish!


    Demi God: Somehow the Heavenly Host managed to stuff your parents back into a Soulstone, and now they're powerless without a host body or a means of escape. You could try to free them... or you could take advantage of their absence and claw the throne away from your unworthy siblings. Lock and load, you're taking over Hell!


    God: Whether it was a reward for liberating your parents, or if you claimed it through victory, you now sit upon a throne of hell and have joined the ranks of the Great Evils. As a lord of the Burning Hells, your armies look to you to lead them. But while you're laying siege to the gates of heaven, you should be ever vigilant of your fellow lords, lest they seek to claim the prize for themselves.


    Ragnarok: The Worldstone has been shattered, and the Great Evils have been fused within the Black Soulstone and transferred into a new host body. Tathamet has been reborn and has slaughtered its way through Heaven, shattering the Diamond Gates like glass. It is the end times, the final battle between Heaven and Hell. Who will finally claim dominion over the souls of humanity?

      Realms: The Great Evils recognize the existence of 3 realms, these are the Burning Hells, Sanctuary, and the High Heavens. Unlike most pantheons, The Great Evils have combined the Godrealm and the Underworld into a single plane of existence. The gods of hell and the souls of the dead reside in the same place, at least the souls who are claimed by them upon death.




      Burning Hells: The realm of the Great Evils is known as the Burning Hells. Each member of the Great Evils rules over a territory of Hell that is dedicated to their station. As you can expect, it is a realm of fire and brimstone, with seas of molten lava and land masses of black rock. Legions of demons patrol the Burning Hells, pledging their service to whichever Great Evil rules the domain they live in. Unlike most god realms, however, the souls of mortals also reside here along with the pantheon. Most souls who end up here are tortured for eternity by the demons, but those who are devout enough to their demon lords have been shown a modicum of mercy, so long as they continue to impress their lords.



      Sanctuary: Is what the Great Evils and the Heavenly Host call earth. It was designed to be a place where Demons and Angels can live together in peace, but things did not go according to plan. According to the legends, the first humans were spawned from the unity of a Demon breeding with an Angel. But at some point in history, all the other demons and angels who lived in sanctuary were wiped out, and memory of their origins was wiped from the minds of humans.



      High Heavens: The realm of the Heavenly Host is known as the High Heavens, and intruders are barred access by the Diamond Gates that stand at its entrance. The Great Evils have been trying since the dawn of time to conquer the High Heavens, and despite several close calls, they have been unsuccessful. The High Heavens are also the final resting place for a different religion, one that was founded on worshiping the Heavenly Host, rather than the Great Evils. The High Heavens is said to be a paradise, with legions of Angels serving as its armies. Souls who come here when they die live in bliss for eternity, but that won't last if the Demons have their way.


      --------------------------------------------


      Founding Members:


      Diablo: The Lord of Terror. Diablo is the youngest of the three prime evils, but he is the one that is most recognizeable in the world, for he has plagued it more often than his two siblings. He is also believed to be the most cunning, for he has managed to cheat death on numerous occasions! Diablo's goals appear to be spreading terror for the sake of terror, and has no real interest in power and conquest. In fact, he rarely sticks around long enough to enjoy his victories before moving onto the next target after he's thoroughly destroyed a settlement. This, of course, is a deception. Diablo's lust for power will not be satiated until he is the sole ruler of Heaven and Hell, so the instant he is finished with one conquest, he moves onto the next!

      In appearance, Diablo resembles a massive bipedal dragon (wingless) with molten red scales and a yellow underside. A crest of horns adorns his head, along with black spikes growing out of his chin and down the ridge of his back and tail. He is enormous, standing roughly 12 feet tall and with hulking muscles.

      In terms of abilities, Diablo is the most powerful of the demons, strange seeing as how he's the youngest brother of the Primes. He is a master at discovering and exploiting his victim's fears, and as the lord of terror, he is impervious to fear as well, which may just be a weakness if he allows pride to cloud his judgement. Despite being the physically strongest of his brothers, Diablo rarely chooses to engage in direct confrontation, instead choosing to manipulate his foes into his own plans. Indeed, many of his victories have actually come from his strategic brilliance, rather than any show of might. As with any self respecting ruler of hell, he wields the power of flame, but also has the intriguing ability to produce bolts of red lightning when he chooses. He has also been known to summon a cage of bones to entrap his opponents in battle, leaving them with nowhere to run!

      Purviews: Fire, Death, Sky, Prophecy, Magic,

      Epics: Strength, Manipulation, Stamina, Intelligence



      Baal: The Lord of Destruction. Baal is the middle child of the 3 Prime Evils, but is also the most reckless and overt with his actions. Only fitting for someone declaring themselves the lord of destruction, after all. Unlike his brothers, Baal never grew tired of the constant war between Heaven and Hell, as any excuse to inflict destruction and mayhem was good enough to satisfy him. It is said that only once did he attempt to fully conquer Heaven, and they came close to doing so, until Diablo's greed for power made him turn on his siblings and cost them their chance at victory.

      Baal's appearance is... difficult to put into words. From the waist up, he resembles a withered old man with horns growing from his cranium. From the waist down is a four legged body with spindly legs with spear-like tips instead of feet. Tentacles grow from his shoulders and back, and a mantle of black feathers is worn about his upper body.

      Baal's abilities seem to be heavily focused on offensive capabilities, which is no surprise considering his station. He has displayed the ability to create a copy of himself that shares all of his powers, but is not quite as strong as the real Baal. He can teleport short distances, control the power of ice and flame, and siphon the divine magic from those around him, momentarily weakening them. He also has the ability to summon tentacles to ensnare his prey, and has even caused mortals to spontaneously explode by pointing at them!

      Purviews: Frost, Fire, Magic, Psychopomp, Illusion

      Epics: Appearance, Dexterity, Wits, Stamina



      Mephisto: The Lord of Hatred. The eldest of the Prime Evil brothers, Mephisto's influence was so virulent that even when he was sealed away, his demonic taint leaked into the world above. Corrupting a priest who lived nearby and driving him to evil actions. Ironically, Mephisto is the weakest of his three brothers, but at the same time he is also viewed as being the most intelligent. A devious trickster, he knows just how to manipulate Diablo and Baal to his own schemes. He keeps them under control, by providing them with opportunities to pursue their own desires, presumably with them being unaware they are of his own creation. Even when compared with Diablo's intellect, Mephisto is viewed as the tactician among the siblings, and is the only one who can organize them for any great length of time. As the lord of hatred, Mephisto lives up to his name. He has caused entire societies to tear each other apart, orchestrated the rivalry between his younger siblings to prevent them from growing beyond his control, and spreads the seeds of discord and mistrust wherever he goes.

      Mephisto's appearance is a nightmarish figure. He resembles a four armed man with S-shaped horns and who's lower body has been mutilated. Instead of having legs, he sports an elongated spinal column and floats above the ground to retain mobility.

      Mephisto's abilities are strange and perverse. The mere sound of his voice was described by a mortal witness to be like "a thousand needles in his heart" when he recounted his attempt to spy on a meeting between the three primes. He also has the power to control the dead, and although he shows no skill in fire manipulation, he can wield lightning, ice, and even poison!

      Purviews: Frost, Sky, Death, Health

      Epics: Intelligence, Manipulation, Appearance, Wits



      Duriel: The Lord of Pain. He is described as being the "twin brother" to Andariel, despite the fact they look nothing alike! Although he did take part in the Dark Exile, he has once more allied himself with the Prime Evils. Sadly, little is known about Duriel, so he is perhaps the weakest of all the Evils, given that he doesn't have much of a Legend built around him. He is the representation of physical suffering.

      The appearance of this malific creature is nothing short of horrendous. His upper body resembles some kind of large beetle with mantis-like blades instead of hands and a singular horn protruding from his forehead. From the waist down, he has the body of a giant maggot. It's probably best not to try and imagine Duriel attempting to produce Scions!

      Duriel is a brute, who loves to get in close and slash his victims to pieces with his blades. However, he has also been known to manipulate ice and summon insects to do his bidding. Sadly his lack of legendary accomplishments seems to have limited his range of abilities.

      Purviews: Frost, Animal (insects), Health

      Epics: Strength, Stamina, Appearance



      Andariel: The Maiden of Anguish. She is the "twin" sister to Duriel though has little in common with him. She is also known as the Demon Whore, and is the most likely of the Evils to produce Scions. She is the only female member of the original Great Evils. As the antithesis to her brother, she represents mental agony. Ironically for a demon lord that was spawned in the depths of Hell, Andariel has an adversion to fire and boasts no susbtantial resistance to its effects.

      Andariel's physical appearance is both alluring and horrifying. For the most part, her body is a giant, naked female of great beauty. However, she possesses four insect-like appendages growing from her back which end in curved blades, and possesses cloven hoves instead of human feet. Her hair is blood red and sticks up into the air, waving about on its own power. This actually seems kind of amusing, since her hair is about 3 feet long. She appears to be in a constant state of ecstacy, although the source behind the pleasure she experiences is unknown.

      As the incarnation of mental anguish, many of Andariel's powers are based on prolonged suffering and emotional torment. In battle, she has proven to be effective at using poison to debilitate her foes either by spewing it from her mouth or injecting it from the mandibles on her back, but she is also a skilled manipulator. Her most favorite tactic is constructing scenarios which twists the victim's mind inside out, tearing themselves apart with their own emotions.

      Purviews: Illusion, Health, Death

      Epics: Appearance, Manipulation, Stamina



      Belial: The Lord of Lies. He is Azmodan's brother and one of the leaders behind the rebellion against the Prime Evils. He is believed to be the most elusive of the Evils, and is a master manipulator, even being able to coerce the Great Evils to his whims. Belial has come to the belief that reality is perception, and that his purpose is to control all of reality. He does not spread lies for the sake of telling them, but rather he uses deceit as a method to control others. People often believe only what their senses tell them, and if he can manipulate those senses, then he has control over their world.

      In appearance, Belial is a gargantuan figure, taking up the size of a palace court yard. He resembles a bipedal reptile with blade shaped forearms and additional eyes contained inside his shoulders and chest.

      Although Belial's appearance makes him seem like a daunting foe, his powers surprisingly focus on keeping him out of harm's way. He is very fond of summoning minions to fight his battles for him, in the form of insects and snakes, and he can produce clouds of noxious poisons. As the lord of lies, it only stands to reason he has the ability to manipulate people's minds as well. When he is backed into a corner, Belial reveals that he has the terrible power of a volcano, summoning eruptions from beneath the ground, and drawing meteors from the sky!

      Purviews: Illusion, Animal (Insects), Animal (Snakes), Fire, Earth, Health

      Epics: Appearance, Stamina, Intelligence, Manipulation



      Azmodan: The Lord of Sin. He is the brother to Belial and the second leader of the rebellion against the Prime Evils. Despite the fact that Azmodan is heralded as a brilliant demon strategist and has defeated the Heavenly Host multiple times in battle, he has the very odd tendency to fully explain what his plans are and where to locate him to his own enemies. It is a mystery how someone like that could achieve the level of prestige he has garnered, but is probably a testament to his ability to succeed even when faced with a handicap in battle.

      Azmodan has a revolting appearance. His upper body resembles an obese man with razor sharp teeth and a crown of horns adorning his head. He has four eyes, and a ridge of spikes instead of hair. His face also appears to be split open right down the middle with a large gash, possibly a war wound from clashing with the Angels. He also has a pair of wings, but they are likely far too weak to support his massive bulk. From the waist down, is a six legged creature with a secondary head in the front. The two beings appear merged as one, instead of Azmodan riding atop it.

      Azmodan's powers include the ability to leap through the air despite his great bulk, the ability to project beams of heat from his eyes, the ability to summon corpses from the sky, and the ability to open gateways to hell and summon more demons to aid him in battle! He is also capable of manipulating the elemental forces of darkness and flames.

      Purviews: Fire, Darkness, Psychopomp, Death, Prophecy

      Epics: Strength, Stamina, Manipulation

                      Legendary Relics: Below is a list of some of the most powerful Relics that are mentioned in this religion



                      Worldstone: According to the legends, this colossal sized crystal was used to create Sanctuary, the realm more commonly known as Earth to the mortals. The Worldstone has a spiritual connection to Sanctuary, and the demons believe that if they can corrupt the Worldstone, they can successfully corrupt mankind as well. Fortunately the Worldstone has been hidden away, and the demons are still trying to locate it even to this day.



                      Soulstones: These crystals function as prisons that can store a victim's soul inside. The Soulstones also have a connection to the Worldstone, and by corrupting them, the demons could slowly corrupt the Worldstone even without needing to be in physical contact with it. During the Dark Exile, the Prime Evils were each sealed within a Soulstone for what was supposed to be eternity, unknowing that the Prime Evils had intended this to happen from the start. Unfortunately the Soulstones were not strong enough to contain the powers of a god, and so they were embedded into human hosts, with the hope that their willpower would be able to keep the demon lords contained. Sadly mere mortals were no match for the will of a being so ancient as the Prime Evils, and their hosts were possessed. Over time their bodies wore out, and they metamorphed into the Prime Evils once again.



                      Hell Forge: This legendary forge is where all the most powerful relics of the Burning Hells are forged. It is also one of the few things in existence that can destroy a Soulstone, freeing (or killing) whatever is trapped inside of it!



                      Black Soulstone: A legendary artifact, this Soulstone is roughly 5 times bigger than any normal Soulstone and is jet black in color. Unlike normal versions, the Black Soulstone is powerful enough to contain all the original Great Evils at the same time. A prophecy tells that should this ever come to pass, they will fuse their essences, and Tathamet shall be reborn. The Black Soulstone is directly involved with the Great Evil version of Ragnarok.



                      Diamond Gates: Apparently the gates to Heaven are less pearly than mortals seem to believe. The Diamond Gates are nigh indestructible, and have withstood the assaults of the Great Evils and their armies since the beginning of time. The same prophecy that foretells the rebirth of Tathamet also foretells demons breaching the Diamond Gates. For this reason, they too are directly involved in the Great Evil version of Ragnarok.



                      Horadric Cube: The Horadrim were mortal mages who were responsible for overseeing the Soulstones during the Dark Exile and ensuring the containment of the Prime Evils. Although they are virtually extinct now, some of their artifacts live on. The Horadric Cube is a tool for transmutation. By placing objects inside, the cube is able to transform them into a more powerful relic. Only certain combinations will work, however, and it can require trial and error to figure out what is beneficial, and what is a waste of resources.



                      Tristram: This shit hole of a town gets no mercy and has fallen prey to demons many times in the past, but its people always rebuild. Why they don't simply decide to move is a question better left to the sages. Tristram's cathedral is massive because it was the original prison for Diablo's Soulstone during the time of the Dark Exile. Diablo was eventually freed and unleashed an army of demons from the cathedral depths. Many wrongly believe that Diablo was destroyed at the bottom level by Aidan, the wandering hero.
