    Question: Which patron god or titan would fit this character for our next game?

    09-25-2024, 12:56 AM
    Got a question and, yeah, I am new on the forums, but it has been buzzing around in my head and on our game table as to this question. I have a certain player that wants to play a male take on the "Mother of Monsters" at our table, but all of us are starting out at the sorcerer level in Scion. So, which patron god or titan for that matter would fit this character? Keep in mind, he also has his girlfriend who is cooperating with the storyline as well; they are on our side/the band's side, and their characters in story have a kind of Loki-Angrboda relationship...partners in crime as well as in experimentation and he is more than happy to be experimented on by her. Their romantic relationship is tight as well; it is why they came it with it and consented to it for our next game; the same way with their characters. It is how his character wound up being a new "Mother and Father of Monsters" that way. Question is, should he be a scion of Loki or a scion of Echidna? Those are the only ones we can legit think off of the top of our heads for this character.

    If it is any additional information, we have integrated Mage into the system and he is a Thrysus mage. The girlfriend is an Acanthus, if it is anything to get a discernment of their characters' relationship as to their paths. Any help or assistance would be helpful as we are at an impasse on this. We also thought Tiamat would be an option or possible Shub-Niggurath if we brought the Mythos into it as well for addiitional ideas. Still, it would be helpful to discern as to where this character would fit. As to how we think he might fit as a character in the band, we think (as well as he thinks) that his character might be more of a summoner except that his summons are actually summoned from him. This would be either creatures or reborn souls weaponized into an army. Essentially, taking the Titans of the Fertility purview's ability for the side of good. As to his future role as a god, he is looking at his character being a god of knowledge, creation, balance, and time of their new pantheon as the band was rejected from their previous one, mainly on the grounds that they disagreed with how things were run in it and actually called the top god out on their B.S. That is a storyline to tell you one day! Creation is obvious, especially as besides this character creating his and his girlfriend's "kids" from his own body, he is also an artist and inventor, making the team's equipment and even serving as team chef as well, being the caretaker of the team and its healer as well as its leader. Balance, he neither leans toward order or chaos, but mostly, he is one for order. Knowledge, he does have the Sage calling amongst his Callings and is one for raising his and his girlfriend's "kids" as a teacher and a parent as well as using his intelligence to solve mysteries and in battle as a strategist/tactician. Time, he is also the team's diviner and does have the Prophet feature as well. That's all I can provide as to this character to help you lot get a feel for discerning him out.

          Thanks for the suggestion. I ran it by the player of this character. He provided additional details to the character as well. Mind you, we are still in brainstorming mode for this and we are trying to hash it out still. Thank goodness for online chat in PM. Here is what we came up with and, sorry, it is long, but we had a lot to discuss. As for why the male character is the childbearing one, we came to the idea that it would be a unique take on the Slepinir story and we liked the idea of a pregnant male badass mage. It would be something different, let alone dealing with a pregnancy from a combination of magic, science, and two initially horny magical teens who got to experimenting and it became permanent.

          He's all for a scion of Loki, btw. So far, we got the fact that the relationship between divine parent and child is going to be a good, tight one; his character is loyal to his father/mother (wierd story there, but his divine father shapeshifted into a woman and gave birth to him). He would have been a twin with a twin sister, but he absorbed her in utero as she died before being born, and, thus, with his own magic and shapeshifting, he can take on her form as well. That also made him an intersexed at birth, but he presents as male, lives as a cisgender male, and his paperwork says he is a transman due to legal work and bureaucracy not being able to keep their facts straight. As to other information about how he relates to Loki, it helps the fact that his mortal parents knew about him/them in advance when he was younger. His mortal family had a family business in the occult and monster/ghost hunting, thus, in part why he thinks talking to the dead and solving their issues to move on is no big deal. He was raised with the supernatural in his life; so, he embraces it as normal. As for his relationship with Loki, it started when he was a kid when he actually came to know about him. He even created a small altar to him and gave him his best Halloween candy to him in addition to handmade art and jewelry from summer camp. When he could, he/they would even visit on holidays or during the summer. Eventually, when he had money from being a young artist and inventor, he could afford greater things for his altar to him, not to mention as he got older, he included offerings of bourbon and whisky on the altar as well. However, he has proven to be a loyal, good son to him, ever the dutiful one to both his divine and mortal parents. Loki treated him well, even providing relics and creatures (case in point, his dog and falcon, later, horse as well).

          He grew up upper middle class, the eldest of four kids, and had to be the caretaker of his younger brother and sisters while their parents were away. His mortal family included not only his parents (a scientist father interested in the paranormal and his adoptive mother, a lawyer to help the family business out of messes, his aunts (one of which is a music and art teacher), and his military uncle who taught him, his siblings, and several neighborhood kids martial arts and sports). His younger brother is a scion of Thor, btw. His little sisters are entirely normal though. The relationship between him and his little brother is good, thick as thieves those two, but with different personalities. He is much more of the guileful and charismatic of the two, more of an intelligent, agile fighter. His younger brother basically becomes the hitter of their band, completely armed with a Lightning Slugger (Mjolnir if it was a baseball bat) and a Thundercaster (a shotgun that produces thunder and lightning). However, his younger brother has always felt the need to defend him due to multiple things in his history (i.e., cardiac condition that he was born with congenitally that was resolved through multiple surgeries and previously being sexually assaulted by an adult that abducted him in his history). So, that explains the younger brother's protectiveness towards his older brother. That just gets amped up when he knows he is going to become an uncle though and going to have a lot more responsibility placed on him as a field commander when he is out of commission or in recovery.

          His girlfriend and her little sister lived next door to them and, at first, they were rivals, but became friends and then something more, even getting fake married on the playground at seven years old. So, they became a power couple right off and Loki approved of it, even helped get them together a bit in his/their own way. After all, he appreciated her gremlin side and the fact that both were geniuses who played off of each other for mischief and chaos pleased him, especially since they weaponized it for good against monsters and the evils against the world. They even are hackers together, taking down evil corporations and people together. As to him becoming the new "Father and Mother of Monsters", that begin at age 16. They got around to experimenting with magic and Inspired/mad science and he wound up being the knocked up one as a result. This worked out well for them as it was discovered that his girlfriend had endometriosis and could not carry a child; so, being the selfless guy that he is (virtue is Charity, btw), he decided to carry for her. This meant a bevy of grandkids for both sides of the family as the biological mortal family that Loki was a part of on his kid's maternal side was known for multiples. So, he had to adapt to that. It was how their first six were born (three shapeshifters, three magi, one of which was part spectral (taking after Hel a bit there). He is the mastermind/thief of his team while his girlfriend is the primary hacker. He is not the sole strategist/tactician/logistician of his team either; his girlfriend backs him up on it. She is protective of him as well, everyone on their team as he is the one often carrying precious cargo, used in battle or not. Notably, they even integrate his pregnancy summoning into strategies to summon up legions of creatures and troops when needed. As to how that is done, it is done via portals connected to him, but it is still painful. However, he is a trooper; he can take it.

          As to education, he went at first to a normal school, public school in New York City. However, it was discovered that, due to his previous trauma and advanced intellect, he need to be in a gifted program with therapeutic features built in. As a result of that trauma, he also has Wrath as his vice. He has a temper, especially against rapists and those that assault children like how he has been through. He also hates bullies in any shape, form, or fashion. He and his team started out there, but eventually went on to boarding school and then university. He and his girlfriend already had a jumpstart on that at an early age as they went to an early college earlier on. They helped their siblings get in and they graduated early together. So, by the time that they were 16 and their siblings were 15, they were out of school and graduated already and working at that point before university. At university, he worked and was a student and a young dad; so, thank goodness that he had his parents and the university had good childcare. He worked several jobs, restarted his art and music career, but was not satisfied with that. He became a scientist/inventor/businessperson (because med school is expensive), then a doctor, and now, his adoptive mom is pushing him into law and politics. He did some military, intelligence, and volunteer service as well. He and his band were known to get into mischief as kids at school, but they weaponized this in their teens as thieves against evil. They saved their worst tricks for evil doers such as the Illuminati in Brooklyn. They solved mysteries and fought monsters and ghosts since they were kids and as teens. They still continue to do so as adults, but more carefully as one of their number is expecting (him) and they got kids in the picture now. His girlfriend has remained with him and they are still a power couple; she takes over for him when he is out of commision as secondary leader. They eventually get married, both on Earth and in front of the Aesir, the Vanir, and the Jotnar/Rokkr. He also tells off Odin for his B.S. as to certain matters; it does not make for easy relations between them. However, relations between the team and Loki are solid. He now has his go-to group and a bevy of grandkids, both legit as well as creatures and reborn souls, out of it. Added to that, their cabal/band makes him look good. They are chaotic, but they bring order. They do good and it makes him/them look good in the process as he is the patron/father/mother of one of them. So, he/they can boast that, "Yeah, I created that and that is one thing Odin cannot take away from me." He even helps get the family back together (Fenrir, Hel, and Jormundgandr), helps stop Ragnarok along with his team and his dad/mom, and probably caused a revolution in Asgard in the process against Odin. Mind you, we are also playing with the Scion: Ragnarok part of it now.

          As a scion and as an adult, he still fights, but has learned to master his new body and condition. Their, his and his girlfriend's legit kids in game, often stay with their mortal grandparents when they are off with their cabal/band as they often travel to places such as Egypt, Romania, and Japan on their journey. He is the healer, leader, and seer of the band. His girlfriend, later wife, is second in command. His brother is the team's hitter and her little sister is the team's grifter. So, they are not just monster/ghost hunters; they are a caper crew. Their main villaiins are those of an evil corporation, one that has been hunting him down since his first pregnancy at age 16 for their biotech division, and a cult that has been trying to kill all of them for years in addition to other threats, both arcane and mundane. He's also the summoner/golem maker and childbearer of the team. There is even talk of him being a surrogate for his younger brother's kids when it is time. Purviews-wise, we are thinking along the lines of more of a dual form with him (one lean for espionage purposes, the other rounded out with a kid or more). We are using the knacks for the goddess of maternity as listed on the forums here for those purposes. His fertile form is more powerful than his lean form. We are playing with house rules, being more of a blend of 1st and 2nd editions. So, he has the Animal, Fertility, Health, and Nature/Wilderness purviews off the bat, given the fact that he is a Thrysus; Magic and Prophecy as well as Mystery are also a given. He also has control over the dead as well; so, Death is additional as well. Elementally, he also controls Earth and Water as well, Frost/Ice in his lean form (like Winter lies fallow before becoming fertile in Spring). He is an elementalist as a mage, so, even Fire and Sky even fall under that as well. Like parent, like child, he also weaponizes Chaos against his enemies as well. Weapons-wise, he is an excellent mage, but does not rely on it solely. He backs it up with bladework, staff, and spear/javelin combat as well as marksmanship, either with a bow and arrow or an actual gun (rifle or pistols, doesn't matter, but he is also his team's sniper and scout as well). He's the wheelman and sailor of the team as well in addition to tech specialist (he built his own motorcycle and the team's as well).

          So, while this is a lot of information from my notes on this conversation with my player, this is what we hashed out. Now, it is just finding out the relics for him. We got a feeling a knife that could become a sword would be useful, maybe a runic staff that could transform into a spear as well. The motorcycle is a given, but we are also looking at books or a laptop as well for him as well. Since he is associated with kids as well, probably something related to that as well. However, while long post is long, what do you lot think? Any suggestions? Any suggestions as to relics, creatures, followers? Again, thanks for the help.
