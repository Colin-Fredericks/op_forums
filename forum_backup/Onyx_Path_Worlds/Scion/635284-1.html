                Alright, here are the first two I came up with. One for Hastur, and one for Shub-Niggurath.


                Maxwell Quinn: Scion of Hastur


                Calling: Professor of the Occult


                Nature: Pedagogue


                Legend: 3


                Details: Although he considers himself to be an educated scholar, Maxwell Quinn has nearly lost his status multiple times due to what his colleagues refer to as "extreme methods" in the field of research. He was not content to merely study the occult, but actually went so far as to practice it. He's been accused of withcraft, ritual sacrifice, babbling in tongues and other disturbing behavior during his career, and yet somehow he manages to retain his status and continue his studies. He paid little thought as to how this was possible, until the day of his visitation, when he attempted to evoke the King in Yellow. It was a surprise for Maxwell when the spell worked, but probably not as big a surprise when Hastur proclaimed to be his father, and that his success in the field of research had been due to his protection!

                Since his visitation, Maxwell's become nearly obsessed with his work. The last time his colleagues attempted to prosecute him, he didn't even bother to defend himself, but rather he shut himself in his home and refused to come out for weeks until he was completely out of food. Nobody knows what he was doing in there the whole time, and frankly, nobody wants to know. He's just another crazy hermit that can't stand human contact, as far as normal people are concerned.

                One thing that others will noticed about Maxwell's work as a Scion is that he is increasing his power stupidly fast. And by that, we mean that he is delving into the secrets of the cosmose with seemingly little regard for safety precautions. Even his fellow Scions aren't particularly eager to learn what the Great Old Ones have to share, and yet Maxwell strides into their ancient temples like he lives there and begins pouring over the forbidden knowledge contained within. Many believe that his eagerness to learn will be bring about his own horrifically tragic doom, but some believe that it is his thirst for knowledge that is the key to transcending the limitations of the human form.

                Perhaps in times past, Mr. Quinn would have been a respectable looking man. But since receiving his visitaiton, he has been largely unconcerned with managing his appearance. His dark hair has grown long enough that the bangs can reach his chin, and it hangs matted and greasy like a mop upon his head. His eyes have become dark and sunken from long nights without sleep, his posture is sagging and the fine yellow suit he once wore has become faded and tattered with over use. He looks like a man on the verge of an mental breakdown, which is only to be expected from a son of Hastur! Despite his ragged appearance, Maxwell is only in his mid 30's and is far from being the feeble old scientist many expect him to be.

                As a Scion, Maxwell's arsenal of Purviews is more limited than one might expect. But the ones he does have access to, he had put a great deal of effort into mastering. Upon his visitation, Hastur presented him with an amulet baring his symbol which allows him to channel the Chaos Purview. He also carries a black journal in which he has enscribed all the spells and prayer ceremonies he has learned during his service to the Great Old Ones.


                Powers: Epic Manipulation, Epic Intelligence, Epic Appearance, Magic, Chaos, Majnun


                Skills: Academics, Integrity, Investigation, Occult, Science, Melee


                Birthrights: Sign of Hastur (1 Purview) 1, Black Journal (1 Purview) 1

                Malik Meyer: Scion of Shub-Niggurath


                Calling: Love Guru


                Nature: Pacifist


                Legend: 3


                Details: When most people imagine a Scion of the Great Old Ones, they envision dark and deranged individuals that scream at the stars and draw symbols on every surface of their house. They do not imagine Malik Meyer, a friendly and generous young man who believes in trading words, not blows. Before his visitation, Malik served as a love guru, traveling the world teaching both men and women the ways of happiness, peace and love making. Especially love making, as a matter of fact.

                However, one should not assume that just because Malik doesn't like to fight, that means he doesn't know how. Indeed, Malik studies Tai Chi, learning to turn his opponent's own strengths against them in battle. When forced into a confrontation, he grapples or throws his enemy around, waiting for them to tire themselves out. Once this is achieved, he is willing to try diplomacy again, should it be possible.

                It was Malik's promiscuous behavior that lead to his visitation, when he ended up having sex with his own mother, Shub-Niggurath. Of course, she was in disguise at the time, but that doesn't change the fact he had just committed incest with a Great Old One. Pleased with his skills in the bedroom, she revealed his true heritage. After which, she encouraged him to "go forth and propagate," which Malik happily agreed to.

                Having sex with The Black Goat of the Woods with a Thousand Young has not left Malik unscathed. Shortly after his visitation, Malik began to exhibit symptoms of being omnisexual. He has yet to find a life form in any reality that he is not willing to mate with. Species and gender mean nothing to Malik when it comes to sexual intercourse. This behavior has the drawback that only those with a broad sense of humor can tolerate working with him for long periods of time. Numerous are the tales of Malik's allies preparing to do battle, only to storm the enemy hide out and find the godling laying in a naked heap with their foe, the conflict having been resolved without the need for bloodshed. Sometimes his more violent or respectable allies have tried to throttle him for being unable to keep it in his pants!

                Malik is a young man in his late 20's who is in very good condition. His body is lean and flexible from years of practicing the Kama Sutra. He has wavy black hair and green eyes and often wears the robes of a guru as his official garb. He also has a a beard that reaches down to his neck and is well groomed. He stands at 6'0 tall, weighing 139 pounds and often sports a cheerful disposition.

                Since his visitation, he has received numerous gifts from Shub-Niggurath, allowing him to channel the powers of the Great Old Ones. Under his robes, he wears an amulet baring the symbol of Shub-Niggurath, which allows him to channel the Animal and Fertility purviews. He also carries a variety of scented body oils which allows him to channel the Health purview. The bottles are also enchanted to replenish their contents over night, ensuring that Malik has an infinite supply!



                Powers: Epic Stamina, Epic Appearance, Epic Dexterity, Animal (Goats) Fertility, Health, Majnun


                Skills: Animal Ken, Empathy, Survival, Awareness, Athletics, Brawl


                Birthrights: Sign of Shub-Niggurath (2 Purviews) 2, Body Oils (1 Purview, 1 Unique Power) 3

                    Maria Fox: Scion of Azathoth

                    Calling: PTS War Veteran

                    Nature: Penitent

                    Legend: 3

                    Details: The children of the Great Old Ones are often the subjects of tragedy, and while Maria Fox is able to remain functional (at times), she is not without her own share of problems. Her career in the military had subjected her to the many horrors of war, but it was towards the end of her service that provided her visitation and the real horrors emerged. During the middle of the night, deep behind enemy lines, her squad mounted an assault that ended in disaster. A nearby explosion sent her toppling over a ridge and into a chasm below, where she came to a stop at the base of an ancient temple to Azathoth. It was the mad god's servants who emerged from the temple, carrying her inside and tending to her wounds.

                    They explained to her half conscious form that she was a child of their lord and entitled to great power. Maria was not in any condition to question what was happening, at least not at first. Even now, she still does not know how long she remained down in that temple, but when her wounds healed, she began to freak out as any rational human would. She continued to kick and scream until her strength gave out and she fainted once more. The next time she awoke, she was in a military hospital and diagnosed with Post Traumatic Stress Disorder. She was discharged shortly after and sent to a mental health facility for treatment. But during the night, she had a particularly strong flash back and forcefully broke herself out, igniting a savage riot that killed four doctors, ten orderlies and thirty patients!

                    Since becoming a Scion, Maria spends her life as a ticking time bomb. The trauma she received in combat, in addition to what she has witnessed in service to the Old Gods have pushed her mind to the breaking point. When she finally does snap, she becomes a raging hurricane of destruction who attacks without sense of morality or hesitation. When she comes out of these fits, she is mortified by her own actions and struggles to make amends for it. Unfortunately for her, she has begun to notice a dreadful pattern. The closer she gets towards apotheosis, the stronger her fits become. It would not be the first time her allies have had to forcefully restrain her in order to calm her down. Maria's greatest fear is that she will degenerate into a creature as wildly unstable as her father, so she values what friends she has beyond measure.

                    Even though she is long since retired from active service, Maria Fox still wears her combat fatigues, or at least when covering the lower section of her body. Her upper body is commonly dressed in a tank top for freedom of movement. Her favorite colors for said tanktop include dark black, army green, or simple gray. Her skin is dark tanned with light brown hair and blue eyes. She stands 5'7 tall and is in very good conditioning due to her military training, weighing around 145 lbs. She is also a fan of wearing combat boots and army gloves and continues to wear her dog tags just incase she forgets who she is.

                    While Maria was tended to in the temple, the servants of Azathoth reforged her belongings into powerful Relics to serve her. The dog tags she wears allows her to channel the Fire and Chaos Purviews, they are also tragically cursed. Any thief who attempts to steal her tags will suffer Azathoth's wrath as one person who they value will die every week until they are returned to Maria's possession. Her M16 (who she has named Henry) is also extremely deadly now, as it never runs out of ammo and is able to draw strength from her Legend in order to increase both her Marksmanship ability and the weapon's firing range (Legend X5)!



                    Powers: Epic Stamina, Epic Strength, Epic Dexterity, Fire, Majnun, Chaos


                    Skills: Fortitude, Presence, Thrown, Brawl, Marksmanship, Athletics


                    Birthrights: Dog Tags (2 Purviews, 1 Unique Power) 3, Henry (M16 - 3 Unique Powers) 4


                    Role Playing Tips: You are not proud of what you have been forced to do over the years. While others may congratulate you on your prowess in battle, you are personally disgusted by it. Your psychotic episodes are abhorrent to you, as they would be to any normal person. And yet you are powerless to stop them from happening in stressful circumstances. All you can do is ride out the storm and hope there's enough left to salvage the situation.

                    Thankfully, you have friends to keep you under control when times are tough. They provide structure and stability in your life after all you have seen and done. It is rare to find people willing to stick around after they learn about your condition, so when you meet friends who do, you value them highly. Without them, you'd probably descend into madness permanently.

                    Orson (Father) Galashiels: Scion of Cthulhu

                    Calling: Prophet of the Old Ones

                    Nature: Fanatic

                    Legend: 3


                    Details: Somebody should tell Orson that there is a difference between preaching your religion and shoveling it down peoples' throats. The so called "Father" Galashiels is a hare's breath away from being classified as a raving lunatic. It is only due to his religious attire and sacred text he carries that affords him any sense of credibility by the impressionable masses who flock to hear his sermons. Prior to his visitation, Orson served as the priest of an isolated town who rarely saw contact with the outside world. During the course of many years, he had twisted the beliefs of his constituents to view his word as law, while quickly disposing of those who spoke out. It was after he had successfully managed to convince the entire town that he was some kind of prophet that he was granted his visitation by Cthulhu as a reward for his hard work.

                    After learning the truth of his heritage, Orson began to perceive godhood as his birthright. He is obsessed with apotheosis and will go to any lengths to obtain that lofty achievement. Almost immediately, he announced his divinity to the people and turned his cult towards the worship of the Great Old Ones, which was made significantly easier with his Scion abilities.

                    Before his visitation, Orson lead his town like a puritan, reacting harshly to anything he deemed immoral or corrupt. After his visitation, both Orson and his flock became something entirely different. The men of his town were blank eyed laborers, while the women knelt at his feet like a bountiful harem. He could have had anything he wanted without asking for permission, but the isolated town he had lived in all his life was an insignificant speck on a dust particle in the universe. And so he eventually left his town behind to further persue godhood.

                    Orson Galashiels is a middle aged man who is approaching his 50's and is starting to develop streaks of gray around the sides of his brown hair. His preferred manner of dress looks like it was modified from catholic robes, now utilizing a Great Old One motiff. His most common outfit is a black robe with embroidery depicting green tentacles wrapping around his body and an octopus pendant clasped around the collar. His eyes are pale green, and yet somehow his glare seems to burn with the fury that only a zealot can manifest. He carries a book under his arm everywhere he goes.

                    In recognition of his skills, Cthulhu presented Orson with a new bible, penned in human blood which allows him to channel the Water and Sky purviews. The pendant he wears allows him to channel the Animal purview and draws strength from the wearer's Legend towards commanding others.


                    Powers: Epic Stamina, Epic Strength, Epic Charisma, Epic Manipulation, Animal (Crustaceans), Animal (lizards), Water, Sky, Majnun


                    Skills: Command, Occult, Athletics, Fortitude, Presence, Animal Ken


                    Birthrights: Bloody Bible (2 Purviews) 2, Octopus Pendant (1 Purview, 1 unique power) 3


                    Role Playing Hints: None can change Fate, and it is your Fate to obtain godhood, or so you vehemently believe. You are the spawn of one of the most powerful entities in the cosmos; how could you be destined for anything less than to join their ranks? So far as you're concerned, apotheosis is a guarantee, and you will go to any lengths to achieve it. You don't care who you have to sacrifice to get there, because the rewards far outstrip the price!

                    Like the majority of religious folks, you do not take kindly to being challenged or questioned. For decades now, you have been accustomed to getting your way, either through gentle persuasion, or forceful coercion. Those who would challenge your supreme authority on matters of faith and governance must be punished as befits heretics and blasphemers.

                    Samantha Baxter: Scion of Yog-Sothoth

                    Calling: Master Thief

                    Nature: Rogue

                    Legend: 3


                    Details: Many consider thieving to be a lost art. With all the technology and surveillance of the modern age, it should be impossible for master criminals to operate like they used to. Gone are the days of elaborate bank heists and silent cat burglars, now it's just petty street level crime. Well not for Samantha Baxter, who takes pride in her work. Coming from four generations of criminal masterminds, she considers thieving to be an art form and attempts to pull off crimes that would be right at home in action films. Perhaps it is her passion for bypassing security measures that caused Yog-Sothoth to visit her and reveal the truth of her family legacy. Or perhaps it was the fact that she got caught breaking into a house Yog-Sothoth was residing in during one of his rare visits to the World. Either way, Samantha learned some things that night, some that she really would have been happier not knowing.

                    As a child of the Opener of Ways, there is almost no security system in the World that Samantha can not bypass. The ones that give her any regular sense of trouble are supernatural in nature, but even then she has her Scion abilities to help her out. Since becoming a Scion, she has utilized her talents for obtaining things that her father's enemies don't want to share. Powerful artifacts, compromizing secrets, whatever is worth locking away from the rest of the world. She is popular among other Scions of the Old Ones, as they are often sent on tasks by their parents that many would consider suicidal, and having a security expert to guide them increases their chance of survival.

                    Samantha is a woman who appears to be in her mid 20's and is in amazingly great shape due to rigorous exercise and practice. She has light blond hair with dark blue eyes and light skin. When in public, she almost always wears a cat suit used by professional thieves thoughout the world. The suit is made of leather and molds to her form almost like a second skin, which allows it to be worn under normal clothing if she needs it. She stands 5'0 tall, weighing 128 pounds, easily being able to slip through tight spaces and support her body weight.

                    In exchange for "agreeing" to serve Yog-Sothoth, Samantha was presented with a series of Relics to aid in her tasks. The first of which is a cat suit that was once used by Samantha's great, great grandfather who worshiped Yog-Sothoth. It enables her to channel the Psychopomp Purview and uses the wearer's Legend to enhance their Stealth Abilities. The second is a pair of black gloves which enable her to channel her Legend for enhancing her Larceny skills and gain access to both the Star and Guardian Purviews.


                    Powers: Epic Perception, Epic Wits, Epic Intelligence, Epic Dexterity, Psychopomp, Stars, Guardian, Majnun


                    Skills: Athletics, Academics, Awareness, Larceny, Stealth, Brawl


                    Birthrights: Cat Suit (Leather Armor - 1 Purview, 1 Unique Abilities) 2, Black Gloves (2 Purviews, 1 Unique Ability) 3


                    Role Playing Hints: It's not about the reward, it's about the presentation. Thievery is your passion, your art. Four generations of your family name became master criminals during their life time and you are proud to carry on the tradition. The modern age doesn't spell the death of your career, it's an opportunity to become the next legendary criminal that mortals will be talking about for generations to come!

                    Apparently your skills have begun attracting the attention of others like yourself. The life of a Scion is dangerous, and the Gods do not give up their secrets lightly. This calls for a special touch to get one's hands on the prize without getting killed in the process, and that's where you come in! So long as they're willing to provide you with "hazard pay" you can get them just about anything they need.

                        The Elder Gods

                        This Pantheon began in the fevered dreams of madmen and prophets. Little is really understood about many of the Old Ones and when asked about their age many reply that they existed simply Before. Some of the Gods believe that such beings are primordial Gods, an early attempt for the Titans to create servitors. Although others think they may have not formed from the Titans at all, but rather from the sea of pure chaos from which the World was originally formed. They are perhaps the Gods least concerned with the pull of Fate. The Old Ones believe that only true understanding and a devotion to a primordial society are the keys to stopping the Titan’s advance. Individually their Scions often try to rip the veil of ignorance that has been thrown over humanities collective eyes and often with an almost religious zeal.


                        Virtues: Piety, Intellect, Duty, Conviction

                        The Unspeakable History
                        While most Gods took up arms against their creators. Some did not so, whether the Elder Ones were simply unconcerned with the war between the Gods and Titans or they in fact betrayed the Gods in favor of the Titans has been lost to time. Most of the other Pantheons have select few inferences about them, regarding the entire group as a curiosity. The truth is that the Gods and Titans both attacked the Elder Ones who they saw as supporting the other's cause. The Gods that fought them are still confused on the particulars of the exchange, the Vhoorl, Old Ones godrealm was much more fluid then and many of the Gods were still young. In any case they fought to a standstill and as the last of the Gods turned back, thinking the best course of action was return to their patricide and worry about traitors at another time, the Titans arrived. The battle that ensued was not a thing that any man (and many Gods) could comprehend, but when it was finished the Titans moved on, not flush with victory but in contempt that such lesser entities would dare move against them.
                        But the Old Ones did not die that day. Many had access to powerful forms of travel and hid among the starry sky. There were those that simply refused to perish choosing instead to slumber for millenniums on end. Their city R'lyeh lay shattered cast from the Overworld it fell beneath the sea. Their pantheon was broken its relics and creatures tossed aimlessly across the World.
                        In time mortals would come to worship them once more falling into half remembered prayers and craven disorganized cults. These Gods woke in spurts and fits coming to for brief spans before returning to their banishment. There are references if one knows where to look. A Greek inventor forging a curious clockwork machine that tracks the movement of the stars with an eerie accuracy. A blind mad prophet in the Middle East scribbling a dark book of prayer and magic sometime in the 7th century. An early American colony swept away in an instant, the only clue a single word. A quiet town transformed by the falling of a star. The loss of NASAs mars rover, believed to have miscalculated its voyage to the Red Planet.
                        The shattering cage that bound the Titan's has roused them and in time the Titan's may learn the true fury of the Old Ones. Their ichor has found its home far and wide across the World. Now those Scions who lived their entire life in ignorance are being made aware of that hideous strength they have inherited.

                        Azathoth
                        AKA: The Blind Idiot God, Nuclear Chaos, Daemon Sultan, That Hideous Name, The Lord of All Things
                        (God of Chaos)
                        This God began outside the ordered universe, the chaotic half of a dual monarchy. It once sat in the center of the universe an amorphous blight of chaos. Within a great spiraling vortex was his black throne a place devoid of light and sensation. He cannot see and so he relies on the input of his servitors. Ultimately the mindless Azathoth has nothing of a goal to speak of; rather his actions are like those of a child varying from whim to whim. His consorts dance about him, shapeless playing a song they have never stopped: a muffled maddening beat of drums, accompanied by a thin monotonous whine of accursed flutes. Those who serve Azathoth do not speak his name and those who worship him often attempt to mimic the foul beating of his palace song. Azathoth holds a book filled with every secret name that ever was, whether he knows the truth within is a subject of debate. Scions of Azathoth are rarely given names or even identity, most are forced instead to fend for themselves. Often times they act in a completely uncoordinated manner, although some Gods fear they may instead have some purpose yet understood.
                        Associate Powers: Epic Dexterity, Epic Charisma, Epic Appearance, Epic Wits, Chaos, Darkness, Mystery, Magic, Prophecy, Pavor Nocturnis
                        Associate Abilities: Art, Integrity, Fortitude, Occult, Politics, Presence

                        Yog-Sothoth
                        AKA: The Lurker at the Threshold, The Key and the Gate, The Beyond One, Opener of the Way, All-in-One and One-in-All, Tawil At-U’mr
                        (God of the Gateways and Guardian of the Paths)
                        When the World was young some Gods still found no reason to lock themselves within creations bounds, Yog-Sothoth remained outside the World. He is as wise as Azathoth is mad and once surrounded the edges of the universe, the strongest half king of the Old Ones. The Gate is the point at which Titans emerged from the primordial chaos, where they divided primordial chaos from the World. Yog-Sothoth is at once the key and the guardian of the Gate. He often takes the form of a conglomeration of iridescent globes which throb eerily with the pulse of the stars. When he appears as a man it is always a silhouette hidden behind a shimmering veil. Among those that follow Yog-Sothoth they often find his statements and goals cryptic, his plans taking years if not decades to come to fruition. For Yog-Sothoth knows all that passes through his domain. Those who come seeking to find answers from him often learn more than they desire. Scions that operate for the Yog-Sothoth serve at his beck and call, while power is his to give it often comes with the price of eternal service or powerful sacrifice.
                        Associate Powers: Epic Strength, Epic Perception, Epic Intelligence, Sun, Psychopomp, Prophecy, Guardian, Sky, Pavor Nocturnis
                        Associate Abilities: Academics, Awareness, Command, Investigation, Integrity, Science

                        Shub-Niggurath
                        AKA: The Black Goat of the Woods with a Thousand Young, All-Mother
                        (God of Fertility)
                        The Wife of Yog-Sothoth, is still remembered as a pagan fertility God among those who still remember. While she walked among men great copper temples were built in her honor and heretical cults which defied simpler faiths persisted long into the Roman era. When Shub-Niggurath takes form she often does so as a great black Goat delivering sustenance to those who call on her. Those who know say that her magic is powerful enough to raise the dead, which Shub-Niggurath has done on occasion and her name can still be found in the incantation of spells that God and Scion use alike. From her husband she has two children the twin blasphemies Nug and Yeb. What servants Shub-Niggurath holds are unintelligent creatures or the dead. She is an enigma among the Old Ones and none are sure if she is in fact a God or Titan. Perhaps only Azathoth knows for sure and none have sought to ask him.
                        Associate Powers: Epic Stamina, Animal (Goat), Fertility, Health, Magic, Pavor Nocturnis
                        Associate Abilities: Animal Ken, Athletics, Empathy, Medicine, Melee, Occult

                        Nyarlathotep
                        AKA: The Crawling Chaos
                        (Messenger God)
                        While this God began as a servant to the Old Ones he has left that calling behind long ago. He now serves as a messenger of sorts and has made the transition to the modern day with much of his initial identity intact, something which few of those like him can claim. Nyarlathotep frequently walks the Earth in the guise of a tall, slender man. At heart he views himself as an educator. Part showman, part lecturer he strips away carefully constructed views of the World to reveal the often hideous and terrible truth. Azathoth still remains his master, of sorts. When the mad beast calls on Nyarlathotep he is quick to answer, whether it is because of the infrequency of these calls, some mutual understanding, or a sense of obligation is up to debate. Nyarlathotep delights in pushing mortals into madness they can not escape. He has few followers and prefers to operate alone and well hidden, out of some half realized fear that the Titans may cast him out again. For now propaganda remains Nyarlathotep’s greatest tool and he has managed to see the stories of the Old Ones have in part reached mankind, through the sordid writings of a half mad New England man.

                        Associate Powers: Epic Charisma, Epic Manipulation, Epic Appearance, Epic Wits, Chaos, Psychopomp, Pavor Nocturnis
                        Associate Abilities: Awareness, Control, Empathy, Marksmanship, Presence, Stealth

                        Cthulhu
                        AKA: Great Cthulhu, Dead Cthulhu, Dread Cthulhu, Q’thulu, Kthulhut, Thu Thu, Tulu
                        (God of the Deep)
                        When the Sea was given breadth and depth to cover the young World Cthulhu reigned over its depths. He built cities which stretched from the ocean floor to the sky and populated them with his brood. Although he is an Old One he remains a distant cousin from those closest of kin, only taking time to spy on their actions from afar. This may have something to do with his parentage he was birthed from Yog-Sothot and the Titan Nagoob. His shape is like that of a great winged-cephalopod set amid a vaguely anthropoid design. While most of the Elder Gods survived the struggle with the Titans Cthulhu was slain and his greatest city R’yleh was cast down beneath the waves. But while a man may die a God cannot and over the countless aeons Cthulhu has waited in foul R’yleh, dreaming of death that will not come. Only his occasional stirrings gave any hint he still lived, but the return of his kin and the escape of the Titans revived Cthulhu and a p of Scions and ancient mortal cults which span the globe work feverishly toward his ascendancy. Prophecy proclaims that when the time is right Cthulhu will rise into a position which eclipses even Yog-Sothoth in power. If Cthulhu can be said to have a flaw it is his ambition, he does not trust his brethren and will do anything for his dream: the World that rejected him aflame with a holocaust of shouting, killing, ecstasy and freedom.
                        Associate Powers: Epic Strength, Epic Stamina, Epic Appearance (Hideous), Animal (Cephalopod), Moon, War, Water, Pavor Nocturnis
                        Associate Abilities: Athletics, Brawl, Command, Craft, Fortitude, Survival

                        Pavor Nocturnis
                        This Purview is derived from the metaphysical connection of the Old Ones to creation. Those mortals that touched on such alien divinity called them 'night terrors'. Each Old One is intimately related to a universal understanding which few can comprehend and fewer still can remain sane after such a grand epiphany.

                        Terra Incognitae

                        Miskatonic County
                        Arkham, Massachusetts is five miles north of Salem and twenty miles north of boston. Arkham was settled in 1692, and incorporated in 1699. Textiles formed the bulk of its industry but as time progressed it became more and more another genteel residential suburb of Boston.
                        Dunwich, Massachusetts is roughly 70 miles northwest of Arkham near the Vermont border. A small farming community that at one time housed several large lumber mills. One strange thing about the area was a set of standing stones set upon a mound.
                        Rather they were. Before these New England towns were built each area was a site where a piece of the Godrealm of the Old Ones fell. While minor in of themselves, they had certain, unusual consequences. In 1892 a meteorite landed a mile or so west of the city slowly it drained the life of everything around it, filling the rustic farm of Nahum Gardner (a local) and surrounding countryside with wholly alien life. In truth it was another piece of the Old Ones realm, it had been cast far from Terra but eventually it was drawn back, like a magnet that is drawn to its opposite pole.
                        For generations the Whateley worshiped the Old Ones at the site of the standing stones and in turn their prayers were heard by chance. The splinter that fell to blasted heath rosed Yog-Sothot, it took him 20 years to finally awaken and investigate. He was surprised to discover the family's veneration. February of 1913 Wilbur Whateley was born. Yog-Sothot had since departed having found other interests. Whately's Visitation occurred while he worshiped at the stones. But he was not blessed with Birthrights as so many Scions are. Whateley instead attempted to use the stones to travel which he hoped resonated strongly enough with the Great Henge to allow him to leave behind the World. At the Terra Incognita Wilbur believed he could utilize the passage of the lost stars to find the scattered members of the Old Ones.
                        He was thought to have been killed obtaining a Birthright from Miskatonic University by the locals. Whateley may have succeeded in leaving (what became of his journey only Fate, and the Storyteller, knows) but his impromptu construction was torn down by the locals. Whatever magic had been worked on the edifice was magnified a thousand fold by the presence of the shards of Godrealm. In an instant in September of 1928 the city of Arkham, Dunwich, and the entire Miskatonic river was stricken from the human world. It took with it the 22,938 people that made up the population of Dunwich and Arkham.
                        There are still ways to the country, it won't be found on a map except by the mad and the sane can no longer recall its existence. Strange back roads, streams that flow into the Miskatonic can be followed, or you can take the bus, if you know which bus to take.
                        Within Dunwich and Arkham life is forever trapped in the vestiges of late 1920's. No one ages in the country, no one worries about it, the people believe there is nothing wrong with the way things are and will eventually fall back into denial if told otherwise. Perhaps some careful study and Godly power could put it back, but for now at least thing will continue as they have for the last 90 years.
                        Dunwich is not a large place but it does contain a piece of the Godrealm tucked beneath those standing stones the locals broke. Anyone with a knack for digging might be able to pull something useful from the crash site.
                        The Miskatonic University contains much of the lore men know of the Old Ones. Occasionally birthrights such as the Necronomicon or other obscurer texts appear without
                        warning. Any character looking to find out about Wilbur can be sure to ask professors Armitage, Rice, and Morgan about him and his family. They should know it was these three fools that broke down the stones.
                        The Blasted Heath can still be found just out of town, the blight affecting the area is no longer active but the results are still evident. The Witch House is on 197 E. Pickman St. a peculiar place to get a nights rest.
                        Many of the Terra Incognitae have peculiar connections, Innsmouth Bustop is one of them. Twice a day at 705 Dyer St. Joe Sargent picks up to the little town of Innsmouth, its 40 cents and the drive is one way.

                        Innsmouth and Y'ha-nthlei
                        Whereas Arkham's population is largely benign and land (if surreal) mostly mundane Innsmouth is a city of perpetual darkness and terror. The population is composed entirely of about 350 Deep One Hybrids almost of which are xenophobic and territorial. It is a claustrophobic place with old nearly decayed buildings that rattle and howl with the perpetual wind. Character's who lose their way may find themselves mentally and physically exhausted by the strange winds which blow.
                        The same methods to find Arkham and Dunwich will help locate Innsmouth or the bus may be taken from the other Terra Incognita.
                        The history of the town is as peculiar as its population. In 1642 it came into being. The most it did in two hundred years that followed was trade to the Chinese and launch privateers in the wars with England. But what the townsfolk were not aware of was that the Deep One city of Y'ha-nthlei lay just off the coast. A demigod of the Elder Gods by the name of Dagon had been the sole ruler of this undersea metropolis. Unlike Dunwich's mound it was not a piece of the broken Godsrealm, Y'ha-nthlei has been a part of the World for millenniums. As the village faltered Dagon offered the people another alternative to a loss of homestead. The population would join the Esoteric Order of Dagon (a cult that personally worshiped Dagon) and in exchange for human sacrifices the Deep Ones under Dagon would provide plentiful fish and gold.There are reasons many Gods turned away from mankind. The Deep Ones began breeding with the population until only Hybrids remained. In an instance of spectacular Fatebinding an American submarine fired on the city nearly destroying it and trapping Dagon within. The two cities were so interconnected that both became Terra Incognitae and have for just over 90 years.

                        Y'ha- nthlei was a cyclopean underwater city, it was beneath Devil's Reef and spiraled up from the ocean floor in many segmented stories. For a great number of years it was the largest city of the Deep Ones. Dagon garnished hold over the city when he took Mother Hydra, an extremely old (and gigantic) Deep One as his consort. Unfortunately for Dagon the Fatebinding rendered him trapped not just physically trapped, but spiritually as well. Consider Y'ha-nthlei to be Fatebound with 10 successes (7 to Strength and 3 cover the city) to Dagon taking on the Role of Jinx, in this case all attempts at Dagon to depart or repair his dilapidated kingdom fall to ruin. The only means to the Y'ha-nthlei are through Innsmouth with the permission of the local population or by finding the remains of Devil's Reef. Roughly 100 yards below the ocean surface the fragments coral can be found. In some place strange lights can be seen coming from 'cracks' where coral meets the ocean floor, these are entrances to Y'ha-nthlei and nearly all have Deep Ones that watch over them.

                        The Nameless City
                        In ancient times, the Nameless City was built and inhabited by an unnamed race of reptilian beings with a body shaped like a cross between a crocodile and a seal. They worshiped the Old Ones and built great subterranean monoliths to their greatness. These creatures moved by crawling; thus, the architecture of the city has very low ceilings and some places are too low for a human being to stand upright. Their city was originally coastal, but when the seas receded it was left in the depths of a desert. This resulted in the decline and eventual ruin of the city.
                        The only clue to its location lies a plaque (the exact location of which is up to the storytellers) made of a strange onyx stone it reads:
                        "That is not dead which can eternal lie, And with strange aeons even death may die."
                        Indeed, the reptilians still maintain some form of life in a vast, luminous paradise in a cavern beneath the Nameless City, preserved and placed along the walls in a subterranean chamber. Also within this chamber are hieroglyphs that tell the story of the city's heyday of prosperity and its fall to the characters. These reptilians may still live on or they might just be a collection of otherworldly dead trapped like the ghosts at Hashima (pg. 107 Demigod).

                        R'leyeh (Sanctum 4)
                        In the summer of 1997 the US National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration’s autonomous hydrophone array in the Equatorial Pacific Ocean recorded a peculiar sound of a nature that suggested a biological origin too large to be produced by any known animal species. The sound was given the name 'bloop'. What they actually heard was the rustling of a dead god and his city R'lyeh.
                        R'lyeh is a sunken city located deep under the Pacific Ocean. When Vhoorl was shattered the largest city of the Godrealm was ripped from the Overworld. Within it lay Cthulhu and it is still considered to be his Sanctum (4 dots). Its architecture is a monstrous thing of non-Euclidean geometry, redolent of spheres, and dimensions bent to the unknowable will of its ruler.
                        Like a corpse it sits on the ocean floor with high slime covered vaults, great tombs which contain the bodies of all manor of lesser immortals, nameans, and giants. The only 'living' thing is Cthulhu who was buried for most of the whole of human history. Many of the newer (C.E.) Pantheons would be a bit of shock to him.
                        Cthulhu himself resides in a single hideous, monolith crowned, citidel his de facto tomb for the last two thousand years. Scion onlookers (who actually find the city) are often awed by the sheer immensity of the city which is covered by frightening gargantuan statues and bas reliefs.
                        A strange stellar calender dictates the rise and fall of the city and on one such peculiar aligning of the stars the entire edifice rises from the bottom of the sea to the surface. Like an island it sits among the mortal world and may be approached by anyone mortal or not. The coordinates are exactly 47º9'S, 126º43'W, roughly located near the point in the ocean farthest from any land.

                        The Godrealm of the Elder Ones

                        Vhoorl
                        In its prime Vhoorl was a chaotic seething realm, greater than the whole of Earth and more diverse. It was something akin to the primordial ooze from which early life sprung and a vast Jupiter sized sea. It was shattered across the Underworld, World, and Overworld and character's willing to put in the difficult hours 'treasure hunting' may be able to find small pockets still in existence. It may not be possible to ever again rebuild this Godrealm, but such an act would be truly epic in scope and journey.
