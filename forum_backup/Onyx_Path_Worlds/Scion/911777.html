    Passion, Language, Harmony! The Missing Purviews!

    06-10-2016, 02:20 PM
    Basically certain Godly powers are missing.

    Passion, yes the three Epic Socials are powerful, but how does a deity or scion cause one mortal to fall passionately in Love (or hate for that matter) with another mortal?

    Language. I get that the higher level powers for this Purview would require altering, creating, or wiping out whole languages. That's what Fate Binding is for, preventing Power abuse.

    Harmony. We've got Chaos, there were more gods of Harmony and Order than Chaos. Besides, it lets in Music and Kingship as godly themes.

    Reactions?

              Originally posted by Astromancer View Post
              Harmony. We've got Chaos, there were more gods of Harmony and Order than Chaos. Besides, it lets in Music and Kingship as godly themes.
              Chaos is supposed to be in opposition to Justice in 1E IIRC. Kingship would undoubtedly fall under it. A Harmony Purview would have problems like Kyman pointed out, it's already a Virtue. The bigger issue I would see is that Harmony, if it were a Purview, would be exceptionally scattered, or be exceptionally rare. A single purview including both Kingship and Music, two huge themes alone, is probably a bit much. Apollo and Marduk are entirely dissimilar entities for example and probably shouldn't share a Purview.

              Furthermore, a whole lot of Music Gods are far from harmonious. Pan, at a thought, is deeply associated with music, and yet totally diverging from any ideas of Harmony. Zeus, though The King without question (as long as you don't ask Hades and Poseidon who will point out they are kings equal to him), has no concerns in the slightest bit about harmony. Zeus just cares about Zeus being king.

              Edit: Random additional note about "but how does a deity or scion cause one mortal to fall passionately in Love (or hate for that matter) with another mortal?" bit. Scion RAW actually addresses this with the Scion of Aphrodite (Donnie Rhodes I think?) has two guns that function like Eros (and Anteros') lead and golden arrows. Instead of making an entire Purview to solve the problem, I've found that following in this idea is easier. (Though, little note. Eros and Anteros' arrows are a terrifying political tool. Like, you can sew utter chaos through Pantheons with things like that.)

                                                      1ed.Left crafting to supernatural races.                                                       And to be fair I'm rather interested in how they are going to go about balancing this purview. If I just take the table on page 151 of Scion Companion and say something along the lines of you can do this stuff but you need to be capable of doing it to put it into an item (e.g. you'd have to be Leg 6 to produce a power that mimicked a 5th level boon) then I can see a lot of people wanting to take this purview and just making themselves awesome weapons/armour/items.
