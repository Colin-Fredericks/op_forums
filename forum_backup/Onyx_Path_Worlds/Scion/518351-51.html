    Originally posted by Neall View Post

    If there's any accusation I hate more than "change for change's sake" I'd really love to hear it, so I can hate it more.

    Scion's going to be nine years old as of April, and - to be totally blunt - it has some deep-seated structural issues. I can't fix the issues in it without ripping the entire engine out, which we were doing anyways for a whole host of other reasons. While rebuilding it, I decided to take Knacks off Attributes and focus them on divine archetypes and the archetypal image, while broadening out the list of Purviews. The way we're doing the divide means that transhuman physical potential isn't tied to any specific archetype or personage, while gods of wisdom and intelligence have a very specific archetype (the Sage). Add to that I also wanted to largely avoid Reed Richards-as-God outside of specific Shards, and the old way of doing Epic Intelligence was gone.

    Arthur, we'll be putting up parts of the system for free in the Alpha. There will be backer drafts when this goes to KS. You don't like it, don't buy it.

                                With your mention of gutting the system and building from the ground up again Neall, I thought of a few more questions prompted from more of the finicky ideas I've toyed with but mostly been limited by 1E's core system. They really are finicky however, most of them are the mechanical / balancing messes that it's totally reasonable to handwave around to make the game fun and balanced. Others of them are honestly a bit stupid, trying to model things in a system which probably should just be ignored for the sanity of everyone involved.

                                It's totally justified to say, "No Watcher, that's insane." to all of these. I'm just wondering if you guys have tried to work with these ideas, or, like myself, looked at them with a great deal of trepidation and looked around for a large rug to hide them under until an epiphany comes. Just idol curiosity if you guys have anything in store in these crazy tricky areas.

                                'Gods' can be wildly different in 'power' across Pantheons, and different Pantheons can have entirely different ideas of what makes a 'God' compared to others. The biggest example that springs to mind is how the Tuatha are very mortal, while the Theoi are supposed to be totally and utterly immortal (With strange exceptions in Aesculapius and Pan) which necessitates why they are imprisoning everything that is Divine rather than just killing things. In displays of power, the Tuatha and the Theoi also exist on wildly different ends of the spectrum, with Greek Deities hurling mountains but not being noted for being particularly strong, where the Tuatha's strongest figures show this by throwing flagstones the length of a hall or or hauling an impressively large club. Will these sorts of disparities be represented mechanically? Or for everyone's sanity will the large mechanical differences be limited to the PsPs?

                                Then we have Fate. In Scion 1E, the Fate represented was very much the Grecian idea of Fate, or the Norse idea of Fate / Wyrd, an unstoppable and insurmountable force even for the Deities that can doom even them (Ragnarok, The Son of Zeus). However, this idea of Fate is inconsistent with other cultures. The Deva noted in internal theology to be 'beyond' their concept of Fate which grips only lesser beings. The Mesopotamians didn't seem to have 'Fate' anything like we see in Scion 1E, with their closest concept being a bit more like, 'The Will of the Gods'. The Orisha who have Fate that one must uphold, bit are not compelled to do so, with the mandate left to an individual to fulfill least bad things happen if they fail or refuse. Then I'm sure there is a Pantheon somewhere that entirely lacks the concept, but it's a bit late here and my memory is not coming particularly quite forthcoming. Are you guys going to have different 'versions' of Fate? I feel like you may have hinted to this with the Norse specifically having Wyrd as a PsP, but I just wanted to ask if it went beyond the doom 'n gloom Aesir.

                                There is also the really tricky Pan-Pantheon Deities like Manannan (Irish) - Manawydan (Welsh), Quetzalcoatl (Aztec) - Kukulkan (Mayan), Kannon (Japanese Buddhism) - Guanyin (Chinese Buddhism). Do you have any idea how they will be handled in Scion 2E? Treat them all as individual Deities or try to work out a way to have Pan-Pantheon figures without being a balance mess running around with multiple PsPs. EDIT: This question has totally already been asked and answered, entirely slipped my mind, sorry!

                                The Deities who annoyingly went and changed over the course of their depiction are also a pain to represent. For example, Aphrodite and Venus are so drastically different in attitude it's shocking. Aphrodite running around being cruel, vapid, and self centered, where as Venus suddenly becomes a bit benevolent with her helping her son Aeneas. Ares who went from being an incontinent, a bit mad, most-despised of Zeus' children, to Mars, a familial and state protector who is painted much more as an upstanding and not incoherently screaming warrior. Or, Set being quickly demonized with the rise of the Cult of Isis/Osiris/Horus. Do you guys have anything to try to explain or way to represent mechanically those strange abnormalities of personality, or just leave that for individual Storytellers to sort out?
