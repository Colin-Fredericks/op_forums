                                        West Africa:
                                        - Akan of the people of Ghana & Ivory Coast
                                        - Dahomey (Fon) of the Denin Nation
                                        - Efik of Nigeria & Cameroon
                                        - Odinani of the Igbo people (Nigeria & Cameroon
                                        - Serer (Senegal and Gambia)
                                        - Yoruba (Nigeria and Benin)

                                        And the above is just the prominent West African Religions...there more in West Africa, and many, many more throughout Africa Herself. It would be easy to do...the hard part would be which Pantheons to choose. I for one would like to see a respectfully done series of Pantheons based off of Real World ones from Africa. Not cobbled together ones from various mashups. Africa has so much to offer, from Her People to Her Culture.
