                            Originally posted by Fuzzykuma View Post
                            Why move God to a primordial? Would you not want to balance it out with demons and a devil figure as part of the pantheon? Why not use angels vs saints and have saint just be moretal people invested with legend by the new cult rules? What would you do with God as a persona?
                            God as he is understood by modern day Christians is more of an all encompassing spiritual presence that embodies certain purviews, the angels likewise are described as lacking free will and are also utterly consumed with the roles assigned to them by God. Neall has stated that in Second Edition, Titans are not evil, but rather similar to old gods who at times take their purviews too far. Considering the children of humanity and angels are the Nephelim, which are described as giants and are often monstrous (to say nothing of their undead corpses) it paints a very strong picture of the angels being Titanic in nature. Because God is a described as an all encompassing presence that embodies Order and Love, that would make 'Him' a Primordial in the context of the Scion Cosmology we've been made aware of so far.

                            Jesus and the Saints are scions that became gods, because they are worshiped in the same manner as gods (Brigid for example, in the Tuatha write up? In the actual mythology she didn't do much of anything. Everything attributed to her is essentially after she became a saint of Celtic Christianity, this is an example of how interchangable gods and saints are). This is also to account for Jesus being a much more 'human' and relatable figure than his father is. Picture ancient Rome, a time when the Greek/Roman gods were in full representation. Apollo has a son in a Jewish community. Scion of Apollo grows up, is disgusted with his lineage, and reflects that in the people of his birth there exists prophecy of a Messiah, he hijacks the Legend for his own and rises to prominence, making use of his father's Health purview and Leadership calling. Boom, you've got yourself a new pantheon there.

                            If you go back far enough to the point where God is 'not' a Primordial, that'd be something closer to the Canaanite pantheon, which is going to get an official release sometime later.

                            Edit: It bears repeating that titans and primordials are not necessarily evil. If you want to include demons and a devil figure, you're welcome to do so - although that entire dynamic was just stolen from Zoroastrianism, I'm really disenchanted with Christianity and the way it's grown from its roots in general. You could do it, though. Have a Primordial of Order raging against a Primordial of Chaos.

                                    Nuts and bolts are a little tough at this point since we don't have the books yet. I have an Angelic pantheon marked out with Jahovah as the head of the pantheon. I've picked out several archangels based on Catholic and Orthadox Christian traditions and assigned some purviews and callings to them. These are, of course, tentative because we don't have many details on what those purviews and callings entail. I've named them "The Adoni" because Abraham repeatedly called Jahovah and his angels that attended him "Lords" and I think their psp is going to be something that's based on psalms. I'm planning on adding a few archangels recognized by Muslim traditions as well and I don't talk about Jesus at all. I think I'm going to treat him as an Incarnation of Jahovah if it comes up.

                                    I am using Samael and Lucifer as different characters. I think I'm going to with the idea that the original Samael died and two of his scions competed for the mantel. The winner took his father's mantle and the looser decided to lead a rebellion against heaven. I'm also going to have a titan called Beelzebub and another called Azrael.

                                    The driving force between making my angelic pantheon is that I have a player who wants to play someone that's part of the Catholic Inquisition. So basically what I'm going to do is have the Inquisition have been reformed in the 1800's to be a sort of black opps group that supplies support to Scions of the Adoni in their efforts against threats to humanity. They would essentially provide the same function and benefits for scions of the Adoni that a parent's cult would provide for scions of other pantheons.

                                    EDIT: I guess I should say plainly that the point of my efforts is create a pantheon that will let my player play the protagonist he wants to play and allow him to have fun with it. I find simison's ideas on making Johovah a primordial interesting but unless the rules in Hero give us a way to play titan spawn as PCs, then I'm going to error on the side of creating a pantheon that at least looks like other pantheons, even if the nomenclature is a bit different in that most of the "gods" are called "archangels".
