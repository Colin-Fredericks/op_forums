    Skype Game Seeking Players

    01-16-2014, 12:07 AM
    EDIT: UPDATED APRIL 13!

    Sup? I'm running a Skype game on Friday mornings, from approx. 11 AM (EST) to 3 PM (also EST). So, you ask, what is the game about? Here's a blurb.

    For hundreds of years, magic has been leeching out of the mundane World. Every passing decade contained less wonder, less mystery and less legend. Monsters and miracles dwindled, making life safer and more stable. Times became less interesting. There were no dragons beyond the edges of the map, and no faeries lurking in the forests. Even the depths of the sea were empty of serpents. Magic was needed for a more important purpose: binding the Titans. Now they’re free, their prison broken and magic is bleeding back into the world.

    In the Overworld, a war rages between the elemental Titans and the divine Pantheons. Ineffable forces have been leashed by both sides, setting whole realms aflame. No effort, and only minutes of time, can be spared for the mortal World. To preserve Earth and humanity against a resurgence of unfamiliar magic and the slowly reawakening minions of the Titans, the Gods have awakened the sleeping blood in a handful of Scions to be their agents on the mortal plane.

    So far, the touch of magic on the World is light, but things are not what they seem. That huge man with the fiery temper might just be a Giant in disguise, and maybe that ‘mutated dog’ your neighbor keeps trying to get rid of is actually a Chupacabra. The world is a strange place, and you’re part of the strangeness. Less than a month ago you learned that you aren’t what you seem. One of your parents is a God, making you a Scion. It’s your job to shepherd the World into a new era. Starting with Seattle. Better hurry, because things are getting hairy fast.

    The setting is low-powered, and I'm running a lighter version of Scion's system. Epic Attributes are stripped down to 1 success per dot, Knacks after your first are no longer free, everything is considered Associated and some other changes. The game will be running at least up to Demigod, staying fairly low-tone and low-power. At Demigod, things will be re-evaluated and any changes made. You can view the house rules document here.

    If you're interested, reply here, IM me or hit me up on Skype (where we'll be playing via voice for sessions, text for non-session scenes). SomnorianBadgerMonster.


    We've played 11 sessions, but some players have had to drop out due to scheduling conflicts. Currently I have 4, but I'd like to get up to 5, maybe 6 PCs. Currently, I have...
    • Teague, Scion of Bridget: A native Seattle indie musician that's demonstrated uncommon bravery, becoming the Band's reluctant leader
    • Diesel, Scion of Heimdall: A brilliant biologist that wants to use science to improve humanity
    • Noah, Scion of Odin: A handsome and valiant frat-boy that wants to impress his father, save the world and defend his friends
    • Harry, Scion of Kalfu: A cowardly young man, terrified of his father and desperate to find a 'normal' life despite the chaos around him
    If you want to check out their adventures so far, click here.
