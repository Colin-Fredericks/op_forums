    Originally posted by Wickedgrail View Post
    My players and I wanted to know how do you deal with multiples actions, especially when you want to combine boons and attacks? One of my players would like to pull out a blazing bow from the fire and shoot an arrow right away.
    For what I know, Blazing weapon takes 5 ticks and 4 more ticks to attack (and maybe 5 more ticks to create flames as big as a campfire). Would you make that as multiple actions? If not, what about if we make the Boon action as a reflexive one and shooting an arrow, counting as one action?
    You can absolutely draw a blazing bow from a campfire and launch a burning arrow right away!

    One of the coolest changes we made from RAW is "Multiple Actions: Two nearly simultaneous actions that are resolved with separate dice rolls. Each of these dice rolls subtract two successes from the result to represent the increased difficulty. The first action always resolves before the second action, so you can jump and attack someone at the end of your leap, or transform your body into water then hide beneath a lake." So the badass Fire Scion in your group can activate Blazing Weapon as their first action (Miscellaneous Action - 5 Ticks) and then fire an arrow as their second action. You use the slower of the two speeds just like RAW, so combining Blazing Weapon with a Long Bow would be a total of 6 Ticks, and a Short Bow would be 5 Ticks. Since Blazing Weapon is diceless, you only need to roll the attack with the bow at -2 Successes.

    Now if there was literally no fire around to pull the bow and ammunition from? Then you might need a Miscellaneous Action to get a good fire started as your first action, then pull the blazing weapon out of the the fire as your second action. In that case you would definitely need to wait for your next turn to actually attack.

                    We've been absolutely bogged down with life, but we are still marching on. We've gotten a lot of testing into the Heroic Boons of all of the Purviews which are all outlined and designed, but not balanced or proofread. This is the same situation for most of the Pantheon Specific Purviews.

                    But, for more of a design commentary on War, we wanted to give both players who wanted to play General, and Warrior to be equally represented. One of the biggest challenges is doing this is to avoid stepping on the toes of Epic Socials and Epic Mentals (In the case of the Generals), and Epic Physicals (In the case of the Warriors). The way we tried to work around these problems was to make Boons that would work in tandem with the Epic Attributes, or give characters access to tools that force people to play along with mythic ideas. Lone Hero (War Seven) is a strong example of this, the Boon gives a War character the capacity to involve a very thematic concept, a champion's duel, or a trial by combat, and forces others to actually partake in this concept.

                    I do expect some people will wonder where Riastrad and the original Fury of War have gone. We spent some time trying to figure out what we could do about them, as we felt that they were exceptionally culturally specific to the Tuatha and the Aesir, and some different ideas were batted around. One was make a Rank 4 Boon that each Pantheon had a different version of, with a Theoi player being able to declare an Aristeia, one of the Aesir being able to Berserk, the Tuatha entering the Warp Spasm, but it would be a terrible time balancing these all against each other (and exceptionally problematic if a Pantheon doesn't have a concept to use).

                    So, we decided that instead, powers such as Aristeias, the Riastrad, Berserking, and other such concepts would better be represented as Guide Powers. For example, Athena would be a Guide for Diomedes (Ignoring the fact that Diomedes is mortal and shouldn't have Birthrights ect), Odin could give the power to Berserk, and... the Riastrad is sort of difficult as CuChulain managed to do it even as a child, but many of the martial Tuatha could give it.

                    We have been using so often that I am more familiar with it than RAW or GBN's War at this point, so I am absolutely sure I have missed some important points to touch on due to this familiarity. If anyone has any questions, ask away.

                                        The Tuatha


                                        The Tuatha de Danann are the first Pantheon we have totally revised from the ground up, setting the basis for our further ones. The Gods of the Pantheon have been completely reassessed, including the demotion of Dian Cecht to being a Lesser God, and all Associated Powers and Skills have been reviewed. If you want to know just why Aengus has Melee, now all you have to do is hover over the association and a mouseover tooltip will explain.

                                        Eight Lesser Gods have been added, and given their own page. Including the serial kidnappers of Fand, her daughters, Niamh, Anne, and Clodna. The recently demoted Dian Cecht, and both his children, Airmed and Miach. Also Nechtan, honestly just because I really like his magical well.

                                        The Cosmology of the Pantheon has been added, including an underground Overworld and an aboveground Underworld. In addition, the Pantheon has been given Six Titans to stand around and lower the property value of Ireland.

                                        Lastly, the entry section of the Tuatha's section of Companion has also been rewritten under the page of History and Culture which includes an addition quickly detailing several little cultural tidbits which will likely come up in game, ranging from the role of women in the society, the weirdness of marriage, homosexuality, and minor other notes. Oh, it also talks about how to deal with the fact the Major Gods are almost entirely dead!

                                            Hello folks,

                                            The Theoi are now complete and up on our site. Just like the Tuatha up there, we have revised them from the ground up adding, and removing Major Gods, including the addition of both Pan and Demeter to the list of Legend 12 Gods. All Associated Powers and Skills have been checked out and evaluated, and just as with the Tuatha, if you wonder why we gave anyone what we did, just hover over the Associated Ability or Power and have a little explanation pop up.

                                            Six Lesser Gods have been added, grabbing the most relevant or interesting from the absolute horde the Theoi have. We really should have more, so, if anyone has a favorite Lesser God they would like to see up there, just let us know and we'll write them up.

                                            A cosmology has been added, but it's Hades and Olympus, it's not exactly rocket science. But, far more than the Tuatha have been given, the Theoi now have a Titan page detailing eighteen different Titans who are both allies and enemies of the Pantheon. Or, sometimes neither. Again, like the Lesser Gods, there is an absolute wealth for Theoi Titans, we just added those we felt were most important or interesting.

                                            Under History and Culture, we present the tale of the creation of the world up to the rule of Zeus, also giving a brief mention to the tricky nature of the Greek Gods Roman sides, and discuss the opposite problem the Tuatha had, that the Theoi are truly immortal, except for three exceptions.

                                            For what's next, we actually have to go back on our word that Asia is out of the picture for the moment. I am currently working on the Kami (almost said Amatsukami, but as I have learned that's not actually the best name for them) with a huge amount of help from someone here on the forums, I am unsure if they want to be credited yet and if so how, so I'll not say anything at the moment. They do require more research than the Tuatha and the Theoi, mostly because a lot of that research is researching where to find sources to research, but hopefully will be out and kicking in the near future.
