    System/Mechanics questions.

    One thing I've found in games that are meant to be high action is that players need to be both confident that what they do will pan out - so fixed ways of spending resources for specific effects or guaranteed success in certain things like 'can always land on their feet' or 'will always have a weapon to hand' so they can make sure they can do the cool thing (i.e.: not playing mother may I with th eGM) and also that they need to keep engaged; meaning time between declaring what you do and the resolution of that thing not having lots of steps with time consuming stuff going on and also fairly rapid turns so that they don't do their thing and then sit down waiting for 45 minutes for their next chance to do something cool.

    So, on to the question.

    What things can a player who is waiting for their next turn do to influence the game, either helping their allies or hindering enemies? E.g.: I spend a [name] point to [hurl a chair at the charging axeman], giving them a +1 difficulty modifier on their attack against my friend!

                                                        Originally posted by Scutarii View Post

                                                        Could you explain what features you think are being thrown away as you see them?

                                                        I'm wondering what Neall thinks he is saying compared to what you (or anyone else for that matter) is hearing.

                                                        Thanks!
                                                        Well, as noted they totally gutted the Epic System to put in 'tier changes' that are supposed to put differences between a normal mortal and gods, etc. Weirdly, if you just left Epics but only let them go up to 5, you get an almost identical spread (0-12 vs. 0-11 autos). Oh, and the Tiers only apply "sometimes". So you can have a skilled mortal beat a god at their best skill. And the tiers apply (sometimes) to all attributes, even something you aren't necessarily known for but have decent stats.

                                                        Then they decided that Appearance is no longer an actual attribute, pushing New WoD's trinity type of balanced attributes (that you attack and defend with) for 'social combat'. While socials needed something of a fix, I'm not for totally re-doing attributes as the actual attribute of appearance was one of the most defining traits for a couple of my player's characters.

                                                        Then in this latest explanation of how Relics work, Donar's gun 'calls storms', but that is all it can do. If he wanted a gentle rain or to shape clouds, he can't. Compared to the different types of actions you could take with the Sky purview previously from his relic, that's very much a limiting change. (Purview 'powers' could be totally unrelated from your Relics, but that isn't what I got from that discussion.)

                                                        Then there is the removing the Titan War shades from the godly political situation because of how things are being re-organized. That kind of reminds me of removing the Camarilla and Sabbat from World of Darkness. Yeah, there's still political things that can be done, but there is no longer that strong dynamic. (This is one of the major points that people I know did not like in New WoD.)

                                                        So there'll be d10, attributes and stats, powers and demigods. But it almost an entirely different game because almost everything is changed to some degree.
