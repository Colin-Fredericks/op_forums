    Originally posted by Shadowflame View Post
    From looking at the real world I would think it would depend not just on the culture, but the deity him/herself.
    I think a lot of people like to delude themselves in thinking that there is an inherent superiority in their culture...or in the other direction, they think that they are more accepting. That's always something that can be played out. Especially if there's a different religion for the scion to begin with. I think most deities with a lot of chaos in them would be the ones that snatch such scions up. For example, I think the only deity in the Norse pantheon that would accept a "gypsy" would be Loki.
    Actually, the Ragnarok book specifically states that the Aesir are very accepting of miscegenation. They already are the result of intermarriage between two separate pantheons and the giants, who are technically Titanspawn. They really don't have a problem with taking lovers from other ethnicities or even other species.

    Thor sired two sons on a giantess. Freyr is MARRIED to one. So's Uller. If the Aesir don't mind three of their most potent members being with members of a race who are technically their enemies, then having sex with a human of African or Asian extraction is nothing to them.

    Also, as has already been pointed out, Demigod introduces a Scion of Tyr whose mother was black. Nothing unusual about him. He just looks like a typical example of a mating between a black woman and a white man. Now, we didn't get a lot of information about him or his relationship with the other Aesir, but there's nothing to suggest that he's treated any differently.

    With their Loyalty virtue, the Aesir care a lot more about whether or not you're doing your part to help the family than they do about what your parentage is. If you're pulling your weight, that's all that matters.

        The Norse weren't just accepting of it, they encouraged it, for the boys at least. Guys were encouraged to marry women outside the clan, so to speak, to bring new blood in. Women, on the other hand, were discouraged from doing so because the clan didn't want to lose them to another. In fact, it's very interesting to note that Loki's case is the only instance we really know of where a female god has a kid whose father is a frost giant. Meaning that Loki, so unlike the rest of the Aesir anyway, had something of a stigma attached to him from birth.

            I think it is worth noting that the ethnicities we associate with the specific religions the Pantheons are based on are largely historical over-simplifications. The wide-spread culture of maintaining ethnic "purity" by selective breeding is far more modern than most people consider. The Pantheons essentially predate it. Some of them would have members that care about breeding for personal reasons, and some of them are going to be influenced more by the relative isolation of the religions that spawned them, but in the end it is doubtful that "what you look like" is important to most of them beyond their own personal standards of what looks worth making a divine baby with.

                                Heroes can use any All Purpose Purview or Special Purview they desire, provided they have have a Relic that channels that purview. Naturally, you also have to buy the Boon you desire to activate within any given purview.

                                Of course, you have to explain how you got a Relic that channels a purview your parent does not normally have access to. The parent could channel that purview themselves (just not at Avatar level), or call in a favor from a friend, or have a bunch of trophies lying around, or some other god or legendary creature could have given it to your Hero for their own countless inscrutable reasons. It can be as big a plot hook, or as small a plot hook as you want to make it.

                                    Scions don't need to share the Purview with their Divine Parent, all they need is a Relic to access the Purview.                                             No. You can buy whatever Boons you have the Legend to justify, but you won't be able to use that Boon without an appropriate Relic.

                                            Originally posted by Woody Spider Deacon View Post

So, Scion 2 now has access to Sky and to the "Wind's Freedom" Boon? Which of the 2 Scions (or both or neither) can spend XP to purchase the Sky Boon "Sky's Grace" while the Relic is still stolen?
                                            This.. is a really weird part of the system. Basically, Scion 1 spent the XP and still has Wind's Freedom on his character sheet. He just can't use it without a Relic. Maybe he has two Relics that channel Sky, or steals a Sky Relic from someone else. He can still use Wind's Freedom just fine. But, for as long as Scion 2 has the stolen Relic, they can also use Wind's Freedom, along with any other Sky Boons known to Scion 1. Basically he's "borrowing" Scion 1's Boons for as long as he has that stolen Relic.

                                            As I said, that's a really weird part of the system and it strongly encourages "cheating" by passing around your Relics to your friends so they can use your Boons and you can use theirs.

                                            Nope. You can be a starting Scion from the Aesir, but say that one of your Relics was given to you by Ares because you did him a favor one time. There's no penalty or anything. Your Relics can come from anywhere at all. Your starting Relics, and Demigod or God Relics, are assumed to be Bound to you, no matter who actually gave them to you. So if Ares gives our example Norse Scion a sword that channels War, it's exactly the same as if Odin had given it to the Scion.
