    I'm super new tabletop rpg's since I only started playing a yr and a half ago, but Scion is for sure the game I am enjoying the most so far and my character is from the Yankee Pantheon. I agree that they are pretty awesome and a great way to introduce the idea of modern day Pantheons. I grew up listening to those characters stories and just always loved them so I jumped at the opportunity to create a Scion based on one of them. Of course, while I like all the characters in the Pantheon, I decided to try my hand at creating a new god for them based on Molly Pitcher and it's worked out extremely well so far.
