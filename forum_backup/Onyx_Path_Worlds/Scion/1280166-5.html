                                                            I admit I may have gotten a bit heated earlier in the thread, sleeping on it a bit may have made me realize something.

                                                            I really don't find the thematic horror of the Cthulhu Mythos that scary. Like, to me, I don't find Cthulhu scary in any sense beyond 'Big monster that may want to eat me', and in that sense it's no scarier to me than say, Godzilla.

                                                            If the universe is big and we're that insignificant and in the cosmic scale nothing we do matters... Well, I don't find that scary. Or depressing, really. Because a short way for me to answer the question of "What's the point if eventually things give way to entropy, life is an accident, and most of us will die in a cosmic instant" is "... I like pizza"

                                                            Yeah, sounds silly, but it's a short way to describe my feelings. Pizza's great. So's cake, spaghetti, and a good hearty chicken soup.

                                                            Does any of this matter on a cosmic scale? Nope, not in the least. But I won't live on a cosmic timeline. I don't see the point in worrying about that. I don't even find any kind of dread in being more than an okay everyman in our world in my lifetime. I just try to do right, maybe make some connections so that people will miss me when I die, do my best to try and make this world a bit better than when I came into it.

                                                            My horror is much more personal. Show me a grave with my name on it, obviously untended, and nobody ever stops by. A funeral that nobody went to because I managed to drive off everybody I knew in life. That, to me, is a million times more horrifying than the implication that I'm not the most important thing in the universe. That's so much more emotionally devastating than telling me that life is nothing more than an accident with no greater plan.

                                                            Ugh, I may have had a point somewhere. Mostly I'll say to any fans of the Cthulhu Mythos I may have insulted... I'm sorry, I didn't mean to insult you or try and say you're a bad person if you like it.

                                                            I still don't think it fits in with mainline Scion and think that it's best done as a separate continuity.
