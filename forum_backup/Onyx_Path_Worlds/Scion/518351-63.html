                                        04-29-2016, 03:41 PM
                                        Originally posted by Yig1015w View Post
                                        Noticed that the fire attack used athletics and that firearms existed as a skill. Does this mean that Athletics will include all ranged attacks not using a mechanical firearmlike type of device? Such as bows, thrown, and spells. Mainly interested in knowing about bows. I assume the Athletics and Firearm's separation for ranged accuracy is due to the fact that some settings will not have technology in general such as firearms and crossbows. Correct?
                                        At the moment, yes.

                                        Second, I loved to see that we get a fire attack ability through purviews right at character creation. Can we expect that a frost/ice or sky purview would also give the option to start with a ranged ice/lightning attack of sorts as well?
                                        Sky, yes. For ice, you can use a marvel to attack as a definitive option.

                                        Finally, are you limited to a single purview at character creation or is that just how the example character was? Could someone begin with access to Fire, Frost, and Sky for instance?
                                        Purviews are generally limited by your Calling levels if you're directly mainlining them or by your Birthright relics; however, Boons are strictly limited, and you have a very small amount of Legend to commit to various powers. If you try to marvel your way through a half-dozen Purviews before you reach Godhood you'll burn out incredibly quickly.

                                        Neall Raemonn Price
                                        Beleaguered Scion Developer
