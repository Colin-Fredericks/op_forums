    Boon issue

    03-30-2017, 05:20 AM
    I know this might not be necessary anymore since we are going for the 2nd edition but still, I'm having trouble with demigod boons from the 1st edition and would like some advice to right them:

    Water boon 5: allows you to extract water from a body you need to touch first. Inflicts [successes] lethal damages. The roll is strength + medicine.
    No Scion with the same Legend can survive this roll. Let me explain: the maximum this boon can inflict is 40L with a Scion Legend 7 (strength 7 + medicine 5 = 12 dice. The maximum will be 24 successes + epic strength 6 = 16 + 24 = 40)
    Against a Scion Legend 7 with Stamina 7, Lethal Soak will be = 7/2 + Epic stamina 6 = 4 + 16 = 20.

    How to adjust that? :/

    Another issue is Sky boon 6. 15 dice to roll is... not much. even with a maximum of 30 successes, against a Scion Legend 7, with Dexterity 7, Athletics 5 = 19/2 + Epic Dex 6 = 9 + 16. DV = 25.

    Only 4L inflicted if the target has only one dot in Stamina.


    I could not come up with any alternative.

    Any idea?
