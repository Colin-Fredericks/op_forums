                                                    I had indeed considered that possibility when composing my suggestion. To the extent that El is typical of Titanic Avatars, he does indeed bear some resemblance to the Gnostic Demiurge; and Yeshua as his estranged son mimics the perceived divide between an apparently cruel Old Testament God vs. a more benevolent New Testament God.

                                                    Conversely, the Elohims’ Titan is one of the few Titans described that isn’t characterized as being outright evil. So if you want to play El in a more positive light, it’s easily enough done. And I intentionally avoided presenting the El/Yeshua relationship as being hostile — whereas the El/Elohim relationship does tend to be hostile. And since El and Yeshua are on good terms despite their differences, what hostility the Elohim have toward El carries over to Yeshua.

                                                    (The basis for my opinion is this: Scion is a game, not reality; and regardless of how much it strives for authenticity in its research, it nevertheless is about myths. Thus, it says nothing one way or the other about the truth of my religious beliefs.)
