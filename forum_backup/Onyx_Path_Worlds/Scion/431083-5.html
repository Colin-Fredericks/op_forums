        108) The Legend of Zeus claims that his power over lightning was a gift from the Cyclopes after the events of the first titan war. It has served Zeus well for many ages, but now they've pulled rank. The Cyclopes have gone on strike, refusing to make more lightning bolts until they are presented with better living conditions, privileges and social rights. If their demands are met, the Cyclopes could prove as valuable an ally to the Dodekatheon as the Dwarves are to the Aesir. But Zeus, being Zeus, does not appreciate being blackmailed. He demands production of his lightning bolts be resumed and is in no mood to compromise. But with limited stores of ammunition, he is forced to temper his rage for the time being.

        The hero's band has been assigned to resolve the issue of dealing with the Cyclopes. But which side will they choose in this conflict? Will they side with Zeus and earn the favor of the Gods, or will they side with the Cyclopes in hopes of forging an alliance with the giant craftsmen?

                    Originally posted by atamajakki View Post
                    So I'm completely new to Scion, but would "the Aesir get tired of neo-Nazis appropriating their imagery and sullying their oublic image, so they task some mortals to kick their butts publicly" be an acceptable story seed?
                    Ignoring the WWII section of Companion, that sounds totally reasonable. The Aesir (+3 captive Vanir in Asgard) tend to intervene personally, but if there is a reason they can't show up themselves (such as the Core explanation of 'Fate problems') I could totally see intermediaries. I don't know if the entire Pantheon would get involved (they might not all notice, or 'get' it unless they have been paying attention to world events in the 21st century) but some figures, totally.

                    Odin, Heimdall, and Frigg (has access to Odin's throne and tends to know what he knows despite his desires) probably have noticed it, even in a setting where the Pantheons are highly withdrawn. Odin is a bit... of an odd duck, but if it bothered him (if it was doing odd things to the Pantheon's Fates, or if it was making him personally look bad), I could totally see him either showing up Avatared down as a Wanderer and running circles around a group very publicly somehow. He might use proxies for it, especially if there is a reason in the setting that the Divine can't fiddle with the world. Heimdall and Frigg both I can see getting mortals, Legendary Mortals, or Scions to intervene in some way.

                    Though both Tyr and Thor probably wouldn't notice, not really being renowned for watching Midgard, but if they did they both would be very interesting. I can see Tyr using Scions or mortals to do something about it. Thor, well, Thor would probably show up himself if it offended him enough and start a fight. I don't know much about Neo-Nazi ideology, but if the anti-Slav aspects of the Nazis is still present, you could even do something very neat with Thor in that regard. We have a Russian myth eluding to Thor helping an invading force of Norse attack the native Slavs of a region (the Bogovi [Slavic Pantheon] have a non-interventionist policy and had to restrain their own Storm God, Perun, from going down and fighting back) so he actually has rare cross-cultural experience and might take the anti-Slav narratives badly after having first hand experience with them. Especially if at some point he and Perun made up.

                    Depending on how you are representing Loki, he could either not really care (the dick borderline evil Loki he is widely represented as), or be very offended (if he is still the prankster + loyal problem solver and causer of the Pantheon) that his adopted family is being insulted. If you're using this second Loki, I could totally see him getting Scions or Mortals together to deal with a predominant group of Neo-Nazis. While he might not opt for beating them up, he might set his helpers out to destroy the organization through divine mockery and cunning. Potentially, the more long-term solution of all of the options.

                                    Demigod to Apotheosis Hook


                                    112)                                     You've heard of a legendary blacksmith who creates weapons for battle hardened Scions that greatly improve their abilities to fight off the Titan forces. The catch is that in addition to the monetary payment, the customers are Fate Bound to their weapon upon receiving it. Well, you found the smith and commissioned from him a mighty blade, custom designed for your specifications. You paid for the services and received the weapon as was expected. But now you found out something quite disturbing about the smith's previous customers. Shortly after receiving their weapons, they began to follow the path of violence and destruction. The smith isn't just any craftsman, he is a demon, and those who buy from him become commanding officers in hell's legion!

                                    You can already feel the evil radiating from your weapon, it pulls at you with urges best not spoken of out loud. Can you find a way to free yourself of the Fate you paid for, or will you give into temptation and become a demon general?

                                        113) Sometimes the Gods choose mortal men and women to act as their prophet in the World. Often at times, these prophets are executed for heresy against some other faith, or laughed off as being crazy and false heralds. In this particular case, you really, really wish the latter had been true. Lovecraft was not merely a science fiction writer, he was a living prophet, recording the legends of the incomprehensible pantheon that features such entities as Cthulhu, Azothoth, Hastur and Shub-Niggurath. Mortals even began to erect cults dedicated to the beings depicted in his work. Now, after countless eons, the old ones have finally noticed our little mud ball is getting quite a bit of attention from other deities, and they want to find out why.

                                        The Old Ones have awoken, and now the Scions have bigger problems than Titans to worry about!

                                                115) "I got an idea..." the phrase usually precludes things that are either very good or very bad. This time results are...mixed. let's not dwell on the unusually grueling battle between the guardian deities of the solar barque for now, it's not relevant just how taxing it was or how close they came to losing Ra's light. And we don't have to think about which of them, in a drunken rage, thought up the idea of taking the fight to Apep/Apophis directly and trying for a more final solution, nor should we dwell on who it was that actually devised an interesting new solution to the problem.
                                                No, what has to be addressed is the result of the "brainstorming session which led to the current predicament of Apep's dark and destructive power being sealed into a human vessel. There's no way the solution is permanent, the mortal can;t just live continually right? But even though the unknowing candidate was chosen specifically for being an innocent soul with a kind nature, the fundamental aspect of the power sealed within him continually manifests as great misfortune all around him. If the candidate is killed, it will free all of Apep's power into the world directly, flooding everything with darkness and death, and transforming the normal waking world into something like unto a permanent Silent Hill. If the candidate is hurt, the power automatically retaliates to incredibly destructive ends. The seal that holds this ploy together won;t dissolve till the end of the candidate's life, which we aren't even sure is finite any longer, and we're concerned that any attempt to live out a normal life (by say siring children) may multiply the problem in horrible and unexpected ways.

                                                It goes without saying that this situation needs to be kept under wraps, but there needs to be a solution. Fast!.

                                                    116) What happens when you mix lycanthropy with the T-Virus? Well, at least one Scion of Loki plans to find out. They're attempting to bio-engineer an airborne variation of the Therianthrope infection, transforming the entire world's population into man-beasts as part of an agreement with Terra. You don't know where their primary lab is located yet, but you do know they're not working on this project alone. They have their own band who supports them, which means this won't be as easy as just kicking down the door and torching the specimens!



                                                    117) A cult of Terra has been abducting Scions early in their Legends and forcing them to participate in gladiator matches for the entertainment of blood thirsty Titan Spawn. Sometimes the matches are singular duels, and other times they're a team effort. Regardless of how the matches are set up, the Scions are promised freedom if they can achieve 100 consecutive victories. Unfortunately, nobody has been able to defeat the arena's grand champion so far, and now you're one of the captives!

                                                    The rules of the arena are as follows

                                                    1 - Matches are set up according to estimated skill of the combatants involved.

                                                    2 - Fights are to the death.

                                                    3 - Disqualified combatants are fed to Nemean Lions.

                                                    4 - Members of the same team can not be pitted against each other.

                                                    5 - Slaves must achieve 100 consecutive victories to earn their freedom.

                                                        118) It's all over the news: Suicide bombings on American soil! So far, all of the targets have been Moonbeans Coffee Shops, but no one knows why. No organization has taken credit for the bombings. Weirdest of all, there are some rumors that it's not intentional bombings at all, that somehow the coffee itself is causing people to spontaneously and destructively combust. Meanwhile, word comes down from "on high" that the powerful, mad ghost of Kaldi the Shepherd (the legendary inventor of coffee) has escaped from the control of the Pesedjet and stolen away a powerful Relic. Could the two be related? (Hint: You bet they could!)

                                                        119) An oil derrick in the Gulf of Mexico was abandoned after their drills found not the rich oil deposit they expected, but some kind of massive undersea cave. Now the sea underneath the derrick is a perpetual whirlpool, drawing things down and never letting them up again. In reality, the greedy drillers have broken a small hole into the Drowned Road, specifically the depths where Cipactli swims. If only the hole were bigger (much, much bigger) the Titan could break through and swim the Earth's waters again.

                                                        To make matters worse, a group of American spring-breakers have discovered the whirlpool, and the strange creatures that swim out of it. They've come to the conclusion that the Lovecraftian being Dagon resides at the bottom, and that making sacrifices to the Deep Ones will bring them riches and power. Kids these days.
