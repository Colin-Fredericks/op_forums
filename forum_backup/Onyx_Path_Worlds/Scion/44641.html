    What happens if a Virgin Goddess, well.....gives it up?

    01-07-2014, 11:49 PM
    I started a thread similar to this one back on the old forum. But I seem to recall that at that time, we were pretty much at our nadir in terms of people stopping by, so it didn't become much of a discussion. Thought I'd try again here.

    For the record? I'm not trying to be crude or disrespectful here. I would like this to be a serious discussion. Jokes are okay, but I think this is a fairly important topic that could be significant somewhere down the road.

    The topic also came back to my mind tonight when I conversed with my player who is playing a Scion of Ishtar. We were working on his Health relic: a set of Babylonian beard decorations, which also gives him a once-a-day Super Seduction power. During our conversation, my friend jokingly said "Yeah, with that thing, I should be able to nail Athena!"

    Typical caveman laughter ensued.

    But after the laughter was over, I began to contemplate the question once again. Now my player may be joking about trying to seduce Athena in our game, but what if he isn't? What if he really does try? More importantly? What happens if he SUCEEDS?

    Really, let's expand the discussion here. What do you think happens in ANY scenario in which a virgin goddess breaks her chastity? Does Fate punish her? Does Fate punish the man with whom she does the deed? Both of them? Or is it just a change of status for the goddess in question? Nothing really comes from Fate. The goddess simply isn't a virgin anymore.

    Obviously, being a virgin goddess is tied quite heavily into said goddess's Legend. It seems likely that there may be SOME ramifications from Fate for breaking with a Fatebound Role. Or is it such a big deal? In the case of both Artemis and Athena, their oaths of eternal virginity were their own choice. Is it possible that Fate might just decide that the oath was theirs to make and theirs to break? If Athena, one day, legitimately falls in love with a man and wants to be with him physically, can she? Or would Fate stop her? If she does it, what happens?

    For that matter, I know there are a few virgin goddesses in other mythologies, but I'm not too familiar with them. Are they ALL like Artemis and Athena, or are there some variations on the theme?

    For that matter, does choice factor in to this thing? In the above example, my friend mentioned the idea of using a Super Seduction power that would basically FORCE Athena to go to bed with him. These days, we call that rape. For that matter, rape is a real possibility in the Scion setting. I mean there's a war going on. What might happen if Athena were to be defeated in battle by some Titanspawn and he decided to take full advantage of his victory?

    There are other ways in which a virgin goddess could be forced into surrendering her chastity. I recall at least one Greek myth where a couple giants were threatening Olympus and demanded that the Gods hand over Hera and Artemis to them. The Gods pretended to comply, but Artemis tricked them into killing each other. Well, what were to happen if a similar situation presented itself and virgin goddess had no choice but to surrender? It doesn't even need to be a force thing. What if some Trickster god managed to trap a virgin goddess into some kind of wager with her virginity as the prize? If he wins? What then?

    So that's the question. What might happen if a virgin goddess willingly gives up her chastity? What if it's taken from her by force or trickery? What kind of backlash might come from Fate? If any? What other concerns might there be? Obviously, the social and/or political ramifications are easy enough to guess. Zeus, would obviously be pissed at ANYONE who deflowered his favorite daughter. What other ramifications might there be?

              Having such a massive change in your Legend may well actually result in a drop to your Legend until/unless you can "fix" things or resolve the crisis. If Athena lost her virginity, she'd lose a big chunk of what makes her Athena. It would be very distressing not just to her, psychologically, but also to Fate itself. So, yeah, hellacious results all around.

              Aside from possibly losing Legend (permanent Legend!) for having such a central part of your character taken away, Fatebindings may also get involved. How exactly depends on how you run Fatebonding. The RAW system doesn't have much in the way of teeth, so it'd be fairly useless. Different systems might impose a penalty on the goddess involved (I am unaware of any adult male virgin deities) until she does something to rectify the situation. Maybe she avenges her loss by murdering the crap out of whoever deflowered her, or she arranges for them to be emasculated, or something. Or she marries them and establishes a new pattern in her Legend. Legends can change.

              If the loss of virginity is consensual, think about why it's done. Think also about why the virginity of that goddess mattered. What did it represent? Was it there to show she was not an adult woman? Was it there to make her independent, free from marriage and servitude to a husband? In at least one version of Greek mythology, Artemis's big thing wasn't that she hated the idea of having sex, it was she hated the idea of being a wife. She made Zeus promise never to make her get married. In a modern world where virginity isn't tied to marriage nearly so much, might Artemis loosen up and decide that as long as she can still be a strong, asswhuppin, independent woman, who cares if she gets jiggy with it? Then you have to think how that affects her Legend.

              In general, I would say that if the goddess rolls with the change in her Legend and either embraces it (willing loss) or sets about somehow avenging her loss (unwilling loss) then she's worked that event into her Legend and she doesn't particularly suffer penalties for it. If she spends time freaking out (understandable) and is unable to resolve the situation, then she's probably going to lose Legend until she finds a way to bind her identity back together. That's basically what Gods are, after all. Their entire being is based on their conceptual identity. Artemis losing her virginity would be like Heracles losing his superhuman strength, or Ares becoming a pacifist. A really more accurate comparison might be what would happen if Hephaestus cured his lameness, since that's closer to the same kind of conditional vs vocational thing.



              You touched on something that pretty much every Scion group is gunna have to deal with: Epic Socials make free will and informed choice a very shaky subject. What possible definition of free will and consensual sex includes one party having Epic Appearance 5? How could any mortal at all ever turn that down? If Aphrodite walks up to a happily married father and tells him to have sex with her, he does that right then. It doesn't matter that he loves his wife, that he would never hurt her or want to betray her. Aphrodite's Epic Socials blow waaay past what he's capable of dealing with and so he does whatever she wants, no possible resistance at all. Is that in any way not rape? Really, past a 2-3 Legend point difference, the issue is the same. There's no real way to beat someone with an Epic Social that's significantly stronger than your resist stat (whatever that may be in your game). They own you, if they want you. They can convince you it was your idea, or make you want them, or just be so sexy that you can't possibly turn them down... but none of that is your free choice because you are so far out of your league there was never a question of you putting up any resistance at all. Not to mention the innate level of coercion going on in any LegendaryBeing-mortal relationship where the mortal is aware of what the Legendary being is. If you turn down the Morrigan for sex, she's probably going to kill you somehow. Painfully. Horribly.

              So, that right there, is a big deal. And of course rape happens all the time in mythology. It's everywhere. All over the place, in every ancient culture. Just buttloads of it. Zeus "ravishes" women constantly, his brothers both do it. Morrigan is out there with her sex-or-death 'seductions' and Tlazlteotl is just waiting for you to fail to resist her so she can murder you for your sins. Gods tend to be terrible people and even the ones that don't set out to be specifically terrible are hugely egocentric and may not be able to possibly understand why someone would not want to have sex with them. Does Thor understand why someone wouldn't want to try his hammer on for size? No. Because he's Thor and obviously anyone he wants would also want him. Avoiding the unwanted advances of a God generally doesn't end well. You get turned into landscape features, or monsters, or you have to flee to the ends of the earth.


              End result? If you have any PC's that have expressed interest in Epic Socials and especially Epic Appearance, you need to have a talk with your entire group. It is going to be a situation that arises eventually. Even if your Epically Sexy PC doesn't want to seduce people, they're Epically Sexy. People will want to seduce them. It's gunna happen. You need to be sure everyone in your group is on the same page. I cannot stress enough how incredibly important it is to do that. I've had issues where players said they were alright with something, then later it turned out they were not at all alright with it and had just been trying to avoid making a fuss. That's not good. Do everything you can to make sure your entire group is comfortable with what's happening at your table. Obviously if someone says they're fine and really isn't, that's not a situation you can deal with easily. All you can do is try to be open and make it plain that you're trying your best to accommodate everyone.

                                  I'd say it's something intensely personal to the Goddess in question. There are a number of myths in which Athena or Hestia are propositioned by various Gods, or certain Gods attempt to rape them. A lot is made of the fact that these attempts never succeed. Hestia would probably be most strongly affected, since being a virgin IS the greatest part of her Legend. By contrast, Athena has a lot more going for her.

                                  Consider also what a culture would consider virginity. The Greeks did not consider lesbian intercourse as sex, and so any of these Goddesses who had sex with a woman (forced or not) would probably still count as a virgin in the eyes of her Legend (hell, she probably still counts herself as a virgin. There's enough nudge nudge, wink wink in Greek narrative to assume that Artemis follows this mindset).

                                  By contrast, consider Guan Yin. She is a virgin Goddess, but unlike the Greek trio, this fact has no relevance to her mythology. It's not a gigantic part of her image, and is really more a side-effect of her being a Buddhist nun than a feature unto itself. She would never abandon her duties for love, and even if she was raped (in which case your player character is an evil, evil man), I doubt the powers of Fate would come down on HER. They would come down on the person who just raped a nun, because that is some epically bad karma (and seriously, Guan Yin is like the nicest God ever, so don't do it).

                                  As for virgin Goddesses in other myths, I am not an expert, but here are my thoughts on the ones I know.

                                  The Yazata Goddesses Ard and Anahita are virgin goddesses, but I do not know if them losing it would impact them heavily since I'm not sure how large a factor it is in their legends. Ard's brother Sraosha would probably gut you like a pig for touching his little sister though.

                                  When the Aztec virgin Goddess Quetzalpetlatl was raped by her brother Quetzalcoatl in a drunken stupor, he had to immolate himself in penance, and she disappears off the face of mythology. So one suspects that losing the one thing that defined her Legend, she ceased to exist. Hestia isn't that bad off, but she should consider this a rather scary idea.

                                  Finally, there is Sarasvati, who holds the rather unique position of being both married, and explicitly a virgin Goddess. Her virginity is a symbol of her association with the Arts and Sciences, higher, intellectual forms of expression and enquiry. The loss of her virginity (also likely to be forced, since she's shown no signs of straying from Brahma for ages) is likely to heavily diminish her power over these areas, while leaving her Water powers as a river Goddess untouched. The rapist should consider also the fact that Shiva and Sarasvati have a strong friendship in Hindu myth, so he's definitely going to have both Shiva and Kali on his tail. I would not wish to be this person.

                                  (As an aside, the Canaanite Goddess Anat is sometimes called a virgin, and at other times is Ba'al's lover. She's never both. If you do choose to run her as a virgin, and if a player gets the idea of raping her in his head, I would suggest you ask him to create a new character since Anat is likely to tear him apart and eat his entrails. This is the Goddess who killed Death himself while he was in the Underworld. It would be a sobering reminder as to why certain Goddesses remain virgins unless they want to).
