    Scion 2.0 Project

    06-01-2014, 03:14 PM
    Scion 2.0


    Hello Everyone,

    We are proud to announce the Scion 2.0 website’s release to the public. We have spent the last month working away at it, moving our rules over to the site from a large series of Google Documents, and filled in the blank spots with some pieces from the now closed John’s Scion Resources.

    We still have a lot of work ahead of us to complete the full revision of the system, including revising every single Purview, Pantheon Specific Purview, and Epic Attribute to better fit into Scion 2.0. We have finished several projects already, which are detailed below.

    The base system of the game is entirely completed, save that some things may be tinkered with slightly in the future, but additionally Animal, Arete, Atman, Chaos, Dexterity, Enech, Mystery, Stamina, Strength, and Water have been entirely completed. Other material is in the heavy playtesting phase and will hopefully be released soon, but we have included a few teasers among the unrevised Attributes and Purviews.
    Chaos


    Guides and Retainers
    Additionally, we have provided a list of possible Guides and Retainers for Scions sorted by Pantheon, which can be found through the link and the Birthrights section of our site. We also have a section displaying the Relics we have used in our games so people can have a better grasp on our pricing system. In the immediate future we are going to be taking requests for potential Relics from mythology that people want to see on the site.

    Future Goals
    Our number one goal following launch will be getting the Divine Pronunciation guide up and running, along with ongoing revisions of all the Purviews, Pantheon Specific Purviews, and Epic Attributes. Along the way we fully intend to add all the PDF pantheons researched and written by John’s Scion Resources.

    We are planning to finish up some other goals, such as rewriting the Virtues to be more thematic. This includes some extremities which make very little sense. Updating the Scent the Divine tables, adding family trees, and creating the previously mentioned Relics page.

    We are incredibly sorry that we have not included the Amatsukami and the Celestial Bureaucracy, for they do have some amazing figures and myths, but we are just not comfortable with them in the state they are in at the moment. We would need to entirely overhaul their Pantheon roster, and their Pantheon Specific Purviews, before we could feel comfortable with having them up on the site. This remains a goal for the distant future, as we stockpile research materials from those regions.

    Questions
    If anyone has any questions, suggestions, or comments to us, feel free to post them here or send them to [email protected]. We are more than happy to discuss things with you, help explain things, or listen to any suggestions you give us.

    We do hope everyone enjoys what we have completed so far, and we hope people continue to enjoy the additional parts of the game we revise over time. Welcome to the Scion 2.0 Project.
