They need a team of specialists who not only have the knowledge, but also the power to counteract their abilities.

I have little doubt they would already be working on technology decades ahead of the rest of the world in this setting.

                    Thus, it becomes an alliance of necessity.

                                                Given the numbers of love deities influencing the World (a good number having a definition of love as a complicating, star-crossed thing), the views of the mortal population on love must be quite different. Love Is Serious. It tends to attract the attention of Fate (or at least, the interest of adequately Purviewed Deities), and it can't - ever - be - easy. It can be maddening, holy, sultry, or outright annihilating - but it can't be lukewarm or mediocre.

                                                Creating one's first account on a dating sites is pretty close in importance to a sexual first time (or even a first communion - let's remember than many a God has its own dating site).

                                                Rom coms make up more than half of the nominations at awards shows, as their dramatic content isn't sappy, but simply adequate accounts of things that happen quite often (and yup, "romantic horror" is a totally established genre).

                                                Best-selling authors write dating sims, that are seriously studied in schools to prepare the pupils for the real life.

                                                There's probably a tacit "infatuation leave" planned by most companies to allow new lovers to cope with the trials Fate throws at them to test their feelings.

                                                A casual love-story, without dramatic twists, or a simple hook-up without transcendental ecstasy kicking in, is regarded pretty much like many people in our world conceive love at first sight: an extremely rare event that can happen, once in a blue moon, but is mostly an idealized fantasy born of wishful thinking and fictitious accounts.

                                                            Bad things that can happen? Alrighty then;

                                                            -Obviously there will be various ongoing (hot & cold) religious wars as while the gods may abide in political detente, fanatical human worshipers tend to take the faith and run with it.

                                                            -Chaotic, mischievous, trickster gods (& demigods) randomly meddling in people’s lives will instill a higher level of resentment in the minds of mortals who, frankly, are tired of all the bother. Life is hard enough just with mortal BS, and worse with more of it coming from monsters & titans, without adding additional BS from the “gods” to the mix. For that mater, as you mentioned, aspiring trickter scions staging epic heists and thefts undermining the financial stability of nations isn’t likely to go over well.

                                                            -Crosspantheon customs will slow tourism and negatively affect global trade as people become wary of any culture outside of their own lest they offend some foreign god or fall prey to some foreign monster.

                                                            -The fact that literally anyone could be a disguised deity (or quasi-deity) will make the dating scene a literal minefield. There’s no telling whether this person you just met is a monster, god, demigod, or scion incognito, so people will be extra-wary. Of course, on the plus side that will bring an end to any non-deific misogyny for fear of being cursed or killed & eaten.

                                                            Hmm… Active gods will hurt the insurance business, devils actually making people do things puts a monkeywrench into legal proceedings, exorcism and cleansing will become a new industry, divination will see a boom in business, more as I think of em.
