                        I presume, that is good place to ask some questions that I have not ask at the old forum.
                        1) How are Favored Abilities denoted? I mean, we have rules for Associated Purviews and Epic Attributes - whatever debatable they are - but I cannot remember anything about Abilities. At first I thought that the highest are taken, but... Well, for example, Set has Brawl, Command, Control, Melee, Presence and Survival as inherited. And his writeup lists four of them at 5 - along with Athletics, Awareness, Fortitude and Integrity. Actually, Control and Presence are 4 - but they are inherited, while others are not.
                        The same is with Hera. She has Fortitude of 4, others Favored at 5 - and has also Art, Awareness and Integrity at 5, for example.
                        So - how?

                        2) It is well-known that characters are upgraded, when they become Demigods, receiving certain number of points on Attributes, for example. And at least 2 of these points should go into category. And we know that no mundane Attribute cannot exceed Legend.
                        So. If I have a character with Legend 4 and, for example, Strength 5, Dexterity 5 and Stamina 4... what should I do with Demigod upgrade at Legend 5? Even if I list Physical Attributes as tertiary, I should place 2 points at this category, so I can raise Stamina to 5... and what with the other point?
                        I can imagine two variants of solving this problem, but, perhaps, someone already solved it?

                            Originally posted by Vi-Zet View Post
                            I presume, that is good place to ask some questions that I have not ask at the old forum.
                            1) How are Favored Abilities denoted? I mean, we have rules for Associated Purviews and Epic Attributes - whatever debatable they are - but I cannot remember anything about Abilities. At first I thought that the highest are taken, but... Well, for example, Set has Brawl, Command, Control, Melee, Presence and Survival as inherited. And his writeup lists four of them at 5 - along with Athletics, Awareness, Fortitude and Integrity. Actually, Control and Presence are 4 - but they are inherited, while others are not.
                            The same is with Hera. She has Fortitude of 4, others Favored at 5 - and has also Art, Awareness and Integrity at 5, for example.
                            Scion RAW picked abilities and Associateds which the developers thought fit the God in question for passing down to their children, and the writers of the Scion Godbook weren't aware of any rule that those abilities should be all 5 or whatnot. Then again the mechanical sheets of any stated character in the RAW books was kinda horrifying in their lack of anything resembling balance or sense.

                            Originally posted by Vi-Zet View Post
                            2) It is well-known that characters are upgraded, when they become Demigods, receiving certain number of points on Attributes, for example. And at least 2 of these points should go into category. And we know that no mundane Attribute cannot exceed Legend.
                            So. If I have a character with Legend 4 and, for example, Strength 5, Dexterity 5 and Stamina 4... what should I do with Demigod upgrade at Legend 5? Even if I list Physical Attributes as tertiary, I should place 2 points at this category, so I can raise Stamina to 5... and what with the other point?
                            I can imagine two variants of solving this problem, but, perhaps, someone already solved it?
                            RAW-wise? No. You've essentially lost a dot of attribute. An ST could let you move that extra dot to another category, or one could remove the idea of categories all together and just give flat-across-the-board increases. (9 total instead of 4/3/2). What my groups do is allow Demigods to have Attributes rated to 8, and Gods to 11. This completely removes the conflict and allows characters to have some diversity when they hit the respective legend-bumps.
