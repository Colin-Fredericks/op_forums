                                    Astrid Thurston, Scion of Thor

                                    Background: Astrid Thurston is a travelling twenty-something punk rock musician (she prefers skald). Barely out of her teens, she comes from a small town in Norway that she’d like to forget about. When she was younger, she repeatedly run away from said town whenever things got heated with her mother, who would constantly berate her for being too much like her absentee father. Consequently, her biggest role models growing up were her friends in similar domestic situations and the burned-out rockers that she would see performing in bars whenever she runaway. The cycle of running away and crashing with friends only to come back for one reason or another finally came to an end when her mother and her new husband died in a car accident during a thunderstorm. Filled with grief and guilt over not being able to resolve her differences with her mother, Astrid purposely got into a tavern brawl that quickly went too far. Actually surviving that encounter, and "adopting" her half-brother soon after, has given her new lease on life and a desire to finally find her long-lost father.

                                    Description: A tall, fit twenty-something with bright red hair, freckles, and even brighter blue eyes.

                                    Divine Parent: Thor (Aesir)
                                    Concept: Rebel Without a Cause
                                    Calling: Guardian or Warrior

                                    Paths
                                    Origin: Angsty Runaway – Astrid ran away from home several times growing up and knows how to survive on the streets, and on the road, and keep her dignity intact. (Close Combat, Integrity, Subterfuge)
                                    Role: Wandering Punk Rock Skald – Astrid has always had a talent for playing the electric guitar and has used it to make ends meet on the road. (Culture, Persuasion, Pilot)
                                    Pantheon: Daughter of Thor (Supernatural) – Astrid knows her father was not like most people. That he was someone thing, like her. She knows it’s her destiny to meet him one day. (Athletics, Close Combat, Occult)
                                    Abilities: Athletics 2, Culture 4 (Music), Close Combat 5 (Baseball Bats), Integrity 3 (Bucking Authority), Occult 2, Persuasion 4 (Making Friends), Pilot 4 (Driving), Subterfuge 3 (Streetwise)
                                    Attributes: Intellect 3, Might 4, Presence 5; Cunning 2, Dexterity 3, Manipulation 2; Resolve 2, Stamina 3, Composure 4
                                    Knacks: The Biggest Target, Close the Gap, Death by Teacup

                                    Notes: Uses trusty aluminum baseball for when the barflies get to handsy and use a reliable motorcycle for the road. Has a six year old brother she’s basically illegally adopted. Social Services isn’t happy about that.
