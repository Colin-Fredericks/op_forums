    Session Results - After successfully becoming part of the cult, the Band prepared to take a shift guarding the Pier. Now, the cult only uses 4 people at one time, and the Band chose not to buckle the rules. So, while the majority of the Band went to the Pier at 4 in the morning, Jamie & Lad tried to procure some more bodies. Katya & Leithe guarded the actual drop-off cargo container, while Vjun & Elias patrolled the perimeter.

    Now, the whole point of this session was to have a big round of combat since they're a big step closer to finding Thor's belt. So, Jamie & Lad are chasing a lead down with a Bloods gang in an abandoned apartment when they get ambushed. At the same time, the Pier is assaulted by another gang. Because of how the turns went, Jamie and Lad finished the apartment showdown before the Pier Assault truly started combat.

    I threw 6 thugs at Jamie & Lad, armed with switchblades, a couple of handguns, and one shotgun. I quickly learned that the book's standard thug profile is woefully inadequate as a challenge against Scions. Three were slaughtered before Lad used his Appearance + Presence to intimidate the rest into surrendering, one thug botching and throwing himself to the floor, begging to be allowed to live.

    18 thugs are attacking the 4 scions at the Pier, and I have no idea if they'll do any damage. The Band's dodge values are high enough that the thugs don't normally hit without a positive modifier. Other than that, it's all about learning combat rules and powers.

        Yeah you will find that Mortals mean next to nothing when coming up against Scions. You could try to do something to make them more threatening for your next encounter, but you could instead use this as a showcase for your players on how powerful they are. They are unquestionably mighty.

        For example, your Perception Scion who got 11 Successes on a roll? Best possible mortal dice pool for that would be 15 (5 Perception + 5 Awareness + 5 Tool) which is the absolute peak of mortal capacity, with probably a small handful of people with such high ratings. Average roll by someone that amazing, stupendous, and randomly carrying one of the world's greatest pair of binoculars, would be 8 Successes. Your Perception Scion beat them by three Successes, which is pretty big at Hero.

                                                If you are planning to give them some sort of Prophecy, using a God is very tricky. Due to the heavy duty taboos regarding the use of Seidr, any God preforming it is going to be super-super looked down upon. It's an effeminate thing, and the Norse take their gender systems super seriously, at least in the realms of Masculinity. So, while Odin is accused of practicing Seidr, he can't really show up to give Scions a Prophecy, it would be really bad.

                                                Freya can work pretty well, but you've already had her show up which unless you want her to be very involved, I might not do. Frigg could as you said be a good choice, but she's busy a lot dealing with Odin as the pair of them... like, compete with each other? They have an odd relationship.

                                                You might want to use a mortal magician of some sort to relay the message, or fall back onto the Ravens. If you want them to take it more seriously, possibly send Odin's Wolves, Geri and Freki, both of whom should be very focused around Greed and Hunger if you want to play them up as characters.
