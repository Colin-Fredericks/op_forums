    Scion: The Revisening, a fanmade project

    03-25-2022, 03:38 AM
    https://docs.google.com/document/d/1...it?usp=sharing
    (fixed the header problem)

    As a big fan of the Scion stuff; it was inevitable that I would make some sort of fanmade project.

    -the project itself is a blend of 1st edition and 2nd addition Scion, cherry picking the mechanics I very much enjoyed.
    -the intent was to make a version of scion that emphasized power fantasy, and epic collateral damage at demigod and higher levels.

    After a few months of having fun while play testing it with some friends, I believe it has achieved goodn'uf status for fandom viewing; and I hope that some of you might have some fun as well with it, as I continue to work on it.
    Feel free to make suggestion, comments, concerns about the project at hand (some might even slip past my stubbornness and have real impact on the project); and if you are as excided as I am, hit the subscribe, slap the bell, pet the hamster or what ever the steps are required to stay updated with convenience.


    [warning] contents may contain

    -mature content; some readers may have weaker constitutions based on certain topics at hand (personally, I lose my crap over dumb stuff, like how to pronounce Zelda character names); I usually try to keep it fairly tame but just in case, here is the fair warning.
    -some knack/boon powers might be worded strangely and you have to read it five times over; sorry in advance for that (let me know what one you need help on and I can start working on translating it to common tongue).
    -spelling and grammar as per norm with any mid project is just awful; completion and polishing edit will be the final step.
    -designer notes are going to be scattered throughout the PDF; some might be out of date or ignored for special exception on powers.


    [testing progress update]

    -tested only up to mid god (legend 12) thus far (11/29/2023); and with only about a handful of the boon/domains tested to 9th dot (animal, death, darkness, forge, fire, guardian, moon, water ), some will still need durations, range, potency and limitations.


    [readers guide]

    just got some good advice from my Exalted friend; the number of pages can be intimidating so rather than getting the reader overwhelmed, here is a guide that gets you to the meat and potatoes of the mechanics taking it down from 150+ pages to 18 pages.
    section suggestions

    -the big rules
    -combat changes
    -Legend,
    -willpower
    -mental and social combat [optional extra credit]
    -character creation [skipping the history generator for demigod and god characters]
    -character advancement
    -birthrights
    -knack rules and boon rules [not the entirety of all the knacks and boons; that you can just casually pick at your favorites]
