    Scion + Exalted = Cool?

    07-09-2014, 04:28 AM
    So, I wanted to add a few things from Exalted into Scion. Some small things, like Prayer rules, Character Creation tweaks, etc., but I'm also interested in adding some bigger stuff, like Martial Arts Forms, Sorcery Spells, Astrology, etc. So, I wanted to know, about where would Exalted Martial Arts Styles fit? Like, do you think Terrestrial Styles are on par with the Hero Cycle? Or maybe Celestial Styles are a better fit? What about Sorcery? Would the three levels fit the three Scion Cycles? I was thinking of using it to replace the Magic Purview, it just seems more interesting to me. I was also thinking of replacing Mystery or Prophecy with Astrology. I'm more interested in the Power Levels and how they stack up against one another than if I SHOULD do it or not. I know how many feel about adding Exalted to, well, anything.

    Thanks for the input!

                              My default advice is not to do it. While they resemble each other on the surface, they use a different mote/legend ratio, a different success ratio, a different resistance system, and a different set of mythology. That said...

                              Terrestrial Martial Arts / Terrestrial Sorcery = Legend 2 - 4.
                              Celestial Martial Arts / Celestial Sorcery = Legend 5 - 7.
                              Sidereal Martial Arts / Solar Sorcery = Legend 8 - 10.

                                                  I'll be the first to admit i don't have the practice with Scion of the Baron and some of the other people present, but the biggest hurdles i see in getting the two games together are the Scion's ludricous amount of sucesses even in comparison to Exalted's, let's be honest here, already inflated standards and matters of mythology (Fate in Scion is completely unlike its namesake in Exalted and closer to Raksha's notion of evolution through the development of an epic narrative, the character of Scion's underworld & overworld in relation to Creation & co, what Scion's Titans & deities actually are, titans & their avatars seeming momewhere between Unshaped & 3rd Circle Demons in thematics & presentation, among other stuff).

                                                  Admitedly, the setting/mythology part is much less of an issue if one keeps the two worlds separate creations/realms of existence (beyond Deep Wyld/Well of Udr), but can still quite relevant.

                                                  Yes, cost differences and other things exist, but purview boons and epic attribute knacks could be ported as charms with little problem in concept, if not actual mechanics.

                                                  Legend and Essence while filling mostly the same function seem to have some differences in treatment and because of this and matters of scale i toyed with the following equivalence:
                                                  Legend Essence
                                                  - 1 (unenlightened)
                                                  1-2 1-2 (enlightened)
                                                  3-4 2-3
                                                  5-6 3-4
                                                  7-8 4-5
                                                  9-10 5-6
                                                  11-12 6-7
                                                  The legend/essence pool one may or not stay the same depending on individual ST's convenience/laziness, but no, i have no particular suggestion on how to calculate such pools beside fake something and filling the blanks with mote pool enhancers like Essence Plethora.

                                                  Don't know if it will be useful or useless but hope it may get your interest at least.
