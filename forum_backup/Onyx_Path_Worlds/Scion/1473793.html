    [Actual Play] The House Always Wins Chronicle

    02-02-2022, 12:02 AM
    There was interest in my posting journals for my game, so I figured I would give it a shot. My game is told in character journals, which are optional, so I cannot guarantee a journal for every character, or any character.

    My game, “The House Always Wins” is set in Las Vegas. The PCs are chosen by their parents to reclaim a Terra Incognita that seems to have recently manifested nearby. After finding and exploring it, they began to set up a spa there, bringing in various Theoi creatures to help, one of the Lampades as a hostess, a Naiad for the spring, an Oread for the mountain, a Satyr bartender, etc. Now they are trying to draw the attention of various Pantheons to get the place running and use it as a base for expanding their own legends beyond it.

    It’s my fourth campaign I have run for Scion, the first for 2e and the first single pantheon game. A lot of the players this time are less familiar with world myth so decided to stay with Greek. To their credit, they wanted to do things right and respectfully. I look forward to teaching them more.

    So, my next post should be introducing you to the PCs, the next post will be some character backgrounds and setting background (from handouts). Then I will post the journals and handouts from the first arc.

    The game is set up with area plotlines, which will slowly move and resolve with or without PC intervention (but have milestones measured in arcs, so the PCs will have time to intervene as they wish) and plotlines centered on the spa (which are usually the main arcs). Every named NPC has a portrait. I am not posting all those pictures but can post any people are curious about. That is a balance when I post games online what to do about the often over 100 NPC pictures.

    I want to stress I am posting this for pleasure reading but also for inspiration for other’s games. So hopefully this will be helpful.

    ETA: Also, check out the sister game The Ultra Violets.

              Avril Hart


              Avril considered herself an ethical hunter. She only killed what she needed, and only of species that were a danger to their surrounding environment: overpopulated, invasive, demonic, etc. So when she first saw the Hind (a soft golden glow, too many antlers) she holstered her shotgun and raised her camera. The Hind almost seemed to wait until her chosen weapon was at the ready before it began to run.

              It leapt nimbly over rocks and between trees, all of its movements too fast to be caught on camera. Avril’s mundane legs shouldn’t have been able to keep up at all, but the Hind slowed when she did. Both creature and girl were enjoying the chase. Avril was only vaguely aware of the passage of time. She grew tired and hungry, but not unmanagably. The sky darkened, but she knew if she stopped she would lose her prey. That was unthinkable. Although the Hind seemed unwilling to outrun her, it was willing to outlast her. If she died of thirst before getting its photo, then she wasn’t worthy.

              A small, quiet part of Avril’s brain was aware that she was being tested. It chafed against the idea. But after all those hours, those bruises, the emptiness in her stomach, she NEEDED that picture. She needed the prize to make the pain worth it.
              And then, for a split second, the Hind was distracted. A sound elsewhere in the woods, some other animal calling out for its kin. SNAP. Avril didn’t need to look through at her camera’s display to know she had gotten it.

              Relief flooded her, adrenaline stopped, suddenly her limbs felt like they weighed a hundred pounds. She leaned against a tree and closed her eyes. She was nearly asleep when she felt a giant nose sniffing her hair. She opened an eye. The Hind was nuzzling her. Avril extended a hand to gently push its nose away. It started chewing a piece of her hair. She laughed shakily. When she found her voice she asked, “Did I win?”

              The Hind answered by lowering itself to the ground, inviting her to sit on its back. She did. The creature’s back was more comfortable than it had any right to be, the hoof beats too rhythmic. Avril wondered if she was dreaming.

              The Hind brought her home.

              (Avril's Guide)

              Imke Scheit


              Imke Scheit was born October 27th, 1943, in Antwerp, Belgium during the Nazi occupation. Antwerp was liberated just before her first birthday. While her family was wealthy, being Germans in Belgium, they found themselves ostracized, and young Imke found herself socially isolated away from the German-speaking parts of the country. She mastered Dutch (being in Flanders) and French quickly, and soon English as well. Still, she never quite fit in, a situation exacerbated by her Queerness.

              Still, she had beauty, and she had money. Her family had kept their fortunes due only to their role in the Resistance, though even that could not save them socially. But Imke was sent to the best schools all women’s education and given opportunities few women had. Unfortunately, she was also kicked out of two schools and three Universities for affairs with teachers and students.

              Still her skills in Seduction, breaking and entering, and generally getting her way were noticed by certain individuals who gave her the opportunity to help prevent Belgium from falling to a third dictator. She was trained by NATO and sent into the field working against agents of the Warsaw Pact. Imke worked in East Germany, West Germany, Czechoslovakia, Yugoslavia, Greece, and Austria against the Communists states, though she had an ambiguous role in Yugoslavia after it broke from the Warsaw Pact. She proved very good at disguising herself as being German (her natural background) Dutch, Danish, Slovene, or Austrian. Moving place to place and shifting accent to accent, Imke was a natural.

              She was also a child of the Goddess of Witches, something she knew from a young age. She found herself able to make people forget interactions and blend into her surroundings with ease. She was also known for being able to compromise nearly anyone, man or woman, she came across if she put her mind to it. She used her gender to get close to wives, girlfriends, and mistresses, and manipulated them into acquiring their paramours’ secrets. If this caused her any guilt, she never showed it. Men were not safe from her either, but such was not her preferred approach. And a lot of the men could spot her as a shark because they were more on guard for such. Still, she was immensely successful and, known as the Lioness, became a favored NATO agent.

              After the wall fell, on November 9th of 1989, she was repurposed for limiting Russian influence in the former Yugoslavia but found herself in over her head. After the death of Josip Broz Tito, it fell apart, leading to not only warring factions but ethnic cleansing. Imke operated primarily in Belgrade and focused on limiting Russian influence as was her assignment, but also did what she could to slow the wheels of Genocide.

              Just before the 1999 bombing began, she was moved out of Belgrade back to Belgium. While she entered Domestic intelligence work, after the World Trade Center Attack on September 11th of 2001 she was called back to NATO intelligence. But she was 54 now. She was tired, and she had little desire to enter into what she saw as a holy war. Especially as she did not perceive herself as being on either side.

              She entered a quiet retirement, painting and gardening at her family’s ancestral Home. By 2007 she was the only one there, save servants, and went out less and less. By the dawning of 2021 she was 77 and getting less active despite being spry for her age. However, that is when there was a twist of fate.

              Hecate needed her now. It was time to pay her back for the gifts she had made such use of in Europe. But this call came with a reward; she woke up and appeared to be in her early 20s, and she felt like it too. After the surprise and screaming cooled down, she was met by an enchanting lady in red, Evelyn Eventide, one of the Hesperides (Imke presumed using an alias), and a new task. Head to Las Vegas in America. Serve Hecate within what should be a Spa that many divinities may come and go through, to see to the interests of the Goddess of Witches and perhaps nudge events and opinions a bit for some of the august personages there. Surely, for one accustomed to getting people wrapped around her finger this was a challenge and a reward?

              Imke did not exactly see going to the USA as a reward, largely perceiving it as a nation of bumpkins. Still, she was young, she was beautiful, and she was ready to take on the world. And apparently Queerness is hip now? Imke packed her bags. It was indeed time for a new challenge.
