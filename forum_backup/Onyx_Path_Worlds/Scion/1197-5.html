                        Originally posted by Pilgrim View Post
                        I want to play a variant setting based on the apocalyptic prophecies of Daniel and Revelations if I was to have rules for an Abrahamic pantheon.
                        Forgive me but, if that's the case, and we all agree that creating a "first among equals" pantheon won't do any good to the line, would not be easier to do it as a fanmade project? Or, and I really hope you don't get me wrong here, play another game?

                        I'm saying that in the most diplomatic way possible. Lots of people want the Abrahamic pantheon in Scion. Not Semitic, not Sumerian nor Canaanite: they want the Abrahamic (and Arabic) ones. On the other side, lots of other people would hate to have it portrayed in the game. I honestly cannot discern if, on both sides, this is more a case of vocal groups expressing their ideas and thus they should not really have an impact on the line development (again, in a way or the other), but I think we can agree that the Abrahamic pantheon, if written as asked, is going to be different from the others. I mean, a quick example, angels, saints and prophets as gods? I'm not saying that's wrong, but it's different from all the other pantheons and for a reason that can be resumed as "just because". As for the Loa (and the possibility of a Yoruba pantheon), the devs said they are going to be quite different in 2E, so we'll have to wait.

                        I suggest the "fanmade" option for the simple reason that if you have to distance yourself too much from the rules of the setting and make exceptions just to shoehorn the Abrahamic in the "canon" game, I can't see why that would be a good idea. Not to mention that there are a lot of other interesting pantheons out there that would prove to be a lot easier and painless to add to Scion for the writers and developers. For those of you who suggest having the Abrahamic pantheon in a separate supplement, in a separate setting, in a separate context, I don't think the relatively limited resources of the line (what? I love Scion but let's be fair, guys) should be used to throw a bone to a part of the fanbase.

                        And I'm saying this as the guy who wrote the longest unofficial pantheon on the old forum, so I can get what people are feeling when they say they have cool ideas and plot hooks in mind. I still think that an Abrahamic pantheon would cause more problems than it's worth. Semitic and Sumerian pantheons? Not nearly as much (if at all).

Now, I have no way to predict how 2E will be (and I've never said that), nor what they exactly meant with that, but at least we can discuss in a general way, basing oursevelves on what we've got.
