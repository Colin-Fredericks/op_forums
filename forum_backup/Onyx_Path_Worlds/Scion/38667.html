    What does a God looks like?

    12-29-2013, 06:48 AM
    I like joining pictures or photos of my NPC's so that my players find it easier to identify them. But I don't know how to choose nice photos or pictures when it comes to Gods. It's always "okay" but not "great". A friend of mine likes to use actors and actresses so the game looks really modern but as demonstrated for the Gaulish pantheon created by Bibliothèque Interdite, it lacks of... power and divinity! I DO know it's really hard to represent gods (the ones in Percy Jackson don't look divine even on Mount Olympus...)

    Do you guys draw them? If so, is it possible to share your art?

      I like having two photos for Gods. One of a regular human person that represents the appearance they take on when visiting Earth and dealing with mortals, Heroes and maybe even Demigods. Then there's their real appearance and for that, I head to movies or art history books. Someone in full-on crazy-ass costume, or an actual illustration of the God from their culture, or even a modern artist's interpretation of that deity.

      For example, Hathor. When she appeared to the Demigods in my game, she appeared as a gold-dusted disco diva and I borrowed a picture of Foxxy Cleopatra for her. When she appeared later, during the God segment of the game, she was Hathor and I showed them images of Egyptian representations of her and somewhere dug up an artist interpretation that I liked. I can't find it anymore. Same thing for Tezcatlipoca, except his mortal avatar was that crazy hispanic lawyer dude from the third season of Dexter.

      I'm a professor! Why is no one listening to me?!

                                              It's not for Scion per se (I haven't been able to play the game yet because the guy I usually play RPGs with is still trying to wrap his head around the system), but for a webcomic I'm working on that shares the "mythological gods in the modern world" theme, I've occasionally used film and television actors' likenesses as reference for my versions of certain gods.
