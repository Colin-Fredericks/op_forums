                        Spirit Totem
                        Hero Quirk:                         The Hero becomes aware if there's anything troubling the spirit world in an area (such as pollution, disrespectful treatment etc.). He lacks precise understanding, but gets a one sentence general feel about the area if he focuses on it.

                        ● Totem Protection: The Scion becomes immune to the attacks of his chosen totem. Animals of the chosen totem never attack the scion unless controlled by someone of greater legend than the scion. Only a totem creature of equal or greater legend may cause harm to the Scion.
                        ●● Spirit Imbual: The Scion can imbue legend points into an object, keeping it as a temporary reserve of legend.
                        ●●● Spirit Retrieval: The Scion can drain a friend's or his own relics for legend by reducing their potency temporarily.

                        Demigod Quirk: The Scion can see spirits of lower legend (1 to 2) and speak to them without paying any cost. He can also force such spirits to leave his presence.
                        ●●●● Spirit Form: The Scion takes on the form of a spirit and becomes more difficult to hurt. His soak increases by his legend and his DV increases by his highest dot in the PSP. He can also step through natural barriers less than a foot thick.
                        ●●●●● Send Away: By displacing an object (or person) into the spirit world, the object becomes unreachable by normal means. Those of greater or equal legend can attempt to pull it back
                        ●●●●● ● Spirit Breath: The scion becomes one with the living world and gains a bonus to his rolls. This bonus is more powerful outside of civilization
                        ●●●●● ●● Honor thy Ancestors: The Scion can imbue himself with the spirit of the world, turninga number of dice into auto-successes.

                        God Quirk: The Scion can see all spirits (of any legend) unless they are of equal or greater legend and attempt to hide from him. The Scion
                        ●●●●● ●●● Totem Creation: The Scion can create a totem spirit out of thin air and use all previous boons on said Totem.
                        ●●●●● ●●●● Totem Destruction: The Scion can sever the bounds between spirits, reducing the potency of relics and boons possessed by those of equal or lesser legend.
                        ●●●●● ●●●●● Spirit World: The Scion can create a whole new realm in the spirit world from scratch. The Scion may enter this realm freely at first, but as the world becomes more defined, it grows more distant and difficult to access.
