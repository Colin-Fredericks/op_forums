                    Jules Clemont, Scion of Eris/Discordia was, like all his half-siblings, subject to her whims at any given moment. However, while most Scions will have her alternatively help or hinder them at random, Jules only ever seemed to receive her scornful moods. Wherever he went in life, chaos would soon follow. On the day he was born, riots broke out in the city. When he turned three, a mass influenza broke out. On the day he passed that test he had been studying all week for, an earthquake hit. Upon his Visitation, a suicide bomber blew themselves up in a crowded stadium.

                    The only thing that changed after his Visitation was that he knew why these events were happening and that he was responsible. People would eventually start to notice that disaster followed him like a crazed lover and he would become known as a harbinger of doom - though if he played his cards right people might believe that he arrives to warn them of it, not bring it upon them.


                    Arman Rahimmi, Scion of Tishtrya. Born in Iran, Arman was very frequently exposed to the violence that religious extremist groups could enact upon anyone, especially 'their own people' and it culminated in him witnessing the public execution of his mother, who had been fighting for women's rights. After that traumatic event, he dedicated himself to trying to end war and instil peace in the near/middle east. After his Visitation, this goal would extend itself to the entire world and, if he could manage it, beyond even that...


                    Brandon Njordson, Scion of Njord. Icelandic son of Njord, I imagine him becoming a god of fire and, after Ragnarok, killing Surtr and taking his sword. As he's a child of Njord, he could potentially try to reform the Vanir as their new king, or he could become part of the new post-Ragnarok Aesir. His godly name would be Brandr.

                    Alternatively, since he's already taken Surtr's sword, he could try to take his role as a Titan Avatar of Muspelheim too! That would probably be more difficult, however.

                        Lycurgus Psomas: The product of Circe managing to entice some wandering Hero into her bedchambers, Lycurgus was made aware of his divine heritage from an extremely early age. Unfortunately, there wasn't a whole lot he was able to do with it, as he grew up on the magical island of Aeaea which had little contact with the outside world. He tried to convince his mother to teach him the ways of magic as a way to easing his boredom, but her focus would be completely diverted whenever some handsome man washed up on the shores of their home.

                        Lycurgus would become a young man by the time he felt he was finally ready to explore the outside world, and it was during his 21st year of life that he got a lucky break when a new batch of Scions arrived on the beach. Their vessel drawn there by Circe's magical interference as she had developed a fancy for some of the men on board. Lycurgus waited in the foliage for them to disembark and travel to his mother's temple, then he sprang from his hiding place, stole their boat and sailed away on his own adventure. So far as he knows, the vessel's original owners are still on the island, likely having been turned into beasts by now!

                        Lycurgus' sudden escape plan didn't leave him much time to pack his belongings. The only possession he managed to bring from home was an olive branch that he uses like a wand to channel the Magic and Animal purviews.


                        Additional Note: Due to Circe's neglectful training regiment, Lycurgus' power is not on her level (at least not yet, anyways). However, he has begun to show symptoms of his mother's personality quirks, particularly a fascination with shapeshifting other people and forcing them to become his pets. Additionally, Circe probably wasn't the best role model when it came to relationships, causing Lycurgus to develop fleeting attachments to others based entirely on their physical attractiveness.

                                                    Figured this guy could be used as either a PC or NPC, depending on how you want to run your game.

                                                    --------------------------------------

                                                    Aodh O Forannain: Not all Scions are lucky enough to receive their visitation in time, not even a son of Aengus, irish God of love and beauty. But where the Gods see failure, the Titans see opportunity, and Mami Wata was all to happy to step into Aodh's life and claim her for himself.

                                                    Irish born and raised, Aodh was born to a fishing family, which meant that he grew up around the sea for much of his life. Aengus has taken the form of his mother's husband during the night of his conception, so neither of them knew about his true heritage. But regardless, Aodh exhibited charming and handsome features throughout his life. With fair skin, silky blond hair and bright blue eyes, it did not take much effort for Aodh to sweet talk any girl in town. Which is probably why he drew the attention of a young maiden who was still suffering from the early stages of therianthropy.

                                                    At 21 years of age, having only just entered manhood, Aodh O Forannain strolled the beach near his home under the pale blue light of the full moon. There, he came upon the form of a young woman sprawled naked and unconscious upon the shore. Not one to leave a girl helplessly exposed like that, he hastily approached her and began to remove his evening coat to shroud her. But just as he was within reach, the tide surged forth unnaturally, sinking Aodh up to his shins in sea water. The mystery woman, however, transformed into a seal right before his eyes and thrashed around in panic, biting his hand before getting dragged out to sea by the receding tide. The next thing Aodh remembers clearly after that was waking up on a rock somewhere off the coast, surrounded by barking seals. And there beside him, stroking his perfectly blond hair, was the mermaid form of Mami Wata, smiling with affection!

                                                    In the days that followed, Aodh was brainwashed into becoming a loyal inhabitant of The Drowned Road. He never did find out who the Selkie was that bit him, and by this point, he's beyond the point of caring anymore. Feigning pity for the god child, Mami Wata 'blessed' him with access to some of her own power. All she asked for in payment was that Aodh agree to be placed under a Fatebond of her own creation. The specifics of this bond is that Aodh can never escape the call of the sea, which he is now spiritually bound to. During the time of the full moon (roughly 3 nights), Aodh is compelled to return to the sea and may not step upon dry land until the moon enters its next phase. In this way, Mami Wata is able to keep frequent check ups on the progress of her new pet, instead of trying to force him to visit her.

                                                    Perhaps if it were as simple as just living near the ocean forever, Aodh's existence could have been overlooked by other Scions. But the young Selkie has grown lonely from his isolation and his sense of romance has been twisted by Mami Wata's influence. He has begun to make more frequent trips to dry land in search of beautiful young girls to woo. Should he find one, he plans to bring them back to the sea with him, where they will become his Selkie bride. And as if that wasn't bad enough, Aodh doesn't seem to have a problem with polygamous relationships, which means he could be thinking of building an entire harem of Selkie concubines!


                                                    ----------------------------------

                                                    Under the patronage of Mami Wata, Aodh has been granted access to the Water, Animal (Seal) and Health Purviews. The Water and Animal purviews are channeled through his harpoon (Walrus Tusk) which adds +2L to the Hasta template. The Health purview is channeled through a golden collar that he wears around his neck. The collar was given to him by Mami Wata in order to emphasize his role as the Titan's pet Scion.

                                                    Purviews: Water 2, Animal 2, Health 1

                                                    Boons: Water Breathing, Water Control, Animal Communication (Seals), Animal Command (Seals), Assess Health

                                                    Divine Powers: Epic Appearance 2 (Center of Attention, Come Hither), Epic Manipulation 1 (Takes One to Know One), Epic Charisma 2 (Charmer, Engender Love), Epic Stamina (Extended Youth).

                                                    Selkie Form: Aodh's seal-man form is capable of amphibious performance, although he feels most comfortable in the water. The supernatural nature of this form enables him to survive underwater up to nearly any depth (an handy substitude, should he lose access to his Water Breathing boon) and can swim at 5 times his normal movement rate.

                                                    Tough Hide: While in his manimal form, Aodh's thick pelt grants him a +4 to Lethal and Bashing soak.
