Unfortunately, Fate isn't content to just let this turn of events go unnoticed. It seems that Zeus is not pleased with your sibling, nobody should consider themselves equal to the children of Olympus, especially not some wannabe Scion turned Titanspawn.

    The king of the Gods wants your brother in chains, enslaved in some forge under Olympus and forced to produce lightning bolts for use against Gaia's armies. But even though you and your brother have been rivals, there hasn't actually been a case of bad blood between the two of you. You have no reason to hate them, other than the fact they aren't pretty enough to meet Olympus' standards of quality.

    So now you face a quandary; do you obey your king and enslave your own brother for the crime of doing what comes naturally to siblings, or do you attempt to persuade Zeus to give them a lighter sentence in hopes of preventing your rivalry from escalating into something more violent?

                                    185) In a world where Scion existence is openly recognized by the public, one corporation is dedicated to giving the average citizen peace of mind in these troubled times. Calling themselves Mythic Industries, they have begun to provide numerous services to the public that allow them to compete with the otherwise vastly superior Scions. Their goal, or so they claim, is to prevent the Scions from establishing a coup by using their divine powers to usurp control of the world governments.

                                    Except that mystically perceptive Scions had noticed multiple discrepancies in their alleged background. For one, Mythic Industries is a multi national corporation that just kind of appeared out of the blue all across the world. Secondly, their idea of balancing the threat Scions pose is to grant supernatural abilities to their customers. And finally, these supernatural abilities unanimously reflect the powers of various species of Titanspawn!

                                    That's right, Hundun, the greater Titan of Chaos is the one pulling Mythic Industries' strings. Somehow, they figured out a way to warp history and are now preying upon the fear, anger and outright jealousy caused by a world where the supernatural has gone mainstream. Through the use of the company, they have been twisting mortals into Titanspawn right under the noses of the Gods. All under the false pretense of maintaining balance and security in a world where some teenager with a knife is more powerful than an entire squad of armed police officers!


                                    He may be an ancient, primordial entity, but you have to admit that Hundun's plan was flawlessly executed. They've already branded themselves as the good guys in the public image, and laying siege to their corporation will immediately draw the scorn of billions of mortals who feel threatened by the existence of Scions. No, the only way to fix this damage is to recreate whatever power Hundun used to rewrite history and stop him from changing it in the first place. Unfortunately, none of the pantheons even thought such a feat was possible until now, which means your research has to start from scratch!
