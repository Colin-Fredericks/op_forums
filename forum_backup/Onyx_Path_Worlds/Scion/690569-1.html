    Karena Kollias:     Scion of Haphaestus



    Details: Even before accepting her role as a Scion, Karena was at least partially familiar with Thaddeus Zurlo, because she worked at one of his family's corporations. As a mortal, Karena worked as a robotics developer, helping to create the world of tomorrow by building more sophisticated machinery. When Hephaestus learned that Thaddeus was putting a band together and that they had prior association with his daughter, he decided to give her a visitation and encourage her to join up. Following her father's advice, she became a member of The Rising Stars and traveled with her "boss" on their path to godhood together.

    As a Scion, Karena's inventions went from revolutionary to something approaching the realm of science fiction. It was not long before she discovered the art of combining technology with supernatural energy, creating machines that were just as much magical as they were scientific. Within a a year of carrying out her Scion duties, she had reached a level of genius no ordinary mortal could match, and she knew it! Eventually she grew bored with the mortal's limited grasp of scientific possibilities and devoted her research to the war against the Titans and their forces. Creating autonomous war machines to engage Titanspawn without having to risk the lives of valued agents. Those of a more warrior mindset would brand her a coward, but she sees them as primitive and ignorant. The days of hand to hand combat ended hundreds of years ago. War isn't fought with swords and bows and trebuchets anymore, it's fought with automatic rifles, incendiary grenades and unmanned drones. If they want to attack the Titans with rocks and sticks, they're free to do so, but this is a war Karena intends to win!


    Sadly, Karena's destiny as a Scion condemned her to suffer a similar fate to that of her father. She is crippled, her arms being torn from their sockets by an angry Cyclops during her Hero days. Refusing to allow his daughter's talent to be lost forever, Hephaestus implanted her with mechanical arms which doubled as Relics for her godly powers. Despite her new limbs being vastly superior to her old ones, she is still quite bitter over their loss and has developed an intense dislike for all Cyclopes who have not been cowed into submission to the Dodekatheon.


    In her full Godly form, Karena takes the form of a beautiful woman with curly red hair, deep blue eyes and mechanical arms forged out of some kind of unidentified golden alloy. She wears a traditional Greek tunic, but often wears an open lab coat over it to remind herself she's not living in ancient times. While wearing the coat, the only part of her artificial limbs that remain visible are the hands, but the implants actually start around the shoulders and continue down from there.


    Karena doesn't visit the mortal world too often anymore, as she feels it is too primitive to keep up with her aspiring genius. When she does visit, it is because she has been seized by inspiration and feels compelled to share her new invention with the World. Unfortunately for the World, these inventions are so highly advanced, that modern society is not prepared to handle them. So their blueprints are classified to the public and being worked on secretly in a government research facility. Hopefully mankind will be mature enough to handle them once their developments are complete.


    Powers: Epic Intelligence, Epic Stamina, Epic Wits, Arete, Fire, Earth, Mystery, Guardian

    Abilities: Craft, Science, Art, Control, Marksmanship, Thrown

    Rivals: Ares, Ptah, Huitzilopochtli, Hachiman, Damballa

        Eustis Baros: Scion of Dionysus


        Details: God of social anarchy and sexual freedom; as well as the estranged lover to Drakon (a fellow Bandmate). Even in his mortal years, Eustis was already showing himself to be his father's son when he decided to pursue a career in the adult film industry. Working his way up the ranks, he eventually found himself in the director's chair and went onto produce award winning films, the titles of which shall not be mentioned here! Life was looking good for young Eustis until the day of his visitation, which set into motion a chain of events turning him into one of the most frustrating beings in the world.

        Eustis was visited by Dionysus with a warning that one of his actors was a servant of Pan who was going to try and convert him to the exile's cause. Awakening his Scion heritage, Dionysus left his son to clean up the mess himself as he had other matters to attend to. It turns out "servant" meant "Scion" in this case and when Eustis confronted the agent, it turned into quite a heated battle, with Eustis just barely coming out on top of the situation. A few weeks later, he managed to join up with The Rising Stars as one of their most volatile members.


        Even before his ascension to godhood, Eustis has always been a Rebel. He despises expectations and scoffs as social norms, instead preferring freedom and expression over conformity. As a Scion, Eustis made it his mission to bring about change to the social order. Sometimes this change was for the better, toppling centuries of outdated prejudice and superstition. Sometimes this change was disastrous, plunging society into self destructive anarchy. He is not particularly skilled in battle, but a silver tongue can accomplish more than any mere sword. He slinks through the background, whispering into the ears of kings and commoners alike, encouraging them to break from tradition and express themselves regardless of what others may think.


        In his full godly form, Eustis appears as an overweight man just shy of obesity and dressed in ancient Greek fashion. His hair is black and braided into a pony tail which he often drapes over one of his shoulders as his preferred hair style. Despite his bulk, Eustis' skin appears to be clean of the usual blemishes that comes with such a body type, instead being clean and smooth to the touch. It is also worth noting, that he never wears undergarments, making his tunic more revealing that others might feel comfortable with.


        As a member of the Dodekatheon, Eustis does not recognize biological Scions. This is not because he is a virgin (far from it, actually), but rather due in part to his own preference. Eustis is homosexual and will only (willingly) take men as his lovers. Because of this, he prefers to adopt unclaimed Scions rather than conceive his own. When or if Eustis does produce a child of his own, he refuses to take responsibility for it, claiming that the child's mother likely date raped him to make it.



        Powers: Epic Charisma, Epic Stamina, Epic Manipulation, Arete, Chaos, Health, Animal (Swine)


        Abilities: Art, Empathy, Presence, Athletics, Politics, Larceny


        Rivals: Aphrodite, Hera, Zeus, Set, Damballa

            Fróði
            Role: God of Prophecy
            Pantheon: The Aesir
            Parent: Odin
            Nature: Visionary

            Info: Fróði was born in Norway as Njord Sigurdsson, as the spouse of a successful businesswoman who had a short but passionate affair with a Swedish estate agent who had lost one eye in a hunting accident. His mother, Sigurd Halvarsdottir, didn't love Njord. In fact she despised him, for his father had left her just shortly before he had been born and he served as a living remainder of that. While the boy never failed to receive proper education and a home, he found himself lacking attention and care and thus drifted towards many occult sects and opinions, making well-versed in both Nordic Religions and Hermeticism, among other things. He still was terribly angry with the world at large, however, and thus joined a spiritual successor of the Thule Society.

            Being forced not only by his criminal record that piled up during his time in the neo-fascistic brotherhood but also by his mother threatening to cut off his financial support soon he moved to Germany, learning the language much faster than even he had believed possible and decided to study German philology, Religious Studies and History in Berlin. He had to support himself, of course, earning money by working in a library and, occasionally, reading Tarot Cards to rich idiots. He was sixteen years old at that time, having skipped quite a few grades.

            As Fate willed it, Njord met his family during one of his library jobs. His boss had soon realized that the boy had a knack for finding old and valuable books that sold well to a certain clientle, not knowing that this clientle mostly consisted of young Njord himself. He had sent him to an antique store in the centre of Berlin where an old man was supposedly sitting on a proverbial goldmine. This old man turned out to be Njords father, Odin, and he told his son exactly what he was. He also foretold his future and saw great deeds there, skipping on only one detail. A detail that hit Njord on his way home in the shape of an out-of-control truck which almost ran the boy over. Njord could save himself, but his eyes he could not. Two shards of a nearby window hit him in the face, blinding him forever.

            Filled with regret over what had happened, Njord decided to get back at Odin for knowing. He guessed, correctly as Odin himself confirmed, that the All-Father knew fully well about his impending blindness but chose not to warn him for it was inevitable. Not content with this explanation, Njord decided to learn the ways of Fate and how to bend them to his will.
            During the next years he grew more and more prominent both as an eccentric owner of an Occult Store and a seer who sometimes made disturbingly accurate predictions. After predicting almost every event of the year 2013 correctly, including several figures such as people to die during a Hurricane, he decided to set out to further is Legend, assuming that he needed more prominence to become a god.

            Nowadays, he is Fróði the Blind, who yet sees more than anyone, safe perhaps for the Norns. Living in a small cave consiting of dead roots from dead Yggdrasil, he is often visited for his wisdom and knowledge of the hidden workings of Fate, but equally feared for the same reasons. Yet, his goal still remains unfulfilled: Changing the destiny of the Gods themselves to finally make his father admit that he was wrong. It is a big goal, but Fróði is certain of his abilities.

            Associated Powers: Epic Charisma, Epic Intelligence, Epic Perception, Jotunblut, Magic, Mystery, Prophecy, Star
            Associated Abilities: Academics, Awareness, Empathy, Investigation, Occult, Presence
            Rivals: Odin; Bastet, Apollo, Tezcatlipoca, Tsuki-Yomi, Legba

                Drakon Riga: Scion of Hermes


                Details: One could say that even before his visitation, Drakon was already a legend in the making. From a young age, Drakon had a desire to get out and explore the world, like his mysterious father who was nowhere to be found. In an effort to track him down, Drakon joined the secret services when he became old enough, hoping to gain access to their information in between missions. Of course being a god, it would have been impossible for him to actually locate Hermes, had he not wanted to be found.

                To call it a visitation is a tad inaccurate, for it was Drakon who came to visit Hermes. Or rather came to arrest him as the trickster God was assuming one of his mortal disguises at the time. It failed, of course, resulting in Drakon being caught in a very elaborate death trap, with Hermes explaining his true origins to him and then promptly leaving him to his fate. With his Scion powers now awakened, Drakon managed to escape the trap, but his father was already long gone and Thaddeus Zurlo was there waiting for him with a job offer. Drakon accepted the proposal, joining The Rising Stars and completing the group.


                During his time as a Scion, Drakon was the ideal super spy. He was charming, he was quick witted, he was a certified bad ass, and he was virtually impossible to kill with anything short of open combat. He's lost count of how many times his enemies have left him in a death trap, only for him to escape using some convoluted plan or gadget that Karena whipped up for him.


                In his full godly form, Drakon is a very handsome man with sharp, angular features, wavy brown hair and deep blue eyes. He prefers to dress in three piece suits even when on Olympus and carries a variety of accessories which are actually Relics custom built for his line of work. These accessories change by the day, but commonly include a pocket watch, cuff links, a monocle and a broach.


                Although Drakon is sexually involved with Eustis, he is not strictly homosexual and sometimes conceives Scions of his own. Luckily for him, Eustis isn't as hung up about the who monogamy thing as the rest of the pantheon, which (usually) prevents him from taking vengeance out on the innocent Scions.

                The children of Drakon share their father's gifts of being both socially graceful and physically capable. They often find themselves joining covert organizations, operating behind the enemy lines or even within their very ranks to take down the Titans while their attention is turned elsewhere.



                Powers: Epic Dexterity, Epic Intelligence, Epic Wits, Epic Charisma, Arete, Star


                Abilities: Athletics, Brawl, Stealth, Awareness, Survival, Marksmanship

                    Kollsvein: Scion of Loki


                    AKA: Kollsvein Fenrisson, Alex Rutfol


                    Details: A God of hunting and an adoptive Scion of Loki. Kollsvein is Loki's grandchild, born to a same sex couple who used a donation from a sperm bank to conceive a child. What they were unaware of going in was the donation came from the dread wolf, Fenris. Loki had placed it among the others as both a prank and an experiment to see whether his monstrous children were capable of producing Scions. Much to his delight, it worked and nine months later the couple gave birth to Alex Rutfol.


                    Much like the Fenris who indirectly spawned him, Alex grew rapidly during childhood. He entered puberty nearly two years early and it hit him like a truck.By the time he was 15, he was almost 6 feet tall and growing a beard. He also exhibited a lot of pent up frustration and continuously challenged other kids his age to physical contests which he unsurprisingly won. That was when Loki decided to pay him a visit at home, revealing both to Alex and to his parents that he was the one who had donated the sample, though he kept the more exact details about where it came from to himself until he and Alex were alone.


                    After learning the truth about himself and reading the prophecy of Ragnarok, Alex developed feelings of apathy towards everything. Yes, the world is doomed, but the prophecy also says that the surviving gods will create a new world to replace the old one. If the world is going to be reborn, does it really matter if the Titans destroy this one? Alex doesn't think so, and instead has decided to have as much fun as possible before the end of the world happens and (almost) everybody dies.


                    Rather than spend his time attempting to preserve the world, Alex (going by his mortal name at the time) sought to increase his legend through trials of skill and strength. He did not battle the Titanspawn because he felt what they did was wrong, he fought them because he wanted to prove he could. Each victory strengthened his reputation and his sense of accomplishment. Unfortunately for Loki, however, Alex was also shown to display Fenris' sense of cunning and was able to recognize a bad deal when it was presented to him. When faced with an open challenge, Alex demanded special assurances to guarantee the competition was fair. If it was not, his dishonorable opponent was sure to lose a hand (or some other body part) for daring to trick him.


                    One of Kollsvein's most noticeable characteristics is his wolf fetish, being fascinated with the creatures to the point of having mastered the Animal Purview. Both as a Scion and as a God, he bore no animosity towards the Fenrir as long as they did not attack him first. The more paranoid members of the Aesir fear that Kollsvein is going to side with the Titans at the time of Ragnarok, perhaps even helping Fenris break the binds that have kept him subdued for thousands of years. Alas, they do not have any magical thread to restrain their newest member and even if they did, he is likely to not fall for the same trick.


                    In his true godly form, Kollsvein looks more like some ancient savage. He is very large, standing around 7 feet tall and with powerfully built muscles. He is estimated to weigh around 250 pounds, though has not been properly weighed in. He is also very hairy, sporting thick patches of body hair on his chest, forearms, face and shins. His eyes are bright green and his hair is dark black in color. His moustache is braided on both sides and he has a third braid in the center of his beard. He wears traditional viking garb, fashioned out of wolf pelts, a constant reminder of his ancestry.


                    Scions of Kollsvein are noticeably rare in number. His viewpoint on Ragnarok has done little to stress the importance of giving visitations to his offspring. Instead, he passes them off to his grandfather who is all too happy to acquire free pawns in his grand schemes. When he does choose to accept responsibility for such a child, they often exhibit a sense of invincibility, overcoming many physical trials that other Scions deem impossible. Watching his Scions prevail against stacked odds brings great pleasure to Kollsvein, who raises a mug in their honor.



                    Associated Powers: Epic Manipulation, Epic Strength, Epic Wits, Epic Stamina, Jotunblut, Chaos, Fire, Health, Animal (Wolf)


                    Abilities: Survival, Empathy, Brawl, Animal Ken, Fortitude, Stealth

                        Wenhua Shizhe: Scion of Sun Wukong

                        Role: Goddess of Immigration

                        Details: Wenhua Shizhe, or "Bringer of Culture" is one of the rare members of the Celestial Bureaucracy who was not raised in Chinese surroundings. In fact, she's not even of Chinese ethnicity with the exception of her father being a member of their pantheon. Born and raised in America, Wenhua's mortal name was Hailey Witt. From an early age, she possessed a wander lust and voiced her desire to travel all over the world some day. Social studies was her favorite subject in school, learning the history and customs of foreign nations, which she took as a means of preperation for her journey one day. Unfortunately reality so rarely often lives up to fantasy. By the time she was 19 and graduated from school, she came to realize that her job had not provided nearly enough financial income for a trip across the world. That was when her heavenly father came to her and revealed the truth.


                        Sun Wukong told Hailey that if she wanted to travel the world, all she had to do was start walking. After overcoming the initial shock of a large, talking monkey claiming to be her father, she decided to take up his advice and set out on her journey two days later, having quit her job when her last paycheck came in. As of that moment, she was technically a hobo, sleeping wherever she could find shelter and eating wherever she could find sustenance. It was far from the glamorous journey she had imagined growing up, but the machinations of Fate kept her going forward whenever she considered turning back.


                        To this day, both Wenhua and Sun Wukong affectionately refer to her rise to godhood as the "Journey to the East" in parallel to Sun Wukong's own adventures. During her time as Hailey Witt, she traveled almost entirely by foot from America to China, encountering many Titanspawn plots and challenges along the way. By the time she became a demigod, she arrived in Brazil, to which she was forced to take a ship to the next continent over. Afterwhich, she made her way towards China, crossing through many small countries in the process.


                        As a goddess, Wenhua Shizhe is part of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs who are tasked with dealing with outside cultures and ethnic minorities. Wenhua's job is to bring the teachings of China to the "uncivilized barbarians" that is the rest of the world. As a goddess of immigration, she is technically supposed to teach her fellow deities the ways of foreign cultures, but in practice the Celestial Bureaucracy has little regard for such things given their high stance on Chinese customs and values. When they do listen, it is usually in an effort to learn more about potential weaknesses or to use as proof of the superiority of Chinese culture throughout the world.


                        Perhaps it is because Wenhua is not actually Chinese or perhaps it is in reverence to her divine father (possibly both), but Wenhua Shizhe does not take a human form when spending time in supernatural realms. Instead, her godly form takes the appearance of an anthropomorphic spider monkey of great size. Her limbs and body are proportionate to those of a human and she stands on her hind legs in a bipedal posture. Her body is mostly covered in red fur with some black around the paws, face and tail. Her clothing (at least in this form) is minimalistic, providing just enough to cover her modesty. When asked why she doesn't dress more appropriately for a woman, she kindly suggests that you try wearing an entire outfit when you're covered in fur.


                        When dealing with the mortal World, Wenhua always takes the form of a foreigner who sympathizes with Chinese culture. Sometimes she serves as an educator or a translator for those planning to visit China. Other times she personally escorts foreigners on their own journey to the east, being the captain of a transport ship, an airline pilot or even just a cross country bus driver. Although her official role is to indoctrinate the barbarians to Chinese ways, she secretly enjoys learning about new cultures as well.


                        Due to her appointed role, Wenhua refuses to have Scions with Chinese men, instead preferring to take foreigners as her lovers. Many within the beauracy have voiced their displeasure that she is sharing her divine ichor with barbarians, but she has tantively explained her actions as placing Bureaucracy Scions on foreign soil and giving China a foothold in those countries. What she does not reveal is her plan to diversify the pantheon by elevating the "barbarians" to godhood within the xenophobic Bureaucracy


                        Associated Powers: Epic Dexterity, Epic Strength, Epic Manipulation, Animal (Monkeys), Taiyi, Psychopomp, Earth


                        Abilities: Survival, Larceny, Athletics, Politics, Brawl, Investigation

                            Haldris: Scion of Hades
                            Previously: Theo (Theophilus) DeMarcus

                            Role: God of Murder, Sacrifice, and Redemption

                            Details: Unlike his father, Haldris is neither patient nor contemplative in his plans. He is direct, excitable, and quick to action. He makes no move halfway, and leaves no business unfinished. That is not to say he is unintelligent or naive, simply that when he knows what must be done, it will be he who sees it through. As God of murder as well as redemption, Haldris plays a key role in the end of many lives, as well as seeing sinners brought back to justice.

                            As a mortal man, Theo was a homicide detective in Chicago, a city which guaranteed both job security and sleepless nights for him. When his mother was murdered in her own home, Theo was met at the scene by a tall, gaunt man who turned out to be his long lost father, known only by the checks that rolled in each month until his eighteenth birthday. After introductions, Hades laid out clear instructions to find those who did this to his once love, and bring them to justice however he was able. Not long after completing this mission, Theo joined his first band, and quickly made a name for himself by killing two Norse children of the wolf Fenris in as many days.

                            Theo eventually left his Band, wandering the world instead, returning restless dead to their respective destinations. In his travels he found himself in his own Underworld, and happened upon the shears of Atropos herself, later claiming them as his own birthright after the goddess reclaimed her ability over Fate. This became an onyx blade of terrible power which he continues to wield with devastating skill. Through this journeying he found he enjoyed sending the living to their final end as much as those already perished, and so became a talented warrior in addition to a skilled ppsychopomp.

                            After his ascension, Haldris became gaunt and pale like his father, though not as thin. He appears athletic and youthful, though his eyes are those of a man of many lifetimes, even appearing as empty sockets at times. His hair is raven black, and falls neatly to his shoulders. Instead of a traditional toga, he wears a cloak made from the water of Styx itself, fashioned for him by the goddess Hecate. It is gray and any who look upon it long enough begin to make out the faces of tortured souls trapped in the fabric, fleeting as a trick of the eyes. He is never without his relic blade, which is able to change its shape to meet his needs, with the exception of always being black as the grave, and carrying a lethal edge.

                            His trips to the world are always brief and direct, and almost never for recreation. On the off chance he has an extended stay, it would likely be in the form of some sort of authority figure with access to death records. It would not be out of the question for him to take the role of prison warden, psychiatrist, or AA/NA coordinator, as a way to bring those who have fallen from grace to recovery.

                            Scions of Haldris are quick to action, and quicker to violence. They are able to immediately and efficiently size up the best solution to a problem, whether it be through fighting or negotiation. His children are rare, but those he has, he maintains a fixed role for within their lives. His gifts to them are practical and directed toward their individual needs, but his direction is firm, and he is quick to set his children right when he sees them heading down the wrong path.

                            Associated Powers: Epic Dexterity, Epic Wits, Arete, Death, Earth, Justice, Magic, Psychopomp

                            Abilities: Awareness, Empathy, Marksmanship, Melee, Occult, Presence

                                Myrminki: Scion of Hera and Gaia

                                Previously: Kyle Junior

                                Role: Avatar of Terra representing survival.


                                Details: Yet another example of the very long list of enemies created through the Dodekatheon's own hubris. Born under the name Kyle Junior, he was to be a Scion of Hera. But when Zeus found out about his wife's infidelity, he was furious and targeted the bastard for his revenge. On the night Hera was planning to visit him, Zeus sent forth a thunder storm that burned Kyle's house down, killing his mortal parents and burning him across 70% of his body. Hera was also forced to renounce her claim to him, lest she provoke Zeus further. But Kyle would be miraculously saved from a life as a cripple. Not by the Gods, but by the Titans; for he was visited that night in the hospital by Gaia, appearing in his dreams to explain she had saved him from the flames and offered him greater benefits if he would merely serve her. Kyle agreed and woke up in bed to find he had regained most of his motor skills and brain functions, as well as having his Scion powers awakened.

                                Though he was only 16 years old at the time of his visitation, Kyle displayed a surprisingly high gift for coordinating others. Joining a band of other Scions who had sided with Terra, he demonstrated his skill by leading 20 mercenaries into battle and later Myrmidons as his powers continued to grow. The carapace armor granted to him by Hera caused his other bandmates to refer to him as the "Ant King" first as a joke, and then more seriously as his Legend continued to grow. Over time, Kyle began to accept the title and even fashion himself around it. During his rise to apotheosis, he became increasingly ant-like both in form and in function.

                                After achieving godhood, Kyle allowed himself to be absorbed completely by the essence of Terra and was reborn as one of its avatars. Now going by the name of Myrminki, he embodies the concept of survival, when species of animals conquer and control their environment. He is the king of all species of ants, with every queen of every colony serving as his harem. As an Avatar, Myrminki has one goal in mind for the World.

                                To conquer and control

                                With his army of ants, he marches upon the World seeking to assimilate others into his hive. Those taken as slaves become Myrmidons, those found unworthy serve as food for the billions of insects he commands! Territories which are controlled by Myrminki are easily recognized, just look for the impossibly large ant mounds!


                                Myrminki can take three forms all of which are styled after the ant. He can appear as a regular sized ant, or a gigantic one of near typhonian size. But his most common form is the bipedal, four armed ant man, which he uses for associating with people or battling agents of the Gods. While in this form, Myrminki's body is covered in a protective coating of chitinous armor, with three fingered claws on each of his four hands. His head is that of an ant, scaled to proportion with his man sized body and sporting wicked mandibles from his mouth. As befitting of a "royal" ant's station, he also possesses a set of gossamer wings allowing him to take to the air when when he so desires, giving him the advantage of mobility over his more grounded foes.


                                As an Avatar, Myrminki no longer functions the same way as regular Scions in terms of ability and skill. However, prior to apotheosis, Kyle's specialized in the use of the Animal (ant), Fertility and Earth purviews and these continue to remain his favorites after becoming a Titan.

                                    Rutfol: Scion of Loki
