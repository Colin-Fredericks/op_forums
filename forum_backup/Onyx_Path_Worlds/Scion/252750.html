    Scion fans! All of you-

    09-08-2014, 07:30 PM
    Which is really what I want to make mention of here: people who post here are all Scion fans or are curious about the game and might become fans. Please remember that common ground when posting.

    Scion is the kind of game that people come to and get involved with from a lot of different directions. This is a good thing.

    Scion is the kind of game that people can and do play in different ways. This also is a good thing.

    Scion is the kind of game that people of different backgrounds and interests in mythology, from loving the Thor and Hercules movies all the way to folks with advanced degrees in mythology and religion, can enjoy. This is also also a good thing.

    Scion is just that big in scope and theme and possibility. And when you hold it in your hands (book or tablet), Scion is yours. We provide the framework in the form of a game book, it is you who decide how to play with, expand, or focus on one small aspect of, what we provide - depending on the games you want to play or stories you want to tell.

    So with all that in mind, please consider how you respond to other Scion fans' posts. A post that says the poster has found new info on how the ancient Scandinavians worshiped a different aspect of Thor is not a threat to you if in your Scion you have been using the Marvel movie version of Asgard. A poster who loves the idea of a version of Scion where you play the children of cereal mascots is not going to force your by-the-book Scion game to change. You are not defending a pure Scion, or a more expansive Scion, or any kind of Scion that you prefer, because Scion does not need to be defended. All you are doing is making another Scion fan feel bad by hammering away at their ideas.

    So, we're all done with that sort of posting.

    From here on in, the moderators will be looking with a jaundiced eye at posts that run counter to the original intent of the thread, and handing out thread suspensions if they determine that you have thread-crapped: basically, if you posted counter to that intention. And all the other rules of the Onyx Path forums still apply as well.

    Additionally, we suggest that if you are starting a new thread that you write a bit at the beginning what your intention with the thread is so that the mods have a better chance of keeping the posts on target. Doesn't have to be a manifesto, it could be as simple as " In-depth analysis of Trickster myths. PHD not required, but would help.", "Having fun with new powers for Greek Gods, not trying to emulate real myths", "The Transformers as Scions. I want to explore a totally different rule-set and setting variant". If you read one of those intention blurbs and grow incensed with white-hot fury, do not post in there because you won't be helping.

    This forum and its posters, here and at WW, have kept Scion alive for years and years, and we at Onyx Path appreciate that more than you can know. We don't want to perma-ban anyone who helped get us here with their love and enthusiasm for Scion. Now, it's time to make sure this forum is a place where Scion fans can freely post their ideas, all of them, and by doing that you will help us grow this new edition.

    Thanks!

                                                      There is a very big difference between personal attacks against other posters and criticism of ideas.

                                                      The Native American Pantheon thread was a fairly contentious discussion, but it didn't get personal. There was no name-calling or insulting. There was, however, strident disagreement with how the original poster was suggesting such an issue be handled. If that kind of discussion isn't welcome, I think that's quite a bad thing.

                                                      Scion, as a game, is more problematic than not. It touches a ton of hot-button issues for many, many people. Religion, history, colonialism, imperialism, syncretism, just to name a few. The perspectives on cultures outside of the Western mainstream is very touchy because all portrayals of those cultures in mainstream media are touchy. There's such a huge history of them being done badly. And yes, I hold Scion's first edition as part of that 'done badly' element. It was extremely poor in its representation of several subjects. Let us not forget that the enemies of the Egyptian Pantheon were suicide bomber angels that served a false God. And that's a fairly mainstream culture!

                                                      When you get into the treatment of places like Africa, South America, North America, Australia and Oceania... well, that's just a sticky subject. Perfectly well meaning people can be absolutely insensitive to the issues surrounding those cultures because they just don't know better. That isn't insulting them. It's hard to be aware of those issues, since lack of awareness isone of the issues!

                                                      So if this rule is in place, and someone starts a thread about how they want to see a Mayincatec Pantheon that represents South America... no one is allowed to point out how incredibly offensive that is to every culture involved? No one can offer resources to educate the posters, to make it a more respectful, representative and well rounded idea? Cause if that's where this is going, that's Laaaaaame.

                                                      I don't care if someone plays to play cereal mascots, or use superheroes. That's their gig. If someone wants to say that they'd like help converting the HP Lovecraft Elder Gods into a Mayan Pantheon.. um, I'd have a few things to say about that. And being able to say them seems fairly important to open discussion, even if it hurts some feelings.
