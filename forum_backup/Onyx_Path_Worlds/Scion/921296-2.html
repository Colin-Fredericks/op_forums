        This is my current character in the game we started a few months ago. Let me know what you think. I based him loosely off of a Paladin class. So far he and his Godly parent that I also made up have seemed to fit very well in our universe.

        Scion of Molly Pitcher - John D'Canter was football jock in high school who just never quite lived up to the potential his mother had in mind for him so during his HS graduation he was visited and told to basically turn his life around and make something of himself. Be the hero she knew he could be. She would accept no less as the Minor Goddess in a newer Pantheon and wanted to prove herself and her bloodline. John immediately enrolled in community college to become a doctor. Alas, the rigors of the curriculum were too much for him so he turned to Firefighting. Physical training proved to be exactly what he was better at and with learning that discipline, he was able to learn the medicinal practices given to him as an emergency responder as well. He worked hard and eventually became a volunteer Fireman in his community of Las Cruces, New Mexico. Armed with a copper fire ax made from pieces of the torch of the Statue of Liberty and an old war hat turned into a firemen's helm, he is now part of a band of Scion's to further defend the helpless from the Titans ho threaten the planet.

                        Originally posted by atamajakki View Post

                        My favorit so far. Does Scion actually have werecritters?
                        Up to an individual storyteller, but, in the very least if you are using canon mythology, there are a lot of similar creatures. Berserkers, depending on how you interpret some Germanic plates, might be shapeshifters. King Lycaon from Greece was cursed in the form of a wolf for hubris. Modern conceptions of Werewolves fall into folklore, and it's never clear if Scion for sure includes such things, but you may as well.

                        There's a bit of a rules hack you can use if you want to play a Scion who is 'genetically' close to some sort of Lesser Immortal. It can function like a Relic mechanically, giving you various bonuses for your Sidhe Blood, Giant's Blood, being related to King Lycaon and having some sort of weird connection to his curse, ect.

                                    Ekadanta Mahadeo was born with everything a Scion could possibly desire in his first life. The legitimate child of Brahma and Sarasvati, Ekadanta was born as a divinity who did not have to go through the years of life and death struggles that less fortunate Scions had to endure to obtain the same benefits. In that time, he was a God of healing and rejuvenation. His ichor empowered tears restored life to what the Titan Vritra had sucked dry. He was loved and celebrated by most who knew him, with the exception of the obvious Titanspawn.

                                    Yes, truly Ekadanta could want for nothing in life. But that was exactly the problem, as Ekadanta came to realize during his meditations one day. He had established himself as a God of healing and compassion, and yet he had never been in a situation in which such things were personally experienced. It was always him offering to help others, not the other way around. After days of constant meditation on the subject, Ekadanta made up his mind.

                                    In order to understand the suffering of others, he would experience it first hand! Much to the chagrin of his fellow Devas, Ekadanta willfully subjected himself to the cycle of reincarnation, transferring his divine essence into an uninhabited mortal newborn. Thus it was that Ekadanta the God, became Ekadanta the Scion, with all memories of his previous life erased from his conscious mind.

                                    His mortal upbringing was the polar opposite of his divine lifestyle. Ekadanta was born into poverty, with his family often scraping to get by. His clothes were tattered scraps, they couldn't afford a proper education, and he was often mocked and abused by others for being inferior to them. Ekadanta had no idea that these terrible living conditions were actually due to his personal requests, and not just some random luck of the draw.

                                    On the day of Ekadanta's 18th birthday, he received a visitation from his original father, Brahma. Although he neglected to mention the part about Ekadanta being a reincarnated god, he did reveal that he was of divine origins and that the Devas had need of him. Vritra, the Titan of Drought. was sucking the World dry, and Ekadanta would be needed to help stop its machinations.


                                    And so Ekadanta became a traveling beggar, wandering the land in search of places touched by Vritra's influence and using his newly awakened powers to restore hope to the people and life to the lands. To aid him in this task, Brahma gave him an ever replenishing water skin full of Ekadanta's own tears from when he was a God. The water skin allows Ekadanta to channel the Water, Health and Fertility purviews, but only works as long as it contains some of his tears inside it. The skin automatically refills itself over night, but allowing it to run dry can leave him powerless for hours while he waits.

                                    He also wears a set of beggar's rags which draw upon his Legend to increase the effectiveness of his Empathy and Integrity. Wearing these shameless garments, he can better understand the suffering of others, while also having the strength to endure his own.

                                            Here's a more fleshed out version of a previous concept I posted.




                                            Stefania Makarovna was born to Russian parents who immigrated to America before she was born, with the only explanation for why they left being that they were fleeing trouble that forced them to abandon their native country. Despite the fact she grew up in the states, her parents tried their best to educate her on Russian folklore. Stefania didn't notice until she was a little older that they appeared to be particularly obsessed with stories of Baba Yaga. Her parents would terrify her with stories about the Iron Hag of Russia, but the impact they had began to diminish as she grew older and more knowledgeable about the world.

                                            On her 16th birthday, she finally received her visitation from Baba Yaga. Most kids expect to receive their first car by the time they turn 16, so what happened instead was quite the shock. When it was time for her to receive her gifts, the Iron Hag of Russia came floating down from the sky in a tea cup that looked to have been plucked from the Disney Land fun ride. After parking it neatly on their front lawn, the old witch greeted her with a "happy birthday" and told her the cup belonged to her now!

                                            And then she promptly devoured Stefania's parents.... It turns out that Baba Yaga was Stefania's grandmother, and her parents had fled Russia in a desperate attempt to escape her. Stefania's mother rejected her Scion heritage and Baba's efforts to train her in the ways of magic. Her mother was an ungrateful child, and ungrateful children must be punished. But this was her first encounter with Stefania, and she hadn't yet given her cause to be offended. And so, standing there caked in her parents' blood, Baba Yaga "offered" to take her on as an apprentice. Using every ounce of willpower not to devolve into a manic wreck after just witnessing the death of her parents, Stefania agreed to the witch's offer.

                                            Since the day of her visitation, Stefania has become known as a student witch, under the mentorship of Baba Yaga, herself. Unlike most Scions who seem to be at least somewhat proud of their heritage, Stefania Makarovna lives in perpetual terror because of it. Her parents did everything they could to warn her about the dangers of her grandmother, and despite the fact she seems like a kindly old woman at face value, Stefania knows that she only maintains this facade as long as she displays proper respect and self control. One wrong move, and she could be the witch's next meal, so she goes to great lengths to appease her mentor whenever possible.

                                            Her one comfort out of all this is the giant tea cup Baba Yaga granted her. It is large enough to fit two average sized adults and is capable of channeling the Wind's Freedom Boon to perform magical flight. Somehow, it responds to her psychic commands while she's inside it, allowing her to pilot the tea cup without a control set up. Aside from its unique design and flight capabilities, the tea cup has the same traits as a motorcycle, but does not require fuel.

                                            Baba Yaga has also bequeathed her with an oversize tea spoon carved from an oak tree. This spoon has the same traits as a Macana when used as a weapon, but it also allows her to channel the Magic, Prophecy and Mystery purviews.


                                            Fortunately, Baba Yaga has not deemed it necessary for Stefania to maintain a constant presence in order to continue her studies, and does allow her periods of exploration in between lessons. During these times, Stefania is all too happy to fly off in her tea cup to participate in her Band's latest exploits, before she must inevitably return to the chicken hut to resume her studies.
