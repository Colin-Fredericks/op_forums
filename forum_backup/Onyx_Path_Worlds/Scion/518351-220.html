                Originally posted by Yig1015w View Post

                Awesome, thats good to hear. Looking forward to seeing all the clarifications in the next draft.

                Also, someone noticed in another thread that there isn't a section in the book detailing a description of each attribute and what 1, 3, and 5 dots mean. (Similar to skills) Will a section like that be added?
                No. That was deliberate.

                When designing the the system I explicitly wanted to get away from Attributes as representing definitive performance benchmarks in a person. Unlike Skills, they don’t have other mechanics keyed to their ratings.

                Storypath isn’t Storyteller or Storytelling, but I’m surprising none if I say many of our design principles were the same. One of the more eye-rolling things I’d see is an insistence that Dexterity 3 meant far less than Dexterity 5, despite it ultimately meaning the loss of two dice - yet one’s supposed to be “I do yoga” versus “I’m an Olympic gymnast”. Storypath does away with that and leaves finer points for your Paths.
