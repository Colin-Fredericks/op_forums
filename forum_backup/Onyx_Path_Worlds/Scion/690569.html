    Your Ascended Scions?

    10-05-2015, 07:29 PM
    So I've had this idea for a while and it won't stop bugging me until I get it out of my system. Anyways, the purpose of this thread is to post ideas / examples of Scions you've had that managed to obtain godhood. I had originally planned to upload 4 characters, but felt that would take up too much space and the ones I have now have gone through several rewrites and imaginings, so I'm having trouble coming up with the other two. So to start off, I'll just post the first two until I complete the others.


    First, let me clarify that I am terrible when it comes to thinking up names, especially foreign language names. I have no idea what would be appropriate for the majority of the pantheons, so I either chose an English name for them, or I used a translator and picked a word or phrase that suited their personality.


    -----------------------------------------------


    Nariko: Goddess of Silence and former scion of Raiden. If her father is the god of lightning, Nariko is the calm before the storm, that first droplet of rain that heralds the beginning of a flood. Nariko follows the path of the shinobi, otherwise known as the ninja. But she is not the black clad, sword swinging assassin that mortals have misenterpreted her kind to be. She embraces the way of nonexistence, channeling the void into herself. In this war against the Titans, Nariko moves unseen and unheard, yet her handiwork is present all the same. She is willing to utilize any means at her disposal to achieve victory over the enemy, regardless if such tactics would sacrifice her honor and pride. As such, she has proven to have the nature of a Rogue, caring little for the rules of fair combat and more on the results of such battles.

    Since her rise to divinity, Nariko has become the patron lady of assassins, saboteurs, the dishonored, the deaf and the mute. Many of her worshipers swear vows of silence to honor their goddess. Her priests communicate with each other almost entirely through whispering. There are no spoken prayers to Nariko, instead her worshipers silently offer their gratitude and requests to her in a state of meditative reverie. Her temples are custom made to either muffle or enhance sounds that occur within it, as a way to either aid their commitment or locate disruptions.

    In her godly form, Nariko never utters a sound, refusing to speak and instead conveying her thoughts in the form of writing or body language. She also covers her entire body from head to toe, leaving her true appearance a mystery to all. Her clothing is the pale blue garb of the traditional ninja, with her face being covered in a featureless mask, save for a pair of slits near the eye sockets allowing her to see. Some legends claim that Nariko has become one with the void itself and that her godly form is actually a set of empty garments wrapped around her divine essence. Regardless if this is true, nobody has yet had the opportunity to strip her down and find out. Few would dare to attempt it anyways, lest they suffer her vengeance.

    In her mortal disguises, Nariko is more prone to social interaction, though remains soft spoken and aloof none the less. She often takes the role of somebody who works behind the scenes, having a part in the action without being openly involved. She has been a financial backer, a consultant, a third party contractor, a private security director and a hitwoman. It is in mortal disguise that Nariko performs most of her visitations, for it is the only time she chooses to actually relay verbal messages.

    Scions of Nariko have a tendency to be introvertive, keeping their emotions suppressed behind a face of disenterest and monotone voice. They do not speak much because they usually don't know what to say or have nothing to add to the conversation. If they had been ordinary mortals, their behavior might render them outcasts by the rest of society for being weird and unfriendly. But as children of the gods, it makes them come across as being men and women of action, preferring to let their deeds speak for them instead of making proud boasts and empty threats!


    Associated Powers: Epic Dexterity, Epic Manipulation, Tsukumo-Gami, Darkness, Illusion, Mystery, Psychopomp

    Abilities: Thrown, Athletics, Medicine, Awareness, Marksmanship, Stealth, Melee

    Rivals: Amaterasu, Vidar, Hachiman, Athena

    -----------------------------------------


    David: God of Audacity and former Scion of Set. Born and raised in America, David knew virtually nothing about the Pesedjet's religious doctrines until after Set visited him. Facing thousands of years worth of culture shock, David earned himself a reputation as a Rebel, constantly acting in defiance of the Pesedjet in favor of his own moral standing and beliefs. His rise to godhood has fed his ego to obnoxious growth. He is loud, proud and cares little for rules and expectations if they conflict with his personal beliefs. He is a potential threat to the strict order the Pesedjet adhere to, which is exactly how Set wants him!

    Since his ascension to godhood, David has become the patron lord of the weak, the crippled, the under privilaged and the boastful. His blind arrogance is both his greatest weakness and his greatest strength. Many times has David overestimated his abilities in this war and landed himself in trouble. However, there are also many times when David has stood his ground, comitting himself whole heartedly to a cause he believes in and facing down the enemy with a fearless expression on his face. Such boldness has inspired the mortals to view him as a role model in the face of adversity and doubt. His fellow Pesedjet, however, view his careless disregard for the rules to be dangerous and unpredictable.

    David enjoys skirting expectations so much that he even began to study the purview of ice and snow just to prove that he could! Disregarding the fact that the Pesedjet is a religion originating from the desert and that such an element is something they would be totally unaccustomed to. Immediately following his ascension, David celebrated his new status by making it snow for seven hours in the Sahara Desert, a 'miracle' which reaffirmed his cult's belief in his divine heritage.

    In his true form, David is the badger headed god, an animal which he adopted as a practical joke but soon became a religious symbol associated with him. His skin is much lighter than most of the Pesedjet, yet still retains a hint of tan from his family ancestry. His clothing is minimalist, consisting of the iconic, white kilt and excessive jewelry associated with males of the pantheon. His personal gemstones are the topaz and the amythest, fastened into his jewelery in a repeated sequence. His body is in great physical condition and nearly flawless in appearance, which further inspires his vanity and pride.

    When in the mortal world, David likes to remain unpredictable. Just when you think you know what kind of disguise he'll take, he likes to pull the bait and switch, choosing a form or lifestyle nobody could have guessed. Regardless of what form he chooses, however, his strong will in the face of oppression remains. David has been a grand architect, a controversial celebrity, a rebel group leader and there was even a time he disguised himself as new convert to his own cult!

    Scions of David are a wild and passionate bunch who's actions are viewed with both admiration and unease depending on who's watching them. They act and speak with such determination that it is easy to be swept up in their charisma and follow them. However, it is also easy for them to suffer from over inflated egoes and lead such admirers to ruin if they aren't careful. David's Scions are excelent motivators who certainly know how to give a rallying speech in battle, you just don't want them to be placed in charge of your main force. Because chances are they'll be the 'no quarter' type of general who fights to the last man.


    Associated Powers: Epic Charisma, Epic Strength, Animal (Badger), Heku, Chaos, Frost, War, Earth

    Abilities: Larceny, Control, Craft, Integrity, Brawl, Presence

    Rivals: Horus, Tyr, Autum-Re, Ogoun, Tezcatlipoca

          In the game we ran (it took 3 GMs working in tandem), we got several characters to godhood, but only a few were actually God of Something (i.e. had maxed out a purview), since it’s been years I can’t remember all the associated powers...and this is not a complete list, there's gonna be more.

          Askrir, Arranger of the Gods; Former Scion of Loki.

His connections allowed him to position himself to; Convince Fenrir not to take part in the battle of Ragnarok, by finding him a place he could roam free. Convince Jormungandr (at another god’s urging) to Invade the Drowned Road along with the forgotten titan Okeanos and take over the power structure, replacing him with Ahzi-Dahaka the minion of Ahriman. Trick that strange titan avatar Tuisco into filling in for Tyr, and thus take over Tyr’s doom, all so that Tyr could arbitrate the duel between Loki and Heimdall, forcing Loki to fight fair (and thus fail to kill Heimdall).

          Cait Llyd, King of the Tuatha; Former Scion of Lugh.
          Started a literal musical band of scions and audaciously used his music career to gather a huge following, seeding his actual adventures in as part of his epic rock ballads. Ascended to the throne after leading the battle to retake Mag Mell as a demigod, and then leading numerous successful campaigns against the Titans menacing other pantheons. Also carried the most geasa ever known to be in effect on a single person without them self-destructing.

          Athanasia, Goddess of Intrigue and Diplomacy; former Scion of Hera
          Formed a cult of personailty to herself culminating in an actual cult at demigodhood in order to accumulate influence throughout the modern world. Embarked on diplomatic missions to various magical beings in order to build up allies for the gods. Worked out a deal to secure the loyalty of the displaced titans of light (led by Hyperion) by assisting with the dethroning of Aten. Embarked upon a quest to restore Niflheim the titanrealm of frost to active status (in order to balance Muspelheim), and earned the loyalty of Utgard-loki (its new dominant avatar) and all of the Jotnar. Successfully pled the case of the next generation gods at the courts of Anu, winning the divine right of rule from the overtitan of Justice/Order.

          Iukine-Rahiti, God of Excessive Force and Destruction; Former Scion of Pele
          Destroyed: facility devoted to producing corrupt titan-scions, a cult dedicated to unleashing aspects of Vritra into the world, in incursion of drowned road beings (deep ones essentially) into Chicago by luring them all into a club and detonating it (and the city block), Several battalions of Firbolg at Mag Mell, a section of Kronus’s forces invading Mt. Olympus (singlehandedly), an army of rakshasa attacking Mt. Meru (along with some other gods).

          Damali-Anshah, God of Dreams; Former Scion of Vayu
          Set up a network of informants and brokers to support all scions in the war all over the world. Established a network of temple/museums that were mystically charged to act as touchstones all around the world while preserving the ancient cultures of ALL the pantheons. Collected all the dead mortal souls who were not corrupted into specters or converted to the worship of the gods into a permanent dreamworld where they were allowed to live on. Created a complex geomantic working to funnel the power of legend up to the overworld to assist all of the gods (and fuel his own projects). Conceived of the plan to substitute Ahzi-Dahaka for Jormungandr at Ragnarok (using Askrir) in order to twist fate to the gods favor, and arranged for the return of Cerebus and Orthrus to Hades in order to secure his Underworld and free up his forces.

          Andariel, Ascended titanspawn of Night; Scion of Nyx (neutral titaness )
          Acted as a spy/double agent unknowingly, used by Nyx to unravel the plots of rival titans along with whatever hapless scions he happened to be with, then eventually gave himself over willingly once he learned her ultimate plan was just to free her husband Erebus from bondage and restore him to prominence. Loaned out to the gods on occasion for their “assistance” with this plan, mostly at his request, eventually acted as a liaison to the gods for the titans of Night.

              Jaina & Ranko: Twin Deities of Rain and former Scions of Poseidon. Two Scions conceived by the same god on the same day in the same womb, Jaina and Ranko share an unusual fate. Only capable of accessing their full power when in proximity to each other, they are essentially one god with two bodies. When seperated from each other, their power is only at half strength, with their abilities and purviews divided between them. But when they get close, they can draw on the other's abilities to compliment their own. Jaina and Ranko also share a form of twin telepathy, experiencing each others pain both emotional and physical. It is believed that if one were to die, they would both perish.

              As deities, Jaina is a compassionate and gentle goddess of rain and harvest. Ranko, however, is a wrathful and and savage god of monsoons and floods. When dealing with others, it is typically Jaina who takes the lead in matters of social grace and etiquette. But when it's time to lay down divine retribution, Ranko takes charge with a mighty storm! They are possessed of a dual nature, with Jaina as a Caregiver and Ranko as a Cynic.

              Since their ascension to godhood, Jaina and Ranko have always been depicted as a pair. Their temples never have shrines or statues devoted to one or the other, but worship both in unison. Their cultists pray to Jaina to bring them bountiful harvests and fair weather, while fearing her brother's vengeance should they offend her. Offerings are made in the form of a share of the crops grown from Jaina's life giving rains, placating the twins with a home grown feast!

              In their godly forms, Jaina and Ranko are nearly identical twins with the exception of obvious gender palettes. They appear as young adults, possibly in their mid 20's. Their hair is dark black and grown to the shoulders in length. Their eyes are dark green and they wear garments fashioned out of the bones and scales of various species of fish. As befits her gentle disposition, Jaina's body is fair to look upon and soft to the touch, while her more violent brother is ruggedly handsome with firm muscle tone.

              When in the mortal world, Jaina and Ranko are nearly inseperable, mostly because they're afraid of being caught off guard by random Titan attacks. Their disguises tend to resemble family businesses or close knit partnerships. The sister acting as the public face of the company while her brother deals with security and legal actions to keep things running smoothly. Despite their closeness, they usually conceive Scions individually unless they're in a particularly kinky mood and feel like sharing their lovers.

              Regardless of which twin the Scion is descended from, they have the capacity to inherit either of their abilities and often inherit a mixture of both. This further leads credence to the idea that Jaina and Ranko are spiritually one deity, instead of consolidating power between two. It is also common for them to share dual personalities, taking on an almost completely altered disposition according to their mood at the time. They can be welcoming and friendly when in a good mood, or violent and destructive when provoked. All in all, the children of these two gods are people who's good side it is best to stay on.

              Associated Powers:

              Jaina - Epic Charisma, Water, Arete, Fertility, Animal (Horse)

              Ranko - Epic Strength, Sky, Arete, War, Animal (Bull)

              Abilities:

              Jaina - Presence, Animal Ken, Empathy

              Ranko - Thrown, Melee, Athletics

                  Name: Kujaku no Oji

                  Nature: Perfectionist

                  Info: God of Beauty and a former Scion of Hachiman. Abandoning his mortal name upon his ascension to godhood, it is believed that only those who personally knew him before such an event remember who he used to be. He is now known to the world as Kujaku no Oji, otherwise known as Prince of Peacocks. In Kujaku's mind, he is a flawless expression of beauty and grace, taking great joy in flaunting his perfection in front of others. Almost everything not following the Amatsukami's religious culture is hideous and deserving of mockery so far as he's concerned. He also feels that warriors who rely on brute strength or endless stamina are unrefined oafs who have neither poise nor elegance and he is fond of taunting them on the subject unless he believes they can defeat him in battle.

                  In appearance, Kujaku no Oji comes across as being sexually androgynous to most who look upon him. He possesses a boyish physique, but is graced with effeminate beauty and charm. His eyes are bright green while his dark hair remains shiny and smooth nearly all the time. His manner of dress is often flashy and over stylized, exhibiting bright colors and eye catching patterns. More often than not, such attire looks like it was designed for the opposite sex, yet Kujaku flaunts it around like it was custom ordered! His speech is perfectly articulated with a dignified tone and a high laugh that is a few notes higher pitched than it should be.

                  Being a child of Hachiman, Kujaku no Oji is no stranger to combat and uses a two handed battle fan that has been painted like a peacock's tail. But when he engages the field, he does so with lethal precision. While his opponent is busy pounding their chest and making grand boasts of their power in battle, Kujaku is already planning which vital areas to target first. A duel with Kujaku is often ended so quickly that his opponent doesn't even realize what happened until it's too late. When facing multiple foes at once, Kujaku's movements resemble dancing as he weaves in an out of range, striking his opponents in the most effecient manner before moving onto the next.

                  Being as vainglorious as Kujaku is, he demands that his Scions exhibit the same level of elegance as himself. If he perceives his child to be ugly or ill mannered, he will disavow his affiliation with them, letting them continue believing they are ordinary mortals unless another god chooses to adopt them. The ones that he does claim undergo strict mentorship in an effort to guide them as close to perfection as is possible. This process has a tendency to leave his Scions overconfident in their abilities, but they perform so well that this arrogance isn't completely unjustified.

                  Associated Powers: Epic Charisma, Epic Dexterity, Epic Wits, Tsukumo-Gami, Animal (Peacock), Health, Sun, Star

                  Assosciated Abilities: Presence, Athletics, Melee, Command, Art, Integrity

                          Lypeos/Theraos
                          Role: God of Social Events and Misfortune/God of Assassins
                          Pantheon: Dodekatheon
                          Parent: Diyonisos/Hades
                          Nature: Bon Vivant/Competitor

                          Info: Lypeos and Theraos are linked together more closely than any other pair of gods has ever been and to speak of one is to speak of the other inevitably. Their mother was a successful actress in her days, being called the "Muse of Cinema" by many and apparently rightfully so for not one but two Greek Gods took a liking to her beauty. And so both Diyonisos, God of Wine and Hades, Lord of the Underworld, approached the mortal in different guises, Diyonisos as a male stripper at her Bachelor Party, Hades as her soon-to-be Husband in the same night. And, unknowingly, she thus became pregnant with not one but two Scions of different gods.

                          Fate, however, intervened and decided that only one Scion was to live. Diego Aramando was born a healthy and strong baby boy, while his half-brother Lysandros died before he was even born, strangled by his own umbirical cord. He was buried shortly after his birth and Diego never received word of his stillborn sibling. His parents agreed that it would be to his best.
                          Diego Aramando grew up to be a handsome young man, a gifted actor and genius vintner who was both admired for his skills on the vineyard and his presence in front of the camera or in the round of the theater, never failing to amaze a crowd. He was equally known for his almost supernatural streak of accidents, however, which came to haunt him more than once. Whenever Diego took up a glass it would end up shattered on the floor, any tuxedo he donned would become tattered before he could undress again and whenever he turned on the radio it just started to air the 5-hour Justin Bieber special and the toggle button broke. Still, he lived a happy life, never being bitter over those "Fun little things" and hopped from Job to Job, working as a winegrower, a model, an actor and a male escort just to name a few.

                          Meanwhile, Lysandros spent his first years quite literally in hell, being cradled by Persephone whenever she was around or shown the workings of the Tartatos by his father, who for some reason had taken pity on his infant soul - or maybe just saw it as a useful tool to execute his agenda. Lysandros didn't receive a new body, that was beyond what his father was able to do for him, but he got taken in and trained by the finest mortals that ever died. Combat, tactics and weaponry were taught to him like reading and writing and before other Scions even got their visitation he was already a well-versed killer, capable of overcoming any mortal enemy - and some supernatural ones as well. At least in theory. But he lacked a body to execute his will and that was where his brother came into the picture again.

                          Diego received his visitation after a particularly smashed night, waking up in an apartment that clearly wasn't his own with a handsome young man who made pancakes - wearing nothing bot an apron. After a short series of misunderstandings, the man revealed himself to be Diegos real father, God of Wine, and that Diegos continous unlucky streak was in no small part thanks to him. But then again, so were his good looks, his stamina and his charismatic personality, so no hard feelings. Among the presents Diyonisos had gotten for his son was a small puch containing five coins which were called "Oboli", originally intended as a token for Charon to do his job. Whenever he was in trouble, Diyonisos told him, he was to place one of those coins under his tongue and hope for the best.

                          Naturally, those coins had been given to Diyonisos by Hades after a long night and allowed Lysandros to take over. Every time Diego put one of those coins in his mouth, Lysandros' Soul took over his body and was able to do as he pleased for six hours before he had to retread into the realms of the dead once more. And Diego was none the wiser, remembering nothing afterwards.
                          Of course, he found out eventually and was shocked by the atrocities his brother had committed. For a long time he tried to ignore the issue, not swallowing those vile coins again and even throwing them away. But Lysandros found his ways into Diegos mind and finally managed to take him over by force, pushing his brothers soul out of his body. It took them three years to overcome their rivalry and finally get Lysandros a body of his own, a statue crafed by Pygmalion himself.

                          Nowadays, Lypeos is the ideal picture of a greek man which short, curled hair, a perfectly toned body and dark complexion. He wears mostly loose clothing and is almost never seen alone, a cheerful crowd following him everywhere he goes. Also, he tends to break things a lot, although he has it under control by now. At least partially.

                          Theraos on the other hand is a fearsome man, his face always hidden by a mask or shadows. He carries two guns and his signature weapon, gloves with razor-sharp fingernails, everywhere he goes. He passes unnoticed and kills silently, for he is the executive arm of Hades, getting those souls that have evaded his fathers grasp. He still uses Lypeos' mind for that end, sometimes, but they have a mutual understanding that what the other does furthers the agenda of the Dodekatheon - for now at least.

                          Associated Powers: Epic Appearance, Epic Charisma, Epic Stamina, Arete, Chaos, Fertility / Epic Dexterity, Epic Perception, Epic Wits, Arete, Darkness, Death
                          Associated Abilities: Art, Empathy, Fortitude, Larceny, Politics, Presence / Brawl, Command, Investigation, Marksmanship, Melee, Stealth
                          Rivals: Hera; Horus, Loki, Quetzalcoatl, Susano-o, Baron Samedi / Hermes; Anubis, Vidar, Tezcatlipoca, Hachiman, Shango

                          (Two NPCs of mine whom I made gods because I felt it was fun.)

                                      Soktes / Angelo DeManes
                                      God of lies, drought, mud, grain, and grudges. Former scion of Sobek.
                                      Where his father is god of the Nile, he is god of its banks and marshes. Where Sobek is god of amelioration, Soktes is god of outright lies, both for personal gain and relieving of others. He is wily and distrustful, but not blind to those in need. He is fierce to those who disrespect him and make of him an enemy, but as an ally he is just as great.

                                      Since his ascension, Soktes has become patron of spies, liars, orphans, drunks, and the personally wronged. His former followers, now a cult of worshippers, tattoo themselves with an image of a monitor lizard, the sacred animal of Soktes. They are instructed to right the wrongs of those who are broken or defeated, be they personal vendettas or legal battles. Not a single member is allowed to reveal their given name, instead living by an alias.

                                      As a hero, Angelo's first adventure led him to join a band, with whom he suffered through a grueling battle that cost them two members and a vicious defeat, earning them a band of enemies. Throughout his climb to godhood, Angelo would lose every member of his original band, enticing him to, upon finding the Black Feather Shroud they had sought for years, rend it to pieces. The relic Khopesh he gained from his visitation, Crocodile Tears, has evolved into the shape of a sickle, the tool of a farmer for harvesting grain.
                                      ​
                                      In his godly shape, Soktes takes the form of a tall, broad man with bronze skin and the head of a great lizard. He wears a great sash adorned in lapis and garnet, set with iron studs in the pattern of a monitor's skin. His kilt is dyed an earthy brown, the color of fresh mud or soil. In his right hand he holds his Sickle, and in his left he holds either a bundle of grain over his shoulder or else a great book, inked in many dialects and languages. While some believe that his attire leaves much to be desired, Soktes is never within earshot of those who would insult him.

                                      Soktes makes regular trips to the world, for varying reasons and jobs. Not the least of which being his continued hunt for the remaining members of the band that killed his fellows, all those years ago. He also maintains contacts with various seedy types, keeping tabs on the Titans and their supporters. In the world, Soktes takes the shape of a large man with a mysterious glint in his eye and an edge in his voice. He isn't off-putting, but puts those around him on edge, unwilling to make mistakes in his presence. He has occupied a multitude of positions, including CEO of an organized farming company, defense consultant, private investigator, captain of a river ferry, and leader of an underground spy ring.

                                      Scions of Soktes tend to be aloof and bad at maintaining personal relationships, due either to their difficulty in telling the whole truth, or because those around them tend to fall victim to a series of deadly happenings. They are the types who are skilled manipulators, but with no problem getting their hands dirty. Without fail, his scions hold onto grudges for a lifetime, be it their own or their enemy's. It isn't unlikely for them to end up in such positions as information brokers, criminal attorneys, and secret agents.                                           Thaddeus Zurlo: Scion of Zeus

                                          AKA: Thaddeus, Zurlo, The Cloud Prince

                                          Details: God of hubris, vanity and the abuse of one's authority. As a Scion of Zeus, Thaddeus is a chip off the ol' block. As a mortal he was born and raised within the 1% community and was pretty much guaranteed to never run into financial problems. His family were business tycoons who had investments in multiple big name companies with a product in almost every household in the country. So when Thaddeus became old enough to become the new head of the family, he had more wealth than he could spend in a mortal life time. Unfortunately for those under Thaddeus' employ, he had practically zero sense of responsibility!

                                          While the middle class worked their asses off to keep the companies running smoothly, Thaddeus was spending millions of dollars on luxury cruises, first class flights around the world and taking narcotics so illegal than a common man would be jailed for life. Only once did his employees come close to taking him down when they started to suspect him of embezzlement and corruption. His real father, Zeus came to him and revealed his heritage while also exposing the identities of those who plotted against him. The Scion's immediate response was to smite the rebellious employees, sending compensation checks to their family along with his sympathies for their losses. His next immediate reaction was to snort coke off a hooker's tits and pretend he hadn't just committed mass murder to protect his lifestyle!

                                          After his visitation, Thaddeus became the leader of a Band of Scions called The Rising Stars, comprised entirely of Scions from the Dodekatheon. The other members in his band included a daughter of Poseidon, a daughter of Hades, a son of Hermes, a son of Hephaestus, and a son of Dionysus. Thaddeus conducted the Band like a group of divas who were accustomed to getting their way and who were never impressed by mortal attempts to please their ridiculously high expectations.


                                          Like his father Zeus, Thaddeus believes that his status alone gives him the authority to bend the rules, if not outright break them. He places very high expectations on those around him, but often at times fails to live up to his own standards. Those who call him out on his bullshit, however, tend to earn his wrath for speaking out of turn. For beings of Legend, this typically results in a severe beating. For mortals, it unsurprisingly ends with their deaths, as Zurlo is not very experienced at restraining himself.


                                          One of his most legendary accomplishments was during his Hero days, when Thaddeus managed to temporarily overthrow Zeus and convince the king of the gods not to punish him! After discovering an artifact known as the Black Feather Shroud and learning it could transfer a god's power into its wearer, Thaddeus used it to steal Zeus' power for himself, becoming the new King of the Gods for about 5 minutes. When the shroud was stolen away from him, Zeus' power returned and Thaddeus was reduced to a Hero again. Before Zeus could vaporize him on the spot, Thaddeus flew into a philosophical speech about how Zeus was Fated to be overthrown by one his own sons. According to Thaddeus, the prophecy had come true, although not in the way they had expected. Zeus was now free of his ancient Fate Bond and no longer had to worry about being usurped in the future! Reluctantly, Zeus acknowledge the wisdom in this and chose to spare his traitorous son, but not before giving him a death glare warning to never try that again. Years later, Thaddeus would earn his ascension to godhood through more legitimate methods.


                                          On his path to godhood, Zurlo accomplished many great deeds; He was trained in magic by the legendary Circe, he stopped the resurrection of Atlantis which would have destroyed the whole damn planet if it had been allowed to continue, Zeus granted him dominion over swans as a symbol of his virility (though only those who have studied Greek mythology would understand the reference), and when confronted by guilt for his past actions, he punched Satan in the face!



                                          In his godly form, Zurlo looks like a younger version of Zeus, appearing to be in his early 30's. His hair is white as the clouds, but he doesn't have his father's beard. His eyes are electric blue and he often bares a smug grin while thinking of his past accomplishments. His skin is flawlessly unblemished and he bares the uncanny physique of an Olympian, wearing the traditional garb of the ancient Greeks.


                                          The only reason Zurlo comes to the mortal world anymore is for some rest and relaxation when he has a free moment away from the Titan war. When he visits, he makes a stop to one of his many bank accounts, draws out a few million dollars, then throws the vacation of a life time. Often these vacations involve unprotected sex and bastard children. But due to his selfish life style, there is an estimated 1 in 10 chance these children will be granted a visitation. After all, raising kids is expensive and annoying, so he'll only bother with the best of the litter.

As a Scion of Zeus, it is absolutely no surprise that Hera would find herself on his short list. The other positions are filled by Gods of Fate, Justice and Disease, all things which can ruin a good time while he's throwing his latest party. Loki is also on that list because he's just an ass hole.

                                              Ophelia Leventis: Scion of Poseidon

                                              AKA: Tamer of Beasts, Queen of the Minotaur

                                              Details: Goddess of animal husbandry, wrestling and livestock. At first glance, Ophelia would be the last person you would expect to be a child of Poseidon. She grew up on a ranch, with the nearest ocean being at least a hundred miles away. But what is often overlooked was Poseidon was also the God of horses. Ophelia may not have had her father's affinity towards water, but she was very skilled at handling animals, and that made her worthy in her own right.

                                              Ophelia's visitation came during her first cattle drive without her parents around to assist her. The 200 heads of cattle she was leading were suddenly attacked by a Cyclops who intended to steal the herd for his own, and was willing to kill Ophelia to get it. When Poseidon intervened and gave a brief explanation of the situation, Ophelia wrestled the Cyclops to the ground, binding him like a subdued calf. Some time after that, she joined up with The Rising Stars, a Band of Greek Scions lead by Thaddeus Zurlo. Afterward, she began to make a Legend for herself by taming a multitude of dangerous animals, bringing them under the control of the Gods.

                                              During her journeys, Ophelia gained dominion over a variety of different animals, but the beast that she is most famous for is the bull. While on a journey to Crete, Ophelia encountered a horde of Minotaur who attempted to use her to satisfy their perverse urges. Rather than kill them all in self defense, Ophelia merely subdued them with a display of force. Afterward, she branded her initials into their hides and claimed them as her slaves. In recognition of this deed, Poseidon conferred upon her the title of "Queen of the Minotaur" for bringing one of his errant creations under control.

                                              Ophelia is one of the few members in the Dodekatheon who does not look like the perfect image of a human. In fact, she looks more like the animals she is so closely tied with. In her full godly form, Ophelia resembles a female Minotaur (the first of her kind, perhaps). Her body is anthropomorphic standing upright with the arms and torso of a human, but possessing the head, tail, legs and fur of a cow. Four breasts adorn her exposed upper body while a cotton skirt covers her from waist to knees. She is noticeably more muscular than is typically common among women, likely due to a life time of wrestling dangerous animals, such as Titanspawn.

                                              Although the Dodekatheon is normally unkind towards inhuman deities, Ophelia has earned a modicom of respect from her peers. Thanks to her, the Dodekatheon have obtained a variety of powerful and dangerous war beasts to throw against the Titan invasion, all of which she tamed by proving she was stronger than them. If the Dodekatheon believe the bipedal cow is an embarassment to their ranks, they have so far chosen to hold their tongues. This may change, however, once they feel they no longer require her services in the war.

                                              Both as a mortal and as a goddess, Ophelia has displayed the personality of a tomboy. She enjoys hard work and dirty jobs, offering no more complaints than any man might to perform suck a task. As a result, many of her worshipers see her as a female empowerment figure. When staying in the mortal World, she has been the traditional rancher, a self defense coach, a dominatrix, a proffesional wrestler, an obedience school instructor, and a tattoo artist.

The enslaved creatures have the initials O.L branded into their hide, marking them as property of Ophelia Leventis. She wisely keeps the Minotaur out of sight when traveling the mortal World, however.

                                                          Melody Kondos: Scion of Hades

                                                          AKA: Melodia

                                                          Details: Goddess of eulogies and funerals, Princess of the Underworld and guide to lost souls. Born to a family of jewelers, Melody is no stranger to the art of fine craftsmanship. But although she was trained by her parents in the manufacturing of shiny trinkets and accessories, her true passion was music, particularly singing. When she wasn't helping out her family's business, she would practice singing, entertaining fantasies of one day becoming famous and joining a band.

                                                          Maybe it was nostalgic memories of Orpheus from so long ago, but Hades was most pleased with Melody's voice. One day, while Melody was finally earning recognition for her musical talent, Hades gave her a visitation, stealing her away from the talent scouts and agencies that would have exploited her for personal gain. Melody was already an exceptionally talented singer, but once her power of Arete began to manifest, her voice was nearly hypnotic.

                                                          In fact, it was her enchanting songs that lead to her now holding the position she does in the Underworld. With the Titan's prison destroyed, souls were no longer instinctively drawn to the Underworld after their deaths. Thus, Melody took on the role of luring the dead to their final resting place, using her music to ease their regrets and help the dead find peace. It may not be as effective as a mind controlling ghost magnet in the center of the realm, but it works, none the less.

                                                          Ironically, Melody got her wish and ended up joining a Band, just not the kind she was hoping for. After her visitation, she was recruited into The Rising Stars, a group of Scions that consisted entirely of Dodekatheon children. With the addition of her in the group, the Band now had a child of Zeus, Poseidon and Hades working together, a very powerful combitination. Being more of an entertainer at heart, Melody was the physically weakest member of The Rising Stars, preferring to solve her problems through words if at all possible. When she did have to resort to violence, she heavily relied on her Birthrights to accomplish the goal.


                                                          In her full Godly form, Melody appears as a hauntingly beautiful woman in her mid twenties. Her hair is black as a moonless night and her eyes are emerald green. Although her complexion is flawless, she has the unusual characteristic of pale gray skin, as if coated in ashes. She primarily wears a hooded, black robe and often seen carrying a harp or a lyre as her preferred instrument. Her voice is melodic in tone and her disposition is compassionate and welcoming unless given a reason to become hostile.

                                                          As the escort of lost souls, Melody is primarily focused with combating the restless dead and the Titans who control them. She has battled some of the most powerful spectres, zombies, vampires and whatever else continues to haunt the World after it's demise. Laying their bodies to rest, and cleansing their souls of madness.

                                                          When visiting the mortal world, Melody takes on disguises that suit her role in the Underworld. She has been a singer at funerals, an exorcist, a mortician and a grief counselor. Sometimes, when she's feeling nostalgic, she takes the form of a jewelry maker to honor the memory of her mortal parents.
