                                                Originally posted by ganonso View Post
                                                When a God Incarnates into a Scion he is not in the Overworld, correct? One of my player is toying with the idea of an Incarnate of Kali and I'm not sure if that would mean the Dark Mother would leave the Titanomachy while Incarnating or if she would be metaphysically in two planes at the same time.
                                                The basic Incarnate Origin requires that your Deity Self (or at least that Mantle) be dead yes... that being said, Hindu Myth is chock full of Heroic Incarnations that are absolutely Player Character material, and whose over-selves absolutely exist simultaneously in their own realms (or whichever realm they want to be in frankly), so in this case the Incarnate Origin doesn't work as written for your character idea or Hindu Myth in general.

                                                That being said, the Yoga Purview exists, and it is absolutely fitting and in line with myth to say that Yoga lets the Deva create Incarnate Scions without needing to die (since Yoga is literally what lets them create Avatars in the first place in myth). So no, Kali doesn't need to be dead for your character to be an Incarnate of her.

                                                Also, kudos on the character idea, I hope it ends up happening... Kali is a pretty amazing Goddess, if I do say so myself
