        I'm Belle Sorciere on kickstarter.

        I've been playing RPGs since 1980 when I came across a game of Traveller. After that it was a matter of getting into D&D, Champions, Call of Cthulhu, Runequest, and others. In the late 90s it was Shadowrun and GURPS. In the early 90s it was Vampire: The Masquerade, followed by Werewolf, Mage, Wraith, and Changeling. In the late 90s it was Trinity and Aberrant. In the early 2000s it was D&D 3e and Exalted. I went through a long hiatus after I bought into the new WoD in 2004 - mostly due to not having any outlet to play, not because I didn't like the nWoD. I came back to tabletop gaming over the past few years when friends started gifting me with things like backing Mage 20 and Exalted 3e. During the hiatus I kept up my purchasing of Exalted 2e and Scion products but I never really got the chance to play them.

        Right now I'm planning to run a Vampire: The Requiem chronicle as soon as I move to Seattle. I have players lined up but right now we're too far away for playing to be feasible. This is fine as I am still in preparation.

        Scion went on kickstarter at the absolutely perfect time as I have more income to spare for kickstarters and new books than I did before. So I admit I splurged a bit on Scion, and I. Regret. Nothing. I am really looking forward to the new edition as it sounds like everything people had trouble with in 1e is being addressed.
