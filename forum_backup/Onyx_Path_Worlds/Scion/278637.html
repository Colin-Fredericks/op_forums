    Totemic Pantheon (WiP)

    10-12-2014, 02:49 AM
    I'm still writing up the founding members, but I've got the Overworld, the Underworld, and the basic premise for them written up, so I decided to post it. I'll be adding the Totems themselves once they're completed.


    ----------------------------------------------------



    Totemic Pantheon: Ages ago, mankind once worshiped nature and beasts, but eventually their religious beliefs advanced to more human deities. For thousands of years, the worship of animal spirits was thought to have been lost, but a band of Scions have begun to restore it in the modern age, though they may have deviated from the ancient versions of the legends. This new Totemic Pantheon addresses its members as Totems, or guardian animals. According to the religion they founded, everybody has an animal that represents their true self, and their mortal followers attempt to connect with that spirit animal and live up to its teachings both metaphorically and sometimes literally.


    This new Totemic Pantheon is definitely unpopular with the other Pantheons that exist, as the human gods and goddesses are loathe to the idea of humans placing mere beasts on the same level of status as they are. The Egyptian deities are more accepting of them given that they tend to have bestial features, themselves, but they still view them as a rival for worshipers and legendary achievements. Another reason why they are hated is because the Totemic Pantheon has no designated culture or ethnicity, it is a melting pot of all cultures, the original founders settling the differences of their racist parents and forming into a unified religion that commingles the bloodlines. Racial ethnicity becomes difficult to identify when you look like a beast, after all. It is quite famous (or infamous) for adopting unclaimed Scions if their parents show no interest in meeting them. But there is also a reason why they are so open to accepting members. The truth is that they are young, and they are new, and in a war against not only the Titans, but the other Pantheons themselves, they need all the help they can get.


    One thing they are proud to preach about is that they do not believe in eternally punishing their mortal followers for any wrong doings. While they do agree that justice must be paid for their sins, the mortal will not have to worry about facing eternity in torment after their deaths, and all of them will eventually be allowed into the Totemic heaven if they learn to behave themselves. This leniency has done much to sway the mortals to their side, and help to establish their growing power among the population! When the other Pantheons impose merciless punishments for defying their rules, the idea of a more forgiving order of gods and goddesses certainly appeals to those who are susceptible to temptation.


    Being new to the life of godhood, the Totemic Pantheon is still in development, as its Scions are struggling to achieve divinity in their fight against the Titan Spawn, and against the angry Pantheons who don't like the idea of mortals worshiping beasts again. However, the original founders of this revived Pantheon count 15 in number. They are based on the Fox, the Mole, the Wolf, the Bear, the Rat, the Spider, the Hawk, the Otter, the Snake, the Squirrel, the Horse, the Stag, the Bunny, and the Lion.


    Among this collection of Totems, the Rat, the Snake, and Spider are the most feared, and worshipers who devote themselves to these Totems usually possess malicious intentions. It is also worth noting that the Totemic Pantheon is chaotic in its social structure. Not only is it a new religion that is still in development, but each Totem has their own idea on how their worshipers should live, which causes their followers to splinter into sub-factions and cults. The wolf Totem, Canis Lupus does his best to establish some form of cohesion among the Totems, and has succeeded in keeping them together as a Pantheon, instead of fracturing into individual deities. Whether they can agree to a unified goal, or if they will forever remain disorganized, however, will be revealed in time.


    While initially the Totemic Scions may retain their mortal visages, they become progressively more bestial in their features as their power increases. By the time they reach full godhood, a Totem resembles anthropomorphic beasts. These changes are usually cosmetic, however, and do not offer any special benefits without invoking divine powers related to such. Because the deities take on the forms of animals and encourage their worshipers to imitate them, the Totems have been mockingly referred to as the Furry Pantheon. But the Totems are not yet powerful enough to bash the face of every god or goddess who calls them it in public, so they bide their time and build their power until they become worthy of respect.


    EDIT 1:


    It should be noted that a Scion of the Totems is not required to become a member of the same species as their parent. They are just as likely to become some other creature who shares the same genus, habitat, or dietary traits. It is also entirely possible for them to be completely unrelated, such as Scion of Vixen growing up to resemble the Elephant, or a Scion of Widow becoming a Kangaroo. This allows the Totems to grow in variety, as well as numbers as time passes on!



    EDIT 2:


    Although they may be new to the ranks of the gods, the Totems have already begun work on constructing Boons and relics to their Scions who wish to join their ranks. One of the most popular boons is the accompaniment of an animal spirit who reflects the beast they feel they most resonate with. The benefits to these animal spirits are two fold. First, the animal spirit functions like a guide and a mentor, leading them on the path towards ascension, and advising them on how to remain in harmony with their inner animal. Secondly, it grants them a stronger affinity towards the animal world, and they find it much easier to advance in the Animal purview. Under the spirit's guidance, they can purchase Animal purview levels at half the normal cost!

    The spirit animal is only a guide and a teacher, however, and does not engage in battle on the Scion's behalf. In fact, the animal spirits make a habit out of fleeing the moment physical danger breaks out, and approach the Scions when they are in an hour of need. Not simply because the Scion asks to be pointed in the right direction. Ascension to godhood isn't supposed to be easy, of course, and the Totems aren't going to hold their hand through the journey.


    ---------------------------------------------


    Overworld: The god realm of the Totemic pantheon is called the Spirit Wylds. Each member of the Pantheon gets their own private habitat designed to be their personal heaven, but nothing prevents them from leaving said habitat and visiting others. Entering another Totem's realm with malicious intent is not looked upon lightly, and more than once the gods have had to convene to settle a dispute between two or more deities who decided to mess with each other and let things get out of hand.

    In the center of the Spirit Wylds lies the Totem Lodge, where all major discussions among the gods and goddesses takes place. It is the place to hold war councils, political debates, trials for crimes against the gods, and discussing strategies on how to increase their power in the mortal world. Strictly speaking, the Spirit Lodge is off limits unless Canis Lupus calls a meeting, and then all able bodied Totems who were invited to the meeting are allowed to enter.


    -------------------------------------


    Underworld: The Totemic underworld is split into three divisions which includes the upper tier called the Wilderness, a middle tier called Widow's Web, and a bottom tier called Widow's Prison. Upon entering the Totemic Underworld, a dead soul is judged by Widow and sent on to either the Wilderness, or they serve a period of time in Widow's Prison as penance for their sins. As a reward, souls who were closely in tuned with their Totem animal in life take on a hybridized form of that animal in the afterlife, much like the gods they worship. While they may gain special abilities based on the structure of their new forms, they are still mortals and do not gain access to the divine abilities of the Totems and their Scions.


    The Wilderness is where most souls are taken upon being judged by Widow. It is a realm that stretches on seemingly without end, and the majority of it is undeveloped wilderness. The souls of the departed who reside here are allowed to govern themselves (as long as it does not interfere with the Totems or their operations) and form a seemingly infinite number of different societies. These societies are usually dedicated to the teachings and philosophy of the Totems they feel most inspired by, and because there is an unspecified amount of territory to roam, there is little danger of two or more societies clashing with one another. The Wilderness is a place where mortal souls make use of what is around them to create their own paradise. The afterlife is as blissful or tormenting as they make it out to be, so it caters to just about anyone's tastes. The Wilderness offers any climate the inhabitants could ask for, allowing it to be suitable to all species of animals. Depending upon the species, some residents (i.e Carnivores) require meat, which they usually acquire by hunting and feeding on other inhabitants. However, this is a form of the afterlife, so no death is permanent. Those who meet their end here are restored the next day, and the cycle continues. Most (though not all) aren't comfortable with the idea of being hunted down and eaten in what is supposed to be their final resting place, but the deaths are only temporary, so their prey has put up with it so far.



    Widow's Web is the border land that separates the Totemic hell from its heavenly counterpart. It is also the gateway that leads both into and out of the Totemic Underworld. Widow's Web is a vast, sprawling network of bridges and tunnel systems caked in webbing, and those wishing to enter or leave the underworld must pass through. If they do not have permission from Widow, the webs will cling to them and ensnare trespassers who do not have the strength or the tools to break free. But if she gives them access, the webs will not hinder them. Those who become ensnared are scooped up by the spidery gate keepers, who whisk them away to Widow for judgement. By default, members of the Totemic pantheon have permission to travel, unless explicitly stated otherwise by Canis Lupus.


    Widow's Prison is where souls are punished in the afterlife, as well as the tomb in which captured Titan Spawn are confined. The prison is an enormous cavern where inmates are cocooned in spider webs and are either suspended from the ceiling, or sealed to the walls and floors. The prison is guarded by a legion of giant spiders who torment the prisoners by feeding on their bodily fluids and leaving them as mummified husks every day, not unlike Prometheus suffered at the torments of the eagle. The plan is that by leaving the prisoners too atrophied to move, they won't have any chance to escape again! Fortunately for mortals, the Totems do not believe in eternal punishment, though Titan Spawn are an exception. Mortals who are taken here suffer for a period of time that is dictated by Widow, herself, until she feels they have paid their dept. Repeated offenses result in lengthier sentences, but eternal imprisonment is still reserved only for the immortal enemies of the Totems.

      Members:



      Vixen: Goddess of pleasure and mischief, and is renowned for her sexual addiction and playful trouble making. Vixen's true origins have become a mystery since joining the Totemic Pantheon, but it is heavily implied that she is a child of the Amatsukami. Evidence in her powers, mannerisms, and style of dress suggest that she was conceived by a "fox wife" a mischievous creature who takes the form of a beautiful woman and seduces men into marriage, only to abandon them once their true identity is revealed. Every Scion she has given birth to has been female so far, but she has been known to adopt males if she likes them enough. Her scions are often tricksters, deriving great fun out of playing pranks on mortals and divine alike, though only the more powerful allow themselves to get carried away with their latest mischief.

      Vixen is a sexual icon among the Totems, and her style of dress rarely leaves little to the imagination, accentuating her chest and rear, while exposing arms, legs, face, and midsection as much as possible, her golden hued fur shines like flames in the light and only adds to her enchanting beauty. She is known to be promiscuous and many jokingly attribute her Legend to being entirely constructed in the bedroom. When she enters the battlefield, her weapons of choice are a pair of steel bladed fans, which she uses to fan the flames of her beautiful (yet deadly) fox fire magic. Much like the fox wives of Japan, she has been known to pose as a mortal for long periods of time, even going so far as to marry human males that she has children with. When the child comes of age, Vixen reveals her true identity and their heritage, before abandoning the family, and leaving the child to continue on the path towards ascension.

      When on the mortal world, Vixen's disguises are pretty easy to recognize. She has been a feisty party girl, an erotic dancer, a super model, and even the manager of her own brothel. Anywhere that mortals (particularly men) are likely to enjoy themselves, Vixen will be drawn to it, as her purview dictates.

      Purviews: Sun, Illusion, Fire

      Epics: Manipulation, Appearance




      Mole: God of the earth and its valuable minerals. Although there is little evidence to support it, some believe that Mole was a Scion of Hephaestus because of his physical disability. Mole is almost completely blind, only being able to see with the aid of special glasses that have a variety of lens adjustments to point out the details in rare gem deposits and stones he finds in the tunnels beneath the ground. When not wearing these glasses, he is forced to rely on his sense of smell, touch and sound to navigate his surroundings. His scions typically manifest the traits of burrowing creatures, often (though not always) having physical disabilities and impairments brought on by a life time underground.

      Mole is short of stature, being only waist high compared to adult humans, and he is fat in build. His hands and feet appear disproportionately large compared to the rest of his body, with thick claws on the tips that he can use to burrow through the ground. When he's awake, he is almost never seen without wearing a heavy apron, like the kind smelters user in their forges. Because of his stocky appearance, Mole doesn't have much luck when it comes to fathering Scions as most women aren't attracted to him. However, those who do share lineage with him are often blessed with the awesome might of the earth at their command!

      When on the mortal plane, Mole has taken on the disguise of people who work with the earth's resources and have included such professions as iron worker, black smith, miner, gem cutter, and even a glass blower.

      Purviews: Darkness, Earth, Fertility

      Epics: Strength, Wits




      Canis Lupus: The Overgod of the Totems and the one in charge of keeping everyone organized, as much as possible for the Totems. Embodying the wolf, Canis Lupus takes on the role of the pack alpha. He sees the rest of the Totems as his pack, who look to him for leadership and structure in an otherwise chaotic society. Granted not every member of the pantheon shares his views on that matter, but enough respect him to the point where he retains his position. In the society of wolves, the alpha is always the strongest and the bravest. When they begin to show weakness, it usually results in someone in the pack challenging them for leadership. In respect to this, Canis Lupus is renowned as a cunning strategist and exceptional warrior, determined to show his pantheon that he is both strong and smart enough to lead them.

      In appearance, Canis resembles a werewolf standing at nearly 7 feet tall and with muscle tone that shows even through his silvery colored fur. His weapon of choice is the spear, an iconic tool for ancient hunters. He wears trophies from his previous kills to accentuate his past victories, although the types of trophies he wears depends on the prey he is facing. If the prey is weak, simple trinkets are enough to maintain his pride. If the prey is strong, then something more glorious and terrifying is required; perhaps in the form of a skull mask, or a cloak made from the pelt of a fearsome creature.

      When on the mortal plane, Canis Lupus always takes the form of a leader, but prefers a role that also requires him to be strong. The foreman of a construction effort, the commander of a military precision force, the reigning champion of a competitive sporting event, and the ruthless boss of a criminal enterprise when he's in a less noble mood.

      Purviews: Justice, War, Guardian

      Epics: Strength, Intelligence




      Ursine: The goddess of strength and self reliance for the Totemic pantheon. Ursine is the bear, a creature renowned for its great size and durability. Some believe that if she wished to, she could challenge Canis Lupus for the position of Overgod, but Ursine does not seem interested in managing the affairs of a whole pantheon, and instead prefers to take care of her own business while leaving the others to theirs. While she is normally docile (as far as deities go), she responds with furious aggression towards anyone who threatens her own safety, or that of something valuable to her, much like a mama bear protecting her cubs. Her ancestry is almost impossible to trace, for she does not speak of who her divine parent was prior to her ascension, and none of the other pantheons have come forth to clarify her lineage. But given that she is a strong, independent female, it is likely that she is descended from the Aesir. Her Scions have no majority towards either male or female, but when others think of her, they tend to imagine daughters being her favored gender.

      Ursine is a hefty woman, standing around 9 feet tall and weighing upwards of 400 lbs in her true form. When in the mortal world, she is still rotund, but is noticeably shorter to blend in with the population. Despite being mistaken for excess fat, much of her weight is comprised of muscle, allowing her to catch fools by surprise when they rile her into a fight. Her species appears to be based on the polar bear, with a pale white shade of fur covering her. When entering battle, her preferred weapon of choice is a pair of weighted gauntlets which are (obviously) far too heavy for any mortal to lift, yet she flings punches as if they were weightless!

      When on the mortal plane, Ursine has taken the disguise of a disgruntled bar tender, a talented chef, a hardened biker, or a woman's weight lifting champion. Typically roles where her large frame and personality are expected, or at least a common enough sight to not raise questions.

      Purviews: Guardian, Frost, Health

      Epics: Strength, Stamina



      Rat: God of rebellion and thieves, Rat is quite the unpopular Totem, at least among his fellow divinities. He is a hard ass rebel who prefers to make his own rules, which constantly leads to him getting in trouble with the other Totems. Rat can be found in the shadows, snatching up trinkets that are left in the open, as well as breaking into dwellings and making himself at home, not unlike his mortal counterparts. His Scions often come from the ranks of street gangs, bikers, anarchists, and bad boys.

      In appearance, Rat resembles a punk, his fur being spiked upward and often dyed with bright colors and tattoos. Parts of his fur have bald streaks, revealing a network of scars that he received from his rebellious life style when he pushed someone too far. He wears and assortment of piercings around his ears, nose, lip, eyebrow, and chest, and when he's on the mortal plane, he often dresses in leather, torn clothing, and studded accessories. Whatever disguise he takes on, it is almost always up to trouble, or generally causing people grief. His personal weapon of choice is the knife, preferably ones that are easy to conceal, so he can whip it out to catch his opponents off guard.

      Being the rebel that he is, Rat isn't exactly one for the subtle art of seduction, and uses more direct methods to father his Scions across the world. If he's feeling "gentle" he resorts to using narcotics to leave his partners vulnerable while he has his way with them. If he's in a less romantic mood, he simply forces himself upon them. It is no surprise then, that most of his Scions grow up in an unstable environment, being dumped into a foster home, or facing abuse at the hands of their mortal parent who never wanted them in the first place. This only facilitates their disdain for society, and Rat comes to visit them when he is impressed with their disregard for public safety.

      Purviews: Psychopomp, Chaos, Darkness

      Epics: Dexterity, Perception



      Widow: Goddess of the Underworld for the Totemic pantheon. Widow represents the Spider, and much like Vixen, her lineage is easy to guess at by observing her mannerisms and appearance. Evidence suggests that she is descended from the Greeks, possibly having some form of connection with Arachne. Her version of hell (restraining people and feeding them to animals) is also reminiscent of Prometheus from Greek mythology. Widow governs the afterlife with the aid of a legion of giant spiders who capture trespassers and bring them to her for questioning and sentencing.

      Widow's true form resembles a centaur, having the lower body of an enormous spider, but from the waist up is a more humanoid form, although it has 8 eyes and protruding fangs making it quite hideous to behold! Her weapon of choice is the bow and arrow, the ammunition for which is usually coated in the venom of some of the world's deadliest arachnids! As a spider, Widow has ridiculously acute senses, both from her additional sets of eyes, and her ability to sense vibrations. Truly she is worthy of being the keeper of the Totemic Underworld, for very little escapes her notice.

      The origins of the spiders who roam about her web and prison is a disturbing legend and one which further adds to the terror of Widow. The legend states that when Widow conceives a Sion, she devours her mate shortly after the the deed is done. These giant spiders are the souls of her lovers, continuing to serve their mistress both in death as well as in life, their souls being reincarnated into Widow's private harem of 8 legged servants. Needless to say, not many people like the idea of being enthralled to their own murderer for eternity, so she often uses deceit to lure men into her bedchambers. When in the mortal world, her disguise is usually that of an extraordinarily beautiful woman with a power fetish. She takes great pleasure in watching the men fight among each other for her affections, eager to see which of them will be her lover for the evening, unaware of the fate that awaits those who win her contest.

      Purviews: Prophecy, Death, Mystery

      Epics: Appearance, Perception




      Red Tail: Goddess of the weather and messenger of the Totems. Having the envious power to fly, Red Tail can travel large expanses of terrain more quickly than the land dwelling Totems, which makes her ideal for relaying messages and items. She embodies the Hawk, a swift bird of prey with keen eye sight. That eye sight also helps her navigate the land beneath her, so she knows where to evenly distribute the weather. She is speculated to have been descended from the Egyptian pantheon, but like most of the Totems, she doesn't talk much about her ancestry now that they've founded a new Pantheon.

      When in her true form, Red Tail does not have access to hands as her arms are replaced with a giant set of wings. However, she can use her legs and talons with artistic precision. Her fighting style relies on the use of kicking attacks, while using her wings to remain airborne as well as to distract the enemy with her flourishing movements. In order to reduce wind resistance and make it easier to control her wings, she typically does not wear upper body clothing. When in her human disguises, Red Tail commonly takes the form of someone who is handicapped, particularly focusing on the arms. Not only is this to show she has trouble using her upper limbs for human functions, but it also symbolizes that her wings have been temporarily clipped until she drops her disguise.

      Red Tail is the only member of the founding Totems who has the gift of flight, which brings her both admiration and envy from the rest of the pantheon. Even her Sions display a personality that embraces freedom and wanderlust, which again makes them both admired and envied by their cousins who don't share their gift.


      Purviews: Fertility, Sky, Psychopomp

      Epics: Perception, Dexterity




      Otter: God of water and the secrets that lie within it. Otter is many things including an explorer, a trickster, a fisherman, and a surfer. In other words, he loves to enjoy life and is very playful in nature, but can be deadly when the situations calls for it. Otter is also highly curious and inquisitive. There are a great many things that dwell beneath the waves, and as the Totem of the seas, he feels compelled to seek them out. This often leads him to clash with Titan Spawn who dwell beneath the waves, causing him to fight them off so they don't venture onto dry land.

      Otter's true form dresses in minimal attire, as too much clothing only provides drag that would slow him down and restrict his movements. There are even legends which state he is a nudist, only donning clothes when he is attending a formal occasion and needs to be polite! His weapon of choice is a two pronged spear that doubles as a fishing tool when he's not skewering his foes in underwater combat!

      Otter's Scions are drawn to adventure with many of them possessing an infamous curiosity streak. Even if they don't hear the call of the sea in their fate, they still feel the urge to travel the land, uncovering hidden mysteries and treasures. When in his mortal disguise, Otter likes to take on the forms of people who satiate his hobbies including sailors, treasure seekers, and water sports athletes. Often his Scions are mothered by women who were seduced by his skills on the water. Otter affectionately refers to them as beach babes, a term he continues to use from his mortal days.

      Purview: Moon, Water, Mystery

      Epics: Appearance, Charisma




      Serpent: God of cruelty and black magic. Serpent is the most hated member of the Totems, having taken on the role of the "adversary" god. When Rat causes trouble, it's because he values his individuality and freedom, but Serpent is evil for the sake of being evil. He takes great delight in causing harm to the other Totems and their Scions completely unprovoked just because it amuses him! When he is confronted with demands to explain himself, he arrogantly replies that a religion is boring without a devil to provide conflict and drama. Serpent is also a known racist, believing that his scaly hide makes him tougher than the soft flesh of some of the other animals he shares power with, and he openly endorses slavery. Given his relation to snakes and his love for causing trouble to mortal and gods alike, he is believed to be descended from the Egyptian pantheon, more than likely with Seht being his father! Serpent has been known to stop his shit and help the other Totems in their fight against the Titan Spawn, but it is more out of a desire to maintain his own grip on power. He can not fend off the whole army of the Titans by himself, and there is doubtfully any other Pantheons who would accept him and his shenanigans, so he recognizes the benefits to keeping his fellow Totems alive... for now!

      In his true form, Serpent resembles a naga, having the upper body of a humanoid reptile, but possesses an enormous (roughly 15 feet long) snake tail instead of legs. His species of snake appears to be the Asp if you were to inspect him, but most people see him appear and know that it's probably best to leave. To Serpent, there is nothing more arousing than the sight of a beautiful woman prostrating her body and soul before him in a sign of total submission. And most of his Scions are conceived by priestesses and worshipers who willingly offer themselves upon his altar in some kind of debauched sex ritual!

      As the god of black magic for the Totemic pantheon, cults dedicated to Scion conduct rituals and ceremonies that would be considered forbidden and illegal in civilized territories. Ritual human sacrifice is just one such way in which they worship him. Because their god promotes slavery, worshipers of Serpent do so as well, and have been known to capture people who become lost in the woods, have their cars break down on the side of the road, or (if they're feeling particularly bold) they simply knock somebody out and toss them in the back of their trunk! In order to prevent their chances of escape, the slaves who prove troublesome are usually sacrificed to Serpent, much to their horror!


      Purviews: Chaos, Magic, Stars


      Epics: Manipulation, Stamina



      Bushy Tail: Brother of Red Tail and the god of merchantry and business. Bushy Tail embodies the squirrel, and has made it his mission to ensure the Totems have all the supplies they need for the coming war against the Titans. Food, resources, and weapons are what he oversees. If his sister is the messenger of the gods, then Bushy is the store keeper. When questioned how Bushy and Red became two entirely different species upon ascension to godhood, Bushy explains that (like many Scions) they have the same father, but different mothers. They are half siblings, but siblings none the less. He is no more lenient in explaining who his father was than Red Tail is, maintaining the mystery for those wishing to know.

      In his true form, Bushy resembles a spritely fellow who could be mistaken for a teenager. Though his muscles are well toned, they are sleak and graceful, rather than buff and heavy. His tail is as long as he is tall, and is almost as big from the excess fur, appropriately fitting his name. His weapon of choice is the sling, of all things. And he likes to use acorns which he has imbued with Legend for his ammunition. Legends state that these acorns are immensely heavy, to the point beyond human ability to lift them, which makes them suitable weapons against the Titan Spawn when they make impact.

      Bushy and his Scions are known for their acrobatic performances. Like the squirrel in the tree, Bushy is excessively nimble, sprinting along narrow walkways and jumping across gaps to land safely on the other side. Perhaps this is the result of training with his sister, or perhaps it is a symbol of their connection to each other. Whatever the case may be, Bushy Tail could make for a decent substitute if his sister were to ever be incapable of fulfilling her duties.

      Purviews: Fertility, Guardian, Pyschopomp

      Epics: Dexterity, Perception



      Equine: God of athleticism and labors, and is believed to be descended from the Greek pantheon for obvious reasons. Baring the image of the horse, Equine is a symbol of perseverance. Whether it's jumping hurdles, racing track, tilling the land, or hauling loads, horses are strong and hardy beasts, and Equine expects the same of his children. His worshipers are typically farmers, day laborers, or athletes, praying to the horse god to bestow them with the strength and stamina to succeed.

      In his true form, Equine is one of the largest founding members of the Totemic pantheon. Standing at 10 feet tall and with powerfully toned muscles from a life time of hard work, mortals have a tendency to stand in awe when they are close enough to touch him. His fur is light brown with creamy white patches, and he has thick tufts of hair around his shins and forearms. His mane and tail are the same creamy color as the spots that pepper his body. Like Red Tail, he never wears upper body clothing, allowing him full range of motion for working. His mortal disguises have been the farm hand, the star athlete, the quiet construction worker, and cargo loader. Strangely he doesn't take on management positions when in these disguises, but prefers to be one of the workers, likely as a means to impress his "bosses" by showing he can handle any job they assign.


      Scions of Equine are almost a boon themselves when they join a Band. Their stamina appears limitless, carrying on long after the point where others would stop to rest. There are even legends which tell of a Scion of Equine carrying one or two allies off the battlefield, slumped over their shoulders when a fight takes a turn for the worst. If the Band needs someone to perform a grueling task, chances are a Scion of Equine will step up to answer the call.

      Purviews: Earth, Sun, Health

      Epics: Strength, Stamina



      Hart: God of beauty and wisdom. Hart is the stag who rules majestically over the creatures of the forest. He is believed to be descended from the Amatsukami, a belief that is supported by his mannerisms and fighting style. While Canis Lupus does his best to keep the other Totems under control, Hart ironically seems to be taking away his influence with a passive aggressive resistance. Although Hart makes no move to directly oppose Canis, few can deny that the other Totems look to him for guidance when they have run out of ideas. His beauty makes him charismatic, and his oracular powers make him borderline omniscient. He doesn't have to force people to listen to him, they do so of their own free will! The Totems who seek his council the most are Bushy Tail, Red Tail, Mole and Vixen.

      Hart's true form is stunning to behold, and could even seduce humans despite being a beast. His body is sleek and graceful, nimbly bounding over obstacles when moving at high speed, and he moves with the gentle grace of a dancer. From his heart grows a majestic crown of antlers number 12 points. Unlike the other Totems, he has no weapon of choice, instead relying on his own martial prowess if confronted with battle. Hart's disguises in the mortal world have included being a teacher, a wiseman, a shrine priest, a ballet dancer, a martial arts teacher, and a yoga instructor.

      Hart is a pacifist and chooses to avoid battle unless forced into it. When he is forced into it, however, he proves to be an legendary martial artist, striking at his opponent's weak spots, and seeing through their attack patterns with ease. His Scions are blessed (or cursed) with prophecy, able to see into the currents of Fate itself, and warn their companions of its machinations before they happen.


      Purviews: Fertility, Prophecy, Sun

      Epics: Charisma, Intelligence



      Lapin: Former goddess of hearth and home, now she is the goddess of vice and sin. Shortly after the establishment of the Totemic pantheon, Lapin was seduced by Serpent's forked tongue in more ways than one. This caused her to abandon her role as the symbol of family ties and become a debauched hedonist, as well as giving Serpent a constant ally against the gods who despise him. Her addiction to pleasure is even greater than Vixen's, and she is viewed as a symbol of how too much of a good thing is bad for you. She is sometimes referred to as the goddess of excess for the Totemic pantheon, due to her addictive nature. This bunny totem now serves as a constant reminder to the other Totems that if they allow Serpent to wrap his coils too tightly, they too can fall prey to his corrupting influence. In an effort to keep her further under his control, Serpent has trained Lapin in the ways of magic, as a "sign of good faith" according to how he describes it. However, he refrains from teaching her the truly powerful incantations, and instead teaches her how to disrupt the machinations of fate to her own ends. Fortunately, the mind of a goddess is not so easy to dominate, and in one of her final moments of clarity, Lapin created a safety precaution that could one day reverse Serpent's hold over her. Now it is up to her Scions to carry it out, if they can figure out how!

      In her true form, Lapin is quite the beauty, enough to compete with Vixen according to some legends. She has wide hips, both due to her beast form and because she was once a symbol of motherhood. Her fur was once white as snow, but since her corruption, it has now become black as night, save for a patch of white across the left upper portion of her face that covers her left eye and a portion of her forehead. According to the legends, this patch of fur is a symbol of Lapin's safety magic, and proves that she can be redeemed some day and reclaim her former station.Her disguises in the mortal world are usually focused on decadence and sinful pursuits. She has been a cheap whore, a murderer, a drug dealer, a thief and even the manager of a public bath house. Her weapon of choice is the bo-staff, which is perfect for bludgeoning her foes into submission, it also functions as a sexual aid when there are no partners around.

      Scions of Lapin are often cursed by the influence Serpent leaked into their mother. Sexual addiction runs strongly in her bloodline, and the phrase "fuck like rabbits" is used to describe them frequently. Some of these Scions give into their temptations, but there are others who reject Serpent's machinations, and struggle to overcome their curse. Those who succeed, often hope to break the curse entirely, by freeing their mother from his control and restoring her to her former position. Those who struggle against the curse, are far more dedicated to studying Magic than those who submit to it.

      Purviews: Fertility, Health, Magic

      Epics: Appearance, Dexterity



      Lion: God of nobility, pride, and honorable combat. Lion's reputation is at odds with Canis Lupus, for while the Wolf Totem takes on the role of being the Overgod, Lion would much rather see himself on the throne, as is befitting the "King of Beasts" that his species is so often called. Lion certainly possesses the charisma and the drive to be a leader, but his biggest weakness is his pride. Lion is too arrogant in his own capabilities to listen to wisdom and common sense, which has allowed Canis Lupus to outsmart his attempts to steal the throne so far. As the god of nobility, Lion sees himself as the bravest and most skilled member of the Totems. As a Hero and even more as a demi god, he was always the most eager to prove his skills in battle, rushing into danger and claiming trophies from his kills to show the mortals how great he was. Now as a full god, Lion views himself as the educated knight among a Pantheon comprised of primitive warriors. Consequently, he is also the least tolerant of Serpent's activities. If he can successfully dispose of the Totemic "devil" where Canis Lupus can not, then surely the other Totems will put him on the throne instead!

      In his true form, Lion appears as a majestic figure. His muscles are strong and well toned, befitting the build of a knight or a general. His mane gleams brightly like the sun, giving him a holy and divine appearance. He also wears golden chainmail that is polished to the point of almost being reflective. Those who are not protected from bright lights are forced to cast their eyes downward to avoid damaging their vision. His disguises on the mortal world have always been positions of lofty status. He has been a military commander, a mega-corporation president, a heavy weight world champion, or a even the leader of his own country, after dethroning the current ruler. His weapon of choice is the broadsword and shield, making him the only founding member of the pantheon who takes up a defensive tool with his weapon!

      Scions of Lion tend to follow in their father's footsteps. They are the bravest of heroes, like the mighty Hercules who faced each of his trials no matter how outlandish they may have been. They often seek to become leader of any Bands they join, and if they are not outright given the position, they will do whatever they can think of to prove they are more worthy of it. Often by outshining the accomplishments of the Band's current leader. Unfortunately their eagerness to please often results in overestimating their own abilities. More than one Scion of Lion has become a martyr, sacrificing their own lives to protect their Band, and failing to ascend to godhood in the process. As a result, those who successfully pass the trials ahead of them are even more worthy of respect and admiration than the ones who failed.

      Purviews: Sun, Justice, War

      Epics: Strength, Appearance
