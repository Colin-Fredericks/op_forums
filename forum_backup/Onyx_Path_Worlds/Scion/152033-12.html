            After thinking on it, I was willing to go halfway and promote Kokopeli and the Hopi tribe's version because, again, the majority knew him and the Hopi.

            Now after all the backlash, I have evidence that I was right at the start. A few weeks ago, I saw the Disney movie "Planes: Fire and Rescue" with my brother. In it, there was a log carrier that was categorized as Native American descent. During a scene where Dusty and the other fire-fighters were schmoozing with a RV couple, the guy told a story of a certain animal spirit. I was half expecting them to use the name for the tribe of the Redwood area. Instead, the log carrier called the animal spirit "Coyote". There was no call against the Disney company of slander of a tribe's belief system. To Disney, and to the viewers of the movie, the spirit is called Coyote, and will always be called Coyote to them.

            If Onyx Path decides on the route that I initially specified, then interest from casual gamers will rise. If they want to know more, then we as responsible players, can guide them to the correct book and/or website containing the info they seek. That's how people should know about the mythology behind the hype, just like Mummy: The Resurrection got me interested to know more about the Egyptian pantheon, and how Hercules and Percy Jackson got me interested in the game of Scion, and mythology in general.

            To be bombarded with a negative response, not to mention esoteric knowledge that only a minority knows and/or cares about, the one who was attacked would think negatively about the person and would turn themselves off (and their friends) from the game because of said experience. And that's one of the worst kind of troll, in my opinion.
