    52) When life gives you lemons, squirt juice in it's eye! All you wanted to do was live a normal life. But after receiving your visitation, that became impossible with the constant attacks Fate is throwing your way. You've tried to keep your identity a secret, you've tried attending school, even getting a part time job, but it's only been a month now and already you've been attacked 3 times on your way to work, twice while attending school, and they almost tracked you back home just yesterday. Retaining your secret identity from the public is becoming more difficult than you would like to think about. Even your friends are becoming suspicious when you spontaneously have to leave in a hurry, just before disaster strikes and a masked crusader arrives to save the day. Fortunately for you, it seems Fate has a sense of humor, and nobody has put the pieces together that you and this masked hero are one in the same. Then one day, you're approached by a group of students who claim they're in the same predicament as you. They were less than pleased to receive their own visitations and have been trying to maintain a normal life. Working together, you may be able to ease the stress of your Scion duties and keep your secret identity a while longer.


    High school super hero story.

        53) Competition was fierce in a surfing tournament off the coast of Hawaii, but you’d been practicing for months to step up your skills and it was paying off. Many had remarked that you were so much more graceful and relaxed on the high seas than you were on land (on land you tended to be a klutz!). Now in the finals it was between you and some guy nobody knew about. He’d been giving you the stink eye the whole tournament and now you were racing him down the waves. You were pulling ahead until he suddenly surged at you in defiance of physics to menace you with a bone-carved knife baring very inhuman fangs. He never made it; instead he was snatched below the waves by very dangerous looking tentacles and never seen again. You won the competition but didn’t go near the water till the sun went down. Everyone assumed that the other surfer lost control of his board and was killed in a riptide, but you knew what actually got him.
        You stood at the docks thinking about this very fact when they creature returned, snatching you off the docks more quickly than you could scream and dragging you to the depths. It deposited you in a cavern beneath the waves amidst the bones of all manner of creatures, and as it reared up you could see that it was some kind of huge squid-monster. The first thing you screamed out was “don’t eat me!!!” which gave the creature pause as it gazed at you.
        “Why would I eat you my son?” it replied in mild tones.
        It was some time before Tangaroa was finally able to get you to calm down, but since then you’ve been charged with seeking out and eliminating evils from the depths.

        54) The much larger whaling ship cut past you and your friends’ smaller fishing trawler with obvious disdain. It had the advantage in the gathering storm whereas you and your crew were likely to be dumped into the ocean at any minute. But it was worth it, these whaling vessels would continue despoiling the sea and destroying its gentlest wildlife if they were not stopped. Your plan here was to attack them the instant they readied their weapons to fire and try to make the sinking of the ship look like an accident. You had all kinds of (very illegal) explosives on board to use on them, and a crew of similarly motivated people to help you.
        Your smaller craft harassed theirs for some time before the storm broke over you both. As soon as the seas began to churn your ship had its hands full just trying to keep afloat while the larger vessel chugged along unhindered. You were in the cabin when you saw it; the seas became a whirling maelstrom that sucked in both ships. You ordered the rest of your crew below hoping to save them while you tried to steer your ship out of impending doom. The larger whaling vessel didn’t fare much better, its mass suddenly working against it. All of a sudden a colossal snake-like creature burst forth from the center of the maelstrom, entangling the whaling ship in its coils and crushing it. With a blood-curdling roar it set about dispatching each and every one of the whaling crew, and then it turned its shark-cold gaze upon you.
        You hadn’t even realized you were screaming the whole time this was going on, you thought it was all your mates belowdecks. When the creature emerged all around your craft, shielding you from the storm in the process, it jarred you right out of your panic to hear it call you by name and tell you “Cease your screaming my child, it is embarrassing for us both!”
        After Ryujin’s stern admonishment you have continued your work on the high seas, attacking those who would defile the seas with their excesses.

            God Level ones

            55) A god of sinister reprutation but indispensable to the pantheons and the war just got murdered. The resident god of the dead confirms the god appeared in their domains without any memory of the assasination. Are there Titanspawn lurking in your Overworlds? Did someone just resolved an old grudge at a very bad moment. Do you even want to bring Loki or Sutekh or Tezcapolica back to life to continue their hinjinks?

            56) You are contacted by the Nemesis who implore you to let her try to punish one of her fellows Avatars. Choice candidates are Kagusutchi for his matricide, Cronos for the castration thing, Mixcoatl of the Aztecs for the possible rape and murder of his mother (plus the quasi extinction of his brothers) or even fellow Order Titan Bress the Beautiful prince of the Fomorian and incarnation of Tyranny. While she assures her punishement will not kill them and that she will reward you for your help in bringing justice to the world (And she is not lying, the Nemesis doesn't lie), there must be a reason your parents gods didn't do a thing like that already. Will you accept or refuse with the risk of being targeted for obstruction to the rightful laws of the universe?

            57) Under the influence of progressive new members the Greek Gods are engaged in peace talks with some of the Titans. While it is out of the question to make peace with Cronos, Gaïa and Ouranos could perhaps be placated. The pantheon has fashioned a relic penis for the latter to be restored to full manhood and be able to take the helm of the Sky Titan. While the Avatar will accept any pact in his state and sure oaths are an important thing to Greeks even Titans, you are not too sure of the consequences of all this. Oh and if you are or know a Greek God with Health, you are charged with the negotiations.

            Not exactly plot hooks but long terms ambitions a Scion could take at CC

            ) Becoming the new Norse god(ess) of the dead letting Hel running free across the worlds and make her own Fate

            ) Even if sacrificing to gods others than the Teotl have no practical effects, worship is a sweet thing especially if you are not in the world risking Fatebonds, You are tasked by your parent to rebuild the old religion and bring back the old practices.That could be problematic.

                58) Well that's just great, you went and got yourself a fate bond and now everybody with a hint of mystical talent is proclaiming you to be the Chosen One.                                                             Well, here we go then. A few of my story hooks which I actually used.

                                                            74) You are a Scion of the Aztecs who got a creature as a gift from your godparent. Sadly, your loyal companion died in your service and your godparent is rightly furious about this. So furious in fact that he demands you bring the creature back. Quiet literally from hell, as you'll descend into the legendary depths of Xibalba, the place of fear, to face its gruesome tests and bring your companion back from the dead.

                                                            75) As if the Amatsukami didn't have their hands full already, The Drowned Road starts making moves against them for no apparent reason. Sending Umibozu, Bake-Kujira and other horrors who possess both the Miasmic and the Piscean template, this attack is a horrible iteration of the thought what would happen when the Titans join forces. It's up to you to find out whether this was just a one-time thing between the fickle Titans, part of a much larger plot or something else entirely. And you will, of course, have to battle against the many strange and horrid things Japanese mythology has to offer when it comes to the vast depths of the sea...

                                                            76) Maybe you crossed the wrong god. Or a Titanspawn that is very adept in Magic. Or, probably, Fate decided to have a laugh on you. Either way you find yourself in the body of another Scion from your band and the rest of them have it no better. Aside of what happens when suddenly the brawny Scion of Ares suddenly has a frail body with a brilliant mind or the booky scholar of Thoth suddenly can punch holes through steel (for some reason, no attributes are swapped...), you have to deal with the fact that this could happen again or probably be a permanent thing. And you've grown to be quiet fond of your bodies. Also, if your bodies and minds are swapped, how can the lovers of your group express their love for each other properly? Hey, get your hands of my body! Don't kiss ME, either!

                                                            77) Kalfu is one of the more nasty gods to cross. And this time you've really done it. When one of the members of your Gang decided not to follow an order issued by him, he turns the tables on you in the most nasty way. The Scion of Bastet gets attacked by all cats she sees, the handsome Son of Amaterasu suddenly has an erectile dysfunction and every drop of alcohol that touches the lips of your Dagda-Scion suddenly turns into water. Luckily, you got a list of things you have to do and every point on this list breaks one of Kalfus curses - but in which order you don't know.
