    if I remember correctly, that's somewhat similar to how it was in oWOD. But it's been many years and I may be quite wrong. I would actually be pretty good with a simpler and perhaps somewhat less defined system. Is the production of Scion 2e constrained to Xd10 sort of systems? As for epics, I just don't think its reasonable to have the triangular scaling. It grossly distorts the possibilities. It makes heroes unable to trick gods and demigods, let alone directly confront them. It makes skills meaningless, and attributes, outside of requiring a minimum amount for epic levels, meaningless as well. I would say that +1 success for an epic dot is actually pretty strong under an Xd10 system. It's really equivalent to 2-4 dice you roll, based on the target number. A god with 10 in an epic stat is a tough customer, but probably even doable by a group of well motivated heroes with some good planning. It might also be a nice place to emphasize that whole "if you don't have boons you're a chump" aspect that is mentioned. Boons give you the edge to overcome epics that might be a bit over you.

        Personally, I have moved onto a One Roll Resolution system similar to what NWOD uses.
        COMBAT
        Scion 2.0 uses a single roll to resolve an attack. You use the most appropriate of your Strength or Dexterity for brawl or melee attacks, and the most appropriate of your Dexterity or Perception for marksmanship or thrown attacks. This result is compared against the Dodge DV or Soak DV of your targets (whichever is higher after all modifiers), and any successes above their DV are dealt as bashing, lethal, or aggravated damage.
        BONUSES AND PENALTIES
        Automatic successes from any source always add to the derived value associated with the Attribute or Ability in question, but bonus dice do not. If something gives you +2 successes to Dexterity then you would also gain +2 Dodge DV and Movement Speed.

        Aggravated = ½ Soak DV.
        Grappled = ½ Dodge DV.
        Unexpected = ½ Dodge DV and Soak DV.

                                                        I'm not up to date on this thread, and I'm not sure I want to spend the time to get up to date, but this little anecdote might prove useful.

                                                        My friend was considering running a Scion game for our group and was unsure of the viability of it. Another friend (one with more experience as a ST) offered to run a brief canned adventure in order to show him what it was like. So the prospective ST made up a middling combat character with a good amount of magical oomph and I made up a buff tank Scion of... I forget. I decided I wanted to be properly buff so I asked if my character could have a coat that was made from the pelt of a Nemean Boar as armor. After all, the soak it gave me was less than what I already had so how bad could it be?

                                                        Very bad, as it turned out, since in that adventure (a pre-made adventure set in WWII, not sure what it was called or where it was from) there was a very explicit line in it. It said "Player character are supposed to go overland at this point. If they travel down the main road, they will not survive for the enemy forces are too numerous."

                                                        Well, that was a load of bollocks, because my tank was perfectly capable of striding straight up to a tank, shrugging off direct hits from its main cannon and using his sword to lever off the top of the tank like the lid of a can and dicing up the soldiers inside like so much dried spinach. All while having the rest of the german army division emptying their machine guns at him on full auto coordinated fire. None of it mattered to him, he soaked it all and didn't take a single box of health damage without having to spend a single point of legend or willpower.

                                                        Obviously, this is an extreme example, but when a character can be capable of this at character creation, obviously there is an issue.

                                                        Again, not sure if this is relevant but I thought since this is a thread about Scien 2e mechanics, this might warrant attention.
