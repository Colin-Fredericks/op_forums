    It’s funny, my view of a Deicon is less ComicCon and more RifleCon.

    Relic hunters would be more mercs (sweaty people venturing in dangerous dark dirty places) than deifans (people hoping to get treasures without risking their lives). They would offer guided visits to well known places and maybe some more expensive trips to less known places they had already been.
    People would not cosplay as gods or heroes (that is the kind of thing that got cities destroyed back then), but there would be some theater plays on the con telling the stories of myths (with proper respect to the gods).
    Fanfics would not be a thing (the real story is far more interesting), but the poetry/ rap/ music contests would be awesome.
    Heroes showing up and giving autographs, giving tips on how to get heroic muscles, showing some prizes (some of them as monster body parts), telling stories about how the Terra incognita adventures went, making tributes for the lost heroes.
    For sure there would be a some kiosks selling some low power relics with maybe one good relic behind a glass with a tag with too many digits.

                            Been thinking on the presence of Heroes in Deicon.
                            While i think there is a fair bit of scion at the Origin presence (those that know of their divine heritage probably lead panels on their life experiences and such) i think that common wisdom is that there are few if any Hero tier scions at Deicon.
                            While fatebinding isnt as harsh for Heroes i think its a bit dangerous to put yourself in close proximity to people who would be obsessed with getting fatebound.

                            That said, thats only common wisdom, in truth there is a surprising number of Heroes who attend incognito and the 'Con is a good opportunity to meet and connect with other Heroes.
                            There is a pervasive rumor of Heroes only events and after parties that people are desperatly trying to find.

                            A Demigod officially attending is probably the event of the century.

                            While in my games Gods son't involve themselves directly in the World as their god tiered power (thats what scions are for after all), it wouldnt be surprinsing for some gods to have a mortal tier incarnate going there to have fun.
