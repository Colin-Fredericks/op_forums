                                    165) Ah, Thanksgiving! A time in which the American people celebrate the day the pilgrims came together and broke bread with the natives. Most people today like to conveniently ignore the events that came afterward, or just move straight on to thinking about Christmas. Well, the Gods are not most people, and they have very, very long memories. Every year around this time, tensions run high between the Yankee pantheon and the spiritual deities of the native cultures, with the Scions being the ones stuck in the middle. Well, not this time, you say! We're going to have a proper thanksgiving festival, and this time we're inviting the gods to sit down and clear the air between them!

                                    Just don't think about the fact that some of the Yankees might have actually taken part in the massacres prior to their apotheosis...

                                            166) Titanspawn are getting pissed off... more so than usual. It was all fun and games when the Gods and Scions were the only ones who worried about Fatebonds, but now even the self respecting monsters are getting screwed over by mortals! We are, of course, referring to the creatures of the night (vampires, werewolves, ghosts, est). They used to be horrifying, causing mortals to lock their doors and windows at night, praying the monsters weren't already inside the house. But now, some asshole Scions in charge of public broadcasting have been spinning the stories of how the creatures are brooding, misunderstood individuals who just want to be loved. It's disgusting, and an insult to to the pride of the Titanspawn and their servants!

                                            But the greatest threat of all isn't that the Gods are trying to humiliate these monsters, it's that they're trying to recruit them! The plan is to humanize the Titanspawn as much as possible, and effectively sever their connection to the Greater Titans, just like the humans once did for the Gods.

                                            This can not stand. It's time mortals remembered what true fear is. The creatures of darkness are about to step into the light, and when they do, mortals will be praying like hell that they go back to whence they came!

                                            (A reverse chronicle in which you play as servants of the Titans)

                                                167) The thu'um, he has the thu'um! Well, okay, it's actually just a new Strength knack he developed that allows him to roar with the force of a shockwave. So when he went around claiming he was Dovahkiin, everybody had a good laugh. That is until the sky above Mt. Everest opened up and a huge black dragon flew out of it. They call themselves the World Eater, and the Valkyries have been unable to do their jobs because an impenetrable fog has rolled into Valhalla!

                                                Enough with the jokes, an army of European dragons have sworn themselves to the World Eater's banner and descended upon the race of mortals. It's open season on dragon hunting for the duration of this crisis. Unfortunately, the only way to stop all of this is for the self proclaimed Dovahkiin to fulfill the obligations of their Fatebond and slay the World Eater in personal combat!

                                                        169) A thorough study of the Greco-Roman pantheon reveals that the gods held very different personalities and roles between which culture they were depicted in.
