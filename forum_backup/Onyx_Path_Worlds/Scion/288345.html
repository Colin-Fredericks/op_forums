    Hyrule Pantheon (Zelda Conversion)

    10-21-2014, 01:47 AM
    ((Accidentally posted this over on Vampire. ))


    The following information is a conversion setting for the Legend of Zelda into the Scion universe.



    Hyrule Pantheon: The land of Hyrule is a magical realm overseen by a great many gods and goddesses. Magic runs strong throughout the realm, and many strange and fantastic races have evolved over the years. But it was not always so, for Hyrule did not even exist until the 3 goddesses Nayru, Din, and Farore came onto the scene. It is unclear if these goddesses were Scions from the Earth pantheons, or if they were completely separate, for they did not stick around very long to record their origins.

    Din, the Goddess of Power created the landmasses of Hyrule. Farore, the Goddess of Courage created all the life forms that dwelt upon Hyrule. And Nayru, the Goddess of Wisdom created all knowledge within Hyrule, giving the people the gift of laws and magic. With their work completed, the satisfied goddesses left Hyrule, but not before infusing all of their essence into a relic known as the Triforce.

    The legends of the Triforce state that it can only be wielded by someone who possesses all 3 traits of Wisdom, Courage, and Power, and that it has the ability to grant the wishes of the one who possesses it. Fearing what may become of its misuse, the Sages locked the Triforce within the Golden Realm and sealed the gate. Eons later, Ganondorf found a way into the Golden Realm and attempted to claim the Triforce. However, he was not worthy of controlling its full power, so upon his touch, the Triforce broke into 3 pieces. The Triforce of Power remained within Ganondorf, but the Triforce of Courage and Wisdom left to find more appropriate hosts, eventually choosing Link and Zelda as their wielders. Enraged, Ganondorf set out at once to conquer Hyrule, and claim the full power of the Triforce for himself.


    Many times would Link and Zelda clash against Ganondorf, who eventually became Ganon. Every time they sealed him away, he would return many years later and renew his assault. But the times are finally changing. The return of the Titans has thrown the balance of power into complete dissaray for Hyrule. Creatures much more powerful than Ganon have begun to enter the realm and lay claim to its territories. Not even Link can stand against these new foes by himself, and the once seemingly invincible Ganon now finds himself in need of powerful allies if he is to hold onto his title as the Demon King.

    The time has come for new Heroes to take to the field, whether they fight in service to the crown, or if they have been seduced by Ganon's promises of power is different for each one. Those who prove themselves worthy may even go onto become immortalized in Hyrule legend!


    One thing that sets the Hyrule Pantheon apart from those of Earth is that the realm is in a constant state of Ragnarok. In the endless war between Link and Ganon's forces, the various deities of the realm have been slain, and their stations passed to their descendants. Because of this ever changing state of governance, Scions are very common within this realm, for the gods are constantly in need for an heir to their thrones if they should ever meet their fate!

    Therefor, the Gods of Hyrule do not live apart from their worshipers very often, but instead live alongside them. Sometimes in their very cities, and other times within some manner of shrine or temple. By living in close relationship with their worshipers, it is much easier to find willing mates to pass on their legacy, than seducing random strangers.



    --------------------------------------


    Realms: Much like the Aesir, the Hyrule Pantheon has a multitude of different realms which they preside over. This is a list of each world.


    Hyrule: The land of Hyrule is where the worshipers of the Hyrule pantheon live, and also serves as the battleground for which Link and Ganon battle for the realm's fate. Although the kingdom is still in the middle ages in terms of cultural advancement, the realm is drenched with magical energy, allowing its people to build wondrous architecture and utilize strange abilities. It is not very hard to acquire a Relic of godly power if you search around long enough. Hyrule Kingdom is primarily ruled by the Hylian royal family, but the other kingdoms are vassal states who live under their rule. Hyrule is home to many races including the Gerudo, the Zora, the Kokiri, the Deku, the Animal Folk, the Gorons, the Fairies, and of course the Hylians. The gateway between Hyrule and Earth is located within the Lost Woods, home to Fairies, Deku, and Kokiri tribes. To cross between the realms, one must travel deep into this enchanted forest, navigating the dangerous creatures and magical illusions that threaten the inexperienced wanderers.


    Golden Realm: The home world of the gods and the former resting place for the Triforce. The Golden Realm is sacred even to the divinities of Hyrule, and only those who pass the trial are permitted access. The gateway to the Golden Realm is within the Temple of Time, but the gate can only be unlocked by acquiring the 3 spiritual stones possessed by the Kokiri, Goron, and Zora people, along with playing the Song of Time upon the Ocarina of time. The Golden Realm is said to be a paradise, but only a privileged few ever lay eyes upon it.


    Dark World: When Ganon infiltrated the Golden Realm and laid his hands upon the Triforce, the Dark World was born. In this realm, Ganon reigns supreme and breeds his army of monsters and dark Scions to wage his war upon Hyrule. The Dark World is said to be a nightmarish image of the Golden Realm and normal people can not retain their forms upon crossing into its lands. In fact, mortals who enter the Dark World are changed into a reflection of their true selves. When Link first entered the Dark World, his innocent heart transformed him into a harmless, pink bunny. It was not until after he obtained the Moon Pearl that he was able to travel the Dark World in his Hylian form.


    Termina: The underworld for the souls of the dead. Although Termina is not necessarily terrifying, it is a bleak world that some visitors may find unnerving if they linger there too long. Termina is comprised of 5 territories, each one of which is dedicated to one of the emotional stages a person goes through before they die. Clocktown is the place where all new souls arrive and it embodies Denial. Clocktown is usually a joyful and carefree place that tends to overlook problems and believe they'll work themselves out. A fine starting point for those so new to death. The Deku Kingdom is located in the swamps and embodies Anger. The waters can be poisonous, and there are lots of deadly predators looking to feast upon unwary travelers. After that, we have the Goron village which embodies Bargaining, for they are a proud race of strong warriors who do not easily recognize when they have lost. Following the Gorons is the Zora village who embodies Despair, for they are constantly under attack by Gerudo pirates looking to enslave their people and plunder their temples. Finally, Ikana Canyon embodies Acceptance, for it is a dismal place that is home to ghosts and other undead. But while Termina may be a depressing land, it may not be the final resting place for the souls of Hyrule. Some legends claim that if a soul is successfully able to navigate through all 5 stages of grief, their soul will find peace and they can move onto paradise.

      Members: The Hyrule Pantheon is constantly swapping out its members because of the war between Ganon and the people of Hyrule. Some members of the pantheon, however, remain constant, and those are the ones described below.



      Link: The God of Time for the Hyrule pantheon, Link was once a mortal hero, but eventually ascended to godhood during the course of his adventures. He is the keeper for the Triforce of Courage, one of the three pieces of the goddesses that created Hyrule. Link possesses a form of immortality that allows him to reincarnate after each death. He always returns as a blond haired, Hylian boy, and always by the name of Link, but where he is born and how he is raised differs between life times. He wields the legendary Master Sword, a blade that has the power to repel magic and slay all forms of evil. His signature outfit is a green tunic, white tights underneath, and a pointy green hat.

      Link is a master in virtually all forms of weaponry, and has wielded a vast arsenal of relics over the course of his lives. However, he is most commonly seen to wield a sword and shield, a boomerang, a bow, a grappling hook, and black powder explosives as his weapons of choice while the other tools of his trade change with the times. Although he has been shown to control elemental magic depending on his incarnations, he is more proficient with weapons than the mystic arts. In terms of skill, Link is a warrior who has very few equals. Throughout many life times, he has singlehandedly faced the legions of monsters that Ganon has at his disposal and defeated them all! Truly he is worthy of the position of Overgod for the Hyrule pantheon! Link's Triforce of Courage is symbolized by a mark on his hand that resembles a pyramid formed out of 3 triangles. The top and bottom left triangles are darkened out, but the bottom right triangle is bright yellow.

      Evidently, Link appears to be one of those Gods who can't keep it in his pants. It seems that everywhere he goes, he finds some way to woo not only mortals, but the other goddesses themselves. Link would not be surprised if he discovers the women of Hyrule have established some kind of Link fanclub!


      Purviews: War, Sky, Fire, Animal, Guardian

      Epics: Charisma, Strength, Stamina, Dexterity



      Ganon: The God of Evil and once lived as a thief by the name of Ganondorf. After stealing the Triforce of Power, Ganondorf became the king of demons, obtaining immortality and nearly limitless magical power. He possesses two forms which he changes between in and out of battle. Although he is vulnerable to the Master Sword, it can not truly slay him, but instead merely seals him away for a number of years. He keeps finding a way to escape, however, and in the past, Link has repeatedly sealed him away again. But with his powers now further increased, Link may need the aid of his Scions to finish the job.

      When out of battle, he prefers his original form, that of the Gerudo King. In this form, he has dark skin, fiery red hair, and is quite tall and muscular. He is usually garbed in armor and wields a two handed sword. When in a serious battle, he takes on his demon form, which resembles a giant anthropomorphic boar. While in this state, his weapon of choice becomes a trident, and he heavily relies on magical attacks. As with Link, he too has the same golden triangle mark on his hand, but instead he has the top triangle filled in while the bottom ones are darkened out.

      Purviews: Magic, Darkness, Fire, Health, Death, Frost, Animal

      Epics: Strength, Intelligence, Stamina.



      Zelda: Originally known as Hylia, the previous Overgod of the Hyrule pantheon, she later renounced her divinity and became Zelda so she could live alongside Link as a normal girl. After obtaining the Triforce of Wisdom, she became the leader of the Sages for a while, until the Titans appeared and she was forced to reclaim her former station as Overgod to keep them at bay. Zelda is the princess of the Hyrule royal family, and possesses the ability to foretell the future. This has aided her immensely in the fight against Ganon's quest to enslave her kingdom, and she has been a friend to Link throughout nearly all his incarnations.

      Zelda resembles a beautiful Hylian girl with blond hair reaching down to her waist. She is a powerful sorceress, gifted in the magical arts of deception, fire manipulation, teleportation, and force fields. At times, she has adopted the identity of Sheik, a male disguise for when she wishes not to be recognized. While in Sheik form, she is very much like a ninja, using a variety of concealed weapons, superb acrobatics, and misdirection to engage her foes. Finally, she is a master archer, and uses the Light Arrows for her ammunition. Legends say that these arrows are powerful enough to stun even Ganon with a direct shot! Zelda has the same mark as Link and Ganon, but it is the bottom left triangle that is bright yellow, and the other two who are darkened out.

      Purviews: Fire, Guardian, Prophecy, Illusion, Psychopomp, Sun

      Epics: Appearance, Manipulation, Wits, Dexterity




      The Sages: Throughout the history of Hyrule, there have been those who hold the rank of Sage. The Sages are low ranking gods and goddesses of different elemental forces of Hyrule and have included the ranks of Light, Shadow, Fire, Water, Earth, Wind, Wood and Spirit. The Sages usually live within temples dedicated to them, and serve as the spiritual guides to the people who congregate there for worship. The position of Sage is hereditary, and when a current Sage dies (for whatever reason), their position is passed onto the next worthy candidate. Traditionally, each of the Sages are selected from one of the races of Hyrule that appropriately suit their element.

      Hylians are chosen for Light, Sheikah are chosen for Shadow, Gorons are chosen for Fire, Zora are chosen for Water, Deku are chosen for Earth, Rito are chosen for Wind, Kokiri are chosen for Wood, and Gerudo are chosen for Spirit. However, in dire circumstances, these traditions have been lifted if there are no worthy candidates among the normal selections. There have been times in the past, when the entire group of Sages were Hylians, but these were rare and it has only happened twice in recorded history.

      Although the Sages may individually hold the station of lesser gods, their combined power is quite formidable. By drawing upon their combined magic, Zelda was able to immobilize Ganon himself with a blast of magic during one of their battles. There is even one legend that speak of a time in which the Sages managed to subdue Ganon and were preparing to execute him, only to discover his immortality, much to their despair. While they were still in shock, Ganon broke free of his restraints and killed one of the Sages with his bare hands before making his escape.


      Purviews: Fire, Sun, Darkness, Fertility, Health, Water, Magic, Earth, Sky, Mystery

      Epics: Strength, Intelligence, Perception, Stamina, Dexterity




      Dungeon Lords: Ganon may be the king of evil, but he is not the only evil god in Hyrule. The Dungeon Lords are gods and goddesses who have pledged their service to Ganon and function as generals in his army. True to their name, they are given an expansive dungeon as their realm, along with a private force of monsters to govern over. The dungeon functions as their base of operations, while they lead the attack upon Hyrule. Just like the Sages, Dungeon Lords are often a hereditary station, and when a Dungeon Lord is slain, they are replaced at Ganon's whim.

      Ganon's first step towards dominating Hyrule, is usually to attack the Temples and dispose of the Sages. To this end, the Dungeon Lords are often chosen among those who have powers mirroring the Sages themselves. Sometimes the Dungeon Lords imprison the Sages within their temples, leaving them unable to lend aid to their people. Other times they simply kill the Sages and corrupt the temple while Hyrule is busy trying to find a replacement for them.

      In appearance, the Dungeon Lords often resemble monsters and demons, as opposed to members of the Hylian races. It is also common that they are of great size, counting themselves among giants in terms of stature. Generally speaking, each Dungeon Lord is unique in their appearance, though there have been cases in which Scions come to resemble their parents in some ways if they end up replacing them.


      Purviews: Fire, Sun, Darkness, Fertility, Death, Water, Magic, Earth, Sky, Mystery

      Epics: Strength, Stamina, Dexterity, Perception, Wits



      Great Deku Tree: Guardian of the Lost Woods. This ancient being is sacred to both the Kokiri and the Deku people. He is an enormous tree, who's roots span an entire dungeon worth of territory. The Great Deku Tree is known for his wisdom and sage advice, as well as lending his protection to the Kokiri tribes. Although he is immobile, the Great Deku Tree's magic is powerful enough to establish peace throughout the forest.

      Aside from resembling a colossal tree, the Great Deku Tree is recognizable by the face of an old man located at its trunk, complete with bushy eyebrows and sunken eyes. His mouth is the entrance to his body, and Link once had to venture inside to cleanse the tree of a curse.

      Purviews: Fertility, Guardian, Earth, Prophecy, Magic.

      Epics: Stamina, Intelligence, Perception




      Jabu Jabu: God of the seas. A gargantuan fish who is sacred to the Zora people. Much like the Great Deku Tree, his body is so large that his insides qualify as a dungeon, although a pretty disgusting one at that. In the past, Jabu Jabu once swallowed Ruto, the future Sage of Water, but Link ventured into his bowels to retriever her. Finding a monster was making the sea god ill, Link destroyed it to cure him.

      Jabu Jabu mostly feeds on fish, the non-hyper evolved kind. The Zora people offer him sacrifices and he in kind protects them. He is not one for words, however and instead prefers silent meditation as he watches over his faithful.

      Purviews: Water, Prophecy, Guardian, Psychopomp, Magic

      Epics: Stamina, Intelligence, Perception




      Valoo: God of the sky. He is a very fat dragon who lives atop a mountain and is sacred to the Rito people. During their childhood, all Rito make a pilgrimage to Valoo, and he grants them one of his scales, which gives them the power to transform their arms into wings, obtaining the power of flight. He is a joyful deity, who loves music, but when he is agitated, he can rain destruction upon the world below. Once, Ganon sent a Dungeon Lord to torment Valoo, and in his rage, the children of Rito could not safely make the pilgrimage. Link had to journey to the inner sanctum of the mountain and slay the dark god that had been causing him such torment.

      Not much is known about Valoo's fighting capabilities, for he is normally a pacifist and spends his time in meditation. When he was being tortured is probably the only time the Rito have ever seen him in such an angry state. However, he is a dragon, so it can be assumed he is of great power if he ever feels the desire to show it.


      Purview: Sky, Fire, Magic, Guardian, Prophecy

      Epics: Stamina, Charisma, Strength, Intelligence




      Happy Mask Salesman: The god of joy and trade. The Happy Mask Salesman is a mysterious figure who travels throughout Hyrule selling masks to people in an effort to make them happy. Some of the masks he sells are powerful relics and possess magical abilities. Others are simply decorative costumes. Little is known about this strange man, nobody even seems to know his real name. He simply appears out of nowhere to do business, and then leaves as suddenly as he arrived when he feels his work is done.

      The Happy Mask Salesman is a very strange figure. Rather than shift facial expressions like a normal person, his features instantly swap expressions in the blink of an eye, as if he were switching to a new mask. He resembles a Hylian male with pale skin and red hair, and although he looks rather scrawny, he is strong enough to carry a pack of supplies nearly 4 times his body weight! The Happy Mask Salesman has also been speculated to be bipolar or insane, for his emotional state fluctuates drastically at the slightest provocation!

      Not much is fully known about the Happy Mask Salesman's full abilities, but over the years, he has demonstrated the ability to lift curses even the ones imposed by godly figures, teleportation across the realm, precognitive information gathering, and possesses a wide array of masks, some of which are magical in nature.


      Purviews: Health, Magic, Psychopomp, Mystery, Prophecy

      Epics: Strength, Dexterity, Intelligence, Charisma



      Great Fairies: Goddesses of Magic, the Great Fairies exist throughout the land of Hyrule and they are sacred to the Fairy race. Legends state that the Great Fairies will bestow a boon upon any Scions who visit them in their shrines. When Link visited them, they gave him the power to use magic, as well as teaching him spells like Nayru's Love, Farore's Wind, and Din's Fire. Unlike their worshipers who are tiny enough to fit in one's palm, the Great Fairies are quite large, standing roughly twice the height of a grown Hylian. They also do not have wings and appear to float with the aid of magic.

      Purviews: Magic, Health, Guardian, Fire, Sky, Psychopomp

      Epics: Appearance, Charisma, Intelligence, Stamina.

          People of Hyrule: The land of Hyrule is home to many different races and ethnicities. Upon awakening to their divine lineage or being adopted into it, the Scions pledge themselves to one of the following people and over time they grow to resemble the tribe they feel most associated with. By the time they reach full godhood, they may even fully resemble a native of Hyrule, although with much more extravagant traits than a simple mortal.


          Hylian: A race of elf-like beings, the Hylians resemble humans for the most part, but they have long, pointed ears. According to ancient legends, these ears give them the power to hear the voices of the gods, but not many Hylians claim to hear voices in their heads, so this may be a simple myth. Hylians are the largest population in Hyrule, and as the dominant race, most of Hyrule's Scions are from their people.


          Kokiri: The people of the forest, Kokiri look very similar to Hylians except for one major difference, they never grow up! Throughout their entire life span, the Kokiri resemble children, and usually have the mannerisms of such. All Kokiri have a Fairy who watches over them and acts like a parental figure. Offering guidance and advice over the course of their lives. Despite the fact that the Kokiri resemble young boys and girls, they still reproduce... somehow... Probably best not to question it.


          Fairies: Tiny, sprite-like beings who possess great magical talent, but little in the way of physical capabilities. The fairy's body is small enough to fit in your palm, yet their magical talents are miraculous in nature. It is said that fairies have the power to restore a person even if they are on the brink of death, and they have fashioned powerful Relics such as the Fairy Bow, and the Great Fairy Sword. Being too small to run very fast, the Fairies instead fly through the air with their wings.


          Goron: A race of orange skinned people with living rock instead of body hair. With a backside covered in jagged stone, the Gorons like to curl into balls and roll across the field, mowing down enemies in their path. The feed on rocks, absorbing the minerals contained within, and are extremely resilient to heat. To the point of living near volcanoes! Gorons are the physically strongest race in Hyrule, but they can not handle cold very well, and they are completely incapable of swimming.



          Zora: These amphibious beings were once fish that swam in the waters of Lake Hylia, but the magic contained within the lake forced them to evolve into the creatures they are today. Zora resemble anthropomorphic fish of various different species. Because they spend the majority of their lives in the water, clothing is a foreign concept to them. Most Zoras walk around completely nude except for when attending a formal occasion when fancy dress is expected. Thankfully for those not accustomed to dealing with Zora, their reproductive organs appear to be internal, reducing the level of discomfort they might cause. As with the Gorons, they can not handle the cold very well, and are placed into a hibernation sleep when encased in ice. Female Zora appear to be rare, but those who exist give birth to many eggs at a time, so it helps balance out to lack of suitable mates. When Zora are first born, it is in a tadpole like state, and they grow appendages and more humanoid bodies as they reach maturity.


          Sheikah: In ages past, the Sheikah once served as the bodyguards to Hyrule's royal family, but now they are a dying breed. The Sheikah culture is heavily based on Ninjutsu, and its warriors are trained in the arts of stealth, martial arts, magic, and athleticism. Sheikah resemble Hylians, but have pale skin tones, red eyes, and purple hair.



          Gerudo: A race of thieves from the desert. The Gerudo have dark skin and most of them have fiery red hair. Almost the entire race is female, with only a single male Gerudo born every 100 years. That male grows up to become their king and rule the Gerudo people. Although a true Gerudo male comes around once a century, Hylian males can become honorary Gerudo if they successfully pass an initiation trial. This trial involves defeating 3 Gerudo captains in battle. It is widely believed those who pass this trial are the males they choose to mate with, for surely their king would not be able to maintain the entire population over the next 100 years.



          Deku: A race of living plants, the Deku have solid orange eyes and circular shaped mouths. Their bodies are made of wood, and they usually dress in clothing fashioned out of leaves. They have the power to quickly burrow into the earth, disguising themselves as ordinary plants, and they can spit great distances to attack foes with projectiles launched from their mouth. The Deku body is so light that it can outrun most Hylians, skip across the surface of water for short trips, and float upon the currents of wind. The Deku are extremely vulnerable to fire and ice, however, but that's to be expected from a race of plant people.



          Animal Folk: Not all beasts in Hyrule are ordinary animals. In fact, some of them are highly intelligent. Possessing the capacity for speech, and a level of intellect rivaling a Hylian, the Animal Folk are more at home in the wilds than in the city, but have been known to lend their aid when they see fit. Kaepora Gaebora, the Owl is one of the most iconic Animal Folk, lending his wisdom to travelers. Others have included Moosh (A blue, flying bear), Ricky (A boxing Kangaroo), and Dimitri (A friendly Dodongo).


          Rito: A race of bird folk who claim to have evolved from the Zora. Rito have a beak for a nose, and talons for feet, while their body is covered in feathers. Although they can not fly naturally, all Rito children undergo a right of pilgrimage to meet with Valoo, the dragon. He bestows them with one of his scales, which gives the Rito the ability to transform their arms into wings, obtaining the power of flight, at the cost of losing their hands temporarily!

                  Virtues:



                  Courage - From terrifying monsters, to righteous defenders, the Scions of Hyrule will be faced with all manner of proud and powerful foes on their path towards divinity. Those who don't have the strength to overcome such trials are doomed to fade away, their Legends dwindling before they even begin.


                  Conviction - The war between light and darkness has been waged upon Hyrule for as long as anybody can remember. A lesser soul might grow weary of the eternal struggle between good and evil. As a Scion, you will be expected to have the strength to defend your beliefs even should they seem hopeless. Failure to do so will see you snuffed out by those who wish you dead, or worse, converted to their side!


                  Expression - Within the kingdom of Hyrule, music has power. Throughout history there have been many songs and melodies written by great masters which unleash magical capabilities for those skilled enough to play them. Members of this pantheon are likewise encouraged to leave their mark on the world in some grand fashion


                  Valor - Only the most powerful Scions of this pantheon manage to survive throughout the ages. A member who seeks to join the divine ranks of Hyrule must be prepared to accept death, hopefully if it comes in the form of aiding those who depend upon them. Valor is a far more important Virtue for Link's forces than Ganon's, however. On the other hand, Ganon's most devoted servants may be prepared to offer their lives in service to his ambitions...

                      Titan - Hundun, great Titan of Chaos: The land of Hyrule has been built on a world of peaceful harmony. The gods and their temples ensure the land of Hyrule remains in balance by governing over its various aspects both elemental and conceptual. But when that balance is disrupted, it threatens to destroy Hyrule. The Titan which poses the most threat to Hyrule and its gods is Hundun, known for corrupting the gods and disrupting the harmony of the land. The evil gods of Hyrule have a tendency for corrupting the temples and poisoning the environment, so it is no surprise that many have begun to speculate that Ganon and his army may be under Hundun's influence. Driving them to disrupt the land and throw Hyrule into turmoil!


                      Ganon swears he is not under any Titan's control, but how can his arguments be taken seriously, when he so closely resembles a servant to the Titan? Hundun may not have Titanspawn of his own, but frankly, he doesn't need any. Ganon is quite adept at making his own monsters. All Hundun has to do is sit back and watch the fun play out, with the occasional influence on his part when he feels things beginning to slip away from his grasp.

                                      Relics of Hyrule: The kingdom of Hyrule has many legends and even more relics tied to those legends. Scions of this pantheon have a massive arsenal of trinkets they can inherit! In fact, there are so many relics, there isn't enough room to list them all. Below are just a handful of examples one can use, however.


                                      Kokiri Sword: A sacred relic of the Kokiri people, Link used this blade for years until he was big enough to handle adult sized weaponry. The Kokiri Sword is only the size of a dagger when used by adults, but for the child-like Kokiri, it's the closest thing they have to a real sword.



                                      Great Fairy Sword: Forged by the Great Fairy of Kindness, this two handed sword was bestowed to Link after he retrieved the 15 pieces of her essence which had been stolen. The blade is rainbow hued with a mixture of green, orange, and purple. The blade also has black roses engraved upon it. Despite it's size, it is amazingly light, as Link was able to wield it in his childhood years.



                                      Hylia Shield: Forged by the goddess Hylia, the original Hylia Shield is said to be indestructable. The shield is so iconic, that replicates of its likeness have been mass produced for use by the Knights of Hyrule. The Hylia Shield is blue, and bares the insignia of the red Loftwing, along with the Triforce.



                                      Sage Medallion: These medallions are passed down from the Sages to their successors, and they bare the crest of the temples they are associated with. The medallions teach young Sages how to focus their divine powers, but a fully realized (God) Sage does not need to rely on such a trinket.



                                      Master Sword: The sword of evil's bane. It was created in ancient times by the first generation of Sages using their combined godly power. The Master Sword has powerful enchantments that allow it to deflect light based magic by striking it with the blade, and has the power to bypass the otherwise inpentrable defenses of evil beings. The Master Sword is one of the few Relics in all of Hyrule that can inflict harm to Ganon, but it has a life of it's own. The Master Sword can only be wielded by those who it deems worthy, only it only chooses those who have good souls. Historically, Link has been it's most iconic user. But the appearance of the Titans, and the presence of foreign pantheons has caused him to loan the sword to other Scions on their quest for ascension.


                                      Megaton Hammer: Long ago, a Hero of the Gorons used this mighty weapon to decapitate a dragon who had been feasting on his tribe. Since then, the Megaton Hammer became a sacred treasure and was passed down from one successor to the next. When it is not being wielded, the hammer is enshrined in the Fire Temple, where the dragon and Hero battled each other.


                                      Fairy Bow: This sacred bow was fashioned with the use of Hyrule Fae magic and enshrined within the Forest Temple. It is small enough to be wielded by a child, suggesting that it was intended to be given to the Kokiri. Despite it's small size, the Fairy Bow has the same destructive power as you would expect from an adult sized weapon, in addition to whatever divine powers its wielder might possess.



                                      Fire Rod: A magic rod that gives its wielder the power to control flames. The rod has a red hand grip, and a spherical ruby embedded as the headpiece. Not only was it useful in combat, Link also called upon it's power to solve various puzzles, and to provide additional light in darkened rooms.


                                      Ice Rod: A magic rod that gives its wielder the power to control ice. The rod has a blue hand grip, and a pyramid shaped sapphire as the headpiece. Link used its power both for combat purposes, and to solve the various puzzles he encountered in his adventures.


                                      Din's Fire: A jewel containing the power of flame. It is said that this relic was created by Din, herself. Before she became one with the Triforce. When it was given to Link, he gained the power to create fiery explosions from his body. When he later gave the relic to Zelda, she demonstrated the ability to hurl fiery projectiles.


                                      Farore's Wind: A jewel containing the power of wind. It is said this relic was created by Farore, before she became one with the Triforce. When it was bestowed to Link, he gained the ability to teleport himself from one location, to a prerecorded one. When he gave it to Zelda, she gained the ability to teleport short distances, but was not restricted to a single destination.


                                      Nayru's Love: A jewel containing the power of protection. It is said this relic was created by Nayru before she became one with the Triforce. When it was bestowed to Link, he gained the ability to use barrier magic. He later gave it to Zelda, where she obtained the same ability.


                                      Light Arrow: A relic that has been passed down through the royal family of Hyrule. These sacred arrows are powered by the archer's magic, and release holy light on impact with their target. They are extremely potent against the undead, and other foes susceptible to light.


                                      Ice Arrow: A relic that is sacred to the Gerudo people. These arrows draw their power from the archer's magic, and release a blast of super cold air on impact with a target. Weaker enemies can even be frozen solid!


                                      Fire Arrow: Enchanted arrows which draw strength from the archer's magic. They release a fiery blast on impact, setting the targets alight! That is assuming they're not incinerated by the blast.


                                      Deku Leaf: A gigantic leaf plucked from the Great Deku Tree. Swinging the leaf like a fan produces powerful gusts of wind, and it can also be used to glide and parachute across distances.


                                      Big Goron Sword: A weapon forged by giant Gorons, the sword is nearly twice the size of a man and requires both hands to wield properly. What it lacks in defense, however, it makes up for in offense with its enormous reach.
