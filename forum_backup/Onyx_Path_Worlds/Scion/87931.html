    Morpheus as a Deity. Help Round Him Out?

    03-10-2014, 12:29 AM
    I have a friend who is hosting a home campaign, and I would like to be a scion of Morpheus, the Greek god, not the DC character. I borrowed a power from Vampire called Insomneum (Found here: http://mentalyunsound.squarespace.co...insomnium.html).

    So far, here is a rough outline:

    Nature:
    Defender
    Morpheus has many responsibilities, but he will protect his family above all.

    Suggested Associated Attributes:
    Physical
    Epic Dexterity [wings]
    Social
    Epic Manipulation
    Epic Appearance [Eyes]
    Mental
    Epic Intelligence
    Epic Wits

    Abilities:
    Art, Awareness, Empathy, Occult, Presence, Survival

    Suggested Birthright(s)
    Star Dust [Dream Sand]
    Looking Glass
    Imaginary Friend (Guide)

    Rivals:
    Tsuki-Yomi, Quetzalcoatl, Damballa, Ptah, Amravati (Devas)

    Suggested Knacks

    Dexterity:
    Untouchable Opponent (wings?)
    Microscopic Precision
    Photographic Penmanship

    Stamina:
    Extended Youth (dreams are timeless)

    Charisma:
    Charmer
    Overt Order
    Knowing Glance

    Appearance:
    My Eyes Are Up Here
    Perfect Actor

    Perception:
    Subliminal Warning
    Fool Me Once
    Real McCoy

    Intelligence:
    Perfect Memory
    Language Mastery
    Telepathy

    Wits:
    Meditative Focus
    Social Chameleon
    Psychic Profilier

    Suggested Purviews:
    Animal (Firefly, Raven) [Two animals *I* associate with dreaming/sleeping]
    Arete (Pantheon specific)
    Illusion?
    Prophesy
    Dreaming (homebrew, see Insomneum)

    Suggested Birthright(s):
    Star Dust [Dream Sand]
    Looking Glass
    Imaginary Friend (Guide)

    What I am looking for is some input for maybe a relic type weapon (maybe a staff or spear?)
    I don't want to make him overpowered, and my character is not physically a combatant.
    Thoughts?

      Sorry to break it to you, but Morpheus does very, very little in mythology, so giving him a stat sheet is going to be difficult. He's not even the only dream God, only one of the many sons of Hypnos. I note that the Defender Nature is not from the book, so I'm assuming it's homebrew? Well, if you're going to make up Natures for him, Artist might be better. He shows no particular devotion to his family, beyond just doing what his father says, but he is a crafter of dreams.

      The problem with giving him Associateds is that Morpheus is not, by any stretch of the imagination, a Legend 12 God. SO, if you were to go by the book, he would not have any Purviews at all. But, if you wish to do away with that rule (as is commendable), he should have Illusion. That's really it. If you have a homebrew Dream Purview, he should have that INSTEAD of Illusion, not in addition to it. I'm just going by my houserules in which Dream type powers fall under Illusion. As for Attributes, Appearance for his constant shapeshifting might be appropriate, or Manipulation.

      I could probably help you better if I knew how you handle sub-Legend 12 Gods. Morpheus is really not deserving of anything more than Legend 9, and by my House Rules Legend 9 Gods just get one Associated power and that's it. I would give him Illusion/Dreams and be done with it.

      P.S. Amaravati is a place in Hindu mythology, not a person. It is Indra's capital, the centrepiece of Swarga. It really shouldn't be on the rivals list.

      EDIT: He should also probably have Stealth instead of Survival. Again, his lack of mythic narrative makes assigning even Abilities dificult, but Stealth seems to fit better. And as Watcher said below, Morpheus has no connections to Prophecy, or any Animals. Plus,. his rivals list strikes me as even more random than usual. I cannot for the life of me imagine why he'd hate Quetzalcoatl. Or really anyone on that list.

      Further EDIT: I just realized that this stat block might be for your character the Scion of Morpheus instead. It's kind of confusing, since he's also called Morpheus. In that case, of course, you can totally use and have any powers you want. However I'm absolutely hopeless at judging balance and mechanics, so I can't really help you on the 'Is he overpowered' question.

          Playing the child of a Lesser God is something I have been doing lately, and it's a ton of fun. So, I hope you have just as much fun with it as I have. It really makes things interesting politically when your Divine Parent isn't that important in the scale of things. A lot less to fall back on.

          Is this write up supposed to be for Morpheus himself? Or a Scion of Morpheus? I can't really tell what you mean, since you refer to him as Morpheus, and have given him a rival list, which is something Scions don't really have until they are Gods themselves. Although, even then, the rival list is kind of silly. Since Gods to not compete for worshipers, having overlap of Divine Roles doesn't really mean anything. Except for some of the Divine Kings who would be offended by others referring to themselves as King of Everything.

          If it is supposed to be Morpheus himself, you would need to prescribe numerical values to these things to get good feedback on if he is overpowered or not. But, if all of the Purviews and Epics you have tagged are the ones you believe Morpheus to have at max, he is horrendously overpowered. Especially for a Lesser God, and many of the things you have given him don't have any mythological backing to them (All Epics, Animal, Prophecy, and Illusion if you are going with the Vampire powers for dream powers are not connected to him sufficiently, if at all). Morpheus is not a God of Prophecy for one, that would fall mostly under the control of Apollo. And, he does not have any connection to any animals that I know of, asides from a very slim connection through his frequent depictions with wings on pottery, but the Greeks put wings on many of their Gods, such as Phobos, Thanatos, Nike, and Deimos, it doesn't have anything to do with a connection to winged animals.

          Under RAW rules, Morpheus, not being a Major God, would have no Associated Powers, only Associated Abilities. But, if your Storyteller is being lenient in that department, I would probably give him Arete, and Illusion (as a filler for his Dream powers, unless your storyteller is going to use the Vampire power set for Dream powers. But, in that case, a Rank 6 - 10, and an Avatar expression would need to be created.) for his Powers. I am not sure what Abilities he would have, I've not read Ovid's Metamorphoses recently enough to remember what sort of Abilities he could have.

          If this is to be a Scion of Morpheus', it looks good. Although, numerical values would be helpful if you want us to let you know how he is. But, he shouldn't be overpowered if you built him according to the Character Creation rules. If you would like a suggested Relic Weapon for this Scion, I would suggest a sword or a spear. Staffs were not really reguarded as weapons by the Greeks. The only example of one being used as such is Dionysus' Maenads with their Thyrsus, since they kill a king with them in the Baccahe.

          EDIT: Oh, additionally, if it is supposed to be Morpheus himself, he shouldn't have a Dream Sand Relic. Asides from the idea of Dream Sand being a modern one, and not Ancient Greek, Morpheus is not a God of Sleep. He is a God of Dreams. Hypnos is Morpheus' father, is the God of Sleep.
