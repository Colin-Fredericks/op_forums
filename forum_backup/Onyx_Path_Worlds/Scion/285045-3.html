                    Originally posted by Evilmarmelade View Post
                    Also, as with many other WoD games, you might want to ask your players about the subjects they consider taboo. It's a game about ancient mythology, and sometimes it was not pretty. There's a lot a myths that features rape, slavery, bestiality, sometimes even pedophilia...
                    Also, with the right powers, it's super easy for Scions to brainwash people, which can make some players uncomfortable.

                    So, yeah, I may sound a bit prudish, but clearly establishing which subjects are okay to use and which ones aren't helps a lot in having a healthy table.
