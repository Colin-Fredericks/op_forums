    Originally posted by Dayen View Post
    I think people overestimate the willingness of nuclear powers to utilize their arsenals, but that's neither here nor there.

    If a deity were to appear in New York tomorrow I have absolutely no doubt that the U.S. military would prepare to blow it up with missiles within the hour. If so ordered, I have no doubt they would execute that attack.

    But really, getting a bunch of demigods hung up on a murder one wrap is the sort of thing mortals can and should be doing to your heroes if they're being particularly sloppy.

    Jail sucks. Jail is something nobody wants to have to deal with. Even in a tabletop game. Especially in a tabletop game. Even if they make bail they're going to have to surrender their documents, which means from a legal perspective they're stuck wherever they are.

    Of course, by all means, this sort of stratagem isn't reserved for mortal foes, whatever and whomever they are. Certainly a Titan could have mortal followers execute these sorts of machinations to slow down the heroes.

    I'm not saying they wouldn't nuke them, I'm saying the ST should have a justified action for them doing so. If it's just a god brawling a Titan Spawn in the middle of the street, I would think blowing up the city as a first response is going to be a PR nightmare. Now if that god had been causing Class 5 tornadoes or 300 yard tall Tsunamis, or city destroying earthquakes, then yes, that would probably be a justified response.

    Unless the realization that there are actual god-like beings walking around causes the government to go bat shit crazy in an attempt to control the situation, I think they would have better reserved judgement on how to deal with the crisis at hand, if any at all. That's why I think a better alternative would be to have mortals (highly trained ones) use weaponry from the mythos that is supposed to be effective on the gods. If I remember correctly, the blood of a Hind (may not be spelling that correctly) was supposed to be a lethal poison to the Greek gods.

No real preference on how the players exit the situation, but a reminder that just because you're a superpowerful being who can say "get me $100 from your bank account" and get it doesn't mean you should.
