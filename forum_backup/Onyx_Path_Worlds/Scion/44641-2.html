    Telgar wrote an interesting Stamina Knack a while back.
    Unmatched Virility / Fertility
    Dice pool: None.
    Cost: None or 1 point of Legend.
    Prerequisites: Controlled Potency.

    This Knack represents total control of the Scion’s reproductive abilities. They can determine the gender of any children they sire or bear at no cost. Further, they do not even need to engage in sexual intercourse with a prospective partner to create a child. Simply physical contact and the expenditure of one Legend point is enough for a Scion to cause pregnancy in their partner, or themselves. Such immaculate conceptions ignore normal gender limits, allowing a female Scion with this Knack to impregnate another woman without difficulty. Barring extensive use of the Health Purview, males cannot be made to bear children, even with this Knack.

                                                I think the ramifications for it would depend on the context in which it happens. So far as I'm aware, there's nothing strictly forbidding Athena from having sex, she just doesn't seem interested in it.


                                                Now Artemis, on the other hand, is seen as the protector of virgins and her legend revolves around fighting off the unwanted advances of men. IF your character manages to force themselves on her, you're pretty much destroying the very thing that she stands for, a guardian against rape. Of course, she's also kind of a blood thirsty maniac and is not going down without a fight.


                                                There are some other deities in mythology who can't have sex for other reasons. For example, many of them are castrated and simply don't possess a sex drive. Osiris doesn't have a penis anymore, which makes it physically impossible for him to have sex.


                                                So to reiterate, it shouldn't have any negative repercussions for seducing a virgin goddess unless their Legend provides a specific reason for why they have to remain virgins.


                                                On an additional note, I disagree with the idea that using epic Socials constitutes as rape. You're not dropping narcotics into their food and sleeping with them when they're out of their minds, you're persuading them through personality and charisma. If social skills and beauty constituted as rape than every celebrity in America should be found guilty for abusing the millions of fans that lust after them.

                                                Sure, your ability to seduce and persuade others may be supernaturally strong, but at the end of it all, it's still persuasion and seduction. Nobody tied down your partner and forced them to have sex with you, they went along with it of their own volition. If they regret what they did later, that's their own fault.


                                                Mind Control, however, is a completely different story.
