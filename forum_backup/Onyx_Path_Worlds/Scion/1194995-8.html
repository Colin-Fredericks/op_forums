                    My main issue with this is the inherent nature of my character's powers to be safely contained in their very bodies or minds is an essential feature of my gaming fun. I cannot stand the idea of abilities being dependent on items that can be lost, destroyed, or stolen. My default solution for this in Hero terms would be to strictly define all Purview-bestowing Relics of my characters as stuff that cannot be really be lost or stolen in normal circumstances, Pantheon cultural flavor be thrice damned, such as tattooes, techno-magic cybernetics, scarifications, or jewels embedded in the character's body, or at the very least stuff that is worn such as rings, necklaces, bracers, belts, earrings, and the like, rather than stuff that is carried or yielded. "I lost the item that channels my powers" is simply not a subplot I'm willing to play in any game. And I'be very, very careful to stay in my Guide's good graces. My character need to be thrown naked in a cell, or travel with their clothes alone, and retain 90% of their abilities, and have to be unable to think, or at least speak or gesture, effectively to lose access to their powers. Rest assured that once I got Hero I checked the Relic (and Guide) rules, to be sure I'd be able to define my own access to Purviews this way.

                                Originally posted by Irioth View Post
                                How often can one expect to rack Fatebonds, before they become disruptive to story flaw, and the character unmanageable. I was under the impression the threshold was once every few sesssions at most.
                                I'm going to ignore the rest of what you said and focus on this misconception right here. You can gain a fatebinding if you so choose once per session. You can also compel it for another fate point, once per session. This binding will be Strength 1, and will therefore go away at the end of the game session unless you do something to lengthen its duration. So, right there, 2 Legend points per session. So, at Legend 1, adding in the 2 "free" Legend points per game session, without going into anything complicated, drastic, or long lasting, that gives you up to 3 Spent, let alone imbued, Legend per session. How many scenes of "Whatever the hell I need it to be, that loosely falls under one of my Purviews, that is not already covered my one of my Knacks, Innate Powers, or Boons" do you feel that you need in any given game session?

                                                I don't have a specific legendary hero in mind as an example, because I wouldn't run a game with the intent of playing Antiope or Cu Cullainne. It's about creating and playing your own myths and legends. In essence, this feels to me like, "here's a game about super-heroes. But the first book is just about playing your mortal secret identity. The second book, that's for when you first learn that you have powers, but can't really do super stuff. (and sorry, "I doom your future generations" is legitimate, but not something that would fit into an action-and-adventure tale very well) The third and fourth books, the ones that actually let you be superheroes, they won't be out for a year or two." It fixed broken stuff from 1st edition, but really seems to have nerfed powers a bit too much. And if you have limited uses, that leads to, "well, I won't use my power now, I might need it later. Or now, because I might need it in the next encounter. Oh, shit, the mission's over and I never used my cool power." It's not just about simulating myths, it's about feeling like you're part of a bigger adventure. So far, despite cool ideas and good mythological information, I'm still disappointed that it' starting off too slowly. Maybe in 2021, when I have all the books, I'll see it differently. But right now, I'm sadly underwhelmed.
