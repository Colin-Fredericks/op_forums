    Given that the folks behind the JSR website are apparently involved in making sure that Scion 2e's gods and mythologies are authentic, I'm not terribly worried. Will it be flawless? I'll be surprised if it is, as I'm not sure that "perfection" can even be meaningfully defined, let alone achieved. But I'll also be surprised if it isn't an order of magnitude better than Scion 1e.
    The fact that Zeus, Jupiter, and Tinia are different mantles of the same god doesn't mean that the Etruscan mythology is being mashed together with the Greek and Roman mythologies; rather, it means that the "fuzzy edges" of the different mythologies is being acknowledged: people of different regions were often influenced by the mythologies of their neighbors.
