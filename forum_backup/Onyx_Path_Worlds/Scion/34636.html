    Doe, Goddess of Nightmares
    AKA: John Doe, the Masked Cat
    As a former member of the Nightmares, Doe was once an adversary of the Occuria, always a thorn on the their story but never really their enemy. She would come to them offering advice on their quest, only to steal their prize afterwards. When they faced inescapable danger, she was there to save them, only to place them on another predicament of her choice. They were her pawns to move on her own game.
    When they were still Scions, John came to love Michael, the only one who could always find her, who understood her. When she hid, he could find her. When she wanted to escape, he would try to hold her. When she couldn't explain, he would understand. Her love and admiration was always hidden, all fell for fake persona of a male who opposed the Occuria and who manipulated them to his wishes, only Michael understood her true self.
    Doe joined the Occuria when they first decided to became a Pantheon and Venat asked her. He wanted Doe to be with them forever, to become anew in this now broken world. There was no choice for Doe. When she left the Nightmares, facing the wrath of Merry the Sleeping Sheep and lost, it was the Occuria who came for her. They were their new family.
    While Doe is a Goddess, she usually appears as a lean man wearing a black cloak with a red hood, her face hidden behind a stylized mask of a cat's skull. Everything in her disguise is perfect, she incarnates John Doe so completely that it is her very being. Her more feminine self is only shown to Venat, the one who understands.

    Circe, Goddess of Sorcery
    AKA: the Witch, the Sorceress
    It was ever so lonely in that isle. Just Circe and her pet pretenders, living in the forest. The occasional pretender came, but either their relation went sour or they weren't fit for her, soon becoming another of her pets. It was in this boredom that she heard that new travelers came to Aeaea and, sensing the Strands of Fate pulling with the passage of a group of Demigods, she prepared herself to greet them. One of them stood out from the group, a marvelous Norse man who sang strange new songs and told her new stories of the would outside, so different from what she remembered. It was probably love... definitely love!
    Circe helped the group in their way and gave Thrunel even greater presents, including the strings of Orpheus Harp, in exchange for his return. And true to his word, he returned after a couple of months and lived there happily for another few months until she became pregnant, immediately parting on a quest to break her free from the island's curse. She now truly loved him for their child and for freeing her.
    Her surprise was even greater with his last return to the isle, a full fledge Norse God freeing her from the power of the Dodekatheon and welcoming her as an Aesir. It was in this new family that Nim was born, a baby goddess that angered the Dodekatheon. In the times ahead the Aesir were falling apart and Thrunel decided it was time to leave and form a new family, as one of the Occuria, which Circe and little Nim agreed.
    Circe still retains her beauty from ancient times, wearing silky robes that barely cover her, always surrounded by a slow breeze that blows her dark hair. Nim is the split image of her mother, albeit more tanned like her father, with red robes covering her small frame and red ribbon adoring her hair.

    Haceli, God of Battle
    AKA: Lee Ace, the Dragon Rider
    Lee is as far from a traditional Scion of Ryujin has ever been, taking almost everything from his mother instead. Son of a US female soldier stationed in Okinawa, he has seen in first hand how the natives treat those who are different, namely the Americans, resulting in an aggressive child. Because her son wasn't getting along in school, she decided to return to the USA, where they remained for the rest of his infancy.
    Lee fell in love for combat, the raw power to overwhelm an adversary and not the traditional martial arts of his ancestry. He trained to become the best fighter possible, dreaming of returning to Japan and defeating the so claimed masters. After school, Lee entered the Army to access even more training, quickly becoming the meanest soldier in close combat, being selected for stealth missions where silent taking were necessary. But he never forgot his dream.
    During training in Okinawa, he challenged the Master in the House of Dragons, who would accept his challenge if he could defeat his protégé. While Lee battled Rei and emerged victorious, he was unable to defeat the master. Taking him as his new student, he took both to Lushan, where they trained in order to learn the secrets of the master's techniques. After six years, Lee was ready and after defeating both master and college, he asked for the true secret. Instead, Ryujin came to welcome the new Scion, guiding him in the coming battles for the Overworld.
    The front line soldier of the Occuria, he is always the first to partake in battle. Through his voyagers with his band, he battled many masters, but no victory was more satisfying than facing the Dragon King for his title and emerging victorious. With this victory, Haceli was ready to leave Japan forever and become the true King of Dragons with the Occuria, although he never assumes the visage of his father.
    Haceli always appears wearing his armor with stylized dragons, always ready for battle. With him, the serpentine dragons follow, many times transporting their God King. Only his head is unarmored, with his long flowing black hair and almost shut eyes revealing his inheritance, although he has already completely disassociated himself from the Amatsukami.

    Ligier, God of the Last Breath
    AKA: the Green Sun, the Poison Sun
    Ligier was born a God, but in a different way to every other god; he was a by product of a man made weapon. When an atomic bomb detonates, it is like a new star is born, consuming everything in its path. The bombs detonated in Japan during the Second World War killed thousands and their deaths gave birth to the God of the Last Breath, who follow them in their journey into Yomi. There, Ligier stood where he arrived, in the middle of what is now known as the Poison Sun Ward, seeing everything around him.
    It was in this solitude of thought that Ligier remained for more than half a decade, contemplating the ghosts who lived outside of the prison Izanami built for him and his green specters, studying the flow of stories and Legend around them all. It was obvious that his light harmed those made of Legend, they feared it so much that many gods tried to approach their source without knowing it to be their brother and they all have fail. Since it posed no problem to those outside the Ward, most gods forgot about it.
    It was as Demigods that the Occuria first came to Ligier, they battled through the Green Legion that protected him and open the doors to find him just sitting there, over an old desk, in a meditative stance. Alex stood deep wounds from the fight and Sven took him back to safety, so Lee and Michael were the ones who had come to the Green Sun. With their last breath, they asked Ligier to accept a present: the Zetsu Beads, built by Michael to help him constrain his power to a manageable level to other gods. When Ligier took the beads and felt his power drain, both were unconscious. He carried them far enough to get out on their own, with a guardian specter to give them safe passage on his lands and a message of friendship.
    When Occuria became a Pantheon, Ligier was their first acquisition, they needed a ruler for the dead in this new world. Ligier parted with the Stone Dwarves of Pharaos to build their Underworld, one where he ruled, and took with him the wall that stood around the Ward. With that stone, the Stone Dwarves built everything Ligier commanded, including stone armors for his specters to live and better stand his maddening power. Now in the center of Malfeas, a great tower stands with Ligier at the very top, like a lighthouse it guides the dead to their new home; which, unlike the others, is full of light.
    Ligier appears as a man with four arms, each with its own set of prayer beads (including the Zetsu Beads), bare-chested and with simple monk pants. He needs little and choses to meditate as much as possible instead of interacting with others, with his Yoroibushi guarding him with all their might and ruling with his power if possible. His meditations set a different point of view of that by most gods, with his discovery of Nen being one of them, which the all the Occuria now use.

    Melia, Goddess of Life
    AKA: Melandru, Mellaggan
    Melia was born from atrocity, from the castration of her father Ouramos, she was the eldest of the Meliae, nymphs of the ash trees. Born fully grown and already feeling old, she lived in the Golden Age of Cronus, guiding her sisters through that lawless age. She was more than welcome to accept the offer of Gaia, who came to her with little price Zeus, son of beloved Rhea, asking for her help in raising the little prince in secret in an hidden cave. Together with her sisters, Melia nurtured and thought Zeus into a young god that would inherit the Golden Age into a more balanced being, capable of feeling the love his father was incapable.
    After Zeus succeeding his father by force, as Cronus had once done to his father, there was little peace. In an instant, the Titans were imprisoned, and while the Meliae sided with the Titans, they were forgiven and allowed to walk the Earth. Endless events passed by and Melia still felt restless... until now. With the Fall of the Elder Gods, new Pantheons came to be and now Melia had a true chance to change what was done wrong in the past. She approached her cousin Circe with a proposition, to ally herself and her sisters to her new Pantheon, she could teach the young gods the way of their eldest, where they failed and how to prevent it.
    Melia appears as she was while a nymph, a tree with the upper portion of her body as an older woman. Her skin is bark, her long hair is made of leafs, she is nature incarnate. While Melia is more than able to make wooden legs for travel, she prefers to stay in the same place, rooted to the fertile soil near water.

    Pharaos, God of Light
    AKA: Sven Ogersen, the Heaven's Traveler
    Young Sven was always a charismatic kid, although he didn't lead groups into problems, he was there to incentive the trouble brewers into overdrive. In his teenager years, he joined a motorbiker gang and doing a lot of bad things to people who didn't deserve. As a young adult, Sven had a crisis of faith due to his dealings with crime and left the gang although not all of his vices. It was in this new phase of his life that Baldur came to him, recruiting him to unite a new group of Scions to partake in the Overworld War.
    Together with his new gang, Sven parted into adventure, supporting his group as the second best in most fields. Sven also took a liking to all forms of light, from fire to the power of the moon, although his reckless behavior placed his band in trouble a few times like turning a Dwarve Community into stone by displaying the power of the Sun. That time it took the power of an ancient water source to give life back to the statues, who now adore Pharaos as their God of Light.
    As a God of Light, Pharaos battled in Ragnarök together by his father's side. In the Aftermath, the world needed the guidance of the Gods, they could no longer remain hidden in this world while the people suffered. With no real place in the new Aesir, Pharaos took Venat's offer: Becoming a Guiding Light in this new world.
    Pharaos is the most expressive of the Occuria, always displaying his wild fire hair in his person with a leather jacket to use in his travels through the Heavens pulling the Sun and the Moon behind him with his motorbike. Pharaos incarnates light in all its forms, appearing as if all his body emanates light constrained by his clothes; with the exception of those in Ligier's Purview, which bring death and guide the dead instead of bringing life to the living.

    Sisyphus, God of Excellence
    AKA: Son of Aeolus, the Immortal
    Sisyphus longed to become a God as promised to him by Hermes, for that he was chained to a boulder in Tartarus, damned to roll it up a hill and if successful, his dream would be true. It almost never became true. Centuries had past, until the Escape of the Titans broke the top of the hill, allowing him to leave and access his new divine powers.
    Sisyphus arrived now a god on Earth, as one of his hated Dodekatheon. He observed the mortals and learned to manipulate them, producing organizations that used the mortal powers in his advantage quietly and without divine interference. He slowly gained proficiency in the powers he was most familiar with, obfuscating other Scions due to his immortality and cunning.
    Commanded by the Dodekatheon, Thrunel was tasked with capturing the Son of Aeolus and re-imprison him in Tartarus, for Fate had determined that no Olympian would fool him again. Thrunel was unsuccessful to do so, but Sisyphus instead helped him on his true task: the release of Circe. Together with Thrunel and his group they tricked the Dodekatheon to release the Sorceress.
    Sisyphus always presents himself as a beaded man in his 40s wearing an expensive suit, appearing as a mortal of great power and influence, even in battle. Due to his divine immortality enforced by Fate, he is completely impervious to damage, battling at a slow pace that upsets Haceli.

    Thrunel, God of Music
    AKA: Alex Thurnson, the Axe-wielding Bard
    As a mortal, Alex Thurnson was a traveling artist, playing his Axe-Guitar in several bands before moving cities, never making it big because of his free rebellious spirit. Stoned out off his mind, he was Visited by a big black wolf who spoke to him, telling him stories of his father Thor and the coming of Ragnarök. And life continued with the addition of a wolf close by, until the Jotun came for him. With the wolf Wargen, Alex escaped the Giants with his new found power. He soon found Sven, a foreign biker in a bar waiting for him. After a quick rumble that started with both brawling on the floor and ended with lots of ale, both became friends. In his road to master his new found powers and using them to produce the best music, Alex joined the rest of the band.
    Most of the legends concerning Thrunel deal with a quick love, many nymphs, alfar... even a couple of medusae, giants and two of the valkyries. But one deals with a great love, Circe. After coming to Aeaea, Thrunel impregnated Circe and their love bloomed; he traveled to the Aesir to ask for guidance and help on his task. The Aesir judged his quest true and fair, negotiating with the Dodekatheon to break Circe's bounds to the island. The Dodekatheon complied and only asked for the quest to re-imprison Sisyphus, which they deemed impossible.
    It was impossible to outwit Sisyphus, in any field, making Lee grind his teeth. Out of boredom, Sisyphus asked the insistent Scion why would he not give up this silly task, to which Thrunel sang a ballad so beautiful that broght tears to everyone's eyes. Sisyphus thus complied with returning to help releasing Circe.
    Thrunel is true to his giant heritage, appearing as the tallest and most muscular of the Occuria. He has long flowing blond hair kept away from his eyes by a winged metal headband in strong contrast to tan skin. Wherever he goes, his black toga and Ragna, his legendary Axe-Guitar.

    Venat, God of Transcendence
    AKA: Michael Huang, the Man-Machina
    Venat became the God of Transcendence because he transcended all the problems life imposed him. While a chinese descended mortal, Michael faced his family problems studying, becoming one of the most brilliant minds on the field of bionics and prosthetics. When he was attacked as a mortal, his few friends killed and he lost his right arm, Nüwa Visited him and gave Michael a chance to transcend those problems by helping on the new Titan War. As a Scion, when there were no tools or materials for the group he would make them, he would produce everything everyone needed.
    His transcendence is clearly visible on his body. First his right arm was replaced with a silver copy of his advanced prosthetic by Nüwa, enhanced with five small robots that helped him as tools of fabrication. His left leg was given as a payment to the Aztec Gods as sacrifice, soon replaced by his own cybernetic leg, a great improvement of his silvery arm. Soon, every time he needed to overcome a given obstacle, he would build a Relic to deal with it. He became one of the Gods of Artifice.
    Michael's problems with the family would not end and as Venat, he decided to leave the Shen to their Bureaucracy. He took the steps to form a new Pantheon with his divine friends and allies, now known as the Occuria. While he is officially the leader, it is Sisyphus who truly rules, leaving him more time to dedicate to the two things he loves most: his craft and Doe.
    Venat's appearance is as mutable as the surface of water, flowing and changing due to the surrounding circumstances. Most of the times, he is surrounded by machinery that enhances his diminished divine body, showing up as an artificer. On social occasions where no machinery might help him, he choses to come in a white suit and a mask, as a contrast to his date Doe, who deals better with the minds of others.

    Cosmology
    Malfeas, the Underwold
    AKA: Land of the Dead Sun
    Malfeas is much different from most Underworlds for the simple presence of its ruler, Ligier is the Green Sun and shines brightly green light over all of Malfeas. Ligier's light is an harsh relentless light that doesn't allow any shadow to exist within his realm, serving as an escape to the utter darkness that most ghosts have to face.

    Exodus of the Poison Sun
    It was an estrange event in Yomi. The Poison Sun Ward had been mostly quiet for years and yet one day all the green ghosts came to the Ward Wall and took the stone. The Shikome were waiting to push the invaders back to their prison, with Izanami at their rear to face the power of the God of the Last Breath, but they never crossed the threshold, they simply took the stone and left Yomi altogether. Confusion struck the Amatsukami even harder after the Fall, but with their hands full with internal strife the end of the Ward was a minor problem.
    Ligier left Yomi in pilgrimage with his Green Ghosts and four clans of Stone Dwarves, a gift of Pharaos to build the Occurian Underworld. They walked for months, making the Stone Dwarves regret their desire to help the restless Green Legion, until they arrived at a large plains made of basalt. Here Ligier ordered the Stone Dwarves to build him a tower high enough for the lightless ghosts to see his divine radiance, so that they would be able to make the same pilgrimage to arrive their new home, Malfeas. The Basalt and Wardstone tower was far better than Ligier's dreams, thanks to the Dwarven craftsmanship, and now a black stone lighthouse adorned with brass stands in the middle of the city of the dead.

    Ascension of the Green Sun
    With his tower constructed, Ligier retreated himself to its top, the Pharos at the Ridorana Plaza, asking the Dwarven clans to build his Green Ghosts some armor that they could utilize in order to better face those who disturb the holy city. The Dwarves build the Yoroi that could be used by the Chosen of the Green Sun, know as the Yoroibushi, and then proceeded with the building of the city. They divided the city in quarters, one for each clan, each trying to outdone each other in feats of architecture while keeping a coherent theme within the quarter and the city.
    Following the new light, ghosts started to arrive, joining the quarter that they first encountered. Now while the Green Legion lives at the Ridorana Plaza and their Chosen live within the Pharos, the foreign ghosts live in the quarters originally intended for them. While it isn't enforced, this segregation developed its own kind of culture that was never stopped by any of the ruler within Pharos and was enhanced by the Dwarven rivalry, symbolized by their statues.

    The City of Under the Dead Sun
    Now that Stone Dwarves have left, the city continues its traditions. It four quarters each try to be better than the others, becoming greater than they were before. From the top of his lighthouse, Ligier watches his city and guides those who wish for a new beginning after the Fall.
    Ridorana is the plaza that holds the Pharos at its center, here the Green Legion lives, with its Yoroibushi patrolling the city and guarding the tower. Around it lies the four quarters that make up Malfeas, clockwise:
    Stadsdel is a modern city into itself, with its buildings growing the farther they are from the other quarters and from Pharos, although all much smaller than it, no structure can block the light of their God. For those who want to perfect the environment in more human ways choose this place, to live in organized blocks of edifices, with modern commodities and all they could want.
    Next lies Chiku, whose construction resembles eastern culture construction, mostly resembling Buddhist architecture, it is a place of reflection where those who wish to reach their inner best go to better themselves. Its temples and open spaces are the most beautiful to see of all quarters.
    Then Home, house of the new world. It is wilderness and open space, perfect for those who arrive after the fall, the new ghosts who have lost the benefits of the older generations. Here is the place for those who wish to reconnect themselves with their past, to better accept who they are so that they can reach perfection. Those who live here build their own houses of their own work, leaving the building of others empty if needed, resulting in an rural looking quarter.
    And finely, Barrio is where those who like to connect with others live. It is a place of celebration, of meetings, of doing new things in order to find the best. It looks like all the cultures of the world clashed and combined their architectures in order to produce their eternal housings, resulting in a quarter that flows from house to house without looking out of place.

    God of the Underworld
    Although young, Malfeas is full of activity, an activity that Ligier oversees and polices. No shadow my exist in his realm, no darkness, those who fail to let go of their darkness must feel the powerful gaze of the Green Sun. The presence of the Poison Sun in Malfeas add another benefit for the protection of the city, the light of the Green Sun burns off the Legend of others, no God or being of larger Legend dares to go too near due to the pain inflicted by his light.
    The ruling class that dictates policy is in fact the Chosen among the Green Legion, they rule and police the city to their best ability to please their God. Ruling with a tight doctrine derived from those who have listen to Ligier's words, yet none has ever faced the wrath of the Poison Sun. The worst crime to commit within Malfeas is to submit to the Green Madness and becoming a specter, committing horrible crimes to the city of their Lord. For those the Yoroibushi are ordered to dispose without mercy, which stikes fear in the dead hearts of the dead who see the enormous statues running among their ranks.

    Passages to Malfeas
    Malfeas is easily accessible for those who accept the light of the Poison Sun.
    Tombs and Funerary Architecture: Those who worship the Occuria place their dead under the Sun to let their souls see the light that will guide them to their new home, most of the times on the top of an ruined building from before the Fall. Towers are also constructed in order to facilitate the dead to reach the other side. When a creature of Legend rests under the sun from a tall building, wearing the funerary clothes of the dead and without moving for at least a full day, he may spend a point of legend to pass into the Underworld.
    Natural Features: To access Malfeas one simply needs to enter an empty city under the light of noon and get to its center, from there he must ask the guidance of Ligier's light and pay his tribute (1 legend) to reach the city of the dead. The only problem is that, Ligier must chose to accept this newcomer.
    Another way to reach Malfeas is to see an atomic explosion and be pushed by its force, instantly reaching Malfeas without any need for legend.
    Rituals: No ritual can grant access to Malfeas, for those who worship the Occuria simply strip bear the dead and leave them to face the power of the sun, only after looking into the sun for a day can someone enter Malfeas.
    Times: At noon of the Day of Exodus, everyone who isn't able to accept the world as it remains sacrifices themselves in order to reach the light of the god of the Last Breath. Any creature of Legend can instead spend a point of legend and ask for passage into Malfeas, although this also suffers from the need of the God accepting the request.

                          So I already concluded the Underworld, although it can still change depending on your feedback. So please comment!

                          Anyway, here is just the Underworld part:
                          Malfeas, the Underwold
                          AKA: Land of the Dead Sun
                          Malfeas is much different from most Underworlds for the simple presence of its ruler, Ligier is the Green Sun and shines brightly green light over all of Malfeas. Ligier's light is an harsh relentless light that doesn't allow any shadow to exist within his realm, serving as an escape to the utter darkness that most ghosts have to face.

                          Exodus of the Poison Sun
                          It was an estrange event in Yomi. The Poison Sun Ward had been mostly quiet for years and yet one day all the green ghosts came to the Ward Wall and took the stone. The Shikome were waiting to push the invaders back to their prison, with Izanami at their rear to face the power of the God of the Last Breath, but they never crossed the threshold, they simply took the stone and left Yomi altogether. Confusion struck the Amatsukami even harder after the Fall, but with their hands full with internal strife the end of the Ward was a minor problem.
                          Ligier left Yomi in pilgrimage with his Green Ghosts and four clans of Stone Dwarves, a gift of Pharaos to build the Occurian Underworld. They walked for months, making the Stone Dwarves regret their desire to help the restless Green Legion, until they arrived at a large plains made of basalt. Here Ligier ordered the Stone Dwarves to build him a tower high enough for the lightless ghosts to see his divine radiance, so that they would be able to make the same pilgrimage to arrive their new home, Malfeas. The Basalt and Wardstone tower was far better than Ligier's dreams, thanks to the Dwarven craftsmanship, and now a black stone lighthouse adorned with brass stands in the middle of the city of the dead.

                          Ascension of the Green Sun
                          With his tower constructed, Ligier retreated himself to its top, the Pharos at the Ridorana Plaza, asking the Dwarven clans to build his Green Ghosts some armor that they could utilize in order to better face those who disturb the holy city. The Dwarves build the Yoroi that could be used by the Chosen of the Green Sun, know as the Yoroibushi, and then proceeded with the building of the city. They divided the city in quarters, one for each clan, each trying to outdone each other in feats of architecture while keeping a coherent theme within the quarter and the city.
                          Following the new light, ghosts started to arrive, joining the quarter that they first encountered. Now while the Green Legion lives at the Ridorana Plaza and their Chosen live within the Pharos, the foreign ghosts live in the quarters originally intended for them. While it isn't enforced, this segregation developed its own kind of culture that was never stopped by any of the ruler within Pharos and was enhanced by the Dwarven rivalry, symbolized by their statues.

                          The City of Under the Dead Sun
                          Now that Stone Dwarves have left, the city continues its traditions. It four quarters each try to be better than the others, becoming greater than they were before. From the top of his lighthouse, Ligier watches his city and guides those who wish for a new beginning after the Fall.
                          Ridorana is the plaza that holds the Pharos at its center, here the Green Legion lives, with its Yoroibushi patrolling the city and guarding the tower. Around it lies the four quarters that make up Malfeas, clockwise:
                          Stadsdel is a modern city into itself, with its buildings growing the farther they are from the other quarters and from Pharos, although all much smaller than it, no structure can block the light of their God. For those who want to perfect the environment in more human ways choose this place, to live in organized blocks of edifices, with modern commodities and all they could want.
                          Next lies Chiku, whose construction resembles eastern culture construction, mostly resembling Buddhist architecture, it is a place of reflection where those who wish to reach their inner best go to better themselves. Its temples and open spaces are the most beautiful to see of all quarters.
                          Then Home, house of the new world. It is wilderness and open space, perfect for those who arrive after the fall, the new ghosts who have lost the benefits of the older generations. Here is the place for those who wish to reconnect themselves with their past, to better accept who they are so that they can reach perfection. Those who live here build their own houses of their own work, leaving the building of others empty if needed, resulting in an rural looking quarter.
                          And finely, Barrio is where those who like to connect with others live. It is a place of celebration, of meetings, of doing new things in order to find the best. It looks like all the cultures of the world clashed and combined their architectures in order to produce their eternal housings, resulting in a quarter that flows from house to house without looking out of place.

                          God of the Underworld
                          Although young, Malfeas is full of activity, an activity that Ligier oversees and polices. No shadow my exist in his realm, no darkness, those who fail to let go of their darkness must feel the powerful gaze of the Green Sun. The presence of the Poison Sun in Malfeas add another benefit for the protection of the city, the light of the Green Sun burns off the Legend of others, no God or being of larger Legend dares to go too near due to the pain inflicted by his light.
                          The ruling class that dictates policy is in fact the Chosen among the Green Legion, they rule and police the city to their best ability to please their God. Ruling with a tight doctrine derived from those who have listen to Ligier's words, yet none has ever faced the wrath of the Poison Sun. The worst crime to commit within Malfeas is to submit to the Green Madness and becoming a specter, committing horrible crimes to the city of their Lord. For those the Yoroibushi are ordered to dispose without mercy, which stikes fear in the dead hearts of the dead who see the enormous statues running among their ranks.

                          Passages to Malfeas
                          Malfeas is easily accessible for those who accept the light of the Poison Sun.
                          Tombs and Funerary Architecture: Those who worship the Occuria place their dead under the Sun to let their souls see the light that will guide them to their new home, most of the times on the top of an ruined building from before the Fall. Towers are also constructed in order to facilitate the dead to reach the other side. When a creature of Legend rests under the sun from a tall building, wearing the funerary clothes of the dead and without moving for at least a full day, he may spend a point of legend to pass into the Underworld.
                          Natural Features: To access Malfeas one simply needs to enter an empty city under the light of noon and get to its center, from there he must ask the guidance of Ligier's light and pay his tribute (1 legend) to reach the city of the dead. The only problem is that, Ligier must chose to accept this newcomer.
                          Another way to reach Malfeas is to see an atomic explosion and be pushed by its force, instantly reaching Malfeas without any need for legend.
                          Rituals: No ritual can grant access to Malfeas, for those who worship the Occuria simply strip bear the dead and leave them to face the power of the sun, only after looking into the sun for a day can someone enter Malfeas.
                          Times: At noon of the Day of Exodus, everyone who isn't able to accept the world as it remains sacrifices themselves in order to reach the light of the god of the Last Breath. Any creature of Legend can instead spend a point of legend and ask for passage into Malfeas, although this also suffers from the need of the God accepting the request.
