                    Having followed this line for a few postings, I came to a realization: Wow - this really raises the bigger issues of including ANY pantheon that represents an existing culture, doesn't it? First Nations North America, Australian Aboriginal, Maori, Pacific Islander, Chinese, Japanese, Hindu/Sikh, Mayan, Muslim...essentially, what is being said here ["be careful that you only play something that you can approach with sensitivity and knowledge, especially if you aren't of the appropriate racial/cultural background"] would make it really tough for the game to include pretty much anything that is still worshiped in our real world. What does that leave us with? Only religions that are extinct, because you can't screw up playing a Scion of Osiris, Frigga, Taranis, or Anat, since no-one can say you're doing it wrong.
                    I'm not sure if this is progressive cultural sensitivity, or taking a game and making it into something it isn't. No, don't play racial or cultural stereotypes that are offensive to the others in your group - but that's a general rule for all gaming, isn't it? Don't include rape, abuse, drug use, suicide, extreme violence, or anything that would upset the people you are gaming with. But I'd be leery of saying "People really shouldn't play Orisha Scions, or Shen ones, or Inue ones, unless they belong to those groups, because that's inappropriate/offensive", because that might seriously limit any creativity or variety in the group and game. After all - is the GM going to ensure that they themselves are only playing NPCs of other faiths with the appropriate consideration? How far do we take this?
