                                                            Leena has “Transcendent Deception” and Arcane Calculus as her purviews. Note that this will be the only time Arcane Calculus is listed in the Pre-Mades. We don’t know what her Ivory Idol does, but if you fix Deception’s name, that’s a starting character. (Unless Transcendent [Purview] is meant to indicate it’s the Awareness-fueled version.)

                                                            I’m not going to dissect each of the others, but I suspect that the designer crossed wires and assumed that a) Scions get two purviews at character creation, b) you get to pick one Purview from a list provided by a Relic, and c) your Pantheon Purview isn’t an automatic purchase.

                                                            B) would make some of the baffling Relic designs last chapter make sense: “it’s fine that a three-dot relic has three Purviews listed, you only get to pick one!”

                                                            And while we’re talking about the PMC...
                                                            Originally posted by Hero, Page 182
                                                            Two of the Skills you choose for your character’s Pantheon Path must be the Pantheon Skills for divine patron’s pantheon.
                                                            At a guess, I’m going to say that Occult is one of the Pantheon Skills, and Leadership may be the other? But that’s if the designer assumed that you have to have one of the skills, not both.

                                                            Then again, PMC are notoriously difficult to get right in pretty much any RPG I can name, which is a shame, because they’re a resource for the players least likely to be able to catch mistakes.
