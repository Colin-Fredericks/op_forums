    [2e] List of Pantheons

    05-10-2022, 12:07 AM
    So something that I've been looking for and haven't really found is a comprehensive list of the pantheons actually mentioned (with full write-ups or just mentions) in Scion. So I thought I'd put one together myself. If there's any that someone else caught and I didn't, let me know so I can fill it in. I haven't included ones talked about by the devs either, mostly because I don't remember where/when they talked about them. Again, if anyone does remember, please let me know.

    Full Write-ups
    Hero
    -The Aesir of Asgard and Midgard
    -The Devá of South Asia
    -The Kami of Japan
    -The Manitou of the Anishinaabek
    -The Netjer of Egypt
    -The Òrìshà of Yorùbáland
    -The Shén of China
    -The Teōtl of Mēxihcah
    -The Theoi of Greece and Rome
    -The Tuatha Dé Danann of Ireland
    Mysteries of the World
    -The Loa of Haiti
    -The Nemetondevos of Gaul
    -The Teros of Atlantis (Fictional)
    -The Yazata of Persia
    Demigod
    -The Anunna of Mesopotamia
    -The Apu of the Inca
    -The Atua of Polynesia
    -The Bogovi of the Slavs
    -The Tengri of Mongolia
    God
    -The Balahala of Visayas
    -The Ilhm of Phoenicia
    -The K'uh of the Maya
    -The Pālas of Buddhism
    -The Zemí of the Taíno
    Once and Future
    -The Plant Don of Wales

    Masks of the Mythos
    -The Elder Gods of the Mythos (Fictional)

    Mentioned
    -The Kalou-Vu of Fiji
    -The Kamuy of the Ainu
