            You seem to be under the impression that military might and physical might are the same thing. They're not. If we were defining Putin's actions in game terms, it would not be Epic Strength, but rather use of the War Purview. You'll note that the Epic Strength and War are not always found in the same gods. Some, like Ares or Huitzilopochtli, are absolute murder machines who wade into combat themselves and start wrecking heads alongside their troops, but then you have strategists like Athena or Odin who are all about going to war and can even put up a good fight if they want to, but are much more regarded for their strategies or cleverness to bring about their victories. Hell, even many of the wrecking balls have socials to back them up. Huitzilopochtli, for example has Charisma out the wazoo, when he commands you, you do what he says, even if what he says is extremely detrimental to you (as his followers found out when they listened to his advice and followed his orders to kill the daughter of the one king they had as an ally and wear her flayed skin in front of him).

            So, military might and physical might are two different things. And, yes, Putin is currently using his military might as a cudgel in the game of international politics. Just as Cronus used his military might and prowess to rule over his Golden Age. And we know how that ended.

            I'm not saying the above to comment on the current state of world affairs (or to belittle or undermine anything about those unfortunate enough to be directly dealing with them), just to point out that the example you're giving is actually undermining your point. As has EVERY example you have thus far provided. Using Putin as an example of physical power being more potent than social prowess is only valid if Putin himself is on the front lines, dodging bullets, taking artillery shells to the face, and ripping tanks apart with his bare hands. It is only through social means, inspiring loyalty or coercion, or intimidation, that you have an army at your back. Without socials, or in supernatural terms, the War Purview, it is difficult to raise that army and convince it to live and die for you.

            The other hypothetical you keep pulling out is the smooth talker who runs into a gang of thugs and gets shot. Well, that's not necessarily the last laugh. Social characters have a lot of contacts and people willing to go to the mattresses for them. The thugs get arrested in the absolute best case scenario. There are many other possibilities, up to and including Keyser Soze levels of vengeance where the thugs watch everyone they've ever loved slaughtered before they finally get killed. This happens. Baldr dies and the entire Aesir lose their shit to make sure the monster who did it pays, and they're not that concerned when it turns out they got the wrong guy first.

            And the best smooth talkers, the Scions and the like with Epic Charisma or Manipulation? They're able to a) tell the thugs this before they shoot and b) get them to believe it. Loki's famous for pulling this exact scenario with Thjazi.

            Power comes in many forms. It comes in physical strength. It comes in crafty minds. It comes in powerful Relics. It comes with power over the elements and over Fate itself. Social power manages to be one of the best, because if you have Social power, then if you lack power in another form, be it military might or a magic sword or the ability to bench press a mountain, then you are better able to convince someone who HAS that power to help you, whether through charm, guile, sex appeal, or intimidation.
