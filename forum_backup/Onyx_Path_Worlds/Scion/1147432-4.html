            Manbat - The Consolation and Complications do seem to have a TV Show type thing, but it also seems to fall into a growing sentiment in tabletop games that I've seen. The Fail Forward philosophy.

            Because there's not much that's fun about going "Well you didn't succeed at the roll, so... Um... Er... I was kind of hoping you would, but you don't find any leads". So let's change the mindset, make it so even a failure helps move the story along.

            ... It also reminds me somewhat of something that someone said about Contrivances in movies. You can have one or two Contrived Moments in a movie to help the plot, but they need to have Complications attached. The example I saw specifically was for Who Framed Roger Rabbit?

            Eddie's hit a dead end in his investigation, and he and Roger duck into a theater. It just so happens that a newsreel gives him a clue as to what's going on. That's the Contrivance.

            But it just so happens that the lead he got? It leads him to Toon Town, where Eddie does not want to go. That's the Complication.

            So yeah, I like the Consolation and Complications. Failures turn into opportunities, and above all else, keep the story moving.

            Disclaimer: In favor of fun and enjoyment, but may speak up to warn you that you're gonna step on a metaphorical land mine
