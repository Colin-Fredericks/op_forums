        Time to add some of my specialties to the mix...

        030) There's tryouts for a new basketball team, looking for all up-and-coming stars. It's only after you get drafted that it becomes quite clear that a different sport's being played. Turns out this is poktapok, the Maya version of the Mesoamerican ballgame, and the two coaches are the Hero Twins, Hunahpu and Xbalanque. Turns out, they're trying to assemble the perfect team to invade the underworld and take on the Lords of Xibalba once again. And once you've signed up, saying no just isn't an option...

        031) You're an established Scion of some acclaim with the animal kingdom. You're known among mortals and gods alike as caring for your four-legged friends. So, you wake up one day to find a herd of glowing rainbow colored llamas on your doorstep, along with a note. It's from Urcuchillay, the Peruvian llama deity, and he's tasked you with taking care of his flock. See, they're meant to be sacrificed to the Inca pantheon as a whole on their next feast day, but something's come up, so he handed his llamas over to you. It's not entirely clear, but if these llamas are sacrificed, the recipient gets a lot of power. If you're not a Scion of the Incas, your own divine parent may want that instead (and even if you are, your parent may decide that no one ELSE truly deserves this honor, they should really have the whole herd). However, if you fail, you have an entire pantheon of angry gods with a penchant for causing natural disasters out for your blood. Not to mention the various other predators looking for some of that tasty tasty meat. And, to top it all off, these llamas apparently lack any sense of self-preservation.

        032) You're a Scion of the Aztecs, and you've just been married to a Scion of another pantheon. Details about this can vary (maybe it's arranged politically, maybe it's a shotgun marriage, maybe it's a Bandmate or NPC your character's fallen in love with), but as you're about to head off on your honeymoon, you're called by Huitzilopochtli. He tells you, in no uncertain terms, that, rather than consummate this union, you must ritually sacrifice your spouse and make their skin into a garment, and then wear it in the presence of their pantheon. His commanding voice tells you that he will not accept any other outcome. What do you do?

        033) The Greek gods are running short on their supply of moly, the protective herb once used to protect Odysseus from being transformed by Circe. The herb's powers are manifold, and are needed again. It's said that some might exist in the Amazon rainforest, and the rumors are true... but the only crop is in the realm of the terrifying and uncooperative Yurupari, forest god of the Brazilian Tupi Indians. He can turn the forest into an impenetrable maze of trees, and trying to find safe haven may result in you running into his crooked maw. Escaping him and stealing it is difficult, even if you could find your way through, he can be any animal in the jungle. He is a very strong fighter and resilient, not to mention a master of deadly poisons. If you wish to negotiate for the moly, there are only two ways to do so. Either you can find a woman worthy of being the wife of his father, Guaracy, the sun god (though he has impossible standards and is very misogynist even by the standards of most gods). Or... (If you're a male) you can beat him in a contest... and presents to you a mask and a flute, and tells you to start dancing. Yes, the nightmarish forest demon challenges you to the Native Amazonian equivalent to a rock off.

                                                    When confronted, the self-proclaimed child of Fenris claims this his father has been making increasingly desperate attempts to free himself from his chains, is trying to carve new fate for himself that leads to the freedom he so desires, it may even change his role in ragnarok.

                                                            49.) Alright rookie, you who Metis is right? No? What are they teaching kids these days?...That was rhetorical, anyway, Metis is a Titan woman that Zeus had an affair with that he ate due to a prophecy that their son would overthrow his old man. Thing is, she was pregnant when she went down the storm god's gullet. She gave birth, the offspring was poking around Zeus's skull with a spear, and when the big guy got his local smith, AKA Hephaestus, to smash his skull open to figure out what was going on in there, out popped a fully grown child of Zeus and Metis. And so Athena was born. What? No I'm not done, that's just background 'cause you don't know what you should.

                                                            Anyway, Metis somehow, I don't get paid enough to know the specifics, escaped from Zeus's belly and sought asylum with a foreign pantheon. Now Zeus figured out pretty quickly where she went, so Zeus storms up to Mexico City demanding that Huitzilotpoctli hand over Metis. Now the thing is, you at least know about a minor disagreement that the Aztec Empire had with a spaniard named Cortez right? Well it turns out that Cortez's mother was yet another of Big Z's one-night-poontangs. Oh he never got a Visitation or even Child Services, but even so the Teotl ain't too fond of Big Z. So of course they refuse to give Metis back.

                                                            So Big Z starts gathering up allies, callin' in favors and the like while the Teotl do the same, and pretty soon it's the Cold War all over again, but this time it's not Uncle Sam and Mother Russia aiming nukes at each other by proxy, oh no, this time it promises to be the biggest divine war ever since the gods locked up the GTs! Oh for the love of, Greater Titans.

                                                            50.) Okay, first off, are you from a bunker, vault, or are you some poor sap from the wilderness? Really? Huh, honestly didn't expect that. In any case, growing up did you ever hear or read any Norse Mythology? Yes I realize you didn't get a great deal of free time but I had to ask. Okay, long story short, the death of the gods and the end of the world, Ragnarok, begins with the mother of all winters: Fimbulvintr. Hold all questions until I finish. Now, at first no one thought much of when the nukes fell. The thing is, three years in, the Norns have spoken up and finally told us, we're in Fimbulvintr.

                                                            51.) Ah good morning young sir, so good to have another child of the *sniff* Aesir visit our fine establishment. Oh do not worry, no passerby will hear our conversation. Now, what brings you to London? You were visited by a woman that told you that you are the child of a heathen god and directed you here? At least you were already in England, we have had some come to us from the far off Colonies. I am sorry to inform you that yes it is true, the heathen gods of the Viking barbarians, the Ancient Greeks and Egyptians, and who knows where else all exist. What this means for good, honest Christians like yourself is something that you will have to determine on your own.

                                                            For now, you are welcome within these walls. You will be given a bed to sleep in, three meals a day, and plenty of training to keep you busy. What? I'm sorry, but now that you have received a Visitation all the monsters of myth will be hunting you. Though you look to be a strong, strapping lad I doubt you have ever had to fight for your life before.

                                                            Pardon the change in subject, but just a forewarning do not stray out at night. There is a serial killer on the loose that the papers have taken to calling Jack the Ripper.
