    Gabe Cyrus: A truly strange Scion of Osiris, Gabe seems to take great delight in being whimsically unpredictable. Which, ironically, makes him quite predictable. All you have to do is think of the most absurd reaction to a scenario, and chances are Gabe will attempt to perform it. Like how he seems particularly bent on mastering the Frost purview, even though his pantheon hails from the deserts of Egypt.

    Gabe was born to a mixed race couple when his middle eastern father married a white woman from America. As you might expect, such a heritage brought no end of questioning from Gabe's schoolmates during his early childhood. But fortunately for Gabe, his father wasn't Muslim. In fact, Mr. Cyrus made it a point that he worshiped the religion of his ancestors, and prayed to Egyptian deities. Even going so far as to hand carve little wooden figurines of the animal headed pantheon for Gabe to play with.

    As he grew older, Gabe's father began to instruct him in matters of theology and Egyptian culture. More than once, he expressed his wish for Gabe to become a priest and keep the old religion alive, instead of seeing it fade away. Gabe didn't put too much stock into it until his father suddenly died of cancer when he was 20 years old. According to his last will and testament, his father requested mummification as his funeral arrangements. It took most of the insurance money to arrange the ghastly procedure of scooping out the organs, along with building a small crypt with which to entomb the body.

    After the funeral was over, most of the guests had left, but Gabe chose to stay in the crypt a little longer to grieve his father's passing. So when the lid to his coffin slid open and the freshly mummified remains of his father sat up and began to speak to him, he responded as well as could be expected.

    The mummy revealed that he was not Gabe's biological father, but rather a devoted priest of Osiris who had been charged with watching over him. With the priest's demise, any protection he offered was gone, and now Gabe's enemies would be coming for him. For he was a child of the gods, and Osiris was his true father. With that, the mummy lay back in his grave, sealed the lid, and returned to sleep.

    This experience was understandably traumatic for Gabe Cyrus, which rendered him half crazy as a result of being unable to handle it. He still retains enough self awareness to remain functional, but exposure to magic, monsters and Gods has driven him to the brink. There are times when he can't tell reality from fantasy, and behaves in a completely whimsical fashion with no regard for consequences. This delirium can prove to be a liability to any Band who's unable to cope with sudden changes to the environment.


    Since his visitation, Osiris has granted Gabe with a wooden figurine carved in his own image. The figurine was made by Osiris' mortal father and allows him to channel the Death and Justice purviews. He also wears an Ankh necklace made of solid gold, which enables him to channel the Fertility and Earth purviews. In exchange for a promise of alliance, Gabe has also commissioned the Aesir for a flail (Avalanche) which enables him to channel the Frost purview, granting him a power that is not inherent to the Egyptian pantheon.


    Osiris has no idea why Gabe wants to control Frost so badly, but he has decided to wait and see how things play out for now. If nothing else, it seems to surprise his enemies when Gabe whips it out in battle.
