                once i reached with successes the defense of my enemy i deal 1 damage, if i do more successes i can do the stunt damage and deal one more damage

                Somewhat. Unless I am sorely mistaken, you need to meet the Defense of your target to first hit. At that point, you use any remaining successes to buy stunts, one of which is the "Inflict Damage" stunt, typically costing 1 success (and there is a Critical Hit stunt to do an additional point of damage). It is entirely, 100% possible to hit your target, but have no remaining successes, so you do no damage.

                The way that a weapon can cause more damage is by having an Enhancement, which will typically be a Relic.

                                        Originally posted by horngeek View Post
                                        How would shields generally be represented?
                                        Through a tag that adds an Enhancement to the Defense roll. The tricky thing is that if you narrate that you’re using shield or no shield during your Defense roll, you’re stuck with that when your turn comes up, or vice versa. You’re either using your shield to block and bash someone with (and your options for injury are limited) or you’re using your primary weapon, so you can't also be using your shield.

                                                    Originally posted by vonpenguin View Post
                                                    One question that comes to mind given the recent revelation that the attributes in the combat section are suggestions, what good is the Epic dex boon that let's you use Dex for any range band in that case?
                                                    They're strong suggestions. You can use any combination of Skills and Attributes - the key factors here are what you're doing (Skill) and how you're doing it (Attribute). You can use your Might to appear lift a refridgerator and prove your virility and seduce someone, for instance - but in a bar, you'd be restricted to flexing your muscles or something. It might not make a ton of sense. Similarly, Might's most often used in Close Combat, but yes, you can use other Attributes if you justify them - the difficulty lies in justifying them, and the Storyguide is the final arbiter there. If you're trying to fight a water horse, Physical Finesse and Resistance aren't going to be as useful as just bashing the thing.

                                                    For the Epic Dex or Epic Strength Boons/innates? You don't need to justify it, it's magic.
