    Sell me on At the Gates

    04-07-2024, 10:01 PM
    So we are few weeks till the crowdfunding of At the Gates game. ( AtG for short from now. ) We got some previews blog posts about it and some tease here and there on Pathcasts. So let's see if you can sell me this game...

    (Correct me if I'm wrong) At the Gates is jRPG-like setting. We talk here about things like Final Fantasy ( FF for short ) , and more like 8 and 9, than 7 - those first two were much more traditional fantasy and 7 was much more urban fantasy. So how does this compare to Fabula Ultima, other jRPG great system from last year? ( We literally have now in Poland crowdfunding for translation Fabula, right now. )

    In AtG we have world of Gaia, where 'world is returning from magical apocalypse' ( World Below? ), briming with magic. Even peasants have spells right now. There is something called the Void ( analog to MtAw or Exalted Abyss? ), we have Deamons ( I assume they are like FF Summons ), we have Elemental Dragons and Elemental beings. We also have Bat and Bear-People in previews.

    Something I missed?

          Danielle in this week Monday Meeting Notes is writing on design concepts of AtG and how she implemented them:

          Originally posted by Monday Meeting Notes
          At the Gates

          Since the ashcan came out, and we’re getting ready to crowdfund this hopefully soon, I wanted to chat a little bit about what At the Gates is, what it isn’t, and why it exists.When I first started working on Storypath, before I ever did the update, I thought the system would work really well for a high-fantasy spell-slinging game. I spent some time coming up with an idea for what kind of fantasy game I’d like to see and play, and it involved people who are diverse, but diversity isn’t gamified. A setting that had a variety of play styles for fantasy, but focused on being heroic. A game that had clear goals, hooks, and made it clear from the beginning what it is you’re supposed to be doing in it. So, I set to work creating this kind of setting. I am a huge fan of political intrigue, hard choices, moral ambiguity, and exploring the other through a lense of curiosity and compassion. So, of course, I put all that into my setting. As I was writing things up, I realized how much games like Final Fantasy 9, Chrono Trigger, The Secret of Mana, and others were influencing what I was writing. The further I got into the concept, the more I realized I had pretty much written the setting of a JRPG that didn’t exist. When I pitched the game to Rich (back in 2019, in the before-times) it wasn’t originally conceptually a jrpg, but he suggested that we just lean into it. So I did.
          I tweaked a few things here and there, but honestly, it was already there. Then when we started working on SP Ultra, I knew that this game was going to be very suited to its style. I was able to add in the concept of magical attacks, teamwork stunts that you can purchase with your bond Enhancement, and a slew of other really fun things that harken to playing a JRPG. If you’re knowledgeable about those games, you’ll see little bits here and there that remind you of many different games. If you aren’t familiar with JRPGs at all, that’s okay. I crafted the world to be rich and full of fun concepts that anyone can enjoy, you aren’t missing anything by not noticing the inspirations, because we didn’t decide to make the game referential.

          If you like fantasy games where the heroes are competent, it encourages you to explore, make hard choices, engage in political issues, fight against evil, and be big heroes, then I think you’ll enjoy At the Gates.

          Danielle

                          Danielle much more on At the Gates mechanics in contrast to Chrono Trigger...
                          Originally posted by Monday Meeting Notes
                          The third and most important reason is that a lot of the cool powers and abilities that you can do in Chrono Trigger gave inspiration to what you can do in At the Gates. I talk about it during the playthrough, but the fact that every character has an elemental affinity and special techniques they can learn on their own, and then combination techniques that they can perform with others. In At the Gates all characters have a set of Arts (special techniques) and Pillars of magic they can cast, and they can choose to utilize bond Enhancement to purchase special Tricks that act as combo Tricks.

                          I have also been answering some FAQ style questions that some people asked while I’m playing. A lot of it is mechanical considerations, reasons for making some of the choices I did, and other behind the scenes things that you won’t get just from reading the manuscript.

                          I’d love it if you join me on Tuesday at 5pm EST to play through Chrono Trigger and talk about At the Gates. This week I’ll be answering more questions, and next week I’ll be celebrating the Backerkit launch! I’ll also answer any questions you put into chat as I play, so this is a great opportunity to hit me with burning questions.

                                      At Character Creation for At the Gates podcast Danielle points that:
                                      • Daemons are thinly vailed allegory for Atomic Weapons! 😲
                                      • Most people in the setting have 'living magic's inside them - and you have many aesthetic special features added for free, like your skin is rock, you got bunny ears or crystals are coming from your skin. 😲 ( No mechanical difference, as I understood podcast. )
                                      • In the settting there is a big Magical Steampunk and science elements also! ( Quote from preview -'When the people of Everend speak of technology, thus, they mean steam harnessed by spirits, intricate brass coils catching the essence of the elements, and crystal cogs powered by magic.')
                                      • Arts - Technique skills: Many require Momentum - Desperation Ability ( your Final Fantasy-like Limit Breaks) - That needs O ( ZERO ) Momentum in the pool! Readiness Abilities - that needs 'AT Least this' number of Momentum.
                                      • Magic - Main Four Pillars ( Schools of Magic ) are - Anima is the magic of the body and Soul, Cognition is the magic of the mind and others, Elementalism is the magic of the land and elements, and Transmutation is the magic of change and manipulating magic itself.
                                      • One of 3 blocks of Pillar is you Keystone - 3 different cooldowns types on them: Instinct cast every single round, Elevated - cast only once a scene, and Iconic - cast once a session. You can refresh Elevated and Iconic by spending Energy. For Keystone, you can refresh Iconic and Elevated refresh them for free once per session.( Choose Keystone by looking what you want to cast twice per session. )
