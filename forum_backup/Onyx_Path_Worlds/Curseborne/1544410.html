      I'm working on going through step by step to see how character creation is actually meant to work.

      [Edit]

      Which I have now done!

      I tried making the "Prom Queen Who Hates Being a Werewolf" character archetype I mentioned on another thread.

      It was an...interesting experience.

      I tried to follow the character creation instructions as they were written, rather than carrying over my assumptions from other games. In particular, this meant picking my Paths (including the Role Path) based on what those Paths represented in-world rather than what stats they gave me. I did this because that honestly seems to be the intent; a lot of what I've been reading as flaws in the system might actually be a consequence of the system genuinely assuming that you don't care about where your dots go very much.

      Taking Primal (Major); Get of Lyka (Minor); Student (Minor) I got the following:

      Artistry 1, Athletics 3, Close Combat 2, Culture 1, Empathy 2, Persuasion 3, Leadership 1, Science 1, Survival 1, Technology 1

      Intellect 2, Cunning 3, Resolve 4; Might 3, Dexterity 4, Stamina 3; Presence 3, Manipulation 3, Composure 2

      Notably a lot of the character's stats wound up quite similar to Izzy's because obviously most of them come from the Paths and they're all the same.

      I think a particular thing to notice is that I wound up with Resolve 4 not because I particularly imagine this character as being immensely strong-willed but because the system kept giving me points in Mental Attributes and Resolve is the Attribute that feels like it says the least about your character. I didn't particularly want to be extremely intelligent or cunning.

                  Originally posted by The Gentleman Gamer View Post
                  Absolutely, if you select the same three Paths as one of the pregens you're going to end up with a similar array of Attributes and Skills, but do keep in mind you're free to create your own Role and if you don't feel that's clear, please submit to to the feedback form. Plus, I think there's more variation available than you're making clear.
                  Variously:

                  The fact that selecting the same three Paths gives you a similar array of Attributes and Skills is kind of what I mean by there being customisability issues with the core system because the way the game is set up, two of the three paths are fixed by the kind of supernatural being you want to be and the other one is basically fixed by your character concept. "Werewolf who is in highschool" is, by itself a concept that could cover all kinds of things but "werewolf" defines your Family and Lineage while "who is in highschool" covers the Role so it makes you quite mechanically constrained.

                  As for inventing a custom Role, this isn't currently just unclear, there straight-up aren't rules for it. Those rules can be reverse-engineered but, for example, there's no information about what Attribute Arrays are and are not acceptable.

                  Finally, yes it's possible to make characters that differ by more than one or two points if you deliberately skew them but that's the kind of white-room exercise that you (quite rightly) suggest we avoid. You still wind up with three characters who are (in old money terms) Physical Primary, Mental Secondary, Social Tertiary which is quite likely to not be what you actually want.

                  I'll repeat that none of this is a problem if, as you have suggested elsewhere, the Storyguide chapter provides options for just using free assignment.
