    I did a thing - Hunter and Construct Linages

    07-07-2024, 04:26 PM

    Helsings (inspired by core mortals, hunters, bits of demon the descent)

    The Curse is a disease and we are… no, we’re not the cure. That’s like saying that we’re here to kill off the Curse. We’re not – after all, we’re cursed too. Can’t deal with Curse without getting some on yourself too. Except, instead of being turned into something that defies the laws of reality as we know it? The curse inverts instead and we become more real, more human than ever, so much that it bleeds back over to the other side again. And, yeah, we can turn that against anything that isn’t native to our world. Just don’t forget that we get just as many angels as we do devils from it all.

    Skills - Empathy, Ranged Combat, Technology

    Practical Training – Bleed 1 curse die. You gain a +1 Enhancement on all rolls that use your Role skills (as modified by any spells or edges) for the scene. Gain a minor complication on all Actions using skills that are not part of any of your Paths.

    Only Human - You can spend 1 Momentum to negate the side effects from any Wicked Success or Cruel Failure. Alternatively, spend 1 Momentum to no longer register as Accursed to any supernatural senses* - this may provoke a Clash against some spells or abilities.

    MAJOR INHERITANCE -
    • Your Lineage Path skills count as part of your Role Path skills for all mechanics, including character creation.
    • You may establish a fact within a scene once per session, as if you had spent a Momentum, without cost.

    * Note - Accursed have a kind of innate, passive ability to sense when someone is another Accursed, and possibly can detect other kinds of supernatural beings/effects.

    Torment: NIMBY - Other supernatural beings hanging around? Not in your backyard! A character with this torment strains against the desire to remove the prickling sensation of supernatural forces from around them. When they succumb, they must somehow remove the source of the supernatural from their immediate presence in some manner. (Helsings using Only Human do not provoke this reaction).

    Torment: Zealous - The world is a large, scary place. And that’s before we start adding in the Accursed, Outsiders, Liminal spaces, and more. Its no wonder that many people turn to religions or other authorities for answers and comfort. A character with this torment has found one such source of answers that works for them, and often feels the need to share the Good News with others. When they succumbs, they spend time demonstrating or extolling their beliefs to any and all present, preferably loudly so that everyone can hear or see.

    Damnation - When a Helsing hits 0 Curse Die, they end up falling into their Role, far more strongly than ever, to the point of obsession, regardless of any moral compass. Any attempt to take actions unrelated to the Role skills suffer a medium complication on every roll. If the complication is not bought off, then the character must perform an action using one of the Role skills instead. With the wrong Role, this might turn a character into a killer.

    Damnation Resolution - The Helsing needs to remember there’s more to life outside of their enforced obsession. The character must somehow add two Momentum to the pool, then spend said Momentum to generate enough hits to overcome the Complication on a non-Role skill.

    Practices – Exorcism (based on VtM’s True Faith, CofD’s Abjuration, Benedictions, Castigation); The Professional (Professional Training on crack, bits of DtD’s Cover / Legend system, Group Tactics); Arsenal (relics, elixirs, and sufficiently advanced technology).

    Families- like above, each family's major inheritance will include key skills added to the "Role Path" skills, which in turn will be affected by the Professional Practice's spells as well as the Practical Training buffs.
    • Slayers - Buffy, anyone? The Chosen One by any other name.
    • Demon Hunter Bloodline - Halfway between an anime ninja clan and the Winchester Brothers. More sneaky than direct combat. Use talismans?
    • Totally Renamed SCP Foundation - United Nation-based group that specializes in isolating hazardous items and “beings”
    • Exorcist - priest-like people, be they christian style or otherwise.
    • Watchers - observe and record, but never interfere? Overlap with Network Zero?
    • Shadow Market - underworld figures that leverage supernatural connections to gather political influence and wealth (the mobster from Dresden Files?) Mercinaries.
    • The Hunt Club - actively seeks out supernatural challenges and battlegrounds for the thrill of overcoming them. Touches into Slasher territory. Antagonist vibes? Anyone can join.


    Lineage-only Edges

    Supernatural Tolerance - Gain a +1 enhancement to any defensive roll against a supernatural power. This does not include Clashes.

    Group Tactics - You may use the highest rated of your Bonds present in the scene for any teamwork roll, even if the teamwork roll doesn’t include said Bond’s target. One person you have a bond with can benefit from your bond rating even if you are not taking part in the teamwork roll.

    Note - When I sat down to do this, I wanted a few things. Above all else, I wanted to avoid pulling fantasy racism into the mix, and I really, really liked the idea of Professional Training being what made Hunters really click. Professional Training has now become the Role path, so I tried to devise the Helsings as being based around interacting with the Role Path.

    This Lineage was primarily inspired by Hunter the Vigil, Supernatural, Buffy the Vampire Slayer, the anime Helsing, SCP Foundation stories, bits of Dresden Files.

      The Constructs

      Constructs. The created ones. Golems, gargoyles, zombies and risen skeletons, living dolls, androids, homoculi, robots and more. Perhaps you are a new being that just came to life, or perhaps you were human but now have a bit of an upgrade. Who made you what you are tonight? Sometimes, it’s another member of the same family, looking for a companion. Or perhaps it was one of the Hungry, or a Sorcerer, or any one of the other Accursed. Maybe it was even a regular mortal or an Outsider or something even stranger. All that matters is that it's your life, and it's now or never.

      Skills - Enigmas, Esoterica, Artistry OR Enigmas, Science, Technology OR Enigmas, Medicine, Survival

      Analytical Mind - Bleed 1 curse die to gain +1 enhancement to all mental rolls. Influence actions not based on logic also have their difficulty raised by 1; this applies both when the Constructed is doing the Influencing or when someone is trying to Influence them.

      Purity of the Machine - Human bodies are failable. They require a delicate balance just to live, let alone survive injuries. The Constructed bodies, in many ways, are far more robust and simple, even those made from wood or flesh instead of stone or metal. Treat the Constructed as if they had Armor equal to their Entanglement. Further, Constructed have no need to eat, drink or sleep, as they’re powered directly by their Curse. Toxins that affect humans may not work on the Constructed either.

      Ship of Theseus - As constructed beings, members of this Lineage cannot heal on their own over time without a spell or other power. Instead, they must rebuild themselves using the same materials they were made from, determined by the chosen Lineage Path skills. Artistry and Esoterica implies specially carved wood, stone or clay parts; Medicine and Survival uses biological parts, akin to the famed Frankenstein Monster; and Science and Technology imply machine parts. Whenever you or someone on your behalf makes a healing roll, they may replace Medicine with either of the aforementioned Lineage Path skills. You are not limited to one healing action per scene, and failure to buy off a complication from combat-relevant status effects does cause them to worsen or gain a new status effect.

      In theory, this could allow a Construct to live forever by avoiding wear and tear and replacing worn out parts. Reality is rarely so kind, especially for those who rely on human brains to function.

      MAJOR INHERITANCE -
      • Perfected Body - So long as you’re holding 2 Curse dice, Actions involving the Physical Arena benefit from the 9-again rule. Once per scene, so long as you’re holding 2 Curse dice, you may choose to bleed 1 curse die to reroll all dice not showing a hit on a failed action involving the Physical Arena.

      Torment: Dysphoria - neither your body nor your mind is your own, but one shaped by another for their designs. When the character succumbs to this torment, they engage in acts of self-destruction, ignoring their own health and wellbeing as they fall into depression and engage in rote, mindless actions instead of speaking up for themselves, or coming across as cold and unfeeling.

      Torment: Abandonment - The Constructed’s relationship with their maker isn’t the best, and often (or entirely) left to their own devices for long stretches of time. When the Constructed succumbs, it pushes them to act out, either by picking fights or engaging in other intimate activities with others, regardless of the consequences.

      Damnation - When a Constructed reaches 0 curse dice, they run out of energy to power their bodies. They have a number of turns equal to their Entanglement (including the turn the character bled their last Curse die) before they shut down and gain the Taken Out (unconscious?) Condition. This Condition does not Resolve at the end of combat like normal, but will if taken to a Place of Deferment. Upon gaining the Taken Out condition, the character loses all memories of the last scene - short term memory loss, not saving to disk, etc.

      Damnation Resolution - The Taken Out Condition must be Resolved via first aid action, using the same rules under Ship of Theseus, or taken to a Place of Deferment. Depending on the individual Constructed physiology, this may be as simple as plugging a robot into a wall socket to recharge or as involved as restarting a fusion reactor or something more exotic, like a specialized serum injection.

      Practices - Electricity (lightning elementalism, control over electronic devices and minor control over human nervous system), Forge (the fires of creation and inspiration, radiation and light), Metamorphosis (control over the body’s shape and abilities, chimera- or transformer-like abilities, building towards your ideal body goals)

      Families - Each of the families are defined by PURPOSE, the reason that they’re made. A robot built for labor is considered part of the same Family as the Golem of Prauge as well as the skeleton servant raised by a necromancer, each just made with different materials.
      • Gargoyle - those built to watch and guard
      • Doll / Android - those built to be a pretty companion
      • Golem / Robot - those built to labor and slave (Mars Rover restored and turned into one?)
      • Effigy - those built to be sacrificed. Alternatively, those actually sacrificed but brought back wrong.
      • Homunculi / zombie / Frankenstein - those who’s making was their Purpose, but now are without.
      • Cyborg - augmented humans rebuilt to be Faster, Stronger, Better

      Lineage-only Edges

      Weatherproof - you’ve been proofed against extreme environments, and don’t suffer from them like others would. This includes a number of exotic environments found Outside or in Liminal Spaces.

      Residual Memories - Whether it's from another’s brain, or the information used to build an AI, your mind has a number of memories floating around that can be tapped into at need. Choose a number of skills equal to the cost of this Edge (1 to 3). You may spend a Momentum to allocate a number of dots equal to your Entanglement to those skills for a scene.
