    [Fan] Mortal Hunters Linage

    06-17-2024, 04:49 AM
    Note: It’s good to have spark for fan creations in me, after all this years. 😎 My reasoning is if mortal Sorcerers have Linage – mortal Hunters in vein of Hunter: the Vigil should also have one. 😊 Also, devs said directly that not all Linages are created on equal 'power level'.

    The Hunters

    ( Some opening flowery in-character description of Linage, from character cursing a lot. 😜 I’m bad at this, so will straight go to next sections. )

    Families – See Hunter: The Vigil Compacts & Conspiracies and their Endowments for ideas. ( Or look over in H:tV subforum for general ideas for those groups – here or here. 😊 )

    Lineage Rules

    The Hunters have several rules that apply only to them.

    Light in Darkness

    Each Hunter is fighting with darkness around the world. They see monsters and other Linages as they truly are. It’s their burden and part of their Curse. Whenever you activate Light in Darkness, it lasts for the rest of the scene. By the cost of 1 Curse dice spent per effect, you can get one those:
    • Gain +1 Enhancement that stacks to actions made in fighting the monsters.
    • Gain +1 Enhancement that stacks to actions made in investigating the monsters.
    • Activate Practice as Family Endowment. ( See Hunter the Vigil for Endowments ideas )
    • Get ‘Look Pass the Veil’, see below.
    Look Pass the Veil

    Hunters can pierce the Veil, separating mundane from supernatural. By spending 1 Curse dice, they can pierce any supernatural concealment of identity of monster ( like Mask of Outcasts, see ‘Slipping off the Mask’ their ability ) for one scene. Only the Hunter see the monsters true visage, but may share it with proper Practice with others.

    Damnation – Things you shouldn’t know…

    Your character must fight or discover other monsters. Whenever your character holds zero Curse dice, she feels overwhelmed by constant fight with darkness or horrible things she uncovered, related to Curse or world. The character cannot cast spells, use her powers, or gain Curse dice until she has mediated on those or being regrounded back by conversation with other mortal, taking one full scene on it.

    Resolution: She must rethink here place in universe or talk heart-to-heart with other mortal to gain 1 Curse die. If her half catatonic states makes things inconvenient for her party, she gains either 1 Curse die or Momentum (player’s choice). If the mediation or talk are in line with the Code of her Family’s tradition, she gains both.

    Option: ‘Regular Joe’ Hunter
    This Hunters take is loosely based on Hunter: the Vigil – in that game there are 3 Tiers of Hunters, each more and more supernatural and inhuman. Hunters Linage is reflecting Tier 2 & 3. ‘Regular Joe’ Hunters of the Tier 1 could be done by changing whole Linage Path of them to Origin Path ( from games like Trinity Continuum or Scion ), only those most mundane. In this option hunter don’t get Curse die – instead she can use non-supernatural parts of Light in Darkness and Things you shouldn’t know… - she CANNOT use Look Pass the Veil. Instead of paying for those powers with Curse die – hunter buys with her Injury, by straining her mind and body. Each usage of Light in Darkness costs her 1 Injutry level. But she also getting normal Injury bonus to rolls from this.
    So what you thinks about it?

    Will Master LostLight like this remodeling of Hunters for new game?

          Looks good! The only thing I'd tweak is maybe the Damnation criteria; that mechanic in Curseborne generally seems to be intended to emulate the more monstrous aspect of the pop-culture fictional sources for the main lineages. In urban fantasy/horror fiction the "monstrous" aspect of Hunters tends to be uncompromising fanaticism, ruthlessness, or tolerance for collateral damage. Damnation traits also mostly seemed designed to cause some kind of trouble or escalation rather than strictly de-power the character.

          Maybe the Damnation could be something like "Conviction; you have to refuse to compromise with another Accursed faction or individual on a matter of principle, or take extreme measures to achieve an objective."

          The Resolution criteria of having to reconnect with What You Are Fighting For seems fine, reminds me of Despair from Hunter: The Reckoning 5th.

              Warning, a lot of random thoughts and nothing cohesive (after all, I was summoned here)

              While I am following Curseborn there is still some things that I need to catch up with (I find it hard to get info out of playtests/livestreams, I just lack the attention span, I need things in writing to digest them). However, I do want to add into the discussion things about how their Curse is likely to be applied/activated- like how for the Dead it is activated by death, and how it is often related to a certain sub-section of how their Curse is defined (different lingering emotions for the Dead, different things to eat for the Hungry, etc).

              I think it would be appropriate if the Curse of Hunters would be somewhat along the lines of anti-thesis- something bestowed a Curse upon them, yet there was a certain backlash from their own soul that cannibalized the Curse and utilized it against its source. Basically, the soul took over the Curse and reused it as a tool against other Curses. As such, it creates a certain dichotomy- the Hunters are those who bear Curses geared towards destroying others, and that naturally mean that Hunters, as Curseborn, are likely to have some personal backlash to their own Curses- for example, they might drive them into being more and more zealot, or physically corrupt them, or make them into beacons which draw Curses towards them. Using H:tV as a source for some broad stereotypes, it could give you something along the lines of-

              Hunters- Curse as a Punishment

              Exorcists- breaking Curses through Faith, punished by Dogma
              Devourers- consumers of Cursed power, punished by Corruption
              Oathbound- hoarders of Curses as ancestral duty, punished by their Vows
              Emissaries- nullify Curses through social interactions, punished by Beaconing Curses
              Zealots- burn their own life to destroy Curses, punished by Zeal
              Augmented- modify their own bodies to defeat Curses, punished by Alienation.

              Obviously, all are temporary names and just rough concepts, nothing polished here.

              Now, if we actually want to translate groups from Hunter to Curseborn, it might a somewhat more complex mission considering that while Legacies do not necessary have a cap on the number of Families that they have, Hunter has a lot of organizations, and if we are talking about including both 2nd Tier and 3rd Tier, well, that's some big numbers. Instead, it might be interested to not fold them all under Hunters, either distributing them to other Legacies or having a few different Legacies derived from Hunters in such a manner. For example, Devourers might be a Legacy (would probably need a different name to be distinguished from the Hungry) which includes Cheiron, the Promethean Brotherhood, the Hototogisu, Faithful of Shulpae, and Les Mysteres. Lucifuge and the Otodo might feel more at home among the Outsiders, or might be related to a Legacy based around "ancestral Curses"- allowing those Dynasts to also include, say, the Aegis Kai Doru, Loyalists of Thule or the Watchers. Exorcists might be those groups who follow religious practices and are, in a way, Cursed by their own religion- the MM, the Knights of St George, Knights of St Adrian, the Ascending Ones, etc. Augmented will be those hunters who use chemicals and technology as vessels of their Curses, giving a place to TFV, VASCU, Network Zero, Operation Nebula and so on. And then we would have those "true Hunters", which means those archetypes which are indeed motivated by strong rejection of the Supernatual and are Driven to destroy Curses- the Ashwood Abbey are Driven by Pleasure, the Cainite Heresy are Driven by Zeal, the Enigmatics are Driven by Inspiration, the Long Night are Driven by Mercy, the Null Mysteres are Driven by Curiosity and the Union are Driven by Duty. Maybe by breaking it apart into more Legacies, the Hunters could gain a better focus.

              Again, just some streamlined thought, nothing cohesive.

                  Viewing the hunters as cursed is a good thing for adding them into the game as it maintains the focus on cursed beings. As for how they become a hunter, it happens in response to them coming in contact with the Accursed following its damnation or using a blatant spell, like shifting. The hunter witnessed the Accursed and became cursed as well.

                  For lineage abilities, the first one, "Perfect Predator," is that other Accursed don't sense their curse, which means hunters don't show as Accursed to even other hunters. It was mentioned in one of the creation vids, or the actual play, that Accursed can spot others Accursed somewhat naturally, so I would make this a benefit to the Hunter in that they are not detected so easily. They can still clock an Accursed, but they are not clocked automatically. A cool second ability, "Always Prepared," is the Punisher-esque ability always to be prepared, where the hunter can dictate a fact in the game, like they spent momentum to have something they prepared for the fight ahead of time. So they would get a Momentum, or two, in every combat scene that can only be used to dictate a fact in the scene. "I figured he would toss me weaponless into the living room at some point; luckily, I hid a gun behind the couch."

                  As for the families, I would lean into the methodology/ideology the hunter implores, which is divided into the hunter families. This, in turn, would dictate their practices and spells. Practice of True Faith, Hacking, Research, and so on. The spells would improve those things, but the Hunter, like several cursed, doesn't know they are using spells since spells in the game are a game term, and it is just how the Accursed pulls at the threads of their curse to cause things to happen.

                  For their damnation, I would lean into what was suggested before the Horror of being a hunter. For the greater good, you pursue your target without thinking of the harm that it will cause, and it will cause harm. It could be emotional trauma inflicted on the innocent or actual physical harm. This could also result in you being hunted yourself by mortal police. Torments like guilt or past actions can haunt you or as another torment; your self-righteousness causes you to live a life where only other hunters can be around you, though you feel that disconnect from society and are tormented by it.

                  Though this is how I would put hunters into the game, I don't feel they fit the game. Not because hunters don't belong in the world, but because they are a single-lineage sort of character that will not fit well with other Accursed and thus runs counter to the game's overall design where you can play single Lineage or mixed. Yes, they can be shoe-horned in, but they are, in essence, antagonists. Hunters would essentially be a single lineage-only game style; thus, I don't feel they fit in an official product. I feel they fit the genre and that having them as a 3rd party source or focus of a single Lineage game or as NPCs brought to bear against the PCs would be fun, though.

                                          I like the idea of the Hunter Lineage being the lineage that only kills accursed if they cross a line in how they treat mortals or if they are truly "Monsters." It gives them room to be in a mixed troupe, which is more in line with what the overall game allows without what I feel would be shoe-horning them in.

                                          I recall something similar being done in the Teen Wolf series with the Argent family, where they didn't like the Wolves but also had a strict code of which ones they would kill. To bring this into WoD terms, The Hunters became the masquerade/Veil/whatnot, much like a sheriff in a vampire city protecting the 1st Tradition.

                                          Tension in the form of "Hunters thinking they police us" is also something I feel would make a cool interaction in the game. The hunters also have some tension in that no one made them the police, so they try to maintain some connections or tenuous power to keep themselves from being wiped out by the other Accursed for killing a member of the family that stepped out of line. They garner friendships with those they feel are not monsters, so when they have to kill someone in the family, they have someone within the family who will stand up for their decision. The families could have some sort of how we go about our hunt vibe.

                                          Arbiters - Arbiters believe that they should not kill without some sort of due process. They seek to capture the accused and hold mock trials where the accused's family is allowed to bear witness or defend the accused. in a city, Arbiters are also the ones that tend to form treaties with other families and Lineages to garner rules and regulations on what is allowed within a city and what is not.

                                          Faithful - The faithful believe that their God's laws are the only laws and hunt those that fall outside those laws. They understand that the Devine, as the creator of all, has put these Accursed here as punishment and wish to see them carry out their sentence on earth, but any Accursed who continues to break God's Laws will find his justice swift and merciless. Atone for your sins or meet your maker is their guiding principle.

                                          Assassins - This Family is mercenary, through and through. They kill for money and don't really care who they hunt or why. They will hunt Accursed and non-Accursed alike. They care little for any code of conduct but being professional in your hunts. They don't work for free, and they are not always cheap, though some will work for favors in the future, but that is a devil's bargain if ever you saw one.

                                          Lab Rats - Scientists and occult experts, this family seeks to discover the source of the Curse, how it is transmitted, and how it can be cured. They hunt Accursed so they can study them and how the curse works. Capturing rare forms of the Accursed or working with lineages in exchange for some data is their purpose in life. Many other Families like to work with the Lab Rats because their ultimate goal is to cure the Curse once and for all.

                                          Huntsman - This lineage hunts for the thrill of hunting. The bigger, the better. They want to challenge themselves, and the more dangerous the game, the more they desire to hunt it. The huntsman will work with smaller Accursed, using them as their CIs to find the kingpins of the Cursed world. This is where the fun begins. This is where you can be truly measured to find your worth.

                                          Protectors - This lineage is not favored within the ranks of their own lineage as they answer the question of "who hunts the hunters?" they believe that, for whatever reason, the Accursed are a part of the natural order of the cursed world and should be protected from those that wish to destroy them, unjustly. They hunt normal hunters and Accursed alike. Any that would seek to kill the Accursed simply because they are cursed. Protecting the natural balance of the world is their purpose and credo.

                                                  There is nothing wrong with that, but the way the Cursed world works, you would just play a human with two fewer paths, no Spells, Practices, Damnation, or Torments.
