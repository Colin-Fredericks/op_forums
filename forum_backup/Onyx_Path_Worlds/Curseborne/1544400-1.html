                Having listened to some of these now, I'm a bit confused how I'm meant to take them.

                They seem to be being delivered in-character (there's a lot of "I've only seen it once, but I have seen it" for stuff that happens in-universe) and some of it seems to either contradict or at least raise questions about what's in the actual books, which makes it less helpful than it could be.

                For example: there's a reference in the Primal video to a woman whose Wild Form was "like a great griffin". Is that, like, actually a legitimate Wild Form? It's true that the only restriction cited in the book is "larger than their human form" but it seems weird that you need a specific spell to turn into a mundane animal but not to turn into a full-on heraldic beast. There's also the recurring question of what happens with wings in that case.

                The idea that Elder Primals also just straight up know how to cure Damnations is kind of a bombshell too. The description in the book strongly implies that they've been looking for that secret but never found it.

                                Having listened to most of these now, a lot of this is absolutely the kind of content I'm happy to see siloed into bonus content (I don't actually need to know the IC myths about Family Origins and some of them are in the book anyway) but some of this really needs to be in the book. In particular it's mentioned in multiple places that Outcasts don't age like regular humans, but the books don't seem to make that clear (and in a lot of places it's stated that Outcasts basically *are* regular humans, they're directly compared to Sorcerers in those terms).

                                [Edit]

                                Oh, and the other thing that struck me as really needing to be in the books. It's mentioned that Ascetics use some kind of keyword to make you literally tell them your secrets. If that's how Ascetic feeding is meant to work it really, really needs to be in the book. (Also that's a flavour text ability which absolutely replicates the effects of game mechanical abilities many of which are gated behind Entanglement, CD expenditure, or other limited resources).
