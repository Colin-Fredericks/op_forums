    Like Nicolas pointed - Curseborne Ashcan is live on DriveThru! Also, crowdfunding for whole book should be in October 2024. 😎



    Reading Linages in Ashcan, here are short summary of Linages and their traits. I compare them to CoD various monsters splats, as they are closest to them – but still, Curseborne Linages are much more ‘general’ and ‘archetypical’. So here we go:
    • Dead – Revenants and Sin-Eaters – You died but stayed in your body. You now can leave the body as ghost, but you risking becoming not self-aware phantasm.
    • Hungry – Vampires and Beasts – You died but become insatiable undead being. You choose your Feeding type of blood, emotions or spirit essence and stick to it. You also gains Undead Strength powers, but those generate Bane for the scene – sunlight, silver or fire are just examples for those. You can gain more Strengths for gaining next Banes.
    • Outcasts – Part demons, angels, fae and a like – You have ‘unhumane form’ that is ‘masked’ most of the time and some supernatural feel about you. Can ‘slip the Mask’ and give a bit of you otherworld nature to mark social interaction with outers. Can go to the Battlegrounds, mixed half alien dimensions, half our Earth. When loosing Curse, you need to reconnect with humans – or being shunned out by any interactions with mortals.
    • Primal – Werewolves and other shapeshifters – You change into Wild Form, animal based Creature, always larger than human. Shapes of common animals, require specific spells. In all forms you have supernatural senses. Without Curse Dice, they transform into Wild Form and Creature need to be satisfied by it’s instinctual behavior.
    • Sorcerer – Mages, sorcerers and witches – You are sorcerer with variety of spells at your disposal, but your magic is also somehow cursed. But we do not have any real mechanical info about them in Ashcan. ☹ ( Waiting for crowdfunding for whole book info on it. )
    We also get 4 Ready Made characters for those Linages ( beside Sorcerers ).

    Wyrd’s Opinion: I generally like this ‘changes to genre’ between CoD and Curseborne. 😊 Only I’m not sure I like that Primals need to burn extra spells slots to have other animal forms, compare to CoD Uratha what have all 5 forms from start. ☹ And we don't know anything about Sorcerers mechanics. ☹

                        My Notes from Curseborne Panel on OPP Con 2024 😎

                        Crowdfunding for Curseborne corebook -> October 1st 2024 ( for KS ending around Halloween 😎​ )

                        What is Curseborn? -> ‘Spiritual Successors’ to WoD and CoD, as Onyx Path own IP.

                        Curseborne is mix of Urban Horror and some Punk and Hopepunk understones.

                        Curseborne is start of very large line - Gameline assumptions for now – You play low level Family member with low Entaglemnet. ( 1-5 ) Second book for ‘Ancient Monsters’ ( 6-10 ), with more Entaglemntes around your Family and Curse. Ideas for Player’s Guide and fictions. ‘Cursed World’ locations book, around world, with important NPCs/SGCs. New Linages and Families book(s?).

                        Is there be metaplot or Lore? There will be events, locations and NPCs mentioned. ( But no sWoD like ‘metaplot’ of ‘and now Betharits are not playable’. )

                        Magic system is similar to ‘At the Gates’ mechanics. 😊Linages have unique magics – but you can learn other Linages powers. So as Hungry you have Hungry spells – but can learn Primals shapeshifts. And Primals are not only animal spells – but also elemental.

                        Will there be Slasher-like beings? There will be creepypasta-like beings, probably new Linage.

                        Every Family have internal conflict – and that that are always some jerks and problematic parts in them. They are all very ‘Found Family’ concept.

                        What’s with a Moths symbols? Active at night, undergone transformation from webs – and creature of darkness seeking light, that also easily die. Symbol as graffiti at the wall in setting.

                        The ‘I did a supernatural deal’ ideas for characters? Most would be that your ancestors made deal and you now need to live with the Curse, after sins of your ancestor.

                        Everyone is using spells and Practices – it takes from universal root of Curse, ‘at the center of the world’. You can pull up by the threads of Curse, to use your magic.

                        Inspiration media – ‘House of Usher’ 2023 TV series, Urban Horror on general, ‘Unsolved Mysteries’ Podcast

                        No AI art – fresh artistist, not from WoD & CoD already doing.

                        Daniel L. was CurseBoi on Discord 😉

                        Curseborne ARG marketing campaign was having the biggest resposnes of every OPP game made EVER.

                                                Originally posted by Professor Phobos View Post
                                                Do we get any info on antagonist groups, full monsters, mortal interference, and similar "opposing force" factions?
                                                We actually get a good look at how weird the non-Accursed monsters are; there's only three fully statted, but beyond the basic flesh-eating zombie, who is actually pretty hardy, we have Drones, who are proof of how scary the malevolent supernatural can be (they're mortals that have had any ability to feel or connect eaten out of them, turning them into meek, helpless shells of themselves - a really distinct scene in the quickstart is meeting two non-hostile Drones, and it's clear they literally can't muster up the will to clean themselves under their own power), and Shattered Spaces, which are haunted areas. Not monsters in the area, the area themselves; while the Outside happily seeps into everywhere it can and creates Liminalities, anomalies in space and time that can be any sort of noun, most of those are only incidentally dangerous, even interstitial zones (sentient pockets in reality that want to be lived in - so they make themselves inviting and secure so you don't want to leave as badly). Shattered Spaces are what happens when it's an utterly malevolent part of the Outside coming through, making the area alive, sentient, and hungry. As well as a beachhead for whatever hell spawned them, sooner or later. See: Silent Hill (though Silent Hill itself may be a Battleground, a portion of the Outside that burrowed into the mortal world, that opens to traumatized people).

                                                    My review I put on DriveThru on Ashcan...

                                                    4/5 Stars

                                                    Curseborne Ashcan is not perfect. I’m not saying is bad…
                                                    I’m old WoD and CoD Storyteller. I was at the start ( and even few years before ) of coming of New World of Darkness in 2004 and stick to all the line of CoD for all it’s run till 2020, supporting almost all gamelines. I’m also Storypath veteran, running 3 gamelines of it to this day.

                                                    And I must say that Curseborne Ashcan is not perfect. I’m not saying is bad – only it leaves me in few places too much to wondering about game. In the end, I’m waiting for crowdfunding manuscript of whole book coming in October 2024.

                                                    Pros:
                                                    • Interesting and ‘dynamic’ changes to previous concepts in WoD and CoD.
                                                    • Interesting model and mechanics of using Curse dices
                                                    • Refreshing look on genere by Linages and their new takes on old monster archetypes.
                                                    • Return to Urban Horror setting after those 4-5 years. 😊

                                                    Cons:
                                                    • Ashcan don’t show ‘bigger picture’. I still literally don’t know how should I run the game or how general world/setting of game looks from characters point of view.
                                                    • Somehow illogical position of Families in 3 Paths system – Families are dependent on Linages already, being subPath locked itself, not free option choosing like in different Storypaths.
                                                    • No real mechanics and descriptions what are Sorcerers in the setting.
                                                    • A bit too much vulgar language to my taste in in-person descriptions.
