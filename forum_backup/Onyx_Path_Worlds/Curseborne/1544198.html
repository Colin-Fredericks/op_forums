    Inheritances

    10-18-2024, 10:05 AM
    How are people feeling about Inheritances and how they fit the major/minor Path dynamic at the moment?

    in a couple of cases I absolutely can see how they work for reinforcing your vision of the character. Get of Lyka, for example, get to pick between leaning heavily into "grr Werewolf" with bonuses to unarmed attacks and an extra dot in physical, or leaning into the more abstract Hunter thing and getting bonuses to investigate and a dot of mental.

    But for a lot of families, or even whole Lineages, the Inheritance seems to be quite a core part of the fantasy that you don't want to give up.

    Variously:

    All Dead need to choose between their Family inheritance (often tied to the edition they need to feed on) and a resistance to damage that feels like it's intent to what the Dead *are*

    All Hungry need to choose between a regeneration bonus that feels like it should be just part of being a vampire or their Family-specific thing.

    Outcasts can't see the true forms of other Outcasts units they're Lineage-major even though Cassie explicitly says they can all do it in her narration.

    Vondnici can't breathe underwater units they're family-major

    Skimming Sorcerers, the Network at least seem to really *need* their family Inheritance to do their thing although to be fair nothing in the rules says you have to stick to your family's form of sacrifice.

    Basically it often seems like a bit of a false choice and, worse, one that's tied to another false choice.

      Yeah, I felt that those need to be pointed out to them to change things up via the feedback form.

      With that out of the way, I was thinking about this problem.
      Give each Family a Major Path and a Minor Path and take out the Lineage Path entirely.
      If you choose, say Battleground Angels as your Minor Path, then you get the Inheritance listed for Outcasts. If you choose for it to be a Major Path, you STILL get the Inheritance for Outcasts but you ALSO get the one for Battleground Angels.
      You just change the power and adjust some on the Lineage but you still get it. This also solves the point distribution issue involved in the other thread as it allows for far FAR more versatility and lets you play against type as a lot of people prefer.

                          Ironically this thread is drifting a bit away from my original intent into something else I was also planning on starting a thread about.

                          FWIW here's a summary of what I believe we *know* about Necromancy, Soul-Eating and Emotion-Draining in Curseborne.
                          • We know Phantasms are a thing (released chapters)
                          • We know some spells affect incorporeal entities (released chapters)
                          • We know Zombies exist as antagonists (ashcan)
                          • We know drones - people with no soul and little will - exist (ashcan)
                          • We know some spells can alter a person's emotional state (released chapters and ashcan)
                          • The Gaki inheritance lets them summon a phantasm
                          • The Gaki Secret Spell lets them attract the attention of incorporeal entities
                          Here's what I think we can reasonably assume
                          • Vorare soul-eating and Black Heart emotion-draining are unlikely to have bespoke rules (dev responses and actual plays)
                          • Gaki don't seem to have an inherent ability to interact with incorporeal beings (Suffer My Sins actual play)
                          • A bit of a stretch but I think sound: the Gaki have a Motif which lets them remove the Physical tag from some spells, which I think *probably* implies that this will let them cast spells on incorporeal entities that they otherwise couldn't. [I misremembered, it's clearer than that, they ignore the Incorporeal tag of the targets of Physical spells, which implies the same thing but much more strongly].
                          • Some of this is likely to change before launch
                          Although I also like zombies-necromancy, I don't mind ghosts-only either and I wouldn't be upset if the Gaki were ghosts-only because that's what their flavour text seems to imply. I'd be upset if the Ritual Magic section or the expanded Spells section of the rules didn't build on that (there's some implication that individual spells have upgrade paths in which case Forbidden Temptation might be able to do more than just uselessly attract ghosts you can't perceive or interact with).
