    [Spoilers] Curseborne Ashcan - How would you expand upon "The Danger of Missing Out"?

    08-26-2024, 01:22 PM
    So I have run the Curseborne Ashcan's The Danger of Missing Out, and I was wondering of possible ways of improving it. For me specifically I want to incorporate more of what we see in the Ashcan itself with the Character Contacts, Aspirations, etc... being incorporated a bit more. But also expand upon what is already written about the purpose behind the Champagne Room.

    What is Really Going on:
    The ending leaves a few unanswered questions:
    • Who is running the club?
    • How did Alex get killed?
    • What created the Champagne Room?
    Part of the description of the Champagne Room mentions that the Mavens and Heirs are worried about their kin going missing in the Champagn Room's vicinity. Which makes me think that there is someone who is Accursed running it, a few options I think could work out are:
    • Brother of Evelyn.
    • An Archon or equivillent
    • A Hungry of another Family who made a deal with the Champagne Room to feed it people in order.
    • The DJ
    Note: if an Accursed is in the Champagne Room the other Accursed will know they are there if they see them. So if the DJ is an Accursed the other players will know they are and vice versa. (I think that's how that works?)

    Sam
    • Aspirations:
      • Hire a New Bartender (Short Term),
      • Score some drugs,
      • find a new body (long term)
    • Role Path - Criminal
    • Contacts:
      • Brooks Timms, Drug Dealer 3 (Dangerous, Fence, Sneaky)
      • Florence Shaw, cursed him 1 (Tech Geek)
      • Norman, Maven Bro 1 (Loyal)
    As far as the aspirations go I feel like Sam is pretty good for the current mission: He could easily find drugs and a new bartender in the Champagne Room. But also if he needs a new body he could possess either of the Drones or Alex so long as the bodies aren't damaged.

    As a Criminal I could see using the Drug Dealer as a way to get access to the Champagne Room if need be, but not necessary. Florence I can't really think of a way to incorporate her, and Norman might be a good way to hint at some Accursed connection to the Champagne Room if he knows something, but can't say it directly.

    Evelyn
    • Aspirations:
      • Blow off some steam,
      • stick it to one of her brothers,
      • prove she's independent to her Mother.
    • Role Path - Influencer
    • Contacts:
      • Allen Griffin, Hungry PR Manager 1 (Investigator)
      • Mother, Hated Mentor 2 (Dangerous, Influential)
      • Pierre, cousin/rival 1 (Sneaky)
      • Sylvia Tanner, marketing friend 1 (Informant)
    I feel like blow off some steam and proving her independence are pretty decent aspirations. Stick it to one of her brothers makes me think that a Brother of hers from the Bathorites is in league with the Champagne Room.

    As far as her role and contacts go, I can see how Influencer, her marketing friend, and Hungry PR manager might know about the Champagne Room. But Pierre and her Mother I'm not too sure about.

    Cassandra
    • Aspirations
      • Win a case
      • Finda Battleground
      • Defeat an Archon
    • Role - Investigator
    • Contacts
      • Simon, Legal Assistant 2 (Informant/Investigator)
      • Aunt Pat, Outcast Mentor 1 (Mentor)
      • Kevin Fleming, owner of an obscure magic show (Informant, Fence)
    I think Cassie's best trait here is the Role as investigator. I feel like I should adjust the aspirations to Win a Legal Argument which could be used against the police, Find a Battleground I might change to Find a Liminality as this would cover the Champagne room. And Defeat an Archon is a bit too out there since we haven't found out what Archons are yet, but I could see modifying it to something like Stop a Conspiracy.

    As far as her Contacts go I feel like Aunt Pat might be the only one useful for this scenario. Though I could see trying to talk with Kevin after the Champagne Room maybe?

    Izzy
    • Aspirations
      • Establish High School as my territory
      • Score some weed
      • Graduate High School
    • Role - Student
    • Contacts
      • Tommy, School Mate 1 (Fence)
      • Grandma 1 (Healer)
      • Elvis, Eight Hand Mentor 1 (Training)
      • Campus Security System 1 (Security)
    Izzy is not as adaptable I feel. Though I think there may be a way of incorporating the school as a fallback position if need be or a place where they could go to do some research.

    Aspiration of getting weed synergizes with Sam's Score Some Drugs aspiration. But the rest aren't as do-able within the adventure. Establishing the HIgh School as territory feels a bit like a more long term goal than a short term one. I'd possibly replace this with a short term goal of successfully track a quarry or solve a problem with intimdation or brute strength.

    I could see Grandma helping out if anyone gets injured. Elvis could be helpful in Accursed affairs maybe. The campus security system could work if the school was nearby the Champagne Room which seems unlikely but might help resolve that issue.

    What do you all think?
