    Are You Meant to Care What Your Stats Are?

    10-22-2024, 09:26 AM
    I mentioned this briefly in the thread on which characters people are making first but looking at the full system, the only way I can make it make sense is if I assume that I'm literally not meant to care what my character's Skills and Attributes are beyond the in-the-moment choices of where to assign the dots I get from Paths.

    As I read the system, Paths (even Role Paths) are (a) intentionally designed as a closed list and (b) meant to be chosen based on what they represent in-world, not on the stats they give you.

    Soft evidence for this comes from the Ashcan characters who all have the from-list option that broadly maps to their character concept. Cassie has "Investigator", not a custom Role for "Lawyer" and Izzy has "Student" even though she's mechanically primarily a combat character.

    Through this lens the system does *work* but it feels increasingly like having Attributes and Skills at all is almost a legacy feature in that case.

    Of course you can go the other way and only focus on what Attributes and Skills you want but then you're ignoring everything about your character you're meant to invest in and also ignoring the plain text of the character creation system.

          Yeah 5-6 dice is kind of my comfort zone as well. Given how many Attribute points you get, it's pretty easy to get a character who can have a dice pool of 5+ in a wide range of things. What's slightly weird is that the game doesn't give you a huge amount of control over *which* things.

          If I play a Hyde, then I *must* have at least 9 of my 16 Skill points in some combination of Enigmas, Leadership, Science, Athletics, Close Combat and Survival. I have to have at least 6 dots in Mental Attributes and 5 in Physical. My Social Attribute pool maxes out at 6 and then only if I am *specifically* an Influencer.

          What I'm struggling with is whether this is a bug or a feature. Obviously it's a radical departure from previous games, even previous Storypath games but I do also want to give it a fair shake. Although the title of this thread is a teensy bit hyperbolic I do think that the system *seems* to want you to view your character's Attributes and Skills almost as something you discover during character creation, rather than something you deliberately aim towards.
