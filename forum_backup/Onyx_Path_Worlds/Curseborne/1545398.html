    Curseborne from a neuroatypical perspective

    11-05-2024, 02:30 PM
    This may be a TMI sort of post but Curseborne resonates with me and my experiences on the spectrum a good deal more than the World of Darkness. I've always been attracted to the prospect if playing monsters/outsiders and people with fundamentally different views of the universe. The "Hidden World" that you see differently from everyone else is something that I don't have to explain the parallels for but I've always felt has been the case. So is having difficulty connecting with other people and even alienating them because of how you approach things so differently.

    I won't try and get into the issues particular to myself or details but instead note that the WOD was once really important to me because of how it made me feel seen as well as validated. Some games more than others.

    Unfortunately, the gameworld seemed to drift away from the elements I most liked about it overtime in terms of roleplay. Vampire: The Masquerade became more Sabbat and less broody philosophers. Changeling: The Dreaming never really did achieve the popularity it deserved. Mage: The Ascension? Well, if you feel society is crushing down on you and trying to invalidate your experiences then the shfit in focus from Revised felt jarring.

    Curseborne, by contrast, captures that kind of otherworldly alienation as well as keeps "you are a monster" within a reasonable level. The Curseborne are not savage beserekrs or ticking murder time bombs but people who do struggle with internal issues. They CAN go crazy but it's under your control unless you want to roleplay that loss and more like a constant neverending temptation than a psychotic break.

    Throw in the Limilarities and how very odd and dangerous feeling the world is while not necessarily being so at all times and you have a really strong representative of how not only I but many others experience the world.​

    Kudos to the writers.

    Author of Cthulhu Armageddon, I was a Teenage Weredeer, Straight Outta Fangton, Lucifer's Star, and the Supervillainy Saga.
