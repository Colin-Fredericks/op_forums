        Originally posted by idpersona
        So I have a question. Does Spirit 2 summon (as in make appear) spirits? Or does it call to them, to come if they want to. There is a very big difference here. I am genuinely curious how this works.

        With the Nexus Crawler example, I'd find it hard to believe that you could put out a call, and have one show up terribly fast, since they generally exist far removed from the Penumbra. I would imagine that if if were possible to have one appear, it would take a lot of successes to do so and thus (logically, to me) require a ritual casting. Or is my understanding of this off (it very well may be)?
        Spirit 2 makes a "call" for nearby spirits. It's like yelling a "Hey, you!. Yes, you, the tree spirit" in the Umbra. I'm not sure of how big it's the area of effect, but I guess the more successes, the "stronger" it's your yelling.

        You need Correspondence 2 to call spirits that are very far. This works like Correspondence, so the number of successes needed depend on your connection with the spirit rather than the distance. Calling your Familiar from the other side of the universe may be easier than calling an unknown spirit in another city. You can call a given spirit only if you know something about the spirit, but you could also call a "generic" spirit.

        Spirit 4 compells the spirit to come and obey you (powerfull spirits are harder to compel, obviously, and may consider it rude)

        Neither of these effects make the spirit appear in front of you, they just call the spirit and inform it where you are.

        Correspondence 4 makes the spirit appear in front of you.

        So, yes, the Nexus Crawler example would have likely failed

                    idpersona :

                    To add to my previous post about calling and summoning:

                    To summon a spirit to fight for you like a D&D mage summons a fire elemental, you would need Spirit 4/Correspondence 4. This it's very hard because you need to control the spirit, and this demands a huge number of successes (not sure how many, but this it's usually a ritual), and then you would need more successes to teleport it where you are

                    However, if you already had a pact with the fire elemental, it would be Spirit 2/ Correspondence 4, and much less successes because summoning someone you have a connection it's easier than summoning a random guy, and you wouldn't need to compell the elemental.

                    Also, Spirit 4 can make the spirit appear in the material world if he can't Materialize (outlandish and vulgar, but quite awesome)
