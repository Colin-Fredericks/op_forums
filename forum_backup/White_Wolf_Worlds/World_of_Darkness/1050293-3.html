                                        I've not played the game, but having the Tremere be hard line Noddists seems like a weird choice to me. From my perspective the Tremere should be the clan least into Noddism. While they're big into occult texts and Noddism doesn't lack for those I've always felt the real meat and potatoes of Noddism lies in oral tradition passed from sire to childer. Since the Tremere don't go back to First City and sire/childer relationships are generally not as emphasized in the pyramid I don't think there would be as much of a connection there. There's also the fact that the more you talk about ancient Noddist lore the more you draw attention to the fact that the Tremere are late-coming usurpers, which doesn't seem like something they'd be keen on. Finally there's the fact that entering vampirism from the outside, specifically as an order of mages, and the methological practice of their blood magic, means that their perspective on the vampiric condition should arguably be broader and open to a larger set of definitions than generally provided in Noddist orthodoxy.
