            How are they actually pulling off supposed mystic feats while being dependant on technology? Technology that they also need to hide?
            Hide the technology in plain sight if you have to. Keep in Mind that an NWO can constantly use Mind Adjustments to make sure no questions are asked - they might have impressive and touching stories on each of their 'foci', to the point when they might be complemented and respected because of their style of Magick!
            -All of the first levels of the Spheres can be simulated with biologically modified or cybernetic eyes. (or other body modifications – it could be any of the senses) Entropy might be more complex, but there is an Adjustment in the new Syndicate books that allows a Syndic to compute probabilities during a firefight; so you can implant such a device into a Grey Suit's brain or inside a mystical-looking item.

            -Carrying a supposed mystic foci for Prime while having a Primal Energy device implanted inside you. Each time you hold the crystal to call upon Prime, you also engage the device. The device is recharged by the Tradition node or Tass normally.

            -Spirit seems impossible - Void Engineer devices seem quite bulky, and spirits could sense the deception. Though if you want, Spirit 1 can be technological glasses pretending to be mystical, and for other effects mystic-looking crystals - or ominous carved black boxes – filled with Dimensional Science tech can be used.

            -Time - say that you can only divine events in regards to people you have a connection to. Predict what people will do using Inspired psychology, while for appearance sake doing some ridiculously over-complicated Tarot spread, that you also prearrange cards for so that what you get matches your predictions. If you're afraid of a Tarot authority busting you, you can instead take something like Magic the Gathering cards.
            You can also have a hidden chronometric device which you use while alone, and then you say you discovered something during a trance. It could be overt – like a seemingly mystical crystal globe that has technology for Time 2 that shows events at a location. Time 3 bullet time or extra actions can be simulated easily with implanted tech or drugs made to look mystical. Rewinding or freezing time devices, if they exist, are too bulky for infiltrations I imagine.

            -Mind - same as Time for mind reading. For Mind influencing you might have to use other methods, like psionics or pheromones – it's hard to simulate Vulgar or near-Vulgar Mind with NWO Adjustments, especially since you won't be able to pretend to call upon the usual governmental authority. Or you can say that it disagrees with you to influence someone's mind, and thus you can only read minds.

            -Technocratic compounds/nanomachines disguised as alchemy for Matter. A technologically bulletproof hoodie can be adorned with 'mystic runes' etc. Such hoodies can even be given out to Mages or Acolytes for protection.

            -Life: use Technocratic mixtures or nanomachine batches put into colorful potions.Cybernetics or other Progenitor alterations can be made to look mystic. All it takes is having an retractable extra arm that seems to come from a mystic ritual (that you faithfully 'cast' every week to maintain it) to convince Traditionalists you have mystic Life 3.

            -Forces – the wand or dagger you carry might have a particle (de)accelarator implanted inside. Those functions can be engaged with a mind impulse, while pretending to use it mystically. Explosions can be created by putting a super-volatile compound into the air.

            -A Correspondence: Data device modified to look like a mirror. You can even keep the interface, just make it look all mystic.

            -Entropy 1 divinations can be faked as mystic just like Time. Entropy 2 and 3 probability manipulations I don't really see as fakeable, as Technocrats need lots of preparation time and resources to do them, as far as I know. Entropy 3 disrupting machines can be faked with an electromagnetic pulse – just hope that nobody has Forces sight active while you are doing it.

            As an interesting option, you can have the supposedly fake mystic foci actually work, possibly cause they put so much technical effort into them - like the Tarot spread showing Prince of Cups, the Moon and the Tower, despite being prearranged differently. This would indicate a Malkavian Prince planning to make the Chantry target of the Camarilla. What will the Technocrat do? Especially since the Mages will give him great respect as they also divine in this direction, confirm the danger, and ward against it.
            Or the 'fake' Prime crystal working for Prime even after the implanted Primal Energy device malfunctions.


            Traditions scanning foci for technology might be a problem. Is it said anywhere that Technocrats can't learn mystic Spheres at all? In the end this is also a possibility – the Order of Reason knowingly used scrying/projection crystals for Correspondence because it was just too good of a capability to deny themselves.

                    Come to think of it, a Technocrat infiltrator would have a much easier time avoiding the above pitfalls by simply pretending to be a member of one of the many technomancer factions within otherwise mystic Traditions. House Thig/Verditus, the Lhaksmists, Techno-shamans, the Alexandrian Society, etc. Groups that otherwise adhere to the basic worldview of their parent groups, but who blend them with modern technology. In this case, it would pay to make the integration of technology and traditional tools more obvious. Hiding in plain sight, as it were.

                    One problem: the Traditionalists rarely fully trust their technomancer kin. At best. Likely for the very same reason they seem so tempting to infiltrate as. I'm willing to bet the Traditions have been burned by this sort of thing before, and are on their guard. Even if they don't know for certain that the technomancer in question is a spy, many would rather avoid giving them too much information. Part of this is just blind prejudice, certainly, but even that works unknowingly in the Traditions' favor when it comes to actual infiltrators.

                                Fair enough, except I question the credibility of any plan that relies on using Mind to fool Mages, when anyone with Mind 1 could have the chance of realizing their perceptions and thoughts are being tampered with. The tactic would work on some Traditionalists, yes, and a sufficiently skilled MiG could make their Effect subtle enough to get past many mental defenses unnoticed. But with this approach, the infiltrator has a delicate tightrope walking ahead of them. They don't need to layer Procedures onto their tools, nor do they need to answer uncomfortable questions if they ever fail (or are simply noticed, as noticing a lie can be almost as damaging to one's cover as the actual truth being known). This is why Technocrats prefer to go undercover as Etherite or Virtual Adept members.

                                        Pick a group of younger mages without those capabilities. Effortless to find out. Just have your MiBs working with recon it out at every level before infiltration (surveillance, sousveillance, a few well-placed attacks to test out their capabilities). Focus is highly individualized. Just make it long term. If your buddies trust you, they'll trust the peculiar way you do things. They will be doing their own peculiar things. And they'll defend your weirdness to other mages, if you are playing your cards right. After all, you don't have to rely on Hypertech or Cybernetics as the Practices... Art of Desire/Hypereconomics, Dominion and Reality Hacking and possibly Martial Arts or even Yoga all will fly under the radar much more easily. Instruments help out too... Drugs and poisons, books and periodicals, dances and movement, eye contact, formulae and math, Household (or mundane) tools, language, meditation, social domination, voice and vocalizations and so on. And that's where the "fool them twice" works really well. Do what they expect and they'll think it's working how they expect it to, even if, as far as you're concerned, it's working for a completely different reason. Make THEM have to invade YOUR mind to find out that you don't have the proper 'think" going. After all, that's rather rude, at best...

                                        The best asset? The Nine Traditions have no internal organization in a meaningful "on the ground" way. None. At all. Compare it to the average other splat... Vampires and their cities, Wraiths and their necropoli, Werewolves and their Caerns, Changelings and their Freeholds. Chantries across towns don't have to have any relation to each other at all. Or even be aware of each other. Much less any other cabals that might be in the area. And since Mage is explicitly not supposed to be a location-based game (according to Brucato) that means that almost every cabal is just passing through from here to there and their path across the Tapestry, that makes the Nine Traditions at the fine grain EXTREMELY easy to infiltrate. It's barely a confederacy at the highest end. Sure, SOME of the individual Traditions arguably have internal organization, but that doesn't cross Tradition lines for the most part. And a large number of the Traditions are a complete organizational mess. The most well-organized loci are the Chantries, particularly large, powerful, old and well-organized Chantries are going to be a problem. So avoid them. That's why you start with a small independent group and move them towards that as an end goal. If you only need to get into Horizon for a single info grab, that's doable. Dangerous, but doable. If you want to take the Seat of Matter... good luck.

                                        It also might be better to stay away from VA's and Sons of Ether. Sure, they do machines too, but the Union ways of doing machine are consistent. Theirs are idiosyncratic. The fact that you're not doing weird individualistic stuff will make you stand out. Both have really bad histories with the Union and apt to be more on the look out, as much as they can be, for infiltration. And the hoary old types in the Chantries of the other Traditions think VA's and SoE's are barely "real" mages either, johnny-come-lately refugees from the Union and are therefore easier for the Union to infiltrate.
