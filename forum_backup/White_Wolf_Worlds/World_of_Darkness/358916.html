    Looking for ideas on introductory stories for a few different cWoD games.

    01-04-2015, 04:39 AM
    Over the next few weeks, I plan on running several introductory stories, 1 each, for a few different cWoD games. As of now, I’m planning on Hunter (the Reckoning), Werewolf, Vampire and Changeling.

    Right now, our gaming group consists of only 2 players familiar with the classic world of darkness (in a general sense) and 2 players that have had some exposure to the system, but not really to any of the games. Out of those 2 players, 1 is familiar with D&D and the other has only role played once, in a game I ran last year, that used the system at its core, but wasn’t any specific game. (I like the WoD system for its ability to make well rounded out human characters)

    So the plan right now, is to have each introduction last only a session or 2, to introduce the themes and concepts of each line and then afterwards we can see which the group enjoyed most and hopefully play that one regularly. I’ve pre-generated 17 characters, all high school students. I’m hoping to go through the transformation (embrace, chrysalis, first change, imbuing, etc.) so the players aren’t top loaded with abilities and can figure things out through game play. Each story will be independent of one another, but using a shared background (the school). So each new story may have new characters, but as we play, the backgrounds will be filled in. Relationships, such as who knows who from before these events will stay and we will build up the school as a setting little by little. All of the characters are tropes (metalhead, cheerleader, etc) just to make it easier on the players. That has already been discussed with each of them and they are all on board.

    So yea…I have a full Hunter story set up, and possible frameworks for Werewolf and Changeling stories. All I can come up with for this sort of thing for Vampire would be centered around a Sabbat siege, as mass embracies, for either side. This is the weakest of the ideas which is why I’m asking for help from the forums. Mind you, if you have ideas for 1 shots for the other game lines listed (Changeling or Werewolf mostly) I’d love to hear them. Or even if someone has an idea on how to run an intro to another game line that won’t over-confuse my players (Demon, Mummy, etc). I am trying to stay away from Wraith with them though, since even talking about the setting had the new players thinking that it might be too depressing or complicated (with Shadowguides) for them.

    Any ideas or feedback would be greatly appreciated.

      For Vampire since your doing a high school setting is to do one of those now a days popular but simple plots about a Vampire falling for one of the players and Embracing him/her after which that player could Embrace the rest or something, this is a one shot after all. Sabbat mass Embrace could also work as could them being chosen for one of the Game's for a Sabbat pack or even a game between two packs, the surviving members could be granted the Embrace as a reward. My Priest Ghouled such a survivor once after she had not only proven resourceful but also managed to outwit and beat one of the participating Cainites.

      For Demon, either DtF or DD, you could mesh it up with the Mage part if you managed to imply there were forbidden texts floating about in the school, just have some bunch messing around with such spells and managing to summon a Demon that promptly Possesses one of the summoners which acts as the starting point for the Demon, again the other PC's could then play the would be summoners, now Thralls, or the first one could make them summon up a few of his friends who then possess the rest of the circle or they could even somehow manage to evoke a sort of mass summoning as they unbeknownst to them open up a limited time only open portal to the Abyss through which the future PC's escape to possess either the summoners or just random people about the school.

      The Demon part works best if the Mage part has some sort of Infernalist, or just a dabbler in the infernal as a bad guy who's grimoire the players miss, or just misplace, after getting rid of him.

              I'd consider starting with mortals for the first game - it gives a baseline introduction to the world and the system, and also being a one-shot lets you kill them off, so it also emphasises how different the supernatural characters' POV can be. The characters could also start normal and become Hunters during the adventure.

              Starting with newly Embraced vampires is a good in-character introduction to Vampire as the players learn along with their characters, but they can miss out on the fun of being immortal and powerful. I'd consider a mixed game there, with the more experienced players as vampires with a few years under their belt and the new players as neonates or as mortals that the vampires have to work with. (To balance that out, maybe in the Hunter game the new players' characters are Imbued and the experienced players play mortals?)

              The Werewolf game could connect the others together, as something supernatural from that setting dwells under the school, perhaps?

                                      Well quite sadly unless your using DD rules for Demons, and even there a newly summoned Demon can have slight identity issues, Demons without the Legacy Background in DtF identify more with the possessed mortals than they do with their identity as demons, that is without any dot's in the background they are almost as likely to think of themselves as mortals with powers rather than anything of divine origins. Thus by controlling their Legacy amounts you can decide which part of the composite being is in charge, yes it's technically the Demon the whole time but if it has no legacy it is overwhelmed by mortal memories and thinks these as his own. Demon Player's Guide has the details on levels of Legacy and the intermingling of personalities, in short no Legacy or very low Legacy mortal memories dominate ergo mortal personality dominates high legacy mortal memories start to feel like dreams and Demonic/Angelic you starts to be in charge.

                                      As for Thralls it again depends upon their Demonic masters Legacy, low legacy master will likely ask of more normal things if it ask much of anything at all high legacy master can command them to searching more names to summon others, to seek other people willing to become Thralls or otherwise suitable people for followers etc.

                                                      In DtF the demonic possession happens to individuals that are either dying or have some debilitation of the spirit and soul which means that "normal" people are usually safe from possession, though as normal the Golden Rule applies but even then there should be some chink in the mortals armour some weakness of soul even if only temporarily, like a depression and hopelessness resulting from a series of misfortunes, you were dumped, you house burned down and your close relative all dies within the month etc. In normal circumstances you could recover from that but in this case it might be enough to make you vulnerable to possession.

                                                      As for the normal possession of dying or spiritually debilitated individuals the players guide offers multiple possible answers to what happens to the human soul. When possessing the dying at the moment of death it usually results in the human soul being kicked out with only the lingering memories remaining (this is the default option), but for possessing the still living these are the option given by the demon players guide(DPG) 1) The Living Ghost meaning that the soul remains in the same body as a whispering voice, which can result in all sorts of trouble including it trying to give out false information and even it's activity is open to ST it could only be able to whisper when the demon is tired or sleeping or just high on Faith. 2) Enslave the Soul, in which case hte demon bullies hte human soul into submission and can therefore force it to give away information or even Faith at the ST discretion.

                                                      The latter option opens the door for the question could a powerful human soul do the opposite if so the DPG offers the option of Enslave the Demon in which case the human soul takes over the shared body (for an example the DPG suggest one to look at Hunter: Fall From Grace and the character of John Coaler specifically). It still leaves open the question of whether the two halves in these cases could ever come to equal terms and form a symbiosis of sort.

                                                      Finally it discusses the option of Drive Out the Soul as something of a goal for the Demon to be achieved through ritual, prayer or just rigorous debauched living and again it leaves the consequences of it to the ST. It might mean the 100% return of the Fallens memory which might result in a huge raise in Torment as well since it is the human memories that act as a sort of buffer for the character Torment.

                                                      But yes the default assumption in DtF is that the Demon possesses it's host at the time of it's death and the human soul departs on it's normal course.
