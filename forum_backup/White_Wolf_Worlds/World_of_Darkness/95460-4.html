            Personally, I've never been a big fan of crossovers. Not because of inconsistencies between the systems or the setting (some degree of suspension of disbelief is required) so much as the settings' individual flavors are often compromised when you try to combine them. I find that Vampire in particular suffers from being crossed with other setting and my personal preference isn't to have other supernatural creatures in a Vampire game at all, much less their cosmologies, aside perhaps from a rumor or legend here or there. Vampire, for me, is a game of vampire vs vampire, vampire vs mortal, and vampire vs self - throwing a bunch of werewolves or mages into that mix throws things off.

            In a Mage game, I like for the Technology to dominate much of the political landscape, having to deal with Pentex or vampires getting in the way. The same thing goes for elder vampires in V:tM and Pentex in W:tA.

            I also think that many of the deep cosmological issues don't have to be answered. When I am running a V:tM game, I don't need to decide if Caine exists or if the Wyrm created the Antediluvians - the characters are never going to know the truth of that, neither should the players, and actually the ST doesn't need to know either. I think that keeping things mysterious is the best option for both crossover and non-crossover.
