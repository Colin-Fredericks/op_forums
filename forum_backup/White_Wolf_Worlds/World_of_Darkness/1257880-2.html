    Book 2 Canto 1
    Time jump. There was more that goes along with this quest, but it's been real-world months and I decided to skip ahead to the three mini-quests. They've been gone over a week in the Dreaming, but return in barely enough time for anyone to miss them (Leland's respite care group home has noticed him missing. Leland is disappointed at the time that elapsed because he'd hoped to run away at least until his foster parents returned from Boston, where his foster mother was having a comprehensive neurology evaluation with sleep study.)

    Muirin emerges from the underworld with a Lat/Long number scribed on her arm in ash. At home, she looks it up and it's in the middle of the Gulf of Maine, pretty far away.

    Muirin asks Sir Arcite to borrow a boat. He offers a small fishing motorboat, asks where they're going. They're going way too far for that small a boat, so Muirin goes home and asks her father to use his fishing boat, a 29' lobster fishing boat. Since, so far, Muirin has a reputation of being trustworthy, I have her roll at diff 6 and she talks him into taking it out for a fun run. She's to replace any fuel she uses (which is likely to be the better part of a tank because she's going to be going a lot farther than he thinks).
    Leland skips going back to the group home and goes straight to Muirin's house. Nikki goes home, tells their parents everything, they "yes, dear" it (I should address Nikki's relationship with their parents soon) and meets them at the wharf, dressed completely inappropriately for a boat trip.

    Muirin starts following the GPS to the coordinates she was given, and it seems like it's going to be boring, so Leland uses Wayfare+Prop to speed the boat up. At over 60 knots, the boat becomes very hard to control and Muirin need full concentration to keep from capsizing it (only success on Dex+Boating was the Willpower she spent, so it's a very tentative success). Nikki notices a whale blow a rainbow into the air, but no one else sees anything unusual, and they don't follow up on it. They use the depth finder to find a spot they can drop anchor.

    The spot they find is a narrow spot of land between two dropoffs where it goes quite deep on either side. Muirin dives down and finds the wreck of an airplane resting on the sea bed. She searches for a bit and grabs some luggage in the back seat and starts to return to the surface.

    While she is down, Nikki and Leland are visited by a Canadian Coast Guard patrol boat. They are within the exclusive commercial fishing territory of Nova Scotia, so being a commercial fishing vessel got their attention. Clearly, by their age and how they're dressed, NIkki and Leland are not doing any commercial fishing, but the guardmen are confused why there are no adults aboard. They let slip that they saw a whale, and the guardsmen accuse them of harassing an endangered species and Nikki uses his bullshitting skills to stall for time. The guardsmen direct the kids to immediately go home unless they need rescue. They can't, of course, leave until Muirin gets back, which she does at exactly the moment they're near to being arrested. Not knowing what they've been talking about, she ducks back down, shapeshifts back into human form, and climbs aboard with the bag. At which point the guardsman figures out they're diving, and apologizes, then writes them a citation for having a diver down without a proper flag. Nikki, casts Dream Logic, spinning a fanciful tale about how they already paid the fine, confusing the entire ship enough for them to race away.

    In the suitcase, they find a deteriorated velvet display box with the wraith, Leonard's, service medals inside. (The medals are Fetters). She isn't sure where to return them, however.

    Next adventure is Nikki's, to resolve Julien's lost Fetter.

        Current life situations, i.e., some blue-book roleplay that hasn't made its way into these session synopses:

        Leland's foster mother, Nicole, has a recent diagnosis of multiple sclerosis. She's still figuring out what life with MS means, but she's determined not to send Leland back into the foster care system. Gary, her husband, would just as soon send him back since they have their own stuff they're dealing with. Leland is at respite care over this weekend while Nicole is in Boston getting a sleep study and having a full neurology evaluation. They decided not to bring Leland with them because of his past history of running away and getting into trouble.
        From the reverse point of view, Leland's misbehavior is a huge source of Banality to them. I'm trying to see just how far Mike, playing Leland is willing to push Nicole and Gary.

        Nikki's parents are "limousine liberals" who claim to be supportive of their gender experimentation, but are really passive-aggressive about it. They have an older sister at the nearby University, who Nikki's player describes as "sorority girl." I'm not making any direct plans for using Nikki's family in Banality, as their player seems to have a narrative going about how this affects Nikki.

        Muirin's father demands a lot of physical labor from Muirin, which she doesn't seem to mind since it puts her near the water a lot.
        Her mother is Undone, and Muirin is wearing her selkie skin. Muirin's 0 dots in Remembrance means that she has no idea what wearers of that skin have done in the past, and she has a reputation, even if she doesn't know it. If her mother gets an inkling about what she's been doing, she might flip her lid about how dangerous it is and suggest that she "grow up" and stop adventuring. Being sworn as a squire to a Gwydion knight means she cannot do that. There's an inherent conflict between her tutelage with the Gwydion and her past selkie lives that she isn't aware of yet.

            Book 2, Canto 2

            Nikki suggests going directly to Amesbury from their encounter in the Atlantic Ocean. The tide wasn't going the right way so Muirin decides not to try to take her father's boat up the river. They return to her home harbor. Refilling the boat with diesel uses up all of the remaining cash from the beanstalk gold.

            It is late Sunday afternoon. Leland returns to his foster family's house. The home phone voicemail is completely full. He deletes all the messages from the respite care home and one from the state police. Gary and Nicole return home. Nicole hugs him, cries, hug goes on for an awkwardly long time. Gary avoids eye contact, calls the home on his phone and tells them they found him. He looks for some sports on the TV. Leland chooses to do all the chores he usually refuses to do (if they'd asked, he'd probably rebel, but they didn't ask, so he's doing it). While taking out the trash, his chimerical imaginary friend walks up, flips him the bird, and wordlessly expresses his anger about being left behind at the group home. Later, while Leland is inside, he hears a crash and a car horn. The imp has thrown the trash can into the street and garbage is everywhere. Leland starts cleaning it up, Gary finds him, blames him at first, Leland says, "I was in with you. Must have been a raccoon." Gary sends him to bed. They never tell him the results of Nicole's appointment, and he doesn't ask.

            Nikki's parents barely noticed they were gone. Muirin's dad asks how fishing went. (GM question: Did she think to delete the tracking data on the boat's GPS? I think that she did not.)

            Gary drives Leland to school so they can swing by the group home and pick up his backpack with his school books. They have an awkward conversation that I was leaving to roleplay later. However, Leland is backing off on misbehaving for a bit. He decides not to ditch school.

            Muirin doesn't ditch school, usually, unless she's fishing.

            Nikki goes downtown, puts out a tip jar, and starts performing.

            In second period the Drama Club is called to the auditorium. They're asked to have a seat. The vice principal, drama director Elden (who isn't a teacher, so isn't usually at school until later), and NHLCoPTeR lady, Mary Maker are there. Elden informs them that the fall play is cancelled. Muirin texts Nikki what is going on and they leave the square for the library*. They also look up Mary Maker for more information. When the librarian finds out what they're doing, she nods and commiserates with Nikki since that library has also been a target for NHLCoPTeR censorship.
            *(The public library is only a couple blocks from the square. One thing I like about setting a game in a town familiar to all the players is the clear, shared sense of place)

            Learning the play is cancelled has a serious effect on Clover, (a new PC!!! Welcome!). For context about what happens next, Clover has Autism.
            She becomes upset by this, but suppresses a meltdown by biting her lip, which begins to bleed. They're furious, trying not to show it, and start looking up Mary Maker on the Internet on their phone to look for ways to get revenge. Leland and Muirin notice that Clover is… off, and begins radiating Glamour. They convince them to go outside. Clover starts seeing through the piskie's and selkie's masks to their true seeming and freaks out. Everyone rolls exceptional successes on Stealthing out of school and they sneak outside and out of sight of the security cameras.

            Nikki arrives in a Uber from downtown and meets the others beside the Fighting Tree. (So named because this is where students go to resolve fights after school. It's exactly far enough off campus that a fight here isn't an automatic suspension). Somehow, Clover can tell this tree loves fights. She had to learn to interpret emotions in a didactic way that many people don't need to be taught, but somehow she can read that the tree enjoys watching fighting. More of her mortal mien has peeled away and she is radiating Glamour, but she's still holding back, like she can stop this from happening. They decide to look for help (GM note: love that they're roleplaying children enough that they still consider "ask an adult for help"). They could call Mahm (the vending machine troll), but they aren't sure how long it would take for him to get there, or if he would at all. They decide that shop teacher Mr Brambleweft is closer. They sneak around the other side of the building and Leland lets them in the back door with the keys he stole back at the beginning of the campaign.

            Brambleweft's class is doing woodworking, so the planer, bandsaw, and tablesaw are all on when they arrive. Clover passes a Willpower check to function at all in this environment, but it's clearly a challenge. Muirin finds noise blocking headphones in the cabinet and they go inside. Brambleweft, a nocker, tells them to sit in the drafting lab. Nockers aren't terribly well-suited to being kind and supportive, but he exudes a little tough love and Clover gives in to shedding her Chrysalis. An explosion of Glamour occurs and an Autumn Sidhe emerges. They decide it's time to ditch school and go to see the Count.

            They are greeted at the castle by a knight guard, who admits them. Lady Braith, wife of the Count sees them first. Clover, without knowing how she knows, says exactly the right words asking for her welcome to the Court. She fetches Count Mathonwy, who greets her with all the formalities, but he takes leave as soon as he can without being rude. Lady Braith begins to talk about making arrangements for Clover to stay at the castle and begin her fosterage. Clover bristles at the idea of being tied down and forced to stay in the Court all the time, and politely asks leave. "We've already lost one fledgling to the Autumn world (referring to D'arcy, so also refuses to live in the Freehold), we'd hate to lose another." This is a serious trigger for Clover, who has been locked up for her own protection many different ways in many different lifetimes. Lady Braith lets her go, but commits to talking about it later.

            That night, Leland and Nikki have nightmares about being visited by ghosts. Muirin wakes up with message scratched on her body in ash. It's the tail number from the plane she found on the bottom of the ocean.

                        Book 2, Canto 3
                        Scene 1
                        Nikki talks his mother into driving them down to Newburyport, Massachusetts (a highly gentrified, seaside artsy community) so they can do some street performing. From there, they hitch a ride one town over to Kurt's house, to find their next wraith Fetter. Outside of the house are a couple of self-storage pods, and a For Sale Open House signs (decorated with balloons). They barge in and the realtor, ingratiating autumn person Edie, greets them (assuming they're with some adults). Leland eats some cookies.
                        Scene 2
                        They take the tour of the house with Edie, looking for any clue for what the wraith told them to look for. They notice that the bedroom closet is chimerically darker than normal, and has evidence there had been some sort of monster nest there long ago, but it looked abandoned. They ask some questions and eventually Edie tires of them and says they could ask the previous resident of the house. Outside, they see a teen boy rummaging around in the storage unit. Nikki approaches him and asks if he's Kurt, which he confirms. They have some shallow chat about video games.
                        Scene 3
                        Inside the house, Muirin ventures to the basement and finds that The Floor Is Lava. Really. The entire basement floor is a lake of molten lava. She fetches the others and they try throwing a couch cushion onto the floor to make an island. The cushion catches on fire long before it reaches the floor and fills the air with acrid, burning-plastic chimerical smoke.
                        Nikki realizes that the furniture in this house doesn't belong to the house—it's all rented by the realtor for the showing. Muirin suggests they enlist Kurt, who they invest some of their personal Glamour into. He remembers the lava floor now, and helps them into the storage units for couch cushions they can use.
                        Scene 4
                        They make islands and take some very impactful jumps between them to get to the countertop next to the laundry machines. Halfway across, they are interrupted as Edie brings a couple of prospective buyers to the basement and sees them climbing on the counter or balancing on couch cushions on the floor. Of course, they walk directly across the lava, oblivious. "What are you doing? Kurt, you're not supposed to be in here." Kurt fails his Banality resist and drops his Enchantment, apologizes and leaves, walking across the lava. Edie threatens to call the police on the others, but Clover insists, with a Sovereign cantrip, that they be allowed to stay because the floor is lava.
                        Edie, not sure how to respond to this, informs the buyers that the basement floor has radiant heating.
                        Nikki drops behind the sink into a crawlspace where he finds some toys and a flashlight. He takes the flashlight and they leave through the back door (leaving the cushions) and return the flashlight to Kurt, who doesn't remember using it to chase monsters away.

                            Book 2, Canto 4
                            Scene 0: Kurt asks sidhe Clover if they might come back sometime. They give him their phone number.
                            Clover's parents text and they return a text with photos from historic downtown Newburyport to keep up the alibi.

                            The kids decide since they're so close, they'll try looking up the girl that Leland's wraith sent him to help, Maggie. They take an Uber to another small, seaside town.

                            Scene 1: With no real leads, they decide to go to get ice cream. The DQ is open and they ask the teen taking their order if she knows Maggie. Not very well, but she directs them to one of Maggie's friends.

                            Scene 2: On the way to downtown, they see people going into the high school. Looks like members of the a sports team going to a weekend practice. Clover thinks that maybe one of the team looks like someone from Maggie's social media photos. They decide to go try to talk to him.

                            Scene 3: Nikki turns into a cat and sneaks in the school through the open door. The others come in with the student athletes and pretend to look for him. The students are warming up for a basketball practice. Nikki hides under the bleachers. Clover asks the athlete to help look for their cat. He doesn't believe her, but comes over anyway because… well teen boys respond to sidhe with Appearance 6. Leland strikes up a conversation with him about Maggie. He reports that her boyfriend, Mark, is "playing at the party tonight." When Leland presses him, he realizes that Leland is 11 and isn't going to the party and he clams up. Clover continues to engage him in conversation for a while. The basketball coach yells at Leland and Clover to stay off the gym floor with street shoes.

                            Meanwhile, under the bleachers, NIkki becomes aware of another animal under there. It's a chimerical capuchin monkey. It bares its teeth at them, with its hair standing up. Nikki tries to play it chill, but the monkey attacks. Nikki flees.

                            From the perspective of everyone in the gym, they see a housecat bolt out from under the bleachers across the gym floor. The changelings see a monkey. Clover commands the monkey "STOP!" using Sovereign, and Nikki gets enough distance to get away, jumps into Leland's arms. Muirin scoops the monkey up in her jacket and it resists, viciously. As they go to leave, the boy gives Clover a slip of paper with an address. She asks, "What time?" "Eight? Nine?" They leave.

                            Scene 4: Outside of the school, the monkey is increasingly difficult to keep wrapped up in the jacket. Leland and Nikki wonder what kind of chimera it is, while most of them are confused by its ferocity, Leland crushes a Kenning roll and realizes that this is a "monkey from off someone's back."
                            It's only a matter of time before Muirin's wrestling fails and it can bite her. She yeets the monkey onto the ground away from her and it takes off running. Leland uses some sweatbands he stole from the gym to make himself really fast (Wayfare-Quicksilver), Nikki is able to keep up with its speed in cat form, and Muirin runs, only to keep her friends in sight. Clover walks.

                            The monkey runs to a grocery store and immediately sneaks inside. Nikki returns to human form, they follow it inside and see the monkey on the shoulder of a bagger—it's Maggie's boyfriend, Mark.

                                    Book 2, Canto 5:
                                    Scene 1
                                    They reunite behind the store. Only Leland saw Mark with drugs.
                                    Leland has new sunglasses due to his piskie shoplifting habit.
                                    They kill some time before the party. Nikki flat out tells their parents "I'm going to a party" and that is good enough. Muirin says she's with Nikki and her parents accept this. Leland just makes an excuse and says he isn't coming home. Clover tells her mother they are going to the theater, playing up how Drama Club has been cancelled and how much she misses it. She successfully manipulates her mom into allowing this despite it keeping her out way past curfew. (The play is "Venus in Furs" and I had Clover's mother secretly roll an Intelligence+Academics roll to see if she was aware of what that play is about and if she did, she'd probably not allow it. She rolled all 4s, so no, she doesn't know how inappropriate it is. Yet. This is a bad alibi, though).

                                    Scene 2
                                    The house is already crowded when they arrive and they can hear the beat of music all the way down the driveway. Leland, suddenly self-conscious about being too young, goes around the side for Clover or Nikki to let him in. Muirin goes with him. Clover goes inside and it's dark with colorful flashing lights, crowded, and very loud. This is complete hell on her autistic senses and she spends Willpower to remain in control of her faculties. She goes through the house to the kitchen, where it is slightly farther away from the larger speakers. Darnell, the basketball player who invited them sees Clover. She turns down a drink by inviting him to dance. Some girls, not sure what her deal is, approach. Clover, realizing that she's really out of control in this situation casts Grandeur, smashing a lamp and going directly to the center of the floor to dance. (Three successes).

                                    Outside, Muirin and Leland find a tall fence around a pool and patio. Muirin scales the fence unnecessarily and makes a perfect parkour jump into the yard. Leland uses the gate. A satyr sits beside the pool smoking from a hookah. He greets them, then whispers "I don't know who they are" to his friends. There appears to be a No Smoking Inside rule that people are sticking to, so about a half dozen people are out here. Three stoners are trying to get a fire in the fire pit started but aren't having much success.
