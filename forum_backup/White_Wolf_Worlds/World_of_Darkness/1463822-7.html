                            I think we have a blueprint for Hunter 2nd edition in Vampire: The Masquerade — Out for Blood. You play a group of mortals who learns about the existence of vampires in a small town and needs to decide how to deal with them. You can meet a seasoned hunter AND the second inquisition (right before the end of the game). The SI tries to use you as bait for vampires while the hunter helps you fight the vampires but then wants you to help with the "war" (which is as desperate as it gets). I think it shows nicely the differences in tone and scope between the various hunter groups. The SI is "the Man" and is not the friend of unalligned hunters, at best it uses them to lure supernaturals in the open. At worst it considers the a liability and deals with them accordingly.
