                                                The only problem with VTM is the combat and XP effecient character building.

                                                For the latter problem, you just need to offer Flat XP rates for Attributes/Abilities/Virtues and then give people a discipline array (2/1/0) because discplines work nicely as X-level.
                                                Maybe Composure is a better attribute than Appearance, and I kinda like willpower being a derived stat, though I'd do it between resolve and courage (Or courage+Conviction if you've seen my Conscience+conviction idea)

                                                For the former, don't roll damage or soak for just take half the dicepool (This obviously doesn't apply to things like fire) and do multiple actions all at once (and decrease the number available) and get rid of the 'declare actions phase and just do stuff.
                                                There's lots of little things to change regarding disciplines and such, but that's the core of the issue.


                                                As for V5's metaplot...

                                                On one hand, A good deal of the metaplot can make sense if you think enough about it. The Camarilla downsizing and then following my economic plan to become the best sect ever would be a good long term strat even if not great in the short term. The Tremere putting up a facade of disunity to avoid looking like a dangerous monopoly that needs to be clipped back is a near perfect plan. The Settites joining the Anarchs to take control of it could work wonders.

                                                The SI only kinda works if you assume it's just elders directing a neonate cull and the organizations aren't actually that connected. It doesn't work at all if you look at it as-written, it's logistically impossible.

                                                The Gehenna war only works if it's a lie. Let's say the Sabbat elders saw what the Camarilla was doing and liked it, so they sent their angry neonates off to explode and quietly joined the Cam (On the condition that they can worship openly, which the Cam conceeded) whilst the Elders disapearing is just them avoiding all the cullings and the angry neonates who are responding. The angry sabbat survivors will someday come back with a vengance.

                                                The Hecatta doesn't work at all. Sure, you can organize the coup, but after than you should just join the Camarilla not... join to become a singular bloodline.

                                                So like, if you assume I'm right here and maybe we've got some misinformation about the Hecata subsect, it can kinda work well. But now the big problem is that sectarian conflict now just looks like Democrats vs Republicans. The hugely asymetrical nature of what came before still works better. I want to be Oppressed and fight it, that's what I play vampire for. Democrats vs Republicans is not as interesting conflict.

                                                Like, there's still a chance for V5's storyline to be awesome, but I don't have high hopes because it looks like they'll go a different way (Even moreso democrats VS republicans but somehow vampires are the little guys that fear big goverment.)

                                                The rules are bad tho. The good things I have to say about the rules are like positive nitpicks rather than something worth talking about. I suppose the very abstract combat system is fast if you're into that. Maybe we can move forward with some of V5's metaplot and more VTM rules.
