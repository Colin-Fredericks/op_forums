                                                                  This is a complicated question, not only because there is the issue of how you change our standard history, but how you change supernatural history to go along with it in each of the game lines. The big three game lines are so different that if I were going to make a Nazis won WWII setting, I would do it differently for Vampire, Werewolf, and Mage. If you're into a combined WoD setting you may not like that option. I don't know if realism should be the biggest concern here, if the world were realistic, look at a history book. In making an alternate history, some degree of realism may be needed, but you should also do what you think would be fun.

                                                                  Firstly, how WWII turns out. I think that instead of having the entire western world controlled by the Nazis, I would have fascism control mainland Europe only, with the USA along with the UK and the rest of the Anglosphere free from fascist control. Then we can add a twist to those Nazi free areas, for example maybe the USA is more leftist than in the real world, at least in some regards. A huge numbers of communists and sympathizers Europe as it was conquered by Germany and came to the USA. The US and Britain (and Canada, Australia, etc.) have been in a Cold War of sorts with Nazi Europe since the war ended, perhaps in the 1950's, and one of the points of interest in the setting is espionage, just like the Cold War.

                                                                  Vampires get along as they always have. Ultimately, they care less about the specifics of human politics and more about how they can exploit things. Most important events in the world of V:tM happened before WWII anyway, so you could basically just adapt vampiric life to the details of the new setting and have that be the end of it. You could make an alternative history of vampires to go along with the world's alternative history - the Lasombra and Tzimisce dominate the Camarilla instead of the Sabbat. Lasombra use the changing politics of Europe to push their social Darwinist ideology and the Tzimisce Nazis conduct experiments as they please. Of course, changes like that would have originate way before WWII. For practical concerns, one major one is that non-white vampires in Europe would have a harder time, maybe more like they would have historically than a game set in the modern era. You could make as many changes as you like or as few. Maybe in this history, electronics aren't quite as advanced and not everybody has a cell phone with a camera, that might take a little pressure off of masquerade concerns.

                                                                  In Mage you could go a number of directions. Two big ones I can imagine is that either the Technocracy stood with fascism through the war and helped them to achieve victory, or we have a setting where the Technocracy isn't as powerful. I think that perhaps the later may be more interesting. During WWII, the Technocracy eventually decided to oppose fascism, but were under heavy attacks from the Traditions and Nephandi. The Technocracy lost much of their domination over reality and so we have a more divided consensus, a more mystical world in a sense. Maybe certain weird elements of Nazi paganism are widely popular and as a result work as part of the consensus. Perhaps weird unconventional forms of science are more effective, death rays, mad scientists, and Sons of Ether kind of stuff.

                                                                  As for Werewolf, I'm not sure. I don't know how different the state of the environment would be or how much most werewolves would care about the different political landscape. Maybe things haven't changed much at all. I'd have to give it more thought.
