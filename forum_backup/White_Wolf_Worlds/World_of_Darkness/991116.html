    Building a Modern Standard of Living from nothing.

    10-21-2016, 05:28 PM
    Suppose a mage was dropped into a vast empty space. The ground is paper white. So is the sky. The two seem to blend into one another in the distance. Perhaps it's a paradox realm, perhaps not. Who knows.

    What skills/spheres would they need to construct a modern standard of living?

    Presuming that....

    A.) This place has no node.
    B.) This place has no raw materials, the ground can be dug away, but it fades away into nothingness upon doing so.
    C.) This place has no strong Resonance of any kind.
    D.) This place experiences a night and day cycle much like any other.
    E.) The Mage has the minimum requisite tools to work their magic. Whatever those might be.

      With Mind, Prime, and Matter you could just turn all thoughts into physical manifestations of reality.
      You could use Heart's Blood to get Quintessence out of your very own body, so as long as you don't mind waiting an hour or three between making things, you can in theory make everything you imagine real.

      Life would be useful for food, or at the very least to get around needing food.

      That's about it.
      Time could be useful if you're impatient and want your body to heal faster.
      Corr. is pretty much useless unless you start building so big that you need it to keep track of everything.
      Entropy doesn't have much function here,
      Forces would only really be necessary if you wanted to have electricity running, but nothing stops you from just creating a battery, wires, and devices with matter alone given enough time.
      Spirit would be outright pointless unless there were spirits in the area or the world was made of ephemera.
      If so, then all you need is Spirit to turn the world into whatever the heck you want.

          Heart's Blood deals lethal damage, so you would need a day between points of Quintessence (unless they change healing rates dramatically in M20), and it cannot be accelerated by healing magic. We will assume that you have air, or else you will have a very, very short stay.

          The first problem is water. If you are physical, you need water, so you need Matter 2 and Prime 2, just to create the water that you need to survive (as well as a container to hold any water you create). The second problem is shelter, which can also be addressed with Matter 2 and Prime 2. The third problem is food, which requires Life 2 and Prime 2 to make edible materials. The fourth problem is sanitation, which can be addressed with Forces 2 and Prime 2 (or with Matter 2 and Prime 2 if you have Science or Survival). The fifth problem is warmth, which can be addressed with Forces 2 and Prime 2 (or Matter 2 and Prime 2 if you have Science or Survival).

          So, your character would need a minimum of Arete 2, Life 2, Matter 2, Prime 2, and Science 2 or Survival 2, all of which are doable for beginning characters. With careful planning, and enough time, you could create yourself a nice little garden in the middle of nowhere. You would not have any modern conveniences, and you would probably go mad from social isolation, but you would be able to survive until you died of old age, alone until the day you die. Strangely enough, it would be a fitting Paradox Realm for someone who had gained Paradox from immortality, so they would have sufficient magic to keep themselves alive, they would just never be able to leave the Paradox Realm until they permanently sacrificed their immortality.
