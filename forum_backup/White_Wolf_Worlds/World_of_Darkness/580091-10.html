                                                        Once they're fully cleaned and pruned of duplicates etc, that's the plan. As it stands I do still need to add some of the novels and W20 content so my work is not yet done. I have another 30,000 data entries to sort through and clean as well, but the end result should be a listing of at least 30 - 40,000 unique supernatural creatures, organizations, and WoD exclusive companies like Pentex (I recorded unidentified vampires etc even with no unique characteristics, for the sake of completeness, so the figure of named creatures will be lower and of creatures with actual features lower still - so maybe 20,000 of those) and a timeline of at least 2700 unique events.

                                                        The plan is to assemble the single most complete database of the oWoD ever assembled and from there establish statistical patterns of clan representation, tribes, houses, how many of each critter live where, and even ratio of representation of gender and nationality (not ethnicity except where it is very unusual, though, as by the time I thought to include that it was rather late in the game. If people pay me I can add it but the amount of manhours involved is insane.) in the published material, along with absolute minimum population ratios.

                                                        For an example of that last one, I ran through the Hunter file the other day. There is an absolute minimum of 1 Imbued (including Bystanders) for every 6 million people if we take the global figure, and 1:1.3mil if we extrapolate from the disproportionate American weighting of the Imbued population. Those figures are far too low to reflect the actual game populations, but nonetheless form a bare minimum baseline that can be compared against other kinds and used as a benchmark for more representative populations. More useful is stuff like how many hunters are dead (15% or so) in the listing, how many are male or female (40% male, 18% male, with 42% unknown or ambiguous), etc.
