                  there was western kuei-jin,
                  there's mention of kuei-jin established in the Americas that predate the Sabbat, and possibly even predate the Tlacique

                  unfortunately, they were destroyed during the war between the Sabbat and the nahualli,
                  as the Sabbat didn't know about the kuei-jin, they assumed they were nahualli
                  there may have been survivors but who knows
                  it's only in the modern nights that the sabbat learned enough about kuei-jin that they realized some of the nahualli may have been kuei-jin themselves

                  on top of that, kuei-jin expansion is harder than cainite expansion,
                  even if a yomi domain were to expend in the west (as is the case in modern nights with mikaboshi's domain)
                  the resulting kuei-jin wouldn't go past the chih mei stage (equivalent of wights)

                  Asian kuei-jin managed to make it past the chih mei stage thanks to Xue, who was active around China,
                  and even then it took a long time for more kuei-jin to expand to the rest of Asia, Oceania and the middle east,

                  europe is even more challenging, just look at how much trouble cainites had,
                  for example, the only reason the Tzimisce managed to settle east europe was thanks to their antideluvian and the help of Kupala

                              San Francisco by Night p.131 has the rules for playing a Kanbujian, Kuei-Jin who took the second breath outside of the Middle Kingdom and who has no knowledge of the Dharma’s and as such cannot take one up but must create his/her own Path.
