    @Killersquirrel59
    Even if one would choose to ignore Bedlam altogether and travel to the Dreaming would be made easier I would still have to disagree about people running to the Dreaming to escape their problems. In general who in their right mind would try to escape problems into a land comprised of Dreams and Nightmares? What real world problem would be so great as to risk Dragons, giant spider's, Zombie outbreaks and what ever else people have dreams and nightmares about?
    Also despite Dreaming being just as dangerous as the Umbra it is actually, as mentioned earlier, harder to get to yet I have never heard of Werewolf being scolded about letting the protagonists escape their problems into the Umbra.
    Final reason for why it is a bad idea to escape your problems into the Dreaming is that you can barely remember what happened in there after you come back into the Autumn World as the Mists affect even Changelings there.
    All in all I really have trouble understanding your position on the Dreaming. It may, admittedly, just be some kind of misunderstanding here or just me being unable to see the forest for the trees as I'm standing in it but still I find claims of Dreaming being an escape hatch away from your problems to be rather absurd idea. To me it is if anything a source of more problems and naturally story hooks. Just like Umbra in WtA.

    In general I do hope that C20 does happen and I do hope that they do not remove the Kiths, Thallain, Adhene and the rest in favor of the custom system, although I wouldn't mind some sort of custom system existing in an appendix or in a C20 ST Companion type of book as an option for those who want such a system. Mostly I think the things that deserve to be changed are connected to the Glamour, Banality and Magic systems. Like mentioned by the Pooka earlier Glamour generation, and the size of the pool being limited by your Permanent Glamour, are way too meager compared to how much an average Changeling needs to be throwing Glamour around. One could without further problems drop Glamour Costs from Birthrights that have them as you already need to use Glamour to either call upon the Wyrd or to Enchant anyone not of the dreaming and amongst each other they are balanced enough. Just why does changing form Cost Glamour for a Pooka and is free for Shapechangers in general considering how limited, and generally, non combative Pooka changing is for example.

    So like mentioned Glamour costs and where they are needed should be reconsidered as well as the ways of regaining it. PookaKnight gave nice options and ideas already, few more and we'd be there. Also I would like to rip Glamour itself apart a bit dividing it into Bright, Seelie, Glamour and Dark, Unseelie, Glamour this would tie into both the magic system and the Courts but that's a discussion for another time. But speaking of the Magic system I kinda like PookaKnights idea about somewhat costlier Calling Upon the Wyrd which would last a scene and make all Cantrips Wyrd without extra costs. Also while I do like the idea behind the magic system, verb and a noun basically, I do think the way it's been implemented to be kinda awkward and clunky in a way that it really does unfortunately loose the Fae quality of it. I would prefer a somewhat more chaotic and wild magic system, I like the magic system of DA:Fae (and non-DA Mage's as well) but it is likely a bit powerful for a modern day changeling chronicle, besides here is where I would like to see a difference between Dreaming Glamour and Nightmare Glamour in action so to speak. Seelie and Unseelie Magic just should feel different to me despite being basically the same.

    For myself CtD has never lacked Darkness, which has been a complain of some, though admittedly the art in the 2nd ed Core hides it a bit from a casual observer but still some of my darkest themed games have involved Changelings as beings of Nightmare made flesh nothing quite compares to them in the darkness scale. In addition to the Darkness of Nightmares CtD has the death of imagination, invention, innocence and joy, whether coming from horrible or pleasant deeds and experiences,as it's core theme which is pretty damn dark to begin with.

    Sorry about the slight rambling.

                                                        I'm not sure the limited nature of Kiths is my biggest complaint with CtD. It is my biggest complaint that isn't a matter of (a) rules that don't do what they were supposed to or (b) things that don't have support in the system.

                                                        Fixing them without going full flexible is actually pretty easy: Take the current Birthrights and Frailties and turn them into defining traits that can be either a Birthright or a Frailty. So if you want a Pooka that cuts out the lying, you can have a Frailty based more on the animal parts. So you define each Kith with four "themes" and each has an attached Birthright and Frailty. Pick two Birthrights and two Frailties from your options. The Kiths are still pretty tight thematically, but you get away from such strong stereotypes.
