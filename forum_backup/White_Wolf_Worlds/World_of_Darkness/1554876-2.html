                                                    07-07-2026, 06:24 PM
                                                    A couple more comments:
                                                    • Per p. 95 of CbARev, Jamal took the Black Throne in 1497. Izhim and his followers left two weeks after that, putting their exodus firmly in 1497. Elsewhere, the book says he ruled from 1494, but that's obviously a mistake as he diablerises the Amr in 1496 before taking the Black Throne.
                                                    • Caine's Chosen merely says Izhim and Djuhah join the Black Hand after the Treaty of Tyre and before the lost colony at Roanoke was founded (1587). Putting a date span might be more accurate. With what we know about their exodus, 1497–1587 makes sense.
                                                    • Also, we technically don't know how soon after they joined the Sabbat that it was before Vestgeir asked them to sever contact with their main clan. You could just soften the word ("Some time after...", etc.).
                                                    • I can't find a particular date for Fariq's defection, but it's said he didn't go to the Convention of Thorns and was "immediately" branded antitribu by his clanmates (TC3, p. 80). Now, this could mean immediately after the Convention (1493) or, more likely, immediately after the Treaty of Tyre (1496). So 1496 makes a better date for that, but "after 1496" would also work.
                                                    • Re: Karsh/Jalan-Aajav/Qarakh, the last of these is never actually confirmed to be 4th Generation as far as I've been able to confirm. He diablerised a Methuselah but was only 7th Generation at the time (and that's after diablerising his sire). In all likelihood, he would've been 5th or 6th Generation afterwards, not 4th. It's therefore more likely that it's just the same person flitting between the two sects (Jalan-Aajav in particular seems to vanish for large periods of time), or there's another solution. Not really a timeline thing, but worth bearing in mind.
