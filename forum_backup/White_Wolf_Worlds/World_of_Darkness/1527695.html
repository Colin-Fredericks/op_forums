    Suggested setting changes to make crossover easier

    01-01-2024, 12:34 PM

    Suggested setting changes to make crossover easier (and other crossover thoughts)


    It occurs to me, that of the Big Five of the Word. Wraiths, Changelings,Werewolves and Mages can be in a crossover and it's mostly ok. At least setting wise. Vampires are a sticking point, because the other 4 all have reasons to suspect them. Here's some suggested setting changes to make it easier to make the Big Five of the Word interact more easily.



    Vampire and Werewolf


    The Gift Sense Wyrm now only sense things that are related to the actual Wyrm. Things that are only kinda related to it don't ping it. That means that Vampires are not automatically hated by the Garou. It's up to each Garou to decide what to do with the bloodsuckers. That means, that yes a vampire can be an ally of the pack or sept. There have been alliances before. They might still be enemies, but if a vampire is hated by the Garou it's because of the morality of their actions and not because of what they are. The spirits feel the same way.

    Abominations are not quite as abominable, but still discouraged.


    Vampire and Mage changes


    The enmity between vampires and mages is mostly political. The tremere betrayed the houses of Hermes. Stole their secrets, that left a huge scar.

    However. It has been more than 600 years since the fight happened, that's a long time. And some Tremere and Mages wonder, isn't time to let the past go? After all the Nephandus want to destroy the whole universe. Shouldn't we fight the Nephandi instead of each other? Under flag a truce was signed. Vampires and Mages are supposed to keep their mittens of each other secrets and not fight each other without provocation.

    The truce also meant that the underground groups of vampire and mages allies cane into the light. Groups made of members of both splats are not much common

    One side note. The vampires know about the other worlds, all the umbras and the dreaming. They know it all exits. After all vampires have had mystics in their ranks for at least five millenia



    Vampires and Changelings changes


    Changelings looooooooove the anarchs. Few things are quite as glamour filled as revolutions. And through the Anarchs, the changelings have discovered your average vampire is honestly not that Banal. The nightlife is exciting!


    Vampires and wraiths remain unchanged

      Vampires don't even trust each other. And for good reason. If you want them to be better received by other things that go bump in the night, you'd have to fundamentally make them into different beings that aren't a net negative on the world.

      Vampire and Werewolf


      The Gift Sense Wyrm now only sense things that are related to the actual Wyrm.
      The Wyrm is a third of reality, so good luck only being "kind of" it. At any rate, Sense Wyrm only discerned the residue of corruption, which is so easy to have stick to you that there were multiple sidebars smacking people over the head with titles like "it's not detect evil!" and a rite for getting rid of it.

      When it came to vampires, though, that's like sweeping dirt around.

      Things that are only kinda related to it don't ping it. That means that Vampires are not automatically hated by the Garou.
      This is major league putting the cart before the horse. Garou and Fera don't typically bother to use any kind of Triatic sense on vampires because the vampires are unapologetically horrible people and the world would be better off without them. There wouldn't be any question of whether or not they're corrupt by nature (big yes! Hello, Eater of Souls!) because their actions are in every way supportive of it. No one is going to believe for a damned minute that they should try to be anything like a friend or even an ally of convenience with vampires.

      It's up to each Garou to decide what to do with the bloodsuckers. That means, that yes a vampire can be an ally of the pack or sept. There have been alliances before. They might still be enemies, but if a vampire is hated by the Garou it's because of the morality of their actions and not because of what they are. The spirits feel the same way.
      This changes little to nothing. Again, because vampires are so incredibly shitty and disdain everything having to do with spiritual life (they think that Garou are "just religious zealots", after all), they're pretty walking sinks for corruption, one and all. Actions have consequences. For the Garou, that's a series of blunders leading to their being in a situation like that one Atreides mission in the original Dune 2 RTS that's pretty much impossible to win without a hex editor. For vampires, it's being constantly terrible and always leaving the world worse than when they found it, at best.

      Abominations are not quite as abominable, but still discouraged.
      Given the above, that's not a reasonable cut back. Vampire Garou have basically just lost all their redeeming characteristics, and will slowly lose all of their Gnosis until they're utterly lost to corruption.

      This isn't even a torturous interpretation of Vampire itself. No one is down on Vampire like Vampire's own lore.

      Vampire and Mage changes


      The enmity between vampires and mages is mostly political. The tremere betrayed the houses of Hermes. Stole their secrets, that left a huge scar.

      However. It has been more than 600 years since the fight happened, that's a long time. And some Tremere and Mages wonder, isn't time to let the past go? After all the Nephandus want to destroy the whole universe. Shouldn't we fight the Nephandi instead of each other? Under flag a truce was signed. Vampires and Mages are supposed to keep their mittens of each other secrets and not fight each other without provocation.

      The truce also meant that the underground groups of vampire and mages allies cane into the light. Groups made of members of both splats are not much common

      One side note. The vampires know about the other worlds, all the umbras and the dreaming. They know it all exits. After all vampires have had mystics in their ranks for at least five millenia
      They wouldn't have access to most of it and little in the way of learning about it second hand. Auspex and Necromancy only give them access to the Shadowlands and maybe the Astral. They have nothing that gives them access to the Middle Umbra, and again, are typically so up their own asses being drains on the world that this is something that they couldn't really have. Even if you do put a vampire in the Middle Umbra, incidentally, the ambient environment tends to have unremitting sunlight that would burn them wherever they tried to hide, especially if they were big enough dunces to try going to the Aetherial Realm. The Dreaming would probably be no more hospitable, since the vampires would have to hang out in the cold, dark, nightmarish places where there's never sunlight, or else, again, probably burnt to a crisp.

      Vampires and wraiths remain unchanged
      So, a relationship that makes the interactions of Dr. Forrester and TV's Frank look healthy and life-affirming? (^VVVVVV^)

                      The problem is, you're trying to change other splats, not what vampires do as a collective that will then inform the biases of the other groups.

                      The average vampire is Humanity 4. That means the average vampire is doing lots of things of a morally dubious nature at best. Even if it might be hypocritical considering what plenty of members of the other splats get up to:

                      V20 page 312:

                      Humanity 5-6 - "Though not constantly horrid, Kindred at this stage of Humanity are certainly at least mildly unpleasant to be around. Their laissez-faire attitudes toward others’ rights offend many more moral individuals."

                      Humanity 4 - "Destruction, theft, injury — these are all tools, rather than taboos, for a vampire with Humanity 4. The vampire’s own agenda becomes paramount at this point, and screw whoever gets in the way."
                      ​
                      So most vampires? They've done enough stuff that, "OK but what has the vampire done?" will be satisfied. The vampires that haven't crossed that line are the exception, not the rule. That's why the other splats default to hating them. Not things like Sense Wyrm.

                      If you want changes that make vampires more functional in crossover play, you need to start with vampires themselves.

                                      I think you're kinda missing the point of the JoJo reference.

                                      First, the default of VtM is that being a vampire is a curse. It has plenty of perks, but ultimately it drags them down. Low Humanity turns them into selfish sociopaths. Their societies are screwed up because they're run by older vampires, who tend to have lower Humanity because keeping up Humanity over time is in direction opposition to gaining power and control in vampire society.

                                      Second, yes, drinking blood is a problem. Let's say you have a really careful vampire that tries as hard as possible to be safe. They only accidentally kill one human per 100 vitae they consume. That's a pretty good record. That's still 3-4 dead humans a year just for the basic nightly blood cost to wake up. A character that's been a vampire for ~50 years (stated as being within starting range in most editions) has killed at least 150 people already... just from feeding accidents for basic maintenance feeding. There's going to be a point where any attempt to say that they're still a good person is going to ring morally hollow. How many accidental deaths is one vampire really worth over the potential of living for thousands of years? What moral calculus do you make that doesn't end with "it's better to be rid of vampires when possible?"

                                      And sure, crossover is cool and fun. There are other games out there that do monster mash games way better than the WoD does, because that's fun.
