                                At this point, AkatsukiLeader13, do you (or does anyone else) have any particular questions about Changeling?

                                Originally posted by Lian View Post

                                Not specifically, but how do you think the High and Middle Umbra act in relation to Sports Seasons?
                                Fair point . . . but that's the Umbra. Across the Gauntlet. There's no such barrier between the Dreaming and Earth, separating humans and chimera (the spirits of the Dreaming). Umbral spirits might play their own version of baseball, in the spiritual reflection of a stadium. But the baseball gnomes are actually there, at Wrigley Field or Shea Stadium. Hocking t-shirts, swearing at the refs, rubbing elbows with the humans* . . .

                                So while the humans at BronyCon are snapping up souvenirs, and admiring the cosplay, the changeling attendees are taking selfies with Rainbow Dash and singing karaoke with the Sea Ponies. And there's no Paradox issue to trouble crossovers. A nocker can build a chimerical version of the Gundam Heavy Arms (giant anime mecha with machine gun arms), then use it to blow away a zombie horde in a Walking Dead-themed area of the Dreaming, while the dragon riders of Pern battle the (Wizard of Oz's) flying monkeys for control of Portland's skies.

                                Such wild crossovers aren't mandatory . . . but they're an option here, in a way that they're not in Mage.




                                *this, of course, assumes the baseball gnomes are human-sized. Tiny gnomes that resemble baseballs, and who find it great fun to be thrown by pitchers, and sent flying by the batter, are also viable.
