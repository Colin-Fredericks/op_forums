                                The fae of the Dreaming are basically like bygones. Except, while most bygones went into the Umbra, the fae carved out a niche for themselves in the dreams of mankind, which allowed them to remain closer to the Autumn World as a result.

                                Changelings are those fae who didn't retreat fully to the Dreaming, but exploited the symbiotic relationship between the Autumn World and the Dreaming to enact the Changeling Way.

                                Because the Dreaming is tied so closely to human dreams, beliefs and emotions, so too are the kithain as a consequence. They traded fluid being for stricter 'roles' in the collective unconscious of humanity, because it nourished them via Dreams in return.
