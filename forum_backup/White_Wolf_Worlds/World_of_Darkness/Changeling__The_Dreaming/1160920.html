    Asking help in translating Dreaming concepts to Lost

    11-20-2017, 02:21 PM
    So, I finally bit the bullet and got the pdf of C20, and I must say: WHERE WERE YOU IN THE ORIGINAL CWOD!? WHERE!?

    To put it simply, I like it, whereas before, the first Dreaming book I read (and threw against the wall) was The Autumn People. However, I am primarily a Lost fan, so I'm more likely to translate things from Dreaming to Lost.

    Which is why I'm here, asking for more experienced help when fishing for suggestions.

    Off the top of my head:

    * Lost changelings and Kithain both exist and are often grudging allies. The Lost have an understandable suspicion of everything originally Arcadian, while Kithain are weirded out by how Lost are Banality-tolerant (and in fact Banality is largely responsible for Lost freedom, as it helped restore their minds after being tormented by Fomorians), but both recognize the other has a right to live, and they both hate Thallain.

    * Huntsmen are adhene who were brainwashed by the Fomorians by having their heart removed and one of their Legacies replaced. This caused them to lose a lot of their distinction from each other, as they're all partially hybrids between themselves and the same type of primal fae.They know what they are, and they hate it-restoring their heart is likely to earn a friend for life, though you have to find it first.

    * Bridge-burners sponsor the Bellrophon Institute and are secretly who conviced Anton Stark Dreamers were suffering the same mental disorder as part of their quest to ruin Earth so badly the Fomorians lose interest.

      Some Ideas

      The Hedge is the border to the Silver gate and The Lost could be the kithain's key to returning to Arcadia because they aren't locked out.

      Dauntain are what happens when a Keeper takes an unawakened Kithain. They are Kithain's fetches. OR Fetches are kithain.

      Hobgoblin=Chimera obviously.

      Goblin fruit could be a special type of natural dross that Kithain can harvest

      If you are having Lost track Banality, strengthening the mask could be a trigger.

      In many ways the Lost and the Kithain are inversions of eachother. The Lost are humans that have to struggle wth becoming more fae, while the Kithain are far that have to struggle with becoming more human. That makes for an interesting story seed.
