    Homesteads (freehold type)

    09-04-2022, 04:55 PM
    Freeholds are usually defined by their function of serving a gateway between the Autumn World and the Dreaming. But one type are homesteads which exist solely in the Dreaming. Instead of serving as a door between worlds, they provide local stability to anchor the local Dreaming into a stable form.

    Now Changelings who inhabit such a homestead are likely to live most of their time far more in the Dreaming than the Autumn World. But they are still Changelings and still need to spend some time there so not to succumb to Bedlam. Given they likely spend around the minimum time required for that, they would have the fewest ties to mortal. They probably don't have steady jobs, places of residence, and other things that require an ongoing presence in the Autumn World. At the same time, I do think they could have sufficient ties to mortals and mortal society to stave off Bedlam especially if they are supported by some Changelings who do have resources in the Autumn World.

    Some sort of vagrant concept would work, but not necessarily be someone who is homeless. They could be various kind of seasonal workers, visiting relatives, criminals, carnies, migrant workers and such. Since mortal attachments are important to avoid Bedlam, it'd be best if such Changelings could run into the same people year after year despite being gone for extended periods.

    I also imagine that any Siochain might prefer to stay in homesteads since it should provide less challenges to them than it does to others.

    Wondering how extensive people have used homesteads in their own chronicles, and how you've dealt with the practicalities of running such freeholds.

    I like homesteads because they can expand the Dreaming and give more depth to that aspect of the chronicle. But it seems to me to provide some challenges to make the concept work.

          I don't think the keepers of a Homestead would be of a vagrant concept. After all, they do have a stable residence: the Homestead itself. And most of them, being in the Near Dreaming or having Silver Paths to known Freeholds, are somewhat tied to specific places.           Homesteads are also likely to have the means to facilitate this to their permanent inhabitants. The Freeholder may have land near to one Trod that leads there and let them claim to live there, maybe even have actual homes there for their staying in the Autumn World. It may grow some chimerical produce they exchange with other Freeholds and from this get either the resources or the excuse to the wandering in the Autumn World. Hence any permanent inhabitant will need to go to the Autumn World with some frequency anyway, the Homestead is likely organized with that in mind.

                          Originally posted by Astromancer View Post
                          The Homestead would need a cover
                          I don't think they explored Homesteads in C20, but no, it doesn't need a cover.

                          The point of the Homestead is that it exists solely on the Dreaming. It is not a Freehold in the usual sense of a place of respite against the Autumn World, it offers respite against the ever-changing nature of the Dreaming. It doesn't even need to be in the Near Dreaming, some are located in the Far Dreaming (and theoretically there could be some in the Deep Dreaming).

                          So the point is that it doesn't have any counterpoint in the Autumn World. It's its denizens that need an explanation to be away from town most of the time, completely unreachable to their human acquaintances, yet they come back from time to time. How to explain where you have been when the place you have been doesn't exist in this world whatsoever?

                          Likewise, Homesteads have no direct links to the Autumn World. They may have trods to there, but not a direct entrance, and even those aren't common. Most Homesteads require a journey through several trods to reach a Freehold in the Autumn World.

                          Changeling has one big problem in that most pre-20 supplements have a visible problem of leaning so much in one aspect of the game the author liked as to effectively ignore and contradict lore of other aspects, at least to a degree. Most of the time it isn't a big problem, but it does means some inconsistencies have to be accounted for to understand a given element of the supplements. Homesteads fit this bill. Their original depiction completely ignores that Changelings aren't the original state of the Kithain and that Bedlam exists for them, so they work as Dreaming Freeholds that were cut from the Autumn World during the Interregnum and now conflict ensues, and their inhabitants are completely normal Changelings just 'cause.

                          You take from that what you want and adapt, as unfortunately must be done with too many supplements for the game. I'd say it has one of the most solid core books pre-Revised (it never got an actual Revised edition) in both editions, but the supplements are way out of pace with the cores. I'm still working some of my own conclusions, but I personally like in V20 to use the rules for Dark-Kin, with a few tweaks, to represent the eventual non-Changeling Kithain that still lives within the Dreaming, like most of House Danaan in V20.

                                  Originally posted by Astromancer View Post
                                  A New Age consciousness Center, one of those places people go to for retreats that involve aroma therapy, massage, yoga, and other things New Agey. Again, who'd notice weird behavior from a place like that?
                                  It's not about places where weird behavior would be accepted or expected.

                                  It's about these Changelings having very limited time to spend in the mortal world. If they are spending six days out of the week at the homestead in the Dreaming, for example, that is not enough time to earn any kind of income that could provide them a place of business, a rent or mortgage, enough time to sell goods or services, etc. Even yoga instructors, massage therapists, New Age shop sellers, tourist trap managers, reiki practitioners, etc. will be working around a full work week in the mortal world.

                                  The moment you starting saying these Changelings are spending enough time in the mortal world to allow them to run businesses, pay rent, utilities, taxes, etc. then those Changelings aren't spending any real time in the homestead.

                                  To me, living in a homestead implies that any Changelings choosing to live there are spending most of their time in the Dreaming. They don't have an immediate access to the Autumn World like normal freeholds. They must first travel to other Freeholds or kinds of Trods simply to get to the Autumn World. Choosing to reside in a homestead implies they want to spend most of their time in the Dreaming. If they wanted to spend so much time in the Autumn World, they'd be using some other kind of Freehold type. As such they have minimal ties to the Autumn World. They need some kind of mortal concept that enables them to meet the minimal requirements to ward off Bedlam despite the limited time they have to spend there.
