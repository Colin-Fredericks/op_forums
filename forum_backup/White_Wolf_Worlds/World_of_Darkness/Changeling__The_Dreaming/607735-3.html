    Hsien bother me a lot. I think they are really cool, but are a bad fit IMO, for changelings. As gods exiled to creation they always made a better fit in my mind for a Eastern equivalent of Demon the Fallen than Changeling, since the Elohim are basically all the godlings from the Mesopotamian pantheons who got demoted after Yahweh was made the "Only True God" when the Torah was edited. Making the splat that used the Hsien rules an equivalent to the Elohim freed me to use the basic changeling rules for statting the creatures in Lo8MD that did conform to what i thought of as Fair Folk. I borrowed bits and pieces from Scion and wikipedia to fill in the gaps.

                        Which did you pick were still gods and which did you feel were proper fae? The HIrayanu in my mind work out to be hitting many of the creatures that are Faeish in myths
                        T

                        Thats true, and thats where it gets complicated for me. I effectively yoinked the Hsien rules to use for KotE Demon spalt equivalent, who i called the Shinma, and left the Hsien as a Dreaming connected Changeling equivalent splat.

                        To further define the Shinma, i borrowed heavily from Scion as i said, but also my one of my favourite novels Barry Hughart's "Bridge Of Birds".
                        So i ended up with a global splat of exiled divinities who reap Faith, who are defined by local paradigm,They use Lores if they dwell in lands where the Abrahamic faiths are strong, but use the Hsien rules in the Eastern WoD.

                        Then i as a consequence had a global splat connected to the Dreaming who appear as the spirits of nature and the Wyld who reap Glamour in the west and Yang Chi/Yugen in the East

                        This was just so i could have one Hsien npc who my Demon player encountered and mess with her head when he pointed out that they were the same thing just filtered thru different local paradigms. And drop clues about the time the world was flat and they served the Celestines in Yu Shan. Totally worth it.
