    Founts - looking for examples/ideas

    09-14-2022, 08:21 PM
    Founts are naturally occurring sources of Dross (Glamour). Dross must somehow be destroyed in order for the Glamour to be released and consumed. The ST assigns a pre-determined number of Glamour that can be consumed this way. After that maximum is reached, the Fount must replenish itself. They're supposed to be direct connections to the Dreaming in the hidden places of the world.

    I'm trying to come up with good ideas and interesting descriptions of them in the setting. Some ideas I have include:

    A piece of land that has a mushroom circle, where eating the mushrooms consumes the Dross. Perhaps tied to a specific time to harvest the mushrooms (when the moon is out, at twilight, etc.) as a limitation. Similar Founts might include any other kind of edible food - wild berries or fruits at certain places or times. Perhaps honey gathered from a wild beehive. Anything that might be a natural intoxicant, hallucinogen, medicine, or otherwise associated with fairy folklore.

    A natural spring where drinking the water consumes the Dross, again perhaps tied to a specific time or event like under moonlight.

    Instead of being eaten, the Dross might be something consumed when thrown into a fire and inhaled like the scent of incense, beeswax candles, tobacco, or essential oils.

    Dross that is created from artificial processes that transform natural plants into something else like alcohol. These grapes might not provide Glamour, but when these grapes are turned into homemade wine (or distilled into liquor, whatever it is) then some of the wine becomes Dross. You can have all sorts of conditions on when this transformation occurs.

    Perhaps certain pools of water or areas of woodland where chimerical creatures manifest and can be hunted and eaten for Glamour.

    Does anything come to mind to people that is very different from the above? Dross can appear in many forms like geodes, crystals, or river stones, but I think those examples are better for concepts like Dream-Stones and not a replenishable source. Rocks aren't replenished, but things like plants and water are. They need for the items to be both be replenishable and destroyable/consumable eliminates many other things. For example, I would like to include phenomenon like will o' wisps or sleeping people who wake up with fairy-locks in their hair and such, but I can't imagine anything "consumable" that could occur.

    While Changelings would be the primary beneficiaries of such Founts, I like to use them as places where ordinary humans accidentally enchant themselves and thus can interact with the fae.

    I am very interested in hearing other people's ideas. Thanks.

      One is a large area, maybe an entire forest or mountain, but the dross is specifically bezoar formed in the stomachs of certain animals. This is tricky to find and get, and then to process into something for consumption, but may be specially rich for its size.

      Light that passe through a certain entrance in a cave our mound, like the light of the solstice in Newgrange, Ireland. Many think the dross are the crystals found within the cave, but it is actually the "trapped light" within. Harvesting has depleted most of the crystals (they may reform by the influence of the Dreaming, although not nearly at a replenishing rate), but "trapping the light" with other things is possible if one finds out.

      Ink harvested from a unique local species of octopus, but only if pure, extracted by making the octopus laugh. Any other method will taint it and make it either useless or dangerous, including harvesting an injured, joyless octopus. To consume it you must use the ink to write or draw something and give it away.

      A pirate server maintained by fans to enjoy a discontinued game. Dross comes in the form of rare drops in certain areas and quests, but you must know the right action to consume each. Some have to be given to certain NPCs, other discarded or just used, still some must be auctioned at the right time, by the right price, and the currency wasted.

      Spontaneously generated ideas that solve an issue another person has. You consume the dross by executing the idea to solve the issue, which may demand quite some investigation to identify the person and the problem. You only need to spend time in the clearing that used to be a meeting place for some ancient people (or things), and the person to be helped either lives nearby or has some easy to follow connection to the place.

After that I started to toying with SCPs in my mind, how to change the concepts of infohazards into dross, a magical idea instead of an object, and it became the later two.

                      I was thinking about how to simulate the idea of fairies taking the nourishment of food (foyson), but not its actual substance. This is sometimes referred to as foyson theft. It is often applied to fairies robbing the milk of farm animals of any nutritional value. And it occurred to me that the concept of founts might be appropriate here.

                      In this example, certain foods become sources of Dross and this Dross can be consumed by Changelings. The nutritional value of the foods is gone, but the physical substance is still there.

                      I imagine people can recognize when food and drink have had its foyson taken - it may seem stale, tasteless, adulterated, etc. But it isn't exactly clear that this is the case.

                      Now in this case, the Changeling isn't physically destroying or consuming the substance in question as the material is still there, but I think it is OK to conceive of its missing nutrition or nourishment as the part that is "destroyed". There probably still needs to be an action for the Changeling to do, but I think someone breathing in deeply, or making a pantomime of their hand "grabbing" something from thin air would suffice.

                      I'm less certain under what conditions that a home's cheese and meat in the pantry, or the milk in a cow becomes Dross in the first place. And it's unlikely such a Fount would last for a long time, otherwise a specific person or group of people would quickly starve. It would be better if it was a short term phenomenon. In this case, the specific area where the Dross forms is not replenished, but the power behind the Fount leaves and reforms somewhere else. To the Fae this might be something similar to how we conceive of weather. One day it rains, then the rain moves somewhere, and then it stops. Some time later it'll happen again.

                      The talent of Kenning would presumably allow a Changeling to not only detect such a Fount, but also to follow the phenomenon like a meteorologist who studies the weather.

                      I would still like to have some sort of event or phenomenon associated with this type of Fount simply for narrating a scene as ST. It's good to have ideas for triggers or signs. But I think that will occur to me in time.

                          Something that the foyson idea is making me think about.

                          So Changelings in WoD are distinct from the Fair Folk/Small People of irl cultures. While maybe the Dark Ages Fae operated different, Changelings ‘feed’ upon glamour. So thinking about this led me to wonder how that would interact with foyson.

                          Here’s my thoughts atm. Changelings don’t actually consume the food stuffs for nutrients (that is, there chimerical selves don’t, their physical bodies need food obviously), they feed for glamour. What the Changeling is feeding on isn’t the life source that a Vampire or Awakened could hypothetically tap into, but rather, a glamorous/chimerical manifestation of said life force as envisioned by the dreams of humanity (and to a lesser extent the world itself).

                          So if a Changeling wanted to drink the cream from a cow’s milk to gather glamour, I don’t think just any cow would do. Rather I imagine that a Changeling is going to be looking for a cow that’s surrounded by humans who consciously or subconsciously view the cow itself as a source of life-giving milk, and it’s from the human perceptions wrapped around the spiritual frame of the cow that Changelings can siphon energy. Perhaps in olden days the dreams of the calves was enough to support the Little People of the oxen and the fields, but not generally any more.

                          That also led me to consider the actual circumstances of a lot of food production in many parts of the world. Would industrial farming, with its unwavering pursuit of profit above all else, be able to generate glamour of the kind that old cottage and pasture farms could? I’m tempted to say no, that rather, most of them would be sources of banality and perhaps even nightmare depending on how bad the conditions have gotten.

                          But those are just my thoughts off the top of my head, hope they were interesting or helpful in some way.

                              In a more limited way (not as a fount), I do use the tradition of people leaving out a saucer of milk or other food to the local fairy that lives in your house or farm as a source of Glamour. It's because it is being done because the human believes in fairies, that such an act serves as Glamour for any Changeling. It's not a fount since it is not naturally replenished. I just classify it as a form of Spontaneous Glamour that provides 1 dot of Temporary Glamour each night it is done. Of course, that was done much more in the past by people. It's not often done now. It's similar to the above, but different in nature because of how the substance becomes Dross as one gains it because of the intent of the person leaving out the milk, cheese, or whatever versus something naturally occurring.

                                      I can see the appeal of using a mechanic similar to Ravishing, but I don't think I'll be going down that route. The Epiphanies are targeted toward to Dreamers not general objects, and I don't see the connection between Dreamers and the food they have (whether it is cheese or bead in the pantry, or milk still inside the cow) as a sense of wonder.

                                      But my main problem is that if you just make it a form or spin off of Ravishing, then Changelings could target food stocks almost any time. The belief that fairies can steal the foyson in food is an established part of fairy lore now, but I don't think it's been done many times. I think it's just an explanation people used to describe food becoming bad or stale, or the cow becoming sick or some other misfortune. It's not a constant complaint. So I'd like to keep it relatively rare and perhaps random (though with some kind of logic to the phenomenon to make the STs job easier), and utilizing founts seems to be a way this could be done.

                                      But whatever works for people if they want to use the concept in their own chronicles as a means to gain Glamour.
