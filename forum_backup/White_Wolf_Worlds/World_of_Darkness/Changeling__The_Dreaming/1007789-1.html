                                        Reno, Nevada
                                        ​It has been a few hours since you took your cousin and fled from the Bullion Records building. As a Boggan, you felt like it was your duty to help when your cousin told you about the unscrupulous behavior of John Macady, the producer in charge of the company. At first, you asked him why He could not leave by himself and sue him. Your cousin then dropped a bombshell when he told you that John would not let him leave due to a loophole in the contract that stated that he had to stay for all the 5 years he agreed to in the contract. So, you immediately went to Reno along with your childhood friends, Rachel, a sidhe, and Carter, a Satyr.

                                        ​Getting your cousin out of the building was a hard task however. It turns out that John did not want to let go of his new investment and you and your motley had to fight your way through Bullion Security to get out of the building. But, you managed to get out of the building and fled to your cousin's house in the suburbs. The house however, was neglected due to the fact that the contract provided your cousin with an apartment in the Bullion building. Unsurprisingly, the electricity was cut and so was the gas and water. You are currently in the house getting ready to leave town in the morning. Your cousin is in his old room strumming his guitar and humming a melody, Rachel is in the backyard staring at the stars while Carter is in the living room, reading a magazine. You, were in the second bedroom in the house, packing yours and everyone else's bags for the morning.

                                        You suddenly hear a window break. You immediately stop packing and go investigate. As you walk through the hall you hear noises coming from the bathroom. You go up to the door and peek through a crack in the door. You see a broken window and a few shards of broken glass. Suddenly, a man wearing a ski mask pokes his head through the window and looks around as if trying to see if anyone was around. After a few moments of looking around, the man started to climb through the broken window. The man was tall, thin and wore blue jeans, a red shirt and a leather jacket. After getting in the room, he stuck his hand up and helped a smaller looking burglar wearing the same clothes in. A few more men climbed in, one was more portly then the other two and the final man was muscular. There were four burglars in total, all of them wearing ski masks. The tall man, obviously the leader, said to them in a hushed voice that had a hint of a Hispanic accent," Remember, we came here for the musician. The boss said not to hurt anyone unless absolutely necessary".

                                        ​You try to sneak away from the door to warn your cousin and your friends. But, right before you reach the foot of the stairs you trip on a rag. You land with a loud thud. You hear footsteps running towards you, the burglars have heard you. " Well, well, well", the muscular burglar said," looks like we got a witness." Luckily, your seeming keeps the burglars from seeing what you really look like. But, it can't save you from being beaten up. The portly burglar cackles as he walks up to you cracking his knuckles. When he gets close enough, he tries to punch you in the face.

                                            Okay, so, we've got one boggan with Chicanery and Actor against four thugs. While the boggan does have backup nearby, they won't be there in time to save him from being whomped by the thugs. Worse, the poor bastard is already down, so he can't even flee. He's getting hit here, and there's not much he can do to avoid it. He probably should have called for help at the first sign of the intrusion, but at this point, it's too late for that. He does have Actor 5, which means that he can cast cantrips directly on the attackers, but the only Art he has access to is Chicanery. There's precious little that the boggan to do to make this a fair fight, much less one in his favor. The best he can hope to do is buy some time and hope that his other changeling friends heard the breaking glass and are coming to investigate as well. Since he knows that the thugs are hoping to avoid a scene, he can also hope that they'll run at the first sign that something's wrong. He uses Chicanery on the closest thug, making him perceive the sound of his cousin practicing the guitar as police sirens pulling up outside the house. If he had Scene as well, he could have attempted to make all of the thugs hear it, but since he doesn't, he has to hope that the one will be enough.

                                                    Carter has a couple options open to him. Running for help may be the best choice, since Rachel has the best magical options to get them all out of this alive. We can get to her later, though, because I believe the goal to this exercise is to see what each one can do individually. Soothsay is his only Art, and at three dots, it doesn't give him a whole lot of combat options. With Nature being his only Realm, it takes all utility out of his cantrips to try and fight these guys with him. Calling upon the Wyrd is a good choice, at this point. It will allow him to move at his full speed, and since he still has the element of surprise, he may be able to charge into one of them, using his horns to hit his target hard and fast. Some satyr characters I've had have also taken advantage of people's superstitious natures to impersonate the Devil when calling upon the Wyrd, encouraging them towards negative behaviors so that they can get ahold of souls, which usually has the effect of people running in terror and seriously reconsidering their paths in life. Sometimes they react with violence, though, so it's always a risk. Soothsay is associated with blessings and curses as well as prophecy, though, and C20 is giving changelings the option of Unleashing an Art, so let's go with that. Carter calls upon the Soothsay Art, says "Save my friends," and Unleashes. We'll assume he's coaxing the Wyrd rather than commanding, so that gives him a much easier time succeeding, but less control over the outcome. What could happen here? A few thoughts off the top of my head: The thug punching the boggan might end up missing, hitting the something hard instead and shattering his fist, his resulting scream distracting the others enough for the protagonists to regroup together. Or, maybe it turns out that a neighbor had heard the strumming of the guitar earlier, called the cops for a noise complaint, and they are just now arriving. Or, the thugs begin getting visions of their fate if they continue on their current course of action, and begin to have a change of heart. Of course, Unleashing can always end up going a bit awry if the character is too successful. Perhaps the thugs gain visions of an apocalyptic scenario, and realize that this small group of changelings are the ones destined to stop it. Instead of beating them down and taking the musician, they realize that they must protect the world's last hope, and found a cult to do just that. Naturally, the vision they saw would be completely true, and Carter Unleashing Soothsay has now conscripted him and his friends into the role of heroes, whether they like it or not. The best part about Unleashing, especially when coaxing, is that you can tie it into the rest of your story pretty easily.
