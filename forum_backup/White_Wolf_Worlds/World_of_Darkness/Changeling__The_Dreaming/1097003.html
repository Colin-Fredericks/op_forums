    Kinain C20, crossover characters, Halp!

    05-26-2017, 02:28 AM
    Opening Notes: Folks who get bitter about people utilizing the rules of crossover characters, may be unfit to answer these questions. Looking for answers to port over some of my old characters...for example i had a Hermetic/Satyr Kinain. With the advent of 3rd edit it was never a problem, even in M20 it wasn't really a problem. The golden rules of crossover remained the same, you may only cross a Major/Minor subgroup, and you may only have the power source of the major subgroup when making a crossover character. But then we come to C20...in 3rd edit Kinain had to be spoonfed glamour to HAVE glamour at all, so awakening was never a problem as there was no glamour in pool to stop the process of awakening for a kinain. But now in C20 Kinain regen their glamour like revenants do blood. This is flat out no bueno as regenning glamour means my character which was fine till C20 came out is now staring down the bareel of "You are violating the rules of Canon for Crossover Characters". That puts me at a conundrum, I like being within canon, and I'm not interested in giving up my kinain/Mage. What should I do? Cheese that someone ravaged my kinain and he woke up in the intervening period where he didn't have glamour in pool and now quintessence rules the show? Further with how things have been borked with Kinain...How exactly does the tribe of fianna even exist? If fae and garou breed, a kinain is almost assured to be the result, and most likely a kinfolk...if that kinain/kinfolk goes on to breed with a garou, the chances are high for a kinain/Garou as that offspring (though sure it can skip generations), Now how can you have a tribe that is -fae touched- if being fae blooded entails having glamour, which would stop first change from happening? There could never be fae blooded fianna garou, because again, in C20 you're staring down the barrel of violating the cardinal rule of crossover "Thou shalt not have the power sources of two supers, unless your name is Sam Haight."...so how exactly do fianna, as a tribe, exist, if all kinain have glamour and regen it?

They are intended to be used for humans with some fae blood not mechanics for Vampires with Fae blood, or Werewolves or mages.

                                                      Originally posted by FallenEco View Post
                                                      Officially, that 'golden rule' is more a recommendation.
                                                      My point is that "golden rule" or "recommendation," it isn't official. There aren't books that present guidelines that include, "don't allow character to have multiple sources of supernatural juice."

                                                      The books that address such characters usually do things based on the themes and metaphysics of the different groups. Imbued Hunters can't be crossover anything. Only normal humans can Imbue, and once a character Imbues, they're cut off from becoming a mage/etc. They can potentially become vampires or wraiths or etc., but they lose all their Imbued stuff. Fera can't Awaken into mages because they're not human enough any more, but some can learn hedge magic Numina. Nothing says that a Bubasti that's learned some sorcery paths can't take the Mana background. Not just nothing as in, "the rules don't explicitly say one way or the other," but the books don't even offer an opinion as a guideline most of time.

                                                      That's my point. The "golden rules" presented in the first post aren't official guidelines, because they're not from the books.
