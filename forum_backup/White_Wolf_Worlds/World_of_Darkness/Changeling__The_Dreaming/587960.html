    Fixing Arts and Realms (CTD)

    07-12-2015, 04:55 AM
    Okay, bear with me...

    I'm currently running a PBP crossover for Mage and Changeling. The concept is 'A Dream of Industry and Wonder' - sort of a semi-steampunk Neverwhere, I guess - set in contemporary Manchester, UK (nicknamed 'Cottonopolis' after its Industrial Revolution-era name).

    I'm wrangling with the rules for the Changeling Arts and Realms, because often the Arts either overlap horribly with Realms (making higher level Realms irrelevant if you possess certain Arts) or seem to necessitate very high level Realms even for basic level Arts (Nature 4 or Fae 5 for some one-dot Arts). It also seems like some Realms are more useful than others in the RAW.

    In short, I've tried to come up with ways that every level of every Art can be used with every Realm. There are only three that - by their very natures - can't be made to work this way, and so I've tagged them Minor Arts, which cost half the price of regular Arts (they are Dream-Craft, Skycraft and Infusion). I'm kind of okay with this, as Dream-Craft requires Fae 5 as a pre-requisite (or it's next to useless), while Skycraft needs Nature 4 and Infusion needs Fae 3 (as well as only being available to Nockers).

    What I'm looking for at this stage is any feedback on the suggestions, including further ideas or pointing out any clangers I've missed.

    The wiki is here: http://wiki.rpol.net/?id=63447/glamour_systems

    What do you think? Note that the reduced difficulty for cantrips is just because I've reduced magick difficulties for mages too, as I want to evoke an urban fantasy setting with some grittiness, rather than a gritty urban setting with some fantasy.

      Okay, so no one replied to this, I think because I just left the link there. I'll copy and paste the bits I wanted feedback on in particular. Realms first (Arts in the next post):

      REALMS

      Overreaching is when a changeling attempts to affect beings or items they can't usually target because they don't have the necessary Realms. This costs one Willpower point and increases the difficulty of the cantrip by +1 for each level of difference between the level of Realm the character possesses and the level required. So a changeling with Fae 1 who wants to use their magic on a sidhe noble would spend one Willpower and be at +1 difficulty to their roll.

      The Target Realm for a cantrip is the one that defines the target. This is always the primary Realm in any given cantrip. If you have Primal 5, you can turn a person into a tree with the Actor Realm without any knowledge of the Nature Realm. However, if you have it, the Nature Realm could be used as a secondary Realm (giving -1 to the difficulty).

      Likewise, you can create fire with Pyretics without the relevant level of Nature (see the modified description of the Nature Realm below), but you wouldn't be able to control it once it's been summoned. You can, however, use other Realms to create hallucinatory fire or even chimerical fire (see below).

      If you wish to target a group of changelings and mortals, you'd need both Actor and Fae as Target Realms, along with Scene as the Modifier Realm.

      Secondary Realms are any optional Realms that would aid your cantrip but are not needed to affect the target. If you want to turn that mortal (Actor) into a tree (Nature), Actor would be the Target Realm and Nature could be used as a Secondary Realm. Secondary Realms give a -1 modifier to the difficulty and are cumulative (you can use multiple Secondary Realms).

      Modifier Realms either add a time delay or extend the range of a cantrip - targeting multiple people or a whole area. Modifier Realms cost one Glamour each to add to a cantrip. So a cantrip that has a time delay will cost an extra Glamour point, but a cantrip with a time delay and an extended range will cost an extra two Glamour points.

      If you wish to turn a group of changelings and mortals into trees, the Target Realms would be both Actor and Fae, denoting the type of target. Scene would be the Modifier Realm to affect a group of people, rather than just one. Nature, however, could be used as a Secondary Realm to reduce the difficulty.

      GENERAL REALM DESCRIPTIONS

      Actor - The Realm of Flesh

      This realm concerns itself with mortals. The higher the realm, the less the caster must personally know the person or the less intimate their connection needs to be. This realm includes supernatural beings such as vampires, wraiths and mages, but not other fae.

      Actor measures the degree of 'intimacy' or 'knowledge' the caster has of their target. Usually this means knowing their name, profession and personality (their Nature or Demeanor, or either of their Legacies). Obtaining a personal possession from someone also grants a kind of 'knowledge' of them which satisfies certain requirements for the purposes of this Realm.

      Actor
      x - Lowly Minion: This person is a minion or charge of the changeling, perhaps a mortal oathbound to them, or a being otherwise willingly under their control (e.g., their human child).
      O - True Friend: This person must be a well known confidant or lover; or you have an intimate possession of theirs (a blood or skin sample, a lock of their hair, etc).
      OO - Dire Enemy: This person is your enemy or rival, but you know much about them; or you have a close possession of theirs.
      OOO - Personal Contact: You have had a long conversation with this person and you know their name; or you have a casual possession of theirs.
      OOOO - Familiar Face: You recognize his face, but you do not need to know his name; or you have a possession used once by them.
      OOOOO - Complete Stranger: You need no information or connection to the person at hand.

      NOTE: Mortal gaming companions fall under Actor 1 or 2, depending on how close they are and whether they secretly hate each other.

      Fae - The Realm of Glamour

      Fae
      x - Own Province: This level of Fae includes the self and any fae creatures oathbound or belonging to the changeling.
      O - Hearty Commoner: Commoner changelings.
      OO - Lofty Noble: Any noble or any changeling with a title.
      OOO - Manifold Chimera: Chimerical creatures or items.
      OOOO - Elusive Gallain: Usable on all fae beings not categorized above: Nunnehi, Inanimae or anything else unexplainable but related to the Dreaming.
      OOOOO - Dweomer of Glamour: Anything composed of Glamour: cantrips, treasures, dross, etc. (NOTE: This includes Quintessence but at +1 difficulty.)

      The description of Fae 5, 'Dweomer of Glamour' (page 189), indicates that this Realm is required to counter cantrips. No mention of this is made in the counterweaving rules on page 206, instead requiring the Realm or Realms used in the cantrip being countered. In short: Fae 5 isn't required but having it means that the other Realm(s) aren't necessary.

      Nature - The Realm of Gaia

      Nature
      x - Home Turf: You can command Nature in your own holdings (typically less than an acre) and any living thing that has been there for a year and a day; any natural thing which you own (e.g., a pet or beast of burden).
      O - Raw Element: The four base elements (earth, air, water, fire); inorganic dead matter and dead organic matter.
      OO - Verdant Forest: Plants and living simple organic matter (fungus, bacteria, etc).
      OOO - Feral Animal: Animals and living complex organic matter (tissue samples, organs, viruses, etc).
      OOOO - Simple Natural Phenomenon: common weather (rain, sunlight, rainbows, etc.).
      OOOOO - Complex Natural Phenomenon: uncommon weather (storms, tornados, volcanoes, earthquakes, etc); anything else in the realm of nature.

      Prop - The Realm of Objects

      Prop can be used to affect objects and items.

      Prop
      x - Personal Possessions: Anything on the fae's person or any possessions she owns.
      O - Ornate Garb: Objects commonly worn (clothing, rings, tattoos, etc.).
      OO - Crafted Tool: An item with no moving parts (swords, shields, but not guns).
      OOO - Mechnical Device: An item with moving parts, but not needing fuel to operate (including computers, which need electricity).
      OOOO - Complex Machine: A single item with moving, sometimes electronic components, but with an easily understandable mechanism (toaster ovens, cars, and printing presses; no computers, TVs, etc.)
      OOOOO - Arcane Artifact: Any crafted item not listed above, with complex components.

      MODIFIER REALM DESCRIPTIONS

      Those who do not possess Modifier Realms cannot modify the range of a cantrip nor can they delay its effects.

      Scene
      (Changeling: Second Edition, pg. 189)

      No change from the RAW.

      At the ST's discretion a cantrip may affect wider ranges if a character has Scene 5 and scores more than five successes on the casting roll.

      NOTE: Affecting targets at long range with Scene still requires the appropriate level of the Target Realm.

      Time
      (Changeling: Second Edition, pg. 189)

      No change from the RAW.

      A changeling may attach a trigger to a cantrip, in effect creating a booby trap or warning system. This requires five successes on the cantrip casting roll and the ST must approve the method for triggering the cantrip. Once triggered, the effect hits instantly and is considered spent thereafter.

      The ST may also allow a cantrip with a trigger to be delayed indefinitely if the player has Time 5 and scores more than five successes. The cantrip will take effect as soon as the requirements for the trigger are fulfilled. Once triggered, the effect hits instantly and is considered spent thereafter.

      NOTE: Extending the duration of a cantrip is a factor of the degrees of success of the cantrip casting roll, and not the Time Realm.

      Realm Prerequisites

      Some Arts require specific Realms by default. For example, Dream-Craft and Infusion require knowledge of Fae 5, while Daedalon requires Fae 3. Others allow a changeling to create certain effects without Realm mastery (e.g., Pyretics creates fire even if a changeling has no knowledge of Nature).

      In general, if an Art creates something you don't have the Realms for, you can still perform the cantrip, but can't modify or change it once it's been cast. You have no control over that fire you've just summoned without Nature 1, nor can you command that chimera you've just created without Fae 3. But there are creative uses of Arts that can avoid the problem of creating or summoning something you can't control.

      For example, Pyretics: To control the fire summoned with Pyretics would require Nature 1 (see the revised Realm description below), however using it with Fae 5 could instead create chimerical fire that would cause aggravated damage (further Glamour expenditure would make this wyrd). Using Actor could create the illusion of fire in the subject's mind, in a manner similar to uses of the Mind Sphere (this could also be done with Fae 1-2 to affect changelings). For more examples see the next section on Arts changes.

      Some Arts, because most or all of their cantrips necessitate using just one or two high-level Realms only, now count as Minor Arts and thus cost half the cost of normal Arts (see below).

      Realms and Transformed Subjects

      When you change sentient beings from one form into another (e.g., Actor into Nature), it's the 'true nature' or soul of the subject that determines which Realm to use (so that mortal you turned into a tree needs to be affected with Actor). This is why changelings who are Undone still require the Fae Realm to be affected and not Actor.

          ARTS

          Minor Arts

          Those Arts that require high levels of one particular Realm to use properly have now become Minor Arts to reflect the narrowness of their use.

          The Minor Arts are:

          Daedalon
          Dream-Craft
          Infusion
          Skycraft
          Tale Craft

          These cost half the usual points cost to buy or increase (round up).

          Changes to Arts

          Primal
          Eldritch Prime, as written, is next to useless without Nature 1 (see the revised Realm description above), however using it with Fae 5 would instead create chimerical versions of the natural elements which will exist in the Near Dreaming for the duration of the cantrip (the cantrip can be made wyrd to nevertheless cause real damage). Chimerical elements conjured in this way can become permanent parts of the Near Umbra (five successes or more).

          Alternatively, use of lower levels of Fae or any level of Actor (appropriate to the target of the cantrip) will cause the target to hallucinate the elements instead, and be affected psychologically by the phenomenon for the remainder of the scene or until they 'free' themselves. Any illusory elements created by Eldritch Prime can be resisted with five successes on a Willpower roll (difficulty 8). Even those who can see chimerical reality will not see the hallucination unless they are targeted - it exists entirely in the minds of the targets and has no basis in chimerical reality. Unlike chimerical creations, they will not linger in the Near Dreaming.

          If used with Prop, the Art causes the item to bend, warp or change size. Such changes are not performed with any finesse, and amount to expanding, twisting or stretching the item temporarily (one minute per success). The degree of success determines the degree of change:

          one success - the item curves or stretches by 10%
          two successes - the item buckles or stretches by 30%
          three successes - the item bows or stretches by 50%
          four successes - the item knots or doubles in size
          five successes - the item becomes radically deformed or misshapen

          Heather Balm heals one level of lethal or aggravated damage, or two levels of bashing damage, per success. The health levels healed can be either real or chimerical, or a combination of the two.

          Holly Strike inflicts aggravated damage.

          With Elder Form, the Realm dictates who or what can be transformed, but Secondary Realms will aid the transformation into other forms. A person turned into a log will still count as a person for the purposes of using Realms (so a mortal would be Actor and a changeling would be Fae). The Realm is determined by the subject's essence or 'soul'.

          Chicanery
          Fugue can be used to temporarily blank out pages in a book, reduce a photograph to a blur or scramble TV reception. Fugue can also be used to make a single computer, camera or other technological device 'forget' key information. In effect, this information is inaccessible, corrupted or deleted, and machines that rely on such information may temporarily malfunction. This is a temporary 'blackout' of data, although it may become permanent with six or more successes. Combining this with Space could affect multiple devices (say, an office full of computers), although to delete information permanently from the internet would involve deleting it from the servers that store said information.

          When used with Prop or Nature, Haunted Heart either affects the chimerical 'soul' of that target (making chimerical reflections of items behave strangely - which is very effective when combined with Willow Whisper), or imbues it with an emotion that anyone who comes into contact with the target (works of art must be viewed, tangible objects must be touched, etc) will feel for the duration of the cantrip, even if they walk away from the item in question. Time can be used to add a delay, so that only a future victim will be affected. Scene can be used to affect a space in a similar manner.

          Captive Heart can likewise be used with Prop or Nature. If used on an object, item or non-sentient being, its usual behaviour will be changed. A knife might refuse to cut, for instance, or a gun might refuse to fire. Alternatively, the outcome would affect any chimerae or spirits inhabiting the target. This Art is often used to create treasures or artefacts that only work for the owner.

          Standard cantrip durations for Chicanery are as follows:

          one success - one scene
          two successes - one day
          three successes - one week
          four successes - one month
          five successes - six months
          six successes - ST's decision

          If the item is broken or destroyed, the effect imbued within it is destroyed too. The Time Realm can be used to add a time delay to the cantrip.

          Legerdemain
          Ensnare can be used to cause damage but, in a change to the RAW, this is not three health levels of damage per level. Rather, the base damage should be considered three dice, with an additional dice for each additional success (beyond the first) rolled on the casting attempt. So a character who rolls three successes can cause five dice of damage, which will be either bashing or lethal, depending on the object used.

          Sovereign
          Regardless of the RAW, Protocol can indeed be cast on a Prop. Any object thus targeted will remain silent or inert until the end of the scene. Thus, mobile phones will not go off, weapons will refuse to be drawn and computers won't boot up.

          Dictum can likewise be used to hack computers, open vaults and switch on the telly. It cannot, however, be used to cause the target to harm itself or make itself permanently inoperable - so it couldn't be used to make a door fly off its hinges or to make an animal jump on the BBQ.

          Grandeur used with Prop or Nature makes said target awe-inspiring. Ever heard about those people who've fallen in love with their cars or the Eiffel Tower? That was probably the result of this Art.

          Yes, you can cast Weaver Ward on someone's mouth, but you need to achieve more (non-cumulative) successes than the target rolls on a resisted Willpower roll at difficulty 6.

          When using Prop or Nature with Geas (or Ban), the cantrip affects anyone who comes into contact with the target until they let go. Thus, a geas placed on a sword compels its holder to complete the geas. A ban placed on the same sword would prevent the holder from undertaking certain actions.

          A geas or ban can also be placed on a location using Scene - barring or compelling anyone in the vicinity. Whereas geasa and bans placed on an individual can only be resisted at the time of casting, a geas or ban placed on an item or location can be resisted instead at the point an individual first comes into contact with the target. The resistance check is a Willpower roll (difficulty 4 + the caster's successes).

          When using Sovereign in conjunction with Scene, Prop or Nature, successes must be divided by effect and the length of time the Art remains imbued within the relevant target:

          one success - one scene
          two successes - one day
          three successes - one week
          four successes - one month
          five successes - six months
          six successes - ST's decision

          If the item is broken or destroyed, the command within it is destroyed too. The Time Realm can be used to add a time delay to the cantrip, and the duration will begin from the moment the cantrip is triggered rather than from the time it is first cast.

          Wayfare
          Hopscotch can be used with any Realm to effect a 'jump'. If combined with Fae 5, for instance, a changeling could siphon Glamour from one being to another (the victim can resist this by rolling Willpower (difficulty 6)). In this case, Fae or Actor would serve as a secondary Realm if used.

          Attempts to move Quintessence with Hopscotch and Fae 5 would be at +2 difficulty and can be resisted by either Willpower, as usual, or any countermagick the mage may attempt in defence (mage player's choice). Each net success equals a point of Quintessence transferred to someone else (and converted to Glamour if transferred to a changeling). The changeling can take as much Quintessence this way as the character has, minus his Arete rating (as Arete takes on the role of the Avatar Background in this game).

          Buildings and complex structures with multiple parts cannot be made to jump with Hopscotch and Scene, because the successes would have to be divided between all the various parts of the building - which would be effectively impossible in most instances.

          Quicksilver allows the user to move faster than should be possible, making them hard to hit and more lethal in combat. The cantrip is automatic, taking place on the same turn it is activated, but costs one Glamour point. Quicksilver should be declared before initiative is rolled and the cantrip roll is made at this stage too (although any actions that result from Quicksilver take place as per initiative), although it can be declared at the end of a turn to begin in the next turn instead. Quicksilver has three key effects:
          • The subject adds the number of successes on the cantrip roll as a bonus to all Dexterity-based dice pools for one turn. It also adds to initiative but does not stack with the effects of the Dragon's Ire (the higher of the two bonuses applies). This bonus applies to each multiple action based on Dexterity when splitting your dice pool, but cannot take the user's dice pool higher than it would be without the use of Quicksilver.
          • The subject becomes harder to hit, effectively giving them a passive dodge. The threshold for any attack roll on the subject (ranged or close combat) rises by one for every success scored on the Quicksilver roll. This does not stack with multiple actions used to dodge, however.
          • The subject may also multiply their speed by the number of successes rolled. It is not possible to act while moving in this manner.
          Quicksilver can be used with Prop to speed up a vehicle or machine, or any other item with moving parts. Drivers not also affected by Quicksilver will have to make Wits rolls to keep up with the transport's new speed (difficulty 8 + successes on the cantrip roll).

          Nature can be used with Quicksilver to speed up an animal. Flowers might bloom fractionally faster, leaves might shift imperceptibly towards the light and fruit might ripen on a tree imbued with Quicksilver, but this would not usually be noticeable with the visible eye.

          By imbuing an item with Quicksilver before throwing it, a changeling can double the distance thrown for each success rolled. Projectile weapons thrown in this fashion add an extra automatic success on the damage roll for every success rolled on the cantrip and the damage is upgraded to lethal if it isn't already.

          Casting Quicksilver may be done while performing any other action, so long as the cantrip only uses a level one Bunk.

          Wind Runner has the same limitations as Hopscotch if cast on an object and may be resisted with a Willpower roll (difficulty 6).

          With Flicker Flash, the caster must know, see, scrye or possess a part/item of both her target and her destination. If she cannot fulfill this limitation, there is a chance the cantrip will go awry (three successes or more required, or the subject(s) will be sent off-course).

          Dream-Craft
          This Art, as written, is next to useless without Fae 5. This is a pity - especially as changelings should be able to manipulate dreams. As a result, Dream-Craft should be considered a Minor Art. Its cost is half that of a normal Art, but it can only be taken once Fae 5 has been learned. Finally, where Fae is used with another Realm to create a Dream-Craft cantrip, it may count as a secondary Realm for the purposes of calculating difficulty.

          Skycraft
          Like Dream-Craft, Skycraft is another Art which, as written, seems to step on the toes of a Realm - this time Nature. The Nature Realm is highly recommended to control your cantrips, but is not essential if you just want to create an effect and leave it to run its course. The natural phenomena described will be summoned, and you'd best be ready!

          Alternatively, a changeling with or without Nature may instead opt to trick a target into believing they're experiencing the same effects but without actually creating the natural phenomenon itself. Rather, the changeling can draw on the power of illusion to make their target hallucinate (Actor or Fae should be used, as appropriate), but such cantrips have no lasting effect in the real world and can only be sensed by those directly targeted. Indeed, even those who can see chimerical reality will not see the hallucination unless they are targeted - it exists entirely in the minds of the targets and any 'damage' incurred will dissipate when the power ends. Hallucinations can be overcome with five successes on a Willpower roll (difficulty 8). Using Fae 5 will create chimerical versions of these effects that will inflict chimerical damage instead.

          Skycraft should also be considered a Minor Art.

          Aphrodisia
          A character with Sovereign can learn Aphrodisia at half the usual cost, and vice versa, although the secondary Art's rating cannot exceed that of the primary Art.

          Chronos
          Despite appearances, Chronos does not require the Time Realm (the Time Realm is merely a modifier Realm rather than a knowledge of Time itself). As such, it is not a Minor Art. However, non-sidhe who attempt to use the Art are at +1 difficulty to do so.

          Contempt
          Condemnation is a peculiarly narrow power as written. If used on Prop, Nature or Scene, it conceals the importance of the subject from anyone who might try to ascertain its purpose or general use. Those wishing to use magic to read or assess the subject must achieve more successes than the initial cantrip - only those successes over and above this threshold count towards the attempt. Non-magical Perception rolls on the subject are at +3 difficulty.

          Infusion
          Like Dream-Craft, Infusion is a Minor Art, and so is half the usual cost to buy or increase.

          Metamorphosis
          The Realm used indicates who or what can be transformed, but secondary Realms can lower the difficulty if they relate to the form the target is being transformed into. As with Elder Form, the true nature of the subject is what matters when determining which Realm to use.

          Naming
          Reweaving differs from Metamorphosis and Primal 5 in that it is a) permanent and b) only affects the nature/demeanour/personality of a sentient being.

          Pyretics
          Nature 1 counts as a secondary Realm for the purposes of calculating the difficulty. Pyretics can create a temporary chimerical fire or balefire with Fae 5, which will inflict chimerical aggravated damage (making this effect wyrd will cause real aggravated damage). It can also create illusory fires that exist only the mind(s) of its target(s) when paired with Actor or Fae 1-4 if the caster doesn't wish to permanently harm the target. Actor and Fae can, however, be used to immolate enemies as normal, but the caster cannot affect this fire afterwards without Nature 1.

          Tale Craft
          Agemo's Blessing can be used with Nature or Prop to enhance the effects of Willow Whisper (Primal 1). Each success adds one to the dice pool for the Willow Whisper roll.

          Murmur in the Crowd can be used with Prop or Nature to invest a target with a sudden flicker of positive emotion. For example, a book might be imbued with the power to move the reader. A tree might invoke wonder and a sense of the sublime. When investing an item with emotion in this fashion, the effect is subject to the standard duration chart, although Time can be used to delay when it triggers.

          The Realm used for Sticks and Stones determines who or what is shielded by that Art.

          Moment of Truth works identically to Phantom Shadows, except the bunk specifies that storytelling must be involved to enact the power. An eshu with Phantom Shadows already needn't pay for Moment of Truth provided they already have Tale Craft 1-4. Similarly, an eshu with Moment of Truth will automatically gain Phantom Shadows provided they also have Legerdemain 1-4.
