            Word counts are a thing. Some things might be cool to have, but you've got to pick what's necessary vs what would be nice.

            In one of my sections there was some stuff I thought would be fun to include, but I quickly realized I just didn't have the space.                         Originally posted by Astromancer View Post
                        I'd like ....

                        A) all previously published arts in the book. Corebook arts, Player's guide arts, Nobles SH arts, Fool's Luck arts, Shadow Court Arts, and the Merfolk arts too!
                        The Arts are a balance of two competing goals:

                        1) Bring back everything cool!

                        2) Ensure that it is actually cool.

                        A lot of the old Arts, we discovered, were like... not actually cool. Nobles: the Shining Host had an Art that did basically nothing but alleviate optional penalties that an optional sub-system introduced into that book tossed onto the sidhe, and which no subsequent book ever referenced again, and which I've never heard of anyone actually using in play. So that didn't seem like a great thing to bring back. Then there was the Art in the Shadow Court which did nothing but go "nuh-uh" to various dots of Sovereign, which is a worst-of-all-worlds combination of expensive, boring, and really limited. Like, you just sit around waiting for the Duke to use Sovereign on you and then hope you have the right dot to shut him down? Why make that a whole Art? Why not just one dot of an Art to let you throw off oaths or bonds of authority, if you gotta have a power like that in the first place, and then give you proactive things with the other dots?

                        On the other hand, Changeling's core array of Arts were always very small and limited in the past, with a lot of things the fiction implied they ought to be able to do not actually falling within the scope of any published Art. (Remember how the books used to imply the Sluagh would hang around gossiping with ghosts, but then there was absolutely nothing except maybe a Merit somewhere that would have enabled that?) Like, Chicanery, Wayfare, Primal, they were great, but there were only, what, 7 powers in the corebook? Gotta have more than that to play with.

                        So what C20 has is a best-of compilation and reworking of the Arts. We looked at every dot of every old Art from allllll the supplements and decided what to do with each. Some of the very limited Arts of old got crunched down to their best one or two powers and folded into another Art. Other Arts got moderate to major power bumps. Others still-- particularly the "Kith-specific" ones-- became core sub-systems. EVERY Art got its dot progression reviewed and overhauled for better consistency, and to ensure that all dots of all Arts were compatible with all Realms-- no more ersatz Disciplines that ignore the Realms system, making all of your Arts much more versatile than in the past. And of course there are several completely new Arts, to boot.

                        No Arts were cut from the current draft for space, to be clear-- all changes were made for better versatility, bang-for-your-buck, and playability. If an old Art isn't there in stand-alone form like it used to be, you will probably find its best features folded into one of the old Arts or cobbled into one of the new Arts.

                                                            I really like what I read in Holden's post about what they did to recombine and beef up Arts.
