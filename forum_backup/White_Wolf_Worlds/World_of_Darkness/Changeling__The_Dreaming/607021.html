    Changeling: the Dreaming 20 wish

    07-29-2015, 04:43 PM
    If I could have one wish for the hypothetical C:tD 20, it would be that the canon Kiths at best constitute a minority of the Kiths still surviving on earth. Maybe they are the largest minorities, because they were better organized and connected, but still just a minority.

    Edit: In Dreams and Nightmares, it mentioned how there were a pair of Chimera who were either once True Fae or had the ability to become True Fae. I wouldn't mind if the book mentions how every now and then a Chimera successfully becomes a True Fae and emerges from the Dreaming to becom a Changeling.

      Bygone Bestiary has rules for creating chimera as beasts. Just add the Willpower, Banality and Glamour ratings for the appropriate seeming, make sure they have the equivalent of two birthrights and one frailty (the birthrights can be bought as special advantages, but the frailty can come from a flaw which they don't get extra Freebies for), and you have a custom changeling.

          That sounds like an awfully big of a departure for a 20th anniversary edition. But if it follows Vampire, Werewolf, and Mage, there should be lots of options. In addition to the nine core kiths, there are the minor kiths, the Nunnehi kiths, the Menehune kiths, Inanimae kiths, Hsien kiths, Adhene kiths, and Thallain kiths. If even part of those are in there, folks shouldn't be hurting for options.

          As for chimera becoming true fae, that was the way it worked before the shattering according to DA: Fae. The world is too calcified by banality for it happen these days, but there's nothing stopping you from doing something with that in your game. In fact, that could be a great seed for a chronicle - a chimera has grown powerful and fundamental enough to the Dreaming it's on the verge of becoming a true fae. Banality is keeping it from realizing it's full potential, and it's up to the PCs to help it cross that line. If they succeed, the Dreaming gets a huge boost as the first new kith is born since the Shattering.

                                                      There are a few things I'd like to see:

                                                      1. More flexibility around the scarcity of Glamour. It's notoriously difficult to obtain in the RAW, which is fine, but maybe options for making it less scarce would be welcome too. Spending weeks to get a few points is really tough, and it encourages players to hunt down dross instead.

                                                      2. Revised Realms. Actor needs work, because you can't affect an enemy until level 5! But you can fry your best friend at level one (unless he's your gaming buddy, and then he counts as an enemy). I think there should be clarification on which level of Fae a changeling needs to affect herself (I always rule a rating of zero in a Realm indicates command only over your own person and those things you own).

                                                      3. More versatility with Arts. Every Realm should be usable with every Art. Maybe each Art needs suggestions of how it might work with different Realms. Otherwise some Realms always prove more useful than others.

                                                      4. Optional rules for enchantment and the Mists for crossovers and other supernatural critters. Some of the coma periods on the Mists chart are so unforgiving that enchantment becomes a very effective weapon. Enchant a mortal and then leave them to sleep for a few weeks without remembering a thing when they awaken. Meanwhile, you burn their house down, steal their things and kill their families (if you're feeling subtle, that is...). The downtime for enchanted humans is ridiculous.

                                                      5. Clarity on what exactly Banality is for all those who argue that Technocrats aren't banal (because clearly Technocrats work magic and create wonder, while the Dauntain clearly use powers too, so it's not just 'anti-magic').
