    Sprite Kith

    07-20-2020, 09:47 AM
    So I am designing a Sprite Kith because a Player wants to have a classic Pixie type character, and Piskies seem to have lost a lot of what I wanted when they changed them into C20, what with the blending into groups and loosing individuality schtick. I might even split Piskies into a Sub Group with Pixies being how they worked in older editions. But here is an early draft of what I am working on for Sprites, a Kith based on the quintessential Fairy like Tinkerbell and the like. So if you have suggestions or Critic I would appreciate it.

    And Damnit why can't I add pictures anymore. It says I'm not authorized to make attachments!    [/COLOR]

    Kith Sprites
    Every dream starts from the humblest beginnings, so minute as to not have size. But their truth can be so grand they can reshape the realest part of the world, the mountains, rivers, and plains or even humanity. We too are started from humblest beginnings, how might we shape the world?


    Sprites were formed from gossamer motes of glamour woven with the faintest glimmer of will and some say were the first beings birthed by the Dreaming. Even if the first fae didn’t start as Sprites it was known the fae of old could beget sprites as an act of will and some fae theorize kiths may have evolved from the most willful of Sprites if not the Tuatha themselves. Before the Shattering however Sprites had no culture of their own, yet with the Shining Host’s retreat into the Dreaming the stranded purposeless Sprites succumbed quickly to banality. Rather then every Sprite being scourged from the Autumn World by the looming winter’s chill winds a miracle happened when the aimless innocent sprites found will in survival, for every five score Sprites that succumbed to being undone one would find motivation and the lantern of their will attracted their fellows. Somehow in the eleventh hour the orphaned Sprites girded in the fading essence of their fellows found the strength and providence to seek refuge in the Changeling Way.[/COLOR]

    If the Sprites oral history is to be believed the Piskies and Sylphs were the forerunners of the Sprites who achieved independent ambitions, and they are treated as mutual kin to this day. The Piskies are said to represent the Sprites who found definition through others, while the Sylphs are those who found purpose in their environment. The Sprites reborn through the crucible of ordeal found purpose in themselves, in finding what their wishes were. They are the dreams of hope, and awe, and numinous wonder found in the freedom to explore the world not as a pet, or slave but as an individual. They naturally are drawn to wander in their curiosity, but they find themselves lured to mortal dreamers heavy with hope, particularly to those seeking freedom and joy, and most of all young children unattached to concepts of impossibility. In the cherished dreams of youths they have found a comforting bastion against the coming winter, and they serve to meliorate the wistful scar that is Arcadia in the Hearts of the Kithain.

    Appearance:
    Sprung from the primordial motes of glamour Sprites are a colorful breed. Their fae aspect are normally short and lithe, and often androgynous. Each Sprite has a unique skin tone from a vast polychromatic spectrum, with hair vivid and of a slightly different tone or even a contrasting shade. Sprites glimmer with a lambent glow with sparkling dust trailing after them, noticeable especially in the moonlight. One their backs they have wings that can appear to be anything from butterfly or Moth wings to the more rare leathered bat wings or feathered angelic appendages. Even the Sprites with more plump physiques will be surprisingly lightweight and nimble. Their eyes, mostly pupils, like jeweled pools are large and will reflect a noticeable curiosity in the same tone as their hair.[/COLOR]


    Lifestyles:
    Unlike their kin, the Piskies, Sprites find great comfort among their own kind. They naturally form into clouds, their version of the motley. They thrive in communities in general, and especially find joy in caring for the sullen and children, so naturally teaching and caretaking young children are natural jobs. Their capacity to instill hope also makes them fine therapists and counselors. Many also find freedom in just wandering without responsibility, traveling where their heart guides them. They are at home in the wilderness and enjoy observing and interacting with plants and animals, shepherding bees and the seasons themselves in their chosen forests.[/COLOR]

    Childling:
    The youngest of the Sprites, known as motes, are the most impish in their habit for pranking, and in this stage they explore their boundaries by learning how far they can push things before they offend potential friends. Sprites curiosity is ramped up to manic degrees and their penchant for exploring can get them into trouble if not lethal danger. They have a strong desire to be accepted and to accumulate friends, but they also can grow jealous when the attention of their new friends turn elsewhere, ironic considering their flighty temperament has them sometimes forget old friends for new acquaintances.[/COLOR]

    Wilder:
    Sprite youths, called wisps, learn to develop quality over quantity concerning making friends, pranks also mellow out in favor of jokes, songs and stories. Social boundary exploration gives way for exploration of physical and metaphysical boundaries, this age group start to learn every nook and cranny of their glens and even the names of the winds and the birds. They develop curiosity over the potential of their own arts and gather into clouds of similarly aged sprites to explore the Dreaming's secrets. They will start to carve out their territory in the local wildlife, claiming all the nests and hives on a particular tree as their own. Some choose a suitably impressive fae to bond with whether they are Sidhe Dames they pledge to, Sorcerous Mentor’s, or even monumentally impressive Dragon Chimera. They have a reputation of being overly possessive of those they bond with.


    Grump:
    The Matriarchs and Patriarchs of the Sprites, also called wills, take up the role as leaders and caretakers of their younger fellows. They may journey to gather clouds of younger sprites to form a community known as frolics, with a notable preference for Glades, but they are also willing to cohabitate with other fae in Freeholds instead. Sprites also start to develop a keen interest in human communities at this age, observing human society at its various levels. They seek methods to bring together the different aspects of the worlds communities, such as harmony between a village and the local pondlife, or between kithain and the elder boughs of their fens. Their lifetime of curiosity pays off in a trove of secrets, whether the location of all the local bat colonies, or fine fishing hollows, or trods to interesting locations unknown to others in the local court.

    Sprite elders are as curious and full of life as ever, but they have finally learned to master their abundance emotions and harness it towards future generations guiding young fae, whether the newest cloud of Sprites or a gallain they have taken under their wing. Their territoriality concerning their frolics, their dreamers, and especially any they take under their wing as an apprentice has caught some of the more serious kiths off guard. If they deem it necessary, their hidden wisdom of the art of the prank comes back to the fore as they harness a whole eager frolic of Sprites towards interlopers and unwanted competition.

    Affinity: Actor

    Revelry:
    Sprites can bask in the rich glamour found in encouraging and listening to the hopes,wishes, and dreams of children, or helping them manifest their dreams. The secret joys of cavorting with other Sprites amidst nature away from the eyes of humanity can tease out glamour during liminal times such as dawn, or the turning of seasons. Sprites may literally drink glamour from the dew during such capers.

    Unleashing:
    The magic of the sprites whirl and twirl in a joyous polychromatic rollick, causing plants to bloom recalling the first sunrise of spring. A smile forms in one’s chest, Echoes of hidden titters sound in the periphery, the gleam’s hue turns wan, and the grounds smell of a midsummer night's shower as dew collects upon nearby surfaces.[/COLOR]

    Birthrights
    Miniscule Legacy:
    The wee form of old returns to the Sprite if they hide themselves from view all but other Sprites, Piskies, and Perosemes and spend a point of glamour. They gain two dots of Dexterity, and increase all rolls to notice or attack the Sprite by 3, as the Sprite is now around the size of a small mouse or bat. The Sprites vestigial wings function allowing the sprites to fly their normal height above the ground or a surface. The Wings Merit cost 2 points. They must spend a dot of glamour to return to standard size. The Unenchanted will perceive the Sprite as a flickering ball of light, like an unusually sizeable firefly or a will-o’-the-wisp but if their appearance is scrutinized because their movements betray their intelligence or their appearance during the day or indoors this may trigger banality.

    Stuff Dreams are Made of:
    Calling upon their roots as possibly the first of the chimera the Sprite may condense their evanescent glimmering dust with a point of glamour. With a Greymare + Appearance roll this dust may be applied to a target allowing them to assuage nightmares, heal with a rest as if they spent an equal amount of nights resting as their successes in the roll. Till the Sun next rises the target treats their Banality as if it was one less. The scent of the dust is that of fading nostalgia, and it tastes like one's memory of their first sunrise. By spending a willpower point it can be applied to transient chimera such as incidental chimera or chimera summoned with Oneiromancy, bringing them a step closer to permanence, making them last an extra week. If applied within a Freehold they become permanent while within the Freehold. Sprites may generate this dust once per sunrise, and subjects of the dust may only be affected by it once per lunar cycle.


    Frailty
    Curious Minds:
    Sprites have a curiosity overshadowing putting Pooka and Piskies to shame. Any social roll attempting to make use of the Sprite’s curiosity is resisted by the Sprite at a +2 difficulty. They can also possess a unique naivete pertaining to subjects of particular interest, if tempted with the opportunity to discover secrets or interesting new information pertaining to subjects close to the Sprites heart they treat ignoring this opportunity as a banality trigger.

                                          I hope I didn't make this entry too bloated. I had a lot of ideas for them and tried to strip away the excess while keeping the Emdowments and Vulnerabilities coherent.                                           I present the Sprite Thallain





                                          Thallain

                                          Kith: Creeps

                                          Weee have beeeen watching yooouuu. Yooouuu have beeeeen alooone, even wiiiith your friends and faaamily. Weee are always here waaaatching youuuu from the Shaaadows. Nooone will saaave yoooou. But… yoouuu were neeeever alone…



                                          The movement in the blackness of an unlit room. The whispers you hear before you drift off to slumber when home alone. These are the signs of the Creeps. They are the shadows of primeval night that dwell in the periphery of your perceptions. They have always been there and they will always be there. The Creeps come from the corners that mankind cannot perceive, the corners that no one can point towards. At the feet of the Elder Dark the Creeps spent a timeless epoch before the dream of light until their masters were banished by the Lords of the Dreaming. But they have always had hiddens spaces to dwell in as the world is not wanting for dark places.



                                          According to the Creeps they were not successfully banished like the rest of the Thallain. They whisper truths about shadows never dying and darkness being eternal. The Kithain believe this to be a cart of horse apples however, as they rarely had reliable accounts of Creeps being encountered until after the rest of the Thallain’s numbers swelled with the opening of the Eye of Balor. The Creeps will congregate into colonies known as galleries, and it's not unusual for other tenebrious thallain to sometimes be found in their company such as Boogies, Gobelins, Ghasts, and Bodachs, as well as the most unsavory of Slaugh. While Creeps do not usually find it easy to lead other fae leaders may emerge within a gallery, or fae of the dark. With their views on inevitable and omnipresent dread and doom they find service to more ambitious lords of darkness an understandable fit.



                                          Where Sprites inspire dreams of hope and joy, Creeps drain joy, and strangle hope. Creeps orchestrate nihilism, anxiety, despair and paranoia in their targets. They watch from unseen angles and gaslight and isolate their victims leading them to a life of ruin, and leaving numbness in their wake. They are prone to obedience towards suitably dominant leaders among other Thallain or even Kithain who can cow them with sheer will and terrifying displays of power.


                                          Appearance:

                                          The Fae Mien of Creeps are bodies of faded homogeneous shadow. Their eyes are twin portals of pale white or some soft hued light of an eerie color. Their mouths are simple yet wide gaping maws of the same shade as their eyeholes. Creeps often go nude making their bodies appear as a silhouette, or they wear billowy robes to conceal their features lending the semblance of shadows garbed in funeral shrouds. It is most common for creeps to either possess no hair upon their body or to have their body overflow with long rippling waves of sable hair, the two styles are reflected in all forms of their body hair such as beards or eyebrows being absent or thick. Their fingers and tongues taper into curled whisps, as do their toeless feet.


                                          Their bodies and shadows appear to be one, and even when gazed at with unenchanted eyes their shadows appear to languidly wreathe their bodies rather than trail behind them as a long twin. Their voices are peculiar to the Creep, some sound more like a chorus of voices, others a sibilant whisper, conversely some only speak with a sonorous din.

                                          Lifestyles:

                                          Before the Shattering the Creeps did not have want, or agency, or need. With the banishment of Glamour from the Autumn World the Creeps had desire foisted upon them, and they resent existence for the burden of awareness and being. Creeps are empty and therefore must sap purpose from others just as a snowball leeches warmth from the bare hand that rolls it. As a side effect of draining hope from those they stalk they cause anxiety and paranoia as they cause an undeniable sense of being watched. Without being dominated by stronger personalities they will eventually cluster with other Creeps they encounter, otherwise they seem prone towards servitude towards ambitious creatures that share the darkness with them. Creeps seem to imprint upon beings such as Sevartal Shadowcourt agents, wicked counts of House Balor, vicious Beastie Pack Leaders, and even crazed mortal dreamers bearing omens from the Elder Dark.



                                          Some unfortunate Creeps dwell in isolation from other supernatural beings, and spend their time under the beds and in the closets of mortal children, literally shaping their childhood fears around their very presence. Creeps given freedom will stalk mortals that gain their interests, they will smell their essence from their hair unnoticed while they sleep in their beds, they will take their garments to remind them of their being, some will even obsess and collect samples of their nails, used tissues, and even waste. They are empty and alone and their instincts know nothing else.



                                          Childling:


                                          In their most juvenile stage Creeps start completely lost. Creeplings as they are known, are confused, but mostly passive, the simplest reaction to their emptiness is to observe, yet they have an unrefined self preservation skill set, so they prioritize their curiosity above stealth. This often leads them into frustration and anger as they quickly discover most mortals react quite strongly to being stalked. Unless discovered by a shepherding force, many novice Creeplings can be weeded out at this stage of development. Even passive infatuation and observation by Creeplings can have devastating effects on the psyche as the mortals invariably stumble upon a disturbing discovery that they are never alone.



                                          Wilder:


                                          In their Wilder stage Creeps are at their most frantic and a thrall to their emptiness. Their confusion lessens and their skill at finding and stalking prey is refined. Those of this Seeming are simply called Creepers, and they refine their voyeuristic palate and narrow their “projects” to targets they find most satisfying. Creepers reveal their utility to their masters through successful indulgence of their habits, and accumulation of information useful to whatever nefarious purpose they are tied to.



                                          Grump: The eldest Creeps eventually master their emptiness and approach their ancient state of being from the first primeval night, that of triumphing over want. Their chosen approaches towards targets have been honed to mechanical perfection, they are never witnessed unless they so choose to distinguish themselves from the shadows. To the targets of their infatuation they are Whispers in the night. If they desire to provoke mounting paranoia, or deliver the terms of a target's execution, Whispers will never reveal themselves, only letting their victims know what the Whisper desires them to know. Through their mastery they do not exist, they are only Whispers in the night.



                                          Affinity: Actor

                                          Revelry:

                                          The emptiness the Creep osmosis’ glamour from the being and reality of a target stalked and surreptitiously observed. The art of moving unseen, taking a memento of their borrowed reality such as a sock, or lock of hair, or spending a night under the bed of an anxious victim is the closest a Creep will feel to being real and having a heart. As long as they are not caught they touch shadows for at least a moment during their viewing they will sup upon their voyeurism like a sumptuous banquet of envy.


                                          Unleashing:

                                          The Creeps eldritch magic is virtually anti-Unleashing. To return to the primordial naught and release the gelid breath of never is an act of null. The most fundamental aspects of reality fade, ambient luminosity dims to dappled shadow. Whispers ever behind you slither into your ears. Hackles rise as the feeling of being watched cannot be shaken. Unobserved objects appear to tremble as if alive and movement out of the periphery of vision catches the attention of a panicked mind. Anxiety nips at one’s heels ultimately crescendoing into a bleeding of hope leaving one’s heart a withered vessel empty of all but nihilism. The scent of everything but one's own dread leaves the air.

                                          Endowments

                                          Darkness’ Shadow:

                                          As beings of void and shadow when in a location of at least dim room level or under enough shadow to cover over half their body creeps are silent and unseen, Creeps gain a -4 difficulty to avoid notice and -2 difficulty to dodge, in all but the brightest of environments they still have at least a -2 to avoid detection. By spending a point of glamour they embrace their nothing and can travel under doors or cracks that a quarter can fit through, as well as being weightless enough to crawl upon the walls or ceiling, through the water, or through rough terrain as if it was as simple as traveling through a flat terrain, this lasts for a whole scene. If a point of willpower is spent while in this state they become more chimerical then material and can avoid all mundane damage from beings that cannot perceive chimerical reality unless it is fire or light based attacks. Fire and light will do an extra two health levels of whatever damage they inflict upon the Creep as much of their form of gossamer shadow is banished by the light which defined Creation so long ago.
                                          Stuff of Nightmares:

                                          Creeps drain the being from those they stalk through their revelry, along with the glamour drained, they steal a point of the targets willpower. If they are already full of willpower, or choose to forgo accepting the willpower point then it is transmogrified into a dark chimera symbolic of the target and or the Creeps fears. This Chimera starts as comparable to a one dot Chimera except they receive one level of aggravated damage when touched by direct sunlight, and they take two extra levels of damage if damaged by flame. These Mares of the Night will obey the Creep until the next sunrise, after that their inherent fear of the sunlit lands frees them of their loyalty and causes them to flee from the Autumn World into the Dreaming, or flee to an appropriately dark Freehold or Glade, or even underground/underwater far away from the kiss of sun or flame.



                                          If the revel that birthed the Mare of the Night lasted over an hour of observation treat the resulting being a Chimera one background point higher. If over eight hours are spent observing unseen raise the chimera by one more level as it gestates longer in the darkness held between the Creep and target. If the target was sleeping raise the level one step further as their literal nightmares are coaxed from their hearts to feed the monstrous new being. If the revelry took place within a liminal space touching upon the supernatural (such as a Crossroads at night, a freehold, a cemetery, a caern, or deep below a cavern under the earth. the inauspicious birth is even more potent, raising the Mare of the Night chimera another level. If used on a supernatural, the chimera is stronger still through the targets connection to the world hidden from mankind. Finally while a Creep cannot absorb more glamour or will from extra victims multiple targets observed under the same time can suffer the ill effects of the Endowment used upon them and add more fears to empower the ritual of the chimera’s birth. It is possible for a Creep to tame a particular Mare of the Night they crafted, guarantee a bit of their own fear, shadow and heart rests within its being. If the Mare of the Night is born with more then five levels of the Chimera background treat each level added past five as if the Chimera has ten more chimera points added to their creation pool.


                                          Chimera built with more then four levels worth of the background will not obey the Chimera unless they roll a Manipulation + Greymare roll, difficulty of three plus the accumulated Chimera levels, to bind them for the night. Creeps are naturally at a +4 difficulty to resist intimidation by Mares of the Night or any of the chimera’s Redes that are turn upon their creator, as they are beings touched by their own fears stitched together with fragments of their true name. Creeps may choose to divide the accumulated levels of the Chimera background created by the Endowment into multiple Mares of the Night instead, and very Creeps are ambitious enough to try to weave more than three dots worth of Chimera levels unless ordered to by a superior.


                                          When observing anyone in person unseen for at least one scene the targeted beings are prone to anxiety. For the next scene the dread within victims cause them +1 difficulty to rolls to resist fear or any willpower rolls they make. The Stuff of Nightmares Endowment works upon supernatural beings, but they are much more dangerous targets, and the Creep has no choice but to avoid notice if they wish to survive making such enemies. While animals may suffer the fear and anxiety they do not feed the Creeps hunger or add to the creation of chimera. Supernatural beings may be a source of Glamour or Willpower for a Creep but they may still lose a point of Willpower. Vulnerabilities

                                          Dark Heartless Nobody:

                                          The empty nothing of the Creeps smother their mortal hearts in darkness. The hollow spirit of the Creep gnaws at their heart and causes them a -1 penalty to all their dicepools due to the phantom pain in their chest if they spend more then two nights without basking in the unnoticed essence of the real beings. They will also not be able to regain willpower through rest. Only intelligent creatures will suffice to ease this pain, and every other night the penalty grows -1 worse. Once it reaches -5 they start to suffer a level of aggravated Damage a night. Creeps possess one less health level due to their lack of self, and crippled hearts. They have a +1 difficulty in resisting orders or persuasion from other Thallain or the Dark-kin.
