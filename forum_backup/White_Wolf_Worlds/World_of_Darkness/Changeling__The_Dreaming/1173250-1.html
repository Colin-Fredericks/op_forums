                        Re-reading that chapter in C20, I can't see anything about the Faerie Blood Merit. I think it's specific to other gamelines. Ergo, having the Merit doesn't make you kinain, it just makes you a blood relative of a faerie. Not all faeries' relatives get the benefits of being kinain. You only get the watered down benefits listed in the relevant core book for the being you're playing.

                        If you want to be a full-blooded kinain, as it were, you'd start with the C20 rules but wouldn't be able to Awaken or become a werewolf. You probably can't become Imbued, either, as your connection to the Dreaming would taint you. You could become a vampire, but you'd lose your kinain powers, although you may become Maeghar if your ST is *very* generous. You potentially could become a Sorcerer, though, and you could become a ghoul.

                        I don't think it's enough to buy the Faerie Blood Merit and then apply the kinain template on top.

                                                        A tenchomancer is somebody who's entire way of working magic is scientific. They are not an end-user, and while you can supplement technology with mystic magic, a technomancer is all about making better technology. In another words, they're all about the science. The Technocracy is quite literally the defining standard of the technomancer. Everything goes in neat, orderly boxes that are clearly labeled. But, not every technomancer is like that. They live for the joy of finding or building something new, neat and cool. There's an entire Tradition full of those guys, but they aren't the only technomancers in the Traditions. Though the "traditional" technomancers of the Traditions are the Virtual Adepts, who could care less about the fae, and the Sons of Ether, who think they're all extinct. There are other technomancers in the Crafts and Traditions, but those are the big groups.
