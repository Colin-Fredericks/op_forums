                    05-13-2017, 07:19 PM
                    A credits switch, plus some alterations, additions, and deletions to characters in the notable members section of a few houses that got a little mixed up in drafts, rather than true errata (more to follow):

                    Playtesters (Credits Page): Drop Pete Woodworth from playtesters, add Stained Glass Hearts.
                    House Danaan (125): "Notable members" section - "Lady Kyra" should be "Lady Jillian"
                    House Danaan (125): "Notable members" section - Add "Huntsman Jonakr, trodwalker guide"
                    House Fiona (131): "Notable members" section - Add "Dame Moria, champion duelist"
                    House Leanhaun (133): "Notable members" section - Change "Baroness Robin, the elusive hostess of notoriously decadent "White Wraith" parties catering to exclusive fae and mortal clientele" to "Baroness Robin, the elusive hostess of the notoriously decadent and exclusive "White Wraith" high society parties"
                    House Leanhaun (133): "Notable members" section - Add "Baron Tarrow, peerless fine arts patron"
                    House Scathach (136): "Notable members" section - Drop sentence beginning "Still, even in a secretive house..." it's not really needed, as we're listing famous Scathach aplenty.
                    House Scathach (136): "Notable Members" section - Drop "Lady Grear, notorious one-eyed Shadow Court assassin" and replace with "Sir Bobby Angel, freelance champion for hire"
                    House Scathach (136): "Notable Members" section - Change "Kit, true rank unknown, a pooka of the Seven League Knights with a knack for using trods to make surgical strikes on corrupt political systems" to "Kit and Kyra, true ranks unknown, pooka sisters of the Seven League Knights who target corrupt political systems"
                    House Scathach (136): "Notable Members" section - Add "Baron Bray and Baroness Aife, procurement and intelligence specialists,"

                    I recognize those sections might be pressed for space, so I tried to balance additions with deletions, especially for houses that were already cramped. If they can't all make it, well, no worries. More to follow later, especially on the sample character creation front (though I see people already caught some of those in previous posts).

                        The Hsien section has been a pleasant surprise. They have been given a considerable space, and the developer have put work in doing a good revision.

                        Even with that, I have found that they are not clear enough. Please note that I am not a native English speaker, so somethings could be misunderstandings. Sorry for that. Anyway, this is a list of what I think is missing or could be better explained.
                        • As some have pointed, there are no rules of changing forms. Does it cost yugen? Which effects apply? Which traits animal forms have?
                        • In the previous edition, the Hsien could heal themselves spending Yugen, but I have found nothing here. How do they soak different damage types?
                        • As in the previous edition, I find the Rank trait quite useless. Won't be better to just use the Title background?
                        • Page 423: "Leadership is now a Talent rather than a Skill". But in page 163, Leadership is listed as talent, as in other 20th anniversary editions.
                        • Page 424: Willpower costs 2 freebies, but since Revised edition, willpower usually costs only 1.
                        • I have not found the Yugen cost (if any) of a single I chih. In page 432, in cohorts, you find "Using multiple Alchemies in a single effect is referred to as a cohort, and costs one Yugen per Alchemy in addition to whatever is needed to generate the effect", but I can't find the "whatever needed to generate the effect".
                        • Page 431: "The dice pool is formed by combining the level of the I Chih being used with the relevant Attribute and the appropriate Chi value, Yin or Yang depending on the nature of the effect." What is the relevant attribute? Above, you can find two attributes per element, but which one is relevant for the roll is something not explained (at least not without a character sheet). You add the level of the I Chih, or the dots you have in the alchemy? Do you both add the dice of the chi value and use the chi value to calculate the difficulty? A single example of an I Chih, how you build the dice pool, and the cost would be great.
                        • Page 431: "Some effects are purely centered in Yin or Yang, and must use that Chi value". Only some? Are there neutral effects, neither yin or yang? How do you build the dice pool and calculate the difficulty?
                        • In previous edition, Water Alchemy was able to manipulate air. Nothing is said here.
                        • Page 433: Direction fortune. Must the target be in the auspicious direction from me or must I be in the auspicious direction from the target? In previous editions, player usually understand the former, but the book stated the latter. Here nothing is said about it.
                        • Page 434: "Speak to Mortals: [...] Humans are made of the same elemental matter and bear the same elemental souls that beasts, plants, and stones do, and as a result Hsien may, through the use of alchemy, address any human and be understood." Does that mean Hsien can talk to animals too? Should it be explicitly said?
                        • Can the Hsien use their magic to heal other beings? In the previous edition it was listed as an example of a yang effect, but then it was never found in the I Chih.
                        • Page 431: Degree fortune uses bloomering a flower as an example. How would you do that with alchemies?
