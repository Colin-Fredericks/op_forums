                                  Well, it means that they finally have a Developer (Congrats, Matt!)
                                  My guess is that, based on the more recent KickStarters, they will develop the book to the point of near-completeness (at least in writing) so that there's a shorter time between the KickStarter and the release of the preview PDF (of course, any content added to the main book itself will always add more time to the production of the PDF too.)
                                  That gives them about 6-9 months to do the KickStarter, and then 3 or 4 months to finish the writing/art direction/lay-out and get us the PDF. Then another 2 months of corrections and the final PDF/PoD. That could take us into April, or May of 2016.

                                  Of course... then we have the physical production of the Deluxe Edition... depending on how specs go, and production goes, that could take another several months after the final PDF is approved. So I'm going to say Summer of 2016 at the earliest, Fall of 2016 is probably a better bet.

                                  Of course, things could be more streamlined by then, but that's just my really rough estimate. :-/

                                  NOTE: I do not have any inside information, and am not affiliated with OPP. These are just my guestimates based on what I recall from involvement and watching other KickStarters.
