                                I decided on my own timeline and the causal events.

                                This one is distinct by turning the previous dates and explanations into legends. Humans were never the cause of dimensional changes, as Banality and Glamour always coexisted in our lives. Reason, science, metal working, all those are sources of Wonder, how could them be the end of the Dreaming?

                                No. The causes are cosmological. Not in the heavens, but beyond them. Few Changelings today know what happened, pieced through a lot of careful research around the world. The dates are supported by data, which is hard for most to collect without attracting Banality, but it is the birthright of the scientific mind to find Wonder in mundane connections.

                                For the rest, the legends are history as their minds are trapped between the unreliable state of time in the Dreaming and the confusing memories of so many rebirths.

                                And it actually was as follows, or so the data points:

                                ~7500 - 7000 BCE

                                Theoretical first event. Nothing is really known, but the later patterns raise the question. During this time were built Nabta Playa and Goseck Circle, oldest astronomically oriented known megaliths. It may be nothing. It may be a lot.

                                ~1400 - 1300 BCE

                                Oracle bones, the oldest writing system of the region known now as China, registered the Shang Dynasty Supernova, an abnormally bright new star near Antares that appeared suddenly, and after some years vanished. The first pockets of what would become the Dreaming appear scattered. Their formation is irregular and slow, but spikes of growth happen during eclipses and the rare other Nova event.

                                The process lasts for about a thousand years, with the Dreaming as we know finally taking form around 300 BCE, as the light of another, 300 years long Supernova, fades.

                                For about 1400 years the situation was stable and similar to the modern world, and the Fae societies establish their Trods and Freeholds.

                                1138 - 1578 AD

                                End and beginning of the process known as The Shattering. In this order, more or less. The investigation needed magic to understand what happened, and it revealed a time anomaly of huge proportions.

                                The first, unnoticed, Trod closed in Aleppo during an earthquake of great intensity. Others would follow, but few at first. The chains of fate don't link the events, but link both to something yet to happen. The closings really get momentum in 1202, when a titanic earthquake felt across the world hits Syria. If a few scattered Fae were wondering about lost Trods, now it really became a known thing.

                                Arcadia shuts its Gates in 1572, under the ominous light of a new star, the brightest ever seen in recorded History. Another Supernova, recorded by Tycho Brahe, shocks the foundations of the Dreaming so violently the energy can barely disperse forward and instead goes back, retroactively causing the Shattering only 5 years before its last herald arrives.

                                For the next 6 years the remaining Fae have to decide between risking a life in the wild, unmoored Dreaming, or shield against Banality in the more stable Earth. In 1577 the arrival of the Great Comet, also studied by Brahe, starts to close the last wild Trods, finishing in the following year.

                                1578 - 1908 AD

                                Known as the Interregnum, this period sees the widespread use of the Changeling Way. This is not any kind of protection: living a mortal life brings forgetfulness to the Fae, if it wasn't part of them before. It's not just Banality, but a loss that adds up each new life.

                                By the end, the Changelings have formed a new mythology based on the influence of human society and academics post Renaissance. That means they embrace the tale of the Dark Ages, of humanity losing dream to fear, religion and reason. A new society has established itself by the dawn of the 20th century.

                                1908 - 1957 AD

                                The reopening of the Trods is violent, although its occurrence is lost to most Changelings. Not that they didn't notice the cause. No one could in the Northern Hemisphere, how could it be when the light of day could be seen at midnight in some places?

                                In the Russian wilds of Tunguska, the greatest meteor impact in known History pierced the sky and reality itself, bridging the three realms with effects felt and seen in Arcadia.

                                The gates aren't open right away. A few Fae in the lost Dreaming, mostly deranged, cross in to Earth or arrive at the gates of Arcadia. A few of the Sidhe also get past the gates. But overall the trickling of Fae into Earth without Arcadian support is to small and hard to track back.

                                1957-1975 AD

                                Our last event isn't really a process, but blurred by the laws of the Dreaming. During the main years of the Space Race the Gates of Arcadia are opened. At which point it happened and how long it lasted is impossible to know, since time isn't linear near the Fae Realm.

                                The stretch of time covers at least from the launch of Sputnik 1 to the Moon Landing, though it is suspected to also cover as early as R-7 Semyorka and Venera 9.

                                Its important to be clear: the Space Race is used to define its boundaries, but the cause is actually unknown and possibly almost mundane, as the opening wasn't a natural event, but a deliberate action.
