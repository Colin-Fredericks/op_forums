                                Adhene (and chimera) killed each other for thousands of years in Deep Dreaming. You know, how big is their Banality score?

                                Zero.
                                Those weren't changelings, though. Changelings are so vulnerable because they have dual souls. Other fae are free to be murderous bastards because their inherent nature is entirely glamorous. Changelings are like magnets for Banality because of their human souls. Actions that would otherwise be meaningless to other fae are thus devastating to their own fragile balance.

                                Killing other changelings is Banal partly because you, and the Dreaming, know that changeling souls are a finite resource in the mortal world. Chimerae in the Dreaming, meanwhile, are plentiful, and when they die, they re-enter the natural cycle. The Glamour released by a dead chimerae gets transferred to something else. Most chimerae are fleeting and insubstantial anyway. They're meant to be created and die rapidly. Whereas the immortal fae soul killed forever in the mortal world, being already half severed from the Dreaming, is just destroyed. Lost forever in an increasingly Banal world.
