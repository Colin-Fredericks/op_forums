    Originally posted by Katsura View Post

    I mean, I see one of original motives of OChangeling that "growing up" is not exactly good thing as a form of escapism and rejection of the world we live in. I would not say I'm most mature person (I'm eccentric, I will admit), but for one I don't see a progression to more adult and grounded behavior as something negative. But that's opinion.
    Depends on your definition of 'growing up' The definition is subjective. If "growing up" means not only do you put away childish things as the saying goes, but you also stop laughing in delight or having any kind of wonder or joy in your life and it's all seriousness with a 9-5 job, stress and conforming to expectations, then yeah.. that is bad. That's what banality is. Some people choose to live that way and they like it. Good for them. That doesn't mean all of us have to live that way. In the real world or in a game. That's what Changeling is about. Not that they are trying to reject the world we live in, but reject the idea that it has to be dull, boring and serious all the time because it's the "grown up" ideal that everyone has to be. Changeling basically makes it ok for people to be eccentric without being called crazy and put in the nut house or given medication to make them robotic zombies who don't do anything outside society's expectations. If you've ever seen the movie Equilibrium, that's a really good example of the kind of thing Changelings are dreading will happen and that's what they are fighting against. Adult and grounded behavior is all well and good, as long as you can remember how to have fun, be silly and laugh at yourself once in a while. All in moderation.

    And maybe this should be a separate discussion and not in this thread. I hope this answers your questions though.

                                                    Originally posted by Obyrne View Post
                                                    It varies. Kindred are nearly all awful for Changelings - most of them are unchanging and cold/calculating. They're basically the embodiment of Banality (which shreds Lings).
                                                    I think Changelings have groups in each of the games with which they could work well. Mage offers very unique interactions for Changlings - especially with the Cult of Ecstasy, Verbena, and OMG the Marauders... Vampires of the more creative side, such as Toreador, Tzimisce, and the Daughters of Cacophony, could work very well - especially for Dark Court. Werewolves should have no trouble integrating the servants of the Wyld into a positive framework. Wraith would be slightly more difficult - but the Keeners are an expression of Changeling values. I have no trouble with the Sluagh holding a seance for their personal kind of reverie. Then there is the Urmen - for those of you who remember the five seeds linking everything together as one - How much more Changeling can one get that that? I would like a little better tie in with the other games in a new Changeling - but it is not like the warp and weft are not prepared.

                                                    The game to me always had an incredible dynamic to work on: How does one balance Subversion of Self for the needs of Society while keeping the Expression of Self for the needs of Self-Actualization. The balance point is the Changeling version of Golconda. While Changeling has the 'highlight of the party' image - that is a very dark and dangerous party. The problem with Creative Wisdom is that exposure to it is brief, blinding, universe changing, and ephemeral. The instant in which the entire possibility of the universe is exposed and immediately fades because it is too great to be comprehensible. The Dream is both inspiring and the narcotic which the junkie can never have again. For vampires Golconda is a desired convenience and relief from the world. For Changeling that balance is the sanity that makes expression in the world both possible and effective in the manner that endures.

                                                            Things I'd love to see:

                                                            Kithbook: Boggan
                                                            Kithbook: Piskie (they ARE a normal Commoner Kith, after all, even if not in the core book)
                                                            An in-depth look at Nunnehi Freeholds (and Freeholds in general)
                                                            An in-depth look at Nunnehi, including Central and South America
                                                            A look at Gallain and other Changelings in Africa and other places
                                                            Another look at Fae animals (steeds and hounds are mentioned in the Appalachia book, but I think that so much MORE could be done with it)
                                                            The Korred from the French book brought into the main stream
                                                            A look at the Inanimae mentioned under House Varich
                                                            A look at the Nymphs mentioned in some books
                                                            A complete re-structuring of Actor and Nature to make more sense and be more usable.
                                                            Another look at how chimera and treasures work.
                                                            A re-design of the silly "by the time you hit 30 you're so jaded you can't possibly believe in magic" dynamic. Expanding the range of Grump and Wilder would really work better.
                                                            A better look at Legacies and Courts. The book often states that Seelie isn't good and Unseelie isn't evil, but then does so much to undermine that, such as having pretty much exclusively negative, horrible Legacies available for the Unseelie and having Thallain, who have TWO Unseelie Legacies, just to show how extra-special evil they are. I think focusing more on "Law and Order" vs "Freedom and Chaos" would work far better. Seelie can be just as nasty as Unseelie, and Unseelie can be heroic (there are even a few heroic Unseelie mentioned in books!)
