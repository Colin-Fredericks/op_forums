    Some people have had the idea that Eshu might be like the Laibon in that the Kithain viewed it as one Kith but they are a group of kiths. I'm hopeful we will get some African Kiths, though not sure if they will be in that Empire of the Sphinx. We should also get Maori Gallain, and Australian Gallain, and either Expand the Nunnehi with Central and South American Kiths, since they are supposed to represent North and South America or they will just introduce a South American Gallain group.

    Another group of Kiths I will add to the Yokai are the Electric Shadows. One Kith with multiple subkiths that represent Silver Screen Tropes. So to start with I'm thinking a Cowboy, Samurai, Gangster, Buffoon, Beauty, and maybe a Mad Scientist as well. With a Thallain Slasher. They will be small in number and a newer kith maybe from the resurgence number just a few dozen at first and slowly growing. I'm thinking they are the result of Unnamed Changelings who imprint on potent and prolific chimera of movie trope derived glamour. Possibly they could even be the result of reborn Unnamed Changelings who instinctually tap and merge with the Glamour of a movie they are experiencing. They wouldn't be limited to Japan, probably starting in Hong Kong and spreading first to the Yokai territories and then quickly to Kithain territory. They would be so rare that they would be dispersed and not concentrated though. Eventually congregating in Hollywood, and then Bollywood and Tokyo.

        Right then so a confession: I've actually been working on my own take on Japanese changelings. For my end of things, I was mostly going off of the "regional kiths" of kithain. The alternative would have been homebrewing a unique society for Japanese changelings including coming up with their own magic system (if I didn't want to just crib the hsien's systems which even then...well...I'm one of the guys who is not a fan of WoD framing all of the supernatural stuff in Asia through a Chinese lens)...so not doing that just yet.

        Also, instead of calling them "yokai" I went with calling them "kami".

        Some of my takes include:

        -Kappa - Mischievous, but surprisingly polite aquatic imps that live diving Japan's rivers and seas to plumb its secrets. (water sluagh)

        -Tengu - Half-crow, half-goblin tricksters that delight in pranking, and sometimes terrorizing, humanity. (avian redcaps)

        -Issun - Small, doll-like kami that love socializing and falling in love with humanity and going on adventures. (alternatively shapeshifting piskies)

        -Zashiki-warashi - Japanese house spirits, they bring great luck to any household they make residence in. (boggans)

        Name changes/re-skins:

        -Obake: Japanese version of pooka. All mischievous animal shapeshifters in one kith: tanuki dogs, foxes, rabbits, etc are here. Otherwise, it never made much sense to me to separate them into their own kiths, since I feel like there wasn't enough to differentiate them the same way that, say, the selkies are distinct from pookas.

                                                        Quixote Syndrome is (or was in the 1st edition material about humans who were potential threats to Changelings) a psychiatric/psychological diagnosis to explain the "delusions" suffered by Changelings (being that mundane humans, especially Autumn People, can't see anything of the Dreaming, so clearly Changelings must be insane and need to be put in mental institutions for their own safety).
