                Hmm.

                Alternate Western Europe in 1500 AD
                • Kingdom of Scotland under James IV, a land mixing Renaissance philosophy, learning and art with ties to ancient faerie powers who serve the great goddess Cailleach, a rising navel power in the north, full of selkies, water spirits, and clans of werewolves, as well as Awakened practitioners of the the arts of spirit-talking, fate-weaving, and seidr, and Enlightened navigators.
                • Princedom of Wales, still ruled by the decedents of Llywelyn ap Gruffudd, but nominally vassals of the King of England.
                • Kingdom of England, under the rule of the fae-tied Tudors and Henry VII or the evil and potentially undead or otherwise quasi-immortal Richard III
                • Island of Hibernia, a land of multiple kingdoms and city states, including the still Nordic-cultured Dublin, and it's own Celtic Church (the papal bull authorizing Henry II of England to bring the land under the thumb of Rome never being issued), holding as many faerie kings and there are mortal ones.
                • The Grand Duchy of Anjou, consisting of Brittany and northeastern France (Pays de la Loire)
                • Kingdom of France, in reality Normandy, Paris, Tours, Orleans and the rest of the Loire Valley area.
                • Kingdom of Burgundy, which includes Switzerland and bits of northern Italy and the German borderlands.
                • Kingdom of Occitan, in Southern France, a land still heavily influenced by traditions of chivalry and troubadours, as well as its odd Cathar monasteries and it's alternate Papacy in Avignon
                • Kingdom of Navarre, the Basque lands of northern Iberia and the edges of France
                • Kingdom of Aragon
                • Kingdom of Leon
                • Kingdom of Castile
                • Kingdom of Portugal
                • Kingdom of Naples and Sicily
                • Malta
                • Italy's Maritime Republics, the northern city states flush with money and full of Leonardo da Vinci inspired clockwork-punk advanced technology, alchemical sciences and masters of Ars Cupidae
                • Kingdom of Bohemia
                • The Hanseatic League
                • The Margraviate of Brandenburg
                • The Duchy of Bavaria
                • The Union of Kalmaris (most of Scandinavia)
