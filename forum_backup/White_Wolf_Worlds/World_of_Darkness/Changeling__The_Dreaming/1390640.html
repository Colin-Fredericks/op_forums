    Can't think of a good adventure

    05-21-2020, 06:46 PM
    I kind of want to run a changeling game where Arcadia is sort of open, the dreaming is strongly linked to the fate of things in the real world and changelings are actually secretly influencing mndane reality in massive ways while keeping the worst things from chaos from screwing up mundane reality. But I've been stumped for weeks now as how to run such an adventure. Mostly, I can't think of a nice enough epic story.

    So far all that comes to mind is something that ends up as a monster of the week as some Arcadian evil dude sends dangerous chimera to threaten the world.

    Any suggestions?

      Well you can definitely go a ton of different roads with that concept, I suppose the first thing that comes to mind for me though is that some Arcadian Sidhe noble, perhaps a bit Bedlam'd after long separation from reality, gets the idea to go back to earth and establish a fae nation using old school King Arthur/Fairy Tale style logic.

      Sending chimera to maraud, seeking out virtuous youths to put through trials to prove their noble bloodlines, seeking out young nobles and capturing them to provide as rewards for those who pass the trials and providing mystic relics for them to establish their own domain with the Sidhe as their mystical patron.

      Problem is, that doesn't fly in the 21st century, so it's up to some changelings to rein this in before things get out of hand, but knowing that they can't just kill the Sidhe without permanently removing the Dream that they represent from the world and drawing Winter closer.

              I would determine a "tentpole" to hang your chronicle on. That would be the chronicle metaplot - the plot element you keep going back to throughout your chronicle.

              In addition to that, you can think up a series of different Changeling elements that you want to introduce.

              That should quickly fill up your game sessions. Think of it as the difference between the "mythology" episodes and "monster of the week" episodes in the X-Files. Some advance the metaplot. The others are just fun single stories that explore the setting.

              In terms of appopriate chronicle tentpoles for Changeling, here are some ideas.

              Feuding Houses of the Sidhe that will eventually lead to a chronicle wide civil war among the Fae. Early on, the PCs see the seeds planted of the conflict, and as time goes by they see relations deteriorate, the two Houses building its forces for a showdown, and the final conflict erupting near game's end. For inspiration think of similar war stories in myth and legend - Greeks vs Trojans, Romeo & Juliet, Arthur vs Mordred, or the Roman civil wars between forces of the patricians vs populists. All of those have very different origins, but include many elements of drama and tragedy.

              A revenge filled drama of a Cursed Dauntain using Banality as a weapon against a major portion of the Fae (who side with his Oathsworm Enemy) for an actual or perceived wrong done to him. Most likely some kind of spurned or betrayed lover. He'd leave a bloody trail of vengeance as he hunts down and destroys those who opposed him. To make this one guy a major threat, you'd need for him to recruit henchmen and allies. Better if the guy can be seen as sympathetic in a way to show how he could retain the allegiance of such allies, or why Fae society in general don't try to stop him until too late. Early on the PCs would get to know him and fight alongside him, become his friends. Then they'd see the nature of his "betrayal" that embitters him. Perhaps even defend him from his enemies. Then he disappears and odd rumors begin to turn up about him. Maybe the PCs investigate or maybe they don't. Maybe the Dauntain attempts to recruit his old friends (the PCs) or not. Inspirations would be any kind of revenge oriented dramas like Hamlet, Lord Soth in Dragonlance, the quest to return Helen to her husband in the Troy myth, Anakin's story in Star Wars as he becomes Darth Vader, or any revenge story like Point Blank or Kill Bill. In order to provide a real plot for the PCs to interact with, you really need to build some kind of organization around the Dauntain. Maybe fellow Sidhe who have sworn an oath to him and have to fulfill it, even though he's using Banality. Perhaps they even intend to kill him AFTER he's achieved his vengeance, but in the meantime feel obligated to him. Or perhaps they swear the Oath too that makes them Dauntain. Or he's secretly supported by more villainous forces.

              A conspiracy of the Shadow Court to achieve some end in the setting. Maybe assassinate the ruling Sidhe to throw the local court in chaos. Maybe steal a powerful Fae treasure that once unleashed will have terrible consequences (intentional or unintentional). Maybe kidnap someone and take them to their Fomorian master. Maybe destroy a famous oathbound circle of knights whose motley is essential to keeping the peace. Who can have them doing all sorts of things. PCs need to encounter various events (some of which they stop, others they don't, some they simply observe or hear about, and sometimes their actions are what the conspiracy wanted), and slowly put together the clues. Perhaps with an NPC showing up later and informing them of everything they missed or guessed wrong before dying - leaving them to convince everyone else of the truth, find any missing links, and save the day. Inspirations - Raiders of the Lost Ark, the Surtur War in Walt Simonson's Thor comics from the 1980s, Oliver Stone's "version" of the JFK assassination, Alan Moore's From Hell (comic and movie) about the secret conspiracy behind the Jack the Ripper murders, Seven Days in May, Invasion of the Body Snatchers, or the movie They Live.

              Pick one particularly Chimerical monster and build a plot around it. So instead of Sauron recruiting every evil bad guy he can find, it's just the Creeping Fear and build an entire "organization" around that kind of monster and assign them a goal. Or maybe its a plague of basilisks. Or a group of boogey men afflicting the dreams of children.

              Or a plot based on a particular horrible event. Maybe some group is killing all the unicorns out there, every single one. Or they've found a way to systematically destroy or corrupt a Freehold's balefire. Or going out and destroying the last vestiges of free dross in the Mortal world one by one. So rather than a generic bad guy doing generic bad guy things, there is a theme to the menace that might stop.

                  I have toooo many problems with having just toooo many ideas for changelings!

                  If I may how about the idea of an upswing in Chimera and Adhene worldwide, but maybe starting out in your setting location. You had the game set in Manila right?

                  So how about Chimera populations on the rise so commoners are demanding the Great Houses deal with it. So maybe in an executive decision they need to start taming Chimera by oathing the most sapient of them into fealty towards the Kingdom, and making even some of the greatest into Titled Squires.

                  So now Local Courts aren't just Nobles ruling Over Commoners with a handful of Kinain, Dreamers, enchanted servants, and Chimerical Familiar hanging around. Now Courts swell into literal miniature cities as a lot of low level chimera need to be given purpose and direction or chaos will ensue, or Nightmares Forces go and nab them first.

                  An idea talked about lately by myself is the idea what if there was a phenomena where humans turned into Chimera for unknown reasons, and when they do they are forgotten by their friends and Family, and thus are known as the Mist Taken. Start by maybe just using their mortal stats but they are Chimerical instead of material, or if you want give everyone 1-3 starting redes. But the big difference from the normal chimera is they have fully human minds emotions, and morality. But perhaps after years they start evolving based on their environment, so the sad and fearful hide in the dark and become wretches, the angry give in to their aggression and become monstrous. This would mean it could perhaps be beneficial to become a Citizen under a Great House so as to anchor them to a community.

                  Another poster had the idea of a Chimerical seed that blossoms into a flower that drains people's souls, I suggested this would be a good vector for the Mistaken Phenomenon, so their seeds drain you soul, treat like an Illness/Poison that you roll for every week or few days and it drains willpower points and then when empty drains dots, and when that is empty your soul is reborn as a Chimera.

                  I also wanted to introduce things like Inanimae from Space, like Meteors and Krofted Moonlanding debris, and Starlight, and Kiths based on Alien mythology like Greys and Reptilians.

                  And a Kith based off of Items of Legend are basically Fae Souls tied to Treasures. So Caliburn could have surfaced in the world while King David is gone and it could have found a host turning them in to one of the Chosen Kith, like how Selkies are tied to their Seal Coats, these Chosen are tied to their Mythic Item, whether Caliburn, Mjolnir, or Aladdin's Lamp. The Thallain version would be things from Horror like the Hook Hand of the Hook Hand Killer, Jason's Mask or the Eye of Balor.

                  And you could deal with connecting the different Kithain/Gallain groups. So maybe there is an Embassy in the City to Host the Merfolk and Hsien and Inanimae?

                  And another knew kith angle I used is the Electric Shadows, basically a handful of Changelings appeared seeming to be one off Kiths based around the dreams inspired by Cinema. So the First few were Gunslingers, Swashbucklers, and Beauties. Then by modern times they turned out to not be one offs and they were just rare representing new kiths born from the dreams of Movies, or possibly Inanimae of the Empire of Flickers undergoing the Changeling way.

                  How about the Local Nobles, lead by House Dougal, want to use Fae Magic and Science to found a Freehold in actual Space?

                  What if another wave of Fae return from Arcadia, not just Nobles but commoners, even new Kiths, like the Winged Monkeys of Oz. It turns out more of the Deep Dreaming's Denizens are in Exile as the Tuatha/Fomorian War annexes kingdoms of the Dreaming like Oz. Maybe the earlier waves like the Resurgence in 1969 was actually the annexation of more traditional Kingdoms like Avalon, Alfheim, and Anwyn.

                  I have even more ideas than these, these were just the ones I'm wanting to use most Urgently!



                  And why not full on have the Fomorians start to Wake up, as well as the Tuatha start showing their hand in bringing War to the Mortal World?
