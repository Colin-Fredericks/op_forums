    The Cast of "Peanuts" as Childling Changelings

    01-10-2016, 11:14 PM
    Just saw "The Peanuts Movie" and thought to myself
    What Kith would "the gang" fit into as Childling Changelings?

    Here we go:
    Charlie Brown: Seelie Boogan (Kind honest hard working Everyman who never EVER gives up )
    Snoopy: Seelie Beagle Pooka (A mischievous trickster and a good friend)
    Woodstock: Seelie Canary Pooka (Playful loyal avian friend)
    Sally Brown: Seelie Knocker (Lovable bossy little complainer and incurable romantic)
    Linus: Seelie Troll (Loyal, caring, patient, wise, honorable)
    Peppermint Patty: Seelie Satyr (Hyper energetic tomboy, stupid but well meaning)
    Marcie: Seelie Eshu (A font of warm calm esoteric wisdom)
    Schroeder: Seelie Sidhe (Dignified artistic chick magnet and poetic soul)
    Pigpen: Seelie Slaugh (Dirty messy but kind and misunderstood eccentric)
    Lucy: Unseelie Redcap (Hateful cynical sarcastic little bitch who gets off on bullying nice people)

    Say, that's quite an idea to kickstart (hehehe) a chronicle!

                  EUREKA!

                  Your post has just inspired me to figure out the PERFECT example of what a "Bunk" is!

                  By all rights, Snoopy should NOT be able to fly his doghouse and fire an invisible machine gun to do battle with his Archnemesis, The Red Baron.

                  And yet, through his "Bunk" of donning a red scarf, flight goggles, hopping on top of his Red Dog House while openly typing AND narrating "It Was A Dark And Stormy Night", he opens a gateway into childhood wonder that takes him AND his Doghouse into the *Narrative Reality* of the raging heroic skies of World War I, perfectly working Pretend (Chimerical if you will) Machine Gun and all.

                  THAT is what a Bunk Is.

                  A silly ritual that "breaks the fourth wall" and acknowledges the absurdity of dreams, thereby giving The Dreaming "permission" to flow through into the concious world. Snoopy is able to REALLY fly his Doghouse like a World War One Ace Flies his byplane because the through the NARRATIVE that his Bunk has written for his Cantrip, he has created a STAGE that allows this script/play to be real, and acted out as a (temporarily) living narrative that will end as soon as the curtains of reality close.

                  A "Bunk", in short, is the Pen/Typewriter in which you, the Director/Author of the Play/Story, can write and articulate the NARRATIVE that is the Cantrip on the Canvas/Paper of Banal-Reality so it is given on CREDIBLE context on which it can exist.
