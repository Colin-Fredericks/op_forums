      I think that's WHY so many people dislike the Kith. It represents some pretty ahh, antiquated ideas about sexuality and purity. Namely, it embodies the idea that sex in any form is always dirty and impure, a necessary evil at best. I'm not sure what it is exactly you want from a swan maiden, so I'm not sure what else I could recommend. Off the cuff you could do something similar by making a Sidhe with like, Metamorphosis? But it's not actually clear to me what it is you LIKE about the Kith. "Sexuality is bad" IS the Kith, so if you don't like that concept (I know I don't), I'm confused as to what it is about the Kith you enjoy. Beyond that, the Kith is a pretty tragic one; you can try to fight it (and if your story teller is playing correctly, you will fail) or you could lean into it I guess? Make a character whose Human nature is a modern person and views sexuality as an expression of romance, while the Kith side is the opposite? And those two natures, you know, DOOM the character? Those are like... the two stories I can see being told with a Swan maiden. Either you're pure and you like that, but die because Tragic Fate, or you're pure and you DONT like it... and die because Tragic Fate.

                                          Originally posted by Penelope View Post
                                          MythAdvocate Can you have more than one True Love in your life or is it literally like a once in a lifetime deal?
                                          Well now, THAT is a complicated question.

                                          True Love in the sense of Storytelling should be a beautiful and terrible thing. And while love and happiness can indeed happen many times, True Love is the kind of thing you never recover from. If lost, at best it should bring a happy ache and at worst terrible pain and hurt. This is especially true for beings touched by the Dreaming.

                                          In my own life, I have truly loved more than once. Some were great mistakes that hurt more than they brought joy. One seemed glorious and full of so much hoping and wonder that I thought would burst, but turned to hurt so deep that it changed me, in ways both bad and good. However, my wife and I have a different kind of love. It is passionate, loyal, steadfast and true. but it took work. There were the early days of heat, tempered with reality and devotion born of affection and shared determination. And she is my treasure.

                                          But which of these are True Love? The quick fire that burns and changes you, or the love that is forged and tempered?

                                          As for me, I prefer the latter.

                                                  Swan Maidens, Selkies, Piskies, they all live very specific lives that don’t mesh with every single character concept. You make the character to tell a specific story and that’s the point.                                                   If you want a more flexible character make a Sidhe or Eshu or something instead. Swan Maidens are tragedy magnets, if you don’t want that then it’s likely the kith isn’t right for the character. If you just want the superficial qualities yeah get a Pooka or Sidhe.
