                  A book about science, technology and futurism as sources of glamour and the dreaming, talking about the histories of invention and science fiction, including all it's variations such as Verne style science fantasy, steampunk, pulp era super science, silver age science fiction, and cyberpunk; changelings who deal with technology (like the Nocker Aethernauts) and who harvest glamour from inventive inspiration; sci fi and technology oriented realms in the dreaming (that can include inspiration from Space 1889 to TRON); technologically oriented treasures, chimera, and maybe Arts, and something that ties UFO lore into Changeling, including changelings who play with that motif (like Sluagh who pretend to be Greys), "alien" abductions, and a hunter group who think changelings are alien invaders.

                                  First we need books to finish things that were only minimally explained, so top of that is Inanimae, so far we only have really NPC rules for them, so nobody wants to make homebrew phyla because with incomplete rules it would be more fun to just make the homebrew a kith instead, like making Yuki Onna, people make them Kiths instead of Phyla because they are more fun as a kith. So not just flesh out the Splinters we have, we also need to make more, so playing them doesn’t feel like a watered down simplified experience.

                                  Same with other Gallain, so we need more on the Merfolk and their Houses, and more on the Nunnehi.

                                  And more on the Hsien, but also their setting!

                                  After the Books of Gallain we could use a Book of Dreams maybe to flesh out the dreaming but also treat as a players guide for higher level Fae beings and more info on Treasures and other Glamour things. But also have high level Magic’s that take a certain level of night to master, so things people learn after they learn other Arts, like Greater Arts and Greater Oaths. Hell I would love a Treasury book, like how you did book of Freeholds but dealing with Treasures, and more on the Gallain treasure. So make it like about 20 pages more then the book of freehold, hell I would buy a 200 page book on that myself!

                                  A Book of Nightmares for both the Creatures of Nightmares like Fomorians and Adhene, but also their unique barely touched section of the Dreaming.


                                  An Atlas book or series of books, I have a feeling that section of the Players Guide is gonna Explode. Maybe also treat as a book of minor Kiths or make that its own book, so you can touch on local Kiths like Hulda and chupacabra.

                                  Then Kithbooks for Clurichaun, Selkie and Piskies!


                                  Basically though I would love you to flourish out the Character options that were only introduced in the C20 book that had to be dumbed down or abbreviated. We never got to flesh out Inanimae Empires or more on the Adhene!

                                  I’ll pretty much gobble up anything Changeling right now, so I hope more rules on crossovers are in the Players guide if not that would be dandy. And more on fleshing out daily life, having families, and ruling kingdoms for the Fae.

                                  And books covering both the Hsien but also their region and how they interact with specific Local Fae. I feel Hong Kong, the Philippines and India for instance would be the real middle ground where Kithain, Hsien and local Gallain meet and live nearby.

                                  But really a Kingdoms book for India and Australia the most, and then Africa and South America!

                                              A Book of Treasures ("The Treasure Chest"?) would be pretty cool, especially if it included guidelines for making your own (both in game/character and out) and a bunch of legendary level ones you could build entire epic campaigns around.



                                              Also, just personally because I'm odd, I've long wanted a book similar to the science/technology book above dealing with religion. How changelings draw inspiration and glamour from it, religious oriented dreamers, what changelings who try to adhere to mortal faiths have to do, changeling specific faiths, and how religious groups - including the Society of Leopold - may view changelings. (I'd also like premade character templates, just because I've always been a big fan of them and Boggin Wiccan Coven Leader and Satyr Theologian are long time go to character concepts of mine.)

                                              Also a genre book on running Changeling as a horror game.
