                                                Originally posted by Firkraag View Post
                                                BTW, how new Changelings handle things, like characters being excited and inspired by science, reason and rationality, but considering knights, magic and fairy tales to be quite boring... unimagintive and even lazy.

                                                Could someome be both creative/imaginative and think, that changeling society is too full of clichés?

                                                Could an awakened kithain see the chimeric reality as patterns of math, interactions of particles and waves and complex webs of mystery, waiting to be unwoven?

                                                Or kithain are pretty much stuck in their 'fantasy' paradigms, like flies in amber?
                                                I'm guessing they could interpret what they see that way, although Changeling never went into the specific subjectivity of the Dreaming a la videres. I mean, yes, it's subjective, or rather it's responsive to the people passing through it, but I'm not sure it's the same as the Umbra where three mages with different paradigms and Essences could see slightly different things. I always assumed there was a kind of objective reality to it, which is the paradigm of myth and dream, or the collective unconscious writ large. Locally, this adapts to dreamers, but three changelings standing next to each other probably all see the same thing even if that thing is shaped by all three of them. If that makes sense?

                                                In which case, the science-loving fae might see balloons and bunny rabbits but understand them as wasted dreaming energy taking a form suggested by the observer, in much the same way heat is wasted energy in other reactions and processes, and in the same way waves and particles only become one or the other when observed. He might understand will-o'-wisps in terms of refraction and reflection, and Glamour in terms of the physics of consciousness. There's loads of fun there for a keen player. But, in essence, he's still seeing the same thing as the other characters--it's just a matter of how he interprets what he sees.

                                                Now, C20 may introduce something like videres for the Dreaming (or I may just have missed it), but I suspect this would be an extra layer of complication the game shies away from, just because players already sometimes struggle with the duality of real/chimerical as it is, without making everyone's version of chimerical reality also unique.

                                                        Yeah, Changeling and Mage share the idea that human belief determines reality. How the two games implement it is pretty different. In Mage, you can have two people view the same thing and potentially see something wildly different based on their respective paradigms.

                                                        In Changeling, two changelings or enchanted humans will always see the same dream realm or patch of chimeric reality as the same thing. The piece of the Dreaming they're both viewing will change over time as the local legends change though.

                                                        Something I've always thought was neat about the Dreaming is how certain archetypes keep appearing pretty much regardless of the dreams that shape the local area. As an example, dragons seem to be native to just about everywhere in the Dreaming. Even areas reflecting high tech dreams still have techo-dragons with laser wings. It's almost as though they've achieved a level of permanence in the Dreaming. How long does a dream have to endure to become a permanent fixture in the ever changing Glamour? Also, how ancient were the beings that first dreamed them into existence?
