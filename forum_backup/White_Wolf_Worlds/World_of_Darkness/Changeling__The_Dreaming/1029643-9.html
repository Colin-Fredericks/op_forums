                            When I choose between distraous consequences that promote Status Quo and those, which break it, one way or another, I always choose the latter option.

                            I just agrued with my friend about it and we came to conclusion, that for Technocracy it might mean, that Mists might work a bit differently. They just have enough on their plate to not investigate it. Like there might be few amalgams, that had noticed alarming signs, but they never get enough funding to investigate their claims. Yes, they detect something, even have somу statistics on their hands, but vampires and werewolves are more tangible and even considering, that there is no conspiracy, relatively harmless breed of reality deviants not a high priority. Not, that they won't turn their eye on them once they'll deal with other issues, that day doesn't seem to be close.

                            We even discussed an idea of cabal of ex-technocracy operatives, who were tasked to deal with whatever threat Mists(they didn't knew, what it is, just a "memetic virus" of some sort) posed, which they have decided, was so great, that they went rogue and came up with a procedure to 'enchant' themselves and waged a crusade on these alien infiltrators. And how that experience changed them. They might still think they fighting a war in a middle of hostile environment, but act a lot like other supernatural groups, a lot like, changelings themselves. Maybe, even their magic is changed to reflect that, maybe even slowly turning into Maradeur...

                            That said, consider one more thing.

                            Despite Mists, fae are known player. Not widely known among younger generations, but there are supernatural powers (Vampire, Werefolf, Wraith, Mage), that let others interact and detect them and those powers are not affected by Mists.

                            Any vampire with Auspex can see fae among other people. They don't know they're fae, unless they have Occult high enough, but the difference is still there. Vampires can prey on changelings if they want, and changelings couldn't hide without assistance of Arts.

                            To consider, that Technocracy woulnd't be able to do something similar, at least, would be unreasonable. The question is how much they can do or care about it.

                                    Most supernaturals consider the Fae extinct and long gone. The Technocracy especially suffers from this misconception, because the Mists work on them near 100%.

                                    So no, I don't agree that the Technocracy can come up with a method to detect the Fae (in any meaningful way). Even if they managed it, the Mists would erase the memory of any interaction in short order so they would be left with the conclusion that they failed or that such creatures aren't really there. Same result either way. They give up trying to look for imaginary (literally) monsters.

                                    And actually thinking about it, Vampires would be hard pressed to find Fae too. Sure, you can get an obsessive one with Auspex looking at every person that walks by. What are the chances that said Vampire is going to actually know what "rainbow highlights" in an Aura means? If they do confront the person to find out, the Mists will roll in and wipe/alter that memory. They too would be hard pressed to actually figure out anything going on with the Changelings because of that.

                                    Out of all of the games, Changelings by far has the strongest ability to hide in plain sight. There is literally no stat you can invest in to protect yourself from the Mists. At least in the other main 4 you can have a high Willpower and be somewhat guarded against the mental manipulations of memory wipes. Not so much in Changeling.

                                    Oh, and I also disagree on the idea that it's easy for Mages to "self-enchant". Very few paradigms mesh well with the concept. It's been a long time since I had this disagreement with one of my friends, but from memory only the Dreamspeakers had a Rote to do so (that very well may have changed in these last several years).
