                        Second... Can we get a few more Arts, or some variety on what's there? Maybe even a few kith-specific arts (yes, we have Chronos, Infusion, and... and... urk, hurp, Talecraft).                         The flexibility in C:tD comes from the different options that you can get out of the combination of Art + Realm (and even multiple Realms, and let's not talk about Fae 5).

                        In BoG, Chris (Howard) had written a introductory section to the Arts and Realms chapter about how they can be paired with much more flexibility that was up until then thought possible. I wondered: "Hey, this is classical fairy-tale stuff and I wouldn't know how to do it in Changeling" and my wife -- who plays Changeling with me -- said: "easy, it's a Dictum. Sleep!" And I realized that she exactly hit it.

                            I perused this thread and often see people complaining about how childish Changeling supposedly is.

                            One of the biggest misconceptions about the games in the cWoD lies in this assumption. It comes from the confusion between tone/ aesthetics and maturity. Yes, Vampire is "dark," aesthetically speaking. But in now way can you relate to a vampire from V:tM. These are alien, blood-drinking creatures driven by their Beast. And this is why I would argue that V:tM is the most fun because the less mature of the games. You play one of these inhuman creatures, for Dana's sake! In Changeling, the issues and themes are deeply relevant to our everyday life. It's about what it means to grow old, to face everyday dreariness and to try to keep your sense of wonder from falling under cynicism and despair. In the 1st edition book, the prologue was called "Never, never grow up" and the author wrote about how changelings were embodiments of people who played RPGs. Gamers try to find fun and recreation in games of imagination; changelings must do it, because this is what they are. Now, you have to try to play one of these guys.

                            Actually, if you think really about this, you may find out that Changeling is the most mature game of all.

                            So it doesn't need to be "dark" aesthetically. It is dark enough. And since it's set in the gothic-punk environment of the WoD it shares the dark aesthetics of the other games. Just describe a mental hospital to Changeling characters with that (and Terry Gilliam's films like Brazil, Fisher King and Twelve Monkeys) in mind... To the contrary, the focus is -- rightly so -- on the fantasy elements of the game. However, what I find difficult to do, as a storyteller, and I think what most people find difficult to do is how to set these fantasy elements in an "urban" setting in order to produce urban fantasy. This is what the storytelling section of the book should focus on.

                                Hey, Mr. Fomorian, that is nice... After the fall of Shadownessence, for a while I kept in touch with Thomas Milare and his wonderfully talented wife, and also with LostSoul, who helped me so much with the Fomorian project... Concerning Thomas, I'm still in touch with him through Facebook.

                                I had a couple of truly bad years; roleplaying become something secondary for me. The Fomorian Project stopped growing, at least in written form; nevertheless, I use it nowadays in my C:tD games, and all my players love it, but I don't really know if I would have the energy and drive to finish it in a printable form, the way you were able to do Kithbook: Boggan.

                                I have been able to integrate all the ideas a lot of wonderful people poured into the Fomorian Project into my C:tD games, and I will always be in their debt. In fact, I would like -someday- to offer them some kind of gesture, some kind of monument to their ingenuity, creativity and wit... The wonderfully talented Melissa Uran even sent me some free art for the Fomorian Project, and they are some of the most treasured computer files I keep in my hard drives.

                                I was also in touch, for some months, with Chris Howard (at least, I think he was Chris Howard) concerning Book of Glamour. I even sent him revised versions of the Arts I had invented for the Fomorian Project (Maoin, Dolum, Soulcraft and Necrotics) and there was some talk about including them in the Book.

                                Then he disappeared, I fell into really hard times, and that is the last I heard from him. I heard something about him burning all his books (or was it someone else?), including the manuscripts for Keys to the Kingdom and Book of Glamour.

                                I'm sorry if I am mistaken, it all happened a real long time ago for me. They were very difficult years for me and maybe my memory is not as good as I would like it to be.

                                All in all, it is good to read you again, Mattboggan. Another good thing brought by the upcoming Changeling: the Dreaming 20th anniversary edition.
