    Alternate Unseelie Legacies

    09-24-2017, 11:04 PM
    I like that C20 has clarified that Seelie doesn't mean "good" and Unseelie doesn't mean "evil". The description of the courts is pretty clear on this, and portrays unseelie as more individualistic than traditional. However, when you get to Legacies, it does seem to be pretty clearly good vs. evil. Most of the unseelie legacies seem to be what most people would consider "bad". Despite my two decades of experience with this game, I'm just now creating an unseelie character that I want to be more "good", but very much individual and against most traditional court structures. He is going to be a pretty nice guy, and dashing, but very much his own person. Yet when going through the legacies, I'm having a hard time finding something that fits, when all of the unseelie ones are brutal, criminal, or otherwise fairly horrible. These legacies do seem to support Unseelie=Bad and Seelie=Good, which they shouldn't.

    How have the rest of you gotten around this?

          I had similar feelings. To deal with it without revisiting everything, I tried to treat UnLegacies as "as darker & endgier", reasoning, that among other things, Unseelie have stronger affinity with darker impulses of humanity, but because of that they have much better handle of those dark impulses and can focus them better into constructive, healthy direction. Something, their Seelie brothers and sisters might find harder.

          We can cover Unseelie Legacies (and their Seelie Counterparts) case by case basis, here, reworking archtypes and bans or just the wording, if it would be enough.

          If you want Individualistic/Reformationist versus Community-oriented/Traditionalist, than you should incorporate it in both sets of Legacies.

          One, potentially good way to do this (I think)... is to make one set of Legacies, common for all Court/Arias, with a single ban and two paragraphs for Seelie and Unseelie specific differences. Of course, you'd need to write more, if you'd need to expand on Adhene Arias/Inaniamae Courts/etc. But it can be fun.

                                      It's sort of an artifact of the 1st ed. One of the things about all of WW's 2nd eds back in the day is that they all came out two years after the 1st (like clock work, with Vampire, coming after only about a year, being the exception). And part of this is that a lot of 1st ed stuff just got reprinted verbatim, even if it didn't quite fit other changes that were made. If there's been a revised ed of Changeling back around the turn of the millennium, you probably would've seen the legacies overhauled a bit.
