      Pretty tepid. They changed it so they ignore the Week of Nightmares and replace it with 9/11, not a fan of that, the Week of Nightmares already worked and they could have just said 9/11 exacerbated the situation.

      And it seems very unsatisfying how they handled King David. They honestly should have just killed him rather then keep this limbo where he is a pathetic wretch.

      I also really wish they would more strongly address the fact that we are still getting all kinds of Sidhe but they don't reincarnate. If I were to handle it I would have them noticeably trinkle making people wonder if the next Sidhe will be the last in the Autumn World.

      I do like they fleshed out some of the government, but I guess that isn't quite metaplot.

      Bannerhouses are interesting, it seems some of the Kings of the Houses are more impotent then say a banner house sometimes. But it addresses a lot of holes in the setting to make them a thing.

      We got some cool new kiths and that is good. I do miss the old Clarichaun, they literally were wee spirits of drunkeness so removing that because they deemed it problematic is problematic in itself.

      I need to look up the old Piskies because the new ones seem to be a Kith of beings that naturally lose their individuality, its hard for me to grock.

      The Lycians are a wonderful addition, I would probably have it they were always a thing but they just reached a population boom.

      I don't like the Inanimae's limbo state now where we don't even have a system for them, it would have probably been better to have left them out for another supplement.

      The Hsien are Wonderful and pretty fixed.

      The Thaillain are great! And the Shadowcourt is wonderfully nuanced. Some don't like that they are tied to activism but I think its appropriate based on the fact that the Shadowcourt is an activist movement. Its a great sign of nuance to have the big back villains infesting them.

      I wish they kept House Danu as the most imperial of the houses. The whole point as I understood it why we don't have things like house Zeus or house Dagda is because its covered by Danu. But they are still a cool house.

      I am not fond they removed the Merfolk houses, I keep that personally I figure they removed them because of space. I would have removed the Inanimae part and used the extra space to explain how the Mer adopt things like Pooka and Selkie.

      I love the edition of the Sevartal, but I wish we got houses for them. And I wish they had some fantastical physical imagery like having starry night looking skin or shadowy skin.

      I'm still digesting Players guide.

      I'm very pleased we got Unleashing. Its even better then the Dark Age Fae. And I love the connections to the Dark Age Fae.



      I would have advanced the Metaplot even more. The Fomorians were waking up so I would have ran with that. We need a Book of Nightmares to give us Fomorian/Thallain Houses and some content about Horrors rising that need dealing with.

      I'm a bit confused with Dauntain, hella cool name but I'm still a bit confused on how to use them. Like one of them is a living contagion that can infect others with their offal, talk about topical.

      I would have liked more on the Red Star and all that jazz.

      I do like interesting bits they gave us in the NPC section with the Vampires and the like.

      Over all solid, especially the core C20 book that really shines even among the 20th ann books.

                      Of the top of my head, and bearing in mind that this is just my opinion and YMMV...

                      •I actually like having High King David Ardry back and I think it keeps him in the sort of mystery state he was in the beginning without forcing an ST's hand to go into a 'War of Three Queens' style plot, although if that's someone's desire, I'd say go for it! I think it's an interesting way of handing the inevitable Bedlam that would come from having the weight of all North American changelings relying on you alongside the Banality of his task and what the future may hold...

                      •I was originally uncertain about the use of the Shadow Court in the C20 Player's Guide, settling the Black Lives Matter & Occupy Movements and their ilk in with the faction dedicated to evil and monstrosity really didn't sit with me and neither did whitewashing the old Shadow Court. In time though, I think it works well as a representation of what the Unseelie Court (Change is Good) that consists of the surface and most widespread aspect of the Shadow Court is doing to combat noble and Seelie hegemony with the shadier stuff and Black Court being concealed deeper within the court which has become less a political organization and more a changeling cultural statement/movement.

                      •I really like what C20 Player's Guide did to flesh out the Parliment of Dreams, Banner Houses, and to diversify the nature of global changeling population and politics.

                      •I can understand some people's issue with having 9/11 be the core of the Evanescence but I think that when one considers that the main lore is discussed from a North American (primary US) perspective, it makes a lot of sense. Just as the Summer of Love and Moon Landing served as peaks of Dreaming Glamour, 9/11 and its aftermath would have been peaks of Nightmare Glamour. The Resurgences & Evanescenes didn't happen at the exact same times globally and I think that works very well.

                      •I can empathize with uncertainty about the new Clurichaun and while I like them fine, they do somewhat stick of wanting to be leprechauns but not having to use the 'L word' for fear of mockery, even if Clurichauns do work well specifically as drunken house spirits. I also like the Piskies a lot and think that they have no personal face is an excellent part of the horror that makes any kith nuanced.

                      •I like the Lycians a lot and I like the Inanimae conceptually but having to use their old art system to make them work is annoying I'll admit.

                      Overall I like C20 a lot and even though 20tg edition didn't have to move the metaplot forward to uphold their mandate, I really enjoy what C20 did with its opportunity.

                                          The Hsien occupied that controversial place that so many Asian splats that WW put out did - interesting but with a valid concern regarding Orientalism and whether or not making them so clearly distinct from their ‘standard’ counterparts was good or necessary.

                                          For the Hsien especially, C20 does a lot to ground them into Changelings in general but provide some distinctions and unique rules for flair, twist, and context. Jade replaces Cold Iron, they use different Legacies, they have a distinct Wyld form - although it’s somewhat similar to what other changelings have, they can gain energy through human religious rites and communal observance, and they practice a distinct rite from the Changeling Way. Plus Five Elemental alchemy.

                                          I like the Hsien and how they’ve entered into an area where, like the Nunnehi & Menehune, they’ve entered an area where changelings can have cultural differences but still be recognizably the same class of entities.

                                          As far as their connection to The Heavens, it could be the High Umbra or Middle Umbra depending on your interpretation imo, but either way it connects to the Hsien’s role as “little gods” who are now trapped on earth, outside the Celestial Bureaucracy, until the world can be restored to its proper order - whatever that is.
