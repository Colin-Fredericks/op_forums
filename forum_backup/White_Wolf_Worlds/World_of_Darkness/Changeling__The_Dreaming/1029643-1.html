    Originally posted by Aya Tari View Post

    I do not think that they suddenly realize anything, they are born Changelings, they just slowly enter into the realization that they are not normal over time. A person who becomes a Grump in their thirties probably already lived a life surrounded by the fantastic and, regardless of their profession, would have likely owned thousands of fantasy books, been into tabletop gaming, etc as their Changeling self unconsciously manipulated them into the more comfortable world of fantasy.                     Originally posted by Crowley View Post
                    In Mage and Demon there's very important laws about "The Naming." (at least on my Mage game, no PC uses his 'birth name' to protect themselves from Sympathetic Magick. and Demons don't go around revealing their Names so they dont become Slaves). is there anything similar in changeling? or they are Safe if everyone knows that the changeling is "Joe Torien" AKA "Jokah of Roaring Engine Freehold" ?

                    Most changeling have two names they can go by — their mortal name given to them by their parents and their faerie name that follows them through their reincarnation cycle. Which one they go by most varies from changeling to changeling. Probably due to the Dreaming influencing the parents, a changeling's mortal name and faerie name often sound like variations on the other.

                    Everything in Changeling also has an additional true name. This true name is hidden and may change over time as the person or thing it describes changes. Only powerful fae magics can divine something's true name. Once discovered, that same magic may alter the true name, thereby altering whatever it describes.

                    Charlie Cantrell
                    Onyx Path Freelancer
                    Changeling: The Dreaming 20th Anniversary Edition, Conquering Heroes, Book of Freeholds, Guide to the Night, C20 Players Guide

                                                        I've always thought of the chrysalis as less a "my life is a lie" moment and more like "this is what my life was always missing." Glamour is powerful, and even though she hasn't awakened to it yet, it's still called to her for her entire life. It's what always made her the odd one out, the one who sees things a bit differently than anyone else around her. She's probably desperately longed for Glamour even if her conscious mind could never figure out what made her so restless.

                                                        With the chrysalis, she finally becomes whole. All the things that were missing finally fall into place — things just out of sight that she *knew* where there finally come into focus. Her identity and purpose are finally something she can grasp.
