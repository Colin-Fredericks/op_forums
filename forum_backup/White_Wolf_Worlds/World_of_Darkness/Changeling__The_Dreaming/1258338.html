    What if The Fae rules a Unified Supernatural Society?

    10-01-2018, 02:39 AM
    What if the Fae capitalized on their superior numbers and godlike potential and became the ruling class of supernatural in most of the world? Like what if like in the Middle Kingdom the Supernaturals are at least cordial with each other?

    So in this setting say either the Fae couldn’t escape to Arcadia because it collapsed, or maybe the Fomorians warred with the Tuatha stirred by the Black Plague?

    So the Fae instead of having their Faeholds dragged off the World just took root. They adapted on mass either becoming Changelings or Anchored Inanimae. Say the Fae decided to take a hand on the reigns of the supernatural world. They involved themselves in the Anarch revolt, volunteering an ultimatum, accept shelter amid their Courts or be driven from their Lairs into the fires of the Inquisition. Making a point of shattering even the Eldest rather then diablerise it, and Driving Lasombra itself into the Abyss. Cainites now operate under the court of the Fae. The Salubri were accepted and the Tremere crushed in their infancy, now only existing as a small bloodline guarded by Gargoyles and Demons. Vampire embraces are regulated now by local lords.

    The Traditions and the Order of Reason war was decided when the traditions accepted position as wisemen and seers for the Fae to tend the sacred places of the world. They broke the Order of Reason. They now exist as a conspiracy across the World, seeking to guide the light of Reason in secret.

    Some of the Early shelters from the Echos of Faith and Banality came in the form of the Fae bringing some of their holdings into the lands of the Dead, not completely dominating the dead but establishing themselves as the Hades and Zeranbooches of the Dead. When given the choice of being soul forged in the atheist war forges of the Dark Kingdoms or finding Dust or Paradise among the Old Gods the Dark Kingdoms are not as they would be if they were undisputed.

    The Fera find War and Peace with the Fae, ultimately they take up their ages old position as the Wardens of Doomsday and they gain perhaps better relations with some Courts over others.

    By modern days Vampires are much less populous then the default setting, perhaps no more then a fourth their number, and many take more supernatural airs, like the Laibon, becoming more prone to demonic or beastial features if they embrace monstrousness.

    Most supernaturals consider themselves as part of the World of Darkness, their name for the hidden supernatural facet of reality. Mostly ruled by Fae Nobles, or Elders, there are some Vampires and Mages who hold positions of power, but rarely officially leaders of anything but their own kind, more commonly generals or Seers of Dukes and Sultans of the Fae.
