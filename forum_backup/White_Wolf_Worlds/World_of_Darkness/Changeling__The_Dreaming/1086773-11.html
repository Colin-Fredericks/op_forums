                    Something minor I noticed in the nunnehi section page 410. In regard to the Saguaro Totem the name Papago is not one used by the tribe. That was a Spanish name derived from what other tribes called them. The name should be changed to Tohono O'odham.

                    Not so keen on the Troll birthright Titan's Power being based on oath taking. Seems as though their very nature of being born from strength was how it was presented in the original setting. I would see greedy folk saying, "Ah...yes. I clearly have two Oaths and so have my Titan's Power maxed out already." Perhaps something basing it on the Seeming age group?

                    I believe it was said in an earlier post that the Scathach became Seelie and Danaan became Unseelie. Was that to have things a neat and orderly even split? I don't necessarily agree with that change. Fundamentally changes their nature in the grand scheme. But then again I saw the text mentions their association being nominal only. Plus until now Danaan was semi official errata...so it is at least good to see them present for roll call.

                    There is also the matter of several House Boons changing. Eiluned lost their Cantrip casting benefit. Liam lost their Cantrip casting toward mortals benefit. I skimmed this section so there may be more that I have not observed.

                    However, I must confess that the Dougal Flaw is brilliant now. Took a page from the Metabarons in the way that they must sacrifice their body to belong. Dougal wasn't Hephaestus and crippled early on...he sacrificed himself in the forge birthing of steel. Distinguishes them nicely from the Balor deformities. Still I think that aspect of them should be embraced in some fashion though. Maybe a mention of their acceptance of disability as something to overcome through craftwork?

                    I love the inclusion of Unleashing and the collecting of all the goodies under one roof with regard to Kith, Houses, Arts, Gallain, Thallain, and the Dark-kin. New artwork is pretty boss too.

                    All in all rolling with the changes....

                                On p. 27, in the Introduction, Fae and Faeries are capitalized, but in the history section of chapter 1 they're not (p. 32a), to my greatest relief. Also, Faerie needs capitalization when it is the fabled land of the fae (another word for Arcadia).
