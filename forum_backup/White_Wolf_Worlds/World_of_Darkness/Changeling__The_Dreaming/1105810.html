    Pentex, Banes, Chimera and Fomori

    06-20-2017, 03:45 PM
    Personally, I LOVE using Pentex in one way or another in my games. Avalon Toys is an employer of more than a few Unseelie fae in my home campaign, particularly Nockers, Goblins and House Balor executives. That said, using Bane-ridden toys, Nightmares and Fomori come with that territory. So I have had to consider where Banes and Chimera overlap. A number of Banes from Werewolf the Apocalypse also turn up as Night Hag Chimera and similar, so I treat Banes as Chimera by another name. Dark, poisoned dreams that rot the heart of wonder.

    Many types of Nervosa and many Nocnitsa and some Urban Legends could be considered Banes, they are certainly cruel enough. And ones that focus of Hunger, Hate and Terror even more so. Plague Nervosa Mannequins and especially Outsiders are very much like banes. All they need is the Possession rede and either the Enchantment or Wryrd rede to make fomori.

    Fomori (in Changeling terms)
    Dreamers possessed by nightmare Chimera are usually only controlled for a limited time. However a particularly degenerate host, or one subject to foul magical rites might become permanent hosts of these chimera. Such persons undergo a terrible change. Permanently enchanted much like The Ritual of Parted Mists, granting Enhancements more like mutations (I typically treat them like Wyrd versions of Redes such as Armor, Skitter and Weaponry). Unlike standard Enchanted mortals, Fomori can regain glamour by eating raw flesh, gaining 1 glamour point per health level from their victims. Most of them look like demented humans to humans.

      Some sample Bane Chimera and specialized Redes.


      Gooshy Gooze

      Gooshy Gooze is guaranteed to be a million laughs. It swishes, it sloshes, it oozes and it goozes. It also forces itself down the throats of unsuspecting children when they’re left alone at night. Gooshy Gooze is another wonderful “non-toxic” creation of Avalon Toys. Like most of the dangerous toys from Avalon, Gooshy Gooze doesn’t kill, it corrupts. Unlike most of the dangerous toys, this is not a simple fetish; it is a creature with a mind of its own and needs no child to do its work.
      Gooshy Gooze devours its prey from the inside, destroying innocence and sanity as it goes. By the time Gooshy Gooze has finished its consumption, it has transmogrified the hapless child into an equivalent volume of Gooshy Gooze. Most of the Gooshy Gooze then oozes its way back to any of 200 factories nationwide; it is subsequently redistributed to any of Avalon’s countless “bubble gum machines” where it can again be picked up for a measly 25 cents. Many children learn from their friends that these particular machines will sometime even dispense the Gooshy Gooze without a quarter; you just have to turn the knob, and as often as not, the Gooshy Gooze is yours.
      Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 2, Stamina 0 (in host body 4), Charisma 0 (in host body 3), Manipulation 0 (in host body 3), Appearance 0 (in host body 2), Perception 2, Intelligence 1, Wits 1
      Abilities: Athletics 3, Brawl 3, Survival 2
      Health Levels: OK, -2, Incapacitated (can only be hurt by fire, electricity or freezing)
      Special: Gooshy Goozes force themselves down their victims’ throats (Strength + Athletics vs. Dexterity + Dodge; three successes required). While within a host’s body it slowly transforms its host into the same substance they are made of, and while the process is occurring, are capable of anything that the child is physically capable of: knifing Grandma, burning down the house, the usual stuff. The transformation is extremely slow, depleting one Health Level a day from the host until the victim is Incapacitated, at which time the change is complete. This transformation costs one of its host’s Gnosis points per day. While in this “breeding stage” the effective Health Levels of the Gooze are the same as those of its host (three to four for a small child).
      Redes: Corruption, Possession, Wyrd.
      Banes: Token (Must use the material Gooshy Gooze to use Wyrd)


      Meat Puppet (Nocnitsa)

      These spirits embody innocence defiled. Meat Puppets wander the Dreaming in search of those young beings (both human and animal) who have suffered wrongful deaths. A Meat Puppet will possess anything from a drowned kitten to a young child beaten to death by her father. Puppets hunger for the feel of young flesh. They wish only to possess a body and wreak vengeance on those with “natural” bodies. However, they are not strong enough to take a living body, so they prey upon the dead. They possess the recently dead cadavers of young children or animals.
      Although individual Meat Puppets are not very strong, they often appear in groups. Since their bodies have already died, Meat Puppets attack unrelentingly and can only be stopped by utterly destroying the host body. Thus, even a group of Meat Puppet “kittens” can be very dangerous.
      Attributes: Strength 1, Dexterity 3, Stamina 1,
      Glamour: 4, Willpower: 5
      Health Levels: OK, -2, -5, Incapacitated
      Redes: Animate Dead, Traverse Dreaming, Wyrd.


      Mukade (Nocnitsa)

      Considered by some to be the very image of the nightmare and corruption, the Mukade are terrifying spirit monsters that prowl the darkest parts of the Dreaming, devouring all in their paths. These hideous spirit-centipedes are large as train cars and reek prodigiously of rot and corruption. Many have human-like faces swelling from the Chitin of their heads, or even along the lengths of their bodies. Their mandibles are frankly immense and drip with spiritual corrosion. In the ages long ago, slaying a Mukade was something of a rite of passage among greater heroes but now the heroes are fewer, and the Mukade breed and writhe in greater numbers than ever in the Nightmare Worlds.
      Attributes: Strength 10, Dexterity 7, Stamina 7, all other Attributes at 6.
      Glamour: 6, Willpower: 7
      Health Levels: OK, OK, OK, -1, -1, -1, -2, -2, -5, Incapacitated
      Redes: Aggravated damage, Armor (5 dice), Blighted Touch, Dream Quake, Fester, Glamour Pact, Scuttle, Weapon (Bite; Str +3), Wyrd.


      Redes

      Animate Dead
      Allows a chimera to take over a dead body of a creature of the Dreaming and send it against the living. Roll Glamour vs local Banality. A zombie thus animated has Physical Attributes of 3 each; 7 no-penalty Health levels and a Brawl of 3 but no other Attributes. It only works with one corpse at a time. Nasty chimera may use any Social Abilities they have through the host body to intimidate, harass, seduce or traumatize living victims. The chimera may use this Rede on corpses of mortals if it also possesses the Wyrd rede.
      Power cost: 10
      Chimera Point Cost: 5
      Use Cost: 1 Glamour

      Blighted Touch
      If the spirit deals damage on an attack, the target must make a reflexive Willpower roll. If she fails, her negative characteristics dominate her personality (Seelie to Unseelie) for the next few hours. If she botches, the change is permanent. The chimera may use this Rede on mortals if it also possesses the Enchantment or Wyrd redes.
      Power cost: 2
      Chimera Point Cost:
      Use Cost: None

      Corruption
      By making a Glamour challenge against the target's Willpower, the bane implants a single evil suggestion that their target must act on before the session is through. The chimera may use this Rede on mortals if it also possesses the Enchantment or Wyrd redes.
      Power cost: 1
      Chimera Point Cost: 3
      Use Cost: 1 Willpower

      Dream Quake
      The chimera can cause the Dreaming to shake with such force that all those standing are thrown to the ground. Everyone within the radius suffers bashing damage from the spiritual concussion equal to half the spirit's Glamour roll, rounded up.
      Chimera Point Cost: 2
      Use Cost: 1 Glamour

      Possession
      The chimera can possess another sentient creature of the Dreaming with this Rede. The chimera must
      touch the target. The Storyteller makes a Glamour roll (difficulty of the target’s Willpower), resisted by the victim’s player rolling Willpower (difficulty of the chimera’s Glamour). The chimera may use this Rede on mortals if it also possesses the Enchantment or Wyrd redes.
      Chimera Point Cost: 3
      Use Cost: None

                                          Corrupted Elementals are fun foes to throw at characters. Parties including Nunnehi or Inanimae characters will be especially horrified with such beings.

                                          Hogling (Smog)
                                          These appear as roughly humanoid clouds of noxious fumes, ominously rolling toward their targets. As with other corrupted elemental's, these banes can be found in polluted areas in great numbers. They normally remain inactive unless disturbed, but will prey on Changelings and other beings when the opportunity presents itself.
                                          Attributes: Strength 5, Dexterity 6, Stamina 2, Perception 3, Intelligence 2, Wits 2
                                          Abilities: Alertness 5, Athletics 4, Kenning 5, Brawl 1, Dodge 6, Stealth 8, Streetwise 6, Gremayre 4
                                          Glamour 7, Willpower 3, Banality 0
                                          Health: -5, Incapacitated
                                          Redes: Befuddle, Enchantment, Fester, Flight, Hide, Poof Begone, Traverse Dreaming.

                                          H’ruggling (Sludge)

                                          These appear as roughly humanoid piles of waste. They lumber and ooze toward their victims, leaving slimy trails in their wakes. As with other corrupted elemental's, these banes can be found in polluted areas in great numbers. They normally remain inactive unless disturbed, but will prey on Changelings and other beings when the opportunity presents itself.
                                          Attributes: Strength 6, Dexterity 2, Stamina 7, Perception 2, Intelligence 2, Wits 1
                                          Abilities: Alertness 4, Brawl 4, Kenning 3, Survival (sewers) 5
                                          Glamour 5, Willpower 9, Banality 0
                                          Redes: Armor (4), Corruption, Fester, Meld (Corrupted Earth), Venom, Wyrd
                                          Health Levels: OK, OK, OK, -1, -2, -4, Inc

                                          Wakshaani (Toxins)
                                          These appear as roughly humanoid blobs of purplish fluid covered with blotches,veins and bubbling wart. As with other corrupted elemental's, these banes can be found in polluted areas in great numbers. They normally remain inactive unless disturbed, but will prey on Changelings and other beings when the opportunity presents itself.
                                          Attributes: Strength 5, Dexterity 5, Stamina 6, Perception 2, Intelligence 2, Wits 3
                                          Abilities: Athletics (swim) 5, Alertness 3, Brawl 4, Crafts (fishing) 5,Dodge 5, Kenning 4, Stealth 5
                                          Glamour 7, Willpower 3, Banality 0
                                          Redes: Aggravated Damage (Venom), Corruption, Element Sense, Fester, Traverse Dreaming, Venom, Wyrd
