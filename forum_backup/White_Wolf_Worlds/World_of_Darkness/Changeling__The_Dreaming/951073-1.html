                                                I think it really does depend on the critter. Vampires use Willpower a lot, so it makes sense to buy it up. Wraiths already begin with high Willpower. Mages begin with high Willpower but it's better to make it even higher. But for changelings, it's probably more important to buy up your Glamour, your Realms and your Backgrounds. Glamour and Banality are far more important traits in the game than Willpower.

                                                Unlike Disciplines or Spheres, possessing Arts alone isn't enough to be able to do magic. You need to have a decent spread in Realms or your effects are really limited. It doesn't help that Second Edition Arts are broken, so players have to spend a lot of time thinking about how the combination of Arts and Realms they're choosing works, which encourages min-maxing. If they don't pick right, they can end up with a mix of powers that are next to useless.

                                                Glamour is also very scarce, so you either want to buy more permanent Glamour or make up for the lack of flexibility in your magical prowess with some Backgrounds instead (Treasures offer an easy fix if you can't spend Freebies on both Arts and Realms; Remembrance opens up the possibility of boosting all combat dice pools; Chimera lets you take multiple chimerical items of equal or lesser value, so you can get armour and weapons together by investing a few dots in one trait).

                                                Rather than Willpower, Title is a better investment if you want to resist Sovereign (it's like Generation and Dominate in that respect). There aren't many other important things you'll need Willpower for. In fact, it rarely gets rolled in the CTD game I run.
