                                Originally posted by Gryffon15 View Post
                                For one thing, I think it helps to justify in-setting the medieval aesthetics of changeling society. If western changelings had been living alongside humanity for the past 600 years, they would probably have bypassed fuedalism (with the history of commoners in the Interregnum saying as much) and the sidhe would have adapted to modern dreams of leadership - mayors, politicians, executives, etc.
                                Some might have, but when dealing with things like the Dreaming and magic, sometimes old forms and titles carry with them a certain amount of inherent power. This is especially true when various oaths of fealty or the like are backed up and enforced by the Dreaming. Even modern urban politics retain a certain degree of feudal nature about them (what with special interest groups, ward bosses, and families of influence) in spite of the best efforts of technocrats over the past 150 years.
                                One of the reasons I wish Changeling's political structure was more reflective of how actual medieval feudalism worked is because a city where the local Duke or Prince or whatever has to carefully balance the needs and wants of the local Redcap gang warlord, the Nocker/Boggin working class guild alliance, the city's "voodoo queen", the Childlings' Court, the Knight Commander of the Order of the Oaken Shield, The Ladies of Grace Adieu Tea Society, the Satyr Bishop of Dionysus, and the Troll march lord is much more dynamic and interesting than one where its just "The Duke of the State of Ohio says I'm the Baron of Cincinnati so you all have to do what I say because that's what the rank chart says."



                                What is tolerance? It is the consequence of humanity. We are all formed of frailty and error; let us pardon reciprocally each other's folly. That is the first law of nature.
                                Voltaire, "Tolerance" (1764)

                                        In my setting a Number of the most storied Treasures from Legend have chosen new wielders in preparation for the coming winter, several of them were lost or hidden in the mists of time since the age of Legends but some were in the possession of notable changelings but left to find more worthy heirs. They bring to them the skein of destined mortals and make them one off Kiths, called Chosen, embodying their legend. So in this Case Caliburn goes to find a worthy Mortal to make into the new High King, just as Mjolnir Goes to find a Mortal to command the Thunder and Lighting but also Jarngreipr and Meginjord also each go to find someone to inherit a portion of divine might. The Thallain of the Chosen are the Accursed, those chosen by infamous and demonic items, they have a greater resonance with Urban Legends, like Freddy Krueger's Glove, and Jason's Mask or even Jack the Ripper's Knife, but also truly ancient and potent Items like the Eye of Balor becoming a Glass Eye looking for an Eyeless Mortal to make the new General of the Thallain.

Lleu seems to be a mix of the Lleu from the Gwydion Myth, which was the Welsh version of Lugh but in Changeling they state Gwydion was a descendant of Lugh rather than the Uncle of Lugh, and Lancelot with probably a greater emphasis on Lancelot. People say David is a reincarnation of the Dream of Arthur Pendragon, but actually his predecessor High King Defyll might actually have been Arthur's Dream and David may have been just a Young Sidhe, or perhaps a reincarnation of Gwydion himself. Lleu would be the champion of the Realm, the Lancelot, Seif would be perhaps a new Galahad (not the dream of Galahad but the role of Galahad), and we could use a new Merlin as well, Duke Guile could be a cool fit.
