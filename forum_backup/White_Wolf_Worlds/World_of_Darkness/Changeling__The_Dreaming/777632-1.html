    Well, as somebody who would love to get into Changeling but is put off by the idea that science=bad (which I understand some writers were lighter on, but it still annoys me) and being confused as to what banality is supposed to be have left me putting it off for the moment.

    The problem is I love the idea of Changeling, I just find it insulting. So my main desire for C20 is to see science embraced as something that can be genuinely wonderful and even creative, and a tightening of what banality is. I think Banality should be the lack of wonder, he acceptance of what is without struggling to find out why, knowing your toaster makes toast without being interested in who is does so reliably, seeing a painting as just a picture and not a carefully put together piece of colour and shape. Then make Banality able to deny Chimerical reality, properly, as in a place infused with Banality inflicts chimerical damage if you aren't sufficiently Banal.

    Blue is sarcasm.

    If I suggestion I make contradicts in-setting metaphysics please ignore me, I probably brought in scientific ideas.

                My example for a ritual, (This is belong to one of my players)

                An eshu named Ishtar has a treasure named as "Sher-Name(Book of Nightmares)". This book holds nightmare chimeras that Ishtar extracted from its enemies or friends.
                In darkness, Ishtar uses his "Soothsay 1 Omen" to understand his opponents most terrifying fear. He uses a certain bunk. This bunk should be a sentence that contains "Nightmare,Fear and Darkness". However, he cannot use this sentence again in his life!.

                After understanding the fear, he starts to create this as a thought in opponents mind. He uses talecraft art for this. He starts to describe it, and chimera starts to emerge. (Opponent rolls willpower to resist it and subtracts success from talecraft cantrip). This cause opponent to think and describe what is emerging.

                In third phase, after chimera described as whole, it emerges from the opponents dreams to dreaming. It starts to attack opponent. It should not have to be physical attack, but can be mental one. In this phase the storyteller(I mean eshu) selects an obvious and ridiculous flaw to this creature (for example giant crocodile tickles). Also, if opponent is a really good dreamer and believer he/she can be add a flaw to chimera (for example sleeping in front of bear can be effective). (talecraft art will be used in this phase also).

                In fourth phase, chimera goes to "Sher-Name" and become a page. And can be summoned as treasure power later.(But only once).
