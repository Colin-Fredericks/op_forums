    Types of Nocnitsa (Chimera)

    10-03-2022, 01:50 AM
    I need to brainstorm additional kinds of the Nocnitsa, a type of Orphan Chimera. Per the 2e corebook, Nocnitsa are chimera born from the darkest of nightmares, perhaps the most feared chimera of all. The only example we are given are the Creeping Fear - monstrous spiders.

    It's a great concept, but it is undeveloped. I don't run Changeling games, but I am currently in a Werewolf game with a pack of stag that wants to be heavily involved with the fae. So the idea that Garou packs of Stag defend the fae from Nocnitsa seems an obvious one. I just need something else other than giant spiders or similar vermin.

    It is an easy temptation to just go high fantasy and begin pulling in the equivalent of D&D monsters. But I want to keep close to folklore or thematically appropriate creatures. I also want to avoid creatures that are best interpreted as "monsters under the bed" for children - those are better classified as a kind of Nervosa, as their intent is to scare irrational children, not actually kill them. I see Nocnitsa as far more malevolent or deadly. Nocnitsa are also similar to "Monsters", but Monsters appear to be a default category of anything else that does fit the categories of Nervosa, Nocnitsa, and Sprites. Monsters might be dangerous, but aren't necessarily malevolent or murderous in my opinion.

    I decided to add the Nachtkrapps as a kind of Nocnitsa. It's a giant, monstrous raven that flies at night. It could also be a Nervosa, but stories of it killing and devouring children upgrade it in my opinion.

    I also use Tommy Rawhead, also known as Bloody Bones or Rawhead-and-Bloody Bones. Again while this could also be a Nervosa, its murderous nature makes it better as a Nocnitsa.

    I also think the Night Doctors would make a good type.

    Aufhockers are also a good possibility since they crush their victims to death.

    On the other hand, the creepypasta example of Black Eyed Kids don't seem to actually murder people, just terrify them. So I'd make them Nervosa instead.

    I am interested in whether people have created or seen used other examples of Nocnitsa in their games. I want to add them to my list as possible chimerical antagonists that Changelines or Garou could fight.

                                      For my own tastes and for my games, I have a great deal of overlap between Banes and Nocnitsa. Below is a small bestiary that are in the background of my games. The Svartalves are basically Scrag banes, and serve as shock troops for House Balor and the Thallain (and are a handfull for Changeling characters).


                                      Gooshy Gooze (Nocnitsa/Corrupted Toy)
                                      Gooshy Gooze is guaranteed to be a million laughs. It swishes, it sloshes, it oozes and it goozes. It also forces itself down the throats of unsuspecting children when they’re left alone at night. Gooshy Gooze is another wonderful “non-toxic” creation of Avalon Toys. Like most of the dangerous toys from Avalon, Gooshy Gooze doesn’t kill, it corrupts. Unlike most of the dangerous toys, this is not a simple fetish; it is a creature with a mind of its own and needs no child to do its work.
                                      Gooshy Gooze devours its prey from the inside, destroying innocence and sanity as it goes. By the time Gooshy Gooze has finished its consumption, it has transmogrified the hapless child into an equivalent volume of Gooshy Gooze. Most of the Gooshy Gooze then oozes its way back to any of 200 factories nationwide; it is subsequently redistributed to any of Avalon’s countless “bubble gum machines” where it can again be picked up for a measly 25 cents. Many children learn from their friends that these particular machines will sometime even dispense the Gooshy Gooze without a quarter; you just have to turn the knob, and as often as not, the Gooshy Gooze is yours.
                                      Attributes: Strength 3, Dexterity 2, Stamina 0 (in host body 4), Charisma 0 (in host body 3), Manipulation 0 (in host body 3), Appearance 0 (in host body 2), Perception 2, Intelligence 1, Wits 1
                                      Abilities: Athletics 3, Brawl 3, Survival 2
                                      Glamour: 5 Willpower: 5
                                      Health Levels: OK, -2, Incapacitated (can only be hurt by fire, electricity or freezing)
                                      Redes: Possession, Transformation, Wyrd.
                                      Banes: Token (Must use the material Gooshy Gooze to use Wyrd)
                                      • Transformation: Gooshy Goozes force themselves down their victims’ throats (Strength + Athletics vs. Dexterity + Dodge; three successes required). While within a host’s body it slowly transforms its host into the same substance they are made of, and while the process is occurring, are capable of anything that the child is physically capable of: knifing Grandma, burning down the house, the usual stuff. The transformation is extremely slow, depleting one Health Level a day from the host until the victim is Incapacitated, at which time the change is complete. This transformation costs one of the Gooshy Gooze's Glamour per day. While in this “breeding stage” the effective Health Levels of the Gooze are the same as those of its host (three to four for a small child).
                                      ---
                                      Meat Puppet (Nocnitsa/Bane)
                                      These spirits embody innocence defiled. Meat Puppets wander the Dreaming in search of those young beings (both human and animal) who have suffered wrongful deaths. A Meat Puppet will possess anything from a drowned kitten to a young child beaten to death by her father. Puppets hunger for the feel of young flesh. They wish only to possess a body and wreak vengeance on those with “natural” bodies. However, they are not strong enough to take a living body, so they prey upon the dead. They possess the recently dead cadavers of young children or animals.
                                      Although individual Meat Puppets are not very strong, they often appear in groups. Since their bodies have already died, Meat Puppets attack unrelentingly and can only be stopped by utterly destroying the host body. Thus, even a group of Meat Puppet “kittens” can be very dangerous.
                                      Attributes: Strength 1 (3 in host), Dexterity 3 (3 in host), Stamina 1 (3 in host)
                                      Abilities: Brawl 1, Survival 2
                                      Glamour: 4, Willpower: 5
                                      Health Levels: (in Chimera form) OK, -2, -5, Incapacitated (In Host) OK, OK, OK, OK, OK, OK, Destroyed
                                      Redes: Animate Dead, Traverse Dreaming, Wyrd.
                                      • Animate Dead: The chimera can take over a dead body of a creature of the Dreaming and send it against the living. Roll Glamour vs local Banality. A zombie thus animated has Physical Attributes of 3 each; 7 no-penalty Health levels and a Brawl of 3 but no other Attributes. It only works with one corpse at a time. Nasty chimera may use any Social Abilities they have through the host body to intimidate, harass, seduce or traumatize living victims. The chimera may use this Rede on corpses of mortals if it also possesses the Wyrd rede.Use Cost: 1 Glamour
                                      ---
                                      Nocturnae                                       (Nocnitsa/Bane)
                                      The Nocturna, or Nighthag, is the warper of hopes and dreams. This spirit wanders the Dreaming in search of sleeping humans. When it finds a sleeper whose dreaming mind appeals to the Nocturna, the chimera will attempt to twist the dream down a more sinister course. If the sleeper dreams of prosperity and wealth, the Nocturna may corrupt the dream into a fantasy where the dreamer gains wealth only to lose it from the betrayal of a loved one. Dreams of love may turn into terrifying nightmares in which the dreamer’s lover spurns and mocks him. No matter what the fantasy, a Nocturna will cause the dream to devolve into a depressing, hopeless situation. In her physical form, the Nocturna appears as a terrifyingly beautiful woman with long tangled hair, sharp claws, and a mouth filled with pus.
                                      Attributes: Strength 4, Dexterity 2, Stamina 2, Charisma 3, Manipulation 3, Appearance 4, Perception 2, Intelligence 3, Wits 4
                                      Abilities: Alertness 2, Brawl 3, Empathy 4,
                                      Glamour: 6 Willpower: 5
                                      Health Levels: OK, -1, -1, -2, -2, -5, Incapacitated
                                      Attack: Claw for five dice (lethal).
                                      Redes: Dreamweaving, Enchantment, Weapon (Claws)
                                      • Dreamweaving: This Rede works only on sleeping victims in the mundane world when deployed by chimera in the Dreaming. Characters sleeping in the Dreaming are unaffected. Chimera not in the Dreaming cannot utilize this Rede. To activate Dreamweaving, the chimera merely needs to encounter a sleeping victim and gain at least one success on a Glamour roll (difficulty equals the victim’s Willpower). For the remainder of the scene, the chimera can direct the course of the victim’s dream in any manner she sees fit. Successive Dreamweavings on the same victim become easier over a period of time. For every two Dreamweaving attempts, the victim suffers -1 to his Willpower dice pool to resist. Repeated use of this rede causes the victim to gain no benefits from sleep. Over time, the target suffers permanent loss of Willpower, insanity, and death.Use Cost: This Rede costs no Glamour to use. It is actually a limited form of Ravaging, as the chimera gains Glamour from the victim over a period of time.
                                      ---
                                      Svartalves                                       (Nocnitsa/Bane)
                                      These “dark elves” make up one of the many races of warrior-chimera that serve the Elder Dark. They are monsters of unrestrained rage and violence, serving not as corrupters, but as soldiers. They are, literally, dreams of murder. They live for the moment when a victim’s life ends, and they revel in the mayhem and bloodshed they inflict. Packs of Svarts often indulge in frenzies of death and dismemberment, expressing only a malevolently dark humor at their victim’s expense. Svarts are lanky and bony, with glistening gray-black skin, greasy black hair, and crystal blue eyes. Their movements are reminiscent of spiders or small predatory animals. Their walk in a slouch, their arms nearly acting as forelegs. This slouch does not impede their movement in any way.
                                      Physical Attributes: Strength 4, Dexterity 2, Stamina 4, Perception 2, Intelligence 1, Wits 1
                                      Abilities: Alertness 3, Athletics (Climbing) 4, Kenning 2, Brawl 3, Intimidation 3, Stealth 4
                                      Glamour 4, Willpower 6
                                      Health Levels: OK, -1, -2, -5, Incapacitated
                                      Attack: Bite for six dice (lethal); claw for five dice (lethal).
                                      Redes: *Corruption, Scuttle, Weapons (claws and fangs), Wyrd
                                      • Corruption: The Svart can inspire frenzy in others. The Svart spends a Willpower point, and anyone in the vicinity must make a Willpower roll (difficulty of the chimeras permanent Glamour), or succumb to the frenzy, indulging in their darkest, nastiest desires. The chimera may use this Rede on mortals if it also possesses the Enchantment or Wyrd redes.
                                      ---

                                      POLLUTION NOCNITSA
                                      Pollution Elementals are also favorite Nocnitsa. These are both akin to Inanimae and Plague Nervosa, but are far deadlier. The Near Dreaming adjacent to large cities are thick with these creatures, as are Dream Realms connected to Industry and many Nightmare Realms.


                                      Hogling (Smog)
                                      These appear as roughly humanoid clouds of noxious fumes, ominously rolling toward their targets. As with other elemental chimera of corruption, these Nocnitsa can be found in polluted areas in great numbers. They normally remain inactive unless disturbed, but will prey on Changelings and other beings when the opportunity presents itself.
                                      Attributes: Strength 5, Dexterity 6, Stamina 2, Perception 3, Intelligence 2, Wits 2
                                      Abilities: Alertness 5, Athletics 4, Kenning 5, Brawl 1, Dodge 6, Stealth 8, Streetwise 6, Gremayre 4
                                      Glamour 7, Willpower 3, Banality 0
                                      Health: OK, -1, -2, -4, Inc
                                      Redes: Befuddle, Enchantment, Fester, Flight, Hide, Poof Begone, Traverse Dreaming.

                                      H’ruggling (Sludge)
                                      Also called Monsturds and Stinklings, these gross chimera appear as roughly humanoid piles of waste. They lumber and ooze toward their victims, leaving slimy trails in their wakes. As with other elemental chimera of corruption, these Nocnitsa can be found in polluted areas in great numbers. They normally remain inactive unless disturbed, but will prey on Changelings and other beings when the opportunity presents itself.
                                      Attributes: Strength 6, Dexterity 2, Stamina 7, Perception 2, Intelligence 2, Wits 1
                                      Abilities: Alertness 4, Brawl 4, Kenning 3, Survival (sewers) 5
                                      Glamour 5, Willpower 9, Banality 0
                                      Health Levels: OK, -1, -2, -4, Inc
                                      Redes: Armor (4), Corruption, Fester, Meld (Corrupted Earth), Venom, Wyrd

                                      Wakshaani (Toxins)
                                      These appear as roughly humanoid blobs of purplish fluid covered with blotches, veins and bubbling wart. As with other elemental chimera of corruption, these Nocnitsa can be found in polluted areas in great numbers. They normally remain inactive unless disturbed, but will prey on Changelings and other beings when the opportunity presents itself.
                                      Attributes: Strength 5, Dexterity 5, Stamina 6, Perception 2, Intelligence 2, Wits 3
                                      Abilities: Athletics (swim) 5, Alertness 3, Brawl 4, Crafts (fishing) 5,Dodge 5, Kenning 4, Stealth 5
                                      Glamour 7, Willpower 3, Banality 0
                                      Health: OK, -1, -2, -4, Inc
                                      Redes: Aggravated Damage (Venom), Corruption, Element Sense, Fester, Traverse Dreaming, Venom, Wyrd
