    Backer Credits missing from PoD

    09-29-2017, 11:50 PM
    Yo. So I just got my PoD copy in the mail. Overall happy that I finally got it (and can start seriously considering starting up a chronicle)...however I noticed a big omission from the book:

    The Backer Credits are missing.

    Checking between my PDF, Backer Credits are not where they're supposed to be (after Appendix 2 and before the Index). And it's not listed in the ToC either. Da hell happened?

    Considering this and how a bunch of errata was apparently ignored, I'm now starting to get concerned this thing really was rushed out the door...somehow...

                                                      So considering how costly life as an Inanimae is in C20 I do think it is a reasonable request to ask to see some results of the playtests to understand why this design decision, completely unrelated to both their DA incarnation as well as CtD version, was decided on.
