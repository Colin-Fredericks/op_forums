      Maybe the satyr has some griffon in his lineage?

                                          Justifying a Satyr with wings is rather easy as said earlier, there are plenty of horned and goatlegged being with wings like succubi/incubi and traditional demonic look in general including mediaval depictions of the Devil itself. Unseelie, and Balor, Sidhe specifically could also have more "monstrous" wings like batwings Etc. While Dougal Sidhe, especially after how their House works in C20' would soar on mechanical clocwork wings.
                                          Trolls and Redcaps fit the bill as far as gargoyles go, as both are know for occasionally manifesting stony skin aswell.
