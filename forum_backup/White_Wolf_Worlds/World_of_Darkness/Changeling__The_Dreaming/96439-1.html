                    Yep; people are passionate about the game.

                    Ajax: to the extent that “soul-stealing” exists in CtD, it is limited specifically to the Sidhe, and is handled not unlike the fae abductions at the heart of CtL — except that the Sidhe are more sympathetic versions of CtL’s fetches. One of the last supplements of the line even provides a rough analogue for CtL’s changelings themselves: the mortals who were abducted to allow the Sidhe to return come back with a vengeance, changed by their experience in Arcadia.

                    Oddly, CtD’s commoners also have a rough counterpart in CtL: Charlatans — fae trapped in the mortal world and looking for a way to go home. The differences are two-fold: first, Charlatans are wholly fae; CtD’s commoners are human/fae hybrids in much the same way that werewolves are human/spirit hybrids: they were born that way, but didn’t realize it until their Chrysalis, when they awakened to their fae nature.

                    Really, CtL and CtD are very much flip sides of the same coin. This is most apparent when you compare Banality to Clarity: in both cases, the high end pushes you toward the mortal side of your being, while the low end pushes you toward the fae side. The difference lies in how each game portrays those extremes: CtL portrays the human side as a good thing (e.g., sane, humane, and creative) while the fae side is something to avoid; CtD portrays the human end as, well, banal, and the fae side, while not precisely good, generally came across as the less dangerous of the two.

                    The above illustrates how I think a Changeling Translation Guide should be done. It also illustrates the folly of trying to incorporate thematic elements of Lost into the official Dreaming game: they are thematically about as polar opposite as you can get while still talking about the fae.

                    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                        V20 felt like it took a lot of stuff from old editions and stripped out the things they wanted, and then stuck what they chose back into the book.
                        W20 had a bit more finessing, but it still felt much more like "we updated some stuff, tweaked some rules, but it's pretty much copy/pasted from what we found."
                        M20 has apparently been completely re-written. Things were "taken" from other books, but more like the essence of those things were looked at and something new that represented it all was created.
                        To be honest, I don't know Wraith, so I can't say if the Wr20 stuff is more like Apocalypse, or Ascension.
                        Changeling only had 2 editions, so either they only have to amalgamate two editions into one with some updates for 2015, or to take the essence of what is/was C:tD and tweak it and write it to emphasize and present it in a way that keeps the game the same as it was for those who love/d it, as well and introducing new people to a game that fits a contemporary idea of "fae" now that Modern Fantasy is a "thing". I don't think that C20 should be re-envisioned, or re-designed... but I think that casting lights in new areas and presenting it a bit differently could help make it appeal to more folks as I think that its reputation was created by those who had different expectations than what the game actually was intended to be about.

                        I think that we'll have to see who Rich chooses to be the line developer, and what their vision for the book/game line is. If this book is going to be announced at Gen Con, chances are, Rich is already talking to folks and laying the groundwork... if he finds someone who he trusts and who's super-eager to take the C:tD ball and run with it like Satyr, then I think it could be amazing.
