    Fae, Chimaera, "city spirits" and "civilization personifications"

    01-29-2021, 06:30 PM
    So i am planning on running a vampire plot where I am going to be including some elements from the greater world of darkness lore, including changeling.

    To sum it up roughly, the big bad evil guy of the story is going to have a dastardly plan to achieve a form of apotheosis, which the players will be working to stop. But I wanted to run the concept by some people more knowledgeable in the system to check if its even compatible with changeling rules without heavy homebrewing.

    My question is, do there exist chimaera/fae creatures built on the dreams of a civilization? More concretely, would the concept of "Rome" and the dream of roman civilization manifest itself as a distinct being within the dreaming? Sorta like a personification of a nation or city, the way Germania is for germany or Uncle Sam for america.

    In short the villian is a mad vampire from a gothic tribe, who went on a diablerization spree in ancient rome during the fall. And in his power trip, he devised a way to not just consume the power of the rulers of rome, but the very city and civilization itself. Essentially wanting to "diablerize" this powerful chimaera spirit and taking its place in a way.


    Would such an entity be able to exist? If yes, is there any in lore way to siphon its power for yourself, or would I have to come up with my own thing?

      (ANYONE MORE VERSED IN CHANGELING CORRECT ME IF I'M WRONG)

      Chimaera if I remember correctly (and I'm sure you know of already) are associated with the Dreaming. They are more in line with individual forms of creative thought given glamour. Cities and general hard-stream urbanism are banal and drab-ish to Chimaera. Chimaera can be very powerful, but they don't really encompass what you're looking for (and again, correct me if I'm wrong about that). Not only that Vampires are inherently banal and don't work well with the Dreaming. The best Umbra they have the most and best interaction with seems to be the Low Umbra (which makes sense they're undead beings) and the High Umbra (with the use of Aspex of course, but without that they don't even interact with this place at all). Some bloodlines dealt with the Middle Umbra, but those are rare; though with your plot it'd make sense for your antagonist to either have the blood magic or Disciplines (since he diablerized alot or maybe he could've learned from those said bloodlines) to somehow have access to it.

      Your plot is utter genius though and I love it!! It sounds like the Cappadocian Antediluvian scheme, but with shift perception toward a particular persona regarding a city.

      I'd generally put the personified concept of Rome in idea form as an Umbrood (Spirit) entity associated with the trappings of Mage: The Ascension. The metaverse of WoD never made it clear if the High Umbra (or its more proper term, Astral Reaches) and the Dreaming is the samething, but like the Dreaming, the High Umbra takes on dialectical conceptions of thought and there it becomes full on entities or landscapes if I remember.

      There other more obvious choice is definitely the Middle Umbra (or the Spirit Wilds). What makes the Middle Umbra and High Umbra different is that the former is more based on Spirits as they are individually, while Spirits (or Umbrood) from the High Umbra take the general idealizations of a form of thought or concept. From what is observed in W:tA, there is a precedent and in-game stuff regarding Spirit personifications of different cities. I don't know if you wanted the Chimaera and/or entity to be C:TD, but the Middle-Umbra seems to be your best bet. Now this makes me want an official Spirit stat sheet and backstory for the Spirit of Rome.

      Though if you want to roll with a Chimaera still, I'm sure you can do it; but again, Vampires have the MOST trouble with even entering or interacting with the Dreaming due to their banality. Not even that I can't think of any blood magic or Disciplines that'd even let them interact with chimerical material and entities. Though maybe if your antagonist is a Malkavian or Kisayd, it might work out.

                      For something like what you’re describing you might instead want to look towards umbral spirits like City Elders or representations in the High Umbra.

                      That said though, this is possible. Humans create chimera and it suffuses into the environment. The Near Dreaming reflects the Waking World so you have chimerical versions of cities there but going deeper chimera naturally take on symbols that represent popular consciousness and Dreams. Just like how some of the most powerful Changeling institutions are backed by their allusions to King Arthur and Camelot (such as King David and Concordia) it stands to reason that there would be similar beings manifested, such as Roma or Uncle Sam.

                      In fact in the C20 Player’s Guide they give rules for a new Child of the Dreaming called ‘Lycians’ who are chimera that have actualized into a personified form akin to Changelings or Inanimae. An example Lycian they even include is a Lycian that embodies the spirit of Rome.

                      •Cities are Banal
                      -This is a take that Changeling used to run with that they have largely discarded for C20, akin to the notion that technology is banal. C20’s philosophy is that glamour and banality are what you make of them. So while many stretches of cities may indeed be banal with some laden with Nightmare Glamour their will also be parts that are full of Dream Glamour and on an individual level kiths that love cities like Nockers and Redcaps will find things glamourous that Boggans and Trolls might find banal.

                      In this vein vampires are also no longer guaranteed to reek of banality. While they are locked in stasis, young vampires are more likely to be filled with Dark/Nightmare Glamour which is joined by a slowly developing banality as the vampire grows older and more static, conservative, and close-minded.
