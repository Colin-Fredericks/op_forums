    So I wasn't originally going to post this, but someone who saw me mention it elsewhere urged me to so I figure worst case scenario it gets ignored.

    There are a couple places where non western Fae are centered around European identity and cultural artifacts unecessarily and it makes me a little reticent to invite players from those cultures to play the game. I'm not talking about the big stuff that's been talked about over the history of the game because I know the writers didn't have leeway to just scrap the big stuff. I mean the couple of places where C20 actually drew stronger ties between non European cultures and the European framing of the game. I'm going to list the places where that can be minimized with a few word changes here, and I hope that they are at least considered.

    Page 376-377 The Huaka'i Po make several references to the Fomorians. I know that actually changing the name of the Fomorians isn't going to happen here, but just calling them the Elder Dark when they come up in reference to non Western Changelings will go a long way towards avoiding people of Non European descent reading this and having that cognitive moment when their culture is wrapped up in a myth that is from the Irish Book of Invasions. No matter how it's framed here (and I mean even in C20 it's a pretty clear invocation of the invasion cycle) that existing reference lingers. It may even be an opportunity to drop some hook about the Elder Dark are known by other names around the world, and were no more monolithic than mordern Fair Folk are.

    Page 394 The sidebar says "The Higher Hunting Grounds, the Nunnehi realms within Arcadia. . . " Maybe it's framed this way in a previous book I haven't seen, but I always saw it described as the Nunnehi's home within the Dreaming. Framing the Higher Hunting Grounds specifically around Arcadia ties them up in a European part of the canon in ways that don't seem to actually contribute to the game. If this could please be reframed to part of the Dreaming it would help to make the game more inviting to Native players.

    These changes aren't huge, but I really do feel like they'll make the game a little bit more inclusive.
