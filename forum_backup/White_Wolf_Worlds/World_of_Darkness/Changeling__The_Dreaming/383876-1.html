The sidhe replaces the human soul, which gets taken to Arcadia (to the sidhe, it's like sponsoring an exchange student). Nobles implies that the time lapse before the sidhe's Chrysalis begins is typically very short, if not immediate.

                    One consequence of the way the sidhe do things is that the common kith remember what it is to be human. The sidhe, however, only have faint and distant human memories, enough to fill them in on the life of the mortal they replaced.

                    What also continues to bug me is...is there a difference in any meaningful, you-can-tell-as-a-player sense between a sidhe who did the Changeling Way merging and a sidhe who kicked out the original human soul? Does one not have memories of being human?
                    Fortunately, House Scathach provides an illustration, having members who followed both of those courses of action. Members of the House who stayed on Earth find their kith Birthrights lessened, and require Fae Realm 1 to affect them (that is, they're treated as commoners for the purposes of cantrips). Members of the House who have recently returned to Earth have their kith Birthrights at full strength, and require Fae Realm 2, like other sidhe. Neither branch of House Scathach suffers Banality's Curse, so there's no way to tell what would happen to the Frailty.

                                        Nobles suggested war, whether internal or external. In the second Book of Houses, House Leanhaun suggested they'd chosen to come back too, and that Arcadia had become seriously imbalanced towards Winter and the Unseelie.                                         We can reasonably guess that the Fomorians are why the final four Houses came to Earth, but why the initial eight Houses came is ambiguous.

Does one not have memories of being human?
                                                        From a player's perspective, Changeling: the Dreaming gave us four "flavors" of sidhe on Earth:

                                                        * Like commoner changelings, many members of House Scathach embraced the whole reincarnation/chrysalis method of survival on Earth, rather than withdrawing to Arcadia. As such, they are hybrid human/sidhe entities who have a full-fledged human life before the Chrysalis. Thanks to their human soul, they do not suffer Banality's Curse (the usual sidhe flaw) or any other special problems with banality. If slain (and given a wake), they reincarnate as Scathach sidhe.

                                                        * Many of the first non-Scathach sidhe to return to Earth (post-moon landing) "swapped" with teen or adult humans - the better to establish a bulwark for their House. They immediately went into a very traumatic form of Chrysalis, as their faerie selves scrubbed the human personality from their new brain. They suffer (more or less) complete amnesia about the former human life, and people surrounding these "first wave" changelings often assumed they were having psychotic breaks, resulting in more than a few being institutionalized as mentally ill.

                                                        The "first wave" sidhe suffer Banality's Curse (gaining "bonus" banality), and more. By the time of a default campaign (the Nineties) their "skins had toughened", but many of the in-character pieces of fiction talk about how badly they were hit by banality in the early days of the Resurgence. One character (in Nobles: the Shining Host) even says that the "first wave" sidhe couldn't use modern technology (in the first days back) without being destroyed by banality.

                                                        * Some of the early returners (and most of the later ones), on the other hand, swapped with very young children (even infants). So, while they don't have a human soul, these "second wave" sidhe had a "human" childhood (which they remember), with human "parents" et al. They're thus more comfortable with the human world and technology - but still suffer Banality's Curse.

                                                        * Finally, the "Immortal Eyes" chronicle gave us a nasty version of the sidhe soul swap. Going into details involves spoilers, but the human soul was cast screaming into the void - not sent to Arcadia. Characters watching (even other sidhe) were horrified at the process, likening it to murder. Last I heard, only one sidhe has ever used this method, though - but its implied that it left him very susceptible to bedlam.

                                                        And its worth pointing out that only the Scathach sidhe are known to reincarnate (again) as sidhe - at least from Earth. The other Houses, having not undergone the changeling ritual, either experience true death, or reincarnate as other sorts of (non-sidhe) fae.
