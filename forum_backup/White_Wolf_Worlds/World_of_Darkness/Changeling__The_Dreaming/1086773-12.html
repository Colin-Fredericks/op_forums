                            Inanimae section, slivers are... a problem. Someone else mentioned issues with them being Unleash-only. In the old system they had their own cantrips, all flavored by the elements. To have them Unleashed-only is bad bad bad. For one Inanimae suffer from banality worse (it really messes them up) and having them go Unleashed-only without any way to control the effects or lessen the Nightmare Die stuff is going to cause problems. Think this section not only needs errrata but re-thinking.

                            In real errata for inanimae, their Slivers are chimerical unless they call upon the Wyrd. This is a problem because they can only Unleash, and Unleashing forces you to immediately call upon the Wyrd and take your true shape, and all effects become immediately real.

                            Mannikins take extra banality from sources may be an errata, because their write-up mentions they move more comfortably among the Autumn. This could be fixed by re-writing the fluff, making it so they like being around people more, but their closeness to humanity also makes them more susceptible to Banality (aka becoming a real boy!!!!). Also, no mention of how Gladeling Mannikins exist.
