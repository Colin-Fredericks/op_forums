    What if Changelings were actually shaped mortal souls?

    09-20-2020, 04:31 PM
    So I was pondering world of darkness stuff while listening to the Mage Podcast and doing rote functions at work. I had one idea pop into my head what if the Changeling Way wasn’t Fae hiding in Mortals but what if it was actually True Fae shaped the souls of Mortals into True Fae Lite ^tm?

    So their memories are screwed but it’s not like they were Fae in their memories of the past, or they could even be memories of their mortal lives encountering them Fae. And so the True Fae could actually be shaped from the stories of the Mortal who they shaped the soul of into nascent Changelings?

    Not saying this is truth, but it would be interesting to explore in a game. Goes with the thought of the nature of Arcadia and the Fae aren’t who we think they are. Hell the Sidhe might not even be the Firstborn they could even be the Changelings of the past making the Tuatha the Firstborn of Dark Ages.

    Thoughts?

              Close, though I do have Lost Style Changeling appear as new wild card phenomena in my setting.

              The idea here is the Shattering was hitting the Fae Lords were retreating into the newly separated Dreaming and before they leave they make pacts and reshape mortal souls into the legends they inspired to sort of act as the stewards of the world, rather then take mortals as servants to Faery (which they did as well to create some of the Dark Age Changelings). This resulted in the orphaned changelings believing they were always Fae and feeling they were abandoned by their Lords. So a lot of their history is made up from communal myths and shared hallucinations/dreamt lies. The Sidhe could be the Returned Firstborn, the Previous Changelings of the Dark Ages who believe they were nobles forgetting their true origins, or even souls of spirited away Mortals or children of Fae born in the Dreaming.

                              Originally posted by valismedsen View Post
                              Are you the author of the Changeling 5th Edition guide, Eldagusto?

                              Would you share some more in-depth rules for Fetches you use?
                              Nono that was Charlie Cantrell my friend.

                              But for Fetches I'm thinking of tying each of them to an Arcadian Sidhe. They return to the Autumn World one way or another, either escape or win their freedom or are even sent from Arcadia. Their time in Arcadia is magnified so they could have spent centuries there rather then the decades they were kicked out of their body in real time.

                              They will return to the Autumn World as a Chimerical beings but they will share a fragment essence with the Sidhe, that might take the form of a scrape of their shadow, or true name, reflection, echo, or something ephemeral and they will use this fragment to guide them through dreaming to the Autumn World. They will then create a husk out of scraps of Nature (like sticks wrapped into a doll with grass twine), Flesh/Blood like a bundle of bloody teeth, or scraps of garbage like rags and cans made into a basic puppet. They will have a husk rating like the Inanimae but they wouldn't have an anchor. They would then choose from a set of customizable Birthrights, and a set of customizable echoes like with Dark Age Fae, they would basically be folklore banes for supernatural creatures so it could be being hurt by churchbells, or not being able to cross streams.

                              they would then choose a favored Realm tied to the nature of what their husk was made out of and if they are slain their body returns to the state of what their husk was made of, so they collapse into a pile of autumn leaves, or bloody dog bones.

                              Their body might look different to their Sidhe counterpart because they might have grown older in Arcadia, or even stayed younger depending on the nature of their stay.
                              But they often feel driven to reclaim their body from the Sidhe, this has driven some to slay the Sidhe, but others have found a way to fuse with the Sidhe into a more complete form.

                              I also have the Spirited Away Changelings, changelings who made deals with Arcadia and spend a century and a day there in indentured servitude before returning to the Autumn World with misty remembrance but also bound by barely remembered oaths to work towards the goals of their patrons. They would be similar to the Fetches but they would have their own body upon returning, you might treat them as being vulnerable to Banality like Arcadian Sidhe. I'm still torn between whether they might be treated like Arcadian Sidhe in needing to take a mortal body like the Sidhe did and thus sending a Tithed Soul which could return as a Fetch.


                              They might even have a chosen favored Art where they maybe they add that Art rating to rolls in that Art, but if I do it that way it might mean the other Arts cost more to purchase.

                              But the Spirited Away Changelings would give a weird impression of sort of protokiths, so some changeling might speculate if this was how the Kiths started ages ago but forgotten by the mists of History. They would also not necessarily be working for the Tuatha, many if not most of would have made deals with Fomorian Forces. Still working on how their Courts would work, they might actually use the Seasonal Courts, or the Courts of Dawn, Day, Dusk, and Night rather then Seelie/Unseelie.
