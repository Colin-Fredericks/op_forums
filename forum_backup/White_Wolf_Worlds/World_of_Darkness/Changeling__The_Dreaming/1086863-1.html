                                            Overall I'm liking it so far. Can't tell you how pleased I am that this is finally a thing, and indeed still getting over disbelief that new C:tD exists at all.

                                            That being said...

                                            This is an artistic choice that it's too late to change, and in the grand scheme of things it's not so huge of a detail, but its representative of a thing that happens a lot and always bugs me when it does.

                                            The Dutchy of Dogwood. West Virginia gets lumped in with Virginia yet again. I still have to explain to people in this the year 2017 that West Virginia is indeed a state. I have to explain why the distinction is important one hundred and fifty four years after West Virginia shed blood to be on the right side of history. I wouldn't be bothered as much if it were at least grouped in with the Dutchy of Appalachia, but for some reason the Dutchy of Appalachia doesn't include the only state entirely within the Appalachians and instead that state is grouped into political union with the state its entire existence is predicated on breaking away from. I also wouldn't be bothered as much if there were even one sentence in Dutchy of Dogwood that mentioned West Virginia, but there isn't. Once again West Virginia is an afterthought, a punchline unworthy of notice.

                                            Also, not to be that guy, but the Eshu illustration doesn't really match up with the Kith's description. Its very... White.

                                            I'm not saying that Eshu have to be the token POC Kith, but the Eshu description on the same page calls out the Kith's African heritage multiple times, and the only core Kith illustration that features a POC is the Recap. The one with the monster teeth. I'm just saying maybe some more thought could have been put into those choices.
