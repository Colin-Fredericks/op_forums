                                          With Swan Maidens, "going further than kissing" probably triggers the frailty... You can talk to your ST, but I think you're fundamentally misunderstanding the Kith. Even kissing can be extraordinarily intimate, and the point of the Swan Maiden is to maintain her "purity". You keep asking how to bypass this frailty, but being sexual in basically any form is as antithetical to Swan Maidens as being dishonorable is to Trolls. Their inability to have sexual pleasure is the point of the Kith. Asking how to be sexual without triggering the Swan Maiden Frailty is like asking how to never help people in need as a Boggan, or how to be a Clurichaun without a horde. Swan Maidens keep it in their pants, or they lose willpower. Anything beyond that is a house rule.

                                          As for legality, there are plenty of extremely normal sexual acts (Including even oral in some states) that are "illegal", but only because our country's base laws were made by Puritans. In some states, even pre-marital sex is illegal. Nobody is ever going to go to jail for having consensual anal.

                                                  Its a mixed bag, though an interesting one for sure. To start, I can see not wanting to step on your friends turf, in terms of not all playing the same Kith. As for trying to reinterpret Swan Maidens, I actually would agree. I think they are a little bit too far from the beaten path, with more birthrights and frailties than they should have. The Dark Fate frailty is an absolutely BRUTAL frailty (and good on you for being scared of it). Swan Maidens don't need the other frailty on top of it. I would have tweaked them a bit personally, let them have sex and lose one of their Birthrights. As for a Monogamy focus, I can certainly see the merit of having a Changeling whose core ethos stems from a desire to embody fealty or love for a single person, though I would personally have just played a Fiona Troll, or a Troll with the Troubadour legacy at that point. Alternatively, to take a page from the players guide, I think a Lycian who embodies Monogamy could be interesting.

                                                  I will say that it's a bit of a loaded discussion for me, because I see the concept of sexual "purity" as a way of controlling people, and, from my subjective viewpoint, think it's actually evil. Swan Maidens embody an idea I actually think hurts real people. It enforces the Slut/Prude double standard, and tells people that virginity is something they should be very concerned about, both telling men they are unfit if they are virgins, and women that there is something wrong with them if they choose to explore that part of themselves. I know way too many people my age who put knives to their bodies because they had been sexually assaulted and felt that they were no longer pure for it. I had a friend who I would console as she cried at night because her mother kept calling her a whore, despite the fact that she had never been with anyone sexually, as if that should somehow make a difference. Swan Maidens are an idea that the patriarchy puts onto people, and its wrong.

                                                  Now, to be clear, Changeling is a horror game, and f*ck if that isn't horror. Remember that Changeling is all about conflicting natures, and is ultimately a game about tragedy; if the Changeling you are demands this idea of sexual purity, but the human you are KNOWS that sex isn't intrinsically dirty or impure, that's an internal conflict that can be a great character arc. In a world where Swan Maidens exist, yeah, I actually think your interpretation is the only way a Swan Maiden could survive. The question is whether we want those ideas informing our culture, or whether those are stories that should maybe be left to the mists.
