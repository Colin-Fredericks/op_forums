    It really doesn't help that MtA and WtA books use the names interchangeably and directly state 'this is where dreams and dreamers go at night'. I guess I fell victim to the biased POV of the splats. Fair enough, but it's annoying that I have to unlearn this now if I want to add more CtD elements consistently.

    Originally posted by monteparnas View Post
    Now, saying what causes Glamour is complicated. We have things like the Inanimae and also Glades, Freehold-like sources of Glamour that exist in wild places without contact to humans. Other than that, human-related Glamour, and as such connection to the Dreaming, tends to occur where people share inspiration instead of around the things that inspire them. If lots of children share stories about a haunted mansion around a campfire, the Glamour doesn't take form in the haunted mansion, but around the campfire, even if the result may be a version of the mansion appearing at its location in the Near Dreaming.
    The mechanical question here is whether or not Glamour is needed to shape the animate and inanimate Chimera that make up the dream landscape of the mansion. As a somewhat silly example, I could prompt one of the image creating neural nets to create a picture of the mansion which then 'dreams up' the result, and then I close the browser tab before even looking at it. I don't think this would produce a 'true' dream-space of the mansion, because there was no wonder involved anywhere in the creation process.

    Originally posted by Kakost View Post
    Maybe looking into Dark Ages Fae may help give a hint on the origins of "Glamour". DA Fae had the "Mists" - which may be hard to define, but in WtA terms it could be called perhaps the Wyld? And in MtA, perhaps it's Quintessence? Anyway, The Mists is the primordial source of magic, whatever that means. In DA Fae, there are 3 basic origins for the Fae, the Firstborn, the Inanime and the Changellings.
    That sounds a lot like the Wyld in Exalted.

    Originally posted by Kakost View Post
    Modern AIs that create "art" thou are super banal. They stifle creativity like nothing else. So much so that people are already asking themselves if "artists will lose their jobs". I cant imagine a heavier blow to the Dreaming than this - masses of dreamers actively GIVING UP on their ability to "dream" because they are being replaced by souless and emotionless machines. "What's the point of trying to make a song or paint a painting if nobody wants to buy my work because "DeepGoogle" is so much better at it? I wont even try".

    That to me would be the final blown delivered by the autumn people, and the final winter to all Fae.
    In the artist communities I frequent, this is mostly an assumed attitude from an outside perspective. The vast majority of artists I know see ML generated content for what it is: a new tool that can help the creative process. There are some who use it as inspiration or template for their own art. Others use assets created by it, and others again shift the creative process into finding the correct prompts to create the desired result.
    If anything, visual ML engines have increased the speed of the process and opened it to a whole bunch of people who previously did not even engage in visual arts. The same already happened before with digital painting (vs traditional methods) or digital audio workstations for music, including fears it would trivialize the respective art.

        Originally posted by Kakost View Post

        What's the point of calling it the Digital Web if it's just another middle umbra then?
        Who said that the Digital Web is just another Middle Umbra? Fundamentally, the Digital Web is (part of) a Realm of mathematical truth, the connection between the Correspondence Point and the rest of reality. You don't need to be digital to be about the primacy of information, though; both the Ahl-i-Batin with their Mount Qaf and the Akashayana with their Akashic Record have demonstrated this. The fact that the Verbena have also figured out how to use biology (specifically DNA) for programming just points to another sector of the broader information hypersphere that the Digital Web turns out to just be one part of. (I would also point you to the Platonic Realm as described in the STV product Paradigm Explored: Number and Shape as a sort of “bridge” between the Astral Umbra and the Hypersphere. It strikes me as the “Hermetic beachhead” in what the VAs would call “digital space”.)

        Though IMHO, using DNA for programming purposes strikes me as more of a Progenitors thing than a Verbena thing. The point remains, though, that computation needn't be a technomantic thing.

        All that said, that's a topic that has much more relevance to the Mage forum than to the Changeling forum. Bringing it (somewhat) back on topic, saying that the Digital Web is “just another Middle Umbra” is like saying that the Dreaming is “just another Umbra”. You can point out some broad similarities (e.g., Chimerical Reality is similar to the Penumbra, and the Near and Deep Dreaming are similar to how the Umbra can be divided into regions near the Penumbra and distant from it; one might even call them the Near Umbra and the Deep Umbra). But despite those similarities, that doesn't mean that the Dreaming is in all ways equivalent to the Umbra. It merely has some parallels.

                                    This has been sizzling in my brain for a while, and I think I made a step forward in constructing a possible Realm. It was still nagging me that the 'stuff' a machine dream realm would be made of would be very different to either Chimera or Emanations. At its fundamental level, it is still emotionless.

                                    So I decided to build with Shannon Entropy and Negentropy. If you read the intro description of the two, you will note some vague similarities to Glamour and Banality in an abstracted way, reduced to informational quality. There are also some inspirational analyses of Shannon Entropy in neural nets I can draw on. I think those are some basic building blocks I can work with, and it touches on the ideas Dataweaver presented here, too. A new and unique type of dreamstuff is also something my player would enjoy to discover and study.

                                    I think divorcing it from the Dreaming and the usual Realms of the Umbra is the practical way to go. The new Realm (or probably rather budding Zone) would still be connected to either, although I imagine it as something that is still in a very early stage of development. Any connections to other Realms would be very unstable and coincidental.

                                    For the landscape I have plenty of examples to draw on from the visual ML programs, though I want to make it a look into the 'mind' of those primitive nets and give it a touch of structured chaos, if that makes sense. I'm also figuring out possible encounters; the way I see it, this realm would be very Weaver-ish and Wyld-ish (sorry for the WtA terms) at the same time, so I'm not sure yet what those would be like.
