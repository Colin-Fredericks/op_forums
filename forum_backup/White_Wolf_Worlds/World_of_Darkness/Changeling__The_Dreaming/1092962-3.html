                        The one thing that makes Nockers likely to make these things and Boggans not is... setting. Boggans prefer simple crafts, Nockers like their mad science. But the point remains that, mechanically, Boggans do it better. Anyhow, here is my re-jiggering of Infusion:


                        This is the fundamental Art of Nocker craft, and most Nockers are conversant with its principles. At its lowest levels, Infusion strengthens and alters chimerical materials, making them easier for changelings to manipulate and work with. At its highest levels, this Art is an act of creation in the truest sense.

                        Infusion is based in part on the premise that there are three basic types of chimera: Incidental, Dreamed, and Forged. Incidental chimera are flimsy and difficult to work with in their native forms, but are also by far the most common form of chimera. They are fleeting and ephemeral, dreamed into existence for a few seconds before evaporating like smoke. This is also the most common kind of chimera found in the physical world, spun of daydreams and idle thoughts. Mortals cannot see them, and they quickly succumb to the ravages of Banality, especially if they are removed from the area where they came into being.

                        Dreamed chimera are somewhat hardier, able to survive indefinitely after the reverie that created them ends. Dreamed chimera spring from the minds of changelings and mortals, and are the most common type of chimera found in the Dreaming. They still can wither swiftly in the face of Banality, though.

                        A big factor in whether or not they can survive is what inspired them and where they are. More malleable, half-formed objects are also more fragile, while something like the chimerical reflection of Notre Dame Cathedral is a towering, fully-formed wonder that stands strong and firm. The more notable the inspiration for a chimera is, the stronger its reflection. A pancake house may have a reflection in the Dreaming, but it is crude and in a state of flux, shifting with every new customer that comes through the doors. The pancake house's reflection is largely Incidental, while Notre Dame's is definitely Dreamed.

                        Virtually all animate chimera are fully formed and realized, but they are also fewer in number, the population to fill the scenery. Most aren't sentient, but many can pass for it. Often the best, strongest, and most difficult-to-obtain components for chimerical item crafting come from animate chimera; a shield crafted of inanimate emeralds might have its binding emblem made of leather taken from a conquered dragon chimera.

                        Forged chimera are those crafted by Nockers and other talented artisans. They fall somewhere between the categories of chimera into their own classification. At one time, anybody could easily pull wonders from thin air, crafting draw dreamstuff with the force of their own will, but with the arrival of the Sundering and Shattering, those days are long past, at least in the Autumn World. Dream manipulation is not what it once was; while they can still create simple things like their voile at whim, creating and banishing them, and skilled Oneiromancers able to pluck items from Dreams and make them manifest temporarily are in demand, there is something to be said for the permanency of items crafted by skilled artisans.

                        Forged chimera are crafted out of materials gathered from Dreams and the Dreaming and put together in laboratories, workshops, and garages. These include many treasures and more potent chimerical items as well as actual animate creations like clockwork and robotic servants, steeds, and pets; in recent years, many of these show a marked steampunk aesthetic due to the growing popularity of this genre.

                        Most who make animate chimera settle for a semblance of life; true sentience is quite difficult to create. On truly rare occasions, though, it does spontaneously ignite, and these events are becoming gradually more common due to march of technology and human fears and hopes of true artificial intelligence.

                        While forged chimera can be created without the Art of Infusion, its use makes the task easier. It was developed over the long centuries of the Interregnum, as a method of coping with the loss of Glamour and magic. It was originally the creation of Nockers, crafted from a variety of scientific and pseudo-scientific pursuits in alchemy, gematria, astrology, and similar pursuits. While the exact date for when it became a fully-fledged Art is impossible now to determine, some believe that it saw its culmination during the early Renaissance period. For a long time the Nocker guilds held tight rein on this art, with few from other Kiths able to ever learn more than the basics, if that. However, as the Age of Exploration opened up the world and new views of liberation and freedom spread, so too did knowledge. Sometimes it was rogue Nockers sharing their secrets, sometimes other Kiths working out the principles themselves or getting a hold of Nocker texts. The Bes Din, the Nocker governing council, tries to clamp down on this, but eventually had to accept that this was impossible, so chose instead to act as the primary source for Infusion; to this day, most who learn the Art register with the Bes Din.

                        While Nockers see this Art as something of a science, many others see it more as an Art. There are lines crossed, as there are truly in many fields, for a blacksmith must have both artistic style and technical skill.

                        Because it IS an Art of the Autumn World, crafted after the Shattering, newly-arrived Arcadian Kithain won't ever have any knowledge of it. Some Arcadians look down their noses at this Art, but others, particularly members of House Dougal, have been fascinated by its development, and House Dougal is without a doubt one of its strongest supporters now.

                        For obvious reasons the most frequently-used Realm with this Art is Prop. However, many of the lower levels can be used in other creative ways to bolster and strengthen the chimerical world.

                        Unleashing Infusion
                        Infusion at its heart is about creation. Unleashing it is rare, but can occur; a desperate attempt, for instance, to strengthen and save a fading chimerical companion or beloved heirloom about to be unraveled by Banality. Unleashings are accompanied by great bursts of power and energy, often in the form of electricity, light, and heat. Example commands might be "save my friend" or "bolster my shield."

                        Infusion Bunks
                        Infusion bunks are some of the easiest and most obvious to imagine; they involve crafting. Hammering, smelting, sewing, tinkering. Even the most hastily-drawn bunk usually involves the tools and trappings of an artisan; a ringing hammer, a few quick stitches of a needle through one's own flesh.

                        Harden
                        This cantrip allows a changeling to "harden" Incidental chimera into a form that can be forged. The target effectively goes from Incidental to Dreamed. Its fate from there depends on what happens next. Left exposed to Banality, it could still soon disappear. Harvested or moved to a more receptive environment, though, the object could survive and stabilize, and perhaps even gain more detail and cohesion.

                        Though typically used in Inanimate chimera, this CAN be used on the Animate. It is not commonly done, as the results can be unpredictable, as a nebulous sprite suddenly becomes a more coherent creature shaped by whatever whimsy might be in the air.

                        System: This cantrip costs at least one point of Glamour always; that Glamour is required to bolster the chimera being affected. The Realm used is determined by the nature of the chimera affected; use the appropriate realm for the non-chimerical counterpart. This means anything inanimate will be Nature or Prop, anything animate will be Nature or Actor; a building or park would be Scene. Fae may be used as a secondary realm but never the primary realm for this cantrip.

                        An Incidental chimera that has had this cantrip successful cast upon it can be forged as if it were a Dreamed chimera.

                        The number of successes determines the duration of the cantrip. For purposes of crafting, this often means you need at least two successes for it to be of much use, but for some other purposes one success could do, like giving that enchanted bully's fear of his old neighbor's pit bull sudden definite form.

                        1 success - One hour
                        2 successes - One day
                        3 successes - One week
                        4 successes - One month
                        5 successes - Permanent

                        Type: Chimerical

                        Toughen
                        This allows a changeling to toughen any chimerical material (even a changeling's fae mien), making it more resistant to damage and Banality.

                        System: As with Harden above, the nature of the chimerical object to be affected determines the Realm needed for casting. Using this on changelings requires Fae. Also, at least one Glamour must be spent.

                        Any chimerical substance affected by this cantrip gains an additional Health Level for every two successes earned by the casting of the cantrip. In addition, all rolls for resisting Banality are reduced by one for every two successes gained.

                        Multiple castings of this cantrip do not have cumulative effects. When used on chimera, the effects are permanent. When used on a changeling, they only last for the scene, and extra health levels gained disappear when damage is taken.

                        Type: Chimerical

                        OOO FUBAR Generation
                        While electricity is not unknown in the chimerical world, it usually either takes the form of lightning or some kind of strange Nocker machine that arcs to anything nearby — most things that ought to use electrical power to operate run instead on steam, water, wind, or pure Glamour. Even most nocker-tech runs on steam; having been around for centuries, steam is the easiest power source to create in the Dreaming, dating back to Roman times. The fae, and particularly Nockers, were far ahead of humanity in its harnessing, and Changeling steam-power is vastly more potent.

                        This Cantrip is the primary method for gathering the power source for any sort of chimerical device that uses electricity or magnetism. Nockers and others have found that much advanced science works poorly in the Dreaming, things like chemical reactions. Even things like explosions and combustion don't quite work the same, which is why chimerical firearms are so very difficult and expensive to craft.

                        At the heart of harnessing electricty in the Dreaming is the FUBAR, also known as Will-o-the-Wisps, Ignis Fatuus, Fiar's Lanterns, Jack-o-Lanterns, Hinkypunks, Hobby Lanterns, Brujahs, Luces del Dinero, Luces del Tesoro, Fi Follet, Feu-Follet, and dozens more names depending on where you look. Seriously, check out the wiki article, there's all kinds of info on the different terms for these.

                        What they are for the Changelings are bobbing little energy fields, sources of pure Glamour that break numerous laws of physics. For instance, unlike magnets that must have two poles, some FUBARs have only one, positive or negative. Many also seem to have a mind of their own, and some have even been known to get temperamental or 'go bad.'

                        Their real-world Wyrd component is well-known by scientists and stories, associated most often with swamps and lowlands.

                        This cantrip allows for a changeling to spontaneously create FUBARs and bend them to his well. It is not actually known if this cantrip creates FUBARs or simply summons them, but either way this cantrip allows for the easy collecting of them, much easier surely than going out and trying to capture wild ones.

                        System: While Fae 5 is best for fully manipulating FUBARs, Fae 3 is all that is required to summon one. Anything less will require an Unleashing, and I don't need to tell you the dangers of using an Unleashing to summon a powerful source of Glamour.

                        This cantrip also only summons the FUBAR; actually containing it is another matter. Naturally, most will have some sort of containment set up ahead of time. These can be surprisingly simple; a metal hoop, a crystal sphere, a wire globe. A changeling can trap a generated FUBAR with an Intelligence + Science or Gremayre roll, with an appropriate specialty in Gematria (the most common Nocker specialty) or a similar esoteric field of study. The number of successes determines the number of Glamour points the FUBAR has.

                        A point of Glamour must be spent to use this cantrip; whether it acts as the seed for the FUBAR or a lure is again a subject of debate.

                        Type: Chimerical

                        OOOO Animantis
                        Golems are the automated servitors of Nockers, and most serve and accompany their creators willingly. Like all chimera, golems may range widely in power, size, and appearance. Like all chimera, they may range widely in power, size, and appearance. The only constraints on a golem's features or capabilities are an artist's talents, materials, and imagination. Most golems have a mechanical appearance, though the use of certain cantrips like Smoke and Mirror (Legerdemain 5) can help to project a more natural appearance. While this method is not the only one to make chimerical companions, it is one of the most potent and stable methods, in part due to the use of a FUBAR and well-established techniques.

                        Golems of old often looked like the "traditional" clay humanoids, suits of armor, or intricate wind-up, clockwork, and mechanical animals. As technology has risen, so has the arrival of '50s science-fiction movie and pulp-fiction robots, steampunk automatons, and anime-inspired androids. Golems are often crafted as pets and beats of burden also, taking on animal forms both familiar and fantastic.

                        System: You must have at least Fae 4 to be able to use this power.

                        Golem creation is an arduous process, even by this method. To create a golem there are four principle steps:

                        1) Gathering the materials. This includes anything necessary to make the golem itself, plus possible machinery to power it up and the FUBAR.

                        2 & 3) The golem's creation. This involves two sets of extended rolls. The first is Intelligence + Science or Gremayre with an appropriate specialization like Gematria, the second is Intelligent + Crafts. The base difficulty is 4 plus the level of the golem, with the chart below laying out the number of successes required and the number of points you get to build with. Each roll equals two full days of work (at least eight hours a day); golem creation is a lengthy process. Use the basic Crafting rules in C20 otherwise. You can shoot for more successes if you want to add greater aesthetic detail to your golem.

                        Golem creation works the same way as Chimerical Companion creation, though for some particularly potent Redes you may need special materials. Treat powers like Legedermain 5 to give the golem a more natural appearance as you would Treasure creation. All golems created via this method are non-sentient initially, meaning you can not buy Willpower with these points. They can be programmed to seem quite life-like, especially when components from animate chimera are used, but ultimately they are not sentient.

                        A non-sentient golem's Intelligence and Wits may not exceed its level; Perception has no such limit. Therefore, a level 1 golem could have a maximum of 1 dot each in Intelligence and Wits. Skills and Knowledges are the best way to augment a non-sentient golem's skill set.

                        Lastly, you must obtain a FUBAR of at least the level you are trying to create; every dot of Glamour the FUBAR possesses over the level you're shooting for translates as one free starting dot of Glamour for the creation, up to a level equal to the golem's level +1. Yes, this does mean it's easier to power up lesser creations. The process of successful animating a golem uses up the FUBAR, though it can affect the transfer of sentience if this is part of the process (See Gilgul below).

                        Level 1: A conversation piece, simple pet, or minor servant. Typically capable of simple functions and commands or low level mimicked behavior. 20 to 25 points, minimum 10 successes for each stage to reach minimum level, plus 1 success on the Crafting roll only for each point up to maximum (so 5 extra successes for a 25 point golem).

                        Level 2: A minor golem, semi-intelligent and able to communicate and be of assistance. Commands must still be fairly simple, mimicked behavior can often seem more convincing. 26 to 30 points, minimum 15 successes for each stage, plus 1 success on the Crafting roll only for each point up to maximum.

                        Level 3: A useful golem, often capable of clear speech and an ability to carry out more in-depth instructions and commands or pass as sophisticated. 35 to 40 points, minimum 20 successes for each stage, plus 1 success on the Crafting roll only for each point up to maximum.

                        Level 4: A golem of significant power, able to understand and carry out complex commands and duties and even look self-aware. 45 to 50 points, minimum 25 successes for each stage, plus 1 success on the Crafting roll only for each point up to maximum.

                        Level 5: A very powerful golem, just this side of truly sentient often, able to follow a wide array of plans and functions. 65 to 70 points, minimum 30 successes for each stage, plus 1 success on the Crafting roll only for each point up to maximum.

                        Yes, this means that it can take over four months to craft a very powerful golem, not including the gathering of materials. Given what they are capable of, you can see why there is demand, particularly because most golems are loyal servants, forged and crafted for the individual they are intended for and thus less flighty than a dreamed-up beast or some legendary chimera that is tracked down and tamed.

                        4) Once the golem's body is built, the changeling must hook it up to a FUBAR-powered generator. That is when this Cantrip is cast, initiating the process to effectively power it up. A botched cantrip roll means the animation process caused physical damage to the golen and the creator must repair it before making another attempt. A point of Glamour must be spent to initiate the process, after which the FUBAR takes over.

                        No matter how life-like it may seem, any golem created through this process is only going through the motions. To imbue actual life into the golem requires the next level of this Art, and thus a fifth step. Some rare golems DO come into awareness on their own; this is an entirely random affair that can not be readily predicted.

                        Type: Chimerical

                        OOOOO Gilgul
                        Gilgul is the Hebrew term for "transmigration of souls," "reincarnation," or "metamorphosis." For Nockers, it is the ultimate act of creation, for it is the process by which sentient life is created. Bestowing the breath of life involves advanced levels of study and skill; the beliefs on this have evolved over time. Many modern Nockers, for instance, talk in terms of monads, similar to quarks, though obviously this is a relatively recent development and this ability goes back much farther.

                        More importantly, the process involves a great deal of time and equipment. This cantrip is most frequently used to bring golems fully to lfie as thinking beings, but can also be used to grant any non-sentient chimera sentience.

                        Nockers take this level of their Art extremely seriously. After several incidents involving rogue Golems and rogue golem creators, they easily convinced the Concordia Parliament of Dreams to pass laws requiring anybody making use of the two highest levels of Infusion to be licensed and registered with the Grand Bes Din. They've also managed this feat in many other Kithain nations. Separate permits are required to levels 4 and 5, and the Grand Bes Din is on the watch for illegal golem creators. They reserve their most severe censure for those who would misuse Gilgul, though, for to them it is a truly sacred act. There are many Kithain who learn only Animantis and never progress to the final level for a variety of reasons, from being turned down to creating golems only for purposes where sentience might be seen as a flaw and not a boon.

                        System: When granting sentience to a golem, the Realm Fae 5 (Dweomer of Glamour) must always be used. Obviously, using Unleashing to achieve this would be an act of true and utter madness. When using this cantrip on other chimera, the nature of the chimera determines the Realm used, though Fae 5 can be used as a secondary Realm.

                        The Changeling must design a device to transfer an imprint of another sentient being's brain patterns onto his creation. This tends to involve rare materials; for example, one modern Nocker used a rod of true silver and a delta-wave helmet to collect strange monads for this purpose. Whatever the case, the device must be built and a mind found for the imprint. Many Nockers choose to use their own brains, naturally, but this does not have to be the case. Some have experimented with having two or more minds involved in the process, in the hopes of creating a unique composite. While this can indeed create a more unique individual, it is also more difficult to guarantee what personality traits might transfer to the awakened creation.

                        The device for the transfer is considered a work of Complex crafting under C20 creation rules; fortunately, once one is possessed, it can be re-used provided it was not destroyed in some lab mishap, so some might instead seek out the equipment of a previously successful craftsman.

                        No matter what, a FUBAR is required to power the transfer. For golems, this is usually the same FUBAR that gave them life; if this is the case, add 1 to the golem's base Willpower for every two dots of Glamour left in the FUBAR after the amount that goes into free Glamour points. Round down.

                        Example: A Nocker wants to power up a Level 2 golem using a Glamour 6 FUBAR. 3 of those points get transferred as an automatic base Glamour rating. 3 remain, raising the starting of the Willpower of the golem by 1 when Gilgul is activated.

                        For already active golems or chimera, subtract the creatures level +1 from the Glamour total of the FUBAR and then apply it the same way.

                        Example: A kindly Boggan wants to grant sentience to his Pooka friend's stuffed skunk friend. The chimera is only level 2, and the FUBAR holds a whopping 9 Glamour, so that leaves 6 after the initial drain, or a +3 to its base Willpower. That is one determined little skunk.

                        Sentient golems and awakened chimera can range from dimwitted to remarkably intelligent. Make use of the chimera's natural Mental Attributes as a base line. When sentience is granted, these go from being rote behaviors and programmed knowledge to a full measure of awareness. The degree to which this awareness goes is determined by a single Intelligence + Science or Gremayre roll, with the appropriate specialty, at difficulty 7, made after the Cantrip roll. The Cantrip roll is all or nothing; if it succeeds, you have a sentient chimera. The creature is friendly and loyal to its creator and/or imprint source and learns fundamental skills quickly. Over time, it may develop its own personality traits, or some may carry through from the imprint source.

                        If it fails, you'll have to boot everything back up and try again.

                        And if one or both rolls botch, the golem gains sentience but the Gilgul goes horribly awry; the golem or chimera is mentally unstable and antisocial (though this may not be immediately apparent). The creator of a rebellious or out-of-control golem or chimera may have to destroy her child to prevent a bloody rampage, something every Nocker fears.

                        No successes: The golem or chimera actually LOSES any degree of intelligent it previously had and is a blithering idiot. It may actually be less useful than it was before, if it was animated previously. The chimera can't even follow simple instructions, but is still alive and aware and loves its master like a half-witted dog would. Creating a fool is one of the risks of creating life, and puts the creator in a moral dilemma of caring for his child or extinguishing its life and hoping the Bes Din NEVER gets word of it. The golem has a Willpower rating equal to the donor's Willpower -1, so you may wind up with a half-witted but determined pet.

                        1 Success: The golem or chimera has a child-like attitude and mentality, and always will. It may exhibit one or two broad traits either from the donating mind or the donator's desires and dreams. The chimera or golem has a base Willpower of 1 and you have 2 dots you can add to Willpower or Mental or Social Attributes.

                        2 Successes: The golem or chimera likely has a child-like attitude and mentality which may slowly develop to something more mature, though rarely beyond what one might expect of a teenager. It may exhibit one or two broad traits either from the donating mind or the donator's desires and dreams. The chimera or golem has a base Willpower of 2 and you have 2 dots you can add to Willpower or Mental or Social Attributes.

                        3 Successes: The golem or chimera often begins with a child-like sense of wonder that will evolve over time depending on how it is treated, and can fully 'mature.' The chimera is likely to exhibit several major traits either from the donating mind or the donator's desires and dreams and develop more of a personality over time. The chimera or golem has a base Willpower of 3 and you have 3 dots you can add to Willpower or Mental or Social Attributes.

                        4 Successes: The golem or chimera may begin with a child-like sense of wonder that will evolve over time depending on how it is treated, and can fully 'mature,' or may begin with a more advanced and well-rounded psyche. The chimera is likely to exhibit a good number of major traits and quirks either from the donating mind or the donator's desires and dreams and develop more of a personality over time. The chimera or golem has a base Willpower of 4 and you have 4 dots you can add to Willpower or Mental or Social Attributes.

                        5 Successes: The golem or chimera may begin with a child-like sense of wonder that will evolve over time depending on how it is treated, and can fully 'mature,' or may begin with a more advanced and well-rounded psyche. The chimera is likely to exhibit a good number of major traits and quirks either from the donating mind or the donator's desires and dreams and develop more of a personality over time. The chimera or golem has a base Willpower of 5 and you have 5 dots you can add to Willpower or Mental or Social Attributes.

                        Every two successes beyond 5 adds another dot you can add to Willpower or Mental or Social Attributes; base Willpower does not rise. Such golems or chimera often exhibit uncanny degrees of wisdom and personality.

                        A sentient golem has no level cap for Mental Attributes like a non-sentient one does, so you could wind up with a highly intelligent level 1 golem.
