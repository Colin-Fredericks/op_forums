    I've never seen that many flaws with the Arts and Realms system and Changelings have never felt weak to me when compared next to the other supernaturals. Banality is often misunderstood and Autumn People is a good guidebook about it. Simply not believing in magic doesn't make someone "Banal" that makes them an average Mortal (Banality 6-7) now sure, this means if you're trying to effect said mortal with Arts things are tricky to do in a couple of seconds. However give it a minute and it becomes a lot easier through the use of Bunks or throw some Glamour at the situation. Faeries have some rediculous magic at their disposal, but it's not always quick to do. As to Realms? I've always liked them. Faerie tales and myth lore are full of the restrictions on the actions of the Fae as well as the warnings of what they will do. All it takes is some creative thinking to get some solid use out of them and a few experience points down the line the restrictions tend to slip away.

            The Glamour economy is one of the bigger problems with Changeling rules as written. Aside from that, if you assume changelings regain Glamour at a reasonable rate, Grumps can't use their faerie magic as freely as a Childling, but it's not crippling. They can still get at least one scene per session where they get to cut loose and be a powerful faerie. For one Glamour and one Willpower point, they can Call Upon the Wyrd. This makes their faerie mien, any chimerical companions they may have, and all of their magic Wyrd for one scene, so they no longer have to spend any Glamour for the rest of the scene. Once the low Glamour pool has been bypassed, as others have pointed out, overcoming most Banality ratings are fairly trivial. That only becomes problematic when you're dealing with a Dauntain or a full on Autumn Person, and any changeling is going to have issues with that.

                    There are several ways around the perceived "flaws" and a lot of them have to do with storyteller theme as much as raw (as a large amount of RAW relies on Storyteller influence for Changeling)
                    I've always seen this as a deliberate attempt to help Changeling Chronicles work in certain ways.

                    Here are a few of the available methods to doing magical stuff

                    1) Just like in Mage where I think 99% of characters out of chargen spend to raise Arete to 2, so it's only 6 freebies to add +2 to your Glamour, that's a Grump with Glamour 5, Wilder with 6 or Childling with 7.

                    2) Enchanting the target of your desires makes things easier and less glamour intensive (although sometimes tricky to achieve in the first place)

                    3) Think of realms as mini Paradigms (since we are in a MTA-CTD crossover topic) it's all about what you can get away with in a Story (namely Your personal faerie tale). Play cleverly and you don't need a high Actor realm to effect mortal and non-dreaming super-naturals. Also if you can get an additional Realm on the target you can drop difficulties by 1

                    4) Bunks! These things are important and handle similar to stunts in Exalted/Scion for the conceptualisation along with Rotes etc in Mage. A simple bunk (level 1) drops the diff by 1 so you should always use them as it doesn't require a dice action. You can, by splitting your Dice pool, get a Level 2 Bunk off in the casting turn itself (so -2 difficulty for half the dice). Given a round or two's prep time life gets easier.

                    5) Perhaps most importantly, Dross. This is so often overlooked and yet it's a major component in Changeling society and the game. It's even used as part of currency amongst the fair ones. The Dross section in chapter 7 covers a lot of possibilities but people only remember the big ones. Need an extra Glamour boost? Well that Graffiti Mural on the wall next to you may well contain a point of glimmering Glamour. Just used a load of Glamour in a fight with the Corby? Go hunting and consume Chimerical flesh to regain Glamour, the same if you've just slain the Manticore, feast and replenish as in the good old tales of heroes. Be inventive, ask the questions of the ST, Changeling is about finding the lingering magic of the Dream in the world. Often in crossover some supernaturals themselves may provide Dross (Vampire blood for example). This is what the Kenning Talent is Really Really handy for.

                    6) Reverie and Ravaging: (Also Dreamers Background) The former is quicker and easier than the latter but it's worth understanding how Both work. Odds are if you have a dreamer or know an npc who you're trying to guide to reverie that they may well be making dross too! (Some online games even implemented systems around this to give people an even standard) and any fae can turn Unseelie for a while and rip the dreams of mortals from their conciousness. The Musing and Ravaging Thresholds are again a roleplaying tool. Use them inventively.

                    7) Treasures: A very handy background often looked at in Weird and Wonderful ways but some clever tricks are overlooked. Say an ancient tome of faerie magic that grants bonuses to Realms while it's being used or a wand that lowers a cantrip difficulty for a certain Art and so forth.

                    8) Remember! When calculating Cantrip Difficulty it maxes out at 10 before you start deducting for Glamour, Bunks, Additional realms etc. So Joe Human with a banality of 7 (cantrip diff banality +4) Calculates down from 10 NOT 11.


                    Now I've said all that if people really feel the fae are lacking and want to play with the system there are some very easy ways to do this

                    1) Realms: All Realms begin at 1 dot and then you get your 5 to spend.
                    2) Glamour: Raise all starting Glamour's by 1, reduce the Freebie points cost to 2.
                    3) Cantrip Difficulty: Reduce to Banality +3
                    4) Drop the +1 Difficulty for Modifier (Time and Scene realms). This is a house rule that I use.

                        Originally posted by Darling_Rose View Post
                        Add to that the necessity to have Fae 5 rating just to fight ANYthing supernatural including your own kind, and the restrictions start to get ridiculous.
                        Fae one is sufficient if fighting Commoner Kithain, Fae 2 for Noble Kithain (Sidhe+any commoners with a Title), Fae 3 for any Chimerae and 4 for ALL other Fae beings or anything else related to the Dreaming. Level 5 is ONLY needed to affect Glamour directly, that is casting effects on Dross or Countering Cantrips or just manipulating raw Glamour in some way. So basically fighting other kithains 1-2 is quite enough but if facing other Fae beings with regularity then you may have use for Fae up to 4 and level five is mostly reserved for Sorcerers.

                        Other supernaturals are covered by Actor which does have it's problems in execution. Basically 2-3 should be enough unless you already are in dire enmity with the being, which of course raises the question that if he becomes an enemy through your cantrip do further castings suddenly require Actor 5 which seems to be the case by RAW.

                        Besides one can nicely move around these restrictions through creative use of Prop realm which does govern amongst other things the clothes and weapons of your enemy which should give you a load of possibilities even with just low amounts of the Realm.

                        The biggest problem with CtD is still Glamour economy since almost everything, even Birthrights, cost Glamour and you have a very limited pool of it that is extremely slow to refill normally. I generally as a house-rule tend to eliminate Glamour costs from Birthrights as a minor fix although I have been planning a more throughout revision of Glamour but that is for another time. Still while that is the main issue the Realms have some fixing to do as well.

                                                The Actor 5 thing just underlines the necessity of other realms. The unseelie sidhe sends a gang of mortal ruffins armed with pipes to teach you some respect, but you only have actor 1? No problem, because you have prop and scene!

                                                - Holly Strike their weapons. This cantrip deals some piddly agg damage, which everyone remembers, but it also obliterates pounds and pounds of matter. ANY matter. It melts butter and vaporizes titanium with equal power. A bunch of hoodlums whose weapons explode when you throw birdseed at them and go "two magic, two magic!" is going to reconsider their career choices.Especially if they end up buck naked as part of the deal.

                                                - Use Dictum on the weapons! It's a little "out there," but if your ST doesn't let you go "out there" while playing changeling, he's not doing his job. Command the pipes to not hit you. May call for some resisted rolls as the thugs try to force their tools to overcome your dictates, but still pretty effective.

                                                - Alternately, use Ensnare's second effect to make those pipes hit them.

                                                - Pyretics 3 makes the pipes catch on fire... not too great if they hit you, but since the pipes are now burning the crap out of the thugs holding them...

                                                To say nothing of what you can do with the rest of the scenery around them. the weird setting for Actor 5 really just means you have to use other Realms to make reality go bonkers around your enemies.
