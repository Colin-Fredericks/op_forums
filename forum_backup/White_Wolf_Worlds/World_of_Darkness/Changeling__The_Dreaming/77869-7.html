                                                            As to the Cantrip card system, it was interesting but limited and impractical. If bunks are supposed to be random activities that 'activate' the cantrip, the cards are mainly suggestions. In that sense the only practical use for them are to have the cards at quick references for your character's abilities and bunks are what you make up anyways. When the cantrip and bunking rules changed in Changeling 2nd Edition, the cantrip cards were made obsolete. I don't think they should return as Cantrip cards because while the artwork is pretty and interesting, I think it does take away from playing the game to include a bunch of cards at the table rather than a reference sheet. To correct the idea that "printing another few ready to cut out cards wouldn't be such a problem" Yes... yes it is. Because all the cardstock printing would have to be handled by a separate printer than the ones handling the book printing. There have been issues finding printing companies that would handle special book covers and Storyteller screens while the books were printing and still have everything ready all at the same time estimated delivery. Mage 20th is going to have its own battle and delays with that process having a lenticular cover. That's why Onyx Path is not offering things like Posters or Bookmarks or even more trading cards in their stretch goals. All those things would need to be done by printing companies other than the ones handling the book assembly. The expense would be far more than even Kickstarter backers could make worth it. Not that they wouldn't LOVE to offer those things though. It's just not possible due to the expense of making it outweighing the worth.
