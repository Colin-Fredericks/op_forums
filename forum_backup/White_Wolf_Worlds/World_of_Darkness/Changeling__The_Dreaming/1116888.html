We had a "thrall immunity" in our last game, as far as changelings are concerned. I might keep that. Kinain would essentially be Nephilim from a Demon's point of view, wouldn't they?

          I tend to kinda agree with zenten. The Fae and Demons are incompatible in a way, meaning that their facts about existence tend to conflict. Devil's Due, basically Dark Ages Demon, had a some information on how it would match to DA:Fae, which I have since then used as a basic guideline.

          In DD it's basically said that the Demons do not remember creating the Fae nor that they even existence during the rebellion which immediately makes them stand out. Are they beings created by the Host after the rebellion or beings brought here from another world created by the divine or most worrisomely are they a creation of humanity through their collective Faith. The Fae see Demons as just another odd being that does not concern them that much since they have so little to give to the Fae, but the Fae do cheer the Demons when they make trouble for the church.

          Of thralldom, and possession, I again like to take the DD approach, even though it is written with DA: Fae in mind. According to it Firstborn and the Inanimae are unable to be bound in pacts and too alien to be possessed but Changelings on the other hand can be both. When taken in CtD terms, and Now C20, I ruled it to mean that only those Fae who had a human component to their soul, that is those that went through the changeling way ritual, are able to be thralls or be possessed by Demons mostly meaning that the Dark-kin and the Arcadian Sidhe, and the few other Fae who have not gone through the ritual, cannot become thralls or be possessed.
