                                Personally, after reading the crafting rules, I came to realise that everything in the dreaming that is not 'natural' has to be crafted by a Changeling. Which is why it mostly looks like mediaeval/renaissance construction techniques with the occasional bit of brickwork/steelwork in highly organised or populous areas.

                                Changeling population is very very very low compared to human norms (maybe 1 in 10k at most by my estimate), even comparing the early middle ages to the modern age the human population is significantly less than 100 times more. This means almost everything needs to be made by hand for yourself, as it's quite difficult to find fae labourers for hire to build your manor house or work your mine or steelworks.

                                There are no high tech modern labour saving devices - you can't drive a tractor into the dreaming and use it to farm chimerical potatoes, and there are no factories producing integrated circuits or electronics components. You don't have a petrol powered cement mixer or prefab wooden frames helping you build your house, you have to do every little bit by hand, including making the tools you need to harvest the materials in the first place.

                                In short, for my stories, freeholds are 'feudal' styled because it's very difficult to build anything else, and I think that fits nicely with the Changeling themes. If the dreaming were totally malleable and you could make of it what you will then we'd just have another bland blend of anything goes that marks out mediocre fiction. I don't want Star Trek/Star Wars/Cyberpunk themed freeholds popping up and pissing about with the immersion.
