                                          It doesn't even have to be in the Dreaming to be post-apocalyptic. Picture a long stretch of decaying rust belt towns with precious little Glamour to go around. There is a Changeling gang leader/warlord/demagogue who controls a large source of Dross and siphons it out to maintain control. Local Kithain gearheads fix up old beaters and drag race them along deserted roads and through the ruins of industry.                                               So it’s a little off topic, but the discussion about Changeling Mad Max reminds me - I’ve viewed Changeling: The Dreaming as a post-post-apocalyptic setting for awhile now.

                                              It’s a post-apocalyptic setting that’s only been starting to recover for the past fifty years. There’s still lots of broken infrastructure of the Dreaming from before the Shattering - lots of ruins and “forbidden zones” waiting to be reclaimed, lost knowledge to be relearned, lost treasures the very existence of which are long forgotten, broken half-remember oaths that once held the world together that need to be reforged.

                                              Things are much better than they were fifty years ago for changelings, but if the Kithain venture too far from the relatively Glamour rich urban environments filled with Dreamers, they’re going to be struggling for survival.

                                              Charlie Cantrell
                                              Onyx Path Freelancer
