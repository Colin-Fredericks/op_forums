            Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
            I thought, for completion's sake (and so we can waste our time mulling over the possibilities and getting everything wrong), I'd start a thread listing all the new additions to the game that C20 has teased. Please add anything I've missed:

            1. Unleashing - We've had a preview for this already. It suggests an overhaul of the system for Nightmare dice.

            2. New Thallain types - Sevartal (svartalf, perhaps--meaning 'dark elf' or Thallain sidhe, I guess), skinwalkers (aside: J.K. Rowling got into trouble for her portrayal of this myth recently)

            3. Revelry - A new way of getting Glamour which appears to be kith-specific (e.g., satyrs will do something related to, I guess, passion).

            4. New Arts - Oneiromancy, Contracts, Dragon's Ire (possibly?), Summer (possibly?).

            5. Ways to cure the Undoing - This is an important one, as temporarily Undone characters can seemingly be brought back from the Mists. The Second Edition rules for the Mists have characters fall into a coma for months on end, which often interrupted game flow.

            What else have we learned or are we expecting from the tidbits we've seen so far?
            Would you mind telling me where did you saw skinwalkers?
