    Okay, inspired by Anatole, I'm going to share something I wrote for NaGaDeMon.


    The New Hampshire Primary
    Every four years, New Hampshire becomes the center of the U.S. Presidential election. New Hampshire has legally committed itself to being the nation’s first primary. If another state reschedules their primary earlier, New Hampshire law requires the Secretary of State to reschedule their own two weeks before theirs.

    People in other states protest that New Hampshire gets a disproportionate amount of attention and gatekeeping power relative to its size, not to mention its racial homogeneity. New Hampshireans rebut that they make up for their size with their enthusiasm. Nowhere is there more passion and attention devoted to politics than New Hampshire.

    And where there is passion, there is Glamour.

    While governance of the Autumn world and tedious electoral campaigning put many off with its Banality, the sidhe, in particular, love politics.

    The sidhe are politics.

    At the outset of every Presidential bid is a flurry of creativity, optimism, and loyalty directed toward a candidate. Before the first primaries and caucuses campaigning is highly optimistic, future-looking, and full of potential. Sidhe are often involved in local campaigns hosting fundraisers and elbow-rubbing formal events, or in the trenches of local offices organizing volunteers, setting up canvasses, and marching—all the while musing Glamour from the candidates, campaign staff, and volunteers.

    Some of the more active houses include Houses Dougal and Liam, whose involvement in campaigns tend to gravitate toward “common sense” populist candidates, Varich and Ailil who often support the more authoritarian or Machiavellian candidates, and Eiluned, who enjoy data-driven, strategic campaigners.

    Ravaging
    Where there are winners, there are losers; the Primary is also the source of tremendous disappointment. Telling a true believer the disappointing truth about their candidate, working a volunteer to exhaustion, or just milking the disappointment of a loss on election night are all ways of harvesting the Primary’s Glamour by Ravaging.

    Dreamer profile: Vermin Supreme
    Every primary, Massachusetts performance artist (for lack of a better term) Vermin Supreme runs for President to make a complete mockery of the election process. Vermin Supreme campaigns wearing a boot on his head as a hat, five or more neckties, and towing a wheeled golf bag containing several rifles and shotguns. His platform consists of improving Americans’ dental health through tyrannically imposed mandatory toothbrushing, giving every American a pony, thus stimulating the economy with the resulting demand for pony-related jobs and services, and the use of zombies as a source of clean energy. (Editor’s note: None of this is made up). The sheer absurd audacity of Vermin Supreme’s campaign is a Glamour firehose for those who can keep up with it.

    Sidebar: If you’re ever wondering what a tier 5 Bunk might look like, going live on CSPAN wearing five ties and a boot for a hat, promising every American a pony, then glitter-bombing another candidate would be a pretty good example. (Vermin Supreme did this to an anti-gay candidate in 2011 and St. Anselm College, who hosted the event, was said to still be cleaning glitter out of the carpet in 2015.)

          Hi, friends. Any chance I could get some feedback on this timeline? I've not read all of the canon and don't want to blatantly contradict anything that too important.

          1969: The Resurgence
          • The sidhe return through gateways to the Dreaming that the commoner kithain did not previously know existed. Portsmouth lies at a crossroads of The Sea Path to the open Atlantic opens, the Coastal Path running along the eastern seaboard, and a navigable trod leading up the Piscataqua River.
          • Sidhe occupy the former Fort William and Mary, known locally as “The Castle” on New Castle Island. They begin construction of a new chimerical structure that will become Castle Elwyn.
          • Commoners resoundingly reject the sidhe on principle. Live Free or Die becomes a rallying cry against the returned. The concept of nobility, especially after their centuries of abdication, is seen as an irrelevant anachronism. The sidhe, of course, think otherwise.
          • Dover establishes a garrison in preparation for the coming civil war. They refer to their resistance as the Republic of Granite.
          • Sidhe attempt to establish a freehold on the Isles of Shoals. Selkies and Merfolk drive them off and capsize their boats.
          1970: The Accordance War
          • Frustrated by their failure to occupy the Isles of Shoals, Baron Tibault ap Dougal establishes a shipyard in neighboring Kittery, Maine at the mouth of the Piscataqua River in order to construct some chimerical ships.
          • The Resistance harangues the shipyard, committing strike-and-fade actions against the shipbuilders and the ships. This significantly slows progress on building out sidhe naval superiority.
          • The shipyard completes only two ships, the Dreadnaught and the Obligation. They try again to occupy the Isles of Shoals and are again repelled by the selkies and merfolk.

          1971: The War continues
          • The Garrison in Dover continues to stand opposed to the sidhe.
          • In a suicidally bold raid, commoners board and steal a new ship named the Compelling. They sail her up the Piscataqua River and scuttle her in the Great Bay.
          • The Dreadnaught sails up the river and chimerically firebombs the entire city of Dover to the ground. The garrison falls.

          1972: Concordia rising
          • The Dreadnaught and Obligation sail from New Hampshire to New York Harbor to provide naval support for the final battles of the Accordance War.
          • David Ardry’s dream of a concord with shared power between commoners and sidhe comes to fruition. Duke Garan ap Fiona strongly supports the concept of a Parliament. King David appoints him Duke of the Duchy of Freewill. The Republic of Granite is dissolved, however the Assembly continues in Concord as an advisory group to the Duke.
          • The Compelling is raised from the bottom of Great Bay and is rechristened the RCS (Royal Concordian Ship) Celia Thaxter and is based in Portsmouth. The Obligation is rechristened RCS N.C. Wyeth with a home port in Kittery.

          1973: The selkies join with the other commoner kithain of Concordia. The merfolk end their alliance with the selkies and place the Glamour-rich Isles of Shoals off-limits to kithain.

              Here's the next page from the end of the War until now:


              1973: The chimerical castle on the footprint of the old Fort William and Mary is completed. The U.S. Government adds it to the Register of Historic Places and opens it as Fort Constitution Park. The Balefire is moved to the lighthouse, where it remains to this day as capital of the County of Herons.

              1974: Greek oil magnate, Aristotle Onassis, plans to put an oil refinery in Great Bay. The public resistance is fae-inspired and is formidable. This is one of the first actions of united commoner and sidhe musing.

              1987: Void Engineer investigators based at the University of New Hampshire discover a “dimensional anomaly” at the Freehold in the Barony of Lampreys in Durham. The Technocracy bring massive banality to the freehold, collapsing the gateway to the Dreaming, extinguishing the Balefire, and causing many kithin to be Undone. Without a freehold, the region is annexed by Baroness Lorraine ap Leanhaun and the capital of the Barony of Lampreys is moved to Rochester.

              1990s-2010s: Portsmouth becomes increasingly wealthy and sheds its industrial and fishing economic base for tech firms and creative industries. Old, closed mills are repurposed for condos, art studios, theaters, and restaurants. Trailing by a few years, Dover, Exeter, and Newmarket follow suit.

              2001: The RCS Dreadnaught is lost mysteriously with all hands in the Caribbean Sea. The Evanescence and the return of the Fomorians is whispered to be the cause. Count Cedrych of the County of Herons is aboard. Mathonwy ap Gwydion is promoted to the title of Count of Herons.

              2010-today: As Portsmouth and Dover thrive, Rochester, the most populated of the communities in the County of Herons, somehow resists the trend and remains rough. Drug abuse, crime, and poverty continue to thwart revitalization efforts. No one can directly point to Baroness Lorraine as a cause for this, but Count Mathonwy quietly suspects that she is not demonstrating the best stewardship of the Glamour sources in her fiefdom.
