                        Different team, and a different kind of project.

                        And seriously, anybody who feels uncomfortable about any aspect of our KSs: please don't back them unless backing them will be fun for you. If you are going to worry and fret and not have fun as the process happens, then don't do it. We really don't want anybody to back unless they are comfortable with the idea that they are enabling us to do some _things_ we want to accomplish, and that in order to do those things the way we want to do them, it might take time.

                        In the case of Wraith20, we want to create a new and definitive version of Wraith helmed by Rich Dansky, so in order to have Rich we have to wait until his full-time day gig allows him the time to write and develop Wr20. Other projects have other issues, and still others roll along just fine. Or at least any of the issues they have don't impact the estimated delivery date.

                        You only need to look at the KS'd projects we have delivered to see how awesome their quality is, and we have not failed to deliver a KS edition yet. So you will get a gorgeous edition and some really cool Stretch Goal rewards. If getting cool stuff is what works for you, then our KSs should be right up your alley. If you need the delivery of that stuff to hit the estimates and you worry until it does or doesn't, and that ruins the fun for you (because some folks actually get fun from the worrying, too), then maybe you'd be better off passing.
