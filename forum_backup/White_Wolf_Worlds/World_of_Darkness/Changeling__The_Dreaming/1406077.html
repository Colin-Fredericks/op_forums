    Make your Favorite Blank into a Changeling House/Kith/Character/Treasure

    08-03-2020, 03:50 AM
    Okay so this thread will be dedicated to translating your favorite bits of fiction into the Changeling the Dreaming Setting. You can make them into Great Houses, Banner Houses, Kiths, Characters, Treasures, or even Freeholds or Chimera or whatever. Feel free to convert them completely point for point or fit them into an already established part of the setting, whatever method you want homage or copy and paste. You can do individual characters or whole organizations. Its a fun exercise with the benefit of making content that can be grabbed for instant use in a game.

    For an Example here are my takes on doing the Lords of Dark Souls as Great Houses, and the Royal Dynasty of Boleterria from Demon's Soul as a Great House.


    Here is my take on a Banner House for Ganon from Zelda.
    Banner House (Tribe) Gerudo
    Sworn: Balor Allied: Ailil
    Nickname: Dark Lords, Calamities (vulgar)
    House Banner:


    Head of House: Great King of Evil Ganondorf Dragmire ap Gerudo


    House Gerudo’s recall journeying from a daunting desert realm scoured incessantly by merciless winds. They remember discovering the Autumn World for the first time in the Resurgence and they mostly underwent the Changeling Way Ritual. They recognized House Balor as kin, and they seem to have a shared bloody history with House Hyrule’s alliance of Banner Houses.


    The Sidhe born of House Gerudo are exclusively female, however every century the male Gerudo Sidhe is reborn bearing the Triforce of Power and made the King of the Gerudo. Gerudo Sidhe possess sharp prominent aquiline noses, flame colored hair, and rich deep gold skin, that sometimes ranges towards a greenish olive hue. The current Great King of Evil of House Gerudo is the warlock Grump Ganondorf Dragmire, a man of singular might and boundless ambition. House Gerudo is favors the approach of courting dark powers, and they make a habit of taming wicked and beastial Chimerae, mysterious Adhene, and even Thallain, it is said some of these beings have even sworn oaths and have been made squires and knights of House Gerudo. Of the kithain they have attracted a number of ruthless changelings, almost exclusively Unseelie, such as Slaugh keepers of the crypt, Troll generals, Redcap reavers, Pooka spies and hunters, and Knocker armorers.



    The Gerudo Tribe favors merciless windswept lands and Freeholds in the style of their homelands fortresses, living communally within. They seek to claim any Freehold they may take by force, and actively seek to plunder and weaken Banner House Hyrule, while stopping at nothing to eradicate Banner House Evil’s Bane completely. Ganondorf’s primary goal is to seize the Triforce Courage, and the Triforce of Wisdom, reuniting the Triforce and using its omnipotent power to turn the Golden Land black and usher forth Winter with the Army of Nightmares beckoned from that ancient realm.



    Distinction: Dark Glamour warps chimerical reality under Gerudo control, simulating the effects of high Title Freehold owners, while amplifying the effects of those already with a high title and those reigning over a Freehold. While in a Freehold they rule over they make all rolls to command, both mundane and supernatural, chimera of darkness, death or malevolence as if their difficulty was reduced by two.


    Fault: House Gerudo may not accept gifts freely given, they may only take or trade.

              Banner House Hyrule
              Sworn: Gwydion Allied: Liam
              Nickname: Red Lions, Delegators(vulgar)

              Banner:
              Head of House: King Daphnes Nohansen ap Hyrule
              Charged by the three supreme goddesses of the Golden Lands to protect the Omnipotent Triforce. They raised up great holdings and cultivated knowledge and civilization. Their recollection is unclear but they seem to remember a war with their nemesis the Demon Emperor of House Gerudo. Their ally the Hero of Time who was destined to banish House Gerudo’s Demon Emperor failed to preserve their realm and a great flood washed away their once great land. This is all that has been pieced together since they discovered the Autumn World.
              Mighty magics and ancient treasures safeguarded this once great House as they sought their place in the worlds of men and fae. They found kinship with the greatest House the Autumn World, and so they swore fealty to House Gwydion. They seek to establish territory in locations where the kithain are weak, such as in the lands of the East, and in a variety of hidden islands and forests. They seek to rebuild their House so that they may assemble the Triforce and possibly return to their homeland of Hyrule and return to the land the civilization and prosperity lost to it.
              Hyrule is ruled by King Daphnes Nohansen ap Hyrule, and his only daughter Princess Zelda is the bearer of the Triforce of Wisdom. House Hyrule possesses the greatest number of Sidhe and Treasures of all the refuge Houses of the Golden Lands. Though they still seek to recruit commoner and noble allies to help them in their endeavors to revive the Kingdom of Hyrule. The rediscovering the smaller Banner House of Clan Sheikah has been a major boon to House Hyrule as Clan Sheikah has served as protectors and spies of House Hyrule long before their diaspora into the Autumn World. House Evil’s Bane is renowned as their greatest savior in the Age of Legend, but some of House Hyrule have felt bitterness and resentment that the Hero of Time was nowhere to be found when they needed them most, losing them their ancestral homeland.

              Distinction: When the earnest hearts of House Hyrule make a prayer filled with faith, Dan and the Dreaming listen. When in danger a member of House Hyrule may pray to the Three Golden Goddesses of the Triforce and a use of Soothesay in response to this danger by the Red Lion or on their behalf may be made at a -2 reduced difficulty once per story. Once per story, if captured or cornered a point of willpower and a prayer for aid may be made and received by either a member of House Evil’s Bane or Clan Sheikah closest to the Hyrule in need.
              Fault:
              The loss of the glorious realm of Hyrule weighs heavy upon House Hyrule. If a week is spent without resting for a night in a Holding held by the House then the Red Lion will drift from the glamour of Three Goddesses of the Triforce and lose access to regaining Glamour from revelry or reverie, until they once again spend a night resting in a Holding dedicated to Hyrule.

                  Banner House Evil’s Bane
                  Sworn: Scathach Allied: Beaumayn
                  Nickname: Heroes of Time, Mutes(vulgar)
                  Head of House: The Hero of Time Link ap Evil’s Bane

                  House Banner:










                  For longer then Hyrule has existed the Three Goddesses of the Golden Land have called upon a champion to preserve their world when calamity looms. The Hero of Time is the chosen champion of Din, Nayru, and Farore designated to protect the sacred Triforce in every age. Eventually the Hero of time failed to preserve Hyrule for some unknown reason, leading to its destruction in an apocalyptic deluge. In the Exodus from the Sacred Realm the bloodline of the Hero of Time gathered together as one and made their way into the Autumn World.

                  Unlike the other Banner Houses that left the Sacred Land House Evil’s Bane was not an organization until the Hero of Time awakened in the Autumn World. The family of the Hero of Time’s various incarnations united and uplifted their family into a proper Banner House of House Scathach, swearing fealty and kinship with them and even adopting other Sidhe and commoners into their fold. Banner House Evil’s Bane is named after their greatest weapon the Blade of Evil’s Bane the sacred Master Sword of Link the Hero of Time.

                  Now as organization rather than an individual House Evil’s Bane has one and all undergone the Changeling Ways Ritual and has spread across the face of the Earth. They travel the world as itinerant protectors fulfilling their two fold goal, protect those in need and accumulate power, wisdom and courage so that they may deliver fallen Hyrule from destruction. House Evil’s Bane has no official hierarchy but the true Hero of Time is said to wear green, wield the Master Sword and bears the Triforce of Courage. Reports have been contradictory about Link’s current incarnation, some believe he is still a male warrior in his prime with chestnut hair and an athletic build, others have claimed he has been slain and has reincarnated as a cherubic boy with golden hair and still numbering in years that can be counted on two hands, and even a green hooded wilder girl has been witnessed believed to bear the mark of the triforce. Some believe the Triforce of Courage has splintered into a multitude of Shards and the Hero of Time was not a reincarnating soul but a different Hylian youth chosen by the Goddesses at the moment of great destiny and each of these past iterations have been reborn into the Autumn world as well as a few of their close kin, an old woodsmen uncle, a blacksmith grandfather, a widowed grandmother, a precocious childer sister a more. They seem drawn to the Gerudo Tribe, particularly their leader Ganondorf the Prince of Darkness whom they have sealed, slapped down and slain over a host of lifetimes. They will not fail the world again, they will protect House Hyrule and make amends to the Goddesses of the Golden Land.


                  Distinction:
                  House Evil’s Bane makes for indomitable and tenacious foes even when outnumbered. Heroes of Time reduce all extra action penalties for all actions by -1. If outnumbered or fighting a foe more than twice their size the Hero of Time gains two bonus dice to dexterity and wits while this applies.

                  Fault: Emulating the Hero of Time House Evil’s Bane may not speak a word but only utter sounds of exclamation, this maintains their timeless archetype of stoic mystery.

                      Banner House (Clan) Sheikah

                      Sworn: Scathach Allied: Gwydion
                      Nickname: Shadows, Bloody Eyes (vulgar)
                      House Banner:

                      Head of House: Sage of Shadows Impa ap Sheikah
                      Few realize Clan Sheikah is older then House Hyrule as they have always existed in the shadows. Clan Sheikah descend from a long line of secret protectors of a lost goddess but it is said House Hyrule was founded by the blood of this ancient divinity of yore. Always few in number they Sheikah have been critical preserving House Hyrule through spycraft, hidden bodyguards disseminated as servants and even assassination.

                      It was said long ago the Sheikah had advanced and intricate Treasures from the gods that could conjure great shows of force, and that their mastery of the Arts have sealed the might of the Gerudo King. However some sort of divide manifested between the Sheikah and House Hyrule and the Sheikah were forced to simplify their arsenal and live apart from the rest of the peoples of Hyrule. Some argued even that Sheikah were simply a Banner House of House Hyrule before this point, afterwards they became a distinct Clan. An offshoot of Clan Sheikah is rumored to exist, this Yiga Clan is said to have nursed the ancient scorn and betrayal from the ungrateful Hyrule to the point where they have sworn their services to the Demon Emperor of the Gerudo.

                      In the Exodus to the Autumn World it is said Clan Sheikah arrived first as scouts and vanguards, gathering intelligence and ensuring a safe position for House Hyrule. Clan Sheikah accepted the Changeling Way Ritual to better help them insinuate themselves into Kithain culture, and they adopted commoner kiths to perfect their alibi’s and spynetworks. Now Clan Sheikah is perhaps the best adapted to the Autumn World, making pragmatic use of technology of this distant land and not shying away from doing what is necessary to ensure the safety of their most sacred charge, Princess Zelda ni Hyrule who is said to be an incarnation of their ancient goddess. Hyrule fell to calamity and Clan Sheikah will stop at nothing to undo this stain and protect their Princess. The disparate cells of Sheikah are said to work under the guidance of a secret elder, the Clan understands Fate itself guides Impa their secret Shadow Sage.


                      Distinction: Sheikah reduce the difficulty of all disguise rolls not meant to emulate a specific individual by 2. If a point of glamour is spent then a disguise roll may be made at the speed of a combat action as their form is veiled with a burst of smoke.


                      Fault: If a Sheikah doesn’t swear an oath of fealty or loyalty towards a noble of House Hyrule their Cantrips will cost one extra glamour point per use.

                          Banner House Belmont
                          Sworn: Beaumayn, Allied: Liam
                          Nickname:Hunters, Killers(vulgar)
                          House Banner:


                          House Leadership: Lord Trevor Belmont ap Beaumayn
                          With roots in Neustra House Belmont started off as specialist group of hunters that would investigate night time massacres of rural villages by creatures of death such as blood drinkers, spectres and demon possessed corpses. They would develop an adversarial relationship with the Cainite Courts of Love. Eventually however their relationship would cool to an understanding of mutual foes as they were aimed at the Voivodes of Wallachia and their Thallain allies. By the time the Inquisition arrived to devastate the night creatures most blatant in their predations House Belmont solidified their holdings in the region and openly warred with the forces of the grim Caer of Castrum Ultra Silvam, a labyrinthine castle guarded by infernal denizens able to translocate through mysterious arcane means.

                          When the Inquisition scourged the foul forces of Nightmare plaguing the mortal world some benign and salubrious supernatural forces also suffered. House Belmont, steeped in conflict with the darkest of night creatures were among the casualties of the Inquisition; this has led to the majority of the House being slain or driven from the land right before the Shattering. So when the majority of House Beaumayn went to Arcadia the lion’s share of Banner House Belmont already fled to the far shores of Arcadia to lick their wounds. The small remainder of House Belmont that chose to dwell in the Autumn World as Autumn Sidhe continued their war with Castrum Ultra Silvum.

                          House Belmont would recruit common fae particularly from the kiths known for truculent attitudes towards the those that stalk the night, such as Dhampir, and Benendanti, as well as penitent kiths of the night such as Slaugh and Phantasms. By the time reinforcements from Arcadia returned to bolster their ranks they have found the Autumn branch of the house has been tenaciously and stalwartly defending the old world from monstrous forces that would overwhelm lesser Houses. With them they have brought the fabled weapons of old which could turn the tide in the Night War and make House Belmont one of the most formidable forces the creatures of Hell could encounter in modern nights. With renewed morale House Belmont has begun to branch out beyond their ancestral lands into the far corners of the world of man. It is said the leader of the House is currently the Autumn Sidhe Trevor Belmont, a changeling with memories of conquering Caer Castrum Ultra Silvum. The House headquarters is in the Underground Belmont Estate in central Romania, containing the largest repository of the houses accumulated knowledge and a vast armory preserved from the inquisition as well as Treasures of legend returned from Arcadia. Their secondary redoubt is the Ancestral Belmont Catacombs in the Pyrenees mountain range in the Aquitaine region.


                          Distinction: Belmont has been battling creatures of the night for over a millenia and many generations have risen and fallen each with their chosen weapons of war. Every member of House Belmont thus may start with two dots in Treasures representing ancestral weapons, often blessed by beings of truth Faith with the aid of House Liam, used to hunt demons and the undead. They may also request access to the vast library of the Underground Belmont Estate, or the wisdom found within the Ancestral Belmont Catacombs. Such knowledge has been gathered from notes kept by pre-Belmont mortal hunters in ancient Greece from before the birth of the Christ, to the journals of a Liam Sidhe companion of a Belmont who wandered the Silk Road as an itinerant Buddhist Monk.

                          Fault: If beseeched for aid against the forces of Hell and the Night (Demons, Vampires, the Dead, Thallain, Infernal Mages, Fallen Garou, Elohim of the Tribe of Lilith, Fallen Angels, Possessed, Fae that ravage and rhapsody, and other such beings) the Hunter is obligated to lend assistance if not they suffer a banality trigger and nightmares every night mortals are preyed upon within 100 miles, thus not being able to recover willpower with rest.

                              Banner House (Clan) Belnades
                              Sworn: House Eiluned Allied:Liam
                              Nicknames: (Azure)Speakers, Witches (Vulgar)
                              House Banner: the Silver Clasp of the Speakers on a Field of Blue
                              House Leadership:Elder Speaker Sypha Belnades ni Eiluned



                              Clan Belnades is the leading Clan of a Kinain led tribe of nomads known as the Speakers. They are a secretive peoples who search for knowledge to collect and maintain through their sacred tradition of exclusively oral history. They dress all their members in blue cloaks reminiscent of monks and cultivate masculine and neutral haircuts and accessories due to centuries of travelling on roads where the attention drawn to women could be dangerous. They are often confused for monks, romani, and jews but in truth they seem to be an ancient sect of Gnostics similar to the Cathars. They believe in a duty to collect knowledge within their minds so that they can keep that knowledge secret from the God of the World.

                              Speakers have a predilection for learning numerous languages and dialects, even ones long thought extinct or unknowable such as Enochian and its predecessor Gemlish, Adamic the tongue of the first of mankind, and cypher scripts meant to encrypt the hidden scroll of magicians and immortals. The breadth of knowledge is vast among the Speakers, and as a whole there is a large amount of kinain among the tribe, as they are generally privy to the secret culture of the Kithain. When House Eiluned left the world of man, the Speakers of Clan Belnades were charged with retaining their custom, traditions, and sacred knowledge in case the commoners would need them. It is said Clan Belnades is just one clan of Speakers and other houses have their own clan each denoted by a different colored garb, with House Liam sheltering the different trains of Speakers around the world.

                              Clan Belnades’ homeland was ancient Wallachia, and like House Liam they believed in altruism and sought to aid communities with their accumulated knowledge. If a community was in danger it was not uncommon for a Speaker train to settle in a community to help them through their times of trouble. By the time of the Inquisition Clan Belnades was long allied with House Belmont in their Night War against the Lords of the Night such as the Voivodes of Clan Tzimisce and House Dracul’s eldritch Caer Castrum Ultra Silvam. After the Inquisition scourged much of Clan Belnades as a tribe of witches due to their profane knowledge of the black arts, Belnades were driven further underground and branched beyond Wallachia’s borders. By the time of the Resurgence House Eiluned Returned, though few were of Banner House Belnades some of the newly returned Eiluned who chose to undergo the Changeling Way Ritual would join the Autumn Sidhe of Clan Belnades and their Kinain. Being naturally insular and secretive they had few commoner kiths adopted into their ranks, but there have always been some accepted into their ranks with each generation. Their love of knowledge has attracted Satyr scholars, their Altruism has caught the ears of humble Boggans, and their participation in the Night War of House Belmont has resulted in Dhampir wedding into the bloodline.

                              Distinction: Clan Belnades receive the Eidetic Memory merit for free, and they may use spoken words and hand gestures as valid bunks for their Arts. mystic hand gestures, or mudras, are methods of speaking supernal truths that cannot be articulated through the words of mortal tongues.

                              Fault: Speakers are Forbidden from writing down their lore or history. Some exceptions are made to send quick messages via letters or email, but they suffer a +3 difficulty to express themselves in written words, and excessive use of writing, both electronic and analog, can earn malice or stigma from other Speakers.
