                Originally posted by Firkraag View Post
                There is entire Book of Lost Dreams paragraph, stating, that Malkavians are immune to Mists. And it's canon until it is revised.


                Let me rephrase. In my world of darkness Technocracy should be able to interact with changelings and chimera, though indirect means, such as memetic analys and statistics, they should have exactly the right tech to determine whether their memory was modified, when and by whom, they can spot inconsistencies in stories and get of everything, other than what they see as fact. They don't have to believe in fairies to know, that certain places are affected by extradimensional entities, that cause nightmares. They don't have to believe in fairies to see, that something corrupts their camera feeds.
                From someone who actually does memetic and statistical analysis, I cannot think of any way that the Technocracy could possibly detect Changelings as Changelings or the Mists as the Mists. Memetic analysis concerns the analysis of memes and statistical analysis concerns the analysis of data. Memes are cultural units of information, so I really do not see how the Technocracy could possible use memetic analysis to find Changelings (at best, they could only use memetic analysis to find mortals who are being encouraged to be creative by Changelings). Data is information that is collected and, if you cannot remember what you are collecting, the data becomes really hard to collect, so statistical analysis would be useless because no direct data is being reported.

                Now, I agree that the Technocracy will notice something, but they will never be able to predict the actions of the creatures of the Dreaming because they are blind to the possibility of the Dreaming. They will probably the actions of the creatures of the Dreaming on Marauders, as the effects of Marauder Quiets and the effects of the Mists have enough similarities that the Technocracy would ignore any anomalies as noise in the data. When the Technocracy notices the actions of the creatures of the Dreaming, they will probably send in their Marauder Clean Up teams to deal with the area (altering mortal memories further) and will never suspect that they are dealing with the Dreaming. After all, the Union be much more likely to accept a Marauder attack as an explanation than a Faerie attack.
