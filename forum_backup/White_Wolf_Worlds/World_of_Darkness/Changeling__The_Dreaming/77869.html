          Yep, I really want a Changeling: The Dreaming 20th Anniversary Edition!

          I'm torn on Kithbook: Boggans. On the one hand, it would be nice to have an official book. On the other other hand, back when it seemed like Changeling: The Dreaming would never in a million years have any chance of coming back, the fan community at the time pulled together and created a fantastic fan version of the book Thad's writing is just as good, or better than, most of the published Kithbooks. A lot of great artists, including Melissa Uran, pitched in art. I tried to do the layout so that it would look indistinguishable from the other Kithbooks. It would be nice if all that effort could be acknowledged in some way. If you're interested in it, there's a link to the book in my sig.

          As for Book of Glamour, I'd love to see it if it could be released now. Otherwise, we're getting very close to Dreaming's 20th Anniversary. It was intended to be a mostly rules book to update Changeling's magic system to revised standards without the expense of actually released a revised core book for the game. If there's going to be a C20 and Book of Glamour could only be released close to it, I'd kind of rather they just put the effort into updating the rules to revised standards and roll any extras into C20 itself.

          I'd rather see the stretch goals used for new things that might not have any chance of happening otherwise - a Book of Nod style book for Changeling, updating the cantrip card rules to C20, print on demand cantrip cards, something that really delves into the Changeling Way ritual and covers the difference between changelings and arcadian fae, and a book on how things transitioned from DA: Fae to C:tD and maybe show how some of the things that weren't mentioned in DA: Fae work in the Dark Ages: the noble houses, the Tuatha, the Fomorians, etc.

          Charlie Cantrell
          Onyx Path Freelancer
          Changeling: The Dreaming 20th Anniversary Edition, Conquering Heroes, Book of Freeholds, Guide to the Night, C20 Players Guide

              I would love to see a C20 Edition!

              Occasionally Changeling is a greatly underestimated game. Some people just do not capture the essence of the game, it's about telling a story where dreams are dying in the midst of a world that has lost its creativity.

              However, the strongest point in Changeling, in my POV, was the metaplot. The game has always had solid NPCs and very exciting events. (I have always been and will always be a fan of using the metaplot, so sue me).

              Since V20, W20 (and most probably M20) will not use the metaplot tool, how C20 should deal with? High King David is dead? The Winter already come? Faerilyth, Morwen and Lenora still playing politics each others? What about the mastermind, King Meilge? The Armies of Dreaming were able to construct a faction and its own realm? Who the Red Branch Knights will support, or they will splat and divide? What about the Guardians of the Silver Dragon?

              Or maybe we receive a toolbox to build our own kingdoms and counties, not only in USA but throughout the world. I wouldn't like that, but it could be a good option.

                                          Seeing how both you and me are both Werewolf fans at heart and I own Changeling books:
                                          Black Furies = Female Satyrs at most, I can only imagine what would happen to a male Satyr in their midst. And other female fae.
                                          Bone Gnawers = These guys probably run into Sluaghs and Boggans at most.
                                          Children of Gaia = Any, as long as they aren't Wyrm tainted.
                                          Fianna = Get along with pretty much any fae, unless said fae is Wyrm tainted. It's only House Daireann that really dislikes the Fianna. Whereas House Fionna has a Fianna working with their knights.
                                          Glass Walkers = Knockers and House Dougal.
                                          Get of Fenris = Trolls only. Have sworn a blood oath against the Sidhe and the oath still stands today.
                                          Red Talons = Probably only a Ragabash Red Talon might tolerate a Pooka, but as a whole, wouldn't mind a Redcap as long as said Redcap was directed towards where humans are.
                                          Shadow Lords = House Eiluned.
                                          Silent Striders = Eshu.
                                          Silver Fangs = Seelie Sidhe and maybe House Varich.
                                          Stargazers = Hsien.
                                          Uktena = Nunnehi, Menehune, other native fae for the areas they frequent.
                                          Wendigo = Nunnehi
                                          Black Spiral Dancers = Them and House Balor go together like peanut butter and jelly. As well as the Shadow Court.
                                          Changing Breeds = Depends on the area. African/Ahadi = Eshu, Hengeyokai = Hsien, South America = ?, Australia = ?, Europe = Changelings, North America = Nunnehi, Menehune (Hawaii), Changelings.
