        Infernalists and Nephandi just want to destroy everything. Including the Dreaming.

        As much as House Balor want to rule a world of terror, this is not what those factions offer. What they offer is horror for the reader, but for the characters is first and foremost the death of Glamour itself.

            Monteparnas has the winning argument here. House Balor is facing the same problem as European Fascists and Conservative Monarchists faced in 1941. Misery works, total destruction ends the game.

            Balor Shide need to ruin/destroy selected individuals, but they need humanity as a whole to continue to survive and have hope and dreams.

                The main problem with original House Balor is the same problem with original Adhene, Shadow Court and some other groups in the game: authors that don't get it and try to add horror, causing what I call Changeling's 50 Shades of Nightmare

                A lot of people, and that included so many authors, thought Changeling was too much whimsy and lacked teeth, so they added very dark villains.

                Which never changed anything and the game remained looking light and whimsical, and then the next one added another dark villain.

                Most didn't get that the core book alone already have nightmarish lovecraftian gods of a primal world forgotten to Humanity and the character option of starting play as a psychotic killer with unending hunger and bottomless pit for a stomach that literally eat living children as appetizers in parties without any consequence.

                Changeling's horror is precisely being a society of entities to whom the most abject evil is just a valid way of life. It's the only gameline in WoD that has absolutely no mechanically or socially enforced moral standard beyond being bigger than life in anything you do, where the thing the most immoral have to fear is only the reaction of equally uncontrollable 'heroes' with no grasp of consequences for their actions.

                House Balor is cool in many ways. Being dark servants of the Fomorians isn't one of them, it's just generic evil. Allying with all those WoD evil parties is something any Changeling can do. Trying to conquer the world through tyranny is a regular Unseelie activity. Killing other fairies with Cold Iron is the whole plot of the war in the 70s. Adding Edgy GrimDark Edginess of Ultimate Evil is a fool's errand in Changeling.
