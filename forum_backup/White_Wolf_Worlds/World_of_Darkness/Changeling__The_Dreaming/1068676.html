                                                  I've always thought the cosmologies fit together quite well if you just ignore who they say really runs everything. You have The One at the top, which is pure Prime, and was shattered into The Shining Ones, who are probably the Avatars of humanity or became the Avatars of humanity after further devolution. Somewhere below The One, or adjacent to It, is the Triat, which embodies each aspect of the Metaphysical Trinity and moderates (or is supposed to moderate) creation through Dynamism, Stasis and Entropy--except it was corrupted/unbalanced, skewing things. Beneath the Triat are the Celestines and Incarna, including some delusional being who thinks of himself as Jehovah and created first the angels then the vampires. No one likes him and he's way too self-aggrandising.

                                                  The antithesis of The One is The Other, which might just be Oblivion or perhaps the Qlippoth, and the antithesis of The Shining Ones are the Malfeans, who embody destruction in the same way The Shining Ones embody creation. The Dreaming, meanwhile, is an isolated chunk of the Umbra which feeds off human subjectivity and the collective unconscious, and all its critters spring from the minds of sentient beings. They may once have had an independent existence like any other being of the Umbra, but now they're dependent on the dreams of humanity.
