              I've experimented with allowing for more kith variation by using the 9 main kiths in Changeling the Dreaming and letting PCs choose a subkith from Changeling the Lost. Converting CtL's kith abilities takes a little bit of work, but I've had good results (and happy players).
              I always thought that creatures born of dreams should have more variety than CtD allows for by default. And by keeping the CtD kiths and allowing variation within each, it means I don't have to toss out anything from the CtD sourcebooks or setting.
              I'm not saying this will work for anyone else, or even that others will like the idea, but it has worked well for at least 2 different games I've run.

                      Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
                      I don't suppose you could share the system you use in more detail?
                      I have notes at home and can put up here what I have once I get back there. For Kiths, the system I use goes something like this: Choose a CtD kith and then a CtL Seeming/Kith. I added a sheet to the character sheet which has space for Kith and Subkith as well as additional Advantages and Disadvantages. I collected most of the Changeling the Lost books because I liked a lot of the ideas from the for use in oWoD. You can find a reference list online for them as well, though since I'm not sure how official the site I've seen is, I'm hesitant to actually link it here.

                      In any case, I did also sort of import the Courts from CtL. I use CtD Seelie/Unseelie for in game alliances, but allow the Court from CtD to determine an additional way to harvest Glamour from human emotions. I always found that using the Glamour system from CtD RAW left too little Glamour for players to realistically use in game.Admittedly I may have missed something in the book, but that's how it always worked out in my games.

                      For a character example though, one of my players wanted a concept close to the base Sidhe concept as presented in CtD core so she wrote up a Sidhe (Fairest-Dancer)
                      Character Concept: Spoiled Rich Girl
                      She had:
                      Sidhe Birthrights: Awe and Beauty, and Noble Bearing
                      Sidhe Frailty: Banalty's Curse
                      as well as:
                      Fairest Advantages:
                      1) Spend Glamour to improve Charisma or Manipulation dice pools
                      2) no +1 dif penalty for untrained skills
                      Fairest Disadvantage:
                      I don't remember how I converted this one and will check later
                      Dancer's Blessings:
                      Free Dance Specialization for expression rolls, regardless of rating and -1 dif to Dance rolls.
                      +1 to Dodge dice pools.

                      All in all these changes boosted a Changeling's ability to function without the use of Arts, which I thought was nice since it meant there was less reliance of having to use magic to overcome situations and/or opponents. The above character was used in a crossover game, so it helped to put the Player on par with other splats in terms of out of the box abilities.

                      Once I have more time, I will post 1 of 2 of the other characters played that show a more diversified character concept. The one above stayed very close to the "beautiful elite" trope that Sidhe often embody.

                      So general rules for how I converted nWod Stuff to oWoD stuff:
                      9-again rule = a free specialty
                      8-again = free specialty and +1 Success

                      The "Spend Glamour for X result" abilities were mostly straight forward.

                          A few of the characters from a high school game from my group

                          Character Concept: Varsity Linebacker
                          Kith (Subkith): Troll (Beast-Truefriend [Dogs])
                          Troll Birthrights: Titan's Power, Stubbornness
                          Troll Frailty: Bond of Duty
                          Beast Advantages:
                          1) Free Animal Ken Specialty (Dogs), -1 Dif to Animal Ken Rolls and +1 Success
                          2) Can spend Glamour to increase Charisma dice pools.
                          Beast Disadvantages:
                          1) +4 dif to use Knowledges untrained (I normally allow Knowledge rolls at +3 Dif untrained)
                          2) Cannot take Intelligence Specialties
                          Truefriend's Blessing:
                          Can Spend Glamour to assist in another players roll, adding 2 dice to that roll

                          Character Concept: Metalhead Punk
                          Kith (Subkith): Redcap (Fairest-Draconinc])
                          Redcap Birthrights: Dark Appetite, Bully Browbeat
                          Redcap Frailty: Bad Attitude
                          Fairest Advantages:
                          1) Spend Glamour to improve Charisma or Manipulation dice pools
                          2) no +1 dif penalty for untrained skills
                          Fairest Disadvantage:
                          I don't remember how I converted this one and will check later
                          Draconic Blessings:
                          1) Can Spend Glamour to reroll a Brawl roll
                          2) +1 to Brawl Dice pools

                          Character Concept: Homeschooled Farmhand
                          Kith (Subkith): Boggan (Elemental-Earthbones)
                          Boggan Birthrights: Craftswork, Social Dynamics
                          Boggan Frailty: Call of the Needy
                          Elemental Advantage:
                          Spend Glamour gain 1/2 Glamour in additional Health Levels for a Scene
                          Elemental Disadvantage:
                          Cannot have specialization in social attributes or abilities (Empathy, Expression, Subterfuge, Leadership)
                          Earthbone Blessings:
                          Can spend Glamour to increase non combat strength rolls.

                                  I've kinda allowed more customisation in my own game too, but haven't yet decided on the mechanics: http://wiki.rpol.net/?id=63447/Chara...%20Changelings. But I like your system. It's a good one.
                                  I like how you've added chimerical characters. I had been debating on just how to incorporate them if a player ever wanted to play one.

                                  My whole reason for even exploring adding CtL kith as subkiths was to add a lot more variety to creatures born of dreams. I also felt there should be a lot more Kith options because of it. and I know there are more kiths written up in various sourcebooks, but I still felt they were rather limiting. And I also don't like the idea of different culture's Fae needing radically different systems.
                                  So the way I've done it, it's gone from 9 Kiths to 9 base kiths, with 6 subkiths for 54 base possibilities, 848 after considering all variations within each subkith. Now, I still don't think it could cover all of the possible Fae dreamed up from all cultures of the world, but it serves well enough for any game I would run.

                                  Part of the reason I even started trying it this way was because one of my players was a bit bothered after reading about the Menehune (from one of the Immortal Eyes books) and this system could let him create a changeling from his culture that was a lot closer to what he's learned about them. He found what was officially written up for the Menehune to be somewhat offensive.

                                  Another reason I tried this out, is that this way Changelings can actually be closer to how they're portrayed in other splats. For example, Trolls in WtA could go toe to toe with a Crinos (stat-wise), but the CtD system never did support that idea (outside of using Dragon's Ire).

                                      I think I might add the idea of doing kith and sub-kith as a character option. It does, as you say, add nuance. I've also thrown in the option of taking Redes as if they were Merits, too.

                                      I actually found the suggestion in Bygone Bestiary, and saw a suggestion online that they should start with Glamour 5 and Willpower 1. Considering that chimerae tend to have lower Willpower traits than most bygones, by the very fact that they're creations of other people's minds, that made sense.

                                      I've also been contemplating the idea of letting any other beast template from Bygone Bestiary be turned into a changeling character. It's easy enough to do. You just give them two Birthrights derived from Special Advantages, Redes and Merits, and one Frailty derived from Flaws and negative traits. You can design the fae mien how you see fit, provided the mechanical benefits and drawbacks fit the two Birthrights/one Frailty rule, and any additional mechanical effects can be treated as Merits and Flaws with the 7pts limit you'd normally get.
