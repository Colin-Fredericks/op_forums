                                              Some Archmasters are just implied, as we don't have their exact stats. Tychoides helped found a Convention, was (is?) hundreds of years old, and lived offworld at the Cop. That's standard Archmage behavior. Kepler was ancient, too. Turing was key to discovering and reformatting an entire Shen into the Digital Web. That requires Archmaster power.

                                              I doubt there are many Archmasters in the Technocracy today as Control would probably discourage them. Computer would definitely remove any new Iteration X Archmasters, at least until the Dimensional Anomaly, as they would be considered threats (this explains why the Matriarch is still around). The High Guild (proto-Syndicate) had Reginald Proctor, but he died years ago. Damian and Basil Rathbone helped reorganize the Order of Reason into the Technocratic Union, but that does not mean that either was an Archmaster.

                                              I'm ignoring everyone from The Chaos Factor since it's been declared non-canon. The World of Darkness books are iffy too.
