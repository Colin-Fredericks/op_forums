      Vampire: You're the bad guy. Your friends are also villains.
      Werewolf: You're fighting a war, and you're losing.
      Mage: You're fighting a war you already lost.
      Wraith: You lost, you died, and now you're trying to avoid a fate worse than death.
      Changeling: You're fighting a war and nobody is taking you seriously.
      Hunter: You're fighting a war where everyone's bigger than you and trying to kill you.
      Mummy: You're immortal... that's it.
      Demon: You're fighting a cold war with mixed results.
      Orpheus: You're mortal and dying temporarily is part of your job.

      “I am absolute, I am perfect, I am supreme. I shall be eternal. My tragedy, is that there is no other fate for me. My powerlessness was that I couldn’t subjugate my journey to the gods, while dreaming of rebirth at the end of distant time, like other pharaohs.” Ramesses II, Fate/Prototype: Argent Fragments.

                                  Vampire: Lots of assaulting people with sudden violence and coercion, and it should definitely remind you of something.
                                  Werewolf: Grendel tries to be Beowulf, and often ruins it by going on rampages.
                                  (with Mokole): Attack on Godzilla's Creed
                                  Mage: Post-modernism, the might-be-game.
                                  Wraith: The Frighteners, only with oppressive ghost empires and lots of Jungian psychology..
                                  Changeling: Desperation wearing a beautiful mask.
                                  KotE: A good justification to remove Noddism's narrative weight from the metatext of Vampire.
                                  Hunter: They Live combined with Darker Than Black and a library of classic horror films.
                                  Demon: Is it Biblical, or Sumerian, or some... third... thing?
