    Religion and Glamour

    07-17-2017, 08:05 PM
    Recently, I had a rather long and curious discussion with one of my players. Long story short, we were talking and wondering about how to handle Glamour and organized religion in our Changeling game. Now I'm in a bit of a impasse and I could really need some new perspectives:
    The issue is this: Is organized religion inspiring or banality inducing?
    On the one hand, organized religion are dogmatic, full of inflexible and unmoving dogma and usually reject frivolity, excess or stigmatize acts of whim and inspiration.
    But on the other hand, some of the most beautiful works of art and artistry were inspired by it. Church altars and stain glass windows tell stories, mosques are marvels of architecture and I could go on. So... how do you people handle Kith who find god and get into these kinds of things?

              Okay, I'm going to speak as a member of a minority among gamers. I'm a born-again, evangelical Christian. I came to that point when I was 28 and spent a good bit of my life up to that point as an agnostic. I only mention that as a preface to my comments so you can see where I'm coming from. I'm not going to try and convert anyone and will be respectful of anyone's beliefs.

              My belief in God and my decision to follow Christ fundamentally changed who I am. Once I made that decision I started down the road of being nicer and more caring. Before that I was very moody and was deliberately manipulative with people I knew. My whole outlook on life changed, and it led me to being a much better person. In a way, I went through a chrysalis when I was "saved", turning into a new being (as the Bible says will happen). Since that point nearly 20 years ago I have really delved into Bible study, learned hymns and worship choruses, and looked at the world around me with new eyes. I'm a scientist and suddenly I started seeing true wonder in God's world. Molecular biology amazed me with God's design. Astronomy astonished me with it's incredible balance leading to our very special planet. Each time I look at any aspect of science I see the beauty of God's creation. I've felt His presence in my life, seen it change me and people around me, and felt my heart lifted through prayer and song.

              Yes, religion can be extremely Glamour-filled! A true belief in a higher being can be incredibly inspiring and uplifting! Some of the most amazing art ever created had religious themes and were made as a way to honor God. Principles such as genetic inheritance and the Big Bang theory were developed by theologians. Many hospitals, charitable organizations, and even universities were started by religious groups. Amazing lyrics and poems have been written by people expressing their love for God.

              Now the flip-side. As a Christian I am absolutely annoyed and dismayed by the frequent closed-mindedness of may church-goers. I've seen so many people hurt by organized religion and pushed away from God by so-called "loving" Christians (Westboro Baptist, anyone?). Look at how religious extremists in many Muslim countries have instituted policies and laws oppressing women and brutalizing homosexuals. Spend enough time behind the scenes in a Christian church and you'll likely come across the people who are gossips and hateful, interested only in the specific tradition of their church and showing none of Jesus's teachings. People in any religion can become trapped in specific rituals and traditions, going through the motions because that's what they are supposed to do, not because they really believe in it. I've seen more people that I can count in churches and religious groups whom I can only describe as Autumn People.

              A true belief in God can be full of Glamour. Organized religion can contribute to that (look at how much wonderful art the Catholic Church has produced). But religious organizations can also be some of the most banal groups around and can suck Glamour right out of a person, stifling their creativity in the name of "god".

              In my games I've never shied away from religion, but I've never pushed it on my players. I've had NPCs with devout faith that were still able to function as well as any other changeling. If you look at House Liam you will see that they had strong ties to religion, especially Christianity, and were condemned for protecting churches. I think that in a C:tD game you can use religion as a horrible thing or a wonderful thing. It depends on the themes of your story.

                  I dont think faith, by itself, could produce Glamour, because Glamour happens when you produce something, when you actually create something Glamour creative and beautiful (even in a sad way) that ties to the Dreaming of the Dreamer. Faith based works of course can, all those Cathedrals, paints, mosaics, and the Whole freaking Vatican, can produce Glamour, but it is not due to their religion imagery or the religion behind it. Is just that religion can be a very powerfull (and rich) fomenter of artistic productions.

                  But, as pointed by Shepherd, faith can make you less banal and more easily able to see the wonder of the world in a religious point of view. I think it is a minority of people who actually get less banal with religion, as most religions and churches I have seen here are quite cynical and more focused on prayers and requests (and payments) than in doing God's good work and actually helping people. Also, being good and nice to people also has nothing to do with being banal or glamorous.

                  So, as a rule of thumb, I would ignore what the church says and the religious aspect of it and focus more on what it does and produces. Does it has a Choir? I might be a glamorous thing, if people there is doing for the music, or for the faith, and if they create musics, not only repeat it. If people are singing there just because they need the money (not quite sure if Churches' Choirs pay people, but follow my thought) or because they are demanded by the family as a tradition or something. It can be inspiring, and even good to hear, but nonetheless banal. Even the impact of entering a church, full of followers praying in unison, with the light entering through the stained glass might be Glamour inducing, but I dont think it would generate Glamour beyond the first times people get inside of it.

                  If you are asking about a Changeling going to church, sure, why not? Maybe avoiding the more banal ones, for obvious reasons, and too much preaching about "the Demon inside you" and "their powers to alter perception" might create a religious fanatic Dauntain, but that is the exception instead of the rule. Mostly will just go to church, do their religious thing, and go on with their lives.

                  // Just saying, I refer to church in a general way. It doesn't focus on any particular religion or faith. The example are more related to the Christian cases just because that is the one I'm more familiar with. */

                  But Shepherd make me though about one thing: could a scientist or researcher generate Glamour by finding a formula or way to solve a hard scientific problem, like a solving a complicated matter on a specific topic after months of research or explaining the way a certain thing in a topic of study work? In a historical example, Einstein could have been a Dreamer on the field of physics?
