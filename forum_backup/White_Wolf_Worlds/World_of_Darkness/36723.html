                  Well basically End Game, the final Orpheus book sees the PCs finally crossing the Stormwall and seeing the Underworld for the first time. At the same time there's a Spectre War going on between Grandmother and a group of Malfeans opposing her, realizing that she will eventually consume everything if she isn't stopped. They'll actually offer an alliance with the projectors against Grandmother, whether they take it is up to the players but regardless the central goal is the same, find a way to stop Grandmother.

                  The book presents a couple different ways of stopping Grandmother, the one stands out the most and likely the one Data's thinking about is tracking down something Grandmother fears and has hidden away in a memory tower. As it turns out that something is Abel, the Abel. Not only that but because of what he is, the first murder victim and all those who followed him, he's become a sort of gateway between the worlds of the dead and the living. With that power he can channel a large amount of lifeforce from the living world into Grandmother directly which will either obliterate Grandmother completely or sate her hunger and put her back to sleep. The catch, it will take a large sacrifice of life to do that. Two of possibilities they suggest is the entire Amazon rainforest or a major city like London, Tokyo or Hong Kong.

                  Homo sapiens. What an inventive, invincible species. It's only a few million years since they crawled up out of the mud and learned to walk. Puny, defenceless bipeds. They've survived flood, famine and plague. They've survived cosmic wars and holocausts. And now, here they are, out among the stars, waiting to begin a new life. Ready to outsit eternity. They're indomitable. Indomitable.

                                              Well after the new God took over she basically hand waved reality. I incorporated the parts of the Gehenna stories I liked. Had reality crumbling from Wormwood and eventually it all came down to Lucifer, Lilith, and Cain having a conversation. They pretty much realized they had been twats for eons. But since the Father was gone (dead, left, up in the air) they couldn't atone for it. They realized the girl with the tattoo was marked to ascend to divinity and needed the push. so ritual, and much talking and convincing later she became the new God and put the world back to rights with a few changes since she wasn't the original creator.

                                              Around this time Requiem was making the big splash and some players wanted to try out some of the clans but weren't thrilled with the new system. Seeing some things I, myself, liked I figured It be a good time to incorporate some of the changes. I also kept some of the effects of Gehenna going for those that survived up to that point (blood bonds broke, humanity grew/decreased, etc).

                                              The players woke up the next day (they were vampires, important note). Roll WP.

                                              Depending on the successes on the roll, they had reactions ranging from Nothing new here, something feels wrong, But that's not how THAT happened, I remember everything!. Some characters woke up in new clans (one wanted to play a Daeva), Some had new clan weaknesses (I used the new Gangrel weakness), and some had new allies, I ported over a few of the new clans (made them children of Lilith, not that players knew that), The Garou all changed into Forsaken, at least minorly (never did finish that conversion, huh.) Things like that.

                                              Things continued and there was a subtle shift in Kindred society. After the initial few sessions I fast forwarded the game a couple of years and had the Cam and Sabbat fall apart and it became more of a Dark Ages vampire society. Princes had their own domain and ruled it. No over arching governing body of vampires. Most keep the simple rules but it was all very decentralized.

                                                  I would probably do it as the end of the world as we know it, rather than the literal end of the world. The antediluvians would simply wake up and behave as they normally did in times past. Of course this would lead to masquerade shattering proxies wars as they use all the technological and supernatural means at the disposal of their followers.

                                                  Ravanna would lead the Ravnos against the Kuei-jin and this would culminate in full scale war between India and China. Haqim would lead the Assamites in a war against all other kindred, using islamic extremists as convenient dupes where possible. Ennoia would lead the Gangrel on a massive eco-terrorist campaign. Salout would take over the Tremere from the inside, while his childer Shaitan would arise in Mexico City having subverted the Regent and inner circle of the Sabbat, and the Baali wars would begin anew.

                                                  Ventrue would awaken and become the dominant force in the crumbling Camarilla, aided by Arikel. Cappadocius will escape from the underworld with the Harbingers of Skulls and lead them against the GIovanni. Lasombra will emerge from the abyss to lead the antitribu to vengeance against the rest of the Clan.

                                                  Tzimisce will go all Deep Rising on NYC spawning horrific monsters and devouring tens of thousands before retreating into the ocean like some kind of twisted Kaiju. Brujah will appear from the depths of time and led his childer against Triole's. Absimiliard will hound his descendants to the four corners of the Earth with the Nictuku. Malkav will rise from torpor and reveal he has been psychically directing the clan through the Madness network and begins a Jokeresque reign of chaos, his childer committing mass practical jokes and terrorism in equal measure. Set would return to wage war on the Children of Osiris and the mummies of Horus.

                                                  To accomplish these goals, the antediluvian would be siring numerous new childer along the way, and scores of 4th generation neonates would be running around.

                                                  Of course the Lupines and Mages will be going beserk at these developments and in the end, all this blatant supernatural carnage will shatter the masquerade so hard that the Dreaming will reintegrate with the world and the world will end up becoming a post-apocalyptic mishmash of high technology and fantastical magic and monsters.
