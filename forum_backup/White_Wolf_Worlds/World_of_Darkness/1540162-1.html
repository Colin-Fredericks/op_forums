    Originally posted by Saur Ops Specialist View Post
    Pentex striking back would also involve waves of fomori... that conceal a type known as normalites. It might not completely freeze a mage's magickal capabilities, but they might be enough to cause horrible damage to their enlightened personnel.
    Yes. Despite (probably) being a problem that the Syndicate can deal with and (perhaps) contain, the Pentex IS dangerous. I was mentioning the "institutional" pathways that it could use to strike back, should it saw itself being cornered. Besides those there are, quite obviously, many pathways to go into that are out of the use of societal institutions - crime and supernatural means, such as Fomori, are some of those.

    Pentex also has allies outside of their ranks, and potential allies, such as other Wyrm servants.

    For all of these reasons AND the need for keeping things rush-rush, is why the Syndicate would take baby steps towards that threat.



    They're smart enough to realize a connection, but don't have the context or frame of mind to follow it through as far as they would need to (their particular vidare cannot conceive of itself being a vessel of the Defiler, what the Defiler properly is, or that there's something subtly different from "Entropy" that isn't well, and that's before we get to the animism at odds with the TU's values). Also, that would tend to go against the Syndicate's absolute info-lockdown and secrecy about the SPD problem; if they're not going to tell the other conventions, they probably aren't going to mention anything that could relate that secret to a group of Reality Deviants.
    Sure, the Syndicate would not be all too willing to share nothing, but if some outside Reality Deviant comes with some fresh and solid Intel, they wouldn't refuse. And they wouldn't so much "share Intel" with them, as so much as "look, if a certain factory were to be blown away, it would be very interesting to both of us". They wouldn't explain why, but they would just try to direct these monsters in the direction they want.

                    I think it's plausible that the Syndicate could officially end up at cross purposes with Pentex, if it's approached right. Assuming that this is not a scenario with Nephandic infiltration, then the Technocracy has a vested interest in the world existing and being a relatively safe (and profitable) place.                     If approached right, of course, but that's for the player characters to figure out.

                    As to which one would win, it'd probably depend on which kind of game we're playing. In a Mage game with Werewolf elements, I think the Syndicate's got a pretty good shot. After all, a fight with the Syndicate is a fight with the Technocracy on a whole. So while there's Fomori on one side, there's Hit Marks, Victors, and Void Engineer weaponry on the other. Both are global entities, but Pentex is one corporation while the Technocracy has its tendrils pretty much everywhere.

                    In a Werewolf game with Mage elements, then Pentex isn't just a powerful corporation with Umbral ties but a tool of one of the Triat. A Syndicate/Technocracy victory might still be possible (especially if you read them as tools of the Weaver), but I wouldn't expect more than a pyrrhic victory, and more likely it'd be the Syndicate going down (perhaps with a surprisingly noble last stand, or maybe not.)

                            I think the degree to which the Technocracy can be effective against PENTEX relies heavily on the degree to which it can play nice with the "Reality Deviants" who were already fighting it. The Technocracy is really poorly suited to handle the Wyrm because they don't understand the Umbra. Their cosmology posits that the Umbra is just a the products of instinctual thought (human or otherwise) rather than fundamentally interlinked with the material world. This renders them unable to see how events in the Umbra will influence events in the material world even if no spirits (oh, sorry, "EDEs") cross over and prohibits them from recognizing that things that happen to nonthinking beings still have effects in the Umbra even if no creature with instinct notices them. In all likelihood they would ignore much of PENTEX's work because it shouldn't have any particularly harmful effect--and if they recognize that it does, they're liable to be reprogrammed because the Union regards animism as reality deviancy even though it is objectively true.

                            This aside, even in scenarios where the Garou are largely unaware of PENTEX, they have more experience fighting the group than the Technocracy does. The Technocracy would be going in without any intelligence.
