                                                            Yep. “Up to some extent.” Which gives the mage just enough rope to hang himself. There's enough truth to the idea that mages are better equipped than Sleepers to make important decisions that it's understandable when they decide to tell you how to live your life. But unlikeNyremne's assertion earlier, mages aren't omniscient; and they frequently overreach. It's easy for them to think that their access to the Spheres means that they have all the answers; but they don't. As Aleph just pointed out, there are answers that the Spheres can't easily provide, and that they may not be able to provide at all.

                                                            And even when Spheres can provide answers, not every mage has access to every Sphere. Let's assume for the moment that one can use an appropriate combination of Spheres to, say, Detect Evil: not every mage is going to have that combination of Spheres; and the ones who don't are going to be no better off than the average Sleeper when it comes to judging whether something is evil. Should they bow down to the mages who do have the correct combination of Spheres?

                                                            Or is turning your agency over to someone else itself a moral lapse? And if it is, isn't that just as true of Sleepers as it is of the Awakened? And conversely, wouldn't taking someone's agency from them be a moral lapse?

                                                            It's a messy thing, all told; but the important point is that even if the Awakened as a whole have better tools, that doesn't inherently put them on the moral high ground.
