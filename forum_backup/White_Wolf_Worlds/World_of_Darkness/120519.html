    The World of Darkness: College Edition (A Game)

    04-27-2014, 05:14 PM
    maybe i seen too many times animal house. but i was trying to explain a friend the whole mythos of vampire the masquerade with a party. so this game started... The idea is to explain the canon of one game..What are the rules?
    -It has to be midly realistic
    -All the races, are just people living on the same building since they are college students
    -Instead of calling a creature by his clan or race, you gotta come up with something like For example
    The Get of Fenris: The metalheads who joined green peace to get chicks and use the image of lita ford as if she was a godness.

    This is My explanation of the WoD and some clans, its not complete and i try to make it as brief as possible.



    In the beginning, there was a college….
    There was a building, an student apartment and at first. There was peace…and for a while, it was good.
    There lived Caine who got kicked off his house after beating his brother abel.
    The Gloom Ones who lived on the lower floor.
    The 13 brainiacs who focused on their own ideologies.
    And The Ones Who Lived There Before But Got Expelled.
    So, caine got to this flat with his roommate Lilith, they started to hang out an they got very close. She thought him the techniques to withstand a party, and Cain was happy with this. They got married and they had 12 children’s, they grow up and got to live in that apartment when they were in their college years, with their parents.

    At the same time, the sons of the neo hippy girl lived on the next door, the 13 sons, brothers and sisters who enjoyed some degrees of metal music.

    So, one day Lilith and Caine told their 12 children’s that they were heading out in a cruiser across the world and they would be back soon.
    So they left the house, and at first. Everything was ok. But then, one of the 12th sons found the “Book of the Party Hard.” And they spreader the knowledge among all the 12th . So, they started this little quiet and very under party.
    The Weirdos heard the music but they were cool with it.
    Hermes and his brothers were too busy studying for the finals.
    There were the Asian students, who were too busy trying to chill out and studied while doing tai chi,
    The glee club which enjoyed the music but it wasn’t their style, besides. They were religious fanatics.
    The Kapa Epsilon fraternity had all the Australians, madchesters blocks, Hawaiian surfers and all the laid backs were cool with the music but they didn’t felt like partying yet.
    There were the hippy ones who liked to hang out with some of the metal heads who were also eco terrorist on their spare time and both supported Greenpeace and PETA.
    There were the Dark Ones, a bunch of guys who study maths, probabilities, and also some of there were studying medicine, their ideals were shared with the Gloomy Ones who lived downstairs and sometimes they went down and hanged out with them
    And last but not least there were “The Chicks.”, you known how most girls go through a wiccan phase after they watch The Covenant and blair witch for a long time? They wanted to hang out with the eco friendly metal heads, and they allowed them because this chicks were hot.
    And the “Posers.” –how the gloom ones called them- were a bunch of guys who read too much into Poe and were some beatnik poets who wanted to be as cool as the Gloom Ones who lived downstairs but they were lacking the attitude
    So, the original 12th on the Enoch apartment were starting to party. And damn did they party hard. Putting all dark rave music and shit. But they thought that mom and dad would get upset if they find out about their party so they wanted to keep it low profile…well.. Too bad because too much people started to hear about this party and they started to crash with them on this ever going party.
    Ravnos bough the gypsies and since they needed more space, they broke a door and started crashing on an apartment right in front enoch.
    The party went very loud and constantly bugging the neighbors.
    The 13th decided they were too tired so they went to their respective rooms to chill out and hoped for the party to die out.

    The Gloom ones from under were tempted and once on a while they went to check the party.
    Hermes and the Omega Eta –his fraternity – were curious about the party, but the noise was bugging them and the finals were upcoming. So one of the guys told to the rest
    “Hey, im going to check whats up, maybe ask them to turn down the noise.”
    “oh ok, hey care to go pick up some soda?.”
    “yeah sure.”
    “thanks tremere, you’re an awesome guy.”

    And tremere went to ask them to turn down the noise, but there were some hot chicks so he and the ones who followed stayed in the party. Soon, hermes and Omega Eta found out that hermes now was passing the answers to the exams so they vanished him, and he was cool with it. Hermes wasn’t ok with the noise so he took his shit and went to study to the library. The rest of the Omega Eta forgot that Hermes took some notes and books and they really needed them. And they knew he had a bit of temper, so they waited for his return……

    So, in order to keep the party running and win a quick buck, this company came in called Pentex and set vending machines on every floor –except on the floor of the Gloom Ones – but the Metalheads knew that this products were threating the ozone layer so they started to beat the living crap of the vending machines and of whom ever consumed this ones.
    On more than one time, the metalheads ended on the party at Caine’s place. And they had heavy argues with the guys who were partying there, but one of the metal heads spilled the drink of one of brujah’s most trusted friends, and he got enraged and went all agro on the metalheads and ever since, the brujah’s now left their student façade and went all Sex Pistols and Anarchist on the rest.
    So as the party carried on, there were some heavy debates. You know, on that time when youre incredibly tired at nights and you start discussing stupid shit, so the remnants of the new 13 started to argue and they separated on factions who had the right to pick the music for the rest of the day/night.
    The 13 survivors of the party divided on
    The Elite, those who thought no one deserved to know about the party
    The Anarchoids: who said “screw the rules, we have a party.”
    And Black Sabbath: who picked their name after some ozzy tunes, but some of them realized that caine would come back and there would be a hell of a kicking.

    Since the party was getting too hardcore, a group of the brainiacs made a College Secret Police in which they keep on a close eye what was going on at the party. They were The Power Ties, the police, The IT department which hacked their accounts and saw what pics the partygoers uploaded, the tourism students who wanted to find a new building just in case, the engineers who saw too much mythbusters, the students of economy and the Science squad. And they formed the College Secret Police.
    As the party in the ennoch apartment grew harder and larger, the laid backs decided to party on as well on their own way, they stole a bunch of drugs from the science squad and from the Hippies and they realized that they could party more hard than anyone, and that they wanted to prove it.

    But one dark day came in. Ravnos and his gypsie friends were making too much noise so the College Secret Police went to his apartment, they kicked him out and dragged everything outside. But by doing this, they didn’t knew ravnos was actually making a tunel to the lower floors to expand his room and make it a club. By doing this, some of the Gloom Ones started to hang out with the rest, and the Exile ones saw this opportunity to come back, leaded by Lou Cypher (a good student who was involved on a Snafu and now was roaming aimesly) followed by The Army of Expelled ones.
    Since the secret police had too much power and there were too many fights near the vending machines, other students started taking things on their own hands and decided to go all vigilante on the metalheads, the glooms, the exiled ones and the partygoers. Since the old ones were too tired for partying so hard, and the vigilantes were enraged and were less tired, they could witstand much more, and they formed the Hunters Union.
    They also made a website to know who was making too much troubles and ask him to stop.

    One day while sleeping, a cellphone rang and one of the 13th picked up still dozing off and he heard the voice of his dad Caine, apparently Lilith was cheating on him with one of the expelled students who were with them back on the day and he was returning sooner to the house. And he was hoping everything was in place. The 13 fellas freaked out, and tried to kick all the party goers off the apartment but it was too late, a hell of a reckoning was incoming…





    yes. the gloom ones are wraiths.
