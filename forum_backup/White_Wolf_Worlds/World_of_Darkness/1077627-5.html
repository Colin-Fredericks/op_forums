    It occurs to me that timing may be a significant factor.

    I mean, the whole reason that the Tremere ended up resorting to vampirism is because they felt their magic becoming less and less effective. Immortality was the big concern, obviously, but the loss of potency and effectiveness seemed to be happening across the board.

    So even though the Hermetic Pillars are amazing, the practitioners are not at the level of strength that they would have been during say 850 AD. They're also about 100 years or so away from the modern Sphere conception of magic. Even if the practical level of deterioration isn't terribly notable (after all, Dark Ages Mage starts around this time), the psychological effects of it might be a factor in why many mages may not be using their magic to full effectiveness, as well as a distraction: since solving the problem of magic is probably considerably higher priority than dealing with a vampire house for a lot of people.

The very fact that you can do this proves that Corona can fully affect astral spirits (something that Mind alone can't do), as you're basically forcing one to come and then using Primus just to give it a sort of body.
                I was thinking the mage would rather do a Corona 4 rote and do their own scouting rather than relying on second hand info.


                Originally posted by Aleph View Post

                            Okay. Changing the designations on the Order end shifts some numbers a bit and also has its effects on the Tremere side.

                            For the mages, the mages of the 10 remaining founding houses with very roughly 115 per house with very roughly 2 Masters, 4 Adepts, 20 Disciples, & 89 Initiates
                            In a hundred years, each house would have 4 Master, 8 Adepts, 40 Disciples, & 178 Initiates for a total of 229 per house, and a total of 2290 mages. Each House would probably be supported by people around 4580 people, which would be probably 1 relatively big town (the towns of the era usually having 2500 people but ranging from 1000 to 8000 people).

                            For the Vampires, this would give us possibly something like 1 Tremere, 7 Pseudo Elders, 49 Pseudo Ancillae, 343 Pseudo Neonates, & 2401 Fledgelings for a total of 2801 Tremere Vampires. (The Disciples group is gone and the 7 followers of tremere are stronger than I thought). If you want to incorporate some minority of mages, it'll probably be something like 1 Master, 7 Adepts, and 14 Disciples.

                            Also, for those who might have missed it,
                            Shall I lower the Gargoyle numbers from what could comfortably be supported by 5 enslaved towns down to 1 enslaved town?
                            5880 to more like 1250?

                            Also, for Order Strategy thus far.

                            I'm thinking that they would transport themselves to the ideal spot via some sort of Vires 3 Flying carpet rote. That way, each Disciple could handle the transportation of themselves and 2 to 4 others. In a pinch, even an initiate can do the job. Eight of the Adepts could be devoted to making their flying carpets glow with sunlight (Vires 4). They'd be at the corners of the formation, forming something like a cube. Corona 3 could be used to make spies out of flying birds. One or two Adepts would be assigned to this. One or two Adepts will be in charge of bending light to keep the group more or less invisible (Until you enter a certain area and are washed in sunlight). All the Adepts will be in charge of countering spells and Disciples with Primus 3 can also do so in a pinch by lending flying carpet control to one of their initiates. One Master will be in charge of keeping the weather on their side and another (Life Specialist) will probably use Anima 5 to create a number of fantastic creatures you could repeatedly get food from, a bit like those goats from norse mythology you could eat each day and use as beasts of burden afterwards, though these would probably be made happily masochistic using Corona 4. An Master (of Mind) would likely be using Corona 4 to Astrally project around to scout more sensitive areas than an army of birds could manage. Any initiate could use Vires 2 to prevent their group from freezing in the cold and it'll be easy to get water by heating snow using Vires 2.

                            This is for getting to the site where they can see Coeris.

                            Also, so far, it looks like the order won't bring much of their mundane followers if any to the Alps.

                                                            Once the Hermetics have reached their destination, they attempt to take the castle for themselves.

                                                            One Master of Mind will be scouting around the Tremere base in Astral form, doing what damage can be done by a spellcasting invisible intangible entity.
                                                            His or her 2 Adepts will probably be doing the same. This groups remaining 4 disciples will probably be going around improving the morale of the mundane troops and looking for any signs of Tremere meddling with the minds of anyone in the castle. The 8 Initiates of this group will probably be assigned to aid the troops in castle defense.

                                                            One Master will be in charge of flying the castle and keeping it glowing, and maybe the mundane affairs of the castle itself. His 2 Adepts and 4 Disciples will ensure that it remains invisible and that every part of it inside the field of invisibility is awash with sunlight. They’ll probably be in charge of the weather as well. The 8 Initiates of this group will probably be assigned to aid the troops in castle defense.

                                                            One master will be in charge of castle defense, coordinating the 300 soldiers as well as the 2 Adepts & 4 Disciples who can counter magical effects, and the probably any medical needs and mundane stuff like feeding the people and ensuring they have water to drink. The 8 Initiates of this group will probably be assigned to aid the troops in castle defense.

                                                            The remaining Master will be using a Anima 5 & Vires 3 to 5 to create elemental bodies and Corona 4 to force spirits to inhabit these bodies and obey the Master’s commands. This Master’s Adepts, disciples, and initiates will be aiding in this process. There will be Vires 3 air elementals built to steal magic items and spell books and potions and the like; Vires 4 sunlight elementals meant to annihilate vampires and evil wizards in the fortress, and a vires 5 storm elemental meant to destroy any incoming armies and dismantle the enemy castle if that becomes necessary. The body of these could probably be made in one casting by a master & the adepts and disciples with expenditures of willpower and quintessence and a spirit from the astral plane made to inhabit the form in the next hour. (So it takes 2 hours to make 1). I’m supposing they’ll make a bunch of these before leaving (maybe 4 with 8 hours of work) and make the big storm elemental upon landing, The journey itself is about 100 miles and it won’t take them very long to get there by flying castle. Once there, they will keep creating more elemental bodies with spirits in them. At a rate of 1 more every 2 hours.

                                                            All the remaining 6 Adepts and 12 Disciples and 24 Initiates will probably aid in the castle defense with a fair number of them (probably a third of the Adepts and Disciples) capable of countering magic.

                                                            If this fails, the masters will likely just get together to use Vires 5 to blast Coeris into dust, leaving 1 master for defense.

                                                            Since no one else has commented on the Gargoyle numbers, and I don’t entirely agree with what can be sustained by just 1 enslaved town, I will use a number that can be sustained by 2 enslaved normal-sized town or one big town unless someone argues otherwise. This would give us 2500 gargoyles.

                                                            Comments criticisms and suggestions are welcome.


                                                            I'll be figuring out the Vampire side of things next.
