    08-02-2026, 09:45 AM
    Anybody remember when back when, I brought up sharper senses for vampires?

    So, according to the new 6E Player Packet:

    Senses. Your senses are no sharper or different than they were when you were a mortal.
    You can, however, heighten your awareness through Discipline powers (see Step 6) or by surging your blood to enhance your natural Attributes (see step 5)

                      So, after almost 10 years, I have to rephrase and reiterate this again, it seems.

                      I'll try to bring you along for a journey to my reasoning:
                      • Vampire is a horror game of a different kind of horror - the protagonists, the player characters, are the monsters and the game (supposedly) focuses on their personal, internal struggles as opposed to adventure-style obstacles and external challenges that require or inflict no moral or dramatic effect on the character.
                      • Vampires are creatures of the night and darkness, and several kinds of vampires (Nosferatu, etc.) are supposed to be inured to dark, poorly lit locations - like movie-style sewer systems. They are nocturnal predators.
                      • The mechanics of the game should serve its themes and accentuate the increasing alienation of the characters from the mortals around them, and that includes sensory aberrations ("only when your friend asks you why you've been sitting in the dark that you realize the light was off", or, "the copperish scent is overwhelming, sweet and inviting. When you open the door, your companion turns and nearly keels over. To you, the smells coming off the fly-covered, shriveled man on the kitchen floor are a mix between aged liquor and the intoxicating aroma of fear and despair, but the scents have lost some of their luster - like a meal going bad").
                      • Light and Darkness are thematic and symbolic. The Light is the realm of vitality and life, hope, truth, it is the tool of heroes and mortals huddling together against the night. The Dark is the realm of predators and danger, death, decay, secrets, treachery and unnatural things with too many teeth. Vampires are Of the Dark. They are the predator lurking in the shadows, watching the mortals huddled around the campfire. This is a personal idea of mine, to have this theme of light vs dark, and it doesn't necessarily exclude vampires from ever using lights (it can be symbolic, holding onto hope) but I don't think that they should be using or relying on it like the thematic mortal heroes.
                      • Heightened Senses and Eyes of the Beast are not precious, perfect powers whose loss would be terrible for the game, as you can simply replace them with more useful effects. But even if you wanted to keep them, you could because:
                      • I didn't say "give them Darkvision", I said "Low-Light Vision would be fine". Both Heightened Senses and Eyes of the Beast give you the equivalent of Darkvision or better abilities, plus other benefits such as premonition or bonuses to intimidation.
                      So... Yeah
