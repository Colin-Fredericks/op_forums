                          I had a rather weird idea after reading Devil's Due, not long after it came out. Given that this was quite a long time ago, it's possible that I've completely misremembered what was in the actual book, but the plot idea stuck with me.

                          I remember thinking that the way the book portrayed creating a demonic player character, sounded a lot like the summoner had carved a piece of the demon itself to have it weak enough to possess a human host without immediately consuming it. I'd started thinking about what might have happened to the rest of the demon. Since demons are not physical creatures the way humans are, could what was left in the Abyss still retain (or reform/regain) consciousness? And then, if it did, what would happen to it when the Abyss was torn open in 1999?

                          So then, you have the player character as the modern demon (created from what was left in the Abyss, having healed enough to have retained its consciousness/sense of identity, escaped, and now possessing a human host), and an NPC adversary made up of the part of the Demon that had been summoned in the Dark Ages, having done god knows what to stay free and alive in the present day.

                          Obviously it's a little too single-player focused to be a group plot hook, but it might make an interesting thread to weave into an existing chronicle. And offer a different sort of adversary for the group. How do you fight someone who is essentially you?
