                      A small print update today with some Standard Color options adding to a few titles:

                      cWoD:

                      Children of the Revolution
                      V20 Companion
                      Changeling: the Dreaming 2E

                      Just as a reminder Standard Color uses a thinner paper stock than Premium Color, basically the same paper as the Black & White books. This usually means the colors are less vivid, but it also means the price drops a bit per page in the production process. Examples of pages: Premium Color First Fable vs. Standard Color First Fable.
