    Dark Wars 2 - World War 2 in World of Darkness

    06-23-2016, 11:51 AM
    I'm a World War 2 buff, so this topic is partially my thing along with those like me, and partially inspiried by last night's episode of Nova about the discovery of U-166.

    While Wraith has The Great War (at least as a historical setting). I've seen limited resources or info about WW2. Most that I've seen covered is from character backstory (mainly the Vampires from the VTMYTE game) and what random bits and pieces that is hinted at like the Get's involvement on both sides of the war. Mortal Timeline and equipment shouldn't be that hard to track down and convert. But supernatural involvement would be since I know during the time period not many were as active as others, Mummies and Demons proper for sure wouldn't be involved. Others would take it as personal preference or duty to country or the right cause.

    Vampire - Primarily served as personal preference. Axis Vampires would take the death camps and the Third Reich as a Godsend for free food and the military machine cranking out resources. Allied Vampires would play politics to try to suede the powers to rise up in arms since a Nazi controlled Europe would be a Sabbat feasting ground. Soviet Vampires would be part of resistance groups sprinkled around Russia during occupation and major conflicts like Stalingrad or picking off Nazi soldiers on the battlefields like Kursk.
    Werewolf - Practically a civil war. Part of the Get of Fenris serving Nazi Germany and the Third Reich doesn't help things either. Packs mobilize to help Allied efforts, serving Army and Naval forces. Other breeds filling out rank and file for ground, naval, and air forces. Many fetishes were created from the gear to even vehicles (tanks, planes, ships, subs). These vehicular fetishes gain much renown and still serve in Battleground.
    Mage - Shit hit the fan, no doubt about that. Traditions, Technocrats, and Disparates all serve both sides. Either as rank and file soldiers proper, resistance fighters, engineers, doctors, pilots, naval captains. You name it, there's a Mage somewhere. Technocrats playing both sides to keep sleepers under their heel while in-fighting split the Technocracy creating two rogue factions that lead to two new Traditions.
    Wraith - Finally recovered from the scars of The Great War. War spills back into the Shadowlands. New Relics, Wraiths, and Spectres pour forth from all fronts like a rushing river. The Dark Kingdoms has a hard time readjusting to their new population boosts, but their fighting continues as the Dark Kingdom of Iron engages with the Dark Kingdom of Jade as soldiers fight on knowing only their orders from the mortal coil. Hierarchy for both sides try to resolve their differences but ultimately fails as the fighting continues.
    Hunters - None of the Imbued are known to be active at the time. Mortal Hunters are either soldiers who happened upon the supernatural either on the front, between engagements, or even resistance fighters finding "vultures" amongst the ruins of the cities or occupied areas.
    Kuei-Jin - Total civil war. Mainland Jin combat those of the Empire of Japan when hostilities began. Imperial Jin serving as soldiers, naval forces, and pilots, while mainland Jin soldiers, tankers, and resistance. Mainland Jin allying themselves with the Beast Courts and other Allied forces when situations gotten desperate, but Allied forces being the hardest to gain support since for the longest while the fighting was focused in Europe.
    Earthbound - Reawakened when members of the Third Reich stumbled upon a couple of Earthbound Reliquaries. It is rumored that the SS and the Thule Society serve Earthbound entities. Unsure of the claims yet. But one thing is for sure, something has woken up with fighting spread out over land, sea, and air.

              The numbers of the Swords of Heimdall probably wouldn't have split the Get of Fenris numbers down the middle. Probably not even if you just count the Fenrir numbers in Europe. The Swords were not particularly keen on expanding their qualifiers for membership, and since Revised and especially in W20, the tribe as a whole became much more diverse in its array of human kin, while most camps were presented as being extremely small fringe groups.

              That aside, Garou tribes don't obey human territorial borders. That's why you have nearly everyone but the Fianna in Russia, and not just Silver Fangs. Similarly, there would have been an awful lot of other tribes, particularly Glass Walkers, living in the cities when the Nazis came to power, and the Glass Walkers were much more likely to hold positions in human society. This leads to some... troubling conclusions about what Cockroach's tribe would have been up to in those years.

                  Here's a few 100% canonical events that take place in and around WW2. Specific citations available on request.
                  The Brujah are behind Stalin signing he Molotov-Ribbentrov Pact.
                  The Jovian Veterans are reactivated by the Void Engineers to fight Nephandi.
                  90% of the Hagalaz are killed battling Nazi Magi.
                  The NWO formally puts its support behind Hitler on January 4, 1940.
                  VA's denounce Hitler on October 15, 1941, and then make sure Pearl Harbour happens as a 'surprise' in December so that America will be fired up enough to enter the war.
                  1941 also sees the full schism between Nazi and Russian Get, presumably also non-Nazi German Get. Bone Gnawers are why half the population of Leningrad survived the siege.
                  1942 and 43 sees Situation Six form to fight the Nazi mages, the Hierarchy issue a call for all free Reapers to go to Europe, and the Euthanatoi start covertly pushing the Traditions to the Allied side by assassinating prominent pro-Axis Traditionalists, and the first Matter Transmission Portals developed.
                  44 sees the LA Anarch revolt and also the Technocracy as a whole officially turning on the Nazis. 45 continues to distract America's cainites by seeing major offensives by the Anarch revolutionaries, while Sabbat tzimisce use the chaos in Europe to being a 4 year process of conquering all of Romania.
                  Garou storm Berlin alongside the Russians and so do the Technocrat-Traditionalist Alliance Forces.

                                  Cynic01 is 100% right about the SoE and their joining the Eight Traditions, (which was the number for a long time as the Solificati were out way way way back (1470) after the First Cabal debacle and the Seat of Matter was vacant for a substantial period). Then it was back down to Eight when the Ahl-i-Batin left in 1922. So it was Eight, not Nine Traditions for the duration of the War and the years that led up to it.

                                  The VA's were still a part of the Union for the duration of the War. Their position is very likely to be almost entirely Allied proponents, considering Turing's position in the Convention, Bletchley Park and where the next significant steps in computing are going to happen (the US). The VA's left the Union (1956), after the NWO's attempted (successful?) assassination of Turing (1954), were on their own for a very short scary time and then joined the Traditions (1961) offering up a copy of the Time Table. Which made it the Nine Traditions again for only the 3rd time (1466-1470, 1905-1922, 1961-present) and just in time for the Pogrom to go into full swing for the heart of the Cold War.

                                  That also means it was Six Conventions - the Virtual Adepts, the relatively new NWO (1920), Progenitors, Syndicate, Void Engineers, and Iteration-X (which may have still been called IBM, but I can't pin down a firm date on when the Computer achieved sentience.)

                                                  On the Mage issue again, particularly the Technocracy, we've been dealing with an aspect of this in my current M20 game. All the PCs are partially amnesiac and time-displaced. One of them is a '40s era Virtual Adept. One of the issues we face in dealing with Union (and some Etherite) characters is what does advanced tech MEAN in the past? Is it just what was ended up actually happening?
