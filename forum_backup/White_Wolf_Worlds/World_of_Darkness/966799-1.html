            I'd honestly have preferred it if Imbued were far more of a rarity and largely ignorant of one another- no Hunter-Net or other organizing forces- although given the de-emphasis on the apocalyptic metaplot I'm not even sure how well Hunter fits thematically anymore- with the book characterizing them as a subset (suitable for PCs given that they don't get as trivially trashed by readily available supernatural powers as ordinary folk) of the wider, disparate range of independent* hunter archetypes in existence.


            *The mere existence of formal institutions like the Society of Leopold and Project Twilight are basically the failure of the Masquerade/Veil incarnate- it's not clear on how we're supposed to take the whole "conceal yourself from mortals" paradigm seriously when there have been institutionalized hunters for decades or even centuries. How has this not caused it all to come crashing down? Can you imagine the Congressional discussion on a bill allocating funding and authority for something like SAD?

                            Something which I think will prove interesting in the process of updating is the sense of impending Armageddon. Reckoning felt heavily tied into the Time of Judgement and all of its millenniarian retinue. There undercurrent of existential dread was always a big part of it, at least from my perspective. The idea that Imbuings were happening because something had gone very, very wrong and that your learning curve as a hunter was going to be steep because cataclysm was right around the corner. We're over that hump now though, and the other 20th lines are generally moving away from that metaplot angle, so I'm curious how Hunter is going to feel without that Sword of Damocles hanging over it.
