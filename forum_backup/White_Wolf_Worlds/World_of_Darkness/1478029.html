    Your flourishes for the setting?

    04-04-2022, 11:07 PM
    This is a list of specific elements and flourishes to define and describe a World of Darkness. While we know the setting is darker than our – that is right in the title- this is usually vague. But something specific is better, and it gives more concrete details.



    In the World of Darkness;



    Office Safety; OSHA (the American Occupational Safety and Health Administration) does not exist. So, work-places, and construction areas, are more precarious.



    The Arts: Corporate control results in a commodified product. This means artist who were successful in the real world never made it big in the World of Darkness. This includes Prince, Willie Nelson, Jim Jarmusch, Francis Ford Coppola, Cormac McCarthy, and Harper Lee. They are not household names and are not studied in schools. (This comes from a post from “No One of Consequence” at the Onyx Path Forums)



    Parks: Corporate sponsors manage of city parks. Tall privacy fences surround city parks and you have to pay to get in or use a pass. But many corporations gave up on the management but left the fences in place. So "Independence Square" becomes "Harold and Harold Inc Park." But H&H abandoned management of the place once the fence is up and they got the check from city hall. Nasty things - natural and supernatural - go on in the fenced off and unsupervised park spaces.



    Noise Pollution: Everything is louder, at all hours, and it is all ugly sounds. It seems like there are always helicopters overhead, roaring away, at night. There is also loud construction work going on at roads, sidewalks and so on. It's unnecessary, nobody wanted it, and every "fix" makes everything worse. Still there are always, always roadworks and constructions. This road has been under renovation for an three months. While driving people lay on the horns all the damn time. (This comes from a post from “Rhywbeth” at the Onyx Path Forums)



    Roads: The roads are being built to damage your car so that businesses can profit on repairs. Somebody or something is funding these things for some purpose. God only knows what they're building or when it will end. That road gets dug up 12 times a year. This traps the locals in their houses during "the street and pavement resurfacing." That road is always closed. (This comes from a post from “Rhywbeth” at the Onyx Path Forums)



    Police: Law enforcement is more violent, brittle and cruel. Private firms handle this in many places. They are not subject to legal restraints on evidence collection or the use of violence. Not that actual law enforcement has much to hold them back. In the World of Darkness the legal concept of “fruit of the poisonous tree” does not exist.



    Sanitation: Lots of services are low priority. Sanitation, health and social services being examples. Vermin, waste and litter are everywhere in most areas. This adds to all around stress and poor health. (This comes from a post from “Rhywbeth” at the Onyx Path Forums)



    Gated: Neighborhoods, communities, and subcultures are more closed off. Anyone who isn't "one of us" or at least known and vouched for by someone in the community is suspect. This includes neighborhoods, gated communities, suburbs, LGBT+ bars and clubs, small towns, etc.

    (This comes from a post from “No One of Consequence” at the Onyx Path Forums)



    Work: The National Labor Relations Act, the Fair Labor Standards Act (1938), Title VII of the Civil Rights Act, and most other labor laws do not exist in the World of Darkness.

          I don't know how much Gothic or Punk applies, but miserable I can do. Other than adding some advertisements that sound like something out of Bloodlines or GTA, because nobody's really bothering to pretty up the lower level corruption and greed, I don't need to add anything. Just exaggerate the stupid shit that already exists and occasionally slap a bit of the paranormal on top.

          I tend to flavour my setting with my own personal miseries;

          Noise pollution and Police. Why is everybody so much more of an asshole that their real world counterparts? Because they're fucking sleep deprived.
          Depressed, disorientated, aggressive, being driven into psychosis if they're not already there... oh, and traffic accidents and such are really high as a result. Insurance companies have their fun. There is a booming industry in coffee, energy drinks and sleep aids and such (none of actually them work, or they secretly do something horrible to you, but hey.)
          Most of it is actually due to the constant police presence. Every night, for hours on end, a helicopter will circle over your house. Low enough to shake the house. It will not leave. Is there violent crime outside your door? Who are they looking for? Is crime that frequent, or can they just not find who they're hunting? Who are they hunting and why? You can't find any information about what the hell is happening from any sources, and all official sources brush you off. Also, are you sure it's the police?

          Pointless contruction. It's unneccessary, nobody wanted it, and every "fix" makes everything worse. Still there are always, always roadworks and constructions. This road has been under renovation for an three months. That road gets dug up 12 times a year. The inhabitants of this street have been trapped in their houses for a fortnight while "the street and pavement are resurfaced." That road is closed, always closed. The roads are absolutely being built to damage your car so that businesses can profit on repairs. Somebody or something is funding these things for some purpose. God only knows what they're building or when it will end. Some sites are under permanent construction it seems. Half the time they don't seem to be building anything. You never see a construction worker, but sometimes in some of them you hear the work...

          Lots of services are low priority, where money is concerned. Sanitation, health and social services being examples. Vermin, waste and litter everywhere in lower income areas adding to all around stress and poor health.
          Maybe 1 or 2 in 20 of the staff in health or social care services actually give a shit about the people in their care. Most of them are just here to get paid while doing everything they can to dodge work or responsibility, preferably by pushing it all onto the ones who give a shit. These organisations and systems are all ripe for exploitation, and exploited they are.

          Arson. Because for some godforsaken reason some people have nothing better to do then destroy the countryside, homes and lives in fire. Sometimes while verbally abusing their victims and emergency services. Somewhere is ablaze. Somewhere is always ablaze. This being the WoD, some of them could very well be hunters who don't understand the concept of the means not always justifying the ends.

                  The existence of an active spirit world and its associate animist cosmological aspects (even if most humans and even a lot of vampires aren't really aware of it) tends to influence both the physical and spiritual atmosphere, including weather, local moods, and the like.

                  The existence of a wide range of supernatural predators (and even mundane ones like serial killers) who lurk both on the edges of human society and even among them has resulted in a near universal sense of xenophobia. Neighborhoods, communities, and even subcultures tend to be much more closed off and extremely wary of strangers or outsiders. Anyone who isn't "one of us" or at least known and vouched for by someone in the community is automatically suspect and seen as a potential threat. This includes ethnic enclaves, gated communities, suburbs, LGBT+ areas, rural clans, small towns, etc. as well as criminal organizations, underground groups, and even the elite power brokers.

                                          Originally posted by Grumpy RPG Reviews View Post

                                          What are the options for an employee if a supervisor makes such an assignment? Is it just to quit the job? Because some states in America have a similar set of laws in place.
                                          Yup, exactly. You got only two options: either you'll quit or you'll be disciplinary fired if you refuse. Of course you can always go to court and try to prove that your employer's actions were illegal.

                                          And there's also another curious thing: imagine that (in Poland of course, don't know about the US regulations you've mentioned) your employer sends you to f. ex. Germany. You've got to be there in two days. He will pay for the transport and the place you'll be staying (but only you). You don't want to leave your family so you quit. But there is a 3 week (for example) notice period so you have to go anyway (or there will be a disciplinary notification in your paperwork). But after those 3 weeks you can return home but you will pay for the trip.

                                          Another example: you go for a vacation. The employer has the right to call you back if you are needed (so not just because he wants you to). But he'll have to return the cost of the trip and/or other costs. I live in Poland and my sisters in UK. Sometimes I visit them and it costs from 30 to 100$ (cheap flights, but only 2 times a week). I once checked how much it will cost return from UK in any other day (imagining that my employer wants me to return and gives me 24 hrs). I would have to make a trip like that: UK-Portugal-Belgium-Germany-Poland (Danzig)-Poland (Łódź), by plane, only the last part by train. Cost? Over 5500$. And the funniest thing: if I'm late my employer not only won't have to pay for the trip but he technically has the right to fire me disciplinary. I checked it a few years ago and then my sallary was about 600$ per month. That's why I got 2 phone numbers, one for my "official" face (work, banking etc) and one private. If your employer can't reach you he can't call you back. Email adress doesn't count, there is no law demanding you to check it. Of course in WoD there could be a regulation like that. Hell, now I'm not sure if it wouldn't be legal for your employer to add a paragraph like that in your contract (based on article 100 labour code).

                                          Ok, those examples are little extreme and I doubt any employer would do something like that (it would generate a lot of bad PR) but as far as I know (and I consulted it with my friend, a Ph. D in Law) it would be entirely legal.

                                          And try not to destroy things from real world, rather make them ineffective. Or more corrupted. Like OSHA. In Poland the counterpart of it have to forewarn the employer of the control.

                                                      While I disagree with one or two of the suggestions above as being a bit too much, here's my contribution.

                                                      Public Lighting: there are fewer lampposts, streetlights, and other light sources on city streets (i.e. it's literally darker).

                                                      and the opposite

                                                      Industrial Lighting: bright floodlights that are always on in industrial areas and that end up creating impenetrable dark shadows (it's amazing how irritating even a single strong light can be, we have one to the back of our house that's on all night, every night)

                                                      Regarding things like labour laws, office safety, etc - the laws can exist just fine but it's enforcement of them that really counts. A few legal precedents can make all the difference between a law that works for the people and one that works for the corporations. Note however that I've never heard of or conceived of a situation in an RPG where office safety or labour laws are at all relevant except as background detail.

                                                      Lastly, to quote 'The Crow'

                                                      'It can't rain all the time': oh yes it can
