                                                Comparing an insignificant being with limited powers like a Methuselah against an Incarna it's like comparing an ant with an elephant.

                                                Note that 8th level powers are defined, and most, if not all, can theoretically be defeated by normal werewolves with normal buffs (they're ruled, and new "invented" powers of the same level shouldn't be more powerful than written ones). The powers of the Incarna, in contrast, are explicitly beyond character sheets. A mere Rank 6 has nothing in front of the true form of an Incarna.
                                                A Rank 6 it's merely a legendary hero, and also can be (theoretically) defeated with all and her (powerful, but stated) Rank 6th Gifts. However, its explicitly stated that an Incarna can't be defeated without plot devices or powerful allies aka. Storiteller's Fiat.

                                                Think that Rank 6 Gift it's needed to summon the avatar of an Incarna which it's much weaker than it's true form. An avatar it's a mere fragment of the Incarna, and yet they usually have Attributes on 20+ (and almost limitless magic). How many Methuselah have you seen with Attributes at 20+. And remember that that those character sheets with 20-25 in Attributes (like the Fenris Avatar, or the Aeon in the Book of Madness) are mere slivers of the true power of the Incarna.

                                                An Incarna it's easily on the power level of an Antediluvian, if only because they both fall into the narrative plot device category.
