And the Malfeans are actually sleeping "peacefully" most of the time, and even when awake, they don't come to the material world. Wyrm Incarnas can invade and corrupt many Umbral Realms... But almost never will come to Earth.

                                                            The truth is: spiritual interference on Earth is a minor thing. Yes, you do have some thousands of water spirits on a river. But, they are not possessing people or materializing, or turning houses close to it into shells. Rather, they keep only making the waters of the River flowing. The millions of earth elementals in a city, will just stay there, sleeping and keeping the soil stable.

                                                            The majority of spirits are just keeping the "metaphysical inertia". They aren't actively "shaping reality", but rather, they are KEEPING it.

                                                            And I'm sure the Union would love to vaporize all the trillions of banes out there if they could.

                                                            Well... Good luck with that.

                                                            Now, while the spirits will pretty much be a minor influence on the material world (in the sense that they mostly keep things as they are, instead of being a major cause for mayhem), Deviants are a real danger... Not to mention that MANY deviants will act UPON THE SPIRITS THEMSELVES.

                                                            I mean... If spirits are the reality builders... How dangerous is a Dreamspeaker acting as a kind of "spiritual syndicate"? Or an Hermetic using "slave labor" to change reality?

                                                            If anything, I'd say this makes the deviants even MORE dangerous
