    How does your Kindred view the other clans?

    09-06-2024, 04:03 PM
    Just a fan experiment and I did a short version of it on another topic.

    I play mostly a 11th generation Malkavian networker who is from the Victorian Era and of course has dealt with nearly all the others at some point and came up with his notion. So here goes.

    The Banu Haqim. My Malkavian views them with wary interest and knows that they are more then warriors and have blood mages and vizers that could give the Tremere, Toreador and Ventrue some trouble down the road.

    The Brujah. Mostly disdain is what my guy feels as he trying to see where the Learned Clan part comes in and views them as bright stars that burn out quickly.

    The Caitiff: Mostly wonderment as how this group will pop up from time to time and makes the Kindred nervous.

    The Gangrel: My Malkavian doesn't deal with them much and just knows as tough survivalists that handle themselves.

    The Hecate: As far as he knows is that the Giovanni and a number of necromantic bloodlines somehow banded together for survival

    The Lasombra: Scary people who can use primordial darkness and only respect power is how my Malkavian sees them and acts accordingly

    The Ministry/Followers of Set. Sees them as a very adaptable clan that is overlooked by the rest of the Kindred and overlook how diverse there network of contacts are and they don't discriminate of whom they have in their pocket.

    The Nosferatu: They may look horrible, but they are strong survivors with sharp ears and know things that can make all the difference and are willing to share for a price.

    The Ravnos: Not many of them around are they?

    The Salburi. My Malkavian has heard of them, but never dealt with them as far he knows.

    The Thin-Bloods: A group that even the Caitiff can feel superior to and should be watched carefully.

    The Toreador: Sees them as a waste of undead flesh that only like to think that they are the best of the best and that a bored Toreador is a very dangerous one.

    The Tremere: My Malkavian is glad is that they are mistrusted so much as they can see nearly as well my kindred's clan.

    The Tzismce: He isn't sure what to make of them as needlessly cruel fiends that revel in being inhuman monsters, which my Malkavian can respect

    The Ventrue: Kindred that see themselves as the rulers of undead society and have the power to back up the claim and try not to get them to angry in there domains.

    So that is how my character sees the other clans and I'm curious of how your player character sees it.

                          Nosferatu (Majority): I used to feel sorry for them, but not anymore, despite their appearance they are fighters, survivors and are far from weak.                           Malkavian: My mistress mentioned that she once traveled with a bunch of them and that they were actually good company, until they had to kill them.
