                        I'd ditch the “might have been”: they wouldn't have been, because the circumstances weren't right back when White Wolf was still making original World of Darkness gamelines. Instead, focus on “could be”: assume for the sake of argument that White Wolf has authorized Onyx Path to make versions of their unique-to-ChroD gamelines but set in the original World of Darkness. I also wouldn't worry too much about making them fully stand-alone; my point with Mummy was that the original World of Darkness' counterpart to Mummy: the Curse is not a stand-alone gameline, and that's OK. Mummy sets a precedent.

                        The key, I think, would lie in making the WoD versions distinct from their ChroD counterparts while still remaining recognizably similar. That's what I was trying for with my Promethean pitch: keep the notion of artificial beings on a journey from subhuman to something better, but shift the focus from “learning to be human” to “growing up” — gaining the independence to do your own thing, then developing the maturity to handle the responsibilities that come with said agency. That's why I was focusing on the “Prometheus Spark” being what separates a Promethean from a plain old ordinary artificial being: the Spark represents the agency that would set Prometheans apart from the artificial beings that already exist in the World of Darkness.

                        Geist isn't going to be a stand-alone gameline, because its subject matter (life after death) is already covered by Wraith. True, Geist approaches it from almost the opposite able from Wraith; its protagonists are mediums and necromancers, living humans who interact with ghosts (thus their “Sin-Eater” moniker); but they still exist in the same world as wraiths do: they have to deal with Stygia and its rules about non-interference between the living and dead. I proposed using Orpheus' projectors as a basis for this because they already exist in the original World of Darkness, but not in a way that's all that compatible with Wraith (so there's some room available to revise them); they already match the “living humans who are connected to death due to near-death experiences” thing; and their core power (projecting their souls into the Shadowlands) would distinguish them from their ChroD counterparts. For this pitch, I'd call the protagonist's projected form his Geist. I wouldn't mess with the “two minds” thing; leave that with wraiths and the Risen. But let them cope with Spite in a manner similar to Orpheus' projectors.

                        I haven't said much of anything about Beast because I don't really get it. I mean, I understand the concept more or less; but I don't get the appeal. As such, I'd never be able to do it justice.

                        My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness

                                    So why not make all the gamelines just spinoffs from Mage? Werewolves are created by mages using Life and Spirit to make Garou. Vampires are just a mage death magic experiment gone awry (making the Tremere a sort of recidivist joke). Wraiths are mages stablilizing the spirits of dead and enabling them to have a post-biological life existence. Changelings are just mages grafting empowered dreams on human souls. Inspired are actually talking to their mage masters and using proxy mage magic....

                                    The independent or quasi-independent game lines do not dangle as accessories to extant lines. They just don't. Not even Mummy. They stand in their own right. There are crossovers and overlaps and places where they experience limited mergers, but there is a core conceit to the line that isn't just "<Insert name of full-on supernatural type> is entirely the creation of <Other type of supernatural>" If you want to equate Prometheans and Sin-Eaters to Kinfolk and Ghouls, subservient half-splats, then it makes perfect sense to say "Geist Sin-Easters? Just Mediums." "Prometheans? Just something Progenitors or Etherites cooked up in lab". In which case, the whole discussion is moot from the outset. The OP seems to be saying that they should be at least about the level of Mummy. (Which, I agree with Data, is pretty much the benchmark for an independent "type").

                                    Otherwise, Sin-eaters need to be more than Mediums or Orpheus projectors. That doesn't capture the essence of Sin-Eaters and their Geists. Sin-Eaters, to be a Mummy-level type splat have got to have more to them than Risen. They need to take the idea of Sin-Eater, with something like a guiding spirit, the "back from beyond", the essence of their Keys and Manifestations and then spin it in all inside the World of Darkness mythospace. Then follow out from that and make the changes. Mediums definitely don't have that. A Wraith20 projector might be a step closer, but, really, it seems like it's going to be "Live Dude who's Live Dude power is being able to be Dead Dude/Dead Dude who pretty much = standard Wraith with a twist of lime". That doesn't encompass the very real power that Sin-Eater's have in the Skinlands.

                                    Soooo..... Hmmm...How about, instead of Orpheus projectors, take a spin off of the whole "zombie" thing that play initially in Hunter? When the Maelstrom/whatever happened that caused the issues that created the various types of the Walking Dead (oh and made the Mummy's do a phase/state change thing), there was something put in place that started shoving the souls of the departing back into their bodies with a huge surge of Shadowlands energy? I'd rather not have the "Geist" side of things be a specter that gets shoved in with... seems too much like a Mummy echo, or a fissure in the soul where "Geist" = "Shadow" in wraith terms, Geists are so cool, they echo the best ideas for Avatars - but... no, not that either, I think, since that makes Sin-eaters some kind of ur-mage. I'm stumped for now, but there seems like there's an idea to get a WoD version of a Geist in there somewhere.... In any case, you end up with a supernatural living human filled with usable death that can use that power. It's sourced in the WoD metaplot/setting.

                                    That's what I can come up with for now.... I'm going to chew on something that keeps Prometheans from being just an addendum to Mage..... There has got to be something with the Azoth thing.... I dunno. If there was some way for the Created to have come from multiple different game lines, where each game line had a way to create automata and then something FIRES some of them up.... That might work. But, really, it's just different types of mages, maybe vampires (yeah, a Promethean vozhd, now THAT would be fun...), but... not really the other lines. So that's a dead-ender.
