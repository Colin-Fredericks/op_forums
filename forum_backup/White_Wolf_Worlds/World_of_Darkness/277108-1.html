                            My purposed Characters for Season 1.

                            Neonate - Jessica Antwiler - Newly Embraced Vampire of unknown origin (currently Caitiff, possibly Toreador, Ravnos, or Follower of Set). She is seeking the one who Sired her, she also has a burn on her right hand for trying to experiment if she was the traditional vampire or those who "sparkle".

                            Prince - Travis Copley - Clan Toreador - To him there is no other Clan who is best sooted to run the Kindred of LA than Toreador. He's a secret board member to most movie studios both major and independent, and he likes to run the city like a set of a film.

                            Primogens - Rosemaria Briceno - Clan Toreador - Eiza Gonzalez - Started as a famous painter and now currently running 85% of LA's art museums. Rosemaria is Travis's right hand girl. Smart, precious, and deadly.

                            Sir Brandon Neeson - Clan Ventrue - Lance Henrikson (pending, alternate casting choice Christopher Lambert) - Combine old money Hampton and Prohibition Era Chicago, that is Sir Neeson in a nutshell. Able to hide money where you need it without anybody noticing or enforce the Traditions with the same ruthlessness as Al Capone. While most would address most Primogen, but all who address Brandon Neeson it is always Sir, nobody knows why but when he is asked to perform Final Death on an guilty Kindred then you'll see why.

                            Elizabeth O' Fallon - Clan Tremere - Elisa Hensen (pending) - Studious and a scholar. Owner of a few occult shops. She advises the Prince when events happen with supernatural influence or the mundane can't explain. Although she doesn't wish it, she is the Emissary for L.A.'s Magi.

                            Icon - Clan Nosferatu - (casting pending) - L.A.'s Archivist. Once a leading actor who delves into historical records; for research for roles and for a fun hobby. He became arrogant with his fame his fame, almost like a tyrant on set. He insulted the Prince for cutting his pay. The Prince sent the previous Primogen to Embrace him. Renamed Icon for his former fame he worked his way up to the Primogen seat, to regain the Prince's trust and to keep an sharp eye on him.

                            Maximilian Giovanni - Clan Giovanni - Sam Neil - A rarity for the Camarilla, a prominent medical entrepreneur with a few secrets beyond the grave. The more independent of the Primogens, representing the Camarilla to the Anarchs and viseversa, as well as representing the dead. One of the most powerful Kindred in L.A. next to the Prince.

                            Dr. Tina Galazra - Clan Malkavian - (casting pending) - A psychiatrist gone off the deep end thanks to the Embrace. After being Embraced she stepped down from practicing to research, still on call just in case. The research lab lab is manned by seven ghouls during the day and three at night assisting her. Her research is on the bit of fringe and controversial. She is looking into the connection between blood born diseases and the human psyche. Both the Prince and Icon are keeping an close eye on her since her work is getting close to a possible Masquerade breech.

                            Sheriff -
                            Pedro Santinni - Clan Gangrel - (casting pending) - A wild streak that anyone would swear he is Lupine Kinfolk. The go to for the Prince and Maximilian to enforce the Traditions. Cunning and savage, there is almost nothing he will do to bring his target to justice. Icon is starting to suspect that Pedro might be a Sabbat infiltrator, even the Anarchs aren't as savage as this Gangrel.

                            Debra Van Brunt - Clan Brujah - (casting pending) - The modern day Valkyrie. She uses the techniques of old with the tools of today. She is the go to for Icon, believing she is the perfect counter to the Gangrel. A fiery temper and sassy to boot. She keeps an eye on the gangs, taking a cut from drug trafficing. However whenever a gang in her territory disregards her rules. That gang ends up arrested with all the evidence pointing to them alone.

                            Harry "The Fixer" Oranos - Clan Tzimisci Antitribu - (casting pending) - A former member of the Sabbat, though it wasn't for very long after being rescued by Sir Brandon Neeson. Another from Old Chicago, he remains under the employ of Sir Neeson. He takes care of the problems that Sir Neeson isn't able to ake care of himself either too busy or his Clan's nature wouldn't allow. While Icon knows about him, but he knows that Neeson's Fixer is faithfully loyal to the Camarilla. The Fixer is the secret weapon of not only Sir Neeson but also the Camarilla as well. Even the Anarchs and Sabbat knows not to mess with the Camarilla without earning the wrath of The Fixer.

                                                Here's what I got for the opening scenes for the pilot.

                                                Jessica awakens to a bloody hotel room. Beams of sunlight leaking through cracks between aluminum foil and curtains as the sun sets indicating from the low hues. She opens a compact to use the mirror within. Looking in the mirror she sees that her skin is paler, blood covering her mouth, and her canines are sharper. She stares in shock at the reflection, throwing the compact across the room, it slams against the wall, popping open and the makeup shattering, the chunks scattering everywhere. She looks towards the beams of light, raising her right hand as she steps towards the light, hoping it is not true, or she might be those other types that sparkle. As soon as the back of her right hand touches the light, smoke sprang forth and sering pain shoots through her body, she recoils in fear and pain. Retreating to the bathroom to get water on the wound, throwing both hot and cold water in a blinding motion. As she soaks the wound, she discovers a dead body in the bathtub. With a series of bites on the neck, limbs, and two on the exposed torso, along with semi fresh blood as if the body was "stewing".
                                                Hunger courses through her mind, blocking out the pain. She scampers over to the tub, almost fascinated by the blood. Her head plunges into the tub, lapping up blood off the bottom like a pet drinking water for the first time after a few days. The door of the main room shatters open. She stops and turns to the bathroom door when two figures enter the bathroom.
                                                The first a hideous creature, warped skull, short wiggling tendrils for "hair", alien looking eyes, fearsome fangs and claws. "In here Sir." It said into the door way. The second emerging, recognizingly human, in a full Armoni suit, a trench coat and fedora not only well worn but also purposely mismatched with the suit. "Good job son." He said in almost a accent that seemed to come from an old Noir movie. In fact both seem to have similar accents and styles.
                                                "It's alright sweetheart. Yer safe now. The name's Brandon, and this is my right hand Harry." The mobster said as he extended his hand to help Jessica up. Still shaken at the sight of "Harry", she gradually takes Brandon's hand.
                                                Brandon helps Jessica up gently, like a police officer would for a frightened child. "Bag the blood for the young lady, standard clean up, Crip hit." He says to the thing he called Harry. Jessica would have sworn Brandon would have said broad considering their look and accents, but she was more confused with what he meant by standard clean up and Crip hit. Is this mobster starting a gang war? Brandon leads Jessica out of the hotel room, once she was outside she sees that it is twilight, lots of purple hues as the night descended.
                                                "I'm afraid to say this sweetheart, but you are no longer human." Brandon says as he pulls out a cigar and a matchbook from his trench coat. "We're what you call Vampires. And no it's not the laughing stocks that the young people like these nights." He cuts the end off the cigar with what looked like a claw before he places itin his mouth and lighting it with a match. Jessica flinches at the sight of the flame as Brandon lit the cigar.
                                                "Yes, fire hurts us, along with the sun and other stuff. But don't call us weak. Our strength is the blood, it is our food, currency, power, and greatest weapon." Brandon says as he takes a long puff on the cigar as he starts leading the way to the stairwell. Jessica follows closely behind like a lost puppy.
                                                The two walk down the stairs to the ground when a large crash erupts from the room as Harry walked out with a blood bag about a quarter full. Brandon points to Harry as Jessica looks to where Brandon is pointing to. She watches almost in terror as Harry jumps from the second story floor and land on the ground without any injury, almost like a perfect landing from the Olympics.
                                                "That is one of many tricks we can use, though you shouldn't do some of our tricks in front of the mortals." He says as Harry rejoins Brandon and Jessica, with Jessica backing up a foot. "And not all of us are as good looking as my old friend here." He says with a bit of a chuckle. Harry with a chuckle of his own, "Good one Sir."
                                                Brandon and Harry leads the way to a limo parked several yards away from the motel. Harry opens the door for Brandon as he enters the limo. He assists Jessica into the limo gently. Harry hands Brandon the blood bag then closes the door. Brandon hands Jessica the blood bag as another car door opens and closes. The limo starts and begins to move as Brandon takes another heavy puff off the cigar.
                                                "What's your Clan young lady?" Brandon asks looking at Jessica trying to start a conversation. "What?" Jessica replies                                                 at last, the first time in quite a while. Brandon gives a curious look, "Your Clan, your family to put it loosely." "I have no idea." Jessica says, "I just woke up and found the room covered with blood, the body in the tub, you and your... um... friend. Other than that I just found out that I'm a Vampire, that's all I know." "I see." Brandon says, " To make things short, Vampires are descended from the first, Caine. There are thirteen different kinds which are called Clans. Think of the Clans as a dysfunctional family." He takes another puff of the cigar. "Each Clan has their own traits, abilities, and weaknesses on top of what we already got from Caine."
