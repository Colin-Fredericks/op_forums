    Originally posted by AnnieHall View Post
    So it's my very first time as a storyteller and none of us has ever played Demon before.
    We're playing the Fear to Tread chronicle and we just finished the first story with the players putting an end to an Earthbound's plans.
    At the end of our last session, one of my players decided that it would be fun to whisper the name of the earthbound in the middle of his fucking living room with his wife and son sleeping upstairs! We ended the session with the cliffhanger but I would like to know what my options are as a storyteller.
    Well, let's see what invoking the Celestial Name does, right? According to p.171 of the core book a Demon takes notice when their Celestial Name is spoken. By making a Faith roll (difficulty 7) a demon can determine the following based on the successes:

    1: Receive a mental picture of whoever said the name.
    2: That, their immediate surroundings and whoever the demon addresses.
    3: That, and they can listen in on what the speaker (and just them) is saying for one turn. For every turn past that one it takes a Willpower (difficulty 8) roll.

    If the Earthbound in question knows the Celestial Name (or True Name, but that's pretty much a Rocks Fall scenario for that character) they can send a message to them via the rules on p.172.

                                                        The 48 hour limit only applies to the recently dead body the soul is about to be integrated into (if a corpse is even used in the first place, a comatose body whose soul has moved on should work aswell). The Wraith (Soul) in question could have been dead for centuries, it doesn't matter. However this does not make the lore super duper powerful. If you ressurect a vampire in this way he will not come back as a vampire and he will not have any of his disciplines. He will be a normal human/risen, perhaps with a long reaching memory, but that's about it.

                                                        Which takes me to the next point you need to decide upon as ST. Does Restore the dead bring back the Wraith as an actual living human or does it "merely" create a risen. If it creates a true mortal then he probably doesn't have any special powers, if it creates a risen it usually follows the rules for them and retains wraithly powers it had before being restored, but also still has to deal with his shadow and other drawbacks.
