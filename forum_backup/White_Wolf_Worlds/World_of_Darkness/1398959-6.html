    Indeed. The fact of the matter is that MtAs has had little in the way of pure human Hunters, partly because it's extremely difficult to tell a Mage apart from an ordinary human. With vampires, you can put on thermal googles and find vampires in a crowd pretty quickly. But how can you tell a Mage apart from a Sorcerer or just an ordinary person playing with vaguely occult nicknacks?

    And then there's the Technocracy which would be so out of the SI's league that they couldn't hope to take them on.

    What makes the SI terrifying for vampires is that vampires aren't as terrifying in the modern world. Modern technology and weapons have made things more even on the playing field.

    Homo sapiens. What an inventive, invincible species. It's only a few million years since they crawled up out of the mud and learned to walk. Puny, defenceless bipeds. They've survived flood, famine and plague. They've survived cosmic wars and holocausts. And now, here they are, out among the stars, waiting to begin a new life. Ready to outsit eternity. They're indomitable. Indomitable.

                    No disagreements here either... and yet ANOTHER reason why the Second Inquisition as a Mage antagonist is a non-starter; too many Mages would be on the same side as the SI against the vampires.

                    The Euthanatos are just one example. The conflict between the Order of Hermes and Clan Tremere was legendary enough to get its own Mage book. Vampirism is antithetical to the beliefs of just about every Tradition and only “we’ve got bigger fish to fry” keeps the Technocratic “We killed an Antideluvean” Union from making the Second Inquisition look like Amateur Hour.

                    Yes, the only way the SI work as a threat to the Traditions is as cat's-paws of the Technocracy ... in which case, why not just restart the Ascension War?
                    I don’t think they even work as a cat’s paw; the other reason why the Technocracy doesn’t go after the vampires is because they largely self-police and preserve the status quo. Sending a half-assed effort like the SI would just rile things up and make the status quo harder to maintain.

                    Similarly, sending the SI at Tradition Mages is basically a backfire waiting to happen. The sort of operations the SI conducts leveled at human religious groups is a sure fire way to win sympathy and interest for groups the Technocracy would rather see marginalized until they’re forgotten and fade to nothingness.
