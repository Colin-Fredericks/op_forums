    Originally posted by Saur Ops Specialist View Post

    First off, Wraith is only partially about personal horror. It's also about the hope of getting out of a miserable situation and stopping the voice of doubt in your head that sabotages everything productive, or at least non self-destructive, that you try to do.

    Secondly, my post was regarding your reading of Demon as "telling off" Werewolf. To reiterate the point, given that Demon was basically acting as a metaplot add-on to Vampire in terms of setting, as Vampire became more and more hardline Noddist out of character, it came pre-told off by all the games between Vampires and Demon. To place stock in Demon as an "answer" to Werewolf is to confuse who came first and who's "rebelling" against an "establishment", because at last I checked, Vampire has higher sales and tends to distort things its way in a manner that's not good for other games when it comes to the metaplot.
    I'm just saying I like D:TF because it has a nice contradiction of Werewolf cosmology where you have two authoratative sources with definitive answers which are contradictory.
