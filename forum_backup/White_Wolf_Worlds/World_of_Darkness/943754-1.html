                            To go to the original question; I generally handle the police and other similar agencies as a complication, rather than a regular presence. They're something you have to deal with when something goes wrong, something to avoid, or something that signals it's time to move on. It's very rare when they show up directly in the game, though there have been exceptions.

                            One notable exception was when the coterie was trying to find a hunter (not imbued hunter, the more normal kind) who had killed a couple ghouls thinking they were kindred. One of the players went to the crime scene of a recent such murder, posing as a reporter (and making judicious use of Dominate to make the ruse work) and started asking the lead detective some questions. He noticed the detective refused to make eye-contact and started getting curious. He subsequently became very interested in this detective and would make frequent visits with the detective.

                            He eventually learned the detective was well aware of the kindred presence in the city, and that he wasn't alone. Most of the police force either had figured out that there were real monsters out there or they had some degree of suspicion that not everything they saw could be explained. Nobody talked about it, but everyone knew. And they knew enough to not want to know more. So when a dead body was found mysteriously drained of all blood, maybe the coroner called it a heart-attack, or exposure to the elements, or a drug overdose. But nobody is going to risk their job by writing down "vampire attack". Maybe that really was a pack of wild dogs running through Starbucks, but nobody's going to report a werewolf sighting. Sometimes they have to get more creative when explaining away the strange things they see, but there's always some rational explanation in the report. Because the truth would just be too frightening.

                            The group grew to love this detective and the perspective he gave on their world.

                                    "Cops? The first thing you need to know about cops as a vampire is they're superstitious. Cops don't believe in vampires. They don't believe in werewolves, fairies (unless they're making a insult), or goat-suckers. They kind of believe in ghosts but even then, they shrug it off when people claim there's one in the hall."

                                    "However, cops are aware there's stuff which is WEIRD out there. They'll believe in all of the shit I just mentioned if it's right in front of them. They'll shoot at it. Call in backup. Run like Hell. Maybe even use the fire grenades no cop is supposed to have but plenty do. Then, afterward, they'll say it was tweakers, PCP, bath salts, body armor, or some voodoo gang with an accent on the racism. They'll even force themselves to forget--all without the use of Dominate"

                                    "That's how cops work and how they've worked since the Dark Ages. Why? Because if monsters are real, then it's their job to take care of them and no one wants that responsibility."
