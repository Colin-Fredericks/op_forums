              It depends on the situation. When I'm STing OWoD, which I do most of the time, I straight up forbid PC crossovers.                                       It depends on the player and their ideas, but generally I don't encourage it. I'll take it over with the players and see why they want what they want and if that works for me. I'm not saying never, I'm just asking why.

                                      I can understand the impulse to do it - playing a vampire isn't so special when everyone else is playing one too - but have also seen it turn into a solo game in the middle of a regular game. Being a bit odd without derailing the whole game is fine, but being totally against the whole feeling of the game isn't.

                                      If a player does it more often than not, I might put my foot down. This could lead to the player sitting a game out, but if they're that stuck on one idea I really don't want to deal with...

So if going this antagonistically it will mean a lot of new vampire characters being generated and your game not really moving along, but hey each to his own I guess.

                                                  EDIT:
                                                  Might as well say my own opinion on this.
                                                  I'll take it up with the player and explain the why my current game isn't favorable for such a character and try to come up with a character concept that is more suitable for the game as a whole with the player. No reason in my mind to go the above way and antagonize the player for wanting to play something else for a change. And sometimes I just might and up integrating the players original idea within my plot so that he get's to play what he wants and the rest what they want, were all there to have fun after all. One of my funnest, and not in a gonzo way, and most enjoyable horror/action games had I think 2 Vampires, 1 Changeling, 1 Ghoul and 1 Wraith (not Risen) and it features Mages as NPC's as well after certain incidents drew enough attention to warrant a Technocratic investigation and certain other incidents drew Hermetic attention. But the plot did involve ancient demon awakening, although everyone thought at first it was a Vampiric Elder, and the efforts to try and banish or destroy it for good which ended up involving casting it's reliquary, this was long before DtF though so no demons/earthbound as that book sees them, into the maw of Oblivion so everyone had their chance to be on spotlight.

                                                  But all said and done it depends on whether I have a plot ready and how the players idea could work with that plot or if I'm just improvising and sand boxing in which case almost anything goes as I have no set plot and are more free to come up with situations and excuses for the characters to get together, if I even need them to get together at all.
