    Other X20 Dark Ages

    05-16-2016, 07:05 PM
    So V20 Dark Ages pulled down nearly $300k during its Kickstarter run and is a Platinum seller on DriveThruRPG. Do you think the success it enjoyed means there's any chance of the other Dark Ages lines getting revived? DA: Fae is well-loved even by fans who loathe Changeling: the Dreaming, and I'm sure Werewolf and Mage fans wouldn't complain about new content; Inquisitor was a fun experiment that felt really different from Hunter: the Reckoning, while Wraith was cut short before ever getting to enjoy a Dark Ages book?

    Anyone else here hoping for this? I've always preferred the Dark Ages take on Vampire over the modern day, but it seems lile a shame to let them have all the fun.

    Remi. she/her. game designer.

              I mean, DA: Mage, Fae, and Inquisitor are mechanically distinct enough from their modern heirs as to basically be their own games that happen to share a background. I've always said that Vampire's weird Biblical mythology grounds it far better in the Dark Ages than today, and that the High/Low Clan divide feels much more organic (and gives players more options!) than the big Sects. I like Dark Ages as basically a separate universe from the World of Darkness, and would love to see it prosper on its own! Wraith has one of its Great Maelstroms during the Black Plague, which is conceivably during the Dark Ages time period, and the whole affair with the Fishers and other Heretics being a major faction alongside the Hierarchy is worthy of exploration.

              None of this takes away from other time periods, though; I'm still dying to see Victorian Mage. I just would really like some more love for the Dark Ages games, partly because they feel so much more coherent.

                                      Once again i express my hope that, in the upcoming 4th edition, nWW (new White Wolf) will have a unified world with one corebook to rule them all (similar to CofD). One could then create one supplement that ports Vampire, Mage, Werewolf etc. to the Dark Ages, with updated rules and descriptions of the various supernatural societies the existed back then.

                                      The only problems i see with this way is that you need three books to run a DA Vampire game (Corebook, Vampire and Dark Ages) and that one book might not be enough to explain the various differences between the modern day games and the Dark Ages ones (for example, diffrences between DA Foundation/Pillars and MD Arete/Spheres for Mage).

                                      One way to solve this could be to, for example, have one corebook and each splat gets one DA and one MD "core" supplement. That way you only need two books and you get a whole lot more room to explain the diffrences between the DA setting and the MD setting for each supplement. This does mean that there will be more DA books (at least one for each splat) but that also means more money for nWW.
