                    Originally posted by glamourweaver View Post
                    And that's ridiculous because Djinn and Fae have identical stories except for their relationship to monotheism.
                    I agree. There is a lot of overlap. Both faeries and djinn are invisible people that live alongside humanity who have wonderful powers, and can be beneficial or malevolent. It's the same concept, different name. If I ever ran a game where it would be important, I'd make most of the Djinn out of the Changeling Kiths (Sidhe for Marid, Trolls for Efreeti, Sluagh and Redcaps as Ghuls, athe others as Jann.)

                    Of course, that doesn't stop Spirits also being djinn any more than it stopped certain European faeries from being classed as Spirits in various Werewolf or Marge books. And plasmic creatures with that name would still dwell in the Tempest like the Angelics and Demonics do.

                    "Djinn" is a loose enough concept that it can cover a lot.

                    What I don't see the need for is to constantly reinvent the wheel every time someone expands the setting into a different part of the world. For that matter, I don't see the reason for different Kiths for the Nunnehi. Archetypes should be strong enough to allow their use in any culture with only some basic changes in "social structure" to better simulate that cultural traditions stories of the little people.
