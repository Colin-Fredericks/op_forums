        OK, so here are my ideas:

        Jungle War - a strategy game resembling Star Wars Rebellion. The story: Golgol Fangs First is dead. You are his succesor. You have to win the Amazon War. You have to send the packs to fight, send recruiters to the other septs to gain support for your efforts. And also battles could be implemented into the game, either RTS style, like in Total War series or tactical, like in Jagged Alliance.
        Red Carpet Fighting Club - inspired by Giovanni Chronicles part IV. A fighting game. Choose a fighter or create your own (standard today) and win the illegal tournament organized by Giovanni clan mobsters. Optional there could be bribery to ease the game or betting (like in The Con video game). And roaring 20's jazz in the background.
        EWW - wrestling video game inspired by Children of the Night book. Choose or create a wrestler (mortal or supernatural) and become the champion in Pentex owned wrestling federation. Also it could include managerial options.
        Corporate Wolf - inspired by events from my games. Business simulation game. The plot: finally Glass Walkers convinced other tribes that to defeat the Wyrm is to tackle Pentex. You are Michael Donelly, a Glass Walker Philodox investor to which entire garou nation money was given. Your goal: eliminate every Pentex company one by one. Choose your path by dominating the global market.
        Prince of the City - inspired by Sim City and The Sims. Simulation of a Camarilla Prince. I've read once about Simsville, a game never released but resembling this concept.
        The Rack - a nightclub management game. You are the owner of nightclub which is an Elysium and you have to take care for it. This means maintaining the supply, the Masquerade and sometimes Lupines attack.
        A caern builder. You found an abandoned/destroyed caern and you have to to make it functional again. You have to choose the participants of the rite from incoming characters (like in HOM&M) basing on their auspice and tribe to avoid conflicts (like in Jagged Alliance or Baldurs Gate), choose the best moment for a rite (so the caern could be effectively reopened; and there should be enough garou to perform the rite), defend it (RTT or turn based tactical game elements) and organise the sept to avoid conflicts (Sims like elements).
        An idea of a game resembling The Sims, for adults of course, for supernaturals (something like The Sims Nightlife). And the quests could be published as mods or DLCs. Of course it would also have multiplayer option, even if I don't like MMO because I saw too many people playing those games and not feeling the climate of those games. There could also be the possibility to export your characters to other WoD games (maybe using my other ideas) so you could be anyone (a businessman in Corporate Wolf, the commander in Jungle War).

        That's all for now. But I found some interesting concepts creators to by other, but I don't want to post them here because I don't want to "steal" someone's else ideas. I think, basing on number of games, that the easiest one to create would be Red Carpet Fighting Club and EWW, but I'd like to hear professional opinion of the programmer.

        And thanks for the explanation. I didn't know that.

        As for artists I still try to convince my girlfriend to help us by making concept arts (she has a master's degree from The Academy of Fine Arts in Łódź) but without any success . I also try to reach my friend, a heavy metal singer, to convince him to help us. Today I will also speak with my brother and ask him if he will find time to compose music for our project. If not I'm concerning other options. As for sound edition I could do it myself (example of my abilities I could send privately).

                                                        Sorry to hear that. I hope that he (or she, I can't make myself to call animals it) will be all right. I can imagine how you feel. I'm a dog/rat person and I have to say final goodbye to two of the latter last 3 months. Do you know who could did it?

                                                        Anyway the ideas. As I said Jungle War resembles Star Wars Rebellion. I imagine 3 mods:
                                                        - You play as Golgol Fangs First. You have to send recruiters to the other septs to gain support for your cause. They could be more or less effective. Their effectiveness will bring you warriors and you have to group them into wargs judging by p. ex. their tribes. As we all know a pair Get of Fenris and Black Fury will be less effective than Get of Fenris and Fianna. And then you have to send them to battle with Pentex and sometimes with Balams like in normal strategy games.
                                                        - The Succesor is mostly the same but you are Golgol's succesor. And you have to prove yourself worth. So your recruiters will be less effective (if Golgol lost his life how your chances look like) and morale of your troops will be lower.
                                                        -Career - you start as a Warg/pack leader and slowly you climb on the chain of command. First of all it is a turn based/real time tactical game (like in Jagged Alliance or SWAT respectively). You get sometimes new warriors, greenhorns and veterans alike (Golgol sends them to your unit). Ultimate goal: became Golgol's succesor (see above, but here you will be nominated by him so recruiters will have normal skills and your troops normal morale)
                                                        Of course you could also include mode that allows you to play as Pentex (less powers, better Technology but your troops are prone to go postal) or as Balams (weakest troops, but fastest movement - after all it's your jungle, tactical modifiers - and you know your yard very well, and don't forget Mokole as some kind of artillery). Of course you could also meet civilians, like Indians or archeologists, some vampires (there was mentioned about Setites and Gangrels in various books) maybe even some mages or mummies. And if you want we could also include all kinds of all other supernaturals).

                                                        Corporate Wolf - as I said purely management game like Capitalism. The plot: Glass Walkers convinced other tribes that to defeat the Wyrm is to tackle Pentex. They gave all their resources to you. And you have to take over Pentex by buying their stocks. You create your character (simple mechanics, like in Bloodlines, Charisma, Manipulation, Intelligence and Wits as stats, Bureaucracy, Computers, Expression, Investigation and Subterfuge as skills). As I let my players create a superheroes characters I would let the player create a character with max skills I've mentioned above. On the other hand one crisis could drown character like that thus ending the game. And now: Charisma and Expression are used to convince other Garou to give you some more money if you're in trouble. Manipulation and Subterfuge to convince independent ivestors (or Syndicate) to sell you their shares. Wits and Investigation or Computers to find some dirt on Pentex companies (thus lowering the price of shares) and Intelligence and Bureaucracy to predict the prices. Of course you have to deal with many competitors also (Syndicate, Ventrue, rich mortals). If you're succesful you could became a Pentex CEO (if we put there some kind of Dark/Light Side mechanics like in Jedi Knight you could be either a saviour or bringer of the Apocalypse in the end), if your judgement will be constantly wrong the tribes could end their support and take their money away (and that could hurt you more than bad investment)

                                                        Red Carpet Fighting Club - either a fighting game or sports manager. In fighting version you choose (or create) a fighter to compete in a Giovanni sponsored underground fighting tournament described in Giovanni Chronicles part IV Nuova Malattia. It could be either text based (like in first two parts of New Star Soccer, you get the description of your situations like the wall is 2 ft behind you, 5 ft on the right and 27 ft on the left. Your opponent is throwing a left hook on your Head. What do you do? And then you choose from some options like dodge, run on left/right/back/forward, punch, kick etc.) and the AI calculate your chances for succes basing on your and your opponents stats. Or it could be normal fighting game but like Pit Fighter coin up rather than Mortal Combat (Giovanni are not stupid so they won't allow other supernaturals to participate). As for sports management game you are a Giovanni vampire. You have some money into your pocket and you have to rent the arena (it could be everywhere, from trailer park to warehouse to nightclub) find some fighters, find some security (it is neccesary, this tournament is illegal so police raid could happen, not to mention the Prohibition and you sell booze there, right) and set ticket prices. And watch as your resources increases .

                                                        EWW - wrestling video game, this time with supernaturals. Extreme Wrestling Warfare was mentioned in Children of the Night. You choose or create a wrestler (either supernatural or mortal, mortals don't have superpowers but more combat maneuvers and I think that the greatest mortal fighters are able to defeat even Garou in Crinos form) and fight in a hardcore tournament. I have some ideas for stipulations, p. ex. Live Wire where ropes are live wires not strong enough to kill but to seriously wound. In normal wrestling it could dissalowed saving one from counting by placing ones leg on the ropes. Mechanics like described above, either text based or normal fighting. It could be also stable manager. This time you are a pack leader who, with his packmates, wants to win the belt and then destroy entire federation. You set your strategy, choose the fighters and hope that they won't be killed (as I said it is a hard core wrestling federation, Wyrm version). Or it could be sports manager where you are the CEO of EWW and you have to find fighters, sponsors, negotiate contracts, evolve storylines etc. Just like in TEW video games.

                                                        And a caern builder (no title for now). Something between Sim City, Jagged Alliance and The Sims. You choose an abandoned caern (there are plenty of them described in various books). You have to complete the proper number of Garou (random generated, like in HOMAM) to open the caern (possible conflicts between tribes), defend the place from Wyrm, mages and all other competitors (tactical game). And, if you want, continue the game after the caern was opened as a sept leader (like in The Sims, possible conflicts as above).

                                                        These are my ideas. Unfortunetaly my friend who was a genius with numbers and could help us with mechanics died 10 years ago so please ask your friends which one of those will be possible to create and let me know. I can help with my imagination creating NPCs and places and of course with music.
