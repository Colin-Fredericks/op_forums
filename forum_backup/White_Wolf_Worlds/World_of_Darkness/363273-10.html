                                            Originally posted by PaladinDemo View Post

                                            They are the only one I can see moving West, cause they are not bound to a city as much as every clan is. But I'm taking in account all the clans, not just Gangrels.
                                            You can go a lot of places by steamboat. Vampires crossed the Atlantic Ocean to settle in the New World. I'm betting that there are several creative tried-and-true situations to deal with travel that don't involve Earth Meld. There are definitely records of wagon trains that actually carried coffins filled with dead folks with them (which freaked out the tribes they encountered). It wouldn't be too hard to put a concealed "smuggler" space in a Condestoga wagon. And, once a vampire arrives at a destination, they're in pretty much the same situation they would be anywhere else that was relatively thinly populated (which was a lot of places vampires, including but not limited to Gangrel, did just fine in back in the day...)

                                            I didn't find it too difficult to work up a fast and dirty version of Mage for the Wild West when the characters in one game ended up going back in time. They got a couple of handouts describing the differences and ended up stumbling across a lot of others I planned out and extrapolated out on the fly. I did a similar work up for my own purposes of the vampire, werewolf and wraith populations of the city.
