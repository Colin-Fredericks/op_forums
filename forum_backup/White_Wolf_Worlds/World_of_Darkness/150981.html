                                                  Depends on the Earthbound. Some are phenomenally weak, so much so that the average vampire elder can fuck them up pretty hard. Others are so powerful you might as well be contending with a Tzimisce Methuselah or some other entity in-splat from VtM that can fuck you up hard core. Any Demon that can flesh-craft a Vampire is probably in the upper category, Overlord at the very least. Seeing as Demons are immune to most of the standard mind-rapey and body-fucking-up abilities (as they are not alive or mortal). Earthbound can't commit diablerie on a Vampire, thankfully, as you kind of need a Beast to do that. Now, on the plus side... it can't possess you longterm. Any Demon that tries to possess a vampire usually screams in Torment and is immediately hurled into the Abyss, don't pass Go, don't collect $200. However, Earthbound are masters of commanding and directing Torment to their own advantage (since they kind of had to or be utterly fucked themselves). Thus a particularly powerful one might be able to possess you for a time, but unless you are REALLY low generation (or have high Fortitude I'd wager) they would quickly drive you into Torpor from the consumptive and body-warping effects of their Torment. Bad news, even if the possession ends and you get your body back... you probably will end up with tentacles or as a bilateral hermaphrodite (or worse).

                                                  Now, as to the means and ways of getting rid of the bastard... there are quite a few. As mentioned, any Lasombra Abyss Mystic with Obtenebration six or higher can probably banish the fucker with a simple ritual. Provided, you know, the Demon doesn't fuck it up somehow (which it might depending on its Lores, or Arcanum... depending on the version of Demon that is being run). Now, there are other options. Powerful Baali, usually sixth generation or lower, can make that Demon their bitch given time. Hell, any Baali with Daimoinon 5 can fuck that Demon up something fierce if they get a lucky roll. "Hey Demon, meet Severity 5 Curse, have fun in the Abyss for... ever?" Of course its also possible a powerful Koldun, Thaumaturge, or a few Methuselahs of other Clans (Valeren Eight or Obtenebration Nine can make that Demon cry for mercy pretty quickly). However, that still means you need to convince Rayzeel or Montano to be your friend. Unlikely even in the best prospects. Of course, any of the Antediluvians could destroy or banish an Earthbound laughably easily (so long as it isn't an Duke or Archduke).

                                                  Beyond the stuff common in Vampire... a group of Garou Theurges with the right Rites could do it. As could some of the crazier things like Firstborn Fae who have achieved mastery of their respective Dominions, or some of the more lucid Mummies. Than there are Mages. Sweet, beautiful, nearly omnipotent (given preparation) Mages. Mages might not be able to command Demons with Spirit or Mind (unless they are very knowledgeable Masters or Arch-Masters), but Prime is a Demon's worse nightmare. Not only can Prime fuck up the Demon's attempt to use Lores, it can also unweave the enchantment on the ring. The Masters of Prime can even permanently destroy Demons, this is a ritual known among the Celestial Chorus (according to rumor). Arch-Masters of Prime are not that impressed by Demons, and Oracles would find anything less than the mightiest of the Earthbound to be jokes. An Exemplar of Prime... wouldn't even notice the Demon, and if it did direct it's (Exemplars are no longer "he's or she's") ire at the Demon it would likely be consumed wholly in celestial fire. The only problem is that finding Mages is hard. Finding Master Mages is damn near impossible. Finding Arch-Mages is a quest worthy of the trials of Hercules.
