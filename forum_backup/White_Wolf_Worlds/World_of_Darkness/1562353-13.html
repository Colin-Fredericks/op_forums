                                On the jumps, I think they could do even better than that. I always found them too small even when given as an option. What I would change, though, is that I'd make the Generation Jump be linked to age rather than just Generation. It's an old lore element that Blood Potency didn't capture well, but should still be there.

                                Divide the age of the victim by the age of the diablerist, round down always. The result is how many steps the act can reduce Generation at once. The a chart of minimum difference: 100 years for -2, 200 for -3, 400 for -4, 800 for -5, 1.600 for -6, 3.200 for -7, 6.400 for -8. That way a Neonate can drop from 13th Gen to 5th if they somehow Diablerize one of the oldest Methuselah around, while Elders doing it on each other get more moderate benefits even if they have a big difference. A 300 years vampire would have to Diablerize a victim at least 600 years old to improve more than one step.

                                But I would remove any XP gain or other more permanent benefit or penalty from Diablerie. I came to greatly appreciate in previous editions the idea that this is about closeness to Caine and potential, but not realization. I'm a staunch defender of the idea that a starting vampire is made with the same dots regardless of their Generation, and only age and actual experience will let them realize their potential.

                                So, Caine himself embraced you 5 years ago? Congratulations, you're 2nd Generation and has 3 dots in Clan Disciplines to choose. Maybe 4 or 5 if you really go all in for it. You may have a greater Blood Pool or something, and Surging Blood will give you more temporary dots, but that's about it, don't feel too cocky, you're just a Neonate like any other. Likewise, Diablerie isn't a path to power, just to Generation. You may get to treat one of your victim's Disciplines as in-clan, but that's the most I'd allow.

                                What I'd reintroduce with clearer rules is some temporary benefits. Maybe a bonus to use the Disciplines and skills of the victim for a couple days. You could get from drinking Elder Blood, but have a slightly greater benefit and duration with Diablerie. Even then, without paying XP to keep the benefits, they're lost after a while.

                                Those temporary benefits could exist in some level to Diablerie of weaker Vampires. We do have a problem in which the game has a theme of diablerist elders and Diablerie causing quite a strong addiction without any effect. I think that giving something for it would deliver this theme better.
