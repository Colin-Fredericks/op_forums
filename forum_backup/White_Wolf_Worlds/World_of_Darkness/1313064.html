    The Nine Realms - A metaphysical expansion of the Umbra to match the Spheres

    05-19-2019, 12:51 AM
    The Nine Realms

    The concept of the Nine Realms is based on the idea that each Sphere gives access to a particular Realm, similar to how Correspondence gives access to the Digital Web, and Mind gives access to the Astral Realm. Each Realm connects to the others where they share similar conceptual space.

    I like the idea of this for several reasons. First, it provides a framework that for why the Astral Realm, the Digital Web, The Dreaming, the Underworld, and the Umbra all work similarly, but have their own unique rules. I also like how's it expands Magick to allow each Sphere to have its own world.

    Through these metaphors, we can provide personal journeys into the Biosphere when someone uses Life Magick to sense the world around them. Time becomes walking between the Shard Realms, looking into and reaching the past without affecting the present, or seeing the future of some other Earth.

    This is very much a work-in-progress. Some of the ideas presented here are more fleshed out than others. I look forward to your input and ideas on how to expand this alternate cosmology to the world of Mage, and the story ideas that it inspires for you.

    Prime



    Realm: The Dreaming
    Concept: Creation and Stories
    Conscious Experience: Identity
    Quintessence Flavor: Glamor

    Glamor and Banality map extremely well to Quintessence and Paradox. Paradox spirits become Ephemera warped by Banality. Paradox Realms are essentially Nightmare Realms.

    Many of the creatures of legend from Gods and Monsters can then be looked at as Fae, although few of them would claim that name.

    Structure

    Material Realm / Chimerical Reality —> Near Dreaming —> Far Dreaming —> Deep Dreaming

    Connected Realms

    Material Realm

    The _Material Realm_ is connected to the Dreaming through _Chimerical Reality_. Unlike most of the other Realms, The Dreaming does not have a discrete Gauntlet, but instead has a permiable barrier based on the incompatibility of the paradigms of the person trying to create a Magickal effect, with the consensus of the Reality Zone.

    Fae magic has a very creative and spontaneous nature, and so Reality Zones that are based on structure and practice would have a high banality for them. Mages have a wider array of paradigms, which allows them to interact with the raw Quintessence of the realm differently.

    Connecting to the Near Dreaming requires the use of a trod, which is a pathway to the worlds of the fae.

    Correspondence

    Given how Dreams are the simulations of the mind, it is possible that one could reach the Digital Web through them. It would also be possible to connect through a mathematically-based dream Realm.

    Time

    The Shard Realms are closely linked to The Dreaming. After all, how many stories start with “What would the world be like if _this_ were different?”

    Mind

    Again, Dream Realms provide the clearest connection to the Astral Realm of thoughts and concepts. The Deep and Far Dreaming are similar concepts as well, so would provide connections.

    Spirit

    Maya and the Supernal Realms are similar conceptual realms, and so the Silver Paths should be able to link to these places.

    Forces

    The Deep and Far Dreaming are places of eternal flux and roiling energy. This shares a lot of similarities with the Elemental Planes. Alternately, passing through the Material Realm is the easiest form of passage.

    Life

    As a Pattern Realm, the best way to reach here is through the Material Realm.

    Matter

    Knocker strongholds my have a connection to the the realm of Matter directly, but as a Pattern Realm, it is easiest to go through the Material Realm.

    Entropy

    The Roads of Balor lead to the Underworld.

    Spirit

    Time



    Realm: Shard Realms
    Concept: Possibility and causality
    Conscious Experience: Continuity
    Quintessence Flavor: maybe Sekhem?

    Given how the the Undying are sort of unmoored from time, and how their Sekhem is related to their connection to their past, this is a possible connection. I’m not super familiar with the system or the lore other than what is on the Wiki, so I may be completely off-base here.

    Description

    Shard Realms are described in various the World of Darkness books. They are essentially alternate Material Realms, where something is different. To use this extension, we take the concept of time from a one-dimensional concept leading from the past to the future to a three-dimensional construct.

    Many works of fiction use the “alternate timelines” model, and that is what we will use to extend the realms of Time. We’ll extrapolate from there, mapping them to our three familiar dimensions of length, width, and height.

    Our regular world only travels in one direction along the length, moving from the past into the future. To get the width, we consider the other possibilities for each moment. Do you turn left or right? Once those choices are made, the worlds diverge, becoming more and more distant as we travel along the length.

    Height is a little more esoteric, but we can think of the height component of time to be different initial conditions of the universe. Like how different would the universe be if π were equal to 3, or space were non-Euclidean? By traveling through this third dimension of time, we can get to some really strange places (much like the Deep Umbra).

    Forces



    Realm: Elemental Planes
    Concept: Elemental
    Conscious Experience: Senses
    Quintessence Flavor: Vanilla

    The Elemental Planes represent realities dominated by energies. Closest to Earth, this manifests as lights being brighter, fires burning hotter, lightning splitting the sky. As one travels deeper into the Elemental planes, matter is replaced by energy. Chaos and motion abounds, and without protection, a mortal body would be unable to adapt to the chaotic forces assaulting them.

    Like the other Pattern Realms, one is able to travel not just in the three spacial dimensions, but also in scale. By expanding one’s focus, one is able to travel the cosmic threads of gravity and see the scale of the galaxy, or scale down to the internal of atoms and experience the realms of the nuclear forces.

    The denizens of this Realm are truly alien, and may not even acknowledge mortal travelers. This does not mean that one is safe. Quite the contrary. Elementals may be attracted to sources of energy to subsume. Large dragons, like rivers of light, roam the reaches of the interstellar space. Swirling vortices of color and sparks float though what would otherwise be a solid mass of roaring fire stretching to infinity.

    Connected Realms

    Material Realm

    Close to Earth, the Elemental Planes emphasize the dynamic parts of the World. Gravity is strange. Light glimmers, and fires burn bright.

    Life



    Realm: Biosphere
    Concept: Living bodies
    Conscious Experience: Embodiment
    Quintessence Flavor: Blood

    By reaching inward, a mage can connect to her own Innerspace, or to those of others. It is possible to scale down to travel inside of a creature, but a weary Life mage knows to be cautious of the body’s natural defenses, as well as parasites, bacteria, and viruses.

    Once this metaphysical connection is made, a Life mage can travel out to the Biosphere. At this level, ecosystems are represented as shifting masses of the creatures and plants that exist within it.

    I had the thought that the connections to the other Realms could be connected to the different chakras, (by combining the connection to the Static Realms). While I think that this could work pretty well, I worry that I simply do not understand their real meaning, and I would rather get some input on that idea first.

    Matter



    Realm: Crystal Realms
    Concept: Structures and Form
    Conscious Experience: Touch
    Quintessence Flavor: Vanilla

    The Crystal Realms, like the other Pattern Realms, are more recognizable than their more esoteric counterparts. Close to the Material Realm, objects seem to shine. Water sparkles, metal gleams, and even dirt takes on a reflective quality. Venturing outwards, one finds worlds dominated by a particular material, be that silicone with glass and quartz, or iron. More complicated materials have their place within the Crystal Realms.

    Farther from Earth, one can travel inward by reducing scale, where crystal structures can be seen directly. Molecules become tangible things. Elementals of electrons move about, changing the structures around them.

    On the higher levels, planets, stars, galaxies, and larger.

                  Spacetime, as described in General Relativity, is 4-dimensional, with 3 spacial dimensions, and one dimension of time.

                  What I'm referring to as timespace is 3-dimensional is just 3 time dimensions.
