                I've got a series that shares some themes and ideas with Orpheus and WtO to a lesser extent. I've actually mentioned it before in the GtSE section of the old WW forums, the Blackwell series from indie developer Wadjet Eye Games. It's a point-and-click adventure game in the vein of the old classics, Day of the Tentacle, the Dig, Sam and Max, Monkey Island. Even keeping the older early 90's era graphics so you don't need a powerful PC to run it.

                The series revolves around Rosa Blackwell, who after her aunt's death discovers the family legacy, a ghost named Joey Malone and a duty to find wayward, restless ghosts and help them accept their deaths and move on. As of this moment there are four games out and available on Steam while the fifth and final game, The Blackwell Epiphany is coming out at the end of this month. Got a great trailer for it too.

                                                The Darkness 1 (and 2 to a lesser extent): Jackie Estacado is the embodiement of a Lasombra, between the shadowy minions, abyssal powers, and overall attitude. Great games with a noir atmosphere and a very bittersweet story. I also highly recommend the comic series of the same name alongside Witchblade, all great reads even if you haven't given them a go before.
                                                Nosferatu: The Wrath of Malachi: A young man turned slipshod novice hunter when he finds him and his family in the lair of an Eastern European Tzimisce lord that plans on turning them into his minions. (Underrated gem here that never got that much publicity).
                                                Fatal Frame series: Japanese Wraith-like horror.
                                                Planescape: Torment: Good lord, just look it up. Fantastic storytelling in a very dated game, but it captures the twisted nature of WoD pretty damn well.
                                                Deus Ex series (not so much 2): Conspiracies, technological advancement, cybernetic-augmentations and top-tier storytelling.
                                                Bloodrayne (1, 2, and Betrayal): Hot, scantily-clad femme fatale vampire sexbomb as a protagonist alongside cheesy and campy writing and very violent hack n slashing. The movies are so damn terrible it hurts though, it's like they asked a teenager to do it for a school project, I wouldn't even say half-assed, it is like no ass at all. But the games are good, but full of fanservice.
                                                Call of Cthulu: Dark Corners of the Earth: For the love of all that is unholy, this great horror game is quintessentially Lovecraftian, play it if you like horror. Hell, play it even if you don't like horror.

                                                While it may not be a game: Hellblazer is also a great read for anyone looking for a good dark comic fix, featuring Constantine, the JL: Dark member that had his own movie with Keanu Reeves. DC recently picked up the rights to it and is making their own new run of Constantine soon. Pretty great stuff worthy of mentioning.
