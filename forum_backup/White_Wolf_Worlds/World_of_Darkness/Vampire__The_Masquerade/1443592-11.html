    V5 thinbloods are just an episode of absolute quackery.

    First off, "Alchemy". Magic in the World of Darkness has always strayed far from DnD's idea "looking through a spell list". Rather, it looks at real world esoteric practices; the Tremere are a Hermetic order, the Settites have a Gnostic belief system, the Assamites draw power from the stars and spirits just as real world mystics did. Alchemy thus isn't just a bog-standard western fantasy way of giving you +3 strength or 8 minutes of fire protection, it's a part-science part mystic approach to transform you (lead) into something greater (gold). It's an exercise of understanding the universe and self betterment. TBA is not only a lazy bog-standard potion brewing exercise, but it's an affront to the larger WoD practice of treating everything like it belongs in a cohesive, esoteric understanding of the world; the embrace is alchemy, with Caine being divine, purer gold and weaker bloods more earthly like lead; the thinbloods shouldn't be different because they're half and half, they should just be weaker. Even if you don't bother with all this esotericism, it's just unfitting that, say, mortals have disciplines up to 0, ghouls have disciplines of... let's say 1 for simplicity, 13th gen vampires have disciplines up to 5 and instead of being right in the middle and having disciplines up to 3 thinbloods instead... have something entirely different

    This esoteric outlook also goes into a lot of other things, for example having abilities beyond the fifth dot is tied to this idea that we can throw off our chains and unlock our potential through enlightenment and self betterment and alchemy; reality is a prison, that kind of thing. V5 is missing that, and not only is it disempowering, it invalidates the beliefs of mortal philosophies and the vampires who follow them: The Tremere, Settites, Ravnos and even V5's own Church of Caine all suffer heavily as a result.

        Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
        V20 doesn't too my knowledge have rules for Dhampirs,can you point them out?
        You can find the V20 versions in Beckett's Jyhad Diary "The Blood Matures", p. 144

        The main things of note with the update are that, unlike Revenants, they still get a blood pool of 10, they regenerate limbs with a Willpower test (diff 8) and blood (vs. a Stamina test, a point of willpower AND blood) and they not only soak lethal, they also treat all aggravated damage they take as lethal (which is why I refer to them as Timex's... they can take a licking and keep on ticking) meaning they can heal from fire, werewolf claws, etc. for a single blood point per health level at a rate of one per turn (until they're out of blood).

        On the downside it also clarifies that while they can be blood bound by drinking vitae, they never become ghouls (from BJD... "The Dhampir’s vampiric heritage makes it impossible for her to become a changeling, demon, mage, mummy, werewolf, or their related kin. She can be blood bound to a vampire, though she does not become a ghoul.")

        Thus, they have a hard discipline cap of 1 (i.e. no hooking up with a low gen vampire to get those higher dots like ghouls and revenants can), which is why I don't find their "soak like a 7th Gen Ventrue" and "heal more efficiently than damned near anything" to be particularly unbalanced (if anything it makes them feel a bit Slayer-ish in that they can get the crap kicked out of them and still bounce back... and get even more Slayer-ish if you give them the Fist of God merit) and also explains why they get fewer Freebies than Ghouls or Revenants.

One of them who was starving drank blood contaminated with something else and, in addition to picking up a resonance-linked discipline, also picked up a trait based on the resonance of the contamination... then they started experimenting and they call it Alchemy because most of these experiments are millennial's whose only experience with alchemy is actually from pop-culture fantasy.

                                    As a note about what I like most about the V5 rules updates if anyone cares:

                                    What I really like

                                    * The generation tied to what sort of blood you can drink.
                                    * Blood Potency tied to age.
                                    * Moving Humanity away from one single inflexible code of ethics to a more flexible series of personal convictions.
                                    * Thin Bloods having permanent Blush of Life.
                                    * Blush of Life just requiring one single rouse check versus a bunch of blood spent.
                                    * The changes to the Malkavian, Gangrel, and Ventrue weaknesses.
                                    * The updated Tzimisce rules.
                                    * Merging Serpentis, Vicisstitude, and Protean.
                                    * Combining the Abyss of Wraith and Lasombra.
                                    * Getting rid of Thaumaturgy Paths to be replaced with Rituals. Thaumathurgy Paths felt like they didn't want to make new Disciplines.
                                    * Loresheets allowing player characters to get much more hardcore into the Lore.
                                    * Being able to choose your own Generation as related to prey style and power level
                                    * Stains as a replacement for just automatic Humanity Loss.
                                    * Having a human ties or loved one to guard against Humanity loss. I had similar "True Love" rules that helped vampires guard against the Beast.
                                    * The revised rules so you can't blood bond someone without them knowing by spiking their drinks.
                                    * Ditto ghouldom.
                                    * The fact that Dominating mortals doesn't cost Blood and is automatic for the most part.
                                    * I like that Hunger never goes away unless you kill someone.
                                    * I like that Hunger is more erratic and unpredictable versus resource management.
                                    * Chronicle Tenet morality allows more flexibility whether you want to run a pacifist angst-ridden weepy game versus an action game with hundreds of bodies.

                                    What I'm okay with

                                    * More flexibility about Nosferatu appearance is a good thing.
                                    * Loresheets as a means of tying Bloodlines to existing Clans indicates we may be getting a lot more variety on these without removing them from Clans entirely. After all, a lot of Bloodlines feel like they should be part of Clans but just "off center."
                                    * Predator Types incentivize roleplaying out your feeding style.

                                    What I don't like

                                    * I hate the Lasombra technology weakness.
                                    * I am not a big fan of giving vampires literal mental compulsions related to their Clan's stereotype.
                                    * The limit of 5 dots in Attributes and Disciplines is lore breaking and makes no goddamned sense.
                                    * The Amalgam purchase limitations are needlessly limiting and make no sense as well.
                                    * Healing ghouls should be allowed to be done by giving them blood.
                                    * Thin Bloods should be able to have at least one dot in Disciplines and not just as a Merit.
                                    * Thin Blooded Alchemy should not be their only thing.
                                    * I think Touchstones don't need to be linked with Convictions whatsoever.
                                    * I don't get the appeal of Resonances and think its a weird thing to introduce this late in the game.
