                                    Originally posted by Rhywbeth View Post
                                    Why, exactly, is V5 so hellbent on everyone playing it being Anarch or Camarilla?
                                    Mainly because V5 is fueled by a nostalgia for VtM 1st edition. A lot of what got set in stone in later editions was open-ended in 1st edition. The Sabbat were mysterious adversaries, the Camarilla/Anarch divide was the core vampire conflict, the Masquerade was a serious issue, Lupines weren't Garou yet, etc. The Independents were off-script side players. Even the basic Camarilla structure wasn't established as the default worldwide, merely the default for Chicago.

                                    The Sabbat was meant to be the road you shouldn't go down, a point of no return. Only later work was done to make them playable and not everyone thought those additions to the game were improvements. The Paths, for example, were always a point of controversy as arguably detracting from the core theme.

                                    Obviously at this point, though, I imagine it would be easier to make the Sabbat playable by toning down the monstrous fascism rather than just eliminating them as player options entirely.

                                    But that being said, if your core vision of the game is set by the template established in the 1st edition corebook + Chicago By Night + The Hunters Hunted and basically nothing else, the playable Sabbat aren't really compatible with that vision, so it's understandable they'd commit to keeping them an antagonist group.
