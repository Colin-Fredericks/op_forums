                            The Assamite and Ravnos corrections are basically an attempt to "fix" what was an orientalist depiction of them and sort of running head first into a whole other set of problems because of the same basic assumptions.

                            1] Assamite Revised is about turning the Assamites into basically their own mini-Camarilla. The Vizier being the Toreador, the Warriors being the Original RecipeTM Assamites, and the Sorcerers being the Tremere. This is better than them being a one-dimensional bunch of terrorists and based on the Alamut novel that inspired Assassins Creed.

                            2] HOWEVER, the problem is that basically is attempting to patch over the hole by missing the problem is that the entirety of the Middle East and its cultures being the Assamites is, itself, an inherently stupid idea.

                            3] This is basically the same issue with the Ravnos as instead of being "thieving trickster illusionist" Clan, they have since become "India clan." Which means that it's even more stereotypical in attempting to cultivate the entire oldest culture in the world and its many-many branches.

                            4] Ebony Kingdoms gets this right for the most part with the idea that if you have ALL of the Clans there, then there's the more appropriate diversity you'd expect for it. That's because Ventrue aren't "Western European Kindred Clan", theyr'e nobility clan, which can be anywhere.

                            My .02.
