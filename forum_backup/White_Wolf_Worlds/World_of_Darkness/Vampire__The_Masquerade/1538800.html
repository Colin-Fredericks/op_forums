    Quick Question does feeding cause unsoakable levels of lethal damage?

    07-17-2024, 08:09 PM
    So the feed maneuver draws three blood points from a target, and each blood point is considered a lethal level of damage. With that in mind you can grapple and feed in combat, and there are other creatures in the wod who can soak lethal damage like lupines, and life mages. So, if you feed from one of those targets does the blood taken out of their blood pool cause them levels of lethal damage? And if so are they able to soak that damage to keep going? Or does it work that when the ten points for mages, and the 20 for lupines are drained they instantly die? Thank you.

          In VDA20 that mechanic gets pretty out of hand if you use Serpentis 2, unless the ST houserules it.

          With Serpentis 2 you have an additional tongue attack which can both inflict damage and drink 3 blood points per turn. Unlike in VtM20, it's not specified that drinking is on the turn after having hit with the tongue.
          You also get to drink 5 BP per turn instead of 3 with your enlarged maws.
          Nowhere is it written that using one prevents you from using the other, so you can declare multiple actions, and attack with both. The tongue will do some damage (probably), and you will drink 8 BP (plus the Kiss, plus the automatic grapple, if the victim survives).

          Humans have 10 BP total. If you take out 2+ health levels with the tongue, they drop to 8- (characters have 1 less BP per health level lost). Drink 8 and they're dead.

          The real issue is that this works equally well on other creatures. The rules on potent blood (lower gen vamps, werewolves) are that they still house 10 BP health wise, but it counts as more for effectiveness once in the drinking vampire's body. So effectively you can still drain to death a damaged (with the tongue) werewolf in one turn. Vamps don't go so easily because they don't lose BP because of wounds, but you can still drain them in 2 turns instead of 1, assuming they have more than 80% of their blood pool full.

          It's pretty wild and STs should consider whether they're ok with this or they want to make some changes.

              Unsoakable levels of lethal damage. Any living beings need blood to live. Without any blood, you die. But in truth, death occurs way before the last drop is drained.

              Werewolves will just keep regenerating that damage however, even while they are being drained.

              Mages with Life can create effects that allow them to survive without blood at all; in this case, they wouldn't really take any damage at all from losing blood

                                          Here's how i play it as ST:

                                          A vampire drinking blood from someone or something (werewolf) inflict unsoakable lethal damage (except for vampires, whose blood reserve is simply drained), because i understand werewolves's skin, muscles and bones can take bullets, claws, explosion, fire and so on but it's hard for me to imagine their blood refusing to flow down the throat of a monster. They will heal quickly anyway. For mortal there's of course no fast healing and mage can "cheat" with reality with vulgar magic to produce more blood or survive without blood with the Life Sphere.

                                          If mortals lose their last health box they're dead, end of story, but the vampire can suck the last blood point in their corpse to get their 10BP. If a werewolf lose his last health box he roll Rage to survivre as usual, heal 1 health per success, enter frenzy, is immune to the kiss and begin the slaughter. Mage are dead except if they used their magic before loosing their last health box to prevent their death.

                                          About the kiss, mortal can't resist except if the ST consider the character to have something special (a strong will, a trauma triggered by the fear or anger... Anything the ST consider fitting to his story as long as it's very rare). Vampires AND werewolves can resist the effect with Willpower roll or Rage if higher than Willpower for werewolf, but in that case they risk frenzying. Mages have to use their magic to protect their mind from the effect or at least get some resistance allowing them to roll their willpower. Beyond that they fall for the kiss.

                                          In fact, while I'm thinking about it, I can't remember if the book said if your can roll each turn to resist the kiss or if you have only one try... Gotta check for the next time

                                          For Werewolves blood gain, they DO NOT have 20BP in their body, each BP worth double their value, if you drink 2BP he take 2 unsoakable lethal damage and you gain 4BP total (remember that drinking werewolf blood increase difficulty for the vampire frenzy test, a valuable but dangerous meal)

                                              I run it almost exactly like lethal damage; that is that one Blood Point heals on a healthy human in one day, two BP recover in about half a week, 3 BP takes about a week to recover and can be a hazard for older or weaker humans to recover without treatment. 4 BP may require medical intervention even for healthy humans or they may have strokes, neuropathy or brain or cardiovascular damage. Too far beyond that and you better call them a paramedic and start giving them warm Gatorade.

                                              That being said, for a combat scene I kindof only count that first point against their bruised level. Beyond that it seems weird to count blood loss less than hypovolemic shock as damage in a vampire game. Hunters should be more fragile after being fed on a bit, but shouldn't really get stunned/killed by it until quite late in a combat, I think.
