          Speaking of Lodin's lineage, in past games i have not shied away from tweaking canon NPCs in a number of ways for the sake of intrigue, more elaborate character dynamics and so on.
          And for a particular relevant example...


          Chicago's Ventrue: For matters of taste, brewing more factions and surprising my players (already familiar with the books) back then, i did some major quartering and tweaking of Lodin's overblown brood, resulting in the following.

          - Frank Gaugham was embraced by Horatio Ballard, in the interest of creating a foil to Capone.

          - Alan Sovereign was embraced by Capone, for basically the same reason.

          - Pham Hong, Kevin Jackson and Joseph Peterson are the childer of Edward Neally, made to help keeping a leash in the Masquerade – and his "brothers" Ballard & Capone – with varying results. It also means the events of Blood Bond can cause considerably larger waves in the city's status quo, since there's not insignificant chance of them being bound directly to Neally, not Lodin.

          - Tommy Hinds is actually the childe of Edgar Drummond, that still has him in a subtle but effective blood bond. Jacob Schumpeter too, but the apple didn't drop too far from the tree in his case.

          - Bobby Weatherbottom stayed the childe of Lodin, mainly because his embrace was partly the reason Anabelle would end up sparking the council wars and i decided to keep that, along with making him a curious foil to Lorraine.

          - Brennon Thornhill was not even Ventrue in my games, but Toreador and a childe of Modius, giving him a pair of very well-placed eyes into the city's intrigue.


          Extra - Portia (Helena) didn't really try (to fail at) playing neonate, presenting herself instead as an ancilla childe of Eletria and according to (false) rumors, Brennon's sire.


          Ok, let me show things play out in my alt-Chicago a little more clearly.


          Lodin (Olaf Holte), Prince of Chicago, childe of Datura, 7th gen

          First Brood (embraced in the 1800s)
          - Edgar Drummond, childe of Lodin, 8th gen (1865*)
          - Tommy Hinds, childe of Edgar Drummond, 9th gen [rogue, anarch] (1906)
          - Jacob Schumpeter, childe of Edgar Drummond, 9th gen (1981 or last decade, depending on ST preferences)
          *Something of interest that i would like to point out is that, if taken at face value, Drummond's embrace date would imply he was a already undead before the Great Chicago Fire, Lodin's childe from before becoming prince and an active part of Lodin's coup on Maxwell. While i did make use of it my game, that kind of tidbit is far from reliable - Khalid's embrace time changed in about two centuries between editions, for example. So take those embrace times with a grain of salt when working on timelines and such.


          - Horatio Ballard, childe of Lodin, 8th gen (1881)
          - Lawrence Ballard, childe of Horatio Ballard, 9th gen (1939)
          - Frank Gaugham, childe of Horatio Ballard, 9th gen (1952)


          ​
          - Edward Neally, childe of Lodin, 8th gen (1896)
          - Jefferson Foster, childe of Edward Neally, 9th gen [rogue, sabbat] (1897)
          - Joseph Peterson, childe of Edward Neally, 9th gen (1972)
          - Pham Hong, childe of Edward Neally, 9th gen (1978)
          - Kevin Jackson, childe of Edward Neally, 9th gen (1984 or last decade, depending on ST preferences)


          ​
          Second Brood (embraced from 1940s to present)
          - Al Capone, childe of Lodin, 8th gen (1941)
          - Alan Sovereign, childe of Capone, 9th gen (1958)

          ​
          - Bobby Weatherbottom, childe of Lodin, 8th gen (1984 or last decade, depending on ST preferences)
          - Lorraine, childe of Lodin, 8th gen (1987 or last decade, depending on ST preferences)
          ​

          Yes, i demoted Hinds, Schumpeter, Peterson & Jackson to 9th gen, while preserving Bobby & Lorraine as 8th gen, such heresy/madness.

          With Lorraine there's not much to it, her whole character is wrapped around the idea being Lodin's brand new childe/junkie lover, so making her the childe of anyone but Lodin is not worth the effort, imho, as you are pretty much devising a whole new character at this point.

          In Bobby's case it is because Lodin's embrace of him lead to Annabelle getting pissed and the result jockeying in the Primogen resulted at least in part in the Council Wars. In hindsight, i must admit any Ventrue attacking her grandchild's new boyfriend with Lodin's approval might get pretty much the same reaction from her, but decided not to rock the boat around timeline-relevant parts (not to mention Lodin delegating the embrace of a major new resource does not mesh very well with his distrust of most of his childer).

          Making Jackson 9th gen puts him in a sort of equal footing with the other kindred crimelords of the city - except Capone. What i see as more incentive to compete than problem overall, if you catch my drift.

          Generation is not a big deal with Hinds, 9th is still pretty respectable and a foolhardy move like embracing an enemy and then letting go feels far more appropriate coming from Edgar than Lodin. Not to mention it gives a much more narrative traction to the demented "rail baron of the dead".

          And Peterson & Schumpeter are chumps, no big reason for them to be Lodin's childer, they work just as well as bad picks of his own childer, imho.​​​

                  Nice idea!
                  I went a bit different apporach

                  Datura is a flower, commonly known as thornapples or jimsonweeds, moonflower, devil's weed, devil's trumpets, and hell's bells are also common names for the Datura genus
                  The generic name Datura is taken from Hindi dhatūra "thorn-apple", ultimately from Sanskrit dhattūra "white thorn-apple" (referring to Datura metel of Asia)
                  So I made Datura descen from Clan Danava aka. the "True-Ventrue"
                  The antediluvian is Veddartha in my stories too
                  So the lineage is: Lodin, childe of Datura, childe Maltheas (he was described in Transylvania Chronicles II, as "a vampire in his forties (Born Late 510s CE Embraced 554 CE) with small frame, dark curls and almond-shaped eyes" fitting for someone from India), childe of Veddartha​ "the Younger", Childe of Veddartha "the Elder"

                  ​Maltheas as a Danava actually makes sense, he is an Inconnu, and Sunset Empries states The Salubri say that Saulot achieved a transcendent spiritual state in the Indian city of Golconda, which falls within territory controlled by the Danava - some even believe that the very notion of Golconda originated from the Danava.
                  As an Inconnu, Golconda seeker and Monitor fits in this.
                  Maltheas' ultimate goal is to eliminate the Tzimisce influence over Eastern Europe, also makes sense, since the Danava are regal sages, ascetics and priests, some believe themselves descendants of the primal goddess Danu, hence the nickname of Children of Danu.
                  As the member of the Raktasadhu of India (the mutual alliance of the the Daitya, Danava, Nagaraja, Eastern Ravnos, and Wu Zao against the ever-posing threat of the Kuei-jin (mostly the Infinite Thunders Court of southern India and the Golden Court of eastern India.), the Camarilla, the Ashirra, the local Sabbat and the Tzimisce Methuselah Kartarirya and her Tzimsice) the Danava battle against Demons and demonic influence is a feature not a bug, so to speak
                  So Maltheas wanting to demolish the Tzimsice powerstructure, fight against Kupala is logical
                  ​Like the Embrace of someone who is from that area, Datura

                  Sure, headcanon, but I think would fly
