    Originally posted by CajunKhan View Post
    I prefer Blood Potency to Generation as well. I never liked the Generation mechanic, which seemed like an extension of Tolkien thinking, where the world becomes less magical every day. Just making Blood Potency very expensive is a plenty enough limiting mechanic without porting in Tolkien style notions of everything being an ever thinner shadow of a Platonic Ideal.
    I too don't like Tolkien's "The past was great and it's just gonna get worse unless we return to how things were somehow" but I don't really feel the WoD was entirely going that way. If anything, I think the WoD has a more grounded way of looking at this kind of thing. Think of historical Dynasties. Usually, they start strong, they decline over generations (or a single ruler, since a long reign gives plenty of time for faculties to decline, especially with the medical knowledge of the time) and after enough declining, either someone makes a glorious revival, or the next dynasty rises up and kicks them out. In some cases I'm in with the Gnostic idea (at some point the thinbloods will rise up, devour their way to thickness, become the new 5th gens, embrace prodigiously, "revive" the kindred race and make an attempt to slay god.) but in most cases the more mundane solutions are king

    The other splats are easier to discuss.
    The Garou are looking forward to the apocalypse because it'll come with a big societal change (edit: Also note that when the Garou pine for their glorious past, it's usually implied that they're full of shit)
    The Garou nation will be revitalized when the silver fangs exit leadership and the shadowlords take the helm. The shadow lords will lead extroadinarily well at first but will eventually plague themselves with infighting and hobbling the strong to maintain their rule. The Shadow lords will be replaced by... erhm... some tribe that only forms in the future.
    Alternatively, a different breed will oust the dominance of the Garou. Perhaps the Ratkin, perhaps Pentex will charge forth and make some better mockery breeds. Perhaps with bats redemption the Bat changers will return, or the Grondr will come back, or perhaps Gaia will make something new like she did with the Foxes.

    As for mage, the Technocrats are really just hinging on the next big thing. There are so many technologies that currently elude us mortals, so many bottlenecks, but once a breakthrough is made, massive opertunities are opened among Technocrats. Just think; if humans just found -the next battery- then Hit Marks, Exoskelletons, Cybernetics... they all lose their vulgarity (so long as you aren't giving them plasma guns) which opens up the options of making.. MORE heinous mechanical monstrosities. Once the mortals can work out how to make Gunther Hermann, the Mages can more easily create JC Denton. As for Traditions, as we all get wrapped up in ever more wonderful fantasy media, does that not decrease the vulgarity of Tradition based magic?

    Gnostic thought, but there's a nice little pattern to some of this stuff too
    -Reality's a prison.
    -Divine figures come into this prison to help us get out.
    -Divine figures progressively become more mundane as they too become trapped in the prison
    -At the lowest point, the figure makes a U turn and soars up back to divinity
    - The figure tries to break the prison.

    We're at stage 3, and it seems the splats are waiting for the next step. The Garou eagerly await their apocalypse, the mages await their ascension...

    Lastly
    Devouring your way to God is a lot cooler than waiting.
    Maybe someone'll uncover a super-unethical ritual to rise in generation. There was this wonderful new age ritual in rites of the blood...

They are likely an impediment or a threat in some fashion to you or your goals, even if it simply by virtue of existing as a powerful potential rival.                                                             Kharnov You're rather refreshing to argue with. I had a longer post but I lost it and I'm upset so I'll try send the gist.

                                                            First, the issue of who's embraced for practical reasons and who's embraced on a whim is difficult because really, there's a scale. A vampire may need a childer for something and yet pick a "romantic" option because, well, why not go with instinct? A lot of "romantic" type embraces would really be from people that the vampire feels is well inclined to be -insert clan here-, especially as vampires tend to stay in their own subcultures and meet a lot of likeminded people that they'll often favour. Even with Cleopatras, the childer isn't just chosen because she's beautiful, few get torn down just for that, but because the childer-to-be is a scheming vindictive bitch that'll fit right in. If the childer is even half a good choice, the sire will have the opportunity to mold the childer into a good choice during their tutalage period. To complicate issues, A lot of sires will lie to their childer; A vampire may insist he has deeply practical reasons to embrace someone he took in on a whim to save himself embarrassment while another vampire might tell a strategic embrace that they chose them for romantic reasons to hide their intentions and garner loyalty.

                                                            The ones that really, really don't fit in are going to have higher attrition rates. So most 'Romantic' embraces will turn to "practical" embraces.

                                                            Now, how does this relate to Generational conflict?
                                                            Because the crippling alienation brought about by the Generational conflict drives kindred to create emotional embraces. Its comparative to income disparity in the real world.
                                                            A lot of crime and deviance isn't immediately practical. A lot of low-income kids at school behave poorly, not because they're stupid (though a good chunk of them are), but because they believe the system has failed them, the teachers are against them, the values of the system are thus wrong, and that to 'succeed' they need to work by their own values; toughness, comedic skills, promiscuity, an inordinate amount of attention on expensive branded clothing; these signify success. As the rest of society further alienates them, they lash out more. To the establishment, these people are failures, but to them and their peers, they are successful, because they've built up groups that celebrate what they see as success. A Vandal doesn't break stuff for money (usually) but to feel better. Similarly, as vampires find themselves marginalized by their elders or hated by their childer, they feel alienated, and as they feel alienated, they need to create support networks around them. The more horrific kindred society is, the more vampires are going to do seemingly irrational moves like sire childer for love. The more accepting vampire society becomes, the less vampires will lash out or need to build their own support networks. Desire for Generation really does an amazing job at keeping things messy, just like divides between rich and poor keep things messy in the real world. From a sociological point of view, Generation makes things wonderfully interesting, while blood potency really mitigates the Jyhad.
