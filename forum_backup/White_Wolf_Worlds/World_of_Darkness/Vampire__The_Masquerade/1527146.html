                                          It's worth noting that one of the scenarios of Gehenna concludes with a little teaser that involves someone who is all but explicitly stated to be Caine pulling himself out of the dust after the end of the world as we know it, and angrily screaming up at Heaven for not allowing him to just *die* already.

                                          I'd imagine that Caine would probably have enough Fortitude to at least mostly-resist the damage caused by sunlight. The vampiric Disciplines come from the power of the Curse, at least in part, but they weren't exactly part of the plan, either, so I wouldn't rule that Caine is uniquely vulnerable. How exactly you'd want to run that would be up to you. You could just decide what level of vulnerability/resistance Caine has and play accordingly, or you could try to calculate realistically how well a character with 10 or 11 dots in Fortitude could soak sunlight.

                                          And if Caine can't soak with Fortitude, then he's gonna burn. The question is what the ultimate outcome will be. I do agree that allowing Caine to be ended by sunlight creates a bit of a flaw if you have a version that might be inclined to end his own existence, so I probably wouldn't make that an option. If Caine is going to be *killed* then it requires an act of murder on the part of his killer, in my opinion, who would then suffer the Sevenfold Curse (I also wouldn't want Caine dying by sunlight to effectively turn said Curse loose on Helios, either).

                                          So if you don't want sunlight as a possible permanent end for the First Vampire, but you don't want him to be immune either, then the best remaining options seem to be:

                                          a) He just doesn't stop burning. It's painful, probably does a lot of damage that he'll need to recover from eventually, but Caine will not suffer Final Death no matter what due to sunlight.

                                          (For people into B-movies, the straight-to-video sequel for Dracula 2000 might provide an example of what this could look like.)

                                          b) Caine is a bit like ol' Prometheus. Caine is absolutely capable of burning down to nothing but ash in sunlight. But come evening, Caine will reform and reawaken. If the ashes are scattered, he'll rise out of the earth itself where the faintest trace of his remains might have landed.
