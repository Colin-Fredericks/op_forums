                                    Lethal wasn't a matter of making Agg rarer, the opposite, actually. With it you have a clearer distinction on what it takes to heal it - or kill you - without looking back on the source.

                                    Superficial is mostly the same as the old Bashing damage. Mortals take it from punches and Vampires from most things, and Vampires also halve it. Everyone heals it easy, in fact the Kindred healed at twice the rate they heal in V5, 2B per Blood Point, which is very close to a Rouse Check.

                                    Lethal was the damage of things that can "kill" you directly, but not take prohibitive amounts of time to do so. In current terms a mortal would be out of commission for days to weeks, and for Supernatural splats their healing methods were calibrated against Lethal. Mortals would die if full with Lethal, and most supernaturals would be OoC, though still alive, Kindred included.

                                    Aggravated was when things were dire. Only fire and arguably radiation were regular natural sources of Aggravated (you could also go for an exquisite wound like smashing bones, lots of healing time and likely crippling, without big danger to life). Otherwise Agg came with Supernatural sources, originally rare ones.

                                    IME CoD did it right: kept the three types but rebalanced Agg to be really rare. And I agree that Hunters in general should be dangerous among other things through the ability to bring a reliable source of Agg to the fight.

                                                            I always ignore heat, radiation, etc., for vamps. Ever since Requiem 2e, I've also lessened damage by having a flat rule: vamps downgrade all damage one step relative to humans. Only the three banes (sunlight, fire, and True Faith) cause aggravated damage. Other sources of aggravated get downgraded to lethal, lethal gets downgraded to (unhalved) bashing, and bashing is downgraded to half bashing. One blood point heals 1L or 2B. Ignore all other special rules.

                                                            So, teeth and claws (including Protean 2: Feral Claws) do aggravated to mortals but lethal to vampires. This makes vamp-on-vamp (or vamp-on-lupe) combat less all-or-nothing, and Fortitude less obligatory. Swords and bullets do (unhalved) bashing damage. Fists and blunt instruments do (halved) bashing damage.

                                                            Under this system, bullets work out a little bit more damaging upfront, but it's made up for with swords being less damaging and teeth and claws being much more tolerable. The 2B for one blood point rule also makes vamps very hardy. This removes the incentive for Blade-style trenchcoats and katanas—American vamps can still use their WalMart assault rifles like normal (if bought before 2015), and everyone else can just fight like snarling beasts with their fangs.

                                                            I've toyed with giving all vamps claws, since we use VTR2e Disciplines in most cases anyway (so Protean 2 is more diverse), but I ultimately ruled against it out of tradition. But it would be cool for making vamps feel more visceral.

                                                            I also like vamps having Kindred Senses ("Predator Senses", I call them, since Sabbat vamps wouldn't use "Kindred"), allowing them to see in low-light and easily detect blood nearby. I'm basically running Requiem at this point, but with a lower target number and Masquerade lore. Requiem just gets the feel of vampires better.
