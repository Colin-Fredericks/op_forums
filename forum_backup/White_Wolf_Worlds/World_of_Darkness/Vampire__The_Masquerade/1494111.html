    New Orleans is the new capital of the Sabbat, & the Cathari have been purged, now what?

    10-01-2022, 07:27 PM
    I constructed a campaign that started with the siege of New Orleans. Once New Orleans was captured, the Grand Inquisitor of the Sabbat declared New Orleans the new Sabbat capital, abolished the Regent position, declared the Sabbat Inquisition to be the governing body of the Sabbat with herself the Grand Inquisitor on top, and declared the Cathari Demagogues and all those loyal to them heretical and began a purge of Cathari.

    There's still some diplomatic stuff to see if the Noddist leadership will back this purge. If not, the resultant civil war will be long and bloody. If the Noddist leadership DOES back the Grand Inquisitor, then the civil war will be short and little more than a large scale wild hunt.

    After that, though, I don't really have any plans. It may well be that this is simply the natural endpoint of the campaign. However, the players are having fun and like their characters, so I'd like to continue if possible. So I came here to ask for help brainstorming for continued story ideas. Thank you in advance for any suggestions.

      Well, how solid is the Sabbat's position in the rest of Louisiana? Because if they don't have a good number of neighboring domains on their side i can see this Grand Inquisitor having something of a problem sustaining its power base. What are the other notable sabbat leading figures, supportive, neutral, antagonistic to account for, both in their vicinity or far away enclaves? How is the situation in Mexico city, Montreal and other places supposedly in the grasp of the Sword of Caine?

      Yes, acess to planes can help with sidestepping distance and border issues, but to a definitely limited degree - long-distance communications can matter and help immensely with building alliances but ultimately every city or metropolitan area is an "isle of herds" of its own to be managed, preserved or exploited according to local powers' needs.

      So on short run, who must be dealt with in New Orleans - is the city fully in the Sabbat hands or are there any Camarilla or independent enclaves that might prove something of a nuisance or actual threat? What packs are in the city and who leads them? How strongly are they in the Grand Inquisitor's support, against it or on the fence? And what might it take to make either of these groups change sides?

      And all of that applies to the PCs too - are they loyal to Grand Inquisitor or could they end up supporting someone else, or even themselves, to supplanting it and/or the Cathari? Any major canon Sabbat NPCs still around that could become a factor either way?

      Any random kindred with Dominate 4-5 that might turn everything on its head by rewiring individual loyalties according to its own mysterious agendas & tastes?
      Draw out a good map of the characters at play in the "field of operations" and the possibilties will present themselves, i think.

      With that done it becomes just a matter of applying the same principle in ever greater scopes.

      Who would be against the abolition of the Regent role and might fight for its preservation, for selfish self-interest, selflessly pragmatic or other reasons? Who would be in favor, but calls bull$#it on the Grand Inquisitor as just a Regent under another name? Where do the other paths stand - and how might those in Evil Revelations, service to the Antediluvians or a number of other secret camps with a vested interest in seeing the Inquisition take a nosedive exploit the situation?


      Also, what about extra complications like mortal hunter groups, lupines, the Technocracy or other groups with a possible vested interest in the well-being of people or the society? Large numbers of Sabbat in the same are usually not exactly conductive to a happy, peaceful and prosperous way of life for the mortal herd, so there's definite potential for extra strife to spring from any - or all - of those sources, with more drama & action rising from its resolution.

                                  Montreal was lost. Many blame Cathari incompetence and recklessness for this loss. What has made it much worse is the clumsy propaganda the Cathari demagogues put out afterwards, claiming that this was a good thing, that the Sabbat had evolved beyond the need for territory, and that the Sabbat was more dangerous than ever without territory. This propaganda was so blatantly absurd and such a ridiculous insult to the intelligence of most older Sabbat that it aroused a "let them eat cake" level of F-YOUUUU!!!!! from the followers of more disciplined paths. Only the sheer numbers of the Cathari prevented an immediate civil war.

                                  The Grand Inquisitor used this widespread hatred to promote her own agenda. As such, it needs to reconquer a reasonably large amount of territory, secure the borders of that territory, and purge those elements that either oppose those two goals, or threaten to cause juicebag stampedes with their idiocy.
