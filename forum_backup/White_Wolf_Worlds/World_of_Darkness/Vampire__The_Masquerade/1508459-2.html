                    How does a sabotaged sprinkler system negate non-flammable building materials and fire prevention in decor choices? By stacking smart choices, the countering of any one thing doesn't lead to complete failure.

                    Once again you are assuming every haven is public knowledge, which is erroneous as a starting point. Some havens can be large mansions, some can be small apartments, but others can simply be individual rooms that have been upgraded and hidden which are part of other businesses. A sub-basement area in a department store, a reinforced storage locker at a 24 hour storage facility, etc. Each of these add-on havens means that the business itself generates wealth, has its own security, and potentially gains you local influence depending on the business. Any attack to destroy your havens would very quickly be on a scale that would make the mortals believe a terror attack was happening, which in turn makes such an attack plan potentially be masquerade breaking.

                    But for the sake of indulging your point of view, let's assume a singular bunker existence, rather than a randomized haven strategy. So every vampire lives in a decommissioned nuclear launch silo (initial purchase price is only about $350K) and it has been retrofitted to be anti-supernatural protected and allow for up to five years of underground existence without fresh supplies and a live in Herd.

                    The only problem is that with such a vampiric society design, there is no such thing as Elysium, no such thing as Harpies, no functional prestige or reputation system. Basically all the stuff that is crowbarred into VtM to make plot hooks happen that you have been harping on about in previous posts no longer exist since every vampire is a self-contained unit and only interacts with other vampires via video call. Hunters can't track you because you never leave to feed or use the hunting grounds, rival factions don't really exist because every vampire becomes a shut in and it is way too dangerous to try to take territory.
