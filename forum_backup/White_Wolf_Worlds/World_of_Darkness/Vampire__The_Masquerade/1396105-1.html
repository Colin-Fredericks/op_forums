            Cults doesn't really support playing non-Hecata Samedi/Harbingers, unfortunately. They do still canonically exist, and it doesn't take much homebrew to make them playable ("you have your classic discipline spread, take the classic weakness as a chargen flaw, now go have fun destroying the Venetians!"), but it's not explicitly part of the book.

            As for why they joined the Hecata, the basic idea is that they saw an opportunity to destroy Augustus and his kin by turning their own great-great-whatever-grandchilder against them. The strength of the Giovanni was always their loyalty to the family, and when that loyalty got badly shaken by the Inquisition, the Cappadocian remnants saw an opportunity to get a foot in the door. "Look how your so-called 'family' is willing to let you die en masse to protect their egos. Do they really deserve your devotion at this point? We were once their 'family' too, and here's a long description of all the ways they repaid our trust."

            (On a meta level, it's based on Luciano's creation of the National Crime Syndicate, where increased pressure and scrutiny from the government led to younger members of the Italian mafia forming tentative alliances with their bitter enemies in the Irish and Jewish mobs, deposing the older Italians, and closing ranks against the outside threat.)

            As for the weakness, agreed. Especially the current mechanical implementation is…not very good. I'm hoping it changes before publication.

                                    Some thoughts:

                                    1. The Old Clan Tzimisce existing outside of the Carpathians is perfectly understandable since the Sabbat didn't bother to go after them and there's a global network of Inconnu that have Watchers in every major city in the world. We also know that plenty of Tzimisce are prone to wanderlust despite their curse due to the fact that the first Tzimisce we've ever read about, Dracula, took his Earth with him to travel the world. Hell, Vlad Dracula could have been the Prince of Tokyo's sire himself.

                                    2. It's also possible that Mayumi Shibasaki is part of a much older Old Clan Tzimisce lineage that has been in Japan from the very beginning. In my own games, which I admit prejudices me, Oda Nobunaga was embraced to the Old Clan Tzimisce and the Nobunaga Clan (Tzimisce) are enemies of the Tokugawa Clan (Ventrue).

                                    3. I'm pleased to say the Kuei-Jin have always been speaking bullshit about their control over Asia as the Cainites were always there and will always will be. I point to CLANBOOK LASOMBRA REVISED which talked at great length about the Clan's presence in China and their sneering contempt at the idea that the Kuei Jin have ever been able to keep them (or any other Kindred) from Asia. No one puts down the Wan Kuei better than Lasombra.

                                    4. Controversial: The lack of Hunger in the city combined with the idea of them being Spirit Ridden makes me think that the Kuei-Jin may be retconned as Cainites who have Spirit Thaumaturgy and use Dragon Lines to feed from people without drinking blood. They would follow Paths of Enlightenment and use other methods to rise through the ranks than just Blood Potency. Presumably they would also reject the origins of Caine and Abel.

                                    5. Another possibility is the Kuei-Jin just flat out don't control the regions of Asia and there have always been Cainites there.
