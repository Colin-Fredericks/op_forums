    Clans/Bloodlines that you have a soft spot for?

    02-05-2025, 08:10 PM
    Which groups do you like but haven't really used lots but still find interesting?

    Mine are the Brujah and Nagaraja lineage.

    The Brujah appeal to my more rebellious side and can't seem to acknowledge that kindred and humanity living in harmony is impossible and the Nagaraja image of creepy and possibly demented necromancers with no clear founder for some reason intrigued me.

                                  Setite and Brujah

                                  Brujah appeals to me because i have anger issues. I can identify with the struggle, and i really like politics. I dont like how it end up in harmful stereotypes of having anger issues, and the whole "Rebel without cause" aesthethic schitck is just boring, but in the end, i identify with them

                                  Setite because i like the Clanbook Followers of Setite Revised, even if it has some... distateful decitions. Even if their theology ends up being basic Gnosticism, i like how it subverts how setites are represented, and the Ministries are just... *Chef Kiss*

                                  In bloodlines side, i like Salubries. Maybe because im catholic, but Saulot best Antediluvian and all that "MUH SAULOT CREATED THE BAALI" is just nihilist crap. You cant have a grim world without hope. You cant have evil if you dont have goodness to contrast it. And Salubries are the contrast. They are the "Good vampire". When others vampires fucks about how they are cursed and that makes them do shit, the Salubries are there

                                                      Cappadocians. Still feels like they've been done dirty in 5e, seemingly totally subsumed by the Harbingers. About 5 years ago I posted a homebrew Cappadocian Hecata bloodline. It was controversial since I gave them Presence due to their rage (even got a writers response).

                                                      -edit- found it:

                                                      Cappadocian (Hecata)
                                                      Optional Discipline Spread: Fortitude, Oblivion, Presence

                                                      The Cappadocian's... countless were sacrificed by our founder, then we were almost exterminated by the treacherous Giovanni, and finally nearly subsumed by the wraith's called Harbingers. The true Clan of Death has returned. The Promise of 1528 has not been forgotten. The Cappadocian's will not be forgotten!

                                                      Bloodline Abilities
                                                      1- Unliving Resolve: The Cappadocian's have endured, and will always endure, once per scene add one automatic success to any Stamina or Resolve Roll.

                                                      2- Necromantic Adept: The Cappadocian's have studied death for countless millennia, gain two extra die on any Oblivion Ceremony roll relating to raising the dead and animated dead add two extra die to resist disintegration.

                                                      3- Fear of Oblivion: The Cappadocian's channel the fury of their lost clan, getting two extra die on any Intimidation roll against Hecata and also causing a two dice penalty on any Social roll from Hecata that is targeting the Cappadocian.

                                                      4- Crypt Guard: The Cappadocian's have learned many practical lessons in securing rest, gain four dots to spread between Haven and Retainers.

                                                      5- Awaken: The Cappadocian's have rediscovered an ancient rite, with it they can attempt to raise themselves or another Kindred from Torpor. Spend two points of Willpower and Roll Resolve + Medicine (difficulty 10 - Humanity) for one turn each success you awaken yourself or another Kindred, but suffering from Hunger 5. Reducing Hunger to 4 or less fully awakens the Kindred. Until fully awakened the Kindred is more an animated corpse and as such may act even while staked (fire and sunlight are unaffected).
