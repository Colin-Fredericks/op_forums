                                Pretty much what the topic title says.

                                Since the odds of getting a proper KotE20 seem rather slim and to make my next campaign run smoother I’m looking at turning the Kindred of the East into Cainite vampires. I’m also hoping that bringing them into line with the rest of the Kindred it will smooth out some of the unfortunate implications of making all the Oriental undead a separate species.

                                In universe, the notion that Kuei-jin are a separate species of supernatural is a combination of propaganda (from both sides) and sincere confusion due to the Eastern Kindred descending from an unrecorded Antediluvian rather than being a bloodline of one the known western thirteen.

                                In this case, I’m running with the notion that there were just 13 Antediluvians is a translation error; the actual translation of the Book of Nod is “and their numbers were 3 by 10” (as was suggested in a footnote in the book of the same name) and not “3 and 10.”

                                The 13 known Antediluvians were just those who found each other after the Flood and sired progeny while others either died in the battle with the Second Generation, never came out of torpor after the flood or chose to use being scattering by the flood/presumed dead to strike out on their own unnoticed... one of these latter ones became the ancestor of the Kindred of the East.

                                Anyway, I figure as a clan their culture falls mostly into line with the fluff of Kindred of the East (i.e. they organize into various courts just as their brethren gather in cities under a prince). They have an utter disdain for embracing (though their Beast has other ideas) and view their ghouls as their half-damned children (i.e. KotE Dhampyrs are actually either ghouls or, in some cases, Revenants).

                                The Dharma are Paths of Enlightenment, albeit ones that retain the virtues of Conscience (Hun), Self-Control (Yin) and Courage (Yang) with their Beast (P’o) opposing their Humanity/Path rating. Their bearings improve social actions just as high Humanity does.

                                Their Clan weakness is a particularly cunning Beast that seizes control whenever they fail a Conscience roll; basically a separate personality in line with the P’o natures. It is while in this state that they will Embrace with abandon; the idea that the wicked spontaneously arise as Kuei-Jin is a lie they convince themselves of to absolve themselves of the guilt of having damned another soul to unlife because they lost control of their P’o.

                                Their clan disciplines are Blood Sorcery (representing their control over Chi... the Path of Blood’s ‘Theft of Vitae’ is where legends that the Elders can feed off the breath of mortals arises), Vicissitude (representing the various shintai; blood, flesh, bone, etc.) and Auspex (representing the Soul Arts that typically revolve around perception of and influence over the soul).
