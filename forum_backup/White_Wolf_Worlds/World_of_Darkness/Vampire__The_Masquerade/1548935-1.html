    In all fairness, given the Followers of Set are so explicitly grounded in religion it seems logical that more than a few of them have (whether through selfish cynicism or genuine conviction) redefined Set in exactly the fashion best suited to serve their own needs.

    Heck, given their explicit commitment to undermining any given Status Quo (and tendency to treat internecine rivalries as the order of the day) it would be more astonishing if the Followers of Set were not prone to throw up as many heresies, schisms, hypocrisies and self-serving charlatans as Christianity.

    Whether these hypocrites and charlatans last long enough to seriously affect the shape of the Clan is, of course, an interesting question.


    DIGRESSION: Incidentally, one of my more inspiring discoveries on the White Wolf Wiki was the revelation that - in AD 1520 - a Setite named Lucretia (6th Generation) had Embraced Danny Larkhill and Mark Sandhurst, who would go on to become antitribu and Camarilla archons.

    Since my original search had been for ‘Lucretia’ (Seeking to get a better understanding of who the stereotypical Snake Priestess destroyed in Modern London by the PC & his coterie in VAMPIRE: THE MASQUERADE - REDEMPTION might have been), being handed the perfect excuse to make her a contemporary of Lucrezia Borgia whose various escapades inspired the ‘Black Legend’ associated with that relatively blameless historic individual (Not least through her own muck-slinging), all of which earned her Embrace by the Followers of Set (After which point she promptly applied the classic Borgia “God has given us the Papacy, let us enjoy it!” mixture of piety, hypocrisy, lethality and ‘merriment’ to her career, growing into the living cliche of a Setite and a surprisingly-heavy hitter*).


    *If nothing else through Generation, showmanship, audacity and completely shamelessness realpolitik, very much in the style of Renaissance prince - secular or sacred.


    Moving on, your suggestion that the Giovannia are rare and fairly discrete in the Free States makes me wonder which of the Clans would be most likely to monopolise - or seek to monopolise - local bastions of Organised Crime (Beyond the street gangs focused upon in LOS ANGELES BY NIGHT).

    The Ventrue, perhaps? Ventrue with aspirations towards their traditional preeminence over the levers of civic & commercial power would seem to be mistrusted by the Anarchs (and it’s interesting to wonder if Don Sebastian was also reluctant to let the Clan of Kings get too close to the pillars of the Establishment in his city, given the character’s apparent love of Control) so pursuing less legitimate business might be a Free State Ventrue’s best bet for getting some power and authority without starting to look TOO much like ‘The Man’.

    I may have noted this before, but I really like the idea of the Free States as a place where Kindred can (and are sometimes positively obliged to) spend their nights in a way that upends the classic expectations of their Clan.

        Originally posted by Atticus Cleanclaw View Post
        In all fairness, given the Followers of Set are so explicitly grounded in religion it seems logical that more than a few of them have (whether through selfish cynicism or genuine conviction) redefined Set in exactly the fashion best suited to serve their own needs.
        I see the Setites as less of a "religion" than a cult. Cults don't tolerate independent thinkers or heretics. The purpose of the cult is to control their members on behalf of their leader. If someone diverges too much, they get destroyed. Of course, not all of them get flamed. The Serpents of the Light managed to survive by defecting to the Sabbat, and I'm sure there were many previous heretics who managed to survive for a little while and some who remain surviving still. But those are outliers. (Of course, even cults have power struggles, but usually this is about power, money, and influence, not independent theologies.)

        One major issue that I have always had with the sourcebooks on the Followers of Set, is that the writers too often want them to be portrayed just like any other clan, and this problem has gotten worse with every new edition. In my chronicles, Setites almost always exclusively embrace only from the inner circles of the Setite cult and thus deal with vampires whose mortals selves have already been effectively brainwashed. Now like with all things, there are of course exceptions. Sometimes Setites do decide spontaneously to embrace someone outside the cult, or misjudge how effectively brainwashed a member is. That's fine. But these exceptions are a small minority.

        I know many people prefer the newer depiction of the Setites than the older ones, but I like my snake cults.

        Originally posted by Atticus Cleanclaw View Post
        Moving on, your suggestion that the Giovannia are rare and fairly discrete in the Free States makes me wonder which of the Clans would be most likely to monopolise - or seek to monopolise - local bastions of Organised Crime (Beyond the street gangs focused upon in LOS ANGELES BY NIGHT).
        I imagine organized crime in LA is not monopolized by Kindred from any specific clan. The Los Angeles crime family is much weaker compared to other Mafia families, and have been in decline since the 1950s. So real life suggests there isn't one Kindred "controlling" the family. There are probably several different Kindred pulling strings which makes the family divided and less effective than their rivals.

        I do intend to have some call outs to famous depictions of LA crime families particularly those elements highlighted by the books of James Ellroy, and especially the movie version of LA Confidential.

        In my chronicle, I'll be having David Geduld's "gang" having some significant influence, particularly in regards to the ports and smuggling. The other Kindred involved will reside in the Barony of Angels, but not necessarily be members of the MacNeil Boys. Smiling Jack has influence in a major criminal motorcycle gang. Simply because the Brujah make up 50% of the Kindred in the Free States, the Kindred of that clan probably make up most of the Kindred with influence in organized crime. Geduld's line of Ventrue has some. Some Caitiff do as well. The other clans not so much. I intend for the Setites to have influence in some "adjacent" industries like porn studios and the like, but not in the crime family itself.

        In terms of Giovanni and organized crime, I have my own fictional version of New York's Five Families pulled from other fictional settings (notably the Godfather) and one of these is the Putanesca Crime Family which the Giovanni control through that family. The clan tends to be "locked out" of any other crime family, but control of the Putanesca still gives them a lot of reach in my chronicles.
