    Tremere

    Sage Advice (1 pt merit)
    In life, you were someone who helped others reach their potential, like a thesis advisor, psychotherapist, or business consultant. Your ability to help others develop a plan of attack for their projects has intensified since your embrace. If you spend a half hour with another person discussing a project the person is working on, and they are willing to implement your input, they make their very next roll related to that project at -2 difficulty. This roll may not take place in combat, and you must have at least one dot in both traits used in the roll's dice-pool. Also, this ability cannot aid you in your projects, since it's hard to maintain the necessary objectivity.

    Shared Servants (4pt flaw)
    Your blood-bonds are not your own, they belong to the whole of Clan Tremere. Your thralls feel little loyalty to you, personally, but anyone you blood bond gains the "Bound to the Clan" flaw from LotC.

    Our Man In (Name of City) (3 point merit)
    The city in which you operate has neither a Tremere primogen, nor a chantry. You may even be the only Tremere present. Though you are still considered a loyal member of the Pyramid, your ties are a bit more nebulous than usual, and you report directly to someone higher than a Regent, such as a Lord or Pontifex. Your present mission requires minimal direct supervision, and is related to a long-term project such as establishing a local chantry, gaining the right to sire multiple times, serving as an ambassador to the local court, or maintaining a way station for traveling Tremere. As long as you show regular progress in this task, the Pyramid largely ignores you. However, if there are any signs of shirking your assigned duties, you will be recalled immediately.

    The downside of this is that you are no longer receiving training in Thaumaturgy, and lack the facilities (such as libraries, tutors, and laboratories) to do so on your own. As long as you are out in the cold, you are very unlikely to learn any new rituals or embark on new paths. You can increase your current thaumaturgical paths, as usual.

    Nerd (2 point flaw)
    You lack many of the social graces others take for granted. Instead, you focus on improving your mind. You avidly follow obscure mortal pop culture, memorizing reams of data about comic books, role-playing games, larping, science fiction, fantasy fiction, and popular science. No physical attribute, social attribute or background (except generation) may ever be higher than your lowest mental attribute.

    Remote Communion (2 pt merit)
    Somewhere along the line you were learning a Thaumaturgical power related to the transfer of blood (such as Vessel of Transference or Theft of Vitae) and something went horribly sideways. The blood of the clan, as used in the ritual that binds new Tremere, automatically appears in your system once a month, on the night of the full moon. Because of this your are, and always will be, thoroughly subject to the flaw Bound to the Clan. However, if you ever find yourself in torpor, there will be a steady trickle of blood to maintain or wake you. No one with the merit Unbondable, or similar evasions of the blood bond may take this merit.

    Experiential Analysis (3 point flaw)
    Whenever you use the power "A Taste for Blood" or similar rituals such as "Blood Walk", they work normally, except there is a side effect: until sunset the following night, you suffer from the clan flaw of the subject being tested. In some cases, this may actually prove helpful, such as when infiltrating the Lasombra.

        Kiasyd

        The Potion - 7 points

        One night you mixed... something. You certainly remember there being some sort of Fera in there, or maybe Kinfolk. There was a Setite helping you... or was it one of those demonic chaps... well the details are certainly foggy. You lucked out and made a concoction of incredible power, and it knocked you right into a week's torpor and robbed you of your memories, giving you the Amnesia flaw with no freebies being granted.

        On the upside, this concoction has significantly altered your Cainite physiology (making you of great interest to any others who should examine you carefully); you only spend blood to rise on every third night, and you rise from Torpor brought about by Lethal damage (or mystical means) as though you had a Humanity of 10. Should you fall into Torpor due to Starvation, you will rise after a week when your body successfully replenishes a point of Vitae from some mysterious reserve (leaving you near-starved and close to Torpor from Starvation damage).

            Non-Ventrue

            Embrace Night Regular (2 pt Merit)
            It is a rarely talked about tradition but all Camarilla Ventrue honour it: The Embrace Night. The time when all Ventrue of the city come together and celebrate the anniversary of an embrace among their midst. It is uncommon to invite outsiders, but it's certainly not unheard of, even if the clan usually celebrates in private. For whatever reason, you were invited once and you made a really good impression. So much so that it has become a bit of a tradition for the local Ventrue to invite you to their celebration. You never knew how much fun the blue bloods could be once nobody looks...

                Gangrel

                Less Beast, More Bunny (2 point flaw)
                Your Beast, though as savage and deadly as any other vampire's, is of a slyer stock than most. It refuses to show itself when you most need to "flash your fangs". You seem harmless and non-violent to most everyone you meet, though this assessment might be false. Animal traits you gain are usually drawn from prey animals, and tend to be cute, though still clearly inhuman, like bunny ears or mouse whiskers. You may never take dots in Intimidate.

                Attack Dog (or bat, or cat, or whatever) (5 pt merit)
                You have trained animals to serve you faithfully, most notably in combat. Through repeated use of Animalism, extensive ghouling, and simple Pavlovian conditioning, you have trained your companions to enter a rage frenzy against a target of your choice. You must be present to trigger the attack, but it will continue until either the target is dead, the companion animal is knocked out/dead, or you command the animal to cease. It takes a full turn action to issue either the attack or desist command to one animal. If your Attack Dog is killed or otherwise lost to you, you do not lose this merit, but it takes (six weeks - your Animal Ken) score in weeks of constant training to train a new one. You may never have more animals thus trained than your Animal Ken score, since out of jealousy your pets would tear one another apart.

                Winged (2 pt merit)
                Somewhere along the line you frenzied and gained a permanent animal feature: a huge pair of functional wings growing out of your back, like a Gargoyle. Of course, this is a gross violation of the Masquerade. You can wear an obscuring costume and roll Manipulation + Performance, difficulty 8, to hide your wings for a scene, though this will fail as soon as a combat begins. You may purchase the discipline Flight without a tutor, at out-of-clan costs.

                Visible Beast (1 pt flaw)
                Whenever you spend blood or enter Frenzy, your Beast bares itself to the world. Your face contorts into a feral mask, your eyes glow an eerie color, and your speech becomes low and growling. Temporarily you exhibit the Monstrous flaw. This lasts until the end of the current scene, or once you spend a willpower point to calm down.

                Infectious Embrace (1 or 3 pt flaw)
                When you feed until a human is empty of blood, the last blood point you take automatically back-washes into the mortal's body. The victim is an entirely normal corpse, except they do not decompose. After 24 hours, the embrace will occur spontaneously. This is a 1 pt flaw if you are aware of it and can take action to prevent them from rising, such as dismembering the victim or just not feeding to the last drop. At 3 points, your character is not yet aware they are leaving a trail of childer in their wake. You can only find out after buying this flaw down to the 1 point level.

                Bedtime for Bumpkin (1 pt flaw)
                You find it hard to sleep soundly during the day if humans are nearby. If any human approaches within 100 feet (30 meters) of you while you slumber, you will fail to regain willpower upon awakening.

                Clan Variant (4 pt flaw)
                Clan Gangrel is known for spawning a large number of bloodlines, and you are one such specimen. You may replace any one of your clan disciplines within another common discipline, and take five points in flaws, for which you do not gain points, in place of your clan weakness. These added flaws should be similar in theme to the main clan, such as Glowing Eyes, Permanent Fangs, or Monstrous. Half of the childer you embrace will share your clan disciplines and have the same flaws. In most cases, Clan Gangrel accepts the new line as one of their own, often not even realizing there is a difference.

                Animal Embrace (3 pt merit)
                You have discovered that it is possible for you to embrace animals. Drain them and give them blood as usual, and the deed in done. They are Caitiff, and afflicted with all the usual weaknesses of other vampires. Lacking a human soul to balance against the Beast, however, they are in a permanent state of frenzy. Some Sabbat Gangrel have taken to embracing animals, somehow caging them and then releasing them into Camarilla gatherings... then running like hell.

                Exotic Animal (1 pt merit)
                You have an animal in your care which is not common in your part of the world, such as a Giraffe, Caiman, or Lemur in North America. This gives you no special ability to influence or train this animal beyond usual, but does grant some bragging rights. Also, while many day-time intruders might figure they can handle a watchdog or even a small bear, few will willingly enter a home where a lion or crocodile wanders the halls.

                Alien (1 pt merit)
                The animal flaws you gain are all drawn from one extremely unusual animal, such as a jellyfish, sponge, earthworm, or even a plant or fungus of some sort. Normal humans who see you will immediately think you are in some way abnormal, such as an unidentified monster or a space alien, but because the animal is not traditionally associated with vampires, they are unlikely to guess your true nature.

                Totem (5 pt merit)
                In life, you practiced a traditional, nature-based religion, possibly even as a Dreamspeaker mage or shifter kinfolk, and developed a bond with an animal totem which remains to this night. All new animal traits gained are from this one totem. More importantly, your spiritual relationship serves to block Lupines using Sense the Wyrm on you; all they sense is your totem. Lupines are, of course, still able to deduce your vampiric nature by other means.

                Civilized Square Peg (3 pt merit)
                Your blood is of Clan Gangrel, but your sire abandoned you a bit too long after the embrace. You met vampires of other clans before he had a chance to train you, so you have spent little time with Gangrel, and do not match any of the kindred community's stereotypes of your clan. You dress in an unremarkable or even fashionable manner, maintain good hygiene, enjoy the city, and have a good grasp of Elysium etiquette. If asked to guess your clan, most would say Toreador, Idealist Brujah, Caitiff or Ventrue. You have a -1 difficulty on pleasant social rolls when dealing with any "vanilla vampire". Other Gangrel find you aloof and, perhaps, ashamed of your heritage. You, therefore, find it hard to socialize within your own clan. You suffer a +1 difficulty on social rolls whenever you are among a group of three or more vampires in which Gangrel are a majority.

                Poverty Stricken (1 pt flaw)
                Whether by personal preference, a mystic curse, or simple bad luck, you can never take the background "Resources", or possess any significant amount of cash.

                    Nosferatu

                    Situs Abstractus (3 pt merit)
                    All Nosferatu go through a nightmarish transformation after the embrace, but yours was insanely brutal. Not only did outside bear the curse of Nosferat, your inside did as well. Now your internal organs are in complete disarray. This turned out to be very useful. All attempts at staking you are at a +2 difficulty, since nobody knows where exactly your heart is.

                    Doppelgänger (4 pt. flaw)
                    For whatever reason, your transformation had a peculiar effect: You look exactly like another Nosferatu. And it's not one of the good ones. They might be an informant for the Sabbat (or for the Camarilla in Sabbat territory), a known renegade or whatever thorn in the side of the mighty would fit. Vampires outside of the Nosferatu clan have no chance of identifying you as anything but that person and even Nosferatu might need some convincing that you are you. This flaw cannot be bought off until the other person is dealt with.

                    Comedy in Tragedy (2 pt. flaw)
                    The curse of your clan has deformed you, reassembled you and dragged you through a nightmarish ordeal. As you recovered and beheld your new, horrible visage, you noted something equally terrible: You look kinda silly. Maybe the tip of your nose has rolled up an your ears are floppy, maybe you have comically large feet or maybe your hands have shrunken to sticks. It doesn't really interfere with your daily life, but in addition to being a walking masquerade violation, all attempts to intimidate others are at a +1 difficulty.

                    Minty Fresh (1 pt merit)
                    Even your life on the streets and close to the sewers could not convince you to stop putting some effort into presentation. Through an elaborate network of plastic bags, cologne and one or two Little Tree air fresheners, you manage to ditch the scent of down below rather well. Your coterie is rather grateful.

                        Ventrue Antitribu

                        Holy Blade - 1 or 3 Points

                        You have a weapon which is also a relic of a major world religion (almost certainly Christianity) dating back to the Crusades. While the blade is nothing exceptional (unless one happened to possess True Faith, in which case holding it would grant a temporary extra dot of True Faith) the legends behind it most certainly are. At 1 point this blade is known to any Knight of St. Gustav with extensive knowledge, and at 3 points most Knights (and many other Ventrue) will have heard tales of the Crusader (and Ventrue) who wielded it. If conversing with a Knight who is aware of the blade's history, reduce the difficulty of all social rolls by 2 points. Many of the Ventrue Antitribu would be willing to give instruction in Disciplines or a Path just to examine the blade, but this must be roleplayed.

                        Incidentally, the blade counts as a potent holy symbol; Baali, Infernalists and The Fallen (should they be vulnerable at the moment of looking) will be uneasy around the blade, and will be forced to roll Courage (difficulty 8) or flee if touched by it.

                        It goes without saying that others will be jealous of the blade's owner, and may seek to relieve them of it. Should the player disgrace themselves sufficiently, or make a powerful enemy, they may find themselves losing their prized possession.

                                        Giovanni

                                        Family Responsibilities (2 pt flaw)
                                        For whatever reason, mortal members of your family always seem to call you when they need help. Maybe they're short on money, maybe their daughter is being knocked around by her husband, maybe their neighbor's house is haunted, or maybe they just need a ride from the airport at 3 in the morning. Every session a member of your family will call you for help with something.

                                        Circling the Drain (3 pt flaw)
                                        Someone you love dearly is facing near certain death from some horrible disease, like cancer, Alzheimer's, a series of increasingly serious strokes, or just from very advanced age. The Anzioni have refused your request to ghoul or embrace your loved one, but you can't just let them die. You will do anything... anything... to save them. Maybe you can earn them the proxy kiss, or failing that you might even arrange for a vampire from another clan to ghoul or embrace them. Whatever you plan to do, hurry up, because time is getting short.

                                        Observant (1 pt flaw)
                                        How can any member of your family not be religious? After all, they know with certainty there is an afterlife, and ghosts sometimes talk about heavenly places that follow the first death. You are an observant member of a mortal religion, likely Catholicism, but the clan also has the Jewish Rothsteins, the Presbyterian Dunsirn, and the Episcopalian Milliners, as well. Whatever the faith, you regularly attend services whenever the sunrise-sunset times allow. Each time you attend you regain a point of temporary willpower. However, it costs you a point of willpower to perform any action which would be counter to your faith.

                                        Grave Countenance (3 pt merit)
                                        Something about you just reminds people of death. There may look entirely normal, but just your presence lowers the local shroud by 1. If another family member has the same trait, the effects stack. At particularly large family gatherings, everyone just expects the walls will start bleeding and a low moaning will be audible. In addition, you suffer a +1 difficulty on all pleasant social among mundanes.

                                        the Bickersons (3 pt flaw)
                                        You are not descended from any of the Giovanni families. You received the Proxy Kiss because you had some skill too valuable to the clan to lose. Everyone was stunned when the Anzioni announced you were to be embraced. First, of course, you had to marry a proper Giovanni spouse, and produce a few kids. The intended and you met on your wedding day, and it was hate at first sight. once a few babies were born, you were made a full Giovanni vampire, while your spouse received the proxy kiss from their father, the local don. In theory, a vampire outranks a ghoul, but your "beloved" has their father wrapped around their finger. Your spouse could have you destroyed anytime they wanted, and never let's you forget it. Sooner or later something's gotta give. Both of you are reaching the point where murder seems the best option.

                                        Kiss of Death (5 pt merit)
                                        Your clan flaw works a little differently from other Giovanni. When you feed from someone they feel intense pleasure, just like any other vampire, but they are thereafter doomed to die. How it happens varies, but once you have fed on someone they must make a Stamina + Occult roll, difficulty 6 every day. If they ever fail the roll, they will die in some bizarre accident within 24 hours.

                                        Sympathetic Necrophilia (3 pt flaw)
                                        Someone you loved dearly died, and you just can't let go. You keep their corpse in your private chambers, so their wraith will remain nearby. This might be... romantic in nature if the beloved was a spouse, but you are as likely to have a room full of toys and candy for your dead child, or watch tapes of your dead grandmother's favorite soap opera with her. Living members of your family have tolerated this behavior, barely, but if anyone outside the inner circle of the Giovanni were to find out, you would likely make the national news. Then you would be able to spend more time with your beloved... as a wraith yourself.

                                        the Marilyn (1 pt flaw)
                                        You were raised fully aware of your family's supernatural affairs, so you never felt the need to posture or act strange. In fact, you seem downright normal. You wear chain bought clothes, drive a sedan, chat in a pleasant manner and, being so normal, you have even been assigned to serve as a liaison to the normalcy obsessed Camarilla. The only problem is, you were, in fact, raised in a family of necrophiliac mobsters, and sometimes it slips. If you ever fail a social roll in a scene where you were trying to act normal, it becomes a botch as you accidentally reveal some truly horrific aspect of your family, because you thought it was completely normal. Maybe while discussing Christmas memories you mention the Dunsirn's recipe for meat pies. Maybe when a neighbor's grandmother dies, you ask whether she has contacted you yet. And didn't everyone fool around with their dead cousin as a teenager? Whatever it is, the mask can't hold forever.

                                        Mundane (1 pt merit)
                                        It's a little surprising you got the embrace from your family because you have no interest whatsoever in the occult. You have never seen a ghost, heard moaning, or seen a ghastly vapor. In fact, you suffer a minor derangement which makes you rationalize away any evidence of the ghostly afterlife. You understand, of course, that you and your family are vampires, but you think they are just deluded about ghosts. On the other hand, you know it's rude to contradict someone's beliefs, especially someone as dangerous as the Anzioni. So, you keep your mouth shut. However, you can never learn Necromancy or Thaumaturgy, and substitute any one common discipline for Necromancy as an in-clan discipline. Celerity, Fortitude, or Presence are the most common choices.

                                        Death Mage (5 pt merit)
                                        The Giovanni started out as a family practicing ancestor worship and the magic that flowed from that. This has taken the form of the Necromancy discipline, but could as easily taken the form of ancestor worship related Thaumaturgy. Your sire is a Premascine who, in fact, followed this alternate path. You have the standard Giovanni weakness, but your clan disciplines are Dominate, Potence and Thaumaturgy. Because of the very small community of Giovanni thaumaturges, your choices of paths and rituals are quite limited (ask your ST for details), though you can learn Necromancy rituals as Thaumaturgical rituals.

                                            Lasombra

                                            I did it myself! (1 pt flaw)
                                            Having no reflection is not an issue at all! You can cope by yourself and you don't need any ghouls or whatever to help out. Basically, you do your own hair, make-up by using light sources and guesswork and decide how well your clothes work by looking down. You are not very good at it. To be frank: You are a never-ending source of hilarity and joy to everyone around you. All social rolls based on your superficial appearance are at a +2 difficulty since you are more clown than vampire.

                                            STOP IT! (3 pt flaw)
                                            The use of Obtenebration has done something amazing! It breathed life into your shadow. While it is still shackled to you wherever you touch the ground, it can move on it's own accord. Too bad that it turned out to be a complete and utter tool! Your shadow will move around you, do offensive gestures at people and generally make your life miserable by misbehaving. It can't actually touch or influence things, but it will do it's best to make you suffer. It will still submit to your Disciplines and you can use "Shadow Play" to keep it in line if you possess the skill, bu tit can be a pest.

                                            Lasombra antitribu

                                            Crown and Scepter (3 pt. Merit)
                                            One of the Blue Bloods had extensive interest in you. Maybe he admired your will to resist the Sabbat's call or maybe he thought it would be fun to have his own pet Lasombra around. Whatever the case, you were given your own small Agoge, Presentation and every other tradition of the clan. You guys just kept the whole "not a Ventrue by blood" to yourself. Everyone treats you as a member of the Ventrue clan and the sheer notion that you are not is considered a humorous absurdity.
                                            If it ever gets out though, you and your adopted Sire will be in deep trouble.

                                                Caitiff

                                                Social Caitiff (1 pt flaw)
                                                Mechanically, the embrace worked fine and you are not a Caitiff. Your sire, however, didn't stick around to explain things. So, although you have your clan's normal three disciplines and weakness, neither you nor anyone else knows it. You will be treated as a Caitiff, until such time as you can get your clan to accept you by producing incontrovertible evidence that you are a clan-mate (and buy off this flaw).

                                                Mutation Caitiff (2 or 5 pt merit)
                                                Mechanically, you are not a Caitiff, but rather the founder of a new bloodline. Pick the clan of your sire, and change up to two of the three clan disciplines to other common disciplines, and work with your ST to design a bloodline flaw, which should be similar to your sire's clan weakness and the equivalent to a five point flaw. Any childer you sire will likely belong to your new bloodline. In the five point version, your sire's clan either hasn't noticed your mutation or doesn't care. In the two point version, they know and have disowned you, albeit on good terms.

                                                Cleaver (3 pt merit)
                                                When you were embraced, you thought it meant you would lose everything, but it hasn't. You still live in the same house, have the same spouse, you found a job you can do at night, the kids are growing up faster than you could imagine, and the rabbit hutch out back is never over-populated. Life is still so normal, it's sometimes surreal. All backgrounds you take must be in some way related to this "normal life". Regaining lost Humanity only costs current rating x1, and needs little more rationale than that you are living as a normal human. If, however, any member of your family is hurt because of your vampire associates, you must check Humanity as if you committed the sin yourself.

                                                Recent Embracee (5 pt flaw)
                                                You have not been a vampire for very long. In fact, you were embraced in the past 24 hours. You have no idea what is going on, and are entirely dependent on the other PCs to navigate this world. You have no dots in any disciplines. Once you learn about your new reality and buy off this flaw, you will quickly learn the first dot of four common disciplines. (Note that you may or may not be an actual Caitiff, but until someone sees some evidence of a clan affiliation, they will assume you are.)

                                                Hidden Caitiff (2 point flaw)
                                                You and, possibly, your sire have realized you are a Caitiff, but, out of fear of censure, have not told the rest of "your clan". You have learned appropriate disciplines, how to fake the weakness, or have acquired a flaw similar to your sire's clan's flaw.

                                                Still Virile (3 pt merit)
                                                You are a normal Caitiff in every way except one: your sex life. Nothing has changed. Everything still works as fine as ever, and you even capable of having children, all of whom will be born Dhamphir.

                                                Disowned by Lilith (7 pt flaw)
                                                Your embrace, in some way, offended the cosmos and the Powers That Be have denied you a portion of your rightful power.
