    Regional Setting - Mid-Atlantic

    07-10-2023, 09:59 PM
    Heya folks! Some of y’all may remember a few months ago where I started a thread about Regional Settings, New England in particular. That went fantastic imo and it was a great time for me and really helped me flesh out a bunch of ideas. I mentioned in that post an interest in doing other regions and I figured I might as well try ti sketch out the next one. I hope y’all find it interesting!

    The Northeast Megacity by Night - Cities of Blood

    •What I’m interested in with this thread is a region of the continental US called the ‘Northeastern Megalopolis’, a term to describe a grouping of cities in the Mid-Atlantic region of the U.S. (roughly the area from New York in the North to DC in the South). This area contains some of the largest cities in the United States: from its northern extent it reached Boston & Hartford (which for our purposes are more accurately part of the New England region) and to its southern extent it reaches Richmond & Norfolk Virginia (which for our purpose are more associated with the former territory of the so-called Southern Lords). Between those boundaries we have: New York City, Philadelphia, Baltimore, Washington DC, as well as Wilmington, Newark, Trenton, etc. To the east there is of course the Atlantic Ocean (and if one were to follow that they would find connections in Western Europe) and to the west one has the Mid-West/Great Lakes Region (which for the sake of this will include Pittsburgh, Buffalo, and the like). All told, in 2020, nearly 50,000,000 people live in this region.

    •The scale of this region in its cultural and literal footprint is truly staggering and honestly each of the major cities in it deserve their own personal treatment. However for the sake of argument, let’s try to consider the broad strokes. With this region we have the naval melting pot & cross-section of the US Colonial project with some of the most important cities of the US (NYC, Philly, DC) being here. This is the (in)famous ‘East’ referred to throughout the Western genre; the cities which would become whirling pools of Finance, Industry, & the Masses. From the Colonial Period through the Revolution through the Civil War and the Gilded Age, Progressive Age, World Wars, Roaring 20s, Great Depression, etc. The sheer amount of history, culture, and politics which one can draw upon for inspiration staggers the mind

    •What does the above mean for the sake of Vampire? Well for one I think that, while both New England & the South might both have their own claims to as much antiquity as can be found in the Americas, the Mid-Atlantic is where that historical connection to Europe via the Atlantic most clearly became concrete in the forms of political, financial, and cultural power - throughout US history European visitors have been able to find the most close approximations of their own venerable cities in these bastions on the Atlantic Coast, in particular New York City. What I imagine then is that historically these cities would be where the children of the European elders (and perhaps even elders themselves) would arrive in the New World, something which would probably be true regardless of sect. For instance in the 19th century I can imagine the ancillae Childer of Europe setting up in the financial strongholds of these cities (Camarilla), the myriad strains of heresy churned in the ethnic slums of the gangs, prisons, docks, & urban machines (Sabbat), and with Anarchs & Independents flowing freely in between

    •By the modern nights (at least of WW’s original publications) the images surrounding the great cities of the Atlantic Coast did not much resemble the grandeur of either the Gilded or Progressive Ages, having far more of Gotham about them than of Metropolis. It was in this vision of the cities, remembered in the 1970s & 1980s in particular, that the blasted out urban hells where the Sabbat haunted were imagined. The city of the Warriors, Escape from New York, the Son of Sam, etc. were the cities of the Sabbat with only the few enclaves of financial, cultural, & political power (such as Manhattan, DC and a few Downtowns perhaps) being imaginable as Camarilla. I mentioned in my New England post that I consider these cities to be the best example of the ‘Status Quo’ of the Sabbat in North America in the same way that the Great Lakes/Mid-West is for the Camarilla (& the Anarchs*)

    •So the question arises then for us to consider imo: when and how do we think the transition from Camarilla to Sabbat would best take place and how do we imagine the contrast between the Camarilla & Sabbat eras being recommended. One idea which I’ve toyed with comes from something pointed out by No One of Consequence with regards to LA. To paraphrase: the noir LA of the 1940s & 50s seems better suited to the Camarilla than the Anarchs and so perhaps the date of the Anarch Revolt ought to be drawn backwards (my preferred candidates being ‘63, ‘65, or ‘68). To carry this forward I imagine that by 1968 these revolts will have spread across the nation (and much of the Western World) and while victories would be had at the heart (California) much of the rest would be defeated (including Lodin’s famous handling Chicago Nights of Rage). It’s in this same spirit that I imagine the backlash coming, a backdraft of sorts. A repetition of Anarch history: the failed revolts and the desperate flight of survivors, veterans, and chaff generated from all sides becomes the kindling for the Sabbat - in this case a massive wave of Sabbat crusades beginning in ‘69 (on another note, I also think that this is a better moment to have the Third Sabbat Civil War occur). These waves of Crusades, fueled by the instability of Camarilla domains and an injection of veteran recruits would see great successes for the Sabbat: including, namely, the Mid-Atlantic strongholds.

    •What we find in the early publications of the modern nights is the aftermath of these Crusades: Sabbat largely running the board in the Northeast except for a few enclaves such as Manhattan, Princes-in-exile, and the barely maintained security of DC (maintained in no small part by continuous contests in Philadelphia and Baltimore). While we would canon books for NYC & DC, here are my two cents for the other two: I view Philadelphia as being the military HQ (ie: Black Hand) of the North American Sabbat and a major nexus for coordination of Crusades with the theoretical tradition of Philadelphia as a city of ‘Brotherly Love’ and ‘Revolution’ being frequently invoked for the Sabbat’s own purpose. Baltimore meanwhile I see as being a semi-constant battle ground, one of the places one might find a Camarilla War Lord active, for the main reason that it’s assumed that resistance from Baltimore is all that stands between the Sabbat and DC (prior to the end of Vitel’s charade anyway).

    •Obviously these cities have changed a great deal from the early 90s and that needs to be addressed as well, but for the sake of this post I’m going to focus on what we had at the inception of the WoD

    So, all that being said, what do y’all think? What do y’all feel this region should look like? What inspirations would you invoke? How does the Mid-Atlantic affect the geopolitics of Vampire in your view? Do you like canon or would you rather see a few revisions made? What are y’all thoughts? I welcome you all to share!

    Thanks for reading!

    *I imagine that the Anarchs as the archetypal rebels within is what we see represented with the Great Lakes and Chicago by Night while the other side of the coin for Anarch archetypes was to be found in (Southern) California

                  Did some more thinking about this and I looked at an overview of New York City & Philadelphia in particular. My reason for that is that Boston & Hartford to the North would make more sense to be talked about re: New England, DC & Baltimore to the South May make more sense to be talked about along with Piedmont and the Southern Lords and Pittsburgh & Buffalo to the West may make more sense to be talked about with the Great Lakes.

                  Looking at NYC & Philly both cities follow a roughly similar historical trajectory:

                  •New York gets founded as New Amsterdam by the Dutch in the early 1600s and for a time it along with New Sweden disrupted the possibility of a contiguous Anglo Eastern Sea Board (until in the 1660s after the Second Anglo-Dutch War New Amsterdam became New York). New Jersey would be part of New York’s balliwick as well. Not long after Pennsylvania would be founded by the Quaker idealist Penn in the 1680s with Delaware entering its orbit (and a couple amusing territorial skirmishes with New Jersey)

                  •New York & Pennsylvania go through the Colonial Period as something of a relative backwater compared to New England & Virginia, at least until the mid-1700s. 1750 would be around the time that Philadelphia overtook Boston as an Anglo port city in the Americas. This is roughly contemporaneous also with Ben Franklin and that part of the pre-revolutionary period so we can say then by the early-mid 18th century the Mid-Atlantic region was picking up some weight

                  •The Revolutionary Period is interesting because it carries on with that interesting reversal of early colonial history of Philadelphia arguably being more important than New York City. As far as the Revolution goes it seems fairly clear that Philadelphia was Boston’s main rival as a Revolutionary power center whereas New York City would be held by the British. The importance of Philadelphia would stick around after the war too, being a temporary capital. New York City wouldn’t overtake Philadelphia as the major port until the 1790s, around the time of the Quasi-War with France. This would also be something roughly contemporaneous with the Whiskey Rebellion/Shay’s Rebellion which could be useful ST fodder.

                  •After 1790 NYC overtakes Philadelphia and the cities take on a relatively quiet early US historical presence it seems. Early industrialization happens, the War of 1812, and the Hartford Meetings which, if they had gone through, may have made New York the northmost part of the Union. Could be an interesting alternative history scenario. Anyway that aside the Eerie Canal in 1825 was a big deal for western development/industrial development which helped push NYC further above Philadelphia along with being a center for Hamiltonian financial projects

                  •A relevant period to mention here would also be the massive immigration booms to the eastern United States from the 1840s & 1850s onward. You’ve got the Famine Irish along with the 48’ers from Germany, Poland, Italy, Russia, etc. This leads into the Tammany Hall/Gangs of New York era of the city. Lead up to the civil war, Boss Tweed, know-nothings, the Burned Over District, the Mason Plot, Dead Rabbits, etc. You get the Civil War and the Draft Riots but even more immigration after all that. The Reconstruction Years lead into the Gilded Age and eventually consolidation of Manhattan, Brooklyn, the Bronx, Queens, & Status Island by the end of the 1800s

                  •After the Consolidation you have the Progressive Era which you could probably sketch from 1898 to 1919-1920. You get Teddy running around and all the anti-corruption campaigns trying to dismantle Tammany Hall & the Spoils system and make a city for the respectable middle sections of society. You’ve still got plenty of Gilded Age corruption and industrial disputes sure, but maybe things are changing. Plus World War One happens.

                  •After the Progressive Era we’ve got the roller coaster from the Roaring 20s to the Great Depression to World War Two. You’ve got the Great Gatsby, Prohibition, the Harlem Renaissance and the Mafia Golden Age running up against Hoovervilles and then the plunge into the Second World War.

                  •After WWII and the Post-War Boon it seems like the only part of New York City which was on the rise was Wall Street which received the dubious distinction of overtaking the City of London as the headquarters of international finance. Meanwhile the rest of the city outside Manhattan began the course into ‘urban decay’ which would define the image of the cities (The Warriors, Escape From New York/Son of Sam, etc.) in the minds of the writers who presented us with NYC & Philadelphia as the Sabbat Strongholds of the Mid-Atlantic

                  The reason I’ve given that cursory overview is that I’m not certain what we have in the way of canon material best suits how these cities could be used in the WoD over the course of history and I’m wondering if others had any thoughts on that subject? That said, I’m also not certain how to define Philadelphia’s role

                      So now let me try to take a look at this in the terms of Vampiric history:

                      •As we know the War of Princes took place in the European Middle Ages which in the WoD culminated with the rupture into Early Modernity as the (First) Anarch Revolt erupted alongside the Inquisition (set in the backdrop of the Renaissance, the end of the Hundred Years War, the aftermath of the Black Death, the fall of Constantinople, and on the cusp of the European discovery of the Americas). We see the Camarilla and Sabbat take shape along with the Camarilla-Sabbat War of the 16th century

                      •While the Camarilla-Sabbat War rages in Europe (probably in Italy for large chunks of it) we see plenty of ambition/desperate Sabbat haul themselves into Iberian ships to follow the course of Spanish & Portuguese history across the globe. This includes all the Sabbat who accompany the Spanish into the Caribbean, Mexico, & Peru as the Sabbat joins a cataclysmic rupture of any pre-existing vampiric traditions and fuses themselves into the continent for centuries to come. Presumably the Camarilla makes attempts to grasp domains in Mexico, Peru, etc. but that’s discussion for another day.

                      •The early colonial ambitions from England in the 17th century make their way to New England and Virginia and are probably backed by Camarilla, albeit perhaps young Camarilla seeking opportunities outside of Europe (so probably a good few fuck ups and quasi-Anarchs). The specifics of how those early attempts go are more appropriately covered with the survey of New England or the Piedmont but they probably involved some amount of patronage from the power players of Britain and contact with the Native-Sabbat hybrid vampires of the Americas

                      •This period also gets us into when the Dutch, still technically waging their war for independence from Spain and also arguably putting together the first engines of what would become Capitalism, get themselves set up in New Amsterdam. I imagine that these early Dutch vampiric settlers would be few and probably of the type described briefly above: autarkis abandoning European vampiric society (esp. Gangrel & Nosferatu) alongside disgruntled fuck-ups, outlaws, or the occasional ambitious jet setter (probably a good number of Brujah in their number). These early vampires are probably of what I call the ‘New Camarilla’/Quasi-Anarch ilk of vampires who are too young to recall unlife before the Camarilla but old enough to realize that their pre-Camarilla elders aren’t giving up the good seats in Europe (and once the Camarilla-Sabbat War settles into it’s comfortable stalemate there wasn’t any new domains opening up in Europe either - so go west young ancillae). The Dutch Young Camarilla has to not only deal with natives and Sabbat but the possibility of Anglo Interests trying to undercut the Dutch interests backing their play.

                      •Which, as history should have it, is exactly what would happen. Around the 1660s with the aftermath of the Anglo-Dutch War (and with the Thirty Years War & English Civil War in living memory) the English take New Amsterdam & New Sweden for the Empire (becoming New York & New Jersey respectively) with Pennsylvania to follow in a little under two decades. So we have an interesting pivot point. The eastern sea board is now continuously Anglo from Nova Scotia to New England to New York & Pennsylvania to the Piedmont. I think we can safely assume that from the perspective of the Jyhad this was caught up in a lot of Anglo-Dutch Elders struggling and eventually having enough successes pile up for the English to let them put Anglo New Camarilla interests into key position in the Mid-Atlantic, albeit certainly a few Dutch & Swedish New Camarilla survived the transition. That said we also have a question to deal with here: this is when the book ‘New York by Night’ proposes that the Sabbat strike and claim New York, presumably leveraging disgruntled Dutch forces & the Spanish Elders who may yet be using them as Catspaws. Now New York by Night implies that from this point onward the Sabbat rule is largely contiguous up to the Modern Nights. I have another idea:

                      •In the 1660s-70s the Sabbat do in fact claim New York and establish it as a foothold of concentrated power in the Anglo Americas. The way I see it the fact that the mid-Atlantic is the straggler behind New England & Virginia makes that thematically viable until the 17th century. I’d say that Pennsylvania probably goes a similar way. Albeit the first few decades it may have been protected by whatever forces were backing Penn’s ambition (Awakened?) after Penn’s demise and in the early 1700s I see the Sabbat of New York sweeping Pennsylvania, New Jersey, & Delaware along with them

                      •However what I would propose is that this would change in the 1700s as well. By the 1720s-1740s I see a new generation of Anglo New Camarilla interests (perhaps even a few who were involved in taking New Amsterdam originally) trying to recoup the Mid-Atlantic and using Philadelphia as a staging ground. I’d say that by the Franklin years and the rise of Philadelphia to pass Boston, the combination of Anglo-American & Anglo Jyhad Players could arrange the ouster of the Sabbat from Philadelphia. Although perhaps one wants to stretch things out and have this plot not fully come into fruition until the US Revolution starts to kick off. One has a few thematic ways one could go with Philadelphia as being either Sabbat or Camarilla but I do think that the British Martial Law during the Revolution is a great moment to have the Camarilla re-establish itself in New York City and to have it entrench itself amongst the Hamiltonian Federalist period while Sabbat remnants raised hell in the western section of the states

                      I hope that this was coherent and people get what I’m getting at here. I’m curious if others have any thoughts or ideas of, if you were gonna sketch out this period, the type of characters and plots you think would make sense to be moving around in it

                              I definitely agree that the Sabbat who are going to be involved in the early history of the region would hardly resemble their modern counterparts (or even contemporary European counter parts). In fact this period is early enough that we might see first generation emigre Sabbat who hid in Spanish boats in the early 1500s and weren’t around for the Vestegir Reformation, meaning that while later waves will be influenced by Vestegir and some of the early emigres will get influenced by Johnny come lately priests, you may still have a strain of ‘Ultra Loyalist’ or maybe ‘First Sabbat/Adamic Sabbat’ who have survived in the Americas through a combination of wildlife predation and both poaching from native populations as well as using them as a body of new recruits and local expertise. Now by the the time that New Amsterdam has become New York iirc Vestegir will have come and gone and the Camarilla-Sabbat War on the continent is cooling into stalemate, so the Ultra Loyalists that still exist are probably a vanishing minority.

                              I’d imagine that many of the early instigators of the Centralization Missions of the Sabbat would have limited impact on the Atlantic Seaboard until we get to the Sabbat Civil Wars. The Black Monastery, the Paths of Enlightenment, The Inquisition & the Black Hand all most likely had involvement from plenty of the elders who went Sabbat for Gehenna Cult or anti-Camarilla reasons and would want those tools along with the Ecclesiarchy to help them entrench themselves. I generally imagine that those movements would be more active in places where Sabbat Elders had to compete with newly Camarilla Elders over sectarian domain control (ie former Lasombra & Tzimisce domains in Europe especially and probably Mexico, Peru, French Canada, etc. after the initial colonial rush when the Camarilla would inevitably come to try to get their foot in). Those forces would probably be reaching out more towards Virginia and New England at this point, so I can imagine most pre-Revolutionary War Sabbat in the Mid-Atlantic being still on the Loyalist side of things.

                              I also agree that at this point the numbers are small. Sectarian conflict such as it exists probably resembles small groups who collectively claim relatively large areas and are private and/or highly mobile. This is an era where active Sabbat can probably be one pack for large areas and can be reasonably imagined to have destroyed whatever New Camarilla vampire set up camp in the domain, allowing them to de facto run the territory.

                              Something I imagine was fairly common in most of the American frontier was a dynamic that went something like this. Highly mobile Sabbat or Anarchs, either individuals or in small numbers, would follow migration patterns and seek to survive amongst the new settlements and wait for them to grow (eaking out an existence either by hunting animals, maintaining small herds, operating as bandits, remaining unseen, etc.). The de facto control of territory probably went to whoever could simply destroy the other or could locate their day-time haven. However while all this is going on the established interests are making investments. Most vampires know that their ambitious childer would probably be destroyed by the vampires who can survive and thrive in frontier life, so you usually have to wait some decades for the settlements to build up. In the mean time you have your proxies invest like how humans would. Silent partners, stock, proxies, interest holders, etc. The frontier Sabbat/Anarchs/Autarkis can serve as de facto princes/bishops for decades but eventually the question becomes how do they respond when the stock holders try to leverage their assets onto the board.

                              Writing this out and thinking about it, it kind of reminds me of the Hearst dynamic in the first seasons of Deadwood. You hear about the Hearst mining interests and suspicion of its agents is present but the locals are still de facto in control of operations on the ground while it prepares to make its claims.

                                      Taking Black Fox’s point about the early colonial period and the limiting effects of the population, I think it’s relatively safe to assume that much of the de facto territorial control of cities in the Mid-Atlantic came from the sole vampires or packs that held sway. We don’t start to get really big population numbers until the early-mid 1800s.

                                      So prior to that point I’d roughly sketch out a timeline of individual New Camarilla vampiric representatives that hung around until the 1670s and early 1700s when the Sabbat was able to drive out their literal presence (if not their influence over material institutions). This remains the case with Sabbat packs having de facto territorial control of the region through much of the 1700s. I do think that New York City changing hands for a few decades, with the Camarilla taking de facto power back during the US Revolutionary War works thematically although I’m less certain what to do with Philadelphia in that same time period. Perhaps during this period we see the precursors contemporary Anarchs who would arise most notably with the French Revolution take de facto control of the city during and after the Revolution, given the First Sabbat Civil War’s effects.

                                      Anyway the first few decades from the Revolutionary War still have fairly small numbers and relatively weak albeit rapidly developing points of influence. I think having the Sabbat recede from the cities and return to activity in the western frontier as the Civil War rages makes sense, perhaps tying them into the Whiskey Rebellion. Meanwhile Hamiltonian-Federalist Financial interests seems prime fodder for New Camarilla with Anglo connections to hold the Mid-Atlantic for the first few decades after the Revolution. I’d say roughly from the Revolutionary War up through until the Andrew Jackson period of the 1830s.

                                      The First Sabbat Civil War would last roughly from the end of the US Revolutionary War (contemporaneous with Shay’s Rebellion) until the signing of the Purchase Pact in 1803. So in that period of stronger Federalist power I’d characterize the domains as more strongly New Camarilla with ties to the Elders of Britain, with a contained Anarchic element desiring a separation from Anglo Camarilla influence. A good point of transition then might be the rise of the Jeffersonian Democratic-Republican period. The Louisiana Purchase seems to be a clear counterpart to the Purchase Pact of the Sabbat, suggesting perhaps that many Sabbat after the Civil War used western expansion as a release valve for the still highly loyalist packs that may otherwise undermine the increasingly conservative ecclesiastical interest in established-entrenched Sabbat domains.

                                      The years of the Jeffersonian interest up through the Jacksonian interest are an interesting place to have a pivot in the American Camarilla I think. I imagine that much of the Revolutionary era & Hamiltonian-Federalist New Camarilla still felt attached to the influence networks of the British Camarilla. However I think the Revolutionary tendency and the sheer distance would become fodder for a group of ‘Anarch’ New Camarilla who desired an independent influence network from the British Camarilla. This see these kindred as backing the Jeffersonians and riding the resulting wave to build walls between their domains and British influence. This tendency may have reached its highest point in the period surrounding the War of 1812 - the notion that, riding the waves of Revolutionary upheaval coming from France, the American Camarilla could support a maneuver to cleave off Canada and further entrench a bulwark against British intervention in their affairs. Obviously this was dramatically unsuccessful. It might be an interesting moment to do stuff with Montreal and whatever the Canadian Camarilla are up to though.

                                      After the War of 1812 and the end of the Napoleonic Wars, with the Bourbon Restoration on the Continent freeing up the British Camarilla, I think we see the Mid-Atlantic Camarilla go increasingly onto the back foot. The failures create an opportunity for the next generation of ambitious New Camarilla to try their hand. Which, after a decade or so of struggle, I think they do.

                                      The 1830s are pretty interesting. You have the end of the Regency/Georgian Era in Britain and the rise of Queen Victoria. You have the July Revolution of 1830 in France which once again topples the Bourbons. In the US you have the rise of Jacksonian Democracy & the Party System alongside a major population boom, backed in no small part by ever-increasing waves of Irish & Germanic (among other) immigration. This is a period which I could see going a number of ways for the Mid-Atlantic.

                                      1. The waves of immigrants carry with them large numbers which can be used to support a Sabbat insurgency (perhaps in the wake of a Anarch coup attempt). Ireland & Germany both have plenty of Catholic enclaves which in the WoD are often Sabbat coded which could provide a pillar for the Sabbat insurgency to retake the cities. These factors would only continue in the following decades as the Irish Famine & the Revolutions of 1848 drive increasing waves of immigration into the great eastern cities. I think the orthodox position of the WoD is that this is in no small part how the Sabbat can be justified to hold the eastern cities throughout most of the 1800s.

                                      2. However I think another option is possible that could be a bit more interesting than a simple Sabbat take over in the 1830s-1840s fueled by boatloads of Irish Shovelheads. I’d sketch out something like this. The end of the War of 1812 & Napoleonic Wars put the Atlantic New Camarilla of the Jeffersonian era on the back foot and a new crop of ‘Frontier’ Anarchs & New Camarilla crop up (bear in mind that in this case western New York & Pennsylvania state could be arguably called ‘Frontier’). The Eerie Canal & Albany Junta crowds begin to prepare a seizure of the domains from the old guard, a move which they can finally accomplish by the late 1830s with some backing from the British Camarilla. By the 1840s the Atlantic Camarilla is weakened by their birth struggle but in power. However this is also as waves of immigrants are providing a smoke screen for Sabbat & Anarchs to enter the city. The Atlantic Cam knows that Britain wants to keep them weak so as to better use their influence so they need some way to create facades. Enter the controversial secret pacts which would form the Tammany Hall years. In secret meetings with both Anarch & Sabbat leaders the Atlantic Camarilla would negotiate a power sharing agreement. The Sabbat would not be recognized of course and low level street fights would occur to keep up appearances, but hidden Sabbat churches throughout the boroughs would be ignored and Sabbat recruitment tolerated. The Institutionalized Anarchs would provide legwork for the Atlantic Camarilla by giving outlets for young vampires unwilling to join the team and also serve as a bulwark against the more Radical Anarchs (Hopefully this ‘Anarchism’ and ‘Socialism’ stuff will just be a passing interest…). The Atlantic Camarilla will work to prevent themselves being coup’d either by the Anglo Cam, the Yankee Cam, or the Dixie Cam and will provide the others institutional cover from the legitimate financial and political sectors. This power sharing agreement would be more or less in the interest of every party, preventing any one from being able to claim sole power and preventing external interests from taking the city. It would probably be able to survive for most of the 19th century but by the turn of the century I think it would be on its last legs and ready for a new rupture.

                                      So those are some of my thoughts on what the Mid-Atlantic Domains may look like from the Revolutionary period through the end of the 19th century. Any thoughts or alternatives are welcome as always.

                                      Thank y’all for reading!
