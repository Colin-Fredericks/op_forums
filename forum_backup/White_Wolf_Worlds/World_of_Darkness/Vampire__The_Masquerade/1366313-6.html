                                I’m mixed on the Hecata like a lot of things and if I ever get away from school enough and back to writing things up for my 20.5 thread I might go into greater detail but here’s my general thinking and what I like to do with them.

                                I’ve always thought the absence of Giovanni Antitribu was odd and that the sheer variety of bloodlines of Death were interesting. Here’s how I’ve taken the Hecata as an inspiration for my own games.

                                The Bloodlines of Death and several of the Giovanni families have long chafed under the supremacy of the Venetians and feared the independent clan crushing down on smaller groups to maintain their monopoly over Necromancy which makes them so viable in the kindred ecosystem.

                                The thought of these small groups forming a coalition for mutual aid, benefit, and security made sense to me. Sure not everyone would join and their would be divides but a common enemy can do great things for collective unity. Plus if you added some Giovanni Antitribu into the mix (those who disagreed with the Plan, those who disliked the family, minor families, Cappadocian-sympathizers, etc) and perhaps a few mysterious remnants of the Old Clan of Death you might have something really interesting.

                                Not only do you have a political instrument that can be used as a counterweight and source of tension with the Giovanni as the Harbingers of Skulls already proved with the Sabbat that the sects will happily welcome corpse-faced outsiders if it means gaining the benefit of access to necromancy without dealing with the Venetian monopoly. A coalition of necromancers could operate in secret, offering their services to any sect, and gaining the benefit of gainful employment that is the backing of that sect against Giovanni reprisal which would offer yet another weight of security.

                                It also adds more spice to a Giovanni game imo. With competition in the market the Venetians have to play differently and theirs more fertile soil for narrative conflict and tension to develop and flourish.

                                I personally do the Hecata differently then V5 does (and I’m not sure about the name, I kinda think something like the Cthonics or Skull & Bones might have been better) but I think the concept is inspiring and has a great deal of potential.

                                                        Originally posted by marin
                                                        Have you read the KotEK chapter in Beckett's Jyhad Diary? Because it pokes and prods at Cainite myth, suggesting that blood-drinkers have been around for all of human history, beyond the timespan of the Book of Nod, that some vampires are self-created, that the Western clans are spawned of elders who refused to relinquish their blood and their power to their juniors...
                                                        Yes but the spawning of the Wan-Kuei and Kindred are completely different. Wan-Kuei were never Vampires to begin with, they were proto-Imbued sanctioned by the August Personage of Jade itself and only became what they were in a series of degenerating events until they were cursed. There were about 10,000 of them all at once and all other new Wan-Kuei thereafter came out from the Middle-Umbra back to their bodies after escaping Yomi. They start off weak but get ludicrously stronger as the ages go on.

                                                        Vampires came from one person who was cursed from one event, being cursed by the God from Biblical lore (not the August Personage of Jade). Thereafter he embraced others to becoming cursed with him which caused generations. Each embrace after makes each subsequent childe more weaker in power as the ages go on. However this can be thwarted by the childe committing diablerie to gain power lost from being a high generation.

                                                        These two splats are nothing alike. They just have a colloquial naming scheme in the form of "Vampire". As a matter of fact, Wan-Kuei at Jina level (5) Dharma (which takes 10 years tops to get to) don't even gain essence from the blood anymore but rather the breath of the potential victim.
