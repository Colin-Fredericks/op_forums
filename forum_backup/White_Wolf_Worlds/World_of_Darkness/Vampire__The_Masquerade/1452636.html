    TC, part 15: worst timing ever, COVID.

    06-17-2021, 11:34 AM
    Last part's here. Two years later...

    We had a brief hiatus while players were busy, played something else in the meantime, then managed to get the next story -- you all know the one -- started just in time for COVID. I mean, it's not as if we had to suspend TC for almost a year and a half, halfway through probably the most important story in the chronicle, at one of the moment critical moments of that story...that would have sucked.

    Excuse me while I return to weeping uncontrollably in the corner.

    First things first, roster recap and player change-ups. My Nosferatu player had to drop the game and so did my Toreador player, real life considerations both, and in return I picked up one of the old vets from my local group -- one of the guys from back when I ran through TC as a player. So, that brings us to the current PC's: an Assamite sorcerer, a Brujah scholar, two Gangrel (one with a penchant for finding creative ways to run away from nasty conflicts, and one that's more socially focused that doesn't run away from problems), a Lasombra abyss mystic, and a Setite warrior who's gone a little loopy in the noggin thanks to eating a Malkavian way, way back in the very first story.

    The Brujah's being played by the new old guy, and his PC is entering the game as an Anarch with an infamous sire (the Brujah Prince from early in the chronicle). I suggested this NPC to him as a sire as a way to get him into the chronicle easily and painlessly...then I realized this NPC has an unbroken lineage that goes through, of all fucking vampires, Dominic. So guess who's starting play with Clan Enmity (Ventrue) and a suggested Mentor...Ecaterina.

    I mean, it's not like the conspiracy of ancillae haven't spent the past two centuries systemically pissing off the Ventrue in every conceivable way, nor been working with Ecaterina for decades to advance the Anarch cause within Transylvania. And even before that, they certainly weren't Jurgen's patsies for fucking over Bulscu and playing an instrumental role in the downfall of both elders, setting them down this eventual path of rebellion against the clan of vampires that attempted to use the conspiracy as disposable minions in the Jyhad.

    They say lightning doesn't strike twice. I say, "they" haven't run a TC game where one of the players wanted to start with a 7th gen Ventrue with a prestigious sire, and later in the chronicle a new player rolled in with a Brujah with an infamous sire at the cusp of the Anarch revolt.

    That out of the way...the past downtime was more letting the pressure cooker build steam, with Europe in slow recovery from the Black Death and the sociopolitical landscape shifting in between mass migration, aftershock outbreaks of plague, and the impending collapse of feudalism. Persecution of key groups continues, especially with the immigration of the Roma and the Ravnos with them. For vampires, the plight of neonates and ancillae don't improve; despite the plague subsiding, the food supply is still short and fights over herds wages stronger than ever, with well-entrenched elders' might fortified by ongoing continent-wide embrace of Tremere as part of court life.

    The Tremere, of course, bringing useful means to avoid the Inquisition and plague, as well as keeping wayward lessers under control, to the table. Of course, the Tremere are out of the frying pan and into the fire. The Usurpers weathered the storm of the Massassa and Omen wars, but even more trouble is on the horizon care of the Ottoman Turks, and the Assamites who act as the vanguard for the Ashirra that accompanies its mortal counterpart. It's doubtless many of which remember well the Salubri, and seek to repay blood for blood.

    Trying times for any Transylvanian prince. Let alone one who, not eighty years prior during the plague, ousted Nova Arpad and seized Praxis over Mediasch with the aid of Count Radu and his get. Nor that prince's conspiracy, who are of course the PC's, with the (social) Gangrel holding the bag as Prince: not that there was any real debate or power struggle over who'd hold the title, he was just the only one willing to step up and proclaim himself #1 on Nova's shit list. The only other PC who'd be even willing to hold a title at this point being the Lasombra, who (wisely) decided to step back from matters of overt politics, having secured his position among the Amici Noctis having learned the Tremere's secret behind resisting the Inquisition, and sharing that knowledge with his clan.

    Thus, for the PC's the downtime was comparatively quiet. Largely thanks to the...suspiciously...competent leadership from the Gangrel prince. Who's been experiencing blackouts of increasing frequency and duration since the plague, during which time he skillfully averted the worst of the Ventrue's and Tremere's wrath through intricate, behind-the-scenes manipulation of Anarchs and Tzimisce alike, while simultaneously forestalling attempts by the Tzimisce to take what they view as rightfully theirs. In fact, under the stewardship of this Gangrel, Mediasch has become Transylvania's greatest hub of nascent Anarch activity, and a key locus from which Anarchs can and must strike across all Transylvania at once...when the time is right.

    Also Hardestadt proposes an alliance of Princes called the Camarilla, gets eaten by some Brujah, and [Lasombra] gets whacked. But those are minor problems for other people half a continent away.

    So anyways it's 1413, and the Gangrel prince has decided now would be a good time to host a tourney to promote unity and cooperation amongst the Cainites of the Siebenburgen, and to renew the bonds and vows shared by those who (nominally) make up the...defunct for all intents and purposes...Council of Ashes. As word goes out, Lucita and Anatole show up, begging for hospitality as usual which the Lasombra PC happily grants, and they share news and opinions of, y'know, practically every domain in western Europe seemingly self-immolating as neonates and ancillae have finally had enough of the elders' shit. Sure is good to have the quiet, easy unlife in good old Transylvania, isn't it?

    So, then Vykos shows up.

    Vykos has never been formally part of the Council of Ashes, but they've been intertwined with the Council's fate since the 12th Century, so of course Vykos isn't resisting an opportunity to show up and fuck around some more. Vykos' reception is predictably icy, and Vykos spends some time asking rather pointed questions out of not-at-all idle curiosity, like for instance whether the Lasombra PC is still in possession of the Libram Terram and if it's being kept well and secure. Of course, Vykos couldn't resist asking the Lasombra his opinion of the destruction of his clan's Antediluvian...nothing suspicious at all about an Anarch Tzimisce asking an Anarch Lasombra their thoughts on an Antediluvian's destruction, in the same conversation in which a book that potentially records the location of an Antediluvian's resting place was explicitly mentioned.

    At this point, Vykos knowing shit Vykos shouldn't know and doesn't really have any business knowing is just an accepted fact of unlife, so of course the Lasombra lies his ass off and wastes no time making arrangements to have the Libram moved to an even more secure and secret location.

    Oh, but Vykos' whistle stop tour of annoying PC's isn't over...yet. They request a solitary audience with the Gangrel prince, and the Gangrel accepts. Vykos uncharacteristically gets to the point, as Vykos has been watching the Gangrel's actions and wants to know who among his clan the Gangrel really is. At this point the Gangrel blacks out again, and comes to standing covered in blood in front of Vykos' rapidly-decaying corpse.

    At this point, Vykos faking his own demise and/or using body doubles when appropriate is just an accepted fact of unlife, so of course nobody entertains for a moment the idea Vykos actually died and gets on with it, with players' attention diverted well away from what Vykos might have said to trigger such a reaction in the PC or why that specific chain of events might have happened.

    They also catch word a gypsy caravan is on the road approaching Mediasch and being followed by Inquisitors. A Ravnos by the name of Anasztaz precedes them, introducing himself to the Setite in secret, claiming to have information and items of value to the conspiracy to trade should he and his camp be granted succor from the Inquisition. Of course the Ravnos' reputation precedes them, and in a city under the praxis of a Gangrel, what happens next is all but a foregone conclusion: the Roma (and Ravnos) are told to get fucked.

    During the day the Inquisitors attack the gypsy camp, burn it, and kill everyone within it. RIP. But at least they didn't attack Mediasch, right?

    The PC's go to pick through the wreckage of the camp, and the Toreador (one of the players who had to drop) at least managed to find Nova's pectoral -- the one that gets important later in the chronicle. They had a good, hearty laugh when they realize it's the same pectoral Ruxandra had given them in the very first story as an introductory gift to Radu -- Nova managed to lose it again -- and returned to Mediasch.

    That's where the game session ended...you know, last year. Before lockdown put the game on hold this entire time.

    So yay, when the chronicle restarts in a few weeks I need to spend a game session or two getting the players back up to speed before finishing the story. You know, the one where [Tzimisce] "dies".

      Behind-the-ST-screen notes...

      I said here a long, long time ago I thought it would be fun to play with nudging the players and PC's in the Camarilla's direction, putting them in the position of vampires at the cusp of being elders, who are -- for better or worse -- viewed by others as having been embraced with silver spoons in their mouths for having domains practically handed to them, and managing to sit pretty in their castles while the world burns around them. Then the perfect storm of "yeah they're going Anarch" dropped in my lap so hard it left my head spinning as to how I'd even rationalize that in the first place.

      First, I had a PC who played Jurgen's childe. Then, I had another PC who wanted to set up a drug smuggling ring through their shared domain, because y'know, Setite. As if that wasn't enough, I had a third PC playing a Giovanni who decided, of all things, he should become BFF's with fucking Vykos. As soon as Jurgen found out about the Setite's drug route, Operation Fuck Bulscu practically wrote itself and ultimately set the PC's down the path they're still on.

      Since it's been a while, a recap: the Setite ended up muscling out the competition and became Geza Arpad's hookup for keeping Bulscu stoned off his tits. The Setite wasn't aware to whom his drugs and slaves were going, and ended up telling the Ventrue PC which means Jurgen found out. Jurgen added two and two, and sensed a power play in the making; Jurgen gave the green light for it, but Dominated the PC's to believe it was their idea to fuck over Bulscu and the Arpad Ventrue, and they were acting completely of their own volition.

      Except, Jurgen didn't successfully dominate all the PC's. Some remembered what had actually happened, and managed to convince the others of the truth -- it was Jurgen's plan all along. So naturally the first thing the Giovanni PC does is barter the information to Vykos, and Vykos goes off and barters the information in turn to Nova fucking Arpad. Little picture, Vykos gets land concessions and boons out of it; big picture, let's not forget Vykos has a methuselah in his head with a teeny-tiny bit of a grudge against Jurgen.

      Knowledge of Operation Fuck Bulscu makes its way to Hardestadt, and that level of Dignitas-forsaking buggery is the end of Jurgen. Not so much that Jurgen did this, of course, but that he was caught doing it. And it's the end of the Ventrue PC, as a PC; she's still around and kicking, doing other things, but as an NPC and she'll be back later.

      In the meantime, Nova decided to try to blackmail the Lasombra PC to give her the Libram Terram, on threat of telling everyone about Operation Fuck Bulscu. Nova wanted the Libram to give to the Tremere, and she wanted to see the PC's grovel and be generally humiliated because...well, Nova Arpad. Yes, Nova Arpad thought it would be a good idea to threaten a Lasombra with revealing his role in the downfall of a massively important Ventrue elder.

      And that's how the Lasombra PC got his offer to join Amici Noctis. His test was to figure out how the Tremere were managing to dodge and divert the Inquisition where the Lasombra were failing spectacularly; it took him almost a century to do it, but he managed to get the information during the Black Plague and spread the word among the Amici. The Lasombra can't do much with the information, because it's down to the Tremere using Thaumaturgy to fuck with Inquisition's bureaucratic methods and paperwork, but at least they know.

      So...what's up with the Gangrel PC punching way above his weight class as a Prince? Several stories ago, they ended up being chased by the Inquisition into Ilias cel Frumos' former domain, and made contact with [Tzimisce] during the time they were there. The two Gangrel PC's were unwise enough to make contact with the Antediluvian's blood, and [Tzimisce] took it upon itself to hitch a bit of a ride. The PC's blackouts are simply due to the torpid Antediluvian taking the steering wheel here and there, quietly manipulating things to make Mediasch a central hub for the eventual Anarch Revolt when it comes to Transylvania.

      And being the current year is 1413...well, you can add two and two where this is going. Yes, I actually sent the Gangrels' players a fully marked-up Antediluvian character sheet and said "you're playing this for the rest of the story". Well, not an Antediluvian exactly...

      But close enough.

      Can't wait for the look on the other players' eyes when I tell the Gangrel players to attack Lugoj.

      Now that elephant in the room has been addressed, what was up with Vykos? Vykos was savvy enough to figure out the Gangrel PC was punching above his weight class, and assumed an elder Tzimisce had killed him and took his place without the rest of the conspiracy being none the wiser. Vykos was right...in a manner of speaking...but his impertinence and lack of etiquette in being so standoffish in another Cainite's domain got him a nice little time-out care of Antediluvian Claws.

      Vykos has little bits and pieces of him out there ready to be revived (I love Body Impolitic so much) so he won't be around for a while, but Vykos has learned a valuable lesson in not being an asshole. Of course it won't be a lasting lesson, but the most important part here is I've foreshadowed events that will come to pass in future stories. No, Vykos' impromptu projectile pee-pee won't be important in this chronicle at all.

      [Further note: the literal first thing I thought of when reading Body Impolitic for the first time was Vykos' penis. I thought for the longest time Hardestadt kept it, but in BJD it's alluded that Rafael did. Either way, a founder of the Camarilla kept Vykos' wiener as a trophy, and that's what matters.

      So, hear me out: what if Vykos used Body Impolitic before the meatus yeetus? Vykos would have had a spy-penis in the haven of one of the Camarilla's founders, and therefore able to eavesdrop on their most sensitive meetings, learn their deepest secrets, and discover their best-laid plans...for centuries. Not only is it hilarious, it's fucking devious beyond measure and absolutely, positively something Vykos would do.]

      So, last key thing of note...I should have expected the players to tell the Ravnos to fuck off. Really, I should have. I've been playing with these people nearly twenty years. But for some reason, I didn't.

      Yes, I had to struggle to find a way to sneak that stupid pectoral back in some way. Yes, they're getting The Treatment. Yes, it's going to be bad since they got an entire clan of Roma murdered, including a couple important Ravnos. And they're going to have to deal with that at the same time they're dealing with the Anarch Revolt, peasant revolts, the Turkish invasion, and a bunch of really pissed-off Assamites running buck-ass wild through the countryside.

      So, the next story..."how 'bout another joke, Mur-ray?"
