    Again, you seem to be taking the orthodox Church of Set view about this. "THEY'RE DOING IT WRONG!"

    You just don't believe the dissidents exist versus thinking they should be stamped out.

    Quite a few of these faiths are older than Set as we know it. Set is not really an "old" Egyptian god. But yes, basically I see plenty of Setites indoctrinate people into their faiths and then these people either reject it later or just flat out lie about accepting it. We have plenty of signature characters who talk about their faith in the Midgard Serpent or Loki while basically thinking Set is just irrelevant.

    Of course part of the problem is a lot of these faiths are polytheist and the Setite Aeon talk is nonsense to them since their "mask of Set" doesn't have a Satanic adversarial relationship to the gods.

    Re Baali: The lovecraftian lot shouldn't be the whole clan.
    -Thralls serving a demon master who wants a cult because of course it does. Such Baali often tangle with other Baali and infernalists who serve different demons.
    -folks who want nothing to do with the bloodline or demons and really try hard to hide their identity. Think of nosferatu Cleopatras. Unfortunately the writers of late seem to be under the misguided belief that Baali are the vampire nephandi or BSD and you need to be willing to get your soul inverted... nah they're just another bloodline that have a link to demons.
    Baali make great anarchs.
    Eh, Baali look at Infernalists and think of them as poseurs. It's ignorance of infernalism by outsiders that the Baali are the "Infernalist" clan. The Sabbat have many more infernalists than the Baali and there's undoubtedly many Tremere who regularly deal with demons in a transactional fashion.

    Baali looked down on these guys as Hot Topic Goths versus their hardcore Punk.

                                                            The Sabbat would always beat the Anarchs. They've got so much more cohesion. Your average anarch would be thinking *It's not on my territory so I won't do anything till it is* and they'll get swallowed one by one as a result.

                                                            Now, as to the Sabbat having the most blood diversity...
                                                            Yeah, of course they do.
                                                            They win wars, they press gang anyone, and those people embrace more into their ranks.

                                                            The Camarilla is built from the best clans for the modern world; Bar dementation They don't have clan specifics, they have three broadly applicable disciplines that let them fit a variety of roles, whilst, say, Obtenebration might be powerful in warfare but it's pretty shitty for nightly living. Generalists in nature are just way more successful than specialists.
