                    I gotta say, I find the "Paragon" merit to be pretty dumb--and I don't usually poo on things in such explicit terms. It's not so much that I don't think it's useful (though at 7 points, I question the value proposition of same), so much as I think the underlying conceit for its existence is dumb--i.e., that there's a generational limit on non-supernatural backgrounds.

                    Folks, let's face it: Vampire aren't real in the real world--but multi-billionaires absolutely are. So why would I should a I need a merit (which specifically operates on the premise that I'm "compelling") to accumulate that much wealth (Resources)?

                    Generational limits should be imposed with regard to improving traits to superhuman levels, or should otherwise only apply to traits that are themselves categorically supernatural. Resources, allies, contacts, influence, mentor, resources (especially resources, which can be inherited without a lick of sweat, charisma, or effort)--these are all things that regular ol' people in the real world we inhabit can and do develop to levels that would be deemed "six dots" and above.
