        I remember something writer Bruce Baugh mentioned around 25 years ago during a discussion about PENTEX, about how in many ways it is/was the result of writers who thought corporations and big business were bad, but at the same time had almost no understanding of how said corporations actually worked or what motivates certain negative corporate behavior, and the end result is something as shallow in its own way as the old Captain Planet cartoon (in which environmental pollution is almost entirely the work of cartoonish supervillains who are literally just doing it for shits and giggles). And I think the early Technocracy is something similar, just involving 80s counter-culture ideas about the military industrial complex and corporate Wall Street.
        And as shallow and silly as some of that early material was, I do at least credit it with sparking a desire to learn more about how things like industrial mining, hospital management, mass media, and the like work, including the tradeoffs between positive and negative impacts.
