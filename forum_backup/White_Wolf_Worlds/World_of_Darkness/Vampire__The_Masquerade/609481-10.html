                Thanks for posting these. They give a really nice insight.

                For those wondering this is the text in question;

                Mortal history records a time, beginning over two thousand years ago, of burgeoning empires locked in combat with one another - the time of the Persians, the Greeks and the Tartars. Thus did the Fifth Generation establish its own order. Meanwhile, the Antediluvians lay hidden and pursued their own mad schemes. This age of wars may even have been of their making, the beginning of their great Jyhad. Whatever the truth, almost none remain to speak of it. I myself have met only one of the Fifth Generation, and at the time I did not know it.

                It is said by some that near the end of this period, the Antediluvians emerged from their hiding places and sucked the blood of all my kind, each leaving but one new Progeny of their line. This legend has it that this was the close of the Second Cycle, that the Antediluvians' lust for blood was so great that they needed all of my race as their Vessels. Those who believe in the Cycle legends predict an Armageddon in the near future.

                They say that the Antediluvians are asleep now, but someday they will awaken and then they will feed. The Third Cycle is coming to a close, and none but the Third Generation will remain alive at its conclusion. The true believers say that each Cycle lasts 2300 years, and soon, very soon, the time approaches. They call it Gehenna, and some prepare for it fervently. As a man of science, these beliefs seem extreme to me, but they cannot be entirely discounted.

                Some of the big metaplot advancements were...
                * Gehenna started then stalled to a stop.
                * Week of Nightmares happened.
                * Cam couldn't deny Antes anymore so they now have a party line of 'Antes are benevolent mothers and fathers!'
                * Sabbat suffered heavy losses due to Cam and hunters; they have lost lots of territory and are on the 'hide, investigate, and recover' aspect, rather than RAR BLOODY MONSTERS IN THE STREETS.
                * The Giovanni broke The Promise and created the Independent Alliance with the Followers of Set.
                * The Gangrel have returned to the Camarilla but hasn't been granted full Pillar Clan status.
                * The Assamites are recognized as a Pillar Clan, with their own Justicar (Tegyrius) and founded the Josians, the anti-infernalist Archons.

                                                    I see that a lot of the early posts in this thread are looking forward to a major revision of the rules but seeing that Onyx Path JUST kickstarted V20 Lore of the Clans I can understand the frustration. M20 is a lot more forward thinking concerning metaplot and evolved many of the organizations within its part of cWoD and I can see that V20 could use a similar update. W20 and M20 aren't really simply "nostalgia products", they are updates to their lines. It would of course be fun to see the same for Vampire.

                                                    I however think that that backwards compatibility with V20 is paramount. Sure, rule updates and a continuation of the metaplot is one thing (I strongly agree with the critique of "Soft Gehenna" but whatever), but VtM4 probably couldn't contradict or make obsolete new or coming books like Lore of the Clans, Rites of Blood or V20 Ghouls. That would be crazy.
