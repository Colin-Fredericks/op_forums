Especially the Cappadocians (the Hecata are comprised of like five different bloodlines when originally the whole clan except for the Giovanni were supposed to have been wiped out in the purge).

                                        Giovanni are also inspired by the Borgia's from Rome. They share the...incestuous thing. And the love for plot an poison.
                                        I'd say they mixed the Borgia's decadent relations, the Mafia's structure from Siciliy, the Venetian merchant's culture, with a bit of financial side from the Medici's family from Flourence.
                                        That said Revised edition did a good job creating strong connections with the Etruscan and Roman (as in Ancient Roman) necromancy.

                                        (And yeah, being an Italian from Rome I can confirm that Italians do love the Giovanni. But I also understand that for an Italian-American from the other side of the ocean it could reinforce some bad stereotypes about italian descendants).

                                        And while I have huge problems with V5 metaplot...I like the Hecata, their development and all, for two reasons:

                                        1) It was long in the making of the metaplot. Unlike many new things of V5 , it didn't come out of the blue. The notion that there were far more Cappadocians in the world than originally tought was already clear withe the Harbinger of Skull and the Infitiores survived in Egypt (they are a big thing in Cairo by Night). Same as the Giovanni structure crippling (the failure of the great plan after the fifth Maelstrom is a focus of Clanbook Revised). V20 did also a lot of work to bridge these events to the V5, through the revelation that the Cappuchin was a shared identity by Lazarus, Japhet's ghost and Byzar, and the matter of the rebelious lesser branches of the family (Beckett's Diary).
                                        So...it worked, it wasn't really a strange thing happening without reason. And more important: it expaned possibilities.
                                        You still can play Hecata like Giovanni, they are still there, only the higher ups got eaten. But you can play them also like a more Cappadocian mystical clan, or a Voodo Samedi cult. More possibilities, not less. In this regard the operation is kinda similar to what happened to the Assamite in Revised edition. Yeah, you can still play the assassin zealot, but you can also play with the Camarilla, or full indipendent and so on.

                                        2) Onyxpath is a better writer, and developer, than the other studios who worked on V5. And Hecata are fully developed by Onyxpath.

                                                See, as annoying as it were to have the Giovanni arbitrarily doing their own thing distant away from the rest of kindred society, the old way of doing things was really great for Samedi/HoS/Nagaraja/Other Remnants/Loose necromancers (Do I include the Kiasyd here?) because of course these guys would serve themselves and the option for little-underdog-necromancer made for good fun RP wise.                                                 Even agreeing to be represented as one clan to the Camarilla would be a stretch since a hypothetical "Hecata primogen" would very likely favour their bloodline over the others who are direct competitors. Remember, Necromancy is potentially very profitable but it is a niche. Even when they all had different disciplines, the Sephulcre path and a few common rituals is like 80% of why a non-practitioner would want to treat with any of the corpse-fucker varieties. A practitioner's services increase in prestation value when there's nobody else, and decrease in value when other necromancers are around. Smart Giovanni-Nagajara relations would likely boil down to "become my slave and hand over the virteous path and I'll let you live" and something like the Hecata isn't going to alleviate that.

                                                    Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
                                                    Come the Hecata and everyone who can see ghosts is going to arbitrarily join the big club at the expense of the freedom they had as independents?
                                                    Freedom was not the right word.

                                                    1) The Nagaraja were a broken Clan after the fall of Enoch. They lost their main sect (The True Black Hand) and were in exile. Most of them had to go to South Asia, were the Kue-Jin imposed them a draconian peace treaty (safe harbor, but no right to embrace).

                                                    2) Same goes for the Harbingers of Skull not aligned with the Sabbat. The nuking of Enoch left them without a home and without political clout.

                                                    3) Samedi were little fish in a sea full of sharks. Their land, the Carribean Sea, was under Setite's yoke. And plenty of big sharks (Camarilla and Sabbat) set their sight to those lands. They were already looking for the protective umbrella of the Camarilla.

                                                    4) The Infitiores where confined to Saqqara, and they needed the Setite help to survive.

                                                    5) The branches of the Giovanni's (Dunsinr, Milliners, and so on) had to find a new support after the collpase of the Giovanni's power structure. Otherwise they would have become minor players, like the Revenant families. And that's the best scenario. In the worst they would have been destroyed, like the Pisanob and the Hidalgo.

                                                    So, most of the little necromantic groups had a very precarious situation and where on the verge of crisis at the time of Revised.



                                                    I don't imagine how the Nagajara would even recieve the invite.
                                                    Nagaraja, being a big part of the True Black Hand, had a lot of dealings with the Byzar, in the persona of the Capuchin. Also, the Harbingers of Skull who are not part of the Sabbat, where members of the True Black Hand. So it probably was something like "Pssst...we are jumping boat! Are you in?"
