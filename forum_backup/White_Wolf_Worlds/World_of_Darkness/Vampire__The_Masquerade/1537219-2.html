    Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

    It's not V:TM if it's Gehenna.
    I agree, but V5 is Gehenna.

    As much as they tried to downplay it and backpedal a lot of things, V5 is Gehenna, and that's why they should have kept investing in V20 in parallel, as two storylines that diverged under the plot power of the Red Star.

    The problem is that V5 tried to be many things, but those things are irreconcilable. Two of those things, being a new setting and being the continuation of legacy, we had good lines already doing. Lines that could do wonders with the money they were denied.

    One third thing they proposed right off the bat to be: the continuation of the plot picking from Time of Judgement.

    Back in the day, if they had decided to pick from BJD and not streamline the intended modes of play, maybe V5 could have been a new edition to V20. But since in the end that was never the intention, the next best way for V5 to thrive is to embrace itself and be its own thing.

            When V5 first came out I quite liked it, as someone who was never old enough to play VTM but dived into VTR, I actually liked how it borrowed elements from both. I also enjoyed the Camarilla and Anarch books and you can see clearly the passion and themes that they're trying to put forward their. The books feel like work of arts, which doesn't help their layout granted but did help to get me into the feeling of what those games were doing.

            After that,the Renegade books have just been more game books than anything. A collection of generic stats I'd expect in an old 3.5 book with some generic advice you can apply to your home setting. That's not what I wanted from VTM, I wanted the world of Masquerade, the world of Bloodlines etc that I loved and wanted to expereince.

            For me W5 was the real turning point, reading the Tribes and how watered down they were, there's literally no reason to have that many tribes or even be a part of the tribe now.

                    Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

                    Fundamentally, I think there was a change behind the scenes that must have happened or there was a shift in priorities somewhere. For me, in retrospect, I was never a fan of V5 so much as I was a fan of what OPP and certain authots (MD for example in THE CAMARILLA) did with the setting. I kind of have to wonder how much of it was them, to quote Blade, iceskating uphill and trying to save as much of the old WOD as possible, though.

                    The Fall of London, Chicago books, and Cults of the Blood Gods books struck a good balance between old material and new material. It felt like a genuine continuation of the Old Line and that they were respecting the necessary struggle between getting new players involved in the game while attracting the old ones.

                    The new books just don't feel that way and seem actively hostile to the old setting and characters. The loresheets felt like a nod in the right direction but even they are now disappearing from the backs of the books.
                    I still play V5. but I haven't bought most of the new books. (I bought Bloodstained Love because I think it's an interesting concept and a lot of people were very dismissive of it.) This is mostly due to as you said: There's this odd hostility and dismissiveness for lore. From the Player's Guide telling you no one cares about your loresheets but you, the DoC loresheet and Gargoyle reference that feel like they were thrown out so older fans would stop talking, and the general way I speak to some V5 fans. They often seem...upset that you like/want old lore. I'm a newer player, but even I get vitriol for saying I like legacy material. I've never experienced this in any other community, and as much as I hear about "grognards", it's usually among them I'm being told what I enjoy makes me a miscreant. It is quite off-putting. I love all the books you mention here, and even though Fall of London is a mess, it's an interesting mess. I'd rather have something with a weird idea that's interesting than something safe and boring.

                    There was definitely a shift in regards of what they wanted with the lore and metaplot. Tis sad, but I'm just reading through old books now for ideas and enjoyment. And I do have all the V5 Onyx Path books. Sorry your love has been crushed, I've watched you champion this edition for years, and I've seen you chatting around elsewhere. To a new player who has similar feelings: Ya ain't alone.
