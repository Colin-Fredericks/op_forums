        As far as Elder PCs go... I'm not opposed to it. I wouldn't allow PC Generation to go lower than 8- after all, older editions made Generation an Advantage you had to buy, and IIRC that was capped at 5 dots- and set the max age so that Elder Characters can't have been older than neonates when the Convention of Thorns went down.

        From a gameplay perspective, I think 9th generation hits the sweet-spot of giving the PCs way more power than most of the other local Kindred, but still not being the biggest, baddest players in town, but they aren't engaging in the same petty errand-running that a PC coterie of fledglings would. A fledgling game plays very differently from a neonate game, plays very differently from an ancilla game, plays very differently from an elder game.

        Mechanically, drop the PC humanity by another dot, give them a couple more dots to allocate Advantages and Flaws, and then give them 80 XP to spend on whatever they want.

            We've been told by the line devs that WW has no plans for traits to go above 5, and we've been told by line devs that there won't be Sabbat-playable anything in the PG (and the words 'too Sabbat' were mentioned when it was said in an interview that the Tzimisce were kicked back for rewrites and might not be in the book due to that, so we know that's a true statement).



            I did some numbers calculations in a discussion about this the other day, and largely the scale down is an issue of how the dice function. I think, even without a Resonance, I ended up with 31 dice for a full-powered BP8 vampire with an assumed stat/skill/Discipline of 7. Even with stats capped at 5 it's still crazy (Toreador with BP 8 and Awe active, assuming max traits and Presence 7: 5 stat, 5 skill, spec +1, Presence 7, DPB 4 = 22. If Torrie blood surges that's 26 dice. If Torrie has Striking and it applies, that's +2 for 28. Not to mention anything else you could attempt to try to stack on it from Loresheets, Resonance, etc.)

            And that's not even accounting for general scale overall, such as Potence above 5; with Potence 6 you need 2 margin to outright kill/torpor anyone that doesn't have Fortitude. Elder Brujah with Fist of Caine, Potence 6, Strength 5 Brawl 5 Spec +1. That's 11 dice to do agg, and you only need 2 margin over your opponent to outright destroy a character with Stamina 5 (as 8 is the maximum health levels).

            What elders need is either 1. something locked to being purchased via high BP (an option I'm not too fond of, but will entertain ideas on) that needs to not just scale upward and stack on more dice adders, or the ability to purchase more powers, especially with V5's design focusing on breadth of powers rather than height (which is my preferred option for elders).

            ​
            Wait the Tzimisce were too Sabbat? What were they expecting?

                    The easiest way to decouple the Tzimisce from the Sabbat, IMO, is to have them come to view the Sabbat as a failed experiment. The Tzimisce were always the spiritual pillar of the Sabbat. If you think the senseless sacrifice of the Gehenna Crusade was a hit to the morale of the pragmatic Lasombra, imagine how devastated the Tzimisce are: dozens of Sabbat dying to subdue a single Methuselah, only for that Methuselah to subsume the will of the poor sap granted the privilege of diablerizing it. The Sword of Caine is devolving into a horde of mindless beasts. This isn't evolution, this is banal regression and subjugation to the Elders of the worst sort imaginable. The dream of the Sabbat has become a nightmarish parody of itself.

                    The Tzimisce who have not turned into Methuselah meat puppets or wights pack up and flee for their ancestral homeland of Transylvania. The Fiends try to collect their thoughts, and it isn't long before bitter recriminations start to get thrown around. Who should roll up but, you guessed it, Dracula. He lays the blame for everything at the feet of Kupala: he posits that Kupala's breaking of their blood bonds was false hope, a plot between the demon and the Eldest. Desperate for revenge, the Tzimisce join Dracula in tracking down and destroying Kupala's physical form. Dracula absorbs a bit of Kupala's essence, and he is declared the Voivode of Voivodes, the supreme leader and ultimate authority of the newly reunified Tzimisce.

                    Politically, I can actually see the Tzimisce favoring the Anarch Movement: I have trouble seeing members of the former Old Clan accepting the idea of somebody having authority over them (besides Dracula) as would happen in the Camarilla, and the former Sabbat Tzimisce will probably choose the Anarchs as a "next best thing" kind of situation, and maybe even try to steer the Anarchs back towards the ideals of the First Anarch Revolt. In any case, I can see the Tzimisce harboring a nice healthy grudge against the Lasombra for siding with the Camarilla, and for turning on their former allies so readily when it suited them, even if a Fiend does decide to join the Camarilla as well.
