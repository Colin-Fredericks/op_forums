    [Dark Ages] Can you be a Vampire in "sunlight"?

    05-26-2024, 09:18 AM
    I'm working on a Dark Ages character: a Cainite on the Path of Community.

    My concept is about a vampire pretty renowned in his village, maybe he's an apothecary or some kind of healer and it's not unusual for people to seek for his help even though they already know he is actually a vampire.

    Do you think this concept can work?

      It would have to be a pretty unique village, but they can all be his herd and followers (also check out VDA20 Companion for the Populace background), so they have a reason not to deliver him to the Inquisition.

      A low gen Cappadocian with The Corpse in the Monster 5 would be particularly suited, since he could be active during the daytime on some days. Medicine and Hearth Wisdom would be the leading abilities, and do check out VDA20 Tome of Secret's section on witchcraft for some details on making drugs and poisons and reproductive health.

      EDIT: as an addition: Cappadocians are particularly notorious for working closely with small mortal communities like monasteries and small towns, and for producing more ghouls than other clans. So it would not be an unusual set up for a Cappadocian to have a whole small village as his retinue, with several ghouls and therefore his identity as a vampire being barely - if at all - concealed.
      Paired with a Lamia it would be a fun duo: she brings disease to those she feeds from, while he keeps them healthy with outstanding healthcare and ghouling. I just need to check whether the illness brought by the Lamia's bite can be cured with Medicine rolls.

          Originally posted by Hermeticus View Post
          I just need to check whether the illness brought by the Lamia's bite can be cured with Medicine rolls.
          It is not mentioned whether it is treatable, IIRC. In older editions it is said to be 'much like' the Black Plague, which would mean no cure but the treatment of symptoms would enhance the chances of survival, if not by much. In VDA20 it deals one Lethal damage per night for two weeks, so strictly by RAW it should be survivable if that damage can be out-healed.

          Cainites living at least semi-openly in secluded communities during the Dark Ages isn't unheard of. An alternative to the philanthropic approach is having the character be local nobility who is powerful enough that even though the peasantry knows, they turn a blind eye out of fear. Those are mostly your Tzimisce voivodes.

          Both variants always carry the risk that eventually someone from inside the community will leak it to the church, no matter how thankful or afraid they are.

              Yes, the concept can work even if I could see two "issues" with it.

              One is that the Cainite in question would be pretty stationary to reap the rewards of his place in that village and that a whole coterie may be more Cainites than that village can support.

              The second is of course that the village would need to be pretty insular/isolated/xenophobic/whatever since if all the people in the village knows, its just a matter of time before someone slips a hint to an outsider that could lead to trouble, if they have much contact with outsiders to slip such hints from the start.

              Now playing a single Cainite in a village, which can't really hope for outside help in cause of problems, and dealing with issues that comes up, could be fun and offer its own scenarios but to me this sounds like a solo play character.

                      I think the most viable way is a hybrid sort of character.

                      Imagine a village which is a little bit remote but not totally disconnected from the trade lanes. The entire population knows the stories of the forest witch who lives near the community but apart from it. If you are brave enough to enter her section of woods or climb to her mountain cave etc. then you can speak with her. She might have great wisdom or insight, she could give you potion that heals you or makes you stronger, she might teach you forging techniques from a long-fallen empire, she might come to the village to slay the raiders which are pillaging it.

                      The village elders know the truth of the matter that the "forest witch" is in fact a guardian vampire who was once a member of the village and has stuck around. Hence why they always suggest you bring a live goat or something similar as "payment" just to listen to your problems. The witch in the woods thing can work even better if the vampire has access to Animalism which allows it to keep track of what has been going on in the area, and make the villagers think that every animal in the area works for the vampire.

                      Depending on if the vampires Animalism or Protean is high enough, there could be plenty of stories of an animal familiar or a shape shifted witch doing things in the village like outing a liar, leading lost children home, chasing off predators like bears or wolves.

                      Being part of the community as a supernatural is possible, it just requires a bit of public relations management.

                                  There is a much more likely and plausible play option than Golconda: the vampire character becomes a Background NPC, and the player character is their ghoul or revenant.
                                  Your interactions with kine become a lot easier, and the role stays basically the same. Perhaps you are a sorcerer or witch who agreed to serve in exchange for knowledge in the arcane arts. While you are handling the townsfolk, your master can stay low and focus on their studies, but is still there if some crisis calls for it. You just have to keep the Herd happy.

                                  Of course, that may defeat the point of playing an overt vampire, but it might be still interesting to play from a third-person POV, so to speak.
