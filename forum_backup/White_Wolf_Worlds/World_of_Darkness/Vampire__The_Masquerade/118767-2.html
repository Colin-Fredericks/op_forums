        I find the Lasombra Antediluvian very fascinating.
        It's so obvious no one really knows what It was all about - even Montano confesses to be ignorant of Its history and motives in the Lasombra Trilogy. And yet we know it to be a creature of such great passions and intense desires that overshadow (no pun intended) any rational approach to It.

        It codified strict laws for It's descendants. It was known to be a wanderer, roaming the world. It's progeny never seemed to live up to Its standards. It often ruled directly over It's offspring, serving as a great Patriarch, the final authority of it's line. It was so intimately connected to darkness that all names and titles people gave to It reflected that (for example the King of Shadows).

        So what if the line of Lasombra was supposed to be the kingship Clan, the heirs of Caine. Imagine Lasombra being embraced by someone of the Second generation as a tribute to their Dark Father. Driven by passions, ruling by strength, crafting a kingdom within darkness.

        The Lasombra Clan symbol is a black crown after all, their ancient symbol for their claim to the legacy of Caine. The Ventrue Clan symbol on the other hand is a sceptre, which in this theory reflects the Ventrue originally intended as a Vizier line.

        Then on the other hand, like has been pointed out by MrAshur for instance, a case can also be made for connecting Lasombra to Lilith (Queen of Night, connected to waters, growing through pain, etc).

        I like the Lasombra antediluvian, there are so many directions you can go with It
        I have been thiinking all day about the ideas above. I am thinking of a "friends of the night" within the friends of the night, low-generation Lasombra who ensure the Clan and Sabbat alike continue this tradition of leadership from the shadows. It's about having power without visibly wielding it, of being the invisible hand that manipulates the Clan, Sabbat and Camarilla alike. The kind who cultivate a young protege, creating childer so that promising young "Erebus" can advance in generation and power. They ensure a hidden hierarchy of low-generation childer cultivated solely by merit and usefulness. It's better than any blood bond! A pure meritocracy with the reward being Generation.

                    I've written a scenario for playing (unnamed) third generation Vampires through several important scenes, such as the rebellion against the second generation and the founding of the Second City. So Caine, instead favoured others of the Third generation, including the Ventrue Antediluvian, and the Second generation were influenced to do the same. In some sense the Jyhad among the Antediluvians may have started here, about the mantle of Caine, shaking the Second City after the murders of the Second generation.
                            The Lasombra Clan symbol is a black crown after all, their ancient symbol for their claim to the legacy of Caine. The Ventrue Clan symbol on the other hand is a sceptre, which in this theory reflects the Ventrue originally intended as a Vizier line.
                            That made this sort of pop out of my head.

                            Dearest Veronica;

                            In our last conversation, you had commented on the ring I wear, and yes indeed it seems curious for a Magister to wear an inverted symbol of our Clan. You have been diligent in your studies it would appear, and friends often notice what others do not. Gunnar would be proud, had he survived, and so now it is time to learn and choose.

                            The name we use does not matter at this time, as you can learn these details later. What is important now is that you know the truth about your history. The Noddists are fools, scrabbling over scraps of second hand stories from those who never knew the whole truth! You have come to me, and for the first time you are ready to understand your conceptions of power are insufficient.

                            You are a slave, Veronica. Although you are a member of the 9th generation you have not achieved a fraction of the potential that you are capable of. The Camarilla prattles on about diablerie and do you know why? Because it stops vampiric evolution. You are aware that your Potence or other powers will reach a finite limit, your fine intelligence or other abilities can only advance so far. You are aware that elders can be smarter, stronger and wield powers you cannot command. Too powerful to be a servant, but not powerful enough to be a master.

                            And that is why the crown is inverted, Veronica. It lies waiting to be picked up. Few realize the crown is our symbol because we were the lords of the 1st city. We imposed laws upon our kindred, ruling openly but also through the shadows if intrigue and influence when rulership was inappropriate. But always the blood, always one must have the power of the blood.

                            You recognized my ring because Gunnar taught you well. I have decided to invest in you, dearest one. I have a childe, I shall command him to embrace two kine, and they shall be for you. You will rise to the 7th tomorrow, but your education will begin tonight. I know you search for an Ancient, and I will help you. We will find it and bind it, and Embrace childer and rise in generation if fortune favors us both. Others will help that I will introduce you too, teaching secrets and powers that as yet are unguessed to you and their aid shall be rewarded as well.

                            You must choose, dearest Childe. Will you accept our aid, joining us as we seek Apotheosis together? Do you have the courage to take the road to becoming an Antediluvian yourself? Or were you unworthy after all, and need disappear into the ash heap of unpitying history?

                            I will have your answer now. Turn and answer, as I am here in the shadows.

                                                        I think there were only seven antediluvians. In the Enoch, the earliest sects were the Cainites and Lilitu. This philosophical division was strong enough, that it caused the clans to split, much like how the Sabbat antitribu feel culturally separated from their parent clan. It happened so early in the clan histories that these antitribu divisions became codified into completely different clans.

                                                        Arrikel's clan would split into the Toreador (Cainite) and Brujah (Lilitu). Ishtar and Arrikel are alternate names for Ilyes and Troile. To the Brujah, one of their own diablerized the founder. To the Toreador, their founder was slain by an outsider, but over time they've become confused as to which one.

                                                        [Lasombra]'s clan would split into the Ventrue (Cainite) and the Lasombra (Lilitu). Mithra's early death by Troile would simply transform him into a creature of shadow.

                                                        Malkav's clan would split into the Tzimisce (Cainite) and the Malkavian (Lilitu). One embraced chaos physically, the other mentally. The elder's mind resides in the madness network, and his body resides under NYC.

                                                        Absimiliard's clan would split into the Assamite (Cainite) and the Nosferatu (Lilitu). One would hunt monsters, and the other became monsters. The Niktuku are actually the remnants of the proto-clan, sharing aspects of both Assamite and Nosferatu.

                                                        Set's children were never swayed by either Caine or Lilith; instead they embraced Set's own teachings (Setite). Eventually, they withdrew from Enoch's politics completely.

                                                        Ennoia's clan would split into the Ravnos (Cainite) and Gangrel (Lilitu). The week of nightmares in 1999 affected the two clans differently: the Ravnos violently self destructed while the Gangrel are slowly withdrawing from the world, forsaking friends and allies, to dwindle and vanish, to eventually go the way of the Salubri and the Cappadocians.

                                                        Ashur's clan would split into the Salubri (Cainite) and Cappadocian (Lilitu). Tremere diablerized Saulot/Ashur, which also caused the downfall of the Cappadocians, and the Baali to recede from a widespread theat in the middle ages to a minor footnote in modern nights. This implies that Cappadocius was gone/impersonated by the time Augustus tried to embrace him. My guess is that [Lasombra], having just fakes his own death in the Anarch revolt, killed and replaced Augustus - this gave him a chance to start a new clan and indulge his curiosities about Necromancy.

                                                            Originally posted by anda View Post

                                                            I'm very familiar with Chorazin but not so much informed about Enoch in the Shadowlands. Could you explain this one?

                                                            It is a reference to the Dirty Secrets of the Black Hand book, about the TalMaheRa/True Black Hand conspiracy/gehenna cult that among other things, had a secret citadel in the Shadowlands (aka realm of dead) that their mystics and leaders claimed was the memorian/spiritual shadow of Enoch, the original First City of noddist legend. It also hid some mysterious beings called the Aralu whom they claimed were some of the sleeping Antediluvians.

                                                            Mr Ashur, me and other people in the forums have brought the possibility that the group was quite mistaken and their fortress was actually the spiritual shadow of Chorazin or the Baali labyrinth in Cnossos and the Aralu not even kindred, but some of the demons/spirits/cthonic powers worshipped by them in eras long past.
