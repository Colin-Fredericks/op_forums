    V20 Domain Background

    09-03-2022, 05:18 PM
    I'm wondering a bit about the rules for the Domain background. Specifically about the reduced difficulty for your hunting checks.

    Is that reduction dependent on the points that you put in the size of your domain or on the overall point in your of the domain background (size+security).

    To me it sounds reads like the latter was the case, and like the size has no mechanical benefit.

      Domain is a Background that is trying to simulate too many things at once and doesn't have enough mechanical room to do any of them well. Furthermore, since Backgrounds don't function like normal abilities or attributes that means they can be gained, expanded, and lost as the story progresses without XP being a factor.

      The size of one's Domain has no direct mechanical benefit, only narrative benefits which have the possibility to lead to mechanical benefits later on. By this I mean the area you control can have other benefits.

      For instance, let's say that your Kindred wanted to be in control of the Docks in their city. Well depending on the size of the docks and how important a trade hub the city is, you would need Domain *** to Domain **** to represent this due to size.

      But being in control of the docks means you can demand kickbacks from the dock workers unions, any organized crime groups that want to move contraband through there, and of course bribes from more legitimate business who want their goods given priority for clearing customs or being shipped out. Thus, this leads to the Docks which is a Domain generating Resources.

      Since the World of Darkness is a darker and grittier version of our own world, then in all likelihood there will be some human trafficking going on, which means you potentially can have essentially a shipping container or three full of people with no rights or records. This can function as a hunting ground or potentially a Herd background if you have something like Dominate or Presence. Without the human trafficking however, unless you are drinking fish blood or feeding on the guards, pickings will be scarce.

      As far as security goes, that once again is more of a narrative aspect than a mechanical one. The Docks could just be a small series of open-air piers with a couple warehouses nearby that is watched over by a harbor master. The Docks could alternatively be a huge area with loads of fences, security cameras, guards, and customs officials, making it remarkably secure. All of this then scales the benefits in other areas.

      By controlling such a key area, this means you are the person to go to for favors regarding transport, thus potentially earning you Fame, Status, and Influence background points.

      Properly played, Backgrounds tend to grow like weeds and give access to other Backgrounds quite quickly. But at the same time if the ST or the Players don't bother to extrapolate the potential benefits and story hooks from having such a location, then it doesn't really matter much.

              The main appeal for domain is security. Having the docks doesn't mean you've become capable of runing an empire there, you were capable before that. However, if you set your haven up by the docs, organized some intricate system with the locals, and THEN the domain is legaly given to someone else... that's a rough time.

              Also if you're just "there" it's free competition, if it's actually yours then anyone who wants to be there is going to be owing you a favour. Most princes will honour your right to punish whoever intrudes in your domain to an extent.
              You can have a stake in enterprises or control mortals out of the domain. That's not a problem. But the essentials to your living should ideally be within your Domain.

                  Originally posted by Mizu View Post
                  I'm not sure about other editions, but V20 specifies that the only thing domain gives you control over is feeding rights among your fellow parasites. You don't have any control over the area so far as mortal society is concerned
                  Domain was an attempt in V20 to condense down other older edition Backgrounds into one and it didn't quite stick the landing. The feeding rights is kind of a Herd thing, while the security aspect is from the optional Haven background. But in the description of Domain it mentions specific things like having control over a high-rise apartment building, but that is more a question of Resources to buy ownership or just a matter of ghouling the owner or using Dominate to transfer ownership.

                  The security aspects in particular is kind of non-functional because it doesn't exactly say how the security works. Anyone with a high Resources score can simply hire security guards, but how do you have security and maintain feeding exclusivity if you are neither rich, nor powerful? This is a key distinction because your security affects both supernatural and mortal visitors.

                  Technically Domain can model a haven, a personal public hunting ground, and to a certain extent territory claimed such as pooled Domain meaning an entire city is claimed. But the costs and variety of scenarios means it is a poor emulation tool. For instance if you own a home that is your haven with people living there to munch on, do you simulate that by taking one dot in Domain or do you get that by taking one dot in Herd and a few dots in Resources?

                  If you own a hospital, is that a Domain for feeding at the blood bank, an explanation for having Resources five, a necromantic ritual parts shop, or just backstory fluff regarding why you were selected for the embrace? Depending how you answer, that then means that certain choices or avenues get locked off in your game.

                                              I think you're underestimating what a horrendous system that is. Rivals would deliberately try to sweep up businesses by ghouling inheritors and forcing out the current owner, or by ghouling a guy and influencing shareholders to make him the new CEO. There are countless ways to do this cleverly. So even in the most wretched of Anarch states folk are going to lay claim to Turf and they're not going to bother about who actually controls the finances. Most Camarilla vampires crave stability, and constant squables make the prince look weak and ineffectual. Ergo something like "The Hospital and these three blocks to the south of it are Erin's domain, she has the authority to dictate how other kindred, bar the I prince, use it. Anyone entering without her permision has commited a crime. It's hers till she lets or sells it or I the prince decide otherwise" is essential to forming stable cities. That's a very extreme example but the point is Everyone's allowed to buy property in the area or send proxies in, but even if bob bought everything within Erin's Domain, it's still her domain. Bob doesn't get the right to set up a haven or feed in Erin's domain just because he or his proxies own everything within.

                                              Domain is a tradition with wildly different interpretations from city to city. But the point of the background is that people recognize a vampire's territory and know that if they act up within that territory it's only right that the Domain holder can act in retribution. By acknowledging X's domain, they can expect X to acknowledge Y's domain, and so on and so forth. This is a virtuous cycle and keeps vampires beyond arms length and therefore civil. If it were up to who owns what in the mortal world, the Ventrue get culled repeatedly, because nobody would be able to withstand that shit.
