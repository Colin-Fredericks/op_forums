                                    I think the Tremere of earlier eras were very much products of the original House Tremere and embraced exclusively or predominantly people who had occult powers, hermetic training, or some other proven aptitude for "magic." They were still getting use to being vampires, still had a lot of mortal members in the House, and wanted the same kind of people. You were recruited first to House Tremere and then embraced afterwards. Old habits died hard.

                                    But the modern era Tremere were clearly looking at other kinds of people who nevertheless displayed a lot of ambition and natural talents who would fit well in the Pyramid. Magic was increasingly a secondary consideration. It wasn't that they weren't embracing those anymore. They still did. It's just they wanted other kinds of people that could help the clan in the modern world.

                                    At the same time, there were always people who were embraced simply because certain influential Tremere, who had enough pull that they were allowed to create childer, wanted certain people as their childe. And this is where we see a lot of character concepts that don't fit in all eras.

                                    1e Chicago By Night is a good example of this trend. The leader was Nicolai, someone who was an apprentice living mage who became embraced after his sire was turned into a vampire. But he embraced Abraham DuSable not for his magical talents, but because he was very useful in other ways for the Chicago chantry. It just happened that DuSable thrived in learning Thaumaturgy. DuSable did well enough to get permission to sire, but settled on the musician Garwood who was essentially a passion embrace and didn't work out. (His other childe, the caitiff Maldavis was very similar). The other childe of Nicolai, Tracy Graves, was essentially of the earlier tradition and embraced because of her occult interest (combined with Nicolai's obsession with her).

                                    In Milwaukee By Night, Carna was clearly a passion embrace that didn't make sense, but the Clan retroactively approved it. Her sire Bourdona was clearly influential enough in the clan (and he was a Prince somewhere) that it was tolerated. A different Tremere doing the same thing might have gotten severely punished at the time. (Carna's embrace date of 1435 was in the middle of the Anarch Revolt; they might have needed Bourdona a lot at the time, or thought retro-approving Carna was a good PR move). But Carna clearly took to Thaumaturgy so it worked out. Dr Mortius was a mortal practitioner of the occult, and that is why he was embraced in an older age. But Victor, the CIA assassin, was clearly embraced for other reasons.

                                    I would say that very early on the Clan only embraced people who would have fit in the old House Tremere. However, as time went by and the world changed, the clan slowly opened up their selection criteria as they identified other required skills the Clan needed to survive and grow in power. Over time this probably changed from something like 90% occult and 10% other, to something now that might be more like 20% occult background and 80% other. Those last figures can obviously be fiddled with - I haven't done any analysis to get them, it's just something I threw out there. But I would say at most it's likely 50/50 in terms of occult.

                                    They probably still want people who seem to have an aptitude for hermetic magic. They want people who could theoretically devote themselves and improve. But it's no longer the primary consideration. Likely any chantry wants some members who have the interest and talent to excel in thaumaturgy. And if a chantry currently doesn't have such an Apprentice, they would prioritize that. But after they have one or two members who can do that, then they might prioritize other kind of members that they need. Perhaps even someone who doesn't fit at all, but whom they needed for a very specific reason.

                                    And of course, embraces that end up being a big mistake can happen at any time for any place, because even Tremere become irrational or make mistakes.
