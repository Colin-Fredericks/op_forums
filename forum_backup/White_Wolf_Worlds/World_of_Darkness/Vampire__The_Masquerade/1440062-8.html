        Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
        The issues with the camarilla has always been individual princes (or the councils/methuselah that puppet them). The actual institution itself hasn't been much of a problem. If anything the Camarilla has been a helpful aid to prevent the worst excesses of princes and anarchs.
        That's another distinction that is easily lost when the game focuses too much on the conflict between the anarchs and the Camarilla: the princes are not synonymous with the Camarilla. In many instances, the princes are antagonistic to the Camarilla. "Historically, there have been many attempts by the leaders of the Camarilla to assert more authority over other aspects of vampiric existence. Each time, the attempt has failed in a wave of bloodshed. The princes do not brook interference into what they consider to be their historical rights and privileges, nor do the Methuselahs desire a competitor who could thwart their aims. Therefore, the Camarilla remains a divided sect of only moderate influence." (Vampire: The Masquerade 2nd Edition, page 42; emphases mine.)

        You could as easily focus on the types of people, anarch or otherwise, who would want to work with the Camarilla to curb the excesses of individual princes. This was at least a possible if not a likely avenue for reform in earlier editions of the game. When you completely separate the anarchs from the Camarilla, like the recent iterations of the game seem to have been keen on doing, it makes for what I consider a poorer and less nuanced setting, not a richer one.

            Originally posted by Brickman View Post
            Where I think I see it differently though is the idea that's there's a worthwhile "Faction" called the anarchs. Sure elders get toppled and new domains set up trying their own style of governance, but they're not part of some worldwide organisation.
            That's really the core of the disagreement. Anarchs make sense to me when they enjoy the protection of the Camarilla but want to see a change in how the majority of Camarilla cities are governed. They do not make sense to me as an independent organization that has severed its ties with the Camarilla and wants to see it destroyed.

            To take just one example of what it would mean to be truly without the support of the Camarilla, consider the Masquerade. The Camarilla is the global conspiracy to hide the existence of vampires from mortal knowledge. This is what the sect was founded to do, and what remains its primary concern 500 years later. In addition to directly protecting all its members from discovery on a nightly basis, the Camarilla indirectly protects every single vampire on the planet, including even the Sabbat. Not out of the goodness of their hearts, but because they have to, in order for the Masquerade to function.

            To say that the preservation of the Masquerade is not an easy task would be an understatement. For many players, it strained credibility even before the era of smartphones and the internet. The Masquerade is the central conceit that you have to accept in order to enjoy the setting. I can suspend my disbelief if the entity responsible for upholding the Masquerade is global, incredibly wealthy, and influential beyond measure in the mortal world. Something like a 500-year-old organization that has dedicated unfathomable resources to the task.

            When the Anarch Movement swaggers onto the stage as an independent alternative to the Camarilla, what I want to know is where did the resources, the connections, the institutional knowledge, the numbers, the everything that is necessary to maintain and promulgate the Masquerade on their turf come from. If the anarchs still depend on the Camarilla's resources to repair the vast majority of Masquerade breaches, well... There's a reason why people say that hypocrisy is the worst offense of all.

                        It seems that White Wolf has confirmed they are bringing back PATHS OF ENLIGHTENMENT so Taggie should be happy.

                        https://www.worldofdarkness.com/news/development-blog-defining-clans

                        Essential Experience

                        From a design perspective, the function of these clans (and the other factions to come…) is to create expectations for the players. Each helps define what a Kindred of that particular clan might expect to do in stories.

                        For Vampire, we describe these essential experiences using the statement “the vampire as X.” The Brujah are the vampire as rebel, the Toreador are the vampire as seducer, the Banu Haqim are the vampire as judge, and so on.

                        Since characters act on the world, whether they’re players’ characters or Storyteller characters, every faction is designed to do something that’s expressed by verbs. That’s one of the big distinctions between designing for interactive media like games and designing for traditional media in which the “audience” doesn’t have input into what’s happening. Players verb.

                        (You’ll see these as other titles in the World of Darkness take shape, as well, from the Sabbat’s Paths of Enlightenment to Werewolf’s tribes.)

                                I suspect that's an inevitable part of them warring with other elders.

                                It's kind of like in athletics. Athletes tend to be physically ruined by the end of their careers, because the only way you win is by being willing to push your body past the limits of its design, and to do so again and again. The guys not willing to do that lose.

                                Elders beat elders by pushing all of their resources to the limit and beyond. In the back of its mind, the elder knows that this will anger those who serve it. And maybe it even feels bad about that if it cares about its progeny. But this military thing has to be done NOW NOW NOW or it will lose to the elder who IS willing to push its progeny to do those dangerous things NOW NOW NOW. It probably thinks, well, if we lose, my progeny are dead anyway, and if we win, I can always make it up to the survivors with gifts.

                                So let's say you have a situation where the elder has three progeny, and he pushes them to do something that gets two of them killed, and causes the survivor to have suffered multiple aggravated wounds, and to have lost both his ghouls. The survivor liked his fellow progeny, and he liked his ghouls. In fact, he was planning to embrace his ghouls one day. And so the elder calls him in, and rewards him with money, and this really delicious kine he bought from the Circulatory System.

                                Now in the elder's mind, its done as much as it could. It only pushed them to do things that absolutely had to be done or they were all dead anyway. And its rewarded the survivor with the best it could afford, what with so many resources having been destroyed in the war with that other elder. It thinks everything is fine and dandy.

                                Meanwhile, the progeny is looking at his or her reward, and thinking about all (s)he has lost, and thinking "fuuuuuuuuuuuck yoooooouuuu!!!!!!!!!!"

                                        My ideal treatment of the Sabbat would be to give them a proper update to V5 and neither roll back the clock on them or make them too different from Revised.

                                        * Have them lose Montreal and Mexico City
                                        * Have a large chunk of them been fighting the Gehenna Crusade (and not embarrass themselves)
                                        * Have the Lasombra defection acknowledged
                                        * Have them have an official policy on the Second Inquisition and changes in teh Masquerade
                                        * Have them have to deal with a United Anarchs
                                        * Have them lose the Harbingers
                                        * Have them deal with the upswing in Noddism and Blood Cults
                                        * Have them acknowledge Drowned Legacies

                                        BUT

                                        * Also have them have remaining territory
                                        * Acknowledge the Paths of Enlightenment
                                        * Have them acknowledge House Goratrix (which means they have their own Antediluvian)
                                        * Have them have destroyed LOTS of Elders and Methuselahs in the Middle East
                                        * Have them holding their own against all the factions like the Drowned Legacies, SI, and so on
                                        * Make options for playable Sabbat
                                        * Incorporate options for all varieties of Sabbat playstyles and antagonists
                                        * Reintroduce the Salubri Antitribu and Kisayd
                                        * Show the new Regent and end the Civil War
                                        * DON'T make the number of Monty Coven secret methusalehs any more than a handful of plotters

                                        I mean, the Sabbat has taken some lumps yes but are they ANY worse off than the Camarilla or Anarchs at this point? I mean, let's face it, the Camarilla lost the Tremere Inner Council and two Clans in the past twenty years.

                                        The Sabbat lost Montreal and Mexico City....but the Camarilla lost London, Vienna, and Berlin.
