If that is the desired effect, that is perfect. If that would rob protagonism to the story we are telling (and in a rpg games computer or not that is what we do) softening up is an option. Playability should come first.

                                            BTW, being a non-native speaker, Shakespeare in his original English is very difficult for me. After a couple of pages, with context growings thinner due to previous misunderstandings, nearly impossible. Lost in translation.
