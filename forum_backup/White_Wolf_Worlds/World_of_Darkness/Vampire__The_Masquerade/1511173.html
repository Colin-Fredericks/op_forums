      I played in an elder game for 5+ irl years and the underlying premise was that Gehenna was ensuing, so, yes, some Antediluvians died, and some in ways the coterie took a direct hand in through various shenanigans.

      But, I mean, it was the End Times, that sort of thing comes with the package. It's difficult to pick out a distinct sort of effect thereby when the world is slowly to moderately to quickly (depending on the particular chunk of the world or society) going from pre to actual apocalypse in stages. But I did enjoy when the STs (there were several that traded off) went with the Tzimisce post 1) finding out for legit confirmed realsies they had not killed their founder at all, clan wide, 2) Not even really playing a role in what actually then later killed their founder for legit confirmed realsies 3) Having a huge identity crisis about 1 and 2, ended up in an extended chain of events that lead to them joining the Camarilla, who were facing the disintegrating global situation with an increasing "we will take anyone that is not outright infernal just for the added strength" perspective. It came off in a way that felt like it made sense as far as the way things were played, and why wouldn't they have a huge identity crisis about that combination of events? If anything would make a clan look at their sect and their place in it and go "what was even the point of us", y'know.

      No one had a Tzimisce pc, before anyone notes "man that would have sucked as an experience for a Tzimisce pc! Just humiliating stuff!" With that said, there were two Assamite pcs, one of whom entirely believed in their clan's core mythos as an in character thing, and Haqim still turned out to be The Worst. We all rolled with the notion that stuff was going to get severely turned on its head and the center cannot hold and what all have you.

      Hey, the Tzim were able to negotiate a justicar out of it, they were fine, relative to the world increasingly crumbling (the Camarilla were not un-desperate).

      (The Sabbat were fine relative to one of their founding boasts being revealed as a big ol nope, it was counterbalanced by "see! we were right!" as far as them there blood gods in fact existing and mostly coming off as everything you never wanted to exist. They picked up a bunch more of the Assamites post a slightly pre revised Clanbook schism that ended up in the Camarilla getting none of them)

                                                          Herr Meister

                                                          Basically the lowest generations are destroyed with the birth of Christ the most powerful and least human of the Cainites are reduced to ash in that moment, including Caine himself. There isn't a week of nightmares or any other ramifications to the weaker generations.

                                                          The biggest change for vampire society is that it's most powerful members are basically free of Blood Bonds and the like all at the same instant. This leads to the Inconnu forming within a decade made up of some of the eldest 6th generation vampires and some of their 7th generation Childer. I have the Camarilla forming sooner for safety against a more powerful church/or less powerful vampire society depending on how you want to look at it, and the Sabbat forms at the same time, basically just a split on views of how vampire society should be ruled. Both sects are manipulated by the Inconnu, I don't have a tal'mahe'ra without the Antediluvians, just a black hand for the Sabbats special forces.

                                                          The biggest change is to the modern times. While in normal VTM the Antediluvians helped human advancement by pushing science and reason to combat the power of the church (in my game), without them the churches hold is much stronger. Christianity never declines and instead continues to grow, while technology is slowed in some areas and never discovered in others.

                                                          It lets me have modern technology that I think is important for a modern game (printing press, cars, guns, finger printing, telephone, antibiotics) while removing all the things I think ruin VTM. In my world WW1 never happens. Without WW1 the circumstances that allow Hitler to rise to power do not exist so no WW2. Because of this medical advances drop dramatically, modern warfare isn't perfected, plane travel advances are slowed, as well as things like canned goods, and most importantly we never invent the computer.

                                                          So while you have film, radio, primitive analog car phones, you lose Satellites, GPS, space travel, digital clocks phones and cameras. World population is lower without some key medical and agricultural advances, the world is a much bigger place without the internet and easy instant communication/information.
