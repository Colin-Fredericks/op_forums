    [V20] 'Vampire, the Masquerade 20th anniversary' Setting Generator

    04-18-2021, 05:58 AM
    Hello, everybody!

    The following can be considered the greatest of all heresies, a mere anecdote or anything in the middle. I have created a (mega)table for the free program Inspiration Pad Pro that generates an alternate setting for Vampire, the Masquerade 20th Anniversary Edition. Those who are also members of rpg.net probably will know that already, as this begun some weeks ago, and its evolution is described in this thread of that forum. From its humble beginnings, this generator has turned into a monstrosity that regurgitates a wall of text of (more or less) 2500 words. That’s 5 pages in Word (with an Arial 11 font) when I copy&paste it directly in that program. There will be grammar inconsistencies in the text, because sometimes the fragments don't fit perfectly when joined…but also because English is not my first language (and this also explains the presence of typos here and there).

    So, in each new setting, the composition of the main factions is both similar and different than the original one. For example, in the Camarilla, there will those who rule, those who (more or less) support them, those who want to usurp them, those who are always protesting, those who stay on the sidelines, those whose deal deal nobody knows and those who are on they way out. But each of these roles will be occupied by a random clan. The same is applied to the Sabbat and the Independents. Also, the geopolitical aspects of the setting are randomly generated: sometimes, one faction will be more powerful in some areas of the USA, and sometimes it will be in others. A bunch of Camarilla and two Sabbat cities are described, too, showing their actual situation and including some plot-seeds. All of them at random, of course.

    And I am talking about ‘Camarilla’’ …but, as a matter of fact, the names of the factions are also randomly generated...as the names of the different positions of power (a Prince may still be a Prince, but it can also be a President, General or who knows what). I can not even imagine how are the probabilities to obtain the original setting as a result at this point…but I suspect that they are astronomically low!

    The most recent version of the generator (9.01) can be downloaded from this link. Also, in this link there is a full examples of settings generated with the most recent version. And…this generator is may keep growing with time. It's just about building on what's already done, after all…

      As example, I have generated one setting (different from the one I quoted before), and I will quote some parts of it.

      The following is the part concerning the Camarilla (or, in this case, the Secret Alliance) composition.

      Funded after the disarray of the Moaner Revolt, which led to the raging claws of the lupines, the main organization of the vampires receives the name of the Secret Alliance, and its people attempt to protect the Masquerade as top priority. This organization is led by the Tremere , with a certain sense of detachment. Those Tremere trust the collaboration of the Lasombra and the later are more or less sincere in that. However, the Tzimisce seek to seize the Tremere's ‘rights’ but it's going to be a long-term project. The Giovanni are always putting a spoke in the wheel and they are getting on the Tremere's nerves. Apart from this, the Ravnos mind on their own business they know more than meets the eye. With respect to the Assamite, practically no one knows what are they thinking, but they are making everyone their fools. To finish, the Brujah seem too eager to leave the organization ...and look, they are already gone! Ah, yes, and there is also the Moaner Movement, which makes a lot of noise and are going to mess all up for good, and the stranded Caitiffs with an unknow sire, which are tipically despised but ignored if they keep a low profile...and they are increasingly resentful because of that.
      The following is one example of the generated cities. It seems that, in this case, the Camarilla…sorry, the Secret Alliance is powerful in Northeast. Ah, yes, and they have selected a militar theme to name their positions. Finally, those ‘Gossipers’ are the ‘Harpies’ of this setting.

      The city of Philadelphia is ruled by a Tremere Admiral, whom no one dares to oppose. The Admiral has selected an old ally as Lieutenant, but maybe it is not a decision as as voluntary as it seems; meanwhile, the General staff is formed by everyone who dares, and it has chosen to play by the rules. Lately, an Elder's ghoul accidentally broke the masquerade and heads will roll. Also, several mortals have been vanishingin the last few nights; even if it had no supernatural origin, the Masquerade is suffering because of it. Finally, the cities's most famous members of the Gossipers has been attacked by some unknown supernatural creature and a reward has been offered for the capture of the culprit.
      There will always, be, also, a not-Chicago, with its own particular seed-plots. And, every time, a disaster will be coming. In this example, the selected city has been no other than...

      At last, there is the city of Jersey City: for decades, it has been considered one of the most important cities of the Secret Alliance, and its tale is both epic and frightening. The warnings about the presence of a (missing) Methuselah in disguise, the surreptitious infiltration of a blood cult which threats to destroy the balance of power and an imminent and dreadful attack by lupine which will unite all factions against the common enemy...or they all will be destroyed one by one , are just part of the whole narration of the city’s myth.
      The following is an example of one of the Independents. Or Unaffiliated, in this case.

      This way, the Malkavian desire to raise their dark god to make the world burn.This resurrection requires some lost magical ritual, but their plans have hit some kind of a wall. For now, the Secret Alliance wouldn't touch them even with a 10-foot pole...or at least too openly. Meanwhile, the Malkavian treat their antitribu in the Unseen Council as heretics even though their customs are a little similar, so they usually find some common ground. Recently, a prophet in the clan is proclaiming that they are being deluded, and the Elders are asking for their head.
      And finally, there is a rumours section, which initially were taken from a pool but finally I found a way to make everyone’s dance without stepping on each other toes. Here there are some examples (that Unseen Council is the Sabbat it this setting):

      "The Tzimisce suffered a curse by the Tremere. The reckoning is near. "

      "The 'Children of Osiris' were vampires who intended to achieve the Golconda, but the Assamite killed them off. A great loss."

      "Some guys who call themselves 'the Heralds of Skulls' have turned to the Unseen Council because they have pending accounts with the Brujah. I can't wait to see what happens."

      "A monstrous version of the Malkavian, following the orders of their antidiluvian, is exterminating them mercilessly. One less thing to worry."

      "Raputin was a Ravnos. Or possibly a Tzimisce. No, wait, he sure was a mage. "

This one is quite cosmopolite, I say, with vampires originated from Dalmatia, Russia, Philippines, Chile, Spain, China and Italy!

                                  Since its beginning, the Sabbat of the Dark has been formed by the members of seven clans. This way, the Sabbat of the Dark is under the command of the Kozlak and they tolerate no open dissent. These Kindred, originating in Dalmatia, have the capabilities of manifesting powers related to fairy magic (Mytherceria), resisting huge damage (Fortitude) and altering their own bodies in various ways (Protean). For all that, they go into a frenzy with worrying ease. The Kozlak trust the support of the Eretica but neither party trusts the other. The members of this clan, originating from Russia, have the skills of communicate and interact with spirits (Spiritus), enduring terrible harm (Fortitude) and hiding without notice (Obfuscate). On the other hand, it is difficult for them to feed on involuntary objects. Meanwhile, the Dang attempt to seize the Kozlak's government but it's going to be a long-term project. These Kindred, originating fron Philippines, have the powers of summoning and controling the shadows (Obtenebration), moving at supernatural speeds (Celerity) and dominating the volition of others (Domination). Nevertheless, they temporarily acquire animal traits when they go into a frenzy.

                                  Somewhat removed from positions of power, the Piuche are always protesting (and the Kozlak are feed up with them). The Piuche, originating from Chile, have the abilties of manipulating the flow of time around them (Vicissitude, manifesting inhuman forms of perception (Auspex) and altering their own bodies totally or partially (Protean). Having said that, they must feed on raw flesh, in addition to blood. Apart from all this, the Guajona mind on their own business but are secretly helping the the Dang. These Kindred, originating from Spain, have the faculties of turning their blood into a lethal weapon (Quietus), employing unnatural forms of perception (Auspex) and projecting a majestic aura (Presence). Regrettably, they must sleep in the presence of soil from their homeland. With respect to the Jiangshi, no one understands what's their deal, and maybe the Jiangshi neither. These vampires, originating from China, have the capabilities of controlling rot and decomposition (Thanatosis), employing unnatural methods of perception (Auspex) and acting at supernatural speeds (Celerity). Alas, they can only feed on a specific type of victim. Last but not least, the Lamia seem too eager to abandon ship and the rest of the clans pray for that night to come. These Kindred, originating from Italia, have the powers of molding the flesh into beautiful or grotesque shapes (Temporis), overcoming the limits of human might (Potence) and altering their own bodies in various ways (Protean). Despite that, they only need two intakes to suffer a blood bond.

                                  Lastly, the Jarracas travel all over the world, and they are viewed as sinister and gloomy doomsayers.                                       There is now a 11.04 Version online. And this time, some Requiem-y thingies have crawled into the generator: there is a new parameter, called 'Disciplines', with four options:
                                      • Basic: Only the disciplines in the V20 manual are taking into account, when required.
                                      • Expanded: Some 'deprecated' disciplines (as Kai, Deimos and the like) are added to the pool.
                                      • Alternative: With this option, the clans wil receive disciplines from Vampire, the Requiem (one 'clan', one 'bloodline' and two 'commons' disciplines) instead of the usual three from Vampire, the Masquerade.
                                      • Mixed: Each clan will get three disciplines from the Expanded list...plus a 'bloodline' discipline from Vampire, the Requiem.

                                      So, when used in a totally weird mode, the generator will give settings with excerpts like the following...

                                      Since its foundation, the Cursed Camarilla has been formed by the vampires of seven clans. This way, the Cursed Camarilla is under the command of the Dang with laissez faire politics. These Kindred, originating in Philippines, have the skills of molding the flesh into beautiful or grotesque shapes (Vicissitude), seeing and entering the world of the dead. (Carrefour), displaying supernatural forms of perception (Auspex) and showing superhuman physical power (Potence). Though, they must feed on raw flesh, in addition to blood. The Dang trust the assistance of the Jiangshi but it's only a temporary agreement. The Jiangshi, originating from China, have the abilties of mentally manipulating the kinetic energy (Kineticism), augmented hearing and sound mimicry (Mimetismo), ruling the will of others (Domination) and hiding beyond human senses (Obfuscate). Alas, they have a deformed and hideous appearance. However, the Nelapsi are trying to steal the Dang's ‘rights’ but they are still far from it. The Nelapsi, originating fron Slovakia, have the skills of manifesting Gargolesque features (including flight) (Visceratika), animating corpses to their bidding (Cattiveria), enduring huge harm (Fortitude) and vanishing in plain sight (Obfuscate). For all that, they can't bear to look at the holy objects of any faith.
                                      The Kozlak are part of a tight family with an ancient military tradition, but they will periodically bring new blood.Their seat of power is in a city in Dalmatia, where they control everything from the shadows. Until now, the Cursed Camarilla has not paid much attention to them, but the clan wants to change that...for better or worse. Meanwhile, the Kozlak consider their antitribu in the Unseen Council as heretics even though their customs are very similar, but this makes their animosity even bitter. Recently, some Kozlak seem to be trying to spread to another city in Dalmatia, and they are making good progress. These Kindred have the talents of interacting with the world of ghosts and spectres (Nihilistics), enhancing fighting preparation while courting (Courtoisie), performing great feats of might (Potence) and projecting a imposing aura (Presence). Regrettably, they only need two intakes to suffer a blood bond.
