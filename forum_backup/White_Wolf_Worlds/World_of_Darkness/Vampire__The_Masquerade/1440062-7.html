                                Anarchs are righteous. That's why they cannot be winner. Not in the Wod, not in Vampires. Or we are not talking about Vampire: The Masquerade.

                                                Well, I disagree with a lot of this.

                                                Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                                                I mean, it's Gothic PUNK.

                                                The whole point is being a rebel against the Man. The Anarchs are the Edgerunner faction.
                                                The punk portion is basically an aesthetic, and while its one that should be included in the game, it should not be the only one. And it's one I personally feel is childish and unappealing. Most players that I know agree with me. But it's also one that one of my best friends, who I met while playing Vampire, likes. And he's very pro-Anarch obviously. So it's fine to be part of the game, but I think defining the game by it is a mistake. The Lost Boys is an entertaining movie, but it doesn't define the genre.

                                                It's a legitimate choice for players, but its primarily aesthetic, not moral.

                                                If I had to define my own style of play and running games, I would call it Gothic-Noir.

                                                Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                                                IAlso, just because they're the moral faction doesn't mean they're the one people want to play. Not everyone wants to be the good guy, especially in a game where you are a traditional fantasy monster.
                                                I've never seen the Anarch as the "moral" faction. They just use certain kind of rhetoric. That you may like that rhetoric doesn't mean it has anything substantial to do with the actual group. The Jacobins and Bolsheviks had a lot of good propaganda, both were far morally worse than the groups they replaced. They were Dostoevsky's Grand Inquisitor on steroids.

                                                You have the dirt bag criminals or cynical exploiters using the Anarch label. Less moral than the Camarilla in general. Equally repulsive as the Camarilla's worst.

                                                You have Anarchs who basically want the same system, but with themselves in charge since then think they'd do it "better". In most cases, that would not be true. They'd either be just as bad, or make such a muck of it they'd be removed from power. Depending on how many corpses they create, they're either just equally moral or less moral than the Camarilla. This is probably an honest assessment of Maldavis.

                                                Then you have the naive fools who are sincere in doing morally better, but utterly unprepared for actual rule. In theory this group is morally better, but would make such a mess of things, in practice it's a wash or morally worse. Good intentions are not enough to be moral. Consequences of your actions matter.

                                                Then you have the failed idealists. People who originally started morally better, but quickly became so angry that they gleefully adopted all the tactics of the enemy (and even worse) that was supposed to have been the reasons they opposed them in the first place. I think this is likely where we disagree because this is where I'd likely put Anita Wainwright who always reminded me of the Weather Underground types so distraught over violence that they mail bombs to people and devolve into terrorism. I consider them to be morally worse than average because they're committing the same crimes they are supposedly railing against, but don't even realize what has happened to them.

                                                Then there's the existential drama queen who creates problems because reacting to them and claiming oppression gives them personal meaning in life. But they are just obnoxious trolls. But they can fool a lot of naive people until they learn better. Equivalent people exist in the Camarilla. So its a wash again.

                                                There are the sad sack victims of society. People who honestly didn't do anything, but somehow became on someone's shit list. Caitiffs. Thin bloods. Social misfits. People in the wrong place and at the wrong time. Bad luck of the draw. These people may not necessarily be morally better, but they're definitely victims who are sympathies go out too. Unfortunately, some of these will become victims regardless of who is in charge. One portion of them won't be the victims in a new regime, but some other group will exchange places with them. As individuals, they may draw on our moral sympathies, but collectively it does not mean they are morally better simply because they are the losers of vampire society.

                                                Then there are the Well Meaning Morons or Useful Idiots. They're sincere saps who actually believe the rhetoric of the cynical exploiters, would-be Princes and the rest. They too will simply be used as cannon fodder or eaten by the Revolution. As individuals I can consider them to be morally better, extremely stupid, and doing great harm as they allow themselves to be exploited. But many PC Anarchs probably fit this category. Eventually those who survive will realize the Anarchs are just as big as a Racket as the Camarilla.

                                                Lastly you have serious, sincere vampires who want continued reform of the system to produce a better form of vampire government, but doing that outside the system. Probably morally better. But this does not really distinguish them from vampires of the same ilk who remain committed to do it inside the Camarilla without using the violence of the Anarchs. So it's really a wash when comparing Anarchs versus Camarilla.

                                                So I never considered the Anarchs to be more moral even in theory as opposed to practice. They just have more sympathetic propaganda to a certain kind of people, and likely have a higher percentage of people who want to be more moral simply because they are younger and more in touch with their Humanity. But it's a wash. While individual Anarchs might be "good people", you can find them in the Camarilla too. And neither group is profoundly influenced by any moral leaders of their kind.

                                                Conclaves are an under represented tool of Camarilla society. The game doesn't utilize them much, and we don't hear a lot about their decisions and how it influences the Camarilla. For many chronicles, they may not even exist at all. But I think it they are utilized in game, it enables people to let off steam, allows slow reforms by building up consensus, and keeps the most tyrannical Princes and elders in checks. Not enough to satisfy everyone, but well enough that it keeps the Camarilla viable and allows many "Anarchs" to rejoin the system after they become more experienced, wiser, or simply jaded and exhausted. I think Conclaves are extremely important to the setting, and explain a lot of elements people may otherwise find anomalous.

                                                In so far there is a moral faction within Vampire, it is not based around vampire politics. Its spiritual seekers of Golconda who have progressed far along that path, and likely do their best to minimize their involvement in vampire society.

                                                            It's funny because I actually feel like I'm the guy who has the much more nasty position on the Anarchs than a lot of the posters here. I certainly don't feel like they're the good guys and I actually was in the process of writing a lengthy speech about it before Black Fox pretty much summarized a lot of my points.

                                                            Still, I recommend people check out my TOP TEN TIPS TO PLAYING ANARCHS blog entry.

                                                            http://unitedfederationofcharles.blo...g-anarchs.html

                                                            One sample that's relevant:

                                                            1:] Anarchs are not the good guys

                                                            An important thing to understanding the appeal of the Anarchs in a Gothic Punk world is that it is shades of gray and black, never white (at least among vampires). The first Anarch ever introduced was Forged in Steel's Juggler. Juggler was a hypocrite to the Anarch cause who manipulated, lied, and cheated Neonates in much the same way as his Camarilla counterpart Modius. He even had his own Blood Bound female minion, Evelyn, exactly like the Prince. The Anarchs are not the Rebel Alliance and whenever they do take over a territory like Los Angeles or Berlin, it's a crapshot whether that territory will become better or worse for their influence. Being an Anarch means you're against the city's Elders and Prince, nothing more or less.

                                                            2:] Anarchs think they're the good guys

                                                            Contradicting number 1# is the fact that many anarchs believe themselves to be the good guys or, at least, the lesser evil. Everything is relative in Cainite society and the fury over past injustices is a major reason why the Movement has not died in six hundred years. There are many idealistic voices among the Anarchs like Maldavis, Anita Wainwright, Jeremy MacNeil, Nines, and Salvador Garcia. Don't get me wrong, they're still ****ing vampires but they have legitimate greviances motivating them as well as a passionate belief things can get better. It's the presence of these individuals that prevents the entire Anarch experiment from being written off as just another bunch of Kindred warring for territory.
                                                            Anarchs are an inevitability of Kindred society because the only way to survive in Kindred society is to push back when you're being pushed by vampire society. Younger vampires have an advantage over Elders in the fact they're willing to team up and risk their lives in a way that the older members of vampiredom won't. Also, the advancement of technology and (slightly) higher humanity means that even if they're just awake an hour ahead of an Elder that's a fucking hour of vulnerability.

                                                            (I always felt 'Wake With Morning's Freshness" was ridiculously overpowered as a result)

                                                            Now this may seem like a cynical non-idealistic interpretation of the Anarchs but I turn to ASSASSINS CREED 3 for how to define them. The Anarchs are the Assassins in that they're ostensibly all for freedom but also hypocritically make decisions for the public in the same way as the Templars as well as strike randomly outward versus a more targeted approach. In AC 3, it's even more underscored. The founding fathers are fighting for THEIR freedom but not EVERYONE's freedom (see Native Americans, the fact they're slavers).

                                                            Most Anarchs don't give a shit for Kine or their freedom. In fact, they're actually WORSE because the Camarilla's laws about Domain and Execution mean that humans get fed on less overall.

                                                            The Anarchs have that unhappy medium between the Camarilla and the Sabbat where they're all about FREEDOM and fighting the power but have enough people appalled by the Beast not to fall to it completely but are just a hair's breadth away from ending up corrupted into something worse than what they're fighting. The Ministry joining the "Second Anarch Revolt" is a sign of the Anarchs likely going down the same road as the First One.
