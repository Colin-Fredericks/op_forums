                                                    If we're talking personal tastes, here's my personal take--my "headcanon" if you will, which is canon at my table. I run a V20 game set in 2020 (began in 2019), mixing a couple ideas from various novels, BJD and V5.


                                                    -The years between 1997 and 2007 are remembered as "The Gehenna Scare." Rise in Thinblood Population and the appearance of the Blood Curse in cities all over the world, combined with the Week of Nightmares/appearance of the Red Star in 1999, sent vampires all over the world into a panic. My take on the Blood Curse is closer to the Withering from the Gehenna novels, elders experiencing a loss of potency and feeding ability only ameliorated by feeding on Thinbloods or younger kindred. While many kindred died directly or indirectly because of this supernatural plague, it eventually petered out, perhaps due to magical intervention or the pandemic simply taking its course.

                                                    -Following incursions by the Wan Kuei in L.A (1997) and San Francisco (1999), defection of the Baron of San Diego and solidification of Praxis by Sebastien LaCroix, the Anarch Free State of Southern California seemed finished. A whirlwind of spectacular events remembered as "the Antediluvian Hoax" or "Jack's Prank," resulted in the death of LaCroix, ouster of the Wan Kuei from Los Angeles (their Mandrinate remains in San Francisco and its "puppet princedom" of San Jose, contra the Free State of Oakland), the establishment of Jenna Cross' "Thin-Blood Empire," (a support/mutual defense network for the renamed Duskborn) and the Free State's miraculous resurrection in 2004.

                                                    -Convinced that the return of Caine and final judgement of all Kindred was nigh, the Council of Seven called together almost all the senior members of Clan Tremere, gathering in the Great Chantry beneath Vienna in 2006. In that year they attempted the Ritual of the Red Sign, a rite researched and prepared to transform the seniormost warlocks back into living mortals. However, in their terror the Council enacted the ritual without full preparation, resulting in a backlash of energy which destroyed the Great Chantry (and collapsed much of the underground environs beneath the city of Vienna). The true story of this disaster and the Council's disappearance remains mysterious to most. Around the same time as this disaster Carna, the Regent of Milwaukee, finishes translating the legendary Book of the Grave-War, breaking her Blood Bond to the Pyramid. Unaware of the shakeup and expecting reprisal, Carna leads her followers to the West Coast and the establishment of an independent House.

                                                    -In 2012 a great conclave was held in Prague, with some of the Camarilla's greatest heavyweights present. Fed up with the hypocrisy of his erstwhile employers and aware of the measures taken by Hardstadt to survive the winnowing, Archon Theo Bell and his coterie raid the conclave and publicly assassinate Hardestadt---the blow is commemorated as "the Decapitation of Prague." Following Bell's example, Brujah all over the world renounce the Camarilla, reigniting the War of Ages into an open conflict not seen since the Chicago Anarch Massacre of 1968. While the Anarchs virtually as a whole now reject the Camarilla's authority, the sects find a truce in a few cities. In following years The Movement is bolstered by the emergence of a splinter group of "ecumenical" young Setites calling themselves the Ministry, and a group of Tremere branding themselves House Ipsissimus.

                                                    -Also in 2012, London falls to a well-organized purge conducted by a body of cooperating government entities. Ann Bowesley and apparently Mithras himself (in the inconveniently weak body of Monte Coven) apparently fall victim to the Vampire Hunters, though the Living God's cult refuses to believe he is gone. A few years prior Las Vegas was similarly scoured, but curiously it remains the only city in the New World to be so purged. Such operations are more frequent in Europe and it is now thought Vienna was such a case; an assumption the surviving Tremere leadership sees no need to correct. This dangerously well-informed alliance of agencies and intelligence services is called a New or Second Inquisition by some elders, and the Camarilla enacts a policy of crackdown on the errant use of trackable social media within its ranks.

                                                    -In 2016, Al Ashrad and Tegyrius lead a deposition of the so-called Schismatics before the Camarilla and seek admittance on behalf of their Clan. Reeling from the loss of first the Gangrel and now the rebellious Brujah, the Camarilla overrides the protests of Clan Tremere--now virtually led by Karl Schrekt---and grants the Schismatics permission to settle in cities under their aegis.

                                                    -Ideas not used: The Beckoning/Gehenna Crusade, the Church of Caine. Despite the Gehenna Scare, the Camarilla remains a "secular" organization which officially categorizes Noddism as no more than a collection of dangerous myths. The Sabbat remains a potent force, with heavy fighting flaring up on the East Coast in the past couple of years---Atlanta, Baltimore and New York City (recently hard won by the Ivory Tower in the siege of 1999-2000) are epicenters of the Sect War. This has given the Anarchs' perhaps optimistic rebellion on the West Coast some breathing room. Washington D.C. maintains an enforced neutrality under an apparently-revived Marcus Vitel, thought killed by Theo Bell and Dux Bellorum Jan Pieterzoon (currently coordinating the sect war from his New York headquarters). The story goes that Vitel had somehow got his tentacles on the fabled nuclear football and intended to circumvent the Judgement of Caine by triggering WWIII, only stopped by Bell and Pieterzoon's unlikely Buddy Cop heroics. Bell and Pieterzoon maintain they killed the ancient but refuse to confirm or deny this silly story. Other independent domains of North America are: Vancouver, B.C., isolated by distance and its massive Lupine population; New Orleans, supposedly controlled by a coalition of "Clans of Death"; and perhaps Vegas, ostensibly reborn as a Free State but under the sway of a great many Followers of Set.


                                                    Not much of this is really relevant to the substance of my Chronicle, which takes place in the Pacific Northwest and far from the great politics of moment. But having absorbed so much Vampire lore over the past few years it was inevitable my preferences would coalesce into a rough big picture.

                                                            OPINION: The Sabbat Lasombra will emerge stronger from this

                                                            Mind you, I don't think the Sabbat are actually going to change from what has been released by Justin Achilli. The big thing about the Lasombra defection is that it changes the Camarilla but it DOESN'T CHANGE THE SABBAT. The Betrayal of the Lasombra is something that they assumed would be a death blow to the Sabbat but the Sword of Caine has long since slipped out of the
                                                            Keeper's fingers.

                                                            How the Lasombra viewed the Sabbat: Useful fools who exist to serve our whims.
                                                            How the Sabbat viewed the Lasombra: A bunch of self-absorbed pricks who didn't realize we knew they were cowards and assholes.

                                                            Part of the driving force of the Sabbat's Civil Wars was the repeated attempt to break the hold of the Lasombra (and to a lesser extent the Tzimisce) so that the antitribu could have equal power in the sect. The Anitribu who reject what was the Old Ways of Clans and their traditions. This is why the Sabbat SURVIVE the Lasombra leaving and also why the Lasombra possibly gravely miscalculated.

                                                            First of all, I really do like what CBN said about the fact that only about half the Lasombra defected. Those Lasombra who already have a good thing going like Archbishops, Priscuses, and people who don't put Clan first are going to find that the defection is ludicrous. But more so than that, NOT ALL LASOMBRA BUY INTO THE PATH OF NIGHT.

                                                            There's going to be ones who ARE true believers, who value their Pack over their Clan, and who may even be self-sacrificing or not worship the narcissitic pursuit of power over all. People who aren't on the Path of Night, Path of Power and the Inner Voice, or Low Humanity but maybe the Path of Honorable Accord or Path of Caine.

                                                            These Lasombra have been betrayed by all their fellows just as much as anyone else and are now, themselves, the Antitribu. To know what it is like to be opposed to everything their Elders stand for.

                                                            Just like the Tremere, adversity is going to make the Sabbat stronger because one of their leading sources of inner turmoil is now gone.
