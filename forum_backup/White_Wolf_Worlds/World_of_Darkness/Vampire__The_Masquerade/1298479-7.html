                    Personally speaking, I think the most egregious and obnoxious example of real life politics needlessly interfering with the game would be Rudi.
                    A gay, islamic Gangrel standing up for minorities and females who advocates for progressive causes wherever he goes and his nickname is "the Bear Gangrel".
                    Ok, if this is the type of character that you're interested in playing, hey, all the more power to you. However, I believe we can agree that some of what is in that character bio has never been an issue in VTM. Havens, feeding grounds, elitism, these were all typical VTM issues that vampires had to deal with. Misogynistic elders were not.
                    Even the Ventrue, the oldest old boys' club in this setting, while they might have been biased towards European males, knew better. It was stated in the Revised Clanbook that any sire who would pass up on a talented non-White female childe on the basis of gender or race is an idiot and it would be his loss and, furthermore, both the Prince of London and the Ventrue Justicar were women. The oppressed minorities in Vampire: The Masquerade were always caitiffs and thin bloods, not real life minorities.
                    Now, none of this means that there weren't issues in Bloodlines 1 that couldn't be fixed such as why would VV attend court in a stripper's outfit. But it does mean that If there is a conflict between the Toreador Primogen and the Ventrue Prince, I would like for it to be over feeding grounds, influence and ambition. Not because the Toreador Primogen is a woman being held back by her gender.

                    So, for the love of God, don't pull a Rudi, Paradox Interactive.
