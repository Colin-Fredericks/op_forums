        Here's my take.

        None of the Antediluvians are dead, despite the lazy approach taken by the Gehenna sourcebook.

        Caine == currently a taxi drive in LA (sorry, I love that bit too much).

        The 2nd Generation == All Dead, by the hands of the Childer. How are they dead when the 3rd Gen are so impossible to kill? Age, basically. The 2nd Gen hadn't been around long enough to become Supremely Powerful. They were only a couple centuries ahead of the 3rd Gen, and were outnumbered more than 3 to one, when the time came to throw them down.

        Alternately, they're no more dead than the Antediluvians, and are the REAL threat of Gehenna. The Antes are the clans only hope of surviving THEIR ravenous hunger and attempt to send the work back into the Stone Age where they can rule as Gods.

        *Assamite/Haqim == asleep under the Mountain.
        *Brujah == Time-stopped the diablerie, let Trolie think he got the power, but he really didn't. Manipulated the fall of Carthage just to punish him.
        *Cappadocious/Ashur == body diablerized, but soul escaped. Currently, the second Charon in the Underworld. About to make a surprising comeback.
        *Gangrel == Alive and well, and sleeping in the Earth, somewhere in northeastern Europe.
        *Augustus Giovanni == active, well, and due for a very unpleasant surprise. Hangs his hat at the Mausoleum in Venice, Italy.
        *Lasombra/Mekhet == actually destroyed Montano. The current "Montano" is a shadow construct made from abyssal darkness that helps manipulate the Sabbat on his behalf while he stays to the shadows (literally).
        *Malkav == body slumbering still beneath Jerusalem; psyche is the Malkavian Madness Network. Rather than possessing one childer, he's riding shotgun in ALL of them.
        *Nosferatu/Absimiliard == body slumbers somewhere in Eastern Europe; mind is driving the Nictuku to finish their job.
        *Ravnos == the thing that fought the endless running battle through India and Bangladeh? That was what a Chimestry 10 Illusion. Suckers.
        *Saulot == diablerized but took over Tremere
        *Set/Sutekh == slumbers beneath the Sphinx.
        *Toreador/Arikel == alive well, and some incredibly famous, drop dead gorgeous "mortal" celebrity, either through Possession, or having found a way to ignore the effects of the sun.
        *Tremere == Etrius' Body, repowered to 3rd Gen by the Antitribu Ritual
        *Tzimisce == Believed diablerized; has actually become the Earthly personification of the Wyrm, like Sephiroth taking over Jenova; is currently corrupting the Earth itself.

                                I find the Lasombra Antediluvian very fascinating.
                                It's so obvious no one really knows what It was all about - even Montano confesses to be ignorant of Its history and motives in the Lasombra Trilogy. And yet we know it to be a creature of such great passions and intense desires that overshadow (no pun intended) any rational approach to It.
                                I've been toying around in my head with some loose ideas about old Lasombra. Directly below is some information about the Antediluvian from various books - see if it reminds you about anybody else:

                                It codified strict laws for It's descendants. It was known to be a wanderer, roaming the world. It's progeny never seemed to live up to Its standards. It often ruled directly over It's offspring, serving as a great Patriarch, the final authority of it's line. It was so intimately connected to darkness that all names and titles people gave to It reflected that (for example the King of Shadows).

                                It kind of reminds me of what we think we know about Caine.

                                So what if the line of Lasombra was supposed to be the kingship Clan, the heirs of Caine. Imagine Lasombra being embraced by someone of the Second generation as a tribute to their Dark Father. Driven by passions, ruling by strength, crafting a kingdom within darkness.
                                I can also see Caine not liking that picture of himself, Caine being a vain creature that never ever did anything wrong (notice his rationales for killing his own brother). So Caine, instead favoured others of the Third generation, including the Ventrue Antediluvian, and the Second generation were influenced to do the same. In some sense the Jyhad among the Antediluvians may have started here, about the mantle of Caine, shaking the Second City after the murders of the Second generation.
                                The Lasombra Clan symbol is a black crown after all, their ancient symbol for their claim to the legacy of Caine. The Ventrue Clan symbol on the other hand is a sceptre, which in this theory reflects the Ventrue originally intended as a Vizier line.

                                Then on the other hand, like has been pointed out by MrAshur for instance, a case can also be made for connecting Lasombra to Lilith (Queen of Night, connected to waters, growing through pain, etc).

                                I like the Lasombra antediluvian, there are so many directions you can go with It

                                            Oh, I love these threads!

                                            My pet theory is drawn from some plot hooks in Gehenna, and implications about how Generation works in Dark Ages, plus a throw away line in the Revised ST Handbook.

                                            Effectively, the potency of Generation is proportional to human population. i.e., there are only as many vampires as the population can support, ever. So, the more humans there are, the more you can stretch out Caine's blood. In the time of the Second City, 5th was probably as high as you could get. 6th were Caitiff, at best. When you get to the Dark Ages, the kine population has ballooned, and suddenly you can go all the way up to 12th, and 13th generation Kindred are mostly clanless. No one really remembers this because Generation is nearly impossible to track accurately, and there's no consistency about who really can embrace and who can't. By the time the 13 Generation aren't always Caitiff, no one gives it much thought.

                                            The modern nights have finally seen the Curse stretch to its absolute limit, regardless of population size. 14th Generation vampires will never be able to breed true consistently, and thus the population of Caitiff and Thin Bloods swells. Time of thin blood ensues, Antediluvians wake due to murder of all the Caitiff, the end.

                                            My alternate theory is that Generation works precisely as written, but Gehenna comes in cycles. Every time the Kindred population gets too big, the Antediluvians wake up, devour most of their childer, and Embrace a few Methusalehs so there'll be something to feast on when they wake up again. The Jyhad is merely a way of fostering conflict so that more childer will be Embraced. The modern nights are reaching the end of the most recent cycle. Yes, it will mean the death of most Kindred, but life on earth will go on, and there will be new vampires to populate it.

                                                    My head cannon: [Brujah] is Ilyes, and was seemingly destroyed by Troile. Ilyes founded Ilium (of "The Iliad" fame) and sired Troile there. Those Brujah that know (or remember) think of the Trujah much like soldiers thought of Conciencious Objectors during WWI.

                                                        Enoch, Irad, Zilla and many other unnamed ones would drop to what we know as 4th, 5th, 6th, 7th, 8th and worse generations according to their individual accomplishments or lack of them up to that point, a badge of secret shame through the following ages, made the lesser of their brethen and childer by failures of their own spirits. So it's possible for one to be both "Antediluvian" and "4th generation" or much, much worse... though admitedly the absolute majority of the lesser ones destroyed themselves long before the modern age.

                                                        Those at the peak of their Roads/Dharmas, the wisest individiual among 3rd, 4th and even unlawfully embraced 5th generations, would become the Clan Founders while many other vampires that were "antediluvian" in origin, those of the 2nd generation among them.
