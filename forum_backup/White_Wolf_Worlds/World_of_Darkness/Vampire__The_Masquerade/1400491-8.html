        hell, they don't even use attomic bombs or spirit nukes...hmm

        Somehow, I feel like Magic resistance would be innate to very non-magical people. The sorcery equivelent to fairy Banality if you will. But as it doesn't explicitly say that, and you're likely to put that in the headcanon box, I think it's likely that demons could remove magic resistance with an investiture. The most important thing to note, however, is that magic resistance is a very rare merit and unlikely to be of importance in the grand scheme of things.

        As for Demonic investments
        So like, every book that mentions them has a different system for them. I've made my own little meta-analysis.

        Answer one: Diamonion 6 can basically do all of that. Just buy it again and again. Personally I feel Diamonion7+ should just be more impressive Diamonion 6 powers or -become a demon and give people investments whilst harvesting worship- Similar disciplines, like Spiritus and Valeren and if we push a little; Protean, Could also endow the vampire with investment-like effects at levels 6+
        In 1e, Diamonion and a bunch of other "infernal" disciplines were granted by demons. Later on, after they dropped a bunch of those disciplines from canon, it seems to be implied that DT is something demons grant and Diamonion is just a vampiric discipline cultivated by those with a lot of infernal contact. I imagine you can develop it by feeding from thralls and demonically tainted people. Of course, Baali are a little charged for their satanism so in some books they're perfectly normal vampires who are close to the infernal and in other books they're the nephandi of vampires. V5 should hopefully set straight that Diamonion as something you learn just from drinking the right kind of blood. (but then again V5 won't have Diamonion 6 so...)

        Answer two: This might be kinda cheating because you're using the demon still.
        Bind the Demon.
        Learn the spells, Learn it's true name, kill it's thralls, command it to serve you, force it into a pretty rock that you'll hide away when you're done with it. It takes a lot of effort to do this rather than just create a normal pact. But it seems to be the case that you can control a demon and then force it to grant you investments. This is of course, highly dangerous because it can go wrong as that demon you've bound so wishes to turn the table on you. The important thing is that you don't become a thrall and get the investments you want. The downside is that you're going to be pretty paranoid (also you probably spent about half a decade of your life to achieve this thing and, player wise, put some precious points into Demonology)

        Looking at all the different DT stuff, I'm gonna go out on a limb and say the best system is to just to put powers into levels and offer them at 3x level... by no coincidence is this exactly like werewolf.... (But perhaps offer discounts if they wanna disfigure themselves for powers)

        (Dark Thaumaturgy, by the way, I'm just gonna throw it out their that I think you can learn those paths, it's just few do because they're pretty sick, people don't like recording them, and they are so heavily associated with infernalism)
