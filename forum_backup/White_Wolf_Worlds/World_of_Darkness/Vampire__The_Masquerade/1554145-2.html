    Yeah my idea for Saulot is basically as follows.
    As an human Saulot is from the ancient tribes of Israel and is so old he's effectively an Henotheist rather than a montheist as an priest of the cult of Yaweh.Following his embrace He initially flirted with Monotheism with the confirmation of Caine and Abel but slowly slid back into Henotheism as he grew more aware of supplementary spiritual presences on earth. He ultimately perceives most alternative 'Gods' as either evil or simply unworthy of worship and see's most of them as a threat to humanity and good. This resulted in him neglecting his spiritual duties to his self and failing golconda as he concluded it was more important to destroy evil and protect the world than cultivate his own spiritual enlightenment-this divide within him is represented by the emergence of the Warrior, watchers and the healer Salubri as his contradictory agenda's are reflected in clan identity.

    in his defense of the world against evil he's had mixed result, successfully working with the other clans to end the Baali threat but also souring relations with the Kuei-jin due to a number of diplomatic gaffes. as the corruption and the depravity of the clans became more apparent he increasingly soured on the other third generation and undermines the most evil of them when he can. He is actively also working to prevent gehenna and is directly responsible for the eldests' first death. some of the strategic blunders of the nastier 3rd gen are due to not realizing Saulot's errors. Notably Lasombra with his failed attempt at apotheosis via obternerbration

    The diablerie of Tremere was a massive fuck up as he initially planned to integrate the Tremere as a magus arm of the Clan Salubri to help exterminate the fiends as well as gain incredibly levels of magical insight. unfortunately Tremere was no patsy and by the time he'd managed to wrestle control of Tremere's body Clan Salubri had been exterminated and he'd lost centuries of time. A massive disaster for his long term plans on a number of fronts and is now scrambling to catch up as the sheer scale of spiritual threats in the 21st century is alarming.

        Pretty cool idea!
        When I want to wander away from the more-or-less canon or "inteded" metaplot, usually I will go with the Followers of Set interpretation
        In those cases the Salubri fall within the domain of Gemini and their Progenitor was Lady Bastet.
        Bastet was the daughter of Ra, born from Ra's strong gaze. She was the goddes of joy, pleasure, music, and dance (hence the Salubri's powers related to songs, like Song of Reyzeel) and protection; She protected homes from evil spirits, disease, and pests like rodents and snakes (explaining the warding and healing powers of Valeren) Her domain was also the Fertility and domesticity: Her connection to fertility was strong, and she was associated with women, children, childbirth, and the home (again healing powers of Valeren and the care the Salubri gave, like a mother), She also was know as the "Eye of Ra," (on of many) a powerful, protective force
        However... Bastet had a darker aspect; as one of the "Eye of Ra", she was not only a a powerful, protective force but sometimes destructive force sent by the sun god to take vengeance on his enemies. When she was called upon to fight in vengeance she was Sekhmet, the fierce lioness goddess of war, goddess of destruction, vengeance, healing and plagues who once almost destroyed Humanity. (explaining the Warrior's Valeren powers)
        When Ra commanded her to go and wreak havoc upon humanity for their failure to uphold Ma'at. She did as instructed and began a horrible slaughter, killing almost everything in her sight. When Ra looked down upon the Earth, he was horrified upon seeing the carnage Sekhmet had caused and ordered her to stop and cease her rampage. However, Sekhmet, who had become overcome with bloodlust, refused and continued her rampage. Desperate to stop Sekhmet, Ra assembled the other gods and together they devised a trick: they took several jars of wine and mixed it with red pomegranate juice (which stained the wine blood red), whereupon they poured the mixture onto Sekhmet's path. Sekhmet, believing the mixture to be blood, gorged herself on it and became drunk, passing out soon after. (In this "Saulot" aka Bastet/Sekhmet rampaged and sired even the Baali as a tool to help to destroy the world - untill she was calmed down)

        In this version Saulot was female, had an affinity for felines and after coming to her senses a very remorsefull "goddess" who knew she almost wiped out Humanity - the Kine and foodsource of the other vampires, thus finally managed to explain why the other clans "hated" the noble and protetive Salubri and eagerly threw them udner the bus when opportunity arised... they never forget their progenitor almost caused the extinction of their whole race and every Salubri carried in themselves this dual nature
