                The Tzmisce, with vicissitude, are a perfectly functional clan. They might do things that have you question such, but on the whole they work: A non-restictive weakness when it comes to food types, Animalism is a good discipline for night-to night living, Auspex is a great discipline, Vicissitude used properly is a great discipline for living a full unlife; they can be beautiful for easy feeding, change their appearance for their safety, it can be weaponized in emergency, it can be used upon others to gain good allies, bloodform is just super convenient... Without Vicissitude they're very functional discount-Ventrue.

                The Kiasyd just don't function.
                The Harbringers of skulls are also shit, in that they have the curse of ugly but don't have obfuscate. But they're saved by the fact that they're exclusively elders and used to be something else when they were neonates.
                Blood brothers totally work. They're freakish but there's nothing problematic about their function.

                Most other bloodlines function pretty well. Gargoyles are questionable on their own but work well as dependents. I feel some bloodlines (Capadocians and Salubri) have limited themselves very badly (which is why they got eaten) but they're still functional as small clans/bloodlines.

                                            09-05-2020, 06:58 PM
                                            Many of the bloodlines seemed to exist as greater setting mysteries to be explored and resolved. They really weren't meant to be as characters, but a plot device. But whether there actually was a greater mystery to be resolved (or it was simply JJ Abrams style Mystery Box hack BS) became irrelevant when their creators moved on, and the new developers and writers dropped it because of lack of interest and moved on to other things.

                                            When I ST my own games, my own rule is to never bring in vampires of a bloodline unless I have a specific plot in mind that requires them (as opposed that I could use any other vampires to do the same). So I've never used those bloodlines at all.

                                            Exception to those bloodlines that are just clans whose antedeluvians were slain since I consider them to simply to be members of the original clan.

                                                        Heh. You know, the first time I learned of Masquerade was back in school.
