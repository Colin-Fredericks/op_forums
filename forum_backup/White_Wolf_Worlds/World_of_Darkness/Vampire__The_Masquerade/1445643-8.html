                I think it’s a silly idea that the Camarilla could take over or absorb the Ashirra. Even if Europeans thoroughly conquered and subjugated the Middle East, that is just happening on the mortal side of things. The ancient vampires of the Middle East aren’t going to want to bow down to a foreign vampire organization just because the humans in their domains got conquered. Also, vampires are inherently conservative and territorial, which means that if Camarilla vampires were going to the Middle East to try to influence vampire politics there, it would likely be younger Camarilla vampires who will be opposed by the mighty elders of the Middle East.

                If there was never a single organized Middle Eastern sect then maybe the Camarilla could make inroads into the region. Not because they were conquered but because the Camarilla mostly allows for local autonomy and so local Middle Eastern vampires might see the utility of joining a centralized organization. Especially if the Anarch Revolts make inroads into the Middle East.

                If we wanted to make the Middle East part of the Camarilla, I would just say that back in the Middle Ages there was no central vampiric government in Europe or the Middle East and that when the Anarch Revolt began, it spread through both Europe and the ME and the Camarilla was created not only by European vampires but by an alliance of vampires across Europe, North Africa, and Western Asia. This makes the Camarilla and the Anarchs (and later Sabbat) not exclusively European from the beginning. There is no reason that vampire politics have to be sharply divided between Islam and Christendom, the elders of the time likely predate both religions.

                My problem with the Ashirra is that it’s too unified. In the West we have the Camarilla, the Sabbat, and the Anarchs along with various other independent groups. Is the ME just the Ashirra? That seems like the default way of thinking of other cultures when you don’t know much about them: they are all together, they are all the same, they are unified, etc. Kindred of the East really went overboard with that and made all East Asian supernatural creatures one big happy family.

                If vampires of the Middle East were to be better fleshed out, I would want to see the conflicts of the region. What sects are there and what are they fighting over? How did mortals influence vampire politics? A big deal is made over the Catholic Church and the Inquisition hunting vampires, why wouldn’t there be some Islamic equivalent to that? It seems like devout Muslims would be vampire killers as much as Catholics would be.

                        The problems with the Ashirra being on equal footing with the Camarilla are the following.

                        1) Resources and power base. No matter how you put it, Western world is the top of the world when it comes to power. United States and European Union account for roughly the 50% of the global GDP. The most powerful armies, technologies and financial power the world has ever known are in the West. Now, maybe the Vampires post revised do not "control" the world of mortals (disputable), but they have access to their resources. This is not colonialism, it's GDP, currency reservers, numbers of nukes, capital inflow. That' s hard data. So the power base of the Camarill dwarf the power base of the Ashirra. In simpler terms, Camarilla has the biggest guns. And we know from Midnight Siege that these guns are always used in Sect Warfare.


                        2) Elders. The Methuselahs are on the Camarilla side. The biggest shot among the Ashirra is Mullah Suleiman ibn Abdullah, a 7th generation Lasombra born in 299 CE. The close second were the Nosferatu Tarique (5th gen, embrace 807 CE), the 5th gen Gangrel Methuselah Zayaat and the Assamite Talaq. On the Camarilla side of things you have people like Mithras, Montano and similar fellows.
                        It's just like what happened in Spain, in Dark Ages. Two factions of Lasombra, one christian, one muslim, fighting for the control of Spain. Who wins? The Christians, because the Amici Noctis say so, and decide to put their weight behind the Christian faction, thanks to Boukephos.

                        3) The Ashirra are not the leading power of the Arab World. The Ashirra are pretty regional, and mostly focused on Saudi Arabia.
                        North Africa is Camarilla- Sabbat- and Setite land. Istanbul was not under their control and in Egypt and Cairo they held barely foothold, and Gerusalem is split in two. Even more it is stated that even in the heart of the Middle East the Assamite are far more powerful than the Ashirra. Both the Followers of Set and the Assamite are more powerful organization than the Ashirra.


                        Which means that any partnership between the Camarilla and the Ashirra is destined to end in an uneven partnership.
