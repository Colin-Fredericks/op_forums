    Faith in Demons

    06-09-2022, 08:56 PM
    So, here I am again with another "Hot topic" lol

    This is something I have discussed with players and storytellers alike, since I was a kid, but I don't have a definite answer. What's your thoughts on the issue of someone having True Faith, but for demons, etc? Since I was a kid, I had many players ask for this kind of things when I allowed (I rarely did, but I did it a couple of times in the last 20 years) them to make infernalist characters. What do you think of True Faith for infernalists? Isn't it too broken and kind of "antitematic"? Or would you say it's acceptable?

                                      It depends on which setting and rules you are using. Clanbook Baali and the Mage book "Infernalism: The Path of Screams" have "Infernal Faith".
                                      Although some folk would like to deny that such Faith is possible, it infests a small but powerful elite. With it, Black Monks and Queens of Hell defile holy places, menace angels and drive the children of God from their sight.
                                      It functions like True Faith, with a caveat that you get the Echoes flaw at level 4 or 5. It also makes you do Highlander throwdown if you meet someone with regular True Faith. The Baali merit melts crosses and gives heart attacks to the priests.

                                      Conversely, DAV20 disavows such things, demanding that True Faith be in something positive.

                                      So ultimately, pick what fits narratively. If your game would be improved with dark miracles and subjugation of creatures of the night to a high power - go for it.

                                      Personally, I don't really like True Faith effects as written, they are very weapon-y, and that's not what I'm about. My characters call upon true faith to provide support and hope. Or, with Infernal faith - soul-crushing despair.
