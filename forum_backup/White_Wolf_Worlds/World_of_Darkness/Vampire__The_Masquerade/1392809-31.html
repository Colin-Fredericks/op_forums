                Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

                What I didn't like

                1. Not much Sabbat information: We get some allusions to what the Sabbat are up to in the Gehenna War and that some are doing their usual thing when the Lasombra defect but I felt they could have inserted a little info about the Sabbat.

                2. Obternation: Frankly, I think some of the combinations of Disciplines don't make much sense. Quietus is mixed with Blood Sorcery? Necromancy is mixed with Obternation? I feel like the combination is a good idea in general but I feel like the choices are counter-intuitive. Obternation and Quietus should have been mixed with Obfuscate. Necromancy should have mixed with Blood Sorcery.

                3. The Lasombra Bane: Is ridiculous as there's no reason that Lasombra should have a problem with technology. This is nothing that was hinted at in previous editions and makes their Bane even worse than the Nosferatus (which was dialed back to "really fucking ugly" not "monstrous nightmare creature"). It also means they have to have ghouls when I think the Lasombra have never been a ghoul-heavy clan, that's been the Tzimisce.

                4. No Lucita: Nuff said. Even more so if she's actually dead-dead.

                5. Lasombra craftiness is told not shown: The Lasombra are going to agree to be Blood Bound to the Princes because they know about the Vinculum and the Camarilla doesn't. Also, they know that they have to kill a bunch of Sabbat on their way out so, "Deliver a dead Sabbat Elder for your defection" is fine. However, that's mostly left to inference. We should have had it spelled out the Keepers were playing the Camarilla.
                2 - quietus was blood sorcery turned into a discipline, even in MtA it was considered blood sorcery.
                Obfuscate is a mind power,
                unless you're suggesting turning obfuscate into a not-a-mind-power, which I might be ok with

                4 - she should've been the one to die along with Talley,
                instead they killed Valez, and Valez wasn't even an elder, actually she's not even ancillae, she a neonate, disgraced abbott disowned by her sire with no known ties to the Amici or whatever,
                and she's the one taking one for the hood?
                and Talley was ok with that?
                and he's the good guy in all this?
                not that I'd be surprised if Talley was too dumb to tell the difference (guy is a plank), and considering the the Camarilla history in V5, they're probably just as dumb

                5 - nah that's just the authors showing you how the camarilla is turning into the sabbat,
                just wait till the sabbat are back and realize the sect got bigger in their absence,
                Lasombra are playing some 18 dimensional chess game,
                Darth Sidious would be proud

The Camarilla allows open religion because they're mostly elders now who don't need to lie to a bunch of neonates/Religious persecution's not cool anymore/The Sabbat elders asked for it. But I still dislike the way the former is done, and the latter... I think the discipline's point was that it was freaky demon shit, it should be very overt. Little shadows aren't really that upsetting. I think, if things were the otherway round, and a once-subtle discipline became very overt, i'd be upset about it, because generally I think powers should follow the path-of-least-resistance when it comes to special effects and the masquerade. But the power has been established as freakishly horrifying for four editions now, and it's overt supernatural nature has been one of the power's "weaknesses" to ofset it's strength. What we have in V5 suits the Camarilla better. What we had before suited the Sabbat better. I can't really imagine someone path-of-nighting with the new less overt obtenebration.
