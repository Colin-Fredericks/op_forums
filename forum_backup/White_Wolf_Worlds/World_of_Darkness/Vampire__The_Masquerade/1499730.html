              Originally posted by MrNatas View Post
              I wonder of the age of the book has anything to do with there being no title "Sheriff" or "Scourge."

              I know there a few books that don't have primogen councils, at least in name, because they where just old enough to not have that kind of title made for them yet.
              Both a Primogen Council and a Sheriff appear in the 1st city module, Chicago by Night. The point is that they were supposed to be a feature of that city, no universal positions to be had at every city. Other cities that were released later had different structures, with only the Prince as a common feature. For example in Denver (Alien Hunger) the Prince is in a Triumvirate with a hidden elder and his Childe. The Prince of Cleveland (Blood Nativity) has 4 Lieutenants. In Milwaukee (Milwaukee by Night) there is a Council (actually the 1st to present the one per Clan idea, because in Chicago there are 2 Brujah but no Malkavian or Ventrue); there is no Sheriff, though, but the Anubi, a institution that fights the werewolves and, as an aside, helps to keep order.
              At some point the developers started getting unimaginative and tried to shoehorn a series of standard positions in every city, regardless of its personality.
              I still like to keep the original style, and for my Chicago+Midwest by Night game I have developed different Domain structures. It's quite refreshing to watch the PC's having to deal with different situations as they arrive, instead of just already having a fixed idea of what to find.

                          The standardization of a domain's "court" really came about because of LARP. The Mind's Eye books seemed to cement in everyone's mind that a domain always had certain positions (that PCs could fill) and that the Primogen had one representative for each clan (that PCs could fill). But that was not how the actual city sourcebooks described it.

                          Obviously you could backfill in some positions. Neally would have been Lodin's Seneschal even though he was not called as such. "Sheriff" would of course be the Sheriff. Many of the "elders" mentioned in the Coterie section would in fact be the Harpies. But positions like Scourge or Keeper of Elysium did not seem to exist.

                          Early books would mention the Primogen and Harpies, but otherwise each Prince seemed to decide for himself what kind of henchmen he needed to get things done.

                          An interesting thing about Chicago's Primogen Council is that it showed Primogen were not about clans, but about powerful individuals. The Ventrue clan, despite ostensibly being the single most powerful clan in all of Chicago, did not have anyone on the Primogen Council. Although there were adventures about Ballard hoping to become one. There were two Brujah on the Council, and one interpretation of the domain's history implied there might have been three Brujah (when Procet was around).

                          So many of the things players "know" from LARP/Revised that cemented certain positions in the Revised era going forward actually did not exist in the original game.

                          = = = =
                          Besides ignoring the actual name for the position, all a Seneschal is someone who handles routine administrative tasks for the Prince. All a Sheriff is someone who is willing to get her knuckles bruised being the muscle for the Prince in order to enforce his rules. So the question of who is the Seneschal or Sheriff of Marcus Vitel in DC by Night is who, if anyone, performed these tasks?

                          I believe this is best answered by the coterie charts, particularly the section called The Prince's Allies.

                          The Ventrue Gino Martinelli handles a lot of things that would often be done by a Seneschal, but is mainly limited to influence matters of mortal law enforcement and the underworld. His childe Karina Dobson also does some of this work, as she arranges political matters with Kindred in other domains. We also know that Vitel's childe Monica Black is mentioned to be "second only to the Prince." She's also one of the Harpies and has extensive influence among the lobbyists and legal fraternity. I would say that the Seneschal position is probably split between them. Monica probably handles a lot of Vitel's interaction with Kindred, seemingly on an informal basis; while Gino does a lot of the actual work managing the city through mortals.

                          In terms of enforcers, we're told that Marcus prefers to utilize up and coming neonates by giving them tasks to do and areas to control, but subsequently undermining them but offering to forgive them if they blood bond themselves to him. I say collectively these Kindred make up the Sheriff, at least in terms of how the Camarilla sees it. Marcus manages these (unnamed and unmentioned coterie) Kindred, but they do the dirty work of enforcing things. Secretly there is also the Ventrue Karin Dobson who does Marcus's intelligence gathering and controls a team of Delta Force trained ghouls who can eliminate people. Of course, Marcus' "real" "Sheriff" and "Scourge" is the Sabbat Jack whom he uses to destroy Kindred he wants eliminated. I believe that most of the DC Camarilla understands that Marcus has a variety of Kindred acting in the "Sheriff" role, but nobody knows the extent of it or who does what.

                          Marcus Vitel likes to delegate a lot of work, but he prefers to work with a dispersed crowd and not concentrate power in his subordinates. He doesn't get his hands dirty himself, but he certainly makes a lot of arrangements with the Kindred who do. That has to keep the many very busy.

                          If I didn't want to reveal to the PCs (and NPC Kindred) how Vitel actually operates, and present something that seems to match the "standard" roles, I would tell people that Monica acts as his Seneschal and Gino as his Sheriff. But then let the PCs figure out for themselves that they don't seem to actually do all the duties they would assume, and that there are plenty of other people doing bits of each role.

                                                          Originally posted by Baaldam View Post
                                                          That as an aside, in Milwaukee back then i'd say this function was played (unofficially) more by Turk and the Union anarch gang than the Anubi
                                                          Milwaukee is a very strange case. It's said that Hrothulf was the original Prince, but when he stepped down he gave domain to the Council of Elders who appointed the next Prince(s). But while the Prince runs the city and administers the Traditions, it seems like it is the Council (collectively) rather than the Prince who holds domain. It seems to have a lot more formal powers (and be formally organized) than the the traditional Primogen. If I was running a chronicle using MbN, I'd interpret this to mean while the Prince has all the formal powers of Prince that the Council could decide to veto a decision or command the Prince to take a specific action based on a majority of the Council votes.

                                                          As for who would be Terence Merik's "Sheriff", my reading of the book is that Merik used several people to enforce his commands - himself (he has some good combat stats and has a Celerity + Protean 2 (Wolf Claws) combination of Disciplines), his ghouls and mortals, and secret use of Lupines to kill people through his Gangrel "wife's" connections with the Lupines. These were Lupines in a "Mouse" pack, but given that this is an early product that does not match the later Werewolf setting we would need to make appropriate revisions. I'd probably make them some kind of Bone Gnawer pack under Rat who had personal reasons to be allies with a fairly humane Gangrel. His use of the Lupines to secretly eliminate his enemies would have ended once his wife was destroyed, so it makes sense that he would create "the Mask" as replacement agents and "the Mask" (aka the PCs) in Psychomachia essentially fulfill the role of a sheriff. Combined with his Domination of Turk and the Union, he did a good job of keeping his enemies divided. And of course Decker and the Anubi were freely providing their services to keep the domain safe from the Lupines in general which would normally be something a Prince would handle. To outsiders, it'd look like Merik was his own enforcer and was very good at using divide & conquer tactics. But he was getting a LOT of help even though it didn't look like it.
