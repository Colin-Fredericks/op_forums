        Some more random thoughts about dusty Cainites history:

        •A major milestone in kindred history during antiquity was the rise of Zoroastrianism during the time of the Achaemenid Dynasty of the Persian Empire (~550-330 BCE) and the importance of this faith both In-Universe and Out-of-Universe cannot be overstated. It is often considered by historians to have potentially inspired vast portions of modern Abrahamic theological beliefs, perhaps even practices which have shaped the world today. A faith that forbid animal sacrifice, called humanity to rally behind a good god of fire and life and day who would destroy an evil god of death and night in a great cataclysm. The comparisons came readily to the Noddists. The signs of Gehenna foretold...

        •This period of kindred history was before the true rise of Rome. The Second City had fallen millennia ago and the antediluvians and their children were dispersed. Troy had fallen and Illyes with it. Troile and Moloch were lovers in Carthage still. Few of Caine's grandchildren were left in Persia to see the new faith rise. Haqim in his wandering perhaps, Ashur from his Caucasus catacombs maybe, but one was definitely there to see it. The perfect one to recognize the significance of this. He who could predict the approach of The One Above: Malkav...

        •For reasons known only to himself, but perhaps because his insights gave him specific perspective, Malkav used his sway over the region to assure that kindred heeded the new Zoroastrian mage-priest who ruled the cities that had once been theirs. Those who refused the elders guidance were either consumed by flame for war against the night or fell to the machinations of jyhad. Open reign and worship fell out of practice amongst the kindred. Persia became the first place to feel the gentle brushes of The Masquerade. All thanks to the foresight of Malkav perhaps.

        •In the centuries to come much of Persia, Judea and other parts of the Holy Lands would become unbearable for kindred. The land itself would begin to weaken or destroy them or even drive them mad. Noddists blamed The One Above, others blamed Caine or Lilith, some blamed Malkav. Regardless, The Web of Faith claimed these lands and all who defied them were destroyed either mentally or physically.

        Only those who followed Malkav's guidance would remain... and some not even then.

                Here's some more ruminations on Kindred history.

                + It's difficult to say just how DIFFERENT life is in the Modern Era from life prior to the Dark Ages.

                + Prior to the Camarilla and Anarch Revolt, there were a handful of cities where vampires actually lived in groups. Before that, cities pretty much existed as dominated by one Clan or another that was very often a miniature pyramid tracing back up to an elder who had been there since the cities founding. Each level of these pyramids would be Blood Bound to the Elders above them and ruled like gods. The only thing resembling freedom was when something miraculous happened and these Elders died, freeing their children directly beneath them or they went into torpor (always scared it would happen again). Being a vampire was, by and large, hell for everyone but a handfull because only a few vampires were masters and even seemingly strong ones were blood bond to their masters as well as heavily dominated or Presenced.

                + The Anarch Revolt actually won despite the fact the majority of its members surrendered. This is difficult to parse because, well, surrender but the fact the Blood Bond was not something immediately inflicted and actually had a lot of opposition from most Kindred (in the context only of, "that I don't want it to happen to me") was a major change in Kindred society. So many Elders and Methuselahs had been killed that the pyramids of power that used to exist simply did not exist anymore and the Vinculum broke many more. Other vampires formed their own lineages and reproduced without bonding their childer. The Inquisition also, ironically, helped bring this out. The fact that cities became cosmopolitan places where vampires had free passage, the Bond had to be enforced by law or was done in secret, and there were ANY rights to Kindred made the Camarilla look ludicrously permissive.

                + Speaking of which, one of the greatest cons by Hardestadt is the LUDICROUS claim he made the Antediluvians didn't exist. He was definitely playing the long game here since virtually every single signatory at the Convention of Thorns knew the Antediluvians existed. Lasombra, Tzimisce, Cappadocias, Tremere, and others were ones most of them knew personally. Ventrue and Toreador had Embraced some signatories as late as the 700s. It was a bet that paid off, though, and is now accepted by many Embraced later.

                + But WHY did Hardestadt do this? This is due to the fact the Camarilla would always be subject to the laws of the Antediluvians and decrees in the past as long as they existed. He was taking an enormous gamble by setting the Inner Council up as the highest authority in the land. Ironically, he DID believe in Gehenna and believed the next time the Antediluvians woke, it would be the last--or he could persuade them to spare him by showing the Camarilla he constructed. It's part of the reason why Mithras disdained the organization so much because it required him to submit to effective children by his standards and he wasn't THAT old of an ancient.

                                Now this might be a headcanon that bothers some people so I'm gonna give a prologue. I'm a person who prefers 20th edition, but I'm generally not bothered by other editions. I respect Chronicles, X5, & previous editions for their niches and what they provide. I don't like discarding things out of turn. I may prefer WoD 20th but I have 0 compunction against stripping ideas from V5 or VtR that I like and think that could be well used to enhance the game. That aside, a bit of context. This is directly inspired by a concept from VtR 2e 'The Contaminent Worm'.

                                •Vampirism is a curse and an ancient one. The One Above, whether or not they are truly the God of Israel, once cursed an ancient murderer who has since been dubbed 'Caine'. All vampires are said to be descended from him and his curse, from him they gain the divisions of clan and generation. Yet the curse is a dark thing. It seeks to torment and destroy. It is said to be apart of the world's fall from grace. Like Adam's curse to toil, Eve's curse of painful birth, Caine's curse would taint all humanity...

                                •The Beast seeks to nourish itself and propagate itself. It paradoxically loathes competition and longs to procreate. It demands power yet also desires to create inevitable rivals to said power. Every kindred strains humanity's capacity to nourish them, yet they grow more and more...

                                •The Curse ties all Cainites to Caine and their specific progenitor. Whether the lesser progenitor of a bloodline or the great progenitor of a clan, that taint reaches through the ages like the Sin of the Father's and reaches all kindred. Brujah rage as Troile's impotent fury rushes through them, Gangrel tend to the fringes as Ennoia did, Malkav spreads his presence like a plague of madness, etc.

                                •This is all to say that the blood carries the taint of a curse and so whatever the kindred touches it plagues. We see this already through Blood Dolls and Ghouls. Ghouls reflect watered down versions of their domitor's curse and The Kiss carries an addictive quality that draws mortals to kindred and drives them to adapt to better suit that Cainites need so as to please them and receive further hits of The Kiss.

                                •Cainites, however grandly or minutely, are a plague upon this Gothic-Punk world. The dingy apartment where the Brujah fledgling makes her Haven finds it's occupants slowly growing more and more restless as the Cainites remains. The great Ventrue that rests below the metropolitan city of New England and drives it to become a mercantile juggernaut. Cainites ooze their curse and all of humanity suffers for it. Not only do they hunt humanity and drain their blood, they share their unique forms of misery with everyone and everything around themselves.

                                -Some ST may not like this as much as it does broaden the supernatural footprint of vampires and diminish certain elements of realism, I personally like it for expanding the thematic impact of The Curse and I feel it feeds into the aesthetics of Gothic-Punk very nicely as well as providing IC justification for why the world mirrors the themes and moods at play in storytelling.

                                            When Saulot traveled East, he met the Cathayans. This proved to him that Caine was a liar. Caine had told his progeny that they were the only of their kind, forever cursed and alone. Yet here were an entirely different race of similar creatures. Worse yet, Caine had told his Brood they were forever cursed in the eyes of the Maker, and yet these Eastern Kindred had found a way to achieve inner peace. These revelations caused great turmoil inside of Saulot. He searched and eventually found a potential way to resolve his inner conflict. He decided to perform a powerful and dangerous ritual said to be able to balance one's Yin and Yang. Perhaps something went wrong, or perhaps something went right, but at the end of the ritual, Saulot found he had been split into two perfectly equal, yet completely opposite beings. Two mirror images, one comprised almost entirely of Yin, the other of Yang... a "good Sault" and a "bad Saulot". Both versions returned west. The "bad Saulot" leaving behind the legacy of Zao-Lot the Betrayer in the East. When "good Saulot" arrived home, he brought knowledge of salvation in the form of Golconda. On the way back west, the "bad Saulot" created the Baali at the organ pit. Somehow when this threat surfaced, "good Saulot" somehow knew that "he" was responsible and thus inspired the Salubri to seek out and destroy these foul creations. Over the eons, these two different Saulots both remained active. The "good Saulot" eventually ended up at Hundorea Castle where he attempts to quietly guide as many Kindred as possible to Golconda. Meanwhile, "bad Saulot" orchestrated the near extinction of the Salubri, the rise of the Tremere Clan, became associated with the demon Kupula in the guise of The Stars Above, and generally plotted to bring about Gehenna. It is the "bad Saulot which was diablerized by Tremere in his attempt to transcend into something beyond Kindred and thus became the White Worm.

                                            This ties into my second point.

                                            Gehenna is indeed a cycle, occurring about ever 2000 years, give or take. But it's not just an apocalyptic event for Kindred, it's actually the end of each round of the Antedeluvians grand game. In their boredom due to thousands of years of existence, the Ancients resolved to play a game to see who could best the others. They decided to convene again in 2000 years, when they great Red Eye appears, marking the end of the game, for now. Then the Ancients all convene near the sights of the First and Second Cities and call their childer to them. They commend those who have been valuable pawns in their games, sending them back into the world to play another round. Those who have disappointed them are instead consumed in order to both sate the Ancients hunger and to reclaim their weak blood.

                                            The most recent round of the Eternal Struggle saw many of the Ancients seek to evolve beyond their original form. Saulot has become a White Worm, no doubt as part of a stage of metamorphosis into something else. Tzimisce became a living Flesh Sculpture that assumed all forms of life in an attempt to evolve. Lasombra shed his human form to become a creature of pure darkness in the Abyss. Ennoia seeks to meld with the very Earth itself. Cappadocius attempted to become a literal God. But now Wormwood has passed, signaling an end to this round of the game. Now the Ancients will meet and Beckon their childer to them as they tally their points. Although it seems possible that Ravnos and Cappadocius may have exited the game permanently. How many more Gehenna Cycles until only 1 remains?

                                                        .This is a useful one for my games:

                                                        * Golconda is actually not that difficult. This is something that would twist and turn the entirety of Kindred society into knots were it discovered. It is also the beginning of a path rather than an end. A Kindred who successfully undergoes the mystical ritual and masters their Beast no longer will frenzy in rage, rotschrek, or Hunger unless they are completely starved. This is actually not that huge of a change and there are more vampires who have achieved it than many believe.

                                                        * Golconda can be achieved through both Paths, Roads, and Humanity. It is not a state of spiritual redemption but UNION with your Beast. Once it is achieved, your Beast is just one other voice in your mind. One may argue it is not actually that moral as monsters who have achieved Golconda exist alongside vampiric saints.

                                                        * A vampire cannot fall out of Golconda. If they hit Humanity 0, they stay there and gain a derangement. Wassail is impossible.

                                                        * The stories of immunity to holy symbols, God's redemption (if such a thing exists), lowered generation, sunlight, and more does not exist. Vampires have achieved similar results with other rituals but these are mystical routes unrelated to the quieting of the Beast. Many vampires upon achieving Golconda seek new rituals, secrets of power, and mental heights so they may grow beyond the state but they are transvampirists rather than merely gone.

                                                            This might turn into a mini series or it might just be a couple for the clans I really enjoy, but let's talk one of my favorite clans.

                                                            GENERAL BRUJAH
                                                            •90-95% of Brujah are of Troile's lineage. The True Brujah exist but they are historically centered around Istanbul (once Constantinople), Baghdad, London, & New England.

                                                            •Brujah are the riotous clan. The clan of rebels and rioters. The revolutionaries. Brujah are both the loyal opposition within the Camarilla, leaders of the Sabbat, and the meat and potatoes of the Anarch Movement.

                                                            •The Brujah are the unofficial third clan of the Sabbat and if any clan can be said to lead the Anarchs, it would probably be the Brujah. Yet even within the Camarilla, Brujah elders make up some of the Ivory Tower's most staunch loyalists. Young kindred something's wonder how it is the Brujah were ever a high clan and this is the reason why. They are leaders.

                                                            BRUJAH HISTORY
                                                            •Illyes was indeed a philosopher, but more significantly he was a warrior-poet. He was not like Cappadocius who neglected all else for pursuit of genius. Illyes cared for learning and philosophy, but he did so to create and invent. He was a warrior who defended his tribe as well as being an inventor that pioneered his people's way of life. When he was brought into the night, that's what he brought to the First City. An engineer, an inventor, a political philosopher.

                                                            •Illyes was a warrior of the First City who fought and defended it. When demons came from the east, they battled for it. Yet as he grew older and older he felt his passions die as he grew trapped by the jyhad and Caine's law. He was bound by the limits placed on him by his elders and his own kin and he began lashing out in search of purpose. This is what lead him to his most infamous embrace...

                                                            •Troile burned with passion and purpose from the moment he met her. She was a raging orator and a fiercome woman and Illyes immediately knew he needed to have her. He took her into his brood and they entered a whirlwind together. She impassioned and challenged him and drove him and the two became infamous lovers. Their flared passions helped ignite the revolt against the Second Generation. Those who blame Troile for the revolt are not totally wrong, yet that isn't the full story either...

                                                            •Later, the two grew distant. Troile ruled Troy and Illyes came to rule in Athens and they turned to politics instead of each other. Troile was embittered in Illyes' return to cool study and power plays that suited himself while Troile continued to battle with her passions. What happened next is mystery. Perhaps Troile's jealousy overcame her. Perhaps Illyes entered into an affair with the ancient Helena or even Lilith herself to gain secret knowledge. Perhaps the souls of the second generation drove them both mad from within. Regardless, Troile devoured her sire during the events of the Trojan War which became a great terror of the jyhad that horrified each antediluvian and drove them further from the fourth generation.

                                                            •Troile would eventually be struck down alongside her lover Moloch in Carthage by a coalition of the other clans and buried beneath the razed city to be tormented forever. Some wonder though if not Illyes or Lilith played a key role in these events. Regardless, the Brujah clan claim Carthage as the Third City and uphold it as a lost paradise. The fury over its destruction is what shall forever more drive them...

                                                            •Troile was buried beneath the ruins of Carthage in furious torpor while Illyes may have drifted like a ghost through time, but their children were not restful. The Brujah have become the champions of change and revolution forevermore, particularly in their loathing of the Ventrue who destroyed Carthage. Brujah united with Gangrel and Nosferatu to lead Germanic tribes against the "Eternal City" of Rome. Brujah ignited, encouraged, and participated in countless revolts throughout history. While to say they edge the cause of these revolts wholly would be a gross mistake, near every revolt has a Brujah sat hungrily in its shadows.

                                                            •The Brujah were a high clan. In Europe, North Africa, and Asia this was true. Sons might have wondered why but once one has seen a Brujah whip nations into frenzy with one impassioned oration and lead skirmishes from the front lines, they could tell that this clan was made to lead and would never accept condescension. Millennia past and the taint of their usurpation faded under cloaks of polite etiquette until it was very nearly forgotten by all but the ancient.

                                                            •The beginnings of the Anarch Revolts can be found in young Brujah. As early as the 10th century the Brujah of Transylvania were rioting against the mistreatment they received from Tzimisce overlords and across the world whenever revolts were had, proto-Anarchs were born amongst the clan. Waiting for then night when finally the others erupted alongside them.

                                                            •Elder Brujah joined the Camarilla at the Convention of Thorns and those who did were committed to the sect, but more then perhaps any other clan they knew the fury of the young. They raged in debate to ensure that the young were guaranteed certain rights to keep them loyal. They knew so well the need perhaps because hordes of their own number now made up the fledgeling Sabbat and the young rogues who would form more proto-Anarchs.

                                                            •The Anarch Movement was perhaps formed in its modern iteration in the new world as part of the Golden Age of Piracy. Far from the freeholds and entrenched sect divides of the old world, these sailors drafted the first democracies since Athens and the Carribean was sometimes called 'Neau Carthage' or 'Carthage by Sea' and other such variations. These Brujah and young Anarchs would soon find sympathetic philosophers in the European enlightenment as well as throughout the Islamic Lands of North Africa.

                                                            •The Anarch Movement came into its own during the American and French revolutions and suddenly the new world was filled with Anarchs as much as it was Autarkis and Sabbat while even Europe was forced to realign as the Anarch Movement exploded in another series of revolts that eventually forced them to be acknowledged by both other sects although they had yet to fully claim their own major territories in the old world.

                                                            •During the 1800s, Anarch territories exploded to life across the new world. North America, South America, Central America, Australia, and the Carribean were all ground and pockets even emerged throughout Europe, Asia, and Africa.

                                                            •The LA Revolts, The Soviet Revolts, The Bolivian Revolts of South & Central America, The Mandarin Revolts of China & East Asia. The 19th & 20th century saw an explosion again of Anarchs and the Brujah were leaders and soldiers in each of these movements. Throughout the world, Brujah Councils and Anarch Free States began to form to contest the tyranny of the ancients...

                                                            BRUJAH OF NOTE
                                                            •Robin Leland
                                                            --Robin Leeland was not the origins of Robin Hood. Well, he wasn't the only origin at any rate, although his peasant revolts after the crusades did genuinely help shape the legend. Regardless of the elder's cheeky adoption of the Robin Hood moniker, Leeland is a prominent proto-Anarch of Europe. A student of revolution and revolt, he remains a Brujah folk hero and a renowned Anarch, although he's more likely to consider himself simply a Brujah rather then either Camarilla or Anarch although both have claimed him.

                                                            •Marguerite Foccart
                                                            --Did she ignite the French Revolution? No. Is she the famous woman of liberty bearing her chest in a certain portrait? Maybe. Marguerite is a childe of Leeland and is similarly famous for her roles in most famously the French Revolution as well as numerous American revolts and revolutions. Like her sire she is her own woman and does as she pleases but when not at the theater or street performances, she is considered a major guiding inspiration of North American Anarchs, particularly given her Scottish counterparts... laze faire attitude.

                                                            •Crispus Attucks
                                                            --The speculation that this black Brujah chose his name as an homage to the famous freedom fighter rather then actually being said figure rages to this night. Interrogate him at your own risk. Regardless, Crispus made his name leading revolts of slaves and guiding them to freedom from the antebellum and civil war days up to the civil rights movement and the modern night. While for a time he acted as his sire (Foccart)'s agent at MacNeil's side, he finally grew disgusted with the man's refusal to act. Tonight he is a legendary Anarch who roams cities of the Midwest, mobilizing black communities into marches and protests against police brutality while also beginning to breath life back into certain Panther style tactics that had been forgotten. Crispus is still not over what happened to Malcolm X or Martin Luther King Jr. Bring those up at your own peril.

                                                            •Jeremy MacNeil
                                                            --The Scotsman could have been the champion of the modern Anarch Movement. After the LA Revolts he was all but the uncrowned prince, the prodigal son who Anarchs flocked to from coast to coast to follow. The Anarchs dreamed off the whole Western United States as a great free state and Brujah dreamed of New Carthage once more. Yet... this was not to be. MacNeil presides over the Free States and watched passively as the Movement slowed, stifled, and collapsed into bitterness, melancholy, and infighting. The Free States are a neo-feudal gangland nightmare which makes the Movement appear like a parody of himself. MacNeil refused the crown, because he had no desire to be prince and see the revolution become corrupt. But in his hesitance, perhaps what happened was worse. The Revolution rotted and the LA Free State is its carcass. Once the golden boy of a global revolution, MacNeil's name is an insult tinged with better melancholy and regret. Some Brujah scream, others sob, others sigh. But all react to the name.

                                                            •Smiling Jack
                                                            --The Brujah idol and the Anarch Rock Star. A pirate who sailed during the first whiffs of revival in the Movement, Jack has been here from the beginning. He kicks cities into gear, he bitches out barons who are a half-step from princedom, and he mocks whomever he pleases to enrage and reinvigorate the Movement around him. Some wonder if he's not secretly a political mastermind. Others have met him. The truth ultimately doesn't matter so much as this: people believe in Jack. He's a walking, talking symbol of freedom. He's what Brujah love.
