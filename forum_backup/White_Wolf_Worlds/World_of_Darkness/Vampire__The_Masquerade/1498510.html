    Should vampires growl?

    12-01-2022, 04:11 AM
    It's kind of a thing with vampires in fiction, the hissing or the buffverse vamps and their growling/snarling. Some people seem to just by default think it's a innate feature of vampires and like it as a way to highlight their inhumanity and predatory nature, which is fine and I also kind of like that aspect of it, though I don't use it. I was wondering if others had vampires growl in their own games?

    Personally, I don't actually picture the Kindred doing that. Except for maybe the Gangrel, who may develop growling or hissing or whatever as a tic/mutation due to their curse. Setites may also perform a snake-like hiss, but that would be a deliberate action on their part. Vampires, in my mind, are stuck with human vocalizations when they make noise. We also hiss and can make a sort of guttural "ugh" noise when angry, so they do hiss/growl, but not in any inhuman way. In certain circumstances they may also produce a sort of "angry death rattle." But the beast itself doesn't bother with vocalizing. When I picture a wight I imagine something that's unmistakably a cannibalistic walking corpse. Wights and vampires in frenzy don't use their atrophied lungs, they don't make noise. The concept of being stalked through the night by some utterly silent, still, ravenous dead thing is something I find more unsettling than a snarling animal in human skin.

    Prone to hyperbole
