    Nightly Activities (Humongous Random Table Time!)

    10-09-2020, 10:47 PM
    I sorely miss random tables in Vampires. As I found myself getting a bit stale regarding what kind of milieu and situations the PCs would end up in when they sought out various NPCs (it would almost always be a random party of some kind), I realized it was time for a random table of such situations, to inspire myself to think out of the box and come up with more interesting ideas in the future. What are the NPCs actually spending their nights doing, and what are they doing when the PCs meet them? Well, here's the answer! I'm open to other suggestions, as I could definitely remove some results from the table in order to add better ones. Note that this table is designed for Mexico City, so there's a couple of cliché Mexican activities represented.
