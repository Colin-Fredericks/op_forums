    Originally posted by Illuminostro View Post
    I don't know where your opinion is coming from, but vampires are eternally locked in stasis, unchanged from the moment they were Embraced. They are the literal embodiment of "Conservatism," i.e., "Stasis."
    This is one of those concepts that the writers and the fans can never seem to make up their minds about.

    Because if vampires are as unchanging as you say, then they have no capacity to adjust, adapt, or learn new things. A vampire who was embraced during the roman empire will never learn a new language, never learn how to drive a car, never use weapons that aren't a sword and shield, they will never accept that slavery isn't an answer, they believe that anyone who isn't of Greek or Roman descent is automatically a barbarian, etc.

    This fundamentally means that XP as a system doesn't work because a vampire is frozen at the time of undeath, thus unable to uprade stats. They can't choose new paths of enlightenment because they always start out as path of humanity. Furthermore, let us not forget that the Gangrel clan weakness changes them over time, the nosferatu clan weakness changes them at the point of the embrace, the Assamite clan attribute means they get darker as they age, and the entire discipline of Vicissitude is based around the concept of making permanent changes to ones form. Never mind the fact that all the paths and rituals of blood magic that were created by vampires couldn't exist.

    Writing that Vampires are frozen in thinking and in their "time" is arguably more a projection of the writer hating on older and more experienced individuals, without actually having a reason to do so. Everything in the WoD is theoretically about being able to adapt and survive, but if one is frozen in stasis, then you by definition can't adapt or create new tactics.

    Other than a mention from Ann Rice, I don't think most other vampire media runs with the idea that vampires are unchanging psychologically as a function of vampiric existence, rather it is a side effect of long-term existence where you have seen everything to the point where you can guess what will happen next because the patterns are so boringly repetitive.

    As a side note, where do you get the absurd notion that Conservatism somehow equals stasis? Conservatism is more akin to "if it ain't broke, don't fix it". Traditional methods work until they don't and then they get updated, but the core values remain largely the same. While Progressivism is constantly chasing what could be possible, but to quote Ian Malcolm "your scientists were so focused on whether or not if they could, they never stopped to consider if they should". Thus revolutions and other progressions in society never know when to stop and often turn in on themselves once they run out of other enemies to fight.

                                        Regardless of the logic behind the original post in terms of what counts as progressive, and whether it would help vampires, here's my own take.

                                        Left wing political initiatives would be to the benefit of a typical vampire, in the sense that they're to the benefit of everyone. A cheap night train; a stranded neonate can get out of Sabbat territory despite their phone being dead, with the coins in their pocket. Easily available shelter, without means testing; a Ventrue ancilla who has been refused shelter by a clanmate (the scandal!) and whose ghoul has gone missing (kidnapped? killed?) can find somewhere to shelter from the sunrise, even if the humiliation of using a shelter for KINE haunts them for decades. Health initiatives to get rid of lead paint and asbestos; healthier herd, better blood.

                                        There are two complications, however:

                                        Vampires are not wholly logical creatures. The Camarilla is a Conservative organization, being a Kindred is seen as a Conservative calling, much like organized crime. Think Sopranos, Ozark. "This is not a liberal profession. It is deeply conservative" (in reference to executing a mentally ill man because he is a liability). This can be more a cultural thing than something rational. Look at the power vertical. Look at the traditionalism, the conservative (small c) sensibilities, the decorum. A typical Camarilla Kindred will be more drawn towards Conservative politics simply because it feels familiar, the aesthetic similarities distract them from the benefits left wing politics could have for Kindred.

                                        In addition to this, there is corruption. Yes, a cheap night train would be good for the neonate on the street. But you're the Ventrue Primogen, and you haven't needed PUBLIC TRANSPORT (ugh) in well over 2 centuries, if ever. In fact the idea that the government might pay to move people around with a subsidized service could be very unnatural to you. You're from a time where if anyone ever needed to go further than their town or village, they'd pay a premium and give up a few days of their life. A random teenager stranded away from their parents being able to get home, rather than being a Sabbat snack, doesn't seem like a natural thing to you, even if it would benefit you. And more to the point, even if you realize it would benefit the Camarilla as a whole... do you care? Do you want to pay tax? Do you want the pax you do pay to be higher? Do you want the Neonates and Ancillae to be having an easier time? Does the welfare of the Kine as a whole matter that much to you, when you're feeding on those who are generally doing okay? And your Ghoul has a lot of influence in the world of corrupt bastards. So do all your business partners. You're making a lot of money off things being the way they are.

                                        This is where Anarchs come in. Neonates and Fledgelings and even Ancilla are getting crumbs at many tables. Trying to claw back some of the power which is being taken by the Elders, being a punk or a rebel, these are anti-establishment and help to balance things out. While many princes will be supporting a candidate who supports the status quo (this doesn't necessarily mean right wing; there are plenty of purportedly progressive / left wing parties who often have leaders who support keeping things as they are, look at the US and UK for two pretty big examples), there will also be Kindred who are trying to scupper this out of principle or as part of the Jyhad.

                                        tl;dr Kindred act out of self-interest in many cases, and they won't always understand what is in their best interests, meaning they will at times act selfishly even to their own detriment, or that of their fellow Kindred

                                                            Originally posted by AnubisXy View Post
                                                            If a younger vampire insults an elder, and the elder responds by killing the younger vampire, the elder is likely to receive some sort of slap on the wrist as punishment. "Well, that youngster had it coming insulting someone who was born before Columbus ever left Italy. Let that serve as a lesson." Meanwhile if an elder vampire insults a younger one, and the younger one murders the elder, you can bet that that young vampire is going to face a blood hunt for doing that.
                                                            I can see this being a common perception, but it would be just as accurate to say that if an Elder got killed by a young upstart, clearly that Elder wasn't worth the blood they fed on. For every Elder that would mourn or try to avenge the death of their contemporary, there are several Elders who don't care one way or the other, and more than a few that breath a sigh of relief because the boons or old deals made are now no longer in play.

                                                            The idea of "fairness" in modern times has lost all connection to the reality of a given situation. If a stupid individual gets into a fight with a smart individual, the smart one is not going to lobotomize themselves so that they two can have an equal fight. A young vampire who picks a fight with an Elder without accounting for the power difference is outright stupid, but so is an Elder who believes themselves untouchable and thus goes about making enemies left and right out of every new vampire.

                                                            I believe the perception that Elders are these non-stop pricks is due not to their age & power, but instead because of bad character writing and storytelling. Elders have survived this long for a reason, yet so many STs have elders be glorified high school lunchroom bullies who are a year or two older and thus have more physical development and more social clout.

                                                            An Elder should either be such a heavy combat build that they don't need to be smart, or they should be acting like Xanatos, where every plan has multiple potential outcomes, but regardless of what happens, the elder benefits. Occasionally you will get an Elder who is both a heavy combat build and a Xanatos clone, but you will almost never find an Elder who does neither of those things well, they just wouldn't survive long enough to become Elders.
