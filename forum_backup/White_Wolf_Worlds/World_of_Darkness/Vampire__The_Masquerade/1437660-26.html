                I have a similar outlook on their Discipline set.

                Animalism allows for a natural progression of social control via subduing minions and lessers: first beasts, then humans, then The Beast.

                Auspex is the expression of "Knowledge is Power" in Discipline form. Tzimisce are often presented first and foremost as a scholarly clan with “a thirst for knowledge.” It's also possibly the most underrated Discipline in the books. It's subtle, but has amazingly useful applications in settings from social debates to hunting and tracking to monitoring one's expansive demesne without leaving one's basement sanctum. Far Sight, Telepathy, Heightened Senses, Spirit's Touch...solid gold to a clan of territorial, anti-social shut-ins.

                Vicissitude is a physical culmination of similar masteries and insights as above, but with an added horror aspect not available anywhere else in the game (outside of a couple static, uncontrollable clan weaknesses, maybe). This is the game's resource for Cronenberg, Charles Dexter Ward, The Thing, etc. Otherworldly, inhuman, terrifying to others yet liberating, enlightening and empowering to Fiends.
