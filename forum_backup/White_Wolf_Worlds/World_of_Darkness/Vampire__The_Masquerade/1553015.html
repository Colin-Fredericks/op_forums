    Alternative Serpentis 5 or 4 For V20 OR DAV20

    08-08-2025, 06:12 PM
    So I saw mother of monsters and well, I don't hate it, but I feel the snake connotation is not really there. I mean it is but the fictional Egyptian snake magic aspect is lacking, Serpentis to me seems to be based on Snake sorcery or more specifically The Conan/Prince of Egypt snake sorcery, where instead of being seen as a symbol of healing and rebirth snakes are seen in the more Abrahamic way as a source of corruption, destruction, change, and avarice.

    Since the other four powers involve taking on snakelike aspects, and all have some combat potential Typhonic avatar's hybrid form also serves as a snake theme, but not as closely as v20's form of the cobra I thought the fifth dot would expand on snakey stuff, mainly transmutation and snake summoning.

    So, from searching old sources namely the combo disciplines, of Rod of the Rattler, and Hatch the viper I have created an experimental fifth dot power.

    Clutch of the cobra.

    While the Followers of the Dark God are attuned to the serpent, clutch of the cobra ensures that the Setite is never without the aid of a loyal serpent by shaping one from their heart's blood. This serpent has a personality similar to their creator and a pattern based on their own color preferences, the setite may speak with this serpent and understand it through tongue of the asp or typhonic maw. The player is limited to a single serpent, and the serpent requires one vitae per month like a ghoul. On death the player may create another identical serpent after a night has passed.

    System: The Vampire spends 2 vitae. The serpent can be up to 3 meters in length and is typically some species of African snake most commonly Naja Ashei, or Naja Haje. The serpent is a ghouled animal and has half of its master's base stats (round down) It possesses five health levels. As a ghoul it possesses a single dot of potence and fortitude. It begins existence with 1 vitae included in the cost but may hold up to ten like a typical ghoul. The snake emerges from the setite's body either through the mouth or tearing free from the forearm or palm. It can stay partially merged in this way, return to beneath the vampire's skin, or be fully disgorged.

    Bite: The serpent can bite on a successful brawl attack roll which deals strength + 1 Lethal. Soak as normal.

    Envenom: On a successful bite (damage after soak) the snake can afflict its venom on a victim this can be done 3 times per scene this deals 1 lethal damage per turn for 4 turns. Mortals take this as damage while vampires lose the equivalent in vitae over four turns.

    Spit: The serpent can spit venom 3 times per scene (shares the same venom pool as envenom listed above) which deals 1 lethal damage per turn for 4 turns on a successful ranged attack. This attack does not damage on its own only the necrotizing venom deals damage, vampires are unaffected unless it hits open wounds or an open orifice in which it functions as envenom.

    All of these above actions can occur while the serpent is still partially merged with the vampire and do not require the vampire splitting actions but instead they use the serpent's dice pool. While merged any damage targeting the serpent can instead be taken by the vampire, if desired.

    Shifting Serpent: The snake on command may turn into a Khopesh,(Same stats as a saber) or spear. While in weapon form the serpent shares identical stats as the weapon of the user’s choice. Whenever the weapon strikes someone, the wielder may spend 1 Blood Point reflexively to poison the target with the same rules as envenom but limited only by their vitae.
    For Dark ages I am tempted to make this the fourth dot power and make Typhonic Avatar the fifth dot, as I feel it is weaker than typhonic Avatar.

    I think it is comparatively weaker than mother of monsters but has less downsides, the stat block should see it being weaker than the example creature created from that until the player is perhaps around sixth or fifth generation with high attribute investment.

    Is it overpowered? Underpowered? I would like some feedback, thank you in advance.

      On a first read, it looks a bit underpowered to me.

      Personally, I really like DAV20 Serpentis 4 and Mother of Monsters, but the theme is not about snakes alone anymore, but mythological monsters. The downside of losing a health level if your monsters are captured is worrying, but the fact that the monsters have your same dots in the physical disciplines can be a major advantage, especially for the Warriors of Glycon who have Potence in-clan.

      Anyway, I think you should boost the cobra's stats or its damage somehow to make it a power that players long to have, instead of one that takes a four-dots discipline slot but doesn't pack enough of a punch. Having half the master's stats means that, with the physical attributes all at 5 (an unlikely assumption for Priest Setites, and advanced even for the Warriors of Glycon), this ghoul has a far from impressive 2-2-2 stat block. If I have Brawl 5, it will have 2. It will basically bite with 4 dices to hit and 3 to do damage, which I wouldn't even consider as support in a serious fight on a 4-dots power, all for 2 Vitae, which vampires higher than 9th gen can't even pull off in a single turn.

              If I can give some suggestions:
              1) don't base the stats on the vampire's stats: Serpentis is used either by Followers of Set (the Priests), who are not interested in investing in physical traits and rely on Serpentis to survive fights in a pinch and surprise opponents, or by the Warriors of Glycon who can be beasts in combat, especially with VDA20 Serpentis. You get two extremes, and therefore the cobra would be either pointlessly weak or too strong. Give it its own stats, so you can control its game impact more easily.
              2) a cool visual is to have multiple snakes come out of the body, Medusa style, even if they don't become detached. I'd consider that over the idea of a single snake. You don't have to stat each snake: you could represent them by specific bonuses to the vampire's attacks, resistance to grappling, intimidation or even charm (like the multiple heads having some variation of Serpentis 1).
              3) because Serpentis is used by a booodline that's not usually trained in fighting and one that is, think very well at how you want the power to contribute to their different needs and capabilities.
              4) Serpentis is very expensive in terms of Vitae as it is, so try to imagine how the activation of the power would work out in actual combat for vampires of different generations.
              5) how about the cobra has also Serpentis 1 and can charm people on behalf of the vampire and do some other cool thing that makes it more useful in social or mystical scenes over combat scenes?

                  Clutch of The Cobra

                  While the Followers of the Dark God are attuned to the serpent, clutch of the cobra ensures that the Setite is never without the aid of loyal serpents by shaping one from their heart's blood. This serpent has a personality similar to their creator and a pattern based on their own color preferences, the Setite may speak with this serpent and understand it through tongue of the asp. A vampire is limited to a number of serpents representing their inherent vampiric potential, one serpent may be maintained per point of vitae the Setite can spend in a round and the serpent requires one vitae per month like a ghoul. On death the player may create other identical serpents after a night has passed.

                  System: The Vampire spends 3 vitae per serpent. The serpent can be up to 3 meters in length and is typically some species of African cobra most commonly Naja Ashei, or Naja Haje. The serpents emerge from the Setite's body either through the mouth or tearing free from the forearm or palm. They can stay partially merged in this way, return to beneath the vampire's skin to lie in wait, or be fully disgorged. A serpent can return to the vampire's body after being disgorged

                  Combat abilities.

                  Bite: The serpent can bite on a successful brawl attack which deals strength + 1 Lethal. Soak as normal.

                  Envenom: On a successful bite (damage after soak) the snake can afflict its necrotizing venom on a victim this can be done 3 times per scene this deals 1 lethal damage per turn for 4 turns to mortals and most supernatural's. Vampires lose the equivalent in vitae over four turns as it renders their vitae inert.

                  Spit: The serpent can spit venom 3 times per scene (shares the same venom pool and damage as envenom listed above) on a successful throwing weapons roll. This attack deals no damage on its own only the necrotizing venom deals damage, as such it must target an orifice/eye/wound to have effect. If this venom lands in an eye they must succeed on a difficulty 8 stamina roll or be blinded for 4 turns, a botch permanently blinds them if they lack supernatural healing methods. If blinded a vampire can heal for a single vitae.

                  Enchanting Gaze: The cobra even at its weakest can use the powers of enchanting gaze to freeze an additional target in place, to aid its master.

                  Potence: The cobra enjoys all the benefits of potence.
