                                    While the most known story about Lambach is the Dracula debacle, he shows up a number of times in circumstances that are contradictory between them.

                                    His real nature and identity is purposefully left uncertain, so he may be anything between 4th and 6th Generation, with varying degrees of courage and overall competency, and he can also be one of the pieces of the Eldest.

                                    The more you play Lambach as a survivalist without much courage or ambition, the more likely that he's "high" Gen and an unwilling instrument of the Eldest. The more you want Lambach to be ambitious and/or powerful, the more he's a low Gen playing from behind this facade.

                                    Personally I like to change my take on him from one game to another, and while I'm not sure I'd call him an antagonist, I do like the character a lot.

                                                            Tabak is officially a 6th generation Vampire, childe of Tabak Ruthven and sire of Danika Ruthven (Lair of the hidden, Timizce Novel Dark Ages)


                                                            There is a large Tzimisce family, the Ruthvens, who descend from a mysterious Lord Ruthven. The Ruthvens have always been the guardians of the Antediluvian. Damek Ruthven was the Voivode among Voivodes, and in his castle, a part of the Tzimisce Antediluvian was kept.

                                                            In Beckett's Jyhad Diary, Lambach is located in the Cayman Islands. But when Beckett meets Dracula, Dracula tells him that his sire has been imprisoned for a very long time in the castle of Poenari. If Dracula's sire is locked away in Poenari, it cannot be Lambach. But if it were Lambach, who is the vampire in Poenari Castle?

                                                            I gave my answer a while ago. Many ancient Tzimisce tend to transcend their individual identities. Yorak was one with the Cathedral of Flesh, Byelobog had become the Pripet marshes, Demendeh transformed into a Virus, the Dragon was fused into Tzimisce and implanted in Vykos, Kartarirya sought to remold itself into a creature capable of existing in multiple states of awareness simultaneously.

                                                            The Eldest itself transcended its individuality, being able to reincarnate in any Tzimisce.

                                                            Beckett's Jyhad Diary also showed us that not only can the Antediluvian incarnate in any Tzimisce, but it can also absorb and rebirth entire vampires. Although Ilyas Cel Frumos had died, he was reborn in the flesh of Tzimisce and now lives under New York, just as Tzimisce once reincarnated in the Dragon after the battle with Samiel. Lambach himself, in the diary, sometimes calls himself the child, grandchild, and great-grandchild of Tzimisce.

                                                            I believe that Lord Ruthven simply did what all the others did: he transcended his individuality, able to manifest in any of his direct descendants, in a lesser version of the Antediluvian’s most characteristic power. Sometimes Lambach is a fifth-generation Methuselah, and sometimes a fourth-generation Methuselah, depending on whether Lord Ruthven manifests in him.
