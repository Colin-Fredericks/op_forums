            Originally posted by Theodrim View Post

            This line of argument needs to end. No, it doesn't erase memory in and of itself, all it does and all it needs to do is interfere with the snack's memory.

            The Kiss forces a state of altered consciousness likened to orgasmic bliss or the euphoric haze of a good buzz or drug trip. This is RAW, as already described. This state of altered consciousness interferes with the mortal's memory, and this is also RAW as already described. Meanwhile...

            1. Humans have a remarkable ability for rationalization and distorting their own memories, both in the long term and short term. If you've ever been drunk -- not even blackout drunk, just something more than a good buzz -- you should know exactly what I mean, especially if you've talked to those around you about what happened especially if they were also drunk. And, this is not a case of memory distortion and problematic recall in the case of altered consciousness; it happens when completely sober. If a mortal is fed from under careful circumstances, as RAW states and already discussed they're more likely than not to rationalize it in a way that makes sense to them and fits the social context of when they were fed from.

            RAW is and should be all you need, here. It's all in the manual. But, to put a finer point on it considering this won't die...

            2. Vampires tend to feed when and where they can control the circumstances and cover their asses. That means Racks. Racks trend overwhelmingly towards being places where psychoactives, even those simple and ubiquitous as alcohol, flow freely in a socially-chaotic atmosphere. That means they attract mortals, lower their inhibitions and make them more pliant, which means feeding is overall easier and harder for third parties to track. It doesn't matter if the Rack in question is a skid row or a trendy night club, the circumstances tend to stay the same. The important part to all this being, mortals in Racks tend to already have altered states of mind, divided attention, and possibly mild sensory overload, before the Kiss is applied which amplifies its effects while also providing a social context for the mortal to rationalize what happened to them after the fact.

            And, lastly...

            3. For reasons that should be obvious, being fed from causes, bare minimum, mild (acute) anemia. Most official sources I've read liken a single-BP feeding to a blood donation, but even that's enough to trigger (non-threatening) disorientation, lightheadedness, and nausea. Now, for that, see above; the symptoms of a feeding mimic hangover and/or mild withdrawal, and if the mortal had been at a Rack it's more likely for them to rationalize it as a wild night out than "vampires did it". More than that...

            Humans can lose approximately 15-20% of blood volume before suffering hypovolemic shock. Blood donation generally takes 8-10% of blood volume. This compares almost perfectly to that, by RAW, humans have 10 BP and can have 2 points taken before requiring emergency care.


            ...and we are definitely talking more prominent and serious symptoms of anemia. Symptoms of anemia include confusion, shortened attention span, disorientation, light headedness, and depending upon type and severity, memory disruption and loss. Which makes absolute sense, because we're talking about a medical condition whereby the flow of oxygen to the brain is compromised.

            So, just to wrap this up...

            Most feedings occur in Racks or other places where mortals are likely to already be in altered states of mind, which interfere with memory. The Kiss alters consciousness in and of itself, which interferes with memory. And, the very act of feeding induces a medical condition which alters consciousness and perception, which can depending on severity interfere with memory. And still, even after all that, mortals' recollection of the event is going to change to recontextualize and rationalize it. It's going to take an astoundingly strong-willed mortal (WP 4+, easy) to overcome all that and remember enough to think something might have been weird about it.

            And everything I just mentioned hinges entirely on the notion the vampire did nothing to the mortal other than give them the Kiss. No discipline use, no additional "party favors" or whatnot, no kinky shit, not even booping the mortal with a one-point blood bond. Although personally, I always house rule licking the wound from a Kiss induces the blood bond.

            I absolutely, 100% agree with this post and will shamelessly quote myself from an earlier discussion on the topic:


            Originally posted by Eddie Fiori View Post
            I've always house ruled that the Kiss produces a low level type of amnesia.

            1. It's the only way I can even remotely begin to justify the continued existence of the Masquerade, and
            2. Less time spent on stocking up on chloroform, breaking into butcher shops/blood banks, or getting rid of bodies leaves us with more time for role playing the encounter itself. I want feeding to be about role play, not jumping through elaborate hoops.

            The way we play, the Kiss clouds the victim's memories in a rough period of three to five minutes before and after the Kiss itself. The victim will then usually rationalize what happened based on the setting (except, of course, for when full or partial recollection would be dramatically correct).

            A sensual Kiss in a club, late at night? "Damn, where did the cute guy go?, Shit, my head is spinning, I've must have had too much to drink. Did someone spike my drink?"
            An unexpected and sudden Kiss in a dirty alley? "Uuuh, what happened? Was I mugged?"

            Et cetera.

            Of course, no Kiss will save a Kindred who blatantly reveals his true nature before the act or otherwise neglects basic tradecraft. Such an act is bound to be remembered.

                                Originally posted by Leandro16 View Post
                                Mechanicaly the masquerade is unsustainable in all editions becuase of Two Factors
                                1. The Overpopulation of vampires (Seriusly they are almost in every city , some of them has to fuck up)
                                2. The vampires have no realiable way of camuflating feeding from his victims (Orgasmic Bite is great to portray the sensuality of the vampire and stop the victim resisting but doesnt erase the memories of the moment).
                                The Masquerade was never sustainable.

                                Let's assume that every vampire ever practices all of the caution and secrecy of a master spy in their nightly unlives and that shovelheads and abandoned fledglings aren't a thing. There have been academic studies into whether the earth's human population could feasibly sustain a coexisting population of vampires. Most of these studies assume that vampires are butchers on the scale of Salem's Lot or John Carpenter's Vampires and would drive humanity extinct within a relatively short time frame.

                                There was one particular study, though, which examined three separate models of vampiric predation upon humankind: Salem's Lot-level butchery, Anne Rice-level restraint (where vampires don't have to kill when they feed, but sometimes do), and True Blood-level restraint (where vampires have a viable alternative to human blood, but sometimes Embrace humans). The conclusion was that Anne Rice's vampires would still depopulate the planet within 60 or so years, unless the population of vampires remained inordinately small.

                                The (literal) statistical variables in the World of Darkness are considerably different than in Anne Rice's world. But I feel safe in saying the WoD is an even less sustainable environment for humans and vampires to coexist in than Anne Rice's setting is.

                                Vampires' nightly blood demands are essentially the WoD's faster than light space travel. This is just one area of the setting where we have to suspend our disbelief.

                                Whether the setting would feel more believable with fewer vampires and better built-in camouflage methods is a separate matter, though. But given what the numbers bear out, this is just one area where we're still squinting our eyes and saying "fooh-fooh" to the science.

                                    The Rice Model assumes a 10% of death per feeding, and 10% of deaths being embraced, but they can feed once a week.
                                    At the very worst Kindred in WoD are killing 10% of feeding. That's someone only draining a mortal when down 10 blood because they like to kill. And you probably won't have that psychopath embracing every 10th one as well. You only do anything like that in War.

                                    Real world maths -
                                    A blood donation (approx 1 bloodpoint) is recovered in full for blood volume in about 28 days. The red blood cells only mature after 3 months, which is why there's a delay on allowing you to donate blood more often.
                                    So a Vampire can exist on a herd of 28 vessels if all stay healthy.

                                    In Game Maths/Mechanics
                                    The 1 level of damage dealt by draining a blood point is healed by (most) mortals after one day of rest.
                                    As such, if just feeding for daily waking, you can have a herd of 1 and survive while they stay healthy.
                                    (Look at the Impundulu Bloodline weakness, it relies on this mechanic)


                                    *Using a Study is only as good as the assumptions used to create the study
