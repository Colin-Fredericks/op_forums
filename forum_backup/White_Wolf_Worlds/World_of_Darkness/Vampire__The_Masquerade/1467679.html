    V5 Kindred of the East: Clan as Dharmas

    11-13-2021, 10:47 AM
    So, we know KotE have been officially axed from V5, and retconned into local Vampires.

    I was thinking how to retcon them in the wider continuity and, as Dharmas were the defining characteristic of KotE, I was thinking of adapting them into Clans.

    I started reasoning, and tried to follow the trend of Kindred of the Ebony Kingdom: not direct portings, but reinterpretations of the traditional concepts.

    These are my ideas:

    THE BIG 5

    DEVIL TIGERS: Brujah; they are splintered in a lot of different groups and are the gadflys of KotE society, and are determined to tear down the world and start anew. Their dealings with evil spirits, sacrifices and violence could be a nice tie to Carthage and the Baali.
    I was also thinking Tzimisce (the sadomasochistic and devilish angle is appropriate) or Lasombra (their ethic is very much the Path of Night), but Tzimisce would be too close to the original concept, whereas the Lasombra of the East were describes as a very different lot (see later).

    Their sorcery and judgemental attitude would also make for fine Assamites, but they're too ingrained in nearby Middle East and India, rival cultures to the Middle Kingdom.

    RESPLENDENT CRANES: Tzimisce. Despotic and autoritharian leaders correcting the sins of their subjects? If we discard Ventrue, which are the dominant force in the Camarilla and strong in India too, I think Tzimisce are the perfect fit.


    TRASHING DRAGONS: Gangrel is my choice. Hedonism and livin' la vida loca are great twists on the traditional Gangrel concept, which is, expressed instead in their emphasis on animism and shapeshifting, setting them apart from the easy choice of Toreadors.

    BONE FLOWERS: Lasombra. Confucian scholars doing Ancient worshipping is exactly the concept of Eastern Lasombra presented in Clanbook Lasombra Revised.
    Other possible choices would be Hecata and Tzimisce.

    THOUSAND WISPERS: Malkiavians. Their simbolism is rooted in riddles, enigmas, meditation and difficult balance, and the idea of serial impersonators seems a nice touch on Malkavians. Nosferatu and Hecata could be appropriate too.

    HERETICAL DHARMAS

    SPIRIT OF THE LIVING EARTH: Hecata. Animistic Shinto spirit traffickers with no qualms about dealing with the Infernal and uninterested into the realm of Flesh seems an appropriate adaptation of the Hecata concept, even though Abyss and the Shadowlands are a different concept. Might need customized Ceremonies or perhaps a Devotion with Animalism.

    FLAME OF THE RISING PHOENIX: Toreador. i have always been fond of the "Toreador familyman trying hard to keep his family going" of the Revised Clanbook, and they seem a very good match with the integralistic Humanism of the Phoenix.

    FACE OF THE GODS: Ventrue. It's an interesting twist, I think, that the Clan most associated with the Masquerade and strict hierarchies could become a group of self-centered would-be Gods. After all, far from the eyes of God-impersonating Methuselhas like Mithras and Tiamat, you can easily play the game and don't fear their wrath.

    SEEKERS OF THE INWARD WAY: Salubri. Tao saints striving to achieve focus and balance, and pre-dating Confucianism, are a good match for the original Golconda blueprint.

    SCORPION EATER: Nosferatu. Despised by all and survivalist to the extreme, they make a good equivalent for the Nosferatu hidden in their refugees and waiting for the worst to pass.

    THE WAY OF TEN THOUSAND SCREAMS: Ravnos. Long time enemies of the Middle Kingdom and descendants of Ravana, bent on destroying reality.

    What do you think?

              Wan Kuei Suggestions

              * Obviously rename them but keep the basic Clan structure.

              * The Wan Kuei as "we" knew them were often spreading disinformation when they encountered Western Kindred and subject to weird racist stories.

              * The Wan Kuei are organized on a Blood Cult level and do not have an international organization but individual courts that are still vast and influential.

              * The Yama Kings are either Antediluvians and Demon Kings or both but no one actually "fights" them except in the same way the Sabbat fights them by claiming X or Y is their pawn.

              * Spirit magic exists among the Wan Kuei but in the same way Koldunic sorcery does. Its a lot rarer.

              * The Salubri are a hated and loathed bloodline for reasons unrelated to the Tremere but reflect the horrid reputation Saulot has.

              * The Cappadocians are still around and were never hunted by the Giovanni in this region.

              * Most of them are on the Bone Flower cult.

              * I would make the Cappadocians the Jiangshi and substitute Potence for Fortitude. Why? Because they have a history of tearing people apart and jumping hella high.

              * The Tzimsice are the rivals to the Ventrue in the region rather than a minor Clan.

              * The Tremere are almost nonexistent here.

              * The role of vampire mages is handled largely by the Tzimisce and Banu Haqim.

              * The Banu Haqim are largely unchanged, Abrahamic, and still Muslim Caine worshipers. They do not report to Alamut, though.

              * The Followers of Set in the region are nonexistent but a Clan of Faith certainly does. The mainstream Setites would not even recognize their fellows here.

              * Ming Xiao was a Tzimsice and we just fought her in her Horrid Form. She was a Devil Tiger and not a very good one.

              * The entire Great Leap Outward business was done for the same reason as a Sabbat Crusade: to send the young to die out acquiring territory and relieve pressures at home. The young
              eventually figured out they were cannon fodder and allied with the local Anarchs.

                  Honestly I'm not a fan of making the Wan Kuei Cainites and frankly I do believe the Hungry Dead could be restructured while keeping the core of what they are intact.

                  There's also the unfortunate implications that by making them also Cainites that makes the Abrahamic Faiths at least partly correct while making all other Faiths incorrect. Which is kind of a bad look. It'd be one thing if this was VtR where they completely muddied the origins of Vampires but we're not dealing with that. We're dealing with a line that ties it's origin to the Old Testament.

                  That said, going with this notion of making Wan Kuei Cainites, I outright hate the idea of making Dharmas into Clans because they don't really work that way. They're philosophical beliefs akin to the Paths of Enlightenment. Railroading them into Clans would be like saying that only Toreadors can take the Path of Humanity while Gangrels have to be on the Path of the Beast and Ventrue to the Path of Honorable Accord. Would you restrict every member of a Clan to a specific Path of Enlightenment? No. So why should we restrict the Dharmas to specific Clans?

                  To echo what others have said, Dharmas should be Cults with their Convictions. In V5 terms, everything about the Dharmas would be a Cult, not a Clan.

                  Homo sapiens. What an inventive, invincible species. It's only a few million years since they crawled up out of the mud and learned to walk. Puny, defenceless bipeds. They've survived flood, famine and plague. They've survived cosmic wars and holocausts. And now, here they are, out among the stars, waiting to begin a new life. Ready to outsit eternity. They're indomitable. Indomitable.

                      Here's a story a friend of mine shared and why I removed the KOTE from my games well before it became official.

                      The Kuei Jin and the "Second Class Citizen" problem

                      I think I started to dislike the origins of the Wan Kuei in canon when it was explained to me by a friend in Hong Kong how utterly offensive they were. Basically, because they are suggesting that Asian people aren't normal people in the World of Darkness.

                      Bear with me.

                      My friend stated that he and his friends in Hong Kong wanted to play Vampire: The Masquerade. They wanted to play the game that they had picked up that had the title V:TM because they wanted to play vampires. They were fans of the Lost Boys, Interview with a vampire, Dracula, and other vampire movies from the West. And my friend HATED Kindred of the East. I don't mean in a small way, I mean that it caused him to feel a kind of burning loathing of White Wolf that he struggled with because he really loved Vampire: The Masquerade and took it as a personal insult that KOTE said, effectively, "I am not welcome as a player."

                      Contrary to the belief of being more inclusive, he said the KOTE said that Asian people were not only DISTINCTLY DIFFERENT from Westerners in terms of having an afterlife, having different spiritual powers, and so on but they were an ENTIRELY DIFFERENT RACE in terms of their supernatural monsters. I suspect that being an atheist influenced him as well because he felt he was being forced into a badly written Westernized version of Chinese mythology in the very same way that people are terrified of forcing him into a very badly written version of Christian mythology. Except Christian mythology was weird and exotic and wasn't something he took nearly as seriously.

                      He said in simple terms, "I want to play a Tremere vampire in Hong Kong. I want to play in the Camarilla. I want to play in the same world as the rest of the World of Darkness. I do not want to be regulated to being their pet exotic monster which fights kung fu demons. I want to drink blood and do politics." He said that inherently the Kuei Jin/Wan Kuei makes Asian gamers second class citizens in the world because they will never be the focus of the game world or what the people who pick it up want to buy. Yet the game is framed that they are the "real" Kindred of the East.

                      I couldn't get his perspective but it did occur to me that the Kuei Jin are every bit as heavily tied to a specific religious system, far more so than Caine and Christianity, because it effects their night to night life. If you are not a believer in traditional Chinese mythology, the Kuei Jin absolutely state that is true and force Asian gamers to deal with that in yoru unlife.

                      A typical Kindred will never meet Caine, God, or angels.

                      A Kuei Jin has direct experience of Hell, reincarnation, and fights monsters for Heaven.

                      They're more like werewolves and Gaia.

                      A Japanese immigrant friend made a different comment that he always saw the Caine myth in V:TM equivalent to Evangelion. Which is to say, "I think Americans take it a lot more seriously than I do because its just weird exotic religious flavor versus someone's real religion. I mean, you don't actually believe Caine is a vampire right? Or even exists?"

                                                  I handled the issue of Abrahmic Christianity in my games a bit differently than other posters. Despite the fact I'm a Abrahmic believer and most of my players are atheist, I decided that I would make a lot of versions of the story of Caine and Lilith spread across different cultural bends in my game.

                                                  Here's what my Noddist PC had a debate with Beckett about.

                                                  1. Followers of Set think Caine is cultural appropriation: A fratricide where brother kills brother and the highest god curses the murderer. Are we talking about Caine and Able or Set and Osiris. The Followers of Set have a fairly decent argument that the Caine and Able story is Jewish Kindred altering their mythology for their own culture. Interestingly, I added some ambiguity that there was once a heretical sect of Folowers of Set that argued that "Set" is not actually Set.

                                                  Yes, there were Followers of Set who believed that Set the Antediluvian is actually Wepwawet or Typhon, a Apophis-worshiping Abomination (literally an Embraced Silent Strider) or Sobek the Embraced Mokole which would explain why the Followers of Set have confusion over 1st and 3rd generation.

                                                  2. Romulus and Remus: The Roman Ventrue believed that they were descended from the infamous Wolf Brothers of Rome. According to their legend, though, Romulus murdered his brother but was cursed by Jupiter, Helios, and other gods in retaliation. They received succor from Pluto and Nyx the Goddess of the Night who fills the role of Lilith in their culture.

                                                  3. Werewolf Legends: The Garou have a mythological origin for vampires that the First Ronin and the first Metis was also the First Abomination. The Garou murdered him for his disgusting nature but a Gurahl used his resurrection ritual to bring him back. However, so Wyrm tainted and disgusting was the first Metis that he emerged as a monster. This story is considered nakedly racist against Metis and not repeated much anymore but was used to justify the War of Rage. It was the Garou's way of blaming vampirism on other shifters.

                                                  4. Hecate: The Greek Goddess takes the role of Lilith and Caine both in this legend, raising one of her lovers from the dead and proceeding to teach him the secret of avoiding it as well as the arts of the gods. He promptly shares it with his brothers and sisters, creating the Antediluvians. The gods seeing the theft of ambrosia and the Antediluvains passing themselves off as the Olympians, curse them and banish Hecate from Olympus. In this story, there is no such thing as the 2nd generation.

                                                  5. The Path of Evil Revelations: The Path of Evil Revelations teaches that Cainites were created by mortal worshipers of Hell making deals with the Archdemons and that all of the Antediluvians offered their children's souls to keep themselves immortal, which they would harvest regularly. There's 13 founders of the Baali in this version and Nergal, Shaitan, and others are effectively apocryphal. The other vampires are just in denial of their true nature, perhaps because the Path of Evil Revelations says that not only are they damned but they just exist to be sacrifices at the next cyclical Gehenna.

                                                  6. Caine is a Risen: Caine is just a Western Risen that learned how to feed on Blood for Corpus and pass on his condition. He's nothing particularly special, just old as fuck.

                                                  7. Norse mythology: Odin became the first vampire when he died on a tree in exchange for greater wisdom. This was so that he could gain the secret to surviving Ragnarok but was cursed with the need for blood but could create great warriors with furious rage to battle the Lupines (Children of Fenris), the Giants (Fae), and other supernaturals. All of the gods are vampires and you should be proud not ashamed of what you are. Gangrel add further that the Christian God is actually Surtur and trying to weaken humanity for slaughter.

                                                  8. The Legend of the Rakshasa: Ravana and his Rakshasa are the Kindred and have the divinely appointed task of tormenting and tempting mortals into sin as well as inflicting pain as well as misery on them. Unfortunately, the other Kindred have fallen away from their task due to passing their gift onto the unworthy. The Antediluvians are all Ravana's sons and daughters but according to Ravnos teachings, anyone can become an Antediluvian by drinking enough blood and living long enough.

                                                  The Ravnos notably believe the Antediluvian killed in India was not the first vampire but that Ravana was killed by Rama in ancient times. Which is why wooden stakes and the Sun hurt them.
