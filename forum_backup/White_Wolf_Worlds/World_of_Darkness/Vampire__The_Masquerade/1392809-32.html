                            Originally posted by Pleiades View Post

                            makes sense, though a dhampir must feel like a huge downgrade from a Mage, unless you use hedge magic
                            Mage is way bigger than just Hermetic-style spell slinging. It’s about having the power inside you to change the world... and often through magic that comes off as pure coincidence.

                            That might be Hermetic spells, prayers of faith, weird or super-science, mystic martial arts or even just the precision art of persuasion (including a magic paradigm called “money makes the world go round/anything for the right price.”).

                            Let’s take a look at what even one dot in disciplines can actually do;
                            - Animalism (talk to and command animals)
                            - Auspex (heightened senses and a fluky danger sense)
                            - Celerity (“Everybody was kung fu fightin’. Those dudes were fast as lightnin”)
                            - Dominate (one word commands on mortals)
                            - Fortitude (iron body technique)
                            - Obfuscate (become one with shadows)
                            - Potence (so many many things, breaking boards and greater leaps are just the start).
                            - Presence (be a master of persuasion).

                            And that’s just the common ones a Dhampir could mostly figure out on their own (and many don’t even require blood - meaning you can hold onto your pool for important things like healing yourself... another useful thing magic in Mage is used for).

                            Throw in rarer stuff somehow and you can get weird (one dot in vicissitude and you can be a master of disguise, one in thaumaturgy and you can eventually learn all the level one rituals. Plus, if you’re Dhampir the odds are high that you’re spending more time with thin-bloods and who knows what the first dot of an incepted discipline will let you do.

                            It’s not earth-shaking, but it can be very flexible. Plus, due to the way the pricing works and lack of much to buy in the way of backgrounds; you’re in good stead to spend freebies on one or more discipline dots and some merits like clear-sighted, iron will unbondable*, virulent strike, true faith, medium, oracular ability, pale aura (good if your concept is to infiltrate undead society as say, a Brujah neonate with celerity, potence and presence 1).

                            Willpower 10 as an almost supernatural level of self-control (or luck... if I recall, the quickie Mage stats from Hunter just gave Mages huge pools of Willpower and let them spend multiple points on the same task to reflect their coinciental effects resulting in supernatural luck) is justifiable for some builds.

                            And if you want the Dhampir equivalent of “money makes the world go round” magic... how about you’re a trust-fund kid with Resources 5, Fame and/or Influence because your thin-blood father knocked up a rich socialite while using his vampiric presence to live the high life... you inherited his magnetic personality, a good family name and a sizable pile of resources when your mom died of complications in childbirth and you were raised by your wealthy grandparents.

                            Likewise, keeping up with the Jones isn’t all that hard... while the vamps in the group are buying up more dots in disciplines, you’re topping off your attributes and abilities for way less than in-clan discipline costs.

                            The point being... there’s quite a bit of subtle magic you can squeeze out a well-played Dhampir; a lot of which are fairly in keeping with those Balkans legends of half-vampire children who are mostly humans with a bit a magic to them.

                            * I’ve gotten more than one Storyteller to let me have unbondable for the lower Mage cost (3 points) since the primary benefit of unbondable for a mortal is you can become a ghoul without being blood bound... but Dhampirs in V20 explicitly cannot become ghouls or use more potent blood to learn disciplines above one dot, so it’s only real use is not being enslaved if someone forces a blood bond on you... a much more limited circumstance.

                                        So far, the best one dot Thaumatury discipline I've found is Movement of the Mind. A pound isn't much, but it IS a set of keys off a desk, a spilled drink or a door rattling like someone's trying to open it at the right time or the petty, but effective, wardrobe malfunction. Add in the first rank of rituals and you've got some reasonable flexibility... provided you could ever get someone to teach you actual blood magic (you'd pretty much need to build your backstory around it; maybe something related to a "sleeping with the enemy" of sorts involving the Arcanum and an thin-blooded Anarch who learned a bit of Old Skool Sorcery and passed it on their kid before getting ganked by someone, but leaving you with your Arcanum parent and their resources to help you puzzle out some reverse engineered versions of the level one rituals).

                                        Likewise, the Medium merit and maybe one dot in whichever of the Necromancy paths lets you actually see what Ghosts are around may not seem like much, but if you're willing to do some role-playing in order to do favors for the restless dead, you could get almost all the benefits of the Sepulchre path in terms of getting ghosts to do things for you and they won't even hate you for it because you've been helping them out too.

                                        But yes, between all the various options like True Faith, the first discipline dots, some of the supernatural merits and maybe some Hedge Magic you can actually pull off something like a linear version of many of the Tradition paradigms and practices if you wished to. Akashics, Celestial Chorus, Euthanatos, Hermetics and Verbena being on the easier side.

                                        Dreamspeaker might also be possible with the right disciplines (animalism, an incepted version of Spiritus, maybe Medium + the first dot in the Path of Spirit Manipulation so you can both see and talk to spirits... then just make actual role-played out deals with them as with the Necromancy version above), but you'd need a very specific backstory to justify it. Alternately Animalism + Auspex + Obfuscate would be a pretty decent Urban Shaman type who knows all sorts of hidden knowledge about the city and what's happening in it.

                                        Hardest would be the hi-tech paradigms like Sons of Ether and Virtual Adepts, but some of the actual Technocrat paradigms like the NWO (auspex + dominate + presence) or Syndicate (all the social backgrounds at high levels) are actually pretty doable.
