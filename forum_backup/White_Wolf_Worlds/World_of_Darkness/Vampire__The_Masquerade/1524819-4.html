                                        The first four (though who thought having two more for later was a good idea is still beyond me) is an odd mix. What comes to mind:

                                        1) Animalism, Protean,and Oblivion are the only V5 Disciplines not covered by the current options. I can almost see a justification here as these aren't the easiest to translate into a video game; esp. if you're not investing a lot of development in areas of play where they tend to shine in TTRPG use. BL1's versions of Animalism and Protean were pretty big departures from the TTRPG versions so they'd have something to do. Oblivion is a lot of work for effects that could be hard to make unique in the kind of game-play we're being given impressions of so far. That said, if they were avoiding these three Disciplines (or at least for now) they're cutting out a lot of classic VtM stuff for players to use first hand.

                                        2) Speaking of, that only actually leaves three options for Clans left: Malkavian, Toreador, and Salubri. If the first point is remotely an issue for the game, I would predict Toreador and Salubri. Malkavian play would be less impactful when you already have a "voice in your head" character to deal with, and Salubri would let you bring the very different playstyle that the Nosferatu had in BL 1 of being visually Masquerade breaking, without having to worry about Animalism.
