                I like the idea of the Camarilla briefly getting their shit together to deal with the single threat as quickly and efficiently as possible. No way even an Antediluvian would stand a chance against the entire Sect if they closed ranks, thought things through and coordinated their tactics. ...And then going right back to their politicking and double-speak the moment s/he'd fled and gone into torpor.

                A big part of the North-American metaplot in Werewolf: The Forsaken is that an eldritch monster spirit went nuts in the region and a true alliance of tribes brought it down... resulting in a schism between the Uratha who want to mobilize into a more coordinated society to deal with all their problems the same way since it worked so well that one time, and the ones rejecting that mentality as making the packs too interdependent and going against werewolf nature by imitating human systems of centralized authority.

                I feel like the Camerilla could do with a similar event to point to, giving more Cammies reason to strive for earnest solidarity between the clans, and even the Sects, and create some of the same tension. Might sound incompatible with the denial of the existence of Antediluvians, but most kindred with any authority only actually seem to pay lip service to that policy anyway.
