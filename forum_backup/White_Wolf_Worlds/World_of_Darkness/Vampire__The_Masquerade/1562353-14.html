                That said:

                1. The SI itself is a fascist structure. A secret intelligence and military coalition to watch and execute people without oversight isn't a good and democratic institution just because their targets happen to be "evil monsters".

                2. It's also a senseless waste of money in the way it works. It dedicates inordinate amounts of resources to operations whose targets are ill defined and benefits not calculated.

                3. What actually motivates the investment in the SI by governments isn't common good, but removing challenges to authority. Vampires are a parallel power running independently and that is a problem for them.

                4. Effective ways of depowering this kind of clandestine institutions circle around finding out the administrative vacuums they occupy, which usually means identifying where regular state failed its actual functions, and correcting this absence. Not just putting lethal force in the streets. The SI is just lethal force in the streets. As far as "solutions" go, it's a bigger threat to the safety of the population than the monsters they're trying to fight.

                5. None of this is new. Since Hunters Hunted in the setting the groups that now comprise the SI and haven't been thoroughly modified by the authors at Paradox were clearly either corrupt or fanatics who would frequently become more of a threat to the Kine than the Kindred. PCs facing the Society of Leopold aren't monsters being hunted by virtuous heroes. They're just victims of the ire of self-righteous fascists that happen to include the undead in the list of social groups they hate indiscriminately.

                6. Also the SI is an international phenomenon. The US alone going bananas isn't the SI losing anything. Your country may be the richest in the world, it's still only one and the SI would be able to survive without much issue if the problem was only in the US. At least in this the V5 authors had the basic competence to know that most of the participants of the SI wouldn't be part of the US machine or trust it.
