                                Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
                                "The Coalition" sounds infuriatingly vague.

                                I get that criminal groups really like to be vague ("Cosa Nostra" really just means "our thing" and is a term transformed into a name by outsiders to the mafia) but "the Coalition" isn't a parallel for "our thing" it's obviously some kind of political body and doesn't have the same deniability as "our thing". It's too defined to be a code and too nonspecific to be a good fucking name. Like a placeholder that never got replaced.
                                If you've ever worked in a Public Service agency though, that's exactly the sort of nothing-name that you'll get. They want something that doesn't sound ridiculous to say (or that would undermine the seriousness of their work), but they also want something simple, generic, and with plenty of deniability.

                                E.g. the UK's G7 Policing op was called Op Trelawny, a complete nothing of a name.

                                A Coalition could be anything in a government context: military, political, social, an old boy's network, etc. It gives them a degree of deniability, whilst still carrying enough weight to carry authority.

                                Originally posted by Kammerer View Post
                                Deprogramming is an odd thing to pick in general. It's extremely out of zeitgeist, being rooted in pop-psychiatric beliefs and conspiracy lore of the 60s-70s, when people thought that "brainwashing" and mind control are actual things that are possible. But lack of effectiveness and scientific developments led to these ideas, and the deprogramming industry, becoming fringe. It still exists in some capacity, but it's a dying jackal snatching its last few victims. Like how TV Psychics are no longer a big thing.

                                You wouldn't put Illuminati as an SI faction in the book, and you wouldn't have SI flying around in black helicopters - you are instantly showing your age with this old-ass conspiracy lore.

                                The difference here is that the SI has objective evidence of actual brainwashing taking place. Not in the sense of psychological manipulation either, but in an almost cartoon supervillain way of simply catching a victim's eyes and muttering a few words.

                                I think in light of that developing a way to counteract it would be a fairly logical step, not only to treat civilians affected but also their own operatives as part of debriefs. Fall of London calls out explicitly that they find ways of detecting Presence and Dominate, even screening for Blood-Bonds via blood-samples.
