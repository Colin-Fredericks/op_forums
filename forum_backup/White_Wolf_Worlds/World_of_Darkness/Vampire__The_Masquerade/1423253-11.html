            If the backstory of the game seeks to make the number 13 significant, then adding new Clans without any explanation just flies in the face of the game’s own lore. The terms Antediluvian and Methelusah are lifted from the Hebrew Bible especially, where 13 is seen as significant in all sorts of ways. Various supernatural associations with the number 13 possibly stemmed from there, but it’s actually had significance to a whole bunch of cultures, possibly because of its mathematical and astrological qualities. When the Vampire game picked up on the number 13 for the number of Clans, it was a Biblical reference, and it adds to the atmosphere of the setting. The answers here - just handwaving it away when it’s been canon in the game since 1991 - just seem really cavalier to me.

            Whether or not it is a serious issue could be seen as an individual thing, but as a marker of a possible new direction of the game under a new development team, actually, I think I object to it.
