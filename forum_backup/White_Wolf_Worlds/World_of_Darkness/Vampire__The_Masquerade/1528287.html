    Blood Stained Love is now available on PDF for preorders

    01-09-2024, 07:12 PM
    Hey folks,

    I am pleased to say that BLOOD STAINED LOVE is now available from Renegade Studios. I am actually excited about this one because I admit, while I didn't like the treatment of the Sabbat or Second Inquisition, I feel like more oft-kilter roleplaying-based works is where 5th Edition is meant to shine.



    Can the Kindred ever truly love?

    Expand your game of personal and political horror to include wide-eyed lovers and manipulative monsters. The book is for Storytellers and players alike, giving you tools, techniques and advice on how to bring romance to the undead. This book includes 6 six fleshed out storytelling characters and six detailed story concepts, all designed to maximize romance potential.

    Features
    • How to play your character in stories of romance.
    • How to build your chronicle from the ground up so that the options for romance are maximized or minimized.
    • Six fully fleshed out Storyteller characters specifically designed for romance.
    • Six detailed story concepts created to maximize romantic potential.
    • Advice and tools for safety and calibration, expanding on the corebook’s Advice for Considerate Play appendix.
    • Tools for defining and maintaining everyone’s boundaries at the table.
    • Sections on alternate methods for playing romance.
    • Merits, Flaws and Discipline powers related to romance.
    ​

    Author of Cthulhu Armageddon, I was a Teenage Weredeer, Straight Outta Fangton, Lucifer's Star, and the Supervillainy Saga.

          They really should've taken the time to remove the text that caused the controversy and worked in this new free document to replace it. The free document is an improvement but it serves to highlight why the original previewed pages were an issue. They mention right there on the first page in this free add-on-document where they quote Emily Care Boss who coined the term: "The overlap between player and character emotions. May be positive, negative or otherwise." And the issue will immediately become apparent when it's contrasted with this quote from the book-preview (which is a good summation of the tone of that section): "Bleed is one of the big reasons why romantic play is fun." Bleed can be positive. It can also be negative. So to give it this blanket "It's fun!"-description is an issue. Because the statement "Bleed is one of the big reasons why romantic play can turn out to be very un-fun." is equally valid. If anything, the free document is too short because now the next step after explaining Bleed in this nuanced fashion is to get to "Bleed-hunting" and explain that in a more nuanced fashion that isn't just "It's fun!". There are methods and practices to pursuing Bleed aka "Bleed-hunting". And having read those texts mentioned at the end of the document, I found those texts still more illuminating than what's offered by this new free document.

          In regards to the links at the end of the free document... There's a reason I had already read those blog-posts because when the controversy happened the RPG-scholar Jonaya Kemper posted a list of links in regards to Bleed on Twitter - and that list you find at the end of the free add-on-document is a copy-paste of that list.

                                      Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

                                      Ah, this is one of those, "Nordic LARP takes method acting way too seriously" things, isn't it?
                                      Not method-acting per se (although I could see it with how intense the tone was when the performing-aspect of roleplaying was discussed in that one link on a Jane Austen LARP) but the two pages that were shown had this upbeat tone about Bleed and made recommendations like how you should play characters that are similar to you or how you should bring in your own life-experiences to the roleplaying to increase Bleed... Because what the section is going for is trying to sell you on "Bleed-hunting". And I think the WOD-lead-developer Juhana Pettersson (on the Renegade-team-side, not the Paradox-in-house-team-side) who has a background in Nordic LARP just got stuck in his own perspective on Bleed when he tried to champion a specific Nordic LARP concept like "Bleed-hunting". He didn't take into account that the wider RPG-community has a much more varied relationship with Bleed than how it's handled in Nordic LARP specifically.

                                      And the previous books (like the V5 corebook and the Players Guide) had a more general and cautious take on it, more along the lines of "It happens, it can be bad, be careful.". So to then take the deep plunge here with "Bleed-hunting" and talk about something like "How to increase Bleed" seems like quite the leap. That's why that additional document was necessary. And that's why I feel like the document should've kept going. You have the groundwork for what Bleed is and now you need a couple more pages to talk about "Bleed-hunting" and how to handle that. You get it from the links provided in the document but it should appear in the published material being discussed in original writing. One, just presenting links is tricky because you never know if things will change (the link to the Expanded Storyteller mechanics in the W5 corebook is already out of date, for example) and two, offering some original writing on the topic lets the writers tailor the text to what VtM is about.

                                      Ultimately it's not like there's anything wrong with proposing Nordic LARP specific concepts. Some people will like it, some people won't. It's a specific approach to roleplaying that can be there as a suggestion. And the issue was that it wasn't presented as a suggestion. Especially since this concerns player-emotions, it should come with quite a few caveats and the stakes are raised because this isn't just about roleplaying in general but romantic roleplaying in particular.
