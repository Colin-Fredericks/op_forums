                                        People playing Panders is extraordinarily rare in my experience, and I have yet to have one person play a thinblood. People want to play VAMPIRES. Full on, honest to god, VAMPIRES. They want to do the cool stuff they see in movies. Full powers, even the full weakness experience of dodging sunbeams Near Dark style during a daylight attack. If someone is playing a Gangrel, they want the full experience of turning into the friggin Wolfman during a frenzy. If someone is playing a Toreador, they want the full experience of sipping blood from wineglasses at lavish parties, and angsting about how the ugliness of the world is literally poisoning them. They want to play VAMPIRES, not cursed humans seeking to kill the REAL vampires and thereby cure themselves.

                                        They don't want to burn down the castle. They want to OWN the castle, which is why playing the guys trying to burn down the castle has never been remotely as attractive as the creators of the game want it to be.

                                                            Caitiff, as far as I understand have three loose purposes: to be the lowest rank of kindred society, to be a catch-all for concepts that don't fit the Clans, and to be a sign of Gehenna as whatever caused Clans in the first place slowly fades. Unfortunately the first makes them unappealing to play, it turns out that six of the seven core clans are broad enough for the second to rarely matter (especially after another six Clans and dozen Bloodlines), and the third is entirely a plot-based thing. Thin-Bloods also served points one and three to an even greater degree, and tended to be Caitiff anyway, which makes them even less desirable.

                                                            Then we get into the simple fact that a lot of people don't want to be playing struggling neonates, they want to play the politics of ancillae or elders. Which makes the social exclusion that perfectly primes Caitiff for a Starving Anarchs games serve as an active detriment worse than being an extremist Brujah demagogue (who, thanks to their Clan, at least had a seat at the table until V5).

                                                            It's not really a surprise Requiem combined both into Revenants and implied that they shouldn't be a player option.
