      I would say, it is a bit complex
      First, because of her liniage. Savona is the Great-grandchilde of Julia Antasia, making her of Tinia's lineage. Tinia was a respected and feared and quite influental 4th generation ancient, who ruled alongside 13 other vampires over the Etruscans (until she was deposed by her childe Collat) It is told that at the peak of her power, she lost the ability to innovate and began stealing concepts from the best mortal minds.
      This is a bit mirrored in her descendants. Julia's political acumen, rhetoric skill, sense of duty, charisma, strong will, common sense, and compassion was quickly cought her sire's attention.
      A few unknown vampires later, we reached Savona
      She was selected for the value of her contacts, but her sire quickly discovered the quality of her Machiavellian mind, perfectly suited for the Ventrue and the Camarilla. A fresh power player, many elders and ancillae consider her a mere “new money” Ventrue. Their wiser clanmates know her actions sway councils, corporations, and individual mortals possessing real power. Where her predecessors focused on vampire politics, Fiorenza believes the key to Kindred longevity lies in the manipulation of the living.
      And this is another piece of the puzzle
      It was not the mortal influance that triggered the Second Inquisition, but the NSA managed to crack SchreckNet and gained firsthand knowledge of the existence of a race of undead bloodsuckers that were spread in all corners of the world. The Vatican, the SO13, the the GRU's Eighth Direction, Israel's Unit 8211, the NSA/CIA Special Access Program called FIRSTLIGHT, France's Calcédoine.. yes, they harassed numerous vampires, but if you watch their events closely, the SI offed the Tremere of Vienna, the Eighth Direction hunts the Brujah of Russia, Calcédoine cleared out Marseille, a historically Toreador stronghold, SO13 has systematically cleared London of vampires, but London since the Fall of Mithras wasn't a Ventrue stronghold, no matter what Queen Annne wanted to belive (tho' Mithras played the long Game in his new Monty suit)
      The Venture mostly been left alone, and I strongly suspect, especially becasue of the mortal influence Savona and others like her built up. In a sense, she was and is truely the future of the Clan.
      Escpecially after the Beconing made the Elders dissapear, most of the Ventrue Elders in theory went away, only a few managed to resist the Call.
      These and her powerplay makes her Hardestadt​ 2.0 in a sense

      Of course this is my two cents in the matter, but Fiorenza Savona worked hard as a mortal and continues to do so as a Kindred to ensure that wealthy and powerful vampires remain in their positions.
      Especially the Ventrue and the ones like her...
      As an ascending kingpin of the clan Ventrue, and acting Ventrue Premier of Mexico City? Paired with the web of Boons and her relative young age? In the Beconing-vakuum of Elders and her influence and view of the world?
      I think more than enough to "validate her" to be in the position where she is now​

          Not to come off as rude, but we all have access to the same information and copy-pasting the Wiki and books doesn't make it make sense.

          Every Ventrue descends from an influential and powerful methuselah. That's just how clans, bloodlines, and the embrace works, especially for a lineage-sensitive clan like the Ventrue. That just means she's a Ventrue. She has lots of money and mortal influence? So does every Ventrue, that's kind of how Ventrues work. She "works hard"? so does every other Ventrue.

          None of this adequately explains how she's mysteriously Hardestadt 3.0. Especially when her character sheet and modus operandi is basically a laundry list of "everything we said we want to move away from, thematically, with v5" combined with "everything that will get you hunted and killed by the Second Inquisition".

          It's funny you mention the power vacuum created by the Beckoning. Here's a better question in light of that, why isn't she being Beckoned? She's canonically 8th, vampires of up to 9th are being Beckoned, correlative to but not dependent upon age. She should by all rights be among them, which is yet another area in which this character breaks lore and theme as established by v5.

          No Ventrue in their right mind should be willing to deal with her, let alone line up around the block to answer to her beck and call. She's too high profile, reckless with her contacts and influence if we're to believe what's said about how she wields them contrasted against established v5 fluff, and dealing with her is far too risky with the Second Inquisition around. The way she's written, if she goes down practically the entire clan goes down, and that's a level of centralization with which even the Ventrue would hestitate.

          So, there's only one of two ways I can see this character.

          Either she is, as you said, working with the SI. Or, she's an infiltrator playing at some Kemintiri-level bullshit. Or, it's an alternate identity for a Ventrue elder or methuselah. Or, the clan is setting her up as the ultimate patsy so that whenever she does go down it looks to the SI they've tagged Ventrue Prime, and leave the rest alone. Assuming there's some lore-consistent, interesting, endgame here.

          I gotta admit, it actually would be funny as hell if Savona was Kemintiri.

          But, I fear the second way is probably closer to the truth: the writers ditched Jan for good reason as the Ventrue signature character, but still needed one, so they made #girlboss without general regard for anything else they wrote.

                                                  There isn't a rule for the Beckoning. It's a tool for Storytellers (and authors) to remove the vampires they want gone and keep the ones they want to remain. It's mysterious in-setting, and some vampires find ways around it, but it's not supposed to be a phenomenon that affects every vampire equally, no more than influenza kills everyone who catches it.
