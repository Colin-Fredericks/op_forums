            Serious thoughts though: I can't imagine Dark Thaumaturgy working with V5's model. Pain/pleasure/taking of the spirit/defiler might be "dark enough" and might be converted to rituals, maybe path of the unspoken would function. But also since thaumaturgy is getting a darker tone I was thinking there'd be no need to make such things 'DT' as the normal Thaumaturgy now only deals in blood and curses. Our glorious fires of the inferno won't work.
            But also... there's no getting around it that this edition is not kind to thaumaturges, and posits thaumaturgy as something of the blood rather than of the soul. Maybe DT won't exist at all and Infernalism will be limited to investments. Blood sorcery does not have the appeal it used to; perhaps there should be fewer folk petitioning to demons as a result?

            And also seriously. The ministry now has little-to-no problem with the SotL. Surely that brings the loyalty of the SotL into question?
            I've got to say I'm going to admit to having some WEIRD opinions on Satanism, diabloism, demon-worship, and Dark Thaumaturgy in the Sabbat. I think that the 2nd Edition version of the Sabbat made infernalists to be forbidden in order to give someone EVEN WORSE than the mainstream Sabbat. I think that's kind of lazy storytelling and I think that the argument that, "The Sabbat is about freedom and demons always end up enslaving Infernalists" is pretty weak as an explanation why.

            For me, I think the Sabbat should have the Road of the Devil/Path of the Devil as an accepted path for its members to follows. I might even argue that the Path of Heaven could exist in the Sabbat too but of the two, I think the Path of the Devil is the more likely to exist.

            I definitely think dark magic and demon-worshiping Sabbat should be accepted weapons in the cause of defeating the Antediluvians. Pentex should be known as a group they have works with.

                                        What ARE you talking about? I love the Sabbat. I just think the Sabbat needs to allow the Infernalists among its ranks to wield their power openly.

                                        Why the Path of Evil Revelations works

                                        1. The Sabbat have a serious problem with the fact that people don't realize the titles it possesses like Priest, Bishop, Archbishop, and so on are ironic. Making them fully aware that they are struggling against the Catholic Church and its minions.

                                        2. The Sabbat are worshipers of Caine and in defiance of the Old Testament God that has cursed them. They revel in the power that their curse and bloodthirst provides.

                                        3. Dark Thaumaturgy and Demonic Investments are powerful tools to wield against the Camarilla and their masters in the Antediluvians. These are at the price of already damned souls. So its money for nothing. Chicks for free.

                                        4. The Sabbat do the kinds of evil things that the demons would approve of anyway. It's a win-win situation.

                                        5. This would make an excellent internal power struggle in the Sabbat between the renewed and powerful infernalists vs. the Inquisition and those who refuse to accept their damnation.

                                        6. There's numerous interesting Infernalist characters from books like RED NAMES, RED LIST that could rise in power with the Sabbat.

                                        7. We're a little past the "mom and pop are SCARED of D&D" era. Demon: The Fallen showed that.

                                        8. It could be a good place to cross over with Demon: The Fallen when it re-releases.

                                        9. Not all demons are purely evil Raveners. There could be some Luciferians or Cryptics to help.

                                        10. The Sabbat trying to unleash Hell and the Children to destroy the Antediluvains means they could have a good battle against the Baali and True Hand.

                                        Do I want *ALL* Sabbat to worship demons?

                                        Hell no!

                                        I think this would be something that would result in a lot of my Sabbat characters to NOPE THE FUCK OUT and lead to a 5th Sabbat Civil War. However, that's a good reason to do it.

                                        But yes, there were many VAMPIRE: THE DARK AGE Road of the Devil cults that were interesting. I don't see why the Sabbat would dislike those.

                                            I feel like correcting some things. I actually do think the infernalists are an integral part of the Sabbat and a major reason I like them. I think that removing them is removing a central part of the Sword of Caine and that I wonder why so many gamers ignore the plot of them "infesting" the Sabbat. I also liked the handling of the Path of the Devil in Vampire: The Dark Ages which emphasized the FREEDOM and resisting God element of the Path. The fact that so much of it is Gnostic with the idea of there being an EVIL God holding mankind imprisoned that must be opposed is an interesting angle to pursue.

                                            One of the reasons I like Demon: The Fallen is the same reason I liked SUPERNATURAL before they reinvisioned Chuck as an Evil GodTM. There's a serious question as to whether God is good or bad in the setting with Evil Angels vs. Evil Demons and you forced to side with Team Humanity or believe God isn't bad. I think that's an interesting spiritual angle to pursue with the Sabbat as well as something that could be opposed by followers of the original Path of Night [Christian version] or the Path of Heaven if it still exists.

                                            Vampire has always dealt with sensitive and edgy topics before and having the Sabbat deal with actually demonic members of its sect and wondering if they can get the best of Hell in deals is something I like. I should mention my interest in this is also because prior to getting back into V:TM after 2004's Gehenna, I played a number of years of STEVE JACKSON'S IN NOMINE which I really enjoyed as a kind of Good Omens-esque experience where you were either angels or demons and both had their upsides and downsides.

                                            In simple terms, I can separate my RL religiosity (non-denominational Christianity) from my horror storytelling.
