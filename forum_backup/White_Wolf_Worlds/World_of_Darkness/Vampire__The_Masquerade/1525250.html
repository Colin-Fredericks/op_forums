    V5 Blood-Stained Love and Bleed

    11-09-2023, 11:59 AM
    As we all know, the next V5 supplement is going to focus on vampire romance. Over the last couple of weeks (like with other Renegade supplements) we've been getting previews of some selected double-pages. The previews thus far ranged from okay to tacky and cringe-worthy. Personally I cringed the most at the idea that one way to introduce romantic drama is that an Anarch domain established a rule that vampire romance has to happen between three vampires or more. The reason: The ruling Anarchs believe that romance between two vampires is a "Camarilla holdover" (that's what the book calls it).

    But the latest preview is different from what can be seen on those two pages. From the section "Advice On Considerate Play" in the V5 corebook to the section in the V5 Players Guide that calls out in a sidebar "Mind the Bleed!" a cautious tone is established. What is worrying is that Blood-Stained Love has a different take on that subject: "You know what? Scratch that. Actually, Bleed is fun!"


    The tone is very upbeat and optimistic rather than cautious. And the sidebar gives some advice where the pro-Bleed-section should come with far more detailed explanations and the anti-Bleed-advice is just strange. The only two modes of roleplaying expressed here are either going full steam ahead in embracing Bleed or playing some too-cool-for-school asshole if you don't want that. That's a black-and-white-distinction that deserves far more nuance.

    Additionally, I can't help but notice how LARP is mentioned there. This book is mainly targeted at audiences looking to play the game around a table. You can't just casually export LARP-ideas/experiences and apply them to the different setting.

                                                  Today we're gonna get those three other pages together with the regular preview. It's a bit weird that those three pages couldn't have been released earlier but well, it is what it is.

                                                  Looking at the online-discourse one thing becomes clear: Both Bleed and how to handle Bleed in a game aren't universally agreed upon matters. And you have some stark differences in how people react to the idea. Some have some very negative associations with Bleed and see it as a thing to avoid rather than to encourage and then I've seen the take of "Bleed is neutral." and with the right tools and safety-precautions it can enhance the roleplaying experience. But reading about the latter and what Nordic LARP considers "Bleed-hunting", I'm not sure it's something that's as easy to sell as the text from the book makes it seem. Because to me at least, the way it's described we're starting to no longer talk about a gaming-experience that involves roleplaying but instead deal with acting-techniques to enhance a performance. You go from playing a character in a game to playing a role.

                                                  And with that in mind, the sidebar also makes more sense because once we look at it as actors in roles, then the identity of the actor matters since obviously while looking at a movie, you wouldn't say every actor can play every role in that movie and some actors because of who they are and what their individual acting-style is are more suited for one role than another. And figuring out what roles an actor is good at and suitable for is of course a whole process.

                                                  But I don't think I've ever been that involved in roleplaying a character or played with someone who did that. When Bleed came up it was more interpersonal drama-related stuff or someone getting too passionate about an in-game situation outside the game.
