                                I think Cults of the Blood Gods is probably the "best" of the OPP V5 books even if it is one that is dealing with an exotic topic that is very much off the edge from what is normally typical for a vampire game. In fact, I'd argue that's probably why it's the most useful of supplements for the long time V:TM fan -- it's something that has not been covered by other books and includes a lot of very interesting and useful material not really delved into by the line as a whole.

                                Basically, we've had a lot of vampire religions alluded to in V:TM but we've rarely had any supplements get into the nitty gritty of their cults as well as the kind of weird crazy-ass religion that Kindred might practice. As My Wife is Scary often comments, traditional human religion doesn't make much sense for Kindred but weird variants on them probably do.

                                The fact that it's taken this long for a book like COTBG to be written is something of a shame and I think the book really does a great job talking about the Church of Caine, Bahari, Church of Set, and other major factions as well as makes up a bunch of minor cults that could be more useful as local branches of the religion. I also give credit for the Ashbringers because a bunch of cannibal drug dealing rich Thin Bloods is the kind of nonsense that you can make quite a few stories out of.

                                Interestingly, the Church of Caine was apparently going to much more Sabbat-like but they were apparently forbidden from getting into those detaisl. I actually like the idea it's a revived version of the Cainite heresay from the Dark Ages.

                                Unlike Chicago by Night, there's less "correction" of the mistakes of V5 here but the Church of Set is arguably what they needed to include so people knew what the hell was going on with the Ministry and their re-branding. Yes, the orthodox Typhonites still exist and yes they're still assholes. It's just they're in conflict with the many variant and frequently polytheistic religions that have sprung from Set's Clan over the years.

                                There's also another minor "correction" in that the book provides rules for adapting Paths of Thaumaturgy as rituals for Blood Sorcery with the Lure of Flames and Church of Caine. It gives a good framework for being able to adapt all of the other Paths of Thaumaturgy in a way that arguably is better than Classic VTM because the Paths were often overpowered as a subject of a discipline. Making them "one use abilities" per night that have to be preprepared is a pretty good idea, IMHO.

                                I have plenty of opinions on the Hecata but that deserves its own post.
