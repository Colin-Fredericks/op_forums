                                        Originally posted by Penelope View Post
                                        I never read Blood Treachery. Is it worth getting? Or is it basically incompatible with the historical stuff in V20 and the timeline in V5?
                                        It is... different. The book is written in the format of a play, breaking down sections into Acts (Act I, Act II, ect) and it handles the heavy exposition by having NPCs talk to each other. There is a lot of PCs to do, but it is still a plot heavily and even railroaded story, just one with a different format.

                                        It is not incompatible, at least no more so than some other books. In terms of strict adherence to the supposed timeline, Blood Treachery poses the question of when Tremere left his body to Saulot and moved into the body of Goratrix. In this book Tremere does not seem to contend with Saulot at all, and has the power and time to deal with the war with the wizards. Other books suggest some time after this point Tremere moved into the body of Goratrix. Yet others suggest that happened back in the middle ages.

                                        In any case, I would say the book is an interesting work, with useful material, but one that does not stick the landing.

                                                    On Ventrue, I find this entry on Hardestadt the Mysterious to be rather interesting. One interpretation of Hardestadt's name could be "placeholder of many".

                                                    https://whitewolf.fandom.com/wiki/Us...the_Mysterious

                                                    Did Tyler truly diablerize Hardestadt? Perhaps it was a ruse and Hardestadt The Elder faked his death (he certainly wouldn't be the first to do this). Hardestadt may have very well dominated Tyler and any others present, altering their memories of the incident, and handed over a newly embraced pawn for Tyler to diablerize while dominated, all to make the death of Hardestadt The Elder more believable.

                                                    Also, sometime after 1222 CE, Alexander of Paris at one point went to Hardestadt The Elder and requested asylum. I find it strange that Alexander, a vampire just shy of 2000 years old, would seek out help from Hardestadt The Elder, who was about 275 years old at the time.

                                                    Hardestadt's heraldic motto was Vincam etiam ab inferis ("Even From The Grave I Shall Conquer").

                                                    I think Hardestadt The Elder faked his death. Moreover, supposedly on the last night before the Deluge destroyed the First City, Caine visited [Ventrue] and entrusted him with the role of caretaker of all of Caine's descendants. Without arguing the Camarilla's merits, I could see a character such as Ventrue viewing the creation of the Camarilla as a way to safeguard Cainites. Hardestadt The Elder, "placeholder of many", could indeed be Ventrue, who has faked his death and taken on different identities multiple times throughout history.

                                                    Yes, Hardestadt The Elder's generation was listed as 5th, but can you truly take that, or many of the other things in the World of Darkness, at face value? Writers lie.
