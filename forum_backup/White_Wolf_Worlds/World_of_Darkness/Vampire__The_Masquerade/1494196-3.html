                    Ok, so a post about the Clan which I feel that I've fleshed out the most at this point in time. Namely Clan Brujah. Even though its fleshed out quite a bit I've tried to trim down on the word count as to not write an essay and get to the point.

                    Brujah in the Ancient Nights

                    The Brujah in the Roman nights have spread across the empire with historical strongholds in Punic North Africa, Phoenicia, Ionia and Attica. Since time immemorial the Clans has been an urban one and tied to cities which has also left large numbers of younger Brujah in Rome, Constantinople, Antioch and Alexandria. Thus the cultural division within the Clan are roughly between those Brujah of a more traditional bent in or connected with the historical bastions and those in the big cities of the empire.

                    Generally the traditionalist Brujah in the old lands adhere to a philosphical interests while those in the big cities are more aligned with political and religious engagements or just live the big city life in a hard, fast and dangerous way. Thus the way I see it is that the Brujah of the big cities are more like the modern Brujah while those in the old lands are more in the Dark Ages form.

                    [Brujah]

                    I've decided to skip a little on the Ilyres and Troile storyline and instead run [Brujah] as a creature which used Soul Mastery, seen below, of unprecedented skill to wander both across the world and between bodies for millennia before finally disappearing from records for a final time with the fall of Carthage. A big inspiration, I will admit, for this version of [Brujah] has been the "Voices of Nerat" as found in the "Tyranny" computer game (I love that game even if I haven't finished it, yet).

                    Bloodlines, Versions and Factions, and I most strongly apologize for butchering non-English names here, please forgive me . Also note that the most factions are not necessarily intended to be Brujah-only, but factions which I can see are very attractive to the Brujah, if one goes by stereotype.

                    Aresidae - A curious but dangerous throwback to older times, this bloodline has but two Disciplines, Potence and Celerity and must roll constant hunger frenzies against the presence of other Brujah which they seem to recognize by some undefined ability, along with a compulsion to Embrace mortals from time to time. Its believed that these presents some kind of early version of the Clan or a discarded tool created by [Brujah] for some purpose with many speculations about what that purpose might have been.

                    Molochim - Replaced Presence with Dominate. Originally created by [Brujah] to serve its needs in Carthage, the fall of the Ancient has since then released this bloodline to forge their own way although cults of the Ancient are common among their ranks. Many old and conservative Molochim maintain a loyalty and nostalgia expressed through ancestor cults to [Brujah]. The old devoutions, as detailed a bit below, are primarily found and taught among the cultists of the Ancestor Cult of [Brujah]

                    True Brujah - Replace Temporis with Auspex. My policy of cutting down on unique Disicplines is at work here and I feel that Auspex can work just as well for the themes of the True Brujah bloodline.

                    Cult of [Brujah] - Gives access to learn "Soul Mastery" Devoutions. I really like the system of bonuses in mechanics for buying certain stuff so that's something I intend to adapt from VtR. Its essentially a cult devoted to [Brujah] and its practices, with some different factions within it from seeing [Brujah] as a philosphical teacher/mentor/example or recognized ancestor to outright seeking a ressurection of the Ancient or holding to a prophecy that [Brujah] will one day return to rule its Clan in the flesh.

                    Neo-Gracchi - Buy Allies, Contacts and Rhetorical (essentially a porting over from Requiem for Rome) Merits connected with an urban setting for half the price in XP. Very similar to the modern Brujah in wanting to affect change for the poor and lower classes by both direct actions as well as political manovering in Elysium.

                    Other factions I've considered are Perikleans (Democratic orators and warriors connected with the Grecophone world), Caesarians (thinks that changes in society can only be achived after control over society has been won by armed force and generally connected with the big cities), Hoplites (Decentralists, local rule and generally connected with the Grecophone world).

                    Soul Mastery Devoutions - This is kind of a work in progress but the the powers I've so far decided on are the following, and I hope to add more in the future.

                    Group Diablerie - Spend a WP dot to participate in a collective diablerie. Each participant that spends a dot gains part of the benefits for a solo diablerie in addition to the dark aura of a diablerie

                    Soul Shackles - Instead of eating a soul the character takes the soul into its own body as a prisoner to be communicated with, later diableriezed or something else entirely

                    Blossom of Hunger - Induced a diablerist hunger frenzy in a targeted Kindred

                    Merging Souls - Merge souls within yourself with each other, the more successes one gets on the role the most control over the merging process the user has

                    Transfer Soul - Bring a soul held within one's self into a new body that one is drinking from. if there's a soul within a body that leads to a battle between the two souls. Taking a Kindred's soul into a living body transform that body into a Kindred body. Only works with mundane human, ghoul or Kindred bodies.

                                        Time to get some life into this thread again. This time it’s a kind of low-hanging fruit in the form of Clan Julii that's getting a post about it.

                                        The Julii in Ancient Nights

                                        Few Clans carries such strong associations as the Julii to a specific group of kine. Roman, republican and pagan the Clan of Julii holds the Eternal City and most of Italia in a vice-like grip. Unlike most other Clans the Julii have no long and extensive history outside of the history of mortal Roman state. Even their own Ancient is strangely absent from the myths and legends that so surround the other Ancients of the Clans. This leads to unflattering rumours regarding origin ranging from the accursed Striges to being a mere bloodline of another, proper, Clan and often in that case a Clan of ill repute in Roman eyes.

                                        As could be expected Clan Julii hails almost entirely from Italian stock and with a larger number of lineages drawing their origins from distinguished Italian gens. Kindred and human lineage are often heavily intervened with each other's fate and actions. This has lead to a great sense of purpose and prestige but at the cost of decline as the world is moving away from Rome itself and Italia is becoming but another province in the empire while the Julii willingly declines the possibility of a greater talent pool from outside of their mortal lineages. Even in decline the Julii control huge resources and there is yet time for the famous Roman pragmatism to alter the development, or so many young Julii hope.

                                        Julii not Embraced according to the familial principles of keeping the bloodline within one’s proper gens are seen as bastard children, or worse, and kept as second-rate members of the Clan, and in some cases denied to actually be Julii at all. Dark rumours speak of Julii Elders bringing destruction to Julii bearing their vitae but not of their gens.

                                        Very much an urban Clan the Julii is almost only found in cities in Italy or along the shores of the western Mediterranean. When they go east it’s almost always as officials on official business.

                                        [Julii]

                                        There are three common versions regarding the founder of Clan Julii. One tying it into the great mythical cycles of many other Ancients in the east, while another claims that [Julii] was made a Kindred by the hated Striges far away from the other Ancients in the early nights. But regardless [Julii] don’t seem to have left many traces after itself. Either the Ancient left the others and went west very early or it went into a hidden torpor before it could make contact with any other Ancient. A third common option is that the Julii is merely a bloodline of another Clan, and its often suggested it’s a Clan of ill repute in Roman society.

                                        Cult of [Julii]

                                        This cult is popular with those wishing to show their pagan and civic loyalty as well as those who carry a grudge against the Striges. The cult is light in philosophy but heavy in practicality. There’s little message or meaning in it beyond to serve the state of the Roman Empire and uphold Roman traditions, which can be more difficult than one would think given the vicissitudes of said empire over time along with its geographic spread. The cult also brings together those wishing to fight the Striges.

                                        Many Kindred also offer cultic honours to [Julii] due to the importance of the Ancient and it’s Clan in the founding of Rome and its Kindred shadow. The cult of [Julii] is thus popular because it says that its cultists are loyal to Rome and most often of a conservative leaning. Any beliefs, opinions etc. beyond that can be kept private by the worshipers as long as they don’t contradict the two meanings drawn from the cult.

                                        Factions

                                        Mos Maiorum – This way of doing things is arch-conservative steeped in pagan, republican and, by now, archaic Roman ways and customs. It’s very demanding way of unlife that involves refusing and distancing oneself from many of the fruits and opportunities of empire and the big city, and can easily attract ridicule from young Kindred. But it has the advantage of opening the door for patronage from Elders and public office within the Kindred community to a degree that few other things can.

                                        Caesarians – A faction considered scandalous by many Elders but not without appeal to young and ambitious Julii. They believe that things can and must change and that with popular support an elite group of Kindred can force through the necessary change against corrupt opposition.

                                        Optimates – A reactionary and strongly republican faction that seeks the protection and advancement of the Senax at almost any price.

                                        Constantinians – A pro-Christian faction among Julii and upper class Kindred with many different sub-factions. Its ranks boost both the faithful and faithless opportunists.

                                        Diocletians – A basic conservative faction that seeks reform of the Kindred community to make it work better than it currently does. Given they often, but not always, find more merits in centralization of power in specific individuals than in institutions they often come into conflict with more republican minded Kindred.

                                                            Continuing and rounding off the Julii is some short info on the "Traditores" that were destroyed a long time ago by the Julii, according to legend either on the command of [Julii] or the command of its most favored Childer and at that time, or so they say, [Julii]'s successor to rule the Clan.

                                                            The exact nature of the Traditores is now unknown, or kept a secret, for only the Julii can claim to have been present when the traitors were given their Final Death and only a few Julii elders can claim to tell what happened in those night when the kings still ruled in Rome and the traitors were laid low. Some speculate that it was an entire Clan, a bloodline independent of the Julii or of the same vitae as the Julii or perhaps only a faction of Julii Kindred.

                                                            What little has trickled down is that there was a quarrel concerning the Striges and an unacceptable demand, and that it lead Clan Julii to destroy the traitors, who sided with the Striges.

                                                            The Traitor Clan

                                                            Nothing is known for sure but what little can be said is that they were slaves and voices for the Striges and that they had abandoned all honor while demanding others did the same in service to their ghoulish masters. What Disciplines they practiced can only really be guessed but there are a few clues.

                                                            Disciplines: Dominate, Auspex, and an unknown third Discipline.

                                                            Weakness: They were said to have been weak of mind, in particular to resisting commands of the kind that Dominate allows.
