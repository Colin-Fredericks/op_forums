                THE CHILDREN OF OSIRIS IS A SCAM THEORY

                One of the theories I had about the Children of Osiris was the discovery that Khemintiri has seven dots in Bardo. Specifically, way back in the Followers of Set book, there was actually a version of the story where Osiris is resurrected by Isis and is torn apart after devouring his son.

                It occurs to me that Osiris never actually lived long enough to create any sort of religion, let alone one that was devoted to humanity and goodness. I was wondering, possibly then, if the Children of Osiris were created by Khemintiri herself. Imagine that she was someone who had serious issues with the Followers of Set (no shit) and wanted an army of her own against them. Being a Follower of Set, though, she knew that religion is a great way to motivate people to sacrifice themselves.

                Rather than use herself as the goddess of the cult and possibly make herself a target, she instead recruits other vampires and weaves a tale about how Osiris was actually a holy, good, and kind figure who wanted Kindred to find their humanity again. She also proceeds to teach that the Followers of Set are pure evil and must be destroyed (no shit). The Children of Osiris thus become a bloodline created by her powerful magic that develop their own Discipline that she proceeds to unleash upon her enemies. All based on a dogma of lies.
                And who is to say differently?

                Set?

                Horus?
                Originally, Osiris childe Khetamon impersonated Osiris and founded the Children. The original Children of Osiris went extinct during the days of Rome, and was refounded by Kheminitri around then, possibly with some help from her lover Mithras. That also most of what we know about the Children except what we have directly from Osiris/Khetamon and his son Horus.

                Who was Kheminitris lover in her mortal days. And now you know why he and Mithras came to blows once in London.

                So yes, its canon more or less.

                                        Originally posted by Trollroot View Post
                                        What have I missed here?
                                        Digging this back up because I was reminded of something.

                                        There is a now extinct Setite Bloodline, the Witches of Echidna. Not long before the Dark Ages, the Witches were given a prophecy (by who?) that their bloodline would be annihilated. Their bloodline was heretical and didn't believe in the sole supremacy of Set but rather in the general supremacy of gods. Set was a god, but not necessarily more important than any other and the Witches were known to worship and use magic that took power from deities other than Set. Before they could be wiped out, they began travelling through Eurasia seeking out all of the factions of Setites, collecting their knowledge and mystical lore and then spreading all of it out the rest of the clan so that their knowledge would survive when they did not. It's assumed that this "spread your knowledge" idea was part of the prophecy involving their demise. Importantly in mythology, Echidna was the wife of Typhon. Perhaps by naming themselves after Typhon's wife this could have been the bloodline's way of making clear their allegiance to the Typhonic aspect of Set.

                                        In the V5 setting, the Followers of Set split into a civil war between the Ministry and the Followers. The Ministry is composed of many of the Setites in the Americas and in Europe. Interestingly many of the Ministry's beliefs are very similar to those of the Witches of Echidna - a belief in and worship of many different gods. It's plausible that the knowledge of the Witches was passed down through the Setite clan and ultimately helped to cause the split between the Ministry and the Followers of Set.

                                        Further, if we assume that the Witches were, in fact, loyal to and being guided by the Typhonic aspect within Set, then it seems perhaps the Ministry in 5th edition is also being driven by the Typhonic aspect as well. In such a case the Followers of Set are now split into a civil war, mirroring the way that Set is locked into a mental war with Typhon over his existence.

                                        *edit* Also as an aside, in mythology, Typhon and Echidna were both specifically noted as having a ton of serpentine, snake-like characteristics (the lower bodies of snakes, snakes for hair, covered in snake scales, etc). Possibly that was the origin of Serpentis?
