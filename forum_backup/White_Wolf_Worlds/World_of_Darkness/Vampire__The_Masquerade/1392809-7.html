    Well, with any large scale change in setting politics, we can always ask the question of how realistic it would be for such a change to happen in such a short amount of time when there has been relatively little change in the past 500 years.

    How is it the Gangrel, such a chaotic Clan of individualists, joined the Camarilla in the first place and then how did they organize so completely as to leave it as a whole? How did the Brujah leave the Camarilla so quickly as a whole? Why were the Banu Haqim accepted when for centuries the were a Clan dedicated to murdering and diablerizing other vampires. The Lasombra joining the Camarilla is no less realistic than any change that has been introduced in V5 or even Revised for that matter. At least the Lasombra being in the Camarilla is something that makes a lot of sense and is something that some people (like me) have wanted for a long time.

    With all of those seemingly sudden changes, we could imagine that these changes happen slowly over time and the books only bring these changes to our attention when they reach a critical mass. We have to keep in mind that role playing books that follow a real world timeline can’t spend a century slowly having Lasombra enter the Camarilla, become more of an influence, and so on. New changes get made with new editions and if they are changes that we like (this is one I like) we admit that it had to seem a bit jarring, but we can better contextualize these changes by imagining the events before the current year building towards this.

    I think that the Lasombra make an interesting dichotomy with the Ventrue, as the Lasombra are more ruthless social Darwinist sorts of rulers, where as the Ventrue are more aristocratic with a sort of Noblesse Oblige.

The Sabbat alone is a faction that deliberately established Paths of thinking (Vampiric philosophy) to fully acknowledge and accept their inhumanity, clinging off the folly vistage of human thought they held before embrace. The Camarilla on the other end attempt to hold some semblance of human morality despite becoming in the condition they found themselves into. Even way from their prominent different beliefs, the Sabbat operate in a completely different way than how the Camarilla go on about. Sabbat has heavy catholic influences, while the Camarilla is secular. Sabbat often stick in elaborate packs, while Camarilla sometimes operate in cotieres- but unlike Camarilla groups, they don't do Vinculum. This isn't even to mention the various Rites the Sabbat has. The Camarilla and Sabbat aren't the same at all- their polar opposites, which made them interesting enemies of eachother in the Jyhad. The Anarchs are the ones who have more closeness to the Camarilla in comparison. Not to even mention if both factions find either in their own territory (when Sabbat still had territory that is) they literally KOS the offending party- apart from unique circumstances of parley (of which are very rare)
                                                            Honestly, I disagree 100%. The Sabbat and Camarilla are nothing more than the same scam by Elders on Neonates. The Camarilla is equally evil to the Sabbat, making the cities they inhabit some of the worst and nastiest places on Earth so nobody will care when people go missing as well as the police being hopelessly corrupt.

                                                            Yes, the Camarilla claims to be about Humanity and the Sabbat about throwing away the shackles of it but both are lying. It's all about power and Domain for both of them. Neonates exist in both sects to be thrown into the jaws of Hunters, other vampires, or in needless conflicts so that the people above them can benefit.

                                                            To quote Doctor No, "East, West - just points of the compass, each as stupid as the other."

                                                            There's no difference between a Archbishop and Prince at the end of the day. Same with a Baron.

                                                            This is why the Lasombra in bulk coming to the Camarilla doesn't make sense. At all. The most of them are just way to aligned with the Sabbat than with the Camarilla.

Lots of Lasombra are going to want to seize an opportunity when it presents itself.
