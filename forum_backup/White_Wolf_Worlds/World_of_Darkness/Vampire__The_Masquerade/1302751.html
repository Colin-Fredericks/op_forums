                          The Big difference is that Werewolf have a huge advantage as starting characters. They get their combat shapes, can get addition action with rage, have claws that deal agg damage, and they can soak agg damage with exception of silver weapons, and regenerate damage.

                                  Two important things to keep in mind;

                                  - Are these V20 Garou, or W20 Garou? Because that makes an enormous difference really.

                                  - Garou almost always have an escape button available, Stepping Sideways, which can give them the upper hand in fights. If they're winning, you're done for. If things are starting to look risky for them, they can duck out into the Middle Umbra and wait for a good chance to ambush you.

                                  To put it simply, Garou are designed for turning monsters into slurry and recovering from any damage thrown at them. If a Vampire is going to kill a Garou it's going to involve doing a lot of damage in 1 or 2 turns, and that means Celerity and/or Potence and an ambush. Far from impossible, but it naturally works in reverse; the Garou will slaughter a Vampire with an ambush. And in a "fair fight" it usually won't be that close, thanks to Regeneration, Rage and the Crinos form, not to mention Gifts.

                                  And this is assuming a 1;1 fight which is... unfortunately very rare. Garou who are part of the Garou Nation (that is, those who aren't Ronin, their equivalent of Caitiff) tend to travel in Packs, and these packs include multiple other Garou. Beyond that, Kinfolk can be quite dangerous. So while you might get your 5 automatic damage (Potence 5) ambush silver sword in the back move to work, attack twice in that one turn (Celerity 1) and kill a Garou practically instantly... unfortunately their death is going to lead to a LOT of pain for you, unless you can escape without being seen at all (from both sides of the Umbra). So you'll either die in the aftermath or be dragged out into the sunlight by kinfolk at some point, or spend the next few weeks glancing over your shoulder until a furry monstrosity jumps down and turns you to slush in an alleyway.

                                      Also remember that one shotting a Garou, even with Silver, has the problem of after hittng Inc allowing them to roll Rage (diff 8) and instantly regenerate succes levels of health, even aggravated, and pushing them to berserk frenzy. So Garou can come back from seemingly certain death situation once per fight.

                                      Like others have said, while generation can help if forced to face a Garou it matters far less than proper preparation. As a side note proper preparation for a vampire facing against a Garou is to be somewhere else while your underlings, Ghouls, childer, neonates pawns Etc. ambush the Garou with Silver weapons, preferably ranged ones and with numbers on their side when they are alone without their pack in a manner that the werewolves associates cannot trace back to you. If not properly prepared going to war with Garou is likely to considerably shorten your life expectancy.

                                                          Originally posted by Enderdragon1201 View Post
                                                          Hello. I was wondering at what generation do combat-focused vampires begin to become comparable to werewolves in a one on one fight in V20?
                                                          As others have said, it will depend on the build and situation:
                                                          1. Is it close combat or ranged combat?
                                                          2. Are both prepared to fight each other (silver weapons, daytime, fire sources, buff time, etc), or its a spur of the moment situation?
                                                          3. Where are they fighting?
                                                          4. What are their Disciplines/Gifts, Merits? Dominate can end a fight before it starts, but a Werewolf merit, Untamable, can make them immune.
                                                          5. What is the rank of the Werewolf? Gift access depends on Rank, only Elder discipline access requires Generation.
                                                          6. How much experience do they have? Though without Rank increases, XP is of limited use for Werewolves.
                                                          7. What Totem traits and ban has the Werewolf if any?
                                                          8. Does the Werewolf have a Pack? With a Pack, the Totem cost can be spread out, and the Werewolf has allies that are also strong fighters.
                                                          W20 close quarters vampire-killing starting build, assumes a Pack with Hummingbird as the Totem and Katanka-Sonnak as a Sept Totem (W20 Rage Across the World, p.29), Ugly McAngry:



                                                          Breed: Metis
                                                          Auspice: Ahroun
                                                          Tribe: Children of Gaia
                                                          Nature: Competitor
                                                          Demeanor: Soldier
                                                          Rank: 1
                                                          Physical: Strength 4 (6/8/7/5) (Claws), Dexterity 7 (7/8/9/9) (Dodging), Stamina 1 (3/4/4/3)
                                                          Social: Charisma 4 (Imposing), Manipulation 1 (0/0/0/0), Appearance 1 (0/0/1/1)
                                                          Mental: Perception 2, Intelligence 1, Wits 5 (Combat Reflexes)
                                                          Talents: Alertness 3, Athletics 3, Brawl 4 (Claws), Intimidation 1, Primal-Urge 3
                                                          Skills: Etiquette 1, Stealth 3, Survival 1
                                                          Knowledges: Academics 1, Enigmas 3, Investigation 1, Law 1, Medicine 1, Occult 1, Rituals 1
                                                          Backgrounds: Fetish 4 (Hunter’s Talons, Mirrorshades), Totem 1 (Hummingbird)
                                                          Rage: 5
                                                          Gnosis: 5
                                                          Willpower: 10
                                                          Glory: 2
                                                          Honor: 1
                                                          Wisdom: 0
                                                          Metis deformity: Eyes of Darkness, Tough Hide
                                                          Gifts: (1) Jam Weapon, Razor Claws, Sense Wyrm; (2) Spirit of the Fray; (5) Kiss of Helios
                                                          Rites: None
                                                          Merits: Self-Confident, Untamable
                                                          Flaws: Double Jeopardy, Foul Temper, Vengeful (Vampires)



                                                          All freebies spent on Brawl, Gnosis, Self-Confident, Untamable, and Willpower; this guy can:
                                                          • Win Initiative most of the time. Max Dexterity + Max Wits + Spirit of the Fray
                                                          • Attack up to three times per turn. Rage 5
                                                          • Force weapon using enemies to enter hand-to-hand combat. Jam Weapon
                                                          • Hit enemies consistently. Max Dexterity + High Brawl + Specialty
                                                          • Do large amounts of damage. High Strength + Claws + Razor Claws + Kiss of Helios + Specialty + Attack roll bonus
                                                          • Double the damage dice pool against Wyrm creatures, like vampires. Hunter’s Talons
                                                          • Get lots of automatic successes. Max Willpower + Self-Confident
                                                          • Immune to the Dominate discipline, Roll Over, Mastery and Obedience Gifts, able to ignore most forms of mind control. Max Willpower + Untamable
                                                          • Recover Willpower after each fight. Competitor
                                                          • Get 2 extra Dexterity dots and the gift: Spirit of the Fray. Hummingbird
                                                          • Get access to the gift: Kiss of Helios. Katanka-Sonnak
                                                          • Step sideways easily to ambush or escape. Gnosis 5 + Mirrorshades + Willpower + Self-Confident
                                                          • Avoid ambushes by Wyrm tainted creatures. Primal-Urge + Sense Wyrm
                                                          • Regenerate in every form. Metis
                                                          • Assume Crinos form instantly. Metis
                                                          • Soak some damage. Min Stamina + Tough Hide
                                                          • See perfectly in darkness. Eyes of Darkness
                                                          • Cheap access to some of the best gifts: (1) Falling Touch, Jam Weapon, Mother’s Touch, Razor Claws, Resist Pain, Sense Wyrm; (2) Burrow, Form Mastery, Luna’s Armor, Grandmother’s Touch, Spirit of the Fray, True Fear; (3) Calm the Savage Beast, Chameleon, Combat Healing, Dazzle, Mental Speech, Rage of the Underdog, Shell, Silver Claws, The Cleaving Hoof; (4) Full Moon's Light, Gift of the Porcupine, Lash of Rage, Rattler's Bite, Stoking Fury’s Furnace, Strike the Air, Tenacious Fury; (5) Halo of the Sun, Kiss of Helios, Protean Form, Strength of Will, Totem Gift; (6) Unstoppable Warrior. Metis + Ahroun + Children of Gaia
                                                          • Buy future traits as cheaply as possible.

                                                          One-on-one, in close quarters, this character will kill most published 8th (and higher) generation vampire NPCs. Optimized starting vampires also die:
                                                          • Celerity 5 (multiple attacks)?
                                                            Ugly McAngry will go first, has nearly as many actions as the vampire, can make weapons useless (Jam Weapon makes silver daggers or silver bullets useless), it is very good at blocking/dodging, only needs to hit once to kill, and has the option to step sideways if losing.
                                                          • Potence 5 (grappling / biting)?
                                                            Ugly McAngry will go first, he is hard to catch, hard to keep in the grapple (Kiss of Helios will hurt vampires every round of contact and cause Rötschreck), can take up to 3 extra actions, only needs to hit once to kill, and has the option to step sideways if losing.
                                                          • Obtenebration 5 (impossible to harm, lots of damage)?
                                                            Ugly McAngry will go first and only needs to hit once to kill. It is stronger than the individual tentacle, destroys tentacles easily, and has the option to step sideways to ambush or escape.
                                                          • Chimerstry 5?
                                                            Ugly McAngry will go first and only needs to hit once to kill. Horrid Reality is very strong but the strategy should be one-hit-kill and step sideways.
                                                          • Necromancy//Thaumaturgy 5?
                                                            Ugly McAngry will go first and only needs to hit once to kill. Depending on the Path and builds the powers are instantly deadly (Memento de Morte background and Mortuario merit make Vitreous Path or Path of the Four Humours fatal; same for Path of Conjuring and silver dust or acid), but here the answer is hitting first and leaving the place.
                                                          • Animalism/Auspex/Dominate/Fortitude/Presence 5?
                                                            HAHAHAHAHAHAHAHA. No. Between the immunity to Dominate, Willpower 10, at least 23 initiative rating, and 24 damage dice, none of these are a threat in close quarters.


                                                          Combat sequence:
                                                          • If surprised
                                                            1. Reflexively transform to Crinos.
                                                            2. Reflexively use Spirit of the Fray.
                                                            3. IF massively outnumbered: Activate Mirrorshades and step sideways to ambush later (go to step 3 of the preparation time sequence).
                                                            4. IF the enemy has weapons: activate Jam Weapon.​
                                                            5. IF enemy is a Vampire/Ghoul/Wyrm-creature: Activate Hunter’s Talon.
                                                            6. Activate Kiss of Helios.
                                                            7. Attack. Dice pool of 13 for attacks at difficulty 6, and at least 12 dice of damage per attack (24 damage dice vs Wyrm creatures).
                                                            8. Next turn Rage for extra actions and attack some more.
                                                          • With preparation time
                                                            1. ​​Reflexively transform to Crinos.
                                                            2. Activate Mirrorshades for a potential escape.
                                                            3. Activate Razor Claws.
                                                            4. IF enemy is a Vampire/Ghoul/Wyrm-creature: Activate Hunter’s Talons.
                                                            5. Reflexively use Spirit of the Fray.
                                                            6. Activate Kiss of Helios​.
                                                            7. IF the enemy has weapons: activate Jam Weapon.
                                                            8. Rage for extra actions.
                                                            9. Attack. Dice pool of 13 for attacks at difficulty 5, and at least 14 dice of damage per attack (28 damage dice vs Wyrm creatures).

                                                          An alternate build, considering the Pack as a whole, not so focused on vampires, but even then those guys could kill most stated 6th Generations with little preparation due to sheer numbers and power versatility.
