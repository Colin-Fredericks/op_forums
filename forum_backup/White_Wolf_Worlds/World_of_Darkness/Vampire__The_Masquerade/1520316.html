    V20 / Trinity trying to convert cryokinesis to discipline

    08-10-2023, 03:02 PM
    Greetings everyone,

    as the title suggests, i am trying to homebrew a new discipline for a Pander character that has a general ice theme going (chill wind, frozen touch, obtenebration, snuffing out flames with his presence, feeding restriction to frozen blood and Tzimisce Clan Flaw to have to rest in ice or snow - freezer)

    Since Pander can create their own disciplines (Guide to Pariahs and Outcasts) the easiest things seems to be taking something that already exists and adapting it. Cryokinesis fits the character perfectly so i wanted to adapt that.

    So first issue is the rolls. Cryokinesis uses "Psi Score" as basis which if i understand correctly, goes from 1-10. The only Vampire thing that could work here is Willpower, also seems to make sense.
    or substitute for an attribute and an abaility roll like koldunism does like way of earth, stamina+occult?

    Second issue is the actual rolls for successes, would that also be plain willpower or stamina+occult? (Stamina seems to make sense for me cause you leech heat energy from surrounding so mus withstand the heat yourself and occult is about the only skill that seems fitting, except maybe survival)

    Third issue is ressource - Bloodpoints seem to substitute nicely for Psi points, but afaik there is no limit on Psipoints per turn, so Cryofreeze needs 3 BP and i was wondering it it was more sensoble to use 1 BP and 1 WP instead so you dont have to charge up 3 turns.

    These out of the way, the actual powers...

    1. Thermal screen seems absolutely useless to vampires as they take halfed bashing damage and there is almost no powers in the game (except koldunism way of wind 2) that actually deal cold damage So id extend that to become an iceshield that adds soak to any bashing or lethal source?

    2. Hypothermic blast seems alright as is

    3. Flash Freeze seems right as is BUT id switch it with 1. Thermal Screen cause extra soak seems way more usefull than freezing liquids unless one allows to freeze vitae inside vampires effectively removing Blood points from their pool temporarily (like net successes in a resisted roll vs stamina and survival)

    4. Heat sink could become real good if it allows to fight off fire damage including incendiary ammo or flamethrowers

    5. Cryofreeze seems good but id change that its either unsoakable in general (not just no armor bonus) or change the damage from lethal to Aggravated (loosing limbs does agg and this can freeze of limbs) Otherwise its weaker than Hypothermic blast and a level 5 power kinda should be awesome

    In General i think tis not inbalanced given that say Koldunism and Thaumaturgy actually give you more flexibility, better shields and more agg damage (like lure of flames) and cryokinesis doesnt trigger Rötschreck tests.

    So im open for your ideas and glad to hear some =)

      It's been some years since I last played Trinity, but if I remember the system correctly and from what you are writing, trying a straight conversion (Psi -> Blood Points) would probably result in something that is more like a Blood Sorcery Path than a proper Discipline. Nothing wrong with that, but I think it's not what you're aiming for.

      Your tweaks seem very fair so far. Swapping the level 3 and 1 powers is a good idea, and adjusting the soak levels for Vampires makes sense.

      My advice would be instead of trying to straight up convert it, look up comparable powers from other Disciplines and use their test difficulty, cost, and effect power as guidelines.

      Some suggestions:
      1. Flash Freeze -> Ogham 1 (the tangle effect can be used to emulate an icy surface)
      2. Hypothermic Blast seems fine as is, maybe compare to Thanatosis 2
      3. Thermal Screen -> Valeren 4, but probably with an extra BP for the protection against Rötschreck to adjust for the higher level
      4. Heat Sink -> Visceratica 4
      5. Cryofreeze -> Quietus 5 seems a fair comparison, if only for the effect power

                              Thanks for the information! I ask because I created an experimental Cold/Ice based Thaumaturgy Path for a Tremere with Touch of Frost some time back. I was not familiar with Trinity, but I did take some inspiration from Changeling: the Dreaming's Kryos cantrips, as well as some cold systems found throughout V20. You're welcome to draw inspiration from these as well - actually, we arrived at very similar ends.

                              •​ Chill - Lower ambient temperature of room or room sized area. Could be used to better preserve food, blood, or a body! Very uncomfortable for living creatures. Can be chilled to a temperature of 0° Celsius.​
                              •​•​ Frostbite - Must touch target to activate. -1 on Dexterity based rolls for the scene per success. Vampires can spend blood points to counter the successes scored on the initial roll.
                              •​•​•​ Cold Blooded - Freezes the blood in the target's veins, one blood point per success. Blood points are unusable for vampires for the remainder of the scene. This causes 1 point of bashing damage to vampires per success, which can be soaked and halved. Damage becomes lethal to mortals.
                              •​•​•​•​ Heat Shield - Reinforces caster's body against heat and flame by freezing their undead tissue, providing soak dice against flame attacks exclusively equal to the number of successes rolled. Caster still vulnerable to Rotshreck.
                              •​•​•​•​ Flash Frozen - Pulls the heat from an object, living or otherwise, and leaves them in a frozen solid state. Fatal to mortals, vampires are immobilized and take 1 point of lethal damage per success rolled.
