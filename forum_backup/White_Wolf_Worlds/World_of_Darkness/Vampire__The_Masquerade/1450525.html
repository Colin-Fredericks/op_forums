    Non-Tremere Apprentices

    05-31-2021, 09:21 PM
    This is something that is occasionally mentioned, and there's even a Ritual to "awaken the potential for Thaumaturgy" (even though as written, it doesn't really do anything), so I'm curious about the lore on this. If the Tremere came across a Caitiff with unusual powers, under what circumstances would they consider "adopting" them? How high a rank could they achieve? There's a situation in my current Chronicle where one of the Tremere players has befriended a Caitiff who was Embraced by a Giovanni and she wants to bring them into the Clan and House, and I can't decide if that's even a thing or not. This is a V20 question, but I am slowly bringing the Clan into a position more like in 5e, where the Pyramid becomes less monolithic, if that matters.

                  Let me preface this with, your story, your world, your rules. What you say goes.

                  There is no "potential for Thaumaturgy" in the sense that one kindred might be something akin to "force sensitive". Its hedge magic/sorcery/liner magic and anyone can do it; human, vampire, werewolf, etc. What's special about Blood sorcery is it uses viate as a "power source".

                  If a caitiff was found with blood sorcery their might be a slim chance of induction if passed off as a wayward Tremere (similar to bloodlines maybe) but more than likely they would just be killed. Anyone else would be tortured to figure out how and where they learned the blood magic, then killed. Then the leak would be hunted down and tortured to figure out if it ends with them, then killed. If its a person that has blood magic they want they would welcome them in under false pretenses, bond them, learn everything they know, then they would be tortured to figure out what else they know, then killed.

                  A regent would never allow an apprentice to teach blood magic to anyone outside the clan, both the person in question and the apprentice would be killed if they did (maybe even just for asking).

                  Immunity to Ward versus Kindred and the ability to astral project without the use of Auspex pretty much guarantee this person is kept alive to figure out how any of that is possible.

                  SO here is how I would play it: Apprentice asks, Prince and Regent say yes, this Giovanni gets brought in, blood bonded to the regent and put in well furnished prison cell in the chanty where they are the regents new project. The apprentice will forget about it and is basically grounded until they relearn the code of Tremere and if they fight at all or give any push back they will be killed.

                      Naturally, your own story takes precedent over lore, but as for the written if you want her to be useful to the clan and learn thaumaturgy there is content for that in V20.

                      You can ally with the Tremere without joining the clan. According to Rites of the Blood:
                      "It is rare, but Clan Tremere will often approve the teaching of Thaumaturgy under the following conditions:
                      • Capturing or destroying a notorious enemy wanted by the Tremere.
                      • Earning a life boon from a high-ranking warlock.
                      • The recovery of valuable Thaumaturgy artifacts or notes
                      • Exceptional acts of loyalty that serve the Clan as a whole"

                      The chantry will submit a request to the supervising Lord, who will investigate the potential student for warning signs that they're a threat to the clan. If the student is found wanting, the Regent is punished for not being careful enough.

                      "A student must surrender five points of her blood to the local chantry, three of which will be filed with the office of the Lord. In addition, the student must sign a blood contract that expressly forbids the use of any Thaumaturgy against a Tremere in good standing."
                      "Rarely, the Pyramid will allow a Tremere to exchange information about blood magic, such as rituals, with non-Camarilla thaumaturges. Sometimes, ambitious Tremere will agree to work under the table with fellow thaumaturges and hope their fellow Tremere won’t discover their secret."

                      Just my opinion, but keeping to the lore, I don't see this getting approved unless she proves absolutely indispensible.
                      A Caitiff has no allies or conflicting loyalties to begin with, so except for the damage done to your reputation, she should be safe. But associating with caitiff will be a mark against the chantry caught associating with her, it's obscene to vampires of good standing. Also I don't see the Giovanni being thrilled about her existence, this doesn't come across as an embrace approved by the Anziani, it does nothing to further the family or their goals and if her sire is alive he's possibly grounded right now on account of disrespecting his elders and might want to clean this little accident up.

                      The Tremere may simply wash their hands of this. They don't need to risk their reputation while offending supersitious elders, their own higher ups and a necromancer and/or his clan just to teach some Giovanni's ignorant bastard their arts when they can just lock her in a room somewhere and take her apart to find out what makes that magical immunity tick.

                                  Going all the way back to 1e Chicago By Night, the Tremere Chantry was instructing the Caitiff Gordon Keaton in Thaumaturgy in exchange for certain services he would perform for them. They didn't do this because they were nice or liked Gordon, but because he was an amoral bastard who do anything to get more power. This gave them someone who could perform tasks they wanted done, but couldn't put their fingerprints on or need someone disposable to do them.

                                  So this is a very old concept in the game. There's no reason to believe this is unique about the Chicago Chantry although it is probably not typical. Maybe there is approval needed from the Lord or even Pontifex and explanation required as to how this serves the Pyramid.

                                  What I imagine is that the Tremere place severe limits on such pawns (and they should always be seen as pawns, never equals to any Tremere). There are probably all sorts of "proprietary" Thaumaturgy Tremere would never teach outsiders (like anything they created themselves like Path of Blood). Keep it to "common" Paths known well before the Tremere show up and are often considered iconic vampire powers that don't have Disciplines. I'd pick things like Movement of Mind, Lure of Flames, and Weather Control. Be even more strict on Rituals. Once someone knows something, it can be taught to others. So Tremere won't want to share anything really good - just some basic stuff they know is already out there and available.

                                  They will also want to dribble out such knowledge so their pawn keeps coming back to them and staying loyal. That limited stock of knowledge may be something they intend to slowly be taught over many years, even decades. They won't want to teach someone all at once because their leverage ends.

                                  And the chantry should always have some kind of plan, even if it is only general at this point, on how to use such a person. Gordon was an "Anarch" because of his Caitiff status, but had no principles at all. So he was obviously useful in providing Anarch gossip to the Tremere, and likely spreading disinformation among the Anarchs. And when the Tremere were ready, they were going to ask him do things. Maybe to other Anarchs, or against certain enemies in the Camarilla that would be blamed on the Anarchs. He also controlled a brothel and that might be useful in expanding Herd, or planting spies (Gordon had high levels of Dominate) in someone else's Herd. And he was skilled in Finance, so he could be useful doing something there that they didn't want trace backed to them. So think about how your Caitiff could likewise be useful.

                                  So personally I would allow it, but make it clear that this Catiff Giovanni is not a "friend" or "ally" or member of the Chantry. They are a valuable pawn and asset that can be cultivated and used, and when needed discarded.

                                                      So the issue is that the Tremere, by their very nature, are the In-Group.

                                                      Most modern occult groups are counter-culture based or are otherwise "outsiders"; Wicca, thelema, conspiracy theorists, Neo-pagans. That kinda stuff. Settite priests embrace from cult members, and Assamite sorcerers look for active occultists because their caste is laser focused on magic, and that's part of the reason why Assamite sorcerers are like 1/10th of the Assamites at best. Assamite sorcerers are small to a fault.

                                                      Not the Tremere. Because for the Tremere, an occult background is a plus, not deciding factor. Their first priority is finding a good fit for the pyramid; someone who's both ambitious and obedient, who works well with others, and who they wouldn't mind living with for a few hundred years. As for magic, Talent is more important than experience; ANYONE can learn thaumaturgy, you're a spiritual cripple if you can't, but Intelligence and willpower, along with a studious disposition, determine if you can use it well. Most magic styles are niche, fringe belief systems that don't get along with one another, but the Hermetic paradigm is something that accepts everything to some extent, it's an unusual "centrist" paradigm that appeals to all with a great amount of compatibility. The Ideal Tremere isn't much different from the ideal of many other clans; having good overlap with Nosferatu, Malkavians, Ventrue and Lasombra.
