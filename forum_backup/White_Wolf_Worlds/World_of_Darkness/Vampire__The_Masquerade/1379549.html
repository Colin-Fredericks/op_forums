    Anarchs: a daring new direction or the same old trope?

    04-08-2020, 07:45 PM
    In its premises V5 did something that I think is really cool with the Anarchs: they were lifted from a Camarilla subsect used to house malcontents and "defective" childer into a full sect capable to take even capital cities from the Cam (Berlin and Washington are two of the most prominent domains that come to mind, although Washington is only "anarch" as long as it suits Vitel's political agenda).
    Three great clans joined the Anarchs suddenly providing them with a lot of "firepower" besides their inherent advantages over the Camarilla (namely, the ability to be more "In synch" with the times). The Brujah and the Gangrel are probably the 2 most militant clans in the whole of VtM, while the Ministry provide access to resources mundane and arcane both.

    So, "now" the Anarchs are in the position to challenge the Camarilla, right?

    Apparently not. Basically all of the most recent material (most importantly Chicago by Night but also the LA by night webseries) has presented the Anarchs as they used to be . A bunch of squabbling, ineffective children on the run from the Camarilla, with powerful members of their "signature clans" staying loyal to the Cam (see Critias and the Wolf Pack) or going Autarkis.

    As far as I can see the new authors are playing it safe and presenting the old trope instead of the new one but that means V5 lacks a deeper exploration of what the new anarchs are and what they are capable of. Storytellers and players are left to their own devices when it comes to insert the new "status quo" into their games as the officially presented trope doesn't help turning the Anarchs into something different from what they were and even their own sect book for V5 does very little to give some structure to their way of doing things.

    Can we expect future products to show more of the "new Anarchs" are are they doomed to be presented as they always were in VtM?

                  The Anarchs, like vampire hunters, were thematically and logistically supposed to be the underdogs, and V5 has either made great sweeping changes for a particular author's personal wish fulfillment or has flipped every power dynamic for excitement or they really like requiem (Bloodlines 2 factions are following requiem). By allowing the anarchs to gain more freedom, power and thus legitimacy, the camarilla has really picked up an idiot ball.

                  It's an example of players not really knowing what they want. A lot of players really want the Anarchs to win, but the reason why players have wanted them to win is because they like the struggle of the anarchs, and because they like the struggle of the anarchs... it's actually better for the anarchs to lose and the Camarilla to remain an all-encompassing heel for them. The Anarchs are far more fun to play with when they're fighting an uphill battle, and whilst a lot of players have played anarch games and want to see the anarchs win because in their games they've made sweeping progress in fighting off the cam... That's an incredibly selfish mindset that does damage to the game. Imagine an underdog sports movie staring Real Madrid, or Star Wars but the Rebel alliance has half the galaxy's military and all the competent leaders. Vampire society is meant to be a long game.

                  I also feel the new anarchs reflect reality less than they used to. In VTM, Anarchs were a vocal but struggling minority that had a few threatened territories where they actually had power. Kinda like how people non-mainstream political ideologies opperate in real life. In V5, Anarchs are equal to the Cam and aren't just a lot of noise, but where is that reality? Where are radicals making effective ground? Where aren't reactionaries winning? Are the rich not getting richer? Has social justice not been hijacked by corporatism? The Anarchs now don't actually reflect the mortal trends. They reflect someone's magical thinking.

                          Then possibly when the moderates get what they want, they'll betray and the fanatics will create their own organization.
