                                    I think DtF cosmology being more straightforward was a point in its favour,

                                    DtF told you what you were, where you came from, what others like you were fighting against and why,

                                    again, I'm not saying this is the right way to do things,
                                    I just happen to like DtF's design as much as I liked the other ones
