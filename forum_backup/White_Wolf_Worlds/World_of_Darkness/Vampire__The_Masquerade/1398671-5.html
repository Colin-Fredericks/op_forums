                                    Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
                                    There are some standouts (Mercury is OP) it should be pretty clear that V5 overcorrected here and it's a bit laughable that some of you wanna pat it on the back for fixing a percieved wrong that doesn't reflect the reality of the game.
                                    I honestly agree with part of this; I don't think V5's solution was the best way to fix the problem. But there absolutely was a problem.

                                    Yes, Thaumaturgy was somewhat balanced…if you had to invest all your time and effort in learning it, giving it a heavy opportunity cost. But in all my time playing V20, MET, and earlier editions, I've never really seen it run that way. Players by default assume they have access to every path and ritual they could ever want, for free, with no cost.

                                    V5 tried to put teeth on the time cost, by saying learning a new ritual takes the square of its level in weeks (so a level-five ritual takes half a year to learn). But in most games this doesn't matter: either the timeframes are long enough that this time cost is inconsequential, or the timeframes are short enough that "takes half a year" is equivalent to "impossible". So this isn't particularly helpful.

                                    Instead, I wish they'd put some universal restrictions on all Thaumaturgy and balanced it that way, and emphasized that you need a teacher for each individual thing you want to learn: no Chantry is handing out free access to the Library of Every Ritual Ever Published.
