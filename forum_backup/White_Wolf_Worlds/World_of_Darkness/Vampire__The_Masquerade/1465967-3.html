                    My opinion may be totally discounted since I am not running any V5 games now or in the near future, but - I have watched and mostly enjoyed LAbN for the past couple years. I think they did well mixing old with new, using fan favorites from Bloodlines without having them overshadow the story, and in their portrayal of the Second Inquisition. The protagonists mostly work for me as introductory characters, since they all more-or-less epitomize the archetypes most associated with their clan. The only one I'd ever consider borrowing for use in a game is Victor, as I see him as best illustrating the strengths and weaknesses of ambitious young anarchs striving to change the status quo.

                    FWIW in my V20 game, set more-or-less contemporaneously, the Camarilla is in no position to threaten L.A.---they have far more important battles to fight, since I pushed back the fierce Camarilla/Sabbat battles of Baltimore and Atlanta to the late 2010s; nor has the Sabbat lost its principal North American cities in Mexico and Canada. More worrisome perhaps, L.A. is in the process of being consolidated under one "super-gang," threatening to herald the birth of a new sect if events aren't reversed there. This new group, adapted from the timeline of the Gehenna Novels and BJD, calls itself The MacNeills---a rich irony considering they have come to represent everything MacNeill struggled to keep the Free State from becoming while he lived; but they have no qualm whatsoever at perverting his memory and martyrdom for propaganda purposes (although once their tendrils have been firmly dug into other Free Cities, a name change to The Syndicate is being mooted). [Sidebar - while the Wan-Kuei in my timeline were heavily checked by the events of Bloodlines, the New Mandrinate still exists and holds on to the core of San Francisco, a city where Kindred are forbidden, while their puppet Jochen Van Nuys claims "praxis" over neighboring San Jose]. On the other hand, the assassination of Hardestadt has grown Theo Bell's legend to the point where he could very well take the place of MacNeill and Garcia, if he cared to fight for it. His disciples can be found in every major city of the USA and speak boldly of his deeds and what can be accomplished.
