    Glanced through Laws of the Night/Gehenna War

    11-08-2024, 06:01 PM
    My initial impression is that with Laws of the Night is that covers old ground from the previous sourcebooks with some new predator types and clan archtypes and now we can play members of the 9th generation as long there from the ranks of the ancillae and a mention that we can play that generation as the Beckoning is not as strong as it is for the more potent generations.

    Personally I think that they should've kept them out of player creation rules and I wonder in how much time will pass before the 8th generation will be available.

    I'll probably pick it up later and get a more through reading.

    Gehenna War at first glance seems to be the better of the two books as it covers how widespread the conflict is and how the factions are enduring it and a list of 7 active ancients and cliff notes of there backstory and rumors of what they are planning and at least we have three new lore sheets to choose from.

    On a side note it seems now that Agata Starek is not a member of the Ventrue clan and is a member of the Brujah, I don't know if this is a typo or a retcon but then we weren't using her in our game anyway.

    So what are your thoughts on the two books?

    "Power that needs to advertise itself is already losing" Grand Admiral Thrawn

    "Coronation Starscream? This is bad comedy!" Galvatron
