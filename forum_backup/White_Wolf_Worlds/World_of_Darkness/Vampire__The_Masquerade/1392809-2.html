                                I'm ok with what they did with the Lasombra although I have aproblem with Oblivion.

                                Let's see:
                                The old Lasombra weakness wasn't a weakness at all. Claiming it was a drawback because hunters could easily spot a Lasombra is willfully ignore how the game works. In game hunters come after you because they've found out you are a vamp, and no, they don't need to carry mirrors around to confirm you are one. This is true if you play Lasombra, Ventrue, Gangrel or whatever wlse suits your fancy, unless the storyteller wants to add a bit of comedy to their story hunters have other methods to confirm a vampire identity (like learning certain individuals are only active at night...). In truth the old Lasombra's clan flaw was and advantage: it even gave them the ability to shrug-off eletronic surveliance systems.
                                Considering the wording in CbN (5th ed) the current clan flaw seems to work as intended.

                                Originally posted by Chicago by Night 5th Edition
                                Anyone seeing the reflection or recording (live and otherwise) of a Lasombra vampire can instantly recognize them for what they are, provided they know what they’re looking for. People with no prior knowledge will know something is wrong, but likely attribute the distortion to irregularities in the reflecting surface or recording errors. Note that this will not hide the identity of the vampire with any certainty, and the Lasombra are no less likely to be caught on surveillance than any other vampire. In addition, use of modern technology, including making a simple phone call, requires a Technology test at Difficulty 2 + Bane Severity as microphones have similar problems with the voice of a Lasombra as cameras with their image. Avoiding electronic vampire detection systems is also done at a penalty equal to Bane Severity.
                                Seems to me the problem arises from the way people define "modern technology": in my opinion it's pretty clear cars, automatic weapons and the like are not meant to be hindered by this flaw, that's technology that has existed for over a century after all! The Lasombra's bane is clearly meant to make uses of phones and other communication systems relying on advanced tech difficult, it's not meant making them unable to shoot a rifle or drive a car.

                                As for Oblivion: powerwise the discipline is now in line with the rest of them. It's weaker than it used to be, but that's because Obtenebration was overpowered in the past (and I suspect a few of those who complain about the current system just wanted to keep it OP). In V5 Oblivion is still strong but you need to study your surroundings and plan your actions a bit more than you did before, and that's a good thing, imo.
                                That said I've a problem with the way Oblivion works because I don't like the idea that you use a discipline and you automatically risk gaining a stain. I don't think that's a good idea: morality degeneration should come from what players choose to do or not do while the current Oblivion system makes stains happen if the players are unlucky with their dice. I mean, sure, you summon the arms of Arhiman and choke the life out of some poor sap? Degeneration roll is a given (well depends but it usually is). You use Obtenebration to hide into the shadow and escape pursuit? I don't think that deserves losing humanity even if you are summoning the essence of the Abyss. Ultimately this system makes moral choices less relevant for Lasombra vampires as their degeneration is basically a given if they use Oblivion on a sustained period of time.
