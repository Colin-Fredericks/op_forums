In my experience, people play VtM because they want to play pop-culture vampires; that's the whole premise of the game.

                    Originally posted by Natsymir View Post
                    Having lived in a god-forsaken part of China, and having a degree in buddhism and Chinese history, I really, really appreciate the Kindred of the East. They're extremely compelling beings with many features that makes them arguably more interesting than Cainites. For example, their beast had more personality, and they weren't immortal undead due to a random stranger's whims, but due to karmic debt, which made for very compelling stories. They also had that desperate distant hope of liberation through insight, which made their existence not entirely filled with doom and gloom. They also -felt- more properly undead than the Cainites, having the appropriate powers to actually interact with other undead (i.e ghost). I've made a long blog post in the past defending the merits of Kindred of the East's concept, it can be found here.

                    I dearly hope the new writers of Vampire don't axe the Kindred of the East, for vampires in Asia shouldn't be forced into the judeo-christian mythological framework of Vampire - the Masquerade, but rather be allowed to exist within their own dharmic cosmology, the second of the two major cosmologies that have dominated in our world. That cosmology is extremely complex and shock-full of otherworldly realms, bizarre beings and fascinating concepts, yet sees way too little love in Western fantasy. Kindred of the East was terrific because of that, though yes, it could have been much better - and it will be, this time around! To make the vampires of Asia just ordinary Cainities with some token nudge to dharmic cosmology, while truly just forcing them into the monotheistic Vampire myth, would be to squander the immense potential of the dharmic concepts that made Kindred of the East so compelling in the first place.

                    Asian cities in general are also soo perfect for World of Darkness' themes, being hyper-active, almost frenetic nexuses of intense human activity and street life, yet dirty, downtrodden and crumbling, with enormous contrasts between new and old, rich and poor, love and death. As long as Asia exists in a vacuum in V5, that potential is squandered, and with India in particular, where the abrahamitic and dharmic worlds meet, and which would therefore be such a great buffer zone for two kinds of vampires, the Western and the Eastern, that is a huge waste. But so would it be to just fill India with Cainites. India needs, and deserves, vampires that, as beings, exist in a state and world befitting the native worldview and all its awesome concepts.
                    Excellent Article I pretty much agree with all your points, except I love Serpentis but I'm biased.

                    If it makes a difference I'm Asian, I was born in Asia, I've lived with Asian Roommates from other Countries, and I've travel to China, and yeah I love Kindred of the East and its a great setting for the World of Darkness. Lil less Japanese Fetishism as Natsymir mentioned in his article. But they have a great set up for a game, and more hooks for making a compelling character. I agree with embraces being kind of cheap, I think my house rule will be Vampires at least need to spend a willpower point, won't make it a dot because Shovelheads are already canon. Hmm maybe V5 should have made Embraces require Exp or Willpower Dot burning.

                    But I've long been an Advocate for the Idea that India is the Middle ground of the World of Darkness it is where the two paradigms of East and West meet and I wish we had a Rage Across India and something like New Delhi by Night, like we had the year of the Scarab and Year of the Lotus we should have Year of the Sharma or something to cover India.

                    And my Favorite City book was Cairo by Night as well! But it was the first Vampire City book I read so I thought that effected my opinion but it sounds like no it was just great.

                    But I would love to see my Mothercountry of the Philippines covered, I know someone has compiled a Storytellers Vault project for it.
