                                                    Originally posted by Aydoo View Post
                                                    I just want to point out that the main perk of being low generation isn't and hasn't been super cool unique disciplines but the massive blood pool and expenditure limit allowed. A 4th generation fledgling is going to be mighty hungry with a max blood pool of 50 and a spend limit of 10 BP per turn. This fledgling is going to be just straight up tougher than most ancillea and some elders.
                                                    Yes and no.

                                                    First, this fledgling lacks many of the most elaborate uses for this Blood Expenditure, so most of it will be Healing and physical buffs.

                                                    Second, the problem with Blood is that it is still a resources that requires some economic thinking. This fledgling still needs to replenish, but has far less experience doing so and not a lot of contingencies in place to ease the process.

                                                    Third, since feeding is predation, it can't be done both subtly and in large quantities, and increasing quantities means that this vampire will attract ever more attention and cause more problems, for a lot of people.

                                                    A fledgling that frequently uses most of the Blood they have at their disposal will find a swift end for all the trouble they'll cause, Diablerie or no Diablerie. Instead, while the eventual big expense if in a tight spot can happen, the best survival strategy is to be as conservative as possible, so very close to actually behaving as a 13th gen.

                                                    By the way, that was probably true since always, so while in ancient times vampires were more powerful at the get go, their regular Blood expenditure probably didn't change all that much.
