                                    Originally posted by Father Enoch View Post

                                    You may not like this suggestion, but the Carthian Movement is basically the Requiem counterpart to the Anarchs. You could take their faction book and mine it for Anarch material. The book describes Humanists that emphasizes living among the herd, a survivalist militia called The Final Civilization, and Radical Collectivists, to show the faction's broad political spectrum. There's also some freakish groups like the Night Doctors, who do medical research on vampires and/or ghouls, and Hives, which are coteries so fanatically collectivist they dress, act, and think alike (almost like Blood Brothers).

                                    I'm NOT saying play Requiem. I am saying Requiem has exactly the kind of stuff you're asking for. Instead of waiting for an Anarch faction book from Onyx or Renegade to eventually come out, you could salvage one released years ago and make your broad spectrum of Anarch factions from there.
                                    Hey, I'm all for it. Especially because I've already done so. I myself have factionalized every sect the game has, as to have an easier time painting the political map of the game, and in a city. It gives a good general direction for an NPC, and grounds for alliances and coups. And there is much Clan material or really deep buried in supplements that corroborate with these additions.

                                    But basically I took the Council of Scales (of which Critias is part of) idea and made it the moderate Promethean Anarch-sympathizer Camarilla group. Which envelops some Carthian (Cartagenian in this case) ideals.
                                    Many orders like the Bitter Ashes, Crooked Cross, Apostles of the Third Caine, Twilight Order... etc. were part of a Lancea Sanctum-like network of friendly groups/orders. Just like military and monk orders would get along. While the Church of Caine (Cainite Heresy) was an opposition to them. After the major sects formed, they kind of pulverised, keeping some old contacts, but either going Autarkis, Camarilla or Sabbat.
                                    And, I took the Ventrue hierarchy, that is a lot of material to keep entirely hidden in a Clan (and in-game, probably one player will interact with that), and made it so as to be more "open". Its taking the idea of Salons from the book Elysium, and making a big club of elite inside the Camarilla, that already is a club of elite. Of course, after the Anarch War, there was a lot or rabble inside, and the High-Low Clan structure had just collapsed, so the High found a way to distinguish themselves. They are like the Invictus. A club dominated by Ventrue, and the most influential Camarilla Kindred, that in the most higher-ups, help decide who sits in the Inner Circle. And well, after the general meetings end, the non-Ventrue go, and the Clan sits pure Directorate style.

                                    So yeah, I really like how Requiem did politics, and stole a lot of it and mixed together in weird concoctions. The re-work I did on Anarchs is a bit more extensive, and falls out of the matter addressed in the thread.

                                    Strange... When coincidence seems too convenient, I prefer to call it fate.
