                To put an interesting frame-work into it:

                * Not everyone bought Chicago by Night 5th Edition (their loss).

                * Chicago by Night 5th Edition published the rules for the Lasombra and made them part of the Camarilla.

                * The Lasombra being part of the Camarilla is a major part of Season 3 and Season 4 of LA by Night as well as Penny Arcade's V:TM game.

                * The Lasombra being part of the Camarilla is the basis for SHADOWS OF NEW YORK's visual novel.

                * It probably will be incorporated into other upcoming V:TM products like Bloodlines 2 and Swansong.

                * Chicago by Night 5th Edition thus has a much bigger impact on the lore than necessarily its sales suggests.

                * V5 is now the shared language for reaching a far larger audience that may not tabletop game anymore. Just like Bloodlines became that for many video gamers but not tabletop gamers.

                I admit part of my opinion is also driven by the fact that OPP may not be the primary purveyor of World of Darkness RPG publications (which is a shame because they are top quality writers) but they are certainly people whose members are heavily involved in the molding of the intellectual property to Paradox Entertainment.

                Matthew Dawkins wrote for The Camarilla and the ancestor cutls that eventually got approved to make Cults of the Blood Gods. He also is writing for Swansong (good for him) so he's moved to working on video game writing as well. He also is the guy who wrote for the Ministry's current status as well as the Lasombra defecting to the Camarilla in the first place.

                Other writers of the gameline from past Editions like Jason Carl, Mark Rein Hagen, and Justin Achilli are also adding their stuff to the work. So it's not like the DNA has fundamentally changed to the game.
