                          Originally posted by Grumpy RPG Reviews View Post

                          I can believe some Sabbat would do that, or at least some young and stupid ones that could be talked into such action. I doubt they would survive long enough to make a Path.
                          The Church of Caine does something like that:

                          "Willpower is all-important to these vampires, who believe that in order to emulate (or even surpass) Caine, one must harden one’s mind against hell’s weaponry. Gnostics are expected to sample a snatch of sunlight, walk through fire, and adorn themselves with holy symbols, just to show their defiance and power."

                          It reminds me of the Mystery of the Ascendant in VtR:

                          "The Mystery of the Ascendant follows one strict doctrine: The sun is the enemy; to overcome its rays is to overcome all adversity."

                          That's bit different, because the Mysterious of the Dragon enable vampires to ultimately accomplish things like resistance to sunlight, so it's a constructive pursuit, and because they see it as a way to defy God rather than getting closer to him. It wouldn't be enough for a whole path, but perhaps there could be a path based around the idea that vampires are great if they can overcome their unique weaknesses. High blood potency may also be an aspiration for them, even for thin-bloods.

                                                          Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

                                                          To be fair, all Packs had a Pack Priest in the old days. The Pack Priest wasn't there to make you believe in their Paths, though, but to make sure you believed in the SABBAT.
                                                          Yeah, I always saw the Pack Priest's role as making sure your interpretation of the Paths served the Sabbat, especially early in its history. You were a follower of the Road of the Beast, a highly individualistic philosophy? Well you just needed to adjust your perspective to see how the doctrine of the Feral Heart better served the spirit of that Road, with its emphasis on Pack loyalty.

                                                          You do see now, my brother, how the old individualistic Road was heretical, and would only lead you to becoming a wight? There was great wisdom in seeking to emulate animals, certainly, but the animal you should emulate was a wolf, loyal to its mate, cubs, and pack. The old dogma would have you emulate a mantis or even more mindless insect, and that is the road to wightdom. Yes, you see it now. Now here, drink some more Vaulderie.

                                                          And so on, interpreting each path in a manner that served the Sabbat. Cathari would probably be hardest, but the right emphasis on freedom, and how loyalty to the Sabbat was necessary to preserve your freedom, would make sure doubting Cathari got their minds right.
