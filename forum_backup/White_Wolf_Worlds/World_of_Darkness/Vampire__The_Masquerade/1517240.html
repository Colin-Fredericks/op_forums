    Vampires must love how humans are enamored by the concept of democracy

    07-05-2023, 11:13 PM
    In an ideal world, people would vote for candidates not because of similar ideology or party, but because they are actually what a city/state/country needs. The issue here is that we don't live in an ideal world. People who know little to nothing about politics have the same vote as the guy who has a greater understanding of geopolitics, law, and economics. This in the sense turns elections into a popularity contest, where candidates who are somewhat charismatic are voted in by people who don't really understand the consequences of their actions.

    For that reason alone, vampires, creatures that thrive on deception and misinformation, must love the concept of democracy. The most common in clan disciplines are perfect for participating in politics, and unlike vampire society where it's a given by all vampires that they are a pawn in another's game, mortals on the other hand have no clue, and at times seem incapable of understanding how those within the government have their own plots and plans that the public is not privy to. In fact voters are so easy to manipulate to the point that it is wholly unnecessary to use supernatural powers.

    For example, voters have been shown over and over again to be easily manipulated whenever politicians demonize another group. Voters will eat up lies from their politicians and other obviously biased individuals regarding these groups, all while having little to no knowledge about the group in question. What I am trying to get at, is that if normal humans can do this, it should be a walk in the park for vampires.​

                                                  I think it would be more accurate to say that vampires love the Iron Law of Oligarchy, and how it tends to turn every system into feudalism no matter what it starts as.

                                                  Humans are tribalistic creatures who care for themselves, their family, and their friends much more than humanity at large.

                                                  The functioning of society requires specialization and division of labor. Which means that some small group, even in a democracy, must perform leadership functions and most people will be too busy performing their own specialization to monitor that group closely.

                                                  This means that there will always be a small group of people with leadership power who care more about themselves, their families, and their golfing buddies than about society as a whole.
