    My joking aside, a couple of points.

    First, vampires are a race of sociopathic tetchy cannibals. They do not need a reason to hate each other. Hate is their default state.

    Second, I suspect the "hate" for the Tremere is something the clan founders passed down through mental powers and through the blood. The surviving and involved clan founders of the Lasombra, Tzimisce, Toreador, Gangrel, Malkavian, Nosferatu and Banu Haqim were all involved in the jyhad. They knew something was going on with Saulot, and then Tremere and Tzimisce but they didn't know what for the most part. They could not or would not act directly against the Tremere as a group of vampires, but they did send out bad vibes.

    I am excluding the Cappadocians (and Giovanni) from this because the Cappadocian founder was a nutter and Giovanni were not really a thing yet. The founders of the Ventrue, Brujah, and Ravnos were also uninvolved.

                                The Tremere aren't hated because of broad strokes of anything. They're hated because they're the kind of organization that works by making enemies.

                                Warlock history as vampires start with a very serious problem: they are completely new to this party, yet they have to carve a position of power and have to do it fast for survival's sake. That means shaking things up a lot and with no subtlety.

                                It also means not having the time to build alliances based on mutual interests, they have to be forced to happen fast.

                                All in all, the result is:
                                1. They antagonized everyone, even allies.
                                2. They consistently employed extreme or bullish tactics.
                                3. They operated tightly as an org, not a collection of individuals.
                                4. They prioritized blocking threats over building any type of good will.
                                5. They focused on short-term pragmatism.
                                6. They built a strong reputation as a group that makes no exceptions, deals with minimal threats with extreme prejudice, and won't negotiate on other terms than their own.

                                You can say that, in the end, the Tremere are hated by everyone because they deliberately chose to be in this position, their whole survival strategy being based on making others hate them and making sure everyone knows they are a monolith and will suffer no exceptions, no deviation, no member that won't endorse and reinforce all your reasons to hate them.

                                                From the beginning, House Tremere was riven by factionalism. Yes, you had the Conservatives (led by Etrius) and the Conspirators (led by Goratrix), but even within those factions there was further factionalism and rivalry.

                                                Besides all being at each other's throats and manipulating each other, they were also manipulated by Kupala, Saulot, and [Tzimisce]. And apparently they provide a neat cover for many Baali (who are their cousins via Saulot's stolen vitae). These parties got them to build Ceoris in Tzimisce territory, become vampires, slay a clan, enslave their own members, and turn Goratrix against his master.

                                                Then, according to V20 Lore of the Clans (p. 210), almost a quarter of the clan defected with Goratrix during or after the Anarch Revolt. Which seems exceedingly high for such a close-knit clan, but it tells us just how many Tremere hate the clan.

                                                Enforced blood bonds ironically have the tendency to make people hate you, even as you enslave them, which is why people like Lord Mendel defected. Of course, he was also probably tempted by access to rituals that were forbidden to him in the main clan (Giovanni Chronicles II: Blood & Fire, p. 50).

                                                The Telyavs went it alone because they hated the pyramid so much and despised what they had become as a result of the Great Experiment. But some of them apparently loathed the clan so much they still preferred allying with Goratrix, who invented the Rite of Usurpation, because at least he offered a route to empowerment (Ashen Cults, p. 55). This is presumably why some of them survive as ersatz Tremere antitribu in V20.

                                                In V5, they fractured into Houses Tremere, Carna, Ipsissimus and Goratrix, which is one of the developments that makes the most sense. I'm only surprised it wasn't seven houses (or more).

                                                        And not only does he inspire love, he then twists that love to his own ends. He was Goratrix's former lover, and at one point considered Goratrix his most faithful "childe", yet he repeatedly sided with Etrius to encourage both parties to compete against each other. This led to great advancement in the clan, but also deep, deep resentment.

                                                        Some of that was under Saulot's influence (such as sending Goratrix away to run France), but some of that was his own selfishness. By all accounts, Tremere was a tyrant who didn't bother hiding that he was a tyrant. He wanted people to fight for crumbs of his affection. Goratrix, on the other hand, presents himself as a charming rogue who's been done wrong by the clan, which means people are willing to flock to him, especially if they have no prior reasons to follow Tremere blindly (as the Inner Council did).

                                                        But even Goratrix, who is probably the clan's most charming personality, is a conceited nightmare underneath it all. He can't stop himself from being a jerk either. Beckett describes Goratrix's autobiography, House Goratrix — A History, as being like "a Tremere Mein Kampf". The Clan Novel Saga goes further and reveals one of his chantries is full of heroic iconography about him—Goratrix as Prometheus, Goratrix as deliverer of the Fruit of Knowledge, Goratrix as the Lightbringer. Which says it all really.

                                                        But that's what you get when a bunch of highly competitive, highly educated, elitist obsessives with very high Willpower get turned into Kindred. You only get to that point if you're arrogant to the point of delusion. Unfortunately, the fact they can mostly back up their arrogance just makes it worse.
