    Made some homebrew disciplines (some thanks to bros on this forum comentating)

    02-10-2024, 05:52 AM
    As ST my 3rd campaign in NY.I decided to include this time Stoneman(caitiff) into the story so i homebrewed two disciplines innate to him.i m using mixture of revised and V20 rules.This discipline will be available later in game to caitiff who joined Stoneman cause(to unite all caitiffs).Judging by story that michaela almost did mass embrace of ventrue i decided that some of those 8th gen ventrues are now controlling caitiffs and using them for neferious buisness while always promising them to be accepted as "true high clan".

    So if anyone has time to spare(would like your opinions)

    Weakness:Stoneskin naturally(possible some mixture with rock giants of FAE) most of his children are huge size.Character weights round 20% more unless in shoes he might suffer penalties to stealth.

    Antimagus
    Those having antimagus cannot learn any magical abilities(Thaumaturgy,Necromancy and so on).Skin becomes magically blueish on bright light.
    Lvl1-5 Antimagus offers counter checks against any magical disciplines and even aretes from mages abilities used against kindred.Kindred can as free action roll 1 dice per antimagus.If kindred is aware it can use 1 blood point per point in antimagus to grant automatic success .Antimagus protects against all rituals,paths even arete of the same level and other magical abilities.
    Lvl6
    On top of giving 6 dices it also affects area around kindred around 1 meter per point in antimagus.Any magical abilities in that area suffer +2 to DC.If DC is above 10 they must roll extra successes.This can even negate some magical items if any used by they wielder of this power such as brooch created by thaumaturgy rituals.
    Lvl7
    Spell soaking skin.Whenever ritual is cast and its denied by Antimagus vampire using antimagus gets all blood used in ritual against him this applies to willpower if permanenth willpower is used kindred returns or gains 1 permanenth willpower(equal to the highest of caster if caster had 7 and antimagus 8 it wouldn’t give permanenth willpower).
    Lvl8
    Counter spelling skin:Any case where Antimagus kindred gains 1 success over original caster or casters they are affected by their own ritual or spell as 1 success is gained(or if it has resistance they must resist that 1 success).
    Lvl9
    Spell immunity:All Antimagus dices are counted as automatic successes at all times.All vitae based spells of lower level automatically fail against antimagus and others have half dice pools when used against.

    Colossoi
    Lvl1
    You grow larger gaining 1 strength over maximum for the remainder of scene.Costs 1 vitae.
    Lvl2
    You grow larger gaining 1 strength and 1 stamina over maximum for the remainder of scene.
    Lvl 3
    You grow larger gaining 1 strength and 2 stamina over maximum for the remainder of scene and one extra bruised
    Lvl 4
    You grow larger gaining 2 strength and 2 stamina over maximum for the remainder of scene and one extra bruised one extra hurt.This costs 2 vitae your skin becomes stone alike similar to Serpentis 3 you can soak all damage except sunlight with stamina.
    Lvl 5
    You grow larger gaining 3 strength and 3 stamina over maximum and one bruised and two extra hurt levels your unarmed and melee attacks do one extra point of damage.
    Lvl 6 – Giants form
    You grow roughly 10ft tall and
    You grow larger gaining 3 strength and 4 stamina over maximum for the remainder of scene double your number of health levels and get extra bruised and extra injured health level.You can soak all damage except sunlight using stamina.
    Lvl 7 – Stone giants form
    You grow roughly 15ft tall and you grown natural armor on you made out of stone like features.
    You grow larger gaining 5 strength and 5 stamina over maximum for the remainder of scene double your number of health levels.You also gain +3 automatic succeses on all melee damage and soak rolls. This costs 4 blood and can be done as free action.
    Lvl8 – Collosus form
    You grow larger gaining 6 strength and 6 stamina over maximum for the remainder of scene double your number of health You also gain +6 automatic succeses on all melee damage and soak rolls.

    Golem of Marble(combination discipline)
    Fortitude 8,Protean 8
    Flesh of marble is always in effects on player without spending blood points extra.Trying any effect to manipulate form or vitae of the golem are at +2 difficulty.

      So if anyone has time to spare(would like your opinions)

      So, let me do a brief recap to see if i didn't misunderstanding anything and maybe throw a question or two along the way:


      - The game is set in NYC, where some Ventrue from Prince Michaela's brood are manipulating local Caitiff under false promises of acceptance into Camarilla society (i'm guessing the Camarilla is dominant or at least ascendant in the city) and maybe even adopting them into the ranks of clan Ventrue, among other platitudes.

      Is this before Michaela gets decapitated by Polonia, after it, with now some of her "Lodin-sized brood, at minimum" mostly free from blood bonds and competing for the prince's chair, among themselves (and probably with Calebros) or in your table's continuity she managed to survive and still keeps the whole gang on a short leash through Bond and dominate?

      Because depending on the specifics the situation can play out in a number of very different ways. Among other things because being part of a large group of kindred known to be 8th gen can make one into a pretty huge target for any kindred who's not, the highly-placed included, depending on their morals. Even passing visitors can be a major risk, as shown in canon. Gathering minions among any kindred would be quite low in my list of priorities indeed, were i in their place - supposing i even cared to stay in a city where many kindred possibly see me as prime cut of beef....


      - The Stoneman is also around, takes issue with this exploitation and is trying to gather all clanless under its banner instead, what might or not turn into the first steps of some sort of coup/revolution.

      On an aside, any Ventrue promising Caitiffs "to be accepted as true high clan" is not only obviously lying - and i mean "you need to have zero knowledge of what Ventrue are like to swallow that" obviousness, but sort of asking for someone, be it a clanmate, well-placed Nos or other kindred with good Obfuscate or even the Caitiff itself, to record the conversation as a tool to get that particular Ventrue humiliated big time in some elysium or court event. The Caitiff and the Ventrue both have to be quite inexperienced as kindred for that to even happen and not to backfire spectacularly.

      Plus, where are those Caitiff coming from in the first place? The Sabbat would probably just destroy/consume anyone they couldn't associate with a coven or pack - and the local Camarilla were possibly doing the same (sans diablerie - maybe) to any unknown kindred as potential infiltrators/shovelheads, either way not seeing much room for a "free-ranging Caitiff population" in NYC before the Camarilla takeover, or in the first decade after it, for that matter.​ Caitiff have to be embraced by someone or come from somewhere, they don't spawn spontaneously(almost) like thin-bloods.

      Unless we are speaking of some sort of unofficial "Anarch/Caitiff enclave" that somehow entrenched itself in some part of the city and neither Sabbat or Camarilla cared to dedicate the effort to fully confront because they had bigger fish to fry....but that on itself would make for a very different from usual setup for Caitiffs (and the Stoneman himself) to work from in the first place - but i digress...​

      Anyway, if the Stoneman is real, as potent or more than Michaela (or Polonia for that matter) and around, why not just present itself in court and cajole/browbeat the local kindred into accepting him as "Clanless primogen"/"Caitiff-herder" or whatever? A kindred of 7th gen or better could just walk over Elysium with the right combination of Dominate & Presence. And that if one is being reasonably flagrant about it - someone less (over)confident and more discrete might set up things by one-on-one with only Dominate 2-3.... Power and scariness always trumps clan, specially if you can convince people having you by their side is better than against them.

      So, 7th gen might be enough to turn NYC's kindred power dynamics on its head, 6th gen definitely would. And from that point onward one enters the domain of elder/meth-flexing because they have power at spades to strut around, not because those level 7+ powers are really needed for the sake of its survival in Jyhad games. ​


      Now speaking of the topic's subject proper - good, old, vile discipline mechanics!

      First off i must say that i find the presence of a weakness something of a peculiar choice - that, in tandem with discipline(s) and mentions of childer to boot, make the Stoneman seem more like the founder of a bloodline and counter to the idea of him supposedly being a "real" Caitiff. Though he might not see things that way, some kindred could question this and call him out as a liar and scammer exploiting the clanless upon realizing that.

      The disciplines themselves - honestly, it feels more like two effects divided/stacked into several degrees of competence/power than the assortments of tricks under a single theme we usually see. They look closer to Thaumaturgy Paths in style than disciplines like Dominate, Protean or Obtenebration, imho. Damn, they look more like Thaumaturgy Paths than most Thaumaturgy Paths that came after the 1e Corebook!

      Personally i feel like they would be more simple and interesting for your group reworked as a pair of Amalgams (discipline combos) than two actual disciplines, imho. Antimagus in particular, that as is reminds me more of some anti-magic aura/field advantage out of GURPS and some other point-buy systems, i would be tempted to retool into something a little more dynamic, involving magical perception, parrying, defraying and/or unweaving among other things perhaps.

      Golem of Marble is just Flesh of Marble as a permanent effect, The requirements are unnecessarily high for such linear degree of benefit/change. I would have made it Fortitude 6, Protean 6 at most (I lie - Flesh of Marble was demoted from elder power to a Fortitude 4, Protean 4 Amalgam in my own game long, long ago, so something like Golem of Marble would probably be "just" Fortitude 5, Protean 4-5 in my games, but that is how i roll).

      Just my two cents of thought for now.

                  Unless you're really satisfied with those Disciplines being weak, I'd at least base them on the Physical ones, because they really are subpar as they are now.

                  Colossoi shouldn't give bonuses to Str and Sta, as Potence and Fortitude already do it better. Instead, you could focus on Health boxes and something to represent increased reach or carrying capacity. I picture a big enough user as being capable to crush an opponent with one hand while remaining perfectly capable of attacking others with the other.

                  Antimagus is too circumstantial as is, unless you throw spells at your PCs literally all the time. Something to detect or harm magic users would make it far more usable, even if it is weak and the main use remains on the current counterspell effect.
