    This strikes me as a situation that would help drive home the fact that while the Free States are Anarch, not all Anarchs are Free Staters - especially when they’re on Sabbat soil.

    It strikes me as highly likely that the sort of Anarchs able to survive in Mexico are going to be extremely mobile, extremely good at hiding or very hard to tell apart from the Sabbat at a glance.

    Also, if I remember correctly the northern states of Mexico played a major role in the Mexican Revolutions & civil wars, so it wouldn’t surprise me if there are a significant number of nomadic packs and/or coteries which have taken a swing through the region at one time or the other (It’s also plausible to imagine that this is the area Cainites who want to avoid getting chewed up in the latest faction fight, but don’t want to desert the Sabbat, go to for a cool-down period*).


    *Since there would be ample opportunities for murder and mayhem directed against rival factions, whether Anarch or Camarilla.

                I will be having a Revenant family be present in LA, although this is either unknown or unappreciated by the Sabbat involved in preparing the siege. Revenants can provide their own challenge to vampires so it’ll be interesting to see if this leads to anything as a sandbox element. Because both Mohammed and Mariel don't come from Mexico or have previously established connections to the Zantosa, they'll be ignorant too. It is even possible that the Crypts could become gang rivals to the crime cartel as they are more likely to be connected to the Latin gangs in LA.

                            Originally posted by Atticus Cleanclaw View Post
                            I wonder if this family of revenants might originally have been drawn to the region by rumours of Christopher and the Nosferatu vampire who came into the region only slightly later, but hung around because they enjoyed getting a little distance from the bosses back in Mexico?
                            While someone else can run with that concept into whatever direction they wanted, I have a far more prosaic reason. The original description of the Zantosa Revenants says their estates are located in North, Central, and South America. They keep stables because they like to eat horseflesh. Some are involved in the drug industry and slavery. That came together in my mind that one of the Zantosa estates are a ranch in Mexico where they control a cartel involved in drug trafficking and sex trafficking. That cartel is involved in Los Angeles because that's where the big money is. Nothing more. I may just name this fictional cartel the Durango Cartel because many Mexican cartels are named after the state they originate in.

                            This group of Zantosa isn't native to LA. Their estate is probably located in Durango or Sinaloa where they originally were just large landowners and ranchers. When they became involved in the drug trade and sex slavery, the cartel inevitably expanded into LA since that's the largest market. Why did they involve themselves in crime? Simply as an outgrowth of their hedonism.

                            The Revenants, of course, have an enormous amount of information about vampires so they have an advantage other crime groups don't have (not to mention their own Disciplines.) However, they know very little about LA's vampires and limit any investigations to strictly business. They don't want to attract attention to themselves, but do want to protect their operations from meddling vampires in LA. Yes, some individual revenants are either present in LA or occasionally visit operations there, but for the most part the cartel's operatives are all mortals.

                            I intend this to be just a sandbox thing. Unless the PCs involve themselves in this kind of thing, I'll probably not make it a plot element. However, if for any reason they become involved for whatever reason, they'll make enemies of people who have tremendous knowledge.

                                                I found another old reference to a character! I thought about including it in the Canon NPCs thread, but felt it was of more relevance here. In the original 1993 Book of Madness sourcebook, it shows the Tremere anti-tribu infernalist Antonio d'Erlette. (The name is an obvious reference to the Lovecraftian character Comte D'erlette, author of the mythos tome Cultes des Goules. It was a reference to Lovecraf's correspondent August Derleth).

                                                The character's description is as a devoted infernalist responsible for recruiting many foolish and decadent nouveau riche of many eras. His ambition is to control the European Common Market (remember this was written back in 1993). Strangely he has no ranks in Finance, but does have Law 4. So he must be a lawyer, perhaps specializing in EU supranational law. Although he has many havens across Europe, he also has them in New York and Los Angeles. The man gets around!

                                                Going by the character sheet (written for Mage, not Vampire), Antonio has Dominate 4, Fortitude 2, Potence 2, and multiple paths of Thaumaturgy (highest is level 3). He is also given Arcane 5 which I'm going to translate into a combination of Obfuscate and a special demonic investment.

                                                While called a Tremere anti-tribu and thus indicating his nominal allegiance to the Sabbat, this Cainite is clearly more interested in and loyal to his demonic master than the Sabbat. For my purposes, he's essentially an independent Kindred with connections to the Sabbat. He looks Mediterranean and speaks with an aristocratic Spanish accent. He might be thought of as Mexican unless a PC knows enough Spanish to realize he speaks Castilian instead of Mexican Spanish.

                                                Considering his interests, he must spend most of his time in Europe, but the mention of a haven in LA means he must visit the city regularly, likely to tempt noveau riche mortals (especially new celebrities). Perhaps he specializes in actors who have or want second homes in Europe? He enjoys dirtying his hands and dislikes delegating anything he can do himself. So he's a good NPC to introduce plot directly into a chronicle!

                                                For my purposes, I can introduce this character anytime the PCs become involved with rich mortal celebrities. It'll be up to them if they discover he's 1) Kindred, 2) Tremere, 3) Sabbat, and 4) Infernalist (perhaps not necessarily in that order). He's powerful, but not that powerful, and he'll be by himself. He's also a way to introduce learning Thuamturgy or lore about the Sabbat depending on what the PCs do. He may also know of at least one of the deep uncover Sabbat agents of Los Angeles. It's likely the Sabbat does not know of his infernalist allegiance. If he does need help, he may try to get one of them to help him, not caring if he blows decades work of Sabbat intelligence gathering, only to save himself.

                                                        Originally posted by Atticus Cleanclaw View Post
                                                        I suspect that this fellow and ‘Papa Crypt’ would have some seriously Bad Blood between them
                                                        In many ways Antonio D'erlette is a "strange" Sabbat. Sabbat don't typically involve themselves in mortal society, yet this guy is trying to take over the Common Market! He also seems to be a loner rather than a member of a pack. He would make a much better Camarilla Tremere, yet since he needs to be an infernalist in order to be found in the Book of Madness, he must "naturally" be a member of the Sabbat.

                                                        I imagine he must be some kind of high end troubleshooter for "House Goratrix" or perhaps the Lasombra. He is likely based in Madrid since that is the Sabbat's stronghold in Spain. Perhaps he even does things for Archbishop Moncada or another important Lasombra. The Tremere AT often serve as advisors or agents to Sabbat leaders, and being able to claim he is in service to a powerful Lasombra would help justify his worldwide travels. As a "hands on" kind of vampire, he would make a useful agent.

                                                        So Antonio's presence in LA might make Mohammed believe that a powerful European Lasombra has her own designs in LA, and this represents an intrusion into what otherwise would be a New World (specifically Mexico based) Sabbat operation. But if he is an agent of a powerful Lasombra like Moncada, it gives him some political protection. Given that the Anarchs lack any real Tremere presence in the Free States, perhaps he poses as doing an independent inspection on what kind of thaumaturgy the anarchs would be capable of producing while he corrupts the souls of the newly wealthy in LA. Since Antonio doesn't actually care what happens in LA, Mohammed actually doesn't have much to complain about. Of course, that probably makes Mohammed think Antonio is just being exceedingly clever in hiding what he's really up to.

                                                        Although it's mentioned that Antonio's sire was an infernalist who knew Gilles de Rais, we have no information on Antonio's own mortal life. I'm leaning to the idea that he is a relatively young vampire, and as a mortal lawyer was heavily involved in Spain's own accession to the EC and possibly involved in the post-Franco democratic transition. Since his sire was an infernalist, his embrace was probably done for the purpose of their demonic master rather than any Sabbat needs. (It's entirely likely most or all Tremere AT in Madrid then are secret infernalists.) Likely Antonio's sire provides some much needed magical protection against the very potent Tremere chantry in Toledo, and therefore is favored by the ruling Lasombra.
