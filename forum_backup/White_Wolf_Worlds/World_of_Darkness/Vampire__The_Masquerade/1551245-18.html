        Originally posted by monteparnas View Post

        More or less.

        They can exist in the same faction if you make it complex enough. VtM more fleshed out setting made it possible, VtR toolbox approach and need for Coalitions to adapt to each city not, neither approach is better.

        With the Sabbat, it's a matter of understanding that it isn't a unified thing, but a rich and complex coalition with plenty overlapping factions. The bottom line is: everything about the Sabbat is true for a small part of the Sabbat, but nothing about the Sabbat describe the Sabbat as a whole, except party line and the Ritae.
        I have to give you props - that mirrors perfectly my take on Sabbat and is sadly lacking from people's opinions of Sabbat as bunch of ravening monsters. Are there monstrous Sabbat? Hell yes, but this is just one of the facets of disparate groups, factions, alliances and influences. It's politics taken to the extreme where orthodox presbyterian, zionist, neo-fascist and ultra libertarian can all get behind Trump because they think he will do something for them.

        Sabbat is the same thing: loyalists, status quo, moderates and ultra conservatives ally themselves with Harbringers of Skulls, Serpents of Light and Salubri warriors because they all consider Sabbat an organization that will help them achieve their goals. And just like in real politics the disparate factions are constantly at each others' throats, and because they are inhumane monster, often more inhumane than other vampires, their conflicts are a bit more bloody but they are not all mindless psychopaths and terrorists. Yes, individual Sabbat are but we are talking about organization with centuries of history that has influence all over the world - if they were all mindless they would have been eaten by Camarilla or destroyed by the Inqusition ages ago.

        I remember how even back in the World of Darkness Second Edition Sabbat domains in Scandinavia were described as not that different from Camarilla's. Sabbat, like most of vampiric groups - be it sects, clans/bloodlines or whatever are not a monolith. They have differing philosophies, preferences and goals and it can be seen in their actions. Yes, that conflict is what's stopping the sect from surpassing Camarilla even tho it is generally more militaristically inclined. But this variety also it the sects strength because they can surprise their enemies with unorthodox approaches.

        As for treating Sabbat as cannon fodder - well, they can be that same as any other antagonists and sometimes we need to throw orcs at our PCs and we grab the first thing that comes to mind. I am not against that. But as any antagonist Sabbat are way better if you consider their goals, practices and ideals. Yes, those might be inhumane or abhorrent but they are there and it gives them more depth than an old school rpg orcs.

                                                I'd say even Orcs are better if played smarter, but there's also a more practical reason for the difference: in a typical fantasy setting the Orcs live in communities relatively close to the places they raid. So their raid parties make a round of the area, maybe for a few days, take what they can and get back.

                                                This is relatively sensible, and while bad isn't a surprise. Locals know the Orcs are there and know that invading their community is a harder call than dealing with a single raid party. Give or take any criticism in how classic Fantasy portraits this kind of violence, its an enemy neighbors situation and has a certain balance in it (as rarely any side is bent on genocide of the other, at least until PCs appear).

                                                But that's the situation you rarely see in WoD. Most Camarilla cities are very far from Sabbat domains and vice versa. Wandering packs may exist, but they're not common and wouldn't be around if they were idiots. The world is full of more dangerous things than them and that's not a hard secret.

                                                So I'd say the opposite of what CTPhipps said: it's precisely because this is not a siege that an incursion of any size should raise alarms. If there was a siege, or a hostile city close by, then meaningless incursions would actually make a lot of sense. Without it? No.

                                                Originally posted by Lysander View Post
                                                Some of those shovelheads might even surprise the sect leadership and gain a victory over seemingly impossible odds and take a interest in them and start to train them like they did to Polina when he gained invaluable Intel over possible lupine threats and saw promise in him.
                                                A lot of people underestimate how varied and unpredictable shovelheads are, and run them as if they were just crazy gang members like a bunch of Joker thugs.

                                                Shovelheads are random people taken from the streets in bulk, terrorized and painfully killed in the process if the Embrace, then buried in a shallow grave still in Hunger Frenzy.

                                                They are not indoctrinated. They are not loyal or Blood Bound. They have nothing in common and no prepare beyond that. What they have at first is pain, shock and Frenzy, feeling their Beast at its worse.

                                                No typical Shovelhead exists. There are thieves and workers and good housewives. Teenagers and the elderly. Most are the kind of NPC that would get even less starting stats than a regular mortal made with PC rules. They have no means to resist the Beast.

                                                How many sireless Vampires arriving at the Domain, Caitiff or not, aren't former Shovelheads who got away? Impossible to know. The terror tactic that makes them also makes accounting for all of them all but impossible. Many will never raise from the earth, though some will just take more time and raise with no one nearby.

                                                For those that raise soon and stick around, when they finally calm down from Frenzy they still have shock, despair and the Sabbat to deal with. Those guys are the only ones with answers for what you are. They'll also kill you if you don't follow their instructions or is just too boring and useless. This makes for some way of conformity with Sabbat ideas, especially in the more stereotypical sense (the only one they know as safe), but that's still quite a random bunch.

                                                What happens is that random is random, and you can luck yourself on someone better than you could dream if you tried. Sure, it's quite probable that those are precisely the ones who gets away and shore up in other domains, but a few certainly stay, follow the group and, in the end, embrace the life as Sabbat, eventually climbing the ladder and discarding the stereotype.

                                                Hunters are smart by default. They don't seek vampires in the street to attack randomly since they're aware of how much more powerful the Vampires are.

                                                What makes hunters dangerous is that you can't distinguish them from other mortals, so it's easy for them to wander around, get info on you, spy and stay relatively inconspicuous. The worse thing you can do is make their investigation even easier.

                                                Once they know everything they can, they plan the strike, and that's the big problem. Unless you're smarter and find them first, they'll have as much control of how and when you'll face each other as possible. If they can, they'll get you by day, while you're sleeping and defense is just mortals, Ghouls and alarms, things they can deal with.

                                                The strength and willingness to kill with fanfare only matters if you have a chance of seeing the strike beforehand. Determined mortals can, with time and planning, literally bring a bulldozer to your haven and lift the ruble from your torpid corpse for you to see the sun.

As per the principle of "our world but darker" you can run then as fully fitting the mold, but even within canon reality is usually less extreme when you actually describe the place. And even then those are just a few extreme cases, most canon Sabbat cities aren't anything like that.
