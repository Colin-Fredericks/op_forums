    I can't put into words how much I hate the fact that vampires can't see in the dark, I'm not kidding when I say that one of the reason I switched to requiem was that they finally had fucking night vision as a natural ability, I never cared for this bullshit about vampirism being a curse, they are night predators so they should fucking see in the dark dammit, regarding masquerade I think that it didn't steal enough from requiem and what it did, it warped it too much and turned it into shit. predatory aura, enhanced senses and lashing out should have been part of the standard package from the beginning.
    That's pure poetry, I couldn't say it better.

    We can have a long discussion about other innate powers they should have. I particularly love the Requiem 2nd package for all the reasons you and Heavy Arms posted (the abilities to track blood are really fun), but if someone doesn't think it's cool, ok, I'm fine.

    Night vision is simply the basic. I'm not saying they must have any particular rule on night vision, just that they need to have something, or something else to complement their senses. Heck, they're nocturnal predators, they need to be able to function at night due to something other than their prey having invented the lamp.

    Outside of that, if I would add something to the VtR package, that would be a bonus on stealth and some forms of movement. Being a dead body that doesn't need to move at all is a superb way to not make any noise or call attention to yourself, you're basically an object. And vampires climbing, walking underwater and other such stuff are very cool ideas that, while perfectly possible within the descriptions we have, could use a small mechanical benefit to help deliver the scenes.

        Originally posted by Newb95 View Post


        I fail to see how being part of a clan should pigeonhole players into specific playstyles, all of the clans can be played in infinite ways (you would be surprised how good of an infilitrator a stealthy Ventrue could be), your idea that vampires should operate and feed only following specific patterns is faulty and would only limit the players experience just like V5 and predator types.
        You're looking at this too much from a player perspective, and in a way you're right, but in other ways you're really off the mark.

        1-"All of the clans can be played in infinite ways" is a great idea but you can really do this more with attributes and skills and if you must, secondary disciplines. You don't need to force heightened senses on everybody to have a sneaky Ventrue that slinks around in the dark, you just need to give him a great amount of larceny and stealth and he can not-to-ambitiously work on getting a level one out-of-clan discipline if you're not satisfied.

        2- Sires more often than not choose people who are like them and will thus adapt to the clan character and disciplines readily. Siring a Ventrue with poor social skills is a great way to embarass yourself and the clan. The talents of the vampire make it easier to feed a certain way, these disciplines make it easier to feed a certain way, and depending on who's game you're in either practicing these talents to feed or these Disciplines to feed will reinforce your way of hunting and the Discipline development for this style. Following this formula isn't mandatory, as you can see with point 1, but it is highly encouraged both mechanically and story-wise. If you want to play a deviant, you're setting yourself up for a challenge: Why take the easy route out and give everyone nightvision?


        A level one Discipline really isn't that much of an investment, and everybody should be investing a few levels into either Auspex or Protean for their other effects anyway. There's no need to give the power right from the embrace.

                There's also a reason I keep pointing to VtR 2e here. Kindred Senses in VtR 2e makes Auspex 1-3 and Protean 2 so much better because they don't need to waste time on some boosted senses. VtR 2e's lower levels of Auspex allow way more creativity on both sides of the ST screen because it's not just a boost to Perception, and Protean 2 lets you manifest fully functional non-human sensory organs. By making better senses a mechanical reality of the base vampire package, you're also letting the Disciplines that enhance senses to do way cooler shit.
