        Personally, I don’t like the Banu Haqim / Assamites joining the Camarilla. Of course, when someone asks a question on an RPG forum, they are typically interested in canonical answers, not personal opinions or house rules. In Revised and V5, Assamites join the Camarilla. Which means that the Tremere monopoly on magic has already been broken, especially in V5 where Blood Sorcery is a Clan Discipline for them. If I were doing V5, like MWIS, I wouldn’t have the Assamites as a Camarilla Clan, but that is a tangent.

        Actually, the monopoly really started to be broken in Revised when there was a huge proliferation in blood magic. Every non-Camarilla Clan got its own form of blood magic, which always struck me as silly. Now, those were non-Camarilla Clans, but making blood magic so common certainly undermined the idea that the Tremere had strict control over blood magic.

        Now that thin blooded vampires have a form of blood magic and the Tremere are no longer unified, and so be more likely to sell or trade away their secrets, there is even less of a Tremere grip over magic. Also, blood sorcery does a lot less than it used to, so monopolizing it isn’t quite as special as it used to be anyway.

        So, I’d say for all practical purposes, the Tremere’s Camarilla monopoly on blood magic is largely gone, even if they are still the resident experts.

                                            Also, why would the Camarilla and the Inner Circle want to cause generalized chaos upon cities that up to that point were relatively stable?

                                            First of all, that would be stupid for the Inner Circle to do, since it would cause massive problems on many cities;

                                            And Secondly it's completely unnecessary, since the Assamites would actually bring a good deal of safety and stability to many other cities that lacked those and that would welcome them with open (more or less) arms.

                                            And yes, the Princes / Inner Circle relation cuts both ways.

                                            But that means exactly that: it cuts both ways.

                                            It's not a good idea for any Prince to simply break the mask and declare blood hunts on the Justicars; but such insanely extreme case would be just that, an improbable extreme case. And such an idiotic prince would surely fall, but because of his own stupidity, not because the Inner Circle didnt like his funny face.

                                            On the other hand thou, if the Justicars started to go insane and willy nilly turn Princes into ashes just because, the Camarilla would be over because every Prince would effectively change their title to "Barons" and the Justicars wouldnt be long for this Earth.

                                            So, to sum it up: the powers of Princes, Justicars and Inner Circle is a fragile balance of political power. And the members of the Inner Circle arent stupid to just simply blow it all up just to behave as spoiled children wanting to have it their way. It's all a big massive political game that they play; they are not vampiric kings of their clans.

                                            Just think about the apex of feudalism. Yes, there was even a king, but should such a king overplay his authority and overstep his boundaries, the lords would dethrone him in no time.

                                            And the Camarilla also doesnt need to play like that. There are several levels of cooperation and "tolerable backstabbing" that allows it to be functional. And in fact, being largely descentralized is actually one of the reasons why the Camarilla has been so successful; if there was a bunch of "olygarch kings" trying to micromanage everything, the sect would've fallen to the Sabbath a long time ago
