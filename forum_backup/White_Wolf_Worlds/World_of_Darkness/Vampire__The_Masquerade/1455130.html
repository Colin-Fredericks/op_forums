    Chris Avellone finally explains what the hell happened with Bloodlines 2

    07-18-2021, 05:38 PM
    https://chrisavellone.medium.com/wha...2-1ad557bf2284

    I tried to cut and paste the article about the story but it turned out to be more difficult than I expected. However, here's a rough summary about what happened during his involvement with the game and why it started off fairly promising and slowly went to shit. I'm not a big fan of Chris Avellone both with his writing and behind the scenes stuff but he gives an excellent summation of things with an actual insider's view.

    I think it's worth reading for anyone who had any real desire to see this game brought to fruition as well as what is likely going to happen with it soon.

      Wow yeah, sounds like a complete clusterfuck. I still can't get over how they had Brian Mitsoda and Chris Avellone who is, if nothing else, one of the biggest RPG writers out there, on the project. And then they fired Mitsoda and threw everything Avellone wrote in the trash. I can't even begin to imagine what was going through the exec's heads when they did that. It really sounds like they had all the ingredients for a successful game lined up, and they had huge hopes and expectations from the general public, but decided to just toss everything on the floor and take then a giant shit on the counter.

      At this point it looks like game has turned into vaporware since, as Avellone says, everyone he was working with on the game at Hardsuit is no longer working with the company, and all of the people he was working with at Paradox are also no longer with that company.
