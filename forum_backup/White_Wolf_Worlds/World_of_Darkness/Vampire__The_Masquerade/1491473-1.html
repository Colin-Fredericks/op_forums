                                                    Originally posted by Lysander View Post
                                                    The next best question is which of the antidulivans decided that the Jyhad was a waste of time and decided not to play anymore?

                                                    In that case we have the Gangrel who withdrew earlier then some and decided to become melded with the Earth and doesn't seem to show much interest in her clan actions at all.
                                                    I actually think a number of them left the game one way or another, already.

                                                    What follows is my personal take for now. I may change my mind.

                                                    Brujah/Troile: buried in Carthage, dead or isolated from the time-stream for too much time. The clan is effectively left to its own devices;

                                                    Ventrue/Siddartha: faked its own death and retired from the Jyhad after noticing that continued development of Dominate and their role in the eternal conflict would mean Ego Death. Now struggles constantly to avoid such fate and keep its grasp on personal identity, maybe also interfering to disrupt mental powers in general, I'm not sure;

                                                    Toreador/Arikel: retired to seek ways to recover its humanity in some sense. Currently lives as most Neonates, keeping a human facade with a human job and human activities, and particularly enjoy leading people through letters, and now the internet, in order to nullify its own preternatural advantages, both as a means to study people and for the pleasure of the challenge;

                                                    Malkav: lost in the depths of the Madness Network, which is the living-but-non-sentient manifestation of Dementation. Effectively retired despite still extremely influential in the Jyhad;

                                                    Gangrel/Ennoia: playing a dangerous game, actually. I always found it deeply weird that a clan with such an uninvolved take to Embrace had such a uniform outlook. I think it isn't actually as disinterested as it always pretended to be, and whispers in the ears of its childer with the goal of causing this culture to take hold for its own end goals. But by doing so, risks being truly engulfed by its melding with Earth just as so many think it did. I'm undecided if it avoided such fate or not;

                                                    Nosferatu/Absimiliard: technically in the Jyhad, but too self-loathing and self-centered to actually be of any consequence. In a sense, the opposite of Malkav, making some effort to participate, but ultimately being inconsequential even to most of its childer;

                                                    Tremere: at large in Goratrix's body, maybe he even kept the 3rd gen powers, who knows. Still game, but recovering from a severe setback after the loss to Saulot;

                                                    Lasombra: deceased. Was a goner even before Gratiano's invasion of the castle, the 4th gen just found out the faint vestiges of a body dissolving into the Abyss and decided that gloating about killing the Ante was better than telling the clan they had been afraid for year from an empty room;

                                                    Tzimisce/Eldest: still game, leaving behind creations from pieces of its body that acquired their own existence. Its true body may be any or none of them, but while originally it sought out Apotheosis Through Metamorphosis, its participation in the Jyhad isn't really a part of this, but just a side diversion that amuses it;

                                                    Cappadocius: not a vampire anymore, dead but doing fine, thank you. As the Jyhad never interested it, current plans and goals reside mainly outside the material plane;

                                                    Set/Suthek: trying to play 5-D chess with everyone, and I mean everyone, even for an Ante it tried to swallow something bigger than its mouth. Simply being "alive" at this point is proof of its cunning, but as its too many allies and foes get and lose land all the time it is hard to say where it stands at any given time. It does a very good job at pretending to be in charge, but it doesn't completely control not even its own cults all the time. If the Jyhad ever ends, it will lose any will to live;

                                                    Haqim: comes and goes from the Jyhad. It despises this game, want nothing to do with it and wisely suggest others to leave, but at the same time it's just too good a player, always seeing opportunities to further its lofty goals through the Jyhad. Do as I say, not as I do, it may achieve to stay away for decades at a time to then come back as an addict;

                                                    Ravnos/Zapathasura: wassail. Simply lost to its own Beast. The thing killed in Bangladesh was barely more than an animal, powerful as it was;

                                                    Augustus Giovanni: so paranoid with Cappadocius' Wraith that it never believed itself to have truly achieved complete Diablerie, but it was actually exactly that. It either died at the hands of the Hecata or pays lip service to the Jyhad while actually delegating decisions to wander the Umbra in search of Cappadocius;

                                                    Salubri/Saulot: still playing the Jyhad, sometimes against itself as it changes plans and has to work towards undoing what it did before. The key difference between it and Set is that Set pretends to have everything under control, Saulot tries to have everything under control.
