            06-09-2026, 03:15 AM
            Originally posted by Michael View Post

            Okay, so what are you basing this on?
            My own impressions based on how it would impact vampires ability to hide amongst mortals. Including making claims like them being gods (sun god in one instance IIRC) when they can't even enter sunlight or stay up during the day without significant effort. All of which grows increasingly difficult after the industrial age and everything becomes much more documented. Nearly everything operates during day outside of specific professions, and even those will sooner or later deal with having to be active during daylight for things like a meeting. A lot of them being important to maintaining a mortal identity, such as various government services that they need to interact with. With some countries being harder or easier depending on their specific circumstances. There's also the fact that any amount of direct sunlight is enough to instantly reveal them, which remains true even if they're one of the few with high enough fortitude to soak the damage in full.

            It takes a lot of work do deal with just how crippling a sunlight weakness as harsh as VtM's is, especially in the modern world. A world infamous for not being very accommodating to people with needs outside of the norm. This gets even worse the more scrutinized your vocation is, such as that of a CEO where you essentially need to be on call 24/7 in the modern world. With everyone paying attention to the things you do.
