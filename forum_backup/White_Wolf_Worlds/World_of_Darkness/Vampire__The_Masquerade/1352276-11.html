    So I've recently been thinking about the late Middle Ages and basically what vampire's backyard, Europe, looked like during the lead-up to the creation of the sects. So, hope people find this interesting as it's something of a fusion of canon and historically inspired headcanon:

    SECT LEAD-UPS

    GENERAL OVERVIEW
    •Due to a period of climate shift, the centuries before the Convention of Thorns were plagued in Europe literally by the Black Plague and famines such as the Great Famine of 1315-1317, not to mention endemic warfare between European powers. This major population bust would have primarily effected humanity and as vampires suddenly found themselves majorly overpopulated, it led to a fierce competition for herds and territories which further drove revolts and endemic warfare, as well as subtly pressuring kindred to centralize in major population centers to survive.

    •This period saw a necessary slacking of feudalism amongst mortals and a slow democratization of ideas. Major rebellions and revolts erupted across Europe and the scarcity of people allowed average men and women to demand more of those above them. This along with the concepts of the Italian Renaissance and the Protestant Reformation stolen fires in the hearts of the Proto-Anarchs as young and desperate kindred chafed under the combination of constant danger and forced loyalty to their elders.

    •The Western Schism of the Catholic Church along with The Papal Schism of constant rivalry and feuding between the Avignon Papacy & Roman Papacy had an incredible effect on people in this period. The battle of popes and anti-popes along with the continued politization and corruption of the so-called 'Universal Church' disillusioned a great many across Europe and the combination of dissatisfaction lead even more to abandon tradition while others realized they must change in order to prevent a complete collapse of order. These realizations within the Church would become the seeds of the Inquisition.

    •This period also saw constant pressures placed upon the region from outside that would cook Europe until it was ready to blow. To the east, the Kievan Rus had been completely shattered by the Golden Horde and Europe recoiled as the Tzimisce Voivodate struggled to hold back the Anda sweeping towards them. Further south, in Spain as well as Greece, the Islamic Caliphates and eventually the Ottoman Empire pushed into Europe and while the Spanish Reconquista pushed the Iberian Caliphate back into North Africa, the Ottomans had great success with sweeping into the Balkans which further strained the Tzimisce as the Ashirra threatened to sweep up into Europe as the Heirs of Rome in Constantinople finally fell and many kindred elders in Europe feared that the Ashirra would sweep up into their domains within Europe.

    •This period of warfare within European Cainites before the Convention of Thorns is called the 'War of Princes' and with domains growing more centralized, youths growing more rebellious, and outside pressures looming and a more scarce herd within Europe, the Cainites began battling fiercely amongst themselves for greater domains and herds and this constant jyhad and its constant bleedover to the mortals of these kindred domains would be one more match upon the rickety powder keg of Europe...

    THE MEDIEVAL FACTIONS
    •Scandinavia (Northern Europe)
    -Primary Clan: Gangrel
    -The lands of Scandinavia remained wild and fierce for a long time and was first explored by the Gangrel, yet as the civilizations of the lowlands pushed up further more and more kindred followed. Scandinavia is now a largely divided land, scattered between traditionalist Scandinavians of the old ways and wilds against the modernized circuit of alliances and factions who were constantly infighting against one another in this wild northern fringe of the War of Princes.

    •Baronies of Avalon (Britain)
    -Primary Clan: Ventrue
    -Alias: Sol Invictus, or The Invictus
    -This confederation of baronies and tributary domains cross the British Isles and are only nominally united under the sheer weight of Mithras' charisma and power to unite them into an almost religious reverence towards Mithras, or Sol Invictus, as the High King of British Cainites.
    -While England and parts of Wales, Scotland, and Anglo-Hibernian Ireland were primarily the homes of the proper baronies which were bound to Mithras' rule; the rest of the baronies proxy domain were largely scattered tributary domains. It remains noteworthy that certain portions of northern Britain were once colonized by formerly Scandinavian Cainites.
    -The Baronies of Avalon once had a foothold in Northern France but in the fierce battles alongside the Hundred Years War, the Courts of Love pushed the Mithrans back into Europe.

    •Courts of Love (France, Western Europe)
    -Primary Clan: Toreador
    -Alliances of Toreador nobility were scattered across France, Burgundy, and the Netherlands and these elders were proud and often open rulers of the night.
    -These rulers often battled internally for domains and fiefdoms to harness more and more power within the fiercely feudal structure of these Courts. This internal strife sometimes lead abandoned or harshly ruled proto-Anarchs to lead peasant and low clan revolts which were harshly crushed.
    -The Courts of Love were not only an internally tumultuous group but an outwardly one as well. They constant skirmished against British Mithrans in Brittany, against the Iberian Houses of Shadow to the south, and the Fiefs of the Black Cross to the east.
    -With the role of Avignon in The Papal Schism alongside the famous life of Joan of Arc, the Courts of Love alongside the Italian Sea of Shadows spent a great deal of time negotiating undeath with fierce faith and the open practices of certain Cainites lead some of the first instances of faithful mortals to rise up against their nocturnal overlords, yet whenever one of these was crushed by the kindred the sense of vampiric superiority endured.

    •Fiefs of the Black Cross (Central Europe)
    -Primary Clan: Ventrue
    -Within the germanic Holy Roman Empire, Hardestalt the Elder struggled to unite numerous fiefs of Cainites into one nominally united confederacy of internally rivaling states. While the battle between Bohemian and Hapsburgian kindred tumulted the Black Fiefs, Hardestalt still battled to govern the whole faction.
    -Hardestalt the Elder and his strong insistence on the importance of the Sixth Tradition, the Silence of the Blood, was a novel concept and did much to ensconce kindred against mortal revolts, such as in the Hussite Revolution, and would become the foundation of the Camarilla's Masquerade.
    -This period also saw the evolution of Clan Ventrue's identity from noble overlords to mercantile barons as certain Ventrue of the Fiefs abandoned knightly orders for the Hanseatic League and chose coin over honor as many Ventrue rebelled against the notion.
    -The Cainites, particularly Ventrue, of the Fiefs of the Black Cross are in near constant conflicts with the Tzimisce Voivodate of the East. While often skirmishing in Hungary, both sides believe only they can hold back the tide of Ashirra pushing through the Balkans alongside the Ottomands.
    -Paganism in the Voivodate also lead the Black Fiefs to harness the Church's power to destabilize the region alongside the purely anarchic force that rose up in rebellion as a minor faction following the Hussites.
    -Fiefs on the fringes of the Black Cross were in near constant external conflicts. Pushing against Scandinavia to the north, the Courts of Love to the West, the Sea of Shadows in Italy to the South, the Tzimisce Voivodate to the East, and even the Ashirra of the Balkans.

    •The Great Patriarchs (Moscow-Russia)
    -Prinary Clans: N/A (Ven, Tore, Bru, Noss)
    -Alias: The Michaelites
    -The Kieran Rus fell beneath the waves of the Anda Dynasty and Golden Horde, but as they swept back, the land struggled to reinvigorate itself as slowly alliances and grudges formed a web throughout the Grand Duchy of Moscow.
    -With the fall of Constantinople, the acolytes of Michael the Patriarch fled Byzantium and many of them pushed out through the Balkans into Russia and the aimless lords of a recently rebuilt land readily accepted them and founded an identity within them: the Heirs of Rome.
    -Considering themselves to be the heirs of Michael the Patriarch's vision, Russian Courts united into a confederacy of great patriarchs as Slavic lord, ancient pagans, and acolytes of Michael created an oddly cosmopolitan blend.
    -To the east the Patriarchs occasionally skirmished with the Voivodate, crusaders from the Fiefs of the Black Cross, or even Ashirran outriders; but they mostly fought amongst themselves for supremacy and the title of 'Patriarch'. Despite this infighting, they did also occasionally skirmish with the Andan Dynasty to the East or various other Dynasties of the Black Courts in Asia.
    -Clan Nosferatu had an abundant presence amongst the patriarchs and that vast desolate land grew a reputation for providing plenty of isolated havens for the masked clan.

    •The Voivodate (Eastern Europe)
    -Primary Clan: Tzimisce
    -Scattered across the eastern mountains and the rural valleys, amongst gothic castles and Slavic tribes the Tzimisce remained insconsed.
    -The Tzimisce had endured the Andan Horde from the East, the Black Fiefs of the East, and they remain steadfast in their surety that they would resist this Ashirran tide following the Ottomans as well.
    -The Voivodate remained in constant conflict with the pockets of Tremere domain that ran along the fringes of their territory and the Black Fiefs, their disdain for the so-called Lords of Germany only growing as they opened their doors to usurpers.
    -The divide between the Old Clan & Main Clan remained strong in these nights as ancient Tzimisce disdained their 'young counterparts' pacts with the Kupala.

    •The Sea of Shadows (Italy)
    -Primary Clan: Lasombra
    -Alias: States of Shadow
    -The states of Italy were numerous and their intrigues provided ample ground for the macchinations of the Shadow clans and their various consorts.
    -The rivalry between the Ventrue and Lasombra continued to stew as the Italian States of Shadow often contested their northern rivals in the Fiefs of the Black Cross.
    -The Lasombra of Italy were tightly intertwined in the Roman Church's hierarchy and corruption was the inevitable byproduct, one that would spread across Europe and drive many to either abandon the faith or raise torches to save it.
    -The Italian States of Shadow were connected to the Iberian Houses of Shadow via the Mediterranean Sea of Shadows, yet tended to consider themselves the superior faction thanks to their lack of compromise with the Iberian Brujah houses or Ashirran defeats, much to the chagrin of Iberian Lasombra.
    -The States of Shadow remained dominant within this region yet as many Lasombra discovered the benefits of mercantile power thanks to the exploits of the Medici, many Lasombra began to form alliances with the Venetian and Sicilian houses of the fledgeling Clan Giovanni, just as the Ventrue had done with the Tremere.

    •The Houses of Shadow (Iberia)
    -Primary Clans: Lasombra, Brujah
    -The Iberian Houses of Shadow are a recently re-emerged faction that had dwelt on the fringes of the al-Andalus caliphate in Iberia before Clan Lasombra managed to wrangle Christian Brujah Idealists into an alliance as they harnessed the ignition of the Reconquista.
    -The Lasombra of Iberia are as old in the region as Mediterranean travel and the Brujah have been there since the days of Carthage and thus for millennia the two were rivals to harness the region. However in the aftermath of the Ashirran conquest of Iberia, the two factions were forced to unite during the Reconquista.
    -Considered to be the less cohesive and more fragmented faction of the Sea of Shadows, these Cainites consider themselves stronger for their use of unity and the Christian Reconquista... an alliance that would (un)fortunately help seed the emergence of the Inquisition from the hotbed of faith & fanaticm in Iberia as well as the many remaining Muslims and Jews from the Ashirra.
    -While primarily skirmishing against the northern Courts of Love and the Ashirra to the south, the Houses of Shadow also consider themselves to be the rivals of the States of Shadow who they consider to be grossly corrupt and arrogant.

    •Ashirra (Ottomans & Islamic Caliphs)
    -Primary Clans: Assamites & Co.
    -Those who consider the Camarilla, Sabbat or Anarchs to be the first sect age wrong. It was the Ashirra. Founded by pilgrims who followed the Prophet Mohammed, Peace Be Upon Him, were united by their faith and spread their faith across North Africa and the Middle East.
    -While considered to be the primary outsider 'antagonist' of European Cainites, the Ashirra have a rich culture and society all their own which the European Cainites only have limited contact with due to their fierce combats and tensions.
    -The Ashirra are currently the prominent sect in the Holy Lands and having witnessed that only certain kindred who heeded the guidance of so-called 'Malkavian Madness Prophets' were capable of enduring the inherent radiance of those lands, were at a loss for why so many European Cainites followed Crusaders in a fervor for the Holy Land.

    Hope y'all enjoy this and it paints a picture of what Europe was like for vampires in the world of darkness and why it becomes the way it does.

        This is a personal nitpick of mine, but I've never liked the sheer number of ancient kindred that White Wolf placed into the New World from the old. In my opinion, moving across countries should be rare enough for creatures that old, much less moving between continents and across a massive and dangerous ocean crossing.

        I usually shift 80-90% of the old world elders and methuselahs back into europe with a few compromises for the most valuable and prominent kindred domains such as New York City, Chicago, Mexico City, Quebec, Los Angeles, etc. Even the canon npcs I keep, I usually back-drift them to the old world and keep new world elders between 250-500 years old as a rule of thumb, with exceptions made for various autarkis indigenous kindred in torpor or ruling from the shadows across the new world.

        This is just something I do because I personally find the number of extremely powerful ST/WW NPCs in the New World to be frustrating due to my preference of the thematic divide between the old world and new world being that the former is ancient, calcified, and conservative while the latter is relatively fresh, turbulent, in flux and frankly just filled with opportunity. It personally fits man of the Gothic-Punk themes of VtM better for me, but regardless, this is just my opinion on the matter. Whatever works for your table.
