            I've been playing around with a celtic ventrue sub faction with links to the anarchs for months. The idea is their opposition to the carmarilla is more built on territorial encroachment than ideological. Anarch ventrue always felt very bland.

            I must admit I'm curious how tremere magic would work once you detached them from the western magic traditions.
            That's the thing, a germanic Ventrue is still a Ventrue. A japanese "Daimyo" Ventrue is still a Ventrue. Just as a muslim Lasombra is still a Lasombra.

            Individuals change based on their cultures; the clan however does not. A muslim Ventrue could be a Vizier, Caliph or Sultan, while a japanese Ventrue could be called a "Daimyo" or "Shogun", and those could be Shintoist lords - but they would be lords still. A japanese Brujah could be a "commoner ninja" that hates the Ventrue Daimyos, but he's a Brujah still.

            Those vampires would have vastly different cultures, but they would find that the blood in their dead veins is the same. A japanese Ventrue Daimyo would consider a venetian Ventrue "Doge" a barbarian "gaijin" regardless, but would at least recognize their common "higher pedigree" than a venetian Brujah rabbel just the same as a ninja Brujah peasant ronim bastard.

            A Lasombra muslim Iman would still think that a Lasombra Bishop is a dirty kafir dog, and the Lasombra Bishop still thinks his Iman counterpart is an infidel heretic, but they still view each other as equals, or at least as slighthly better than some italian/russian Toreador degenerates. And they may even merrily join in hating some gangrel pagans.

            And so it goes with all others. Cultures and individuals change according to place and circumstances, but the overall aspects of each claim remains.

The etruscans were a civilization of a bunch of city states, much like Greece itself, with Rome being just simply one of those, but like the Macedonians ended up conquering the greeks, so too did the romans conquered the remaining etruscans. The etruscans had the - wait for it - "cult of the mages", who were divinator oracles that predicted the future by reading the entrails of animals; that cult survived throughout all of Rome, all the way up to the christianization of Rome, and is the ancestral foundation of the Order of Hermes kabbalism in real life that would form the basis of european middle ages mysticism.

                The etruscan Tremere could then actually be an offset of the Ventrue clan in ancient Italy, who started as a bloodline but eventually divorced itself from the blue bloods - and after that yeah, ok, they eat up Saulot and become a "proper clan".

                        From a Doylist perspective they have always been a clan, and since we're discussing the clans as game elements instead of their specific lore (after all, their regional ties do make sense in their specific lore, that wouldn't make a lot of sense to discuss), this is a strictly Doylist question.

                        The Tremere are a bloodline from the 11th century because they're meant to be a bloodline from the 11th century. Otherwise they could have toppled the Salubri at any previous time or just have been a clan all along. The reason their lore is what it is is to bring them from Ars Magika, period.

                                    For me, the difference between Gangrel and Ravnos is that the Gangrel are much more bestial and are generally loners.                                                 And that sleeping in a new place will start to restore Willpower, just like for the Tzimisce.
