I don't want to have to hunt down the various Expanded Universe products, as cool as they ,might be (I used to be a VTES player among other things), to hunt down the things that are, or are not, declared canon by whoever holds final sway over that (Paradox, maybe?). Keeping up with the metaplot got bad enough when it was only RPG books and fiction books, now I have to parse other stuff too.
                                                            I consider myself a semi-dedicated player/ST to vampire. I did bounce off V5 hard, but in general, I try to keep up. A relative newcomer will have ever more issues navigating the plot mire.

                                                            Them being from Montreal will ensue that Montreal in game seems real to Montreal in our reality, which is a very good thing indeed. I do recall the travesty that was Dark Ages' "history" and "lore", especially in Eastern Europe. However, Montreal By Night does not take place in our reality, it takes place in VTM's reality. So ensuring continuity with that (aka not making the Sabbat an afterthought) comes before ensuring that Habitat 67 or Gay Village are located where they should be.

                                                            That aside, Montreal By Night is my favourite Sabbat book, and I will mourn the loss of the Dark Christianity aspect of the Sabbat.
                                                            It's entirely valid issues. One of the things that depresses me is the fact that it's just not financially viable right now for me to take a trip with the family down to Disneyworld in order to visit Galaxy's Edge. I'm also having trouble keeping up with all the new products for the Star Wars franchise's new canon. I think that in the new age of social media, though, that it's much easier to get the stories and information about things like the plotline of CHAPTERS than it would be in 2004, though.

                                                            I'm also of the mind that CHAPTERS seems to have been made by people who love Montreal by Night and will incorporate all of that lore into the setting. The Sabbat have suffered badly in the city but that doesn't mean their loss of control isn't a valid story of itself.
