    [V20] Clarification about vehicle maneuverability

    10-25-2021, 12:47 AM
    The V20 source book says that the most dice a driver can have in their driving pool is equal to the maneuver rating of the vehicle they are in. As the majority of passenger vehicles have a maneuver rating of 6, what purpose does it serve to have a getaway driver ever have more than one or two dots in Drive? Or for a stunt driver to have the Daredevil merit since the bonus dice will literally never come into play.

      A customized vehicle will have a higher maneuverability. If you want to get more out of your car, tune it accordingly.

      Most getaway drivers won't rely on speed or maneuverability either, focusing on not getting attention to begin with, so depending on the driver, putting many dots on Drive isn't a thing to begin with.

                  I would ignore the rules except in cases where the manouverability of the vehicle is super important.

                  Racing down a highway with large spaces between vehicles? Doesn't matter
                  Racing through a dense pedestrian area and trying to avoid civilians? Hugely important.


                  I would also use the caps creatively.
                  IE, if you have eight dice for driving and shooting, but your vehicle has a six die limit, the player can use six dice for driving and two for shooting or whatever

                  IMO, If you're getting into heated car chases with police, you've already lost (even if you can get away) and vampires are city bound and don't get out much, So these things rarely happen in my game

                      Splitting dice is another huge advantage of having a higher pool than your Maneuverability, that's for sure. I wouldn't ignore it in easier situations because I'm not even sure a roll is necessary on then and the car is the car. But being able to split your pool for complex navigation (let's lose them!), shooting and other stuff, that's a must.

                      Although even that is for a more cinematic game. For anything more grounded MyWifeIsScary is right, if you're being chased you already lost. Nowadays you can even confirm with books and YouTube videos from real ex-criminals about the matter, the whole point of a getaway is not being chased to begin with. And a stunt driver wouldn't use a common car right out of the factory.
