    Changing the Dhampir

    04-08-2017, 06:17 PM
    One of the oddities of cWoD is the idea that Dhampir are a new thing when they have existed in Balkan folklore for centuries. I think that it would be interesting to have any sufficiently human female Kindred (Humanity 8+) to be able to bear Dhampir children without being 15th generation (exposure to Vitae, not genetics, should matter, so the father would be inconsequential). I think that it would give the Kindred of the Camarilla a reason to maintain their Humanity and be the reason why the Sabbat created the Revenants.

    The generation of the mother should be important to the Dhampir because they are constantly exposed to her blood for nine months. When they are killed by lethal damage, Dhampir would get a Stamina roll (difficulty mother's generation minus 10) to effectively be Embraced by their mother's blood. Other than that, they would gain other benefits from a low generation mother. Would you want to see a change in the Dhampir?

                                                  I did a write up on this a loooooong time ago (it's pretty clear from the tone of the writing that I was like 19) - it's still up on the salvaged BJ Zanzibar's WOD mirror site (it's there on the front page). I believe 9 Humanity was required to father/mother a dhampir in that treatment, but I've long since decided (like you) that 8 Humanity is a better threshold.

                                                  The key notes:
                                                  • It's easier for male vampires to sire offspring. They only had to spend blood to mimic life functions for one scene to make their contribution.
                                                  • Female vampires COULD have children if their humanity was high enough, but it was a difficult process. They had to maintain their high humanity for the duration of the pregnancy and also expend one more blood each night to awaken (failing to do so, or running out of blood resulted in the vampire's body rejecting the fetus).
                                                  • It was therefore far more likely that a dhampir would be born from a human mother. The birth was likely to be incredibly taxing and difficult, so before the advent of modern medicine, mortality rates for a mortal mother during chidlbirth were very high.
                                                  • Because I allowed dhampir to be born from lower generation vampires, they each had an "effective generation" - the same as if they had been aired by their vampiric parent. So a 9th Generation vampire would produce a dhampir child with ab effective generation of 10th. This increased the dhampir's blood pool and allowed them to learn higher level disciplines than ghouls (possibly). It also made them immune to dominate from vampires of a higher generation than their effective gen.
                                                  • Dhampirs also had a "Bloodright" discipline that tried to mimic folkloric abilities like seeing invisible vampires or killing vampires with conventional weapons.

                                                  I'm currently working on an updated, unofficial supplement for Dhampirs that updates some of these ideas but in a more "V20" and less "amateur hour" way. The rules aim to:
                                                  • Use the existing Dhampir mechanics as a jumping off point, but I expand on them using re-purposed "Special Advantages" rules from Ascension's right hand / bygone bestiary to give them the potential to "unlock" more vampiric traits. These traits are like special merits to help differentiate Dhampirs from Revenants or Ghouls.
                                                  • Detail the different ways Dhampirs might interact with Kindred society (so far only the Camarilla but I plan to talk about the Anarchs and Independents more, the Sabbat view Dhampirs as sacrilege - a debasement of Caine's blood) - these are similar to the Ghoul splats like Independant, Vassal and Revenant, but instead detail dhampirs who try (or have succeeded at) acclimating to Camarilla society, those who hunt vampires like the Dhampir of legend, and even those who try to pass themselves off as vampires. So far 3 of the 5 basic "splats" have write-ups.
                                                  • Discuss the Dhampirs of different clans (still in the early stages)
                                                  • Offer options for running everything from Time of Thin Blood-style "Dhampirs are a modern phenomenon" to a "what if" scenario where dhampir have been a part of vampiric society for a very long time (presenting them as the Camarilla's answer to Revenant families). The section for this isn't written yet, but the goal is to take the Gehenna-style "sliding scale" approach, while the default assumption is "Dhampir *appear* to be a modern phenomenon but they may have existed in other times (a la V20 Dark Ages).
                                                  Still a lot of work needed on this. The next section I plan on working on is re-purposing my old write-up to detail how lower generation vampires can produce Dhampir children - I definitely think I'll lower the Humanity threshold to 8, and specify that only those on Humanity can sire children in the mortal way.

                                                      I particularly liked the part on male pregnancies:

                                                      with mortals. Some assist the user, and some allow the
                                                      user to curse another so he’ll unknowingly impregnate
                                                      the next mortal with whom he has congress. Wait, you
                                                      say, surely you mean that he’ll impregnate the next
                                                      mortal woman with whom he has congress, yes? Ha ha.
                                                      No. Occult pregnancy is by its very nature a perversion
                                                      of the essential human procreation—a piratical invasion
                                                      of something sacred and primal. Distinctions in what is
                                                      and is not possible have already left the building, and
                                                      that leaves us with female vampires impregnating women
                                                      and male vampires impregnating men (with variations on
                                                      these themes). While the woman carrying the child of
                                                      her lesbian vampire lover would certainly be surprised at
                                                      the development, she at least is biologically (and socially)
                                                      equipped to contend with the life growing within her, the
                                                      swell of belly and breast, the hormonal swings. She has
                                                      the physical means for the baby to be born, as well.

                                                      Pity her poor clueless male counterpart.

                                                      Carrying pregnancy and giving birth is not something
                                                      human males are biologically prepared for, nor socially con-

                                                      pregnancy seem like a fading nightmare. Surgery can save the
