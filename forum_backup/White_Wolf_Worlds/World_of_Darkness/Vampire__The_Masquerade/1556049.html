    More info on Precolumbian Kindred

    12-01-2025, 04:44 AM
    in the writeup for the Tlacique on page 64 of Lore of the Clans, mention is made of
    vampires [in the New World] already: Nosferatu, Gangrel, and a bloodline calling itself the Tlacique.

    my assumption is that the former two are in reference to the Manitou mentioned across pages 24-25 of Revised Edition's Clanbook: Nosferatu and Mictlantecuhtli's (perhaps by extension the Slumbering Giant's) bloodline from Diablerie: Mexico, respectively. while the Tlacique have a handful of writeups to their name, these other two seem more marginal; have either been detailed anywhere outside of the aforementioned sources? are there any other (non-Drowned Legacy) bloodlines from the precolumbian Americas im missing?

          None, this was essentially an abandoned thread.

          Even the Tlacique may actually be a Drowned Legacy, as do the other Kindred that were already there given that DLs may have the ability to pass as Kindred.

          In canon Masquerade Kindred are definitely a localized phenomenon despite their apparent success. If any reached the Americas before Columbus, no evidence of their passage was really found beyond a few individuals that may not actually be what they seem to be. Vampires outside the Middle East is a far newer phenomenon than previously thought by Europeans.

              The Baali
              They are always a safe bet, Nergal/Huitzilopochtli landed before Columbus and built a cozy little empire for himself and his brood
              Tezcatlipoca... was something, either a Lasombra or either Sutekh's kid, still up to the air

              The Psianob are either from a Cappadocian survivor or a DL of their own right
              Menele came over at one point prior to the major European settlers to hang out with the natives
              V5 Chicago by Night introduces us to Nerissa Blackwater, a 4th generation Caitiff. She's been hunting in the Lake Michigan area since 4000 BC
              The mostly-torpid Tzimisce antediluvian was brought over to what would become New York by the Szantovich Revenants shortly after Lugoj's botched diablerie. That would have been at or a little after 1413...

              And of course the Phoenicians reached it probably too (this one supported by why Menele knew where to go)
