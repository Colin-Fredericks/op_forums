    Storytellers Vault is opening to Vampire: The Masquerade 5th Edition

    10-02-2021, 12:58 AM
    From the site;
    Vampire: The Masquerade 5th Edition is coming to Storytellers Vault!

    This month, Storytellers Vault is opening to Vampire: The Masquerade 5th Edition (V5) - which means that you can publish your V5 materials starting from October 15th.
    This download includes all of the Word and Indesign files, fonts, and graphics to create products in the style of Vampire: The Masquerade 5th Edition for the Storytellers Vault community content program.
    For more information on creating and publishing your own Vampire: The Masquerade 5th Edition supplements, be sure to review the Content Guidelines and FAQ.
    https://support.storytellersvault.co...ontent-Program
    So, I think my next RPG project will be making a V5 version of Hunger from Beyond and Inn Between Worlds.
