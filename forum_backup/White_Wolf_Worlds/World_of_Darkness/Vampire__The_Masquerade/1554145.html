                                      Originally posted by Lysander View Post
                                      If there's a character that I don't really like outside of Uri Shugi is his fellow clan mate Fatima who rebels against him because his edict of only worshiping Haqim and no one else. I can understand that her devotion to her mortal faith is laudable, but you didn't have a problem of putting fellow kindred who believe in the same to final death before hand.
                                      Fatima is something of a victim of the moving goal posts of the Assamite/Banu Haqim clan in that she was meant to be a progressive character for a very reactionary group that ended up with her being now extremely unprogressive for a much less reactionary clan. Originally, she was the first female Embraced for the Assamites when they were in full "crazy diablerist murder cult" mode. Which was a bit of misogyny for vampires who have always been way more egalitarian than regular humanity (because if a 4th generation vampiress tells you to eat your eyeballs, you will do it).

                                      Then the Assamites became the three castes and suddenly only the Warrior Castes were a bunch of crazy murder nutters. Fatima suddenly is just a little less special as the wizards and Judges had plenty of female members. Even then, the conflict between Islam (certainly better than the Path of Blood) and the Path of Blood happened. It's just Fatima was portrayed as a holy warrior who was religiously faithful to both the Path of Blood and Islam. Ur-Shulgi decides you can either believe in God or Haqim but not both but Fatima tries to thread the needle.

                                      Which results in the Clan Novels ending with Fatma being spared by Haqim.

                                      Two problems:

                                      1. The good people at OPP note that the whole "Islam vs. Blood Gods" thing is still pretty orientalist and make the split much more secular and for a variety of reasons. The Banu Haqim are now up against the regular clan versus the Web of Knives which means that Fatima is the primary female member of a psychotic murder blood cult in the regular clan versus the mainstream clan. Which now makes her an outlier in an outlier.
                                      2. Fatima's desire to serve both God and Haqim is a moving meaningful struggle to reconcile faith with cause but is undermined by the fact that Haqim's blood cult is a murder religion against pretty much every religious tenant of Islam. So its unintentionally ridiculous. A bit like me trying to reconcile my belief in Jesus with being a practicing Sith Lord. It makes her a massive hypocrite and religious heretic akin to the Giovanni version of Catholicism. Ur shulgi isn't right about many things but trying to thread the needle here is just insane.

                                                      I'm going to make a controversial pick: Post-Lasombra Trilogy Lucita.

                                                      Lucita is more or less the iconic Vampire: The Masquerade character even if she was only introduced at the beginning of Revised. She has five novels, a comic book series, and has been adapted to the LA by Night stream by the lovely Cynthia Marie. There's many modules and fan content that stars her like Lucita's Six. I love Lucita and so do many other fans as the perfect embodiment of the dark superhero world of White Wolf.

                                                      However, the decision to have her join the Sabbat was one of the dumbest decisions in all of gaming. Essentially, I can't help but think that someone up high thought that the Lasombra Signature character being a Antitribu was a bad idea. So it fell to the authors to justify it in universe.
                                                      And now we're stuck with it.

                                                      Worse, it becomes even more ridiculous with the fact that Lucita is now the outlier. She defects to the Sabbat because she feels that the Camarilla is ignoring the threat of Gehenna as well as a spiritual void in her soul.

                                                      And in 5th Edition:

                                                      1. The Sabbat are now mindless blood horrors with no real spiritualism.
                                                      2. The Camarilla now believe in the Antediluvians.
                                                      3. The Lasombrra have defected because they understand the Gehenna Wars are full of morons.
                                                      4. White Wolf's owners know the Lasombra are awesome and everyone loves them in the LARPs as well as games like Shadows of New York.

                                                      So Lucita is either dead at Talley's hand or head of a bunch of cultists in Madrid doing nothing.

                                                      Oof.

                                                      I'm not even sure her defection even makes sense anymore since the primary reason for her spiritual collapse was Anatole dying (combined with killing Moncada) and he's supposedly alive again.
