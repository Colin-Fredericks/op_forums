                      It depends on the technology and entertainment available to the time period.

                                          A lot depends on just how late it is, and what the weather is like.

                                          Best-case scenario, it is an hour or two after sunset in the winter in a mild climate. So, say around 8pm or so in rural Florida? (I used to live here.)
                                          • Most towns have a movie theater. Keep it light, though. No subtitles. Nobody wants to be alone at night after watching Czech nuwave arthouse cinema.
                                          • Hit a local bar. Play some pool. Watch some tv. There might even be music/dancing, or a big game on.
                                          • Most retail is open until 9 or 10. Drug stores, groceries, bookstores, diners, thrift shops, etc. Not the opera, but passes the time.
                                          • Fast food... especially the parking lots... can be a real social scene. Convenience stores, too.
                                          • Church events might be good. Concerts, speakers, evening services, etc. It's sociable, if you can pass for a normal. Be careful of True Faith.
                                          • If bat form is an option, sneak into a house, and watch the family. Or, just hang and watch whatever they're watching on tv.
                                          • Again bat form (or if you're just good at being ignored), and hang out at a medical center or police station. Lots of workplace dramas to watch.
                                          • Find a house... or a clearing in the woods... with a party and crash it.
                                          • Find water and go swimming. You're beyond mosquitos and drowning, so, yay.
                                          • High school sports! OMG, in some places a well-timed football game can be a Very Big Deal.
                                          • Go bowling. Seriously, on a quiet night you can milk the lane for hours.
                                          • Show up at a chain restaurant in uniform and begin working. If it's busy, it's possible they'll be so relieved no one will ask questions. Don't forget to split your tips with the kitchen.
                                          • Wander through backyards until you find a dog you can chat with.
                                          • If you get really lonely hit some 12-step meetings. They welcome pale strangers, and won't be offended if you refuse the donuts.

                                          Mediocre-case scenario, it is late at night, like after 10 or 11 pm. Again, a mild climate helps.
                                          • Movies might have a late-show on the weekend. Pick something deep and meaningful. Try to get somebody to discuss it with you at length in the parking lot.
                                          • RHPS!
                                          • Bars become more boisterous on a weekend, more depressing on a weeknight.
                                          • Walmart is usually 24 hours. Everyone looks like a vampire at 3am under fluorescent lights, so you're probably safe.
                                          • Break into a store and browse. Nightvision makes a bookstore your personal library.
                                          • Find a sex-worker and hire them for talking. This is weirdly common. Possibly, the saddest thing ever, but common.
                                          • Cruise alleys looking for a kid sneaking out of the house. Follow them. No one sneaks out to do something boring.
                                          • Get a cheap (or, expensive, I don't mean to be "clannist") motel room. Hot shower. Cable tv. Internet. Not a bad way to spend a few hours.
                                          • Cow tipping. (Ask first, just to be polite.)
                                          • Diners are often 24 hours. If you tip well, the server won't care if you don't actually eat. A twenty for an untouched cup of coffee, and a few hours of chit-chat sounds about right. Avoid busy times, or at least tip much better.
                                          • Walk. Run. Walk. Run. Repeat.
                                          • Plan how you will become the Prince of Miami and pass all sorts of crazy rules. "No hunting except on salt water boats!"
                                          • Sit on a park bench and read. You have night vision; use it.
                                          • Speaking of night vision... know what daysiders can't do? See sweeping mountain vistas and charming landscapes at night. You can. Go on a leaf-peeping tour of New England at Midnight. So much less traffic at 1 am!
                                          • Find a stray dog and, using Animalism, teach them truly amazing tricks. Like how to dance the tango, or sing a Liza Minelli song. Drop it off at the animal shelter to be instantly adopted. (Now, can I spend xp on Humanity?)

                                          Worst-case scenario. It's 3am. In western Nebraska. In a snowstorm.
                                          • Hitchhike. People are much more likely to pick you up in a snowstorm. Tell them your life story. Your elaborately fictional life-story.
                                          • Play Santa. Break into the Dollar Store and steal a trashbag full of toys. Deliver them to random porches.
                                          • Turn on Obfuscate and "water-ski" off the back of a snowplow.
                                          • Steal a ski-doo! Run it on streets!
                                          • Turn into a wolf and mount a dog doing its business in a backyard. When the owner looks outside and freaks, stare at the human, smile, and keep going.
                                          • Find somebody walking five miles to their low-wage job. They're obviously a survivor type. Embrace them. Spend five minutes explaining things, then walk away.
                                          • Steal a car and get into a minor fender-bender. Dealing with the other driver and police should take an hour or two. Is there no end to your boredom?
                                          • Shovel the snow from all the driveways in the neighborhood. If anyone confronts you, tell them you're staying with "the Millers down the street". There are always Millers down the street.
                                          • Walk into a hospital emergency room and tell them you're not feeling well. Let them examine you. Do not use blush of life. Zany hijinx surely ensue.
                                          • Stand in the main intersection and scream, "I am the uncontested vampire Prince of this metropolis! None may defy me!" Bonus points if there is no traffic light.
                                          • Build seven snowmen in the town park. One for each clan.
                                          • For God's sake, just get a hotel room like a normal person. It's warm, quiet, and comfortable. Read a book. Watch a little tv. Maybe wander wikipedia.
