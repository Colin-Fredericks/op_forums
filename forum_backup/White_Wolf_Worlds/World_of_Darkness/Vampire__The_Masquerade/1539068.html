              If I had to rate the clans on the subject of scale when it comes to danger, I would say that following are my top picks (V20).

              1. Giovanni
              Clan founder is awake and actively directing the clan, they have Dominate and Potence which covers power, and they are one of the richest clans. They are not bound by faction issues and they use Necromancy which clans who lack that power can't fight. But then there is the big plan to bring down the barrier between the living world and the lands of the dead, which would see most of the world perish.

              2. Baali
              They lack the centralized command structure of other clans, but technically speaking they do have the ability to unleash greater demons on the planet baked into their clan discipline, so no need to learn magic or setup networks. One Baali just level grinds the XP and diablerizes their way to the right generation and the world dies.

              3. Tremere
              I could go on about the Pyramid clan structure, magically secure havens, dominate and auspex, and the fact they are universally hated and considered too useful to kill, but let's be real here. The Path of Conjuring, at level 2 allows you to make 55 gallon drums of the scariest chemical weapons known to man. Thus every tremere who learns two dots in the path is technically a WMD with a reload function. At level 3 you can make complex devices like modern cell phones or 1950s nukes.

              If you have level 5 Path of Conjuring and level 5 Dominate, you can make a fake living body, use dominate to directly control it, and use it to fight in daylight or do suicide missions where you jump out just before death. Basically PoC5 + D5 basically makes the combat from Altered Carbon a reality for your character. Even if you kill the Tremere, you are never sure if you killed them for good.

                              My top 3:

                              1- Dominate Malkavian: a clan whose individual members are well rounded dangers on every aspect. Obfuscate and the overall opinion on the clan means you'll usually get away with almost anything you do. And what you can do is to find each and every secret you want about anyone and anything, plus mess with their minds with extreme finesse.

                              Since anyone can develop Physical Disciplines without a teacher or someone else's blood, you can develop this extra set of tools as you wish.

                              2- Tremere/Giovanni: pure access to power. Any clan with access to some sorcery, Necromancy, whatever, has a hell of a tool at their disposal. Individuals have a versatile array of options to either excell at one form of danger or cover a lot of different approaches. The clan as a whole has all the variety and power as agents.

                              Tremere and Giovanni both take the lead by having the biggest arsenal of their respective arts and being extremely organized as a whole to use their assets to maximum effect.

                              3- Nosferatu: as a clan, they're the most organized information brokers around, and the most disciplined in using their arsenal to maximum effect, while their own secrets are made particularly hard to find as they literally bury themselves and their havens beyond the reach of most others.

                              Simply there's no clan effectively more powerful. They're the power behind every throne and no one can have the luxury of being their enemy.

                                  Which clan is the most "dangerous" is nebulous in my opinion, as there are many ways that can be interpreted. All clans are extremely dangerous in their own ways, and all clans have their own weaknesses and limitations. So I am going to respond with which clan I would least want to have as the Clan Enmity flaw for my PC (regardless of what kind of PC it is). (I am also writing from a pre-V5 perspective since I don't follow that.)

                                  1) Tremere. They have access to a lot of different powers and are very organized. It'll be hard for any individual character to withstand that in a city.

                                  2) Malkavians. Less because they are actually all that powerful, but because who knows how each one may attempt to screw with you. Hard to defend against things you'll never guess that are coming.

                                  3) Ventrue. Hard to settle on the third option as there are many good candidates. Ultimately I decided on Ventrue because they are also extremely well organized, are masters of Dominate and Presence, and have a lot of influence and control in both mortal and vampire societies. In most domains they are going to make things very hard for you.
