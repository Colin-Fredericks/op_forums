                                As I said, I think calling the Tzimisce bad leaders isn't actually true. But they do have a different style of leadership than the Ventrue. A local ruler isn't less of a ruler just because they want to keep local, and the Tzimisce still keep a strong network among themselves. Their rules on hospitality specially guarantee that, despite their propensity to isolation, they still get news on the world beyond their land and communication with other lords.

                                They are also as good rulers over anyone on their domain as any vampire can be, on a broad sense, because for such old lords the people is a part of the land. They aren't equals, maybe not seem as "people", but they need to be cared for, even if sometimes culled to not grow too much in numbers, because they're as much of a part of your possessions as anything else, and the Tzimisce take care of their possessions in their own way.

                                I still think that Dominate isn't a good addition to their style of leadership. Their more spiritual side still calls for another kind of Discipline. It is important to remember that they aren't just owner of their lands, but a part of it. Their Disciplines are meant to represent their connection. Auspex let them be constantly in tune with their lands and surroundings, Animalism makes them not only command every denizen of the land, but also hear them, and through them get news of what the very land feels.

                                Viscissitude is the only Discipline that really makes sense to be changed, although I see no need for it. But if it was, it should be by Koldunic Sorcery, or at least Thaumaturgy.
