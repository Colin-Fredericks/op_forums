Brujah being fiery and passionate, Toreador being artistic and seductive, Ventrue being proud and authoritative, est. In that respect, clan affiliated traits serves to highlight those characteristics and provide a stronger connection with whoever / whatever is responsible for their creation.

                        It's not telling you that the other clans can't explore different roles outside of their stereotype. It's just pointing out that falling into that stereotype is much easier, due to having natural advantages that come with doing so.


                        If you present the idea that any clan is able to excel at any field they desire, then it makes the whole premise of their distinction pointless. You may as well take a page from V5 Sabbat and treat everybody as Caitiff.

                                    The very idea that Brujah will be better warriors is a gross misrepresentation of what Clan Traits can be. At most the Brujah would excell at once particular aspect of combat, if at all, as the point of those traits is to highlight Clan Identity, not lock up roles.

                                    Let's look at the Ministry. Their Clan Traits are basically Legacy Serpentis, one of the most reviled yet loved Disciplines in VtM's history. A power set that always felt weird, suboptimal, ripped off from Protean for the most part, with zero synergy to anything else in the Clan. But nothing was more evocative, immediately iconic of a Follower of Set, than snake traits.

                                    Now they're all there without commanding space among Discipline powers, accessible at a fair price for the relative low reward, because no one really takes them thinking they'll be a game changing addition to your supernatural arsenal.

                                    And better than V5, it's properly bound to Clan rather than translated as a fucking Amalgam.

                                    What's iconic about the Brujah? Rousing the masses of downtrodden in anger, cowering the powerful through messages and actions rather than being in same room, clandestine violence that can't be properly armed, spotting the dynamics of power in a system and being passionate about it. Maybe even a rare control of Celerity that leans more in the area of having time to think, slowing the world around you, and other feats of hyper attention and reaction that look less like being fast and more like, you know, bending time.

                                    I personally think the playtest did a poor job with the Brujah. But that's a ridiculous reason to judge the whole concept of Clan Traits. We don't even know if they'll really be unique, or just harder to transmit. But the concept is good and has a lot of potential to re add iconic traits and powers that never really fit the Discipline structure that much.

                                    Which includes virtually every Bloodline Discipline and some Main Clan Disciplines.
