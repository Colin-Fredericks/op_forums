                            The official writer can say shit like that, but if 1+1 doesn't equal 2 then they're lying out of their arse.
                            I admit, it's because I'm a huge Lasombra fanboy.

                            As much as the Tzimisce? No.

                            As much as the Malkavians? No.

                            But the Lasombra are up there and I tend to view them with the lens of being the biggest bestest most ruthless Sith Lords out there. As such, I might be biased toward viewing anything they do as automatically succeeding and being a political masterstroke. Even so, I feel like the Lasombra as written are people that have managed to ride a very terrible series of choices and emerge on top of a difficult situation. I perhaps am also taking the view that I think some of the side-eye they're getting is because people who dislike the way the Sabbat were treated in V5 are trying to frame the Lasombra defection with the most contempt/scorn as possible.

                            They don't ike the development and thus it is influencing whether they think it was a smart move or not in-universe. That is entirely fair because if your table has the Lasombra being part of the Sabbat as the bestest thing ever for them then it will never be a round peg in a round hole to say that leaving them was a good idea.

                            Nevertheless, I am influenced by the following factoids:

                            1. The Lasombra got out before the Sabbat went to shit.

                            In-universe, the Sabbat have become even more radicalized and ready to give themselves entirely up to the Gehenna Crusade as well as suicidal self-sacrifice. The Lasombra look smarter for being the one people not to drink the Kool-Aid.

                            2. The Lasombra were the ones who made the Treaty of Chicago's provisions

                            Kevin Jackson is a new Prince, an Ancilla and it is very clear from the beginning that Makarov (wait, no that's the villain from Call of Duty) was sent there to be executed from the beginning. It was a deal they chose to make and manipulated Jackson into following up on. Their reasoning? Purging their Clan and eliminating Sabbat Elders is a way to remove their enemies and get paid for it.

                            3. Yes, the Lasombra have taken a hit but so everyone else

                            If the Lasombra were the only people to do a purge of their Sabbat loyal members (plus other Sabbat Elders of the Tzimisce and Antitribu). yes, it would be devastating to them. However, the rest of Kindred society has also been beaten badly. The Second Inquisition and Beckoning is supposed to have inflicted massive casualties on everyone.

                            4. The Lasombra already had a decent-sized Camarilla presence

                            The Antitribu were only a Bloodline in the Camarilla but that doesn't mean they've not had any influence or power (Montano, the Prince of Venice, and so on). The Sabbat defectees are joining another group that makes them large enough to be the "main" Clan.

                            5. The Camarilla needs the Lasombra

                            The Camarilla fucked up by exiling the Brujah and losing the Gangrel so them recruiting the Banu Haqim into their ranks/the entirety of the Ashirra and now the Lasombra is a good way of making up their losses. The Camarilla for all the talk of it being in a bad place is now back up to 7 clans and controls much of the Middle East since it's merger.

                            6. There's a lot of open real estate lately

                            The Sabbat becoming a mobile army and the losses of so many Kindred means there's a lot of spaces for surving Lasombra to move up in the Camarilla. Princedoms, Seneschals, Scourges, and Sheriffs. They're not going to be the dregs of Camarilla society because a Lasombra who goes to Detroit and sets up a new Princedom is going to be the top dog there and undoubtedly knows plenty of people already.

                            7. The Lasombra know their Antediluvian is alive now

                            As per BJD, they have their reasons for defecting to the side of the Antediluvians.

                            Basically, the fact the Lasombra are able to escape the Sabbat's sinking ship with all of its treasure makes them look badass to my mind.

Besides, imagine how pissed the Sabbat will be with the Lasombra traitors! And since very few will lend a hand to the Shadow Clan, they will also have to look after themselves... let's see their Darwinism take them from between a rock and this hard place.
