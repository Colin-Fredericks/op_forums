Blood Potency with Generation..? WTF!), curbing and absorbing Disciplines into eachother, capping Disciplines to 5 only, etcetera... VTM and VtR are separate gamelines that are supposed to cater to different audiences.

                I literally acknowledged that VtR inspired multiple mechanics in V5, but I think ascribing insidious motives to the decision is nonsensical. The thing is that touchstones, blood potency, and the five dot limit all work fine in VtM. I would argue VtM needed such a mechanic from the beginning.

                I don't think generation would fit in VtR at all, as it's built around no known origin point for vampires, whether per clan or as a whole.

                I don't have any issue with adding new discipline powers to existing disciplines to accommodate Chimerstry, Vicissitude, etc.

                I really have no issue with limiting disciplines to five dots.

                I like touchstones, but I'd like them to have more carrot to them than they currently do. Right now they're all stick.

                I don't think "this is supposed to be this way" is necessarily a convincing argument. V5 wasn't even really intended to appeal to long-term VtM fans, it was meant to bring in new fans. Now, whether that was successful or not is another matter and I have no data on it, but I think all the things you listed are actually improvements over older editions of VtM.

                If I were this annoyed about V5 I'd stick to V20... which, actually, I did until I learned that Justin Achilli was running things. Except for a couple discussions about SI and concern about "You are what you eat" coming out badly, which it did not.
