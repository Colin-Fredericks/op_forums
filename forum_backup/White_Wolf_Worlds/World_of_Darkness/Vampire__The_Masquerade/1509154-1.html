So has there been changes to what "thin-blooded" means both mechanically and lorewise? Yes it has and I'm not saying it hasn't but, those mechanical changes have no text saying they are blood thickening while the text I have provided above directly supports my statements.​

                                                            'Caitiff' is a term that actually has two different functions, one mechanical and the other social.

                                                            There's no reason that Blood Walk or a similar ability shouldn't work on someone who is a Caitiff purely in the social sense. Which is to say that they are technically a member of a Clan in a mechanical sense, having in-Clan Disciplines and a Clan weakness, but do not know their Clan or its traditions because their Sire was unwilling or unable to pass along the knowledge.

                                                            How Blood Walk would work with someone like a "true" Caitiff or a Pander is an interesting idea to explore, and I should probably go and re-read the description of that as well as the Pander ritual to get an idea of how such a thing would work. But I do think there's an argument for the idea that while a Caitiff or Pander may no longer have a metaphysical bond to their Clan/Antediluvian, you can still track their vampiric lineage, as it were, using Blood Magic (I'll call it what I want, fight me ).



                                                            Regarding the question of how Caitiff are such a thing when fairly basic-level Blood Sorcery can resolve the questions of lineage and stuff, I figure there are a few pragmatic answers. The first being, who cares? It kind of doesn't matter if a magical solution to the problem exists when no one cares to apply it consistently or systemically. Certain cities might put a policy into place; others might just "solve" the problem with a Scourge.

                                                            Meaning that using Blood Walk to resolve an issue of lineage is largely a personal problem for the Caitiff in question. Taking action to resolve said problem then involves a few necessary steps:

                                                            1) Finding out the solution exists. An implicit part of the problem for "social" Caitiff is that no one bothered to teach them this stuff in the first place, so they aren't necessarily going to be aware that there is an arcane solution to their problem.

                                                            2) Finding someone with the necessary abilities. While the Path of Blood is pretty common, Blood Walk is a separate ritual. And while practitioners of Blood Magic aren't *too* rare, they aren't super common either, so there's a limited pool of potential helpers.

                                                            3) Trusting a freaking Blood Sorcerer with your Vitae! This applies especially to the Tremere, who are pretty well-known for using the "undesirables" of Kindred society as guinea pigs, but I'd say it holds up in general. Vampires are assholes, and Blood Mages are no exception. My mind always goes to the conversation in BJD where Vykos is grateful that their discarded bits are being used as a trophy rather than winding up in the hands of the Tremere. Just because you give a Tremere a sample of your Vitae for the purposes of checking your lineage, that doesn't mean they'll *only* use it for that, or even that they will honour the agreement in the first place.

                                                            So getting the answers can be difficult, and even dangerous, for the Caitiff in question. But even if they did get the answer, what good would it do them? A lot of Kindred don't really care that much about their Clan as a whole, so being able to tell someone that you're from the same Clan isn't going to guarantee much. It doesn't change the fact that the Caitiff doesn't really know anything, and teaching them is a *burden* that their own Sire already opted out of, so why would someone else bother?

                                                            Even in the cases of high-profile and powerful Caitiff, like Mukhtar Bey, if they do have a lineage/Clan to discover, it never helped them before, so why bother trying to figure it out now? A Caitiff that reaches that level has already managed to climb the ladder without the benefits of a known Clan, so why make a big deal out of it now? That doesn't even go into the possibility of taking pride in Caitiff status, as the Panders do.
