                          Originally posted by Garygeneric View Post

"Pimp whoremonger" lacks the same empathetic oomph as a "lonely widower," which was Louis' delicious appeal to begin with. Also, Lestat's favorite past time of dragging Louis (and later Claudia) through high society so they can feed on the upper crust will be historically problematic.

                          I guess I just feel that if they have to completely change the characters and story, why not simply tell a different story?
                          I'm guessing they started with the idea that they absolutely had to make one of the leads black. Which then created a problem because you can't have a black slave plantation owner be a protagonist. While a few free men of color did own slaves back in the day, having one be the protagonist simply isn't going to fly. The company would be buried under the protests. So they then likely decided that they needed to give their Louis a bit of that plantation owner vibe without actually making him a plantation owner. So they settled on brothel owner as a kind of clumsy workaround.

                          It all has the feel of something a committee of executives would do. You can see the chain of clumsy thoughts as they sit around a table and try to workaround one thing, it leads to problems elsewhere, they come up with a clumsy workaround for that, and on and on until they've got something so far removed from the original material that they might just as well have made up something out of whole cloth.
