    All that said I don't think modern 'witches' will be well suited to unlife as warlocks in anything resembling an efficient and pragmatic magical organization that can reasonably politic with vampires. I had a short rant with some really out-there analogues, but it's all-too easy to get nasty. Point being, Thaumaturges, no matter their style (Hermetic, Heaven and earth, Settite clerical high magic, Punk magic) Need exceptional intelligence, Willpower, a well rounded belief system, and a strong commitment to goals. If you yourself are a Tremere, You are unlikely to find all of these qualities among Idealists who aspire to be Hollywood witches, people embracing a fad, or folks needing a support group. These people make for great Herd dots, but rarely vampires, and even rarer thaumaturges. Even when you do find such people, you are not likely to find many, it's probable that they don't play well with others, and in any case it's highly unlikely that you'll be able to make an organization that can rival the Real Tremere.

    Thaumaturgy is hard and requires sacrifice. It's not meant to be for everyone. Would you be happy if necromancers could distance themselves from the dead and still practice their arts?

    Now, the interesting thing, is that I kinda like the Idea of House Carna.
    But that's working under the assumption that this harmful new-wave feminist stereotype they've got going on is really just a facade to distance themselves from the Tremere, and really they're Hermetic Tremere Group B in secret and perhaps the whole plan is to use different Tremere brands to get a chantry into every single city, especially with cities that have historically been hostile to the Tremere brand.

                                    Are you genuinely confused? Or do you know exactly what I mean and have decided you don't like my precise choice of words?

                                    Tzmisce, especially Kolduns, are infamously conservative. Their magic is as traditional as it gets, and their sire-child relationships are strict. Above all, be they shapers or wizards, Tzmisce value intelligence.
                                    They're not going to take in soothsayers, wiccans, people who practice 'feminine magics' or who like the idea of 'freedom' as you imagine it. People like that don't make good Tzmisce, nor Tremere, nor do they make good ashipu, or priests. Shit I doubt Bahari of all people want the kind of people you seem so desperate to include. These people might pop up as independent thaumaturges, but they're not going to be all that good. Remember; there's a little rule in V20 at least that suggests you need at least 3 dots of Occult if you're going to actually use magic that hasn't been drilled into you by a tutor and magic isn't an inate part of your blood. Do you actually think these people are going to have those three dots of Occult? I mean, three dots of occult in relation to magic, not into UFOs. I'm sure you can strike gold and find the right person from one of these groups, but how likely is that, and can you fill a chantry with them?

                                    Look, I get your desire, your want, to make magic more inclusive. From an out-of-character perspective, you're half justified. But it's not right in character, you need to know it's your headcanon, and insisting that a legitimately open-to-mystic-nonsense House Carna would have chantries anywhere with knowledge equal to an apprenticeship of two Assamite sorcerers and their stockpile of magic hash is laughable, let alone the Gold standard of Tremere chantries. The psychology is against you. The sociology is against you. The metaphysics of the WoD are against you. Any knowledge of how business organization works would tell you that House Carna, if we take their description in v5 at face value, would not work well in any market that's competitive.

                                    When I make a Ventrue character, I don't make them uncouth with working class values. I don't make Ventrue without social skills. I don't need to. I don't need my Ventrue character to have my skills or my values. I'm happy with making a Ventrue with Ventrue values; perhaps I'll spice them up with some off-ventrue thinking, but at the end of character creation I'l have a Ventrue of clan Ventrue. Imagine the shock and horror it would cause if suddenly half the Ventrue became insufferable hobos who wanted Anarcho-socialism?

                                    Why then, is there a group of Tremere who fundamentally break the rules of being a Tremere? Why is that Ok? The first rule of Thaumaturgy is that it takes a lot of effort. But now there are next to no standards and anyone can do it and be part of an effective organization. Imagine how much that sucks to people who've actually put effort into it?

What If, to accommodate people like myself more, V6 came out and said that now half the Malkavians are now completely sane, and instead... their weakness is that they know they're in a tabletop game and they get upset if the storyteller declares the difficulty's pretty high for the dice pool they've got? Oh and maybe dominate isn't that nasty anymore and only makes you a little more persuasive rather than forcing people to act.
