        Quick try, just made it now, just for fun:
        (In this world, all Clans have 3 Disciplines (signature ones are bold). Additionally, each Character must choose a 4th Physical one to treat personally as "in-Clan"). For those that want to use specific things of current lore, like Salubri's 3rd eye, just create Bloodlines/Loresheets such as "descendant of Saulot" and assume Antediluvians are "disputed" (no one knows if Absimilliard or Malkav is the "true" Antediluvian of the Cursed).
        • The Clan of Rulers (Ventrue, Lasombra, Banu Haqim)
          • These vampires believe they are meant to rule the world with an iron fist, to make the laws and enforce them over anything that "lives": kindred, men, and beasts alike.
            • Bane: Grounded (same as V5 Tzimisce)
            • Compulsion: Arrogance (same as V5 Ventrue)
            • Disciplines: Dominate, Presence, Animalism. (Most common Physical: Fortitude).
          • "We are the Kings and Judges of Mortals and Immortals alike; our word is the law and you shall obey".
        • The Clan of Shepherds (Toreador, Brujah, Ravnos)
          • The Rulers want to live above humans; the Shepherds want to live among them, side by side. Some prefer to mingle with the rich, others with the poor, others with nomads and other with artists, and some simply try to maintain their own normal "lives" among their own people (or the closest to it that they can get).
            • Bane: Feeding Preference (same as V5 Ventrue)
            • Compulsion: Affective Empathy (same as V5 Salubri)
            • Disciplines: Presence, Auspex, Obfuscate. (Most common Physical: Celerity).
          • "Kindred are meant to live side by side with humans, for good or ill, we are two sides of the same ccin."
        • The Clan of Magic (Tremere, Hecata)
          • The Vitae is magic, but this Clan's Vitae is even more than most. They "live" to learn all kinds of things, and to study and refine the magic in their blood.
            • Bane: same as V5 Lasombra (problems with mirrors and high technology)
            • Compulsion: Cryptophilia (same as V5 Nosferatu, but many times it becomes a "hunt-for-magic-secrets")
            • Disciplines: Blood Sorcery, Oblivion, "Thin-Blood" Alchemy (in this world, it works with non-Thinblood Vitae, so its called just "Alchemy". Common counterfeits are Auspex, Dominate, and Obfuscate/Chimestry). (Most common Physical: Fortitude (including Obeah)).
          • "I know much, and what I don't yet know, I shall discover soon."
        • The Clan of the Devoted (Salubri, Baali, Ministry)
          • Some people think they know what is the right path to follow; these kindred KNOW what they must adhere to. Many are religious or follow some kind of strict behavior code; some follow an old religion (or a kindred-exclusive one), some follow the path to Golconda, some follow infernal tenets or a mission to "corrupt souls", some enforce a personal code of "right and wrong", but all of them are very rigid in what they believe, and feel their Blood compelling them to do "what must be done" and to "guide other souls" or even found their own Blood Cult.
            • Bane: fundamentalist people are easy to enrage when confronted. Same as V5 Brujah.
            • Compulsion: Judgement (same as V5 Banu Haqim)
            • Disciplines: Presence, Dominate (note to "Obeah" lvl 5), Auspex. (Most common Physical: Potence).
          • "Are you lost? Let me show you the right path."
        • The Clan of the Cursed (Malkavian, Nosferatu)
          • These vampires are the proof that cainites are cursed; the Embrace inevitably distorts them forever. Some carry their curse on their flesh; others, on their mind.
            • Bane: Cursed Flesh (same as Nosferatu) or Cursed Mind (same as Malkavian)
            • Compulsion: Feral Impulses (same as V5 Gangrel) for the Flesh-Cursed, and Delusion (same as V5 Malkavian) for the Mind-Cursed.
            • Disciplines: Auspex, Obfuscate (includes Chimestry, for "bleeding" their Madness to the world, or to bring nightmares to life), Protean (they see it as the mark of the cursed, since it is clearly supernatural). (Most common Physical: Potence; madmen and monsters are commonly pretty strong and uncontrollable).
          • "Don't be fooled, we kindred are all damned, my curse is just easier to spot than yours."
        • The Clan of Metamorphosis (Gangrel, Tzimisce)
          • These vampires accept that they are not human anymore, and embrace their journey to accept themselves as they are or even to become something else. Some get inspirations on animals and their own bestial urges; others, in alien or mystic concepts of transcendence.
            • Bane: same as V5 Gangrel, but not limited to animal-themed: these can be "monster themed" or "cronenberg themed", based on the character daily actions and beliefs.
            • Compulsion: Transgresssion (same as V5 Ministry), as an urge to "transcend" - or to force others to "transcend" - their 'human' shackles.
            • Disciplines: V5 Protean (includes Vicissitude and One-With-The-Land), Animalism, Dominate (includes Vicissitude). (Most common Physical: Celerity).
          • "I'm more than man or animal; more than alive or dead - I'm something else."
        I'm surprised that i just needed 6 Clans o.O

        hope you all like it

            Personally I use the requiem aproach of 5 clans + bloodlines converting most of the other clans to bloodlines

            For example in my games the LaSombra are a bloodline of the Ventrue born in ancient Rome. However they proclaim not to be a bloodline but the original clan who fell in prestige after the original founder was diabolized by the Ventrue in what was effectively the original anarch revolt. The reason for said revolution was that they where using Obtenebration for dealing with otherwordly beings. The LaSombra criticize the ventrue because their lack of ruthleness was the reason why Carthago became a treat (the eldricth beings allied with the brujah) and later Rome fell. One of LaSombra aspiration was finding Ventrue resting place and diabolice it to make the clan great again.

            Discipline spread: Dominate, Animalism, Obtenebration, Vigor.

            Bloodline Weakness: The LaSombra suffer agravated damage from silver and cannot see their reflection on silver mirrors. Also their deals in the past with eldritch beings make them have a penalty of -1 in all rolls against spirits.

                Here is another possibility inspired by an earlier reply. For these Clans, they have no physical Disciplines, rather all vampires begin with one point of each physical Discipline and can buy them as in Clan Disciplines. Then each Clan has three Clan Disciplines, one of which is a Discipline unique to that Clan.

                Ventrue/Assamite: Dominate, Quietus, Presence
                Weakness: They only get full nourishment from the blood of one kind of vessel, much like the Ventrue weakness, but still get half nourishment from other sources. Furthermore, they cannot drink the blood of other vampires, and will vomit it up if they attempt to do so.

                The Ventrue are the ancient nobility of the undead. Not only kings, but knights and judges. They are honor bound and traditional, seeking to keep the laws of the Cainite race. They are the Clan most likely to be princes, as well as sheriffs or scourges. The greatest of crimes against the Cainites race is that of diablerie and their revulsion to it is so strong that they cannot even consume the blood of other cainites.

                Tremere/Malkavian: Auspex, Dominate, Thaumaturgy
                Weakness: Insanity, as Malkavians

                An order of human wizards who used their magic to transform themselves into vampires. Unfortunately, their magic and the dark secrets they have learned twists the mind, and all have some form of insanity.

                Nosferatu/Ravnos: Animalism, Chimerstry, Obfuscate
                Weakness: as Nosferatu

                Hideously ugly vampires, this Clan either hides within sewers or abandoned building, away from the prying eyes of humans, or the travel from place to place as nomads. Many use their skills to become spies or information brokers, some are even assassins or thieves. They are much like the Nosferatu of canon.

                Toreador/Lasombra: Auspex, Obtenebration, Presence
                Weakness: As the Lasombra, they have no reflection in mirrors and cannot be captured on film. They are also captivated by images of themselves, such as paintings or sculpture.

                Dark lord and ladies of the night, this ruthless Clan of darkness sees themselves as fit to rule others. They are also passionate and beautiful, hedonistic, and peruse sadistic pleasures as well as mundane ones. They are most attracted to works of art that reflect dark or macabre aspects of existence.

                Salubri/Brujah/Setite: Obeah, Obfuscate, Presence
                Weakness: When they drink the blood of unwilling vessels, they must make a hunger frenzy check with a difficulty of 4 + 1 per blood point consumed. Furthermore, drinking the blood of unwilling vessels increases all difficulties to resist frenzies by 1 for the rest of the night. This effect is cumulative for multiple unwilling vessels.

                This is a Clan of scholars, rebels, and non-conformists. Many have traditionally upheld more humane or compassionate values - that vampires should show mercy to humans, avoid murder or enslavement of them, even that you vampires should have rights that cannot be violated by the elders. Others reject rules and structures entirely, preferring absolute freedom. Members of this Clan form the backbone of the Anarch movement.

                Baali/Giovanni/Cappadocian: Dominate, Necromancy, Obfuscate
                Weakness: These vampires are cold and pale, unable to use blush of health. Furthermore, any mortal with a Humanity of 6 or higher counts as having +1 point of True Faith when attempting to combat these vampires with religious items. This can effectively give a humane mortal a True Faith value when they had none previously.

                This Clan of vampires serve demonic masters and practice dark magic that allows them to command the souls of the dead or even the forces of death itself. The center of the Clan is an ancient inbred family of demon worships who pass the curse of vampirism through their family, though others exist outside of the family as well. In addition to practicing dark magic, they also peruse wealth and temporal power.

                Gangrel/Tzimisce: Animalism, Auspex, Protean
                Weakness: these vampires must sleep surrounded by or within their native soil. If they do not do so, they gain an animal feature. Each animal feature reduces a single social attribute by 1 point and this is cumulative. If Appearance is reduced to zero this way, then the vampires becomes an obviously inhuman monster. Each night of sleeping with the soil removes one animalistic feature.

                This Clan are masters of the wild places and tied to the land itself. Some merely travel across the land preying on animals and living at me with nature. Others lay claim to a domain and rule it, and it’s inhabitants, as masters. The elders of this Clan are extremely traditional and territorial, emphasizing the rule of the elder over his domain.

                            Anyway, here are a few more combination Clans. Once again I gave each Clan a signature Discipline and no physical Disciplines, those will be universal.
                            Ventrue
                            Ventrue/Lasombra: Dominate, Obtenebration, Presence
                            Weakness: they have a strong preference for the vitae of a certain kind of vessel (virgins, old men, only animals, etc.) and any other kind of blood only provides 1 blood point for every 2 consumed. Furthermore, they take +1 level of damage from any exposure to sunlight.

                            The Venture are the lords of the undead. Masters of not only kindred and kind, but over the night itself. While they have a twisted sense of honor, they know that they are superior and it is their right to rule others. While many have an old fashioned sense of obligation and honor, others hold to a cruel Darwinian outlook that the strong must crush over the weak.

                            Tremere
                            Tremere/Baali: Dominate, Obfuscate, Thaumaturgy
                            Weakness: Mortals with a Humanity of 4 or higher count as having +1 level of True Faith against the Tremere and mortals with a Humanity of 8 or higher receive +2 levels of True Faith. This allows a human with no True Faith to harm or repel a Tremere with holy symbols or items.

                            An order of infernalists used their dark magic and pacts with demons to transform themselves into vampires. While hated and despised by many other Clans, they have managed to use their unity, ruthlessness, and dark magic to earn themselves the respect, if not the affection, of other Clans. The Clan is strictly hierarchal and it is said that the further one rises in the pyramid, the more demonic their rituals and ideology become.

                            Noferatu
                            Nosferatu/Cappadocian: Animalism, Necromancy, Obfuscate
                            Weakness: as the Nosferatu weakness, they have an Appearance of zero.

                            A Clan of death, the Nosferatu resemble hideously rotten corpses, though they also command powerful magics over ghosts and even death and destruction. Removed from the world of mortals and even other vampires because of their appearance, the Nosferatu practice their necromantic rituals far away from prying eyes in catacombs, sewers, ancient graveyards, and abandoned buildings. Ghosts make informants and spies without rival and this makes the Nosferatu masters of acquiring secrets and earn many favors among the kindred for this, as well as providing magical services - though despite their utility they are still feared and few other vampires endure their presence longer than they must.

                            Zantosa
                            Toreador/Tzimisce/Giovanni: Auspex, Presence, Vicissitude
                            Weakness: as the Tzimisce weakness

                            The Zantosa are a twisted and sadistic Clan of hedonists and flesh shapers. They use their powers to make themselves, and often their mortal slaves and playthings, beautiful beyond compare. The Zantosa love beautiful things and surround themselves with fine art, lively music, and especially beautiful mortals the sate their desires. At the heart of the Clan is the Zantosa family, an ancient family in inbred ghouls, with a number of other revenant families in their service as well. While not their primary focus, the Zantosa Clan also craves power and mastery over others, often through acquiring wealth, but even more frequently through manipulation or seduction.

                            Salubri
                            Salubri/Ravnos/Brujah: Animalism, Auspex, Chimerstry
                            Weakness: if Salubri attempt to drink the blood of an unwilling human, they must make a frenzy roll with a difficulty of 6 plus the number of blood points consumed.

                            The Salubri a a diverse Clan of mystics, scholars, rebels, and wanderers. They have never fully been able to abide living under the rule of kings or princes and have often been involved with Anarchs or even mortal revolutions or movements for greater rights or freedoms for the lower echelons of society. As their curse makes it difficult for them to take vitae from humans by force, they have usually been one of the more humane Clans and often opposed oppression and cruelty toward humans. When they cannot influence the political landscape, they often leave and are perhaps the Clan most likely to take up nomadic lifestyles.

                            Gangrel
                            Gangrel/Setite: Animalism, Presence, Protean
                            Weakness: the Gangrel weakness

                            The Gangrel are in many ways closest to their animal nature, for the are masters of the wild, both of beasts and of deep and dark forests. The Gangrel are not only hunters though, they have a deep spiritual connection to primordial nature - they are the pagan priests of the vampire world and their animal magnetism draws flocks of worshippers among those mortals who makes their homes near wild places and who live in harmony with nature themselves. Like druids of old, the Gangrel lead their following in offering sacrifices of blood in sacred groves by the light of the moon. They also protect their lands and flocks, as any territorial predator might.

                            Malkavian
                            Malkavian/Assamite: Auspex, Obfuscate, Quietus
                            Weakness: the Malkavian weakness

                            The Malkavians are a Clan of seers and madmen, much as how they are in the canon. Though perhaps even more notoriously, they are a Clan of killers. Malkavians feel a compulsion to stalk and kill, often mortals, but sometimes even their own kind. While they are feared for their madness, they are also highly desired agents and allies. They often find respect in the court of princes as spies, assassins, or agents and their insanity is tolerated as long as it can be properly directed.
