                                        Vampire society is absolutely okay with racism and sexism, insofar as it's okay with almost everything. The reality is that in the end, the rules are made and enforced by the most powerful vampires. If the Prince of some city hates Catholics and makes any Catholic vampire jump through so many hoops to stay in his city that most of them end up leaving, then until someone more powerful than him comes along and knocks him down, that's just how it is. The Camarilla doesn't send Justicars to go beat down vampires for being racist, or sexist, or hating on ethnicities or religions or anything else. As long as the Prince is making sure the Masquerade is upheld then the Camarilla probably won't bother with him.

                                        At the same time for the most part, older vampires have realized that being racist or sexist is pretty stupid. Vampires come in all forms, and some of them are going to be a lot more powerful than you are so pissing them off won't get you far. It's hard to be a mysoginist when you're dealing with female vampires that are hundreds or thousands of years older than you and could snap your body or mind in half like a twig. Whatever beliefs about mortals might be, for the most part vampires will accept that the blood of Caine makes everyone equel. So to some degree this is just a matter of natural selection. Vampires who are virulently racist or sexist probably won't last for more than a couple of decades because they'll piss someone off that's stronger than them. But there may be some vampires that are openly racist or sexist (look no further than Mithras) and those vampires are likely to be very old and very powerful.

                                        But it's also important that while racism and sexism do exist, to some degree, within vampire society, the gameline should not be encouraging players to play racists or sexists. This was a big problem with Werewolf, where you'd have literal neo-Nazi's show up to LARPs as members of the Swords of Heimdall, spew all sorts of racist bullshit and then say, "Well I'm just playing in character and the game says that's okay." Even if players want to play those sorts of characters, the gameline shouldn't be putting those examples and concepts out in front and saying, "This is the sort of character we want you playing as."
