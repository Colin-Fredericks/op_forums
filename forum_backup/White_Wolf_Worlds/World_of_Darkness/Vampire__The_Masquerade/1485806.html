    Serpentis homebrew

    07-04-2022, 07:21 PM
    Hey all! First time post after a long time just reading!

    I am in the process of homebrewing a V5 system with modified V20 disciplines for a home game, and I've struggled a lot with Serpentis as to me it lacked narrative significance and thematic unity. So once I've finished reworking it I decided to share it here. Perhaps this can serve as an inspiration for others because in my opinion this version of Serpentis can be a great source of new events in any game!


    A little preface: After a time of research, I've settled on the lore that Set the Warrior initially was of the 4th generation and he subsequently diablerised Typhon the Corruptor to reach the 3d generation. He then started to rework the discipline of Protean to suit his own flavor and needs of a Warrior. However, Typhon slowly overtook Set in his body (coinciding with the time Set was vilified in Egypt as his persona shifted, and as the overtake was slow the first power is the least affected), adding his corrupting twist on the discipline. The duality of two souls in Set's body (similar to Tremere and Saulot) lead to the duality in the discipline itself, where the desires of Set to grant power to his childer intertwined with the desires of Typhon to allow his childer to free (or corrupt, depending on your point of view) those around them, possibly leading to an increase in his progeny. Note that "The Heart of Darkness" in my version simply becomes a Blood Sorcery ritual.



    Serpentis

    Serpentis is believed to be the legacy of Set himself, a gift to his children. It is believed that the origin of Serpentis is Protean discipline, which Set reworked to suit his own needs. The Followers of Set are very careful to guard this Discipline’s secrets, only teaching the art to those who they deem worthy. Most vampires fear the Setites because of the powers of Serpentis and its connection to snakes and reptiles; this Discipline can evoke a primordial fear in others, particularly those who recall the tale of Eden.

    The most guarded secret of the discipline is of course it's duality, and the more subtle nuances are never taught outside the clan. The Ministry believes that everyone, Kindred and kine, wraps their true soul in layers of self-deception and social conditioning. More old-fashioned Theophidians speak of the Chains of the Aeons. Setites who prefer the language of modern psychology speak of "character armor" and "false consciousness". Sentiments of this have been voiced by Set himself in arguments with Malkav over the nature of Truth, in which Set stated the belief that the soul already contains everything and by breaking one's preconceptions one could access this knowledge. The Gates of Revelation describe the process of shedding the imposements of the Aeons to achieve freedom, and mark the spiritual development of a cultist, even when others outside the cult would often accuse the Ministry of "corruption". As losing one's illusions is rarely a pleasant process, most of the Gates are difficult to pass and involve highly uneasy practices. Setite doctrine claims this as the result of adaptation to the prison of the Aeons, and seek to open the eyes of their claimants to their own slavery.

    The individual powers of Serpentis help the Ministry sow the Seeds of Revelations in those around them, and although they are merely seeds, when they blossom there is always a chance to lead the lost soul through a full-blown Gate of Revelation.

    Blood Resonance: Sanguine


    • The Eyes of the Serpent

    This power grants the vampire the legendary hypnotic gaze of the serpent, allowing them to see in total darkness as well as to mesmerize those around them. The Kindred’s eyes become gold with slitted black irises, and mortals in his vicinity find themselves strangely attracted to him. A mortal who meets the vampire’s beguiling gaze is immobilized if the Kindred so wishes. Until the Kindred takes his eyes off his victim, the person is frozen in place, as if in awe, unable to look away. The Kindred can even mesmerize other vampires with this power, though the effect is likely to rouse the victim’s ire.
    • Cost: Free
    • System: By catching the eye of a mortal victim the vampire can immobilize them, keeping them frozen in place as long as they themselves maintain eye contact and as long as that eye contact is not forcibly broken by a third party (e.g. someone stepping between the Kindred and the victim, or closing victim's eyes with their hand). Catching the eyes of someone actively attempting to avoid the vampire’s gaze requires a contest of the user’s Resolve + Intimidation vs the target’s Wits + Awareness. It is impossible to catch the eyes of someone squeezing their eyes shut or wearing a blindfold.
      In order to paralyze a vampire in the same way, the user must win a contest of Charisma + Serpentis vs. Wits + Composure. If attacked or otherwise harmed, vampire victims, unlike mortals, can spend a point of Willpower to break the spell.
      The effect can only be maintained on a single victim at a time. Speech is still possible, though shouting is not. It is also worth noting that this is different than normal eye contact since for the Eyes of the Serpent to work the target must be able to see the vampire’s eyes.
      The user may choose to activate only sight in the dark or only mesmerism, or both, but either way his eyes will still turn gold with slitted black irises when he uses this power.
      While the sight in the dark is active the user ignores any sight penalties imposed by darkness, including supernatural.
    • Duration: As long as desired for sight in the dark; until eye contact is broken or the scene ends for paralysis


    •• Serpent's Silver Tongue

    The vampire may split his tongue into a fork like that of a serpent in order to better carry the revelations of Set to those around him. He can also lengthen his tongue at will, up to half a meter, making it a terrifyingly effective weapon in close combat.
    • Cost: One Rouse Check
    • System: The lash of the tongue’s razor fork causes aggravated damage. If the Kindred wounds his enemy, he may drink blood from the target on the next turn as though he had sunk her fangs into the victim’s neck (see p.213 V5 Corebook). Horrifying though it is, the tongue’s caress is very like the Kiss, and strikes mortal victims helpless with fear and ecstasy.
      If the Kindred is using his tongue as a social weapon, without lengthening, he adds a bonus die to Persuasion pools and if he manages to touch the victim while this power is active, he may opt to sow the Seed of Terror in them. Corresponding to the Revelation of Terror (the inducement of raw fear), the Seed causes the next sufficiently strong fear the victim will experience to take on truly epic proportions without triggering a Frenzy if the victim is a vampire or a ghoul, forcing them to experience the raw terror in its fullest without escaping into the safe embrace of one's Beast.
      When the Seed of Terror starts to sprout, the victim may try to burn the growth before it blooms by rolling Willpower at Difficulty 5. If successful, the victim regains a temporary Willpower point if they are not already at max. On a fail, the Seed of Terror blooms and the victim's temporary Willpower is reduced to 0. They are considered Impaired, receiving a -2 dice penalty to Social and Mental tests. They still get to roll one die if required to roll their pool, as per the usual rules. This lasts until whatever caused the fear has passed, usually until the end of the scene. Each Seed can bloom only once and when it withers, the victim loses a temporary Willpower point.
      Only one Seed of Revelation may be present in the victim at any given time.
      The split tongue does not affect the user's speech, but the visual change can of course be noticed and/or felt.
    • Duration: One scene for the use of tongue as weapon



    ••• Serpent in the Flesh

    By calling upon the Blood, the vampire may transform his skin into a mottled, scaly hide to become more supple and flexible, yet harder to hurt. He can also disguise the transformation, if preserving Masquerade is a concern, or if he would like to be subtle about sowing the seeds of Revelations.
    • Cost: 1 Rouse Check
    • System: The user's skin turns into scaly and mottled hide. The user can spend one Willpower point in order to force the monstrous hide to appear beneath his skin instead of replacing it to preserve the Masquerade but retain the advantages granted by this power. Otherwise, the vampire's appearance is obviously inhuman if observed with any degree of care, though casual passersby might not notice, if the vampire is in darkness or wearing heavy clothing. The monstrosity adds two bonus dice to Intimidation pools against mortals.
      In this hardened yet more flexible form the vampire may slip through any opening wide enough to fit his head through. He also adds an extra die to his Conflict pools, an extra point to his Health track, and an extra die to dice pools to escape grapple. The vampire also reduces any superficial damage received by 1 before halving it and can therefore receive no damage at all in some cases.
      While in this form and touching the victim, the Kindred may opt to sow the Seed of Wrath in them. Corresponding to the Revelation of Wrath (the inducement of extreme anger to forget social conditioning, calculation, and even survival in that moment, stripping the soul bare), the Seed causes the next sufficiently strong anger the victim will experience to take on truly epic proportions without triggering a Frenzy if the victim is a vampire or a ghoul, forcing them to experience the raw rage in its fullest without being able to then explain their actions away as the Beast's fault. When the Seed of Wrath blooms, the victim feels a really savage, towering rage, but unlike Frenzy it does not take control away from the Kindred instead giving it back to them and allowing them to shed social conditioning, calculation and even the survival instinct in order to be who they truly are at their core.
      When the Seed of Wrath starts to sprout, the victim may try to burn the growth before it blooms by rolling Willpower at Difficulty 5. If successful, the victim regains a temporary Willpower point if they are not already at max. On a fail, the Seed of Wrath blooms and the victim's temporary Willpower is reduced to 0. They are considered Impaired, receiving a -2 dice penalty to Social and Mental tests. They still get to roll one die if required to roll their pool, as per the usual rules. This lasts until whatever caused the anger has passed, usually until the end of the Scene. Each Seed can bloom only once and when it withers, the victim loses a temporary Willpower point.
      Only one Seed of Revelation may be present in the victim at any given time.
    • Duration: One scene for hardened skin



    •••• Serpent's Avatar

    The Cainite may change his form into that of a huge black cobra or become a Typhonic Beast, a creature of legend. The serpent weighs as much as the vampire’s human form, stretches over three meters long, and is about 50 cm around. The Typhonic Beast, or Set's Animal, stands as a tall jackal with a hard, forked and spiked tail, with severe, pointed ears and a long snout. Typhonic Beasts are red, black, or a combination thereof. In a modern city they can be easily seen as a unfamiliar breed of a big dog, except they are capable of human speech.
    • Cost: One Rouse Check
    • System: No test is required. The transformation takes one turn, during which the user may take no other actions. Upon transformation, the vampire gains the Physical Attributes, senses, and native Skills associated with that animal, Setite's Animal uses the stats of a Jackal, but can also speak which allows more freedom with regard to discipline use) and also gains that animal’s natural limits of communication, manipulation (most animals can carry one thing in their mouth), and so forth. The vampire can use other Disciplines, at the Storyteller’s discretion. (By and large, they can use Auspex, Animalism, Celerity, Fortitude, Potence, and Protean; many powers of Dominate, Obfuscate, and Presence pose problems; other Serpentis powers and Thaumaturgy are completely off the table.)
      Clothing and small personal possessions transform with the vampire, while bigger carried items get left behind.
      While in this form and touching the victim, the Kindred may opt to sow the Seed of Desire in them. Roughly corresponding to the Revelation of Desire (the inducement of pleasure in revolting acts such as criminal activities, sexual preferences, etc., to shatter the false self-image of the proband), the Seed causes the victim to act upon the next sufficiently strong desire that they will experience and will try to suppress.
      When the Seed of Desire starts to sprout, the victim may try to burn the growth before it blooms by rolling Willpower at Difficulty 5. If successful, the victim regains a temporary Willpower point if they are not already at max. On a fail, the Seed of Desire blooms and the victim suffers -1 dice penalty to all pools whenever not working to achieve the desire they tried to suppress. If a direct opportunity to achieve the desire arises and the victim wishes to resist the impulse, they need to roll Willpower at Difficulty depending on the strength of stimuli (e.g. if the desire is to kill a person, then seeing that person in a crowd would be Difficulty 1, but meeting that person in a dark alley with not witnesses where he on top of everything he offends the carrier of the Seed would constitute a Difficulty 6). This lasts until the desire is fulfilled. The victim may try to burn the blooming Seed once per night following the night of the blooming by rolling Willpower at Difficulty 5. In case they are successful, they loose 2 temporary Willpower points. If the Seed withers on its own once the desire is fulfilled, the victim loses only one temporary Willpower point.
      The victim is not necessarily fixated on following their desire at all times, but it is at the forefront of their mind like an intrusive thought that gets in the way of concentrating on other things. The victim still understands consequences of their actions, but whether they honor them or ignore them is a decision rooted in victims willpower and their personality - who they are and what they will do to gain what they want and get rid of the itch or whether they are strong enough to keep suppressing the desire again and again.
      Only one Seed of Revelation may be present in the victim at any given time and it can bloom only once, after which is withers.
    • Duration: Animal form lasts until the next dawn unless ended voluntarily before that



    ••••• Serpent's Breath

    Set was known as a god of deserts and storms. An elder Setite with Serpent's Breath can call a scorching sandstorm from his patron founder's source of power and expel it from his mouth. This whirling, screeching cloud of hot sand scars and pits everything in its path. The cloud is just a meter across, but that is more than enough to catch a Cainite unaware and riddle him with deep wounds. The power can also be used more subtly to bring those around the Setite closer to experiencing a Revelation.
    • Cost: One Rouse Check
    • System: The vampire exhales the roiling mist that is one meter in diameter, and that can affect a target up to 3 meters away. To hit, the player rolls Dexterity + Serpentis. The opponent cannot block or parry this attack, he must dodge by diving out of the cloud's volume. Successes remaining after the opponent's Dodge roll do levels of aggravated damage. The cloud dissipates at the end of one turn. The sandstorm can also be used on inanimate objects, destroying roughly 3cm of thickness of the substance per use.
      The user may opt to sow the Seed of Satiety in their victim if they manage to inflict at least one damage with their breath, or, alternatively, if using more subtly and as a social weapon, by touching the victim and breathing out a single grain of sand into their ear without inflicting any damage at all. Corresponding to the Revelation of Satiety (the overindulgence of a desire, until enjoyment palls and what once gave such pleasure now cloys and disgusts), the Seed causes the victim to repeat the next thing they enjoy until it no longer provides enjoyment, however long that might take.
      The behavior that makes the Seed of Satiety bloom should be a clear spike of joy for the victim, and the specificity of the behavior that the victim keeps repeating depends on what exactly the victim enjoyed - was it sex or was it sex with this particular person; was it dancing in general or was it dancing in this particular club; etc. Usually the victim will try to recreate the behaviour including all circumstantial details, but in the absence of possibility they will try to get as close as they can, experimenting, learning what exactly about the situation gave them joy, and stripping the repeated behavior in the process down to its very core. It is up to the Storyteller to decide when the victim stops enjoying the behavior, but usually it takes longer to achieve satiety with a more complex behavior.
      When the Seed of Satiety starts to sprout, the victim may try to burn the growth before it blooms by rolling Willpower at Difficulty 5. If successful, the victim regains a temporary Willpower point if they are not already at max. On a fail, the Seed of Satiety blooms and the victim will have to repeat the behavior nightly. If the victim wants to refrain from repeating the behavior on any night following the blooming of the Seed, they have to succeed on a Willpower roll at difficulty 3. If the victim is Kindred or Ghoul, they additionally suffer the danger of frenzy if unsatisfied and they might have to roll Willpower against a Difficulty 2 to resist such frenzy.
      If the victim is unable to or refrains from partaking in their joy on a particular night, they receive a cumulative penalty of one die on all of their dice pools (to a minimum pool of one die), which goes away if they either partake again or break out of the spell completely. To burn the Seed Satiety the victim will have to refrain from partaking in the behavior for the number of nights equal to the dots the user has in Serpentis. In case they are successful, they loose 3 temporary Willpower points. If the Seed withers on its own once the repeated behavior no longer brings joy, the victim loses only one temporary Willpower point.
      Only one Seed of Revelation may be present in the victim at any given time and it can bloom only once, after which is withers.
    • Duration: One turn

      To be honest, what i did in my own games was kind of the diammetrical opposite. Simply put, "one clan/bloodline, one signature discipline" is something I'm not fond of in terms of design space. It all too easily leds into one having to create ever more niche powers to go along with any new group of kindred or vice-versa, not to mention questions about revising/updating clans with only "vanilla" disciplines and no signature of their own (like the whole crossedition Dementation retcon mess, for example).

      Personally, i prefer a "less is more" approach when it comes to disciplines and in my games "Serpentis" is simply Protean with some clan-specific theming.
      Eyes of the Serpent/Enchanting Gaze, Tongue of the Asp/Typhonic Maw & Form of the Cobra/Typhonic Avatar are simply variant powers to Eyes of the Beast, Feral Claws & Shape of the Beast respectively, to which i added a "Sand Form" level 5 power, because why not.

      The heart removal power i turned into a ritual, that is what it should have been all along, might be said. (In my games the ritual background, much like the WtA version, covers not only social stuff, but also a occult effects, so i have exploited it regularly as a more freeform space to insert new powers or bring back old ones in a slightly modified format)

      As one might guess, i didn't limit myself to Serpentis but did much of the same "compression" and adaptation with the signature disciplines of all Sabbat & independent clans Vicissitude [Protean], Chimersty & Obtenebration [Obfuscate] and Necromancy & Quietus [Blood Magic], with a tweak here and there.
