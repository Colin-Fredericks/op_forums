        Not only that, but either side of the table is gonna be annoyed by this solution specifically.         If I had to give my opinion, I'd refund the EXP spent on the disipline(s), yes you can have multiple instances of TBA. Not only that, but I'd also allow the new full-blood access to starting disciplines, 2 in one and 1 in another, at least a week or less after the attack. They lose their Thin-Blood Merits and Flaws, but those cost no EXP and have no dots so their loss is actually harmless in the grand scheme of things.

                        I agree it wouldn't work in most cases, but if they are making the fixed &quot;potions&quot; then it might work. There are examples of some of these &quot;potions&quot; being used on other kindred and mortals so the evidence would suggest it actually is possible in this very specific case. Either way I would never run a world of darkness campaign where I'm refunding exp lol I just wouldn't. That's just no consequences baby town stuff to me. I would absolutely give them disciplines and a predator type and all of that.

                        I'm not sure what sanctity of traits is, but from the context it sounds like a &quot;conservation of experience&quot; rule which has never been in oWoD or V5. BUT it does sound interesting and I'd love to hear more about it or read more about it. Again that doesn't sound &quot;dark&quot; to me but that's just me.
                        Official called the Sanctity of Merits in Chronicles of Darkness, it is the rule that basically says if you lose a merit for story reasons you'll get the dot back to put elsewhere. This rule, like many others from Chronicles, made it's way in v5 specifically thanks to the fact advantages, which includes merits and backgrounds, now cost exp. Effectively it says, if you lose a merit or flaw you'll get the value back in the form of dots, the more common method, or exp. It also says that if you get a dot because of story it goes away at the end of that story, UNLESS you spend exp to keep it.

                        This rule makes sense, and like I said in earlier post on this same thread it doesn't normally extend to other traits. This is because you can't normally lose dots in anything else after character gen, outside of Humanity and Blood Potency. Yes BP can go down naturally during torpor, but that's besides the point.

                        Not refunding EXP when you may need to isn't giving consequences, it's saying the PCs choices don't matter. They choose to dabble in TBA, and then later to become a full-blood. If we go by the logic proposed at the start of this thread, they should lose TBA and get to replace it or get their exp back. If they are forced to keep something they literally can not use, then what is the point of having it on the sheet? It's just a reminder that the choices they make can result in them feeling worthless and trash. This isn't a video game, it's a ttrpg based around what's best for the story. Taking away a player's agency like this specifically takes away from the story, and even creates a disconnect between ST and PCs.

                        Now that all said, Humanity doesn't work with this argument. For one thing, Humanity normally shouldn't require you to use EXP to increase in v5. Namely because it's 10 x new level RAW in v5, making it near impossible to buy until you've played for at least a year per RAWs own suggestions. I only bring this up in case someone decided to ask about it.
