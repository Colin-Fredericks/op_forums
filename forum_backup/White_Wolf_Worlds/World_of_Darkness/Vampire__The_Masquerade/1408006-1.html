                                                Here is some of the head canon I am using for my current game. We are currently in 1999, using V20 rules. Once this current story arch is completed, we will do some down time wrap up of what the groups current characters are doing over the next 20 years, and then playing the same city, but in modern time using V5 rules.

                                                Blood pool -> Hunger
                                                Various blood plagues over the years have change the interaction between vitae and human blood. The latest was caused by Baali during the Week of Nightmares, which is capped off by the solar Eclipse of September, 1999 ( which coincides with the battle of Ravnos)

                                                Tzimisce
                                                Using inspiration from the forums here, several koldunics including Dracula attempt to lessen the connection that Kupala has over their blood magic, and the corruptive influence he wields over Vissiccitude. As a result of the ritual they perform, non Path of Metamophosis vampires (and anything else) that were touched by Vissiccitude have that taint literally pour from their body, semi sentient. The blobs try and form together to recorrupt things around them. This begins an internal battle of wills for many Tzimisce, where the younger members of the clan begin to question and defy the still corrupted elders. The clan will eventually fracture, with the Tzimisce defecting from the Sabbat and the still corrupted Assuka remaining loyal.

                                                Tremere
                                                Another unintended consequence of the above ritual occurred in the Tremere. Because they had originally used Tzimisce blood in their rituals to make themselves vampires, they suffer greatly during the purge. Internal politics leads to infighting, with the added complication of the clan's blood so changed to deny the creation of blood bonds, allowing greater autonomy and free will among the clan.

                                                Ventrue
                                                Hardestadt is Ventrue, continuously reinventing himself in a younger generation. Always viewing himself a Shepard of humanity and the other clans, he created the Camarilla to do just that. Seeing the direction the sect was going, the rising human hunter movements, the Anarch movements and the rising Thin Bloods, he, approaches Jan Piertezoon and Theo Bell with a proposal. The current status quo is stagnating, and needs to be reinvigorated. Theo Bell will kill Hardestadt publically, galvanizing a figure the Anarchs can rally around and give the Anarch movement new life blood. The Camarilla will consolidate around the majority of the historic High Clans.

                                                Lasombra
                                                The founding clan see the strife and infighting of the Tzimisce, and begin to look inward for direction. Many take pilgrimages to the Abyss, some of them encountering a powerful, destructive entity. Some of the clan embrace the being, while other shun it, leading the the schism and defection of many Lasombra to the Camarilla

                                                Sabbat
                                                After the 4th civil war and the defections, those that carry the banner of the Sabbat are touched or corrupted in some way. These can be the Assuka, the Shalimite Lasombra, infiltrating Warrior Assamites with hidden loyalty to Ur-Shilgi or more openly infernal Baali and apoostates. The sect seems to have no unified goal, with those claiming to be Sabbat sometimes fighting amongst themselves more so than agaisnt the Camarilla or Anarchs.

                                                Church of Caine
                                                Those former Sabbat still cling to many of their ideals and notions, taking the Sword of Caine and redirecting it. Members of the Church instill themselves into both the Camarilla and the Anarchs.

                                                Nosferatu
                                                Not fully trusting the Inner Council when they demanded access to Schrekt.net, a dummy .net was created and handed over. Once it was seen that the Camarilla leadership was leaking selected information to the Second Inquisition to squash their opponents, the Nosferatu as a whole "defect" from everywhere. Publically, they remain a part of the Camarilla, retaining a seat on the inner council. Privately within the clan, it is even more so Clan above Sect. The assumed nature of the Niktuku as the servants of Absmiliard isn't quite qccurate. Absmiliard hates all of his children equally. The Niktuku are those Nosferatu that keep other Nosferatu from attracting his attention. They will seek out an destroy Nosferatu that become too famous or garner too much attention.

                                                Ravnos
                                                The master of illusions, performed the greatest slight of hand and distraction. Drawing the attention of the Technocracy and the spirit nuke, they are attempting to realize their goal of transcendance by becoming a yama king by using the irradiated chi created in the nukes wake. The remnants of the clan redouble their efforts of hunting and erradiating demons and Keui Jin.

                                                Gehenna
                                                It is during one of the cyclical Gehennas, the one of 1999, that many other changes occur. it is in the aftermath that you see the rise of the Thin Bloods, along with the more mutability of the blood and vampiric powers.

                                                        There are many different ways an ST can handle Set. That's because the game uses several different inspirations for this antedeluvian, and every ST will latch onto different elements.
                                                        • We have the general story of the unique WoD mythology that applies to every antedeluvian.
                                                        • We have the layer of Egyptian mythology known to popular culture.
                                                        • We have the specific euhemeristic interpretation of this in VtM. For those unfamiliar with the term euhemerism is an interpretation of mythology that is the mythological personages and events are based on real people and events. One thing I liked about early WoD is that A lot (but not all) of mythological accounts are euhemeristic. It gives me a lot to play with as ST.
                                                        • More informed people also know that Egyptian mythology, like all mythologies, changed over time (and place!) bringing forth new ideas, new gods, new stories, and the religious officials constantly updating, reconciling, and transforming these stories to make sense to them at this time.
                                                        • We have the Robert E. Howard use of Set as the name for the "Great Serpent" of evil that is found throughout many of his tales.
                                                        Combined, these are extremely contradictory and incoherent. So any ST concerned with this will need to choose what elements he prefers and emphasizes. Personally, I love the use of Howardiana in the World of Darkness, but it is obvious many people don't understand its place in the game (if they are not familiar with the works of Howard, it goes over their heads), and some who do know it don't like it.

                                                        So I like to preserve the Followers of Set as a cult of Wyrm worshippers. They have a very specific dogma and goals as such. That specific dogma and their success as a clan sets them apart from Sabbat (and other vampire) infernalists and the Baali. They have a very similar understanding of the Triat (which these other groups don't) as the Garou, but within their own mythology (this is one reason why the Setites and Black Spiral Dancers aren't allies; they consider the other to be heretics).

                                                        (I think the clan works less well as a Vampire specific element, but works extremely well as a World of Darkness element that helps tie the game to others. When I first played, I didn't care for this so I minimized the Setites role in my games. But since I now like a more integrated setting (while avoiding a Monster Mash style) I find a lot more use for them. It also helps explain why they are an Independent clan - they're so busy with non-vampire stuff, they actually don't want to deal with the diversion of vampire politics.)

                                                        In my chronicles, the Setite antedeluvian was a high priest of the Wyrm before the Flood. Let's call him Typhon to distinguish him from the Wyrm (aka Apophis) and Set (the Egyptian personage). Set, Osiris, Isis, and many of the other Egyptian pantheon were real historical personages post flood, somewhere around 5000-4000 BC. Typhon embraces Set, making him of the 4th generation. Typhon also embraced other 4th generation childer in other lands, but only Set survived long term (Set's childer are the dominant lineage, but a few vampires descended from other destroyed lines did survive - these often cause problems within the clan because their teachings don't mirror the dominant Egyptian strand). When Typhon eventually went into torpor, Set "pulled a Hardestadt" and essentially took his place, and gradually it was his name that was assigned to the antedeluvian. Most of the historical accounts of Set are actually of this 4th generation vampire, not Typhon.

                                                        Typhon eventually left Egypt moving south down the Nile, leaving Set "in charge" of what would become the dominant block of his descendants. Set was eventually forced out of Egypt by Horus, the Cult of Isis, and Silent Striders (among others). Things went well for a long time, and the common people just remembered the good stories about Set (before he was embraced). Then Set returned to Egypt during the Hyksos Invasion and began purging the land of his enemies. This is when Set's image in popular Egyptian imagination changed, and he became outright evil.

                                                        [I've never made up my mind though about who embraced Osiris. While many feel he too was embraced in the same clan as Set, I prefer to think of him as one of Cappadocius's childer. Osiris is a Greek variant of Ashur which is one of Cappadocius's possible names, and Osiris' eventual role as a lord of the dead fits within the Cappadocian theme. I also like the idea that this means the Children of Osiris (which is an extremely small sect in my game, not a "bloodline") became the home of some of the more humane Cappadocians escaping the Giovanni purge.]

                                                        Noddists have a hard time figuring out stuff about Set because he's not the actual antedeluvian, but this isn't clear to anyone outside an extremely small circle of the Setite clan. So Noddists come up with things like the Set-Osiris myth is just a misinterpretation of the Cain-Abel myth.

                                                        I run the clan according to strict gnostic-style cult lines. Clan/religious secrets are only revealed to trusted individuals who advance to the next level. Much teaching requires esoteric knowledge because everything is not what it seems. The clan controls many different occult, religious, or philosophical front organizations that recruit and teach normal humans that begin the process of indoctrination. Eventually some of these people advance to the next level and become aware of vampires and taught Set-specific teachings. They become retainers to actual vampires (some become ghouls, but not all). And the clan only embraces a select culling of these people in order to ensure their loyalty to the greater clan/cult organization. (This greatly changes how people become Setite vampires. The clanbooks assume the Setites embrace like the other clans, grabbing suitable people off the streets, instead of operating in an actual cult like manner of indoctrinating people first.)

                                                        ​It's not for everyone, but it works for me.

                                                        However, because of this (and other head canon), it really prevents me from using any of the V5 major setting element changes except as a matter of broad inspiration.

                                                        My preference would be for the game to stop trying to pretend there is one inherent canon anymore. Every edition changes discards many elements introduced in prior editions, and only keeps what it wants. It needs to understand many players and STs do the same. It would be better to adopt a toolkit approach and give these metaplot elements only as ideas an ST could use in their game, and to state there are multiple versions of the WoD - the early Andrew Greenberg version, the late second edition version, the Revised era of Sabbat Crusades, and this new V5 version. Clarify how they are different, and how each makes for a different game. Make it clear ALL are appropriate versions, and that STs can create their own version.

                                                        Originally posted by Konradleijon View Post
