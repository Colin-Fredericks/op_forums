                                                        Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                                                        It's a correction of a massive flaw in V5. I would not be the huge V5 fan if not for OPP fixing most of the problems but they couldn't handle the Sabbat issue. Now most of my issues with V5 are fixed.
                                                        So, to be clear, you're willing to give stars to V5 Sabbat for something it isn't (timely)?

                                                        Because this doesn't feel like a consistent standard.

                                                        So I think I have to judge it on the context of a Sabbat antagonist book versus a Sabbat player book.
                                                        Plenty of the flaws you have directly stated exist in the book apply to an antagonist focused book.

                                                        The lack of Path rules makes it harder for STs to know how to handle Paths when the PCs interact with Path vampires. If the PCs feel like they can't take a Sabbat pack head on, and want to weaken the pack's leader by undermining his Path (like you can by attacking an NPC's Touchstones to undermine their Humanity) the ST is forced to adjudicate this on the fly as there are no mechanics to base the attempt on.

                                                        The lack of Loresheets means there isn't a good way for players to buy into Sabbat focused stories by adding connections to the Sabbat (former Sabbat members, a sire that's still Sabbat even if the PC isn't, etc.).

                                                        The lack of information on an number of key topics to the Sabbat so STs can understand the Sect's updates.

                                                        The short book and high price.

                                                        All of these things are negatives for an antagonist focused book doing the job its supposed to do.
