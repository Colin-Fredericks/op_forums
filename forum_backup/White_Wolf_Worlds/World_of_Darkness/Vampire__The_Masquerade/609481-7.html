                                Originally posted by Murder-of-Crows View Post
                                WHAT? That makes even less sense. If Gehenna happening in 2004 is cannon, then I don't see how the game line is viable at all. We either have no vampires at all, or we have mostly neonates who know nothing about the decades of backstory (thus,rendering it moot) and end up with a Requiem-like situation...
                                Originally posted by TwoDSix View Post
                                Almost certainly no sects. We are back to a more Dark Ages approach. I also don't think that the only survivors would be neonates, but that a good number of older vampires would survive by basically hiding until it was over. I'm also assuming that Gehenna will push the standard generation down, in the case of having a handful of 4th-8th generation vampires in the world and making most PCs fall into the 14th-9th bracket. I expect to either see something happen to the 14th and 15th generation vampires or for 16th generation vampires to appear. We've had 10+ years to rebuild vampire society and rediscover the backstory, I believe that things such as establishing new sects and finding Beckett's chronicle of Gehenna could be things that you could incorporate into a post-Gehenna chronicle.

                                And maybe, just maybe, the Camarilla and/or the Sabbat WON Gehenna. What would happen if the current 'elders' destroyed their forebears and decided to let Noddism flourish and have all the old backstory be better knowledge? With the Antediluvians undeniably dead and the threat of Gehenna averted, what need do the biggest, baddest vampires around have to worry about not having always been the pinnacle of power, what matters is that they are now.
                                Originally posted by Lucius View Post
                                Post Gehenna? Now I have this image of a Mad Max style world with vampires stuck in my head.
                                Originally posted by moogle001 View Post
                                The point is to be Vampire the Masquerade but more modern. VtM is not post-apocalypse. Even if some sort of Gehenna occurred, we're going to be left with a game of personal horror in Gothic cities. I would assume anything resembling werewolf's apocalypse is not on the plate. If there was Gehenna, it was probably very contained. Personally, I question Gehenna happening at all.
                                Originally posted by Phelios View Post
                                In V4 I would forget about Gehena altogether and pick it up from the Camarilla-Sabbat war. Bring back all those characters we use to love and hate and play from there.

                                Gehenna is the end in VTM and although I must admit a post-apocalyptic setting is kind of appealing, it would be a whole different game and better served as a supplement.
                                Color me somewhat skeptical of V:tM4. When I first heard of this, the first thing I thought was "Why so soon after V20?" Then I saw the response that 5 years is not all that long between editions, so that mollified me, at least on that front. While I am a fan of metaplot, I do see the appeal of discarding parts of metaplot that don't make sense or that I dislike. But then again, Storytellers have always had that option.

                                As for V4th being "post-Gehenna," I can't see that being viable. The idea of a "quiet Apocalypse" has always struck me as trite and silly. The entire point of an apocalypse is that it's supposed to be the end of the world. That's not the sort of thing that can creep up on you or anyone else (or especially everyone else) without the world, y'know, noticing.
                                It's not like people wake up one morning, go to work and remark around the water cooler with stuff like this:
                                "Hey, did you know the world ended last week?"
                                "You don't say? I didn't notice a thing."
                                "Wait, what? The world ended? Why am I always the last to hear about this sort of thing? They didn't shit about this on CNN!"
                                "Well, they did mention it on Fox News, but then Fox is always predicting the imminent end of civilization, so I didn't pay attention..."
                                "Huh. The world ended. Go figure. So what are you guys doing this weekend?"
                                No. I'm hardly an expert in eschatology (despite being a big fan of Werewolf: the Apocalypse), but I'm quite certain that apocalypses don't work like that. Even taking the "Wormwood" scenario from ToJ: Gehenna into account (an example of a "quiet apocalypse" if ever there was one), there's no way V:tM would be even be the same game if the metaplot tried to be "post-Gehenna." No matter how you slice it, whether it's a "quiet" one or not, an apocalypse by its very definition is the total up-ending of the status quo. If OPP is bringing the metaplot back for V4th, the only way it will make any sense is if Gehenna is still on the horizon.
