            * Vienna was, as I was saying as far back as 1999, a huge target for anyone who wanted to take them out.

            * The Tremere were apparently under the White Worm's control if Etrius feeding it was any indication and that has been dealt with by the Second Inquisition.

            * House Carna is an aggressive un-dominated and power hungry group outside the normal parameters of the Jyhad.

                                                            Neo-pagans exist across europe. But I'm not sure as to how many take it seriously and how many are just having fun larping or 'getting in touch with their culture'.

                                                            Historical Real life witchcraft was more about poisoning your neighbours dog , saying aloud the very bad things you want to happen to someone, not being all that religious, and in general being a very unpopular person. Poisonings were the cause of a lot of witch killings. Some very bored people with not enough to do started writing very spicy books that got a lot of attention and later on some competing forms of Christianity might have gotten a little too excited over the prospect of killing witches to prove the efficacy of their faith over other branches (It added variety to burned heretics). Killing witches for the most part was far less common than you'd think, and far too many like to point at it as religious madness

                                                            At some point witch hunts caught on in the new world despite the lack of religious conflict because... You've seen what Americans are like, right? Those puritans were England's crazy rejects.

                                                            Modern real life witchcraft is inspired by movies inspired by other movies who looked at those fancy books some very creative men wrote back in the day, practiced by women who don't fit in all that well (just like the historic lot) and need a sense of belonging. So they form friendship groups and adopt strange fashions and stuff, and then they get some feminist wicca stuff in there because it sounds nice and the stuff that those men with too much time on their hands wrote is more than a little mean to women.
