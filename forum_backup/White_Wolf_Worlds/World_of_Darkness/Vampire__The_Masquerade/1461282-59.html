    I don't think Antes really play that kind of role in politics one way or another, so their being alive or not is irrelevant.

    To me it makes sense for the Sabbat to forward the Antitribu moniker as meaning you don't have loyalty to your clan. That's it. Saying it has to do with the Ante still existing? It never had anything to do with it and it doesn't make sense. This is a statement to your clan, not to your Ante.

    A bunch of clans either think their Ante is already dead or don't care one way or the other. The Ventrue and Salubri Antitribu don't call themselves like that to piss off dead people. Ennoya and Absimiliard couldn't care less, as one is indifferent and the other is an enemy to his clan anyway. Arikel and Malkav aren't supposed to be great leaders of their clan, at most an inspiration.

    Only Haqim and Set are really all that into being the overlords of their clans, at least from the PoV of their descendants. They're pretty much the only ones that could feel offended by the whole Antitribu thing. None other. No Ante cares. The clans care.

                        My take is actually that Gehenna is as much a losing situation for the Antes as it is for their descendants, and so many of them already get out of this game. Power is good, but protect one's own Ego and getting rid of problematic needs like low-gen blood takes precedence. So they're actively trying to NOT be blood-sucking heralds of the end of the world.

                        That also means any Ante is either limited by the effects of their Transcendence, by being in the process of achieving it yet, or by being a dumb loser that actually turned into what their childer fear.

                        Cappadocius turned into a Wraith to diablerize God. If he achieved it, he's for all purposes Ego-dead. If not, he found whatever else to do as a happy ghost.

                        Lasombra is a big jerk, mustache-twirling villain, an pretty much trying to eat an energy field bigger than his head. Anyone can see from a long way that his plan is stupid. He's either Ego-dead, dead-dead, enslaved, or simply failed and is a "common" vampire again, powerful as may be, ready to fulfill his role in the Gehenna prophecy and maybe enter Wassail.

                                            Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                                            Very true.

                                            Oddly, I suppose the Tzimisce and Lasombra becoming Antitribu as a fig-leaf toward parity with the other Antitribu strikes me as a change that is actually justifiable in-universe. Ir's not one of the changes that I think actually harms the sect and makes sense in-universe.

                                            But I can see why people feel its a change for change's sake.
                                            I just have a really hard time seeing it. For the Sabbat in the Lasombra to don on the antitribu label would mean that they accept that their position as author of their clan has slipped from their fingers. That functionally they're the losers, big losers, and now the fate of Clan Lasombra lays with the Camarilla, at least for the foreseeable future. I just can't see the Lasombra being willing to admit that they failed and lost everything they spent the last 700 centuries building and working towards. Now that could be true from an objective viewpoint, but I just can't see them embracing that.

                                            For me the most likely scenario is that the Sabbat Lasombra insist that they're the "real" Lasombra and that they're ones who control the destiny of the clan. And that all of the traitors who ran off to join the ranks of the Lasombra antitribu in the Camarilla are now also Lasombra antitribu. And then the turncoat Lasombra new to the Camarilla would insist that no, they're the "real" Lasombra and it's the Sabbat who are now the antitribu due to the fact that the Camarilla Lasombra have the most bodies and and that therefore the Camarilla Lasombra are now the "main branch" of the clan.

                                            Funny enough I would expect that the only Lasombra who would choose to call themselves antitribu would be the old-school Lasombra antitribu who were always a part of the Camarilla and never a part of the Sabbat. While 5e gives us a sort of "Kumbaya" view in which the ex-Sabbat Lasombra are largely accepted by the Camarilla, in reality they would face a lot of weariness and even discrimination from members of the Camarilla who might not be willing to let bye-gones be by-gone or, at the least, have a tough time entirely trusting their backs to some guy who spent the last couple of hundred years on a Path drinking other vampires souls and trying to destroy the Camarilla.

                                            So the actual Lasombra antitribu, the ones who have always been part of the Camarilla, might find it useful to keep that distinction in order to draw a line between themselves (who have spent centuries proving their loyalty to the Camarilla) and the newcomers who only just joined up a few months ago. So they'd call themselves antitribu and you'd have other Camarilla vampires calling them that ("What? You think Fred can't be trusted because he's a Lasombra and that means he's ex-Sabbat? No no, Fred's one of them antitribu Lasombra. He's been part of the Camarilla since he was embraced 90 years ago. He's not some monster.")

                                            Something would be fairly similar with the Tzimisci which has never really had an antitribu group and I really can't see the Sabbat Tzimisci being willing to gracefully accept the fact that they are no longer in control of the future of their clan either. People might call Tzimisci Sabbat "antitribu" but I can't see them calling themselves that.

                                            I think ultimately this is just goes towards v5's desire to simplify things and having the Lasombra fight over who is and isn't antitribu is felt to be too complicated, so the authors decided to just make all of the Sabbat clans antitribu in order to make a clearer distinction between the factions.

                                            * Edit* Out of curiosity, as I haven't read the new Sabbat book, are the Serpents of Light now called "antitribu" or do they at least keep their non-antitribu label? Because rejecting the label of antitribu entirely was a pretty important part of their identity.

                                                    To me the defector are called exactly that: the Lasombra Defectors. And no Antitribu will accept to be confused with them.
                                                    The thing abiout SABBAT: THE BLACK HAND is that it actually does have a lot of really fascinating and interesting ideas for Sabbat culture that are worth talking about. Unfortunately, they've been overshadowed by the larger controversy of them being regulated to being an antagonist sect. It kind of makes discussing the intricacies of it all hard as the antagonist only element is the 800 lb gorilla in the room. This isn't me dismissing the element as you're not going to be able to have much opportunity tio discuss these intricacies with a Sabbat trying to diablerize you.

                                                    However, getting back on topic (SHOCK - I am terrible at this), The Paths replacing Clan is something that I actually really like and am glad that the Sabbat are going for. In a way, the Lasombra and Tzimisce ANtitribu elements don't really matter because the "New" Sabbat are a group that is fully molding into a single post-clan sect. Being Lasombra as a concept no longer will matter in the Sabbat because all of the traditional Lasombra power structures, cultures, and beliefs will cease to exist.

                                                    The Lasombra who actually CARE about being Lasombra and their 13,000 year heritage dating back to Enoch are the people who have moved over to the Camarilla. The Lasombra who were actually in charge of maintaining their traditional Clan based structure are explictly the ones who were the first to leave--perhaps noting that the Anti-Clan fervor that was part of the Gehenna War would ultimately destroy what kept the Lasombra unique.

                                                    The funny thing is this has happened before in the sect. The Assamite Antitribu almost entirely left the Sabbat and rejoined the mainstream clan when Ur-Shulgi broke the Tremere Curse.

                                                    The stronger identity of the Sabbat are the Paths of Cathari, Death and Soul, and other ideologies. So it goes:

                                                    1. Sabbat
                                                    2. Path
                                                    3. Clan

                                                    In importance.
