                          It is something that can be forgotten for stretches, The Toreador weakness is one that I sometimes feel needed a good concept change altogether. There was a post about changing a weakness for Tzimisce to can't enter a dwelling without invitation and I mentally thought of Toreador being a better candidate. I'm not saying that one is perfect for them, I actually think it fits the Ventrue best but it is so abstract for Toreador that it can slip through the cracks for periods. I don't like hard and fast rules like at least once per session or anything. It's not the worst clan weakness but surly one of the most vague.

                              To a large degree, I think Clan Weaknesses are more an issue of flavor text so my gaming group doesn't worry about them too much. While it does come into play as a meaningful limitation (it impacts the game and actually changes how a scene plays out) sometimes, it's mainly something players and the STs make an effort to introduce into the game as a roleplay opportunity. Or as the hook for the ST to introduce a new plot.

                              So my experiences likely aren't very helpful to you.
