    Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

    There's several things here that need to be noted.

    1. One, it's Jewish myth not Christian. Caine and his peeps have nothing to do with Christianity beyond being adopted by it. This is like saying Herakles is a Roman myth.

    2. Second, they changed the origin so Caine sacrificed Able rather than killed him out of jealousy. So it is a satirical parodic take on Jewish mythology. Like telling a story where Lilith is a heroine rather than the enemy of pregnant women. I feel that this impacts its interpretation as an artistic presentation of religious iconography. Like saying, &quot;The Chilling Adventures of Sabrina&quot; (where she's the daughter of Satan) is Christian propaganda.

    3. Chinese mythology is banned by the People's Republic of China with ghosts illegal to talk about. Use of them at all is a form of civic protest.

    4. I feel that associating it with real life atrocities due to creating a unifying mythology is demeaning to said real life atrocities versus honoring them.

    I make my arguments primarily based on the fact that my association with V:TM fans in Hong Kong and Tawain online are that they tend to hate the Kuei Jin/Wan Kuei and don't WANT them fixed. They want to play Cainites in Hong Kong by Night and their hometowns like any other fans. Why? Because they want to play the game and not be Othered. There's exception to this rule but V:TM fans tend to be fans of the Camarilla, Anarchs, Sabbat, and other groups that Kuei-Jin/Wan Kuei can't participate in like, say, the Laibon or Drowned.

    I'm willing to be persuaded otherwise but I feel that the perspective of fans involved from the cultures should be the primary guide on this. Over in TECHNOCRACY RELOADED, Asian fans had some opinions on the Five Metal Dragons for instance.

    &quot;We want to be Technocrats. Just not ones subordinates to Europeans.&quot;
    Creating a unified mythology was part of the point of those genocides, don't aid and abet them. That's the point either honour different religions, mythologies etc by not declaring Abrahamic myth true (DtF also does this, and should not have, as much as I like parts of that game), leave things ambiguous, leave room for the interesting conflicts and mechanics of other vampires, other magic traditions, other afterlives etc etc, leave that room for stories, don't straight jacket things into the middle east and Europe are right, everyone else is a deluded, inferior Johnny foreigner as you want.

    Fair point I should have probably said Abrahamic myth, not Christian, the slight alteration to the Caine story is well within the wiggle room of interpretation that faiths in that tradition have used, with different translations, traditions etc disagreeing.

                                            Interesting comparison to the Sabbat, Lian. I never thought of that.

                                            I think the biggest weakness of the Kuei-Jin/Wan Kuei isn't actually their origin story, though. If I may be so bold as to make a suggestion, the biggest issue with the Wan Kuei is the fact that they are so far removed from V:TM that it's almost impossible to incorporate them into a game with Western vampires. As stated, if a Chinese gamer wants to make a Eastern vampire character, he basically is stuck playing an Eastern vampire game because the characters are set up to never want to have anything to do with the Camarilla, Sabbat, or Anarchs. Which means that they're their own splat that divorces them from the setting. It also deprives Asian settings of the Camarilla, Sabbat, and Anarch conflict that most Asian gamers will want to participate in.

                                            If Eastern vampires weren't all hostile and actually partcipated in Camarilla and Anarch politics as equals and were accepted as vampires, it'd be a very different scenario, IMHO.

                                            However, even if they were retconned, they entirely warp the narrative because once introduced you've got Hell, Akuma, Demon Princes, and so on that make regular vampire tussles over turf and humanity look silly.
