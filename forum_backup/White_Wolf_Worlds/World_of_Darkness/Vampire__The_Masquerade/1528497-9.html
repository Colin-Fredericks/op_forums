                                                        Mismanagement is such a broad term its kinda meaningless. It's kinda like saying, "Well, the biggest reason we didn't win the sports game is they scored more than we did."

                                                        IMO the core issue is that Paradox didn't understand (and I'm not sure they have figured it out yet) what the IP needed to expand past what Onyx Path was doing so things could grow beyond a TTRPG popular enough to keep a small-but-medium-by-TTRPG-standards company going.

                                                        Even something like, "make a new edition that's easier to translate into video games," is more nuanced than it can seem and Paradox clearly didn't seem to understand that. For example, what kind of video game you're making is going to change what mechanical directions you'd want the TTRPG rules to move towards drastically, or if your video game strategy is to have a lot of different types of video games use the same IP, you might not care that much about the TTRPG mechanics vs. keeping a consistent mood and theme so the franchise feels consistent in that sense.
