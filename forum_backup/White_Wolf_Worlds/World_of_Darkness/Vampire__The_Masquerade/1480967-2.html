                                        I work at one of the biggest banks in the continent, precisely at one of the very few sectors that deal with the security systems in a regular basis, and for several branches on top of that, around 40 of them. I can state as a fact that you're right.

                                        Motion sensors are mainly used for two things: automated lights and doors (main use by far) and alarm systems. But the main security system is still watchmen and cameras, and reinforced programmed doors for valuables. What will happen if an alarm sets off is that both the watchmen will be on alert and remote surveillance will be called on the cameras. And here's the thing, animals sometimes activate the alarm, or they go off because of some other accident, so an alarm going off for no relevant or even identifiable reason, while not common, isn't strange.

                                        Outside that, footage will rarely be reviewed. Several cameras working 24/7 and recording is a lot, but that just means that there's no way any business would have the personnel to watch that amount of footage for no reason, or even be able to keep the records for long. Four or more cameras working 24/7 is a lot of data even at low quality, and isn't a lot of cameras (we use far more even in a small branch). We use a very solid server to store it and yet we delete without review after a few months. Only if something happens that requires reviewing that'll happen, usually to settle down some customer's allegation or to confirm if some employee was doing something stupid.

                                        So yes, an Obfuscate user may be perceived by motion sensors and later footage review. Yet if they're cautious enough, and it doesn't take much, the worst that will happen is that the live feed is watched, watchmen are called, everyone is fooled, and the footage will be deleted without ever being seen unless something is subtracted, and we're talking the Nos as spies, not robbers.

And there arent that many Tremere to begin with...

                                                Tzimisce. Great Discipline spread. Likely the LEAST to care with finding what the Nosferatu are up to. or spying beyond their demesne. They have experiments and land to play with.

                                                Potence is VERY handy if not sexy. But Nosferatu dont use it like Brujah, for combat or intimidation, not as much. Nosferatu need it more for Survival concerns, floods and moving heavy shit in the sewers as well as digging a temporary haven in the riverbed mud. And back to the hunter theme, Potence+Obfuscate = hit first, hit last, no one saw shit. Boogeyman. Potence makes Staking a vamp easier AND carrying them down to the sewer while obfuscated all that much easier. Not to mention nabbing food or someone else's ghouls.

                                                Animalism is not widely used because most Players dont understand animals. Animalism is a Network leveraging tool. Animalism has NO COUNTER other than Animalism. Over 20 years and i have not seen a thaumaturgy counter to Animalism, nor have i seen Auspex being useful against it. Because who cares about animals stunts, rite?

                                                How does spying with animals work? Basically in 2 general ways. Having animals track a target. Possessing an animal and using their abilities. You have to study the abilities of animals but in short: Auspex1 in V20 claims to "double" the range(and sensitivity) of the user. This doesnt come close to what most animals can do, especially for olfactory. Ravens and Crows appear to identify individual humans communicate identified individuals to each other. they can also solve multi-step problems with some primitive tool use. Can roam about 40miles/day so tracking people/cars shouldnt be too hard, Auspex cant do this, especially several targets at once.
                                                Dogs can scent track way further than humans, up to 130 miles in some cases. Average large city is only a few miles diameter.
                                                Etc... Little wonder the Nos know where all the city vamps live.

                                                Possession: Ride an eagle/hawk and track during the day, watch that important meeting from afar and lip read. Ride a dog and listen from several rooms over, or even from another building if close enough. Possess a burrowing animal, dig your tunnels, and listen in from inside the building. And in all the Lore, how many vamps worried about pest control? Almost none, if any.

                                                Imagine a few Nosferatu somewhere down in the grimey sewer, and they rarely, if ever, go to the surface. Their main choice of travel is to possess animals, have them ferried to the surface, and they go about spying. Even during the day. Does anyone other than the Nos know about them? Not likely. And if they did, what could they do about it?

                                                Now, to that network effect, most Nosferatu can cheaply get Animalism1 and they are in. They are part of the Network. They can share animals, communicate between them, and each vamp can deploy several animals at once. Auspex cannot do this and Auspex 1, 2, 3, and 4 REQUIRE you to be in person to use. Most Nos if they wanted, could likely get Auspex1, depending on ST.
                                                Nosferatu already have the spy network. Other clans can communicate with each other, sure. But how many can leverage clanmates for general info like Nosferatu?

                                                Now, back to Nosferatu being impartial info brokers: Remember, thats the image. Trappers need to know the preys behavior. Nosferatu for damn sure use that bartering to their advantage. But they dont want the typical things other clans want, so no one notices. Or cares?
