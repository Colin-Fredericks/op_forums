        Maybe it wasn't Vicissitude or Koldunic sorcery at all
        It could have been Chimestry
        Even at level 6, it makes terrible things
        Horrid Blade of the Demons create a dangerous weapon out of nothing
        Shared Nightmare/Mass Horror like Horrid Reality, except it affects multiple people (Horrid Reality: Places the mind of the victim in an illusionary world of the vampire's creation)

        At level 7
        Suspension of Disbelief: Make an illusion seem completely real or what is real seem unbelievable

        At level 9
        Mayaparisatya: Temporarily make an illusion completely real or alter a person or object that already exists, possibly even erasing them from existence
        Truths of the Universe: Make a created illusion permanently real

        Hazimel/The Rakshasa is/was a 4th generation child of Zapathasura, so safe to assume, his eye, witch he made an amulette, potentially could chanel his powers, if not all ,the signiture power of his clan at least, and even if you just take these publised powers and not some Elder made ones even those are capable of those things what Leopold did was described in the novels

        And of course, Leopold was Tremere Antitribu and insane (maybe even had the Eldest behind his eyes, or at least had vicissitude posioning, but something was not okay in his head), yes after the Gangrel incident, he later collected the remains of the defeated Gangrel and created a gargantuan sculpture stretched in every direction at every angle to almost fill a very sizable cavern nearby. The work was not only of stone; flesh and bone adorned the sculpture and were incorporated into it. Entombed within the work were kindred limbs, the bodies of a dozen or more, few completely intact, some still moving. Leopold considered that abomination to be his masterpiece and subsequently went into torpor for weeks.
        And this is in my humble oppinion a bit of a clue
        Shaped STONE and Flesh together.... Hazimel was a stonemanson
        Vampire bodies tend to start to decay after Final Death and those Gangrel died... the fact, it was writen like that, not to mention the moving part, suggest, at least in my oppinion, Leopold not used Vicissitude, but shaped reality with his insane mind fueled by Hazimel's 4th Gen power.

        While Leopold slept, Hesha Ruhadze took the Eye and left. Leopold woke up to find his "vision" was gone and began looking for it. Fortunately for him, he retained some of the powers of the ancient artifact and left for New York.
        This in my humble oppinion further supports, it was reality alteration, not vicissitude
