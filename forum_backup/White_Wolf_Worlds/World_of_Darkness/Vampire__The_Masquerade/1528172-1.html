                                        Some kind of blood magic makes more sense for the Ministry than Protean, really. But the problem there is how much Blood Sorcery has become overly focused on blood in V5.

                                        Obtenebration would probably also have made more sense as a general blood sorcery path of shadows, if Blood Sorcery hadn't become so narrow. That way, Abyss Mysticism would just be another tradition of blood magic, like Akhu or Dur-An-Ki. You wouldn't need to force any parallels between the Abyss and the Underworld, unless you said all blood magic came from the same source (which you shouldn't).

                                        If you limit a character's blood magic paths and rituals to their tradition's themes, you could even fold in Necromancy too. The difference would be that a necromancer would have to take paths that relate to their themes of death, decay and the Underworld, but there might be a few "crossover" paths that could be taken by multiple traditions (e.g., Hands of Destruction could be flavoured as fire for elementalists and kolduns/entropy or ageing for necromancers/wrath of the gods for ashippu/corrosive shadows for Abyss Mystics, etc).

                                        This would work especially well for things like Akhu and Wanga, which always sort of crossed the boundaries between Thaumaturgy and Necromancy, anyway. Suddenly, they wouldn't need Thaumaturgy paths that replicated Necromancy powers, because those traditions would just get access to the same paths (filtered through the appropriate cultural lens).

                                        You'd cut down a lot of the redundancy, you wouldn't need to force connections between things that don't need them, and each strand of blood magic could be different again. You can also build limitations into magic as a default, because each blood magician can only learn magic that fits into their worldview.

                                        Banu Haqim might get a list of paths that favour combat, demon-hunting and judgment, while the Lasombra might get a list that focuses on primordial darkness, corruption and extradimensional spaces (e.g., putting people in shadow prisons or travelling through shadows). If you want a weird or unique path, you have to justify how it fits your "paradigm", and you may need to adjust it with your Storyteller to make it a better fit.

                                        It works better to stop blood sorcerers being good at everything, too.
                                        ​​​​​
                                        ​​​​​​Getting back to the core topic, though: for me, VTR2e's approach is best. Streamlined but broad core Disciplines + Devotions seems the best way to handle it. The pick and mix approach increases decision paralysis and creates trap builds, by its very nature.
