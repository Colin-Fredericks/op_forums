    [V5] What breaks if...

    11-03-2021, 11:35 AM
    We've started a V5 Chronicle now that there's STV support for the new edition (meaning there are more ways to incorporate the things we want). At the moment we're trying to give it a fair shot by playing RAW for several sessions before we move into homebrew territory.

    So, at the moment, I'm just noting the things that are problems (or that might escalate into problems) and thinking about how we might address them.

    We're the kind of table that rolls frequently (if it has a dice pool in the book, then we think it should be rolled), so obviously that impacts things that don't work as well if you don't Take Half a lot.

    The things below might be used in isolation or in combination, so I'm looking for things that break the game or have interactions I may not have foreseen.

    With that in mind, what breaks if...

    1. ...instead of rouse check re-rolls (which have always felt clunky to us), you just ignore rouse costs on those Discipline levels instead? We roll enough for Messy Crits and Bestial Failures to be a thing anyway, and mostly just simplify those by adding +1 Hunger when nothing else is appropriate, so it's not as if Hunger won't ever go up. It will also make elders formidable, which seems appropriate, as they'll have free licence to use their Disciplines without cost at the highest levels of BP.

    If the above is too lenient, we could use the suggested rule from the book to only count every other rouse check. For re-rolls, just halve the rouse cost and round down before applying. I suspect you could track this easily using the slash and cross system (slash a Hunger box if the first rouse check was called for; cross it out if the second was called for). You'd then only roll Hunger Dice equal to the boxes fully X'd out.

    2. ...instead of the complicated Blood Potency tables, there was an easily remembered formula for everything. Is it game-breaking if you just say that your rate of healing, Blood Surge, Discipline dice bonus and Bane Severity are all exactly equal to your Blood Potency? The only exception would need to be rouse re-rolls, at half your BP, rounded up; and Thin Bloods, who get a minimum rating of 1 for healing and Blood Surge.

    3. ...instead of human-only Touchstones, you can pick non-mortal Touchstones at the cost of -1 Humanity at the start of play (for each non-human Touchstone). Is that too harsh a trade-off?

    4. ...instead of mandatory Touchstones and Convictions, you make them Backgrounds that mitigate Humanity loss, but you otherwise use the Humanity and Paths from V20 with the V5 Stains and Remorse system.

    5. ...Hunger could be applied in two ways? Let's call these Gnawing and Goading Hunger. Gnawing Hunger eats up your regular dice pool in the way we're familiar with, and represents the Beast impinging upon activities that do not directly feed its needs.

    Goading Hunger, though, is a bit more seductive. It's the Beast increasing your power to undertake the things it wants you to do: violence, feeding, hunting, seduction, etc. On these rolls, you add your Hunger Dice, instead of replacing standard dice. This represents the Beast encouraging your behaviour and giving you a greater dice pool (with the increased risk of Messy Criticals that creates).

    6. ...we say that Bestial Failures are only when you fail a roll with Gnawing Hunger and at least one Hunger Die comes up with a 1. Similarly, Messy Criticals only occur if you roll a critical on a roll with Goading Hunger Dice, rather than with Gnawing Hunger Dice.
    This makes these rarer. You could make it even rarer by saying you need to roll more successes overall on such a roll than your Composure (and still get a 10 on a Hunger Dice). That reflects 'losing control' and overachieving because of the Beast's goading.

    7. ...Resonance only granted Willpower. If your victim's Resonance fits your current Ambition or Desire, you regain 1wp for every Hunger slaked.

    8. ...you replaced Blood Surge with something a bit more deadly. Instead, you rouse the blood and can take any number of extra Goading Hunger Dice (up to a maximum bonus of twice your Hunger) for the next roll. This could lead to quite epic actions at the risk of loss of control.

    9. ...keeping the usual Blood Surge mechanics, you instead let the bonus apply to a single Attribute for a whole scene. You can buff multiple Attributes this way.

    10. ...Bane Severity were replaced by the VTR method (e.g., based off Humanity).

                          My take on this is as yet untested, but based on general observations:

                          Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
                          1. ...instead of rouse check re-rolls (which have always felt clunky to us), you just ignore rouse costs on those Discipline levels instead?
                          That depends on how the characters will use their Disciplines. At first nothing breaks, but ignoring any cost is like putting a spamming button on Disciplines.

                          You just have to take that into account. If it seems like a problem, then trading double rouse for 2 or 3 uses before rousing is enough.

                          Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
                          2. ...instead of the complicated Blood Potency tables, there was an easily remembered formula for everything.
                          Its easier to transcribe the table somewhere. But that aside, it actually is an easily remembered formula! Te only difference is that it isn't Thing = BP, but Thing = ½ BP. Half of those things round up, half round down, so you have half of the effects increasing at even BP and half increasing at odd BP.

                          Making it Thing = BP will make a far bigger increase in power from one BP to another, and that's especially relevant as now you have a range of BP inside each Generation.

                          Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
                          3. ...instead of human-only Touchstones, you can pick non-mortal Touchstones at the cost of -1 Humanity at the start of play (for each non-human Touchstone). Is that too harsh a trade-off?
                          Definitely too harsh a trade-off.

                          If you decide that non-human Touchstones work for you, just do it. If you decide your game doesn't need Touchstones just do it.

                          As a mechanic they're thematic, not necessary, so nothing at all breaks when you change them to be worth a penalty.

                          Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
                          4. ...instead of mandatory Touchstones and Convictions, you make them Backgrounds that mitigate Humanity loss
                          It's already what they are.

                          V5's Humanity is an excellent rule with a poor presentation that fails to easily describe how it works.

                          What makes or brakes it is your choice of Chronicle Tenets. It is perfectly possible to determine that your Tenets are the Hierarchy of Sins of any Path you want, and it is also perfectly fine to decide that each character have its own Tenets instead.

                          But by doing so you may be loosing on actual interesting possibilities to better define your chronicle, as now you can add or subtract "sins" to fit your themes.

                          Convictions are exactly like Merits that help you to keep your Humanity, nothing else, and they're not so much Mandatory. I think you should keep them as is, as 1 to 3 is a pretty fair number, but by all means you can decide to create a Merit if you want a player to have more. A player that don't want any Conviction is also perfectly fine.

                          Touchstones exist to force characters to interact with mortals in their keeping of their Humanity and color it by their Convictions. They don't help directly, just anchor Convictions, but it is just a thematic rule that you can change freely.

                          On my side, I think it is kind of self-evident why they are tied to Humans, but changing it to anything else is pretty common. The big thing with them is how you define Harm and Destruction. I think that ignoring the RAW on that part and customizing those definitions for each case is the best way to get the most out of them for the least trouble with rules.

                          Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
                          5. ...Hunger could be applied in two ways?
                          That would change a lot of what Hunger have always been in the game, and change a lot of rules.

                          So my take on this is that no one can really predict how those options will affect your game. This is a very big homebrew.

                          Instead of applying them as you thought them right away, experiment first with the game as is, then add those options with care. They'll change many rules and rules interactions, and increase the bookkeeping in your game.

                          Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
                          6. ...we say that Bestial Failures are only when you fail a roll with Gnawing Hunger and at least one Hunger Die comes up with a 1.
                          My first impression is the game counts on dice-generated complications to reduce other rules for triggering stuff. I may be wrong, but I would try it out before concluding that they happen too frequently.

                          Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
                          7. ...Resonance only granted Willpower.
                          Resonance is another thematic rule. It exists for players to have more to interact with in their feeding. Simplifying is perfectly valid, but I think they add a nice touch as they are, so I wouldn't recommend doing so.

                          Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
                          8. ...you replaced Blood Surge with something a bit more deadly.
                          With the current rules I think their deadliness is on-spot. If you change the Attribute maximum, then it may be worth it to increase their potency just a little.

                          Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
                          9. ...keeping the usual Blood Surge mechanics, you instead let the bonus apply to a single Attribute for a whole scene. You can buff multiple Attributes this way.
                          That seems reasonable to me. I like the rules, I don't like their duration. But you'll have to take a look at Disciplines just to be sure this is balanced with dedicated powers.

                          Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
                          10. ...Bane Severity were replaced by the VTR method (e.g., based off Humanity).
                          Nothing breaks, but themes change. As is, Bane Severity acts as a Price of Power theme, which reinforces the difference between Neonates and the powerful Elders.

                          Making it be based on Humanity will change the theme to Risks of Evil, and creates a new mechanic incentive to stay at High Humanity. You may go another route and, following closer to original but unsupported themes in VtM, make it related to age, giving it a Price of Immortality twist.

                          Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
                          11. ...BP acts as a cap on traits (minimum 5 for all). So elders can have superhuman traits again.
                          I'm sincerely not sure. On one hand I'm as taken aback as everyone else by their lack of superhuman traits. On the other hand I'm not sure it was actually a good idea to begin with.

                          One way or another the current scheme keeps elders more manageable in terms of numbers/dice, and I think mechanics like double Hunger rolls are better than just moar dice.

                          Can't say it isn't a worthy idea to try out, but I think that looking for other ways to highlight their greater power may have an actually better pay off.

                          Another ever present question is how much those characters can still be meaningfully interacted with from a rules perspective. Trait Inflation makes it hard to believe in some events in the lore where Kindred of less age and higher generation explored elders' weaknesses as the rules actually make it too hard anyway.

                                          Ah, so I think I haven't explained Gnawing versus Goading Hunger properly. The idea is that you still only have one Hunger rating, it's just that you replace dice on some rolls (e.g., those that require concentration) and you add it to others (e.g., those that make you deadly). It's exactly the same accounting method. What's different is the ST might say, "Add your Hunger Dice this time" instead of "Replace black dice with red dice."

                                          My thinking here is that, RAW, if you have a dice pool made up entirely of Hunger Dice, you can't re-roll them, and in moments of crisis this makes you less likely to succeed at some of the things the Beast would seem to want to encourage, such as murdering your enemies or hunting for prey.

                                          Paired with VTR2e's Critical rules (so a Critical is 5+ successes), this would then increase your chance of Messy Crits on those rolls but make you more dangerous overall.

                                          Goading Hunger would probably replace Blood Surge and the Discipline dice pool bonus, though. Having all three would probably be unnecessary. Basically, Goading Hunger is a bit more like taking Shadow Dice in Wraith.

                                          Overall, I'm not worried about Unbeatable Elder Syndrome. I actually think that's a plus. But if I went with a boost in one area, I wouldn't add in all the others.

                                          That leads me to another idea, though. We could just allow re-rolls of Hunger Dice, provided they're not "fixed" with a 1 or a 10 already. That would get around the issue of a character who rolls all Hunger Dice on an attack and therefore can't re-roll, thus failing at something the Beast would want them to do. It also means you increase your chance of a Messy Crit or Bestial Failure on those rolls.

                                          In regards to rouse checks and things, I think we want to keep hunting to once per session. We will have to think of some way to limit spiralling Hunger in the end, I think. But I'll see how things pan out.

                                          As for Resonance: I'm not afraid of the 'mana bar'. It's neutral as a game mechanic, IMO, and the way people talk about it can definitely be quite snobby.
