                                    It was a very interesting thread! I have definitely come around to some of the ideas that you suggested, though still of a different mind about other things. Glad to hear that other people enjoyed the thread as well! I haven't updated it in a while because my ideas have mostly just been sitting on the back-burner, being idly pondered rather than moving in concrete directions. But I may make a post sharing my latest ideas some time soon. It's been a while since I've looked at the thread, tbh, so I'll need to remind myself what the last update was about, haha.

                                    The whole thing with Misery that I posted about above actually got me wondering two things about Siegfried:

                                    1) His relationship with Julie Foster. Off the top of my head, I can't remember if the text actually comes out and says whether or not they're lovers. But whether or not they are, how does Siegfried having a thing for picking up fiery young women every few decades or so affect their relationship? It is pretty clear about the fact that Julie is in love with him, so I wonder how she might respond to such things.

                                    2) Siegfried's childer. I don't think any previous sources really explore the idea, but a vampire as old as him has probably Embraced at least a few people over the centuries. Misery's story gives us at least one "type" of relationship he would have with potential childer, but I also figure that he'd be likely to Embrace warrior-types, more like himself, with a more utilitarian attitude towards their potential uses.
