                                  Disclaimer, just my opinion. Perhaps it qualifies as edition warring, I find these things difficult to judge myself, so I'll let moderation decide:

                                  Even if it's just putting V5 information in its own section on the wiki, separate from the information for other editions, I feel like some form of official gap between 1e - 20th and the new games needs to be put in by this point. Like the Ravnos and Sabbat are wholesale different between 20th to 5th. (Not a WoD specific issue of mine. It's a feeling I actually got looking at the Forgotten Realms wiki sometimes. It's been a while since I've been there, but edition updates sometimes left lore and mechanics all over the place and pre-3.5e stuff just didn't seem to be there sometimes. (This page is still using 3.5, that one's 4th! Wait, is this one 5th?) V20 also had slightly different lore to previous editions (The Rossellini as a revenant family, a great chunk of DAV20, etc), and I would support having that in its own edition specific section too.)

                                  Let's leave aside one's personal opinions on whether V5 is a legitimate update or a reboot. Even if it is a fifth edition of Vampire: the Masquerade it's part of a universe that isn't the 5th edition of the World of Darkness. It could be the perfect continuation ever but it's part of a whole with gamelines that, despite the name, are not continuations of Hunter: the Reckoning and Werewolf: the Apocalypse. What, VtM fans can have more of their setting, but fuck everyone else? Overwriting VtM's wiki lore is (supposedly) legitimate in such a case (and yet still very irritating if you want the older lore), because it's a continuation, but H"5" and W"5" are going to need their own pages (or else you're just wholesale pushing out a game people still love and play to be replaced by another different one, which is... not going to go well.) This is a fucking mess that only looks to get messier, and I feel some kind of boundaries need to be put somewhere.

                                                  Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

                                                  Frankly, whether it is or not full of retcons, it isn't considered such or a reboot by Paradox.

                                                  It's continued the official continuation of the line and lore.

                                                  It's not really a matter of opinion, it's the update and official and that's final.
                                                  I don't intend to deny V5 is a continuation, just acknowledging that others do and putting that aside. My own problem arises when I start getting a literal headache because the rest of the WoD so far, by official declaration, isn't.

                                                  Editions change things and tweak things. They can be placed (and are placed) in continuity, but looking at them individually all editions 1e through to V5 are like worlds unto themselves if taken in isolation. There's a reason some people favour one edition over the others, be it Revised vs V20 edition wars, sticking to 2e or even the occasional player who doesn't acknowledge that there was anything past the first edition because it stops being the exact same setting with every update. It doesn't disqualify what came after to dislike it. (I mean, over the editions massive changes have been made, such as "There are Chinese ventrue" -> "Lasombra have been playing king maker in Imperial China since forever" -> "Chinese vampires are descended from an undead chaos god, the Japanese clans adapt to their environment and are descended from the kami Hiruko" -> "Kindred of the East is its own splat and the VtM regulars have never had a foothold there, ever" -> "We have never heard of this "wan gui" you speak of.")

                                                  While V5 is a massive overhaul, I won't reject it as a form of VtM just because of that, however jarring these changes might be. But WoD gamelines usually come together. WtA was WtA from 1 e - 20th, VtM VtM from 1e - 20th. Whatever changes they underwent, big or small, however disconnected the gamelines are from each other, they usually "went together" as part of a whole WoD edition. (Hunter, admittedly only had a gameline in Revised, but I still think using its name for something else was rude and KotE, in the same boat, no longer exists at this point. Not a problem for me, but still unfortunate for the fans and has a knock-on effect on the wider WoD.) WoD5 is a jumble of continuation and reboot and as a whole now confuses the shit out of me and is bound to lead to arguing and confusion.

                                                  Having some form of organisation won't stop people screaming at each other, but at least will make it easier to navigate and find specifically what you're looking for, and help keep editions stepping on each others toes. Nor does V5 need singling out for this treatment, since all editions vary, though it will likely need more space due to a bigger amount of mechanical changes (as such its own wiki is a good idea) and any crossover content is going to bring problems because W5 is not the setting that would've crossed over with V20 and previous. Maybe it doesn't matter in the long run, but it is a paradox that make my head hurt to think about.

                                                  It's also weird to compare it to V20 because, as much as its popular, V20 REMOVED ALL THE METAPLOT until Beckett's Jyhad Diary.

                                                  You can't comment on how big of a retcon the Ravnos have gotten in V5 without commenting on V20 not having the Clan destroyed.
                                                  V20 left it up to players if the Week of Nightmares was canon in their games or not, same with the various curses placed on the Children of Haqim. I didn't consider "massive metaplot events may or may not have occurred according to your preferences" on the same level as "this clan now has a totally different weakness, a completely different discipline spread, and their signature discipline is now a different thing (Chimestry used to affect tech and had vague Changeling connections, now it's just Obfuscate)." YMMV.
