Malkav has likely become the Madness Network, a psychic link connecting virtually all members of Clan Malkavian.       Malakai - Some scriptures (Beckett's Jyhad Diary, Gehenna and in ​The Black Hand: A Guide to the Tal'Mahe'Ra) claim he had a twin sister, Malakai, Black Hand scriptures say Malakai is imprisoned deep within the Shadowlands because she is so mad and powerful that she warps reality around her. (Malakai is mentioned in the Shaal Fragment as an antediluvian who seeks "peace" during the first Gehenna. However, since Arikel is said to seek unity, the peace Malakai seeks may not be diplomatic in nature. Rather, she could have sought peace by journeying to the Shadowlands or entering Torpor.)
      Beckett's Jyhad Diary suggest, she sleeps under Jerusalem

      Nahema - Chaining the Beast mentions one branch of the Path of Lilith, the Path of Awakening. The followers of it believes that Arikel and Malkav were twins, children of Lilith, and that they had a younger sister, Nahema, who also received the Embrace. According to legend, Nahema descended into Hell, but never lost touch with her siblings, her spirit forever bound to them and their childer – it is Nahema's spirit that whispers in the Malkavian Madness Network, that burns in the hearts of the Toreador. Some of her worshippers call her the Dark Muse, the Anti-Muse, and the Demon Queen of Madness, and she is said to be patron saint of serial killers and torturers

      The Eldest - masquerading as Lugoj, let his body be transported to a hidden crypt beneath what would become New York City by Zantosa Revenants that knew of his ruse. There, it entered a meditative state to reach Metamorphosis. In the process, its body dissolved to a puddle of slime, then transformed into fungi, then into plants and every other living creature in existence, exploring the infinitude of life by assuming its shape. It started to infuse itself into other creatures, beginning with plants and animals, to master them and unite all Creation into itself. The Eldest appears to have had more trouble than it should in its quest, lost in itself looking for an answer it could not find, riven by the loss of its true bonds to the Earth. Eventually, sometime in the early 21st century, it found its way back to itself, then left New York to seek its siblings' counsel

      Haqim - The last time anyone has seen the Banu Haqim Antediluvian was in the British Isles in 121 CE, where he was engaged in philosophical conversations with the Ventrue Prince of London, Mithras - which is interesting by itself, since Mithras would be diablerized in 1996 by one of Haqim's own descendants...​

      Arikel and Ishtar - the Toreador Antediluvians are interesting.
      As for Arikel, the Toreador scholars claim that their clan's Antediluvian was killed in the time of Enoch by the plotting of Absimiliard and Set, who supposedly cut out and ate her heart. Yet, there are records that indicate that she had not experienced Final Death, and had awakened from Torpor around 260 BCE, acting as an advisor to Prince Alchias.
      Ishtar tried to take the Tablet of Destiny from either Ennoia or either Enkidu Outraged by this action, Ennoia set out to kill the Toreador Antediluvian and tracked Ishtar to Sumer. Ishtar fled but not before unleashing upon them some great monster known as the Bull of Heaven, while she escaped to Crete. In Crete, Ishtar resided with King Minos, who desired and demanded her gift. Initially, she refused, but then compromised to embrace his son. Unworthy of the embrace, the son fell into the clutches of the Beast. His erratic behavior had him imprisoned by his father Minos, in what was an underground maze.
      Both of the Toreador Antediluvians went off the map, but were in Greece

      Dracian was the name of a thief that was Embraced by Irad in order to spy on the third generation. Irad suspected the other third generation neonates of plotting against the three members of the second generation and their sire. Dracian, for his part, took to the plot like a fish to water. Some sources claim Dracian to be a member of the second Generation, Embraced by Caine himself during his wanderings and the sire of the Ravnos Antediluvian and Ennoia, who betrayed him and was cast out of the First City as a result.

      And finally
      Who is/was Typhon?
      According to Gehenna, one of the 23 Antediluvians (p. 154)

      Thank you for coming

      But the answer is a bit... more complicated, considering Gehenna: Time of Judgment is a bit soft-canon, more like a What if...? and ideas than hard facts

      What we know about him is from the Isis Fragments (World of Darkness: Mummy Second Edition, p. 16-21)
      In that he was stranger that came on the night of a full moon, presenting himself at their court and beseeching an audience with Osiris.
      It is said that Typhon was cold, not only in manner but in body, and although his beauty was breathtaking, the only emotion that crossed his fine, pale features was a cruel humor.
      In Osiris' court, Typhon warned the ruler of Egypt about the imminent conflict that would sweep throughout the land if they were unprepared.
      For four moons this pattern continued, as Typhon would reappear on the eve of a full moon to speak privately with Osiris, and every time he did so, the king grew filled with quiet dread. On the morning of Typhon's fourth and final departure, Osiris was found ill and near death, pale and bloodless on his bedchamber floor. They later discovered that he had been embraced as one of the kindred.

      Now Osiris is belived to be a 4th generation and in Cairo by Night it was said:
      Osiris was reported to manifest the "vigor of a thousand bulls" (likely a reference to either Fortitude or Potence), the "movement of the desert winds" (Celerity) and the "sudden irresistible power of his speech" (likely referring to Dominate or Presence ) after his Embrace
