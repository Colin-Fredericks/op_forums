    Originally posted by Grumpy RPG Reviews View Post
    I think the Drowned Legacies are more interesting when they have no easy connection to the known clans. So making the Cipactli just a Settite bloodline would be dull. But I can see using the known disciplines for the sake of mechanical simplicity.
    I Agree, but unless they're totally different species than the KotE, then this is always a possibility and something that people will always be looking for. And to be fair to myself, i did say i didn't think it was anything as simple as that. It was worth it to make the Toreador/Hat/Nails remark.

    Having re-read Nerissa in CbN today, we know they'll use classic disciplines (or at least things that are similar enough to use those names as a holding place for now), and knowing what we know about the flexible nature of Disciplines in V5 (Protean/Serpentis/Vicissitude have merged for example), just the Headline names only gives us an indication of the types of powers they have.

    We've had a totally separate species of Vampire (KotE), and also variations on the main clans where they diverged a significant period of time ago (The African Legacies). A possible theory is they'll be something in a similar vein to cainites, but even further removed than the legacies, perhaps something that predates the antediluvians. Now, could it be the second generation survived in some way, or other antediluvians apart from the classic 13 that escaped in the flood and made a new base (Calling themselves drowned certainly has some interesting connotations when we consider the biblical deluge). Could it predate even them? Nerissa is a Lilith cultist, perhaps the drowned legacies don't have a Dark Father in Caine, perhaps their ultimate progenitor was Lilith? she taught caine and there are so many conflicting stories about her, she could just as easily share what amounts to the same curse. If this is the case, then we'd have a species that share common DNA to Cainites, but branched out much further up the tree - Lillian Marsupials to Cainite Mammals as a more real world example.

    Will we ever know for sure? whilst i look forward to knowing more detail, part of the fun is the speculation. As long as their is a definitive answer (even if it's open to interpretation) that is hinted at then i'm happy.

                                            Some kind of Drowned Legacy appears in the manuscript for Chicago by Night 5th. Actually, there might be more than one type of Legacy present - the text is vague. In any case, what do you suppose it is, assuming it is a single Legacy? I am inclined to say either a baykok or a mishipeshu.

                                            Baykok: It is said to fly through the forests of the Great Lakes region. The cries of Baykok are also described as being shrill. Described as "Death" in The Song of Hiawatha, it is said to appear as an extremely emaciated skeleton-like figure, with thin translucent skin and glowing red points for eyes.

                                            Mishipeshu: To the Algonquins, the underwater panther (mishipeshu) was the most powerful underworld being. It has the head and paws of a giant cat but is covered in scales and has dagger-like spikes running along its back and tail.

                                            I'm leaning towards the bayok, which were also called the pakak. But it could be the mishipeshu, given the tendancy to sleep at the bottom of Lake Michigan.
