    Originally posted by monteparnas View Post
    The point is that this is a change in how most fans want the Technocracy to be.

    Original Techy works on the assumption that Fascism = Modernity, and is a symbol of Luddite ideals the fandom never bought as much as the authors and shied from it even more with time. So much so that this movement started already in 2nd ed.

    Like it or not, most people don't see very well the idea of the Technocracy as representing such things all the while it defends proper and safe science and collective development (because they do, while the Traditions focus on individual development), so changing focus to the Nefandi in this regard is also an old strategy already (implied, for example, in the Syndicate R&D shenanigans).

    Personally I think they should develop more the role of Threat Null, that is already a mix of parody and reference to Old Techy, but in a way that makes more sense than just proper academy and formal institutions being universally tools of evil and our future depending on our timely salvation by mediums and deluded charlatans.

    I think we could even have a very interesting game if the focus changed to both Trads and Techy fending off a renewed War between Threat Null and a similar Ultra-Luddite Traditions-equivalent. In this sense I do agree that focusing on the Nefandi is kind of dull and too much on the steps of Werewolf.
    I always felt this was an issue with the fundamental conception of the Technocracy and I feel like there were two ways of resolving it. The problem with the Technocracy is it is a World of Darkness and if you had people who believed in racial equality, science, and the promotion of social justice among the poor...THE WORLD WOULD NOT LOOK LIKE IT DOES NOW. The Technocracy can either rule the world behind the scenes, be effective, or be progressive but it certainly cannot be all three.

    The solution is one of two things:

    1. The Technocracy is supportive of racial heirarchies, looter capitalism, disinformation, and a boot on the face of humanity forever.

    2. The Technocracy is actually a bunch of progressives with guns but not nearly as powerful.

    2# is fine...if they're the protagonists.

    But we I supported 1# because I believe the Technocracy would always side with big oil over clean energy because I'm the anarchist Goth market for the classic WOD.

    It's weirdly a lot less of a disconnect in V:TM and Werewolf because "The Man" is objectively evil there.

    Anyway, I'm not going to whine about it. They made their choice and you just go with the flow. Sometimes you like changes to a setting, sometimes you don't.

                            A friend of mine had the view that Mage: The Ascension's critics and supporters on the issue were also talking past one another as the two people weren't hearing the other's arguments.                             The critics of the Traditions vs. Technocracy conflict tend to see the matter as having a strong Luddite bias with conspiratorial overtones. I've once seen the Traditions described as the "lunatic religious fringe, anti-vaxxers, and alternative medicine nuts worried about the Democrats." They think the work takes a strong anti-Modernist tone to it.

                            The proponents of the Traditions vs. Technocracy conflict see it as indigineous people as well as underground persecuted peoples versus fascist corporate Far Right authoritarianism. The Traditions aren't anti-science to them because the "true scientists" are the Sons of Ether and Virtual Adepts representing open source free research versus Big Pharma/Big Tech.

                            It's not that the two sides disagree, it's that the two sides are not even having the same argument. In W:TA the werewolves ARE aggressively Luddite ecoterrorists but Pentex is an embodiment of corporate fascism and arguably religious environmental disdain ("we're the chosen so we don't care what happens to the planet and we'll usher it to its doom"--which is a stretch but not an utter one). So you know that the werewolves are incredibly flawed but still on the right side of things. No one is arguing Pentex isn't the bad guys after all.

                            Both sides above are angry and full of venom at the Far Right and their effect on the world but they each think the OTHER side of the Technocracy vs. Traditions vibe is the Far Right.

                                                        The Technocracy vs Traditions divide is a Mainstream vs Counterculture conflict: it's in clear debt to the great cultural and political trends of the 70s and 80s: it remained long relevant, as anti-globalization movements were an important source of politicization until the recent crisis.

                                                        Now that anti-vaxxers, racists and fundamentalists are joined in a new political bloc fighting against both left-wing countercultures and the mainstream neoliberal order, it seems of course outdated.

                                                        I think both Traditions and Technocracy would be less interesting if their respective bad and good angles were erased, but some sort of shake up could be imagined.

                                                        If the 90s and 2000s had people fighting against "the End of History" and the one-and-only Truth, now we have the problem that there's no coherent and accepted Truth, and the world is seemingly going to hell because no one can really agree on how to act.

                                                        I could imagine a setting shake up where Consensus is falling apart, with a growing number of spontaneous Awakenings that lead unprepared people to Quiet.

                                                        Traditions and Conventions could split up between:

                                                        -Those who think the Earth is ultimately doomed, and try to save what is worth, by facing the Umbra (a libertarian "right"), ranging from Syndicate and Void Engineers to Virtual Adepts
                                                        -Those who wish to retake control by forcefully creating a new Consensus (an authoritarian "centre"), with Hermetics, Euthanatos, NWO and Batini, perhaps the Templars
                                                        -Those who wish to protect their communities and personal identities (a libertarian "left"), from Dreamspeakers to Disparates
                                                        -You could also have a neutral faction that tries to heal the rifts of reality, led by Unitarian Choristers and benign Progenitors.

                                                        With the Nephandi as the common threat, and the game emphasizing the agency of Orphans.

                                                        It's a very rough draft, but you could play up on the growing tensions between Traditions and the Rogue Council, or the looming Techcnoratic Civil War.
