    How would you roleplay gaining your first dot on a Path of Enlightenment?

    02-22-2022, 11:35 PM
    Let's say you're in a Sabbat game. You started out as a shovelhead, got recognized as True Sabbat after several bloody victories, and have been roleplaying transitioning from Humanity to a Path. You make your roll and, BOOYAH, you succeed and get your first dot in a Path. How would you roleplay that character having achieved this precarious state? Triumphant? Twitchily mumbling the teachings of the Path like an honorary Malkavian? Some other way?

                      To force oneself to discard that last shred of human instinct requires "utter perversion and horrific acts" that would give pause to even a gibbering, barely sapient violent wreck that can remorselessly torture, violate and kill as much as it wants without any more thought than flicking a light switch.

                      Said horrific acts, in my take on it, must be in service of the ethos of the path one seeks to follow. For a split second as humanity is lost, the vampire is finally, utterly free of any desire to care about anything but feed, kill, feed, hide, feed, flee, feed, feed, feed, and the beast is about to have a party over here. In this moment, as everything washes away in the last frenzy, the vampire glimpses a brief flash of "Truth" in its actions and is anchored. It wants to know more. It wants more in existence than just the blood. It wants purpose. It wants Enlightenment.

                      For paths that use Instinct, this is the first time a vampire experiences riding the wave - moving with the beast instead of being drowned by it as it goes ahead with said party. For paths that use self-control, the vampire reasserts its will and the frenzy stops dead.

                      An observer also wouldn't notice a great deal of difference in the vampire's personality at this point. At one dot it's still a twitchy death-machine that only just bothers to pay lip service to its own sapience out of a vague desire to care about something it has the barest understanding of. It's just that before that was human instincts and now it's a steadily growing blind fanatical zeal. It's an upgrade from only caring about kill, feed to only caring about kill, feed, Understand.
                      At this point the next step is stabilising oneself by seeking out a deeper understanding of the rest of the hierarchy, particularly the next dot, so as to fufill your new glorious purpose in undeath. And (mechanics wise) racking up xp to spend on your path rating in said pursuit. Although the training preparing you for the path and the act of resisting wassail has probably lead to a fair amount of xp gained in preperation for this moment, so it shouldn't take too long to pull yourself together

                          Hard question. Someone at a Humanity rating of levels 1-3 going to a Path rating of 1 is actually degenerating and less able to control the Beast (or at best makes no improvement). So there's no change in that. It's something else we need to concentrate on - the motivation.

                          My instinct is to go to classic vampire movies (pre-Anne Rice and other modern/sympathetic takes) as inspiration. In those, the victims who are turned are heavily controlled by their lust of blood, can maintain their pre-turn personalities only briefly and only when attempting to fool the people who knew them when alive, and act psychologically dependent on their "master" (the vampire who turned them - but for Sabbat this could be towards the entire pack with which they have Vinculum). The vampires want to please their new master, the person who is teaching them how to survive in this new state - one which now has the Beast, and it is heavily influencing them, and their previous personality is heavily destroyed and damaged.

                          Previously when they were still acting under some aegis of Humanity, the Sabbat pack was mocking and degrading them, but providing them the initial basics of their new Path. The new vampire, losing control to the Beast (because their Sabbat pack is not supporting their current path of Humanity), can either let the Beast overwhelm them - which they should realize will lead to their own destruction at the hands of the pack which they love/trust because of the Vinculum - or can switch over to some new Path and begin to get support/affirmation of the Sabbat pack members for the first time. Only much later when they actually improve on their Path Rating, do they actually begin to understand it as a real philosophical discipline.

                          To me, that sounds a lot like cult indoctrination. While they welcome you in the beginning (creation rites), once they isolate you from your old friends and family (this is immediate because they abducted you) they begin to break you down from abuse (creation rites for shovelheads and then whatever torment they subject you to as False Sabbat) and then love bomb you when you do the things that please them. So in essence, its an abuse victim becoming dependent on their abuser and starting the first stage of their programming into the cult (Path) that they do not know much about. It's a desperate reaction to abuse as the victim becomes dependent on their abuser. It's when their final resistance (attachment to Humanity) finally breaks, and they adopt the most base teachings (the easiest to handle) of the Path to win approval.

                          Only then do they begin the long process of tutelage to properly instruct them on the Path. And given the things the Sabbat do, a lot of Sabbat vampires likely don't advance much, don't advance quickly even if they do eventually master a Path, or get destroyed before any real headway is made.

                          = =
                          Now in a non-Sabbat game where a vampire, not belonging to a pack, converts to a Path of Enlightenment out of some kind of ideologically, religious, or other dogmatic reasons that they willingly adopt despite being at a higher point of humanity, then it might be more like a traditional conversion story. They probably studied the Path sufficiently ahead of time and considered it intellectually, that they are capable of truly understanding the Path and can proceed on its development on their own, aided perhaps by lore they collected or a trusted Mentor (not an abuser/victim relationship, but a more positive teacher/disciple relationship). In that case, the story would be more like them adapting to a steep decline in Path ratings that initially unsettles them, but they should be able to rather quickly build their Path rating up. In this case, the real crux is what made them finally "convert" which would more likely be some kind of religious epiphany or ideologically conversion decision.

                          I imagine this is how most Independent clan vampires start the Paths most associated with them, but the long term teaching is what happens before the human is embrace - while they are human or ghoul. The Setites probably use a more classic cult abuse/love bomb indoctrination so they can create a pool of humans who can later be embraced and begin the Path of Typhon. But the Giovanni selected for the Proxy Kiss and ghouldom would be more like a mentoring program for the ambitious or favored future necromancers who can best make use of the Path of Bones. The Assamites would probably be somewhere in between. But in all cases, the idea would be to provide the intellectual development of the Path first to a non-vampire so the vampire can quickly adjust and build their Path rating quickly.

                          = =
                          I'm sure there can be other styles, but I'd probably begin with these.
