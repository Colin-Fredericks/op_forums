    Power to know all languages

    07-15-2023, 04:01 AM
    Hello everyone!

    I'd like some advice. I'm trying to write a 6 dots Serpentis power for Dark Ages called "Wisdom of the Serpent" which basically lets the character know all languages, tapping into varied symbolism like the Serpent of Eden being a temptator from before the time of the fall of Babel's Tower and the division of languages, Set being a god of foreigners, two snakes being on Mercury's staff whilst Mercury is god of communication etc.
    I am wondering whether it's OP for 6 dots and whether werewolves, mages or others have a similar power and at how many dots. Also, I wonder if I need to add a notice excluding some specifically supernatural and secret languages and how to phrase that best to avoid confusion between what's included and what not.

    The origin of the power is that I'm drawing inspiration from Aspect of the God (Guide to the Low Clans), which gives you five forms with different functions, one being the ability to intuitively understand the language of the area where you are and pass for a local, but instead of 1 power granting 5 forms, I want a few powers granting an improved version of each of those 5. In this case the improvement is that I don't simply know the local language: I understand all languages, written and spoken, and I have -1 diff from all social rolls (cumulative with the same bonus given by Serpentis 1 in Dark Ages 20) on account of intuitive understanding of customs, body language and any other aspect of communication that is not simple language.
    The power is free to activate (like Serpentis 1) and can be always active, as I find it weird to know languages at one moment but not at another: what if I'm thinking about a poem in Akkadian and suddenly don't know what it means anymore? It just gets weird.
    If needed for balance and flavour, I thought of giving it the side effect that, despite your linguistic and social proficiency, once you learn this power, people subconsciously perceive you always as a foreigner, even if you're in your homeland or live somewhere for years, unless you're actively trying to pass for a local (like with a Performance roll and / or Mask of a Thousand Faces).

    Is this too much?

                  You could adjust the power by making it more conditional; I recently made a Path with a Level 3 power that allows to pick up new Abilities (including languages) through immersion that still requires XP, but reduces the cost.

                  For example, your Serpentis power could require another person present who has knowledge of the language and whose mind you borrow with the Discipline to use them as a proxy for 'understanding'. That would still allow easy conversation and reading/writing in an extant language, but for dead languages you would need someone who knows them. It also limits the scope to the vocabulary and literacy of the proxy - if they don't know a word, you don't either. And it provides a good reason why you would lose the understanding of the language once the power runs out.

                      The opening of the mouth ceremony (or ritual) was an ancient Egyptian ritual described in funerary texts such as the Pyramid Texts. From the Old Kingdom to the Roman Period, there is ample evidence of this ceremony, which was believed to give the deceased their fundamental senses to carry out tasks in the afterlife. - this served to restore the senses of the newly deceased (resident in spirit within the mummified body or a statue), so that he or she could once again breathe, speak, eat, drink, see, and hear.
