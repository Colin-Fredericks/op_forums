                                    When I read this thread I got an idea, I want to share:
                                    What if Lambach Ruthven is a disguise for Ionache, the 4th gen methuselah (PGtHC) and contemporary of Yorak and the Dracon, who was allegedly responsible for the construction of the Tzimisce Voivodate during the early middle ages and held the office of Voivode of Voivodes for the first time?
                                    I came up with this idea for the following reasons:
                                    1) Ionache disappeared right around the time Lambach was allegedly embraced (1000 CE), if I unterstand your research correctly. He seems to be quite forgotten around 1230, which seams a bit weird given his deeds and age.
                                    2) Lambachs rivalry with Triglav over the Embrace of Shaagra would be more understandable if he was in fact Ionache, whose construction of the Voivodate would nicely fit to the search for new-Leadership that marked Shaagras embrace (Cb-Tz-Rev).
                                    3) The family name Ruthven, that "Lord Ruthven" as their founder (as LotH put it) would indicate, always seemed a bit anachronistic to me for a medieaval or even older dynasty.
                                    I like the idea of Lambach as decadent and cowardly, which doesn't sit well with the architect of the Voivodate, I know. But maybe his disappearance was due to an event that changed him/his actions?

                                                Canon lore considering the Ruthvens is a hot mess.

                                                Clanbook Tzimisce Revised says Lambach was a 4th generation childe of the Antediluvian.

                                                Gehenna, Transylvania Chronicles, and at least a few other books say he was 5tth generation.

                                                Encyclopedia Vampirica calls him at 7th.

                                                Some sources say he was Tabak's childe (or was it sire?). Some say he was his broodmate. Beckett's Jyhad Diary calls into question whether he was even Vlad Tepes' sire at all.

                                                The idea that Lambach might have been another guise for Ionache is interesting. The latter's disappearance isn't explained anywhere.

                                                Ultimately though, I like Lambach as a coward. The setting has plenty of fiendish masterminds already. Lambach adds some needed variety and nicely illustrates how being of low generation isn't an inherent indicator of competence or badass-itude. Just luck.

                                                I also like the amount of contradictory information there is floating around about Lambach. Even for GMs who have a clear origin/generation in mind for him, misinformation is everywhere in the Jyhad and Kindred investigating the Tzimisce elder could be expected to get lost amidst that school of red herrings .

                                                Blood and Bourbon, my New Orleans-based Vampire chronicle.
