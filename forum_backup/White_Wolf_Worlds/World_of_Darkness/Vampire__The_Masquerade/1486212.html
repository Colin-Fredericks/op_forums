    Do you think the Tremere will ever have competition in the Camarilla?

    07-11-2022, 07:56 AM
    IMHO the Tremere are unlikely to ever have true competitors when it comes to Blood Magic. Now sure there's Hacktivist Thaumaturgy if the Digital Draculas decide to rejoin the Camarilla, but even then their biggest advantage is going to be that they possess knowledge of the Path of Technomancy, something that the Tremere also have. Now don't get me wrong, Hacktivist Thaumaturgy has amazing rituals, but there's very few of them and are practically useless without some sort of wifi connected device. Now compared to the many extremely useful rituals of Thaumaturgy, and the fact that most of them don't require a wifi connection, you begin to understand why the Tremere are a pillar of the Camarilla.

    It's the same issue with all the other Blood Magic Traditions; many of the rituals are good for what they were made for, but very few are going to be seen as useful to the Camarilla. And although the Tremere are seen as untrustworthy, do you really think the Camarilla is going to trust a vampire who comes from the Followers of Set, Tzimisce, or Assamites? The FoS are definitely going to try corrupting you, the Tzimisce have a clan culture of torture, and there was a reason the Assamites were cursed by the Tremere in the first place.

    So what do you think?

      It's worth remembering that in V5, the Tremere's Pyramid was shattered, with Vienna bombed and many of their ruling elders slaughtered. Whereas before the Tremere were one organization, now there's a lot more rivalry and jockying for position. So suddenly the Tremere are finding themselves in competition with other Tremere in a way that wasn't the case a couple of decades ago.

      Furthermore, the Banu Haqim have joined the Camarilla, and they have a long history of blood sorcerers including an entire caste of blood sorcerers. Also, many Lasombra have also joined the Camarilla and would have likely brought their knowledge of Abyss Mysticism with them. Abyss Mysticism is pretty niche, but it still offers new magical options that the Tremere don't necessarily have immediate answers too.

      And then on top of all of that, the Hecata have swelled up into being their own major faction by bringing in members from a half-dozen different bloodlines (though you're definitely going to see the Hecata fracture at some point). While they're an independent clan, you'll likely find members of the Hecata in most Camarilla cities and they bring powerful blood magic of their own.

      So the Tremere are still the primary blood sorcerers of the Camarilla, but they suddenly have a lot of competitors and their Pyramid was cracked and nearly collapsed. Two decades ago, if a vampire in the Camarilla found herself needing to hire the services of a blood mage, her only choice would really be a Tremere. Assamites, Lasombra Antitribu and Giovanni/Samedi were all fairly rare. Now suddenly that same vampire has options. The Tremere don't have a monopoly on the Camarilla's blood magic like they used to, and they've been fractured anyway.

              Even without V5 it is worth mentioning that the Tremere monopoly of Blood Magic in the Camarilla has nothing to do with trust and even less with the power and variety of their magic, even because most people in the Cam can't really tap into their clan-wide variety, only in the few Paths practiced in their city.

              The Tremere had a monopoly because they bargained it and that's that. Most practitioners of Blood Magic never had any interest in selling their services, or would only do so in very particular cases. The Tremere put a price table on the door. They look for clients and actively offer their services. While for others magic is a secondary aspect of clan culture and a tool for the clan's goals, for the Tremere the goal is to develop magic and the means is to get patronage by selling it.

              And finally, they keep others outside of the trade just by bullying and business malpractice. They actively go after the competition while they're weak and the Tremere are necessary. If a Follower or Koldun starts to sell magic to the Prince of a city, the Tremere from a dozen cities will fall on them to find any way whatsoever to destroy the newcomer's reputation, mark them as a criminal and kill with prejudice or capture for interrogation and then as research subject.

              You can bet the Camarilla will grab any opportunity to be rid of this monopoly that is good to the Pyramid, not to the sect. But they're very adamant about staying the only game in town.

                  When the game was first published, the Tremere were supposed to have a complete monopoly on any kind of blood magic. Too bad for you, other six clans, you'll never have blood magic and need to go to the Tremere! Of course, the game starting add all these other vampires who also knew some form of blood magic. The Sabbat had the Tremere anti-tribu, and some of the Sabbat Paths seem to implied that non-Tremere AT knew them. Then we had more Paths known to the Tal'mahe'ra. Infernalists had Dark Thaumaturgy. And soon more Paths started appearing for this clan or that clan. Within five years, the idea that the Tremere had a monopoly was simply idiotic, and this got worse in Revised. Every faction or clan seemed to have some version of it EXCEPT for the other six Camarilla clans.

                  I find that silly. The other six clans not having blood magic was an artifact of a time when it was intended for only the Tremere to know it, and that go thrown out the window. IMHO, keeping that while saying everyone else can have access to blood magic is silly.

                  This is how I handle it in my chronicles.

                  First, some ancient paths of blood magic pre-date the Tremere and are out there. These Paths are known. People can learn them provided they find the resources or mentors who can do so. I prefer to keep these Paths as those that people popularly assume vampires can do anyway. So these open Paths are Movement of Mind, Weather Control, Lure of Flames, Spirit , and Elemental Mastery. Are these common? No, not at all, though Movement of Mind is the best known of all of these. Finding mentors is difficult. And while these aren't Tremere secrets that the Tremere actively suppress, they do what they can to discourage this knowledge from spreading. The Kindred who know any of them are usually elders who know one or two Paths, and they might teachit to someone else in exchange for very useful boons. Movement of Mind though is known well enough that even some Anarchs might know it (I think vampires flying in the night sky is a popular enough trope it has to be simulated somehow). When the Tremere choose to teach non-Tremere some Thaumaturgy in return for services, it is one of these Paths they teach (like how Nicolai is teaching Gordon Keaton in 1e CbN) as these aren't Tremere secrets and already known. Rituals aren't openly known though.

                  Second, there are secret societies within the Camarilla of individuals who know blood magic and actively develop it and recruit new members. There aren't many of them, and they don't have many members. If you combined all of them, their numbers fall for short of the Tremere. But their members include the other six Camarilla clans. The Kindred here often know many or all of the Paths listed above as well as a few more Paths I consider fairly typical like Neptune's Might or Conjuration. These vampires also know various Rituals as well. These aren't dabblers, but actual "magicians" like the Tremere. The Tremere views these as rivals and do what they can to undermine them. They can't replace the Tremere as a source of magic for the sect, and they are absent in most domains. But there's enough of them so that the sect is not entirely dependent on the Tremere.

                  However, I also make sure that there are plenty of Tremere-only Paths (Path of Blood obviously), and sometimes even make certain Paths proprietary only to certain chantries or factions within the Tremere. I want to make being part of the Tremere special for those players who want to play them and like that they have some toys not available to others, and to make internal Tremere politics important if they want to learn a lot of Paths.
