                                      In its discussion of elder characters the Dark Ages Storyteller Companion (WW 20003) it has a section on Methuselah's thirst. On pg 76 had an interesting list of potential triggers for Methuselah's Thirst in a sidebar as well as system rules for it.
                                      - Character is a diablerist
                                      - Sire is addicted to Cainite vitae
                                      - Character's Road rating is 3 or less
                                      - Character follows the Roads of the Beast, Metamorphosis, or Blood
                                      - Character is active for 500 or more years
                                      - Character has a Derangement related to blood or feeding
                                      - Character has created five or more childer
                                      - Character has placed five or more Cainites under blood oath
                                      - Character has a Nature other than Architect, Caregiver, Cavalier or Martyr
                                      Storytellers who prefer a systematic approach should require players to make a Willpower roll for every few centuries of game time. The difficulty of the roll is the number of "trigger conditions" the character possesses, with a minimum of 2 (one trigger equals difficulty 2, seven triggers results in a a difficulty of 7). Success on the roll means that the character need not drink vampiric vitae (though she may choose to). Failure indicates the Cainite is wavering, experiencing a thirst for Cainite vitae and drinking it when the opportunity presents itself but not yet required to do so. Such characters must roll again after a period determined by the Storyteller (perhaps a few years within the chronic). If the roll fails again, the character must drink vampiric blood to survive. A botch at any point means the character can only draw nourishment from Cainite vitae.
                                      Basically there was no one specific factor that caused a vampire to develop Methuselah's Thirst. Similarly a vampire might have a number of potential triggers but still be sated by lesser blood regardless. Essentially it's up to the ST to determine when/if that change occurs for a character.

                                      As for your second question, yes a vampire could embrace someone to feed on them if they could only drink Cainite vitae. However unless the vampire in question is already blood bound or participates in Vaulderie, they're going to need to worry about the blood oath. As for your blood couple, they'll likely end up in a situation where they're addicted to Cainite vitae. For them hunger frenzy could be extremely dangerous since their partner is likely to be the closest source of palatable blood.

                                      While I wouldn't normally mention it Requiem touched on a situation similar to this due to the Coils of Blood. Essentially there was a power that gave you an extra blood point for what you took. So two vampires with the power who fed off each other (thus each generating blood through the act) were called a Hungarian Marriage. It was described as a bad thing because they'd both be hopelessly blood addicted time bombs that are one frenzy away from disaster and a blood addled predator hunting their own kind. Larger groups end up in a daisy chain standoff where if one dies the link of blood bonds collapse and the hunger can easily lead to destruction.
