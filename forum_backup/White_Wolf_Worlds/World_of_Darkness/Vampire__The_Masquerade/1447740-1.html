    Originally posted by Ventrue Busboy View Post
    I'm going to second Portland, OR as an excellent city that has been oddly untouched in 30 years of Vampire (or even White Wolf as a whole). It's an interesting city in many respects---a unique city council form of government (with a long history of corruption---at one point there was an IRL scheme by Labor Unions to literally seize control of Oregon's government), zoning laws keeping it dense and compact despite its population and the largest amount of parkland in any city of its size (Forest Park, which is over 8 miles long, is hard by the city). And of course the iconic Mount Hood looms over the skyline like an American Fuji. Memphis, TN is a similarly sized city with an interesting local culture with its famous music scene and iconic skyline including the famous pyramid.
    I'm actually a player in another game master's Portland chronicle. We've only just begun, but he seems to have realized the city's potential indeed.

                        El Paso-Juarez, on the Texas-Mexico border. It's a huge population center, with roughly 2.5 million people, a rich history, and whiplash-inducing dichotomies (El Paso was awarded "Safest City in America" at the same time Juarez was exhuming mass graves, including one with 16 decapitated bodies. For context, downtown El Paso is a 20-minute walk over a bridge to downtown Juarez).

                                            I've only been able to find a few references to San Diego in my perusal of the VtM books, mostly in relation to the Anarch/Camarilla conflicts in Los Angeles and California. From what I can tell between the supplements and the unofficial White Wolf wiki, it doesn't appear to have been covered in much detail. What I can find says this: during the founding of the Anarch States. Tara Kearny was Baron, though doesn't seem to have been particularly respected by either those in the city or the Barons of other territories. She got ousted by her rivals, moved to Oceanside to lick her wounds, then defected to the Camarilla, wiped out her rivals when they were weak from infighting, and installed herself as Prince. After that, depending on which edition you're looking at, she either still rules in San Diego, was killed during the Final Nights, or was abducted by the Baron of Los Angeles and hasn't been seen since (though there are rumors that she's met her Final Death).

                                            Other than that, I've found little to nothing about my hometown. That's not surprising, since San Francisco and Los Angeles are more iconic, more populous, more culturally influential and financially powerful, and both hold greater weight in the public imagination. I'm certainly not complaining, or arguing that it should have been more fleshed out since it obviously wasn't necessary to the story being told. On the other hand, I would think that it would be a bit more on the radar of Kindred society. Consider:
                                            • While the population of the city itself is a bit over 4 million, the city also has 35 million tourist visitors over the course of the year, making it a prime feeding ground. This number is further bolstered by the number of military personnel from surrounding bases who visit the city on leave, and a large population of undocumented immigrants who survive within the city's shadow economy.
                                            • Much like El Paso upthread, San Diego is a significant border town. In campaigns where the Sabbat still claim rulership over the major Mexican cities, this territory is going to be on the front lines of any major war between them and their rivals in the Camarilla or among the Anarchs.
                                            • As a major international center of trade, San Diego is going to be a target of interest for the Ventrue and any other Kindred interested in the acquisition of material wealth and financial power. It's also a playground for the wealthy, with a number of CEOs and multi-billionaires either living full- or part-time in high-priced communities such as La Jolla and Rancho Santa Fe.
                                            • The Navy maintains a major base in San Diego, as well as many other facilities separate from the main base itself. The Marines have Camp Pendleton to the north in Oceanside, the Marine Corps Recruit Depot in the city itself, and the Miramar Marine (formerly Naval) Air Station. This is an obvious source of blood and useful ghouls, but also potential progeny for the Banu Haqim, Brujah, Gangrel, and other clans who traditionally place an emphasis on strength, martial prowess, and ability to survive.
                                            • The nightlife and party scene is as expansive and varied as one would expect from a tourist city that also has three major universities (UCSD, USD, and SDSU), a constant influx of military personnel, and a large number of young, active professionals who reside in condos along the beach and in downtown lofts. At one point in the 1990s, Garnet Avenue in Pacific Beach allegedly had more bars per block than any place in the U.S. outside of New Orleans, though I can't verify that fact, and don't know if it's still true even if it was the case at the time. Either way, there's no shortage of areas to qualify as The Rack.
                                            • The art scene in San Diego isn't large or well-known in comparison to other cities, but it's remained vibrant and consistent for many years, from Shakespeare at the Old Globe in Balboa Park to slam poetry readings at coffeehouses and Art Walks in various neighborhoods, a Toreador has plenty to work with.
                                            • Comic Con, Comic Con, Comic Con. Toreador with interests in popular art, movies, and television. Ventrue looking after their financial interests in the entertainment industry. Tremere investigating how their occult insights somehow ended up in the pages of a web comic with an almost literal cult following. Nosferatu being able to wear full face coverings and mingle without anyone looking at them askance (2020 notwithstanding). Malkavians actually blending in. And, of course, the fans, an enormous number of whom are willing and possibly even experienced Blood Dolls. I can't imagine a number of Kindred NOT being in attendance, though a Prince would likely forbid any feeding at the Convention Center, the common areas of surrounding hotels, and anywhere related events were being held.
                                            That just scratches the surface. There's also the enormous homeless population drawn to the idea of not actually freezing to death during the winter months, biker gangs in the outlying areas of the county, and a host of other items that are ripe source material for characters, adventures, and entire campaigns. Just a few:
                                            • Most obviously, if Prince Tara is no longer in the picture, then who is in charge? What sort of struggle is happening among the local Primogen? Are the Anarchs going to seize the opportunity to reclaim their lost territory? Are the Sabbat, if they remain a factor, looking to take advantage of the turmoil?
                                            • Since 1983, five mayors or acting mayors of San Diego have resigned, four of them as a result of scandal, and one after only three days of serving in an acting capacity. Was this simply bad luck (or bad judgment) on the part of the voters, or were other machinations going on behind the scenes to cause this repeated chaos?
                                            • Exactly how have the Anarchs and Camarilla kept the Sabbat from sneaking over the porous border for the past several decades? Is there a truce of some sort, whether spoken or unspoken? Is the border neighborhood of San Ysidro essentially a no man's land, or possibly a demilitarized zone? Or does this have something to do with how human smugglers and traffickers really got the nickname of "coyotes?"
                                            • What was with the fires set through Balboa Park in 1978, ones that resulted in the destruction of both the Globe Theatre and the Aerospace Museum? Human arsonist? Hunter who had narrowed down the possibilities of the location of a vampire's haven? Sabbat trying to invade or make a point with their typical panache? Or something even more sinister?
                                            There's plenty more out there, from the decades it took to approve a new downtown library despite widespread support from the community (I think that someone's haven and feeding ground was in danger) to the stunning redevelopment of downtown from a blighted area to prime real estate (I can only imagine that this had something to do with the shift to the Camarilla). As far as the answers to these questions go, I have ideas, but that's best saved for another thread. I'll post them if I have enough interest in doing so, or others are interested in exploring the possibilities.

                                            If anything I've written isn't up to date or is inaccurate regarding the actual canon, I apologize. I've done my best to track down references to San Diego in the available material, but it's entirely possible that I missed something. If any WoD supplement (VtM or otherwise) that you know of has further information regarding the city, I'd love to know where to look. While I'm not slavish about following metaplot, I do like to get it as close as possible to the game setting, even if it's all going to fall apart upon first contact with the actual players.

                                                Originally posted by No One of Consequence View Post
                                                Miami and the surrounding area, mostly just because I'm a fan of Carl Hiaasen's fairly bizarre crime novels.
                                                Miami was one of my two cities of choice, back in the day. (The other was Denver, 'the mile-deep city', run by a Nosferatu who dwelled over a seemingly bottomless pit in a played-out mine under the city.)

                                                For Miami, I tried to work in some local flavor, as well as making it a 'pact city' trapped between a Camarilla/Sabbat status, with factions of both present in a weird sort of cold war state (because the game at the time was going to have some players from each faction, temporarily united against a greater threat). In between some more serious stuff (like a mad Ventrue living in Coral Gables, who considered it his own personal fiefdom and everybody else just kind of ignored and let him call himself 'Prince' even though he very much wasn't), there was some over the top stuff like a Nosferatu who bred ghoul 'gators in the Turkey Point nuclear facilities cooling ponds.
