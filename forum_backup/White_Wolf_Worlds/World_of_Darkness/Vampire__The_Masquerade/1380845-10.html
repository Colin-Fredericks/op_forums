                                                    Well, you're asking a very complicated question of WHO ARE THE SABBAT and the thing about the Sabbat is they're not any one thing. The Camarilla is composed of more than just, say, "Really rich snobby feudal vampires stuck up their own asses" but they obviously compromise a lot of lot. The Anarchs can't just be described as, "a bunch of violent angry gangbangers and revolutionaries" but that obviously compromises a lot of them too.

                                                    How do I run the Sabbat

                                                    Stage 1: The Shocktroopers

                                                    For the most part, the average Camarilla or Anarch is never going to encounter the Sabbat save at their most basic. Like meeting a werewolf, they are going to encounter the Sabbat that are feral and wild with a mission to kill or destroy them. The frontlines of the Sabbat are composed of Low Humanity (not Path) vampires who are addicted to the Vaulderie, diablerie if they've tasted it before, and are on their way to wassail.

                                                    The shocktroopers of the Sabbat are basically the lowest common denominator of the sect. I've said the Texas Chainsaw Massacre and Near Dark but they're basically Sabbat who are all enslaved to the Beast. They're given leave to drink as much blood as they want from whoever they want with any other thing they want to do allowed. They Embrace who they want, when they want, and are a bunch of soccer hooligans writ large.

                                                    In a way, the Shocktroopers are a tragic fate because they are going to leave short and violent lives. The Sabbat inducts them, brainwashes them, uses them, and then lets them burn out without caring. After all, there's always going to be more of them. Shocktroopers are pretty much the stereotype of the Antitribu and the "Malkavian Anti with a chainsaw" stereotype comes from.

                                                    Most Nomad and War Packs have this rep even if it's not quite true.

                                                    Humanity: 1-3

                                                    Stage 2: The Cultured Warrior

                                                    Essentially, one step up from Shocktroopers are the Sabbat that can be expected to live to be Ancilla and are people who were generally either Embraced outside of a War Party and bothered to be educated in the ways of their Clan. Most Lasombra and Tzimisce are this way but Antitribu are also like this. Cultured warriors are Sabbat who, ironically, have more control over their Beast than most and tend to be a bit higher humanity. They're still a bunch of murderous violent psychopaths but tend to kill only when they need to do it. Head soccer and random blood orgy killing sprees are viewed by these as gauche. One of the ironies is that an Anarch or Camarilla defector who joins with one of these packs will find that they aren't THAT dissimilar to "normal" coteries.

                                                    Maybe murder is a bit easier, maybe there's more beating of ass if people step out of line, but these are the Sabbat who hold domain, have jobs, and can have coherent conversations. The Sierra Van Burace types. Many of them are immersed in the Sabbat's religion and are prepping to go to Stage 3 but others are just fine being vampires living their unlives. They form a larger portion of the Sabbat than the Camarilla or Anarchs ever give them credit for.

                                                    Humanity: 4-5

                                                    Stage 3: The Zealots

                                                    The Zealots are the members of the Sabbat who have spent at least fifteen to twenty years in the sect studying the ways of the undead as well as getting immersed in their particular region. Almost all of them are Ancilla or older. They've had time to adjust their mindsets to something more alien and less human. The Zealots are the devotees to the Paths and have begun their journey into important roles in the sect. Paladins, Templars, the Inquisition, the lowest levels of the Black Hand, and becoming Priests who actually know what the hell they're talking about. Many of them are Bishops because the Sabbat has higher advancement opportunities. The Zealots are people who have moved past Cultured Warriors and Shocktroopers to the fact that humans are an irrelevance. Killing them is neither an end to itself or a pleasure but just, "I need food, I take it. I'll spare it if I'm not hungry, I'll kill it if I am." They tend to be very well educated in Kindred history, myth, and legend.

                                                    Path Score: 1-4

                                                    Stage 4: The Elders

                                                    This is when a Sabbat successfully reaches about a hundred to two hundred. At this point, a Sabbat is almost always removed from humanity as much as a wolf or a eldritch abomination from the Penumbra. The Elders of the Sabbat aren't actually that different from the Camarilla as the majority abandon any real loyalty to the sect and pursue their own sinister agendas. They are almost identical to the Elders of their Clans prior to the Camarilla. They seek to expand their domains, power, and jockey for position with their rivals. Most are extremely cynical about their sect and often see it as a tool rather than a cause. Their Paths are more important than the Cause.

                                                    Path Score: 5-8
