                                    IMO, Flaws work as encouragement for clan unity: If the Ventrue didn't have mutually exclusive feeding preferences they would see eachother as direct competition rather than fellows they can work with and depend on, and to a lesser extent the Brujah would have similar problems were they not so likely to frenzy away their holdings and get in trouble with the authorities. With clans like Nosferatu, Malkavians or Gangrel you've got that shared weakness to bring disparate folk together. To paraphase some social scientist: Strength makes rivals, weakness binds people together.

                                    The no reflection flaw worked for Lasombra: It encouraged the paranoia and the Darwinism, it encouraged them to set their standards high because if one of them got caught and the lack of reflection was widely known they alone would get purged. It encouraged them to set up courts of blood and to care so much about their image.

                                    The technology issue... does nothing for the clan. Like, nothing. Absolutely nothing. It just makes Lasombra worse, not in a good way like how Nosferatu or Ventrue or any other clan is encouraged by their flaw and needs to get clever and work around their issue, it just makes Lasombra lesser.
