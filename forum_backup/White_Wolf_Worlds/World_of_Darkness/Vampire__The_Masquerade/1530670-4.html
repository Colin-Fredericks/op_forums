                    Originally posted by Matt Corleone View Post

                    V5 made the metaplot and the mechanics damn near inseparable, which is why I don't use it. I stick to older editions. So that's not the issue.

                    The issue I have is that the specific WoD metaplot was horrible and the way WoD uses its metaplot has always been bad. It was used as a way to try and force a thematic purism upon people, and V5 was merely the apotheosis of what certain past WoD developers had been wanting to do for years, to purge the "badwrongfun". So yeah, the metaplot helped ruin WoD as a setting IMHO. Unironically, give me Alien Hunger, Chaos Factor, and Dirty Secrets of the Black Hand over Beckett's Jyhad Diary or Time of Thin Blood or any V5 book ever.

                    So I say fuck V5, fuck the metaplot, fuck BJD, fuck "street level" angst, and fuck "true art personal horror"
                    Thank you. The metaplot, and the signature characters for The Clans, has always been terrible. The main one I extremely dislike seems to the the fan's favorite: Beckett. It seems the consensus at White Wolf, and now beyond, that he should be the 90's edgelord wetdream. Mainly. a snarky contrarian, sarcastic asshole, who should be immediately destroyed for being a smartass, but somehow escapes via Deus Vampex. Alan Moore's Constantine. But no, he's "BADASS..."
