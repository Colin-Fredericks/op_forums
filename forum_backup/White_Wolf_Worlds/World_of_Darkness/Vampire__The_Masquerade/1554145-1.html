        Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
        I'm going to make a controversial pick: Post-Lasombra Trilogy Lucita.

        Lucita is more or less the iconic Vampire: The Masquerade character even if she was only introduced at the beginning of Revised. She has five novels, a comic book series, and has been adapted to the LA by Night stream by the lovely Cynthia Marie. There's many modules and fan content that stars her like Lucita's Six. I love Lucita and so do many other fans as the perfect embodiment of the dark superhero world of White Wolf.

        However, the decision to have her join the Sabbat was one of the dumbest decisions in all of gaming. Essentially, I can't help but think that someone up high thought that the Lasombra Signature character being a Antitribu was a bad idea. So it fell to the authors to justify it in universe.
        And now we're stuck with it.

        Worse, it becomes even more ridiculous with the fact that Lucita is now the outlier. She defects to the Sabbat because she feels that the Camarilla is ignoring the threat of Gehenna as well as a spiritual void in her soul.

        And in 5th Edition:

        1. The Sabbat are now mindless blood horrors with no real spiritualism.
        2. The Camarilla now believe in the Antediluvians.
        3. The Lasombrra have defected because they understand the Gehenna Wars are full of morons.
        4. White Wolf's owners know the Lasombra are awesome and everyone loves them in the LARPs as well as games like Shadows of New York.

        So Lucita is either dead at Talley's hand or head of a bunch of cultists in Madrid doing nothing.

        Oof.

        I'm not even sure her defection even makes sense anymore since the primary reason for her spiritual collapse was Anatole dying (combined with killing Moncada) and he's supposedly alive again.
        Technically she was introduced in Vampire the Dark Ages, though her trademark was on Transylvania Chronicles I. That was the main issue while white-wolf was on trial over Underworld's Selene with Sony.

                            Another couple of characters I have an issue with:

                            Khalil Ravana: I think someone thought it'd be fun to have the Ravnos signature character be fun critique of the fact that everyone assumed the Clan was full of bad stereotypes by...making a character who is every awful stereotype combined. Khalil is a diablerist, liar, thief, and repulsive piece of crap without any real redeeming qualities. His sheer lack of virtues does make him stand out in the Clan Novels but not in a good way.

                            Basically, Hesha is a priest of pure darkness and Vykos is a diabolical mastermind while Khalil is the kind of player character who pulls out a shotgun to blow up an Elder then rob their house during a quest giving session. There were some interesting ideas with the guy like him being possessed by Hazimel as well as the genuinely funny joke he did with Ramona. "Hey, don't you disrespect Romani. They're not a bunch of thieves and murderers. I AM but that's just me. Really, I'm a genuine disgrace to my family."

                            Evil Saulot: I think someone really thought revealing Saulot was an evil mastermind was one of the coolest ideas ever and never realized that it wasn't nearly as interesting as they thought. "Okay, he's a bastard. Now how does that make him different from any other Antediluvian?" I also felt that it was revealed in a way that I think didn't really do the necessary legwork. Revealing him as the guy who created the Baali was supposed to be, "Oh, he made the worst of the worst!" Except V:TM Cainite lore is based on the premise that God punished Caine by turning them into undead. You could easily read Saulot punishing the Baali founders by making them 4th generation and then going, "Oh shit. That was a bad idea." Arguably, it's way more interesting.

                            There are worse ideas, like making the Inner Circle of the Third Reich into vampires, but I think we've all collectively agreed they're so stupid they don't count. The worst element of them really was that they got all of Berlin by Night discounted rather than going, "Some disgusting punks with Vicissitude pretended to be Nazis. Eventually, Rudi and company took care of them."
