              Novel Dracula is somewhere on Path of Evil Revelations; he’s a literal Satanist whose vast magic powers came from learning black magic at Scholamanse from Satan himself.

              Selene is somewhere on the Path of Honorable Accord; it’s basically the path for loyal soldiers and killing her betrayer absolutely fits that path morality.

              Netflix Castlevania Alucard is probably Humanity 7-8 with a maxed out Conscience score to keep from degeneration when he has to kill humans in self defense. He’s also someone’s twinked out homebrew version of a Dhampir.

              Pre-Soul Spike’s Humanity is 0 in the 1e sense (not mindless, just irredeemably evil), just with a mechanical restraint that required him to behave more like a man to avoid punishment. Post soul he’s basically been de-Wighted and is just utterly wracked with guilt for his past actions… probably stupid high Humanity and Conscience initially (hence throwing himself onto crosses to burn himself out of guilt at one point). I’d say after a while he settled out at about a 6 or so Humanity.

              I am not familiar enough with the others to judge.
