                                    The Tremere are OP at face value, but really, it makes sense when you look at it critically.

                                    Unlike their vampire enemies, they are highly organized and provide a very vaulable service for which to gain favour. Thaumaturgy is like the tech sector of prestation: Little investment, lots of gains. Even with the Noble Roads being popular about this time, the Tremere have a big advantage in their early nights against their devided enemies. And Cities are smaller during this time so once the Tremere really establish themselves in a place, they've basically got the whole area covered.

                                    Unlike the order of Hermes, The Tremere can recruit very, very quickly and low level vampires>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>low level mages. In an attrition war, of course they'd win. But the biggest thing about this is that these wars, the Omen war (Does that apply to the Hermetic war or just the inter-vampire stuff?) was a protracted struggle that lasted for hundreds of years. The Tremere as a clan might have constantly been fighting, but on a local level they only fought occasionally.

                                    The Tremere never had plot armour, they were just what would happen if a very organized faction of intellegent bloodmages emerged among the vampires. They're not a Mary sue faction, because they're known to be agressive and untrustworthy and, as intelligent as they all are, bloodmagic is not a calling for social butterflies; They've certainly made mistakes. Consider this: You guys who don't like the Tremere have fallen for the same reasons the people in universe don't like the Tremere. That's some brilliant literary work right there.
