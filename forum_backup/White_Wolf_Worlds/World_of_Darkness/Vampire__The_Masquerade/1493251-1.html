                            Originally posted by Thoth View Post
                            I could easily see the vampire lords living in technologically advanced fortresses, while the mortals live simplistic agrarian lives on the surface. The tricky part is do you create a privileged class of mortals who are more educated to handle thing beyond just having knowledgeable ghouls.
                            The problem is the infrastructure needed to keep those fortresses running, specially against a rival that simply decides to go "screw this BS" and build a mortal army to retake the world in exchange for a less dumb society,

                            With futuristic tech we can easily picture a bunker with all the amenities we can imagine and completely self-sustaining. But with actual modern tech this simply isn't a thing. An actual modern bunker is extremely comfortable for the standards of a cave and can keep going for decades without external resources. But they still miss a lot of actual benefits if there's no technological society outside and it will run out eventually, and not that much time from the perspective of a vampire.

                            Not to say that plunging humanity back to pre-industrial times, if achieved, which I'm skeptical of, would impact population greatly. Just the effects on food production alone would cause widespread famine. The actual amount of tech "allowed" would greatly impact this, but unless those vampires are omnipresent and omniscient enough to squash any development in its crib, I'd say anything beyond the 10th century is a risk of "dangerous weapons" being developed and hid before they can notice, maybe not even that. So we're talking a world that sustained less than 400 million souls. Could it house more under vampire rule? Maybe, but I doubt that it would be a lot more.

                            Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
                            Let's imagine a scenario
                            That's an extremely contrived scenario and still a false dichotomy within it.

                            You're trying to equate "survival" with "following this plan and only this plan". And the character that comes up with it probably will try to pull the same argument.

                            But the thing is, this plan needs a huge level of agreement between them and there may simply be other plans that don't have so many problems. Even if this is, for some weird reason, the only way to survive, it would be necessary to convince almost every vampire that it is the only way to survive. It is not that some will choose to starve instead of being so "evil", it is that many will have their own plans on how to survive they believe hold a better outcome for themselves.

                            I don't have much more to say for now. I think this proposal can work in a myriad unique scenarios in very different ways, but a baseline that makes sense seems hard to come by.

                                                        vampire rulers could potentially be better than a lot of RW rulers. The vampiric aristocracy would have fewer needs than human aristocracy (IE they don't eat, want to ensure everyone's well fed, and they're active for less time in the day so they'll spend less on luxuries) and so apart from blood tithes they would likely be less economically draining than, say, Bolsonaro or Liz Truss or whatever regressive feudalistic crackpot you want a comparison to.

                                                        But yeah, we're talking abuse of power here...

                                                        Somewhat reasonable example of how things could change. Now I'm not a Brazilian* so feel free to correct me when I'm wrong, and I actually had a longer more detailed post but it'd maybe be a bit inappropriate, but anyhow:
                                                        Brazil is soon to have a second round of voting in its presidential election. It's between the incumbent and far-right Bolsonaro and the socialist Luca, who is almost certainly going to win the popular vote if I'm reading the first-round right. However, given the election interference seen so far it looks very possible that Bolsonaro will cheat or refuse to step down, and given how many powerful people have a vested interest in keeping Luca out and Bolsonaro in, it's likely that things will be a lot worse than Trump's capitol hill riot. From the English language sources I have, it seems violence is probable.

                                                        Does Bolsonaro need Vampires to stay in power? No. The means of violence are largely on his side. However, it would be a great time for vampires to step up their control. When your choices are fascism or socialism, any Vampire worth his blood will be overwhelmingly in favour of fascism. The reality of undead living relies on inequality and corruption so that vampire can feed easily without getting due process. Socialism or any movement towards the left would reduce the inequality which would in turn undermine that corruption: thus, Bolsonaro strengthens the vampires, vampires should therefore strengthen Bolsonaro. When the repressions begin, they can only continue until any resistance has been hounded out. In a modern world, where people have the internet and outside information and are exposed to alternative ideas, is that likely? No, you'll continue to foster dissent, so you can only get more extreme with your methods. So now we move into fiction: Vampires take over a fascist Brazil. With no international power willing to intervene, we might get an undead state.

                                                        Vampire rulers are optional, but they work.
