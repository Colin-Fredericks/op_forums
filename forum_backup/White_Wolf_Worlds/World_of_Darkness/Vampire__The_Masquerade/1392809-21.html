                        Originally posted by Sergeant Brother View Post

                        I think that the idea of eliminating the Venture in favor of the Tzimisce (and maybe the Lasombra) is actually kind of cool. When they came out with V:tR, they did away with the Lasombra and Tzimisce, perhaps with the implication that those concepts should fit into the new Ventrue Clan. Though that just makes everything a bit too generic. It V:tM is to be streamlined, maybe it would be cooler to get rid of the Ventrue in exchange for two leadership Clans, the Tzimisce and the Lasombra. Tzimisce to represent old fashioned leadership with a strict sense of honor, a tie to the land, and so on; while the Lasombra are the ruthless social darwinists who have no honor or adherence to any strict hierarchy, anyone who earns it has a place on top. Having those two archetypes actually lessons the need for a generic leadership Clan.
                        I see Requiem's Ventrue as a fairly even mixture of Lasombra, Tzimisce and Ventrue without the weirder disciplines. It's been said that their raison d'etre isn't so much about ruling as winning. As blank as Ventrue look next to Sabbat and Independent clans, they do have a relatively tightly knit culture, which isn't how any Requiem clan functions. They aren't the clan with Majesty, so they lack the personal appeal of VtM Ventrue, but they do have Animalism, bringing them closer to the pre-bourgeois landed gentry concept. Speaking of which, I wouldn't reserve that concept for a single clan.
