                                        Didn't know that they'd lifted this from the MMO bucket, but the choice of art is an important decision, especially in the clan write up section. Only four of the eight look like they might have something wrong with their faces, and none of them have visible physical deformities.

                                        Just having a slightly messed up face is, well, kinda weak for the clan that is meant to be horrific. The book is horrible in terms of style and clarity overall, but this section in particular is very poorly set up to explain the Nosferatu flaw visually.


                                        Originally posted by Shawarbaaz View Post
                                        Old World of Darkness is fraught with stereotypes oh my god. Leaked pictures of a mental asylum? Must be Malkavian! Sewers? Nosferatu. Crappy apartment? Totally Gangrel. It's not like the characters are defined by their personality instead of their clan right?

                                        Stereotypes are very useful to story telling though. So long as you stay away from overtly negative real world ones, like many point at the Ravnos as being a bad example of. Having a clan stereotype gives a base that lets players come up with better stand out characters, and also gives them a "comfortable" niche that they can use to see if they have an affinity for the clan. Not all stereotypes are bad, and a lot have a grain of truth in them somewhere. Pretty much everyone has "stereotypical" mental images of certain groups, and designing characters to conform to or buck these trends is fairly central to storytelling in any medium.

                                        Introducing new players to a setting can use stereotypes to make adjusting easier by giving a shorthand that almost everyone will grasp very easily. Those stereotypes you're listing are nothing to do with characters, they are clan related, and with good reasons. If a Malkavian wants to blend in, being around a concentration of people who act erratically by "normal" standards is a reasonable choice. Nosferatu hanging around sewers, is again a fairly reasonable choice, there are large homeless populations that form communities in real world city sewers that they can blend into. Not sure how a crappy apartment would nod in the direction of a Gangrel though

                                        Now, to reiterate, Negative Stereotypes Are Bad but not all stereotypes are negative.
