        Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post

        Entertainment has been a primary use of artistic expression for political ends for all of human history. It's hard to get people to listen if you can't hook them in first.

        While this is true, and there are many stories out there which had political undertones and became hugely successful, the difference between then and now is how they portrayed their message in a much more subtle art style than waving it around in people's faces and beating them over the head with it. It's been many years since I've written any stories, fanfiction or otherwise, but even I still remember one of the core tenets of good story telling: Show, Don't Tell!

        Ripli, from the Aliens franchise is a strong, independent female. But at no point in the movie does anybody come right out and state as such to the audience. Instead of telling people that Ripli is a strong character, it shows us that she's a strong character. And it does so in such a fashion that doesn't portray her as being a Marry Sue... except for Alien Resurrection, but we probably don't count that one.

        If the purpose of the medium is to be artistic, then the art is what you should focus on first and foremost. If the purpose of the medium is to be political, then don't insult the viewer's intelligence by claiming it's something else. Nobody is falling for that crap, and the franchises which were under the impression to the contrary are finally realizing their mistake and dialing back on the ham fisted politics.
