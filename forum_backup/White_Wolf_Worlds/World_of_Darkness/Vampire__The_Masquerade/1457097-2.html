    Some of my design notes for V5 games and the inquisition, mostly a list of hard idea's I applied and gave to players to show I plan to run them. Apologies if they're a little fragmented. I've edited out some stuff for my specific players but I hope they provide some useful suggestions:

    1)The second inquisition not a unified front but a series of differant govermental organization engaging in low key co-operation and is often obstructed by realpolitik. For example Firstlight has deliberatly kept information on compromised russian politicians from the 8th directorate due to mutual distrust. The society of leopold and other theocratic sects are the only ones which operate pan nationaly and they have to tread lightly.

    2) The level of effectiveness of 2nd inquisition organizations success rate varies greatly nation to nation, The United kingdom and Austria being most succesful with Russia and India lagging far behind. Several misguided or corrupt goverments are currently directly engaged in covert negotiations with the Carmarilla and Ashirra sects to see if some sort of backdoor agreement can be made. some countries have no formal goverment vampire hunt on.

    3) Goverment approaches to the Vampiric menace are not limited to engage and destroy missions and economic pressure is being applied where possible. EG aggresive crack downs on sex industry in area's were is has been infiltrated or the dissolution of certain corporations.

    4) Most vampire hunters institutions are so black op that they often cannot rely on local authorities co-operation and fear of infiltration means it's likely to stay that way.

    5) while various institutions are making progress on the knowledgue of the vampiric world, their's still a dangerous lack of understanding or multiple levels. For example Necromancy is still a total unknown Leading to heavy losses when engaging Heceta vampires due to ignorance of wraiths and the underworld.

    6) The second inquisition has made some feelers into attacking other supernatural groups and outreach to others at the same time a number of factions are aware of the decline in vampire power and are moving in for the kill. For example the British Verbenna/order of hermes are directly backing SO13 resulting in heavy casualties in clan Tremere and the total anihilation of all british house Carna. By contrast House red crest has had several of their kinfolk disapear into 8th directorate cells and one high profile termination of a sept elder. Israels unit 8211 prioritzies Fallen over kindred. The outusider only group institutionally involved

    7) Sect responses have been from passive masquarade re-enforcement. Sabbat and Anarchs have formed ad hoc kill teams to engage and destroy hunters, when possible Sabbat have gone so far to create Templar units with this in mind. The Carmarilla has been engaging in institutional subversion and back door deals where possible. The Sabbat has also began taking the masquarade a lot more seriously.

    8)Anarchs are the most vulnrable sect with their lack of institutional influence, bad organization and their overt affiliation with radical political groups. The Sabbats casualty rate is probably highest overall in the initial period but now the sect is reacting numbers are flattening.

    9)Clan casualties vary greatly, the most heavy hit are Caitiff since their lack of suport and inexperiance, this will be something of a break in V5 meta but the Caitiff have been largely exterminated as easy targets. Least hard hit clan is clan Ravnos as their nomadic nature, low numbers and survivalist mentality has meant they've largely slipt under the radar.

    10)Some forms of influence and infiltration of human power structures is more likely to draw inquisition attention than others.Typically localized corporations and ironically crime syndicates are far safer than civic services or goverment for example. National Military is probably the most likely to generate attention.

    11)Some goverments have been making moves to weaponize vampires, while they've had no real successes with pure blooded Vampires. Thin bloods have proven to have real potential....

    hope this helps.

                            You have very fair points, and the question is addressed already by Ragged Robin next post, but let me elaborate.

                            Originally posted by Vysha View Post
                            I could easily see a situation where Isreali hunters focusing more on demons than vampires, not because of religious preconseptions and beliefs
                            Just to take this out of the way in case someone mistake what I'm saying, but that goes without saying. I'll focus on the question if this is a good option in general, not if it can be done. It can, totally.

                            Originally posted by Vysha View Post
                            as the site of Jesus's ministry and close to his eventual death-resurrection would make it a very key place for demons to go after for any number of reasons (in my head, I'm picturing Fallen going relic hunting around there) due to its historic importance.
                            Not how Demon works. Since you said you're not that familiar, let me summarize.

                            D:tF focus on two main moments, before the Imprisonment and after Escaping. The first occurs in a Time Before Time fashion, so it links to no particular place and as their war was worldwide, things from that time can be found all around the world. A relic from the War is as likely to be in the Levant as in the Moon. The second was 22 years ago, so figures.

                            In the mean time Lucifer and the Earthbound were wandering the Earth in one fashion or another, and D:tF does say that their hands are likely to be at any and all religious movement, since their main resource is Faith, but that ultimately also means that even the Abrahamic religions didn't got such a special attention. Demons do recognize that they have a stronger "memory" of past events, but in general their history among religions don't place them as more likely to be linked to relics or something.

                            So Israel have no particular significance to D:tF as a whole. It is certain that some Demons may search for answers there, but not a lot more than anywhere else.

                            Originally posted by Vysha View Post
                            Meanwhile Kindred would have equally as many reasons to avoid the area (True Faith would presumably be much more common around there).
                            Vampire deal far more with the area since the beginning of the game. Almost every clan traces its origin to the region or its neighborhood, it was the main hub of Cainite activity in the ancient world, and it still have a strong presence of vampires.

                            Most glaring, in V5 there was a frigging vampire war in the area, with elders from far and wild feeling a pull to the region, the Sabbat going to war there en masse, Antedilluvians and stuff all over the place. So it was officially teeming with vampires regardless.

                            Originally posted by Vysha View Post
                            Isreal isn't just important to the Judeo in Judeo-Christian religions
                            Jerusalem is important to three big religious branches, not two. But the two that live there in big numbers aren't Christians. This isn't just something in the setting, this is something in the real world, where real people live and consume these games. In an in-game perspective, the Israeli military focusing on Demons seems contrived if there is no explicit event leading to it. In an out-of-game perspective this would left a bad taste in the mouths of two huge ethnic groups that historically don't have a lot of reasons to feel nice and cuddly with Christians hijacking their stories AGAIN.

                            Keep in mind that the post I was answering to was about design notes for the game, not a personal chronicle. Ragged Robin explained that this piece was left there by accident precisely because it was actually about local gaming.
