      There might have been a ritual somewhere, but it's not something a lot of attention has been drawn too. (might've been there to hype up the Baali, with questionable relevance to the other revenant families)

      But most fluff says they're a lucky possibility when it comes to breeding ghouls. I don't think anyone's written down the mechanics for selective breeding humans because... it's more trouble than it's worth, at least for an official publication. Eugenics programs rub some the wrong way. It'd be somewhat complicated, because you've got to consider the father's Domitor's gen, the father's age, the mother's domitor's gen, the mother's age (ghouls do get more potent blood with age, unlike vampires) random chance.

      I'd perhaps use the Garou's chances of breeding true for reference. 1/100 for two ghouls. 1/10 for a ghoul and a revenant, and certainty for two revenants. This works nicely to discourage 'dabbling' and keeps revenants rare while also supporting some horrific incest, But I think the 1/100 is really too low. Perhaps for each dot of Gen the Domitor has, the odds are increased by 1, so if a 10th gen (***) creates two ghouls and has them breed, there's a 6/100 chance. Just speculating makes me feel like I need to roll humanity.

          Yeah, it's most likely a ritual, but there does seem to be another (remotely likely) option: get Dhampirs to breed. That would require Embracing right down to the highest Generation possible, though, and then getting them pregnant. It might not even be possible at the time of your Chronicle, depending on where you're currently up to.

          It's by no means a guaranteed thing, but Dhampirs have similarities to Revenants, so in theory, breeding a lineage of Dhampirs could end up with something akin to Revenants.

          Whatever solution you go with, I would focus more on the story elements rather than the mechanics. Have them seek out forbidden lore, drink the blood of a Koldun or Baali to learn the right rituals, seek out some arcane artefact (e.g., the Chalice of Gilgamesh is an example I plucked out of thin air), test their Humanity/Path along the way, find the right vessels for this damned seed, breed generations of mortals in some depraved fashion, and then finally, if they're still putting in the work, have a baby born with their own vitae who is promptly worshipped as a herald of great/bad things by the inbred cult that the PCs have formed around them.

          This is the sort of thing that takes at least a Story to achieve, but probably a whole Chronicle. We're talking decades, if not centuries, of game time.

                      IIRC the only official rules for creating Revenants treat it more as a byproduct than the intended effect; there's a level 8-9 Valeren/Obeah power which can resurrect someone, and mortals come back as Revenants in some versions.

                      But otherwise, yeah, it's something where the details tend to be kept vague. You are probably going to have to create some kind of house-rules to represent it, in the form of a Blood Magic ritual or, as has previously been noted, a system for what will essentially be a multi-generation eugenics program.

                          The easy fix is to make it an extended action over centuries. Start off with Manipulation + Subterfuge to coerce your mortals into the right place. Etc, etc.

                          When your PCs finally reach a certain number of successes, they achieve their goal.

                                  IIRC MET specified something about the origins and rituals the Tzimisce used to create Revenants (there were two) in the MET Sabbat guide for LARPing purposes (although they also suggest Revenant creation was a peculiar part of the Tzimisce homeland, which *probably* means Kupala's involvement I think?). I suppose you could borrow those and adapt them to the game.

                                  I also know Requiem's 'Ghouls' supplement has 'Ghoul Families/bloodlines' which are their equivalent to Revenants (actual Revenants were something else entirely in CoD) and there were also rituals that are used for creating VtR dhampir which might also be adapted for revenants. I'm not sure how much those two correlate since I haven't read the requiem stuff in awhile but it might be something worth pursuing since I'm pretty sure both Ghoul Families and Dhampir had rules in Requiem.

                                  I also believe Dark Ages Vampire went into Revenant creation in more detail (but not necessarily rules.. that would require more digging than I'd have time for ATM)

                                  20th edition mentions Koldunic rituals possibly being involved for some and basically implies that a specific combination of magics and unique conditions created Revenants to leave it open ended for the Storyteller so that may not be a fruitful avenue.

                                  Broadly though what I recall Fatal Addiction (and the idea that Revenants 'breed up' from Ghouls to the same state that Dhampir 'breed down' from thin bloods) the idea is that the vampiric stasis messes with cell division/replication and/or both the fetus and the mother can have adverse effects due to potence and other disciplines if they aren't properly controlled. There's also mention that ghouling can result in miscarriages although whether this is a consequence of the aforementioned issues or a separate biological problem brought on by ghouling I don't recall. Either way the challenge of breeding Ghouls is maintaining a delicate balancing act where the fetus can still grow/develop (even at a slowed rate) without being miscarried and without uncontrolled discipline use by the mother or child fucking it up - presumably the rituals involved (or the other conditions) play a role in mitigating that delicate balance.

                                  Edit: As an aside when it comes to stuff like Dhampir and Revenants.. there actually are a number of disciplines that could result in such beings. Kharnov mentioned the Obeah/Valeran power of Heaven's Gate (9th dot IIRC in most cases) but it was originally a ghoul rather than Revenant (20th Anniversary changed that, I think Dark Ages?) The level 6 Bardo power 'Boon of Anubis' also created a 'Ghoul' by allowing a mortal to survive the embrace (although IIRC 20th changed THAT to mortal) - honestly given the mechanics of THAT power I thought there was more interesting potential there if reworked, like Heaven's Gate.

                                  There was also the Necromancy/Mortis power 'Gift of Life (5 dots - which also went by Benedicto Vitae in DA20) which allowed for a sort of semblance of life (they can eat and drink and have sex and enjoy that stuff) but their blood remains vitae. That obviously has certain implications for creating Dhampir rather than Revenants...

                                          I am pretty confident that I can state that there are no official rules for creating a Revenant family.
                                          That being said there are plenty of implications on what is needed.

                                          By looking at the different families we can make some conclusions about their creation. The most famous families being those born from Tzimisce experiments, Bratovich, Obertusa, Grimaldi and Zantosa plus a few lesser known Tzimisce creations, Ducheski and the Oprichniki. All of these families are mentioned to be bred over centuries, though it is implied that Koldunism and Vicissitude might have eared the process. This is pretty likely if for no other reason than the fact that the Tzimisce is the only Clan that has managed to create several Revenant families.

                                          Kairouan Brotherhood, created by he Assamite Sorcerer Caste, is mentioned to have been bred from experiments created by studying and experimenting upon kidnapped members of Tzimisce Revenants.

                                          The Rossellini family is heavily implied, almost downright said, to be the results of unknown applications and rituals of Necromancy.

                                          Rafastio claim to be the first Revenant family to be created though this is indicated to be a lie. They became Revenants through the Tal’mahe’ra but it is unclear how, a deal between the witches and Cainites was struck but whether they became Revenants due to the efforts of Tal’mahe’ra or by combining their own magic, and Magick, with Cainite Vitae is up for grabs.

                                          Enrathi are another creation of the Tal’mahe’ra, and again their origins are clouded by mystery. It is implied that the Rafastio had a role in their creation but other than that no solid clues are offered.

                                          Servants of Ventrue Anushin-Rawan are heavily implied to be succesful bred by the Ventrue due to a Baali altar and it’s infernal energies affecting the island where he experimented.

                                          D’habi, possibly the oldest Revenant family was created by Nergal from his most loyal Warrior-Priests. It is mentione that he did so ”using infernal methods that the other Clans couldn’t understand”.

                                          This information is pulled from V20 but for the most part it does align with older implications of Revenant creation, including the mention of D’habi being the first ones and that their origin lies in infernal methods. Though despite the original creation method all of the methods usually mention centuries of breeding as part of creating a stable family.

                                          Also V20 has this to say about Revenant creation, Ghouls and Revenants page 87:
                                          ”Revenant families are created using different magics in unique sets of conditions.”

                                          Though it seems by most sources the D’habi were the first Revenants soon followed by the creations of the Tal’mahe’ra and only then followed by the Tzimisce.

                                          And that about as official it gets, no mechanics but many implications of different methods and ultimately saying that each family has a unique origin.

                                                  The MET supplement to the Sabbat has quasi-creation rules (for MET, which isn't the same thing as the tabletop game even if there is some overlap) in the rituals involved and while it states there are variations there are certain core commonalities (again including the rituals) - but it's also covering a very Tzimisce-centric approach to it (indeed the suggestion is there is something about Tzimisce territory that makes it much easier for them to do it compared to others.)

                                                  But just because rituals are involved doesn't mean it is easily achieved. The rituals (and circumstances) seem to take it from 'nigh impossible' to 'low chance'. Revenants still have low birthrates and the supplement makes clear inbreeding is an unavoidable part of the process because of all the complications involved. Four rituals as outlined in the supplement. The first two cover male and female parts of conception and these simply gurantee a chance (not a certainty) that a potential revenant could be born. Then you have to enact a year long ritual once the potential revenant is born (with no guarantee of success once again) that will protect that potential further. Then there is a fourth and final ritual that can only be enacted on the third generation of (at the earliest) of breeding (meaning you also have to enact the first through third rituals on the parents and grandparents of this potential revenant) - the fourth ritual is meant to manipulate the vitae and completes the process, guaranteeing that a Revenant dynasty can be created (or breed true, although ghouls apparently can no longer be used to produce them... yet another reminder of how inbreeding is largely inevitable.) The last ritual is also year long before the child reaches puberty.

                                                  The only point that it gets relatively simple is when you have a viable breeding stock of Revenants (revenant parents can breed true with some degree of success, although the chances seem to vary). And there's plenty of details I'm leaving out (like the rituals being variations of debased fertility rights for women, or dark, highly symbolic rituals involving swords or impalement in some fashion for men. Or burying children created from the process in mounts of corpses for an entire day...) without getting into all the grimdark baggage that comes from simply being part of the sabbat or a revenant family.

                                                  The rituals aren't the problem. The challenge is adapting MET stuff (which seems to be revised edition) to tabletop which is challenging but not impossible even though the depiction of Revenants here is not even like the revised depiction. It has more in common with the implied depictions of Earlier edition revenants (in MET they can apparently learn intermediate or advanced, non-Elder discipline levels, one per century, and they can live for longer than 300-400 years - possibly as old as 500+ years. Which is similar to the 750+ age of 'Elder' revenants mentioned in the original Black Hand book.) They're also seemingly more in line with KOTE's Dhampyr which are very different from ToTB Dhampir or G:FA Revenants (which are just weak ghouls who can produce their own vitae.)

                                                  That said if those differences don't *bother* you MET might be a good source for a VtM versin of the KOTE Dhampyr like I also said (one idea I considered adapting was that the generation of the ghoul's domitor - and thus the blood they have consumed - will affect the potency of the potential revenant. Higher generation vampires might produce something little better than a weak ghoul, whereas a Revenant produced on Elder or Methuselah blood might be as potent as a higher-gen vampire. Of course the stronger the blood the older the ghoul is likely to be, and thus that could adversely affect the chances of reproduction even more adversely than they already are, since the curse is stronger and likely antithetical to life itself. The chance of vampiric flaws manifesting in a stronger ghoul can also be greater to balance out that power.)

                                                  But if one wants vaguely official 'rules' to build revenant creation off of then MET is probably your best bet.
