    Running the Free States - 4 - Canon Gangs & NPCs

    02-18-2025, 08:52 PM
    This thread is for discussion of actual canon gangs and NPCs from Los Angeles By Night sourcebook and other media (other sourcebooks, Bloodlines and other video games, etc.)

    Atticus Cleanclaw wanted someplace to comment on this thread from several years ago where I first laid out some of my ideas on making the Free States more interesting. I'll be using some of those ideas in my own actual chronicle I'm currently planning. But people are free to discuss any canon characters or coteries, and can take them in different directions than I indicated.

    The Los Angeles metropolitan area can hold something like 150-170 vampires per the typical rule, and if you consider an anarch domain likely has even more, there's immense number of NPCs. Of course, PCs won't run into every single one. A lot of vampires will just be window dressing. So I'm making a list of concepts I like from No One of Consequence thread on pre-made character templates found here. It is an easy way to create a lot of instant characters whenever I need some random vampire characters. I'm not using all the templates, and I'm adjusting them as needed like changing clans since I need a lot more Brujah and Caitiff than I would for most domains.

          Anyway, that little reminder of my nom-de-plume’s origins aside, I’m a little later than one meant to be posting my first notion for a canon, but hitherto un-described character with connections to LA in particular and the Free States in general.

          So, in lieu of anybody you’ve actually heard of, I proudly present to you -

          COUNT RIGATONI

          - The Tenth Generation Malkavian with my favourite name in VAMPIRE: THE MASQUERADE, the lick directly responsible for creating Bela aka Hollywood Dracula (AKA my favourite gonzo character in all Los Angeles) is described as someone who (In AD 1956) dressed up in the Classic movie vampire costume (Complete with cape and possibly-affected Hungarian accent), approached a first-class Bela Lugosi impersonator, offered to make him the Prince of Darkness and then proceeded to do just that (Whether the man actually said “Yes” at any point remains a riddle lost to the ages), at the low, low price of driving the poor man quite insane.

          - With that in mind, it occurred to me that if ‘Bela’ is explicitly
          built as THE Classic Movie Vampire, it might be fun to depict his sire as someone completely obsessed with Movie Vampires (and, to an only slightly lesser extent, vampire movies): somebody who adopts a series of guises very deliberately modelled on Classic (or at least popular) movie vampires and thereby offers us, somewhat in the style of a miner’s canary, an insight into how Pop Culture’s conception of vampires changed and evolved over the decades (Whilst never quite escaping the long shadow left by what arguably remains THE Iconic screen Dracula).

          - My mental image is of an American with immigrant parents (The name ‘Rigatoni’ is Italianate, with specifically-Sicilian antecedents, but since it suggests a pasta dish and not any kind of Patrician, I’m assuming that ‘Count Rigatoni’ is very, very not Italian: I’m tempted to make him an Irish-American as a nod to Mr Bram Stoker’s Irish roots) who was embraced by a Malkavian circa AD 1920 (A nod to the year in which Mr Bela Lugosi himself reached the USA) by a sire who took an interest in his career, but did something less than a bang-up job of selling their Childe on the lifestyle.

          - To say that the usual painful introduction to Kindred society (Coupled with the compelling, but not very attractive, vision of Graf Orlock in NOSFERATU) left the future ‘Count Rigatoni’ with somewhat melancholic tendencies would be putting things mildly: doing his best to soldier on regardless, Our Hero … well, our subject … cut his teeth, figuratively and literally, then went in search of an inspiration to carry him through the rest of his half-life existence.

          I’ll give you two guesses where he found it and the first one doesn’t count (No pun intended).


          DIGRESSION: As a further nod to Mr Bram Stoker, I’m tempted to make the character’s birth name something like ‘Isaac’ or ‘Jacob’ (Abraham - Isaac - Jacob).


          - Given the infinitely more glamorous vision of Mr Bela Lugosi smouldering away on the Big Screen (Also, THE BRIDES!) the newly-dubbed ‘Count Rigatoni’ went All In on emulating the accent, the style, the smoulder and then did his best to find a town where he could live his Best Life as a European expat doing his best to avoid the attempted extirpation of the aristocracy on that continent.

          It seldom worked for very long, but it usually worked just long enough to cheer him up and get him an entree into the more good-humoured elements of Kindred society (Note: it’s possible that ‘Count Rigatoni’ originally settled in Los Angeles because Don Sebastian was one of the few Camarilla princes inclined to treat him as a Court Jester, rather than an incitement to breach of the Masquerade - Cocaine by proxy is one Hell of a drug).


          - Given a new lease on life when the Free States were established (If only because the Anarchs were rather less systematic about enforcing potential Masquerade breaches and, it should be said, because ‘Count Rigatoni’ had been in LA long enough to relax his persona a notch or two, our subject attempted to return the favour when he learned that his onscreen idol was at Death’s Door, racing to extend the Embrace.

          Whether he found Bela Lugosi in no fit state to be considered for vampirism or genuinely confused a high-quality imitator for the man himself remains unclear (Bela’s memory is … well, anyway, ‘Count Rigatoni’ will neither confirm nor deny, but he transformed his only known Childe within 24 hours of Bela Lugosi’s death and that’s all we know).


          - Their relationship in the years since remains … complicated.

          For one thing, whenever the two of them are together ‘Bela’ tends to treat ‘Count Rigatoni’ as his very own Renfield (‘Rigatoni’ enjoys this treatment in surprisingly large doses, but does not have an infinite capacity for being Star-struck): for another, ‘Rigatoni’ tends to bring out those deeply, deeply repressed memories in ‘Bela’ which make things quite unpleasant for everybody in the vicinity (Especially Hollywood Dracula himself).

          There’s also the small, but significant detail that the two tend to be very, very obvious when together (and Count Rigatoni, not being a ‘National Treasure’ of the Anarch Free States, therefore tends to be sent off on his travels after a certain point, sometimes even quite peacefully).


          - When NOT coming to worship at the feet of The Master, ‘Count Rigatoni’ can usually be found keeping his cursed melancholies at bay by adopting the latest Pop Culture vampire trend (Usually, though not invariably, from the big screen), adapting his persona to match said trend, and finding some out-of-the-way pitch to pass himself off as the inspiration for (Rather than inspired by) that trend.

          While these efforts sometimes prove abortive, often enough they allow him to make a life in the local vampire community, generally allowing him to relax the persona into something a bit more naturalistic than affected (Sometimes for years at a time): in the end, however, the depressive (Arguably self-destructive) tendency gnaws away at his contentment - resulting in him either doubling down on his current persona until forced to flee or abandoning the persona and heading back to study at The Master’s feet once more (For some reason Hollywood Dracula always, always cheers him up), seeking further inspiration along the way.


          - Incidentally, ‘Count Rigatoni’ is not meant to be a ‘Fish Malk’ (He adopts these many variations on his deeply theatrical persona not for his own amusement or as part of a delusion, but as a coping mechanism intended to lend a little glamour to the fairly bleak lifestyle of the Kindred or allow him an escape from his own gnawing insecurities): he is, however, an Anarch (If only by virtue of the Camarilla having a much lower tolerance for his theatrics and the Sabbat having quite possibly the least friendly sense of humour in the World of Darkness) and can reliably be found in the vicinity of the Anarch Free States (All the better to provide an escape route).

          He’s an eccentric, but he’s not crazy stupid.


          - Incidentally, it should be noted that ‘Count Rigatoni’ bears a striking resemblance to Mr Peter MacNicol, a leading cause of his tendency to (A) periodically question his fitness as a vampire (B) devolve to the role of Vampire Renfield when in proximity to ‘Bela’ (C) Get into fights with those who take his attempts to embody the latest Hollywood Vampire trends exactly as seriously as they deserve, but fail to do so with any discretion.

          Given he’s still alive after many, many, many of these fights, ‘Count Rigatoni’ may be more dangerous than he looks.

              N.B. I’m assuming that - given his love of Vampire Fiction and general interest in being the Perfect Vampire, ‘Count Rigatoni’ has a surprisingly-extensive knowledge of vampire lore (Which may make him an excellent ‘spotter’ when it comes to a vampire seeking to conceal their identity behind some obscure reference and a valuable resource when trying to puzzle out the weakness of a more potent foe); I’m also fond of the notion that his commitment to theatricality, obsession with the ‘Ideal’ vampire and a certain amount of raw Ham make him surprisingly well-qualified to serve as a Harpy (Cutting overinflated egos down to size almost as ruthlessly as he surveys his own faults).

              Also, it deeply amuses me to imagine him as an old associate of Los Angeles’ very own ‘Surf Baron’ (One imagines that the latter’s commitment to making vampirism Fun is pure balm on Count Rigatoni’s frequently-troubled soul).

                      No One of Consequence, I believe that Free States LA does boast a Surfer Dude Baron - a Brujah, if memory serves, though one could also imagine a Toreador with an eye for athletics getting caught up with the ‘Aqua Licks’.

                      I recall Black Fox mentioning that he didn’t see the ‘Surf Baron’ as an actual Baron, per se - just the ringleader of a coterie (Possibly including the aforesaid ‘Aqua Toreador’).

                      Part of the reason I’m very grateful to him for creating this thread is that I wanted to push the notion that, while ‘Brujah Bodhi’ might not run an organised Barony (Insofar as Anarchs are ever organised), it’s conceivable that as the ‘Big Kahuna’ of the Surf-and-Turf vampires in LA and spokesperson of the ‘Sleep all day, party all night, never grow old’ Kindred (Not to mention an extremely popular, charismatic dude) he can wield influence and put together a posse that - especially when other Anarch leaders are talking ‘Big Time’ rather than ‘Fun Time’ - effectively allows him to function as a Baron, at least for short periods of time.

                      Even if (on a night-to-night basis) he functions as a dude with a coterie ‘and a whole lotta friends’, rather than a Baron (Especially if the coterie itself frequently serves as a hard core around which a floating population of casuals can condense into a bloc).

                      Basically, if anywhere in the world can produce a ‘part time baron, full time spokesman, party time dude’ it’s the Anarch Free States (and I think that could be a dynamic worth exploring, in terms of the unusual fluidity of the arrangement).

                              Black Fox, looking over your article on BLOODLINES characters in the ‘Anarch domains’ thread, it’s interesting to note that his sire appears to have been a female Malkavian committed to his asylum and that his Embrace appears to have gone unnoticed for some years (Suggesting that his Sire was either ignored or something of an unknown quantity) before he was approached by the Camarilla.


                              It’s also amusing to note that the game’s scriptwriters (either accidentally or on purpose) invented a new technique of medical (Psychological?) analysis called ‘dactopintalosm’ (With the name suggesting some sort of ‘phrenology for fingers).

                              With that in mind, it’s easy to suspect he might have been an old Victorian and that his sire may well have been one of the first Malkavians in the LA region (Which, coupled with his apparent preference for ghouls over childer, might help explain why the Clan of Mad Oracles has a relatively minor presence in the area).


                              Incidentally, the significant period of time between his Embrace and his acceptance into the Camarilla may help explain his survival of the Second Anarch Revolt - he wasn’t part of the Don Sebastian regimen for long enough to become a target (One can only wonder if he was spared, despite being an apparently-spontaneous Embrace, because either somebody in Don Sebastian’s service or the Don himself believed that having an in-house medical expert might help the Prince of LA cope with his alleged substance abuse issues).


                              Also, it’s downright hilarious to imagine such a solemnly Gothic and Science-minded Malkavian being obliged to deal with a Clan-mate (and for all we know a Brood-mate) willing to dub himself ‘Count Rigatoni’ and downright eager to embrace Bela Lugosi as the REAL Prince of Darkness.

                              ‘Bela’ he can deal with as a broken mind in the grip of a fiercely upheld delusion, but ‘Count Rigatoni’ does what he does by choice, as a coping mechanism which the ‘Good doctor’ finds deeply offensive.


                              On the other hand, if he ever perceived the true nature of the Voerman Sisters he’d probably have locked them up for intensive study (No euphemism here, he’d find their condition an absolutely fascinating case to study): otherwise I suspect he’s prefer the organised psychopath over the disorganised.

                                                  In LABN, Steve Booth's gang of vampire surfers is a very real thing. I'm just not using it in my chronicle. Booth will still be there with a small band of bro vampire surfers, but they're not some gang of warlord surfers, and I'm moving them from Long Beach to Santa Monica Bay. They'll be "slacker" vampires who pretty much just want to feed on drunk party goers and party on, and not really care about politics.

                                                  I don't intend them to be jokes because they're all physically fit and mostly Brujah, and Booth could be disruptive if he started caring about politics or if he felt his pals were being muscled out. However, I want them as a counterweight to the heavy politics a lot of other NPCs will be doing. I just have much more interesting factions I'd rather use as "gangs" or baronies.

                                                  Booth and his buds wander up and down the beach from Malibu to Palos Verde, and they even have a party boat they sometimes take down to Santa Catalina Island. They're harmless enough (and actually responsible in their feedings) that the barons of those area - Fortier, MacNeil, and even Geduld - tolerate them. They actually have a decent size herd of blood bonded mortal surfers that party with them, so they're mostly self sufficient.

                                                          One thing I need to do is decide how to portray the Sons of the Crypt. We're actually given very little information about them despite them being an important element in the setting. I'll be making a distinction between the vampire "gang" and the actual mortal gang they control because I just can't bring myself to use the same name for both. Your mileage may vary.

                                                          I don't want to get too much into its leader also being a secret member of the Sabbat. I will eventually post a separate thread on the Sabbat in LA. However, some of that will be addressed here.

                                                          I don't see the entire gang (LABN gives them 25 members, making them the largest gang/coterie) being Sabbat. That many would probably be exposed. But I expect there is a small core group of Sabbat (likely only Mohammed's childer, but possibly one or two recruits) inside the gang who comprise its leadership. I'm not sure what a good number of actual Sabbat inside the Crypts would be.

                                                          I imagine Mohammed teamed up with some surviving anarch Brujah to form the original vampire gang, and they were the original leadership. Most of them have "unfortunately" perished in their efforts at expanding territory, so Mohammed is the only surviving original member. To him, most of the Crypt's Sons will need to be destroyed once the Sabbat launch their next crusade. They are just disposable cannon fodder he uses to take out the anarch leadership. However, I imagine when the Sabbat do attack and the initial war bands assemble, he will offer some of the Sons the opportunity to join with the Sabbat and perform the vaulderie.

                                                          I imagine Mohammed poses as a Brujah. And I might even change his clan from Lasombra to Brujah anti-tribu. (To change things up, I may even have a vampire in some other "gang" have the Flaw where they do not cast reflections to make PCs think this other person must be Lasombra and therefore Sabbat).

                                                          Should every member of the Sons be recruited from the Crips (or one of their allied gangs)? That would limit them as a "gang", but maybe that's OK. There are certainly other vampires they might make use of, but given their extreme territoriality, I'd doubt they'd allow even allies/friends to feed. I think keeping a very strong "us versus them" attitude is essential for Mohammed to use them as a weapon against the other anarchs. So he might allow some exchange of boons or favors, but nothing like allowing them to feed on a regular basis. Only if they go full-in and join the Sons of the Crypts and accept Mohammed's absolute leadership would he allow them to feed. I think an all or nothing attitude is essential.

                                                          I suspect every member of the gang is Brujah vampire native to that part of the city (mostly black, but a few may be other ethnicities from ethnic gangs allied to the Crips). I'll be watching The Wire and stealing a lot of characters from that show to transplant them from Baltimore to LA just so I can have some real characters instead of goons without personality. For these neonates, a lot of their culture and loyalty is to Mohammed as one of their own, and wanting one of their own to be the real ruler of LA, not some Scotsman who's actually an elder in vampire terms.

                                                          Of course, I'll also have plenty of other black vampires not affiliated with the Sons. Mostly in the Barony of Angels, but also scattered in others.
