    Transylvania Chronicles, pt. 20: Drunk Storyteller Makes Shit up Edition

    09-20-2021, 01:09 AM
    Last installment's here.

    The last game session was thankfully short, meaning this will hopefully be a quick write-up. Some asshole in the game had a rough week at work trying to get a promotion, worked a half-day Saturday, and discovered the Oktoberfest beers were on the shelves picking up dinner on the way home. As it turns out, Yuengling Oktoberfest is pretty fucking delicious, and that asshole spent their Saturday day-drinking and was already half in the bag by the time the group even met on Discord.

    So anyhow, the last game session ended with the coterie day-sleeping in the castle, so as to not get murdered by Assamites or anything. They rose, went to court, and Eyulf tells them after sleeping on it and thinking about the sabotage in the city already, and the final death of Bulscu's latest childe, perhaps it's better if they leave Sibiu and join Bulscu. Eyulf has his reasons for wanting them out and the PC's didn't ask; if they had asked, Eyulf would have lied to them, but the bottom line is he doesn't want weak links in the city (and their exit will make for a nice distraction) for Assamites to murder and replace, or worse, coopt or find some way to control.

    Naturally, they want to return to their havens and make some last-minute preparations, and Eyulf grants their request, under the same caveats as before: no vampire goes unattended, every vampire must be accompanied by one with appreciable Auspex.

    So of course, the Tzimisce PC goes off on her own. She gets ganked. Her Auspex goes off passing by a beggar who had been outside the castle, and this beggar shifts into Zulo form and pulls a dagger made of bone; she takes a nice chunk of aggravated, and shifts into Zulo form in response. That triggers an unexpected response, as the vampire that attacked her stands down, apologizes, and introduces himself -- it's simply rude for one Tzimisce to attack another in the streets of another vampire's domain without a proper introduction, and all. The PC's merits -- unbroken lineage and prestigious sire -- are already paying off; once the other Tzimisce learns she's a Ruthven, any intent of simply killing her on the spot ends.

    This other Tzimisce -- an Anarch -- lets her know the Anarchs are helping the Assamites, mutual enemies and all, and the city was infiltrated the night before. He asks if the PC is still bound to her sire (she is), and if she is declared Anarch. When the PC replies no, the Tzimisce offers to teach her the vaulderie as reparation for his rudeness, and suggests she rethinks her allegiances once her mind is free of her sire's influence. They part ways, the Tzimisce returns to her haven inside the city, and collects what she thinks she will need for her exit.

    Now, I should mention at this point, we mutually agreed to run a revised version of the Fifth edition Tzimisce clan flaw when we read about it. Fifth edition conditions, older edition consequences. This Tzimisce's "domain" is her pack of selectively-bred hellhounds. Guess what her player deliberately chose to not bring along for the trip, under the assumption her hellhounds would be safer inside the city about to be besieged by 16,000 Ottomans (and also she didn't anticipate the trip taking longer than a single night for some oddball reason).

    Oops. She won't be forgetting that lesson for a while, I can promise you that.

    The Gangrel and Setite PC's go off on their own, because the Setite needed to see to his ghouls and make preparations for his cult. On their way back, their Auspex sense starts going off, and they realize they're being hunted. They start making their way through backalleys and trying to hide their trail, but with neither character having Obfuscate that wasn't happening; their Auspex sense just kept getting stronger, and they realized they were being herded. The Setite found and smashed through the back door of a shop and ran inside, the Gangrel behind. It's at this point fire pots start raining from the roofs above them, setting the alley and doorway on fire, forcing Rotshreck checks and blocking their way off; they have to go through the front of the shop to escape.

    They hear the sound of footfalls on the roof above them, and they run out the front. They immediately start getting pelted with arrows, and the elder Assamite's monster Dexterity, Archery, and Celerity ratings start paying off as he manages to do appreciable damage through sheer overage. The Gangrel doesn't know for sure but he suspects it, this is the same motherfucker that lit him up in the last game session. They manage to soak most of it, but the worst is to come as the Gangrel manages to look around with Auspex up, and notices an Assamite wielding dual blood-coated daggers leap from the roof within stabbing distance.

    This was the most pants-shitting moment of the entire game session, as this was one of the two Assamites that killed Bulscu's childe. One dagger had Scorpion's Touch at like five successes, the other had Baal's Caress at seven, meaning that dagger was doing Strength plus eight aggravated. The Gangrel basically had one round to not die, as he was higher on initiative than the Assamite and I'd already declared that Assamite was taking five actions -- two dodges, two stabs with the Scorpion's Touch dagger, one stab with the Baal's Caress dagger. The Gangrel took two actions, and went to town with his claws, managing to kill the Assamite before the latter could act.

    Next was the third most pants-shitting moment of the entire game session. The Setite spotted the Assamite sorcerer down the street, running around the alleyway corner and preparing to use blood sorcery. Another case of kill or get fucked as the sorcerer was about to drop level 4 Lure of the Flames on them both; not bad damage-wise, but the Rotshreck is a bitch. The Setite charged and took a big ol' chomp out of the Assamite sorcerer's face with Typhonic Maw, killing him on the spot.

    Then came the second most pants-shitting moment of the entire game session. That Assamite elder got tired of fucking around with his bow, drew his envenomed daggers, and started throwing. Luckily he only had two, but those two were enough to seriously fuck up the Gangrel with aggravated. The Gangrel and Setite ran for it, the former spending a Willpower point to ignore wound penalties, as the Assamite picked his bow back up and started pelting them with arrows again.

    Oh, those two players wanted a piece, but I pointed out despite it all they never saw the Assamite elder. Because dude has a Wits+Stealth pool of gangbusters, and I was rolling high on his rolls to stay Obfuscated after attacking. And, they realized they were woefully outmatched, even two-against-one. I said two weeks ago that Assamite in particular is no joke, and I wasn't kidding; that Assamite would've gone through them like toilet paper in a Chipotle bathroom.

    The Lasombra, however...oh, boy. The Lasombra went to Tenebrous form, straight back to his haven, made his arrangements, and got the fuck back out in Tenebrous form. He didn't have any issues, to the player's relief as he'd been in the hotseat a lot lately and needed the break.

    So, the excitement over, they got back to the castle and escaped using a new route Eyulf had dug, entirely separate from Zelios' designs. They got out, covered their tracks, and began the search for Bulscu's column of soldiers on their way to Sibiu. They needed to day-sleep, found a spot in the wilderness, and bunkered down. It's about here the Tzimisce's player realized how serious the Tzimisce clan flaw is.

    One day later, after more hours of searching, they found Bulscu's encampment. I took my time and made sure to drive home the unfamiliarity of the circumstances in which they found themselves -- with the exception of the Tzimisce PC, this was the first time any of them had seen Hussite war wagons or wagenburgs, arquebuses and cannons, and Hungarian hussars. I gave them proper time to ruminate on the fact they're borderline elders in a world rapidly evolving around, and beyond, them, surrounded by unfamiliar and alien inventions and signs of the times.

    They were ushered into Bulscu's tent, and he informed them that during the day, Gyorgy Lepes -- the bishop whose monetary fuckery had caused the Transylvanian peasant revolt -- was killed leading an attack on the Ottomans, many of his soldiers died, and the remainder rides to Sibiu to regroup. But his mortal pawn spoken about in his letter (Janos Hunyadi) lives, the decisive battle will be fought outside the walls of Sibiu, and his relief column must make haste to ensure they arrive before battle is joined.

          Yup, Hunyadi was appointed Voivode in 1441.

          How I'm planning the battle to play out is going to be...key to illustrating the differences between now and the Dark Ages, proper. Way back in the day, I had a huge battle scene that involved the Black Cross meeting in battle against Rustovich's forces. It was in one of my early, early reports -- the Black Cross formed a shield wall and held the pass in which they were fighting, while Jurgen sprung a trap of barrels of black powder buried in the mountains. When the barrels were lit and exploded it caused a rockslide, destroying much of Rustovich's forces and evening the field while Koldun duked it out with Tremere in a thaumaturgical battle that played out, well...everywhere.

          The peak of the battle for the PC's, was a few of them got pulled out of the shield wall and caught in a salient, being forced to fight szlachta around them while dealing with a fairly beefy Tzimisce elder using both Entrail Saraband and Living Testudo to offset action economy.

          Now, due to the march of technology (and that it was a daytime battle), they're going to have to find some place safe to sleep and hope for the best. When they wake, they'll have learned Hunyadi was able to rout the Ottomans with his battlefield subterfuge. Then does the fun begin, since they'll be in pursuit of the remaining Ottoman forces -- and the Assamites coming out to play, fighting rearguard actions to cover the Ottoman withdrawal.

          There'll be a time skip of a few months with a bit of a montage, as Hunyadi continues gathering forces and pursuing the Ottomans, culminating in the Battle of the Iron Gates. Which was a battle so huge, it did last well past nightfall -- though by that time, it had long been decided.

          I will say in advance, I have a bit of blue booking fun planned for...the story after next, assuming the next story gets run. If the players are good little Camarilla pawns and serve Bulscu well, they'll get an opportunity to blood hunt a couple members of the Conspiracy of Isaac. If they're not, I'll just skip straight to the first proper Dracula story.

          Either way, come the Siege of Belgrade, rumors will surface Cainite defenders of the city felt neither the weight nor burning of the sun during the siege's climax on the 22nd. Apparently, the combined prayers of all Europe for the defenders to succeed, resulted in a miracle and the Curse of Caine was lifted just a single day. Complete bullshit and propaganda of course, but you know vampires. Those who were there and those who claimed to be, will gladly attest to the miracle and claim they were the first to sally as the sun rose.

          [My players have been told repeatedly and are well aware my master blue book is from an in-universe perspective and reflects the sum knowledge available to PC's, and as such includes both misinformation and disinformation as befits the characters' limited knowledge bases, information networks, perspectives, and experience.

          Case in point, while the earlier master blue book had a hell of a lot of information on which Ventrue were doing what, it's all but barren of any mention of Ventrue aside from the odd instance in which a Ventrue's shenanigans intersects a player character's. Because there's no longer a Ventrue PC in the game to whom that information would be relevant or knowable. Likewise, Setite info has been woefully lacking for the duration the Setite PC was isolated from his clanmates, but now that he's back in communicado, it's trickling back in.

          Or, in the case of player characters' dealings with Vykos, for the duration that was occurring the master blue book was absolutely chock full of disinformation about the Tremere. Now that Vykos' little game is up, they're getting much clearer and accurate information about the clan.]
