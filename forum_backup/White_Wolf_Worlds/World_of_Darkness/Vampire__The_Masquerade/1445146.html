    The shaal fragment thread

    04-21-2021, 06:56 PM
    After looking across the forum, I'm surprised that there's not much talk about the shaal fragment in BJD, so I guess I'll start one.

    According To BJD, the shaal fragment is a rare part of the book of nod which is possessed notably by Vitel, which allow Beckett to study it, even though he hide certain part of it (notably prophecies about a certain gangrel).

    Here's the fragment with Beckett's commentary


    " And when She (1) made preparation to return, and the world broke in two.
    The Childer of Caine on one side, The Childer of Lilith (2) on the other, And it was Gehenna.
    They named her Shaal for her questions (3), Though her names were many and maddening to hear.
    The Childer's Childer recognized opportunity in chaos. Arikel (4) sought unity, Ashur (5) sought divinity, Loz (6) sought war, Malakai (7) sought peace.
    Malkav sought wisdom through bargain with Ilyes (8), Who told his brother, "Your wisdom shall come with the centuries of madness, Allowed to purify into something whole, The vitae of the mad will pour afresh in years to come. The old forms shall wither on the vine, And declare the end times. Only the children of Enoch, Shall see the new age (9)."
    The breaking of the world near complete, The sacrifice of a generation prevented Shaal's Gehenna (10). She would try again, And again. Each time a new sacrifice, More burdensome than the last (11).
    Malkav's blood shall pour forth, The Book of Chaos in the Underworld written (12), To prevent Shaal's Gehenna. Sargon's get will understand, But they will not share their understanding. The Brides of the Dragon will understand, But their fee will be high (13).
    Her hatred is as the phases of the moon. Gehenna follows the path of Her ire.
    Beckett's commentary:

    1. If She is “returning,” who is She? The Crone, perhaps? Zillah?
    2. Not the first time I’ve seen a reference to the childer of Lilith. No reason the Clans may not have separate progenitors, ultimately.
    3. Shaal is “to ask” in Hebrew.
    4. Most Toreador I know of revel in disharmony. A shame they’ve fallen so far from the tree.
    5. Could this be a reference to Capadocius?
    6. This name is unknown to me.
    7. As is this one. Loz and Malakai could be childer or grandchilder of Lilith, of course.
    8. Ilyes is one name I’ve seen given to the Brujah Antediluvian. Not a typical recourse for wisdom.
    9. Far too much ambiguity in this statement for my tastes.
    10. Could this be why the Second Generation fell?
    11. If this truly is a part of the Book of Nod, it’s the only one to refer to multiple Gehenna events.
    12. A Malkavian is said to have penned the Book of the Grave-War. Was he compelled to do so by an elder of his bloodline?
    13. If the Dragon is Dracula(as was once assumed), a trip to his castle may be in order. "




    A few commentaries of my own


    "The Childer of Caine on one side, The Childer of Lilith (2) on the other"

    I don't think this is litterally about descendant of Lilith against those of Caine, but more of a form of allegeance, the antediluvians chosed their side. We know of two antediluvians who clearly joined Lilith: Malakai and Mekhet. As the bahari hate the "classic" 13 antediluvian, one can pressume the 13 known joined caine.

    Following such assumption, we could conclude that the caine side won, and the lilith sided antediluvians were killed or trapped and erased from cainite history, perhaps to stop their childer from following their path. Malakai is described as being trapped in the underworld and Beckett, in his malkav fueled visions in BJD says "Mekhet is free and Malakai follows".


    "The Childer's Childer recognized opportunity in chaos. Arikel (4) sought unity, Ashur (5) sought divinity, Loz (6) sought war, Malakai (7) sought peace."

    The mention of "the childer's childer" imply that the 2nd generation also took part of this war, and the 3rd conspired for their own interest.
    Ashur in that case could refer to Saulot, Capadocius or the Eldest, but the quest for divinity is closer to the plan of Capadocius.
    Loz is one of the name scribed on the tombs of the Aralu in Enoch. That give weight to the idea that the Aralu, or at least some, were allies of Lilith, following my previous theory of the erasure of the losers, the fact that Loz is an unknown antediluvian put him in the Lilith side.
    Malakai is described as the first collateral victim of the Jyhad, becoming mad alongside her brother. She is totally unstable, able to disform reality with her madness. Seems legit that she seek peace, howether, the fact that she seek peace imply that she was already mad, while the following verse mention Malkav getting his madness following a bargain.

    "Malkav sought wisdom through bargain with Ilyes (8), Who told his brother, "Your wisdom shall come with the centuries of madness"

    Ilyes being the true Brujah progenitor, he would have been able to prophecie that with his mastery of temporis.

    "Allowed to purify into something whole, The vitae of the mad will pour afresh in years to come."

    I take this as a prediction of what happens in the chapter Madness in Jerusalem. Malkav merging with his clan, making it more "oracle" than "lunatic".

    " The old forms shall wither on the vine, And declare the end times. Only the children of Enoch, Shall see the new age (9)"

    As BJD was leading the way to V5, I see this as speaking about the Beckoning, the old forms being snapped away. It could also be about the Withering, the loss of power of the old vampire in the no longuer canon Gehenna book.

    "Malkav's blood shall pour forth, The Book of Chaos in the Underworld written (12), To prevent Shaal's Gehenna"

    The chapter were the fragment is presented mention that this book is indeed the book of the grave-war. This book being about breaking blood bonds and hunting the shadow sleepers (one appearing in Children of the Blood), we can assume those play a role in Gehenna.

    " Sargon's get will understand, But they will not share their understanding. "

    Sargon is Capadocius real name according to dark ages novels. his get might be referencing to Lazarus and the other Capuchin members, who prepare an end of the world of their own.

                                  When I read it, my impression was that it was very old and that several of the things it refers to are in the rearview mirror today. Also likely a compilation from different sources.

                                  1: Probably Lilith. Could be the Crone, Atiratu, last daughter of Eve or Zillah.
                                  2. Could also be another kind of hungry dead, or supernatural.
                                  3. Likely Shaal is the "she" of the first line.

                                  "The Childer's Childer recognized opportunity in chaos" -This sounds like a prophecy of, or reference to, the Fall of the First City.

                                  4. Arikel is generally considered the firstborn of his/her generation.
                                  5. Ashur is a name that has been associated with both Cappadocious and Saulot, both of which looked to ascension to win the Jyhad.
                                  6- Loz is the name of one of the Aralu. It has been tentatively connected with the Toreador clan founder. Which is more commonly said to be Arikel. Although some older Toreador claim that Arikel was not the clan founder. Loz is one of the people Iontius can draw from memory.
                                  7. Malakai was one of the "Fair is Foul" weirdness. Malkavs sister.
                                  8. Ilyes is Brujah. And probably Typhon.
                                  9. The Setites hold the site of Enoch, but the occasional really low-generation Ravnos seem to pop out of there.


                                  "Your wisdom shall come with the centuries of madness, Allowed to purify into something whole," Sounds like a prophecy of Malkavs becoming the madness network.

                                  9. No idea.
                                  10. Seems a reference to a cyclical Gehenna. Could refer to either the fall of the second generation or the loss of the original fourth generation. Could also be a reference to the wars against Shaitan, from the second city and at Knossos. Shaitan has tried to break the world again and again. Could Shaar be Shaitan/Rayzeel?
                                  11. Chaos in the Underworld may refer to the sixth maelstrom.

                                  Sargon would probably be the author of the Sargon fragment. It has been listed as the mortal name of Cappadocious. "Sargons get" could be the Cappadocians, the Harbringers, or just one of Capadocious childer.

                                  Saulot turned out to be the Dragon. The brides of the dragon could be the Tremere Inner council or the childer of Saulot that the Inner Council diablerized, if Saulot was indeed bringing them back.

                                  13. The last daughter of Eve is supposed to have a moon as her mark.
