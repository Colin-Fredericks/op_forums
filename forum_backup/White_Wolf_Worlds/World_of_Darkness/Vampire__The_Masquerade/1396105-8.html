                    Originally posted by Taggie View Post

                    I honestly don't see what the Old Clan do that the Ventrue and Lasombra don't already cover
                    Honestly, Taggie it may be one of those things where we're just coming at it from such different angles that we're never going to be able to reconcile. In my case, I love the Old Clan Tzimisce more than I ever love the Lasombra or Ventrue while feeling "ehh" for the mainstream clan that I feel like gets dominated by Vicissitude Flesh Monkeys a bit too much (i.e. people who only care about the Discipline vs. the actual Clan Culture).

                    But for me, the Old Clan Tzimisce bring to the table a much grander focus on the feudal lord element than either and make their own. Ventrue characters have all since gone onto be merchants, CEOs, and businessman that are obsessed with accumulating power. They are people who are snobby elites that have a flaw and Disciplines that reflects their removal from the common people as well as power to manipulate. The Lasombra, by contrast, have powers related to the Stygian darkness and controlling people while also being people who represent manipulators that control people through religious devotion (be it church or Sabbat).

                    The (Old Clan) Tzimisce, by contrast, are a beautiful encapsulation of "The Lord is the Land and the Land is the Lord." Their Disciplines give them the ability to control the animals, survey their territory, and impress their will upon the peasantry in a way that makes them the absolute masters of their domains. It is a much more intense relationship to Domain than either the Ventrue or Lasombra have because they can just pack up and leave anytime they want. Tzimisce, by contrast, ARE defined by their love of the land and soil that it might as well be their own flesh.

                    Their attitudes towards honor are also completely different. The Ventrue have completely abandoned honor in the 21st century, now defined by slimy weasals like La Croix, Lodin, and Hardestadt (who is a fallen Jurgen as far as I'm concerned). Even Jan Pieterszoon doens't care about the letter OR spirit of his agreements. The Lasombra are a clan of liars and manipulators worse than anyone save maybe the Followers of Set (and I argue they're at least honest among themselves).

                    The Old Clan Tzimisce by contrast are people for which the bond of your word, domain, and personal honor are absolute. They may be alien inhuman monsters with Roads dating back to Pre-Christian times but they take this shit seriously. You can expect them to heroically fight off a bunch of your enemies as long as you're their guest but may flay your skin off slowly for a 1000 years if you insult their ghoul dogs.

                    A lot of the Old Clan Tzimisce carry a sense of antiquity, responsibility, and power that being a vampire lord should. If you're a fan of Strahd, Dracula, or Carmilla then they are the vampires for you. It's also kind of cool that it's a dying clan like the Salubri as the modern world leaves them behind. They cannot change because their clan is all about the Good Old WaysTM and slavish devotion to ancient tradition but that doesn't make it wrong. They also have all the cool stuff of the mainstream Tzimisce clan like the Revenant families, just without the gross monsters and even then they still have hellhounds and probably summoned Koldunic monsters.
