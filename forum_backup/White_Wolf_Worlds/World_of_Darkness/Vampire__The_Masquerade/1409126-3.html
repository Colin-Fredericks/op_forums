                                You know, this scenario doesn't have to involve any sort of East versus West conflict. If the hungry dead are the dominant form of vampire across the world and have been so for millennia, then they would be as natural to Europe as they are to Eastern Asia. Vampires would escape from Hell, normally perceived in a Christian sense, and inhabit their undead bodies. The Yin, Yang, and Po would be referred to with different terms, likely involving the trappings of Western religion or spirituality.

                                In fact, the traditional mythic vampire of Europe is more similar to a Kuei-jin than a Kindred. Traditional European vampire myths usually involved a person who died and then arose from the grave because of something bad about their life or death - not being transformed by another vampire.

                                In this alternative setting, nobody would see these vampires as inherently Asian, they are just the kind of vampires you have everywhere.
