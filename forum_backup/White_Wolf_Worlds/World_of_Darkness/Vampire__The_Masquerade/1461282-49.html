                                To begin with, I'll always repeat that pre-V5 Camarilla don't have the Scourge as an official sect position and this change in nuCam is idiotic. I won't buy those arguments for the Scourge if I don't buy their premises.

                                Assuming the Prince just want to get rid of Thin Bloods, the first thing to look up is how other Kindred in the domain look upon them. If they see a Thin Blood as an easy to control servant, as a safe citizen, or what. Remember that Thin Blood hatred is based on a very specific reading of a very specific legend. To buy into it you have to both believe in the prophecy and in the specific interpretation. After all, they're given as a sign, not a cause.

                                For many (how many no one knows) Thin Bloods aren't worse than any other high generation. Which means they're actually good as they can't raise to your power level even with time, and being unable to sire is just icing on the cake as they won't compete with you for this right, ever.

                                Second, Thin Bloods don't have rat faces and five arms. No one is stupid to think you can just look at someone and say if they're a Thin Blood. Someone walking around and offing people first, justifying later they were "Thin Blood" isn't really helping your cause. This is not about humanizing them, this is about trust.

                                In third, the Scourge isn't just about Thin Bloods. It is about everyone the Prince deems "undesirable". Of those, only the Thin Bloods may be "dehumanized", all the others are pretty much Kindred. So it's still empowering someone to execute people without proper judgment. By the way a judgment that was explicitly considered proper protocol specifically for unsanctioned Embraces until yesterday.

                                You totally can have the Sheriff doing this stuff. You'll just be giving a little more work for them, inside their obligations anyway. It is not like a Kindred domain have a huge population where the police's phone never stops ringing. You can also have an executioner that isn't also judge and jury, and/or an investigator that acts as "sanitary authority" and dehumanize Thin Bloods without raising so many concerns for everyone else's safety.

                                Now, can I picture some Princes going forward with that idea? Absolutely. Especially powerful tyrants that do rule even other elders by fear alone, or wannabe tyrants too stupid for their position. Those Princes do exist, for various reasons. Can I picture this becoming an official Camarilla policy regardless of very specific local circumstances? Only in a version of the setting where the Inner Circle went through dramatic brain surgery and are still too drugged to think straight with the neurons left.

                                        Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
                                        The worst possible choices? Wouldn't put it past the idiocy of the nuCam.
                                        Actually it would be a great position for people like that. The Scourge goes around dealing with people who aren't in the Camarilla - thin-bloods, people who have refused to introduce themselves to the prince for whatever reason, unsanctioned vampires, the Sabbat, Anarchs, etc.

                                        The Scourge doesn't really operate within the Camarilla hierarchy, he can't make demands of other vampires in the city and most people just generally view the Scourge as the Prince's lapdog and hate him. This means that politically the Scourge has no future, and moreover is pretty much forever tied to the Prince. If the Prince were to perish the his Scourge would probably get killed too. At best maybe the Scourge could become the new Prince's Scourge. Further the Scourge is pretty disposable - if the Prince wants to kill the Scourge he can do it, come up with some semi-plausible reason ("We caught the Scourge engaging in unauthorized diablerie") and nobody is going to cry for the Scourge. No one with a decent understanding of Camarilla politics is going to volunteer for that shitshow of a job, so there are two people the Prince will pick.

                                        The first group are people who honestly enjoy going around killing others. The very real monsters and bullies who don't mind being a a killer. These guys could even be serial diablerists (and as long as they're snacking on people who aren't a part of the Camarilla the Prince can look away and dispose of them if they fuck up and get caught). These people don't mind or don't care that they're going to spend unlife working for the Prince and getting no real respect from anyone else.

                                        Alternatively the Prince will take someone he doesn't necessarily trust or someone that he doesn't want to see rise in the city's politics and "make them an offer they can't refuse," essentially forcing them into being his Scourge. This is where you might put an Assamite or an ex-Sabbat. It keeps them occupied and away from the levers of power in the city and ensures that nobody is really going to want to work with them. It also ensures that they have to present a modicum of loyalty to the Prince, since if the Prince dies they'd likely be next (plus the Prince has an easier time of disposing of them if he feels it's necessary).

                                        Meanwhile with the Sheriff, the Prince is only going to want to appoint someone he can trust to the position (it's likely the Sheriff will eventually find out some of the skeletons in the Prince's closet) and further the Sheriff very much is not disposable. If the Scourge dies it's easy to pick a new guy and tell him, "Go find unsanctioned vampires and kill them." If the Sheriff dies it could take years or even decades for a new one to get up to speed on what's going on in the city, especially if the previous one didn't leave any records behind.
