    [V20] Path of the Focused Mind

    04-09-2024, 04:01 PM
    Greetings,

    I am not sure of how the rules for Path of the Focused Mind work.


    FIRST QUESTION:

    Originally posted by Rites of the Blood p. 139
    Unlike most Thaumaturgy, the
    powers of this Path do not require an action to invoke    ,
    although they still require the normal blood expenditure
    and Willpower activation roll. Thaumaturges with this
    Path may use any single power from this Path during a
    single turn.

    • Readiness
    ...
    System: This power is only usable on the thaumaturge
    herself. Every success on the activation roll adds one die
    to a special dice pool for the remainder of the turn.     These
    dice may be used on any Wits-related rolls or actions that
    the thaumaturge performs during this turn. Alternatively,
    each die removed from the dice pool adds one to the
    caster’s initiative rating.
    If initiative is rolled before the turn starts, how can the use of the power help you at all? My understanding is: you roll initiative, THEN you can use your Path of Focused Mind 1st dot power on your turn, the special die pool could be used for the initiative BUT it was already rolled... So what good is it for if it only last one turn?

    Can someone clarify?






    SECOND QUESTION:
    The 4th dot power describes:

    Originally posted by Dual Thought, Rites of the Blood P. 140
    System: Successful use of Dual Thought allows the
    caster to take two actions without penalty during her turn    .
    (Note that this power specifically lifts the restrictions of​
    multiple actions detailed in V20, p. 248.) The extra action
    granted by this power must be a mental action    , whether
    it’s the use of Disciplines such as the use of Auspex or
    Thaumaturgy, or the contemplation of some problem.
    If the character is using both actions to solve a problem,
    she has two separate dice pools to draw from. These two
    actions happen at the same time, as determined by the
    initiative rating of the character. You may not use the
    extra action to re-cast Dual Thought.​
    If the powers do not require an action to invoke, and you get a normal and an EXTRA mental action, could you use the normal action to recast Dual Thought? The restriction is on the extra action, not the normal one as I can read.


    Thanks,

      Off hand those look like poorly written powers.

      1) Yeah, I don't think it can work as written. Each turn your roll your Initiative you roll a d10 and add your Dex+Wits. So let's say the character goes 3rd. When their turn comes up, they can't increase their Initiative, or at least it won't do any good because the other two characters have already acted. Further, the pool only lasts for the one turn, so they can't spend points to increase their initiative for the next turn.

      My recommendation is to allow a character to spend points to increase their initiative for the next turn. So if they take 4 points away, then the next time they roll Initiative at the top of the next turn, they'll get an extra 4 points to the roll. Alternatively, you could let a character activate this power outside of the normal action sequence, while Initiative is being rolled, letting them choose to boost their initiative.

      2) By RAW, no, you can't use your normal action to re-activate the power because you don't use actions to activate the power in the first place. I think it's pretty clear that the author forgot that Focused Mind powers don't require actions to activate. Technically you could activate it an unlimited number of times per turn. So what happens? One ruling is that it would just let you take an unlimited number of actions (until you run out of blood point). That's obviously busted as fuck. Alternatively there could be another reading focusing on the first bolded line the main thing - it allows the caster to take 2 actions without penalty per turn. So if you've already taken two actions this turn, activating it again won't add more actions, the power will just be wasted. I think the easiest solution is to just rule that the power can only be activated once per turn.

              As for question 1, it is as voidshaper said. Reflexive actions like spending blood are still actions and yet don't happen according to initiative. The powers of the Path are not even actions, they are faster than reflexive actions.

              As for question 2, there is another limit to this path that isn't in what you quoted here @Ibeaumanior, namely that can only use one power of this road, once, per turn. So casting Dual Thought twice is not possible anyway. The authors specified it just in case players and ST forgot that limitation (which is written in the same paragraph that says powers from this path don't require actions to cast).
