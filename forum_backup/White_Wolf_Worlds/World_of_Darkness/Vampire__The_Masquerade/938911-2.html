                My Antediluvians are vastly powerful and vastly limited. Being closer to the source of vampirism, they have more power, but the curses also have a much stronger grip on them, and are harder to blunt or shake. Sunlight comes down like a hammer, and despite their mastery of disciplines, there is no power or ritual that will let them soak it with stamina. They needed more kine blood to sustain themselves and fuel their powers when they were young, but now they (mostly) have advanced cases of Methuselah thirst, where only the 4th or 5th generations vitae will quench their thirst. Once again, powers and disciplines that Kindred further away from the source can use to lighten the vitae cost of disciplines and actions do not function for them. Fire burns hotter when it burns them. Those that still have physical forms have grown less human and more vampire in visage over time.

                Saulot sacrificed most of his clan and centuries of time, but is currently in control of a body that has not yet fully developed Methuselahs thirst, allowing an unprecedented freedom to act.

                Antediluvians are very different from each other though. Most seek to transcend their curses in some manner. Spiritually, biologically, or through magic. Most are on some path of enlightenment, although not all.

                They spend most of their time in torpor, making subtle moves with blood-cheap disciplines. Occasionally they wake, devour those of their childer and grandchilder too slow or dim to protect themselves, and make moves in the temporal world, then sink back into glutted torpor like fat ticks.

                I don't hold with the direct assumption that Antediluvians have 10 in the three disciplines of their clans. Most are less tied to clan disciplines than their descendants assume. Also, I don't hold level 10 disciplines to be part of the linear xp progression. Level 10s are mastery on an almost conceptual level, and a degree of magnitude greater than level 9. It generally takes millennia to master one. After 15 000 years, the antes average two each. Absimilliard, Tremere and Augustus Giovanni have no level 10s. The ones concentrating on spiritual transcendence and esoteric matter normally have more than the more temporally oriented ones.

                Antes do, however, have a monstrous number of dots in disciplines in general, and can be expected to have multiple level 8 and 9 powers in all the common disciplines.

                Also, I've assumed that despite the reputation of Caine as the source and inceptor of all disciplines, beating the original clan disciplines out of the metal of the Curse was not easy and took much longer than assumed. Many of the tales of the first and second city, with kindred fighting like cats in a sack, makes more sense if viewed through the perspective of Antediluvians being limited to the first agricultural civilizations as the only areas with the population density to sustain them, and initially lacking the disciplines to just go off to some remote corner of the world and feeding on nomads.

                        One of my favorite character sheets comes from Legend of the Five Rings - specifically, from Way of the Dragon in the game's first edition. In that edition, they "statted" Togashi Yokuni, champion of the Dragon Clan.

                        Togashi Yokuni was actually Togashi, the legendary founder of the clan, in a secret disguise as one champion of the clan or another for over a millennium at that point. Already a god when he descended in the beginning of civilization, Togashi had become terrifyingly powerful after 1100 years on Ningen-do, and in fact, his stat block basically can be summed up with the line "Yokuni gets what Yokuni wants." Unless you are threatening him with an entity of similar power (i.e. an Elemental Dragon), you lose unless he doesn't want you to.

                        And that's how I see Antediluvians. Unless you're playing Methuselah, you're not going to be in the range of power needed to threaten an Antediluvian directly. You're just not. They've all mastered their own Disciplines to a degree that you cannot fathom (even with just level 9 disciplines, you're doing things most vampires can't understand), and have likely mastered many others to a degree that is still beyond non-Elders' capability of achieving. Their mundane potential at tasks that don't involve modern knowledge (and in some cases, even those), before factoring in powers of the blood, can outpace a neonate or ancilla who isn't dedicated to the same task or using their powers to try to match.

                        Ultimately, to threaten an Antediluvean, you need either significant planning or significant power (see the bit about Methuselahs), and likely both. Now, their plans you can potentially undermine - their plans are subtle and vast, but they still can be unbalanced or upset by someone with enough resources. But to actually fight an Antediluvean, especially one who's been active for a while rather than just sleeping, is insanity in these nights in my view.

                                        One misconception that I'd like to add here is the meaning of "time" in cainites. The power of the cainites is in their blood, their essence, it's a metaphysical concept, it's not like a simple formulae like: "oh you have 15.000 years of existence, so you should be able to master a discipline to level 10..."hell no! Time is just one of the aspects of the vampiric existence and there are MANY rituals of all sorts that allow a cainite to absorb the powers and knoledges of another cainite (Blade of the Forbidden Flower, of the Tremere even allows you to place the essence of another cainite's abilities and disciplines into a weapon and there are many other similar powers...), and I'm not even mentioning the fabled Amaranth. With that being said, it's pretty obvious that if you would make a character sheet for an antedeluvian like Augustus Giovanni or Tremere (something imo completelly unnecessary...) you should give them a level 10 in their field of expertise (i.e: Thaumaturgy and Necromancy etc). This is so obvious that, with all due respect, it's hard for me to read somebody arguing that it shouldn't be the case when it's so obvious and you can even see it in the cannon etc...The World of Darkness isn't a linear and simplist world like that of D&D etc (with all due respect for the players of these systems and worlds, as I'm only implying that they work with different aproaches to their respective realities). What has always attracted me, as an amateur philosopher myself, is this very aesthetic of WoD, I simply cannot understand why people argue over such irrelevant issues as "what should the powers of a given cainite be etc...", it all depends on the World that the storyteller wants, it should fit their story and that's all. In short, nothing is "simple" in the WoD.

                                                First of all, the freebies/xp points given to a character are just guidelines (as MANY other things in the WoD), as a matter of fact one would expect someone to have acquired more knoledge if he or she had more time to do so (or at least the "common sense" see it this way...), but this is just a guideline, it's basically just one of many factors. People are different and so are cainites, there are average people and kindreds and gifted individuals who can do more with little time, so to speak. There are also genius who are able to come up with groundbreaking inventions/discoveries, like an Einstein, a Gutenberb, a Karl Benz etc and the same goes when one speak of cainites. So, with this in mind, every one is unique, it's not just math that defines what one should be able to do with a given time, again, as I mentioned, it's all subjective. Finally, getting to the point, cainites have their powers and even knoledges in their essence that is in their blood, and there are many ways to get this power/knoledge (i.e: Disciplines etc) by consuming the cainite's essence, be it through the well known act of amaranth/diablerie or through rituals that consume and therefore transfer all of the cainite essence to another cainite.
