                                    I think the biggest power in the Antes is to control their children. For me, no Blood Bond is the same as the one with your forefather/mothers, and they can simply can command or subtly affect their descendants. So if you really want to destroy an Antediluvian level Cainite, it either must permit you, or you will need the help of other lineages. Divide and Conquer is really the biggest advantage and chance of success to challenge the 3rd gen. But hell, Antes are so alien and lost in their own millenniums-long ambitions and plans that they probably out of themselves and not really lucid.

                                    Yes they can and have been killed. Thirteen have just been the most prolific, and some of them were cannibalized, while other 'clans' of ancient times were eradicated or made so small nobody knows or cares; hell, there might be some on a Masquerade against other vampires just to survive them (like Inconnu or that group of weirdos in BJD). But they have been dead, and from some only echoes remains, cuz the power of the Blood and their Curse don't just disappear, they spread, and so might their mandates.

                                    We don't talk about the Beasts of the Antediluvians, but I also don't see them following Paths, but their Banes and Beasts would be insurmountable. So something happened... Who knows, maybe their banes were simply ways to let their Beasts empty their strength, or to really bind them. Or maybe Caine (or whoever) did it, but for similar reasons. Either way, I also think something happened, like, I believe there is a pact of some sorts between the Antediluvians. Like of mutual non-aggressions, even if just after the 2nd City. So they can't really fight each other, just scheme with their childer. And hell, maybe it goes further, maybe Nod and Caine is all a big freaking sham.

                                    Maybe the known Antes are really just old Methuselahs who nobody can prove are not the founders. Cuz yeah, for me the power between Methus and Antes is really close. And both are alienated and incomprehensible, and don't care anymore to be so; Japphet and Cappadocius tried to be understood , but the Children of Isaac and Camarilla and everyone else just thought "I don't get it, it seems really big and bad, just better be safe and fuck it, get this old man out of here". And so they let thigs play out and maybe it worked and we are just to ignorant, or maybe we screwed up their plans, and will never know if it would indeed be better.

                                    In the end, they are like trying to understand the origin of life on Earth, you may get some really good theories and ideas and proofs, but hell, it is so hard to interpret. And worst, you can't even investigate very well, cuz much of this was manufactured and/or falsely stated, and for a society that lives in the shadows of humanity you can't expect clarity and true-to-facts history (cuz that doesn't really exist).
                                    But they are a fun concept and theme to explore. Especially because everyone has a different idea, and my PCs can see clearly that someone of thousands of years has a lot of different lives, characters and faces. And some of the strongest themes for ancient vampires and the young ones is that of history repeating, the curse of patricide and fratricide.

                                    Strange... When coincidence seems too convenient, I prefer to call it fate.

                                    -Blood Omen: Legacy of Kain d=

                                            "How powerful are yours?"
                                            As powerful as the pcs believe them to be
                                            "What can they do?"
                                            Whatever the characters imagine they can and credit them with.
                                            "What CAN'T they do?"
                                            Actually turn up in a story except in rumours of their involvement.
                                            "And what does it take to kill one?"
                                            The end of existence, as they're either intrinsically bound to some facet of existence itself and beyond mortal reach and comprehension (such as the Abyss, or the Earth itself, or whatever), or they're an idea that never actually existed in the first place and vampiric society is just a big paranoid superstitious mess so they'll stop existing when you stop thinking they do.
                                            -
                                            The Antedeluvians - and their power levels - should forever stay a mystery, as far as I'm concerned. They're more effective that way.
                                            Maybe they're pulling the strings behind politics. Maybe it's a garden variety methuselah or elder. Maybe you're just paraoid.
                                            The horrific and confusing events that sometimes take place, leaving death and destruction in their wake, might be the end times and maybe it's just a bad Tuesday. I will never explain anything, just leave little hints that might just be lies.

                                            If I decided to confirm their existence with their turning up, there would be no fighting them.
                                            A) they aren't going to fight you because they don't actually realise you exist in anything more than an abstract way. Like you know air particles are a thing, and you might send them flying when you exhaule, but you don't really perceive them on your level of existence.
                                            B) As said; merged with cosmic forces. Pick a fist fight with the sun for all the effect you'd have.

                                            (We do not acknowledge the Week of Nightmares under my roof. That was all one giant reality-exploding fever dream due to unknown mystical forces. The Ravnos are fine. And Cappadocius was actually never the real ante for the Clan of Death. "Who was then?" "How was Cappadocius 3rd generation?" We just don't know. It's one of nature's mysteries. The deity Hekate. It's the founder Clan of Death, man, dying and growing a new one is totally in theme for them. etc, etc.)
