          In the case of Caine, he wasn't actually dead yet and possibly his Avatar was never twisted like his creations, who are a wholly different breed of creature than Caine himself. He learned to pass on his curse after all. They're effectively the results of a magical rote like the Men in Black or Hit Marks or zombies versus Caine himself, a mage.

          Or the story is all metaphorical.

          What she taught him, though, appears to have been Hedge Magic that took the form of the 13 Disciplines. So, if we were to analyze vampirism scientifically, vampires are all Blood Mages.

          It's just Thaumaturgy is the most magic-like of them all.

          To answer your second question. Since Lilith would most likely fall under the Verbena banner, she would have better understanding of blood magic, and that's why Disciplines are fueled by blood. Hermetic mage would probably just make Caine, Dr.Strange-like character.
          One thing to note is that the Traditions didn't exist prior to the Dark Ages as we know them and, furthermore, True Magic wasn't divided into Nine Spheres. Magic was a lot more flexible and the divide between True and Hedge magic wasn't 100% real yet.

          Basically, the Traditions did a Scientific Revolution that made magic what it is today.
