      We don’t know anything about the top level yet, but we do know a few ground-level things:

      1) the Vienna chantry was destroyed by the 2nd Inquisition in what is probably their biggest raid to date. If you’re wondering how they got into such a well magicked stronghold, technology notwithstanding, then you’re thinking correctly. Somebody up the chain had to let them. Possibly Saulot, or possibly Tremere or Carna trying to get st the Saulot-controlled portion of the clan.

      2) the rupture if the pyramid and the Council of Seven has caused the Tremere blood to reject the clan bond, leading to the possibility of splinter houses. Carna’s faction has split into its own house within the clan. Goratrix is still kicking around and has his own house again. Most of the clan still think of themselves as House Tremere and firmly within the Camarilla, but there are options now, as the clan is no longer monolithic. More trilithic. But the houses are in dialog with each other and not necessarily antagonistic. They just no longer have a central authority and thus have different standards for initiates and can operate independently.

      3) it’s not clear the degree to which the main House Tremere is still actively Saulot-controlled, but if it is, his power has been reduced. That’s good for both Goratrix (the real Tremere) and Carna, who now have more freedom to operate. Overall the clan has moved to training and working in smaller groups, with the master/sire-apprentice relationship being key. It’s also true that some young Tremere have to pursue their studies on their own, hunting down texts and such because there isn’t some big university-chantey with all the stuff already there.

      It’s possible I’ve missed some illuminating flavor text here and there, but that’s the lion’s share of what we know. I look forward to seeing if there are Anarch Tremere now. Lots of folks assume House Carna has gone officially Anarch, but she denies that’s the case. I imagine her flight to the Free States in BJD was a matter of survival as much as anything. Also not clear on the degree to which Goratrix is now operating apart from the Sabbat, possibly going Anarch himself now that the Sabbat is a mess (again). The question is where he can most effectively battle an Antediluvian for control of his clan.
