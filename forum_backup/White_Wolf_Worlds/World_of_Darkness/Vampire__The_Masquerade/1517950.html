    So what kind of interests or hobbies do you give your pc's when they have downtime?

    07-14-2023, 11:57 AM
    This is related to my detailed backstory post and I'm indeed curious to what hobbies or quirks you give your kindred.

    My Malkavian loves the smell of coffee when he is relaxing with a good book and finds it soothing and is a big basketball fan and never misses a televised game of his favorite team or is a big fan of country music and sometimes sings his favorite songs at local shows.

    So what kind of quirks and hobbies do you give your player characters?

      Given that vampires have a theme of being anachronistic if I add a down time hobby it will relate to their mortal years.

      An ancient Egyptian Follower of Set who used to be a Craftsman in his mortal days makes museum quality replicas to sell to collectors. Producing a war chariot with original techniques involves a fair amount of down time since you have to wait for things to cure or dry.

      A Toreador who was a music composer during Mozarts era collects rare musical instruments and is learning audio mixing software.

      A Tremere collects rare books, even if they are not arcane in nature. His collection has overgrown to the point where he runs a Book Store specializing in rare tomes.

          Parkour - an activity much improved when you have access to enhanced strength, speed and reflexes and limitless stamina (plus less concern over potential injury)
          Cars - street racing, car modding, taking an engine apart and putting it back together just because, taping NASCAR races to watch later, whatever: Cars.
          Before cars it was trains, before trains it was ships and boats.

          Taxidermy - Not every clan is blessed with vicissitude and when creating zombies and famunculi it's important to know how to keep a corpse preserved and tidy. It's also useful to create elaborate Frankenstein art pieces out of various animal parts for your collection of interesting looking pets/spy cameras - the Victorian con men were inspiring.
          (Also led to an embroidery side hobby)

          Linguistics - because one of the advantages of living forever is that you do actually have time to learn every single language.

          Travel - Whether one physically wanders or simply Astral projects around the world.

          Hiking and Camping- except it's the kind of hiking humans (and vampires lacking significant amounts of protean and fortitude) would struggle with. Deep sea exploration, spelunking, walking holidays in the heart of the Amazon, that kind of thing.

          Chasing the rush you get from stuff like swings, paragliding, bungee jumping... except you turn into a bird, or a bat, and actually fly around.

          But you know what hobby takes a hit when you're nocturnal? Bird watching.

          Architecture and Interior Design + Homesteading - and if anyone has a problem with that you can eat them...
          Or maybe adopt them, if you're desperate for conversation.

          Gardening - If you've got animalism you can probably work out some kind of deal with the local wildlife to stop them ruining your plants. You can even donate any produce you grow to the needy, if you're in a high humanity mood.

          "The Sims, but with real people." -
          A hobby of one of my Toreadors who likes to buy property, pretty it up and then start looking for people to play stylist/matchmaker with so she can move them in and play dolls with their lives for a bit before getting bored, taking all their money and moving on.
          (She also plays the actual games.)

          Prone to hyperbole

              My Tremere had an interest in animated constructs, and in consequence a collection of life-like dolls. One of them even served as a laboratory assistant. Some visitors to the Chantry expressed discomfort after noticing the eyes of the dolls followed them.

              The Tzimisce's pride and joy was their orchard of fruit trees and distillery for palincă​, which had been in care of their family (and bloodline) for generations. It actually produced some income as a luxury product, but that was more of a side effect. They were fiercely defensive of it, and even slight criticism of the product was taken as a mortal insult.

              The Malkavian had a library of empty books. His particular derangement filled them with all kinds of wonders when he 'read' them, and it confused the hell out of everyone else. One Tremere even got obsessed with trying to break the 'magic ward' on them, it was hilarious.

                                  Originally posted by Rhywbeth View Post
                                  Travel - Whether one physically wanders or simply Astral projects around the world.
                                  Worthwhile to mention that there is travelling, and there is also tourism, and also exploration. Different Kindred might have all sort of preferences and attitudes that might place them in definite and welcome contrast to other Vampires interested in crossing distances and visiting new places.

                                  Vampires were actually at times at the forefront of for example Victorian Era exploration, and of course the discovery of North America and exploration of South America. They also had a stake, desire, and ambition to follow along the colonial, trade, and diplomatic forays into Africa and Asia.

                                  Travel on oceans and seas is also a known interest and activity for many Vampires. Most notably the Lasombra and the Gangrel are linked to and associated with watery areas and regions.

                                              I really only had two vampire PC's with interests and hobbies beyond the norm.

                                              One was a high-Humanity Setite neonate, who had this whole "we purposely trained him wrong...as a joke" thing going on involving two Setite elders who made a "determinism versus free will/nature versus nurture" bet. He was a veterinarian whose practice was reptiles and amphibians, a "hots" keeper, and in his free time he researched the effects of vitae on snake venom, and vice versa. He also split his time between keeping overnight between two zoos, one of which was the US's largest venomous snake collection and venom research facility in the country.

                                              All fun and games, until one noticed his three ghouls were a black mamba, a gaboon viper, and one big motherfucker of a reticulated python. His MO when out and about was to let the mamba hide in his suit, and slip out to scout around or start attacking people if necessary. He kept most of the city's zoo animals blood bound (but not fully ghouled), and the zoo was his haven. Probably the tensest scene in that entire chronicle came out of that, but that's a story for another time.

                                              The second was a Lasombra who was unhealthily obsessed with outer space and space phenomena, on the notion it was the true Sea of Shadows and closer to Oblivion than any place on Earth. So, this Lasombra stayed up to date on basically any and all news and scientific articles about space, astrophysics, and astronomy, and had a rather large portion of their financial portfolio sunk into the aerospace sector.

                                              I'd add the Telyav I've spoken about before here, but that was only "weird shit" from outside the perspective of a new age-y practicioner of natural and animistic magic who actually can have friendly chit-chats with plants, toilet water, and electrical outlets.
