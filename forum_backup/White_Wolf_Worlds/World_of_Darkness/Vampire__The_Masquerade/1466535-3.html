            Great we're aware of the problem.
            If I may, I'd like for you all to forget my prior arguments, clean the slate, so I may explain my view without too much erhm... heat. Right? Forget everything I said before.

            I think the OG 7 clans would benefit from discriminating against the other clans, and since this is the world of Darkness where everything's ever so slightly more awful, and we're talking about vampires, of course they're going to discriminate.

            The 7 work well together because they each provide services the others aren't so good at, which outweighs the conflicts of interest. The most perfect example would be Ventrue working with Nosferatu: They've got utterly different skillsets, generally focus on very different resources, and have very different feeding grounds; It's all positives and it's extremely beneficial for both parties to cooperate.Of course there are exceptions, but generally, they can hold hands in friendship, though the Ventrue will want to wash his hands after. This may be less clear with other clans; The Toreador in particular do overlap in terms of skills and what resources they want with the other clans, but their broad range of skills really enables them to fill gaps and maintain balances of power: The positives outweigh the negatives.

            The other 6 clans are all more specialized, but excluding the Tzmisce and perhaps the Giovanni, have far too much of an overlap in interests with the 7 clans. The most obvious example: Ravnos are direct competitors with Gangrel in everything, but with more reliability problems. (You might think of Chimestry as a possible service, but generally Money>Chimestry so it's rather niche) But Lasombra certainly step on some toes and Settites are resource hogs. Assamites with their caste system are like their own little cooperation with specialists in every field.

            The Lasombra compete with The Brujah for skills and the Ventrue for resources. That's two enemies right off the bat, they've got fingers in the interests of the other clans and they make worse neighbours than either of these clans so.. isn't it good to take advantage of how the Lasombra are difficult to produce and ensure that the clan remains small and marginal? The positives of the Lasombra (They're sociopathic living weapons with mind control powers, obsessed with hierarchy) are outweighed by the negatives of the Lasombra (they're sociopathic living weapons with mind control powers, and they dress poorly). They're bad for the vampire... economy.

            IMO.In the Camarilla. There's very likely some informal protectionist agreement. The Gangrel don't want others dealing much with the Ravnos, their competition, The Ventrue don't want others to raise up the Lasombra, and so on and so forth. Of course they can't make this actual policy, because there are very powerful people from the 6 clans in the Cam and many Camarilla cities are dominated by one of the 6. and many would certainly enjoy the Assamites devaluing the sorcery of the Tremere, but ultimately such a move would push the Tremere to deal more with the Lasombra, Ravnos and... they don't want that (I don't really think the Assamite Sorcerers could really challenge the Tremere overmuch since they lack the numbers and unity, but a hit is a hit) On the street level, this manifests as a lot of slander, sensationalist stories, and general discrimination. To work fairly with an outsider can be considered a faux pass. Working with A Settite/Assamite/Giovanni/Ravnos/Lasombra/Tzmisce is usually a dirty, shameful thing, and you best be secretive about it. Even if the individual rejects their clan's sect, older vampires put a lot of attention on lineage and filial piety, so rejecting your independent sect to be with the Camarilla is a sign of disloyalty and rebeliousness that few elders are keen on seeing.

            It helps that most of the non-camarilla clans are unlikely to have sufficient members in a Camarilla city to really stop such defamation. They are kept as minorities and are treated as such. To make things worse, The six mostly don't like eachother and are far more likely to kill a different member of the six than they are to band together and better their place in the Cam.
            Equally hateful is the Camarilla fixation on the Masquerade. The Cam, in my view and supported by a few texts, doesn't actually need to try so hard to keep the Masquerade, rather the masquerade is often used as a pretense to punish neonates and keep them afraid of the law. The 6 clans all have disciplines that are horrific masquerade breaches. You may argue that Protean and Thaumaturgy are pretty flashy, but most of Thaumaturgy is very subtle, and of Protean, it does more good than bad when you think about it (Sinking into dirt>finding a "corpse" and calling the authorities.) Quietus and Vicissitude leave behind permanent evidence of things-that-aren't-right while chimistry? Oh boy. Obtenebration and Serpentis are horror shows and Necromancy is... fine, if you're just dealing with ghosts. Are these disciplines so bad if used responsibly? Of course not. But are neonates punished proportionately for their masquerade risking crimes? Not in the slightest.

            Are Vampires from these clans in the Camarilla? Yes.
            In some cities, they may be completely equal to the 7, if not superior.
            But in many cases there will be many caveats and a good chunk of prejudice. Elders will impress bad attitudes on neonates. It's going to be difficult to get permission to embrace.
