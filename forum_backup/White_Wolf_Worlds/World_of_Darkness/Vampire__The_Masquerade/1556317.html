    Notes Towards A ‘Lone Star By Night’

    12-07-2025, 06:29 PM
    “Texas is the reason/that the president’s dead”
    -The Misfits, ‘Bullet’

    It’s often observed that classic White Wolf lore was very western centric, particularly the United States, particularly the Midwest, Northeast, & West Coast. This has created a situation where some of the largest cities in the US today, see Houston or Dallas, have barely anything to go on about them.

    My hopes with this project is to sketch out the outlines of what a Texas vampire setting might look like with you all. I enjoy trying to build in verisimilitude for my games and big open areas like these are begging to be filled in. I invite everyone to share whatever thoughts come to mind. I’m not from Texas or the South and I’m not a historian of the region, so my additions will be the best I can put together based on what I do know.

    Additional Disclaimer: I’m sure some canon information does exist and could be useful to build on but I’m largely treating this as a blank state, using the general knowledge of the setting we have from 1e/2e as my template

    Now, beginning in no particular order, I’m going to through out a number of spit ball ideas. I may add the first drama of a historical sketch later as well:

    Brainstorm

    •For potential modern settings I’ll include ideas both for a hypothetical 1990s start date and a contemporaneous 2020s start date

    •I don’t have the strongest feeling for what distinguishes the notable Texan cities but I think any setting guide probably needs to touch on the Texas Triangle (Dallas-Fort Worth, Houston, Antonio), Austin, El Paso, the Rio Grande, Hill Country, Texarkana, North Texas/Panhandle, West Texas, & Gulf Coast

    •A vibes based reading of three Texan cities:
    -Dallas (I see Dallas having the most classic Ventrue environment; big business, finance, etc; but with a bit of a more modern feel, the 80s boom and tech industry in mind; I imagine a relatively young Camarilla Ventrue prince whose stuck in the mode of 80s finance guy trying to keep a palatable face on the real power players who all cut their teeth in the 19th century or older, some of whom definitely have Dallas-the show style affectations)
    -Houston (Houston I see as doing away with the polite fictions of Dallas, instead having a Brujah Prince, probably the oldest in the state, who openly runs the city like a temple to blood and oil; if you play the game and are on the right side, it can be very comfortable and secure, but the rulers of Houston have little patience for those who would rock the boat)
    -Austin (I see Austin having a Toresdor prince, and being the city with a significant Anarch influence, the Prince maintaining a libertine posture and relatively lax enforcement of the traditions in an effort to keep the Texan Anarchs fat & lazy/inside the tent pissing out-sort of vibe)

    •I think a significant section could be dedicated to the vast swaths of Suburbia where Autarkis, Thin-Bloods, Caitiff, & Nomads can all maintain carved out fiefdoms in new developments, condos, and strip malls

    •I see a major element of the Texas Sabbat taking inspiration from Texas Chainsaw Massacre and Rob Zombie movies. With isolated revenant enclaves/safehouses modeled after TCM & House of a 1,000 Corpses and nomads who are a mix of Near Dark & the Devil’s Rejects

    •A subculture of hunters that take more influence from the post-70s evangelical movement than the Catholic Church, operating out of strip mall churches and blending into backroads, trailer parks, and cookie cutter suburbs

    •Some sort of commentary on the rise of the Sun Belt from the 1970s onward, perhaps with a new breed of young Camarilla princes merging alongside the cities of the region, a sort of triangulation between the old blood and the Rust Belt princes of the Midwest & Northeast who gained so much influence in the late 19th through mid-20th century
    -Maybe tie this to the LBJ years and his clique’s national influence to tentpole this transition period before having the figurehead of this be more Bush Oil Cowboy/Safari Club inspired

    •Use of the Dallas/Austin counter culture of the 1980s & 90s as a breeding ground for local anarch tendencies (see the history behind: Bill Hicks, Alex Jones, Matthew McConaughey, Linklater, “Slacker”, MDMA, the Dallas Tech Boom, etc.)

    •Any version of this project that uses the 90s as a starting point is going to have to touch on the militia movement as well, perhaps using it as inspiration for Anarchs, Sabbat, and/or Hunters

    •You also need some kind of Ross Perot reference in there for sure

    •You also need to have some significant writing about Texas’ relationship with Mexico but that also touches on addressing Mexico’s place in the World of Darkness which goes beyond the scope of this post, but is definitely worth its own thread

    Non-Exhaustive List of Potential Inspirations
    •Westerns (et. all)
    •Near Dark
    •Texas Chainsaw Massacre
    •Rob Zombie movies (esp: House of 1,000 Corpses, The Devil’s Rejects)
    •No Country For Old Men (& Corman McCarthy in general)
    •Breaking Bad, Better Call Saul (more southwest in general than Texas I know, but I’m counting ‘em)
    •The Righteous Gemstones & Nashville (also not Texas obviously but useful viewing IMO)
    •Slacker
    •Any biography of LBJ
    •Dallas (the TV Series)

              Texas is an extremely big region to cover. Texas has always been a hybrid between a "southern" and "western" state, and the DFW area seems to have a strong midwestern influence. It is really lots of different regions, more than most states. What combines it together is a strong state culture that prides itself on the Texan War of Independence and its existence as a separate republic for a decade.

              A major reason why LBJ was so powerful in the Senate was that he was the conduit between his fellow Democrats and the Texas oil money that fueled a lot of campaigns in other states. There's just a lot of political influence to be gained from that. That's probably why Meerlinda, the Tremere Councilor for North America, is based in Dallas, Texas. John Diamond (Pontifex for Commerce based in Bridgeport, CT) and Peter Dorfman (Pontifex for Politics in Washington, DC) may be responsible for Tremere influence there for North America, but they all report directly to her.

              The US territorial Pontifexes are organized into Eastern, Central, and Western regions. Other than the US Western Pontifex (Abe Powell) being based in Sacramento, I don't know where the other two are based. I can't find them in a (very quick) perusal of the clanbooks, and I don't believe they're mentioned in any canon By Night sourcebook. However, I imagine Texas falls under the US Central Pontifex. Regardless of where it is located (I would probably place it in St Louis as a convenient place), whatever Meerlinda wants to happen in the Texan chantries probably gets done without question.

              Because of the state's cattle and cotton economy, wide swaths of the state are probably "controlled" by Lupines. Not to mention that the resource extraction economy will attract a lot of attention from them. While short distances between major cities are probably OK to travel with the modern highway system at night (most of the time), that would not have been the case historically. And entire regions of Texas are probably off limits to vampires because of Lupine activity. Any cowboy or small town football vampires in Texas better be Gangrel and keep close to the Masquerade.

              I imagine the Texas Chainsaw Massacre inspired Bratovitch revenants are one of the few spots the Sabbat can somewhat safely move between when trying to cross the state, but the vaunted "lupine hunters" of the Bratovitch likely keep their head down and only have a few trophies based on isolated Ronin, Lost Cubs, and Lunatics that they can safely ambush on rare occasions.

                                      Originally posted by Atticus Cleanclaw View Post
                                      Of course these are a hypothetical maximum and the exact figures may differ.
                                      The Camarilla and Sabbat numbers are always based on the metropolitan urban area. So one should never include the rural population as the basis for vampires. Even though the suburban population is counted as part of the metro area, it's also always mentioned that the typical American suburb is NOT conducive to vampires. It lacks the high population density that lends itself to anonymity, high crime rates, and active nightlife that attracts modern vampires to the city. Instead, there's an implicit assumption that suburbanites commute to the urban core for the nightlife so that they contribute to the vampire population requirements, even though the suburbs themselves are usually not amenable to vampire populations.

                                      Yeah, there will always be some Gangrel or other nomadic vampire in the rural areas, but they will be much lower than the typical ratios and most often are already factored in by the metro area populations/sourcebooks.

                                      As for the Garou, I have never found that ratio to be helpful. Unlike vampires, Garou population is not dependent on the number of available humans because Garou do not feed on humans. Instead, Garou population is usually based around kinfolk populations that don't have direct correlation to the normal human population. This is very much in ST fiat situations. The Yukon Territory of Canada has less than 50,000 people. So per that ratio, there may not even be one Garou. Yet there's around 5000 wild wolves throughout the entire territory, and most people would accept that there should be very high numbers of Wendigo kinfolk and likely other tribal kinfolk as well. There's probably a lot more Garou there than, say, in Vermont which has a population of almost 650,000 or even Massachusetts which is over 7 million. A flat 500,000 people : 1 Garou will always lead to much more Garou in urban areas than rural areas, which is exactly the opposite of what it should be.

                                      Instead, I work backwards based on how many caerns I think should be in that territory. Research the geography and local legends. Assign the number of caerns and their rankings, and then assign the number of Garou appropriate for those caerns.
