        In the DA book, the primary unifying element that turned the Ashirra from a vampire-religion into a sect proper was the Baali; whom the Salubri and Assamites play a key part in the sects strategy to keep the Baali down. Their cooperation with the Assamites to help save Salubri from the purge happening in Europe was one of the big catalysts that prompted their evolution towards being a more unified front as they felt the Salubri would be essential for fending off the Baali.

        So considering that keeping the Salubri safe from the Tremere was basically one of the founding alliances of the Sect that elevate it from an attempt to create Islam-for-vampires, the Ashirra would absolutely despite the Camarilla for tolerating the Tremere (even if that would likely have softened by the modern nights) for allowing them to undermine the warriors needed to stop the Baali from spreading.

        And of course, the Ashirra antipathy towards the Tremere isn't really necessary for them to want to resist the Camarilla. "This is our domain, you don't get to declare yourselves masters of it," is plenty enough reason by itself.
