    Fourth member

    08-06-2024, 10:12 PM
    Basically I need a fourth member for a cortege and I need ideas for one who would complement the group along with some background for him. A summary of the others:

    A Brujah who takes care of network security and is a cyberactivist in his spare time, but he isn't the stereotypical anti-social hacker, actually he is quite good at socializing, but lacks in the physical department;

    A Malkavian who has always been involved in crime and has done almost everything, he is the violent type, and short-tempered to the point that he is mistaken for a Brujah (yes, the flaw), naturally he is the muscle of the group, the shooter and the getaway pilot when necessary;

    The third is a Caitff, who does not know who his sire is, because he lost his memory and there is no identity or birth record linked to him, legally he doesn't even exist. He has a unique discipline that works like Vicissitude, but faster and limited to his own body. He is also good at acting and opening locks, has knowledge of human anatomy, but is terrible with technology.

    That's basically it, making it clear that is an anarch cortege and for certain reasons, the fourth member cannot be a Tremere or even have any proficiency in thaumaturgy. Other than that, he can be almost anything.

          Answering the first two questions, the cortege was gathered by the Baron to resolve internal problems in a more discreet way that he could not get directly involved in.
