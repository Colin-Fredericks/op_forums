    Originally posted by Kharnov View Post
    As monteparnas explained before, each Generation below 8th adds one extra "dot" that you can have in Traits, Disciplines, etc.

    So 7th Generation can have 6 dots.
    6th Generation can have 7 dots.
    5th Generation can have 8 dots.
    And 4th Generation can have 9 dots.

    10 dots is limited to Vampires of the Third Generation (or lower).


    P.S. After taking a closer look at the sheet again, a couple of comments.

    First, the Kraina and the Ways of "modern" Koldunic Sorcery are generally considered incompatible (though it's been a while and I can't remember if there's an official ruling on it, tbh; I'lltry to find it when I get home later). The lore behind it is that the Kraina came from the traditional practices of Koldunism, while the Ways were a product of the Tzimisce making pacts with Kupala for greater power. So you should probably decide whether you want her using the Kraina or the Ways; she's old enough to reasonably have either option available, though I think the Kraina are mostly used by the Old Clan in the Modern Nights.

    Secondly, for a 4th Generation vampire from the Dark Ages, her Attributes and Abilities are probably a bit too low, with nothing over 5 that I could see. Given how many different skills and Disciplines she has, she doesn't necessarily need a bunch of 6+ traits, or many at the maximum limit allowed by her Generation, but she should probably be 'superhuman' in some areas, like Strength.
    But bloodlines have their own disciplines that go up to ten even though they don't have Antediluvians, only Methuselahs.

            Originally posted by Samuelalpha86 View Post

            But bloodlines have their own disciplines that go up to ten even though they don't have Antediluvians, only Methuselahs.
            In most cases they're not canonically expected to exist at such levels, some aren't even expected to get near it (not all Bloodlines have Methuselah, not all of them even have members of lower Generation than 8th, it varies a lot), but as Kharnov said there are a few ways around it.

            Mostly they're there because lowering Generation is a thing. Even getting to 3rd Generation doesn't require anything not already in the rules and the setting, so in some chronicle somewhere it will happen. So this is reason #1, you can always have a character (PC or NPC) that somehow got there, even if it's only in a given chronicle, and still be "legal".

            The #2 reason is that no character is limited to only maxing out their In-Clans. So while there's no Daughter of Cacophony below 6th Generation (if that much) even in legend, any 3rd Generation, including pretty active people like Tremere, Saulot and Augustus Giovanni, may develop higher levels of Melpominee, they just have to learn it first.

            And #3 common way to achieve 10th level Disciplines, that I already mentioned before, is Golconda. One relatively common effect of Golconda is the limit on Disciplines (and on Disciplines only) being removed. Those characters can achieve 10 dots in any Discipline, just like an Ante, as long as they have the XP to do so. This has never been actually applied to many NPCs at all, even NPCs that did achieve Golconda, and none has 10 dots, but is still legal by the rules. There's some history and nuance to this ruling, but overall that's the gist of it.

            But none of those ways to achieve a Discipline at 10 is anything nearly reliable or expected. Being 3rd Generation is way, way more contrived than being "just" a Methuselah. Golconda shouldn't be possible to reach through a Path of Enlightenment, it's specifically a Humanity thing.

            Overall, anyway, level 10 Discipline is overkill. Level 9 powers can already be godlike feats. By the way, the "Plot Device" isn't an effect restricted to Antes, it's a simplification of the rules for level 10 as specifying powers at this level is mostly useless. This was done once in a very old supplement, but by Gehenna the book the devs decided that making rules for powers at this level is a waste of time. So, yeah, a non-Ante has basically a limited number of powers, but those are just barely defined Plot Devices.

                There IS a loophole in your case for Vicissitude


                Make her an Asakku, a Kindred whose souls have been corrupted by excessive use of Vicissitude (see The Black Hand: A Guide to the Tal'Mahe'Ra)
                As a vampire on the Path of the Asakku - a Path on its own right, but less an ideology than a state of being prompted by the obsessive study of Vicissitude and communion with the dark intelligences behind it* - increasingly surrenders to her Vicissitude she gains special abilities. These Transmutations represent the Discipline's will expanding into other Cainites and living things, and its ability to take firmer hold of the vampire's consciousness.

                As a vampire on the Path of the Asakku increasingly surrenders to her Vicissitude she gains special abilities. These Transmutations represent the Discipline's will expanding into other Cainites and living things, and its ability to take firmer hold of the vampire's consciousness. A follower of the Path gains Sinister Mastery at the first dot of the Path, and another Transmutation of the player's choice at every even numbered dot thereafter.

                Transmutations
                Sinister Mastery - This ability (possessed by all Asakku) reduces the Vicissitude experience cost multiplier by 1. Combination Disciplines featuring Vicissitude are purchased at half cost, rounded up.

                Blood Infection - The Asakku drains a vampire of all of her blood and fills half of her blood pool with the Asakku's own. The Vampire becomes an Asakku and is converted to the Path of Asakku at a one dot rating, and trades one dot of her weakest Discipline for a dot of Vicissitude if she doesn't have any dots. One she reaches four dots in Vicissitude she can advance further up the Path of Asakku. Blood Infection counts as two steps towards the blood bond.

                Inner Babble - The Asakku's mind is filled with sounds and images, and her Aura shows random emotions. Telepathy attempts and attempts to read the Asakku's aura fail.

                Occultation - The Asakku's increasingly alien nature becomes something the very Earth seeks to abjure. Fate erases the Corrupted's footsteps. Freak events erase records of her presence. This Transmutation may be selected multiple times.Potentiation - The Asakku Vicissitude rating can exceed her Generational maximum by one dot. The power can be taken up to three times, but cannot exceed nine dots.

                Seeding the Garden - The Asakku infects the soil of an area with their vitae. Any animal who eats the plants or drinks water from this area becomes the Asakku's ghoul after a month. If the animal is eaten, the predator becomes the Asakku's ghoul.

                Soul Whispers - Any creatures who have drunk the blood, had Vicissitude used on them by the Asakku, or had the Sinister Obsession derangement can have the Asakku use their senses, use the Auspex, Animalism, Dominate, and Vicissitude Disciplines through this connection as if they were touching them, and relay emotions and instincts, such as the sense that someone the target sees is an enemy (though not information explaining why this is so). If the target possesses at least a dot in Vicissitude, the Corrupted may utilize any rank of Vicissitude through the target, using it as a proxy.

                Because, well, they want to transcend Vampirsim... and it limit (Clanbook Tzimsice Revised)


                A curious relative of Vicissitude, Sanguinus, the Discipline of the Blood Brothers have a power, Walk of Caine that temporarily lower your generation by raising that of others in your circle, BUT no matter how big the circle, no Blood Brother can drop to an effective Generation lower than Fourth. Likewise, a Thirteenth Generation Blood Brother can drop to an effective Generation of Fourteenth, (Vampire: The Masquerade 20th Anniversary Edition/ Guide to the Sabbat )
                But again, there is no actual rule against other Kindred to learn Sanguinus under specific circumstances, but need some work, to actually use it, like having a circle; usually diablerizing a Blood Brother works. For a single vampire to use its full powers, they must be in a blood-bound circle with at least one other vampire with Sanguinus knowledge.
                So if you REALLY want to take this, theoretically​ her child/team and her​ could eat the Blood Brother cicrcle thecnically replacing them - then keep them in VERY tigh blood bond and in the basement.
