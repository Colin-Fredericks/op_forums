                V5 suffers from a number of urban myths: that you are forced to play a positive character bemoaning the vampiric condition is the most common!

                Author intent, as Charles has underlined, is very much to say "Vampires are vile creatures that taint everything they touch": you can play a perfectly evil character, either by tailoring the Chronicle tenets, or by creating relevant Convinctions that can easily represent Paths, as it is suggested in the Corebook and in Cults of the Blood Gods).

                Even the fact that Convinctions are tied to Human Touchstones is not necessarily limiting: those Humans must remind you of your values, it's not required you love them or that you have a positive relationship with them.

                An evil backstabbing Lasombra politician might groom a human puppet without any moral qualm, a murderous Frank Underwood who will absolutely do every shit to get it to the top, and use it as the Touchstone for his "Only the Fittest Survive" touchstone.

                A sociopathic Lasombra nihilist on the Path of Night equivalent might have as Touchstones his favoured victims, whose continued misery remind him of his Convinction "No rest for the wicked".

                Why shouldn't you be able to play Palpatine, Tywin and Walter White? Looking at Clan stereotypes in the Chicago book, they are very much in line with these kind of archetypes.

                I think, btw, that Revised Lasombra suffered from a lack of focus: they are a clan of social darwinists, but also of nihilists, but also of powers behind the throne and manipulators, that are into Church culture (organization, social mobility, hierarchy) but also piracy (extrem anarchy, unchecked competition) but also old style limpieza de sangre aristocracy (extreme conservatism and racial hierarchies) ... Lots of cool concepts, but perhaps a bit too many for a single clan, and not one of the biggest.

                                                            There's a couple of points here that need to be addressed:

                                                            1. The Sabbat have really taken a beating in V5 as not just a sect but as a concept: Paradox has decided the Sabbat are something they flat out either do not like or do not want to particularly see succeed as a playstyle. As such, they've decided to take the Sabbat Civil War of BJD and the "Gehenna War" to destroy the sect. As the Sabbat are my second favorite group after the Anarchs (who the Sabbat are the poor cousins of the aweseomeness thereof), this is somethin I don't like.

                                                            However, they have REALLY gone to town on making the Sabbat collapse into parodies of themselves. I don't like it. It's not something I appreciate but they have been hammering the Sabbat collapsing for the better part of five years now.

                                                            * The Lasombra defection
                                                            * The Tzimisce defections
                                                            * The destruction of some of its most popular Paths of Enlightenment.
                                                            * Defections to Blood Cults
                                                            * Massive casualties due to the Gehenna War(s)
                                                            * Loss of territory

                                                            And so on.

                                                            I don't know who has a bee in their bonnett (forgive my Southernism) about the Sword of Caine but the Sabbat really is wrecked beyond all repair. Barring a new edition, those who have fled this sinking ship seem the best off.

                                                            2. Some people have collective amnesia about the Lasombra Antitribu: I mentioned this above and I think people are pretending that it wasn't the case in V1,2,Revised,20 because they're so pissed off about 1#. Believe me, I get that because I find the treatment of the Sabbat in 1# to be ridiculous. It's my 2nd biggest complaint about the game and frankly, might be my biggest.

                                                            However, the Lasombra Antitribu have always been the most powerful Bloodline in the Camarilla and always JUST SHORT of having their own seat on the Inner Council. They have a 4th generation Methuselah in the inner circles of the Camarilla after all, which is arguably more powerful than all of the other Inner Council combined.

                                                            The idea the Lasombra need THAT many defections to go from being a bloodline to a full Clan in the Camarilla is a stretch. Remember, there were Lasombra Princes in the previous city and not just Marcus Vitel. Really, the biggest weirdness about the "defection" is acting like the Lasombra didn't ALWAYS have a steady line of defectors from the Sabbat. They weren't exactly A LOT of them and plenty did get tracked down and killed but they weren't ever unheard of either. An entire city once defected.

                                                            3. I get the impression Justin Achilli doesn't have complete freedom here: The Sabbat depicted in The Sword of Caine are a swarm of zombies and make the previous Revised take on the Sabbat look positively enlightened. However, if you talk with Justin Achilli on Twitter or other social media, he says that the modern Sabbat are just a cover for the Elders who are sitting in their bunkers sending them all out to die.

                                                            That the "true Sabbat" is tsill around, just waiting Gehenna out.
