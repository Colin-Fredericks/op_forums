                    I for one always got the impression that the attack in Vienna was conducted via a drone, there was an explosion that destroyed the chantry. Shortly afterwards Special Forces swarmed the area eliminated the remaining vampires and locked the place down.

                    So the question then becomes what do they need asset-wise to do that?
                    1 Drone
                    1 Missile
                    30-60 Soldiers
                    Permission to operate in a NATO country
                    A cover story that will hold up to public scrutiny.
                    A cover story that will hold up to governmental scrutiny.

                    Military Resources
                    Those first four things can easily be sorted out by a high ranking enough military commander, especially if part of an international alliance of several of them, with intelligence-sharing arrangements. (We can assume that the SOCCOM drone strike was authorised after US intelligence shared information with Austria about the target. The forces were likely Austrian ones associated with Austria's special forces and intelligence services.)

                    In the world of darkness especially where shadowy conspiracies gain access to this sort of tech all the time, I don't think it's beyond the pale.

                    Civilian Cover-Story
                    Now the civilian cover story can be as simple as they stated it. There was a terrorist attack with a very large explosive device, there is nothing in the text stating they claim it was an Islamist drone strike, nothing saying the public are aware a drone strike was involved. As far as Joe-public is concerned, one moment there was a building in Vienna, the next a crater swarming with special forces and emergency responders.

                    How are any of them going to be able to tell a conventional bombing from a drone strike with the information they'll have access to? Journalists won't have been able to get anywhere near the site before the SI can secure it. Any emergency worker involved will likely have had to sign the official secrets act, and the majority won't be able to differentiate a drone strike from a conventional bombing either. (Nor would they care to, they'd have more pressing concerns.)

                    Anyone who tried to provide a counter-narrative would just be tarred as a conspiracy theorist. Psy-Ops 101, the SI is more than capable of such basic misinformation.

                    Governmental Cover-Story
                    Okay so this now needs to deal with several different types of government agencies:

                    1) The Military/Intelligence Apparatus: We know the SI doesn't integrate entire agencies, armies, or even units. It's a shadowy conspiracy, a few commanders or spies here, a spec-ops team there. The majority of people in the military/intelligence community have no idea 'blank-bodies' are a threat or that there is an international coalition to deal with them.

                    Will some people be suspicious in those groups? Sure and those suspicious people will be dealt with by the conspiracy. (Recruited, re-posted, discredited, killed, etc.)

                    The vast majority though, won't know an operation has even happened. The information would be kept on a strictly need to know basis. At the end of the day Sgt Smith's superior officer might've been directly involved in Vienna, that doesn't mean that Sgt Smith knows anything.

                    2) The Native Civilian Government: Who are the government going to ask about a terror attack? The military and intelligence agencies, who will have been the ones to position themselves to answer these questions? The members of those institutions who're in the SI, who'll use every trick in the book: off the record disclosures, black-ops, the official secrets act, keeping information secret for 'national security, etc.

                    The civilian government will be fed just enough information to make sure they come to the conclusions that the SI want them to. What sort of resources would that take? Control over which EOD techs examine the site, which crime-scene investigators are involved, and then which agents write the reports for committees and ministers. That's not a lot of people in the grand scheme of things.

                    3) Foreign Governments: Now foreign governments are going to be interested. But you can be sure that anything a domestic government doesn't have a full picture on, a foreign government will have even less of a picture on. A domestic government is going to keep the details under control, if only so they can dictate the narrative.

                    Notes - The above still doesn't address:
                    1) Secrecy amongst SI assets, within their own organisations.
                    2) The likely contribution of Camarilla assets in covering things up for their own good. (Last thing they want is mass public interest in a former vampire hotspot.)
                    3) This doesn't address that we're not given the full story. We're not given an exacting account of what the cover-up looked like, we don't know exactly what the public was told, how the investigation was handled, etc. Primarily because that's not the important part of the story, the important part for playing the game about mythical vampires is the 'government' blew up Tremere-central and the damage that's been done to the clan. Partly to change the status-quo of the Tremere, partly to show how dangerous the SI is.
                    4) This event happened once, this was the SI throwing all their: influence, assets, control, and power. It's not like this is a common-occurrence happening left right and centre, there is no indication that the SI can do this casually. (On the contrary, other depictions of the SI would seem to show the exact opposite.)
