                Originally posted by Bluecho View Post
                No consider that, by the standards of Deep Time, vampires (at least according to Noddist thinking) are practically newborns. Assuming, of course, vampires are "only" 10,000 years old. A Lovecraftian Vampire campaign could easily challenge these assumptions, and present the idea that vampires, or their antecedents, have been around far longer than most can possibly imagine.
                In terms of Deep Time and vampires; it’s worth noting that recent archeological and paleontological finds have pushed the emerence of anatomically modern man back to at LEAST 300,000 years before present (i.e. long enough that we could fit all of our recorded history 60 times over into the amount of time modern humans have been around… and yet, even knowing how quickly all evidence of our civilization would crumble to nothing, we presume we’re the first advanced civilization in all that span of time?) and the oldest structures (see Göbekli Tepe) to 9500 BCE and featuring sculpture far more advanced than we would see in the immediately periods.

                Lots of myths/folklore from around the world reference previous ages of men who were destroyed by calamity (even the Bible mentions a pre-flood iron-working civilization that would have been destroyed about the same time Atlantis was supposedly wiped out by the gods for its wickedness.

                Basically, you could easily extend vampires back into past forgotten civilizations; Atlantis, Mu, the Gold and Silver men of Greek Myth going back hundreds of thousands of years. Caine (if you want him to be the actual first vampire) could have sired and destroyed entire lines of vampires (excepting a few he missed who became the Sleepers like Namtaru) many times over… or Biblical Caine was actually the 31st (or 101st) Generation since Blood Caine (akin to Mitochondrial Eve; a hypothetical first vampire we may as well call Caine regardless of their actual gender for lack of any better name) and Namtaru and the other Sleepers are vampires so ancient that even the concept of humanity is alien to them.

                Hell, you could easily tie in the previous ages of KotE into the lost pre-history… entire civilizations rising even higher than our present one but then wiped away by the hundred thousand years since their fall.

                Vicissitude could be both vampiric and an “alien” infection from a time so long ago that it may as well be an alien world and created by a vampire a dozen times more ancient than even Tzmisce (indeed, the Antediluvian may have just been that elder vampire’s first victim in the “modern” era.

                Ennoia tried to earthmeld to the center of the Earth and become one with Gaia… only to be consumed whole by a master of Protean whose already been there 50 times longer than she’s been alivd.

                Maybe they are even the Neverborn… their dreams so ancient that they were carving their labyrinths in their dreams before the most recent time that man has rediscovered writing to record their existence.

                One hundred to two hundred millennia is so long that ANYONE still alive from back then would have to be alien beyond our understanding. Their plots and schemes span multiple rises and falls of civilizations… the entire rise and fall of Atlantis 12,000 years ago could be just an intermediate step in a gambit that won’t reach fruition until AD 3000… and is just the equivalent of a “five year plan” to us.

                Were Adam and Eve even the creations of God or an attempt by the Vicissitude ancient to recreate humanity after its childer had consumed them all in an attempt to grow a new food supply for itself and the curse spread to Caine to provide the food for a hunger beyond even the Methuselah’s curse (the humans being just the food supply for the vampires it needs to eat)? How horrific do you want the truth to be?

                300,000 years (that we have evidence for… who knows the actual number) is an unfathomable span of time in which all of recorded history is barely a speck… we don’t even need lizard people to get to incomprehensible beings from before history.

                    The CALL OF CTHULHU game notes that both Deep Ones and Ghouls are actually far younger than the typical "Old One" or, say, the Yith. Part of the "horror" of Lovecraft is the fact that both the Deep Ones and ghouls are branches of humanity that evolved in parallel to humanity or were mutated by their association with the magic/weird science of the Great Old Ones. Humans themselves were created by the Elder Things but that gets a bit into continuity that HPL himself didn't really believe in.

                    However, the reason ghouls and Deep Ones can breed with humans is, well, they are human.

                    They are arguably a young race themselves and may have every bit as many mistakes about the nature of the Great Old Ones as humans (and while you may automatically dismiss this, in fact humans have quite a bit of knowledge about the Great Old Ones as the Cult of Cthulhu and academics as a whole have common access to the IDEAS behind them even if they're mostly ignorant as a whole).

                    Another idea to consider is not just V:TM and Lovecraft but V:TM and Howard.

                    It's a very different feel but might actually be easier as the realization that Enoch took place at the tail end of the Hyborian Age and wasn't even THAT important as Sorcerer Kingdoms ruled by monsters is a fairly common thing back then.

                        Originally posted by Chris24601 View Post
                        it’s worth noting that recent archeological and paleontological finds have pushed the emerence of anatomically modern man back to at LEAST 300,000 years before present (i.e. long enough that we could fit all of our recorded history 60 times over into the amount of time modern humans have been around… and yet, even knowing how quickly all evidence of our civilization would crumble to nothing, we presume we’re the first advanced civilization in all that span of time?) and the oldest structures (see Göbekli Tepe) to 9500 BCE and featuring sculpture far more advanced than we would see in the immediately periods.
                        I have some things to say.

                        First, before someone reaches the wrong conclusions, this is perfectly fine for fictional thought, but a little off from a real life standpoint. We can say for sure there was no other industrial civilization in the last 300.000 years because on that scale it would still leave a lot of surface and buried evidence, and even for longer time spans there are some evidence that we can pick up today. Although there is a possibility of a short-lived industrial civilization not being detectable by current methods after about 2 millions years, it is unlikely still. And our modern form isn't really likely to be far older than 300.000, while the initial 100.000 was a guesstimate at best, the current span isn't likely to be far off the mark.

                        On the other hand, Göbekli Tepe isn't the oldest structure we've found, it is the oldest complex structure from the Neolithic, when those became relatively common indicating a species-wide change from nomadic to sedentary societies. Actual oldest structures go back another 10.000 years, they're just localized events and less likely to be permanent residences.

                        Then we have the possibility of civilization under other species, which WoD already semi-recognizes in Werewolf. Other Homids shared the planet with us for most of our 300.000 years, and more predated us for more than 4 million years, beyond that 2 million magic mark. Before that we had other primates, a greater variety of mammals, dinosaurs and reptiles, and who knows what else could be civilization builders in the fictional past. We could have competing civilizations and sequences of civilizations.

                        And with supernatural forces involved, how many of those could have left enduring abominations wandering the Earth? What if the Nictuku aren't a bunch of zealot 4th gens, but something stranger still? Maybe guardians of some buried secret the Nosferatu sometimes get too close to find, but at the same time they engendered the Nosferatu as a protection for this secret. They may well be Shoggoths as far as we know.

                        What else could lie buried beneath the lands and oceans of a 5 billion years planet? What could come from the stars in a 14 billion years universe? What else could disguise as human so well that entities completely used to see the supernatural as human-derived wouldn't ever expect to exist? Which of the weirder things out there the main splats could be completely wrong about, and be older and more dangerous?
