                                                            Hello there and sorry for the late reply I do not have Email notifications enabled ^^'

                                                            Thats a rather good question. I'll try my best.

                                                            Most of the background for the chronicle is based on a, imho, excellent Storytellers Vault supplement called "Vampire: Age of the Living Gods". It of course deviates from established canon but to me and my group that is no problem at all.

                                                            The supplement is set 2350 BCE in the "Fertile Crescent" region, which is a rather large region consisting of many contemporary countries like Irak, Syria e.g.

                                                            The supplement sets in a century after the Fall of the Second City in VTM.

                                                            The political situation is that of city-states like Uruk, Ur and Akkad eg. ruled by kings during the day and "Living Gods" and their Heralds during the Night. You can probably see where this going ^^'

                                                            These "Living Gods" are predominantly the surviving Antedeluvians like Arikel (Ishtar during that time), Cappadocius and Saulot (Ashur during that time, this special case is left deliberatly vague in the book), Troile (as Anat) and Ennoia (as Ereshkigal). More living gods are coming from the ranks of the future Methusalehs so from the 4th and 5th Generation. With some exceptions every City in the region has a Living God that feeds on blood sacrifices made by the kine. Everything in kindred society revolves around these blood sacrifices and the kindred have constructed a literal pyramid scheme around their godhood. There are also five political factions among kindred with different or overlapping goals.

                                                            Concering technological aspects and science I cannot answer that much in detail tbh. Architecure and Astrology play a pronounced role and of course agricultural development and bronce/copper metallurgy.

                                                            A very interesting thing the author did is that he did not shy away from certain lvls of crossover between the other WOD splats. I guess here is where it deviates the most from the established canon, since it involves Fera who should be extinct already (not an exoert on WTA lore) like the Apis. The author also incorporates some stuff from Demon the Fallen which is rather interesting.

                                                            I hope that answer was satisfactory^^'
