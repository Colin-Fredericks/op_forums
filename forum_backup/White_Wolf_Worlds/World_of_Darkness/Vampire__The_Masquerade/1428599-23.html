        To be sure, aren’t most of these explanations/discussions to be found in the actual sourcebooks where the Clans are introduced to V5?

        I mean, the short answer is that these are all Clan reactions to change. The change in V5 lies in the rise of the Second Inquisition, and the new threat they present. Vampires in the game have been left with a dichotomous choice to join the Camarilla or Anarch movements in order to survive. For the Lasombra, they more naturally feel they can progress in the institutions and structure of the Camarilla. For the Ministry, they thrive more in the lawlessness of the Anarchs.

        You might disagree, by which you can justify your own reasons why not at your game table, but for me, the developments are quite internally consistent with the outlook of each Clan respectively.

                                                            I pegged the lasombra mostly as pragmatists, honestly. Yeah, the Camarilla has an impressive brand, but it's a train wreck, A total train wreck in V5. Kinda like Harley Davidson; super famous even to people who don't like bikes, but actually a garbage company that reports nothing but losses.

                                                            I'm not questioning that some Lasombra would want to join the cam. After all, HD still sells a few bikes to dentists upon the mid-life crisis, but the majority of Lasombra should clearly favour the ship that isn't sinking, the Anarchs. They don't even have to call themselves anarchs. They just have to establish their own factions within the anarchs, bringing with them some of the former sabbat that were in their packs; it makes more sense given that they can take their pack with them to join the Anarchs, rather than abandon their pack to join the Cam. Since anarchs are a disorganized mess, it should be very easy for any Lasombra worth their blood to claim turf. Their Tzmisce friends can come with them, their Salubri friends can come with them, their ravnos friends can come with them. I don't think I can fully articulate how perfect the Anarch opportunity is for the Lasombra, and how utterly raw the Camarilla deal is. The Lasombra aren't meant to be masochists, but sadists (because they got potence rather than fortitude; true story. All clans with fortitude are masochists. Just try and refute this. It's just facts.) and so becoming the Camarilla whipping boy is a million times less sexy than going to anarch town and putting the boot to a few faces.
