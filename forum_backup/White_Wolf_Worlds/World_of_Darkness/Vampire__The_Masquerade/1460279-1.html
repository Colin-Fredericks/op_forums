            If City Gangrel have to exist, they should have Animalism, Obfuscate, Protean.

                        I reorganized the clan Disciplines for the chronicles I ST a long time ago. Strangely, some of the same ideas cropped up in V5. This is what I did:

                        I gave the Malkavians their original Discipline set as I never liked Dementation. Auspex, Dominate, Obfuscate.

                        I combined several Dementation and Chimestry powers into their own Discipline which was a "secret" Discipline only a few vampires knew that was rumored to be an Infernalist gift.

                        So I had to find a substitute for it among the Ravnos. I gave them Animalism, Fortitude, Obfuscate. I never used Ravnos as NPCs and never had a PC play one, so I never got any complaints.

                        I also combined Obtenebration and Mortis as the same Discipline. We'll call it Oblivion just to reflect V5 though it wasn't that. Though Necromancy itself was kept separate as its own thing that Giovanni (and other necromancers) could learn at Out Of Clan costs.

                        So Lasombra had Dominate, Oblivion, and Potence.

                        The Giovanni and Samedi had the same set as the Cappadocians. Auspex, Fortitude, and Oblivion. Although not that I had several discussions here about the Cappadocians, I'm thinking of changing that to Auspex, Potence, and Oblivion.

                        Tzimisce had Animalism, Auspex, Protean. I never liked Vicissitude so I eliminated it in my games. I thought Protean was a sufficient substitute.

                        I didn't like Serpentis or Quietus as their own Disciplines, so I made them their own set of Thaumaturgy Paths and rituals. So I just replaced their clan Disciplines of the Setites and Assamites with Thaumaturgy. They just had access to different Paths than the Tremere did.

                        I never liked the idea of sub-clans within the same clan, so I eliminated as those as having any kind of Discipline difference or different clan weakness. Those bloodlines might still exist but as a particularly prestigious, infamous, or renowned lineage. But if you played Giovanni or Samedi besides the Cappadocian weakness, they all had to take a Flaw that simulated an additional curse.

                        I only used other Bloodlines if there was a specific chronicle plot point that had to use them so I never put much thought into them.

But for the Ventrue, especially considering their weakness, Presence is a better trait than Auspex. Charisma is just better for Ventrue than Perception, and the AntiVentrue really seem like they've gone the extra mile to be without charisma. Being a Ventrue is about talking your way into anybody's bed, not choosing who's got the best bed.

                            I really do like the Antis, but they're a lesser product.

                                    Dominate is more something you use if shit hits the fan or you want shit to hit the fan. You're underestimating Awe, which to be granted, isn't the best written discipline. But if you want to enrapture an audience, win a debate or rile people up, Awe is a strong carry. Awe is also subtle, you can get away with using it on other vampires.

                                    Entrance is strong because you can turn a stranger into your best friend In a single turn. This greatly simplifies the process of getting a blood bond because frankly, getting three drops of blood into someone on three different nights is a massive hassle, and with a few turns (or less if you're of low generation) you can make everyone in your room your best friend, Cornered by a group of thugs? Suddenly someone's advocating for you. You don't need to speak a word, worry about eye contact, whatever; you have an ally among your enemy getting you out of trouble. Presence works on animals, vampires with better generation, spirits, basically everything. Dominate is predictable, Presence isn't. Dominate can make people hate you, presence, yeah, they might hate you, but you've got plenty of time to avoid that.

                                    At low levels, Presence is more sustainable, though Dominate is cheaper.

                                    A good Ventrue will understand that leading people isn't about telling people what to do, but actually making them want to please you. It's about ballance, knowing when to carrot and when to stick. AntiVentrue don't have that ballance, they're all nutters.

                                    Curiously, I just read the 2e writup for Ventrue. It sells them really well as social, charming people. Dominate isn't social or charming. Auspex doesn't correlate to social clans, but everyone with Presence? They're highly social and charming. Sometimes you gotta think less like an optimizer and more about what feels right for the clan.
