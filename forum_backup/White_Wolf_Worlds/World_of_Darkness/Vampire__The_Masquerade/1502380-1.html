            Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

            I'm hoping that the Tzimisce write-up is identical to the free revision they already released.

            Ditto the Salubri and Ravnos.

            I mean, why do free write-ups if it's not canon?
            For one thing, given the way Renegade/Paradox/whoever the current team that does these things has shown in a few books by now how they treat layout and page content, any page that is straight up identical to a previous working makes for multiple pages of an already pricey book with a repeat of material people already have. By which I mean, every page of this thing that repeats something that already exists is that much less space, and not in a small way, for anything new to justify its existence and cost with.

            It wouldn't be so bad if the layout was better/there was more content per page/there were more pages per book/the price was something else, or that I didn't expect a bunch of this book to be empty spaces, repeated internal artwork of its cover, big ol larp pictures taking up most of if not an entire page, and so forth, but there's no reason not to expect that at this point. So that being the case, they can at least put some effort and variety into such writing/rules stuff as the book is going to contain.

            At the very least they can throw in some options for said clans to go alongside the existing ones (alternate banes, etc). Even just that would be something as far as at least having been previously vaguely promised and at least hopefully implied by the promo copy (hopefully).

            The idea of instead flat reprint wouldn't bother me so much or even at all in other circumstances. It is legitimately a really good thing to collect all 7 of these clans in one book instead of making people buy two+ whole other books in addition to the core, but the massive overpowering counterweight of that is having seen at this point how these books actually look upon being published, without a whole lot to suggest that is going to change in the future. The material is barely there, and anything they get to just copy and paste more or less makes it even more scant.

            Also there is the inescapable underlying note of "it has been 5 years of this gameline now of it all, but hey, you finally get a player's guide." A player's guide with a bunch of clans would have been amazing a couple of years in. But this long into this edition? For all that the rolling, repeated marketing theme of WoD5e is that it is mostly targeting people coming into tabletop right this very second and everyone before that second has ceased to have relevance, I'd still like to think enough consideration exists for whatever people have been buying this stuff from the 2018 jump for the past 5 years that they can give them a book that will not simply blow already thin page space just regurgitating things they very likely already have. Listen, I get it, the previous fanbase is a "legacy" fanbase of a "legacy" edition, with all the shrugging brush off that language implies, hey sure, fine, no country for old men. At least work to give this ersatz-replacement fanbase fresh stuff they can use, or really even simply a useful spin on pre existing stuff (whether optional or not), these people have existed for more than a few years now.

            That or do better with use of written content page count vs art choices and empty space on a page, but that's pretty much asking for miracles at this point.

            So, yeah, at this stage of things, it would be something if there was at least some variation of some kind, in some way. This is not a company that has otherwise built up a lot of faith in the idea that they won't do as little as the book allows for doing within its page count, layout and price point.

                                    Originally posted by Knightingale View Post
                                    Yeah, in the January 2023 update video Outstar did talk about these being new writeups. So it isn't a copy-paste-job. As to how different they are, who knows... The way Outstar talked about is that it was done with the goal to bring more clarity. And she did say her favorite was the new write-up for the Banu Haquim which gave a more accessible presentation to the Clan in describing what the Clan is about. That's her take on it at least.

                                    The deep dive from Renegade-Con mentioned that the book would have more than 200 pages (but the guy didn't remember the exact number but it's in that ballpark).

                                    Also what hasn't been mentioned in this thread yet from the Deep Dive: The book will have character-creation rules for mortals and ghouls (called "castoffs"). And it will talk about options for character-creation like what if you only want to do a oneshot.
                                    They confirmed 256 pages in the chat on the stream.

                                    Here's everything in detail on the two upcoming V5 books, from the stream (I haven't gone through WoD news and added to this, will probably do that later today):

                                    Summary from the stream for the Player's Guide...Conceptually the PG is a main companion to the core book, giving you two books to comprehensively cover most things you'll need at the table with most of your player options
                                    • Will have the 7 core clans and all the clans from the other books all compiled. When asked about copy/paste, it was mentioned the core clans would be presented with a fresh perspective so it's not just a cut-and-paste job
                                    • Will have variant clan banes and advice for how to use the variant banes, which can be applied to individual characters or a whole clan
                                    • Disciplines have a whole set of new options, as well as copying/compiling Disciplines from the other books such as Oblivion. Example: Animalism focusing on interaction with animals (they talked about many animalism powers focusing on the Beast already, so swinging the other way), mention of some new BS rituals
                                    • Whole chapter expanding on playing 'cast offs' such as Caitiff, Duskborn, ghouls and mortals. New TB Merits and Flaws, clarifications on TBA access
                                    • Confirmed page count of 256
                                    • Repeats and clarifications for creating your own characters. Quick ways to 'get into' building character for things like one shots.
                                    • Collected Predator Types and added a few new PTs, mentioned trapdoor spider-style or stalker style without 'naming' them
                                    • Coterie tracking sheet will be provided. Collecting the clan coterie merits from companion and adding to them for things such as TBs (mentioned something about an immortal heart merit to remind others about humanity, caitiff with versatile blood and salubri sharing their restraint with others).
                                    • Advice on personalizing chronicles. Additional options for playstyles, sample slates of personal convictions to go with some character concepts/inspire them. Also some advice for using Touchstones, what happens when Touchstones become aware of kindred society, gaining new touchstones, characters with no touchstones keeping them tied to the mortal world. Specifically no objects as touchstones.
                                    • Simplified rules for Projects, some new rules for Memoriams. Expanded considerate play/advice for dealing with sensitive topics.
                                    • PDF is 'safe bet' to see early, especially with preorder
                                    More Player's Guide info from the WoD News...
                                    • Revisits all aspects of character and coterie creation. Includes streamlined guidance for new and experienced players, including an 'instant vampire' section.
                                    • Collects all the Discipline powers from non-core sourcebooks except for a few that need the excess material from the book they came from or were plot device-ish (example: Ex Nihilo and its need for the deep explanations of the underworld from Cults).
                                    • 40 new Discipline powers, powers for all of the clans. There will be some clarifications and errata such as different rolls to existing Disciplines as well.
                                    • Won't be reprinting powers from the Sabbat book (as this is a player facing guide, no need to reprint antagonist powers)
                                    • New Predator Types including Grim Reaper, Montero, Pursuer and Trapdoor. No other context given. Leaving out Roadside Killer in the Predator Type compilation.
                                    • New Coterie Merits, Flaws, Clan Coterie Merits and Coterie Domain Traits.
                                    • The guidelines for non-traditional chronicles include things such as all-thin blood, troupe, streaming/online play, chronicles without a storyteller, other styles of play.
                                    • No Loresheets in PG​ - EDIT: It was confirmed the Bloodline loresheets from Cults will be reprinted

                                    Not a lot on Blood Sigils, they said there'll be more in the March 1st stream. From the stream for Blood Sigils (more to come in March per the stream)...
                                    • Narratively, different types of blood magic are becoming available to lots of types of Kindred due to recent changes, which drives a lot of what's in the book.
                                    • Not just a book about BS and TBA dots, but also helps integrating them into the story.
                                    • Will have some 'weird stuff in the book' when asked the question about artifacts/things created by sorcerers (not confirmed to what degree, both to not spoil as well as in case anything gets cut)
                                    • A sample story outline/sketch in the book with examples of how characters can get drawn into the underground blood magic scene in a given city and things like trading rituals or 'mercenary' use of the BS/TBA
                                    • Can just take the book for more cool powers, but there's a lot more in it than that
                                    • More information on the March 1st stream
