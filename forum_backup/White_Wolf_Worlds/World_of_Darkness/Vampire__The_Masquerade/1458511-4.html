                        Originally posted by monteparnas View Post
                        Still, they were far from defenseless, and the efficiency of the Purge is still strange without some other help, like Saulot helping it...
                        The other point to bear in mind, is this is the Medieval period through to the Renaissance, where transportation and communication were neither the greatest, and likely more importantly Medieval through Renaissance Europe. The quiet part of this that rarely gets spoken out loud is, "...from Europe". As in, the Cappadocians and Salubri were eradicated...from Europe.

                        Canonically, surviving Salubri were smuggled out of Europe by Assamites (and likely other Ashirra-affiliated vampires), despite their number having eventually dwindled to seven. And of course, there were the Salubri who lived in the far east relatively safe and free from harm. Last, and as already mentioned, the Salubri of the Laibon. And newer lore it may be, the Warriors simply decided that rather than wait to be killed off, they'd commit Suicide by Tremere instead and take as many of the bastards out with them as possible.

                        With regards to the Cappadocians, the situation really speaks for itself. At this point in the metaplot, everybody and their cat found a way to dodge the purge, with only the genuinely stupid and true believers in Cappadocius' ridiculous post-Feast mandates sticking around to be eaten. There's Lazarus' brood and whatever fate befell them, namely whether they formed the Samedi or infiltrated the Ghiberti family (if I remember right); the ones who escaped to Kaymakli and eventually joined the Harbingers; the damn Harbingers themselves; ones who had the fuck-ugly version of the flaw and managed to pass as Nossies; the ones who by some miracle managed to claim they were Premascines and straight-up infiltrated the Giovanni...and, the obligatory Cappadocians in the far east and among the Laibon, who managed to escape Giovanni notice for likely centuries until the clan made inroads into those areas.

                        That alone narrows the landmass in which these clans' "eradication" happened, immensely. We're down to one continent of seven. The requirements of "eradication" in an age with comparatively poor record-keeping, communication, and transportation is pretty much "I've never seen one/haven't seen one in decades, nobody I know has seen one, so they really must have been wiped out after all". The memetic ball gets rolling, and suddenly everyone knows the Salubri and Cappadocians were exterminated.

                        Realistically to me, that it happened -- in Europe -- is totally plausible. We're talking about a thousand dead vampires, tops, over the course of 400 years. Those are rookie numbers compared to what the body count of multiple Crusades, the Shadow Reconquista, the War of Princes, the Black Death, the Inquisition, and finally the Anarch revolt. And, vampires of the time were clearly not so stupid as to believe the clans were, to a member, wiped out, especially elders who are full aware how slippery vampires can be when pushed.

                        That's where I believe the "outside help" came in: how this obviously false, logically-deficient, totally unbelievable narrative of these clans' eradication became memetically-driven "truth" in the first place.

                                Originally posted by monteparnas View Post

                                Because you don't. It's utter, incomprehensible, nonsense.

                                Feeding is only indirectly about one's discipline spread -- that is to say, if one is using disciplines as a crutch to build/maintain herds, or to "brute force" the act of feeding -- it's more about the company one keeps, and the social, economic, and political circles with which one involves themselves. Absolutely, certain disciplines make the act of feeding easier. In fact, I'd argue the single best discipline for feeding in the entire game -- for Dark Ages, anyhow -- is not a single social discipline in the game, but rather Potence. One good, hard, bonk on the noggin of someone who won't be missed, and you've got yourself a whopping ten free blood points.

                                            Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
                                            Obviously, you can feed without relevant disciplines... a few times.
                                            No, you can feed without relevant disciplines consistently, unless you're a Giovanni or Nagaraja. You're completely overlooking the basic aspects of the Kindred condition.

                                            Every single Kindred have an intoxicating Kiss. You don't need to kill anyone, whatsoever, just bite. They'll hardly understand or remember clearly what happened unless you bare your fangs in front of them, which is completely unnecessary. You hunt at night, when a lot of dangerous things are already out there and few people will be able to witness anything you do, both for most being at home and for humans having a harder time seeing in the dark than you. Although not the most efficient tactic, you can use Vitae to increase your strength and overpower humans.

                                            Disciplines can help, but almost every Kindred hunting strategy, from seduction to invading homes at night, works fine without any of them whatsoever. You don't need specific supernatural powers, you need at least one dice pool of five dice in your sheet and figure out how to use it for your advantage. If you can't do that sure, you're not going to make it, but this is hardly a high bar even for accidental Embraces.

                                            Auspex and Fortitude help you with far more than that, but if you can't think of a way of consistently feeding just by being a vampire, that's another problem. What you need to be a good enough hunter to survive is to adjust to a feasible strategy in your hunting grounds and build up a Herd from there. One successful hunt every two nights is enough to get you through your unlife.
