    Animal blood is bland or unpleasant for vampires?

    01-23-2022, 01:46 PM
    I was reading a very old module, Ashes to Ashes. About feeding from cows, the text says: "It tastes good but bland, and human blood is much more appetizing". It compares it to eating simple porridge compared to feasting on delicacies ( human blood ).
    Other sources describe animal blood as utterly disgusting.

    Well... it's not the same thing.
    Definetly, it's not.

    Bland porridge can still be moderately pleasant, expecially if one never tasted anything better. An actively unpleasant food is a source of suffering.


    So, in your opinion, feeding on animal blood is, for a vampire is like the life of a monk ( humble and bland ) or the life of a flagellant ( actively unpleasant an painful )?

    - - -

    ( secondary questions )
    ( I was thinking about a distopic settings were vampire population exploded and reliable sources of sustenance must be developed )

    * The blood of OGM pigs ( modified with human DNA ), more similar - genetically - to human blood, would taste better? Mabye also be more nourishing?
    * Animal blood laced with a small quantity of morphine and/or alchool could ameliorate the taste?

      I actually make this a point with Generation. My house rule interpretation is the following:

      1. Thin Bloods find Animal Blood appetizing and pleasant. It is not only entirely possible to subsist on animal blood as a Thin Blood but easy. As such, they are the only vampires who have a semi-decent chance of remaining "good" people no matter what.

      2. 13th, 12th, and 11th generation vampires find animal blood nutritious but bland. I wouldn't even say porridge without sugar but drinking a protean shake. Vampires of this generation pretty much use animal blood as a means of slaking their hunger and fuel. It's very useful and can prevent you from frenzying. It's also something said vampires drink en masse in order to heal injuries in lieu of killing someone.

      3. 8th, 9th, and 10th generation vampires find animal blood to be fucking NASTY. It's like drinking from a brown pond in the middle of the forest with leaves. It might even make you sick. Unlike 5th Edition rules, vampires CAN keep it down and feed from it with a point of willpower per scene and use it for healing or subsistence but it is a horrible experience all round.

      4. Lower generations still? Don't even think about it. Its impossible to keep it down.

      Human blood?

      Human blood is like sex and heroin for regular vampires and thus incredibly addictive.

              Vampires are the embodiment of murder. So in my mind, flavorwise blood is blood. In each case the blood tastes the same, but the half psychological/half mystical urge to commit murder builds more. This translates into increased frenzy difficulties, inability to regain willpower, that sort of thing.

                          Something worth considering is that animal blood is, ounce per ounce, less sustaining that human blood. We see this in the charts for how many blood points you receive from animals. Vampires must drain four whole rats just to get a single BP, and even full grown cattle only have five. Despite the latter being much larger, and therefore having a higher volume of blood in their system.

                          What this tells me is that, setting aside anything else about the symbolism of murder or personal taste, animal blood is just really watered down. Like a broth. At least porridge is a dense mass of carbohydrates. Drinking animal blood must be like trying to subsist on milk or whipped cream or heavily fluffed up bread. You can subsist on it, but you need to eat a whole lot more to get anything out of it.

                          For a vampire, feeding from animals might seem like a huge chore. Chugging a great deal of it, just to get the same value from a relatively small amount of human blood. Not to mention most animals will die from it, necessitating the body be disposed of, to escape notice.

                              Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                              I actually make this a point with Generation.
                              I generally do something similar in my chronicles, but have it more be a function of age and Humanity rather than Generation. But it's a tendency rather than a hard rule. The younger and less depraved the vampire, the more likely animal blood is merely bland rather than disgusting. As a vampire gets older and the age wears down on your Humanity, your diet becomes increasingly limited until certain Methusalehs can only drink vampire vitae.

                              So PCs, who are supposed to be young vampires and likely start with higher humanity, have certain advantages that most of the NPCs of the chronicle don't have because animal blood is more palatable to them. And it's possible that if a PC becomes really nasty, or if the chronicle proceeds for a long time in game, that the players will begin noticing the changes in their tastes.

                              There are other factors as well. Generation can be one of them, as are things like feeding restrictions because of Flaws or Clan Weaknesses. And I'll allow Gangrel to more easily consume animal blood since many of them are supposed to spend a lot of time in the wilderness.

                                                  Editions are always tweaking and changing stuff. Personally, I think it's best to take from all over the editions based on your own tastes. 1e's way of doing things is as canon as V5's

                                                  I'd say that "animal blood is fine, but not as tasty" and "animal blood tastes like raw sewage" are both true statements, depending on the vampire. Some might not mind subsisting on animal blood, despite the extra effort and that they'll never enjoy it like they would human blood. Some enjoy sipping cow blood between victims, like chewing gum for the flavour, but don't consider it a meal. Other vampires would gag and have to choke it down.

                                                  For physical benefit, ease of "digestion" and psychological enjoyment, prey is ranked, with a rapid plummeting in quality/enjoyment; vampire > fresh human > stored/dead human blood > mammals > other animals

                                                  Vampires are built to convert fresh human blood into vitae. Blood that differs is harder to convert, and has a lower yield for effort. It's all around less rewarding than feeding on healthy humans. And however many wasps you eat, I don't think a vampire can hold down haemolymph any better than a cheese sandwich.
                                                  I would rule that your pig example would actually be a pretty good human substitute, aside from the missing high of the Kiss.

                                                  The drug example would do nothing for the taste or the hunger/blood pool, I think, but the high might make it more worthwhile as a human alternative to the desperate. A sort of poor substitute, but a substitute all the same.
