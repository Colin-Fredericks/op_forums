    Can a vampire build himself a living body with Vicissitude 6?

    05-25-2023, 05:55 PM
    From Player's Guide to the High Clans:

    With normal Vicissitude, a Tzimisce may take the arm from one person and graft it to another, but the recipient is unable to control the arm and it soon begins to decay. With this power, the user may take body parts from living creatures and graft them on to others, living or undead, and allow for the recipient to use the new limb. Exceptionally monstrous and disturbing creatures can be created with this power or one may graft extra organs into others, allowing them to function longer while wounded.

    System: Removing a limb requires an Intelligence + Body Crafts roll (difficulty 6). Extracting an organ is an extended Intelligence + Body Crafts roll (difficulty 7), with 10 successes required and each roll representing an hour of work. The process of grafting must then begin within an hour. or the limb or organ dies. Attachment is an extended roll of Intelligence + Body Crafts (difficulty 7), requiring 10 successes and each roll representing half an hour's work. Attaching an appropriate limb, such as a human arm to a human, is difficulty 7. Attaching an inappropriate limb, such as horse legs to a human, is difficulty 8. Inserting organs is difficulty 9. Attaching limbs to a vampire is somewhat easier (difficulty 6). Theoretically, one could put organs inside a vampire, but this has no effect.

    The effect of attaching extra limbs is entirely up to the Storyteller. Extra arms might help in grappling and multiple attacks and extra legs might aid stability but the main effect is generally freakishness rather than effectiveness, since coordinating the extra limbs is very difficult. Extra organs grant the recipient an additional wounded health level per organ, though a maximum of two can be grafted.
    ​

    So, could a vampire replace his undead body piece by piece with limbs, muscles, bones, organs ecc. untill he has an almost-completely alive body and achieve something similar to life?

      Since it mentions that organs fail when implanted into the undead, I think the answer is "no". My reading is that while you can, for example, add another pair of arms and those arms become part of you, that they are as undead as your original pair are. For example, if you attach your oesophagus to their stomach and swallow food, then their stomach could digest it, right? You could use auspex to share their senses, replace your tongue with theirs to taste food, use their reproductive system to have kids...
      And if you wear a ghoul as a meat suit, does this protect you from sunlight?

          A thing to consider is that vampirism is a curse on the soul itself, replacing the body parts won't give you a complete work around.

          A good example of this is a vampire using Thaumaturgy Path of Summoning to create a living body duplicate of themselves and then use Dominate to put their mind/soul into the body. Sure, you are not going to burn in sunlight, but you are still going to rolling for freak outs and staying awake the whole time.

          Furthermore, an ST might consider the concept that if you over mod your body to the point that it is considered normal/mortal, there is the a chance that your vampiric soul might disconnect. The result being like if your body was destroyed while you were possessing a mortal or you were astral projecting with Auspex. You can't regain willpower and slowly decay until you meet final death.

                                              Yes, you can graft a living body part from one living being onto another living being, and that new body part will function and be alive.
