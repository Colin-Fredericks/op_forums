                  How about something that makes the previous levels much easier to roll, meaning statistically more successes, meaning narratively more consistent and impressive results when Presence is used?
                  For example, Presence 1 is nice, but getting 5 successes on a diff. 7 roll to influence the whole room with a dice pool of 14 dice maximum (for vamps who can reach 7 dots in each trait) is rare. The average is 4.2 successes, and that's with the maximum dice pool. One could go heavy handedly to Majesty, but it's not the same and it's not subtle at all. The other powers have similar statistical limits, somewhat lessened for those where the difficulty depend on the victim's traits, if they have low traits.

                          Maybe look at level 7 powers from other Disciplines and adapt something?                                   It's important to remember that originally only the level 1-5 powers of a Discipline were "standard". At level 6 and above, characters could develop their own powers of that Discipline rank. Elder NPCs who were generation 7 and stronger who had Disciplines at 6+ always had a description bloc of what their specific Discipline at those ranks. Thus powers are often very particular to what that NPC wanted. As originally written, PCs could not normally learn those Disciplines because their generation was too weak so there wasn't a need to provide anything for players except as a rare exception.

                                  The descriptions of Rank 6+ Disciplines in the Revised sourcebooks were mainly taken from existing canon NPCs that existed in earlier editions, and anything that was missing was simply filled in using those examples as guidelines.

                                  So I see the description of level 6+ powers simply as a rough guide to use for STs and PCs when designing their own powers. If you don't like a power, create something you do like.

                                                          Here's the first Amalgam power I created for my Malkavian character.

                                                          Shared Resilience came about because I thought that good help is hard to find and smart kindred wouldn't needlessly sacrifice assets needlessly.

                                                          Shared Resilience Fortitude 2 and Auspex 3
                                                          . Prequistes: Shared Senses/Toughness
                                                          Cost: 1 Rouse Check
                                                          Dice Pool: Stamina+Auspex
                                                          System: The Kindred can choose to extend some of their Fortitude powers to Ghouls/Humans affected by there Auspex. Anyone thus anyone imbued gains additional health equal to the fortitude dots of said Kindred.

                                                          Duration: 1 scene

                                                          My character likes to use this ability alot as he still cares about his agents be it ghouls or freelancers that have impressed him with their work. It's useful and not to over the top.
