          The followers of Set have easy access to thaumaturgy, though it's not in-clan. Assamite sorcerers have Quietus and Thaumaturgy, though we're inclined to forgive them because quietus is pretty bad RAW

          IMO, one of the defining features of rare disciplines is you can't use them all the time. They have very specific use cases and large drawbacks. Few disciplines are as powerful as Obtenebration, but Obtenebration powers are showy masquerade breakers and use blood resources so it's not something you want to use on a night-to-night basis IE while looking for blood.

          The common disciplines on the otherhand can be used all the time: they aren't very showy and only Presence is resource intensive. They make unlife easier, and there's a good chance you might develop them naturally through hunting in a particular way. The clans with three common disciplines are the most successful and widespread clans, because they just have an easier time hunting and a lower skill floor.

                                                      As Shadeprowler pointed out, what you communicate to your players and how makes or breaks the game, specially when your group is new.

                                                      Different players have different styles, preferences, degrees of familiarity and/or investment in the lore, metaplot, pet clans, bloodlines, canon NPCs or even book events, so taking some time to just discuss fluff & stuff, so you get everybody in the same page or have a good idea of how to balance out their likes and peeves to make the kind of chronicle you want is advisable.

                                                      In fact I'd say this is more important than ever nowadays when you can have VtM players of quite different generations and play experiences, going from people who have been around since Under a Blood Red Moon was hot news just out of the prints, 30-somethings who experienced the setting through their mom or dad's Revised era collection of books and clan novels or got their PDFs after falling in love with some Bloodlines or Redemption mod to 20-somethings hooked by the Winter's Teeth or some of the new V5-related slew of games.

                                                      (For reference, yeah, i have all of those in my recently started V20-esque group on Discord)


                                                      Part peep-talk and setting intro, part brainstorming as the players react to things, conceits and whatever hooks you feel won't spoil things too much, they come up with questions or ideas of their own, that you might redirect or co-opt and use as hooks to directly connect them to your plans (be the open ones or the plots you are hiding for the future).

                                                      Through this you get to inform the group of some of the things you have in mind for the chronicle, polish, fine-tune and adjust directions according to how they answer to that, not to mention questions or ideas of their own they bring to the mix. Plus, having the whole group rambling together about all fluff and all kind of pet novel, book or game references is very helpful in getting everybody engaged, immersed and sometimes weaving their characters together on the spot.

                                                      (Damn, initially i was looking to play in someone else's New Orleans game, but i started discussing a Gehenna cult concept i have and how it sort of connected with Berlin by Night and Bloodlines both among other funky bits, what led into some reminiscing of the videogame, from there to Redemption - and the Legacy of Kaine series - and a few hours later from player in a "table" of three i found myself as ST of group of six and their ensemble of weirdoes for PCs. )

                                                      But yeah, preparation is how you find out what kind of homebrew or lore hack can roll fine and hook the players and what to dial down or abort and steer away from (successful Vienna and Venice raids by 2nd Inquisition - or a retooling of Thaumaturgy & Necromancy paths might be somewhat problematic if you have diehard Tremere & Giovanni fanboys or girls, for example) before hitting a minefield, due to keeping one too many cards close to the chest until the game proper started.​
