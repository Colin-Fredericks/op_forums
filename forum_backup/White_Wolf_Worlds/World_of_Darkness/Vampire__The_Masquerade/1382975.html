    Humanity variant - The Beast

    04-21-2020, 11:33 AM
    Putting some thoughts to a reworking of Humanity that would accommodate Paths and such within the current V5 system. What if we measure how strong the Beast is within you, not how much Humanity you retain. The higher your rating, the stronger the Beast has become. The lower the rating, the better you can interact with humans and relate to them. This would take the current systems of abilities based on Humanity score and reverse it.

    As you go through unlife, the Beast would gain victories (stains). When the Beast has accumulated more victories than its current rating, the vampire risk Degeneration. They can choose to be impaired for the remainder of the session or allow the Beast to gain a stronger hold, increasing their Beast rating by one and dropping the victories to 0.

    I think this system might better reflect a goal of having Paths, as they were always a way to keep the Beast at bay. Additionally, with some of the Loresheets we have seen, they simply get reworded to say that the Beast gains no victories for certain actions you can undertake.

                                      Originally posted by SarcasticJazzHands View Post
                                      Legendre - That is an interesting system. How do they each raise/lower?
                                      Humanity is raised, and Beast is lowered, by spending XP. It's the only way and it's expensive.

                                      Humanity is lost the usual way: through the performance of immoral acts and omissions. The system is very, very customized and doesn't resemble VtM RAW at all. There are things called "Inhumanity Traits" which function as a sort of categorical immunity to moral degeneration. The gist is that if you commit an act that registers for your Humanity score and don't have immunity for that sort of act, then you check to see if you lose humanity by contesting your Humanity against the number of Inhumanities you have. If you lose, you lose a point of Humanity.

                                      Beast -- which starts at 3 -- is gained a few ways. The first is committing immoral acts/omissions while in frenzy. If you undergo moral degeneration while in frenzy, you gain beast instead of losing humanity. You can also gain Beast by voluntarily frenzying, or by failing to attempt to resist a frenzy. You can also gain a Beast Point by using up all of your "rerolls" during a combat. (One of the additional rules is that when you are in Frenzy, you can reroll a number of combat or physical contests equal to your Beast Rating.)

                                                      Originally posted by Black Flag View Post
                                                      I think people get caught up in the terminology of previous editions...
                                                      That is why I thought to present this option.

                                                      So, more put together elevator pitch for it:

                                                      Beast is a rating of the strength of your inner predator. When you do predatory things, things that empower the Beast, it can grow. When you connect with humans and Humanity, the Beast can shrink back. When you do Predatory acts, if they are inline with Chronicle Tenets and personal Convictions, you can lessen the Ravages of the Beast. If you choose to take Touchstones (which should be treated differently with this system) they are points of contact, interaction systems to keep the Beast at bay. Touchstones, therefore, can then be people, as well as places, items or even routines, things that connect and remind you of when you didn't have the Beast.
