The Gehenna War, the fate of the Sabbat, the fate of Signature NPCs, and the mysteries of things like, "Why did Theo Bell kill Hardelstadt?" "What is the forces behind the Second Inquisition". "What is the cause of the Beckoning?", "Is Lucita dead?", and "What was the Vermillion Wedding's repercussions?", "How will the Anarchs develop?"

                Basically, I was in full binge watch prestige TV mode and the results ended up being the increasing realization that either only certain creators were actually interested in exploring these questions or they weren't actually questions that had answers. They created the mysteries without ever actually intending to answer them. THE LAST JEDI really annoyed me as a movie but it wasn't until THE RISE OF SKYWALKER that I realized they were just making it up as they went along and didn't have any actual plan.

                I mentioned THE SECOND INQUISITION book disappointed me more than THE SABBAT book and I think that can be summarized in the realization the Second Inquisition didn't have any history, NPCs, cause and effect, or personality to it beyond, "They will come and fuck up the vampires." This is a SHARP contrast to two of my all time favorite WOD supplements: PROJECT TWILIGHT and THE GUIDE TO THE TECHNOCRACY.

                Project: Twilight detailed the SAD department extensively with NPCs that represented competiting factions, goals, ideologies, and operating procedures. The Guide to the Technocracy didn't have any NPCs but it had loads and loads of personality. You could side with the Union but also understand if the ST wanted to treat them as a bunch of fascist murderers. Both sides were represented and were consistent. It also was almost entirely removed from the previous depiction of the SAD, Inquisition, and NSA.

                I wanted books full of mysteries and answers and got....stats.

                                                    Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

                                                    For me, this is a very familiar feeling because it's basically the same one that I had for THE FORCE AWAKENS as I was incredibly interested in the world-building and mysteries that had been set up. The Gehenna War, the fate of the Sabbat, the fate of Signature NPCs, and the mysteries of things like, "Why did Theo Bell kill Hardelstadt?" "What is the forces behind the Second Inquisition". "What is the cause of the Beckoning?", "Is Lucita dead?", and "What was the Vermillion Wedding's repercussions?", "How will the Anarchs develop?"

They created the mysteries without ever actually intending to answer them. THE LAST JEDI really annoyed me as a movie but it wasn't until THE RISE OF SKYWALKER that I realized they were just making it up as they went along and didn't have any actual plan.
                                                    This is a bit us WoD5 critics kind of clocked to at the start: all these setting changes were not made to add more stories, but to SUBTRACT existing ones. They were written to sweep away NPCs and factions from the old continuity, so V5's writers would never need to deal with them ever again.

                                                    They were never meant to be mysteries. They were excuses. Excuses for why the setting is suddenly a huge blank slate.

                                                    At least V20's "Metaplot Agnostic" approach didn't invalidate the old fixtures. It just made them optional. And it still took time to make its own plot as the edition went on, like with the Montmartre Pact or BJD. They knew the metaplot had value, even if it shouldn't be a shackle around the wrists of Storytellers.

                                                    V5's "mysteries" were nothing of the sort, because they were never meant to be. All they existed to do was wipe the slate clean. And then V5's writers proceeded to replace it with basically nothing.

                                                    I mentioned THE SECOND INQUISITION book disappointed me more than THE SABBAT book and I think that can be summarized in the realization the Second Inquisition didn't have any history, NPCs, cause and effect, or personality to it beyond, "They will come and fuck up the vampires." This is a SHARP contrast to two of my all time favorite WOD supplements: PROJECT TWILIGHT and THE GUIDE TO THE TECHNOCRACY.

                                                    Project: Twilight detailed the SAD department extensively with NPCs that represented competiting factions, goals, ideologies, and operating procedures. The Guide to the Technocracy didn't have any NPCs but it had loads and loads of personality. You could side with the Union but also understand if the ST wanted to treat them as a bunch of fascist murderers. Both sides were represented and were consistent. It also was almost entirely removed from the previous depiction of the SAD, Inquisition, and NSA.

                                                    I wanted books full of mysteries and answers and got....stats.
                                                    The Second Inquisition is a continuation of my point above. V5's writers plainly did not conceptualize hunter orgs like this as potential protagonists of their own stories. (Something made depressingly obvious by H5's open, textual, seething contempt for hunter orgs). The SI was intended exclusively as a Vampire-facing fixture. An antagonist faction that existed to hammer Kindred down when they got too uppity, and to enforce greater levels of secrecy and insularity. Especially on the part of the Camarilla, which V5's designers didn't want players acting within anymore. (It's the same reason why the Sabbat got blown up; the Gehenna Wars are not playable, because V5 did not want people playing Sabbat characters).

                                                    In earlier editions, SAD and other groups got detailed write-ups, internal factions, and NPCs, because they were meant to be playable. Now the SI are baddies thrown at VtM players if they deviate from the street-level play V5's was meant for.
