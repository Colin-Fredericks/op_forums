            1 - Fragmentation ammunition and explosive ammunition are destructive enough to rip pieces off, I suggest that this is enough to cause Lethal damage

            2 - Dragonbreath ammunition with a shotgun can quickly set a target on fire, as a house rule I use the shotgun's damage roll as lethal damage and in addition it sets the target on fire causing 2 aggravated damage with difficulty 8 to absorb the damage with Fortitude per round, the target can spend 1 action to "put out the fire", in addition I cause Rostshreck effect due to the flames, I also require a Rostshreck test if a Cainite wants to shoot with Dragonbreath ammunition.

            3 - Headshots effectively cause Lethal damage, the shot is made with difficulty 8, however if this shot is made with fragmentation or explosive ammunition it causes Aggravated Damage as a house rule.

            4 - The Aim maneuver adds Perception dice to the shooting test, snipers shooting at the head can be lethal.

            5 - I only allow the Dodge maneuver against firearms shots if there is cover, the difficulty to dodge is 8 in this case

            6 - distance is essential, calculate exactly how far away the shooters and the targets are, most firearms have a reasonable range, shooting, retreating away from the target's line of sight and on the next turn returning to the original position and shooting again is a possible maneuver

            7 - explosives are efficient, tactical mines, white phosphorus grenades and incendiaries certainly cause aggravated damage, something around 8 to 12 damage dice are quite efficient to at least cripple Cainites, in addition to causing Rostsherck

            8 - flamethrowers and molotov cocktails are efficient weapons against any Cainite, 3 aggravated damage per round by flames enveloping the target's entire body, in addition to causing Rostsherck

            9 - thermal vision devices are accessible, if you are not dealing with Eyes of the Beast or Sharp Senses, a team that eliminates the light from the area can cause an unpleasant surprise effect, if the target has the above disciplines use the opposite effect, flash grenades can certainly blind vampires

            10 - Heavy Crossbows exist and are very powerful, firearms with special ammunition are more effective, but a precise shot to the heart with a Heavy Crossbow can turn the game for humans

            11 - Never ignore the other disciplines, Animalism can counter packs of wolves and bears, depending on the battlefield, vampires with Animalism must be prepared and keep wild animals in strategic places to defend themselves in isolated places, parks, zoos and sewers are perfect

            12 - Never ignore mental disciplines, Dominate can create meat shields, with Presence and Majesty few attackers can approach and those who fail can no longer act against the vampire holding Majesty

            13 - Chimerism can create false targets, with Obfuscate level 5 a Vampires can change the appearance of everyone around them, confusing attackers and targets

            14 - Do not underestimate the Beast and Humanity. When vampires in combat have few blood points (something between 7 or less), consider making Frenzy tests out of hunger. Seeing blood everywhere can make the Beast take control. I have seen elders lick the ground with blood like animals and be surprised by devastating attacks due to their lack of control.

            15 - Do not ignore degeneration tests. Monstrous actions, mass murder and brutality are rewarded with degeneration tests. Degenerate vampires with low Humanity levels raise suspicion about their actions. Rivals and adversaries will use this to accuse them of being a threat to the Masquerade. No one wants a degenerate vampire walking around. If he becomes a degenerate nail, the hammer of traditions and the fear of other elders should be the immediate motivation for a Blood Hunt. Be a monster and you will be treated as a degenerate. bandit, cameras and cell phones are everywhere, a hidden mortal recording the bloodbath caused by vampires and delivering it to an enemy can cause more damage than any group of armed mortals, blackmail and veiled threats can cause more destruction than just armed mortals

            16 - If all else fails, use Napalm, it burns and sticks to the skin, not even water can erase it

                        What enchantment would be applied to the gun rather than the ammo?

                        I could see a "luck" enchantment that means the gun never jams even on a botch, a cooling charm so that the barrel never warps due to sustained rapid fire, a durability enchantment that means you never have to replace the parts, or perhaps a cleaning charm that means the barrel never fouls regardless how infrequently you clean the weapon. But none of those affect damage on target and most don't interact with RAW for guns.

                        The closest thing by the rules you get is using the Thaumaturgy rite of Ward vs. X on a bullet which doesn't deform and doesn't over penetrate so it stays in the body and keeps zapping the target. This requires you to spend a great deal of time warding individual rounds, and then making sure you are using the right ammo for the target.

                        To keep from wasting shots, the gun would have to be low fire rate, relatively precise for the range used, and perhaps most critically of all it must be easy to swap ammo types on the fly depending on who is fighting. The on-the-fly ammo change is the part that kills the action economy with most weapons because even if you use magazines to fast swap ammo, you still need to clear the auto-loaded first round.

                        So long range sniper rifles to keep you safe while you swap ammo types or something like a double-barreled shotgun close range because you are reloading it every two shots anyways, you just have to have a vest or belt system figured out ahead of time so you don't have to think too hard about which enchanted shell to reach for.

                                    Originally posted by Theodrim View Post
                                    Doesn't work by RAW, the act of firing the weapon disrupts the ward sigil.
                                    Perhaps I am misinterpreting the RAW

                                    VtM 20th page 234
                                    Bullets often warp upon firing, however, and for a ward to remain intact on a fired round, the player needs five successes on the Firearms roll.
                                    Bullets warp in a couple ways. The first is if the projectile is made of a soft material, such as lead, which means yes firing the round will distort its shape and it will further distort on impact. The second way is if the bullet is specifically designed to deform post impact to increase wound size, such as with hollow points, ball ammo, or on the other end of the spectrum is frangibles which disintegrate into powder once in the wound to prevent over penetration.

                                    But if you use a bullet designed for armor penetration, the projectile doesn't have parts that warp by design and isn't made out of soft materials. So, you can more or less guarantee the ward will stay intact through firing and impact. The issue at this point is that armor penetration uses a lot more propellant to achieve its goals, so even if you warded a bullet the extra damage would just be stacked on top of the initial damage and stop once the bullet over penetrated the vampire body and kept going.

                                    To counter the over penetration issue, you merely reduce the number of grains of propellent used in the casing. Firearms enthusiasts manufacture their own custom ammunition all the time. The trick is figuring out the right amount of powder for the bullet to stay lodged in the targets body and keep zapping them round after round until someone digs it out.

                                    Now all of this is based off of using premade components that you buy in bulk and assemble on your own with standardized tools and measurements. It takes less than five minutes to assemble a single bullet this way. The time-consuming part is the warding, which requires one blood point and ten minutes to setup, but then takes 10 hours to finish curing.

                                    So, unless you have a large Herd close at hand, you are unlikely to make more than a few of these bullets per night which means you can't spray and pray as a shooting style with warded bullets. Furthermore, you have to have the right type of warded bullet for the right target so this cuts down how much this can be leveraged in play, unless the player goes completely mental and multi-wards every bullet, but that just increases the production cost and time.
