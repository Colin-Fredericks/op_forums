                                                    Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
                                                    The problem with the indian ravnos was exactly that. 'don't look at the gypsies, look at us, the indians'. Apart from perhaps Tzmisce (who have an excuse, even if not a good one) No other clan has been so bound to a location, and yet the Ravnos have alway competed with the gangrel for the title of most free spirited and mobile clan. Just makes me wonder wtf they are doing different in india. The indian ravnos had some good writing around them, but they're held up like a distraction, an excuse, a 'model community' and perhaps some writer's excuse to show off how much they know about culture on the subcontinent. What we need is a Ravnos writup that doesnt focus on one country at the expense of ravnos everywhere else.
                                                    This is my problem with the Revised depiction of Ravnos as well. The clan is still too heavily tied to a human culture. It's a good second draft of the clan, but it's still one or more drafts away from an acceptable final product. Though I also think some other clans could use another one or two drafts as well.

                                                    What needs to happen is to look at the common themes of "wandering gypsy tricksters who steal from anyone not them" and "demonic magical masters of illusion," and build on that to a more universal archetype. And of course, a major thing with all the clans is that they contain multitudes. For every vampire that adheres to the stereotype or image, there are those that don't. Yet collectively they should still seem to evoke the clan. So you need examples of Ravnos that don't fit the general type yet still seem like Ravnos.

                                                    That by itself is challenging enough, but it's more difficult because you also need to jettison the poorly thought out Clan Weaknesses and Chimestry Discipline. So a complete rewrite is needed.

                                                    The other problem with the Ravnos being delegated "the Indian clan" is it ignores that Vampire has always had prominent examples of existing clans in India. It's OK for the Ravnos to have a powerbase in India that they don't elsewhere and even be the leading clan in India. But their scope needs to be beyond that, and India should stop being seen as exotic to the Camarilla and Sabbat when we have had early prominent examples like Vasantasena and Unmada (plus Bindusara and others). (Perhaps my biggest complaint about the Revised era was its decision to stop treating the Camarilla like a vast worldwide conspiracy of vampires to a modern multicultural approach of dividing vampires into sects based on human cultures. The first is awesome and inspiring. The second is boring and trite.)

                                                    I don't support V5 so I don't know how well its done handling the clan, but I imagine it likely hasn't done what is needed to get it to a place I find acceptable.
