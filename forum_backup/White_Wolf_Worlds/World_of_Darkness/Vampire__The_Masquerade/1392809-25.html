    Edit:

    My View of Vicissitude

    I feel like Vicissitude has always been in need of a tweak gameplay-wise for balance issues. Vampires should be able to affect themselves with Vicissitude but flesh-crafting others should be a higher-level ability, especially if it's supposed to be permanent. I think the best way to handle it would be to combine the Path of Biothaumaturgical Experimentation and Vicissitude so that affecting the appearance of someone else requires a laboratory/torture chamber so that the various horrors and monsters that the Tzimisce are famous for can be created.

    Mind you, it's always been established that the more elaborate Tzimisce creations like vozhd require knowledge of Koldunism to create. Those who think they could create a human centipede with just Vicissitude are ignoring the text of the game. Mind you, I am biased as the Path of Biothaumaturgical Experimentation is something my characters have always loved and the most underrated path. It should absolutely be a Tzimisce-exclusive Path (or maybe Tzimisce/Tremere since Gargoyles exist) and its Path levels adapted to rituals for V5.

    https://whitewolf.fandom.com/wiki/Bi...xperimentation

    I don't know if they're going to do it this way for the Tzimisce but I hope they do.
