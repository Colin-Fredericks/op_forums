                Anarchs and Charity

                Ultimately, I find myself once more disagreeing with MyWifeIsScary. Ironically, it's because we're actually on the same page but use different math to reach the same conclusion. It's not 1+1=3 versus 1+1=2 but 2-2=0 and 0+0=0. Both of us like incredibly dark and unforgiving World of Darknesses. MWIS believes that this is achieved by thinking that the majority of Princes and Anarchs are going to be solely pro-vampire selfish dirtbags who are only focused on their own survival. A lot of vampires are like that in my games, yes. However, no, those guys mean shit in the end. No, the scary ones are the ones who BELIEVE. Those are the really dangerous ones in the World of Darkness.

                This is actually something that comes up in Vampire: The Masquerade: Winter's Teeth if you haven't checked out that awesome comic by Tim Seeley. It's only had two issues out but they've already woven a pretty complicated character arc and discussed how Humanity affects the vampires involved.

                https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.p...asqueradeVault

                The primary protagonist of the books is Cecily Bain, a former Anarch turned Camarilla Stooge Brujah. Cecily was an Anarch in the 70s to late 90s fighting against the system. Cecily believes she's "grown up" and that the Anarch cause was stupid. She thinks the only way to survive is to selfishly pursue her own interests at all costs. She only works for herself, her sister (who is now dying of old age), and disdaining any emotional attachments as impediments.

                Cecily is, in a word, fucked.

                Her lack of emotional attachments and causes is seriously screwing with her Humanity. It's also resulting in her lashing out at everyone around her in a way that has made her numerous enemies. She kills another Kindred's Touchstone to show her contempt for caring for mortals and instead of him breaking, he's gained a life goal of destroying her. She's tried to teach her childe that killing "evil" people doesn't help because they have loved ones and it's only made her childe hate her. Her attitude of, "We're parasites, why care?" pisses off everyone.

                Cecily isn't WRONG. Vampires ARE awful but they need a kind of anchor to keep them sane. A self-delusion that they're not purely evil parasites. At least if they're not completely alien monsters like the Sabbat. This is actually baked into the fundamentals of the setting, IMHO.

                1. Brujah have their causes and every Brujah cares about one cause or another.
                2. Toreador justify their existence by cultivating art and beauty.
                3. Nosferatu have their bands of the homeless, runaways, and lost.
                4. Ventrue build their kingdoms that they feel an obligation to even if the health of Amazon.com comes before its workers or Chicago before its citizens.
                5. Tremere justify their existence by pushing the boundaries of knowledge, science, and magic
                6. Gangrel are the only ones who actially are purley survival focused and live like animals from night to night.
                7. Malkavians? Okay, fuck if I know what motivates the Malkavians, but they DO have a motivation.

                Every vampire needs something to get them through their immortal lives and make them feel.

                However, the trick to make it truly dark is that their attempts to help the cause will almost always make it worse. Michael Corleone wanst to protect his family by getting them out of the Mob life? Well, watch the bodies pile up and the horrors he perpetuates get bigger as he does more evil than his father to go legitimate. Scarface wants to be a big man? Well, watch his sister become addicted to cocaine and her husband get murdered because Tony wants her to be a virginal pure Touchstone for his family image.

                The Banu Haqim are a perfect example of this as they are totally 100% convinced of their righteousness and are among the most alien psychos of all. Ditto the Followers of Set who have reversed good and evil but will passionately die for the cause of selfishness. Vampires will almost always have a cause but they will almost certainly make it worse. Annabelle, Lodin, and other Elders almost certainly have "pet causes" they're involved in (like mobsters and corrupt corporate executives) but these things are salves on the wound of their humanity. It allows them to do worse.

                But don't take my word for it, take the Doctor's.
