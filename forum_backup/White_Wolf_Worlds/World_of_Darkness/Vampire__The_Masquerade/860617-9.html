    136) A Molochim Baali who specializes in analyzing and "sanitizing" Dark Thaumaturgy, and other forms of Infernal blood sorcery.

    Any blood magician sufficiently familiar with Infernal sorcery understands that much of its methods and ritual components are, for all practical purposes, unnecessary. No one needs to sacrifice infants, draw circles in feces, desecrate holy symbols, or chant litanies to the infernal host. Other forms of blood magic get by perfectly fine without them. Rather, Infernalists do magic this way because it's the only method afforded them by their demonic patrons, who have a vested interest in ensuring both the damnation of their supplicant and the perpetuation of evil in the world. The way the magic is formatted is designed for this. And because demons don't explain why a spell must be cast a certain way, it ensures that 1) the supplicant is entirely dependent on their patron for all magic, and 2) that evil is done even if the pawn spreads their secrets around. If an Infernalist takes an apprentice or writes a spell down in notes, the knowledge passed down will be of spells and rituals that will Damn a soul just for casting them.

    This Baali knows this. As part of the Tal'Mahe'Ra, he makes it his mission to undo such activity.

    An experienced occultist and blood magician, with knowledge of multiple different practices, this Baali goes around collecting the notes of Infernalists. When he isn't capturing them and forcing the secrets out, of course. Once he's collected this knowledge, he carefully analyzes it from the perspective of a magical theorist. Excising redundant elements and making less morally repugnant substitutions. While the removal of Infernal texts (and Infernalists) is a good in and of itself, it's also important to extract genuinely useful magical secrets from tainted sources. If an Infernal Ritual can perform a feat otherwise unavailable to "clean" blood magic, this Baali will aim to correct that mistake. To derive a formula for safer consumption, while removed of as much atrocity, depravity, and blasphemy as possible.

                    Quinn Wade (10th Gen Caitiff): It doesn't matter what anyone says, when it comes to side effects of the embrace, Quinn is convinced that he hit the jackpot. But hey, you'd be glad to be a Caitiff too, if your sire was a member of the Nosferatu! At least he doesn't have to disguise himself as a damn leper when he goes out in public. But the true icing on the cake is that the circumstances behind Quinn's embrace were intended to serve as a punishment. It turns out that Mr. Wade was not a morally upstanding citizen in life, and the clan of sewer rats thought they could impart some much needed humility by turning him into one of their own. But when the first week passed by and Quinn exhibited no symptoms of turning into a hideous monster, they kicked him out of the warrens to fend for himself.

                    In his mortal life, Quinn was a member of the city guard, who abused his position of authority over the peasant folk. His crimes at the time included extortion, police brutality, accepting bribes, tampering with evidence and even sexual assault. But at least his influence was limited by the fact he was a low ranking guardsmen. After his embrace, however, he began using his supernatural abilities to quickly escalate himself through the ranks and has now become the captain of his own night watch garrison, with a retinue of guardsmen under his control. Now it's not just mortals who need to fear him, because he's also been directing his corruption towards other fledglings who he feels are too low in status to attempt retaliation.

                    Of course, this would have to take place in the dark ages, too. A time where the heads of state were largely unsympathetic to the plights of those beneath their station. Which basically means the Prince is content to let Quinn go about his business so long as it does not negatively impact his own schemes. Besides, having a kindred who's not affiliated with any particularly clan as a member of the city guard may prove useful in keeping the serfs and vassals under control.


                    Mr. Wade appears as a young caucasian male in his mid to late 20's. He has short, chestnut colored hair and dark green eyes. He stands around 5'9 in height, with a moderately fit build of 162 lbs. He is rarely seen in public without his guard uniform on, which serves as both a symbol of his position in society, as well as constant protection in the event of an assassination attempt.


                    Quinn Wade is a follower of the Road of Sin, on the Path of Pleasure. In the time since his embrace and rise to power, he has also taught himself the disciplines of Potence, Celerity, Fortitude and Auspex.

                        Marshall Blueburn (11th Gen Malkavian):

                        At first glance, Mr. Blueburn would appear to be a healthy, functioning individual. But if one were to witness his feeding habits, his deep seeded madness becomes immediately recognizable. Even Marshall is not entirely certain why his hunger manifests in the way that it does, but the Malkavian can only willingly feed upon the blood of those who have been rendered physically powerless to resist his advances. Whether they have been forcibly tied down, rendered comatose, crippled for life, or suffering from severe brain damage, Mr. Blueburn is a sadistic predator who derives pleasure from knowing that he has absolute control over the lives of his prey. In a horrific effort to hide his appetites from public awareness, Marshall has imprisoned a few blood dolls within the confines of his Haven and has surgically amputated all four of their limbs to ensure they will never be able to escape on their own!

                        Both prior to and after his embrace, Marshall Blueburn serves as a charity organizer who helps collect money for those with physical disabilities, or life long injuries. The big difference is that now Blueburn has decided to extract blood from his clients in exchange for the financial support he offers them. There's a poetic metaphor in there somewhere, but Marshall chooses not to dwell on it.

                        Marshall has the appearance of a 31 year old caucasian man with chestnut colored hair and a full beard. His hair, both facial and that which grows from his scalp is trimmed short and neatly groomed into pristine condition. He dresses in modest, yet fashionable attire to showcase that he takes pride in presenting himself among others, without lording it over those who are less well off than he is. Marshall stands at six feet, three inches tall and weighs 152 pounds. He has bright green eyes which once exhibited a soft compassion, but now carry a sinister gleam behind them.


                        Faction: Anarchs

                        Demeanor: Caregiver

                        Nature: Sadist

                        Flaws: Dark Secret

                        Merits: Natural Leader

                        Derangement: ASPD

                            142. "Franceso the Eskimo" Giovanni made his money the old fashioned way: he stole it under direction from his 97-year-old ghouled father who abducted and brutally raped Frank's mother over a period of six weeks to punish her old-money New England family for getting too uppity around their betters, Clan Giovanni.

                            The Milliners, understandably, held little esteem for the resulting bastard baby boy, but given his pedigree they couldn't kill him outright. They could, however, disown Frank's mother, force her to shame his father by naming the baby after him, and send her to be a common waitress at a diner in Perth Amboy, NJ.

                            Frank grew up as a complete hellion. By grade school, he was beating up other students for their lunch money, or just for fun. By junior high, he was beating up teachers for their lunch money, or threatening them for grades. By sixteen, when he was 6'5" and still growing, he was mugging old ladies and swiping car stereos for spending cash. Frankie would argue he just played the hand he was dealt.

                            Exactly where he went bad is hard to say.

                            His mother, broken by her torture and familial rejection, had taken up with a string of boyfriends and did more drugs than most Ramones. One of her boyfriends had lived with them for a few years in the late seventies, when Frank was a small child. Frank would never tell anyone why he was terrified every time the man came into his room late at night, what the man did, or that he was both relieved and crushed when the man just vanished one night while driving home from a drug-deal. One thing Frank did learn when the police came to question his mother about the man's car (which was found idling on the Pulaski Skyway, with the passenger door open) was that no one could ever actually love him. Something in Frank was broken. Wrong. Ugly. Furthermore, since everyone will always fuck you, you better fuck them harder and first. If only to prevent your own pain.

                            Okay, maybe how Frank got broken isn't such a mystery. Not that anyone, including Frank, has ever figured it out.

                            In any case, by the time Frank could be tried as an adult, he was robbing convenience stores and breaking knees for local loan sharks. He got the nickname "Francesco the Eskimo" because of how he dressed. It didn't matter what the weather was, or how much it made him sweat. Frankie always covered up: long sleeve shirts, long pants, a loose fitting jacket, and a knit cap pulled down over his forehead. Sometimes, he even wore gloves. The guys he worked with assumed it was a ploy to make him hard for witnesses to identify.

                            This, as it happened, was true, but mostly it was that Frankie hated people to look at him. Anytime Frank saw himself in a mirror, he would fill with such self-loathing it gave him agita, driving him to eating Tums as a regular snack. He would shower with the lights out, and no photo exists of him after the age of eight. In fact, Frankie wasn't bad looking, and spent considerable time trying to look better. He worked out at the boxing gym for hours on end, he ate nothing but health food, and he jogged maniacally. He always wore expensive (if somewhat oversized) clothes and jewelry. He didn't just go to the corner barber shop, like his buddies. He went to an actual salon, with a stylist. Most people might even say he was a handsome young man. Still, Frankie remained convinced of his own repulsiveness.

                            On New Year's Eve 1990, when Frankie was 19, he and his buddies robbed a liquor store in Carteret for partying money. It went bad. Real bad. The clerk and two customers were killed. One of Frankie's buddies was captured, and snitched within hours. Frankie was on the lam, and his name was all over the news. Frankie's father, now a neo-nate Giovanni vampire, took notice and tracked his son to a cheap motel room in Asbury Park. Frank, Sr. offered him sanctuary, if he would serve him. Frankie enthusiastically agreed.

                            Frankie spent the next few years working out, and learning how to fight. He took boxing classes, and spent hours at the shooting range. He also did all the usual things a son does for an aged father: cooking, cleaning, and kidnapping runaways and hookers to eat for dinner. You know, like a good boy. Frankie knows he did a lot more (and a lot worse) than he remembers, since his father heavily edited his memory for most of those years. He does know that he's wanted for by the feds for a string of bank robberies from Nashua to Nashville, and his father is suddenly richer than Croesus.

                            Eventually, the time came when Frankie was offered the proxy kiss. The catch? He would serve his mother's family, up in the leafy enclave of Duxbury, Massachusetts.

                            Those years with his cousins were preppy hell for Frankie. Oh, they smiled and laughed all the time, in their LL Bean sweaters and khaki pants. They played touch football on the lawn. They spent summers on an island off the coast of Maine. Frankie slept in the fucking pool house, while they lived in a center hall colonial mansion. Oh, he drove a Mercedes, but as their chauffeur. Guess who had to pick up after their labradoodle when he shit on the putting-green-perfect lawn? Guess who picked up their dry cleaning and pruned their hedges? Guess who had to kill realtors and cops and assorted witnesses anytime the Milliner vampires asked him to, sneering through their perfect white smiles at the absurdity of having to talk to Frankie at all? He wanted to beat their blonde heads in with a shovel, and burn their bodies at noon.

                            So, one sunny Thursday afternoon in September, he did.

                            He expected to be killed for that, but, turns out this particular branch of the Milliners was skimming a tiny bit too much from their tributes to Venice, and Frankie was rewarded with the embrace. The Giovanni anzioni haven't told anyone who killed the Duxbury Milliners. They believe Frankie was the last survivor of a Sabbat attack, who was rewarded with the embrace for his valor in defending the family. Venice uses this secret for leverage to keep Frankie in line.

                            Frankie has been sent to Phillipsburg, NJ, to handle gun-running and other business the clan has with the Sabbat. He's learned about the existence of Vicissitude, and desperately wants to learn it. Being undead has done nothing to diminish his body image issues. In fact, the knowledge that he is doomed to spend eternity looking like he does... 6'8", 400 pounds of muscle, with a square jaw and broad shoulders... depresses him deeply. He has a long list of things he wants to change: the shape of his eyes, the distance from his lips to his nose, the length of his limbs, the curl of his pompadour hair, a mole on his beefy neck, and the size of his... ahem. He would do anything, including selling out his family, to get his flaws fixed. He hasn't found a Tzimisce he trusts enough to actually ask. That, and his shame at this obvious weakness of his.

                            In the meantime, Frankie is kicking ass, cracking skulls, and making big bucks for a Giovanni elder in Scranton. Frankie still turns out the lights when he showers, which makes it hard to get all the blood and gore out of his hair at the end of the night, but he's determined to play the hand he's been dealt.

                                        145) A Caitiff, her life was that of a modern business woman, working in a marketing firm. With her glasses and short hair, she had an attractive librarian-esque look. This and her social acumen apparently aroused the passion in her sire. During what was supposed to be a meeting to talk of an ad campaign for an art show, her sire was overcome by desire and drained her dry. Guilt motivated her to embrace the mortal, and then panic led to the pale woman fleeing. When the business woman awoke, the only sign of her assailant was a note tucked into her hand. "I'm sorry. You are a vampire now. Run, or others of our kind will kill you."

                                        The newly embraced Caitiff discovered the full gravity of her undead state in the nights following, and so finally dismantled what remained of her life, making off with a briefcase full of cash and what worldly possessions she could pack. She tarried just too long, however, as other vampires attempted to murder her when she tried to give one last goodbye to her family. She leaped from a cliff in her panicked flight...and discovered that she could literally fly. It was the only thing that saved her from her attackers, who could not follow her rapid egress on the winds.

                                        For the next few years, she took refuge in a small town, where no other vampires took residence. She honed her powers of Flight, and further discovered more advanced senses and an ability to charm people. These allowed her to make a comfortable unlife amid scattered rural homes. Unbeknownst to her, her high Humanity allowed her to accidentally evade the notice of Lupines who made their homes nearby. It wouldn't be until later that she discovered, from talks with other vampires, just how precarious living in the backwaters had been. How close she might have come to death.

                                        Indeed, she didn't even know this danger before she decided to leave. She had been a young go-getter - an aspiring people person - in life. Living in isolation, in a place with no night life and no "peers", was an enervating experience. Restlessness overtook her, until she resolved to return to the cities, in order to earn a place in vampire society.

                                        Her transition into larger Kindred society will not be easy, however. Caitiff and ignorant, she'll have to claw her way up just into tolerance, let alone acceptance. Still, as a woman who worked in business, she was not unused to glass ceilings. She cut her teeth on being persuasive, undaunted, and useful. And really, there's plenty utility that can be found in someone who can fly. As far as she's concerned, not even the sky is the limit.

                                                147) Joining that snake cult was meant to be ironic. His circle of friends heard about a cult, and decided to join as a joke. You know, because that's what you do when you're young, bored, and jaded with the world.

                                                Like his friends, he got initiated into this secret order that venerated snakes. When they got back from the temple, they all had a good laugh. So they kept going back. To make sure they weren't thrown out, they studied the material they were supposed to. Poking fun at the hokey texts together. As their membership in the order went on, though, his friends started reading the books on their own. So when he showed up to rites, he found that they all knew the material better than he did. He just figured they were really committed to the bit, so he worked to keep up. The laughs dried up some time after that; everyone was too busy studying and doing rites. But - idiot that he was - he presumed that the joke was still on.

                                                Then the rites and rituals became more...intense. His group's scholarship allowed them to advance to higher ranks of initiation. And the rites began to involve things like ritual blood letting, psychedelic drugs, and animal sacrifices to their snake god - Set. It was all starting to weird him out, but he couldn't say anything. One of his friends was advanced up a rank before anyone else, but disappeared soon afterwards. The order officials wouldn't talk about it, save to say that "not all members have what it takes to handle the higher stages of initiation; pray to Set you are made of sterner stuff". The others seemed only slightly saddened by the friend's disappearance. He started to think maybe his friends didn't see this as a joke anymore.

                                                By this point, he didn't have the strength to pull out. After the next advancement, he realized why his missing friend had disappeared: the greater rites were actively dangerous. Harrowing leaps through over fire pits, walking across adder-strewn walkways, ritual near-drownings. All of his friends were willing to engage. When he privately tried to persuade one of them that maybe they were going too far, his friend - a buddy for years - snapped at him, and told him not to submit to weakness. "The Aeons trap us with fear", he said, "don't let yourself be chained down by them".

                                                That same friend died sometime later, in a rite gone wrong. One by one, they all did.

                                                He was left with his last old buddy at the gates of the final initiation, where the high priest would choose the one to receive the ultimate revelation. By some miracle, the poor sap had managed to make a mark for himself in the cult. It was just him and his last friend. And he'd faked it too well. Somehow, the high priest considered HIM the most worthy. He just wanted to fit in. Just wanted to go along with the joke. And he kept on keeping a straight face - an implacable mask of faux religious fervor - as he was given the "honor" of accepting Set's blood.

                                                Now he's a vampire - a Setite - and his position as a cult scion is a complete sham. His last friend - the only one to last as long as he did - got fed to him to slake his first thirst. Something about a sacrifice for the greater glory of Set.

                                                He's in a new city, expected to start his own cult. In truth, he's just trying to think of a way out of the nightmare his life - his unlife - has become. His initiation into the Followers of Set was a joke, alright. And the joke's on him.

                                                    148) A Camarilla Malkavian whose insanity takes the form of a delusion that they are an undercover space alien. They believe that their "native species" are naturally vampiric (though they don't think of it in such mystical terms, preferring a "scientific" explanation for their blood thirst), and that all "vampires" are humans converted to the alien race. And that the entirety of Kindred society is an elaborate means of infiltrating Earth, so "Homeworld" can exercise subtle control. Hence why vampires spend so much time burrowing their roots into human society. The Malkavian thinks that they only know about this because their particular caste is designed to interface with the alien hive mind. Ergo, Clan Malkavian are the emissaries between the infiltrators and "Command", while all other "Clans" fulfill some other purpose relevant to the infiltration (the Ventrue are obviously those given command, the Nosferatu ferret out secrets, the Brujah are muscle, the Toreador charm the Earthling populace, etc).

                                                    However, this Malkavian decides that the Ventrue have done a poor job leading, since they've allowed "Kindred" to forget their purpose, and become distracted by politics and infighting. Large portions of the vampire population have even gone rogue, and now worship the "First Sleeper Agent" as some kind of god, rather than giving allegiance to their off-world alien masters. This Malkavian therefore takes it upon themself to get "Kindred" society back on track. But since their fellow agents have "deluded themselves" as to their ultimate purpose, they don't take talk of the True Masters in Space well. The Malkavian will have to be more subtle, and spread stability and unity of purpose through indirect means. This "Jyhad" is a waste of their time, but it's a tangle of conflict that must be worked within, since convincing "agents" of its folly is impossible. All the while, the Malkavian works to pursue what they think is the agenda of the alien masters, as told by visions and voices and impulses. Surely there must be some interference, if their connection to the masters is so garbled; nonetheless, the Malkavian must trust that they do their Masters' will.

                                                    When not working, the Malkavian feeds their obsession with all things Alien. They visit UFO conventions and keep up with forums dedicated to sightings and abductions. They watch alien-related films and TV, read every book published on the subject, and has played Space Invaders, Destroy All Humans!, and X-Com hundreds of hours each. Their haven is filled with posters, alien plush dolls, and other extraterrestrial paraphernalia.

They have little hope of breaking into government bases or cracking into government mainframes looking for what they know, but the Malkavian does pay attention to those informal and underground attempts to obtain and understand alien tech. They are currently investigating leads into the Thal'hun and the Star Council.
