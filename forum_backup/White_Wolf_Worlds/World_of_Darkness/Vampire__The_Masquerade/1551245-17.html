                                                            Why this pack/loner is here? Why they risked their lives that way? How they got here in the first place? What are the long term intentions of whomever directed them? How they survive the trip if they're too dumb to foresee their own demise?

                                                            Not all packs are smart, sure. But most should be, and the dumber ones still have someone pulling the strings. They're Lost Boys style, but they're still Vampires, immortal monsters with cursed powers and usually an investment from their sires, even if less than the Camarilla.

                                                            And why wouldn't the Camarilla worry all the same? The incursion may mean nothing from time to time, but the Camarilla doesn't know. A Prince that pays no heed to such occurrence is risking showing the Sabbat that this city is easy to take.

                                                            So, an incursion occurred. Is reconnaissance? A message? The prelude for further problems? A test that yielded results?
