    ST Vault idea research

    07-24-2022, 11:15 AM
    Hi all!
    I am considering the idea of creating a supplement for the Storytellers' Vault.
    Before starting the hard work, I have decided to do some market research to see if people would be interested in the supplement.
    The idea I am working with is a V20 supplement set during the Russian Revolution and Civil War. I would deal with the birth of the Brujah Council, it would show different Anarch societies that would not survive the civil war and the Council repression and, of course, it would explore an area of the WoD that has barely (and badly) been described so far.
    I am planning to include different types of campaigns, from the political game in Petrograd in the years previous to the revolution to the anarchy and violence of the civil war, with several in-betweens.
    So, what do you guys think about this? Would it sound interesting? Is there anything that you would include, any advice that you would give me? Thank you very much

                  I am fully aware. It even touches my sensitive political buttons, as my political ideas would have placed me in the side of those betrayed by the bolsheviks. So I am planning to show the civil war as a struggle between four axis (red Vs white, but also freedom Vs authoritarianism in both sides) and to show how war and violence paves the way for authoritarians to win (an issue that is still relevant nowadays)

                                          It's a tricky one. One of the pitfalls, of course, is the old 'vampires are behind everything' problem of older material. It seems you're aware of that.

                                          You'd need to strike the right balance of creating a historical backdrop with the appropriate context, and having Cainites respond to that, without also minimising or trivialising the things that actually happened. But a big part of that is tone, so if you can avoid the salacious (looking at you, V5 Camarilla) or the exoticising (and you, WoD: Ethnic Slur), you'll already be ahead of the worst WoD books.

                                          All of that said, I would definitely buy this book. It sounds really interesting. I'm also generally interested in alternate takes on core rules stuff, too, even if I can't immediately use setting stuff. (I think many of us yearn for V20 stuff with better rules, and historical settings are one of the loopholes for offering a revised core system.)

                                          One more (potentially) contentious question: does your Russia have the Wan Kuei in it? I can see arguments for and against, but given the diversity of modern and historical Russia, and it's huge size, I could definitely see Wan Kuei in parts of it.

                                          As V20 hasn't officially delved into the Hungry Dead, that gives you a bit of a clean slate on the matter (though Hsienfan has an excellent unofficial update on the subject). Maybe it's just something for an appendix or sidebar, but it could be neat, as I don't think KotE ever really dealt with Russian Hungry Dead in much detail.
