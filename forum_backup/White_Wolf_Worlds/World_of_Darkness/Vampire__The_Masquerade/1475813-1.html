    A thin blood alchemy book makes sense. V5 is weirdly focussed on them as a player option (occasionally to its detriment) but as presented alchemy stinks so this is going to be very useful for their 'vision' moving forward.

    It'd be nice to get some more blood sorcery powers which establish more meaningful distinctions between tremere and assamite blood magic. It would also be good to implement some powers that feel more like magic as opposed to corebook were its presented as just a vitae manipulation power line up with some rituals stapled on.

    It's a very very good thing that they're releasing a players guide with the other clans. Needing 4 supplementary books to play 20+ year old player options was outright embarissing for the company. Especially when compared to revised or v20.

                        I think Vampire magic should have some general rule for it with interchangeable catalysts for distinct groups/styles. That said, I really don't mind the theme of blood for the Tremere, as it plays well on their specific history: alchemists (not only, but greatly) that became vampires by the power of a blood-based concoction and lost their previous magic in the process, now examining the power of the Blood as a substitute.

                        But while I think versatility in execution, at least from an OoC perspective, would work nice, mechanically I don't miss old sorcery either.

                        On the matter that dots sell books, if they want to keep churning books at a steady pace that'll be entirely true, and magic is always a good vector to do this. Heck, even Mage managed to do this with a core system that made every new spell redundant by definition.
