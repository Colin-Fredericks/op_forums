                                Outside of perhaps Al Amut, the Tremere's Vienna Chantry was probably the most well protected fortress in the entire World of Darkness.                                 I could see if the US military did something like dropping a Massive Ordnance Penetrator (Bunker Buster) on the place and basically destroying it (of course an explosion that large would also inflict significant damage to the city of Vienna, kill a ton of other people and there's no way that could just be swept under the rug, and of course they'd probably need the assistance of another supernatural being to help them pinpoint the chantry in the first place), or if there was heavy outside assistance from the Technocracy (Hitmarks and such) but as described with SWAT teams doing room sweeps and whatnot, it just doesn't feel legit and projects a feeling of, "There's no verisimilitude in the World of Darkness because the authors can and will do whatever they want even when it doesn't make a whole lot of sense from within the setting."
