                                In terms of what sells on the Storyteller's Vault, a rough hierarchy of what is successful would run something like the following list:
                                1. Core rulebooks (noting that you are not supposed to create an entirely standalone rulebook).
                                2. Books about powers, and other material which can be easily imported into any chronicle.
                                3. Generalised sourcebooks with a primary focus on supernatural society.
                                4. Generalised sourcebooks with a primary focus upon the mortal world.
                                5. Location sourcebooks.
                                6. Adventures.
                                7. Gaming aids and other similar paraphernalia.
                                That's a fairly generalised list, though; how successful a book will be is also influenced by a number of other factors. These include:
                                • Choice of game line; a product written for Vampire will normally outsell a product written for Wraith.
                                • Demand for the subject matter; there is clearly a lot of demand for a good Sabbat sourcebook.
                                • Accessibility of subject matter; a location sourcebook on Britain will sell a lot better than a location sourcebook about Syria.
                                • Price; consensus is that a price of $1 for every ten pages is the general market rate right now.
                                • Quality of product; get a decent preview up online.
                                • Reputation; an author how has a reputation for delivering quality may sell more books than a new author.

                                So, there are a lot of factors at play. Focus on the right product and market and you can sell a large number of books, regardless of quality (though you may not get terribly positive reviews); conversely, you could write the best adventure for Demon: the Fallen and you would struggle to sell many copies at all.
