      Well this is obviously an incredibly topical issue and one that I think naturally needs to be handled with delicacy and care, both because of the deal and present human context and because of our proximity to it. While tables can do whatever they’re comfortable with, we have to be considerate of these factors when having broader and more public discussions.

      That said, vampires will do as they always do. There will be a certain level of street level vampires who have the same street level concerns as any person but with a radically different set of needs. On a larger tier vampires are parasitic and cancerous, drawing blood and resources from whatever host they find themselves within. Just like anyone else, they’re going to be recalibrating to rapidly adjusting circumstances and trying to find the safest and most profitable way forward.

                                                      Afghanistan's actual history as a fractious, chaotic shitfest of geographically isolated and mutually hostile tribes and families overthrowing each other every few years deserves respect, and lends itself to a fictional history where humanity there has proven immune to foreign colonization through sheer bullheadedness. It would be entirely appropriate if some of the region's incredibly temporary kings and presidents had been under the influence of this supernatural creature or that, but the thing is that none of them ever last because here comes somebody's cousin and his posse over the hill to stage yet another coup.

                                                      If we're looking for a clan that would tolerate a barren mountain country where tribes come and go and it doesn't matter as long as the land remains theirs, we're maybe talking Tzimisce. Maybe.
