                    Their military bent doesn't seem that out ot place: in the old Ars Magica book, the Tremere example character was a swordsman, after all, and Tremere were the undisputed master of wizard duelling. So, harking back at those mythical times seems appropriate for a militant group.

                    To me, they don't seem nutters at all, just a radical group engaged in covert war with other Tremere factions. Hardly strange, in a fractured society with strong tensions between competing factions: leaders may sit at discussiont tables, but on the ground some clashes are bound to happen.
