                                        The thing with Meths isn't that they're supposed to show up and duke it out with you and totally wreck your shit. They're supposed to wreck you whilst doing their absolute best not to expose themselves and wreck your shit.

                                        They don't dominate you to kill yourself (namely because that's a stupid idea and only serves as showing off) but they get elders to send neonates after you. They in turn Dominate your friends and family... all the while the Meth stays at a safe distance. The exception to this is the Niktuku, who stay completely hidden but still come to kill you personally. You're supposed to fight a Methuselah with your brains. You hunt them down through the clues they leave or you draw them out...
                                        The thing with V5 is that you can't really support these hidden methuselah. They can't really work through elders because they're only slightly stronger than elders and would likely be attacked by such elders. The Dominate and Auspex powers that allowed for 4d methuselah chess to be played are gone, and the physicals of the game have changed to the point that, though Celerity and Fortitude 5 might be really good for one-on-one fights, Potence is pretty useless if you can't land a hit, and numerous enemies will destroy you. Oh and the Methuselah's have got all sorts of logistical problems like Vitae-only feeding and extra hunger dice...

                                            the sabbat used was defined by their enemies,
                                            the evil zealous extremism we know them for wasn't a choice but a necessity, a direct result of the unsurmountable adversity they faced,
                                            one that pushed them further and futher into the dark side after each challenge

                                            the methuselah were a huge part of this adversity, along with lupines and whatever else

                                            alot of the dilemmas you face as a sabbat and the compromises that follow rely on elders and methuselahs being a real threat,

                                            - you could say you don't want the vinculum, that being tied to so many others is too much, but no vinculum means you're open to being blood bound and alone against your enemies

                                            - you can say that you don't want to take on a path of enlightenment, but humanity is too restrictive when faced with your enemies

                                            - you can say that you hate diablerie, that you wouldn't lower yourself to blood magic or infernalism, but what choice do you have when faced with all powerful methuselahs?


                                            now all those dilemmas are gone, the Anarchs or the SI are more than enough to challenge the ancients,
                                            the sabbat is an unnecessary and unjustified compromise,
                                            edgelords for the sake of being edgelords...if that's the angle the devs are going for, then the sabbat are the true antagonists, not the ancients
