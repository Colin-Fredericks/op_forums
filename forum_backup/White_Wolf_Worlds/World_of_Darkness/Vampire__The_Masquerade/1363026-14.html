    And FINISHED on my second read through.

    Thirteen Thoughts on Let the Streets Run Red



    1. Ramrod sacrificing himself to save Tyrus is one of the most genuinely touching things I've seen in V5. The fact that he was willing to do it despite the fact he's a Humanity 3 monster made it all the more poignant. I think all the evil in V5 and grittiness made it even more powerful.

    2. This book has a really surprising collection of likable characters and quite a few of them are geeks too. I wonder who plays Revenant: The Ravishing LARPS in Gary or Chicago as real-life vampires.

    3. Walter Nash is a tremendous character but I kind of feel a bit disappointed we didn't find out he tried to claim Praxis of Chicago for like a month in 1992 or so. Maybe Lodin made him Acting Prince while he visited London once or twice because he knew no one would accept him long term. Still, I love the FIRE DEPARTMENT is his secret weapon.

    4. The Night's Cross inclusion makes me want them to have a full write-up for V5. I liked them in The Chicago Folio and it'd be cool if they became a full-sized organization acting in the American Midwest.

    5. I have a tiny regret we didn't see the Seven Fires mentioned or used.

    6. Pretty good LGBT representation. Honor is at least bisexual given her feeding habits and her friend i suspect is fully lesbian. Eletria is bisexual and I think there's implications for Allicia to also be the 1920s equivalent of bi. Warrington is a gay man of color too.

    7. Keisha is a fascinating Setite and in another universe, I suspect she'd have been drawn to the Celestial Choir or Cult of Ectasy.

    8. I did not expect Milwaukee to make a good case for itself as a police state. As nasty a person as Decker is, he's probably got the safest city for HUMANS as you can get. High Humanity
    vampires may actually support the dictatorship.

    9. Parovich is a wight. Couldn't have happened to a nicer asshole. The Sabbat attack also seems like it happened fairly recently. I mean, wights are pretty hard to cover up.

    10. Eletria's blood could probably heal Lodin and his mind. Interesting possibility. I also note she's powerful enough to kill Helena on her own if events happened.

    11. I feel like the endings to Power Play and Rusted Jungle could use more explosions but that's just me.

    12. SULLIVAN DANE IS ALIVE! Woot!

    13. Love Modius and Juggler's updates. I think Evelyn should remain Baron of Gary, though.

    Author of Cthulhu Armageddon, I was a Teenage Weredeer, Straight Outta Fangton, Lucifer's Star, and the Supervillainy Saga.
