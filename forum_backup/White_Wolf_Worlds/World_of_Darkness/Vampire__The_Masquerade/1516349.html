    (V5) Harbingers...not entirely sure on the lore.

    06-27-2023, 12:44 PM
    I don't know if this is addressed in Cults of the Blood Gods or not (and I don't have access to the book yet to find out for myself), but I'm confused and trying to reconcile some things for myself regarding the Harbingers.

    Joined the Hecata, the ones who claimed to join the Sabbat ditched them like a bad habit, replaced their nasty-ass flaw for the painful kiss, still chasing their blood vendetta but keeping it on the DL for now, not subject to the Beckoning. Loresheet confirms some percentage of them actually did devolve to wraiths, and "procured" shiny new vampire bodies to terrorize the Skinlands once again. Got all that.

    The v5 content I've seen seems to have conveniently left out they're all methuselahs, and they don't embrace. So, my question: is "recruitment" open, or are they still in the process of reincorporation? 6th-8th generation fledglings running around hardly seems prudent and would pose more of a vulnerability than anything, and contrary to the themes and general depowering 5th edition puts on the forefront. Are they ganking other clans' or bloodlines' neonates, burning out those pesky souls, and shoving wraiths into the bodies for "childer" of appropriate generation?

    If an Hecata of another bloodline pulled that stunt, would the result be eligible for the Harbinger loresheet? Would that be a stunt Harbingers would endorse, or would they drop the hammer for doing it? Would this not be the single nastiest sect infiltration tactic this side of Vicissitude and Dominate?

                  For now there's just a lot speculation but more or less that's what I'm speculating. I speculated on this a bit in the Lazarus thread I made earlier this year. I don't think that the Harbringers are capable of siring children. They're dead-dead in a way that normal vampires aren't. They get the bodies of other vampires and shove the wraith-soul of one of the Cappadocians that was trapped in Kaymakli into the body, allowing it to animate and control it. Mechanically they're mostly vampires, but a little different.

                  Taking the speculation a step further, these wraith-souls need to possess the body of someone with the same bloodline that they had in life. So the Harbingers, as Cappadocians, need to possess the bodies of vampires with Cappadocian blood - the Giovanni. The Harbringers never really had a feud, per se, with the Giovanni, they just needed Giovanni bodies to possess. With the Family Reunion, there's now a whole lot Cappadocian-descended vampires all hanging out together, which makes it really easy for the Harbringers to gain access to new bodies to help their wraithly brethren that are still trapped in the Underworld. It also seems very likely that Lazarus knows exactly what the Harbringers are doing and is supporting them.

                  In prior editions, Lazarus was noted as attempting to recreate the Spell of Life (the magical spell that Osiris used to create the Mummies). Lazarus spent time with the Followers of Set in an attempt to unravel this spell. It's possible that the creation of Harbringers (wraiths possessing and becoming vampires) could possibly be a step in that process as a Harbringer is something that is a little bit close to a Mummy in some respects.

                  What this means is that at some point the Hecata are likely to implode when the other factions realize that the Harbringers are hunting them down, likely with the assistance of Lazarus. At that point you'll probably see a war within the Hecata between the Harbringers and everyone else.
