                                    Some various suggestions for fluff:

                                    * Miami, San Salvador, Cape Town, Caracas, and Juarez/El Paso are all Sabbat cities. Miami should be the capital of the American Sabbat but each of the Sabbat regions should have their own local leader.

                                    * The Drowned have taken huge chunks of South America and Central America but not all of it as it turned out plenty of local vampires prefer the Sabbat to centuries old pagan ones.

                                    * The Sabbat have a large presence in Istanbul but are behaving because it's an Infernalist/Nephilim city. Mary the Black is the "High Priestess" of the city.

                                    * The Sabbat are actually mostly in a state of truce with the Ashirra as they have bigger issues than the Muslim vampires.

                                    * The Tzimisce should have lost enough Elders to New York City, Blood Cults, Anarchs, and the Camarilla so that they're no longer a majority Clan either. As such, the Sabbat is now finally egalitarian in its treatment of Antitribu.

                                    * Vykos is the leader of the independent Tzimisce. They might return to become Regent of the Sabbat but are on a bit of a walkabout given their centuries of being under control of the Dracon and the knowledge the Eldest is Shub-Niggurath.

                                    * Drop the Antitribu line and they just identify by Clan now.

                                    * The Serpents of Light had a huge number of defections to the Ministry.

                                    * The Loyalists were weakened by many joining the Anarchs. Many Loyalists are unironically of the mind they're going to mold the Anarchs into a New Sabbat.

                                    * Polonia is Regent and he's operating out of Dubai where he has the Sabbat on their best behavior with the locals.

                                    * Lucita was regent for about a week and was overthrown by forces unknown. It was probably Marcus Vitel using Talley. She's missing presumed dead.

                                    * The Sabbat have killed hundreds of Elders and a dozen or more Methuselahs in the Gehenna War. As nasty as the casualties are, they've done more damage to Elders and their masters than they have in the previous 600 years.

                                    * The Seven Fires are a breakaway Sabbat sect that are the purely evil mysterious terrorist Sabbat that have lost their minds. The Sabbat consider them traitors and extremists even by their standards but they're supported by others in the sect as well as "not my problem."

                                    * The Revenant Families have either defected to join other powers, been Embraced, or have started passing themselves off as Thin Bloods.
