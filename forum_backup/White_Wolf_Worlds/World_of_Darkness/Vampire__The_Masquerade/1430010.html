    Arikel

    12-26-2020, 08:52 PM
    I did say in the thread about the Tremere and the beckoning that I was going to do a thread on Arikel/Ishtar, and what this Antediluvian might be up to. After reading the Encyclopedia Vampirica, it seemed to me that Arikel might be following a strategy in the Jyhad totally different from the other Antediluvians, and hiding in plain sight. Full frontal, even.

    What we know of Arikel is scant. The first born and oldest of the Antediluvians, the Prodigals state that Arikel and Troile together invented the Humanity moral system. And I assume even the Antediluvians are not past the need to keep their beasts on a leash.

    We also know that Arikel is conflict-shy. When Ennoia sent her spawn after Arikel, the Toreador Antediluvian fled the Gangrel neonate. When Ennoia embraced Gilgamesh she fled again. This seems peculiar, although she may have been avoiding Ennoia herself and assumed that the spawn were stalking horses. Or Arikel may have tried to avoid collateral damage, if Humanity was actually important.

    Arikel vanishes from the world of the kindred after embracing Iontius, a very peculiar member of the Fourth Generation. His embrace date seems to be unknown, although it is after the Greeks became a people. He has painted the Dracon, so he is at least predates the final death of that Kindred. Iontius behaves differently from other methuselahs. He is active, he does not seem to worry in the least about methuselahs of the fifth generation seeking his potent vitae. He does not care to sit on a throne in the lands of the dead, stand in the desert and look up at the stars while smiling a cold and alien smile, sacrifice villages of innocents to dark gods, or any of the other hobbies members of his generation enjoy. Iontius goes to parties, falls in love and has his heart broken. In which he is very much like his sire who fell in love with mortal men.

    Iontius has also broken the curse of God.

    He has freed himself of the need to drink blood. He is capable of feeding on the breath of mortals when having sex with them. One would think this would be huge news, especially as kindred of his age and generation face tighter and tighter blood supply restrictions as Methuselahs Thirst overtakes them, and they spend more and more time in torpor. While Iontius just gets laid.

    In fact, even the Antediluvians cannot escape this curse. As the "Time of Thin Blood" states out of character, in the "This is what really happened in the WOD "section:

    As vampires age, they must feed on increasingly potent blood. First the blood of animals no longer sustain them. After several centuries, only the vitae of other vampires can satisfy their thirst. The true Antediluvians are more than ten millennia old. They passed the "Methuselahs Thirst" stage long ago. Did the Antediluvian utterly destroy its childer and grandchilder...
    Well, Iontius just gets laid.

    Curiously, this is well known, but no one seems to care or feel that it is important. Even kindred who suffer from Methuselahs Thirst seems to feel that this is unimportant.

    A well known theory, even printed in his biography, claims that there never was an Iontius. It is just an alias for Arikel. (Presumably to stop having to dodge Gangrel every so often). Strangely, no one seems to feel that this is particularly important. And the two does seem very similar in terms of personality, from what little we know.

    Currently, Iontius has taken the name Derek Zeel, and works as a porn star. Unusually, the Camarilla who tends to take a dim view of Kindred with high media profiles, can't seem to make itself care. Iontius has been doing this for a while (since before the first Toreador Clanbook) and the Camarilla has not been able to force itself to feel any particular concern. Now porn star seems a peculiar job for a refined kindred artist -but there are Presence powers that let a Kindred extend its abilities through media. And Iontius can feed on sexual release.

    Internet porn is very popular and reachable by a very large slice of earths over seven billion kine. And while there are a lot of small fish out there. the really big suppliers are surprisingly few. I think the really huge, monstrous kindred blood-orgies of Knossos and Carthage, Shaitans sacrifices seem to have involves thousands of people. Now if Iontius truly is Arikel and can feed through media, then while the other Antediluvians hides in the shadows and feed laboriously on the Fourth and Fifth generation... by my calculations Arikel could be taking in something north of a billion -with a b- blood point equivalents per year. VEUs as Dr. Netchurch called them.

    That makes the greatest amounts of blood any kindred has wielded look like a thimble besides the ocean. I have no notion what would require that.

                      Going back to your original point, I do agree that Arikel has acted very differently from other antedeluvians in terms of the Jyhad.

                      From some of her names and activities in the ancient world, she seemed to be going for the archtypical "Great Goddess" trope (she possibly invented it in the WoD). This implies may have had some kind of public persona in order to cultivate worship of her. This is something many of the antedeluvians did, but she seemed to push it harder and longer than others. And in the cosmology and metaphysics of the WoD, this is something that can eventually become a means of transforming you into some kind of deity. That's a stretch, but it is consistent with known facts about her. In the meantime, it also gives you control of entire cities, empires, armies, etc. So she likely had a lot of temporal power at certain points in her existence. (It also makes you a prominent target which is likely why she disappeared at some point, and she has the reputation of running from fights.)

                      If we assume that's her strategy, then how could she pull it off modern day? She wouldn't be able to be worshipped directly (though who knows what is conceivably possible once Gehenna begins and the world as we know it breaks down), but there may be different things she'd be able to do - infiltrate various neopagan religious groups, undermine established religions she couldn't corrupt, maybe masquerade as a vision of the Virgin Mary, build on celebrity culture and lifestyle brand companies to get people (especially women) to emulate her (jewelry, perfume, luxury goods, beauty and wellness) either directly or through controlled proxies. That can be expanded upon (including... porn).

                      So it is one option though definitely not the only one.

                                                      It is important to consider the source here I think.

                                                      While Iontius has been around since the 1st ed Clanbook, as far as I can determine, the idea that there never was an Iontius and that it is an alias for Arikel originated in the Encyclopedia Vampirica by his fellow authors. They include Bindusara, a sixth generation methuselah who has seen 23 centuries worth of nights and been a contemporary of several active Antediluvians, Aristotle de Laurent who complied the modern version of the Book of Nod and Albertus Magnus said to be one of the wisest sages of the Cainite race. These are some of the premier noddists among the Kindred, and the last two have known Iontius since the renaissance, and may be the people who know him best. Those are the Kindred who felt that this may be rather more than a "normal" ancient member of the fourth generation.

                                                      It should also be said that Iontius feeding method is unique in another way besides just breaking the command of God. As far as can be determined the Kine suffers no ill effects from it. Rather the opposite. Iontius has gone so far towards humanity that he is no longer parasitic, but more symbiotic. Whatever else might be said, that is very like what we might expect from Arikel.

                                                      Finally, Iontius illustrated the Encyclopedia Vampirica, it says, under considerable time pressure and from memory. The kindred he chose to sketch include Baba Yaga, Caine (several pictures), Ur-Shulgi, Malkav, Shaitan as he looked before Caine struck him down, and the Aralu Loz who is "assumed to be another name for Toreador". As well as a large number of other kindred, but those are the ones who stand out in my mind as somewhat peculiar people to be able to sketch portraits of from memory.

                                                      The only one of the authors he did not include a picture of on the biography page is himself. But his description does fit the picture of Loz.
