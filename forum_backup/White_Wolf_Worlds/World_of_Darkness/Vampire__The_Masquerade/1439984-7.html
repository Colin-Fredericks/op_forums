                        Originally posted by Penelope View Post
                        CTPhipps do you have anything you want to add? Or have you already said everything you need to on this subject?
                        Generally, my perspective on the subject is influenced by my opinion as a genre writer and the fact that I actually almost had my Supervillainy Saga books picked up by Michael Bay's company (we had a few phone calls so, "almost picked up" is perhaps stretching it). My books being a parody of the MCU and superheroes in general so representing the sort of "lower tier" intellectual property that can get bought up and potentially made much bigger.

                        For me, people are absolutely correct that Vampire: The Masquerade is not something that is 100% necessary for anyone going to make a vampire show as plenty of modern vampires are influenced by it (and its derivatives) already. It also started as a toolkit to be able to gather all forms of vampire media together as well. So much so when Underworld WAS sued over it, the reaction even from fellow geeks was not complimentary.



                        Hell, my own writing in urban fantasy owes itself to White Wolf giving me a bunch of ideas and I drew from the Sookie Stackhouse Mysteries, Laurel K. Hamilton, and other worlds similar to White Wolf. That isn't actually a BAD thing for any future adaptations, though. Because the thing about this is creating a market for the genre is a good thing.

                        Forever Knight
                        Teen Wolf
                        Moonlight
                        Buffy the Vampire Slayer/Angel
                        The Vampire Diaries/The Originals
                        Supernatural (which was going to have a White Wolf-based "warring Lupines and Werewolves in Chicago" spin off, I shit you not)

                        All of these things are pretty similar to what a White Wolf show would be like but they don't make it HARDER to do a spin off, quite the contrary, they give any potential makers of a WOD show an in to pitch the show. I'm pretty sure that the process will consist of White Wolf assembling their own pitch and licensing it to a smaller company that is looking to break big but that's not a bad thing. I also suspect that it will probably be more likely to be done by fans of the video game than the other.

                        But I think the odds are quite good, IMHO, that they're laying the groundwork for it now. Still, I wouldn't be surprised if the V:TM show is created first in Europe and then adapted in America later.

                        Edit:

                        Some Additional Thoughts

                        I'm actually of the mind we already HAVE the V:TM TV show in LA By Night. It's an unconventional show consisting of freeform roleplaying but the first episode on Youtube (which is after its successful run on Twitch) has a million views. It's also a four hour long show and that's pretty fucking impressive.
                        It's also an adaptation of a tabletop game anyway so watching people roleplaying Geek and Sundry style doesn't mean it's NOT a TV show that's adapted from a video game. It certainly benefited the brand and is probably responsible for 50% of the positive name recognition of V5 after a rocky start.

                        I personally would also always adapt V:TM first because of not only the name recognition but simply because I do think of it as the "best" game. Still, I think it'll be creator driven so if someone wanted to do Wraith: The Oblivion the TV show first because the guy likes that game more than he'll do Wraith: The Oblivion more.

                                                        Originally posted by Chris24601 View Post
                                                        First, blood and wounds there from are special effects.

                                                        Also, parasites taking advantage of and feeding on hapless mortals is NOT cool or a good look in the present days.

                                                        You don’t want protagonists with an end goal of “become a blood instead of sex version of Weinstein, Epstein or [insert crony capitalist/government dirt bag here] only get away with it” as the end goal for your protagonist these days.

                                                        Even the CW couldn’t sell that these days... which is why The Originals (where the vampires were pretty much monsters with protagonist-centered morality to justify it) gave way to Legacies where the protagonists protect ordinary humans from monsters and vampires even feeding from humans is portrayed as morally degenerate.

                                                        In the 90’s after the Soviet Union collapsed, America kicked Iraq’s ass, the giant sucking sound of corporate greed hadn’t started hollowing out what’s now the Rust Belt, and the message that endless peacekeeping wars were a good thing was still fresh enough to be plausible; Gen-Xers had the luxury of sympathizing with vampires forced to feed from the innocent to survive.

                                                        These days people don’t sympathize with the crony capitalists, the short-selling vulture investors who steal people’s jobs just to pad portfolios already stuffed with more than they can spend in a lifetime, with elites who victimize women and children for their twisted pleasures. The government and law enforcement who abuse their power and target helpless citizens because they believe they won’t be held accountable. They don’t care about how angsty they might be over their heinous acts.

                                                        No, they want those assholes burned to the ground and vampires are charactures of every last one of the dirtbags people are pretty much done with (to paraphrase a discussion about Legacies vs. Vampire Dairies that demonstrates the spirit of the age; if Damon started one of his pity murder sprees that was a staple of TVD to show how tormented and miserable he was on Legacies, the audience wouldn’t be expected to sympathize, they’d be cheering after Hope beheaded him before the fourth message break of the episode).

                                                        VtM as written is toxic in the current entertainment environment. If your vampires aren’t of the friendly neighborhood variety right now they’re only going to be usable as villains for actual protagonists to slay. Seriously, try finding something featuring vampires in the last decade or so that hasn’t either portrayed them as monsters to be destroyed (ex. 28 Days of Night, Van Helsing) or softened them down to friendly neighborhood levels (ex. Vampire Academy, Legacies, Twilight). Even the proposed Disney+ Blade series is about a vampire HUNTER with vampires as villains.

                                                        Short version; vampires aren’t cool protagonists these days outside of the skewed demographics of a forum about a game where you play vampires (where comments are mostly limited to the same half-dozen or so people again and again).

                                                        If you want a WoD shared universe to get off the ground in the present day you’re going to need GENUINE protagonists to launch with, not villain protagonists like VtMs vampires. Hunters (of the HH variety) and Mages are the lowest production budget options for those with some wraiths and werewolves as runners up if you had a bigger budget.
                                                        There are ways to get around vampires being predators of the innocent. One path being you can make your protags concentualists, feeding from blood donated to them from volunteers. Another is the Spike route, only feed from animals blood. A third is for your protags to be villans for villans, feeding off scumbags or even other vampires. The last method is to rely on someone who has the supernatural ability to produce cruelty free blood, any catholic preist with enough true faith can preform Transubstantiation for Vampires who agree to walk a more virtuous path.
