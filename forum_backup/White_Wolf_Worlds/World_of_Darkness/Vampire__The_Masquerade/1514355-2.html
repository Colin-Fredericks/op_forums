                    OK? But let's look at the relatively similarly lore-agnostic VtR 1e and just how many books that covered more niche topics there really were to deal with (yes some of these are partially covered in some V5 supplements, but none in detail despite older books dedicated to these topics):

                    Expansion on how to run coteries, different types, what happens as character goals drift over time, etc.
                    How do Kindred that decide to travel extensively manage (and this is definitely still a thing in V5)
                    Ghouls (though probably also any expanded Thin-Blood stuff as they ate up a lot of narrative space of things like Revenants).
                    City building and how to run vampires controlling mortal institutions
                    Stranger antagonists than other vampires and hunters
                    Social influence as a tool of control
                    How to handle scaling up the stakes of the game beyond local matters
                    Guide to homebrewing Discipline powers, Amlgams, Rituals, etic.

                    All of those fall into the "tool-kit, not world building," kind of things. And I'm pretty sure most of those are more useful topics to get to before we get into more details on vampire sex.
