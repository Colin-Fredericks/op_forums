    05-13-2020, 02:31 AM
    So in the WoD I see three different theories:
    1. ALL VAMPIRES are infernalists, which mainly comes from the Werewolf I think. Garou think all vampires are minions of Wyrm except few who has Humanity 7+ or follows Path of Beast and so on. So if the first tip is right, why do infernalists have infernalist hunters? Why can infernalists be corrupted? Why are Baali considered infernalists by other infernalists?

                  Speaking wholly from the perspective of Vampire the Masquerade and assuming that, while other gamelines may exist as they stand, the vampires of VtM take priority in the nature and themes of the setting.

                  Also, 'Infernalist' is a term that needs some definition. For this post I'm going to refer to 'Infernalist' as a person or group who, knowingly or unknowingly, works with/for/or on the retainer of demons, devils, and like creatures of darkness. Individuals or groups that are themselves infernal by nature, I shall refer to as 'Diabolic'. Anyway, moving on:

                  1. The notion that all vampires are servants of The Wyrm comes from Werewolf. While an argument could be made that if the Wyrm does exist, vampires unconsciously serve its goals by their nature of existence. However vampires do not, generally, serve the Wyrm or the Devil, consciously. Vampires, if they are biblical in origin, are certainly dark, likely as the result of Caine's curse and forbiddance from G-d's light. So they could arguably be called diabolic (The Holy Inquisition certainly thinks so), but they are not, from VtM's setting perspective infernalists. Some are, but not all.

                  2. Calling the Sabbat 'infernalists' is actually rather tricky. They are certainly malicious and if any vampires are diabolic they would be and many on paths of enlightenment have become fundamentally inhuman which is something their philosophy celebrates. However, despite their Black Mass-style lexicon, they aren't actually infernalist. In fact, they're more honestly Abrahamic (particularly Christian) heretics. Their foundation mythology is predicated upon placing Caine in a position of near divinity, related to the Cainite Heresy, with his grand-childer as wicked blood gods. The Sabbat is a theocratic organization which makes the Camarilla propaganda of them being 'infernalists' go down easier, but they generally aren't. Yes they've been known to work with Pentex, Spiral Dancers, Nephandi, etc.; but that's largely due to common moral ground and a uniting cause against relatively moral factions. The Sabbat are very dedicated to their own bizarre form of faith, so much so that they even have their own Inquisition, an elite organization created to hunt down and destroy infernalists within their ranks. Heck, the Lasombra were infamous for their corruption and ties with the Catholic Church. The Sabbat are definitely malicious, evil by human standards, and possibly diabolic; but they aren't infernalists.

                  3. Baali are infernalists. They come in different flavors and styles, but yes, they are infernalists. Some make deals with demons for power, others intentionally corrupt souls, others sacrifice to feed hungry gods, and some commit atrocities to appease The Children Who Slumber Below. While their might be renegades, by and large, the baali enter into pacts with infernal or otherwise dark beings, and as such are definitely infernalists. One can also see evidence of this from their signature discipline (Daimonion), their gift for Dark Thaumaturgy, and their natural adversion to ALL religious iconography.

                  Many kindred do hunt infernalists, if not actively then when one appears, because most kindred aren't willing to sacrifice their humanity and subscribe to the philosophy that they are fully and irrevocably damned. Every sect, except perhaps atheist Anarchs or particularly apathetic autarkis, will hunt down infernalists. Not only because infernalists are dangerous and bring chaos, but because they refuse to tolerate them.

                  From the days of the Baali Wars of the First and Second City, kindred have hunted infernalists. Brujah and Assamites to this night still frenzy at the allegation that their founders and ancestors lay with infernalists in Carthage. Kindred have destroyed infernalists within their midst for millennia both because of faith and pragmatism.

                  Infernalists are just bad to have around.

                  Sure, vampires can become corrupted. They can theoretically become fomori (its tricky) or otherwise be possessed. But such things are considered anathema by vampire society. Some work with dark powers, but they are outcasts and pariahs. Baali and their ilk are hunted with extreme prejudice when they appear. Few wish to keep their company.

                  While vampires may be unholy and diabolic, many of them desperately cling to humanity. Noddists even claim that the Archangel Gabriel promised them chances for redemption if they sought them out. Some do worship dark deities, from Bahari to Settites, but even they hunt true infernalists on principle. Perhaps even moreso then then the nominally secular Camarilla.

                  Vampires may be cursed and lost as damned, but they don't want to loose themselves to that darkness. That's a large part of what makes the kindred so tragic.

                  So, ultimately, while they may be diabolic, many kindred are not infernalists.

                  Although calling someone an infernalist can be a great way to get them destroyed...

This is notably why the Sabbat destroyed the Cainite Heresy because the Cainite Heresy very much has the belief that Kindred are beloved of God rather than its enemies.                           The Path of Cathar is like a literal inversion of the Cainite Heresy, reveling in the Demiurge's evil.

                          HOWEVER, the Sabbat has no common cause with demons because despite their common enemy, demons are very much about destroying reality and also enslaving mere mortals as inferiors. The Sabbat run the tricky path of claiming that God is their enemy, they have no limits on their morality, and oh, by the way, don't mess with demons. Most Sabbat who actually meet demons and believe they are really real will have little objection to dealing with them as morality does little to hold them back. Indeed, Koldunic Sorcery is [from a Garou perspective] Wyrm-based nature magic that was taught to them by Kupala. It's ENTIRELY infernal and so are many Sabbat ritae but the Sabbat PRETEND it isn't.

                          The Lasombra also are in contact with many creatures in the Abyss that is, it turns out, where the demons banished to Hell were all sent.

                          So the Sabbat are in denial.

                          The denial also spreads in the fact that they are heavy investors in Pentex and have alliances with the Black Spiral Dancers but are completely ignorant about the nature of their allies. Much like the Syndicate of the Technocracy, they deal with Pentex because Pentex is maybe the largest economic organization in the world outside of China and the United States. The Sabbat don't understand the BSD religion and don't have any interest in doing so since its apocalypse is different from their apocalypse. They also would be very surprised to find out that the corporation they work with wants to bring about the End of the WorldTM (and Pentex doesn't actually think it will--it believes wrongly its fighting for world domination not destruction).

                          Zettler is a high-ranking Sabbat Pricius but as far as the Sabbat know, the secret infernalist is just the guy who writes the billion dollar checks that keep the sect going. Any Grimaldi or Lasombra working for Pentex are probably on the Path of Evil Revelations but still working for the sect's aims. After all, if the Antediluvians take over, how will their demonic masters?
