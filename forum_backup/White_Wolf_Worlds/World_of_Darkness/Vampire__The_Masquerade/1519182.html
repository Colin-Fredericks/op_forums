                              Originally posted by Lysander View Post
                              I find that I do it fairly often.
                              Plenty, lately. Especially for my 7th gen Warrior of Glycon from VDA20.

                              Current favourites:

                              Potence 6
                              Sculptor of the Heavens: grants automatic successes on sculpting and building rolls involving hard materials, using your supernatural strength to manipulate them with great ease. It's inspired and mirrrors a Celerity 6 power, Precision, which gives automatic successes on creative rolls which benefit from great precision. The idea is to allow my character - otherwise a warrior with no dots in Crafts at the moment - to produce statues, build temples and inscribe hyeroglyphs in the rock to glorify Set. What he lacks for in skill - which he could pick up on with time anyway - he makes up by being able to manipulate stone like butter and using huge blocks for buildings.

                              Obfuscate 6
                              Taken by the Duat: make people disappear with you when you do a successful hold on them. If you were already invisible and succeed in the hold, they suddenly vanish from sight without you breaking your Obfuscate. The victims stay invisible until they manage to escape your hold. Sounds they might make are not an issue because in this edition Obfuscate 1 does the sphere of silence that used to be Quietus 1.
                              Crocodile Strike: still need to write it in detail, and it might end up being merged somehow with Taken by the Duat, but the idea is to make it easier or automatic not to break the concealment given by Obfuscate 2 when you do sneak attacks. In this edition sneak attacks are already possible with Obfuscate 2 and you can avoid breaking the concealment with a Wits + Stealth roll (difficulty and number of successes required are determined by the Storyteller), but this power aims at making sneak attacks super easy without needing to reactivate Obfuscate.

                              Serpentis 6
                              I wrote a lot of powers for Serpentis drawing inspiration from Greek and Egyptian mythology. These are only some of my favourites:
                              Bite of Ammit: upgrade the Typhonic Maw (Serpentis 2) to be able to drink 1 blood point per damage inflicted when attacking with the tongue or bite, and if you kill an enemy with the tongue or bite you can diablerise him automatically.
                              Typhon's Wings: Typhon is depicted with wings sometimes, so you basically get wings, 2 free dots in Flight and keep Flight as an in-clan discipline (basically the same as a Daimoinon 6 power) when you activate Typhonic Avatar (Serpentis 4).
                              Skin of Mehen / Helios' Dracos: Mehen is an Egyptian snake that guards the boat of Ra; one Greek myth says the chariot of Helios is carried by winged snakes. It upgrades Skin of the Serpent (Serpentis 3) to protect against fire and sun damage like it does from other damage (normally it works against all damage except sun and fire).
                              Amphinekros: the name means "dead in both" like amphibios (>amphibian) means "living in both". It's based on the link between snakes and different realms of existence, especially life and death, but also demons and jinn. I'm still working on it, as it started as a power to travel through the Underworld, then I thought instead of making it something to perceive and attack spirits, ghosts and demons (like the sorcerers' Quietus 4 from V20's Lore of the Clans), and the last idea has been a way to use the forked tongue and snake's great smell to sense nearby supernaturals and get detailed info on them, not only for the immaterial ones (spirit, ghosts, demons), but also vampires, lupines, etc. Depending on the successes on the activation roll, it should tell me not only direction, distance and type of supernatural creatures nearby, but also their clan or tribe and possibly generation (for vampires) and age (for lupines). It's still work in progress to make sure it's not overpowered, but it mostly does things other powers in some disciplines do at a lower level, but combining them or making it easier to activate or giving more accurate results. If I stick with the idea of it being smell-based, the name is probably going to change to something like Ophidian Smell or Scent of the Stranger (i.e. the supernatural). The ability to attack immaterial beings will be based on using the body parts transformed by Serpentis, not the whole body or weapons, so it's really the snake-ness of the character that allows it to act across the Gauntlet / Shroud. Also, it specifically does not pierce Obfuscate because the latter is a mind control power at the end of the day which makes you ignore the presence of the obfuscated character, so even if my smell picks up the scent of an obfuscated vampire, he will simply ignore it.

                              EDIT, or rather, UPDATE: I figured the ability to smell supernaturals should be a combo between Auspex 2 and Serpentis 2, while the ability to strike immaterial beings should be a Serpentis 6 power.
