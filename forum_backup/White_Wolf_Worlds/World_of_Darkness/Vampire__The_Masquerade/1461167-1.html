    My mergers would probably be as follows;

    General - all common disciplines can be learned instinctively; disciplines cost x5 for clan, x6 for other common disciplines and x7 for special disciplines.

    Gangrel/Ravnos/Old Clan Tzmisce: outsiders and border lords; disciplines - Animalism, Obfuscate, Protean; weakness - has obsessive attachment to something (a people, place, activity) and suffer +1 diff to all Self-Control checks per night you are separated from it.

    Ventrue/Lasombra/Giovanni: imperious masters of the living and the dead; disciplines - Dominate (mental control), Potence (physical control), Necromancy (control of the dead); weakness - increased difficulty with all actions related to emotions.

    Brujah/Assamites: warrior philosophers; disciplines - Auspex, Celerity, Potence; weakness - increased difficulty with frenzy checks for each blood point spent during the night.

    Toreador/Malkavian: sensualists/hedonists; disciplines- Auspex, Celerity, Presence; have an addiction (exacerbated from their mortal life) and lose willpower each night they do not partake of it.

    Tremere/Setite: a merger of Gnostic and Hermetic philosophy; disciplines - Fortitude (resist change by others), Protean (embrace change of self), Sorcery (transform the world). weakness - they cannot enslave others with the blood bond (some believe this is psychosomatic and related to Clan’s focus on breaking free of the trap that is the mortal world). Instead of Saulot, Tremere attempted to diablerize Set in order to learn more of the origins of Hermetic magic (believed be from Thoth/Hermes Trimagestus and Isis), but instead of being locked in constant struggle, the two beings merged into a Rebus.

    Nosferatu/Cappadocian/Samedi: reclusive and hidden students of life and death; disciplines - Animalism, Obfuscate, Necromancy; weakness - monstrous appearance.

    Baali/Tzimisce/Nagaraja: the monsters under the bed and creators of horrors; disciplines - Daimonion, Necromancy, Vicissitude; weakness - has a victim preference and must spend willpower to feed from non-victims.

        Brujah + Toreador = The "social predator" clan who are the most likely to interact with and take part in the night time cultures of the living, driven by dark passions and prone to fixations on their subjects of interest. (Celerity, Potence, Presence)

        Ventrue + Losambra = The "high lords of the night" clan who see themselves as the natural leaders of vampire society by right of personal strength and perseverance, prone to extreme stubbornness and a need to control. (Dominate, Fortutude, Potence)

        Gangrel + Ravnos = The "nomadic outlaw" clan who refuse to bend the knee to anyone who can't earn their respect, seen as wild and almost uncontrollable by others, driven by their baser instincts. (Animalism, Fortitude, Protean)

        Tremere + Setite = The "corrupt witch" clan who are cliquish and power hungry, having originated from a mortal sorcerer who turned themselves into vampires and maintaining their mastery of dark magics, devoted to their founder as an unliving god, but cursed by the pact the clan founder made. (Dominate, Presence, Thaumaturgy/Blood-Magic)

        Nosferatu + Tzimisce = The "creepy outsider" clan who often keep to themselves in their own hidden and well defended territories where they pursue their own arcane and uncanny interests, their presence often making others uncomfortable. (Animalism, Auspex, Obfuscate)

        Malkavian + Assamite = The "exotic mystic" clan who delve into the hidden corners and forbidden crevices of the world in search of esoteric truths and strange enlightenment, seeing the world much differently than others and driven by strange obsessions only they understand. (Auspex, Celerity, Obfuscate)

        Cappadocian + Salubri = The "haunted necromancer" clan who walk with one foot in the underworld and deal with the souls of both the living and the dead, often having trouble distinguishing between the two and perpetually surrounded by restless dead. (Auspex, Fortitude, Necromancy)

        That's just what occurs off the top of my head. If I tried, I could probably come up with a unique Discipline for each (Eros, Command, Protean, Hemourgy, etc.).

            I'll try my take here. I'm going for some strange mix here and there on purpose, but still in a way that I want to keep most of what I like in each on the final result. For the Disciplines I completely ditched the usual spreads, kept to the idea of 4 per clan, and redistributed them based on some goals:
            - I don't want gimmick disciplines on the in-clans (I have no problem with them as out-clans);
            - No magic in-clan;
            - Every discipline appears at least 3 times;
            - The most varied spreads possible, two clans shouldn't differentiate by less than 2 disciplines;
            - One Physical Discipline per clan if possible;
            - Possibility of getting any combination of two disciplines as in-clan if you want (I failed, but got close).

            Here I present my current result. I'm not entirely satisfied as I couldn't combine yet Animalism + Presence, Dominate + Obfuscate or Obfuscate + Presence, and I feel that two clans with Dominate + Presence isn't right, but for now I couldn't get around it in a satisfactory way. I'm still working on weaknesses, too.

            - Assamite + Cappadocian (The Scholars)
            Disciplines - Auspex, Fortitude, Obfuscate, Protean
            Possibly the most organized clan, focused on researching the mysteries of the world with patience. Their oldest elders are theologians, and the most significant of its factions are centered in two great hidden temples, fighting over interpretations of God, life, death, and the meaning of the undead condition. But they're not priests in the end, and they foment research in the nature of the cosmos in whatever form it takes. The clan have astrophysicists, archaeologists, philosophers, spies.

            - Nosferatu + Tzimisce (The Monsters)
            Disciplines - Animalism, Obfuscate, Potence, Protean
            Linked to the land and to old stories, members of this clan have a hard time taking part on mortal society for long periods, even for the standards of the Kindred. They are the goblins and the hags, they're the strange fairies with strange rules about them, they're the embodiment of old legends about creatures decidedly not human in appearance or demeanor. They do encounter others in their own terms, hiding in shadows or receiving guests in their isolated domains, and form a complex society.

            - Salubri + Setite (The Priests)
            Disciplines - Auspex, Celerity, Presence, Protean
            Given the insight in the souls of others and the power to stir them, those vampires can wield all forms of faith when they put their minds into it, for good or ill. Many are religious themselves, and that personal faith counts a lot to determine what they'll do with the souls they collect. Some are healers of body or mind. Some are prophets. Some are avengers. And some serve dark gods that only want to drown the world in chaos. Some in the end serve no god but themselves .

            - Gangrel + Lasombra (The Survivors)
            Disciplines - Animalism, Celerity, Fortitude, Obfuscate
            Once upon a time the founder of this clan wandered the wilds looking for ways to never die. It found four answers: flee danger whenever possible and strike your prey before it reacts, hide to never be found by enemy, harden your flesh to withstand the unavoidable blows and trust only the instincts of animals, never the reason of humans, then explore the ends of the world and the darkness beyond so you'll learn everything that can harm you. Most of the clan think of it as a useful coward.

            - Malkavian + Tremere (The Mystics)
            Disciplines - Auspex, Animalism, Dominate, Potence
            While some want to know the workings of the cosmos or the will of the gods, others wanted to thwart it and shift the universe to their own designs. Knowledge must be tempered with wisdom if one wants to avoid the consequences of transgression, but humility was anathema to the first elders of the clan. To this day their childer toil under the curses they brought, some seeking now for the wisdom to alleviate their suffering, others pressing even more on the outskirts of reallity.

            - Ventrue + Toreador (The Shepherds) - Dominate, Fortitude, Presence, Protean
            Every vampire depend on mortals, mingle with them, guide and nurture for their own interests. But no clan is so prepared for the task of controlling mortal masses. Able to go wherever they want and face whatever awaits there for the opportunity to bring down the will of their targets and infiltrate clusters of beggars and houses of aristocrats with the same ease, they have the power of the very world Kindred live in and need to feed from.

            - Brujah + Ravnos (The Idealists)
            Disciplines - Celerity, Dominate, Potence, Presence
            I'm too tired to continue now, seriously. Something something, Brujah as philosophers and Mayaparisatya, 'cause original Path of Paradox is dumb.

                Wow, those are some cool Clan combinations. This is a fun thought exercise. Let me try my hand at a second grouping:

                Lasombra/Brujah: Celerity, Obtenebration, Potence, Presence
                Weakness: because of their violent disposition, they are quicker to anger especially at disrespect from underlings, suffering a +1 difficulty to resist frenzy. They are also more vulnerable to sunlight than other vampires, taking an extra level of damage from sunlight.
                These are the brutal darwinians of the vampiric world. They seek to rule not to guide vampire to some greater good or out of a sense of duty, but because it is right for the strong to rule the weak. They don’t necessarily respect in law or hierarchy that they perceive as protecting weak rulers, so often this Clan ends up as revolutionaries. Just as often, when they have the power, they cruelly crush dissent.

                Tremere/Malkavian: Auspex, Dominate, Obfuscate, Sorcery
                Weakness: they have a derangement, as Malkavians
                Once an order of wizards who sought immortality, they transformed themselves into vampires, much like canon Tremere. Though their pursuit of the d’ordine arts of magic and their contact with chaotic forces has taken a toll on their minds, driving them insane. Often this insanity takes the form of obsessions with knowledge or power.

                Toreador/Setite/Ravnos: Auspex, Celerity, Fortitude, Presence
                Weakness: Like the Ravnos, this Clan has a single vice, almost always related to some kind of hedonistic pleasure.
                This is a Clan of hedonists and pleasure seekers. They love their vampiric state and fill every moment with beauty, pleasure, and degeneracy. While they are active in the arts, they are just as likely to be debauched nobles. Many also believe in tempting others to indulge in their vices and many of the their herds and ghouls are as much addicted to vice as they.

                Giovanni/Cappadocian/Baali: Fortitude, Necromancy, Potence, Presence
                Weakness: they are pale and cannot use blush of life. Furthermore, they are affected by symbols of faith or goodness brandished by anyone with a humanity of 6 or more - count as people as having one additional point of True Faith. An ancient inbred family of demon worshipers were transformed first into ghouls, then many were embraced by a vampire Clan. This family was able to overthrow their parent Clan with the help of demonic masters and become a full Clan themselves. Their dark rituals involve speaking to and commanding the souls of the damned, sometimes even ripping the souls out of still Irving victims. The original family line still exists as revenants and forms the core of the Clan, though they sometimes embrace others as well.

                Nosferatu/Salubri: Animalism, Auspex, Obfuscate, Potence
                Weakness: These vampires are ugly, but not to the degree of the Nosferatu. They may never raise their Appearance above 1. If they feed on a non-consenting human, they gain the full Nosferatu weakness for a number of nights equal to the blood points consumed.
                This is a Clan of vampires cursed to ugliness, though in being so cursed many have found redemption. They are a Clan of scholars, philosophers, and seekers of knowledge. They often accumulate vast libraries in their subterranean abodes, a feat now facilitated by modern technology. They sometimes sell their knowledge to others, though they seldom do so if they believe that the knowledge or information will be used for wicked ends.

                Tzimisce/Ventrue: Animalism, Dominate, Fortitude, Protean
                Weakness: They gain only half the blood points when they drink for a source that is not “theirs” - meaning their herd, their ghouls, their animals, their childer, their slaves, their subjects, etc.
                These vampires are lords, of land, of people, of animals. Still like feudal lords of old, they possess a territory and all within is theirs, at least this is what they wish and seek out. By far the most conservative of Clans, they still rule large domains with an iron fist. Though harsh, they are also fair and honorable, having a strong sense of Noblesse Oblige. Some of these vampires have territories in the wilds, like a territorial predatory. Others rule neighborhoods like a gang. Many still live in ancestral castles with farmers working the land. The power of this Clan has waned in recent years.

                Gangrel/Assamites: Animalism, Celerity, Obfuscate, Protean
                Weakness: these predators are filled with ravenous hunger for blood. Each blood point below 10 causes them to gain an animalistic feature. Their Social attributes have a maximum of their current blood pool. At zero blood, they are bestial in appearance and behavior - breaking the Masquerade. Furthermore, they have a +1 penalty to difficulties to resist hunger frenzies.
                This is a Clan of hunters and animalistic predators. Like predators, they love the thrill of the hunt and catching their prey, be it man or beast. In the wilderness they prey upon animals. In the urban jungles, humans are their prey. Some even go for big game - vampires or other supernatural creatures. Because of their skill at hunting and killing, these vampires are frequently employed as assassins, either for pay or to gain status within kindred society.


                Unfortunately, I feel like I’m still not quite able to abandon the signature Disciplines entirely.

                            I haven't thought very deeply into how this would work overall, but I have the feeling that these guys would create a very unstable vampire society. I couldn't decide on discipline spreads so I just picked one clan-specific discipline and I guess the other two/three are up in the air. Could vary by individual, or vary by in-clan factions (ie, bloodlines) Might use these for Requiem, actually.
                            1. Shapeshifters that tend to be the alien/beastial type
                              [Gangrel+Tzimisce]
                              Protean/Vicissitude
                              - Mutable flesh, the pro is that you can mould yourself and others. The con is that, when you frenzy, your flesh moulds itself without any input and won't change back to its original state and sooner or later you're going to be very visibly inhuman. You get one such mutation at embrace. Some go for an animal vibe, others go for an abstract vibe, some just go for cool body mods.
                            2. Lord of darkness
                              [Lasombra+Ventrue]
                              Obtenebration
                              - The Abyss eats your reflection and now it lives inside your corpse. I might actually go for the full V5 Lasombra weakness here. Voice doesn't echo, recordings don't pick up any sensory imput from you, image doesn't show on any surfaces... Unable to use tech or hide what they are very well, the easiest way to get by is to either forgo human society altogether and become some kind of eldricht monstrosity or to be the world's worst control freak and delegate.
                            3. Monsters by monster standards
                              [Assamite + Tremere]
                              Thaumaturgy/Quietus (Mashup shifted to focus on manipulating vitae for tracking and torture, with other blood magic paths added on as secondary)
                              - Cursed only to be able to feed on vitae, so understandably these guys are a) prefered dead by the others vampires and b) tend to band together for protection. Blood sorcery and organisation makes them preferable on your side than against you, despite the cost, though some of them don't want to be on your side and prefer to take the Banu Haqim "divinely ordained judge, jury, executioner" role.
                            4. Deader than thou
                              [Hecata+Nosferatu]
                              Necromancy
                              - Painful bite, no blush of life. Tend to be fond of places that have or had ancestor worship, where they tie their mortal descendants to them in cults (large or small). Or they live in hidden communities, Nosferatu Warren style. Creepy cillages of the dead out of view that nobody wants to think about, learning all your secrets. They make great spies and investigators - or zombie rental stores. I quite like the idea of them also using Dominate, Auspex and Obfuscate in a way that mimics wraiths (invisibility, posession, lifesight (auras), memory manipulation, etc).
                            5. "Protectors" of Humanity
                              [Brujah+Torador+Salubri]
                              Obeah/Valeren,
                              - The Brujah's emotional instability with the Toreador's pretentious and dangerous obsession with society and culture, topped off with the Salubri "holier than thou" crap. Absolutely no emotional filter. What they like is an obsession. What they own they control to every inch. What pisses them off triggers a frenzy. Clan culture often hyperfocuses on various aspects of society (art, philosophy, politics, history, religion, military, science, w/e), combined with their "medical" capabilities, they tend to see themselves as the "good" vampires. They preserve and nurture culture, partake in human social movements to change society for whatever they consider better, and shepherd their flocks whether the humans want that or not.
                            6. Seers/Mystic/Failed a Sanity check types
                              [Ravnos+Malkavian]
                              Chimestry,
                              - You get to see the cosmic truth of the universe. A) the Truth is horrifying B) your mortal shaped mind can't comprehend it, so the information is poorly translated, intrusive and traumatic. On the other hand this horrific insight comes with the ability to manipulate the projection of the false world around you. Yay.
                            7. Eviler and Holier than thou
                              [Setite+Baali+Ravnos]
                              Daimonion,
                              - Ravnos DAV20 weakness tweaked a bit, every one of them has a compulsion. Vice, virtue, folkloric, totally random... Available to interpretation or bizarrely specific. Might be "unable to lie", "slay at least one adult man every midnight of a new moon", "don't enter a house without permission", "if there are grains of rice in front of you, stop and count every one," etc. That and their insight into the dirty hidden secrets of the world around them has created a clan culture where the party line is "we are divine monsters ordained by [insert higher power here] to shepherd and/or destroy this unclean world."

                                                        Alright, here's another set of recombined Clans along the same lines:

                                                        Tzimisce/Nosferatu/Cappadocian: Animalism, Fortitude, Necromancy, Potence
                                                        Weakness: These vampires are pale and cold, unable to use blush of life. For each night that they go without sleeping surrounded by earth from their homeland, their Appearance is reduced by 1. If their Appearance becomes 0, then they are obviously inhuman, appearing as a decaying corpse. Appearance returns at the rate of one point per day of slumber in their homeland’s soil.
                                                        These vampires are lords of death, removed from mortal cities and civilizations, they are feudal lords whose power was at its zenith centuries ago. Now they find themselves removed from the mortal world, still clinging to their ancient estates, served by animals, their ghostly and zombie servants, and old inbred revenant families. They are among the most conservative of Clans.

                                                        Tremere/Assamite: Auspex, Celerity, Obfuscate, Sorcery
                                                        Weakness: When they arose in the Middle Ages and began to slay with vampires, they were hunted nearly to extinction and only avoided it by accepting a necromantic curse – taking an unsoakable aggravated wound for every point of vampire blood that they consume.
                                                        A mortal group of mystical assassins and vampire hunters who sought to use stolen vampire blood to gain immortality. Later they usurped another Clan to gain full Clan status. They now offer their services to other Clans – as wizards, spies, or assassins.

                                                        Ventrue/Malkavian: Auspex, Dominate, Obfuscate, Presence
                                                        Weakness: All vampires of this Clan have a single derangement, usually related to megalomania, narcissism, or something else related to craving power or pridefulness.
                                                        This is a Clan of master manipulators. They crave power and prestige, they want to be loved, admired, to have the power of influencing others’ hearts and minds. They are known to be masters of manipulation, whether through magical means or mundane, and are the vampires with the greatest amount of temporal wealth and power.

                                                        Toreador/Lasombra: Celerity, Obtenebration, Potence, Presence
                                                        Weakness: Cursed to cast no reflection, members of this Clan are obsessed with their own image and may be captivated by painting or other likenesses of themselves.
                                                        These vampires are lords and ladies of the night. They are passionate, love beauty and death and darkness. The see themselves are royalty of sorts, fit to rule, to be pampered and pleasured by servants. In the modern era, many take up gothic styles, wearing black or even symbols of darkness or evil.

                                                        Setite/Giovanni: Dominate, Necromancy, Obfuscate, Potence
                                                        Weakness: As the Giovanni weakness
                                                        An ancient inbred family from Egypt, who claim to trace their ancestry to the pharaohs and priests of ancient Egypt, they are a mortal family as much as a vampire Clan. They practice an ancient form of Egyptian necromancy and still worship the gods of Egypt and incorporate that religion into their magic. Not only do they pursue the mystical arts, but also dedicate themselves to acquiring wealth and power, using their mental powers and family connections to create a web of influence across the world. Most members of the Clan draw upon the family and so are of old Egyptian ancestry.

                                                        Salubri/Brujah: Auspex, Celerity, Dominate, Fortitude
                                                        Weakness: When members of this Clan drink blood from an unwilling human, they must make a frenzy roll with a difficulty of 5 + number of blood points consumed. Even if they resist this frenzy, drinking blood from the unwilling increases rolls to resist frenzy by 1 for one day per point consumed.
                                                        This is a Clan of philosophers and even humanitarians. They have long hoped to overcome the weaknesses of the vampire state, pursuing legend of Golconda. They have also long been rebels, seeing the oppressive nature of power structures and supporting Anarchs and other movements that resist oppression by the elders. They also strive to protect humans from exploitation by vampires. While some of these vampires settle into a single city, forming the backbone of Anarch movements, others prefer a nomadic existence to avoid corrupt vampiric politics.

                                                        Baali/Gangrel: Animalism, Fortitude, Presence, Protean
                                                        Weakness: Vampires of this Clan suffer more from True Faith than other vampires. Any human with a Humanity of 6 or higher counts as having +1 point of True Faith against these vampires, which means that even people without True Faith can harm or repel these vampires by brandishing symbols of goodness. Furthermore, when vampires of this Clan frenzy, they take on a bestial animal-like appearance that could violate the Masquerade.
                                                        This is a Clan of witches, living deep in the woods away from humanity, sometimes surrounded by human cults or servants, they may be the origin of many myths of witchcraft. Many of these vampires worship demons or pagan gods of evil, some even practice sorcery or receive demonic gifts from their dark rituals.
