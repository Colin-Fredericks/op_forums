    My Tattered Facade view.

    01-02-2026, 03:13 PM
    The other day I picked up VTM Tattered Facade sourcebook and I found it a useful addition to my collection as it gives more ideas on what kind of nightly horror a "typical" kindred would have and how hard it is to maintain the Masquerade and what sort of people you would encounter during the night when most of us would be in bed asleep.

    There's is some helpful storytelling ideas and they come up with more clan archetypes and what sort of skills and disciplines that would be fitting. Not to mention what type of struggles that each clan would have and some story ideas and I personally liked the Collector archetype for the Tzmisce the most with the Ventrue Headhunter and Ministry Procurer archetypes a close second and third.

    They do have some neat new and darker discipline abilities and amalgams and a set of new lore sheets. I should mention that they have a section on what kind of horror vibes that you can work with from gothic horror to eldritch themes that would fit in a H.P. Lovecraft setting.

    All in all, I found it a good addition to my collection of VTM books.
