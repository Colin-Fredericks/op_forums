    Favourite Clanbook?

    07-22-2022, 12:30 AM
    My posts would probably lead many to believe that I would say Tremere, or another blood magic clan. But honestly, I found the revised venture clanbook to be perhaps the easiest read of all the clanbooks. I dont know how to describe it, but for a lack of better term I would say it was the most "simple" read, if that makes any sense. Not my favourite clan, but an enjoyable read nontheless. Someone else can probably explain it better than I can.

    What about you?

                                  I don't like any of the clanbooks. The clan descriptions found in the earlier Players Guide had almost all of the information needed for those clans. In comparison, the clanbooks were mostly bloated versions of that more concise information. Only a handful of clanbooks provided any additional useful information, and what new things they did provide were relatively minor.

                                  For that reason, if I had to pick one that was the most useful out of a bad batch, it would be the original Nosferatu clanbook simply for providing the story of the Nictuku. That at least is a story element that continued to have traction after it was published. There were some other tidbits that could be pulled from the other clanbooks, but they didn't have the same staying power. For example, the concept of the Ventrue organizing themselves against the "Secret Masters" was potentially interesting, but there was sufficient information provided in terms of how that would affect the Ventrue or could be used for individual Ventrue PCs.

                                  What would have been better in my opinion was simply expand the Clan descriptions and organizations in the Players Guide with a lore section that included things about their history and antedeluvians. It probably would only be an additional two paragraphs. That's how bloated the clansbooks are.

                                  Certainly something along the Players Guide lines were needed for the non-seven clans of the corebook. The original Players Guide did not have anything for the Sabbat clans (which had yet to be published except for their names) or the Independent clans (which introduced them). So the clanbooks for those six clans at least introduced something we didn't have. But all of them were extremely flawed. The Sabbat clanbooks failed to distinguish the difference between Sabbat culture and non-Sabbat culture for those clans, lamely insisting they were in fact the same. The clanbooks for the Independent clans were either too focused on one aspect (the original versions) or lamely rewrote the clans so that it dropped their original concepts (the revised versions). Out of these, the best clanbook was the original Giovanni clanbook which provided some great flavor. Unfortunately, it has too many flaws and was an early example of writers deciding to ditch canon in order to expand the character types allowable to PCs.
