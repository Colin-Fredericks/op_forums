                    There are some simple tricks that get a lot of mileage.

                    Potence 5*, decent strength, decent brawl.
                    Use a blood point for Potence, if there's spares, use them on strength.
                    Roll decent dice pool with 5 automatic successes added. On a success, the target is unable to take any action but try to break loose. Old rules at it's finest. Against this attempt, there's a decent pool with 5 automatic successes on top. Good luck without [automatic successes].
                    Then bite. The target can take no actions to defend themselves, so it should hit. Deals 5 automatic aggravated damage + successes from roll. Opponent still locked in hold, if they manage to survive, remain active or whatever else. Rinse and repeat. Add a seasoning of celerity to accelerate the process.


                    Retainers 5 (because one might die!)

                    Adds +1 (max 4) per retainer to the opposing's attack and defense rolls. It gets one sided quickly.


                    *It works with lower, 3 is pretty fine, but is not as guaranteed. Winning initiative can be critical, but once the hold is achieved, it's irrelevant.

                    Neither require a particular clan or selective interpretation of rules. Neither requires much of preparation. Neither is flawless either, and [automatic successes] is a good counter to both, but it will solve most problems.

                            The thing is, it does not drain a lot of "character resources" and you can actually still make a character out of it. Now, it can't compete with spirit of the fray and the likes, but then we're not even talking starting characters.

                            As for defenses, the chance of one-shotting characters with is abysmal without automatic successes or multipliers. It happens. For defenses, you could shore up with class 5 riot gear (-3 to dexterity based dice pools, oh no!), replicating Fortitude 5 in the process. Can still completely fail a soak roll. It happens. But if you want to go "rocket tag", you have all those options, and unlike throwing a bucket of dice, with 5 automatic successes, you're nearly always guaranteed to win.

                            But if you have 5 retainers, one is likely to win the initiative and that's enough. If they manage to use a Hold (ghouls automatically have Potence, increasing the odds), it's over as no other actions than breaking loose is possible, and then you can do whatever.

                            The bottom line is that you never need much more than the basic Hold combat manouver. With Potence you just make it fast and effortless. But I'm not sure white-room scenarios will show you that, try it in actual play instead.

If it's an activation required, it's still another action. But once again, it's so oddly specific counters that fill little to no purpose against anything else. But they exist, and that's fine!
