    Running the Free States - 1 - the Anarch Council

    02-03-2025, 08:26 PM
    I will likely run a chronicle set in the Anarch Free States starting in the next few months. I’ll be using some of the ideas I and other players discussed on some other threads on this forum. However, as I'm putting together my notes, I've realized there are a lot of practical considerations that aren't addressed, and I need to figure them out. So I thought to share my ideas here and get other people's feedback, ideas, and commenary.

    Rather than post everything on one big thread that gets clogged up, I’m posting them as separate threads so each subject can get discussed on its own. I’ll add links to the other posts here as edits though, so people can access all threads on this topic.

    = = =

    There is an Anarch Council, but that Council does not govern the Free States. Instead it coordinates and supports the Anarchs outside the Free States. The Anarch Council itself is a seven person council that oversees the revolution outside of the Free States. I guess it is something like an Anarch Comintern. This is just one of many things neglected in the Los Angeles By Night sourcebook. While it does not govern the Free States, I imagine internal politics does govern its decisions in some capacity. And it can be an easy source of plot hooks and intrigues for PCs. So I want to figure out what to do with it.

    It is headed by Jeremy MacNeil himself, three other Brujah, and a representative each of the Gangrel, Nosferatu, and Ravnos clans per the Brujah Clanbook 1e.

    The three other Brujah are the Anarch leaders of San Diego, Sacramento, and Seattle. If we accept that as true, we can figure out who they are. Tara is the obvious person to be from San Diego. Later in the Brujah Clanbook, we’re told Marguerite Foccart dwells in Sacramento. She’s a perfect person to be on the Council. That only leaves Seattle, a domain we know little about other than that Justicar Petrodon is stationed there.

    Dark Alliance: Vancouver seems to be the most we have, and all it says is Anarchs have a very hard time in Seattle as Petrodon’s Archons are adept at rooting out and destroying them. Therefore, I will arbitrarily assign Salvador Garcia to this role. First, because I think the author of the Anarch Manifesto and Status Perfectus should be on the Council. Second, he is obviously engaged in supporting the Anarchs outside the city. Third, I will interpret the statement that Garcia is the “leader” of the Seattle Anarchs to be a purposeful tweak at Petrodon as the two vampires are rivals and are in indirect communication with each other.

      I am reluctantly putting in Ireena from LABN as the Ravnos representative. I say reluctantly because her biography in LABN is not very impressive. The Council does not really gain anything by her presence. She’s very young, only tenth generation, and not really leading anyone. She doesn’t bring anything to the table.

      However, she was part of the group that attacked Don Sebastian’s ranch, and identified the havens of the elders of LA so the anarchs could attack them. She wanders the Free States and the Pacific Northwest, doing what she can to damage the Camarilla. She helps anarchs wherever she can, and fights the Camarilla and Sabbat. I am forced to admit she is the most likely candidate. She actually has no political influence among other vampires. She seems to just do her own thing although her presence probably brings some sympathy and occasional support from other Ravnos.

      She’s probably not going to argue much with the Brujah on the Council. She either misses any votes or debates entirely, or likely agrees with whatever was decided. She’s a reliable partner to MacNeil, Foccart, and Garcia, and that is probably the only thing that matters. She’s figleaf to show the Anarchs as being more representative than what they really are.

      As such, I imagine PCs will likely assume she is of much more importance than what she really is. She's also a way to introduce the idea of other Ravnos dwelling in the Fee States or visiting there without the harassment and problems that are encountered in more traditional domains. It could also be a complication for Gangrel in the Free States as their rivalry with that clan is not part of Camarilla dynamics. And many Barons aren't going to welcome them any more than Camarilla Princes would. I also think it is possible to show that the Ravnos are their own thing, and aren't actually anarchs. The clan may be very loosely organized and its members have a lot of freedom, but Ravnos neonates aren't at war with their elders in the same way the anarchs are at war. Ravnos elders are different from the elders of other clans, but they are still elders. This clan has different goals and ideas than the anarchs even if there are some common interests.

                  Given the nature of the Anarch Free States as a place where the Revolution came, The Man went down and quite a lot of the Revolutionaries promptly cried “Job done, PARTY TIME!” whilst others hollered “KATANAS! KATANAS! KATANAS!” and the most important single grown-up was so determined not to become a tyrant that he decided to play Cincinattus when it was really, really time to play George Washington, I’d argue that it makes perfect sense for the Anarch Council to be at least a little bit more of a talking shop with enormous potential for either rationalising the Free States Revolution (and taking this show on the road) or falling prey to the ‘tall poppy’ syndrome so inherent to those who have overthrown their ‘elders & betters’ and are fully prepared to spend the foreseeable future tearing down anyone (or any group) who starts looking a little too much like the Inner Circle.

                  In other words, the Anarch Council has to walk a fine line between exerting it’s influence and avoiding being see as The Authorities if it wants to avoid becoming a might-have-been (Like the Kerensky-led version of Russia): with that in mind, it makes perfect sense that it’s members would have to eat a certain amount of humble pie, whilst also keeping a weather eye out for the Camarilla answer to the Freikorps, the Free State’s equivalent to Lenin & the Bolsheviks, as well as the fear of next year’s Stalin emerging from their own ranks.

                  Basically, it’s easy to imagine the Council’s members as an interesting mix of True Believers seeking to spread The Revolution, pragmatists attempting to fit a safety valve to the Katanas-Trenchcoats-Nineties Action Movie mayhem of the Free States (Presumably by throwing some of the louder enthusiasts against Camarilla strongholds or Sabbat forward bases), Exiles looking to drum up support for their own comeback tour, cynics looking to profit off The Revolution (Of only by building their own reputation in Anarch circles) and perhaps the tidy-minded, looking to impose some actual structure on vampires of the ‘Wild, Wild West Coast’ (Especially in it’s beating heart, before everybody suffers a heart attack and/or BUFFY THE VAMPIRE SLAYER becomes a chillingly accurate documentary).


                  Put another way, I’d argue that Ireena of Clan Ravnos makes a perfect member of the Anarch Free States - especially as the ‘Face’ of the Council - as she’s active, idealistic and takes the lead without being someone the Anarchs will look at and see as a potential threat to their freedom to do whatever the **** they feel like (Much less somebody who has ‘Future Prince of LA’ hanging over them like a gathering storm), not least due to her own fairly puny generation.

                  All of which means she has relatively little muscle of her own, but is also a ‘tabula rasa’ more likely to be judged on her (Impeccably Anarch) actions than on any past history.


                  The real problem, of course, is coming up with other members for the Anarch Council who have enough age and blood potency to be useful, but come
                  across as enough of a clean slate (or possess sufficient local goodwill) to avoid contracting a dangerous case of Tall Poppy Syndrome.

                  Well, that and ensuring that members build up the Free States as a whole without conjuring up a personal base of support so potent they pull a ‘crown from the gutter’ move, a la Napoleon.

                  (I’m assuming that, whether as associates of the Anarch Council or as agents thereof, it’s the Player Characters who will/would have played the key role in making the Free States and their Revolution work, with the canonical fate of LA serving as a prime example of what happens when they adventure off on a frolic of their own instead).

                          Right, now we get to the Clan Specific bits (The following are all from Clan Gangrel):-


                          AVENGING INTERNEE - A Japanese American girl who had an experience of the Second World War that, coupled with life as a budding 1950s housewife, left her with such a burden of quiet anger that a passing Gangrel noticed and gave her the chance to let that anger Roar.

                          Embracing her wild side, though more interested in clawing out territory in the Urban Jungle, she came to Los Angeles and very quickly learned that the vampire authorities were no more loveable than the All-American establishment : to say she embraced the Anarch Revolution is rather like saying a starving mother embraces the prospect of a steak dinner …


                          COUNTER-CULTURE REVOLUTIONARY (aka ‘Feral Hippy’): Was keen to become one with nature even before that Gangrel rose from the Earth the way a dolphin leaps from the sea and rejoiced in their new animal vigour after somehow convincing a Gangrel to make them a vampire (Possibly even the same one, though our hero remains somewhat unclear on that particular point).

                          It seems highly likely they did so by demonstrating a willingness to do whatever it took just short of making themselves as enough of a Yes Man to be marked down for perpetual ghoul-dom, then proceeded to build a career as a surprisingly-groovy Feral (Not least as something of an ambassador between other Gangrel and the local vampires).

                          Unsurprisingly became caught up in the undercurrent of pure loathing towards the LA Camarilla (and towards the Camarilla as a whole) this particular specimen became outright carried away with enthusiasm for the Anarch Rebellion and the Free States, but (to the surprise of many) has turned out to be enough of a Bug Picture thinker to start advocating that the Anarchs take their show on the road, inevitably bringing him into the orbit of the Anarch Council.


                          It now occurs to me that the logical way of making this sometime Hippy a logical fit for the Anarch Council would be to show him having some history as a smuggler, with at least some history of running drugs & guns, as well as general smuggling (Not least of ideas).


                          THE SPIRIT OF 1976 - A Native American Rights advocate of somewhat violent temper who caught the attention of an elder Gangrel and was, in part through sheer disillusion, won over to the idea of becoming a vampire.

                          Arriving in Camarilla society with the mixture of American Pride, disillusion, outrage and pure cynicism towards Authority so peculiar to the 1970s, he swiftly learned to love Vampire Society just as much as he loved the US authorities.

                          Having been a budding Anarch ever since their days as a fledgling, they are finally beginning to bloom into the Revolutionary they had dreamed of becoming, though whether this will be a cure or a vehicle for their persistent cynicism is an interesting question …

                                      CRACKPOT THEORY TIME - As you might imagine, when writing up ideas for Vampire Los Angeles circa the 1990s, with the benefit of 30 years and some months of hindsight, BUFFY THE VAMPIRE SLAYER came to mind and ANGEL: THE SERIES came calling with a welcome basket, as well as what may or may not have been Menaces touching on the probable consequences of not becoming a key influence on Vampire Los Angeles.

                                      All of which is a prelude to and an excuse for my latest Crackpot Theory: the most entertaining way to include pastiches of ANGEL and other noteworthy BUFFY-type vampires into the Old World of Darkness would be as member of Clan Nosferatu.

                                      Two Words: GAME FACE.

                                      On a slightly more serious note, Black Fox, you’ve noted before that you’re disappointed that Clan Nosferatu are too often depicted with a culture of slumming it: by taking advantage of the fact that The Master was literally modelled on Graf Orlock (The original NOSFERATU) and that his descendants (As well as all other vampires in the TV series) are depicted with some blatantly monstrous visages* to build pastiches (Presumably affectionate pastiches) of those characters as members of the Clan legendary for producing the stuff of Creature Features by the nature of their Curse, you would swiftly acquire a charismatic selection of Nosferatu with a stereotype-busting range of personalities.

                                      Also, making the ‘Order of Aurelius’ Nosferatu would allow you to explore the major differences of tone & style between the Buffy-verse and the World of Darkness (How different would the characters of Angel and Spike be if they could never STOP looking like monsters, for example?).

                                      I’m running out of time to go into more detail - Battery down to the dregs - but it does strike me that ‘Damon’ (A Nosferatu who seems to think he’s a born Brujah, with a brutal history and a strong vein of tragic romance who may or may not be one of the more prolific vampire hunters in Los Angeles and is certainly a sometime Vampire PI) might make for an interesting member of the Anarch Council.


                                      * (Albeit ones they can put on and out off, probably with an act of deliberate will).

                                                      Fantastic work,Atticus Cleanclaw!

                                                      I wrote a novel for submission to 5th Edition as a pitch for reviving the book spin offs of VAMPIRE: THE MASQUERADE last year called KNIGHT OF FREEDOM that was one of two I completed. The project was rejected but the other one would have starred Lucita. Knight of Freedom had the premise that Theo Bell has been hiding in the Anarch Free States for the past decade or so since his assassination of Hardelstadt and kept expecting the hammer to fall down. The joke was that Theo Bell was living a comparative life of luxury being passed around between the Barons and the Camarilla had no real interest in assassinating him for reasons that were revealed in the novel. But also because Theo Bell worked much better as an unliving Non-Red List symbol of Anarch perfidy than someone they wanted to martyr.

                                                      Among the things I established in the book was the following:

                                                      1. Baron Louis Fortier: The ruler of Bel-Air and one of the most powerful Barons around as well as the richest, he's also someone all of the Anarchs are generally embarrassed about. Mostly because he's a Ventrue French potentate who rule exactly like a Prince except perhaps an unusually generous one. Louis is notably someone who is aware of the fact that as ruler of a huge chunk of LA, he's more powerful than most Princes in America and has the mind that if he allied with the Camarilla, they'd swiftly replace him with someone else. Ergo, he's quite happy to be like Napoleon and claim he's all for the Revolution while wielding immense power. All of his wives have left him but he continually ends up finding new young Kindred mistresses since he has a most- Un-Elder obsession with Sex.

                                                      2. The MacNeils: This is not actually a Barony but a Clan of Brujah descended from the Beckoned or dead Jeremy. They constantly feud for power and routinely end up staking each other but never kill one another. The current leader is his youngest progeny who helped steal the Stone of Scone in 1950. Annie MacDougal is a pain in the ass for all Anarchs because she insists anyone not like Jeremy is failing to be Anarch enough. Many think he bloodbound her before he went away because no Kindred could be that stupidly loyal.

                                                      3. The Undisputed Baron of the Valley, Victor Temple: Yet another Ventrue who has weasaled his way to the top, he's a lot more accepted by Baron Fortier but only because he's younger and hipper with a reputation roughly akin to Suge Knight's. His affable demeanor conceals as much violence and ruthlessness as any other Ventrue ruler, though. He is also somewhat careless with the Masquerade and this is likely to get him killed well before his other stunts. He recently has begun feuding with longtime ally Nelli-G.

                                                      4. Hesha Ruhadze: The Ministry archaeologist and Theopidian joining with the breakaway sect was something few expected but he's taken over the Church of Set and become heavily involved in counter-culture Satanism as a trick to lure in vessels as well as attack traditional religion. He lives in Pasadena and generally leaves most of the regular Anarch business to lesser childer as he focuses on Clan business. Generally, Hesha's opinion of the Anarchs can be summarized as, "same blood, different vessels." He is a cynical old snake who thinks there is no difference between the Camarilla and Anarchs than the former smell better but he's dealt with plenty of peasant revolutions and rabble rousers before. It'll all come together eventually around an elite he's already a member of.

                                                      5. Nelli G: The beautiful Toreador descendant of the Griffith family, Nelli G is a habitual traitor to the Kindred race and has allied with the Second Inquisition to eliminate her enemies in Hollywood. She's also surrendered Griffith Park to the Garou, causing a small war with the local Gangrel (led by Ramona). Her humanity has gone down rapidly since the murder of her sire and doesn't seem to be stopping as the power has gotten to her. She is the Lady of Hollywood and its strip, controlling kindred who want fame, money, drugs, and blood. Her second in command is Vivi and she is already plotting against her, which Nelli approves of as it keeps her sharp.

                                                      6. Nines Rodriguez: Nines is pissed and is unlikely to stop being so any time soon. He's been essentially given a role to help protect the Masquerade in the city by all of the Barons allowing him free passage as well as to that of his gang. It's a role that Nines notes is suspiciously similar to that of a Sheriff even though he considers himself a Cleaner. He also feels like some people want him to have even more power that would make him, you guessed it, a Baron of Barons. One, of course, with lots of powerful Barons as a Council behind him. Nines is disgusted and is wondering if they should put Theo in charge just as a F-U to the system. He HATES Hesha.

                                                      7. Theresa/Jeanette: The very much disputed Baron of Santa Monica and several other territories, Theresa and Jeanette are direly opposed to one another but between them control everything but the Pier. They are surprisingly accepting of Thin Bloods and other immigrants. She's also one of the most ruthless in destroying them or playing them off against her "sister." Theresa is notable in that she also controls a large chunk of Las Vegas from the Asylum franchise there and is debating giving up one territory for the other. Basically, Sin City is ripe for picking and the Hecata have made her an offer to be their figurehead.

                                                      8. Smiling Jack and Jenna Cross: Smiling Jack was, unsurprisingly, Beckoned given his age and obsession with Noddism. What's weird is he claims he's back from the Middle East and with greater insight than ever. His adopted childe, Jenna Cross, has also become head of one of the largest Duskborn groups in America with over forty members. While based in Santa Monica, they are spread all through California and on the web. It is here they are working on using Thin Blooded Alchemy to try to win a place in the Anarchs equal to a Clan.

                                                      9. Wei: : An invasion of the Tzimisce from China briefly disrupted the life of many Asian American Kindred in California. Wei was embraced by one of them and helped lead a revolt after their leader was assassinated by the LA Ripper. Wei is now in control of Chinatown and generally has the view that they're Americans and weird clan abilities shouldn't mean much. Still, he's as power hungry and ruthless as any other Kindred leader. Ironically, Wei has a big secret he's keeping from his former Anarchs that will destroy him. The irony is that it's not a typical Kindred secret: it's that he was a former undercover LA detective.

                                                      10. Ramona:​ Ramona is weirded out by the fact that as an anti-social Gangrel who never was close to her clan, she's become a celebrity for her part in the defection to the Anarchs. Ironically, her aloofness means that people take her "fuck off and figure it out for yourself" attitude as wisdom. Reluctantly, she's become a den mother for other Gangrel in the city and many Dustborn and Caitiff. She has also claimed Griffith Park for her territory and the expected massacre by the Garou Nelli G counted on didn't happen. They apparently are having their own issues and the caern there is temporarily abandoned. Ramona rarely spends time there anyway and mostly uses her "dominion" there as a title to scare people away.
