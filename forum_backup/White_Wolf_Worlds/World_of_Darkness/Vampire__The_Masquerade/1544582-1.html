    Well, like I briefly mentioned, I played a game upon which we were all 4th gen at the second city.

    My very own character was a former exiled prince, ambitious and merciless, you know, the sort of narcissist prick that believes the world is his birth right. He was to be the rightful heir of the throne, but as a tale old as time, got replaced by his sister nonetheless. All because the other members of the family thought that my nice little prince would become a tyrant or something (and he absolutely would).

    Well, he then had to flee for his life. With nothing else going for him, with no allies, no safe heavens, exiled from his own land and a stranger without connections on foreign lands, he decided to try his luck out based on some crazy rumours he heard about the "city of dark magic", and he decided that he would pledge his entire kingdom to any dark wizard that could help him enact revenge on his usurper sister and traitorous family and servants.

    And so he went, until he finally came at the gates of Enoch, basically as a homeless person.

    Despite his pitiful state however, he was still a prince, and determined to get what he came for, he started to work towards getting to know such strange and imposing kingdom.

    The fact that he had a massive silver tongue didn't hurt either.

    Oh yes, we all began playing as mortals. And we actually played quite a handful of sessions as mortals, each one of us, and neither one of us really decided which clan we would be, that was decided upon us by the masters of the city.

    So, I managed to get a job with a merchant, and my skills amazed my employer, so I was able to accommodate myself fairly easy. And at all of my empty time I would try to find out any information that I could about the "Dark Magicians" that ruled the city.

    Eventually, I learned out about the clan Lasombra, and thus I decided that they would be perfect for me to pledge my allegiance (no, my character didn't know what a "clan" was, nor what the "Lasombra" really were; for him, they could just as well be some sort of "guild" of these magicians or something).

    Therefore, he tried to make his move, to directly approach these "nobles" and make a case for them to accept him as a member or at least a vassal.

    That... Didn't work quite like I expected. Little did I know that my former home was known by the "dark wizards" of Enoch, and they were not on good stands; there were many things running on the background by the part of the ST that I didn't know about.

    Therefore, someone from my kingdom approaching the Lasombra... Settled some fetters. And a few of the Antidiluvians came to "extract" information from me.

    To be very specific, SET FUCKING HIMSELF came to torture the truth out of me. And as a mere mortal being tortured by freaking Set, I quickly spilled my beans, I was trying to deceive them in order to help me get my revenge.

    Upon hearing this, Set immediately set me free and got absolutely delighted. "REVENGE!" He claimed with a smile upon his face. "Why didn't you just say so? Hahaha" he laughed in a diabolic tone.

    Then, something I was not expecting happened. You see, upon searching about the "dark wizards" houses, I thought the Lasombra would be perfect for my purposes.

    However, it was Set that... Set his eyes on me. And I became his property.

    He turned me into his ghoul (yep, I was still not a vampire, and that's several sessions of game play already), and introduced me into the clan.

    At first I was... Shocked. That was... Not what I had signed for.

    You see, I was a wannabe tyrant, but that's because my character was a narcissist that believe he knew better than anyone else.

    But his purpose with his relentless tyranny was to make his kingdom greater.

    What Set offered thou, was just decadence.

    But then Set said "this is the natural state of things. In due time, everybody gets corrupted. Give it a try, even the noblest soul will fall under the right temptations. Your pathetic attempts to improve society is just an escape from the truth. All kingdoms become decadent and crumble in the end".

    Taking these words to mind, I decided to put it to a test. Despite getting into the city as a homeless, I was a skilled and merciless merchant who weren't shy to use some cutthroat methods when necessary to get things my way, therefore at that point I had already amassed a considerable amount of wealth.

    Thus, I set a plan to test Set's truths; I approached a poor peasant with a beautiful daughter, I obviously used my resources to look for someone with undoubted honor and honesty, who were considered as a pillar of the community and admired by his peers, and with a daughter that was deemed by all as a model of purity and chastity.

    Thus, I approached this man, posing as and honourable rich merchant (and the rich part was true), to propose to marry his daughter. It took some convincing, but after showing beyond any doubt that I was an honourable and honest man, he finally accepted the marriage proposal.

    Thus, I took my new beloved wife to my estate and... Began her "processing".

    I forced her to lay down with other men. With slaves. With women. Even with animals.

    I was expecting her to break at any point and finally succumb to total debauchery. But. That. Never. Happened.

    Not just that, despite hating all the things I did to her, she still never moved against me.

    And... That changed me. No. Set was wrong.

    Well, meanwhile, my whole love story was not the only thing happening on the city. First of all, Cain - yes, the very first generation indeed. We actually met him - finally vanished for good.

    Oh yes, I had also met my other table friends. They were all vampires by now, I was the only ghoul left. Asdif the wise (Capadocian), Panthaleon the warrior (Gangrel) and Nonain (the only female player). Actually, Nonain was NOT a vampire yet, she was still fully mortal, being courted by the clan of Roses, but she at all the time refused to accept the dark gift, and rumours started to spread that Absimilliard was starting to take an interest on her.

    Anyway, I became good friends with these, and managed to make myself a sort of permanent resident of their house.

    At some point, they were out the house, and I got visited by a certain Nergal and a certain Shaitan... Yeah, that's right. They wanted me to... Deliver a certain message to Asdif and Panthaleon. They said they were planning something big, and wanted their help.

    Eventually, talking with this guys, I ended up talking too much and spilling the fact that I believed that Set was wrong... Which they delighted to hear (now, of course I didn't willingly confess that to them, but a recently formed ghoul have little chance to resist 2 4th gens, so they simply forced it out of me). They then fully supported me, and claimed that the Antidiluvians were oppressors that should be deposed, and that was their purpose, and that if I managed to do so I would be doing the right thing and whatnot, and that's why as a friend of Asdif and Panthaleon I could help to bring them to the cause and whatnot.

    Thus, I went with my wife out of the city, in search of these two.

    Set had given me a barrel with his vitae thou... But I never ceased learning.

    Believing the time to be of essence, I told my wife to drop the vitae on my mouth once my heart stopped, and then stabbed myself in the heart.

    And my beloved and obedient wife did as I ask, despite not knowing what was going on.

    And that's the story of how my character became a vampire under the city of Enoch
