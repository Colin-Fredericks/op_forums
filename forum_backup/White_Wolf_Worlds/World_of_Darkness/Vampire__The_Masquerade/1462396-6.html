            Originally posted by Ragged Robin View Post

            Point taken.

            That's certainly an.....interesting interpretation of goratrix and that time he cut off his wily. Is goratrix even a real name?

            Goratrix is supposed to be Gallic/French in terms of background. There are actually a whole host of Gallic names ending in -rix (Vercingetorix, Ambiorix, Dumnorix, and so on). The ending means "king", and is not too distantly related to the Latin "rex". I would presume when Goratrix was first created the writers invented a Gallic-sounding name which doesn't actually mean anything, but fits in with the types of names used by powerful Gauls in antiquity. In game terms it's quite easy to justify as a name taken on by Goratrix when he became a mage to emphasise his importance.

            Of course, if your cultural frame of reference extends no further than Harry Potter, you are probably going to assume that a name ending in -rix is female.
