    Personally, I like to run Thaumaturgy and necromancy as something that's relatively easy to buy into but something that becomes very expensive to progress in if you're not blessed with being one of the magic master races. Knowledge for the first two levels can be bought from Nosferatu, Malkavians, Anarchs, Settites, underperforming Tremere, the internet... It'll cost a lot, you'll generally waste a lot of time, but it's great business for the seller. Once you've chosen your style, it's difficult to change, and thaumaturgy is limited by knowledge, so whoever sold you Thaumaturgy 1 is probably going to be the person selling you Thaumaturgy 2 and so on. In most disciplines, the first dot is most useful, but this isn't true for Thaumaturgy and to some degree necromancy; the first dot is a taster (though it requires significantly more investment than the second and so this isn't like drugs where you can give away a freebie).

    Learning thaumaturgy will get your average vampire into a good amount of debt, and the more lucrative abilities are hoarded or sold at exorbitant prices. So although access is widespread, few people actually bother much, other disciplines come with no strings attached after all. A cheaper method would be to join a cult. But then you're in a cult.

            Originally posted by CajunKhan View Post
            In my opinion, vampires should have SOMETHING that allows them to duplicate classic Dracula powers like weather-control, making doors open and close, and making all the candles in a room light or snuff. However, I really don't care how that is achieved.
            That's my preference too. You want to be able to emulate certain tropes. Which is why I made certain Thaumaturgical Paths like Movement of Mind, Lure of Flames, and Weather Control knowledge that pre-dated the Tremere, and is known to the general vampire population. This doesn't mean such knowledge is common - in the sense that any jack vampire knows how to do it. Just that there are vampires who know it regardless of clan, sect, or any other faction. It's just finding the mentors who can show you such "tricks".

            The idea that this knowledge was gained through something like the Scholomance, rather than a "natural" power of vampires, is fine by me.

            You only need 1 dot in Lure of Flames and Movement of Mind to do what is above. And that's the limit of most practitioners. A few get to rank 3 in Movement of Mind so they can fly. If it's in the Lost Boys, I want even Anarchs to have the chance to learn how to do it. David learning it from a mentor who then taught his own crew is OK as the little secret of their Anarch gang. Weather Control is limited to actual scholars of the Occult, but there are a fair number of those.

            Outside of this general knowledge pool, most other Paths are limited to specific factions, and the Tremere have a lot of exclusive knowledge.

            I would not say Blood Magic is common, meaning it's available to most characters. It's just ubiquitous in the sense that such (general) knowledge is spread around and available. But learning such occult mastery is hard. It's more of an art than a science. And it is not something commonly taught, or has manuals that show you how to do it. Most characters have no aptitude for it. And many who might would rather spend their time on other things. I want to keep it relatively rare in the setting. Every ST will find a different balance they like for their games.
