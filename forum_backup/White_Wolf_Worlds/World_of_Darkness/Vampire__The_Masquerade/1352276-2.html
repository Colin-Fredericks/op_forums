    In my chronicle, I'm gonna decide that the Lasombra actually DID succeeded in killing their antediluvian back in the Renaissance, and that Cappadocius, Set and the Ravnos antediluvian are also, truly dead. Consequently, though they don't really understand why, elders of their clans don't hear the Beckoning. The effect of this on the setting is relatively minor, as the Ravnos elders are almost extinct and the bulk of the elder Setites are already in the Middle East anyhow, and the Harbingers and the Lasombra were mostly in the Sabbat, and went off to fight the Gehenna War regardless. But it will have something of an impact in my Mexico City chronicle, were several old Lasombra remained behind to guard Sabbat assets, and the Lasombra antitribu will suddenly become a bit of a stronger faction in the Camarilla. This creates, I think, a cool, dynamic imbalance in the setting, and a little bit of a mystery to boot.

            Sex, Sexuality, and Blood

            These are just some flavor rules for my games. Warning - for immature audiences only.

            + Most vampires gradually start to become pansexual due to the fact Feeding and Sex are intrinsically linked in their minds. Some vampires remain either strongly heterosexual or homosexual but this is due to personal identity that most discard with time. Asexual vampires feel the Hunger as something as a wholly alien desire.

            + All vampires are physically capable of sex with the Blush of Life and enjoy it if they're so inclined to do so in life. However, the experience of sex pales to the feeding and most find sex without feeding unsatisfying.

            + Vampires are capable of ghouling through their use of fluids during sex. This is one of the few "covert" means of ghouling or blood bonding someone.

            + The Kiss is magical in nature and prevents the body's blood from spilling out in arterial spray. A vampire can feed extremely fast or extremely slowly by controlling it via their body's innate magic. It's why every feeding from the neck or thigh doesn't kill a subject.

            + The Kiss knocks away roughly five-to-ten minutes of a subject's memory after they're fed on. They tend to fill in the blanks themselves on the circumstances they find themselves ranging from being naked in a bed, alone in a bathroom, or just having zoned out.

            + The Kiss is incredibly pleasurable and equivalent to a lengthy orgasm. If they do remember it, subjects very often let themselves be fed on the same way they'd want regular great sex. It is not hard to addict subjects to it. What they think is happening often depends on the vampire setting it up.

            + Feeding is like blood and sex for vampires combined and every night you feed, it is a rush that justifies unliving another night.

                I have to politely disagree about the sexuality.

                Newly made vampires may attempt sex, and try to cling to the human sexuality, to avoid the Beast. But it will not work. On a good day it will be like when a vampire drinks alcohol for fake being human, only to later have to spit up the alcohol.

                Most vampires quickly come to seeing sexuality as simply a tool for controlling others. They enjoy it in the same way someone might enjoy cleaning the house or some other chore. But they can lie and fake it, as a part of manipulating other people.

                The Kiss can cause people to have blackouts, but does not produce pleasure in a mortal.

                        -Vampires feel attraction to healthy seeming vessels, because the blood is likely better stuff. My headcannon is that vampires gain Attributes not by training, but by feeding on people with good (3-5) attributes that are either better than their own or perhaps have some quality they lack in their own attributes (a different kind of intellegence...) Virtues are 50/50, talents are 70/30. Skills and knowledges are 80/90% learnt and 20/10% parasitic. There'd be more impressive people in the world if vampires weren't sapping their potential.
                        I'd say good looks are a health signifier, but sexual promiscuity mitigates that with disease potential.

                        -Sex makes vampires feel good for the power dynamic/ego/humanity connection rather than the physical side of things. It feels about as good as a backrub unless your humanity is high, you use a ritual or maybe you pump some extra blood.

                                I'm going for the funny and inconsequential here rather than elite.

                                * Nero attempted to Embrace Lindsay Sterling. Kathy Glens just barely stopped him.

                                * Alan Sovereign claims he visits the Succubus Club and Red 6 to maintain ties to younger Kindred. In fact, he is addicted to hip hop and metal.

                                * Damien talks like an old man about the Anarchs, mocking them as only an Ex-Goth, Ex-Punk.

                                * Helena doesn't admit it but pretty much prefers to live as Portia and be a perpetual party girl than she does a 3000 year old methuselah.

                                * Critias has an insane amount of nastiness to say about the female Doctor on Doctor Who. It has offended many of his students and alienated what few Kindred also get it.

                                Anarchs

                                * Anita Wainwright looks eerily like Sombra from Overwatch in her new identity and people assume she's cosplaying her.

                                * Gerard is in love with Anita and she is completely oblivious to it.

                                * Bobby Weatherbottom remains at the top of the hackers of the world because very little about hacking is actually technical, just socially done.

                                * He is ADDICTED to Mr. Robot.

                                                Some more Chicago bits

                                                * Modius secretly controls a number of Freemason lodges under the erroneous impression this gives him vast occult power.

                                                * A bunch of Chicago Order of Hermes members believe Modius is the most powerful vampire in the city due to his control over Freemason lodges.

                                                * Modius has discovered what a cellphone is and is devoted to the Angry Birds.

                                                * Allicia loves Daisy Ridley for her role in Murder on the Orient Express and has since gotten into "The Star Wars" because of her.

                                                * Bennet Steadman is utterly in love with Evelyn Stephens and has an equal amount of feeling for Maldavis. Both ignore him and it really annoys him as he's not used to being blown off by anyone.

                                                * Bennet's plans on betraying Adze and Erzulie are due to the fact he's not quite as comfortable with their "lifestyle" as most other vampires are. Kevin Jackson finds this irritating but is slowly leading him on to believe he'll receive Adze's former holdings.

                                                * The song "Stagger Lee" is about Adze. Sweetie Pepper loves it.

                                                * Malkavians have been known to hear the Shaft theme play whenever Adze enters a room. Indeed, in Chicago it is quite common for many Kindred to have theme music in the Cobweb.

                                                * Crook was inspired to make his bars by imagining a Long John Silvers you could drink at. Only in Chicago would this work.

                                                * Thea Noel MacCraine has been on "The Real Princesses of Chicago" and has been invited back to do a follow-up due to her insane catty behavior. She's a favorite due to the allusions to Satanism, orgies, and drugs that were from before she was a Ministry member.

                                                * Noah "Flyboy" Grewal is immune to Presence. This has utterly confused and fascinated Helena. His ambivalence to her charms is like catnip. I mean, she's still one of the most beautiful women in the world but Noah generally prefers women who aren't "skinny ass white girls."

                                                * Olaf One Eye is a Vicissitude-sculpted Jan Pieterszoon Dominated into thinking he's ANOTHER famous presumed-dead Ventrue, placed there by Vykos to hide him in what is an incredibly convoluted or just simply sadistic plan.

                                                * Kevin Jackson actually hates rap, R&B, and hip hop. He considers this factoid something that is genuinely dangerous to come out to his followers. Even more dangerous is his love of country. The only clue to this is his cellphone full of Nashville hits and collection of Johnny Cash vinyl records.

                                                * Horatio Ballard is unaware of the horrifying and disgusting rumors about him spread by Son. He is a bastard, truly, but not THAT sort of sicko.

                                                * The curse of Billy the Goat on Wrigley Field was actually another example of Ballard's revenge. It being overcome was at great expense by the Anarchs.

                                                * Despite this, Ballard LOVES baseball and it is the one thing in the world that unironically brings out his jolly jovial side. Night games bring him out with a custom-made hat, pennant, and catcher's mitt.

                                                * Al Capone grew sick of being a gangster decades ago but knows image is everything among the Kindred. He actually made far more money with a money-laundering scheme's legitimate side in the 80s than he ever did on hooch and it's only grown since. It's ridiculous, really, but paid off. I mean, who the hell would buy bottled water?

                                                * Maxwell's mortal name was Jean Baptiste Point du Sable. As such, he REALLY has a claim to Chicago's Princedom: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_B...Point_du_Sable
