    Initial impression of Gehenna War book.

    12-03-2024, 07:18 PM
    My first impression of the sourcebook is favorable as it brings the war of the ancients much closer to the New World and what are some of the signature characters thoughts on what is happening or what they think is starting with some of the cults.

    Some of the new merits and flaws make sense in this scenario and there is some additional character concept ideas to work with and I do like the additional powers and bonuses that my 11th generation Malkavian could learn though it does look like that high level elder and Methuselah powers are still off limits for the current setting.

    All in all it was a good purchase and see what stuff we can repurpose for our game.

    "Power that needs to advertise itself is already losing" Grand Admiral Thrawn

    "Coronation Starscream? This is bad comedy!" Galvatron

      I liked the book very much. I loved the ideas for elder Methuselah and Blood Gods powers, but I must say that I'm not a big fan of unstatted characters and powers. Still it's by far my favourtrite V5 book, as it's at least a very good attempt to bring back the importance of the notion of "cosmic horror" to the setting (something V5 basically tryed to wipe out in the beginning).

      Me and my friends were even joking that for the idea for V5 to be "street level", it's funny that their ideas for powers for Methusaleh and Blood Gods are sometimes even more impressivethan the ones we have in V20. Best v5 book so far, made me even think about adapting my table to V5 (but, it's unfortunately too much work for little gain, I still think).

                      For what it's worth, when I ran TC I reverse engineered a formula for elder and methusleah experience allocation based on elder creation rules, published NPC Stat blocks, and storyteller rules of thumb for NPC generation. It held up exceedingly well until generating characters of 3,000 years of age or older, or so...at which point there started to be more experience points to spend than there were dots on the sheet, reasonable or otherwise.

                      For funsies, i made Japheth according to his published (not canonically sensible) age by my formula. After giving him literally every attribute, ability, and discipline at 9 (accounting for out of clan cost), maxed virtues, every background available at 5, I still had about a thousand experience to spend. I had to conclude at that point, even bothering to stat out methuselahs is kind of pointless.

                      Hilariously enough, ur-Shulgi is woefully weak as compared to what it should be according to White Wolf's own rules.

                      "Statless" methuselahs isn't so much to preserve mystique, I think, as it is just shorthand for "they have everything maxed, it's pointless to create a stat block to demonstrate this".
