    How Many Tzimisce Antediluvians there are? (other Antes too)

    10-04-2021, 11:47 PM
    I mean, sometimes, it looks like some Antediluvians are more than in one place at the same time, and that would be fine, but mostly it is treated as 'one of this just can't be it', but the case where this is least apparent is the Eldest Tzimisce. And i would like to keep track of the blood sucker mess, before I tried to list the others.

    TZIMISCE ELDEST LINES
    •Well, first there seems to be only one, that was in the First City and all that, and then went to Transylvania and made dealings with Kupala, maybe freeing it and getting some power in return, but anyway, it survived the Deluge. After it, during the Second Baali War, Samiel went to him and killed the old bastard, and he took Samiel down with him. The bogatyri left and went to the four corners (supposedly each carry a bit of his flesh, so there's already wide plausible opportunity). It seems though, the Eldest's most core self germinated in the womb of Dracon, his favored childe, which shows that he is capable of doing this switch (although it might only be possible to do in direct children who are powerful enough to sustain it). After a hard pregnancy to say the least, the dead father-child was born and Dracon gave it to Yorak and his Cathedral of Flesh. All this happened in Ancient Times. [I call this form the "Cathedralic" one]

    •We got then an interesting albeit disconnected one, that is the god-tree form, that was under the care of Damek Ruthvan in his Sarmizegetusa domain, where a giant labyrinthian forest lies within a mountain of sorts. There is a tree there, that, in 1232(33) Ilias cel Frumos, when he was there with Myca Vykos and Malachite searching for info on Nikita of Sredetz, a huge old-ass tree that emanates what Ilias, a koldun sorcerer, believes is the power of a god, and when he is embracing it (more the tree embracing him) he asks what this god wants, but it couldn't respond, but gave him a red seed. Some time later, when Nikita of Sredetz (actually Dracon) faced the three (Ilias, Vykos, Malachite) plus Markus Musa Giovanni, Ilias had the previous night (or so) eaten the seed in a ritual looking for answers. So, when Dracon appeared he recognized not Ilias, but his creator, the Tzimisce Eldest. I'm a bit confused who is simply destroyed and who is melded together by the Eldest, but Ilias self is destroyed (and supposedly his body turns to ash for it can't contain the power of the elder for long), but I assume the Eldest turned Dracon and Vykos into balls of flesh and ate them (he does this to Vykos at least), so... Ilias, Vykos and Dracon, plus something if not the whole of the Eldest become one. It seems the purpose of the Eldest was meld Dracon with someone who wanted to live, because Dracon wanted to die, but the Eldest wanted him to live on. So we might say Vykos is carrying or is in some form the Eldest.
    This goes in three hypothesis then; First, the god-tree might have been given to the Ruthvans by Yorak, since they are a family who cares for the Eldest, so it would be the Cathedralic one turned into what I would call the "Arboreal" one. Second, the Eldest still lives partially as the Arboreal one, even if it gave one part of itself to Ilias. Third is that the Eldest didn't simply got destroyed after it imparted Dracon in Vykos, but actually is part of Vykos as well (which at this point might as well be Sascha Vykos).
    All in all there might be one Eldest, the Cathedralic that was given to the Ruthvens and made into the Arboreal one, which then lives on as Vykos. There might also be a Cathedralic one that lives as the Cathedral of Flesh, an Arboreal one that lives in Damek's domain yet, and one that lives in Vykos. Technically, at least one must be true, but all could be.

    •Alright now, last one is the Eldest who was in Sernog Monastery in 1413, when Lugoj Blood-Breaker, Lambach, Velya and other Anarchs (and technically the Transylvania Chronicles PCs too) raided the place, killed its guardians and Lugoj diablerized the Eldest in human form. First, they found this location because Lambach Ruthven knew the place, his family being one that always served the Eldest closely (with all that possibility that Lambach does all he does in purpose, and isn't simply an idiot coward, but it doesn't matter that much). Second, Lugoj didn't diablerise shit, sometime, some place (possibly during the raid on the monastery), the Eldest impaled the sucker on a wooden hook, took Lugoj's form, and created a dummy of an Antediluvian who he diablerized for the appearance of a successful Anarch incursion. He made a brief speech and went into torpor shortly after, calling Lambach "a good lad", for he knew everything but couldn't bring himself to say it at the time. I call this the "Monastic" one, and its origins aren't clear for me. Either way, "Lugoj" (Eldest) was taken much later by the Zantosa revenants to NYC underground, where he basically turned into Kars from Jojo and tried every single living thing on Earth, basically trying to achieve the Metamorphosists dream goal. Becoming the giant flesh beast that ate Lambach's arm when he went down there to see it. This Eldest was being taken care of by powerful Tzimisces, one of which was the then dead ilias cel Frumos, according to Beckett's Jihad Diary. But the Antediluvian form skipped town, and nobody knows where he went.
    So... what Eldest went to the Sernog Monastery? The Cathedralic? Did it matured into an independent form nurtured by Yorak and the Cathedral of Flesh? Was it all just like a new extension of the Cathedral of Flesh planted there in Sernog and didn't actually had a human form until it disguised itself as Lugoj? Because it couldn't be the (possible) one that Vykos had in himself. So it could be the Arboreal one, but that was replanted there in Sernog Monastery, maybe just a new seed that the Ruthven family planted there and it turned into a new form (the Monastic) that did all the Lugoj switcharoo. Either way, this form went to become the most famous and freaky mountain of flesh in the subways of NYC for most of The Masquerade's metaplot.

    -Possible Forms:
    1. Any of the flesh bits that the dispersed Bogatyri carried.
    2. Any of his childer of the 4th Generation.
    3. The Cathedralic one purely (a dead fetus basically) that was cared by Yorak.
    4. Part of the Cathedral of Flesh, that ended up eating Yorak in 1493.
    5. The Arboreal one in Damek's Ruthven domain.
    6. Part of Sascha Vykos, since 1232 or 1233.
    7. Fake Lugoj's sprawling form in the USA, the Monastic one.

    so yeah, people, what do you think? 1 and 2 are cheating basically, but which you think are more true? are any of my three main lines wrong or misinterpreted?

    Strange... When coincidence seems too convenient, I prefer to call it fate.

          I always liked the notion from Revised that the True Antediluvians (i.e. the genuine pre-flood variety not late-comer diablerists like Tremere and Giovanni) were all looking for ways to transcend their vampiric state… Malkav became the Malkavian Madness Network. Ennoia earthmelded into the heart of the world and, heck, might be acting as substitute Gaia at this stage if the W5 stuff where the original was ganked during the Apocalypse is true. Lasombra became the Abyss.

          In that version Tzimisce became Vicissitude (basically taking the soul-eater concept and applying it to the Founder’s plans for trancendence). There is no single Tzimisce, just his will subtlely guided every practitioner of his Art.

          I mean, Saulot has divided his consciousness into at least two vessels… it’s not exactly a crazy notion that virtually all of the original Antediluvians have either died or transcended by now and what’s left are just shells, pretenders and a couple of oddballs who couldn’t get their acts together and actually aren’t any more potent than a Methuselah as a result.

          Let’s see;
          - Haqim: unknown
          - Brujah: diablerized early, Troile in torpor beneath salted earth of Carthage.
          - Ennoia: merged with the Earth and became something else.
          - Lasombra: merged with the Abyss and became something else.
          - Malkav: became the Madness Network.
          - Absimiliard: possibly Obfuscated himself out of existence… or sitting at the bottom of the ocean making monsters.
          - Ravnos: may have become something more, but was obliterated by plot device.
          - Arikel/Ishtar: may or may not be porn star in Hollywood (it’s so ridiculous it must be true).
          - Saulot: possibly split into white worm and child.
          - Cappadocius: partially diablerized and became spectre, possibly as part of failed plan to diablerize God.
          - Tzimisce: became Vicissitude inside every practitioner.
          - Ventru/Typhon: probably diablerized by Set and absorbing him may have resulted in a composite personality Set-Typhon.

          That would account for all the 13 Clans (with a couple of unknowns), but is technically one Antediluvian short. If you wanted to run with it, I’d suggest Haqim and Absimiliard having achieved some type of transcendence such that only the lost hedonist Arikel and the latecomers still banging about as corporeal entities.

              Limiting The Eldest's possession to just Its immediate childer, what a generous interpretation! I don't imagine the Eldest as Vicissitude Itself, but rather the master of the Discipline to such an extent that It might as well be.

              I don't know if I care as much about trying to nail down how many forms the Eldest might be split between, but to respond to the examples listed above:

              I've always considered the original Cathedral of Flesh more as a distinct entity than an extension of the Eldest. As vulnerable to domination by the Eldest as anything else Vicissitude based, most likely, but not anymore a specific part of the Eldest Itself than Yorak or anything else linked to it by blood.

              I tend to view the God-Tree as a phase, an early experiment comparable to what ended up happening under New York, trying to figure out if it could cheat the limits of the Curse by assuming a radically different biology. I had always assumed It eventually took on a different shape and left, but I haven't read the Clan Novels so there could be a bunch that I am missing.

              Continuing from the "never read the novel" point, my knowledge of what happened with Vykos, the Dracon, and the Eldest/Ilias is rather limited. But I tended to assume that what was put into Vykos was primarily the Dracon, not the Eldest Itself. But then, my views about the omnipresence of the Eldest in Its bloodline make the idea of it "putting itself" into Vykos rather redundant.

              I don't think I ever really considered the question of whether or not the Eldest still parades Its Lugoj-suit around sometimes, to reinforce the idea that It's gone. I probably ended up assuming continuity, generally: that the Eldest reborn from the Dracon became the God-Tree, which eventually became Fake-Lugoj, which eventually became The Thing Beneath New York.

              A related question, one I find rather interesting and suggests a significant shift in the portrayal of the Eldest, has to do with the reappearance of Ilias in BJD.
