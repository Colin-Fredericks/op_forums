Is it possible to have Setites who reject the idea of Set and continue to follow whatever mask they were originally devoted to? Sure, but those would be in the minority. Loyalty to Set is something that the Setites would focus on with new recruits, and anyone who was able to mask their loyalties for such a long time, before, during and after the Embrace would be an exception.

                But in v5, apparently those Setites aren't just a small number, but actually a large portion of the clan (and seemingly always have been). You've also got this new reinterpetation of Set which is apparently so common that together these two groups have been able to entirely subsume identity of the clan throughout almost the whole of the world.                 Now there would certainly have been ways that this could be explained. For example, maybe several of the Hierophants have awoken and claim that Set spoke to them. Non-Egyptian Setites are now anathema and are to be sentenced to the Eye of Ra.                 Instead though we end up with this new interpretation of Set, which is apparently now the default interpretation for Setites (Ministry) throughout the setting, and also those Setites who worship beings other than Set went from being the exception to being incredibly common. And the problem is that it doesn't feel like there's any reason for why this massive and sudden shift occurred. That's why it feels very hamfisted and unnatural.

                So to be clear I'm perfectly fine with zealot cultists having internal schismatic devisions, but I would prefer to have seen those either occur more organically or for there to be a good reason for this to have suddenly happened besides, "New Edition, New Ideas."

                        I think the issue is that there was never a proper section on integration. People go "So this is a Camarilla city, but this section is a Barony. That means Anarchs. Now If I want some FoS/Giovanni I should... Are they guests, do they have representation, do I need to give them a whole city on their own?" Because it sounds like it sometimes "Cairo is Settite, Las vegas is Giovanni, Venice is Giovanni except on thursdays... I guess they really keep to themselves"

                        Really, the Independent clans having their own cities is kinda... dumb. Clans, and this goes triple for independent clans, are specialised and favour some disciplines over others, so it works better for everyone to have a division of labour/separate competitive interests. Giovanni are a prime example. If the city's full of necromancers, the city's corpses/bloodbanks have too much competition, while many good sources of blood remain unmolested. At the same time, the Value of necromancy services is very limited, because everyone can do it. A city of electricians needs a plumber.

                        Realistically, how to incorporate other clans is probably going to be up to the prince; Are they given their own baronies or ghettos, or are they treated like normal vampires of the 7 main clans. Do they have a primogen representative or do they borrow the power of an existing primogen (Perhaps The necromancers use the Nosferatu, The Lasombra and Tzmisce usually use the Ventrue, The Settites alternate between Malavians or Toreador, The Assamites are often under the control of the Sherrif, the Ravnos are sent to the sun) . However, because this isn't discussed at all, it does get a little dicey for new storytellers to come up with ideas that sound 'right' (as in, not a house rule).
