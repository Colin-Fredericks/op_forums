            Thanks, Taggie! I appreciate you taking time to give us your review.

            1:] I'm very confused by this description as I felt like the Lasombra Clan write-up in CBN5E *OVER-emphasized* the role of faith. The Lasombra haven't been involved in mortal churches and religions for 600 years since they were driven out by the Inquisition but the book suggests that a major reason the Camarilla would want the Lasombra was their heavy involvement in churches and how that could be used against the Second Inquisition. I'm not sure where you got the idea the Lasombra are being forced to have Touchstones either since Talley is the only character in the book who doesn't have any. Proof the Sabbat don't bother with them.

            2:] I felt it was there to help emphasize the Lasombra are evil and corrupt in a way that is innately inhuman rather than clinging to Humanity. I'm not really sold on it, though, because I don't think vampire should ever have a "One Ring" or "Dark Side of the Force" effect. You should lose Humanity because you violate the tenets you believe in, not because of evil magic.

            3:] Honestly, I feel like the Camarilla being champions of Humanity was always stupid and I wish in Revised they'd had Roads and Paths be a thing more Elders are on. The Camarilla is corrupt, vicious, and inhuman in ways that only Sabbat Elders could hope to match. I can't imagine them caring about Humanity and keeping vampires on it unless it's part of some larger plan to cripple the Neonates and keep them weak. Which is possible but not the way they're presnted. If I'd rebooted the Camarilla I'd make Roads and Gehenna Cults go hand in hand.

            I mean, it's LAUGHABLE that the Camarilla would give a shit about humanity. They're the boot on humanity's face and the reason the world sucks.

            If Cults get rid of Touch Stones and tenets in favour of a proper morality system, not the perverted sadistic disgusting ,tamagotchi Sim we have now, and don't involve Loresheets, they may help.

They can be True Love or the one good thing in your vampire's life. You have a serious issue with abusive relationships in gaming and good for you but that's not something that I think the game is advocating for.
