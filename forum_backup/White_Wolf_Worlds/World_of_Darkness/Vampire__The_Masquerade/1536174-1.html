    What comes to mind would be the following places:

    The first isn't even a Haven properly. One player simply had a van that he "bought" from a "former drug dealer" and it had a compartment in the floor that he used to hide "merchandise", but he expanded this compartment to use as an emergency shelter and made a point of reporting that it was only big enough for one person. In fact, there was a time when he woke up in a towed vehicle parking lot.

    Another once had a Gangrel that had zero dots in resources and therefore slept, in the literature section of the public library. It was supposed to be a joke, but that was never a problem, I don't know if that's very realistic.

    I already had a Toreador who was a college student before the embrace. Her haven was the attic of a disused dormitory, but it still received power and even water, it could be used so much that it didn't attract too much attention. The character was a hacker so she also kept a lot of equipment there, as well as clothes and other amenities.
