                                                        About that shovelhead discussion thing, the White Wolf Wiki gives an insight on it: "In 1st and 2nd editions of Vampire: The Masquerade, Sabbat vampires were created through an initiation ceremony. Sabbat initiates were Embraced by their sire, bashed over the head with a shovel (to knock them unconscious before they Frenzy), thrown in an open grave, and buried. The whelp then had to dig his way out of the grave, during which time he would be dogged with bizarre hallucinatory visions thanks to the Embrace's mystical properties. When (or if) the neonate emerged, he was completely stripped of his humanity and ready to learn how to be a Sabbat."

                                                        I think its kinda weird, to Embrace each one of the drained mortals, wait some secs. beat them in the head to KO them (I would expect the frenzy to kick before that and render KOs null), and then throw them in the grave. But, its better that than nothing at all.
