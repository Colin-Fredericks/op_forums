    An ancient Greek Perspective On Kindred Mythology

    08-14-2022, 03:54 PM
    Disclaimer - In real life, I am not Greek. This is written from the point of view of a 6th Generation Methuselah in my setting who was originally from Thrace. It's always been my feeling that Kindred who are older than 1000 years old would have distinctly different views on Caine, the Book of Nod, and Cainite lore. In my setting, the legends of Caine and Enoch in their modern form appeared 500 years ago, with wildly contradiction accounts. Vampires lie, after all. As always, your Masquerade will vary.

    An ancient Perspective On The Book Of Nod and Cainite Legendry
    Young man, let me make a few things perfectly clear - your understanding of the Book of Nod is a purely Christian understanding. However, both the vampiric condition and our eschatology is older than your “Caine” myth. I am over 3000 years old and I was a student of Aristotle, sitting at his feet while concealing my nature. Most assuredly, the Kindred of my time were not christians. Your Book of Nod is a Christian invention, written from a Christian perspective. Everything in the Chronicle of Shadows and other Kindred mythologies committed to non-oral media are mythologies. Kindred lie, fledgling, and our curse is deeper and more ancient than what can be contained within any mythology.

    What is fascinating is that in all the mythology of the Kindred that I have heard from Zhonghu, Bharat, Anahuac and many other lands, is that there are always a council of progenitors that usually are 7, or 9, or 13, or 108 and a fallen city where they all dwell or place from which they hail. Aside from that, the myths are varied. Each culture has its own understanding of vampires. Some become culturally aware before the Abrahamic religions, and some after. But Kindred existed in nearly all lands before the rise of the various Yahweh doctrines.

    It is my feeling that the Kindred curse is terrifying, deep and unfathomable, and that we Kindred construct these myths in order to understand our curse. As a student of Aristotle, I am a materialist. While I have seen much in my three millennia, I have seen nothing that can absolutely convince me of the objective truth of any set of spiritual beliefs, especially since in my travels I have witnessed phenomena that *contradict each other.* I have seen gods in Zimbabwe, Roman Lares, nymphs and fauns, and all measures of wonders. Everything that can be conceived of factually exists. Human wizards have a saying - truth until paradox. They are absolutely correct.

    This is also one of the reasons why the Sabbat hunt ancients like me. You see, they *know* that their Christian eschatology is just a myth, but can’t bear to accept that truth. So, they hunt down people who are evidence of it, like me.

    Because I am feeling generous tonight, let me share with our myth of the Kindred, from the times when I was a Neonate, like you.

    The Tale of the Children of Orpheus and the exile from the Underworld

    Orpheus was the first Kindred, and Eurydice was the first Wraith. Back when you drew breath, you were told the tale of Orpheus and Eurydice, but were not told the entirety of it.

    When Orpheus quested to find his wife, he found her in the court of Hades. Hades allowed Orpheus and Eurydice to be together, but Orpheus was bade that he must now live as a shade of the dead - supping on emotions and memories, and never invading the sunlit world unless bade by the Furies. For a long time, Orpheus and Eurydice were happy together. As a demigod, he even had children with his beloved.
    Indeed, Orpheus had 9 children. I will share the names which you know:

    Tyrannos, the Chief: He was the eldest of the Orpheans. Skilled in rhetoric, charismatic and enduring, he led the children of Orpheus.

    Oneiropólos - The Visionary, the child of dreams. She was always dreaming, and so was regarded to be mad, but also a prophet.

    Χακίμ or Hakim the Vizier. He was the oracle amongst the children, and first developed Blood Sorcery. He spent much time among the Persian magi.

    Skotia - She who was shadow, who most deeply understood the underworld. She was darkness, and commanded it.

    Kappadokíou - The sage of the dead. Along with Skotia, he knew the lore of the ghosts and inhabitants of Hades’ realm best, and was also a priest.

    Eros - The son of Ares and Aphrodite, the subject of the myth of Eros and Psyche. Psyche eventually grew old and died, and Eros the godling followed her into the Underworld, and he was welcomed by Orpheus and Hades. Eros was the artist of their tribe, still creating works of beauty to honor his beloved wife and mother. His jealous mother didn't appreciate the fact that he gave up Olympus for her, and had the arrogant boy cursed to remain in the underworld. It is sometimes argued that Eros was the ghost and that Psyche was adopted into the clan of Orpheus, becoming the blood drinker instead.

    Logios - The debater. The sage among the children of Orpheus, who challenged Tyrannos at every turn.

    Lykos - The Wolf. She was a friend of the shapeshifters and went on their hunts.

    Síranges - The Tunneler, who had befriended and then betrayed the furies. Their descendants pursue the tunnelers, hungry for eternal vengeance.

    The Hydra - he who lurked with the powers of chaos in the depths, he was called The Dragon, and could take any form, or mold others, and was always, always hungry.

    These were the clan names of my time.

    Orpheus and Eurydice raised their children in the underworld. They fed from rivers of blood and blood fruits, and never hungered. For creatures such as them, the underworld was paradise.

    When Orpheus and his wife sought to leave the underworld, his children followed them. It was appropriate, for unlike their children, Orpheus and Eurydice were man and woman, and were permitted to die a human death. But the 9 had been born in the Underworld, and had walked the Elysian fields and Tartarus. They were not of the sunlight world.

    After consulting with Athena and the Furies, Hades permitted the children to follow their parents. However, Hades bade them not to look back, or they would be cursed. Eurydice did, and was taken back into the underworld, and became the very first Wraith. The children of Orpheus, born in the underworld, had never truly died, and so were undead. They were also demigods, of the 3rd generation from Olympus, being that their father Orpheus was a child of great
    Apollo. Terribly, Apollo regarded this as hubris as well - this is why all Orpheans are burned by fire and sunlight, their grandfather Apollo seeking to return them to Hades, who is their true lord.

    To stave off the call back to Hades, Orpheans always, always hunger for the blood of living things. They can not sustain themselves on food and drink, and can only take what is offered during sacrifice - blood. However, Hades will have his subjects return, and as they defy the will of the gods, they become more and more monsters, in truth. They are supposed to be dead, and their very existence defies the will of the Olympians.

    Orpheus created a city with his children, meant to be a symposia for all learning, but that is a tale for another time.

    The others, such as Ravannah, Sutekh or that 3 eyed sage? They weren’t Greek, so I can’t speak about them.

    Xerphones of Thrace, 2022 AD, Boston

          I just switched out the Biblical Flood with the Flood of Deucalion of Greek Myth.           In my chronicles, while the Book of Nod is pre-dominantly monotheistic myth that formed around 1500 years ago as Christianity displaced the Classical pagan religions, there are plenty of Noddist scholars who incorporate earlier myths in their quest for understanding of their own origins.
