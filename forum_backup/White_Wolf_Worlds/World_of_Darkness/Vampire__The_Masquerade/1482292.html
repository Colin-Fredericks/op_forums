    Demons in VTM lore vs Demons in DTF lore

    05-29-2022, 06:03 PM
    Demons in VTM lore are shown to be extremely cruel and powerful beings with extremely strange and terrifying powers that would make any vampire scream and run to their sires.

    However in DTF lore, demons are perhaps the weakest supernatural's to walk the earth. Their powers, known as lore's, are either discount disciplines, or knock off blood magic paths. I am not joking when I say that you could put an earthbound up against an old clan tzimisce elder, and expect to see the elder teabag the earthbound while its cultists start running from the elders ghouls.

    Compared to the disciplines that vampires posses, the lore's of the fallen are pretty pathetic. Now don't get me wrong, when I mean pathetic, I mean pathetic for a demon, a fallen angel that has been around since the beginning. The lore's feel better suited for a vampire than creatures like them.

    How about we talk a bit about cultists, let me remind you all that the earthbounds who have powerful cults honestly lucked out, you could look through the wiki page of most earthbounds and see that while the earthbound went to sleep for centuries, their cultists were able to grow strong without any of their help. Unlike vampire elders that either built or took control of a company/industry by the sheer power of their ambition and confidence, and now control a good portion of a country's government. In other words, a vampire elder is probably better than any demon when it come to leadership.

    And honestly, what can the fallen give you anyway that a vampire cant? I can assure you that a vampire can give you anything a fallen can, maybe even better.

    Lets not forget that regular fallen need a human host or else they are screwed. This means all you need is a mortal with a gun/knife/baseball bat/anything that can be used as a weapon, and you have technically slayed a demon.

    Oh and blood is easier to get than faith, case closed.

    *In all honesty Kupala should not have been an earthbound, he should have been some powerful nature spirit, because looking at the powers an earthbound posseses, it makes no sense why the eldest would have formed an alliance with him.

    *I know that vampires who serve demons are supposed to be hated by the rest, but if you're going to serve a fallen from the DTF, I feel like you're more likely to be laughed at. So it makes more sense that the baali serve creatures like neverborn or banes.

    I could be wrong, so correct me if I am.

                              While I completely agree that the "demons" VtM talks about usually aren't Fallen, but any number of other beings, I think the arguments on the Fallen in the OP are at best simplistic.

                              Comparing Lores to Disciplines isn't an objective thing because Demon never had a Second edition and was published in a tight schedule, so their design suffers a lot. Even then they're a mixed bag, some are weak, some are pretty strong, and in general they have pretty easy access to even their most obscene powers in comparison to other supernaturals.

                              And outside that, their Innate Powers combined are what makes the Fallen extremely dangerous if they're cunning. They can perceive supernatural powers from miles of distance, ignore the most common non-physical effects that could hinder them and with a very basic setup for a Demon they can come back from the dead freely in a new body. Combat will depend a lot on the details of a given Apocalyptic Form.

                              Lores usually have no upper limit on how much the effect improves with successes, and the Fallen can use their Thralls to gather huge amounts of dice easily. Faith also definitely isn't harder to get than blood: each Thrall you make gives you Faith every day for as long as the Thrall survives, regardless of distance or anything else. Getting enough Thralls to fill completely every day isn't hard, and you can go further and travel the globe once in a while to setup so you collect Faith several times a day.

                              If we talk about Infernalism and Pacts, though, most of this is irrelevant. Vampires summon entities they can't comprehend to bargain their souls. Those entities don't need to be specially strong, they need to convince the vampire, and to be able to pull off the Pact. Fallen are perfectly capable of doing that. In the context of a Pact they get a lot of leeway outside their usual power range based on the Faith Potential of the future Thrall, most boons described can be accomplished with relative ease in this setup. On top of that the Fallen gets permanent attachment to the Thrall that can be used in several ways, including draining Willpower and Health to death with just a thought and with no resistance whatsoever.

                                                      That's not so simple.                                                       Incidentally, I had forgotten to talk about the powers of the Earthbound, that the OP didn't seemed to understand since they have this exact feature: the ability to extend anything they do to Biblical Proportions. In addition to new Lores and more powerful Thralls, more worshipers and opportunities to extend their senses and so the reach of their Lore, they can increase the magnitude of said Lore to affect large areas for huge amounts of time, easily outdoing Elder levels of Disciplines for a fraction of the effort. A full power Earthbound (Faith 10, Mastery 5) spends far less XP on that than a Methuselah getting to 9 in a Discipline and can expand any power in area with a base radius of 10 yards (a very common thing) to a 5681.8 miles radius, which is enough to engulf the entirety of the Americas and then some, and this can be applied with equal ease to all their Lores.

                                                      Originally posted by Hello View Post
                                                      Doesent that require a lot of faith though?
                                                      Define "a lot".

                                                      We're talking three Backgrounds here, Cult, Worship, and Mastery. They're as hard to get as common Backgrounds goes, and Fallen can buy Backgrounds with XP like Mages. Cult defines how often the Earthbound get Faith, varying from 1/month to every day. Worship defines how much Faith you reap each time, which is double its rating. Mastery defines how many times you can extend a parameter of a Lore by a factor of 10 directly 1:1. Each such increase spends 1 temporary Faith.

                                                      An Earthbound with each of those at 3, strong but nothing exceptional, gets 6 Faith every week and spends 3 to maximize a given parameter. That's enough to increase the area of a storm, for example, from 10 yards radius times Permanent Faith to almost 6 miles radius times Permanent Faith, which for an Earthbound can be easily 6 or more, so little less than a diameter of 72 miles. If the Lore itself requires Faith expenditure that would be 4, which is sensible, but still something this character can do reliably every week if they want to.

                                                      Now, Earthquake uses successes to determine area, which isn't common, allocating 3 successes to it makes for an area of Permanent Faith in square miles, the above All 3 Then 6 Earthbound would be capable of covering an area of 6000 square miles, which seems about right for the coast of LA alone and more than enough to take the entire city (but only 1/6th of the Metro area). That's uncommon as an area definer and naturally makes for a smaller one than the usual radius base of most area-affecting Lore. An All Max Demon would be able to affect a 1,000,000 square miles area for 6 Faith that replenish completely every day, which is sufficient for almost a sixth of the entire Russian area, more than a fourth of the US, about 4/5th of India, and enough to be the 10th country in the world by Area.
