    Originally posted by The Gentleman Gamer View Post
    My intention with that section (where Koldunic Sorcerers jealously guard their power) stems back to Tzimisce territoriality, the idea of the crotchety old mage in his tower, and to act as a reason for Tzimisce disunity when opposing the more organised and cohesive Tremere. I feel it stands up.
    Though this post is seven weeks old, i only read it just now and feel compelled the reply to it now.

    As an explanation of Tzimisce disunity that section manages to be both wrong and unnecessary. It's wrong because most Tzimisce are not Kolduns, so establishing that the Kolduns don't get along with each other fails to explain the behaviour of the rest of the clan. It's unnecessary because it was already established that vampires in general are factitious and prone to infighting, with Tzimisce Voivoides in particular being a jealous and territorial lot who see each other as their only true rivals, and everyone else as nothing more than temporary inconveniences. The Tremere on the other hand have themselves been established as a highly unified clan with a rigid top-down structure. That the Tzimisce would take a long time to present a unified front and the Tremere would do so from the beginning is both self-evident, and detailed in every source on the Omen War. No further explanation is necessary.

    That's not the worst part though, the worst part is that this makes it very difficult to actually play a Koldun. A crotchety old mage in his tower is an NPC archetype that is very difficulty to do as a PC. Honestly this is a problem that affects the whole Kraina system, it really feels like it's meant for non-player characters, which is rather unlike the version of Koldunic sorcery presented in Player's Guide to the High Clans and reprinted in V20. The Krainas had interesting ideas, like the notion that each region has its native Koldunic path, and that each Koldun finds it easiest to invoke the path of their homeland, but the execution left a lot to be desired. Making path weaker outside their region of origin, for example, is something that doesn't affect NPCs who are likely stay in one place, but absolutely affects PCs who are likely to move from place to place. Also i honestly found some of the powers somewhat underwhelming.

    The mechanics of Koldunic Rivalry are what really makes Kolduns unplayable. Vampire is a game that rewards patience and planning, so your sorcery forcing you to immediately attack your rivals directly is tantamount to your sorcery forcing you to commit suicide. Seriously, what kind of self-respecting Koldun makes a beeline for a rival source of power, instead of enacting long term plan to bring the opposing Koldun to heel and then forcing their secrets out of them?

    Frankly the whole thing was just executed in an extremely clumsy way. If you had simply written mechanics through which one Koldun could steal knowledge from another, then jealousies and rivalries would develop all on their own. Just like the mere existence of Diablerie makes low-gen vampires fear high-gen vampires and seek to control them. There's no need to have mechanics making vampires roll Willpower every time they see a lower generation vampire, or risk frenzy every time they witness an advanced discipline power. No, the mere fact that those things can be stolen by devouring someone's soul is all the incentive vampires need to drive millenia of conflict. The same principle could have been applied to Kolduns, and it would have been both more interesting and more player friendly.

        So I've read Dark Thaumaturgy chapter and it's pretty bad. In a lot of ways. Let's go through the path to see exactly what's bad about it!
        Fluff
        Retcons is the name of the day! Apparently blood magic is morally wrong because witchcraft is wrong. This is a rather bizarre surprise to spring on us at this point. Witchcraft was okay when Mage did it and it was okay for the entirety of VtM's original run but now it's suddenly bad?
        Next, Ur-Shulgi has been an infernalist all along! I told you guys that Assamites are an evil satanic cult, but no one ever listens.
        Next, more Setite fapping.
        The Setites despise Dark Thaumaturgy. As a clan devoted to their cults, families, and worshipers, they give would-be thaumaturgists advice on what deal may be good or bad. Serpents excel in earning the trust of other Cainites, though their counter-deals are said to carry as many conditions as those with demons.
        I'd understand if this said "Setites despise Dark Thaumaturgy because worshiping people other than Set is not kosher within Set's religion", but apparently it's because Setites know thaum too well? And don't Ventrue and Toreador, the original social clans, also excel in earning the trust of other Cainites? And for that matter, don't Tremere? You know, the clan that brokered the biggest vampire alliance ever? The clan that's actually in a vampire alliance unlike Setites?
        Next, new Dark Thaum nerf, as if it needed any. Dark Thaumaturgy can now be resisted with dif 9 willpower rolls, so it basically either doesn't work or critically succeeds as the target botches its roll.

        "Die Heriroschaft des Wyrm"
        First things first: "Heriroschaft" is not a real word. German word for "tyranny" is "die Tyrannei" or "die Gewaltherrschaft".
        Aaaand it only took two paragraphs into the introduction for someone to botch their writing roll!
        The zettler’s recent disappearance, and other Cainites’ subsequent discovery of his perfectly preserved scrolls detailing the Path, has led to a boutade of intrepid infernalists arising in the area.
        Didn't you asshole wrote a page ago that the only way to learn Dark Thaum is from a demon? How are people learning it from scrolls?
        The path's "price" is really not a price at all. The first part where you can roll Wits + Occult is meaningless, because no one will ever roll 5 successes on dif 10 roll.
        The second part where a person with True Faith can cancel out your spells is also meaningless, because the books keep hammering in how extremely rare the people with True Faith are.

        Malfean Infection
        No one ever will be excited to have this power. This is like "give impotence to a green-eyed blonde chinese transvestite". So you are just going to use it maybe once per campaign if you remember that it exists. But even if you do use this power - it doesn't work. This power doesn't remove the True Faith from the person, only its powers, so the person can just bless themselves whenever and be free of your dreadful curse.

        Bane to the Soul
        Are these the WtA Banes or not? If I got a WtA werewolf into the game, can they enter Umbra and punch the demon? Would their gifts work?
        Not a terrible power overall, for provoking frenzy and pyrophobia.

        Dissonant Miserere
        There's a name that flows right off your tongue.
        Through chanting, incantation, and inspired clack, the infernalist penetrates her victim’s mind with the endless toiling noise of her ravings.
        Through chanting that lasts one round that it takes you to activate the Discipline. Demons like brevity, I guess.
        Fairly nasty power, although I don't know how the victim is supposed to know that they can ask the demon to go away. Is that an innate knowledge they get when the ritual activates?
        Also does the victim have any hearing penalties for the fact that there's a constant demonic rock concert going around? You'd expect that, but I guess it's only willpower.

        Feed the Wyrm
        Who takes the aggravated damage? The text implies that it's the thaumaturge, but I suspect that the intent was for the victim to be damaged.
        Appearance does nothing and this is Dark Ages where no one sees shit without a torch anyway, so you might as well live with your tumors instead of selling out to Wyrm.
        Does this thing work on Lasombra? They don't have reflections and the demon is supposed to appear in the mirror.

        House the Maeljin
        Oooh baby! Free dots in any discipline for the low low price of roleplaying an asshole? Sold. I'm playing a vampire anyway.
        Do the Maeljin dots stack with existing dots or do you only get dots 1 and 2? Can I get elder disciplines from Maeljin? Level 11 disciplines?

        This time table is nuts. Why doesn't this use the same time table the previous powers did? Who'd want to kill their buddy only for nothing to happen because this was the end of the scene? Why does it go from One Week to Heat Death Of The Universe?

        Rego Calatio
        God punishes us for everything. Open yourself, and let the devil inside!
        — Ahab the Traitor, infernalist Salubri of the wilds
        So is this a canon statement that Ahab definitely exists and is not just a Salubri legend? This statement implies that Ahab is Christian - did he convert in the last two thousand years?

        A demon summoned by Rego Calatio remains on Earth until the sun rises, or it hears church bells.
        Apparently I need to invest into a supply of ear plugs for my demonic army or move into Syria.
        Why don't the nightmares from the Price segment use Nightmares flaw rules?

        System: Each level of this Discipline requires the sacrifice of another being’s life, the majority of demons requiring the death of an innocent in a circle of bloodied bones.
        What does the minority require? Demon summoning is effectively random, how do I tell in advance what I need? Also cats are pretty inocent, can I sacrifice cats or is this supposed to be human-only thing?
