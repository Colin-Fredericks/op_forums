                                            I'm reminded of another fandom I'm a part of with the STAR WARS universe and roleplaying game. One of the most popular villains of the Expanded Universe was Grand Admiral Thrawn who was a nuanced and fascinating character who presented the "reasonable" side of the Empire. So much so that the Empire started getting a lot of its own comics, books, and stories. There were also people who unironically argued the Empire just had some bad eggs.

                                            Unfortunately, it started hitting its nadir with Dark Horse comics STAR WARS: LEGACY where they had originally intended to write out the Jedi Knights and have Imperial Knights with the ending of the final timeline story in the EU being the rise of a new benevolent Solo-descended Empress.
                                            And fans HATED THIS.

                                            The obvious reaosn being that while the Imperial fans were a significant minority, the fact was that the "nuanced" Empire took away from the fact they were inspired by, well, all of the worst elements of empires in RL. They were ranting comic book villains and that by portraying them as misunderstood law and order types, you weren't adding nuance. No, you were simply rewriting them.

                                            It's an interesting balance to strike because I feel like that if you remove the awful parts of an organization in the World of Darkness, you aren't adding nuance. You're not even being a fan of the group because that's not the group. The Sabbat were created for the purposes of allowing vampires who were able to do the things that, by the rules, other vampires would hit wassail for.

                                            To use the immortal scene from Near Dark, the Sabbat exist so that a group of vampires can go into a bar and proceed to kill every single human in there for fun. There is no greater reason or purpose. It is to revel in the screams, terror, and helplessness of mortals that die for no reason other than a monster's hunger. That is boring as fuck if that is ALL the Sabbat is. The Sabbat is many things: post human philosophers, Noddist scholars, mystics of dark forces, and a militant theocratic army for Caine.

                                            But being an absolute violent evil asshole that would herd humans into camps to bleed like cows are milked is PART of what the Sabbat is. It's one of the things that I feel like was lost with some of the Post-Revised Technocracy. The Technocracy should have nuance, should be able to explain their goals in a coherent rationale manner. The mark of a good villain is that they can summarize how they got from Point A to Point B even if it's, "As a Bond villain I'm rich as fuck and get all the ladies and henchmen." But the Union should still be the guys who murder supernaturals indiscriminately, torture, brainwash, clone, and oppress.

                                            I'm not a fan of the Sabbat in V5 totally. I feel they have ditched a lot of what makes the sect unique but the Sabbat should never NOT be the most overtly evil and monstrous of Kindred. That is their thing and you shouldn't take that away from them.

                                            Part of what I love playing a Sabbat is being the "Only Sane Man" and my Sabbat games are often like my THE OLD REPUBLIC games where you are the only Sith Lord who isn't an self-defeating schoolyard bully.

                                                    Some rules I've used for Roleplaying my Infernalist characters:

                                                    1. Infernalists are rational

                                                    There are a bunch of bug fuck nuts evil demon-worshipers out there. Generally, you are not one of these people.

                                                    2. Infernalists have a decent argument to worship demons

                                                    You are a vampire, you are Damned. Therefore, there is no risk to damning yourself more. There is also much profit to gain.

                                                    3. Infernalists get good bang for their buck

                                                    A good infernalist knows that research is key with demons. Some are untrustworthy bastards while others will give you exactly what you want as long as you pay the price. The trick is being smart enough to know which is which.

                                                    4. Psychopaths kill for no reason, Infernalists kill for power

                                                    Yes, I quoted Grosse Pointe Blank. An Infernalist might revel in torture and evil like a Sabbat but most will be the quiet type who is measuring how many human souls he can trade for that extra Infernal investment.

                                                    5. Infernalist religion makes internal sense

                                                    God is evil
                                                    Demons are evil
                                                    One of them pays you if you work for them. God ignores you.

                                                    Which do you think is worth worshiping?

                                                    It certainly works better than Caine who ignores you and is evil.

                                                    6. Infernalists can have friends

                                                    After all, we're all damned here.

                                                    7. This is just a game so have fun with playing your evil sorcerer

                                                    Nuff said.

                                                    8. Infernalists are not slaves, well anymore than any other religion

                                                    As Dracula says in Symphony of the Night. Belial is not going to dictate how you do your nights. The Sabbat does. Its thus a nonsensical distinction. You worship the Queen of Thorned Whips because you like what she's selling.

                                                    9. You may have sold your soul but you're more likely to have PLEDGED IT.

                                                    An important distinction. Besides, you're going to Hell when you die anyway and as a vampire you can maybe buy it back, become a demon yourself, or so on.

                                                    10. Fight fire with fire

                                                    The Sabbat cannot beat the Antediluvians. Maybe Kupala can.

                                                            Hey folks,

                                                            I thought it would be a good idea to have an official place to discuss product from the Storyteller's Vault, recommend your favorite pieces, and critique the ones that you feel are worth modifying for your campaigns. I feel like with the addition of 5th Edition material to the product that it has a bigger relevence to the fandom (and it had a pretty big one before). Which is to say that there is value to the setting element that some Sabbat are soccer hooligans. One of the elements that is intristic to the setting is the loss of Humanity to become degenerate animalistic killers. To see the higher functions and genius of the vampire lost to become ravenous brutal monsters enslaved to their appetites.

                                                            I feel one of the things necessary in the Sabbat isn't that they're 'evil' but the fact the Sabbat must struggle to lose their fight against the Beast and the extreme diablerie-happy mindless violence as well as fanaticism that infects their sect. Not everyone in the Sabbat can or should be able to master the Beasts via Paths of Enlightenment but will dwell near the bottom of it. I feel like one of the struggles the Sabbat and Sabbat characters shiould have to deal with is the members of their sect who are pointlessly sadistic and evil monsters. The ones dragging down the sect with their massacres and revelries of destruction. The hunger fo diablerie and Beast that threatens all.

                                                            Because the question becomes, "Is the Sabbat worth saving?" or "Is this just the true face of vampires?"

                                                            Which is to say, "The nuance is great but it should be nuance with room for ravenous half-feral monsters like 30 Days of Night."

                                                            I also agree the new Sabbat need more enlightened philosopher kings too.
