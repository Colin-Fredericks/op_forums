                                                03-24-2019, 01:18 PM
                                                MRH created Vampire: The Masquerade in the counterculture district of Atlanta after driving through Gary and seeing the poverty as well as social devastation then driving into Chicago. It's always been an intensely political game and took a stand very early by including large number of black, female, and gay NPCs when that was controversial for a tabletop roleplaying game. It also dealt with black magic, satanic themes,redemption, and sex when those things were banned outright from Dungeons and Dragons thanks to the efforts of BADD.

                                                I think a big question is the fact that a lot of people are suddenly going to wonder what qualifies as POLITICS and need to take a moment to ask what they think is going to be talked about. Because when I hear V:TM and politics, this is what I think.

                                                * Corrupt politicians
                                                * Wealth inequality
                                                * The option to rescue someone getting beaten by white supremacists and killing them all, only to later have a mother ask you to see if you can pull her boy out of the gang.
                                                * Homelessness
                                                * Corporate coverups of unsafe products (Pentex!)
                                                * Jokes about how the locals only care about weed legalization
                                                * A corrupt church that is just a cover for a Setite cult and feeds off the runaways it "helps" vs. a rich but human one that wants to kick out all the homeless from the neighborhood.
                                                * The PCs investigate a young woman who has been assaulted horribly and vampires now want killed. She is the victim of a bad memory wipe and actually was fed on by vampires. They can kill her, fix the memory wipe, or help her get out of town. Maybe also kill the vampire but she can't expose him.

                                                WATCH_DOGS 2 is an intensely political game and it was just silly and fun.

                                                GRAND THEFT AUTO for all of its weirdness is also a very political game.

                                                Let's wait and see.

                                                I admit I'm VERY biased on this end because I write intensely political vampire fiction. All of the reviews of my books (which I THOUGHT were escapist) emphasize it.
