                                                            The Tremere aren't a perfect well oiled machine, but they're as good as you can reasonably expect a professional organization of vampires are going to get.

                                                            Secondly, look, Tremere mostly embrace the kind of people that fit in with the Tremere. A massive hierarchical structure isn't a deal breaker for most. The people who join House Carna* are the malcontents of the Tremere; the people who didn't do all that well, and of course whatever House Carna has managed to embrace in it's short existence; fledglings. Now that's not working too well. In a conflict*, My money would say the Tremere are going to win 9 times out of 10, and in any city with the presence of both there will be conflict* because nobody, particularly vampires, wants to allow a competitor organization pop up. (Also, you should consider that, if a House Carna wins, the princes will take advantage of the situation and execute the lot for their crimes against the Tremere, or at least heavily sanction them) Only in instances where the senior Tremere turn do the Carnites have a decent chance of winning.
                                                            House Carna are not going to have a wealth magical resources or books, because only an idiot would let someone photocopy their closely guarded secrets or allow some neonates to lose their treasures.. Not to mention that apparently, House Carna seeks out soothsayers and such, and Hermetic resources aren't going to work all that well for such people.

                                                            So, 'Hut' Carna is going to pop up in very few places, and almost always as a total replacement for the Tremere . Also I question how much influence Carna would even have outside her own continent.

                                                            This is all under the assumption the this split is legitimate and Carna isn't a Facade for Tremere. Honestly, when I first read Carna's stuff, I thought it was a joke/ obvious Tremere conspiracy right of the bat, but as I read more the benefit of the doubt slipped away and I'm writing with the assumption they're the real deal. It's not something for the Tremere, It's for people who don't like the Tremere (see also: Witches of Echidna)

                                                            Ippsisimus, I haven't read much of, but it sounds like it works a lot better even as a legitimate House. Siding with the victorious V5 Anarchs is a strong move in any case, and it just sounds like they're testing hermeticism rather than screwing it.
