      1. 3 founders - Nergal, Moloch, and the Unnamed. Ur-shulgi isn't one of the original 3, as he was embraced after the Baali became a threat great enough to get the clans to work together.

      2. There have been numerous "Shaitan" after Nergal was supposedly destroyed. And all claimed leadership of the Baali.

      3. The Baali are a little of both. They are a clan by embrace and a cult by the apostate ritual to convert a vampire to the clan.

      3.5. The main difference between infernalist and Baali is clan, really. Baali are unique in their discipline spread and clan weakness. Infernalists can be any clan or even mortal. Demonic influence isall that is required.

      4. No one knows for sure who embraced the 3. They don't even know, or are willing to talk about it. No one is exactly willing to keep them alive long enough to find out, the Baali are a threat to all other vampires. Cappadocious is speculated because of the "Ashur" name, which is what he went by as a mortal. Old clan Tzimisce have a legend where Tzimisce was the one that embraced the 3.

                  It's supposed to be a mess. You've gotta sift through the truth and the lies. But also you gotta sift through some bad author ideas because they weren't able to tell the truth from the lies. (Diamonion causes a degeneration check unless you're on evil revelations? Please...) The entire LotB section was garbage too (a lot of that book was).

                  I... I still haven't gotten round to reading some key metaplot books like Beckets Jyhad diary, but here's what I've pieced together.

                  The Baali were allegedly made when an Ante, who is purposefully vague so your storyteller can surprise you or because you're hearing third-hand stories from a bunch of lying vampires, embraced three people. Nergal, who'd apparently feel at home as a chaos devotee,, Moloch, who's like your average selfish, charismatic vampire that just so happens to be linked with the infernal and The Unknown, I don't know if the unknown is ur shulgi, but if not, it's a woman who the other two hate. Do they hate her for out-eviling them (perhaps she's the originator of the swarm baali) or do they hate her for being a cursed angelic figure; again, it's up to the storyteller to make this stuff interesting (Things get less interesting when you know the answers for sure. It's actually nice to have the Storyteller reveal stuff you don't know about once in a while)

                  The Baali are a bloodline with an unknown ante. They aren't one cult. I'm not even sure if they're all infernalists. Individual Baali are free to start their own cults, to worship different demons, and to bullshit about their origins. There is a group of allegedly non-infernalist baali who sacrifice to elder-god demons in the hopes that those demons stay asleep. There are Baali that revere the Wyrm of WTA fame. There are Baali that host insects within their undead bodies and revere "the swarm". There are probably a lot of Baali hiding within the Tremere as payment for helping them with the salubri, doing nothing to draw attention to themselves.

                  Imagine a game of Chinese whispers but everyone's just telling lies that sound good. That's how the Baali are meant to be. The best thing to understand them, is accept that you're not supposed to. OG Set was probably a good guy too, while we're at it, but look at what his cult is now, and how many variations there are of it.

                          As mentioned the sort of choose your own origin the Baali have make specifics difficult to place. That said here's my various theories.
                          • Founders
                            • Because of Baali Apostasy it's hard to say what constitutes a founder.
                              • Antediluvian - Unknown, Saulot, Tzimisce, or Cappadocius are the most likely possibilities to have sired the three (Nergal, Moloch, Unnamed).
                                • Given the Salubri-Baali antipathy and history, along with Saulot's study in the east (which would have exposed him to the Howl of the Devil Tiger Dharma), he seems the best of the likely progenitors. Beckett's Jyhad Diary mentions Ashur being a state, not a name, thus my guess is Saulot frenzied at the sight of the sacrificed and he slaughtered the First Tribe. Then he either tried to create his own devils to punish the wicked or was wracked with remorse and bled into the pit in hopes of saving something.
                                  • An interesting thing I noticed is that the Watcher line of Salubri possess Obfuscate. If Saulot's discovery of the First Tribe coincides with his return from the East it would mean that he had been embracing Watchers. In this theory the Baali are Watcher Apostates, keeping Obfuscate and gaining Presence and Daimonion, Saulot's vitae altered by the mass death and the inherent power of the First's Tribes Well of Sacrifice.
                              • The Three (Nergal, Moloch, Unnamed) - The only survivors of a group of mortals known as the First Tribe that made sacrifice to some being from which they gained power.
                                • Nergal - Seeks to awaken whatever the Children of the Outer Dark are and usher them into this world. Attempted to do so at both Mashkan-Shapir and the Labyrinth of Knossos. Infernalists through and through they seek to corrupt and destroy the world.
                                • Moloch - Seeks to keep the Children of the Outer Dark asleep and their consciousness away from this world. They do so through careful sacrifices and terrible actions which lull the beings into their slumber.
                                • Unnamed - Unknown. Possibly a female which both Nergal and Moloch hate, or could be Ur-Shulgi (though I'm becoming less convinced of that as time goes on). More likely this is left undescribed to allow for the ST to place whoever they want there.
                              • The Apostates: There are those who were made Baali after their embrace, either through another or their own hand.
                                • Ur-Shulgi (Assamite/Banu Haqim Sorcerer 4th) - Embraced and later put through the rite of Apostasy by Haqim in the pit of the First Tribe. It seems the intent was to make a double agent that would gather the Baali and thus make them easier to destroy. It's possible that the breaking of the blood curse and his current actions are either a sign he's corrupted or again part of a larger play to wipe out the Baali.
                                • Cybele (Malkavian 4th) - Given that Nergal is described as her sire in Baali lore, it's likely he put her through the rite of Apostasy at the Labyrinth of Knossos. Through Petaniqua (who may be a Malkavian Apostate as well) has a line of infernal Malkavians.
                                • Azaneal (Lasombra 5th) - A Lasombra that found Chorazin. Probably read the scratching on the chamber where the sleeper had been kept and through it conducted the rite of Apostasy himself. Founder of the Angellis Ater Bloodline.
                          • Shaitan/Nergal/Slave Boy
                            • Again the choose your own origin muddied this significantly.
                              • Slave Boy/Ba'al the Destroyer - During the time of the Second City a beautiful slave began gathering Cainites to join him in service to dark forces. Later cast out of the city into the sunlight where he had to flee.
                                • Ur-Shulgi - Who I now consider the likeliest candidate. Haqim discovered the threat of the Baali and sought the means which to gather and wipe them out. The failed Baali assault stopped by, Ur-Shulgi, makes me think that the Slave Boy was a method of judging those who would be susceptible to the wiles of the Baali.
                                • Nergal - Huitzilopochtli has this as his origin. It's possible but I think it's more likely that Nergal took the story for himself and tweaked it. Would explain the discrepancy during which City it took place, of course he would want to claim that he was older.
                              • Nergal - Most likely one of the three Baali embraced following the First Tribe's destruction, sometime after the Flood during the time of the Second City. Found Mashkan-Shapir where he began studying the interred entity's scrawlings. Later moved it to Crete and crafted the Labyrinth of Knossos (which I consider a massive Well of Sacrifice) where he was prevented from awakening it by a coalition of clans. Fled from their to the Americas where he later took the guise of Huitzilopochtli.
                              • Shaitan - A title for the leader of the Baali. A number of Cainites have claimed the title over the ages.
                                • "Ashur" - Whichever Antediluvian embraced the Baali of the First Tribe might have been referred to as Shaitan.
                                  • Ashur may also have been the name of the city of the First Tribe.
                                  • Ashur may be a state of mind, one which all Antediluvians have experienced where they succumbed to the sins inside. Could be analogous to Frenzy or Wassail, could be something worse.
                                • Slave Boy - Whether Ur-Shulgi or Nergal they would have claimed the title to gather the Baali around themselves.
                                • Nergal - Claimed the title after discovering Mashkan-Shapir.
                                • Azaneal - Claimed the title after his time in Chorazin, likely after he deciphered the scrawlings on the tomb.
                                • Unknown others - Various sources have mentioned others claiming the title over the ages. It makes sense given the fractured origin and ability to bring other Cainites "into the fold".
                          • Cult vs Clan, Infernalist vs Baali
                            • Cult vs Clan
                              • Mainly this comes from the Baali habit of either forming cults or founding Nests. Because they gather others to fulfill their cause their line takes on cult like qualities.
                              • The rite of Apostasy allows a non-Baali to be re-embraced into the Baali, gaining Baali Disciplines. Thus membership in the cult can lead to membership in the clan.
                            • Infernalist vs Baali
                              • Infernalists are those who make deals with or serve demonic entities. Many Baali engage in this activity, and the works of the Molochim can seem the same (and depending on how you look at it, is the same).
                            • Road of Sin, Path of Pleasure, Path of Evil Revelations, Path of the Hive/Via Hyron
                              • Further muddying the waters is that the Baali are responsible for the creation of a number of Roads/Paths.
                                • Road of Sin
                                  • Tanitbaal-Sahar founded the Road of Sin during his time in Carthage. He's likely the source for the city's corruption as Moloch would have become more and more occupied by the joint blood bond between himself and Troile.
                                • Path of Desire
                                  • Further work by Tanitbaal-Sahar during his time in Rome with Camilla.
                                • Path of Evil Revelations
                                  • Offering service to evil/demons is something ancient but the Path wasn't codified until Lady Intisar at the Black Monastery in 1666.
                                • Path of the Hive/Via Hyron
                                  • The "original" Path the Baali followed. Later maintained by the Molochim and the Order of Moloch. Because of the need to found a Nest to protect against infernal corruption non-Baali might adopt it's teachings.
                                  • Makes a distinction between demons and spirits. Later was altered by Sahar-Hannibaal to allow for the use of Blood Magic (originally Dur-An-Ki/Thaumaturgy and later Kraina of the Well).

                                  Originally the're was only Shaitan, childe of Ashur during Chaos Factor (1995) up to Dark Ages Companion (1997) and he had 12 followers from the other clans.

                                  Then it changed to 3 (Nergal/Shaitan, Moloch, and the Unnamed) in Clanbook Baali (1998) and most of the publishing time of revised edition, in which Saulot sired all three of them.

                                  Due to an tease left out of Clanbook Assamite revised, it was believed that ur-Shulgi could have been the third "unnamed" in 2000, though this was only speculation, since the same author gave another origin (also left out) about Haqim embracing himself from 2 second generation he killed.

                                  Mariah's diablerie of Michael the Patriarch made her 4th generation Baali (Dark Ages Europe, 2002).

                                  Darkening Sky for Dark Ages which never came out, was re done and explained that Azaneal (an unknown generation influential infernalist) was 5th generation (2014).

                                  The Baali founders were back to two, Nergal and Moloch. The Re-embrace, which allowed you to convert a cainite from another clan into Baali, justified ur-Shulgi possibility of being Baali (V20 Lore of Bloodlines, 2017).

                                  This is the publishing changes that happened back in the day and the reason why it's so confusing since the latest publication tried to include as much as it can of the previous one or chose to ignore some facts and justified them as lies and myths. Later I'll update the dateline of which the Baali and all their stuff happened.

                                              "Flood":
                                              Historical accuracy: The disappearance of the Clovis culture is being recently tied to the Younger Dryas extinction event, which apparently was caused by a meteor impact in Greenland (Hiawatha crater), assumingly causing a massive worldwide rise of the ocean level. This happened in 9500 BC.

                                              Second City (after the war with the 2nd generation):
                                              Demon-venerating vampire War. Haqim leaves the Second City and returns with the sorcerer caste. For a century, Assamites and Salubri purges every single devil-worshipper in the Second City.
