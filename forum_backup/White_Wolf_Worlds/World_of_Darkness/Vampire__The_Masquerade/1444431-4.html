One can even imagine how badly the oWoD Umbra will be borked if they try to adapt nWoD's understanding of cosmology into it, but much somehow much more worse.

    Even then, just simply doing the streamline/dumb-down method won't work in this case with how they've been doing oWoD so far in this case.     Even like on what we agreed on earlier about if in ANY possible scenario of the Dreaming being dumb-downed; and it being subsequently folded into the High Umbra; still on its own wouldn't make any sense. The Dreaming by itself has its own lore weaved into it that doesn't broach much as what went on, or goes on in the High Umbra. The only tenuous similarity they bare is both being heavily shaped by the schema of thought itself; but the difference in them lies in the fact that the High Umbra is more toward the cerebral line of thinking and knowing; while the Dreaming is much more of the happy ups and nightmarish downs of unconscious or imaginative lane-line of thought. This is why the Dreaming when developed most likely was drafted into its own thing.

    I honestly think V5 shouldn't be touching or doing new stuff with the Umbra at all by this point. In pre-V5 for VTM, that aspect was never really dwelt with to begin with; And when its time to get around to revisiting the Umbra when W5 and the subsequent rest start coming along, they should get the original writers of most of the Umbrae or anyone among White Wolf with the most experience with that part of oWoD to begin redeveloping it for new editions.
