                            My understanding of this issue is:

                            1) There is ongoing dispute between the Princes and the Inner Circle as to how much authority the Inner Circle has over them. It's like any feudal kingdom where the powerful barons and magnates resent royal authority. The Inner Circle constantly tries to gain more authority, and the Princes constantly try to keep what autonomy they have or regain what they lost.

                            2) Justicars have various duties, and one of them is to investigate. Things like overpopulation in general, disorder, the presence of Caitiff, and so on can are probably within the domain of the Justicar to investigate. What direct actions they can take is another.

                            3) One of the primary powers of the Justicars is to call a Conclave. They can use them to dispose Princes legally, and to do all kinds of chicanery provided they are able to introduce the matter for deliberation and then obtain the necessary votes for the measure (likely doing their best to hide all evidence they are behind it). Conclaves and their rulings are actually quite a big deal, but they are under represented in the setting because we've been given little guidance on how ST should run a conclave in game and that many players wouldn't find such an experience enjoyable. However, conclaves are a way to justify a lot of things in the setting that seem out of the ordinary, but also seem justifiable.

                            4) How Caitiffs are treated is very nebulous in the setting. We have plenty of sourcebooks that present domains as having lots of Caitiff. These are generally domains where the Prince's authority is weak, and the elders are weak or divided. We also have plenty of sourcebooks that present domains seemingly without Caitiff at all. These are generally domains that have a very strong Prince who is able to assert his will over the Kindred of that domain, and the elders are strong or united. To me that suggests "properly run" domains don't tolerate Caitiffs. Those that are inadvertently made simply disappear. However, when authority in a domain is weak, and anarchs are around, the population of Caitiff rises.

                            5) I don't think the Inner Circle, as a rule, is stupid enough to openly state they are against Caitiff and want them destroyed. It's a PR disaster that gains them nothing. However, they clearly don't like them and probably remove any Caitiff troublemakers behind the scenes secretly, and intrigue to support those Princes and elders that hold the view they should be gotten rid of.

                            6) Like a lot of concepts introduced in Revised and later editions, those are perfectly fine if you intend to run a chronicle in the metaplot direction they want. However, lots of players don't want tor run chronicles using the metaplot. In which case, ignore those innovations you don't like.

                                    Originally posted by Black Fox View Post
                                    My understanding of this issue is:
                                    1. Originally the Inner Circle was hinted to exist and was a conspiracy, not a formal thing. This partially changed in the sense that they got inconsistent depictions as being a formal thing. But as said, inconsistent. Only Revised almost completely formalize them and then V5 goes all the nine yards.

                                    That said, this is mostly irrelevant. Politics still mean they're not micromanaging every city and don't have absolute power. Straining things too much will do them no favors. So they're not sending people for petty reasons (Adeline in the game had personal reasons to be there) or to just bluntly impose their personal views.

                                    2. Justicars can investigate, but their authority is nebulous. Most avoid making enemies with reason, especially among Princes. Karl Schreck from the Tremere is known as the most willing to step on toes in the line of duty, and as result he was once sent to the Inner Circle packed in a box. The Prince who did this is known and never faced any consequence. Schreck is the single most important Justicar until recent nights, not a mere Archon.

                                    So they do need recognition, even if it's usually not advisable to just deny them. It's a political game, as always, but not one where the Prince is in a weak position by default. An Archon that abuses office... Just look at what happened to Adeline despite being abnormally powerful for an Archon and arriving at a moment when her target was, indeed, in a weak position.

                                    3. Conclaves are a rare thing nonetheless. The Justicars know better than calling one without very good reason. A Prince that tolerates the Caitiff isn't, by itself, reason to send an Archon. Calling a Conclave is just madness.

                                    4. Sorry, but there's nothing nebulous about this, it's one of the few things that has been quite consistent in every single edition. The Caitiff are feared by many Elders as signs of Gehenna, but this is formally against the law. What happens is that how this tension resolves or even how deep it runs varies.

                                    In most Domains there's some balance: no one will bat and eye if a Caitiff disappears, and the Sheriff will frequently ignore crimes against the clanless, or even participate in them, but things can't get out of hand, and out of hand is not a uniform thing. How Princes deal with it is ultimately their problem, and Princes that give some real protection to Caitiff do exist, as do exist Domains where local opinions don't favor being too harsh on them.

                                    Finally, being a Caitiff isn't a crime, but being an unauthorized Embrace is. Standard procedure is to forfeit the lives of both Sire and Childe. In Bloodlines it is a plot point that LaCroix spares the Fledgling to ease tensions and avoid even more support to Nines.

                                    Given that Caitiff are more common among the unauthorized Embraces by an abnormally and not understood margin, it's natural that strong Princes will end up with less of them regardless.

                                    5. That's explicitly not true for a few reasons.

                                    While their actual beliefs are uncertain, the Inner Circle established the secularity of the Camarilla and forbade Noddism. One of the few things outside the Traditions that they expect from every Prince is to curb beliefs in Gehenna as much as possible, as they are this as an even worse threat to the status quo than the actual Gehenna (that they really aren't as convinced that will happen as others).

                                    IIRC the Camarilla does a 180º in V5, but their stance on Caitiff doesn't change significantly.

                                    Overall the Elder's superstition against Caitiff isn't seen as disruptive to the Cam, not something to be supported even from the shadows, but Caitiff are also not important enough to be worth too much trouble.

                                    6. I don't like, but that's beside the point. With or without metaplot, the notion of an Archon coming to a city only because they think the Prince should be culling the Caitiff is ridiculous.
