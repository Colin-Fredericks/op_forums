                        Originally posted by monteparnas View Post

And as far as we know this had massive support from Rome's population regardless of their political differences at the time. So we know for a fact that completely mundane facts about the war between the two was enough for Rome to raze and salt the earth there. We don't need Super-Evil vampires to explain the vampires of Rome doing the same thing that happened anyway, with added violence because they're vampires.
                        My point is that prior to the formation of the Camarilla and the Sabbat, we generally don't really see massive alliances between multiple vampire clans waging war on one another. The rare exception was the Baali Wars. Even with the Tremere Wars, the Tzimisci, Nosferatu and Gangrel didn't team up and work with one another to fight the Tremere, it was mostly each clan doing its own thing. And then post-Camarilla, the times we've seen vampire alliances fight with one another such as the early Camarilla vs Assamites or Camarilla vs Sabbat. None of these conflicts was due primarily to political or strategic motivations involving the world of the kine.

                        Vampires take action because of personal desires involving the world of the kine all the time, but this virtually never results in massive cross-clan alliances and conflicts. Those seem to the driven by events involving the world of the kindred.

                        If there was a massive alliance of vampires involved in the Roman/Carthage War, and if that war was carried out primarily for political and strategic motivations, that would make it an exception among exceptions and a totally unique moment in Vampire history. Instead, due to the involvement of the Baali, what happened with Carthage squarely fits into what we've seen in the Baali Wars and other similar conflicts involving cross-clan vampire alliances.
