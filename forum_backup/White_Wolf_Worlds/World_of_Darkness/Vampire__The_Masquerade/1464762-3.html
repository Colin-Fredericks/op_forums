            Well, back on topic.

            1. Ur Shulgi is going to stay undead because vampires usually stay vampires. They usually don't stop being undead until they are forced into final death.

            2. I don't think all the blood magicians of the Banu Haqim died. Most of them joined the Camarilla.

            3. It's debatable how much Ur Shulgi's cult closely matches the teaching of Haqim, given that all of it seems to be all over the place.

            My thoughts of Ur Shulgi are the same as those on the other 4th generation vampire, 3rd generation vampires and for that matter the Chosen in the Forgotten Realms; they only matter if the PCs encounter them for some reason. And even if the PC encounter them, the game should be about the PCs and not these nearly untouchable NPCs. A campaign sending the coterie anywhere near Alamut is not in the top five kinds of campaigns I would run.

            That said, he is a bit of a Cthulhu figure; a figure of terrible power and terrible age and his rising will be part of what ends the world. While the PCs cannot fight him, they can deal with the local chapter of his cult.

                                        Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                                        I feel that this falls apart under the point that the reason there's a 5th Edition is because Requiem never achieved anywhere near the popularity of V:TM or....well, 5th Edition. Mind you, it keeps coming back to the fact that as much as some elements seem to drive fans crazy, 5th Edition is a smashing enormous success.

                                        Sort of like Star Trek: Discovery. You may not like everything but it's something that has made an enormous impact and revived the franchise.
                                        At least for me, it is not about making a new setting. It is about driving the setting to its logical conclusion without holding up. They held up a lot.

                                        Metaplot-wise, the whole point of V5 is that the Gehenna came. Revised ended with the literal End of Times. The Old World is dead. Whatever we have now is what raised from its ashes. Several themes were affected by the Millennial tone of the game, that we're living in the End of History and nearing its culmination.

                                        Do you want to advance the clock but keep the themes untouched? V20 existed for that. Follow through it instead of weakening it. Work on a V28 or something and make the bolder updates without pushing the envelope too much.

                                        But to go ahead and say "this isn't a Legacy Edition, this is the World Continuing", then you can't be shy of throwing old themes through the window.

                                        Create a Gehenna Scenario and run through its conclusion. Have the world really upside down. Break the Masquerade with a sledgehammer. Put Antes walking in the open. Have scars of the End Times and theories about the Red Star. Divorce the setting from the real world going full Alternate History. This is not Kansas anymore.

                                        They didn't do that, and that is my biggest complaint, something I won't withdraw lightly. Is it deal breaker? Nope. As I said before, when everything was said and done, the only deal breaker for me was NuCam, everything else is passable. Badly written, but acceptable concepts. But at the core, it is the shyness from making it properly post-Gehenna what I identify as the root cause of most of the bad writing and what makes V5 fall short of its potential. It is what makes me simply see V20 as better lore-wise and don't consider V5 lore as worth my time.
