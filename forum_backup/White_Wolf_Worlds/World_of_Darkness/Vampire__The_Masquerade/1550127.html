    Nosferatu Warren Design

    04-14-2025, 08:06 PM
    I will probably be having a Nosferatu as one of the PCs in my upcoming chronicle so I wanted to give some thoughts to actual design of the local warrens. It's been a long time since I last had a Nosferatu PC, and back then I handwaved a lot of things. Now I want to get into some of the actual details of the clan as shown in the Players Guides and Clanbooks.

    The Clanbooks only mention a few basic design elements.

    There's an antechamber; a room or rooms where non-clan visitors are brought to wait to ask favors from the Nosferatu if the clan really wants to make them earn it. It can typically only be accessed through a series of confusing mazes filled with cramped tunnels, vermin, and traps. It's part defense to discourage unwelcome visitors and part torment to evaluate the visitor before deciding to grant his request.

    Then deep within the war there is the chamber of horror, basically a very large meeting room that can hold all the clan's members. It's supposed to be the most defensible place in the warrens. Presumably it is the place of "final battle" if the clan finds itself under attack. When the local brood meets in a Hosting, it's supposed to be the responsible of the Nosferatu who called it to provide the meeting place. So the chamber of horrors is not the place where the clan meets per se, although I do imagine individual Nosferatu do decide that is the place where they'll be meeting. But that's only an option, not a requirement.

    Then there's a place for a spawning pool, a place of fresh water that the eldest Nosferatu provide vitae so they can create various fearsome ghouled animals who will be part of the warren's defenses.

    Not mentioned are any lairs for individual Nosferatu who want an actual place to slumber during the day or hideout in general. I assume they are there though.

    Everything else can just be consigned to the categories of "traps", dead ends, or other defenses.

    Do people have any examples of traps, interesting flavor text, drawn maps, curiosities, or other design elements they'd like to share? Curious as to how people run or design the warrens in specific ways, especially in regards to making it an interesting roleplayinh experience for Nosferatu PCs.

      Also interested in people's opinions on including more outlandish elements associated with the warrens.

      The Animalism level six power Song in the Dark allows the Nosferatu to summon what is explicitly an underground dwelling monster of the underworld. Presumably this could be anything from a fomori, monster, bygone, creature from the Dreaming, or some underground umbral realm. I've never actually seen this power use, but it's mentioned in both clanbooks. Has anyone actually seen this used? If so, was it used for anything other than providing yet another creature to fight against intruders in the warrens?

      Similarly, has anyone ever used a plot of something hunting Nosferatu in the warrens? Or at least a mysterious, unknown creature the Nosferatu believe is there, but don't know what it is? If so, what did you use? What complications happened? How was it kept fun and entertaining?

      I'm looking for other elements that I could use for the place than just having a "safe spot" for the PC to go to, or some place the PC meets her clan. I figure if I'm going to create such a place, I want to be able to use it to tell stories that I otherwise wouldn't.

              Some random points I remember:

              I'm a big advocate of warrens with a fungal garden, if only to convey a sense of Clan culture. Players may not use it as a resource, but it is a good set piece. In the same vein, some communal areas that emphasize the Clan as a whole instead of the specific warren help with that - something that allows easy communication with other Clan members or warrens, a library with book exchange, a 'war room', that kind of thing.

              Having a private property in control of the Clan above the inner warren limits visits of city maintenance crews to the more sensitive parts. There should be some thought put into what's directly above.

              Don't skip on modern infrastructure: server room, generator, access to mundane communication nodes etc.

              In terms of traps, it's surprisingly difficult to come up with something that is smart enough not to spring on the wrong person. Especially on the outer perimeter where you don't want the attention caused by a worker falling into a spike pit, so it's better to put deterrents there. Fungi with harmful spores or gas irritants seemed to work well - anything that makes people want to go away and doesn't seem too out of place. Animalism is the second line (rat swarms, spawning pool creatures), and the lethal traps should be confined to the inner warren.

              Generally I prefer warrens as a safe space for Nosferatu, so putting in a 'hunter' of sorts seemed the wrong direction for me. Closest I got to that was warren residents telling newcomers and visitors the story of the Elder Who Sleeps In a Forgotten Secret Room and may awake hungry at any moment. Just a scary bed time story, right? But overall, the Nictuku fill that role already.

                          That's interesting. The Revised Clanbook mentioned the possibility of "subhuman" ghouls whose exposure to Nosferatu vitae eventually made them unable to function on the surface and who lived underground, and that some would become cannibals and eaters of the dead, clearly mirroring Lovecraft's ghouls and films like CHUD.

                          It also made mention of possible subhuman ghouls who somehow developed immunity to the blood bond and began hunting vampires on their own. So not quite the same as urban legend animals resistant to Animalism, but in the same vein.

                          The clanbook mentioned that such reports were unverified, but then gave mechanics. Typical wink-wink type stuff of ideas that are not strictly canon, but leaving it up to the ST if they want to include them in their game.

                          I'd like to include something mysterious and possibly dangerous in the warrens that the Nosferatu NPCs can't confirm or control, but want to make it acceptable to my players. If I include Vhujunka or Flying Polyps, it might break their genre expectations. Some ghouled animal that broke free and is on the loose might make a good compromise. Maybe some enormous boa constrictor who could potentially swallow and eat an entire Kindred/ghoul/mortal.

                          I might also include some of your "alleymen" - abandoned, "rogue" ghouls - who try using parts of that subterranean infrastructure as hideouts as they search for vitae, and the local Nosferatu may need to periodically eliminate them in order to prevent outsiders from exploring too much of the warrens and obtaining useful information. Maybe such ghouls are desperate enough to try and find the spawning pools so they can get their fix. A PC can respond to this in many different ways so it might make a good dilemma for them.

It has lots of dangers and pitfalls. Plenty of critters that could become monstrous. And Kindred could easily burrow deep enough to avoid the sun. It's probably the "original" warren.

                              My head canon is that Grendel and his dam of Beowulf fame are wilderness dwelling Nosferatu so I often fall back to their lairs as examples of how Nosferatu survive in the wilderness.

                              Now there is some potential of Gangrel or Lupines intruding, but they aren't found everywhere, and both can mitigated in different ways.

But there is also a general sense of the Nosferatu digging too deep and uncovering strange things, and a general sense of paranoia. Most players will realize the Nictuku won't be showing up in a normal chronicle so they're relegated to interesting lore that have no impact in the setting. Even good roleplayers can't help be motivated by metaknowledge of the setting.

                                  However, if I have something else that is unknown that has obviously entered the environment, then the player's imagination will take over. And I'd rather have in mind something real present down there than just some unknown Mystery Box level BS that I make up all the time.                                   A "hunter" in this case does not need to be someone actually hunting and destroying Nosferatu. Just something down there with the Nosferatu that they don't control which can feed into the clan's natural paranoia. But something a PC could conceivably find trace of, hunt for, and eventually encounter if they wanted. Then based on what it is, the PC (and clan) can decide what to do about it - destroy it, negotiate with it, ignore it, change the design of the warren, etc.

                                                  Curious as if there are good defenses to things like astral projectors and ghosts spying on or exploring the warrens? With enough patience, such beings would be able to see through Obfuscate and eventually follow a Nosferatu heading towards the warren. It seems to me that most warrens won't have anything, and the Nosferatu will just need to accept that this is always a gap in their defenses.

                                                  There's always the alternative to use some sort of blood magic ritual that prevents that, but I think it'd only be the very rare warren that has something like that.

                                                  However, if there are things like tinfoil hat or thin sheet of lead can prevent that kind of intrusion or scrying (perhaps not effective against everything), then I think many warrens would know to incorporate that into any kind of construction.

                                                  There's also the possibility of other kind of reprisals. Like if the local brood discovered such a thing and recognized the person responsible, then they'd freeze that person out of any future information sharing, and alert other broods to do the same. Or if they have friends or allies with similar powers, to ask them to intervene on their behalf. If so, they might want to cultivate such "friends" so they can call in such favors.

                                                  Likewise the issue with animal spies. Other Kindred with Animalism could potentially do that as could Lupines. Of course, such animals would likely be eaten by the creatures of the spawning pool, so that may just be good enough.
