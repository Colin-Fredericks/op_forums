                                          Its interesting who each sect volunteered/forced to participate in the vermillion wedding. On the face of it the Ashira seem to have a lot more at stake in the alliance than the Camarilla. Both are influential but one political hostage is by far harder to get rid off than the other. A methuselah commiting to a blood bond, no matter how mutual is a far bigger deal to a clan than if a young elder did the same. It would be interesting to hear the story from the Assamite or Ashira side of the coin. Did Tegyrius volunteer for the wedding, was he involved in the scheming and negotiating to form the alliance or was he thrown under the bus like Victoria Ashe as some sort of object lesson? We may never know.

                                          In previous editions there was a ritual to vanquish the blood bond other than by partaking in the viniculum. I wonder if they still or will exist in V5. And do the Assamites have access to it?
