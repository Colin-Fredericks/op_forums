    Dark Thaumaturgy is actually pretty cool

    06-03-2023, 05:13 PM
    Now I have never actually played as an internalist, but after looking at Dark Thaumaturgy paths for a bit I can kind of see why some would want to be one. A lot of the powers are just really interesting and creative. For example, in Path of Evil Revelations at the lowest level, the internalist can see into the spirit world, but at the highest level, they can create a demon from someone's sins and have it hunt that individual down. Or Tyranny of the Wyrm which can affect true faith, graft bane spirits onto an individual, and even turn someone into demon bait.

    Of course, the mechanics and drawbacks behind the powers limit their effectiveness, but for the most part, they sound pretty amazing.​

                                          How then do I make DT more tempting? Well it's cheaper and easier to learn
                                          -DT is always at in-clan costs, and learning paths through DT has them at current rating x3. The character can convert DT path levels to regular path levels by paying the XP difference.
                                          -DT is added to a vampire's thaumaturgy score, so someone with thaumaturgy 2 and DT 2 has a rating of 4. This goes to usual generational limits. While the Path of Blood can raise these limits, it's quite taxing to learn rituals above the 5th level while using this power.
                                          -DT users can add their DT rating to their occult rating for rituals, up to the usual generational limits. Vampires can use their DT rating instead of their willpower for path rolls.

                                          -DT requires a working relationship with one or more demons. Should the relationship break down, the character's DT becomes useless. With the right knowledge, the vampire can still pay the difference in XP to recover DT paths.

                                              Also I try to balance the paths a bit. There's no such thing as NPC-only magic in my games so things should be adjusted accordingly.

                                              Lure of Flames is straight up fire, a bane for vampires. Vampires can only resist it with specific magic like Fortitude.
                                              Fires of the inferno is balefire, corrupt fire, not a bane for vampires. Any powers that resist fire or toxin can be used to resist balefire, but also vampires and ghouls can use their stamina. The damage is aggravated for healing purposes. Mortals and Werewolves cannot soak balefire with their stamina, unless they have resist toxin or something like that. Difficulties to avoid fear-frenzies are reduced by 1 for vampires.

                                              Balefire doesn't spread into natural fires.
                                              Balefire is radioactive. Exposure to the fire's glow, victims of the spell, or the caster of the spell can cause sickness.
