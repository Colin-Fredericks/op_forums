                                Now I admit my understanding of 5th is still limited since I haven't been as active in reading WoD material lately, but my impression of the SI has always been that the term reflects humanity's greater awareness of Vampires (if not the supernatural) in general and the challenges that creates for Vampires to operate and thrive in secrecy. It's not an open war (except maybe wherever the Beckoning is happening) and it may not even be a cold war. At most its maybe more like counter-insurgency (which is mostly intel and small unit stuff against terrorists, which you could kind of see Vampires being treated as.) And even then there's not really much of a concerted, unified effort. There's a broader awareness and willingness to cooperate - especially if middle men exist to facilitate communications between various groups - but by and large each nation/state has its own approach to tackling the Supernatural (or possibly several that don't cooperate like the US.) It's a bit like early HtR - you had individuals and even small groups that might be tied together by shared identity/creed or goals shared over Hunter-net but they were far from a unified monolithic entity (pretty sure 5th has even made it clear the SI isn't and shouldn't be treated like a monolithic entity. A singular, unified effort is what you need for a conventional war but small unit stuff is a different matter logistically and militarily and I'm not sure we'll ever see MBTs and howitzers turned against vampires in any coordinated manner. Vampires aren't dumb enough to engage in direct warfare like that after all.)

                                Basically they're a bit like the Hellsing universe's 'paranormal' organizations (groups that largely have defined 'turf' they oversee that other factions don't intrude on) or the BPRD from Hellboy (if you want a more 'international' flavor without the boundaries) and all the complexities and feuding potential that can bring. I'd also see the Technocracy as one of those 'middlemen' seeking to facilitate coordination (without necessarily controlling the entire thing - it's not like they don't have a huge number of other challenges and threats on their plate that they can afford to exclusively focus on Vampires, especially if the vampires aren't making themselves an existential threat that *requires* a higher priority yet.) I'd also see Rome and the Vatican (and factions like the Society of Leopold and allied groups) also acting as intermediaries between different groups. Both factions would have a vested interest in bringing together the disparate groups under a single, unified banner since that would greatly improve their ability to eradicate vampires, but that doesn't make it easy to do by any means (as others have noted there's paranoia and distrust in such echelons and even without that we know that vampires infiltrate intel agencies like any other governmental body which is a further such challenge to unification efforts) I'd also see supernatural 'multinational' corporations - something like Pentex or Shinzui industries (in some ways this also overlaps with the Technocracy too for that matter.) A megacorp - especially with the rise of PMCs and the like - could just as easily imitate a political state without necessarily having all the handicaps of that state (again think Pentex, but devoted to hunting Vampires.)
