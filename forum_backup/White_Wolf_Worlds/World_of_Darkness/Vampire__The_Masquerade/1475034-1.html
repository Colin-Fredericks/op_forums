                        The problem with Sabbat members of the Path adjusting to the Camarilla would be that the Sabbat Honorable Accord is heavily based on the Code of Milan. You can't simply sweep out the Code of Milan with some other system, adjust your loyalties, and be fine. Betraying the Sabbat would be the equivalent of "utter perversion or heinous acts" on the Path of Humanity, and doing so deliberately. Doing so would be equivalent of a normal person suddenly engaging in mass torture and killing of the worst serial killers. Nor would it be the Beast driving them to do that. It would a deliberate choice.

                        As an ST, at the very least I'd rule that there is no Path check, but immediate loss of several Path levels. I might even lower their Conscience and Self-Control.

                        And after the betrayal happens, realistically how can they continue their adherence to a Path which they betrayed on a fundamental level? After all, aren't they themselves now among the Dishonorable? Which means they can't ever NOT associate with the dishonorable. Is it now impossible for them to ever increase their rating above 7? Yes, they can somehow adjust their following of the old Sabbat Honorable Accord with some new Honorable Accord variant that is aligned with the Camarilla, but nevertheless they are dealing with the fact that they committed a Path sin equivalent to a human person voluntarily committing mass torture and killing. You just can't move beyond that. You've told your Beast that the very foundations of your Path is now open to negotiation, and it is going to begin pushing you to your limits. Once you've made the ultimate betrayal, you can't tell yourself "No, I would never do that."

                        Honorable Accord would be a Path that is theoretically acceptable in the Camarilla, as it uses the Virtues of Conscience and Self-Control, and would lead to behavior that is consistent with Camarilla society and the Masquerade. But that would be an entirely separate variant of the Path of Honorable Accord found in the Sabbat which internalizes the Code of Milan. As Ragged Robin Robin said, any Sabbat member with it who defects is effectively choosing to abandon the Path. If they were going to do that, it'd just be best for them to convert to an alternate Path as it'd be hard for them to ever say they could become "honorable" again.

                        =
                        I think most Sabbat Paths would be incompatible with Camarilla society. The only ones that would be acceptable would be those that would seem to fit in with current society and not threaten the Masquerade.

                        Despite the Path of Caine being based on Conviction & Instinct, few of its "sins" would require behavior disruptive of the Camarilla's goals. There probably would be certain changes from the Path practiced in the Sabbat from one practiced in the Camarilla, but that could easily be explained by an discovery of "lore" or an "improved pedagogy." A few defecting Lasombra might follow such a Path.

                        The original Path of Harmony using Conscience could probably transfer to the Camarilla as well. But its adherents would mostly be Gangrel living away from cities, and thus ignore most of vampire society anyway. They'd just be transferring their allegiance from the Sabbat hierarchy to the Inner Circle. But that isn't a sin, especially if the Sabbat ever did anything like attempting to exterminate all followers of the Path. However, there's probably no Lasombra on this path.

                        A Path of Honorable Accord independent of the Sabbat/Code of Milan could coexist within the Camarilla, but for reasons already explained, I don't think any Sabbat already on this Path could actually defect.

                        The Path of Power and the Inner Voice could conceivably work within the Camarilla, but there's two problems as I see it. First, it works best for former Sabbat who were relatively young and had not achieved much Power yet. For older vampires who had progressed along the Path and gathered power within the Sabbat, defecting to the Camarilla would be admitting several Path violations that would erode Path ratings and empower the Beast. Secondly, while nothing on the Path would seem to be disruptive of the Masquerade, it's probably intensely politically destabilizing. Every domain which has members of this Path would cause even more trouble for the Prince or other leaders. The Sabbat could handle such chaos. Chaos was normal for a Sabbat domain, and it had various methods of channeling dissent and ambition like Monomacy. The Camarilla wants stability. Camarilla vampires are equally ambitious as Sabbat ones, but at least they are not on a Path that requires them to never admit defeat and keep pushing things in order to not violate their ethics. They can retreat and plot again without empowering their Beast. So in fact, I don't think this would make a good Path in the Camarilla despite its surface appearance. Young, ambitious Lasombra defectors from the Sabbat could follow this Path, but I suspect as they cause problems for Princes and other Camarilla elders in the subsequent years, they'd slowly be purged.

                        So I think only a few Paths could potentially work in the Camarilla.

                        As I said on another thread, if you want more Lasombra, the best thing is to just increase the number of existing Lasombra antitribu in the Camarilla. Maybe cause some things to happen that would justify a Lasombra AT on the Inner Circle and give them a Justicar. Maybe Montana succeeds in destroying Gratiano, and the Sabbat implodes in Civil War, raising the Lasombra AT's prestige and numbers in the Camarilla? Or your own ideas. But that won't do much to spread Paths within the Camarilla.

                            For the matter of the Honorable Accord, I think context can facilitate transition for at least some members without breaking many or any precept of the Path at all, because Code of Milan or not, it isn't the Path of Following the Sabbat:

                            10 Failing to uphold all the precepts of your group (in most cases, the Sabbat). The group the character identifies with may be the clan or pack, not necessarily the Sabbat as a whole.
                            9 Failing to show hospitality to your allies. Depends on whom the character may consider allies.
                            8 Associating with the dishonorable. A number of member could see the new Sabbat as ignoble here, depending on how that vampire sees the recent Sabbat decisions.
                            7 Failing to participate in your groups' rites. It is far more linked to clan and pack than the sect as a whole.
                            6 Disobeying your leader. Wasn't it an order from the Amici? How do you chose between two leaders?
                            5 Failing to protect your allies. See 9.
                            4 Placing personal concerns over duty. Only a sin if the vampire is acting out of cowardice and not out of an order or a sense of duty to the clan.
                            3 Showing cowardice. Betrayal or defection aren't the same a cowardice, especially if the betrayal in question puts you in great personal danger, which happened to some.
                            2 Killing without reason. Not a thing in this context. They either didn't need to, or had a reason.
                            1 Breaking your word or oath; failing to honor an agreement. Most Sabbat members have oaths for their packs, not the sept as a whole, and even then it doesn't mean an oath can't be revoked. It would be a complicated thing to do, but is feasible.
                            All in all, I think it is quite feasible that some Path followers managed to defect without much trouble. Maybe risking a dot or two, but as long as they manage to avoid or justify the level 1 (worst sin possible), they would be able to make the transition. That isn't to say that it would work every time or even on most of them, but I don't see it as being an instant Path Collapse either. Context, as always, matters.

                            But for the OP question, it is unlikely that the defection will affect the Camarilla to such extent.

                            Thing is, Roads were a thing. Options to Humanity existed in a more amenable way even, far before the current Paths were invented. Many Elders still personally remember them, some still follow them, and finding about them isn't that hard for Ancilla or Neonates. Yet the Camarilla take the decision to excise them and focus exclusively on Humanity, their arguments, opinions and the reasons for this still stand.

                            Now, it doesn't mean that finding Paths among the Cam is impossible, or that the newcomers won't keep their Paths when possible. It depends on your take on the whole matter already, because the game never had a hard stance on how rare or common they were in the Cam to begin with beyond somewhat rare.

                            In my view it is unlikely that the Cam will see with good eyes Path followers that are too open about it, or the active spread of Paths. But they're also unlikely to outright forbid and hunt down such vampires, the same way they never hunted down Noddists just for being so. Problematic Path Followers are bound to be found red-handed anyway by the usual laws, non-problematic ones are better off not being forced to betray their beliefs and risk Wassail.

                            What is a problem with modern Paths is that Humanity is the most stable one for the newly Embraced as it is more intuitive for them. I see a lot of people arguing that Humanity isn't the best Path for the Cam, but they usually ignore the many aspects it covers, and the first one is to keep Kindred from going nuts from the get go. There's no need for indoctrination and tests, there's no need to risk Wassail in the learning process, you just say that most things most people learn all their lives to be wrong are still wrong and that's it.

                            Other than that, Path followers exist in the Cam already and will increase simply because most defectors in a Path won't change at first. They may spread a little, but in a short term it wont go far, especially when you add to it just how hard it is to switch to a Path. In time it may come to change, but I don't think it really will do so.

                                                While I think there are variations of the Path of the Honorable Accord that would work well in the Camarilla, I don't think most adherents of the Path in the Sabbat could defect.                                                 Originally posted by Players Guide to Sabbat
                                                It is little more than a code of conduct which serves to keep some semblance of order in a very chaotic sect. In fact, those on this Path are often referred to as followers of the Code. The Path is spelled out in a book called the Code of Milan.
                                                Originally posted by Guide to Sabbat
                                                Although the original chivalric precepts upon which this path bases itself fell out of use by the Renaissance, some members of the Sabbat adopted similar philosophies, and the path adopted the Code of Milan after the first Sabbat civil war. Sabbat followers of the path turned their loyalty to the idealism of the sect and letter of the code, in the process acquiring the moniker “followers of the Code.”
                                                Originally posted by V20 Corebook
                                                The Path of Honorable Accord grew out of bygone nights, as vampires of the Dark Ages grappled with the problems of human morality by finding a harsh and unyielding code of conduct to replace the ethics of mankind. Sabbat followers of the Path turned their loyalty to the idealism of the Sect and the letter of the Code of Milan, in the process acquiring the moniker “followers of the Code.”
                                                And the Code of Milan is pretty clear in its loyalties to the Sabbat. Here are some of the relevant precepts of the Code.

                                                Originally posted by Code of Milan
                                                The Sabbat shall remain united in its support of the sect's Regent.

                                                All Sabbat must put the good of the sect before their own personal needs, despite all costs.

                                                All Sabbat shall protect one another from the enemies of the sect.

                                                All sect members shall protect Sabbat territory from all other powers.
                                                I can't see how any Sabbat on the Path of Honorable Code can defect to the Camarilla and possibly remain on the Path. Now there may be some rare Sabbat on this Path that do not use the Code of Milan as its basis. They might be able to continue on their Path after defecting. But they should not be representative of the vast majority of the Path.

                                                Trying to find wriggle room for justification for defection is something that lawyers and sophists can do. It's not behavior that people generally associate with "honorable" people.

                                                If you are at the point where you point to members of the Sabbat as not being "true" Sabbat, you don't defect to the Camarilla. You remain true to your ideals, proclaim them as false Sabbat and attempt to rally the "true" Sabbat while destroying the apostates.
