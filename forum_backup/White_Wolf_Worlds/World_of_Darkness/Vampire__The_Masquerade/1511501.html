    Antediluvian blood properties

    05-12-2023, 10:15 PM
    So, as you probably know already, there are some stated "miraculous" effects of drinking the blood of some beings, among them, perhaps the Antediluvian ones are the most potent. As stated in books published, we only have Malkav's blood and Tremere's blood. Just for fun, giving that a single drop from Malkav's blood is stated to heal all wounds and derangements (save for the permanent one of the Malkavians), increase willpower by 1 and drop the generation by one, what do you guys think drinking the blood of each of the Antediluvians effect would be?

              The blood of the Eldest contained the essence of the Tzimsice antediluvian (like the Carnage symbiote bonded with the blood if the sociopathic serial-killer Cletus Kasady) an became basically inpossible to destroy, able to reform from nothing (well a drop of blood) and manifest itself in anyone who tasted Tzimisce blood... ever - not to mention the vicissitude poisoning

You own a small vial containing the Blood of the Ravnos Antediluvian. What you do with this vitae is up to you, and what it does to anyone who imbibes it is up to the Storyteller.

Meanwhle, after the Baali Anaduk made a deal with a Demon who gifted Anaduk a succubus who could consume the Tablets of Ma-ri-ah and become the product of whatever was contained in them and since the tablets were incomplete, Ma-ri-ah was only partially recreated, full of disjointed and partial memories and corrupted by hate; she slew Anaduk upon waking and gorged on his blood, becoming a 7th Generation Baali in the process. After Mary the Black ate Michael, she became a 4th Generation Baali, not a 6th or 5th. Her blood was thicker.
                                                      So by this logic, a 4th Generation could be easier to lower to 3 than a 6 for example. Some suplement even state something along the line, "A vampire's vitae itself seek perfection" and thats what urges the vampires to consume each other, to reach to source.
                                                      Third is a bit blatant reasoning I know... but the description of Malkav's blood not mentions any generation limits; you drink it, you lower your generation.

                                                      ...but if you are basically a Blood God already?
