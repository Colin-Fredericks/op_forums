                        I was thinking how local Tremere factions might rise around strong leaders with power and charisma to establish their own houses.

                        Based on the list of possible active members I compiled, and the very helpful contributions on this thread, I was thinking about adding rumors about the existence of the following Houses in my game:

                        V5 Official
                        House Tremere – Camarilla, led by Schrekt. Traditionalist Tremere magic.
                        House Goratrix – Sabbat, led by Goratrix (or Tremere in Goratrix's body). Specializes in unorthodox magic.
                        House Carna - officially Camarilla with Independent and Anarch sympathies, led by Carna. Witchcraft.
                        House Ipsimus – Anarch. Crowley inspired mysticism, possibly led by the vampire Crowley (was a Malkavian in A World of Darkness 1st Ed, might be a much older deluded Malkavian from London by Night)

                        V20 Semi-official
                        House Telyavel – Sabbat, could possibly have independent members. No official leader, could be a descendant of their DA leader Deverra. Telyavelic magic.
                        Digital Draculas aka Hacktivists – Anarch. Unknown leader(s), maybe Masinka St. John? Probably accepts non-Tremere members. Technomancy.
                        House Trimira – India based, officially Camarilla. Unknown leaders. Accepts non-Tremere members. Sadhana.

                        Rumors in my game
                        House Calinot – African based, local Camarilla and Laibon courts. Officially Independent. Led by Elaine de Calinot. Laibon magic.
                        House Cincao – Latin American based, local Camarilla and Sabbat. Led by Xavier de Cincao (or his Drowned Masters?). Might be a front for the Drowned Legacies. Specializes in Latin American magical traditions.
                        House Virstania – Independent. Led by Virstania. Mostly composed of Virstania and her Gargoyles. Gargoyle creation.
                        House Trismegistus – Camarilla. Dr John Dee (or someone assuming his identity). Composed of former members of the Elite, Trismegistus, Hashem and Quaesitori factions. Kabbalah and Angelic (demonic?) summoning. Might be infernal.
                        House Yomi – Quincux and other Kuei-Jin courts. Led by Oliver Thrace. Kuei-Jin and Akuma magic. Infernal.
                        House of the Dream – Istanbul based, officially Camarilla. Led by Thereza Kymena, composed of former members of the Brothers of Absinthe and the Third Eye factions. Salubri friendly. Reveres the new Trinity of Constantinople. Potentially infernal.
                        House of the Serpent – Cairo based, officially Camarilla. Led by Kahina, composed of former members of the Eye of the Serpent faction and allows non-Tremere members, mainly Setite/Ministry. Akhu.
                        House Auram – officially Camarilla, with Independent and Sabbat sympathies. Led by Therimna, composed if former members of the Auram Guild faction. Specializes in Alchemy.
                        House Liban – France based, officially Independent. Led by a descendant of Mathieu de Calice, composed of former members of the Humanus League and the Golden Path of Harmony factions. Golconda seekers, specializes in the goal of curing vampirism.

                        Any other ideas for Houses of suggestion on these? Some of these could benefit from naming suggestions...
