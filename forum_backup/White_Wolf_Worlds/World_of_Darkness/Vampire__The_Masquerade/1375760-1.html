                    Considering the nature of his embrace, I see no reason why Ur-Shulgi's childer would need to also be Baali. Assuming he's the Unnamed, he has a different sire than Nergal and Moloch. Despite the story about Nergal, Moloch and the Unnamed crawling from the Well of Ashur, it's entirely possible they didn't all receive the embrace or crawl out of the well at the same time.

                    While Clanbook Baali mentions a Cainite of great power and majesty found the well of Ashur one night, it could have actually been several antediluvians, an idea I've toyed with. Clanbook Baali describes an array of powers that the Cainite possessed, and while even a relatively young antediluvian might possess them all, I think it lends credence to there being multiple antediluvians present. Saulot, along with Tzimisce, may have both bled into the well. Haqim might have been present (either known or unbeknownst to those who bled into the well), which could explain his knowledge of the well's location and function. Then he later returned, embraced Ur-Shulgi, and tossed him into the well. Ur-Shulgi's mastery of Blood Sorcery in what appears to be a relatively short time could very well be explained by him being a Widderslainte.

                    Ur-Shulgi became the leader of the Baali during that time, and led them in open war against the other clans (which is what Haqim wanted). The Baali were defeated (though obviously not entirely destroyed). Afterwards (or perhaps during/near the end of the battle), Haqim supposedly tore the demon or whatever it was from Ur-Shulgi, but even after doing so, there was an infernal taint left behind. I suspect that even Ur-Shulgi himself was unaware of this for a good while, so it may not have been evident to others until it began to take hold of him/it. Later, Haqim lied about Ur-Shulgi's embrace, and claimed he had found the boy dying on the battlefield and the Embrace was the only way to save that innocent victim.

                    What I love about all this and the rest of the Baali lore (much of the World of Darkness lore really) is that it's laced with hints and clues and plot hooks that inspire a variety of ways to use the Baali and their origins in all of our stories (without requiring us to come up with it all ourselves). The same storyteller could run two separate campaigns that involve the Baali, switch up some of their lore, and be able to keep to them mysterious even to players well versed in the Baali's lore.

                        I got into this in the Refrigerator Thoughts on Saulot thread, but here's what I've been doing in my current Dark Ages game. Obviously none of this is canonical. I also haven't really revealed that much of it to my group, yet.

                        In the East Saulot split his Hun and P'o, so his P'o could go about doing good works, preaching Golconda and be a perfect being. His Hun was allowed to exist to do what was necessary, like found the Baali.

                        The Baali actually have a purpose and that's to keep performing atrocities to keep the sleeping Children from waking up. It's performing relatively minor evils to prevent cataclysmic levels of destruction from happening. The atrocities keep the ancient demons dreaming. They also serve as a means of unifying the rest of the Kindred and for a time at least turn them away from their petty Jyhads.

                        The Watcher caste of the Salubri serves as model to build the third group of Baali descended from the nameless one. This third group is supposed to keep an eye on the other Baali to make sure they play their role and don't go too far. Whenever one of them gets it into their head that it'd be a good idea to wake up one of the sleeping Children, the nameless ones step in. They either influence events so the rest of kindred society becomes aware of the Baali plot or they intervene directly and destroy them. So it was the Baali watcher group that influenced the Assamites and Salubri to attack Chorazin and pushed the Ventrue in Rome to attack Carthage.

                        I haven't really fully fleshed out some of the mechanics for the Watcher Baali, but have the one the group encountered using a modified Valeran that has a variation of Ending The Watch that works on unwilling subjects, including kindred. This lends some real gravity to the stories of Salubri being soul stealers and it not being purely Tremere propaganda. For a discipline spread they get the modified Valeran and Damonion. I'm on the fence if they'd have Obfuscate or Auspex for another discipline. I suppose since it's an NPC only group, mainly populated by very old vampires, I could give them both.
