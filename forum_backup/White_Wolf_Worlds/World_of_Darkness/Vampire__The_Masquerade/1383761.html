    [V5] So do Vampire still have great sex? Or no sex?

    04-25-2020, 09:54 AM
    Clarification: I in two different VTM games one base on V20 Dark Age (Using modified V20 system) and the other using V5 (with some changes).

    As I'm reading up more on both systems and their lore (including the meta plot/overarching story), one thing struck me when I was reading up the effects of Blush of Life in V5 and how it affects having sex.

    So in V20 and more so in V20 Dark Ages, its stated that vampires can have sex. A Vampire needs to use Blush of Life, but they can do it, even if it pales when compared to the kiss and everything uses blood in place of body fluids. In Dark age V20, it is even written clearly the Vampires can have great sex.

    But in V5, it states that unless you have high humanity (8+), a vampire can only "fake" having sex???
    Given that most vampires start at 7 humanity or lower, does this means most vampires loses the ability to have sex when they are turned into vampires?

    I'm just kinda confused... did something happen in the overarching story that changed the way vampires have/enjoy sex? Was it all a lie?
    I haven't read as far as the Camarilla, Anarch books or any other V5 books, so can anyone enlighten me what happened to vampires and their libido in V5?

          Nothing changed in the overarching story. In some of the material in previous editions and in Vampire media the idea that Vampires don’t care about sex is present. Basically they care about getting blood more than actually have sex.

          In the newest edition they went into that a bit more and put mechanics behind it since hunting is more focused and one method for that hunt is to lure your prey with sec. the goal in the vampires mind is not the sex but the blood.

          This is tied to humanity because humanity is a more complex thing in this edition and represents how close to being human or to being a beast you are rather than in prior editions where it was just primarily how moral you were.
