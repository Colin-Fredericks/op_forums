                                                Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

                                                I mean, the existence of the Tzimisce Antediluvian is a good reason to consider them enemies of their Clan isn't it? I thought the reason that the two Clans considered themselves Clans was because they claimed to have destroyed their Antediluvians. Which is now known to be false in-universe. The reason the Antitribu is because they are against their founders.

                                                Or did I miss the reasoning?
                                                So it's a bit complicated. At least as per the Revised Guide to the Sabbat, the antitribu were the vampires who had turned their backs on their clans, but this was a conscious political decision rather than an absolute objective definition and it's a label some groups chose to take or chose to reject. Take for example the Salubri antitribu.


                                                Originally posted by Revised Guide to the Sabbat page 28
                                                While the Sabbat seems like a strange place for those who take offense at the destruction of Elders (at least to other Sabbat) the Salubri antitribu seem to regard the sect's larger philosophy with only lip service. In their minds, their allegiance with the sect begins and ends with the war on the Camarilla. In the tradition of the antitribu, the Sabbat Salubri have turned utterly away from the "clan" that spawned them, regarding their progenitors with antipathy for what they consider weakness and fatalism.
                                                So the Salubri Sabbat considered themselves antitribu and called themselves antitribu. But they were not trying to kill their Antediluvian as he was widely believed to already be dead (in fact that was the reason they were part of the Sabbat, because the Camarilla had given the Tremere the de facto okay to Blood Hunt the Salubri). They called themselves antitribu because they had turned their backs on the peaceful philosophy that was the predominant concept to their Clan.

                                                So note that in this case, the Salubri antitribu weren't out to kill Saulot. He was dead and if anything they were out to take vengeance for him. The Salubri antitribu don't even hate or try to kill regular Salubri. They pity them and view them as weaklings and cowards, but they don't necessarily want to wipe them out. If anything they want to take vengeance for their deaths at the hands of the Tremere. So for them, they considered themselves antitribu because of the way their goals and actions went against the more typical clan beliefs of fatalism and pacifism, not because they were opposed to Saulot.

                                                We can also take a look at the Serpents of Light who were, defacto antitribu. They rejected the clan, they wanted to kill Set, by any definition they are antitribu, but they reject the label and don't call themselves antitribu. Instead they tended to consider themselves almost more like a new Clan, choosing to completely cut their ties with their forebearers and instead and create a new identity for themselves rather than call themselves "Followers of Set antitribu" because fuck Set (and also it's kind of a mouthful ).

                                                And of course, you had the Lasombra in the Camarilla who called themselves antitribu because they recognized that the Sabbat Lasombra had functionally taken control of the Clan's identity, and so they wore the "anti-tribe" badge as one to vocally and clearly note their rejection of the Sabbat Lasombra - which was an important political distinction to make in Camarilla society. After all, you don't want people getting confused over where your loyalties lay, so you call yourself a Lasombra antitribu so that the other Camarilla guys go, "Oh he's antitribu. He's okay, he's one of our Lasombra."

                                                Note that they absolutely did not call themselves "Lasombra antitribu" because they opposed their Antediluvian. That had nothing to do with why they called themselves antitribu. It was simply a political label they used to draw a clear distinction between themselves and the Lasombra in the Sabbat.

                                                So who is antitribu and who is not and why they call themselves that or choose not to call themselves antitribu is a pretty complicated topic and there's no definite, absolute rule. Ultimately if a group considers themselves antitribu then they recognize that they form an off-shoot of the "main" clan and oppose it in some way, whether that opposition is philosophical or physical.
