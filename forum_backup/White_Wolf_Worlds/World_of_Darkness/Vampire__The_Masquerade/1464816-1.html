        General Points on the earlier books: -

        - The mechanics are not compatible with V5 without conversion - Stats are different, Disciplines have been consolidated and changed, feeding mechanics and clan weaknesses altered, etc.
        - The V5 game world has changed significantly with the near-collapse of the Sabbat, the Camarilla both gaining and shedding clans, and the Anarchs resurgent. And the Second Inquisition, of course

        Of the V20 books: -
        - Lore of the Clans and Lore of the Bloodlines are still largely relevant, apart from the Hecata
        - Beckett's Jyhad Diary is stuffed to the brim with interesting characters and metaplot, although you may need to do some supplementary Googling. If you have any doubts about how deep and detailed this game world is, this book will dispel them.
        - Anarchs Unbound is outdated but it shows the Anarchs on the cusp of their breakout into the big time and might be valuable.

        The older third edition books, Guide to the Camarilla / Sabbat / Anarchs detail the Sects as they were early in the millennium, which may be useful for recent-historical games and depth of background.
