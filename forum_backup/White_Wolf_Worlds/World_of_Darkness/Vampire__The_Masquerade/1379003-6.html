            Just because metaplot is often forced doesn't mean it's ok to write something that very clearly isn't good. Adding more bad things rarely makes something better.

            I don't have a problem with hunters, I like hunters. However, the escalation of the Hunters into the SI doesn't make sense. Logically it doesn't make sense to make Hunters a larger threat than they were in V20 (which yes, had some excellent books) and thats a deal breaker for me; thematically it's a complete inversion of what the World of Darkness is and what it's like to run a Hunter Game. In my opinion, Game wise it's more fun to play hunters as Domestic terrorists than MiB, there's actually another group for MiB, I'd go there if I wanted that kind of game.

            Vampires don't even have to be blood gods to be protected from large scale hunts like this. They're entrenched. Unless there was a public outcry to get rid of the vampires, goverments wouldn't. The scenario of V5 doesn't sociology into account. It doesn't take history or politics into account; It doesn't take Ma Boi Hermes, who probably provided the metaphysical paradigm of the world of darkness, into account; It doesn't even take the literary notion of satire into account. Everything is wrong with the second inquisition. It's like watching new writers take over a show and not know what it's about, only all the examples I can think of don't even come close to the scale of the disconnect V5 has.

            V20 had excellent hunters that made (more) sense and were a joy to run. V5 hunters are the overly emotional reaction of a bad storyteller who got a bit too upset that his players didn't take his game seriously enough. It might actually be great for an individual table to have this scenario, but it really doesn't work for the overall WoD. It's a logistical farce unless you dial it down considerably, and even then you'd be better of just following V20's (wonderful) ideas on hunters.
