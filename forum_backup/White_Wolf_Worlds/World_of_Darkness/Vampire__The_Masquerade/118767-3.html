                        I don't know. Ennoia, outside of in the physical sense (and that was largely due to the older Gangrel Flaw of Animal Features being permanent), isn't really described often as being monstrous as apolitical, though somewhat motherly.

                        Malkav is likely the MMN, so monstrous in the sense he is disembodied, but alien, no one knows.

                        Tzimisce is, currently mindless/soulless, and more like a carnivorous plant or disease. If it did have it's sentience back, it would very likely be extremely alien. Same with Lasombra, as it has literally merged with the Abyss, and it's will and sentience have been supplanted.

                        Absimilard has been overcome with grief and self pity, unable to take every dang thing in existence hating it all at once exists in seclusion. It might be interesting to see how he might react if someone jut gave him a dang hug?

                        Troile and Ventru are assumed truly dead, though the insinuation is that Ventrue was a super megalomaniac, viewing all others as his pieces to use and abuse.

                        Neither Tremere or Giovanni where close to human even when they where humans, and both are right bastards, now just right bastards with eternity, power, and resources. Tremere new full well exactly what taking the vampiric elixir would do (turn them into vampires and destroying their Avatars), and tricked a hell of a lot of other Mages into doing it, then went on a mass Diablerie and forced his "Childer" to do the same. Then, after the last one, he was locked away inside his own body fighting against the soul of Saulot, so he can't have a humanity any higher than 1 or 2 tops, assuming he even followed Humanity. Power and the Inner Voice, the Beast, and Evil Revelations are also possible, though they too would not be high just because he didn't have any time at all to build them.

                        Giovanni might not have even followed Humanity even when he was mortal, and he too lead his Clan-to-be on a mass slaughter and Diablerie campaign. All of the above basically applies to Giovanni as well, except that he likely did have the time, and probably chose not to give a crap.

                        All indications about Ravnos point to him being a terribly inhuman creature, perfectly fine killing anything that got in his way, and that includes his own Clan. It's highly likely that he both had an alien Path (or Paradox, original version) as well as nearly maxed out, (as he was able to bring most of his powers into play during the daylight hours of his 3 day solid last stand). I say that because the clouds and tempest wouldn't have mattered, it was still "day time" outside, and he would have been restricted to dice pools and Discipline levels no higher than his Path Rating, and it's basically assured he utilized both Chimeristry and Fortitude 10. If he didn't, holy shit. . . ! ! !

                        Haqim, could really go one of two ways. Gehenna presents him pretty definitely as both the Force of Nature of Murder and Diablerie itself, but also, contradictorily as the true judge of his own Clan's inner loyalty and spirituality, (as it hunted down Fatima, but found her worthy and didn't killer her on the spot like it had all other renegade Assamites). One version places him in a sort of combination role of martyr and defender of humanity against the evils of Cainites, while the other presents him as nearly utterly mindless, killing anything at all that happens to be noticed by it as a wandering bloodfog shadow. In either case, no player is likely to ever encounter him and survive, so it's probably not all that relevant.

                        Set is currently truly dead and inactive. Should he be resurrected, who knows. Clan propaganda suggest like some of the others, he is a "defender of humanity" in a way, opposed to other Cainites/supernaturals, but, he also 100% believes himself (and is believed by his Clan) to have truly been a god BEFORE becoming a Cainite, if he even is a Cainite, and was generally accepted to have been opposed by every single other Antediluvian, Sect, etc. . . Very likely that he would view everyone as at war with him, and active enemies, probably including even his loyal Clan. Very much the same deal as Ventrue, the world exists for his pleasure, use, and abuse, period, though even more so than with Ventrue, Set knows for a fact that he should rule as he is an actual god.

                                                I have a few theories. Probably wont get them all in this post, and I wont go into a lot of detail.

                                                One is that Ravana and Ennoia are in fact the same being, a 2nd Generation Cainite that was more akin to some primordial, demonic beast than anything even remotely human. An Indian Rakshasa, one might say. It split into two by that high level Protean power, or a stronger variation of it, and became two Antedilluvians.

                                                Another is that Saulot is Ventrue. He was embraced as the Vizier of the First City, and Lasombra as it's King in Caine's stead, but Caine resented Lasombra because it reflected the Father's great sin of pride, and instead he chose Ventrue as King, and his Clan were groomed into leaders. The two Clans have obviously been at odds about it ever since. Ventrue himself, however, didn't like this outcome at all, and utilized it's immense power of Dominate to fake it's own death and drift from the memories of it's contemporaries, and left for the East, where he became Saulot, and upon his return the Salubri were born, now possesed of a mystical third Eye but a weakness eerily reminiscent of the Ventrue. The reason his death didn't affect the Ventrue is because when he left for the East, he severed his conection with his old Clan mystically, through some power now lost to history. He was the kind of person who is never satisfied, and in one of his more fickle moments he created the Baali, which he soon realized was a mistake. Later he grew tired and resentful of the Salubri also, and attempted to make yet another Clan, this time by becoming Tremere. It didn't exactly work, yet.

                                                Also on the subject of one Antedilluvian holding two Clans is the Toreador and Brujah. There is no Arikel, or it's just a second name for Troile, a childe of Ilyes'. The fact that Ilyes was extremely old and powerful, as well as a sorcerer who dabbled in time (True Brujah are an experimental bloodline of his), the fact that by his death his progeny was widespread and hardly in contact any more and the face that Troile ended up eating most of him muddled the bloodlines and over time, produced the Toreador and Brujah.

                                                Set is a 4th Generation Gangrel with the aspect of the snake and also a sorcerer who ate a member of the Second Generation and became something different than other Methuselahs who devoured Antedilluvians, akin to a strange god that then made the Setites. It's either not powerful enough to contain the power it has, exists in another plane, or has been defeated and scattered, or any combination of the three.
