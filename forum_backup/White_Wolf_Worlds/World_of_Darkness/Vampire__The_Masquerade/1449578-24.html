        Originally posted by Taggie View Post


        I would think so, the design seems to be geared to one true way of playing, with no room for other styles, so it's Humanity, Anarchs or Cam, low powered games, rare rolls and that is all.
        There's another aspect to this OneTrueWayism: they want everyone to be failed superheroes. They've always said that this isn't a superhero game, and for the longest time I thought that meant you could explore all sorts of variant play. But putting everything they say together and reading between the lines, I've realized something: the one true way they want you to play is that you try incredibly hard to be conventionally heroic, and you fail. You try to be a superhero with fangs, and a combination of hunger and frenzies and the wearying complexities of vampire politics drag you down and make you fail.

        That's it. That's the only way the game is supposed to be played.

        You can't progress on an alien morality and become a Path Paragon. Golconda is a pipedream that even Saulot fell from. You're supposed to try to be a vampire superhero, every single time, and lose, every single time. That's it. That's all. That's the game. That's their completely suffocating vision for the game.

                            I remember someone talking about the devs adding humanity into 1e with the intention to have most players laugh it off. It's something that'll tell you that you're inhumane, but won't actually do anything beyond give you a longer torpor time. Diablerie was only as punishing as murder in pre-revised, so you'd only degenerate into a wight if you were really indulging in sick stuff. It functioned to discourage disturbing evil, but not much more. You could have fun with the mechanic if you seriously wanted to be human again, but for most, you can happily hover at 3-6 and not be worried about it.

                            Humanity post revised, well, Diablerie would drag you down, so you paradoxically stopped doing it when your humanity is low, the complete opposite of how the story said it should go.

                            V5? All kinds of messed up. Being super humane is impossible, but it does give you an advantage in Diablerie, so say your prayers before you chow down on Mr Clark. I just don't think I can handle how... how do I say this diplomatically... Trying to Force you to care about something is how you get people to reject it. The humanity system is not robust enough to support the undue attention it is given.
