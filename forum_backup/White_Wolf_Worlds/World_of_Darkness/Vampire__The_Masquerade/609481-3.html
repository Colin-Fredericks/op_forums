    I am cautiously intrigued. I have V20, and I've fiddled with it and ironed out all the quirks so it runs perfectly for me and my troupe. The metaplot does not interest me; if this new edition pushes it too hard, I'd hesitate to pick it up. Perhaps this is an opportunity to rebuild from the ground up, rather than play to the nostalgia like V20 did/does, and I'd be happy to see a more objective/critical outlining of the Clans and such, but if all this new edition does is clean up the writing for Vicissitude so people stop complaining about it, then it's not for me. I agree with DarkwaveDomina when she said
    Originally posted by DarkwaveDomina View Post
    This edition must be suitably distinct from V20.
    This edition must be substantially better.
