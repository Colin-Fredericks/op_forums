                            Originally posted by monteparnas View Post

                            Maybe, but those are not only widely apart places, but still huge areas.

                            The problem remains exactly the same: it talks about the Gehenna War as happening in a distant land near something relevant, but it actually covers a continent-size area in a very active part of the world that's well known (and home to some players) and doesn't really give any meaningful goal once you arrive.

                            Where are they heading?
                            What are the victims of the Beckoning doing?
                            What the Sabbat is actually engaging?
                            What the locals, including local Kindred, have to say?

                            I won't rest with an answer that keeps treating anything outside the G7 countries as exotic tokenism, I want something that's both actually respectful with the place and useful to run games there. I'm not against making it as fan material, but wished we had any improvement at this point.

                            For now it's still exactly as if all the by Night books were in European cities, and then for this war they said the Beckoning is moving elders to North America, then just never touch the matter again as if no one lived in North America. Saying they used as covers events in opposite sides of Canada doesn't do much.

                            As for the source of the Beckoning, it isn't all that strange that those to be punished get to resist going to be punished. But I don't think the Antes are the best option.

                            Not only we have the problems Shadeprowler pointed: no other clan or faction has shown particular resistance, neither do Caitiff and Panders, only the Hecata.

                            But also I just find it too cliché with no payback for the narrative. Especially if you think the Antes may not be all in the same place, sharing the same plan.

                            For me the Beckoning reads more like the kind of pervasive effect, neither forceful or precise, but ever present, that's either a passive result of something happening/someone's presence, or some kind of natural law itself.
                            For the record, I agree with you that it's vague and stupid and offensive. But also, I don't think you'll find a canon answer that you like any better. At least, not in V5. V6 may fix this for us.
