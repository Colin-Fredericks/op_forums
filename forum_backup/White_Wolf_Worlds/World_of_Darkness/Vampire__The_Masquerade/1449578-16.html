    Originally posted by AnubisXy View Post
    Just as a personal note I purchased the 5th edition core book and ended up buying Chicago by Night, but with what happened with the Sabbat book, I won't be purchasing any future V5 books. Again, there was material I liked, and material I didn't like, but the decision to make the Sabbat an NPC only sect sent a message to me that I'm not the person they're publishing these books for. Which is fine, they can write whatever they want, but like with Heavy Arms, this will be the first edition I'm skipping and don't purchase any further books for (and this is coming from a guy with a dozen boxes of WoD books for every gameline sitting in my garage).

    Plus, past just Vampire, it's also making me extremely leery about the upcoming Werewolf 5th edition and other possible editions of other games beyond those as well. I may end up finding myself just writing off the World of Darkness as a whole, which would make me pretty sad considering I've been following this setting since I was in high school.

    I mean I bought V5 Core book after all despite them ignoring every single fix and improvement suggested in our playtesting submissions but pretty much gave up after a few months as the mechanics of the rule set kept draining the fun out of every session. That said I'm not too fussed as I've read all the V5 books that have come out and can see I'm not missing anything and its freed up the cash to spend a hell of lot on V20 products from Storytellers Vault that, for myself at least, seem to be much better in terms of writing quality than any of the non-OPP V5 products and up to and in cases exceeding the quality of those.

    So I'm fine, personally.

    But I do feel for those fans who have stuck with V5 hoping that it would do something, anything to allow them to play the games they loved to play with VtM through the decades. So I can understand there would be feelings of being cast aside and ignored.
