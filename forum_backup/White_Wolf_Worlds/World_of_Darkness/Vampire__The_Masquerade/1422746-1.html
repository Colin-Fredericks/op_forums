                                                Shakanaka & Alphari My initial response when I first woke up was a bit unfairly hostile, so I'm going to remove it, apologize, and offer a better reply.

                                                I agree that the vast majority of Metamorphosists are Tzimisce that focus on the use of Vicissitude to achieve their goal. And I agree that Vicissitude was the inspiration for the philosophy, and that it has an important place in the tradition of the Path. I do not want to remove Vicissitude from the centre of the Metamorphosist movement, in the Dark Medieval or the Modern Nights.

                                                BUT

                                                As I have noted above, I believe that the philosophies of Metamorphosis are extremely flexible and variable in interpretation, and I believe that to be an opinion backed up in the text. While the majority of Metamorphosists likely are Tzimisce wielding Vicissitude, I think trying to exclude other forms of transformation simply because Vicissitude was "the original" is narrow-minded. And, to be fair, part of why I agree that the majority of Metamorphosists are still Vicissitude-wielding Tzimisce is because narrow-minded hubris is one of the most common flaws among the clan, and neither the clan nor members of the Path are the sort to go proselytizing it to others. But a highly individualistic Path is going to produce a diversity of interpretations, expressions, and deviations.

                                                That is what I am referring to when I talk about others being "influenced" by Metamorphosist philosophy. To use my example of a more Humane Metamorphosist, all you need to do is alter one premise of Tzimisce ego and Metamorphosist philosophy; that the former Humanity of the Vampire is not some absolutely inferior past state to be moved beyond, but is instead a necessary half of a synthesis that will produce a higher state of being if achieved.

                                                When I say "influenced," I merely mean that the ideas within it may have provoked thoughts leading to new ideas that can be traced back to Metamorphosist concepts, even if the Metamorphosists might disagree with what has been done with their philosophy.
