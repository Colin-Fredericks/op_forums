            For my Vampire setting, I don't use the metaplot from the third or fifth editions, so there's no Week of Nightmares but Antediluvians can in fact be killed. It's just really damn hard to do so. Veddharta isn't dead but was the first of the Antediluvians to fall into a deep torpor, which helped fuel the misconception that the Ventrue Antediluvian met Final Death.

            In fact, I'd have it to where you can actually prevent Gehenna by killing an Antediluvian WITHOUT diablerizing them. Problem is, the majority of the folks who'd have both the motive to take down an Antediluvian and the means to do so are in it for the purpose of diablerie.

            Tremere diablerized Saulot and Augustus Giovanni diablerized Cappadocius, and contrary to canon, Giovanni was very thorough in his destruction of Cappadocius, annihilating him completely.

            Saulot's soul was able to survive the Tremere diablerization but he had planned for that. With that said, I'm going with the "unironic Lawful Good heroic saint" depiction of Saulot from early 2e. He foresaw the end and realized he needed to let the Tremere rise to power for both Kindred and Kine to prevent Gehenna or at the very least be able to survive it.

            As for the Baali? If I were to include them in a chronicle, I'm gonna go with the "Always Chaotic Evil" Chick Tract-tier strawman Satanist versions of the Baali from First Edition, but I'm gonna totally rewrite their backstory. Saulot had no hand in their creation but instead it was the work of Sutekh. A failed experiment of Set gone wrong.

            The first Baali was the Methuselah Shaitan, his name a corruption of Set-Han, in fact, Shaitan tried to usurp Set alongside his siblings Moloch and Nergal. They went renegade and consorted with demons to try and gain more power and overthrow Set. Yet the Baali are far more base and crude than the Setites in their quest to spread total corruption.

            But that was long ago and the Baali no longer care for Set nor his Followers while the Setites still hold a deep-seated hatred of the Baali, though most Setite neonates and ancilla believe the Baali to be extinct, if they even know they exist at all. Even many Elders believe the Baali to be a mere historical footnote.

            But the most ancient of Setite elders still know and still remember. They know the Baali are still around and that Set has not forgiven them for their treachery.

                        Ever since BJD came out I’ve run with the option from the end where Enoch says he and the Antediluvians aren’t nearly as powerful as they lead people to believe.

                        In game this means that even Caine started out as about a 4 BP/2 dot discipline cap thinblood vampire, but over millennia his blood thickened all the way up to my 50 BP/9 dot cap.

                        Gehennas occur every time the blood thickens enough for a new generation of vampires to hit the power ceiling… which simultaneously frees these ancients from old dominations by their sires and grants them the power to take on the other ancients. This leads to powers struggles and violence and the final deaths of some of the weaker “old bloods” and “new bloods” as a new balance of power is established among the max strength vampires.

                        In my game it is the 5th Generation that is rising into the 50 BP/9 dot category, threatening the old power bases, by the time it’s done every Generation will be effectively a step up (i.e. 14th Gens will be able to access their full blood pool and learn the fifth dot in their disciplines; 8th Gens will start to be able to raise their traits to 6 and their blood gets 33% more potent).

                        Which means Antediluvians in my setting are just 4th Gens with a LOT of extra XP compared to the 4th Gen Methuselahs… which means they can certainly die and many have likely been replaced over the millennia.

                        Biblical Cain may not even be the 1st Generation, just the first known generation… and some other vampire was the true “Blood Caine” (in the sense of Mitochondrial Eve; the vampires’ common ancestor. We’ll never know their true name and they might have even met final death millennia before Biblical Cain was even born… so we may as well call this hypothetical creature Caine).

                        So, basically, not only can an Antediluvian die, unknown numbers of them certainly already have… but the ever thickening blood means there will always be others to rise and replace them. Over millennia history will forget they ever weren’t a part of the oldest generations.

                        In my home game notes Biblical Caine was actually of the 21st Generation as measured from Blood Caine. Lilith was a vampire of the 20th Generation when she embraced him after killing nearly all of the 19th and prior generations.

                        Biblical Caine believed himself the first vampire (believing Lilith to be just a blood witch) and so Cainites number their generations from him, but a few of the older survivors of Lilith’s purge (such as the sleepers worshipped by the Baali) do confuse things in the historical record (some of Antediluvians rumored to be Lilith’s children are, in fact, of the same Generation as Biblical Caine, but are counted as Caine’s childer because while they have the same potential, Caine has more experience and so is perceived as being of an older generation).

                        Hell, because of the cap, Shaitan/Nergal is probably the most singularly powerful vampire in my setting as he’s achieved max vampiric power level with demonic investments layered atop that.

                        TL;DR… setting a power cap plus thickening blood lets me play rather fast and loose with Antediluvians and their mortality.

                            If I were going to actually stat the Antediluvians, then this would be how I'd go about it. Bear in mind this would be for my own V20 headcanon that basically is summed up as "The entirety of Revised and V5 never happened"

                            Attributes: 13/11/9, Abilities: 19/12/7
                            Willpower: 10
                            Path Rating: Humanity 0 (with a few noted exclusions listed below)
                            Disciplines: All in-clan Disciplines at 10, plus Potence, Celerity, and Fortitude at 5 (if out-of-clan), Auspex at 6 (if out of clan), and Thaumaturgy at 5 (Tremere has Thaumaturgy 10, obviously)

                            When it comes to the "Humanity 0", that doesn't necessarily mean a Wight. For some Antediluvians, it can mean they are just criminally overpowered Wights but others hew closer to the 1e definition of Humanity 0 (basically an irredeemably corrupt yet functional villain)

                            Now, for certain specific Antediluvians, they have Path ratings that are very different.

                            Sutekh is at Path of Typhon 10

                            Lord Tremere is at Humanity 1, whenever his soul is in control of his body

                            Augustus Giovanni would either be a "1e Humanity 0" Kindred or he'd be on Road of Death 10, I'm honestly not sure which one fits better.

                            Saulot is at Road of Heaven 10 and his soul has achieved Golconda since I prefer the early 2e "unironic Lawful Good saint" version of Saulot and the Salubri.

                            Cappadocius would be at Road of Death 10 as well

                            Ennoia would be Road of the Beast 10

                            I'm just a trans guy and gaming fan, unabashed hedonist and unusually upbeat nihilist, fairly laid back. He/Him.

                                                        Here's just a handful of stat sheets I made for the various Antediluvians. I don't have the Gehenna book with me anymore, so they aren't going to be compatible with anything in there. I'll probably make some more posts until I get all the Antediluvians stated up.

                                                        Also, if you see Necromancy 10 or Thaumaturgy 10, assume that means all Paths and all Rituals are known to their absolute fullest extent on top of any plot device powers.

                                                        Also, Clan Bushi do exist in my games as a rare Japanese bloodline believed to be tied to the Salubri and the Ventrue, so that's why Saulot, Tremere, and Veddharta all have the Kai Discipline.

                                                        I'm going to start off with Saulot and Tremere, though I'm not going to even bother with the "White Worm" aspect of Tremere.

                                                        Now, with other Antediluvians such as Tzimisce, Lasombra, and Malkav, I may try to stat out their non-humanoid forms such as the Cathedral of Flesh or the Malkavian Madness Network.

                                                        Saulot (pre-diablerization) - AKA The Salubri Antediluvian

                                                        Physical: Strength 7, Dexterity 7, Stamina 7
                                                        Social: Charisma 8, Manipulation 9, Appearance 5
                                                        Mental: Perception 10, Intelligence 9, Wits 8

                                                        Path: Road of Heaven 10
                                                        Willpower: 10
                                                        Conscience: 10
                                                        Self-Control: 10
                                                        Courage: 10

                                                        Disciplines: Auspex 10, Fortitude 10, Obeah 10, Valeren 10, Bardo 9, Obfuscate 7, Dominate 5, Presence 8, Animalism 7, Temporis 9, Kai 5, Potence 5, Celerity 5

                                                        Abilities: Medicine 10, Empathy 9, Occult 9, Subterfuge 5, Alertness 9, Athletics 6, Awareness 10, Law 4, Melee 7, Survival 6, Stealth 5, Brawl 3, Expression 6, Performance 2, Etiquette 6, Science 3, Technology 1

                                                        Health Levels: 9 (OK, OK, -1, -1, -1, -2, -2, -3, -5)

                                                        Notes: Has successfully achieved Golconda and would've been able to achieve Rebirth had he not been Third Generation (a headcanon of mine is that Antediluvians are so close to Caine that they cannot achieve Rebirth at all even if they reach Golconda. 4th Gen and lower can defeat the Curse of Caine but it's easier the further away you are from Caine in your generation. The only 4th Gen Methuselah who ever successfully achieved Rebirth was Osiris, who founded the Children of Osiris)


                                                        Lord Tremere - AKA The Tremere Antediluvian

                                                        Physical: Strength 5, Dexterity 7, Stamina 5
                                                        Social: Charisma 9, Manipulation 9, Appearance 6
                                                        Mental: Perception 9, Intelligence 10, Wits 9

                                                        Path: Humanity 1
                                                        Willpower: 10
                                                        Conscience: 1
                                                        Self-Control: 7
                                                        Courage: 10

                                                        Disciplines: Auspex 10, Dominate 10, Thaumaturgy 10, Potence 5, Celerity 5, Fortitude 5, Valeren 1, Vicissitude 2, Animalism 6, Presence 8, Obfuscate 7, Kai 3, Chimestry 5, Kineticism 5,

                                                        Abilities: Occult 10, Awareness 10, Leadership 10, Science 9, Subterfuge 9, Academics 9, Alertness 8, Empathy 8, Expression 8, Intimidation 7, Law 7, Crafts 7, Stealth 6, Etiquette 6, Technology 6, Larceny 6, Survival 5, Computer 5, Medicine 5, Politics 5, Melee 4, Finance 3, Brawl 3, Athletics 2, Streetwise 1, Firearms 1, Drive 1,

                                                        Health Levels: 10 (Same as Saulot but with an extra OK level)

                                                        Notes: Lord Tremere is one of only two Antediluvians who have been awake after 1500 alongside fellow usurper Augustus Giovanni. Though he often spends long amounts of time in torpor, his soul is often traveling the Astral Plane to divine more arcane knowledge nd lore and he has occasionally been semi-awakened from time to time over the centuries. Thus he has been able to learn about elements of the modern world.
