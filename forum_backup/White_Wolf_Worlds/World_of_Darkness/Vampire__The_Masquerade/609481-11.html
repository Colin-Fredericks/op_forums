                                                            If you've read the new Revised Convention Books, you'll get an updated metaplot anyway. In that, a load of weird things happened around the millennium, and things calmed down again. This echoes the Dark Ages setting, where lots happened, and then it all died down until the Final Nights. There's also a suggestion in the MRev Convention Books that this is a slightly different universe to the one in which the ToJ supplements took place--either that ToJ was one possible future (or rather, many) and this one is another, or that the ToJ is at an even further point in the future.

                                                            V4 might do for the oWoD what Rise of the Machines and Salvation did to the Terminator franchise--delay the apocalypse and show us a different possible fate. (Hopefully it'll be far better written that T3 or T4, of course.)

                                                            I suspect that V4 will continue in the same vein as the MRev Con Books, probably with a different metaplot to the BNS/MET stuff, but maybe with some overlapping ideas. Everything up to the ToJ supplements happened, and then a series of supernatural catastrophes cleansed the way for a new age (as per KOTE, ROTDM and that old 2e quote about a cyclical Gehenna), in which vampire society has been shaken but not destroyed. Another Gehenna may come, but perhaps far off into the future.
