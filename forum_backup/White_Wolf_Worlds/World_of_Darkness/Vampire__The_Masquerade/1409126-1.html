                        So, I'd probably go in a slightly different direcion.

                        For example, why aren't Cainites dominant, considering they are contagious and the Wan Gui are not? There could be several reasons. Maybe the Embrace doesn't work on everyone. In the aftermath, Yomi expanded to cover most of the world—except for those pockets, where Caine's get was sucked into the Lands of the Dead, where they had to learn how to feed on dead souls.

                                    Push the Wan Xian's fall from grace far enough back into prehistory and the ancestors of Native Americans were carrying the karmic debt with them when they crossed the land bridge into Alaska. Push it far enough back and the ancestors of every human tribe and nation were carrying it before they left Africa. That's part's easy - it's just a matter of how far you want to turn the dial.

                                    If China remains the cultural capital for this kind of undead in this setting, it can easily be chalked up to population density and China's phenomenal history of being able to hold a culturally coherent empire together for millennia.

                                    Conjuring up a mechanical reason why the Kindred variety of vampire hasn't become as universal could take a little work, but that's an opportunity to have some fun tinkering.

                                    Maybe they have the Ghouls' weakness that their true age catches up with them if they don't feed, making torpor deadly for an elder who isn't influential enough to ensure that he's fed while he sleeps. This ensures millennia of generational turnover that makes an individual Kindred a relatively fleeting blip on history's radar.

                                    I'd assume that Kindred are not treated as true heirs of Cain's (without the "e") Old Testament punishment in this setting and have some other origin, even if it's shrouded in mystery or lost to the ages. That gives you some freedom to play with the concepts and mechanics of Generation. Maybe the raw power of Kindred is capped at the equivalent of Eighth Generation, reflecting a spiritual deadness that prevents them from having anything remotely resembling a Bodhisattva among them.

                                    Both options have the benefit of making it much easier for rival supernaturals to cull the numbers of troublesome Kindred. It also completely justifies the belief among some Ventrue elders that other supernaturals are holding them back.

                                    It all really depends on what you feel like you need to keep and what you're willing to deconstruct.
