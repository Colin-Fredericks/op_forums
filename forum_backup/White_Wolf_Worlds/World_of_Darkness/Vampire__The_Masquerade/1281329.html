    [V5] - Active Tremere after Vienna's Fall

    01-17-2019, 02:24 PM
    I am compiling a list of publicly known Tremere (at least, to members of the Clan) to give an idea of how they were impacted by Vienna's Fall, which my players are interested in investigating. I adopted this plot from V5, but made it more conspicuous and secretive (no televised attacks, only those who keep up with the SI know something happened). Only upper echelons of the Camarilla and the current Tremere leaders know the Clan has been crippled and broken, but try to keep a facade of normalcy (which is certainly not difficult to see through if one pays attention).

    I'm also assuming Vienna was an inside job to deal with the Great White Worm, but not all Councilors might have been aware of the coup. I think Meerlinda sacrificed her close ally and former friend Etrius, and those close to him, because they would not let go of a stratified Tremere Clan subservient to the Great White Worm. I don't think Meerlinda supports any V5 faction of the Clan, despite what Schrekt or Carna would claim. She has not made her survival of Vienna known yet, in my game.

    I realize my choices are somewhat arbitrary. I included some notes that might interest my players: some of this information will come in-game from a former Dark Ages Ceoris member - hence the inclusion of long lost vampires that this source would know.

    Any opinions or suggestions are welcome! Did I miss some important Tremere? Have I got the wrong information? (Some are from the Wiki). Would you select different members to fall/survive?

    Inner Council
    Etrius – Eastern Europe Councilor. Destroyed in Vienna.
    Grimgroth – Western Europe Councilor. Has a long history and major enmity with the Order of Hermes, taking part in the Modern Massasa War. Might have defected to the Sabbat, or destroyed in Vienna.
    Meerlinda – North America Councilor. Hiding.
    Xavier de Cincao – Latin America Councilor. Destroyed in his Chantry in Caracas in the late 20thc. Replaced?
    Abetorius – Middle East Councilor, a Child of Meerlinda. Either destroyed or hiding in Constantinople, where he once was Regent.
    Thomas Wyncham – Far East Councilor, British. Second Councilor responsible for Asia, living in Hong Kong in the 1990s. Unknown location since his Childe, Oliver Thrace, defected to the Quincunx. Wyncham might have been destroyed in Vienna, or is hiding in Asia.
    Elaine de Calinot – Africa Councilor, disappeared in her Chantry in Algiers in the late 20thc. Replaced?

    Fallen in Vienna (either close to Etrius or prominent enough to have been called to Vienna)
    Astrid Thomas – Etrius’ child, High Regent of the Vienna Chantry. Destroyed in Vienna with her Sire.
    Ulugh Begh – Childe of Meerlinda, Embraced in 1253, Pontifex of the Balkans under Etrius since 1992. Founder of the Children of the Pyramid, leader of the Elite, a Quaesitor and great Salubri hunter. Justicar wanna-be, probably Archon. Destroyed in Vienna.
    John Diamond – Childe of Meerlinda. USA Pontifex. Destroyed in Vienna.
    Peter Dorfman – USA Pontifex and Regent of the Washington DC Chantry. Destroyed in Vienna.
    Nicolai Antonescu – Primogen and Regent of the Chicago Chantry. Destroyed in Vienna.
    Gabrielle di Righetti – Tremere Justicar around 1987, when she declared Dylan Bruce an Anathema and appointed his name to the Red List. At that ceremony, her Quaesitor was Pontifex Johannes Dee, and her witness was Petrodon. Destroyed in Vienna.
    Augustine d’Erlette – Childe of Celestyn. Master librarian of Vienna. Destroyed in Vienna.
    Claas Drescher – Childe of Ardan Lane, active in Ceoris and Eastern Europe. Sire of former Justicar di Zagreb. Founder of the Krakow Chantry. Destroyed in Vienna.
    LeDuc – one of the original Council of Seven. Once Regent of Paris, stepped down in 1133 for Goratrix and traveled to the Levant (to hunt Salubri?). Destroyed in Vienna.
    Ardan Lane – Childe of Celestyn, held power in Romania and administered the abandoned Ceoris at least until WWII. Destroyed in Vienna.
    John Dee – Childe of Meerlinda. Regent of the former London Chantry and former Pontifex of the British Isles. Probably fell in London, or was destroyed in Vienna.

    Active Camarilla (known to be active, or held a title that would allow to become local leader, but not to be called to Vienna)
    Karl Schrekt – Grandchilde of Etrius, Child of Lotharius, and former Justicar. Current leader of Camarilla Tremere traditionalists.
    Carna – former Regent and Primogen of Milwaukee and one-time Prince of Marseilles. Current leader of House Carna, a splinter group from the main Clan with sympathies to the Anarch Movement. Officially still Camarilla.
    Fanchon – Founder of the Camarilla. Inner Circle. Unknown whereabouts.
    Anastaz di Zagreb – Childe of Claas Drescher. Justicar elected from 1998 to 2011. Was active against the Sabbat in Montreal.
    Ian Carfax – Long-time Archon of Justicar Schrekt. Current Tremere Justicar.
    Lady Aisling Sturbridge – High Regent of the Chantry of the Five Boroughs in New York.
    Lord Ephraim Wainwright – Regent of the Kenilworth Chantry in New York City.
    Eugenio Estevez – Regent of the Maupassant Room Chantry in New York City.
    Dr. Mortius – was stationed within the Milwaukee chantry, translated the Book of the Grave War. Unknown whereabouts.
    Patricia von Bernstein – Primogen of Frakfurt, secret head of the Order of the Wyrm. Infernalist, has access to a Pit.
    Maxwell Ldescu - Childe of Schrekt, former Primogen of West Berlin. Regent of the Berlin Chantry.
    Tomás Marcello – Childe of Xavier de Cincao. Prince of Caracas.
    Abigail – Regent of the Miami Chantry and Sire of Hannah
    Hannah – Primogen and the Regent of the Atlanta Chantry. She also acted as an Harpy. Might have survived a rumored assassination attempt. Currently inactive.
    Luna Demian – current Regent of the San Francisco Chantry.
    Abraham DuSable – current Regent of the Chicago Chantry.
    Lille Haake – Primogen and Regent of the Amsterdam Chantry.
    Sylvia Kilver – member of the Cairo Chantry, a slave of the Setite sorcereress Kahina.
    Hypatia – Regent of the Cairo Chantry, slave of the Dream Court Mathuselah.
    Valois Sang – Camarilla agent within Montreal. His Sire was the Prince of Quebec. Active?
    Maximillian Strauss – Primogen in Los Angeles and Regent of the local Chantry during the brief time the Camarilla had taken control in 2004. Left LA by 2005.
    Bourdona – Sire of Carna. Prince of Marseille.

    Independent
    Therimna – Childe of Goratrix, known as Ute Kovacs in modern nights. Was one of the first Tremere and one of the developers of Thaumaturgical Alchemy. She is an apostate of her Clan and acts as a mercenary under the guise of a Tzimisce.
    Theresa Kymena – Salubri sympathizer. Regent of the Constatinople Chantry until the Fourth Crusade. Created Dream Magic. Her future fate is unknown.
    Ponticulus – a mage of the House Tremere, and one of the closest advisors of the founder of that house. He died in 980 during the ritual of consecration of Ceoris, but later returned as a wraith. Might still haunt Ceoris.
    Virstania – Creator and Mistress of Gargoyles among clan Tremere. She helped them to revolt. Unknown whereabouts.
    Celestyn – knowledgeable of Saulot and Kupala. Either was destroyed in Vienna, or is hiding in Asia.
    Oliver Thrace – Child of Wyncham, defected to the Quincunx. Infernalist.
    Wan Zhu – Child of Thrace, fled her Sire to SF and joined the New Promise Mandarinate there, against the Camarilla.
    Valerius Maior – Antitribu who survived the destruction in Mexico. He is part of the Red List, formerly the third Anathema and recently re-evaluated to the fourth. Infernalist. Childe of Mistress Fanchon.
    Kyoko Shinsegawa – placed on the Camarilla's Red List following the death of Genina.
    Rutor – Childe of Jervais, tried to be Prince of Copenhagen. Member of the Red Sign.
    Epistatia - Eldest Childe of Goratrix (E.1022) and the first outside of Council of Seven to be Embraced. Amnesiac and estranged from the vampire society since the first Massasa War.

    Active Anarch
    Monica Chang – an Anarch sympathizer, part of the Digital Draculas. Originally from Hong Kong, Chang was Embraced in the 1880s. After being transferred to Washington DC, she stole most of her Clan's research on Technomancy and fled to Chicago, to the Ventrue hacker known as Hurricane.
    Maldavis – currently active in Chicago
    Masika St. John – creator of the Technomancy. Whereabouts unknown.

    Sabbat
    Goratrix – House Goratrix leader, possessed by Tremere (Sabbat?).
    Heinrich Himmler – Childe of Ldescu, was unbondable and defected to the Antitribu, but was also Primogen in East Berlin until the Blood Curse in 1997, after which he left. Possibly destroyed in 1998 in Mexico.
    Paul Cordwood – Childe of Epistatia, vanished during the Gargoyle Revolt; rumors claim that he joined the Tremere antitribu and acts as a Bishop of the Sabbat. Possibly destroyed in 1998 in Mexico.
    Deverra – head of the Telyavelic Tremere of Lithuania and part of the inner circle of the Gangrel Khan Qarakh. Deverra sacrificed herself to Etrius allow the other members of her coven to escape. Her remains were ritually preserved and taken to Ceoris. Might be behind the Telyavs, or has a Childe who is.
    Orlando Oriundus – Childe of Goratrix. Became Tremere Antitribu and attained the title of Bishop within the Sabbat. Possibly destroyed in 1998 in Mexico.
    Malgorzata – Childe of Goratrix, and the leader of the Conspirator Faction. Captured in Ceoris in the 1570s by Tzimisce anarchs during a raid. Taken prisoner and tortured, she revealed all she knew about Ceoris' defenses and Tremere's plans. Goratrix found her several horrific decades later, but left her there to linger. Malgorzata is described as a Sabbat Priscus in VTE, which I like.
    Elena Mendoza Vasquez – Regent of the Mexico City Chantry. Destroyed in 1998 in Mexico.

    Long Lost
    Jervais – Childe of Malgorzata. Responsible for the destruction of the Telyavelics. In 1931, Jervais met destruction at the hand of Spanish Republicans while on a visit there. Rumors are that the attack happened at Goratrix's behest. Alternatively, destroyed in an attack against Hunedoara in the 1500s.
    Lotharius – Childe of Etrius, once Prince of Vienna and last Pontifex of Austria, destroyed by an Assamite in 1529. Sire of Karl Schrekt.
    Mendacamina – Childe of Etrius, loyal to Goratrix and mage hunter of Ceoris. Was Regent of the Vienna Chantry from 1232 to 1230, heavily involved in the Massasa War of the time. Destroyed in 1284 by Etrius.
    Curaferrum – was the castellan of Ceoris and a loyal servant of Etrius. When the Gargoyle Revolt hit the chantry Curaferrum was diablerized by their leader Virstania.
    Bitiurges – Childe of Etrius, was Antitribu during the 18th and 19th c. Destroyed in 1977 in Germany.
    Edward Bainbridge – Childe of Elaine of Calinot, was Primogen of London during the Victorian Era. Probably killed before the 20th c.
    Nickolai - survivor of the Tremere antitribu massacre in Mexico City. The killer of Justicar Petrodon. Sire of Leopold. Had the Eye of Hazimel. Destroyed by the Camarilla in NY in 1999.
    Lord Casmir - a member of the Conspiracy of Isaac. Destroyed sometime before 1848 (probably much earlier).
    Andre Malotte – was active in London. Was responsible for and destroyed in the Great Fire. Was one of the minor conspirators in the Conspiracy of Isaac. Had the Sargon Fragment.
    Helena Taylor – former Regent of the Washington D.C. Chantry. Her fate following the Sabbat assault on the city in 1999 is unknown.
    Kasper Von Aupfholme – Childe of Mooreau. Sire of Hypatia. Former Councilor of Africa. Disappeared.
    Preston Varrick - former Regent of Tokyo. Perished in Hunedoara.
