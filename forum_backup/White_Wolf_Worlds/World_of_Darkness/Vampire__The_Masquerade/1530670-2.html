                My favorite moments in the book:

                * Mark Decker hosting Beckett in the baseball field as an Elysium.

                * Marcus Vitel making his Doctor Doom-esque return.

                * Beckett's horrifying encounter with Helena.

                * The Sabbat Civil War meetings

                * The introduction of Maria Saldanova, Lucita's sister.

                * Christof and Ecaterina teaming back up.

                * Vykos getting the Dracon out of their brain and regaining free will for the first time in centuires.

                * The Assamites asking for asylum and the Tremere freaking the Hell out.

                * All of the Assamites briefly frenzying for Kindred blood.

                * The climax where all of the Kindred are digging for Enoch's coffin.

                * Hesha being stunned that the 2nd generation exists and what that means for his faith.

                * A bunch of Archons hiding in the coffin like a clown car.

                * LASOMBRA!
