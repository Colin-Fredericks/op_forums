        Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
        I think for me it's important to emphasize that the horror of VTM is less about the vampire's feelings or connection to god and more about the impact they have on the people around them.
        That is probably the case if you're not religious in the slightest, which many vampires are not.

        I've always had fun with the idea of a person being an atheist, getting embraced, and getting REALLY UNCOMFORTABLE with the fact that so much religion seems to be objectively true. And God and his Angels are assholes against EVEN BIGGER ASSHOLES. Which is essentially HELLBLAZER'S premise. John Constantine is a super hardcore punk atheist secular moralist stuck in a world with objectively real assholes in charge of reality.

        Which is interesting enough, HOW THE GAME IS SUPPOSED TO BE PLAYED in 1st Edition. Caine is the first vampire and he's cursed by God but you're not actually supposed to accept God is good and that it was justice. V:TM was made in the Bible Belt but the counterculture district of Atlanta. There's a strong religious undercurrent to the setting but the fundamental attitude is that Christianity may be literally correct in this world (or partially) but that doesn't mean it's not horribly unfair, authoritarian, and immoral to modern morals.

        If you want to understand the "personal horror" in terms that a non-religious player may enjoy, essentially it's part of the Gothic PUNK element. Which is to say, preferrably in a Monty Python-esque voice, "You're just walking along minding your own business when a supernatural creature from a mythology you don't even believe in comes up to suck out your blood then force their own into you. Without even your consent! . Now you can't go out during the daytime, hunger for the blood of your neighbors, and are now are subject to murderous killing sprees every time you try and argue with the DMV or get cut off in traffic! Oh and a bunch of crazed Baptists and Catholics think you need to die for their religion! Which would be fine to say fuck off regarding but then the demons show up trying to recruit you too! Lousy sabbat!"

        There's no wrong way to play V:TM and go ahead play Angel or Hannibal King as a vampire superero or Blade. You can also play a Mob Hitman or government assassin turned vampire. The Signature Character for 1st Edition was basically a vigilante drug-fighting private detective. 3rd Edition abandoned all personal horror to play bisexual shadow ninjas and pop star Toreador Elders. Plus whatever the hell Theo Bell is when he's not being Blade.

        However, there's actually a lot of fun going with the basic, "You are a perfectly normal twenty something to forty something person who has been turned into a vampire. Your entire understanding of how the world works from evolution to the government to the economy is turned upside down. How do you deal with that?" It might not be 100% weepy horror but yes, in fact, it is fun to imagine, "You can never see your family again and if they ever see you, the local vampire mob boss will kill you or them or both."

        ​It's fun to roleplay the sucky elements of vampirism without the stereotyped Louis, "WHY AM I SO ALONE! *kills woman*."
