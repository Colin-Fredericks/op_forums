        They call Saulot "the Dragon". But they have not said why. I hold that its because he has sown dragons teeth. And that it was time for them to gnaw their way out.

        The ultimate master of Valeren, the discipline of the soul, called to his blood and bid his childer return. And grandchilder, and their childer... every Salubri diablerized by a Tremere, starting to eat their way back up through the souls of the Tremere. Saulot was reclaiming his clan from Tremere. Improved with upgraded Thamaturgy that the Tremere had so obsessively been developing and focusing on for centuries. Really, where did they think that obsession was coming from? And the idea to bond all the young Tremere to their elders... well they mostly ran out of Salubri after a few centuries. There is no Salubri souls in the young Tremere. Once the council of Seven is all Salubri, that was going to be a convenient way to keep the younger ones on a leash. The plans of the Antediluvians are long.

        The destruction of Vienna was Tremeres desperate sacrifice move to keep all the resources out of the hands of his old enemy. Kill all the old Tremere, recreate the clan through the younger ones. Carna, Anarchs etc. Maybe Saulot underestimated the old mages will again. And using the Inquisition against your rivals, letting them kill your childer... that is an old tradition among the kindred. There were very few who could disable ALL the protections and wards of Vienna.

        It is almost funny. "Soul eaters!" was the Tremeres big propaganda push against the Salubri. Saulot must smiled in cold and ancient amusement at that.

        Thats my headcanon of the fall of Vienna, anyway.

                                        Best to worst possibilities
                                        1- Vienna was an inside job; The Co7 realised the pyramid needed a drastic rebranding to survive the near future, especially with the radical shift in sects from Camarilla to Anarch domination. The split of the house allows for chantries in all sects and reduces the troublesome image of the Tremere being an evil monopoly; It's the perfect move.
                                        2- The ease of which Vienna was destroyed was exaggerated, it really took a siege to destroy. This works nicely with any other option.
                                        3- Vienna was due to some BS Saulot/Tremere antideluvian moves that escape the scope of human reason.
                                        4-Vienna was due to the movements of other methuselah.
                                        5-This is a case of mistaken identity; a non-SI group took out Vienna.
                                        6- the SI is the Technocracy (and Vienna might've been given to them by other vampires)
                                        7- The SI is really that powerful despite being a mortal coalition.

This caused most of vampire society (Including even the sabbat) to adopt the masquerade. As for why they didn't do it to Vienna. Probably because it's the Tremere HQ and it relied upon True Faith hunters and relics. While sending said true faith hunters into an area filled with other occult knowledge and material. Best case that happens with that situation: The hunters kill the Tremere inside, and then they die. Next best case: The hunters die but inflict moderate casualties on the Tremere.
