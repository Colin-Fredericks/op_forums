    Originally posted by Black Flag View Post
    Achilli did the Requiem, but only after being the lead developer throughout the Revised edition of Masquerade, which, if anything, made the Sabbat more prominent than they'd ever been, even putting them in the core book.

    However, he never cared for paths of enlightenment, nor have any of the developers since 2e. The general consensus is that the Sabbat's function as the low-Humanity sect is undermined by giving them all these paths that give them an out. So anyone hoping for a return to the 2e treatment of the Sabbat... yeah, don't hold your breath. It was never going to happen. A more realistic hope is that the Sabbat will have some means of using their cult brainwashing/rituals to slow their degeneration, in a manner not unlike the blood cults we see in Cults of the Blood Gods. They are, after all, basically just the biggest of the blood cults.

    As for antitribu, it's not really a thing in V5. Any individual can be in any sect, but the game doesn't treat them as a distinct bloodline, since sect loyalty and heredity are independent of each other. However, it is true that each clan's leadership associated with a particular sect, which suggests that's where most of them will end up because they'll have better representation there. But there are members of each clan in each sect because it would be weird if there weren't. They're not monoliths or hive minds.
    Then they are of no interest to me. Mindless degeneration is dull, which is why the VtR designated bad guys are so flat and uninteresting, Paths and the philosophy behind them, if done well, is by far the most interesting concept in WoD. So far as previewed the Cults are a hollow mockery of that. humanity should be hard, it needs killing under any circumstances to be a degeneration check, with anyone who kills being right on the edge of wassail, or it is meaningless, Paths need to be the way out of that, just as hard, but for different reasons, for 'Beast I be least Beast I become' to mean anything
    Last edited by Taggie; 07-02-2020, 01:13 AM.
