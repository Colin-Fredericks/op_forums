                                                How Mark Rein Hagen viewed the Anarchs

                                                I feel the need to bring up an irony of 1st Edition Anarchs that I believe is kind of interesting and relevant to this thread. I've talked with V:TM's original writer many times and read his discussions and one of the funny things about them is that he absolutely intended the Anarchs to be one of the main factions--and he also absolutely wrote them to be tools.

                                                Mark Rein Hagen envisioned the Camarilla Princes to be the "enemy" of the PCs but he also envisioned the system that opposed them to be equally corrupt in its own way. The working class rhetoric and "fight the power" of the capital A Anarchs was something he envisioned as having been co-opted by the Elders every bit as much as the more overt Camarilla.

                                                To use an example from non-vampire media in The Boys, Vought Media is our heroes' enemy because it controls all of the superheroes and is allied with the Right from the US government. It is opposed by an idealistic US Congresswoman who attacks Vought all the time. Our heroes initially see her as an ally against Vought and indeed give a huge amount of information to them as well as surrender many advantages. Which proves to be a mistake because it turns out she's ALSO in the employ of Vought and they attempt to control their own opposition.

                                                Mark Rein Hagen envisioned the leader of Chicago's Anarchs, Juggler, to be someone who ultimately was there to be sound and fury signifying nothing. We also find out this also applies to the High Humanity Maldavis. The Council Wars were waged by Maldavis in the Eighties and she came INCHES of overthrowing Lodin before the rug was pulled out from under her. Why? Because Maldavis was never more than a stick the Primogen of the city was using to reign in Chicago's Prince and force concessions out of him.

                                                The PCs might become free weapons against the Elders but never an NPC to follow.

                                                V:TM is fundamentally a game about PARANOIA and BETRAYAL so that you can never really "win" the game by allying with any particular faction because you never know who their loyalties really lie with.

                                                And this is actually why every Elder eventually is isolated and lacks the support of a coterie.
