        Milwaukee aside, I'd like to see more published material about cities in the rust belt. They'd be fertile ground for examining how deindustrialization and rapidly-shifting demographics have wrenched Kindred society in modern nights, and how Sabbat-Camarilla-Anarch conflict impacts that. Detroit, despite having been mentioned in several books, has never had a "By Night" book and that's incredibly promising territory.

        I'd personally like to see more stuff regarding the South, in places that aren't Atlanta or New Orleans. Or Florida, for that matter. Two places I've always thought would be really cool to examine would be Knoxville, TN, and Huntsville, AL, for the same reason: their proximity to locales important to WWII and the Cold War (Oak Ridge and Redstone Arsenal, respectively). That could have some really cool implications for inter-sect conflict, conflict between vampires and hunters, or even changing up which clans really matter in those locations.

        I've mentioned before I'm fond of having Lasombra get obsessed with space in modern nights, the same way they get obsessed with the sea. In one chronicle a long time ago, I'd written up that a single Lasombra elder basically single-handedly took over the entire domain of Huntsville, AL, in the '50s and forbade any other vampire from entering the city on pain of Final Death, except for this lone Tremere chantry that was populated by physicist and computer scientist apprentices and their regent. Then one night the Lasombra just disappeared and was never seen again, and rumors persist the Lasombra sneaked onboard a rocket in tenebrous form and got launched to space.

        It was almost certain the Lasombra wasn't actually the first vampire astronaut and it got tagged by an enemy or rival, and even if it did go to space, it almost certainly met Final Death doing so.
