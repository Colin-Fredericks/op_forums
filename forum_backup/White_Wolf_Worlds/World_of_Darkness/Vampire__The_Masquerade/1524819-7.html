            Yeah, but the way Paradox and Chinese Room have handled the previews thus far was also counterproductive. The 15-second Clan videos are a great case-study in bad PR. And then to even let it all end with a whimper in the form of the Ventrue non-reveal where they couldn't even show off 15 seconds... And people love BL1 for its roleplaying-aspects - which they still haven't shown off. If anything, showing the roleplaying-gameplay in the non-combat-sections would have been the more relevant thing to show off to BL1 fans. Unless of course, the game doesn't really have any of the sort and is quite linear instead. But then you'd already know anyway that BL1 fans won't react positively.

            Or the introduction of Phyre as the main-protagonist... they handled that horribly. Of course, it's a named and voiced character like a Shepard but you'd think they'd show off customizability by introducing the female and the male voice/model and then show the character-creation-screen at the beginning where you can modify the face, hair etc. (you know, the stuff you do at the start of most RPG videogames). Instead, I still occasionally see comments where people believe you have to play a female Phyre and her look can't be changed. But of course, they'd think that because the previews thus far are strictly limited to female Phyre and one particular look.

            Personally, I think they would've been better off to have waited till this Summer to start previews. Because the only way it makes sense to me is that it's handled this horribly because there's a lot they simply can't show off right now. And the previews are more stop-gap-measures than anything else. As far as first impressions go, though, that wasn't a good move.
