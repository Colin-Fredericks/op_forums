                Also this is not how they reincarnate, and this thing about breaking with the Fianna because of Kinfolk isn't in the books, and I know at least one confirmed occasion when the Galliard Group completely invented non existing lore, so I guess this was one of those.

                Regular Changelings reincarnate the same way anyone does. They get a whole new, randomly defined life after they die. It's not a change or exchange upon the human soul. The new child is a Changeling since conception.

                Arcadian Sidhe do the exchange thing, but not upon Kinfolk, I'm not sure they'd be able to if they tried. They send the original soul to Arcadia to live as a noble, or so they believe, and this is not something other Fae are totally ok with, there are many who want them to stop, and many Sidhe who did stop and underwent the ritual of the Changeling Way to enter the regular reincarnation cycle.

                Changelings have a huge variety of goals and motivations. They're living stories, they don't just require imagination to survive, they live it with their full being. A Changeling hero is just as heroic and selfish as it comes, and a villain just as despicable as it gets. You can start play with a character that literally eats children for breakfast for the lulz without any consequence, but you can also be a literal paladin that learns about the problems each splat deals with and swears an oath to protect Humanity against all the horrible servants of the Wyrm and other Big Evils, at risk to their immortal Fae souls, for no other reason than that being the right thing to do.

                                                            But that cuts in all directions, so what puts evil Fae in check is that no one will stop good ones from messing with their schemes and possibly their faces.                                                             Reincarnation doesn't protect your memories and personality, either, so while they treat it as true immortality and so death isn't a big deal, they actually for all purposes die like a normal person and what comes later is only the same character in a very lose sense.
