          I don't think there's a concrete answer written anywhere so I can only offer how I interpret things, you'll have to decide what he was for your personal canon. The following is my nerd rant

          DAV20 takes place in 1242 (2 centuries since Augustus' embrace) and offers the Giovan(n)i as their own separate entry in the Bloodline section who still share the Cappadocian clan disciplines, and the younger Giovanni retain the deathly pallor as a weakness. But it states that Auggie and his closest descendants never lost the ability to use the Blush of Life yet already had a painful bite, even though they shouldn't be afflicted with the Curse of Lamia for another couple centuries, which is hypothesized in-lore to be because "the horrors he saw as a mortal man carried through his blood".

          I assume everyone who had the painful bite by 1242 were the only ones who knew of and were directly involved in the conspiracy to usurp the Cappadocians, and subconsciously the Blood was instantly distancing itself from the parent clan as if it had a mind of its own

          Vitae is quite spiritual and doesn't always have codified rules for what it can and can't do when its so potent, so maybe their refusal to consider themselves Cappadocians for eternity put the foot in the door for the bloodline to begin. The younger Giovanni who didn't know of the plot might not have considered themselves Cappadocians either but they didn't have the ability nor intention to overtake the parent clan, so maybe their Beast's were resigned to being ashy skeletons forever. Meanwhile the eldest Giovanni had entered unlife knowing the clan they were brought into was just a stepping stone and not their final form, already preparing to shed the skin they were forced to wear so long as Cappadocius was the Antediluvian

          The diablerie of Cappadocius definitely cemented the Giovanni as a clan wholly removed from the Cappies, but I think it would have happened anyway after a few hundred more years if the Conspiracy of Isaac never succeeded, with the caveat being the vast majority of the bloodline wouldn't have the painful bite and kept the deathly pallor. The Gio clanbooks explain that Potence and Dominate naturally suit the macho demeanor of a proud Italian man, so those might very well have become the bloodline's Disciplines naturally kinda like how the City Gangrel slowly shifted theirs yet kept the Gangrel weakness

          Its worth noting that Augustus' childe Cladius (the main instigator of Cappadocius' death) didn't have a single dot of Auspex or Fortitude even after 400 years when the diablerie happened, so I think he was an entirely different clan from the first night he was embraced, along with most of the other main conspiracists. At the same time its pretty trivial to pickup out of clan Disciplines at such a low generation so I can't actually point to that as hard evidence, that's just how justify that part of Cladius' loresheet. Still, you'd think an Elder would pick up at least one or two dots of Fortitude by accident if they were naturally inclined towards it

          tldr: 1. Augustus and his main goons were always a separate bloodline, 2. the diablerie itself might have swapped the clan's disciplines to what the eldest Giovanni preferred and the Lamia curse finished the job by giving the future generations of the clan Augustus' gnarly bite, and 3. he never had the Cappadocian curse

          This is all based on how V20 posits things so I can't offer any insight as to how the previous versions reconciled the Gio/Capp divide, its basically just me filling in the lore's blanks with speculative headcanon. Man do I love Cappadocian lore tho​

                          According to VDA20, Augustus Giovani and his closest clanmates had a painful bite, while other Giovani had the Cappadocian weakness. I presume these other Giovani with the Cappadocian weakness were embraced by Cappadocians, not by Augustus or his childer, and were Giovani by mortal family and affiliation, rather than by embrace, as part of the broader plan to bring the family into the clan.

                          Also, the painful bite is not the same as the Lamia weakness (infectious bite), even though it would be later called Lamia's curse. And the painful bite becomes the weakness of all Hecata in V5.

                          I don't know if the authors had a clear metaplot idea on this issue. An answer may be given in V5 Hecata material, since Nagaraja, Harbingers of the Skull, Giovanni, etc. now all have the same weakness (the painful bite), but we still don't know why.

                          I like the idea that Cappadocius was an entropic mage before the Embrace, given that he and his descendants all have the signs of jhor. I can't think of a reason why Lamia and Giovani would have two different bite-related curses, nor why all Hecata would take this curse later on, but there's clearly some good reason implied.

                          Augustus Giovani was a death mage before the Embrace. Perhaps he avoided the normal Cappadoci curse in part (he still looks like a corpse, but he can hide it with blood) because he could take precautions against jhor before the Embrace. He shows, however, a psychological sign of jhor: obsession with tearing down the Shroud.

                          A very weak theory, more poetical than probable, could be that Lamia got her curse because Lilith is the Mother of Monsters, but the curse of Caine (and Lilith's possible dislike for the children of Adam, though she doesn't seem bitter in Revelations of the Dark Mother over that, and doesn't have much reason to be) twisted that into infecting humans somehow. Lamia was not interested in death particularly, neither before nor after the Embrace, so her bloodline have no sign of jhor.
                          Augustus was embraced within Cappadocius larger plan to diablerise God and was already on his own a callous devourer of wealth and souls as the head of the Giovani necromerchants. He might have avoided for himself some marks of jhor (he was quite mundane and attached to life and power for Cappadocian standards after all), but his greed and disregard for the pain he inflicted manifested in his own curse.

                          Nagaraja, Lamia and Giovanni all destroy or infect or hurt what they feed on. If Cappadocii and Samedi (and jhor tainted mages in general) obsess over death and take on its visage, Nagaraja, Lamia and Giovanni manifest Oblivion's destructive Maw who seeks to destroy all life.

                                  I submit to the gallery that Augustus Giovanni was not embraced, and is not a vampire.

                                  All information that we have about Augustus is very clear that he was drained of blood by Constancia and Japheth using primitive medical tools, then Cappadocius gave them a quantity of vitae in a clay pot which was used on Augustus. They only used some of the vitae, the rest being sealed with wax and being ensorceled, which became known as the True Vessel. Augustus is only said to have had one childe, which is Claudius whom was his biological child, and there is no proof except of rumor that he actually embraced Claudius. Claudius was "killed" by Augustus when, Claudius sacked the Erinyes Temple and potentially destroyed the True Vessel. His body was given to Lazarus/Capuchin for some reason.

                                  Do note that one of the scenarios that happen in Giovanni Chronicles I has it, during the "diablerie" of Cappadocious there's and aside if the players were to some how kill Augustus; Cappadocious will bring him back to [un]life some how so that Augustus can finish the "diablerie". I say "diablerie" in quotes because Augustus only gets Cappadocious' blood and not his soul, which is the requirement for a diablerie, as Cappadocious' soul flees beyond the Shroud. Before the Giovanni were introduced into the clan, the Cappadocians were masters of the physical body, disease, and its death. Logically antedeluvian level Mortis should be something like "Create Revenant," or an equivalent undead creature.

                                  Therefore, Augustus is a Psuedo Vampire Zombu type creature, that was created to be the repository of Cappadocious' earthly power so that he could explore the Underworld and attempt to diablerize God which he believed was accessible from there. After he would use Augustus to re-inhabit the physical realm with his new power, but well the Maelstrom really did a number on him, and he became a Spectre.

                                  As for the other Giovanni, they're just Cappadocian offshoots like the Lamia, and gained their own version of a similar "curse." The painful bite has several supposed origins: Curse from Lamia, As a result of the True Vessel Curse by Japheth, or a botched embrace. There are supposed records of Giovanni Cappadocians with the painful bite long before the Conspiracy of Issac, so the whole Lamia curse thing is mostly apocryphal.

                                  In my opinion the Samedi are the same experiment that Cappadocius tried with Augustus, but it was done by Lazarus instead on The Baron. The difference between a 3rd generation and a 4th generation, trying the same thing would be why the Samedi had such an extreme version of the Cappadocian flaw. Lazarus was the one who embraced Lamia herself, and was for some reason deathly (heh) afraid of her because of something she told him.
