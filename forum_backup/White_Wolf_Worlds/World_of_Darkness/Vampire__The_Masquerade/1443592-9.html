                            Predator types are too crude. The idea is fine, the execution is a joke.                             Speaking of predator types; the idea of resonances is very much at odds with the idea that you get disciplines through hunting a certain way/practicing the disciplines/ that they're a natural extention of you and that clans who are very charismatic and tend to seduce are more likely to have presence whilst those that are more forceful are going to have potence. I don't think the first four games explicitly said how vampires cultivate disciplines, but the implications are pretty strong. Resonances could certainly be a secondary thing for learning powers, but they aren't going to be the main thing.
