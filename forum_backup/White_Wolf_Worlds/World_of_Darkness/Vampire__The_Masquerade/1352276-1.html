    Second Inquisition

    + A good sixty vampires from London simply left the city, including Lord Camden's group, for the surrounding U.K. countryside because the idea of fighting the Inquisition was ridiculous.

    + Mithras actually was the one who gave the London police, MI5, and SAS evidence of vampires because he wanted to have a cat and mouse game with hunters. It was a glorious war.

    + Yes, he was killed, but was he KILLED?

    + The Second Inquisition is composed of an alliance of the Knights Templar, Judges, Inquisition, various Muslim groups, CDC scientists, SAD, an NSA subgroup, and several CIA black ops groups. It has also recruited the surviving Hunters and a bunch of Technocracy acolytes who form various conspiracies they didn't know answered back to supernaturals.

    + There's about 5000 to 7000 or so members total and its threat is somewhat exaggerated. It seems omnipresent due to the below and their access to mundane resources combined with Kindred paranoia.

    + Whenever the Second Inquisition was sending hordes of well-armed soldiers with no regards to casualties into vampire lairs or engaged in shoot-outs in the street, that's actually the Technocracy because they don't have accountability, limits on their funding, or a problem just cloning some new Men in Black or HIT marks.

    + The "Hellsing" Group of the Union is a relatively small group of fanatics in the Union who went that way due to Ravnos. They don't control the Second Inquisition but are patronizing it like Marcus Vitel and taking advantage of it.

    It's mostly a combination of the former Invictus group and John Courage's friends having found a new windmill to tilt.

    Lucita

    + Lucita was shown Underworld as a joke but has since become obsessed with bad girl action movies.

    + Lucita is about 5'0 and this bothers her a great deal, so she uses Dominate and Obternation to appear taller.

    Vykos

    + Vykos was gender fluid in a time when they had no name for this and they've developed an interest in the community/subject since then.

    Baby Chorus

    + Baby Chorus basically sounds like AC/DC and they based their sound in-universe on them. One of AC/DC's songs is a straight up cover.

    Author of Cthulhu Armageddon, I was a Teenage Weredeer, Straight Outta Fangton, Lucifer's Star, and the Supervillainy Saga.

        Melinda Galbraith, Sabbat Regent and devotee of Nergal, used to subtly steer the Sabbat Inquisition against rival infernalists in order to raise herself above suspicion.

        For all of the Second Inquisition's rampaging, there are still whole colonies of Gargoyles out there. This is because Gargoyles are cool.

        The Guruhi made peace with the Niktuku and reabsorbed them into the main clan centuries ago. What's hunting the Nosferatu is something else. A good bet would be Black Spiral Dancers, Unseelie Fae, or some other supernatural that isn't above competing for living space in a sewer.

        Half the Antedeluvians have, at one time or another over the ages, decided that the best way to survive the Jyhad is to develop their signature disciplines to the point where they can become something other than a vampire. It remains to be seen whether any of them have transcended their respective curses well enough to have avoided the Gehenna War.
