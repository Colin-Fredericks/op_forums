                                Originally posted by Amalasuintha View Post
                                I see. Though not sure a lot of WoD characters can compare to the insane stunts of dnd high level mages. They're a special batch of complete nutty stunt. Granted I have a friend who's good at that so maybe my view is skewed.
                                That was an extreme exaggeration, especially because they model completely different kinds of stories. D&D is for a game where a group of mortals can feasibly grow enough in skill alone to challenge a Methuselah in their Haven and win.

                                Problem is: class level in D&D is about experience. You're not turned or born as a 10th level character. Sometimes, in some editions, you can have Hit Dice that give you more power to represent your innate abilities, but those are not Class Levels.

                                An Epic Boon is something you develop late in your career as you continue to amass power and experience. So...

                                You can't be Embraced into an Epic Boon, and
                                You can't Diablerize your way to an Epic Boon.

                                Without serious revision in the basic rules, and this doesn't seem to be the goal, this means a newly Embraced Fledgling is 13º Generation, will remain 13º Generation until the 20th level, then lower Generation by taking Epic Boons.

                                Unless the class has some very specialized way of handling Generation within itself, the only way in the regular rules to translate how Generation work to D&D 5th would be either as a Background or an Origin Feat. And that would require enough balance between them that you don't feel one as a more optimized choice than the other. Or would need strict control by the GM on which Generation everyone will be at.
