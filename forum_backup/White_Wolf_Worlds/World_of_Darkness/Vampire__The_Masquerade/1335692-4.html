                        I understand what you mean, but first the problem is that to have a mature discussion about this we need a mature reception to this. In the sense that, when you simply take the opposite stance and start to argue that the change shouldn't happen, you just help to polarize thoughts and lead to the immature reactions that lead to immature developments, be them in whichever direction.

                        R.I.P. Gender Bender Ghostbusters, really wished you ended up a good movie. You didn't.

                        But for the matter at hand, this is one of the situations where you can't really hurt much of the established setting with this. All we like to discuss a lot about them taking what's said about them at face value, the fact is that the setting primarily treats Antediluvians as legends, everything said being said by someone within it with limited knowledge and bias. Most of them have assumed genders when all witnesses to their very existence, if there's any, came into the picture already so late that even identifying sex or gender was hard or impossible. It hardly plays any role, either, with very few exceptions.

                        So here we have it, adding stories and theories about the Antes that contradict with the previous ones isn't just possible, it is actually how the setting is supposed to work. With that, it isn't hard to implement representation and diversity to the story while improving it without negating previous statements.

                            Been a while since I posted here, and it'll probably be a while still after, but I have to say; I find this take a bit strange for VtM.

                            Vampire's setting has always bent and twisted its past around current politics, current trends and the current climate. V5 is literally the WoD through a 9/11 lens. VtM1e and 2e was cemented in a world where Anne Rice and the X-Files universes were the norm, with secret conspiracies and historical figures mixing with each other on the regular, where major wars were smokescreens for undead politics... until Revised literally undid that to make kindred have a smaller impact, around the time inevitable nihilism in the face of inevitable apocalypses captured the zeitgeist of pop culture.

                            VtM has long been retaining itself to keep up with politics and trends. Having the in-universe mythological monsters with genders other than Male and Token Female is actually less of a change than we've already seen.
