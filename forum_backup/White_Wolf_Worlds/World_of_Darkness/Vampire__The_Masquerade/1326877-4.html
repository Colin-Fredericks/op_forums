                                                Well, I didn't get no info at all about cancellation or production difficulty (perhaps it could be rationalized from the fact of Covid is going on but...), and I do remember hearing about it in the last videos. Something like "oh upcoming products...Sabbat guide book, Player Guide, Werewolf Apocalypse, yada-yada". So I'm assuming yes.
                                                What I would love to see is more useful systems like Response Algorithm (present in the Anarch book) that offers a base net of responses from a big individual or group to the actions of the coterie; Institutions simple attributes and rules for use (present in the Camarilla book) that makes it so that big groups, corporations, agencies and organizations can be represented mechanically. I find these very awesome, expanding the game and giving more tools that fill me up with ideas.

                                                But also, more Background options, more Loresheets (specially ones that are related to events, clans or ideas and can be used at more chronicles), more Merits and Flaws cuz the ones available are awesome but we need more, and the Coterie Merits (in the Companion) is a very cool idea. More Discipline power options would be nice but it could also unbalance the game or some such... so I'm not that interested.

                                                Other than that I think they will surprise us, for there really isn't much the game really needs right now, it's a very complete experience. So, more chronicles, lore or examples (of Touchstones, Convictions, types of coteries and predators) is mostly what I imagine.
