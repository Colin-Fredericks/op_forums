                                YMMV strikes again. I grew up in the typically insular US-centric bubble that WoD developed in, so learning about these things doesn't surprise me because I always saw the setting as blinded by its own conceits. It doesn't make the system terrible mind, and I still enjoy it, but I do think its worth noting those flaws and problems they create (notably things like KotE and KotEK) that stemmed directly from such similar 'blinders.' Something I find charming is going to annoy someone else because they have a context informing that which I lack. Won't be the first time such things have bitten WoD on the butt, and probably won't be the last.

                                I think its safe to say while some of the Pre-V20 (or even V20) concepts can be recycled, they probably wouldn't ever be lifted out and transplanted verbatim and without change. Times change, and so will the material to at least some degree. For example, while the 'origin/nature' and alot of the fluff of the KotE game may be changed, I think alot of the mechanics and their intentions could be adopted in some form. I always did like how they tied 'development' to virtue/belief rather than to generation. I consider that a good 'alternative' to Vampire development, and I think it provides a more workable and nuanced approach to Golconda than the 'all or nothing' approach. And given that Saulot either received or stole the Golconda concept from the East it fits rather well IMHO.

                                Indeed you could adopt such mechanics to alot of belief systems OTHER than humanity. Under Golconda or other methods. Like I think it has uses in salvaging parts of Ebony Kingdom that made them weaker than 'European' Kindred, of course I always interpreted that material to sort of ignore generational limits anyhow so it wasn't THAT great a transition since there were similarities to KotE there too.

                                        I really like the Wan Kuei (or Hungry Ghosts, if you prefer), and would love to see a playable update for the Storyteller 5th edition, and don't mind in what format it comes (supplement, splat, companion)... And yeah, to get the culture, politics, views and history right and weave it well with the WoD part of the game (which is the most important) is a tricky problem, that requires sensitive and smart solutions. A lot of talking I would say.

                                        For me also, the Wan Kuei are more like a "spiritual/ghost vampire", as opposed to the "blood vampire" that are the Cainites and Laibon (let's call them Kindred for simplicity sake), and maybe the Drowned Legacies too. As such, these spiritual vampires are much more akin to wraith (restless dead), demons/changelings/mummies than the kindred, but I think it's fair to call them either vampires or hungry ghosts. But what is interesting for me is that as souls that came back to their bodes not only they are akin to revenants (as real world folklore), but also that they could and maybe should be present in other cultures. In my view, Hungry Ghosts or spiritual vampires should be present in practically every region with animistic culture. And they could, more than most, rub shoulders with mummies often (as the Undying would mostly sniff them out to find if they are following a righteous path or not), as they are both beings of spirit returned to their body in some way. And interesting note also, is how in Demon the Fallen, it is said that "in the beginning" the reality was multifaced, and the Creation was as much Abrahamic angels with wings molding the world, as particles and electrons and heat making the universe in the course of hundreds of thousands of years. That would mean that they could be Olorum's (Yoruba) many faces or even agents shaping the lands and skies, or Atum who made Tefnut and Shu who made Geb and Nut (Egyptian) whose creations and breeding made the world itself, or even the Chinese Taoist "the Way gave birth to unity, unity gave birth to duality, duality gave birth to trinity, trinity gave birth to the myriad creatures". Everything could be right, and the idea that after the Fall, the Age of Wrath and the Deluge the multifaceted reality became shattered and not singular permits that all myths and religions to be somewhat true in it. I mean, you could say that August Personage of Jade, and the Dragon and Empress could be all analogs to "angels" or even to the creator himself. In that way, the Cainites and the Wan Kuei would both have been cursed by angels (I mean, most of the curses of Caine were laid down by angels). In that sense, they are basically two sides of the same coin, the Kindred and the Hungry Dead.

                                        The spiritual vampires as I said could, in that way, be present in America, Africa, Asia, Oceania and even some regions of Europe too. Just as the Kindred should also be. I mean, I agree, the blood vampires should be worldwide phenomenon. As they are not bound by land, belief or spirits (and that's maybe), so they probably already are in Asia, having their fill, and probably wrestling with Hungry Dead for territory, herd and secular power. I would even assume that the tradition Asian Kindred, like the Laibon of Africa, wouldn't be too keen on having American or European Kindred (what we could say are the Cainites) invading their territory, and would try to keep them away (maybe that way the Cainites think the Wan Kuei did what mostly other Kindred did?). So yeah, they would coexist, and some fun power dynamics could be had, and still maintain that: while the written material until now was true, there has been some misunderstanding (like there not having Kindred in Asia, only Hungry Dead, before Cainites). And I mean, we saw how V5 maintained a lot of truths while reshaping the setting and the system (which I'm biased to say I loved wholeheartedly); blood, generations, bonds, disciplines, clans, it all kind of changed, but yet stayed the same.


                                        So in the end I want to see the Wan Kuei anew, and I think they should still be non-Kindred (but that there should be traditional Kindred in Asia, and other continents, as well), even if the P'o, Hun, Yin and Yang and Dharma part be a little retooled or at least simplified, cuz I remember how time-consuming and annoying was to keep track of the Hierarchy of Sin, even worse when there were two simultaneously with the Laibon, or even worserer when there were multiple Paths of Wisdom in a table. And even now with V5 sometimes I forget the Ressonance and Humors when in play, so I want less with more punch (personally). I understand that it could be just me being bad at multitasking d=

                                        Some other idea would to irritate both old and new fans alike by making the Wan Kuei capable of Embracing via rituals (like something out of Mummy), or even a newfound power to spit the Dharma infused chi into a corpse (like the Embrace via vitae) of a willful soul, making it comeback most the same way of the Hungry Dead (with some 2 months time till then maybe). Of course, this expenditure of Chi and especially Dharma would be heavy (like Embracing in 5th edition), and not something to make Wan Kuei pop like water on hot oil.
                                        Buuuut I would only make this if people really wanted to make the Wan Kuei more akin to Kindred, and would be more of a metaplot change than anything (a new way to do Wan Kueis). I mean, technically the Bodhisattvas were gone, the last Great Maelstrom wrecked the shit out of the spiritual world, and all the Hungry Dead society would be in disarray or at the very least at a moment of change and reorganization. Not much different from the changes of V5. But who knows, I'm not sure we are going to get Wan Kuei anytime soon, and although I love them and is homebrowing them up, I'm not in a hurry to see them 5thfied.


                                        Strange... When coincidence seems too convenient, I prefer to call it fate.

                                        -Blood Omen: Legacy of Kain d=

                                                    The funny thing is that several things in V5 were more or less taken from KotE. The concept of Coterie or Wu composition having mechanical interactions, the concept that like Demon Shintai disciplines are built with choices, etc.

                                                    KotE will always be a niche game due to the cultural knowledge requirements on the part of the ST & Players to make it really click. Vampire on the other hand has been largely removed from any particular cultural influence on a game level from the start. Thus KotE will never be a flagship line for White Wolf, but it is an excellent side project that adds some much needed variation for playing a "vampire" in the main line.

                                                    Sadly it's not like White Wolf can just try to sell KotE in parts of asia to make up for the line not selling in western countries. The biggest markets with disposable income in asia each comes with its own issues. Japan is the home of anime, south Korea has e-sports, and china..... has made it illegal to use time travel or other story elements that revise history, thus making most of the meta story of WoD that is based on real world history an automatic problem, this is also on top of the variety of problems where the dark, gritty, over sexualized, edgelord nature of WoD would probably be banned by chinese cultural ministers.
