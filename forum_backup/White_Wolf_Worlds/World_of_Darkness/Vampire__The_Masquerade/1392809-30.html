        Lasombra in V5

        What I liked

        1. Betrayal-Five: I liked the idea of the Lasombra betraying the Sabbat because I've always had it told to me the Lasombra are treacherous, they're untrustworthy, they only care about themselves, and they are Social Darwinists to the extreme. We rarely got to see it, though because they were so neck deep in the Sabbat and hunted down anyone who joined the Camarilla. It worked for me because it was a genuine surprise they would turn on their fellows like Littlefinger did Ned Stark. They TOLD US they were untrustworthy after all.

        2. More Lasombra in the Camarilla: I've always liked the Lasombra and feel like their strengths and clan culture play well to the Camarilla. They're riddled with intrigue, infighting, politics, and subtlety. That kind of thing exists in the Sabbat but it's not really an environment that brings out their absolute best since the Sabbat are more like Klingons than Romulans. Monomancy means that you never really get Toreador games of gossip and frame-ups.

        3. The Antediluvian being alive is out of the bag: This happened in BJD but this is now much more overtly so. It's been a secret for 600 years and it's nice to see it finally revealed to the rest of the Lasombra clan even if it's by word of mouth rather than the alternative.

        4. The idea of a "Lasombra Silent Majority": I like the idea that Sierra represented. There's apparently a bunch of Sabbat who would love to gather power among mortals and mundane resources but are forced to avoid it because of the responsibilities to their pack. It shows that the Camarilla would have an appeal to some of them so they could just build their own kingdoms of resources.

        5. The Cult of Shalim: I believe Shalim is the Lasombra Antediluvian rather than an ancient eldritch horror because I like the idea that it's transcended things and wants to unmake creation but either way, it's nice to have a kind of Nephandi-esque group.

        6. Marcus Vitel is responsible for the Lasombra Defection: I love a lot of the classic NPCs and it's great when they get mentioned or incorporated into the existing plotlines. Marcus Vitel having retaken Washington D.C. from the Sabbat with the Anarchs, only to work to get his Clan to convert to the Camarilla (despite the fact the Camarilla almost killed him) strikes me as the kind of genius chessmaster moves you expect from a Methuselah. I also appreciate the inclusion of Talley.

        7. The Sacrifice is kickass: Possibly the best Chronicle ever made for a vampire supplement. Making the Lasombra join the Camarilla based on PC decision may be a bit fanservice but it's fanservice I can get behind.

        What I didn't like

        1. Not much Sabbat information: We get some allusions to what the Sabbat are up to in the Gehenna War and that some are doing their usual thing when the Lasombra defect but I felt they could have inserted a little info about the Sabbat.

        2. Obternation: Frankly, I think some of the combinations of Disciplines don't make much sense. Quietus is mixed with Blood Sorcery? Necromancy is mixed with Obternation? I feel like the combination is a good idea in general but I feel like the choices are counter-intuitive. Obternation and Quietus should have been mixed with Obfuscate. Necromancy should have mixed with Blood Sorcery.

        3. The Lasombra Bane: Is ridiculous as there's no reason that Lasombra should have a problem with technology. This is nothing that was hinted at in previous editions and makes their Bane even worse than the Nosferatus (which was dialed back to "really fucking ugly" not "monstrous nightmare creature"). It also means they have to have ghouls when I think the Lasombra have never been a ghoul-heavy clan, that's been the Tzimisce.

        4. No Lucita: Nuff said. Even more so if she's actually dead-dead.

        5. Lasombra craftiness is told not shown: The Lasombra are going to agree to be Blood Bound to the Princes because they know about the Vinculum and the Camarilla doesn't. Also, they know that they have to kill a bunch of Sabbat on their way out so, "Deliver a dead Sabbat Elder for your defection" is fine. However, that's mostly left to inference. We should have had it spelled out the Keepers were playing the Camarilla.

                        Originally posted by Chris24601 View Post
                        To me, the Thin-Bloods feel like a completely different entity than they were in Revised/V20.

                        In the previous editions they were weaker, but still functioned like other vampires for the most part. They took damage the same way (though sunlight was only lethal to 15/16th Gen it still burned them with no option to mitigate it completely) and had normal vampire disciplines for the most part (inceptors were rare) that they learned up to the 4th, 3rd or 2nd dot depending on their generation. Biological activity was a rare side-effect in 15-16th gens of having spent large amounts of blood and not a regular occurrence.

                        Now they take damage like mortals, can walk around in sunlight and be biologically indistinguishable from a human, pick up random disciplines from feeding while not (usually) being able to actually permanently learn any, but can mix their blood up with things like nylon or what have you to create what amount to magic potions.

                        I don’t even want to think about how V5 intends to implement Dhampirs given these changes (and on the few occasions I get to play instead of run, Dhampirs are my jam). Hunger dice don’t fit the previous lore where expending their supernatural mojo left them exhausted rather than hungry.

                        For similar reasons picking up disciplines from feeding doesn’t fit Dhampirs since they can’t actually feed on humans at all, their supernatural energy is restored with time and rest. And a lot of fun with a Dhampir was specifically in having a very limited, but also specific set of tools to use to help you survive in the world with vampires.

                        The problem I foresee is that with how they’ve set up V5, the thin-bloods ate quite a bit of the Dhampirs conceptual space while also incorporating things that make them utterly un-Dhampir-like (in the sense of how they worked in Revised/V20) so you can’t really use a thin-blood to play a Dhampir.
                        While TotTB said Dhampir is technically a Revenant there are different rules in BJD diary where it is said, that Dhampirs can feed and so create more vitae from plain blood instead of Revenants that beside drinking from a Vampire there is only the option to wait.
                        But being able to create blood from Vitae is after comparesing the only real advantege in comparison to Revenants. While Dahmpirs pay at Creation same for Disciples as normal vampires they get less freebies then Revenants and need to pay the same amount of XP as Ghouls for learning disciples but can not even go beyond one Dot even if they would drink Vitae from a low generation vampire. So as much as I love Dhampirs from my opinion they overdid with saying "Dhampirs are just Vampires with ultrathinblood."
