    Well...
    The Vitae must flow directly from the sire to the childe.
    It cannot be placed in an intermediary vessel or otherwise delayed, because its supernatural properties quickly decay after leaving the vampire's body.
    However... there are exceptions

    One of the best known is Cappadocius, who was not even present when Augustus was embraced, Japeth and Constancia used his vitae in a clay jar that later become the True Vesseal
    Considering the Cappadocians had a ritual to preserve blood, they may have used some form of ritual magic to prepare the vitae (as a side note, the Ventrue Antediluvian said to stored in his lost fortress - among many thing, like a claw of Fenris and other funny things - a pot filled with Caine's vitae... )

    The other lesser known is the Baali Embrace
    The Baali drains his or her victim to near death and tosses him or her into the pit. In just one of the corpses the Baali hid their blood inside one of the corpses' heart. If the victim manages to find the heart before they die and drink the blood, they are considered worthy of becoming Baali. Ironically, this makes the Baali the only clan that regularly gives their victim the choice of becoming a vampire or choosing to die.
    Still the Baali, while present, parted with his vitae and hid it; the organ pit, the coprses, the heart preparation... The Embracing ritual of the Baali is one of their most disturbing rituals and it takes time

    Both cases magic is probably involved (or if you take the Ashur was the Baali progenitor, and Ashur = Cappadocius, then it could be the unique trait of his blood, the result of the touch of the Underwolrd or basically anything)

    With the Blood of Zapathasura? Well it state it is thick, sentient ropes vitae, maybe, perhaps, probably "him"; ol' Zapatashura became Venom... and probably it is his Heartblood

    Not many diableirised a 4th or 3rd Generation and remained mentally intact however
    I think - I could be wrong - there is no recorded, canon act of Amaranth (well maybe the first when a Children of Haqim ate Amarantha) when the consumed not took control in a form or another or outright survived
    Maybe Minos? Tho' one could argue Helena changed after she ate her sire
    And Yezidian refugees from the remains of the Aral Sea refer to the force of ontological evil as "Minos, who is the son of Arikel", who rests deep within the salt flats of the dried out sea. They believe that, when "the other Eye opens", this force of evil will rise again. This implies that Minos, or another being that assumed his identity, is apparently still active in some form.​
    Arakur of Ur was diablerized by his own childe Lantla who was being controlled by the demon Drakonskyr, who later became Tiamat, but since Drakonskyr already set up a shop inside Lantla's soul and mind I think Arakur not really had any chance in that case

    Even Troile and Ilyes is questionable

    A bit of side tangent, but in the Second City, He had the reputation of being cold and passionless, Troile - whose gender is ambiguous, was known for being a passionate and fiery person, in stark contrast to that of their sire - Troile was Embraced by the "Learned", sometime around 3000 BCE.
    According to the Tzimisce Clanbook, Mekhet was a sibling of the Eldest (and thus possibly another childe of Ynosh/Enoch) who, with the Brujah antediluvian "played shadow games with mortals, delighting in their own cunning and agendas"
    ​There is a little inconsistency In DAV: Road of Humanity, the Prodigals told of a story in which Arikel and Troile were troubled by the way the Cainites of the First City behaved, and turned to Saulot for aid. The Healer stated that only empathy would help them retain a link to humanity and so, both Troile and Arikel went to work on a project of art that spanned a hundred and one years, before they brought it before Caine. The work inspired exactly those emotions within the audience that they had inspired in the mortal population. Caine was deeply touched by it and called Arikel and Troile to his side, forgiving them past trespasses. These prodigals hold this as the origin of the Road of Humanity
    That is not really a question, Troile was Embraced in the Second City, so the Troile in the First City was porbably Ilyes - Dirty Secrets of the Black Hand and Gehenna refer to Troile as female, suggesting that Her Sire was in love with her, but could not express it as he knew not how to deal with his emotions.
    It actually fits nicely in the whole picture
    At first, back in the old days, Ilyies and Arikel had a working relationship, so to speak, both interested in Mortals.As well as he had a working relationship with Mekhet, whom he "played shadow games with mortals, delighting in their own cunning and agendas" - from a Vulcan-like cold and passionate point of view this could mean experiments, cruel and cold social experiments and so on; the; the project of art that spanned a hundred and one years coud be from his early days, when he was freshly Embraced and still had that spark in him... and gradually lost it. But trusted Sualot enough to turn to him with his concerns.
    Ilyies was allegedly slain by his childe, Troile, who may have committed diablerie on him in the process BUT some Brujah, such as a commentator on the Erciyes Fragments, believe that his death was a lie spread by the Ventrue.
    In Gehenna's "general description" some stories, however, claim that Troile and [Brujah] had been the same person, as few to no records exist that place them together, and that [Brujah] faked his own destruction and assumed Troile's identity. (also in Gehenna the scenario "Fair is Foul" reintroduces Brujah to the childer of Caine under the name of Ilyes. Here, Ilyes faked his own death; having complete mastery over time, he escaped his destruction, using his trademark Discipline, Temporis,)
    Clanbook: Brujah Revised mentions records claiming Troile had no gender or changed it on whim.

    When he is not escaped via Temporis, peronally I tend to go with the later version; a bit headcanon, but could work. So, Embrace, especially Embracing your lover was a direct violation of Caine's dictum. Because of Ilyes's lack of emotion - or more precisely, he could not express his love as he knew not how to deal with his emotions - Troile feared that her Sire had the intention to destroy them, in an attempt to rectify his mistake of Embracing progeny. Thus, Troile paniced and attacked his Sire. Who consumed Her. And the act shattered his already unstable mind and assumed Her identity. A bit of reverse Mithras-Monthy-way. This would explain why Troile wasn't punished with final death for the Diablerie. It not happened. The others looked the confused and bloody mouthed Ilyies and pitied him, who began to pretend he was Troile. Even some crossdressing could have been in play. The "had no gender or changed it on whim" part could be viewed as the vampire basically genderlessness, since they are dead bodies, like Angels, basically have no gender, just gender identity.
    From there, he was Troile

    The official version according to clan Ventrue members states that theri Antediluvian may have been destroyed outside of the walls of the Second City by the Brujah antediluvian... which is firmly denied by the Brujah Clan. So probably it not really happened, since the Venture Antediluvian was Embracing left and right later on...

    Sorry, back to the original topic, so eating that blood maybe would be a mistake and probably not really usable to Embrace
    But then again, if it is his sentinet heartblood, basically him in a new liquid form? Maybe?
    Would be a good idea?
    No
    Absolutely not​
