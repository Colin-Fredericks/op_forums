    Do the Affects of Blood Bonding Work if the Kindred is in Torpor?

    10-02-2021, 09:58 PM
    For instance, say one kindred has another kindred staked in his basement and drinks from them. Does that kindred need to worry about the affects of the bond while the other is staked?

      Originally posted by omenseer View Post
      For instance, say one kindred has another kindred staked in his basement and drinks from them. Does that kindred need to worry about the affects of the bond while the other is staked?

      If there's vitae in the torpid kindred to be consumed, i'd say yes, definitely.

      That kindred - unless if possessed of 6+ Animalism, Auspex, Dominate or Presence - might not be in condition to consciously exploit such a bond, but a budding sentiment of devotion/love/loyalty/worshipfulness for the torpid kindred will certainly develop.

      A twisted sort of attachment to the sleeping one and its wellbeing that could go into all sorts of peculiar directions depending on their previous relationship and how much the "drinker" actually knows or not of the sleeper, its mind, likes, dislikes, allies, rivals & etc.

      That said, i can definitely see some individual neonate, ancilla or even elder theorizing that a kindred's vitae might be somewhat inert, attempt exactly that and slowly develop the bond and some sort of fondness without being quite conscious of it because it has one's "paramour" in a handy box close by and safe.

              I don't see why not. The blood bond isn't a consciously activated power. It's part and parcel of drinking another vampire's vitae.

              Whether the bond is enough to get the staked vampire out of trouble is another question. Gehenna had a fun scenario with a zealous priest (I think also a hunter?) who'd chained up a vampire in his basement, drained her enough times to become fully bonded, and he still kept her chained up. The bond just made him madly possessive and might have also kept him from killing her.

                      I would say yes, absolutely. This is one reason why having a Methuselah’s Thirst is so inconvenient, you eventually end up having to kill vampires.
