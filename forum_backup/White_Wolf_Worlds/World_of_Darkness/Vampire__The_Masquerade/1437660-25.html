                    The tunnel vision is strong with this one.
                    Prestation isn't something Harpies keep in their pockets and get to chuck out when they decide they don't like you. If a harpy were to decide that they weren't going to acknowledge a deal, and another third party comes up to acknowledge that the harpy did indeed witness a transaction, the harpy reputation and status will be trashed so hard that they might even get blood hunted. Prestation is sacred. It transcends sects. You only reneg if you're absolutely sure the guy will die before news can get out you didn't play fair.

                    Bloody the Ventrue's nose and he'll screw you just so, and then if you're dumb enough to survive and make good friends with his enemies then... well... you're only cementing your status as a loser, right? Getting closer to the anarchs will help you? might as well save your failing political career by tattooing a swastika to your head. Between the Ventrue and his Ventrue mates and you with your neonate anarch buddies.... Most are going to side with the Ventrue, unless he really is out of favour. Going against a well established Ventrue will have you ostracised because it's simply not worth upsetting a powerful market force for... some fool who wants to bloody noses.

                    Granted, most of this is the same for Toreador. The Toreador has an advantage in that they expect a lower bottom line: A Toreador's allowed to be petty, expected to be petty. But that's a double edged sword. A vampire who's petty is simply someone you want to avoid dealing with. Get a close relationship with a Ventrue and fall out? His response will be proportionate and appropriate. Bonus? The Ventrue will let a lot of minor issues things slide, and when you try to patch things up, you've got reasonable confidence that when he accepts your reconciliation, he won't go back on it. Get a close relationship with a Toreador and fall out? By reputation, they'll escalate the conflict, they'll never let it go, and they're probably mad about something really minor you did. They'll take great joy in that you gave them an excuse to victimise you... That's not someone you really want to get all that close to.
                    If you have to depend on someone for business, your average Ventrue will always be better than your average Toreador.

                    Speaking of Averages....

                    Ventrue almost always hit night society after a clan-scrutinized selection and a period of heavy tutelage. If they lack in one, they'll usually be made up for in the other. 9 times out of 10, your Ventrue will at least be adequate in all the things that matter to being a Ventrue, if not exceptional.

                    Toreador are infamous for lax embraces and frequently hap-hazard tutoring. There certainly are prestigious, well selected, well groomed, well taught Toreador that can rival the best Ventrue, but in modern nights, they're the minority. Even if you've got a discerning sire, there's a good chance that much of your selection and education revolved around art, rather than the practical. With elites, the mediocre, and absolute trash-tier embraces that naturally fall in with Anarchs, the Toreador have little consistency, and most often the greatest enemy of a Toreador is...another Toreador. The clan culture is toxic. the brand value of the clan suffers in comparison to the blue bloods as the clan is messy, decadent and disharmonious.

                    (also, if you read those "the view of" sections where the clans talk shit about one another? The Toreador cop way more flak than the Ventrue. The Ventrue often get grudging admiration from the low clans. The Toreador just get hate.)

                            Vampire society is in many ways feudal. This doesn’t just mean that vampires are like lords that have control of domains and have fealty to higher lords, it means that politics is based on personal relationships. Who someone is matters and your personal ties to them matter. This is where the Ventrue draw much of their power. Maybe being willing to paint a target on their own backs does put them at certain disadvantages and perhaps they would have longer unlives if they didn’t have Fortitude but instead had a Discipline that let them avoid danger rather than survive it. But then they wouldn’t be who they are, the leaders of the vampiric world.

                            The fact that they are willing to take on those dangerous positions, which also include responsibilities, and put themselves in harms way does earn them respect and in a world where personal relationships matter, respect is power.

                            The two other Camarilla establishment Clans - the Toreador and Tremere - have Auspex as well as a social Discipline. Yet despite every Clan being selfish, the Toreador and the Tremere both throw their support behind the Ventrue. Can any of us imagine the Ventrue and Toreador supporting Tremere as the leadership Clan of the Camarilla? Can we imagine the Ventrue and Tremere supporting the Toreador as the leadership Clan? I can’t in either case. Because the Ventrue actually have what it takes to be leaders - putting themselves in harms way, taking on the responsibilities, taking on the blame, painting big targets on their backs. They also take their leadership role seriously and despite being bastards like all vampires, their leadership actually is beneficial to the vampires that they lead. Which I think most Camarilla vampires understand deep down, which is why they support the Ventrue or at least work with them to facilitate their rule.
