                                    I think the basic idea of vampires running some kind of underground criminal blood-slave network is fine. It makes sense for the setting. And some version of it has been around forever - even the first edition corebook's section on Gary, IN as a setting mentioned the ghoul Williams and his "auction" that sells kidnapped victims to be used as vessels to the area's vampires.

                                    The Circulatory System is simply a more developed version of this idea. There are human trafficking organizations even today in the most modern countries. Until the people reach their final destinations and find out they are not sold to a brothel or sweatshop, it'll just seem like any other human trafficking organization. In fact, they can even be used in that capacity UNTIL some vampire makes the final purchase. Or it could even involve a cover in more legitimate organizations. It could be a modelling service, travel nurse program, migrant farm labor, a temp agency that covers a specific kind of employee like the hospitality service, or something else. All that is needed is something that attracts people to want to move for a job and perhaps put up in corporate dormitory to allow for surveillance. Then you just need to be judicious on who is selected to be the meal while not jeopardizing the network as a whole. So if law enforcement does uncover parts of the organization, it could appear perfectly "normal" though criminal. It no more uncovers the existence of vampires than exposure of actual human trafficking organizations in the real world does.

                                    If done well, I don't think it is any increased danger to the Masquerade to any other of the activities of vampires in the setting. I have not read any actual information on the Circulatory System so I don't know if it is not implemented well in practice. But I think the general idea can work (just as much as anything else), and an ST can always make adjustments as needed so it fits their particular vision of the setting.

                                    But if the ST wants, of course something could happen which does produce a risk to the Masquerade just like anytime a vampire does something it might lead to someone figuring out the truth. Maybe a mistake was made. Or maybe it was implemented in some incompetent fashion. If that makes a good plot for an ST, those options work too.

                                    Now I don't see this as a "sect" like how we talk about the Camarilla, Sabbat, or Inconnu. But there can be plenty of organizations within the Camarilla similar to this. Or one of the Revenant families may do the same thing for the Sabbat. Or one of the Independent clans runs a similar organization for their own needs, or to build business with the other sects.
