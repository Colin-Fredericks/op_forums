                    In terms of "what to do", with all of this said and done...

                    In DA I would allow players to make the decisions, with my guidance. If they really want a close relationship with Fire Spirits, I can say that their Koldunic experience begins with a hot spring resting over a bed of microvolcanic activity, and let them have Way of Fire. If they really want a close relationship with a Forest, not a specific element, I would let them design a Kraina by combining 5 existing Kraina and Way powers into a progression which matches their environment.

                    In Modern Nights I would use a similar system, but the Ways are far more common and Kraina are exceptionally rare. Some have even grown feeble since the place they were born from has grown feeble. Imagine a Koldun who took up residence in the forests of the English Midlands... their powers would have waned exceptionally, forcing them to swap over to Ways. On the offchance of this ever happening to a PC I would "refund" them their dots in Ways with a very, very small penalty (liquefy the dead Kraina and give them 90% of the EXP back to put into Ways).

                        I liked the way that blood magics and things were handled in Tome of Secrets, but as of writing this I'm playing a Lasombra Abyss Mystic, and I found that section lacking a little. It all felt like it was built with an antagonist and chronicle in mind, rather than a player.

                        The first two rituals were pretty much useless, since there isn't quite a definition of a "true mystic" and oubliettes are supposed to be about as rare as mystics are.
                        Abyssal Blade is an interesting concept, though armor doesn't contribute more than 5 dots to a person's soak rating, with a -3 penalty to dexterity. The agg damage to mortals is alright, but they'll probably die anyways if a vampire is attacking them. Agg damage to vampires, however...
                        Implant the Bezoar is nice, kinda op considering there's not really a chance for the victim to resist the Bezoar and it starts draining crazy amounts of blood after only a few nights.
                        One With the Dark is another interesting concept, one that seems designed with the player in mind, perhaps. The appearance boost is a little strange, but I'll roll with it. Summoning the Blatherskites, also cool, a little tedious but worth it for the slaughter that comes after.
                        Angra Mainyu Consumes Spenta Mainyu is definitely a cool ritual. I absolutely love how crazy it is, throwing up darkness-tar that puts out all lights in an area and doesn't allow them to light back up? Inspiring.
                        Lord Aludian's Orifices is a unique one. I don't usually play games where the sexy-times come into play, but if people want to use it, that's their thing. It certainly gave me a giggle.
                        Boukephos' Gateway gives me maybe the most trouble and the most joy out of them all. This is another one I thought was an amazing concept, but not very well executed. Yes, it's hardcore with those 2 conviction rolls, but you better have lots of conviction or this is destined to fail. Not only that, if you fail, you can't get on the Road of the Abyss? Not fun, especially for a mystic using One With the Dark. That -1 blood on all obtenebration abilities, tho.
                        Beckon the Ingurgitant, great ritual, clever usage can lead to lots of murder, I like it.
                        Boukephos' Chosen Oubliette is a little silly, another ritual that seems like it's designed for NPCs to attack PCs with. Not to mention it's a level 9 ritual, so unless you're Lasombra you don't have much hope of attempting this.

                        The oubliette background is pretty cool, I'd only ever buy up to level 3, but then again anything passed that is something that the writer seems to have handcrafted for a chronicle to be done in. It's the same for a few of the denizens of the Abyss as well, people like the Umbriferous Man and the Minacious Legion.

                        All in all, I'll probably cherry pick one or two rituals out of this book for Abyss Mystics, and then look elsewhere. Sorry for the giant paragraph, this is my first post and I have lots to say about Tome of Secrets.

                            LeoBlood
                            ​Agg damage to mortals is actually meaningless. Mortals don't make distinction between lethal and aggravated damage. Abyssal Blade also doesn't deal agg to vampires and doesn't bypass Fortitude, so its effects are highly questionable.
                            One With the Dark is full of problems. It never explains what a "master Mystic" is, where you learn "language of the Abyss", how do you tattoo your body when all appearance changes go away the next night, who are the "Cainites named in the ritual". The whole thing is basically unusable without a full rewrite.
                            Summoning the Blatherskites starts with an extremely derpy "Mystics publicly snub those who practice this ritual". Abyss Mystics are an extreme minority of the clan Lasombra, who are a minority of the general Vampire group which in itself is a minority of humanity. We are three levels of minority deep and are basically talking about being snubbed by the other two members of the school's anime club because you said you like Naruto.
                            Boukephos' Gateway is either too risky to ever use or has no danger at all. If the failure penalty doesn't affect vampires who are already on the Road of the Abyss, then you (under the supervision of your sire) do the ritual until you succeed and there are no stakes. If the failure penalty does affect the mystics, then you never try the Gateway because the price of failure is setting your concept on fire and making a new character.

                            Oubliette is the background I just don't get. It doesn't work as a defense for your spooky mansion because effects take hold only after you spend days in it. On top of that, being an owner of Oubliette doesn't make you immune to the effects so you can never get the xp discount - you lose 1 WP each night and you need to spend 20 nights. Unless the Oubliette is filled with roleplaying props for you to milk Nature/Demeanor - you aer fucked.
