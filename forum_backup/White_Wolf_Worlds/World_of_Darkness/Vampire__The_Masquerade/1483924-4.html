        Originally posted by monteparnas View Post
        True, but his reasoning isn't.

        Demanding such a scene is, indeed, pretty stupid, though.

        Sometimes as an ST I just ask the player how the PC feels or cope, or I describe the feeling in general and let them decide how they externalize and deal with that, if I feel confident and it is interesting at the moment, which usually means the first time of a new character or some dramatic session. But requiring lengthy remorse monologues? Like, what?
        Yeah, it's obnoxious. You can't really RP that stuff, beyond letting it flavour some of the character's actions. Though making amends should be grounds for buying up Humanity again (see V20DA for a system for that).

        Actually I think SetiteFriend is right here.

        Humanity in VtM isn't about believing in a certain code or having a healthy response (it isn't mental health exactly). It is about feelings. How do you deal with your feelings, or lack thereof, is another matter (albeit still a pretty relevant one for the character). You can have a character that pretty much doesn't think killing is ever justifiable, yet are below the Humanity rating (for V20, for example) to feel about murder. If they commit a murder they may feel a little bad for going against their belief, but it is more abstract, not a response to the fact they actually ended a life. Likewise, you can have a character that whose beliefs completely justify murder in a lot of circumstances, yet it doesn't mean actually doing it became emotionally easy.

        In V5 it is about the feelings become gradually more muted instead of things slipping in or out of the list, which may be a better or worse take for it depending on the character and the Chronicle Tenets, but the baseline is still the same. Convictions may help with this notion as they may signify situations where your belief in the justification is strong enough to help you cope emotionally. But otherwise it is about emotions, not beliefs, and as such rationalization is an indirect factor.

        Of course, if you rationalize it may also mean you're more likely to do it again.
        So, checking the text again, you're both right RAW. V20 and Revised talk about the degeneration roll as 'he feels enough remorse or somehow justifies it to himself', and I think this is symptomatic of how Humanity has been poorly articulated throughout the game (even in V5).

        In my view, as soon as you start saying killing is okay in some circumstances, you've started down the slippery slope of allowing killing. And the downward spiral of Humanity is supposed to, at least in part, portray that slippery slope, until you reach the point at which killing is commonplace.

        Paths are different. But having a strong Conscience suggests a person should be feeling remorse, not rationalising (which is the logical ego overriding the superego, and therefore overriding Conscience).

        That's why I much prefer VTR2e's breaking points and detachment as a system, to be honest. It completely sidesteps the quagmire. I guess we also shouldn't expect too much logic from an RPG.

                                            Except that the whole Abyssal Mysticism thing never really involved going to the Umbra. It was just a rationale of where Obtenebration's shadows really came from to explain all the things they could do that don't really make sense. You don't need there to be an Umbra, with the Abyss Realm, for Obtenebration or Abyssal Mysticism to work as they do in VtM... it was just a connection that was cool to have for crossover material.

                                            The simplest explanation is that they wanted to keep some avenue of Abyssal Mysticism open in V5, but didn't want to put Obtenebration into Blood Sorcery, so they combined it with Necromancy into Oblivion instead. Lasombra can, in theory access Ceremonies so they keep some of that mystic flavor without having three Clans with Blood Sorcery as in-Clan.

                                            If you dump Abyssal Mysticism, it's much much easier to make a few Obtenebration inspired new Obfuscate Powers/Amalgams and call it a day. No connection to another Realm, no overloaded Oblivion, etc. Oblivion only makes sense as it currently stands if it was designed to keep it connected to another dimension. It's not really simplifying anything when the Underworld was already part of the larger Umbra in the grand WoD cosmology to move the Abyss from the Near Umbra to the Dark Umbra instead.
