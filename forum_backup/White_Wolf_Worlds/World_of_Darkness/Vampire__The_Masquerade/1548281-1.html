    Somewhat-belatedly, but as promised, my response to your own thoughts:-

    (1) I tend to see these little contradictions as an excuse to tell an interesting story that explains them and gives some insights into the character - for example, bourgeois to petite-bourgeois Germaine might have adopted them as protective colouring in the distinctly Nineties atmosphere of Anarch LA (Incidentally, I wonder if transfers would vanish from vampiric flesh the way actual tattoos do?).

    These tattoos might have been inflicted on him by an opponent seeking to make it much harder for such a slippery agent to conceal himself and Germaine may be exploiting them in the short term whilst looking for a way to remove them.

    A more subtle variation on the previous point is that they represent a formal covenant (A symbolic way for the character to ‘burn his bridges’ and acquire further admiration in certain circles), whether as part of a shift in his long-term plans or simply as an expedient.

    Or he might simply have lost a bet with Smiling Jack.


    (2) Germain definitely has some ‘Chief of the Secret Police’ vibes, rather than ‘Fearless Leader’ energy: one suspects that his place on the Red List isn’t the only reason he’s in no real danger of becoming head of the Anarch movement - though one could imagine a scenario where he sneaks and wheedles his way into power DEATH OF STALIN style.


    (3) It amuses me to imagine Germaine deliberately going Full Revolutionary when holding a conversation (Such polite conversation!) with Baron Fortier in French, with particular emphasis on addressing him as ‘Citizen’ and using the Revolutionary calendar when giving dates (Something he never bothers with otherwise), whilst Fortier retaliates in kind (For example by giving dates as ‘In the Year of Our Lord **** on the feast of Saint such-and-such’, also by placing emphasis on Ancien Regime etiquette and titles when mentioning various persons).

    Bonus points if they’re doing all this covert linguistic sniping in the EXACT SAME ACCENT (I really do like the mental image that they’re from the same region of France, albeit from different generations - Fortier being the older - and different social classes).

    Anyway, your description of their distinct capabilities
    makes me suspect these two would make a very dangerous combination, if only they didn’t love to loathe each other.


    (4) I’m not going to lie, the mental image of the deeply-snobbish Vignes keeping themselves afloat in the Anarch Free States mostly because they throw the best concert parties is a thought to treasure (Though I wonder if some of the more thoughtful Anarchs regard them as a useful ‘miner’s canary’ in terms of ascertaining how local Kindred with natural inclinations towards the Camarilla way of doing things feel about current developments in the Free States).

    I’m also deeply amused by the notion of Mr Vignes perpetually lawyering his way out of the trouble his Grumpy Old Snob tendencies get him into on those occasions when he attends a Rant (I sometimes wonder if a key reason the Vignes ménage, with all it’s potentially volatile elements, held together for so long is that each of it’s members were terrified by the thought of going their separate ways in the Anarch Free States).

    I wonder if Idiot Fail Childe (but still a Toreador) Christopher ever listens in on these concert parties? He seems to have some unstated, but oddly-close tie to the Vignes (Possibly because he likes the idea of being the young tearaway in this family dynamic, whether the Vignes household like it or not) and their tendency to celebrate the finer things in life may be a key part of their appeal.

    - I would suggest making Catherine Dubois specifically a colonial Frenchwoman (Born overseas in some French holding, possibly in Guadeloupe) to help explain what drew Fortier to her specifically - her family is very much the sort of person he himself was in his breathing days.

    - I’d bet Germaine would wear a shirt AND a cravat with ladies present and Higher Culture in the air (Not a tie though, that would just be selling out).

    - One imagines that Elena can understand enough French to know when to smile and nod, as well as take simple instructions (But that her eyes tend to glaze over a bit when things get more complicated).

    - Given that Jeremy MacNeil hails from a highland clan of the 1600s, it’s possible that one of those Linguistics dots is for English, given that his native language would almost certainly be Scots Gaelic (In fact I’d bet that ‘Jeremy’ is an anglicisation of his birth name).

    - It amuses me to imagine Jeremy MacNeil carefully avoiding the Vignes gatherings as a rule (Partly because they’re expert snobs, partly because Mrs Vignes kept giving him excessively familiar looks and he Does Not Want that sort of Drama), but Crispus Attucks being a semi-regular guest (Partly because, as suggested above, the Vignes make an excellent ‘test audience’ for non-Anarch reactions, party because he misses New England - and the Vignes are New England to the Bone - partly because the Vignes are always deeply, deeply happy to demonstrate their Abolitionist credentials with such a nice Negro Gentleman - a Revolutionary War hero too!).

                        Since I want to make Germaine a prominent character in the chronicle, I should also include Countess D’Adhemar in some way. Her desire to destroy Germaine and his inclusion on the Red List are essential to any story that includes him. She could also serve as a good antagonist in general. Of course, we know very little about the Countess or her quest so once again STs need to do some heavy lifting here.

                        First, we need a new name for her. Countess D’Adhemar is probably her mortal name, but it shouldn’t be how she is known currently in vampire society. There probably lots of other Kindred who were once countesses, and her family name or title doesn’t work for me. While I could give her a random French feminine name, I’ll use something based on Adhemar. That is based on a masculine Germanic name, and it had a feminine form of Adamardis which was later reduced to Aenor which was later replaced in turn by Eleanor (at least according to Wikipedia) so that is what I am going with. Or perhaps a variant like Leonora or Nora.

                        Second, I’ll be placing Eleanor in San Francisco. It’s the closest Camarilla stronghold to the Free States and gives her some opportunity to observe Germaine's comings and goings to Los Angeles. Germaine travels a lot, but Eleanor herself needs some kind of base somewhere and San Francisco makes sense. It also makes it easier for PCs to somehow encounter her if they ever visit San Francisco (or possibly even to confront and attempt to destroy her.) An appearance at the Vampire Club is probably essential if the PCs ever visit that. Germaine may not be aware there is any “Eleanor” in San Francisco, and even if he’s heard the name does not know she is Countess D’Adhemar.

                        Third, we need some backstory for her after 1793. Eleanor is likely known as a prominent French Toreador and childe of Madame Guil (though one she has cut off), but her involvement with Germaine and French Revolution are unknown. She should have her own reputation she’s established in the past two hundred years while she’s been secretly seeking Germaine’s destruction. She likely pursued him to North America fairly early though not immediately given her commitments in Paris. Let’s say after Napoleon’s downfall when it became safe again to travel between France and the United States. That gives Germaine at least a twenty year head start. Maybe she entered through Boston (like Annabelle Triabel did) or New Orleans, a domain with close links to France.

                        Since we’ve assigned Germaine some activities in Mexico, it would make sense for her to have spent some time in the Camarilla domain of Veracruz. Maybe even arriving there first during the first French Intervention in 1838. Perhaps she even met Lodin while he was there (1847-1869 or so) which covers not only the Mexican-American War, but the Reform War and Second French Intervention. This tulmutuous time would be good cover for Anarch activities in Mexico. Then after Germaine left, her going to New Orleans as a base would be convenient. Then at some point after the Free States were created she might choose San Francisco thinking Germaine would make use of that.

                        Fourth, what should be her general abilities? She must have skills in political manipulation and treachery, but she was also a skilled enough fighter to almost kill Germaine back in 1793 and did destroy numerous ghouls of Critias who attempted to defend him. As Madame Guil’s childe, she is of the seventh generation just one less than Germaine, and is slightly older than him. Overall, she should be an equal opponent although probably less skilled in combat, but more skilled socially.

                        She should have some agents and spies in Los Angeles. Probably mortals and ghouls, but she may have one or a few Kindred informants. Perhaps she’s seduced or even blood bonded some anarch Kindred who visited San Francisco or got close to the border in San Jose. (I need to start a new thread on the Camarilla’s agents within the Free States since it is large enough for its own topic, and I’ll be sure to mention the Countess there as well.) Germaine keeps himself isolated from most Kindred (although we’ve built some ideas here on who he might associate himself with when he’s in LA) so the chances these spies ever ingratiate themselves with Germaine is slim. However, they should a description and know what to look for even if they don’t know it is “Germaine of the Red List” they are looking for. So the PCs might witness someone observing Germaine at some and investigate. Perhaps this is even what begins the process of them figuring out who he actually is. Another possibility is for the PCs not to realize it is Germaine being observed because they witness this at some event where other Kindred are in attendance like a music salon hosted by Fortier with guests like the Vignes couple, the Brujah Idealists,

                        I think any attempt to destroy Germaine in Los Angeles itself is a fool’s errand. Too many things are stacked against that. So any assassination attempt would likely happen when Germaine leaves to enter a Camarilla domain. For story purposes then, the PCs would need to discover someone trying to determine Germaine’s future itinerary and destination. That still means she needs some other combat oriented Kindred to accompany her when the final hit goes down.

                        Fifth, their previous relationship as lovers during the French Revolution can provide us some good character movements. Though their relationship was mainly about exploiting one another for their own secret ends, it is stated she was enamored by his handsome face and impertinent manner. More of a plaything than than a boyfriend, but that doesn’t mean there wasn’t real sparks during their lovemaking. There might not be a personal attachment and it’s likely the two have never seen each other since 1793, but there should be some chemistry besides the hate. When the truth comes out, I’d want the PCs to see some kind of personal reaction rather than if some unrelated Kindred was trying to kill Germaine.

                        Ideas and thoughts on Countess D'Adhemar are appreciated. She has the potential to be an important antagonist in the chronicle.

                            I think we can all agree that the sensible question when it comes to la Comtesse d’Adhemar is “We all know that she and Germain are DANGEROUS LIASONS, all the time, but are talking Glenn Close & John Malkovich, Lindsey Duncan & Alan Rickman or Annette Bening & Colin Firth?”


                            Some slightly more serious thoughts, in brief:-

                            - I’m assuming that the lady was born somewhere in the late 1740s/early 1750s AD (Making her a contemporary of Favourites like Lamballe or Polignac, who had the ear of Marie Antoinette): it’s possible her sire ‘Gilles’ (Presumably Madam Guil often employed a male guise during her days as an Anarch, prior to the French Revolution) Embraced her as much because the Countess was getting “too long in the tooth” as to reward her for getting close to the Royal Court


                            - From what I can tell, ‘Eleanor’ (or ‘Eleanore’ in French usage) wasn’t really popular in the French Aristocracy of the 18th century: all the Eleanor’s of that era which I’ve been able to trace are English or Germanic or even Swedish

                            My own inclination wouod be to make her birth name ‘Marie Joseph’ and show this an attempt to curry favour with Maria Josepha, who married the father of Louis XVI in AD 1747 (Suggesting the future Countess could have been born in that year).


                            - I wonder if making her a native of Lorraine (The ancestral homeland of Marie Antoinette’s father, acquired from Austria by France and a subject of possessive interest to the Imperial family as a result) would help explain how she caught the Queen’s interest


                            - I’m also tempted to suggest that the Countess was specifically tied to the Orleanist party at court (Since that was a faction of the Royal family that seems to have pursued some sort of whispering campaign against Marie Antoinette and would have been quite useful to the future Madame Guil, as she worked to lay out the groundwork for a future Anarch revolt): incidentally, the former Duke of Orelans actually voted for the execution of the King, his cousin, yet his heir Louis-Philippe somehow became King of the French anyway (Despite no fewer than three generation of the Royal families’ senior branch standing in the way).

                            I’m not saying it was vampires, but in the World of Darkness it absolutely could have been vampires


                            - Given her title and the Salic nature of French succession laws (Also the obvious opportunity to suggest parallels with Merteuil of DANGEROUS LIASONS) I’m assuming that the Countess married into her peerage and was widowed at a relatively young age

                            If you want to run with the name Eleanor/Eleanora as her birth name, you could suggest that she was the daughter of foreign nobles who married into the French aristocracy (Adding another parallel to Marie Antoinette that might breed mutual sympathy).

                            Whether she left a line of descendants is an interesting question


                            - I’m assuming the future Madame Guil, then an Anarch (and at one point one of the most notorious diablerists in France) acquired the Countess as a way to put a respectable face on her operations: also, perhaps, as somebody who could tutor her in how to pass in High Society (In addition to acting as her mortal agent)

                            Given that the French Revolution was NOT the start of the Second Anarch Revolt, it seems reasonable to deduce that a key part of Kritias’ interest in planting a mole close to the Countess was to ensure that this would be the case (I.E. In order to make sure that the Mortal Revolt would not be accompanied by a Vampiric one)


                            - Finally, at least for now, it’s interesting to wonder if the Countess served as ‘Madame Guil’s in for the Camarilla: it’s even vaguely possible that she agreed to pose as Sire of the latter to muddy the waters, then required that Germain be put on the Red List as quid pro quo for letting her ‘Childe’ completely overshadow her for the rest of Camarilla history.

                            Whether or not this was the case, one can easily imagine the Countess pursuing Germain as a way to keep a safe distance from her remarkably cruel Sire (Who could well have seen her as a loose end to be tidied away, given her awareness of at least some of Madame Guil’s secret history).

                            She might be formally ranked as a subordinate to the Toreador Justicar (If this is the case then Petrodon of Nosferatu is likely to regard her with some small suspicion - given the traditional tensions the clans and the possibility of one Justicar sticking their oar into another’s home waters).

                            Alternatively, the Countess might well be Madame Guil’s ‘Anarch Whisperer’ an agent who keeps up relations with the most interesting parties in the Free States (Either through formal or informal channels).

                            In any case, it seems highly unlikely that Madam Guil would share her Sabbat connections with her Childe (In fact my previous thoughts on the Countess was that she might know too much for Madam Guil to permit her to exist - taking up her Childe’s feud with Germain as a way of making peace with her memory after ‘recovering’ her vitae).
