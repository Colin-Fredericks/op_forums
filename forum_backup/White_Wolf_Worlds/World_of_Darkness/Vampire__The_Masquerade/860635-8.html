        Originally posted by Hawthorn View Post
        So caveat beforehand: I've not read this book yet.

        So something I'm kinda worried about here in regards to Bloodline write ups.

        From what people have posted here there is a sorta explanation for why the Kiasyd in Dark Ages V20 were different from any version we have seen before. Seems there has been a lot of really good conversation between different writers in regards to it. So now the V20DA one fits into the setting a little better and provides an extra option for players in V20 too if they want. That's kinda awesome.

        So with that in mind I was wondering about some other bloodlines and there various write ups across the V20 line.

        Lamia: We get the basic version of these guys in V20 in a couple of paragraphs. They get a bit of a reinterpretation in V20DA and a sidebar suggesting that they eventually cut a deal with the Giovanni and go into hiding (possibly in the Underworld) until the final nights. From the posts here its suggested that the Harbingers kinda created a bloodline that is the "Lamia Reborn" basically.

        So this has me scratching my head a little. Particularly because said "new version" does not get a full right up. Obviously a ST could use the either option of "hidden survivors" or "new creations" but I was wondering how easy it might be to reconcile the two groups if you wanted to do that? Also does this book detail a new version of the Twilight Garden Necromancy path first introduced in V20DA?

        True Brujah: Once again they get a pretty minimal summary in V20 and an expansion (and slight thematic update) in V20DA. From comments here it seems they don't get a full write up (which honestly I don't mind, as while they do need more fluff, we have a chapter on them coming up in Lore of the Bloodlines), but I guess some new info it included right? Did any of the writers on this book also do their upcoming write up in Lore of the Bloodlines? Is it the same writer as the one that did their write up in V20DA? I wonder because this bloodline has actually been pretty consistent in the fluff since their inception and I'm hoping they stay that way.

        Old Clan Tzimisce: So basic idea of a few paragraphs in V20. Couple more paragraphs in V20DA. General theme of "No Vicissitude" has been constant in them, along with a "dark reserved nobility". Soul Eater and confusing contradictory clan fluff has slowly been getting stripped off them since Revised. From comments and reviews I read this book seems to basically bring that to a close. Likewise, from comments it seems this book explains how they got rid of the Vicissitude attribute in their blood and why they practice Koldunism more then other Tzimisce. So all good stuff. Apparently this book also explains why Koldunism changed from a regional based magic to a more abstract elemental based magic between Dark Ages and Modern V20. All good stuff.

        My only question is the V20DA idea that they called themselves and were called "The Dracul" included? As I always thought it was weird they called themselves "Old Clan" rather then something like "True" or had an original name or something. Its a minor thing, but it would be nice for consistency. Also I know one of the new paths of Koldunism is a V20DA style one based around Enoch. What is the other one?

        Harbingers of Skulls: So these guys got same paragraph treatment as most Bloodlines in V20, and it was pretty much a straight rehash of their Revised edition fluff (though its nice to now there is now an explanation for how they survived in the Underworld without blood). Rites of Blood expanded on them a bit in the Sabbat chapter. Lore of the Clans has some interesting new hints about them from the Giovanni perspective.

        I'm curious as to how this new book meshes the idea they are members of the Black Hand with the old fluff of them being Sabbat members? Is it a case that some of the elders (the more revenge driven ones) went to the Sabbat while the more occultish ones went to the TBH? Also, who wrote up the Harbingers for this book? Are they the same writer as for Lore the Bloodlines? As honestly as a bloodline they have never had the largest word count, so new fluff is welcome but I'm hoping it can be presented as consistent new fluff.

        Baali: Oh god...these guys. Fluff consistency and them really don't get along. V20 has the "Hail Satan!" version in it. Rites of Blood has the "Infernalists on Steroids" (AKA the NEW BAALI of Azreal's mindset) version that fits with the old Dark Ages fluff pre-Clanbook Baali. V20DA too a careful view on them and basically said all their different backstories might be true to a certain degree (basically "ST's pick your own Baali background"). So which flavour do we get in this book?

        I've given up hoping for fluff consistency with this bloodline. I'm just curious which flavour we are getting more of (or even a new flavour) person I'd like more of the Cthulhu "Children" flavour, but meh. Likewise, I'm wondering if they share and writer in between V20DA (Neall I believe?), GttTMR (since I've heard info about them is scattered all over this book) and the upcoming Lore of the Bloodlines? Since at least three sources will then hopefully have some fluff consistency for them.

        Also do the Hand now fight them or support them? I remember Dirty Secrets really stressed the hate on that the Hand had for the Baali. So I was wondering if they are discussed as members or as possible enemies (or both!)?

        Nagaraja: OK, so these are the big one for me. I'm a massive fan of this bloodline. Despite the fact that they pretty much typify a lot of things I disliked about Masquerade. Special snowflake among special snowflakes (check), nearly unplayable (check), completely different characterization depending on writer (check), weird niche bloodline discipline (check). Still despite that I really adore them.

        So their fluff pre-V20 was pretty badly muddled (despite there not being a whole lot of it) and scattered across about three Vampire books and three Mage books. V20 did a pretty good job of reducing them down to their most basic concept and giving players a little more wiggle room with them. V20DA added to that with some updated and consolidated background that kept the skeleton of their awesome old fluff without overstuffing it with issues from Mage. So I'm pretty happy with how they are sitting at current in the V20 line.

        Does this book add new fluff to them? Does it manage to rework some of the weirder and clunkier stuff from their Dirty Secrets fluff in and make it work better? Does it do a good job of fitting in their Mage fluff ties without getting to "into" Mage fluff emphasis? Also was their write up in this book done by Neall like V20DA and is he the writer for their chapter in Lore of the Bloodlines?

        Also the Idran get a write up in this book apparently? How is that handled, as I thought the few members that were not hunted down by their fellow Chakrivanti ended up as the Nagaraja thanks to the botched ritual? Are they Litches?

        Thanks in advance for anyone who takes the time to answer these questions.
        Props to you for the detailed query! However, my honest advice here has to be that you're best off reading the book. You're asking a lot of questions that are better covered by the content we've written. For my part, I will answer that I wrote the Harbingers for this, for Lore of the Bloodlines, and provided Cappadocians some fair coverage in Beckett's Jyhad Diary. Also, they are not exclusively in the Sabbat, even if several of their number rest within that Sect.
