    Found some additional mentions of New England in sourcebooks.

    In World of Darkness: Sorcerer, the section on the Ancient Order of the Aeon Rites mentions their Boston temple is a well respected charity called Saint Simon's Rest. And that the local Bone Gnawers have taken it upon themselves to protect it. So not directly relevant, but for those STs who like to include elements of other games, or just want an Easter egg to include, that's there.

    Of more importance is that it reminded me that the Arcanum has a Boston chapter house there. In the original corebooks, they just mention that Boston is one of three foundation houses of the Arcanum. However, in the revised (and V2) corebook it mentions that the Boston chapter house was burned down by Cainites in 1910. Intrigued by the change, I checked a friend's copy of Halls of the Arcanum and discovered that this was first mentioned there and provided some real information. (The same sourcebook seems to have decided that rather have three foundation houses, the Arcanum now had a single foundation house in London and Boston was downgraded to a chapter house.)

    First, I believe the use of the term Cainite rather than Kindred indicates the intent that it was the Sabbat who burned down the Arcanum's Boston chapter house.

    Second, the actual Arcanum sourcebook says that vampires torched the Arcanum chapterhouse in Boston on either July 16 or 17, 1910. Robert Brooks, a member of the Arcanum, had infiltrated a mystic group called the Children of Hecate who had eleven members. At his third meeting with them, he attended a magical sex rite in a remote grove outside Boston that summoned an ebon-skinned Dark Lady - the cult’s Hecate - from the ground who joined the orgy. Brooks later noticed that her love bites was from blood drinking. Brooks escaped from the orgy and returned to the Boston chapterhouse, but was followed. The next night the chapterhouse was burned down probably by people associated with the blood cult.

    This "Dark Lady" could be either Sabbat or Camarilla. Blood cults are not unheard of within the Camarilla, and Sabbat rarely utilize humans as pawns. Furthermore, the Dark Lady seemed to operate along and not as part of a pack. If not for the use of the term Cainite in the corebook (a term not used in Halls of the Arcanum), I personally would assign this vampire to being of the Camarilla. Although the Boston Kindred could believe it was the Sabbat who did it.

    The powers used by the Dark Lady indicate Protean (Earth Meld allowing her to rise up from the ground), Potence, and Presence. She may or may not have had any Blood Magic. She didn't seem to use any, but the Children of Hecate viewed her as a goddess of magic and were performing rituals for her. I'm inclined to think she just used Disciplines and the cultists didn't learn any kind of real magic.

    The use of Protean suggests Gangrel, but the use of Presence and her description of beautiful make me think she's more likely Toreador. Not unusual for vampires to know out-of-clan Disciplines. Entirely possible it could have been a vampire of any other clan like Brujah. Assuming the Dark Lady survived, we have a female vampire with possibly her blood cult, the Children of Hecate, still around. Her description as ebon-skinned could mean the vampire was either dark skinned African woman, that the vampire had used black body paint because black furred or skinned animals were sacred to the goddess, or possibly a result of arising from the ground and covered her body with the dark (likely wet) earth. I have my own preference, but STs will decide on their own.

    So STs can create whatever character they'd like to correspond to the few known facts. I do think this is important because the 1910 attack on the Arcanum seems to be the reason the Camarilla forbid Kindred from interacting with it. It seemed to have created a big problem. Earlier sourcebooks stated that the Inner Circle proclaims - and that this was backed by Brujah Justicar Ryland - that the Arcanum was not to be interfered with. I don't think "Ryland" ever gets mentioned again. My personal interpretation is that Ryland was probably the Brujah Justicar around 1910, and that he held a Conclave in Boston that investigated and addressed the issue. So that is a good bit of history to drop. If a Justicar actually investigated the incident, he either would have likely found and destroyed the Dark Lady, or it shows the Dark Lady was either powerful enough to hide her involvement, escaped, or was too powerful to be officially punished and possibly just reprimanded and her involvement covered up. Perhaps that is why it was blamed on the Sabbat.

    For my own purposes, I'd rather have the Dark Lady and her cult still be around. It is just too interesting an angle to not have for an existing character.

                    Found more references to New England is canon sources. A while back Atticus Cleanclaw mentioned the New England ties of Christopher Houghton and his sire Matthew Lubbock, but Los Angeles By Night has at least one additional connection to New England.

                    The Ventrue Edward Vignes and his brood (his wife and butler) were originally from Boston, but had to flee the city after they "backed the wrong faction in a war among the Boston Kindred." We're not given any other information except we know Edward was embraced in 1882 by "Sir Andrew McCardle" and subsequently embraced his wife that same year and his butler in 1894. We can derive certain things logically that are not mentioned outright. That Edward was able to legally embrace his wife the same year as his own embrace and his butler a decade later ought to mean that hire sire must have had some pull. I imagine Sir Andrew obtained permission both for him to embrace Edward and for Edward to embrace his own wife at the same time. For Edward to be able to legally embrace again twelve years later is also proof he had impressed the Prince in some fashion. Politically unimportant neonates just don't get legal permission to embrace much. Late nineteenth century Boston did see explosive population growth and the Great Boston Fire happened in 1872 so there may have been some room to allow for more embraces, but I still see Edward having two childer to be noteworthy. This was also done at a time and place when the Ventrue overall were not powerful. The Boston Ventrue had been emasculated since the end of the American Revolutionary War. So Sir Andrew was either very skilled and/or cooperated with a known power, like Prince Quention King. Now in actual truth, I don't think the writer of the Los Angeles By Night sourcebook gave much thought to this backstory. He likely only wanted to set up his little family soap opera within the Vignes brood, but having done so we need to fill in the details.

                    The other big thing is what exactly was this "wrong faction in a war among the Boston Kindred" that drove the Vignes all the way to southern California for their lives? It had to be after 1894 when he embraced his butler, and before 1944 when the Anarchs overthrow Don Sebastian as we know the Vignes brood's arrival predates the Free States. The big event that happened in New England during that time was Operation Longbow of Pendragon, Warwick, and Biltmore. So I will go with that and state that the Boston Ventrue of Sir Andrew opposed them and paid the price. Sir Andrew most likely died, and Edward decided it was best for him and his small brood to flee.

                    So while this is only a brief period of time, I do think it provides some more knowledge on the intrigues of Boston leading up to Operation Longbow, and the names of some Ventrue who could act as sires for other Ventrue characters (particularly Sir Andrew McCardle). I'd probably make Sir Andrew (of the 8th generation) the sole surviving Ventrue of the original British Ventrue who ruled Boston until the American Revolution. He'll be the progenitor of most of the Ventrue embraced in Boston, and he'd have had earlier childer than Edward embraced between 1783-1895.

                    Probably not relevant to a New England chronicle, there are some things we can speculate base on the character sheets of the Vignes brood. While the 62 year old Edward's profession is stated as "gentleman", he his Abilities include Finance 3, History 3, Law 3, Linguistics 3, and Politics 3. I would say his career was most likely as a lawyer with strong ties to the business community. He also has surpisingly strong combat Abilities in Brawl 3, Firearms 2, and Melee 2. That is probably just because the sourcebook writer wanted a strong enough martial character to survive in the Anarch Free States. While we could say that these developed while he was a vampire (particularly after 1944), we also have another convenient way to explain them: the American Civil War. Edward would have been in his forties at the time of the war, but there are plenty of historical examples of men in their late thirties and forties enlisting. As a college educated lawyer, he likely would have immediately become a commissioned officer like captain, major, or colonel with one of the Massachusetts volunteer regiments. He probably served in a combat role, not just as a staff officer. While this service might just have been out of patriotism, it might also mean that Edward was involved in the abolitionist movement to some degree and he might have offered his legal services to cases resisting the Fugitive Slave Law and others. If so, then it's possible that Crispus Attucks might have remembered Edward's work as a mortal and appreciated it. In the aftermath of the 1944 revolt that established the Anarch Free States, Crispus might have intervened to make sure Vignes and his brood survived unmolested in Pasadena. And in our version of Operation Longbow that showed Pendragon finally "betraying" the Brujah Anarchs by his personal tyranny, having Vignes be an exile because of his opposition to Pendragon would be another reason why Crispus would vouch for him. Edward Vignes might be a Ventrue, but he also had a history of doing the right thing in Crispus's eyes.

                                I admit that the history of Victorian Vampire London is something I’ve pieced together from various entries on the White Wolf Wiki, but the most relevant section may be found on the ‘Valerius’ article linked to below - whilst Mithras was absent from London & Britain between AD 1798 and 1885, with Valerius being left in charge throughout his absence, it was only after an incident in 1848 (I was mistaken, this was actually a Sabbat attack) that Valerius seems to have started acting as more than a steward, moving away from Mithras’ policies and making London a more open city, with consequences intended and unintended (In fact London under Valerius between 1848-1985 strikes me as far more interesting than the official setting for VICTORIAN AGE: VAMPIRE, which takes place after the return of Mithras).


                                https://whitewolf.fandom.com/wiki/Valerius


                                Incidentally, it’s possible that this relative liberalisation of London by Valerius may have been a key precondition for the schemes of Roman Pendragon that saw the latter ousted from Great Britain by his own clan in AD 1885 (Presumably because the unexpected return of Mithras and his immediate murder of the Tremere primogen persuaded potential backers that denial preferable to being murdered by an angry Vampire demigod).

                                Incidentally, going by the history of Roman Pendragon as presented on the White Wolf wiki, it would seem that he became “the most prominent Brujah in England” at some
                                point between AD 1768 (Having been sired in 1708, it took his sire sixty years to be convinced he was fit to be introduced to Cainite society) and 1885, after betraying his sire and leaving Switzerland (Where she had kept him, rather against his will, apparently employing him against Guillaume, Prince of Geneva).

                                The exact period of his departure from Switzerland and arrival in England is unclear: We only know that he’d spent a century working himself into a position of preeminence amongst the English Brujah by AD 1885, suggesting that he betrayed his sire and headed back to Britain at some point in or after the 1780s (It’s possible he headed to New England first, using the American Revolution and/or it’s aftermath to start making a name on the international scene, then parlaying that prestige into some sort of position back on Old England).

                                I think we can safely say that the departure of Mithras and his increasingly long absence after AD 1798 would have piqued Pendragon’s interest and I would bet money that the abortive Sabbat attack of 1848 was the point at which his star really began to rise - given his later career as Anti Sabbat par excellence - suggesting that the period between 1848 and 1885 might well have seen increasing tensions and a series of manoeuvres as the respective ambitions of Valerius of Clan Ventrue and Roman Pendragon of Clan Brujah began to collide.

                                At least before Mithras upset that particular chess board.
