    Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
    You write books and you think all events need to happen in accorance to themes, and they can't just naturally happen according to logic and reason...?
    Unfortunately, I think you basically are just writing yourself into a corner here.

    1. I am God of my universe so every event that happens in my books happens according to my whims and decisions.

    2. The two most important things that will happen as established by previous games will be: The Rise of the Surveillance State and The Rise of the Antediluvians.

    So, therefore, according to your logic, updating the V:TM setting to 2020 will result in the Second Inquistion and the Gehenna Wars. You've just established that following the natural course of events in previous games and RL will result in the two things you hate most.

    How do you reconcile that?

    Well, that seems to be how V5 is doing things, and it's really a weakness. While I can (and have) rationalize some things like Camarilla exclusivity or house carna (I suspect these rationalizations were not what the writers had in mind) I really, really can't make sense of the Sabbat. Maybe there's some notion of magical realism but it still doesn't make sense.
    The Sabbat was always going to have to change as the depiction in canon is not one that can continue with an age of cellphones and globalized intelligence services.

    A big thing I don't like about the weakness of the lasombra is the dependence on ghouls. If it was the ventrue, who's whole thing was conntrolling people with two social disciplines, who organize themselves on boards and cultivate herds like no other clan, it'd make a little bit of sense. It'd be the perfect weakness of a bloodline with Dominate, Obfuscate, Presence. A Lasombra is, however, a Professional. A domineering loner who surrounds herself with tools*, which is why they have dominate, no presence, and potence instead of fortitude. Their Obtenebration drives people away. They're not supposed to be a clan that needs to talk to larry the ghoul to make a phonecall, they are the ones to make a phonecall. How could such a demeaning thing effect such a proud clan?

    *Clan stereotype, not a universal truth. Smart Lasombra are nice Lasombra.
    I agree, I think the tech-bane element of the Lasombra doesn't make sense with them and ghouls don't work for the majority of Packs. For those Lasombra who live in a communal haven, where would the ghouls even exist? There's some Paths that would have their brethren murdering them just because.

    Originally posted by Pleiades View Post

    Honestly, I think it depends where you define logic. Some versions of the Lasombra are so anti-Camarilla they make no sense ever defecting while others are people you have to wonder about ever not being Camarilla.

    the design choices pre-v5 favored the Sabbat,
    the way the lasombra, Tzimisce and other factions were fleshed out gave alot of depth to the sect,
    their history, their conflicts, their struggles etc

                                                    Anecdotal Story: Is it fun playing a Sabbat who "gets it" versus the Sabbat who "don't"?

                                                    Alternate Title: "Stop being stereotypical, Shovelhead!"

                                                    This is going to be anecodtal but one of my all-time favorite characters to roleplay was Tet-Anubis, a Serpent of Light who was a follower of the Path of Caine. Well before V5 came out, we had the character running through the Transylvania Chronicles and dealing with the fact that he was Embraced as a Follower of Set but defected when he discovered the existence of the Antediluvians as well as "proof" of Caine.

                                                    One of the things that we roleplayed out was the idea that Tet-Anubis joined the First Anarch Revolt because he was disgusted with the control of the Elders and their casual corruption. He wanted to help create a higher morality for Kindred and transcend the bonds of Cainite existence to become a superior godlike being, moral but wholly alien to humanity.

                                                    One of the things I liked was the Storyteller said that Tet-Anubis was surrounded by Sabbat in the Modern Day who couldn't really walk-the-walk. They were Bestial, stupid, arrogant, and about as far from intelligent philosopher vampires as they were going to find. He compared it to the Brujah Clan after Carthage and how it had degenerated from the Learned Clan to being the Rabble.

                                                    I really enjoyed that because it was a theme that was explored in MONTREAL BY NIGHT and other Sabbat supplements where there's the "real" Sabbat who are alien, arcane, eldritch, and inhuman geniuses ala Dracula or even Deacon Frost while there's the other who are just a bunch of Near Dark mooks. I feel like that's a great thing they've sort of canonized.

                                                    Not just in V5 either as we have other Sabbat founders like Giangaleazzo and Vasantasena coming to the conclusion in Revised and V20 that the sabbat are a failed experiment. For me, it was a great roleplaying opportunity.

                                                    "Are the Sabbat worth saving? How do you go about reforming it? Is it possible to reform it or should you just start a new sect/join another?"

                                                    For Tet-Anubis, he ended up joining the True Hand who I roleplayed as the "Real" Sabbat.
