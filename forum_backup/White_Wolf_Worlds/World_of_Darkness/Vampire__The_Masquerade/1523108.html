    09-24-2023, 08:48 PM
    When it comes to gameplay backgrounds like domain and herd are really only good for blood, and in most scenarios, blood is already trivially easy to attain and store.           Backgrounds and I have a love/hate relationship.

          I love how they can be adjusted by play rather than by XP and that often what they are capable of is more than just the sum of their parts. On the other hand, I hate the fact that the game system as a whole seems to have a collective melt down when a player leverages Backgrounds in a way that isn't just handwaving the bookkeeping.

          Personally, I would love there to be an expansion book that adds depth to the Backgrounds, in the same way the Exalted 2nd Edition Oadenol's Codex book created whole new aspects of play just by adding depth to the Manse background. You are free to use the simple Background found in the core book for faster character creation or you can use the expanded rules for far more intricate use of the background.

          Domain suffers from being so abstract that it is bent into whatever a given game needs rather than having its own mechanics. For example, if your domain is a local hospital, the rating could mean that you are an orderly who practically lives there and considers it their personal hunting grounds or personal plot point area (Being Human). Alternatively, Domain could mean that the hospital is owned by you, meaning that it gives you a Resources score, its blood bank counts as a Herd score, the fact that you can get off the books medical work done gives you Influence and Status scores not to mention an Ally score.

          There comes a point where having a high enough Resources ranking means that you start "buying" background dots which in turn magnify your ability to acquire Resources, but since Rank 5 is as high as you can go in the system, you have to start upgrading your Coterie mates Resources to take advantage of Pooled Backgrounds, but with little effort you can max out all of them. What exactly does game balance look like when the coterie has a Resources 25 ranking? Your coterie GDP and Influence levels is greater than most nations and that can be generated by starting character grade stats.

                  Backgrounds should be something gained or lost continually.

                  Every vampire in the city is looking for safe blood, and an edge over their competition. Every hunt is dangerous. It represents exposure, it represents the prospect of hunters, law enforcement, or some idiot with a cell phone camera recording them. It represents possibly stepping on the toes of other vampires who are also hunting, or worse, protecting THEIR herd.

                  That Domain 2, Resources 3, and Herd 3 that your Strip Club Haven represents is a tempting target for that Ventrue ancilla with a childe. What do you do when some starving Brujah douchebag wants to feed from a stripper - a member of your Herd? What if YOU are that Brujah douchebag? Or that Ventrue?

                  You want Backgrounds to mean something, then make them mean something. A vampire with no Backgrounds has no money, no safe haven, no influence, nobody to help them out. Every hunt should be an upward slog, every daylight hour a threat. A vampire with tons of Backgrounds should feel like they're on Easy Street. Resources, mansions, free blood, constant streams of intel, and lackeys to do the dirty work are all on the table. Give them the Golden Spoon, then threaten to take it away. This is what makes Elders so cagey and paranoid about their interests anyway, let your group get a taste of that.

                          If you want a handwave-y but quick "boost" to Backgrounds, let players add them to relevant dice pools, and apply them liberally.

                          Domain might add to dice pools to remain hidden in a large hunting ground, or the ST might use those dots as a penalty for an intruder who wants to avoid your traps.

                          While Retainers gives you well trained ghouls, Herd gives you weak nooks to throw in the path of others. A herd might also give you access to information, or a bonus to Intimidation or Status-related actions where having numbers on your side would help. Your herd might also be able to provide you with a safe house at a time of crisis.
