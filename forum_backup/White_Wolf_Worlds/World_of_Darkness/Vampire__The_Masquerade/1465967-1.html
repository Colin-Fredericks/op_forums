                                    Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                                    The Christian God is an evil Wyrm corrupted weaver spirit called the Patriarch according to the Black Furies. A pagan Mother Goddess is the supreme deity.

                                    In Mage, God does not exist unless humans imagined him up because everything is due to what you believe being true.

                                    Changelings state the Tuatha De Daanan and Formori are the supreme deities of the world because they are the original incarnations of dream and nightmare.

                                    Wraith explicitly makes it clear there is no Heaven for the majority of beings, just the Far Shores.

                                    And YEC isn't even true in DEMON: THE FALLEN. Demons routinely discuss events happening millions and billions of years ago. Not that the majority of Abrahamic faiths believe in YEC either but that's separate.

                                    We also know its wrong in other lines because the Mokole remember back millions of years.

                                    These are not small details.
                                    The official stance on the "Abrahamic" faith in the books is complex, but is there.

                                    I think the first thing to do is go down with this "Abrahamic". No matter what some people pretend to mean, we're talking Christianity. As much as there is a relationship between Christianity and other Abrahamic faiths, most of the themes and myths we're talking are Christian.

                                    Now, the game lines were ever cautious in not making the Christian faith as the only possible explanation for things. But they always kept it close by all the same.

                                    Werewolf is the line that more promptly refuses the Christian mythology, but let's not read more than it says. The Garou have several, inconsistent, interpretations about the world, and the Umbra is a complex place. There we can find many entities that are born from the concept of the Christian God without it meaning that this god is any of those entities. So it never really contradicted Christianity but in the demonstrably fallible beliefs of the Garou.

                                    Mage really doesn't work like that. The books do define that an underlying reality does exist below the Consensus, and that the Consensus can only go so far about this. Although little is known of this underlying reality, it may include an omnipotent, Magick and Consensus-Immune God. Indeed, it is the belief of many important Traditional Paradigms.

                                    Changelings don't think the Tuatha and Fomorians are the supreme deities. They believe they are the Primal Kithain. Big difference, as the Changelings don't actually consider in this the origins of the world, or even of the Dreaming, just of their species. They do assume there was a world and a Dreaming before the Tuatha, and probably before the Fomorians.

                                    Heaven isn't as directly important as people think. Heaven and Hell as we understand them are medieval constructs. Also, Wraith doesn't negate them either, as the Wraiths also have a mysterious "after" they suspect much but know little about.

                                    Demon may not follow YEC, or even actually need the Christian bible to be true to begin with, no matter how it seems at first glance. But it just can't pretend to hide the iconography and what will be the presumed truth if it must come out.

                                    Remember also that you can be Christian-based even when you try to avoid it. Witchcraft is an example of an absolutely Christian theme, as it didn't came from pagan religions, but from Christian prejudices and misconceptions about some practices (not all of them religious, even).
