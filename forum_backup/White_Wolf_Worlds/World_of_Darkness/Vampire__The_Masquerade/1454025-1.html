    For my two cents, I would say the Tremere are the most dangerous clan in V20 and earlier versions.

    As others have stated, the combination of Thaumaturgy & Dominate more or less gives that a massive lead in power. The fact that they have Auspex means they can collect large amounts of information as well as protect themselves from any Obfuscate based clan. The Rituals and the variety of Paths means that even if you don't have the right knowledge on hand, the clan solidarity means you can more that likely put in a call to have a specialist temporarily assigned to your chantry until the problem has passed.

    The chantries are described as routinely being able to withstand centuries worth of warfare from multiple clans. Thus it is safe to say that when bunkered down the Tremere are one of the hardest clans to actually get at. Even then the Tremere often have artificial minions in their chantries so they are one of the few clans with more than human/animals ghouls at their beck and call.

    The Tremere are better hackers than the nosferatu thanks to the path of Technomancy. Thus any thing that is dependent on computers is weak to the clan or can be put to their uses. Money is a non-issue for them since they can just produce their equipment with Path of Conjuration or just produce precut gemstones and sell that for money online. With a big enough bank account the clan can play in the same sandbox as the Ventrue, the Giovanni, or the Lasombra when it comes to business influence.

    The Tremere clans biggest weaknesses are two things specifically.

    The first is that because of the clans focus on the arcane, they have very stringent standards for who can be embraced or ghouled. While this means a higher baseline of quality control for neonates, it also means that they can't replace losses quickly or easily. This is part of the reason the clan relies on Gargoyles and other artificial minions for front line duty as well as defaulting to "bunker down" and outlast the siege mentality.

    The second issue that the Tremere have is that the writers can not stop tinkering with the clan, its backstory, its metagame placement, etc. If you compare the Tremere to pretty much every other clan, they have had more fluff related twists, more characters responsible for pivotal moments, and more plot points than any other group. Is San Francisco being invaded by Kuei-jin, call in the tremere to contain them, do you need the plot point containment to be breached, a tremere character is canonically a traitor. Do you need the assamites/banu haquim hobbled, call the Tremere in. Do you need Saulot to do something, have the sabbat Tremere immolate for no good reason. It goes on and on, the Tremere have been treated as plot filler so many times that it's laughable. Then of course the Tremere were introduced as the only clan to have magic, but then the writers started giving magic to every new clan or clan update. Yeah the writers are truely the Tremeres only real enemy.

    It is arguable that the only clan that comes close to the meta-power or meta-influence of the Tremere on the setting is the Baali for their supposed corruption of some other clans and their max level disciplines can unleash WoD brand Cthulhu on the setting. Alternatively the Giovanni could do something similar by bringing down the barriers to the lands of the dead, but the critical thing to remember is that in the case of the Tremere they have done these things already, while the Giovanni and Baali are largely just potential plot points.

Defection that will as well be taken with more ease and less apprehension than if it was a Ventrue or a Tremere.

All other clans that aspire for leadership: Ventrue, Tremere, Tzimice, Lasombra, and even Brujah have either their antediluvian removed from the Jihad or should rightfully fear their wrath after some... unfortunate events during the first Anarch Revolt.
