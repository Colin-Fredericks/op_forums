                        Originally posted by GreatHornedRatkin View Post

                        So they're a whack job? Okay!

                        I mean, I understand and agree with the assessment that playing suboptimal characters can make for fun and engaging role play opportunities. But actively punishing the player for not utilizing them is ridiculous.
                        The thing is - TTRPGs are still entertainment, and unlike something like movies or books, people playing TTRPGs are deciding what to include in their interactions with this form of media. So I can understand that the developers might want VtM to be angst filled drama but they cannot stop people from portraying a coterie of losers who gather each night to make pranks on government officials or celebrities. VtM doesn't need to be personal horror, it can be comedy, action, science fiction or romance. Hell, often it can be all of those things in one Chronicle. And TTRPG developers should realize that people like different things and they do not have to put dozens of books about comedy in VtM but also they should not make STs and players feel inferior because they want to run VtM for laughs.

                        I was not engaged with the game when V20 happened but from what I have seen of the books OPP understood that people want different things from the setting and their approach to the metaplot was a reflection of that. You could play pre-Gehenna, during the Gehenna, post-Gehenna or ignoring the events of the Time of Judgement altogether. And they understood that even the silliest thing from the previous editions were someone's favourite parts so when they handled them they tried to make them fleshed out and interesting to both appeal to the original fans but also make it appetising to people who never liked them. I never cared much about DSotBH but "Black Hand..." is one of my favourite WoD supplements. Just imagine, Baali who do not want to destroy the world but want to save it.

                        So I am hoping that V6 can bring more tools for people wanting to play varied games of VtM and there are some rays of hope with elders and Sabbat being made playable again.
