                                                            AnubisXy Shadeprowler
                                                            The character is 7th gen from the DAV20 playable Niktuku. They are presented as progeny of Baba Yaga, hunt down Nosferatus preferably but also other vampires and are often bound to their sire or ancestors or Absimiliard (I can't remember how it was phrased exactly).

                                                            My idea for a playable one in a coterie was to have his sire pose as a Toreador for centuries, so he officially comes off as Toreador. Both clans have Auspex and Celerity and both value beauty in Infants. The lack of talent in Presence can be explained as a quirky lack of social engagement compared to most Toreador, with hunting and fighting being treated as a form of art. One dot in Alternative Identity would be all that is required to be accepted as a quirky Toreador in the Domain as long as I and my Sire don't draw attention to ourselves.

                                                            I don't think Clan Enmity (Nosferatu) would be a must: obviously if you let slip that you are Niktuku and want Nosferatu vitae, some will come for you if you look weak, but the basis of their relationship as I understand it is that 1) the Niktuku avoid drawing attention, either isolating themselves from Cainites or pretending to be from another clan; 2) Nosferatus don't hunt for Niktuku, they simply try to survive them and play defensively. Other clans don't know or don't care, unless they discover a serial killer of Cainites, in which case the punishment is the same regardless of clan or bloodline.

                                                            As hunters, their discipline spread is quite effective. Sure, they miss out on Obfuscate, but they can still hide with a massive pool of Dexterity + Stealth + Celerity and having extra blood per turn to pump Dexterity. A surprise attack with both Potence and Celerity is potentially fatal and can be struck at a distance (thrown weapons). Auspex obviously helps hunting prey anywhere, each power in its own way. Young ones are not hideous and can hide comfortably in plain sight.

                                                            Generally, allowing for 7th and higher gen Niktuku means putting them on the same level as normal characters, because the Niktuku's main advantage to be boogeymen is their age and low gen. Between an Assamite and Niktuku of similar age and gen, the Assamite is probably more dangerous. Even a Warrior of Glycon is more dangerous.
