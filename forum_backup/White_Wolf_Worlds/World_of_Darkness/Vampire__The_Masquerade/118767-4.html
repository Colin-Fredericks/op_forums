    The original kindred of India were the City Gangrel. The bloodline is in fact older than the Gangrel we know. The Indian Gangrel were nearly wiped out by the Ravnos, and had to leave India, starting a feud between the clans.

    (The vampire myth we know is in fact Indian in origin and came to Eastern Europe by the silk road. It refers to creatures with glowing red eyes, claws and which turn into bats.)

    The Ventrue, which admit to having no Antediluvian and who are suspiciously absent from early Kindred history between the mythic age of the second city and the rise of Rome, are in fact a bloodline.

        A short number of mine:

        * The Crone is Lilith. This is one that's very popular and almost certainly true. Lilith is a 2nd generation vampire with Thaumaturgy who assumed a dark visage (or simply aged even with her old blood) before tricking Caine into loving her. She is a harbinger of Gehenna and the mother of her own Antediluvians.

        * There's a huge number of non-13 Clans spread throughout the world. There's local ones to South America, China, and other places with perhaps as many as fifty. It should be noted that while Caine is the originator of the vampire myth, he's a figure from Africa originally so this expansion is something that just reflects modern world elements.

        * Malkav and Saulot are the same Antediluvian. They're the Before and After Golconda. No one knows which.

        * They might also be brothers.

        * Saulot embraced the Baali as a revenge Embrace. It was meant as a punishment and resulted in a bunch of 4th generation Infernalists running around. Saulot spent the next few millennia hunting them.

        * Caine created the Six Traditions. He expressly forbid Diablerie, though.

                                        The Clans of Kindred are divided into two camps: the Clans of Caine and the Clans of Lilith. The first Gehenna (as alluded to in Beckett's Jyhad Diary) was a civil war between the members of the Second and Third Generations who either sided with Caine or Lilith depending on how they felt about Lilith's story that Caine betrayed her. The Laibon are descended from Methuselahs or Antediluvians who decided to stay neutral and basically just stopped talking about Caine, hence the reason why the Laibon are so very close to Cainites in some regards, but very different in others.

                                        Eventually, the tide of the first Gehenna War turned against Lilith, and she and whatever loyal Kindred she had left fled across the Atlantic to the Americas. Lilith and her Clans started to make their Havens in bodies of water deep enough to shield them from the sun as a way of ritually distinguishing themselves from the traitor Caine and his pawns. Hence the reason why the Ancestor in CbN V5 and its "Cultivar" cult are intrigued by the Bahari: they are Cainites who have rejected Caine's propaganda and support Lilith. This also means that the Drowned who have been referenced in BJD and V5 are the Clans of Lilith. By extension, this implies that the Pisanob are Drowned who infiltrated the Giovanni/Hecata, and the Tlacique are descended from a blood sibling of Set, my money being on Anhur, Bastet, or Sekhmet.

                                                    First off I just wanna say that those books of apocrypha sound really interesting and I need to check them out someday.

                                                    Anyway now, to share some Noddist head canons of my own:

                                                    •There were seven, or at least seven, members of the second generation embraced by Caine. They were the King and Queen of Enoch, Enoch, Irad, Zillah, The Crone, and Dracion. The city of Enoch had the misfortune of being one of the earliest settlements while also not knowing the tale of Caine's exile. They welcomed him into the city and he slowly grew to take power over it through control of and the eventual embrace of the King and Queen. However Enoch's discovery led to the young prince's embrace and a series of tragedies that saw the king and queen destroy themselves, beginning the dark path of Caine's brood.

                                                    •Enoch (the wise, to lead) and Irad (the strong, to battle) were kindred of The First City and so was Zillah (the beautiful, to comfort) who Caine had embraced for compassion. Caine's curse was to wander though and his desire to be loved compelled him to find a wise woman who promised to help him. This was The Crone...

                                                    •The Crone was not Lilith herself, but a follower of Lilith, one of the first bahari and a cultist who sought out Caine to humiliate, pain, and teach him. She later went on to pass the embrace onto Ennoia, Lilith's daughter.

                                                    •Early in Caine's wandering he was alone, weak, and deeply pained. He was found one night by a nomadic people and their leader, a passionate man, lent Caine aid and comfort. In gratitude, Caine offered the man his thanks and loyalty and would eventually show it by embracing him, Dracian, father and eventual sire of Zapasathura.

                                                    •The vampire who eventually wandered down the Nile and discovered the pre-historic Egyptians and spoke to their leader was Dracian. He would watch the domain and eventually decide to embrace both Sutekh the ambitious warrior and Osiris the wise leader.

                                                    •The First City was for a great deal of time the domain of Caine, the three childer who stayed by his side, and their own childer. However in time outsiders such as Ennoia, Sutekh, and Zapasathura would visit Enoch and realizing that his brood may grow beyond his control without careful husbandry, he forbid the third generation to embrace. This was perhaps the beginning of the end as every antediluvian would eventually disobey...

                                                    •The thirteen antediluvians are the most well known of the third generation however there were others in the first city, those who like Caitiff were without the Curses From Caine. Little of these kindred is known, but their legacy remains...
