                    I posted my favourite clans here. The Corpse fuckers are so-so and I've never played one (if I did, i'd play a Samedi hands down and they don't even have it in clan.) The Tremere are a multi-faceted clan with a lot of variation in what you can do (so long as you serve the pyramid) . The main distinct playstyles that come to mind are that you can play a manager, you can play someone who doesn't give a fuck about politics and just wants to wizard, and you can play gestapo. All three of these are fun, and you can of course combine all three of them into the most typical tremere you can. You could also play against type somewhat and be the face of the clan. The Ventrue... yeah, ok, there's a bit less versatility, but it's mostly the same: you can play the accountant, the CEO, the firebrand priest, the crusader... You can manage your servvants and ghouls and private army and mob and herd or you can just say "I send in some guys to deal with it" (Honestly, you do need a little bit of a discussion with your GM even when you're minimalistic about this sorta thing) or as a Ventrue you can just dominate some people on the fly or prep a dominate for a plan or...

I do like getting meticulous about things, but to me the best way of doing that is giving the storyteller a piece of paper with some important things and a simple overview of what my character owns/runs in the background and saying 'you got a problem with any of this?' That way you can keep this stuff out of the way of the game until it's necessary. You can handwave a lot of background stuff in vampire if you don't want to delve into it.

                                    My favorite has changed as I have gotten older. Back in the day with 1e I was all about the Malkavians (I was also in middle school so...you know).

                                                        Here's my preferences. Note that they are built on the clan's culture and not on its mechanics (which would be just disciplines and clan flaw after all).

                                                        Brujah: because I like how they play against the classical stereotype of the vampire incarnated by the Ventrue. Brujah dream... and they rage against those who would stop them from acheiving it. I also like the duality within the clan, philosopher kings, warrior scholars, rebels with a cause, fighters for justice that always risk becoming what they swear to destroy because of their fury.

                                                        Assamite: back in the old days, when I was young and stupid and the clan was introduced I thought the Assamites were the good guys. After all they hunted down other vampires who abused humanity. It took me a while to realize that was a naive reading of the Assamites. Fortunately revised came along (and libellus sanguinis 3 as well) and with it came one of the best (if not the best) clanbook of them all... and it was clanbook Assamite. Rather than staying as a generic blood cult of assassins the clan gained much depth and a fascinating history populated with incredible figures. Because of their nature Assamites were never easy to play in normal VtM games but to this day I find them one of the most rewarding clans to play and to include in any chronicle.

                                                        Lasombra: like the Ventrue but better. These guys want results and are willing to do whatever it takes to get them. It's impossible to talk about them without mentioning their shadow-mastery but that's not all they are. They can be surprisingly human (back in DA I dare say they were one of the most humane clans) or the most wicked of monsters sometimes because they believe that by becoming monsters they are serving God's plan to make humanity better. All that said, it's very simple, the Lasombra manage to have the good qualities of the Ventrue and add an undeniable cool factor the dull patricians never dreamed of having.

                                                        Edit:

                                                        About the Tremere. Keep in mind House Tremere actually predates Vampire the Masquerade as a game. It can be argued the Tremere influenced and shaped the game in a big way, especially considering how Tremere himself was the favoured character of the guy who invented VtM and used to be the powermongering bad guy even when he was a wizard and the idea of him becoming a vampire had not yet formed.

                                                            Favourite Clans, eh?

                                                            Well, I'll echo some sentiments already expressed by others; it would be easier to list the ones I like less than the rest, to be honest. Let's give it a try, though.

                                                            I have great affection for the Nosferatu, especially falling in love with the Dark Ages clan picture. I love Nosferatu (the movie), Shadow of the Vampire is great fun, I just enjoy this aesthetic, in this game and others. With such a striking Clan Weakness, they're arguably in a position to get a unique discipline... but their spread is already pretty darn perfect! The downside is how the focus seemed to grow more and more towards the icky spectrum rather than monstrous. Granted, some artworks aren't even ugly, but obviously inhuman still.

                                                            On that note, it's silly the glowing vampire eyes are actually a super-rare supernatural flaw and most can't see in the dark. That's worse than Garou keeping human senses in wolf form, since they're at least supposed to spend some time not as a wolf. Vampires are night hunters.

                                                            I also like the Malkavians. Kinda. I like Malkavians who are done well, but fishmalks are, sadly, a thing. I like to think my Malkavians were at least okay, since I never heard any complaints. Only surprise at hearing those actually were Malkavians, now and again. I've come around to sticking with Dominate too. Dementation is, shall we say, a bit situational.

                                                            Being a sucker for Egyptian lore, especially Set, the Followers drew me in too. Even if they're quite miscast. Were I the writer in charge, I'd sprinkle in one or more different origin myths, like hints that their Founder is actually Typhon, not Set, and that the identification with the Egyptian god of foreigners happened after Typhon first visited and sired childer there.

                                                            The Giovanni. Necromancers are great. Necromancer vampire family, with an (un)healthy dose of Bathory sprinkled in? Sign me up! And yes, it does help that they're a very European Clan and that's where most of the chronicles I've played were situated. I wonder if that's the case for others also? It just feels... more right to go with a Giovanni over a Samedi when your game is set in central Europe.

                                                            As noted above, there are also Clans I like less, and those I want to like more than I end up doing.

                                                            I want to like the Tzimisce, but they sometimes strike me as overdone. Plus, they shed the darkly noble aspect almost entirely by modern times, with the freaks now the heart and soul of the Clan. I absolutely LOVE reading about the different lineages in the revised Clanbook, about the Eldest's own childer; in many ways, they feel like the most CLAN of the vampire Clans to me.

                                                            Assamites. Never been a fan of one-note murderfactions. It was a wonderful idea to break them that up a bit.

                                                            Ravnos. They appear to have so much potential, and yet, nobody seems to know what to do with them, so they end up having a bridge dropped on their collective heads. For the Western branch, maybe they could've done something in the manner of a religious movement believing themselves demons possessing dead bodies..? Then have them duke it out with the Lasombra? I'm sure there's SOMETHING that can be done with them.

                                                            And least liked... are some of the bloodlines. Is that cheating?
                                                            The Daughters of Cacophony and the Kyasid. The former are kinda just there, and the latter read like someone's self-insert.
