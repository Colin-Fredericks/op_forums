    Wan Kuei, the Sabbat and the Gehenna crusade...

    06-14-2020, 06:48 PM
    So I found a fun little quote in BJD while talking about all the other WK stuff.

    It comes as a surprise to all but E Division when
    the Wan Kuei begin a campaign of terror in the
    Middle East — in cities from Tehran to Medina.
    The Ashirra are stunned by the flagrant hostilities
    they’d until now avoided. E Division knew the attacks
    were coming, and even helped orchestrate
    some of them. They quietly observed, making
    note of all active asuratziyya agents. E Division will
    be using their Archons and unknowing dupes to
    burn out the attackers, while rallying the Ashirra
    against the Wan Kuei — who they style as a mutual
    enemy. Is there a link between these attacks and
    the rise of the Gehenna Crusade the conservative
    Sabbat priesthood is preaching? The possibility of
    a Sabbat — Wan Kuei may be too terrible for most
    Kindred to consider. But what if it’s true?

    I thought this was INTERESTING given what we now know about v5. The Ashirra have become close friends with the camarilla. The Sabbat has gone on Crusade.

    I've always thought of the WK has being presented as "what if the systems in the sabbat you know.. actually worked?" Dharmas actually are PATHS that lead to ENLIGHTENMENT rather than just ignoring the Beast. Wus are Packs with actual spiritual connections. Ritae are... rites.

    Add to this another Open storyhook from the long ago days Saulot and the WK. That he failed to figure out the key to becoming a WK. That enlightenment was just out of reach and then "well maybe the pcs can figure out!" occasionally popping up here and there but we also know that whatever Saulot's game with the Salubri, Tremere and the Salubri Antis seems to have come to a head with the Tremere breakup and Vienna being hit....

    We Also know broadly v5 is trying to combine groups decreasing the number of random factions. The Sabbat has even lost the church of caine interesting enough?

    So what if the next time we see the WK and the Sabbat they are one and the same? The Thousand Hungry Dead.
