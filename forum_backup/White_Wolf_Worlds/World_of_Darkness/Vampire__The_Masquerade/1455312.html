    Baali or infernalists as Player Characters - doable or not and how do you do it?

    07-21-2021, 01:15 PM
    Greetings,

    Is it doable to have a Baali (or an infernalist) player character in a group when everybody is on board?
    We have at our disposal clanbook - baali (black dog), all the V20 books and from storyteller vault the 2 books related to baali.
    We also have path of screams, book of madness and devil's due from old lines to look into. (have been playing for well over 2 decades)
    1. Are there any tips and tricks to make this work? (except of course the part about don't go into details which should remain unnamed and we have easy stops if it should not work out,...) I'm asking cause this is the first time we are going to play the "bad guys", if there is something like good guys in the world of darkness....
    2. Is there any way to make demonic investments works because as they have no XP component, they scale pretty harshly?
    3. Are there any other books we might need?
    Thank you very much for any and all advice even if it's, don't do it.

      1: Just hope that he's a good player with experience playing 'normal' vampires. "Chaotic evil" is not a license to be stupid. Try your best to be impartial; Don't stack things against him because his character is morally reprehensible, stack things against him when he's left evidence around that he's a threat to the status quo. Vampire's are too politically inclined to do the right thing; Not everyone's going to try to purge the witch for dealing with a devil, blackmail is a likely option.


      2: Look at werewolf. most Investments are essentially Gifts. Optionally, you can make them cheaper by pairing flaws with the gifts. Moderating what 'gifts' the player can take/what level they are is important, because just granting the vampire werewolf gifts at the level werewolves get them can be a terrible mistake. But 3/4/5x level is fairly balanced; take your pick. Alternatively, every downtime you could let your players allocate time to gathering backgrounds/rituals, and allow the infernalist to use this time to secure new deals (which distracts them from gaining backgrounds)

      3. Freak legion's cool. Not the same thing, but relevant enough. KotE had an interesting system for infernalism, but I haven't touched those books for a wihle.
      Use DAV20 for Diamonion. It's not perfect, but it's by far the best version.

          I've never tried to make demonic investments work for anyone who isn't an NPC.

          If you have the Demon: the Fallen core rulebook and/or Damned and Deceived, the supplement for Demon players' thralls, I'd recommend using the system for thralls as a substitute for investments. For every point of Faith that a human thrall's pact would cost them, substitute one extra point of Blood lost every day instead.

                              That sounds more like a Nergali to me, albeit a slightly more pragmatic one. That's more Chaotic Evil territory, but it could be Neutral Evil with the right balance.

                              KotE has a good system for investments, as others have said, and probably meshes the best with V20 since it uses Revised rules.

                              I would consider whether he's 'out' as a Baali or whether he poses as Tremere (a common con, since the Tremere and Baali share a blood link via Saulot). The latter would make more sense, since Baali are uniformly hunted.

                              You could perhaps have him pretend to be Malkavian (superficially, some Daimonion powers look like Dementation at the lower levels), but I think Tremere is an easier cover to maintain unless there are loads of Tremere in your setting who will notice he isn't part of the Pyramid.
