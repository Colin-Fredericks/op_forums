    v5 Baali homebrew book...review/issues

    10-26-2021, 03:36 AM
    So im not sure about the clan baali guide.... its fandmade and looks highly professional. First impression - wow this could acutally be an official thing. However there is some issues with the book that i just want to mention as suggestion for improvement.

    1) Clan Bane: Double damage from true faith, gains repelled by (holy symbols) like the folcloric bane. Issue: How do you then explain the Baalis presence in the church hierarchy up to cardinal?
    Problem 2: Doesnt take into account severity of the curse / bloot power at all.

    Solution: gain a +X dice penalty, depending on severity of the curse, on any action on "holy ground" or acting against someone / thing with true faith. Gain +x damage from true faith that cant be soaked.

    2) Humanity....now that will be its own topic cause really, The Tzimisce and in this book the Baali gained an ability that basicly allows them to cheat their way out of consequences for their actions. You caaaan do it but the paths, while more confusing and complex, at leat make sure you actually have to adhear to some tennets

    3) Discipline. Given how presence works in v5, shares abilities with daimonion and that dark thaumaturgy would be integrated into blood magic, id change the clan disciplines to obfuscate, daimonion and blood sorcery, making the dark thaumaturgy paths into choosable points instead of the standard powers.

    Daimonion 1: Overall well done but maybe replace the OR with AND. so at 5 net successes you get all these informations not just one. Remember it can be resistet.
    Since it is also the signature move for Baali AND prerequisite to further powers id remove the rouse check. You can do the same thing with auspex for free.

    Daimonion 2: can stay as is

    So Daimonion 3 dots. 3 dots is quite a bit to gain and you get....1 aggravated damage...that you have to aim and hit...and can be dodged. I am underwhelmed. If you can use the normal dodge rules it should do damage based on net hits. So i roll 4 successes, the enemy dodges with 2 successes - takes 2 aggravated damage. Sounds better to me at least specially given in the current state id have to make 1 rouse check per shot...

    Daimonion 4 & 5: I am overall fine with but just one rouse check? Id increase that to 2 for the 4 ppower and 3 checks for the level 5 power given it can really weaken an opponent severly...and for weeks or longer...

    Dark thaumaturgy:

    First, it was stated at least in v20, that vampires cant use sorcery anymore (just like they loose the ability to use true magick if they were awakened) but they can use thaumaturgy instead. Hence, i wouldnt classify dark thaumaturgy as "sorcery" but actually integrate it as options for blood sorcery, the discipline

    Second, id add a lot more optional powers from official dark thaumaturgy paths before jumping to rituals

    As example you can take all the official paths as they are, also system wise and just change bloodpoint cost with rouse checks and you got a lot of options to add to the discipline depending if you do path of phobos, pleasure, pain etc. In special path of the unspoken can be used very well to disguise yourself as malkavian.

                              My opinion is that the Baali are better off fully converted into a cult rather than a vampiric bloodline. Here's my (very rough) draft of a Baali loresheet.
                              1. Unaussprechlichen Kulten. For millennia the Baali have cultivated the paradigms and cults to serve their dark masters. As a Baali yourself, you can partake of their services. Gain Status (Infernalists) ● that applies to any and all infernalists.
                              2. In Azaneal's Footsteps. Following the teachings of a great shaitan, you have tamed your Beast, leashing it to the needs of the Masters Below. Stains from all actions done in service to the Baali or Infernal agenda are reduced by one, to the minimum of zero.
                              3. Sense the Sin. [Cult of Shalim 4 is already Sense the Sin + Fear of the Void Below, we are going to reuse it.]
                              4. Lure Diabolic. It is one thing to force the victim to its knees. It is completely different to let them descend on their own. You can push any being, mortal or otherwise, to indulge in degenerative corruption. With a successful Manipulation + Persuasion roll, you can persuade any individual to break a Tenet or Conviction, gaining at least one Stain. When it is done, the difficulty of Social checks against the victim is reduced by one until the end of the story.
                              5. Condemnation. The power of cursing is the pinnacle of the dark arts of the Baali, and you have studied rigorously. Though you can not doom entire bloodlines like the Baali of old, you have harnessed enough infernal taint to doom a few individuals. Once per story, you can make a Resolve + Composure check to lay a curse on a person. No successes indicate mild annoyance, like the loss of a Background dot, while 4 or more mean crippling doom such as the impotence of vitae or use of all dice as hunger dice in every roll. The Baali can reverse this curse at any time they wish, though they will likely demand a high price. The Baali's death does not lift the curse, but at the Storyteller's discretion, a spiritual quest and divine absolution might.
