                            Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                            I think your problem is you're taking it as an absolutist statement versus a generality.
                            I’m not a psychic, so I can only take statements as they’re written. As an author, you know words mean things and that written text requires more care than the spoken because there is no inflection or emotion behind it to provide context.

                            “Mage's Euthanatos would never be on the Path of Humanity”

                            Never means “never.” It does not mean “rarely.” You do this quite a bit with your statements; use absolutist terms that apparently you don’t mean. I suspect you’d get far less pushback on the boards if you’d just clarify a bit. For example...

                            “Many of Mage’s Euthanatos would not be on the Path of Humanity.”

                            ...is apparently both closer to what you intended to say and also something I wouldn’t even disagree with.

                            * * * *

                            To the topic at hand though; I’m going to disagree on Paths being superior to Humanity. I think only using humanity is something V5 probably got right because, ultimately, the Paths are constructs created by humans (the writers of the game and, in universe, humans cursed with vampirism) that basically amount to “subset of human motivation we want to justify as playable” in the same way fantasy races generally are just representations of a subset of broader human nature.

                            Also I’ve seen damnably few players actually be able to divorce themselves enough from humanity to be able to play one as anything other than varying levels Humanity with an excuse for why a particular sin is actually justified.

                            “I can feed on the dying and not be a monster because I’m ending their pain” doesn’t feel like a path of enlightenment to me... it feels like a high conscience rating justifying why harming others isn’t ‘really’ harming others. “No,” you tell yourself, “its actually a good thing and you aren’t getting any closer to the Beast for doing it.”

                            Ultimately, the Paths feel like a cheat on the personal horror elements to me by giving you a choose your own morality system where whatever sins you want to commit are justified so you can keep playing the PC instead of losing them the same way you would in any other game with a PC morality clause (ex. WEG Star Wars and Dark Side Point accumulation eventually turning you into an NPC).

                            They feel like a workaround that was added to the game because the mechanics for the Humanity 1-4 range that most Sabbat would otherwise have were given far too severe hindrances to allow them to be playable (emergency daylight action and torpor length particularly) without some alternative morality systems that let them have better daylight dice pools and torpor lengths short enough to not make a torpored PC effectively “dead” as far as the chronicle is concerned.
