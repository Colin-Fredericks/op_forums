            I think the whole "technology is more dangerous" approach misses context badly.

                                                Originally posted by Lysander View Post
                                                The Nosferatu shouldn't be overlooked as a very serious threat as they have sharp ears and eyes with the use of Animalism and being able to hide themselves or bolt holes and look like others with use of the Obfuscate Discipline and for plain old intimidation value you can't go wrong with Potence.                                                 Nosferatu Obfuscate is so powerful, everyone forgets how dangerous they can get...

                                                How many Nos signature characters are there? Everyone has their Lucita waifu fantasies. Obfuscate prevents that kind of attention.

                                                Nos, along with Gangrel, Lasombra, and to a lesser extent Setites and Tremere, are just a notch under the Assamites as far as assassins. IMO, Assamites are a bit wasteful. Nos(and Gangrel) are better at Infiltration than the Assamites. Nos smoke the Assamites in Arson. And Animalism clans can attrit non-Animalism clans which is why they usually hang outside the city, or at the borders - ive used this in DA games.

                                                Nos discipline spread is very cost efficient in V20. Compare to Celerity and Quietus which are pretty costly blood-wise. Same with Brujah and Celerity+Potence.

                                                Nos Boogeyman can abduct someone(Potence to easily carry a subdued person) and take them deep down below - think that movie Aliens with the colonists glued to the walls with eggs implanted in their chest. Think about it: a strong enough Obfuscate4 and Potence could let a Nos just...nab someone in a group and disappear them and the group would be left wondering if that person slipped off to the restroom.


                                                Leatherface serial murderers and arsonists. Wassail or not? Coterie gets to find out!


                                                Local disease outbreaks. Oh, yeah, local drug outbreaks in a city must be the Setites.


                                                Obfuscate+Animalism5 utterly RUINS plots and NPCs(less so in the Sabbat, but still)."Shame about X, she keeps losing it at Elysium. Prince is gonna have to do something about that. The Curse can be difficult for some, after all. I wonder whats gonna happen to her turf? And she owes that rather large Boon to Y. Y better get payment soon."

                                                Nos racket #1: Obfuscate3 and mask as Vamp Y. Diddle Vamp X's ghoul while masked as Y. Start shit between X and Y. Sell info to either or both X and Y. Profit.

                                                Nos Racket #2: Find struggling Neonate. Become his friend and offer support(feeding/friendship/haven/etc). Help establish that Vamp. Set up scenarios to help that vamp get Boons over other NPCs(see racket #1). Profit from Proxy-Boons. Bonus if local Elders do 'Horse-Trading' with Boons. Its a dirty game, after all...

                                                Nos racket #3: Lies, damned Lies, Statistics, and Countermeasures. Nos info is reliable, everyone knows! Dont believe? Ask the Dominate-using Primogen, he will verify Nos X tells the truth! Because Nos X saw the (obfuscated by another Nos)truth with his own eyes! Keeping some Broodmates in the dark has some advantages!


                                                Vid on YouTube: SABBAT PACKS HATE WHEN YOU SHARE THIS ONE EASY AGGRAVTED DAMAGE TRICK!!! COMBAT WILL NEVER BE THE SAME!

                                                Some decades ago, my friends did some math on Revised Combat. I dont remember the details but idea goes: once a Char can deliver 4 Aggra damage/per attack, anything more is pretty much wasted. Even 3 is a lot. Delivery of that damage becomes the bigger factor. Everyone forgets every vamp can do Aggra damage with a Grapple+Bite. But few vamps have the dice pools to do so easily and reliably. So which dice pools facilitate the easiest delivery?

                                                Now, once could invest tons of XP in Disciplines like Quietus(poorly written in V20 and a waste past lvl2), Lure of Flames, or high level Combo powers like that Brujah flaming-fist one.

                                                OR THIS THIS ONE SIMPLE TRICK...

                                                Have the shop skills to take a $15 machete, drill holes in it, dip in Tar/other sticky viscous flammable material, and have a cheap flaming sword. Be sure to wear your leathers while using.
                                                Which Clan will tend to have more members that have basic Shop skills? The one that lives in scrap heaps? So, a Nos with Dex2+melee2 using a machete(S+1?) and Str2 + Potence2 will usually net 1-2 successes to hit vs non-elite opponents and for 1BP/turn do 5-6 damage per single attack with 2 of that damage guaranteed. Fortitude5 nets 2.5 successes on average. It gets dumber if you think about using Athletics.

                                                Incendiary sniper rifle headshots are the other avenue but that favors Malkavians and Toreadors.

                                                Lasombra Tentacle King keeping you down? They get squeamish when a Nos with a Flaregun walks into the room. Monomancy Trials get interesting if ST remembers Nos and Gangrel may have Animalism5.

                                                All in all, you cant blame people for sleeping on Nosferatu as being helpless. They cant see through Obfuscate.
