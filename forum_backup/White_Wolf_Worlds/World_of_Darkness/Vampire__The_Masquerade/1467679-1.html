    I just made Hungry Dead a worldwide phenomenon but they are outcompeted by Cainites because they 'breed' like cockroachs and take a tenth of the time to become relatively stable; the vast majority of Hungry Dead likely are killed as Wights if they rise in Kindred dominated regions and no one is any the wiser.

    Obviously I did a fair bit of work to refit Hungry Ghosts as presented in KotE Core because they are 100% Sinocentric ( and only Central Mainland at that) and pay only bare lip service to any of the hundreds of other cultures in the region, despite the semi-random use of Japanese terms. But luckily alternate Dharmas/Paths are easy enough to homebrew and ' Shintai'/Disciplines are even easier to brew based on folklore and legend.

    That said I find the Hungry Ghost model works way better for, non-movie/novel based European folkloric vampires and those of other regions like Oceania, South America, Africa, Pre-Colonial North America etc.

    All of the KotE books beyond the core acknowledge the existence of Cainite populations throughout Asia so I'm not sure why that keeps getting brought up. Its a big world there's room for both.

                    You can straight up say it works better for folkloric undead, period, because it does.

                    The thing we call a Vampire isn't in any sense a folkloric creature, it is strictly a pop culture phenomenon created out of a few key works of great influence, namely The Vampyre (Polidori), Dracula (Bram Stoker), Nosferatu (Murnau), The Vampire Chronicles (Anne Rice) and indeed, yours truly, Vampire: the Masquerade (Rein-Hagen). Together those works created the entire vampire mythos out of nothing.

                    So, while it isn't a meaningless feat in any sense, the rules for the Kindred aren't actually good at representing anything but itself.

                                    Honestly I'm not a fan of making the Wan Kuei Cainites and frankly I do believe the Hungry Dead could be restructured while keeping the core of what they are intact.

                                    That said, going with this notion of making Wan Kuei Cainites, I outright hate the idea of making Dharmas into Clans because they don't really work that way. They're philosophical beliefs akin to the Paths of Enlightenment. Railroading them into Clans would be like saying that only Toreadors can take the Path of Humanity while Gangrels have to be on the Path of the Beast and Ventrue to the Path of Honorable Accord. Would you restrict every member of a Clan to a specific Path of Enlightenment? No. So why should we restrict the Dharmas to specific Clans?

                                    To echo what others have said, Dharmas should be Cults with their Convictions. In V5 terms, everything about the Dharmas would be a Cult, not a Clan.
                                    Dharmas have structure, concept and prominence that are equivalent to the role of Clans in Western society: O dp agree that they are better as Path equivalents, however if we go with no KotE, but with a KotEK situation, there is significant conceptual overlapping and they provide an easy way of tailoring Clans to an Asian landscape.
