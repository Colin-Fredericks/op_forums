        My irresponsible and reckless speculation about the Sabbat is…
        1. The classic Sabbat no longer exists. It has been altered and shattered beyond repair and recognition.
        2. There will be multiple small factions and cells calling that each believe themselves to be the inheritors, or survivors, of the “real Sabbat.” These groups will call themselves different things.
        3. Some of these survivors will be content with survival. Others will seek some ideology to justify themselves and their bloody appetites.
        4. Lucita will probably be involved with one of the more agnostic cells, or at least one with the fewest religious trappings.
        5. Sasha will probably be involved with one of the more pious cells, or at least one with more religious trappings.
        6. Mimes will be involved.
        7. Multiple cells will be controlled, directly or indirectly, by former Sabbat members now controlled by some elder they diablerized.
        8. A massive part of the jyhad is a dog chasing its tail, with the ancients setting the younger vampires after each other in bloody minded games. What the Sabbat left behind, and what those remain do, will simply be a part of this process.
        9. The fractured nature of the Sabbat remains means they might well be found anywhere, in any city, among any sect. Anyone you know may or may not be a former member of the Sabbat. Maybe they are acting the way they do, or saying what they say, because they used to be (and still are at heart) Sabbat.
