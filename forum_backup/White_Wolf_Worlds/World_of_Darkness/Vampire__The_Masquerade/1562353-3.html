                One thing I'd do with the Lasombra: Have a bunch of Sabbat infiltrators inside the Camarilla now. The genuine defectors would panic as they try to figure out which of their clanmates is a traitor, but they'd be unable to risk losing the support of their Lasombra antitribu peers more generally. Meanwhile, the moles would be carefully setting up their assassinations and strategic coups, with some willing to play the (really) long game.

                This would fit the timeline of a resurgent Sabbat, its crusaders glutted on elder vitae, returning to reclaim their turf. Within the Black Hand, I wouldn't have Jalan-Aajav meet his end at the hands of the SI, but if he did, there's always the new hulul (Nizzam al-Latif, per Caine's Chosen: The Black Hand), leading an exultant Assamite antitribu. Failing that, there's also the one and only Dastur Anosh, who's faked his death so many times he could be Fatima at this point and we wouldn't know (not that I'm seriously suggesting that).

                In London, Monty Coven could do as BJD suggested, and set himself up as head of the British Isles. Or, if he's truly dead or set on independence, there's always Lady Astor (Giovanni Chronicles III, Ventrue Chronicle) who's been biding her time. What better time than in the wake of the SI's purge of the Camarilla? Or there's her chief rival, Doctor Streck, the anarchist warlord who somehow manages to disappear every time the actual fighting breaks out. Meanwhile, on the continent, Strohmann, the Nosferatu antitribu who likely smuggled the first Samedi to the Americas on slave ships, has been building his forces in Central Europe.

                Montreal would be fairly straightforward. Per BJD, Ezekiel was infected by Blood Rot, and one possible outcome was that the Sabbat would allow the Camarilla to take over, wait till the Ivory Tower Kindred contracted the disease, and then sweep back in. I wasn't fan of the idea that Metathiax, or at least his monstrous servants, was just a vozhd in V5; that was already a retcon, and a silly one at that. What would happen if the Camarilla were the ones who had to deal with infernalism for a change, though? Maybe some of the fleeing Camarilla forces had already been corrupted before they left. That'd certainly be interesting.

                I'm keen to see how V6 squares the circle. Since V5 was so at odds with prior editions anyway, and was quite happy to completely reimagine things (e.g., the Baali), I don't see why V6 can't be permitted the same leeway.

That's not hard at all, just use the old description of its ethics. That won't be a "Nature a human could follow", it would literally be the Path. Done. A few Paths may still have some kinks, but very minor and easy to hammer down.
                                                            And that does not compute as a way of looking at it for me. Like at all, that would just be 'humanity but weird' Not the completely different way of looking at the world and a different way of dealing with frenzy, with all the culture and ideology that went with that. I am not claiming it's invalid, I am claiming it doesn't work at all to make me able to use it for verisimilitude. If you can, go have at an good luck, it literally is antithetical to how rules feel to me, to me they ARE the simulation of the world, as integral to it as the lore, when Lore and rules disagree, I have mental work to do to make it fit for me. Again this is not a value judgement it's explaining how I function (or honestly dysfunction sometimes), (this btw is why you may have seen me argue dice roles as accessibility tools if I am trying to be a charismatic character let me set out a framework and roll for it)
