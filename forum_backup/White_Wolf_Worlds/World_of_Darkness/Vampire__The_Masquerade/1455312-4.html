    To me the key to the Sabbat is to stop thinking of homo sapiens are inherently more sacred than any other species. Homo sapiens are not sacred. Homo sapiens are just the ape that happened to win. Once you take that as a given, then vampires' treatment of homo sapiens ceases to be relevant. What matters is how do you construct a society of vampires designed around vampire needs? Whether actions are evil or not then become about whether those actions are constructive to a vampire society, not about what effect it has on homo sapiens, as homo sapiens are no more relevant than trees in this context.

    The treatment of homo sapiens is therefore only relevant in an extremely indirect way. If you found someone torturing a doughnut and twirling their moustache, you wouldn't call them "evil" or "monsterous", but you would consider them irrational and foolish and even goofy. It's a doughnut. Why are you torturing a doughnut? I mean, it's a doughnut for Caine's sake.

                    Another Set of Tips for Running Infernalist Chronicles

                    1. Evil versus Evil

                    There's a bunch of competing Demon Lords, Infernalists, and monsters out there to fight.

                    2. Use Demon: The Fallen for creating Patrons

                    There's a lot more nuance and interesting twists you can do by having the player character's demonic patron not necessarily fit into the stereotype of Bwa-ha-ha-ha evil.

                    3. Infernalists are not Nephandi

                    You can have them want to destroy the world but plenty of them are much like Spike. "We like to talk big, vampires do. "I'm going to destroy the world." It's just tough-guy talk. Struttin' around with your friends over a pint of blood. The truth is, I *like* this world. You've got... dog racing, Manchester United... and you've got people. Billions of people walking around like Happy Meals with legs. It's all right here... But then, someone comes along with a vision. With a real... passion for destruction... Angel could pull it off. Goodbye Piccadilly, farewell Leicester bloody Square. You know what I'm saying? "

                    4. Personal Revenge

                    As an infernalist in the Sabbat or Baali, there's undoubtedly some assholes you want to destroy with your power. Maybe you ARE worse than the Prince who had your mortal family killed because
                    you talked to them about being a vampire or the Sabbat bishop who Embraced you against your will but that doesn't mean it won't be satisfying destroying them.

                    5. Demons vs. Cthulhu

                    Worshiping the Devil may seem to be the worst thing you can do but at least they are...human. There are things that are incomprehensible creatures of madness and chaos out there and preventing them from unmaking creation is a good idea.

                    6. Are the demons using you or are you using the Demons?

                    This is a theme worth exploring.

                    7. Power Gaming is permitted

                    A Infernalist actually will do a lot to sink their EXP into Dark Thaumaturgy/Blood Sorcery. This isn't a bad thing as they are people who measure how much they've succeeded in their level of personal power.

                    8. Infernalists still struggle with the Beast

                    Being a monster doesn't mean you don't have to worry about becoming a mindless monster. You may also have a few Darth Vader-esque regrets. But you have COME TOO FAR TO BACK DOWN NOW!

                    9. Good guys being Knight Templar fanatics

                    Make use of the Templars, Society of Leopold, Werewolves, Imbued, and so on.

                    10. Downtime is always fun

                    Some demon worshipers just hanging around having a beer is hilarious by itself sometimes.
