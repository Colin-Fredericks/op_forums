        No, I don't think the Ventrue are anything but Bastards, but they're lawful bastards, at least, they want to appear to be lawful, and they don't want to appear as bastards.

        Reputation is very important for Ventrue. It means more allies, and less people going after your special and limited herd. That means, more often than not, doing the right thing by others, investing in other vampires, giving people a clap on the shoulder and welcoming them into your house with a beaming greeting. You don't learn 4 dots of etiquette purely because it lets others know of your good breeding, you learn 4 dots of etiquette to please others and to avoid offending the wrong people even in difficult circumstances. Reputation means you need the semblance of honesty, a good dose of fairness, and you can't be so catty like the Toreador are. The Ventrue are famed for their old money values, which means they look down on those who are taking risks and recklessly expanding. Such behaviours invite hostility thus undermining the stability. The Ventrue need reputation for stability, and stability for their herds. They can't be free and fickle like the Toreador, they have something to protect more than shiny things, they've got a herd to worry about.

        Draconian Ventrue are rare, and can only really exist in cities with lots of ventrue that can shelter them, or with individuals that have a broad feeding type and don't have to overly worry about herd (IE total hack Caitiff that are posing as Ventrue) or when the Ventrue is so phenomneally powerful and has half the city under his thumb.

        It's really not fair to draw examples from city books. It's not the job of writers to provide good examples of the setting, it's up to writers to provide drama and intrigue, so dysfunctional Ventrue leading failing states are going to be grossly overrepresented. (see also: Nosferatu with hearts of gold, Malkavians who insist they're healthy, Brujah Barbarians, True-faith Lasombra, Toreador who are useful)
