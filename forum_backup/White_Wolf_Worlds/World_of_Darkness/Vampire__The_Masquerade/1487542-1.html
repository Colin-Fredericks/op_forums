                                Speaking of my favorite NPCs:

                                (I fully believe a lot of these will come as no surprise)

                                Signature Characters

                                Lucita: Original Recipe Rebellious Lasombra Princess. She's an archetype and also the closest thing to an Anarch in the Signature Characters, She wants to live free of her sire and be with the ones she loves (Anatole, Fatima, and maybe Beckett). She was done dirty by the Lasombra Trilogy and I blame her being Dominated by the Antediluvian.

                                Chicago by Night

                                Lodin: My original love-to-hate character, I absolutely loved the way the books presented the multi-faceted nature of Chicago's Prince. He's just a DICK. Lodin worked very well as an antagonist because he was a snob, controlling, vicious to his enemies, and an all-round asshole. However, he wasn't STUPID and if you wanted to be a guy who became one of his goons or agents, then he would reward you for it. You could reasonably side with Lodin in a game and even if you were an Anarch or Independent, he was a guy that you might team up with against a potentially greater threat.

                                He was a multifacted guy with people he loved, weaknesses, and strengths both. He also wasn't too powerful so that if you wanted to use Lodin as your archnemesis, yes, it would be hard but there was a chance you could take him out. I was saddened by Olaf One Eye because Lodin deserved better. I almost feel like my Anarchs should heal him (probably via diablerizing Cristias) to become who he was.

                                Modius: I have a super-soft spot for Modius because he's so damn ludicrous and pathetic but if you take a moment to look at his stats, he's still DANGEROUS. I like the idea that Elders can degenerate so much they become a shadow of themselves and the fact that he's the Prince of Gary, Indiana makes it an interesting "starter villain" scenario. The PCs can overthrow Modius pretty easily if they work at it but that leaves them with Gary. Do they want it? I also like how petty and cruel his manipulations are as well. What he did to Allicia is particularly awful and yet perfect for a vampire.

                                Allicia: I still love her decades later. The whole "Victorian romance heroine" feel to her and her relationship to Modius worked well for me. I regret they didn't include the offbeat weirdness of her owning the Torch stripclub, though.
