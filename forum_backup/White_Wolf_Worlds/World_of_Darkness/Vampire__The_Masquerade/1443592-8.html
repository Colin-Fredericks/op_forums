                                                So, we’ve had few days for some poll results to come in and I think it has some interesting results.

                                                The first poll about which edition you prefer is currently sitting at 29 for V20 and 6 for V5... so 83% prefer V20 and 17% perfer V5 (I added 1 to the V20 count because MWIS couldn’t like his own post).

                                                The second about which edition you’re actually playing is sitting at 15 for V20 and 1 for V5 (the like count is 14:1 but I couldn’t vote on my own post).

                                                Now we can’t account for thread attrition, but by raw numbers that would mean 54% aren’t playing VtM at all, 43% are playing V20 or earlier, and just 3% are playing V5. The raw numbers would also indicate that just 17% of people who prefer V5 are actually playing V5.

                                                Taking the numbers for the second poll on their own, 94% of respondents are playing V20 or earlier and just 6% are playing V5.

                                                Regardless of how well the IP as whole might be doing, I think those numbers tell a story of the tabletop portion of the IP is not delivering content most of the IP’s fans in that medium would find useful.

                                                Serious suggestion for the people at OPP; I know it’d be a longshot getting Paradox to approve, but I think there’d be a market for a book that took V5’s concepts (ex. Thinblood Alchemy, banes as alternate clan weaknesses, new V5 powers as V20 style alternate/combo powers, etc.) expressed .them in V20 mechanical terms and which used a more objective presentation of the metaplot updates.
