    I'm pretty sure Mary would not consider it something to be "cured" exactly. Trisomy 21 (aka Down Syndrome) is one component of who he was meant to be, from birth. You wouldn't cure someone being left-handed, or unusually tall, or having a birthmark. That's all just part of who a person is. If he woke up tomorrow without that extra copy of the chromosome, he would no longer be Michael.

    Besides, Mary is not aware of any of the more obscure bloodlines and disciplines. She didn't even know ahead of time the emotional toll ghouling would have on Ed. Ghouling would presumably freeze Michael in time as far as his cardiac condition's progression goes; but then most ghouls sooner or later frenzy and that can't be good for someone with a weak heart.

    Copore sano (Obeah 3) heals wounds, which might help in the moments of cardiac arrest, but is not a long term solution. Mens sana (Obeah 5) soothes madness, but Down Syndrome is definitely not madness, though it might help when he is in emotional turmoil, just as with anyone. In theory, Biothaumaturgy or Vicissitude might be able to stabilize or even fix his cardiac issues, if the practitioner has enough mundane knowledge of cardiac surgery to touch the heart without killing Michael.

    Possibly mage intervention, but his DS is integral to his natural pattern and making him not have DS would cause serious pattern leakage and draw paradox like little else.

    The only long term fix is probably the embrace, but that raises a lot of other issues.

                        #57 Larry Khoury grew up tough. He was the oldest of seven boys from the side of town good people rolled up their windows when they drove through. His father came home late from the plant every night. Some nights so drunk he could barely walk. Other nights, the bad nights, he was just drunk enough to get angry. At what? It didn't take much. Anytime he was about to go on a tear through the boys, splitting lips and blacking eyes, Larry's mom would put herself in the line of fire. A few jibes about dear old pop's manhood, and she got the beating, not the kids. Even with mom running interference, by the time it ended, the seven brothers had had four broken arms, three broken legs, twelve dislocated shoulders and six cracked ribs between them.

                        When Larry was sixteen, in 1946, his father got it bad. His brothers were all out. Pops had mom cornered on the third floor of the rowhome they lived in and was just slamming and slamming and slamming. She was bent backwards out the open window, woozy, with blood flowing down her scalp, dodging his fists. Pop lifted his fist one last time, loading up a haymaker, and the iron doorstop came down on the back of pop's head. Larry's mom gasped, swooned for a moment and recovered her balance in time to see Larry holding the doorstop. It was in the shape of a small bulldog, and was solid iron. It was dripping a foul liquid, grey and oozing. His father was on the floor, his skull split open, brains clearly visible on the rug. She never knew a person had so much blood in them.

                        She sat there, on the radiator below the windowsill, numb, still bloody, not knowing what to do. Larry went immediately into action. He had planned this moment for sixteen years. He gathered a shovel, a heavy blanket and some lime from the outhouse. He slung his father over his shoulder and staggered down three flights of steps to the dirt floor basement. As far as Larry knows his father is still there.

                        He went out that night with his friends, got drunk and got a tattoo: "Mom", in hearts and flowers on his shoulder. He got a job at his dad's plant (there was suddenly an opening), and moved out three weeks later, after hand concreting the basement.

                        He worked at the plant until 1950, when he joined the US Army to go fight in Korea. He was an infantryman, and saw a good bit of frontline combat. He was a quiet, grim eyed soldier and though his the officers couldn't quite find anything to criticize in his service, they didn't trust him. He was brave, but a little foolhardy. He was a good leader within his unit, and the men respected him, but somehow when things went south he never seemed to be around. If the COs were able to admit it to themselves, they would have seen that every time Larry dropped an enemy combatant he would suppress an ecstatic shudder. He kept careful count of the enemy he killed. Anytime he got leave, he would add to his collection of tattoos. One for each kill. By the time he rotated home, he had 14 new tattoos. None of them larger than a few inches, and all easily hidden under his clothes.

                        Stateside, he joined the Detroit PD. He gained a reputation as an honest cop, but one who pushed the edge of acceptable violence. He always slammed his knee into perps' backs a little too hard, and pulled runners off the fire escapes into the concrete with a little too much enthusiasm. But if he seemed to enjoy it a little much, so what? No one in the department ever complained, of course, who would you rather have watching your back, someone a little too rough or not rough enough?

                        In 1966, Larry, service weapon drawn, cornered a drug dealer who had been cutting his product with dangerous chemicals, killing at least a dozen druggies. His hands were up and he was begging for his life. Larry took a deep breath, shuddered a bit and pulled the trigger. The dealer's brains exploded across the floral wallpaper. Larry felt the greatest release of his life. He was on desk duty for a few weeks, while the paperwork was processed. By the time he was back on the street, he had gotten his sixteenth tattoo.

                        The downtime was too much for him. He had tasted blood and he wanted more. He started wandering the worst neighborhoods on his nights off. Dressed well, and acting drunk. Muggers picked him often, but they never mugged anyone again. He developed a source for body disposal; local organized crime, the Detroit Partners, was happy to oblige. In exchange, he just had to keep the other cops away from "victimless" crimes like prostitution and the numbers racket. By the time he retired from the Detroit PD in 1986, he had exactly 427 small tattoos covering his body from his toes, up to just below the "V" neck of his t-shirts, down to the caps of his shoulders. Not one line of ink showed in uniform.

                        His last day on the force, the Detroit Partners threw him a retirement party at a fancy restaurant Larry could never have afforded. It was a small affair, a dozen guys, mostly from the Lebanese family that handled clean-up for the Italians. One of the big wigs Larry had never met before invited him back to an empty banquet room behind the kitchen. Charlie was a small man, but looked very athletic, the sort of guy who could rip you in half with one hand. His eyes were a vivid blue, but his skin was a dusky grey, the color of the streets after they put down cinders and the snow starts turning to slush. He asked Larry to tell him the story of his tattoos. Larry was confused. How did this strange looking man know about his tattoos?

                        Larry told him the story of every tattoo. He began with the most recent, a pair of dice in flames he got on the back of his knee, after killing a man who beat his wife and gambled away their money. Showing the collection and recounting each victim's sins took hours, working backwards. The last, of course, was Pops. Charlie said nothing, asked no questions, but just nodded gravely. Charlie placed his hand sympathetically on Larry's bare shoulder and offered him a job, as a ghoul providing him with "security". In 1993, after seven years of good service, Larry got a promotion.

                        By then, he was tattooed from his fingertips and palms to his jawline, and it crept up onto his scalp and down onto his temples and forehead. Only the center of his face remained blank. Otherwise, his tattoos were packed as tight as a rainbow bramble. Finally, now, upon his embrace, he could begin hunting, judging and executing even more vile monsters, those who can only rightfully be judged by the Banu Haqim.

                            #58 Alexis Gourdon is a victim of one of the worst embraces, one performed out of spite, rather than any real interest in recruitment. Even worse than that, she was embraced into the Toreador, which means her sire had a taste for the theatrics!


                            Alexis' father was a hunter who kept his double life a secret from his family. She had no idea that every night, he would sneak out of the house to go slay monsters and be back home in time for breakfast. But one night, Mr. Gourdon chose the wrong prey, which lead to the tragic circumstances that came after. A pair of vampires (twins even before their embrace) had been stirring up trouble in the city, and Gourdon was determined to see them exterminated. He managed to locate them, but was only successful in destroying the sister, while the enraged brother escaped into the night, rather than stay behind and risk final death. Gourdon decided to call his victory there and headed home. He arrived just three hours before dawn, enough time to slip into bed and get some much needed rest after a successful hunt. But just as he finished placing his gear in its passcode protected storage containers, the door to his house exploded off its hinges and a pack of Sabbat poured into his home, lead by the brother who had escaped that night.

                            Mr. Gourdon didn't have time to retrieve his gear again, because the lightning quick Toreador was already up the stairs and slammed him against the bedroom wall, momentarily stunning him. His wife was startled awake and began to scream, before a clawed hand was clamped over her mouth and a gun pressed against her temple. One of the brother's packmates had followed him upstairs and was now holding Gourdon's wife hostage.

                            Most Sabbat would have been able to just slaughter the family and be done with it, but no self respecting Toreador would have passed up an opportunity to bask in their victory, and so they spent the better part of an hour beating the hell out of the furious hunter. All the while, Mr. Gourdon didn't give him the satisfaction of surrender, until two more packmates entered the room, dragging the 16 year old Alexis by the shoulders like she was a toddler. That seemed to break her father's stoic front as he demanded they leave her alone. The vengeful brother turned to Gourdon, flashed him a fanged smile and then fell upon Alexis, embracing her on the spot.


                            Alexis would awaken the next night in her own bed, stripped of her clothing and with another man spooning her from behind. With a startled shriek, she jumped out of bed and turned to see the man who attacked her laying on his side with the same grin on his face. "Welcome to the Sabbat." he said.



                            Since then, Alexis' life has been one of passive aggressive torture. Her sire (now known formally as Richard) considers his abusive mistreatment to be his way of striking at her father even in death. When he isn't coercing her into acting like a blood thirsty monster, he uses her as a slave for all manner of sexual fantasies. It's blatantly clear he has no regard for her as his childe, and it's entirely possible that he plans to drive her into a draugr state and then cast her aside. But what is she supposed to do? Alexis knew nothing of this world when she was mortal, and her sire hasn't exactly been the best teacher, forcing her to learn about vampire society through personal experience and second hand mentoring by other vampires. If it weren't for the fact she knows her clan lineage, she might be considered a caitiff!


                            As it currently stands, Alexis has neither the knowledge, nor the courage to abandon the Sabbat. Alexis has seen more than enough evidence to know that attempting to run would result in a painful death. And as if that wasn't bad enough, being forced to partake in the Vaulderie allows Richard and his friends to toy with her emotions, making it even more difficult to resist giving into their demands. Her only hope is that somebody arrives to show her a better way, hopefully before she's too far gone to be recognizable as anything other than another shovel head.

                                        Here's one with which I came up just last night, which I plan to use as one of the PC's fellow city-dwellers when they move into Kronstadt in my TC game. Details omitted because I haven't thought that far ahead yet:

                                        In the city, there is a particularly noteworthy brothel. Noteworthy because all the women there are clean, exceptionally beautiful, devoted to their matron with a near-religious zeal, and never seem to age. Needless to say, the matron is a vampire, equally exceptionally beautiful, but also quite mad. No one is quite sure what clan she is -- she has the disposition of a Malkavian, but the refinement and taste for beauty of a Toreador.

                                        She is obsessed with...babies. She pushes her prostitutes (her herd) to get pregnant, as often as they can, forces them to carry to term, and midwifes for them. After they give birth, she takes them as her own, fawning over them by night in her haven which doubles as a creche, as a chosen ghoul runs the brothel itself. The only time she leaves her brothel is to buy, or even steal, others' babies according to her own inscrutable standards. She even goes so far as to wet nurse her babies, feeding them drops of blood from her own breast but never enough to lock them into the stasis of ghouldom (i.e she stops short of feeding them a full blood point at a time), and even apply the Kiss (but not feed) to quiet upset babies.

                                        Of course, once they grow past infancy, she quickly loses interest in most and shoves them out the door to be cared by the prostitutes or whoever will take them. Those she keeps, continue feeding at her breast until adulthood, and go on to be the next generation of exceptionally-beautiful, long-lived prostitutes.

                                        Because she's a Zantovich, and and out raw insanity or misguidance is breeding her own family of revenants (albeit not doing it very well, as her methods don't include inbreeding). She fleshcrafts the dogshit out of the babies, and keep them blood bound, as they grow into adulthood and beyond. Likewise, she fleshcrafts her prostitutes regularly to prevent them from becoming physically marred from repeated childbirth. Her "babies" are her territory, and she is obsessed with them and their welfare...at least, until they stop being babies.

                                                #62 David Dawson is a practical man raised by practical people in the very practical town of Pottstown, PA. His parents are retired, a general contractor and a home ec teacher. After high school, he earned a bachelor's of science degree in Engineering Technology in Construction Management Technology from Temple University. He married his high school sweetheart, Aayisha, who had gotten an accounting degree from Temple.

                                                He used his degree to help run his father's growing contracting and construction business. Aayisha kept the books. His three brothers, an electrician, a plumber and an HVAC guy, all worked at the business. Aayisha and David had three kids: David, Jr., Alana, and DeShawn. In the twenty years, since graduation the business grew. They mostly did re-models on older homes and re-purposing of large, old homes into commercial offices. They did some new construction, too, like the big apartment complex over by King of Prussia. David was bidding on a strip mall rehab project in Norristown, when he met his sire.

                                                More exactly, David was standing by the side of his truck just before dawn, eating a Wawa breakfast sandwich and waiting for the client, when his sire pounced upon him. She had destroyed a Sabbat pack in direct combat inside the abandoned strip mall, and she was badly wounded, low on blood and nearly a goner. She found David, dragged him back to her haven, a 1960s era bomb shelter in the midst of suburban tract homes. She closed the hatch moments before dawn. She pounced on David in a desperate bid to avoid torpor, and drained him to a husk. Then, realizing she may have been trailed, she fed him her blood and hid in her usual crypt, an old 300 gallon water tank. As she locked the hatch, she saw David rouse. Anyone who disturbed her rest tonight would find a frenzying young vampire first.

                                                He strained against the zip ties she had bound him with and began screaming, then frenzying. He bit wildly at the air. Any who entered would surely have been destroyed. Then, he slept his first day.

                                                That night she arose to find David in full frenzy, starved for blood and slamming against the walls. She wrapped him in the tarps she usually used to carry bodies, threw him in the back of her van and started driving up and down alleys until she found what she needed. It was in the back of a row-home fronting on Lafayette Street where she saw a Labrador Retriever doing it's business in the yard. She rolled down the window, whistled and the dog jumped in the driver's side window. She pulled the tarp off David's face, and he fed for the first time.

                                                That took the edge off. He felt the van jostle and bounce on rough roads and heard the river nearby. She pulled into a gravel parking lot and explained that they were on an island in the middle of the Schuylkill River, used for power transfer stations. She told him, "You're a vampire. Feed on animals, not people. Ever. Don't tell anyone what you are. Ever. The Sabbat are a vampire cult. If you meet one, run like hell. Don't make other vampires. Ever. Respect your elders. Don't trespass on another vampire's turf. Ever. The Camarilla are the good guys. Forget what I look like. Good luck, you unlucky son of a... "

                                                She cut the tarp open and kicked him out of the side of the van and the final words were drowned out by the sound of gravel spitting from under her tires. He saw her toss the dog out the window into the river.

                                                He doesn't remember how he made it home to Pottstown or the next few nights. Aayisha helped him figure out most of his condition, He learned how to flush his skin and look human, he learned how to increase his strength and speed. He was feeding on cows from the dairy farms a few miles away. He accidentally ghouled Aayisha during sex, but after some testing they realized she hadn't caught the full vampire virus.

                                                After a few weeks, a press gang showed up at his door, demanding he appear before the Dux Bellorum of the Five Counties, who held court at a State Police Barracks in West Philadelphia. David only had one question for them, "Are you Camarilla or Sabbat?" The Dux Bellorum was impressed that a seeming Caitiff knew that much, in addition to the rudiments of the Traditions. David's company has been working for them ever since. No one respects him, of course, or invites him to the parties, but he was used to that.

                                                On the other hand, Dawson and Sons has more business than they know what to do with. They build havens and fortified meeting spaces. They light-proof safe-houses and concrete over Sabbat Gangrel while they sleep in the soil of abandoned lots. Mostly, though, they provide practical services, like unstopping the plumbing at a mass house for the herd, or rewiring an interrogation chamber. It's been five years, now, and the Camarilla is close to a break through (they always are...) and David has been quietly instrumental.

                                                David Dawson is still a practical man doing practical work.

                                                        #63 Egidio Giovanni: Life in the mob is never easy, especially not when you're part of the family and expected to make them proud from day one. Fortunately, Egidio seemed to have the knack for doing just that, and once he was old enough to enter the family business, he began to establish legitimate business fronts for the Giovanni family. Businesses that allowed them to make large sums of cash while they secretly worked their occult rituals away from the public eye. Yeah, Egidio was related to those Giovanni, the necromantic vampires who spend their nights raising corpses and fucking their cousins!

                                                        Perhaps if things had gone differently, Egidio would have been accepted into the inner circle, receiving The Kiss and becoming a true vampire. But tragedy struck when the family did a little digging into his geneology reports. Egidio was not a Giovanni by blood, his mother had an affair, making him a bastard. Well, needless to say, the Giovanni did not like that, and decided to take action against them.

                                                        His mother, they simply killed in her own home, but Egidio (who had seen more than enough of what went on behind the scenes), they decided to make an example of. They dragged him down to one of their underground cemetaries, filled with reanimated corpses, and tossed him into a dirt filled pit to be eaten alive. Perhaps he would have been, if the Giovanni had tighter security measures in place. Something had managed to infiltrate their cemetary, something that bore a deep grudge against the blood drinking necromancers living just a few floors above them.

                                                        Seeing a potential ally in Egidio, the creature lunged at him before the shambling corpses could notice his presence, draining him of his life's blood and then reciprocating the act. With the now limp body of Egidio slumped over its shoulder, it slipped through its secret escape tunnel, the Giovanni none the wiser.

                                                        Egidio awoke the next night to find himself in a terrible state of decomposition, he had been embraced by one of the Samedi, a bloodline of zombie-like vampires hailing from the Carribean. His sire's rotting form had allowed them to blend seamlessly with the rest of the shambling dead. The Giovanni had a spy within their organization and never even noticed!

                                                        Egidio was pissed at his sire for what he did to him, but he was even more enraged at the Giovanni who would cast him aside for the simple reason that he wasn't a blood born Giovanni like the rest of them. He had given 30 years of hard work and dedicated service to their operations! Reluctantly, he agreed to partner with his sire and use what resources he could to bring them down. Most of his assets were seized by the Giovanni with his presumed death, but he still managed to scrounge together the contacts to establish a shipping operation, smuggling things in and out of the country. His hope is that he can eventually smuggle enough Samedi into the country to battle the Giovanni in force, but such an operation takes time and precision to carry out successfully.

                                                        He may not be privy to the secrets of Giovanni Necromancy, but he finds the art of Thanatosis to be a satisfying replacement!


                                                        Perhaps the worst thing out of all this, is that Egidio has no idea their sire is his biological father, having received the embrace shortly after his conception and forced to sever contact with his mother. If his sire is aware of their familial bond, they have chosen to remain secretive about it, probably for good reason.

                                                            #64 Once upon a time, in a faraway land called Dolancourt, there was a little girl named Noëlle, who lived on a farm at the edge of a deep, dark forest. Noëlle's father had hair the color of midnight and her mother had hair the color of noon. So, naturally, Noëlle's hair was redder than the sunset.

                                                            Noëlle's father and mother had many students they taught to sing and play the piano. Every morning Noëlle was awakened by her parent's music and every night they sang her a lullaby.

                                                            Noëlle had two problems. First, she was very scared of the deep, dark forest where the villagers all said an evil witch lived, who ate children. Second, the adults were fighting a war, and everyone was very scared. Because of the war, three of her mother's students came to live with them. Noëlle's mother explained that these boys were now her brothers, and if anyone asked, Noëlle should say they always had been.

                                                            One day, when Noëlle and her mother were in the barn, soldiers came to their farm and pounded on the door of the house. Noëlle's mother became very scared. She gave Noëlle a cabbage, a barn jacket and a bucket of water and told her to run, as fast as she could into the deep dark woods. Noëlle refused at first, of course, and cried and begged her mother not to make her go there alone, for the woods were very large and very dark. But, her mother put her hand over Noëlle's mouth and held her close. She said, "Now, now. Do as I say child. Run as fast as you can. Go through the woods, to the village on the other side. Tell the preacher at the church what happened. If you get scared, just listen, and you will hear me singing to you. And always know that I love you."

                                                            And her mother began to sing softly as she shoved Noëlle out the back door of the barn: "Au clair de la lune, Mon ami Pierrot, Prête-moi ta plume, Pour écrire un mot. Ma chandelle est morte, Je n'ai plus de feu. Ouvre-moi ta porte. Pour l'amour de Dieu..."

                                                            Noëlle ran as fast as she could, and ran and ran and ran, all day until it was night and she grew very tired. Late in the night, she woke, thinking she heard her mother singing, and climbed over a rise to see a tall white tower, glowing in the moonlight. Noëlle began singing her mother's lullaby, hoping she would be found. "...Ouvre-moi ta porte...", she sang and the door on the tower opened. An old, old woman beckoned Noëlle to the tower and took her inside.

                                                            Noëlle was very scared at first, but the old woman was not a witch. For many years, Noëlle stayed with her, the only food she had, the thick broth the woman gave her. The woman was strange, she slept all day and was gone all night, but Noëlle and the old woman came to love one another very much. They sang beautiful songs together and played many games with people they found in the woods.

                                                            One night, the old woman asked Noëlle if she would like to hear music all the time. Noëlle, thinking of her mother's promise, said yes, very much. And so, the old woman held her tight, and for a few moments, she saw her parents and they said they loved her very much. As they faded away, and Noëlle awoke, they were singing that lullaby. And they have never stopped singing since.

                                                            Noëlle hunted alongside the old woman for many years. First they hunted bad men like the ones that came to Noëlle's house. Then hunters and hikers and lost travellers. It was easy because if they wanted to, they could make themselves very beautiful and could use their songs to lead the men astray.

                                                            Finally, after many more years, the old woman said she was growing weak and needed to sleep. Noëlle, of course, begged her not to, but the woman said she had no choice. So she climbed into the cellar of the tower and went to sleep, leaving Noëlle with no one for company, except the lullaby she always hears.

                                                            Sometimes, Noëlle gets lonely in the woods. She sneaks into town and hears little children being told the story of the witches of the woods. It's about an old woman and a little girl who will eat you if you go into the woods. Noëlle wishes with all her heart that the story weren't only half-true.
