    Publications for Baroque VtM

    05-10-2021, 12:27 PM
    Greetings,

    are there any publications for playing VtM (regardless of edition) during the Early Modern period? I'm looking especially for the period that falls under the term Baroque/Rococo/Absolutism etc. (depending on your preferred terminological framework - I'm aware that they don't mean exactly the same, but I hope that my question is clear), so especially 17th century and early 18th century.

    I'd really like to play a V5 present-day campaign with older vampires, where I make excessive use of the Memoriam and where we play nearly 1/3 of the time during the Baroque time period with a plot revolving around J.S. Bach.

    I will probably make up most of the stuff but I'd really like to know, which hitherto publications touch the time period. It seemed like there was some big gap between V: DA and the Victorian Era.

    Thank you all in advance.

                              You might get some additional data by reading the history of individual characters found in various books. They often refer to historical events even though that time period is not really emphasized. Chicago By Night has several characters who lived during the time period you wish. Annabelle Treabelle was born in 1698 and embraced in 1722, and was born to a musician and Parisian prostitute. The Sabbat Phillipe Rigaud was born 1650 and embraced in 1673 was a French soldier w ho participated in Louis XIV's invasion of Holland in the Franco-Dutch War. We know the Brujah elder Critias was active in Europe doing this time, but probably spent most of his time then at a Swiss monastery.

                              The Nosferatu Alexander Danov was embraced possibly around the time of the Battle of Tannenberg in 1410, though he did not pick up his current name until the mid to late 19th century in Russia far after your time period. We know the ancient Gangrel Lucian must have existed during that time, but his history is an entirely blank slate during the period.

                              Whether any of these characters would play a role in your chronicle, is complete personal choice. But they could definitely be used or name dropped during your chronicle. Is this kind of thing helpful to you? I have no idea.

                              But the details are there provided you want to read up on a lot of old By Night books, or any of the other supplements that had lots of NPCs in them. There's a significant number of elders who were either embraced during that time period, or were already elders by that era. Berlin By Night, while a rather terrible sourcebook over all, does have some native German vampires who would have existed during that time period (and lots with no association with Germany or way after that time period). After all, J.S. Bach visited the court of Frederick the Great.

                                  For Deutschland and Osterreich during the ancient regime and the Holy Roman Empire, I would recommend reading the books of Feder & Schwert. They are not entirely canon, but some of their characters are, as the Vienna governors of the Clan Tremere and the Justicar Ulugh Begh. I would also recommend you Berlin by night (although many consider it the worst of the 15 by nights). Gustav Breidenstein was a powerful Prince and an important political player. Finally, I could imagine Hardestadt reverenced in the Holy Roman Empire as the ruler he was during the Black Cross Fiefdoms era.

                                          Yeah, Berlin by Night was ... "a terrible waste of potential" probably sums it up best. Berlin in the early 90s was a fantastic place to set a Vampire game, with neonates being influenced by the various music scenes in both parts of the city, and memories of life under the Stasi, and ancilla being from the Weimar and Imperial eras (and even a few who remember the last days of the Holy Roman Empire), and elders who remember the Teutonic Knights or even Charlemagne or Rome. Instead it was this crazy alternate dimension where WW2 was the only thing of any historical importance which occurred in the city. And vampire snakes.
