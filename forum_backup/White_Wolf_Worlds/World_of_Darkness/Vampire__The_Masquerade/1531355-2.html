                    Basically being Vampire Russian Imperial Movement or Comintern to contrast to the Sabbats vampire ISIS.

                    Stop being rebels without a clue, and start being revolutionaries with bite, leave the wannabe gangsters, liberals with fangs and protestors as scapegoats, recruit any enraged survivors and remember, throwing bombs killed kings, it still works on Princes.
                    That's good for Anarchs, but...

                    That's suffering from a general 'fighting the last war's fight' error.

                    Problem is if that's the only strat the Anarchs have, they will fail due to it.

                    The Sabbat are better at infiltration and can seduce Elders due to offering a cure to degrading Humanitas(the Paths) and or more loyal and unified in vision than the Anarchs.

                    Packs of shovelheads are more disposable and plentiful than Anarch gangs. Sabbat also has more consistent means of dealing with internal strife.

Now the Cam Elders will be leveraging the police and FBI to hunt down not just the perps, but also all the people associated with them. Shouldn't be too hard if they can access the ability to track cell phone use.

                    And even if the local Elders cant flex the FBI, they will respond in kind: hire private mercs to infiltrate the Anarchs. Set a trap: hey Elders are gonna pinch the resources of the Anarch to anger them, then Cam will host one of their traditional events and allow the Anarchs to bomb that one as well.                     Then, and decent Elder of the Cam won't kill the Anarch leaders outright, thats boring. They will catch each leader, Dominate/Entrance/Torture/Bond/use Thaumaturgy/or offer to turn coat, and release them back to the Anarchs to help it crumble from within. This not only defeats the Anarchs but discredits them as well. The Revolution(tm) turned out to be some campus ruffians who were mad they didn't understand power. Kids these days...

                    And Anarchs are initially tied to their human connections moreso than Cam. Mainly due to recruiting.

                    Plus, from a V5 narrative, are the Anarchs actually winning??? They are gaining turf, yes.

                    But,

                    Any good investor know when an investment t no longer runs or grows, it consolidates.

                    There would be no move more Camarilla than giving up turf to the Anarchs just to let them ruin it and draw attention of human organizations as well as the Sabbat to themselves and away from the Cam. A Buffer State. The Cam can take it back in a few decades after the 'HUD Scam' runs it's lifespan and there is opportunity to expand power again for cheap.

                                            Well the thing is that the Anarchs are intended for very small-scale stories. The player character gets abused by his sire, and joins up with other player characters who were abused by their sires, and they rebel against that tyranny. If you make them any bigger than that, they kind of break in terms of narrative purpose, because they then become just another version of The Man. 5th Edition doesn't seem to fully understand this, and so attempts to make them a sect, but almost immediately runs into the problem of trying to avoid making them The Man in the process. It tries to thread an impossibly tiny needle, and fails. It's like making Captain America President and then trying, somehow, to keep him as the antiestablishment guy fighting for freedom.

                                            I find it funny on a certain level; they knew they wanted the player characters to be rebels, which lead them to making Anarchs the default, but then immediately ran into the problem that if Anarchs are the default, then Anarchs become The Man in any story that lasts longer than four sessions, and therefore needs to flesh societal and bureaucratic issues out.
