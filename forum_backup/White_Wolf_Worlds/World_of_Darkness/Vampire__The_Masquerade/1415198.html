      Nope. And I wouldn't bring em up if other people didn't. I like that you're not supposed to know.

      I played one game where the ST didn't understand how rare 5th gens were supposed to be and had him as the city's Brujah sheriff (The prince was only 8th or 7th gen). That was terrifying and... Anti-climactic. I had earned his Ire and we had engage in a game of Cat and Mouse with me and The Boys taking out his supporters while doing our best to avoid him and find a weakness or a valid trap or equipment to do such a thing. We discovered the old prince had an old flintlock pistol loaded with silver specially prepared for the methuselah; then a new character completely unrelated to our shenanigans snipes the sheriff in her first game. There was tension in that he didn't die in one hit and rage-frenzied towards the shooter down the streets before getting shot again and meeting final death, but ultimately it was an assamite hit of little relation to us.

                      Never an Antedeluvian (at least not since I was a dumbass kid in the '90s), but I'll make rare use of a Methuselah. I prefer to use them as an impending sense of dread, rather than something to be directly interacted with.

                      In a Dark Ages game I dropped conflicting rumors for months to the players that an Antedeluvian or Methuselah was entombed somewhere in the city. Threw in some random craziness with a few groups of mortals getting up and chanting "The Child of Dionysus awakens" and having no memory of it after the fact. Then some Assamite the group was pursuing busted into the tomb and got killed immediately. The Methuselah gave them some prophecy and pointed the group to a prophetic stele and tablet in the back of the tomb. I had a laugh when the Salubri warrior in the group turned back to ask her a question after inspecting the stele and the Methuselah was just gone. The players just had this look of dread and one of them said "I don't know whether a crazy ancient Malkavian running around is a good thing or not?" She basically told the group, get out of town before it's destroyed, but behind the scenes was starting to drive the Prince crazy into making bad decisions that would lead to it's destruction.

                                      It depends on the chronicle. I completed a Chronicle of Ages as ST and I had antediluvians dying by the end of it. Methuselahs did appear even from early on as NPCs.

                                      Generally speaking Antes and Meths need to be used with care in any given chronicle. If you want to tell a story about the plight of the common fledgling having meths running around in the open is probably going to be counterproductive, if you are telling a story about the unspeakable secrets behind the conception of the race of Caine, meths and possibly even Antes are narrative tools you shoudl seriously consider including.
