                I’m also going to mention some sorcerers here. To me, using mages or sorcerers doesn’t really matter to the setting or the PCs in a Vampire game. They serve the same narrative focus. Personally, using the original WoD: Sorcerer sourcebook is actually a lot easier for me than Mage.

                I’m mostly leaving what sorcerers will be present as pretty open. I can plug more in as needed. However, in terms of NPCs planned in advance, I will be putting a member (perhaps two in case the main one needs an assistant) of the Nephite Priesthood here.

                Why? Because there are actually a lot of Mormons in southern California (I think about 80,000 in LA metro although it’s over 700k in California as a whole in the early nineties). Mormon involvement in California is quite old, and there’s been a Los Angeles Branch since 1895. There’s also significant Spanish speaking Mormons in LA because of Mormon missionary activities in Latin America and immigration to the US. Having a member of the Nephite Priesthood can provide a little extra kick to Mormon vampire hunters just like the Society of Leopold does for Catholic vampire hunters. Why shouldn't they have their own Van Helsing? They might never show up in the chronicle, but I like to think ahead in terms of potential hunters. They'll only come into play if one of the PCs for whatever reason targets someone in the Mormon population. The Los Angeles Mormon temple is located in the Westwood neighborhood which I believe is in Louis Fortier's barony. I don't know any of the local demographics, but I assume it's fairly spread out with some local clumping like with most religious denominations.

                There is also the Thal’hun based out of San Francisco. I have an odd liking of them because they show that Paths of Sorcery don't need to be "magic" per se. They are just collection of mechanical effects that can utilize a lot of different flavor text. Their fringe pseudo-science of vibrational resonances creating effects (their Paths) isn’t quite what people expect from “magic”, but it might be something explored by occult, pseudo-scientific, or perhaps even musical oriented characters. Easy enough to have a small group of Thal’hun in LA itself. Very unlikely any vampire PC will come across this group, but sandbox elements like this can present STs options based on PC actions. Most likely, it’ll just be some Easter egg type stuff hidden under normal California weirdness. Any PCs who run into them will need a very compelling background that adequately provides a good reason why they might encounter them.

                If I'll introduce anyone other than this, it'll probably a group from the Ancient Order of the Aeon Rites because of the all Thelemite activity that's been done in LA since at least the twenties. Any other occult groups will probably just be fronts for other supernatural groups.

                        There's a distinction between Mormon vampire hunters (who could be anyone) and the "sorcerers" of the Nephite Priesthood. That group of sorcerers is based on a "secret" book of Nephi that Joseph Smith did not include in the Book of Mormon because it was not meant for public knowledge. Instead, the now celestial being Nephi revealed himself to the Mormon Uriah Spence in 1849 and restored the lost "Nephite Priesthood" who can unleash certain Paths of Sorcery as miracles they can call upon God through prayer. Although I don't think it is stated so in WoD; Sorcerer, I believe any such Nephite Priests must be male because the Church of Latter Day Saints does not ordain female priests. (Other STs, of course, can do whatever they want.)

                        The Nephite Priests mostly wander the Mormon Corridor, but can go anywhere where they feel they are needed. California is outside the Corridor, but has a significant population in numbers so some Nephite Priests probably frequently visit there. So if a PC vampire happens to prey on the local Mormon population, I would not have one show up at first. He should be the equivalent of summoning up the heavy supernatural artillery when everything else doesn't work (like Father Merrin in The Exorcist). There's no reason why one of the priesthood can't have more experience in killing vampires than others, the equivalent of someone like Sullivan Dane or Caiaphas Smith.

                        I have long thought the Nephite Priesthood is one of the more interesting groups in WoD: Sorcerer, and they're an element you can really only use in certain locales. Although I doubt this will actually come up in play, I like having it in my back pocket for LA. Depending on how things go, maybe I'll even make some future member of someone's Herd or other background, or a rival to someone's Influence be a Mormon just to see if it can build up to something where this guy appears.

                                Originally posted by No One of Consequence View Post
                                I sometimes wonder if any of the older Thal'hun members were involved with Jack Parsons and his occult groups.
                                Per WoD: Sorcerer, the Thal'hun post-date Jack Parsons quite a bit (he died in 1952), and their brand of fringe pseudo-science does not match Parson's Thelemite style occultism.

                                As written, the Jeva prophet Khuvon incarnated in 1961, taught his disciples for five years before being "freed from his mortal shell", and afterwards his seven disciples formed the Star Council of Zoraster to carry on his work. "Jeva" were the 300 members of the extraterrestrial race Hui:xa who phased into a different space-time continuum and became beings of pure energy so they could teach other planets enlightenment and the science of thal.



                                Reading between the lines, someone had a psychotic break in 1961, called himself Khuvon, proclaimed a space alien philosophy to some friends, and died in 1966.

                                Thal'hun seems like Scientology by way of Richard Shaver combined with UFO religions like Raelism. It fits well with the California New Age movement from the sixties and seventies.

                                There's probably an actual new religious movement associated with it, and only a small handful of people who could be counted as "sorcerers" themselves. WoD: Sorcerer says there are 55 (as of 1997) people in the Thal'hun society itself. I would say those 55 are people in various stages of actually to master "thal" (not all actual "sorcerers", those who can actually "set a resonance" are probably quite small, maybe 1-2 dozen. However, it would make sense their beliefs are held by a few hundred or thousand people worldwide (they have a newsletter!). Their headquarters is in a mansion in San Francisco, not far from Golden Gate Park.

                                As crackpot "religions" / pseudo-science go, it's not a bad one to use in the LA Free States. However, there is probably even less of a chance the PCs will run into them than they will the Mormon Van Helsing. I'll probably name drop them though as just another weird California thing.

                                            The Arcanum sourcebook mentions that the San Francisco chapterhouse was opened back in 1910. Even if a chapterhouse is located in San Francisco instead of LA, that is still close enough for the ST to run any Arcanum centered plots and have any Arcanum scholars in LA be heavily supported by that organization. (And it might provide a reason for PCs to visit SF and thus the Vampire Club.)

                                            One of my great personal frustrations with the Vampire setting is that while the Arcanum always seemed liked it would be an important part of Vampire chronicles, that never seemed to occur. The close relationship between certain members of the Talamasca and vampires in Anne Rice novels never translated to the World of Darkness. Perhaps the more lax approach to the Masquerade in LA will provide more opportunities for an Arcanum scholar or investigator to attach to the PCs?

                                            There just happens to be a current thread on the Arcanum in the general World of Darkness forum and using them in a chronicle so I'll link that here. General comments on the Arcanum can easily go there, but if people have specific comments on the San Francisco chapterhouse or using them in an LA Free State chronicle, they'd be welcome here.

                                            I want to have at least one Arcanum scholar (and perhaps multiple mortal informants/investigators utilized by the Arcanum who aren't aware of the reasons/purpose they are paid) in Los Angeles. I haven't decided how integrated/knowledgeable that scholar is with the Kindred of LA. However, I've been wanting to include the Arcanum heavily in a Vampire chronicle for a long time, and it is probably now or never. Maybe give that scholar a slight edge like some form of psychic numina like Clairvoyance, Precognition, or Psychometry. Something that could justify the scholar being able to make contact with one of the PCs. Not any highly developed Psychic Power, just enough to provide an edge and justify contact.
