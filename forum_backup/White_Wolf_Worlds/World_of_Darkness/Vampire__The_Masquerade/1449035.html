    Help me decide on the clan, concept and theme!

    05-19-2021, 03:56 PM
    Hello everyone.

    So I'm joining a chronicle set in the Victorian Age in the the nordic regions, and the game rules used are the V20, V20DA, clanbooks and other revised material, but we're using the character creation model from V20DA, alongside this I have around 40 xp to develop the character further, and age-wise it's around the 100 year mark. The chronicle is Camarilla centric, though independent clans are also open.

    My problem is that I can't really decide on the clan, concept and theme because there is simply too many good and interesting ones to choose from, so to help me narrow down my choices I took a look at what disciplines I would like the character to based around and which supporting discipline I would like, furthermore I also wanted a fourth in-clan discipline through the merit additional discipline, which of course should be explainable/justifiable somehow. Currently I would like the concept and theme to be more centric to being a magus of a sort
    Though, it's of importance to note that I would it like to preferably be having a norse/nordic angle to it. I'm open to other compelling suggestions, but do bear in mind that it does have to fit with the chronicle taking place in a nordic region. To help you help me, I've assembled most of my work related to figuring out my options and the potential combinations, where disciplines on the list, are simply the ones I know I enjoy playing with and thus would prefer to have a character with some of these.

    Discipline Wishes + Clan Possibilities
    Animalism : Gangrel | Nosferatu | Ravnos | Tzimisce)
    Chimerstry : Ravnos | Nephilim
    Dominate : Giovanni | Lasombra | Tremere | Ventrue | Malkavian (Dominate)
    Necromancy : Giovanni
    Obfuscate : Assamite: Warrior | Follower of Set | Malkavian | Nosferatu | Gangrel(City/Greek)
    Potence : Brujah | Giovanni | Lasombra, Nosferatu
    Presence : Assamite: Vizier | Brujah | Follower of Set | Toreador | Ventrue
    Thaumaturgy: Assamite: Sorcerer | Tremere
    (Blood Sorcery Equivalent)

    Quietus isn't on the list, but it's one of those that could be nice to have, I make note of it here since Assamite is on the list above, and could have a bearing on the final decision.
    Considerations with regards to the most common ones which aren't on the list: Celerity and Fortitude, these aren't completely out of the question, if the concept and theme would benefit from these as in-clan rather than and out of clan, then I would be open to consider these as well.
    Obtenebration, was also one actually considered, but since the chronicle already has several users of this one, I thought I'd skip it.

    With that being said, I know that I really would like the character to have Thaumaturgy or an equivalent of such and Necromancy, but this means that it's either Giovanni, Cappadocian, Tremere, Assamite: Sorcerer if which ever of two discplines missing is added through the additional discipline merit. Of these, the "strongest" candidate by far would be Giovanni, as it already has three of the ones thats on the list, but I don't really know if I want to be playing a Giovanni for this chronicle, especially because I'm not sure if and how I can make the norse/nordic theme fit with the lore of the Giovanni.

    If we're looking at which has just at least three of the ones as in-clan disciplines including if added through the additional discipline merit, but not necessarily a blood sorcery discipline as a clan discipline to start with. This broadens the list of potential clans to choose from significantly, at the sacrifice of only starting with one of the two blood sorcery disciplines I wanted, which also opens the question, if only one of these, then which one? Other supporting disciplines on the list, could be added as out of clan, though it would make most sense if it was non-signature clan disciplines.

    As far as I can tell, this means that the following clans and bloodlines with the discipline combinations would be:
    Brujah: Potence | Presence | Thaumaturgy or Necromancy.
    Lasombra Antitribu: Obtenebration | Dominate | Potence | Necromancy or Thaumaturgy - The chronicle already has several of these, so skipping this one, even though it could be cool.
    Malkavian (Dominate): Dominate | Obfuscate | Necromancy or Thaumaturgy
    Nosferatu: Animalism | Obfuscate | Potence | Necromancy or Thaumaturgy
    Ravnos: Animalism | Chimerstry | Fortitude | Necromancy or Thaumaturgy
    Nephilim: Auspex | Chimerstry | Presence | Necromancy or Thaumaturgy
    Ventrue: Dominate | Fortitude | Presence | Necromancy or Thaumaturgy

    The strongest one of the ones with direct access to a blood sorcery equivalent and their combination:
    Giovanni: Dominate | Potence | Necromancy | Thaumaturgy
    Tremere: Auspex | Dominate | Thaumaturgy | Necromancy
    Cappadocian: Auspex | Fortitude | Necromancy (Mortis) | Thaumaturgy

    This one is potentially among the top picks, but depending on which edition I choose from, will change it's clan disciplines, and would most likely be subject to further justification.
    Assamite: Sorcerer:
    - VTM Assamite Sorcery | Obfuscate | Quietus | Necromancy
    - VTDA - Assamite Sorcery | Auspex | Quietus | Necromancy or other from list
    - V20DA - Assamite Sorcery | Auspex | Celerity | Necromancy or other from list

    Technically, Caitiff or bloodline progenitor could also be an option, although I don't want to just be playing a nice set of disciplines and abilities, but actually be playing a character with depth, meaning and importance to the game and character story.

    As mentioned before, I'm open to compelling suggestions, I'm certainly not against playing a character with only access to one blood sorcery equivalent, but should be fairly centric to the theme. As you probably can tell by now, there are so many options available, hence why I need a bit of help deciding. And of course, feel free to use this for when figuring out potential character concepts based around disciplines.

    Some of the concepts I've been working with, as a one line inspiration:

    Concept #1 - The Nordic Magus (A viking mage equivalent)
    Concept #2 - Bloodline Experimentation
    Concept #3 - Master of Illusion and Manipulation
    Concept #4 - Master of Ravens and the Dead
    Concept #5 - Priest/Crusader of Corruption
    Concept #6 - The Burned Nosferatu
    Concept #7 - The Highwayman
    Concept #8 - The Twisted Ventrue

    Thanks for reading, and if you decide to to pitch in and help, thank you, it's much appreciated.

    // Inc.

                      Ravnos.

                      The clan traditionally divides itself into a number of lineages known as Jati (an Indian term that refers to lineage, kinship group and caste). One of the ones I use in my own games, the Seidr, originate from the Nordic and German areas and are based around the cultural concepts of female seers/wisewomen (volva/spakona) and witchcraft (Seidr). They're descended from the original Indian Brahman caste bloodline (what the Roma Ravnos would call Phuri Dae, but I just call Seers) and so have Auspex as a clan discipline in place of Fortitude. This plays into both the traditional Germanic folklore about seers as well as the powers/magics traditionally associated with Seidr in Norse folklore/mythology (illusions, mind reading, astral projection, and being able to project ones consciousness into animals). This can be augmented by vampire runic blood magic (described in the Dark Ages book Wolves of the Sea; it used mortal Sorcery paths IIRC, but I just adapt Thaumaturgy for it, using paths like Spirit Manipulation, Warding, and Weather Control).
                      (While Seidr is traditionally a woman's art, it is practiced by men, and while my Jati is majority female, it's not gender exclusive or anything.)


                      (rough draft with no XP spent)
                      Concept: Nordic Seidr Witch
                      Clan: Ravnos (Seer bloodline)

                      Physical: 2/2/2 Social: 3/4/3 Mental 3/2/3
                      Talents: Alertness 1, Awareness 2, Empathy 2, Intimidation 2, Subterfuge 2
                      Skills: Animal Ken 2, Crafts 2, Etiquette 1, Larceny 2, Melee 1, Performance 2, Stealth 2, Survival 1
                      Knowledge: Investigation 1, Medicine 2, Occult 4 (Nordic witchcraft)

                      Disciplines: Animalism 1, Auspex 1, Chimerstry 1, +Runic Blood Magic (Path of Spirit Manipulation) 1
                      Backgrounds: Herd 2, Influence 2, Retainers 3 (coven)

                      Conscience 2 Self-Control 4 Courage 4
                      Humanity 6

                                  Last I spoke with the STs, we were discussing a potential Assamite angle, but he weren't too keen on an Assamite sorcerer, because it wouldn't be very likely for an Assamite sorcerer to be in Scandinavian lands, as in it wouldn't have very plausible reasons for being there.                                           Keep in mind, the Giovanni do not restrict their overall membership solely to those belonging to their original bloodline. The Giovanni are always on the lookout for other groups having something to offer their clan. Thus we have the lesser Giovanni bloodlines such as the Scottish Dunsirns, the Mexican Pisonab line and the Miliners of the United States - just to name a few. A little research into the Nordic mythologies reveals the death cult of Hel, who await the construction of Naglfar, a boat made entirely from the fingernails and toenails of the dead which Hel shall use upon it's completion in the Final Nights to ferry her army of wraiths.

                                          Sounds very close to the Giovanni goal of collecting enough souls to bring about the Endless Night. The Giovanni would likely be quite eager to incorporate such a labor towards the advancement of their own agenda. Not to mention the prospect of adding into their necromantic repertoire a possible new path centered around the fabrication of powerful items from the bodies of the dead.
