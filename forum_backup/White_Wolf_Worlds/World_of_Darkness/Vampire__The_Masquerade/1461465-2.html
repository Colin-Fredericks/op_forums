                Parovich wasn't an infiltrator. He was a traitor. His backstory had him as a close friend of the Prince of New York who was there when the Sabbat killed him, and Parovich was allowed to defect instead of being killed. He was working for them as a double agent, with his loyalty secured through the vinculum (not that this term was ever mentioned). Of course, the Sabbat simply planned on killing Parovich after they took Milwaukee.

                And why wouldn't they? After he served their purpose they would naturally dispose of him. Nobody is going to set up a traitor as one of their leaders. He was never really going to be an archbishop. Parovich was stupid for believing they'd let him live, but the nature of his first person backstory narration definitely made him seem to be somewhat crazy so it's not surprising he never figured it out. His reason for being Sabbat was simply because "they were going to win anyway". He was likely suffering from severe post-traumatic stress. The Sabbat here is just a big scary boogeyman.

                And Parovich wasn't sitting idle. He was trying to keep Milwaukee in continual political chaos so that the domain would be vulnerable to a Sabbat takeover. That was his mission.

                The problem, as I see it, is that there's enough little chinks in the story to not quite sit right. It's hard to see this crazy Nosferatu being a trusted voice on the council. And if Milwaukee By Night was written after the first Sabbat books, they'd probably present him differently to better reflect the Sabbat as it was developed. (Just like how the Sabbat in Blood Bond doesn't seem like Sabbat either - though we could retroactively make them a Nomad pack of Loyalists). In fact, while there are a lot of cool stuff in MbN, any ST running it after 1994 likely would need to change a lot of things to present it in better compliance with the setting. Not too different, but just enough to remove the inevitable "What?" from the players and improve the setting. That's why I'd be totally down with switching things up in terms of who Parovich is really loyal to. You'd have to make changes anyway since you can't run it out of the box.
