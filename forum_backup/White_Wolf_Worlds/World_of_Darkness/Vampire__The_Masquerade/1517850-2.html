                                    Originally posted by Lysander View Post
                                    I would recommend playing it as it has lots of little subplots that spur other adventures and the fallout of the Camarilla losing such a prized domain to the SI and the return of ancient blood god named Mithras who is back and is in the process of regaining his former power and purging himself of whatever remains of Monty Coven essence in there shared form. All in all I think it was a decent sourcebook, but I like Chicago by Night and Cult of the Blood Gods better myself.

                                    To be fair I use it as means to flesh out my Malkavian's backstory more as he is from London England from the Victorian Era and what kind of interactions he might have with the major Kindred powers there before he departed for the New World before the eve of the 20th century.
                                    If you are looking to flesh out that aspect of your character, i really suggest looking into the second and third parts of Giovanni Chronicles, Blood & Fire and the Sun Has Set, since considerable chunks of those adventures are set in London (in 1666 and 1848 respectively) and may provide you with a respectable number of NPCs, plotlines and loose threads to weave together with the threadbare basics offered by London by Night or the bits on the city in 1st & 2nd edition World of Darkness.

                                    (second part of the Sun Has Set takes the PCs to 1882 Egypt, what while quite a move can make for a fun flashpoint or sidetrek of sorts to any ancestors or past acquaintances of your Malkavian's backstory, be they directly connected to the search for the Sargon Fragment or not)

                                    Other alternate materials to possibly mine for NPCs and fluff tidbits would be Bloody Hearts: Diablerie Britain, Kindred Most Wanted, Dread Names: Red List and BJD, for the references to Tiamat and her descendants the methuselah Gotsdam and his childe the Anathema Dylan Bruce (plus BJD has a chapter on its own on London that can provide for some good complement and contrast to Fall of London).

                                        Speaking again of forgotten characters in previous books, London as described in World of Darkness and Giovanni Chronicles seems to have a history of "high-class diabolist cabals" in the form of the Crimson Bacchanal between 1660s to 1840s at least, that has counted kindred such as Mallotte, Duke & Duchess Amber (estranged childer of Mithras - supposedly*),​ twins Freia & Frigga (Sabbat infiltrators, childer of notorious insane vicissitude & brainwashing master known as the Sculptor) and Anne among its ranks through the ages, while in contemporary times (or at least the '90s) many were the tales of Count Zaroff's Hunt Club.

                                        Also the Foundlings coterie and specially Tobias with his ties to the leveler movement in the 1600s, if surviving the events of Blood & Fire, could evolve into a foundation for the Anarch movement or at least one of its factions within the city.

                                        [In fact when a friend STed the Sun has Set, along with the Ambers as allies/patrons of Lady Anne, "Mr. Leveler" also made a comeback as a 7th gen local anarch leader, somewhere between Juggler and Lenoir in behaviour, with the "Foundlings" as his extended coterie/anarch faction/pupils plus some stealth contacts among the Tremere through a few "strays" from failed intrigues on London he rescued through the ages, including acquaintance with Rose Chandler, that by 1848 also happened to be an archon and pupil of one of the PCs, for something of a subplot/monkeywrench in the main plot]

                                        Meanwhile the Sun has Set has a good part of its pagecount dedicated to a story arc in 1848 London that could serve as a very good companion piece for London by Night in scenery and cast, with a score of NPCs such as Toreador Hortense Holden, his childe Victoria Ash**, Gangrel Alice Seymour, the Dunlops, lady Astor of the Lasombra, Streck's pack and others i forget.


                                        *The Ambers embrace date of 701 would actually be right in the middle of one of Mithras torpor periods, meaning they can't be his childer (unless he found some way to embrace kindred while asleep, who knows). Possible alternate explanations are their being childer of another Ventrue 4th gen (Tiamat?) that the Sun-God later brainwashed & adopted for reasons his own, OR they are the childer of one of his own estranged childer (like Cret or Verus) who joined his ranks and later rose to 5th gen through diablerie.

                                        **Not that one​, just an homonym - though considering they have the same name, clan and generation, not to mention the better known Victoria has a childe amidst London's court, making them into one and the same (falsifying her descendance for who knows what motives) would be all too simple. Or have them both for a bit of confusion, humor and petty rivalries. ​

                                                - Cretheus since the middle ages has become a leading member of the Inconnu.
