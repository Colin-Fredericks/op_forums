              Horrible is a simple term, but what makes one act more or less horrible is highly subjective.

              I had a Giovanni who was business focused, property ownership, business take overs, etc. One branch of his business was a human trafficking group specializing in children. Once the kids were "retirement age" of 12+ and were too mentally and emotionally broken, the Giovanni would sell them to Nagaraja as food or necromancy components. All in all it was a win/win/win, the Giovanni got multiple returns on investment, the Nagaraja get fed, and the kids get put out of their misery. This also secured a limited form of Necromantic knowledge trade.

              In a different game I had Follower of Set who turned the mortal daughter of a rival Ventrue into a ghoul warrior hell bent on exterminating her fathers bloodline. This was an ancient setting game, the Ventrue had earned his embrace during the 2nd Punic war. Two millennia later she was still butchering mortals descended from their family line, if anything the modern invention of genetic heritage companies like "23 and me" just gave her a longer kill list. And all of this was over the fact that the Follower of Set had lost a trade deal when Carthage fell.

              In a third game, we ran an all Assamite coterie with members specializing in different methods of death. My character was a sniper with knowledge of explosives, which meant the ST had a habit of trying to force face to face combat. This culminated in a game where our target was a high ranking Garou who ran the local werewolf pack. Rather than trying to fight them directly my sniper simply targeted the local kinfolk and would take out any pack member who was trying to protect them. After a month of this all the kinfolk were dead and most of the pack had either been killed or driven off. Eventually the pack leader went mad with grief and impotent rage, allowing for a much easier kill shot.

              So which one is the most horrible to your sensibilities? The kids who my character never directly interacted with, the vengeful ghoul who all I did was provide a story and some support to, or the sniper who personally butchered an entire community just to drive his true target out into the open?

                      Well, my Tremere in Transylvania Chronicles...

                      ...had an ongoing deal with Vykos (who was his one ally at the start of the chronicle, due to having known each other as mortal mages) to whack his Tzimisce rivals in exchange for him whacking my Tremere's rivals, sharing information and just committing general treason all over the place, because fuck each other's clans but especially the Tremere.

                      ...feeding the coterie's Gangrel a nonstop supply of gargoyles for about a century, mostly because it amused my Tremere to see what creative means he'd come up with to do it. Finally told the Gangrel to knock it off when the clan started getting suspicious as to why he needed a new gargoyle every few years.

                      ...performed a backyard abortion as part of an improvised ritual to appease Kupala (at least temporarily) during the 1314 story. It kinda worked.

                      ...had about this reaction to Dracula's "hundred orphans" stunt:


                      ...koldun genocide. Want to learn koldunism to openly mock the Tzimisce, but can't do it the easy way? Just diablerize enough to piece together memories of how to perform the correct pacts, and learn ways and rituals yourself.

                      ...the following exchange:

                      Me: "I tell my apprentice to do as I say, or I'm going to kill him."
                      ST: "Roll intimidation."
                      Me: "I'm not trying to intimidate him, I'm telling him flat out what the consequences of refusing to follow my orders will be. It's his choice."
                      ST: "Well he refuses, he thinks you're bluffing."
                      Me: "I hit him with LotF level 5. Six successes on my Willpower roll."
                      ST: "Shit, well, okay...he frenzies, runs away screaming, and dies."
                      Me: "I go to my next apprentice and tell him to do what the first was going to do, or I'm going to kill him."
                      ST: "You don't have to tell him, he's already on it."
                      ...then there was the minor case of mortal genocide, murdering almost the entirety of Transylvania's Saxon population (and destroying every Tremere chantry in Transylvania, except Ceoris for obvious reasons) in the course of about a week. It wasn't so much about the genocide as it was making a statement to the Tremere -- the genocide was more or less my Tremere's resignation letter and announcement he was joining Goratrix in exile. Got my Tremere on the Red List, but I got around it with even more planning than the genocide itself entailed.

                      ...cat eugenics. That really speaks for itself, but while my Tremere was laying low from having being put on the Red List, he got bored and spent a century or so learning Biothaumaturgy and adapting Sculpt the Perfect Servant for animals. Use the ritual on a pregnant cat, pick the best of the litter, use Biothaumaturgy on them to jack their attributes into the stratosphere while being able to do things like speak, breed them, wash, rinse, repeat.

                      The cat ghoul my Tremere ended up with was one unholy terror. The only PC with higher mental attributes was my Tremere, and that cat was pretty close to him. The little fucker figured out how to circumvent my Tremere's ritual protections over his library, had been raiding it while my Tremere was daysleeping, and learned thaumaturgy. My Tremere found it funny and endearing his little cat ran around around hunting mice by setting them on fire...until the night it managed to kill an entire Sabbat pack on its own, because somehow, some way, it managed to learn Thaumaturgy 3.

                      Then it got hilarious. My Tremere even made him his own little purple silk collar with mirrored name tag, as foci for Pavise of Fouled Presence and Incorporeal Passage.

                      ...people eugenics. The cat experiment turned out so well, my Tremere replicated it with humans. Didn't work out nearly as well as the cat, but his ghouls could give neonate Brujah a run for their money at least. Went to great effort to ensure the human ghouls never learned Thaumaturgy.

                      ...hunting and slaughtering entire packs of lupines (and Sabbat), to reduce them down to principal foci because. My Tremere had diablerized so much from his koldunism stunt, he developed methuselah's thirst by the age of 500. Werewolves were just the easiest snacks to find and cover up the deed.

                      ...partnering with the Setite and Lasombra PC to get into the darksteel trade. My Tremere was a hella necromancer, atop everything else. The Setite would do his thing to make sure the mortals were primed and ready to pass on as the best-quality" ore", the Lasombra would make them pass only as Lasombra can (making sure they were nice and exposed to Oblivion in the process), my characters' spirit slaves would forge them. The favors we got in return went towards empowering the Setite's and Lasombra's skinlands holdings, making for a nice little growth model. At the time the chronicle ended, my Tremere had damn near cornered the market.
