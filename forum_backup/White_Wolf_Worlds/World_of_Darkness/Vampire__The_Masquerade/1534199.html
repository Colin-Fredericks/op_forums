    Gangrel = Old Clan Tzimisce

    04-14-2024, 01:26 PM
    The thought occurred to me that it would be interesting if the Old Clan Tzimisce are the Gangrel. There were always a-political Gangrel roving the wilderness - but more politically minded Gangrel become rural lords - predatory owners of the land and its subjects. Lacking common mind control Disciplines, these lordly Gangrel focus on blood bonds and breeding ghoul families to serve them. Dracula was one of these, having the Protean Discipline as the book shows.

    When Tzimisce join the Sabbat, they lose their closeness to nature and over time Protean morphs into Vicissitude. Independent Gangrel continue roaming the woods, but the more politically minded Gangrel (Old Clan Tzimisce) who are hyper conservative elders who are extremely territorial join with the Camarilla.

    The standard Tzimisce, then, are regarded as Tzimisce antitribu by the Camarilla Gangrel. These Camarilla Gangrel are more political than in canon, being like the Old Clan Tzimisce and favor complete obedience to elders, the sanctity of domain, utilize ghoul (and revenant) servants.

    What does everybody think of this alternative viewpoint?

          Its a different idea even if I'm not sold on it.

          The merits I can see are fewer clans, which means the clans can be broader in concept and that fleshcraft is less spread out in very focused forms and can be tied together in a single shapeshifting Discipline. Especially if you divest the Setites from Serpentis you can have the Gangrel as the only Shapeshifting clan which in turn allows them to be, as I mentioned above, broader in concepts.

          The negative parts I see is that the way shapeshifting is spread between Gangrel, Tzimisce and Setites you can also get more focused, if narrower in concept, clans. From my personal perspective I am pretty set in my ways I'm not sure that I would want, or my players could accept a change of this magnitude to the setting. So there's a conservative reaction to these changes that could make it less attractive even if it technically would be an improvement.

              I think if I were doing a more complete Clan simplification, I would do something different entirely with the Setites. Maybe combine them with Lasombra and/or Baali and make Obtenebration their Discipline.

              Back to the Tzimisce and Gangrel. I do think that this change would be unpopular with some, for conservatism I’d for no other reason.

One advantage I see is that by incorporating the Old Clan Tzimisce concept into the Gangrel it gives the Camarilla Gangrel a bit more to do. It makes them more of a part of the Camarilla political landscape and a reason to be there. It’s kind of like adding OC Tzimisce to the Camarilla without the baggage of another Clan being there. Canon Gangrel were always a little bit out of place in the Camarilla - since they were so focused on wilderness and/or being nomadic that they didn’t have a big role in the political landscape of the Camarilla. - which is likely why they are made independent in later editions. Making them OCT creates this other political faction in the Camarilla and allows those interesting OCT ideas to be used more completely.

              Those are just my thoughts on it.
