                                            Just to be clear about the timeline there, the Wieck brothers ousted Mark Rein Hagen from White Wolf (due to severe financial mismanagement) before White Wolf acquired the rights to Pendragon, which was then sold to Steve Wieck's personal TTRPG company by CCP as part of their divestment of the smaller WW IPs that CCP didn't predict would be valuable as licensed products as it was clear that the WoDMMO wasn't going to happen.
