          The main issue with signature characters is that to be believable they have to fall within a certain range of behaviors and traits, but every clan and bloodline is intentionally designed to encompass a wide range of potential character types so that anyone can find something to play in that clan regardless of what they are into.

          When most people think "artist" they automatically think Toreador, but when most people think "Toreador" they think more along the lines of hedonists, socialites, artsy people who are looking for their next muse. To me, an artist is someone who refines their technique and understanding of their art rather than being distracted by living life. Consider the Dancer, Martial Artist, or the musician who practices for hours on end, only to be put in the same category as someone who is overly impressed with their own poetry.

          Another good example of the lack of focus is the average Malkavian. People see the word "Insane" and automatically think Malkavian, despite the word "Insane" being a legal term rather than a medical one in the modern world. A person who can't tell right from wrong is insane, but a person who knows right from wrong but does it anyway is sane. Thus the Jack the Ripper character from the Johnny Depp movie "From Hell" is more of a malkavian in my mind than say Hannibal Lector, who for the record always felt more like a Nagaraja to me.

                  I am not a fan of signature characters. They are a marketing tool and nothing more. They might be good, interesting characters, or cliched, bland characters in and of themselves, but in the end they are just a marketing tool. They exist so marketing and sourcebook writers can refer to standard copyrighted characters and build a brand. They might be good for that purpose, but I am uninterested in them.

                  Clans do have archetypes, and we have lots of examples of characters who fit those archetypes. The various Players Guides and Clanbooks are filled with them generic concepts that can be easily customized and fit into most chronicles. I usually find the best city by night sourcebooks to have more compelling characters of the clans than any signature character.

First, New York is so big and so dense that it attracts more Kindred as there are so many moving parts, it can be easy for any authority to lose track of other Kindred.
