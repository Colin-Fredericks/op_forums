                                                    Malkavian Headcanon

                                                    * The most common Malkavian derangements are as follows:

                                                    1. Paranoia
                                                    2. Guilt and self-loathing
                                                    3. Narcissism/Megalomania
                                                    4. An addiction of some kind (usually blood)
                                                    5. Poor impulse control (particularly anger)
                                                    6. Obsession with specific mortals or concepts (Gehenna)

                                                    In other words, a good 50% of Malkavians are functionally identical to your typical Elder vampire in behavior.

                                                    * Malkavians' Signature Discipline is not actually Dementation but Auspex. Many develop extra facility with it than other Kindred and it provides many of their uncanny "insights" that other vampires could have if they had less of a filter or more willingness to venture guesses based on what they discover.

                                                    * A common Malkavian flaw is using Auspex all the time on everyone and everything or being unable to filter it when you touch people or objects.

                                                    * While they never really reach Fishmalk status (that's an in-universe stereotype with no basis in reality), almost all Malkavians enjoy the occassional fucking with people.

                                                    * Malkavian elders are every bit as obsessed with power as Toreador, Ventrue, and Tremere. They just prefer to use proxies, catspaws, and pawns. Many cities with a more "traditional" prince are actually under the control of a Malkavian.

                                                    * Saulot was Malkav's brother. The two of them created the first Roads of Enlightenment.

                                                    * There's a popular theory that Saulot WAS Malkav Pre-Golconda. There's an equally popular one that Malkav was Saulot Post-Golconda.

                                                    * The Book of Nod was written by Malkav (allegedly).
