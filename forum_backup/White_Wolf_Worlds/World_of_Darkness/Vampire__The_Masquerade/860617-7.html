                    I have to respond here because I think I've had a cool concept, but I'll never be able to use it.

                    Marshal Williams (A.K.A. Marshal the kid, fastest gun in the east.)

                    The fact that Marshal was embraced at a young age might matter if he weren't a Nosferatu. Marshal wears a cowboy get up like you might see children wear, but his badge looks like it might be real and is rusty, and his hat is pink with a (blood?) stained hole in it. Marshal loves cowboys and tries to be a heroic cowboy in every way. Although not from the west, Marshal calls everybody "pardner" and mixes in other "old west" terms as he's talking. His guns may actually be real, but he never uses them. He has a giant pet lizard that he calls "spot".

                    Unlike most Nosferatu, Marshal doesn't view his embrace with disdain. In fact, he almost views his change as being the end to trauma, rather than being a traumatic event itself. You see, when Marshal and his sister were playing "cowboys and Indians", they found their father's gun. The gun went off in Marshal's hands and he ended up shooting and killing his sister. Marshal was inconsolable. He was a monster that had done the most horrible thing imaginable, and no one was going to convince him otherwise. That's why that Nosferatu decided to show Marshal what it REALLY meant to be a monster.. but things didn't go according to plan. The change hurt Marshal and made him as twisted and ugly as any Nosferatu, but in some way it was actually uplifting and healing for Marshal. He views his change as a sign that justice can exist in the world, but it sometimes needs to be helped along like the Nosferatu helped him.

                    Marshal knows that his change doesn't mean he's been forgiven. It doesn't take away the past, it only allows him to carry on into the future. Now Marshal patrols the most violent slums he can find, trying to protect the innocent. (He keeps the brim of his hat low and pulls his bandana high to try and cover his features as he's crime-fighting). At night he creeps into abodes of criminals and takes their blood. (and sometimes he gives 'em a good whoopin') He particularly tries to protect children and targets those who would harm children a little more zealously than he does others.

                    Even though Marshal isn't necessarily young, he has an allied Nosferatu who is like a school-marm. She looks out for Marshal and he usually does as she says, often complaining about it like a child complains about homework.. but anyone who gets to see Marshal's lair or his ability with scrounging and fixing things would probably figure out that Marshal has been around longer than he lets on.

                            110) The Nosferatu have a long and proud history of spawning urban legends of monsters and witches who prey upon the foolish and the guilty. Most of these legends stem from their elders, who have been around for centuries and lost touch with the modern world. But now a newcomer has joined the party, in the form of an Autarkis named Ash Parrish. Embraced as an act of retribution, rather than reward, Ash did not appreciate becoming a hideously deformed monster, and rejected the ways of clan and sect in favor of doing things his own way. Separated from the vast information network of his clan, and unwilling to listen to the guidance of others, Ash found himself living among the fringes of society like some kind of wild dog.

                            While initially humiliating and deplorable, Ash eventually began to find comfort in giving himself over to his more bestial nature. Coping with his inhuman actions became so much easier when he stopped perceiving himself as a human in the first place. One night, he decided that if this was going to be his existence from now on, then he might as well commit to it wholeheartedly. After slipping into a household one night and devouring a helpless child without alerting their parents, he cast aside the last of his attachment to his humanity and adopted the Path of the Feral Heart. Now, mortals and kindred alike have reason to fear getting too close to his territory, for Ash makes little distinction between them. He is like a wild animal, unfettered by the trappings of a civilized world. And to make matters worse, he still has a human brain, which makes him hyper intelligent compared to any natural predator.

                            For now, Ash's preferred hunting grounds are low income, suburban neighborhoods. The kind that don't attract a lot of government attention and which don't have too many cops enforcing the streets. He is especially fond of feeding on people inside their own homes, and often uses his vampiric abilities to gain entry without being noticed. If his victims are lucky, they'll already be asleep by the time he catches them. But if they prove to be a bit of a night owl, then things are probably going to get violently messy.

                            As both an Autarkis and one who follows the Path of the Feral Heart, Ash has absolutely zero interest in the political machinations of kindred society. If you wish to convince him to join your coterie, you must appeal to his more immediate concern of long term survival. The one who wears the crown is not as important as what they intend to do with said crown. And if they intend to make unlife difficult for Ash, then he may be willing to disrupt their plans. If, however, their goals do not directly inconvenience him in some way, then he is content to ignore the issue, regardless of who else gets hurt as a result of his apathy.

                                    112) One does not have to expend much effort to be repulsed by the research of those who dedicate themselves to the Path of Metamorphosis. But even they are rarely so dangerous as the ancilla who has come to be known as Dr. Gregor Steinhauer. Growing up in Germany, Dr. Steinhauer was an aspiring geneticist who was obsessed with transhumanism even before his embrace. He sought to unlock the secrets of the human genome and use it to achieve self controlled evolution. Intrigued that a mortal would harbor such a similar ideology to their own path of enlightenment, Steinhauer was targeted for embrace by a member of Clan Tzimisce, who fostered encouragement for his work.

                                    Now, Gregor's use of Vicissitude has taken a truly dark turn. While other metamorphosists focus on the the bones and muscles of their experiments, Dr. Steinhauer tries to shape them on the genetic level. The goal of his research is to create self replicating experiments who can pass their forms down through natural reproduction, instead of having to be manually sculpted. He prefers to create his experiments in pairs, consisting of one female and one male, before placing them into forced breeding programs to see if he can produce viable results. Thankfully for the outside world, his experiments have mostly been failures. No matter how much he warps them on the outside, their genetic makeup is still human (or whatever creature he used), which means they continue to produce offspring of that species. After regaining his composure from his latest failure, Gregor looks over what went wrong, before trying again with a new batch of test subjects. As for what becomes of the failures, that depends entirely upon how useful Gregor feels they can be in other fields of service.

                                    Unlike most of his clan, Gregor appears to be relatively normal looking. He's been too busy conducting experiments on other creatures to worry about his own modifications. He appears to be a German male in his late 40's with dark black hair and a handle bar mustache. He stands at a height of 6'2, weighing 173 lbs. His eyes are pale blue, and he wears a pair of circle rimmed glasses. As with many doctors and scientists, he prefers to keep himself in pristine condition, with his research facilities being cleaned to immaculate status before and after conducting his experiments.
