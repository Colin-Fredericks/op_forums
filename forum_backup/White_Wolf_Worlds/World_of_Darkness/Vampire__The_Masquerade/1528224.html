    What Road suits Witches of Echidna best?

    01-08-2024, 04:37 PM
    Hello there,

    I was re-reading the introduction of Warriors of Glycon and Witches of Echidna in the core manual of VDA20 and I realised that the idea of Typhon and Echidna and snake-things is really way deeper and more marked than I had grasped with my first reading last year. The WoG (Warriors of Glycon) are not only leaders of the clan, but also care about the "gifts of Typhon and the Mother", while the Witches go 100% the "Mother/Snake is the real deal and our god, while Set is just a form for it and we're way older than the Egyptian Hellenistic zealots worshipping the aardvark-headed god" route.

    I love it. I feel there really needs to be more stuff written to fill the gaps in clan history, because the Dark Ages Setite and their ancient history is really being reframed.

    I am however confused on Roads. VDA20 offers only two strictly Setite Roads: Road of Set and Road/Path of Apep. Road of Set seems to be that of the Orthodox Setites of Egypt. Road of Apep is definitely the way of some heresies like the Northern Europeans followers of Jörmungandrson. But:

    1) would Khay'tall and the Children of Judas be on the Road of Apep? Or are they more likely on the Road of Sin?
    2) what about the Witches? I get that they're a bit odd in their beliefs, but I can't decide whether they should be read as mainly aligned with the Path of Apep or with that of Set.

      Update: I re-read the bit in the manual about the two roads and it says explicitly most of the clan is on the Road of Set, while the Road of Apep is a minority. Therefore the bulk of WoG and WoE (Witches of Echidna) should also be on the Road of Set in theory (I'm deliberately ignoring the fact that many setites might be on other roads altogether because it's irrelevant to the question, even though true in itself). So I'm guessing it's just a difference in interpretation of the Road of Set or cultural setting whether one focuses on Typhon and the Mother or Set/Sutekh.

          In my games I run them as a group that's functionally a Setite version of the Bahari. For them, Echidna is to Typhon-Set as Lilith is to Caine. Mythologically, there's actually a bit of overlap between Lilith and Echidna, specifically in their roles as "Mother of Monsters." As a result, the most common Road among the Witches of Echidna is a slightly modified Road of Lilith and most of those are on the Path of Making.

          *edit* The other Road that can be found in numbers among the Witches of Echidna is a modified Road of Metamorphosis. Some Witches claim that vampirism originated with Echidna, and she is the one who turned Typhon-Set into the first vampire. Vampires were the final and most powerful all of her monstrous children. Specifically Echidna gave vampires a portion of her divine nature, a seed that if properly nurtured can allow them to ascend into divinity. This is how Typhon-Set became a deity, and it's something that every vampire can accomplish with time, knowledge and a full understanding of their condition.

          So they aspire to grow stronger and ascend to divinity by mastering their body and soul. By seeking to make other vampires his servants/slaves/followers, Typhon-Set is trying to prevent them from achieving apotheosis and becoming divine rivals to himself. Witches on this Road are considered super-duper heretical by mainline Setites, and to a large degree, it was this sub-group's beliefs that caused the Followers to wipe out the Witches. */edit*

          So in my settings, there was a lot of mingling between Witches of Echidna and Bahari since they had overlapping beliefs and similarities to the point that people who weren't overly familiar with the Witches of Echidna would believe them to be another Bahari Cult. The Followers of Set were always a bit suspicious of the Witches of Echidna, but traditionally the Witches never really opposed Set, so they were largely left alone. Eventually though their heresies simply got too much for the hardliner Setites to allow anymore, and they purged the Bloodline. Most of the Witches teachings, knowledge and magic was hidden and preserved by the Bahari. A handful of Witches even managed to hide among the Bahari so there's a few surviving members of the Bloodline even in the modern nights.

          Beyond those two Roads though, no other Roads are all that common among the Witches of Echidna, at least in my setting.

                  That seems like quite a cool Set up. I like the Metamorphosis bit particularly because I was thinking of something similar recently (see below).

                  I'm not wholly convinced I want to tie the Witches to Lilith for my games, though I can see why one would go that way and respect that it's a sensible take. My issue is that 1) I prefer Echidna to be something new and the whole emphasis on Typhon, Echidna and sacred multiheaded snakey monsters to be a new facet of the ancient Setites that's not absorbed into older metaplot lines like Lilith. I think Lamiae and Baharis are enough for that; 2) one line of the WoE's writeup mentions her "shedding her skin and sex from mother to father to son", which makes me feel like she's not meant to be taken in a motherly or feminine way specifically. Snakes are particularly uncaring for their children.

                  I rather like the idea of warriors of glycon, witches of echidna and followers of set seeing each other as a brotherhood of monsters like the mythological children of Typhon and Echidna and having a connection to ancient snake-centered myths. Some of these myths includes some dethroning: the first ruler of the cosmos for the Greek was Ophion - presumably a snake-related being since the name has to do with snakes in Greek - who was then deposed and replaced by Uranus, then Kronos, then Zeus. Zeus battled Titans and Giants and imprisoned them in Tartarus, and then Gaia and Tartarus mated and gave birth to Typhon specifically to punish Zeus for killing Titans and Giants, Gaia's progeny (there's another myth about Typhon being generated by Hera to punish Zeus for his affairs, but I'm not looking at that right now). Typhon almost succeeds in killing Zeus until he is defeated thank to various gods working and plotting together. I like to imagine WoD's Typhon as having in himself the desire for freedom and chaos of all those titans and giants imprisoned in Tartarus by the Olympian Aeons led by Zeus. Thus, the children of Typhon call to subvert the Aeons' rule and lead humans and supernaturals to the primordial freedom of the world. Like snakes, though, neither Typhon nor Echidna are necessarily benevolent towards their children: Echidna saps blood from her witches regularly, while Typhon might break free of his prison under Mount Etna one day and devour his children if he is not well pleased.
                  The Witches know this and work accordingly. And as the clan of faith and of multiheaded monsters, the ancient clan promotes religious variety in cults, as presented in the Tome of Secrets.

                  I see Set and his followers as an almost late (though still ancient) addition to the clan, who have a characteristically "orthodoxy-minded" approach and is particularly obsessed with Set/Sutekh, who may not even be the original Typhon, but perhaps his youngest child who diablerised him. The "Set faction" of the clan tries to get rid of the older brother lineages in the Middle Ages, only to be effectively infected by Typhon's spirit (who also had taken over Set/Sutekh) and becoming, as time goes on, the variegated faith clan it will come to be in the Modern Nights.

                  With all this in mind, I'm setting for: Witches and Warriors and Followers are mostly on the Road of Set (though some call it the Road of Typhon or the Road of Typhon and Echidna); a few, particularly the get of Jormungandrsson, are on the Path of Apep which is less about waking Typhon up or freeing people and more about destroying the world. I'm thinking of developing a Path of the Monsters for my Warrior of Glycon character which emphasises use of Serpentis and the physical disciplines to become a monster of legend able to strike fear in the hearts of the Aeons and their agents and restore the freedom and might of when the titans ruled the cosmos. This is a sort of Road of Metamorphosis, but I need to work on how exactly combine it with the Road of Set. In time, this can be an inspiration for the Path of the Warrior that Warrior Setites follow in the MOdern Nights, without knowing it was inspired by an older ethos of the Warriors of Glycon.

                          I just skimmed through the Revelations of the Dark Mother and realised the link Lilith-Echidna-Derech Lilith-Path of Making is WAY tighter than I had realised. I'm actually altering my headcanon to have Lilith as Echidna, Typhon as either Caine or a 2nd gen from the ones who were with Lilith in D'hainu who would go on to sire Osiris and be diablerised by Set. Or Typhon could be a being of Abyss altogether, who fathers Glycon as well as the Witches by joining with Lilith when she's in her Serpent form.

                          Typhon (as 2nd gen vampire) has Serpentis as his discipline. The Warriors of Glycon are his true descendants, along with the Witches (who take after Lilith though). Since he's a 2nd gen, and since snakes are cold blooded, he could also be the real origin of the True Brujah (Temporis=ouroboros myths). Osiris would be another childe of his (with Potence, Presence and Blood Magic). They have different disciplines because he's a 2nd gen. Set diablerises him or Glycon (3rd gen) when he attacks D'hainu with the other Antediluvians and gains Serpentis this way. Tough luck, Typhon/Glycon has been taking over Set intermittently since, allowing: 1) Typhon/Glycon to go unrecorded as a childe of Lilith killed by the Antediluvians in D'hainu; 2) the Warriors of Glycon and the Witches of Echidna to survive in Cainite society and be seen as parts of the clan of Set, though they do not retain memory of their true origin, unlike the Lamia, and therefore do not follow the Road of Lilith. High level Obfuscate is very annoying. Lilith hid her link to them so they could survive, and Set hid his crime or even forgot it had to do with Lilith, so he could advance his schemes.
                          Eventually Typhon takes over Set's body amd clan permanently, but not before Set manages to orchestrate the extinction of the other two lines through the Followers of Set.

                          EDIT: a neater presentation of the idea:
                          Typhon is a Demon connected to/escaped from/pulled out of the Abyss who was also embraced by Caine or Lilith and is part of those 2nd gen vamps who protect Lilith's garden of D'hainu. He has cold blood like the Trujah, Serpentis as one of his disciplines, Potence as another. Given the fact that pacts with demons may allow a vampire to procreate, or that snakes are a symbol of fertility in mythology and Glycon in particular was known for bringing miracle pregnancies, Typhon and Echidna (=Lilith in her Serpent form) bring to the world the Witches of Echidna (who take magic from their mum) and the Warriors of Glycon (Potence and Serpentis from daddy, Obfuscate from mummy), who become one of the most prolific "clans" (counted as one, despite being two) of the real, real ancient world. Typhon also sires Osiris and Ilyes independently. Osiris never has anything to do with D'hainu, but Ilyes goes to the Second City, sires Troile and others, is diablerised, and Troile manages to discover D'hainu through his soul's memories and spills the beans to Caine and the other Antediluvians, who attack D'hainu and murder Lilith's children. Typhon is forgotten because Set is the one who diablerises him, steals Serpentis, and decides to use Obfuscate 11+ to make everyone forget about him (only 6 children of Lilith are mentioned in Bahari texts) and to make the Warriors of Glycon and Witches of Echidna think they're linked to him (Set, not Typhon, while he takes on the mythological identity of Typhon, nonetheless) and forget their true origins.
                          In time, Typhon takes over Set's body and clan, but before he can do it Set manages to drive the Warriors and Witches to extinction when he realises they were somehow remembering their snake origins and coming to realise he scammed them and the Egyptian Set has little to do with them, while Typhon and the Mother are way more important to their origins.

                          The clan is the clan of faith because Typhon is a demon and hungers for faith, even through Set's body and clan. The Setite's curse of double damage from sunlight is Set's own from Caine, while the weaknesses of the Warriors of Glycon and of the Witches of Echidna are not true weaknesses, but the hunger of Typhon and Echidna manifest in their children: Typhon hungers for power and faith, so Warriors of Glycon want power and conversions, and he's a demon incarnated and embraced, so the body is paramount for him; Echidna hungers through her daughters, syphoning blood from them to sustain Lilith herself as she carries on with her plans.
