                                                    So, as I mentioned before, I like the Tzimisce changes, though I think I might have gone with Blood Sorcery instead of Dominate. Not a big deal for me though. Its hard to do Tzimisce when there is no Sabbat, so the Tzimisce as they are seem a bit homeless. I do like the idea of Camarilla Tzimisce though, so I feel like this version of them are a little less heretical in a Camarilla game.

                                                    I like the Lasombra in the Camarilla, though I’m so sure about how they were put in their. Mass exoduses always seem a bit far fetched to me. I think that if I were going to do it, I would have it so that the Camarilla Lasombra had been gaining in power and numbers gradually over time and that when the Brujah left the Camarilla, essentially starting their own Anarch Revolt, the Camarilla Lasombra said that they were tired of not having full Clan status and said that they would go to the Anarchs if they didn’t receive full recognition as a Camarilla Clan and all rights and privileges included, so the Inner Circle decided it was worth it and so recognized the Lasombra as a full Camarilla Clan and then a bunch of Lasombra across Camarilla territory can come out of the closet and it turns out that there were lots of them. It could be from this position of power that other Lasombra could join the Camarilla.

                                                    On the topic of the Lasombra, I’m not sure I like Oblivion and all of the implications of combining Obtenebration with Necromancy. It seems to change the character of both the Lasombra and the Necromancy Bloodlines (Hecata)

                                                    I like the changes with the Ravnos. I long thought that they were hopeless and kind of wrote them off or heavily altered them. This, I suppose, counts as a heavy alteration because they aren’t ethnic at all anymore, which is what the Clan needed for rehabilitation. A long time ago I said I would give them Celerity, Obfuscate, and Presence and this edition almost brought them in line with that. I think that they are better in this edition than in any previous canon edition. I’m not sure how much it matters as they are still small.

                                                    I mentioned earlier that I didn’t like the Assamites/Banu Haquim in the Camarilla and I still feel that way. I’d have them in the Anarchs if anything, with the Ministry in the Camarilla. Someone wanted me to go into more detail about why and I will in a future post.

                                                    I don’t like what happened with the Tremere. I feel like the change is too drastic and undermines a lot of what made the Tremere so flavorful. They are supposed to be a unified extremely hierarchical Clan, removing that makes them less interesting to me. Also, their Vienna Chantry getting blown up seems too gonzo to me as well.

                                                    I don’t know if I see the Gangrel as Anarchs either. They seem more appropriate as an independent Clan, the Anarchs are too political and organized. Really, I’m not sure if they should have made the Anarchs into a Sect like the Sabbat. I have often wanted VtM to be more about Elders vs Anarchs, but by having Camarilla Clans and Anarch Clans, it seems to become the same sort of conflict as existed with the Camarilla and the Sabbat. Open warfare with individual cities held by one faction or another, each Sect having its elders and neonates, rather than the elders vs the youth.

                                                    The Hecata seem like they shouldn’t be a unified group, as has been said before. I think it might be interesting to have certain of these bloodlines side with the Camarilla, certain side with the Anarchs, certain with the Sabbat. Then you can have a Necromancer regardless of the Sect your Chronicle focuses on.

                                                    Actually, I could see a similar approach with a number of Clans: detach Clan from Sect by focusing one Bloodlines/castes of each Clan joining different Sects. They did do this with the Tremere, kind of, with the difference Houses, strangely one of the Clans I think such factions are inappropriate for.

                                                    I guess over all, my response is mixed.

                                                        It strikes me that Dominate is a bit of an awkward fit for the Tzimisci. The history of the Clan involves them having created the Vinculumn specifically because the Elders were Blood Bonding new vampires and sending them off to die in war against the Tremere. The Blood Bond was their primary tool, because in older editions the clan didn't have enough Elders specializing in Dominate for that to have been the explanation.

                                                        It's also why the Sabbat Tzimisci all favored Visscisitude (besides spoopy alien infection) because the Sabbat Tzim were nearly all descendedfrom the Elders who relied on the Blood Bond to keep them in line. The Old Clan, who favored Dominate, never had their childer rise up en masse to slaughter them, at least not to the same degree because they relied less on the Blood Bond and the Vinculumn does nothing against Dominate. So the fact that a significant proportion of Tzimisci were forced to rely on the Blood Bond rather than Dominate to control others was a pretty central point to the Clan history.
