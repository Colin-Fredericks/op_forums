        I would say it work regardless of Genreration, becasue of two reason... well three
        First is the above reason from BJD - I know I sound like a broken old record^^
        The second is the bit logical aproach; it is easier to thicken an already strong vitae than a weak, "diluted" one; we even could see this in the diablerie and several generation lowering effects; farther you are from the source, the harder to reach it; Mithras was a 4th generation Methuselah​, Montgomery Coven was a 11th Generation Child and after the deed, he not achived 4th Generation, only lowered to the 6th. Meanwhle, after the Baali Anaduk made a deal with a Demon who gifted Anaduk a succubus who could consume the Tablets of Ma-ri-ah and become the product of whatever was contained in them and since the tablets were incomplete, Ma-ri-ah was only partially recreated, full of disjointed and partial memories and corrupted by hate; she slew Anaduk upon waking and gorged on his blood, becoming a 7th Generation Baali in the process. After Mary the Black ate Michael, she became a 4th Generation Baali, not a 6th or 5th. Her blood was thicker.
        So by this logic, a 4th Generation could be easier to lower to 3 than a 6 for example. Some suplement even state something along the line, "A vampire's vitae itself seek perfection" and thats what urges the vampires to consume each other, to reach to source.​
        It's a good argument, I must say, regarding Montgomery and Mary the Black, specially. I remember when I first read about Montgomery I thought with myself that it was too obvious who was in charge there lol, I only felt sorry for Mithras for having to deal being a 6th generation vampire with a much weakened character sheet (even though Mithras original character sheet didn't make him justice in the first place and I always changed it, but at least it was better than Montgomery-Mithras one. Mary the Black has a fascinating story, I just always wondered how on earth (or better in this case, on Hell lol) wasn't Michael able to easily control her after the deed.

        Third is a bit blatant reasoning I know... but the description of Malkav's blood not mentions any generation limits; you drink it, you lower your generation.
        Would be logical, actually, the Antediluvians were Embraced before Generations became a thing, in a sense their vitae is a "saved version" what a Cainite blood should be like. Their progeny would be still a 4th Generation Child of course, but that is because of the nature of the Embrace; even their strong vitae is not capable to fully bridge the chasm between a Mortal and Immortal Blood God...
        ...but if you are basically a Blood God already?​
        This reason is probably enough in itself, I'd say, my main concern isn't even PCs of 4th Generation (as I'm yet to have one in 20+ years of experience as a Storyteller), but more to the scenario really and antagonists. After my group of PCs helped my game's antagonist acquire the Sargon Fragment (and perform the Ritual of Apotheosis, yes they even protected him while he made the ritual and other NPCs were trying to "save the world" and stop him lol), this kind of thing really worries me, because giving too much power to certain characters in a story could be a good way to basically end the story lol. In any case, your reasons are good and I have to agree with them. Regarding players dropping their generation and having to deal with the consequences, that's also something I always tell my players, beware hahaha...

                                        On topic
                                        As I imagine things, vampires slowly build up their attributes and disciplines through feeding, and accumulate power which can be bestowed to ghouls to raise their attributes/disciplines. They're siphoning away wealth from the masses as parasites and redistributing it to a chosen few (ghouls/childer). Antedeluvians have been siphoning away wealth into their ultra-concentrated blood for millennia, their blood so rich and their attributes so exaggerated that a single drop is worth years of ordinary feeding.

                                        Also, In older books, elder blood could raise a drinker's disciplines temporarily. As I rule this, one dot for every level beyond 5th is given to the drinker provided the new level doesn't exceed the donors. So if you have 4 Potence and you drink from someone with 8 potence, you temporarily have 7 potence. But if you had 7 Auspex and drank the blood of someone with 8 Auspex you will only go up to 8 Auspex.
