    Eh, probably 12th or 13th, as it's supposedly the most common in the modern nights. Most likely clans being Nosferatu or Malkavian.

    If I found myself waking up as a Thinblood, I'd leap at the chance to perform Diablerie the very moment I was informed about its existence.

    This is something which I think that many people forget about low Generation. Low Generation gives potential for incredible powers but you don't get it at once nor do you grow your power faster than anyone else. It just that most low Generation Cainites have had time across millennia to develop their potential.

    Yes, but another thing to keep in mind is that 5th edition seems to have nerfed Diablerie somewhat. By which I mean you can only reduce your generation by 1 step, as opposed to previous editions where you had the chance of skipping multiple levels.

    Any 4th generation, even a newborn, is a rare and precious opportunity. If you want to reap the benefits of eating one, you need to work your way down by diablerizing them in numerical order.


    Or, if you're following a Path of Enlightenment (let's say Nodhism), you could farm their blood and use it to mass embrace a bunch of neonates, whom you subsequently diablerize to speed run your ascension.

                Originally posted by Baaldam View Post

                By RAW, i remember no edition where diablerie would bump generation by more than 1 step. At most a "you as ST can allow more, if you feel it's appropriate for your chronicle" and leave it to personal preference on individual tables.
                Montgomery "Monty" Coven, canonically been bumped from 11th to 6th after he ate Mithras - and that diableire was incomplete

                But The Black Hand: A Guide to the Tal'Mahe'Ra, introduces rules for diablerie that lowers the diablerist's generation by more than one, gaining Discipline dots from Disciplines that the victim had higher than the diablerist and how the victim can possess the diablerist.
                Its on page 180.

                "If the victim is of a lower Generation than the diablerist, the diablerist automatically lowers her Generation by one.
                Additionally, if the victim was two or more Generations lower than the diablerist, the diablerist rolls Stamina once per turn for each additional Generation after the first, until they fail a challenge or run out of rolls (difficulty9).
                Each success lowers the diablerist’s Generation by one and permanently grants them all benefits attributed to the Generation achieved."​
