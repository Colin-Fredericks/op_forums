                                        Honestly, here's some thoughts about the Thin-Bloods. I wanted to post in more detail but I've been kind of stuck between a rock and a hard place with house repairs, hotel stays, and a few other bits of unpleasantness.

                                        Some thoughts about the changes to the Thin-Bloods for V5.

                                        1. I'm not really a fan of Thin-Blood Alchemy: Honestly, I think it's a weird thing that isn't justified in story. I want to know how every Thin-Blood knows it, who came up with it, and why they're now doing it. If this was invented by a Thin-Blooded version of Walter White or Elon Musk that makes sense but then it would be "Thin-Blood Chemistry." I mean, it's also not something that could easily be spread unless the Thin-Bloods have their own version of Hunter.net and that would be more interesting to me than the Discipline itself.

                                        I think it might have been more interesting to just restrict Thin-Bloods to having a single Discipline and offering them a Clan cost to raising it. Something like Telekinesis, Bat Powers, or what we have in Bloodlines 2. Maybe also allow them to have a single dot Discipline in anything else. It isn't a strong build but is certainly a workable one. So a character might have "Bat Powers 5, Auspex 1, Presence 1." Add in the Daytime activity, permanent Blush of Life, and ability to drink animal blood regularly--a very solid character. Then again, some people like to play ghouls so maybe a workable build is missing the point.

                                        2. I like the relative Humanity of Thin-Bloods: One of my favorite parts of Bloodlines was the discovery of the Thin-Bloods under Santa Monica's pier. They're a likable bunch that is completely unlike the rest of the Kindred you meet. The sweet love story between Lily and E, Rosa's visions, and that poor guy who still believes there is a cure for his condition. They really do seem mostly harmless and you could argue that is because their Beasts are so weak. Still divorced from humanity, still Damned, but almost human.

                                        Yes, they are the Half-Elves of V:TM.

                                        3. I love what they bring to the War of Ages: I like the idea of the Thin-Bloods at war with the rest of vampire society and having a real understandable motive to become repeat diablerists. The fact the Thin-Bloods are tied to modern humanity and have access to all the tech as well as modern world's cultures puts them as the perfect embodiment of the Movement. Then again, I just love Jenna Cross and her army of Thin-Bloods ready to go guerilla on the Elders.
