            Maybe this thread's a bit old, but I've seen older restarted so it seems like it should be OK?

            The Good
            Hecata: It’s the fucked up insular family-centric dynastic politicking of the Giovanni (now with more fucked up dynastic politicking!) without sacrificing the forbidden scholar vibe of the Cappadocians or badass aesthetic of the Harbringers and Samedi. For that, I will forgive this clan anything. (The Nagaraja shouldn't be here though.)


            The So=So:
            Tremere: I love the new weakness, I like schisms and squabbling over how to rebuild, I don’t like the new houses existing instead of building on the Anarch sorcerers. Put the Hermetic paradigm back on those vampires. Put it back.

            Tzimisce: Clan flaw is an improvement. Vicissitude is not and I feel it should’ve been a ritual discipline building off protean. Again. Auspex. Put it back.
            Failing that, do a Hecata and make the choice to swap either Auspex or Protean out for Dominate, if you must play Old Clan.

            Toreador: I guess? I prefered the old weakness perhaps, seeing as I despise Toreadors being the "arts and social clubs" clan. It's limiting when there are many other aspects of culture and society to get dangerously fixated over. Military, Politics, Religion, Science, History... Where are my mad scientist Toreadors whose concept of art is the Mass Spectrometer and they keep zoning out to gve lectures about molecular chemistry and what that means for our deepening understanding of the universe?? (OK, done being daft now)

            Thin-bloods: Their unique flaws and merits are good, but that alchemy stuff should be exceedingly rare. If not nonexistent.

            Nosferatu: The mental image of SchreckNET now being a mass of ancient languages and a new cipher every week is amusing to me. They're basically the same, as far as I remember.


            The Bad
            Ravnos: Put that DAV20 weakness back or so help me.
            (And while we're at it, put fortitude back.)

            Lasombra: Obtenebration. Is. Not. Necromancy. Furthermore what are the antitrubu, chopped liver? Go join the Anarchs.

            Salubri: Why are you still alive and annoying and why are your disciplines Like That. What happened to the other two guys?

            Ventrue: Prefered the Ventrue weakness to be debilitating.

            Gangrel: What do you mean the traits are even less permanent than before??

            Brujah: Being a wide spectrum of idealogies and individuals, the idea that the Brujah quit enmasse is not appealing to me. They can't all be anti-establishment rebels, you'd just end up with the loss of the troublemakers and the Camarilla full of reformists and fiercely pro-Tradition guys who aren't strapped to the teeth in leather (plus the ones whose rage isn't focused on Anarch vs Cam, so had no need to leave).


            The Ignored
            Banu Haqim/Assamites: I’ve never cared about this clan.

            Malkavian: Malkavian weakness never reflects their insight as I imagine it anyway.

            Setites/Ministry

To be blunt about it, a two-hundred-year-old, impulse embrace, himbo whose full extent of unlife is Elysium smack-talk probably doesn't have a bright enough bulb to think of say, stellar spectroscopy, in terms more complex than "ooh pretty rainbow". You'll get rhetoricians, writers, and the occasional philosopher in the fold because they use their word-hole real pretty, but the full utility of that to the clan is going to be...well, Elysium smack-talk. Vampires just tend to not look beyond their own, largely socially constructed and socially reinforced, frames of reference for value in the embrace -- and when the odd embrace occurs outside that frame of reference, they either adapt through conformity or get outcast (something for which Toreador are notorious).

                    I'm not trying to throw shade anywhere, I'm just pointing out the clan doesn't really have a predilection for intellectual pursuit...and where they do, it's generally instrumentalized to far more topical affairs. It's one thing I plan to play to the hilt in my Transylvania Chronicles game come the Renaissance, when the clan takes a good, hard look at the genius it embraces during it, and says to itself, "nah, fuck these nerds".

                                                Or to put it another way, there aren't many flower children in the ranks. Compared to the horde of beret-wearing, AK-toting, dynamite-slinging howlers who smash shit up, go home singing songs about how they stuck it to the man, and sit around doing fuck-all watching the shit they just smash get rebuilt, better and stronger next time, while the people they rage against just keep piling up the cash, favors, and power on the back of the broken window fallacy.

                                                Because it ain't about "the cause, man". Just like every other damn vampire. And that's where the useful idiots come in, those poor deluded fucks who are either too young or plainly too damn stupid to do anything but be "true believers" in "the cause"...at the end of the night, useful idiocy is still idiocy.
