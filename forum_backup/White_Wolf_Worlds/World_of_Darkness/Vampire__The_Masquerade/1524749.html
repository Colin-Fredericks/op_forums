    The St. Clare Bloodline

    10-30-2023, 08:16 AM
    going back to 1e I had victims game using Hunters Hunted my character was "Clare Blake" a Potent Blood Psychic and later Ghoul didn't get to do much with her, come 2e with an other GM started a "Minions" game Ghouls mortal Acolytes and such I remade her with a few minor changes to her backstory changing her from Prey to a Retainer got to play her off and on till just before Y2K when the gaming circle split up.

    Well about six weeks ago some of the old gang got back together again (like we do every couple of years.) and we started a 2e game, hard up for a concept I dusted off Clare and had her embraced. one story down the GM wants me to found a new blood line since I've already got a unique discipline

    the Bloodline will be named for St. Clare House as a joke it's the characters Haven, the Disciplines will be Auspex, Numina, there at this time is no third discipline but given Clare is a former Ghoul and plans to embrace only (psychic) Ghouls that are willing it may default to Potence.

    the Numina Discipline allows the Vampire to tame the power of Psychic Blood, the "core" Discipline lets them spend Blood of an appropriate type to manifest that Psychic ability up to a rank equal to their Numeina Discipline rank, further when rolling to control Psychic manifestations the Kindred may add his Numina Discipline score to the dice pool. to date to learn this Discipline a Kindred must have bean a Psychic in Life, though so far it has bean contra-indicated a Kindred may be able to learn this Discipline If they have a sufficient supply of Psychic Blood and is willing to consume it regularly over the course of months or years or possibly longer. when a Kindred first develops this Discipline they receive One Rank in a single Numeina Path.

    Numina Paths, much like Thermataugy there are a number of "Paths", one for each Psychic power, a kindred may only follow one path for each rank they have in the Numina Discipline. to follow a Numina Path the Kindred must have possessed that Psychic ability in Life or it is theorized that if consumed and controlled significant amounts of a different type of Psychic blood as a Kindred already possesses they may be able to learn that Path. Each rank in an appropriate Numeina Path​ lowers the Target number to control manifestations of that type of Psychic Blood. Further a Kindred may spend Blood points to manifest Psychic Abilities equal to their Path rank in that path Psychic ability.

    Numeina Discipline and Ghouls, Ghouls have not bean able to learn this Discipline, one Ghoul who is already Psychic has bean trying to learn this Dicipline, instead they have come close to manifesting a new Mortal Psychic Ability     instead.

      Clare Blake
      Born: 197X (early 70's)
      Ghouled: 199X (early 90's)
      Embraced: Less than a Month ago
      Apparent Age: Late Teens (can pass for mid Teens or early 20's with little work)
      Generation: Eighth
      Clan: Caitiff
      Nature: Avant-garde
      Demeanour: Conformist
      Appearance: A slim girl on the shorter side of average height with short black hair fair skin and Silver/Grey eyes, even when not trying to pass for someone younger she can be mistaken for that.

      Born in to a family of Fundamentalist Evangelicals she was largely ignored because of her gender, when she proved to be a perceptive and clever child her parents belittled and marginalized her. in elementary school when she began to outperform the "Anointed" boys academically that's when the Deprivations and Beatings started, during middle school her psychic gifts begun to awaken once she constantly started to know things like the dirty little secrets of Church Elders and school administrators her parents began to threaten her with being burnt as a Witch. a few months after starting junior high she ran away from home. after a few months on the streets she was found and fostered by a New Age/Progressive Catholic family who recognized her gifts and connected her with an old Hippy friend who was a Psychic/Mystic, for close to two years she was happy, safe and learning to come to terms with her abilities until social services stumbled across her true identity and returned her to her family. now better able to control and conceal her powers she blended back in to her congregation and her faith based school, in her time away she had learned many of the things done behind closed doors at her church and school where morally wrong and criminal, after she was ignored when she tried to report them to authorities she arranged for an outside adult to "Stumble" on some of the misdoings, when the secrets where revealed her Church and School where dismantled due to endemic financial crimes and child sexual abuse. Her life improved significantly when she was able to attend a a more mainstream faith based high school and the parents were absorbed with finding a new church that matched their narrow world view they ignored their children.

      After graduation she moved to <Chronicle City> (Chicago) for Collage, in her freshman year she had to take a number of Theology electives to get her parents to agree to let her study out of state, but after she turned eighteen she was free to choose her own Major and other electives, choosing to major in Psychology with a minor in Social Work. in her new home she soon fell in with the Goth and Alternative scenes and sort out the mystic fringe, this is where André, Philippe and Bruce (a group of Vampires experimenting with Psychic Blood) found her, after a while they decided to Ghoul some of their herd in part to keep them from aging out and also to find out what would happen when they Ghouled a Psychic. Though there where a handful of short brakes (sometimes due to neglect other times as part of an experiment) in her Ghouldem she had bean around thirty years.

      then one night the Vampires hit upon a new experiment to find out the effects mixing Booze and Drug tainted blood with the blood of Psychics and Booze and Drug tainted Psychic Blood, in fact this was little more than a Blood Bender that may later have been replicated under more scientific conditions, but as the night pressed on and the blood begun to be depleted a trio of unidentified vampires stormed the meeting place Ghouls fell quickly defending André, Philippe and Bruce and Kine where drained to fuel the battle, but the newcomers fought with a power and fury only found among elders, the local kindred where dispatched to their final deaths at the cost of two of the attackers as the survivor turned to leave he noticed that Clare still lived and he descended on her to drink her to death, but as she was about to expire in her death through she latched on to her attacker instead draining him stealing the embrace and giving him final death.

      Newly embraced she burned though her stolen blood healing her shattered body and fell in to a torpor, when she awoke a week later in a room full of rotting bodies splattered with spoiled blood and a mouth full of the decaying flesh of her erstwhile sire, she was near mental brake and falling in to a frenzy from a lack of Vitae, she washed her mouth out and broke open the blood fridge, drinking all that she could find, ironically much of it her own mortal blood, now sited enough to control herself she bathed and redressed herself. She then descended on the Rack looking for two things, Fresh Blood and a Kindred who knew where that nights Elysium was being held, it took her hours to safely acquire both.

      Knowledge in hand she quickly rushed to the Elysium hoping to arrive before the nights business was concluded and the Elders returned to their havens before the dawn, but the night was still younger than she anticipated, after begging her way in she fumbled her way through the formal wording to throw herself on the mercy of the Prince. Unimpressed he let her convey her story, when she was done the Prince dispatched the Sheriff and some of his men to investigate the scene of the past weeks battle, they returned to confirm the basics. Clare was bound and over the next three nights the primogen meet in council and debated the fate of Clare and the implications of the trespassing elders who slew André, Philippe and Bruce. André Clare’s Patron had bean of the blood line of the Toreador Primogen and had advanced a number of his ghouls for the embrace to her with Clare being near the top of the list.

      In the end the Prince decided to Parole Clare for a reason yet unknown to any outside the Primogen and even then they don’t know his true reasoning for any certainty.

          The St. Clare
          They are no Bloodline, just a American Caitiff who has stumbled on to some new trick, she has taught to a few other Caitiff all but One now Missing or Thankfully Dead….. You say she has a pack of Psychic Ghouls she may soon Embrace, I foresee a Blood Hunt in coming Nights.
          • Unidentified European Tremere Elder
          The “St. Clare” are yet to become an actual bloodline since the prospective Founder has yet to embrace any Childer of her own and appears reluctant to do so, but she has be an able to teach three other Caitiff who where Psychics in life the discipline she has discovered, of those one is confirmed dead a second after a brief time under house arrest is now missing and presumed dead the third is under parole and gravely mistreated by the Prince of his home city.
          If the Bloodline is founded it due to the requirements necessary to learn the discipline fundamental to the Bloodline it will have to start small and will only be able to grow slowly.

          Nickname: Mind-Freaks, Psi-Freaks, Psichos, Psychos ect.
          Appearance: There is no definitive appearance for the St.Clare as there is as yet only one who could be conceded part of the Bloodline, but so far most of the prospective candidates are young and female, but not all fit this image.
          Haven: so far there is no template for the bloodlines haven.
          Background: to be a candidate they have to be a Psychic in life and the Progenitor is inclined to prepare them for the Embrace by a time as a Ghoul
          Character Creation: Mentally gifted persons with strong personalities, lots of self control and strong wills are required, the progenitor is favoring those display a higher morality, background can be widely varied but at least initially Generation is required since they will most likely be embraced by the bloodlines progenitor who is of the eighth generation or one of her Get.
          Disciplines: Auspex, Numina and likely Potence.
          Weakness: The prospective Founder has not displayed any noticeable traits that could manifest as a Bloodline Flaw, apart from her Aura having a distinctive composition and falling somewhere between the brightness of the Kindred and the Kine.
          Organization: at this time there is no organization and only an informal one forming amoung the Progenitor and a small collection of Psychics she is assembling.
          Bloodline Prestige: At this time retainers who display the most self control, strength of will, Intellect and personality alongside Moral Fiber and development of their psychic gifts are favored.
          Quote: Focus yourself, Feel your connection to the wider world and those people and things in it. Master Yourself and you can Master the World around You.

              Re-awaken the Psychic Blood (Specific Numina)

              At the Sixth rank of the Numina Discipline the Kindred can partially reawaken their Psychic blood from when they where a Mortal, instead of having to of having to possess Blood of a specific Numina they can spend any Blood Point to activate the Numina path for the seen, there is a variant of this Discipline rank for each Path/Numina Ability. wile this Discipline Rank is in effect they are effected as if they had Blood from a Donor holding the ability in their Blood, so far only Numina held in Life have bean able to be "Re-Awakened"
