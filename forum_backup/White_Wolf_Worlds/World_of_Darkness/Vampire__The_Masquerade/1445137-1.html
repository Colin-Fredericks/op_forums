    They both sound fine. Really, being a Gary Stu in these games is, in my experience, less about basic concepts and more about whether you are willing to roleplay weaknesses and accept bad things happening to your character without bitching out the ST. I've seen people with completely reasonable concepts lose their shit because the ST gave them a stain. And I've seen people with concepts that sounded like they snuck something crazy past the ST on character creation actually be wonderful players because they were fully committed to roleplaying the weaknesses and complications inherent in their out-there concept.

        Originally posted by Lian View Post
        (I've never really understood why Nos get a pass at being the internet clan.. they have no real aptitudes for it.)
        It's not about aptitudes, it's about them being the only clan that has managed to make information-socialism work. As individuals, Nos are indeed inferior to both Malks and Tremere at information gathering, but as a clan, Nos win this. Nosferatu freely give each other information, with the understanding that while Nos A may not profit from information he gives to Nos B, one day Nos B will freely give information to Nos A, and Nos A will profit then.

        That's not something that happens with the Tremere, who hoard secrets as they attempt to climb over each other reaching for the top of the pyramid.

        Malks are likely as not to confuse the information they gathered with the voices in their heads, and may not be able to communicate it in a clear and concise manner.

        Socialism, even a clan-specific information-socialism, is inconceivable for the other clans. Nosferatu are unique in having made it work.
