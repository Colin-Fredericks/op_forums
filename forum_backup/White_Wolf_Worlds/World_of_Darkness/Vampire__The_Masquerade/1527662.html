    Progressive Policies are pro.....vampire?

    12-31-2023, 04:15 PM
    A recent discussion with a player about how vampires would influence mortal society ended up leading to a strange realization about which side of the aisle they would lend their influence to. Usually vampires are seen as the wealthy elite who control society from the shadows for their own amusement or gain, consuming the lives of the common man to sate their hunger for power, blood, and money. As such most people I have spoken to about this default to the assumption that vampires would back only conservative policies. But when you take examples of real world policies and apply them to vampiric interests, conservative policies don't help vampires much if at all.

    Vampiric interests being the ability to keep themselves hidden, the ability to feed, maintain safety/security, and the ability to acquire & maintain wealth.

    Progressive examples we came up with:
    • Light pollution is a problem so we should have fewer street lamps, the reduction will also mean less energy used every night. Environmental policy which makes hunting easier for vampires.
    • The police are corrupt and fascist, thus they should be defunded. Political policy which means vampires can act up with much less chance of cops being able to respond or effectively patrol areas.
    • Gun violence is a problem; thus second amendment rights should be suspended and concealed carry permits are restricted. Much less likely that prey will be armed, hunters will have a much harder time acquiring weapons or running around with them.
    • People who are arrested for a crime should be given mental health screening to make sure they get the help they need. A policy which allows anyone charged with a crime to bypass the normal criminal legal system and be put in a mental health facility without trial. Then a ghoul doctor can give a false diagnosis or a vampire with Dominate or Dementation can produce mental illness to ensure the person is locked up.
    • Lobby for family court to favor single parent households over nuclear families. The less stable of a home life children have, the greater the chances of them being somewhat broken in adulthood. This means a greater number of people who can disappear and have no one to come look for them. This means more feeding options and less trouble when embracing replacements.
    • Using social media ties to push alternative lifestyles as normal. A vampire with a couple ghouls with the Herd in tow looks remarkably similar to a polycule out on the town.
    • By pushing single parent households and non-traditional relationship models, traditional religions and their beliefs are seen as bigoted and backwards. This means fewer and fewer people believe. This weakens the power of the various churches and makes True Faith even more rare to encounter.
    • Open boarders' policy on migration means large swaths of undocumented immigrants who can be food, followers, or pawns without trace.
    • Reduce the requirements for graduation from high school so that you don't need to be proficient in math, reading, writing, history, or science in order to graduate. Theoretically this reduces the burden on people of color when it comes to graduation, I honestly don't get the logic. But as far as vampires are concerned, an uneducated humanity is far easier to control. Alternatively those mortals who teach themselves and rise to the top have the sort of skills and mindset required to be potential new embrace material.
    • Back policies which assist homeless individuals without actually solving the homeless issue. This creates a readymade scape goat for when anything crazy happens and enough "political noise" that vampire behavior is hidden from public view.

    The conservative examples we came up with:
    • Increased development of rural land surrounding cities to keep shifters further and further away.
    • Reduced taxes on businesses, though to be fair conservative and progressive business owners bellyache about this equally in my opinion.
    • Removal of inheritance taxes, every time a vampire has to fake their death and leave everything to themselves, it costs them a chunk of resources.

    An interesting example of a policy that could go either way depending on how you look at it:

    Public surveillance systems in cities. Generally conservatives are against such systems and progressives are for such systems. But at the current time facial identification software is far more accurate at recognizing people of with pale skin than people of color. This makes the progressives instantly switch sides on the topic and see it as a tool of oppression.

    The catch is that Facial ID systems would be a default masquerade threat for all vampires regardless of how much easier it would be to track and control mortals. Though since all vampires other than assamites grow more pale, then the current software would actually be very good at IDing cainites. So this policy can be pushed by pretty much any camp.


    So what sort of policies have you used in your games to push a vampire agenda?

                                                      My inclination is that vampires have influence over politics just like real world powerful people have influence over politics - they aren't usually partisan, they control both sides of the aisle and make it so that no matter which party of politician wins, they do too. There are exceptions of course. Sometime a actual anti-establishment candidate wins and sometimes you get a billionaire that is actually really partisan.

                                                      So vampires influence both (or all in nations with more major parties) and only their versions of right or left are allowed to win. Whether it's a Republican or a Democrat, they are still going to ultimately serve a blood sucker before the American people. Just like in real life, just more literally.

                                                      Though one issue that often comes up is the idea that vampires would want to influence politics to make things worse. More crime, more poverty, more corruption, and so on. This leads left leaning people to think that vampires would support right leaning politics and for right leaning people to think that vampires support left leaning politics. I don't think that this is necessarily true though.

                                                      Sure, a pack of Sabbat or a bunch of Anarch neonates might like it if there were more crime and poverty so that they could more easily get away with crimes. But are those the kinds of vampires who are going to be deciding who becomes the mayor or governor? No, certainly not. Camarilla elders, especially Ventrue, Toreador, and Tremere are going to be pulling the string there. These kinds of vampires aren't going to be on the streets committing crimes, murdering random homeless people, and other such nonsense. Those vampires are going to establish a safe and reliable method of feeding that doesn't involve random violence. Having a herd, buying from slaughter houses, getting blood from hospitals, and so on. Even relatively young vampires of less affluent Clans are unlikely to frequently engage in random crimes, at least if they're smart. By the time you become an influential elder you're never going to be getting your hands dirty like that nor would you tolerate your allies or servants doing that either.

                                                      So would these sorts of vampires want a bunch of crime, homelessness, corruption, and general chaos? I would say no. They want happy contented people who go to work, take care of their responsibilities, obey the law, and sit down in front of their TV/computer to zone out and don't ask questions or get too involved. Trouble like poverty and crime makes people pay attention, that isn't good for vampires. Shepherds don't want vicious sheep that are hungry and fighting all the time so that they are too distracted to notice humans slaughtering them. They want peaceful docile sheep that go where they are told. Of course, vampires do want corruption in that they are going to want police and politicians to do what the vampiric rulers want, they aren't going to want those police and politicians breaking laws and taking bribes all the time. These servants should generally be law abiding except in those rare cases when their true vampires call on special favors.
