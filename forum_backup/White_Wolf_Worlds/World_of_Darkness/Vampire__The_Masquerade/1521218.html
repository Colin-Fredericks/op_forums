      Jade is... well... a mineral found in the Middle Kingdom. Jade is an umbrella term that encompasses two different silicate minerals: jadeite and nephrite. Jadeite is the rarer variety and fetches the highest prices. Nephrite occurs more abundantly and usually has more affordable prices. Both types of jade can come in green, red, black, gray, brown, yellow, and white/cream colors. Jadeite can also show lavender, orange, and blue hues. Popularly associated with jade, green colors usually command the highest values. However, every color has the potential for beauty. Generally, blue is the color of jade that is the rarest because it comes from only a few deposits found in Guatemala, Japan and Myanmar. Green and lavender jade are more common.
      In Kindred of the East, when attuned with a proper ritual, the common jade becomes a living substance capable of holding Yin and Yang Chi.
      Of course, different colors of Jade have different properties.
      Black Jade (Tortoise Jade) - The Water Jade is attuned to Yin and can hold up to five points of Yin Chi. Artisans often carve it into teardrop shapes or those of Water creatures.
      Red Jade (Phoenix Jade) - This is Fire Jade and exemplifies Yang. It can hold up to five points of Yang Chi. It is often carved into fire shapes or images of Phoenixes. It is also just as likely to be left in raw shape with little carving.​
      Blue or Green Jade (Dragon Jade) - These colors of jade represent the Element Wood. It can hold both Yang and Yin Chi: up to three points of Yang and two points of Yin. It is often carved into floral shapes or those of animals of the forest.
      White Jade (Tiger Jade) - Exemplifying the Element of Metal, Tiger Jade can hold up to three points of Yin and two points of yang Chi. It is often carved into martial shapes and is a popular adornment for pommel grips of weapons, even pistol grips.
      Yellow or Grey Jade (​Mandarin Jade) - This jade, representing Earth, is balanced and the most valuable because it can hold either type of Chi up to five points. It is often carved to represent noble beasts or the trappings of kingship like fans or scepters.
      Yu Ying Jade - The Perfection of Jade - a rare form of Jade that bestows eternal life.

      The Jade Talisman Backgrounbd from the Kindred of the East book exactly this
      You own a talisman of true jade, jade attuned to the energies of Yin or Yang. Such a talisman can come in any shape or form — perhaps it’s a figurine, a ring, a circlet, or even the guard of a sword. Talismans attuned to Yin tend to be fashioned from black or white jade, while talismans attuned to Yang are typically made of green, red, or blue jade. In any event, the talisman can store energies from either the Yin or Yang World (never both), then offer them to its wielder as the appropriate type of Chi. Whenever a jade talisman is an area where the Wall is weak (6 or lower), the player can choose to make a roll for the talisman, using a dice pool equal to the number of dots in this Background. The difficulty of this roll equals the Wall rating + 3. For each success, the talisman may absorb one Chi Point from the region’s ambient Chi. Only one attempt to absorb Chi may be made per night, and a talisman may hold a maximum number of Chi Points equal to the dots in the Background (so, a three-dot jade talisman allows three dice to absorb Chi and may store a maximum of three Chi points at once). Subsequently, by touching the talisman and spending a turn in concentration, the vampire may utilize this Chi. Chi from a talisman may be used in normal fashion, to power Disciplines, heal wounds, etc. The vampire may still spend only as much Chi per turn as his Dharma rating allows. Talismans of true jade are exceedingly rare and greatly prized. You may have to defend your talisman against rapacious rivals. Occasionally, talismans are used as prizes in duels and gambling contests, but these contests must be of great consequence to merit such a stake.
      • One Chi Point
      •• Two Chi Points
      ••• Three Chi Points
      •••• Four Chi Points
      ••••• Five Chi Points​

      Land of Eight Million Dreams detailed the idea about Jade weapons; just as the Kithain are sensitive to Cold Iron, the shinma/hsien, the "Little Gods" can be sensitive to Jade. If the proper ritual awakens the chi of the jade it can cause aggravated wounds to the hsien.
      It should be stated that even ordinary mortals, even with no training, can attune jade through prayer and meditation. Jade is widely known to be of protection against spirits and the hisiem are still spirits even if they are trapped in human bodies.
      The Shih (the demon hunters of the Middle Kingdom) know this and often carry jade weapons.
      However, jade is also a power item for hsien - it can increase their magic, and many powerful treasures are made of Jade. Slightly paradoxical - but it works. Basically what it comes down to is - Jade has power. Anything with power is a two edged sword, this is why they have Jade Talisman: True Jade in Land of Eight Million Dreams
      Hengeyokai: Shapeshifters of the East mentions a few Jade Fetish or Talens but actually these are like other Werewolf things, somethat that is made out of Jade and atuned with a ritual or gift.

      In short, Jade is... well jade somebody properly atuned, you can buy it anywhere as a mineral, have an artisan to carve it and if you know the ritual, atune it
      The confusion​ comes from Wraith: The Oblivion:
      White Jade of Wraith is a "Jade" manufactured from the plasm of ghosts in the Yellow Springs. It is used to manufacture Artifacts in the same way that soulforging is used in Stygia. White Jade is made through a specific skill in the Way of the Artisan arcanos, and can only be created out of a wraith drained of all Willpower. Artifacts constructed from White Jade are less durable than artifacts constructed from True Jade, but it is far easier to acquire and therefore far cheaper.
      True Jade is is a more solid and durable form of Jade found in the Dark Kingdom of Jade, it is basically Relic Jade - a relic is a memory given shape in the Underworld that can survive for centuries. In this case some Jade. But compared to the white Jade made from plasm, even with the added relegious and cultural significance of Jade... it is rare. Very rare. Not to mention It is similar to Stygian Steel and causes aggravated damage...

      EDIT:
      Personally I tend to use a bit of a hosue rule to seperate the two kind of True Jade
      The Wraith True Jade is a relic of a Jade
      The "mortal" True Jade is a Jade that Jade spirit been "awakened" with a ritual

                  Yes
                  The power of jade for any shen, particularly a Kuei-jin, cannot be overemphasized. Jade acts as a lodestone for meditative and spiritual energies, as a receptacle for elements of the soul and as a tangible connection to the Yin and Yang Worlds.
                  For example from the Companion, Taste of Jade, a Lvl 8 Jade Shintai power attune a piece of ordinary jade to Chi energies, turning it into a piece of True Jade for a time. Kuei-jin who have mastered this power are frequently adorned with jade from head to toe, and each piece of green stone is a focus for powerful Chi energies that they can call upon with only a thought.

                  The Imbuing the Jade ritual enables Kuei-jin to imbue jade with properties that allow the gem’s possessor to cross into the spirit worlds or utilize dragon lines. The vampire crafts jade replicas — tiny likenesses of the Kuei-jin — for use when following in the crab’s footsteps, whereas she carves small boats for use when riding the dragon’s tongue.

                  ​Jade is very special material in the Middle Kingdom. Jade is a "Soul Substance". Vapable if protecting the soul from the various elements. It is generally considered ti bring luck. Jade also preserves life, extends life, and purifies the male spirit. The colour and quality of jade can make differences in how i can be used, as deffense or even as a weapon.
                  To "attune" a Jade usually you need to spend a Yin or Yang Chi.

                  Blue and Green Jade is used for defense, it requires spending of one Yin Chi in order to properly ward an entrance with blue or green jade.
                  Yellow and Gray Jade is usable either deffense or attack; either color adds damage to a hand-to-hand strike against a shen (collective term for supernatural beings) if the holder expends one Yin Chi. The attacker rolls Wits + Occult (difficulty 7); for each success the aggressor inflicts one health level of lethal damage. For defenses, the holder spends one Yang Chi and the player rolls Wits + Occult (difficulty 7). Each success negates one health level of mystical damage (Disciplines, the claws of shen, etc.) from one attack.
                  White Jade is used for warding against the shen by atuning with Yang Chi - ghost even take damage if try to enter a warded area
                  Black Jade is a weapon and requires Yin Chi to awaken. Once awakened, the stone causes aggravated damage on all attacks against shen for one night.
                  Red Jade brings luck, by spending a Yin and a Yang Chi to empower the red jade. Once per story the character wearing empowered red jade can reroll a botch in either offensive or defensive combat. Empowered red jade remains "awakened" indefinitely though, at the Storyteller's discretion, it may need another sacrifice of life force in order to bring luck.
                  Yu Ying Jade is awesome, for 2 Yin Chi and 2 Yang Chi to dedicate the jade to you, it returns the favor one hundredfold. - a bit hyperbole, but it indeed replenish the individual's Chi by absorbing latent Chi from the world

                          Simple
                          Relic is a term used by Wraiths to describe objects across the Shroud who are not formed by souls - things that inspired great passion in life, and which linger on in death.
                          No longer in existence in the Skinlands, a relic is a memory given shape in the Underworld that can survive for centuries. It is a term that can apply to any item or device which crosses the Shroud with an Enfant, and they can be anything of value to that specific wraith, but usually have a symbolic tie to an individual or group of individuals.
                          Most relics are ideals in some fashion and are generally tied to a wraith's self-image and may have special capacities associated with that.
                          These items are a significant source of wealth in Underworld society, and are commonly bartered between the dead.
                          Some of the most valuable relics have also been fashioned into artifacts.
                          The reverse object of a relic is a phylactery from Mummy: The Resurrection; something in the world of the living into which a mummy can bind the power of the underworld, quasi an earthly manifestation of a relic.
                          Now White Jade (Tiger Jade) is a kind of Tremolite Jade which is a variety of Nephrite, that's usually white, off-white, or cream in colour. White Jade is a gemstone that holds a lot of meaning and plenty of healing properties. White Jade has been used with this purpose for hundreds of years. It is believed to have a calming and soothing effect on the mind and body, promoting peace, harmony, and tranquility. It is often used in meditation practices and is thought to help individuals achieve a greater sense of awareness and spiritual awakening.
                          BUT
                          The White Jade of Wraith:the Oblivion, the Dark Kingdom of Jade is a "Jade" manufactured from the plasm of ghosts in the Yellow Springs. It is used to manufacture Artifacts in the same way that soulforging is used in Stygia. White Jade is made through a specific skill in the Way of the Artisan arcanos, and can only be created out of a wraith drained of all Willpower. Artifacts constructed from White Jade are less durable than artifacts constructed from True Jade, but it is far easier to acquire and therefore far cheaper.
                          The True Jade of Wraith:the Oblivion, the Dark Kingdom of Jade is a more solid and durable form of Jade found in the Dark Kingdom of Jade, it is basically Relic Jade
                          BUT (again^^)
                          There is a "mortal" True Jade, like how there is a mortal world' White Jade and with Taste of Jade, a Lvl 8 Jade Shintai power you can attune a piece of ordinary jade to Chi energies, turning it into a piece of True Jade for a time.
                          Obviously the power won't turn the ordinary Jade in to a Relic for a time
                          Actually it is stated in the Companion as with any other wraithly matter, relics and artifacts of the Underworld cannot survive in the Land of the Living; if you try to bring over such object? It simply disperses in a puff of insubstantial plasm.
                          So this two are two different things in my humble opinion

                              Simple
                              Relic is a term used by Wraiths to describe objects across the Shroud who are not formed by souls - things that inspired great passion in life, and which linger on in death.
                              No longer in existence in the Skinlands, a relic is a memory given shape in the Underworld that can survive for centuries. It is a term that can apply to any item or device which crosses the Shroud with an Enfant, and they can be anything of value to that specific wraith, but usually have a symbolic tie to an individual or group of individuals.
                              Most relics are ideals in some fashion and are generally tied to a wraith's self-image and may have special capacities associated with that.
                              These items are a significant source of wealth in Underworld society, and are commonly bartered between the dead.
                              Some of the most valuable relics have also been fashioned into artifacts.
                              The reverse object of a relic is a phylactery from Mummy: The Resurrection; something in the world of the living into which a mummy can bind the power of the underworld, quasi an earthly manifestation of a relic.

It is believed to have a calming and soothing effect on the mind and body, promoting peace, harmony, and tranquility. It is often used in meditation practices and is thought to help individuals achieve a greater sense of awareness and spiritual awakening.
