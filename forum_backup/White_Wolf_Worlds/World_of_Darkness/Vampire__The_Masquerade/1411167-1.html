                                                        Wow, thanks for sharing! I never had the chance to look at the mechanical aspect of each of the 10th level power.

                                                        And oh boy, they are all over the place!

                                                        Some powers like, Animalism, Auspex, Dominate, Necromancy, Obfuscate, and presence seems strong, but are pretty interesting. I think i like to dominate the most. The only thing i dislike a bit about it is the mental link, which should, imo, is too ‘’Auspex-like’’.

                                                        Daimonon 10th dot is just a plot device, so i wonder why make it a power.

                                                        Obtenebration and Chemistry are very scary, but as 10th level power, i think they are fine.

                                                        Vicissitude 10 just feels like Buu from Dragonball, but it guess it’s also fine for a 10th-level discipline.

                                                        Serpentis feels cool thematically, but mechanically it just feels plain weird. Why is it limited to attacking? Imagine the bearer of the mark of damnation walking into a daycare getting attacked by a bunch of children, or into a mall getting attacked by regular folks. IMO, it would work better if it made people shun the bearer instead of straight up attacking at all times.

                                                        Thaumathurgy starts playing with things i think vampires should have never been able to deal with. The Sun and fire are supposed to be a weakness, having a power completely nullify it just feels wrong to me. At worst, it should have been a fortitude power instead. Then again, i might be a hypocrite, cause i’m ifne with Requiem's mystery of the ascendant.

                                                        I had heard of protean 10 before, and while it’s weaker than what i had been told, this one is just plain silly to me.

                                                        Valeren and Obeah feel okay thematically, but kinda weak. I can see the appeal thematically, but it’s so limited and situational that it just feels pretty ‘’meh’’. I just can’t see an Antediluvian really caring about resurrecting mortals.

                                                        Finally, Quietus. That one made me laugh out loud, it is so unbearably weak compared to the others. There's an indestructible vampire, a vampire commanding animal armies, a vampire with a will so strong they can rewrite someone else whole personality, a vampire able to banish someone into literal oblivion, and then there’s a vampire who’s mildly better at blood bounding… In Requiem, this power is available to an Ordo Dracul with the 3rd dot of the mystery of the Voivode, which means a character could start with it.

                                                        Anyway, thanks for sharing, that was a fun read!
