    Voodoo. Setites and Samedi

    07-06-2024, 09:13 PM
    Reading though new Orleans by night it discusses how the Setites have used voodoo as a way to get into New Orleans even with heavy resistance by the camarilla. This of course was before the development of the Samedi who also I suppose would be likely to use voodoo in New Orleans.

    I am prepping my v5 new Orleans campaign and I feel like this would make a great focal point for friction. There is even a Samedi level four ability for the Samedi lore sheet that allows for "my Setite friend."

    However, I don't really have enough familiarity with voodoo to really explore a good way to create a good dynamic. I want an incompetent camarilla beset by anarchs led by setites and hecata led by Samedi both groups in conflict over their natural voodoo base.

    Help me brainstorm some ideas for this conflict or give me some good information on voodoo politics!

      It was before the development of the Samedi, and also before the development of the Serpents of Light. I'd imagine that the Setites who used voudon or voodoo ended up peeling away from the main clan, becoming the Serpents of Light and joining the Sabbat. This would have largely moved the traditional Setites out of New Orleans and gave the Sabbat a solid base within the city. Of course, keep in mind that the Serpents of Light aren't hardcore Sabbat members. Their membership in the sect was mostly to serve as protection from the Setites annihilating them. They do have the ultimate goal of killing the Setite Antideluvian, so there's at least some overlap between their longterm goal and the Sabbats.

      In the setting of V5 (which I assume you're looking at due to the mention of the Hecata), I would imagine most of the Serpents of Light would have left the Sabbat and joined up with the Anarchs and Ministry. Again, they were never hardcore Sabbat members, and it's hard for me to imagine them destroying their cults and powerbases in order to become a bunch of roving wanderers. There might be a few Serpents of Light left in the Sabbat, but the majority of the Serpents of Light would unite with the Ministry, which would be more than happy to welcome non-Set worshippers.

      This would set New Orleans up into a really crazy place. You'd probably have a handful of traditional Followers of Set trying to take down the Serpents of Light and Ministry. You'd have some Sabbat in the city also gunning for the Serpents of Light. With the Hecata, there's probably some major tension between the Samedi and the Serpents of Light. At the same time, keep in mind that the Harbringers of Skulls abandoned the Sabbat to join the Hecata, so there's bad blood between the Hecata and the Sabbat.

      And then you have the Camarilla, which ostensibly claims to control the city and even has a Prince there, but the reality is, New Orleans is a city split between the Camarilla, the Hecata, the Anarchs, and then you have the Sabbat moving in to cause trouble and even other tertiary groups like the mainline Followers of Set who are hunting down members of the Ministry.

      So you've probably got this sort of cold-war situation between the Anarchs (specifically the Serpents of Light/Ministry who would be the main leaders of the Sect in the city), the Hecata, and the Camarilla which occasionally goes hot when someone crosses or kills a member of the other faction. And all 3 groups are hated by the Sabbat, so when the Sabbat shows up they may temporarily put aside their differences and team together to put them down. At the same time, if any conflict between them breaks out, the Sabbat is going to move in to cause as much chaos as possible. In fact, I imagine the Sabbat focuses pretty hard to trying to cause trouble for one faction, while framing a different faction, hoping to se New Orleans collapse into out and out war between the Camarilla, Anarchs and Hecata.

          New Orleans By Night was published in 1994. The Samedi bloodline was introduced in the Vampire Players Guide 2e in 1993. So the Samedi actually predate NOBN.

          While the Setite involvement in voodoo cults was quite early (detailed in 1992's A World of Darkness), the original description of the Samedi doesn't make any use of voodoo. Of course the name is obviously derived from Baron Samedi, but he's not mentioned at all. We are told that the Samedi appeared in Italy and the Caribbean (and only a few members in North America at all). The mention of the Caribbean leans towards voodoo, but many Caribbean islands don't have that religion and Italy certainly doesn't. As originally written, people might have thought we were not meant to take the Samedi as being any more involved with voodoo than we are to assume the Ahrimanes were Zoroastrians.

          But in 1994's Kindred Most Wanted, we are introduced to the Baron as the sire of Genina. We are also told of the Baron's "sister" Brigette. That the Haitian slaves spread the rumor that the French transported Baron Samedi from Africa to Haiti. and that after the outbreak of the Haitian War of Independence the Setites "took" the Baron and Brigette. "Took" is a very neutral term, and I don't think the rest of Genina's chapter refers back to them.

          That the Baron had a "sister" (likely meaning they had the same vampire sire) with a French name could imply that she sent the French ships to Africa that took the Baron to a French colony in the New World (perhaps he had recently woken from torpor or was just hiding there). If so, then the Baron's only real involvement with voodoo was probably using the name as an alias to strike fear. He may not even had intended to use it, but was given that name by terrified Haitians who encountered or witnessed him, and they immediately thought he was their Baron.

          Despite the Setites "taking" the Baron and Brigette, nothing bad seemed to happen to them. The Setites didn't kill them or imprison them. Most likely they probably just let them go, or even helped them move somewhere else, likely for a large bribe, favors, or boons.

          I think that's about all we know from the original canon. Don't know how Revised or later edition developed things. But originally it did not seem that the bloodline had all that much to do with voodoo. Now you don't need to keep to this in your chronicle, but it might be useful to know.

          So where does that leave us in wanting to use Setites and Samedi and actual voodoo?

          You have the real voodoo - the actual real world religion. By itself, it has nothing to do with any vampire or other WoD supernatural creatures.

          Then you have the Setite temple cults that merely use the cover of voodoo to corrupt people and turn them into Herd, minions, etc. The Setites might portray themselves as some kind of manifestation of the loa, a particularly powerful houngan, or someone unknown manipulating things behind the scenes.

          Then you have the Samedi, which despite their name, have only tenuous connections with voodoo except that the Baron found it convenient to masquerade as the loa.

          You also have, in World of Darkness terms, the wraiths connected to the Mirrorlands and the Island Below the Sea (the Dark Kingdom of Haitian/voodoo wraiths).

          If I was going to have any ongoing connection with the Samedi and voodoo, I'd probably lean into this WoD version of the Island Below the Sea and associated wraiths. The Samedi have the powers to deal with them. And you can easily justify some of them having information and connection to them. So they have allies and friends with those wraiths (developed for the purposes of helping them avoid the Giovanni wraiths), and those wraiths may not like what the Setites are doing to their people. In this scenario, the Samedi bloodline likely doesn't have much involvement in actual voodoo (although individual Samedi might). But as allies to the wraiths, they need to back their play or they'll lose important allies.

          Just some ideas for you.
