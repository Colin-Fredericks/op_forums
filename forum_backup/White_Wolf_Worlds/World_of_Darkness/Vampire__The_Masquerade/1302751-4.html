                        I think if we restrict this purely to physical combat, and disciplines/gifts that raise your combat ability directly, and ignore all the "autowin" disciplines/gifts (I mean both splats have at higher levels enough powers of the type that if you optimise for them and go first in combat your opponent is done) I think the point where Vampires really get something that could pushes them in physical combat way above Werewolfs would be 6.                             Flower of Death is only for hand to hand, though, so you'd have to risk coming within arm's reach of a character with a staggeringly higher Strength (say, by using Gift of the Spriggan or Warp-Fury* for the most mid-tribe of all) and getting grappled. Spirit of the Fray means that they can wait for you to do this and interrupt you. Several tribes can also just flat out wait for you to waste massive amounts of blood trying to hit them while Strike the Air is engaged, and you'd need all the extra dice from Flower of Death just to try hitting a high-Rank Stargazer (literally nothing beats Circular Attack now, it can flat out give you Exalted-style perfect dodges in addition to being able to just redirect an attack to someone else).

                            Of course, range is also a possible Achilles' heel for the lick you've proposed. Most of these solutions are Rank 3 or higher, but they're also in the style of "dump damage on target, they have to deal with it", like Electroshock, which can be targeted from far off and rather unobtrusively just as long as the Glass Walker can connect a circuit. Red Talons, in contrast, can just howl at you if they can see you, and you take levels of lethal. Not agg, but they don't have to spend anything to do it.

                            There's also Gift of the Giants, which allows Garou to Ultraman up to any size to become bigger than the biggest thing in the scene. A wonderfully versatile trick from Apocalypse that can be used in response to anything with higher physical stats than you to get higher stats than them. The only downside is that you're unlikely to learn it readily outside end of the world scenarios, where you typically have more important enemies to fight, like Grandmother Thunderwyrm or the zmei.

                            So, yeah, there may be something from Vampire that outstrips Garou combat capacities, but it's not Flower of Death.

                            *See Werewolf: the Wyld West. The tl;dr is that it's a potential one way ride that injures the Garou significantly at the end but boy does it pack a punch before that, basically doubling Crinos stats, and then some for Dexterity.

İ had the time to raise my attributes to 7 with blood and activate Serpentis 1 to 4, so physical attributes were at 9, and the tail had Strength 12. Note that the VDA20 manual has no rule that attributes can be raised to 10 max, and transformations were considered exceptions by the ST anyway, even with the max10 rule.

                                            Wolf has Spirit of the Frey, so he attacks first, trying to tackle me to throw me from the castle walls where we were fighting. Difficulty 8 because İ am armed with sword and shield. He fails. İ grab him with my tail (Serpentis 4), rolling 15 dice (the tail has Strength 12 instead of 9, plus Brawl which was 3 back then) and with Potence. İt was difficulty 7 İ think, because of declaring multiple actions, and 1 less dice, and İ think İ made some 7 success total. He's grabbed (hold). He had spent Rage to get 2 extra actions. He uses one to try and activate the Falling Touch gift thing, which fails (had it succeeded İ would have argued that no physical law or game rule implies that falling forces one to release holds, so he might have fallen with me). With the second he tries to break my hold. He fails critically. The ST does not like to roll a thousand dice, so he declared that İ succeeded without rolling at my next action that turn (biting him for blood with Typhonic Maw, drawing 5 blood points) given the critical failure. He had that gift to do aggravated damage with his blood, but İ soaked it all thanks to Stamina 9 and Serpentis 3. As houserule, ST makes me roll for frenzy when drinking the blood. İ fail, so İ frenzy. Given the hold, the critical failure, the lack of other relevant gifts, and the statistical unlikeliness of him breaking the hold somehow, amd his being already half exsanguinated, the ST had the fight end there: next turn just had me finish drinking him and that's it.
                                            Later a coterie mate arrived, İ attacked him in frenzy but he saved himself after some damage thanks to Rego Motus, and then Vladimir Rustovich arrived and complimented me for the swift kill.

                                            Rolls could have gone otherwise, İ don't have his stats either - only know he had the Troll Skin gift for armour beyond those mentioned - and maybe he was statted with a bit less xp than me because he was the enemy of another coterie member with less xp, but this is how it went.

                                            We're currently going against more werewolves and one of our plans - maybe the favoured one - is to make them frenzy through a Malkavian and provocation and then İ take them all if they don't attack each other, depending on their rage and gnosis scores.
                                            Without gifts due to frenzy, the worst they can have is be in crinos with Strength 9, Dext 6, Stam 8 and Brawl 5, and +4 to my difficulties due to multiple opponents. We did the maths and by raising my Stamina, doing a clinch with the tail to one of them every two turns (difficulty 10 but they don't dodge in frenzy and Potence gives me 5 automatic successes) - which deals enough damage to fill the track with bashing damage and making him lose consciousness for at least one turn - and killing him off with the tongue or bite to top up blood (he's unconscious, so no need to roll to hit at diff. 10) or with a squeeze of the clinch (no roll and enough damage to end him without Rage rolls to keep fighting) in the other turn, İ can go forever killing one every 2 turns until the ST gets bored of rolling. The Stamina can be raised and kept raised to a level where they statistically can't make damage, and that's based on a fully optimised though frenzying Garou. They roll 10 dice to hit at diff 8 (wielding 2 great swords, so +2 diff to hit and -1 die too for reach) or 9 (if the Garou unconscious in my tail counts as some kind of shield) to hit and Strength 9 + the claw or bite damage (can't do anything else in berserker frenzy). İ don't dodge, but soak with ever increasing Stamina (need to keep spending 1 blood point on it per turn to prevent it from dropping) at diff 5 (Serpentis 3) with specialisation. İ can frenzy due to their blood, but it won't change my simple actions.
                                            The point is that low gen, experienced and optimised vampires can reach situations like this and hold off a raging werewolf army.

Rage extra actions, for example, would probably have to change significantly. As it stands, I and every werewolf do 1 action with multiple actions penalties (+1 difficulty, -1 dice) unless they do not declare multiple actions; then every werewolf does all their rage actions, no penalty; then I and every werewolf do our multiple actions with increasing penalties (each one higher in difficulty by 1, and -1 more dice). Multiple actions (which are distinct from Rage, but not from VDA20 Celerity) cannot be used to do more than 1 attack unless you have things like extra limbs (Serpentis 2 gives you 1 extra tongue attack per turn, and Serpentis 4 one extra tail attack) or Velocity 3+ (your attack actions, instead of being the default 1, are half your Velocity dots rounded up), and penalties still apply (but Velocity mitigates them to some extent).

Granted, some may have low Rage ratings and not frenzy because they just don't have enough dice to roll, but we're attacking a sept, not a pack, so even those in berserker frenzy with higher gnosis than rage will not attack their packmates, but will attack members of other packs. Before I've even left Obfuscate to join the fray and give them a target, they're gonna be at each other's throat. This may not be how Garou fight when they're in control, but here they are not.
