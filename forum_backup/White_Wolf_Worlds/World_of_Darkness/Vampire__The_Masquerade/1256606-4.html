        Originally posted by Aleph View Post
        And the whole point here it's that vampires skulking with flashlights and candles it's anticlimatic, not that it's necessary. It's just silly to play a night predator that has problems seeing at night.
        If there is too much darkness in your chronicle that you need powers to see in the dark, hopefully that is intentional design by your Storyteller for the mood he's trying to create, otherwise he's just doing a bad job and punishing you for not being able to see in the dark. And you should get a different storyteller.

        According to Vampire lore, it seems likely that vampires received disciplines after the curse, NOT because of it. If this is the case, the 'Curse of Caine' is fitting as it banishes you to darkness in a world where there is only torchlight to see by which you are terrified of. And your fellow man who might keep you compay in this new dark world? You eat him. Eternally banished to walk the earth in darkness and eat people, and to be afraid of the only light source you are able to use... THAT is a punishment. That is a Dark Ages kind of damnation. And to top it all off, you're hungry. The hunger never ends. So no, I don't think that anything compensates for the curse. With the disciplines coming after the curse, being given by another source, the point of the curse was just that. To curse. God gave no compensation or mercy.

        To be clear, I'm not arguing for players to be punished while in the dark. My point seems to be missed. I'm just arguing why the it seems logical that the curse of Caine would exclude vampires from being able to naturally see in the dark. I'm rationalizing here BECAUSE vampires CAN'T see in the dark naturally. My arguments stem from being able to strategically use darkness in storytelling for mood, and for lore and role play reasons. Makes sense to me. Maybe it does't make sense to everyone.

            Humans have been able to operate at night pretty well ever since fire was "tamed". Maybe the day's toil in the field ended when the sun went down, but once you could light a hut with a flame, you could keep doing things. As other people have said, you can move about and travel at night pretty easily without an artificial light source. Maybe human eyes are not specialised to cope with low light, but they can. They may not be great for hunting animals at night but hunting other people is perfectly achievable.

            If you can't cope with moving about in a darkened house slowly and steathily, that's a personal failing. Let your eyes adjust and move slowly, most people can do it if they actually try.

            As for nosferatu, i've always pictured them having lights down there. The pitch dark entrance to the warren is part of what puts people off coming down. I don't think they actually live in the shit and piss, they most likely have a dry comfortable haven somewhere down there.

            This peculiar nightsight thing seems to be one of the more "Vampions" issues that i have seen.
