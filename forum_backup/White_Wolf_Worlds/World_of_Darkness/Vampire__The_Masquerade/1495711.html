    Redesigning Psychomachia

    10-27-2022, 11:52 PM
    Psychomachia is the introductory adventure to the 1st Edition sourcebook "Milwaukee by Night", published in 1992 and the second "by night" book after Chicago.
    In its own words, "Psychomachia is a psychological thriller and a detective story all wrapped up in once...".
    It is one of the most railroaded stories in the history of RPG's. I remember a critic saying that it's probably the only adventure which relies in PC's being captured TWICE. It also features a crazy old lady hunter with a flamethrower, a lot of inconsequential combat, NPC's with no other motivation than moving the story forward and as much player agency as a chess match between Saulot and Tremere.
    On the other hand, it has some amazing elements that really stand out as some of the best jewels I have ever seen in RPG's supplements. The first one is a crime scene so chilling and well crafted that could have been included in the film Se7en. https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven_(1995_film)
    The second one is a string of collective "dream sequences" that explore in deep the personal horror of being a vampire and what are probably the PC's deepest fears.
    And the third one is the story plot twist, which will be explained later to avoid unintended spoilers.
    As a result, I have always wanted to run an adaptation of this story, and have recently done it in both my tables, with a great success (my players have told it's one of the best games they have ever had). So I wanted to share my experiences and the changes I have made to make this work.

      So, what changes do this story need? These are the most important ones I have identified:
      1. More detective work. This is supposed to be a detective story, so the PC's need to do some investigation. Instead of a vague "there is a vampire murderer in town threatening the Masquerade", I created a string of previous murders for the PC's to investigate. They should be able to visit the crime scenes, read police and autopsy reports and interrogate witnesses. Among a lot of useless or redundant info, there should be some hard to spot clues about the identity of the murderer.
      2. More player agency. This is an RPG, so players decisions should be relevant to the outcome. The PC's can't really defeat the villain on their own, but they can find enough evidence to convince the city elders to do it. Or speak the right words to make the villain kill himself. Or even die...
      3. A more trimmed and focused plot. There are elements in Psychomachia that add nothing to the plot. More specifically, the hunter and the Lupines are a waste of space. The dead Justicar is a forgettable joke. Instead, the story should link with existing local plots and politics, to help with point 2.
      4. Less combat. This is both optional and adaptable to your game and player group, but I think this story can be run with almost no combat at all
      5. More moral choices. This is a story about exploring the nature of vampires and their Beast. The story should present some hard choices for the PC's to make.
      6. No forced captures. This should be obvious. The first one is completely removable as it adds nothing at all to the story. The second one should be optional, and the PC's should be able to avoid it if they have done their detective work properly.

          Now, the first specific example: Cairo 2022 chronicle.

          Warning: Spoilers Ahead

          I run the story with the same basic plot and intent as the original one: the Prince has killed his loved one and fallen into Wassail but is still managing to keep a facade of self control, which he uses to send the PC's on a hunt for the crimes he has been recently committing under the influence of the Beast. As for the intent, this is a introductory story designed to have a "fight for the Princedom" chronicle, in which PC's have a lot of agency despite being Neonates; so, there was an emphasis to showcase the city's factions, current conflicts and intrigues.

          Part 1. The Prince's request.
          The PC's are three Neonates (a Nosferatu, a Malkavian and an Ashirra Lasombra) who don't know each other. After a brief personal intro they are summoned by the Prince and tasked with solving the vampire murders and finding his missing lover, a Ventrue Fledgling. They are given a folder with all the police and autopsy reports obtained by the Prince's contacts and permission to investigate the Fledgling's quarter in the palace; they find his laptop, crack it and get access to his schedule the nights before his disappearance (they don't know the Prince has had those files tampered with).

          Part 2. Following the trail of the Fledgling.
          The PC's backtrack the Prince's lover last known movements. They get involved in political intrigue between two Ventrue Elders in the Opera House, have to use favours to get help from a Toreador and end up meeting a stranded Sabbat pack in the Cities of the Dead. The pack offers them to join in the hunt for werewolves, but they refuse. They end up finding out the Fledgling was never there, but his bodyguard, one of the Prince's ghouls, was. That will be a very important point later

              Part 3. More detective work.
              While they get their contacts to work on finding more about the ghoul, the PC's switch gears and go investigating the crime scenes. First they enter the Hajj district, where they are chased off by a Nosferatu fanatic in the first "combat scene" of the story. Later they go to the new city to investigate another scene and they make another breakthrough: the murderer used a high level Quietus power. That greatly reduced the pool of suspects and really helped them later.

              Part 4. The "diner scene".
              My favourite scene from the story. The PC's received an anonymous tip telling them of a freshly committed murder just a few minutes from where they are (no coincidence here, the car they have been using all the time had been bugged by the murderer). They arrive on time to find clues. The Malk uses Spirit's Touch, spends a Willpower point and ends up rolling 4 successes. Case solved, they now know the Prince is the murderer. I even introduced a whole insert on what happened in the Prince's head, and how he has made a last attempt to redeem himself and save his city from the Beast by recruiting the PC's as a last resort to stop him.
              The players are shocked and panicked, as they realise they have been fooled all along and have a massive target on their backs. But they also have a last task they feel obliged to fulfill for honor's, humanity's and the Masquerade's sake.

                          Part 5. Against all odds.
                          The PC's realize they have been ambushed and manage to run away before the police (alerted by the Prince) arrives to the crime scene. But some of them have been captured by CCTV and they hear in the news that the police is looking to question them. They have to hide, change Havens, etc... They decide not to get rid of the bugged car, not to tip the Prince on what they know and still pretend they don't suspect him, in order to win time, a great move that it will rewarded later. The Lasombra had bought a level 3 Mentor and this was the right time to use the background. They tell her the story and she advises then to contact the Banu Haquim city Elder, as he is one of the few kindred able to slay the Prince. If they can convince him, they may stand a chance. They first have to show some evidence to the Elder's Childe to get an audience and then access his Haven, a mosque with a huge ambient Faith which the Elder hasn't left in decades. The Malk manages to get in and the Elder reads his mind. He's convinced of the Prince's crimes, but tells them he won't act without some legal sanctioning. They are tasked with convincing either the two remaining Ashirra spiritual leaders or six out of eleven of the Prince's Counsellors. Considering the Ashirra leaders won't be very willing despite being only two, they decide to go the civil route. Again a good choice, as one of the Ashirra leaders is the Prince's spiritual advisor and a completely stubborn guy who had been almost impossible to convince.

                              Part 6. Political maneuvering.
                              The PC's have to embark in a race against the clock to convince four more councillors that the Prince has gone crazy (they already had Lasombra and Banu Haquim) before the week the Prince gave them to catch the killer runs out and they are summoned back to palace to present their findings.
                              They decide to target the "sensible" councillors first, the ones that may convinced with evidence. They have meetings and manage to track the Prince's ghoul, until they capture him (second combat); some of his memories have been wiped out but he still remembers the Prince killing his lover; they keep him safe to present him in the Council meeting. Finally, they also meet the (rumoured) leader of an anti Prince conspiracy and the politically motivated Councillors, trying to shore up support before the meeting.

                                  A secret meeting is called to discuss the Prince's removal.                                           So, how were my six principles applied to the Cairo 2022 version of Psychomachia and how did that work?
                                          1. There was a lot of detective work. It provided some clues that were not key to solving the mystery, but for gathering the support to move against the Prince later
                                          2. The result of the story was almost predestined. The Prince had enough secret enemies that the voting outcome was almost predetermined. Still, the players had to do the legwork to move the pieces and make it happen. Some decissions and steps also helped set the conditions for the aftermath.
                                          3. Many of the original scenario elements were left in the background. The players heard legends of Lupines in the cities of the dead, what made that scene extra tense They were chased by the police instead of hunters.
                                          The plot was grounded in the city political intrigue, because the Prince's lover was the Childe of one of the Ventrue leaders, so his rival become a suspect for a time, adding to the intrigue.
                                          4. The chronicle was advertised as an intrigue heavy one, so the PC's were not at all combat oriented. They suffered a little during the climatic final fight, which was crafted to their level.
                                          5. Hunting werewolves for power, how to deal with politically corrupt leaders and the final temptation of Diablerie added a lot to the story.
                                          6. Because they solved the mystery fairly quickly, they were never captured. That made us miss one of the three highlights of the story, but the political intrigue was a good substitute for this game.

                                                      Second iteration of Psychomachia: Chicago 1991
                                                      In this case I run the story as part of an ongoing Chronicle based on Chicago by night 1st edition with some additions. In this case I kept the setting and the main characters from MbN with a few tweaks, but adapted the plot to fit with the overarching chronicle.
                                                      The PC's (a Toreador, a Nosferatu and a Lasombra antitribu) form an organisation named The Mask ( this name is actually from the aftermath of Psychomachia), whose official job is to protect the Masquerade in Chicago. Since the Sabbat took Cleveland a few months ago, Chicago has become the target of a siege and the Mask has taken the role of investigating Sabbat infiltration and then reporting them to the Sheriff's Office for cleaning up.
                                                      A month earlier a Sabbat nomadic pack from Detroit had gone through Chicago and killed the previous Sheriff (Balthazar) in the Opera (I am also running a slow burn version of Blood Bond). They had later heard that the pack had gone to Milwaukee and gotten into a fight with some Anarchs.
                                                      The week before the story the characters had located a smugglers hideout that was being used by another nomadic pack in Canada. They raided it, managing to capture a Caitiff who was being converted and forcing the true Sabbat that was also there to run away.
                                                      As you can see, this is a slightly more experienced group and they have some fighting competence, although this is not their stronger suit. The info they obtained allowed them to heavily disrupt the pack's operations, which involved creating weapons caches and safe havens around lake Michigan in preparation for the assault.

                                                          Part 1. The set up.
                                                          The Mask receives a call from Lodin's second in command. Terence Merick, Prince of Milwaukee and a good ally of Lodin has reported a few incidents of murders that break the Masquerade during the last week; he claims the nomadic pack that killed Balthazar may have returned to Milwaukee and be responsible. They are tasked with finding out whether this pack is still in Milwaukee and, if that's the case, finding them, asking Merick for permission to act. They are not supposed to fight the pack, but to support the new Sheriff of Chicago and her team in doing it.
                                                          The Mask is flown to Milwaukee in a rush, so they can examine a fresh scene and look for clues; they are not given time to feed (which will become an important point later). They are greeted my one of the Prince's ghouls, a nice chap who is a police detective. He gives the PC's the police reports and his own notes about the previous crimes. He tells them that they are going to visit the crime scene before is completely cleared. The victims are two young street dealers affiliated to a mortal gang with links to The Union, a Kindred Anarch gang that has been unifying the streets recently.
                                                          But before they actually make it there, they are ambushed by members of this mortal gang and during the ensuing firefight their vehicle gets destroyed and the Prince's ghoul is killed. They capture one of the attackers unharmed and Dominate him, but are only able to find out that the hit has been ordered by someone called "Rob".
                                                          They call for help and are directed to a safe haven, where they also take their prisoner. They find out the place is rigged with CCTV as they open the doors to some prostitutes the Merick has sent for them to feed. They manage to avoid any killing and go to sleep.
