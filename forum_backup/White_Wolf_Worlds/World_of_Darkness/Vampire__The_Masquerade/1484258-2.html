                    Originally posted by Querion View Post

                    I don't think the actions of a Methuselah account for much in the spirit of the thread. They're the exception of vampire society, not what you're usually seeing on the streets, much less in the depictions cited in the OP.

                    Originally posted by Shadeprowler View Post
                    Baba Yaga was a great example what could happen when some ancient with her caliber decide, to actually act not just sit around twiddling her thumbs...
                    Actually I don't think she was a great example of anything.

                    First, the whole Iron Curtain was a terrible idea, in-game and out-of-game. Out, it alienated Soviet (in the geographical sense) players and made a huge chunk of the world inaccessible for reasons. It was a continuation of the Cold War after its end just because most of the American writers at the time didn't knew better, they had no idea on how to depict Russia at all without the lenses of the regime (and maybe didn't wanted to show that it's transition from Socialism was actually a huge political and economic crisis). In-game it just made no sense, never got a reasonable explanation (Baba Yaga wasn't one) and had no apparent consequence to the rest of the world nor got any attention despite being literally a sixth of the total dry land on Earth and bigger in both area and population than the US and Canada combined.

                    Then her demise was.. deplorable. It was among the dumbest tales in WoD's publication history, and that isn't a small feat. Railroaded, deus-ex-machina, bland characters, references pulled from a well-known folktale without anything that really resembled the folktale except the creature deciding to appear to the players as the girl just 'cause. Badly written in every conceivable way, but it was urgently needed because at the time they noticed they really had to get rid of the stupid Iron Curtain A.S.A.P..

                    Her reign shouldn't have kick-started the apocalyptic world, it should have attracted the combined fury of so many other big players in the stage on her head. The only thing she gained was a big red target, and with everything other splats can pull out under extreme pressure, I don't buy that she had such a thorough control over the Silver Fangs in their own turf while behaving like a deluded maniac.

                    Nothing in the setting, not even the literal awakening of Ravnos, ever had the sheer power and span of what she did, yet she didn't show any ability, skill, insight or behavior that could lend credibility to such a feat, and ended up dying an ignominious death in Russia: The Exposition.
