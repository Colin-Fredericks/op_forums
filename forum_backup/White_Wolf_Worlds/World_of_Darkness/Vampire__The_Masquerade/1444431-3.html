    The difference between the Lasombra of the Anarch Revolt and the Antitribu has always been rather huge, though. One of the things that should be remembered is the fact that the Anarch Revolt amongst the Lasombra was one carried out at the top down, not one that was carried from the bottom up. The leader of the Lasombra Anarchs was Gratiano, a.k.a. a frigging 4th Generation Vampire. He may have been the Prince Harry of the family but he was still a Prince. Not a Baron, Count, or Duke let alone a peasant. Gratiano walked into the castle of the Lasombra Antediluvian because he was a guy who belonged there.

    The reason the Lasombra "won" their Anarch Revolt is because the moment that the Antediluvian was dead, everyone did switch their allegiance. The idea being that "cynical opportunism" is actually something that is ADMIRED among the Lasombra. Montano, a vampire who was older than dirt and of somewhat noble character (as ancient fiends go), was the one who was the odd man out for wanting to continue the fight and not siding with the Anarchs.

    If not for the Friends of the Night, he should be head of the Clan because he's older than dirt himself. Instead, they sided with Gratiano because the Clan's leadership chose to endorse the act of patricide and diablerie that Gratiano supposedly committed. I have my suspicions they may have been ordered to do so by Lasombra himself because it's not like they would ever know their own thoughts were not their own. We also do know that Lasombra arranged for the faking of his death and has literally been hanging around in his own castle this entire time when not possessing Gratiano. Why? Who knows. Antediluvians can get bored too some days I guess.

    From a Watsonian point of view, this helps set up the events of V5 because it is established that the Lasombra are a clan with no honor. When their Antediluvian fell, they immediately turned traitor on his former loyalists. When the Sabbat discovered Lasombra was still alive and were going on a very dangerous self-destructive crusade, they switched sides again. Why? Because they are a bunch of scheming seflish bastards among a race of scheming selfish bastards.

    Now, the writers didn't HAVE to write the Lasombra this way but they have ALWAYS written the Lasombra this way.

    Since I haven't read V5 I can only comment given my perception based on how people have reacted on these forums. But it seems the defection of Lasombra into the Camarilla wasn't properly predicated. I have not heard once how these defectors could have overcome the vaulderie and vinculum to do so, or why the Camarilla would have trusted such defectors if they were still in effect. This is just lazy writing.
    The Vinculum has never made you loyal to the Sabbat. The Vinculum makes you loyal to your pack mates. Nothing would stop you from defecting even if you might hesitate to kill them. Oddly, it might mean that if a Lasombra defects then he'll possibly bring his posse with him. Given the nature of the Gehenna War, I'm pretty sure that plenty of Lasombra did lose long term packmates or the defectors were primarily Lasombra loyal to other Lasombra alone.
