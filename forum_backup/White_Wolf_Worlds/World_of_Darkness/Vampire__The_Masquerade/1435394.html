                      As for combination disciplines, my rule of thumb is that in principle all combination and advanced disciplines are custom made and invented by the vampire who uses them. As such, nothing prevent one from hearing of a power and developing it on their own, so that some powers will be more or less widespread. And one could still develop it without hearing about it. Ex.: Brujah have Burning Wrath, but anyone could be interested in a similar power. Warrior Setites could do it comboing Skin of the Adder and Potence, with the caveat that it's less discrete than Burning Wrath (I imagine it with the skin of a horned viper, like the nickname of some Warrior Setites), but could do maybe 1 more dice of damage. Nosferatu with elder level Potence or maybe with Animalism+Potence since Animalism harnesses the Beast and aggression, but maybe they would require a higher level of Animalism (like Potence 3 + Animalism 5), etc.
                      As long as it makes sense, I'm not bothered with teachers when it comes to these powers. VtM unfortunately does not have much of a system to give an advantage when learning from a teacher vs self-learning, which is what would be more of a sound motivation to seek out teachers and trade favours for powers. There are some small things in some editions (revised had a secondary skill for teaching, I think) but nothing substantial.

                      As for merits, as long as it makes sense, no problem. And I like creativity, so a good story about why your Pisanob Giovanni has a mix of Giovanni, Harbinger and Nagaraja merits is welcome with me, even if it ends up with you having crazy dice pools and advantages with necromancy. If it made you think hard for a plausible story and you're going to interpret its consequences, Im going to encourage that. Sometimes the need to justify some odd combinations brings about very good and detailed pc characterisation.
