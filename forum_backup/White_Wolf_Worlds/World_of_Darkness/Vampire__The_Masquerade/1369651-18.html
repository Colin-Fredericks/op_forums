                                        The take that the Ventrue are super popular is a new one for me. They're in charge most of the time because, most of the time, no one else cares as much as they do, IMO.

                                        But what matters is the Lasombra perception of the Ventrue. And broadly speaking, they've always hyped themselves up as superior to the Ventrue. So regardless of whether or not the Lasombra actually CAN take the Camarilla from the Ventrue, I don't find it especially hard to believe that they THINK they can ("They" being the Friends of the Night, "mainstream" leadership of the Clan, etc.)

                                        As a side note, I'm generally inclined to agree with the point made above about "Clans leaving/joining the Camarilla" being a much messier affair than such statements might indicate. Lots of Brujah and Gangrel are still with the Camarilla. A bunch of Lasombra are probably still with the Sabbat. And it is even messier with the Brujah and the Gangrel because they AREN'T Clans like the Lasombra or Ventrue, with sticks up the- I mean, centralized and organized leadership capable of making decisions collectively for the group.

                                        The thing for me is, across many Clans not just the Lasombra, one of the most common sentiments you see among Sabbat Elders is "what the fuck has happened to us?" There are probably lots of younger Lasombra that buy in to the Sabbat ideology and the bonds of vaulderie, along with Elder Lasombra among the Abyss Mystics who definitely fit in with the more esoteric groups that reject Humanity rather than wanting to mingle with it. But the Lasombra have largely been depicted as a Clan of shadowy manipulators wielding power from behind the scenes long before the Masquerade came along. And given that even the Sabbat maintains the "Silence of the Blood," the whole idea that the concept of the Masquerade would offend the ego of any Lasombra just doesn't really hold up to me. Between the radical wings of Abyss Mystics and anarchic packs of shovelheads, I could see the "mainstream" Clan Lasombra, the predatory social Darwinists, looking at their membership in the Sabbat and saying "well this was a good experiment, but I think it's time to abandon ship" and thinking that stealing the Camarilla out from under the Ventrue would be just as much of a Take That as beating the Camarilla with another Sect. Especially since I don't really get the feeling that the Camarilla cares much about how "nice" you are maintaining the Masquerade. If all your asshole dickery can be covered up with "gang violence" or what have you, I don't think the Inner Circle is going to get too worked up if the Lasombra maintain their Masquerade with the same brutality that they did in the Sabbat.

                                                            I think most of the mid-ranking members of the Camarilla (that is those embraced before 1950) probably have some fairly good ideas about the Sabbat, know those guys are dangerous, and either have their own stories or know other vampires who have been hurt or killed by them. And ~50+ year old vampires are the ones who hold more of the power in any given Camarilla city than vampires embraced less than 20 years ago.

                                                            I view it kind of like if the US government had granted amnesty to half of Al-Queda members, then invited those Al-Queda members into the US, gave them citizenship and then they started taking positions of political leadership. Everything else aside, I think a lot of Americans (even people traditionally liberal) would find that to be a pretty questionable and concerning policy and there would be a ton of pushback. Younger people born after 9/11 might not care all that much, but most people older than that would be very weary of that. And for most people in the US, the Al-Queda/US dispute only goes back around a quarter of a century. Meanwhile the Sabbat has been a fanatical fundamentalist organization that's been at the throat of the Camarilla for a few centuries.

                                                            I have a hard time imagining that just because the Inner Circle of the Camarilla said, "These guys are cool, trust us!" everyone else in the Camarilla would just nod and go along with it.
