    Originally posted by Sergeant Brother View Post
    For an alcoholic, being offered a drink may cause an overwhelming temptation that requires massive amounts of willpower to resist, for others they don’t even want a drink and it’s no temptation at all. That isn’t a moral difference, the alcoholic genuinely experiences a stronger temptation which is harder to resist. Just as some people experience stronger sexual desires (and related temptations) than other people for complex biological reasons that are usually beyond their control.
    This is a great example to draw in parallel to the Beast.

    I rarely drink and have a friend who's an alcoholic. If I have one drink, stopping is easy. If my friend has one drink, stopping is incredibly hard. I wouldn't say either of us is "stronger-willed" than the other. Like you say, alcohol is just a stronger temptation for them than for me.

    As far as alcoholics go, my friend isn't that bad. They don't drink and drive, drink and get physically abusive, or drink while at work. Drinking only really hurts them. But I used to know someone else who would become abusive while they drank and saw no problems with drinking and driving. (They're not in my life anymore.)

    I tend to think of the Beast in a similar way. Any vampire who's exposed to blood is going to be tempted. Any vampire who's exposed to blood while starving is going to be incredibly tempted. There's no way around that. But some vampires are more conscientious about the effects of their addiction than others, some make better decisions about it than others, and some ultimately want to stop being "alcoholics" more than others.

                                                People with power are bad enough. Combine that with an essential need to do others harm...

                                                My vampires are people first. Their environment and difficult circumstances gives them a few sociopathic tendencies, and indeed vampires tend to embrace people who are already willing to screw others over to help themselves, but they're mostly very capable of positive relationships. Despite the so-called Jyhad, vampires cooperate and support eachother more often than they go against one another, and this is especially true in smaller cities. Most of their issues are felt more by mortals than other vampires. Vampires actually try to kill off the extremes, the super nice and the super evil. The two are risks in their own ways. Even in the Sabbat, someone who's drank the kool aid a little too much threatens the rest of the Sabbat either directly or through attracting attention. Vampires may want to shake the pot occasionally out of boredom, but natural selection favours those who crave stability.

                                                    Overall, pretty awful. As the sire of one of the PCs in my Northwest by Night chronicle put it: "never become attached to your herd or retainers. Don't hesitate to sacrifice them if necessary. We are eternal; the kine are not." Elder vamps see people as playthings and resources at best, with the exceptions who have held onto their humanity (or walk one of the more benign Paths) being rare as owl's teeth. Ancillae at least affect the same attitudes, and many of them (not bound for a long unlife as these things are reckoned) have descended into depths of monstrosity. And no matter how vile a vampire may behave, as long as they keep the Traditions, they are not to be interfered with. The Camarilla countenances downright sadistic behavior: from the Toreador who raises up mortal artists and celebrities only to leave their lives in ruins once she moves on, to the Ventrue who controls the city's sex trade. As long as these Kindred respect the rules of vampiric society, they are treated as Solid Citizens of the Domain, even enjoying Title and high Prestation. When I set out to create my World of Darkness I wanted the PCs to feel that there's no Big Good for them to simply bank on. If they want to resist their Beasts, they'll have to find that wherewithal within themselves and be resourceful if they want to leave the city better than they found it.

                                                        Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                                                        Every Storyteller has their own preferences for how they like to portray being a monster. "Personal Horror" was the byline of the original game with the idea the player characters would portray a reluctant monster that was horrified by the actions they might undertake under the influence of either the Beast or the Hunger. Some fans HATE Personal Horror. They prefer to play monsters who revel in their vampiric power or hate the idea of feeling guilt over feeding on what nature clearly intended as their food.

                                                        Others have a preference for vampires who are different sort of monster who prey upon the evil doer and are more Nick Knight or Angel than cursed monster.



                                                        This is mostly about PCs, though, when people typically discuss it. PCs are able to defy the nature of their curse far differently than the majority of vampires. This is more a thread about how bad you think vampires in general are and specific groups ranging from Clans, Camarilla, and so on. How high is the average vamp's Humanity? What does that really mean? How often do they become wights?

                                                        A general all purpose discussion of how dark being one of the undead and their life is morally and ethically.

                                                        I haven't played Vampire in decades, so I don't know very much about Discipline Combos, for example. The one thing that kind of bothered me was that most of the Disciplines could not Improve The World in any way or create anything of value. You could have a Ventrue using Presence and Dominate to organize a bunch of mortals toward some positive goal. The main ingredient that allows the goal to be achieved is the group of humans doing human labor. When I look at the most up to date list of Vampiric Disciplines, they (still) seem to be for:

                                                        - Controlling Others, human or vampire
                                                        - Stealth, Investigation
                                                        - Winning Combat

                                                        I know there will be someone who says something like, "This Thaumaturgy Ritual makes a mountain appear.". You could use Celerity and Potence to do manual labor for the benefit of the human population, but how often does anyone do this? Generally, Vampire Disciplines are for some purpose to do with surviving, killing or undermining (like with Dementation) other Vampires and acquiring power over others.

                                                        This nearly complete lack of blood-derived power to improve the world and the wide variety of powers applicable to self-interested competition informs the question, "Am I going to be good or bad?". A new Vampire assessing the powers available to them isn't going to conclude that their function is to do good for others. They are going to be calculating how they can use these powers to uncover dirty secrets, eliminate competitors, hide, raise an army and climb up the undead hierarchy.

                                                        If you are looking to do Worldbuilding to encourage PCs to play Vampires with positive, constructive goals, have a wide variety of Disciplines (not just niche combos) that are directly applicable to those goals. I know one might say that the challenge is to play the doomed hero trying to do their best despite being a creature that only subtracts from the world, but if we have arrived at a point where a large section of the player-base hate the core theme, creating Constructive Disciplines needs to be considered.



                                                        Thank you for passing time with me in conversation. My Hacks.
