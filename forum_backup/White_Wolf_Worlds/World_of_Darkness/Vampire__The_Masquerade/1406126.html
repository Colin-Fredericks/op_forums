    Some questions - input and critique needed

    08-03-2020, 02:14 PM
    So i started a hyborean age setting crossover (we discussed that about a year ago or 2 here) so its actually set preflood so the antedeluvians are all around as well as kain and 2nd generation.

    Now i thought - who says there have only been 4 second gen vampires? afaik we know there was more than 13 of the third generation but many didnt make it.

    So why not create a clan, with its own set of disciplines to give the players new and interesting encounters?

    my idea was to have a (hyborean age setting) clan called the "Ymirjar" consisting of the giants based north of nordheim / hyperborea (which also may have hyperboreans in it)
    the idea is that the hyperboreans are ell known for very powerfull witches and witchcraft (white Hand) necromancy, mind manipulation and ofc they could be mages. but what if its an actual clan based on ice cold and death?

    Id make Ymir a legendary 2nd generation vampire (a giant) and Bori the 3rd gen progenitor (fahter of odin kidna goes toegether with the same name 4th gen gangrel)
    my idea was to give them 2 existing disciplines and a new third one. maybe even make it 2 clans. Now to the Hand fitting would be necromancy, dominate and some magical ice one, for the giants potence, fortitude and an icebased one.

    Clan weakness could be a penatly to physical attributes if your body temperature (and thus for vampires room temperature) raises above 30°C or the need to rest in snow (similiar to tzimisce weakness) or you can only gain nourishment from frozen blood that you have to eat (kinda in between ventrue and nagaraja weakness) or a "giveaway" like a chilling aura that freezes nearby liquids

    My idea was an ice based discipline similiar to existing thaumaturgical paths

    Kryonis

    * Freeze: water (and blood) by spending X bloodpoints you can turn X ammount of water into solid ice to say walk across a lake, or chill the blood of an opponent by touch reducing his actions / round. Cancelling Celerity / werewolf rage forcing those to move at normal speed
    ** Frozen weapon: by cutting yourself with a blade you may use your blood to create a sharp layer of vitae ice on the weapon allowing it to cause aggravated damage +1 lethal damage in addition
    *** Icebolt: Similiar to the video game "blood strike" ability condense water from the air, freeze it to an icicle and throw it causing aggravated damage (but not returning blood ofc)
    **** Iceshield: similiar to bloodhsield with the difference, that the blood freezes. maybe additional soak against lethal damage and reduced soak against bashing damage
    ***** Deep Freeze: Deep Freeze an opponent if they fail to resist (roll against strength? Stamina?) rendering them unable to do anything until they unfreeze. If the resist succeeds, targets still take half of the original success rolls as aggravated damage
    ****** Hailstorm: Summon a sturm of ice that does X damage (blunt or aggravated?) to an area, destrying cars and the like as well
    ******* Avalanche: Summon a huge avalanche of ice cold snow to burry an area (could also be used to create a save resting spot to escape the sun)
    ******** Boris Breath: Freezes the target in time, shielding them from any source of damage but also rendering unable to do anything until the spell is lifted at will or time limit per success - one minute, hour, day, week, year, decade, century, millenium
    ********* Ymirs Scream: Causes ice age like conditions in a big area (city maybe) chilling the temperature to -50°C (thus rendering most technology useless, that wasnt built for arctic conditions) causing snow up to the hips impedeing movement, solid freezing sources of water and any living creature has to win a roll against the user to not be frozen (like deep freeze) might kill mortal creatures by causing 1 point of bashing damage / 10 minutes (frostbite)
    ********** Invoke the ancient ice: temporarely lower your generation to 3rd, at the cost of suffering 1 point of aggravated (frost) damage per hour and reducing your movement speed. (cancels celerity effects) Become immune to fire, and gain +3 stamina and strength. Sunlight will be reflected off your skin, granting you immunity. Causes you to look like a frozen corpse / icelayer reducing your soak against bashing damage by 3 but increasing soak against lethal by 3

    For the fun of it, vampires having more than 3 dfots in this ability dont turn to ash on death but rather snow powder that disperses in the wind or melts to a little puddle
    Gouhls of either of those 2 clans become highly resistant to the effects of cold and frost

    what do you think? o you have ideas on what to change, other powers to add?
    have i forgotten something?

                          Made a V5 update and would love your input ^^

                          Discipline:

                          Kryonis Kryonis is a unique discipline houseruled originally for an Hyborean Age (Conan) crossover game where a vampire clan hails from Ymir and is lead by his Daughter Attali. It originates from Ymir 2nd gen and Attali 3rd gen and has a frost theme Since its unqiue it doesnt have any amalgams However you could make some with either blood sorcery or fortitude A sideeffect of usage is inspired by v20 flaws - Anyone having the discipline at 1dot gets ice blue eyes, pale skin and hair might turn white with advanced usage. When using powers, the eyes may glow - at 2 dots your skin becomes cold to touch - anyone with 3 dots in the discipline gains an unnaturally cold aura that may cause a fresh breeze following them in hot summer or into a sauna, snow and ice may linger on their clothing or hair longer than it should or when it shouldnt be there - at 5 dots the cold you radiate is physicly noticeable. Plants wither in your presence and mortal creatures may start to shiver (Bearbeitet)

                          Disciplines that you use on others are rolled with resolve + kryonis. those used on self with stamina + kryonis (resist the cold) Resisting rolles are Stamina+survival as they deal with cold

                          Any power costs 1 hungercheck, but you can add an additioonal hunger check for 1 automatic success (more blood more ice - the water in the blood crystalises) All powers of Kryonis are potential masquerade breeches and always look supernatural

                          Finally for resonance, melancholic could do but id houserule that victims that feel cold have fleeting resonance, those with hypothermia intense resonance and frozen corpses (which need to be ingested with iron stomache) have the strongest resonance

                          Level 1
                          * freeze - You can freeze 1m³ liquid per success for success hours - alternatively you can apply this on your feet and walk on water that freezes beneath you for success minutes - finally you can freeze metal which may jam doors, result in guns failing to fire, or cause hydraulics to rupture

                          * Hypothermia - any net success causes 1 point bashing damage to the target (frostbite) Vampires affected by hypothermia also need 1 additional hunger check for any use of discipline as the cold blood refuses to "burn" properly


                          . Level 2
                          * ice shield for 5 minutes per success the skin is layered with Ice that halves incoming damage (except bashing which is already halved) At 4+ successes the ice layer becomes thick enough to make fire and sunlight damage into bashing damage (and halved) (Bearbeitet)


                          . Level 3
                          * Frozen Weapon By covering a weapon (or magazine) in cold blood any hit by that weapon causes +1 aggravated damage per hit. lasts for 10 minutes per success

                          * Icicle Conjures a spear of ice thrown at a target. Causes Net Successes aggravated damage and causes hypothermia until the projectile is removed from the body
                          Icicles can be dodged and are rolled as ranged combat resolution. If it is aimed for the heart it functions as a stake for vampires


                          . Level 4
                          * Deep Freeze Target suffers net successes aggravated damage and is frozen solid and cant take any further combat related damage until broken free. To break free the victim can attempt 1 roll / turn to escape and suffers net successes bashing damage each turn until freed. Vampires take no damage from the cold / turn but instead have to make 1 hungercheck / turn to not fall into torpor


                          . Level 5
                          * Hailstorm Conjures a hailstorm of 5 m² per success around the caster that deals 1 point bashing damage per success to everyone inside except the caster and causes hypothermia

                          * Avalanche Conjures 10m³ per success of snow that burries people underneath. Everyone caught in the avalanche suffers Deep Freeze until they free themselves from the snowy grave. The snow melts normally but can provide shelter from sunlight for 1 day if the caster burries himself in which case he doesnt take damage.

                          For those who want the whole clan id pair it with Fortitude and potence fitting the theme of giants

                              Level 1
                              • Freeze: Freezing stuff is fine, the walking on water part is the only questionable part, not only because it is strong for a level 1 (Transversal does that at 3), but the logistics of it don't work unless walking on a pond. 1m³ is already a lot, so I wouldn't add successes to it, 1m³ per rouse check (Which can be rerolled since it is a level 1 power), and it stays frozen based on your discipline rating.
                              • Hypothermia: Bashing Damage isn't a thing in V5, assuming it is superficial it isn't that strong, aside from making vampires do another rouse check. I'd personally move it to level 2, as you can't reroll it easily, as on level 1 it is easily "spammable", and it'd put it on the same level as Extinguish Vitae, which does something similar
                              Level 2
                              • Ice Shield: Again, bashing isn't a thing, and superficial damage is already halved, and it doesn't affect fire and sunlight (main sources of aggravated) normally, so it doesn't affect anything. Honestly I don't think there is a place for this power, it really just does what Fortitude does.
                              Level 3
                              • Frozen Weapon: Aggravated Damage to vampire is really rare until level 5, this power is both overtly niche and really powerful for combat. Things also don't generally last for precise amounts of time in V5, I'd make it last for a scene, same as Scorpion's Touch.
                              • Icicle: Again, aggravated damage isn't a thing (besides flat amounts) until level 5, so this is horribly powerful, not to mention applying hypothermia, which I think you mean the earlier power.
                              Level 4
                              • Deep Freeze: Stupidly powerful, aggravated damage and immobilizes target, plus rouse checks to not fall into torpor.
                              Level 5
                              • Hailstorm: Not sure about the mechanics, but the storm comes from where, is it hampered by being inside?
                              • Avalanche: Again, the snow comes from where? It's hard to imagine it in action.
                              Further Thoughts
                              • Unique disciplines aren't a present in V5, nor are disciplines that inherently grant you flaws, both of which brings additional design concerns, such as if should the discipline be stronger due to it's rarity and consequences. Not that you can't do it, it just clashes against V5 discipline design.
                              • It's a discipline severely lacking in non-combat usage. Aside from the first level, it has very little utility.
                              If it wasn't an unique discipline, I'd make it a series of Blood Sorcery amalgams and power re-flavorings.
                              • Freeze (Blood Sorcery 1 - Oblivion 1) - Blood Sorcery representing how you transform the blood, Oblivion for how it annihilates heat.
                              • Hypothermia: I think a re-flavouring of the Fire in the Blood ritual could work, changing it from fire to ice. If not, a Blood Sorcery 2 - Oblivion 1 amalgam, doing mostly what you said.
                              • Ice Shield: It's just Fortitude, ate best I'd make it a ritual that grants you limited discount Fortitude for a time, at the cost of being incredibly obvious.
                              • Frozen Weapon: Re-flavour Scorpion's Touch to a more icy vibe.
                              • Icicle Conjures: Re-flavour Scorpion's Touch to a more icy vibe, and instead of spitting blood, you throw frozen Vitae icicles.
                              • Deep Freeze: Re-flavour Cauldron of Blood or Baal's Caress. Cauldron now aggressively freezes the victim, Baal makes the target slowly freeze till torpor.
                              • Hailstorm & Avalanche: Make it a level 4-5 ritual, that changes the weather.​
                              Hope this is helpful!
