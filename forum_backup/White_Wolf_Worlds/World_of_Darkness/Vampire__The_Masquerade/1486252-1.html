    I think two things should be remembered when doing an Anarch Domain:

    1. They should be distinct enough not to be the Camarilla 2.0: The fact that Anarch domains can rapidly become "Camarilla lite" was brought above and this is true but it should still have enough differences that it's not a complete joke. If nothing else, "We have the power and they don't" is actually a fair argument for a vampire.

    2. They should be seriously flawed: The Camarilla is a source of many fun adventures because it is a flawed and terrible system. Which means the Anarchs should also be flawed and terrible. There's no such thing as a vampire paradise after all.

        Rust Kingdoms

        "Gary, Indiana may not look like much but it's ours."
        -Arthur Caldwell, 9th generation Malkavian

        Rust Kingdoms are the most among common Anarch domains as there was a simple realization among the Anarchs of America, Australia, and other locations that there was a massive feud among Kindred for the greatest of domains: London, Paris, Chicago, New York, LA, and so on. However, there is actually a lot of SHITTY domains. The Gary, Indianas and other Rust Belt Cities actually had advantages that other more populace domains did.

        These lands largely tend to surround larger cities so Anarchs have access to them but they are mostly ignored by the Princes as "flyover country." Rust Kingdoms are conspicuously dangerous as a large vampire population will be noticed FAR quicker by independent Hunters as well as having much larger percentages of werewolves. They also tend to be much easier to dominate and are less monitored by the Second Inquisition. They do not tend to be large numbers of Kindred but a gang of 7-12 Kindred is not uncommon.

        Rust Kingdoms tend to pop up in places that already have preexisting economic anxiety and tensions that allow death to go unnoticed. They are also places that Anarchs are the most obviously the same thing all vampires are: a plague on the locals.

        Cornfields

        "Norman Bates disposed of plenty of cars in that swamp. That's all I'm saying."
        -Dave Johnson, 13th generation Lasombra

        Cornfields are not really Anarch domains but Anarch outposts. Highway gas stations, Big Box stores, and large rural farms are places that usually serve only a single small coterie of vampires or perhaps even one. Cornfield Anarchs are still Anarchs, though, and provide locations where Anarchs can take refugee after being driven out of a city. Basically, when the heat gets too high or they're wanted for a Blood Hunt, Anarchs are sent to Cornfields where they stay until they figure out what new city to go to or arrangements are made. Hospitality is not extensive and boons are still traded but usually these places have a history of disposing of bodies. After all, frequent traffic and missing strangers works well for these.

        Cornfields also have a tendency to be run by vampires who, in the absence of true rivals, get a little "strange" in their beliefs. Heavily religious "families" worshiping Caine, Lilith, or Mithras are not uncommon.

        No Man's Land

        "Don't cross 13th street, Dave, if you're flying Cammie colors."
        -Tinderbox, 10th generation Nosferatu

        The Camarilla ejecting the Anarchs from their rank resulted in something that had existed before but hadn't really been formalized and is now just a nightly reality of Kindred in all but the most fortified of Ivory Tower cities. Simply put: the majority of Princes do not have complete control over their own cities.

        A No Man's Land is what happens when the Anarchs of a city gather together in a specific domain and then eject or kill any Camarilla official that tries to subject them to their laws. More than one Prince has sent all of their Sheriffs and deputies to eliminate the Anarch presence in their domains, lost them all, and then found themselves helpless before their enemies.

        Generally, No Man's Lands are far less extreme with the Prince and Baron (and it's almost always a Baron) basically recognizing a detente. The cities basically become two different domains with the Anarchs and Camarilla generally sticking to their sides or avoiding any overt violence. Quite a few Princes and Barons choose to ignore their larger sect politics and carry on as they have for often centuries or carry out "sham" war that only target's the others enemies.

        This is notably the status of New York with the late Boss Callihan, who was lovers with and a puppet of Prince Panhard.

            Network Domains

            For some Anarchs the Revolution isn't about taking over the city, anymore. Physical frontiers are outdated. What is society, power and authority, other than connections you have, the information you control and the recognition of your peers?

            Network Domains form around an idea and a few Kindred working to put it together. One puts together a networking application and the connections between apps to be added. One offers the servers for the data, another puts together logistical arrangements for shopping and delivery services that can move things across the world. VPN, databases, search engine, blockchain for smart contracts and trade, in cryptos and Prestation. No single piece need to be physically near each other, some don't need to even exist in physical form.

            Once the system gets momentum the site is a Domain of its own, ready to create havoc on the usual power structures of the Damned. Members bring information from wherever they are, helping the community to piece together the situation across the world. Those with the skills and the tools to cross information brought by members and found in the web (or their own databases) can piece together the secrets and going ons, follow the trails of Prestation, the gossips of Elysiums and pierce layers of secrecy over the true goals of Princes all over the place, while those who deem themselves authority on the physical world are none the wiser.

            The Network Domain can do more than offer a place and information to its members. It ships resources and place people, it pools ideas and expertise, it makes the political chess of the entire world easy to visualize and understand, it filters Disciplines and generation out of the social totem pole, it coordinates agents and servitors. And if the Prince itself finds out what's going on? What will they do, join the community as a noob?

            Distinctly from most Anarch Domains, Network Domains have a lot of Titles. Administrators, moderators, record keepers, fact-checkers, Anime Gif Creators, everything goes and can be coded directly in the system, so there's never doubt about who's the title holder. There are public and private spaces, each with their own encryption, and systems of layered and/or parallel hierarchies behind the initial facade of equality, ripe for exploration for those who learn to master the social system. Some are clearly structured and at the hands of the app's owners, others are wild spaces maintained by invisible watchers, and others still are bold enough to have Open Code, playing straight with the digital system, but in the hands of Kindred that specialized in gaming on-line social dynamics.

                A shared haven / mutual support domain (San Fernando Valley):
                This is a domain where a few "nomadic" anarch gangs take haven. They spend most on the time on the road, and only come home for healing and resting, so the area can support more vampires that it normally would. They may share some resources (haven, allies, contacts and influence) or even have a schedule for the different gangs to come home.
                A few vampires may have permanent residence. They help the nomad gangs keep their resources safe and in turn the gangs defend them. Mess with the domain and you will have a few gangs giving you more trouble than the small domain is worth.

                    Based of my own setting

                    The Republic

                    Based on a mixture of classical athenian thought and enlightenment ideas the Republic is structured as a direct democracy. Comparatively low numbers of kindred allow for this to be quite workable on a nightly basis. The baron being more of an adjudicator and sheriff than direct ruler, were most decisions are decided by secret vote at the assembly.

                    The domains tend to be quite resilient to Sabbat or carmarilla incursions due to the legal obligation to defend the Republic and a comparatively unified front, the Republic also tends to establish legislation to protect against eldar monopolies on power, such as limitations of domain, power and established legal rights for all kindred. However such cities often have erratic political climate where informal leaders are quickly overturned and factional infighting results in paralysed leadership.

                    Popular in academic Liberal areas the Republic tends to surface in well entrenched brujah territories. Although a variety is beginning to emerge in Scandinavian gangrel with a 'thanes assembly"

                        Kleptocracies

                        Another type of Anarch domain that is very common is the Underworld-dominated one. Vampires have long had ties with organized crime because they are useful means of moving around cash, goods, people, and services without breaking the Masquerade. Indeed, much of the violence inherent to the Kindred condition is passed off as criminal activity. However, an interesting form of Anarch domain has emerged somewhat organically and that's where a vampire-run criminal conspiracy ends up taking over a region.

                        The irony of kleptocracies are they don't tend to actually have been attempts to take over an area but more often end up ruling over an Anarch domain by default. Basically, a Camarilla or Sabbat presence collapses due to internal issues or Anarch assault before the latter move in. As often as not, the Anarchs cannot agree on a form of government or move to establish control over organized crime as their primary method of revenue as well as influence. When the dust settles and more idealistic forms of Anarch government have been discredited, the remaining Anarchs appoint the local crime lord or lady as the Baron.

                        This sounds like a specific story but it's actually happened a dozen or more times among successful Anarch takeovers. It turns out money, guns, and blood trafficking (with or without the Kine still attached to it) are how a domain keeps the lights on. Simply put, if you know how to run a criminal organization, you know how to run a city's Kindred. More idealistic Anarchs may not like when organized crime takes over but they at least have the means to protect the Masquerade, get Neonates out of jail before sunrise, and dispose of bodies. The fact so many Anarchs are recruited from gangs makes them the equals but opposites of Coplands.

                        Cynically, many believe Kleptocracies are inevitably going to fall in line with the Camarilla's ethos but this is rarely the case. While organized crime is something the Camarilla routinely controls, most kleptocracies take a Miltonian "It is better to reign in Hell" view or, more precisely, "better to be a big fish in a smaller pond." Few of them would be Princes or even Primogen in cities not run by Anarchs and enjoy most of the privileges of both in their domains.

                                I can really see some naturally born domains.

                                Smaller satellite towns around bigger Camarilla cities, that works almost as an Anarch embassy or common house(s). And those Anarchs that live there are pretty much accustomed with Kindred coming and going. And are somewhat well informed with what happens in the Cam territory, maybe have some contacts with Sheriff or Seneschal.


                                A nice one would be a "Gangrel's Rule" (or werewolves rules if you like) Anarch domain, where you do what you can get away with. Can't protect your territory? Goddamn, you don't deserve it. We have some really hard disputes? Let's go talk with the eldest (or strongest alpha) of the city to see what we are gonna do about it. Wow, seems like we have some personal problems with our neighbors... better challenge their chief for a monomacy. Or a hunting challenge.


                                I also like to think there might be some even more feudal and reactionary domains than Camarilla's. Like, it is full-on peasants, knights, kings and all that, blood tax (requiem style, which by the way has a ton of fun different princes types), calls for war... But also, feasts, grand courts and tournaments. It probably is envision and maintained by one or a coterie of really rich and mad Kindred.
                                Which makes me think there might be some cities that are really close to Camarilla in some ways, but don't actually want to be part of it, or have some critical problem with one of its points. So they actually have some good relationship with adjacent Camarilla domains but aren't part of it.
