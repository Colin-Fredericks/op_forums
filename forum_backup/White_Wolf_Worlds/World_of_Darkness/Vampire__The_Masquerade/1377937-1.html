            Originally posted by Draconis View Post

            Pinyin is a newer romanization system for Mandarin, and the most official one at this point. "Hsien" and "Xian" represent the same word, but "Hsien" is in Wade-Giles romanization (the most popular system for most of the 20th century) and "Xian" is in modern pinyin.
            Yeah it more conducive to Mandarin the most widely spoken of the Chinese Dialects, and it comes at it more like it developed for native speakers to translate their words, while Wade Giles comes at it more from European languages trying to translate the sounds into romanization, if that makes sense. But seeing Wade Giles dates the books, it brings to mind when I read books written like in the 80s. Its good for some flavor, like it evokes the old Hong Kong writings before they stopped being British in 97.

            •If one likes the Kuei-Jin as written then sure, that justification works fine, heck, its built into the Kuei-Jin from the ground up. They kept kindred out of all of Asia, the Anda and Salubri were flukes, maybe a bit during European Imperialism but that's it. If you like as written, that's fair and fine. I'll be honest though, I still don't think it makes much practical sense. Kindred are like rats, once you have them there almost impossible to fully root out. Asia has an enormous population and ancient trade routes to Europe, India, the Near East, and Africa; giving plenty of opportunity for millennia. Plus, Kuei-Jin and Kindred, to the best of my recollection, feed on distinct parts of mortals and so there's not necessarily any need for instant and extreme antagonism between them, or at least, no more reason then what also exists with every other supernatural species. Plus, if we compare supernaturals, the separation seems even more flawed. Just about every other splat hates vampires on principle, yet vampires haven't gone anywhere for all the mages in France or werewolves in America or changelings in Ireland. It just seems like the separation is a Hand of God (as in the writers) forced division between Asia and The Rest, effectively creating two settings, which is a lot of people's biggest complaint about Kinsred of the East to begin with.

            •You yourself included WtA in your argument, so I figured that pulling from other splats in my rebuttal was fair game. We've also already talked at length about our differing views regarding the concept of Multi-Resonance Layered Reality, so probably no need to get into it here.

            •Even assuming that the Wan Xian and Kuei-Jin had the power to fully eradicate the presence of kindred and the willingness to do so, how do you justify in canon why the Wan Xian only ever tried to eradicate vampires outside East Asia, since there mandate would imply it spread to all the world? How do you have the Salubri and Saulot the Thief? The existence of the Anda bloodline, the Ravnos war with the Kuei-Jin in India, that kindred have made footholds in canon and that a prominent reason why the Mandinarate (or whatever it was called) was successfully established was that an already scattered and collapsing California Free State was collapsing and undergoing Midnight Siege from the Camarilla and Sabbat? Taking that all together, it doesn't make much sense to me, unless one gives Kuei-Jin power on scale with archmages and incarna.

            •V5 aside, I also doubt much of this would get taken up by the writers, but this was a thread asking individuals their opinions and routes, so I brought up my own. I prefer a long standing kindred presence in Asia which co-exists alongside the Kuei-Jin with unique bloodlines and organizations, but a retcon of such proportions would probably require at least an entire book dedicated to it and given the eternal controversy of The Kuei-Jin Question, I doubt anything will ever come to that.

            At the end of the night, these are just my personal thoughts, opinions, and headcanons. Whatever you do, as long as you and your table are happy, is valid af.

                I REALLY like Kindred of the East as it is. Yes, it is complex, takes time to get used to, it's a different culture therefore a lot of research is needed, but once you get past that, it is a REALLY GREAT GAME.

One thing that still bugs me is how the corebook is organized (it's a mess!), yesterday I needed to see some rules while STing and it took me a while to find exactly what I needed.

                I recall Saulot's image from Clanbook Tremere Revised (pg. 14) and Transylvania Chronicles IV (pg. 44) and none were bald, where else was he depicted? On Saulot being asian, it would explain the origin of Mokur (he was from the Jomon people, who are believed to be from 10000 BC up to 1000 BC during Japan's prehistory) and tying him to Japan island would make sense.
