                Originally posted by Natsymir View Post

                wait now, shouldn't it be the other way around?
                As I understand it?

                Canonically, the Lasombra don't believe in aristocracy. The Lasombra believe that even the lowliest peasant can rise up to the highest ranks in the Clan if they're badass or cunning enough. This was actually a progressive attitude in the Middle Ages and partially why they leaned toward dealing with the church. While it rarely worked out that way, nothing particularly prevented a peasant born clergyman from rising to be Pope since Peter was a fisherman rather than a nobleman.

                In the Sabbat, they became the Clan of Ayn Rand meets X-men's Apocalypse.

                So some Lasombra are aristocrats but they're very much of the "self made man" belief and that if they are arsitocrats, they're from the kind who EMBODY it.

                The Ventrue, by contrast, had the view that some people were "born better" and normally took from the military aristocracy of the world and considered peasants as well as Low Clans to be beneath them. The current shift toward merchants and businessmen was something that happened in part because so many of their ancient Houses and bloodlines were annihilated by the Inquisition and they underwent (what they would consider) a degeneration the same way the Brujah went from being the Clan of Socrates to the Clan of Sex Pistols.

                Even now they tend to have heavy classicist tones and probably a racial one if you look hard enough.

                I'd argue the Ventrue are closer to how the Tzimisce view things but the Tzimisce figured out how to MAKE themselves better.

                    Originally posted by The Gentleman Gamer View Post

                    Yeah, I hate those bloody Keepers and Magisters. You're lucky I didn't make them afraid of the dark as well.

                    In seriousness, this is an interesting discussion, but I would urge people to credit the writers and developers with at least a modicum of sense, and keep in mind the metaplot is in part designed with the long game in mind.

                    I look at the metaplot, and yeah, I can see lots of potential there. The issue is -right now-. Taking all the cool stuff away for a number of years for a big reveal leaves us dry and hanging -right now-. Meanwhile the vagueness of "these things that happened between revised and 2009" might be there to build up mystery, but have you also considered it just looks vague and gives the impression that the writers are just making big sweeping changes with vagueness because they hadn't really thought too much about how they wanted to do something, they just wanted something done. I see a lot of potential, but I see a lot of potential for disaster, and it seems clear to me that you could have avoided all this had the metaplot moved with the present rather than something done retroactively. 'Things now' are bad. So 'things later', presumably being writen by the same people who chose to make 'things now' bad, causes concern.

                    As to my comments about Lasombra.
                    No, I don't believe you hate lasombra (Did you write their section in LotC? That was solid). But V5 was written by a team, and have you noticed that there's a huge disrepency between the mechanics of the game and the story now? Elders and Ancients are supposedly scary, but they're subject to the same five-level limitation as high gens and ordinary mortals. Thin bloods are no longer -between a vampire and a ghoul- but rather lost such powers so they can become absurd apothocaries. For the Lasombra: Dominate has gained numerous new restrictions and is weaker, Potence is now a strange collection of absurdly specific strength-based gimicks rather than a simple strength enhancer and no longer fits the old image of the clan, Obtenebration has given up it's identity to be a necromancy bastard and the shadow powers it once had are weak and laughable... Yeah, I think you can forgive me for believing some people on the staff do not like Lasombra. Sure I can justify playing them more in V5 with their escape from the Sabbat, but why would I want to play them in the first place? They are weak whilst their culture has always been -we are not weak- . Not to mention I can't play with paths as they don't exist, and Abyss mystics can't exist because using Oblivion that much would mechanically drain you of your humanity.

                    So things right now look bad. Saying 'oh, we're long term planners and things are coming in the future...' Yeah, nah, no thanks. The mechanics work against the fluff; your team is literally working against itself. And then there's the times where the new fluff is working with the mechanics and... It's not an isolated incident. We could be having the same discussion about the Tremere: The fluff has potential to un-fuck itself and be a grand master-stroke of writing, yet we shouldn't speculate for the future when things are fucked -right now-. Also Thaumaturgy has been mechanically re-written to... not be thaumaturgy? And they also have Dominate but it now sucks... and they can't blood bond... so they should be getting murdered by their ghouls or something.

                    I can credit some of you with sense, but 'the long game in mind' shouldn't mean that the here and now should suffer like so.
