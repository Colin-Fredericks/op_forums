    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
    Something I think VtR 2e did well (esp. since it redeemed one of the worst rules in VtR 1e) relates to the idea of having something between VtM 1e-20th's "nothing or frenzy" and V5's "here's a half a dozen ways the Beast might screw up your character's plans," approaches: Lashing Out.

    When VtR 2e vampires go Beast to Beast, the loser effectively gets smacked with a Compulsion in V5 terms (though there are just three universal ones instead of Clan ones and all that). It has that "Beast gets under your skin and you do not so smart things without a full blown Frenzy" part that the earlier games lack, but isn't as agency ignoring as V5's mechanics tend to be. It's never an accident, because there's always at least one vampires that is purposefully making it happen.

    It's easy to picture how street level politics are full of Lashing Out as vampires push each other around for dominance and control, but polite high political vampire society considers such antics beneath them (not that they aren't all ready to do it at the drop of a hat if cornered or need to put a youngster in their place without drawn out conflict) and uncouth to maintain the appearances of civil vampire society.
    I really like Lashing Out, too. I've been thinking about how to pinch it in my V20/V30 hack.
