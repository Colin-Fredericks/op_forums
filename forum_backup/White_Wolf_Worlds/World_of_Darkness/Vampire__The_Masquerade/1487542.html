                                                      I can't say I have an all time favorite. I am not a fan of the signature characters although some are certainly fine concepts. Most of the By Night books after Chicago are simply terrible with either generic NPCs, characters who are obviously a former PC of the writer or one of his friends, or NPCs that terribly clash with the city they are in.

                                                      I won't mention any characters from CbN because they're too well known. That the World of Darkness has basically pimped out this book THREE times is a testament to the strength of its character concepts (though not their character sheets).

                                                      But out of the rest, there are a few gems in the dreck. More for their potential than how they were used in their books.

                                                      One of these is the Ventrue Upton Rowlands in Dark Colony. As written, he's founding stock of New England with strong ties to Connecticut politics and business and Yale. He's from a line of Ventrue that embrace within the family. He's a Prince of two towns, and leader of the Gemini League, an undeveloped coterie based in a local social club whose main attribute is an interesting name. All good stuff, but not put to much good use in the dog's breakfast that is Dark Colony, a very poor By Night book that has a few good things in it.

                                                      But so much potential! A Ventrue from American Revolutionary stock, New England old money, and who controls Yale University? How can you have this guy not be involved with Skulls & Bones secret society! This guy should be the vampire symbol of the Eastern Establishment, wielding vast influence. There should be other vampire Rowlands either descended from him, or through his own sire (Aunt Georgia) scattered over the country. His control over Skull & Bones should mean indirect control through that entire network of highly placed Yale graduates throughout business and government. He is the leader of all the Ventrue in the New England area and controls their collective action through his leadership of the Gemini League. He has his own spy network ferreting out threats to the regional Ventrue.

                                                      When it comes to the Ventrue Board of Directors for the entire North American continent, this guy should be sitting there along with Horatio Ballard making the major decisions.


                                                      This is a character that should be more prominent, but neglected because of where he was originally published.

                                                      There's certainly a few others out there that are sadly neglected, but have great potential. If one of them comes to mind, I'll post again.
