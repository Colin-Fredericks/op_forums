    Originally posted by Mr Gone View Post
    Thanks! Seems to jive with the info above. But, you think I use the Ghouls logo and symbol from V20? I could do that..
    Yep, I'm inclined to lean that way namely cause I think it will help the sheet blend well with the Clan sheets, otherwise you can just use a generic to represent anything as normal.

    Originally posted by Mr Gone View Post
    Ok, I have a few questions here. So, move Flaws to page 1 in place of Clan Bane? And change the current Disciplines section to Backgrounds? You don't think the sheet would need any Merits? And none of the info like Blood Surge, Mend Amount, etc? Even cutting all that, there wouldn't be room for Disciplines on page 1 if it needs to stick to the same format as disciplines are currently. Even if it was a line per discipline there might not be enough room. So, that might need to go to page 2...
    So, for moving the Flaws up to page 1 that was namely to accommodate that there wasn't really anything great to slap into the Clan Bane position that isn't needed on the Ghoul sheet and just leaving it void would look kind of crappy - so might as well have viable information available in that place. Pulling the Background over to the Discipline section was mostly tied to the fact that Ghouls need substantially less space to record Disciplines/Powers than Kindred do (at most they will likely have 3 different Disciplines, and that seems a stretch, and the highest level they will be able to get them to is 2 points, and that is again rare) and I was really hoping with that reduction in space that those could be placed into the space for the Rouse Checks and such (none of that needs to be there, Ghouls can't do any of that - they use Mortal healing times just chopped in half, that is generally easy enough to remember and doesn't need a spot for a blank since it is consistent across all Ghouls whereas with Kindred there can be variance).

    Granted, if you don't think the Disciplines will fit that might all be a problem then, though the Disciplines would use exactly the same format. The would have a blank line with two dots, and then a #1 and #2 line each and that is it. So each Discipline takes about 3 lines, but we'd only be clearing 5 lines from that section so it would only really accommodate 1 Discipline. One might argue that is fine for most Ghouls, but I feel a second is probably needed. An alternate would be to include less spots for Backgrounds and place 2 truncated Discipline spots below the Backgrounds. If we go that route, and are still cutting 5 lines of space in the other area we could actually push the other area down and add in a spot for Merits above it (and below Flaws - or vice versa, put Merits on top of Flaws). That might look a little odd though.

    Originally posted by Mr Gone View Post
    As for possessions, I could shift it over to the right column and expand the notes section, or I could add in a spot for info on the Domitor, like on the V20 sheet...
    Oh, I forgot about the Domitor section, that could actually be really handy instead of just expanding the Notes section (which was admittedly a cop out on my part). Yea, I'd go that route.
