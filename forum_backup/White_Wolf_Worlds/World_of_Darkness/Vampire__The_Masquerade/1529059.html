    Ballard family as a Giovanni minor clan

    01-20-2024, 10:02 PM
    V5 Chicago gave us a brief plot hook where the Boston Giovanni approach Horatio Ballard with an offer to have his mortal kin become a minor family in the Hecata. My Midwest focused game is overly infested with the Giovanni families so I kinda wanted to pursue this idea, but I struggled to think of a way to make the Ballards unique from the other money-making Giovanni associate families like the Milliners and Dunsirn. Horatio Ballard has a knack for sniffing out opportunities since he learned about vampires after a few heart attacks and even managed to find Lodin, plus V5 CbN has him embracing technology by testing a few "SchrekNET-lite" projects which he doesn't really understand the danger of when the SI inevitably crack into them.

    Anyway, I interpreted this as the Ballard family becoming more adventurous and experimental with their investments and schemes, being the technological Steve Jobs-esque family who relies on Horatio's status and (quickly depleting) wealth to test the financial waters to tap into markets which are rapidly expanding in the modern age (general tech, AI, weed dispensaries, OnlyFans, maybe dropping their own crypto-currency when they saw how BitCoin took off). Their Disciplines could be either Dominate, Presence and Necromancy (Oblivion) to embrace the Ventrue's overwhelming leadership ideal, or maybe Fortitude, Presence and Necroblivion since they're used to kowtowing to Horatio and embrace the soft-power kind of mindset. Maybe not even bothering with Necromancy to go with Auspex, Fortitude and Presence/Dominate? Auspex would fit a minor family on the lookout for the right opportunities

    Would you have the Hecata extend the offer to the Ballards in your game? If so, should Horatio jump ship and not even wait to see what happens when the Promise of 1528 expires just to reap the benefits affiliating with the Hecata could give him considering how he's not exactly thriving at the moment? What Discipline set would you give them?

    A little off-topic but has anyone created their own Giovanni minor family, maybe went political with the Kennedy's to really mess with the Milliners? Let this be a general minor family discussion thread if you will. Thanks for reading if you did : )

                                      Interesting fact,

                                      One of the things I noticed they changed for Chicago V5 is that Horatio Ballard's "Creepy Uncle" qualities have been removed. I think they wanted to get away from the Edgelord elements of the original Chicago by Night as much as possible. This is why Jacob Schumpeter is killed off by the Second Inquisition (though I believe that may have simply been a preference by Matthew Dawkins, to which I say, *fist bump*).

                                      The new version of Horatio Ballard is much more focused on being a Ventrue who has lost his ungodly amounts of wealth and is trying to rebuild it. Which, honestly, since he's got Dominate and Finance 5 shouldn't be that difficult. Financial crime is something I doubt that vampires have difficulty with.

                                      Though if I were doing Ballard in "our" world, I'd reveal that he was completely taken in by Madoff and tried to fix the problem with real estate and ended up getting hit by 2008's Financial Collapse. Which, in other words, means he's lost not millions of dollars but BILLIONS. Maybe enough that if it was ever found out by the rest of Clan Ventrue, he'd be the laughing stock of the clan or staked because a lot of that was the Clan's money.

                                      My take on the subject of Ballard joining Clan Giovanni is that it is a desperation ploy because Horatio Ballard has to suspect that joining the Giovanni Clan is something that he, as a Ventrue, has no place in. The thing is that Clan Giovanni is kind of on the outs and the other Clans are on the rise, which means that whatever plans they HAD for the Ballard clan (small c) is probably gone but individual families like the Pisanob might be willing to ally with them anyway.

                                      There could be "leverage" from the Ballards, especially if Horatio offers to set the Hecata up in Chicago or some other element of the family. The Hecata has money to burn after all and Ballard has a serious cash flow problem.

                                      But that doesn't mean the Hecata want to SHARE with a disgraced Ventrue.
