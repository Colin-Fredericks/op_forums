Sure; a rifle plate wouldn't protect you from a rifle, but at least it would reduce the damage. In V5, a rifle plate won't do anything for you if you're a vampire, and riot gear won't do anything but hinder you if used against weapons common in a riot. If VTM was 'bad', V5 is "terrible" because armour doesn't actually do anything outside of some very speific niches.
