    Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
    The system, not the specific powers within, was good.
    My issue with the system as a whole, then, is that it's too rigid. When there are multiple things you want to make available at a low level, you have to either group them all into a single power (Auspex 1 sharpens your senses AND breaks invisibility AND gives premonitions) or shove one of them up to level six or higher (Obten 1 was taken, so seeing in darkness has to be Obten 6 now). Sense the Beast (new in V5) is a good Animalism power, and helps reinforce that Animalism is both the discipline of beasts and the discipline of the Beast, but there's no room in the fixed 1-5 hierarchy for it, so it can't exist in V20.

    One solution is what Requiem 1e did: at some levels there's a choice of two powers and you can only take one or the other, not both. Another solution is what Requiem 2e did: there's one power per level, but some powers unlock devotions (combo disciplines) that require only that specific power and give an alternate power of similar potency. I'm not a huge fan of either of these techniques.

    If it were up to me, I'd keep the "multiple options per level" idea from V5, but get rid of the opportunity costs. Someone who takes Obten 1 (Shadow Play) can also take Obten 1 (See In Darkness) without losing out on anything else. Not all levels need tons of options, but there are a few places where they'd be really nice to have.
