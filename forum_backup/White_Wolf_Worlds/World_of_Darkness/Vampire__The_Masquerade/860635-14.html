The Children that survived the utterance of "Let there be light!" were the big-bad things like Kupala and such.

                        At least I thought this was, if not outright stated, then pretty heavily hinted at across Vampire, Wraith and Mage fluff before Demon the Fallen came out. Maybe that's just me remembering the subtext as text though, been ages since I reread the Baali fluff.                         Damn...stupid fallible memory.

                        Still if Guide to the Tal'Mahe'Ra does make that "text" for new fluff I'll be happy. Even more so if it can clean up/integrate the "the Baali actually worship the Earthbound because Demon says so" fluff-up that occurred late in Revised Edition.
                        Yes, they were Malfean-like beings in VTDA (well, in Clanbook: Baali, anyway). It was DTF that retconned them into Earthbound. I always liked the VTDA mythos better, because it wasn't too heavily tied to a Judeo-Christian paradigm. Or rather, it could be considered more Gnostic and Qlippothic than the DTF alternative, at least.

                        TBHAGttTMR presents both options. So DTF fans can have their characters deal with the Archdukes and their minions, and Cb:Baali fans can deal with Cthulhoid nasties instead.
