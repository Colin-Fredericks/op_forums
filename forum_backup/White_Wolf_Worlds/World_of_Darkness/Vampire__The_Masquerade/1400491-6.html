    The fact that the Camarilla are now openly, rather than secretly, religious indicates to me that they made some consesions with some Sabbat elders who were going to prop up the organization when the Camarilla let a lot of it's members go to the anarchs and the Sabbat sent their young to the middle east. (The added religious appeal unfortunately makes them look like Republicans, but I don't know if that's happenstance or the desired result)

    There are a lot of cities with sensible Sabbat left behind (with the firebrands purged) and the Sabbat are needed their to keep their tendrils into mortal society and ensure a seemless transition (The Sabbat completely leaving these cities, and the Camarilla completely moving in, would be an absolute logistical disaster) . All Sabbat with good connections are going to be canidates for the Camarilla transition (Or a purge) whilst the loose lot too involved in the circlejerk to have any serious mortal holdings are of absolutely no use to the NuCamarilla.

    Hey, It could be that absolutely nobody is heading towards the middle east and really the guys arranging transport are just mass killing sabbat in their sleep.


    The Camarilla getting new options works with how they're now the religious group. But I never thought it strange that the cam had so few clans in it as those clans clearly have the most reproductive potential (In nature, generalists do better than specialists, and clan specific disciplines are the result of specialists)

    I feel they'll make a lot of treaties, especially concering the two T's; The many many elders in the Camarilla don't like Vicissitude (which might be the Tremere's doing), and many elders don' t want it to spread into their cities. Conversely, the Tremere aren't to enter certain cities because they'd certainly fight with the former Sabbat (perhaps this is why the Tremere so masterfully staged Vienna; So they can enter these cities under the guise of house Carna)

                How I would do the Gehenna War's Aftermath

                My take on the Gehenna Crusades and this is just speculation is that they are going to emerge as something that's actually analogous to the real-life Crusades. The Sabbat will return from the Middle East with a bunch of ancient tomes, scrolls, clay tablets, and stone writings that will go a long way to increasing their knowledge of ancient Kindred history as well as the power of their mystical factions. A bunch of barbarians entered the Middle East, what will emerge is a much more educated and intelligent group (at the cost of much bloodshed).

                They will also have many vampires who will have emerged changed from the ancient blood they've drank. There will be a couple of Mithras-types among them but, by and large, it will simply be that the Sabbat was victorious NOT over sleeping Methuesalshs but having exterminated hundreds of Camarilla elders who were Beckoned.

                The Antediluvians will have drank their bloody fill during this time and have altered the memories of the Sabbat the same way the Tzimisce and Lasombra Elders did. They will remember being the ones to have killed so many Cammies and have achieved greater victories over the Ivory Tower than they have in their entire history since the First Anarch Revolt. Whether it is a lie or not doesn't matter.

                The New Sabbat will emerge as a tighter more occult-based organization with almost none of its previous Anarch roots but much more akin to the True Hand or the Children of Zillah. Alien paths and ritual will almost completely dominate the sect with very little of the FREEEDDDDOM stuff that is now the province of the Anarchs.

                They will claim to have defeated the Antediluvians and will be secretly their foremost pawns.

                I also think that the Sabbat will have destroyed Alamut and claimed numerous domains in the Middle East with conflict in Jerusalem, Syria, and other notable places. They won't regain all of their cities but they will re-establish themselves in many places with Miami, Rio, Tripoli, Venezuela, and other places as good bases for their activities. They will be more interested in evangelizing than before with agents spread throughout the cities, designed to take them over from within.

                                                        Kludge is an ill-assorted collection of parts assembled to fulfill a particular purpose. That describes the contents of most RPGs settings that have gone on for more than a few years. Bits of things get added onto and stick around just because.

                                                        The Sabbat appeared in 1st edition VtM as a metaphoric fire to the skillet of the Camarilla. They were the monsters lurking in the dark, just part the edges of Camarilla power. If the characters didn’t like dealing with the Camarilla – too bad. You can’t leave, because the Sabbat will get you.

                                                        The expansion of the game line meant they had to be explored at some point, which meant the Sabbat lost some of its mystique. They stopped being an unknown terror and became a bizarre blood cult.

                                                        Yes, they were more splatter than horror. That wasn’t so much by design as by accident.

                                                        Whatever the Sabbat become can move deeper into actual horror territory.
