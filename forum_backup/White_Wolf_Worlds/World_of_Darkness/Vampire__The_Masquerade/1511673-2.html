    The birth of a monster

    Enoch was a second generation kindred known as "The Wise", but what many didn't know is that behind his noble band he hid desires, desires that would even make the most disgusting individuals silent to repulsion, to purify himself, he saw no choice but to resort to witchcraft, after years researching in secret he apparently found a solution, a ritual that would purify him from theses disgusting desires, which worked, but not the way he expected, in the ritual Enoch expelled A part of himself and this part was his first child (as far as we know) somehow Enoch released his more "rotten" part and it became something not alive, but un-live, very similar to a cainite, but at the same time not a cainite, Cainites had a human part because they were born humans, but that... thing didn't have it, didn't born a human, it born a monster. Enoch later introduced that creature to his brother and his sir, as his child, perhaps because he felt responsible for create him or to hide the truth that he was so embarrassed about his creation and this later resulted in other embraces, from him and from his fellow brothers so the third generation of Cainites was estabilished and with each new child it became more apparent how different that creature was, they barely knew what Enoch had created. and what nightmares he could create.

                        Depending on the STs appetite for the mythic rather than urban human horror, the Second City and the founders predate even mythic Greece (Minos being fourth generation and the Minoans being one of the earlier Greek civilisations).

                        If you take Minos as an example his legend is the gift of a sacred bull by Poseidon and his refusal to the sacrifice said bull, depending on which myth you follow and of course the Minotaurs defeat by a demi-god Theseus.

                        As far as I’m aware VTM doesn’t confirm or deny that the Antediluvians were all human or not, it’s just an inference that they were, so given the existence of mythological or supernatural beings in VTM there’s nothing to say they weren’t Demi-gods or some form of inhuman (aka Set).

                        According to purely Abrahamic Lore Caine is descended from humans that live for over nine centuries (Adam 930 years) not all of them are vampires (but could be) and they exhibit beyond human abilities - Jacob wrestling an Angel for example, the same lore has Angels visit plagues on Egypt, destroying two cities and turning a woman to salt (just for looking the wrong way) so Jacob was a pretty extraordinary individual.

                        The Patriarchs also pre-date Caine and the Kindred.

                        The Antediluvians are pre-flood so alive during the same patriarchal age or it’s equivalents in say Babylonian or Cthonic history so around at the same time as these long lived people or Demi-humans.

                        So depending on your use of mythology it’s quite possible that some of the Second and Third Generation are embraced from these demi-humans.

                                        Originally posted by Arcanus View Post
                                        As far as I’m aware VTM doesn’t confirm or deny that the Antediluvians were all human or not, it’s just an inference that they were, so given the existence of mythological or supernatural beings in VTM there’s nothing to say they weren’t Demi-gods or some form of inhuman (aka Set).
                                        The exact nature of the antedeluvians before the Flood is something I like to keep open as well. They might have been - more or less - typical humans as we know them today. Or they might have been Biblical humans with relatively long lifespans compared to modern humans. Or perhaps some kind of pre-Adamite man or something akin to Theosophical root races that preceded ours. Or perhaps the Nephilim or some kind of angelic race from the Book of Enoch.

                                        You could stay totally scientific, go Biblical, prefer some kind of esoteric explanation, keep it mythological, or use other approaches or blend them together. I've always like Mike Mignola's Hellboy/BPRD universe handling of the origin of vampires with the Black Goddess and Hyperborea. It hits all the right notes for me, and I would want any details of the antedeluvian world and the origin of vampirism for my chronicles to create the same kind of feel.

                                        Something else I've considered is that prior to the Flood (which I make equivalent to the Severing when the spirit world and material world began to diverge and hence when "real" history began), the antedeluvians might not have been vampires as we understood the term. Vampirism might have been some kind of survival mechanism as a result of needing to survive the Flood and enter the post-deluvial world.

                                        If my players ever wanted to explore a heavily Noddism inspired chronicle, I would probably have various NPCs (some in secret Gehenna cults) offer various explanations of what the antedeluvians were before the Flood. In my experience, hearing different - but potentially viable - explanations for the same phenomenon tends to unnerve players, who as a rule crave certainty. And that is very good for any kind of horror RPG.
