        The different groups in all the vampire sects (cults, factions, clans, etc) all have different ways to justify ways to justify the supposed superiority of vampires over humans. The Church of Caine asserts they are anointed by God, and thus backed by Divine Rules, to justify that dominance. The Ministry rejects pretty much as rules (divine and otherwise) and says "do it" because they can (which might make them the least hypocritical if not the most honest). They all differ in how their justification works, and will fight to the death over that justification, even if what they all really want is the power to hurt each other and eat people.

                If you see the typical Sabbat of the Modern Nights like the SIth then I would say that we have a completely different view of them.                 The thing is that failing as a sect DIDNT MEAN THEY WEREN'T INTERESTING. Because dealing with that failure as a Sith-like Sabbat Elder was really interesting to roleplay.

                It's also not a belief that I am alone in as the Sabbat Civil War as described in Beckett's Jyhad Diary included a section about the Sabbat Elders wanting desperately to make Lucita their leader because they felt the organization was completely falling apart. Later, V5 confirmed that quite a few Lasombra defected because the Sabbat were just utterly given over to the Beast. I was just ahead of the curve by 15 years.

                Some Sabbat are genius philosopher Kings.

                Most Sabbat are guys eating babies.

                I mean, I like the occasional baby myself but it's not a lifestyle.

                Star Wars: Old Republic Sith

                What's kind of bizarre is that this is actually the same plotline in Star Wars: The Old Republic if you're a Light Side Sith (or even a Dark Side Sith). One of the running themes of the Sith Warrior and Sith Acolyte (with Imperial Agent having a view from the outside) is the majority of Sith are fucking morons. Not just morons. FUCKING morons. They relentlessly abuse their allies and engage in petty cruelties to prove how hardcore they are. This engenders no loyalty in their troops as well as undermines any alliances they make. It also leads to infighting that ends up destroying dozens if not hundreds of competent Sith that would otherwise be allies against the Republic.

                It actually is the focus of the KNIGHTS OF THE ETERNAL EMPIRE expansion as you find out that the Sith Emperor, the guy everyone thought was a Darth Nihilus wannabe who wanted to destroy the universe, actually agreed with the LS Sith and Darth Malgus that the Sith Empire was doomed unless it went dramatic reforms. Hence he created a SECOND force-using Empire and then trained all of his servants to be loyal, happy, and patriotic with a fanatical desire to protect him personally.

                The Sabbat are inverted in the War of Ages

                The funny thing about, "The Sabbat are shooting themselves in the foot" is that its the only time I side with the Elders rather than the Neonates in vampire. If you own a copy of New York by Night, one of the last supplements of "Classic Vampire" then you get more or less my argument in its entirety from the life of Katherine Wiese a.k.a Ecaterina the Wise. She's pretty much the ideal philosophical Sabbat. She rebelled for good reason against the Elders during the Dark Ages and wanted to build a New CarthageTM with the Sword of Caine.

                By the 20th century, she thinks they're all a bunch of violent thugs that want to kill her because she's lasted longer than most.

                I've admittedly met plenty of Sabbat fans who HATE this interpretation of the Sabbat and blame Justin Achilli for making the sect into a bunch of screaming morons but I feel like it's something that there's plenty of textual support for.

                    I think the Sabbat works because it's such a contrived clusterfucked entity.

                    Some guy did a study and found that, with religious groups, the more severe and restrictive demands made of it's members, the more successful and long lasting the group will last. So the more moderate the sabbat gets, the weaker it'd become You've got a long list of -they're out to get us- enemies that keep everyone together, and you've got the viniculum. Because you're always at war you've always got a target for your aggression and no-time to go for the higher ups. Any time someone starts to be a problem (In spite of the viniculum) you can just kill em off and replace them.

                    As for killing babies like... what's the point? Like it might have a use when you're trying to intimidate your enemies (Or to prove a point when YOUR FUCKING GHOUL THINKS SHE CAN EXIST AND HAVE A NORMAL LIFE OUTSIDE OF YOUR CONTROL, wait sorry that's a Camarilla reason to kill babies... nevermind) but doing it for the sake of doing it... like... you're just going to alienate yourself. Most Sabbat are embraced from fairly neurotypical stock. It might be fun to powertrip and kill a few people for fun but you're just gonna alienate packmates, create enemies, and necessitate a clean-up if you're killing kids. That's across the line for a lot of kindred. There aren't actually any paths that would support the activity. Chivalry doesn't forbid it but it's pretty darn dishonourable, Bones might have you watch a child starve but you're not going to kill the child yourself with that.

                    Honestly I imagine the worst excesses of the Sabbat (especially in sabbat domains) usually lead to monomancies (if not wassail). Imagine you're Max, once a single parent of three, and your new back mate Andy thinks it's cool to dash the heads of kids on concrete: You're certainly going to want to dash Andy's head on concrete, or at the very least stop him. Andy's just made an enemy for the rest of his unlife.

                    It's probably something you'd see more among anarchs, since the viniculum isn't around to keep you with people who aren't of the same mind, you can find coterie members with more in common (as if you could trust such psychopaths)

                    Food for thought:
                    Tzmisce are exceptionally polite for two reasons:
                    Firstly; So they don't wear out their welcome with other clans.
                    Second: when they bring someone back for horrific vicissitude practice they can justify it with 'this person offended me/broke the rules/did something wrong'
