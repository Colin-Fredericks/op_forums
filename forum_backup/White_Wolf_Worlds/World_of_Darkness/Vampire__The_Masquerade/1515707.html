    Got roped into running a v5 game...

    06-20-2023, 09:08 PM
    It finally happened, and as I'm not as conversant in post-v20 metaplot/lore as I'd like to be, I'd like to throw out the chronicle notes I have so far for a vibe/consistency check and to pick some brains as to potential relationship mapping and plot hooks.

    - The game, as all my modern nights chronicles, will be set in Louisville. The game's over Discord and three of the players aren't local, so I'm prepared to provide help on local/regional knowledge.

    - Covid and the fallout of the Breonna Taylor protests will take thematic center stage, juxtaposed against a Second Inquisition that is going to be an extremely present, very existential, threat.

    - The local SI is going to be a loose network of rogue operators out of Fort Campbell. They learned about the existence of vampires early, cut their teeth hunting Sabbat and Shepherds of ur-Shulgi in Afghanistan and Iraq, and work with fundie Baptists rather than the Society. The fundies are their eyes and ears, and without the methodology or oversight of the Society, they're going to be incredibly unstable and unpredictable.

    - The catalyzing event for the SI being unusually dangerous in Louisville, is that during the sect war a coterie of Camarilla vampires pilfered some military-grade equipment from Fort Knox and local KY National Guard armories. The SI managed to sweep the theft under the rug, but hunted and captured the vampires responsible, interrogated them, and used that intelligence to hit Elysium in a decapitating raid that killed the city's Prince, Primogen, and court attendees.

    - When Covid lockdowns started, the vampires left standing opted for voluntary torpor, with only the sheriff staying active to "keep the lights on". When the Breonna Taylor protests started, a whole lot of Anarchs came into the city. Most were just looking for havens and domains, but among them was a pack of Sabbat defectors who couldn't resist the siren song of Camarilla vampires in voluntary torpor, and used the protests as cover to go right back to their old ways.

    - The sheriff wasn't able to assert praxis on their own, or even keep the peace, and the city's already considered a lost cause by the Ivory Tower with no help forthcoming. They settled for negotiating with the new arrivals to fill vacant domains while keeping the Sabbat defectors off the torpid Cams' scent. But really, it was a stalling tactic in hopes the SI would deal with the new arrivals, long enough for the torpid Cams to rise.

    - As of the start of the chronicle, the city's horrendously overpopulated for the heightened risk the SI poses. Nobody has praxis, and the city's unofficially led by a triumvirate: a Setite with ambitions of declaring himself Baron, the sheriff who's actually Hecata, and a Tzimisce who's the ductus of the former Sabbat pack. This is already a recipe for disaster, made worse for the fact the torpid Camarilla are about to start awakening.

    Now, the NPC character hooks I've already thought about...

    Why is the sheriff an Hecata? They're there to babysit a Shallowing, and "joining" the Camarilla was a matter of convenience. They just hit it off with the former Prince, had a skillset suited for the position, and it helps with "the real job".

    The Setite is a sleazy televangelist megachurch type. So naturally, he's going to have history with the SI as I'm setting it up. I haven't worked out the exact details yet, but I'm thinking something along the lines of him either being on their radar but (still) too high-profile to simply disappear, or an informant. Maybe he's the one that provoked the fundies into vampire hunting to begin with, deeply regrets this, and is trying to talk them back?

    Probably the most predictable of them all, the Tzimisce doesn't give a rat's ass about the Sabbat bullshit, just wants his own damn domain already, and if a couple Cams trip and fall into his tummy along the way it's just good business. I'm not lazy, I'm cunningly subverting expectation by playing it completely straight.

              Given the closeness of Cincinnati, I'd also decide what is in control there.
              The way I plan to run the Camarilla, is that in terms of it being past editions' "vampire Technocracy" it's very much a paper tiger. Limited options for transportation and communication simply means Justicars are less capable of exerting influence over individual cities, and Archons less capable of direct intervention to ensure Princes and Primogen toe party lines. So, cities are reverting back to a more "Dark Ages" status quo where sect allegiance and obligation matters far less than whomever holds praxis, and it's ultimately up to who holds praxis to decide how participatory the city is in sectarian politics and regional power plays.

              So where in past years, Louisville would have at least one Archon visit to straighten matters out, now they're on their own. Especially with as you pointed out, an Hecata sheriff who's nominally Camarilla, but still an outsider and therefore someone not to be trusted. The sheriff's "indie cred" is absolutely why the Anarchs didn't just "take care of the problem".

              Chicago as always has its hands full with its own BS, and from what I know of the v5 metaplot thus far, the Great Lakes Cams are generally consolidating their own cities and looking towards other Great Lakes cities. I've heard Indy got a pseudo write-up in LtSRR, but I have yet to read into it -- but I expect it's yet another "Anarch-run, but the Chicago Cams are sticking their noses in it" scenario. But given how big agritech, pharma, and medical device industries are in Indy, there's a strong argument to be made for it to be a key Camarilla holding they'd be willing to fight to preserve.

              Cinci's a stickier wicket. I've always run it as a Sabbat city, following from Pittsburgh also being Sabbat. Interstate and Ohio River traffic would make the cities poorly defensible, least of all from southern and New England Sabbat (one thing I did in a past chronicle, was have a Sabbat pack that were modern-day river pirates). The Sabbat defector pack would likely be from either Cinci or Pittsburgh. One idea upon which I struck that I like, is Cinci's a Camarilla city care of a Lasombra coup, hence the pack getting the hell out of dodge.

              Don't forget the Drowned Legacies. Maybe, as they're digging up the supposed sleeping spots of ancient Camarilla vampires, they find something... else.
              I've done the "ancient sleeping evil" thing more times already than I like. It's something I'm going the extra mile to avoid. Not to mention, the torpid Cams I mentioned are all going to be ancillae.

              The SI wiped out the elders on the Elysium hit, hence the city being in complete disarray for a decade; the oldest vampire in the city is going to be maybe a century old. The city's vampires going into voluntary torpor was basically just a reaction to the double whammy of an unusually dangerous SI, and Covid -- can't hunt, curfews in place and remote thermometers/FLIR cameras everywhere, online communications monitored, "culture warriors" everywhere, and a highly contagious disease...better to just nap through it and let the SI peter out in the meantime.

              The sheriff was the lone exception. Being an organovore who can feed on Pathos, has its benefits...

                                                  Necro it may be, I'm on the cusp of actually running this and figured I'd give an update and crowdsource for feedback and ideas.

                                                  I'm reworking the backstory a bit, and throwing in some stupid shit that happened in past chronicles with my old local group. Now, the beginning of the shitshow was the 2008 Kentucky Derby, which in this past chronicle was...disrupted...by Sabbat PC's being, well, Sabbat PC's. One of the elders' hobbies was horse breeding, one of his horses made it into the Derby, and this elder tasked the PC's with sneaking into Churchill Downs and doping his horse with a little extra blood the night before the race.

                                                  The elder actually had a pretty cunning scheme in mind. His horse had a phenomenally racist name; this elder realized if his racist horse won the Derby, it would cause civil unrest in Louisville and therefore perfect cover for a siege. While, at the same time, giving some of the Louisville Camarilla a severe prestige and influence hit from having to maneuver through the larger social and political ramifications of a horse with a racist name winning the biggest and most famous horse race in the world. So, he tasked the PC pack with the job, knowing they'd be convenient stool pigeons if the whole scheme failed or backfired.

                                                  The pack did as told, but they also had the bright idea of doping every other horse (and overfeeding them) for shits and giggles. All the horses frenzied when the race started, started attacking each other and their jockeys, and several jumped the railing and started attacking spectators in the infield. Being this is a globally-broadcast live sporting event, it turned out to be the biggest public Masquerade breach since the publication of Bram Stoker's Dracula. Needless to say the cammies had their hands full cleaning up the mess and hunting down those responsible, except the Sabbat pack who did it ganked the elder responsible, fled Louisville, and went to ground.

                                                  And being this was on the cusp of the Second Inquisition, and all the old ways just backfired. The SI massively ramped up activities in Louisville, and this culminated in the 2018 Elysium slaughter.

                                                  Meanwhile, the Lasombra performed a mini-Giangaleazzo in Cincinnati, ate or drove out Sabbat of every other clan, and unilaterally declared themselves Camarilla without having delivered a single Sabbat elder to an existing Camarilla vampire. Essentially, flipping off Kevin Jackson, Sierra van Burrace and Talley, the Amici Noctis, and the Inner Council simultaneously. And from there, they've been eyeballing rapid, aggressive expansion in every major metropolitan area nearby -- including Louisville.

                                                  Which brings us to why the Lasombra are so interested in Louisville. I've always played up Waverly Hills Sanatorium's prominence and supernatural legends in my chronicles to varying degrees, and this one will be no different. This time, the entire property is a shallowing, and its morgue is the site of a nihil. Hence, the Sheriff/acting Prince being Nagaraja, and why her ass is parked right in Waverly Hills -- she's there to keep stupid mortals or other vampires from fucking with the nihil, make sure Hecata can use it freely, but most importantly ensure nothing too nasty comes from "the other side". The Lasombra want access to the nihil so they can fuck with it, make sure Lasombra can use it freely, but most importantly ensure anything too nasty coming from "the other side" is under their control.

                                                  What's happening on that "other side"? well, Covid caused things to get...weird. The Hecata and wraiths expected a Seventh Great Maelstrom, but...the opposite happened. The Tempest subsided, and as if that wasn't ominous enough, the Sea of Shadows froze over. If Great Maelstroms are precipitated by mass death, suffering, hatred, anger, and violence, then something approximating its inverse is appropriate for times of mass suffering, fear, isolation, and dread, but considerably less death than expected. I'm not exactly in love with the idea of calling it the (first) Great Doldrum, but I can't think of any better name right now. Either way, I think it's a fitting supernatural allegory for Covid lockdowns, social distancing, and global fear and uncertainty.

                                                  More to come as I flesh this out further.
