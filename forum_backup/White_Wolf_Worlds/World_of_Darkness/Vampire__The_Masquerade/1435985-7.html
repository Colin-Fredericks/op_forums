                                    I think it depends on how you define "win" or "lose." I'm not inclined to make absolute statements about whether Gameline 1 Faction A would win an all-out conflict against Gameline 2 Faction X. Too much depends on the particular tactical and strategic decisions taken, the metaphorical or literal "roll of the dice," and particular decisions about the nature of the setting that essentially amount to Storyteller fiat depending on the table you're at. I would be very confident saying that trying to get into an open shooting war with Vampires would very likely sabotage the agenda of the Technocracy, because the Masquerade would not survive it. I tend to see the Second Inquisition as a whole in several categories. There are the alphabet agencies, there are the Inquisitors, and there are the regular hunters that are just getting better equipped and organized now that they have more quasi-legitimate backing from the first two groups. Then you have groups like the Arcanum that aren't heavily committed on the front lines, but are providing backing in the form of intelligence, equipment, etc. I imagine most Technocrat involvement as that, but more. All of these different groups are communicating more, are cooperating more, but they don't share everything, they don't really trust each other, just enough to become a much bigger problem for vampires that generally don't trust anyone if they know what's good for them.
