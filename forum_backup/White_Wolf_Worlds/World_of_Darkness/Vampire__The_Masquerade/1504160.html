    Ever make a unique Path/Discipline that you know will never come into play?

    03-01-2023, 09:42 PM
    As many of you know, I am obsessed with the umbra and Inhabitants and feel that they are criminally underrepresented within VTM. So what did I do? I made a nonsensical and somewhat incoherent Blood Magic path which can affect any and every type of spiritual being. Whether it be a simple flame spirit or even the soul within a living creature's body, it doesn't matter because it can affect them all! I call it "The Others". Why did I choose to name it that? Absolutely no clue.​​​​​​

    The Others-
    1. The Senses- The practitioners have attuned their senses to the spiritual beings around them and can now speak with spiritual creatures such as ghosts, spirits, demons, astral creatures, and even those that are in inanimate and animate things, such as fire, water, souls in bodies, etc. When this ability is activated, the practitioner will speak in the language the creature they are interacting with is most comfortable with.
    2. Carrot and the Stick- The practitioner can either feed a spiritual being Vitae or harm the being by feeding on it. When feeding, the vampire can choose to satiate their bloodlust or strengthen their willpower. This technique can be used to reward or punish the spirit, and can help a vampire survive when hunting live prey is simply out of the question. When used on the inanimate or animate ones, it will strengthen the attributes if they are fed, or decrease it if they are fed on.
    3. The Summon- The Practitioner can now summon spiritual beings to their side. To do so they need at least rudimentary knowledge on the specific spirit they wish to call, for example one will need to know the appearance and name of a wraith, or the concept of the spirit they wish to summon, such as one of sadness. This power can be used to summon spiritual beings from their physical shells as well but will be more difficult.
    4. The Command- The practitioner can now command spiritual beings to follow their orders. It is best to use ‘Carrot and Stick’ when using this power as it cements your position as either a terrifying creature who will punish the spiritual being for not following orders properly, or a benevolent person who is willing to reward the summoned being for listening to them. Or one can simply take the “Tough, but fair” approach. It can be a bit tricker when using it on those which are within either inanimate, such as spirits of rocks, or animate shells such as souls within the body.
    5. The Binding- The practitioner can now bind spiritual beings to either inanimate or animate objects, creatures, or places. They have also gained the ability to create fretters, fetishes and the like, as well as strengthening those bindings. They can also weaken and free them from their bindings. If done on those that are either inanimate or animate, you can expect many different outcomes.

    Has anyone else wasted too much time making unique Disciplines/Paths that may never come into play?

                          I made two paths for the ultimate Tremere paper-pusher character I actually got to play for a bit.

                          Ars Notoria (based on the art of the same name from the Lesser Key of Solomon)

                          1. Speed-Reading, volume by successes
                          2. Eidetic Memory, details by successes
                          3. Learn contents of medium by touch, volume by successes
                          4. Same, but regardless of language
                          5. Same, but with a radius effect instead of touch (aka fun in libraries)

                          Bureaucrathurgy (based on the few canon rituals around paperwork)

                          1. Understand filing systems, procedures etc intuitively
                          2. Summon a perfectly filled out copy of a form
                          3. Mini-dominate through formal letters
                          4. Document alchemy (transmute/refine media)
                          5. Magically cycle through document iterations (turn first edition works into second, convert an application into an approval etc)

                          The second one especially was surprisingly more fun than I expected, and rituals like Expedient Paperwork are really underrated.

                          Custom Sorcery: For Ananasi | Mortal: Hekau, Mašmaššūtu | Necromancy: Dust Path, P.o.Fertile Grave Soil | Thauma: Ars Notoria
