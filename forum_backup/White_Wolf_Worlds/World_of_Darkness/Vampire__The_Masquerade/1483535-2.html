        Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
        I generally state that True Faith can only exist in a divine entity of some sort.
        It doesn't necessarily solve the question because demons are arguably divine entities of some sort. That depends a lot on how you interpret divine entity, the setting and the nature of demons (or the demons you're talking about).

        Can a Bane Incarna be considered a divine being? What if they're a Totem? Is a Fallen or an Earthbound a divine being? They're direct divine creations with the power and purpose to shape the world, as they did it in the beginning. What about the Hsien, the Little Gods? By all accounts they're exactly that, small in their power and standing, but still gods in the Heavenly Hierarchy. If a Setite does achieve Gnostic Enlightenment, ascending to the level of the Aeons, will this be a divine person? What about an Oracle? If you attain the status of Yama King, are you divine by virtue of your place and standing in the Cosmic Order, despite your nature?

        Not that I think your take isn't valid. It isn't canon, but is certainly valid. But it does raise a lot more hard questions than it seems at first.

                Originally posted by Kakost View Post
                That's... A good point. And a massive punch on my stomach, ouch!
                By the rules, there's nothing that actually impedes a Setite from having True Faith if you want that.

                True Faith is deemed rare, first because it takes humongous levels of faith, not just "much". A lot of people are really, truly devout to their religions and that still doesn't give them True Faith. And second because True Faith represents a particular way of expressing this Faith, it is the ability to ward evil and perform miracles, which isn't exactly the same as performing magic, so even a person with enough faith to have it may not have the inclination to act as needed to wield it.

                A Catholic priest with enough Faith that is happy with his place in the Lord's plan as a servant and minister will have zero reasons to ever try to do anything like the powers of True Faith, and so probably won't have it as a trait and won't ever use it if he has.

                Yet, it doesn't means you can't have a Setite character that has it, not any Setite Sorcery, but actual True Faith being wielded in the name of Set. Nothing against it in the books.
