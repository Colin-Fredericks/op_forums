                            Now, all of this is pure speculation, so don't throw a fit if it doesn't adhere to canon.

                            Caine’s murder of Seth is probably a reference to the extinction of Homo heidelbergensis, Homo rhodesiensis, Homo neanderthalensis and Homo antecessor by Homo Sapiens Sapiens (either that, or it’s a reference to organized warfare, which dates to 14,000BC). The “Antediluvians” are approximately 77,000 years old (at most). The oldest human civilization (as we define the term), dates back 5,100 years ago in Egypt, which is 100 years older than Mesopotamia. Ironically, none of the clans who claim to be the first of the 3rd Generation are likely candidates, despite their respective ego. I’ve based this hypothesis on the origins of their various cultural names. I figure that some of these individuals subsumed various cultural identities over the thousands of years, some of which might predate their introduction to a given society. I also am deliberately linking werewolves into the Biblical cosmology. This is due to the evidence presented in Devil’s Due, the Demon Dark Ages book, and because honestly I don’t particularly like the concept of fanatical environmentalist warriors being crossed with the concept of werewolves. There’s no basis for it in any occult legend I have ever read or heard of, and it sounds pretty farfetched. You will also note that the 2nd generation doesn’t necessarily conform to what you and I would think of as the game rules, as they spawned other supernatural races than Cainites.

                            1st Gen
                            Caine

                            2nd Gen
                            As far as anyone knows, the 2nd Generation was murdered by the 3rd Generation. Sometime afterward, each of the Antediluvians wandered off to embed themselves into different cultures.
                            Zillah: Sekhmet, a fierce huntress who guarded the pharaoh and controlled diseases
                            Irad the Strong (mortal name Mehujael)
                            Enoch the Wise/Lawgiver
                            Jabal
                            Tubal (Xue)
                            Adah (Gaia)

                            This is the rough order, based upon age of the parent civilizations, listing first the second generation progenitor, and then the Antediluvian offspring. It’s likely that the Cappadocian progenitor is the oldest out of the 3rd Generation, despite other clans’ claim to the contrary.

                            Egypt Zillah the Beautiful/Sekhmet (apparently Caine liked ‘em predatory) 3100bc
                            Tzimisce, Lasombra, Absimillard (Nosferatu), Haquim(Assamites)

                            Mesopotamia (Sumeria), Enoch the Wise/Lawgiver 3000bc
                            Arikel(Toreador), Malkav(Malkavians), Illyes (Brujah), Saulot(Salubri), Osiris/Sargon of Akkad (Cappadocians, probably the oldest of the Antediluvians)

                            India Jabal 3000bc
                            Veddhartha(Ventrue), Zapathusura(Ravnos)

                            Peru Adah (female) 2500bc
                            Gaians (shapechangers), Adah basically became Luna/Gaia. Yup, they’re NOT as old as they think they are. This is confirmed by demons (Devil’s Due). Native to the New World, but many migrated to Africa, Europe, and Asia. Note that the current theory is that Europeans introduced the concept of werewolves to the New World, so this assessment is purely fictional of course, it’s just the best I can come up with and still have Garou be relatively old as a race.

                            Central Asia, Irad the Strong aka Mehujael 2000bc
                            Ennoia(Gangrel), Set(Setites)

                            China Tubal (Xue) 1750bc
                            Kuei-jin, aka Chinese vampires, had approximately 5 disciples who later became the Quincunx.

                            3rd Gen
                            Assamites: Haquim, he invested in the Middle Eastern peninsula (Persians).
                            Brujah (female): Brujah (Greek Athenian) who was originally a 4th gen of Illyes, (male) who himself was a Greek Spartan. Illyes diablerized Troile. Note that Celerity came first, not Temporis. Eventually became the Phonecian Carthaginians, because she was pissed that the Greeks fell and the Romans took over. Brujah eventually invested in Portugal and Spain, not just Greece, once Carthage was razed.
                            Cappadocians: Cappadocius/Osiris, Originally Egyptian (competed with Set), but gave up on them for the Hittites/Turks after Egypt fell. However, he never invested as much effort into a civilization again. Prefers to enter any culture that has a significant amount of death worship, like Venice, New Orleans, Mexico’s Day of the Dead, Egyptian pyramids, the catacombs in Rome, etc. Became very Italian later.
                            Followers of Set: Originally a somewhat decent person who saw what Cappodocius had created, and wanted it. Lasombra wanted Egypt as well, so he began corrupting Set at the same time Cappodocius gave up on Egypt. Despite Set’s devolving ethics, he entrenched himself in Egypt which frustrated Lasombra into leaving for the Minoans. Set arrived in Egypt with the Hyksos invaders, as their deity. Later on he became ever more evil, and was demonized by the Egyptian culture. Soon after, Shaitan (Baali, 4th Generation childe of Set) was sired. During the height of Egyptian power, Shaitan diablerized Set.
                            Gangrel (female): Ennoia, controlled the Scandinavians/pagan Europeans
                            Lasombra: Angra Mainyu/Apep, Competed with & corrupted Set for Egypt during the Hyksos invasion, but later gave up for the Minoans who became the Sea Peoples. They in turn became the Spanish.
                            Malkavians: Malkav (male), very Babylonian, but his childer migrated all over Europe and Russia.
                            Followers of Set/Setites: Set (himself corrupted by Lasombra) aka Hyksos invader of Egypt
                            Nosferatu: Absimillard. His childe the Matriarch, a 4th Gen, liked the Southern Germans in particular, but she didn’t really invest in a people, as she wandered to the largest human cities. Cripples and social reject/survivor types.
                            Ravnos: Zapathasura, got into the Indian Romani migration into Europe. In 1099 Jerusalem, Zapathasura was diablerized by Tremere. In many ways, he is Rakshasa.
                            Salubri: Saulot, the monotheistic religions instead of a specific people. They end up influencing much of the medieval and Renaissance Church, displacing the Lasombra (the latter is far too fractious).
                            Toreador (female): Arikel/Ishtar/Aphrodite/Astarte who will eventually become French/Gallics
                            Tremere: Austrians, and invested most spectacularly in Hitler and the Nazis.
                            Tzimisce: The Eldest adopted the European Slavs/Rus.
                            Ventrue (female): Veddhartha, an Indian, who liked the Indus river valley types, then ancient Romans (then Holy Roman Empire, then Germans). Basically she followed the “power groups” to a certain extent.

                                            I wouldn't go so far. If we want to have some historical accuracy even knowing that the lore is deliberately written in a vage, obscure and inconclusive to create a mistery I would use some criteria to narrow down the possibilities.

                                            I start with the time line of human condition (https://www.southampton.ac.uk/~cpd/history.html).
                                            Around 10.000 BCE we are moving from hunting nomads behaviour to farming settlements.

                                            The part below is from https://www.history.com/topics/pre-h...rtile-crescent
                                            ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                                            The Fertile Crescent around 9000 BCE had began to practice agriculture by domesticating sheep and pigs around 11,000 to 9,000 B.C.
                                            Domesticated plants, including flax, wheat, barley and lentils, first appeared around 9,500 B.C.

                                            One of the oldest known Mesopotamian cities, Nineveh (a) (near Mosul in modern Iraq), may have been settled as early as 6,000 B.C. Sumer civilization arose in the lower Tigris-Euphrates valley around 5,000 B.C (b).

                                            The origins of Sumer civilization are debated, but archaeologists suggest Sumerians had established roughly a dozen city-states by the fourth millennium B.C., including Eridu and Uruk in what is now southern Iraq.

                                            Sumer is the earliest known civilization in ancient Mesopotamia and may have been the first human civilization anywhere in the world. They called themselves the Sag-giga, the “black-headed ones.”

                                            Ancient Sumerians were among the first to use bronze. They pioneered the use of levees and canals for irrigation. Sumerians invented cuneiform script, one of the earliest forms of writing. They also built large stepped pyramids called Ziggurats.
                                            ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

                                            The First city is placed around the crescent fertile and it is described as an exiting important city. The tale follows and Cain arrives at this city. Therefore we must rule out anything that comes before 9000 BCE because the tale tells about a very import and big city.

                                            The reasoning goes on. It is said that Cain when arrived at this city, later referenced as Enoch, had been wandering around and might have visited the Sumerians and brought back some technology from that people. Well, assuming this is true we narrow down even further the possibilities and rue out anything before the Sumer civilization, so nothing before 6000 BC. The next exclusion is due the fact that the Sumer civilization must be already there sometime in order to develop knowledge.

                                            It is not a strong rule and this can be overlooked but to develop technology nowadays we rely on language to formalize the knowledge so others can read and understand what to do. If we assume that this was a sine-qua-non condition than we must accept that cuneiform written was present at 3400 BCE (oldest tablet found so far) (c).
                                            - If we don't overlook the writing requirement than it is possible to accept that Cain was around the Sumerian cities somewhere between 4500 BC ( from (b) ) and 3400 (b) and brought what he learned to this city later known by the WW Lore as Enoch.
                                            - If we overlook than Cain returns from the Sumerians after 3400 BCE.

                                            Now we have one thing to assume. The city was there before Cain and it was bug for the standards by that time. So maybe it was settled around the same time as Nineveh, meaning 6000 BCE.
                                            The city becomes The First City after Cain arrives and rules it. So it might be happened:
                                            Hypothesis1 - somewhere between 4500 BCE ( from (b) ) and 3400 BCE (b) OR
                                            Hypothesis2 - close as possible from 3400 BCE

                                            The WW Lore also tells us that this city was destroyed by the Deluge. But it is unclear what deluge is the one. The WW Lore from (https://whitewolf.fandom.com/wiki/Deluge) gives us:
                                            - Genesis Flood Narrative - Great Flood to circa 2350 BCE
                                            - Black Sea Deluge - plausible inspiration to circa 5600 BCE

                                            If we established that the First City as the Lore now defines was destroyed by a Flood than we must rule out the Black Sea Deluge from 5500/5600 BCE.
                                            It induces us to conclude that Cain ruled from 4500 BCE until 2350 BCE OR, at least from 3400 BCE till 2350 BCE depending on what hypothesis are we assuming to be true.

                                                            I’d personally set the Flood at about 12,900 years ago to align with the Younger Dryas comet impact theory and similarly tie the First City to an Atlantis situated at the Eye of the Sahara (which back then was a tropical savanna with lakes and streams) that lies just south of the Atlas Mountains (and is also where the historian Heroditus placed Atlantis).

                                                            It should be noted that the actual story of Atlantis didn’t include any sort of superscience... they were considering an advanced civilization because they had agriculture, architecture, astronomy and an organized religion. Also worth noting is that Atlantis was just the capital and the king Atlas divided the realm up between his ten male heirs.

                                                            Just a bit of stretching and you could make that 13 heirs and say what mortals call “Atlantis” was actually the First City and survivors carried the stories to Egypt where they were recorded (note too that Egyptian mythology says they were originally a colony established by a great power to the West).

                                                            When one considers the technological backslide of the Bronze Age collapse (we still can’t decypher Linear A because literally nothing yet found that could translate it has survived) you could conceivably drop a Neolithic or early bronze age civilization in c. 14-15k year ago that was wiped out by the flood accompanying the Younger Dryas comet impact c. 13k years ago and humanity basically had to rebuild from there finally getting back on track about the time of the earliest Levantine, Egyptian and Indian settlements.

                                                            Throw in Neanderthal* werewolves who once hunted Humanity to near extinction c. 50-60k years ago until we finally found an edge that could fight them (vampires? ghouldom?) and you’ve got yourself a greatest hits of Cryptohistory.

                                                            * https://treeofwoe.substack.com/p/when-orcs-were-real (they use orc, ogre and boogeyman and even link it to our ancestral fear of the dark, but in WoD cosmology I can’t help but think of the Garou Impergium and that one of their intermediate forms is said to resemble a Neanderthal).
