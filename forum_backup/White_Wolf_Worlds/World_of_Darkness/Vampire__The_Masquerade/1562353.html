    FAQ on 6th edition

    07-30-2026, 06:13 PM
    Vampire: The Masquerade 6th Edition FAQ - Paradox Interactive
    https://www.paradoxinteractive.com/games/world-of-darkness/news/vtm-sixth-edition-faq
    World of Darkness


    Hello Vamily,

    Thank you for the amazing reception to the announcement of Vampire: The Masquerade 6th Edition. We’re so happy to finally share the alpha playtest with you!

    We know many of you have questions about what we’re doing, and we’ll do our best to answer them in the upcoming months. We’ve got many dev diaries coming to cover these topics in-depth, but for now we wanted to put together a brief FAQ to cover some of the most commonly asked questions that we’ve seen from the community.

    Note that we’re still in early development and a lot of things are still subject to change. If you have more questions, don’t hesitate to reach out to us on our social media channels or on the World of Darkness Discord.



    General White Wolf Questions: Why are we doing a 6th Edition?

    We want to create a solid baseline to support various styles of play, and a strong kickoff point for settings that will come after we’ve created the new Vampire: The Masquerade book.



    Does this mean all 5th Edition products are out-of-date/not usable at my table?

    The 6th Edition line of books will have different mechanics, but many of the same characters and metaplot advancements will remain relevant. That means that, while not fully backwards-compatible, much of the lore in the 5th Edition books will carry over to the 6th Edition books.



    What about Mage, Changeling etc - are they not receiving 5th editions?

    They are not, but that doesn’t mean that some of them won’t get some attention in the future.



    What about Werewolf and Hunters - are they receiving new editions already?

    At the moment the White Wolf devs are fully focused on the new Vampire: The Masquerade edition.



    V6-specific Questions:

    Will we have to learn new systems, i.e. Hunger mechanics vs Blood pool? Yes, there will be new systems and mechanics to learn, such as “Drama”, “Quickening”, “Choose Your Pain”, “Devil’s Bargain” and the new “Beast/Nature” tracker. But don’t worry, playing and running the game are both easier and faster. You need fewer steps and less prep to jump right in, and the rules help you push play forward without friction.



    Will there be more metaplot progression?

    Yes. The history of the World of Darkness will be preserved, advanced and represented in the rules.



    What’s Drama?

    Storytellers now have a resource called “Drama” that they can spend to complicate the PCs’ circumstances or introduce new twists into the Story.



    What’s Quickening?

    Quickening is a new mechanic that rewards players for rolling well, giving them a resource they can spend to improve their chances in future situations.



    Will I be able to use my V5 dice at the table?

    No, V6 uses a different system to count successes and failures, so we use regular D10 dice. We’ve also made changes to the Hunger system, so the Hunger dice are not relevant to V6.



    Where did my Health and Blood Points go?

    Health and Blood Points have been combined into a single resource called Vitae.



    What’s the Humanity Scale?

    The Humanity tracker has been updated to the Humanity Scale, which tracks the inner struggle between your monstrous and humane sides, as they constantly press their urges onto you.



    How does physical and social conflict work now?

    Physical and social conflict share a unified procedure, making both equally important and supported by the rules.



    How will the Discipline powers change?

    Each Discipline will have multiple powers, and many Discipline powers now scale with you, growing in power as you gain dots. The alpha playtest shows off many of these new powers, and the final book will include powers for each Discipline up to 5 dots.



    How many clans will be in the final book?

    There will be 14 clans total in the book; the alpha playtest includes 7 so far (Brujah, Gangrel, Lasombra, Ministry, Nosferatu, Toreador, and Ventrue).



    Can I play as the different stages of Vampirism?

    Yes, the final book will include rules for playing a ghoul, a duskborn, a neonate, an ancilla, a caitiff, or an elder.



    What about playing as Sabbat or other sects?

    In V6 you will be able to align with the Camarilla, the Anarchs, the Sabbat, Independents or The Unbound.

    Is there a release date yet?

    No, not yet. We are still in the alpha phase of development and want to ensure we can polish the material and implement the valuable feedback we’re receiving before we announce specifics like a release date or cost.

      • Beast and Nature Tracker: The inner struggle between the Beast and the remains of your mortal conscience is represented by the Beast and Nature Tracker. Every night, both sides of you fight for dominance. It’s up to you to decide which side you feed and which side you try to contain.
      • Frenzy and Outburst Rules: When you ignore one side of your internal struggle for too long, its impulses can grow too strong for you to resist. In these situations, you risk partially losing control of your character, who is compelled to act in ways to satiate the neglected side.
      At a glance, this system sounds like the coolest addition. Go too bestial and your Humanitas can frenzy as your conscience screams that this is lower than it's willing to go.

      Demeanor's gone, since Vampires are liars and manipulators and their demeanors can be anything they imitate, but Nature remains, and is part of the mechanic for how the Vampire's human side imposes itself. During Outburst, the Vampire's impulses reflect their Nature, and in Frenzy, their impulses reflect their clan curse.

      I have a soft spot for clever and elegant attempts to impose order on chaos (I was born that way, my moon sign is Virgo), and someone was cooking when they came up with this. It's very aesthetically pleasing to me, on paper.

              It's a pretty small thing, but I can't really get myself past the wealth resources, but vehicles as a separate resource you have to put points into. But then I've never really been into separating off things you can spend money to have as a separate thing to spend points on from spending points on money. I suppose they could just clarify that you can in fact spend money on goods and services. It otherwise just feels odd and almost punitively granular, especially in a game whose only really clearly embodied statement seems to be wanting a simplified and streamlined experience.

              Other minor little bits and bobs: I'm amused at them basically giving into "fine, vampires can have sex and enjoy sex" without the grudging/passive aggressiveness that has accompanied that previously.

              I'm in favour of it taking being close to a bonfire to trigger fire fear frenzy as an explicit thing. Better for historical games certainly. As far as, "Fine, your supernatural monster can just make a normal defense roll to dodge bullets", a lot of it seems to be very "we're going to try to lean into things people expect from general modern vampire media experiences at this point".

              Otherwise honestly I was cold to a bunch of it. The rules seem to be saying that you're now very, very limited in how many total discipline dots you can have, which I'm pretty bleah on. I mean it's certainly hitting some personal preferences that the SI is not being treated as much of a thing from what little info there is, and that touchstones seem to have gone the way of all flesh (I think there were ways to salvage it, but the v5 implementation was awful, and that showed in the ceaseless parade of the majority of suggested ideas for use the books would keep giving for them being to use them to threaten and punish players). I'm not really into no passive bonuses from disciplines anymore, or sectioning off some powers as clan only.

              People super into V5 are probably going to be irked that it's not really anything like V5, people who don't want an extremely streamlined VtM experience (which seems to be its goal) are not going to be happy with that. People who are just desperate to hear the Sabbat are an official playable option again will be happy about that. People who prefer VtM to... how to put it, be about VtM, in the way that D&D isn't really about fantasy overall at this point so much as it is about itself, will likely not be thrilled by some of this stuff (I know people who lost their shit about even grudging "vampires can have sex" for instance when previous editions would note such, in some way)

              Feels like an extremely mixed bag at the moment.
