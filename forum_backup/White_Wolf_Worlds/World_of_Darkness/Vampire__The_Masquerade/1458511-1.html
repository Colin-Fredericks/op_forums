    I don't think the Tremere would have a worse time in the cam with the Tzimisce in power. I think they wouldn't be in the Cam at all.

    From an in-game perspective, the Tremere managed to get their place by offering their magical services to the Ventrue. The Tzimisce don't need that as they have their Kolduns. From an out-game perspective, the Tzimisce is already a clan with a mystic outlook, so it would still make sense for them to have Kolduns or at least other kind of magical support than the Tremere.

    The Tremere and Ventrue could be easily exchanged by the Giovanni as a support on the corporate and criminal worlds where the Tzimisce have less of a hold. They would give the Cam the same areas of influence, but with another dynamic. The Tremere could be relegated to a bloodline, maybe the Salubri remain an Independent clan (keeping some or most of its reputation pre-Tremere, including as arrogant, self-righteous pricks), and the Ventrue could be actually controlling the Sabbat if they keep their cool, or be relegated to their Antitribu cousins.

    Such arrangement would make for a very different beast in the game in general, but may be interesting to run. It isn't the most probable alternate setting, but I do think it is a plausible one with relatively few adjustments.

    But then, it does have its Achilles Heel: it lacks vanilla. I'm not sure how far we can go in a game without vanilla.

While Dominate and Presence make ruling by an iron hand easier they do not their wielders to grow into competent leaders quote on the opposite having these is likely to make you a poorer leader since you start to rely your rule solely on these powers, that is you rule by forcing others to serve and thus breed enemies for yourself. Auspex provodes tools to rise to the position you wish to have and is much more conductive to ruling wisely and with foresight.

            Animalism on the surface this only give one power over the animals of the land, which back in the dark ages does actually enforce the idea that you rule your whole land as even the animals bow down to you. And since you can control the predators in the woods you can keep the villages under more tight control through fear of the wild. But much more importantly, and often forgotten when Animalism is spoken of, it give one power over the Beast within all Vampires. The Ventrue may bend wills of their servants or force them to love them but still can die under their servants claws and fangs should they just momentarily loose themselves to their Beasts. The Ventrue are also slaves to their Beasts where a Tzimisce hold their Beast in thrall and that of others as well. For all their precense Vampires are controlled by their Beasts, it drives their hunger, it makes their society so predatory. The fear of the Beast, and loosing to it, is the driving force behind the whole philosophies of the Kindred, Paths , Roads and yes even Humanity are all just methods of trying to keep the Beast in check. So in a society of predators Animalism can be surprisingly effective.

            Vicissitude, this is made famous through the efforts of the Metamorphosists who are the undeniable masters of the craft and the Szlachta and Vohzd used in open war. Still it, as mentioned, provides a powerful tool even outside those uses. As mentioned it can be a powerful tool used to keep the silence of the blood, it can be wanted ability for a society whose members are so unchanging as the Vampires are. Think about it. They can help you get or loose tattoos, lengthen or change your hair style, give you that shape you have wanted for a hindred years. These are all useful things but require trust and do not help in leadership that is true. Still for a leader this can be a powerful art. It provides you with warriors much more fierce than any other, assassins crafted to a specific look and able to infiltrate anywhere, it is no illusion to be pierced, and it provides both the stick and the carrot for your vampire subjects. Serve well and be revarded beauty beyond compare transgress and be punished with eternal scarring or much worse.

            Ventrue, Toreador and Lasombra all rule like men and are well suited to rule over men where as Tzimisce rule like Vampires and are well suited to rule Vampires. They are masters of the Beast, insightful and informed rules with means to reward or punish the otherwise unchanging beings that Vampires are. Also they are the Clan that can create the most effective armies without sacrificing immortal lives and throwing Vampires into the front lines. This quality alone should be quite a selling point for letting then lead instead of Ventrue who just want to throw other Kindred Clans onto the front lines, which is also why Gangrel finally left Camarilla getting tired of their immortalities being sacrifices so that the Ventrue could sit on their thrones. And this is before we take Kolduns, who are the true masters of the land, into account, but they are the exceptions like the Metamorphosists not your average Tzimisce.

            In summation I feel that on average the Tzimisce are much more suited to rule Vampiric society than most, but they also have a reputation created by the 1-2% that join the ranks of the Metamorphosists rule the minds of many players and st’s.
