            Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
            Is it, though? Because saying that the vampire are from the Middle East and the same race but culturally the country they were Embraced in doesn't seem like a big colonialist statement.

            Mind you, part of this may be the fact they canonized Caine as the origin.

            But the fact is that if they're not Cainites and have different rules.
            Yeah, the idea of a western-inspired vampire isn't terribly problematic, the issue is with VtM having its roots in the Bible and and the setting generally going, "Yup, the Bible is correct and other religions/cultures are wrong." You have a handful of other alternative origin like the Followers of Set, but the rest of the gameline makes it pretty clear that Set is full of bullshit and he's not really the first vampire or anything like that. it's pretty clear that those guys are wrong. And you have say, the African vampires, but it's also painfully obvious that their origin story is also wrong and they're descendants of the Biblical vampires due to the way their Generation limits work.

            I wish VtM was more culturally agnostic and presented a lot of different possibilities for Vampiric origins. The idea that Book of Genesis is essentially a historical document is cool for the type of game VtM is supposed to be, but it's also problematic because of what that says about the history of other cultures. Since, by the time KotE was written, you couldn't really divorce Kindred from the Biblical origins (despite a few half-hearted attempts to do so), I think trying to create another type of origin story for a different part of the world (the 10,000 Immortals) was a cool idea. It didn't work out in practice, but I really respect the attempt and think we got the chassis for a really cool game, and IMO, the best overall setting for the World of Darkness.

                    At the end of the day, the problem with Orientalism, or any other racist otherism, is to write about a place and culture without input from the people.

                    KotE is the "best bad idea" in WoD, a lot of people like it, and it certainly could have worked with minor adjustments.

                    But it was a part of the process of making Asia work entirely different from the rest of the setting just for the sake of being "more Asian" for American readers, all the while ignoring what actual Asian players wanted for their countries in the setting. And in the process making the new "Asian" options the only one canonical for most of the place.

                    Making KotE a rarer splat and letting most of Asia be "normal" Vampires would probably solve most problems. The "Bible is True" problem is also a thing, but a broader problem than just Orientalism. But again: have consultants that can bring input from actual Asians for a change is the actual key to solve the conundrum.
