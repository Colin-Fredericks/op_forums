                                                Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
                                                Isn't the shitty latin intentional? The Sabbat gave their hierarchy clergical terms for a joke after all.
                                                No, the original authors just didn’t know what they were doing. The results don’t resemble anything that actual Latin speakers (including the founders and elders of the Sabbat) would think to come up with. Also, if you want to make a joke on clerical titles and terms, you don’t mangle Latin word roots into nonsensical phrases; using the actual Latin in a blasphemous context is a much better tactic and one that shows up in actual occult contexts. But even then, there’s a lot you can do within the Latin to lampoon the Church, but that would require knowledge of the language, which the authors have never had or bothered to seek out.

                                                As for the idea that the shovelheads have mangled it over the centuries, that also doesn’t hold water. We have countless real-world examples of what illiterate lay populations do with received terminology like this, and it doesn’t in any way resemble what we’re meant to believe the Sabbat use. Phonetic and morphological simplification, perhaps even turning chunked phrases into words, I could see; coming up with Latin roots the original founders never would have used in this context, mixing up nouns and adjectives, throwing things into completely different declensions—nobody would ever do any of that unless they had no knowledge of Latin and were trying to create something from scratch (i.e. the real-world explanation). In reality, people tend to just drop inflections they don’t understand, not add completely nonsensical ones. Or better yet, just use the vernacular they’re already familiar with.

                                                In short, they either need to go with actual Latin or just stick with English. There’s no way for someone with no proficiency in Latin to convincingly model something that people with proficiency in Latin would come up with, regardless of context or purpose. We also should keep in mind that knowledge of Latin was quite widespread in the traditional stomping-grounds of the Sabbat right up until the 20th century. It’s anachronistic to treat it like this arcane thing that nobody actually knows about. Also, most living languages spoken by Sabbat recruits have some of the same features that the books fuck up with the Latin, making it obvious the creators were English speakers.
