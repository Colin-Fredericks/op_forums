Transfer $500K (liquidate some stocks for a paper lose while you're at it, since your stocks portfolio isn't a banking account, hence you need to transfer it to spend it), put on the form that it's for a real estate bid, put in a low ball bid, lose to someone else.                             There is a significant difference with Resources, in that it's a common Background, with even an implied idea that having it means your character has some idea of how to make use of it in broad strokes (ex; V20 says to call for Wits + Resources as a potential roll for managing your character's finances). Rare Discipline and Thaumaturgy Paths are pretty easy to tell if a player might get disruptive with them by trying to leverage that sort of "homework." Coming up with reasons to have them in the first place, and likely Knowledges like Science to explain how their characters would know specific applications are pretty big flags a player intends to use some of the powers that can be extremely potent with some creativity.

                            IME it's far easier to deal with people that don't know specific in-depth topics in play, because we naturally abstract most of that anyway. A big part of all these mechanics in the first place is so that characters can perform actions that the players can't. It's great if a player does some research if they make a character that was an avid rock climber before being Embraced so they can naturally role-play various things. But it's easy enough to hand wave, "Can I use Athletics instead of Survival for some rope work since my character should know how to tie ropes climbers use?" and not worry about the specific knot names and which is best for what

                            Most players that do know topics in depth do also know not to go overboard with it because it's a game where we're all trying to have fun. But it's a lot harder to deal with players who are correct about various obscure topic, make characters that have every reason to know those things, and then use that to try to "get one over" on the ST or even other players.
