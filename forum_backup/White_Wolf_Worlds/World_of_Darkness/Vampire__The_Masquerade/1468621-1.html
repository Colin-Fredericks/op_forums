    Originally posted by Karos View Post
    Copying the V5 Timeline I started making in another thread, and re-focussing it to Gehenna, you get something like the below.
    Thanks. Hope my ideas seed yours well. To make things easier I'll take the liberty to un-quote the timeline.

    My draft stops at the beginning of 2005 for now, I'll update it later.

    1998
    1) Destruction of the Tremere Antitribu. - RCT

    1999
    1) The Week of Nightmares begins.
    2) Zapathasura, the Ravnos Antediluvian awakens.
    3) The Red Star Appeared, at the culmination of the Week of Nightmares.
    4) Zapathasura, is destroyed.
    5) Investigations on the Week of Nightmares begin, resource spending on Masquerade maintenance start to ramp up.

    2000
    1) Death of Melinda Galbraith - MCbN
    2) Keeping the lid on the Week of Nightmares shut is increasingly hard and mortal opposition is on the rise, although feel can be called Hunters.

    Circa. 2000-2001
    1) Aajav and Vasantasena work out the impersonation of Galbraith by Sikorsky and work to cover it up to ensure Sabbat unity. - BJD
    2) Maria Sandoza may also be aware and instruct the Sabbat Inquisition to support/allow the conspiracy. - BJD

    2001
    1) 9/11 attacks.
    2) De Pollonia claims partial responsibility for the 9/11 attacks. - BJD
    3) The Red Star is officially noted by NASA.
    4) At an unrelated meeting in the day the Red Star is first seen, agents of several organizations discuss signs of the 1999 Typhoon being linked to "blank body terrorists" and the possibility of another wave of attacks. Lack of solid evidence and difficulty on getting attention from their superiors keeps the group from amassing information in time.
    5) The group meets again 6 days later with news of the assassination of Ahmed Shah Massoud. Finally armed with evidence of blank body involvement they officially form their coalition. The group later to be informally known as the Second Inquisition is born in September 9.
    6) It is too late, though. Only two days later the deadliest terrorist attack in history leaves at least 3,000 dead and 6,000 injured in the US. Although the role of the Sabbat will never be confirmed, the attack has surgical effect: it destroys key evidence of the blank bodies threat. At the insistent of newly arrived US agents the group declares its official founding at September 11.

    2002
    1) The Red Star is officially designated '28978' Ixion (Greek equivalent to Caine).
    2) The Society of St Leopold reaches the Coalition. They're still fringe to most of the group, but some agents pay them more attention.

    2003
    1) Op: Iraqi Freedom begins.
    2) Sabbat follow soldiers headed to the Middle East as part of Op: Iraqi Freedom. - BJD
    3) Project FIRSTLIGHT is formed by the US intelligence agencies, headed by Felicity Price. - V5CG
    4) Spending on the Masquerade is on the rise overall, and cover-up of Zapathasura is becoming untenable despite current geopolitical state making it easier.

    2004
    1) The Withering starts and lasts throughout the year. Although it is theorized to begin at January 21st with the 3rd Generation, it wouldn't be confirmed until it reached the 6th generation. It lasts exact 12 lunar months, reaching a new generation each time, up to the 14th. Later investigations could confirm its effects reaching up to 4th generation.
    2) By April, with resources running thin and challenges increasing, the Zapathasura Cover starts to break down, leaking information to bigger actors while Kindred attention is increasingly elsewhere. In April 22nd the Inner Council declares Imminent Masquerade Collapse and starts to debate what to do from there, although this information trickles down slowly.
    3) From July on the Camarilla goes through what's arguably its worst crisis. Coordinated actions from what starts to be called the Second Inquisition considerably hinders control over mortal institutions on a critical moment, the Withering reaches the 8th and 9th generations, making its full effects public knowledge among the Damned, hunters activity is on a peak, the loss of most Blood Bonds even with Ghouls creates localized chaos, accusations of diablerie are on the rise.
    4) In December it is formally recognized among intelligence agencies the world around that some form of anomalous activity exists and at least two non-human conspiracies got out of hand. They work to keep the lid on this for now, but prioritize information gathering. Finally the Coalition tries to report.

    2005
    1) The Withering stops at January 10th, although it isn't easy to notice at first. Most start to recover from its effects shortly after, some take longer. By July it has almost completely stabilized in current status.

    2008
    1) A SOCOM task force destroys the Prime Chantry in Vienna, destroying the Tremere Pyramid. - V5C

    2012
    1) Conclaves occur in Chicago and Prague, Theo Bell assassinates Hardestadt (and possibly Jan Pieterzoon). - V5C, VCG

    2) The Brujah formally leave the Camarilla. - V5CG

    2013
    1) The Gehenna Crusade begins in earnest. - BJD

    2) Queen Ann is decapitated in London, effectively ending the sterilisation of the city. - V5C

    3) Mithras attempts a ritual to restore himself to full power as a Methuselah during the purge of the city. He is believed killed but may have simply gone underground. - FoL
    4) Roger de Camden becomes Prince of Edinburgh and sets up the city as the headquarters of a newly revived Cult of Mithras. - FoL

    2016
    1) Lucita and Beckett attend the Palla Grande, having been invited by Sikorksy (still in the guise of Galbraith). - BJD
    2) Sikorsky (still in the guise of Galbraith) is destroyed. - BJD
    3) XXX Seraph/acting-seraphs are destroyed. - BJD
    4) The Fourth Sabbat Civil War begins. - BJD

    2018
    1) Kevin Jackson and Sierra Burrace successfully negotiate the defection of a portion of the Lasombra. The first sacrifice is a Sabbat soldier named Malankov. - V5CbN
    2) Carolina Valez is killed by the Lasombra after being forced to confess her "crimes." -V5CbN

    3) Julian Sims arranges for the diablerie of Banu Haqim Elder Aila by the Courier. He also experiments with a special formula that will make them stronger faster. - NR

    2019
    1) Sophia Langtry attempts a coup against Helena Panhard with Anarch leader Torque. She is killed and her adopted childe is blood bound to Henry Arturo. - CoNY

    2) Julia Sowinski is Embraced by Caren as part of the Lasombra defection to the Camarilla. - SoNY

    3) Prince Lettow of Tuscon, Arizona and Julian Sims of the Banu Haqim do battle with the Second Inquisition. They successfully kill Inquisitor Samantha Donati with the help of the Courier. - NR
    4) Jasper Knowles successfully brings a kindred, the Lampago, back from wightdom after driving dozens of others to the state to figure out a cure. He is subsequently killed by Julian Sims. - NR
    5) Prince Lettow is Beckoned. - NR

    8) Deputy Cecily Baine investigates the murder of Prince Samantha Merraine and determines it was her friend, Calder Wendt. - WT
    9) The Mortician's Army attacks the Camarilla court of the Twin Cities and fails to assassinate Justicar Ian Carfalx but kill a Primogen plus several lesser vampires. They make use of a double agent named Alejandra. - WT
    10) Calder Wendt is rejected as Prince by Ian Carfax, who appoints Cecily Bain instead. - WT

    11) The Mausoleum is attacked by the Second Inquisition with the covert help of the Harbingers of Skulls. Many senior Giovanni are killed. Augustus Giovanni's body disappears. - CotBG

    2020
    1) Baron Callahan of New York is murdered by the Camarilla. - SoNY
    2) Julia Sowinski investigates the death of Callahan and blackmails the Camarilla of New York to making her Lasombra Primogen. - SoNY

    3) Prince Arundel of Ottawa is kidnapped by the Quebec Tremere with the help of his Senescha, Eden Corliss. Eden's childe helps rescue him and stops her coup. - PoK
    4) Archon Adelaine attempts to eliminate Anarch leader Robert Ward by using his relationship with Arundel against him. This results in Adelaine's death and the destruction of her allies by a vengeful Prince Arundel. - PoK

    2021
    1) The Sabbat has completely dissolved, its upper-echelons largely eliminated, its Ancillae decimated, and the majority of its ranks made up of ravening shovelheads. The sect cedes the majority of its territory to its enemies. - V5STBH

    2) The SI purges the majority of L.A. - L.A.
    3) The surviving Anarchs (including Victor Temple, Nelli G, Jasper Hartwood, Annabelle, Nines Rodriguez, Gary Golden, Imalia, Mitnick, X, and Jeanette/Therese Voerman) begin rebuilding L.A. - L.A.
    4) Eva another surviving L.A. Anarch destroys the last of the Camarilla Tremere in L.A. - L.A.
    5) Eva also provides documents and equipment to the SI. - L.A.
    6) Annabelle departs for New York. - L.A.

    2028
    1) The Promise of 1528, between the Camarilla and the Giovanni (now Hecata) expires with unknown consequences. - CotBG

                    To me, the Masquerade is a fairly recent, newfangled idea. For ancient elders who have been around for thousands of years, when they wake up they're going to start acting like the blood gods they are. They're going to want to rule, not hide in the shadows from humans. These guys are going to flagrantly violate the Masquerade, and for some of them they might not have ever heard of a Masquerade and have no clue that they're supposed to be hiding. Some might embrace the usefulness of keeping their presence hiddin, but some of them will view the whole idea of hiding from humanity unfitting for god-kings. And once some vampires start blatantly breaking the Masquerade and projecting their existence as vampires the Masquerade crumbles.

                    Basically the Masquerade holds as long as the vast majority of vampires understand that it's in their best interest to keep hidden from humanity. But Gehenna is built on the idea that you have these ancient vampires of peerless power, who predate the existence and need of the Masquerade by thousands and thousands of years; vampires who are used to ruling over humanity as unliving gods, waking up to slaughter other vampires and resume their position as god-kings.

                    I just can't see the Masquerade lasting very long once a Gehenna War gets rolling.

                            Originally posted by Grumpy RPG Reviews View Post
                            That said, I see the fall of the Masquerade as more of a process than an event.
                            The thing is that V5 take us to a moment where this war has been raging already for some years. In fact, depending on how you look at it, it has been happening since 1999. So it's hard to imagine we're still in the initial stages of the End of the Masquerade.

                            We can have a fulfilling game about the End, this is what Time of Judgement was all about. I think among other things that a clear timeline helps to set a game during this period, or bring the events themselves in the present to play such a game.

                            But ultimately V5 isn't about playing the End of the World. It is about playing at the New World that came after. Remarkably unchanged in some aspects sure, but still the New World.

                            Here we can give our shots precisely about how this End happened. Not because this is necessary to play the End, but because it is necessary to play the after. This is to this day a glaring hole in the setting waiting to be filled. This is not a prophecy coming, anymore. This is background information, this is history.

                            Of course we can also give our shots about what we would have done differently, but it doesn't change that in the spirit of the thread this is about a world where the Gehenna War is a thing. And I think most agree that it should be a huge thing with sweeping consequences.

                            AnubisXy I agree, although I think it pays to consider what the Masquerade means. Those old vampires probably have a notion of the Masquerade, if by other names, obviously. But what's really new is the Camarilla's notion, that is now ubiquitous in Kindred society. Hiding as much your presence and nature as convenient is par of the course for any predator, and the basic notion of not shitting on your peers' strategies by giving up their secrets sounds reasonable as a proto-Masquerade they would know, follow and have a name for.

                            CTPhipps, this same question is more pressing to the Law of Caine than it being real or not. How you call the rule or when it came to be is immaterial as long as it is accepted, and a law of Masquerade seems to be very ancient as a notion. But what does that mean?

                            So, the Camarilla's reading isn't the only one around and, indeed, is new. It is objectively, demonstrably new. In fact, several societies that arguably had the power and means to hunt vampires existed prior to it and yet the Kindred are still alive. The Camarilla's party line paint the Masquerade in a very strict form for a reason, and pretends to have this as a natural rule for the Kindred, but it actually isn't.

                            Now, is the Camarilla's Masquerade a good idea? Maybe. Does it work? Well, it worked for more than 500 very hard years on secrecy. But it isn't natural or the only possible one, and certainly not a reading any vampire out on torpor for more than this span of time would even recognize.

                                    Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
                                    I see issues with the Masquerade as a Teething issue of our rapidly changing information age.
                                    If you see the Masquerade as a binary thing that is only broken when literally everyone knows the truth and believes it beyond doubt, then sure. But as I see it a Masquerade where people are fighting on the internet if this is truth or not, the way people fight over flat Earth and vaccines, is a failed Masquerade. At least in the way the Camarilla defines the Masquerade.

                                    More importantly, the point is the Masquerade as a game theme and its place in a world during/post-Gehenna War. Because the Masquerade doesn't really makes sense, not much more than vampires make sense, it exists because it is a theme of the game, not because it is a natural conclusion of it.

                                    So, thematically speaking, what do we have with the Gehenna War? This is a profound change in the status quo. Even if something that resembles that status quo remains, it does so in another context entirely. Then throwing the old style Masquerade and bringing a new set of themes around the notion of a Masquerade makes sense. It makes particular sense because the setting was built around the notion of an extremely fragile Masquerade, to the point that most of the laws and resources of the Kindred as a society are built around keeping this Masquerade.

                                    Keeping the old Masquerade in the face of the greatest war ever only weaken both themes for me. If the Masquerade was so resilient all along, what all the effort was for? If it can't affect the most obvious target, what the War is for? If I didn't wanted the fucking Gehenna to have an impact and instead stay in the old status quo... I would just do that. Like I sometimes factually do.

                                    No need for V5 lore, no need for Gehenna War or in any other form. Take V20, ignore metaplot and have fun. Use V5 rules if you want. Perfectly doable, and as fine as can be.

                                    That's why I complain about V5 not committing to an idea. Want to make the game be like in the old days? Fumble Gehenna, post-pone it or whatever. Want to go along with Gehenna happening? Accept the consequences, and it does include doing away with old style Masquerade.

                                    But doing away with old Masquerade doesn't mean doing away with any Masquerade, just changing what it means. Keeping it uncertain enough for the government to never recognize vampires as a thing is an option. Making true information hard to discern in the midst of junk data is another. Or just keeping people from knowing details about you, your domain, your community is other perfectly valid Masquerade, even more desperate to be kept in the midst of a larger information leakage.
