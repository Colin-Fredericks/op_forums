            DarkKing Religions mix a lot of attitudes, but overall there is an expectation that followers will study, and study hard. If this is to reach the same conclusions or what, doesn't change what's being asked is a scholarly approach.

            For this I'll conflate Noddism with the Sabbat religion for ease writing, they're not actually the same and the relationship is complicated.

            Think all Bible study groups and whatnot the average Christian church houses, even if to teach prescribed doctrine. Advanced classes frequently trade tips and stories on experiences during pray. This is a repeating motif.

            More dogmatic ones do the opposite to stop followers from "wandering dangerously through the text", but most want people that at the very least learn apologetics, if they have no good reason.

            And the religious side of the Sabbat does a lot of that and other takes. Doctrine teaches the members should study Noddism and the vampiric condition. The reality:
            1. From the beginning a lot of member pay only lip service to Noddism or Caine,
            2. Most Paths are religions themselves distinct from Noddism, they use excuses and pay lip service,
            3. A lot of members don't give a fuck, they're followers, not thinkers, and decidedly don't study as doctrine prescribes,
            4. A minority, big but still minority, actually do as they're told, study, yet half end up religious zealots like the Black Hand, the Sabbat Inquisition, etc.

            Part of what makes the Sabbat interesting is that it is a political philosophy wrapped under a religion for morale. The religion is real, but the overall fanaticism is not, or at least is more nuanced.

            And as religious practices frequently are IRL, if you focus on doctrine you miss what most members actually live there. For most of them, being the Sword of Caine isn't about study, doctrine or whatever. It's about the Ritae and the experiences you have with your siblings in Caine.

"Killing without studying death afterward" is a level 2 sin. This is probably where the difficulty comes from as someone attempting to "study" at a Blood Feast or during a Sabbat murder hobo crusade is probably hard to do. "Needlessly preventing a death" is a level 1 sin. This path doesn't have a problem with killing mortals. The real complication is the need to "study" it afterwards. This will create some odd behavior, but should not present too much a trouble. It's just more likely a Path adherent will prefer isolated "intimate" murders where she can study the effects rather than killing sprees.

                            Originally posted by JagneStormskull View Post

                            And killing to create shovelheads isn't arbitrary or without purpose, it's right there on the tin. The purpose is creating shovelheads.

                            Weren't the first Paths formulated things like Metamorphosis and Death and the Soul, since they were basically re-dos of medieval Roads and the Tzimisce founded the Path system?
                            Basically, yes. The Lasombra and Tzimisce both claim credit for the paths in their Revised clanbooks (Clanbook: Lasombra Revised, p. 26; Clanbook: Tzimisce Revised, p. 23), but they're both unreliable narrators. I think they probably contributed in their own ways, and moreso than most clans, though each claims they were the biggest contributors for obvious reasons.

                            Giovanni Chronicles II: Blood & Fire attributes most of the paths to those at the Black Monastery, meaning Cainites from a wide range of clans, but four paths were developed directly or indirectly by Lasombra and Tzimisce there, too (two apiece). The full list is here: https://whitewolf.fandom.com/wiki/Black_Monastery (Lady Vadislava and Zara Slatikov are Tzimisce; Lord Alexandre and Lord Marcus are Lasombra.)

                            That list fits the canon elsewhere, as Lady Vadislava worked on Death and the Soul, Slatikov (not in GC2, but added later in Chaining the Beast) worked on Metamorphosis (but contributed to others), and Lords Alexandre and Marcus contributed to Power and the Inner Voice. These three paths all derive from Dark Ages Roads (Bones, Metamorphosis, and Kings, IIRC), meaning they likely emerged early on.
