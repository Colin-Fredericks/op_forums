    Now that I've read others' posts and given some thought, I have some ideas. First, the Malkavian theme overall is an attempt to understand reality. And that Book of Nod excerpt has his asking "Why" which is a basic philosophical question. So in terms of what Malkav is actually doing is trying to understand existence. He's a philosopher, and probably most concerned about metaphysics and eschatology. Once he moves on to asking Caine "Why?", the next natural steps would be to ask God "Why?"

    In regards to Gehenna, which should be an end of the world scenario (I heavily dislike the idea that Gehenna can happen multiple times or that the game as we know it can continue afterwards), Malkav is probably most concerned with its actual purpose in the grand scheme of things. Why is this happening? What is the divine plan? What is Caine's role in it? What is my own and those of the other Antedeluvians? What is going to come afterwards?

    Only once he understands the purpose of such things, would Malkav really attempt to do anything. Because he doesn't know what he should do until then. And by the time Gehenna starts, Malkav probably still won't know. So best to do nothing until he does know. He hopes that before the actual End of the World comes, he will figure it out. Maybe he even has decided that he has a role to play, and therefore it is critical he must survive until the end so he can do whatever it is that he needs to do.

    Now this seems to make him a very passive Antedeluvian then at first glance. He has no master plan. He is not trying to build an empire. Or change the nature of reality through occult practice. Or destroy the other Antedeluvians. And so on.

    But I do think we can have Malkav do things. He observes. He studies. And he likely conducts "experiments". That leads to some practical ideas.

    First, it can explain why he is so protective of his own clan and produces things like the Malkavian Madness Network to give them an edge in survival. They are one of the primary means by which he conducts experiments (at some point, some of them are going to do something interesting).

    Second, he might intervene in some specific ways to preserve the existence of particular vampires (of his own clan or even of others) for the same purpose. He wants to see what happens if they survive or are successful. This does not always mean he only preserves lives of vampires. Sometimes "success" might mean the destruction of vampires. For example, maybe Malkav came to the conclusion that Saulot wanted to be destroyed. He finds that interesting. Why would Saulot want that? Oh, look, there's Tremere (Etrius, Goratrix, whoever) attempting to find an Antedeluvian to diablerize. Hmm, they aren't as smart as they think, and they're never going to find one. If I want to see what will happen by Saulot being destroyed like he wants, I should help them. Lasombra is always destroying his childer. What happens if I do something that encourages that Montano lad to stay loyal and always obey. What will Lasombra do then?

    Third, he will want to collect knowledge. He can't conduct every experiment himself. Other people will do lots of things on their own. He just needs to benefit from what they learn. If he wants to understand what Cappadocious has learned in his study on death, he needs someone who can gain that knowledge for him and bring it into the open. Maybe that explains why Lady Theophana joined the Conspiracy of Isaac - it was a way to bring out Cappadocius's knowledge to the greater population. Or any number of other incidents that seem contrived, incredibly lucky, or inexplicable.

    Fourth, we might use this as some kind of presence behind the Inconnu - who mainly seem to exist in the setting to monitor things.

    Fifth, we could decide any number of mysteries are critically important to Malkav like the nature of Golconda, what the Beast really is, and so on. He really wants to know everything about that, and some of his most important "studies" or "experiments" are based on that.

    Given this, the ST can still decide how Malkav approaches his increasing knowledge. How benevolent or malevolent his approach can suit whatever the ST wants. Not all knowledge is equal, and the older Malkav gets, he probably gets more philosophical and esoteric. So earlier on, there might be more practical approaches along the lines of natural philosophy, and later on it becomes more metaphysical.
