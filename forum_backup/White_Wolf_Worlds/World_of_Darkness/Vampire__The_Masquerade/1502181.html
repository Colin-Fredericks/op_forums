    Is the V5 rulebook an appropriate gift for a 19 year old?

    02-05-2023, 03:24 AM
    Last Thursday my eldest niece made a joke about going through a vampire phase and over the next couple of days I wondered how she would react to Vampire the Masquerade.

    I asked for her email address so I could send her the rulebook, but now I'm having second thoughts. As I mentioned in the title, my niece is nineteen, so technically she is old enough. But whether its because she's my niece, or because I'm way into middle age, part of me is suddenly doubting myself.

    How old would someone have to be before you felt comfortable introducing them to the game?
