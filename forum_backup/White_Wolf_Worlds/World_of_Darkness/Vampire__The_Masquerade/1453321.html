    Full-bloods Can NOT use Thin-blood Alchemy

    06-23-2021, 10:15 PM
    I've recently been watching L.A. By Night and in it the character Eva seems to have learned TBA, I've also done some searching and have seen some people allow non-thin-bloods to learn it. Let me preface this with the obvious "you're game, you're rules" but it is my contention that TBA is an EXCLUSIVLY thin-blood discipline on the bases that it require specifically thin-blood to work, as thin-blood has properties that "thicker-blood" does not have. I will defend this contention:

    (pg184) The Duskborn change as they feed. Thin-Blood Alchemy is the mastery of this process.

    From this we can see that TBA is an extension of the thin-blood’s ability to “pull” more from the blood they feed on. An ability the full-bloods do not have, at least not to the same functional extent (see below).

    (pg228) Drinking blood with intense temperament gives the drinker one additional die for dice pools involving a discipline that corresponds to that Resonance. This bonus lasts until the vampire’s next drink of blood dilutes it, or until the vampire’s system empties of blood when their Hunger reaches 5.

    From this we can see that a full-blood merely gets 1 additional die for pools, they must HAVE the discipline and even then they do not get extra dots or powers, they merely get a “boost” to their current powers.

    (pg113) Whenever a thin-blood feeds they gain one dot in one Discipline associated with the
    Resonance of the blood consumed, together with one level one power in that discipline. If the Resonance is Intense or stronger, they gain an additional dot together with a second power.

                  If you really look into what is "alchemy" then really, it doesn't make sense for thin-blood alchemy to exist. Alchemy is about, metaphorically speaking, turning lead into gold Thick blood is magical and divine, thin blood is mortal and mundane. Caine is 1, mortals are 0, and everything between is a decimal place. Caine is methaphorical Gold, mortals are metaphorical lead. Being half and half doesn't shouldn't give you an advantage, purity gives you an advantage. Purity is divinity, or at least, the absolute best state for magic to flourish, whilst thin blood is muddy and mundane, and really a rather terrible state to be in. WoD benefits a lot because it draws from actual esoteric practices and ideas, but this TBA? It's DnD alchemy, or pop-culture alchemy, it's simple potion making, devoid of wider metaphysical conotations, existing only for gameplay/because someone thought it would be cool. To truly be Alchemy, you can't limit it to thinbloods.
