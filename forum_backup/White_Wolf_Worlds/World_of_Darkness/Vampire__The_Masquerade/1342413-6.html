                    Going back to that thing about the Gangrel being pissed off that their childer are being used as shock troops, there is precedent for it.                     "They say that we’re a cold, hard lot. We abandon our childer and let them live or die on their own. It’s true, to a point. There’s also no mother like a Gangrel, and once the child has successfully survived the fall from the nest, a Gangrel sire can be like a lioness. The Tremere had been kidnapping our childer, twisting them into abominations. We were promised [joining the Camarilla] was a way to stop that from happening — a way to force the Tremere to give it up and seek peace." - V20, Lore of the Clans
                    Your kids are getting killed because they suck at surviving? Sucks to be them, pick a better childe next time.
                    Somebody is systematically attacking your kids or getting them killed? Now the clan is pissed.

                    Similarly, back then the other clans brushed off the Gangrels losses and concerns about the Tremere, so after repeatedly getting their concerns and opinions dismissed and being talked down to, the older Gangrel who aren't already done with the "high clans'" shit may well've decided the Camarilla wasn't worth it.

                    They've also been arguing about the decision at gatherings since the decision to join was made (a decision they almost didn't agree to). A lot of them never agreed, and those that did were often very meh about it.

                            That is indeed a very interesting way too look at it! The Gangrel are really into the trope of not liking change at their surroundings or themselves, and if it changes (in a bad way) they will either lash out or move-on, they don't like to be too confrontational, like an animal, they prefer to bark or bite if it gets too close, but don't much like to have to make slow changes, longtime plans and dragged confrontations. It's like they adapt, but at the same time they don't want to change, it is a weird conformism. Although there is no law right? So some might just be simply carefree.

                                                            One thing I always thought was interesting in the High/Low Clan divide is the question of the rights, obligations and preferences each has, and the differences between them. I imagined a separation akin to noble and merchant classes at the (european medieval) time. Like a Low would have to offer access to a Heaven or Herd he/she/it has if a High asks in a moment of need, or that if a Low insults a High, the one insulted has the right to defend its honor (and put the Low to death). Things like that.
                                                            Besides that, there is the construct of clan unity some Clans like to promote, I assume the Ventrue, Toreador & Tremere triumvirate like to do it best. That is why they are more worried about the actions of each member of their own clan, and coax and mold them to act accordingly, all the while trying to make other clan individuals look bad to pain an encompassing image of a Clan of freaks, barbarians, crooks or monsters. That way, when a X clan dude does something, they can all use it as an excuse to charge and tax that Clan more.

                                                            As such, I see that when Xavier acts like a "hey I'm crazy, I am seeing things that don't exist and all your vision is wrong about it, and you're all idiots by not seeing it too", maybe then the other Clans just used it as an excuse to grind and press more favors, troops and resources from the Gangrel. "You gotta make reparations for the slide and insane allegations of Xaviar that made you all look bad" would probably be a common speech. So, the Ven-Tor-Mere trio (and others of course) could've have squish every last drop they could from Gangrels deliberately, or weighted their hand way too much. So, more by the actions of other Clans over the Gangrel than the Clan itself, they felt "fuck, this ain't worth it" and were moving out, tired of getting the shaft. There is even a chance most Gangrel don't like to be thrown in the bucket of a Clan, where you got to act and respond as a representative from your clan. As such, when they are pressed for "reparations" of some sort, they get sick and tired of Camarilla worldviews.

                                                            In summary, maybe Cam used Xaviar's scandal as a grievous outrage against them, and used it as an excuse to clutch and submit the Gangrel Clan even further, and to them this was the last drop of a long series of many small and some eventual (and big) problems they had with the sect. So they left. They were being treated as a medieval Low Clan again, that was inherently owing obligation and forgiveness to the all-mighty High Clans, so yeah, better go Anarch. I believe this would give more context to the Gangrel exodus.

                                                            Strange... When coincidence seems too convenient, I prefer to call it fate.
