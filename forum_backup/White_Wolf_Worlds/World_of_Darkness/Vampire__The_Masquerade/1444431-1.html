                        My Lasombra thoughts?

                        1. Honestly, I'm not a big fan of the new weakness. I think the Lasombra having a weakness with technology comes out of left field and frankly is a ridiculously crippling flaw in the Modern World. Harry Dresden is very hard to believe having it in the Modern World, let alone one of the most powerful Clans. Its so ubiquotous that I don't think that it matches other Clan Flaws but overwhelming is worse than the others even as they dialed down the Nosferatu, Ventrue, and Malkavian weaknesses.

                        This is adding the mirror distortion thing on top of it that is a classic weakness of the undead. I feel like keeping the Mirror weakness and adjusting it while leaving Lasombra able to be recorded is something they should have done. Maybe add an additional quality like making them vulnerable to silver instead. So yes, the technology weakness isn't canon in my games. A Lasombra without a sports car or limousine or supervillain lair is not a true Lasombra.

                        2. I think Lasombra joining the Camarilla makes a pretty good balance and the story justified it well for me with the discovery that their Antediluvian is not dead. I feel like that is the kind of thing that would utterly shake the foundations of the Clan among the highest and lowest. The highest realizing that they underestimated the kind of fight that they were taking on and the lowest getting a taste of REAL power.

                        I made a comparison between the two "Clans of Faith" a long time ago with the fact that the Lasombra represent those who religion is a tool to exploit and that don't actually believe while the Setites are the fanatics who believe to the point it utterly warps their worldview. The Sabbat was never meant to be a thing Lasombra died FOR, it was a thing the Lasombra created for other suckers to die for them. So them noping the fuck out of it was something I believed in.

                        3. I think the Lasombra joining the Camarilla is something that they lucked out on. They would have done it over decades if there wasn't a variety of factors that kind of percipitated it happening all at once now. The Gehenna War meant that it was NOW time to make the jump for them but the Camarilla were only in a place to make it happen because the Tremere down, the Brujah were gone, the Gangrel had already left, and the Anarchs were on the rise. Plus the Beckoning meant that the Camarilla's biggest guns and smartest players were gone. In short, the Lasombra knew that now was the only time in the history of the world they could make this deal and not getting squeezed.

                        My view on the Camarilla is they think the deals and restrictions they're making are enough to finish off the Sabbat or severely weaken it while also keeping the Lasombra at bay. I also think that they never intended to give the Lasombra a chance to gain power. However, right now the Camarilla is in a state of chaos and the Lasombra know that very Games of Throne-esque ideal.



                        4. I also have the suspicion that the Lasombra's defection is percipitated by the fact that there's a lot more Lasombra who feel like the Sabbat is a money and time sink. While this is just an observation, Sierra Burrace probably is more common than most Keepers admit. A highly ambitious, very driven Neonate or Ancilla who notes that her other vampires are uninterested in mortal power or wealth despite how much their Disciplines could make them. The Sabbat takes massive amounts of your time with rituals, hunting, and more that could be used to build a personal power base. Instead, pack politics means corraling your fellow Sabbat far more than is really useful if you'd like your own drug empire or hedge fund. Even Lasombra that are forbidden from being a Primogen or Prince for sixty years (which is a rule that I imagine most Lasombra scoff at, "You don't become Prince by obeying rules"), that's time plenty of them would like to focus on gaining mortal influence that they've been denied.

                        5. Even so, I agree with the 50-50 split as a general rule. Cardinals and Archbishops aren't leaving their positions unless they can get something similar. I fully see the Sabbat Lasombra also perhaps having UNDERestimated how many of their kind would defect. One of the jokes is that Talley may have killed or tried to kill Lucita to defect only to have plenty of Lasombra trying to kill HIM to defect. Probably them targeted by their fellows. Which is what you get when you're a clan of relentless assholes.
