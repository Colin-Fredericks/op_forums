    V20 Giovanni and unusual Necromancy Paths

    09-29-2023, 11:42 PM
    Greetings,

    I would like to know if the Giovanni (both as a group and individuals) have learned the others paths that are associated with bloodlines, I will ask my questions and suppositions:
    1. Ghiberti (Cenotaph path)
      These guys are part of the clan but the Cenotaph path is mentioned as something that Ghiberti created, would it be unusual in the clan as a whole? Certainly the Giovanni elders would have learned it as "the price of admission" from the Ghiberti, so Augustus certainly has this, but maybe not the other ancients.
    2. Cappadocian paths (Corpse in the Monster, the Grave’s Decay)
      I think this is relatively easy to learn, old Giovanni should have access to this from when they were Cappadocians, Augustus and some of the Anziani should know this, and some favoured childer at least.
    3. Lamia (Path of the Four Humors​)
      Who would have learnt this? Augustus? Some Anziani? Would they have shared it with someone? I think no one has it, or if anyone the Premascine, Augustus perhaps.
    4. Nagaraja (Vitreous Path)
      I think no Giovanni has learned this, right?
    5. Samedi (Thanatosis discipline)
      Ambrogino stats mention him having this at level 1, would others have this? I think it unlikely as the Rites of the Blood supplement mention that Giovanni have barely made progress copying the effects with rituals.

    Any opinion, answer or idea is helpful.

    Thanks,

              I don't believe we've ever gotten an official writeup of a Giovanni character written out as knowing the Path of Four Humors or the Vitreous Path. But I would generally say you're ideasare more or less the same as my own on the matter. It's plausible that you could find Giovanni practicing all of those Paths and possibly even other rarer ones besides (like we never got a write up of a Necromancy Path for the Setites, but it wouldn't be surprising if they had one, along with unique Necromantic rituals, and at the same time I would not be surprised if there were a handful of Giovanni who learned it).

              But of course some of these would be very rare and exclusive like you point out. Like the Lamia Path, there might only be 3 or 4 Giovanni who know it. And some like the Nagaraja Path, would have even fewer practitioners. Still, the Clan is big enough and has members all over the world that if you wanted to introduce a Giovanni into one of your games that knows one of those more rare Paths, then it's perfectly reasonable to do so.

                  On the matter of necromantic paths with their roots on the Cappadocians or Lamia i'm somewhat divided - on one side, anziani who were around before the destruction of Cappadocians and the general mayhem that followed should have a much easier time getting in touch with such practices than any kindred out of the clan.

                  On another, the Giovanni were embraced as a group pretty much for doing their own thing when it comes to necromantic arts and basically incentivized by Cappadocius to push further with their path of inovation, instead of delving on past works of other branches of their "family".

                  Plus, there's the matter of how ingrained or not was the Path of Four Humors to the Lamia's Lilith-worship, what could affect considerably how "jealous/possessive" of that knowledge might they be or not even in relation to other branches of the Cappadocian tree.


                  Honestly, half the time my attitude is "gank this" and ignoring most of the lore related to them, just for the sake of sparing myself headaches - or bamboozling a bit with player expectations, with reveals like "the Cappadocians are still very much around, but doubled down so hard into the fringe christianism that hunters bump into them more frequently than the other kindred, who confuse them for Angelis Ater or path of evil revelations covens half the time" or for extra bizarro-facts "the Giovanni have no relation to the Caps (closer to what we now know as Lasombra, really), nor did they make any actual sort of pogrom against them - their own deep connections and obsession with the Church and its orders was at the root of their doom. That said the Giovanni did exploit church connections to stealthily loot much of the necromantic resources captured by the Society of Leopold and other hunter organizations of a religious bent between late middle ages and renaissance".


                  What reminds me that i never really delved in to Lamia, how does the Lilith-worship maps into the greek mythology hints of the bloodline/cult name - was it always about Lilith, or did it become so due to cross-polination with Cappadocian obsessions over christian symbolism. And how being a mystery cult priestess embraced by impulse - and possibly against her will - evolves into a "bodyguard lineage" anyway? There's some ill-fitting bits that could be indeed quite entertaining to lampshade and explore in a chronicle.

                  (Maybe when the PCs bump into Serena with all their globe-trotting, i guess)


                  Anyway, sorry for the digression​ and rambling and back to the topic..... there are points in favor both of the Giovanni as a clan possessing a wealth of knowledge on paths rooted in Cappadocian lore through "inheritance" and their rarity on an individual basis for a number of factors, being more the thing one will learn through an anziani that was already around before Augustus "coup" or maybe some of its post-inquisition pupils or descendants, that could or not form a minor branch family of their own alongside Dunsirn, Milliners, Rosselini and others.

                              I'm a firm believer that certain Paths of Thaumaturgy and Necromancy should be exclusive knowledge to select groups or individuals. It shouldn't be assumed that any vampire can learn anything they want. A good ST makes decisions on the rarity of each of the Paths and who has access to them. Some Paths can be extremely well known. Some are known only among a Clan. Others are known only to certain factions within a Clan. Some might belong to specific individuals. So while I have no knowledge of canon in this instance, here is what I would do for my own chronicles.

                              Cenotaph Path. This is a secret of the Ghiberti. As a small family in the Giovanni clan, hoarding this knowledge would provide them an edge in Giovanni clan politics. But it is entirely possible the family provided knowledge of the Path to select Giovanni family vampires, either to pay off a patronage debt, cement personal alliances, or to gain favor with certain powerful individuals. In terms of the Hecata and the events of V5, it really depends whether the ST wants the Ghiberti to remain loyal to the Giovanni as a whole, or if they seek to widen their opportunities by integrating with the rest of the clan. But in either case, the Ghiberti have an incentive to keep knowledge of the Path their family secret. Why lose the only edge they have?

                              Corpse in the Monster is the standard Dark Ages power of Mortis. As such, it's likely well known among any survivors of Clan Cappadocian. Many premascine Giovanni would know it as well, and there's probably a significant number of younger Giovanni who might have learned it. As such, this is not a big secret, and is probably shared among the Hecata just like any other discipline, just perhaps more rare.

                              The Grave’s Decay. I am not familiar with this Path, but it appears to be the higher level powers of Mortis, much like how the higher level powers of the original Necromancy Discipline were split off as their own Paths. As such, I would regard this Path as more secretive among the Cappadocians than Corpse in the Monster. Fewer of the old Cappadocians and Giovanni know it, but it is not an exclusive secret of some group. This should be on of the rarer Paths, but eventually information about it will become promiscuous among the Hecata as more are taught the Path in exchange for favors. Knowing it is likely some distinction as being a favorite of some elder. But it is not impossible to learn.

                              Path of the Four Humors. I regard this as a secret of the Lamia bloodline. They would not share it. If you see the Lamia/Gorgons of the Hecata as more a faction than a bloodline (meaning you don't need to be a childe of a Lamia to be one, but is a society within the Hecata that you can join), then joining their society/faction gets you to learn the Path. Otherwise, it is not shared. Their identity now seems to be as a Bahari cult among the Hecata. So if you join the faith, you join the Gorgons and can get the path. If you are not Bahari, you are out of luck.

                              Vitreous Path. Known only among the Nagaraja. And quite frankly in my chronicles, I would never have the Nagaraja be part of this clan whether it is called Cappadocian, Giovanni, or Hecata. They are completely unrelated to this group. If you don't have the True Black Hand as a sect in your chronicle, just don't use them.

                              Thanatosis. This Path is a secret among the Samedi. Like other groups with access to a unique Path, teaching it to outsiders causes your small group to lose the one advantage you have over others. So don't teach it to outsiders. That is just dumb. But again like the Lamia/Gorgons, what does the ST consider this former bloodline to be? If in V5 they no longer have their old curse and aren't significantly different from other Hecata, so is this group just a lineage now? Or is it now some kind of faction or secret society within the clan? If just a lineage, eventually their distinction within the Hecata will disappear. It just becomes lore about some older generation vampires. The old Samedi will gradually disappear as a distinct entity and just become an integrated part of Clan Hecata. If so, knowledge of Thanatosis will eventually spread to others. On the other hand, if they are some kind of secret society within the clan that keeps its own traditions and identity from any outsiders, then it'll stay a faction secret they only teach to those Hecata who end up joining their secret society.

                              So these would be my decisions if I was running a chronicle. Yours may be different.
