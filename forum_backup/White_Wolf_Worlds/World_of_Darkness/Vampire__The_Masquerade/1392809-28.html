        I am saying your outrage is not justified because of what you are implying MyWifeIsScary's point was. His point was that Vicissitude's thunder is being stolen by Thin Blood Alchemy essentially doing what it does, but better, and with a far lesser barrier to entry. He was not saying that he has some sort of distaste, or that there is no room for, players who want to have gender-diverse or questioning PCs. You are painting him as intolerant by misconstruing him (at least, if I am reading your post right).

        Profane Hieros Gamos is under the spotlight here because it is specifically designed to deal with the concept of gender identity. The issue is that the amount of very specific and numerous things you'd need to change for an entirely perfect gender reassignment are very, very numerous, but a level 1 ritual trivialises it and in doing so, raises questions as to what Thin Blood Alchemy is capable of and why this powerful body transmogrification is suddenly so easily accessed. Becoming stronger is quite simple, atomically. You just have more protein in your muscles, thus more muscle fibres. It is a different ball game than gender reassignment.

        And I agree that the game should not recreate the medical complications of gender reassignment, but there was also a much better way of doing this than what the game presents. I personally have quite a distaste for this ritual. I don't find it empowers my identity, I find it erases it. I, and many of my trans acquaintances, find ourselves creating our outward identities in the face of our clashing birth genders. We look past our biology for ways to express our gender identities, and many of us eschew the idea of surgeries out of the idea that we don't need modifications to our outward genitalia to express, and feel, like who we are. Profane Hieros Gamos throws all of that under the bus in a level 1 thin-blood finger snap, and I find it more making light of my situation rather than empathising with it.

        I don't claim to speak for all trans people, however. I'm sure there are people who appreciate the inclusion.
