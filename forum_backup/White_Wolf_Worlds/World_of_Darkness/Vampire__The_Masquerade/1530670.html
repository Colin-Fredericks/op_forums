    What Beckett's Jyhad Diary plots were your favorite? Which would you followed up on?

    02-12-2024, 05:33 PM
    What the title says.

    Talk about your favorite Beckett's Jyhad Diary bits and what you would have liked to have seen more of in a hypothetical future edition.

    + House Carna got some follow-up in V5 but I think that it deserved to be a lot more of a central figure than they got with the destruction of the Seven. Imagine if they were the Anarch alternative to the Camarilla Tremere.

    * I actually would have really enjoyed seeing how Chicago functioned under an oligarchy with no Prince. I also wanted to know if BJD was an in-universe document then did that mean Portia's masquerade was blown.

    + I really wanted to see who would be the new Regent of the Sabbat: Lucita, Polonia, Jalan, or perhaps some other dark horse figure like Joseph Panders.

    + The fact the Tal'Mah'Ra went from being a complete joke to something taking over Northern Africa and becoming a major sect in its own right.

    + Ur-Shulgi isn't a Beckett's Jyhad Diary plot but they seemed to be more interested in detailing that the Web of Knives wasn't the whole of the clan than the Revised version.

    + Mary the Black, Michael, and their search for the new member of the Istanbul Trinity.

    + Vykos regaining their free will after millennia.

    Author of Cthulhu Armageddon, I was a Teenage Weredeer, Straight Outta Fangton, Lucifer's Star, and the Supervillainy Saga.

              Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

              What sort of plots would you have wanted instead if you were willing to spend money to purchase a book of plot points?
              Okay, here we go...

              First off, we start off with the setting assumptions of the Revised corebook. Not Revised as a whole, just the corebook.

              So the Gangrel are still in the Camarilla, there was no Week of Nightmares, Assamites are still Independent, it's still Clan Giovanni and not Hecata

              The Tremere Antitribbu are still extinct for some damn reason and the Sabbat still lost control of New York though. Thin-bloods aren't as prominent as they are in the canonical V5 though.

              Here's how I'd go about it...

              1. Capone became Prince of Chicago with the explicit backing of Annabelle Triabell, Nicolai, and Tyler. Meanwhile, Kevin Jackson, Son, Ballard, Balthazar, Gordon Keaton, and Edward Neally all met Final Death throughout the remainder of the 90's and into the early 2000's. Meanwhile, the Anarchs of Chicago were able to fully expel Juggler from their ranks and a Triumvirate of Maldavis, Damien, and Gengis rule the Anarchs. Meanwhile, O'Leary, Tazmosius, Houdini, and a few others are currently MIA and Bret Stryker is now the Sheriff of Chicago at Annabelle's request.

              2. The Sabbat crusade to take all of the East Coast largely ended in failure (only Atlanta was successfully conquered and even that was a Pyrrhic Victory) but the Camarilla were able to take all of New York and the Sabbat lost most of the Northeast in the ensuing counter-attacks. This has left the Sword of Caine in a weakened state, though they still remain a menace. As a result, the Sabbat began focusing more of their efforts outside of North America and bolstering the smaller domains they already have.

              The city of Charleston, West Virginia (and by extension all of the State of West Virginia) is the second-largest Sabbat domain that's east of the Mississippi, north of the Rio Grande, and south of the Saint Lawrence. In fact, Charleston has become increasingly prominent and prestigious among the Sabbat due to its potential as a staging point for a future crusade to retake the East Coast.

              3. Victoria Ash continued to be a pop star but her career began to flag around 2002 or so. Now she's more of a "Hey, remember me?" punchline and is spoken of in the same breath as Mandy Moore, 98 Degrees, Vitamin C, B*Witched, and all the members of NSYNC not named Justin Timberlake.

              4. Mictlantechtli was diablerized and met Final Death at the hands of some neonates from Chicago around 1992. Said neonates were instrumental in the War of Chicago and helping Capone stabilize his power base and securing alliances with Annabelle and Nicolai.

              5. Juggler and Modius met Final Death in 2011. Alexander Danov left Gary shortly after the War of Chicago in 1993 and eventually made his way to Hunendora Castle. Michael is MIA, while Lucian, Alicia, and Evelyn Stephens all left Gary around 2000 or so. Lucian was recalled to New York, Alicia and Evelyn retreated further into Chicago and it's suspected that Evelyn suggested the idea of an Anarch Triumvirate

              6. The events of Bloodlines happened with a female Tremere fledgling helping Maximilian Strauss take over the city of Los Angeles and the Ankaran Sarcophagus now in a warehouse somewhere.

              7. Vykos met Final Death in 2004 at the hands of Caiaphas Smith. Jan Piertzoon also meets a similarly grisly end to an unknown assailant

              8. The Final Death of Vykos (at the hands of a mortal hunter no less!), Giangaleazzo defecting to the Camarilla, the loss of the Tremere Antitribbu, and the failure of the East Coast Crusade have all forced the Sabbat into more defensive positions though rumors persist of a potential second crusade to take more territory in the Americas.

              9. On December 31, 2009, Melinda Gailbraith mysteriously disappears. Nobody knows if she's met Final Death, is in Torpor, or just walked out. The very next night, Joseph Pander is called to Mexico City and is proclaimed the acting Regent, with the understanding that his position is supposed to be a temporary one.

              10. In 2010, the Sabbat hold a grand convention in West Virginia with the provisional Regent, the Prisci, the Cardinals, and various elder Archbishops of note all meeting at the mansion of Sergei Von Bratovitch, the Tzimisce Archbishop of Charleston. There, they discuss the future of the Sect and make several sweeping changes. Lucita de Aragon arrives as a guest of honor as well, having rejoined the Sabbat in full.

              First off, they are shifting their blood war against the Camarilla into more of a cold war. Though they still will send threats to the Camarilla and Anarchs about a future crusade, it's all just a diversion while they work on a much grander plan, one jokingly nicknamed "Operation Country Roads" at the conference, but said jocular moniker ends up sticking anyway.

              A vote is held to determine the new permanent Regent and much to everyone's surprise, Joseph Pander is narrowly elected to be the new Regent, with Lucita being the deciding vote. His provisional figurehead title is now one of real substance. And from there, the new plan is to lead a much bigger and more meticulously planned crusade, one that is focused entirely on the Middle East, with their main focus being on taking out the Antediluvians. It will not be a mass exodus but instead an extended multi-step grand strategy and the first packs are deployed to the Levant on December 21st, 2012.

              11. The first act of Operation Country Roads is a surprising success, with the Ashirra losing control of the cities of Mosul, Bethlehem, Aleppo, Tripoli, Beirut, and Amman while the Sabbat sustains minimal casualties (mostly shovelheads). Damascus falls exactly one year later with Carolina Velez, the disgraced former Archbishop of Montreal is now the Archbishop of Damascus.

              12. Now we go into the Second Inqusition, AKA the one idea V5 had that had some genuine potential (even if it was botched badly in practice)

              The sheer bloodshed in the Middle East following the events of Operation Country Roads and the Sabbat's mounting successes in the Levant along with their continued depravity in the Americas ends up creating a lot of buzz online, with many videos of blatant Sabbat atrocities ending up on shock sites like LiveLeak.

              In 2014, Bob Schnoblin, using his CIA connections, is able to secure the formation of a federally commissioned joint task force comprised of civilian, military, and religious authorities to probe further into the chaos consuming the Middle East. He dies shortly after the commission is approved but he hand picks William Shephard of the FBI to be the new leader of the task force.

              This new task force comprised of the NSA, the FBI Special Affairs Department, the Department of Homeland Security, the US Army, and "spiritual consultants" (i.e. The Society of Leopold, and to a lesser extent, the Order of Saint George) and with their combined effort, they blow the Masquerade wide open. This doesn't get leaked to the general public, with operations against the Kindred being written off as "counter-terrorist operations".

              Mithras is killed and London falls into chaos, but the attempts by the Second Inquisition to drone-strike the main Tremere chantry in Vienna fail due to the interference of Thaumaturgy. Marcus Vitel, Prince of DC, who narrowly resisted the Sabbat crusade and did all he could to prevent a Second Inquisiton, is killed at the hands of an elderly Sullivan Dane in 2017, but not before taking Dane down with him.

              The SI successfully eliminates all the Kindred in the wider Washington DC metropolitan area, causing the Camarilla leadership to have a collective "OH SHIT!" moment

              The SI focuses its efforts primarily on Camarilla and Anarch forces at home in the West, ironic given that the Sabbat were the Sect whose actions were the most directly responsible for its creation. However, the Sabbat do lose Atlanta due to SI efforts, allowing the Anarchs to take over once the dust settled.

              13. Rumors of a Sixth Great Maelstrom and the demise of the Tal'mahe'Ra were greatly exaggerated. As the Second Inquisition tore through the West and Operation Country Roads continued unabated in the Middle East, the True Black Hand became more prevalent and active.

              14. Sutekh is dead. Killed while he was still asleep in torpor. Set met Final Death in a mysterious and great explosion in Egypt. Nobody knows if it was the Sabbat, the Second Inquisition, the Tal'Mahe'Ra or something else entirely. The Followers of Set have become severely fractured as a clan. A splinter group calling themselves the Ministry of Set emerged in 2018, but was swiftly eradicated by the Tal'Mahe'Ra.

              15. The Harbingers of Skulls leave the Sabbat and the Cappadocian Drenis leaves Transylvania. Drenis begins embracing many people and recruiting Thin-Bloods as an attempt to revive Clan Cappadocian. Sporting a goth style with Wiccan motifs and calling themselves the Hecata and join forces with the Harbingers and the Samedi, they try to directly attack the Giovanni estates in Venice but fail miserably, being wiped out to the very last one. Many Samedi refused the call to join up with the short-lived Hecata movement and survived.

              16. Beckett is invited to Chicago by Rebekah the Monitor and is inducted into the Inconnu in 2021 and is told that something truly major, bigger than anything else in the history of the Kindred is about to happen very soon and he will play a major role in it

              17. Menele awakened shortly after the death of Lodin and was convinced by Rebekah to forsake his war with Helena and instead join the Inconnu. Around the same time, the Children of Osiris and the seven surviving members of Clan Salubri joined the sect, becoming de facto sub-factions of them.

              These are just my personal ideas and I know you probably won't like them and neither will most V5/BJD fans but eh, I might as well share them anyway.

              I'm just a trans guy and gaming fan, unabashed hedonist and unusually upbeat nihilist, fairly laid back. He/Him.

              If everything is political, then nothing is!

                                                  Originally posted by Matt Corleone View Post
                                                  Can I just say I hated all of them and would rather provide my own plot points instead?
                                                  It wasn't all bad in BJD proper, the writers did make a nice work at unearthing and updating many of the past citybooks plotlines plus delving into many of Revised era events, but in a more open-ended "all this stuff is optional, use as is, ignore or rework as you prefer" perspective, plus some bits from the novel and the two games.

                                                  The fact some people in higher places would then jump from all the positive feedback into "a bigger, more bold leap" - and break their legs in the fall, badly, by not paying attention to how one lands, well, that's life. And Revised-era vitriol all over again, oh yeah, i know, but let me not digress too much. BJD on itself is far from bad, I enjoyed it as both fluff piece, compilation of the crazy from past editions, with some aim toward pointing optional alternate directions.


                                                  - Carna's Rebellion is a funny case - 1 or 2 years back i got into BJD pretty much because of her little stunt in Milwaukee starting the whole trainwreck that is Beckett's misadventure through the whole book. The Book of Grave-War had already figured in some of my own games long before it and having her (instead of its better known translator, Mortius) breaking the bond and then going "i wanna do Anarch Revolt 2.0, Tremere-only edition" because of her many resentments & emotional issues trumped over her sense i very much enjoy.

                                                  Last year my idea of a chronicle was pretty much making her mess with Beckett and Vykos into the starting point as the PCs dealt with the sheer chaos of the Sabbat siege of Milwaukee, the maze of nonsensical intrigues of its kindred, the Lupines and a number of other subplots & complications (Merick still is prince, though he has considerably cut on appearances since the 90's) while deciding if they stay to carve a niche, go into a Indiana Jones/Tomb Raider romp of their own alongside or against Beckett and who knows what else, among other things.


                                                  But then i got in a Discord group, lots of random talk of fluff, editions, crazy ideas past & present happened - and i ended up with a whole different chronicle concept & group in my hands instead. Story is kind of centered on the "False Caine" plotline - or at least my own version of it, where no one found a mustache-twirling (female) setite behind the curtain and Karl Schreckt didn't get to defeat a 5th meth that, even before power boost, was three times his age already just because status quo is god. The Justicar called "Caine" an impostor and a threat to order and Traditions to be contained & punished - and got completely trashed into torpor in front of dozens of kindred already quite awed with "Caine" for his troubles.

                                                  Things only got weirder from there, as the princes either disappeared (or got disappeared with) and the hundreds of kindred from all over the world that moved into Berlin turned into the largest undead hippie/new age commune ever. And the headache would only spread as groups of kindred, some late responders to "Caine's Beckoning", a lot of Malkavians (that were not called) gathering in neighboring cities to witness from a distance (that in particular had already been happening for weeks before Caine's arrival in Berlin) and groups of anarchs, noddists and occultists in general looking for some sort of opportunity or to prove someone wrong. The situation was hectic, the Camarilla attempted to retake the city - and completely failed in doing so. That one of the leaders of the personality cult was another Justicar, gone rogue and turned into one of the "mad ancient" confidantes, only made everything into more an epic embarassment for the Ivory Tower.


                                                  And then a whole year later, it was all over. Just as suddenly as the spectacle started, it ended - some of the more diplomatically and philosophically adroit cultists (that included the aforementioned Justicar) had led negotiations between both sides, resulting in an armistice of sorts, "Caine" giving a few last words of counsel and support to his true witnesses and then leaving on its own for a personal pilgrimage and the whole his flock disbanding to all directions, individually or in groups, to follow personal visions of their own, exchange ideas and info.

                                                  And sometimes start spiritual journeys/pilgrimages of their own, not infrequently tracking down all sorts of rumours of new Caine appearances, to follow in his steps, learn from them - and perhaps even exchange new revelations or ideas with the First of All when they do succeed in finding him.

                                                  So ended the "Third City Crisis" and started the "Witnesses of Caine" proper as a somewhat unusual and iconoclastic noddist society back in the '90s. Los Angeles has been a particularly popular visiting spot since a series of incidents that would lead into the destruction of Prince Lacroix and a number of Sabbat, infernalists & foreign undead causing trouble.


                                                  The PCs' coterie is strongly connected to the Witnesses, though technically only one of them is an actual full-fledged member, who was literally tracking down Caine/Kapaneus/Ankla Hotep/etc under the cover of his tour as a DJ (having sleeper buses and a roadie crew is very convenient to move the occasional kindred around too) when he would bump into not one but two kindred who actually met Caine in LA - one of them being a Gangrel reversed from centuries as a Gargoyle by the First, to boot - sort of recruited them, while also bumping into other peculiar kindred that sort of tagged along for all kinds of reasons.

                                                  They also happen to presently be hunted by two different groups of Gargoyles with opposing religious manias (Heaven and Devil) and Dylan Bruce, who's after "Dracula's Sword" (actually the Sword of Nul, but try to convince the self-proclaimed "childe of Dracula" otherwise....). Plus the Vukodlak - though technically Erik (the ex-Gargoyle) and sister Agnes (the Salubri warrior-nun) are really after freshly-awakened Christoph's steps, actually....
