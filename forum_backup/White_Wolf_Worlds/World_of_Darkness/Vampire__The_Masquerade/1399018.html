                          This dips a bit into Requiem discussion a bit I think, but they have a term for this in 2nd Ed: "the Perversion." (https://whitewolf.fandom.com/wiki/Perversion)

                          As noted above, such a situation can work pretty well for the Kindred involved in the relationship, as long as their artificially-enforced relationship doesn't turn sour. But the reason it gets such a bad rap in VtR has to do with the effect it has on the couple's relationship with the rest of the world.

                          Remember, a blood-bond is not supposed to be depicted as a 'healthy' love or attachment. It's more akin to the sort of brainwashing we associate with "love potions," and often tend towards obsessive. Now, take two vampires, with a hunger for blood they constantly need to satisfy, and a Beast that can go off the handle over all sorts of things. Then make those two vampires OBSESSED with each other. Taking care of each other is now the most important thing in both of their unlives. And they are now immune to blood-bonds to other Kindred.

                          It's a bit of a ticking time-bomb for a "Bonnie and Clyde" situation, or a psychotic rampage of revenge if one of the lovers gets killed off in the inevitable conflicts of vampire existence. Along with the risk of them developing an addiction to vitae or diablerie and then protecting one another as they go off the edge.

                                      True Love is so amazing that it can allow a farm boy to beat a fencing wizard in a duel, out-wrestle a giant, and defeat a man who makes Plato, Aristotle, and Socrates seem like morons in a battle of wits.

                                      But what about a mutual blood bond? The problem with the blood bond is that it is a magically imposed obsessive love, not actual love. Real love between people is based on qualities of those people, how they interact with each other, in shared values and experiences, on elevating each other. The blood bond doesn’t need those things, so the obsessive feelings of love could continue even if the person who shares your mutual obsession makes you miserable, even if you grow apart and are destructive towards each other - the unnatural love persists. That sounds very dangerous to me.
