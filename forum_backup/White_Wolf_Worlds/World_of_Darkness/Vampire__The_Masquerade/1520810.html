      I think the question is fairly meaningless. The man is a stand-in for Satan on earth. Even though he is given stats, as one of the Dark Lords he is clearly in ST fiat territory as a storytelling device. His counterparts among the Dark Lords are either not given stats, or given stats merely for one of their "major manifestations." We should proceed cautiously.

      Any combat scenario would degenerate into STs making very difficult decisions on interpreting game mechanics that are meant to be nebulous and open to ST interpretation as a storytelling device. How do you interpret stats like Arcane 7 or Destiny 8? What kind of astonishing insights against your opponent can you get with Dream 5? What kind of things can you do with Entropy 6? What stats are you going to assign the antedeluvians or Caine since those are not detailed? An ST could argue Al-Aswad is simply incapable of being killed by any vampire, and it would be hard to argue. Or another ST who assigns all sorts of incomparable powers to Caine would argue of course he could destroy Al-Aswad. It just comes down to personal preference and how an ST wants their chronicle setting to be like.

      I would say he is meant to be more powerful than any legitimate character who has "real" stats. And that he is at the same level of general power as other characters who are only really meant as storytelling tools.

      For me personally and how I view the setting, Al-Aswad is the kind of character who shows up in a later stage of the Apocalypse when Gehenna is mostly resolved and the surviving antedeluvians are facing off against the other great terrors of the world on a mostly ravaged Earth where the living envy the dead. RPG mechanics no longer apply.
