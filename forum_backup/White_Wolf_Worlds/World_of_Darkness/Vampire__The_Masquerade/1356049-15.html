    Originally posted by Kammerer View Post

    That's because there is nothing like that in the loresheets. Before the write-up of Oblivion, there is a sidebar that presents as an optional rule an option to swap one of the disciplines for a 'bloodline' discipline. For example, the Giovanni swapping Auspex for Dominate and Nagaraja swapping Fortitude for Dominate. Note - this is an optional rule and using it will break some of the 'singature' disciplines that got shoved into Amalgams. For instance - the Giovanni without Auspex will suck at doing ghost necromancy and Nagaraja without Fortitude can't get the ghost-eating power.
    Thanks for the explanation. I'd gotten the impression that you would have to buy levels of the bloodline loresheets to have the Discipline spread alteration, but I'm actually glad its a simple case of "here's the option to switch out Disciplines."

    Which, frankly, if I'm going to play a Nagaraja I'll just get the dot of the Low Clan loresheet that gives a fourth Discipline you can treat as an in-Clan, and assign it to Dominate so I don't have to suck at one of the things the Nagaraja were always meant to be good at.
