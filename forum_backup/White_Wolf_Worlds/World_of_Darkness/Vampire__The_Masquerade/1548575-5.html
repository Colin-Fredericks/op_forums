            No One of Consequence the original Hunters Hunted had Dennis Quincy, "The Uncanny Danny" who I'm pretty sure was intended as an homage to James "the Amazing" Randi. He'd be perfect as a leader of illusionist/mentalist hunters.

            Atticus Cleanclaw It's a bit unfortunate for a LA chronicle that Houdini's wife died (1943) prior to the revolt against Don Sebastian (1944). If Ehrich ever visited her in secret, it would have been done when Sebastian still ruled, and thus has a high chance of any LA Tremere observing her in case Ehrich ever showed up. So he doesn't have much of an emotional stake in LA itself. I see him mostly as using LA as a convenient spot because it lacks any official chantry. Ehrich may have some anarch sympathies, but he doesn't seem to be an enemy of the Camarilla in general.

            In terms of the Giovanni and the Necromany discipline, I will note that Ehrich's (sole?) appearance in 1991's Succubus Club predates even the 1e Players Guide (where the Giovanni and Necromancy made their first appearance). A future "Ghost" game line was already planned, but there was literally nothing that character or story could refer to in terms of actual vampire powers. His Disciplines are rather low key - only Auspex 2, Dominate 2, Movement of Mind 3, and Lure of Flames 1. A more recent rendition of the character would probably expand on his powers.

            Character knowledge of both Thaumaturgy and Necromancy have expanded greatly from the early game. Given Ehrich's interest, it would make sense a world travelling rogue Tremere seeking occult knowledge would have picked up some actual wraith lore and necromancy in terms of how the game portrays the setting today. Since LA lacks any real representation of either Tremere or Giovanni, I imagine it must have other "occult refugees" looking for a place without either clan. They could be part of some occult coterie (whose members might belong to other coteries as well) with whom Ehrich stays in contact.

                    At some point in the Crossovers thread, I intend to get to Hunters. I'll probably address both of those characters. James Randi was a frequent guest on Johnny Carson and other TV shows debunking the paranormal so the Uncanny Danny could take his place. Might be a good use of the Magic Castle to have it be home to a secret group of atheist, scientifically inclined hunters trying to figure out a materialist explanation for vampires. Deborah Rose Keller explicitly lives in California. She seems very Orange County to me. As a public advocate investigating occult related crimes and highly skilled in investigation, she'd be another interesting antagonist. As you said, it's natural both would have some kind of organization (especially in the case of Keller).

                    Showing a large variety of local hunters, something that is a result of not having an obvious Prince-like figure doing the hard work of maintaining the Masquerade, would be another "feature" of the Free States.

                    What will be more of a challenge for me is running hunter plots that do not rely on a direct confrontation with the PCs, but working around them. I find hunter plots to be the least fun and successful plots in Vampire because ultimately if you run the hunters smart, the PCs will get destroyed during the daytime, and if you run them dumb so the PCs live, I find it a waste and the players don't have fun in either scenario.

                            I've decided to add another canon character. I already had designated a character as "pornographer", but that was merely intended as filler. A placeholder for an unimportant character I might bring in whenever I needed someone in the scene because the PCs did something unexpected. I am slotting Derrick Zeel from the 1e Clanbook Toreador into that role.

                            However, I will be ignoring the idea that he's actually the fourth generation methusaleh Iontius. While Iontius is mentioned as one possibility for Zeel's real identity, I always took that as one possible option among the many rumors told about him. It was all up to the ST. I know that officially became canon when the authors decided the illustrator of the Encyclopedia Vampirica had to be Iontius and confirmed Zeel was him, but I don't like that for various reasons. I also know they made Iontius be from San Francisco, and I'll be ignoring that too.

                            The original write up says "gangs of powerful anarchs surround him and keep other Kindred from bothing him". To me, that pretty much cements that he should be from the Free States. Where else are there going to be gangs of powerful anarchs? Furthermore, if "few have actually seen him in person", why would he have multiple gangs of anarchs explicitly protecting him? Wouldn't they have met him? So my interpretation is that he's just a reclusive Toreador, and the "gangs" protecting him are just a reference to the Los Angeles anarchs in general.

                            Now Los Angeles is not the only place a pornographer could be. The industry has been split up into several locations, but I think Los Angeles (specifically the San Fernando Valley) has been dominant since the seventies, and is about to experience a boom in the nineties (my chronicle will begin in 1992). So placing Zeel in LA is a no brainer for me.

                            "Derrick Zeel" is simply a stage name, like Dirk Diggler. In the rare times he actually sees another Kindred, he'll be called that too, but no one believes it's a real name. While Clanbook Toreador says Zeel "owns" the video production company that makes his films, I'll be taking that to mean he controls the company. Actual ownership would likely entail a situation where any enterprising investigator could determine Zeel's real name. Although that could lead to a scenario where the "real person" Zeel is impersonating is a deceased child whose SSN he could use, I think the danger of that being exposed would just create too many liabilities.

                            There's a few things that make the reclusive porn star someone whom PCs might encounter. The first is that he's "a major figure in the slave trade", which I assume is similar to the "auction" done by the ghoul Williams in the Forged in Steel setting of Gary, IN contained in the 1e and 2e corebooks. Except that it is much, much larger in scale. The porn industry (and LA in general) just has access to a lot of "disposable" people that won't be missed. Zeel's "slave trade" offers a lot of mentally/emotionally/physically broken mortals as slaves to be used by Kindred willing to pay Zeel. I imagine there are various anarchs who have Herds courtesy of Zeel's slave trade, and this may be the reason why "gangs of powerful anarchs" would protect him.

                            While mostly based in LA itself, it can accommodate anyone in the entirety of the Free States. And he also controls some additional outlets in Camarilla domains like San Francisco, and indirectly traffics people to other domains. I imagine William's Auction in Gary is in contact with it, even if it is mostly local dregs Williams sells. In order to feed this machine, Zeel is likely already involved in the mortal sex trafficking. While this includes a pipeline from Latin America through Mexico (and I may utilize the crime cartel controlled by the Zantosa Revenants that I stated in the Sabbat thread), it also includes ocean traffic through the ports which requires an agreement with David Geduld.

                            The second is the idea that Zeel has "cults dedicated to him in every corner of the world." Does this mean it's an extremely large and expansive blood cult? Is he some kind of erotic Adonis or Tammuz like figure who's revived a neopagan cult dedicated to him? (Note that if we assume Arikel would be identified with Venus/Aphrodite/Ishtar/Inanna, then Zeel's identification with Adonis might make some believe he must secretly be a childe of the Toreador antediluvian.) If this Adonis-like cult is worldwide, it is highly likely the vampire established it BEFORE he "became" Derrick Zeel, and his worldwide cult simply recognized him in his porn films (which again, may lead to rumors that he is a far older vampire). Or something else? Does this worldwide cult somehow feed into his extensive slave trade? Does Zeel travel a lot to maintain the cult (or perhaps just sends out ghouls to do so)?

                            I will make the "one Thaumaturgist" who claims Zeel is Iontius, an anarch Thaumaturgist. He's onto Zeels' real identity, much like Charlie is onto Pepe Silvia. (I may have to name that anarch thaumaturgist Barney.)


                            This gives me a lot to work with, and multiple ways I could introduce new NPCs and plot hooks to the PCs depending on what they intend to do.

                            Concentrating on these plot hooks do a lot more for me as an ST running a Free State chronicle than making him Iontius. I really don't need another powerful Toreador methusaleh in LA. So whatever others may do, this is what I am going with.

                                The "X is actually an ancient Methuselah" feels like a well early Vampire kept going back to more often than necessary. 🤔

                                Regarding Zeel's widespread blood cult and not having it be so obviously traced back to him and his porn company, perhaps it operates as the secret inner circle of a hedonistic underground sex cult. Something that combines stuff from certain episodes of Law & Order: SVU and Eyes Wide Shut. Under such a sex cult, Zeel may be known by a totally different identity. And just personally, it would amuse me if said identity was "The King of Skin" or something equally over-the-top ridiculous.


                                And if you wanted to have something really weird (and very 90s) to lead back to Zeel, there is always the world of local "indy fed" professional wrestling. Between about 1995 and 2000, there was a San Francisco based promotion called Incredibly Strange Wrestling that operated out of a single venue and did shows that were a mix of luche libre style wrestling matches and performances by local punk, psychobilly, and thrash metal bands. The from 1999 to 2002, there was Xtreme Pro Wrestling in Los Angeles. Clearly trying to do what ECW had done in Philadelphia, it focused on hardcore/deathmatch style wrestling. What makes XPW quasi-memorable is that it was founded and run by a pr0n actor/producer, with both him and various talent appearing regularly in the promotion.
                                I think it would be easy to combine the two ideas into a single promotion; one started by Zeel as perhaps a way to entertain younger LA area Anarchs and perhaps endear himself to them. The early 90s (especially 1995) was essentially the nadir of American pro wrestling between its two major boom periods, but it did still have its local die hards, especially in parts of southern California, and 96 and 97 would see the start of its resurgence. Something that starts around 94 or 95 as this totally underground thing of local music acts interspersed with exhibitions of Mexican luche libre and Japanese deathmatch wrestling, eventually growing into the WoD's version of ECW, full of ultraviolence and sleezy sex appeal, thanks in no small part to Zeel's ability to get them PPV access.

                                At any rate, such a thing would be a good excuse to have an appearance by El Diablo Verde. And even the sort of thing the Fake Rapper archetype from the original Brujah clanbook might find himself involved in.

                                    Originally posted by No One of Consequence View Post
                                    Regarding Zeel's widespread blood cult and not having it be so obviously traced back to him and his porn company, perhaps it operates as the secret inner circle of a hedonistic underground sex cult. Something that combines stuff from certain episodes of Law & Order: SVU and Eyes Wide Shut. Under such a sex cult, Zeel may be known by a totally different identity. And just personally, it would amuse me if said identity was "The King of Skin" or something equally over-the-top ridiculous
                                    I think it should be under a different name too which is why I wondered if it should be established before the identity of "Derrick Zeel".

                                    I think it's a fairly simple thing if the cult is local and based around Zeel. That is probably just a typical blood cult. However, a worldwide cult is something else entirely. If he is in LA (or whatever location the ST wants him in), then what benefit can he get from cult members in Sydney, Australia; Hamburg, Germany; Buenos Aires, Argentina; and so on? It certainly isn't blood.

                                    Now if he was Iontius (which I am not using in my chronicles), and Iontius can "feed" somehow on sexual energy instead of blood, maybe that somehow can get transmitted long distance? However, I really don't like that as a concept, even for a fourth generation methusaleh vampire. So I won't be using it for Zeel who I imagine may still be of some potent blood and perhaps at least an ancillae, but still nowhere near in power for an ancient methusaleh.

                                    The worldwide cult has to be part of some long term plan even if it is just something mundane like making sure he has available herds should he relocate somewhere. However, that still requires some amount of management and effort, and that's a lot of resources being used now for something that does not provide immediate benefits. Does he visit them through astral projection via Auspex? Does he have an elder power of Presence that can be effective over long distance (even if limited in other scopes)? Just send ghouls out every now and again?

                                    Is the cult somehow magical in nature? By that I mean its practitioners may learn some form of sorcery (at least the most talented members)? If so, that may explain why one "Thaumaturgist" believes Zeel is Iontius. Maybe he was involved at some point with Zeel's cult, either to learn their version of mortal magic, or trying to help them learn a sorcery version of his paths, and then witnessed something he thought was evidence of Zeel's age and power? Could it be infernalist in nature?

                                    Is the cult male centered (like his porno audience would likely be) or female centered (like a cult of Adonis/Tammuz would likely be) or appeals to both sexes equally?

                                    Regardless, what is the cult doing that benefits him? There has to be a plan, and there has to be a goal. It doesn't even need to be his - in typical VtM fashion, perhaps he is just a pawn of another vampire (Iontius slumbering in torpor somewhere?).

                                    I am leaning to a cult based on Adonis/Tammuz simply because of its natural ties to an Aphrodite/Inanna figure whom would naturally be Arikel. Maybe the cult is truly directed towards her, but an Adonis-like figure is a useful intermediary. That might make it some kind of well hidden Gehenna cult, but specifically on behalf of one antediluvian, not to them in general. Of course, that would be something that could only be revealed to someone deep into the cult. There'd be several layers to pierce before anyone from the oustide would get proof of that. On the surface it just appears to be various sex cults connected to a local LA blood cult with some occult mumbo jumbo.

                                    It can be nebulous enough so that NPCs and players may never know and can pick one option of a confusing mess of what it could be. As long as the ST knows what its true purpose is, she'll know how to portray it and run it.
