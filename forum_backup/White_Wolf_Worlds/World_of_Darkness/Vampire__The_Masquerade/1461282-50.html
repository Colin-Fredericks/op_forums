                The way the Scourge got fixed by the Prince in my campaign was they made it an office under the Sheriff and essentially made it akin to an on-staff bounty hunter. The scourge is only sent after specific individuals after the Sheriff and/or Prince has determined they were a problem. Similarly, kill orders were for specific individuals for specific offenses and not a blanket policy.

                They were still a mad dog, but the fact they appeared to be on a chain quelled a lot of the discontent amid the court. The Scourge was also required to be on hand for all presentations and spend their non-hunting time in court so as to be familiar with those recognized by the Prince.

                The Prince still tended to go harder on thinbloods though as he had a blanket ban on siring for those of the 13th Generation* in his city (he tried to control all embraces, but this was a “the answer for you will always be ‘No’ so don’t annoy me by asking” policy) and gave out of town thinbloods who presented themselves 24 hours to leave his city; so by definition any thinblood in his city was either an unsanctioned embrace, had failed to present themselves or ignored the Prince’s order to vacate; but only the last one would be a kill-on-sight order for the Scourge (a complete unknown would be dragged in to determine their sire or why they’d failed to present themselves before execution… all of these offenses being actual violations of the Traditions rather than just because they were thinbloods).

                Basically; Sheriff = investigative branch, Scourge = enforcement branch. It still made the Scourge an asshole no one liked, but it prevented a lot of blowback onto the Prince.

                * side-bar: the Prince also put a population limit on creating ghouls just so his subjects would have one more thing they needed to curry his favor for.
