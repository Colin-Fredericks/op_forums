                                                            Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                                                            That didn't mean you weren't a monster, though.
                                                            Or, to look at it another way, just because you're a monster doesn't mean you can't do good.

                                                            Yes, yes, vampires are monstrous.

                                                            All things that live (or "live" in their case) want to live and avoid suffering. The price for this is the death and suffering of others. Creation can not be initiated or sustained without destruction. You cannot exist without the sacrifices of others. Your house is built of dead trees and minerals torn from the Earth (destroying habitat in its wake), warmed by the death of primordial beasts, when you got that house many others did not, it's paid for with a job that you got and so others didn't, you may feed on meat gained from the death of animals and even if not the death of animals was required at some point to grow the plant matter you eat. Animals, plants and human labor are exploited for you to live your life. The food chain demands death or the world collapses. Atoms strip each other of electrons. The universe cannibalises itself. Even if other life forms don't matter to you (which is how we got into the current climate mess) your existence will still lead to some degree of deprivation and suffering for other humans, somewhere. You were forged from history, which is a fucking hellhole.

                                                            To exist is a monstrous act that brings suffering, harm and death to others. Nature is monstrous. We don't even need vampires, the stuff that actually exists is a fucking horror-show. But what are you supposed to do? Your own suffering and death doesn't actually change anything, in the end. You can decide "that's just how it is" and not care, you can develop standards by which you decide which lives are less important (deserve to suffer more than) others, you can look for ways to minimize the damage you do while you seek a better way forward.

                                                            And this feeds into ethics, philosophy, religion and the cycles of conflict and misery that come from it. People have long looked at the world and each other, and themselves and asked "why are we so fucked up?" "what can we do to stop this?" "is there a way?" "is there even any point?"
                                                            And so comes dharma, original sin and redemption, the illusions of desire and escape of enlightenment, the laws of God by which one should live to live a life that benefits you and the world, fascism, communism, Social Darwinism, so on so forth.

                                                            Vampires all have ideas about how to cope with what they are, why they are, what to do about it, on levels both individual and as collectives, and conflict arises from it.

                                                            To me that's the core of vampires. Exploring monstrosity through exaggeration. Our own monstrosity, the monstrosity built into a fundamentally awful (yet beautiful) universe, and how we cope and what it makes us into. A one note "Vampire BAD WRONG EVUUUL," cheapens the whole experience. The idea that vampires should kill themselves drives me up the wall, and frankly feels like... victim blaming, for lack of a better word. You got murdered? Defiled forever, abomination, kys. Lovely. "Even if you do good things, they don't matter because you're a monster" means there's no reason to give a damn at all. A vampire is still a person, a vampire still experiences suffering. If you don't value a person's right to exist and avoid suffering, you shouldn't be surprised that they don't give a damn about you. Goes in both ways, for kindred and mortals.

                                                            Good and evil are ultimately what you do. A vampire is a monster, yeah. They're not good, their motivations don't even need to be good, but they can do good. The Toreador who has a flourishing domain that is perfectly clear of litter and is a sanctuary for nature in the urban jungle because they find it aesthetically pleasing, has done good. Vampires who push for eco policies, and against wars, or just to make life better to live because they fucking live here (and will be here to deal with the fallout for centuries longer then us) are doing good. The people who live in the Koldun's domain are grateful not to live with the threat of extortion rackets, or dying in a drive by or a gang war, even if it's because the holder is a territorial nutcase who likes a tidy domain.

                                                            Exploring this and coming to your own opinions is far more interesting than having EVIL printed in all caps fifteen times a page (exaggeration, but that's what it feels like. Not just in V5, this annoyed me in previous editions too. I got it in the first page, I'm literate and capable of critical thinking, you don't need to keep fuckin' telling me!)
