    What lore changes did you LOVE added to Vampire: The Masquerade?

    11-17-2025, 02:36 AM
    I know, it's an odd title.

    POSITIVITY?

    But yes, sometimes retcons and changes to the game actually work out really well versus the opposite. Say what you will about Ur-Shulgi but the worst NPC in the history of the gameline was also effective for doing what was a massive boon to the game and that was facilitating the end of the "Murder Cannibal Terrorist" take on the Assamites/Banu Haqim.

    The Ravnos have been revised three or four times and each time for the better. So much so that I actually think the 5th Edition versions are the best of them all.

    Same for the Tzimisce as I think there's far more room with "Dragons in Human Form who rule over their feudal apartment building and consider everything in their hordes" than there is with fleshcrafting Necroscope vampires who are just alien and evil because.

    OPP was no stranger to this themselves and I loved how they handled virtually every single plot idea in BJD.

    Author of Cthulhu Armageddon, I was a Teenage Weredeer, Straight Outta Fangton, Lucifer's Star, and the Supervillainy Saga.

                  Souleaters are described as an undefinable force or collective consciousness, fragment of a spiritual entity (most likely a Bane from the brood of the Eater-of-Souls). The Souleaters are native to the Deep Umbra, and some sources even claim that Vicissitude and Sanguinus were in fact parasitic diseases that originated in the Umbra and were brought to the physical realm by a 4th generation Tzimisce named Andeleon
                  An infectious spiritual disease that can take over any creature, eat away the body, mind, and soul of the infected individuals until they are consumed and overtaken by it, becoming Souleaters themselves manifesting things that would put Cronenberg to shame
                  The Souleaters could be the cousins of the Chulorviah - if you want to have some fun



                  V20 introduced the Asakku - Kindred whose souls have been corrupted by excessive use of Vicissitude. The changes inflicted on the Asakku force them onto a new path, the Path of Asakku, which grants its followers Transmutations – powers that make it easier for the Asakku to learn Vicissitude, convert other Kindred into Asakku, and other abilities
                  They may call the presence they summon forth a "shadow within" or a "Watcher", (one being named Aretstikapha) or identify with one of the Qlippothonic demons associated with Vicissitude.

                                      I can't really now remember anything but the already given OP examples, but they're really good.

                                      With the changes Tzimisce and Assamite went from pointless to be among my favorite clans. I think the same can be said about the Followers of Set as they amassed an increasing number of schisms and deviations and the ones already there became more nuanced. And for all of those, keeping the option for their "classic look" was just as important.

                                      Ravnos I have one disagreement: getting nuked in Revised was both bad and dumb.

                                      Ok, I have another example, opening up Blood Magic. As interesting as the Pyramid is with their carefully cultivated monopoly, each expansion of sorcery options brought interesting ideas and made the setting more deep and complex. I'm very happy with the form it finally took in Rites of Blood for V20.

                                                          Some other thoughts I had about lore changes.

                                                          1. I really liked the implication that Sacha Vykos as we knew them as the serial killing psychopath flesh monster was actually the Dracon influencing Vykos. That was one of the more interesting ideas of Beckett's Jyhad Diary and it's a shame we didn't get to see them afterward. The Signature characters got washed away except for Victoria Ash. But I do think that a lot of the original Vykos assumptions depended on transphobic ideas that I believe OPP recognized.

                                                          2. I'm going to say that I think making Kevin Jackson Prince of Chicago was a fantastic idea and I liked their take on him for CBN5. Kevin is a guy who was intelligent and powerful enough to fill Lodin's shoes but also someone awful enough that being an antagonist wouldn't be out of the question for PCs yet not SO awful as the PCs couldn't work for them either. It was the same way Lodin was.

                                                          3. I'm going to give a controversial decision here and state that the Invasion of the Anarch Free States the Kuei-Jin actually was the best thing that could have happened to the Anarchs. Basically, it set up events for Bloodlines and created a lot more enthusiasm for the Anarchs than they'd previously gotten. Plus, it set up LA by Night where the Anarchs have a Council and actually seem to function as a group.

                                                          4. I'm of mixed feelings with the idea of making the Kuei Jin nonexistent but I do think making it clear the Cainites are throughout Asia and things like Tokyo having a Tzimisce Prince are very good for international gamers. I also do think retaking New York, Mexico City, and probably Montreal were good ideas. Because really...Canada is ruled by the Sabbat?

                                                          5. Boston by Night is a special case too as Matthew Dawkins did a book that reconciled the lore from DARK COLONY to SWANSONG and that is EFFORT for the fanbase.
