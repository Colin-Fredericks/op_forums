    So what does this mean for the Free States?

    Well, it means some of these celebrities might not be able to leave southern California. They run the risk some Justicar or archon may do something. Of course, their celebrity might also protect them as harming them might cause more trouble than what it is worth (because of the ensuing media attention and law enforcement investigation).

    However, it could also mean the opposite in that if they behave and follow the rules, maybe Princes might welcome them as part of making sure the Masquerade is strengthened. Or there is a greater chance they might be offered benefits in exchange for spying or doing some errand for the Justicars. After all, how many of these top vampire celebrities who just happened to be embraced in the Free States want to be able to visit wherever they liked just as they did when they were mortal? For that reason, embracing these highly privileged, rich, famous celebrities might be seen as a security risk by some anarchs.

    There’s also the possibility that celebrity vampires embraced elsewhere who do run afoul of some local Prince (maybe for Masquerade reasons) seek refuge in Los Angeles.

    Also, if celebrity vampires screw up in LA and breach the Masquerade, those breaches still need to be cleaned up and the violator perhaps eliminated. The Status Perfectus still maintains the need for a Masquerade after all. A certain number of celebrity “overdose” deaths, “suicides”, decapitating “accidents”, and other tragic demises might be the result of vampire celebrities being eliminated for their crimes.

        What might it mean for Petrodon?

        LA celebrity vampires are a threat to be managed, but if possible turned to the Camarilla’s benefit. Many celebrities would want the ability to travel elsewhere on occasion, and many would welcome a more stable (and therefore safe) environment than the chaos and violence often found in the Free States. The potential for double agents, spies, and so on is not insignificant. (It is also not inevitable as there are plausible reasons why some celebrity vampires would prefer the Free States over the Camarilla.)

        There’s also evidence that suggests at some point the issue of celebrity and the Masquerade will be addressed by a conclave. After debate and deliberation, some blanket policy will dictate how the Camarilla will handle such “threats” including destruction of such vampires (or at least how the Justicar who called that conclave will do). This would have very potential big impact on such celebrity vampires in the Free States. I’m thinking that might be a very good story idea to introduce at some point in the chronicle. Perhaps the PCs and certain NPCs might attend such a conclave.

            I just wanted to pop in and suggest some notions for Vital Signs and Topaz Jet that occurred to me - my knowledge of the LA musical scene is sufficiently shaky for me to regard these as especially sketchy sketches, but hopefully there will be enough to go on with.


            VITAL SIGNS - A name that has ‘Rock and Roll band that just keeps rolling like the Stones’ written all over it, so let’s imagine that long term admirers of the band are entirely convinced that it’s not so much that the band has broken beyond all repair, it’s that they’ve hit the ‘break up’ phase of their break up/make up cycle (Possibly with an eye to coming back as their own grown-up sons after a suitable interlude).

            - Members Tom, Rick and Harry are all creations of the same Sire (Tradition suggests a Toreador, but I like the idea that their boss/manager/vampire dad is a Ventrue who decided that his way of graduating from the agoge with honours would involve making the LA music industry his personal preserve: as he’s a Ventrue working his way very carefully through a whole thicket of thorny Toreador complicated by watchful Anarchs, this process is taking longer than hoped, henchmen or no henchmen).


            - Mental image of their being roughly the same age by the calendar, but embraced separately (Mostly as a reward for being moderately useful henchmen and superbly useful cover, because Rockstars can get away with an amazing amount of weirdness and criminality, petty or otherwise) so that one is forever twenty something, another middle aged and the last … well nobody’s quite sure because that dude was frickin’ ageless even before he was a vampire, if only because he NEVER looked young.


            - Being allowed to remain a band is, of course, their incentive to do what the Boss tells them when the whole Anarch Free States say that they are in no way obliged to do so (Why do they want to be a band? “Groupies!” “The music, dude” “It was either that or get a real job”).


            - Nevertheless in addition to the usual clashes of artistic temperament, the lads are currently being pulled away from each other by the fact that Harry is so hungry to be THE rockstar he might shatter the Masquerade if he only he had the nerve, while Rick is more eager to advance himself as a vampire thsn a musician and Tom just wants to play his music, dudes, chill and let’s just be Rock n’Roll vampires.

            Y’know, when the Boss doesn’t need us to be gangsters.


            - Mental image of Vital Signs, musically speaking, being a no-frills, hard-driving rock and roll band with more than a hint of punk: in terms of sartorial style they tend to Go Big and change by the whim, not least because it’s
            much easier to avoid people noticing you don’t age when everything else about you changes by the Fashion season.



            Topaz Jet I have a less vivid mental image of, but I tend to imagine them as a pair of singers (calling themselves ‘Topaz’ and ‘Jet’ of course) who were turned by a pair of vampires - representing two clans - who were somewhat interested in seeing whether this would pull a close team apart over the long term, but far more interested in sealing a covenant with a shared business endeavour.

            The ladies themselves, of course, were coming of age when their chances of becoming more than backup were clearly visible, but far enough in the future that they had serious concerns they’d be too old to seize the day when their Big Opportunity came (At least by the standards of the LA music industry): offered the chance to be eternally young at the price of fheir managers becoming their owner-proprietors, the future Topaz and Jet seized their opportunity and (some decades later) are finally in a position to be the Bug Stars they’ve always wanted to be.

            Now if only they didn’t have to be POP STARS to get there.

                Originally posted by Atticus Cleanclaw View Post
                I just wanted to pop in and suggest some notions for Vital Signs and Topaz Jet that occurred to me - my knowledge of the LA musical scene is sufficiently shaky for me to regard these as especially sketchy sketches, but hopefully there will be enough to go on with.
                Those are some interesting ideas and certainly builds on the information provided in LABN. My problem is that in terms of NPCs, I already have something like 6 NPC musicians and adding five more bumps that number to 11. That's a lot of similar musician concepts. Even for LA, that may be a bit too many vampire musicians. And I haven't even begun to add other NPCs that would cover other types of celebrity fame.

                I originally had 3 NPCs of my own based on pre-existing characters my players would recognize, and I want to keep them. Another two are filler characters based on the Fake Rapper and Gangster Rapper templates in the original Brujah Clanbook. As filler characters, I can easily jettison them or add them as "new" vampires later on. The last was Delilah Monroe from the Chaos Factor sourcebook. I could easily replace her as I already have someone who is representative of the SoCal punk music scene, but I really like the idea of including an actual canon NPC and do something with her. Having two SoCal punk musicians isn't bad for me because I have very different plans for these characters, and one of my players is into punk music and can contribute a lot to the setting there.

                I was originally just planning on having all the members of the "now defunct" Vital Signs be destroyed. However, I really like your idea of the band as a "break up/make up" type band that was wildly popular. Rather than have them be native to LA, I'm considering going full "Rolling Stones" and making them part of the British Invasion (perhaps somewhere between the Stones and the Animals in terms of popularity) and already being in their forties by the time they were embraced (and thus having lots of complications from previous marriages, children, etc.). They would have been embraced in London (perhaps by Lianna in World of Darkness 1e). Their sire would be notorious in musical circles as one of those women who somehow ruined the band (explaining why they broke up the last time).

                They were subsequently chased out of London because of Masquerade issues and went to LA because it was really the best place for them to be safe. They'd be powerful enough in the music industry that if they still wanted to produce albums they could - nobody is going to tell them they cannot come in only at night. And they could easily play single night gigs in SoCal and get everyone excited they might be getting back together. Having such a worldwide popular band would address a lot of issues concerning Fame. Although there may only be three now, perhaps there were a fourth or even a fifth before those were destroyed (always described as some tragic death appropriate for such hard rocking musicians like an overdose).

                Just to reduce the number of musician characters, I may just have Topaz Jet be the name of Delilah's band and throw some of the other existing characters into it. It doesn't need to be any of their main bands, just a side project they employ when they want to play locally for a Kindred audience.

                There are no right or wrong answers here. STs just need to figure out what works best for their chronicles and which canonical elements they want to employ or not.
