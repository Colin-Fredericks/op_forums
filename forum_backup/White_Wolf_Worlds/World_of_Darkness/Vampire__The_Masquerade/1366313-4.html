                            l like different traditions having different claimed origins backed by the rules, stops having to contort myths onto the clan system, allows abilities to be added to reflect those myths without it becoming poke-discipline.

                            KotE was a mess in execution, the idea of hungry dead unrelated to Caine was a good one.

                            On the 'from a dead religion' idea, actually would still like different strains of vampire, unrelated to each other, add more mystery and the fear of the unknown. (related to my dislike of Obtenerbration and Necromancy being linked, one was Cosmic Horror, the other was the traditional Horror of, well, necromancy, keep things muddy and unclear, just what is behind these things, what more monstrous terror stalks the universe, allows different flavours and themes to be played with, allows unknown unknowns to lurk)

                            Also allows things from the whole cloth to fit, a weaver cursed breed of blood drinkers, magical experiments, spectre ridden corpses etc all slot better into an already obscured field, look less obviously like splat crossing.

                            As to why they would be in VtM: adds mystery, adds to possibility of strange bedfellows alliances, adds story possibilities, all the time sticking to the theme of monstrous supernatural undead parasites lurking among humans. Also reduces the need for bloodlines.
