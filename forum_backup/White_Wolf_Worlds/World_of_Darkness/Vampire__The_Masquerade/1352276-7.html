    [Regional Age Variance]
    Vampires, Cainites specifically, can be mapped to a certain extent by following their expansion. Their are exceptions to these rules as clans such as the Gangrel, Malkavians, Nosferatu, Ravnos, & Lasombra (where sea travel could be involved) have spread to basically wherever humanity was, throughout prehistory. Throughout the Neolithic there were Ravnos amongst Kuei-Jin, Malkavians amongst the Laibon, and Nosferatu amongst the Drowned Legacies; etc.

    That aside, let's break it down somewhat:

    •The Middle East: Mesopotamia, Egypt, The Levant, The Iranian Penninsula, the Caucuses, etc. The Fertile Crescent was the womb of human civilization and it is almost certainly where Enoch and the Second City were located. (Some kindred claim Enoch was Ubar... perhaps Ubar was the title of the Second City?). Within the Middle East, you'll find some of the oldest kindred in the world. Methuselahs sleep beneath elder cities which they ruled 5,000 years ago and the blood of ancients is strong here. Noddists who have ventured here know, that while the appearances of modernity and today's struggle may disguise it, there is no better argument for the truth of Nod then to venture into this ancient land. The land where history walks and myths grow hungry...

    •Beyond The Middle East, we have the next general ring of growth which has essentially two phases imo. First you have the dispersal after the Fall of the Second City, the Baali Wars, and those exiled or given to wandering. These reached the Mediterranean, Europe, North Africa, and Asia. While they were spread loosely at first, the second phase (perhaps caused by the Bronze Age Collapse and transition into the Iron Age) would see kindred fill out Europe (including its north and islands), North Africa (down to the Sahara and those few willing to venture below where traveling clans had long reached in Sub-Saharan Africa), and filling out the kindred populations of East Asia, where kindred society would develop particular distinctions over the millennia due to sheer distance from the relatively connected Near East, Europe, and Northern Africa. These became the land of younger methuselahs (a phrase with horrifying implications) and true elders. These lands developed into the cultures and states that are the recognizable predesccors of the nation's that we would come to know today. This is where the Jyhad truly came into its own, now spanning continents with its plots and tactics as 13 clans battled with the direction of now primordial ancients. Kindred here are entrenched and time moves slowly for the kindred. Nearly two millennia passed and much of it began to look the same for the elders to stand above it all. Little could shake these settled lands. Until, finally, humanity rose up with the fires of Inquisition...

    •The New Worlds
    -The Americas and Australia cannot be understated in the history of the Jyhad. After over ten millennia in the Middle East and almost half that time in the Old World, the kindred had grown so incredibly static and fossilized that time may as well have become still for those who could see above the petty drama of specific cities and dynasties. The New World changed the scope. Both for the machinations of ancient and elder as well as for the young and desperate who chafed the yoke of the Old Powers. Kindred accompanied the first colonists of the colonial period with a hunger that rivaled that of the imperialists. These first kindred, largely young turks who were both hungry and disposable became the vassal waves of the old powers and while they carried ancient interests, they also began new ones. Young kindred could strive for domain and power while ancients knew the chessboard. It truly was a land of opportunity for vampires and given the relative time scale of cainites, with less then a millennia under their belts, the land of the new worlds remain the turbulent domains of young powers and young dynasties that can still battle in heated contests for supremacy and control.
    -[Due to the dangers of travel and the tendency of old kindred to remain within their long won domains, I have vanishingly few old world elders and even fewer methuselahs in the new world in my setting. Reading canon WW it felt like any B+ city had a half dozen true elders and maybe a methuselahs to its name, which struck me as both ridiculous and needlessly limiting, not to mention undermining the thematic divide between New and Old Worlds. This is why in my own setting, while old world backrooms and conspiracies may have shaped the new world, relatively young kindred are the authors of vampiric domains and legacies throughout the new world.]

    [Break Down]
    •Tier One: The Middle East (Ancients & Old Methuselahs)
    •Tier Two: Old World (Methuselahs & True Elders
    •Tier Three: New World (Young Elders & Ancillae)

        Things I do in my games that are just background:

        Masquerade Headcanon

        * Serial killers are viewed as far more common than in RL and is a thing that regularly happens.
        * Occult-style murder hysteria is still believed in because, well, it's true in the World of Darkness. It's like shootings sadly in that it's a real thing that people tune out on the news.
        * Organized crime is still a major thing in the United States and functions like it does in certain countries as an untouchable pillar. When a house full of bodies is found, people assume it's an organized crime disposal site.
        * Due to the deteriorating situation in the world, protests and riots are actually more common. This is often stirred up by the Camarilla and Sabbat to cover up their activities but happens naturally.
        * There's every bit as much conspiracy theory as there is in RL. Anti-vaxxers, UFOs, lizard people, pedophile conspiracies, and government black ops. The thing is, these are ALL WRONG but they misinterpret the real evidence for other conspiracies. So, the irony is that the real stuff going on is obfuscated by layers and layers of weird.
        * There are thousands of experts who know the truth. Probably tens of thousands actually. The thing is that people who know about the supernatural and believe get recruited into one of the groups adjacent to it. If you're not killed, mind-wiped, or too scared to hide then you're likely to be a secretary for the Technocracy or an Arcanum professor.
        * People do believe in the supernatural a lot more than they do in the quote-unquote real world. It's just there's every bit as much bullshit if not more than in the real world. Fortune tellers, astrology, occult stores, Wellness, crystals, and so on are in every phone book. Finding the real stuff is almost impossible.
        * Horror practical special effects never went out of style. That combined with CGI means there's always massive numbers of Youtube videos of fake horrible murders because that's just a thing do. Interspersed between them is the occasional RL snuff film.
        * Cellphone jammers are a real thing that a lot of vampires carry around and other supernaturals. There's also an enhanced version that prevents video and pictures from being taken.
        * Police are just X more corrupt in the world. The New York City level of corruption never went away because it serves Camarilla interests and is just how police do business (many countries are this way anyway).

                                                    Vampires, like humans and lupines, spread to near every continent before the dawn of writing. Because of lupines and their ilk, vampires suffered tremendously in Northern America and Australia, where there wasn't enough humans to hide among. Among other reasons, the clans that could hide well and do well with less people, such as the Gangrel, Nosferatu, Ravnos, Lasombra and, curiously, the Settites managed to survive in these regions, but the Brujah, Ventrue, Toreador and Tzmisce didn't stand a chance.

                                                    For southern America, obviously, the latter clans could do well; There were cities for them, sometimes even temples dedicated to them. Of course, the European vampires did not like this one bit, and were overwhelmingly successful in wiping out the local competition. To this day the Europeans are searching for aboriginal ancients to devour in South America, dreading the time they may wake up and reclaim their lands.

                                                    If there were ever vampires on Australia before colonization, they were especially terrifying. Sparse food and plenty of changing breeds is not a good environment for vampires, and the bushfires, while not nearly as bad as now... An aboriginal elder of Australia's gotta be on the same level as a methuselah anywhere else.
