            Will this thread get back heat once the Sabbat book comes out (we are halfway in this month of release and not much word), or will newer threads be made? I mean, certainly new ones will...

            But I think the Sabbat will become more cultish and guerrilla than ever, with those who stayed behind trying to infiltrate Camarilla or mingling (more) easily with the Anarchs and Hecata. I also believe they would be represented as more religious, and almost desperately trying to get recruits and resources (also weapons and artifacts) to fuel their Crusades, which are happening all across the globe, to where the Beckoning calls. So the paths of enlightenment will be much more tied to cults, packs and initiations I would think, which is cool. And Sabbat will have much more clear purposes of destroying the enemies, fuel their crusaders buddies abroad and deciding their own fates, because they Are At War Now, and there is no escaping that, you either fight this war or you desert the sect.

                Part of the difficulty in running Sabbat in V5 is the shift from Anarch vs Carmarilla. Considering they've upped the short sighted nastyness of the Carmarilla and the Chaotic Brutality of the Anarchs as well as the raw hypocrisy of bothleaves the Sabbat at something of a loose end. Rather than modify the Sabbat to suit V5 what I'd do is flesh out the Anarchs better by providing more distinct insitutions, philsophical and cultural standpoints while reverting the Sabbat and Carmarilla to their 3rd/20th norms. This creates an expansive rather than reductive standpoint to jump of with on Sabbat V5 and frankly the Anarchs.

                                                            Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
                                                            Despite the inclinations of players, Brujah arent meant to fill the role of big dumb guys. they aren't the VTM equivolent of half-orc barbarian, .
                                                            That's kind of a half-truth. The Brujah are clearly intended to fill a role equivalent to orc barbarians, in that they easily go berzerk, have enhanced intimidation, and attack with savage strength and speed. However, being an orc does not mean intelligence has to be a dump-stat, it just means you won't get as much use out of it as a class designed around intelligence. Also, all vampires are effectively multiclassed into Bard, as they all have the ability to create ghouls, which is to say the ability to make someone love them and have enhanced healing. A Brujah elder is something close to an orc with levels in both Barbarian and Bard.

                                                            The main thrust of your point still stands, though: Brujah aren't intended to be stupid, they just don't get as much out of being intelligent as a Tremere or Malkavian or even a Nos.
