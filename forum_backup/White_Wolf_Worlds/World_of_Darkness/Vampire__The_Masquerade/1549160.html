    Would have been cool if there was a tradition that focused on animating stuff.

    03-11-2025, 02:46 PM
    Everyone knows what Elemental Mastery is, but imagine if there was an entire blood magic tradition based on it. A talking table that can walk, a piano that runs around and bites people like a dog. Like in those old cartoons where the inanimate objects and forces would suddenly act like people or animals. Imagine just walking into a Tremere chantry and everything is alive, moving, and talking. The couldron tells the Tremere alchemist that they're using the wrong ingredients. Or the Chandelier judges you for how you look. Or the blood packs cut you off because you've been drinking too much. The air itself always has something to complain about.

    I also think that it would have be a pretty good parallel for spirits and wraiths. Also, I don't know if this was mentioned before, but Elemental mastery and the Green path are somehow really good and interesting at all levels. Usually the majority of the paths, at least in my opinion aren't that great. But for some reason these two are pretty nice.

              While I agree with Atticus here, there are ways to make it more thematic. Like the big oil painting that watches you as you move, or floor boards trying to grab you as you walk over them. It becomes very on brand once you apply animation to statues and such. Golems and other constructs have a place in Hermetic tradition for the Tremere, and the Setites even have their own Path for Ushabti.

              I had a Tremere character who was making constructs when he was off the clock for Clan business, pretty much as a hobby. Animated dolls work great to freak out visitors to the Chantry, and statues/golems are a bargain alternative for when your Lord doesn't give you perms for a Gargoyle. Don't underestimate the shock value of having a Weeping Angel guard your library.

              I think pairing Elemental Mastery with Green Path has some merit. Both Paths are good for the witch and warlock type of blood mage, and if you put animated clutter into a witch hut it doesn't feel as out of place as in a stately Chantry full of hermetics who approach magic like science.
