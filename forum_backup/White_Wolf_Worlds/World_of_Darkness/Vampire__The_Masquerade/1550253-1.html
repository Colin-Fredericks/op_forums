        Suggestion: Why Vannevar survived as Prince of San Fransisco

        My interpretation of San Fransisco for the World of Darkness was that Vannevar Thomas was someone the Anarchs loathed to a great deal because he represented something that was an ideological challenge to their ranks: The Prince no one really hated. I think this was actually canon for awhile.

        While about 200 years old and an Elder even by European standards, albeit barely, Vannevar had spent most of his time in San Fransisco as a much younger vampire. Frequently, like Lodin, he was actually finding himself the underdog against much nastier vampires. As such, the older vampires in San Fransisco considered him an improvement over the previous princes and the younger vampires generally didn't have quite as big a collection of the atrocities to point to as other domains. One might argue he was comparatively a soft touch.

        This is not to say that Vannevar Thomas was a "good" person or a weak prince. He still staked and burned people who threatened the Masquerade and played the games that Kindred do. However, the Second Anarch Revolt stopped around San Fransisco simply because of him understanding two things.

        1:] Vannevar understood that while he could not be loved and feared by all Kindred, he was certainly not HATED.

        2:] He was able to rally the Kindred of San Fransisco to believe the Anarchs coming to "free them" intended to do so in order to seize the locals domains. Which had the benefit of being true.

        3:] Both the Eastern Kindred and Sabbat had their designs on the city and were willing to repulse the Anarchs alongside Vannevar. Ironically, he'd deal with both in Dark Prince.

        If I were to make an interesting scene, I imagien Jeremy MacNeil meeting Vannevar and finding that Vannevar was NOT a Camarilla loyalist himself. Indeed, he'd almost been overthrown several times by the Justicars and happy to cut a backroom deal with the Anarchs. Prince of the City notably makes it clear that he was almost overthrown multiple times for the Archons pick.

        This actually becomes the canon end of Vannevar's reign as he gets himself replaced for being too soft on the Anarchs and Thin Bloods. Probably because, like the prince of Berlin, Vannevar understands the Prince better than the petty tyrants under the increasingly mad Inner Council.

        Author of Cthulhu Armageddon, I was a Teenage Weredeer, Straight Outta Fangton, Lucifer's Star, and the Supervillainy Saga.

                My read on Prince Vannevar Thomas is that he’s a genuine moderate: he’s not old enough or deep enough in the Old Boy network* to be heart-and-blood Camarilla, but he’s too invested in good order and the quiet life to really favour the Anarchs; as for the Sabbat, I suspect he prefers to keep his distance.

                Therefore Prince Thomas is here to do business and that business preserves the Balance of Power (Which, at least in San Francisco during the Age of the Free States, is very much to the Camarilla’s favour - although I suspect the Inner Council has debated that point a time or three, with no firm agreement between the factions).


                *From what we can gather, his Sire lived as a Caitiff and a vicious one at that, it was only after decades as a vampire that Thomas got in touch with the Texas Camarilla, so it’s easy to imagine there being something of a question mark over his lineage - worse, he never went through the Agoge, so his Ventrue credentials are somewhat in question on that score as well.


                In all honesty I would suggest making San Francisco, not Los Angeles, Sebastian LaCroix’s home base during the early 1990s - he’d be far more at home as the Clan Ventrue’s eye on Vannevar Thomas and the Camarilla’s occasional messenger to the Free States than as a Kindred with specific ambitions towards Los Angeles (Which makes it all the more hilariously apparent that his being made Prince of the latter city was a backhanded compliment and very possibly a reward for services rendered that also worked as a punishment for undermining a fellow Ventrue).

                Heck, there might even be a steady stream of hungry Ventrue into San Francisco, looking into how Prince Vannevar Thomas does things and arguing over whether he’s a proper Ventrue, not just a successful Ventrue (More than one of them has even left the city alive afterwards, although not necessarily of their own free choice).

                                            The conflict between the Kindred of San Francisco and the anarchs is the status of nearby San Jose, the northernmost free state. Ten anarchs, in a single coterie with no baron, defend their turf there. In the description of the barony, it is said they fought off two attempts by Vannevar Thomas to reclaim the area and that currently there is an uneasy truce. In another section, those attacks are described as feints merely intended to keep the anarchs in line, but that Vannevar is planning a serious attempt to actually take back the city. The difference is probably based solely on the perspective of the respective groups.

                                            One would think San Jose would be important as it is the leading city of Silicon Valley. Controlling that would seem to be an obvious draw for any ambitious Kindred whether Camarilla or anarch. So the lack of any detail or acknowledgment of its significance is surprising in the LABN sourcebook. However, in the World of Darkness, it is entirely probably all the good things are already heavily defended by either lupines (Glasswalkers) or mages (Virtual Adepts or Technocracy). Kindred who get to close just end up disappearing mysteriously so those Kindred who are there end up being the ones involved in much less attractive industries.

                                            An actual attack by Vannevar that takes San Jose would probably be very destabilizing in any chronicle and lead to a drawn out war between LA and San Francisco that would quickly draw in Petrodon. If the ST wants that, that is all very good. If the ST does not, it would probably be best to not have that event occur, or wait to do it only after some other event that would consume the attention of the LA anarchs. That way either only a relatively small group might attempt to help out as others are too focused on this other event, or by the time the anarchs could decide to march on San Jose, most of them accept it as a fait accompli.

                                            The issue with the Free State is that it is the definition of mob rule, and mob rule is fickle but passionate. All it takes is one charismatic figure and the mob is off. So Vannevar always risks a confrontation if he takes the offensive. Ironically, the nature of the Brujah means there are lots of charismatic leaders who effectively cancel out each other because everyone has their own passion project.
