                    Originally posted by Herr Meister View Post
                    Well, I think it being similar to another discipline, especially celerity, is really thematic and in the end, almost unavoidable when one thinks of time/speed powers. It happens with basically every splat across the WoD, but in the case of Temporis, because of the relationship between True Brujah and Brujah, it's even feels fitting.
                    From a design perspective, if you're going to make two traits that are distinct and concurrent, they need to follow three criteria:

                    1 - Be distinct enough that players can appreciate the choice between one and the other as meaningful;

                    2 - Be "balanced" enough that players can see reason to take one or the other depending on circumstances;

                    3 - Be able to pass those tests on most occasions, instead of just in exceedingly strict scenarios.

                    The problem with Clotho's Gift is that it isn't just similar to Celerity, but that it completely substitutes Celerity. While it follows a somewhat distinct mechanic, it is too similar to Celerity in the final effect to be said the difference is meaningful and the only advantage Celerity has is giving bonus Dex in V20, which simply does not beat 3 BP to get several extra action for 3 rounds or more, specially when that will be just one power, not the whole of the Discipline.

                    If it was the whole of Temporis, then things would be a tad better. If Celerity still cost 1 BP for complete activation, then it would be better balanced. In both cases there would be meaningful advantages in either choice, but they would be so similar that still any player that found their own preferred mechanic would simply ditch the other Discipline for all characters.

                    Clotho's Gift could have more distinct mechanics still following on the theme of accelerating through time. Or it could be an Elder Level and level 5 be another thematic power. Kiss of Lachesis could, with just minor adjustments, be a good enough level 5 power, and is my go to simple substitute for Clotho's Gift.

                    Originally posted by Herr Meister View Post
                    While there's no denying that Clotho's Gift is in fact very powerful (it's the power I feel the need to curb or ban), I consider every level of Temporis from 1 to 5 as very useful and good. Only level 2 is already enough to "break" an enemy fighter, or at the very least put up a good resistance, so to speak.
                    They are, just not nearly as much (comparatively).

                    Specially when you add their costs, rolls and requirements for effective use, they're a little below average in comparison with most other Disciplines, This is not the worst, it is still close to average and very useful indeed, it is a solid Discipline choice. But I think the majority of those powers could use a little tweak here and there to really be on par with the others, nothing major.

                    My biggest issue in this sense is Hourglass of the Mind. Seriously, a perfect sense of time is neat, but a 1-point Merit at most. And the sense that something happened to time, without the ability to say what, and still with an Awareness roll at normal difficulty, this is another Merit, 2-points at most, probably 1.                     Multiple actions do not work in any system whatsoever, not without very clear boundaries to limit its effectiveness.

                    You can take a clue from Requiem again, benefits on Movement and Initiative are usually good. Another option is to let the description of an action be of multiple, fast-paced repetitions of the same task, but the mechanical effect be just a bonus to the action, something M&M 2nd ed does. As a wild example, Celerity could just let you spend 1 BP to turn the bonus dice to automatic successes, or to reduce the difficulty of a Dex-based task, including both attack and defense.

                    Another option, which may be interesting for Clotho's Gift, is to follow the rules in D&D's Time Stop, where the limit is due how long the world takes to react to your actions, so direct interaction becomes tricky, but there are clear uses. This make some sense if you consider that while your movement is "faster", it still carries the same energy and the transference isn't completely in your new time flux.

While WoD doesn't have such trait, I think this is a nice compromise for the worry about Dex being too powerful and makes it more flavorful at the same time: instead of acting as a flat Dex bonus, it could passively work as a defensive Dice Pool that you always have access to. You won't attack with the help of Celerity, but even if ten thugs attack you, with Celerity 4 you have a 4-dice pool against each and every attack before even declaring anything.

                                                On top of that there are the active uses: spend Blood to go first on Initiative for the round, or to interrupt someone else's action in the middle, both limited to a number of activations in a scene equal to your rank, and then the movement, where you get to multiply your movement (and movement alone) by 1+Celerity. I would add the option to transfer the defensive pool as a bonus to attack or to increase it as active options.
