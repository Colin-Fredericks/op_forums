              Yes the max before freebes are spent is 3 in any ability and this ensures that the character is more balanced. The 10 discipline dots can be spent in any discipline so ling as at least two of the disciplines are clan disciplines. For instance a Nosferatu who has Potence 3 Obfuscate 4 and Fortitude 3. You are not missing out on anything because the 3 dots you spend in age gives you 55 freebee points that can be spent on anything plus you always start with a base freebee points of 20. Next question is virtues after embrace instead of before. Yes you can boost abilities above five if you have generation 7 or lower. I would like to end this with this, I have made minor house rules to character creation for Elysium rules, though for the most part it is RAW.

                  Thanks for the kind words! We appreciate getting feedback on the game. Glad it is good reading, feel free to use anything from it in your own games.

                  Ok, to answer your questions, the PCs starting in 1483 with (mostly) normal starting stats for a DA character. There were slight modifications.
                  ---
                  Attributes: 7/5/3 Abilities 13/9/5 Backgrounds 7 Disciplines 4 Virtues 7 Humanity/Path Con+SC/I Willpower Cou Secondary Abilities 5/3/1 +Area Knowledge (Home City) 1 Clan Knowledge (Clan) 1, 21 Freebies. Base Generation 12th, 13th gets one extra Background point.

                  You speak Czech and your native tongue. Šárka speaks Czech and Slovak. Editor's note: As she is Czech
                  ---

                  When we moved to 2012 I had them update in two stages. I'll just copy and paste from my instructions

                  ---
                  You will upgrade your character in two stages

                  -You will get +1 Status (Eleanor, Rosabella, Diego) +1 Sabbat Status (Jocelin, Krzysztof) +1 Clan Prestige (Šárka)
                  -You get +1 Clan Knowledge (Brujah/Malkavian/Toreador/Gangrel/Tzimsice/Followers of Set as appropriate) -You will get +1 in Area Knowledge: Prague
                  -You will get +1 in Area Knowledge: (City), where (City) is whatever City you spent the most time in over the centuries. You can't put this in Prague but can put this in whatever city your home town is if that is where you spent your time. If you were all over the place equally for time, it is whatever was the last city before Prague
                  -You will get +1 in Camarilla Lore (Eleanor, Rosabella, Diego) +1 in Sabbat Lore (Jocelin, Krzysztof) +1 Clan Knowledge: FoS (Šárka)
                  -You may now choose Merits and Flaws. You may swap Flaws or Merits out 1 for 1. You may only spend additional Flaw points you get on Merits or Backgrounds, not Attributes, Abilities, Secondary Abilities, Disciplines, Virtues, Willpower or Morality.
                  -You can write an account/draw a comic/create a puppet show/whateva of your PC for the last 500 years, or alternate offer pictures/descriptions of your haven, herd, etc. It is possible to earn 5 Freebies this way for three total pages (2/2/1). Like extra Flaw Points, these Freebies can only be spent on Merits or Backgrounds.
                  -Now is the time if you are changing Nature, Demeanor, Virtues, Morality, or Specialties.
                  -You can roll for certain backgrounds on the Background Chart at this point, a total of up to 5 rolls. Note if you choose to do this, you are equally likely to gain or lose backgrounds, so think about it before you do. You cannot "gamble" Generation. You are free to not gamble any backgrounds, of course Editor's note: The chart referred to is the one in Ill Omens and Dragon Ascendant. No one elected to do so

                  At this point, all Freebies from downtimes or flaws must be spent before you do the next "phase"
                  Once you have those points spent:

                  -You will get 550 XP
                  -You will have a total number of Additional Languages equal to Intelligence+Academics-2. This means the total number of languages you speak that you did not spend Merit points for should be Intelligence+Academics given you got two free right off (Native+Czech).
                  During this time only, you may buy backgrounds with XP, at 3/x3 XP. I'd prefer you not buy Merits this way (outside Languages) but may allow it if you have very few Merit points

                  This is a good time for Sect Based Merits/Flaws. I will allow others, but I prefer only ones you could have acquired over time through activities (ie, not ones like Blush of Health). Rosabella will have to lose Former Ghoul. Eleanor can take Infamous Sire, if she so wishes, but does not have to. Some Merits you could easily have picked up include Elysium Regular (1) Rep (1) Sabbat Survivor (1) Boon (1-6) Lawman's Friend (2) Rising Star (3) Mole (3) Primogen/Bishop Friendship (4) Broken Bond (4). Some flaws you could have easily picked up include Dark Secret (1) Recruitment Target (1) Special Responsibility (1) Sympathizer (1) Cat's_Paw (2) Escaped Target (2) Failure (2) Former Prince (3) Hunted Like a Dog (3) Blood Hunted (4) Laughingstock (5). You do not need to take Sleeping With the Enemy regardless of your relationship to fellow Conspiracy members. Remember you can take a max of 7 points of Flaws, and I prefer no more then 10 points of merits, not counting Languages (because they are not really merits in my mind). If you want more then 10 we will discuss it.

                  I will note a few Flaws more common to Elders you could have picked up from some of the DAV Material.

                  Degenerate (1-5) Lose this many points from your Morality. You cannot drop your Morality lower then your Continence/Conviction, however. Remember your Morality represents your maximum virtue dice, maximum dice rolled during the day, Bearing if any, and on Humanity how much blood you have to spend to eat, drink or screw.

                  Jaded (3, Instincts Virtue Only) You are really jaded, but when you get excited you get really excited. +2 difficulty to Instincts rolls to control yourself during Frenzy. If you are Brujah...it's bad, total of +4. Note this does not apply to rolls to resist Frenzy if the difficulty is low enough.

                  Restricted Diet (2) Your Curse has progressed to the point you can no longer feed on Animals, even if you could have when younger. Note this Flaw makes you unable to take the Animal Succulence level 6 Animalism power.

                  Thirst of Caine (7) You can only feed from Vampires, even if you could have fed on others while younger. Humans, Animals or Revenants cannot sustain you. I recommend against this one, but here it is.

                  Now, I know folks are discussing pooling points on Herd and Domain, possibly others (Šárka and Krzysztof may want to on Occult Library, along with anyone else who might buy Blood Magic in the future of the game). I highly encourage people pooling points to spend freebies earned from Flaws or Downtime on these points so we don't have to worry about who buys what level and spends how much. If you don't write a Downtime/Haven Description, don't acquire any flaws, and don't "sell back" any Merits for Freebies, we will discuss how to arrange XP expenditures on such. I'd rather not deal with that unless it turns out we have to. This may be as good a place as any to discuss what backgrounds we wanna pool.
                  ---

                  My original plan was to give +X attributes, +Y skills, etc. But I wanted things done more organically and, honestly, XP spending at this level meant less chance of oddness like it being cheaper to, say, stack discipline dots for higher levels rather then having dots in several. XP seemed to work OK

                  All that clear?

                                      Originally posted by Nintendogeek01 View Post
                                      Disciplines: 10 (7 freebies per dot) Ordinary rules restrict you to in-clan disciplines at this stage, does that still apply here?
                                      Personally, I don't think Elysium rules need much more freebies. In fact, be careful if you are STing. There is a lot of room for min/maxing.

                                      In Elysium rules, pcs can begin with up to their gen maximum in any discipline. They start with 10 dots, and only five of those must be in-clan.

                                      In V20&pre the first level of a discipline costs 10xp, whether it is in-clan or out-of-clan. Raising in-clans is cheaper than oocs, so if there is a high level ooc power you know you want, buy it now. An efficient build will buy at least one dot in each in-clan, fulfill the five dot requirement, then quickly move on to oocs. Ideally, as many level 1 oocs as possible.

                                      For each trait (including disciplines, attributes, abilities, etc.) of 6 or higher after spending freebies, the character must take a derangement for each. Seems like a bad deal, but in some early editions removing derangements is not that hard.


                                      Some legal builds with no freebies spent on disciplines:

                                      A Ventrue with Dominate 3, Fortitude 1, Presence 1, Protean 4, Obtenebration 1. This takes the minimum ic, hits 4 dot ooc, and picks up a difficult to acquire discipline.

                                      A Malkavian with Auspex 1, Dominate 3, Obfuscate 1, Obtenebration 1, Vicissitude 1, Bardo 1, Mytherceria 1, Temporis 1. Gotta catch 'em all!

                                      A Brujah with Celerity 2, Potence 2, Presence 2, Protean 2, Thaumaturgy 1 (elemental mastery), Spiritus 1. Combat max!

                                      A City Gangrel with Celerity 5, Protean 2, Potence 3 Note, the agg damage multiplication going on here.

                                      A Toreador with Celerity 8, then two more Celerity 8 powers Don't forget to buy up your generation, and to choose your five derangements! A total basket case. A very, very fast basket case.

                                      A Tremere with Thaumaturgy 8. Five in primary path, plus one in five more paths. Buy elder gen, and pick your derangements. Crazy powerful. Oh, and ask your st about level 8 rituals.

                                      A Noiad with Protean 4, Animalism 1, Auspex 1, Dominate 3, Presence 1 Noiad flaw is no feeding from animals. This is no problem at all, here.

                                      An Old Clan Tzimisce with Animalism 1, Auspex 1, Dominate 3, Protean 4, Presence 1. Same disciplines as the last one, but different flaw.

                                      An Old Clan Tzimisce with Dominate 3, Animalism 1, Auspex 1, Vicissitude 5 Okay, fine, the fluff says OCTz dislike Vicissitude. Elysium mechanics says "any discipline".

                                      A Malkavian with Obfuscate 5, Quietus 1, Obtenebration 1, Thaumaturgy 3 (path of mercury) World famous infiltration expert, if that weren't an oxymoron.
