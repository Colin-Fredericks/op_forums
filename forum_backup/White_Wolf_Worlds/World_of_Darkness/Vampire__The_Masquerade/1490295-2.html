            Yeah, the Giovanni couldn't directly take their wealth from the Underworld and bring it to the real world or vice versa. But they could use their accumulated wealth in the Underworld to make alliances, or hire the services of powerful wraiths or groups of wraiths who could use their powers to affect the real world. Likewise they could use their real world resources to affect the Underworld, either protecting or destroying the fetters that ghosts are bound to.

            "Work with us. We know you don't like Richard across the street. Do what we want and we'll burn down the historical building that serves as his fetter, then purchase the land and repave it into a parking lot. But if you don't want to work with us, maybe Richard will and it could be your fetter that gets turned into a parking lot. It doesn't much matter to us which way this goes, but I imagine it matters to you. So think about it. But don't take too long because maybe Richard might make a decision before you do."

                            Yes, the Giovanni (or other necros) relationship with Wraiths is a covert operation, and goes a lot less "we enslave every wraith" and more into covert cooperation (hidden from the "authorities" - aka Stygia or other dark kingdoms). But, despite that, there's still the enslaving of SOME wraiths (or Spectres). It is worth reminding that slavery IS more than just common in the Underworld, so I doubt any of the Ghost allies of the Giovanni would ever batch an eye over it - as long as they believe themselves not to become their next victims.

                            There are benefits to that for the necros no matter what, even if Fatalism is redundant for a vamp with Presence or Mnemosis being sort of redundant for one with Dominate. First of, the necro wont need to look another in the eyes if his ghost pal uses Mnemosis on his behalf. There's also plausible deniability. It's gonna be a lot harder to connect Ghostly activity to a necromancer than if the vamp were using ghouls and/or neonates to target a rival - that is, if the victim can identify that he is been targeted by ghosts at all. Nobody can accuse you of using Dominate to erase memories if you have the perfect alibi of being in a party at the Elysium.

                            On top of that, despite some level of redundancy, Arcanoi and Disciplines are NOT exact matches. Thus, this means that there are some effects that go beyond what vampires can commonly do - and on top of that, those would be largely unknow to the vast majority of non necromancers.

                            Yes, there is an additional difficulty with "collecting" Wraiths with specially useful Arcanoi - particularlly because the "ghost authorities" dislike them.

                            But... Even that also have it's pros. Practitioners of those, and even more, guild members of hunted guilds, would be a lot more willing to "pay for protection" - to anyone capable of protecting them.

                            The Giovanni can greatly help in that - being mobsters in both the Skinlands and the Shadowlands. Those Wraiths could count on Giovanni help for protection, in exchange for services, in a truly mutually benefitial relation.

                            And that's not all. Necros probably wouldnt have much trouble working with SPECTRES too. At least, not problems related with morality. Necros need not worry about Spectres trying to "convert" or throw them in Oblivion (well, at least as long as they remain in the right side of the Shroud).

                            Giovanni wont be world-shakers in the Underworld, but they certanly are a powerful criminal gang.

                                                Again, the point is that the character is a Necromancer, first and foremost. While they can get plenty benefits from diversifying sources of influence and power whenever they can, most of the time they're dealing with ghosts and the Underworld.
