                        The thing with Hardestadt was that Tyler diablerized an unnamed Ventrue elder who promoted the Camarilla (published in 1991 and then 1993 the 2nd ed). He was introduced by name in Children of the Inquisition (1992) but wasn't even mentioned in Clanbook Ventrue (1994). As he was mentioned as 5th generation (yet no single trait at 8 in neither GC1 nor GC2) perhaps dates of him in the past didn't add up and decided to create "the Younger" in TCII (1998) but the pieces still fit together.

                        Anne was introduced in A World of Darkness (1992) and her character was assumed diablerist in GC2 (1996) due to the existence of Valerius being of higher generation than her. Bindusara was introduced in Clanbook Ventrue (1994) and mentioned as Valerius's sire again in GC2 (1996). Possibility of messing up by the writers, yes, but it's still fixed with no problems.

                        Veddartha was introduced as Mithras' sire in 1992 and as 3rd generation vampire, it was considered the Ventrue Antediluvian such as Absimiliard is considered the Nosferatu's (both from the same sourcebook). If white-wolf writers messed up dates on Hardestadt, they fixed it in TC2. So if the book had some major metaplot fix, why create another one? This is the reason I believe that there is a massive misinterpretation of Maltheas not being considered the 4th generation Ventrue he was intended to appear as in TC2.

                        I focus on what is and trying to make sense of it as thoroughly as possible but I understand that many already left that road since revised came out and simply stopped fixing things and started to give misconceptions as a way of not interfering on what was considered canonical by 2nd ed. In my games I do alter canon events at will if they suit the story but I like to keep things as is. I enjoy knowing the full lineage of every PC and understand when and why an elder might have a hand in the PCs actions, therefore, researching this helps me a lot with my stories.

                                                            But I have to say I understand why WW needs Ventrue to be dead. If Ventrue is still active, it creates one big problem: He is on-course to win the Jyhad. Because he controlls the Camarilla.
                                                            Yes technically it his clan that dominates the organisation not him and not completely. But with Dominate 8 9 10. It does not matter. The clan is merely an extension of his will whether they know it or not.

                                                            Now that in itself is not the real issue. The true problem is with Ventrue win-scenario being the Camarilla, he is a proponent of the status quo. A "good" antediluvian if you will. That is content to let his slave elders continue doing his bidding while he fight off his more apocalyptic minded brothers.

                                                            Also Ventrue's death do solve a number of issue I have with the vampiric history. For one the weakness of clan Ventrue in the Middle East, where most of their 4th generation hail from (like most clans) and yet all the ones we know quickly settled outside the region.

                                                            But without the protection of an antediluivian it makes much more sense. While Toreadors had Arikel in Mesopotamia (where she got the name), Brujah Troile in Phoenicia (from where she would later settle in the Carthaginian colony), Set in Egypt and Haquim/Cappadocius in Anatolia/Persia. They were not really in a dominant position and thus had to sought their fortunes elsewhere.
                                                            It also explains why Mithras, Alexander and other Ventrues would go the such forsaken places as Paris or London after the collapse of Rome when Constantinople, Damas and Baghdad was were the cool dude were.
