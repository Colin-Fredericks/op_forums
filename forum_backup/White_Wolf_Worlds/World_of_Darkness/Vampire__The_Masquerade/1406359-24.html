    Originally posted by Grumpy RPG Reviews View Post
    A Vér Hanaja

    In the reedit of the book this section is titled “A vér hangja.” The text reads that this means voice of the blood in Hungarian.

    As an aside, A Vér Hangja is also the title of a song by the heavy metal thrash Hungarian band Jogos Önvédelem (this popped up when I did a google search of the term). According to www.metal-archives.com the band is associated with “White Pride, Antisemitism, Nazism…” and produces music described as “pure anger through and through.”

    Right. So I don’t know if the band, or the title of the track, influenced this section but both this section and the band are more or less operating from the same song book.

    All that to say, in this section a bigoted vampire talks about the importance of good blood and how bad breeding has debased the quality of human blood, and that this is debasing vampires. While the narrator does not speak of any particular human race or group as inferior, it would be a surprise if the narrator did not have a lot of hang ups about race, and probably religion and sexuality as well. The narrator also espouses a lot of what sounds like Sabbat ideology about vampire superiority and listening to the will of vampire blood.

    Anarchs are vampires, and vampires cannot be good people so Anarchs cannot be good people. Some of them are worse than others, and this one is worse than Garcia or Sterek.

    It is useful, if not good, to be reminded, of the fact some Anarchs are going to be utter rubbish and that it will be fine if the PCs kill them.
    This seems like a perfect character article for Svein "Azrael" Fortinbras who is a character from CHILDREN OF THE REVOLUTION. He's a character who rejected the Sabbat, is a sincere Satanist, a religious terrorist, and a heavy metal Scandanavian Follower of Set.

    https://whitewolf.fandom.com/wiki/Svein_Fortinbras

    Racism and Anarchs

    He hasn't been affiliated with white supremacy because the books are usually coy about this (with the exception of Thomas Ewell due to the BLINDINGLY OBVIOUS fact that a Confederate soldier would be racist) but I could easily see him as one of those Alt-Right pagans that are yet another reason to be embarrassed by the movement. Not a dig at pagans, FYI, just racist misogynist ones.

    I've always had white supremacist Anarchs among my antagonists. This despite the fact I have many more black revolutionary, hippie pacifist, and antifa Anarchs. That's because, as you say, the Anarchs are everyone not Camarilla. So a bunch of Skinheads and Nazi Brujah have shown up over the years to ruin the Anarchs' day and make the Camarilla look better.

    It's funny because this WAS a V5 controversy that Brujah would include white supremacists when they were listed in the original V5 manual. I felt that that was overblown because as early as Berlin by Night, Dieter was an actual Nazi Brujah. Also, probably the only one that was actually usable among the Nazi characters in the book as he wasn't a member of Nazi High Command or tastelessly inserted.

    (I've used Dieter a few times as a villain in my campaigns, particularly as a Sabbat infiltrator of the Anarchs)

    Neo-Nazis, Confederates, white supremacist pagans, Old Nazis, and probably Brujah KKK members are all things that I can see among the Anarchs. Because they're the sect closest to mortals and their ills. I also see that they're the sort of people that if they ever show up at any place other than the areas they control, they will get their asses kicked or have a war with the other Anarchs. Theodore Dooley, Maldavis, and Carlyle aren't going to work with them against the Camarilla. They may even work with the Camarilla against the Nazis.

    And that's why the Anarchs weren't winning their war against the Feudalists until recently (but at least they kept the Nazis from winning).

            Long and Boring Essay on Left Wing politics vs. Right Wing with vampires

            I think that's a somewhat overly optimistic view of Left wing counter-culture or how it would interact with vampires. I also feel like Ring wing ideologies are not going to fair much better among the undead either, surprisingly enough. A friend of mine said that he felt that V:TM "cheated" by having vampires seemingly evolve past the prejudices of their mortal days and that most should be incredibly racist, misogynist assholes. I disagree with that actually, even among the Camarilla [especially among the Camarilla], for reasons I will get into.

            I feel like lots of vampires would be tied to Left Wing groups because there's always going to be the fundamental desire by vampires to self-actualize by deluding themselves into doing something useful with their powers. Very few people are the villain in their own story and even vampires who are Humanity 4 are going to have their pet causes, IMHO. Kevin Jackson wants to help the black community, another vampire might support battered women, Theo Bell has issues with slavery [modern or otherwise], and Al Capone still believes he's a patron of the downtrodden. In fact, I actually had a very interesting idea that the Toreador's entire thing is that promoting art is their spiritual purpose in life and gives their unlife meaning. It justifies all sins that they create something LASTING more than mortal life and made the world more beautiful.

            The thing is that this is always going to backfire in some way. You're inevitably going to start feeding on the people who are important to you. Your mindset is also going to be warped by the Beast so every problem looks like a blood bag. Police brutality? Well, obviously the solution is to just straight up MURDER the cops who killed Breonna Taylor (plenty of my fellow Kentuckians would support that solution--but probably won't help race relations in the USA).

            Kevin Jackson in V5 is actually a really good example of this in that he wants to help the black community and become the "Good" ending of Lincoln Clay in Mafia III. Build schools, support STEM, patronize the community, and so on. However, KJ makes his money selling drugs and beating loans out of people. Mostly black people. He was a vampire in life and undeath hasn't made him any better. I like this because it's got poignancy and makes the horror even better, IMHO. I think Right Wing causes are actually harder to maintain too because vampires are going to have just as many problems as Leftists maintaining any connection to the causes they held in life.

            Thomas Ewell and the General of Atlanta may believe the South will rise again but for the most Kindred, they're going to have trouble remembering why they cared about a bunch of Kine and their lost war as time goes on. They might miss the easy feeding but everyone is a potential food bag now.

            White, black, European, gay, man, woman, or straight mean little versus the common bond of being an undead predator and rival for power. To quote Dirty Harry, the average vampire "hates everyone equally." Then again, in my books all vampires are kind of omnisexual too because blood is sex and sex is blood so really all humans are the same in the end. I admit I stole this from Anne Rice who had Louis go through the epiphany that blacks and whites are equal--they're both prey animals to him.

            But that's my take on it.

            Lots of activist vampires will exist. They will just make things so much worse for the people they're trying to help. Likewise, you will have Neo-Nazis and Nazi-Nazi vampires but they will have to be REALLY dedicated to not drop the whole thing after a century or so.

            I totally do agree vampires will genuinely manipulate Right Wing groups and keep a populace divided, supsicious, poor, and terrorized because it's easier to hide. It's just I don't see them sharing much ideology with them. In Sons of Anarchy, there was a Neo-Nazi gang that was fighting the, themselves racist, titular gang because they sold weapons to minorities. Only at the end does the Neo-Nazi find out his boss just wanted the Sons out of the way so he could take over their business.

                                                #Motstå

                                                Motstå is a Norwegian word meaning “to resist, withstand, oppose.” Probably by coincidence it sounds somewhat like the English word “monster” if it were pronounced by a child or someone with a bad Boston accent.

                                                This is not a bad section, but it feels… odd for lack of a better term. It recounts in first person a night in the existence of a secret Sabbat defector to the Camarilla. This defector is sympathetic to the Anarchs, finding them to be closer to his own political sensibilities. At the same time the narrator is more or less going native inside the Camarilla, trading in favors, killing the Sheriff’s lackies and enjoying their night to night existence.

                                                The story provides another depiction of subverting the Camarilla from within, but it also depicts coming to call the Camarilla home for reasons of conveyance if not for ideology.
