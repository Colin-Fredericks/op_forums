                    I always felt the Haven rules were overly complicated as a background and took away from the "fun" of using it. So this is the rules I presented to my players as an alternative for V20.

                    Haven (Revised)

                    Not all vampire lairs are built alike and while Resources can generally provide most Kindred with a safe refuge from the daylight, others go to elaborate lengths to make for themselves the perfect home for their activities. This should be considered a rough guide for providing PCs the most bang for their Background. Players who want to make their own Havens are encouraged to acquire the resources in-game as well as spend the time necessary constructing one of these or spend x2 the level of EXP to buy this.
                    ----

                    * Nothing Special: Without resources, the player has a apartment, small house, or sewer lair safe to retreat to every night. Combined with Resources, this may represent a secret exit from their home, incinerator, or blood extraction system. Fire extinguishers are hidden in every room.
                    ** Kindred Amenities: In addition to the above, the PCs have a haven with a fully functioning security system and reinforced barriers against intruders. Without resources, they have a luxurious penthouse, large home, or underground basement lair. Sprinklers are built in.
                    *** Proper Vampire Home: In addition to the above, the PCs have their own holding cell and sound proof chamber to deal with Kindred business long term. They may have a small mansion, penthouse, warehouse, or factory. A panic room is available. Expect excellent fire suppression systems with their own tanks.
                    **** Scooby Doo Villain: The haven of the PCs is the envy of entire packs or Princes. A large mansion, small estate, their own downtown building, or an abandoned subway station. It is decorated in whatever fashion they wish and both difficult to find as well as breach. Private elevators and hidden entrances are possible as well as booby traps. The PCs may sleep in a literal vault. The building is almost certainly fire proof.
                    **** Phantom of the Opera: The player character has a home that could be ridiculous and Masquerade-breaking if belonged to anyone who stayed alive in it. A palatial country estate, a abandoned amusement park, a transplanted castle, or an entire underground complex. It may be filled with death traps, secret passages, torture chambers, or occult laboratories. Magical wards are available from the local Tremere as well.

                    Author of Cthulhu Armageddon, I was a Teenage Weredeer, Straight Outta Fangton, Lucifer's Star, and the Supervillainy Saga.

                                VtR doesn't, to my memory, but Mummy the Curse does. You can spend Tomb dots on Geometry (how the Tomb helps you regain mystic points) (max 5), Perils (traps: each as an Availability cost in dots that you pay with the dots you put in Perils - there is no maximum number of dots) or Provisions (same as Perils, but it's anything you want in your Tomb beside the traps). Tomb dots can be pooled between players.

                                I'd have no problem using a similar system in VtR, while for VtM and VDA I think that CTPhipps' Haven background is more in line with and fitting VtM's backgrounds system, which is looser and less detailed than Chronicles of Darkness's Merits system.
