                                                  We have a confirmed Mitochondrial Eve from whom we all descend so it stands to reason that there was, at some point, a first vampire from whom all others descend and whoever it was they may as well be called Caine.

                                                  Even if they were not the literal child of Mitochondrial Eve, they would still be a direct descendant of her and since this vampire’s actual name is lost to history (Cain’s name in the Bible means either “smith/builder”; likely in reference to building Enoch; or “to get/begotten” in the same way that Adam is “Man” and Eve is “life-giver”) Caine is as good term for this first vampire as any.

                                                  Both Mitochondrial Eve and Y-chromosome Adam existed c. 150k years ago and the earliest evidence of the humans from which we’re all descended leaving Africa is c. 125k years ago (into the Middle East... a region one could say is “East of Eden.”

                                                  Presuming that “Caine” came into being somewhere in that time range it’s entirely possible for “him” (there’s probably a 50/50 chance whoever the first vampire was was actually a woman) it’s sensible to believe that both Laibon and Western Kindred share a common ancestor. This common ancestor, by definition, would also have preceded the first Wan Kuei by millennia since no modern humans had yet reached the Middle Kingdom (so if you wanted to do a “Clan Wan Kuei” instead of the KotE “Risen” you could certainly justify it in the setting).

                                                  Another really wild thought to consider is that “Blood Caine” (in the vein of MitoEve) and “Western Caine” (the one Western Kindred count their generations from) don’t have to be the same person.

                                                  There could be a hypothetical Generation 0 (of which Lilith might have been a member) or even a Generation -169 (13x13) prior to the Caine that Western Kindred count their generations from.

                                                  Alternatively, just as Tremere and Giovanni and Troile ate their way up to Antediluvian status, maybe Western Caine ate his way up to 1st Generation; diablerizing whoever had that power at the time (because that’s something that could have happened dozens of times over a span of 150k years).

                                                  Maybe Gehenna is just a sort of contest of champions” where younger vampires get eaten as fuel for the ancients to kill each other off until one of them diablerizes the current 1st Generation to become a “New Caine” who sire progeny and starts the process all over again. Thin-bloods ot 14+ generation may have occurred multiple times before recorded history... there’s just little evidence of them because after 15k years any of the few who did survive that Gehenna either died later, diablerized their way up to a lower generation and/or just went into torpor and never woke back up (and are thus completely unaware of scientific advances like agriculture, the wheel and written language).

                                                  Lilith and The Crone might have been some of the last survivors of the previous cycle (Lilith being one who won the last round, a Gen 0 essentially, who Embraced Caine... while the Crone was a higher generation survivor of the last round who tricked him into a blood bond).

                                                  That could also explain certain confusions regarding who’s what generation and who sired who among the eldest generations... because diablerie has always gone on and thus some of the Clan founders might be 2nd Gen now or they replaced a prior 3rd Gen before written language even existed to record it.

                                                  Hell, given what “Life After People” depicted, modern civilizations could have risen and fallen so hard that nothing remains half-a-dozen times in the span between MitoEve and now. Maybe those pale “ancient aliens” who taught the Egyptians to build the pyramids were actually the last vampires of the previous cycle.

                                                  You can go as crazy as you want to get with it.
