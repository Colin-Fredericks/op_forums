                I like the idea as presented in Gehenna that Ventrue faked his death and is patiently waiting for the apocalypse to happen to wake up and take over as the god of the next era.

                But I have to say I understand why WW needs Ventrue to be dead. If Ventrue is still active, it creates one big problem: He is on-course to win the Jyhad. Because he controlls the Camarilla.
                Yes technically it his clan that dominates the organisation not him and not completely. But with Dominate 8 9 10. It does not matter. The clan is merely an extension of his will whether they know it or not.

                Now that in itself is not the real issue. The true problem is with Ventrue win-scenario being the Camarilla, he is a proponent of the status quo. A &quot;good&quot; antediluvian if you will. That is content to let his slave elders continue doing his bidding while he fight off his more apocalyptic minded brothers.

                Also Ventrue's death do solve a number of issue I have with the vampiric history. For one the weakness of clan Ventrue in the Middle East, where most of their 4th generation hail from (like most clans) and yet all the ones we know quickly settled outside the region.

                But without the protection of an antediluivian it makes much more sense. While Toreadors had Arikel in Mesopotamia (where she got the name), Brujah Troile in Phoenicia (from where she would later settle in the Carthaginian colony), Set in Egypt and Haquim/Cappadocius in Anatolia/Persia. They were not really in a dominant position and thus had to sought their fortunes elsewhere.
                It also explains why Mithras, Alexander and other Ventrues would go the such forsaken places as Paris or London after the collapse of Rome when Constantinople, Damas and Baghdad was were the cool dude were. Too much dangerous people sleeping underground.
                Totally makes sense

                    I think it's not entirely fair to say -Constantinople, Damas and Baghdad- were where the cool dudes were and many of the famous ancients left there out of fear of competition (Though overcrowding is certainly a cause for concern)

                    Perhaps the vampires who moved saw the future potential in other regions. Set clearly made a good choice with Egypt, which was the civilization for the longest period of time. Now when it comes to moving to forsaken places like London or Paris; Perhaps that was a bit of ingenious long term planning and foresight? The rise of western european backwaters like Britain and France in the early modern period was inevitable and forseeable with an understanding of geography and economics, These ancients could astral project themselves to survey land and had the inhuman levels of intelligence needed to predict the future. India for example might be an incredible place with everything that it needs... but having everything that it needs keeps it somewhat complacent. The British isles were a shitty place to live where society is forced to innovate for a good standard of living; it's also an island and in an excellent spot to take advantage of that huge western continent... Given enough foresight it's not hard to believe that the have-nots of western europe will grow to conquer comfortable, resource rich lands. Mithras really picked the best possible spot for the Jyhad, and I don't think that's luck.

                    and sure, western europe is now the comfortable, complacent land that will soon be outpaced by a rapidly modernizing Asia and eventually Africa, but the last four hundred years and how the world was globalized will be influentual forever.
