    [D20] How to use Lupines as a threat but not entirely wipe the coterie?

    07-08-2020, 03:21 AM
    So... I'm running a D20 game (and if you're in that one - you know who you are - stop reading here), and recently the coterie killed a bunch of kinfolk. They really didn't have another choice, so I don't wanna hold it too harshly against them.

    They're currently not in a city, can't go to one either. Some of the kinfolk had found them and realized what they are, and were about to find a werewolf pack to tell on them. There was little the coterie could do to stop them except wiping them out.
    They have maybe 10-15 armed soldiers with them - 5 of them ghouls, but that's about it.

    Now if a pack of werewolves were to find the remains of their kinfolk, they'll be surely pissed. They'll start to track down the killers and will eventually find the coterie (again they can't move - they're here on Sire's orders).

    Now if they're smart, they wait for a sunny day, rush in start murdering the guards (none of which have silvered weapons so its gonna be a massacre). Even if one of the kindred wakes from the noise, they can't actually do anything since they'd have to enter sunlight. Then they just tear their shelter to shreds and watch them burn.

    Obviously that would be a somewhat disappointing end to the coterie and the game.

    The big problem is that the Coterie doesn't know when and even if at all the attack is gonna happen. It's likely gonna be weeks or even months after the killing of the kinfolk. The pack just doesn't come by that often. And I doubt the werewolves would post a notice "Tomorrow at midnight you'll die. - Sincerely the local werewolf pack".

    One of the vampires (there's three total) made sure to have his weapon silvered and he's generally a combat monster, so I wouldn't be surprised if he can actually one-shot a lupine (at least a young one). The other two... less so.

    So, i'm currently looking for ways how to make this work, use them as a threat in the future, without it just being a combat encounter, or a TPK.

      Are they sitting where the kinfolk where killed, or are they a little distance away. IF the latter, then lots of wolf howling in the night from that direction would give them some warning, and allow for a hasty defence.

      Also I'd go with a small (possible 3 wolf) pack, so there is a hope of dealing with them.

                      First of all I believe that there's always a choice so they didn't have to kill them. During my games those characters would end dead (especially if they have Humanity below 7, which means they smell Wyrm).
                      BUT

                      It's your game so I will try to help you with that. I will give you four options, ordered by propability.

                      1. You could have the pack going after a bigger threat so they won't go after the coterie ASAP. From now on the coterie should shiver everytime they hear a howling.
                      2. The garou will let them live if they will turn their over Sire(s) AND at least one Kindred for every Kinfolk they killed (or for any of them). That means everything: location, defenses etc. Of course they could send the pack aftert a Cainite so powerful that he will easily defeat the Garou. BUT he can also take one prisoner and find out characters betrayal. And the pack could also go after the coterie when they will deal with their friends. They are traitors and creatures of the Wyrm. In this option the best tribes are the ones who don't mind get their hands dirty p. ex. Uktena.
                      3. The leader (or the entire pack) is very fond of challenges. They are also very honourable. I would make them Fianna or Get of Fenris. The leader decides to challenge one of them (or all of them if you want) for a fight to death. If the Cainite wins they will go unmolested. And let the dice roll (either figuratively or literally). Or they could attack at night cause slaying an unarmed sleeping enemy would be dishonourable.
                      4. Let the Garou (this time I think Children of Gaia would be the best) attack. When they will be on the coterie heads a gurahl (werebear) show up. He gives the Garou a choice: he will put his life on the line if they let the coterie walks away (this will work especially when at least one of the Cainites was Embraced by force). Gurahl knows a Ritual which can revive the dead but could also ends his life (more details in Gurahl sourcebook or old Werewolf Players Guide).

                      Also remember that ordinary bullets (especially from automatic weapons) could stop werewolf as quickly as silver ones. You just have to fire more of them. So the guards could be a factory.

                      And remember also about Garou nature:
