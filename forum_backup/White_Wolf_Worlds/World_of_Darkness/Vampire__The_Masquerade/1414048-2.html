        Yeah...

        Kindred reproduce exponentially faster than Hungry Dead, they get more powerful faster and don't require anywhere near the time investment that Hungry Dead do to bring them to fully active status. They're disciplines give more bang for their buck in 9/10 cases and their ability to mass produce fanatically loyal superhumans essentially on a whim means that they win the foot soldier war without even trying.

        Hungry Dead are on the back foot whenever they come in contact with them. To even get halfway to what a stock standard Brujah can do they need to make very specific investments in abilities and building their powers....and it will still take them at least twice as long to do so.

        Kindred have all the advantages. Only places Hungry Dead have a chance is if they're prepared well ahead of time and squatting on a Dragon Nest.

                                            I suppose the best way to summarize my opinion is basically saying, "What would the benefit be of Werewolves starting a war against all of vampire kind?"

                                            Garou have every reason to hate vampires and they are Wyrm Tainted. However, the average vampire has no idea what the Worm is or any involvement in Pentex save maybe their stock portfolios. As such, UNDER A BLOOD RED MOON shows just how big of a clusterfuck such a war would be after just about a week's time. It's not that the Garou can't severely damage the vampire population of the city, it's just the fact that rapidly it's going to become an enormous slog that distracts them from their real mission. Killing Jeremy MacNeil and Salvador isn't going to do a damn thing about Mikaboshi or the Qin Emperor of the 134th Layer of Hell.

                                            At the end of the day, the question is not whether conquering California is profitable (even though it is completely divorced from the Eastern Spirit World and its Dragon Lines) but whether or not the Anarchs' guerilla warfare and Kindred in general can make it UNPROFITABLE.
