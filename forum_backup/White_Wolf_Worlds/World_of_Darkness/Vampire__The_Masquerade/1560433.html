    Chronicle concept: stay-at-home coterie

    06-07-2026, 12:22 PM
    Premise - a chronicle were all the pc just stay at home or in the cars or wherever and use disciplines to move around.

    Disciplines (assumes V20)

    Dominate - Can literally possess people to use as puppies. Venture, Tremere, some Malkavians.

    Auspex - 5 dots let's you move around outside of your body. Bad parts: you are immaterial and also naked. Good parts, you can fly, move through walls, and are invisible outside of supernatural sight.

    Auspex notable also contain 1 to 1 telepathy so you can still talk to the rest of the coterie.

    Auspex 6 even let's you set up a telepathy network so get those dots in mentor and have your sire help out.

    Malkavians, Toreador and Tremere.

    Animalism - let's you possess animals like dominate possess humans.

    Needs a bit more from the St to be useful though.

    If someone else has auspex you can at least talk with them.

    Nosferatu and gangrel has animalism.

    Brujah is out if luck. Replace them with... Giovanni :/, necromancy path of bones can possess a corpse.

    Not sure what other disciplines there are, or if it could be done in v5.

    The gimmick

    I don't actually think it works, one guy doing the remote control thing probably works, even animalism if the St helps out some but as a group it feels difficult.

    But I can imagine a campaign built for it. All the streets are lined with cameras, hunters are at every street corner, and the haven needs to be guarded literally 24/7 because something - or someone - important is there.

    Or it's just part of City culture, all the other kindred does the same thing.

    A kindred fight in stay-at-home city is just one guys possessed bat fighting another guys possessed ghoul while an auspex guy tries to drag a necromancers soul out of the corpse he wears this week.

    For adventures just do lots of investigation. And the city should be filled with animals and some ghosts.

                  Npc concept: Nosferatu or Tzimisce that refuses to rise from their grave.

                  They live there now, use Animalism to move around.

                  Nosferatu would work thematically, they are supposed to be freaks and loser archetypes, and being the ultimate sloth is in their wheelhouse.

                  Plus in canon there are badly disfigured Nosferatu who spend their unlife fixed to one spot. But those tend to be affixed to a nosferatu warren.

                  Tzimisce work better mechanically because they have Animalism and Auspex. Possessing an animals requires eye contact, so either we bend the rules a little, use psychic projection (which does let you use Animalism with St approval), or the poor guy has to pop his head out of the ground. It would be like like nine dots or so of disciplines though, not so easy.

                  Feeding:
                  Possess an animal and hunt prey. Drop at grave and drag into the soil. Grave is littered with small birds and rodent bones.

                  Coterie or clan comes by and pours in blood.

                  Maybe trade prince or kindred blood for favors.

                  Local urban legends say if you 'sacrifice' an animal at the creepy grave good things will happen.

                  I was thinking you could use the Tremere power that lets you call blood from a distance to eat the buried, but it's not like they would contain much blood, difficult to learn and you might arguable need to move to use it

                  Using them in a chronicle.

                  Play up the horror tropes I think. And hide the true nature of the guy until later. If it never comes up then it never comes up.

                  There's two likely scenarios I could see that it would be easy to introduce the character.

                  Scenario 1:
                  Players want to trade nosferatu for favors, or contact local nosferatu for... probably still favors.

                  "Oh yeah you've heard one holds domain at the graveyard but you have no idea how to contact him."

                  They pull some contacts, do some rp:

                  "No ones seen him but he's pretty good, go there, bury some blood at this unmarked grave and state what you want and he'll hear of it."

                  They go to the grave, you describe it as worn down and tinged with weeds but not entirely dilapidated and oh it's also full of crows. They don't see any nosferatu.

                  Scenario 2 is they if they need to hide a corpse and decide to bury it at the local graveyard. Assuming they don't know anyone holds domain there. Obviously they accidentally pick his grave (you find an out of the way grave in a discrete corner). Ideally they have a Malkavian so you can describe the corpse moving but only he saw it.

                          Two Tremere chantries built in accordance with this concept:

                          The local Tremere chantry lies closed off and has been so for over two years now. It works only trough intermediates, possessed ghouls, Auspex communications and the odd Elemental or grotesque thaumaturgical creation venturing outside to gather supplies or attend what few meetings are absolutely necessary.

                          No one except it's members knows why, and they won't speak.

                          Unknown to the outside city, the chantry is in a slow burning crisis. It's regent was slain while heading outside, and all the other older kindred has either been killed or fled the city. What's worse when the chantry requested replacements they were refused and instead told to evacuate and join up with a larger chantry in a separate city. When this was attempted the kindred were beset by werewolves and only some of them narrowly escaped. The Tremere clan attempted to send a rescue party which also were beset by Lupines, and ultimately resorted to send a small party of gargoyles, not to rescue the cut off chantry but to investigate what was going on.

                          These gargoyles concluded that the chantry had greatly angered a spirit of art, who had instigated all ill that had befallen the chantry, and would likely continue to do so until either sealed or successful in it's quest to destroy the local Tremere. Thus the clan has ordered the chantry to seal the spirit and considers the matter a local one. If it is not settled within ten or so years they may allocate someone else to help resolve the matter

                          The chantry is desperately trying to increase their thaumaturgical capability while heading out as little as possible

                          ---

                          Premise two: Tremere chantry but they use some rite to allow Auspex ghosts to be seen and have large Auspex balls were anyone with Auspex can join and dance and talk and such.

                          Rites:

                          This rite may already exist it feels somewhat obvious, but if it does I don't remember it and I don't have the books right now to check.

                          Maintain the Visage Lvl 3
                          A little known use of Auspex is that the traveller can in fact materialize with some small exertion of will, if for but a moment. It does so as a translucent and ghostly visage. This ritual stretches that moment.

                          System: A Tremere draws a a circle or enclosed line on the floor, using vitae, cerebral fluid, and chalk. Once the ci A kindred using auspex who spend one willpower to become partially visible for a turn May remain visible indefinitely while within the circle.

                          The circle can be 3*The Tremere's Thaumaturgy yards in diameter, and multiple Tremere can cooperate to extend the space further.

                          There exists at least one ballroom where those who throw their soul outside of their body can float around and dance but filling it up has proven difficult.

                                          This idea was not originally made for this chronicle concept, just wanted an idea how to run a game without cellphones.

                                          But it would fit pretty well

                                          Coldbooths

                                          A coldbooth is any communication link between two or more regions of a single city that is controlled, staffed or otherwise operated by Kindred and can be used by a coterie, clan or sect. They have become more common in modern times due to the expectation and need for faster communications.

                                          The moniker comes from 'phonebooth' combined with the conceit that kindred are 'cold' bodies.

                                          But the actual link the coldbooth is composed of is arbitrary. It is common that it is a simple landline connection, hidden among the city infrasteucture yet isolated from it, allowing a hard end to end connection but any mode of communication still counts, be it a telegraph line using morsecode, a pneumatic tube system, or even just a courier service. Messenger birds, encrypted or encoded radio waves or in some cases even supernatural means may all be used.

                                          The Camarilla is torn on the concept of Coldbooths. On one hand it helps speed up communications in a time where that speed is often needed. It helps encourage the young and reckless away from using cellphones which helps alleviate the number of them who end up dead or worse, tracked, and allows the Camarilla to expend somewhat less resources on controlling local avenues of mass surveillance. But a city that relies overly much on Coldbooth has a clear strategic target, and the resources required to both operate Coldbooths and keep them secure easily wipes out any savings from keeping one less ghoul within a cellphone tower, or saving a few hours on a Kindred Dominating local bureacrats. Anarchs greatly favor them, and sabbat use them more commonly than most would think, though in tbe sabbat case their coldbooths more commonly relies on supernatural means than technology.

                                          Thus the availability of coldbooths wary greatly, with even many larger city not featuring any at all.

                                          Usage in game:

                                          The actual intended function of coldbooths in game is to let players be in a chronicle were their characters cannot use cellphones without completely taking away the convenience of cellphones.

                                          If Player A and B are in different parts of the town, want to meet up but don't know where the other is, then instead of describing them going home, and trying to get in touch and coordinate over hours or days the storyteller can instead go: 'oh you know a coldbooth in the vicinity, go there drop a message, B can pick it up whenever he feels like checking a cold booth in his area of the city.'

                                          If Coldbooths become an established part of the chronicle they can then be used to hook other aspects of the game into; recurring npcs can staff them, or you can run into an npc visiting one. You can tie side quests into them, help extend or guard one, use them for plot points (perhaps one of them gets attacked while the pcs are there, or a location of one is swapped but the pcs does not know the new spot.

                                          If pcs become familiar with the concept you could even have them set up their own communication networks. 'Personal Coldbooth' would be an easy background to make and would help someone to be very useful in a chronicle were cellphones are too dangerous
