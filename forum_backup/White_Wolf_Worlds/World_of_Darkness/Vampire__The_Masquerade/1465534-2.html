If the Second Inquisition controls Western militaries alone so tightly that they would not, as an example, start redrawing their entire operational procedures and strategies to reflect that terrorists have military drone strike capacity deep into Europe and reporting to their government how mind bogglingly bad that is, then it is so powerful that it may as well in fact be the pre Reckoning Technocracy, but now focused on killing vampires, in which case again, how are still vampires. If the Second Inquisition's control over armies, governments, the media and what have you is so absolute that this would not in fact happen, why are still vampires.

                                                Terrorists who can military drone strike the Western world would have intelligence agencies, media organizations and all sorts of things freaking right out and demanding/needing to know how the hell that happened. The SI not just being able to maintain its cover story in the face of that, but preventing the most likely responses of all that kind of thing means they are so globally powerful and influential that again, why are vampires.

                                                If you can maintain your suspension of disbelief that there would not be cries for retributive violence across the EU from political parties and people in the street alike at whatever they think would qualify, that there would not be "thinkpieces" in the media blaring about how now anyone can be attacked from wherever by terrorists with military effing drones and the destructive capacity they wield, then your understanding of the world and mine differ so sharply that there is not likely to be mutual comprehension here.

                                                It's not like there haven't been terrorist attacks in the European Union of a similar size in RL. An RL that has far less overt violence and horrifying events than the WOD. Who exactly would be reported killed?
                                                Name me a terrorist attack in the EU that involved not simply bombings or shootings or stabbings on the ground, but the acknowledgement that terrorists now have long range military drone strike capability that can take out buildings and such was why the incident happened. You seem to want to handwave that detail and nothing works like that. Going "the world of darkness", should in fact mean, since this is a world even darker than ours, that the response should be even harder terrified racist freakouts. The SI cover story involves saying a terrorist organization can now do the long ranged destructive thing that major developed countries can do.

                                                The demand would be for some, let's call it a "war on terror" maybe, with the responsible parties, until the idea that Europe can be hit with military drone strikes by terrorists can claim to have been resolved.

                                                A public story that terrorists have military quality drones they can attack European cities with and the inclination to do so, with a corroborating incident to back it up, is like a step below a public story that terrorists have nukes they can attack European cities with, with a corroborating incident to back it up.

                                                There is a difference between "a dude can shoot some people" or "a dude can blow himself up" and "now terrorists can just fire off powerful guided missiles at European cities from afar. The idea that even just the media alone would not be screaming that message is.. reflective of a take on history and society that does not seem to interact with the one that exists, let alone the one that would then be even worse in a world that is supposed to be even worse. Nor acknowledge that an organization that could prevent these reactions would have to be so globally powerful that the idea that its actual enemies could still exist anyway feels so thin as to be nonexistent as far as trying to deflect all this with "but conspiracy!"
