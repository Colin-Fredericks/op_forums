    Sect war thread: My cult's the best cult.

    04-27-2021, 04:54 PM
    So I felt myself enjoying a good sectarian dispute in another thread and want to see more people involved in this sort of stuff.


    Actually the best, really
    Followers of Set. Great guys and gals, always got your back. Friendliest sect in the game.
    Obviously the next Best:
    Camarilla. A sect for everyone.
    Excellent:
    Sabbat. Probably the most fun to play, make more sense when you get into it. Not for everyone.
    Good
    Assamites Loyalists- Ninja families looking at court like an all you can eat buffet.
    Children of Osiris- I just love saying "OH THE HUMANITY"
    Baali Hives.
    Ok
    Inconnu
    Ashirra- I'm starting to understand them. There is perhaps a logic to it, but they do seem a bit token on first glance, and they're very underwritten.
    Giovanni. It's a family, not a sect, honest.
    Not being in a sect.
    Bottom tier
    TBH. They seem like a pretentious lot.
    Garbage, doesn't even qualify as a sect. Join if you want to lose the Jyhad.
    Anarchs. You're either a poorman's camarilla or a poorman's Sabbat. Fun to play I guess.

    Objectively correct list here. Change my mind (all contesting posts may be reported to the mods for HERESY... oh, there's no rule against that here....)

                                                          I lean into the idea that the the Camarilla views itself as the UNIVERSAL government of vampires. You are considered to be under the Camarilla's authority unless you are one of the unfortunate few to actually be on the Shit List. They don't even acknowledge other movements as EXISTING, generally---a Neonate embraced in a Camarilla city in the Western World probably won't know what the Sabbat is beyond vague stories of "murderous vampires in the wilds." They'll only know about the Anarchs if they talk to Anarchs. Powerful Methuselahs who can scoff at the Camarilla's claim of authority like Mithras of London, Siegfried of Vancouver, and Marcus Vitel in Washington D.C. are limited to rumour outside their sphere of influence. For young Kindred in an American city these are wild tales, while the Authority of the local Prince seems paramount and absolute. Of course this culture of silence actually feeds into the mystique and lends credence when an Anarch Legate or Sabbat recruiter appears at the Rants or in the Racks of a Camarilla Domain. The Streisand Effect does some of the work for radical Sects in bringing curious young childer to their door. But since the Camarilla doesn't even recognize these other positions generally, a vampire might find themselves summoned to court or even appointed to a role in the Domain regardless of whatever allegience they profess.
