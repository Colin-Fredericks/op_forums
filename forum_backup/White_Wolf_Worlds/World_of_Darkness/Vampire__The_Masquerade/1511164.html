              Thhe Toreador Antediluvian is a funny thing
              First, becasue possibly there was two. not one of them
              Of Arikel, the Sculptor, the twin of Malkav (and Malakai) we had some information. But not much. The Toreador can't even agree in the gender, she was probably a male tho'
              In DAV: Road of Humanity, the Prodigals told of a story in which Arikel and Troile were troubled by the way the Cainites of the First City behaved, and turned to Saulot for aid. The Healer stated that only empathy would help them retain a link to humanity and so, both Troile and Arikel went to work on a project of art that spanned a hundred and one years, before they brought it before Caine. The work inspired exactly those emotions within the audience that they had inspired in the mortal population. Caine was deeply touched by it and called Arikel and Troile to his side, forgiving them past trespasses. These prodigals hold this as the origin of the Road of Humanity
              According Clan Book: Baali, among his brethren, Nergal was the one most adept at dealing with other Cainites. It is said that he served as Arikel's wench, debated philosophy with Troile and traded vice secrets with the Tzimisce Antediluvian
              Revised Toreador and Ventrue Clanbooks told us, that she was killed by the Nosferatu and Setite Antediluvians, who cut out and ate her heart
              Minor inconvenience it seems, since she - as Clanbook: Ventrue Revised mentions - had awakened from Torpor around 260 BCE, acting as an advisor to Alchias, the Malkavian prince of Syracuse
              According the Nosferatu (and Transylcania Chronicles) Absimilliard and Arikel were blood bond lovers. Absimilliard had spent a long time in torpor at Enoch. After torpor, the Toreador say that he and Arikel met in 2nd century Rome.
              Beckett's Jyhad Diary is the last info about Arikel; Beckett, deep within a pit under a ruins in Ketef Hinnom lost himself to the madness afflicting the Malkavians. While affected he said and wrote the following quotes: "Malkav. Malakai. Arikel. Lilith. Children and mother. Mekhet is free and Malakai follows​" (According to the Tzimisce Clan, Mekhet was a sibling of the Eldest (and thus possibly another childe of Ynosh/Enoch) who, with the Brujah antediluvian "played shadow games with mortals, delighting in their own cunning and agendas". Assamite lore states that Mekhet was a great liar. The "Fair is Foul" scenario in Gehenna features Mekhet as one of the Apostates, Antediluvians who never entered into the Jyhad and who allied with Lilith against Caine. Here, Mekhet is a potent pre-Egyptian blood magician whose body resembles that of a mummified Samedi)
              Her "canon" line consist Amarantha (who was mistakenly diablerized by an Assamite judge while he was administering punishment to her. Amarantha's name is one of the possible sources of the word amaranth, the traditional word for diablerie. After her death, there was a cult named the Amaranthan started by her childer as revenge by diablerizing the said same Assamite judge. Her descendants still practice their activities and belief to hunt and devour Diablerists.- more about her in Clanbook: Assamite Revised, and Children of The Blood, Cults of the Blood Gods), Mi-ka-il (aka. Beshter aka. the Archangel of Constantinople, Michael) and Iontius aka. Derrick Zeel (an ancient Toreador methusela who somehow gained the ability to feed off his victims through sex rather than by normal vampiric means. - more about him in Clanbook: Toreador, Encyclopaedia Vampirica, Beckett's Jyhad Diary, and DAV: France by Night)
              And Minos (the sire of Helena. He was diablerised by his childe) Yezidian refugees from the remains of the Aral Sea refer to the force of ontological evil as "Minos, who is the son of Arikel", who rests deep within the salt flats of the dried out sea.
              Our other contander is according to the Toreador revised clanbook, as told by Katherine of Montpelier, Ishtar, the Bull-dancer She was a mortal who lived in the City of Ubar. Perhaps it was her beauty that led to her Embrace. Thus, she became Antediluvian of the Clan of the Blossom by the time Ubar was renamed the City of Enoch. After the Deluge destroyed Enoch, the Antediluvians rebelled against their sires. — Caine's childer, the Second Generation — who they are said to have killed. Thus, Caine returned to the Antediluvians, who at the time resided in the Second City, to curse them. According to the Toreador, Caine cursed all the Antediluvians, except for Ishtar, whom he believed was innocent in the matter.
              Fearing possible vengeance from her angry siblings, Ishtar left the Second City to reside in Sumer. The people worshiped her beauty and made her their goddess. She also came to have a lover whom she embraced, that was known as Tammuz.
              Ennoia and Ishtar had a little history, The Toreador claim that Caine cursed all the Antediluvians, except for Ishtar, who remained utterly beautiful. Caine believed that she was not involved in the murder of his childer. His mercy on Toreador outraged all of her kindred siblings, in particular, Ennoia who had been cursed as "the Beast". Not to mention Ennoia possessed the Tablet of Destiny until Ishtar tried to take the item from her. Outraged by this action, Ennoia set out to kill the Toreador Antediluvian.
              As Ennoia tracked Ishtar to Sumer, she embraced Ereshkigal to help her in the attack on her sibling. When the Gangrel came to confront Ishtar, Tammuz interceded, and ultimately gave his unlife so that Ishtar could flee. She then sought aid from the king of Sumer, Gilgamesh. However, he had already been embraced by Ennoia. The Toreador fled from the pair, but not before unleashing upon them some great monster known as the Bull of Heaven, while she escaped to Crete.(interesingly, according to the Garou, Gilgamesh was offered unlife by vampires, but was talked out of it by a Child of Gaia named Siduri Sabitu. It is said that Gilgamesh was the first human to organize resistance against vampiric rule and the Garou tell that the inborn suspicion against the undead stems from his early influence. But the two story not contradict each other; it is possible Gilgamesh indeed resisted... but finally gave in)
              In Crete, Ishtar resided with King Minos, who desired and demanded her gift. Initially, she refused, but then compromised to embrace his son. Unworthy of the embrace, the son fell into the clutches of the Beast. His erratic behavior had him imprisoned by his father Minos, in what was an underground maze. There, he was fed "tribute", such as maidens, to squelch his hunger. Over time, the son became the fabled "Minotaur" who was killed by a mortal known as Theseus
              The Minotaur is another interesting one. Either the earliest record account of a Cainite in an extreme path of Wassail to become a Wight after an embrace... or according Werewolf, it was the last Apis who want insane. So again, the two story not really contradict each other; it is possible Ishtar managed to embrace an Apis, and her spwan was an Apis Abomination. Ouch
              But Minos also recieved the Embrace... just not from Ishtar, but Arikel a bit late (after Ishtar left probably)
              So Ishar's line is Tamuz (Clanbook: Toreador Revised) and the Minotaur.
              Ishtar, like Arikel leaves the scene in this point, nothing about them untill the Gehenna...
              Except maybe, the Laibon Legacy, called the the Ishtarri. They claim to be descended from the Babylonian goddess Ishtar, andlack the personal connection with Africa. The Ishtarri are the only Laibon Legacy that are not native to Africa; however, through manipulation and diplomacy, they have become an important part of Laibon culture. (The original outline for Kindred of the Ebony Kingdom confirms that the Ishtarri are intended to be a Toreador bloodline. The Ishtarri seem to have a combination of the weaknesses of the Toreador and Ravnos clans. Like the Ravnos, the Ishtarri each have a vice that they absolutely must indulge, lest it drive them mad. However, such vices focus on the beautiful and/or sensual, giving them something in common with the Toreador. Their Disciplines are: Celerity, Fortitude, Presence)

              Different Antediluvians? Perhaps, I like the idea, a Clan not necessarily have to be from one source. Even the Nosferatu toyed this with the broodmate of Absimilliard, Lucien. Only mentioned in Gehenna tho' Lucian is a supernaturally handsome man who effectively leads the Apostates under Lilith. It is suggested that he is in some way related to the Nosferatu: he may have shared a mortal parent or Second Generation sire with Absimiliard, or he may be the true Antediluvian of the Nosferatu, or the founder of a supposed Nosferatu bloodline such as the Nictuku or the Guruhi. The Guruhi itself is interesting and somewhat support a common ancestor; the Guruhi seem to share a weakness with Clan Nosferatu, though theirs is much more complicated. The appearance of a Guruhi changes based on their moods, reflected by their Orun and Aye; a Guruhi in a good mood with high Orun appears normal or may even possess unearthly beauty. However, one with a low Aye is revealed as a monstrous creature, much like the Nosferatu.Their Disciplines are: Animalism, Potence, Presence. But the original outline of Kindred of the Ebony Kingdom confirms the Guruhi are intended to be a Nosferatu bloodline.
              The interesting part?
              The Guruhi are mostly unified, despite an old legend which says there are two "lines" of Guruhi. The second line was supposed to take over Africa, replacing the original line when it was deemed "unfit". This division is mostly forgotten and rarely taken seriously, except by a few Guruhi fanatics.
