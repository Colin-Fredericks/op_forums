    [V20] Importing My Favorite Aspects of V5 to V20

    09-25-2021, 10:24 PM
    I started with V5, and I did, and still kind of do, enjoy it. However, I have since played V20, and it has become my favorite system. That said, there are some things that V5 did that I actually like, so I stole them and repurposed them for V20. I also added some things that I don't like as much, but decided to give people more options.

    Antitribu
    In the past, the term Antitribu was used to describe division within a clan in which opposed the main body of the clan. The resulting bloodline would have been labeled <Clan Name> Antitribu. However, in V5 the term has undergone a change that I actually approve of. The term now means “anti-clans”, and is used to describe the Sabbat’s distaste towards the idea of acknowledging clan or lineage1. I really prefer this change, so here’s how to add it to V20.
    • Change: The word Antitribu is added after the clan, or bloodline, on the character sheet to show the character shares the Sabbat view of clans, even Lasombra and Tzimisce. Mechanically, there is no difference between an Antitribu and a normal member of the clan or bloodline. Roleplay wise, any Antitribu will rarely hide their derision for clans, lineage, or similar concepts in social situations1.

    Hecata
    I actually don’t hate the Hecata as a concept, but I agree that it was weirdly implemented. Adding them to a V20 is actually pretty easy though, and here 3 options on how to do it.
    • Option 1: Just Make them a Sect. It is that simple. Read Cults of the Blood Gods to learn how the sect could operate.
    • Option 2: Make them a new Clan. This is also pretty easy to do, but honestly, Option 1 is even easier. To make them a clan, simply give them Auspex, Fortitude, and Necromancy as in-clan disciplines. Also give them the Giovanni weakness. After that, you’re done.
    • Option 3: Just replace the weakness of the Hecata Bloodlines. So I like this option the least, but here’s how to do it. Basically, take the Cappadocians, Harbingers of Skull, Samedi, and Nagaraja bloodlines and replace their clan weakness with the Giovanni clan weakness.

    Tzimisce Bane
    The best thing in V5, in my opinion, was the change to the Tzimisce’s clan weakness. So I literally just took that weakness and gave it the old weakness’s penalty. If you’re to lazy to do this yourself, don’t worry I got you:
    Each Tzimisce must choose a specific charge — a physical domain, a group of people, an organization, or even something more esoteric — but clearly defined and limited. The Kindred must spend their daysleep, surrounded by their chosen charge. Historically, this has often meant slumbering in the soil of their land, but it can also mean being surrounded by that which they tonight rule: a certain kind of people, a building deeply tied to their obsession, a local counterculture faction, or other, more outlandish elements. For each day of rest not surrounded by their charge, halve all dice pools (round down), cumulatively, until the pool reaches one die. A full day of proper rest with their chosen charge will restore the dice pools to their normal value.2
    Tremere Bane
    Due to in game events, the Tremere blood has actually lost the ability to bond. If you want to simulate this change, here’s a rewrite of the bane from V5 for your V20 game:
    Tremere vitae can no longer Blood Bond other Kindred, though they themselves can be bound by Kindred from other clans. A Tremere can still bind mortals and ghouls, though it requires two additional draughts of blood for the bond to form.3
    Assimite Changes
    Call them Banu Haqim, and use the weakness on page 428 of the core book4. Also pretend the skin growing darker with age was never mentioned. That is all.

    Ravnos Bane
    I’ll be frank, I didn’t want to rewrite the Ravnos or Tremere V5 banes for V20. However, I did it because I did Tzimisce, and felt obligated to do it. Here I go, I guess.
    If a Ravnos slumbers in the same place more than once in seven nights, they roll their Courage (Difficulty 5+each day they slumbered in the same place). They receive aggravated damage equal to the number of successes as they are scorched from within. This happens every time they spend the day in a location they’ve already slumbered less than a week before. What constitutes a location in this regard depends on the scope of the chronicle, but unless otherwise stated, two resting places need to be at least a mile apart to avoid triggering the Bane. Furthermore, a mobile haven, such as a movers’ truck, is safe so long as the place where the truck is parked is at least a mile from the last location. Ravnos characters cannot take the Domain Background at character creation.5
    Salubri Bane
    Haven't they suffered enough? The answer is yes!

    2 See pages 18-19 of 5th Edition Companion for the original bane. It's basically the same though.
    3 See page 97 of Vampire The Masquerade 5th Edition for the original bane. Still basically the same.
    4 See page 428 of Vampire The Masquerade 20th Anniversary Edition.
    5 See page 9 of 5th Edition Companion for the original bane.

                                      One of the main changes I implemented was a variation on V5’s take on Generation. Not the blood potency part, but the idea that instead of costing background points the PC generations (8-16) all had various tradeoffs.

                                      The big one was adding a Bane Strength based on generation and adjusted clan weaknesses accordingly (ex. Brujah have their difficulty to resist frenzy increased by Bane Strength).

                                      Here’s the chart;

                                      8th: BP 15/3; Bane 3, +1 diff to degeneration checks.
                                      9th: BP 14/2; Bane 3
                                      10th: BP 13/1; Bane 2
                                      11th: BP 12/1; Bane 2; full nourishment from cold blood
                                      12th: BP 11/1; Bane 1; cold blood
                                      13th: BP 10/1; Bane 1; cold blood, eat food merit
                                      14th: BP 8/1; Bane 0; cold blood, eat food, soak sun w. stamina (aggravated)
                                      15th: BP 6/1; Bane 0; cold blood, eat food, soak sun (lethal)
                                      16th: BP 4/1; Bane 0; cold blood, eat food, soak sun (bashing)

                                      7th and lower generations start breaking the balance curve (increased trait caps, much higher blood pools with the trade off being mostly slightly higher bane strength; 4 for 6-7th, 5 for 5-4th; and increased difficulty for degeneration checks; +1 for 7th, +2 for 5-6th, +3 for 4th) so there’s still a point to diablerie, it’s just more a tradeoff from 16th to 8th before you start getting the real rewards (and, by the time you’re a veritable blood god, who cares about trivialities like morality?).

                                      In reworking the weaknesses so they’d have something Bane related I also took some inspiration from V5’s banes; ex. Ventrue can spend Bane willpower points to feed from someone other than their chosen prey type.

                                      I really didn’t like the V5 Ravnos bane; it’s way too severe relative to the others and makes them much harder to integrate into a normal PC group. So instead they lose Bane willpower on waking if they failed to indulge their vice during the prior night. I also loosened up what counts as a vice a bit… one in my campaign is a shopaholic and must buy things they don’t need and another is addicted to their tech blogs and must catch up on them every night. Basically, if it eats up part of your life that could be better spent it’s a vice.

                                      I also prefer the Tzimisce flaw of having a “charge” that might be territory but might also be a people. It adds more flexibility to the concept.

                                      I also tweaked a few discipline powers. Ex. I like V5’s take on Presence 1 as adding dice to your normal social tests… basically like V20 Potence and Celerity. Particularly since the effects of default Presence 1 use the same dice pool and difficulty as a mundane Performance check which has similar resul In terms of affecting attitudes.

                                      So, yeah, mostly I fiddled on the mechanics side. My latest campaign just had news of the attack on Vienna hit about two weeks ago game time and no one knows what happened and the local regent/Primogen for city has gone missing in the aftermath and he’d divided the duties of deputy regent and clan whip to two different underlings who are now vying for power as acting regent and acting Primogen respectively (the one point both agree on was the Regent/Primogen had left for Vienna for an important meeting a week before the attack and so he is presumed dead; no one’s bothered to investigate the disappearance, but there are still clues that could be followed to see if that is really the case… if anyone cared enough to bother).
