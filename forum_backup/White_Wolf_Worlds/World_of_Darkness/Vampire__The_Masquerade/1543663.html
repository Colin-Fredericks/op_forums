    As per the title, in V20 is Obfuscate a mind affecting effect? this is for purposes of other gamelines powers that defend against such effects, such as Mind 1 defensive usages.

      Yes. While how to handle it in cross-overs is always going to be a bit complicated, as written Obfuscate is mental trickery, not physical illusions and the like. A common example is that cameras (not having minds) will record a character using Obfuscate to move unseen, even if someone watching the image on the camera's screen live doesn't see them in the moment (since that viewer has a mind and is still impacted).

          I like how it's framed in VDA20, even though it's not super clear: Obfuscate has an area of effect of 5 metres per dot of Wits+Stealth of the vampire (or something along these lines). That helps drive home the point that it's a mind trick and has limitations accordingly. In this way it can be imagined like a sort of psychic cloak cast on the minds of those within the area of effect, not those outside of it. It forces players to think carefully about when and where to use it.

                      The fact that Obfuscate is a mental interaction rather than light bending or illusion means that security cameras do actually record a hidden vampire, it's just that anyone viewing it has to beat the Obfuscate rating to notice the vampire in the footage.

                      In recent years this in turn suggests that AI facial recognition software would be able to find Obfuscated vampires in media since it doesn't have a mind, but the humans who manually review the footage wouldn't be able to perceive the vampire.
