      Strictly speaking, each Storyteller can allow whatever they like at their table. But the vast majority of Cainites have an extremely hard time interacting with the spiritual world of the wider WoD. It's also a question of how much feeding/stealing that you're talking about; if you're talking about the vampire consuming the spirit in it's entirety, that sounds like it has a fair bit in common with Diablerie, and you have to potentially settle some questions about whether or not consuming a spirit in that fashion might still produce Diablerie marks in the aura. It's also the kind of behaviour that could put ashing a Kindred at the top of the local Garou's list of priorities. If you're not talking about completely consuming a spirit in the process, then it has a lot of potential to become very broken if more than a rare handful of spirits can produce that effect. Alternatively, it's the sort of thing that should require the development of a Discipline or some kind of Blood Magic.

      I'd argue that the angle you're approaching this from kind of seems to try and avoid one of the central conflicts of the game, the struggle to retain Humanity when doing what you need in order to survive often threatens it. And while only a small handful of vampires interact with the Umbra, there are those who have been at it for millennia, and you'd have to question why such a powerful tool wouldn't be more prevalent if it was readily available to them.

      Regarding the idea that the Umbra is the source of vampirism, not the Abrahamic God, the Garou have their own story of the First Vampire, "the Bloody Man," which could be a foundation to build on if you go ahead with your idea. Depending on the version of the game you're playing, other options for lowering Generation, temporarily or permanently, exist.

              Consuming Banes would probably clash with any effort to pursue Golconda, as you'd be regularly consuming the tainted essence of Wyrm spirits. It would also probably run the risk of having the vampire in question register as even more Wyrm-tainted than usual to the Garou. But if you lean into the idea that Vampirism really is of the Wyrm, it's not completely unreasonable. Although I would still strongly recommend requiring some kind of special power or technique, not just let a vampire sink their fangs into a Bane and diablerize it.

              My understanding is that "werewolves get along with vampires in Golconda" is a bit of a mixed bag. I think there's a non-English source that supports the idea, but I've never heard it come up else where. Of course, vampires with high Humanity (8+ IIRC) do not register as Wyrm-tainted to the Garou, but that's as likely to be seen as some kind of trickery as anything. I don't think most Garou are familiar with the idea of Golconda, with a lot of RIPANDTEAR first, ask questions never. But exceptions exist.

              Regarding other methods of lowering Generation, especially if you're playing the X20 lines: the Banu Haqim possess a ritual that allows them to lower Generation without diablerie by collecting tons of vitae and turning it into an alchemical mixture. Tremere Thaumaturgy has the Path of Blood, which allows one to temporarily lower Generation at the final level. There's also a ritual that allows multiple vampires to benefit from a single diablerie, but I can't recall the name.

                                          Apologies for being repetitive, but to some extent, it's a "depends on the table" question. Even from the canonically listed options, there are probably options that a Storyteller could choose to leave out if it just didn't fit the themes of their story.

                                          Aside from Diablerie, the rituals listed above are the only options available in canon AFAIK. Something I suspect canon may support, and that I would probably approve, is that lowering Generation might be possible through pacts with powerful spirits or demons. But not run-of-the-mill spirits and demons. It's the sort of thing a demigod or demon prince might be able to provide, but not your average Jaggling.

                                          Again, lowering Generation is supposed to be very hard, if not impossible, if you aren't willing to murderrapecannibalize the soul of another more powerful vampire in the process. It's supposed to be a component of how Kindred society is stratified between elder vampires and their juniors. Generation wouldn't be precious if it was just a Trait that anyone could increase by grinding. Depending on who you ask, the power struggle over Generation is one of the most defining aspects of vampire society in VtM.

                                              Originally posted by Cain Loup-Noir View Post
                                              How many way are there to lower the generation permanently? As i only have the v20, v5 and the black hand book i don't know if there's more in previous edition or other books
                                              The thing is, we don't know your ST's position on the matter, and it makes all the difference. Like, all of it. Is it only the players planning this? Is your ST open to rules suggestions? Or are you planning on trying to sell your thoughts as The Logical Canon? Or yet, is your ST on board already and asking for ideas? This all makes for very different situations.

                                              As default, the only canon ways to permanently change Generation are Diablerie and the Assamite ritual. And this last ritual is what it is, good luck getting your hands on it and then better luck using it all the way up. The canon do not outright forbid other methods existing, but do not assumes them either.

                                              Not only that, but as pointed before you're probably putting a lot of weight on the wrong place. By itself, lowering generation only gets you more blood and blood per round. Even attaining higher levels of Disciplines is easier said than done, and maybe not all that interesting. Seriously, a lot of those powers are pretty lame for the hideously high cost in XP. Get other disciplines instead. Certainly pumping more blood is a great advantage, but a 3rd gen neonate isn't really all that more powerful if it have only a beginner's sheet.

                                              By the way, blood still needs to come from somewhere. Pumping more blood means needing more blood. How do you pretend to manage your sources? Most of the time a 8th gen consumption rate is more than you really want to use, even if you're a Blood God.
