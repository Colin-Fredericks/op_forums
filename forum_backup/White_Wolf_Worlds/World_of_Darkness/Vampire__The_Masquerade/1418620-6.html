                            Ravnos' Weakness

                            I think the reason that the Ravnos had Virtues as a flaw was because it actually fit better with the duality that the RAVNOS represent in the Revised Clanbook. In the book, it strongly represents the role of the Ravnos as religious figures who exist to tempt the faithful to evil rather than people who just engage in criminal behavior as well as vice for no reason.

                            By adding virtues as a thing they can be addicted to as well as the vice, you open a whole different bunch of characters that can be created. You can have the super-crazy chaste figure who stalks the lecherous like Michael Myers, you can have the super-charitable who regularly gives up all their fortunes, and other people who can show how vampires twist the virtuous into something horrible.

                            So you could have a bunch of super-religious, super-fanatical virtue police in addition to the criminal "I'm just going to make Gambit or Gypsy from comic books." I think that can only be good for the Ravnos as you might have some virtue-addicted Sabbat who fit well into the religious arm of the Sword of Caine.

                            More options=Good.

                                    Some good V5 Sabbat plots that are off the top of my head:

                                    1. Tremere

                                    Goratrix has refounded the Tremere Antitribu but it's actually Tremere in his body, being a 4th generation with the power to resist the Beckoning (assuming that the Beckoning for the Tremere Elders wasn't to Vienna only for it to detonate). As such, the Sabbat have one of the few active Antediluvians with a goal of stopping Saulot/Kupala/The Eldest. He may also "just" be a 4th generation vampire.

                                    2. The Harbingers of Skulls

                                    The Harbingers of Skulls have possibly killed Augustus Giovanni and taken over their clan. They certainly hated Cappadocias and hated the Giovanni Antelivudian too. They don't suffer the Beckoning either. So what does this mean for their membership in the Sabbat? Do they consider their part "done" or do they want to support another round? Or will the Sabbat react like, "What is this Hecata bullshit?"

                                    3. Ravnos

                                    You guys are right, if any Ravnos are still alive then it's probably the ones in the Sabbat. Do they want to rebuild their Clan, do they have any ties to the old Clan culture, or are they really just another type of Panders now?

                                    4. Lasombra Loyalists

                                    The Amis Noctis and Marcus Vitel have defected with an X-number of other Lasombra who each killed a Sabbat Elder on the way out. Even if less than half defected, that's going to seriously hit the Lasombra where it hurts: their pride. Worse, they're going to look like fools and that is something they cannot abide. It may actually finally remove them from their leadership role. Also, are they being Beckoned? Would they even notice? Will it become common knowledge they fired at their Antediluvian and missed?

                                    5. The Salubri

                                    The Tremere have had their Inner Council and a huge chunk of their Elders destroyed. The Antitribu may continue on their crusade to finish the weakened Tremere off but others may question the value of this, especially since avenging Saulot seems counter-productive for people who fight Antediluvians. What do they think of the revived Tremere Antitribu?
