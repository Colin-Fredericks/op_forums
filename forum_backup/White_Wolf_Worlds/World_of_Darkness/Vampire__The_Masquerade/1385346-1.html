        So, a little houserule with big, yet setting harmonious, implications; Obfuscate 1 is like an at-will version of the old Arcane background: Basically, the more obfuscate dots you have, the less important you are, the less memorable you are, and when the level's high enough, the more people want to attribute things you did to others, the more people misplace documents concerning you... If your obfuscate exceedes someone's willpower, the effect is even more profound, and for antideluvians with 10 obfuscate....

        Also Obfuscate 1's hide thing remains as a feature (I also weaken obfuscate 2 so it remains relevant) but the main thing is that obfuscate grants you "arcane".

        People wouldn't remember the actual antideluvians, they'd just remember stories.

        (all that said, I continue to write on this forum under the assumption that nobody knows my houserules and my -masquerade is solid- arguments do not factor in my houserules; unlike some people I can seperate my way of running things from the books as written)

                                                    Maeghar and Kiasyd, like a lot coming out of the True Hand, set off what would normally be considered red flags if fans wrote it. They're fae-vampires, and they look a bit like elves (unlike actual fae who can look really effin' weird), and they're so special they have TWO or even THREE unique disciplines, and..!

                                                    Obfuscate and Presence both sound quite fitting. Sneaking, hiding, that underscores their overall creepy factor while Presence could be a manifestation of the wonder the fae can evoke in people. So, if the Kiasyd/Maeghar are a clan, I'd say Obfuscate/Dominate/Mytherceria or Obfuscate/Presence/Mytherceria. Which puts them close to the branch family of the Malkavians and the Setites, respectively, which I find makes a lot of sense.

                                                    Tying it back to the original topic: There was some fluff in the revised Malkavian clanbook about how they and the Setites, unlike the other clans, catch a glimpse of their Founders in the last moments before their rebirth into undeath.
