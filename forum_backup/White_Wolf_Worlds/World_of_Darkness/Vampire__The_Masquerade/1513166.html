                  Originally posted by CajunKhan View Post

                  It's going to be somewhat dependent on how you do things. You can ghoul a mortal without blood bonding them by not having them drink from the vein. Ghouling isn't in and of itself a stain, but bonding is (V5 core pg. 240). And ghouls aren't slaves unless you blood bond them; you could have a positive relationship with the ghoul without the staining. It'd be somewhat hard to keep up with, but you could do so.

                          I think this is comes down to the perspective
                          If you not bound them - tho even that could be worked around with clever RP - you not really enslaving them
                          Just always give them the option to chose
                          They will always chose you, and not because of blood or some faustian deals... in a Hmanistic tenets oriented game even as a monster your goal is not to be one
                          The ghouls works for you if you not bound it - and truth to be told, an average Joe spends his meaningless life working to a company in a soulcrushing job witch is way worse
                          In this case WoD 'atmosphere" eg. Darkness with a capital D works on your favor - but the sad reality is, even IRL our jobs where we toil our life away for minimum wage (or better) is way worse than working to a vampire who' only asks to manage her daily affairs while sleeps
                          Look deep in to your soul and answer me: wouldn't you chose the vampire and be a part something bigger and have the perks?
                          Or anyone you know?
                          Would you regret it?
                          In the grand scheme of things, you give some extra meaning to the life of that ghoul - sometimes giving them the gift of "immortality" - even a few years of prolonged life - is meaning so much to them, you can't possibly comprehend
                          When my grandfather asked every year from my grandmother what she wants for her birthday, she always answered "one more year with you"
                          Of course you don't have to use elders as ghouls but I think you can see my point

                          Thats why it is important to chose your ghoul carefully by the way

                          Remember, it all started with the Humanistic tenets that were picked, so the "they would see ghouls as disposable slaves and see it as more proof of how far you have fallen and how you have to justify your actions of having them around." not really a factor, because... you wont. Thats the whole point.
                          You simply wont
                          Ideally you talked through the chosen Tenets in your group, so that all players understand what they mean and what kind of actions would consist violations of these Tenets
                          So if they remain true to that, or at least try - and people tend to forget how important the fact, you at leats tried and not gave in to nihilism at the first second - keeping a ghouls wouldn't be an issue
                          And if not? Well you not really cared about those stains anway in the first place

                              I'd say that it's not just a factor of how you treat your ghouls. The horror of ghouldom is in the very nature of this condition.
