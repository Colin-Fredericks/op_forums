                    I think they have a little too much material on them.
                    And goddamn, I'm a guy who really loves them.

                    I would prefer if they got a "thats it, thats how it is" material, rather than a "hey, those ten other supplements werent the entire true, actually the truee coonspiracy is that the Salubri have yet ANOTHEERE FUCKING BLOODLINE and Saulot has its fingers in yet another pie".

                    Their concept is great. Martyrs, and ok, I accept having Warriors and Healers, it really drives that medieval feel they have (which is a shame that for most of Dark Ages they are... hunted, dead). I love Saulot's ambiguity, which yeah, can be quite a boring cobweb sometimes. The whole third eye... I think it makes people want to play them way too much just because of it. I wished it was a little more specific or situational, like the Banu Haqim darkening (although the Haquimites case for me is pretty darn stupid, and I'd rather have them have their skin losing color not for age, but because of diablerie; they're judges, it would make sense for them to show their 'true colors', and for the Path of Blood fellows would be a mark of dignity).

                    The mindset Salubri have to have is pretty cool, if we are following a good willed one. Because surprise, you have to eat blood from people, hurt and drain them to continue. So... "I'm hurting you so that I may continue to help you". A beast I am, lest a Beast I become. This way, even if you are playing a nice vampire it is still messed up. And there is so much room for villainy, hypocrisy, fanaticism, madness, despair, hate and anti-heroism in Salubris.


                    In the end, they have tons of setting problems. Not only by playing one you are automatic in a hit list, but you weren't even supposed to be alive.
                    Also, they always felt like the classic "I'm different, I'm special, I'm awesome" option, the magic dark elf with a hood from a massacred clan with unique Mary Sueisms. And you can't forget they are supposed to be rare and always hunted, so they don't even feel playable. They look awesome for a book, a novel, not a playable setting.

                    Strange... When coincidence seems too convenient, I prefer to call it fate.

                    -Blood Omen: Legacy of Kain d=

                                My favorite clan, and they're as interesting as any clan with the same amount of development would be. That is, they depend a lot on you liking the basic concept because they're one of many Bloodlines that have been around for a long time but got a fraction of the page count of the main clans. If you only count Healers, they're more fleshed out than the Daughters of Cacophony and less than the Serpents of Light.

                                For the metaplot, as far as it goes I think they're overused as plot devices instead of main characters, which is a big part of the aforementioned problem with little depiction. Saulot is a fleshed out character, almost, actually one of the better fleshed-out Antediluvian characters alongside with Cappadocius, for greater irony, but other than that they've been basically plot devices.

                                That's to say that their lack of impression, especially when it comes to Metaplot, is less their fault (as in them being a boring idea) and more authorial decision (and not necessarily a bad or good decision, it just is). At the end of the day, for the purposes of the game, they are indeed a Bloodline and not a clan, meant more as options for distinct characters than as an actual main organization.
