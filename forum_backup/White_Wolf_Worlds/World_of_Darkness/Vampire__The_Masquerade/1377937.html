    04-02-2020, 09:08 AM
    People say Whitewolf is canning Kueijin. I mean I don't want them retconned, if they did they would need to rewrite the whole regions history.

    But one possibility I see them doing is have the Wan Gui go through similar Changes as the Cainites, ultimately ending up making their character Creation closer to that of the Kindred, even to the point of having their Disciplines kind of merge with the Cainite Discipline options.

      Setting aside 'The Kuei-Jin Question' which a lot of good digital ink has already been spilled over and some of my contemporary thoughts are along with the most recent of those threads, but that aside, I'll just suffice to say that I think the Kuei-Jin are fine but problematic when they replace White Wolf's traditional vampiric slot with themselves.

      Honestly, I think Asia would be best handed by having its own vampiric sect(s) appropriate to local cultures and leadership styles alongside an existing population of Kuei-Jin and vampiric style bygones (but the latter is more the domain of discussion for other gamelines).

      In my own headcanon and home games I've always considered Asian Vampires to fall under an umbrella term of 'Black Courts' which represent a federation formed from a variety of pre-existing dynasties, courts, and organizations which drew together under a common banner in response to the existential threat of Camarilla, Sabbat, and the Anarch Movement during European exploration, colonization, and imperialism.

      Throughout most of its vampiric history, I'd say it's likely that Asia witnessed a situation similar to dark ages Europe (Courts of Love, Baronies of Avalon, Voivodate, etc) with significant regional powers eventually forming as a reflection of mortal geo-politics but following a mideveal paradigm of tradition and law until they were forced to modernize somewhat with the creation of the Black Courts (or whatever you wanna call it, I have a friend who uses 'Scarlet Court' instead).

      Regarding clans, I'd keep the primary 13 and scatter them as geographically appropriate as well as exercise the right to create bloodlines in order to provide for specific and regional vampiric lore to be represented; in the great White Wolf fashion of having nearly every splat represent most everything in some form or another.

      I'm also perfectly happy to explore alternative folklore, philosophies, mythologies, and theologies for the vampires of the continent and their purpose and origins. I've always been a fan of creating a vagueness about the past and vampiric origins and this is a prime time for it. I don't know enough about the vast spectrum of Asian religions and philosophies to propose one at this moment but I'm sure that, to an interested body, there's plenty of inspiration to draw upon for a story to be made.

      Ultimately, that's how I take it. We have vampires (in the Cainite since) everywhere else, the exclusion of Asia is glaring and should be rectified. I'm happy to have them co-exist alongside Kuei-Jin, Drowned Legacies, Bygones, or any other vampiric representative for those uncomfortable with the Cainite Abrahamic Tradition, but for me personally, having kindred having always had a presence in Asia is a retcon/headcanon that makes sense and would be supremally useful for writers, storytellers, and players alike.
