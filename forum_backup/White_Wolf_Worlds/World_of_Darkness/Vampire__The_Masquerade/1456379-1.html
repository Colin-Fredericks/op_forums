    In re a Wight "reclaiming" their sanity and returning to (any) Path:

    It theoretically could be done. However, the only vampires justifiably capable of it were given the "penny-flavored Capri Sun" treatment in the 1100's by the Tremere.

    In terms of controlling a Wight, it's another story entirely and one wouldn't actually need the blood bond to do it. If you're an elder with Animalism, you're in luck since certain elder-level powers are capable of controlling other vampires' Beasts. Which is the perfect segue into...

    In re Antediluvians and the Beast:

    Starting with Animalism 7, the vampire starts to gain specific control over their own Beasts, being able to enter and leave Frenzy at will. With Animalism 9, the methuselah basically gets the bonuses of Frenzy permanently without any of the drawbacks, chiefly preserving their higher function and morality. Those two powers predictably begin to blur the line between vampire and Beast, really raising the question of whether morality really applies to them in terms of actually falling to Wassail, and what the implications of that are.

    And Antediluvians get Animalism ten.

                Using wights as shock troops is risky as hell - I'm reminded of Marauders using Bygones. Is dropping a Dragon on a Chantry or Lab effective? Yes. Yes it is. Surviving the consequences is the hard part.

                A blood bound wight on a figurative "leash" could be handy, but equating it to a pet dog might be a misstep. A dog has been bred for domesticity and submission over many, many generations. Instead picture a powerful roided out wolf on a leash, one who happens to like you a lot but is not even feral, let alone tame.

                That said, Dominate does work on frenzying vampires, so it would work on Wights. It's just +2 difficulty to activate, and failure could be more severe. Using Dominate 2, 3 and 4 to completely reshape a wight's "mind" and "memories" so that you're its "father" or "mother" could be interesting.

                        Wights seem usable, to a degree. If they're not hungry, afraid or angry then wights don't care about much. If it doesn't consider you a danger, and considers you more beneficial to it alive than dead, the wight is not inherently disposed to harm you... And as I type this I realise that, in other words, they're vampires. (disclaimer: wight passivity highly situational, safety not guaranteed)
                        They're also capable of complex thought, as shown by their capacity to understand language. So it might be possible to make an "agreement" with a wight and get it to do things for you, possibly even follow complex commands. It will just go horribly, horribly wrong somewhere down the line.
                        It might be possible, through time, to condition them towards manipulation and domination.

                        A blood bond might make social rolls possible, and unless I'm imagining things, dominate is easier to use on a thrall anyway.
                        Wonder if a dementation + blood bond combo would work. Use Eyes of Chaos to track down a wight with an appropriate nature (child, conformist, caretaker), blood bond it so that it "cares" about you which should hopefully make it more susceptible to social rolls, and then psychically drug it into mind-numbing apathy until such a time that you need to pull the trigger and detonate that bomb.

                        More than attack dogs, it'd be an idea to domesticate them for research as they make fascinating areas of study.
                        What happens when a wight commits diablerie on a vampire with a path rating? Does the victim become dominant, becoming the typical hybrid of diablerist and victim, with the victim's path rating? Effectively returning the wight to "sanity"? Or does the wight automatically win, gaining a whole new set of memories and knowledge it doesn't give a damn about (but may be able to exploit in some fashion)?

                        As animalism affects the beast directly, can wights be communicated with using feral whispers?

                        I'd love to get some kind of magical brain-scanner and observe their "brain activity".
