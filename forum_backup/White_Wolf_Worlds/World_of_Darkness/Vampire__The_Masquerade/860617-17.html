    260. The Goblin King

    That's what he calls himself these nights. If he even remembers what his real name is, he's refusing to divulge it to even his closest allies. The only clue we have as to his original identity, is that he was embraced somewhere between the ages of 10 and 12 years old, by a rather sadistic Nosferatu. For reasons that should be blatantly obvious, he didn't have a snowball's chance in hell of being accepted by the Camarilla, driving him to fall in with the Sabbat crowd instead. His sire's pack mocked him, belittled him, and basically made his unlife a constant nightmare; all under the pretense of trying to 'toughen him up' so he could be a proper vampire.

    Well, it worked... perhaps a little too well, because the Goblin King got his revenge. When a raid on enemy territory went bad, as is so often the case with haphazardly assembled Sabbat strategies, the abused fledgling seized the opportunity to diablerize his sire, stealing all of their power for himself. He would have loved to do the same to the rest of the pack, but the situation was growing increasingly more dangerous by the moment, so he was forced to bail and leave them to the Sheriff's men.


    While Nosferatu typically have a reputation for being tech savvy, the Goblin King is the opposite. He was far too young during his embrace to have any practical knowledge of computer and electrical systems, but destroying them is something that comes easily to him. In the time since his embrace, the Goblin King has become something of a domestic terrorist, focusing his attention on bringing down power grids, disrupting construction efforts, instigating wild animal attacks, and a host of other 'mysterious accidents' that keep happening throughout the city.


    Appearance: A child sized monstrosity, who's posture remains hunched low to the ground. Instead of walking upright like a normal person, he lopes and skitters across the ground like a feral rodent. His skin has taken on a putrid yellow hue, while his ears are huge and tapered like those of a bat. His most outstanding feature is his nose, which is both comically long and abnormally thin. Hooking down over his mouth like some kind of withered carrot.


    Roleplaying Tips: You are an agent of misfortune. Like the mythical creature from whence draw your namesake, you live to cause chaos. Your favorite method of spreading chaos involves ruining somebody's day without them even realizing you were present at the scene. In doing so, your reputation takes on a more mythical quality to it; you are the invisible monster spoken about in medieval fairy tales. The kind who is always to blame when something goes wrong, but never caught so you could face actual justice.

    Animalism is your primary discipline, as the power to control the city's animal population further solidifies your kingly status. Obfuscate comes second, as it assists you in evading capture or detection, while Potence remains your emergency last resort option.


    Clan: Nosferatu

    Sire: Unknown (destroyed)


    Nature: Child

    Demeanor: Eye of the Storm

    Generation: 11th (formerly 12th)

    Embrace: 1999

    Apparent Age: 12

        261. Hermanus di Celano, the bookbinder
        Hermanus was the product of a marriage between a Giovanni maiden and the son of a Bavarian merchant family. The third child to be born of this arrangement, somewhere near the start of the 13th century. After a childhood spent in Munich, the pious youth left for an Italian monastery. No more than a year later Hermanus took his vows.
        When not in prayer the young monk spent most of his time in the scriptorium, copying books. A task he was neither the best nor the worst at. Certainly someone they could miss when the local lord asked the monks to be provided with a new scribe. Although he showed only obedience, Hermanus was anxious about this new assignment. It was the first time he'd left the monastery in years. The world outside its walls felt alien to him.
        He was quickly put at ease though. The castle and its inhabitants were quite welcoming. The lord's young wife proved to be especially affable. They soon became good friends. Lady Liviana had a warm personality and appreciated a keen mind to converse with. She found her often absent husband to be quite boorish. It soon became apparent his mistress was interested in more than just intelligent conversation though. Hermanus tried his hardest to hold on to his vow of chastity. But his thus far sheltered existence meant he had few tools to resist her feminine wiles. The start of a romance that would last for years.
        In time the entire household knew about the affaire, except for the lord himself. He spent too much time in other beds to know what was happening in his own. And yet one night the lord stormed into his chambers, sword in hand, catching the two lovers in the act. The last thing Hermanus saw before being run through with the blade was Liviana's head rolling across the floor.
        Hermanus who had expected to find himself in Hell, instead awoke in an underground crypt. Ermacora, a grandfather believed to be long dead, was watching over him. He informed the fledgling Cappadocian about the exact nature of this afterlife, and the Giovanni family's place in it. Augustus had recently decided to hold off on Hermanus' Embrace, until his untimely death had forced Ermacora's hand. The new Cainite let out a wail of great pain after being told saving Liviana hadn't been possible. Hermanus drank with great satisfaction from the bound lord, who was sobbing and begging, presented to him next.
        Soon after sire and childe left for Venice, where Hermanus would reside most of the following centuries. He proved to be an especially avid student of Necromancy, the art that could reunite him with his love. After never reaching that goal Hermanus in time lost all interest in the lands of the dead. However, not before accidentally developing a Necromantic ritual that proved of great value to the Clan. This rite permanently bound a soul to a book so it could act as its guardian. This involved binding the tome in the skin of the ghost's former body. In time many deceased mortal Giovanni have received this honor. More often than not by Hermanus himself.
        He has since mostly retreated from the affairs of his Clan. To them he's known as the mysterious bookbinder of Celano. Having made his haven under the ruins of the castle where he was once so happy. His only companion is Alda, a woman Embraced for her superficial physical resemblance to Liviana. She is neither the first nor the last to which he has done this. But the only one he hasn't destroyed soon afterwards.

        Image: Over the centuries Hermanus skin has grown gray and rough, giving him a monstrous countenance. His dark soulful eyes are the only human feature left of the vampire's face. Despite his bookish ways Hermanus had grown tall and broad-shouldered in life. The robes and cloaks he commonly wears only increase his apparent bulk. Beneath which the vampire hides the sword wound through his belly and back. Another souvenir from his mortal years is the tonsure shaven out of his brown hair's top.

        Roleplaying Hints: You've never enjoyed your unlife. It has been an existence of constant emotional and physical pain. And yet in your own detached way you perform your duty to your Clan and family admirably. It has provided you with a ghostly connection to nearly any Giovanni library in the world. Once your Clan's hold over the Underworld grows powerful enough to retrieve your lost love, you'll know about it and they'll repay their debts to you.
        You generally act somber and aloof. Only certain women can get a rise out of you. Passion cause they remind you of Liviana, often followed by fury once the brief illusion again dissipates.

        Name: Hermanus di Celano
        Clan: Giovanni (Premascine)
        Sire: Ermacora Giovanni
        Nature: Celebrant
        Demeanor: Loner
        Generation: 6th
        Embrace: 1229
        Apparent Age: Indeterminate
        Physical: Strength 4, Dexterity 3, Stamina 5
        Social: Charisma 4, Manipulation 5, Appearance 0
        Mental: Perception 5, Intelligence 4, Wits 3
        Talents: Alertness 3, Athletics 2, Awareness 4, Brawl 2, Empathy 2, Expression 3, Intimidation 3, Leadership 1, Subterfuge 4
        Skills: Animal Ken 1, Crafts (bookbinding) 5, Etiquette 3, Larceny 1, Melee 2, Stealth 3, Survival 2
        Knowledges: Academics 3, Enigmas 3, Finance 1, Investigation 4, Law 1, Medicine 2, Occult 4, Politics 1, Science 2
        Disciplines: Auspex 6, Dominate 2, Fortitude 2, Necromancy 5, Obfuscate 3, Potence 2, Presence 1
        Necromancy Paths: The Ash Path 2, The Cenotaph Path 5, The Sepulchre Path 4
        Backgrounds: Domain 2, Generation 7, Spirit Slaves 5, Status 3
        Virtues: Conscience 3, Self-Control 1, Courage 5
        Morality: Humanity 4
        Willpower: 5
        Merits/Flaws: Language (German, Latin + mother tongue Italian), Open Wound, Sanguine Incongruity

            Pre-gens I wrote for my upcoming wild west Giovanni game:

            Ennio Putanesca
            As a boy you immigrated from Sicily to New York with your parents, and their undead master. Together they were supposed to build up a powerbase, for Clan Giovanni, among the city's burgeoning Italian community. Sadly the local Sabbat didn't take kindly to these efforts, killing your father a few years later. Subsequently you took his place, as a ghoul, at the Giovanni vampire's side. You turned out to be more business savvy, and all around shrewd, than your father had been. Your family managed to grow influential in the criminal underworld without antagonizing the city's ruling Cainites. A success for which you were rewarded with the Embrace. You're now assisted by your own trusty ghouls; the brothers Giorgio, Giuseppe and Guido.

            Image: A handsome Mediterranean man who was about 20 years old when becoming a ghoul. Except for a moustache your face is cleanshaven. A dark suit is your preferred attire. You usually also wear a red neckerchief.

            Roleplaying Hints: You come across as a shrewd businessman. A true professional who's determined to build Clan Giovanni an American crime empire. Your heart isn't really in it though. Cause after all these years you're still haunted by your father's untimely demise. You want better for yourself. First and foremost you want to survive!


            Nicolasa Cortés y Rossellini
            You grew up on a hacienda in the Central Mexican countryside. The Cortés branch of the Pisanob family had fallen on hard times. There were no servants to work the land anymore. Instead it was you, and your siblings, who spent their formative years toiling all day. And yet you still found the energy to study Pisanob Necromancy deep into the night. Your efforts did not go unnoticed. A vampire, a great-grandfather on your mother's side, took you as his ghoul. A thankless existence assisting, and protecting, the Italian leech as he travelled the Americas. Though after years of loyal service this did eventually earn you the Embrace.

            Image: An attractive woman, of mixed Aztec-Italian ancestry, in her early 20s. You're dressed for travel and action, like a bandida. This consists out of a simple blouse and ankle-length skirt, usually so dusty that you can't see the dried blood. Upon your belt you keep a revolver holstered. Your dark hair, on which rest a wide-brimmed hat, is tied in a single braid, hanging down your back.

            Roleplaying Hints: Eternally hard working and dutiful, with plain manners. No one appears to be more loyal to Clan Giovanni, and its Italian elders, than you. In truth you're just working inside the hierarchy hoping to destroy it. Your people should be their own masters, not beholden to European monsters.


            Margaret Dunsirn
            Born and raised on a family estate outside of Glasgow, Scotland. You first realized your kin were different when seeing Mother carve up a lazy maid's body in the kitchen. Besides learning about Dunsirn cannibalistic traditions, in time you were also informed about their links with Clan Giovanni. You were immediately enthralled by the possibility of becoming a vampire yourself. However, the Giovanni prized your family for little else except their talents at generating wealth. And unfortunately women couldn't legally own any property of their own. The exception would be a wealthy widow, which you made sure to become. After your husband's untimely demise you simply used his fortune to buy the Embrace from a lowly Giovanni vampire. You've since emigrated to the land of opportunity, hoping to make your own mark.

            Image: A fair emerald-eyed lady, appearing to be around 30. You let your ginger curls, held together by a bow, cascade down your back. No longer do you adhere to the fashions from Europe or the Eastern Seaboard. You instead dress as a frontier town's woman, in dresses that are plain, yet neat.

            Roleplaying Hints: The belief that the strong should consume the weak is deeply ingrained within you. Hence your continued striving to be the woman on top. Though you understand how off putting this can be within the misogynist society you're forced to operate in. So your level of ambition generally reads as appropriate for a good Christian widow who's just trying to make do.


            Demetra Giovanni
            A child of Venice. You'd been told you're the cumulation of centuries of fine Giovanni breeding. Unfortunately that came with weak health. In your early teens you became infected with consumption, wasting away at a rapid pace. An undead grandfather stepped in with the Embrace, so that your potential wouldn't be lost. He didn't have permission from the family to do that though. For his crime you were banished to the New World. There you came under the care of Sister Virginia Maria, an aunt who'd been similarly disgraced.

            Image: A little young lady with dark eyes. Your pale features are stunningly perfect, like a porcelain doll. An innocent face framed by black curls, over which you usually wear a bonnet, tied under your chin with a bow. You wear a matching dress with hoopskirts underneath.

            Roleplaying Hints: You've been hearing the dead whisper to you since birth, and yet you're still constantly overwhelmed by the supernatural world you were thrust into. You're more of a spoiled brat than an innocent child though. Cause the penitence you show, for your unsanctioned Embrace, is just a front. In truth you believe yourself to be a Giovanni of the highest caliber. You just need some help getting used to this undead existence.


            Gabriella Rosselini
            You grew up on a family estate back in Italy. From a young age it was clear you were a brilliant girl, who grew immensely knowledgeable in the dark arts. Unfortunately you weren't a pureblooded Rosselini, but a mere mortal destined to wither and die in just a handful of decades. Even as a young woman you feared this ticking clock, craving the eternal youth of the undead. Finally it was decided an elder from the Giovanni family's main branch was to Embrace you. They told you this was quite the honor. After the initial excruciating transformation you agreed with them. But by the next night it became clear something had gone wrong. You started to smell, your skin became discolored. You were decomposing! An irreversible process that continued for several more nights. Your eventual appearance made other Giovanni, kine and Kindred alike, uncomfortable. Which is why they shipped you off to America.

            Image: A cadaverous looking lady, like a week old corpse. Your milky, and glazed over, brown eyes are the least of it. Cause the outline of your skull is visibly protruding from beneath gray skin. This you usually obscure by dressing like a lady in deep mourning. Most importantly by having thick veils draped over your bonnet. Unlike a grieving widow you make heavy use of perfume, as to hide the smell of rot.

            Roleplaying Hints: You present yourself as a font of knowledge totally devoted to your Clan. In truth you hate the Giovanni with a passion. It is they who subjugated your family, their cursed blood who afflicted you so terribly. As the Giovanni usurped the Cappadocians, you hope the Rosselini can do the same to Augustus and his get. You play nice with them only to help make this dream a reality. That one of your new coterie mates has a Rosselini mother seems like a possible opportunity.


            Sister Virginia Maria
            You grew up in Venice, near your family's undead leadership. A legacy you desired no part in, turning to Catholicism instead. Unfortunately you were the most beautiful Giovanni of your generation, a young woman with many admirers. One of your cousins seemed particularly smitten. You took holy vows attempting to finally escape his advances. He eventually followed you to the convent though, more insistent than ever. In the subsequent altercation you stabbed him to death, damning your soul. As your punishment, because Clan Giovanni had earmarked him for the Embrace, you had to take his place in undeath. Furthermore you were banished to the American continent, settling in New England. More recently you were entrusted with the care of Demetra Giovanni, your niece, a child vampire.

            Image: You're always clad in the black and white garb of a Benedictine nun. This habit, veil and wimple cover nearly every inch of you except your face. But what an exquisite face it is; pale perfection further adorned by soft brown eyes. Your delicate hands are nearly constantly grasping at the silver rosary hanging down your chest.

            Roleplaying Hints: You feel ashamed for your actions and the creature you've become. For these many sins you wish to suffer like the lord Gesù Cristo on the cross. But you have a duty to your family to take care of little Demetra Giovanni. So you do your very best to hide your self-hate behind a motherly veneer.​.

                268. Mocel, Mesa Trueno's mistress
                A Tlacique who's claimed a mining town, far north of her bloodline's ancestral lands, as her personal domain. Other Kindred tend to assume she's a Gangrel. An impression she does nothing to dissuade. Though Mocel doesn't confirm this either, fearing how Outlanders would respond to such a deception. Cause in truth she's all on her own now.
                During the early 1800s Mocel grew up in the mountains of Central Mexico, belonging to an isolated community that had managed to defy the Church's conversion efforts. They still believed in the Aztec gods, powerful beings of darkness and blood. These weren't like the invisible Christian god, cause Mocel's kin had all seen these deities with their own eyes. Eventually, as a young woman, came the night she too met one of her gods. Mocel had broken a taboo, been careless lingering at a sacred shrine past dusk. Once the Tlacique emerged from the earth the girl's life was forfeit. They immediately pounced upon her like jaguars, taking all Mocel's blood as a sacrifice. However, to the young woman's surprise, she rose again. Those she'd once thought of as gods had made her one of their own.
                The fledgling soon discovered that her undead condition was no great honor. The Tlacique were in a war for their survival against the Sabbat. Mocel simply owed her Embrace, instead of being left an empty vessel, to the bloodline's great need for more foot soldiers. Unfortunately the brood had underestimated the betrayal their newest recruit felt after learning her people's gods were nothing but blood drinking parasites. So rather than fighting for them she fled at the first opportunity, mere nights after her Embrace.
                Mocel's flight brought her to the Mexican Republic's northern reaches, specifically the lands of the Akimel Oʼodham tribe. The Tlacique made an abandoned Spanish mission into her haven, at a place called Mesa Trueno. It wasn't a pleasant unlife, with only coyotes and rattlers as company. But at the time she was laying low, fearing the others were looking for her.
                No other Kindred came for her. Though eventually the Church sent nuns to reclaim the mission, forcing Mocel to trade in her haven for a nearby cave. Primitive surroundings the young Tlacique soon found an alternative for, as a mining camp formed in Mesa Trueno. While men dug the surrounding hills for gold, Mocel moved into the newly established brothel. There the vampire could hide in plain sight, feeding off sex workers and patrons alike.
                As more years passed the camp became a town, now part of the New Mexico Territory. One of the miners, a Frenchman named Henri Corbeau, had hit it big. A rich vein, in the mountain overlooking Mesa Trueno, made him a rich man overnight, who bought up all the other nearby claims, or sent outlaws to deal with anyone unwilling to sell. Though meanwhile he kept patronizing the local brothel. So as Corbeau used his newfound wealth to bring Mesa Trueno under his control, Mocel took the opportunity to enthrall the man.
                Under the guise of being Henri Corbeau's mistress the vampire became the true power in Mesa Trueno. She moved her haven yet again, to hidden quarters in the bowels of the massive mansion Corbeau built overlooking the town. Through secret corridors she had the run of the entire property. There Mocel dwells still. Though after her thrall's recent demise, during a devastating quake, her grasp on the domain's become tenuous.

                Image: A slender beauty of indigenous Mesoamerican extraction. Mocel's ebon curls fall halfway down her back. Around her neck she a wears gold pendant, set with a ruby. A gift from Henri Corbeau. Her overall attire is quite expensive, but considered too risqué for a lady. Because by 1863's standards Mocel's gowns are overly colorful, show too much cleavage, than what is thought proper. The image of a high-end sex worker is exactly what she's cultivating. This Tlacique now spends much of her time in the shape of a black jaguar though.

                Roleplaying Hints: Shook out of your complacency since Corbeau's death. You had learned to appreciate the finer things in unlife. And will now not shy away from violence to defend your privileged existence. Though when possible you prefer to rely on your feminine, and vampiric, wiles. While in dealing with Kindred machinations you're clearly out of your depth, from lack of experience.

                Clan: Tlacique
                Sire: Acatl
                Nature: Bon Vivant
                Demeanor: Gallant
                Generation: 10th
                Embrace: 1832
                Apparent Age: 22

                Social: Charisma 3, Manipulation 3, Appearance 4

                Talents: Alertness 2, Athletics 1, Awareness 1, Brawl 1, Empathy 1, Intimidation 2, Leadership 1, Streetwise 2, Subterfuge 2
                Skills: Animal Ken 1, Etiquette 2, Melee 2, Ride 1, Stealth 2, Survival 3

                    I'm starting a new Victorian Age Vampire/Wild West storyline soon. These are the sires I wrote for my PCs:


                    269. Evica Mumir
                    You grew up on the Serbian countryside, the daughter of peasants. This was around the turn of the century, when the land was still part of the Ottoman Empire. You fell in love with one of the Turkish soldiers garrisoned near your home. Against your parents wishes you converted to Islam and married the man. Tragically he was killed shortly after, in the Serbian Uprising against the Ottomans. You attempted to follow him, hanging yourself from a tree. However, an ancient Gangrel resurrected you with its blood. The elder had felt your pain, centuries earlier losing its mate to the vile Necromancers. You were to become the instrument of its vengeance, which eventually brought you across the ocean.

                    Image: A brown-eyed, plain looking, young woman, wearing simple working class dresses. You keep your hair hidden beneath a bonnet, which you never untie in public. Not just for religious reasons, but because this hides your lupine ears. Your beast shapes are those of a pale wolf and a white butterfly.

                    Roleplaying Hints: You appear quite meek and softspoken. And yet most kine find you deeply unsettling because of your predatory stare. You try to be a good Muslimah to the extent the curse allows, hoping that when you're finally reduced to ashes you'll be reunited with your husband. Though you're almost equally determined to fulfill the quest bestowed upon you by your sire.


                    270. Wim Beceu
                    You were born and reared, in rural Flanders, during the 1790s. The son of a country doctor, studying to follow in your father's footsteps. Until you chose to fight at Waterloo, on Napoleon's side. You fell in battle, but was raised again as a Brujah vampire. The lady who'd done so discarded you soon after. So you returned to the village of your birth, though not to your family. Instead you hid from the sun in barns, or submersed in ditches. An elusive presence who repaid the blood you took, from sleeping peasants, by completing their chores. Your existence became folklore, believed to be a ghost or fae creature. Until years onward a local priest was implored to drive you off. You left Flanders, feeling betrayed, stowed away on a ship to the United States. There you've since been eking out an unlife making yourself useful to the East Coast Camarilla. Currently by tracking down a diablerist Giovanni who escaped into the western frontier.

                    Image: A plain looking man, in your mid-twenties, with a face covered in blond stubble. Your brown eyes radiate a quiet anger or frustration. You care little about how you look, wearing the practical clothing of a field laborer. Though beneath that unimpressive garb you have an athletic build.

                    Roleplaying Hints: You try to be a good man, following the example of your father. But the shame you once felt, for being an undead leech, has long since been replaced by something darker. You despise how humanity has rejected your intended kindness time and again. You seek to follow the example of more successful vampires; using the kine as pawns. Though at the moment you're still visibly struggling with your upbringing.


                    271. Mrs Yejide
                    You originally hail from Central Africa. There you lived alone, in the jungle, cause your people suspected you of being a witch. In truth you never cursed anyone, but had learned much about such practices from your mother. Your sire's thoughts weren't even know to himself. But you assume he Embraced you to preserve all this knowledge. Once your mind was caught in the Cobweb you learned of the world's end. Though also how the demise of all things might be delayed. On impulse you crossed the ocean to America, hidden aboard a slavers' ship. The subsequent decades you survived travelling between Kindred domains along the East Coast, attempting to discover what had brought you to this land. Mere weeks ago you finally found your purpose here, when encountering a coterie heading west, to the New Mexico Territory. It's there you know you can make a difference.

                    Image: An African woman who was about forty at the time of her Embrace. However, across the sea you've chosen to walk in the guise of a white woman much of the time. A matter of survival, even for the undead, in a land where people of your race are often horribly mistreated. So your eyes appear blue, your skin light, though your dark curls remain the same. These are often covered by a bonnet, cause you complete your disguise by dressing as an American middle-class lady.

                    Roleplaying Hints: Quiet and reserved, choosing to be an observer of the chaos around you. Staying out of the affairs of others, kine and Kindred alike, would be your preference. Self preservation is your only interest in postponing the end of the world. When you do speak it is slow and deliberate, hoping to hide any traces of an African accent.


                    272. Charles Moore IV, Adeptus Secundus
                    You're the son of a smalltown lawyer, from New England, who followed in his father's footsteps. The difference being an adventurous spirit. When the California goldrush erupted you shipped out to practice law in San Francisco. This boomtown, which had people from all across the globe arriving, was an ideal place to pursue your passion for ethnology. You grew absolutely fascinated by the many Chinese conceptions of the afterlife. Eventually you realized there was a truth to them, after witnessing some unsettling phenomena. This supernatural expertise, coupled with your growing influence in local politics, caught the Tremere's attention. Once your health started failing a visiting Witch wasted no time administering the Embrace. In the decade since you've yet to truly prove your worth to the Clan. Perhaps this latest fact finding mission, into the western frontier, is your opportunity to shine?

                    Image: A handsome middle-aged gentleman, with a neatly trimmed brownish beard and moustache. Sad eyes, azure in color, peer out from behind wire rim glasses. Your tailored suit does little to hide how emaciated your body is. A top hat only adds to how lanky you appear.

                    Roleplaying Hints: You go through unlife as if it's a painfully unpleasant duty you're fulfilling. Cause your existence is no longer your own, belonging to the Pyramid. Though truthfully you do savor any time your knowledge is of use to Clan Tremere, glad to make up for your overall ineffectivity at studying Thaumaturgy.
