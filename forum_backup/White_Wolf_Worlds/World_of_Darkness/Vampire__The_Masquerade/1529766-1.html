    Children of Blood #4: Landon Dankworth AKA "Father Christian Patriot"



    Type: NPC

    Summary: A Thin Blood radio host who is everything wrong with America or the guy telling you everything wrong with America. He is also ruler of his own town.

    Analysis: I love Father Christian Patriot more than most of Matthew Dawkins' creations because I hate Landon more than any other character in Vampire: The Masquerade. That include Balthazar, Lodin, Modius, and many other classic villains of the setting. I may have gotten way more use out of them than others but I've inserted the character's radio shows in the background of numerous games (they're set in Tampa, Florida after all) as well as done a decent couple of adventures about shutting him down.

    He fills that "You sit in an O'Tolley's dining room" or "you fill up your gas at an Endron gas station" role that helps flesh out the setting. My players share a collective Rush Limbaugh and Alex Jones trauma that makes it so none of us want to hear THEM in the background of stories but can grit their teeth at the Father Christian Patriot show. He's the perfect sort of character to also comment on the local occult news in an annoying way. When the players finally investigated his hometown, it was a culimination of numerous appearances in the background.

    The book notably has a sidebar:

    PLAYING WITH PREJUDICE

    A character such as Father Christian Patriot can be
    played up or down to the extent the players of your
    Vampire: The Masquerade game enjoy. Bear in mind
    that when invoking characters such as he, humor is
    one natural outcropping, as many people find comfort
    in mocking such bigotry, while hatred or upset
    is another. His views, and the views of many of his
    followers, are abhorrent. Therefore, it is vital for the
    Storyteller to check with the players before utilizing
    a character like this to that extent. If the players and
    Storyteller don’t want an antagonist like Dankworth
    spewing his prejudicial bile, then he can focus entirely
    on his misguided pride, be adjusted to suit the
    story, or be replaced by a different antagonist.
    I think this is good advice and I admit whenever I do my snippets of the Father Christian Patriot show, I tend to be a lot more circumspect than I would when referring to real people. The Hollywood elites, West Coast liberals, and THEM (you know who THEM are). As part of the "humor", I also have him liberally mention vampires because his audience assumes he's speaking in code. I can't do anything about the real Alex Jones but we can certainly do something about Landon Dankworth, the discount Florida version.

    Adventure Hook: I actually combined Landon Dankworth with Out for Blood the interactive novel that also has a radio show run by a Kindred in a small town. In my combination universe, Landon is the childe of Amanda Chastain who is the one funding his growing Right Wing talk show. It makes an interesting twist to have him be the puppet of someone who looks like Ms. Orange County 1957.

                Children of the Blood part 5: Gloria "Gums" Flores



                Type: NPC

                Summary: A LA beautiful beautician who has been Embraced by the Nosferatu and now wants to join the Nictuku.

                Analysis: Cleopatras are one of the darker concepts of the Nosferatu and we have them as people who are usually depicted as the vain, corrupt, or self-absorbed being punished in a kind of Twilight Zone-esque way. Here, Gloria is just a fairly decent woman from all accounts who happened to succeed with her cosmetics business before being horribly turned by a careless sire.

                Gloria has been understandably devastated by this horrifying action but it lacks the usual "community" of the Nosferatu where the Embraced person is welcomed into their oddball community. Their old life may be over but they are now part of a much-stranger community. At least, that's how I normally see the Nosferatu going on. Here, she seems painfully isolated and is now looking for a way to get revenge on her entire clan.

                One of the more interesting plot threads in Forbidden Religions was the possible retcon about the Nictuku. We know from To Grandmother's House We Go that they were supposed to be real but they've never quite made much sense within the game world. There's supposedly one Nosferatu 4th generation that wasn't one of their ranks that escaped Absimilard's Blood Bonding and that's why he's hunting them all down. However, Baba Yaga is a 4th generation that was clearly up against dear old dad but doesn't seem to be the progenitor of all Nosferatu. Indeed, some Nosferatu believed she was a Nictuku.

                Now, this clearly only matters to lore junkies but the The Withered Ones cult is a bunch of Nosferatu who hunt down other members of their Clan in order to impress the Nictuku. However, what I felt was being implied by the supplement is that the Withered Ones might BE the Nictuku. They're a bunch of crazy cultists worshiping the Boogeyman and there is no such thing as the Nictuku. It's always been just manifestations of their self-loathing. Maybe the Nosferatu who killed Baba Yaga was just a rival or one or her child (or Absimilard himself).

                I will say I have a small disappointment with Gloria. This seemed a great opportunity to tie her to one of the BLOODLINES characters. Either Gary Golden or Imalia could have been her sire and it would have gave the character a bit more continuity with the LA Nosferatu community. I love those kind of connections, though. On the other hand, I like both characters even if Imalia is a bit on the evil side herself. Gloria's sire was particularly cruel and arbitrary even by Nosferatu standards.

                In any case, she's a great Nosferatu and one that I very much enjoy.

                                                        My interpretation of the Lasombra "Social Darwinist" philosophy is that it is there to avoid Embracing Jacon Schumpeters. If anyone remembers that character, Lodin Embraced him because he was worried he'd made rivals by Embracing Ballard and Al Capone who were becoming too powerful as well as independent. Lodin assumed he was Embracing an up and coming financial genius. When, in fact, Jacob Schumpeter was actually rich solely because he was insider trading with the help of his father in law (which was already a problem because Jacob was abusing his wife).

                                                        By taking away all of their mortal ties and wealth, the Lasombra wants to see if the Fledgling is capable of bouncing back on their own merit. Which is useful because you can't rely on inherited wealth or other benefits in Kindred society.

                                                        Also, much like the Sith, the Lasombra disdain loyalty. If you're NOT taking a shot at your sire then you've failed as a vampire.
