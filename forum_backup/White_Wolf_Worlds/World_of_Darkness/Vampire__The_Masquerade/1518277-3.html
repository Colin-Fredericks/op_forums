                        Originally posted by CajunKhan View Post
                        Depends on what the Wyrm IS, which is often contradictory. Sometimes the Wyrm is a very humanistic version of evil, which makes no sense in a world where male lions murder the children of rivals, and ducks constantly commit sexual assault, and are considered natural and of the Wyld. Sometimes it's all forces of decay, which means that everything from bacteria to vultures are of the Wyrm.
                        The Wyrm isn't really anything in a singular sense anymore. It's three fragments trying to understand what went wrong and succumbing to their vices in the meantime, with their emotions becoming Incarnae that seek to keep the whole from healing, else they would cease to exist. However, it can certainly twist a lesser being into another form; typically, this is as a result of a long series of actions wherein said being keeps digging itself in deeper, after some being aligned with the Wyrm notices it do something and decides to poke at it. Presumably, more beings don't respond than not; then, though, every so often, someone is caught up in self-loathing and obsession, making a perfect mark for a bane.

                        That's the Wyrm in aggregate - not evil, but a big fan of it. It's tainted and dirty; nothing loves it; it feels nothing but emptiness, and some part of it wants that all to end. It stopped caring about how a long time ago, or lost most of its whole agency in being split into three parts. The functional details don't really differ between those two outcomes. It's feeling around in the dark for a way out, always, from where it was bound. Not much direction from the top, there.

                        As for other points, some things to keep in mind

                        1) To emphasize, Gaia and Gaian spirits, what one would consider "natural" are not of the Wyld. They are at a balance point. Only Wyldlings are of the Wyld.

                        2) Beings at this point can end up doing an awful lot of things that are wrong, mistaken, and unpleasant for others. This doesn't automatically make them corrupt; as noted, it takes specific spiritual attention to do so. As such, a lot of the lack of corruption can be attributed to banes either not noticing what animals do or not caring because the bane is attuned to something else or they prefer to play mind games with humans. It could also have to do with, in W:tA, humanity's rejection of Gaia and the Wyld in favor of the Weaver, which can give banes an easy in on any given human.

                        3) Decay of organic matter into new soil is, to be quite honest, so conceptually and mechanically distant from the decay that the Wyrm really likes (of the living spirit) that it might as well be Gaian. As such, no, scavengers and detritivores aren't really aligned with the Wyrm in any sense that matters. The other form of decay, atomic, is elementally of the Wyrm and as such requires no attention from banes to register as being of the Wyrm, but it probably requires some hard, ionizing stuff, because the authors don't count the banana dose as significant enough to register to a Gift.
