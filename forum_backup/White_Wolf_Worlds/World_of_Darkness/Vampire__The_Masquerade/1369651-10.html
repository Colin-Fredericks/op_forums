                                I think the Wan Kuei should be in V5, especially since they are in BJD which has been the metaplot bridge between V20 and V5. I think they should be a different splat, but I also think there should be some sort of VtM style vampires existing in Wan Kuei territory. However I think that Kindred of the East and Demon the Fallen should be merged mechanically as they have many similarities.
                                • Similarities/Merging Mechanics - This is far from complete or exhaustive, more of just some of the things that crossed my mind
                                  • Both involve a demonic essence escaping an extraplanar prison, having a human frame, and dealing with some aspects of human mindset.
                                    • For Demon's the P'o represents their tormented side while the Hun their angelic
                                    • The "human" they are influences what nomenclature they use - a side effect of multilayered reality and incomplete memories of their time before their imprisonment
                                  • Both involve game-crossover
                                    • Demon tries to explain various WoD splats through itself, Wan Kuei interact with each of the various types (ghosts, hsien, spirits, etc)
                                  • The Archdukes can be compared with the Yama Kings
                                    • The Baali's Children of the Outer Darkness could be another group of such entities
                                  • There's tales of pacts with the devil signed in blood, what if that's simply mortal misunderstanding of low "Dharma" (more on that below) Demons feeding on blood while succubi and the like are misunderstanding of Demons that can feed on breath
                                    • With some tweaking Followers, Pacts, Thralls, and Herd could be merged into a one or two backgrounds
                                  • Coming up with some sort of storage method like Jade shouldn't be that difficult, whether it's retooling the concept of reliquary and/or using different types of gemstones to be equivalent to the different types of Jade.
                                  • I don't think Faith and Chi are irreconcilable. At the basic level Yin+Yang Chi is like Faith while Demon Chi is like Torment.
                                    • People showing signs of possession could be explained as mortals misunderstanding a "Yin" aspected Demon or suffering a "Chi" imbalance
                                  • Retooling Rites, Lores, Disciplines, and some of the inherent abilities into a single system just given different nomenclature.
                                    • Apocalyptic form isn't that different from demon shintai, and the lore of flame could be ghost-flame shintai as just a couple of examples
                                  • Both games have redemption among their themes Demon with trying to get rid of their torment and KotE having the road to enlightenment
                                    • The Houses are translated into Dharmas, representing the Demon's understanding of what their role should be.
                                      • If the idea of an Demon being able to choose a different House from what they were before they fell, just explain it as the influence of their human side. Demon made a big deal of the spark of divine within humanity, that spark allows a Demon to tweak their role
                                        • It could feed into the whole idea that it's all part of the divine plan - the angels made choices that caused them to fall now they have a choice to fulfill a duty; in the same way their human part has a second chance at life to fix what sin sent them to hell in the first place
                                        • Or it could be that their imperfect memories means they misremember what their role is or confuses it with a close ally, and that as long as they are attempting to fill a role as an angel it's a step towards redemption or that in falling they lost their role and part of their redemption involved finding one again
                                  • Earthbound and unrepentant/pure evil demons are equatable with akuma
                                    • Perhaps have some mechanic for akuma to bind themselves to a location, the Wan Kuei use of dragon lines and dragon nests could feed into this. By binding themselves to a single location they form an artificial dragon nest that has aspects similar to a broken mirror
                                  • Tales of nephilim and the son of a demon/the devil are mortal misunderstanding of the "dhampir" or "Yang" aspected/imbalanced Demons
                                  • Neither got another edition to smooth over and fix the mechanical issues within them, meaning their rules are less refined
                                • There's nothing saying they have to be the same at all, just use the same mechanics
                                  • Any of the similarities above could be coincidence if the ST wants
                                • Things this method fixes
                                  • Abrahamic religion is no longer treated as being correct. Bringing in the idea of a multilayer reality allows for any number of deific entities to be the source of the Wan Kuei/Demons
                                  • Regionality is more easily explained as the influence of their human part
                                    • It explains why Wan Kuei haven't been rising in the west, they have they're just Demons/Angels/Jinn/Ifrit etc
                                    • It could also be related to conflicts between the Archdukes, Yama Kings, and any other equivalent as they've made their little hells and claimed territory in the mortal world as "theirs" (or they are actually different)
                                • VtM style vampires/Cainites in KotE territory
                                  • They could be like the way the Drowned Legacies are described, something slightly different while still very similar to vampires
                                  • They could be new clans descending from other antediluvians (similar to the above with "Caine" being a being cursed by a deific entity for murder in a multilayered reality)
                                  • They could be bloodlines of the existing clans like how Kindred of the Ebony Kingdom appear
                                    • Any disconnect from the main body of the clan is because whatever Methuselah founded them had no desire to deal with their kin (just look at Dracon slaying Triglav for ways Cainites don't always get along with their broodmates)
                                  • In another thread I posited the idea that Xue might be a 3rd Generation vampire that left before the other Antediluvians rose up and slew their sires. As such he was uncursed by Caine. Instead his childer's Clan curse is a combination of a stronger beast (the P'o) and their Generation being tied to enlightenment rather than who sired who. The former is the product of Xue's experiments with the beast and trying to find balance while the later is Xue limiting his childer knowing that too much power in the hands of the unenlightened is a recipe for disaster
                                    • A similar idea would be to have Xue be an equivalent to Caine, not necessarily the same being but another who was cursed (by the August Personage of Jade perhaps) and ended up with the same flaws as western Cainites
                                      • In the same way maybe Caine isn't the sire of all the current Clans of vampires. Maybe the Setites and Banu Haqim are on to something with tales of their progenitors

                                I admit it's hardly perfect and it won't please everybody but I think it solves a number of religious and regional concerns that regularly crop up when KotE are discussed while still allowing everyone the chance to have what they like. The same is true of the brief discussion of VtM style vampires

                                    Originally posted by Thoth View Post
                                    Another way to look at KotE is "do you want to play vampire, but without the usual baggage"?

                                    V:tM is a stand alone system while KotE is a kitchen sink cross over system.
                                    V:tM is about being isolated from humanity while KotE has a strong family connection theme and other Asian community values
                                    V:tM is about hiding the supernatural while KotE is about managing the supernatural balance
                                    V:tM is about Caine screwing up and you being collateral damage, while KotE is about you screwing up your life and paying for it.
                                    V:tM is about being an emo teenager for eternity while KotE is about growing up/maturing for power gain.

                                    I would say that what needs to happen with the Kuei-jin is their mechanics need a serious overhaul to remove excess bloat and crunch, then fold the heretical dharmas into the original ones, make the dharmas much less antagonistic to everyone, and finally retcon the california invasion. KotE was a good alternative flavor of vampirism, but it was used in a poor fashion in relation to the metastory of the game line.
                                    That's interesting. I haven't read even the core book yet but this sounds very interesting and more attractive to me than the normal VtM take.
