                                                        03-21-2021, 12:31 AM
                                                        Originally posted by archderd View Post
                                                        not really, basically everybody i know homebrews all the time, not just V5 but any system we try. it's just "don't like it, just homebrew it" is not an excuse or even a decent defense of some of the baffeling decisions in V5 or the poor execution of many of it's ideas. V5 has a shit ton of issues, the ability to homebrew said issues doesn't change that fact.
                                                        I think there are some really good ideas in the fluff and some of the mechanics. For an example of a mechanic I like; I generally prefer the combo of Banes and Clan Compulsions to a lot of the older weaknesses; many of which could be either crippling or ignored entirely depending on the campaign. I like that moving Quietus into Blood Sorcery means that you can give every caste of the Assamite/Banu Haqim clan the same disciplines rather than having them be separate bloodlines. I think the opening Presence power (+1 to social rolls per rank) is way more useful for social vampires than the V20 rank 1 power where its unclear if its all that much better than just having the attribute+ability at 5 dots each without the discipline.

                                                        That said, ALL of those ideas were easier for me to import into V20 than try and make V5 work for my players because there were so many other things that just didn't work for us. Like turning Potence from something extremely flexible even at low ranks (strength has a lot of applications) into specific powers where converting a ghoul with Potence means they either jump (and not all that much better than a skilled athlete would be expected to be able) or deal extra damage to mortals... no breaking a steal door of its hinges or throwing a loaded trash bin through the air with enough force to bowl guys over; just video game power one (Force Jump) or video game power two (semi-Damage Boost vs. Mooks; how often does it matter if you kill vs. just knock out human opponents?).

                                                        Our games have always been a bit more combat detail focused so the amount of abstraction in using Pool vs. Pool tests w. margin damage to the loser and a strongly suggested limit of three tests to keep the story going just isn't appealing with that mindset and a single opposing Firearms vs. Firearms test instead of two sets of Firearms vs. Dodge tests just doesn't remotely capture the level of detail we prefer.

                                                        So there's a few gems of ideas there; I just really wish they hadn't gone the WotC route of completely rewriting the system with a new edition instead of continuing the polish the previous engine... ex. V20 moving Dodge into Athletics made a HUGE difference in terms of expressing concepts than having it as a separate "feat tax."

                                                        V5 cleaning up the abilities into Physical, Mental and Social to match the attribute groups instead of as the rather more arbitrary Talents, Skills and Knowledges and having most Discipline tests be Attribute + Discipline Ranks instead of Attribute + Ability opened up more flexibility in picking Abilities for concept reasons instead of just to max out the Discipline tests were both good ideas; Hunger dice instead of Blood Pool and Stains and Touchstones instead of Virtues were ideas that really only work in a few types of game styles vs. the broader number of styles the older editions could support.

                                                        I like a lot of the metaplot elements too; though I think they play a LOT better by introducing them one at a time every few sessions into an existing campaign instead of "oh, and all this stuff happened 5-10 years ago between editions." For example, right now there's quite a bit of buzz with the news that the Tremere Prime Chantry got blown up. Rumors in the local Elysium are flying about who may have survived, who may have died, which vampire group was behind it (no one expects The Second Inquisition) and where the local Tremere Primogen has disappeared to are stirring up all sorts of conflict, particularly since the Tremere Whip and the next in line for Regen of the city's Chantry are two different Tremere and while the Tremere PC is keeping it dead secret right now (along with all the other Tremere), the change to the Clan Curse with all the Blood Bonds breaking is absolutely something that's happening too and why, even if they don't have all the details yet, the Tremere PC knows whatever went down is BAD.

                                                        Having it happen in the Now makes it far better story material than how it was presented in V5 as an event that occurred a decade and a half ago with all the fallout already sorted. Right now its shaking up everyone's plans... particularly given the missing Tremere primogen's stranglehold on the city's charity blood drives making him THE source you had to buy from if you wanted to be a Bagger in the city. There's now a bunch of vampires including the PCs ready to go to war over that resource.

                                                        And again, there's nothing about the fluff that is particularly hard to just bring into V20... the biggest mechanical-based fluff change is Thinblood Alchemy which I HATE anyway so that's a development I'll just ignore as I write the other elements into the campaign one at at time.

                                                        Next on the agenda will be as soon as the Tremere strife is nearly settled, the 8th Gen Giovanni who's been an "ally" (more mutual business partners) of the PCs is going to have a problem because apparently he was deemed problematic and in need of removal from the mortal coil as part of the Family Reunion... so some Harbingers will be coming to town to remove him before the actual rituals are to be enacted to bind the clan/bloodlines into the Hecata. So he's going to start calling in all the markers of all the Kindred in the city who owe him for his century and a half of smuggling in whatever they happened to need (at cost for those he felt favors owed would be more useful than more money in the bank).

                                                        So, again... great story potential in making it personal and in the Now instead of a settled past event.

                                                        Because of this... if I had to pick one edition where I could only play it Rules as Written, I'd stick with V20 in a heartbeat. If I can homebrew to my heart's content I'd still take V20, but I'd happily add the ideas from V5 I did like into it.
