    (V5 Homebrew) City Gangrel/Grey Hunters loresheet

    01-19-2024, 07:34 PM
    Back on my BS again, this time I'm taking a look-see at my favorite path, and...well, not one of my favorite clans, but one I personally underrate. One of my favorite bloodlines? well, the inner munchkin gets happy in the pantsular region when I look at that sweet, sweet discipline spread of Celerity, Obfuscate, and Protean. It's about the only one I consider just as busted as Szantovich and Telyavelic Tremere.

    Like some others, I'm taking the thematic overlap and using that to create a single loresheet I feel does justice to both, but incorporates the neat aspects of either into a single whole.

    The Grey Hunters (Gangrel only)

    We know the deal. They're Gangrel who have adapted to live in cities, as opposed to be general-purpose all-weather survivalists, and have a slightly different "code of conduct" than other Gangrel. They're simultaneously closer to their Beast with less fear over letting it off its leash, but more restrained about the circumstances in which they do, in order to protect kine and the Masquerade in such close confines. As with anything else related to the Gangrel these nights, it's almost exclusively found within the Anarchs where slightly more feral behavior is tolerated than within the Camarilla, under auspices of personal "freedom" and "responsibility".

    Now, fun stuff.

    • Adaptive Hunter: Grey Hunters don't just go where the prey is, Grey Hunters do as their prey does. This means throwing off the shackles of habit and preference, to hunt as effectively as possible in whatever environment the Grey Hunter finds themselves. Any time you hunt by a method other than those dictated by your Predator Type, add +2 dice to your hunting roll; however, this bonus cannot raise your hunting pool higher than your default as determined by Predator Type.

    •• Urban Chameleon: Grey Hunters have learned various "tweaks" of Protean unavailable to other vampires, which help them survive and hunt in their chosen turf. Skin may shift tone or luster to blend in with the vampire's surroundings, or their facial features might shift slightly to throw off others' ability to recognize or provide an accurate description. These changes are largely unconscious but can be suppressed at the Grey Hunter's will, and they tend to be so subtle they rarely endanger the Masquerade. Upon learning your first dot of Protean (or if you already possess it), gain +2 to Stealth dice pools in urban environments, and those trying to identify you in person or via surveillance footage suffer a -2 dice penalty to their respective rolls.

    ••• A Better Leash: Despite their chosen environment, Grey Hunters still understand the value of their Beasts and the importance of its use. They never hesitate to employ it as needs dictate, which in turn mollifies the Beast as it knows it gets to play when the time is right. When you Ride the Wave, mitigate a single Stain from any action which incurs them as if you were upholding a Conviction.

    •••• Hunting Lodge: Havens are important for any vampire, but for Grey Hunters they are a unique point of pride. A Grey Hunter's haven is a place for them to sequester themselves away for safety and isolation, as well as demonstrate their hunting prowess. Your base Haven rating counts as two dots higher than it is for purposes of security and privacy (to a maximum of five), and for each trophy of a noteworthy kill (for example, a werewolf's pelt, elder's fangs, or Inquisitor's rosary) you display in your Haven, you gain a single dot of Status towards other Gangrel. Such trophies are often fleeting, and should you lose your trophies for whatever reason you lose the accompanying Status until recovering them (or getting new trophies).

    ••••• Big Game: The most dangerous hunts are those with the greatest rewards -- and best stories. Any time you feed from a Deadly mortal (your Storyteller will inform you when you have) or supernatural enemy other than vampires, slake an additional Hunger. Should you drain and kill that enemy, immediately gain the effect of a Dyscrasia corresponding to that enemy's Resonance regardless of their temperament. Alternatively, if you commit Diablerie you do not automatically lose a dot of Humanity; instead, roll an immediate Remorse test with a single die. This does not impact Humanity lost as a result of failing the contested roll to assimilate your victim's Blood.

      I love the City Gangrel so I had to chime in, I don't play v5 so loresheets never really made sense to me. Can't offer any advice on that aspect

      This current writeup relies pretty heavily on their Sabbat origin. Given how the metaplot has diminished the Sabbat, I'd prefer if a loresheet for them abandons their inhuman predator status and tries to reconcile them as Anarch Gangrel more suited to a city that should give them a greater preference for nightlife/clubbing which makes them more similar to the Toreador and Setites. Hell, maybe they're skilled enough with conforming to the Masquerade that they become a majority Camarilla bloodline. Insert your "Prince's lapdog" insults here. Their discipline spread makes them perfect for being a Sheriff's Hound

      As Gangrel they'd be more feral than an Anarch Toreador, sure, but they could be so much more than just rat-faced loners who leave corpses in an alleyway. If the Volgirre can play nice then the other Sabbat bloodlines should have that chance too (besides the Salubri antitribu). Less predatory homeless dudes, more vapid club kids who know the latest trends and how to exploit them. The Toreadors can have the pretty ones, the City Gangrel get the hangers-on

      Without any available Paths of Enlightenment they'd have to either rein themselves in or accept being the designated wight clan, stains will add up no matter how many Remorse rolls you're succeeding at. The 2 dot power you've proposed really focuses on what I'd like to see for them. Whereas Toreador are the trendsetters and the Ministry are the leeches who use nightlife for their own purposes, City Gangrel should be the ultimate chameleons who shamelessly use promiscuity and clout-chasing to survive. Survival over everything like the Country Gangrel focus on.

      Obfuscate any face you need, say whatever it takes to have a quick bite, then Celerity out before anyone even knew you were there. Protean to meld into concrete while the other Gangel have to find a quaint dirty park to sleep in

      Edit: I like the 3 dot power however, maybe the City Gangrel could have really perfected indulging the Beast and have an easier time riding the wave than anyone else. I'm picturing scenes like The Hangover where they're trying to figure out and fix the things they did when they let the Beast take the wheel. Sabbat-lite, if you will

          Folks, let's try to not downvote for offering civil, good-faith feedback, please?

          Originally posted by abugintherug View Post
          I love the City Gangrel so I had to chime in, I don't play v5 so loresheets never really made sense to me. Can't offer any advice on that aspect
          A lot of your thoughts here depend heavily on how v5 is constructed, and that might shed some light.

          This current writeup relies pretty heavily on their Sabbat origin. Given how the metaplot has diminished the Sabbat, I'd prefer if a loresheet for them abandons their inhuman predator status and tries to reconcile them as Anarch Gangrel...
          Well, that's the rub. The Lasombra outright defected to the Camarilla, sure, but the "reasonable" contingent of Sabbat at large ended up running with the Anarchs. Does my writeup rely heavily on Sabbat origins? yeah, there's really no way to get around that. They're the sect that pushed paths, and the sect city Gangrel by and large called home. Which is in my opinion silly, city Gangrel really are better suited for Camarilla or Anarch membership over Sabbat, but that's neither here nor there.

          Like I said, I'm basically just taking the city Gangrel and Path of the Beast, smooshing them together, and framing the result around the Path of the Grey Hunter. In essence, doing what I did with the OG Ventrue antitribu homebrew loresheet: freedom from the Sabbat allowed revivalism absent the sect's corrupt partisanship. That homebrew suggested martially-inclined and politically disaffected Ventrue revived the Knights of the Blood based around a mythologized ideal of Jurgen von Verden, and likewise here, Sabbat defector Gangrel brought the Path of the Beast to Anarch Gangrel, and together they managed to revive the Path of the Grey Hunter -- reviving an old idea, which modernized results in something greater than its constituent parts.

          ...more suited to a city that should give them a greater preference for nightlife/clubbing [and like commentary, snipped for length]...
          This is where v5 mechanics actually work in our favor. It's surprisingly easy to make a social Gangrel in v5 without sacrificing much. Fortitude alone is what it always should have been, and more. Protean actually has a power selection that lends itself surprisingly well to builds which aren't straight combat monsters, and Animalism is honestly the best it's ever been.

          But ultimately, it all boils down to Predator Type and what that offers a character. Most notably, the opportunity to start with an out-of-clan discipline of the player's choice. It's not even down to a choice of Celerity or Obfuscate, practically any discipline (most notably Auspex or Presence) is on the table, depending on selected predator type -- and those align with whether a character has a social, physical, or even mental bent. A Gangrel character doesn't need a unique discipline spread to function similarly to how we would identify a "city Gangrel" in older editions -- they just need dots in the right skills, and a matching predator type.

          Which is where this loresheet really shines, particularly that first dot.

          Without any available Paths of Enlightenment they'd have to either rein themselves in or accept being the designated wight clan, stains will add up no matter how many Remorse rolls you're succeeding at...
          If you're referring to the five-dot merit with this, that's huge -- so big I was reluctant to even put that in there on first draft, least of all without some major drawback. The Humanity loss for diablerie in v5 is automatic and unavoidable. The five-dot merit as I wrote it, gives a Gangrel with it a 50/50 shot of not losing it.

          The 2 dot power you've proposed really focuses on what I'd like to see for them. Whereas Toreador are the trendsetters and the Ministry are the leeches who use nightlife for their own purposes, City Gangrel should be the ultimate chameleons who shamelessly use promiscuity and clout-chasing to survive.
          To capstone what I said earlier, in v5 that's really a matter of predator type -- which really does offer a lot of potential options for characters.

          Edit: I like the 3 dot power however, maybe the City Gangrel could have really perfected indulging the Beast and have an easier time riding the wave than anyone else. I'm picturing scenes like The Hangover where they're trying to figure out and fix the things they did when they let the Beast take the wheel. Sabbat-lite, if you will
          It's actually of somewhat limited utility. Riding the wave is automatic in v5, you don't have to roll for it. At best, it lets players cut a little more loose than they otherwise would while riding the wave.
