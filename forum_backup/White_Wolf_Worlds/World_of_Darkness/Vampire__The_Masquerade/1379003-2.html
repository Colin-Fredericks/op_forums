                                                        This discussion again.
                                                        The OP isn't right, but his detractors like NecroticBinder are more wrong.

                                                        Humanity in WoD is worse than our humanity in the real world. It's more apathetic, more corrupt, more foolish. Even in our own world, we have rampant systemic injustices and nobody in 'civilised' society is doing about it, and if they are, well, they're powerless. That's more apparent in the world of darkness, which is even more of a neoliberal nightmare than what we're living in. Humanity is weak, and is no more likely to rid itself of the vampire menace than it is to become a socialist utopia. There's actually very little incentive for anyone with a possition of power (A very self-selective bunch) to work against rather than with a powerful, immortal vampiric benefactor. If you're happy working with something immoral like the military industrial complex, what's a bloodsucker to you?

                                                        Because of this, Vampire hunters in the WoD have always been either people at the very bottom fighting the man at the top, or dogmatic nutters (Yes, SAD is the latter). Neither of which presents an existential threat to vampires. If pre-nerf vampires ever got wind of a large organization hunting them, they'd descend, dominate, corrupt and destroy, so vampire hunters have always been small-off the grid groups. They need to be small and secretive to survive, and they have to be small because only madmen or those seriously and very personally agreived by vampires would decide to hunt them. When you consider the logistic nightmare of the so-called second inquisition, it clearly doesn't work. You need funding, backers, large information networks... You simply cannot stop pre-nerf Vampire characters from absolutely wrecking the system. There can be no global network of vampire hunters, because each group needs to be small and secretive and there's no feasible way to find other groups and share information with them without compromising themselves.

                                                        The Masquerade has always been a two-way hornet's nest. The Vampires don't want to start anything with humans because, never mind nukes, Tanks and Jets will ruin them. Most of the Humans who know, don't want to know. They don't want to use tanks and jets and endanger countless lives, they want to ride the gravy train that the vampires offer. The overwhelming majority of politicians and government agents want to keep the status quo as is.
