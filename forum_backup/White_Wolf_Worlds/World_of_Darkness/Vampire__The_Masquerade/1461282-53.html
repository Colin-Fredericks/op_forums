            But for realsies: The OG seven clans were the best at what they did.
            I do agree that the OG seven are the best, at the very least, at being the default options for players. And indeed it seems the main reason to keep the Gangrel out and kick the Brujah from the Cam is just to put them on the Anarchs, so the Cam doesn't have monopoly over the main body of the Big Seven.

            Some of your arguments don't add up so well, as you have an exceedingly strict view on the role of Disciplines in a Kindred's existence and clan effectiveness, but I know where this line of argument goes, so I'll focus on just a few picks here.

            You shouldn't brush off the Toreador so easily. The combination of Auspex and Presence is extremely powerful as a social weapon. It is less controlling, but actually more powerful than Presence + Dominate, or even than Auspex + Dominate, for their role in Kindred society. Celerity is also the most versatile physical Discipline even if you take out the broken rules on multi-action, as it can be easily both defensive and offensive. They simply excel at setting and riding social mood like no one else.

            Lasombra represent a different and interesting approach to leadership if compared to Ventrue. I wouldn't brush them off so easily, although the past as Sabbat is certainly a huge problem now. But were them in the original Cam, I totally see them and the Ventrue working together, as rival or allies interchangeably. More probable than any of the two controlling the Camarilla is the two being always disputing it but being collectively a de facto diumvirate, maybe a triumvirate with the Tremere.

                        Eh if you heard the phrase "an entire clan of bitches" and didn't think Toreador, there's something wrong with you.

                        Clans with Auspex are known for intelectual pursuits than those who don't have Auspex. (You could argue they're often obsessive)

                        Clans with Celerity are arsehats, by vampire standards. (I'd say fickle, but once again Assamites ruin that notion)

                                Originally posted by Muad'Dib View Post
                                I think that after the Tremere, they arguably have the strongest Disciplines set. Auspex and Presence is an especially potent combination, covering a lot of bases. Celerity is there to make sure that they cannot easily be directly threatened, making them able to use their social prowess more extensively.

                                Auspex is just strong as a suplementary discipline. But thinking about it more deeply, it's primarly an investigative discipline, not a social one (though it is certainly very helpful with social cues, depending on the edition.) One physical discipline, one social discipline, and one mental discipline.
                                In no particular order, here's some reasons why Ventrue>Toreador socially.
                                1- Toreador disciplines are expensive in OG VTM. Celerity and Presence are blood guzzlers whereas most disciplines aren't.
                                2- Ventrue have better combo disciplines.
                                3- Toreador are poorly organized and fight as much among themselves more than they fight outsiders. Ventrue are famously rather supportive of one another.
                                4- The directness of Dominate is a lot better on mortals than Auspex. Auspex may be more useful against vampires, but You've got less need for going against other vampires when you're much better at acquiring wealth from mortals and, really, if you really think you've got enemies, Dominate's a lot more fun to abuse.
                                5- Dominate is front-loaded with goodness. Auspex has it's best stuff at the back.
                                6- Until V5, Dominate was very competitive with Auspex when it comes to investigations.
                                7- In V5, Fortitude has social resistance powers. A big point up for Ventrue.

                                Most importantly
                                The Toreador don't have much of a weakness.
                                The Ventrue have a crippling one. Potentially the harshest in the game.

                                This actually give the Ventrue a much stronger social impetus. The Toreador make enemies because they're bored. The Ventrue, more than any other clan, Avoid enemies because their lives depend on the stable development of their herd. They're driven to be socially responsible, to care about reputation, to band together with their clanmates. The Toreador just want to see something beautiful so they can become jaded and temper their weakness with apathy. Socially speaking, you can predict what a Ventrue is going to do, and it's much harder to predict a Toreador. That sounds like a weakness, but from an outsider perspective it makes them the better choice for an ally by a good mile. Dependability >>> Sas. (the mountain range of resources help)

                                (I'm not trying to say the Ventrue are nicer than the Toreador. They'll totally crush you if a cost-benefit analysis tells them to. The Toreador may be bitches, but they might spare you with enough abstract reasoning)
