                    Hi again! Recently I had a request from David Tveraas to make some character sheets for some games he'll be running at Gen Con based on the video game Vampire the Masquerade: Bloodlines. He needed a 5th Edition sheet in the Bloodlines style, but then he was thinking he would like some sheets based more on the system of the video game, but that could be used for 5th Edition and V20/Revised.


                    So, I revamped my 5th Edition sheet to be more in the Bloodlines style, and then I set about making two sheets that were based on the Bloodlines system, but that had elements from V5 and V20. And of course they needed to be printable.


                    I'm really happy with how these sheets turned out, so I hope you like them!

                                                        Hello again everyone! After I released my Vampire the Masquerade: Laws of the Night Mini Foldable sheets, I had someone from By Night Studios reach out and ask if I could work on a new project that was along the lines of the mini sheet. The idea was to make a 2 page sheet, with 4 panels each, that you would print double sided that you could then cut to make 4 mini sheets for "quick characters". This sheet has just the basics (attributes, skills, health, willpower, humanity and disciplines) and could be used for both NPCs and Players for those who are new or just want to jump in quick.


                                                        So, I've made two versions, one is more for NPCs and the other is more for Players, the only difference being a slight change in layout where the NPC version has a limited skills list while the player version has the full skill list. These sheets could be used for both Laws of the Night or a regular V5 game.


                                                        I think these turned out pretty cool, so I hope you like them!


                                                        You can download the new sheets in the V5 (Minimalist) section of my site here: https://mrgone.rocksolidshells.com/wod.html#vtm
