    Ideas for Vampire characters? +

    04-27-2020, 08:31 PM
    I miss the old Clan books, mainly because I used to really enjoy reading through some of the, often very original pregenerated characters that used to be included in the back. So, on that basis, can anybody present their original ideas for characters, possibly with stats (in any edition).

    Just to quickly get going, I’d like to cite a short movie, called The Voorman Problem as an inspiration for a Malkavian vampire:

    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EaB2wiiOgHM


    That is, the character believes himself to be a God, that created the universe and everything in it (including you and all your memories) as a toy to play with, about seven days ago. In the context of vampire, (s)he would be able to use a high level of Dominate to be able to persuade and undermine everything other people assumes in the world today as being wrong. Like for example, Belgium doesn’t exist. Cloud Memory, Dementation (obviously) and The Forgetful Mind would be the basis of the Disciplines, possibly at the expense of everything else. The type of predator could be a Consensualist, persuading victims, but what I think would be more fun is that the character is a Scene Queen - with the scene being psychiatric treatment. The character would actively seek out psychological assessment and treatment and, after the interviewing psychiatrist mentally begins to collapse, works it into the interview process to be able to ‘sample' the blood in some sort of religious or scientific experiment. As the reputation of the patient begins to attract more pychiatric assessment, the vampire might start to enthrall them into doing extra work too. Social and Mental Stats high, especially Manipulation and Intelligence, and Physical stats low.

    I may type out the details later on, if people have updated their own ideas first.

      Hey. I could add a character for an V5 Ancilla game I made tonight. Maybe you'll like it:
      Vampire V5
      Character: Victoria »Vi« Herschel
      Core Concept: Whorehouse Jewel

      Name: Vivian Hawthorne
      • Born in 1850 as the twelfth child of Sir John Herschel, the astronomer and his cousin Margaret Brodie Stewart, Victoria was thought to be the last child of the family, her mother being 40 years old and it had been four years since she had last conceived.
      • The long line of scientists, astronomers, and inventors was not restricted to the male side of the family, although the girls were expected to learn only to entertain themselves and their future spouses.
      • She grew up with her father a stranger, as distant in his love and recognition as the spheres and moons he had chosen to spend his time with.

      Background:
      • Victoria had eleven older siblings, her oldest sister having married a prime minister’s son when the little blond angel was only two, her one younger sister Constance Ann was born when Victoria was five.
      • The house was full of literature, charts, trinkets from South Africa, private tutors and staff, taking care of all needs as they appeared with a strict distance that never provided a loving home.
      • Her official introduction into the gentle society was barely a splash as neither Julia Edith, Matilda nor Francisca had been married yet and it would be years before Sir John Herschel would give his daughter away.
      • Victoria was a rebelious child, never accepting authority and the relentless strive her father and brothers had for the ephemeral stars, fighting against the societal shackles that bound her siblings into the same kind of loveless relationship her parents had while envying the common peasants for the freedom to see and experience the physical world before them.
      • When Mr. O’Toole, a man she had inexplicably felt drawn to, took her to an opium den, he took her virginity and had he not embraced her right then and there, she would have bleed out.

      Embrace:
      • Craigh O’Toole was still a Neonate and grandchilde of an elder of the learned clan who was only ever refered to as the Skullsplitter, a warrior of nordic decent who had called northern Ireland his home for the better part of half a millenium.
      • Craigh’s Sire was less then pleased by the transgression but had an uncanny way with words, making many people outright forget Victoria Herschel’s existence, including her parents and siblings, but also potential suitors, servants and staff.
      • It was quite an undertaking, costing her dearly and for the transgression she and her Sire were banned to the new world.

      Unlife:
      • Victoria has spent close to 150 years in various cities around the country, most of the time blood bound to Mr. O’Toole, he himself being a toy to various elders of the clan.
      • She created a Childe herself back in the 1970s, a truck driver by the name of Joseph Clover, who is still in her employ and close to her heart though she sometimes wonders whether his embrace was her own choice.
      • For one thing it gave her a sense of being part of the age of aquarius, a renewed love for the celestial bodies of her childhood and the occult world she found herself in, as well as keeping her in touch with modern times.
      • Nowadays she owns a roadside whorehouse on the outskirts of the city, run by her ghoul Rosie and mostly frequented by truck drivers and whoever Victoria chooses to summon.
      • She has taken them name Vivian and is working as one of the girls herself, the prized piece of ass, feeding in the process and leaving the men and women wanting for more paying with coin, boon, and information to have her drink their blood.
      • There are about a dozen other girls working the premises and Joe and his boys try to make sure they notice anyone being smuggled into the city, bringing them to Rosie’s.

      Clan: Brujah
      Sire: Craigh O’Toole
      Generation: 11th
      Blood Potency: 2

      Attributes:
      Strength: 3 | Charisma: 4 | Intelligence: 2
      Dexterity: 2 | Manipulation: 3 | Wits: 3
      Stamina: 1 | Composure: 2 | Resolve: 2

      Health: 4 | Willpower: 4 | Humanity: 6
      Blood Potency: 2

      Skills:
      Victoria learned the trade first by her Sire, then during many years feeding on street urchins turning tricks and hookers selling themselves in port towns along the east coast. Persuasion 3 (Seduction)
      She had to understand what people want, what they care for, what they would do to get what they crave and after a century she reads people like open books. Insight 3 (Desires)
      In many professions such as this, truth and honesty are not tools of the trade but rather pitfalls to avoid when trying to get by night after night. Victoria had to lie about her origin, her state and sometimes her Sire and Clan to survive to the modern day. Subterfuge 2
      When you work in a whorehouse you inevitably deal with the societal underbelly of the city, dealing with hudlums, gangsters, smugglers, dealers, politicians and the eventual murderer. Streetwise 2
      Back in her childhood days, though she was in a constant state of rebellion against her distant father, Victoria picked up a lot of knowledge about the stars, trajectories, gravity and the high heavens which she infrequently updates through books and online papers. Science 3 (Astronomy)
      Victoria began her unlife as a lover, not a fighter and to this day she has found it far more fortuitous to scare someone straight then to deal with traumata, bloodstains and a corpse. Intimidation 2
      She remembers how to behave properly in a high society setting and though it might seems extraordinarily classic by now, older Kindred will most certainly respect her efforts if not her manners. Etiquette 1
      It goes without saying that she does have the occasional public figure visiting her premises and spends a night or two a month entertaining local politicians, lobbyists or staff members, gradually accumulating a pool of political knowledge and secrets. Politics 1
      The same is true of wealthy patrons and men and women working for them, doing her taxes for her, giving her advice on spending and saving, wanting to please for that sweet, sweet Kiss. Finance 1
      Unlife is not as peaceful as anyone would hope and Victoria has had to learn how to fend for herself. She is scrawny but muscular and she packs a punch but tends to fight dirty when she has the chance. Brawl 2
      For more than a year now Joe has taught her how to fight with the heavy fire axe he sprayed matte black, a weapon he himself carries with him and Victoria having a second one just like it hanging over the mantlepiece. Melee 2
      Joe also taught her the basics of driving, a skill that had eluded her until the turn of this century and one she thought she would never learn. It still is hard for her to grasp the concept of rude drivers without following her great grandsire and splitting some skulls. Drive 1
      Victoria learned how to pick a lock and hotwire a car in case of an emergency that hopefully never arrives. Larceny 1
      The hardest lesson she had to learn if you ask her, would be how not to be seen or heard, something that goes directly against who she deems herself to be. She still is not very good at it. Stealth 1
      Victoria really tries to stick with the times though, proving that not everyone embraced around the time of the civil war is a relic. She works her phone, orders stuff online and knows how to download new science papers whenever she feels like it. Technology 1

      Languages:
      Victoria Herschel was born into the gentle society of england and spent her living days in Kent and London. While some of her older siblings spoke Dutch as well, due to being born in South Africa, she never picked up on their secret code. English
      Only after her embrace, spending decades with O’Toole did she pick up on his native tongue and is fluent in Gailge by now, though hardly anyone ever speaks it around her anymore. Gailge

      Ambition:
      »I want to have the world remember my mortal existance.«

      Desire:
      »I want to have Rosie’s made an Elysium.«

      Convictions:
      »Never again let harm come to my family.«

      Touchstones:
      Rosie O’Bannon, Victoria’s ghoul and madame of the house. She is a woman stuck in her middle years since the 1990s and swears on her eventual gravestone that she knew Charles Manson before he got famous. Victoria saw the rebelious fighting spirit and Rosie’s blood called out to her. After a malicious run-in with a John, Victoria ghouled her, corrupting the spirit to conform to her needs. Rosie feels responsible now and takes care of the girls and regular business. Every time she tries to run away, she finds herself returning a day or two later, her will to live slowly eroding in this lusty cage.

      Predator Type: Siren

      Disciplines:
      Force of Personality 4: Awe, Daunt, Lingering Kiss, Summon
      Force of Blood 1: Lethal Body

      Flaws & Advantages:
      Family is one of the most important things for Victoria and she has always believed blood to be thicker than water, which makes her so much more susceptible to the Blood Bond. Flaw: Bonding 1 (Blood Junkie)
      Not only that, family is for life. Maybe it was her lack of a loving home, maybe it was the eradication of her existence in the memories of her loved ones, but Victoria’s vitae yearns to belong for eternity. Flaw: Bonding 1 (Long Bond)
      O’Toole taught her to never drink on the free men and women of Ireland and he made her believe this to be her own idea and decision. Of that he is not even sure himself though, it all originating down the line perhaps. Flaw: Feeding 1 (Prey Exclusion: Irish)
      Victoria’s kiss is one for the ages. Her bite is highly addictive and she spreads this bloody high freely, often forgetting that she has done so and losing interest in her plaything after a short while, leaving longing Johns and Janes as husks in her wake. Flaw: Retainer 1 (Stalkers)
      Sometimes she is so caught up in her lovemaking and blood drinking, she does not realize who she fucks over. Sometimes they are of strong will and resist the lingering kiss long enough to come searching for revenge instead of redemption. One such man is Pavel »Evil Eye« Miroslavic, a ghoul of a local Tremere who has already burned down Victoria’s last haven and waits for his opportunity to see her burn. Flaw: Enemy 1

      She goes by the name of Vivian Hawthorne now, a fake I.D. she aquired with the help of one of her more talented patrons. He made sure there was enough data in the system to back it up but once his task was complete he vanished into the maw that is the masquerade. Mask 1
      Victoria is a beautiful young woman, pale and slender as a bone, luring Johns and Janes to her establishment and into her bed, to drink from them and have them pay for the priviledge. Looks 2 (Beautiful)
      She feeds on her patrons, she feeds on the other girls, sometimes she consumes so much blood in a night, she is not the only one to look like a corpse, but her addictive kiss makes them come back until they die. Herd 4
      Victoria is accepted throughout the city, she and her whorehouse have been fixtures of the local Kindred scene for more than two decades now and she can put you in contact with almost everyone worth talking to. Status 2
      Only rarely does she take money for her services to her peers. Usually she is paid in blood and boons. Secrets will get you far as well, as long they either lead to blood or boons. Almost all of her cash is used for upkeep of the premises and girls. Resources 1
      Rosie used to be someone she could identify with, but their relationship has turned steadily since Victoria is giving blood to her former friend and longtime business partner who has, in all honesty, become a servant. The madame is charming, tough and the honeypot who seems to be in charge. Retainer 2
      Victoria spends her days in the basement under Rosie’s place on the outskirts of town. She knows it’s not the safest bet, but her last Haven was burned down half a year ago, and for now it will do to keep her cards close to her chest. Haven 2, Merit 1 (Watchmen), Merit 1 (Luxury)

      Weapons:
      Victoria does not keep weapons on her, they would ruin her flow. There are however two unregistered 9mm guns hidden in Rosie’s Place, only she can never remember where exactly. The one weapon she actually trained with is the matte black fire axe Joe has gifted to her and now hangs over the mantlepiece in the lounge as an implied thread to all who dare cause trouble.
      Heavy Melee + 3 Damage

      Experience Points:
      0 left, 35 spent

                                          Andy
                                          Brujah.
                                          Concept: You were a fitness junkie, maybe you were in the forces. You lost both legs in an accident, and you were determined that it wouldn't be the end of you. You've honed your upper body to the apex. You became a paralympic contender and have always strove for greatness. So much so that your passion and drive attracted the embrace. With the power of potence, your physicality has reached the point where you're more mobile out of a wheelchair than an average person; Able to sprint and leap with just your arms to the point of masquerade breach, leading you to be dependent on a retainer to take you around in public, kept to a wheel chair. This is greatly depressing for you, but your greatest fear is that someday, you'll frenzy, and you'll leap off that chair and kill your beloved assistant. It's hard to move a body quietly with just two limbs. You feed primarily from animals and the sleeping, for although you long to hunt in other ways, you're just too memorable to try.
                                          Rollplaying hints: You're somewhere between a drill sergeant and a motivational speaker; constantly ready to inspire your coterie.

                                          Teddy
                                          Tremere
                                          A poor black male with no access to education; Your teen self was enraptured in the gangsta life, and then you went to prison for what you later realized was a series of stupid things. you were intellegent. As the years went on in prison, you eventually wanted to better yourself. Finances, history, sociology; You were going to be the first man from your neighbourhood to come out of prison in a better state than you went in. You were going to get good with money and rise to the top, and you had plenty of time to study. You enjoyed studying and spent all your time in the prison's library, going on to tutor other inmates, of all kinds, in your fields. You became a model prisoner, and you attracted the attention of the Tremere. After an arrangement, you left the prison ghouled, eager to put your financial theories to the test in the real world. After proving yourself so useful in finances and streetwise, you earned the embrace. Hermetic theory has become like a religion to you; You're like a born again christian toiling in the libraries of the chantry. It doesn't matter if the clan's oppressive; you've spent your life in prison, and the condition of the chantry is certainly better, especially as you're the main breadwinner.
                                          Rollplaying notes: you're used to dealing with unreasonable people and try to avoid offence; You try to come off as friendly unless it's clear someone's looking for problems, then it's time for an ass whooping. You believe all problems can be solved with education.

                                          The Crusading cape.
                                          Malkavian.
                                          You were a body builder, definetly; your parents were gunned down by poor people before your very eyes, maybe; You were old money rich, very questionable. That were far too invested in comic books; Certain. What the truth is and what is the lie you've constructed is makes no matter. You are who you are right now. You have an absurd amount of money, great connections, and a fantastic relationship with the city's Elders who find your antics entertaining and your politics in line with their own; you largely have a free pass on what others would consider 'poaching' so long as you don't hurt anyone personally close to anyone. You have a burning, sadistic relationship with the impoverished denizens of your city. You don't kill, you maim and rape, and you've got a lot of friends in the businesses that benefit from the violence you dish out. You dress up in a super "hero" costume in order to fight the dregs of society and your profeciency with obfuscate allows you to largely get away with it, you've ghouled a minor to fight with you and you've infected madness into a few opponents in the hopes of getting a longstanding rogues gallery.
                                          Rollplaying notes: You don't consciously use any of your disciplines, they just augment what you think are mundane actions. You are completely enraptured with your sick delusion and your personal goals are only to perpetuate it. You will do whatever the prince or primogen asks as he has taken up a special roll in your fantasy. You don't try to get anyone in debt with prestation; you just do what is right according to your sick sense of justice and assume that they'll just keep trouble away from you behind the scenes or that maybe you'll use them as a contact at some point. If someone gets you to break character, you'll frenzy, and it's well known at court what kind of requests will and won't work from you. They also know you'll clean up someone in their yard without compensation, so long as they word their request the right way.
                                          . If possible, ask your storyteller to allow you Flight 1 or 2 (For use with a gliding cape)
