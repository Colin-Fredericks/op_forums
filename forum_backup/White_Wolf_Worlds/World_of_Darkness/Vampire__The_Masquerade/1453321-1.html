                        I don't think in terms of evolutionary steps but more in terms of cycles. In a sense most things in WoD loop back onto themselves, and that was true with Thin bloods even before V5. After all the Thin-bloods had inceptor powers just like Caine and low-gen vampires did, and in many ways Caine is like a thin blood/Caitiff himself (less tightly bound to the curse compared to his progenitors but also more 'human' - although in his case its more because he's the genesis of everything whereas the Thin bloods are a result of the vampiric curse weakening in one or more ways.)

                        In that same vein I've always seen TBA as a remake of the 'inceptor' ability in a different way and it kinda makes sense. Thin Blood/Duskborn seem to be more of a catch all for all the low-gen and dhampirs put out in ToTB under a single class and the 'original' idea for Dhampir (inasmuch as there is an origin in what little is known in legends and such not influenced by media depictions) have the mortal descendants of a vampire being more prone to sorcery and magical powers. Hence an affinity for TBA would make sense from that view as well.

                        To be fair my 'headcanon' for such tends to conflate both V5 and pre-V5 material (time of thin blood and V20) interchangably rather than treat them as distinct, so that could muddy the applicability of the above in discussion since I've always preferred a more nuanced approach to thin bloods than typically depicted (and even V5 leaves out certain details... like it was techncially possible for pre-V5 to have lower generation thin bloods - the lowest gen I've heard the curse weakening to was 6th - but V5 and some latter depictions seem to want to treat them as exclusively high-generation phenomena.)
