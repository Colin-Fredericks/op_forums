    Predator question - How to be a Consensualist as a Tremere?

    I'm playing a Tremere character in a discord game set in the 1960s in Asia.
    He is an academic type with a goodish personality.

    Im thinking of going with the scene queen predator type and have him feed on members of a university, but I'm also really drawn to the Consensualist predator type, given the personality I have for this character. He is basically a good person when compared to most kindred.

    I'm just not sure how to rp and make the Consensualist predator work tho. Can he just tell people he wants to feed off them and just make them forget it with dominate?

      How the Tremere have fallen...

      Dominate/Blood bonds- So long as they say yes, it's fine, right? Maybe they're just taking advantage of the Clan's herd and don't know better, Maybe you've got some wanna-be and you've told them you'll turn them eventually but for now they need to be drank from.

      Predator types aren't realistic. You're not going to soley feed one way through your unlife, it's at best a preference and at worst you pick what's mechanically best and then start the game by moving away from it.

          "Is the Concensualist inconvenient?" Understatement of the century.

          The best way to pull this off is to have a herd that's always with you and you can feed on at will. Going out to feed on random people is... unlikely, extremely time consuming and considered Masquerade-breach-y. Having to convince people that 1) you're a vampire, and 2) to risk their lives so you can eat them is going to be tricky without a large amount of trust between you and the target and no disciplines. Even the people that are willing will often require more time than a standard feeding roll.

          Consentualists work best when you can create a group of Bella-types that are interested in you over the long term. Consentualists are gardeners and farmers, not hunters and predators.

                                                  Predator Types describe different phenomena, with the result of overlap and duplication. This makes them highly flexible.

                                                  While most Predator Types describe how a vampire puts fang to neck, "Consensualist" is about the mindset of the vessels. If the vessel gives consent (Which is a complex, complex, complex, and... did I mention complex?... topic.), the feeding itself can be accomplished through the methods of nearly any PT. If there are sexual overtones, it is Siren. If among friends and family, Cleaver. If the vessel is a vampire, Blood Leech. If in the context of a fight club, Alleycat. If in a house full of junkies, who let you feed on them while they are passed out, you have Sandman.

                                                  You could choose nearly any other PT with the background note that the vampire prefers to gain consent. Is there a particular mechanical reason you specifically chose Consensualist and Scene Queen as your top two? If you're not doing it for a specific discipline or background, I would advise you to look elsewhere.

                                                  Consensualist's most distinctive element is the free dot of Humanity. The problem is, this can be lost very easily. With one bad roll, you can lose the most distinctive thing you got from the PT.

                                                  Plus, Prey Exclusion is a rough, rough flaw to play. If you feed without consent, you gain stains... which makes it more likely you will lose that dot of Humanity. You might also gain stains simply for observing other kindred feeding on the non-consenting. This could make your character rather disruptive of hunting for other pcs, as well as making attendance at vampire social events difficult.

                                                  That is, of course, if you ever get invited to anything, considering you have Dark Secret (masquerade breaker). Understand the mechanics of Dark Secret. It is actually the Infamy flaw, except because it is not known it has a reduced value... in this case 1 dot. So, once revealed... and given you must spend a good chunk of your time scolding others for not getting consent when they feed to avoid Stains, it will be revealed... it becomes a 2 point infamy (masquerade breaker). This gives you a 2 dice penalty on all pertinent social rolls, which is a best-case scenario. In the Camarilla, this likely means a blood hunt.

                                                  Note, Consensualist gives you the Dark Secret (masquerade breaker) flaw, which reveals to a 2 dot Infamy flaw. However, the Fame background specifically mentions "serial breacher of the masquerade" to be a two point secret, and thus a 3 dot Infamy. V5... sigh... gotta love it.

                                                  Meanwhile, Scene Queen is less weird, mechanically.

                                                  Except, for one big thing. The flaw Prey Exclusion (a different subculture from your own).

                                                  Okay, this might make sense if what you're trying to say is, "My character is an academic, and wants to feed on academics." Except, that's not what the flaw does. It makes it so you gain Stains for feeding on non-academics, because... you... ummm... think anybody other than academics are evil and need to be fed on to reduce their population? Or, maybe, academics are so wonderful they... what... purify you as you feed?

                                                  Plus, the flaw (per RAW rules, V5 p181) allows the ST to give you Stains for allowing anyone else to feed on your excluded vessels. So, if your Prey Exclusion is "anyone who is not an academic", you risk getting stains for allowing a fellow player's pc to feed on a pedophile, unless, of course, the pedophile happens to have a PhD. In which case, dig in!

                                                  Or, does the flaw mean you select "a subculture other than your own" in the sense that if your subculture is "academics", your must choose a prey exclusion for a single other subculture, like "bikers" or "aristocrats"? If so, why would being a leader of one scene necessarily pit you against another subculture?

                                                  If your chosen vessels are a different specific subculture (like an academic who refuses to feed on anyone who isn't a biker), you get the weird situation where you stridently campaign for the entire coterie to only feed from this other, unrelated subculture. This would be roleplayed as, "Hey, my vampire friends, I know we all need to feed. I really must insist you only ever feed on bikers, because I am an academic. I will not allow any feeding on non-bikers!"

                                                  So... my suggestion would be avoid Consensualist.

                                                  Scene Queen, maybe, is fine if you don't choose prey exclusion. Though this still leaves you with Disliked... and, really, why would every single person who isn't an academic dislike you?

                                                  Have you considered Cleaver (representing that you have maintained your social ties in the university)? Or, Bagger (representing access to blood stores at the university's medical school or research labs)? Or, Osiris (representing what a dynamic and brilliant academic your pc is... take "lectures" as your entertainment specialty)? For that matter, take Sandman to represent access to the undergrad dorms.
