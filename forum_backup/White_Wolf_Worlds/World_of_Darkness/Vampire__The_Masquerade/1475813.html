                      Originally posted by VentrueStereotype View Post
                      i couldn't find any announcements. Do we have the links for them?

                      It was in the RenegadeCon stream yesterday, and Outstar said on discord they'll have announcement pages next week.

                      Here's a longer breakdown of the four Vampire announcements.
                      • Vampire Player's Guide due summer 2022, with all the non-core clans in one spot, updates to the Companion material, alternate banes, character creation walkthrough and other mechanics.
                      • Vampire Character Journal, an organized journal for all of your character stuff, allies, relationship map, etc.
                      • Vampire Starter Set, box set w/all tools to learn how to play or to teach people how to play VtM or update old players to V5. Come w/pregens, quickstart rules and story.
                      • Blood Sigils, a Blood Sorcery and Thin Blood Alchemy book, will not only have BS and Alchemy mechanics but also stuff for the Blood Sorcery scene in general, providing locations, component information, and other source material that will give you Story Hooks for your game
