    Alternative V:tM - Creating New Clans

    05-27-2022, 12:13 AM
    Years and years ago, I created a thread where, as a game of sorts, people collaborated to create an alternative V:tM setting. By creating new Clans. I thought it’s been enough years, it might be fun to play that game again.

    Sometimes there are threads or discussions about alternate versions of various OWoD games, like randomly assigning Clans to the Camarilla and Sabbat or eliminating all mages. Well, this will be a similar but perhaps more collaborative thread. Creating our own Clans from scratch and filling up the Camarilla and Sabbat with them. So, there will be 13 Clans, including 7 in the Camarilla and 2 for the Sabbat, plus 4 independents. The Camarilla and Sabbat will be at least similar to the canon organizations in that the Camarilla will represent the rule by elders and will be more conservative and following the Masquerade, while the Sabbat will have been born of the Anarch Revolts, will be more radical, and ostensibly values freedom from the elders. Of course, with different Clans, these organizations will be quite different from canon and important ways.

    We can start by each person essentially taking a turn by creating a Clan with its name, Disciplines, weakness, the sect it belongs to, and adding some brief description. Subsequent turns will add additional Clans, creating them as to fit into the Camarilla or Sabbat along with those Clans added previously. As more turns are taken (assuming he thread takes off) the differences between this setting and canon V:tM will become more fleshed out. Bloodlines can be added as well, along with Clan variants and schisms. Just try not to step on anybody else's toes if you add something to what they've created before you. If the thread lasts long enough to create all 13 Clans, we can flesh out other aspects of this Alt V:tM, like how the Anarch Revolts happened, differences in cosmology, and so on.

    I'll start with an example Clan.

    Pontiki
    The Pontiki have always been a Clan that have lived amongst the lower levels of society. Beggars, refugees, oppressed minorities, the homeless. The Pontiki embraced from among these people and considered them their flock, feeding from them but also protecting them from the worst excesses of the more powerful members of society. As the Pontiki empathized with the plight of struggling mortals, they soon too began to side with the plight of struggling vampires and were among those who lead the Anarch revolts and helped to create the Sabbat. Though they often eschew material wealth, they are masterful spies, information brokers, and assassins - often trading in favors rather than money.

    Nickname: Rats

    Sect: the Pontiki are a Sabbat Clan, one of the founders of the Sabbat in fact and are still heavily influential in the Sect. There are also numerous Pontiki who are independent since they object to the Sabbat’s hierarchy or their treatment of mortals. Very few Pontiki are in the Camarilla, though some side with the Anarchs in their attempts to undermine Camarilla rule.

    Appearance: Pontiki come from all ethnic and cultural backgrounds, they are quite diverse in that regard, though groups that are poorer, oppressed, or otherwise disadvantaged are over represented among the Pontiki. Since they have poor backgrounds, they tend to dress on fashions popular within those subcultures. Even among Pontiki who have some measure of wealth and power, they will give the appearance of poverty or at least styles that are popular among poorer or outcast people.

    Haven
    Many Pontiki live in abandoned buildings, in makeshift shelters, or in vehicles like vans or trucks. It isn’t uncommon for a number of Pontiki to have their haven within a large dilapidated factory or warehouse. Sometimes they may live among homeless or in homes owned by gangs or outlaws.

    Background
    Pontiki childer are nearly always selected from the poorest members of society. In modern America, this could mean the homeless, immigrants, convicts and criminals, the insane, crippled people, addicts, or runaways. In various times and places they have drawn from slaves, religious or ethnic minorities, or itinerate laborers. Over the years, the Clan has a culture of eschewing material wealth even when the means exist for gaining it.

    Clan Disciplines: Animalism, Celerity, Obfuscate

    Weakness: the blood of the Pontiki has never been able to create a blood bond. Perhaps this weakness, which dates back to time immemorial, was the reason that the Pontiki struggled to gain temporal power and so found solace among the dregs of society.

    Organization: the Pontiki are not highly organized, in fact they often reject the notion of hierarchies, though they are a tightly knit Clan and informally help each other in times of need and show respect to their elders and other Pontiki of noteworthy skill or reputation. In practice they must sometimes obey the will of superiors both within and outside of the Clan, though they often chafe against these situations.



    Current Progress:

    Camarilla:
    Cicatrix: Auspex, Dominate, Obfuscate
    Netjeru: Dominate, Fortitude, Necromancy
    Dracul: Daimoinon, Protean, Sorcery
    Bagava: Animalism, Potence, Presence
    Sukkal: Animalism, Auspex, Celerity
    Vegulans: None

    Sabbat:
    Pontiki: Animalism, Celerity, Obfuscate
    Polyergus: Auspex, Presence, Vicissitude

    Independant:
    Cecaelia: Fortitude, Presence, Protean
    Hags: Animalism, Obfuscate, Sorcery

    Bloodlines:
    Mara-Ma: Fortitude, Potence, Vicissitude
    Savages: Fortitude, Potence, Protean
    Strix: Celerity, Fortitude, Potence
    Children of Veles: Carnophagia, Obfuscate, Potence

                      Well, I can give it a shot.

                      Cicatrices
                      singular: Cicatrix

                      (I'm no good at names, but whatever, White Wolf's choices seemed kin of weird to me half the time. So I named a bunch of vampires "scar tissue")

                      Curse: Flesh of the Corpse. As per the flaw, these vampires cannot heal properly. The health track can be recovered, but wounds leave ugly necrotic-looking gashes and holes, and limbs do not regenerate. Clan horror stories tell of the fools who've been reduced to nothing but a head attached to a dead heart, who will remain so for the rest of eternity.
                      When they use the blush of life, any wounds they have start bleeding, costing an extra blood point on top of that spent to activate the blush.

                      Disciplines: Dominate, Fortitude, Auspex.
                      When the rest of your untrustworthy bloodthirsty kind have a significant advantage, controling others is vital. Likewise being able to avoid getting hurt in the first place is ideal, and monitoring thoughts and emotions certainly helps with that. Astral projection and possession are also invaluable in never having to leave a well fortified haven. It's also not uncommon to find Celerity and Obfuscate out-of-clan amongst these vampires; can't hurt you if they can't catch or find you either! (and auspex, which I somehow forgot existed)

                      Sect: Camarilla. Being so... delicate, these kindred are more aware than any other the value of a peaceful, stable and well controlled status-quo. They tend to lend their abilities to making sure the Ivory Tower lives up to those values. They keep a keen eye on events and individuals, making sure nobody rocks the boat. Or else.

                      Many of these vampires keep a staff of carefully conditioned spies and bodyguards to protect themselves.

                      Childer are typically picked from those who serve the needs of the sire, clan and sect. People who show themselves capable in maintaining the environment the clan needs. Often they come from the sire's ghoul retinue, going through a period of assessment and grooming - followed by yet another one after the embrace.

                      A lot of them develop intense paranoia and control problems.

                          CECAELIA
                          First spotted in the ancient Mediterranean but now thought to have inspired tales of sea monsters around the world, this secretive breed of Kindred enchants, devastates, and disappears beneath the depths from which they came.

                          Nickname: Sirens

                          Sect: Independent. The Cecaelia are a force to be reckoned with in many coastal Camarilla or Sabbat domains, extorting tribute and concessions from Kindred whose fortunes depend on maritime trade and tourism.

                          Appearance: Many Cecaelia either shun clothing entirely when not on land, or wear the clothes they were embraced in - water sport gear, diving apparel, and naval uniforms are not uncommon.
                          Some who wish to play up the legendary enchanter of the sea may keep a collection of waterlogged apparel in their havens, but shiny jewelry, whose sparkling could be mistaken for prey fish, is nearly never worn unless the Cecaelia is in the mood to hunt a shark.

                          Haven: The ability to sink safely into the sea floor is a part of every Cecaelia's birthright, but many find or submerge some sort of large container until they learn the power - or if they have treasures they wish to store.

                          Background: Humans have worked or played on the water for ages; modern archaeology has even uncovered evidence that Neanderthal Man built and used working sailboats and explored. A Cecaelia could come from nearly any conceivable walk of life, with selection for the Embrace depending entirely on the whims of the individual sire. Infatuation and longing are often the motivation, and the Sirens have a reputation for beauty when they put on their game faces. Social Attributes and Skills are usually predominant, with Physical Attributes and Talents a close second.

                          Clan Disciplines: Fortitude, Presence, Protean (Mariner variant)

                          Weakness: An eternity spent submerged and subsisting on the blood of fishes and sharks takes its toll. Neonates merely look freshly drowned, but features of fish and other horrors intermingle with the once-human to disturbing effect as the years wear on. A Cecaelia's Appearance score only counts when the Siren is actively spending blood to appear human (V20, p. 269) for a scene. In all other scenarios, the Siren's Appearance score is effectively zero and they cannot pass for a normal human.

                          Note that one of the Discipline powers for which the clan is best known (Entrancement, V20 p. 195) uses Appearance + Empathy for a dice pool, and this is when the ability of the blood to pass for human sees the most use among the Cecaelia.

                          Organization: A great many Cecaelia are Autarkis, dwelling alone in lakes and rivers where the feeding isn't abundant enough to sustain more. Whole broods can be encountered in bays and on the shoreline near cities, where the flow of traffic is rich.

                          The extent to which the clan has any organization beyond that is unknown, but the best way to hide a secret is to put it under the ocean...

                                      Yara-Ma
                                      The Yara-Ma of Australian wilds are little known, and less cared for. Kindred view them as a dangerous threat for their ability to walk around in the sun without fear. Despite this, members of the bloodline rarely take part in vampiric politics, and often prefer to sleep in fig trees most days, where they wait for mortals to rest under before they attack. Their ability to walk in the sun makes many believe that they are not in fact kindred at all.
                                      Nickname: Drop Bears

                                      Sect: The Drop Bears rarely participate in modern politics and will rarely join the Sabbat or Camarilla. However, both sects will often hire the Yara-Ma as day-time assassins.

                                      Appearance: Yara-Ma often twist their features and traits to look more koala like to become less noticeable in the trees. However, just as many in the clan will take their time to look especially monstrous if given the chance.

                                      Haven: The Drops Bears often hunt in the wilds and will most often make their home within trees and caves. The bloodline often prefers to sleep in fig trees, a practice taught from sire to childer.

                                      Background: Yara-Ma only embrace individuals who show a resilience and ability to survive harsh environments. People who resist their feeding are often embraced after a long observational period.

                                      Clan Disciplines: Fortitude, Potence, Vicissitude

                                      Weakness: The Yara-Ma have nothing to fear from the sun, and do not need to sleep during the day. However, they instead feel the urge to sleep at night and often will not wake without great difficulty. The Drop Bear’s Humanity/Path rating is considered 2 dots lower for waking and remaining active at night. This weakness is the primary reason the drop bears do not take part in the undead's politics.

                                      Organization: Drop Bears are solitary by nature and will only mentor their childer for a month at most.

                                                      Netjeru
                                                      The Netjeru have their origins in ancient Egypt among the ruling noble families and high priests of the Old Kingdom. For thousands of years the Netjeru have preserved their religious traditions, worshipping the gods of ancient Egypt and practices their necromantic magics. More strangely, they have also preserved ancient bloodlines as well - the blood of ancient Egyptian kinds still flows within the veins of inbred ghoul families who serve the Netjeru and who the Netjeru almost exclusively embrace from. The Netjeru are extremely conservative and hierarchal, with harsh punishments for disobedience or heresy. They also believe that their bloodlines are superior and that other mortals should serve them.

                                                      Nickname: Mummies

                                                      Sect: The Netjeru are staunch members of the Camarilla. They belief that the elders should rule and that childer should obey. Those Netjeru who join the Sabbat are hunted ruthlessly by the parent Clan.

                                                      Appearance: As part of their ritual embrace, the Netjeru go through a process much like mummification. This leaves their bodies as withered corpses covered in linen wrapping. Most wear ornate garments of ancient design over their bandages, many with golden masks crafted to resemble their living faces.

                                                      Haven
                                                      The Netjeru see themselves as royalty and seek the most opulent dwellings from which to rule their subjects. This often includes grand mansions or even ancient buildings, particularly in their homeland of Egypt. The Netjeru surround themselves with revenant family members as well as conventional mortal ghouls, to serve them and act as their herd, they seldom interact with normal humans.

                                                      Background
                                                      Almost all Netjeru are embraced from one of the families who claim ancestry from ancient Egyptian kings. These families have inbred with each other for millennia and often have odd characteristics as a result. They tend to be somewhat alienated from mainstream society, even having exotic accents as their first language tends to be ancient Egyptian.

                                                      Clan Disciplines: Dominate, Fortitude, Necromancy

                                                      Weakness: The Netjeru cannot embrace normally, their embrace requires a long process where the childe is slain and their body carefully preserved, with organs removed, and flesh wrapped is specially prepared bandages - mummification. The end result leaves the childe with an appearance of zero, like a Nosferatu, though the bandages reduce the shock of seeing one somewhat. Those Netjeru who are not embraced in this way do not gain the full advantages of vampirism and are treated as thin blooded vampires (15th generation) and have that flaw instead.

                                                      Organization: The Netjeru are highly organized and have are ruled by a High Priest, usually eldest conscious Netjeru, who has beneath him advisers from each of the royal families who serve the Netjeru. Beneath them is a hierarchy based on region and family affiliation. Despite the strict hierarchy, sometimes the families feud amongst each other and this can undermine Clan loyalty - though they always try to maintain a facade of complete cooperation.

                                                          We’ve got to have at least one classic…

                                                          Dracul

                                                          When Vlad Tepes offered hospitality to a nameless wanderer and shared with him his struggles in protecting Wallachia from the Ottomans, the stranger offered Vlad the power to defend his people for eternity if he would but make himself an enemy of his enemy; the Biblical God.

                                                          He accepted and at Scholomance, an underground fortress in the Carpithian mountains, he studied the blackest of arts under the stranger for seven years until one day the stranger vanished… never to be seen again.

                                                          Vlad, now commanding the powers of Hell in the name of his sire, whom he knew only as The Dragon, returned to his realm in Wallachia and began to form his own brood.

                                                          The other clans, whose founders are millennia upon millennia older, at first regarded these upstarts as a joke, for it so clashed with established dogma that the Second Generation had long been destroyed, but between the story of his creation, the powers he displayed and even the Methuselahs of other clans being unable to bend his will it was concluded that somehow this relative neonate was the childe of either a forgotten member of the Second Generation or perhaps even Caine himself.

                                                          To set the others at ease, Vlad Dracula willingly joined his Clan to the fledgling Camarilla and they remain among the most devoted to the success of that institution. He also suggested the creation of cover stories to discredit stories of vampires by making their fictional representations a part of popular culture; using whispers to one Bram Stoker as a test case with similar methods to inspire authors such as Rice, Whedon, Rein-Hagen and Meyers to make vampires into something everyone believes are fictional and so anyone who claims to have seen one is automatically discredited.

                                                          Clan Disciplines: Blood Sorcery, Daimonion, Protean. While they are not “in Clan” disciplines, animalism and dominate and the physical disciplines are known by enough elders that one of the Dracul rarely has to step outside of the Clan to obtain them.

                                                          Clan Weakness: Your soul is corrupt and seeks to corrupt others. You make virtue checks at +1 difficulty and, when you feed from someone you consider “pure” you must make a Self-Control check (difficulty 8) to resist giving them some of your blood (making them a ghoul or vampire depending on whether you drained them dry or not).

                                                          Organization: Most members of the Dracul were innocents who were blood bound and as ghouls made to perform sinful and debasing acts before finally being embraced. The most promising candidates are sent to Scholomance, from which Vlad Dracula now rules his Clan and instructs his brood in the secrets of blood magic and the command of even darker powers.

                                                          Accidental childer (typically those killed during a frenzy where the sire doesn’t even remember following the compulsion to share their blood) are essentially the Clan’s antitribu (typically joining the Anarchs or Sabbat) and in place of the compulsion to inflict their blood upon the innocent, instead crave the blood of other vampires (with a self-control check needed to avoid drinking a vampire they feed from dry).
