        ANTEDILUVIAN HEADCANONS

        •After leaving the Second City for good, Haqim departed eastward and after a period of exploration, chose the early settlements that would become Susa as the kine domain for him and his clan while the Zagros Mountains of the Persian Penninsula would fulfill the role of geographically defensible location for the security and concealment of what would become Alamut, hidden so deep within inaccessible sections the mountains that it would require kindred capabilities to reach.

        •After the Second City, Ilyes and Troile would both participate in the founding of Troy as their combined masterwork, but later on as Ilyes grew more interested in broader politicking and other cities such as Athens, this became another one of the many tensions that would lead to Troile rising against her sire-lover.

        •The Indus River Valley Civilization and its collapse was tightly connected to the falling out of the once lovers Ennoia and Ravnos and the horse-back nomads of Ennoia that eventually drove off Ravnos and his clan as they forced their chosen people eastward, beginning the long rivalry of Gangrel-Ravnos.

        •[Lasombra] departed from the Second City filled with ambition and seeking both power and eternity. He departed to the Aegean where he would study certain Lilian and/or Wyrmish secrets that allowed him to begin channeling the Abyss while also being one of the stewards along with Akriel of the Neolithic Mediterranean.

        •Saulot and The Eldest wandered together for a time, out into the Far East, traveling and studying philosophy for a time until a disagreement between the two antediluvians developing philosophies of trancendence caused them to part ways, but this travel has led both ancient's clans, although more prominently the Salubri, to have significant numbers throughout Asia.

        •[Ventrue]'s disappearance has been explained via numerous rumors and fabrications over the millennia, yet all but a few vastly overstate the truth. The true reason that [Ventrue] departed the Second City was that the curse of Caine restricted his feeding to that of the First City and slowly the ancient's blood grew thin. While this issue may have been further aggravated by [Lasombra] or Troile striking out to weaken [Ventru] but the means for his disappearance was torpor as Caine naturally refused to revoke the curse, a large part of the reason for [Ventrue]'s pilgramage. They have risen for brief periods over the millennia but never for longer then a century due to their curse, yet always they have managed to shape the history of their clan and all kindred as only they can...

                                Personally, if I had to canonize any kind of Kindred of the East timeline, I would do it like this:

                                How I did the Wan Kuei Invasion in my campaign

                                Note: I hate the Great Leap Outward as a name and will never use it.

                                The Wan Kuei have their own version of the War of the Ages and with the rising tide of population in the world, more humans were turning into new Kuei Jin than the older ones could control them. Thus, the Chinese Wan Kuei came up with a perfectly devious and evil plan of getting their rebellious Communist and Capitalist childer out of their hair. They decided to do as Henry IV and busy giddy minds in foreign quarrels.

                                The plan basically consisted of using a bunch of rabble rousers to create a populist racist war against the Kindred of the West. All manner of slander, libel, and accusations were directed at the Cainites (some of which was true). They gave the younger vampires a ticket, told them to go invade California, and kill the Foreign Devils who were hideous monsters every bit as evil as the Yama Kings.

                                This, of course, was all bullshit but a surprisingly effective bullshit that worked just fine from the Wan Kuei's perspective. Fighting against the Anarchs eliminated a lot of their extraneous childer and didn't have a likelihood of causing problems for the Wan Kuei at home because the Free States didn't have a way of retaliating against their Chinese enemies. Hell, they barely understood they existed let alone their strains of influence.

                                The Camarilla and Jade Courts had a few meetings after the initial assumed this was a full-scale invasion. Several large cash payments, exchange of stolen relics, and negotiation via the Tremere in Hong Kong resulted in roughly this being communicated:

                                "No, we're just going after the Anarchs in California."
                                "Oh, well, that's fine."
                                "We know you'll try to take it back."
                                "True but we'll let you murder the Anarchs first."
                                "Wouldn't have it any other way."

                                Notably, La Croix was selected as an expendable Prince by Lucinde for this exact reason.
                                There were a few higher-ranking Wan Kuei among the invaders but generally, they were like Ming Xiao and had managed to tick off people back in the homeland that this was a kind of exile. Much blood was spilled and it looked like the Anarch Free States would collapse. However, time eventually turned against them.

                                In simple terms, the Wan Kuei have no real gain from holding territory in California since they don't appear there naturally. They can feed on the humans there but domain isn't really as big a deal there since many can feed easier than Cainites. They also weren't going to be getting any of the wealth or power of the Cainites they slew since most Anarchs didn't have either. In the end, the Wan Kuei gradually lost of a war of attrition against the Anarchs. The Camarilla managed to briefly step in with La Croix but the death of both he and Ming Xiao resulted in the Anarchs retaking LA.

                                They were, to use a colloquialism, a paper tiger.

                                It was also thoroughly unnecessary too since the elimination of the Ravnos Clan also meant there was plenty of new territory to feed opening up in India, though the local Immortals weren't inclined to let bygones be bygones there either.
                                Anyway, the joke may be on the Jade Courts and Camarilla both as it eventually happened that the surviving invaders finally did stop to talk with the Anarchs after years of brutal warfare. It didn't take much to convince the soldiers on the ground that they'd been had. Mostly because you had to be an idiot not to see the writing on the wall when reinforcements were requested, only to get the response, "Sorry, not our problem" and their passports back to their homeland revoked.

                                The Dance of the Thrashing Dragon and the Anarch Iconoclasts have since come to a bit of an agreement to leave each other alone. They have also cooperated more than any of the Elders of either the Jade Courts or Camarilla would have expected. In simple terms, following the collapse of the New Promise Mandarite, plenty of Wan Kuei gangs now exist in the Anarch Free States indistinguishable from typical Anarch Baronies.

                                How did the Anarch Free States rebuild itself to full power in less than 20 years? Not just the fact Anarchs Embrace faster but the fact Thin Bloods and Wan Kuei are supporting the dream. Someday the Wan Kuei of California may repay their elders for their betrayal but right now, making sure any who try to establish business in California suffer is enough.

                                Oh and perhaps helping their friends in Hong Kong who resist the Jade Courts.

                                                A tuna walks into a sushi bar and tells the chef, "You killed my father!"
                                                Chef says "That's my business, fish."
                                                The tuna thinks this for a moment and says "Very well. Then I challenge you to a game of chess. If I win, you stop being a chef forever."
                                                "And if I win?" asks the chef.
                                                "Then you can feed me to your customers," says the tuna.
                                                So they start to play. Now, tunas love to play chess so in a few moves the tuna has the chef checkmated.
                                                "Looks like I win," says the tuna.
                                                The chef nods and plunges his knife into the tuna's belly.
                                                "                                                But I won!" says the tuna.
                                                "So did you father," says the chef.

                                                ====

                                                This is not actually a random bit of wackiness but the Malkavian Madness Network warning the PC in Bloodlines against siding with La Croix. La Croix has murdered the PC's sire and the player character is playing a game of chess with him. The thing the PC needs to understand is that La Croix is going to murder them no matter what they do, just like La Croix murdered their sire. You should not expect the unjust and corrupt to abide by rules and if you abide by the rules with them, you will get screwed.
