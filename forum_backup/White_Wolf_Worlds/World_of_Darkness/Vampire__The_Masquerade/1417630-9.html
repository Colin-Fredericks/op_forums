                        I get the impression you missunderstand what Tenets are and what makes them different to Convictions. Tenets are the moral Groundrules. They dont have to be part of your Path, they just should not go hard against them.

                            In my opinion, the only thing holding the Sabbat together are the bonds of Vinculum. Their whole philosophy of vampiric supremacy runs counter to working with other Licks that, when you get down to the brass tacks, are competition for your food. Tenets, as others said, set the tone. Dont betray your pack, acting like a human, falling to Frenzy will be different from denying your Beast, treating humans as equal, not supporting your pack. There may be some overlap, but it's going to run different.

                            I always saw the original Creation Rites as a sort mystically induced, personality breakdown similar to how boot camps do it (but occurring in one night of digging out of the grave, vice 8-16 weeks). Before you can build a good Sailor, Soldier, Airman, Marine, you must break them down and then rebuild them how you want. Not everyone makes it (not every Sabbat survives Creation Rites). As you progress in the military, you may be subject to further trials that break you down to build you up how the unit needs you. Paths form a similar function.

                            The Creation Rites break down your Humanity so you can be built up as a proper Sabbat. The original description in the 2E Player Guide mentioned the more you gave in to your Humanity loss, the easier it got to dig out. Kinda went off on a tangent here, my apologies.

                            In a mixed Path pack, there will always be conflict. Whether you have V5 Tenets or the old rules. Some of the Paths do not work well together (it's why the Black Hand is selective in who they recruit). And, to bring it back to my starting point, it's why the Sabbat has Vinculums. Without some kind of mystical enforcement, the Sabbat would rip itself apart.

                                                The thing with tennets and a sabbat game is thus:

                                                Cults do not necessarily fit the whole party, so they often do not work as chronicle tenets. Yet, if everyone's got their own cult convictions, then there's a lot of haggle that needs to happen. Generally, you want to avoid haggle. Not only do cult members need to work with the storyteller, they also need to work with eachother.

                                                Paths, they're simpler. You just need to know the tone of the game and take one. They're in nice, neat little packages.
                                                But like, in general, you've got to haggle for so much in V5. It's so over complicated. Individual discipline choices, convictions, loresheets, you'll maybe get weird with predator types...

                                                Also, paths were stronger. Like, convictions just slow degeneration. Paths actually completely stop degeneration or even reward you for acts heinous to humanity.
