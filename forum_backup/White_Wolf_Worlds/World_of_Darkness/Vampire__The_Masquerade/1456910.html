                                                      Darkening Sky implicitly puts Azaneal at fifth generation. He could also be sixth, if his childe is a diablerist. Or theoretically even higher, if she was a serial diablerist.

                                                      I like the idea that becoming a demon frees Azaneal (or any such ascended Baali, like Mary the Black) from generational limits. The Curse of Caine isn't what they derive their power from anymore, so its limits no longer apply. They gain power however demons gain power.

                                                      Power comes in lots of varieties, like being an incorporeal entity and no longer harmed by sunlight and fire, but in terms of "innate" supernatural power (that is, trait limits), Azaneal could have either gotten an instant boost to 8 or 9 (depending where his generation lay), or he could have just been happy to be a demon who no longer labored under a glass ceiling.

                                                      By the Modern Nights, he's probably stronger than he was upon his ascension, but I don't see him as an equal to Saulot or Kupala. He's probably an 8 or 9 dot figure.
