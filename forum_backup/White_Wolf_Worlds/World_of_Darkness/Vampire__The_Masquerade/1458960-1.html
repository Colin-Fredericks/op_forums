            Let's see if I can create an organized, categorized (but not exhaustive) list.

            1) Historic reasons why Vampires in general might know about the Technocracy:

            - The Tremere and Order of Hermes thing. "My enemy's enemy is my friend."

            - The need for sleepy ancient Vampires to adapt their Abilities. An ancient Vampire has trained in the use of a spear. He goes into torpor, wakes up a thousand years later with the desire to kill a rival Vampire. He grabs his trusty spear, Obfuscates and explores the world, feeding along the way. He discovers that muskets are the weapon of the day and that all those nights spent mastering the spear are a bit of a waste. He might want to use those Mental Disciplines to investigate who is behind technological changes in the field of war and other areas of life. Imagine he trains at horseback riding (with Animalism) in order to be able to communicate with allies faster than his enemies can. He goes into torpor again and wakes up in the Internet Age. Vampires who become aware of technological change would want to make contact with those responsible in order to receive advice about what to expect in the future.

            - Navigation, Astronomy and Shipbuilding Technology. Vampires connected to European Monarchies would have had a keen interest in the development of science and technology that facilitated travel by ship across oceans. There could easily have been Technocracy Constructs funded by ambitious Vampires.

            2) Milieus in which Vampires and Technocrats might meet and have common ground:

            - A Toreador architect meets an It-X Macrotechnician architect at a builders' conference. They are both passionate about building-aesthetics and skylines. Each tells the other a little about their Clan/Convention.

            - A Vampire with a Herd becomes interested in the ability of humans to recover from blood loss, replenish and produce more blood. He starts consulting people in the medical community and meets a Progenitor Mage equally interested in studying a willing Vampire test subject.

            - A competitive Lasombra Vampire wants to improve her chess so she can impress others in her Clan. She meets an It-X Statistician at a chess club. He is in the process of developing the most cutting edge chess learning software. She is more than happy to test it out.

            3) Overlapping interests that more often lead to conflict, mandating information gathering:

            - The Syndicate know how to deal with sleeper investors so that the Convention can steer the global economy in the direction they think is best. A substantial portion of global wealth is held by Vampires who have built their fortunes over centuries. The Syndicate can't easily influence these wealthy Vampires who are playing a longer game than sleepers. Vampires have difficulty maintaining claim over their fortunes due to having outlived their mortal identities. The Ghouls who Vampires use to maintain their fortunes make for easy targets of Magick during the day.

            - When Vampires select humans to become their Progeny, they look for Extraordinary qualities. The Technocracy seeks to bring all Extraordinary Citizens into their fold.

            - NWO's Ivory Tower rewrites history when necessary to shape the Consensus. Vampires who witnessed events being rewritten might object and try to oust the Awakened Academics from their respective universities.

            4) Circumstances under which Mage Actors would willingly supply information to Vampires about the Technocracy (excluding the subject of Mage Ghouls already discussed at length on the forum):

            - If there is conflict between Vampires and the Technocracy in a given area or field of influence, Tradition Spies can advise Vampires about who they are dealing with, what weaknesses to target.

            - A peace treaty is signed, after some conflict, describing what Technocracy interests Vampires agree to leave alone. A Construct whose location was already discovered (attacked even) by Vampires during the conflict is described in terms of the good work it does for Humanity.

            -

            Thank you for passing time with me in conversation. My Hacks.

                    The reason why vampires are frequently seen as the least informed of the splats is just that as the first splat they were developed without influence of others in their basic cosmology. But there is no reason for them to be well versed in the Union ways. But again, things vary a lot among individuals and depend a lot on the ST view on things instead of any objective fact.

                    Auspex can identify Mages at level 2, and is a rather common Discipline. Tremere were a part of the Order of Hermes until the early 13th century, as the Order took decades to expel them even after learning they'd become vampires. Nosferatu are extremely effective information dealers and Obfuscate is just too effective at what it does if you're not expecting it. But above all, Ancillae and Elders play a very long term game.

                    Kindred were Illuminatti-like conspirators guiding human society way before Mages thought to do the same. A lot of them have more than 200 years, which predates the Thecnocratic Union itself, many have more than 400, predating the betrayal of the Solificati, and the entire Order of Reason have about 700 years. A long history for Mages, but there are vampires that have been active for longer than that.

                    So those creatures are long-lived, organized, and wherever the Union spread its tentacles the vampires were already present. They're actually likely to have ghouls and sleeping agents inside the Union proper, depending on the ST. So it was not a joke, depending on the ST those vampires can go all the way from total ignorant about the Union to be in actual control of your bosses.

                                I think they are Kindred who know about the Technocracy, but they are a very, very small group (at most a hundred? Maybe a bit more.) But these Vampires do know the Convention names, goals, and outlooks, some of the capabilities (including the fact that Hyperscience is comparable in potential to Mystic Magick), can ascertain levels of influence relative to a situation, are aware that Technocrats are different from mortals, have ways to contact Technocrats etc. This includes some Vampire Princes and Primogen who have vague (yet strangely profitable) deals with the Technomancers.

                                Then there are some Kindred (maybe around a thousand, maybe more) who know a bit, who have an inkling. Maybe they talked briefly to some Technocrats or agents, or maybe they tried to ascertain some facts from watching the results of Technocrats' actions. But ultimately they miss in major ways with their comprehension of the Technocracy. Some see it simply as some secret, advanced part of the mortal governments. Others think it is just a conspiracy of old money families who fund secret research in science. Those that have some more information just cannot understand that there the NWO and the Syndicate are wholly separate and different. The variety and potential of Hypertechnology are not known about, and Technocrats are assumed to simply be powerful and influential mortals, rather than Mages. These Kindred do have a distinct impression that there is some other group or faction out there which matters and is capable...but they just lack the information and insight required to ascertain facts correctly.

                                In the book Midnight Siege the Giovanni narrator recalls how once the Masquerade got maintained by an outside force despite a Sabbat attack, and that the location of Sabbat headquarters got pointed out to the Camarilla through a reference in the news. Later he encounters an old man on life support in a hotel, who is being wheeled out by mirrorshades agents. The Giovanni Vampire makes brief eye contact and notes that this person is older than anyone who he knows. Probably a Technocrat of the Syndicate or the NWO.

                                From time to time Technocrats do make some rather overt actions that might be visible to Kindred. Say a new prosecutor or a judge is brought into a town, and he not only becomes very influential and hard to budge but also seems to be following a clear agenda at times. And has three bodyguards attending him at all times, just in case.

                                            Originally posted by Muad'Dib View Post
                                            It takes a lot of information gathering and confirmations to get to the truth about the Technomancers.
                                            As I said earlier in the thread, there are several possible ways to work this out.

                                            By Vampire RAW there is no difference in the aura regarding Paradigm, so all the subtlety means nothing. You can add it yourself? Sure. But you can't say this is the only correct approach. The V20 table is unclear if a Mage is detected only when performing or not (previous editions were more clear on detecting Mages regardless of conjuration), but again it doesn't mean one answer is more likely than the other, it is up to ST interpretation. Finally it isn't clear if the sparkles count as shade, color or pattern, so it can take between 1 and 3 successes. Finally it doesn't differentiate Mage, Sorcerer or Magic Merit user.

                                            You can, by RAW, decide that it needs to be 3 successes and only during casting, that even then it is completely impossible to distinguish the kind of magic user, and you will be keeping with the spirit of the rules if you decide that subtle technomancers take far more to even perceive as something strange. But a ST can also decide that 1 success is enough to detect any magic user and 2 or 3 are enough to identify types. Not to mention how much information a vampire with 3 or more in Auspex can glare.

                                            Now, what will the Kindred learn from this tidbit of information? At most I think the number of vampires that heard rumors about those sciency types with weird auras would be greater than just 1k world-wide, but otherwise your take on the subject is pretty valid. It just isn't necessarily the only valid or interesting one, or keeping to the opening post, the one to expect from mezlabor's ST. How can I know?

                                            Clan Nosferatu keep the shrecknet, so I wouldn't dismiss their information so readily. They are professional information brokers and tightly united as a clan on this front, and their databases are digital, so not hard for them to access. Shrecknet was already canonically praised by the Virtual Adepts as a mysterious network they couldn't yet hack into, so it isn't something to be brushed aside.

                                            What I'm getting at is that even with full consideration for the lore across every line, this is far from settled. The books keep from entering too much crossover territory on purpose, so the amount of knowledge and relationships between splats is completely up to interpretation. And in this thread's context we can only try to guess what will be mezlabor's ST take on this.
