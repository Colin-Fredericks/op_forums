    Sorcery, Blood Magic and Infernalism

    01-05-2022, 04:35 PM
    In the supplement for Mage "The Sorcerer" there is some room for conversion from sorcerer to vampire.at page 55, including an example: a sorceress called Chula versed in Shapeshifting, Conjuring and Ephemera that - after the Embrace - developes Protean, Thaumaturgy and Necromancy ( despite being a Toreador ).

    Then, there are some hints at possible crossover between Sorcery and vampiric Disciplines: a flaw - Blood Magic - that force the sorcerer to burn or bleed his own blood in addition to Willpower to fuel his magic ( Sorcerer Revised, page 56 ) and also hints that the sorcerer could have learned magic from a vampiric mentor or from stolen vampiric tomes.

    There's a 5 point merit that allows to play a Ghoul sorcerer.

    Finally, there's a sidebar about sorcerers and infernalism at page 83, including the option to buy dots in various Paths through Infernal Pacts.

    ---

    In the Dark Ages: Tome of Secrets, some room is left for ghouls learning the Path of Blood ( Thaumaturgy )

    Path Magic Path-based magic has appeared in many different World of Darkness books, including Hunters Hunted II. Greater witches may learn the “Hedge Magic” Paths there, along with any appropriate Psychic Numina. Adapting Blood Magic If you prefer, you may also adapt magical Disciplines such as Necromancy, Thaumaturgy, or Koldunic Sorcery into mortal equivalents. Witches might learn rituals as well. Paths and rituals with powers that affect vampires but not a wide spectrum of other beings are generally unavailable. For example, mortal witches cannot usually learn an equivalent of the Tremere’s Potestas Vitae (also called the Path of Blood). You may make an exception for ghouls
    Let's take a look at the Path of Blood, more specifically the level 3 and 4 powers:

    ••• Blood of Potency
    The thaumaturge gains such control over his own blood that he may effectively “concentrate” it, making it more powerful for a short time. In effect, he may temporarily lower his own Generation with this power. This power may be used only once per night.

    •••• Theft of Vitae
    A thaumaturge using this power siphons vitae from her subject. She need never come in contact with the subject — blood literally streams out in a physical torrent from the subject to the Kindred (though it is often mystically absorbed and need not enter through the mouth).

    ---

    So, here's the questions:


    1- Could a sorcerer-ghoul combine his sorcery ( Willpower and living soul ) with the power of Vitae to develope Disciplines of higher level?
    [ example: a sorcerer specialized in Alchemy - 5 dots - with 13th generation vitae in his body and some stolen tomes of Thaumaturgy tries to raise his Path of Blood over 1 dot, probably by "refining" the vitae into alchemical elixirs ]

    2- Could the above sorcerer-ghoul strike an Infernal Pact to raise his pseudo-Discipline as he would with Dark Thaumaturgy?

    3- Could the above freak use Blood of Potency to raise the generation of his stolen vitae?

    4- Could he drain vitae from common mortals by using Theft of Vitae?

    - - -

    Last little thing:

    if a vampire can turn into a mortal through a costly ( 9 points ) Investment, or get some aspect of his curse lifted by a little less costly Merit ( Blasphemous Pacts, 6 free points )..could a mortal go the oppsite direction and gain some aspect of the vampiric curse / power through a Merit ( Blasphemous Pact ) or Investment?
    For example, having a "virtual generation", being able to convert mortal blood into Vitae... mabye also being able to perform Diablerie?

    - beside, I've found a level 5 Dark Thaumaturgy ritual that allows to "drink" the soul of a common mortal:

    Soul Leech: The Infernalist with this power can draw souls out of the recently deceased with a successful Wits + Intimidation roll (difficulty of the soul's Willpower). The Infernalist cannot retain the souls, and drawing them out with this ritual destroys them while replenishing the Infernalist. Each soul thus absorbed gives the Infernalist five Blood Points and heals one wound. The Infernalist also receives a rush, stronger than any blood drinking could give. Souls go through complete agony during these few moments. They appear as vaporish clouds of swirling light wisping around the Infernalist, making terrible moaning sounds for the entire duration. The faces of the dead can be seen in the vapor.

    The next question is could a Ghoul use that Rite to consume the soul of a fellow mortal?

              I simply disagree with the very notion of ascribing a Generation to Ghouls in that way.

              While humans they simply don't have Generation as an aspect of their being. And while you don't affect the Vitae in your system, the level of Discipline permitted by the Generation of your Regnant is how the very concept of Generation manifests in a Ghoul.

              So much so that it doesn't actually depends on that Vitae still being present in your system at the moment, just if it is what empowered you as a Ghoul in the first place. You get a kick from a 7th Gen Regnant, even if you spend the Vitae right away you're still the Ghoul of a 7th Gen Regnant at least 'till the end of a month, arguably for some months even if you refresh on weaker Vitae later.

              Then I'd say simply lower the effective Regnant's Gen and be done with it. And any effect that look for actual Generation still finds the same thing it found in the first place when looking at a Ghoul: this is not a Vampire. If you want to be treated as one... well, you do have Thaumaturgy, figure out some ritual.
