                                                    I finished to read the Guide to the Tal'Mahe'Ra yesterday. In general, I liked. It has an abundant background and metaplot, with some mistakes of detail, but in general I think it's an improvement since Dirty Secrets of the Black Hand, it's ok.

                                                    Now, there are some big things I disagree with.

                                                    The real origin of the Nagaraja. I think it's more credible the original version as a failed intent to get the inmortality, not cannon fodder (a neonate vampire is not better than a experienced mage) for the Idran in their war with the vampires.

                                                    In the description of the clans of the Tal'Mahe'Ra (Harbingers of Skulls, Old Clan Tzimisce, Nagaraja, Maeghar), Where are the True Brujah? I would add a short description (a few lines for each one) about the presence and position of other clans in the Hand (antitribu Assamites, the Ventrue of Cretheus, Toreador, Gangrel et all).

                                                    About the Maeghar. I think they were a bad idea in DAV20 and they are a bad idea now, and I think the Tal'Mahe'Ra is not a place for them. Their presence seems forced.

                                                    About the rules: I like the arcaic sorcery of the Idran, a coherent point of view in their isolation of the Traditions. I dislike the kraina of Koldunism. The kraina of Enoch is Necromancy and the kraina of the Well is Dark Thaumaturgy. A type of blood magic shouldn't encompass the domain of others.

                                                    And the last chapter, the Chronicle of Tal'Mahe'Ra...it's horrible, the worst part of the book (A ghoul or whatever with Auspex 8, Dementation 9, Fortitude 9? Please...It's a joke). A bizarre story that breaks the spirit of the background of the book. And the statistics and features of the characters at the end should be revised.
