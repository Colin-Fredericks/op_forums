        Not going into my personal preferences here but something I came across recently that I figured might be interesting or potentially relevant to those following this thread of discussion.

        I’ve recently been reading the Giovanni Chronicles (the second one is the one I particularly noted for this ad it contains ‘The Lilith Fragment’) and the TMR 20th book and particularly its section discussing Lilian (Bahari) theology.

        Now one could certainly make an argument that Lilith as a symbol of the feminine against patriarchy is rather new in the grand scheme of things, although given her origin from Jewish apocrypha as being a woman cursed for refusing to ‘lay below Adam - Man’ and becoming a demonic figure in patriarchal leaning old Jewish culture, I certainly see where the connection to be made. Plus using Out-of-Universe comparative sources is also a bit misleading imo as the canon origin of the vampiric race is another figure from Abrahamic theology. Plus in-universe mystical texts are given greater weight as being plausibly accurate accounts of reality by canon, due to the history of the meta plot and the gothic nature of the setting and the appeal to history.

        Anyway, those disclaimers aside - let me cut to it.

        Bahari (Lilian - reverence of Lilith as a philosophical and/or Theological figure) teachings tend to paint an ancient sex dichotomy which poses her as a feminine counterpart to masculine figures such as G-D, Adam, & Cain. Lilith is portrayed as a powerful mystical figure whose learned to harness her great power via tribulation. Regardless, most accounts posit her as a wild wise woman who teaches Cain who eschews her ‘crazy wisdom’ in favor of civilization - a classic left-hand and right-hand divide.

        Anyway, all of that said, this may provide additional reasoning for why so many antediluvians lean towards a masculine presentation and how a contrast can be drawn through using Lilian philosophy as a counterpoint to Cainite philosophy.
