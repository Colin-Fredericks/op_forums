                                            For me, it was an excellent plot I give credit to the ST for.

                                            The Ex-Girlfriend

                                            I was playing a Ravnos PC who was a guy who had been down on his luck, sort of geeky, and had gotten "lucky" by being Embraced. He was meant to be a decent enough person, for a vampire, but also had severe issues with self-esteem. The ST and I agreed we'd take advantage of 5th Edition by showing a dark and twisted story about "The Ex-Girlfriend" which would be carried out throughout the campaign.

                                            The short version being that he had as his Touchstone as his High School Crush that had gone on to be the local news anchor woman, who was interested in becoming an investigative reporter. The PC found himself meeting her and couldn't resist wowing her with how he was "dark", "mysterious", and "dangerous" now. This, of course, just inspired her to start investigating him more and he had to end up blood bonding her to save her life.

                                            You can see where this was going.

                                            The PC ended up using her for feeding and erasing her memory of vampirism while also being her "boyfriend" even as he knew that the Prince would have her killed (and probably him as well) if he didn't maintain the Masquerade properly. In practice, like all ghouls, she was his slave. She'd lost her job as well from a violent freak out she'd had.

                                            Eventually, another PC, playing mine's sibling, tried to intervene to make things "better." She used her extensive Dominate powers (that she'd taught him) to restore her memories. This resulted in her freaking the fuck out and becoming psychotically angry as well as DEMANDING THE EMBRACE. Because her own humanity had degraded under the blood and all she saw was the power of being one of the undead.

                                            The Ravnos PC arranged it...as a Thin Blood so she could keep some of her humanity.

                                            Instead, she was furious at being turned into a "weakling" and he had to end up killing her after she tried to diablerize her way into being a real Kindred.

                                            Author of Cthulhu Armageddon, I was a Teenage Weredeer, Straight Outta Fangton, Lucifer's Star, and the Supervillainy Saga.
