    Sabbat characters

    10-17-2024, 09:47 PM
    So some things happened and I looked a little deeper into the Sabbat and ended up making some mini archetypes of Sabbat characters, or not. Basically I would like to know if I got it right what a Sabbat character would be like and if there was any way to improve it. Just clarifying to make it clear, these characters were designed for prose and not as PCs or NPCs, although I think it is possible to make the change. Also sorry for the large text.

    A Ravnos who specializes in infiltration and break-ins, but sometimes prefers a more direct approach just for the challenge. What attracts him is not the prize, but the adrenaline of executing the heist. He has a special fondness for trinkets, especially jewelry, not as valuables, but as trophies. In some cases, he collects them with parts of the victim still attached, such as a ring still attached to the owner's finger, for example, if possible he takes everything discreetly, but doesn't mind starting a bloodbath. He uses animalism in conjunction with bats, but his real focus is on fortitude, which he uses to jump straight into a fight.

    A Malkavian girl who, as a mortal, had an unusual fixation on the color red since she was very young. As a teenager, this evolved into episodes of self-mutilation, which led to her admission to an asylum. After she became an adult and supposedly treated, she caught the attention of her sire, who saw her potential and embraced her. After the embrace, her fixation on red returned more severely, and she now claims that blood speaks to her as if sending vague (and occasionally correct) warnings. Due to her aggressive nature and thirst for blood, she began to wear loose clothing, preferably full of pockets where he keeps knives and other sharp objects that she found or stole. She uses Obfuscate to play small pranks or hunt prey, Auspex to heighten her senses, especially smell, Dementation to shake her victims, and Domination to erase memories or torment others.

    A young Assamite who has practiced with archery since forever, even winning some awards. As an adult, he got a normal job, but archery was still an important part of his life. In his free time, he would go hunting or practice with his bow in the middle of the forest in search of an animal. During one of his outings, he met the kindred who would become his sir and mentor. Impressed by his skill with the bow, he made him his ghoul and eventually embraced him. Devoted to the sect, this Assamite fights for the Saba and his lord because it gives him purpose, but what really excites him is the chance to hunt other kindreds, the greatest hunt of all. Although he excels most with the bow, he has a set of other skills learned from his mentor. He excels in the use of Obfuscate to infiltrate and Quietus to poison his arrows and blades, and also has some Potence and Celerity for more direct combat.

    A beggar who has lived on the streets for as long as he can remember, life has been anything but easy for him, living off the garbage, often fighting for food or just a place to sleep, and he has even been involved in a few robberies. He was found by a Gangrel who embraced him on impulse, but surprisingly he adapted very well to his new condition, even preferring his new “life” as a predator who hides in the streets. Over time, his curse made him develop a hyena-like mouth with a few spots scattered across his body, as well as a muffled laugh that he lets out unintentionally. He uses Protean to hide during the day or to grow claws, Animalism to attract animals when necessary, and Obfuscate to hide.

    A Nosferatu who was originally the Ghoul of a Tzimice and who took care of “his pets” while he was away or during the day. He is good at his job, but one night, due to carelessness, his sir’s favorite pet ran away and was eventually slaughtered. Disappointed with his carelessness, his sir convinced a Nosferatu to embrace him, so that he would be marked for his mistake. Since then, the Nosferatu has had little contact with anyone other than his master or his pets, and even when he receives visitors, he does not speak much, as his deformity makes it difficult for him to speak. His teeth have grown so much that they no longer fit in his mouth, forcing him into a grotesque and unpleasant smile. When he needs to communicate, he usually does so through gestures or through animals using Animalism. He also has a bit of Obfuscate to hide when necessary,

    A rather special Ravnos, during his embrace something went wrong and when he awakened to his unlife his eyes were permanently closed, making him completely blind, but that didn’t stop him. Eventually he learned to use animals to be his guides and his eyes. He particularly likes crows, they are his favorites. They are very intelligent animals and, like him, they like shiny things. He obviously has a good command of Animalism to control several minions and some mastery over Auspex for when they are far away.

    A Tzimice with a rather peculiar disturbance. For some reason, no one knows for sure why, he has gotten it into his head that he is a Gangrel and when someone tries to prove him wrong, or just tell him something different, he goes into denial and becomes quite anxious, in the worst case scenario he can even become aggressive. His madness is so great that he has used Vicissitude to change parts of his body in an attempt to imitate the Gangrel curse. He uses Vicissitude, obviously, and in combination with Protean he uses it to mold parts of his own body into weapons, the most frequent being transforming an arm into practically a bone axe, in addition to having some mastery of Animalism to attract animals, but the real reason is that this brings him closer to a true Gangrel, at least for him.

    A police officer with an impeccable sense of honor and a sincere desire to protect the innocent and the unfortunate, but unable to do much within a corrupt system, he still tries to remain firm in his convictions, until one night he was shot during a seizure, at least that's what they officially say, the truth is that he didn't want to accept a bribe. Admitted to a hospital bed, he was embraced by a Salubri Antitribu, now he wanders the night becoming something different, he still believes in his sense of justice, but he no longer makes judgments as a person, but rather as a Cainite, not as an equal, but something above humans. He has a wide command of Valeren to make his judgments, a considerable level in Fortitude to withstand attacks and a little Potence for when he needs extra strength.

    A Pander who was originally a band vocalist known within a niche, but after being embraced began to collect a significant group of followers who would do whatever he told them, kill and even die for him, as if they were members of a cult. He uses Presence and Dominate to control his crowd of followers or get out of trouble and a little Dementation to play with other people's emotions.

    A Nosferatu who before the Embrace lost his family due to a tragedy, since then he lived on the streets due to depression caused by grief, but one night he was Embraced and recruited for a siege with other kindred, he and a few others survived forming a pack that aims to help each other to survive, but for the Nosferatu this pack became his new family and he would be destroyed before allowing anything to happen to them, little does he know that this has already happened, several times, from time to time he goes from one pack to another always with new kindred, but his mind was so affected that he simply doesn’t notice this change, regularly changing the names of the pack members. He mainly uses Potence to quickly kill or immobilize his targets, Obfuscate to hide or stalk without being seen and Celerity to hunt.

    I just kept writing.

      How do you view the Sabbat?

      i tend to view it as the 'other' Camarilla, ultimately run by Ancients that are playing power games.

      But instead of the Feudal structure of the Camarilla and its Blood Bond, its a decentralized terror Cult that can offer a way out of a Blood Bond and has an option for Elders that struggle to keep Humanity(the Paths). Lower level members are Packs that do the day-to-day Sabbat crap. Upper level is infiltrators and operators.

      Their unlives tend to be shorter and more brutal.

      If we were playing s Sabbat story we decided if our Character was chosen by a Sire, a defector, or a Shovelhead. Playing a Shovelhead was my favorite. The most mundane random nobody can become a monster or someone trying to not become that monster.

          I think that just as the Camarilla has many Cainites who only see the Sabbat as a means to an end, however, there are also those, especially among the elders, who really want to have a countermeasure against the third generation.

          As for its members, besides the obvious inhumanity and brutality, I think of them as being classified into categories based on their survival skills: there are those who are destroyed within a maximum of months after the Embrace, which includes most of the Shovelheads and those Embraced on impulse; those who survive for a year or two; those who survive for three to five years; a decade; two decades, fifty years; a century; after that there are the elders and above them the Methuselahs. As for numbers, for every 10 new Cainites, seven fall into the first group, and that is being optimistic; as for the others, less than half manage to rise to the next category, whether by luck or skill.

          As for how they behave internally, I have an idea, although I'm not so sure, so I made these concepts or mini archetypes above based on the idea I have of a Cainite with amoral thinking or an "inhuman" concept of freedom and they were thinking that they would survive the first few months, but they have less than 20 years as non-living beings, most of them didn't even make it to 10 not that it wasn't possible, but they haven't gotten there yet.

          I hope I have been clear.

                      Honestly, I always thought throughout much of VtM's gamelife, it was kind of the opposite. It's the elders of the Sabbat who don't really care that much about the Third Generation. Stories about the Antideluvians being undefeatable, all-devouring bloodgods are just propaganda they use to convince the younger Sabbat members to die for them.
