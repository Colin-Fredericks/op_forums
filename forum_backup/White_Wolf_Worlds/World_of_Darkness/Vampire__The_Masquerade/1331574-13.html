                                                            Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

                                                            Is Loyalty to Caine truly different from loyalty to Demons?
                                                            If that's a question you would pose your Sabbat players with in your game, I doubt it's a Sabbat chronicle except a very very homebrewed one. It has nothing to do with the Sabbat so many people enjoyed playing since it was made playable.

                                                            Taggie put it perfecty, but I'd like to add something very important. The infernals serve someone. The Sabbat serve only themselves. 'I am my own master, not God, not Satan, only I chose this fate, and I fully embrace what it means to be a vampire'. Loyalty to Caine is rather metaphorycal - it's a loyalty to a role model, not some specific God-Emperor of Vampirekind. It's a loyalty to predatory self vs loyalty to some otherworldly masters.

                                                            The former obliges you to accept both freedom and the responsibility which comes with it. The latter is voluntarily handing your own fate into the hands of the ultimate oblivion, relieving yourself of the burden of self-reliance and the necessity to think and decide for yourself. You are an automaton in service of something hardly comprehensible even by a post-human mind. No true Sabbat can accept that. There are few exceptions, of course, designed exactly to provide additional contrast and a cautionary tale, but making them a core theme and playable characters is not what playing White Wolf's Sabbat is.
