                                              Lore-wise the fae related to the Kyasid is already established as the Changeling from CtD, so I don't think that Oblivion fits in this sense as an "official-like" option. I do agree that it works very well as a combination of Auspex and Dominate powers/amalgams. But then the lack of Auspex would hurt them, and I see some options for a Discipline spread:

                                              Traditional Spread: Auspex, Dominate and Oblivion. It keeps the original spread with all its pros and cons.
                                              Optional Spread: Auspex, Celerity and Dominate. It gives them a physical Discipline and I still find Oblivion not that fitting for them except for screaming Lasombra.
                                              Middle Ground: Auspex, Dominate, Potence. It isn't as fitting as Celerity, but is the Physical of their parent clan.

                                              Personally I would change the powers instead of just adapting them, but I think this also goes against creating an "official-like" option. Folderol and Fae Sight are ok powers, more circumstantial but empowered by the lack of rolls, but Aura Absorption is kind of lame. Chanjeling Ward and The Riddle Fantastique are better on concept than the actual effects they got.
