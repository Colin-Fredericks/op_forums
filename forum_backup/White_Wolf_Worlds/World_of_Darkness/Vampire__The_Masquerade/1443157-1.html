    A big question is how much of a "Masquerade" do you want for this? (Or other-splat equivalent). Batman is relatively low-key compared to even the milder forms of Superman.

    Given "world of darkness" I like the Idea of a Malkavian Batman that drives people mad and turns them into his comic-book enemies; the overwhelming majority of Batman Villains are, after all, mad, kinda like how most of spider-man's enemies are either scientists or engineers or own a company focusing on science and engineering.


    Perhaps not malks:
    Penguin should be a Ventrue, perhaps a Toreador (or malkavian... or a ravnos?) . The whole deformity thing doesn't work as well as a rich man in a dapper suit doing crimes for fun.
    Poison Ivy just happens to know the green path. Perhaps she's an Assamite too.
    Bane is a very erudite Brujah.
    Dr Freeze is kinda the most difficult one because everyone fits so well into vampire* that I don't want to bring in a mage. We could have him a Caitiff inceptor, a rogue Tremere with an Ice-based path, a Koldun... there are options, certainly.

    *Wraith, Formor and demons are close enough to vampire that it's not much of a tonal shift to use them.

    I'd be gentle and 'Batman' would really have a lot of 4's and 3's as stats, none of that 'peak human in everything' crap (well, he could do that with drugs and tech, I wouldn't be adverse to that)

    Animalism 3, Ausp 3, Celerity 2, Dementation 5, Fortitude 3, Flight 2 (needing a gliding cape) Obf 5, Obtenebration 2, Potence 3, Protean 2, Thaumaturgy 2.

                            Peyton Riley (the second Ventriloquist) is a Daughter of Cacophony who uses ventriloquism rather than singing as the Performance focus for her Melpominee discipline.

                            The Eraser (Lenny Fiasco) is the vampire you turn to when you need to cover up a potential Masquerade breach. He will go to the scene of the crime and erase any evidence that might potentially tie it to vampires - or even any sign that it ever happened in the first place, if feasible - and in exchange, you will own him a serious boon in the future. (And yes, he is a real Batman villain.) Clan is probably irrelevant, though in my head I kind of like Ravnos. (Tremere also works well.)

                            Orca (Dr. Grace Balin) is a Gangrel Mariner, marine biologist, and environmental activist. (Because I actually liked Larry Hama's brief run on Batman.)

                            The Clock King (the Batman: The Animated Series version) is an Assamite Vizier/Scholar with an obsession with time-keeping, planning, and minutia. Everything he does is planned down to the second and his ability to plan and predict events are second to none.

                            Warren White, The Great White Shark, a former inside trader and crooked financier who was sent to prison for a long time. He was embraced by a vengeful Nosferatu angry at White losing (read, stealing) his money. Now, with pale white, hairless skin, no nose, lips or ears, and serrated teeth, he runs a mini criminal empire, financing other criminals' (and vampires') exploits in exchange for a cut, all from his jail cell.

                            Dragonfly, Silken Spider, and Tiger Moth are Public Enemies, a punk rock trio well known to the city's underground clubs. No one knows their real names (though they sometimes refer to each other as Phyllis, Roxanne and Mary). Pick whichever Clans you want, though I think Ravnos, Setite and Toreador (in some order) would be interesting.

                                Let me tackle another Mr. Freeze:

                                Victor Frieze was a Tremere scientist who was attempting to experiment with technomancy and necromancy both to resurrect his dead wife. His sire, Maximillian Strauss for a Bloodlines connection, was furious at this dillution of Blood Sorcery as well a needless sentimentality for a past life. Destroying his laboratory and leaving him to burn in the fire, the magic involved as well as necromantic artifacts resulted in him becoming doubly cursed. Perhaps also because his fury awakened something in the blood the Tremere stole from Saulot or Tzimisce as well.

                                The Freeze Bloodline, of which he is the only one, is possessed of a Disciplines of Cryos, Blood Sorcery (Technomancy), and Fortitude with his Old Disciplines being fairly weak. It also has the Discipline of being weakened in great heat and taking double damage from fire or sunlight.

                                Freize is doubly cursed because while he continues to experiment on his wife's body, the Giovanni have already informed him that his quest is doomed. Nora Freize did not produce a Wraith when she died and has gone on to whatever true afterlife awaits mortals (or Oblivion). Instead, he seeks Biothaumatigical Experimentation or other secrets that could only produce a facimile of his wife or a body for something...else.

                                If you want to tone down the crazy (why) he can be a blue skinned frozen man in a trench coat with the power of ice. If you want to play it up, he has created a Masquerade-breaching technomancy modified diving suit from the 1940s to counteract his curse.

                                                    One of my Weird Ideas which I'm unlikely to ever implement in an actual chronicle would be to introduce "Batman" (more likely some similarly inspired vigilante) as an aberrant force (no relation to the eponymous splat) in the setting. He'd be a "normal" human albeit one with peak physical stats and high-tech gear. A challenging foe for a Neonate perhaps, but no match for Vampiric Disciplines. The real interesting question (for me) would be in the reaction of Kindred society to this strange occurrence. A human with billions of dollars in resources waging a one-man crusade on crime..... if left unchecked, he will inevitably begin to interfere with the Kindreds' clandestine operations, which frequently utilize the mortal underworld. Do the PCs elliminate him? Do they attempt to subvert or make use of this singular mortal? As the heir to a vast fortune and business empire, does he unwittingly have any attachments to Kindred assets already? What happens when intrigued Kindred begin competing to influence this odd mortal? Could he end up ghouled or even embraced? A somewhat silly idea, but one which could make for an interesting scenario maybe.
