    TC, part 17: Operation Bulscu F*cks Back

    08-01-2021, 11:14 AM
    So, it's 1437. In the past 24 years, all hell's broken loose.

    The Tzimisce joined the Anarch revolt and spread the vaulderie. Mass degeneration and diablerie. Dogs and cats getting along. Vampires running around saying Gehenna has at long last come. Motherfuckers coming out of the Hussite wars talking about clockwork soldiers, airships, tanks, rotary-barrel cannons and rocket-propelled artillery before vanishing without a trace. Ass, blood and guts everywhere. Assamites making lunch of every real, perceived, or fantasized enemy of their clan across the Mediterranean.

    Among the mass bloodletting were two of the PC's mentors. The Lasombra PC lost his teacher in Abyss mysticism to the fangs of Gratiano's lineage, and one of the Gangrel PC's (the one who isn't Prince) lost his mentor to an ambush by an Anarch pack -- after that mentor who had been an Anarch became disgusted with the Anarchs' degenerate behavior and moved to join the Camarilla instead. Lots of allies dying, too, including those they regarded as their most-liked and best-trusted.

    The Lasombra PC's sire may not be long for the world either, as she didn't just go antitribu, she pulled a Lucita and became an Archon out of disgust for the behavior of Gratiano's lot. Being she's an apostate on the Path of Cruelty, she's degenerating quickly out of the need to not be a complete piece of shit for once in her unlife. This really ends one of two ways for her: Wassail or Final Death, because she's old, tired, done running, and if she's on the way out she's taking as many motherfuckers out with her as she can.

    But at least the Lasombra PC got roped into Constancia's schemes, as she had met the PC's way earlier in the story when they were Vykos' puppets. The PC impressed her for his knowledge of the occult, and now a century or two later approached him to offer Necromancy paths adopted to Abyss mysticism to the best of her ability, in exchange for being her agent and helping smuggle Cappadocians out of Europe. All Constancia told him, is she knows the Giovanni are planning something and as many Cappadocians need to get out of Europe as possible, as soon as possible. What the Lasombra PC doesn't know, is he's helping ferry them to Kaymakli where they can either become Harbingers or Harbinger Chow™.

    No way that can go sideways. But at least they're moving up in the world, having graduated from being the catspaws and patsies of elders, to being the catspaws and patsies of much older elders.

    The Gangrel PC who lost his mentor to an Anarch attack, joined the Camarilla and cashed in his Tremere clan boon for revenge. The Tremere told him they'd take care of it, and a year later he got a new retainer: a gargoyle that used to be the pack that diablerized his mentor. Tremere don't play, y'all.

    So, the story...

    A Transylvanian bishop catches wind of an impending Hungarian coin debasement and suspends tithe payments in full, and when the new coinage enters circulation demands back payment. Transylvanian peasants decide it's torch-and-pitchfork time. A monster Roma migration happens, and Mediasch is all but besieged by the poor, besodden bastards and the city's population suffers.

    And Bulscu rises from torpor, refreshed and ready to open up a great big can of whoop-ass.

    For those of you new to my chronicle write-up, the PC's were directly responsible for Bulscu's fall from grace. The Setite PC muscled in on the "slaves and drugs" trade route and unknowingly became Geza Arpad's new hookup, the Ventrue PC okayed it and told her sire (Jurgen). Jurgen added two and two the drugs and slaves were going to Bulscu, and okayed the entire scheme in a power play to knock Bulscu out of the Jyhad.

    The scheme fell apart when one of the other PC's told Vykos, and Vykos traded the information to Nova Arpad for cash and prizes. Nova gave the information about Jurgen's power play to Hardestadt, and that was the end of Jurgen.

    So, here we are. The PC's are getting the Treatment, they've all but been abandoned by their former Anarch allies, and a great big fuck-off elder is leading a relief column of Hungarian knights under auspices of aiding the restoration of order in Transylvania straight to their doorstep in the heart of Transylvanian Saxony.

    Enter the new Brujah PC. This character is the childe of Eyulf, the Brujah Prince from earlier in the chronicle, whose entire schtick could be summed up as "fuck the Jyhad", his methodology being to hilariously troll the fuck out of everyone. This is the guy who sent Nova unexpected relief when the Tzimisce made war against the Siebenburgen openly, literally for no other reason than owing him a boon would piss her off. Then he plotted to overthrow himself, knowing everyone would naturally assume Nova was responsible, costing her a great deal of prestige and goodwill when the plot inevitably failed. I described him to the players as "imagine Heath Ledger's Joker in Alexander Skarsgard's body".

    Eyulf's triumphant return to Transylvania was walking straight into Sibiu and beheading the Hungarian Ventrue puppet prince, and immediately pronouncing his intent to join the Camarilla. Knowing, of course, this move would royally piss the Ventrue off, but on the other hand forestall reprisal due to the precariousness of the nascent Camarilla's circumstances. So the PC's capacity for introducing unmitigated chaos into the Jyhad has impressed Eyulf, and he sent his childe to get them the hell out of Mediasch before Bulscu arrived.

    The Brujah PC, of course, didn't make it through the Roma unmolested, having lost a number of personal effects on the way including his letter of introduction from Eyulf. He managed to make his way into the city regardless, and were heard by the PC's.

    Of course, before they could execute their "get the hell out of dodge" scheme, an envoy of Bulscu arrived. A Ventrue claiming to be Bulscu's childe, bearing gifts of lupine pelts harvested by Bulscu on his journey, and gold and spices liberated from Ottoman raiders, again by Bulscu. And, a gift of information: Vykos is busy making alliances between Ashirra and Anarch, because it's Vykos. The envoy invited the PC's to remain in Mediasch as vampires of honor and dignity, so Bulscu can look the conspirators against him in the eye, and measure their worth as Cainites before delivering justice. Or they could flee, be forever remembered as cowards, and receive neither quarter nor mercy from the Magyar warlord.

    Not that the PC's gave much a shit about any of that, because they were busy doing math. If Bulscu had only in the past year risen from torpor and his entire lineage save Vencel Rikard had been wiped out in that time, it meant they were being talked shit to by a 7th generation fledgling. Delicious, generation-lowering, math, and among the PC's number was a certain Vitae-addicted, diablerist, Setite warrior.

    Everyone else saw the obvious trap and nope'd right out. The Setite, however, decided to follow the Ventrue out of the courtroom and jump him in Typhonic Beast form. The Setite also has no Obfuscate and fuck-all for stealth, and this Ventrue fledgling with his mighty Auspex of one succeeds in his danger sense roll, turns around, and Dominates the Setite to sit before casually walking away like a boss. The Setite sends his ghouls after the Ventrue envoy.

    The ghouls were actually older and more powerful than the envoy, but despite this the envoy manages to (barely) hold the line until the other PC's hear what the Setite's pulling and intervene. They get the Setite's ghouls off the Ventrue's ass and let him go, then proceed to the more important task at hand: getting the fuck out of Mediasch and regrouping at Sibiu.

    Which they manage in their own ways. The former Gangrel prince has long had an escape plan involving trained eagles that match his flight form, and he executes it. The other Gangrel literally shovelheads the village idiot and leaves the body on the throne before shifting and flying out. The Setite and Brujah use the Ottoman gold "gifted" by Bulscu as a distraction, throwing it out in the streets and causing a riot to slip away undetected.

    The Lasombra packed up his most valuable books, burned his own haven, and started to slip out in tenebrous form before getting intercepted by a wraith. He gets lured into the Shadowlands, and Constancia has a little chat with him about Bulscu and his intentions, before offering the Shadowlands as haven for the day and reiterating the need to help get Cappadocians out of Transylvania. Constancia's wraith ferries him through the Tempest to Sibiu and plops him out in the Skinlands; since the Lasombra's Road of the Abyss and has pointed out he's working to improve his Road rating, I count the experience as a Moment of Truth for him. It's metaphysically the closest he's been to the Abyss since the event that started him down the Path: botching the absolute hell out of an Obtenebration check and being momentarily possessed by it.

    The Brujah, on the other hand, gets intercepted by the Ravnos again. This time it's Vassily Taltos, who returns the PC's personal effects (including the letter) and apologizes for the conduct of his clanmates and fellow Roma, and that he was a collateral victim of the Treatment which was intended for the other PC's. And, Vassily wants to make reparation by offering the tale of Dominic's demise, as Vassily was a trusted confidant and envoy of the Brujah luminary: Bulscu was once Dominic's servitor and confidant (true), Bulscu betrayed and staked Dominic in hopes of the Ventrue embrace (true), and Dominic fell when Transylvanian Saxon servants of the Ventrue discovered his daytime resting place, staked him once again, and left Dominic for the sun (lie).

    The truth being, of course, Dominic is imprisoned in the Cathedral of Flesh, and Vassily is the one responsible, having betrayed Dominic to Yorak.

    So, having escaped Mediasch with their skins intact and having regrouped at Sibiu, the PC's meet with Eyulf who explains the situation as best he sees it. Bulscu's out of torpor, and while he was in it almost his entire lineage met Final Death, and the Magyar Ventrue have been pushed so far into a corner they had no choice but to make peace with the Eastern Lords. In the meantime, Hardestadt founded the Camarilla, and is making great headway rallying elders and Princes to it. So, Bulscu has arisen from torpor in circumstances in which he has to make a sudden, major, power play to ensure he has enough clout to join the Camarilla on his, rather than Hardestadt's, terms.

    Taking out a coterie of ancillae, all of whom are reviled by the Clan of Kings, would be enough to do it. And get a healthy dose of revenge for the whole Operation Fuck Bulscu thing. But mostly it's about sticking a finger in Hardestadt's eye.

    Eyulf offers a third way, join him in saying "fuck the Jyhad". And the first step is one none of them really want to take, but it's the most expedient route to securing their own safety while fucking with other vampires' plans: join the Camarilla. Y'know, we gotta play Jyhad to fuck the Jyhad.

    Thus, two and a half centuries and numerous tales of ignominy later, we return right back to square one: under Hardestadt's boot. One can only imagine how pissed they'd be if they knew who "Hardestadt" really is.

    And...epilogue to the game session: Bulscu enters Mediasch and discovers the shovelhead. What this shovelhead's sire never discovered because of his tendency to abandon childer (Gangrel), is that it fucked with their own and they've unwittingly spawned bloodlines. So, this shovelhead's writhing on the throne, his body wracked by uncontrolled Vicissitude, and he's spouting off all the collected Gehenna prophecy and lore the coterie has collected ever since their trip to Tihuta Pass.

    Including a new one about the impending downfall of the Clan of Death, and when the final true scion of its number falls so does the last hope to avert Gehenna.

    And Bulscu mercy-kills this shovelhead, dominates the Ventrue present to forget everything they've heard, and to summon the Tremere.

                      A brief post-script...

                      ...among my players have been a number of them who have asked me in chat how I'm handling PK'ing in the game, namely how I'm allowing it and how to approach it without starting drama. Funnily enough, their PC's are also the ones behind Operation Fuck Bulscu, and/or have the heaviest stakes in the Camarilla/Anarch divide.

                      It begins.

                      These are also the characters who are currently inhabiting a domain run by a Brujah on Path of the Tyrant, and they happen to be the PC's more useful to that Prince in Final Death than unlife. I did warn them their biggest threat right now are the giant fuck-off elders they've pissed off and settling old grudges may perhaps be for another night, but they seem to have not taken serious note of it nor the plural nature of the word "elders". I have been warning them since the very beginning of the chronicle that starting in the 1400's, I'm turning the heat all the way up, they should expect these stories to be the hardest-pushed they've been in years, PC death can and likely will be sudden and unexpected if they fuck up, and I won't be stepping in as ST to chill things out when and if PC conflict occurs.

                      These are the rules everyone expressly acknowledged and agreed to at chronicle's start. So, I put out a general warning, PK'ing is absolutely allowed and everyone should have backup characters ready before the next game session.

                              Originally posted by Vendrin View Post
                              Is that an internal order of reason Divide? Are the hussites supported by the proto technocracy? And is the order of reason so bad at suppressing rumors that vampires in transylvania hear about it? 😁 Guess the NWO really is needed!
                              It was a little bit of a shit show, yeah. If I remember my TSC lore correctly, The Order was split nearly down the middle by the Hussite war, the Craftmasons and the Gabrielites being the chief belligerents -- Craftmasons supporting the Hussites, Gabrielites supporting the Catholics as one would expect. To the point, indeed, the Order was deploying their best weapons in the war, openly, with regard only for covering up the affair after the fact. If I remember my Technocracy lore right, some of the details weren't successfully memory holed until the Reorganization and Great Housecleaning.

                              In real life, the Hussite war was very much the moment the rest of Europe stopped and said, "what the fuck?" with regards to these newfangled weapons of war, like war wagons, the wagenburg formation, and firearms, and the strategies surrounding them. The Hussites, who were comparatively underequipped, undertrained, and under-supported, were going into pitched battles against some of the best military forces Europe had to offer at 5-1 or even 10-1 odds, and not just winning but winning decisively. It was the first European war in which artillery and firearms were the decisive factor in battles, whereas prior they had only been siege weapons or mere novelties worth more trouble than utility...at least to the best of my reading.

                              John Hunyadi would later employ these very strategies -- and the people who used them -- against the Ottomans, in his campaigns across Hungary and the Balkans.

                              But at least in terms of my TC chronicle, it's a little bit of a (subtle) foreshadowing the "Inquisition" is...under new management. And their threat profile is significantly evolving. And that I'm planning on the 16th Century having much more of a swashbuckling, high adventure, theme as a bit of a palette cleanse/breather between the Black Death, Anarch Revolt, and Convention of Thorns, as kind of a "congratulations, you survived mini-Gehenna" reward.

                              EDIT: Of course, there was also no way I wasn't mentioning the Defenestration of Prague in the blue book. One of my absolute favorite moments of European history.
