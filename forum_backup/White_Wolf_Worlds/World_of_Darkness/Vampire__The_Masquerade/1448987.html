    What are the extra side effects of the Lasombra's V20 Clan Weakness?

    05-19-2021, 10:50 AM
    Off the top of my head, there's the psychological toll of no longer remembering your own face and not readily noticing a change to your appearance, should such a change take place without your knowledge.

    Is there any technology, particularly common everyday tech, that uses mirrors and reflections? Or out of date technology that used to? (Not like, "Lasombra can't function in the 21st century", so much as "Lasombra are occasionally inconvenienced".)

    I seem to recall something about automatic doors using reflections in mirrors to recognise when somebody is in front of them, but I'm not sure.

    The curse doesn't exactly follow scientific logic, but if the curse is because they're sort of walking black holes that absorb light into nothingness rather than reflecting it, could that have any other consequences beyond a lack of reflection?

          Not quite answering your question, here, but for “scientific logic,” I would have had the Lasombra affected by silver, as a counterbalance to the Outer Darkness they tap into. Historically, silver had an adverse affect on all supernatural being, not just werewolves.

          Since early mirrors and photography used silver in their creation, the lack of reflection would be attributed to this. Admittedly, that would be much less of a nuisance in modern times, to be sure, but coupled with additional damage or other negative physical effects from silver would be an effective clan weakness that would stand the test of time.

                          It's really, really awful when you think about it enough. You're going to become a control freak.

                          First, modern cities are filled with reflective surfaces; Large shopping windows, hundreds of cars with rear-view mirrors, wet roads illuminated by the lights of the street. Either you stick to the right streets on dry days or you'll spend your time looking over your shoulder wondering if anyone of a hundred people noticed that you don't have a reflection. Maybe you'll have to trouble yourself with taking care of a few because better safe than sorry. Your enemies know you have this weakness, and they'll do their best to insert MORE mirrors at the places you depend on. The hunters tipped off to your weakness can confirm you're a vampire in a crowd from a mile off.                               Back when they first created the Lasombra and Tzimisce, they seemed to want them to be the Dracula clans. One was Lugosi Dracula, and the other was Mexican Dracula. (Mexican Dracula is widely considered the superior of the two early Dracula movies, interestingly) So they gave them the classic weaknesses of no reflection and resting in their native soil.

                              However, they noticed that Lasombra players were using their weakness as an advantage, as a way to stealth past security cameras. And they haaaated that. Everything about being a vampire is supposed to be unrelenting misery. Heaven forbid your weakness have even a tiny, situational upside. So they've been fiddling with it ever since.

                              This is just the latest fiddle attempt, and boy did they go overboard. It's now an absolutely horrific weakness, especially for a High Clan. It feels more like the sort of weakness that might have fit the Gangrel if they hadn't been given the weakness they actually were given.
