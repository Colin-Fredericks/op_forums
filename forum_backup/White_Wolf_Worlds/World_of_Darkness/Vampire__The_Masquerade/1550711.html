    Running the Free States - 8 - the Sabbat

    05-05-2025, 09:05 PM
    The Sabbat is interested in claiming southern California as well. Like Petrodon, they have their own agents there. So let’s first cover the information provided in the LABN sourcebook itself.

    The most important is the undercover operative Mohammed al-Muthilim, the gang leader of the Sons of the Crypt. He rules a 25 man gang, the largest gang in Los Angeles. I believe a significant number of these - say five including himself - are true Sabbat, the others are just canon fodder he intends to convert or destroy once the Sabbat Crusade is unleashed.

    Another important undercover agent is Mariel St John, one of Fortier’s lovers/brides. We’re not given much background to her before her arrival in the Free States several years ago, but she was supposed to already be a well known vampire in LA when her book on the French & Indian War was published. I assume she’s originally Sabbat from Canada (it would help explain some of her own historical knowledge), but she had to have some kind of backstory that would not be suspicious. Ventrue do keep track of their lineages although I assume this is a bit harder for any Ventrue in LA itself. It would have been better if the sourcebook dealt with this a little bit, but I guess Ventrue do have cases of illegal embraces after all. If so, maybe “Mariel St John” is the name of a mortal she killed and assumed her identity. If we assume Nightstar was an early Ventrue anti-tribu and that Mariel is also from Canada, it is possible Louis and his mistress are consanguineous.

    The other mention of Sabbat is a three Cainite pack in Simi Valley who appear to have just arrived there randomly and is not part of a coordinated plan. They are probably just some nomad pack taking advantage of a secluded area within the Free States. This is probably just an easy story hook so the PCs can destroy the Sabbat and gain some street credibility soon after they arrive in LA. It’s probably what I’ll do, so my design of the pack will be to showcase the bizarre features of the Sabbat. Showcase their inhumanity and bizarre ideology. Maybe show off a signature Sabbat Discipline like Obtenebration. But in the end, the PCs should be able to destroy them with a good plan and some help.

                              Originally posted by Atticus Cleanclaw View Post
                              Incidentally, is there any canonical information on the personality of Maria?
                              I don't think so.

                              It's important to realize like San Diego was not a very large city in 1945. It had only passed the 100k population mark sometimes in the twenties. The city was just above 200k in 1940, and all of San Diego county was 289k. In 1950 the city was 334k and all of the county was 556k. Even with the normal differences in the World of Darkness, there would not be a large Kindred population here. Probably just Maria, perhaps a few of her childer, and some independent vampires who recently moved there.

                              Don Sebastian himself was only embraced in 1870, but as Prince of a rapidly growing city, he's certainly a candidate to be Maria's sire. The only thing against that is she'd be a very inexperienced neonate herself. At least Don Sebastian was supported and mentored by Christopher Houghton. The deserts of the American southwest just could not support a large population of Kindred in the early twentieth century and earlier. So it's equally unlikely any Princes of similar small cities in the region would have experienced childer either who would go there and rule. The only long term vampires who would be there are most likely Gangrel and possibly Nosferatu who were rural based and likely did not rule any urban areas.

                              San Francisco would be the closest large city, but even here it only has a few decades head start on LA. Still by virtue of having one of the best natural harbors in the world and epicenter of the California Gold Rush, it would be the natural attractor for the first Kindred who wanted to head to the Pacific Coast and claim stakes. If Maria was a Kindred of some age and experience, she might have come here first. If she was someone locally embraced, she might still have a few decades head start on any other regional neonates who were ambitious.

                              Still, any neonate attempting to become Prince would have to expect that the greatly growing cities of Southern California would attract competitors to be Prince. Lodin was a neonate when he became Prince, but he was of still potent generation, mentored extensively by TWO wise and powerful elders, and possessed a strong natural talent for politics. He did a lot better than he should for someone his age. Most neonate princes won't hold onto their title long once their city grows to a certain extent. An elder is going to move in and take over.

                              So I imagine Maria would probably be at least be an ancillae or young elder who previously migrated to San Francisco (most likely) or Los Angeles, and wanted to try her hand at being prince by moving to San Diego in the 20s or 30s. I'd probably say she is most likely from Camarilla controlled Mexico who left when the Sabbat consolidated their control over that country during the Mexican Revolution and the burgeoning victory of the centralizers against the Loyalists in the Second Sabbat Civil War. (We should note that the population of Mexico overall was also quite small until its explosive population growth post-WWII. Outside it's Azteca/Purepacha heartland, most of it would be empty. She might be a descendent then of either Datura or Eletria of Veracruz, but could be from any unknown vampires.

                              Maria does not need to be from Mexico despite her Spanish given name, but I think it's her most likely heritage unless she's even older and comes directly from Europe or old Spanish colonial cities in Latin America. Cities like Cartagena, Lima, and Bogota weren't very big, but they were so much older than Southern California cities and were likely settled by Kindred of potent generation. If you really wanted to have fun, you could make Maria a descendent of either Helena or Tyler who dwelled in Cartagena at some point.

                              However, I actually don't think it's important where Maria came from. She's been destroyed for half a century at the time of LABN sourcebook, and she did not leave any legacy. She was likely only prince for one to two decades, and she's mostly a forgotten footnote. Regardless of who she was, there's no impact to the chronicle.

                              Discussion of San Diego is perhaps taking up too much space on a thread meant for the Sabbat in the Free States.

That is simply not mentioned in Revised and later sourcebooks. Many players erroneously assume the Sabbat appears almost fully formed soon after the Convention of Thorns.

                                          It's why I always default to what's in the original corebook as what information should be known to the PCs (at least those not of the Sabbat). If we go back the 2e corebook, this is what's known about the Sabbat.
                                          • It's also called the Black Hand
                                          • It originated as a medieval death cult
                                          • Members worship at the places of the dead
                                          • It requires strict devotion and adherence to the will of the group
                                          • Sabbat initiation is designed to destroy any vestigial will in the new vampire
                                          • They hold vampires holding onto their humanity to be blasphemy
                                          • It is solely concerned with power in all its forms
                                          • All who oppose them are burnt
                                          • They ritually cannibalize their elders so younger, stronger, more aggressive vampires can take their place
                                          That's what I tell any PCs what they know about the Sabbat. Now we can all see the hidden truths beyond these statements, but there is very little to suggest that the Sabbat has any origins among the Anarchs. I think very few anarchs know of any common origins. Sure, very elder anarchs like Robin Leeland know the story of the Anarch Revolt, the foundation of the Camarilla, and the Convention of Thorns. But even he knows nothing about how the vampires who rejected the Convention became the Sabbat. Robin himself would have been one of the anarchs who accepted amnesty and joined the Camarilla only to become dissatisfied later.

                                          I assume at most, Kindred may hear some statement about how only the worst criminals and cannibals of the Anarch Revolt rejected amnesty and were hunted down and destroyed. Decades later the Sabbat appeared, and it is likely those few surviving cannibals and criminals helped start it.

                                          Furthermore, if you are some young Sabbat neonate, how much history do you really know? Or do you just believe that Caine founded your sect in order to defeat the rebellious and tyrannical antediluvians and their pawns in the Camarilla, and you need to exterminate them so you have a chance to survive? Does ancient history like the Anarch Revolt matter when the Sabbat has abandoned caring about its actual causes, and the important story is now about the destruction of two slumbering antediluvians? What does the Code of Milan have to do with freedom? Knowledge is power, and the Sabbat leaders would not want to give that up. The immense turnover of members in the Sabbat means very few have any recollection of the original Anarch Revolt or any access to someone who could (or would) tell them. For most Sabbat their history probably begins with the Revised Code of Milan and end of the Second Sabbat Civil War. Historical memory is oral. They don't have library books and internet articles to tell them independently what happened.

                                          Obviously there is still some historical memory left in the Sabbat, and the Loyalists carry the torch of the principles of the anarch revolt. They might try to find some kinship with anarchs, but I suspect many anarchs just view such statements as a ploy, and if they ask questions about what happened to cause the Sabbat to turn out the way it did, they'll receive some very unsatisfactory answers.

                                          Players might find it interesting to see the difference and parallels (even on these posts we see things like Caine and permitted diablerie come up), but I think the overwhelming number of anarchs would just reject the idea they have anything in common including origins. For anarchs who have little or no contact with the Sabbat, they might seem like a possible ally against a common enemy. For anarchs who have had contact with the Sabbat, they're just another enemy that needs to be defeated.
