        If you are going to run a game exploring slavery in the 1870s, I don't understand why you'd want to set it in (or more accurately, to involve) any Western country when slavery and slave trading is almost universally abolished by then. If the time period and the issue of slavery is so important, you should either set the the story in Africa or in one of the Muslim countries where it is still legal (although the European countries are putting more and more pressure on them to abolish it). Although if you are going to go that route, it is probably best to move the game's setting from the Gold Coast to another area in Africa where the slave trade was still ongoing by the Arabs and Ottomans, such as southern Sudan.

        If you want to keep it in the Gold Coast region, then you need to go far enough back in history so that your slavery scenario is possible which is a pre-Victorian game. Since your scenario involves the threat of a long ocean voyage, it probably requires a setting somewhere in West Africa and a much earlier time period. The Arab slave trade is much more likely to involve land routes, or very short sea routes. Although you could set it in eastern Africa along the coast of the Indian Ocean (using the ports of Zanzibar, Mombasa, and Mogadishu for example). Ships collecting slaves there could travelling to Oman or the Persian Gulf area.

        Also, if you intended the "raiders" to be white, you should know that in the age of slavery, the Europeans bought their slaves quite peacefully from the indigenous African slavers. Long before the Europeans arrived, Africa had quite a large slave market to supply its domestic needs and the Arab slave trade. The arrival of the Europeans just expanded it greatly once slavery in the New World took off. (In the area of coastal West Africa though, the slave market would have been much smaller and underdeveloped before the Europeans arrived since it would have met only its local needs, and not been connected to the Arab slave trade. But that would be moot much later on.)

        ​Your Laibon PCs would have been captured by one of the African states conducting warfare against other Africans for that purpose. If you are going to go that route, maybe it was another Laibon who wanted to destroy your PCs (either specifically because they crossed him, or because he had animosity against rival vampires from that region in general) and thought selling them to the Europeans would prolong the agony before their inevitable destruction.

        To be honest, it seems like you don't know enough about the actual history or culture of the Gold Coast in order to run a game there. If you want to set your game in a historical era, you should do a lot of research first in order to get some details right. I'm guessing your gaming group likely won't know too much either, so it's not like you need that many details. But you need to getting the main parts right. Doing the right historical research and getting the major details right will likely eliminate most of your concerns.
