        The Profane Hieros Gamos formula was completely rewritten in errata. The original version was a level-one ritual, maybe only usable by the alchemist (it's unclear), which changes gender but nothing else. The new version is a level-three ritual, explicitly usable on others, which turns someone into their ideal vision of themself, whatever that may be.                     I lost my post.

                    CTPhipps s
                    Playing with stories like VTM has a very real impact on how we view the world. I don't think it's fair at all to say that whether a transition is easy or not within a story would have no impact on the issues in real life. VTM has an absolutely huge trans following; I've seldom played a game without a trans player at my table, and they enjoy the escapism or catharsis of either playing the opposite sex or by playing a transitioned character who can strike back.

                    In my experience, Trans players have almost exclusively played CIS characters of their desired sex. I see little reason to undermine players who want to challenge themselves with Trans characters by offering them an easy,trivialising, patronizing ritual (That's way too powerful for it's level; looking at other transformative powers this'd be easily at leasta level 5 ritual if you want kid production capabilities) and, I don't know, I can't imagine my Trans players choosing to play "Well, I was born a man, but I met this thinblood and I'm now a woman and it needn't be mentioned again"
                    "It's an unreasonably attractive cis girl or bust" - paraphrasing of someone I've played far too much with.

                    Vicissitude is the ultimate deviant discipline. It horrifies the establishment, and it empowers people to be who they want. It's the most wonderful beautiful allegory for both people struggling to fit in and people who want to stand out. It's the bestest most wonderfulest most creative Dr.Gothic-Punk Discipline ever. It cannot be watered down, sanitized, undermined, normalized or otherwise subversed in todays age. It represents too much. (I'll start imprisoning those who say it should be Protean whenever I get round to building appropriate accommodation)

                    If it were up to me, I'd give Vicissitude "Procedures" (Mechanically similar to Rituals) that let them do all sorts of very specific things with the body, and I'd write several in regarding sex changes. That'd work far better than sorcery both in thematic and practical terms.

                    Taking away Vicissitude and turning it into Protean is about more than just the clan's identity. It's an attack on a sacred cow. Perhaps the gender changing ritual was well meaning, but it completely undermines the discipline of those who want to change their body. (I also feel it's too "nice" for the "World of Darkness") Maybe you've just gotta have a certain mindset to appreciate and understand Vicissitude, it sure as hell has it's in-universe detractors, and in-universe propaganda seems to influence out-of-universe opinions.

                    Summary:
                    -Vicissitude is art.
                    -Art influences our lives
                    -Vicissitude is the best.
                    -Vicissitude is the best thing for characters who don't conform.
                    -Real life people who don't conform are either going to play characters that do conform or they're going to go all-in on nonconformity; I don't believe any magical conformity ritual will be of any need, it just sounds "supportive" to have it in the game.
                    -[Eldest] was an absolute madlad to steal this stuff from the gardens.
                    -Vicissitude should get it's own rituals to represent practitioners experimenting and mastering the quasi-science of vicissitude.
                    -Making Vicissitude a branch of Protean would be the complete wrong direction for all the reasons.
                    -Protean is a low clan discipline for people who wear wolf and moon shirts and sleep outside, Vicissitude is a Chad High clan discipline for transcendent land-owning doctor-wizards with good manners.
                    -Making Vicissitude Koldunism would be... Less bad, but still bad.
                    -This is still relevant to lasombra, because Obtenebration has a lot of parallels with Vicissitude.
                    -You can base an untold number of character concepts on just Vicissitude. No other discipline lets you really do that. It would be an absolute shame to hand off features of Vicissitude to something else.

                    I'm having a hard time expressing my thoughts and feelings on this one. Sorry. There's some stuff I just can't attempt to articulate without seeming like a madman. We are imperfect people. In order to be evocative, you have to be hard. You lose that if you just want to come up with a nice solution to everything you find "problematic".


                    Edit: I was writing this before your second stop post came through, but I really don't want to delete it post.

                                                            Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                                                            Mind you, I feel like the V5 Thin Bloods are weaker than the Revised version. The Thin Bloods previously had 3 dots in Disciplines didn't they? Now they can have none.
                                                            To me, the Thin-Bloods feel like a completely different entity than they were in Revised/V20.

                                                            In the previous editions they were weaker, but still functioned like other vampires for the most part. They took damage the same way (though sunlight was only lethal to 15/16th Gen it still burned them with no option to mitigate it completely) and had normal vampire disciplines for the most part (inceptors were rare) that they learned up to the 4th, 3rd or 2nd dot depending on their generation. Biological activity was a rare side-effect in 15-16th gens of having spent large amounts of blood and not a regular occurrence.

                                                            Now they take damage like mortals, can walk around in sunlight and be biologically indistinguishable from a human, pick up random disciplines from feeding while not (usually) being able to actually permanently learn any, but can mix their blood up with things like nylon or what have you to create what amount to magic potions.

                                                            I don’t even want to think about how V5 intends to implement Dhampirs given these changes (and on the few occasions I get to play instead of run, Dhampirs are my jam). Hunger dice don’t fit the previous lore where expending their supernatural mojo left them exhausted rather than hungry.

                                                            For similar reasons picking up disciplines from feeding doesn’t fit Dhampirs since they can’t actually feed on humans at all, their supernatural energy is restored with time and rest. And a lot of fun with a Dhampir was specifically in having a very limited, but also specific set of tools to use to help you survive in the world with vampires.

                                                            The problem I foresee is that with how they’ve set up V5, the thin-bloods ate quite a bit of the Dhampirs conceptual space while also incorporating things that make them utterly un-Dhampir-like (in the sense of how they worked in Revised/V20) so you can’t really use a thin-blood to play a Dhampir.
