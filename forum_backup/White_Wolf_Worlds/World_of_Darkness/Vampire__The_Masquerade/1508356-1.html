                            Originally posted by Herr Meister View Post
                            Just for the sake of curiosity, do you think the diablerie on Lasombra really worked? Isn't it TOO strange that Gratiano is a nrealy forgotten figure in the Sabbat, even after the deed?
                            I don't run Sabbat games, so that's not really an important question for me to answer. But in a general perspective, I view the Sabbat as a horribly compromised organization that is a poor, degraded corruption of the original Anarchs so my ideas on the diablerie of Lasombra follow that general idea. Gratiano and his co-conspirators only found an empty tomb. There was no diablerie or destruction of Lasombra. They merely claimed to have destroyed him - possibly a result of Domination screwing with their memory.

                            In my chronicle setting, some of the Lasombra anti-tribu believe that Gratiano's plot with the Anarchs was really a plot by Lasombra to see who in his clan were really loyal to him. (We must remember that originally the Lasombra clan was one of the hardest enemies of the Anarchs prior to Gratiano's attack.) Lasombra embraced Gratiano for the explicit purpose to "test" the entire clan. He knew what Gratiano was ultimately going to do. He wanted to see what would happen if his clan thought he was destroyed. Gratiano was his tool to do so. Now he does. So these anti-tribu believe Lasombra is alive and is going to wipe out his entire clan (except for them) once Gehenna arrives. Not all Lasombra anti-tribu believe this, but a substantial minority of them do. Are they right? Who knows? Montano cannot give his opinions because he has been in torpor for the last several centuries.

                            Gratiano isn't a complete non-entity in the Sabbat, but he obviously is not in a leadership role that he "should" be in given his deeds. (At least Lugoj Bloodbreaker isn't a leader because it is known "he" went into torpor soon thereafter.) Why isn't he? It isn't because of some principle - Gratiano was always an opportunistic shit who sought power. It is because he is not actually good at being a leader or politician. He has some talents of course - good enough to be Archbishop somewhere and claims he isn't more because of some principle. In reality it is because he just isn't great enough to survive the politics of the Regent, Cardinals, and Consistory. This really confuses a lot of Sabbat. A lot of Sabbat project their ideals upon Gratiano and believe everything wrong with the sect would be made "right" if he were Regent. Gratiano uses this support when convenient, but knows if he actually tried to lead the sect it'd blow up in his face. He just isn't as good as a plotter as many other in the Sabbat.

                            I do not claim this was "canon". It is just the interpretation I like best for my own chronicles.

                                Originally posted by Herr Meister View Post
                                By the way, you reminded me of something I've always wondered. What was Montano doing all this time since the Anarch Revolt? Some say he suppedly got a seat in the Camarilla's Inner Circle or that he had some kind of protection, but nothing is really clear.
                                Montano should be incredibly ancient. I dislike the idea that he is active as a result. In my setting, he was actually quite active in Camarilla politics early on. If he had stayed awake, he might have even rallied enough of his clan to join the Camarilla and have a seat on the Inner Circle. (One of my setting ideas is that a great number of Lasombra hesitated to join the Camarilla but were not originally Anarch, but eventually decided on the Sabbat as something they could "control" once the post-Thorns Anarchs became organized enough to become Sabbat.) But he entered torpor relatively soon after ("soon" might mean one or even two centuries), and there was no other Lasombra anti-tribu of his credibility and authority to take his place, so they were never able to be any political equal to the traditional seven clans.

                                I think Montano being awake and active leads to too many questions that cannot be adequately answered. So torpor was my best solution.
