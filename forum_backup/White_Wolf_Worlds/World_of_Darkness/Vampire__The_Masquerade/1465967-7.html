                    Consider that when it was released in 1993 we were still rocking on the newly released Windows 3.1, the Internet was still dial-up with AOL and Netscape were the primary means of accessing it and the beta iteration of the pdf had just been shown off at a conference.                     If you wanted to do research back then, you pretty much had to rely on whatever physical books were in your local library. So I’d be far less likely to impute motives like racism to what is more likely just what sources were available in libraries of the American Midwest c. 1990.
