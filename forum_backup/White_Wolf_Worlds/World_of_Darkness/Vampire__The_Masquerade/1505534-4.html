        Heavy Arms I am going to make an addendum post to try and justify my perspective on this issue. Let's look at the V5 Disciplines of the Kindred and what they do:
        •Animalism-control over animals and connection to the Beast. An assertion that a vampire is at the top of the food chain and holds dominion over everything below.
        •Auspex-extrasensory perception, the ability to see things differently and look beyond the surface. As useful in creative endeavors as it is for intel.
        •Blood Sorcery-the usage of the essence of humanity as a mere tool.
        •Celerity-agility, speed, violence. Combat.
        •Dominate-right there in the name, a power used to dominate others to serve your ends.
        •Fortitude-the ability to withstand pain, punishment and domination. Combat.
        •Potence-strength and brutality. Combat.
        •Presence-the act of manipulating someone's emotions for your benefit.
        •Protean-transformation through inhumanity.
        •Obfuscate-deception, stealth, stalking, ambushing.
        •Oblivion-channeling darkness itself and dancing with death.

        These are the evolutionary tools of the vampire. They are oriented around hunting, dominating and killing other creatures. This is why I find the blood bag discussion misguided. Only 1 clan should really be concerned with such things and that's the Salubri, which of whom I feel get devalued by the pervasive Humanity worship and robbed of their opportunity to stand out for who and what they are.

There's only stumbling around in the dark with everyone else. Only chosen decisions, their consequences and whatever meanings and morality one paints onto it (and belief certainly does have power in setting).

Vampires are also not human, so there's room to say that an irrational level of anthropomorphism is applied to them, while on the other hand, as former humans with the equivalent thought patterns, there's the argument that human morals should apply as they will think in human modes (at least initially).

There isn't a good choice here if minimal damage is your goal, but in a vacuum, that's your least harmful option.)

                For your average person most other options are too constrained (video games, traditional acting, etc.) or too open (writing, improv, etc.) to be easily accessible as a fun hobby; esp. if you want to work in a social/communal aspect.                                     There are a few holes in the argument that can change the outcome.

This means that they will be more lethargic, have slower reaction times and be less functional than if they hadn't donated. So depending on what happens in their life, that debuff could kill them or means someone else dies because they weren't in top form.

                                    3. On the assumption that all vampires came out of the coffin and society calmed down enough to start to integrate them. Chances are that the Blood Bank would be in charge of feeding the vampire population. This would give the government a good way of tracking the overall population of cainites, it would also mean that the government could restrict vampiric power by limiting blood deliveries to only one bag per night. Every clan that has high blood use disciplines would either have to go completely covert with their feeding or lose functionality.
