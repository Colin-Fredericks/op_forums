    Originally posted by Pangur Bán View Post

    Just a side note:
    This is established for pretty much all kindred, as getting a messy critical can result in stains on p. 207 of V5 core with the example of a vampire using Auspex and gaining a stain because of a messy critical. I think it could reasonably be argued, the same could happen when using Obfuscate to hide. This feels like less of a thing about the Lasombra specifically but more of a way V5 handles chance interference in the characters moral stature.
    Gaining a higher blood potency for Lasombra seems to be the intended key objective here, since that would allow re-rolling the rouse check and thus avoiding a possible stain. In practice it doesn't make it Lasombra PCs degenerate that much faster than other characters (unless intended), but since the V5 design includes one gaining stains by chance (messy crit, loss of touchstone, …) this does not strike me to be out of the ordinary. Now if the whole concept of potential humanity loss due to chance is not one's cup of tea, well… the Lasombra's slight increase in potential to gain stains would not be the hill to die on in that battle in my opinion.
    Yes and I don't like the fact a messy critical can result into degeneration either, but at least that's something every vampire risks. In Oblivion's case risking degeneration is an intrinsical aspect of the discipline (so only the Hecata face the same problem with the same frequency). I think the developers wrote it that way because the discipline is now supposed to make you manipulate the essence of the Abyss, which is considered to be Evil (with capital E), but even so, making you risk degeneration every time you want to use one of your powers is not the right way to represent such things imo.

                                            I think people are seeing "modern technology" and missing the fact that that term is defined for this context in the section right before the bane, where it also explains the reason why.
