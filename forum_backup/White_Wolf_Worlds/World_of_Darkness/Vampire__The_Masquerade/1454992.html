    [V5] More Tempting Diablerie Homebrew

    07-15-2021, 05:38 PM
    Okay, so story time. I originally wanted to work on a homebrew that gave vampires access to the Elder Discipline Powers and higher attribute/skill dots. However, as I looked at multiple books such as V5 itself, VTR, and V20 I realized something: It’s not needed. Okay hear me out, yes Elder Powers were just amazing, and we should have more discipline power choices but as it stands in V5, the role of Elder Powers is already handled well in the core Level 5 powers, and the higher attributes/skill dots are handled well/better with Blood Surge in this edition.

    With all that said, as I was working on the thought I ended up thinking about Diablerie, as you do, and how odd it is in V5. It isn’t tempting enough. This is an act so awful and evil, but yet so so tempting that we should fear it. In V5, the downsides are just straight up overwhelming compared to the potential upsides. It is so bad that the few times it has happened in game; it was just sad and basically nothing happened. This annoys me, so I rewrote the rules of Diablerie a bit to make it so much more tempting for players to attempt. Keep in mind that these rules make it more tempting, but also carry at enough risk to cause some caution.

    Diablerie +
    The rules for committing Diablerie remain unchanged. However, the rules pertaining to the effects have been rewritten/edited to make them so much more tempting and interesting. They are as followed:

    ■ The diablerist gains 3 stains, which can be reduced to at minimum of 1 based on the diablerist’s convictions. If the diablerist accumulates more Stains than they have empty boxes on their Humanity track, they lose 1 Humanity automatically and all Stains before adding the remaining Stains to the now empty boxes.

    ■ They must then roll a contest of Resolve + their own Blood Potency vs. the victim’s Resolve + Blood Potency. Even if the diablerist fails this contest to assert control, every counted success rolled on the Humanity + Blood Potency roll gives them 5 experience points. These points can be saved for later or spent immediately, but can only be used to increase Blood Potency (to a maximum up to the victim’s Blood Potency) or on disciplines known by the victim.

    ■ Should the roll fail, the diablerist gains an additional number of stains for each success by which they failed. If the diablerist accumulates more Stains than they have empty boxes on their Humanity track, they lose 1 Humanity automatically and all Stains before adding the remaining Stains to the now empty boxes. If this would corrode their Humanity to 0 (zero), their prey’s mind replaces the diablerist’s; they become the host body of the Blood and personality of their target, becoming an SPC.

    ■ If the victim was of lower generation, the diablerist lowers their generation by one for every 3 generations of difference (minimum of 1). If the diablerist was of a generation higher than 14th (Thin-Blood), they will always be considered of 14th generation for this purpose.

    ■ Black veins become visible in the diablerist’s aura. They persist for a year, or if the diablerist’s generation was higher than their prey’s, they persist for a number of years equal to the original difference in generation.

    ■ The diablerist must make a Composure + Resolve roll at a difficulty of the victim’s Blood Potency. On a failure, the diablerist becomes addicted to the sensation of Diablerie and the taste of vitae. From that point forward, whenever the diablerist tastes of kindred vitae they must make a Hunger Frenzy with a difficulty equal to the number of times the diablerist has commited Diablerie (This is 2 + Bane Severity + The number of past Diablerie for diablerists of clan Banu Haqim). During a hunger frenzy, even one not provoked by kindred vitae, a diablerist specifically will seek vampiric vitae first and foremost, only settling for fresh mortal blood if no avaliable kindred are in the immediate area.

                                  IDK why it is from a design perspective, but from RAW in V5 there is a rule for gaining and losing advantage dots specifically. To summarize, if you gain a dot in an advantage, a merit or background, you lose it after the story is over unless you spend EXP to keep it. This is because in V5 advantages cost 3 exp per dot to get after character creation, likely to give players more agency in how their story develops or something IDK.

                                  On the other hand, if you lose an advantage/flaw, yes to also applies to flaws, you'll either get to pick another advantage or get a refund in EXP or dots depending on if you spent EXP on it or got it in Character creation.

                                  Most STs for V5 specifically will apply these rules to other things as well, but nothing else in V5 gets temporary dots other then advantages and disciplines for Thin-Bloods, which only lasts between feedings, so it doesn't tend to come up much.

                                  So if I had to guess why they don't give away free dots is to give a sense of game balance even if you can get EXP whenever the ST wants to have it.

                                              Which reminds me.

                                              Blood potency is mechanical cancer in V5. You really don't want it beyond a certain amount (I think it was 5, but i haven't looked at the table). The Drawbacks far outweigh the merits. If you want Diablerie to be desirable, you need to curb these side effects and make Generation/potency a more-pros-than-cons deal. Reduce/Axe how hard it is for high potency vampires to sate hunger, reduce/axe bane severity.


                                              You should also get rid of TBA, or at least port TBA into blood sorcery that anyone can learn. You absolutely don't want TB's to have a comfortable little nieche for them to occupy. The TB's aren't meant to be in a happy place, mechanically they need to be encouraged to eat their way up, or to try and become human again. Giving them 3/4 discipline caps again might be the foundation they need to become invested in the vampire system and give them the impetus to diablerise when they hit their early limits. Scrapping the "you get a clan when you eat up" rule is also wise. Having them remain Caitiff when they reach 13th gen is also good, since Caitiffs are mechanically advantaged.
