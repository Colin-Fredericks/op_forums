                    IMHO Bloodlines is a decent game. I use Ashcroft as a hometown of one of my player's character (love the town's history). I have to admit that I'm considering buying PS2 only for this game (to copy map of Ashcroft also and check if there is some info in which state it lies). As for Reckoning I think it's a wasted chance. The idea of chasing true love for 800 years is great, but after spending this time under the debris would drive the main character crazy. Also I don't like hack&slash games and gameplay of Redemption is like Diablo's: clean the level, back to town, sell items and go to another level. Not to mention historical/continuity error.

                    As for Kindred I think it was quite good, especially Fiori-Luna conflict and the ending. And by lucky coincidence I got a copy of "A fatal case" book, written after Mark Frankel's death by somebody of the crew describing Julian's history and wereabouts after the end of the show. It wasn't published thought, only printed in maybe 50 copies to distribute among the rest of the crew.

                    I don't know how well you know Werewolf setting but if you do watch this:

                    https://youtu.be/rSN6Cbct2GQ


                    All cutscenes from cancelled Werewolf video game. After some upgrades it became great adventure for one of my players (however didn't end well). Now I'm dying to see this upcoming W:tA game.

                                                    Ok, I didn't say that W:tM-R was entirely bad. I just think that a big potential has been wasted. When I heard for the first time about this game I thought that it will be something like Transylvania or Giovanni Chronicles, a few levels for each century and cutscenes describing downtime. As for NPCs it has some non/ anti canon elements (for example Azhra is described in Transylvania by Night and is a very old and powerful Cainite resembling Vukodlak). But there were some interesting characters like Luther Black or Otto (London arms dealer). Another flaw: some historical inacuracies (the game is set in 1148, and Swordbrethren were founded almost 60 years later and, as far as I know, cut their hair short, grew beards and never fought in Bohemia). But I still used NPCs during my games. I love mix of video games and tabletop. During my campaign Serena (from V:tM-R) as a PC survived Transylvania and Giovanni Chronicle (yeah, I merged them; PCs from each of them met in Silchester), when Christof awoke in London she rushed to meet him (she was late). There she almost met Ryan McCullough (from cancelled Werewolf: the Apocalypse Heart of Gaia game; he was the one who attacked Christof near the Tower of London; I assumed that after the opening cutscene from Heart of Gaia he attacked the first creature he intinctively sensed as an enemy). Ryan in turn was born in Ashcroft (from Hunter series) just like Heather Poe (from Bloodlines, IMHO it is very possible that they are the same age). That's why he attacked PC from Bloodlines, out of pure jealousy. Heck, I even used Football Manager series so my players could bet (and Diarmid "Mad Dog" Dunsirn from pregenerated characters for V:TM is an ex Celtic Glasgow coach) and Truck Driver game when my characters was a driver (he also participate in underground street racing circle so I could use NFS series ]:-> ).
