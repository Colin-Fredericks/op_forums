                The Tremere and the Nagaraja were mortal mages (respectively founding members of the Order of Hermes and a small subsect of the proto-Eutanatoi Chakravanti mages, called the Idran) who found a way to manipolate vampire blood turning themselves into vampires without needing the embrace.

                The Giovanni on the other hand were a family (or according to DAV20 a sort of business/criminal enterprise between likeminded young people) of renowned necromancers and traders who Cappadocius wanted to include into his clan for their knowledge of Necromancy. Augustus Giovanni accepted the offer of the embrace by the eldest Cappadocian himself and his bloodline was thus created by "normal" kindred means via the embrace.

                Edit: as for the Antediluvians and diablerie. Tremere diablerized Saulot, lowered his generation but then realized Sauolt allowed this and was trying to devour his soul and take control of his House for yet undisclosed reasons. Therefore the attempt at diablerie was not completely successfull. Tremere fled his own body at the end of the 20th century but might again be thrid generation due to a ritual he enacted by sacrificing the whole of the Tremere antitribu.

                Augustus Giovanni was allowed to diablerize Cappadocius because the latter vampire believed itwas a necessary part of a ritual he was trying to enact to "diablerize God". Cappadocius failed, Augustus Giovanni became a 3rd generation vampire BUT he did not consume the ancient's soul. It instead became a wraith and after a few centuries a spectre. This left Augustus as a Vampire with the potential of a 3rd generation vampire WITHOUT the knowledge of an antediluvian. Again the diablerie attempt was only partially successfull.

                    Where do the Capadocians have presence?

                    It really, really, really doesn't suit them.

                    The whole Giovanni eats the capadocians is really allegory for how well organized aggressive capitalists can defeat a lot of complacent, inoffensive, old fashioned competitors in their field. Big business taking on out-of-touch traditionalists. It'd be weird for those no-connections corpse people to have presence. They're not meant to be likeable.

                    Fortitude, Auspex and necromancy were excellent at defining the Capadocians. If I had to replace a discipline of theirs, I'd pick obfuscate. Potence and Dominate, meanwhile, were perfect for the Giovanni, even if it makes them lasombra clones, it fits them wonderfully (The potence compensates for the clan weakness, it also shows them as aggressive, Dominate is just the perfect social discipline for how they run things)
