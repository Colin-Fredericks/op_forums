                                    Originally posted by monteparnas View Post
                                    Oh, not just that, but I'm thinking if the Japanese presence in Hawaii have any impact in this pop picture of the Japanese overtaking the West Coast.
                                    I think it was just the logical extension.

                                    WW released the KotE books, but there were complaints that those books were mostly useless unless people were setting games specifically in Asia. If your games were mainly set in the US (which most games at the time were) why would you bother purchasing the KotE gameline? So WW introduced a story element to bring the eastern kindred to the US where they would be more available for gaming, and also as a way to encourage people to buy the books. Of course, this being the World of Darkness where everyone is at one another's throats in an eternal conflict, that ended up being an effective invasion. And the East Coast was chosen as opposed to say, the Deep South because of demographics - that is, there were much larger existing Asian-American communities on the East Coast than there in other parts of the US.

                                    Basically WW wanted more people to buy the KotE books they were publishing, so they had an event take place in one of the more popular parts of the setting to get people to buy the books.
