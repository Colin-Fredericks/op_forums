                Reasoning

                In my opinion it makes sense to stat the Antediluvians. There are a number of canon stories that have the PCs interacting with Antediluvians, even at comparatively young power levels, and there are points where an Antediluvian's location isn't unknown.
                • Transylvania Chronicles
                  • Tremere in the Dark Ages where the PCs are neonate-ancillae power
                  • PCs can be present at the "diablerie" of the Eldest (Tzimisce) at Sernog monastery
                  • Tremere again in the modern era (in the body of Etrius)
                  • They interact with the Jyhad maneuvers of Saulot and Absimiliard
                • Giovanni Chronicles
                  • They are involved in the events around the "diablerie" of Cappadocius
                • Fountains of Bright Crimson
                  • PCs interact with the blood of Malkav and a being who appears to be a friend to Malkav
                • General Antediluvian locations and interactions
                  • Until his "diablerie" [Lasombra] was generally within the Castle of Shadows in Sicily
                  • Cappadocius' embrace of Augustus
                  • Tremere in Ceoris
                  • Arikel rumored to be in Greece
                  • The Eldest's form in the tunnels of New York
                  • Saulot in Anatolia
                  • Set in Egypt (circa the era of the destruction of Knossos)
                  • Troile in and beneath Carthage

                So the idea that it's impossible to interact with an Antediluvian is counter to what's been presented in the metaplot already. As mentioned having stats means you have a meaningful measure of how the PCs interactions can go, and make it more than just relying on pure fiat. There is an argument to be had for using fiat when interacting with such beings (some of the aforementioned metaplot references do just that) but I always found that to be somewhat unfulfilling. With the reasoning for statting Antediluvians laid out we can move on to actually concocting those stats.

                Methods

                So I looked at a few methods for creating Elder characters and they either ended up with too many dots (where you can just assume a 10 in all stats), too nebulous an amount of dots, or so few dots as to not even get 9s in clan disciplines (see the spoiler for some of the attempts). I used the Eldest because it's one of the two presented in the original post, I like the Tzimisce as a clan, and the history of the clan is such that there is a lot of room for non-clan discipline discussion. So instead I ruminated on it for a bit and came up with a method that keeps some variation among the Antediluvians while still making them feel potent compared to any other character. Much of these are based in comparison to the stats of Japheth, being thousands of years older than that being they should be greater in scale.
                • Core Concepts and Themes
                  • As ancient beings a single concept may not fit the many activities that could fall under their aegis. As such determine what sort of concepts would fit their existence. Additionally determine what sort of thematic elements would be associated with the Antediluvian.
                • Nature and Demeanor
                  • Assign a nature appropriate to the Antediluvian's Core Concepts. Their Demeanor is whatever is appropriate for the moment, these are beings that have participated in the Jyhad before recorded history and have theoretically played at nearly every role conceivable, so it stands to reason they may put whatever front they feel is most beneficial to them forward.
                • Attributes
                  • Assign the following values based on the Core Concepts, the exception being any stats that would be zeroed (ie Absimiliard and Appearance, at which point assume the lowest available value was assigned). The Antediluvians have been around so long that I consider it safe to assume their baseline Attributes would be above what a character made using base V20 or base DAV20 rules could achieve, additionally this puts them above Japheth's canon Attribute listings. The values also give them potent strengths while being more nuanced than flat 10s
                    • 10, 10, 9, 9, 9, 8, 8, 8, 7
                • Abilities
                  • Choose three Abilities related to the Antediluvian's Core Concepts, they start at 10. Then assign the following values to other Abilities that fit Core Concepts and Themes
                    • three 9s, three 8s, three 7s, three 6s
                  • Prioritize the remaining Abilities as Primary, Secondary, and Tertiary. Primary starts with 5 in each, Secondary with 4, and Tertiary with 3.
                  • If there is an appropriate Secondary Ability in play that fits the Core Concepts or Themes then assign a value of 10.
                  • An exception could be modern age abilities if you feel such ancient beings shouldn't have them so easily. In that case reduce as desired, or use the Vampire the Dark Ages trait listings and assign values you feel are appropriate to modern abilities.
                  • Essentially this means half the Abilities are at elder values, which I feel well reflects what would be the many activities these ancient beings might have flitted upon to pass the time. Otherwise barring some reason for a deficiency the sheer time they've had to do everything would put them above the average.
                • Disciplines
                  • The three Clan Disciplines start at 10.
                    • When you have thousands of years you're going to achieve absolute mastery in the powers that your vitae has affinity to. The exact nature of level 10 Disciplines varies from those power found in the early Players Guide to the Plot Device of Gehenna.
                    • Generally I view level 10 Disciplines being closer to Plot Device. Essentially they can produce any effect that might be plausible to fit the frame of what the Discipline does. They can of course be countered by appropriate other level 10 Disciplines. Should there need to be an adjudication have both Antediluvians make rolls appropriate to the Discipline and compare the result, the one with more successes wins.
                      • One exception to this challenge is if more than one Antediluvian attempts to counter an effect (or make an effect occur). The group with the larger number succeeds. This also offers a convenient explanation for how the Third Generation defeated the Second. For every action the Second attempted there were at least two of the Third to counter and strike back.
                      • The other exception is Caine. For me Caine is a true Plot Device. If the story says something should be done or undone, especially involving Cainites, Caine can will it so. This is not to mean that he can never be interacted with, merely that whatever success or failure occurs during that will have dramatic story effects. Whether you ascribe to single or cyclical Gehenna, Caine getting directly involved (and it is known that it is Caine, I always assume he keeps some eye on humanity and his wayward lineage) is a Gehenna level event.
                  • The three physical Disciplines (Celerity, Fortitude, Potence) start at 7, unless it's a Clan Discipline.
                    • These are so similar to the quickening of the blood that there is no reason even those Antediluvians who follow mental pursuits wouldn't have developed them to beyond what a starting V20 or DAV20 character's potential. It's also quite likely they would have been some of the first Disciplines developed and thus the longest extant.
                  • The other common psychic Disciplines (Auspex, Dominate, Obfuscate, and Presence - this concept comes from Time of the Thin Blood for making Dhampirs) start at 6, unless it's a Clan Discipline.
                    • Similar to the above physical Discplines these have been around for ages and are available to many clans, it stands to reason that every Antediluvian would have achieved the barest elder powers.
                  • If the Antediluvian does not have a form of Blood Magic among their Clan Disciplines select one appropriate to their Clan, region, or Dur-An-Ki that starts at 5. Their primary path is either one that fits their Core Concepts, Theme, or when in doubt default to either Weather Manipulation or Movement of the Mind; the former would have been useful along the ages for agrarian societies while the latter would reflect inhuman mental capacity. Select one Ritual per level that fits Core Concepts, Themes, something useful to agrarian society, or something that fits inhuman mental capacity. Assign a 3 dot value to any secondary paths that would be appropriate to their Core Concepts or Themes.
                    • Quite simply Blood Magic has been around for too long for such ancient beings to not have dabbled in them at some point, especially since it's implied they were worshipped as gods in ancient times.
                  • Assign another 40 dots to Disciplines, these may be added to any of the values above as well.
                    • Generally avoid bringing other Disciplines to 10 unless there is some Core Conceptual, Thematic, or metaplot reason to do so. No Antediluvian should have more than 5 Disciplines at 10.
                    • Keep in mind any Disciplines available to pre-modern Bloodlines of the Clan, you should probably assign some dots to those to reflect some blood affinity.
                    • Do not worry about the idea of Clan specific Disciplines, the Antediluvians are so ancient and kin to each other that if there was something they desired they could have gathered it from some poor whelp (possibly a source of interaction for a character, especially in pre-modern settings)
                  • Assume the Antediluvian possesses any alternate Discipline powers that fit its Core Concepts and Themes.
                  • Combination Disciplines
                    • Feel free to select any combo Disciplines that fit Core Concepts or Themes
                      • The Antediluvians have had millennia to experiment with their Disciplines, if there's a way for them combine them that fits their mindset they would discover it.
                      • Just because they don't display proficiency with a Discpline at some point in the metaplot doesn't mean they haven't dabbled in them.
                • Backgrounds
                  • Given their disparate natures it's harder to pin down their connections to other Cainites and the world around them. This is one of those things that is going to have to be adjudicated by the Antediluvian's Core Concepts and Themes.
                • Virtues
                  • Unless there is a Core Conceptual or Thematic reason for these to be reduced they start at 5. These are beings ancient enough that unless they have decided to walk a different path in the modern era they have been supremely self assured long ago.
                • Willpower
                  • Unless there is some compelling Core Conceptual or Thematic reason to be reduced Permanent Willpower starts at 10. These are beings that have survived the wrath of Caine's curses, the Great Flood, and millennia of ages such feats would instill the most supreme of confidence. Fortunately with temporary Willpower being able to fluctuate there's some variance.
                • Humanity/Path/Road
                  • Set to a value appropriate to their Core Concept or Themes. In general assume very high or very low values depending on whether they are currently adhering to their morality or letting it fall to the wayside as they listen to their beast.
                • Merits and Flaws
                  • Because we won't be using Freebie Points for this system, assign Merits and Flaws that fit the core concepts of the Antediluvian as well as anything else thematically fitting or story necessity.

                Okay so now that we have our method laid out let's try building the Eldest. I will be using the modern ability list since it's been implied the Eldest has been learning since its was beneath New York. For Koldunic Sorcery I give values for both the Kraina system and Ways system.
                • Core Concepts and Themes
                  • Core Concepts Oracle, Koldun, Fleshshaper
                    • As a mortal the Eldest was an Oracle, since then it can be assumed to have been a Koldun and Fleshshaper.
                  • Themes Change, Rebirth, Experimentation
                    • The basic tenets of Metamorphosis and Azi Dahaka are built around change and experimentation. The Eldest has been reborn at least once via Dracon and in theory again after New York.
                • Nature
                  • Enigma - If there's any being whose motives seem inscrutiable, it's the Eldest.
                • Attributes
                  • Strength 8, Dexterity 10, Stamina 8
                  • Charisma 7, Manipulation 9, Appearance 9 (subject to change pretty much at the Eldest's whim)
                  • Perception 10, Intelligence 9, Wits 8
                    • Being an Oracle I set its Perception to 10, being a Fleshshaper I set its Dexterity to 10, Intelligence 9 and Manipulation 9 fits the Koldun, Appearance 9 fits the Fleshshaper. Being so focused on its own transcendence it has least capability in appealing to others to please others.
                • Abilities
                  • (Secondary) Alertness 10, Athletics, Awareness 8, Brawl 8, Empathy 9, Expression 6, Intimidation 8, Leadership 7, Streetwise 4, Subterfuge 9
                  • (Tertiary) Animal Ken 9, Crafts 3, Drive 3, Etiquette 6, Firearms 3, Larceny 3, Melee 3, Performance 3, Stealth 3, Survival 7, Body Crafts 10 (if using Secondary Attributes or DAV20 Vicissiutde rules)
                  • (Primary) Academics 7, Computer 5, Finance 5, Investigation 6, Law 5, Medicine 10, Occult 10, Politics 5, Science 5, Technology 5
                    • Befitting an Oracle its Alertness is 10, a Fleshshaper it's Medicine is 10, and a Koldun it's Occult is 10. Animal Ken 9 fits a Koldun as the lord of the land their tied to, Empathy 9 fits that an entity continually changing and being reborn would have insight into the motives of others and Subterfuge 9 represents being able to think and act like any one of those.
                    • Computer, Science, and Technology being at 5 might at first seem incongruous, but as an Oracle, and assuming Beckett's Jyhad Diary is correct, the Eldest has spent quite some time learning and absorbing information about the modern world. If you feel this is inaccurate simply make Talents Primary, Skills Secondary, and Knowledges Tertiary
                • Disciplines
                  • Clan - Animalism 10, Auspex 10, Vicissitude 10
                  • Physical - Celerity 7, Fortitude 7, Potence 7
                  • Common Psychic - Dominate 10, Obfuscate 7, Presence 6
                  • Blood Magic - Koldunic Sorcery 10
                    • Primary Kraina - Kraina of the Well (V20 Black Hand Guide to the Tal'Mahe'Ra) 5 or Primary Path Way of Earth 5
                    • Other Kraina/Ways - Transylvanian Kraina 5, Genius Loci 5 or Way of Wind 5, Way of Spirit 5
                    • Rites/Rituals - All Transylvanian Kraina and Genius Loci Rites or Any Koldunic Ritual applicable to Core Concepts, especially those dealing with Spirits/Demons.
                  • Other Disciplines - Protean 9, Valeren (Healer)/Obeah 3, Necromancy (Bone) 3, Serpentis 3, Obtenebration 2, Chimerstry 3, Sanguinus 5, Thaumaturgy (Blood) 1
                  • Discipline Expenditures
                    • Koldunic Sorcery +5 (5/5) - Obvious for a Koldun, also ties to the metaplot of its interactions with Kupala.
                    • Dominate +5 (5/10) - Pretty much everything points to the Eldest having discovered Vicissitude later in its existence, with Dominate having been its original third Clan Discipline.
                    • Protean +9 (9/19) - Being so close to Vicissitude I would assume the Eldest would have studied it and Inward Focus feels like a very good starting point for the kind of internal understanding to lead to the creation of Vicissitude. It fits the Core Concept of Fleshshaper and the Theme of Change.
                    • Obfuscate +1 (1/20) - Veil of Blissful Ignorance seems the perfect explanation for how the Eldest impaled Lugoj without anyone save Lambach noticing, even if he were 6th generation he could have Auspex 7.
                    • Valeren (Healer)/Obeah +3 (3/23) - As a fleshshaper it makes sense that the Eldest would learn a power that can repair it. Tied to the Theme of Rebirth. A being with Vicissitude has no problem dealing with the third eye.
                    • Necromancy (Bone) +3 (3/26) - The manipulation of bone, even those of the dead, could intrigue the mightiest fleshshaper.
                    • Serpentis +3 (3/29) - Another Discipline that warps flesh and thus might be a dalliance of the Eldest's/
                    • Obtenebration +2 (2/31) - Assuming metaplot of it absorbing knowledge from its descendants, a few of which would have learned Obtenebration from their Sabbat Lasombra cohorts, and be present in the shared vitae of the Vaulderie.
                    • Chimerstry +3 (3/34) - Since the Dracon spent ages with the Patriarch Michael, it stands to reason that being may have learned some of the Discipline that Michael made such use of. The Eldest being reborn of the flesh of the Dracon could have easily picked up on this proficiency. Also tied to the Theme of Change.
                    • Sanguinus +5 (5/39) - Born of vicissitude and fitting the metaplot point of the thing beneath New York absorbing things. I like the concept of this being instinctively developed during that time.
                    • Thaumaturgy (Blood) +1 (1/40) - Between the number of Tremere who were slain by the Tzimisce during the Dark Ages and tied to the idea of absorbing knowledge and learning it seems a decent dalliance for the Eldest to learn the most basic skill of his clan's foe.

                  • Combination Disciplines
                    • Any involving Vicissitude.
                    • See the Reflected Form - As and Oracle and Fleshshaper those who cloak themselves in a form not their own will not fool the Eldest.
                    • Empower Minion - Stories of the Bogatyri could be signs of this power at work.
                  • Backgrounds - Retainers 5
                  • Virtues - Conviction 5 Instinct 5 Courage 5
                  • Humanity/Path/Road - Path/Road of Metamorphosis 10
                  • Merits & Flaws
                    • Merits
                      • Bioluminesence, Dracon's Temperament, Haven Affinity, Promethean Clay, Oracular Ability
                    • Flaws
                      • Faceless, Hunted Like a Dog (Sabbat), Clan Enmity (Tremere), Methuselah's Thirst
                    • Special
                      • With its mastery of Vicissitude it can be assumed that the Eldest may adopt any Physical Merits or Flaws that it so desires.

                Conclusions

                Obviously this method produces some prodigiously powerful NPCs, but that's kind of the point. You're building entities that pre-date recorded history and interacted with a being that was directly cursed by god. That said if you wanted less powerful elders just reduce the number of automatic dots by an appropriate amount (for example the automatic dots in the Common Physical and Psychic Disciplines could be lowered and thus lead to less broad Discipline spreads).

                They aren't supposed to be easily defeated by most elders, it's why of the five known diablerie attempts in theory only 1 of them succeeded being Troile (though it's possible that was planned by Illyes), otherwise Saulot fought for control of Tremere's body (and eventually won), its implied [Lasombra] used the attack to slough off its frame and become a being of the Abyss, the Eldest faked its destruction, and Cappadocius sought apotheosis and thus part of his soul was kept away from Augustus (leading to the theory that Augustus isn't actually 3rd Generation instead actually 4th until he can consume that last fraction of Cappadocius' soul).

                Similarly they will completely outclass most PC coteries, but again that's kind of the point and how they were portrayed. These stats keep them being supremely powerful among vampires but at least with some nuances among that rarified scale. You mentioned V20 Black Hand, compare Sahar-Hanibaal a being who at about 2000 years old has successfully maxed out all his attributes, has numerous maxed out abilities, and 4 maxed out Disciplines; and assuming the dates presumed the Antediluvians are 6 times as old as him.

                Previous attempts.
                • Children of the Night
                  • A simple method, the complexity lies in adjudicating how how old the Antediluvians actually are to some measure.
                    • Various speculations on the forums in the past have put the First City at 10,000 BCE or older
                      • It may not matter as much how much before since it could be placed before the creation of the Gauntlet, at which point you're essentially in a nebulous mythic age
                    • Japheth is stated to have been embrace 7225 BCE, Baba Yaga 5000 BCE so the Second City existed sometime before 5000 BCE
                  • So for our purposes we're going to put the Antediluvians at 10,000 BCE, meaning in our time period they would be 12,000 years old. This leaves plenty of cushion for the era of the First City and the period of Caine's ban on embrace.
                    • Start with a base character and add the following dots
                      • Attributes: 2 per century up to 5 centuries then 1 per century = 125 dots
                      • Abilities: 5 per century undead = 600 dots
                      • Disciplines: Take the square root of the time undead and distribute among Disciplines = 109 dots
                      • Backgrounds: Think about what kind of character it is and assign apppropriate values
                    • Well it seems we have an issue, with this method you end up with 10s in everything except Disciplines. While this could be valid, it kind of defeats the purpose of providing stats, it would be nice to have some variation among the Antediluvians
                • V20 Rulebook
                  • Methuselahs are given 1000+ XP with a basic rule of thumb that "idle" Kindred have Discipline dots equal to the square root of their age.
                    • This method ends up being less useful because the XP amount for beings as ancient as the Antediluvians has no foundation, you're just pulling the value out of thin air (yes I know we're trying to do similar but I'd like there to be some methodology to it)
                • Elysium
                  • Start with a character with better starting stats
                    • 10/7/5; 20/12/8; Disciplines 10; Backgrounds 15 (the Elder backgrounds of Age Generation and Elder Status cost 4 points per dot); Virtues 7
                    • Add freebies based on the Age Background
                      • less than 50 yrs old
                        • 20, Humanity normal
                      • 51-200
                        • 50, Humanity -1
                      • 201-350
                        • 75, Humanity -2
                      • 351-500
                        • 95, Humanity -3
                      • 501-750
                        • 110, Humanity -4
                      • 751-1000
                        • 120, Humanity -5
                      • So assuming you stay at the 10 freebies+ per dot as set going from 4 to 5 then you end up with 170 points and Humanity -10
                  • So let's try statting the Eldest with these points
                    • The Eldest is said to have been some sort of oracle as a mortal, so we'll use that as our starting concept and grow from there into what we know about it later
                    • Attributes: Mental, Physical, Social
                      • Strength 3, Dexterity 4, Stamina 3
                      • Charisma 3, Manipulation 3, Appearance 2
                      • Perception 5, Intelligence 4, Wits 4
                    • Abilities: Knowledges, Talents, Skills
                      • Alertness 3, Awareness 3, Brawl 1, Empathy 3, Expression 2
                      • Animal Ken 3, Crafts 1, Etiquette 2, Survival 2
                      • Academics 3, Investigation 5, Law 3, Medicine 3, Occult 3, Politics 3
                    • Disciplines: Auspex 10
                    • Backgrounds: Age 3 (12), Herd 3
                    • Virtues: Conviction 3, Instinct 4, Courage 3 So here's where it gets messy since the Eldest switches off of Humanity at some point, for the sake of assuming more powerful we're going to build it with Metamorphosis virtues
                    • Freebies: We currently have 75 from our Age, so we'll spend 28 to get to our theoretical 10 Age (leaving us with 142 and Metamorphosis -10). So let's get to spending the rest.
                      • I've hit a snag. Just getting the Clan Disciplines to 10 burns through the rest of our Freebies Animalism +10 (70 fp), Vicissitude +10 (140 FP). And even if you assume they only have 1 Clan Discipline at 10 (a view I do not ascribe to) that still leaves almost nothing for stats elsewhere, and leaves them far under the stats provided for Japheth

                                My house rules on Antediluvians (including Caine and the Second Generation) to make them more interesting is this... unless they have completely surpassed humanity (i.e. either lost to a massive waking hunger frenzy or completely transcended like Tzimice, Lasombra and Malkov) they are no more powerful than a 4th Gen.

                                Further, for those that haven’t transcended completely, these wake ups are cyclical and localized (mini-Gehennas if you will). They wake up hungry and in a frenzy and in this state have rank 10 disciplines and an infinite capacity for blood, but after a time (hours to days to weeks depending on their humanity/path level and availability of the blood of their clan... because only clan blood has the potency and flavor to satiate them) they come out of the frenzy and lose the heightened powers granted by the fresh-woken Beast.

                                Then they’re active for a century or two (usually pretending to be someone else because they aren’t actually the powerhouses they’re portrayed as after that initial frenzy) and slip back into torpor as all methuselahs are wont to do to repeat the cycle.

                                In terms of statting the Antediluvians up though, for me once they’re awake they’re effectively “just” a 4th Gen vampire with maybe 2500 years of activity (they’re only ~10k if you count their many torpors). The blood god legends are misinterpretions of their waking frenzies from back when the vampire populations were much smaller, less experienced and more localized.

                                This makes the Jyhad/War of Ages a bit more interesting to me.
