                    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
                    Obtenebration [...] was made as a ST primary power (along with Vicissitude) to make the Sabbat more freakish, and then had to be revised a bunch of times to actually make it functional in PC hands.
                    I've seen this sentiment repeated a lot over the years, but it doesn't appear to be based on anything concrete as far as I can see. Obtenebration is not mentioned anywhere in The Storytellers Handbook. I haven't seen so much as a brief overview of the discipline in any of the books that predate The Players Guide to the Sabbat. While the antagonist section of The Storytellers Handbook skims over the first 3 levels of Vicissitude, the scant few lines we get on the discipline are not enough for me to consider the first full Vicissitude writeup in The Players Guide to the Sabbat a "revision." The former is not even enough to be a first draft. What's more, The Storytellers Handbook and The Players Guide were both released in 1992, months rather than years apart. It's likely that both books were in development simultaneously, and the brief snippet of Vicissitude in the former was simply intended to be a preview of the latter. Obtenebration, Vicissitude, and Dementation were all designed from the start with players in mind -- it's called the player's guide to the Sabbat, after all. How successful the disciplines were as player material is a different matter entirely, but I think it's time we put to bed this notion that the disciplines of the Sabbat clans started out as primarily Storyteller powers.

                    That said, I do agree with you that if discipline consolidation is your goal, Obtenebration should have been folded into Obfuscate rather than Necromancy. Like you say, "Obtenefuscate" would turn two kind of shaky powers into one strongly thematic discipline. It's not like shadow fuckery wasn't already a part of Obfuscate from the beginning -- the first dot was always called the Cloak of Shadows, for crying out loud.

                        Obtenebrate 2 is my favourite ability, more than the tentacles. Shroud is really cool, but the fluff suggests it should be far more powerful than the mechanics suggest.
                        Abyss mysticism is really cool.

                        I nerf Obtenebration in the following ways:
                        -Shroud costs blood.
                        -Obtenebration effects fear any fire with a soak value higher than the user's obtenebration rating. It cannot approach within a metre of such flame (distance may be more extreme with more powerful fire)
                        -I fluff shroud so that people within can feel a pressure/flow from objects and movements. Also gunshots exiting the shroud leave trails of darkness... because otherwise a +2 difficulty with complete sensory deprivation is absurd.
                        True Faith blows away 3xdots of faith in metres of shroud and destroys arms of the abyss . If someone is transformed (Black metamorphis, Tenebrous form) then being in this range is the equivelent of touching a holy object and deals faith damage. For those vampires particularly adverse to faith, their weakness applies to their obtenebration. Touching the holy object itself does double damage while in such forms. Abyss mysticism has related problems when in the range of faith.
                        -Aggravated weapons (Burning blade, claws) hurt Tenebrous form.
                        -Wards against vampires work for Obtenebration.
                        -Bright light weakens tentacles and gives them a difficulty increase.

                        But I also buff shadow play to effect more targets as Obtenebration gets better. And tentacles can be used as a shield for -obtenebration- dice.
