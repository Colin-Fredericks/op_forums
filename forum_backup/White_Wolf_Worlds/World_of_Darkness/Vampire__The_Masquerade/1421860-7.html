    Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
    One of the things that has been organically emerging in my game with the Tremere is the fact that each of the Houses have been coalescing into different styles of Blood Magic. They've been less about their political and social aims and more about simply what sort of sorcery the Tremere in question practices.

    House Tremere: Wizard Classic. The Tremere use wands, wear robes, have spellbooks, and are very much the Order of Hermes in 2020 as they were in 1220. They are not updated because what worked for them then works for them now.

    House Carna: They're doing the nature worship, sex magic, cauldrons, and athames. They're all classically trained witches and wizards but experimenting with more primeval stuff. Oddly, they don't necessarily know what they're channelling but are growing in power, nevertheless.

    House Ipissmus: I've been using these guys lately as the Tremere technocrats despite their Thelmic name. Guys with Silicon Valley laboratories, computers, bio-thaumaturgical experimentation, and more. They're the House Thig of the Tremere versus Carna's Diende.
    Well, to be completely honest, i still have to define a clear image of House Carna in my head, as while i kind of like the idea of a bunch of resentful Tremere ladies playing "Wicca/Verbena gone Wild", it doesn't quite fit with my image of Carna as serial black widow (and possible diablerist), at least not yet, but then i'm the first to admit i have barely dipped my toes into V5 lore.


    About House Ipsissimus.... Doesn't Cult of the Blood Gods mention Mortius as a member of the house? He definitely doesn't fit with that "technocratic magic-dipping mages" image you're painting, at least to me. Guess i'll keep to the Thelemic in my own take.
