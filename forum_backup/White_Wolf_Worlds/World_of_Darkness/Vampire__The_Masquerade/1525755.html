      Well, what I'd want is for Bloodlines 2 to come out, it not be nearly the hot commodity Paradox is banking on, and them finally giving up on the vain dream that they have the ability to resurrect VtM as a major brand without actually putting the work to rebuild that brand. Paradox needs to recognize that they are not a TTRPG company, they've gone through multiple core partners and have PR problem after PR problem after PR problem; at a certain point it can't be the companies licensing from Paradox that's the problem (even if the part of the problem is that Paradox sucks at picking people to make their stuff, which seems pretty true on the video-game side of the brand).

      While part of me wants Paradox to sell the IPs to someone else, there's very few companies that are actually going to be better that can afford something like that. Paradox needs to find someone to manage the brand that's actually going to recognize the brand is limping at best on the excitement of V5's release having some decent hype. They need someone that understands what Paradox wants out of new TTRPG books (existing brand recognition to expand into far more profitable products), and then give that person the authority and financial backing to start a new era. Whether it's V5 or V6 or whatever. They need better community engagement, more outreach to communities that the past teams have just tossed under the bus, and also the spine to do things like cancel licenses if a partner is actually dragging them down (By Night Studio's bullshit this weekend) rather than just making something that isn't the personal vision of someone at Paradox (dumping Hunter Studio's version of W5 that seems like it would have been much better received by the fans but wasn't to the individual tastes of one person at Paradox who isn't the audience for the entire game). They need to stop giving a shit about when on the calendar GenCon is so they stop rushing major releases and causing themselves far more problems that whatever perceived benefit of having a GenCon release date is.

      After that? I'd want an edition of VtM that fulfills for more people what V5 promised to be: a version of VtM for the 21st century. They had so much talent available to them, and it was squandered on ideas that weren't fresh in 2010. If they want a system that's better suited for translation to a video game? Fine, actually commit to that. If they want a true rules-light that can expand to taste take on VtM? Commit to that. Professional skilled game designers can make fun games with those sorts of directives in mind; but games suffer if direction gets yanked around a lot.

      Then give us board games, card games app versions of those games, novels, comics, whatever else. Build the brand up with actual stuff people will enjoy. Let gamers know VtM is back for real, and in the background Paradox is doing two things for their own good: funding marketing and distribution that smaller companies can't do on their own to hit large audiences (esp. other languages), and use the laid out well defined vision for what VtM is supposed to be to work on video games in the background so that once gamers feel like VtM is really back, then you can show to an even wider audience something big, and real, and actually plant a flag for VtM that it still has it's own space in mainstream pop culture.

                  As with other posters, most of what I want is the same thing.

                  Bloodlines 2
                  Just let it be good, the original was lightning in a bottle as far as storyline, writing, and atmosphere so I don't expect the sequel to be its equivalent, but I would like it to be at least as good as VtM Redemption.

                  V6
                  The hard left turn that V5 made as far as story scale, metaplot, and sad to say progressive politics always felt like a cash grab to reach a theoretical wider audience. I feel that V6 should be more neutral. Rather than a metaplot driven game line, the WoD catalogue should be more plug and play with its design and modules. Basically all factions and systems are optional. The game line "All Flesh Must Be Eaten" comes to mind, with how each supplement gives optional and expansion rules, rather than rules and backstory bloat. As others have said, make sure V6 actually supports other styles of play beyond street level, yes the lost boys are cool, but so is vampire Amazon or vampire Microsoft. The rules need to be a bit more robust for elder play and established vampires who are doing societal chess moves.

                  Virtual Tabletop app
                  As much as TTRPGs and LARP are the historical core of VtM gaming, the reality is that the current state of the world is just not conducive to in person play. So a somewhat robust VTTP app is a part of the answer. The app should integrate ST tools, a PDF library, and group VOIP functions. Preferably with tools that allow for maps to be displayed and character portraits to be easily uploaded and highlighted when the person is speaking.

                  As for the monetization of the app, I would suggest that the app itself is free, with the basic character sheet functionality, dice roller program, and ST functions being available. The monetization comes from the notion that you can purchase a full price physical copy and scan the QR code for a free digital copy to unlock in the app, or you pay half price to just unlock the PDF in the app and forgo the physical copy. This is based on the concept in UK law that says you are allowed to keep a digital copy of any physical media you own. Then once this is formalized and stabilized, create a similar app for each game line. Perhaps this VtM app would be where the more simulation-esque aspects of the game could be run and tracked.

                  Vampire City Builder
                  Yes this is an out of left field idea, but it should be within the realms of possibility. Think of a game such as Frostpunk where your focus is a single city with an overworld map to interact with. You are the Prince/Baron/etc. of the city and you have to contend with various factions, influence how the city itself develops, what vampire laws you choose to enforce or ignore, plan for invasions by other supernaturals or mortal hunters. Ironically the notion of having a vampire leader solves the immersion breaking aspect of many city builders where the same leader runs things from the stone age to the far future. This can then be expanded upon by having different scenarios such as a city in the dark ages, or in the post apocalypse. Alternatively, you get replayability from the concept that as Prince you can choose what your supernatural population consists of. Running a city of only Nagaraja will have very different design than say an all Toreador city. But a city with a mix of clans, bloodlines, and other supernaturals will play out differently based on what critters are in the mix. It is crucial that the developer understand the grim dark nature of WoD, perhaps get the maker of Frostpunk and This War of Mine to make the game, since they seem to have a better grasp on dark morality than many western developers.

                  Netflix Series
                  Just pull the trigger on making a small-scale series to raise awareness of the WoD. I would suggest an animated series so that special effects are not a limiting factor for the series. Spend the money on good voice actors and non-woke writers who enjoy the setting and the source material and want to make a love letter to it, rather than using the setting to spread their politics or ideology.

                                      A book on the Nosferatu underbelly, the Goblin Roads, Night Couriers, Nomad Packs, Ravnos wanderers and all other outcasts going around in strange, dangerous and fringe places.

                                      I'd be happy if "Blood Stained Love" paved the way for some sourcebooks providing ideas and tools to create different kind of horror chronicles and themes.

                                      A book about the Gehenna War, with informations on the Ashirra and some advice on how to run Elders in the V5 framework, would be useful too.

                                      A sourcebook about Asia, detailing the not-Kue Jin.



                                      In the world of "I'd like but difficult to see", a Children of the Night supplement would be interesting for us nostalgics. Generally speaking, a bit more metaplot would of course be useful and nice. Also, following on the Fall of London theme, some chronicle books detailing other relevant Falls (Fall of Berlin, Fall of Venice, Fall of Prague, Fall of Wien, Fall of Mexico City) and something on the new Infernal Trinity in Istanbul.​


                                      On the subject of who's steering the ship, I'm fairly positive on the latest books but not keen on the soft-retcons on the original books: I'd be divided about bringing the license back to OPP, as they did really solid work on their supplements, but I feel that in an effort to bridge divides they soft-retconned a bit too much of the original pitch of V5, particularly on the role of the Camarilla. PLEASE PLEASE PLEASE don't bring back unique disciplines, levels over 6, Paths of Thaumaturgy. And also PLEASE PLEASE PLEASE go on with this global approach with presenting things: I'm really happy that the assumed setting is not 100% modelled on an American metropolis.
