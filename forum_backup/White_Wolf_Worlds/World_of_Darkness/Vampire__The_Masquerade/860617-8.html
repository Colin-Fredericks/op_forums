    116) If one wishes to transcend the limits of the physical form, they must start by transcending its mental limitations. Such is the philosophy of Eliza Andrade, who's advancement on the Path of Metamorphosis involves modifications of the human brain. She claims to be seeking the perfect cerebral structure, which will provide the analytical capabilities to pursue other avenues of evolution in a more expedient fashion.

    Prior to her embrace, Eliza was a neuro surgeon who performed many successful operations on the human brain. In her current state, she would describe her work as being crude and ineffectual. Since becoming a member of clan Tzimisce, Eliza has implimented Vicissitude into her work on the human brain, modifying it in a way that she believes is properly suited to comprehend the secrets of the physical form. Her creations are as alien in their mindset as they are in their appearance. The 'useless' parts of their brains have been replaced by ones Eliza deems more suitable to the information she's trying to utilize, and their neural pathways have been almost completely remodeled. When it comes to her Szlachta, less is more, as the saying goes. Rather than churning out a bunch of drooling abominations, she devotes time into her work, producing creatures of wicked cunning and sophistication.

    Eliza has also gone so far as to perform modifications to her own brain as well. The first thing that stands out upon seeing her is the bulbous cranium atop her head. Nearly the size of a beach ball, her veins bulge beneath the surface of her skin, while the brown hair on her scalp looks like a poorly fitted wig, due to the proportions of her skull. Her arms, hands and fingers have also been modified into more spindly appendages that can perform surgery with greater precision than her former limbs ever could. Beyond that, she appears as a human female of Hispanic descent with brown eyes and a modest physique. She was embraced in her early 30's and still looks as such to anybody capable of ignoring her physical modifications.

            120) Bad Dog (9th Generation, City Gangrel):

            "Grrrll... Bark, Bark!"

            Not many people know who Bad Dog was prior to his embrace. He's been a vampire for over 70 years now, so most of his connections to his former life are long gone by this point. In these nights, Bad Dog finds himself serving as an attack dog for Sabbat war parties, using his clan's abilities to track down their enemies and then tear them apart once he catches them. Because of his numerous frenzies over the past 7 decades, Bad Dog has lost the ability to verbally communicate in human languages. But those with access to the Animalism discipline or establishing a mental connection will find that he is still capable of holding a conversation if need be.

            As a Gangrel, Bad Dog is accustomed to surviving on his own. But as a member of the Sabbat, he's no stranger to working as part of a group. It's just that many of the packs he's joined over the decades haven't exactly survived longer than a few years before they do something stupid and get themselves killed. If a pack wishes to recruit him into their ranks, they're going to have to convince him that they're competent enough to survive, instead of rushing head long into danger like a bunch of shovelheads.

            As an Ancilla, Bad Dog has accumulated a permanent animal feature during his time as a vampire. Instead of having a human head atop his neck, he has manifested that of a doberman's. This head can only speak using barks, yips, growls and whines, lacking the biological components necessary for proper speech. Aside from his canine head, Bad Dog is a dark skinned man standing at height of 6'1 and having a decently muscular physique of 213 lbs. He is often seen wearing a crimson hoody, which he leaves unzipped to expose his bare torso underneath. The rest of his outfit usually includes a pair of tattered jeans and some worn out red and blue sneakers. He was embraced in his early 20's, leaving his body in good condition, as far as vampires go.

                121) Silvain Bardin (8th Gen, Nosferatu)

                "The Lord taketh, and I taketh back!"

                In the Bible, when Job lost everything of value in his life, he realized that everything he had was given to him by the grace of God. So of course God would have the authority to take it all away if he felt inclined to do so. Unfortunately, for Silvain Bardin, he did not receive the same epiphany. Before his embrace, Silvain was what most people would consider a good Christian. He never performed any of the deadly sins, he gave his excess wealth to charity, he attended church every Sunday and he rejected the paganist heathens whenever he came upon them. So you can imagine his shock when he drew the attention of the Nosferatu, who embraced him for reasons that are yet still unknown to him.

                Over the next several weeks, Silvain would lose everything he ever loved or cared about. His friends, his family, his home, his job, his self respect and finally his faith. If this was how God rewards his faithful, then believing in God is worthless, or so Silvain had come to believe. In a fit of anger, Silvain pledged himself down the Road of Sin, vowing that he would indulge himself in death what he had denied himself in life. He now favors the Path of Pleasure, immersing himself in debauchery and opulence to distract from the hideous monstrosity he has become. He even became involved in the business of trading slaves, using the money to establish his own brothel and register it as a place of Elysium for the city's kindred to meet and socialize.

                Before his embrace disfigured him, Silvain was a Frenchman in his early 30's. Now, he is a haggard creature with a protruding overbite, completely bald, gnarled ears, long fingers and a sloping posture. His eyes have also become polychromatic, with the right one being a bright shade of blue, and the left being a dark green.

                Notes: Silvain is intended to be a Dark Ages character with a position as Keeper of Elysium.

                            122) Bastion (11th Gen, Gargoyle):

                            "Oh, don't look so surprised. After all, you used me as a research subject for years. How could I not be expected to pick up a few tricks of my own?"

                            Most people would agree that eavesdropping on somebody's conversation is considered rude and taboo. But for Bastion, it was his only means of achieving freedom from under the iron thumb of the Tremere. Created as a sentinel Gargoyle, Bastion spent his existence as the personal assistant of the Chantry's regent. It was his job to gather the necessary components, prepare the ritual chamber and clean up the mess afterward. His job to fetch the arcane tomes and record his master's notes as he rambled on into the night about ideas and formulas for his continued research. What the Tremere didn't realize, however, is that Bastion was doing more than assisting his master, he was also learning from him. It took a long time, about 20 years worth of grueling servitude, but Bastion slowly began to understand the fundamentals of Thaumaturgy, and eventually learned how to counteract their magic.


                            Now, Bastion is free of his master's control and has earned himself a modestly comfortable position among the Camarilla as an arcane scholar and adviser on matters of the occult. As one of the rare few outside of the clan who have the privilege of knowing Tremere secrets, Bastion's assistance has become invaluable to those who find themselves on the opposite side of the warlocks. Unfortunately, this also marks him as a target by the jealous Tremere, and it likely his knowledge of counter magic has been the only thing shielding him from their wrath in the time since he's escaped.


                            While all knowledge of his past life has been wiped away, one can assume that Bastion was previously a member of clan Nosferatu. His appearance as a Gargoyle is truly hideous, with a stretched maw full of sharp teeth, A pair of short horns growing from his temples which curve around the side of his head, and sunken eye sockets that add a shadowy tint to his menacing glare. He's relatively small by Gargoyle standards, in the sense that he is able to fit into human clothing after modifying it for his new body type. He primarily wears elastic outfits, finding them cheaper and easier to get a hold of than ordering custom jobs.

                                                132) Yasen Ivanov Peychinovich is an Old Clan Tzimisce Autarkis.

                                                A Bulgarian Boyar in life, he was embraced in the 1100s and to this day still hold dominion over his old domain. Skilled in his Clan's discipline and Kuldonic Sorcery, he has a strong disdain for the Vicissitude using upstarts that pollutes his once glorious bloodline.

                                                Through judicious use of Dominate, blackmail and bribery and proper use of lesser branch of the family tree, he has succeed in keeping the old ancestral domain within his grip. A strong believe of the old tradition of Hospitality, he will not tolerate any violence done upon his guest or from his guess. This trait has made his Domain a de facto neutral ground for his old coterie (a Setite, a Lasombra, a Ventrue and a Malkavian (A Dominate one in Modern Night, but knew Dementation in Dark Age)). A Greek Cappadocian doctor used to complete the lot; his death has made it such that Giovani are forbidden on his land upon penalty of Final Death...a faith even the upstart Tremeres managed to avoid.

                                                A true Lord, he is very protective of his subjects, which had as lead to a surprising outcome in the course of history. Deep in one of his domain's mountainous forest, there is a Caern historically maintained by Shadow Lord packs and their kinfolk, leading to a polite relation between them.

                                                    133) Daniel thought the fighting was over. He’d done his six years, two tours, and was planning on getting his law degree. Maybe become a judge.

                                                    The Sabbat war party put an end to that.

                                                    He survived his creation rites and bound to his pack by the Viniculum, took what he learned fighting terrorists to become Frighteningly effective. His humanity on a downward spire, his sire looked forward to teaching him the Path of honorable accord.
                                                    Then his life changed again, as an encounter with a sole Lupine (what the hell was one doing in a Cam city anyway?) His pack, and the chains of blood bonding him to it shattered, he took an unfiltered look at his “life," and was disgusted.

                                                    Changing his appearance (thanks to a 'trick' learned from a pack mate), he went underground in the city, feeding on the homeless veterans while defending them from worse predators. After all who is going to believe a homeless crazy with PTSD who rants about blood angels?

                                                    Other vampires, that is who. When brought before the (Ventrue) Prince and his story torn out of him, Daniel found something he had long thought denied to him, mercy.

                                                    Now Daniel, Ventrue antitribu, is a member of the Camerilla, if not in good standing. He lays low, using his knowledge of the law, albeit limited, to help disabled veterans and build his herd as a result. He hopes that by helping others he can regain his diminished humanity. He knows that he’s always one misstep away from Final Death, and tries to always have an escape plan ready. But he also knows the Sabbat would consider him a traitor of the worse kind, and has nowhere to go.

                                                    (Ventrue antitribu, with one dot of vicissitude and the probationary sect member flaw, prey limitation is veterans though he tries to limit his feeding to veterans who have seen combat, so people under estimate him.)

                                                        134) Mother gave us life, and we love Mother. Mother asked us to protect, we protected. Mother asked us to fight, we fought. Mother asked to leave, we left. Now Mother is gone, so I have return home to wait for her.

                                                        She has no name, yet she doesn't need one. An old gargoyle Ritually created in 1130A.D. to defend Ceoris, she did as the Mother of Gargoyles asked, never questioning, never faltering. Defeating enemy both during the Omen War, the Massasa War and the Anarch Revolt. Then came the Gargoyle Revolt and she fought the Tremere she once protected under her Mother's guidance. Once free she left with her mother and her brood. After a while some of her broodmates and herself left and starter to wander eventually returning to the ruins of Ceoris where they still live. Having parted early on, none of the possess any knowledge of Visceratika.

                                                        Unable to embrace, and with none of them strong enough in Thaumaturgy to master the Gargoyle Creation Ritual, the quietly spend their life in the mountain, avoiding human, garou and the local Tzimisce, content in their simple existence, feeding off of animals or the occasional lost hiker.

                                                            135) Vadzim Vasilyevich Jakavienka is a brilliant seismologist and geologist born in the Russian Empire's city of Minsk in 1913. Somehow surviving tge was he grew up in the Byelorussian Soviet Socialist Republic whee his keen mind and scientific acumen was quickly notice by his teachers which allowed him to study at the university where he was quickly notice. Once his study were done, he did some field work up until he received the invitation to join the Soviet Antarctic Expedition where his expertise would be welcome. Delighted he agreed.

                                                            In 1953, On his way to Mirny Station, an encounter on the ship changed his life. One of the sailor was a Kindred, who found that life on the sea suited him better and was a good way to stay ahead of them. On that faithful night he become one of Malkav's Brood. He became a bit more excentric and focused, but nothing that caused more than the occasional raising of an eyebrow, but the time they landed at Mirny, he knew the basic of what it was to be Cainite as well as gain mastery of the basic skill in his Clan's Diciplines. His latent Obsessive-compulsive tenancies were accentuated by his Blood, but that actually proved to be boon more than a bane.

                                                            To this day he still live and work on his study at the station, making judicious use of Dominate and Obfuscate as well as the ocasional bloodbond to avoid raising suspicion and the thick polar suit allows him to briefly and safely travel during daytime (help with a bit of Fortitude) which allows him to weather the Antarctic Summer more easily.

                                                            On the Station he is known to be an excentric scientist, a bit disconnected with reality, still referring to the state and it's governmental organs by their old Soviet era names and often seen or hear signing the BSSR or the USSR Anthem.
