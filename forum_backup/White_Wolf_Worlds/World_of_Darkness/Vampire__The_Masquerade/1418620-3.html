        Some quick thoughts on a V5 Sabbat in light of the events in BJD and the V5 Books.

        Sabbat realigned along lines similar to Crusader Orders led by charismatic individuals such as Polonia, Lucita and Dracula, retaining pack and path affiliations but allowing for a more regional variation and outlook.

        Path’s to be reintroduced – characters are free to create tenets to reflect this, but each Path to have ‘Ideal’ Tenets illustrated – this would reflect the divergence from Humanity, and the fact that the Vampire is basically following and forging a morality code which is not hardwired into them like humanity is supposed to be. They could be written as the personal tenets of those who came before, and passed down to fellow adherents.

        In the same way Touchstones do need to reflect the altered morality – they might be human, but they could be a collection of fragments from the Book of Nod which the character turns to in times of crisis, or the shared Ritae of their Pack to renew their faith and purpose.

        V5 changes to Tzmisce: Gaining Blood Sorcery for Vicissitude. Blood powers go to the Path of Blood and Fleshcraft/Bonecraft/Zulo to be rituals. This would reflect their Dracula/Necroscopic origin – based on a line in the Chicago Folios.

        V5 Changes to Ravnos: Become an accepted clan of the Sabbat as the largest faction is there. They use a form of Obfuscate to create illusion and misdirection. Their Bane would be a variation of the Requiem Nosferatu, appearing ‘wrong’ impacting their interaction with others, notably mortals.

                        For my games I usually take a kind of 20.5 tack as I’ve explained on this server before, but as I return from college business, I’ll try to elaborate upon that and say how I tend to handle the Sabbat in those instances. Hopefully people enjoy and find some inspiration in it.

                        So in 2004, after the ups-and-downs of the East Coast Crusades, the Red Star caused what may have been a near miss with a Gehenna. It’s a mystery and no one knows for sure. Regardless, it certainly caused a stir in vampiric society.

                        New York City had fallen and the Sabbat was scrambling to cover their pride and respond to the dark portents around them. The factions grew more and more divided as a result of this and the fuse of the Fourth Sabbat Civil War was becoming primed.

                        The Ultra-Conservatives in Europe were still stagnant, the Conservatives in the Americas were wary of the loss of key assets, but the most dangerous were the Loyalists. Not only were Panders and their Loyalist allies pandering for a less rigid hierarchy, The Gehenna Prophetess had raised a call for a crusade into the Holy Lands and hordes of Sabbat were answering.

                        The Status Quo bloc struggled to keep their rank and file orderly while also having to show support for the Gehenna Crusade or risk further insurrection or even retribution from Gehenna Crusaders. The Old World and the New saw ideologues flocking to the Crusade, exasperating the plight of wightdom within the Sabbat’s ranks as domains had even fewer Enlightened to indoctrinate new recruits away from Humanitas.

                        Whenever the final death of the regent becomes known, the Fourth Sabbat Civil War became unpreventable. Giangaleazzo watched the mayhem and heard the worrying reports of shadows waking in old Mediterranean tombs and decided to cash out alongside a generation of Status Quo Lasombra who were either disillusioned, unwilling to crusade, unwilling to risk their hides in Civil War, or thought the Status Quo & Hyper-Conservative factions were loosing too much power and influence.

                        This movement alongside an influx of Gangrel & Salubri, and exit of many Assamites & Harbingers; the destruction of so many Goratrix Tremere, this all led to catastrophic results within the sects ranks. The Black Hand was gutted by the losses and struggling to recoup while the Inquisition furthered the chaos by going on a frenzy of investigations against both over-zealous Loyalists and under-zealous Status Quo. Those calling for the Prophestess to be declared a heretic caused even more tension, bloodshed, and monomancy.

                        The Sabbat is not gone. Historically, the Sabbat rises from each Civil War stronger, reborn in the crucible. Yet this may be different. Never has the fighting been so vast and upon such sharp ideological lines. The revolt of the Loyalists leads many Conservatives to resist attempts of reconciliation until they are brought to heel and the treason of the Lasombra and so many Conservatives leads many Loyalists to howl for the heart’s blood of all the non-believers in the sect’s ranks.

                        The Sabbat struggles to maintain its prominence along the North American Coastlines, throughout Central & South America, across war-then regions of sub-Saharan Africa and the Middle East, and their old holdings in Mediterranean and Eastern Europe.

                        The battle is fierce though and will occupy every level of the midnight society until its complete. Perhaps the PC’s will play a key role in shifting the balance of their domain in their faction’s favor though...

                            Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
                            Another thing that bothers me is that Viscissitude is a product of the body, some people fear it as a disease.

                            Though v5 clearly does not agree, Thaumaturgy is a product of the soul. It is not unique to vampires. mortals may practice it. Lupines may practice it. I don't know about changelings but I suspect wraiths cannot. Vitae just makes things more convenient, but it is an entirely foreign thing to the Beast. Which is why it cannot be used in frenzy for 4 editions (much of this applies to necromancy: Quietus and Obtenebration obviously were normal disciplines that worked in frenzy)

                            So, from the perspective of someone who got invested in the game before v5, It's mighty weird to have Vicissitude, a very... fleshy Discipline, to become a sorcery.
                            Mechanics wise, I always felt that Vicissitude was just flat out overpowered as its problem. So making it either Protean OR Blood Sorcery is a mistake.

                            How I would do it is make its power to affect others a combo discipline.

                            Here's how it would work:

                            * Tzimisce get Animalism, Protean, and Blood Sorcery. They get thus the full Dracula Combo that simulate the vast majority of his traditional powers.

                            * Tzimisce Protean allows them to shapeshift THEMSELVES into wolves, bats, Zulo form, claws, and changing their appearance with Fleshcraft or Bonecraft.

                            * Tzimisce Protean /Blood Sorcery would allow them to Fleshcraft OTHERS with surgeries and their labs on a permanent basis.

                            The Tzimisce would thus still have one of the most powerful Disciplines and combos in the entire game but not outrageously so.

                            As for Thaumaturgy, I should note I have a much more "crunch" based view of magic.

                            Vampires can't teach their magic to ghouls or mortals or werewolves. They draw their quintessence for their spells from the blood that is provided to them via a quintessence processing machine (i.e. the vampire themselves). Vampire blood is magical and thus can power magic. Hedge magic requires another source of magic via spirit, rituals, or sacrifices. True magic uses the avatar and drains it from the universe.

                            It's a complicated non-instinctual process unlike Frenzy, so why you don't see Wights using it or other high level disciplines.

                                I understand that some want Vicissitude to be Vicissitude. I can appreciate that.
                                I based my suggestions on answering the OP of bringing the Sabbat to V5, which seems to be following a policy of pruning Disciplines & Bloodlines in favour of individually tailored power-sets within fewer Disciplines.
                                For Vicissitude, Protean seems a great fit at first, but that wouldn't cover the Tzmisce's command of Blood, Flesh and Bone sufficiently for me. Also if we were to try the reduce Disciplines & Bloodlines then Koldunism is either out altogether or so warped it no longer serves its purpose. By adding Blood Sorcery to the Tzmisce I imagine a discipline which allows Koldun's & Vicissitude practioners to sit within the same power set (No it isnt perfect, but this is an idea based on the change being inevitable - if that wasnt the case then I would keep the two bloodlines)
                                I also thought about Vicissitude being a Path of Blood Sorcery (The Path of Flesh & Bone? The Way of Vicissitude?) but I have no evidence there will ever be more than one Path in Blood Sorcery. so I proceeded accordingly.
                                Also as mentioned in adding Vicissitude to Blood Sorcery I think it draws the Old & New Clan closer - You need your Translyvanian Count to skitter headfirst down a wall like a spider? There's already a ritual for that.
                                I also sort of assumed that Blood Sorcery was not Thaumaturgy (though Thaumaturgy is part of Blood Sorcery) It was a catch-all category which represents the core principle of 'Vampire Magic' within the game. Your average Tzmisce Koldun, Tremere Thaumaturge or Banu Haquim Sorcerer might not see themselves the same. But from a game perspective I dont mind all my Rituals in one place where, as GM, I can say 'The Fleshcrafting Ritual is a speciality of the Tzmisce, as a Banu Haquim Sorcerer you need a Tzmisce Mentor before you can even think about learning that'

                                Just thought I would lay out my thinking.

                                                    Settites had protean through the lense of faith. Yeah, they had protean, and you should check out the literal meaning of protean, so I don't mind the change all that much, but thematically, it undermines the setites to have their HOLY GOD-GIVEN POWER just be the same shit as the power of the dogs they've historically derided.                                                     Now, back to vicissitude.

                                                    Things broken about Vicissitude and how to fix them:

                                                    Problem: being able to fuck someone's look up forever by just smearing your hand over their face
                                                    Sollution: Give vicissitude a minimum time to be administered properly; a quick smear heals easily, a methodical surgery? that stuff stays forever. Well, if you're at the mercy of a Tzmisce long enough for a methodical surgery, you've been at their mercy long enough to die. Also, I really want to put emphasis on the surgery aspect of the power, so this would help.

                                                    Problem: Raising Appearance (rules are unclear on the limit)
                                                    Solution: This is a two step process: Firstly, Put in a fucking limit. Seriously; I don't think all the surgery and photoshop in the world is going to take you beyond 7 or 8 dots of appearance. that's some magical stuff. With V5 that's less significant because you use merits for beauty, and though i'm sure there could be a 6-point beauty standard you can't get at character creation to represent the glorious visage of those blood gods that desperately need an improvement, it wouldn't be a gamechanger.
                                                    Secondly: Vicissitude, like real life plastic surgery, is going to have a social stigma to it. Even ignoring religious fears, sectarian fears, general conservatism, and crazy ideas about space aliens, Vicissitude is a nasty discipline to learn (all that trial-and-error leads to a lot of missing animals... or people. All that blood you consume to fuel it... )

                                                    Problem: It gives you lots of crazy shit like bone spikes that could be OP.
                                                    Solution: Procedures. Give that stuff 'rituals' with pros and cons. Document a load of changes, how difficult it is to learn (speculate about how many 'materials' you may need to sacrifice) and give storytellers and surgeons the examples to let them create their own freakish abominations.
