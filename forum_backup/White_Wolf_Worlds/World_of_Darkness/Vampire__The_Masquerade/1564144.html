    House ruling Gargoyles [V20]

    09-17-2026, 03:24 PM
    I recently made a Gargoyle NPC under V20 rules and noticed how dissatisfied I was with the way their variants/Discipline spread works. Combined with their severe Bloodline flaws and the somewhat underwhelming Visceratika as a Protean variant, I want to house rule some of that for my games.

    Thematically, I liked the Dark Ages approach of deconstructing the Disciplines into powers and enchantments, as that was fitting for them as a half-construct. However, for a modern Bloodline, it's pretty convoluted (and expensive; 10 XP per dot is silly).
    As a test, I made a Caitiff with the Monstrous, Weak Willed, and Amnesia flaws. That was more than enough to give them the Additional Discipline merit for Flight.

    What I think I will do is get rid of Visceratika and let them be able to pick Disciplines from their 'parent' Clans + Flight at Caitiff costs, with Auspex, Obfuscate, and Protean as suggested defaults. Visceratika 2 is an easy alternative power for Auspex 2, and Protean 4 can be Horrid Form from Vicissitude instead of Shape of the Beast.

    Also, instead of the Weak Willed variant as a Bloodline flaw, I think I will change that to the Dark Statue power but as a disadvantage: At dawn they turn to unconscious stone, regardless of whether they are outside or not, and are completely immobile. So the Disney Gargoyle flaw, just because I really like that.

    Having said all that, I'm pretty sure I'm not alone with house ruling modern Gargoyle variants, and I would be interested in what others have done with them. IMO as written, they are difficult picks for PC characters, and lack a bit of appeal in 'being' Gargoyles aside from Flight and the looks.

          I'm bothered by their Weak Willed weakness, but not by Visceratika.

          The thing is, this is not a Protean variant at all. It's a Castle Guardian power, a very consistent one:

          Skin of the Chameleon give you +5 Stealth and you don't have much reason to run within a building. For mobile surveillance it's better than Obfuscate 1.

          Scry the Hearthstone gives you direct information about everyone in the building at once. Bond with the Mountain works on stone, and as the level five power clarifies, also with concrete and cement, possibly other similar materials (clay, ceramic, plaster, asphalt?)

          Armor of Terra is a nice set of minor bonuses until you notice it halves every fire damage dice pool before soak. Flow Within the Mountain is a free pass into any building on top of almost undetectable stealth as you're completely out of sight, and is not an Elder Level.

          Together they make for a very effective means to control and move through your own Haven, and guard or invade any building. The fact its stone-melding effects work on city ground make them way more useful than Earth Meld, and even more depending on the city or if you convince the ST to include plaster.

          Would I, as ST, include plaster? Yes. Not only is it thematically close to concrete and other building materials, plenty of cities around the world, in rich and poor countries alike, use a lot more stone and concrete to build than the US, so this power already works in walls in actually most of the world. I don't see a need to let the US be the snowflake and diminish the potential of this. You already pay the price by making it obvious to everyone you have Visceratika (as you turn grey at level 4 if you're not a Gargoyle).

              Visceratika is pretty good for what it is meant to do, ie provide the tools a guard needs. In Dark Ages the powers work fine, but in a modern setting I would say stealth alone isn't sufficient for a character with the Monstrous flaw. It's possible to play one without Obfuscate, but in a crowded city it can make things awkward especially when moving about with the Coterie. I know nobody looks up in cities, but social interactions with mortals (something Dark Ages Gargoyles weren't even expected to have, as opposed to Nosferatu) become almost completely impossible.

              There's also that the stealth and awareness aspects of Visceratika don't scale. The Scouts who need to be out and about have both Auspex and Obfuscate, and I think that's more fitting for an independent Bloodline no longer tied to their ancestral purpose - more for gameplay reasons than canon ones, though. I wouldn't even mind putting Visceratika on top of those, but if you add Protean in the mix it does become rather redundant. Still, no reason to not make it at least optional.

              Oh, and Earth Meld for Gargoyles should absolutely work on stone and 'city materials' instead of soil.

                  Obfuscate uses Stealth rolls. Skin of the Chameleon is a considerable boost, especially against pesky Auspex users.

                  Scry the Heartstone gives a blank statement Auspex can't provide, but can explore. It's not doing the same thing, Auspex is the best for an immediate area, but this is a complete scan on the whole building that includes how many are Obfuscated within.

                  Armor of Terra is still halving fire before soak. It effectively doubles anything you have for soak against fire, on top of being a Fortitude dot that adds to Fortitude itself.

                  I really think this is plenty of synergy between powers and that you're greatly underestimating how Visceratika can be used outside being a guard, especially in a modern city.

                  During the Dark Ages it gave you awareness in a castle or temple. In a modern city it can give you a few floors in an apartment or office building at once, the immediate layout of any facility, a way in and out of vaults and locked rooms. Almost everything around you is rock or cement, almost everything happens inside buildings, and beneath everything there is a lot of infrastructure that you can use.

                  But for this same reason, I would not give this access to rock to Protean. Not only are the metaphysical rationales distinct, the change in usefulness is substantial.

                  You can add other Disciplines to their spread, I think the second Weakness is severe enough to justify and Flight is useful, but lackluster IMO. But I really wouldn't cut Visceratika even as a player.

                          This surprises me.

                          I recently played a Gargoyle and was surprised by how well their kit worked. Not only was it strong, their powers fit with their lore quite a lot.

                          They are a bit St dependent, the st needs to let a monstrous kindred function without obfuscate. Gargoyles look like monsters and have wings. The st we played with let me wrap up in a bandaged face and rags and pretend at being a burn victim. Wings could be wrapped up back and hidden somewhat. There also wasn't constant rolls to move in the city. Without that it'd be too inconvenient to run the bloodline.

                          The second drawback is they want a lot of disciplines. They only need Visceratika 2 and flight 1 but they really want as much as possible.

                          The third is they can't carry much with them.
                          Their stealth ability, Visceratika 1, requires them to be naked.
                          Flight needs a lot of dots before you can carry a lot with you

                          Given those drawbacks you have some extreme strength, the biggest one being synergy.
                          All the V20 gargoyle powers fit into each other.
                          They all let you do what gargoyles were made for: guard domains, invade domains, scout, fight.

                          Visceratika is really good here.
                          It's thematic, all its powers hint at the parent clan powers. I like that a lot more than them just having the original disciplines outright.

                          It's also so very useful. You have a strong stealth ability, a strong scry ability that massively boosts stealth, a sleep safe ability that along with flight makes it trivial for you to stay outside (keep scouting, investigate a place to dawn), an extra boost to fortitude that also makes you fire resistant, and a way to make it even easier for you to get into some places.

                          Beyond that potence is great on its own and greater for a stealth stabber, fortitude is useful especially if you aren't carrying armor and flight makes it even easier for you to get into buildings.

                          Protean 2 would be great for them. The eyes almost more than the claws.

                          But all in their kit really help them in their given role and most of their powers lock into each other. And it fits with their lore, feels really elegant.

                              I'm increasingly of the opinion that Flight and Visceratika should be folded together, for a few reasons.

                              - 3 disciplines is the norm
                              - Gargoyles have a lot of downsides to them, so Visceratika being an extremely good Discipline is fine in my view!
                              - it makes learning "Flight" come as a package with learning Visceratika, and learning Visceratika means getting a stony hue to your skin and sprouting wings, at least in my view!

                              The net result of this is that Gargoyles can realistically exist without Obfuscate, since their mobility Discipline and their Stealth Discipline are tied together. You're sorta stuck being a naked weirdo who travels from rooftop to rooftop, descending into Elysia through agreed vertical entrances, but that fits very neatly into the fantasy of the Gargoyle. And if you want to eschew the Gargoyle Archetype, as a Gargoyle, you can always learn Obfuscate 3 and start wearing clothes!

                              I am also not a fan of Gargoyles having Appearance 0. I think it makes sense that many of them do, but ultimately their Curse should be "You look like a Gargoyle! Big stone guy with wings!" with a side helping of "You're easy to enslave", "You don't have a Primogen", "You're a social creature who suffers penalties if you're alone in the world, no master, no allies etc." and all the social stuff that comes with it. I played a DAV20 Gargoyle who wound up with Appearance 6 (via Vicissitude), as it happens! It's worth noting that Nosferatu's Clan Curse mentions that Appearance is stuck at 0, while none of the Gargoyle ones do, so you can alter their Appearance score with Vicissitude, RAW!

                                  Hm, the more I read here, the more I think just giving players access to the pool of 'ancestral' Disciplines + Visceratika at Caitff costs is the easiest compromise. Animalism isn't a bad fit for Gargoyles imo, but Vicissitude would be a bit of an outlier. Maybe a variant that works on stone and steel instead of flesh and bone could be interesting? Visceratika used to have Level 6 power for animating stone. Or just exclude it from the pool.

Since most of them are in the lower ranges of the Discipline anyway, I think that would just complicate things unnecessarily though.

                                  For Flight, I considered making it an Ability instead of a Discipline. I don't like the idea of locking it behind a specific Discipline level since Flight is a signature ability for Gargoyles, so folding it into Visceratika is an attractive alternative.

                                              OK, now I am trying to formalize it, but I'm trying to chose a new Weakness.

                                              Basically I eliminated both Weaknesses. The appearance went to Visceratika itself, the Weak Willed is just gone.

                                              In Visceratika, the Weakness is Remarkable Silhouette: your bulk is such that you always stand out. It doesn't affect the Masquerade or your Appearance rating, you can have fantastic beauty, and you can give any lame excuse to be big and grey with hard skin. You can cover yourself in a trenchcoat and will still look like a burly sore thumb in the crowd that people will remember for weeks. You're not just easy to remember and identify, you're a reference point as in "follow the street, turn left on the giant freak".

                                              For the actual Gargoyle Weakness, some rough ideas, I can flesh out anything I actually decide upon:

                                              1. Uncontrolled Memory
                                              The Gargoyle can't remember its past, but it is still there. Sometimes something act as a reminder, but since it comes without context the Gargoyle is left with shock, confusion and unusual feelings without much to do about it. This may or may not include the possibility of rebuilding the past even centuries after the fact.

                                              2. Identity Uncertainty
                                              The amnesia effect is ongoing, sometimes the Gargoyle suffers a partial memory loss, but in a specific way the Tremere engineered long ago, they remember events but forget their own role in them. The Gargoyle's memory becomes a tangle of scenes where it can be anyone, and only others can offer any anchoring.

                                              3. Bestial Features
                                              Add to Visceratika, so I'm thinking on exploring it a little differently, as disabilities. The features are visually subtle (until Visceratika), and have no sufficient development to concede benefits (unless bought separately), but they cause actual limitation. Wings that don't unfold get tangled and limit space, a muzzle makes speech and feeding awkward, clothes must be tailored or they don't fit and degrade fast. All in all, they need adaptations to live comfortably, or even functionally, and being stranded from their accessibility tools is painful or dangerous.

                                              4. Disney Gargoyles
                                              Because why not. Turn to stone during the day, if not enough make this stone no more resilient than the normal state. It's a very soft stone.
