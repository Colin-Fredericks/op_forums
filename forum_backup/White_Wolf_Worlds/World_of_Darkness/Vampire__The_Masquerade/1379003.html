    Why not the Imbued instead of the Second Inquisition?

    04-06-2020, 12:23 PM
    I really don't like the Second Inquisition.. It just feels.. so cliche and horrible. From somehow breaking into Schrecknet, then the Vatican getting involved, then this group of mortals just wrecking everything.. Yeah no. The Camarilla nor Sabbat wouldn't be taken out by such a thing in their pre-V5 unnerfed state realistically (even with the whole Beckoning nonsense, lets be real). The worst egress of this Gary-Stu faction was how they somehow bombed the Vienna Chantry- a magic fortress/university basically that has probably existed longer than all the operators within the SI combined and literally can't be comprehended by mortals at all because it always teleports IIRC. Not only that this Gary-Stu faction has cleared out cities with high level Kindred, some Anchillae too who probably weren't compelled by the Beckoning.. somehow... The Second Inquisition.. is horrible.

    What would have been way more compelling was to bring the Imbued in as the primary Human antagonists instead of the SI. More interesting, has clear abilities that would've allowed them to spot Vienna in the first place with True Sight, a clear precedent of their power is known so there is a logical justification for how their KO'ing the Camarilla and the Sabbat (especially the Sabbat), and they would've been generally more compelling. Because I don't understand the fuss about the Second Inquisition, their faction doesn't make sense.

                                                          To go with a different explanation, V:TM has always been done as a conspiracy-heavy socially relevant game with the conflict between capitalism and the have-nots being a major source of the War of Ages-themes. The Anarchs predate the rise of Occupy, Bernie, and other grassroots resistance movements with a hefty dose of crime as well as victimhood of police brutality. The Second Inquisition is an attempt to embody the post 9/11 surveillance state and deal with the fact the Masquerade would be fundamentally different in our post-War on Terror world where trillions of dollars are spent gathering the data of every citizen on the globe, spy satellites (or Google), plus a shift from large scale military engagements to small squad-based military attacks.

                                                          A lot of fans are dealing with this shift because humans have kind of been shit on by previous Editions and the Masquerade was de-emphasized to the point that it was assumed that humans were all but helpless against the undead.

                                                          A mistake, IMHO, because hunters are an eternal part of vampire lore. This is just about restoring them to their rightful place as a main vampire antagonist.
