                                There are two main concerns, one is light safety, the other is enemy safety. Light safety only requires something like a body bag or coffin/large box. Enemy safety just requires your bag or box to be in the attic, basement, closet, or crawl space, preferably disguised as something else.

                                One of the best things to do is to have an ornate coffin in the basement rigged with claymores or IEDs so if anyone opens the lid the room gets shredded, while your actual sleeping space is in the attic. Alternatively, if you don't want to bother the neighbors with explosions, then just rig a steel plate door to the basement room with the coffin so that opening the coffin lid makes the door close and lock tight, at which point the halon fire suppression system goes off and displaces all the oxygen in the room.
                                That's not as easy as it sounds; boxes and body-bags can have holes that let light in. You can't be to paranoid enough; if some sunlight gets in, just a little, you could be at final-death before you even woke up.

                                The best way is make a haven mortal-proof is have it in a cemetery or, I took this straight from Ann Rice, is, invest in Potence and get a stone so heavy, no mortal could lift it.

                                        Originally posted by Iguazu View Post
                                        That's not as easy as it sounds; boxes and body-bags can have holes that let light in. You can't be to paranoid enough; if some sunlight gets in, just a little, you could be at final-death before you even woke up.
                                        Only people who haven't built things with their own hands would have that problem. Sunlight might cause damage to vampires, but it doesn't burn its way through most materials in a time table short of years or decades. A simple pine wood coffin is light tight, if you are worried, then give it a coating of varnish or paint to plug any potential gaps. If you are extra paranoid, then you just use a body bag inside the box. Now someone may want to suggest that vermin might nibble on your body bag or coffin while you are sleeping, but animal instinctively run away from the undead, so that won't be an issue.

                                        Originally posted by Iguazu View Post
                                        Claymores and IEDs are a bad idea; for one, that draws attention of mortals. If you want to wake up one evening with a bunch of firefighters and detectives examining your body, yeah.
                                        Hmm, it's almost like a secondary suggestion about a halon system and a locking door was right there in the post to deal with having nosy neighbors.

                                        Explosives, despite what tv and movies show, generally don't create huge fireballs that create devastation. A relatively small explosive charge inside a contained space can kill a human, hence why you generally don't fire RPGs inside a small room, the back blast of the launcher is enough to damage/kill you. Now that pressure level isn't enough to damage the structure, it just pulps the mortal.

                                        Contained within a basement, probably being set off mid-day by hunters with all the daytime activity noise going on, the people across the street will hear something, but they won't be able to tell where it came from. Since there is no structural damage as a visual indicator, where are they going to send authorities to if they even bother to call?

                                        Originally posted by Iguazu View Post
                                        The best way is make a haven mortal-proof is have it in a cemetery or, I took this straight from Ann Rice, is, invest in Potence and get a stone so heavy, no mortal could lift it.
                                        In my experience those are some of the worst ways to protect yourself. A cemetery is open to the public during the day, so no one will challenge some strangers wandering about even if they are in groups. Since most cemeteries use modern equipment rather than grave diggers, the sight of someone driving a backhoe around is expected (which means a heavy stone slab is no protection), and last but not least cemeteries tend to be one of the first places people think about when it comes to the undead. So if you are being tracked by hunters, if you always head toward a cemetery before dawn, it's not hard to figure out where you are going.

                                        Also just for a moment, it is worth considering that despite the size of most modern cemeteries, only a small fraction of the space available will be dedicated to buildings that function as tombs that can be entered and exited. Which means there are relatively few places to look for a place that could hide a vampire who is not using Protean, I say that because once you have protean, you can sleep anywhere there is enough earth to meld with and thus the cemetery is no longer a requirement.

                                        On the subject of Protean and Potence, even these can be turned against you with a creative hunter. In the case of protean there was a mention of a sabbat pack who slept in an empty lot during the day with earth meld. A camarilla vampire found out where they were sleeping, purchased the lot, and had it paved over during the day. Without the elder variant of earth meld that allows you to swim through the earth, the pack was functionally defeated.

                                        Likewise with the notion of potence and a heavy stone slab being the answer, this can be turned against you just as easily. Any crypt structure that can hold up a stone slab of that weight will probably be too tough for a vampire to damage. Meaning that all a hunter has to do is weld shut or cement the crypt shut and you have essentially played yourself. According to the strength lifting/breaking chart, depending on the design and materials involved, you would need a combined strength+potence score of 9 or 11 to have a chance at breaking out of that scenario. That is a very heavy investment in both strength and potence, far beyond what a starter vampire with a reasonable character generation would have.

                                        Don't get me wrong, having several backup havens like a fake crypt at a cemetery is smart, but using that as your primary haven tends to cause more problems.
