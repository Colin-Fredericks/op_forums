                  Originally posted by Martian1912 View Post
                  So how did Hecata become a blended clan?,

                  LW
                  A combination of factors. If I remember right, it went like this;

                  Everyone is sick of Augustus Giovanni. The Harbinger infiltration of clan Giovanni as directed by Lazarus comes to a head (they've been recruiting dissatisfied members of the younger bloodline, as in Lore of the Bloodines). Between the HoS, younger Giovanni sick of their abusive elders and the cadet branches being done with being the "lesser" families, Augustus and any elders loyal to him have been purged, cleaning out the old leadership and leaving space for new leadership.

                  Augustus is presumed dead.

                  Enter the "Family Reunion." Every bloodline of the clan of death is brought together by the mysterious figure who guided their history (The Cappuchin, Lazarus, the Baron Samedi - aka, probably Cappadocius' own kid/s. The gorgons claim Lilith sent them dream visions to join.)

                  Vengeance currently sated, Methuselah manipulation in place and in the interests of survival, the bloodlines are reforged into their original clan, (possibly via a ritual that involved sacrificing August Giovanni.) They're now named for a multi-faced goddess of ghosts, crossroads, witchcraft, guiding in and out of the underworld and blah

                  Also a handful of surviving Nagaraja turned up at the reunion so they sort of got adopted.

                  Since the Giovanni bloodline is by far the largest chunk of the clan, Hecata clan culture is largely theirs, with a strong death scholar undercurrent from the Cappadocians'/HoS' return.

                  Prone to hyperbole
