    So Predator types

    05-29-2022, 06:28 PM
    Several of the Predator types include flaws that don't seem like they would last long term.

    Bagger - the vampire kills the enemy, pays off the debt and then some, gains so much political power that the enemy becomes irrelevant.

    Blood Leech, Consensualist, Farmer - the vampire loses so much Humanity, perhaps even adopts a Path, so that any squeamishness about feeding from humans becomes silly.

    Siren - the vampire eats the spurned lover.

    How do you deal with this? Do you put an experience tax on them to pay off the flaw? Do you simply have the flaw evaporate once the situation has been dealt with in-character?

              Two things about this. One, flaws don't go away as easily in v5. If you kill an enemy you'll develop a new one, for example. If you lose a flaw because of the story, you'll have to redistribute their dots into something else because of the sanctity of merits.

              Two, there are no raw ways to develop a path in v5. In addition, killing always provides a stain to humanity. That said, your odds of degeneration is extremely low at lower levels, but never 0.

                  Flaws are supposed to help you create an interesting story, taking flaws just so you can work them off is contrary to their purpose. I'd allow players to work off some flaws, but always return them to a minimum of 3 or 2 eventually, either as the same flaw or a new one.
