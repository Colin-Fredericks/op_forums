    Originally posted by monteparnas View Post
    I think one option as could have is for Hunger Dice to work similarly to Bane Severity, or now Generation Modifier: it's a variable that other effects call for. You could have powers, flaws and other effects that aren't universal, and each of those call for Hunger Dice.

    That way they're there but we have fine control over when they are rolled and for what, and can explore new takes or ditch those that didn't work without rewriting their system.
    I really like this idea. Ages ago, when a few of us were coming up with ways to merge Hunger and blood pools, or Hunger and Conditions, I had suggested using the Hunger Dice as your blood surge bonus and a bonus to perception (based on Kindred Senses from VTR2e). But this is a bit neater and potentially broader, and mirrors Bane Severity, as you say. I'll have to give it some more thought.

    Originally posted by monteparnas View Post
    I'd still be unsure about it being the best fit for the Ministry. I'd instead consider Blood Sorcery or, depending on how they make it, Tellurgy.
    I'd stick with their classic Disciplines (Protean replacing Serpentis), with the option to get Blood Sorcery via a Merit or as a bloodline. I'm not overly fond of V5 Blood Sorcery, though.

    Originally posted by monteparnas View Post
    That's an interesting idea for Nos. But yeah, Feral Whispers was an important staple, and wasn't just a Gangrel thing as other clans had Animalism and made plenty use of Feral Whispers.
    I came up with a bunch of these based on the Revised Quickstart Clan Advantages. Like the Setites had "Rouquin Insight" (rouquin = "redhead", after the role of redheads in Egyptian superstition), which allowed them to sense someone's hidden predilections. Ravnos could seal oaths with blood (a thing they do socially in lore, but there's never been any mechanics for it). Things like that that don't already appear in the game.

    Originally posted by monteparnas View Post
    That's complicated. With the Salubri back in the scene, can we really say they're a Bloodline and not the proper holder of Clan status? But even with their diminished power (of it stays a thing), can we really deny the Tremere role in modern vampiric society?

    Also, they both fit legitimate roles as distinct vampire archetypes. The "righteous vampire" that can also be "the soul eating mystic" wasn't really big in the 90s, but now it is and is not covered by the others.

    I think that would be a good solution OoC and still be an open question within the setting: who gets to be the 13th Clan? Not all vampires agree on that, and taking sides become one more political tool at hand.
    That's a good idea. I think the Sabbat would recognise the Harbingers and Salubri over the Giovanni and Tremere, for obvious reasons. The Camarilla would prefer the classic 13. The Anarchs might be a free-for-all and recognise 15 clans instead of 13 (with Cappadocians instead of Harbingers).

                            "The In Memoriam mechanic was inspired by Ericsson’s love of Highlander. As he described, it sounded like its intention was to be a more extensive, drawn out, and narratively heavier version of Blades in the Dark’s Flashback mechanic."

                            "Ericsson was deep into the club and techno scene in Stockholm when writing V5, which inspired a subcultural component."

                            "Photography was chosen over art in the corebook as a way to appeal to potential licensees: it’s easier to go down in fidelity than it is to go up (ie can abstract more from a photograph into illustrations than from a simple illustration into something more detailed)"

                            "The beginning arcs of V5 was meant to be starting off as low-powered, Thin-blood focused, and a rags to riches story"

                            "Ericsson said the two biggest complaints about the older editions of VtM were Celerity and the iron power ceiling with the elders/Prince in the city"

                            "Ericsson doesn’t believe the Nosferatu have a new look in V5; he also believes the Nosferatu is the nicest of them all, because they manifest their curse entirely on the outside"

                            "Bloodlines 1 was the deciding reason they landed on oWoD/Masquerade instead of nWoD/Requiem"

                            "Ericsson said Lasombra always felt more Camarilla than Sabbat."

                            "The truth of their powers were revealed to them as they gazed into the abyss (their Antediluvian or a methuselah awakening), it wasn’t what they wanted to hear, and are now afraid of themselves, their Elder, and have retreated from the Gehenna War in sheer panic, hiding what they are. The change of the name from Obtenebration to Oblivion is from that realization: they are tapping into the dead where demons and the most terrifying of the dead, the Malfeans, are imprisoned."

                            "Ericsson seems to display a particular disdain for Lasombra from how he talks about them: “edgelord clan” “Mary Sues”"

                            "Touchstones was introduced as a reinforcement of an old vampire trope from vampire literature: “I have crossed oceans of time to find you.” The one mortal who is so important that keeps the vampire’s humanity afloat."

                            "He said many of the rules for V5 were meant to represent vampire literature"

                            "Ericsson says that super long chronicles are hard to maintain within the framework of V5 in general, thinking your character will become too powerful"

                            "Thin-bloods are treated more as being infected or in liminal stasis rather than fully becoming dead like full-blooded vampires"

                            "The intent of Sabbat V5 was to go back to their mysterious feel in 1st Edition: execution of that was left to Karim and Khaldoun. The V5 Sabbat book is the initial perspective on the Sabbat- only in Second Edition do players start playing them."

                            " Since they wanted the nastiness level of the Camarilla to be near the level of where the Sabbat were in 2E/Revised, while the Sabbat would be the 30 Days of Night inhuman vampires. They did not want the Sabbat to be nearly identical to the Camarilla but with religious titles instead of nobility titles."

                            "One of the first ideas was going to be an introductory box set, where your starting character would be Thin-blood only."

                            "Ericsson partially blames the complexity of Thin-blood disciplines on Bloodlines 2019. Many of the powers they didn’t want to include, but felt they had to for that planned videogame."

                            "The ultimate design goal of V5 was to return to 1E- the feeling of being a vampire."

                            "They wanted to return the mystery and the danger to elders, especially being more inhuman, and their relation to the Gehenna War."

                            "Long arcs were planned out for every single title (Werewolf, Mage, etc.). The Gehenna War was never meant to last forever;"

                            "Ericsson’s vision was for elder to return to V5 as scarred PTSD veterans from the war with Antediluvians, coming back more monstrous than ever. Players would have to deal with these monsters coming back from the warfront."

                            " The Gehenna War was meant as a continuation/allegory for America’s war in the Middle East, as well as what is happening in the Ukraine."

                            "They were planning on having a miniatures game set in the Gehenna War, and after some seasons of that, one of the factions would win, and the elders would either retreat or return in glory to reclaim their positions, that the PCs now inhabit.
                            “Season” breakdowns planned for VtM according to the points above:
                            Season 1- Beckoning
                            Season 2- Sabbat warring on the homefront
                            Season 3- Gehenna War minis game
                            Season 4- Elders return home as monsters"

                            "Ericsson thought that the Revised metaplot was too dramatic, too fast, too much, namely their rate toward Gehenna being too fast, and having it be an immediate event rather than a prolonged event."

                            "The Camarilla branding the Thin-bloods was meant to show the Camarilla’s callousness; they learned their morality in the 1600’s and 1700’s, “Scarlet Letter” type people, but Ericsson couldn’t provide a concrete answer when the chat protested that having multiple people bearing an identical brand on their face in a densely populated area would be a near-Masquerade breach, stating he’d need to reread it and refresh what his mental process was."

                            "Guidelines given to Ericsson as writer were to have no mention of demons."

                            "A big reason why they wanted humans to have a bigger role in V5: “If everyone’s a vampire, no one’s a vampire.”"

                            "The Anarchs have control of Berlin now as a follow-up to the results of a LARP game that Ericsson played"

                            "Ericsson said the hardest design aspect of V5’s narrative was dealing with modern technology, and accounting for the hyper-surveillance state that emerged from 9/11. They decided governments become aware, but they also maintain the Masquerade because of the mass panic that would ensue instantly if average people knew about vampires."
