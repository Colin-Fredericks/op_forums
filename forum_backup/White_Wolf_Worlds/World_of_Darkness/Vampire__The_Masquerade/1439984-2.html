                                                            I’m going to buck the trend a bit and say if you really want to build a World of Darkness shared universe akin to the MCU... don’t START with Vampire; start with Mage.

                                                            Mages, particularly newly Awakened ones are, bare none, the closest to mortals in abilities and perspective to normal humans of any of the main WoD splats. That makes them ideal for an opening viewpoint character and subtle fx requirements to start.

                                                            But also important is that a big part of Mage is that everything man has ever believed in exists in somewhere in the shadows. This preps the stage for the larger universe with meeting vampires, werewolves, wraiths, changelings, etc. before spinning off an associated series where we get more details on the specific supernatural type.

                                                            I also really think VtM on its own just doesn’t have enough unique on its own to make it a viable IP for a Netflix style series. But the larger WoD as a shared universe IS fairly unique (and rarely as well put together; ex. the Vampire Diaries and its spin-offs).
