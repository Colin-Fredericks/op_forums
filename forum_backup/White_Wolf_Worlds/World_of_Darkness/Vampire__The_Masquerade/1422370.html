                                                      In this case ,The Fall of Vienna is the centrepiece for the Tremere ploy to restructure and rebrand the pyramid. In which nobody loyal/important has died.
                                                      The Tremere have long, long been a victim of their own success. The Tzmisce are reviled, the Lasombra are feared, and the Ravnos are vilified, but they don't do it quite on the scale of Tremere, who are the devil you have to deal with rather than the outsider you're free to despise. Tremere prestation is almost one-way, and they're far less smooth than the Setites when it comes to trading favours.
                                                      The Tremere might be great. But the Tremere as a brand is terrible, and the moment they can be replaced, people will be dancing on the street. Providing an Illusion of choice and giving people the idea that they can deal with a Tremere without dealing with 'The Pyramid' is good. Going down to the laughably harmless witch-larp Tremere for a favour just doesn't seem as damning as going up to meet with THE Tremere.
                                                      It also lets them put Tremere in cities that would never tolerate "real" Tremere.

                                                      And as far as gameplay and immersion go, it's the only sensible choice to have all Tremere houses be loyal Tremere in truth. Otherwise, your city needs to pick a side and have only one kind of Tremere, because HT would stomp the naturally far smaller and weaker HC to keep their monopoly. If you have players that want to be with different Tremere houses (lord save you) these houses secretly being aligned is the only way to really do it.
