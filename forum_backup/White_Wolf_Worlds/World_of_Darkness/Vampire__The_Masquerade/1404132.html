    Question about Diablerie?

    07-22-2020, 09:03 PM
    Suppose I’m a 10th Generation Caitiff. I manage to Diablerize a 5th Generation Tzimisce. What Generation will I become? Also, will I inherit the Tzimisce’s Disciplines, including Vicissitude?

      It likely depends on what edition you're playing, but the V20 Black Hand book has some expanded rules for Diablerie in that edition, which includes rules for gaining Disciplines, possession, etc. If I recall correctly, you cannot immediately gain dots in a Discipline you don't already possess through Diablerie, but Diablerizing a vampire of a different Clan does often allow you to learn in-Clan Disciplines without a teacher (though still at out-of-clan cost) IIRC.

      In the more meta-sense, even just drinking the vitae of a Tzimisce is treated by some as opening a vampire to the influence of the Eldest via Vicissitude, so Diablerizing a Tzimisce would likely run the same risk.

                      Originally posted by Penelope View Post
                      Would I become Fifth Generation?
                      You would automatically drop to 9th generation upon completing the diablerie. You can then roll a Stamina roll with a difficulty of 9 once per turn for each subsequent generation drop until you fail the roll, that would be where your generation lies.

                      Then you would have a willpower contest between the victim and yourself. Your difficulty would go up by 1 for each generation you just gained and theirs would go down by 1 for the same. If they win they possess you.

                      And, as has been pointed out, you would be connected to the Eldest. Which might not be so rewarding for the generation drop.

                          Remembering all the different rules and going with -most books say X-...

                          Disciplines: You will temporarily get some of the victim's disciplines due to ancient's potent blood. Maybe they'd last for a scene, maybe a night, maybe you'd get lots of dots, maybe you'd get a few dots each... it's not really clear (Their disciplines -5 would be a good housrule, so if that vampire had 8 vicissitude, you'd have three more vicissitude to play with)

                          After that temporary boost is done, you will be able to permanently buy any of the disciplines the victim possessed without a teacher, except for sorceries.


                          Generation:
                          Annoyingly, the rules just say to use storyteller's discretion if the gen gap is huge. Personally, I'd make you 7th or 8th gen.

                                                      Originally posted by Penelope View Post
                                                      Suppose I’m a 10th Generation Caitiff. I manage to Diablerize a 5th Generation Tzimisce. What Generation will I become? Also, will I inherit the Tzimisce’s Disciplines, including Vicissitude?
                                                      Under the normal/more usual rules, you'd drop by one step (to 9th), and would not directly gain any disciplines/abilities simply from the Tzimisce, as such (save for justification to spend XP you have to advance "x", and/or other alt-rules that are available). Using the alternate rules for diablerie that allows you to advance your generation more so, you add your generation and the victim's, divide by two (round down) and subtract one, to no lower than the victim's generation (if they are lower) or your pre-diablerie generation (if the victim is higher/weaker than you but you, for example, didn't know that at the time, thinking you were diablerizing stronger blood). Ex: 10(you) + 5 (Tzimisce) = 15, divided by 2= 7, minus 1 is 6. You would become 6th generation in the more advanced rule -- much more powerful than you were, but still a step down in the power that the victim originally had (on the premise that your soul can only process "x" amount of power at that moment and the excess is lost).
