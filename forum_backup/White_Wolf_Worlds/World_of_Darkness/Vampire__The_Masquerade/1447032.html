    Question on: "The Children of the Outer Dark" (Baali Lore)

    05-02-2021, 05:19 PM
    So, I've finally been taking a look through Clanbook Baali, the Lore of the Bloodlines, and the V20: Tal'Mahe'Ra book to find out more on the Baali, and one of the points of contention that I'm finding myself in, is on what the Children are. Are they supposed to be Earthbound, or some other spirit entity such as a neverborn? Some of their abilities seem like some of the Demonic Earthbound lores presented in D:TF. However in many supplements that detail the Baali, they keep the terms "Demon" different from "Children" as in to say that they're not the same? This entire process of trying to figure out what's what is confusing me. What do you all believe?

                          I don't think V20 did the Baali much justice, and I recommend you'd read earlier books. Though they're a little more... spicy. Black dog territory.

                          Now "outer dark" implies they're from outside of creation, deep-umbra style (though not necessarily the deep-middle-umbra). But there are also children in the ground (likely earthbound) and then there's a bunch of other crazy demon shit the Baali are into.
                          Honestly, the Children are vague so you can create your own story. They might be worshipping Cthulu or the Wyrm or whatever. You can even decide that the Baali are full of shit and their sacrifices were meaningless/really went to an ordinary demon. The Baali aren't one group, there are many, many, many different Baali cults with different interpretations and different objects of worship. The only thing that really seems to be common in baali demon worship are the wells of sacrifice, but they don't agree on the purpose of these wells.

                              I've always seen the true Children as being the Neverborn, with their 'bodies' as mere intrusions upon this world of entities that don't neatly fit into creation. The Wells seem like Nihils in Wraith parlance, and fit the descriptions of those (and how they corrupt the world) from Dark Reflections: Spectres.

                              The Neverborn also have dreams which are seen as beings of the Outer Dark in Mage, which implies some connection between the two types of entity (see The Book of Madness Revised).

                              However, I think various other creatures have been mistaken for Children through the aeons, and these might be Wyrm spirits, Earthbound, Antediluvians or worse.

                              The Children are first mentioned in VTDA Clanbook: Baali which was before Earthbound were a thing, so please bear that in mind.

                              DTF came along a few years later and then retroactively reinserted itself into the mythos of the other games (e.g., Wraith's high-powered NPCs became Fallen; everything mysterious and exotic but vaguely demonic became Earthbound).

                              I prefer the 'mysterious and exotic' answer, rather than the DTF answer. But you also have to bear in mind, every game has its own biases and assumes its cosmology is true. So just filter and adapt for the game you're running.
