    [spoilers] LA By Night ending a.k.a The State of LA in the WoD

    10-30-2021, 11:01 PM
    So, LA by Night is over and I have to say it did a lot better at ending in a "suitable" V:TM way than I expected to. One of the complaints about the series has been that it was a little too Light and Soft for what is a horror game. Personally, I don't think that there's a "wrong" way to play V:TM but I did think they could have gone darker.

    Well, I'm glad they did.

    + The Anarch Free States have been restablished as per Victor declaring it. I get the impression that most Anarchs will react to with a, "They weren't re-established decades ago?" Assuming they care at all. It will be noted by the Camarilla, though.

    + The Camarilla have abandoned LA again. The fact they sent Vannevar, the failed Prince of San Fransisco, makes me think he was always a Judas goat like La Croix. You don't send your best to LA. Its a white elephant and if you lose the failed Elder then it's fine. If you don't, also fine.

    + This was confirmed by Jason Carl in chat but is relevant that the Kuei Jin/Wan Kuei will have no role in V5 whatsoever. They've been effectively retconned out of the game line. This is notable given how big a role they had in Bloodlines.

    + Victor, Nelli, and Nines are the only remaining Barons. Victor wants to organize but, of course, that would defeat the purpose of being an Anarch.

    + The Second Inquisition are confirmed to have killed Katya and Aurora, which makes them not look like complete losers.

    + Ib was offered the position of Prince of LA. Which we don't know if she accepted. She's allied with Victor but, again, the whole Judas Goat thing.

    + The SAD guy, Drew Handler, wants to make a deal with Nelli. He's willing to dial down the hunt in Los Angeles as long as they keep a low profile. He'll still kill the occasional vampire but has no intention of starting another war.

    + X committing himsef to possibly permanent torpor to cure his inability to feed (the death curse from the Inquisitor Adrian) was a powerful moment and helped spare the game from being too sacharrine.

    + Eve gave up 70 years of information about Clan Tremere from her diaries to the SI in hopes that they'll destroy all vampires. Wow, I'd say she helped bring down the Clan if not for the fact that its set after Vienna.

    + She also commits suicide or hopes that you can achieve Golconda by literally stepping into the sun.

    + Annabelle heads off TO NEW YORK! Which will apparently be the next Chronicle.
