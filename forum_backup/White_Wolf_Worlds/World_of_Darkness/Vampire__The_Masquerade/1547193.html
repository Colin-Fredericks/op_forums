    Sahar-Hannibaal Generation

    12-23-2024, 01:27 AM
    As some of you might be aware of, Sahar-Hannibaal is the leader of the Moloch faction within the clan Baali. He's a notorious cainite and has been covered in some books. One thing that I find confusing is his generation in his published character's sheet in the Guide to the Tal'Mahe'Ra, where he's listed as being of the 7th generation, yet has quite a few traits at level 8. In your opinion is his generation a typo or is he one of those characters who have traits above their generation limit (like 2nd edition Valerius Maior, for instance, another one listed as 7th generation who had level 8 traits, his v20 character sheet changes it, though)?

      In his case, Daimonion provides an explanation for the boosted attributes, especially if you assume he uses the Dark Ages version of Daimonion 6 (in V20 it's an infernal pact, in DA it's an Investiture which better fits the Order of Moloch). He also has Dur-An-Ki to fall back on.
