    Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
    These are some interesting ideas. I admit it's a bit of a missed opportunity to make so many of the setting changes things that happened in the past rather than things you could play through now.

    I loved sourcebooks like The Time of Thin Blood and Nights of Prophecy at the time. You could make a lot of money playing through Transylvania Chronicles: The Fall of Vienna, The Hunters Hunted: Rise of the Second Inquisition, Diablerie: The Gehenna War, etc.
    Speaking as someone who's been introducing V5 metaplot into their V20 campaign as current events, it works SO MUCH better than "Oh yeah, this happened 5-10 years ago. Deal with it."

    Vienna just exploded, the local court is in a tizzy over who was behind it, the Tremere primogen and head of the local chantry left for Vienna to consult with his superiors just a few weeks before and hasn't been heard from since (it's now about three weeks out from the event). The Tremere (including one of the PCs) are currently in a scramble over their leader's resources (the primogen/chantry head played his underlings against each other by using different underlings to be his whip and his second in command for the chantry... which means the acting primogen and acting chantry head are now two different vampires). The other clans see the weakness too and are starting to move in on other Tremere assets throughout the city because "possession is 9/10th of the law" and "as long as the Prince gets his cut it'll be fine."

    And I haven't even had to touch the actual event directly... that's all just local fallout in a campaign set entirely in a single city.

    The whole Banu Haqim joining the Camarilla has also been going on in the background since the start too with a "goodwill ambassador" from the Banu Haqim (Sorcerer caste) arriving at court and helping out various kindred with their problems and basically ingratiating himself to everyone with any power. Hell yes they have their own agenda, but the PC's haven't figured it out yet. He smiles very much at the misfortune of the Tremere (who are the one faction who wanted nothing to do with the "Assamite" and his offers of assistance).

    Just before the current crazy settles I plan on dropping the Fall of London news on them... because that'll make certain NPCs (and probably some PCs) even MORE paranoid and prone to doing something stupid.

    The V5 metaplot isn't horrible; I think it primarily suffers from being dumped on everyone all at once as past events. If they'd actually occurred through various releases over the last 15 years in real time the way say, the events in Time of Thin Blood did, then I don't think they'd feel nearly so contrived and therefore received nearly the pushback they did from coming all at once and upending so many things, even if, over the course of 15 years of continuing releases, all the same things happened.

                                    I see infernalism as playing with fire. (hellfire, if you will)
                                    Yeah, you can get burnt, but that's not a garantee unless you're stupid about it. Fire can be incredibly useful if you're using it in a controlled manner.
