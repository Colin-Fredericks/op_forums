    How come they butchered the Nararaja Path/Disciplie and nobody said a thing!?

    06-21-2022, 04:59 PM
    I'm appalled to find this out now. I was by chance rereading VDAV20 some moments ago when I came upon the Nararaja path, more specifically the Vitreous Path. Am I reading it right, or does the level 4 and 5 now only cause 1(1!?) Aggravated damage if used for attack (the other level 4 application is to summon spectres to "make boo and nothing else", so I'd rather ignore it for now lol))!? One of my players asked if it meant 1 per success, but by the text it states only 1. I mean, how can a level 4 power cause 1 level of aggravated damage? Somebody please confirm it was just a mistake and it's supposed to be 1 per success. Even though I'm definitely no big fan of the Nagaraja, far from it (And this comes from a "fan" of the Tal'Mahe'Ra, but this is a topic for another thread...), I feel this is a bit too much, isn't it? Their old level 4 of Nihililistics caused 2 damage per success, it was good. I loved the idea that they made it a path, because it makes little sense for it to be another discipline, but at the very least I expected them to keep the powers in the same level, not to completely butcher it.

                                                  My first guess, before reading the entry in V20DA, was that it was 'one damage' in the way an attack with a knife is only 'Str+1'. So, it would be 1 + successes.

                                                  But on reading the rules for Curse of the Maelstrom (Vitreous Path 4), it seems the damage is almost beside the point. The point is to mark your target as blighted for a night, thus making social attempts harder. It's pretty naff for a four-dot power, but I don't think it's an error.

                                                  A simple fix to make it more powerful would be to have that power inflict the Haunted Flaw on a location or individual for one night per success. Nice and easy, feels in-keeping with the intent, and doesn't leave things as vague as the RAW. The main difference is that your ghostly stalker is a spectre, so much more aggressive than usual, but the Flaw would otherwise cover it.

                                                  Something more flavoursome would be for it to reduce a target's Appearance by one dot per success, which can only be healed as aggravated damage. It has a strong social and roleplaying effect but isn't lethal nor is it OP at level four.

                                                  As for Night Call (Vitreous Path 5), the rules seem even weaker here, and I'd be tempted to say it's '1 + successes' for the offensive use. With that being aggravated damage, that's a satisfactory effect, though a bland one.

                                                  But you could have it add a Flaw like Hand of Hades instead (the exaggerated version of Touch of Frost, which withers plants and slowly sucks the life out of those around you). The duration would be one night per success again.

                                                  The thing with V20DA is that some powers are much, much stronger, or better detailed, while others are pretty weak or missing key details.

                                                  For example, as written, Night Cry has no duration listed for the difficulty reduction. So does the modifier apply for a turn? 'All actions' implies multiple actions, so probably not a turn. So is that a night? A chapter? A story?

                                                  Likewise, Curse of the Maelstrom has a duration when you target an individual, but not for generally invoking spectres (I assume it's supposed to be until sunrise too). It could have done with another editing pass.

                                                  Generally, though, I like most of the Discipline changes.
