    [V20] PEACH Making Kuei-Jin into Kindred

    05-06-2020, 06:16 PM
    Pretty much what the topic title says.

    Since the odds of getting a proper KotE20 seem rather slim and to make my next campaign run smoother I’m looking at turning the Kindred of the East into Cainite vampires. I’m also hoping that bringing them into line with the rest of the Kindred it will smooth out some of the unfortunate implications of making all the Oriental undead a separate species.

    In universe, the notion that Kuei-jin are a separate species of supernatural is a combination of propaganda (from both sides) and sincere confusion due to the Eastern Kindred descending from an unrecorded Antediluvian rather than being a bloodline of one the known western thirteen.

    In this case, I’m running with the notion that there were just 13 Antediluvians is a translation error; the actual translation of the Book of Nod is “and their numbers were 3 by 10” (as was suggested in a footnote in the book of the same name) and not “3 and 10.”

    The 13 known Antediluvians were just those who found each other after the Flood and sired progeny while others either died in the battle with the Second Generation, never came out of torpor after the flood or chose to use being scattering by the flood/presumed dead to strike out on their own unnoticed... one of these latter ones became the ancestor of the Kindred of the East.

    Anyway, I figure as a clan their culture falls mostly into line with the fluff of Kindred of the East (i.e. they organize into various courts just as their brethren gather in cities under a prince). They have an utter disdain for embracing (though their Beast has other ideas) and view their ghouls as their half-damned children (i.e. KotE Dhampyrs are actually either ghouls or, in some cases, Revenants).

    The Dharma are Paths of Enlightenment, albeit ones that retain the virtues of Conscience (Hun), Self-Control (Yin) and Courage (Yang) with their Beast (P’o) opposing their Humanity/Path rating. Their bearings improve social actions just as high Humanity does.

    Their Clan weakness is a particularly cunning Beast that seizes control whenever they fail a Conscience roll; basically a separate personality in line with the P’o natures. It is while in this state that they will Embrace with abandon; the idea that the wicked spontaneously arise as Kuei-Jin is a lie they convince themselves of to absolve themselves of the guilt of having damned another soul to unlife because they lost control of their P’o.

    Their clan disciplines are Blood Sorcery (representing their control over Chi... the Path of Blood’s ‘Theft of Vitae’ is where legends that the Elders can feed off the breath of mortals arises), Vicissitude (representing the various shintai; blood, flesh, bone, etc.) and Auspex (representing the Soul Arts that typically revolve around perception of and influence over the soul).

                      I see two general overall methods for converting KotE into Vampire. The quick and simple which makes them most like other Cainites and a more long and complex keeps more of their unique elements intact. Given that you are looking to keep them as Cainites, you would probably benefit most from picking bits and pieces you like from the various options presented.

                      I will be referring to Kuei-jin as WK (Wan Kuei) from this point forward.

                      Quick and Simple

                      The primary mindset behind this method is the philosophy of "close enough", find something similar enough among Cainite rules and call it good.

                      • Clan
                        • Wan Kuei - WK are all childer of Grand Arhat Xue
                          • Disciplines - Auspex, Thaumaturgy, Vicissitude
                            • Overall I think the Discipline spread you selected covers a fairly broad amount of what the KotE rules grant WK.
                          • Weakness - Xue's philosophical studies mark all his childer for good and ill.
                            • All WK gain the Medium Merit however this awareness of the supernal world comes at a cost. All WK suffer from the Short Fuse Flaw and the Multiple Personalities Derangement. Their other personality is their Beast, made more powerful and cunning by Xue's using it for spiritual ascendance (see P'o under Longer and Complex below for types).
                      • Dharmas - Find a Road that's close
                        • Howl of the Devil Tiger
                          • Road of Sin (Path of the Devil)
                        • Way of the Resplendent Crane
                          • Road of Kings
                        • Song of the Shadow
                          • Road of Bones
                        • Path of a Thousand Whispers
                          • Road of Metamorphosis
                        • Dance of the Thrashing Dragon
                          • Road of Humanity
                        • Flame of the Rising Phoenix
                          • Road of Humanity (Path of Breath)
                        • Tempest of the Inward Focus
                          • Path of Self-Focus
                        • Face of the Gods
                          • Road of Kings (Path of Devaraja)
                        • Spirit of the Living Earth
                          • Path of the Eightfold Wheel
                        • Scorpion Eaters
                          • Road of Sin
                        • Akuma
                          • Path of Evil Revelations



                      Longer and Complex

                      Rather than sacrifice much of what make WK different from Cainites, this method seeks to preserve some of that divide while still reflecting WK within the rules framework of Cainite rules.

                      • Clan
                        • Wan Kuei are not Vampires in the traditional sense but instead spirits that have returned to this world after escaping Yomi. If you are treating them as purely Cainite the following are considered the Clan Weakness of the descendants of Xue.
                          • WK do not possess Generation as normal Cainites. Instead their strength of blood is tied closely to their enlightenment (see Dharma below). A WK has an equivalent Generation based on their Dharma rating. If you are treating them as purely Cainite then these peculiarities comes from Xue's studies and understanding of balancing power with enlightenment.
                            • 0 - A WK in the chi'h-mei state is treated as 13th Generation with the Vulgar and Conspicuous Consumption Flaws
                            • 1 - 12th
                            • 2 - 11th
                            • 3 - 10th
                            • 4 - 9th
                            • 5 - 8th*
                            • 6 - 7th
                            • 7 - 6th
                            • 8 - 5th
                            • 9 - 4th
                            • 10 - 3rd
                              • * - Upon achieving this level of enlightenment the WK can feed on breath rather than just blood. They can use the Valeren (Watcher) 5 power Soul's Breath.
                          • P'o - WK's beasts are more aware than Cainites, a personal dark side.
                            • P'o Archetype - Each WK selects a personality for their P'o to represent the cunning of their particular beast.
                              • Bandit - Your P'o is a rampaging, savage monster
                              • Barbarian - Your P'o rejects the true ways in favor of the decadent Cainites
                              • Deciever - Your P'o undermines the Hun through lies and treachery
                              • Demon - Your P'o is a grotesque monster who lives only to destroy
                              • Fool - Your P'o distracts you from the true path in favor of the easy way
                              • Legalist - Your P'o insists on rigid obedience and even more rigid punishments
                              • Monkey - Your P'o is a capricious creature bent on preventing inner harmony
                              • Slave - Your P'o seeks to abuse you before your "superiors"
                            • Shadow Soul - In addition to Fire Soul (Frenzy) and Wave Soul (Rotschreck) WK have one additional state of mind to worry about, Shadow Soul. During Shadow Soul the P'o of the WK seizes control of the WK's body for the scene. Shadow Soul is resisted with Conscience/Conviction.
                              • Triggers
                                • Acts in violation of Dharma
                                • Botched Willpower roll
                                • Emerging from Fire Soul or Wave Soul
                                • Botched Discipline roll
                                • Great stress, trauma, or shame
                                • Meeting conditions under the P'o Archetype
                          • Optional - If you aren't making them purely Cainites I would suggest the following changes.
                            • Eat Food - All WK can eat and can digest what they consume (thought it offers no nourishment) and can get drunk (though it takes 10 times the normal amount of alcohol).
                            • Cannot Embrace - As souls escaped from Yomi they cannot pass on their state to another.
                            • Cannot Ghoul - As they are not Cainites their blood has no power to enslave another.
                            • Cannot Feed from Animals - The blood of lesser beings lack the spiritual strength to sate a WK's needs.
                            • Fangless - WK do not naturally have fangs, they must invoke the power of their P'o to create them. A WK may spend 1 BP to grow fangs like those of a Cainite or 2 BP to grow a fanged maw that inflicts Str +2 aggravated damage. Such fangs last for a scene.
                            • Yin/Yang - As beings of Yin and Yang WK can spend blood to do the following (this replaces the ability to spend BP to imitate life).
                              • Gain Deathsight or Lifesight for one scene
                              • To rise each night the WK can choose whether they want to be Yin or Yang aspected.
                                • Yin Aspected - Need not spend a BP to rise for 10 days. Gains the Eerie Presence Flaw (and loses Yang's Blush of Health) as their cold aura and pallor unnerves mortals.
                                • Yang Aspected - Must spend a BP to rise again the next night. Gains the Blush of Health Merit (and loses Yin's Eerie Presence) as the brightly burning Yang energy makes them seem more alive.
                                • Alternate Staking - WK can only be staked by the material opposing their current aspect.
                                  • Yin Aspected WK can only be staked by wood
                                  • Yang Aspected WK can only be staked by metal
                            • Spirit Speech - A WK can spend a BP to speak with spirits for a scene (assuming they can detect them).
                      • Disciplines
                        • As spirits returned to flesh WK do not have clans. If you are treating them as purely Cainite then their lack of Clan Disciplines is from Xue's expansive use of various powers to achieve enlightenment, unlike him to be a jack of all trades they end up as masters of none. As such they purchase Disciplines as a Caitiff. They may place their starting dots in any Discipline but should keep an eye toward what would be common among their Dharma.
                      • Dharma - Function like Roads in DAV20
                        • Virtues - These decisions are influenced by the DAV20 version of Virtues over the V20 ones. DAV20 takes a much less inhuman approach to the alternate Virtues. Aura modifiers are applied to social interactions with mortals enlightened monsters have better control over their facades (they all can imitate life as well, spending one blood point for each dot of Road less than eight). The interaction between Instincts and Frenzy is described in such a way as to be less destructive sounding and more functionally directed (in my head I picture a Devil Tiger focusing on punishing a particularly wicked target while a Thrashing Dragon might end up indulging their desires with abandon).
                          • Howl of the Devil Tiger
                            • Virtues - Conviction, Instinct
                          • Way of the Resplendent Crane
                            • Virtues Conscience, Self-Control
                          • Song of the Shadow
                            • Virtues - Conviction, Self-Control
                          • Path of a Thousand Whispers
                            • Virtues - Conviction, Self-Control
                          • Dance of the Thrashing Dragon
                            • Virtues - Conscience, Instincts
                          • Flame of the Rising Phoenix
                            • Virtues - Conscience, Self-Control
                          • Tempest of the Inward Focus
                            • Virtues - Conviction, Instincts
                          • Face of the Gods
                            • Virtues - Conviction, Self-Control
                          • Spirit of the Living Earth
                            • Virtues - Conscience, Self-Control
                          • Scorpion Eaters
                            • Virtues - Conviction, Instincts
                        • Aura - WK are not merely guided by their Dharma but also influenced by Direction, the astrologically determined guide to their manner. As such each WK should select an Aura suited to their Dharma, Direction, or both. For example an East facing Bone Flower might select Silence as befitting their Yin laced Dharma or Normalcy to better function among the mortals they are to see to.
                      • Powers (Disciplines, Chi, and Rites)
                        • Kindred of the East was designed with crossover in mind from it's inception. Things like Ghostsight, Spirit sight, and the like are built in from the start. Similarly their Disciplines tend to be a mix of different effects that are thematic for the Discipline but do not have a direct 1 to 1 analogue to Vampire Disciplines. After trying various methods of determining Clan Disciplines, and not being satisfied with them, I returned to the simple Caitiff solution. Anything would be kinder than KotE Current Rating x8 for Disciplines.
                        • Daimonion - This Discipline's effects are similar to a number of different WK Discipline effects especially in Cultivation, Chi'iu Muh (especially the P'o effects), and Conflagration/Tormented Essence is very similar to blasts of Ghost-flame Shintai.
                        • Dementation - If using the DAV20 rules I would remove the required acquisition of a derangement (they already have the P'o).

                              Thank you for the input everyone. Definitely some good ideas here.

                              Just for some added clarity on my intentions; I feel stories benefit greatly from conservation of detail. Fantasy stories particularly benefit from having a “unified theory of the supernatural” where the primary deviations from the real world spring from a single source.

                              So, for this Vampire campaign I’m not using the animistic Garou of WtA or the consensual reality of Mage because they conflict with Vampire’s supernatural themes of suffering under a divine curse.

                              So the Lupines here are going to be a mortal lineage suffering under another divine curse recorded in myth as the story of Lycaon* Similarly, magic comes only from God (Faith and natural magics like alchemy/herbalism which use the natural properties of things created by God), Vitae (twisting the divine power of the curse) or calling upon spirits (dead or Fallen).

                              And in the city I’m setting this tale in there is a rather sizable Chinatown where I don’t want the supernaturals to be limited to a few alchemists and the same old western vampire Clans, but I also want to keep to the themes of Cainite vampires rather than those of KotE (nothing wrong with them, but the focus on transcendence from the sins of one’s past life just doesn’t mesh as well with the theme of trying to hold onto your humanity in the face of a curse laid upon you through no fault your own as I’d like).

                              Thus, eastern vampires from one or more clans adapted to their environment, but are still descended from the same Curse. They are their own Sect, but they and their ancestors are still every bit a part of the larger Jyhad and War of Ages that faces all Cainites (hmm... perhaps the Yama Kings they so fear are actually the fractured stories of their own slumbering Antediluvians).

                              Another part too is that I just really loved the 1e Core Book passage about Other Clans; “There are said to be thirteen Clans in all, but it is possible there are many more than that, especially when one considers the unknown secrets of the Eastern Kindred, who undoubtedly have their own unique Clans.”

                              Having it only be the thirteen clans after all and turning the Eastern Kindred into “Not Actually Kindred” just feels like both squandering that ancient bit of lore and muddying up the themes of Vampire in favor of making them more cross-over splat compatible.

                              * If someone in game really wants to dig into how a story about Zeus punishing a mortal links to the biblical God cursing Caine, then I get to have a scholarly NPC dive deep into an explanation of proto-Indo-European mythology and how classical polytheism arose from conquest-based syncretism favoring political stability over religious purity; i.e. Deus fater, father Zeus, Iupiter, the Sky Father wasn’t actually a promiscuous dick... but every PIE tribe had an origin myth where their people arose from a union of their Sky God and an Earth Goddess.

                              When one tribe conquered another they either said “Oh, your Sky God and ours are the same” and added another goddess their Sky God slept with to the list or they said “Oh, your God is just a weaker brother/child of our Supreme God” and so you got your Hades/Dispater and Poseidons added for political expediency.

                              The myth of Lycaon is just one more of these tales... a mortal who attempted to test God’s (i.e. the Sky Father) omniscience via filicide and cannibalism and was cursed for it.
