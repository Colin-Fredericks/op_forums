    [V5] Upcoming Projects

    03-22-2021, 08:56 PM
    I thought we'd have a thread to discuss the various upcoming projects and products that we have to look forward to from White Wolf.

    Onyx Path

    * Forbidden Religions: The extended material for Cults of the Blood Gods' blood cults with a whole bunch of new ones ranging from Golconda cults to the Infermal. I'm very impressed with the writers.

    White Wolf

    * The Sabbat: Don't know much about this one other than it has been very hotly anticipated and is being written by Justin Achilli. It will also contain information on the Paths of Enlightenment.

    * Second Inquisition: We know even less about this but I am hoping for big things from this one. Fingers crossed for an update of William Shepard.

    * Book of Nod: Hardback reprint.

    Comic Books

    * Winter's Teeth: The comic has been renewed as an ongoing due to high sales for the first 5 issues. Hopefully, it will continue on for a long time yet.

    UPDATED 9/22/2021: Finished with issue #10

    * Crimson Thaw: A continuation of Winter's Teeth lasting for three issues.

    UPDATED 9/22/2021: Issue #1 now available

    Audiobooks

    * Walk Among Us: Being released on May 5th in non-audiobook format.

    UPDATED: Now available

    * Novels: The Clan Novels, Dark Age Clan Novels, and Grail Covenant will remain with Crossroad Press for the time being.

    Video Games

    * Bloodlines 2: We don't know what the situation is there but hopefully the new developers will try to finish what was already done.

    UPDATED: Indefinitely delayed

    * Swangsong: A Telltale-like adventure game where you are three vampires involved in a plot to take over the city of Boston just after it merges its domain with a number of surrounding territories.

    * Out for Blood: A Choice of Games sequel to Night Road, except you're one of the Second Inquisition on a mission to slay as many vampires as possible...or become one.

    UPDATED: Now available on Steam

    * Coteries of New York/Shadows of New York: They just released the soundtrack on vinyl. They've also got plans to release this on physical disc in stores.

    UPDATED: Now available in physical format.

    * Parliament of Knives: A Choice of Games political game of high stakes and Camarilla fru-fruness.

    * Bloodhunt: A battle royal game coming soon to a PC and console near you.

    Board Games

    * Chapters: They just received a bunch more pledges to continue development and are apparently continuing on with development.
    Last edited by CTPhipps; 09-22-2021, 10:52 AM.
