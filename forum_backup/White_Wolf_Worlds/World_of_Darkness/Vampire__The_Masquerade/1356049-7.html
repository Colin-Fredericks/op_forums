        That's not exactly what the manuscript claims. SOME Hecata believe the reason their bloodline is the only one not suffering from the beckoning is their antediluvian was truly killed. Some others retort he did came back as a spectre. Some others suggest Cappadocius' soul was broken in 3 fragments and that's the reason the beckoning is not affecting the Hecata. Other suggest even different theories. Anyway, the reason behind the beckoning is not spelled out. It MIGHT be the Antediluvians but it can as well be something else. What's stated outright is the Hecata are not affected by the Beckoning which is pretty huge and that's part of the reason they are probably the most powerful clan in the modern nights a condition that allows them to stay independent of the other sects.


        @Grumpy RPG Reviews:
        I don't think so. The endless night being foiled is pretty big (it was Augustus' shot at winning the Jyahd) but in terms of development Augustus was still a 3rd gen vampire. He did not get all the memories and power from Cappadocius but he was still the guy who invented necromancy as a kindred discipline. Him being alive somewhere has more potential for future stories than him being dead for good. That said, his fate is left ambiguous, what's sure is (1)he's not in charge anymore and that (2) his clan rebelled against him and those loyal to him, (3) the one who orchestrated his downfall was the Chapucin and he's also the one who allowed the various branches of the Hecata to find a common ground
