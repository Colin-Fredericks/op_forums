            Originally posted by Kakost View Post
            Well, before of V5 many of the clans cultures were just simply that, just a cultural thing, not something intrinsic. So the Ventrue were loyalty due to culture, the gangrel are primitivists due to culture and so, save a few exceptions (like nosfs).
            True to a degree, but their weakness usually has some relationship with their culture and, more importantly, their cultures make sense as social developments. They give their members something that helps to survive and find a niche within Kindred society. They also have some momentum, something that doesn't outright force members to be like the rest, but at least reward those who fit. The Ravnos have a culture of... not fitting? Not even trying? Squandering opportunities and burning bridges for giggles? For all of eternity?

            Gangrel are survivalists, and while they're loners most of the time, they usually know each other and have some means of contact. If you fit the bill of the clan, you'll adapt to your territory, hunt in the wild and be self-sufficient. If you fit the bill of the Ravnos as we have it until now, you'll be a pariah everywhere and end up with nothing to your name after centuries, because you leave everything every time. And why? How to get a good enough explanation (we do have explanations for clan cultures) for this and what good does it bring to the game outside a very specific trope with limited use? (Nomad Ravnos arrives, everyone expects petty problems, the newcomer delivers, the city get rid of the troublemaker)

            Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
            monteparnas
            How do Ravnos organize? Phones? Before the invention of phones? Animalism? Birds?
            Potentially, they could learn a little thaumaturgy and communicate with their sires.
            My first idea was for leaving messages being a more common thing than sending news, specially in the past. But instead of Hobo Code-style shenanigans, it could be through stories and even books. A member of the clan disguises and embellishes their messages as tall tales and stories the locals will likely repeat for a long time, maybe they leave behind cheap story books of mediocre quality, and those stories have hints other Ravnos are likely to get that it is a message of a clan mate.

            Of course, such messages are unreliable in accuracy as the reader may misinterpret some things, but that's not necessarily a bug. When a Ravnos arrives at a city, they look for the places and people most likely to still remember old local tales or something like that.

            This is still a forming idea, but basically I think they would have a system to leave messages more than to spread news, as they focus more on finding their own next roads than being quick to contact the others. Their original Discipline spread could help, Animalism lets them spread tales and information through animals, Fortitude let them leave messages in places that are hard to reach because they're dangerous, and Chimerstry may be used to leave or hide messages.

            But otherwise I think just using letters would have been enough for most of them in the past, and a common information to spread would be your next destination, so most members always have a rough idea of where at least a few others are at any given time. Some could have magic rituals and whatnot, but that wouldn't be the rule even if we alter them into a Blood Magic wielding clan.

            Originally posted by Aleph View Post
            A role that's not taken could be that of a "bard" or "mysterious mystic", keeper/seeker of legends. As Nosferatu gather information and trivia that they can tipically verify, Ravnos could instead trade on unverifiable stories.
            That's an interesting approach, although I also think they could trade in news among the Kindred, too.

            In the past travelers were an important source of information about the world, and the Ravnos could be the ones transporting news from one place to another, even letters and packages, depending on how much they could be trusted for this. And I do think that easing the "untrustworthy" aspect on them is necessary, anyway.

            So when a Ravnos arrive, it marks the arrival of news and stories from other Domains, the things that would hardly get out even today in Kindred society. They bring legends and mysteries, but also gossip and more up-to-date information on what's going around the world. It would be a prime opportunity for a devious member of the clan to fabricate stories for their own benefit, but would also be a niche for those who make a name for themselves as good on their words.

                                                            Originally posted by monteparnas View Post
                                                            As long as that's put in such a way that they can live in the same place for decades exploring details and niches, I think it may work. I do want the possibility of a Ravnos staying for a reasonable amount of time to form connections and taking part in politics, as that both give us an easier time with PCs and increases the range of uses for them in the games. But then, most would be wanderers sooner than later, and that part would be a reasonably justified clan culture.

                                                            Not necessarily the winner, but definitely pin that idea for later!
                                                            Well, it could be that they can stay in the same place for decades, but once in a while they just need a "weekend off" to "recharge the batteries". So, every once in a while they have an urge to travel somewhere new. This gets worse for elders, because while neonates can explore neighborhoods of their own hometown they never went in before, the longer you live the smaller the world gets... So after exploring all neighborhoods of your town, you'll have to explore neighbor towns; after that, neighbor states. And so it goes, always searching for somewhere new.

                                                            This could have all sorts of impacts - first, elders have a harder time, and the older the Ravnos, the more prone to becoming a full nomad.

                                                            On the other hand thou, going into torpor is a great way to "refresh the scenery" without leaving your home!

                                                            Now, while this is a nuisance for them and even potentially dangerous, it also means they arent just a bunch of weirdos that every once in a while pop up out of nowhere, cause a ton of mayhem and disappear back again, as in the weird Doctor Who episode of the week.

                                                            They could live in a fixed location, take permanent residence in a town where they'll have their power base, and engage in cutthroat backstabbing politics like all the other "pretty" clans.

                                                            Their insatiable curiosity however is what gives them a fame of "trouble makers"; more often than not, you'll find a Ravnos sticking their noses where they dont belong, if you get my meaning. It's just irresistible to them, their curiosity is simply too great.

                                                            This means they would make great spies and infiltrators, for instance.

                                                            Also, a small inter clan culture that I believe could be a nice touch; you would have two main "cultural zeitgeists" within the clan, like you yourself already realized, some of the Ravnos would just simply "live in the roads", and never stick on one place for long. Those would follow all the traditional rituals - present to the local Prince, enjoy their allowed time of welcoming, and taking off. Those travellers would do great for both/either Camarilla or Sabbath, bringing outside news.

                                                            And the second time would be those that take permanent residence (althought having constant absences to explore close places for some periods of time).

                                                            Now, those two groups need not be totally permanently distinct, as if two forever apart bloodlines. Instead, they can be fully interchangeable: some Ravnos spend a century or 2 in a single city, disappearing 1 week every month to explore around, but after those 2 centuries he takes off and spends 1 entire century wandering all over, until deciding to take residence in a new city on the other side of the globe for the next 50 years and so on.

                                                            Also, another important aspect of Ravnos culture would become "Claim of Hospitality"; traveller Ravnos can most of the time count on being welcomed by other Ravnos that have permanent residency somewhere.

                                                            This culture of the clan would also cause lots of political problems; imagine a Prince that has problems with overpopulation, but the local Ravnos keep insisting on allowing their clan members full hospitality.

                                                            Also, cainites being naturally paranoid, it aint easy to deal with those annoying nomads always sticking their noses were they dont belong.

                                                            So, I guess Curiosity could be an excellent Weakness/Compulsion for them, that would make the Ravnos far more interesting and part of the world rather than just the weird happenstance that annoys everybody once in a while.

                                                            And, they would still be competition to the Gangrel.

                                                            Just imagine the Ravnos "clan's reunions", where they share stories of their travels and share among one another tales of exotic lands and incredible adventures.

                                                            Ravnos would also be incredible bards due to that; I envision Toreador being fascinated by their storytelling skills, while nosfs are always all too eager to try to farm any useful informations among their stories, and Ventrue giving the grump face to those undisciplined rabble constantly bringing dynamism to their courts.

                                                            I mean, I guess it's better than making them automatic trouble magnets that cant interact with the cainite society

                                                            This could even create their own peculiar
