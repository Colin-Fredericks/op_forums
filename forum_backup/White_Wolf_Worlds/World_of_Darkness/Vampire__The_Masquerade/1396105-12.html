            To address some of @Taggie's points:

            1. If you can't appreciate the classic literary Bram Stoker's Dracula with its depth of characterization, use of history, and variety of powers as well as occult references than you are the one in the wrong. The dismissing of a desire to have a clan that PROPERLY respresents Dracula is nothing to be ashamed of and your dismissal of it is flat out disappointing. I also have read Necroscope and while they're fine, Stoker obviously 100 percent trumps Lumley. Dracula got improperly broken up among Gangrel, Ventrue, and Tremere.

            2. I think the Old Clan Tzimisce should be available as options for the Camarilla and Anarchs because they're a fascinating bunch of people. The Dark Ages Tzimisce were a bunch of awesome characters and the Old Clan Tzimisce are the ones who carry their spirit into Modern Times. They should also be part of the Blood Cults.

            3. I mentioned I have a distaste for "Flesh Monkeys" over in another thread. Those are people who don't have any real respect for the Tzimisce's feudal lord status, honor codes, or inhuman ideologies beyond Vicissitude. I basically referred to them as Fishmalks for Tzimisce. I don't think that's the case here but it's very irritating that you keep putting down the idea that Vicissitude somehow is more important than actual WRITING for the Clan and makes me think you have a somewhat shallow view of them.

            4. I have no interest in getting rid of the Sabbat Tzimisce and I don't even dislike Vicissitude or its effects. I think the Old Clan Tzimisce lose a lot of their flavor if they don't have the Modern Tzimisce to contrast themselves against. I've stated repeatedly I want the Sabbat to return and be detailed in ways beyond "they're off fighting the Gehenna Crusade."

            Originally posted by Taggie View Post

            Considering that you consistently call the Tzim as they are in V20 and previous editions, degerates, and your attack on Viscititude, turn about is fair play.
            Yeah...they're SABBAT. Since 2nd Edition, the Sabbat have been portrayed as the barbarian horde at the Camarilla's gates.

            And spare me the argument that I attack Vicissitude because that holds no water.

            How I feel about Vicissitude

            Vicissitude is probably the third or fourth most important element of the Tzimsice. It's important to the clan and allows a lot of philosophical and interesting elements. It's overused, overpowered, and is in dire need of a rules update. Nothing makes my eyes roll more than the idea of a guy who envisions a Tzimisce as a guy who makes bone and flesh chairs without explaining how this relates to his desire to transcend his vampiric weaknesses, scholarship, or Feudal Lord status (Tzimisce are scholars and scientists every bit as much as feudal lords). Flesh Monkeys are a problem that has resulted in the Clan getting Flanderized (see TV tropes) into a parody of itself.

            Tzimsice should absolutely have all of the powers that they had from Vicissitude as an option for them because a huge chunk of lore is related to Vicissitude. Revenant families can only be created by a combination of Blood Sorcery and Vicissitude (probably) rather than just breeding ghouls. Also, while deformed evil monsters are not the most important part of the Tzimisce, they WERE a part of the Tzimisce.

            I do think that Vicissitude is getting chopped up. They mentioned Tzimisce as having Protean in Chicago by Night and the context is that a trans woman used that to complete her transition prior to medical advances (and post-Embrace). In effect, that she used Protean the same way a Tzimsice used Vicissitude. I don't think there's any point discussing whether this is a good idea because I think it's going to happen whether people like it or not. So the only thing to do is discuss how to implement it rather than complain about it. It's been there, done, and now it's time to follow up on the effects.

            If you don't understand what I mean by this, I think Vicissitude is not getting removed, I think Vicisstiude is getting nerfed. I think the Protean replacement will probably mean that Tzimisce will be able to change their bodies but won't be able to change the bodies of others save at higher levels or with some combination Discipline. I recommended Blood Sorcery/Protean for warping others because I think Tzimisce should have Blood Sorcery as a Clan Discipline in place of Auspex. I really think they need to adapt all of the Path of Bio-Thaumaturgical Experimentation to Tzimisce and allow them to have access to it. They always should have had it rather than the Black Hand.

            But yes, without the ability to warp flesh of themselves and others, the Tzimisce lose a lot.

            Of course, all of this is for V5 because none of he matters here apply to previous Editions and if you don't love V5 why would you care?

                                            Okay, there's a lot to unpack here, Taggie but you've been extremely misinformed about a lot of things. Not the least of which being that you seem to be ignoring the existence of the Strigoi and moroi. The moroi are especially notable because they're actually the sexy vampire that English people claim to have invented but are certainly not. The vampire legends of Romanian were combined with witches but while living strigoi existed, so did undead strigoi with one becoming the latter.

                                            Believe me, I spent years investigating this stuff and almost did my dissertation on it.

                                            As for the claims about Bram Stoker here, you're making an elaborate series of justifications that all fall apart under a judicious application of Occam's Razor. Which is the fact that Bram Stoker's Dracula is not actually the origin of any of the Old Clan Tzimisce's ideas or the subject of some great conspiracy of political theory. In fact, it's basically just a rewrite and combination of Polidori's THE VAMPYRE and Le Fanu's CARMILLA. This is pretty much confirmed by Dracula's Guest where it's ABUNDANTLY obvious that he's just combining the two stories.

                                            However, we actually DO get the politics of Dracula in the OTHER edition of Dracula. Which is THE POWERS OF DARKNESS which is effectively Dracula when it was written originally before switching around the locations, removing the sex, and removing the anti-racist anti-militarist anti-Social Darwinist politics. Dracula is then described as a German supremacist and a believer of the mass murder of inferior races as well as the creation of a massive ethnostate with himself at the head of it. Dracula's Romanian heritage doesn't matter because he's already taken over vast amounts of Germany's nobility with his secret cult. Dracula's Guest references that the vampires are vast in number in Germany and he's turned much of their nobility.

                                            Stoker removed all of the politics of DRACULA for the purposes of sales, probably, and to avoid being overtly political under the censorship of the time but was making a early anti-fascist anti-racist screed.

The fact that Carmilla takes place in Austria may have been an influence.
