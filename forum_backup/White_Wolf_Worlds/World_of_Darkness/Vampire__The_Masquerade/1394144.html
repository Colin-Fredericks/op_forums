                                                  Originally posted by Penelope View Post
                                                  I’m probably gonna get to run a short (like five or six sessions) Vampire: the Masquerade game sometime around the end of the year. Which would you recommend, the Transylvania Chronicles or the Ventrue Chronicle? Or maybe the Giovanni Chronicles (though I heard the type in the PDFs is really small and blurry)?
                                                  TC give me a nostalgic feeling. It's an important product, one that shaped metaplot, but in all honesty it's not that good.
                                                  Compared to the VC, the TC are much more extensive (4 books vs 1) and the various stories sometimes feel disjointed, simplistic and sometimes fail to give the ST a coherent sequence of events (as it's often the case, the ST needs to fill the gaps).
                                                  The VC on the other hand are a self contained much les ambitious project that works well within itself but completely rejects the lofty goals of the TC (a chronicle of ages introducing the end times).

                                                  All in all they are two very different products, if you are willing to work a lot doing the TC can certainly be more rewarding, otherwise go for the VC, which is easier to run and less work intensive for you as the ST.

                                                  P.S.

                                                  The Giovanni Chronicles are similar to the TC in scope and a little better in the execution imo. Be aware that the 4th book ("Nuova Malattia") implies a radical change compared to the previous three.

                                                          Originally posted by Penelope View Post
                                                          I’m probably gonna get to run a short (like five or six sessions) Vampire: the Masquerade game sometime around the end of the year. Which would you recommend, the Transylvania Chronicles or the Ventrue Chronicle? Or maybe the Giovanni Chronicles (though I heard the type in the PDFs is really small and blurry)?
                                                          Transylvania Transylvania Chronicles starts at the end of XII century and lasts till 1999 (and you could fill the gaps with single adventures like "Under the Black Cross"). There are also rules for downtime (additional XPs for periods between parts of TC) do I think it's too big for 5-6 sessions. On the other hand I think that the story is great because it allows PCs to influence the metaplot.
                                                          The same for Giovanni Chronicles: it starts in 1444 and ends at the end of XIX century so it could be too big. Also I find the story less interesting and periods of "inactivity" between parts are greater than in TC. On the other hand the last part ("Nuova Malattia") requires new characters so I think it could be played during 5 or 6 sessions. Still it's good to read parts 1-3 just to know the story.
                                                          Ventrue Chronicles are the worst. It is only one book, composed of 3 stories. In the second one (almost 700 years after the first one) you play a a Child of the character from the first one, in the third, at the end of XX century you play as a Grandchilde of the character from the first one. So it could be played in 5 or 6 sessions but the players won't have time to get into characters. Also the PCs should be Ventrue so your players could not like it.
                                                          I'm not a big fan of short games, I prefere the ones which ends by character's death (or player resignation). If I were in your place I would create a big campaign, starting by Ventrue Chronicles (as some kind of Prelude, before the VC beginning), then move to TC (to let PCs gain some XPs and to let players get the atmosphere), then back to the first part of VC, then back to TC and so on. If someone's character dies (and if I don't have any NPC to give to the unfortunate player/s) then I would run first part of GC for them and after that bind the two stories (which could be done during Convention of Thorns in TC part II). BUT it would last waaaayyy too long than your 5-6 sessions.
