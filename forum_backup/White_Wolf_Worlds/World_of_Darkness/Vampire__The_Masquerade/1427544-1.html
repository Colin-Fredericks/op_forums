            OP is playing V5 right?
            In V5, you need to minmax. Having an 'ordinary' dicepool of 4 will have you fail half the time for easy tasks. Anything less than 5 dice might as well be 0.

            If you're playing a different edition, Having 5 in an attribute and 5 in a skill might be a reason for embrace. It's fine, and you probably won't end up so weak with other stuff.
            Your player sound really new either way. 5 in an attribute or skill is a strong example of deminishing returns.

            Also 5 dots of melee for a 'knife wielding assassin' is... a little silly. You never want to get into an even knife fight where you can use your skills, because you'll both get cut up either way, you want to use a knife when your opponent is unarmed or unaware or a knight in heavy armour... you don't need five dots of knifing. If your opponent is gonna actually fight back with a weapon then you'll want a longer weapon.
