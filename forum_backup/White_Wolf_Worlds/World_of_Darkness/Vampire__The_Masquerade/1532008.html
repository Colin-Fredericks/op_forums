                  There are at least four places called Abyss in the cosmology. Their relationship is not given, so they may be related or not, ST's choice:

                  1- DtF Abyss, prison of the Fallen;
                  2- Middle Umbra Abyss, source of Harano;
                  3- Obtenebration's Abyss, lovecraftian realm;
                  4- The Oblivion, bottom of the worlds.

                  You can work any relationship you want between them, all of them have been hinted at some moment to be related or the same realm as each of the others.

                  But all those hints include good reasons to take them with a grain of salt, specially because they don't really fill similar roles or work together in any capacity.

                  Unless you want to specifically unveil such secrets in your game, they work just fine as distinct things and any relationship is mere theory, discussion fodder, with no actual effect.

                              The general idea I'm working with is to make a distinction between realms of the Inner Darkness and realms of the Outer Darkness. That is, if we look at the Umbra as if it were a lens to focus emotions, faith, ideas, dreams, and all the other abstract stuff there are two ways for this to happen:

                              1. Mundus projects into the Umbra, which creates Spirits, Demons, Ghosts, Gods and whatnot as ephemeral representations of those concepts.

                              2. Something from outside projects into Mundus. This would be all the eldritch things- Neverborn, the Children, things from outside our universe.

                              The canvas for these realms is the primordial dark, ie the biblical Tehom or whatever you want to call a Nothing that can Create. Around and between everything flows Oblivion, the absence of everything, null, the as-Nothing-as-it-gets.

                              I'm actually writing a Baali NPC at the moment who made a science of the layers of Dark and how to access them. The V5 version of the Oblivion discipline irks me because it conflates both concepts of inner and outer realms by way of combining Necromancy and Obtenebration. For a V20 variant, I would rather combine Obtenebration, Nihilistics, Daimonion, and some of the Dark Thauma paths.
