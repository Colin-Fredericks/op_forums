            The thing is that none of the many "Wars" in WoD are truly unlimited warfare. They are actually "gang raids", with very restricted local conflicts erupting time to time between local forces.

            That holds true for the Jihad, for Camarilla vs Sabbat assaults, for the "war" between Tremere and Tzimisce or the "Massassa War" of them against the OoH, it's true about the Ascension War or the Weres war against the Wyrm. It's small localized groups maneuvering against one another in the shadows, trying to locate one another, mustering resources when they do and striking at one fortified position at a time when they manage to get a jump on their enemies.

            The only REAL wars in the WoD are waged by Wraiths in the Underworld, between Dark Kingdoms or against countless hordes of Specters during Maelstroms. In those you do indeed can get fights with MILLIONS of forces on each side.

            In any other case, it's a thing of a few dozens of individuals on each side, with a few hundreds on some rare occasions, at most of the time.

            And an "open war" between splats wouldnt be much different, I mean, they all already live constantly into "open wars" amongst themselves - Camarilla vs Sabbat, Traditions vs Technocracy and Weres vs Wyrm. That "open war" however is like a war of attrition between gangs.

                                        There's enough bullshit at the higher end tiers for all of the splats that it's plausible that any one vs one confrontation could end in the favor of one splat or another. For example, werewolves have a gift that just stops vampires from being able to spend blood points. If a werewolf hits a Methuselah that's invested most of their exp into Disciplines that require blood point expenditures than goes in for the attack, that Methuselah is very likely going to die. On the other hand, if the Methuselah has invested more heavily into Disciplines that don't require blood points, then he's likely not terribly affected and you're going to probably end up with a dead (or Dominated) werewolf.

                                        Ultimately any sort of confrontation runs into the issue that characters won't have every single Discipline or Gift or Sphere, and so they're going to end up with potential holes that another splat could exploit to defeat them.

                                        This is also how things are explained "in game" where you might have splats defeating one another in ways that "don't make sense" in terms of optimized white room combat. Going along with this, in the idea of vampire vs werewolf, in general from the in-game perspective, a werewolf will defeat a vampire. A pack of vampires can defeat a werewolf. An Elder vampire can defeat a werewolf, but a pack of werewolves will kill an elder. Then you get into Methuselahs who are capable of killing packs of werewolves. Of course then you have Rank 5 Garou and a pack of those guys will trash most any Methuselahs unless they're on the level of someone like Baba Yaga and even then that sort of fight could go either way.

                                        Ultimately the most powerful of vampires (Antediluvians) sit on the apex of the WoD power level scale, on par with Wyrm Talons, Yama Kings, Neverborn, etc. Defeating one is something that would take multiple werewolf tribes, or a huge cross section of the Technocracy to oppose and (possibly) defeat.

                                        So vampires are both the "punching bag" of the WoD and also some of the most powerful and dangerous critters in the WoD.
