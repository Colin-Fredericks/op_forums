    Could the embrace reverse the effects of aging?

    07-11-2023, 04:14 PM
    I don't remember if this is canon or fanon, but I've seen it said that the Beast may alter the body during the embrace in order to make it a better predator, including mending damage or even making cosmetic tweaks according to whatever set beauty standards to make them more alluring to prey (although I'm not sure the Beast would understand that concept). Assuming this is the case, could the protean/regenerative properties of the Embrace affect the damage that causes us to physically age in your opinion?

      The Beast altering the body is one of those concepts that gets mentioned in the fluff, but never gets a mechanical explanation. In the same vein as how low humanity/path vampires supposedly take on a more monstrous appearance.

      Many aspects of VtM are kept mechanically loose so that whatever head canon or concept you come up with can squeak by without too many issues. A good example is that while the Storyteller system allows you to spend XP to make changes to your character, it is never really explained how a Vampire was Appearance 1 at Embrace gets to be an Appearance 5 when they are supposed to be static in form for all time.

      But even the idea of being a better predator is a bad way of explaining the change because an Appearance 5 vampire who has only single dots in all mental traits won't survive for long, but we don't get fluff about all vampires getting an automatic IQ boost just from the embrace.

                  I was primarily thinking of the damage of aging: thin, fragile skin that damages easily; weak joints and a spine bending under its own weight; failing sensory organs... The embrace typically repairs damage, flaws representing permanent injury seem to be outliers, so the embrace may affect the symptoms of age. Your skin thickens and firms into a better protective barrier, your spine straightens, your eyes clear again. You may still have white hair and some lines on your skin, but the overall effect would make you seem younger.

                  Children don't really count, they've prevented from growing; ageing adults are going in the opposite direction and physically degenerating and becoming damaged.

                              There are various examples of vampires "de-aging"in media. What happens to Count Dracula after he arrives in England is only one example. Therefore, I wouldn't mind if this trope somehow enters play. The question is what is the best means to do so.

                              It certainly shouldn't be free. There should be a cost. I would probably make it dependent on the vampire spending blood. Such "de-aging" only seems to happen after a vampire feeds a lot. Probably a fairly heavy cost in Blood Points, but once done the vampire "resets" to a particularly time period in their youth (probably limited to their prime adult years - somewhere in their late twenties to mid-thirties) for a certain amount of time - maybe a week or cycle of the moon.

                              I would probably also require something else to be required. Maybe it requires a special Merit that is only available during character creation, or some level of Combination Discipline or advanced Discipline. It all depends on how exclusive you want this to be. Although this trope is used, we don't see it done all the time and I wouldn't all vampires doing it.

                              But I would only have this be cosmetic (no improvements in any stats), and only temporary. Eventually the vampire would need to consume and burn large number of Blood Points again. There might also be some other cost that is more precious than just Blood Points, like permanent Willpower.

                                          I like the idea of a Flaw (probably 2 or 3 pts) where the vampire is however young they were at the Embrace when at max blood pool. But the lower their supply, the more aged they appear. Developing wrinkles, liver spots, dull eyes, and greying hair. Until they look almost cadaverous when their "tank" is near empty.

                                          I peg it at 2-3 pts, because it's a Flaw that won't affect the vampire too much in casual contact with strangers (unless the vampire spends a ton of blood points on the sly, whereby the shift in apparent age may be noticeable). But long term, it makes any attempt to maintain a mortal identity or even just long-term mortal relationships a dicey affair. Needing to keep one's BP topped off whenever interacting with certain mortals, to maintain the charade. Or having excuses for why you look more or less vibrant from encounter to encounter. ("Yeah, I've been having a rough week. I need a spa date" or "I got Botox recently").

                                          On the other hand, some vampires with the Flaw might exploit it to maintain a duel identity (the classic "here's my son, [Name] Junior; give him the same deference you would give me, as I've taught him everything I know"). Though this means their "old" identity necessarily requires balancing on the knife's edge of hunger. Especially as the charade continues, and the vampire needs to go lower and lower to authentically fake natural aging.
