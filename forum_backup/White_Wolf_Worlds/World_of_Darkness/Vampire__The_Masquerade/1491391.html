                          The Storyteller System has always had its Traits be as broadly applicable as possible. The system breaks down if you treat it as an actual skill or discipline. "Drive" for example includes anything to do with automotives. It includes driving them, repairing them, detailing them, designing them, etc. Yet in real life, these are all different things and being highly skilled in one of them does not necessarily mean you are equally skilled in others. It is just a limitation of the system. It's not designed for crunch, but to quickly resolve narrating the scene and moving on.

                          As such, Technology just means "relevant to all things technological." As Ragged Robin suggested, in this case it is best considered to simply be a luck roll. How that plays out in the actual scene requires the ST and/or PC to decide what it means, and the suggestions given by SetiteFriend work well as some potential resolutions.
