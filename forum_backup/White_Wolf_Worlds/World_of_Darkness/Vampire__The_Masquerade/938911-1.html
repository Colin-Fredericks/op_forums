    In my 2015 VtM, the Antediluvians are bound for ten thousand years, each guarded by an ancient order of ghouls trained to perverse their eternal sleep. The ghouls are provided artificial Vitae through ancient relics that transform animal blood into the blood of 4th generation vampires, though the artificial Vitae may not be used to create blood bonds or new vampires. Each order obeys a six thousand year old 4th generation vampire, each a child of the trapped Antediluvian that they guard, though Tremere is trapped by a 4th generation child of Saulot and Giovanni is trapped by a 4th generation child of Cappadocius.

        Originally posted by Black Fox View Post
        I don't use the Antedeluvians as active components in my chronicles. I only use them for historical event explanation, and to lay out a very general idea of the Jyhad so it provides structure to the politics and events happening in the setting.

        The antedeluvians were in torpor for much of recorded history. I had Cappadocius as the most active (since his actions are just too important to the backstory of the Cappadocians and therefore the Giovanni). In order to make any future Gehenna truly terrifying, I had his three partial awakenings from torpor all coincide with plague epidemics. I thought that it was appropriate the founder of the Clan of Death be associated (caused?) with mass death. I wanted to associate an antedeluvian rising with the idea of terrible calamities that would inaugur the end of the world. The first was during the Plague of Cyprian around AD 250 when the antedeluvian was converted to Christianity. The second was the Plague of Justinian around AD 541 when the Feast of Folly occurred. The last was during the Black Death. The association of Cappadocius' rising with great plagues that imperil humanity is one reason the Camarilla elders looked askance at the Giovanni purge of the rest of their clan mates.

        None of the other antedeluvians were active during the historical period. They've been in torpor or dead since before 3000 BC. Any rumors of their activity, particular with Set, is actually caused by one of their first childer, 4th generation methuselahs of extreme age and power. Prior to that, the antedeluvians were the old god-kings of the Mesopotamian city state legends although a few wandered off to other areas (Set, Ravnos, Saulot). (The Second City was Babel).

        There is a throw away line in the Vampire corebook that the Ventrue antedeluvian is known to be destroyed. That clan is the only one not manipulated by its antedeluvian, and therefore this is the main historical reason the clan is trusted enough to be the traditional leaders among the vampires. I push this heavily. Neonates don't care or don't believe, but I have the NPC elders make cryptic references to that every now and again so the PCs know this is what all or most of the elders actually believe.

        The historical destruction/diablerie of several antedeluvians during the Middle Ages are all essentially faked no matter how successful they seemed to be.

        For the structure of the Jyhad, I had to determine which were truly destroyed and which weren't. Then I had to figure out what each of the survivors wanted in broad terms, and how the setting backstory worked with it. Although I must admit I have not given it much though. I have no details only broad outlines. Usually, it's not important at all. However, if some PC really wanted to investigate the Jyhad, I have at least an idea of where to go.
        Nice description, Black Fox.

        I actually make Gehenna a myth which the Antedeluvians have lived in spite of. The prophecy of Gehenna when the Antedeluvians will arise and devour vampiredom dates back to the Second City and Malkav in my games. This despite the fact the Antedeluvians were frequently ruling alongside their progeny as late as the 14th century (with Cappadocius being the last to fall asleep with the Giovanni).

        The Antedeluvians when they arose generally handled matters like this:

        1. Antedeluvians rise.
        2. Antedeluvians summons all of their clan
        3. The Antedeluvian eats the ones they hate and spares the one they don't, often feeding them blood to lower their generations.

        (So basically like the Feast of Folly but Cappadocius didn't reclaim his blood)

        The idea they would some day lose control and devour ALL of their children, leaving a nightmarish wasteland is as much the Antedeluvians nightmare as it is their progeny.

                I've never used them, but if I did they'd essentially be plot-based. Basically slightly weaker Caine stats (but even Infinity -1 for stats is pretty spiffy) with a correspondingly broad or narrow impact on the plot as necessary.                         One thing that I would like to implement if I ever run a chronicle involving the clan founders, would be the fact that just as an Ante's power is almost god-like, their original curses/weakness given by Caine are equally strong and crippling. This means that the clan weaknesses we know are much more severe or magnified, and in some cases near fatal to the Antes. Let me explain and give you some examples:

                        • Tzimisce: The soil dependency of the Tzimisce affects the founder much severely in the sense that he doesn't just need a handful of his ancestral's home soil, but massive quantities of it, if he ever rests in foreign soil he starts losing his vampiric powers until he's driven to torpor. Obviously, the Eldest is practically immortal when in his home land, but if he was ever buried in foreign ground it would almost immediately incapacitate him, needless to say that if he was ever 'killed' while resting or trapped in foreign soil, it would be enough to destroy him entirely.

                        • Ventrue: Just as the Ventrue can only drink from their particular blood type, the original curse would manifest on the Ventrue Ante as not just an incredibly specific and narrow feeding preference, but as an allergy to any other type of blood, an allergy that makes any other blood pure poison to the Ante and could potentially destroy him entirely, or leave him completely incapacitated for a final coup de grace.

                        Obviously some of the other clan weaknesses would require a bit more tampering, but ideally they should all have the potential to be used against the Antes in some way, and possibly being the only way to truly destroy them.

                                            I just want to throw in a thought: even the antes were young once. I'm on the side that everything in the world is working along the same game-rules, so in my book they always had the benefits of being 3rd generation, but they also were neonates and ancilae for a time with closer to human stats and low disciplines (3rd gen doens't automaticly mean 9+ stats from the start for me...), so in the beginning probably they and their progeny had an easier time destroying and diablerizing them and the major 13+ were the cunning enough to outlive their peers or the childer of their less-cunning ones. After that, along the thousands of years they were growed insanely powerful, but their surviving methuselah children also, so not an unbridgeable a power gap between them and older antes are more powerful than younger ones (although they might sleeping for a long time and younger ones might be more active and even among the original antes some of them might be more active than others, so again, the power lever could be varied).

                                            Another bit worth considering that the antes were active in days when the technological level was infinitely lower. Sure, among the first settlements of humankind they seemed as gods and ruled as such. Now? They'd have a lot harder time. That's one of the reasons why I think their awakening wouldn't mean the end of the world, but more that they'd try to wrestle the control from (or of) the sects and the methuselash and turn the shadow war against each other to 11. Sure they'd snack a lot of neonates and ancilla after awakening for breakfast and later along the way and those are the reasons why everybody are frightened of them and...

                                            ...people always construct myths and apocalypse tales. Much younger religions than the antes themselves have them and people "see the signs" every-fucking-time. I don't know why vampires'd be different. It's also in the methuselash's interest to spread these tales to undermine the workings of their biggest rivals. I think most of the vampire origin story is just that: a story which has bits of truth but warped and mystified along the ages.

                                            I also like the idea that the antes don't really want to rise and rule, or eat everyone. Maybe the ones whom didn't go to torpor willingly, they probably plot revenge, but lot of them just had enough of the world and bored themselves to sleep or trying to be something more than vampire.

                                                        For something more useful then the power of plot, which has been stated so often it is essentially useless someone could give any example what so ever of a Antediluvian that they had using the power of plot.


                                                        Okay for something more useful, I have the Antediluvians essentially very mighty, but essentially weaker then Incarna spirits until they transcend their human form, and then they essentially start becoming more like a spirit. But Antediluvians I have them like the mightiest Methuselahs, like Baba Yaga, Ur-Shulgi, Japheth, ect. But they have access to Disciplines at the Tenth Level, and have a bit more exp. Why a lot of the Mightiest Methesulahs are beefy is because they could play around and invent 8-9 level disciplines, and combo disciplines, and Rituals. Antes I don't like having all 9/10s, but they do have high stats and some stats maxed. I imagine each Ante has most disciplines they aren't interested in at 5, some at zero for instance like say if say Veddartha doesn't want any Tzimice taint from Vicissitude. Their clan Disciplines at 10, and maybe a few more at ten, particularly if a bloodline has them, like giving Set Potence 10.

                                                        I have it reaching the tenth level of a discipline makes it easier for an ante to develop lower level powers, essentially allowing them to learn powers at the cost of the level. The Antes I have are essentially using Caitiffs no favored disciplines rule. But like other Ancient level disciplines level Ten can be learned multiple times, but is always expensive. You can track a lot of an Antes history by each time they learn an Alternate level 10 discipline for instance, Lasombra gave up his human form by learning Obtenebration 10 a 2nd time transforming him into a true creature of the Abyss, perhaps one more bump up and it transcends vampirism and becomes essentially a spirit/alien.

                                                        This allows me to say some Antes are better at things then others that also mastered it. Like say Lasombra mastered Potence to the Tenth level, but it only learned it once, but what if Brujah/Troile/Ilyes learned it twice essentially representing a more thorough and diversified super strength. So Maybe it would take Lasombra a night to shatter a mountain, but Brujah could shatter one in a scene, or if expending great resources maybe shattering it in an instance.

                                                        I have it the Antediluvians that survived each have combos/powers to protect them from curses, and control, each likely different and unique. And they have redundancies in place for sticky situations they are in danger, for instance Samael died slaying Tzimisce but the Eldest gestated in the Womb of his Childe, but things like this reincarnation are speedbumps that consume several centuries for the Ante in question. But Antediluvians can be overwhelmed by Methuselah level powers, particularly if caught in a weakened state, think Galactus getting whomped if trying to eat Earth when weakened by hunger. But if preparing Antes often have rituals or combo powers that can harm beings a step above them, like Set Cursing Tribes of Fera and Incarna.


                                                        Ravnos was essentially going to loose to three beings one step below him in power, but he got jumped by a Conspiracy of Magical Scientists using Archmage effects/artifacts.
