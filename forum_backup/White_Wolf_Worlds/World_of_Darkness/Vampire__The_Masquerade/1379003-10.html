                                                            What they gained in terms of magic power and cohesion was balanced by the distrust and the internal power plays. Tremere are hated, and distrusted, this is clear from pretty much every sourcebook. Most vampires in the Camarilla just think that the Tremere are evil wizards plotting something. Do you remember D.C. By Night? Probably the most powerful clan in town, and yet they were incapable of ousting Vitel because no one trusted them and most vampire prefered Vitel to Peter Dorfman.
                                                            And outside the Camarilla? Well, it's not like the Tzimisce have forgot, and when sieges and crusades start, the Tremere Chantry is the first place that gets nuked.


                                                            Even more Clan Tremere, just like the Order of Hermes, is built for internal conflict. Since the original Clanbook Clan Tremere is plenty of factions that fight against each other. The main fight was between Traditionalist and Transitionalist, plus several minor factions like the Human League, the Guardians of Traditions or the Elite. What a Tremere gains in terms of protection against rival clans a Tremere lose in terms of trust inside his own. The worst enemy of a Tremere is not a Brujah or a Ventrue who fight against his dominion: it's his own apprentice, or his Regent, or his best friend that was coopted by one of the secret societies inside the Clan.

                                                            This was true also in Dark Ages where the fight was between Etrius' faction and Goratrix. House of Tremere is a whole sourcebook about the inner fightings inside Ceoris, with plenty of factions, distrust, plots and daggers in the shadows...

                                                            Which is consistent with the Tremere concept.
                                                            It is just like the Order of Hermes,They have more men, more artifacts, a better political structure, and the most powerful Chantry in the world, and people like Caeron Mustai and Sao Cristavao who had too much power, and used it badly. The Hermetic had several edges that put them far ahead the other Traditions (which is a point that is often treated in the metaplot, several books in mage tell us that the other Traditions distrust the Order of Hermes and its power).
                                                            And just like the Tremere your worst enemy is not the Man in Black from the NWO, but the brother from another House. It's the mage from House Janissaries that will spy on you and use you in the game of the Order, is the Quaesitor who will judge you for you errors, or the Tytalus who will challenge you for power and dominance. Doissetep was a nest of vipers, that collapsed for the internal strife between Porthos and Mustai.

                                                            Now, I understand that probably many storyellers are lazy, and make things easy for Tremere players by not offering a correct picture of the inner treachery of the clan and strife for power between clan mates, but this is not a problem of Clan Tremere, is a problem with lazy storytellers and lazy players who do not study sourcebooks.
