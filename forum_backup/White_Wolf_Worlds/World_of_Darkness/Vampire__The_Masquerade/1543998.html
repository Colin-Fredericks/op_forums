    1 - Which one is the best in your opinion and why, what makes it different from the scenario?

    2 - Which one did you use for the longest time or modify to better fit into your WoD universe?

    3 - Which one is the worst or most disappointing and why?

      Well, the best in my opinion is probably a tie between Cconstantinople by Night and Dark Ages Paris by Night for V20 (this last one wasn't translated into English, unfortunately).
      Both have very interesting stories and characters to explore and characters who affect the whole history of Vampire. I've used Constantiniple for the longest time, naturally, as it's much older and has many important vampire characters. One thing I like in particular about these two, besides the characters, is that they tried to kind of run away from the same political struggles that characterize most of the "By Night" books, that is, they present different powers/ideas/creatures that are not found outside of them, I won't give spoilers, but what I mean is "crazy ideas" about powers and things one character can make with this powers, like animating things, etc. As a very experienced ST, I tend to get bored easily, especially after so many years, more than 2 decades of storytelling, so it's always refreshing to seee some "crazy ideas" that offer new venues to explore.

          I remember Chicago and LA being the two we probably used the most back in the day. (In hindsight, I really wish that LA's Anarch revolt had occurred in the late 60s/early 70s rather than the 40s, simply because I feel that Film Noir LA Vampire works way better with the Camarilla in control.)

          The biggest missed opportunity IMO is Berlin. The idea of a single city that has been physically divided for 40+ years and only recently reunited but has two rival Princes and Courts is a really great and unique Vampire setting. And Berlin's fascinating history and culture (especially the music/club subcultures of the 70s and 80s, Cold War spy intrigue of the 60s, the film industry of the 20s, and the 19th century University scene) only add to that. But instead, WW2 is presented as pretty much the only thing of note to have happened, and the book is very much the worse for it.

                  Not gonna really be shaking things up too much with these takes but…

                  1. Chicago by Night really is the gold standard that every other ‘by Night’ title is compared against. It’s a great time capsule and a great introduction to vampire as a setting and filled with hooks to start playing with at your table. Possibly its main flaw is that it was TOO influential

                  2. This is my most unique take but I’ve got a special fondness for DC by Night. It was one of my first WoD books and I’m from the DC metropolitan area so I’ve got a fondness for the city (it’s far more than just Capital Hill!). The cover is iconic (even if only to me) and makes wonderful use of the Washington monument (or, as I called it as a kid, ‘the big pencil’) as a gothic landmark. Now that said it’s not perfect. Marcus Vitel is a somewhat gonzo concept but his long lasting popularity speaks for itself. Other than him and his brood though, the rest of the city’s vampires are somewhat shaky. Lots of potential though.

                  3. The one that personally irks me the most is LA by Night because it’s arguably one of the most responsible forces for stunting the development of the Anarch movement and it’s taken a long time for its ideas to be scavenged into something useful on these forums. The objectively worse one though, in my opinion, has to be Berlin by Night, for the sheer weight of the missed opportunities. (I wouldn’t even mind the vampire snakes if they were done well, but, it is what it is)

                                          I'm not really a fan of these sorts of books in general. I'm generally of the mind of "catch a man a fish, you feed him for a day, teach a man to fish, and you feed him for a lifetime." City-books are catching the ST a fish. That's basically the source of the idea that Chicago by Night was too influential: the authors didn't really appreciate how much people would treat it as a model of how to do vampire cities instead of an example of one of many ways a city might look. People were, effectively, trying to copy the book's fishing to try to learn how to fish since no book really covered that first.

                                          So, a bit of a "cheat" of the question, but my favorite city-book is going to remain Damnation City from VtR 1e for sometime because.... it's all about teaching you how to fish. All the others from all the editions and WoD vs. CofD fall into a tie for me otherwise.
