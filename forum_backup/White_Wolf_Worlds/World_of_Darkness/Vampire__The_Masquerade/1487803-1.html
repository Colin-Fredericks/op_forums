                Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                I'm going to suggest something surprising: I don't think most vampires actually believe what you're talking about if they aren't the kind of person who believed in them to begin with.

                You first have to deal with the paradigm shift that &quot;vampires exist&quot; obviously and that's going to be something that modern secular materialists are already going to be blown away. They might be able to adjust the majority of their worldview by believing vampires are natural predators, some sort of mutant, or otherwise creatures of science. These people are wrong. Vampires are magical and defy all conventional science to the point that you CAN scientifically study them but it would create an entirely new field of study as well as state the majority of what you know about others is wrong.

                Which leads to point two, &quot;magic is real.&quot; If you're a modern secular materialist, you're going to have enough trouble with vampirism but that's going to get into the fact that Tremere (and let's stick with the base Seven Clans to start with) can do something fundamentally DIFFERENT than what other vampires can with superpowers. They can mark a wall with a squiggly line in blood and then you flat out can't enter that place anymore. Plenty of vampires will try to say, &quot;No, no, Blood magic is just Disciplines with a bit more weirdness.&quot;

                But taking a step further, &quot;Spirits are real.&quot; Well, after Point A and Point B, it's probably less of a leap than you might think. Because if you're not a modern secular materialist then you've probably come from a society that already believes in spirits. Plenty of cultures believe in angels, demons, and so on anyway. Many more believe in an afterlife so the idea that both of these exist is something that will probably be less surprising than you might think if you're anyone other than someone who dismissed these things as superstition in life.

                Even then, I'd argue if you ARE a modern secular materialist vampire, you might actually dismiss these things because you know at this point:

                * Mind control exists
                * Memory alteration
                * Magic that can do weird stuff
                * Possibly illusion powers as the Ravnos are as common as the Hecata
                * The real life Charlatans

                Assume our modern secular materialist vampire, call him Fred the Brujah, hears that the Hecata are a bunch of mobsters (because the Putenesca is assumed to be the Hecata by 90% of people), that can talk to the dead and control their spirits. Are you going to believe that or that the rumor mill has gotten out of hand or they're a bunch of frauds like everyone else who &quot;talks to ghosts&quot;? (And aren't necessarily frauds in the World of Darkness anyway but are still probably 90% frauds).

                But if you AREN'T Fred, by the time you find out about spirits, you've already been led far down the Rabbit Hole and may come from a culture that already believes in spirits and the supernatural that you might react to the discovery with, &quot;Okay, sure.&quot;

                I am a religious person in RL but if I'm a vampire, I might go, &quot;Oh, Zeus is real too. I believe it.&quot;
                I must confess, vampirism would blow my mind away. After that, I dont know if I would be more or less prone to abandon my materialism afterwards. Maybe any kind of weird after would just be "well, whatever. Unicorns you say? Yeah, ok"

                                                I agree with Asmodai and CTPhipps. When it comes to the World of Darkness, we must assume Ignorance is the default.

                                                The World of Darkness is a realm of hundreds of concurrent and competing conspiracies. Even conspiracies within conspiracies. All of whom have a vested interest in maintaining a tight lid on any and all information. The conspiracies keep information secret even from each other, because information is not only dangerous, it's currency.

                                                When I imagine what your "average" vampire knows - most of which will be Neonates, simply due to attrition - I look at how Vampire's setting was presented in 1st edition. A Neonate doesn't even necessarily know who's in charge in a city, let alone the political situation both within their sect and outside of it.

                                                Vampire politics in many ways is a dance of pretending you're stronger than you are, pretending you know more than you do, and looking for ways to learn even basic things about other vampires. If you're lucky, you've got a Mentor willing to pass down knowledge because they like you or because you're useful. But even Mentors won't necessarily reveal all to their students/Childer. Either because they want to keep a grip on them, because they fear this information being used against them, or because there are gaps in their knowledge and don't want to tip their hand about it.

                                                As for matters of spirits, a vast, VAST majority of vampires will never encounter one. At least, they may go centuries before they have the opportunity. (Not surprising, since a majority of all vampires won't reach Ancillae age, regardless of sect). Most spirits that do interact with vampire politics (if not directly with vampires themselves) do so from a position of anonymity. Most don't have the power to manifest visually or in auditory in the physical world, and those that do may not want to (manifesting is not free, nor always necessary). If a vampire's unlife was touched by the presence of a spirit, they're unlikely to know it happened. Or else it involved some weirdness the vampire couldn't explain.

                                                Those vampires who truck with spirits are few and far between. Giovanni are reclusive, and Tremere users of Spirit Manipulation are similarly rare. Nor are they likely to "summon" a spirit where other vampires can see it, save in the most dire circumstances ("summoning" a spirit isn't free). Most vampires with the Paths or Rituals needed to do any spirit stuff don't have the skill necessary to "summon" anyway. When a spirit is used by a vampire, it's likely to be indistinguishable from regular blood magic to the lay vampire.

                                                We know, from the books, that Tremere will occasionally make big displays of arcane might. Usually a complicated and time-consuming Ritual, triggered to go off at a time and circumstance to give the illusion it was a spontaneous working. Because, yes, Clan Tremere must periodically make themselves seem stronger than they really are. But Clan Tremere also benefits from keeping their full capabilities a secret. Remaining mysterious, so everyone both respects their power and comes begging for Boons.

                                                This can create the impression, among ignorant vampires, that Tremere can either do basically anything, or are charlatans. A secular materialist vampire doesn't need to think Tremere are "doing magic". They can be lulled into thinking they have some weird vampire power - as all vampires do - that they dress up in mysticism to bilk superstitious Elders from the Dark Ages. (The secular materialist is wrong, of course, but characters are allowed to be wrong.)

                                                My point is that while a vampire could, due to being a vampire, open themselves up to accepting other supernatural things, this doesn't mean they will be forced to do so by circumstance. Keeping others in the dark as to what is real is a standard tactic of the World of Darkness conspiracies. The World of Darkness is a large, dense place. A vampire could spend centuries studying, and never learn half of what exists printed in our sourcebooks. Let alone encounter them personally. Accepting that ghosts or spirits might exist, and encountering one, are two different things.

                                                Magestuck: The Ascension

                                                    Originally posted by Hello View Post
                                                    If I was a neonate, ancillae, or even elder who just saw some vampire summon a spirit, politics, and angsting over my humanity is the last thing I'm going to do.
                                                    You're a walking corpse that by all means should be stiff and rotting in a coffin. You lack vital signs and has been like that for years. You do not age, and can have a realistic expectation of living unabated and unchanged for centuries to come. Your internal organs are shriveled beyond the ability to even pretend basic functions, yet they process blood as if you were fully alive, even delivering the effects of drugs your dead body can't actually metabolize in any way. From the blood taken you're able to call forth mystic powers that already can't be explained by any school of thought known to humankind.

                                                    Are you that surprised that ghosts and spirits, the things about 60% of the world population already believes to be real, are real?

Even a completely normal human would, as do the many normal humans that can swear to their gods that they witnessed such phenomena irl and do so frequently. Imagine a creature that is A) Immortal, B) supernatural itself and C) dependent on politics for survival.

                                                    Unless you're under the impression that somehow everything else in a vampire's nightly life is perfectly mundane, there's plenty of hints of terrible forces behind the world in the basic assumptions that vampires exist and wield Disciplines. Spirits are probably the simplest explanation already for a lot of people. And as far as most vampires can tell, spirits existing don't change a thing, as if they exist, they always existed and that changed nothing.
