                      If I may add
                      Because when a Kuei-jin reaches Arhat status, they shed their undead curse, break free from the cycle of unlife, and ascend to the Hundred Clouds
                      A few postpone the ascension but there is exactly one canonical Arhat who actively refused to leave the Earthly plane. Her name is The Most Serene Black Pearl Mistress, a master of the Song of the Shadow Dharma
                      Thousand Crane Mother, the revered Arhat and spiritual progenitor of the Dance of the Thrashing Dragon Dharma was also around but she is a bit of a wild card, more of a legend (she do exist and became depressed whe nher favorite pupil, Black Earth Boy died and technically dropped back from Dharma 10 to 9)

                      So basically just two were around at the near end of the Fifth Age, when the Week of Nightmares happened

                      But the Keui-jin had several Bodhisattvas... yet why just three?

                      Simple
                      Because none of the great Dharmas actually require you to wage a war against the Yama Kings or the Forces of Yomi
                      Yomi and the Yama Kings are the part of the Celestial Order, part of the system
                      And the Wheel Must Turn - that means the Demon Emperor must rise, that will be the start of the Sixth Age and the defeat of the Demon Emperor will signal the start of the Seventh Age
                      So the funny situation is: the Yama Kings work hard to hasten the arrival of the Sixth Age, many belive one of their members will be the Demon Emperor... and of course the Demon Emepror would want to stop the Wheel to rule forever
                      But the Yama Kings are not fools... they know, when the Demon Emperor rises and the world become literal Hell, they will be under the Demon Emperor, serving
                      ...and neither of them want to serve or be slaves so none of the Yama King actually want the Wheel to stuck in the Sixth Age and have an eternity serving and fearing the Demon Emperor
                      Lord of Centipede, the entity who is the Wyrm in the eyes of the Hengeyokai (and the Kuei-jin - as an interesting side note, they also belive the great dragon of balance and harmony wanted to bless the Tapestry, just got too close and it pulled him in, he paniced and started to trash around as he entangled in the threads, tearing out chunks of the Tapestry [that later became Malfeas] after managed to get out, he hid himself in shame and without his presence the Blanace and Harmony also lessened in the world giving space to corruption and such things. Some tales say he is hiding in the realm of his brother, the Ebon Dragon. This tale is eerily similar to how the Wyrm got trapped, and many belive the bottom of the Dark Umbra, Oblivion is the mouht of the Wyrm... just a funny little lore tidbit) just wants to coil around and shatter the Wheel and the Demons of Yomi not always the allies of the minons of Lord of Centipede...
                      It is a complicated

                      So back to the point
                      ​​None of the great Dharmas actually require you to wage a war against the Yama Kings, they are enemies, if you serve them? That is a death sentance, you became an Akuma. But small deals here and there? Learning from demons, as long as you reamain loyal to the Court and your Wu? Or to the Havens? It is okay. Especially because the Yama Kings are not always working together and a clever little Kuei-jin, risking the attention of Yomi, can navigate, playing them against each other. Risky but not strickly against the rules
                      Even the Devil-Tigers who seek to master their own inner demons ultimately want replace the Yama Kings to restore proper cosmic justice while usualyl in the front lines when they are young and destroy Demons are not always at war with Yomi.
                      They want to replace them and sure that could mean destroying them, but by Havenly Mandate lesser demons bow their heads already before the Devil-Tigers and serve them. And ehen you consider there are still Yama Kings who are still fullfiling their duty give nto them by the August Personage of Jade, like Haha No Fukami, the Mother of the Depths, the "Empress of Pearls" who was the primal ocean mother during the First Age, but changed to her current duty at the request of the August Personage of Jade? It is not that simple.

                      So while the Ravnos were a thorn in the side of the Kuei-jin that conflict was not widespread, not a "global" thing. More like a local war that lasted a few Ages but mostly happened in India especially bothering the Infintie Thounder Court and other local Courts
                      The Bodhisattvas of others? The Five August Courts of the Quincunx probably smiled with glee seeing the indian "brothers" who just gave them headache struggles and unless it involved them somehow? Not really bothered to help.
                      The Uji of Japan? The Green Court of Korea? The Golden Courts of Malaysia, Vietnam, Laos and Thailand?
                      The Golden Lion Court of Singapore?
                      Same

                      And here comes the magician's trick!
                      Black Earth Boy, Rising Majestically to the Western Sun, and Eight Thunders Sage marched to face Zapataashura not because they were particularly keen about the idea, or wanted the best for the world
                      They did it, because it was prophesied
                      The three of them were fated to stand as a holy vanguard to fight, banish, and eventually defeat a very powerful Demon-King
                      Not the Demon Emperor, but something very dangerous
                      They never ment to stop the rise of the Demon Emperor, they never belived what they are fated to fight against will the one who claim the tittle - after all, they were destined to fight and ideally won
                      They not even tought what they will face will be a Yama King at all - sure, the nightmares that week usually featured the Demon-King Ravana​ but...
                      ...but the Kuei-jin actually were aware, Ravana is out of the Game! His Kingdom in Yomi, Lanka was ruined, his Rakshas barely been able to hold it together; he largely abandoned his city and withdrew into his scorched palace. Hanuman (the Kitsune and the Nuwisha both claim Hanuman as one of their own, though the legends suggest that Hanuman was a monkey) torched the whole place with holy white fire. It is a minor miracle Ravana' s general, the Raksha Thratri not replaced him (under Thratri' s command , the rakshas work together with a coherence they never had under Ravana and many observers wondered if Thratri was destined to usurp Ravana's place and become the next great Yama Queen)
                      Only when India tested its first nuclear weapon in 1998, May 12, dragged him out of his depression and he stirred - but only because He believes he knows how to harvest the souls of the nuclear dead. - okay this last one the Kuei-jin not know, but it is not that important actually just worth to mention

                          The true identity of Ravana and whether Ravana and Ravnos are the same being has always been up in the air. During the Week of Nightmares, Ravnos and Ravana were said to be one and the same, and psychics seeing visions of the Ravnos antedeluvian battling the Bodhisattvas specifically heard the name Ravana. But then later in the Ravnos clanbook, which released after the Week of Nightmares material, it's stated that Ravana was not Ravnos, but one of Ravnos' childer, who chose to throw in with demons, and eventually after becoming a Yama King, Ravana showed up to taunt Ravnos.

                          Personally, I have always threaded the needle by saying Ravnos split his soul into his Hun and Po, which seems like something one could reasonably do with plot level Animalism. His higher soul became Ravana, a Yama King and his lower soul (his Beast) slumbered in his body until it eventually awoke up and went berserk during the Week of Nightmares.
