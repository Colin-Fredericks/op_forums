            #115: A Tremere attempts to rope some of the PCs into helping him dispose of some issues with his work at a women's health clinic. He is performing experiments involving abortions, and needs help with covering up deaths, disappearances, and funding. He will offer a minor boon for this help.

The faith healer can perform real miracles apparently, and it would be bad for the human cattle to believe in miracles. Also, she embarrassed the elder when he came to try and get her to fix his face. He will offer a minor boon for this help.

The Toreador's thinking is this prevents the artists from disappointing people with future mediocrity and the murder gives the work a lovely patina tragedy. The Toreador asks for PCs help with such a murder. He will offer a minor boon for this help.

A Malkavian is using the clinic as part of human smuggling. He tasks the PCs to do something about the investigation. He will offer a minor boon for this help.

A Ventrue tasks the PCs with kidnapping an owner so the Ventrue can lock them in a cage for a week or so and blood bond them (that's how this Ventrue rolls). He will offer a minor boon for this help.

                    "Her sweet unbeating heart, her beautiful pallor, her lovely fangs..."
                    Such have lately been the thoughts, feelings, and attitude of a Ghoul belonging to a local Neonate. Some would comment that perhaps he has been a hopeless romantic all along; while others would point out that this is simply a peculiar manifestation of the Blood Bond. The truth is that a pair of Toreador Ancillae - a woman and a man - have revealed themselves to the Ghoul (after double-checking that he is one of the Ghouls who is aware of Vampires) and took him under a sort of mentorship. Through heartfelt anecdotes, tearful confessions, passionate ruminations, and judicious use of Presence the pair of Toreadors has convinced the Ghoul that he can serve and please his mistress in much more...intimate...ways that he hasn't considered before. All that was needed was a bit of soul-searching, and with the (seemingly) earnest help of the pair of Ancillae the Ghoul's passions have indeed become profoundly aligned to the ideal of unique and exceptional vampiric beauty.

                    The Ghoul is ready and willing to assume the role of a cherished companion. In what little free time he has he took up running to get into shape. He has also bought blood on the side from a blood bank, to surprise his mistress with a gift. His Ancillae benefactors have warned him about the possibility of rejection and punishment, but even they - in all their toreador matchmaking wisdom - do not have a full picture of what might happen.

                    Whether it will be when he will pour a glass of blood for her out of a champagne bottle, or after he complements her fangs, or as he strips naked to offer his body, his mistress might get a hint. Once the Ghoul confesses his feelings and ambitions to the beautiful Vampire woman, and will offer to satisfy her in any way, she might have three realizations. First - her servant has fetishized her being undead, without her prior approval. Second - without her permission he dared to assume that he might be cherished as a lover by her. Third - all that the first two gave him is that he became desperately needy, clingy, annoying, and cloying. One, even two, of these might have resulted in a harsh but fair punishment. But all three together might just be too much according to the alluring Neonate, as she is demanding and prideful.
                    She won't abandon him, of course not. This man is a loyal servant after all. But it is very likely that the romantic Ghoul will end up not only with a broken heart, but with many broken bones. Especially if he reveals in the end that he agreed to relationship advice given by the pair of Toreadors.

                    Can the PCs help this Ghoul before his mistress scourges him? Or maybe they will take upon themselves the task of punishing the Ghoul? Can the pair of Toreador Ancillae be reached? And just what is their angle for doing this, other than dilettante curiosity?

                        121: More of a black humor occasion, but still works for a minor issue that needs solving and could lead to genuine horror and dark moments.

                        There's some kind of scene, a group. It's about comics or a bookclub or an actual academics meeting, whatever. The point is that it's a gathering for a some very passionate people with some very firm OpinionsTM about the subject matter, and any meeting is liable to turn into an argument.
                        Then kindred get involved - specifically Toreador and/or Brujah - who also have very firm OpinionsTM, to the point where it'll build their tempers up a hairsbreadth away from the beast making their point for them. The meeting is now liable to turn into a massacre, a masquerade violation, or at least a murder. The Brujah don't deal well with opposing viewpoints. Toreador can get dangerously petty if you offend their taste and artistic/academic qualifications, and may hire the coterie to help humiliate an offender in a vicious fashion. Maybe it's their art being criticised. The group is just regular people with a passion for their hobbies, but a vampire has decided they have the wrong opinions about something harmless and so must die or worse, apparently.

                        Possibly the coterie is passing by and one of the party can't help getting drawn in, and they're the one who gets offended.

                        (Everybody thinks the Toreador are soft and less animalistic until you walk into one of their monthly bookclub meetings. Somebody is arguing about the historic context for something, while the opposing viewpoint in the back, frothing bloody foam at the mouth, howls "I. Was. Literally. There. You. Hack!!!")

                            The player characters are approached by a Ghoul of a Malkavian Elder and given a thin (32 pages) notebook. It is filled with dense handwriting with rows upon rows of short descriptions of mortals. "Young working class with a beautiful wife, and at least one child", "Middle-aged lonely businessman or lawyer", "Short and thin, dwelling near the city center", "A clown or another circus performer with many friends" and so on.
                            The Ghoul explains that his lord requires mortals to possess with his formidable Dominate powers, and requests help in finding and acquiring them. It is not just about the bodies, the physical qualities - the Elder is also interested in what sort of existence these mortals have, so that he can experience new circumstances and interact with a variety of persons. The Ghoul elaborates that the PCs' job would be just to find the mortals and lead them to an agreed-upon isolated place - a parking lot on the city's outskirts, an isolated dark alley, an abandoned office building, and similar. From there they will be kidnapped into a vehicle by a team of Ghouls led by the Elder's Grandchilde, who of course will use his mental powers to subdue the acquisition.

                            The Malkavian Elder promises tangible rewards if the PCs bring him many mortals to overtake. It is their choice whether it will be pull within mortal institutions, information in regard to Kindred society and their goings-on, or simply cash given for the time and effort. Or some other agreed-upon method of recompense. Perhaps even a minor Boon, though only if enough particularly interesting mortals are found and acquired.
                            The Ghoul messenger assures there little to no risk involved. The actual kidnapping is done by the Grandchilde and the Ghouls. After the Elder fulfills his intentions, a mortal's memories of the whole occurrence will be removed or altered, and he will be put back on the streets without further ado. And the Ghoul's lord has principles - no women or children will be singled out for possession. Finally, the Ghoul notes that the work of finding the mortals can be quite interesting. Certainly, there is a lot of variety to encounter, and the Elder provides freedom of choice as to who to kidnap from within the described mortals. Though sometimes in a notebook there are written indicators of priority.

                            The proposed work seems straightforward and rather easy. Beyond the promised tangible rewards, there is also the fact that the very act of cooperating with an Elder might bring future benefits beyond the outlined ones.
                            However, what if the Malkavian uses Possession to further his schemes, pranks, and plots in regard to other Vampires? If the PCs help him, they might be held responsible for events and outcomes they knew nothing about. And there is also the possibility of the PCs losing themselves in the undertaking - after all, there are hundreds of thousands of mortals in the city. And it just seems certain the Malkavian Elder can and will regularly fill another thin notebook with rows of tiny, laconic descriptions of wanted mortals.

                                A trio of Malkavian Ancillae - two men and one woman - have become fans of the Joker from Batman comics after watching the newest Joker with Joaquin Phoenix. Fascinated with the deadly criminal clown, they watched all the Batman movies featuring the fictional character, and browsed through some comics. Get enough Malkavians in one place, and they come up with very surprising ideas and plots. Through intense and intricate psychological conditioning (including the use of Disciplines) they want to create their own Joker, a Ghoul who will become an amalgam of the various takes on the character. They will work on him to such an extent that he will even be uncertain exactly how, why, and when he became the Joker; mimicking the mysterious past of the character in the media.
                                He will be their elite, stylish errand boy and enforcer, though the Joker will also be given some freedom to express and develop his newly instilled tendencies. And there will be no Batman, nor a commissioner Gordon to stop him.

                                The Joker would be provided with weapons, information, funds, thugs, and safehouses by his shadowy benefactors. The Malkavian Vampires are so pleased with their idea that their attitude is along the lines of - "Just how cool is that?". Certainly most Vampires - and their Ghouls - in the city will never be prepared for someone like him. The weak will be confused and terrified, while the strong will be impressed. There will be more to him than just violence - he will of course have a sharp sense of humor, and a kind of rugged wisdom and insight. Though the Malkavians just cannot agree on what kind of look to go with for the Joker, but surely with time, they will reach a consensus that will result in a very distinctive appearance. And if the Joker gets killed or captured, then they can make a new one - so the Joker will be returning, just like in the comics.

                                    A Nosferatu Ancillae approaches the player characters and tells them about a curious new visitor at the Elysium. Apparently, it is a Tremere Vampire, but not the kind of they might be used to. He promises that what she will propose and offer might either greatly interest or greatly amuse them.

                                    And fair enough, there is a very attractive and nicely dressed female Tremere Vampire in the Elysium, and she approaches the PCs and introduces herself as a Carnian Vampire, belonging to House Carna under the leadership of Carna. She informs them that she is currently seeking a mortal men who could potentially be a new lover for Carna, and she requires aid in finding and securing such a man. Like a litany, she recites the requirements for such a man.

                                    “Must of course understand how to worship and appreciate the woman he loves, and definitely should love magic for the sake of it. Must be caring, insightful, and obedient. All in all, the man should know his place, and must care about the relation of course! Never speak up. Ideally, he would enjoy nature and the great outdoors, and walks during beautiful nights. And for finding such a man, such a potential lover for the esteemed Carna, she and I offer to you the Friendship of House Carna. I am sure it will bring you great joy and many benefits, if you will only earn it with this simple, but profound and meaningful, help given to Carna.”

                                    The PCs can inquire the Carnian Vampire about the specifics of the arrangement in regard to this potential lover and what is intended for him, but the Tremere Vampire will be politely secretive about this. She will however give information about the task of finding the man. It turns out that the man does not need to have all the qualities described initially – but at least over half of them should be present. It is also valid to inspire such qualities in a potential lover for Carna, to guide him in this. If asked about what Friendship of House Carna entails, the Tremere Vampire will smile nicely and just say that it is built on mutual trust and loyalty, and that it can indeed be truly wonderful for those involved.

                                    If the PCs heard rumors that Carna has harmed her lovers in the past, the Tremere Vampire will vehemently deny this, saying “None of this happened! In fact, all lovers of Carna simply left her…men are so uncertain in their feelings in some circumstances…Or maybe there were manipulated and misled by other Vampires plotting against Carna…manipulated and misled into leaving her!”

                                    After the PCs leave the Elysium, they are approached by the Nosferatu Vampire who informed them about the Carnian Vampire. He grins and asks how was it, and before the PCs can reply he goes on a tirade
                                    “See, this is what happens when a 23 year old wiccan is embraced because her personal insights in her so-called book of shadows somehow impressed the esteemed Carna. Seriously, you know what she offered me in return for finding information about this potential lover? ‘The Friendship of House Carna’…Looks to me like an empty promise with no guarantees in return for my efforts. I did not even bother to ask whether this would be a Minor or Major Boon because, seriously, Carnian Vampires? As far as I see it they are Tremere Vampires who bought into and got dazzled by the propaganda and outlook of a Vampire Elder cunningly establishing herself. More of the usual games within Clan Tremere, if you ask me.”

                                    It is up to PCs to evaluate and consider who to believe. Maybe the Nosferatu has a point…but it just might be true that the Friendship of House Carna is real and precious. It just might be true that there is much in loving magic for the sake of it and admitting the meaning and importance of the sacred femininity. Of course, the benefits and value of the Friendship of House Carna might just depend on what Carna decides about it…but that just might simply be the right way it works.

                                        An independent Ghoul recently talked to a group of Kindred he met on the streets near the Rack. He behaved appropriately enough - after all, he has been doing it for over 15 years so far. Yet he made a slight mistake...he called himself a 'free Ghoul' while offering his services. However, those of them who talk out loud about this, going so far as to say they are 'free Ghouls' (or using some other lofty term)...this is different.

                                        So it is that a hunt has been declared by the Vampire Elder. It might end up in tragedy, but it is thus that a point needs to be made..and some nightly entertainment has to be provided from time to time. Thus Ghouls - loyal vassal Ghouls, unlike this particular fellow - have been given details and directions about this so-called 'free Ghoul'. His car will be set on fire, and his apartment will likewise be firebombed (no worries, the fire likely won't spread around - the Ghouls have been informed to call the fire brigade on time ). He better get the hint to get out of the area quickly, cause soon after this it will be fair game in regard to eliminating him. If he flees the city, the Elder's declaration will most likely follow him wherever he goes. After all, can he hide for long following over 15 years of getting his Vitae fix?

                                        Thus the only chance for the independent Ghoul is to carefully move around the city, staying in relatively safe public or well-hidden areas while hoping for the best. And actually, there is a way out for him. All he needs to do is to rethink his life choices, consider his situation, seek out a Kindred, apologize for his behavior, and offer his services to this one Kindred in particular, as a vassal Ghoul. Even if his service is not accepted, once the Elder gets informed about the attempt, he will deem that the point was made well enough and will call off the hunt, most probably... An experienced Ghoul can often be made useful again, after all. And his mistake wasn't that serious...

                                                The mayor of the city has been trying to be too good for his own good. Did he really think his recent actions through? He gave a financial grant of 20,000 $ to a group aiding young writers, which is not bad in itself. However, he was clearly familiarized extensively and respectfully with details about a particularly reputable occult and esoteric store which hosts regular debates and lectures about culture, mysticism, history, and anthropology. And the details were described by a Tremere Ghoul working at the city hall, no less. Yet the mayor chose the dreary and formulaic prose of young writers. The Tremere Regent just won't have it. The esoteric store has an almost ideal location in one of the city's central areas, among its customers are some rich and noteworthy persons, and the blood bonded staff has proven capable enough in various tasks that Tremere Vampires set for them. The debates and lectures have been going good as well, and the Regent thinks that the place might be useful in recruiting mortals who might serve or be influenced by Clan Tremere.

                                                As such, the mayor has to go, despite his pretty good over five years tenure. Clearly, he is aversive to even the merest hint of the mystical and the occult. The Regent has some plans in regard to the city for the next months and years, and for the plans to succeed at least some of the important and influential mortals in the city need to be open and interested in regard to the mystical and intellectual ideals.

                                                Luckily, the city hall has been - with the Prince's agreement - under the influence and direction of the Tremere since the last nine years. As such replacing the mayor by the Tremere won't attract undue criticism or retaliatory actions from other Kindred. Most Vampires in the city will be informed about this coming change.
                                                It is unfortunate that the Prince has declared that the mayor, along with several other key mortals, cannot be blood bonded, Ghouled, or subjected to mental Disciplines.

                                                The question is thus how to arrange the mayor's downfall. The goal is to make him resign as soon as possible due to some scandal or ruining of his life.
                                                There is the option of arranging for a mistrial in an ongoing trial of several die-hard leaders of organized crime. And after this putting forwards a media campaign that will spin the criminals going free as a result of ties with the mayor. Certainly an idea.
                                                Pulling some strings and asking the politicians and businessmen who bribed the mayor for favors over the years to come forward? A fitting downfall, but might take relatively long.
                                                He has a wife (almost two decades of marriage) and a lover (since three years). If his lover would be blood bonded or subjected to Dominate and publically came out with the relation, would this be enough? Might just be what will be the easiest.

                                                Murdering is not an option because of the Regent's dislike of such crass methods, and a general unspoken rule in the Camarilla to avoid directly harming important mortals. Thus the Regent will have to carefully analyze and decide just how to ruin the mayor's life. And then he can start considering who might be the best next mayor - which would be the mayor who will benefit Clan Tremere the most, of course.

                                                        A Brujah Neonate wishes to put his Ghoul - one who he is almost certain in regard to the Embrace - through a trial. The man is largely noteworthy and interesting because he is a vehement intellectual, insisting that human societies and their idealogical ideas can be fully appreciated, understood, and influenced only when rigorous logic and careful analysis are applied. He has an air of confidence around him, but this is all in regard to high and serious matters. He lacks experience in the more simple, straightforward activities and achievements...like beating up a person till they bleed and their bones are broken.

                                                        So the Neonate has come up with a plan - he will have the Ghoul take part in an underground cage fight, at a widely attended and popular place. His opponent will be one of the regulars, a simple brute selected in part for his appearance, as the Brujah knows his Ghoul for some reason dislikes men with stereotypical working-class features. The Ghoul's courage will be bolstered by the Brujah's Awe and encouragement. Thus he will most likely enter the fight without fear, despite lack of prior combat experience. After all, he is a loyal Ghoul who is aware of his greater strength and toughness, and also has quite the contempt for mundane humans.

                                                        Still, the Brujah will not risk the Ghoul losing the fight or being seriously harmed. His servant is important for him, and it would also damage the Vampire's reputation if his Ghoul would be defeated. As such, the opponent will be heavily drugged by a woman, who is a Malkavian Ghoul, offering him a drink laced with a sedative. Also, to encourage the Brujah's Ghoul to take it out on the defeated opponent, the audience will be incited to excitement and aggression by the Brujah Neonate and a few of his fellow Brujah. Thus the Ghoul might get some more direct, practical experience in matters he has never thought to involve himself in, like taking it out on a defeated opponent without fear or hesitation.
