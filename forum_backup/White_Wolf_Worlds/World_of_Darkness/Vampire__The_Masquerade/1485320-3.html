    Originally posted by Ragged Robin View Post

    A number of things
    -a feudal society is not the same as an absolute monarchy.
    -the carmarilla genrally isn't an absolute an absolute monarchy anyway even locally. More of decentralised obligarchy if anything.
    -a substantial number of people in 20th and 21st century peoples and cultures are ruled by autocratic obligarchies who can and will kill you. To a worrying degree in fact.
    -vampiric society cannot replicate human society, their ecology is just too alien.
    I feel as if none of this would remotely effect the culture shock, horror, and disgust that a person Embraced would feel upon being introduced to Kindred society. You may disagree but I think the point still holds.

    As for tribal societies being nicer and more humane than feudal societies....not really no. I've never heard that put forward outside of hyper romanticised thinking. You could probably argue they're "as bad" but most pre modern societies have a problem with endemic violence and kratocratic tendencies.
    Basically, I tend to have the view that tribal societies are primarily viewed through the "primitivism" lens of historians who are engaged in a great deal of cultural posturing. The dismissal of their own societies and traditions for supposed Western advancement. Basically, "tribal" gets applied to a lot of complex cultures even today.
