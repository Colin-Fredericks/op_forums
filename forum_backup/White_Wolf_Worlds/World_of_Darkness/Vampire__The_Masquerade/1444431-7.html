        Originally posted by Shakanaka View Post

        It IS worse since such things degrade the quality of the franchise. If things happen for no reason, with no build up; or if things are nonsensically splattered despite firm precedents otherwise to the contrary, the immersion or taking the setting seriously peters. Especially in a roleplaying game on its own. What if you have a Lasombra from the previous edition and you doing a test run chronicle with only V5 lore background developments in mind? When visiting the Abyss, are you now trapped in it permanently due to it being the nigh-impenetrable prison of the Eloheim? Or are you just cut off from it entirely? What happens now?

        And apparently, which I forgot to bring up, was the fact that (and I literally just seen this off the wiki) the [Lasombra] Antediluvian apparently became one with the Abyss itself? So now with V5 linking the Abyss to the "Low Umbric" version of it, has the Lasombra literally become a prison now? And if we apply retroactivity here (since what has been written for V5 now has done retroactivity by making the Lasombra/Obtenebration linked to the Low Umbric Abyss when it wasn't before), how in the world could [Lasombra] even pull this off; if we're to fully believe that the Abyss is completely inaccessible reality-wise with it being under Oblivion?

        Gratiano apparently killed [Lasombra] during the 1st Anarch Revolts, right? That was in the High-to-Late Middle Ages and certainly was waaay before the Week of Nightmares; there is no way [Lasombra] could've gotten inside (or even the possibly of Obtenebration being developed, but I already talked about that point). Even his article on the wiki says the "Abyssal entities" weren't interested in him because he was former flesh; but in actuality per D:tF's own lore (which of course has been strewn into involvement due to retroactivity), the Fallen would've been VERY interested with [Lasombra] with the fact he seemingly or somehow became their prison; or the fact that he could leave and come back at will, while they were stuck there in torment.

        And with clause of retroactivity; with the correlation of merging Obtenebration and Necromancy into one convenient lazy Discipline called "Oblivion", and the new implications of the Lasombra now having associative spheres with the Low Umbra; how come there is no new word or plotpoints with discussing the Lasombra having anything to now do with Necromancy or the Low Umbra itself? Will that ever be addressed? Because not any of the Lasombra Clanbooks really at all speak of or did things with the Low Umbra or Necromancy. Everything about it makes no sense or broken in a way that it didn't need to be.
        Ok, so let's start with the fact that entire tirade about the implications of merging the Lasombra Abyss with the DtF Abyss is almost entirely you frothing yourself up, unless you can provide me a source that actually says the DtF Abyss exists in the X5 Universe and has been merged with the Abyss of the Lasombra. You're driving yourself into a fit when 50% of the thing you're ranting about is literally non-existent. There is no Demon the Fallen 5th Edition. You don't know how much the metaphysics and backstory of THAT will be retconned to ensure that all the stuff you are going off about doesn't ever happen. Do Demons and Hunters exist in V5, canonically?

        Please cite me some sources on that. Or maybe, um, chill out?

        Now, to be clear, I'm not here to stan for V5. I'm here because I'm sick of people ignorantly conflating THEIR preferred interpretation of the World of Darkness as the objective standard by which all changes are judged superior or inferior. If this was just "I don't like this," I would have no complaints to make. It's when these arguments go beyond that, to declaring that any interpretation of the World of Darkness that doesn't fit YOUR standards is "degraded." Hell, I wouldn't care if you said "degraded for me" or "degraded in x way" because it would be *qualified,* given context.

        There are clearly people who enjoy V5, who are willing to defend it. By what objective standard are they "wrong" exactly? It may be fair to criticize V5 for catering too much to one playstyle to the detriment of many others, but some people do seem to think it brings significant improvements to that playstyle. Stop saying "it's degraded" when what you really mean is "I don't like it anymore." That's your opinion, you have a right to it. But stop assuming that YOUR opinion is the Truth on a subject that is pretty obviously as subjective as whether or not pineapple belong on pizza.

        Let's take one of the favourite targets that people have taken in this debate, the idea of the cosmology being "boiled down" or "reduced" into an over-simplified D&D-esque cosmology. I understand with the criticisms of that position, and I'm even inclined to agree with them to an extent. But you understand that, when it comes to stuff like writing, clarification often IS better. These games are about giving people the tools to tell stories, and people who want the tools to tell a straightforward mythical story have just as much right as the people who want a convoluted contradictory occult mystery. Making it DIFFERENT doesn't mean its DEGRADED, it means the Target Audience has changed.

        And yeah, that sucks, and I'd sympathize with you a lot more if you didn't cross the line into constantly implying that other people's ways of enjoying the game are wrongbadfun.
