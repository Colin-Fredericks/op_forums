    New Era Lore & Headcanons

    12-05-2020, 01:01 PM
    So vampire has always enjoyed a wealth of attention attached to it as the main world of darkness flagship game line, but for those of us who enjoy using canon materials as inspiration for our home games, it’s impossible not to note that after 2005 and until the release of V20 and V5 (and even then comparatively) there’s a slight drought of material, at least relatively, so in the spirit of crowd sourcing I extend questions to y’all.

    How do y’all think vampires have/would have reacted to the past two decades? Whether political, cultural, or technological developments; what do you think might have happened or would be interesting if it had happened? What do you think might have happened to various sects, clans, factions, or even individual vampires?

    Now to head this off at the pass - I’m interested in V20 and V5 and everything in between. I’m happy to see people post ideas which could fit within either paradigms or neither. All I ask is that this not become a pissing match in either direction - every perspective is valid so long as it is meaningful to the people who subscribe to it and facilitates their enjoyment of the setting.

    With that having been said, to sum up, my intention as OP is for this thread to basically serve as a resource for ideas, inspiration, headcanons, and potential plot hooks to illustrate the setting of the World of Darkness in the era following the end of the revised gameline era.

    All that said, I hope folks enjoy this thread and it can provide both inspiration and a creative outlet!

      To get it started with some ideas...

      •The Gangrel Exodus
      A large segment of clan Gangrel left the Camarilla after the departure of Xaviar. While certainly not all left and in fact many remain amongst every sect, the Gangrel always had a cultural bias towards independence. They have always been a clan of survivors and their culture has often seen them eschew the society of their cousins of the blood.

      Now the Gangrel are spread across the world and independent domains stripe the wilderness. Scandinavia and the American Heartland have both proven strong examples. In Scandinavia, alongside the rise of the Anarchs and the Valkyrie faction, the Gangrel happily establish domains across the wilderness and hinterlands of Northern Europe. Similar practices follow in the Midwest and Southwest where Gangrel have become well known to stalk the back countries and wildernesses which others must now avoid. While the accounts of new Viking & Native American cultures are somewhat exaggerated, the old ways have always been popular amongst the outlanders. The reports of Gangrel raiders however are fairly accurate and many suburban and urban domains have been forced out of necessity to come to agreements with independent domains of Gangrel to keep them from wanton poaching and raiding of resources. In many ways, despite the shock of many ancillae, elders find this to be actually a return to the norm for much of Clan Gangrel.

      •The Web
      Though the elders may not have liked it, the democratization of the internet and the digital revolution made vampires online impossible to avoid. Many neonates and almost fledgelings were born and embraced into a digital world and it’s become so expansive that it’s more suspicious now to not be online somewhat then to be online if you’re a western youth. While the elders and ancillae tried to forbid the use of the internet this was impossible to enforce and seeing any attempts as ceding cyberspace to the Anarchs the other sects were forced to adapt. Now as ScreckNET (the public version at least, the Nosferatu aren’t giving up the moderator powers or their own layers of operation) spreads across the neonates like wildfire alongside techno-mystical applications like FangBook and the BloodSpot, though the Camarilla and Sabbat elders watch it warily, almost every neonate (the majority of kindred) are online to some degree.

      This has naturally been a boon and a curse. Fears of Masquerade Breaches over YouTube were realized yet the impact was made less disastrous due to the sheer flood of misinformation which the internet allowed for. While Nosfetatu, Alastors, and Templars scour the internet for genuine masquerade breaches they come across more good-looking fakes done with special effects then they do breaches themself. However this being the case is no reason to get lazy, as young fledgelings are often warned and yet sometimes seem to struggle to comprehend.

      The internet has made its own cultures and instant communication has allowed for vampiric culture to develop even more mystery and rumor while somehow being more informed. Legends, gossip, and stories fly rapidly with accounts of the jyhad stretching the globe. Ventrue and Toreador flaunt their skills to the praise of hundreds world-over or find themselves global mockeries after failures. Brujah are able to whip one another into even greater frenzies in digital echo chambers and chat room rants. The Malkavians are a wealth of information and misformation who’ve made the internet a second home (and none are better at memes). The Nosferatu walk through the internet as social masters as they can be nowhere else; in the age where information is king they are emperors.

      While the Camarilla and Sabbat as institutions struggle to keep up with neonates tutoring ancillae who discourse with elders (as well as legions of dedicated ghouls and thralls operating and monitoring) the internet remains an existential terror for many ancilllae and most elders. While they have struggled to gain more prominence and presence, and corporate & government data mining has been something of a relief to them, they remain in dread because of one undeniable truth. The internet is the domain of the Anarchs and the young - and the young shall ever rise up against their elders...

      •Globalization
      Vampiric domains are historically isolated fiefs and throughout the majority clan cultures have been what connect kindred across geographic lines. Sects and Proto-Sects may have united disparate domains but always due to proximity and common interest. Given the secretive and careful nature of kindred, while stories of global struggle were ever popular, most kindred knew nothing of their kin a few day’s travel away much less across continents and even the globe.

      This is no longer the case. Rapid travel allows one to traverse the globe in a matter of hours. Instant communication allows for messages to travel in seconds. The kindred, as antiquated as they are, could not deprive themselves of such an obvious resource. While Anarchs and youths may have realized the possibilities first, elders and the older sects had more resources with which to exploit those possibilities.

      So now, though always through proxy, clans and sects can communicate in instant. An elder raises a finger, a ghoul transcribes a message, and in minutes the elder’s descendants from New York to Hong Kong receive their instructions. Sects are able to mobilize and communicate more effectively then ever before, even through the intricate matrix of codes and proxies which elders demand for a sense of security. When a Camarilla city in Canada falls to a Sabbat siege; the Camarilla leadership in Europe can react to the news in moments and begin ordering misinformation and spin doctoring, while Anarchs in the US can spread the news across the Movement in instants, and Sabbat as far as Argentina can celebrate a victory for the Sword of Caine.

      What more this era of travel and communication has made it inevitable that contact between the sects of the west and other sects grow. Bloodlines, independents, and groups such as the Laibon and the Vampiric Courts of Asia inevitability become slowly more known and while secrecy and difficulties making contact remain, with every year the world gets smaller and smaller...

                      Rather than do a bunch of important GIANT CLAN AND POLITICS changes, I'm going to do a bunch of small inconsequential character details and hope people enjoy those for what they are.

                      Chicago by Night 5E

                      * Kevin Jackson shaves his head every night due to the embarrassing "Kid and Play" $ sign haircut he's permanently stuck with for the rest of his life. The Tremere are actively trying to get hold of these hair samples.

                      * Damien is genuinely surprised that his fellow Anarchs are now MORE committed to the cause than they ever were during the Camarilla days and some are actually willing to die for it. A lot of his old friends don't believe his defection while new ones think he's a monster.

                      * Baby Chorus sounds a lot like AC/DC due to the fact that it's a lot harder rock sound than Goth nightclub. That's Kathy Glenn's influence. Their most famous song is one that AC/DC covered in, "If you want Blood."

                      * Alan Sovereign has an intimate knowlege of hip hop, R&B, and gangsta rap. It's not part of any weird plot, it's just he developed a fondness for it.

                      * Horatio Ballard feeding on the children of his family was actually a slander created by Capone.

                      * One of the things that Son blackmailed Ballard into doing was being a independent wrestler called the Human Blob. Oddly, he actually enjoyed this and ocassionally still does weekend matches under a mask. He even hired El Diablo Verde to help train him.

                      * Bobby Weatherbottom is the founder of the Red Question and the business he wiped out the fortune of was Ballard.

                      * Bobby is considering kidnapping Thin Bloods to start feeding on in place of his girlfriend. They're the only viable alternative other than torpor. He's negotiating VERY carefully with Bronwyn.

                      * Ballard is actually flat broke. He uses Dominate and Presence to live with his richer family members. It'll take decades to get back his original fortune and it hasn't helped Bobby has continually screwed him over.

                      * All of the Primogen and Nosferatu actually know that Khalid is Cedrick Calhoun. They just assume this is him fooling everyone else and are getting a bit annoyed he hasn't dispensed with the pretense yet.

                      * Cedrick Calhoun's racist "Middle Eastern Wise Man" interpretation of Khalid HAS fooled Kevin Jackson completely.

                      * Joseph Peterson is now working for the RED Network and was involved with the Trump campaign. He's thorougly aligned with Pentex now and does frequent commentary for the new.

                      * Nerissa Blackwater is just a 5th Generation Brujah and her claims of mysteriousness are largely that, claims.

                      The Chicago Folio

                      * Kevin Jackson exiled the Wolf Pack because he hates the idea of Archons having authority over him. He also distrusts Gangrel due to their leaving the Camarilla.

                      * Prince Decker has made numerous offers to the Wolf Pack to join him in Milwaukee and they're honestly not sure why they haven't taken him up on it yet.

                      * Al Capone's quest for enlightenment has led him to the Church of Caine. He's still not entirely certain he wants to leave behind his Catholicism but the local branch is close enough that he gets by it.

                      * Gabrielle Twitch streams Revenant: The Ravishing games with Arthur Caldwell and Bobby Weatherbottom plus multiple other vampires. It is a huge Masquerade breach.

                      * Lianne Miller got her name from X-Factor comics.

                      Let the Streets Run Red

                      * Walter Nash also goes by "Sir" in conversations despite never being to England in his life.

                      * Walter Nash used to be put in charge of Chicago while Lodin was visiting London. This was not because he trusted Nash but because he figured Nash would irritate everyone so much that they would be delighted when he returned (and he was right). This is why Nash has considered himself the Prince of Chicago sometimes.

                      * The Sleeping God may be a Earthbound or Bane imprisoned by the Uktena. It might also be nothing.

                      * Honor is a lesbian and her sire is quite irritated with that discovery since he fell in "love" with her from a far. Decker is many things but not a rapist so he's attempting to adjust his feelings accordingly.

                      * The Ministry teachers in Indianapolis are actually REALLY BAD at their job. Their attempts to merge Christian fundamentalism and Setite logic fail for everyone as they don't know the Path of Heaven or other ways. As such, they're just driving their subjects to the Beast.

                      * Evelyn Stephens is actually Blood Bound to Juggler but he did a much better job of managing it.

                      * Gregory Stephens and Sullivan Dane took out Lucian despite the fact he was Beckoned.

                      * "Charlie" is not a renamed Michael but Michael's childe who was actually a child when Embraced. Charlie diablerized Michael and was mentored by Son.

                      * Arthur doesn't have a split personality but is a sleeper agent for Maxwell among the Anarchs. Ironically, Maxwell couldn't prepare for the fact that Arthur has the True Love merit to Allicia.
