    -----I don't think that's threadcrapping, but just in case:

    But now there aren't setties. Everything I liked about them is a gross parody of them.

    I feel a good portion of things I don't like about V5 is that a lot of the changes seem to be.... things people wanted to happen.

    Not things that should or would happen. Things people want to happen. The writers are finally eating the cake, and leave nothing for the readers.
    One of the interesting things about Revised was that they greatly expanded a lot of the concepts of the original V:TM clans. The Giovanni went from being a bunch of Italians and their families to having Aztec clans, American Jewish clans, and many other variations. The Followers of Set benefited from having a bunch of expansions too so there was an Aztec Bloodline, a Norse Bloodline, and so on to go with the Caribbean Bloodline. V5 seems to be following up on a lot of these conflicts and how these international groups might have problems with one another.

    For me, the CHURCH OF SET has the benefit of being everything the Old Followers of Set are. They're exactly like the WEB OF KNIVES in that if you want to play the most badass mutherfucker who ever lived who drinks the blood of the Unclean then you still have someone to follow in Ur-Shulgi's blood cult. You just don't associate that with Muslims anymore (and thus avoid the Unfortunate Implications). The Church of Set is all the "God of Chaos, Lord of Storms" Egyptian sexiness that existed beforehand but has decided to reign in the ecumenism of old to something that coincidentally increases their power base.

    The old stuff is still there but there's now MORE to play. You also have potential enemies to play off against and engage with if you're lacking storytelling. The Ministry can fight the Church of Set and the Church of Set can fight the Ministry. Or maybe you can ignore each other and attempt to do your own thing in the same city.

    I've made an embarrassing number of Lucita fanboy jokes because I based like 5 characters on her ala Drizzt Do'Urdem BUT if I had to say who my favorite character was in V:TM it would almost certainly be Modius....then Anita Wainwright BUT THEN Hesha. I've made like 4 characters based on him so he's only a little behind Moncada's childe. He's just the coolest bastard this side of the Nile and one of the genuinely scary people in the setting. His Clan Novel is my favorite of the entire setting.

    I actually made a pitch about writing a Anarch novel with him to Jason Carl.

    Him leading the Anarchs....well, that has possibilities. Also, I like the idea of him struggling with the Ancient Old Fogies in Egypt.

                I'm a bit late to this discussion, but I see Vicissitude as a sort of stumbling block on the Path of Metamorphosis. The Path is about transcending the state of vampirism, but too many Tzimisce get caught up in the fleshy aspect and forget the spiritual aspect. This was touched on in BJD I think when Beckett was told how the Eldest was lost for a time (or something like that) but no more and now was going to consult with It's siblings.

                Or in other words, Vicissitude was supposed to be one means to an end, but thanks to its utilitarian uses, becomes the end in itself. As to why more Tzimisce get caught up in it... Maybe it's corruption of Kupala? Maybe it's the Eldest, using it to weed out the wheat from the chafe of the Clan? Maybe it's the all too human leftover in each vampire that seeks to shape and control their environment? Or maybe it really is an Umbral parasite.

                I guess what I'm getting at is Tzimisce don't need Vicissitude to be cool. Imagine the ingenuity (and lengths) a Metamorphosist would go to in order to fully embrace his philosophy without the ease (and that is the key, I think) Vicissitude offers? Vicissitude makes Azhi Dahaka easy, perhaps too easy. Transcendence shouldn't be easy.

                And to go back to the thread topic, I think Ravnos remnants should hold a special place within the Sabbat (not like what the Lasombra and Tzimisce were, but something to emulate, have in your Pack as a good luck charm, etc...). They are the one Clan that actually has no Antediluvian. Sure, it's not common knowledge, but astute Sabbat will notice none of them are experiencing the Beckoning.
