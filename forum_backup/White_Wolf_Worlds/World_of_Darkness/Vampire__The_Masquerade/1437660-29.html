Presence works the same, and Fortitude in an era of handguns and bombs? It's arguably the most valid physical (even if mechanics leave something to be desired) As for the weakness? It's actually far less of a problem now than it would've been ever before in the past. nightlife actually being a thing, Larger, denser cities, mobile apps massively cutting down the effort required to search for new herd members, the anonymity of too-many people letting them get away with what others would perceive as a weird fetish, and the Ventrue can easily create support groups, charities, group things, whatever to get his eggs in one basket. Surely now is the primetime of the Ventrue.
