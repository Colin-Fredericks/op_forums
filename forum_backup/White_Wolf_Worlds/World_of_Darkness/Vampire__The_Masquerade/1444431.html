      I am mostly indifferent to the change of Obternation to Oblivion. It feels a bit random, but not enough so to be an issue.

      I like the idea of at least some of the Lasombra joining the Camarilla. The old segregations of the game (such as no Lasombra, Tzimisce or Giovanni among the Anarchs, no Tzimisce among the Camarilla, and so on) always felt counterintuitive to me, a bit of social rules imposed on the setting which did not reflect real human behavior. That some Lasombra would decide the Sabbat is a disaster and want to jump ship makes perfect sense in a "one step back, two steps forward" kind of way. And it opens game possibilities for players.

      Of course some Lasombra will stay with the Sabbat.

      All the clans have been rewritten in ways small and large in V5. The changes to the Lasombra are simply part of that.

      I'm fine with how they have been updated.

      Talley did not kill my raven-haired dark angel... I mean, Talley did not kill Lucita because that would be a waste of a good character. And she will probably be a leader of one of the Sabbat factions.

          Well. From reading the Chicago: By Night book I never got the impression that the Lasombra joined the Camarilla per se. Some did. The short adventure at the end of the book kinda makes the PCs convince the Prince to go through with it, and he is giving the Lasombra quite a harsh tax to pay. And this is only Chicago, we don't know if other princes agreed. The Lasombra in Chicago feels more like refugees than anything else. I mean, even if the Lasombra is the ones who wish to join, we don't know how other Camarilla elders reacted to it, and if there's a consensus about it. We only know that Kevin Jackson agreed (as far as I know).

          I think that we won't really know until we will get the Sabbat book. As much as I liked Chicago: By Night, it is nothing but a flavor book IMO, and the canon there is very sketchy, werewolves are being killed there like they were fledgling vampires, I mean the prince killed the alpha of a pack with a decorative sword he took down from a wall. Or the fact that one Nosferatu is impersonating another Nosferatu and no other elder with auspex apparently notices it.

          About Oblivion. It should have been its own thing. I liked it when I didn't know what was the darkness that the Lasombra summon exactly, tieing it to Necromancy kinda steal all the mystery. I think game-wise the powers are different enough to warrant their own disciplines, and I think that it is very confusing for new players who don't understand the lore, kinda muddy the water with regard to the specialties of the clans who use Oblivion. I would really prefer it if Necromancy was part of Blood Sorcery, because, in a way, it is.

              I have thought of two possibly interesting stories involving a Lasombra -

              1. Two recent emigres have arrived in the city about the same time: a warrior assamite, and a lasombra. The Prince has decreed that both shall serve his court as Scourge for the remainder of the year. At the end of that time, the one whose performance has pleased him best will be allowed to stay, and the other must find haven elsewhere. Both of the dangerous new arrivals have made it clear they intend to win the contest. Now the Domain's Caitiff and Anarchs have serious reason to be afraid....

              2. The relatively young Ventrue Prince of a Western American city has been caught en flagrante delicto with his Lasombra Scourge. The two vampires have formed a mutual blood bond, an act disparaged within the Camarilla as perversion. The region's Ventrue Elders are outraged, and clamor for the Prince to be removed for this violation of dignitas. The Primogen however, are divided--some continue to support the Prince, while others consider the opportunity to place their own puppet on the throne.

                  ..Haven't we already had one of these from you?

                  First thing the new lasombra weakness is uncharacteristically emasculating for one of the most man's-man* clans out there. How can a clan known for their agression, fierce individualism and self-made bosses be lowered to have a ghoul take their phone calls for them? How can a clan known to trust no-one not even their reflections come to put so much faith into a retainer? compounded by the fact that this weakness is just so... modern. Vampire's are meant to be unchanging immortals, not fellows who go through version updates every few decades; not being captured by technology would mean they had virtually no weakness before the later modern era. It just feels like someone completely missunderstands the lasombra, or they perfectly understand the lasombra and hate them and want to change them to be more nice or something outrageous like that.

                  *Yes, the Lasombra have women, and they're excellent, goal oriented monsters who enjoy stepping on people.

                  The Sabbat are gone, and as for the Lasombra joining the Camarilla.. again, this just feels like an attempt of emasculation. They've gone from kings of the night to wipping boys at the bottom of the rung in the declining camarilla. Did they come dressed in Gimp suits? When did such an S clan become a purely M clan? The part that doesn't feel like it's an emasculation is the part where the Cam is now openly religious and that somewhat ties into the Lasombra being the posterboys for religious vampires... but the removal of a secular camarilla is also something I don't like, with the "heroic" Brujah being replaced by notably catholic authoritarians it just seems like the Cam have become an idealistic left-wing imagining of republicans (who, if we're being honest, should probably be keeping the Brujah)


                  How should the Lasombra have been updated?
                  Barely.
                  You could just have a few more defects and have the Magisters embrace a few more children and apply for a council seat. You don't need to give the clan an idiot ball and have them defect en mass to a dying cam with so many restrictions where the anarchs are very much open and aren't going to bust balls for members. You should also consider that defections are going to be done on a local level and it's rather absurd to have some rules for international defections.

                  Also Obtenebration could've used a slight nerf. Oblivion is unwarranted overkill and really messes the clan's identity with the old Giovanni.
