        Originally posted by DarkKing View Post

        Speaking of which, i doubt the usual "counter terrorist operation" cover story is going to actually fly with this one.
        It didn't fly once in the entire story.

        Every single time it's used, it's only to convince couch potatoes living far from the area. For anyone actually living in the action zones it is absurdly obvious for several reasons.

        First: since 2001 "terrorism" is used out of proper context and without proper evidence. If that's true IRL, imagine in setting.

        Second: SI uses it even more stupidly. They link to groups and make terrorism allegations out of thin air and people notice both in the affected area and nearby.

        Third: people aren't stupid. Locals know their circumstances, academics and observers know those things follow dynamics and goals. Few believe in "terrorist activity" in those coverups, except people who want to.

        So this is just white Middle Class US/EU citizens seeing for themselves for a change how their excuses elsewhere are actually perceived by decidedly unimpressed foreigners and/or minorities.

        What matters is how you interpret the setting/understand those allegations. IRL institutions tell this kind of lie partly for the comfort of a very small population that fund them, partly as legal protection: what everyone knows don't matter if can't be proven.

        If you take at face value as "the Second Inquisition really cover their tracks and it is all seen as counter-terrorism", then this game can only reflect a very limited world view, because no one outside the US believe in the US terrorist narrative the way it is told (and no few see the US as the terrorists).

        And I'm talking narrative style, I know the SI isn't just (or even mostly) US personnel. But it is predicated on a lot of US culture.
