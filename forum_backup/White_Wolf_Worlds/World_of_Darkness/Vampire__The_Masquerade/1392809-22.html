                    Originally posted by Sergeant Brother View Post

                    Now I'm curious. What is your interpretation of those Clans?
                    Take note this is only true in my home games and I never would argue this is true in canon. Let's head off any worries I'm misinterpreting these clans on these forums. I do not bring my fanon into my canon discussions.

                    1. Tremere: I generally treated the Tremere as a ticking time bomb of intrigue, infighting, and suppressed rage in the Pre-V5 universe. They are constantly jockeying for power, fighting duels, plotting against one another, and following a Social Darwinist philosophy imposed by the Council of Seven. They're all Blood Bound to the 7 and the 7 have the power to destroy you at any time because they have samples of your blood like in Dragon Age. Their superiors also regularly use Dominate on their lessers and condition them to obey unlike the majority of Kindred. Expect to have your thoughts read regularly too. It's really a kind of hellish existence and it's severely lacking in freedom. I draw from Psi-Corp in Babylon Five and the Sith from The Old Republic. "I have no mouth but I must scream" is painted on the souls of any Tremere who would resist their superior.

                    It was, in simple terms, a cult in the classical sense that broke you down and rebuilt you -- whether you wanted it to or not.

                    So when the Council of Seven were destroyed, it was an explosion of in-fighting, Civil War, and settling old debts as well as people who were like, "I AM FREE AT LAST! FREE!" Sometimes people who you would have never suspected were chomping at the bit themselves. The Tremere are now rebuilding but are a little confused, really, because so many of them aren't the same sorts of people without the Blood Bond (plus excessive use of Dominate) dominating their every thought. It's left them weak politically and disunited but they're now expanding and far more willing to fight than they once were. Its honestly shocked a lot of vampires too because so few of them realized what was festering under their robes.

                    2. Malkavians: I tend to go with the view that Malkavians are not often as overtly insane as many appear but they're actually just "weird" and that is confused as insanity. A large percentage (maybe 40%) Malkavian derangements are some combination of emotional instability, paranoia, narcissism, sanguinary obsession, and psychopathic lack of empathy. The thing is that actually just means they're not that different from a typical Elder. So almost half of the clan is NOT insane from the perspective of your typical vampire. A Malkavian with rage issues is basically indistinguishable from a Brujah. The other 60% may have hallucinations, schizophrenia, and multiple personalities but they are normal "most" of the time.

                    Malkavian CLAN CULTURE on the other hand is based around gestalt consciousness, prophecy, religious ecstasy, pushing the boundaries of consciousness, and serving as the gadfly of Kindred society. This is what Kindred assume is the Malkavian derangements when it's actually their culture of being a bunch of psychedelic hippies as well as religious mystics.

                    3. Thin Bloods: I heavily emphasize that Thin Bloods are humans with fangs. They are a subject of both envy and loathing of other vampires. Whatever the Curse of Caine has done to vampires, torturing their souls and leaving them shells of who they were, Thin Bloods are...not. They are people with more or less the same spirits and souls they had in life. Hell, they aren't even undead. Not really. This means that they can maintain the Humanity of regular people and live the normal life a Kindred can't. It means that other Kindred may want to protect them to save what little remains of their souls or destroy them because of jealousy. They're perfect for being Friendly Neighborhood Vampires because they're the only vampires who can be really.

                                    Oh, someone asked me what my difference between Lasombra and Followers of Set were. Since this WAS a Lasombra thread, I'll share it.

                                    Lasombra vs. Followers of Set

                                    At my home table these are the two "Clans of Faith" and come at it from very different perspectives. So much so that the two have almost nothing in common once you get down to it.

                                    For the Lasombra, Faith is a tool. They manipulate it, weave it, and export it. Lasombra are involved in televangelism, megachurches, Faith-based lobbying, and behind every single Catholic Priest who has a dirty little secret. They love creating cults but they are cults that exist solely to make sure that young beautiful vessels service the leader while older members empty their pocket-books. Some Lasombra sincerely believe but for the vast majority of them, religion is very much the opiate of the masses. It's a scam that they milk for all of its worth.

                                    This is, to an extent part of their problem in V5 because the Sabbat is their magnum opus for creating a religion that is complete bullshit as far as 80% of the Lasombra is concerned. They don't believe in Caine, are quite confidant Gehenna is a lie (they killed their Antediluvian after all), and use the Antitribu as a bunch of cannon fodder. Because that's what they are. The Tzimisce are the only clan they even remotely respect and the Lasombra assumed they were in on the scam.

                                    That's why the Gehenna Crusade horrified them, not necessarily because of the fact that it happened. The Lasombra were expecting to head to the desert, kill some Ashirra, dig up some temples, and call it a day. No, the problem was THEY FOUND OUT THEIR RELIGION WAS REAL and it has TERRIFIED them.

                                    The Followers of Set/Ministry have a very simple difference from the Lasombra in this respect. They believe. The Followers of Set will set up a cult, they will fill it with blood bonds, sexual drug orgies, and they will set themselves up as gods to be worshiped with sex slaves as well as cash. Just like the Lasombra. The thing is the FoS are of the mind that they are doing this for the greater glory of whatever god they venerate. It's a means to an end of enlightenment that they would drop if they didn't feel it was working. The Followers of Set all start at the bottom of their cults and they work their way up. Some faster than others but their worshipers aren't rubes. They are actual members of the flock.

                                    The scary thing that the Followers of Set provide that almost no other Kindred Clan does is certainty and genuine community. They are prepared to give their unlives for the Cause, just like the Sabbat, and its with full knowledge that it is for the greater Kingdom of Darkness ahead.

                                    This is one of the reasons why making a deal with the Setites is so dangerous. Because they're not actually trying to scam you. When you're lost, alone, and don't have a friend in the world--the Setites will be there for you. Not because they're trying to trick or entrap you but because they KNOW their unlife is better and want to share it with you.

                                    It's just that the thing they're sharing is the god the Devil prays to.

                                    Lasombra are Jim Baker and Falwell, Followers of Set are Masada zealots.
