                            The key difference between Changelings (both types) and Demons is that Changelings are born into what they are. Even as babies they've already got the soul of a much older, eldritch being within them. With Demons however, they end up fusing with the body of a normal human, typically one who is much older and has already experienced a childhood, years as a teenager and even frequently been an (normal) adult for some time.

                            Ultimately though, the process of how Hsien are born is why White Wolf considered them to be a type of changeling and put them in with Changeling: the Dreaming. Likewise they felt that Wan Kuei, as blood drinkers, were better classified as a type of vampire and added them to Vampire the Masquerade, rather than a type of Risen and linked them with Wraith: The Oblivion.

                            I think there are definitely strong arguments as to why Wan Kuei might be better classified as something besides Vampires, and Hsien aren't really Changelings at all, that was just White Wolf's logic as to why those groups were put in with each other.

                                                    Originally posted by AnubisXy View Post
                                                    The key difference between Changelings (both types) and Demons is that Changelings are born into what they are.
                                                    That's not actually true, especially because you're retroactively attributing to White Wolf an intention based on distinctions that would only exist several years later.

                                                    Changeling came 7 years before Demon, in a time when their publishing schedule and development pace was insane, it could easily have been 21 years at Paradox pace. It came mid to early 2nd Edition and Demon came near the end of Revised.

                                                    When the Hsien were created Demon didn't exist in any relevant form as a future product. It isn't like the Kuei-Jin who were thought as a counterpart to an established line.

                                                    KotE was designed as a foil to VtM. They're not treated as Kindred because of having Kindred like traits, they have Kindred like traits because that was the point of their design. White Wolf had a theme for the Year and KotE was the result, the end, however good or bad that is.

                                                    Now, could they change association to Wraith, instead? Yes. Could they treat as a standalone line? Also yes. They did with Mummy, that's actually one of the oldest splats but as another VtM expansion.

                                                    But the question isn't what they can. It's where it brings more benefits. Keeping within a line means facilitating communication, enforcing a minimum compatibility and keeping them as a same set. KotE is a VtM product not because it is metaphysically close to Kindred, its because they're meant to interact with VtM and enforce the division of territory between the two groups.

                                                    And that's also the reason Hengeyoukai stayed a staple while KotE, being far more popular, got ignored, sidestepped, and overall became a big problem for the publishers. Not because it was a bad game, but because it was a problematic part of VtM.

                                                    Moving it to Wraith would improve things? Not really as they didn't had much reason to interact with Wraith at this level, either. If KotE is ever to become a thing again (which I hope it does), it should be as its own line.

                                                    Now, Changeling. First things first, the Sidhe do exactly this very thing, possessing an adult body and sending the soul somewhere else. They have zero need to be children first.

                                                    Even the others don't start life as supernatural beings, that's simply not true, at least no more than any Changing Breed. They're not special until their Chrysalis, and that's as likely to happen after 13 than before.

                                                    It is true that both Changelings and Hsien have in common the themes of fused souls born this way that tend to come into their powers at childhood, but that's a secondary design choice made because the intention to have them related predates any decision on who they are.

                                                    It's also true that there are exceptions among Changelings and going Changeling style is an option in Demon, especially today.

                                                    What I think is that the Hsien have little to nothing to contribute in Changeling. They don't interact meaningfully with the Kithain. They don't deal with similar stuff. Their energy sources are thematically closer to Faith than Glamour. A crossover between the two splats has no common ground to work with, which is ok but also means it's just any crossover.

                                                    Have they something to contribute as standalone? Maybe, but I think a participation in Demon actually does benefit both lines. They share metaphysical roles and possibly some history. They have reason to have strong opinions on each other. To feel kinship or hate. They dispute the same resource and have a similar situation as exiles.

                                                    Seriously, they have every reason to be canon to each other and establish interactions. To be a factor for the other splat. They're just not the same line because of a 7 year difference on when each was published.
