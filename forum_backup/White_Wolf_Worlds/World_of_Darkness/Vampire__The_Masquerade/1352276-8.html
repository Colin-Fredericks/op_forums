                            Baali

                            * The Baali are effectively wiped out and have been for millennium. Carthage was their last heyday and after Carthage, the ones in the Middle Ages were eventually smashed as well. They remain a Kindred boogeyman, though, because that allows other Kindred to believe they're at least not as bad as THOSE guys.

                            Sabbat

                            * Polonia is technically Regent of the Sabbat but has long since lost control over the Gehenna War and is terrified of what he's unleashed. Unfortunately, there's no chance he'll ever be accepted among the defecting Sabbat so he must ride this dragon until the very end.

                            Anarchs

                            * The Ministry fully expected to find a bunch of drooling morons when they joined the Movement. They did, to an extent, but also a lot of intelligent angry people with legitimate grievances. Some of which were reflected in their own relationship to their Elders. After all, what sort of God of Chaos has an orthodox church as well as inquisition? Its affecting them as much as its affecting them.

                            * The Anarchs aren't mentioning it but a lot of the reason for its new aggressive stance as well as rise in success is due to quite a lot of Sabbat Elders defecting. Vasantasena is one of them and has been a major influence on them but it's worrying as it well could mean history repeats itself.

                            * Tyler has defected to the Anarchs fully but not for the fact that she's regained her idealistic younger self. It's more that her ties to the Sabbat were exposed and her Blood Bond to Helena broken. As such, she got the hell out of dodge. She's now setting herself up as a Baron of Barons in the movement since the modern Anarchs are actually amendable to "leadership" in a way she's surprised (due to the prevalence of new Gangrel and Brujah members.

                                    CAINITES OF THE EAST

                                    (Disclaimer: A lot of good stuff has been written about the relative merits & flaws of the Kuei-Jin. I personally respect their niche, but think they do have serious issues. Yet this probably isn't the best place to go over those. My group hates them so in my games I usually banish them to a solitary mountain where they play out eternal morality plays. However in general, I'm more or less fine with them to an extent, but I usually change the way I run Asia as a continent from canon due to my and my own group's issues with it. I'm not a scholar of Asian cultures or histories and I can't write up the same quality of posts that some others have about what native Asian vampiric cultures may look like. I can only share what I, myself, tend to do and say that I try to be as respectful as possible and to inform myself as much as I can before I ever present something detailed in-play. Anyway, without further ado, my headcanons...)

                                    •Asia has vampires. From Jiang-Shi bygones and similar Chi drinkers that somewhat resemble the Laibon & Drowned Legacies in form, to the Kuei-Jin of old infamy, and of course to the damned Children of Caine. The Cainites are naturally not the first vampires of Asia, yet many of been here for so long that only immortals could truly care enough to split hairs...

                                    •The first Cainites who visited Central & Far East Asia did so during the days of the First and Second City. Caine & Lilith almost certainly spent time their in their prehistoric wandering while forgotten 2nd Gen & Antedeluvians may have sired creatures that one would call the 'Laibon/Drowned Legacies of the East'. Who can say? Yet, we do know that by the time of the Second City, some antediluvians and their exiled children had wandered through. Among the first were of course Ennoia, Ravnos, Saulot, and the Eldest. Wandering kindred in this time would come to reach almost everywhere human feet touched on the globe after all...

                                    •By the time that the Second City fell and societies began to arise in the Indus River Valley, China, and South East Asia; several kindred lineages were established in Asia. Predominant were the Gangrel, Malkavians, Nosferatu, Ravnos, Salubri, & Tzimisce. In time trade and travel would cause most clans to be represented to some degree, however minute. This allowed a vampiric culture that was somewhat similar politically to the ones of kindred in the Middle East, Northern Africa, & Europe to arise in Asia simultaneously, although naturally distinct as the kindred adapted to and adopted the cultures of their host peoples.

                                    •History marched on and the kindred reflected their host cultures. Vampires followed the lead of humanity and dark territories were carved across Asia. Regional cultures divided clans between high & low and elders ruled over the young. The kindred learned how to co-exist with the Kuei-Jin and other supernatural through treaties and compromise. The Jyhad waged as ever as grudges, ego, alliances, and treaties spread across Asia much like it had in the west; creating something akin to the War of Princes there as well. What was different however, was that the sheer scale of distance that Asia enjoyed from Europe and its limited contact with the west inured it from the flames of the Inquisition. While the young still revolted against elders, supernaturals still warred, and the humans grew furious with their nocturnal overlords and the Silence of the Blood was founded and kept; the kindred of Asia had neither the opportunity nor inclination to participate in the great experiment of the Camarilla or Sabbat. Sure India had its Jati and certain kindred lineages declared themselves 'Emperors' or 'Magistrates' of great swaths of territory and warred with their neighbors and rebellious childer to maintain their power; yet these kindred never needed adopt the sect culture of the west and this would become the notable difference between East & West that set the stage for the conflict that came following European arrival...

                                    (I've got more but I'll end this text wall here and add the rest later if people seem interested or the mood strikes me)

                                        Further thoughts & head-canons regarding Asia:

                                        •The introduction of western kindred to Asia was slow and sparse for a great deal of time, trade routes through the Middle East did connect the continents somewhat, as well as traders along the south seas. The Anda Gangrel who followed the Mongol Invasions further helped create ties between the continents as western Lasombra & Brujah amongst others became aware of vampiric realms beyond their sights. This stewed for a time until the kindred of the west finally had opportunity to expand out from the hoary halls of dusty Europe and followed the kine's imperial adventures into Africa and Asia alike...

                                        •The Sabbat in Spanish and Portuguese ships were amongst the first western kindred to arrive in Far East Asia and they broke out in hunger for ancient blood and new territories like a plague. The Cainites of Asia were bewildered by these pale newcomers and their so-called 'Crusades' and while some weak domains did fall and the native kindred allowed enemies to fall to the Sabbat amaranth, eventually coalitions formed against these savage invaders and as the Cainites of Asia determined to hold fast to their own domains against Western Invasion, the sails of imperial expansion had brought the Camarilla and Anarch Movement to their shores...

                                        •Just as China, Japan, India & other nations fought imperialist expansion, so did their native Cainites work to combat the western invaders who sought to envelop them into their War of Sects and unite two branches dynasties of the Jyhad again. While the Camarilla in time would see some successes and adoptions within domains where they could wield mortal institutional powers as Europeans and even in a few domains where the low clans were willing to destroy their ancient masters, though here the Sabbat & Anarchs were far more likely to see successes as they triggered eruptions of unrest and revolt throughout portions of rural East Asia. Despite this though, the western sects were largely a minority position in Asia. The ancient hierarchies remained largely empowered throughout the continent and sub-continent and it was largely only along the coastlines and islands where the Europeans had seen the most success that the western sects could truly claim to be in power. Yet, if nothing else this invasion did one thing, it forced the Kindred of Asia to formalize their web of alliances into something resembling a sect to avoid being turned against each other and destroyed one after another. Thus rose the Black Courts...

                                        •Something must be noted as far as clans are concerned: Asia is not and was not Europe and as such some things remain that shocked European Kindred upon arrival. For one, while they are few in number, Cappadocians remain in Asia, largely under the guard of their more locally prominent bloodlines, including the Nagaraja. This is likely why the Giovanni have often found themselves viciously contested outside of a few places such as Hong Kong and Tokyo when attempting to claim domain in East Asia while by contrast the Harbingers of Skulls of the Sabbat have something akin to a regional Renaissance. For another thing, Clan Salubri remains. While largely of the Watcher Caste, their Warrior & Healer kin who successfully fled Europe to escape the Tremere do now have places throughout Asian domains as well and so during the western invasions it was the Salubri who championed movements to turn back the western sects and then create the Black Courts, with Asia being considered the most dangerous place in the world to be Tremere. Finally, those Tzimisce lineages here were embraced prior to The Eldest forming their covenant with Kupala, meaning that they largely represent what would be called in Europe 'Old Clan Tzimisce' and thus, with the same vigor the Salubri championed against the Camarilla, these Tzimice championed against the Sabbat.

                                        •While the Sabbat and Camarilla both have faced incredible challenges while introducing themselves to Asia, another group were able to do such with much more success. The Anarch Movement was better able to spread itself across Asia for two prominent reasons. The first being that Asian Cainites had no memory of the Anarch Revolts to concern them nor did the Movement contain such problematic clans that had created issues for the Camarilla and Sabbat. For another, many Asian Cainites did not recognize the Anarchs as a sect. The Anarch Movement was deemed more akin to a philosophy or ideology, an argument not without some merit, and given the freedom with which ideologies were used to passing across the continent so long as the local ruler was not disturbed and the fierce autonomy that many domains still claimed, the Movement was able to slide like sand through a sieve into Asia while more centralized and solidified sects such as the Camarilla & Sabbat got stuck. This would prove to have disastrous consequences for the Black Courts come the early 20th century as the Anarch Movement ingratiated itself too many young kindred and low clans who chafed under the rule of the high clans and elders both. With an abundant population who slowly became sympathetic to their cause and a mortal movement to draw recruits and inspiration from, the Anarchs struck. As Communism & Republicanism toppled Imperial doctrines throughout Asia, the Anarch Movement raged across the continent and subcontinent, leading something akin to a second great Anarch Revolt. While this revolt did not destroy the Black Courts or old order, it did establish numerous Anarch domains of countless stripes across Asia while destabilizing and forcing the Black Courts to grow more centralized as it began to resemble its western counterpart, the Camarilla. As the 20th century came to its own, just as the new world became a fierce battle ground of the War of Sects & Jyhad, so to did Asia follow in kind...

                                            The WOD and History

                                            * There's a certain level of Pulpiness to the World of Darkness which contrasts against its Gothic Punk horror veneer. This is something that is notable only to its supernatural inhabitants and is a bit of a "dying of the light" against the darkness and shows what the world COULD be like if it could resist the horror beyond.

                                            * In Ancient Times, vampires often were considered somewhat less malevolent than they are today. A god that demands blood sacrifices, keeps harems of slaves, and is prone to murderous temper tantrums IS a typical god to most cultures. Vampires also did things like controlled the weather, kept away werewolves in the Impergium, and had the few powers capable of healing the sick or injured. Carthage, Mithras, and other vampires come from this view that is less "vampires are damned and dirty forever." It also, mind you, backfired in some places as we see with some of the bloodiest and most evil gods (Kronos getting diablerized by Zeus for example).

                                            * Alexander the Great, of all people, helped bring about the end of the age of the god kings. In this reality actually being a demigod (or believing he was a demigod so much he became one) and destroyed dozens of local vampire despots. His generals turned against him when he was either Embraced or they received offers of power that trumped their loyalty.

                                            * During Medieval times there was a genuine bunch of supernatural soldiers wandering around the world doing the Arthurian thing than there ever was during real life. They fought witches, vampires, and trolls while sometimes also being witches, vampires, and trolls. Sadly, the conflict among them resulted in supernaturals being weakened enough for the Inquisition to slaughter the majority of "good" supernaturals and slander their name. Now the very idea of vampire Pilgrim Knights and so on is derided by even those who are descended from them.

                                            * The Papal Inquisition gets most of the flack for vampire hunting but it was actually the Protestant Witch Hunters that did the majority of damage. The big difference being that vampires tend to equate all Christians with Catholics (at least at the time) and also that while the Catholic Church's Albergines Crusade and Spanish Inquisition are monstrous, the Protestant Witch Hunts actually tended to be far less discriminate. They did, however, do the most damage by going after supernatural SUPPORT groups. Tens of Thousands of Kinfolk, Kinian, Herds, Ghouls, and Acolytes did more to break the supernatural's power than actual slaying. They also killed about 10 for every person even tangentially related.

                                            * The French Revolution would have never succeeded without the covert aid of the Anarchs and this applies to many revolts but this is due to the supernatural influence cancelling each other out rather than the reverse. The Reign of Terror, though, was even bloodier and more ridiculous because that's what happens when vampires are in charge of a social movement. They make it worse. Something similar happened with the Bolsheviks but they hardly needed help.

                                            * There was a steampunk League of Extraordinary Gentleman style period and fantastical Wild West in the 19th century that was far in excess of what happened in RL. Lots more outlaw towns, violent showdowns, and so on than ever was in reality. The Technocracy won against local native wizards and people with the help of the Camarilla, signalling the downfall of traditional power structures of indigenous people and the rise of a world-wide union--or so they thought. Eventually, they were driven out but the scars they left lasted lifetimes and left people no longer believing in their local heroes. Yes, there's a pattern of heroics getting smashed down, why do you ask?

                                            * World War 1...was not remotely supernatural. Indeed, it is one of the least supernatural wars of all time.

                                            * WW2 was every bit as Pulpy and full of wackiness as you'd expect with Nazi mad scientists, armies of zombies, Satanic cults holding torch-lit ceremonies in crumbling castles, and the Technocracy as well as Traditions openly collaborating with the governments. The irony being that as far as the Nazis knew, most of these groups weren't actually under their control. They all believed they were in command of the fascists (or allies) but mostly just lent their aid while believing they would rule the world in the end. Hitler may have been actually replaced and killed a couple of times but if it walks like a duck and talks like a duck, is it really not a duck?

                                            * WW2 was also the last time supernaturals overtly played an important role in military history. Alan Turing was the last openly supernatural person of importance and killed for that (as well as homophobia among the Technocracy). Now supernaturals operate almost entirely through proxy groups like Anonymous and the Virtual Adepts or Occupy Wallstreet (as well as the Tea party) for the Brujah.

                                                Now, to detour from my usual essay about why Cainites in Asia is cool, here's a more Western headcanon that came to me on my car ride home today and I really liked.

                                                THE PEACE OF KINGS

                                                •In the time of the First City, Caine was King and Enoch was the Prince for whom the city bore the name. Caine was first amongst the Damned, yet he had been cursed by The One Above and he knew that to claim the title of 'King' was to insult The One, yet in his spite he readily did so. This was another instrument of his tragedy.

                                                •By the end of Enoch Caine had proclaimed his rulings, wisdom, and warnings which his children gathered in the Book of Nod. Amongst these warnings is that none should call themselves 'god' and the tradition arose that only Caine himself would be called 'King', for any other to do so would be usurpation and while [Ventrue] and [Lasombra] were willing to throw down Enoch to be called 'Prince', neither desired to fight and be destroyed by their grand-sire, whom remained King.

                                                •The Children of Caine were rebellious and spiteful as Caine himself was towards his Maker. The antediluvians called themselves gods in the time of the Second City and only by its destruction did they realize the truth of Caine's warning, that The One Above was a Jealous God and would never endure those damned such as them to be called 'god' and rule over humanity. Yet, their children rebuked their sire's wisdom, and proclaimed themselves gods again in the first cities of man after the Deluge and Fall of Ubar (Second City). Those who did would soon find themselves emerged in great tragedy, tribulation, and often truly destroyed. As blood gods fell to ash forever, the tumultuous children sought for an answer and they found one in the warnings of their elders and the Book of Nod. The One Above would never tolerate them as gods, so godship they rebuked. Yet this would not be their final time of suffering for their own arrogance.

                                                •While the Children of Caine had largely learned that they could not be gods, their arrogance did not cease, it merely transformed. They ruled the night with fabulous power, so they must be recognized. If not as gods, then as kings. So these children ruled as kings in Assyria, Mycae, Egypt, and all across Creation and for a time they rejoiced in bloody reigns and indulgences until again ruin befell them and they desperately sought the reason why. The Noddists rebuked these night kings for claiming titles not their own. Neither Caine nor The One Above had granted them kingship and if they wished to avoid another great Deluge or personal destruction, they must turn it aside. So, those who remained, greatly upset, did so turn away from kingly things. They claimed the titles of 'Prince' so as to not displease their Dark Father and they chose shadows so as to avoid the gaze of The One Above. Yet not all...

                                                •Legends say that proud Set was the last vampire who refused to forsake his crown and was thus struck dead by The One Above and/or Caine. This is unlikely as many reports show that Set had faded into the shadows by the beginning of the Pharonic age. Ones who did remain ruling as open blood gods and kings were Troile and Moloch in Carthage and the greatest Cassandra seers of Rome predicted untold ruin if Carthage remained. So Rome rose its legions and marched with Ventrue at its head and they destroyed the city and cast down its false gods to avoid destruction themselves. So some of the romans say at least. Regardless, vampires were no longer gods nor kings. Yet, as Rome saw, vampires controlled kings and priests. The false humility of these 'Princes' was magnificent to see and the vain kindred luxuriated in their triumph. Rome was the perfection of vampirism within the Order of The One Above, Caine, & Nod as well as all gods. Yet Rome fell...

                                                •Some blame Lasombra treachery. Some blame Ventrue arrogance. Some blame Toreador decadence. Some blame Gangrel brutality. Some blame Malkavian madness. Some blame Brujah vengeance. Some even blame those who did not heed Caine's supposed warnings that his children must never tolerate those who worshiped The One Above in their midst and say that Rome fell the day that Jews and Christians walked its streets unmolested. Regardless Rome did fall and its fall seemed apocalyptic. It seemed like Gehenna. Some Noddists proclaimed it to be the third fall. First gods, then kings, then princes who ruled kings. All these things had threatened a ruin that had finally come. Others claim that Rome fell for a hundred reasons, all mundane. Regardless, the seeds of The Peace of Kings was born here in the fertile soil left by The Gehenna of Rome and The Book of Nod.

                                                •Europe rebuilt itself and so did the kindred. They were proud and eternal and thus determined to rise again. So they did. The dark ages witnessed the Courts of Love, the Voivodate, the Black Fiefs, Mithran Britain, The Constantinian Dream, and Ashirra; amongst numerous others. The kindred rose again and they did so whispering in the ears of kings, bishops, and all other great men and women they could. As they grew in power and control, corruption grew rampant and the world became a chessboard for the petty conflicts of the Jyhad which used kings and kingdoms as its pieces. This War of Princes plunged the World into ever deepening spirals of misery, trapped childer within the cruel chains of their elders, and made mortals slave to nocturnal overlords. Perhaps if the Noddists had not been so preoccupied with mourning Rome they would have seen the signs of what was to come...

                                                •All the proud trappings with which they had rebuilt the splendor of Rome erupted into flame and crumbled into ashes with the Inquisition. The Cainites had pressed too much upon the kine, kings and peasants alike refused to live in fear. The Lasombra were discovered within the heart of The Church and faith in her cracked in ways that shattered Europe even further and drove Her stewards to adopt the Inquisition and redeem themselves in the flames of zeal. Childer who had been pressed into slavery and lorded over by tyrannic elders erupted into revolt as Anarch Revolts shook Europe. The Doom of the Salubri, The Fall of Constantinople, the Black Death. Europe heaved and the War of Princes imploded upon itself as Europe blazed. Only as the fires began to fade could those who remained come together in shaken horror. To make a pact so that this may never come again. Some chose the veil of secrecy, the society of their fellows and its protections; they authored the Camarilla. Some others chose the threat of terror, the society of free monsters; they authored the Sabbat. Some walked the paths they always had. Regardless though, whether they called it The First Tradition or The Silence of the Blood; all who survived heeded the Masquerade...

                                                •In the debates that forged The Masquerade, another pact was made. A pact made as Noddists observed the trends of the Second City, Carthage, and Rome repeated over again. Debates that were theological and secular, practical and traditional. From these debates, another pact would be made. The Peace of Kings.

                                                Never more would kindred rule openly. Never more would they rule the kine with an iron fist and fangs bared. Perhaps Kings truly were empowered by Divine Right and protected by The One Above, perhaps they were simply unwilling to suffer abuse like they had in the War of Princes all over again. Regardless the pact was made. From those nights on, kindred would no longer control, only influence. No longer direct, only sway. No longer rule as kings, only whisper as princes.

                                                The Sabbat would reject The Peace but in time they would be forced to accept it out of necessity, out of the coalition that rose up to defend the kings and out of the fact that kings and queens now knew how to reject them. Not until they were forgotten could they try again to rule as once before and by then it would be too late. These new kings would have new protectors and the cost of assailing them would be too high. Those who loathed The Peace would bide their day. Whether tomorrow or at Gehenna, they say, it will fail and they will rule again. Perhaps.

                                                Yet, The Peace of Kings remains. Vampires do not whisper to The Pope. Vampires do not dictate to Prime Ministers. Vampires do not command Presidents. Now... perhaps they bribe politicians, perhaps they seduce congressman, perhaps they blackmail generals... yet only to influence. Never to control.

                                                That is simply what noble monsters do...
