        Originally posted by Denstoffy View Post

        Could you share some of the (cool) things you did and why and how you did them? Always interested in stories regarding this and I imagine quite a few other people.
        The character concept was very specific and therefore likely to not be applicable or relevant to all, or most, campaigns. The ST knew I'd have a hard row to hoe, so allowed me a key merit to the concept: Unholy Aura. It's basically anti-True Faith, and has more or less inverse, ongoing, role play and mechanical prerequisites. So, considering that merit, the character was basically an Inquisitor hunter.

        Remember, disenfranchised crusader. That character hated the Church, and all would-be tyrants and manipulators. Lasombra in particular held the character's ire, being as they are a crossover of both worlds.

        That was a short-lived TC game that made it to the Dracula stuff before it disbanded, due to time constraints for the ST, and general dislike of the Dracula stories without a clear idea how to cut the content and still make it work. In the 1413 story there's a particularly nasty fight with an Inquisitor, that ended up being a complete non-starter when that Inquisitor's faith backlashed against him when used against my character. The Inquisitor's faith broke, and my character embraced him on the spot; the character wasn't about to let him live, so he staked the Inquisitor with his eyes open, crucified him against the east-facing wall of the church, covered him in pitch, and stacked a good amount of wood beneath and around him, also covered in pitch.

        So, my character just shattered the Inquisitor's faith. Then, he embraced the inquisitor, forever damning his soul. Then, when the sun rose and the Inquisitor caught fire, he'd meet final death in Rotschreck-aggravated agony, and the fire would catch, spread, and burn down the church.

        That story, the PC's are basically screwed no matter what. Nova Arpad's involved, and the best they can hope for is not to piss her off, and that's if they managed to achieve every last goal set before them and with flying colors (an almost impossible task). My character...well, not only did he actively sabotage Nova's plans, he quite obviously and deliberately broke the Silence of the Blood several times over in her own backyard, then proceeded to straight fucking murder an Inquisitor and burn down a church, practically guaranteeing job security for my character for the foreseen future.

        So, needless to say the seventh-generation Ventrue elder was pissed. To which my character told her, to her face, to fuck off because she had neither the clout nor leverage to make good on her threats, she was well out of time and goodwill with the Tremere and Ventrue, and she'd be lucky to survive the decade with no allies and no favors amidst an Anarch revolt. I'd been using Daimonion on her ever since the first story, so my character and a nice, neat little arsenal of self-doubt, uncertainty, mental trauma, and fear to nail her with Fear the Void and Psychomachia. Torpored her on the spot.

        Her staked body made a nice little decoration for my character's new domain, at least until my character ransomed it off for formal recognition of my character's praxis from the Tremere and the establishment of a chantry in his domain. Filled of course, with Tremere that were totally Tremere and not at all Baali infiltrators, because Baali blood is for some reason indistinguishable from Tremere blood to all but the most practiced blood sorcerers.

        The ST was laughing his ass off, because he knew I was biding my time and chomping at the bit to pull something, but he didn't expect me to go from zero to nuclear at the drop of a hat and with zero hesitation. The other players...they'd all been my friends for years at the time, and we'd gamed plenty together in non-VtM games, but they were horrified out of character.

                Originally posted by Denstoffy View Post
                Greetings,

                Is it doable to have a Baali (or an infernalist) player character in a group when everybody is on board?
                Id argue you will have a much better experience when only the storyteller and the player who is a baali, know about it and the others dont. You can still make your questions about taboo topics, but i would not tell other players that one is a Baali. If they know beforehand their characters will likely "immediately" work it out at the first opportunity, they will somehow be aware that they are being corrupted and if the Baali is one day revealed as such it wont have any real impact on the players.
                It also limits the experience for the Baali player, cause he doesnt have to be nearly as careful hiding his true identity and likely wont even bother trying to corrupt the others.

                Only way i see it work well if everyone knows is, if the entire group plays as Baali

                1) id recommend my ultimate baali summary thread for seeing options. "The Baali" as such doesnt exist and some like Molochim are way way better PC material than Nergali

                2) You are the ST? You can limit them if they threaten your game. Demons may not want the Baali to have the investments after all.

                3) Depends. For Playing a Baali clan book Baali is enough. Heck dark ages core rule book is enough. if you want all the lore, theres over a dozen books including werewolf ones mentioning baali and giving some background lore...which i hope have in said thread about baali summary to make it easier

                I also did a youtube series (sanguine archives) on the Baali from an IC perspective if you dont like reading ^^

                As general advice - depends as which clan you want to pose as. A true Molochim Baali will play 95% of his game as a legit character of the clan their character poses as. if you are a Baali infiltrating Clan Toreador, youll be playing a Toreador and be indistinguishable from a Toreador in RP. The devil is, literally, in the detail. Heck as Toreador Antitribu you might not even have to conceal much at all
                ...and for fairness, Nergali are playing chaotic stupid, which is sadly why many groups dont give Baali a chance. You can play Baali horriiiiiible. And the people playing them well are few and far between.

                Its Daimonion. Daimoinon was a typo that somehow caught on. But its a combination of a sweet snack and vegetable. Daim-onion. Welcome to a ruined discipline by dad joke

                Technicly you can make pacts with DTF demons i was on a crossover server where that worked very well for my Baali character. You dont have to read DTF for playing a Baali but, i would encourage it just you are aware of the different demon houses and factions cause your character surely would be aware of why its a bad idea to summon a reconciler or ravener.

                To Molochim and greater good....dont be fooled by that. Molochim want to prevent hell on earth yes...but because they want to rule the world and ruling the world isnt as nice when the world is fire and ashes. If you want to play a "good" Baali - Order of inner Light. Those are the only Baali that try to be good and redeem themselves in the eyes of god

                If you want to play "a baali" i found a good way to explain it is...play a Tremere with a sip of Setite and a hint of Tzimisce.

                For a tip how to lay a Baali well i recommend Ander Woods (Woodwwad) video on Nephandi. A Baali isnt going to tell you, he is whatever he needs to be to further his goals.

                                                    Demons don't add only servitude, they add for the Thrall another dangerous element: the Unknowable Purpose. This is an important piece of horror, where you have the constant uncertainty about the ultimate goal of your service.

                                                    The Unknowable is different from the Unknown. You may not know the ultimate goal of your sire, prince, bishop or even Ante. But at least in principle we work with the idea that this goal is knowable, understandable and limited in some scope, even if it may be irrational, abhorrent and ambitious.

                                                    But demons seem to operate in another level even for Methuselah. They act and scheme in the highest scope possible, their goals can be only be grasped on legends. You know not what you're serving or to what end it uses you.

                                                    And that's scary and give the impression of ultimate evil, even if it is actually just asking for lemonade because it is tasty.

                                                            Originally posted by Denstoffy View Post
                                                            Greetings,

                                                            Is it doable to have a Baali (or an infernalist) player character in a group when everybody is on board?
                                                            We have at our disposal clanbook - baali (black dog), all the V20 books and from storyteller vault the 2 books related to baali.
                                                            We also have path of screams, book of madness and devil's due from old lines to look into. (have been playing for well over 2 decades)
                                                            1. Are there any tips and tricks to make this work? (except of course the part about don't go into details which should remain unnamed and we have easy stops if it should not work out,...) I'm asking cause this is the first time we are going to play the "bad guys", if there is something like good guys in the world of darkness....
                                                            2. Is there any way to make demonic investments works because as they have no XP component, they scale pretty harshly?
                                                            3. Are there any other books we might need?

                                                            Thank you very much for any and all advice even if it's, don't do it.
                                                            Didn't read the rest of the thread. Here are my two cents.

                                                            From personal experience, there's not a whole lot that an infernalist can do that "normal" PCs won't in a regular game. PC vampires will regularly kill people for petty reasons, torture suspects to get information, steal, engage in corruption, and do a whole lot of heinous shit. So draw the line not with the characters, but with the players. Human sacrifices most certainly won't cause trouble with your group. Raping babies would. Negotiate where the "we don't do this ever" line is, and don't cross it. Just talk to people about it. Talking is good.

                                                            Forget about the existing rules for investments. Talk to your infernalist player about what he wants to do as an infernalist, and what he wants to get out of being an infernalist. E.g., I like infernalists a whole lot because I like demons as a concept. My end goal in playing an infernalist is to interact with demons and do demon-y things. Getting an NPC to sell their soul - that's a reward in of itself!
                                                            Once you've learned what the player wants to get, you can tailor the rewards the group gets without heaping extras on top. Maybe everyone gets a personalized reward from the story, and the infernalist's are the investments from their patron. Or the infernalist trades part of their soul (in form of xp). Or something else. IMHO, infernal boons as part of the story reward are easier to balance than infernal boons as a separate abuseable mechanic.
                                                            Books can be used for inspiration for the boons I guess.

                                                            Discuss with the non-infernalist players what is keeping the group together. The allure of being a secret infernalist is strong, but IMHO, not worth it. It just means you spend less time actually playing an infernalist because all the time in the party is spent pretending that you aren't one. And at least the players definitely should know.
