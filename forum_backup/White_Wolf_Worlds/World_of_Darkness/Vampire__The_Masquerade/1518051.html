      His sire was Pierre l'Imbecile according Clanbook: Malkavian (Revised Edition) p.99 and Transylvania Chronicles 3: Ill Omens p.74, he was originally a 10th Generation Vampire
      Pierre l'Imbecile was and still is a a 9th Generation Malkavian (actice, or at least not reported to be dead) as per Transylvania Chronicles 3 and Transylvania by Night
      Sadly we don't know who Embraced Pierre, we only know he Embraced Anatole in 1193 in Paris where Anatole born 1173 under a blue moon, and he was a a pious vampire.
      As for Anatole, in Transylvania Chronicles IV: The Dragon Ascendant, on p. 88, on his character sheet he was stated as 6th Generation after in Transylvania Chronicles 3: Ill Omens he commited Diablerie
      He became the Voice of Kupala after - as per Transylvania by Night, p. 79-80 - a 6th generation Malkavian, the previous Voice of Kupala allowed Anatole to diablerize him, making Anatole keeper of the mysteries and the harbinger of Gehenna, and passed him to mantle
      Octavio himself was originally 7th Generation, Embraced by a Roman Malkavian, Marcus, but Octavio's ties to the dark soil or the Carpathians forced him to commit several vile acts, culminating in the staking and diablerie of his sire, becoming 6th
      So it is 100% certain Anatole was a 10th Generation Malkavian and eded up as a 6th Generation one where he currently is
      He had a Child tho, an unnamed one, Anatole mentioned that he had lost a childe to the Chosen of Calomena (Chosen of Calomena was a cult of clanless Caitiff in medieval Constantinople, that sought to the destroy vampires. Led by Stanislav, they were originally from the Balkans and were seen as mad prophets. They thought themselves the champions of the fabled Calomena, the sister of Caine. - they were mentioned in Constantinople by Night)

      So the Line is Malkav (3) - ? (4) - ? (5) - ? (6) - ? (7) - ? (8) - Pierre l'Imbecile (9) - Anatole (originally 10, now 6 after the Diableire of Octavio)

                          Anatole's a compulsive diablerist, so any individual childe of his could theoretically be anywhere from 11th to 7th generation. He was embraced 10th and ate his way up to 6th. It's also important to note, this would be an exception to the "age loosely correlates to generation" rule -- younger childer of his would be lower generation thanks to elder vampires' tendency to trip and fall into his tummy. So basically any generation at which a PC could start works for a vampire of Anatole's lineage, especially given Malkavians' tendency toward broken lineages making the matter easier to adjudicate.

                          He's also a Dominate Malkavian, so any of his lineage would be likely to have their discipline spread adjusted accordingly. Not a big deal in v5 if that's what you're playing, as Dementation was rolled into Dominate as amalgam powers, but still.
