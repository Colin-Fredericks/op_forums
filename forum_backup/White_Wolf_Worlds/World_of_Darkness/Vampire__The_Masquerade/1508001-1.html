                                                    Christof Romuald​ was Embraced by Ecaterina the Wise, in Prague during the Dark Ages. She was originally an eighth generation vampire, but committed diablerie in the late 20th century.
                                                    (Redemption 's opening cinematic suggests that Christof was Embraced in 1141 CE. The game's timeline presents several inconsistencies with both in-setting and real-world history -- such as Christof's own sire, Ecaterina, not being Embraced until 1150. The 1194 date listed in VTM: Beckett's Jyhad Diary is probably the more accurate.)
                                                    So he was/is of 9th Gen (possibly 8th if diablerizing the Setite Lucretia)

                                                            Yes, it's hard to be sure about anything regarding Cristof, because he came from a PC game and in the game many things are changed/different from the established lore of the WoD. The date of his embrace is just one among these many of these discrepancies, another being Ecaterina's embrace, also the fact that she seems way too powerful to have been embraced only some decades before embracing Cristof (not to mention her telekinesis shenanigans, that always made me and my friends, kids at the time we played the game, spend hours wondering what she did), the Methuselah in Prague in the game is known as Vukodlak, but is known as Shaagra in vampire books and is a "she" (even though gender in vampire is something definitely not that relevant, ESPECIALLY for a Tzimisce who could change completely in a blink of an eye). Not to forget the fact you face Etrius in his own chantry and not only isn't destroyed, but also give the Tremere Councillor quite a beating lol.

                                                            To sum up, all in all the game differs a lot from the books, so it's hard to say what is "true" there and in this case I'd normally prefer to take the information found in Becket's Diary.
