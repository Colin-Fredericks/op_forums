      I don't really like any Clan over the rest as they're all great.

      The only exceptions to this is that I don't like Baali, because I don't think they fit my understanding of the Vampire setting as being a dark grey one and I think that the Giovanni makes for a better Necromancer Clan than the Cappadocians.

      The reason as to why I think the Giovanni are better than the Cappadocians is that I find that mafia necromancers gives me more ideas on them as opposed to recclusive scholars.

      One can argue that Giovanni concept steps on the toes of the Ventrue concept but the same is true between the Cappadocians and the Tremere.

      But I'm not saying there's something wrong with the Cappadocians as I like them too, but I find the Giovanni to give me more ideas and the mafia necromancer concept is more interesting than the reclusive scholar/necromancer/mage concept.

      I should disclose that I find organized crime to be a pretty interesting topic and while I'm haven't seen or read extensively on the topic there are lots of ideas there. Sopranos + necromancy makes for a cooler concept with more popular cultural influences and ideas than the Cappadocians, in my opinion.

This development allowed the creature to ultimately leave its grave and begin a new life as a human.                                       My three favorite clans have always been the Followers of Set, the Cappadocians and the Tzimisce.

                                      The Followers of Set have a lot of really interesting lore around them. The idea that Set was originally a 4th generation vampire who diablarizred one of the third generation to create his own clan is kind of cool. Then you have the idea that there are somewhat periodic purges, and hinted ideas at a war between Typhon and Set, etc. Plus the whole Conan/Egyptian vibe is pretty cool. The only thing I wish they had played a bit more with was the idea of religious conflict within the clan. There should be inquisitions and schisms and such. Instead the clan was generally always presented as to being on the same page, which always bugged me a bit. The split/rebranding into the Ministry in 5th edition is definitely my favorite thing to come out of the new edition and it's something that I include in every other VtM game I run.

                                      For the Cappadocians I've always thought Necromancy was pretty cool, and it opens up a lot of cool actions and idea via the Wraith game. The Cappadocians, as a nearly extinct clan in the modern nights, always felt a bit cooler to me than the Giovanni. Plus, in lore, there were a lot of issues with the Giovanni (they could only have 3 vampires sired each generation, they didn't really have anything to do with any of the other factions, etc) which tended to make them a bit harder to play or use.

                                      Finally for the Tzimisce, they're just the classic concept of a noble vampire, with the addition to Vissicitude, which is just about the coolest power in the game. Lore wise they also have the Path of Metamorphosis, which inhabits some really cool conceptual space where a vampire seeks to embrace and transcend their inhumanity to become something alien.
