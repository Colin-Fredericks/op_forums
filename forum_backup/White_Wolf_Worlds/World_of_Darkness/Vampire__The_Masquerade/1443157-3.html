                                                    The thing with Batman is that in most incarnations his attributes are something stupid like... 5/5/5, 5/5/5, 5/5/5. I heard he uses a version of Venom (bane's wonder-steroid) which makes that somewhat more possible but you simply can't be nine fives without getting magic or hypertech involved. Honestly I find the idea of an all-natural batman less realistic than the comedian from the 5th dimension that sometimes deigns to plague superman. Furthermore a number of Batman's signature gadgets aren't feasible with todays tech; the grappling hook gun and the gliding cape being big offenders, but also I don't think you could really do batterangs either. (also a lot of Batman's enemies make extensive use of knockout gas, which isn't a thing afaik)

                                                    Giving out lots of low level disciplines, or getting generous with mage $enhancements is really the way to make more reasonable characters. Especially because at 5/5/5 physicals... that really isn't enough for a few hours of high intensity activity while carrying a belt full of heavy tools while also accounting for all the injuries you're going to pile up. You've also got the issue of Batman being tall and built like a body builder, which might be great in a brawl, but acrobatic feats aren't kind on big and heavy dudes; there's a reason why olympic gymnasts are tiny, it gives them an advantage. On the otherhand with younger robins... high-skill, relatively small size; without relying on a dangerous weapon, a semi-competent man just needs to engage in a grapple to win. Both have a need for Potence.
