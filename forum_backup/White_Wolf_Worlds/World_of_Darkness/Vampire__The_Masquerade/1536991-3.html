                                        Without reading this entire thread, I disagree with its entire premise. The issue wasn't with the Sabbat. It was with its players, myself included. I remember way back when in 1852 when they first came out, my and my game group gleefully got together every Friday night with a case of beer, and a head full of 20 yr old visions of horror. These days, the kids would call it "cringe". We all sat there and "roleplayed" our cringiest gross-out fantasies and drunkenly tried to make our friends cry. And as stupid as it was, it was fun. These days, we've become more sophisticated, nuanced, mature. The times have changed as well. Could you imagine the hubbub when a stream shows players giggling while they imagine kick-fucking babies down main street with my edgy Tzimisc-ombra with my trenchcoat with a cock fleshcrafted to my forehead cause I want my enemies to see exactly how hard all this dumb shit got me. As we grew older we wanted to relive the fun...not necessarily the cringe, but the fun.

                                        So the older more "mature" me looks at the Sabbat and sees true horror. religious extremists. Those who will stop at nothing to get your attention and make you see "God" My way is the only way all other ideas are heresy, and no matter what you say or do join or die. "Caine akbar! ""Caine vult!" - pick your poison for your game, the devil came be whatever you want it to be, but whatever it is, it is "god" and its worth killing everyone for; and worth dying for. Humans are cattle and Camarilla are pretty much the same- "Heathens", the blind.

                                        With this line of thinking, it makes the Anarchs necessary. Anarchs are bad...malcontents, rebels, but they at least kinda share our morality. Not like the monsters, the religious zealots that are the sabbat. This also means the sabbat are not mindless monsters. They are worse. They are calculating monsters. They are "the enemy" they hate our values, our very state of being and will stop at nothing to destroy us and our ways.

                                        Look at the real world. The middle east hates the western world, our core values, way of life etc. The extremists, though at a disadvantage continue to resist. Violence comes easy. Why? easy. Their values are different than ours. We see conflict and shake our fists seeing dead children, screaming about injustice. But, thats through our eyes and our morals. We weren't raised with their line of thinking. One could argue that the West is wrong, trying to push our values on others, or vice versa. Here's our paths of enlightenment. Different clans, "tribes" seeing things differently but in line with one central thought. Caine is right. The Antediluvians WILL kill us all. This isn't up for debate. This is scripture. And if you deny or question it, you are helping. Its you or me bud and I won't have your toxic ideals influencing my childer. I'll kill you first. I'll kill everyone first (I know this comes off as very anti muslim, but thats not the intent. In fact, the Christians did the exact same thing over the Crusades I'd argue its human nature and the nature of the radical faithful)

                                        My older, more mature self see's this and gets excited to play. Wants to have the heated debates with my friends within the confines of the game. To challenge and be challenged. To try to wrap my mind around ideals that are foreign to me. To be the "monster" more than your standard blood drinking licks. But I can also understand a business like Paradox, looking at this and saying, "after all the heat we've gotten there is no way in hell can we condone this. BUT there is huge demand for it so lets put it out there as the 'enemy'. People will be smart enough to figure it out...besides...think of the cost of the page count!" In the era of streaming, companies have to be super careful and sacrifice elements to avoid heat. And god knows vtm has brought enough to Paradox...

                                        Currently running: Gary/Chicago By Night
