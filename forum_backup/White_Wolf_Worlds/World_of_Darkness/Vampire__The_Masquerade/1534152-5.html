    My chronicle's a continuation/adaptation/mix-and-match of older chronicles' timelines (with a new group). In that timeline, Louisville was seized by the Camarilla during the Civil War, waging a war alongside a loose coalition of independent and autarch elders aligned with the Southern Lords, cheekily named in retrospect the "Antebelluvians" by locals. The Ventrue led the war effort as Dux Bellorum of all domains south of the Ohio river, and she made Louisville her own little social experiment, only granting veteran Kindred domain and station (and only allowing those who had experienced war the right to stay) in the city. Think the UCF from Starship Troopers (the novel, not the film), but as a Camarilla city.

    The Ventrue is Nova Arpad's childe and Bulscu's protege, embraced in 1457 after the Siege/Miracle of Belgrade*, and she was a crusader-turned-Archon until her appointment as Dux Bellorum. Needless to say, to call her the Camarilla's 800-pound gorilla in the region may be an understatement...to the Camarilla's chagrin, as she rolls hard on the "Lawful Good+, D&D Paladin" archetype. To the point she managed to broker a treaty with the Catholic Church and Society of Leopold, for Louisville's Camarilla to police its own for low-Humanity, rogue, and hostile vampires.

    [* From my Transylvania Chronicles game. The Miracle of Belgrade happened during the peak of the battle in 1456, vampires defending the city who followed the Roads of Humanity, Kings, and Heaven found their curse of Caine lifted for a single day, able to continue fighting at full strength without getting burned by the Sun. This Ventrue was a peasant girl and a camp follower of John of Capistrano's peasant-crusaders, who ended up fighting alongside Bulscu and saving him from an Assamite's sneak attack. She impressed Bulscu, who in my TC game rose from torpor and went full-on "good guy", who arranged to have her embraced by Nova as reward.]

    The Nagaraja was a nurse in WWI, would have died from the Spanish flu but got embraced, and went back to Europe for a second round in WWII by dominating her way into the nurse corps. The ancilla Brujah was a Soviet commissar (he was Primogen until the SI hit Louisville, at which point he defected to Anarchs believing the SI hit to be an inside job), the Toreador was a Spanish republican turned French resistance who seduced Nazi officers for intelligence. The Gangrel's brother fought in Vietnam, the Tremere's mortal boyfriend died in Vietnam, and the Malkavian served a tour in Vietnam; all three went onto protest Vietnam. The Nosferatu and Caitiff are newer entrants to the city (under the Nagaraja, not the Ventrue), but the Nosferatu got involved in the Mexican Dirty War.

    That much military expertise and combat experience made Louisville one tough motherfucker for the Sabbat, who besieged the city multiple times but never came close to taking it. The best the Sabbat (and surviving Antebelluvians) could do was guerilla warfare type shit, leading to the 2008 Kentucky Derby Masquerade breach I've mentioned in other threads. That's what put Louisville on the SI's radar, eventually leading to the hit. The SI in Louisville is comprised of Protestants and military, rather than Catholics and intelligence agencies. The hit was an inside job, but by the former Malkavian Primogen; he had a premonition the only way to save Louisville in the long run was to let the SI clean house, and leaked the location of Elysium to them.

    So, you have a half dozen ancillae (and old neonates) who were all hardened by war in life, and went on to become sect war veterans, up against nearly a dozen Anarchs only three of whom have any significant age.

            I'm of the mind that Nosferatu would always be the largest population of any clan for the following reasons.

            1 - Information brokers, means they'd need to keep tabs on as many areas as possible.

            2 - Nobody comes to visit unless they desperately need something. Which means the clan is probably able to get away with illegal embraces a lot easier.

            ------------------------

            After them, it would probably be a toss up between Brujah and Toreador for second most numerous. They represent the most common archetypal tropes for modern vampires, after all. Plus, their tendency to embrace on whims of passion.

            ------------------------

            Coming in third would be the Ventrue, because somebody needs to make sure the city doesn't fall to chaos. Unfortunately, there's only so many authoritative positions to go around, and no self respecting Ventrue would be satisfied working as someone else's subordinate.


            -----------------------

            For least populated, it would be the Gangrel, Tremere and Caitiff.


            Gangrel, because it is within their nature to stay on the move and typically avoid industrialized society.

            Tremere, because who the hell is practicing hermetic blood magic in this day and age?

            Caitiff, because the ones who were abandoned by their sires are probably executed as illegal embraces.

                        Some thoughts that occurred to me with regard to this post:-

                        - It makes sense that Nosferatu would be able to evade scrutiny to a greater degree than most Clans, but I would argue that their preference for going undetected strongly suggest that they would work to keep their number of Embraced down: it might be wiser to depict Clan Nosferatu as generally well-represented not because they make
                        more vampires, but because more of the ones they make survive.


                        - I’d argue that the Brujah and the Toreador epitomising the modern vampiric archetypes strongly suggests that they’re the most visible vampires, which to my mind argues against their being amongst the most numerous (The entire point of The Masquerade is that vampires who get spotted all too often wind up a greasy stain: the less discrete a Clan, the less numerous).

                        Although you’re dead right about their embrace rate being quite high (It might well have to be, in order to keep numbers up despite the two Clans having dangerous tendencies towards Vampire Romance).


                        - No arguments over Clan Ventrue: it’s not hard to imagine that a Clan dealing with a Curse that makes blood difficult to come by without careful resource management would be especially meticulous when it comes to keeping the pool of rivals with insider knowledge small, carefully-managed and firmly in your corner.


                        - One wrinkle regarding the Gangrel is that their very mobility makes it easier for them to get away with quasi-legal or otherwise dubious Embraces: being able to pull recruits from unusual places might also make it easier for them to avoid competition with other Clans.

                            My Chronicle is set in Brooklyn, NY, which has a little over a hundred Kindred (The Five Boroughs total about 400). I don't design every single one (YIKES), but I have a rough idea of how many are affiliated where.

                            The Camarilla is 38 strong - 4 Banu Haqim, 1 Brujah, 2 Lasombra, 5 Malkavians, 6 Nosferatu, 7 Toreador, 4 Tremere, and 9 Ventrue

                            The Anarchs have 50 members across a number of gangs, some may be recognizable from New York by Night and The Crimson Gutter: The Night Forum (9 Brujah), The Thrill Kill Gang (4 Caitiff, 2 Gangrel, 2 Thin-Bloods), The Midnighters (2 Lasombra, 1 Malkavian, 1 Toreador), The East Flatbush Dominions (5 Ministry), Number One Heartthrob (2 Lasombra, 1 Malkavian, 1 Nosferatu, 1 Toreador, 2 Tzimisce), The Day Trippers (3 Thin-Bloods), The Carna Island Chantry (4 Tremere), The Night Shift (My PCs--2 Gangrel, 1 Hecata, 1 Nosferatu), and unaffiliated Anarchs (1 Brujah, 2 Caitiff, 1 Ministry, 1 Nosferatu, 1 Ventrue)

                            There are 4 Hecata, 3 Banu-Haqim loyal to Ul-Shulgi, and many unaffiliated Kindred (1 Brujah, 8 Caitiff, 1 Malkavian, 3 Nosferatu, 2 Ravnos, 1 Salubri, 1 Thin-Blood, 2 Tzimisce)

                            In order, it's 14 Caitiff, 12 Brujah, 12 Nosferatu, 11 Ministry, 10 Ventrue, 9 Toreador, 8 Malkavians, 8 Tremere, 7 Banu Haqim, 6 Lasombra, 6 Thin-Bloods, 5 Hecata, 4 Gangrel, 4 Tzimisce, 2 Ravnos, and 1 Salubri. The demographics of the other boroughs are very different, with the Camarilla clans overrepresented in places like Manhattan and Staten Island, while The Bronx is even more Anarch-coded than Brooklyn. Queens, well, I don't actually have a strong vision for Queens, although I'd imagine it's a patchwork like Brooklyn.

                                                            For GURPS VtM is literally GURPS Vampire: the Masquerade, plus a Companion. There's also GURPS Werewolf and GURPS Mage, but only corebooks.

                                                            This city of yours is definitely an interesting concept, but also an ecological nightmare. Not saying you shouldn't do it, rather that you should add the consequences to the fun.

                                                            Setting the Masquerade aside, a vampire need 1 BP a day just to exist, that's 30 a month. 1 BP is also 1 of damage to the victim and depletion of iron. Blood is replenished in less than 24hs, but iron reserves take about a month. There's a reason it's advisable to not donate blood more than two or three times a year.

                                                            If we assume a minimum 3 months cycle to be sure everyone remains healthy and bleeding, a vampire would need to reliably cycle through 90 mortals just for basic needs. Every extra BP uses is another mortal out of the cycle for 90 days.

                                                            Add accidents, causes for those people to lose blood elsewhere, donations, and vampires that take more than 1 BP per vessel (which is common), and this city is under extreme stress if things aren't very tightly regulated.

                                                            Tourism adds a buffer, but be wary of that. The areas that attract them are probably disputed heavily, as it's rare for them to wander literally all around the place. They're also tricky targets, easy to disappear with if you can place a trap, but high profile if you're not cautious. One less boarding the airplane is nothing, a gruesome death of a visitor in a touristic place is serious problems.

                                                            A vampire that can control a few venues where hunting is easier and impose rules can amass quite some influence with it, providing safe feeding in an otherwise severely compromised area. And the Prince has every reason to keep a tight leash on tourist feeding outside such venues to avoid problems that can drive away future visitors.

                                                            Remember: inhabitants just live there and have little choice. Tourists can watch the news and just go somewhere else. This isn't uncommon.

                                                            Kelowna is also relatively safe. It's one of the highest crime rates in Canada, but mostly as property crimes and drug traffic. Domestic violence is somewhat high, but people going missing, murdered or something are things that will call attention.
