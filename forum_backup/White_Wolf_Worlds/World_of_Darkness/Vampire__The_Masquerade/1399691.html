    Shalim

    06-28-2020, 07:35 PM
    So... in Chicago by Night there's the descriprion of this character, Rabbi Michalis Basaras. He's a childer of Giangaleazzo of Milan who apparently met a shadow creature calling itself "the Traveller" (it also has another name given in Cults of the Blood Gods) who told him of "Shalim" claiming it was the progenitor of all Lasombras and meant to bring everything into the Abyss. Michalis joined the cult of Shalim and is working to bring oblivion to reality. The passage also mentions that Giangaleazzo may have been part of some conspiracy of sorts and he was just waiting to betray the Sabbat until something triggered the event.

    So here's my questions:

    -Who or what do we think "the Traveller" really is?
    -Could "Shalim" really be Lasombra itself after spending a few centuries into the Abyss?
    -What is Giangaleazzo exactly up to in your opinion?

                                                  Shalim:
                                                  • Grandmother from Orpheus. She would be the most important figure in Oblivion if she exists, but I'm not sure if the origins match up or how she'd be connected to Kindred
                                                  • An unknown progenitor of kindred, including Caine, whose actions are attributed to God in Noddist myth. This barely answers question, but I like it anyway.
                                                  • The Lasombra antideluvian. I suppose if a kindred can master the void of universe, this could work, but I'd prefer something more primordial.

                                                  Apolleon the Traveler: He's actually called an "ancient Lasombra" in Cults of the Blood Gods, which narrows it down. Lasombra himself is unlikely to be called a Lasombra, but it's possible. He spends most of his time on the seabed, so he probably isn't well known. I'd rather see Zarathustra being his best self in Antioch.

                                                  Giangaleazzo: Nothing to do with Shalim I imagine, but I should think that he's interested in his clanmates joining him in the Camarilla, whether he likes it or not. If nothing else, it could draw vengeful attention from the Sabbat away from him.
