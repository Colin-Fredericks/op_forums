                                      In the chronicle setting I ST for vampire, I state that all the clan names as we know them from the books are conventions dating from the 17-18th centuries when they became standardized for the sake of convenience. Every clan has always been known by a variety of "names".

                                      The "Followers of Set" or "Setites" is simply the most popular name used by neonates and such of the Camarilla and Anarchs, but that is considered somewhat vulgar (though acceptable). However, the "official" name is the Rouquin. Just like Gangrel means wanderer, and Ventrue means pot-bellied, Rouquin means red-headed which I think is appropriate for the Setites in multiple ways. But hardly anybody uses that name (certainly not the Setites) except for formal occasions by the Camarilla - though those Setites who are "members" of the Camarilla publicly use that term.

                                      I've never come up with an alternate name for the Giovanni, and since I don't follow V5 metaplot at all I completely ignore the Hecata.

                                                      In my chronicle setting, some Setites are attempting to rebrand themselves as Clan Mekhet. It's a nod to the clan's Egyptian origins but carries none of the religious connotations. Some of the Setites pushing the new name are younger and less religious, and just want to ditch the Set label, while some older and more devout serpents cynically think the rebranding is a good way to lower other Kindred's guards. "Hey, we're Clan X, just like you are. Forget all the worships-a-god-of-evil stuff." It isn't a universal thing. Some Setites like the Ministry (mostly religious ones who didn't worship Set, and clanmates making overtures towards them), while some old schoolers just want to keep being the Followers of Set.

                                                      I've long renamed the Giovanni as the Sangiovanni in my games. Since, you know, it's an actual Italian name. Another alternative I've considered using is Giovannini (also a real Italian surname).

                                                      Blood and Bourbon, my New Orleans-based Vampire chronicle.
