            TL;dr version

            * Talk a lot about how Humanity is the pillar of V5, and V5 being a 'return to basics' for Vampire.
            * Talk a lot about what the development thought process was and why doing this as an antagonist book.
            * Book is written from the standpoint of using the Sabbat as SPC antagonists and talk about why
            * Book can be used for PCs if you want and all rules are there, but isn't written as a PC book
            -- Quote from the stream: Justin Achilli: “I’m sure there are players who are going to play Sabbat once they got the rule book. I have no doubt about it. Everything is there if you absolutely want to do it!”
            * Talk about some Discipline powers, like one that lets you yank your vitae out of a ghoul from anywhere in the world, and one that leaves shovelheads as sleeping time bombs in a domain. Heavy talk about how the things that are done are Stain worthy, BUUUUUUT...
            * Talk about whole packs being on Paths, and concepts of Paths. Mechanics seem to just be 'the SPCs can ignore humanity and Stains entirely.'
            * Talk about Antitribu and 'things that distance you from your Antediluvian'.
            * Justin wants to have a preview document with art, a path example and some antitribu thoughts at the Elysium thing in June. No confirmation though as nothing is specifically nailed down for it.

                            Originally posted by elmerg View Post


                            I'll be interested to see if Paths are anything but a named handwave though, based on the description and outstars continued comments though.
                            The impression I get is that it won't be so much a handwave as a monster manual label for pack types. Want to tell a there-but-for-the-grace-of-god story about the players becoming too Ayn Randian business/politics ruthless? Plug in a Power/Inner Voice Pack antagonists. Want to tell a story about the players becoming too feral? Plug in a Feral Heart pack antagonists. Want to tell a story about the players becoming too ruthless in their enforcement of Camarilla rules? Plug in a Honorable Accord pack antagonists.

                            That's why they keep talking about Paths in the context of entire Packs following them. In a Sabbat player game, different players will be on different paths and part of the fun is the internal conflict between pack members.

                            In a Cammy game, the Sabbat are there to be the clearly themed There-But-For-The-Grace-Of-God-Go-I role. So mono-path packs caricaturing your Ventrue's obsession with power and order, or your Gangrel's animalistic behavior.

                            Your players are debating whether to kill that Duskborn because he is violating the masquerade by keeping a family who know his secret? Throw an Honorable Accord pack who kill their own families to protect Sabbat Secrets at them.

                            They are there for the ST to wag his finger at the Cammy players and say, "if you keep going down this path, you'll end up like those guys. Don't end up like those guys. Keep your humanity. Forgive that minor masquerade breech from a Duskborn keeping his family. Interact with humans instead of prowling the forest in search of prey. Don't be like those guys!:

                            So, yeah, mono-path-packs designed to be plugged into finger-wagging lessons from the ST.

                                                I didn't get chance to watch the interview yet, but I wouldn't break out the champagne just yet, CTPhipps.

                                                We still don't have the full picture of what the V5 Sabbat are going to be like, but it looks like they were truly devolved into pure monsters.                                                 With the Sabbat turned more and more into creatures that no longer even share a language with other vampires, we can expect them to be not only completely alien and monstrous beyond any previous depiction - we can expect that all they're capable of is to provide punching bags for Camarilla and Anarch characters to fight against, with zero other options (you can't talk to them).
