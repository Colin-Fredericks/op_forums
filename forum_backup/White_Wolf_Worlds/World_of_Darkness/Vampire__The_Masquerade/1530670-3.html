    Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

    Yeah, no.

    https://www.kickstarter.com/projects...ts-jyhad-diary

    The Kickstarter for it was made in 2016. V5 was created in 2018. BJD is from a place of love for the lore by Richard Thomas while V5 rebooted a massive chunk of the setting. Also, V20 had been moving away from metaplot agnoticism for awhile. Which was good because metaplot is awesome.

    I don't think we're going to agree on this, though.
    Yeah, we're not going to agree on that and just politely agree to disagree.

    As far as I'm concerned, metaplot is awful and it ruined the setting, it was a tool for certain nameless devs to police the setting and take away elements they thought were "badwrongfun" and the fact that some of the more toxic elements of WoD fandom hold onto metaplot and the whole "badwrongfun" rhetoric as holy canon doesn't help either.

    As for your assessment of BJD, all I can say is I feel you are wrong. V20 only started moving away from metaplot agnosticism near the very end (not counting the BNS LARP stuff) and it was part of a move towards what was essentially proto-V5.

    This was in preparation for an aborted "V4" from Onyx Path that resumed the metaplot, and that was like a proto-V5. There was also a lot of smug and pretentious semantics arguments about how V20 wasn't "really" a fourth edition because it was metaplot agnostic.

    There were quite a few V20 fans who were pissed at this announced "V4" and a shift away from metaplot agnosticism. Then Paradox swooped in and used this Onyx Path V4 as the springboard for what would become V5. Parawolf also did the only good decisions in their entire history of owning the WoD IP, they created the Storytellers Vault and also officially recognized V20 as the fourth edition of Vampire and threw away Onyx Path's semantics games regarding V20.

    BJD was essentially a V5 book in V20 trade dress, even if it didn't realize it fully. The proposed Onyx Path V4 was merely the dress rehearsal for V5, the Chainmail to V5's White Box D&D.
