                                                            Well the thing is, the lead up to the Family Reunion saw a rebellion within the Giovanni as some grew fed up with the stranglehold on power that the Clan Elders had as well as their abuses of said and/or Augustus's endless promises of the Endless Night with little to show for. And then there was the start of a rumor that the Promise is set to expire soon, meaning their neutrality with the Camarilla will end, it's not hard to see why they would become resentful and bitter towards Augustus and the Elder. Add in some of these younger Giovanni, either fed up with their Elders or seeking new avenues of necromantic power to gain more recognition and power, reaching out to the Bloodlines of Death aka the 'taboo' Bloodlines that their Elders ordered them not to have anything to do with. Which led to both sides realizing that they've got shared animosity towards the Giovanni Elders and that by working together they can all get something they want out of it.

                                                            In a lot of ways, the Giovanni was the Camarilla in mini but worse due to the utter control the Giovanni Elders had over everything within the Clan. It's unsurprising that they would eventually face their own mini-Anarch Revolt.

                                                            But unlike the Anarchs, the rebel Giovanni didn't actually want to completely tear down the power structures of their Elders, they wanted to take them over which is ultimately what happened. The old Giovanni Elders have all either died or realized what was happened and submitted to the new regime, relinquishing their direct political power in the Clan for their unlives. Well at least those that did engage in the Clan politics as some of the Elders that focused instead on Necromancy were spared and allowed to continue their scholarly pursuits.

                                                            As a result of all this and the reorganization into the Hecata, three of their families are now independent of the Giovanni while the others with the power vacuum created by all the carnage has allowed many to rise in power within the Giovanni. So many aren't really looking to leave the Giovanni just as they've gained power though I don't doubt some families still within the Giovanni are looking at the possibility of achieving the Bloodline status that three of their fellow families managed. And at the same time, the Giovanni Clan long fostered a shared loyalty to the Clan. Sure, you may plot against your rival Giovanni and advancing your own power but you don't betray the Family.

                                                            In another World of Darkness, they could potentially spiral apart into myriad groups but V5 is a world with the Second Inquisition hanging over every vampire's head, a world where the largely static world of the Kindred has many sudden and often violent changes, and in a world where the Giovanni-Camarilla neutrality could expire in less than a decade. This is not the time to go off on your own. This is a time for coming together to present a strong, shared front against the many threats out there.

                                                            And really that does seem to be a growing thing in V5, a consolidation of groups in the face of a changing world. Any thought that the Anarchs are not a Sect in their own right has been tossed out the window, along with any pretense of Cam-Anarch peace. Two of the four Independents have joined up with the Sects while the third has consolidated itself with the Bloodlines of similar ability and lineage into something that's a cross between a Clan and a Sect. While the remaining Independent Clan... well we don't really know what the future of the Ravnos will be just yet though frankly not looking good for them which is a shame but that's a whole other matter. The Sabbat are seemingly being pushed back to their original NPC boogeyman role and I wouldn't be surprised if like the Lasombra, the Tzimisce see a sizable defection to one of the other Sects.

                                                            As I commented on in my previous post, one of the things hammered in repeatedly within the Hecata chapter of CotBG is that while the Bloodlines of the Hecata have their grievances with each other, they can no longer be overtly hostile to one another. Even the Harbingers acknowledge that such things can no longer continue as they had. Hell in their section of the Chapter they admit that killing Giovanni is no longer a means to gain status among their kind and that one of their own elders has recently proposal to help discourage such murders.

                                                            The Cults are an interesting bunch and could be potentially useful in chronicles, whether as antagonists, NPCs or even as groups the PCs are apart of.                                                             In fact both the Hecata and the Cults are stuff I would readily port over to older editions of VtM.
