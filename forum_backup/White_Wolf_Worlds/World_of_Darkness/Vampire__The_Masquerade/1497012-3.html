                    V5's Sabbat is a really poor attempt to copy what Onyx Path did with Belial's Brood.

                    Wow, another 'V5 is Requiem' complaint. Yes, it's the main thing I dislike about it. Buyback to BB.

                    Belial's Brood were intentionally introduced as a throwback to 1e Sabbat, satanic vampires who revel in being evil. They were also the easy bad guy because acting like that is, to put it simply, stupid and so most members of the Brood were going to be low BP Neonates. Then the writers decided to add depth and they became the 2e Sabbat, more philosophical than alien but. With that mixture of legitimacy and hipocracy. You could actually play a full Brood chronicle, and it could have just as much variety as one starring the core Covenants.

                    Then Requiem 2e came out, and the Brood weren't there. They were just gone because the focus was elsewhere. Even Secrets of the Covenants didn't say anything. But when they did appear the new version was awesome, removing the hipocracy, flipping them from being doomed to eventual Draugrhood to being Draugr who had developed minds, and just bleed awesome. They made me really want to run a Belial's Brood chronicle, and ideally get a full book on this new version. They might have lost some stuff, but they gained for everything they lost.

                    V5 Sabbat meanwhile just lost. Lost their space in the core, lost some of their traits to the Anarchs, lost their interesting alien members. It would be as if the Brood had returned as satanic anarchists who tend to die young.

                    Blue is sarcasm.

                    If I suggestion I make contradicts in-setting metaphysics please ignore me, I probably brought in scientific ideas.

                                                Vampires need mortals to survive: you cannot totally distance you from them, even when justifying evil choices.

                                                That those Vampires who thought themselves a different and superior species were deluded, was already implied when they canonized that Paths were a bastard version of Roads, aimed at providing cohesion to the Sabbat.

                                                Making it so central and evident was bound to be controversial, but if one is willing to engage with the system, the Convinction-Tenets-Touchstone trio still gives you huge flexibility.

                                                As for how dark were, and how much you were supposed to be a killer, fluff implied you would have snapped and killed: perhaps rules didn't support it as much, but I do distinctly remember Clanbook Toreador Revised speaking of the fact that eventually you would awake with pieces of liver on your face.

                                                Going to the Sabbat topic: complaints here are well more grounded. I like the general idea behind the new Sabbat (medieval death cult was always my fav), the book is good for providing Antagonist ideas and using them in your Chronicle, but not engaging with the past plot seeds of the implied Sabbat fall, I feel it was definitely a weakness.

                                                Working more on it would have justified more the rise of the Anarchs, and provided a more organic evolution towards the new metaplot.
