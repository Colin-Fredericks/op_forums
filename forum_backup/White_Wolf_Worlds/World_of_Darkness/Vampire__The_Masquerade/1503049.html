                  I don't think there is a hard canonical answer on that distinction. It is something every ST and gaming group will determine on their own. We know that most vampires feeding on someone, and their victims being fed upon, is incredibly ecstatic (ignoring the exceptions like the Grip of the Damned and such). But the actual issue of sex is more nebulous.

                  This is what I hold in my own chronicles: Vampires are corpses. Corpses do not have the biological functions or impulses of living humans. Sex would mean nothing to vampires physically. However, mentally their mind - still composed of their memories, habits, and emotional connections - still retains its beliefs from when they were arrive. This gradually declines over time. So a neonate would still react towards people who would have aroused or interested them while alive, but an elder has lost much of that and increasingly views humans like humans view livestock. Eventually the reality of being a corpse that preys on the living eliminates much of the residue personality and reactions from the time when they were alive. This does not mean that even an ancient, monstrous vampire could not somehow be driven to actual sexual passion - it is just very unlikely and should have a story behind it.

                  Of course, Humanity - and sometimes other Paths - also has a role in this. A vampire who retains high Humanity, even if old, will likely retain more of their mortal mind, while even a young vampire whose Humanity is low will see people only as vessels of blood.

                  I believe as long as a vampire is at Humanity 7 or above, they appear human. Their face appears flushed, they even appear to breathe, and I would also hold that if they wanted certain parts of their anatomy to work then it could do so. So actual physical sex is possible. At Humanity 6 and below, that is not the case. Corpses don't get aroused, self-lubricate, etc. The other thing is that vampires can spend blood to appear more human/alive. So even low Humanity vampires could simulate sex if "needed".

                  But at the end of the day, both vampires and their human victims would vastly prefer the act of the vampire feeding than any kind of sex act. Why would you willingly choose something that is less pleasurable and requires harder work to accomplish (and for a vampire isn't "natural")? Most things involving sex is more a habit for the new vampire than anything else, and it'll erode as the vampire becomes more used to being a vampire than thinking themselves as a human with special dietary needs.

                  Different STs, different gaming groups, different chronicles will approach the matter differently, and that is fine. But the above is how I handle it.

                              It's one of those maddening/weirdly personal things that touches on people's morals so it changes from author to author and storyteller to storyteller. On the human side of things most can't knock romance off the table if we want characters to be relatable, and romance for most people is affirmed by sex. sex is also common in vampire media as vampires are used to discuss taboo things
                              and cassanova type vampires aren't supposed to be a weird minority and it doesn't make sense for them to have difficulties when they're doing it once a night. But on the otherhand you're a predatory magic walking corpse and your groin doesn't have any value beyond decoration and potentially excretion (there are at least a few references to beastial gangrel peeing to mark their territory. But you are also consuming a lot of blood which doesn't exit the body in most cases where it's used now that I think of it.)

                              I personally can't imagine vampires not having sex, especially paths like Cathari/Sin on the table, and I feel like abusing your vampire powers to have wild orgies or unreasonably beautiful servants as a status symbol is something that at least an antagonist should be doing. That said, I understand why some wouldn't want your players doing that. Things like the blood bond also change from person to person. As some and I describe it, 3 points is better than real love, even if you sometimes get a little unhealthily obsessive, it'll bring you genuine joy and fulfilment which is quite absent in a lot of lives ; while others insist it's a false love and a gross violation of free will and thus expect only the worst from it.

                              Sex aside, I do think Vampires are going to be attracted to certain people. As I run it, vampires gain attributes by feeding from people with superior qualities. Someone with a PHD from an elite school or an Olympian is going to be extremely "nutritious", if a bit high profile to go after regularly. Idiot Savants and people with extreme physiological biases are the secret joys of vampire herds. but in general more attractive people correlate to having better physical and mental capabilities and are better for regular feedings (and also they make the vampire more attractive over time) This drives the disparity between vampires further, because the established vampires have great feeding advantages over the vampires feeding from the inept who fall through the cracks.
