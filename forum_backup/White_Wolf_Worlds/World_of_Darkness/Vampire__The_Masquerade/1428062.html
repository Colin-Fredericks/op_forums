    Tremere Path of Flames for V5

    12-11-2020, 03:04 AM
    Hello everybody! <3

    I've been working on a modification/adaptation for the Path of Flames for a while now and this is it! I tried to adapt the concept to V5 but also make it a more fleshed out discipline of its own.


    Quick summary:

    - Completely reworked and fully adapted discipline for V5.

    - Two new mechanics: Mastery and Ignite.
    While Ignite is more of an extra and not an integral part of the discipline, Mastery is very much so. I created the Mastery-System in order to balance the quite potent powers of the discipline, giving it a unique character and playstyle. It is a high risk - high reward playstyle, that maybe saves your coterie in the last moment - or dooms it for good. Either way, it's going to be flashy

    - 14 new powers

    - 6 new rituals


    One last thing: I'd really appriciate it, if you shared with me your experience playing it. I see this as an ongoing project and would really like to keep working on it. It would especially interest me, how the Mastery-System worked for you and what your experience using it was. This is version 1.0 and it will need some balancing down the way. You can find the contact information in the preface of the document.


    Thanks a lot and have fun playing with the Path of Flames, if you decide to do so :>


    Comboi <3

                              Thanks Ethryo and your players for the critique! Every point is noted and added to the other responses

                              A few responses and questions to your players points:

                              The Mstery roll slowed down the game a little, not that much. But one extra roll - sometimes every round - was a little minus.
                              That the system slows down the game is an interesting point and is noted. A question I have: did you think the Mastery-Roll was a good way to balance the powers? Did it affect your Willpower pool in a meaningful way?

                              Prometheus Presence had a little too long range.
                              Hephaestus' arrows has too long range.
                              * Aodh's volley has too long range
                              Yep, I got that response pretty often this is going to be fixed in the next version.

                              Too many prerequisites - You have to choose from start to go either fireball lobbiing way or seer way. No other discipline has so many prereqs
                              So I thought about that quite some time. First I only had the prerequisits between the range and close-combat abilities, so you can't mix them and get some kind of invincible firelord. I chose to expand that to the seer-abilities too, but I can see myself removing the prerequisits here, because it doesn't affect combat that much and those are more utility abilities anyway. It will depend on the further responses I get, but right now I think this will be different in the next version.

                              Promethus' Insight says that "the caster is also more aware of fires and a bit more resistant to falling into Rötschreck" But this is not supported in the powers description.
                              You are right, there is a sentence missing in the system description. It should be the same as "A Sense For Flames".


                              I hope you guys had fun playing with the discipline <3
