                                        In terms of this discussion, I’m defaulting to Mage, as it has the most extensive breakdown of the cosmology (WtA is second, but it mostly deals with the Middle Umbra and only has limited contact with the High or Low or Deep Umbra)...

                                        - Cthuhlu and it’s associated Elder Gods are absolutely a Deep Umbra entities. Alien geometries and similar madness that the rational mind can’t even fathom can only exist beyond the Far Horizon because reality simply can’t support them (sufficiently high madness Marauders drop out of reality and end up here). It’s an endless black void outside creation wherein things that cannot be dwell (it is also sometimes called the Outer Darkness).

                                        -There is an Abyss in the Middle Umbra; the result of some cataclysm and is know to the wolves as a place where things dropped into its bottomless pit are lost forever. A group of Wraiths who were blown out of the Low Umbra by the 6th Maelstrom and wound up here; unable to return to the underworld they made a home along one of the paths that winds along the walls of this Abyss. No one knows what lies at the bottom because it appears to be truly bottomless.

                                        - The DTF Abyss exists in a layer beneath the Underworld/Low Umbra. Given the revelations in Orpheus about the nature of Oblivion, it’s unlikely that this Abyss is actually IN Oblivion, but rather that Oblivion was used by God as an impassable barrier to keep this Abyss separate from the rest of creation. It is also noted that no human soul is capable of entering this Abyss; it was created solely to imprison the demons for eternity.

                                        - The Neverborn of the Low Umbra have never entered the Abyss, they reside in Labyrinth surrounding Oblivion; similarly, the Labyrinths of the Nephandi are not in the Low Umbra, but are reflections of the true Labyrinth in the Middle Umbra (many of the entities worshipped by the Nephandi were actually just the dream projections of sleeping Neverborn). Neither the Malfeans (neverborn or triatic) nor the Nephandi have any affliation with the Demons of DtF.

                                        So, other than the aforementioned ghost legion that wound up in the Middle Umbra due to the 6th Maelstrom, none of the Abysses in WoD cosmology are associated with undead spirits; making the merger of the Abyss with what is merely the Low Umbra a bit tenuous at best.

                                        My hunch is that the decision was made to merge these for V5 (even though its going to make things weird if they ever get around to a Mage5) for the same reason they merged Quietus into Blood Sorcery and split aspects of Serpentis up into Protean and Presence... the V5 developers have expressed a desire to eliminate Clan exclusive disciplines in this edition and by combining Obtenebration and Necromancy using the tenuous excuse that the Demon Abyss and Low Umbra of Wraiths are spiritually adjacent (even if they don’t actually interact any more than humans watching a zoo aquarium interact with the fish) they’ve essentially made the gestalt discipline non-exclusive because two clans have it as a discipline.

                                        Regardless of the spin some are trying to put on it, it IS a retcon, just like Thin-bloods got retconned to not having real disciplines, just Thin-Blood Alchemy and being able to take merits that allow them to actually walk in daylight without taking damage (whereas previously 14th gen took damage normally and 15-16th took lethal with no option to take none) and being able to transition back to mortal with some effort.

                                        But those retcons are pretty typical whenever a setting desides to change its mechanics. Some fantasy settings will try to include cataclysmic events that result in the setting now following the new rules (see every major upheaval in the Forgotten Realms setting corresponding to an Edition change).

                                        Others just pretend it’s always been this way and the easiest way to square it in this case is to employ the “Worlds of Darkness” model outlined in some of the Revised Mage books that basically said “each game takes place in its own universe where some things are the same and others are different; echoes are felt in nearby worlds and sometimes these worlds even touch and allow crossovers to happen.

                                        In other words, VtM1-3e took place in one set of universes (that ended with Gehenna), V20 is a collection of universes parallel to that where the metaplot never happened, and V5 is in another set of universes with a few more differences, but still close enough you’d call them part of the overall cluster (with CoD being on the distant edge of the WoD cluster with a few similarities still, but even more differences).
