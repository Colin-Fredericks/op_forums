                                    My first impressions of the Companion: as a Tzimisce lover I'm happy to see my favorite Clan coming back to being playable options in fifth edition. I feel like there was a lot more to them than this book's iteration of Tzimisce, but I like the lore and what they did with them in V5.

                                    I have mixed feelings about Vicissitude. I feel like it was mostly an extremely harsh nerf, the penalties for using Vicissitude now are incredibly punishing and incredibly restricting, to the point where I just don't want to use it as a player. The Weapons use of Vicissitude, for example, is basically just Feral Weapons except worse in every way, shape and form and also costs you a literal arm and a leg.
                                    I think that representing Vicissitude alterations through Merits (the points to which you get from the roll successes) would have been much more preferable, for the most part.

                                    Reducing your Attribute points is horrendous. With Experience costs sky-high, people are forced to min-max and specialize. A Vampire character in V5 isn't likely to see many Attribute increases.

                                    Furthermore, this cost makes no sense considering the devs then ALSO limit the number of alterations and even go so far as to limit the Weapons alteration to Single change per subject. Why limit and punish the player further?

                                    Also, Tzimisce can't harvest other people's bodies for "materials"?
                                    I don't see why Vicissitude needed to be the Discipline to get smacked with nerf-sticks while Dominate and Presence are things that exist, and are pretty much mental Vicissitude...

                                    I also don't like that Fleshcrafting is a Dominate Amalgem. Feels very restricting. What if I wanted my Tzimisce to build towards Animalism, instead?
                                    Very limiting.

                                    But, I haven't played V5 yet, so I can't judge it yet.
                                    I'm very excited to play Tzimisce if I do get a chance to play. Maybe one day we'll be able to play Sabbat again, as well.
