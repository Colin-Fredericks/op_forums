            This would work well for Backgrounds like Allies, Alternate Identity, Contacts, Domain, Fame, Herd, Influence, Mentor, Resources, Retainers....
            Yes! Retainers!

            Like so:

            RETAINER POINTS:

            * 7 points, 1 Retainer max
            ** +10 points (17 total), 2 Retainers max
            *** +12 points, 3 retainers max
            **** +21 points, 5 retainers max
            ***** +28 points, 8 retainers max
            ****** +36 points, 12 retainers max
            ******* +46 points (160 points total), 16 retainers max.

            RETAINER PACKAGES: (Purchase in order for each Retainer, multiple purchases are possible for increased price as shown (X/Y/Z))

            1. Basic Retainer Package: 1 RP
            You now have 1 more retainer. Your retainer has a Willpower of 2, a Humanity of 8, and 23 dots in attributes total (they do not get the free initial dot).
            NOTE: I run a 12-attribute game. 23 dots isn't as much as it sounds like.
            · Only two attributes can be at 3;
            · No attribute can be over 3.
            Select any five abilities and put one dot in each of those five abilities. Then select one ability with no dots and put two dots in it.

            2. Attribute Package: 3/5/7 RP
            · Add 2 dots in attributes;
            · No attribute can be set over 4 with this.

            3. Willpower Boost: 4/4 RP
            · Add 1 Willpower point.

            4. Gifted Retainer: 4/5/7 RP
            · Add a single dot to an attribute; that attribute may be set up to 4.

            5. Combat Package: 3/5/7 RP
            · Add 2 dots in combat skills;
            · No ability may be set over 3

            6. Gifted Combatant: 4/7/12 RP
            · Give your retainer a 4th dot in any one combat skill;
            · Requires that the ability be already at 3.

            7. Supreme Combatant: 16 RP
            · Give your retainer a 5th dot in a combat skill;
            · Requires that the ability be already at 4.

            8. General Abilities: 2/4/6/8 RP
            · Add 4 dots in non-combat abilities
            · No ability may be set over 3.

            9. Specialty Package: 3 RP
            · Pick two related non-combat abilities (i.e., Drive/Repair, Empathy/Animal Ken, Occult/Linguistics, Computer/Finance, Law/Finance, etc.) and give the retainer two dots in each of them.
            · This package may not raise an ability above 4.

            10. Specialization: 2/4/6/8 RP
            · Add a 4th or 5th dot to a non-combat ability.

            11. Ghoul Package: Free!
            · Add a dot of Potence (and Fortitude if Gangrel);
            · -1 Willpower
            · Prepare to have to feed your retainer on a regular basis.

            12. Experienced Ghoul: 6 RP
            · Requires Ghoul Package
            · +1 Vampire Lore and +1 Tremere Lore if the vampire is a Tremere.
            · +1 Willpower and -1 Humanity
            · You may spend 12 xp on the ghoul

            13. Treasured Servant: 16 RP
            · Requires: Experienced Ghoul Package
            · +1 Potence (and +1 Fortitude if Gangrel)
            · +1 Vampire Lore
            · +1 Willpower and -1 Humanity
            · The ghoul will have Status equal to your status minus two
            · You may spend more 15 xp on the ghoul.
            · Your ghoul receives 1/2 as much XP as you do during the course of play.
            · Your ghoul will have a personality and must be RPed; he/she may be a pain in the ass sometimes.

                Alternatively, a Tremere or Nosferatu with max retainers as a hacking group is frightening both because of the damage they can cause and because having the Retainers spread across the world means they are very difficult to target or counter.

                        I run help like thus:

                        Herd/Mob/Cult/Gang/Cult or whatever will have at most 4 dice if you want to use them for anything. It's more likely they'll have less.

                        A 1 dot retainer will give you somebody with 5- 6 dice in a narrow role (fighting, finance, criminal doings etc) or 4 dice in many things. A 2 dot retainer will give you around 6 dice in many things or 7-8 dice in something specific. A three dot retainer could give you someone with 9/10 dice in something specific and 5-6 dice in a few other areas, or it could give you a 2 dot retainer with an added supernatural ability (a mortal thaumaturge or necromancer most likely)


                        You can use resources to acquire help. Their pay is roughly determined by the level of their desired skill (some fields may differ more)
                        1 dot- 3-4 dice
                        2 dot- 5-6 dice
                        3 dot- 7-8 dice
                        4 dot- 9+ dice (they'll likely have fame too)

                        You can calculate how many people you can pay by multiplying each dot of resources by the last: 1,2,6,24, 120 and so on.

                        Though it may seem Resources is just a better investment than retainers at character creation (at higher levels at least) Retainers bought at character creation have several advantages over help acquired through resources or obtained later
                        -They're accustomed to the peculiarities of your schedule and mannerisms.
                        -They don't have other commitments, and will break the law if you desire it.
                        -They're relatively stable if you've blood bound them.
                        -They're loyal, even if you don't blood bond them (though they will need some compensation; money, status, sex, you keep their cancer riddled child alive.. etc )
                        -If you've picked an animal retainer, they start intelligent.

                                I rewrited most of backgrounds for my game. for resource i gave exact numbering for money(including cash, bank and assists) with money being rather low till 3 points of background which was the limit at character creation. i also did limit most of other backgrounds but not necessarily at 3.
