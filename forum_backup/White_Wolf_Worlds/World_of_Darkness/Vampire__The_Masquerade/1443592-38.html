        If getting kindred to cooperate across sect, clan, age and generation (or even if they share these things) is like herding cats, getting all these human organisations across the globe to cooperate is asking for a fucking miracle.                         Ok, slightly less sleep deprived and having thought on it.

                        VtM wants to explore the themes of privacy invasion, your personal information being available to those who know how to look (and how that can make it harder to hide that you're undead) in an era when companies are
                        installing their creepy, annoying voiced wiretaps in your house and blah. I accept it makes sense to explore that.

                        There were various ways that could've played out that would impact vampiric society, and what V5 did is not the way I would've done it. I would have built on the path of technomancy (whatever happened to that...) and the Anarchs' forays into tech and the web and subsequent evolution of vampire internet culture from V20's Rites of the Blood.
                        I wouldn't make the SI their prime concern. Worry more about what other vampires are doing with your information and how they'll hold it over your head. Are your secrets really safe? Really? Is the information you have faked? Can you tell? Fangbook scandal?.
                        This isn't a new worry, it's just gotten worse in recent years.

                        But ultimately, while I might not like the exact details of (or flat out scream "put that back!" at) the newest additions/changes to the setting, it offers me enough ideas and toys that I find interesting to play with that it's worth it to use V5 and edit it to my preferences. The masquerade finally slipping badly enough for humans to become vaguely aware on a global level that there's something sharing the night with them and occasionally start poking around is one of them. I know I'm cherry picking stuff, and I'm ok with engaging in suspension of disbelief if it's entertaining me.
                        Once I'm done moulding things to my will I'll probably end up with a V20-V5 hybrid of some sort. (V25?)
                        To be honest, that's kind of what I'm hoping for from a sixth edition.

                        Also V5's necromancy works fine for playing one of the Rosselini, since these guys love pushing the envelope on torture and pointless cruelty to the point where they're looked down on by a family that has branches who are slaving warlords, cannibals, mafiosi, and of course the infamous necromantic patrician family.
