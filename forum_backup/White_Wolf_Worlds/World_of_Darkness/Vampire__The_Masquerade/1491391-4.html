                        Also, I think it's an interesting counter to the Curse-Of-Ugly that the Nosferatu, Gargoyles and Samedi have.                             My 2 cents: old Lasombra weakness is too strong, but served 2 purposes: 1) identify them as Vampires <and> Lasombra; 2) make them obsess over their own image (paintings, ghouls that comb and groom them, etc.), because they can't do it perfectly by themselves.

                            I like the new "weird in mirrors and cameras", because it maintains both points whilst <not> being an automatic Masquerade Breach: they still can be identified <IF you know what you are looking for>, and they still <can't see their own image> perfectly (I like to imagine it embossed and/or distorted, so they can be recognized, but you can't see fine details - you couldn't draw him based on his reflection alone).

                            About the Tech roll, I understand it to be "tech savvy" to use different strategies, such as "did you know some smartphones accept input if you press this and that physical button, but only for such and such actions?". But I really think it shouldn't exist, I'd made it simply something on the lines of penalty in Technology rolls <as a factor of> Bane Level coupled with Tech acts "glitchy" near them if they fail their roll.
