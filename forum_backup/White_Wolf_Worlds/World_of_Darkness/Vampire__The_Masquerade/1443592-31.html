                    Originally posted by Vilenecromancer View Post

                    I'm asking out of genuine curiosity. Even without those factors, how can V20 tell more stories then V5 if we go by RAW? This isn't asked out of anger or anything, I just want to understand both sides here.
                    OK, so, I'm going to start a basic VtM story (yes, this is based on play experience), and then get to where V5's mechanics shoot it in the foot:

                    The Coterie of PCs have been working to expand their current small Domain, and having been eyeing a Domain controlled by the Brujah Primogen's childe. The Tremere PC's let the rest of the Coterie know that there's an place of occult importance in there they're willing to reward the PCs heavily for access to. It's got a good rack and an established Elysium that the PCs want to use to further grow their power.

                    The PCs have been trying to figure out what this Brujah's price is, but they just can't seem to figure out something to get him to budge. He's just stubbornly holding it despite having other areas under his control that would be plenty to keep him a big player in the city. Something is up. So the PC investigate. They actually end up missing that the Brujah's Touchstones are all in that area, because he's been hiding that secret tightly and that's exactly why he's not budging. However, instead they find out that there's Sabbat after that occult spot too, and the Brujah didn't seem to be doing anything about it.

                    They take a risk, and ignore his Domain to stop the Sabbat. In the process they find out that the Sabbat had 100% irrefutable proof that the Brujah is a diablerist without permission. Awesome, they've got the Brujah by the balls with some double blackmail. Now he's surely cave!

                    So the PCs send their best social type to do the blackmail....

                    In my not-V5 game, we blackmailed his dead ass... and then set him up to die anyway.

                    But this is where V5 gets directly in the way. Why? How good your blackmail material is doesn't matter. No bonus dice, no difficulty modifiers, no extra successes or whatever. It's a straight roll off between the Brujah and the best social PC, with +0 Willpower damage because its a one-on-one (after all, you don't blackmail someone in front of an audience because that ruins the point).

                    The results are going to come down to either the NPC losing the social conflict, taking the WP hit, and then counterattack the PCs on the grounds of Domain violations, or the NPC wins the social conflict and then the PC either has to fork over the blackmail material or taking the WP hit and... the NPC comes after them on the Domain violation since they already played their hand.

                    So, basically, you can't actually blackmail people in V5 unless they have extremely low WP, and you have a really big dice-pool over theirs at which point rolling was perfunctory anyway. Anyone worth rolling against turns the situation into a no-win.
