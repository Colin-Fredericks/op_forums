        Originally posted by Gryffon15 View Post
        Taking some inspiration from the last post for this one, some notable figures who have been caught within the cobweb and serve as vessels for an ancient unknowable madness.
        Awesome job!

        •Rasputin
        -Perhaps the most infamous kindred urban legend, the mad monk's story has maintained the most resilient persistence amongst the Moon Clan who occasionally claim to have sited the figure throughout the streets of eastern europe or preaching to street congregations or in 'private sermons'. Many skoff at the claims, yet the stories continue.
        I think the Rasputin of BJD is not the actual Rasputin but just one of his many imposters. Why? Because the real Rasputin is not so easily intimidated by the Drowned. The "real" Rasputin is a prophet of Gehenna very similar to Anatole in his role as well as actions. He is currently opposed to the Brujah Council reforming that has tried to kill him repeatedly. As expected, he has proven very hard to kill permanently.

        •Vasantasena
        -The Gehenna Prophetess and one of the most prominent figures in the creation of the Sabbat, Vasantasena has been a leading Sabbat Loyalist and Noddist for centuries, disgusted by the corruption and politicking rampant within the sect. She is known known as a leader of the Gehenna Crusade in the Middle East as she strives to lead the Sword of Caine against the most ancient vampires lying torpid there.
        I actually have Vasantasena as one of the few vampires who has achieved Golconda but this makes her MORE likely to fight than less. She's one of the individuals who understands what the Paths were for.

        •Bela
        -Bela is a strange guy. A known specter of Los Angeles, particularly old Hollywood, and a man who does bear a striking resemblance to the famed actor Bela Lugosi, his delusions as to being the infamous 'Count Dracula' have many kindred in stitches, particularly anarchs. Despite the violence and tensions of the West Coast Wars, Bela has surprisingly survived and gained a sort of cult celebrity amongst many young kindred across sect lines who follow his unlife with genuine delight and bemusement. He would surely have been destroyed long ago if it weren't for the fact he was generally harmless and that whoever killed him would suddenly become a villainous black hat for dozens if not hundreds of young vampires.
        I actually have the real Bela Lugosi unrecognizable as a vampire since he's an old grey-haired man. He is bitter about his transformation but uses Obfuscate to disguise himself as his classic self when inclined.

                From the wilds beyond and from the streets below, there are kindred who come stalking out and they are the beasts and outlanders, the survivor's brood.

                They are GANGREL...

                •Xaviar
                -The once-justicar of Clan Gangrel who led the clan in an exodus from the Camarilla after they had discovered slumbering beasts below and in 1999 Xaviar and those who saw him as a worthy leader left the Camarilla. The Camarilla elected a new justicar in 2011, but in the meantime and into the modern day Xaviar is recognized as a leader of the Independent Gangrel and the modern culture of Gangrel outside the sects, mixing modern outlander temperments and a fear of the legendary beasts below. Xaviar is rarely seen but those few who report seeing him carry stories of cthonic beasts below the surface.

                •Brynhild/Brunhilde
                -The leader of the Waelkyrige (Valkyries) and a descendant of the All High (Odin) who is known as a major Scandinavian warlord of Clan Gangrel leading the traditional viking faction of the clan in Scandinavia with a loose alliance with the regional Anarch Movement and carving their own territorial stretch throughout Scandinavia with colonies across the world amongst the Gangrel.

                •Art Morgan
                -A well known wandering Gangrel and supposed knight, Art Morgan is a source of comedy to those who know of his Quixotian claims to the title of 'Justicar'. Known to have vast friendly ties and to be a chivalric wanderer, Art Morgan was rumored to be considered for the potential new title of Justicar but while young kindred are fond of him, he wasn't considered politically or morally capable to be a clan justicar.

                •Karsh
                -One of the Gangrel who remained in the Camarilla after the Gangrel Exodus, Karsh was Imperator of the sect or leader of its warlords and Dux Bellorum. Known to be the sect's greatest war leader, rumors of his activity cross the globe, with the most common reports currently placing him either in Eastern Europe, Turkey, Southern Russia, or South America.

                •Jalan-Aajav
                -One of the Sabbat's leading warlords and a noted Black Hand Seraph, known to be operating in the Middle East as a part of the Gehenna Crusade while leading hunting and warring expeditions in the Middle East while his agents in Central America trying to maintain access and control despite growing instability due to the migration of warriors.

                •Beckett
                -One of the most significant and well-known Gangrel, an archaeologist and historian as well as a researcher of vampirism, Beckett is known amongst kindred of every sect for his discoveries and while the younger Camarilla members support his discoveries and past theories elders are less likely to be amused and some are driven into a fury. His writings and reports are often circulated by young kindred as one of the most valuable sources of vampiric information many kindred have.

                •Geoffrey Leigh
                -Once a well known archon and now the Justicar of Clan Gangrel as of 2011, Geoffrey Leigh now has the unfortunate responsibility of trying to draw the Outlander Gangrel back into the sect of the Camarilla. This has placed him in the unfortunate position as a formerly prominent archon of Victorian politics tasked with drawing several indigenous factions, the Scandinavian vikings, warrior cultures, and Xaviar's followers back to the Camarilla while many of
                them were drawn into the Sabbat or Anarch Movement. His results have been... varied.

                        Some more NPC ideas:

                        * Menele has felt the Beckoning and returned to Carthage to discover his sire Troile alive and well or at least her astral projection.                         * Moloch has successfully taken over the True Hand due to the fact the Aralu are not actually sleeping Antediluvians but they are his childer. The Underworld Enoch is actually the Baali's former city of Acheron. He has effectively rebuilt the Baali's former strength and the True Hand is now his vessel for seeking out infernal relics and working to put demons back to sleep. Moloch has also devoted himself to Troile.

                        * Speaking of the True Hand: The Harbingers, Nagaraja, and True Brujah all went "Nope. Buh byyeeee" when the Baali were accepted by them. This proved to be a wise decision.

                        * Shaitan awoke in Mexico City and proceeded to eat a large chunk of the Sabbat there before seemingly being killed by the Second Inquisition working with Imbued. Stories abound that it was Sullivan Dane wielding the Sword of Leopod. Sullivan is less than confidant that he killed Shaitan or that such a demonic creature can be killed but he killed something very old and powerful. The loss of so many Sabbat to the 4th gen's appetite is also why Mexico City fell to the Camarilla.

                        The Drowned alternatively claim Shaitan was always just Huītzilōpōchtli and was never a Baali or that they destroyed the imposter. If the former is true, the ancient may have rejoined them.

                        * Mictlantecuhtli, after destroying a bunch of diablerist Anarch idiots in 1992, has finally shrugged off his thirty-year-wake up. The Drowned Bloodline founder has since gone to rejoin his fellows and begin taking back Central America from the Sabbat. He's a bit bewildered by things like, uh, Christianity, let alone technology but is adapting nevertheless.

                        * Dracula finally killed Kupala permanently. As such, all Tzimisce no longer have the instinctive need to be complete assholes and Vicissitude is now much harder. He's not sure what affect this has had on the Eldest, though. who had apparently been feeding off the Archdemon for literally millennia. Dracula has since left the Inconnu and is now exploring the New World, looking for brides.

Effectively, she is now ambassador to the Camarilla from Africa's Kindred despite their failry loosely organized nature. The primary job she has is trying to convince them that they already HAVE a Kindred society, thank you very much, so stop acting like it doesn't exist or is unexplored territory.

                                        Al Capone faked a vampire of his bloodline being tossed in a oil barrel and dumped into Lake Michigan. Unwilling or unable to follow her, Christof entered a deep funk and ended up joining the Camarilla as an Archon due to its feudal familiarity.

                                                        Having since rejoined with Ecaterina the Wise, he's surprised her by actually helping her regain some of her long-lost humanity. The struggle between her centuries of Sabbat cruelty and Christof's earnest devotion to "good" (albeit, a Medieval French nobleman's idea) have worn down much of her defenses. She also finds him impossible to manipulate due to his straightforward blunt approach to...everything.

                                                        A romance might have sparked between his mentor and him if not for the fact that his heart remains heavy over Aneska and he's heard of her brutal deeds in the Gehenna Crusade. "The Red Nun" has devoured dozens of Camarilla Elders and several ancients supposedly.
