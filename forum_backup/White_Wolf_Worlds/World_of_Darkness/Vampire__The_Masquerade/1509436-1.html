    The CS would be more effective if it remained neutral and spanned all the sects. I think most of us can agree on that. But I think the OP is onto something, as others have spotted: localising things and having a plethora of smaller affiliations (cults, "businesses", etc) PCs can identify with is definitely something that makes sense and allows for more diverse (and more precarious) "urban sandboxes" in VTM. Especially because such affiliations can cross sects, allowing players to mix Camarilla, Anarch, Sabbat and independent lineages with greater ease, without sacrificing some level of antagonism and paranoia.

                I've taken a little time away from VTM, actually, so I haven't been giving it too much thought.

                Anyway, here are some rudimentary ideas I have about the CS:

                Like I said, it's a pseudo-sect, not a full one and even more individualistic than all the other sects, but there is a little spark of culture there. One idea I had was that the CS has a fight club. It's a place for employees to blow off steam and express their ephemeral moments of nihilism with each other after a hard day's work.

                I also see it as filled with opportunity. I think it would attract a lot of Sabbat defectors who have grown tired of an unlife in Caine's service. There'd also be a lot of scrappy, young Camarilla cubs looking to make a name in a space that the elders don't have a firm grip on, probably from parents who aren't too high up in the Tower; think of them as the lower middle class kids that have to get a job to support themselves. I see it as extremely technologically literate. I see it as a melting pot where desperate Ravnos rub shoulders with ambitious Tzimisce, almost like a micro-America. It's a new land of opportunity and it's all up for grabs.

                I envision it as very close to a criminal organization: robbery, assassination, trafficking, and hustling. It's vampirism with a ski mask on and not a drop of pretentiousness. This is business, but when you work hard, you also play hard.

                As far as infrastructure, I'm still working it out. I have Miami as the headquarters because in the corebook, iirc, they reference that the CS actually started in Miami. I think this fits well, with the port of Miami and just all the general hedonism and traffic that goes on there. It's a flashy place and an aesthetic backdrop for a new and chic organization.

                        Not really a "Sect" per se

                        The Circulatory System is an international human trafficking network that smuggles important vessels between domains and chronicles the powers of the blood vintages they move.
                        Vampires take advantage of the Circulatory System illegal services to obtain blood with a specific resonance from selected vessels. The Circulatory System is an exploitive network and cares little for the long-term well-being of their vessels, beyond the dip in profits the traffickers suffer when such kine die prematurely. Many Kindred can look past such minor issues to the pioneering studies the System makes possible by unlocking new and valuable features of the Blood.

                        It is a lore sheet thing, from Vampire: The Masquerade 5th Edition Corebook, p. 386 ​, representing a character with a connection to the Circulatory System (as a client, trafficker, or former mortal vessel turned vampire, etc)

                                                A vampire worth his salt should have nailed down a safe and sustainable stratagem for hunting, be it seducing horny club goers, feeding from the sleeping, house calls, or something of that nature. The occasional drunk mugging or homeless assault can be sustainable in some territories. If you can't make a good strat, you are a liability, you should die and your sire made a mistake embracing you. Vampires are a society entirely populated by people who hunt and the most valued thing politically is hunting grounds. Sure, they pretend not to be hunters, but you're not going to get any respect if you aren't self sufficient in getting human blood. Nobody should need it, and the people who need it shouldn't really have the capabilities to afford it. A Ventrue with very specific blood needs or a Tremere who "needs" a specific type of blood both has the support of their clan and should have the resources and acumen to arrange for their own procurement.

                                                The more people involved in a crime the more likely you'll get in trouble for it. Assault might be a more dangerous strat than the other options but the individual nature of the process keeps it miles better than a sophisticated international people-smuggling ring. There are just too many points of failure and too much reliance on others.

                                                And yeah, It's incredibly pretentious. You're making massive masquerade risks, risks against yourself, violating humans in one of the worst ways possible, and risking how many accomplices... for better tasting food. It's edgy bullshit.

                                                    So in my opinion, like the Inquisition, the Circulatory System should be a loose cooperation between disparate groups, not an actual organisation in and of itself.

                                                    As for clan makeup; If specific clans need to meddle in trafficking to get specific blood - particularly financially rich, socially structured clans with the requisite disciplines like the Ventrue and Tremere - they will have set up their own circles of kidnappers loyal to themselves.                                                     The Hecata need this least of any clan.
