But even if it is true, the fact that "V5 elders are weak" could be so many people's takeaway is a serious problem with the mechanics. Your mechanics are supposed to clearly illustrate how the world functions, not obfuscate it. They can be the best mechanics in the world and I'd still consider them bad game design if they lead to such prevalent misconceptions among the fandom.
                                            The short version is that you can pump up your stats a large amount, heal a lot more damage, and require far less effort when using Disciplines. Theoretically, this does eliminate most of the problems of Attributes over 5 if not Disciplines.

                                            Example:

                                            According to V5 rules, Helena has Blood Potency 7. This means that she can add 4 dice during a Blood Surge.

                                            Which means that she naturally has Strength 5 and Dexterity 9 but goes up to Strength 9, Dexterity 9, and so on. The big difference from other is you Blood Surge one Attribute Pool at a time versus one Attribute. So you improve ALL your Physicals, Socials, or whatnot with one use of it.

                                            If we still had appearance, she'd "normally" be Appearance 5 and naturally one of the most beautiful women on Earth (I use Angelina Jolie as a basis because why not) and yet would be Appearance 9. However, she can still up her Socials with a Blood Surge to the superhuman level she's famous for.

                                            The big difference is that supernatural potency is not "on" for Methuselahs like them at all times. They have to activate it.

                                            So V5 rules wise, Helena hasn't actually "lost" any of her Superhuman Attributes. Just Disciplines.
