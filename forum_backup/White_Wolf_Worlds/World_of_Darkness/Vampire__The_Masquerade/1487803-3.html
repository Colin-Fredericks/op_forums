    Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
    If you're a mage or a changeling, you can flat out go to Heaven and talk to your dead aunt and uncle....because you believe you can.
    That's actually not true. That's not exactly how Mage works and not how Changeling works at all.

    For Mage, while your beliefs shape a lot of things, it can't make and populate a new Realm in the Umbra out of your wishes. Mages explore the Umbra to look for those things, and they know human souls aren't going in bulk anywhere they can reach. You may reach realms that match your superficial image of Heaven, but won't hind your dead relatives there unless those are spirits fooling you, that's a different thing from your beliefs coming true and Mages know the distinction, they're Will-Workers, not Imbeciles.

    Changelings... have nothing to do with belief. Imagination, yes, not belief. And the Dream does not give you whatever you want. If there is a place that seems like Heaven, Changelings will still know it is just a piece of the Dreaming, fleeting as they all are, and inhabitants are dream entities, something they're relatively able to distinguish and most of the time are Chimera. But you can't go there just 'cause you believe or want to, and dead souls have no reason to go there any more than they have to go anywhere on the Dreaming.

    Added, Changelings do not know where fae souls go upon death if it's not going to reincarnate for some reason. They're not meant to die in the first place, but when they do, their destiny is anyone's guess. The Sidhe have a legend for their own souls that involve traveling to a strange, grey space that's not in the Dreaming, and may be in the Dark Umbra, but it is a confuse legend and don't cover deaths by Cold Iron as far as anyone can tell.

    Originally posted by Kakost View Post
    WE, the unkown Gods that play with the lives of the innocent creatures of the WoD for our amusement, we know that a Mage can go to the spirit world of dreams where the Chorist will find a bunch of winged humanoids singing songs to the glory of the lord because that's the creation of the Mage's mind.

    The poor critters of the WoD have no way of knowing any better thou.
    And that's the key point.

    OoC the setting has too many blanks and inconsistencies for us to give a definitive answer about anything. IC it gets infinitely worse, as the characters do not have our view of the whole. They have fragmented access to fragmented information whose sources they can and should question. In the end, it is really hard that any particular character will have access to information that breaks their worldview even more than becoming supernatural and being introduced to their own society already did. And even if they do, this info will always come in such a limited, biased way that they can interpret the bigger picture however they want.

                The point is that for some splats, Heaven and/or Hell absolutely exist. Whether those are objective truths or not, they're accepted as truths by those splats. Every Wan Kuei was once in Yomi and managed to escape. And for them, the denizens of Hell pose an existential threat to the world, so combating Hell is a major part of their storyline. For Werewolves, Heaven and Hell are just human myths, those places don't exist. "Gods" do exist, but they're just high Umbral spirits (and the Christian deity would be dismissed as the Patriarch, an aspect of the Weaver). But the Ancestors spirits of werewolves live in the Umbra and be communicated with, and if a werewolf lives their life correctly they can join them in death. For them, there's no doubt that an afterlife exists.

                For the Giovanni, I would suspect that a common belief is that anyone who dies simply gets sucked into Oblivion at the moment of death. Only ghosts with strong wills or ties to the living are able to resist the pull of Oblivion and continue existence in the Underworld. For them, believing in Heaven or Hell is simply a matter of faith no different from mortals. There's no evidence that either of those places exist and that good souls go to heaven when they die and bad souls go to hell.
