                I do think they may decide to either remove the Fera or make them unplayable, but not in the same way it was done with the Sabbat. Instead of presenting them as an antagonist faction, they would present them as dead or dying out after the events of the last two decades, whatever they be.

                Going for the antagonistic route is unlikely. As much as the Fera are presented as resentful from the Garou, they were never presented straight up as antagonists. There is a difference in narrative roles, and their presentations on books always focused more on playing them as their own game than fighting them. Indeed, it is the Garou that appear as antagonists in their books.

                But I'm not sure if they'll go this route either. They may very well just be very slow into churning out rules on Fera. One thing I think in this line is that the Fera don't actually detract so much from the themes of the game as the Sabbat seems to. The Fera still have the same goals, similar beliefs, and so on. They have different rules within the same outlook on the world, while the Sabbat is the same rules with opposite outlook.
