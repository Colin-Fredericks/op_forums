                        You are correct in that the books itself is very clearly written from an US standpoint.

                        However the counterpoint I can say is that the WoD is very clearly our world but ‘Darker’ and has been since the inception of its universe. The metaplot uses wod creatures as you have said as the main reason why certain human decisions or achievements have been done which is done globally. IMO that still doesn’t give it a pass on making cultural misunderstandings as when reading through old WoD, its very clear (aside from possibly DtF) that the creatures and beliefs are also shaped by the human society they have to hide behind. This in a sense requires a creature to mingle and understand the people they are pretending as or possibly what they believe in.

                        As such, ultimately when roleplaying these creatures we’re going to be picking up what our characters went through, how they perceive things and what cultural understandings they have been exposed to and base their viewpoint on.

                        The thing about VTM in lets say Europe is that even though its a ‘western’ culture, the fact that the errors portrayed does cause gripes in Europeans and thats why there are lots of fan sites who try to translate VTM into parts of their country to make it more accurate and also try to stop using their languages as exotic in its lexicon (an example in vtm is papillon and butterfly. Papillon is french for butterfly but in vtm context means red light district whilst the term butterfly in vtm means someone who mingles in high society. Does that mean French vampires swap these terms and say Butterfly in their vocab or suddenly english is the native default for vamps?)

                        Whilst I definitely don’t speak for everyone, from my experience, there are a decent portion of people who don’t go beyond the stereotype of who they characterise because they aren’t aware. If someone is aware but plays the stereotype ironically, then thats fair enough. However, too often you get a response of ‘wait, that isint normally how they talk/ think etc?’.

                        As such, some of the first exposures of different cultures could be through WoD. Some people will be interested and research deeper whilst a fair amount will take what is written at face value. In this case, better to have a more accurate understanding as your first basis over stereotypes. Building on your point, isint it better to have games that are fun but also insightful on other cultures and their mythology?

                                                Been doing some brainstorming for positions within the HG Courts. The names are subject to change as this early draft stuff.

                                                Ancestor: The Prince analogue. Nothing much that needs to be said that isn't already in the core book.

                                                Council of Preceptors: The Primogen analogue. Advisors to the Ancestor on all manner of things from political or spiritual. Made up of the eldest of the local HG. The number can vary between courts. The more orthodox courts will seat only those of the Major Paths while others will seat a member for every Path present in the court. Smaller courts will have less simply because they don't have enough Preceptors to sit on the council. Regardless it is seen as improper for their to be more than one Preceptor for each Path on a Council.

                                                In smaller courts the Councilors might also possess other positions within the court and in the case of the Warden and Instructor, it's preferred that the roles are handled by those Preceptor rank due to their importance.

                                                Grave Warden: Responsible for monitoring graveyards and local body dumping sites for HG taking their Second Breath. They virtually always have a coterie under them to assist them in capturing the Ravenous Devils that rise. Even if the HG are just beginning to establish themselves in a city, the Warden will have a coterie from another city assigned to them until the local population has reached a level where they can have their own.

                                                Devil Instructor: Responsible for breaking the Ravenous Devils from their mindless state and beginning their instruction in the Ways of the HG or destroying them if they are unable to regain their minds. In smaller courts this might be the same HG as the Warden though most prefer to keep the roles separate so they can maximize their efforts. The Instructor might also have a coterie under them (often made up of Devil-Tigers) but not always.

                                                Master of Ceremonies: The HG analogue to the Keeper of Elysium and filling a similar role though they will actual officiate ceremonies within the court, especially those courts of a more spiritual bent.

                                                The Guardian: Akin to the Sheriff and Scourge, tasked with maintaining and protecting the local HG territory as well as hunting and destroying wayward HG sentenced to death. Like the Warden they will have a dedicated coterie to assist them in their tasks.

                                                The Monitor: A position that only exists within cities with a Cainite population as their role is to monitor the local Cainite population. This can be done by mundane or magical means. Unlike the other positions, there can be multiple Monitors in the court. There is also in some courts within Cainite a sub-group of Monitor some times mockingly referred to as the Eyes of Zao-Lat/Saulot where HG of non-Asian/Indian descendant pose as Cainites to infiltrate local Cainite groups as very few Cainites are aware that non-Asians and Indians can become HG.

                                                I have a few other position ideas including one that defends and maintains Nodes within the court's territory but they're in the initial phase at the moment.

                                                Homo sapiens. What an inventive, invincible species. It's only a few million years since they crawled up out of the mud and learned to walk. Puny, defenceless bipeds. They've survived flood, famine and plague. They've survived cosmic wars and holocausts. And now, here they are, out among the stars, waiting to begin a new life. Ready to outsit eternity. They're indomitable. Indomitable.
