                                  I've used her as a background character in a couple of games who was working as a secret patron for the player characters, or the secret patron of their secret patron. In life she was a prophetess, and it's believed that she was Embraced somewhere in South or Central America 3000 to 4000 years ago. After becoming a vampire she became obsessed with the Abyss, spending a great deal of time there and is currently the foremost master of Abyss Mysticism in existence. She is also the only Lasombra that is (ostensibly) still loyal to Lasombra - she helped him with his plan to become one with the Abyss and continues to serve as his hands in the real world, moving between the Abyss and Earth.

                                  Sybil is technically a member of the Lasombra Antitribu purely due to the fact that she did not join the Sabbat. The Sabbat have attempted to kill her a few times over the centuries, but those assassination attempts were unsuccessful (the last of which was spectacularly so). Still, she doesn't appear to oppose the Sabbat or carry a grudge (and has contacts with some of the elder Lasombra within the faction and has even assisted them on a few occasions) so they have mostly ended up adopting an "unlive-and-let-unlive" policy with her. She also maintains contact with Montano and some of the other elder Lasombra Antitribu. None of them have any idea that Lasombra still exists or that she's still working for him.

                                  In the Modern Nights, she's mostly a forgotten and apocryphal individual, and only a small number of Lasombra have ever heard of her, and even fewer know that she still exists. She's very much a puppet master, who hides in the Abyss, using her powers of foresight to push other vampires into certain courses of action to conform with the future that she's seen. She professes her loyalty to Lasombra, but Lasombra has begun to doubt that. At the same time, he also can't do anything because she's irreplaceable to him and his plans. He can't sire any new childer and it would take centuries for any else to approach her mastery over the Abyss. He's in the uncomfortable position of being almost entirely dependent on her, but also not being able to trust her either.

                                                      No. I tend not to use any childer of the antedeluvians in the chronicles I ST. Even when I use ancient Methusalehs of potent blood as responsible for something (usually in torpor), I stick to the fifth generation vampires who never met their sire's sire.

                                                      I also have an internal rule on Lasombra lineages based on the idea originally discussed in Montano's chapter of Kindred of the Revolution - except for Montano, Lasombra eventually destroys all his childer because they always disappoint him for not being loyal enough. Such childer might live for centuries or even thousands of years and have various childer of their own, but he eventually destroys them. Montano is the only exception.

                                                      So I would have the Sybil be a childe Laosmbra created after he embraced Montano. Just so she can exist in order to embrace Sejanus. But she was destroyed herself before Lasombra embraced Gratiano. She might have been the only childe created by Lasombra inbetween Montano and Gratiano. It is precisely this history of Lasombra destroying his childer that prompted Gratiano to join the Anarch Revolts - he didn't want to eventually end up on the menu.

                                                      I just think the "story" between Montano and Gratiano works better, if they are only surviving "siblings".

                                                      Another possibility is that Sybil still exists and is 4th generation, but isn't the childe of Lasombra. Instead she diablerized her way to 4th generation (perhaps devouring another childe of Lasombra with his approval, or another clan). I'd have to use this if I wanted Sybil to still be around and somehow manipulating Vitel and others in the DC area. But if I remember correctly, while we are given hints that the Sybil was involved in both Vitel and Marissa, I don't think we actually got any details of her plans like we did with Helena and Menele in Chicago. So I think it'd be very easy to run a DC chronicle and not utilize Sybil as a real background presence.
