                                        Absolutely yes, they should be. Kuei-Jin were probably what I wanted the vampire themes to be about (solidarity based on ideology and spirituality rather than politics or genealogy, default etiquette, education and discipline, preserved traditions, close-knit society, focus on restoration and growth rather than on survival and redemption, more general awareness of the ambivalence of the world around, and many more)

                                        It definitely had an absurd amount of cheese and shallowness in regards to Asian culture and mythology it was based upon, and I clearly realize it was not a game of undead of East Asian culture, it was a game of undead of westerner's image of East Asian culture. That's what I'd like them to improve, adjust the existing dharmas, make 'heretical dharmas' baseline along with original five, bring more dharmas, expand the regions poorly highlighted in original KotE (Myanmar, Indonesia, New Guinea, Oceania, Manchuria, Russian Far East/Siberia/Mongolia), expand the bridge between kin-jin and kuei-jin primarily via India.. that sort of things.

                                        I really would like to see a reinvigorated and polished version of KotE in V5, not the total redesign or outright removal.
