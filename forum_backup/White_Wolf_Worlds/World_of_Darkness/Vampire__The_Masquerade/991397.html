    Why did Cappadocious allow himself to be eaten?

    10-22-2016, 11:05 AM
    As inspired by a recent trend where all the Diableries were, to borrow a phrase from another fictional universe "just as planned". Tremere is Vampire Jesus, Tzitimze is Lugoj, Lasombra is in the Abyss, or something. But how does getting devoured (but apparently not fully, see the True Vessel) serve to push his plans forward?

      There are a few reasons, the most reasonable being that he was utterly beyond sanity and believed that by freeing his soul he could then diablerise God.

      Another theory is that with Augustus being arguably the most accomplished Necromancer in such a short space of time (four centuries) Cappadocius wanted to inhabit the Giovanni, and have all that power at his fingertips.

      Yet another theory is that Cappadocius' soul was already split between multiple locations, and he wanted to pursue their reunification and ultimate ascension.

      None of the above seemed to go to plan for the silly old bastard.

                                                  Cappadocious wanted his physical body destroyed so that his spirit/soul could work full time on the non-material plane to enact whatever his plan was. He choose diablerie rather than another means because he was quite sure that if necessary he could "return" to physical reality by overpowering the psyche of the diablerist because his blood was in them.

                                                  Of all the Antedeluvians, Cappadocious seemed to be the one who most had a plan of action (that the rest of us could see). His actions of the Feast of Folly, embrace of Augustus, letting him embrace his family, and all the weird things up to and after his embrace indicated there was some master plan in place. Whatever the plan was, it involved making sure that he culled all the ones who disappointed him and saved the ones he wanted that would prove useful in Gehenna.

                                                  I assumed that his plan involved coming back to earth, wiping out the Giovanni except for a few individuals who secretly served him, and bringing back all the odd ball Cappadocians he had made sure were preserved. Of all the clans in Vampire, I always thought the Giovanni were the ones most obviously doomed.
