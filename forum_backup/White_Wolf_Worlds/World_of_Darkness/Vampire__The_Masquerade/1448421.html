    [V5] So, what happened to the Giovanni's potence?

    05-13-2021, 10:45 AM
    The rules say just swap out Auspex for Obfuscate or Dominate or take a merit. That works for the Samedi, who had near enough the same disciplines, I asume the Lamia swap auspex for potence. The Nagaraja already had auspex, where they differed was in having dominate instead of fortitude. I suppose they swap that instead.

    But the Giovanni had two disciplines that don't match the original Cappadocian layout (I used to wonder if they were somehow some kind of splinter group of Lasombra because of it)

    Like, if a Giovanni embraced in 1995 has two dots in necromancy, one in dominate, OK then, she can keep those after whatever metaplot magic went down and she wakes up one night suddenly knowing how to see auras and take a bullet to the face or something. But what if she has a few dots of potence? Does the Giovanni punch a wall one day and discover her ability to break rock with her fists has irreversibly changed into the ability not to get her fists broken by rock? Does she keep her dots in potence, but now has to pay out-of-clan costs for it? Do the older members not change their disciplines at all?

    I can exchange auspex for dominate or get a 4 point merit to have dominate too, but nothing is said about the potence.

    How do I translate my V20 necromancers? Did I miss something?

          If you’re converting an existing character use their actual disciplines regardless of what the current version says are in-clan/out-of-clan disciplines.

          As far as the disciplines being different, V5 is built on the idea that you are creating all new characters for it and that the metaphysics of the setting haven’t actually changed even if the mechanics have and, if played that way, the change doesn’t matter much; you make new characters by the current rules.

          I’m pretty sure the decision to swap out the disciplines for Hecata is largely because, with the merger of necromancy and obtenabration into oblivion in V5, Lasombra and Giovanni would have had identical clan disciplines and, if you’re in the business of selling supplements, you want your options to actually have mechanical differences. So, Losombra came out first and kept all its toys and Hecata had a mystical event to explain why it’s spread is different going forward.

          In my own V20 game where I’m introducing V5 plot elements, my plan is for the local 8th Gen Giovanni to NOT be invited to the Family Reunion because his sire/grandsire was on the reunion faction’s hit list and so now will call in all the favors the PCs owe him for his assistance in smuggling in various items in exchange for protection from the Hecata-hired assassins now coming for him. And since he nor any of his immediate line have not been to the reunion, they’d not have any metaphysical changes even if they would affect others (who will be new NPCs and so built to whatever standards I wish).

                                          Discipline spread is an odd little meta concept made for the player's convenience more then anything. Heck even in earlier editions Malkavians swapped disciplines like it was bloody needles. That said there may be more reason then, they'd be too similar to V5 Lasombra. When they were first created as a bloodline, in story, in Dark Ages they had the same spread as the Cappadocians, aka what Hecata have ATM. This is because they were originally a Cappadocian bloodline, which explains that much. In addition, it also makes sense since all the related bloodlines have a similar spread, and since V5 is attempting to make all Bloodlines in V5 related to a main clan with the loresheets, then it makes sense to use the original Cappadocian disciplines instead.

                                                  The creation story is backward though.
                                                  Giovanni were introduced in "The Player's Guide" in 1991, with no mention of Cappadocians, but the general skeleton of their future development:
                                                  Legend has it the Clan founder hoped to bolster his own power by Embracing a coven of Venetian Necromancers. This coven was actually a tightly knit family of Giovannis who had strong trade interests in the Mediterranean region. They repaid their Sire for his gift by slaying him and all his other descendants. The immediate reaction from Camarilla around the world was fear and horror, and many joined together to hunt these Devil Kindred. After almost a century of intermittent warfare, the Giovannis made peace with their foes by promising to maintain a low profile and keep out of Kindred politics, a pledge they have apparently maintained to this day.
                                                  Since Cappadocians aren't invented yet, the 1993 "Book of Nod" lists Giovanni as the Clan of Death:
                                                  They brought in the Kingship Clan [Ventrue],
                                                  the Clan of the Beast [Gangrel],
                                                  the Moon Clan [Malkavian],
                                                  the Clan of the Hidden [Nosferatu],
                                                  the Wanderer Clan [Ravnos],
                                                  the Clan of the Rose [Toreador],
                                                  the Night Clan [Lasombra],
                                                  the Clan of Shapers [Tzmisces],
                                                  the Snake Clan [Setites],
                                                  the Clan of Death [Giovanni],
                                                  the Healer’s Clan [Saulot],
                                                  the Clan of the Hunt [Assamites],
                                                  and the Learned Clan [Brujah].
                                                  Cappadocians did not appear as a playable clan until five years later, when in 1996 "Vampire: The Dark Ages" came out. Although as a narrative clan they already showed up in 1995 in the "Giovanni Chronicles".
