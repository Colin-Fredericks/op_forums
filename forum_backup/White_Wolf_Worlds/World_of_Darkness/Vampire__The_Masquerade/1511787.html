    The Promise of 1528, the Giovanni, and Politics

    05-16-2023, 07:58 AM
    I don't think the Promise of 1528 has ever actually been built on. Camarilla don't interfere with Giovanni business, Giovanni can't play politics or hold titles and Venice holds the annual Inner Circle meetups and that's it, as far as I recall. That vague explanation generally gives the impression that Giovanni can't play politics or put a toe in the court of the Camarilla without breaching the contract. I sometimes see complaints that this makes them borderline unplayable.

    Has anyone else ever defined the Promise further, ignored it or otherwise tweaked it in their world building in order to determine how you play a Giovanni in a Cam game?

    I personally defined the Promise as an addendum of sub-clauses to the Traditions specifically involving the Clan. The idea that Giovanni do not involve themselves with Camarilla politics at all ever is a misunderstanding borne of the fact that the closest most vampires will ever come to them is rumours (it's a very small clan as far as I'm concerned). The Promise is a treaty that in my experience has occasionally lead to entertaining political bullshit.

    (I'll just shove all my babbling under a spoiler tag)


    The Masquerade
    Unchanged, though it is one that the Camarilla holds over the Clan's heads the most. You're very involved with those mortals' lives, what happens if you slip up, Aunt Why-Haven't-You-Aged-At-All-And-Why-Does-Dad-Also-Call-You-Aunty? (Nah, it's fine, that's what Dominate and Mnemosynis are for.)


    The Domain
    The Giovanni have no jurisdiction in domains held by a Camarilla vampire and are largely subject to the local rules just like everybody else. Likewise, areas that are the domain of a Giovanni are out of Cam control, and the Camarilla has no right to tell them what they can or cannot do or police their kin within those borders. As with the Inner Circle and the clan stronghold in Venice, the kindred of the larger domain in which the Giovanni reside have the right to make appointments to inquire into their business and inspect their domain to ensure the Promise is being upheld.
    Giovanni cannot hold official Camarilla domain or titles (prince, sherrif, justicar, etc), however they can hold unofficial positions, trade boons and be rewarded with domain (which would then changes from Camarilla to Giovanni territory). This domain can be withdrawn by the prince.
    (Mortal relatives count as herd and retainers for the clan at large, and it's counted as a breach of the Second Tradition to lay a finger on them. The fact that it's illegal hasn't stopped that from occurring of course...)

    The Progeny
    "Thine Elder" in this case refers to the Giovanni elders. They do not need the permission of a Prince to sire from their own family - however, the Fifth Tradition also comes into play here.
    This exception only applies to blood relatives however, a Giovanni absolutely needs Camarilla permission to embrace some rando off the street (and Anziani permission, which he 'aint gonna get). Embrace requirements can be skirted by simply marrying families into the fold. Sure, Augustus needed Camarilla consent to embrace Francis Milliner, but if you marry all his available descendants into your family you don't need permission to embrace future childer of Milliner stock.

    In reverse, you need permission from the prince and the clan to embrace one of their mortal relatives. The answer will generally be "hell no" from both sides, but such a childe can be successfully negotiated for if you can name and pay the right price/s. An illegal embrace will result in the sire being punished as the prince sees fit and the childe handed over to the custody of their mortal family to deal with as they see fit (which may involve raising them alongside their regular fledglings and making use of an out of clan skill-set).

    The Accounting
    "Until thy Progeny shall be Released, thou shall command them in all things." Which in this clan is forever. Giovanni are never "Released" as other vampires are, at all times their sire and the rest of the family are accountable for them. On the one hand, one may think that this would give outsiders pause before punishing a Giovanni; you cannot accuse a neonate of something without also dragging their clan elders into it. It means tangling with the whole Clan and their dog, alive, undead and dead... Sometimes true. In practice this can also mean that if a Giovanni gets into trouble and it causes a political headache for everybody then they get skinned alive by their family for causing trouble and sent back to the Camarilla as an apology gift so that they can take their strips of hide too. So, you know: don't get caught.
    Accounting also applies to ghouls. There's not a lot you can do about the wraiths.


    The Hospitality
    As a prince you have no ability to stop a Giovanni embracing any family member they like... but you do have the right to say they're not allowed on your turf. Domain holders may impose limits on the number of Giovanni who may be present in their domain at once, or even ban the Clan access altogether. Visitors to Giovanni domains must be invited or give notice and gain permission. Both Clan and Camarilla are free to punish trespassers as they see fit, short of Final Death.
    A Giovanni domain is also essentially a guest domain within a prince's domain. It must be officially recognised as a legitimate domain and the necromancer in charge (capo is such a stupid term for a Northern Italian clan that's supposed to be merchant princes...) - the necromancer in charge is best off keeping an amicable working relationship with the local Cam, because they can revoke that acknowledgement. If that recognition is rescinded, the Giovanni are obligated to pack up. (Although what a fucked up family of insulted necromancers leave behind may not be pleasant to deal with...). Likewise, a prince may refuse to allow the Giovanni to hold any domain in the city at all. For example; princes with an investment in protecting surviving Cappadocians may do so, such as Mukhtar Bey (and maybe Mithras? I think?).

    (Venice, obviously, is undisputed Giovanni domain and the reverse of the above. Meanwhile, due to their status as Strongholds, specific regions of cities such as Boston, Edinburgh, San Fransisco, Mexico city*, Morocco*, Hong Kong*, Beijing* and Shanghai* can't be revoked by Camarilla law (*or couldn't be in the hypothetical scenario where the Camarilla had that level of control over the cities))


    The Destruction
    Giovanni are not allowed to kill outsiders without the go ahead from the Camarilla and vice versa.




    The Promise is a contract between the Giovanni and the Camarilla, it does not have any impact on relations with the Sabbat, Anarchs, Children of Haqim, Followers of Set or the Ravnos. Those tend to boil down to case by case bases involving individual domains. Although there is a slight issue if for some reason you wanted to play a Giovanni in a Sabbat game, because Giovanni neonates aren't technically allowed to run off, join a pack and take the Vinculum... would make an interesting character though.

              There is not much to be fleshing out actually that wasn't covered in the official materials in my humble opinion
              The The Promise of 1528 was quite simple; it was the treaty between the Camarilla and the Giovanni that formally accepted the Giovanni as the successors of the Cappadocians.
              Got it name because in 1528 the Foudners of the Camarilla made a promise to Claudius Giovanni (the son and the 5th Generation Childe of Augustus Giovanni, ironically a Premascine himself, technically a Cappadocian) who represented his Sire/Father during the "peace talks" - since the whole Conspiracy of Isaac was something the young Camarilla not really appriciated. The Founders of the Camarilla had learned of a plot against the Cappadocians and hoped to extinguish this threat in order to gain the allegiance of the Clan of Death for their sect. Ultimatelly the Camarilla failed, and initiated "Plan B"
              "Plan B" was basically the work of Lady Jadviga Almanov of Bohemia, a 6th Generation Ventrue and was a stern rival of Hardestadt and his ideal of the Camarilla, instead believing in the old ways where every vampire was his own lord and answered to no one. She had strong ties with the Giovanni bloodline of the Cappadocians and was the one who proposed the betrayal of the Cappadocians even to the higher echelons of her Clan. Her elders approved, believing that this would strengthen the ties with the Giovanni and leaving them indebted to the Ventrue, BUT in the same time authorizing Hardestadt to thwart her scheme, so the Ventrue could win regardless of the outcome
              After Cappadocius and Japheth was out of the picture, Humuusa, Byzar was belived to be dead (the later was just in torpor in her Child, Alexia Theusa's personal collection), like Caias Koine and Lazarus was also belived to be either dead or hiding somewhere, but it was more or less common knowledge who knew the Clan of Death, Lazarus was "the faithless son of Cappadocius", no one left to take up the mantle of the leadership, ust Augustus - so from the Camarilla/Ventrue point of view it was logical to support them.

              The young Camarilla sold the Cappadocians out to the Giovanni. The Camarilla needed that deal (they were at war with the proto-Sabbat, they couldn't start another war on the behalf of the Clan of Death) and so that was the agreement: the Giovanni get to destroy the Cappadocians, without interference from the Camarilla, and the Giovanni will stay out of the Camarilla business. And the Cappadocians remember, they tried to join the Camarilla after all, and so they hate the Camarilla for this Great Betrayal.

              The Promise included the assurance of non-interference of both Giovanni and Camarilla into each other's affairs. It also granted the Giovanni authority over the city of Venice, although the Inner Circle claimed the right to hold their annual meetings there.
              And... basically thats all
              In the grand schemes of things, as the world changed and the Grand Masquarade started, the Clan of Death not had a room in a new order; especially after the Inquistion and Anarch Revolt and the young Sabbat around
              Dessicated corpse like or deathly palor Cappadocians would be too dangerous to have around and (thanks to some wraithly interference, who hated Cappadocius and hold back the Cappadocian Curse from the Giovanni...) while the venitian merchant necromancers were... well human looking.
              AND a Bloodline! Augusutus kept his line small... Japeth warned him when he started the Embrace and allowed the Family to expand whats happend with the Clan of Death in the Feats of Folly (one of the mean reason of the event was the number; the Clan numbered above 12.000 Cainite before the Antediluvian thinned them out). Augusutus nodded and decreed, stop. Keep our numbers small and strong and fly under the radar.

              The non-interference of Camarilla affairs don't necessarily menas a Giovanni can't play politics; Don Michael Antonio Giovanni was the Prince of Las Vegas until 1996 according Outcasts: A Players Guide to Pariahs (tho worth to mention,. other soruces say Benedic is the Cainite Prince of Las Vegas, an 8th Generation Ventrue)
              "Camarilla affairs" is a broad term... gives plenty of room to wiggle, and the political atmosphere is always depends on the local situation.
              If you treat the local Giovanni as guests or like an Ambassador - or just reasoning, if we won't let them a bit near (so we could keep an eye on them), they will find others to be friend with, like roaming Sabbat packs who would buy weapons and information, or seek shelter, Anarchs, and god knows what; no reason to antagonize the Giovanni with purging them from the city. they could be usefull - you can have opporunity galore when it comes to powerplay and politics!
              (The Camarilla tolerated the Tremere becasue Blood Magic was something unique and usefull to have... a necromancer could be more valuable. It is rare when you need a Tremere to help... but death is something comes up regualrly when you are a vampire...)
              It really depends of the interpretation of the "Camarilla affairs" of the locals, especially Princes and Primogens.​

                                          I have the view that Kindred treaties are something that is observed with the assumption they're not actually expected to be OBEYED. They're expected to EXIST. You can ignore them or bend the rules but that's only if you are in good standing.

                                          Otherwise, they're excuses to lay you low.

                                          Very "Doctor Doom" handling of the letter of the law or how authoritarian governments handle hypocrisy.

                                          The short version is the Giovanni promise to stay out of the Jyhad in exchange for the Camarilla not lifting a finger to stop the Cappadocian genocide (necrocide?)

                                          HOWEVER...

                                          * The Giovanni use this as an excuse to devote themselves to increasing their power among mortals the entire time.
                                          * The Giovanni exploit the fact they're using their mortal relations to expand their power base.
                                          * The Giovanni generally did go to Camarilla cities and introduce themselves to the Prince, obey the Traditions, and kept their heads low.
                                          * Buttttt they relied on bribing the Princes to ignore them increasing their power bases. Basically in any city with a massive financial institution, the Giovanni had a claim but they did so propping up the local Ventrue who were happy to take the money.
                                          * Eventually, the Giovanni start outright breaking the Promise like when they took over Vegas and Boston. But by this point, the Camarilla doesn't actually care that much since the Giovanni aren't the Tremere but aren't diablizing other clans like the Sabbat either.
