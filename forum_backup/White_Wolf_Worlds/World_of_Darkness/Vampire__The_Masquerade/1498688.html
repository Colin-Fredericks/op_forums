              The Addams Family has always been one of my inspirations for Revenants along with movies like The Texas Chainsaw Massacre, the Hills Have Eyes, and the Burbs along with many others. Obviously there is not real horror in the Addams Family, but useful in the sense of people who appear human but act very differently and don't appear entirely sane.                                       The Addams Family make perfect revenants and if you took all that they have done over the years and played it seriously instead of for humor, then they would be incredibly dark and twisted. Kids trying to kill each other, casual talk of murder, pouring boiling oil on Christmas carolers, implied incest, etc. If I was going to use a preexisting revenant family, I would agree with others that Obertus might be the best. If I were going to make the Addams revenant family, I think that I would give them Fortitude as one of their family Disciplines (since they always seem to be able to resist harm) and Thaumaturgy since witchcraft seems very common in the family and it also possibly explain other miscellaneous powers (like Uncle Fester and electricity). I’m not sure what the third Discipline should be. Fester seems to have Potence (he lifted Tully with one hand and turned him upside down, no normal human could do that) and Gomez’ acrobatic abilities during his sword fight suggests Celerity.

                                      I’m not sure what the weakness could be. Some Addams lack social skills, but not all do. Some are ugly, but most aren’t. Maybe each family members gets a derangement and that explains the various quirks that each family member seems to have.
