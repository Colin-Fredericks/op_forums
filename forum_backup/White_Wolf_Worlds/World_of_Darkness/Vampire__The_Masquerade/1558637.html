      ​The Chalice of Veddartha appears in many of the parables and legends that Scions tell of the First City and the rise of Clan Ventrue. To properly use the item, it must be saturated with at least 10 blood points. It will then show a variety of scenes in its weavings; tales vary as to precisely what can be viewed there, but accounts have stated things as different as the Great Flood, the near future, the founding of the Second City, the secret weaknesses of a mighty Methuselah, and Gehenna.
      All the visions, however, relate directly to the Kindred, past, present or future. There is so much to see in the tapestry that gazing into its depths requires a full night and a roll of Perception + Occult; the more successes gained, the more useful or relevant the information acquired. A botch may either give misleading information or lead to the discovery of a horrible truth about the Kindred, resulting in a Derangement.

                  Realistically the Mithras and Maltheas sire problem is most likely an editorial issue and mistake (using an established name and not checking its history) but one that makes a fun mystery.

                  Here's a few of theories I'd like to posit:

                  1. [Ventrue] using his mastery of Dominate and Fortitude, allows/forces a direct Childe to diablerize him, but takes over the body. This is to avoid the curse of the Methuselah's Thirst.

                  2. [Ventrue] is both [Ventrue] and Veddartha, and perhaps many others. High level Dominate [lv9 Puppet Master] allows you to practically clone yourself by conditioning someone else to think they are you. You can see similar with Hardestat the Elder and Hardestat the Younger/Jurgen Von Verden and eventually the failed Hardestat the Youngest/Jan Pieterzoon. (This last bit was one of the plot points of the Gehenna novel.)

                  3. Here's a wild one. The modern Ventrue and Brujah [and their variations, Danava, Antitribu, "True"] are sibling bloodlines of a single Antedeluvian. So where's this one come from? Well it hinges on a line in Clan Book Revised: Ventrue.

                  [CbR-V] PG12 "Trying to hold the disparate community together, [Ventrue] attempted to listen to and resolve the Cainites' problems - even the depredations of his sister, the witch."
                  Its a very curious line "even the depredations of his sister, the witch". First we have the idea that [Ventrue] had a sister. Is this a blood/brood sibling? A biological sister? Secondly, [Ventrue] is defending against this sister's depredations. A depredation is another word for attack or pillage. Third this sibling is called "the witch." Are they a mage? Well perhaps but it seems instead of being a witch they are being referred to as the witch. Its a title or honorific. Interestingly enough in modern times there's this little clan called the Brujah that borrows the Romantic (Spanish) word for witch as their name even though they have nothing to do with sorcery. This said clan seems to have a real big problem with the Ventrue as a whole and is constantly at odds with them.

                  My favorite interpretation of this is that [Ventrue] was actually Ilyes and that this sister is Troile. Troile originally being what we would consider Ventrue blood until she diablerizes her brother and creates her own brood that becomes known as the Brujah. [Ventrue]'s blood continues on otherwise due to having had sired a large brood, through the Ventrue bloodline. This helps explain [Ventrue]'s disapearance, Troile's ascendance to 3rd gen, and the antipathy of the two clans even though they have forgotten their origins.
