    [V5] Custom Predator Types

    07-02-2021, 09:30 PM
    I love 5th edition of Vampire, and I will continue to enjoy it for years to come. However, I do have some problems with design elements, my biggest being Predator Types and Loresheets. This here post is designed to solve my problem with Predator Types specifically. To me, they feel like they narratively restrict the characters or is only considered for power gaming. I’m more concerned about the narrative aspect personal, and that is how I made what I present today. I came up with this guide after doing some analysis of each Predator Type, and came up with the possible guidelines used to make Predator Types. Did someone already do this? Most likely, but I haven’t seen it online personally, so I made this under the assumption it doesn’t exist already.

    Original Purpose:
    The point behind this homebrew was to give players specifically the ability to design their own Predator Types. That said, the rules are written under the assumption the Storyteller will look over the Predator Type they made and work with the player in its design. A Storyteller can easily use this guide to make Predator Types and the players may never even see these rules, ultimately do what is best for your game and group.

    The Homebrew Guide:
    When either creating a Predator Type here are a few things to keep in mind:
    • Your Predator Type either gives you a specialty in a skill you already have, or gives you 1 dot in a skill you do not currently have dots in.

    • Your Predator Type gives you 1 free dot in a Discipline, either in or out of clan. Blood Sorcery is usually restricted to Tremere and Banu Haquim. Try to pick a Discipline that is appropriate for the Predator Type, whether based on the common resonance of the victims preferred or if it aids in the hunt rolls.

    • You gain a net total of 1, or 2 with Storyteller’s permission, dots worth of advantages and flaws. Increasing your Humanity with your Predator counts as a 3 dot advantage, and decreasing it counts as a 2 dot flaw. Increasing your Blood Potency counts as a 7 dot advantage and requires Storyteller approval.

    • Choose an Attribute + Skill dice pool that best reflects how the Predator Type prefers to hunt. With Storyteller approval, certain backgrounds such as Fame or Influence may also be added to this pool.

                  Originally posted by adambeyoncelowe View Post
                  What’s your Loresheet fix?
                  Sadly my issue with Loresheets can’t be so easily fixed. The problem is the odd, almost inconsistent, nature of Loresheets themselves. Sometimes they represent knowledge of a subject rather then just strictly first-hand accounts, but their advantages are flavored as if your character was a part of the lore, or has first-hand experience. The Pyramid Loresheet from the book Camarilla represents it best honestly. It acts like you can take it to represent knowledge or study of the subject, but the advantages you get from it are worded in a way that you are/were a member of the Pyramid. In addition, the advantages shouldn't give you benefits as written because you're not a member of the Pyramid. It doesn't make sense, in or out of game.

                  Also sometimes it also seems like Loresheets are a natural progression, like The Cobweb from Chicago By Night which has higher-level advantages that straight up don’t work, or logically shouldn’t, unless you get the lower level advantages too.

                  Overall, a weird system that has to be worked out on a case-by-case basis honestly. I just recommend as a Storyteller talking with the players about possible personalizing Loresheet advantages and reflavoring them altogether to allow for players to get more out of them. Overall, not a totally satisfying solution honestly, but it’s the best I was able to think of after looking through all the Loresheets.

                      Ah, yeah, I see what you mean. Some are stealth Discipline powers or similar, and others, as you say, are just packages of Backgrounds and knowledge.

                      I would probably move the powers to Supernatural Merits (or, heck, make them alternate Discipline powers) and keep Loresheets for the social and learned benefits of being a part of the lore. Make them explicitly the benefit of having a first-hand connection to that lore. Each dot should stand alone.

                      For progressive 'powers', those should be alternate Discipline powers gated off by Bloodline or Clan (this is part of the problem of getting rid of Clan-specific Disciplines--it pushes those powers into awkward places).
