                                                    Auspex was there because the Necroscope Wamphyri are telepathic.

                                                    I suppose I sound like a broken record at this point. It's just strange to me how people don't seem to care why something was designed the way that it was. It's typical for any long-standing game line: Just keep repurposing, readjusting, and regurgitating elements of the game while completely losing the reason why something was put in the game in the first place. I honestly don't get why someone would insist on using the Tzimisce in the game, only to completely ditch their original identity and repurpose them as the "Dracula Clan." That dude doesn't need a clan. It's obvious that Dracula's classic powers were split between the 10 original 1st Edition disciplines. You can do Dracula with just about any existing clan, from Ventrue to Gangrel to Tremere.

                                                    (Of course I know that Dracula was pegged as Tzimisce in 1992's Children of the Inquisition. But the dude didn't define the clan. He was explicitly presented as an independent rebel who went against typical Tzimisce conventions and behavior. And even that Dracula was only the second interpretation of the guy in the game line. The penitent Dracula who writes to Mina Harker in the introduction of the 1st Edition core book barely even knows what shape-shifting is. He's heard stories, but that's all. Not exactly the vampire superstar of 1999's Children of the Night, with his big dick swagger of 34 points in various disciplines, including Protean, Vicissitude, and Thaumaturgy.)
