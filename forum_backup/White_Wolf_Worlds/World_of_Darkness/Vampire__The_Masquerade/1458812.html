    What If ?

    09-05-2021, 07:56 AM
    What if all the Antediluvians died in a certain way around the year -0-. (There is no doubt, they are truly dead)

    There could still be a rebellion against the elders leading to the establishing of the Camarilla and the Anarchs /Sabbat. Or would they only be anarchs and no Sabbat?
    The Sabbat would no longer be against the antediluvians but that could quickly become against ancient vampires (methuselahs? Would they claim to rebel against Cain?
    The Tremere would not diablerize Saulot and would not have an antediluvian. Would they have survived?
    The Giovanni would not have diablerized Cappadocian but some Capadoccian methuselahs. Would they have survived and thrived?
    Would the Leaders of the sects behave differently knowing there is no threat of those ancient monsters awakening?

    How would the world of darkness be if vampires didn't have this threat having above their heads?

      Sabbat would likely have formed anyway based around the Ritae, Vinculum being still the most important, and they would rebel and war against the Elders who ruled through bloodbonds and against the Camarilla which is an organization of Elder puppets (as far as the Sabbat is conserned).

      Tremere would likely still survive though they might have had a rougher but even without an Antediluvian they still had Mastery of Blood magics to offer in exchange of alliances formed.

      The Giovanni would have diablerized Cappadocian Methuselahs but they might not have such a clear victory over them. I would say they would survive but their war against the Cappadocians could drag on for centuries.

      Camarilla leaders would act as they always have, securing their and being pro-Elder ivory tower.
      Sabbats war would likely be less urgent and due to that they might act less openly than bow and undermine Camarilla rule through more insidious ways, likely trying to convert and turn Camarilla neonates to turn on their elders.

      World of Darkness would be just as bleak and hopeless even without the Antediluvian threat.

      Above are only my personal opinions on the matter YMMV.

                  How much of the game really lingers on the objective truth of the Antedilluvians? For most of the game it could well be just a legend or they could have all died before the Greeks had a writing system.

                  You may take this in two ways. One is that their demise is a certainty for the players, but can't be proven to the characters (most likely). The other is that somehow it is proven beyond fair doubt that they existed but died, so believing in then is less like Christianity and more like Flat Earth.

                  On the first take little to nothing changes. Almost nothing was really predicated on the Antedilluvians existing and even the newcomers that diablerized some Ancients wouldn't change a lot if they didn't. Tremere and Giovanni weren't leaders because of generation and the diablerie actually weakened their contact with their respective clans, as Tremere went into torpor and Augustus obsessed even more with the Underworld. The diablerie of Antedilluvians was far more symbolic than anything in the formation of Sabbat, and they are the original Anarchs, far more preoccupied with their Elders. Fighting the Ancients was more of a religious theme than anything the Time of Judgement. And the Setites are a cult that don't even believe in the Noddist doctrine, believing Set was either 2nd gen, 1st gen, God or whatever.

                  Now, if even Setites must deal with the fact that Set is dead, or go full Flat Earth wacko, that can change... a few things, but just a few. The Sabbat must tinker a little more with their religion, but not a lot. More focus on god and less apocalyptic tone. Well, still apocalyptic, but in the original sense. Same package, same content, but some changes in text in the gift card. The Camarilla would have an easier time convincing neonates that Gehenna is a silly superstition because Gehenna IS a silly superstition. The Setites would probably split even more, with most focusing more in the Gnostic aspects of their religion and taking Set more as an Exemplar, but a few would form a cult even more obsessively dedicated to finding and resurrecting their god, similar to some Protestant extremists.

                                                      Originally posted by Denstoffy View Post

                                                      I am warming up to this idea of a masquerade within the masquerade. It would even streamline the Kuei-jin if one would be so obliged as an offshoot of an ancestor of Caine. That would shake things up quite a lot though.

                                                      So traits and disciplines stay at 9? or is it only disciplines and the rest can go to 10 (which is an improvement over 9, but nothing spectacular) and how do you do bloodpool and spend per round (same?)
                                                      I literally just use the limits for 4th Gen (9’s for traits, 50/10 BP), but with the sheer amount of xp (or maturation points if you’re using that system) that a 14,000+ year old pre-deluge vampire has over a 4000-5000 year old vampire basically gives a true Antediluvian 9’s in multiple attributes, abilities and disciplines.

                                                      Also worth noting is that, given how many have their own clan-defining disciplines (ex. Gangrel/Protean, Ravnos/Chimerstry, Lasombra/Obtenebration, Tzmisce/Vicissitude), I’d argue that some form blood sorcery is common to many of the true Antediluvians as well (on the theory that all disciplines are offshoots of the blood sorcery Caine learned from Lilith* that got regimented and it’s use normalized) and that gives them an edge as well.

                                                      As to such a seemingly small blood pool of just 50; even 8th Gen elders have blood bound, dominated and duped guards they could feed from in emergency. A 3rd gen presumably has an assortment of dominated fresh methuselahs they create simply to fatten up and then feed from as needed and then kill before they can become bound.

                                                      Ravnos during his rampage had countless supernaturals to feed from as they tried futilely to stop him. Werewolf blood is twice as potent as human blood, so that’s 3x2=6 BP/turn sucked out of one to recharge itself. A 6th Gen has 3x the potence of human blood by volume so 9 BP per turn.

                                                      Basically, an Antediluvian on the warpath has plenty of blood to fuel itself... and fueling itself also removes opponents at the same time. An Antediluvian NOT on the warpath who’s active rather than using high level Auspex and other disciplines from torpor will also presumably have entire cults devoted to keeping itself fed.

                                                      And yes, in my world the Wan-Kuei (i.e. the “kuei-jin”) are an offshoot of a 2nd Gen sired by Blood Caine before Caine** returned to found the First City. This ancient in turn sired its own 3rd Generation who became the mythical Yama Kings*** (the gist is that the servants of the Yama Kings and the Wan-Kuei are essentially sects... the servants seek out particularly wicked mortals, manipulate events to bring about their deaths and then give them the Second Breath/Embrace... the Wan-Kuei are a sect that doesn’t embrace and only adopts those they can rescue from the servants of the Yama Kings. Their Dharmas are essentially means of achieving what’s known in the West as Golconda (which is why those who reach high ranks can transcend generational limits just as those in Golconda can).

                                                      * I particularly like this take because its roughly in line with the origin of Dracula’s supernatural powers in Bram Stoker’s novel; his magic powers were the result of learning the black arts from a hidden school (called Scholomance) supposedly run by Satan himself. Caine being instructed in blood magic by Lilith (a demon in most Middle Eastern myths) is a nice echo of how it worked for THE vampire who basically established all the modern tropes.

                                                      ** Blood Caine is my shorthand for the actual first vampire in the same way humanity’s earliest female common ancestor is called “Mitochondrial Eve.” They may or may not be Biblical Caine (i.e. the vampire the western Kindred count their generations from) or even male, but since no one living has even seen them and we have to call them something “Caine” is as good a name as any. Regardless, the embrace of the common ancestor of the Wan-Kuei occurred prior to Biblical Caine’s founding of the First City.

                                                      *** While the Western Kindred diversified into distinctive clans, the Yama Kings did not... they are all effectively “clanless” in that there are no obvious characteristics that set the line of one Yama King from another.
