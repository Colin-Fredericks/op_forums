                                    How I'd update the Tzimisce to V5

                                    Disciplines: Animalism, Blood Sorcery, and Protean

                                    Clan Bane: Tzimisce have to have the soil of their homeland (or soil from New York or Transylvania) or they must roll a second time for Hunger Dice every night. A Tzimisce without native soil is thus potentially constantly hungry and dangerous. I think they should also have something that makes them suffer from a distraction and lack of empathy from mortals if they make a Bestial Failure. Perhaps make it harder for them to avoid stains due to their detachment from morality and coldness.

                                    Current Status: At some point, they started taking Ruthven seriously and captured their Elder with the help of Vykos and Beckett. As such, the Tzimisce determined their Antediluvian was still alive and had transcended the state of vampiredom to become something else. Some Tzimisce were incensed by this and threw themselves into the Gehenna War to lower their Generation and gain power to hunt the creature while others sought arcane solutions.

                                    A few ordered others to infiltrate the Camarilla and Anarchs to maintain their survival. Others, under Vykos' direction have reached out across the world to the Ashirra, Laibon, Inconnu, Russian Council, and Wan Kuei Tzimisce in order to increase their ability to deal with this threat. The Old Clan and Modern Tzimisce have since made peace and now acknowledge only a single clan. Sect now means little to the Tzimisce who are Tzimisce first and members of whatever their current organization is second. Old grudges still remain but they hold fast to the codes of conduct that predate all the current sects.

                                    Anarch Tzimisce: Anarch Tzimisce are rarely followers. They have set themselves up as Lords of former Sabbat domains, small towns, and crossroads that they hold absolutely. They are not a clan who serves and tend to have a small force of enhanced ghouls to serve as their gang. The Vinculum is employed among these Anarchs and while a few have Tzimisce "knights", these Barons (called "Voivoides") are often more traditional than most. The Anarchs tend to find these gangs of football pads, swords, and shotgun-wielding warriors a bit silly but don't turn down their aid.

                                    Camarilla Tzimisce: Camarilla Tzimisce are far smaller in number than the Lasombra defectors but exist. Many of the younger ones include plastic surgeons, healers, Masquerade protectors, and those who exist to help compromised Kindred escape the Second Inquisition. These flesh sculptors serve a valuable purpose in the Modern Nights of instagram and cellphone cameras. Few Tzimisce are Princes but many have since become Seneschals, Sheriffs, and Scourges. The exception are the Far East and Eastern European Camarilla where they rule as they always have.

                                    Blood Cults Tzimisce: The Tzimisce tend to be drawn to the Church of Caine, Bahari, and other traditionalist Cainite religions. The Path of Metamorphosis has suffered in recent years due to the revelation the Eldest follows it. As such, its members have either turned to worship their Antediluvian or been forced to repudiate their Path. The Church of Mithras appeals to some despite its gods' Ventrue origins as does the Nephilim among the Dracon's lineage. Some have also taken up worship of infernalism, particularly Kupala.

                                    Sabbat Tzimisce: Many expected the Tzimisce to take advantage of the Lasombra's weakness and seize control of the sect. Instead, the Tzimisce have done their best to keep the sect functioning from behind the scenes. Their defections and alliance with non-Sabbat Tzimisce are looked at with suspicion but those who were Archbishops before remain so as do Cardinals as well as Pricius. The Sabbat Tzimisce have begun researching powerful magic, WMD, and other tools that go well beyond the ability to kill normal elders.
