    Running the Free States - 2 - Germaine

    02-06-2025, 08:29 PM
    Germaine was the only Anarch on the original Red List as revealed by the Kindred’s Most Wanted sourcebook. As such, he should be a figure just as well known as Jeremy MacNeil, Salvador Garcia, and Smiling Jack IMO. He’s not as he wasn’t really mentioned or developed outside that sourcebook. So I am going to correct that in this chronicle. I'm going to have him show up and interact with the PCs at some point. If you haven't read that sourcebook, there's going to be spoilers here.

    As written, Germaine is one of the childer of Critias so he has quite potent blood. He was an earlier supporter of the French Revolution, but mostly as a mortal and ghoul. He was not embraced until quite late, hates his sire (whose name he does not know), and was used as a scapegoat by Toreador vampires (Countess D’Adhemar and her sire, Madame Guil).

    Both of them also supported the French Revolution for their own reasons, but had to hide it because they were effectively traitors to their own clan who dominated Paris and France. They basically blamed Germaine for everything, and when the Camarilla created the Red List, Madame Guil put his name on it. She doesn’t really hunt after him, but her childe the Countess does because Germaine could reveal her personal secrets and therefore get her killed.

    It's mentioned explicitly that Geramine does not "deserve" to be on the Red List. He's there out of pure spite. However, simply that is on it means a lot of Kindred, particularly anarchs, must think he's some tremendous badass that has done something even more outstanding than even MacNeil, Salvador, or Smiling Jack. I imagine there's some Chuck Norris style jokes told about him.

    Germaine actually left Paris very early for North America because he wants to kill his sire whom he blames for his troubles. Critias seems to find this funny because he actually helps him secretly to escape the Red List hunters.

    We’re not given any escapades of Germaine. So I must invent various actions and events that he's done. We just know he helps the anarchs, doesn’t mention his name, and is always on the move. Always trying to find his sire (whose face he remembers). He must use various aliases as he tells no one his real name, but probably gets identified as Germain after he leaves. That has built up his legend. He is very much a loner and does not make friends.

      I’m inclined to use him as a kind of wandering Batman/Robin Hood/Scarlet Pimpernel of the Anarchs. He tends to show up in a Camarilla city and helps out Anarchs in trouble before moving on again. He’s a lot like Smiling Jack except he tends to fix problems instead of fucking things up, and doesn’t embrace childer of his own.

      I'm thinking Germaine does not reside in Los Angeles, but probably frequently visits there for some R&D. He ignores MacNeil and the Anarch Council because he just does his own thing. He still keeps his identity secret because the Trophy Clan benefits are a lot, and would attract a lot of Kindred (even among the anarchs) to betray him if they knew about him. That is also why he moves around a lot. I imagine everyone on the Anarch Council has figured out who he is, but keeps his secret. His main concerns are survival and trying to destroy his sire.

      I wonder if he should visit Louis Fortier when he is in town as both are French. It'd probably be nice to speak in one's native language from time to time. The Vignes family in LABN has dots in Linguistics, and as older upper crust Americans from the 19th century probably know French so I was already thinking there was some "French Roundtable" where French speakers would meet in Louis's mansion for social entertainment, and it would be a good place for Germaine to occasionally visit when he's in town.

      In terms of things for the actual PCs, I see him as someone the PCs might run into time to time, perhaps even have him as someone who helped them during their backstory, or have him appear when needed to get them out of one bad jam. Then slowly reveal clues that this guy they know happens to be on the Red List.

      So I intend for this to be a mystery that the PCs can discover on their own at some point. I am also thinking about how to introduce Countess D'Adhemar in this as she's the one Kindred most likely to want to hunt Germaine, and if I use him I pretty much need to introduce her too at some point. Otherwise what is the point? If I have a Red List character show up, I need to involve the Red List as a topic at some point.

      If you have any ideas on stories that I - as the ST - could tell about him, feel free to put them here.

          Having done some research on Monsieur (Pardonnez-moi, Citoyen) Germaine, I’ve done my best to puzzle out a rough timeline for the fellow, despite one or two apparent contradictions:-

          - My best guess is that he’d have been born in the early to mid 1760s (Given that he was made a ghoul at some point not long before the French Revolution and would have stopped aging at that point, even before becoming a vampire in AD 1792).

          - We know he was old enough to have political opinions in 1776 (and extremely passionate ones) so one might assume he was a teenager (and not an especially young one).

          - He’s noted to have joined a ‘Girondist cell’ prior to the Revolution, but this is an anachronism, since the Girondists themselves only received the nickname in 1792 and were apparently not in existence as an organised body prior to the Estates General summoned by King Louis just before the French Revolution: one assumes that while Germaine shared their ‘Romantic Republicanism’ he wasn’t associated with the Girondists until rather later (Possibly because he either joined the movement during his period as a Double Agent or just because he was actually from the Gironde, like many of that faction’s founding members).

          - I’m further assuming that, while not an Aristocrat himself, he was from the sort of bourgeoise family with an eye to either acquiring a title or ‘marrying up’ and would have been given a solid grounding in how to enter polite society (Not unlike a number of the French Revolutionary leadership).

          - He’s noted to have been brought before Critias and made his ghoul (Along with the rest of his ‘Girondist Cell’) before the French Revolution (Critias apparently keeping residence in a small Swiss village): given he seems
          to have been recruited specifically to work his way into the confidences of the Countess d’Adhemar (Sired 1789) it seems
          likely that this occurred in the late 1780s.

          - Note that at this time the Countess d’Adhemar’s
          sire, the future Madame Guil, was one of the most dangerous Anarchs (and ferocious diablerists) in France: it seems highly likely she made the Countess a vampire in order to have an ‘in’ to the French court (possibly to the outer layers of the Camarilla as well), only for Critias of the Brujah to detect this gambit and take measures intended to plant his own agent close to the Countess (Presumably to make certain that the French Revolution would be the ‘right’ sort of Revolution and given himself the exact same ‘in’ with Royal circles).

          My guess is that the future Madame Guil (At this point known as Gilles and possibly going around in drag, given that ‘Gilles’ is a masculine Christian name) attracted Critias’ attention by virtue of being the Childe of Philippe Vollgirre (A Toreador of the 5th Generation who was old by 1579 and the epitome
          of a feudal tyrant: it’s easy to imagine him as an old enemy of Critias as a result, possibly even as far back as the days when Marseilles was a Hellenistic port, given ‘Phillipe’ is a name with Greek origins).

          I’d bet cash money Critias recruited Germaine in order to acquire a true blue Revolutionary who actually liked (or at least knew how to get along with) the ladies, a quality I expect to have been in short supply amongst Critias’ circle.


          - Incidentally, one expects that the Countess may have been a protege of the future Madame Guil (possibly her ghoul as well) prior to her Embrace: in fact I would guess that she insinuated herself into court circles as a mortal and was embraced only when sufficiently deep for her ‘disappearance’ to attract unwonted attention.


          - Anyway, for at least a couple of years (Possibly 1789-1792) Germaine (Possibly as ‘Count Germaine’, though whether in the character of a Swiss or a Frenchman) used d’Adhemar to work his way into French Court circles, facilitate the Revolution and make life more difficult for the Toreador, acting as Critias double agent.

          - At some point after the Revolution he was rumbled, went on the run and ran into Critias, who made him a vampire in 1792 and taught him the use of his new powers before the Methuselah went his own way (Incidentally, this timeline of events very probably kills my pet theory that Critias was in Philadelphia at the time the US constitution was being drafted stone-dead): Germaine proceeded to thoroughly enjoy Revolutionary France, not least by hunting down and feeding on counter-revolutionary elements.

          - This period of Revolutionary bliss seems to have lasted into 1793, at which point the Countess (with the help of some agents) ambushed him in a park and attempted to express her displeasure at his misuse of her: Germaine escaped, but was obliged to flee to the New World, one jump ahead of the Toreador (Apparently in search of his sire, whom he suspected - fairly or unfairly - of having played some role in the ambush).

          - Note that the newly-dubbed Madame Guil makes her debut with the Camarilla after the Revolution (Apparently helping the French Toreador help clean up the mess made of their assets) and begins the process of acquiring the base of support that will allow her to eventually become Justicar (My presumption is that she was not yet a Justicar at the time her second child’s was sired in 1802, but that she had been firmly accepted by the Camarilla before this point).

          What role, if any, the Countess d’Adhemar played
          in this process remains unknown (It was presumably only after Madame Guil became Justicar for Clan Toreador that she was able to place Germaine’s name on the Red List, at a guess sometime towards the middle of the 19th century).


          - After this dates become few, but we know that Germaine travelled across the United States and learned a fine contempt for the Camarilla, involved himself with the Anarchs and was courted by the Sabbat (All of which may well have helped to place him on the Red List).

          He also continued to be manipulated against Madame Guil by Critias (Whether knowingly or unknowingly).


          - We know that at some point he only narrowly escaped destruction by the Justicar Petrodon in Seattle (Probably after the Justicar escalates his efforts against the Anarchs in 1984 and certainly before the latter’s Final Death in 1997), going on the run towards the East Coast (Suggesting he’d somehow burned his bridges to the Anarch Free States or that, as a badly-mauled fugitive, he needed to make a move his opponents would not reasonably predict), apparently arriving in Chicago at some point in the late 1990s and hooking up with Damien to teach the local Anarchs where they came from and where they ought to be heading.


          - Incidentally, Madame Guil, the Justicar who placed Germaine on the Red List, appears to have gone rogue from the Camarilla herself (Having hooked up with Luc, an old flame from her Anarch days now associated with the Sabbat - as a Templar), an old flame having burned out any commitment to the Ivory Tower (Whether Luc reciprocated by dumping the Black Hand or the ex-Justicar was planning to turn Sabbat remains unknown), only to be destroyed at some
          point before 2012.

          Whether or not Germaine himself played any role in this sequence of events, he must have been mightily tempted to fly in and dance on her ashes.

              Apologies if this is stating the obvious to the readers, but I wanted to make the facts on which I intended to build some suppositions perfectly clear.

              For the record, those suppositions are:-

              - That Germaine very consciously adopted his nom de
              plume from the late Comte de Saint-Germain, who may or
              may not have been a previous associate of Critias or even a member of his circle.

              - That Germaine may well have arrived in the Western Hemisphere at Saint-Domingue, not the United States and played a role in the war with the British and Spanish there, before proceeding to the United States (Possibly after the British were finally convinced to leave in 1797).

              (Alternatively, he may have played some role in the Whisky Rebellion of New England as it reached it’s climax in 1794).


              - Which would mean that Germaine, loudest and proudest of French Revolutionaries, would have arrived in the US of A just in time to fall foul of the Alien & Sedition Acts of 1798, which took a rather dim view of such rhetoric.

              If he arrived in the United States a bit earlier in the Adams Administration, one might suggest that he played a key role in ticking off Kindred Elders to the point where they lent their backing to the Acts.


              - I’m assuming that, ousted from the US proper and deeply disillusioned by Napoleon’s betrayal of Haiti and the Revolution, Germaineah have lay quiet for a time (Possibly in what shortly thereafter became the Louisiana Purchase), only to take up arms against Great Britain in the War of 1812 (Napoleon or no Napoléon, a chance to hit back against the ‘English’ on behalf of the United States would likely cheer this vampire up).

              Note that if the Camarilla sided with the British, as seems plausible, this may have been a key precondition for Germaine’s place on the Red List (Coupled with his activities in France and Haiti, not to mention Madame Guil’s
              steady ascent of the Ivory Tower).


              - One further suspects that Germaine would have been enthusiastic in his support for the Upper and Lower Canada Rebellions of 1837-1838 (Possibly the Patriot War which took place toward the end of that period) only to be richly disappointed by the British holding on to the Canadas, albeit with some degree of reform.


              - If his hopes of the New World had been consistently disappointed, he may have spent time exploring the North American interior as a sort of nocturnal Voyageur, slowly being drawn back to European-American civilisation by the impending Crisis of the US Civil War, only to find his attention being drawn still further south by the Old World meddling in the new.

              (Alternatively, he could have tried to return to Europe, inspired by the ‘Year of Revolutions’, but been thwarted by the wave of reaction and counter-Revolution across the continent).


              - Assuming that Germaine WAS severely disappointed by ‘Napoleon the Great’, it’s quite possible that he played some role in Mexican resistance to the ‘Second Mexican Empire’ (As backed by Napoleon III) between 1864-1869.

              It may well be at this time that he made a closer acquaintance of the Sabbat: My suggestion is that he could have been approached (At this time or earlier) by the former Anarch ‘Luc’ (A Frenchman active as an Anarch not long prior to the French Revolution to boot), a contact which (If he had not already been placed on the Red List) might well have been enough to earn him that ‘honour’.


              - NOTE: If the Countess d’Adhemar were part of the French efforts against the Mexican Republic (A useful cloak for efforts by the Camarilla to dislodge the Sabbat from Mexico, the city and the nation), then it seems likely she and her bete noir would clash.

              If she, like her brood mate, were an Archon by this point, her injury or destruction would be another point in the case for Germaine being put on the Red List (and a feather in Germaine’s cap, so far as the Anarch’s were concerned).


              - Incidentally, while Germaine only ‘officially’ reaches Chicago in the late 1990s, it’s quite possible that he came close to Critias on one or more previous occasions (Especially in his efforts against British North America) only to bounce off a far more powerful vampire who was also his sire.


              - It seems possible that Germaine could have been in New England when Jeremy MacNeil was ousted (In fact the two of them may have been ‘tidied out’ at the same time, being equally associated with the Anarch tendency).


              - Assuming a pattern of operations against British interests, it’s possible that Germaine was recruited by his clan mate Roman Pendragon for the effort to make Great Britain a Brujah stronghold during the absence of Mithras (An effort brought to a speedy end after the return of the Prince in the mid-1880s).


              - It’s also possible that Germaine could have come back to Europe during the establishment of the Third Republic following the Franco-Prussian War: how long before the French Toreador or other interested parties tracked him down I leave to your imagination.

              - It’s a racing certainty that if Germaine played no part in the establishment of the First Anarch Free State, he’d have showed up in LA as swiftly as Lenin arrived in Petrograd after the Russian Revolution (Though probably not with the exact same impact), making LA his ‘home away from home’ from 1944 (Though whether he’s the sort to make lasting friendships in any area is an interesting question).

              - I like the idea that his brush with Petrodon in Seattle was closely associated with the latter’s escalation of operations against the Anarchs in 1984 (Presumably Seattle had been Germaine’s focus for some little time).

              - Finally, I really like the idea that Germaine (Whether directly or indirectly) played a role in the sequence of events that pulled Madame Guil out of the Camarilla: whether he did so as a deliberate gambit or because he’d given Luc information that allowed the latter to identify his old paramour is an interesting question,


              - Oh, and it amuses me to imagine Germaine and Smiling Jack as an effective combination but a distinctly Odd Couple: something of a case study in the difference between a professional Revolutionary and a part-time Anarchist, part-time terrorist (With Germaine the organised fanatic and secret agent, Smiling Jack the disorganised rabble-rouser and jackhammer of social structures).

              The two of them don’t like each other AT ALL, but they’ve done more damage to the Authorities than to each other.

              Even if Germaine is the only being on Earth to refer to Jack as “English Jack” (Which the latter accepts in the spirit it was offered).

                                  The high level of Music ability for the Vignes is interesting as is Vera having Acting 2 (which I think became Expression in Revised onwards). This is a talented woman, and Edward is no slouch either. I imagine the two of them often played string instruments together. Probably violin duets, but Vera probably plays multiple instruments well. This is probably an artifact of their mortal lives, and it is interesting to build upon things like this when trying to fill in their backstory.

                                  Fortier and his two childer also have Music, but only at 2. Still, I want to imagine these five holding somewhat regular concertos and other performances. Vera being the soloist, and Edward perhaps filling in on one of the non-stringed instruments if needed. There is no reason why the Free States can't have some class.

                                  Catherine Dubois's profile says Linguistics 1 (English) so I assume she must be French. Elena also says Linguistics 1 (English), but we know she is Spanish which is mutually intelligible. I imagine living with Louis she's picked up some actual French even if it is not shown on her profile. We don't know much about Mariel St John (who has no Music), but she has Linguistics 2 so I imagine she knows French as well (almost a certainty as a historian of the French & Indian Wars). So there's no reason why the Vignes brood (even their butler Henry Vignes has Linguistics 1) and Fortier and his brides can't spend an entire evening talking French and playing music from time to time. It's enough that Germaine may even bother wearing a shirt for it.

                                  Interesting, both Jeremy MacNeil and Crispus Attucks have Linguistics 3 as well. Chances are very high they both know French and Spanish as well as another language, as yet unknown. So perhaps even Jeremy might attend these parties. (Smiling Jack is even more polyglot with Linguistics 4 which makes sense given his pirate background. He almost certainly knows French, but I can't imagine him even appearing at a party hosted by Fortier or Edward.) All four of the Brujah Idealists "studying" the Free States have high Linguistics, and they probably all know French. Plus both Professor Habersohn and Countess Andrea Visconti have Music 4. So they might attend these parties as well with another two playing alongside the others.

                                  If you are going to have all these people know Music, it should show up in game in some way.
