                Originally posted by Lysander View Post
                The question should be asked if any of us could truly handle being ageless and not go mad after seeing loved ones die and the world change beyond your recognition?
                I think it is a matter of perspective. As you get older, the world changes, people die, technology advances. This is the very definition of a normal mortal life. But vast swaths of humans aren't going off the deep end, that requires excessive social media use.

                Yes humans are social creatures and the vampiric requirement to either avoid attachments or move locations every decade or so can be socially limiting, but this is no different than going to a different state or country for a job or long term education. You reset your connection to the local community every decade or so during your life. The first decade you are just a child who doesn't deal with the world, during the teenage years is either awkwardness or know it all behavior, then during the twenties you tend to get a taste of adult life and wish for your earlier years, etc.

                A better question is how many people can survive in a world where fair play is a luxury rather than a default expectation and the concept of killing you is considered to require less paperwork.

                        Originally posted by Lysander View Post
                        Indeed many of us wouldn't like being part of undead society depicted in Vampire The Masquerade where you are dealing with old and nasty beings who see democracy as a fad and murdering pests is considered acceptable
                        Once again, it's a matter of perspective.

                        Those who are older, have more experience, and been building resources & influence longer have power over those who are younger, less experienced, and have less resources & influence. The world has worked that way more or less from the beginning. Compared to the last ten thousand years of human history, it is a fad to use democracy as a primary governmental style or not to simply kill those that are giving you problems.

                        Vampire society is just human society with a depressing aesthetic style. You could argue that the presence of the Beast and the Hunger make it different, but to tell you the truth when I look at the sheer volume of people who are on mood stabilizers, anti-depressants, anti-psychotics, or require therapy for identity issues, vampire society begins to look rather stable and calm by comparison.

                        If you sit and talk with military personnel, police officers, or martial arts practitioners (combat rather than sport), there is a certain appreciation for the honesty of a fight. You live or you die, doesn't matter how it happens, who did what, or even if there were greater moral issues at play. All the other stuff is just noise that distracts from the struggle to survive. That kind of clarity on what matters allows one to improvise, adapt and overcome.

                        I suppose it boils down to which level of reality most concerns the given individual. Do they care more about their personal feelings regarding a situation or do they care more about the big picture ramifications. For example, murder is wrong because it disrupts society, unless the person who is to be murdered is more disruptive to society by being alive, then the act of murder becomes the lesser evil, and there for acceptable. What everyone really is arguing about is the criteria for who is the most disruptive.

                        VtM gives you the fantasy of being free of death, free of entropy, free of getting old, this in turn means you instinctively want to run wild and indulge in everything unlife has to offer. But the setting is setup so that if you step out of line, you get executed rather than being sent to detention. Some people can't handle that kind of increased stakes, but others see it as just an extension of what life already was.

                                Originally posted by Yumlum View Post
                                How hard is it to survive World of Darkness and a newly embrace Vampire? I think it would be pretty hard, the sun is now your death sentence.
                                You're not mentioning the facts that if you don't feed regularly (and as a 13th gen vampire, that means pretty much every day,) or if you're frightened, or made angry, you may become an uncontrollable killing machine (frenzy.) Which means that as a 13th gen vampire, you're weak, and can't control your emotions. You can't hold a day job, you have to sever familial connections if you don't want to eat them. Unless you have a rich and connected patron, you're soon, relatively in immortal time, going to be basically a homeless feral animal. Before you descend into being a literal feral animal (wight.) I know some people like to run this game as "Super Heroes With Fangs," or as "An Outlet To Be A Psychopath Via Sabbat," but if you play the game as it was intended, being a vampire really, really sucks, pun intended. And most don't survive the first few years. It's descried as a game of personal horror for a reason.
