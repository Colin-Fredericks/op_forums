    Originally posted by InspectorG View Post

    It's not like some newb Nos with a 4 dice pool will try to pull this off with any proficiency. A lot of the Nos tricks are gonna require skill and planning. And they likely won't be trying that ploy every night.

    Cameras? Well, there's a Nos Merit for that, which I don't see why and modern player wouldn't take it. Very strong without being OP, IMO.
    While your points are valid for a PC Nos, the problem here is that you must apply the same assumptions to Nos as to other clans, and you’re clearly not doing it.

    Any merit is a rare ability to begin with. It can be cheap or just too beneficial for a player not to get it, but that’s because players buy Merits. Characters don’t, they either have it or not. And every Merit is, again, rare by definition.

    The frequency of Auspex X to Obfuscate X doesn’t have all that of a weight, either. For using this setup as a reliable strategy, the risk is too much. Surely it is a thing you can do if and when you’re certain about how vulnerable your victim is. Otherwise, though, it’s asking to eventually fail hard, and the one time you fail will already be a big problem.

    Finally, the very Nos culture plays against this. The Nos rely heavily on their reputation for good information. If they start to be caught fabricating shit here and there, that’s a very big problem. And hence Obfuscate isn’t an unknown power, sloppy or frequent use of this strategy is enough to tip others on it even if no one gets you with Auspex.

    Overall that’s a very hamfisted and sloppy use of Obfuscate if used in the way you described, and unlikely to nearly play as well as it apparently does in your games.

"rare" is subjective in this case i would think. We started using them more and more after Revised, partly to MinMax chargen, partly for something different.

        Risk? Thats a naive take. You act like vampires dont have Beasts, ambitions, or desire for power just to survive. Ever play the boardgame Diplomacy? Someone ALWAYS gets fucked, the lesson of that game is dont be that person, and if you want to win you will be screwing just the right person at just the right time. Ever play Poker? Take too little risk, you lose. And some Poker players on some hands assume LOTS of risk on a bluff and it works often enough to be viable.

        Nosferatu culture? Again a naïve take. Maybe Nos at your tables are too scared of their own shadows to risk tarnishing their reputations. At our tables, Nos can be just as ambitious as any powermonger Ventrue or Tremere. As catty and vengeful as any Toreador or Brujah. As crazy as any Malkavian. As subversive as any Setite, etc. One of the Nosferatu character archetypes is called a 'Leatherface', literally a serial murderer...what about its reputation? The Clan's for not putting those guys down?

        Apply that logic to the Tremere and how they stole immortality. Kinda risky pissing of some 3rd Gen Vamp, yes? They did it anyway. And it worked. It may be falling apart in current Lore but howm many PCs actually play dealing with that fallout? probably not many.

        Sloppy Obfuscate? If you say so. I even said they wouldnt likely try it very often. Its a viable strat that can work, and can make memorable play. like a Tremere boosting his Generation to Dominate an Elder, etc. A Setite trying to get the heart of some desperate and powerful Elder. A Toreador trying to mindread an Elder at Elysium. Ever try anything wild like that? or do all your chars stick to boring fetch-quests and seducing potential ghouls?

        In our games stuff like this usually plays well: if it works, its memorable and fun. if it doesnt work, its memorable and fun. Hamfisted? Maybe. Or maybe your tables are boring?
