                                                Originally posted by Aleph View Post
                                                Because they did. You say so on your very post: "The Council of Seven has been a pain in the ass of every Tremere player since it's existence..., etc". Implying that you wanted to break those toys in the first place.

                                                It just happens that "every player" never really encompasses every player, some liked those toys
                                                ^This.

                                                I see a disconnect between value judgments that players have. Some see the Council of Seven - and powerful Elders in general - as being nuisances and hindrances, getting in the way of players being able to do whatever they want. Others - such as myself - see them as challenges to be overcome in game. They exist to be fought, contested with, opposed, and otherwise made antagonists of, so the PCs have a greater sense of accomplishment when they beat them.

                                                If you're a Tremere PC - or a PC of a group that opposes the Tremere - the Council of Seven is a big deal, and will almost certainly hamper your movements and provide conflict. This, to me, is BY DESIGN. They are tools that exist to facilitate the power fantasy of younger vampires getting one over on the Elders that keep them down. The Council of Seven makes any Tremere-focused game challenging, by pitting the PC (whether Tremere or Tremere Enemy) against powerful blood sorcerers who hoard knowledge, set Pyramid agenda, and act as magical autocrats. Them being around provides a goal to strive for (whether it's destroying the Council, subverting their authority, or gaining their favor).

                                                A Council of Seven that is dead and its power structure irrevocably fragmented can do none of those things. Because they are dead.

                                                Writing that into the base metaplot of the game does a disservice to any players/storytellers who wanted to use the Council of Seven (or any number of Elders) for those very reasons. If you happened to dislike them, you could always have removed them from your home game, and that would be valid. Destroy them in canon, and NO ONE gets to use them.

                                                        But I never play with canon Elders, so, ironically ***I*** wouldn't be bothered in the sligthest if Lucita and all the others would have vanished to kingdom come or were never mentioned again.
