                            Originally posted by Penelope View Post
                            Who are Gary and Stu?
                            Gary-Stu is a masculine form of the Mary-Sue trope/cliche. While the specific definition is varied, it's generally used to connote a character that's unrealistically by in-setting standards successful/competent/etc. and skates past any flaws or mistakes without consequences. Frequently these characters are associated with wish fulfillment by the author.
