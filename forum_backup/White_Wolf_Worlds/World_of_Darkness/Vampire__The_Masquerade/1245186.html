                                      Mind if I post a few?
                                      Trapdoor Spider

                                      It's not that you never leave your Haven, it's just that you think it's unreasonable to have to eat out for every meal. Why go out and find humans with blood, when you can just find some humans and keep them in your Haven at all times? You've established a trap for humans. They come in, you detain them, and feed on them for as long it suits you. The exact nature of the trap varies. Abandoned warehouses with rumors of valuable equipment left behind, dying malls with closed off anchor stores, and mysterious ruins with the sort of spooky reputations that draw urban adventurers have all worked for vampires. Obviously, this garners attention from the authorities, but if only a handful of "house guests" go missing every few months and you are careful to make it look like they died somewhere and somehow else... well, it happens all the time.
                                      • Add specialty Larceny (Kidnapping) or Stealth (Hidden Rooms and Passages).
                                      • Gain one dot of Obfuscate or Celerity.
                                      • Lose one dot of Humanity.
                                      • Spend five dots between Herd and Haven.
                                      • Your Haven gains the 2 dot flaw "Compromised", since neighbors are bound to have suspicions.
                                      • Gain a one dot enemy, as concerned family members search for their lost loved ones.

                                      Sleeping Beauty's Prince

                                      You feed on humans. Well, what's left of humans, anyway. In the old nights, people who were unable to do the basics of life, like breathing, eating, or clearing their throat simply died. The modern medical system has led to a population of patients with minimal consciousness or highly diminished mental capacity. Many have suffered strokes or traumatic brain injury. Add to them the catatonic, the severely mentally disabled, and those with advanced dementia, and you have a vast supply of vessels who can't complain. It's the height of evil to exploit those least able to defend themselves, yeah, but hey, you gotta feed, and your vessels don't seem to mind.
                                      • Add specialty Medicine (Long Term Care) or Subterfuge (Hospital Access).
                                      • Gain one dot of Obfuscate or Fortitude. (Weirdly, most of those in long-term care are actually pretty tough, otherwise, they would have just died.)
                                      • Humanity is half for purposes of Torpor length mechanics, minimum 1. (V5, p 241)
                                      • Gain four dots in Herd.

                                      Patron

                                      You dislike all the skulking and scheming necessary for most types of predation. It doesn't need to be that complicated. The world, as it is, runs on money, and you use this to your advantage. You find people who lack wealth (or influence or drugs or a place to spend the night or whatever) and offer to fix their problem. With the internet, you can even order ut your meals, online. All your employee has to do is go somewhere private with you, put on a blindfold and let you... kiss them. Most figure you're just a rich pervert, but it feels good for them, too. In any case, they get paid, you get fed, and the masquerade is upheld.
                                      • Add specialty Streetwise (Illegal Deals) or Larceny (Illegal Deals).
                                      • Gain one dot of Auspex or Presence.
                                      • Gain two dots of Resources.
                                      • Gain one dot of Mask.

                                      Cryptid

                                      Nobody believes in vampires, right? Well, not "nobody", exactly. Know what nobody-nobody believes in? Aliens, chupacabras, and sasquatches. You have concocted some incredibly fanciful identity for yourself in which you present yourself as some obscure piece of occult apocrypha. True Believers are so ready to believe in ghosts or mermaids or whatever, that it will never occur to them you're a vampire. Some even seek to serve their paranormal masters. "After the probe, the alien took blood samples." "The chupacabra jumped out of nowhere! It got my dog!" "The succubus appears at midnight, and it feels sooo good!" "The Delaware Water Gap Mermaid? That's just a tourist legend, still, be careful if you swim or boat at night."
                                      • Add specialty Occult (Cryptids) or Performance (Impersonating Urban Legends).
                                      • Gain one dot of Obfuscate or Dominate.
                                      • Gain one dot flaw, Stalkers, as the curious hunt down the legend, or the real cryptid takes umbrage at your appropriation.
                                      • Gain Status Flaw one dot Suspect, as you stretch the definition of the Masquerade.
                                      • Spend three dots on Herd or Retainer.

                                              A few more...
                                              Mutawwi

                                              You have come to realize that the embrace doesn't excuse you from trying to help others do the right thing and avoid doing the wrong thing. You actively seek out those who do evil and feed from them when they are in a compromising position of their own making. How can someone go to the police about vampires if the witness was in the middle of molesting children, dumping a body for the mob, or making a major drug deal when the feeding happened? Sometimes, you even set yourself out as bait. You act like a stumbling drunk with fistfuls of cash, a little old lady wearing expensive jewelry in a bad neighborhood, or an unconscious woman at a frat party. When a criminal takes the bait, you do your duty by draining them.
                                              • Add specialty Insight (Bad Intentions) or Academics (Religious Law or Civil Law).
                                              • Gain one dot of Auspex or Celerity.
                                              • Gain the additional Conviction (The guilty must be punished). This is a free Conviction, unconnected to a Touchstone.
                                              • Gain Flaw: Prey Exclusion (the innocent).

                                              Booth Buffet

                                              You practice a profession which requires a great deal of personal contact in a private or semi-private setting. Maybe you are a hairdresser, a chiropractor, or a tattoo artist. You get your clients to relax when they are alone with you. Sometimes, they even experience an unexplained sense of euphoria as you work on them, which keeps them coming back. They have come to accept that after an appointment they look so awesome, but feel so... drained.
                                              • Add specialty Craft (Specific Trade) or Medicine (Specific Field).
                                              • Gain one dot of Auspex or Dominate.
                                              • Gain three dots among Resources, Herd, Contacts, and Fame.
                                              • If you get a Messy Critical while rolling to use your associated skill, you acquire an automatic Stain instead of other effects.

                                              Valkyrie

                                              You travel the world from warzone to warzone posing as a mythic angel of the battlefield. (Or, maybe you are unlucky enough to make your home someplace where war is endemic.) You have chosen a side and focus your feedings on the "enemy". You recon bad-guy positions, assassinate enemy officers, and protect the unit you have attached yourself to. Your unit fears and respects you... even if some are not certain you exist. They certainly don't think of you as a vampire; those are soft little goths with capes and body glitter, right? Whatever they think you are, you have saved your comrades' lives more times than they can count, and they are superstitiously loyal to you. Given the fog of war, who is going to believe a monster with fangs massacred an enemy village? And, who is going to notice a few more enemy bodies on the pile? There are times when you find yourself literally ankle deep in blood. Whoever said "war is hell" clearly wasn't a vampire.
                                              • Add specialty Survival (War Zones) or Brawl (Armed Mortals).
                                              • Gain one dot of Celerity or Protean.
                                              • Gain four dots between Allies and Retainers. (The unit you have most recently attached yourself to.)
                                              • Gain two dot Flaw: Obvious Predator.
                                              • Gain Flaw: Prey Exclusion (Your Side).

                                              Kennel Master

                                              Your best friends and closest associates are animals. In fact, they join you on your hunts. The easy part for you is grabbing a human and feeding from it. The hard part is getting it alone so there are no witnesses. You have trained your animals to chase humans into your clutches, lure humans into isolated spots, or, in the boldest version of this method, to simply drag humans to you by means of claw and fang. Kennel Masters may seem like the most spectacularly unsubtle method at first glance, but humans who survive your feedings likely remember being chased or dragged away by animals, before you "rescued" them. Urban humans killed by wild animals may be rare, but they are not usually considered a sign of the supernatural. In wilderness and rural areas, such incidents barely make the local news.

                                              (Note: Storytellers who are feeling generous are encouraged to allow players who chose the Farmer predatory type, but have since lost the ability to feed on animals, to replace it with this type over time. Subtract all effects of the previous type before adding Kennel Master.)
                                              • Add specialty Animal Ken (Training Attack Animals) and an additional dot of Animal Ken, not to exceed 5.
                                              • Gain one dot of Animalism or Protean.
                                              • Gain three dots in Retainers. (Your animal friends).
                                              • Gain one dot flaw Dog's Best Friend. (Same as Ugly flaw, but represents awkwardness among humans and some animalistic tendencies.)

                                              Ghost-maker

                                              Mortals only notice someone is missing when they are missing without explanation. Your modus operandi is incredibly complex, but also astonishingly effective. You identify a human who has few close friends and family. You then arrange for them to plausibly disappear. You kidnap a victim and force them to send a letter to their landlord breaking the lease, send a resignation letter to their place of work, and send notes to several acquaintances telling them about a dream job that begins tomorrow, out of town. You clear out the victim's belongings and bank accounts. Your victim becomes a missing person who nobody is missing at all. Then you hold the victim (or victims) captive for as long as you can, and feed at your leisure, all the while setting up your next acquisition.
                                              • Add specialty Subterfuge (Business Communication) or Larceny (Kidnapping).
                                              • Gain one dot of Dominate.
                                              • Gain three dots between Herd, Haven and Resources.
                                              • Gain a two dot Mask and the Cobbler trait.
                                              • Gain Flaw: three dot Enemy. (Your captives need constant upkeep and monitoring.)
                                              • Lose one dot of Humanity.
