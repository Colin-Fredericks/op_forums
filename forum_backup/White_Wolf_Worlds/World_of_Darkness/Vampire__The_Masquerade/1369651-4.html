        Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
        I'm not sure that Caine being the origins of vampires in the world is any particularly important thing, though, as the origin of a supernatural creature when werewolves are animist, changelings are Celtic Paganism, and Wraith is Nihilist Cosmic Horror.
        I mean in some respects it's not important, but in other respects it is important. If Caine is the origin of vampires then it basically confirms the Judeo-Christian creation myth which in turn means that Creation myths of other cultures or religions aren't correct and that their own myths are just misinterpretations or bastardizations of Christianity. The werewolves, Changelings and everything else are peripheral to VtM and their own origin stories don't matter in the context of VtM. But the Wan Kuei were an actual part of the game line in a bigger way, and managed to bring in a competing mythology that said, "No, the Christian myths aren't the truth, we have different ones and they're every bit as valid," which I really liked.

        It would have been nice if we had seen non-Christian Vampires get to bring something similar to the table, but all we got was the Followers of Set's story, and then had stuff like KotEK which basically reconfirmed the whole Caine/Antediluvian story.

                        Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                        Some of the games I played in had him cursed by the Sun God of whatever culture it was done. I also had Osiris and Set, Romulus and Remus, and other "brother murder" legends with Caine just the latest adaptation of it.
                        I've done the same with my own homebrew and in house games. But it was just nice having an official sect of vampires that had nothing to do with the Christian mythology, the Biblical Caine or his Antediluvians. The world's a big place, and I always found the way Vampire leaned too heavily into the Abrahamic religions while largely ignoring everything else was somewhat problematic (though understandable considering who the people writing the game were and who their primary audience for VtM was - when VtM was first being written it wasn't being written for audiences in China or Southeast Asia or Africa or South America).

                        My hope is that if, in 5th edition, White Wolf does decide to get rid of the Wan Kuei and replace them with more traditional vampires, we don't just get recolored "Player 2" clans like we did in Kindred of the Ebony Kingdom. Instead I'd like to see vampires that don't have any obvious or even subtle ties to bloodlines that more typical vampires trace themselves back to, and have their own competing origin story which would (hopefully) would be presented as at least as accurate a story as Caine and the Antediluvians. Of course there would still be more traditional Kindred clans in Asia, but ultimately I think getting rid of the Wan Kuei and replacing them all with the regular Toreador, and Brujah and Malkavians would end up making the setting feel smaller and the less interesting.
