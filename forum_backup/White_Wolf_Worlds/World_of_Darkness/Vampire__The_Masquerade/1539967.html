          For me it is a toss-up between Auspex and Dominate (V20).

          Both are hyper efficient in that they don't require blood points to use, they have applications in both combat & social encounters, they can be used without invoking a masquerade violation in front of mortals, they both allow for a character to gain boons from others, and they allow for asymmetrical approaches to problems.

          A Dominate character is one of the most efficient starter builds because you just drop all three dots into Dominate, spend your points for the supporting social skills, and then buy the Generation background and you are more or less completely functional. Using just those levels of Dominate will then allow you to gain boons, get out of fights, make others fight for you, clean up masquerade issues, and gain practically endless amounts of additional Background dots once game play starts. From there you can focus your XP gains into other areas because you are more or less done with Dominate XP spending. The result being that this works with any clan with Dominate as a clan discipline.

          An Auspex character takes longer to build out and often requires specific choices to make work, but once they are rolling full force they tend to upend many stories because they can bypass the standard cat & mouse dynamics of many vampire games. Obfuscate clans lose a lot of their thunder when an Auspex specialist is running around, most mysteries are easily solved, and once you get the higher-level powers it becomes ridiculously easy to gain blackmail information. Alternatively, an Auspex specialist with Aura Reading and a high caliber gun is a nightmare in a pitch-black combat environment, Nos & Malks get so angry when you pick up a shotgun and turn off the lights.

          An example of a potent Auspex build would be a Tremere who at character generation put all three dots into Auspex and then burned bonus points to get the last two dots in it. Since Auspex isn't dependent on Generation, they can freely use their Background points to buy a very powerful Retainer in the form of a loyalist Gargoyle who is combat focused. Since both the Tremere and the Gargoyle reside at the local chantry, which is heavily warded, there is no easy way to get at them. But the character can astral project all over the place and use Telepathy to cause all sorts of havoc. From there the Tremere can generate boons by being an information broker, which leads to gaining Obfuscate, which the character then teaches to the Gargoyle so he can be protected even while out and about. For extra flavor and to throw your poor ST a bone for upending many story plots, grab the Oracular Ability merit so that you can be even more of Seer or Prophet.
