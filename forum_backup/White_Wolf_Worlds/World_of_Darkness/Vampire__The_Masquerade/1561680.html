    [V20] Vicissitude questions (flaws, other supernaturals)

    07-14-2026, 09:42 AM
    Greetings,

    For V20, can you help me answer the following regarding Vicissitude, mainly for Vicissitude 2 and 3, Fleshcraft and Bonecraft:
    1. Can/Does Vicissitude fix Flaws?
      Seems to me all of these can be remediated: Short, Disfigured, Child, Dulled Bite, Fangless, One Eye, Deformity, Lame, Monstrous, Permanent Fangs, and the aesthetical effects of Flesh of the Corpse
    2. Does Vicissitude have any effect on Werewolves/Fera, or would their regeneration fix it eventually? Like, would lowering or raising Appareance be undone by the regeneration? Organ, flesh and bone realignment be reversed by regeneration?
    3. Would a Vicissitude induced change be considered your default state for Life Sphere magic effects?

      1. Yes with caveats. By the rules it can't do so permanently without XP spending, it either just explains the effect or reduces XP cost if the ST is feeling generous.

      Other than that they probably rearrange things instead, solving one problem by causing other, which also fits with Vicissitude not creating tissue, so it has to work with what's already there.

      2. Every change by Vicissitude is reversed as if it was Aggravated damage, so Shapechangers would regenerate everything back over time. They can't choose not to.

      That said, this is not as fast, so it isn't a complete waste of time.

      3. That's hard to define. At first yes, the change is legit to the Pattern, but there's a grey area for Present vs Original vs Ideal Pattern, so I'd say it depends on Paradigm and effect.

          On the 3rd, my gut feeling it's that if it "heals" as aggravated damage then it's not really a natural change to the Pattern in the way Life Magic understands it.

          Think about it like this: You can heal Aggravated Damage on yourself with Life 2 (it costs Quintessence, but it's otherwise treated as normal damage), but you can't "heal" a natural disability at that level. Both are conditions that directly involve the Pattern (Agg it's damage to the Pattern), but one it's your default state and the other isn't

          It's a violent and unnatural change to the Pattern, that can be permanent (as unnatural spells can be Permanent), but it's not going to require a supernaturally Permanent transmutation to undo (one that would always be detected as magical, and can thus be Unweaved) - I would say it can be fixed with Life just like a Permanent Life 3+ "alteration" can be reversed with Life

                      So in older editions, changes wrought by Vicissitude was generally permanent. It could be healed as Aggravated damage, both by vampires and other supernatural beings like werewoves, if you don't like the change. If you do like the change, then you can just keep it forever. It was possible to use it to get rid of certain character creation flaws such as the ones you mention. It can't get rid of clan curses, so a Nosferatu's appearance would go back to normal and a Cappadocian would start looking like a pale corpse again. Also, note that there was never any cost for removing flaws or getting merits post-character creation.

                      So, using Vicissitude to get rid of flaws is a bit meta-gamey and something a lot of ST's never really liked. "I'm going to take these flaws for bonus points and then since my character has Vicissitude, 5 seconds into the first sessions I'll permanently get rid of those flaws. Hooray for free BP!"

                      A common house rule is to make players buy off flaws that they got at character creation. So if you want to get rid of the Child flaw, you'd have to spend exp (usually x2-x3 the cost of the flaw). Otherwise the Vicissitude changes would not permanently take.

                      Anyway, this was always more of a meta-gaming issue. How you want to do things would be up to your ST. Trying to game the system by getting free BP at character creation by taking flaws you plan to immediately get rid of is pretty frowned on.

                      As for its actions with Life Sphere effects, I'd generally say no, Vicissitude wouldn't be treated as your natural state, but the effect of an outside curse. So utilizing certain Life Sphere effects would remove the Vicissitude effects.

                          Instead of just making it cost x amount of xp to remove I would make it part of the story. It should take time for the character to learn how to use the power to alter themself in such a way to over come the flaws.

                          I would go one of two routes:

                          First would be a more direct route, requiring a Dex + Body Crafts roll at high Difficulty (10 or 11) Its hard to understand what is "normal" when you've never been "normal" yourself. Removing the Short flaw for example, may lead to the character in question over compensating and being Monsterous instead.

                          Second would be an indirect route, where the character is taken out of play while they work on their self over time. During this time they lose out on benefits of play and xp gained by the rest of the troupe so it balances the initial BP gain.


                          As for Mages, they're just mortals so it should effect them like any other mortal initially, and then require a Time and Life effect to reverse.
