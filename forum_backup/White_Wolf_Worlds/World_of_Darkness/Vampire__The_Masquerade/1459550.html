                      Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
                      Aren't 16th gens essentially Dhampir/Revenant territory rather than vampires?
                      In V20 a Dhampir and 16th Gen are very different.

                      Dhampirs have a 10 point blood pool that can boost stats and heal at normal cost and while they can’t feed on mortals, naturally regain 1 point per day. 16th Gens have only 4 they can spend on anything other than rising and pay double for anything but rising and since they can only spend 1/turn means it takes twice as long to activate any disciplines/heal etc. and have to refill after practically any use.

                      Dhampirs are immune to sunlight. 16th Gens treat sunlight as lethal damage.

                      Dhampirs can eat and digest food. 16th Gens can only hold it down, but gain no nourishment from it and must regurgitate it eventually.

                      Dhampirs treat treats agg as lethal (so can soak it with Stamina and heal it at 1 blood per health level), but takes full damage from bashing and can be killed by lethal damage. 16th Gens take half from bashing, but otherwise take damage and heal just like any other vampire.

                      Dhampirs can learn only the first dot of any discipline and drinking vampire blood does NOT make them a ghoul (so they can’t learn higher tiers like a ghoul with a low gen domitor can), but can bind them. 16th Gens can learn up to 2 dots in any disciplines.

                      A Dhampir can’t improve its “generation” and would only become a vampire if embraced. A 16th Gen has been embraced and so can improve its generation through diablerie.

                      Dhampirs have a weaker Beast, they’re immune to hunger and fear frenzy and have -2 difficulty to resist anger triggers and can never lose their last dot of humanity. 16th Gens suffer the full effects of Frenzy and can enter Wassail like any Kindred.

                      Dhampirs age very slowly. 16th Gens are actually immortal.

                      Basically V20 16th Gens are a step up, barely, from a standard ghoul or revenant and a clear step down from 15th Gens. Dhampirs in V20 get a degree of hybrid vigor (its a real thing with crossbred species) and are in several ways stronger than their 15-16th Gen parent... mainly via traits that could be better described as “augmented mortal” than “weak vampire.”

                                      I always took the notion that Dhampirs and Revenants were identical to be mostly in-universe attributions from guys like Netchurch who really want to pretend “science” can explain the vampire condition. But even back in Revised, revenants burned blood volume just like ghouls and vampires, while right from the start dhampirs’ pool was entirely a spiritual charge to their blood.

                                      The BJD updates just make it clearer that there is a difference between the inbreeding of ghouls and the hybrid vigor (outbreeding) of a dhampir. Most notably isn’t even the size of the blood pool, but that dhampirs are more physically resilient than revenants (they treat all aggravated damage as lethal and have a much easier check for regrowing lost limbs), do not suffer fear or hunger frenzies and no matter how much vitae they consume cannot become ghouls (I give them the vampire price for unbondable for this reason). It doesn’t take much in the way of merits to turn them into what I’ve presented them as in my campaign; spiritual antibodies against the Curse of Caine.

                                      I honestly prefer that there are differences precisely because inbreeding plus blood magic shouldn’t produce identical results to a natural unforced endpoint to the Curse of Caine. There’s more story potential in dhampirs as something new and different.
