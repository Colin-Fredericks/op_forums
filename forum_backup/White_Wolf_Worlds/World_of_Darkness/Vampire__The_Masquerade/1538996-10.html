                                                It reminds me of Shadowrun to an extent. Original recipe Mage made it clear that the Traditions may have their differences but everyone knew they were doing the same sort of magic with different flavors. The Technocracy were fascists and why the Earth was the shithole it was with the rich and powerful crushing the poor. Gradually, the Technocrats became more and more reformed while they kept arguing the Traditions were just one bad day of turning on each other (and would absolutely start slaughtering each other once the Technocrats were gone).

                                                In Shadowrun, it basically had a similar trajectory. The first editions are all about being a bunch of Robin Hoods and angry punks striking at the Man and often using the man's nunyen to do it. It's about living free and dying hard. Later editions (4th and 5th) then took a turn to the grimdark where the Shadowrunners were a bunch of cold blooded sociopaths who scorned the idea of doing anything but wetwork for the corporations.

                                                Vampire has its own trajectory to get us back on the original subject.
