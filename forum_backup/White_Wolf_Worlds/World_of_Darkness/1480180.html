    Merging DTF and Devil's Due

    05-05-2022, 12:55 PM
    Not the first one to take a crack at this at this, but I liked the way both DTF and Devil's Due handled the concept of demons, so I've been attempting to try and make a middle ground system with the best of both worlds and some ideas I've seen on the forum.

    Basics:
    1. Stat Separation: A demon's apocalyptic form and their mortal form have separate trait limits. To preserve this, All of a host's Attributes and Abilities are frozen at the moment of Possession and XP the demon spends to raise these attributes only apply in apocalyptic form. Like any other Apocalyptic form feature, a Demon may roll her permanent faith at a difficulty of 6 or spent a point of faith to manifest one or all of their apocalyptic form traits. If a demon were to be exercised from their current body and the original host's soul reinstated, they would find their body exactly the same at they left it.
    2. Manifesting Apocalyptic Form: A Demon that wishes access one or more of their Apocalyptic form traits, but doesn't wish to reveal their full demonic glory in a way that would immediately attract attention as all nearby mortals are struck by Revelation, may choose to manifest individual traits rather than their full strength. Each trait manifested this way requires a turn of concentration and either a faith roll or point of faith(same as manifesting one's full Apocalyptic form).
    3. Torment: I want Torment to directly correlate with Apocalyptical form abilities so that players always have the temptation to give into their darker side for easy power, but for that power to have horrible consequences.
      • Thoughts on implementation: Torment functions as it does in DTF with some added features.
      • Apocalyptic Form: As a demon's torment rises, they remember more of themselves and the powers they once wielded. Every Dot of torment gives a player 5 points spend on Apocolyptic form features and their maximum number of features is Permanent Torment x2(so starting players end up with 15-20 AF points for a max of 6-8 features depending on house)
      • Compulsion: Having temporary torment above their willpower score forces a Demon to re-experience with the lingering memories of the fall and contend with the weaknesses of their house. The demons must roll willpower at a difficulty of their permanent torment or spend a willpower point, success let's the demon resist the effects of compulsion for a scene otherwise they must act in accordance with a House Flaw(not sure If I should make this more similar to shadow-guiding, the 7 deadly sins thing rom Devil's due, or the v5 hunger system's compulsions)
      • Withering: Having Permanent Torment above the character's Faith score causes the demon to Loose Health levels. Subtract the difference between a demon's Faith and Torment scores from their Maximum health(starting with bruised). Each health level lost this way is treated as a level of aggravated damage that the Demon may not heal until their torment is lowered.
      • Trait Limits: The less attached a demon becomes to mortal life, the less they're affected by mortal limitations. A demon's trait limits in Apocalyptic form are either her permanent Torment Score or 5, whichever is higher.
    4. Lores and Faith: I want to put a greater emphasis on demon's dependence on both humans and each other to reach their full potential. For that reason, I want to put greater emphasis of backgrounds like Eminence, Paragon and Mastery.
      • Eminence: A character's Eminence trait is now front and center one of The most important traits they have. A character's permanent Faith score is limited to twice her Eminence and her unmodified Eminence score is her maximum for Legacy, Paragon, and Mastery.
      • Faith: A character's maximum temporary faith in a human body is now set to 10, independent of her permanent score. A character only requires either of their Temporary or Permanent faith scores be higher than their permanent torment to avoid the effects of Withering. A character must also have at least one point of temporary faith in order to keep their immunity to mind control, resistance to lethal damage, immunity to aging, and immunity to non-magical diseases/poison. A demon without temporary faith is as vulnerable as any mortal.
      • Lore and Rituals: Lores function as described in the DTF core rulebook when demons work alone, but in when in concert with their fellow fallen, thecan touch a semblance of the Glory of the ancient Angelic Choirs. When multiple demons are working together in a ritual fashion(ritual rules in DTF Player's guide), treat each lore used as a Mage's sphere with the same concept as the base lore. This has a number of limitations however:
        1. The Ankida/ Ritual leader must have an Eminence greater than or equal to any of the Mudu or Khauiki participants. If anyone of higher Eminence were to try to participate in a supplementary role, the ritual would be disrupted as the Ankida's metaphysical leadership would automatically shift to the Greater existence.
        2. A ritual's level is determined by the highest rating of the lores required to perform it and the Ankida may only Lead rituals of a level equal to or lesser than their eminence, even if they possess the lore at the required level. An Ankida of lesser existence simply does not have the ability to command such greater powers.
        3. All rituals by default are limited to being able to target 1 individual or object, last only 1 round, and require the target be within a range inside the ritual circle(a maximum of 10 meters). to increase these parameters, the ankida requires investment in the Mastery background.
      • Mastery: All fallen characters are now given access to this background, but it now has some limitations. Mastery allows a fallen to spend faith to multiply the number of targets, duration, OR maximum distance from the Ankida a Ritual may effect by the Permanent Faith score of the Ankida. This occurs multiplicitavely for each point of temporary faith spent, so if an Ankida with a Permanent faith score of 5 spends 2 faith points to extend the range of a ritual, that ritual will be able to effect a target up to 250 meters away. An Ankida may only spend a Total number of extra faith points on a ritual equal to their mastery background. If the Example Ankida only had a Mastery of 2, she would not be able to spend any more faith to increase the number of targets or duration of their ritual.
      • Legacy: A demon may use the Higher of her Torment or Legacy for determining her apocalyptic form traits. Like Torment, a character has 5 AF points for each dot in legacy and she is limited to a number of apocalyptic form features equal to her Legacy x 2. Unlike Torment, Af points gained from Legacy cannot be used to buy "High Torment" features. Points from Legacy and Torment do not stack, a player may only use one or the other.
    Any Thoughts?
