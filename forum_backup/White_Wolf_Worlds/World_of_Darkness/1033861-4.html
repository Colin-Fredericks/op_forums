            The best advice I've ever had about this is to treat it as conflicting religious origin tales. Every supernatural group has their own stories and beliefs and while there are figures that have lived through those times they are so godlike that consulting them is next to impossible and they are likely to just give you the corporate pitch rather than the real truth. After all they didn't get that old by telling the truth all the time and giving out the secrets of how things came to be and why they are happening like candy. So any community encountering another communities oring tales will instantly say that isn't how it happened at all this is how it really happened. Which in turn leads into conflicts of who has the right story. Which in turn causes the communities to clash as the have been for the last several centuries keeping the young busy with each other and leaving the old ones free to pursue their own goals and interests with that much less interference.

            As I said that is the best answer I've found. It doesn't solve the problem but makes the problem part of the story thus resolving it becomes a possible goal for the group.
