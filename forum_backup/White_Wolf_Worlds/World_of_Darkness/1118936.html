    Hiding House Affiliation (House Scathach): if possible, how to, and which one

    07-21-2017, 09:40 AM
    Hi All,

    I am writing as a fairly new CtD player who has read most (though not all) of the books, but has not played yet. Considering a House Scathach character concept, and the ST so far is fine with it so far with our other players' suggested kith and concept choices. No other players have voiced an interested in a Sidhe character at all. I am worried about the political issues of the character's house affiliation, especially if this character ends up being the only Sidhe in the group. We might have a Troll with Metamorphosis 3 who the ST believes could serve as both our group leader and possibly as the 'Face' of the group. I am inclined to have this character run with tertiary social attributes (potentially a Charisma 3, Manipulation 1, Appearance 2+1 as an 'Autumn' Scathach) and not much emphasis in social abilities because I had played the Face in our previous game. Noting that we are using the Book of Lost Houses as a resource and not the older boons/flaws; if there is a book that updates House Scathach a third time, feel free to let me know!

    One preliminary concern would be navigating any court issues with a Troll as our spokesperson if my fae mien indicates that I am a Sidhe. Is metamorphosis going to actually help the Troll in some way for that purpose? Otherwise, why is a Troll and not a Sidhe addressing the other Sidhe nobles at court?

    Were I to hide my House affiliation (as the ST has asked if I would be okay doing; not sure if that includes the PC group or just anyone outside of it yet), I am concerned about the following issues:

    -Any Sluagh; they are able to see through any illusory magic (would that include Metamorphosis above for the Troll?). I just don't think I could do anything to make my fae mien seem more like an Arcadian Sidhe without a Sluagh (if there is one at court) noticing.
    -The inevitable Pooka childling who does not yet know that cantrips to make Sidhe appear foolish automatically fail. Then against my character, the Pooka succeeds. Could Greymare permit me to counterweave these cantrips (provided my character was aware of the Pooka attempting this?).
    -Any Changeling with a Fae 1 Realm rating being able to affect me with a cantrip (though again, they would have to decide to try instead of just assuming I'm nobility).
    -Members of House Gwydion who could see through my lies (not sure if this character is going to have a high willpower; 2 freebie points instead of 1 for willpower [like all other splats that I know of] dissuades me from spending freebie points on this at character creation, and apparently they kept this in C20?). This probing could come up if my fae mien does not look that 'otherworldly' (Appearance +1 instead of +2?). I mean, the character would have an Appearance of 3, so he would hit minimum Arcadian Sidhe standards.

    I realize we could perhaps consider a Treasure (hopefully not too expensive) that just hand-waves most of this away (possibly by hiding lies, or by infusing my weakened blood in some way, etc., or both). Other than that, are there Arts that would work for this (that hopefully would not be too easy for a Sluagh to see through?). I welcome any suggestions, including how to just navigate this in the best possible way if I can't really avoid any of the above.

    Were I to fake a House affiliation, what would you recommend?
    -Liam is a safe choice in that nobody is going to scrutinize a Liam 'oathbreaker' because they already know he is a loser/exile. I won't gain much, but I imagine the risk is low.
    -Fiona could be a decent choice in light of their friendliness to commoners, but the chronicle at least starts (I think) or spends much of its time in the bay area of California, which is Fiona territory, no? In that case, it might be harder to be from House Fiona when everyone there should know who I am and have seen me around. I guess I could claim to be from another area.
    -Eiluned also aren't trusted, so there is cover there, and perhaps members of Gwydion would just assume not to use their Boon on me (knowing it's useless)?
    -Dougal might not be the worst option if I faked a deformity, as I am considering putting a couple of points into Craft to reflect House Scathach's tendency not to rely on Nockers for their arsenals. The faked deformity could be a nice surprise if and when I had to fight (which would hopefully be rare and a complete victory given the House Flaw).

    As a final note, I have not been intending this character to be engaged in regular courtly intrigue as some sort of spy or anything. I wanted to stay true to the House's aversion to court politics and holding titles (etc. etc.) and focus on dealing with Fate (as well as being fairly scout/combat-oriented). So the above is in tension with my desire to not have high social attributes and abilities, but my character just needs to get by/pass at court until we can get out of there and get on with the show. All other PCs so far appear to be commoners.

      Several things here from my point of view. This is my wife's favorite house, so I've had a bit of experience running it with PCs and NPCs. And trolls are my personal favorite kith, so I'm good there as well.

      First of all, House Scathach is looked down on by most other noble houses as being "less" than sidhe. Because House Scathach stayed behind (for the most part) during the Shattering most other Arcadian sidhe see them as little better than commoners. So even though the character is sidhe, most other sidhe would see that person as only marginally better than a troll, satyr, etc.

      Of the commoner kith trolls are usually seen as the most "noble", and the majority of them were loyalists who welcomed the sidhe after the Resurgence. It would be quite appropriate for a troll to represent a motley at court events. Some nobles might look a little odd at a troll speaking for a sidhe, but if they know that sidhe is Scathach it wouldn't seem so strange. And Scathach are not known for their social or political skills. They tend to stay away from those things, which is one of the reasons you rarely see them with a title.

      Which brings another question. Why would you want to hide being House Scathach? They tend to straddle the line between seelie and unseelie, and while they are regarded as being marginally noble they are also not hated by any of the houses. I can't think of an obvious reason why you would need to hide your affiliation. Most nobles are either going to ignore a Scathach, figure out how to hire them as a mercenary, or watch them closely to make sure the nobles themselves aren't the target.

      As far as I know, there is no way to tell someone's house affiliation just by looking at them or talking to them. On the surface there isn't anything physically distinguishing Gwydion from Scathach, Ailil from Eiluned, or Liam from Leanhaun. In fact, it's specifically stated in the Balor section of Book of Houses 2 that they often masquerade as Dougal since both frequently have physical defects, and such deception allows them to infiltrate the Seelie court and further their own evil plans. Now, there are ways of telling affiliation with time, such as Scathach being nearly silent in combat, Fiona being fearless, Eiluned not being able to resist mysteries, etc. If your character says they're of any other house there is no way for someone to tell that it's a lie (other than the Gwydion boon, a good Perception + Subterfuge roll, etc.). Just keep in mind that if the lie is ever discovered and made public the character will likely never be trusted by other nobles again, and they will be especially shunned by the house they are masquerading as.

      The Appearance issue really isn't an issue. Let's say your character has an Appearance of 4. Who is going to know that without the fae mien it's a 2 versus 3? Mortals may, but kithain see the fae seeming, not the mortal one. Only truly extraordinary sidhe have an Appearance greater than 5, so having a lower stat isn't going to be unusual.

      Okay, so you're faking your house. As you said, Liam is a good choice as they aren't going to be scrutinized. But if you're going to fake being Liam, why bother? Scathach may be seen as more commoner than sidhe by not taking refuge in Arcadia, but they are not oathbreakers. Fiona would make sense as they are supposed to be good fighters as well (not statted, but mentioned many times) and are more inclined towards commoners than the other houses. Depending on the character's personality a Scathach isn't likely to want to participate in Fiona revelries. Eiluned might seem a good choice, but if any house is going to wonder about a new and unknown member it's going to be them. Since they're used to (and obsessed with) mysteries any Eiluned in the area aren't going to rest until they have figured out where this new sidhe came from and who they really are. I'd advise against masquerading as them. While Dougal might also seem good, faking the deformity might not be easy and the instant you reveal that it was false the whole scheme is over. You will also need to be REALLY good with crafts and gadgets to be accepted as one of theirs.

      So those are my thoughts. It all goes back to why you are hiding your true house in the first place.

              The situation used in the book was good mostly for showing the perspective of House Scathach rather than the other houses. You want to cause chaos in court as a sidhe? Be of House Liam and start making demands of the Gwydion. Openly flaunt yourself as House Balor in a region dominated by seelie houses. Be of House Leanhaun and routinely participate in Rhapsody. All of those are going to cause far more social problems than being an average Scathach.

              But it definitely depends on your ST! I have a soft spot for House Scathach, so they're relatively well treated in my games. However, I did have a high ranking one be the main villain in a story arc. He had received a vision that a descendant of the king was going to open the Triumph Casque of Sorrows. Since House Scathach meddles in fate, he was trying to kill everyone in the king's family to prevent that vision from coming to pass. So not all Scathach are "good".

              If your sidhe is newly sained and with a grump troll who has experience, it would absolutely make sense to defer to that other character. Yes, a new sidhe in town is going to raise a lot of questions. But once everyone finds out that you're Scathach most of the nobles are going to sniff, turn their noses up, and concentrate on more "important" kithain.....all while having their spies keep a close eye on the character to make sure that the noble in question isn't a target of assassination. Or, at least, that's what I would do.
