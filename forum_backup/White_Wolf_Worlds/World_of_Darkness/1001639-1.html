        Examples
        I've learnt to disarm a guy by letting them stab into me, i then twist my body to get the sword out of his hand.
        I can climb really easily on account of not getting exhausted.


        I can try to dodge by recklessly jumping the fuck out of the way with my great strength and i can worry a little less about the landing
        My strength is 6. I can hold someone up with one hand as a human shield and an improvised weapon.
        My strength is great. I can throw someone up into the air in a single move . They'll land next turn. or I could bat them where i want them
        My strength is twice that which my weight should allow. Getting up should be a free action in combat.
        I have potence and fortitude. I can use my fingers, now very solid, to poke holes in people and gauge out flesh. All the impact onto a smaller surface area.
        I can dislocate joints by squeezing my enemy with just my hand. Use light congratulatory pats to annihilate people.

        I have enought dexterity that I can attempt insane attacks. Like If I get 3 successes on a difficulty 9 strike to the neck, any unsoaked damage kills, paralyses or starts suffocation.

        I've fleshcrafted this particular modification...
        I've used obfuscate to hide my movements
        I've used obtenebration: Shadow play and tenebrous form hides my movements. Arms of the abyss can be used to protect me and pull me out of danger/block for me.
        I've used chimestry on this blade so that people dodge badly.
        I've made dominate commands as part of a feint
        I've used presence to put off my enemies.
        Dementation has made my enemy more reckless/conservative

        And for the most part none of this needs new disciplines, not even combinations.

                Disarming by being attacked could be a Protean/Fortitude or Vicissitude/Fortitude Combination Discipline. I would say Protean 3 and Fortitude 3 or Vicissitude 3 and Fortitude 3, and I would call it Tar Baby. I would require 1 blood pool and 1 Willpower per turn to use Tar Baby. Each time you are attacked with a melee weapon or unarmed attack, make a reflexive Strength plus Brawl roll. If the number of successes exceeds the successes of the attack, the attack deals no damage and the melee weapon or unarmed attack is stuck. Each turn, a user of a stuck weapon or attack may spend an entire action to get free, though they are required to succeed on a contested roll of Strength plus (Ability) versus your Strength plus Brawl if you choose to spend an action to resist. Any stuck objects are released when you stop maintaining Tar Baby.

                                Blood buffing is best when it is combined with Disciplines.
