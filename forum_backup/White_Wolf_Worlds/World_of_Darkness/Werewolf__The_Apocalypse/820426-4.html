        Yes, the option to be any age as change has been around for a while as a lost cub or late bloomer. Now the LARP system has another, newer option to use additionally along side that. One with more options for stories of all those non-puberty Garou running around. Chosen by garou, Accident, act of violence, BSD shovelhead.

        Garou genes and measurements or spirituality????

        As above by Saur Ops, Storytellers guide, page 20. Really not hard to find at all. It is the first question and answer in the FAQ.
        "Is there such a thing as a "Garou gene?" Can it be detected with DNA analysis? Can werewolves be cloned?
        Not really, no."

        Because Gaia chooses. She chooses them from birth, it's her choice.

        Page 204 players guide has a bit about kinfolk too and being a relative.

        "If someone is related to a werewolf, is he automatically Kinfolk? What about genetics? Usually, but not always. Being Kin is not just a matter of blood ties; it’s also spiritual. Gaia knows who She wants mingling with Her warriors. She may choose to bless a distantly related human with Kin blood while a direct descendent of a werewolf may not be Kin at all."

        Key words to me "Gaia knows who she wants"

        The interesting thing to me about all this biting business is that now those who will of Gaia's chosen (you've heard that term before right), are now no longer just 'Her' chosen. Garou get to pick and chose, BSDs get to go crazy with it and possibly turn whole little communities. Is it still Gaia's choice? did she give the ability to her children to do it? or is there something even bigger or perhaps diabolical behind it all? Maybe it's the Wyld in its latest rolling acts of chaos trolling the universe?

        I don't really like the idea of the Garou becoming vampire like in their blessings and sacrifice to Gaia. It lacks the whole natural feel and closeness to nature they have in ...well..breeding. Creating life, in the hopes of continuing it. That and the difficulties in having to wait for nearly a couple of decades (yes, I know lupus don't take as long, you know this, I know this, we all know, stop being pernickety) to see if one will be the next hero Gaia needs. But it does create new options and possibilities for other players to explore, some might not be liked,some might be frowned upon, others mihght be embraced or marvelled at in a LARP community. So although not a fan, I look forwards to seeing and hearing about what players are doing with this new opportunity, but mostly...what the STs and Writers are planning to do with it.

        Is it Gaia's will to pass this ability to the Garou? If not, where did it come from? What other changes may come? What new dangers might the nation face from this 'outbreak'? In Garou Heirarchy...who gets to choose and act to recruit? Will the Tribal Totems be connected to those bitten? How will the Totems feel about those bitten and not Gaia Chosen? What if the Bite's affects dont last forever or fluctuates/wanes/slumbers/remiss' at certain times? What if a Garou chooses someone but their totem wont accept them or another Tribe challenges for them? and that's just off the top my head, so I imagine the writers have a lot more in mind for down the line. I might not like it, but I bet others will and have fun with it.
