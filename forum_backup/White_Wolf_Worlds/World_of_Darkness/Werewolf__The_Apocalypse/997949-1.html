    Originally posted by Aya Tari View Post
    Corruption is different than disease and decay though. Disease and decay are biological or physical processes while Corruption is a mental or spiritual process. By definition, Corruption is unnatural while disease and decay are natural.

    For example, death is a natural process and, in order for new life the flourish, old life must die. If accidents or violence do not cause death, then disease and decay will likely cause death (parasites and toxins usually weaken an individual and are rarely direct causes of death in nature). The Wyld, by creating new life in the form of diseases and parasites, promotes the natural cycle. The Wyld also creates fungi, which use decay to remove corpses, which also promotes the natural cycle.

    However, immortality is a perversion of nature, it is old life refusing to make room for new life, and represents Corruption. Since the Wyld can only create, immortality can only be achieved by appealing to the Weaver (by strengthening the order with the body) or the Wyrm (by redirecting destruction from the body). In this case, the source of Corruption is not the Wyld but, instead, the Weaver or the Wyrm.

    Um, disease and fungi are wyrmish as they destroy things(I believe this is also mentioned in the books as the reminders of why the wyrm is still a necessary part of the triat). I don't know if that was a typo or not, but just in case.

    Madness, Deformation, and Physical mutations are normally blamed on the wyrm even though these don't directly destroy anything(because the garou typically blame almost anything bad or "evil" on the wyrm). I feel these should be attributed the wyld, as the harmful mutations caused by random creation and chance(which are More common in nature than helpful ones.)

            The Wyrm is the Wyrm of Corruption. Its minions do not even seem to disagree on that point, they embrace Corruption. They are trying to tear down the world and remake it in a more pleasing form for them.

            Madness, Deformation, and Negative Mutations are created by damage to the organism, which is a form of Corruption (positive mutations occur when the damage changes a gene into something useful, which would be a form of Creation). Madness is caused by the Corruption of the brain, Deformation by the Corruption of the body, and Negative Mutations by the Corruption of the DNA. The Wyld really has nothing to do with it.

                                                Now, this does not mean that some minions of the Wyrm cannot be heroically fighting against the Corruption within their souls. For example, any Fomori with Autonomy 7+ is probably sufficiently independent from their Bane that they can decide their own actions and some of them are going to decide to fight against the Wyrm. I have introduced fomori to VtM, so they have Humanity and Virtues, so I cap maximum Humanity at permanent Autonomy. I also have been it that Sense Wyrm can only detect them as fomori if their Humanity drops below '7'.

                                                I use a group called the Sisterhood of Lilith, which is an underground rail for female fomori who train their members to resist the influence of their Banes. I give them a proprietary Hedge Magic, Corruption Resistance, which retroactively increases their initial Power Points by one per level and retroactively increases their temporary Autonomy by two points per dot of permanent Autonomy sacrificed per level of Corruption Resistance. While they may not buy back permanent Autonomy, the extra Power Points and temporary Autonomy gives them flexibility and allows them to avoid further Corruption.
