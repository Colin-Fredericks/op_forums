Not because of any change in this person's behaviour or aptitude, but because of a realization regarding their heritage and their heritage alone.

    "Oh, thanks but fuck no. No I think I'd rather join one of the other tribes now. I'm actually kinda shocked you even reached out."

    The point of this isn't to say "hereditary blood purity divine right monarchy is bad" (I'm reasonably assuming you're all on the same page as me there), it's more to say "this tribe's obsession with blood purity will inevitably come off as racist, even if the Tribe isn't all-white".
    That's not how any of that works in setting, though. Spirits (and Falcon itself) are the arbiters of pure breed, not genetics tests or skin color. We know for a fact that there are groups of asian, african and arabic groups of Silver Fangs in addition to the ones descended from nobility of Europe.

    As for them denying good leaders, sure it's their problem, but at the end of day that's how you get Shadow Lords.

                        On the whole Gaia-thing, I think there used to be an idea there where defining what Gaia wants you to do was a more personal thing. I know it's a wild take on Gaia in WtA in general but it starts with the introduction in the preview in the Truths-section where it says: "Werewolves are charged with an unreliable greater purpose by Gaia, a spiritual entity who is herself inherently unknowable​" (Emphasis mine). So you got that and if you look at the "What Do Werewolves Do?"-section, it pretty much mirrors what has been said in the Discord-Q&A - with one exception:
                        From what I've noticed, V5 has its own specific style of play emphasizing the preservation of Humanity. Per my experience, H5 manifests as a mystery/survival style game. Is there a style of play that W5 will emphasize?
                        From the introduction to the book, what do werewolves do?:
                        • Fight to take back what has been lost
                        • Building their own legend
                        • Define their relationship with Gaia
                        • Explore the mysteries of the Umbra
                        • Look out for others of their kind
                        (Emphasis mine)

                        Of course that point "defining your relationship with Gaia" isn't there anymore. There are two more things: Back in July last year when Justin Achilli and Outstar talked about H5, they briefly mentioned W5 and JA talked about how werewolves would have Convictions similar to vampires and that their Convictions would be personal beliefs about Gaia. By October, though, similar to how the idea of "Objects as Touchstones" was jettisoned, the system of Convictions was also abandoned. But another thing that also links up with this wild take on Gaia is that JA mentioned back in October how Gaia-affiliated spirits can also be antagonists to Garou.

                        I guess, we'll see with the whole write-up how much of this idea of "defining your personal relationship with Gaia" is still in there...

                            Trying to explain my point better.

                            First off, I overall like w5 ideas for various reasons and also because I am a fan of werewolves in general. But I am intelligent enough to understand people's skepticism and mistrust. I am very cold about the various editions. Now I have 5 edition of werewolf and if w5 will end up in dust in my shelf no problem no hard feeling I have 4 other editions to chose. I like them all.

                            Why I am saying this ? just to be sure you understand that I'm trying being pragmatical as much as I can.

                            That being said, the problem about Hart Wardens from what I see is the totally lack of focus. I like the overall description as protectors but it clashes both whith some archetypes as much as other things.
                            The fact they are a tribe that stays with one foot in society and one foot outside makes the tribe totally unfocused and vague in its nature.
                            a tribe, by definition, is militant. Not in the sense of politics or social ideas but by the fact that " I'm part of my tribe and we do x things because of y ". Even CoG or Silent strider are militant in some ways.
                            This part is absent especially when we read the archetypes. The huntsman and the urban legend are kinda ok because their vibe is much more rustic and in line with the totem but still, they can be part or every tribe but not as much as the other 2 archetypes which can be... almost everything, especially glass walkers or bone gnawers. That means that every kind of "protector" or "bard" could be part of the tribe, but this helps to destroy the focus, the "why" the Hart Wardens cooperate and are United.

                            I really cant see how a emcee like pack and a hillbilly slasher like pack can get along just because the have Stag as totem. I really, as a storyteller, dont know how to fit in a pack two different characters like, the wolf farmer and the techno modern barbarian that use technology to spread stories. What the fuck I'm supposed to do with Stag here ?

                            just my opinion.
                            this preview really left me cold as a fianna fan from the older editions.
                            ok the point is, you can play whichever character you want with whatever profile, just make him a protector or a welcoming but traditional bard or host, but still. Again, I understand they wanted to create conceptual tribe and "create your local version of your tribe", but like forsaken 2 teach us, even if you create something conceptual, you can put focus on it. What are the galestalkers be? Cold people? Wtf.


                            Edit : this kind of stuff is the kind of stuff that will make people play Get of Fenris and Stargazers home ruling the fuck out of them because , mark my word, their descriptions will be more focused and centred.
