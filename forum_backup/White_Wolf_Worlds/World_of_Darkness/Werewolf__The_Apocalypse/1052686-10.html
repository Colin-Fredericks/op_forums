                Baba Yaga is a 8,000 year old Methuselah who controls armies of banes, fomori, Kindred, etc. The reason why the Garou were unwilling to go after her was that it would have taken such a large population of Garou to chew through her army that the forces of the Wyrm would have surged everywhere else. That is different from having few dozen scrub Kindred killing a Garou and declaring their territory a 'kill zone' for Garou. The former is a matter of practicality (Baba Yaga is one of a dozens of Big Bads that the Garou are aware of and the Garou have to use a cost-benefit analysis when dealing with Big Bads), the latter is a matter of honor (the Garou cannot go about their tasks if every scrub Kindred thinks they can collect a wolf pelt). While the Garou might avoid Baba Yaga because they do not want to be decimated during their battle against her, they are not worried about a few dozen lucky scrub Kindred.

                When it comes to beginning characters, beginning Garou trump beginning Kindred. Beginning Garou are capable of going out during the day, beginning Garou can summon legions of spirits to help them in their conflicts, beginning Garou soak aggravated damage with full Stamina outside of breed form, beginning Garou regenerate without spending anything outside of breed form, beginning Garou turn into 8' tall murder machines, etc. Since the vast majority of Kindred die within the first ten years of existence, the average Kindred will never catch up with the average Garou because Garou will always be better than Kindred of the same level of experience.

                Of course, ancient Kindred are a horse of a different color and, after a thousand years of existence, ancient Kindred are capable of winning a fight against any single Garou. However, ancient Kindred are wise enough not to hunt Garou because Garou rarely come alone, and they have better things to do with their immortality than risk it by kicking the hornets nest that is the Garou Nation. Even if they kill one of the Garou, the rest of the Garou in the area will temporarily put aside their grievances to ash the ancient Kindred, much like Appalachian Mountain Clans used to stand united against outsiders but would fight tooth-and-nail against each other when they were left alone.

                On another note, Garou really do not suffer from any particular disadvantages in modern society (at least not compared to Kindred). On the first point, 80%-88% of Garou are Homid, so they were born and raised among humans, so they are capable of fitting in without issue as long as they do not develop excessive rage (anything above Rage 5 is probably going to have a Garou relegated to combat roles such as Caern protection and Umbral strikes). On the second point, Garou do not really have to worry about getting caught on film because 1) the majority of Garou do not have high enough Rage where they will go berserk at a drop of a hat, 2) there are even odds that a Garou will run away as they will enter into a berserk frenzy, 3) they look like any other human when they are not shapeshifting, 4) they have common Gifts that torch technology or that hide them from people, and 5) the majority of humans will be running away screaming due to the Delirium instead of snapping photos when they witness a 8' tall murder machine. On the third point, Garou tend to stand united against outsiders, even if they fight each other to the death over minor points of honor when they are not facing an immediate external threat (the other shapeshifters wish that the Garou did not stand together quite so well against external threats).
