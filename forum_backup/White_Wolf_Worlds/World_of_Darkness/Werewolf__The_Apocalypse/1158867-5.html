                The Garou as a whole are at best grey hats with good intentions. That's pretty much the unintended theme of the game that having good intentions doesn't justify you. And the Garou have plenty of rotten stuff to them that made them not "good guys", from their treatment to the kinfolk and their justification to breed that all put them as second class citizen and the issues of consent and abuse of power and underlying homophobia. Their ableism and genetic discrimination on the Metis.

                The fact that they create the equivalent of child soldiers, brainwash newly change Garou by telling them that if they die fighting the equivalent of the "40 virgins" awaits them in the afterlife. And the fact that the method they push to fight the wyrm is the less likely to shield any result whatsoever.

                Originally posted by The Laughing Stranger View Post

                The Garou most definitely have a party line. That includes

                "the wyrm is the enemy and their minions must be killed" which as I said before is not always the path to victory.

                "Garou are justified in their higher status in relation to kinfolk and human after all the litany says so. And Gaia created both to fulfill specific functions."

                "A challenge is the most honorable way to settle a dispute."

                "Honoring your ancestors is the mark of a great Garou."

                "Purity of linage is a sign of a respectable Garou".

                "A Garou dying fighting the Wyrm is a hero" which fails to account that said Garou could not have died needlessly and found a better way to deal with the problem at hand.

                Basically, the party line is "The Garou are heroes and justified in both their action and violence because their enemy threatens the world and Gaia appoint them to save it."

                They are, in the end, religious zealots who happen to have an enemy that is actually an objectively bad guy. That does not make the Garou as whole the good guys.

                Except they do a shit job of it. If the Garou stop doing absolutely nothing the world would be worse but by the same token, had the Garou been wiped out before, the world would be a much better place. And regardless there is the middle ground that is "if the Garou stopped following their obviously flawed methods, retrograde society and stop infighting they could concentrate on making a difference in the world and hurting the wyrm in ways that mattered".

In which a raid to Pentex kills an entire building full of people but that's okay because they worked for Pentex and thus they were bad and killing the whole place obviously solved the problem.

                Games in which players "take" a mate by winning her in a duel because she/he was obviously attracted to him/she for him/she purebreed, treated as perfectly fine or even in a humorous manner. Games in which when another Garou dies a pointless death because he chooses to face the Wyrm alone its praise for all the players without anyone pointing out that it was a pretty stupid thing to do.

That the fight of the Wyrm isn't, some glorious Ron-Spencer-esque massive metal-cover-album battle decided with might and fury but a slow, long and frustrating fight piece by piece to make things better.

                    WW2 wasn't just America vs Nazis, and even that was a set of whole lot of gray. Yes, the concentration camps for Japanese Americans people were unexcusable, but that is not what the soldiers fighting the war were setting up. Yeah, Americans weren't the goody good people, but there are plenty of people who owe their lives for the American war effort.

                    Plus, heck, my country allied with the -nazi- because we wanted our lost lands back. Ended as badly as you'd expect.

                    But to me, the point stands. At least someone is -doing- something, even if it is a bit late and very difficult. Garou fucked up and now they have to lay in the bed they made, but boy DO they lay in it.

                    Another thing is that WtA is a setting that can easily go multiple ways, it can be a spiritual game. It can be a Ron Spencer splatter fest, political drama and a game trying to make some good in the world and it all still feels consistent. To me, that is a good thing. The fact that these threads often end up as very deep conversations on morality is a huge signpost of the setting making us think.

                    Please note, I am not defending the garou nation for what they did. But I do think they have their heroes. Kinda like the American soldiers fighting in WW2, or the Finnish soldiers fighting in the Winter War. The politics back home are twisted and wrong, but your fight is still justified. Whether it is taking down a regime of horror (with the aid of another regime of horror) or fighting to keep your country.

                                I'm going to commit the grievous twofold sin of enacting necromancy on a thread on a game I've never played, but if my perspective as an outsider means anything:
