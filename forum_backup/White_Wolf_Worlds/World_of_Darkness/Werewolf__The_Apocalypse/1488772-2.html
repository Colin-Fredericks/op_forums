        Originally posted by Kakost View Post

        Hmm... I always thought both the Tempest and the Labyrinth were more like "theater of the mind" places; also, if they can have places like Asgard, Olympus and Gehenna or Nifelheim, I thought that perhaps the human collective minds had more of a pull on what could appear down there. I mean, I would understand fully destroted cities in there or even fully "new" cities otherwise built by the dead such as Stygia, but not mythical places like the Celestial Bureocracy, unless other things showed up too

        Human minds have as much pull on the Low Umbra as on the other Umbras. However each of the Umbrae is a different facet of the mind and for the Low Umbra that facet is memory. Relics are the memories of places and objects, Wraiths are, in some ways, the memories of people. You can even argue (and I often do) that much of the substance of the Tempest and the Labyrinth is made up of memories, fragmented memories and suppressed memories respectively.

        In that sense, elements of WWII will show up in the Underworld, but they aren't going to be coherent realms. The Tempest may sometimes throw up rains of shrapnel, barbed wire, and ashes, for instance. There will be halls in the Labyrinth that will be bunkers from the front lines or [insert imagery I don't care to describe] from the camps. Distinct cities and zones, though, tend to be made by Wraiths or Spectres.

        Gods and afterlives, even those having to do with death (like Hades and, well, Hades) are the stuff of the High Umbra and they will show up there. There are elements of myth and legend that show up in the Low Umbra, but usually in mysterious and undefined ways. Anubis and the Lady of Fate show up in the Underworld, for instance, but we don't actually know what they are. They could be anything from monumentally old Wraiths (the Lady might be Eve, for instance) to things from outside the Underworld.

        None of this is meant to limit you from putting something like Valhalla or Tartarus in the Underworld, just to point out that the structure of that Umbra doesn't lend itself in that direction.
