    Silver is too expensive to be used for bullets. Silver blades can be created and be used by generations for centuries. Yes, a silver weapon would be expensive like a house, but that worth it. Supernatural creatures don't need silver, Normal soldiers can hurt werewolves with incendiary or explosive bullets. Silver is better, but with the same money you can buy enough ammuntion to cause the same damage. Let's say it is qualty vs quanty.

    Normal agents (FBI, CIA, S.W.A.T, police) will not use silver weapons, the main reason because supernatural creatures is a hidden secret, (the true challenge for garous isn't killing humans, but to avoid be discovered) and technocracy covered agents don't need silver because they have their own "cheap" ways to hurt freaks.

                    Also... regardless of the various books' attempts to stat up such things, a high powered incendiary around against a flesh and blood target is pretty pointless. This is something where over penetration really matters beyond much dispute: burning things generally takes time, which means an incendiary that just passes right through a target without stopping will do nothing more than a normal bullet.

                    This is another situation like silver where a slower moving projectile is generally preferable because even more than silver and its magical interaction with Garou, an incendiary round needs the right conditions to actually burn things.

                        As a random on-topic reference calculation, a 00 Buckshot shell filled with around 15 pellets of 999 fine silver would cost, minimum, around 28 dollars per shell.
                        8.38mm diameter per pellet equals a volume of 308.13mm³,
                        which means each pellet weights about 3.235 grams (0.114 ounces),
                        times 15 means 48.525 grams (1.712 ounces),
                        which means a rough price of 27.25 dollars for the whole payload. Not counting gunpowder, shell plastic, primer, profit calculations by the silver suppler and manufacturing of the whole ready shell.

                        EDIT:
                        Even with 925 Sterling, the price would be around 25 dollars, still.
