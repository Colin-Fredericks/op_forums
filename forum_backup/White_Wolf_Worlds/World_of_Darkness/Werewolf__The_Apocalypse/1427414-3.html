                                    While a lot of people don't like the addition of the Bitten... I think it was better handled that you might expect.

                                    It's actually the first successful attempt to make the "Meits shock troops" concept make sense, as the Bitten (while mechanically the Breed of their birth) are sterile, but can actually be produced in numbers. Though those numbers require giving up a lot of kinfolk that don't survive it.

                                    Introducing bite-based werewolves to WtA is something that most people aren't going to get past, but it's actually horrific (in a good way for a horror setting) addition in its implications. The BSDs can have their kinfolk have a bunch of kids while young, and then bite them to turn some of them into shock troops and toss the corpses of the rest. This actually puts the Gaians in a position where their morals and their goals are hard to line up. The BSDs can flood the field with new bodies without resorting to Metis (the Bite takes 28 days to set in, so hey can even pick which Auspices they want more of). If the Garou don't start using their kinfolk even worse than they did before, they're going to get swamped. If they do, they're probably going to doom themselves in the long term.

                                    I'm not going to argue about whether it is a good or bad thing to put into WtA this far into the game's existence, but the concept itself is actually pretty solid.

                                                        It's worth noting that the Bitten are physically and spiritually sterile: they can't perform the Bite any more than can have kids (after the Bite).
