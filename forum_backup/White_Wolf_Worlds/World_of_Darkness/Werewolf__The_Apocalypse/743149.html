    10-30-2015, 07:04 PM
    What have you done with the idea of purging vampires of their Wyrm taint? What would you do with it? Say you take a vampire to the endless silver night of Erebus and plunge them into the molten silver lake. She emerges whole, unbroken by trial, perhaps reforged instead. What happens? Does the vampire gain more control over her Beast? Does she become a creature wholly different from what she was before? Good or ill, what do you think happens with the vampire?

                      That depends on what you think vampires are in the cosmology of Werewolf.
                      Sharing your head with a being of destruction and hunger that can take over your body and receiving supernatural boons sounds suspiciously like a Fomor to me - and what usually happens if you remove their corruption is that they die. Particularly if they should by rights be a corpse and are only animated by supernatural powers.
                      However, there are sources that indicate that a high-humanity vampire doesn't register as Wyrm-tainted. I don't think this makes any sense considering that even if they love rainbows and kittens they're still animated bloodsucking corpses. You don't get much closer to the spiritual embodyment of death, consumption and corruption than that.
                      But if you should decide that the Wyrm taint isn't an absolutely inherent and necessary part of vampirism, you'd likely get a vampire who finds it relatively easy to raise their humanity and who may have lost the Beast (as with Animalism °°°°°)

                          The description for the Rite of Cleansing in W20 states that it can't purify creatures of the Wyrm, whose taint is innate, instead causing them pain. It specifically mentions vampires as a creature to which that happens.
                          You can think of the taint that can be Cleansed as a sort of spiritual sticky goop that clings to people and things who have been too near the Wyrm. Anything else is beyond the scope of a Level 1 rite.

                          I've never had occasion to really htink about how I would handle the situation, since I don't like mixing games and I had vampiresonly appear briefly and rarely as one-time antagonists.But I think I'd go with a vampire being intimately Wyrm-tainted, in increasing amounts based on age and depravity. No need to muck with Humanity score and Golconda in my games which tend to be werewolf-centric; teh best vampire will always be a creature of the Wyrm and the best ways to purify it involve fire, the sun and separating his head from his everything else.

                              I will be gladly joining the PURGE IT WITH FIRE bandwagon. Vampires are a plague that needs to be exterminated, and practically everything they stand for and what they draw power from is antithetical to shapeshifters. They would have zero inclination to help them beyond relieving them of unlife.

                              Now. There is one special case. Let's say that a character gets vamped that really shouldn't have been - a father, a brother, a wife. Let's say that that character shows an amazing level of restraint, and that the other Garou is so beset by grief (and so endowed by excessive willpower) to actually decide that he wants to cure them, no matter the fact that him attempting it will likely alienate him from his pack and the Garou Nation. That person is going to die horribly while protecting their loved one.

                              But lets say they don't. Their commitment is absolute, they persuaded someone somehow to help them. Their solution has to be within the boundaries of Werewolf (no Mages, Mummies or Pikachus). The way I see it, such a Garou would have to cover the ends of the Earth seeking lost lore and learning of what exactly made the vampires and how their curse is transferred (n.b. this might not actually be the story from Vampire, but something more resonant with Werewolf). Upon learning more about the vampiric state and the underlying causes, a quest should be undertaken into the Umbra to find the entity that has cursed them and bargain with it to cure this one. Another option would be getting a Gurahl to help - if anyone can cure such a horrible thing, one of them can. But consider that it's going to make the Death Bear rite look trivial in comparison. Another option would be to go into Pangea and actually gain attention of Gaia - if they manage that, I'm sure the old girl wouldn't mind helping such a pair whose love is so true. Of course, actually getting her to react would require something monumental considering how much her own horrifying demise seems to phase her.

                              All of these are about curing vampirism. I do not believe that you can purge a vampire of its fundamental taint due to the combination of three factors
                              • they are still undead and therefore unnatural and a pox upon existence
                              • worse yet they will always need blood to keep themselves going
                              • The Beast stays with you and it truly makes you want to do really, really bad things


                              If you eliminate the taint, you will have to eliminate the undeath, the taking of life from others and the beast. The Creature you will have after that will no longer be a Vampire by any definition.

                                          Yeah, I think I will also be climbing onto the Kill-the-Wyrm-tainted-blood-drinking-pseudo-fomor-with-fire,-sunlight,-claws-or-whatever-else-is-handy bandwagon as well. As noted above, purging a vampire of its "taint" (Wyrm-aspected or otherwise) also strikes me as being beyond the scope of a Lvl 1 Rite. (And let's not even get into the logistical and/or practical barriers to getting an undead creature into the Middle Umbra, as opposed to the more-possible-but-still-not-easy-for-vampires-to-access Lower Umbra/Underworld.)

                                          A vampire registers to Sense Wyrm if its Humanity score is 6 or lower. But the Beast is something that is innate to the vampire condition. Every vampire who has not achieved Golconda (assuming Golconda is not a myth) will feel the strain of the Beast inside their souls (assuming vampires even have souls). The Beast is essentially a supercharged id that rules over the baser instincts of a vampire. Must feed. Must sleep. Must fight or flee. That's it. And every vampire, whether on a Path of Enlightenment or on Humanity, is absolutely terrified of losing herself to the Beast. but since the Beast is such a fundamental part of vampire nature, it can't really be purged. Even vampires in Golconda don't get rid of their Beasts, they just managed to accept the Beast as a fundamental part of their natures, assuming they passed their suspires (no easy feat, and you only get one chance).

                                          No one can force a vampire to be more humane. No one can force a vampire to feel remorse for her crimes. No one can force a vampire to seek redemption. The vampire in question either honestly and truly seeks to redeem herself or she doesn't. It has to come from within, not without.
