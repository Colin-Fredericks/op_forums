    I think the Red Talons benefit from nuance, as do many of the more difficult character types. Also, I think they're more identifiable than it seems. The Red Talons are angry. Not just angry; they are Anger. Think of all those times you've wanted to lash out angrily. Maybe just take away every gun or bomb in the world. Or wipe away politicians, start the system over. Maybe you hate the way the system favors the 1%, or can't stand that we've cut down half the world's forests since the Industrial Revolution, and deforestation is still a problem. Animal abuse. Pollution (a literal, real, terrible thing, not some vague Captain Planet concept). Crime. Disease. Ignorance leading to extinction (of wolves, say, as in their continued plight in Idaho).

    Every time you've gotten angry about human evils, and especially when you get so angry you want to lash out in reactionary format, you want to hate, that's what the Talons are like. All of the above, plus the keen ability to feel the world's pain. Theirs is the animal reaction you have when you lash out, only they are capable of thinking and yet feeling that overwhelming anger. They see how the Wyrm thrives in the human world, wears humanity as a shield, and also how the Garou Nation has done basically nothing to fix the problem. Of course, it's a problem that's hard to fix without complete extermination of the human race. That part the Talons understand, and while they might be only too happy to go that route, other Garou might be wise enough to know that just eradicating humans wouldn't heal the wounds left in their passing.

    The Red Talons are the part of you that wanted to see Michael Vick fed to his dogs. They're the part of you that just wanted to destroy your bullies. They're the part of you that hates the way everything has become so entangled, complicated and infuriating, especially for those on the bottom. They're the part that longs for something simpler, like a sunlit patch to lie in, the feel of the wind through your fur as you run unchallenged through a wild, untamed land. They exult in the most basic thrills life has to offer: a hot meal, a warm companion, a green world to enjoy.

    I think the Red Talons are one of the most challenging tribes to play, because you do have to reach deeper. You have to step outside your usual comfort zone. A Red Talon version of me would gladly disembowel this version of me just for sitting here writing about this stuff on my computer. It's an interesting roleplaying challenge stepping into that frame of mind, and all the more so if you want to make a character more nuanced than "GRAR! RIP! SMASH! TEAR! HUMAN ARE BAD, ME WOLF AM KING!"

                                    For the assumptions of Werewolf, not every form of "adaptation" is necessarily a pure manifestation or tie to the Wyld.
