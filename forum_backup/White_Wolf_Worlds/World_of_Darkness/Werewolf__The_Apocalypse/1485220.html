    Blood Cauldron on werewolves

    06-28-2022, 06:59 PM
    The real question us how blood cauldron is working when the target can soak agg damage?

    First hypothesis the blood is actually boiling but the body of the character, may be it a werewolf or a vampire with fortitude, resist the burn. However the victim still lose his blood. According to V20 a wounded character lose 1 blood point per health level checked, so a werewolf soaking the agg damage would just convert them into unsoakable lethal damage du to the blood loss like an hemorrhage

    Second hypothesis the blood is not boiling at all thanks to it's supernatural resistance, so fortitude or werewolves stamina in other form than their natural can completely protect them against blood cauldron

    The description of blood cauldron make me think that the answer is in the second hypothesis, especially with the exemple of the ghoul surviving it by soaking ALL damage with fortitude

    Also does the rank 1 homid gift master of fire working on the blood cauldron? My friend argue me that's it's not the same burn as a fire because it's a liquid

      Due to crossover weirdness a Werewolf has much more blood points than a human. So if you are in a form that can soak it, you're probably going to be fine. Even if you don't soak it, the blood loss isn't in addition to the levels of damage it's the consequence of damage. That pretty much only applies to Vampiric biology, as a normal human will just be dead, and a supernatural will be very pissed off with 3-5 Levels of Aggravated damage.

      And Master of Fire is about Fire. It can help you with the Path of Flames, but won't do much against Path of Blood, which is essentially about touching someone to make the blood in their veins cook on touch - there may be heat from the reaction, but there is no fire, it's just magical blood of the vampire causing a change in the temperature of the blood in the touched target.

                                      Vampire rules clearly state on V20 page 278: Consider all damage — bashing, lethal, and aggravated — to be soakable with the werewolf’s normal Stamina.
                                      Still, Sergeant Brother might have a case. The write-up of the power in V20 states only Fortitude allows to soak, so fringe powers like visceratika's Armor of Terra, are excluded from the soak.
                                      Strangely enough the example lupines in the book have Stamina, but rarely fortitude - despite the being able to soak aggravated damage with Stamina.

                                      So, we can only guess if this is intentional or not.
                                      The argument, that the power is written from the POV of a vampire game and "only Fortitude" is meant to mean "only means of soaking aggravated damage" (which is, in practice only Fortitude) has merit, but so does the argument, that the V20 book has other powers that allow to soak agg, especially lupine stamina, and that these might be excluded here on purpose.

                                      In the end, the ST will have to make a judgment call.
