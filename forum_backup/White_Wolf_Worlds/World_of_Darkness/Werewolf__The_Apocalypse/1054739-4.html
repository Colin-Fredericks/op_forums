    Originally posted by 11twiggins View Post
    The Gnosis roll would stay RAW to make them even more extra rare, but you could say that those who willingly submit to the embrace can increase the difficulty of the Gnosis roll.
    So you already know whether they are willing or not doesn't matter. Otherwise a few dots Presence would make Abominations much more common.

    Originally posted by 11twiggins View Post
    They aren't naturally evil, but their tragic nature drives them towards being dehumanized. Make that true but more-so for any Garou or Fera who get embraced and don't die.
    Fera weren't really "humanized" to begin with. Though I agree their inherent nature mixed with the embrace makes for a tragic story.

    Originally posted by Aya Tari View Post
    the average Fera with Gnosis 5 has a 16% chance of becoming Abominations when Embraced
    I think your numbers are off. I'm looking at a WoD probability sheet and I'm showing only a 2.1% chance of botching with 5 dice.

    Originally posted by 11twiggins View Post
    First, Kindred have to find the Garou. Second, they have to defeat the Garou. Third, they have to subdue rather than kill the Garou. Fourth, they have to Embrace the Garou. I doubt that more than one-in-twenty-four hundred Garou are Abominations, so there might be a baker's dozen Garou Abominations globally.
    Agreeing with all of the situational difficulties and from there I would say the number of active abominations is probably in the single digits (I suppose the difference between that and a baker's dozen is trivial though ).

    Originally posted by 11twiggins View Post
    - They can never regain permanent Willpower or Humanity or Virtues, if they lose a dot it's gone forever and they can't upgrade them. This feels kind of unnecessary in my opinion, as it's making things Impossible rather than simply Difficult or more difficult. Plus it feels a tad defeatist. It isn't a target to overcome, it isn't something you can have any hope for. Abominations are presented as being this sad angry thing with zero hope, but I'd argue that a sad angry thing with a glimmer, a fantasy of hope is even more cruel.
    I don't mind that rule, but in the end it doesn't matter too much. Change whichever rules you feel like.

    Originally posted by 11twiggins View Post
    - Lots of Breeds have "um, well, a spirit says they can't" attached, which adds the question of why the hell the Powers That Be let abominations exist at all. The Foxes and Ravens struck bargains to be spared the embrace, and that feels... odd.
    The foxes have a few odd rules about them, so that one doesn't bother me so much. The Corax make a ton of sense. And I don't think they specifically struck any sort of bargain so much as Helios is so anti-Vampire (by both game's lore accounts) that is just isn't meant to be.

    Originally posted by 11twiggins View Post
    If they can do it, it opens a can of wyrms. For example, why don't Garou Abominations get burnt in moonlight by Luna's scorn? Helios is making extra doubly sure to char-grill every Corax Abomination. Especially odd is that the Ananasi can't be Embraced. You'd think they'd take to it, being natural blood-drinkers from birth.
    Luna isn't spiritually opposed on a fundamental level to the Vampires who are creature of the night? Just about all of the Fera vulnerable to Silver (and so aligned with Luna) can be embraced. Whereas all of the others cannot. Maybe it's their tie to the night/moon that allows the Embrace to have a possibility of taking hold. This would then explain the Ananasi as well since they lack that metaphysical link.

                                                        Hm, I do not think that the Ahrimane angle would work because the Ahrimanes are derived from the Sabbat and were founded during the 19th century in the southern USA (and they never got above the 8th generation anyway). If you want a Methusaluh, you really need to have them come from an established Clan (other than the Giovanni or the Tremere). Of course, an ancient Lhiannan might be a good alternative to an established Clan, but I do not think that they ever got above the 7th generation.

                                                        Whatever her origin, the Methusalah might have been able to develop a type of blood magic that is similar to Koldunism but depends on developing a Spirit Pact with a Gaian Incarna rather than Kupala. It could be functionally identical to Koldunic Sorcery, though I would think that it would depend more on charming nature spirits rather than dominating nature spirits. I would think that one of the consequences of her connection to the Gaian Incarna would be to make her blood incapable of effecting anything protected by the Gaian Incarna (she could not bond, embrace, or ghoul Fera or Kinfolk).

                                                        The reason why she would be accepted by the Garou is because she bears to mark of a Gaian Incarna. Regardless of her origin, she would be a powerful warrior of Gaia and would have been vetted by a Gaian Incarna. I would make it the Totem associated with the Caern and, as long as she slept within the borders of the Caern every night and maintained the terms of her Spirit Pact, her Spirit Pact would give her (Caern Level) in blood points upon awakening and immunity to the weaknesses of the Kindred while she was within the borders of the Caern until the next time that she slept.
