        1: Shadow Lords
        This tribe has done a stand up job of being the ones to do the things the kings cant be seen doing for the Nation. Their codes and ethos can hold and call for a lot of diversity in service to gaia from the shadows. Their hearts like rolling Thunder and their vengeance like lightning cleaving through the sky and their patience and moments of cunning as eerie as the calm after the storm.

        2: Children of Gaia
        I really enjoy how well this tribe has endured with their peaceful, merciful, pure Totem of Unicorn in a society of psychopaths.
        Because they are just as strong, just as devoted and just as badass if you get on the wrong side of them. Sure they get played as hippys and naieve dogooders a lot. But for every one of them theres a non-apologist doing or sacrificing what needs to be done in Gaias name.
        Plus I really like how they've identified one head of the Wyrm as theIr anti, their direct opposition of all they hold dear and fight against in thought, concept and physically.

        3: Black Furies
        There's a beautiful elegance to this Tribe. As dangerous as they are their mysyicism and long established lines of spuritual broods under Pegasus and the traditions and ties they've woven make them as unique as they are ferocious. Strong females one and all. I dig all of this.

        4: Red Talons
        Primal doesnt mean stupid. Instinctual doesnt mean ignorant. This Tribe has a heavy focus on the insight that comes from a different perspective. They get used a bit for pvp and derailing of stories by some in a side quest to confront humanity but when you cut to the core of them theres a reflection of loss and lines of extinction that's very real and demands a foot (or paw) be put down and say "enough is enough."

        5: Silver Fangs
        They get a lot of hate, but they take it in their stride. They get a lot of the blame, but they continue to make those difficult decisions no one else really wants to make or be held accountable for. They have a rich and incredible infrastructure that although seen as dated by most they still continue to honor those that came before them and uphold old deals and bargains made centuries before. I really like that they take their role seriously and honor it as much as themselves while taking the slings and arrows of all around them. Noblility. I really them.

        I dont really dislike any of the Tribes, they all have their pros and cons and I accept them as they are. Imperfections and all, especially for the imperfections, because being perfect and always right would be boring. To me anyway. The world is dying, the Apocalypse is coming (or already has), when will you Rage and why and what for? You're happy to die but what are you willing to live for? Now fight for it.

        But you asked, so I guess the tribes I would least favor to play would be...

        1: Stargazers
        I love the introspective and insight. But these guys seems to unattached to the realitys the Garou face. Kalindo looks cool, but I dont think it really is. I think removing them from the Nation and heading to the beast courts was the right move for them.

        2. Bonegnawers
        I love the whole hobo thing and the urban outcasts living on the fringes of society and surviving in poverty as the underdogs. But really, personal survival over sacrifice and duty isnt my bag. Sure they snub their snouts at everything, but it seems more like they do it because they lost it. Not because they didnt need it (pure breed, strict adherence to the litany, ancestors etc). If you cant respect yourself yada yada...

        3: Fianna
        I think I'm just maybe a little tired of the whole "we're drunk and all Galliards" direction I've seen from day one. Plus, I dont like Changeling which for some reason they are heavily into and occasionally put more emphasis on than other things more common to the nation. Although they do have some interesting camps and I do like the proposed extreme emotional states.

        5: Bunyip
        I still like them more than the Nuwisha and wonder if maybe they should have been the Tribe (breed) that left the world in exodus on Dream and Spirit Quests and have maybe made a return with all they have learned and found out there as the apocalypse draws nearer with Gateways and Pathways for all to follow and see for themselves. But I think it's also fitting theres a tribe that was wiped out by the garou themselves with no good coming from it. So maybe its appropriate they are here on my list.

            Wow! So much good stuff on this poll!

            First off, thanks for the input! I was highly curious before, and I was surprised at some of these results! For the curious, the poll so far is:

            1. Silver Fangs and Shadow Lords (9/0)
            2. Get of Fenris (7/0)
            3. Bone Gnawers (7/1)
            4. Uktena and Red Talons (6/1)
            5. Glass Walker and Black Furies (5/0)
            6. Silent Striders (2/0)
            7. Fianna and Stargazers (3/2)
            8. Wendigo (1/1)
            9. Children of Gaia (2/3)


            The numbers at the left are Favorite votes, while the ones on the right are the Least Favorite votes!

            Very surprised at how loved the Silver Fangs, Red Talons and Uktena are, and how many negative votes Fianna and Children got! Also, I was expecting a bigger Get and Bone Gnawer turnout! Interesting stuff! Keep voting, oh my peeps from Werewolf fandom!

            My own votes are:

            1. Get of Fenris: Absolutely love them. I like their stance on strength, and the meritocracy inside the Tribe, just as I like their hipocrisy in how some Get abuse those rules to bully and abuse the weak. I like flawed Tribes because they give the player an opportunity to shine. Furthermore, their view of the viking legends being completely bogus is highly attractive (although I feel that, if such was the case, they could have gone way deeper into that subject) and their roots on Germany, and the Third Reich, are also awesome plot fodder. Also, vikings. Cuz vikingz. And hot valkyries.

            2. Silver Fangs: The first Tribe I played for the reasons most people probably did; they had white fur, were the heroes of legend and that was enough at the time. I do love their roots in Russia, the aspect of aging, crazy leadership that young pups have to somehow deal with WITHOUT forgetting they ARE Silver Fangs. One of my main gripes with the Tribe is Albrecht who goes all 'I don't care, bro' about his Tribe's customs, where I feel a good Silver Fang player would have worked within the system to try and heal her Tribe. Also, Clan Crescent Moon is the best.

            3. Shadow Lords: The dark knights of the werewolves, the guys who will do what others won't. Their homeland is riddled with great plot hooks with Kupala, the Tzimisce, the mortal systems of oppression and violence, and if you don't like that? Their Camps and outlook are some of the most unique amongst the Tribes.

            4. Black Furies: I love the idea of the Furies. They have a strong theme of Feminility and Motherhood, they have a kickass homeland and aesthetics. I love the idea they can embrace what it means to be Female, in all aspects, without sounding like angry, privileged bloggers. Their understanding of the struggles of women all around the globe comes from a deeply spiritual place, and their connection to mysticism and the Wyld is just icing on the cake. Also, great Camps.

            5. Silent Striders: Why u no love the Striders, forum?! Come on, now! They have an amazing, and unique, perpective of what it is to be a werewolf without a home, without territory. Their goals are clear, their station unique, even their forms are unique-looking. They hail from Old Egypt, they are the sworn enemies of one of the best Vampire Clans and if that was not enough, they also have their whole drama with their ancestors. Great Tribe for players everywhere!

            Least Favorite: Stargazers

            They just don't fit. I like their idea, but they are too different. They fit with the Hengeyokai.

                    Interesting question, and interesting answers. I'll give my two cents, though I'm not from the cut the OP asked (I'm also from Brasil).

                    0 - First I should point that I'm also a fan of other Fera, though not so much of the Emerald Court (not so much into the Kung Fu Land of WW). The strange Ananasi are the first to me, but there are few if any Fera I don't like.

                    1 - Children of Gaia: The only thing I hate about them is the poor treatment they received from game development. The idea of a beast of Rage and Righteous Fury holding to humanistic ideals is very appealing to me. It is a hard tribe to play, and it doesn't have all that canon material to really support it. Instead, you have CoG Revised... but the core principle and all the possibilities... I simply can't not love the Children of Gaia.

                    2 - Black Furies: Greek warrior-women with mysticism and discussions about gender, and space to work all of those questions in the way and dept you wish. I simply love them. You can be a mother, a damsel, a witch, a bloody murderer, a humanist, a misandric bitch, or simply be whatever you could be in any other tribe. You can raise the questions of gender with the most simple roles, because you're a woman not doing a woman's job, or you can throw all the questions, because your tribe justifies whatever you want to be. There are so much you can do with them, that it is a shame that so many players only see the misandric warrior-bitches.

                    3 - Glass Walkers: A tribe that evolved? Who wouldn't like it? It is complex, but all tribes are. What most get my attention on the tribe is the idea that you don't have to be a tree-hugger to be in the service of Earth. And that you can change, progress, evolve.

                    4 - Uktena: It doesn't seem to me as much a cultural stereotype as most tribes are, specially a non-european tribe. Of course, they do have theirs. But at least you can graduate without the canon setting saying that you are a traitor to your culture.

                    5 - Stargazers: Hard to say what I really think of this tribe. A part of me want to cast them as one of the worst. But I simply can't. The biggest problem of the Stargazers is their relationship with the East, in the sense that they are THE Eastern tribe, being mostly defined simply by being from the East, and the East being defined by being their homeland. But if you take this aside, them the themes and interests of the Stargazers are interesting. Maybe, though, they could work better as a camp of the Children of Gaia...

                    This list was awfully hard to create. I'm not so sure about the rankings, and I'm in doubt about other tribes that I think deserved attention. The Shadowlords, the ones who do what have to be done even if this means they will be hated by their peers. The Silent Striders with their link to the Dark Umbra, the ghosts of humanity, and ancient grudges with the Followers of Set (my best vampire PC was Kin to the Silent Striders when alive, and she is deeply interested in the current station of the tribe. She is a loyal Kin, even in death and knowing they will hunt her). For most of my life as a player I loved above all the Bone Gnawers, the underdogs that do the work of Gaia even being poorly treated and almost never recognized by their efforts. Silver Fangs, Get of Fenris and Fianna are very interesting tribes from more than one aspect.

                    My most hated tribe, though, is the Red Talons. I don't get very well with the Wendigo, either (their hate of the white-people takes too much from what could be a great tribe), but the Talons are over the top. Heavy Arms already surmised very well their failings. They're not a bad idea, by themselves. But they're an idea best handled as a camp of any other tribe. They have no culture of their own except for being wolves, and wolves have no culture in the way we talk about when talking about tribes (wolves do have non-innate behaviors that should be called cultural by scientific standards, but this goes beyond the scope of this discussion for me). A lupus is already an option in any tribe, and hating humanity is already an option for any character, even homids. By the way, given that the game implies the shapeshifters being older than humanity, but never gave any hint to how they shifted into something not human, all the stances in with the Fera claim to be completely separated from humanity seem completely silly to me. It is an option for a character, of course, and even a camp, but an entire tribe? Not. Not at all.

The paragraph following the header "Legendry of the Humans" on page 21 would have been a perfect starting point to just leave the concept up in the air; "those ridiculous humans and their stories, they even claim Fenris was born from Loki. They don't know the truth." (Paraphrased.) The explanation of the importance of the Norse deities should have been left simple and vague, and more space should have been given to the beliefs and culture of the other Germanics who contributed to the culture of the Fenrir. The day that a Get of Fenris Tribebook writer steps outside the box of "everything must be Viking" and uses the concept of the Interpretatio Germanica as a means to connect the various Germanic peoples across each region and era will be a great day.

                                                        When I said before that the Fenrir were involved in Viking culture, I should have said thoroughly embedded. All but one of their camps are named for members of the Aesir, or Vanir allies. Their Gifts reference many of the same. If the "evil wolves as the opponents of the people's gods for the sake of the Veil" angle had been more thoroughly explored, or better yet, the entire concept of the Get of Fenris to begin with, that would have been a much better way to sell "Viking werewolves."
