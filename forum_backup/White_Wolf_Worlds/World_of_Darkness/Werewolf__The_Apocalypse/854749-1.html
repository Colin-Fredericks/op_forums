        Originally posted by Innocent Flower View Post
        Rabble- Not a camp? Alright. Sure. Still, it's a 'group' and that 'really hard shove' has to be a really hard shove.
        "Camp" has a specific definition for the game. The Rabble is a general term for the majority of the Tribe that exists on the fringes of Garou society rather than actively engaging in Garou politics like the Rat's Teeth do. The Camps are societies within a Tribe, and pretty much only the Rat's Teeth are engaged enough to organize such things (and there's a nice list of them under "camps" in a few books that's pretty consistent).

        And it doesn't matter if it's a hard shove. The point is that they're not Ronin. They're just not falling in line with the mainstream Garou Nation. The Gnawer's acceptance of the Rabble might keep a lot of them from going full Ronin, but the whole point is that the Gnawers offer Garou that don't fit in with the traditional notions of the Nation a place within it instead of leaving them out in the cold like the other Tribes.

        Calling the Rabble Ronin-in-all-but-name is completely misrepresenting them.

        It was a joke.
        Why does that make it better?

        If you say something that's meant to be silly/funny/etc. and it bombs? It doesn't matter if it was a joke. If a comedian tells a joke to an audience and nobody laughs... is the comedian going to get anywhere by reminding everyone that they're telling jokes? Nope. Everyone already knew that.. and still didn't care about the joke part of it (or worse reacted poorly because it was a joke).

        Asmodai, Not so much a rape camp as it is a really carefully monitored maintained wolf territory. Furthermore, Lupus are only burdened by child for 3 months, and have one season for it.
        The "rape camp" thing is a factor in the logical conclusion of actually making an active lupus breeding program work.

        A Lupus might only be pregnant for 3 months, but the pups need raising.

        As well if you only rely on natural wolf breeding patterns, not only do they only breed once a year, but they only breed one female per pack. Going with my insanely inflated estimation of 700,000 wolves in the WoD, wolves generally have even numbers of both sexes, and average packs are ~7 wolves, you get down to only ~15,000 female wolves reproducing each year out of that 700,000 global population (and that quickly gets to ~1,000 per Tribe on average including the BSDs).

        "Carefully monitored maintained wolf territory," isn't going to lead to the wolf-kin popping out an army of Lupus wolves. with so few of the females breeding. That means the non-mating females need to be forced into going against their instincts to breed. Which is how we get to the, "oh no, not again," issue of hyperbolic stated rape-camps.

            One thing to keep in mind is that Garou society is extremely conservative an devoted to their values which include dedication to nature and the natural. I would imagine that the great majority of Garou, especially those with the most access to wolf kinfolk, would find the idea of these sorts of tactics abhorrent. That doesn't mean that these sorts of methods wouldn't be effective, but I think that only a few Gaian werewolves would be willing to do this and those who would want to are going to catch a lot of flack from more conservative Garou.

            I think that the Black Spiral Dancers would do this, they probably do, which may be why the BSD's are winning. BSD's can use all sorts of breeding tactics that other Garou are going to object to - including treating kinfolk human women as harems, breeding wolf kinfolk in captivity, and even high-tech solutions like artificial insemination. With a bit of funding from Pentex, BSD's could probably set up a wolf breeding facility that could churn out huge numbers of wolf BSD kinfolk and new BSD lupus Garou. I see no reason why BSD's wouldn't do this or any number of other tactics to increase their numbers.

            I don't know if Glass Walkers would want to breed wolf kinfolk in captivity, I think that they would likely feel uncomfortable with that and even if they didn't, they would piss off the Garou Tribes who actually have the wolf kinfolk and so it wold lead to strife within the Garou nation.

                            -The Dingo population was taken in. As I understand it, purely wild dingo can have children who breed true. Humans have done quite the bit of damage to nature with their dog breeding programs (IE bulldogs are a biological mess, despite being the cutest bludgers in nature).
