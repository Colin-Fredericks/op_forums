                                Here are some (very nitpicky) points/issues I have of the ahroun chapter. On the whole, it is SOLID and really well written, these are just -minor- things.

                                John North Wind's Son did not know Alberch before he got the crown. The Silver River pack (which John is part of) is mostly made up of cliaths and fosterns and was formed long after Alby got the crown. John first met Alby during the attack on Jo'clatth'mattric. So his opinions on him make little sense.

                                As well, Alby's whole concept is that he is -not- the overly proud Fang Lord but pretty humble.

                                The metis in the last fiction part, as we do not know their rank or age, seem far too aware of human concepts for their age/status.


                                Like I said, these are minor. Just some things that stood out to me.
