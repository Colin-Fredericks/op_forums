                        Archaic is not the designation for anatomically modern humans (it is used in the context of this thread to identify the unclassified archaic homo sapiens subspecies that we know interbred with modern homo sapiens from genetic analysis but have yet to properly identify because we have not found the bones). Modern human beings are mostly Homo sapiens sapiens, the only existence contemporary subspecies of Homo sapiens, though there are several other subspecies who contributed to modern human beings. By the way, while capitalizing the species and subspecies names are not technically correct, it prevents confusion when you are talking to people who do not have degrees in anthropology, biology, or medicine (which I think qualifies most of the people of this forums), so it is done out of courtesy.

                                    Personally, I don't like the way revised and W20 depict what the Garou believe of the Impergium.
                                    Garou should be proud members of a proud lineage of ancestors, who more often than not, are bigger than life paragons of virtue. It is not as if the Garou had adopted Renown recently. Wisdom and Honour probably were already around back in prehistoric times. So, I used to interpret the Garou's culling of humans as exactly that. An occasionally occurring event, when Garou travelled to the nearest settlement and killed those who weren't protected. Sure, their decisions might not have been that informed, but they weren't random either. It was about their belief, that the land could not sustain that amount of humans, so the Garou culled as humans cull herds of sheep and other animals. Obviously, the practice was not perfect, but source material tells us, that the motion to end the Impergium was discussed at great lenght. It's in not said, that the Silverfang, in his hybris, agreed to a contest of gamecraft and was bested by the Stargazer, who appeard as the enigmatic trump card. It is, imo, implied that both factions came to a standstil because the pros and cons of both sides were compelling arguments. And the leaders who had gathered there were smart and clever leaders and the spokespeople of their respective tribes.

                                    What, imo, makes no sense is killing humans for fun, keeping them as cattle or slaves, terrorizing them for fun. Sure, humans can be bullies and that is a natural phenomenon, but the majority of Garou back then was lupus born and such behaviour is not normally found in the animal kingdom. Therefore I think it doesn't really fit the Garou mindset as presented.
