            Well, it depends on if their research is public or secret, and if they understand that there are supernatural threats. The Desolation Islands are under the authority of the French government, but the only people stationed there are around 100 administrators and scientists during the summer and around 40 people during the winter (there are more humans on the South Pole during the same season). It would be very easy to establish a secret base especially if the French government gave you it's blessings.

            The facility would be devoted to the 'Garden of Eden' project, but the location would be ideal for research into genetically engineered plants and plant diseases (such as bioweapons designed to destroy enemy food crops or artificial viruses that mutate enemy food crops into producing toxins). If the facility is used for military purposes as well as civilian purposes, you could have as many security personnel as research personnel.

            I would suggest a secret civilian-military research facility with French authorization. The base would use geothermal energy from Mont Ross (a local volcano), allowing it to minimize its fueling requirements. With 1,000 personnel year-round, it has 100 administrative personnel, 400 security personnel, 400 research personnel, and 100 support personnel during the summer and 1/5 that amount during the winter (a caretaker population). Personnel are rotated every six months and are subjected to a communication blackout during that time (only emergency and official communications are allowed).

                    I agree, though they will 'smell' of the Triat at the extremes. A secret research lab doing genetic engineering of advanced bioweapons is probably going to bear a hint of the Wyrm. Of course, how much depends of how much the mutation of plants matters depends on the ST, but I would suggest that research into plant diseases would create an unwholesome spiritual atmosphere.

                    If you decide to go with the Desolation Islands, you will be 2,000 miles from the nearest inhabited area (Madagascar), so any spiritual taint will likely go unnoticed by the land dwelling Fera (unless it directly threatens Sea, Rokea will not care or notice). The Wyrm will notice though, so the personnel could start to exposed to Wyrm Taint while at the facility. If people associated with the specific company start coming back from a unspecified research center as Wyrm Tainted, then the Fera might notice. The Desolation Islands are a long way off of the beaten track though, so it might be a quest to get there before something really bad happens.

                                I am not a huge fan of time travel, but given the outline provided by Shattered Dreams, the Abyss does offer that as an option. If Pentex can have goons hunting dinosaurs in the Jurassic period, why not have some other similarly well-equipped scientists doing... botany...

                                It makes sense to base this operation in a remote location, like some desolate island. Maybe the corp. is concerned about seeds escaping confinement or anachronistic diseases compromising the samples. It might even be that their project isn't bad in and of itself, maybe they are trying their best to solve world hunger or preserve extinct plant species.

                                The conflict arises when the constant journeys through the sphincter of the Abyss draw the attention of vile things lurking in the gulf. Given that the humans don't really understand that they are dealing with the spirit world (rather than a 'temporal anomaly' or however they explain it), the token security could be overrun and possessed or mutated, and the away teams gathering their precious samples become stranded. It might be a real mess... and a fun/challenging scenario for some Garou and Fera to solve!

                                Edit: and as far as roping in your players, having strange dreams come to either the pack or their elders is always an option. Unusual messengers via spirits, Corax or even Kami would also be good attention-getters.
