                    Abominations do not lose the ability to shift between forms but they are not able to regenerate, healing only by spending blood points
                    But able to enter the umbra!
                    ...and retain their silver vulnerability
                    They still retain the gifts and rites they possessed before their Embrace but learning new ones is not out the question

                    Abominations cannot increase their Gnosis trait at all - so they are stuck on a certain spiritual level and they also suffer from constant depression and psychological erosion. Their dice pools are halved, and they must spend a Willpower point to play any scene with a full dice pool. They can never spend a Willpower point to gain an automatic success on an action

                    They cannot spend points from different pools (Rage, Gnosis, or blood) in the same turn but can enjoy the benefits of all of them (Rage boost, blood boosted abilites, etc)

                    Now Abominations gain a new Trait: Ego (Ego represents how well the Abomination can resist the bestial urges within it. the Wyrm-ridden emotions), which is associated with their own form of Path, called the Hierarchy of Wyrm Taint - their starting Ego is equal to their Gnosis score
                    The weird thing is? Only if an Abomination's Ego drops below seven, only then it will bear the stench of the Wyrm; Its totem will desert it, but Bane totems will welcome alliances with the Abomination. This taint can only be removed if its Ego is once again raised to seven or more. - but this suggest, like vampires with 7 or more Humanity they do not ping automatically the Sense Wyrm nor technically tainted and actually able to speak with willing spirits (even after they denegenrate down some questionable spirits probably listen not just Banes) and his personal Totem not break their bonds after the Embrace only if the Abomination Ego drops below 7
                    This is a bit strange, but not totally out of the blue, like Vampires, Abominations not necesarily appear as "Wyrm" creatures - unlike the Black Spiral Dancers who always Wyrm-tainted
                    Allonzo Montoya spent around 200 years before even started to pondering about serving the Wyrm (okay he is a Malkavian ex-Shadow Lord, but still) and Pariah, despite in theory could be a "proud Ventrue AND Silver Fang" for example feels shame for his vampiric state of existence and he will never look the other Garou in the eye, nor raise his voice or paw against them, except in self-defense while Poison Tooth, a Lupus Theurge Black Spiral Dancer was Embraced by Baba Yaga herself was "born" to be wyrm-tainted while Zubeida arguably had it wors,e as a Silvent Strider she ended up as a Follower of Set, so she have some... problems and The Black Hand prevented her destruction and for decades worked to end her lust for suicide. They have not completely succeeded, and there's a need to constantly watch her for recurrences. She does serve the sect willingly because she feels it does some good.


                    Still Abominations are undead and the Sun is not really their friend but like the Kuei-Jin in the Umbra they are okay under the "Umbral sunlight"

                    They have their own set of problems but spiritually and phisically they are powerhouses
                    Mentally? Not that much
