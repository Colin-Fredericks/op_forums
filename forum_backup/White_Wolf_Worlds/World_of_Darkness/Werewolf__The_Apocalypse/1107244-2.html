    Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

    Actually, Heavy Arms I've described why Garou society is tearing itself apart.

    Which canon describes all the time.

    Everyone hates everyone.

    You're assuming there's some kind of Metis hive-mind which means every one is going to act the same way. Some Metis are going to try to win the respect of the Fianna and Silver Fangs. Some will be loyal because they're family and you don't betray your blood ties, no matter what. Others will murder the Fianna who abuse them if they're strong enough in challenges or at the very least bloody themselves as much as possible. Others will renounce their tribe and find better lives in the Children of Gaia or even Bonegnawers.

    The idea of "cutting and running" is a charge which is going to not fly, though. Yeah, you may take an honor hit but if a Rank 1 Fianna Homid says that about a Rank 4 Metis then said Metis cuts him apart--because Garou society is all about what you've earned and bullying the guy under you.
    Thing is, if this metis makes and kills that many non-metis garou, the other metis in the tribal sept will suffer from it. That is something that most metis would be very aware, since they possibly grew up in the same sept. There will always be new ones, and if you fuck things up, they will pay the price more than you do.

    And that is assuming the metis gets away with cutting apart a cliath homid. Especially among the Fianna, the Elders would try to find a way to punish the metis for daring to kill a homid.
