    lessons of a Ragabash...

    11-20-2015, 11:32 AM
    Currently playing with a new player who's donned the hat of group ragabash and is really looking forward to teaching others through Ragabash style pranks and lessons.

    when talking about ruled and the litany in a group discussion it seemed almost all ideas the group came up with could have litany violation potential. So I realised I'm probably not the best to give advice on pranks and new ways of looking at things as my brain kept saying 'Respect the territory of another' and such.

    so I was wondering what examples you folk might have.

    Mostly I've seen bonegnawer ragabashes spiking drinks, ragabash taunting proud ahroun to frenzy, or just silly things and minor annoyances (no sugar or tea in the pantry etc inviting in ants from left over food hidden in rooms etc). I realised I've never really seen players pull stuff off, just npcs using gifts and created story arcs to enable a prank or lesson.

    so what do you have or have witnesaed?

    I once saw a very dark rafabash once throw down in a grievance challenge with another ragabash who was known to be somewhat excessive in all he did. So the second ragabash challenging the excessive one managed to knock him out, then climbed a rock face and dived off to land on the unconscious one. Ending in both garou being so hurt and injured that they each ended up with a battle scar once they raged up.

    His lesson being, excess will lead to self destruction at the cost of self and those around you.

    Whether that was right or wrong is one thing, but what lessons and pranks have you seen ragabash characters pull off?

    Thanks in advance.

              Alpha of the pack relies heavy on his fetish weapon and slowly forgets his natural weaponry. Soon the pack is tasked with hunting down a small group of Wyrm agents. The night before the attack have the Ragabash steal the weapons. So when the time comes the Alpha can't waste time looking for his weapons and must use what Gaia has given him. After successfully (hopefully) defeating the enemies the Ragabash returns the weapon while reminding the alpha that he shouldn't forget that his claws, fangs, and his pack are more important than a fetish weapon.

              Pack has earned some renown and status among the Sept. They start getting cocky and begin dismissing certain threats as they deem them beneath their notice. So very sublte and careful the Ragabash leaks word to the enemy of a certain location to one of the kin to the pack. Then leak word to the pack that the Kin is in danger. The pack now has to go hunt and destroy the threat. Afterwards the Ragabash carefully points out that no threat should be ignored and the Litany is in place for such things.

                      The lessons of a Ragabash should be teaching, but not outright deadly. Taking away the Klaive is fine, but not if the enemy would just outright ignore the slashes of werewolf claws for they have soak pools beyond good and evil (Think some Demons... Or mummies... Or a pack of rage 10 fueled Ahroun BSD Abomination Metis... You know, the stuff you REALLY want to reduce their numbers as fast as possible or take your legs and run so fast, you can see yourself arriving at the scene 5 minutes ago.)

                      And they shouldnt ever result in a break of the Litany - as fun as it would be to make your cocky team members drunk and doped up and then, once they pass out, put them into the same tent (best in the male/female combination), it would imply that they broke Litany in the loss of self control and it would pull QUITE a severe punishment: Death in the conservative groups, loss of honor in the liberal ones... and if the Ragabash is found to have played a trick too late he might share their fate.

                      I found an essay that kind of hits a few nails: https://muresources.wordpress.com/20...sful-ragabash/

                      They are not a "threat" in comparison to the rest of their pack as they are the omegas, but they should put the pack to a test so they stay alert. Like making sure that the booooring guard duty at the baern is at least exiting* at /some/ point during the 2 weeks the pack has to serve for getting drunk at the Moot - which might actually turn out to have been just another trick by the Ragabash so they don't get cocky after their last exploits!

                      The big thing: Ragabash should think out of the box and they are not beyond the point of shoring black humor against themselves and take a beating for the team at times (when they are the omegas) - if one of the group ignores the cubs in the caern, he might comically exaggerate and copy that behavior by walking around ignoring everyone and shoving them out of his way, untill someone calls him out about it in public, at which point he can get the focus to his buddy for a moment to make him realise his error before taking the (hopefully lessened) beating



                      *definitions of exciting vary from the sudden detonations of fireworks on 4th July over a texan militia stumbling into the baern searching for illegal mexicans to a group from the O'Tolly's Franchise surveying the place for a potential new "meat factory" together with a Fomor bodyguard. BSD Strike teams are usually not thought to be exciting, unless you happen to be a Pack of Gets with an extra Ahroun.
