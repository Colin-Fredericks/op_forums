                            This is not about being the strongest, is about scale. The character can do a lot of interesting things, arguably far more interesting than just changing the world to show off. But changing the world just by virtue of being strong is not a reasonable expectation.

                            I think this exercise is actually valid and really interesting if taken seriously, as hard as it may be to take W5 seriously, but it's actually doable. I think there's a lot of interesting stories to be told about a W20 Garou transported to a world where the Wyrm won and the remains of the Nation fight to have a less shitty demise rather than saving the planet. A world where Gaia is dead.

                            But the OP idea of a single character derailing a whole setting assumptions and structures by being "unbalanced"? That's bullshit and purely
                            childish tantrum over a petty frustration with the new setting.

                            Paradox made an awful job with W5, yes, we all know that. It's badly written AND disrespectful with fans, dully noted.

                            Disparaging the fictional proposal at every opportunity for narrow-minded self-gratification disguised as thought exercise isn't better or more interesting, it's actually less. As far as the declared purpose of this thread goes, nothing would change in a big scale.

                            A lot of more interesting questions on such a scenario exist:
                            - How would those characters translate mechanically?
                            - Who's the guest? Tribe, Sept, Auspice, Breed...
                            - Is the journey sudden or long? Deliberate or accidental? Do they know they're in another world rather than another time?
                            - What actually makes sense to be transferred? Totem bonuses? Gifts? Rank?
                            - How they understand this place, their reason to be there and the local werewolves?
                            - How they arrive? How are they received?
                            - What interesting goals we can set to this character in an objective, measurable manner, so that any performance analysis can actually makes sense.

                            For now no one flags this thread because no one really liked the new setting, if not for the reasons you think. But this is edition warring for the sake of it, which is both against the rules of the forum and boring.

                                    I feel like, given the completely undefined aspects of what it porting from legacy to W5 or back fully entails, there's a lot of overstating on either side.

                                    A high level W20 Garou in W5 is not going to struggle taking down "Septs" because W5 doesn't have the large level 5 Caerns with associated large Sept communities guarding them any more. Most Septs are just a pack or two and some kin. The higher level "room nuke" powers in W20 can address the numerical disadvantages (as can possibly things like summoning groups of high level spirits to aid them in combat... but again are those W20 spirits or W5 spirits?). But the decentralization also minimizes the kind of impact that can effectively have. Taking over a few small Caerns by crushing a few packs... makes you a local badass and then what? You haven't really changed the world in any meaningful way. You ability to turn that local power base into a bigger force for disruption only matters if you can start to translate W20 powers to W5 characters to start to build up a following of Garou that have a reason to join your cause instead of just avoiding you.

                                    If you're stuck going alone, eventually the numbers issue will go beyond what even high level W20 PCs can mitigate because W5 Garou will gang up in bigger and bigger numbers the more of a threat your W20 monster poses. Recruiting W5 Garou that are stuck being W5 Garou can delay this, but eventually the opposition to one tyrannical Garou of immense power with overwhelm getting some followers if they're stuck at W5 Garou power scaling.

                                        A W20 Garou can definitely disrupt things further, but in the same sense any PC can. Their plans and behavior as a character will matter far more than their power level.

                                        Some will depend a lot on the setting itself. This Garou is not in their world anymore and we've been sidestepping, but now I think is a good moment to face the reality that coming with everything they have there isn't likely. They are in W5 world now, and have to follow W5 assumptions to some degree.

                                        For starters, what do the Spirits think of them? And what they get from the Umbra itself? The spirits could very well be wary of this interloper as what they truly is: an unknown alien entire. They look like the local werewolves, but isn't.

                                        Rank 0 is likely as they have no recognition here. Gifts and Rituals that rely on calling spirits also fail. Rituals in general are open to debate. Gifts I think would mostly remain, but not unchanged.

                                        Everything concerning Garou society is gone. No Pure Breed, no Sept, no Tribe. No, your tribe's "equivalent" is not really the same. It is not the same social construct, just a semantic echo so to speak. No Totem of any kind.

                                        The only known spirits available are any bound in a Fetish they brought, but those will face the same unknown, potentially hostile environment.

                                        We're talking an Umbra that dishes Aggravated damage into local Garou just by being there. What can it do to the interloper? I for one think such a game would be way more interesting, and more believable, if they got a heavy fall in power until they manage to rebuild the context for their abilities to exist in the new world. And the very possibility of saving the world is still up in the air, since Gaia is dead and in thesis not replaceable.
