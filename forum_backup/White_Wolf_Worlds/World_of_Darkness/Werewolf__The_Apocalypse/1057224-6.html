                    - Sense Wyrm is just Sense Bane. It can detect Fomori (since there is a Bane in there), but it doesn't detect unpossessed servants of the Wyrm (including BSDs). Conflicting views about what are and what are not "of the Wyrm" permeates conflicts among the Gaian Tribes, as well as facilitates events like the Second War of Rage.

                    - Pentex is an investment and consulting firm that has very little on-paper involvement with the various independent companies that serve the Wyrm. They are off-the-books cultic coordinators that act as middle men between these various other corrupted businesses and the Maeljin Incarnae - but it's not one giant super-corp anywhere that can be bureaucratically tracked.

                    - Most of Magadon's drugs are legit. It would be too easy to trace negative affects otherwise. They primarily serve the Wyrm through fomori experimentation under the auspices of medical research, and by pushing the opioid epidemic.

                    - Children of Gaia are not a Tribe. I might feel differently if they got a decent Revised-quality Tribe Book, but they didn't, so I simply do not have a sense of them as a culture like I do the other Tribes. Instead, they exist as a multi-Tribal camp dedicated to inter-tribal peace, equality, restoring relations with the Fera, and opposing all war not directly against the Wyrm itself as feeding the Beast-of-War.

                    - Black Tooth still lives. He and his Pride dominate southeast Africa and he names himself King of Cats. While his brutality toward all who would oppose him is horrific, he is also actually fighting the Wyrm's minions. The Ahadi exists as a resistance movement against him, but many who may hate him are reluctant to throw their support behind it because he is seen as an effective commander against the Wyrm.

                    - Yava are largely metaphoric (I have a list of what they all actually mean), but work in many ways like "True Names" of the Tribe. If a Bastet knows their Tribe's Yava they are virtually immune to mind-reading and mental-influence supernatural powers from someone who does not know their Yava. If someone discovers a Tribe's Yava, members of that Tribe can not even exert the normal defensive rolls or willpower expenditures against mind-reading or supernatural mental-influences.

                    - The Silver Crown is still missing (I'm thinking of throwing the Tribes into chaos via the "Rightful King" being revealed to be a Metis); Jonas Albrecht does not exist as an NPC (he's clearly someone's PC, as opposed to characters like Konetziko who comes of more as an interesting plot hook for pcs to interact with).

                    - "Lunatics" do actually go through the First Change, they just completely reject their nature, and as a result cannot control their shifting, generally only doing it in Frenzy under their Auspice moon. They are in short Classic Hollywood Werewolves.

                    - The Beast Courts are less idyllic. After all, look at the ecological disaster that is modern China - clearly not a sign they have their shit together. They are very hierarchical, and the survival of the Zhong Lung is a closely guarded secret. Generally, the Nezumi, Tengu, and any Bagheera or Garou who join have no idea, with the upper ranks of the Courts they're aware of being dominated by Khan, Same-Bito, and Kitsune, who in turn are aware of the Zhong Lung above them. Naga keep to the shadows and pass themselves off as Zhong Lung to the upper Courts when they have to.

                    -Normal Ananasi exist in East Asia and obviously hate the Kumo.

                    - (long list of what seem to be ParaWolf imposed edits on Changing Ways to ignore...)
