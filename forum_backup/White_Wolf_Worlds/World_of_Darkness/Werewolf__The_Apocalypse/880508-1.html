                                Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post
                                curious something I asked somewhere else I didnt see an answer, idea of what a garou or kin lawyer, can say to an imminent domain claim on a septs lands.
                                Depends on the situation and the area, currently in my state there is a pipeline that is being proposed. The survey company was caught on private land twice. The pipeline is being sold that only public land will be used. If suddenly this becomes seizing property to build the pipeline then the lawsuits will start. There is a property near me that was seized by imminent domain to expand a highway but in fact was going to be used as a massive roadstop and plaza. The court case is still happening as the state wants to sell the land to a developer. A developer that could not buy the land as it was part of a farm heritage trust. Imminent domain laws generally have to have a clear purpose: building a road, revitalizing a blighted area, or serving the public interest.
