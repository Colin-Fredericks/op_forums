                                            Returning to the OP topic. Here is how I imagine the skinwalker's Homeland to look like and originate. I can be a little verbose... overwordy so please forgive me.

                                            This assumes that Homelands are distinct and separate from a spirit's realm.

                                            The spirits in the earliest nights guided the kinfolk who were skin dancers to the homeland of their tribe. The Garou, however, even in death held hatred for the skinwalkers whom they blamed for the murder and betrayal of their family, tribe and nation. The skinwalkers were hounded out of the homeland of their people. Sometimes the skinwalkers were able to carry off a piece of their homeland with them. These early spirits somehow found their way to each other, either guided by Minotaur, Owl or by chance. In a stable Pocket realm, shapeless and barren, the realm began to take shape with the stolen tidbits of other Homelands. For the longest time the realm remained sparse, almost torturous in its emptiness.

                                            Over time the Skinwalkers began to fill their homeland with purpose. The Skinwalkers developed rituals and quests which allowed them to sacrifice Caerns or significant spiritual places and items which would then ascend (descend) to skinwalker's pocket realm. Some sacrifices were the holy places of the tribes. Some skinwalkers sacrificed Spirals to remove them from the material world. Most fetishes and places, however were sacrificed in spite. As the tribe progressed and realized they were a tribe, thanks to modern communication technology, potential Elders were often given the Quest to steal directly from another nation's homeland within the umbra as a challenge for Elder Rank. The reasons could be many but the results would remain the same. The Skinwalker homeland grew into a patchwork maze made of many varying elements. Patches of dessert abutted deep dark forests. Tundra formed cliffs around deep chasms with verdant dangerous rivers below. Fetishes and Talens lay scattered in the dens of predators or created walls in a maze. This is how the Skinwalker homeland came to be.

                                            I wonder what Chiminage Minotaur paid to owl to lead his people here or if he circumvented Owl and found some other means to collect his people.

                                            ​Just my thoughts.
