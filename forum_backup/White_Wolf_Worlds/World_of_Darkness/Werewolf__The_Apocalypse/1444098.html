    Moon Paths, Moon Glows, and Penumbral Hazards

    04-15-2021, 08:17 PM
    I've been completely neglecting something in my chronicles. There is a talen called a Moon Glow which protects umbral travelers from incidental umbral hazards (like hostile spirits attacking the Garou). I've never actually used this, but now want to make sure it is useful. However, in order to do so, I have to think of incidental umbral hazards that would normally happen. Because if I don't include them in the game, there's no benefit to using the talen.

    I don't normally have such hostile spirits unless the Garou intend to attack something in the Umbra. So while I might use Moon Glow to allow the PCs to avoid encounters until they reach their destination, it still seems like I am not having enough potential danger. So I wanted to research the description of the Penumbra and Umbral travel in more detail to see if I am neglecting anything.

    Because a Moon Glow is created by binding a Lune, I reasoned the reason the Moon Glow works is probably because other spirits think you are a Lune, and they are choosing to avoid interaction with Lunes. I remembered something like that in the rules. So I found it. That's found in the section concerning Moon Paths. We're told Moon Paths are defended by Lunes during the full moon, and that hostile spirits stay out of reach of them, just beyond sight (they gather because they can see the Moon Path and are hoping any Garou would foolishly step away from it, but can't directly attack the Moon Path because the Lunes are too powerful). Moon Paths become more dangerous in other phases of the moon, and are very dangerous during the new moon because there's no Lunes guarding one. So that's exactly what I am looking for.

    However, I just realized something. At no point does the book actually tell me what Moon Paths connect. I know a Moon Bridge connects two caerns. I know you can walk through the Penumbra like normal. I know Gates exist that can permanently connect a point in the Umbra to another point in the Umbra. I know spirit airts are tracks made by the travel of spirits, acting like deer trails in a forest that can be followed. And I know that quests to the Near Realms involve lots of esoteric travel finding the right spiritual correspondences that lead you deeper and deeper into the umbra.

    But I don't know what Moon Paths actually do. The book says they exist as shafts of moonlight in the mists of the Penumbra, and that entering their light allows you to travel on the moonbeam. But nothing else is said about where they lead.

    Are they simply a Storyteller device to provide a shortcut to some destination? Can you use them to travel to someplace other than the Penumbra? How does one know where the Moon Path would take you? Can you determine where you go, like pushing buttons on an elevator to select your floor, or is the destination pre-determined, or is it completely random?

    I searched online to learn more, but all references simply repeat the information in the corebook and Umbra.

      In our LARP it works like this:
      If activated a Moon Glow Talen will emit moonlight. This has two effects. First, all observers in some (undefined) distance will see the light and naturally assume a lune spirit is on the move. This will make most predatory gaian sprits think twice before approaching/attacking the group. It won't have any effect on spirits that would naturally attack a lune, like powerful banes, or affect any guardians who guard a specific place and are approached by the group that carries the moon glow.
      The second effect is, that the light emitted is considered moon light, and therefore makes the moon path reflect it in the vicinity. Normally, a moon path is best visible at full moon, and nigh invisible at new moon. But it is there, and the light from the Moon Glow will make it visible again. Since Moon Paths are stable, usually hazard free, paths through the penumbra, near umbra and realms, this makes for a relatively safe travel, even when the moon phase wouldn't allow it, or in areas where the moon isn't permanently visible.

                                          3) Travel through the metaphorical steps of your journey, in mist or shadow, until you find the entrance or Pericarp of the Realm you were seeking (assuming you succeed, the Umbra is a messy place).

A Moon Path "hidden" in these indetermediate Umbral mists works better, than one in the Penumbra whose presence should be well known.

                                              1) While a Moon Glow talen may be useful in the Penumbra, it's real purpose is to protect Garou on one of these extended Umbral voyages.

                                              2) In terms of "dire spirits" that threaten the Moon Paths; these spirits - whether they be Banes or other hostile spirits - are more likely some kind of spirit "native" to this interdimensional realm rather than the typical spirits found in the Penumbra. Such Penumbral spirits do travel through these intermediate mists (either for shortcuts between the Penumbra, Domains, or to one of the Near Realms) because they produce spirit airts. But it might be best to create brand new spirits that occupy these mists. It would help distinguish this "layer" of the Umbra, and define the typical menaces that a Moon Glow protects.
