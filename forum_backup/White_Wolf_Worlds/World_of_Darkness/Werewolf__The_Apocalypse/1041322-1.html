                                                    Perhaps it was their mistreatment of the Homid Ratkin and Human Kinfolk? Anyway, the strange thing about the Ratkin is that their Kinfolk do not directly effect their population but, unlike the Corax, it is the Kinfolk who sacrifice to become Ratkin. When 90% are potentially killed by the Birthing Plague, 'reproductive' strategy becomes important. If you go with a K strategy, you select the best Kinfolk, with the highest Stamina, so they have a good chance of surviving even if they are rejected by the Birthing Plague. If you go with an r strategy, you do not care and you let 90% of the Kinfolk die.

                                                    For Nests that go with K, you have low Ratkin population, but you should get better individual training. For Nests that go r strategy, you have high Ratkin populations, but you have poorer individual training. I think that default would be strong r, so I would give beginning Ratkin from strong-K Nests large bonuses to reflect the differences (+1/1/1 Attributes, +2/2/2 Abilitues, +3 Level One Gifts, and +6 Freebie Points). It would be the difference between 'lots of ninja' and 'one ninja'.

                                                        90% of Kin don't die from the Birthing Plague. Even in the old setup, they didn't. But especially in the new setup (as of W20). This is the System now: "the ritemaster rolls Wits + Rituals (difficulty 7). The victim must make a Stamina roll with a difficulty equal to (10 – the ritemaster’s successes) to survive the hallucinations, visions, and fevers of the Birthing Plague."
                                                        So lets assume the Ritesmaster gets on average 3 successes. That means the Kin rolls stamina at dif 7 to survive. With a Stamina of 2, that leaves the kin a 56% chance of success (not dying). If you go with the Ritesmaster getting 2 successes instead then the chance of a 2 Stamina Kin passing the roll is still 46%.
                                                        Now the chances of the Kin coming out of the other side as a Ratkin are 10-20% based on breed. But all in all I think the actual attrition rate is much lower than 90%.

There’s a lot of those half assed black birds perched all over the damn place squawking bullshit at each other and causing a racket…...

He continues to rummage a gold nugget here and a gold nugget there and sets himself up with a round of whiskey shots to bless his new home.
