    How would Garou Law punish rape committed *upon* Metis? (Heavy Issue)

    07-01-2017, 10:34 AM
    "Garou Shalt Not Mate With Garou" was clawed/chiseled into the First Tablets of Litany to ultimately prevent the creation of Metis, convoluted philosophical justifications to condemn lovemaking with contraceptives between Garou notwithstanding.

    Now, herein lies the dilemma.

    War Rape amongst Garou do unfortunately happen (however rare), and unfortunate "children of violence" do frequently result. Naturally, the worthless beast responsible is given a jolly-good-castrating if they are allowed to live at all, with the poor mother STILL bearing the stigma of being a "Charach"

    But what if the victim in question is a Metis, from whom NO new distorted/disabled/disgraceful monster will EVER be born in spite of the rape, AND already being a second-class-citizen in Garou Society, on top of that?

    Naturally, The Black Furies would tear the brute apart and mail each piece to known rapists in the Garou nation as a warning. A Metis Sister is STILL a Sister.

    I would imagine however, that the alcoholic whoremongering-hypocrites of the Fianna would be MOST lenient in sentencing the rapist, ESPECIALLY if he too is Fianna. And if the poor lass herself is Fianna? Why, those child-abusing leprechauns would more than likely give the rapist a slap on the wrist and let bygones be bygones. After all, to them the Metis are not even living things, much less their proudly-trumpeted "beloved family and kin", and what happened would likely be regarded as little worse the glorified masturbation. "No Metis, No Fuss; have a pint of Guiness down at the pub with us Laddy, we all slip up sometimes." (SPITS in contempt)

    Sorry, had to get that off my chest: I REALLY despise trash who love to sleep around but are not willing to love, care for and raise the consequences of said sleeping around.

    Long sentence short:

    1) Would rape committed against Metis, who themselves cannot bear distorted/disgraceful children, not be judged as harshly as a result in Garou Law?

    2) The Metis herself would probably be treated/hounded/disgraced worse than her rapist, wouldn't she? Considering the already-disgraceful circumstances of her very existence.

    3) Or maybe, just maybe, would her family and kinfolk love her and defend her honor, even if the Litany Breaking committed against her would not result in what the Law was written to prevent?

    4) How would the other 11 tribes handles this thorny dilemma?

    5) Are there ANY such things as Fianna who would show respect and kindness to a Metis, for that matter?

    Sigh... the things you think about when you are morbidly depressed.
