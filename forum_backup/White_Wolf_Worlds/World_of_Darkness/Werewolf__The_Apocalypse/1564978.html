    So how would you feel if the hypothetical W6 abandon a fixed setting?

    10-26-2026, 09:03 AM
    So let's assume that WoD 6e survived past M&C 6. And White Wolf subsequently decided they should take a different approach please everyone for (or maybe shortly before) W6 rather than V6 style Canon reset.

    Basically White Wolf have w6 discard with any pretense of a fixed setting/metaplot to a even greater extent than the heavy pared back setting of W5. Somewhat like the later books in the chronicles of darkness.

    Though maybe a bit of mechanical framework and genral organisation so the ST has an easier time to set up they own version of WtA.

    An example writing up a series of presets on the default state of the garou nation (Mainly weather it's fallen or not) and giving tribes a of alternative states (with the cult being a extreme deviation) connected via a flow chart akin to HOI focus parths.

    Effectively making the W6 source book into a narrative Lego set for a ST to put together rather than a pre-made setting primer. With supplements adding more pieces to the lego set such as going into detail on the alternative parths that the tribes can take and more setting elements like the fera.

    Like would this be a good idea for W6?
