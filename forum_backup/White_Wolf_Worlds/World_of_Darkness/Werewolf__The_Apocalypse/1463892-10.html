                            I'm disabled and I'm pissed that they're throwing out the Crinos-born. Instead of retconning the entire history of the game and making it damn-near impossible to play a disabled character now, they should have fixed the concept. Get rid of the awful racist name. Give them Disabilities instead of "deformities" and don't make "monstrous" or "unspeakably hideous" one of them. Emphasize that there are more and more septs and Garou that are more accepting and that you can play in an accepting sept if your players don't want to deal with IC abuse. Take the awesome ideas in Guardians of the Caerns and emphasize those: that the Crinos-born are sterile only to prevent Garou from abandoning their Kinfolk and going off the rails (and ALL Fera have limitations to breeding without their Kinfolk, even the Naga and Kitsune), but their disabilities are not the "curse" the Garou assume they are. Emphasize how all disabilities are socially constructed in the first place. The Crinos-born Bastet aren't considered cursed because they have a birthright of incredibly powerful Gifts which no other Bastet can copy. They get this because they are closer to Gaia. Give the same to Garou Crinos-born, maybe say they were robbed of their birthright somehow and are now reclaiming it in the eve of the Apocalypse. Make it clear that the "curse" is made up by the Garou and that there's nothing whatsoever "wrong" with the Crinos-born and they're not one bit less capable.

                            My preferred interpretation is tied to the fact that the Garou did not have any Crinos-births until they started the War of Rage. They started genocide against other Changing-Breeds who were of Gaia, because they looked different and had different abilities and ways of serving Gaia. So Gaia gave them Garou who were 100% Garou and 100% capable of fighting, but simply looked or acted a little different. Garou who could never be gotten rid of even with genocide, because more would always be born. Until the Garou learn to accept their own children as equals, they'll never learn to accept the Fera as equals. It was meant to teach them compassion and acceptance for diversity and different abilities, to teach them the value of ALL of Gaia's creations, but many of them fucked it up in their hubris and came to the opposite conclusion.

                            Just eliminating the entire PC type is just spitting in the face of any disabled player who wanted to play one, like me.

                            And eliminating the Native American tribes and the Umbra is just beyond the pale horrible. It's clear these writers don't give a crap about the game and want to turn it into something unrecognizably different. I'd support renaming the Native American tribes, but not just declaring that you can't play a Native werewolf anymore. (Saying all Native Americans are one tribe instead because they're "all the same culture" and have no diversity is racist BS and almost as awful as eliminating them from the game.)



                            If they really feel that they need to separate disabled characters from Garou-on-Garou breeding, they could make a much smaller retcon: the first Crinos-born was also the first born with an obvious disability. Ever since then Crinos-born exist and are sterile, but to prevent asshats from breeding an army of Crinos-born cannon-fodder only 10% of Garou-Garou matings result in birth. Also, randomly some Garou births (of any Breed) have obvious disabilities, unrelated to parental behavior. Then Gaia's lesson can't be ignored even if nobody broke the Litany ever again. Then players like me could play be a disabled Homid or Lupus, too.

                            This would require getting rid of the awesome-sauce Gifts Bastet Crinos-born get from being closer to Gaia, though, and that would make me sad. Bastet Crinos-born are awesome and I'd like to play one.



                            I highly doubt any of these decisions have anything to do with trying to avoid making minority players feel bad while playing. Remember the anti-trans hate that Paradox forced Onyx Path to add to Changing Ways?

                            Making oppressed minorities invisible and unplayable is just one more way to take a shit on us.

                                    The Nagah kind of don't have much of anything forcing them to stick with kin.
