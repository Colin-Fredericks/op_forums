    Primal Urge as a hability in Apocalypse was a mistake, IMHO, but once it is there, I agree with the uses listed in W20.

    In this case, though, I don't use it for detecting vampires simple to keep things interesting. First because it doesn't sound right to me for a splat having the hability to know without effort about the presence of others. There are gifts to do that, if you want. Second it serves precisely to give more value to point-investment and action-investment on knowing things. Third giving this for free kills the mistery.

    Already played with a ST that used Primal Urge to give EVERYTHING to the werewolves. Theoreticaly it was a crossover game, but playing a vampire with this guy was almost a nightmare by itself.

    Either way, what best serves your game. But I would still keep it just to vamps using Obfuscate or Chimestry. Werewolfs AREN'T natural animals and having superb instincts isn't the same as being a natural animal. They're not more natural in this regard as a human being, and humans can't do this.

                                First, I would like to remember some people that we are talking about using Primal Urge to perceive vampires using the Brush of Life. To players that don't know Vampire well, this is a use of blood that puts the entire methabolism of the vampire to work again. During this time he will blush, breath, wink, drink, sweat, everything a normal human being of his physical age should do, without need to concentrate. It already costs blood, a lot of blood to the less Humane.

                                About Primal Urge being animal instincts going wild, yes and no. After all, animals don't have Primal Urge, it is a Fera thing. So it is sort of a mystical sense. Could be better used setting of the allarm for powers in use, like the even more powerful Supernatural Awareness of Demons do, maybe with some effectiveness to tell when someone is using, as an example, a hiding or illusion power. That way, Primal Urge could tell of the presence of an Obfuscated or Chimerstry-Hiden vampire, without pinpointing him. It isn't just a vampire, but one you SHOULD have noticed if not for his cursed powers.

                                Either way, as I said, I do the way I do for the sake of interesting games. I don't see anything wrong with a high-difficulty dice roll revealing that something is off. If at least it doesn't tell what is off.

                                By the way, I think the way animals react to vampires are much more similar to the way HUMANS react to Werewolfs. Sounds to me much more like a supernatural response, like an unnatural fear, more linked to animal purity than instincts.

                                    Hi!

                                    Since detecting Vampires with PU and gifts is the topic, some thoughts by a mainly VtM player, but we play crossovers often. Much depends on what perks and Disciplines (Vampire gifts) or not the Vampire possesses.
                                    Every Vampire can use his/her supernatural blood to get the metabolism working again for a time. The Vampire will breathe, have standard body heat, sweat, normal skin colour, etc., gaining the blush of Life.
                                    There is an advantage of the same name you can aquire, which means that your metabolism never really stopped. You have the above features, but it costs you nothing except the cost to buy the advantage and is always on.
                                    There is an advantage "Inoffensive to animals" which lets animals think of the Vampire like a normal human.
                                    For animals and Garou it becomes even more problematic if a Vampire has the Animalism discipline, which even a single level of makes you quite attractive to animals
                                    Vampires with Humanity 7+ (there are more of those than one might believe) won´t register on most "warning scanners" the Garou have. (Considering some level oddities of Humanity, I´d say 6+)
                                    Tremere and other Thaumaturges might have paths or rituals (like domino of life) which can help obfuscate their true nature.
                                    There is an advantage which gives a Vampire a normal humanlike aura.
                                    And not to forget the "Sanctity" advantage, doubly effective if the character in question is really a nice guy/girl. This advantage makes the character gain the label "a nice one", because you have the vibes of one.

                                    These are just some things that play a role in detecting a Vampire. The true masters of the masquerade will not be found out with most Garou gifts. Vampires with all or most of these advantages are not the norm, but common enough that even cautious Garou might "fuck up" occationally.
