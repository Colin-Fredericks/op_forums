    I think from my experience that there are many ways to handle it:
    1. Most traditional Garou will likely try to eliminate him as weak (as Litany says, read Valkenburg Foundation opening story as an inspiration).
    2. It will severly hits his Renown (ignoring the wolf part of the nature).
    So most likely he will be an Omega in his pack (somebody has to).
    Resolution of this problem
    1. Valkenburg Foundation (some sort of an asylum for deranged garou).
    2. Powerful entity with mind altering abilities could help (as Mage with Mind Sphere mentioned above could help). Of course it left you with the ancestors problem.
    3. Quest to umbral Legendary Realm (to solve the ancestor problem) and/or to Summer Country (it is believed that there mental disabilities could be healed).
    Or, if you plan to run a longer story and like heroic stories you could not let his character heal himself, mock and hurt this character for the entire story and, at the end, use this flaws as an advantage (like during a great battle for the caern when everyone shifts into crinos/hispo and him making his way to the enemy camp, his flaws preventing him from raging, and turn the enemy artillery against him... or his septmates if they went too far). Hell, I almost see it, BSD fighting his pack, fomori shooting the cannons, and this character, who says them that there will be a new target. Fomori let him aim the cannon and...

    "BSD is chasing your Kinfolk brother/sister/father/mother in crinos. He/she runs and then he/she trips. It's only a matter of seconds for BSD to get him/her..."
    "You see Raging Fire, this big f@#$%*g Ahroun, who first called you a coward for many moons and then took your Kinfolk lover for himself. She couldn't say no, the Elders decided that Raging Fire will be a better mate for her. And now, he is a hero again, standing over 2 BSD corpses. Right in front of your cannon. Noone will know. And, if you will be lucky enough (and years of using the homid form let you hone your gunnery skills) you could also hits at least two of those Elders..."
    And, to satisfy my need for tearful endings, let him be crucial for victory (if he didn't start to shoot his septmates) and then accuse him of helping the Wyrm (someone saw him using the cannon with fomori around him).
