                        They wouldn't have a monopoly on the Celts for the same reason. It's just that originally, the Celts were likely strongly Fianna, but it doesn't have to be exclusively so. Some of the Celts in the lands that are now Germanic might have been Get of Fenris for example. Or that there was an overlap between White Howlers and the Celts at the same time as the Fianna.

                        In my personal canon for my Chronicles, I see the White Howlers being a very ancient Tribe whose human kinfolk were the original inhabitants of Europe (Old Europe - the Europe of the Neolithic and older) before the Indo-European languages arrived. That's because some people think the Picts were one of the last of the original inhabitants of Europe. It is believed the Basques are also one of the surviving groups of such people. So originally I think the White Howlers occupied much of Ice Age Europe, and only some time later did the Fianna, Get, etc. arrive.

                        I don't mind some Tribes being strongly linked to certain cultures or ethnic groups. It is just not exclusively so (though I think the higher Pure Breeds will be strongly associated with those).

                        The Celts (and any other ancient cultures) as a group did not exist at the time of the Tribes first formed. However, I believe if you are going to go with the idea that certain cultures are strongly associated with certain Tribes, then that logic should not be limited to where those cultures/languages exist now, but also to where they existed in the past. Since the game says there are ties to the Celtic peoples now, then it is natural to say that was true way in the past when the locations of those peoples were very different.

                                As a Fianna spanish player i agree wholeheartedly with this thread. Speaking very generally and loosely about Spanish history, the pre-roman invasion spanish tribes were classified in three broad groups: Celts, Celtiberian and Iberian (based mainly in its culture and the grade of external forces -read it as Greek / Phoenician / Carthaginian forces- influence in them).

                                The south-east Peninsular areas, closer or in direct contact with the Mediterranean Sea (that is to say, Murcia, Comunidad Valenciana, Cataluña) were the Iberians; the most influenced by foreigners. This influence would greatly diminish the further you'd travel into the Peninsula, with the Celtiberian tribes holding (more or less) the south of actual Aragon,Madrid and roughly the north of Castilla la Mancha. The Celts would hold roughly a third/quarter of all the Iberian Peninsula, more or less the Northwest. There were some tribes, like the Vettones (Mainly in Salamanca, and the north of Caceres) or the Lusitanians (fierce warriors from Portugal) who are considered proto-celtics nowadays.

                                The Spanish areas in which you can really feel the celtic roots are mainly Galicia and Asturias (well, also León,as the Astures were as much above the Cantabrian Range as in that southern area from them; el Bierzo is one of those places of León in which you cannot deny the celtic influence), but also Cantabria and País Vasco.

                                In our game group, is general consensus about Galicia, Asturias and León being Fianna protectorates. Silent Striders are fairly common in the Peninsula, of course, since they were there way before the Muslim invasion of the Peninsula, and after it their numbers just increased. There are also Black Furies (mainly along the Iberian areas) protectorates, and Blood Talons clinging to the forestal areas in the center and north of Spain. Madrid; Barcelona and some other big cities have their fare share of Glass Walkers and Bone Gnawers. Children of Gaia are common in Salamanca and Caceres, trying to make amends with the Bull spirist and Apis ghosts remaining in the area (in Salamanca are common the "Verracos" - big stone sculpures depicting bulls-). [Well, in fact the Apis are not completely extinct in our game]. Silver Fangs tend to cling to Madrid and Barcelona areas, with some surprising protectorates in Pais Vasco, Granada and León (as they were really important back in the Middle Ages). Shadow Lords are rare, but usually they can be found in the same places as Silver Fangs, clinging to those same power areas.

We picked Spain because of the rich culture, amazing history and beautiful landscape, as well as the lack of a stereotypically prevailing Tribe, which would ease the new players (who want to create characters of various tribes) into the setting and WtA in general. Part of this is actually lack of knowledge of local myths, as well as local current geopolitics.

                                            I figured the sept would be ruled by a small (and decaying) group of Silver Fangs (maybe Blood Red Crest or Gleaming Eye), which are clinging to power at the expense of the sept. At the caern proper there would be significant Fianna (maybe local or from País Vasco), Get of Fenris (descendants from Northern Italy Get, now local) and Black Furies (local) presence, a Shadow Lord emissary, as well as travelling Silent Striders. The Glass Walkers and Bone Gnawers would have a minor caern (Level 1 probably) nearby (maybe in Granada, the Generalife Park looks like a good location).

                                                Canonically the tribes most frequently mentioned with Spain are Children of Gaia, the Glass Walkers, the Silent Striders, and the Shadow Lords (for some strange reason I don't understand). Silver Fangs have been there too.

                                                If you go by history and match ethnic groups with Garou tribes, you get a different mix. You likely start with Fianna (from the Celtiberians) and Silent Striders. Maybe even small numbers of Red Talons. Glass Walkers would arrive when the Romans came. Spain was a very developed part of the Empire by the time it fell. The Goths and Vandals who arrived during the barbarian invasions probably had some Get of Fenris with them. The general population of tribes probably settled there with some additional influx from Berbers arriving after the Muslim conquest. So sprinkle in some Children of Gaia, Black Furies, and Bone Gnawers as needed. Plus one SF house.

                                                The exact mix today is up to whatever you need it to be for your chronicle. As my base of the most populous tribes, I'd start with Fianna. Then the Get and Glass Walkers. Then the others as you'd like. Increase or decrease their relative numbers as you'd like. If you want to add an odd tribe that doesn't seem it should be there, come up with some excuse in Garou history to explain them holding one or more caerns.

                                                Your ideas seem plausible and wouldn't cause me to raise an eyebrow. I think you can trust your judgment.
