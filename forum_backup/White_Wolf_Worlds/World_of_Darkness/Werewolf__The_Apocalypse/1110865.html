                  The Dutch have been in South Africa since 1652. A small group of female Homid Bastet and female Human Bastet Kinfolk could have easily interbred with Dutch men if they thought that there was something to gain. Children of mixed race would have probably started springing up in 1653 and, with a century of the mixed race daughters exclusively breeding with Dutch men, they would have been indistinguishable from Dutch women and could have married into the families of the Dutch 'colonists'. By the time that the British started the get involved in 1795, a good number of the Boers in South Africa could have been descended from the group of female Homid Bastet and female Human Bastet Kinfolk (though the vast majority of them would not have been Bastet Kinfolk). Over two hundred years later, all of the white Afrikaans could be related to the Bastet just from having a small group of female Homid Bastet and female Human Bastet Kinfolk interbreed with the first Dutch men who came to South Africa.

                      Changing Breeds make new kin whenever they breed with them.

                      The indigenous population where the Simbra dwell now are black. I'd make that the default especially for feline born simba. I'd only have white homid Simba if the Simba managed to breed into the white population of Africa over an extended period of time. Given the realities of South Africa and the European colonies in sub-Saharan Africa, that would be extremely difficult to produce a truly white Simba despite the original Bastet book saying there are Simba of British and German stock (the primary European colonizers of the 19th-20th centuries; I guess the Portuguese being in the area for several centuries longer don't count!).

                      Originally posted by Helur View Post
                      It makes more sense that some ancient bloodlines from southern Europe and northern Africa (Italy southern france and Greece were full of lion population) are still alive and claimed territory in southern Africa breeding with europeans.
                      This is probably the best source of truly native white homid Simba. Lions didn't become extirpated until 1st Century AD in Europe, and didn't disappear from the Caucasus Mountains until the 10th century. The Barbary Lion in North Africa survived until the mid-twentieth century. Various white colonists from Spain, France, and Italy had conquered and ruled parts of North Africa intermittently from the Middle Ages until the mid-twentieth century. And of course, North Africa was part of the Roman Empire and open to immigration from the north, and the region was famously conquered by the Vandals during the fall of the Western Roman Empire. So there is lots of "white" homid stock from there.

                      So the most likely scenario is that there were white Simba in Europe until the first century, at which point homid Simba made sure to emigrate to North Africa in order to protect their remaining wild feline stock and their skin color ranged from the slightly darker color of Berbers to the whiter skins of Europeans. After the Arabs invaded, the typical skin color became duskier, but even here "whiter" breeding stock could be obtained from merchant ties to Europe, European invaders, and of course Arab slavers raiding the European coasts. Then once the French arrived in Algeria in the 1800s, there was opportunity for Europeans to carry the Simba legacy. I'd have any white homid Simba elsewhere be a result of immigration from the old French Algeria and similar. Most white homid Simba probably look more North African.

                      As for feline Simba, I always assume that the features of their homid forms simply adopt whatever is the dominant form of humanity in the area where they live (Id'do the same in reverse - homids turn into the animal as it would make the most sense as locally). After all, the magic works so the Fera/Garou can blend in. Thus in Africa, it would be almost 100% black. If for some reason, a pre-Change Simba was taken to America for a zoo (or kinfolk lion give birth to a Simba), then what that Simba actually changed, their homid form would match the local population. So most likely it'd be white, but in many American cities, there'd be a good chance the appearance would be black as well with the greater chance based on Pure Breed since feline Simba's pure breed most likely reflects a strict African heritage. Under this, it would even be possible for a Simba's homid form to appear Asian if one was born in say the Ueno Zoo in Tokyo - but let's face it, that would be exceedingly rare and exceptional. Such a homid form, even though it looks local, may still display some features that seem exotic or strange - for example she might tan far easier and far darker than typical).

                      Ultimately we're talking about magic stuff here. Do what you think makes sense.

                                                      While theoretically the Boer could interbreed with native Africans, pick up the Simba "gene" and eventually produce white homid Simba, I think that would be anachronistic for the most part.
                                                      The Boers (and later the English) did breed with native Africans in South Africa, but they typically did not keep breeding with their descendants so the family line would become become "white" again. Instead, they became their own mixed population of Coloureds. There's a huge difference between rolling in the hay with someone to produce a baby, and then letting that child, once he or she grows up, actually marry into your society. A mixed baby that was half white and half black simply wasn't accepted as white back in those days. Children of such relations in South Africa became Coloureds, not white. Like any mixed peoples, you can have wide variation between them with some being very dark and looking like Africans and others being able to pass for white. But that doesn't seem to be what Helur is looking for, and the amount of native African blood in the actual white population has to be exceedingly low which would complicate justification for "white" Simba.

                                                      It would only be within the last twenty-five years or so when official legal prohibition against interracial marriages ended that you'd have a scenario where a fairly light skin Coloured who was a Simba kinfolk would marry a white, and their child could be seen as white and have a white homid Simba. But given the relatively low rates of intermarriage and the general Changing Breed dynamics of producing a Fera, they would still be extremely rare.

                                                      You might also have a situation where Portuguese from their old colonies in Angola and Mozambique, who were more open to interracial unions, became Simba kinfolk and emigrated to South Africa after the Portuguese empire in Africa collapsed in 1974. The Portuguese would have been accepted as white and established white Simba there as they intermarried with the local whites, but again this would likely produce only a rare few white Simba.

                                                      On the other hand, once South Africa began producing diamonds and gold in 1868, white emigration to South Africa skyrocketed. The white population of South Africa went from around 180,000 in 1865 to 1.2 million in 1910. This would provide cover for some of those remaining few Mediterranean Simba kinfolk to arrive and marry with each other and the rest of the whites.

                                                      Depending on what Helur wants to do, this could be advantageous for him in a chronicle about racial tensions. It wouldn't just be white Simba and black Simba, but Coloured Simba plus divisions between the white Simba based on their origin (the Mediterranean Simba, the Portuguese Simba (which might be a comination of Med and local), and perhaps a rare few white Simba who truly have a local origin as descendant from a local African).
