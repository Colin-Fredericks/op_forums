                                        a) Being a Ronin can be extremely dramatic for a story and give to the game a unique a mood, this game may introduce to us all a great new character. Apart from that, being ronin is not only about rejecting gaia, a character may become ronin by other ways, like killing a packmate while making a quest, taking drugs repeatedly, a prophecy of a grim looking theurge, or even being wrongly accused, being disapointed by failing an important personal goal or keep away from a forbidden garou love.
                                        All the above can make a garou go ronin or being forced to do so.

                                        b)Later on the story I think is a werewolf core theme to enter a tribe and a pack. Probably you may have pack assistance in the game later on, depening on what tribe you may choose, or ally with. Maybe there will be differend endings depending on what choices you make, garou, remaining a ronin or even wyrm. The scenario may be linear but thats not a bad thing, especially if the various choices you make change dramaticaly the way you reach the ending and not just the ending itself. I heard there will be influence between areas and the choices you make to the one may affect the other.

                                        c)Pentex being the primary antagonist doesn't mean it will only contain humans, it may have lordly fomories, black spiral leaders, first teams, sabbat vampires of nephandi mages, not to mention the various manufactured horrors, I don't think that you will win boss fights only by shifting crinos. Plus a malphean nightmare may run the current company, or an ancient beast waiting to get out of its slumber for a grand bam finale.

                                        d) Taking Rage as the core theme of the game, is an awesome choice. The idea that you will need high social skills in order to gain more dialogue options and avoid frenzy amazes me. Or even the possibility that frenzy is the right option to solve a problem. Rage is the heart of werewolf the apocalypse because it is a power and a weakness at the same time, it is righteous fury and unbound monstrosity, not to mention the possibility to lose and start over a quest because you frenzied and killed an npc or took multiple headshots because you didn't notice the five snipers on the upper floor, or that you got pissed off with another garou who talked bad about your grandpa. Its wonderful !
