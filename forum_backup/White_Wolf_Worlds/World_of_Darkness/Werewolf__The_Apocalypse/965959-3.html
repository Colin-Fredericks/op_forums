                                    The Rite of Feeding the Land superficially cleanses a small amount of land (a 20 foot radius for every point of Willpower lost and health level of lethal damage suffered before the victim dies), but the spiritual guardians of the land ignore Wyrm incursions from then on (Red Talons, p. 73-74).
