                                    Originally posted by Damian May View Post
                                    Eh, theres a whole bunch of things mentioned in the ST text that give opportunities to regain Rage that don't appear on the Rage chart, even more in W20 as it cut the chart even shorter.
                                    ST text?
                                    What are you referencing to?

                                    I think the basic section "Regaining Rage" hasn't changed much since 2nd editon. The reasons to regain RAW (not counting Gifts, Rites, Merits etc.) has always been fairly limited. Not as limited as Gnosis and Willpower, but still.

                                    Originally posted by Damian May View Post
                                    Either way, even with the extremely miserly rules of W20 your gaining a minimum of 2 Rage from humiliation and anticipation of a fight, pretty sure shifting to Crinos, losing your hands in the process and leaping out of a chair to attack a vampire counts as a confrontation in anyones book. Which is enough to allow shifting and at least one extra action....
                                    Well I disagree.
                                    The way I read it, it is anticipation of a confrontation that grants the Rage, not the fact that there is a confrontation. So, if the alpha gives the command to raid the fomorer ridden complex you have been sneaking towards to for the last half hour, that's the feeling of immediate action the garou dwell in an gain a point of Rage. Awaking with a Black Spiral standing over you body ready to claw your face, not so much. It is a confrontation alright, but there is no anticipation of the immediate action, there is just the action. No time to dwell in the feeling. I mean it states, the Rage gain is the game mechanic representing hackle-raising. If there is no time of hackles to raise, or your attack is cold and calculated, then there probably shouldn't be a Rage gain.

                                    With the humiliation, it is a similar thing. I would not necessarily say, that being held captive by the vampire in itself merits a rage point regain. The Garou has to do something that proves humiliating. I didn't read the comic and have no clear idea about the circumstances of this particular situation, but I would set the bar higher. If the vampire was rubbing it in, that he bested the garou, sure, that would be a circumstance I would think Rage is regained.

                                    Rage is the stat that possibly changes the most during the curse of a single sitting of werewolf, especially if combat is involved. But it is still a resource that is limited and doesn't work like "MCU-Avenger-era Hulk" (meaning "I'm always angry" - and can basically become Hulk whenever he needs to, or the plot does, removing the old tag line "You wouldn't like me if I'm angry").


                                    -----------

                                    That said, I agree that additional actions are a bad mechanic. Most games I know that had them got rid of them or limited them in newer editions. V20DAs approach was pretty good, but I also liked V5s approach to Celerity, removing them completely.

                                    There are plenty of other ways to incorporate game benefits to combat situations when Rage is spend, that would not simply allow the character to get a free use of all the attack dice he has. I'm experimenting on a more balanced combat system, and currently I grant per Rage spend bonus dice to the general dice pool, depending on the permanent Rage. The improved dice pool might be split for multiple actions, but they all need to be handled when it is the characters turn, there are penalties for splitting the dice pool and so on.

                                                        Originally posted by BadFurryFanFic View Post
                                                        Wow. With that tiny amount of Rage regeneration how do the Garou PCs in your games even survive against jaggling Banes? It seems like a single short combat would potentially leave them without much in the way of resources. I mean it would be an interesting play experience but would certainly mean that a lot more Garou die during relatively minor Monkeywrenching or your average Umbral quest.
                                                        Well, spirit combat has it's own problems. But, spirits can't spend Rage, Gnosis or Willpower, so they are limited to one action. If they don't have the Armor Charm activated, they are down to soak damage with they Willpower score, solely. So, their high Essence, which is their Health too, they last relatively long, but against a pack, they are pretty weak - because they have just one action. As it should be.

                                                        Fights among garou often work like this: The non-combat-oriented characters spend no rage, for they want to use Gifts with Gnosis or they want to keep their rage. The combat-oriented characters spend the maximum amount of Rage they can, on offence mostly, but some defensive actions, for the enemy does the same. Then, the first initiative round handles all characters and after that, there are two to three rounds only the characters with extra actions do stuff (unless one of those character who doesn't have any extra action gets hit and needs to soak).
                                                        And since the characters are pretty combat oriented, their attacks have plenty successes, targets don't have actions to block/parry/dodge, therefore a lot of successes turn into dice for damage. Damage needs to be soaked and this can lead to non-combat characters being incapacitated without a means to do anything about it.
                                                        And, even if that doesn't happen, the players have a lot of time their characters can't act, because all the additional actions of the other characters need to be handled first.
                                                        It is just not a practicable game mechanic in my opinion.

                                                        Besides that, there is rarely combat after combat after combat, and a level up after 4 and a half encounters of an appropriate challenge rating, for that is d20-style gaming. So, for the way I would ST werewolf, there would be plenty of other things to do, besides combat, needing some time, therefore leading to regaining Rage by seeing the moon. Also, the occasional regain, do to botching a roll with at least a moderate relevance to the story. And, in most cases, my PCs are the ones doing the attack, so there is one Rage for the anticipation of combat. I would also grant that, if they stalk through enemy ridden ware house, for they might be attacked at any point, but not, if the combat is really a surprise, like being attacked by a banquet at a Shadow Lord sept.... well....
