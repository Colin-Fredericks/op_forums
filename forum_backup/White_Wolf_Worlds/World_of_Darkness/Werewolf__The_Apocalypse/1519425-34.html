            Also, I don't know how "Partisan" W5 really encourages, when it's stated attitude is a paternalistic, condescending denial of the efficacy of Direct Action. The edition doesn't want you doing traditional Partisan activities, like being disruptive or engaging in asymmetrical guerilla warfare. It honestly doesn't even want you engaging in positive change through "official" channels, by the way it frames the fight as being lost.

            What W5 seems to want, tonally and textually, is for Garou to hunker down in the werewolf equivalent of apocalypse bunkers and try to survive a little longer. That's not being a Partisan, that's at best being a prepper.

                                            Originally posted by Psicose View Post
                                            Does anyone know if there is any mention of vampires or SI in W5?
                                            There is a short bit on vampires in the antagonist-section with two example-antagonists, the "Dimebag-Dracula" and "Count Orlock or some shit". And personally, I think the writing on those two antagonists is the most flavorful and evocative writing you can find in the book. As the names already imply it has this sardonic tone and judgmental perspective on what vampires are. But it's evocative in the sense that despite just spending a couple dozen words on it, you can picture the NPC it's talking about and it's flavorful because the text sells the sardonic tone.

                                            Second Inquisition is mentioned and is part of the larger portrayal of mortal hunters as "agents of the Weaver" in W5. Considering that H5 makes a huge fuss about how bad hunter-orgs are and how they're really just another force reinforcing the status-quo, it follows the logic of that book. Project Twilight gets a loresheet and mentioned as a werewolf-specific hunter-org but in W5 it has been officially disbanded by the director of the Special Affairs Division - and yet somehow still exists (another infamous "Who knows"-bit).
