                                I used to run a story centered on BSD using fertility clinic described in Freak Legion (for producing fomori). The same idea was used in first "When will you rage" (story about Samuel Haight) My conclusion? It is entirely possible BUT:
                                1. You have to track every woman in which you put a potential Garou and that would be hard
                                2. Explain them that (in case of First Change happening) you take over their child.
                                3. Try to avoid them suing you for leaking of the data (because how a Garou would know about the origin of this child)
                                4. In case of the early symptoms of the First Change (and I believe that most of the clinic's clients are ordinary human females/couples, in other case it would be useless) most of the kids would end in the mental health institiutions and that would propably means Magadon sedatives.
                                4. I think that, even if Pentex and all its subsidiaries are unknown to most Garou, most of them can sense something weird around its products and can warn their kinfolks. Humans (eg. clients) don't have that luxury and that means exposure on Wyrm products since early childhood for potential Garou. Not to mention that Banes can alert BSD of potential Garou.
                                5. What if some BSD wants to donate?
                                6. What if some other supernatural creature would like to use your clinic (Giovanni trying to create Rosselini revenants).
                                7. Most likely a Garou created that way would be Weaver tainted (causing mistrust in most septs) or worse Wyrm tainted (as Magadon produce many things you would use in that clinic).
                                So basically I think it's possible. The question is what for? And would the costs be acceptable?
