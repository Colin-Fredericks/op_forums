                  Wolf spirits are very common Gift teachers (~45 Gifts in the books, and plenty of lower level ones with many Auspices and Tribes having at least one Gift taught by a wolf spirit in the 1 to 3 range). Obviously as Garou-born, there will be some level of obstacle, but that's generally in your ST's hands for the tone of the game rather than rather than something wolf spirits are going to struggle with.
