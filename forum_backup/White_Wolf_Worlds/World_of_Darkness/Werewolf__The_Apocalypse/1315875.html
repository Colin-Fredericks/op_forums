      It's not "advanced" Weaver devices... just Weaver devices that work. You can put a broken gun in a Magpie's Swag, but not a functional one (making it more useful to Gnawers who are likely to want to carry around a lot of junk since they have Gifts to get around things being broken).

      As for what to fill it with?

      For most Tribes, I see these being a go to thing for ritualists. A lot of Rites have material components to them, so whatever ones they lead/participate in frequently tends to dictate a lot of what goes in the Fetish.

      Gnawers and Striders have a tendency to want to be able to carry around their basic essentials since they're less likely to have a solid home base to store things safely. So they're more likely to be extremely utilitarian about the contents. Whatever they want to have regardless of the circumstances. Spare clothing, water and food, fire sources, things like that.

      After that if a Garou is trying to do a human style job (usually Walkers but not exclusively; esp. jobs that are easier to hold despite Rage because they involve lots of time away from people), then this Fetish tends to get put to use for all the tools of the trade. Art supplies, crafting tools, assassin weapons, etc.

      Most characters that use firearms or other Weaver-tech are going to want to Dedicate or have Fetish versions of those tools so they don't have to worry about getting them out of the bag/satchel in a pinch. Spending two turns getting your gun out of your bag instead of having it ready to draw could mean a huge difference in a fight. Though I have seen a Spider's Satchel used by a GW sniper for all their sniper gear and rely on a Dedicated sidearm since sniping doesn't really work if you're caught without time to setup anyway. That all said, if your character has low Gnosis and wants to carry a bunch of stuff around just for the creature comforts it's a good Fetish to take.
