            Originally posted by Lian View Post
            The game presents learning from another CB as in all ways harder than going to a spirit. So why would anyone ever do this?
            Because you don't always have access to the right spirits, or you don't want to do things "above board" for various reasons (ex: the Bringers of Light or the Grey Raptors are both Tribal camps that do a lot of things in secret and might consider teaching each other directly better than sending their members to outsiders to learn questionable Gifts; the difficulty of such learning might also be considered proof of the younger members' dedication to the Camp). This has always been presented as a back up option for special circumstances; not a default.

            There's also groups like Ronin and the fringes of Gnawer society that operate away from Septs and Tribes, leaving them with less access to traditional spirit-based learning.

            I presumed that was how you got Gifts from Spirits you don't already have a relationship with(Tribe, Auspice, Breed) with some leeway for more common Gifts.
            What spirits teach Gifts pretty much has nothing to do with splats. Owl spirits teach Gifts all over the place (including in lists that are non-Strider Tribe lists). If the spirits that teach individual Gifts were more closely aligned with their linked splats? Ok, this would make sense. As it is, things are a giant mess and there's no consistent way to say, "Oh, yeah, spider spirits were totally cool with teaching you this Theurge Gift cause you're a Theurge, but not this Glasswalker Gift because you're not a Glasswalker," if you're already on good terms with spider spirits.

            Your access to spirits comes for numerous sources besides splat. I've already mentioned things like Sept and Pack as being sources of in-roads with other kinds of spirits.

            Actually this is a bigger question, how do CB know what gifts they can learn? How do they know who to Summon and why? Is that part of their training? Indoctination into a tribe etc?
            Personal experience with other shifters. Occult and Rituals if you feel like giving it a Knowledge roll. Lores if you like those. Otherwise... the books don't really consider this worth thinking too hard about.

            You can also just look at this from a more simple perspective. A Garou is looking for a Gift to aid them in doing X, so they ask for the help of a spirit that they think has the appropriate symbolism (I want to help keep people calm, so I'm going to Dove), and the spirit "decides" the Gift to learn, even though the player is selecting a more specific Gift out-of-character.

                            Gifts are something a spirit bestows on a garou. Axis Mundi has a longer paragraph about the gift learning process. So, if a character petitions a spirit successfully this is usually an accomplishment, and not a deed to be frowned upon. That also is true because a lot of gift choices don't make sense. 1st level 'Resist Pain', 2nd level soak gift, 3rd level damage boost - both true for the martial Get of Fenris, and the peace loving children of gaia - leaving the martial Bone Gnawer ahroun just asking "What the hell?"

                            As Heavy Arms said, players rarely go after gifts that are completely out-of-character.

                            There are some gifts, that I consider archetypical for a tribe. "Clap of Thunder" or example. If another garou would learn that gift, the Shadow Lords might just find a way to kill him. Accident during a challenge. Volunteering for a suicide mission. 100 Scorpions in the bed (don't travel to Dorne).

                            But besides that I rarely have NPC repercussions, or renown issues with gifts. The spirits will make it harder for a garou to proof to the spirit that he is worthy, though.

                                                            Things to keep in mind:
                                                            1) Tribe used to mean A LOT back in 1st and 2nd edition. The tribes weren't "the Chess Team and the Football Team from the same High School" like it's presented nowadays... the tribes were North Korea, Libya, France, Columbia, etc... if you got caught sleeping with each other's kinfolk, trespassing in each other's territory, or trying to steal each other's secrets (or in ownership of them afterward) there was outright murder to pay. Much like the Floppy Disk icon, we just all accept it means 'Save' these days despite half the world's computer users having never seen a disk in their lives, many of us still carry around vestigial canon in our heads that's not really been reprinted since.

                                                            2) Tribe STILL means a lot. Tribe is more than just some habits and lineage. There's a reason you can't round up some scottish garou and reform the White Howlers. Tribal gifts involve ancient pacts, loyalties to totems, and kinship with spiritual broods. Knowing the list of Fenrir gifts, what teaches each, and going out to ask a bear spirit to teach it to you isn't something you can do at your local library. Bear and her brood didn't make that deal with France... she made that deal with Libya. So even if you somehow knew the deal existed, asking about it is a borderline international incident and sure as hell a grave party foul.

                                                            3) Out-of-Breed and Out-of-Auspice are very different from Out-of-Tribe (for 'See the Above' reasons), and given the trends in overall theme and attitude in subsequent editions of Werewolf, these two are probably barely an issue in Garou society nowadays compared to in the past... but Spirits aren't that malleable. You probably still need a in-Breed/Auspice reference with you when you summon the spirit, or learn it from another shifter directly, or have a silver tongue and a really good bribe. Because spirits aren't people... they don't deviate from natural order with anything resembling ease. Their minds simply don't work that way, unless you're dealing with a more powerful Jaggling.
