    Tribes appreciation thread

    01-22-2018, 01:01 PM
    Every tribe always do something really stupid, and every tribe has a bad stereotype.
    So, rule of the thread: describe one of your characters or an entire tribe that broke his tribe stereotype during a game session, in a really good way.

    I'll start with my character, Yaro, an ahroun silver fangs, outcast rom his italian/german house. Living in santa Fe he was an outlaw and a thief, using his power to steal money and do bad stuff.
    By the way he found himslef trapped in a bad situation with some mages, black spiral dancers and more: he fought like a true warrior against Pentex, bsd, changing his life and becoming a true hero... then, against an incarnation of the Defiler, he founded a sept and, advancing in rank, he led the garou and the mage together against a legendary treath, winning and giving new hope to the garou nation, showing that a true noble, will show his myght. From outcast to outlaw, from outlaw to warrior, from warrior to king, from king..to legend.

    I think that despise the problems of the silver fangs, two of the most badass and heroic garou in the metaplot are two silver fangs ( tamara and albrecht) and a lot of them will still show that their tribe is far from destruction.

    ( tribute artwork to Tamara and the sept of the crescent moon by me)


    let's hear about your characters!

          Characters in my games who've broken the tribe stereotype have done so terribly.

          I had a shadow lord that's strength was a farce and he was really just weak, incompetent, and quite unsavory. I dupped him very easily into helping the spirals sack the sept and joined them soon after.
          There was a silver fang that was modest and humble, and really didn't want to lead or fight. Just wanted to play a family simulator. This one lasted a while, and it was good in the claith-fostern years, but by adren she'd just retired, anthro was a harano drag, and the player always wants to go back to her.
          Same player, another silver fang, had a garou lover. Lived in a BG/UK/SS sept in the desert to get away from the family. Got caught too many times. Exiled to a sept where they keep the defective SFs, Forced through renunciation for repeat offences, would've maybe joined GW if we kept going.
          I had a stargazer player once, and though I tried to explain things to him, he was a comic relief character. Ate a bunch of rats in a bucket.
          there was a GW die ultima who wouldn't play professional. Player felt like his character was getting bullied, but she was a colossal arse to everyone including those with rank. Got exiled. I think this one actually genuinely died from challenging the sept, but I felt bad and retconned it on account of the player being more emotional than the character would've, given the initial exile timeskip.

          Yeah, playing against stereotypes has a good chance to just make an awful character.

              Savage Beast is a Metis Wendigo Ahroun who was living in isolation due to his grotesque appearance and violent urges. He abandoned the Sept where he grew up, against the will of his Tribal elders.
              He passed most of his cliath years fighting Huge Wyrm monsters in the wilds and making fetishes from parts of their corpses after cleansing them first.

              He killed garou who challenged him for territory, courted with a silent strider metis for a moon season and lost her, hopping for one day to meet her again (true love).
              Established a city hunting ground vanishing all wyrm presence from the area, put in danger local Gnawer population and managed to correct his mistake before the Gnawers realize what actually happened. Defeated all ahroun in a Fenrir's sept with one blow each, without killing them. He has been captured by BSDs and he escaped, taking back his fetishes and killing more than 10 spirals along with the warden of the hive.
              At Adren Rank took fenrir ahroun as students and guided multi tribal packs through their rites of passages, all the members of the packs returned to their septs almost dead and with at least one battlescar from silver each, but noone ever died or lost combat function.
              At athro rank he killed infamous vampires, multiple Spiral packs, even 3 Nexus Crawlers by using their powers against them (in game), united 3 local caerns,gnawers, fenrir and silver fangs.
              Also created a full Metis sept and organized them in order to form a first line of defense for the major Fang sept.
              For rank 5 Challenge he killed a manifestation of Bestia Belum by jumping inside its very jaws and ripping its head in half with a single brutal swipe, protecting his fenrir and fang allies from taking damage.
              He is the first Wendigo who gained the respect of multiple tribes to the point of being seeked out for leadership, in dire needs.
              And last but no least he is the only modern Wendigo who remained faithfully hardcore to his tribe and tribal totem without having any relationship with his tribemates, but growing relationship and respect with other tribes.

                  I played a wide eye Silver Fang idealist in a game. However the DM had both a grudge with the tribe and though it was pointless to play a good SF and a bullshit houserule that to have a Totem you had to have a Garou of the tribe of the totem which to this day i cant find anywhere in the books mentioning that.

                  So the DM made my character had to change tribe if he wanted to have Trinity as a totem (and it was the perfect fit for my PC). So he switched tribe to the Children of Gaia getting the OK from Unicorn himself. The mental gymnastic i had to make to justify that made the character just put tribe as unimportant shit, even going so far as saying he was the tribe of his totem (Trinity) and if tomorrow the totem switched to a Get he would follow it (yeah the DM didnt consult me on that one just sprung that surprise on me after telling me it was possible for the totem to accept even if we didnt have a Children of Gaia in the party). So he would schew tribal culture with a "take whats useful and throw away what isnt" mentality.

                  And another member of our pack was a Fenrir galliard who was a nurse. He was jesus, not two ways about it, a common saying of the game was that "we dont deserve him". He was a Fenrir who had internalize the core of the tribe without buying into the stereotype (because of his upbringing as an outcast of a cursed linage). Which was a nice contrast with another PC who was just a boring ass Viking forger, and how the Galliard was 10 times the Fenrir the viking was. Sure the viking had the superficial elements, spouting family and bloodlust for battle but in the end he was a craftmen who just spouted an ideology. The galliad was a geeky 17 year old boy (who was into medieval combat larps) who never gave up, he would fight in anything he believes in to the bitter end and never back down from his principles. Also the player was stupidly lucky with dice so he never lose a fight ever.

                  And thats when my PC shine through because we face a situation which almost tarnish the Fenrir's morality. A kinfolk he befriend was taken by an asshole Fianna against her will. And because of politics both knew that they couldnt drag the whole sept into a war. Rory was close to become a shaded individual when my character step in and sacrifice his moral to protect those of Rory.

                  The Kinfolk was also involve in a plot with dream parasite (stolen from Lovecraft) that had taken her family and it spread by telepathy, it didnt affect Garou (it would infect them but just remain there powerless) but it would infect kinfolks who were close enough for enough amount of time. She wasnt infected but her family was.

                  By the end of the situation, when the Fianna ask him why would he return the kinfolk? My PC children of Gaia said "because otherwise i will kill your entire family. I wont do it myself but i will go to your Sept and tell the truth, that kinfolk you are taking has a spirtual infection, how it infects kinfolks and Garou alike though it wont do anything to Garou. What do you think the Garou nation would do them? Tell me. They will do what they always do. They will overreact. If they dont kill your kinfolk family to quarantine, they sure as hell they wont want any of their precious kinfolk family to get an infection. No one will wanna breed with another member of your family ever again, your family name will mean NOTHING, your linage will end with you and forgotten as a footnote in Garou history. I know you dont care about the well-being of your family but I do know that you care of them as bargain chips and will bring the value of your family to 0.

                  I will kill your entire family to protect mine using the biggest monster i can find, the Garou nation and I will do it without a second though"

                  And from that moment on the character was a very interesting Children of Gaia willing to sacrifice his morality for others. Making small changes in the Garou nation for the better by compromising his morals. As he said "Some of the things i do haunt me, honestly. But them i go home and look at my child and think if i can make life for him a little bit better. If i can change the Garou nation a little bit for the better for him it will be worth it. Some stupid Garou think that all that matter is drawing a line on the sand and saying "i will no cross this" and then either die or get made into Ronins or live a life sleeping well knowing they never compromise. Its fucking selfish on their parts, those Garou change nothing, they left the world as they found it just to safeguard their morality."

                  By the end, he was the last of his pack to die. Dying of old age. Having trained generations of Children of Gaia in the Sept he live in, made small changes nothing song worthy in the Garou nation but little reforms here and there. Guide them to see that the true enemy of the CoG was the Garou nation, that it could be reformed for the better but sometimes the problematic elements (mad SF leaders, overambitious SLs, Fiannas.....all the Fianna) should find themselves in the front lines or deal with to ensure a better tomorrow.
