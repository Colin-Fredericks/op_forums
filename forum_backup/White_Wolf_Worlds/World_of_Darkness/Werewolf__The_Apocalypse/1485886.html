    Nuwisha pretending to be a werewolf?

    07-06-2022, 01:44 AM
    How would you make a nuwisha who is pretending to be a werewolf, and then go about tricking other people into thinking you really are one? I mean, it seems simple enough to say you are a bone gnawer ragabash, and then just pretend like you are one. It would probably trick people for a while. However, I am curious about how to go really deep, to trick everyone. Even other players. Even so they don't notice out of game as well!

    What are some things, that might give you away? Well rage seems like one thing, though ragabash only have one rage, so saying you are 'saving it' seems fairly plausible. Gifts not matching is another thing, though there are some overlap of gifts. That might work at lower levels when no one expects you to know more than a few gifts, but more noticeable later on. I suppose as a ragabash you could get in the habit of telling the dm secret stuff, and play it off as playing tricks and what not, then people might not notice if you secretly active a gift.

    I am just curious on what other people's thoughts are.

                      Coyotes can be distinguished from wolves by how they invade urban areas and learn to not make noise and to use crosswalks to avoid being hit by cars. And no, I am not joking. Research has shown that they are very opportunistic and very circumspect about their traces.

                      In terms of audiovisual and presumably scent ID, coyotes from the eastern US are probably more likely to be able to "pass" as wolves than those on the west, because of some interbreeding with wolves that went on with the eastern populations of coyotes. By anecdote, they also seem to be better fed when not eking out an existence in a literal desert (a neighbor lived in Cali for a while, said that all of the coyotes that he saw tended to be scraggly and mangy).

                                  Personally, I would ignore that statement in the W20 Changing Breeds book. Anyone who sees a coyote in real life immediately knows it is not a wolf, even if it is the first time they ever see it. Coyotes have just as many fox-like features as wolf-like features. When the Lewis and Clark Expedition first saw a coyote they immediately knew it was neither a wolf or fox, but something different. They decided to refer to it as a "prairie wolf", but don't let that designation fool you. They clearly knew it was not a gray wolf. Likewise when the Spanish first encountered it, they initially called it the Indian Fox before deciding adopting the Aztec word for it (coyotl) was better. Yet we wouldn't say that a Nuwisha looks like one of the Kitsune. Some people thought they look closer to jackals, but again not a jackal. So it's obvious people notice the similarity between a coyote and these other canids, but also clearly distinguish them as something else.

                                  The Crinos form is also sufficiently different. Nuwisha are around 7.5 to 8 feet, while Garou are roughly 9 feet (8-10). Nuwisha are around 250% of normal homid mass, while Garou are bewteen 400-850 lbs. Does that mean a Nuwisha could be mistaken for a small, scrawny werewolf? I would still say no because you would think a small, scrawny werewolf would also look small and scrawny in homid, and their homid form wouldn't. He'd look like a normal homid who just happens to have an extremely small and scrawny Crinos form which is not how this goes. And if you do have a Nuwisha who is a small and scrawny homid form, his Manabozho form would clearly not look enough like a Garou Crinos.

                                  It might have been possible that the first time European Garou met a Nuwisha they thought it was some kind of strange Garou (though the Three Brothers obviously knew they were not). But this isn't 500 years ago. The Nuwisha are known. Coyotes are known. A particularly ignorant and gullible Garou might not know any better, but most are going to know the Nuwisha is a Nuwisha, and his cover will be quickly blown. Certainly almost any Garou in North America will be able to identify him. Most Eurasian Garou will as well. Although it is always possible a particular sept of Bone Gnawers who are completely ignorant might not know that what they are seeing is a coyote and believe it is some strangely small wolf with many foxlike features (especially if he claimed to be Metis). But you are really stretching the gullibility of a small number of Garou here to do so, and I don't think that is what you want.

                                  And let's not forget, it would be super easy for any Garou who is even lightly suspicious to find someone with the Scent of True Form Gift who can immediately confirm that person is not Garou. Not to mention most spirits would likely already are able to distinguish the Changing Breeds from each other. Even one that may somehow be ignorant of the Nuwisha at minimum should recognize it is not a Garou or wolf IMO.

                                  So I think this would need a supernatural Gift to work independent of how a Nuwisha looks, sounds, smells. It seems like there may be such Gifts, but of course duration would be a critical factor. If it is something you cannot spam constantly, the Nuwisha would need to limit the amount of time spent among the Garou, and that could become suspicious in itself.
