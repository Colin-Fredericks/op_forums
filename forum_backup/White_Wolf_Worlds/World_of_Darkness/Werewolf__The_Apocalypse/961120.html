                      If you want a western-like setting, I have to agree that El Paso is probably the way to go. It's out in the desert regions, but there are still mountains, plateaus, rivers, waterfalls (albeit small ones), and forested groves hidden throughout the landscape. Then you've also got the elements of there being no major cities for a hundred or more miles in any direction.

                      You've also got a military base there (Fort Bliss) and you're right on the border of Mexico with Juarez on the other side (which I can't imagine has fared well in the World of Darkness).

                      You could also consider Big Bend national park, located down in the 'elbow' of Texas. It's huge and would probably be a prime location for a Caern.

                      If you want to step away from Desert and spaghetti western, most of North and Central Texas (anything east of the desert and west of the big cities on IH35 coming down the center-right of the state; or the entire "panhandle" at the top of the state) is where you're gonna find that small-town, good ole boy region. Country music, conservative people, bibles, football, and plains as far as the eye can see. Not too different from Kansas or Oklahoma... but don't tell them that.

                      The IH35 corridor is where you've got your big cities (minus Houston). Dallas is sprawling and industrial. Dotting the highways that ring the city are smaller towns/cities that are considered part of the Metroplex though aren't Dallas/Fort Worth proper. Lots of industrial plants in this area as well, continuing down south to Waco.

                      Further south is Fort Hood, one of the largest military bases in the US and a constant source of young infantry soldiers who like to make bad decisions in every city within driving distance.

                      ---Nothing below this line will probably line up with the setting you're looking for---

                      Keep going and you hit Austin, which is basically a county-sized chunk of California that somehow air-lifted into the center of a red state. Parks, hippies, hipsters, foodies, Bernie Sanders posters, computer companies and internet start ups. Half the city is somehow college students, despite having a couple million residents in the metroplex.

                      Next you hit New Braunfels (and Canyon Lake to the West). Smaller town, with a big chunk of income coming from the massive water park and river/lake tourism. Lots of nice parks and nature areas around here, though you'd need some WoD tweaking to make anything private enough for a caern.

                      After that is San Antonio- wide urban sprawl. Shorter buildings because land is cheap. Heavy hispanic influence but still unmistakably Texan. The Alamo is dead center in the city's downtown and a daily tourist attraction to hundreds. Again, you'd need some setting adjustments to use it for anything.

                      The Urban line dead ends at Corpus Christi... aka fishing boats and SPRING BREAK! This might make an interesting setting, but again... nothing like the Preacher game you're looking for.

                      As Mahjemm01 said, East of the corridor slowly turns from semi-urban hill country to a brief patch of tall forest, followed by a gradual transition to swamps and Louisiana. Houston and College Station (Texas A&M university) are out in the middle of that.

                                  Well, I'd take a walk around the Alamo with Google Maps.... it's the literal center of downtown San Antonio, much as a Capitol building or Mayor's office might be in other cities. Its neighbors include an IMAX theater, and a hotel with a shopping mall as its first few stories, and snaking around it and the rest of downtown is the Riverwalk (kind of a canal boardwalk) that includes bars open until 2am and the drunkards that stagger around after closing.

                                  So... you'll want to adjust the setting or provide a reason this battle can go somewhat unnoticed. Certainly a few city police are assigned to watch the monument at night as well to protect against vandalism.

                                  Size-wise the Alamo is surprisingly small. If you include all the outer walls, its footprint is probably smaller than most Walmarts.

                                  To make it happen, I'd set it around 3-4am, with some sort of supernatural power being used to drive the normals away. Maybe a Curse of Aeolus-type power to keep out the riffraff. After that, the Alamo would be the low-ground with numerous tall buildings and parking garages providing vantage over the park/monument... so the Garou could expect to be well pinned down while foot soldiers tried to cross the streets and plaza that lead up to the walls. Honestly... it'd probably make for a pretty exciting tactical challenge.

                                  Of course in the WoD, the Alamo and the lands around it might be home to no shortage of Wraiths as well... so maybe fewer high-end businesses would have decided to set up shop right next door. It's up to you how much your WoD differs from reality.
