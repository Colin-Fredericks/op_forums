    Spirit broods/Incarna for Breed Gifts

    09-16-2026, 06:41 PM
    I have said on other threads that I am a big believer of using spirit broods as a tool for the ST. It provides STs an organization that can make running the Umbra easier.

    One of the things broods do is explain why certain spirits teach what Gifts. It’s not that Gifts are limited to only certain broods. Gift descriptions often given multiple types of spirits who teach that Gift, and the same Gift is often included on multiple lists (Sense Wyrm, for example, is on the list for Metis, Theurge, Black Furies, Silent Strider and Uktena lists in W20, and can be supposedly taught by any Gaian spirit). It’s just that many spirit broods have “authorization “to teach the same Gift. Spirit broods are an easy way to explain why certain Gifts are on the list for one’s auspice, breed, and tribe.

    However, spirit broods have Incarna. So I would like to identify the various Gift teaching broods (of one’s auspice, breed, and tribe) to an Incarna. (If you don't care about such things, I understand.)

    The Tribal Gifts are the easiest to explain. We know there are tribal totems for each other them that have their own spirit broods. Auspice Gifts are also easy. Auspice is determined by the Celestine Luna, and she ultimately is the one who decides which Gifts can be taught “in class” to each auspice. If we don’t want to ultimately direct all auspices to Luna herself (or perhaps the Incarna Phoebe), then we can “invent” Incarna for each of the moon phases. No matter what one decides, we can quickly move on.

    The Breed Gifts don’t have such an obvious patron though. Presumably the Wolf Incarna (under whatever name) is the ultimate patron for the Lupus Gifts. Or perhaps as aspect of Wolf like Old Wolf of the Woods if we don’t want Wolf himself to favor any one breed over another. That is easy to figure out. However, we don’t have obvious patrons for the Homid and Metis Gifts.

    W20 does say that many homid breed Gifts are taught by ancestor spirits than nature spirits because humans have become strangers to the world of spirit. In lieu of a “Man” spirit, perhaps there’s some kind of “First Homid” ancestor spirit out there that is effectively the patron for all homids. This paramount homid spirit doesn’t necessarily need to be the ancestor spirit of the first ever homid Garou. just an important early one. Although I do think such a figure should pre-date all the tribes, and likely pre-dates the Pact. However, normally ancestor spirits don’t increase in power to become an Incarna. The only examples we have are Black Spiral Dancers who have done that (Number Two and Hakaken). That’s not good precedent. So perhaps its more like a City Father type figure (an Epiphling) that is like a “Man As Tool Maker” or something. If there’s a Patriarch, why can’t there be a Toolmaker, Lawgiver, Firebringer (or Firestarter), Namegiver, Tamer, or equivalent?

    The W20 description for Metis Gifts is more interesting. It says Metis Gifts tend to be an eclectic collection of “pacts” and powers. Since they are scorned, metis have learned to make friends where they can and take what allies they can get. This implies that perhaps originally metis Garou did not have a ready list of Gifts for their breed. After all, the metis are not “natural” in the sense there’s already an existing spirit that embodies the concept. Metis only emerged later.

    There is a creature in the Books of the Wyrm called the First Metis, but I extremely doubt that he should be a patron whose spirit brood is responsible for the Metis breed Gifts. It just creates too many problems for the setting.

    I like the idea that only gradually did metis form a list of Breed Gifts through individual metis actively making pacts, not just for themselves but for their entire breed. As these individual pacts accumulated, eventually one spirit gained enough power to collect, organize, and protect this eclectic group of spirits. Maybe Father Mule who would also be the pack totem described in Children of Gaia Tribebook, Revised? Since mules don’t seem to have existed until 3500 BC at the earliest, the spirit would likely not exist until then, meaning a comprehensive breed list of Gifts available to all metis might not have existed until well into human history, perhaps not until the Classical Age.

    Those are my initial thoughts. Does anyone else have an opinion? Interested in comparing them.

                                  Originally posted by Amethyst View Post
                                  Mule sounds insulting, so probably not. Firebringer could work though.

                                  Sorry. I can’t tell if you’re being sarcastic or not.
                                  I'm not being sarcastic. Father Mule (specific name, not just "Mule") is an existing canonical pack totem. It is described in the revised Children of Gaia Tribebook. It is a Totem of Respect. He's described as the sterile son of Horse, and that he is particularly fond of metis. Lupus and Homids, in general, don't like the totem for their own reasons. Father Mule will not accept pack members who are "fertile". That's not just metis, as both men and women homids and lupus can be infertile for whatever reasons. They might be infertile through a genetic disorder, medical issue, injury, or naturally barren for whatever reason; and homid women can be post-menopausal. All would fit the definition of not being fertile. However, I think in the main, Father Mule will be a totem to packs that are all metis except for a few exceptions.

                                  There's not many existing pack totems that are clearly associated with metis, and thus there are few existing candidates that make sense as a potential Incarna master of its own spirit brood that provides breed gifts to Metis. In fact, it's the only one I remember that clearly favors metis.

                                  Not all hybrids are infertile, but many are. That's why I think a sterile hybrid like a mule would be appropriate for metis since they are naturally sterile as well. Furthermore, people find mules useful. While some hybrids are noticeably inferior than either lineage, mules possess hybrid vigor and display improved traits over their parents. People purposefully breed mules because they are hardier and live longer than horses, and are more intelligent than donkeys. They are also more cooperative and patient in temperament. It is rare for mules to become pregnant, but it has happened. However, rather than being a detriment, I think that might provide even more enthusiasm of metis for the totem.

                                  Humans associate a lot of positive traits to mules. People who have them care just as much for them as they do their horses and donkeys (or other animals). So that could explain why Father Mule had enough spiritual strength to attract lesser spirits to itself for protection. It could then proceed to gather spirits around it for protection, and many spirits who were already providing some Gifts to metis would be inclined to join it.

                                  So even when I considered non-canonical spirits as candidates for a patron of the metis breed Gifts, I haven't come up with a better candidate than Father Mule. I would love to hear suggestions though.

                                  The spirit brood of Father Mule would likely be limited, but that's OK. Not all spirit broods will be as large as Stag's. It'll include a lot of metis Ancestor Spirits, lesser mule spirits, the spirits of other sterile or low-producing hybrids, and other oddball spirits. The description of the Father Mule pack totem says that Garou who follow the Horse or Bull pack totem are well disposed to Father Mule, so those spirits of domesticated animals are well disposed to him. So while he may not have all animals spirits of horses, bulls, donkeys, etc., he may have some specific variety of such spirits in his brood (in the same way that multiple spirit broods have a Raven spirit in them). Probably quite a few pack animal versions of the spirits (camels, yaks, goats, llamas, etc.) may be included in the brood.

                                  After reviewing the W20 corebook's metis breed gifts, I see there are some elemental spirits, various animal spirits (rats, lizards, snakes, moles, wolves, chameleons, octopi, cats, birds, turtles, porcupines, spiders, venomous creatures), some epiphlings (spirits of hate, intellect, fury, trickery, madness), Lunes, and Chimmerlings. So if we assume that while such spirits outside this breed gift brood can also teach these Gifts, that Father Mule must have attracted a wide variety of (renegade/orphaned/lost) spirits to it. Personally, I assume that there was likely a concerted effort by many of the greatest ranked metis to win chiminage of some powerful jagglings or even lesser incarna to provide gifts to the Metis breed, and some of these ended up joining the brood of Father Mule. We're also told that the fifth level Totem Gift is taught directly by the Tribal Totem, but that is obviously a favor provided to the metis separate from any spirit brood that provides the other Gifts.

                                              For the ur-Homid ancestor spirit you could also perhaps do something similar to Wolf where, even if we want ‘Human’ or ‘Wolf’ itself to be beyond the range of contact, we could have ‘Old Man of the Field’ or something as the counterpart to Old Wolf of the Wood. One snag could be deciding whether or not the ur-homid brood-master should be specifically an ur-ancestor for human-born garou or a pan-human ur-ancestor type spirit. The big minefield is probably going to be whether or not these spirits have or can share accurate information about the origins of the Garou (and other changing breeds). For the sake of ease, one may want to avoid this by saying that the stories told are contradictory or that the ur-ancestor spirits don’t discuss such matters anymore (for whatever reason).

I could also see something like Old Teacher working as a catch-all for law-givers and tool-makers and various other legendary figures who teach hunting, weaving, agriculture, horticulture, writing, etc. (perhaps Old Teacher or Firemaker ought to also have some ties to Mages altho that’s not really our priority here of course).

                                              For the Garou-born, maybe if one wants a precursor to Father Mule, one could lean into the angle of the breed being unwanted and despised and have something like Foundling, a spirit born from things like infant exposure and bastardy, as an original brood leader (what Gryphon is to extinct and monstrous beasts, Foundling is to abandoned things). Various spirits who are on the outs with their original broods then might serve as a catch-all from which the garou-born can derive their pacts once they enter Foundling’s sphere of influence.

Behaviorally modern humans are around 50,000 years old, and anatomically modern humans around 200,000 years old. But archaic humans using fire is probably around 400,000 years old. So it predates lots of other things that distinguish humans from animals (or modern humans in general). There's also various lore in Werewolf that indicates the importance Garou hold on that.
