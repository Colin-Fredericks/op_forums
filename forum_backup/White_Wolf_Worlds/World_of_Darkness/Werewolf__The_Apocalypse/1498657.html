    Teaching Gifts between Breeds

    12-03-2022, 06:58 AM
    Does anyone have a super quick explanation for how teaching between breeds works?

    For instance, a Garou asks a rat spirit to teach them Danger Sense, a level 1 Tunnel Runner gift. The spirit refuses, naturally. The Garou *forces* them to, using a Gift or a Rite, or threats of destruction. What now?

    In another example, a Bastet tries to teach a Garou a Gift. What then?

    Does being in a mixed-breed Pack like the Ahadi help? Is it usually impossible? Are some gifts just beyond the Garou?

    Naturally some require anatomy they lack! And others the spirit world won't want them to have.

                      My take:
                      There were rules on this, iirc, in the revised Storyteller's Handbook. One should consider a Gift from another Fera-Breed to be one rank higher. Only Gifts up to rank 3 should be able to be learned and only if they do not require special anatomy of the changing breed to work.

                      In general Gifts aren't limited to replicating Charms, so basically any ability imaginable could be available as Gift if one finds a spirit who reasonably could bestow this ability. Of course the ST should keep the whole thing balanced. But discovering Gifts, or rediscover them, is a source for Renown. And Gifts, even within a Rank, vary in their relative power or usefulness. 'Rage across the Heavens' has Gifts that are hard to come by, taught/bestowed by difficult to find spirits, but more powerful than other Gifts in the same level.

                      Teaching Gift from Garou to Garou is regarded as wrong and rarely done, but possible, so Fera to Garou instruction would also be possible.

                              I believe theoretically most Gifts can be learned regardless if it is not normally available to that Fera. Meaning that the Gift is not impossible to learn or use. Gifts are actually more like a "blessing" bestowed upon one of the Fera, rather than a natural talent that can be improved or a skill to be learned. Even when a spirit is not the "teacher", the other Fera is just telling them the secret of being able to use that blessing.

                              If you think the Gift allows something that would be impossible for the person to do (it lacks the anatomy or whatever), then yeah feel free to disallow it. Don't do things that don't make sense to you.

                              Mechanically it is just a matter of extra XP and assigning the appropriate level of the Gift. I know in earlier editions, Gifts could be learned at different levels based on the Fera, breed, auspice, tribe, etc. I think that has become more streamlined, at least among Garou so you don't have the same Gift at different ranks depending on breed, auspice, tribe in W20. But the Fera lists could still very well have different Gifts at different ranks - I haven't looked closely enough at the various Fera lists. If that is all you are looking for, the boost it by 1 extra XP beyond the normal "out-of-breed/auspice/tribe, and decide what level of Gift it should be for that new breed.


                              However, the issue has always been one of IC repercussions and how the ST wants to handle that. The books are very clear that the spirits are very angered if they are not the ones bestowing the Gifts. In Axis Mundi we're told that agreeing to not teach a Gift to anyone else is one of the basic chiminage agreements to learn Gifts in the first place, although the IC chiminage to learn Gifts is usually handwaved in practice in most games. But we can assume that 1) spirits don't want non-spirits making the decisions of who learns what Gifts, and 2) the standard practice of learning Gifts from spirits should always include a prohibition on teaching it to others. In other words, doing so should always be seen as a violation and crime against the spirits. It doesn't matter what the reason - the other person is part of your pack, you think it is essential to accomplish some important goal for Gaia, it's your best friend, etc. You are breaking an important oath. And that gets punished in the same way violating the laws of gravity punishes you. It's a bedrock law among the spirits. I would think teaching something out-of-Fera breed is probably even worse as there is ostensibly a reason why spirits do not normally teach that Gift to the other breed.

                              But what does that mean in practice? This is where it gets harder as different STs run games very differently. But in broad measure I would recommend 1) the specific spirit who taught you that Gift becomes your enemy, 2) the person gets a lot of spirit notoriety in general, and 3) the buddies of the offended spirit (that spirit type or the entire spirit brood allied to it) is more offended. That is easy to say, but what are the consequences of that? IMO it should mean the spirits are less likely to teach spirits to that individual Fera (although I would say to be fair to players that they should still be able to learn their basic Gifts available to them, but perhaps with some delay in time, additional chiminage, or other inconvenience); that some spirits will actively complain to others about that individual even naming them and sometimes asking people to harass or punish them as part of a chiminage service they request of others; that spirits stop being friendly and won't answer questions, help that individual, etc. Each ST needs to figure out what is the right balance for their game in terms of preserving the setting, setting standards, but still keeping things fun for the players. Some STs will be very strict and others won't care at all about the roleplay. It's their game and their choice. I am not going to police it.

                              Of course, such individuals do not need to be in the doghouse forever. There can always be quests and chiminage designed to assuage the anger of the spirits, repent, spiritually purify themselves, and restore the status quo.

                                  It also seems important to point out something here:

                                  You need to know a Gift exists to ask a spirit to learn it. Garou don't get to read the gaming books, and know what's a Gift, what's a Background, what's a Merit, or whatever.

                                  The vast majority of the Garou have no idea what the unique Gifts of the Fera are to ask spirits to teach those Gifts to them.

                                  This is, inherently, the advantage of groups like the Beast Courts and the Ahadi: exposure to other changing breeds and more knowledge of what Gifts they know, which you could then try to learn yourself.

                                  Far before any mechanics need to come in to play, there is a basic rule for spending XP: You have to be able to explain how your character improved IC. If you can't explain how your PC obtained knowledge of a Gift to even start the process of learning that Gift, you can't buy it. That's an essential rule of character advancement in the game. Since most of the Fera are exceedingly guarded about sharing their unique Gifts, just don't make this easy.
