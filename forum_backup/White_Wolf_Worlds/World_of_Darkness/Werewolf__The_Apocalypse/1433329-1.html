    Only about 3 hours in, but I'm loving it so far. Enjoying the various Pentex references throughout and the main character and supporting cast are fine. Definitely could've used some better voice acting. It's been giving me Terminator: Resistance feelings, which was reviewed poorly, but I found it to be an excellent game, no doubt helped in part due to my love of the source material. Similar instance here, I'd say. Could Earthblood use some more polish? Absolutely. Developers could have certainly used a bigger budget(couldn't we all) and it's definitely not a AAA game, but it shouldn't stop any World of Darkness fan from having a ton of fun with it.

    I'm satisfied.

        Okay, so from an artist's/Lore fan's perspective;

        The visuals are great, the crinos forms LOOK crinos and behave crinos. You can move freely in lupus and lupus animation is solid. You can tell when a character is a Silver Fang or a Red Talon. The Caern Totem and other spirits look fitting.

        But then there is this Red Talon Caern that apparently does not tend to their spirits at all. Led by an Uktena(??) and they are framing her and her sept as WRONG for not liking humans.

        You also mostly fight humans/fomori, which works just fine but you see the same models most of the game. There is also the issue of having a Fianna Ahroun do stealth, which works as well as it does in canon. Lots of crinos use. Given Cahal is in a pack, too, it is just weird how much he does alone.

        Thrall of the Wyrm seems to be something of an endgame, since the Silver Fang leader gets killed by Cahal once he succumbs.


        For what it is, it is not bad. Just disjointed.
