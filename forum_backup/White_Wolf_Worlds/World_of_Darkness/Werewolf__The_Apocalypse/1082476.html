    Destroying the Garou Nation

    04-22-2017, 09:48 PM
    In the thread for the 5th Edition, one of the controversial items is the split in the Garou Nation down the lines of 'Traditional' vs 'Modern', and the way the divide fell between the Tribes.

    However, it occurred to me that some divisions already exist. Obviously, the Uktena and Wendigo haven't been happy with the way things have gone since Columbus arrived. The Shadow Lords are split into a seperate sub-tribe divided by their home range (East of the Carpathians to the Pacific Ocean are Haken). Baba Yaga's curtain causing yet another split, as the Tribes in Russia found them selves cut off from their main people, which would have put huge pressure on the Silver Fangs. The Amazon war found itself a Get of Fenris that discarded the typical stance on working with Bastet and Mokole, and the various Garou in Africa had to actually take a subservient role to the Simba and Ajaba over the course of two tyrants reigns and the building of the Ahadi. (And the Red Talons had to also put themselves beneath the Mokole) And don't forget the Stargazers ping ponging pack and forth. (As if they had the numbers to matter.)

    So what if the concept of a 'Garou Nation' simply couldn't hold up under the fractitious strain and it simply ceased to be?

    Instead of one, overriding voice in the world, more focus on local politics of the Tribes and how they get along?

                      If we look at it from a historic perspective, think about the Nation as Britain. When they started out to conquer new lands they managed to take Canada, the United States, South Africa, and Australia among other smaller grabs here and there. Eventually one those factions broke away from the first and in time the others did as well. Sure, they are all still allies but each primarily focuses on it corner of the world and their are always political barriers that need to be hurdled.

                      So, I have to agree with Aya to some degree. Right now the garou tribes may not like one another and their is internal strife but they are united under a common doctrine which guides them in their corner of the WoD. If you were to start breaking them up the way you are proposing then the most likely result is that the geographic coalitions would start to segregate themselves further. This separation of leadership would cause power struggles for the Wyrm to wiggle into and eventually it would start pitting faction against faction.

                      From a storytelling stand point this would be a nightmare. Right now you only need to deal with cultural differences on a tribal scale. If you were to throw in Factional cultural differences on top of that you would have to plan out all of your chronicles very differently. The Ahadi and the Courts are great but when outsiders come in they are at a disadvantage and a good part of it is mechanics like the way rank is handled in these places is different so if you want to gain rank you have to start over and each faction also has additional pools of gifts that aren't shared with outsiders.
