                        I think even if you ignore the previous editions and just want to go with the version of the Cult of Fenris as presented in the W5 corebook, there are issues in how they are portrayed.

                        In the adventure within the corebook called No Matter How Small you get two weird bits about the Fenrir. One, the Fenrir have abandoned a caern to look for Wyrm-followers to fight but it's established that when the PCs re-awaken the caern and settle down in it, now all of a sudden the Fenrir want the caern back. They care so little that they were willing to just abandon it but they also care so much about the caern that the moment other Garou move in they will intervene...? It's weird. Two, you never meet any Fenrir-Garou but instead you get to interact with Cult of Fenris' human allies - which is also weird. Why would this supposedly extremist Garou-tribe bother with human allies? Kinfolk isn't a thing anymore after all. There's also that lingering connotation again with the two human allies being portrayed as stereotypical rednecks. And I think what would make more sense here as well is that when the main-antagonist of this adventure who is a lone Fomor does his ritual at the abandoned caern one of the side-effects is that it attracts the attention of BSD rather than the Cult of Fenris. The Cult is out there looking for a good fight. And it would make more sense for the Fenrir only to start caring about the caern again once BSD start to move in. And so the follow-up to the adventure is a three-way-fight between the BSD, Fenrir and the PC-Pack rather than the straight-up PCs vs Fenrir contest the adventure advices the ST to pursue after the adventure.

                        With the Renegade-adventure Deepest of Wounds it's set up that until five years ago your local Sept was neighbors with a Cult of Fenris caern without any issues but then somehow their caern "dried up". And that's when the Cult of Fenris left and gave out the call that they wanted other Garou to join them (which some did). Then this adventure starts when the survivors of this mini-crusade come back to look for more resources and new recruits. What's weird is that without the whole kidnapping/torture/brainwashing-sub-plot all this would be about is the disagreement between Garou who think staying and guarding the caern is more important than venturing out to look for enemies and those who think something needs to be done about the miserable state of the world. And the easiest way to make sense of the kidnapping/torture/brainwashing-stuff is if these Garou who come back are actually converted BSD. On their mini-crusade these survivors got corrupted and fell to the Wyrm. So yeah, I think bringing BSD into this would make this adventure better.
