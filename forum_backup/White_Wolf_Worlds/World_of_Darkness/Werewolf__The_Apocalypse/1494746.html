      Mockery Breeds will have Rage and Gnosis unless otherwise stated.

      Mockery Breeds don't have any Renown to speak of unless one of the following happen; they become part of Gaia (a huge deal which would be earthshaking, and require great wisdom and hard work from forward-thinking Garou/Fera) or they fully surrender to the Wyrm and gain Renown through service to its third of reality; note that most corrupted beings are not fully turned over to act as puppets or direct servants, it's a big deal. Yeren are noted to have their own way of gaining Renown, though it's not formalized, it just mentions the kind of things that gain them renown.

      Generally speaking a Mockery will be stuck using only level 1 gifts, they have the potential to contain more but the spirit world does not respect, like or trust them. Even Yeren advance slowly, and they're as close as a Mockery Breed gets to being respected in the Wyrmish world - note that in Book of the Wyrm they have level 2 and 3 gifts, which is incredible, but no level 4 or 5 gifts. Samsa can learn Gifts from Cockroach and his Brood, but given that they are stuck at rank '0' they can only learn level 1 gifts.

      No idea for images, sorry!

                  Originally posted by Mr Gone View Post
                  twig I've got another question about Mockery Breeds. Do you think they'd have need for any Rites or Fetishes? I'm gearing up to start on the sheet, so I wondered if they need space for those...

                  Thanks in advance!
                  Wereapes have one Rite that we know of, in which they "Pass Over The Shadow Of The Ape", it is a rite which turns an adult human (a sociopathic lawyer, businessman, banker etc.) into one of them. The other Mockery Breeds don't have any Rites that we know of. This is because Rites are a special connection between Garou/Fera/Ananasi and the spirit world, a pact. If you do the rite correctly the spiritual laws of reality just bend to make something happen. This privilege is unique to groups who are recognized by the spirit world.

                  Technically any Mockery, such as a Samsa runaway, could have a Fetish in their possession, but it's very abnormal. I'd wager the only Mockeries who possess Fetishes with any regularity are, again, the Wereapes, as they are fast becoming the up-and-comers in Pentex. This gives them opportunities to purchase/steal/acquire Fetishes in ways others can't.

                  Basically with these sheets you do run into the fact that only the most recent creation, the Yeren, are an approximation of a regular Werebeast. Most of them will have very sparse sheets. And honestly that's fine - if you wanted to play a fully realized changing breed there are tones of options!

                                              I'd measure the point with samsa gifts on a balancing of factors:

                                              -Samsa are incapable of gaining renown, and cannot gain gifts from any tribal connection (Lets be honest, even the Glass Walkers are helping them begrudgingly because Cockroach tells them too).
                                              -They'll likely only gain a gift on personal tuitilage with a spirit. (This is possible as cockroach spirits are purposely, going out of their way, trying to help them as stated in Book of the Wyrm).
                                              -They're ONLY going to going to learn gifts outside a caern as its unlikely even the Glass Walkers would let them near one. (Something likely to be overlooked by cockroach spirits; maybe omiting need-of-tribute at least for the first gift for the sake of character creation).
                                              -Samsa cannot learn ANY OTHER gift than what cockroach spirits teach, as stated in BotW (they're still a mockery breed, so only cockroach will help them).
                                              -None of the cockroach taught gifts are below level three.
                                              -It will likely be VERY difficult, if not impossible, for the Samsa to gain another gift. Lacking renown may mean any cockroach spirit will likely demand more in tribute to compensate.


                                              Conclusion:

                                              Something HAS to give for this to be playable; even the possessed had slight modifications to differentiate them from NPC fomori and make them playable.

                                              So, here is a compromise. Rather than 3 level one gifts at character creation, a starting player Samsa (this wouldn't work with other mockeries) would instead get 1 level three gift.

                                              Gaining any other gift would be MUCH more difficult and expensive than the equivalent would for a garou, AND they can only gain gift from cockroach spirits. (Gaining new gift for a Samsa could potentially be a significant event for them in the story).

                                              Learning a gift via a shapechanger mentor would likely work about the same as it would for normal garou, excluding some awkwardness that may make it more difficult.

                                              Note: really only 2 options of gift, 3 for a metis (which Samsa DO have):
                                              -Intrusion.
                                              -Render Down.
                                              -Wriggle (for metis)

                                              (Spirit Path is worthless to a Samsa as they are COMPLETELY INCAPABLE of sidestepping into the Umbra, as stated in BotW).
