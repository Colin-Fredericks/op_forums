                                                Heres the fallen Nagah and some more homebrews/headcanons

                                                The fallen Nagah known as the Vritra, or the Drought Kings. They are not the Crown of India and Africa but take the name to both mock the loyal Nagah and to spread more dissent among the changing breeds, hoping the Garou will slaughter the loyal Nagah crown out of mistaken identity much as the Bunyip were slain. The human memories of the Drought Kings have permanently associated the word Vritra with drought.

                                                The Drought Kings almost always fall to the Wyrm through there Ananata, the den-realm that all Nagah have. The Ananata is naturally full of water, but somehow those of the Nagah who become the Drought Kings are drained dry. How this happens varies, but when it does and if the Nagah survives the loss (given it usually happens violently) and the subsiquint hunting by the other Nagah (as they recognize the wrongness of a drained Ananata), drives them to the Wyrms service, as being deprived of the connection to the sacred element of water drives the Nagah utterly mad.

                                                Drought Kings wage an eternal shadow war upon the Nagah, taking every opportunity to mock there still loyal counterparts. While most Drought Kings are solitary, when a Nest is formed it is always three Drought Kings, each representing a Triantic Wyrm head. Those who serve the Beast of War are savage brutes, those who serve the Eater of Souls are gluttinous and wasteful, and those of the Defiler are cunning and scheming.

                                                The Drought Kings as a whole serve Atlacoya, a Bane totem of Strength. Once a Gaian Incarna of drought, she was one of the first totems to be corrupted by the Wyrm. Atlacoya seeks to reduce all of reality to naught but a dry plain and watch as the few survivors vicously fight over what little food and water remains. Like Ammit’s realm, the drought stricken plains that serve as Atlacoya’s realm are isolated from the rest of Malfeas, but unlike Ammit Atlacoya does not have plans to reestablish those ties. No, instead she is focused on her plan to reduce Mexico and South America (which is the place that her realm connects to in the material world) into a drought stricken wasteland.

                                                Atlacoya also gives her blessing to the Black Spiral Dancers, and Bastards who follow Atlacoya never need water or hydration and can cause dehydration in others. The ban is particularly damaging, for it decrees that they must never let blood water the ground. Her servants masters of harming without spilling blood as a result.

                                                Heres the general stuff

                                                This is probably canon, but I see the entire Triat as utterly insane and hostile to reality. If the Garou managed to defeat the Wyrm and completely remove its influence, then the Weaver or the Wyld would quickly replace it as the apocalypse causing threat.

                                                --------------

                                                Apophis from the Midnight Circus and the main antagonist of Mummy the Resurrection is actually a head of the Hydra/Corrupted Wyrm. It would have been the fourth of the Triatic Wyrm heads, but something happened to make it the most independent of the heads, even able to slither out on its own into the deep umbra.

                                                Apophis is also the Hydra’s back up plan. Should the Garou or some other force somehow manage to do the impossible and destroy the Hydra, then Apophis would quickly grow in size, power, and heads to become a new Corruptor Wyrm. This new Wyrm would be even more powerful than the original, because unlike the original it can move in the Umbra instead of being bound by the Weavers webs.

                                                The only way to avoid this scenario is to free the true Wyrm, the Balance Wyrm’s freedom would destroy the Hydra entirely and force both Weaver and Wyld back into balance.

                                                --------------

                                                Queen Ananasa and Gaia are both extremely overestimated by the Changing Breeds. Yes they are both extremely powerful spirits, even by Celestine standards, but they are not equal or superiors to the Triat.

                                                --------------

                                                The Yama Kings, like most vampires, do not actively serve the Wyrm. Oh sure, they do the work of the corrupter, but so do a great many humans.

                                                ---------------

                                                The phenomenon of Skin Dancers/Skin Walkers has existed since the Banestorm back in prehistory. The skin dancer tribe of Samuel Haight is merely a recent version and made prominent by the urban legend status of Haight. It is not just Garou either, as technically the rites can be adapted for any shifter. A great example of this exists in the Raven Mockers, skin dancers of the Corax.
