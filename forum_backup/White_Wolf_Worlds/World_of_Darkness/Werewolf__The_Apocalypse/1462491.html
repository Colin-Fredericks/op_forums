    W20 Totem bonuses and stat limits

    10-02-2021, 07:58 PM
    Greetings,

    Can Totem granted stats (Attributes or Skills) put a Werewolf above 5 in a trait? If not, what happens with those extra dots?

    (The case comes from someone that has Strength 5 and Brawl 5, with Polar Bear as Totem you get +1 Strength and can get +2 Brawl too).

    Thanks

      There's never been a good answer to this from the devs.

      The Totem write-ups have (for multiple editions) been a mess. You have two effective choices: treat the phrasing differences as meaningful and carefully parse each Totem carefully (+2 Subterfuge means your Subterfuge is actually higher for any rolls with a difficulty based on your Subterfuge rating, vs. +2 dice to Subterfuge which means your Subterfuge is still X, but you get two dice when rolling it), or treat it as writers trying to avoid sounding repetitive and use consistent applications.

      Both options are going to run into official rules that don't seem right, which is part of the problem.

          Heavy Arms is right about the write-ups being a mess. I never thought +2 Subterfuge to be a increase in difficulty though. Because: I general the bonuses aren't given "per character" but "per pack". Meaning, that you should grant the bonuses to characters from the pack as the pack sees fit, and reassign them on a turn-to-turn basis if necessary. You explicitly can overspend on Totem background to get additional features and 3 dots in Totem more than the base cost is effectively that the totems "powers" are granted to another pack member.

          What isn't clear, but I usually was assumed, is that the bonuses can be split. So, I usually allow for bonuses to increase ratings above 5, but even if I didn't then the bonus of +2 Subterfuge would be two dots, to be divided within the pack, imo.

          But, yeah, you could make it differently and still be within a reasonably valid interpretation of what one can read in the rules.
