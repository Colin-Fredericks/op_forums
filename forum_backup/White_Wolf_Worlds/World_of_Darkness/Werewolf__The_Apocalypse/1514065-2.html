                    the Wyrm as it is known today, the traitic wyrm, the urge wyrms, and the elemental wyrms, are not the true Balancer Wyrm. The Hydra and those who serve it do not want to free the real Wyrm, as it would destroy them on principle. No, they want to loosen the chains holding the Hydra back from rampaging.

Rich and Powerful humans selling there souls to the Wyrm, poisioning the earth and being the bane of the Garou. Pentex is merely the latest and most successful of these orginizations, and the one most likely to succeed in the Omega Plan, which all of these organizations have had in some form.

It is only a matter of time before Neuro-Dynamic Labratories begins to churn out Minotaurs (Mockeries of the noble Apis), Hog Walkers (Mockeries of the Grondr), Bloodlicks (Mockeries of the Camazotz), Saber Maws (Mockeries of the Khara) and, once they get there hands on the work of Cernonous, Hollow Walkers (Mockeries of the Bunyip).

                                        more homebrews and headcanons

                                        More on Mockery Breeds (if you want to use the ones I create, please ask me first)

                                        Mockery Breeds can only be made from the dna of Gaian shifters who are dead, or are very close to dying out.

                                        The War Wolves are made using DNA from the various dead/dying Garou breeds, such as the half eaten remenants of the Croatoan and the Singing Dogs (who might as well be dead, given there are four left).

                                        The hollow walkers, once they become a true mockery breed, will be a source of tension between the Black Spiral Dancers and Pentex. The dancers consider the Hollow Walkers theres, as the first pack and there creator walked the spiral. However, Pentex argues that Bunyip were different enough to nullify the argument. Enterprising Garou could use this to sow dissent in the Wyrms ranks.

                                        From the Apis come the Minotaurs, who are obsessed with mazes and used by Pentex as guards for various things. The influence of the Minotaur totem upon them is a cause for concern among Pentex management. They completely lack any gifts and magics that the Apis used as Gaias matchmakers, though they are very lusty.

                                        From the Camazotz come the Bloodlicks, beasts of bat and human merged together. They were an attempt at making sneaky spies, but they quickly proved uncontrollable as there bloodlust was insatiable and the death screams of the Camazotz and the rage of the Bat Bane totem and the attempts of the Gaian Bat Totem to redeem them makes them utterly insane. Pentex tried to put them down, but they failed when the Bloodlicks proved to be able to split into swarms of bats. Now the Bloodlicks wage an endless war against both Wyrm and Gaian forces, and will only settle upon one side once Bat has.

                                        From the Grondr come the Hogzillas, a very successful Mockery Breed using modern day boars and pigs. All very fat no matter the form, they are the exact opposite of the Grondr in function. Where the Grondr cleansed spiritual areas, the Hogzillas defile the land. They radiate an aura of uncleanness, and Pentex creates them frequently, even if they are unpleasant to be around. The only problem is that the Skull Pigs, upon encountering Hogzillas, will attack them to the exckusion of all else. Even if there is a raging Garou pack bearing down on them, the Skull Pigs will focus there full effort on killing the Hogzillas.

                                        From the Khara come the Saber Maws, a successful mockery breed often deployed alongside the Kerasi. They posess a strange ability to grow bones from there bodies and even manipulate the bones of others.

                                        From the Okuma come the savage Aku-Kuma, this mockery is perverted, lazy, gluttonous, and blood thirsty. Despite the wyrm taint in there souls, they have a cute and cuddly animal form. Pentex uses them in various showbussiness ventures such as zoo displays, tv shows, and other concerns. The Aku-Kuma love to corrupt young kids, which is exactly what Pentex has them do. In spite of this, there loyalty to the company is completely dependent on the company providing them the depths of sleaze and vice that can be accessed by fame and money. The company is more than happy to provide it, but that could change.

                                        From the Ao Varna come the Terror-pins, a powerful breed perfectly suited to various tactics that Pentex could once not acess. They are masters of manipulating memory and the Mnesis, and are also able to manipulate the elements. One of there favorite tactics is to manipulate dreams, turning them into advertisements for Pentex products. They also plant false memories of betrayal in the companies foes, delighting when raging Garou slaughter each other over a false memory.

                                        From the Ratkin Bards come the Pipers, and unlike most mockery breeds they do not originate from Pentex. Instead they arise from an old Wyrm cult from mideval times, with Pentex finding the methods to make them in the modern day. The Pipers mo is like that of the famous pied piper, ie they drive a plague of vermin to a town and offer to get rid of it for a price. The plague will be bad enough that they accept, and once the plague is gone one of two things will happen. One is that the town will willingly stiff the Piper out of greed or poverty. The other is that the town will pay the piper, but the Piper will up the prize to ridicoulus levels that the town will refuse to pay. Either way, the Piper retailiates by stealing all the children of the town, using there pipes to lure them just as they lure vermin and banes. The fate of the children is horrible, as those with even a trace of Kinfolk blood in them are given to the various Wyrm Shapeshifters, much as the Ratkin kinfolk were taken by the Garou. The rest are taken to Malfeas to become slaves, sacrified to uncaring Banes, or other more nightmarish fates.

                                        --------------


                                        When a Changing Breed goes extinct or is otherwise lost, the Wyrm seals away the fallen members of there breed in a stasis alongside other forgotten horrors such as the Midnight Shadows and the fallen Grondr (who will be identical to the Hogzillas, as Skull Pigs are not technically shapeshifters any more). But once the Apocalypse is in full swing, the Wyrm is going to crack open those seals and let the long forgotten monsters out, letting the fallen Nagah slither out and the Xibalbans fly once more.

                                        ------------------

                                        The Ratkin in the modern day are basically the Skaven, being corrupted by the Wyld and having various Wyld-tainted splinter totems of Rat that are all inspired by the faces of the Great Horned Rat.

                                        -----------------

                                        The deception of the Nagah is not complete. They have a presence in the Beast Courts, and rumors of snake shifters exist in many places. Since banes can not possess them, the fallen Nagah are sealed away by the Wyrm, but there continued existence means no Mockery Breed can be made from there remains.

                                        ---------------------

                                        My next post will be on the fallen breeds from the Fera that don’t have them, as well as an idea on the Simba. But what you think of this stuff?

                                                Arcanus , thanks for your kind words, and now heres the next post.

                                                More headcanons and homebrews

                                                Inspired by the W20 Baset Breedbook, I think that the Simba are in a pecarcarious position. While Black Tooth is dead, his followers are mostly alive. If the Simba allow there pride and the manipulation of the former members of the Endless Storm to lead them into the Wyrms corruption, then the Simba will become the Scarred.

                                                The Scarred are to the Bastet what the Black Spiral Dancers are to the Garou. All are servants of the newest Bane Totem, that being Holo the Rhinoceros Lord, and the Simba Homeland will become a new Wyrm Realm like the Spiral Homeland. The Scarred Realm, as there homeland is now known, enters a permanent night, a symbol of Helios retracting his blessing.

                                                To become one of the Scarred, one must endure a hunt upon the savanah of the Scarred Realm while banes, wyrmish monstrosities, and the Scarred all hunt them. This is the fate of all captured Bastet, and the only way to survive is to accept Cahlash as ones truest lord and master.

                                                At the hunts end, the new Scarred earns there name, making a lasting scar on there body using either Silver or Gold (if Simba aren’t vunerable to Gold, a sign of Helios turning on them). Only true Simba can gain a leadership position, and the Scarred target there uncorrupted breatheren above all others, for not all Simba fell.

                                                The hellcats and Histpah still exist, but they quickly become subdivisions of the Scarred.

                                                ------------------

                                                Now the fallen breeds for those who don’t have them. I will count skull pigs for Grondr, and fallen Gurhal are in the MET book of the wyrm

                                                The Apis who fell to the Wyrm became the Moloch. Like the ancient god whose name they take, the fallen Apis revel in killing children and using fire, or rather Balefire, to kill those who oppose them. Unlike the modern day Minotaur Mockery Breed, the Kao have full acess to the charms and magics that allow the Apis to be Gaia’s matchmakers, and they use these powers to great effect.

                                                In the ancient times, the Moloch would create entire vilages devoted to worshiping them, and would often sacrifice the unfit children to bane totems and heads of the hydra. These blasphemous villages attracted Gaian defenders, but the defenders were powerful formori who through twisted magics bred true. The Moloch are now in stasis like the Xibalbans, but they left one last curse upon the foes of the Wyrm. They pooled there magics together to create the Minotaur Bane totem, and now slumber awaiting the Wyrms call at the end of days.

                                                The fallen kitsune exist in two types. The first is the Kyuubi No Akuma created by Autochthon, found here (https://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/...ave-lost-faith).

                                                But there exists another breed, one that is truly devoted to the Wyrm, and is known to exist among the kitsune that exist outside of the middle kingdom (for they do exist, just hidden like most Fera who do not live in the Beast Courts). They are the Huli Jing, and they are no cowards like the devil foxes who sell themselves to the thousand hells.

                                                While the reason for there fall may vary (a great many decry the unfairness of the curse), when the kitsune who becomes a Huli Jing falls, they fall to the elemental Wyrms. Where they were once tied to the elements of Gaia, now they tie themselves to the pollution of the Wyrm. They serve the Elemental Wyrms proper, and care only for there dark goal of replacing all the elements of Gaia with there Wyrmish counterparts, though they will obey the elemental majein and with other heads of the hydra, a few even having positions in Pentex and its subsidiaries as consultants, possessing a much better relationship with the company than the black spiral dancers.

                                                The Huli Jing paths keep the names of there uncorrupted kin as a calculated insult, but the elements are very different. All are nightmarish, and the kin of Renard (as kitsune are often called outside of the middle kingdom) must fight the fallen with all there might. Each path has a nickname that is more commonly used.

                                                The Eji of the Wyrm, known as the Mutators, are warriors who love to kill in gruesome wayxs, of which there are many by the usage of there element of Ooze. A combination of Sludge and Balefire, it is a powerful mutagenic substance that functions much like mutagens do in science fiction works, but also with horrible side effects. A normal human exposed to the Ooze become nightmares unimagined as trace amounts of animal DNA are unlocked, creating things like a Fomor or a shifter that lives its short life in constant agony, or has junk DNA unlocked to make things Gaia never meant to occur. The supernatural fare even worse, as the Ooze makes even more extreme mutations out of the unnatural powers. Even spirits are not safe from the Ooze, as Pattern Spiders soon gain elements of things like rats or scorpions or what have you. Not even Banes are safe, but these wyrmish spirits endure the mutations very well. Pity the kinfolk who is exposed to the Ooze, as they soon become a chimera of nightmarish proportions as every shifter bloodline, no matter how distant, manifests at once, possibly resulting in a thing that has the muzzle of a wolf, the wings of a bat, the legs of a giant spider, and the gills of a shark.

                                                The Doshi of the Wyrm, known as Smokers, are magic weavers that use the element of Smoke. A combination of Smog and Balefire, the element of Smoke is gaseous poison in all its forms. The Smokers inflict lung cancer and horrible diseases upon all who challenge them. Not even the Garou can fight at full strength when there lungs have been reduced to a lump of charcoal.

                                                The Gukutsushi of the Wyrm, known as Trippers, are illusionists much like there Gaian counterparts, but they create there illusions through the element of Intoxicant. The Trippers combine Toxin and Smog to create and secrete drugs and alcohol at every instant. Each breath they take comes out as cigarette or vape smoke, there claws hollow like needles and inject heroin, and there tail drip hallucinogens. There toxins can affect any being, and they love to addict the changing breeds to unique drugs only the Wyrm can provide them.

                                                The Kataribe of the Wyrm, known as the Slick, are not storytellers but saboteurs, using the element of Oil to slip in anywhere and destroy morale. The blasphemous child of Sludge and Toxins, Oil is perhaps the strongest of the combination Wyrm elements that the Huli Jing embody and advance, for it is so ubiquitious. More pain and Wyrm Taint have had Oil at there source than any other, and the Slick work to keep it that way. They are experts at slipping past defenses and committing sabotage of all types, from assassination to destruction of knowledge and beyond.

                                                I’m gonna have to do my fallen Nagah ideas another time, but I want peoples thoughts on this stuff.
