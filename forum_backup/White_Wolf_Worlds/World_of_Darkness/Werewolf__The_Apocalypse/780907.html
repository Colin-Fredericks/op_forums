          I can't tell if Dwight is being sarcastic.

          I definitely feel Werewolf has a strong left wing bias, and especially in first edition there is a sense that Wyrm taint is anything double unplus good. Whether that shows up in any chronicle depends on the ST though.

          It doesn't have to be that way though.

          In my games, I only apply Wyrm taint to things that are either "unnatural", supernatural evil, or intrinsically unhealthy to Gaian life (Earth's biosphere) - that includes radiation, cancer, toxic chemicals, any number of carcinogens, etc. I shy away from anything that makes it look like a particular political ideology is "tainted" - although I have no problem showing particular individuals in any political tradition being of the Wyrm provided they engage in obvious evil.

          I use Wyrm taint as a kind of spiritual pollution or miasma, so people can show taint who aren't evil themselves simply because they are exposed to it.

          As an ST, I use it as a way to prod PCs to investigate story hooks, not as a "detect evil" that doesn't need the players to think.

                      It depends too much on the group, ST and players, because it depends on your view of the game and also in your ideologies, shared and, specially, not shared. If you all strongly agree that a given idea is evil, who am I to go against you making it Wyrm-Tainted in your game? But for me, as an example, even the ideas I'm strongly against shouldn't be instantly Wyrm-Tainted, because I like to make ideological differences a thing in my games.

                      What you have to be aware, aside tastes among your troupe (that you should to be aware anyway), is ideological differences between you. No matter how much you thing idea X is wrong, someone, maybe someone you don't expect, will think it right. And maybe you'll hurt this person if you present her idea as Wyrm-Tainted.

                      An RPG is the perfect place for the ST to make every political view it wants completely correct. That can be incredible disgusting for players who don't share this view. DON'T use this to market your ideas.
