                                                            Originally posted by Psicose View Post

                                                            Don't you want to rethink that sentence? If art wants to say something, and W5 wants to say that the world is lost then that could also be seen as the author's point of view. I know people who think like this.
                                                            The issue is that the W5 corebook doesn't commit to it. You get 90% pessimism about how everything sucks and then these claims about how young Garou are supposed to do better. But there's very little to no word-count committed to the idea of what it even means for young Garou to "do better". And at the same time, you got plenty of word-count on why Garou suck and why the world is doomed.

                                                            The issue isn't that W5 can't have a different goal but that the way that corebook pursued this "re-imagined" goal was lackluster.
