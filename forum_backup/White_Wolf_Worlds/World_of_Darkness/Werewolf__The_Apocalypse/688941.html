          To frame my response, I am not a changeling person, never understood the appeal of the game. That said, based on everything I have read, I don't see anything outwardly that links them directly to the Wyrm. According to the book "The Shadow Court", the Fianna deal with the Seelie mostly but those of a less traditionalist nature have been known to deal with the Unseelie on occasion. The Shadow lords had also been known to make brief alliances with the Unseelie, but both sides don't trust one another and are likely to betray the other so their alliances don't tend to last long. House Balor and the Black Spiral Dancers have a long standing alliance with one another (Which used to be reflected in the BSD gift Unseelie Faerie Kin, not sure if that one is still around).

          All in all most of the Changing Breeds do not like to deal with the Fae, most don't trust them and their reputation among them has been justly earned. If I remember correctly, the Get of Fenris kill both sides indiscriminately for some slight they Fae made on them in the bronze age.
