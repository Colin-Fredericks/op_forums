                                                            I'm talking about different kinds of stories when I reference the "breeding elements" thing - the removal of Kinfolk and Metis has fallout.

                                                            The Black and Native American boy who grew up with an ultra-conservative Uktena grandmother who knew he would be Garou but never thought he was good enough, who drove him away from his own people with her impossible expectations for her blood.

                                                            The Stargazer Theurge who grew up with a Lupus father visiting her and her sick mother once a month... once a year... once every two years... and as she gets older and older the visits get scarcer, until they stop entirely. He couldn't smell the wolf on her; she was Kinfolk. He probably had a lot of other kids to visit. And so she devoted every waking moment to saving Gaia as a Kinfolk. To getting her PhD in Environmental Conservation, to fighting for reintroduction of Wolves and Birds of Prey into environments where they dwelled historically, to- and then she has her belated First Change. And guess who is suddenly father of the year?

                                                            The PB5 Fianna Galliard who grew up among the Mother's Fundamentalists, monstrous people who wanted perfection and only perfection. Only they were pure. Even other Fianna were failures. Bitter, hateful people, who tried to make them into the 'perfect son', a role they could never fulfil. And so they run away from their extensive tightknit Garou family to join a pack of outcasts their family would despise.

                                                            The Metis Child of Gaia who was raised by Kinfolk. Who knows his parents left him to be raised by supportive and kind guardians, but will never know who they were or why they broke the Litany. For love? For lust? Because trauma brought them together?
