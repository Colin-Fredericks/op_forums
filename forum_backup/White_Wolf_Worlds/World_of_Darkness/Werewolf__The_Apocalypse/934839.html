    Competent Kinfolk

    07-20-2016, 09:27 PM
    One of the rules concerning sorcerers is that they can be Kinfolk (as well as Kinain, Ghouls, Rom, etc). Kinfolk Sorcerers are much more competent than standard Kinfolk, they begin with a minimum of five levels of Numina or, if you use the Equality for All rules, they have higher Attributes, Abilities, Backgrounds, Numina, etc than the standard Kinfolk. I was curious if anyone uses more competent Kinfolk in their stories, how they interact with their Kin, etc.

                  I do not know if Kinfolk are that rare. Eighty percent of the population of the Garou probably come from Kinfolk-Kinfolk unions (the majority of the members of the Garou seem to die before they get around to reproducing and, even then, they produce Kinfolk around ninety percent of the time) and, if eighty percent of them are Human Kinfolk unions, the population of human Kinfolk to Garou would probably be around one hundred-to-one (when you take in account the fact that fifty percent of Kinfolk who probably mate with mortals, especially in the contemporary era). Even if Garou are only 1:500,000 of the global human population, that would mean that Garou Human Kinfolk are 1:5,000 of the global human population. While they are rare, they are not that rare, as there would be around sixty-four thousand of them in the US.

                  Kinfolk are probably much more likely to be open to the idea of Numina and are probably much more likely to seek to learn Numina so that they can be useful to their Garou Kin. I would think that their Gaian blood would also give them a much higher probability of developing Psychic Phenomena. I would think that the proportion of the Kinfolk population who have developed a minimum of one level of Numina would be around 1:2 and that the number of Kinfolk who have developed Numina up to a PC Sorcerer level would be 1:100, making them as common as their Garou Kin. Of course, they would still be relegated to doing the basic upkeep of Garou society, but I imagine that they would receive more respect from the Garou that the average Human Kinfolk.

                          A few Numina will not make a Kinfolk equal to one of the Garou. I think I was able to price the advantages that a Garou has over a comparable Kinfolk at around 100 bonus points, plus or minus 25 bonus points, so you would have to have a Kinfolk with around 14 more levels of Numina than a Garou has Gifts to get anywhere near the same level of competence. And that Kinfolk will never be a combat monster, regardless of the build, because they just cannot match the combat power of a Garou.

                          That is why I think that competent Kinfolk are relegated to the basic upkeep of Garou society. While competent Kinfolk may be useful agents, they should never try to fight like they are Garou. Heck, no one should try to fight like Garou unless they are another type of Fera, and some of them are better off on the sidelines in any case.

                                  Well, I think that the two level one Hakken Gifts would be actually much more interesting for a Kinfolk (or Garou!) to possess. The first, Dream of a Thousand Cranes, requires you to choose a die pool, make an origami crane that represents that die pool, and roll Charisma plus Occult (difficulty 6). Each success adds one die to that die pool for the remainder of the scene, meaning that you could increase Ability die pools, Attribute die pools, Damage die pools, Initiative die pools, Soak die pools, etc with minimal effort (I would personally rule that each use of the Gift replaces the previous use of the Gift during the same turn, to prevent people from making Superman). The second, Fair Path, requires you to dip your hand in clean water and roll Charisma plus Etiquette (difficulty 6). Each success adds one die to any roll involving Social Attributes for the remainder of the scene, meaning that a social build character (Charisma 5 and Etiquette 5) could effectively increase any die pools that use Social Attributes by five for a scene. An optimized Kinfolk with just those two level one Gifts (which would take some doing after character creation with the default Kinfolk character creation rules) could potentially increase any die pool by seven dice and gain five bonus die on any roll involving Social Attributes for a scene (meaning that if they are choosing to boost Etiquette, they could boost their Etiquette by twelve dice for a scene).
