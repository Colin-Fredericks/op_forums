    the introduction of new fetishes in the sept

    07-31-2015, 10:56 PM
    How are new Fetishes introduced into your game? I'm not talking about new types of Fetish. But Fetishes in general, especially those the PCs get after character creation.

    It's something that vexes me. Fetishes seem like it should be an important part of the game, but they aren't quite the equivalent of D&D's golfbag of Swords +1.

    I see them as something the local Sept Elders would give to heroes and warriors to reward them for service in the same way the old barbarian chiefs would give their companions golden armbands and such. Of course, the chiefs could also give their warriors loot seized from the enemy. Garou can't give out many fetishes won in battle because the foes they fight don't have them (or if they did, they'd likely be Wyrm Fetishes the Garou would not want).

    So we're left with Fetishes that are obviously hanging around, most likely the fetishes they won themselves earlier on. But there is a big deal of scarcity in that. Only so many Fetishes exist, and those that do, their owners probably want to keep most of them for themselves. This makes it unlikely for many Garou to be rewarded that way. There is also the issue that many Fetishes are easy to be destroyed, so not many older Fetishes likely exist.

    So it seems to me that new Fetishes have to be created constantly. Perhaps by the Sept Elder(s) asking the resident high powered Theurge(s) to create new Fetishes just for the purpose of rewarding the younger Garou (at least the low powered Fetishes that seem to be common; especially Talens). There might also be a small stock of older/rarer Fetishes that belongs to the Sept, that are given out to especially promising Garou (who intend to remain at the sept, and won't move away to live at another sept).

    Is that how others do it? Do you introduce Fetishes differently? Where do Fetishes come from in your chronicles.

      Some septs and/or tribes (like the Fianna and Silver Fangs) have ancestral fetishes that are passed down from father to son (or grandson or great-grandson, depending on how many generations are skipped until a descendant breeds true). I suppose a sept would keep such a fetish in trust until a Garou of that family line would breed true, unless of course the Apocalypse War was going badly when a descendant hadn't yet come along and the fetish needed to be given to a worthy Garou in the meantime.

      This brings up the question of who is the proper person to get the fetish? Hammer & Klaive says that the wishes of the deceased previous owner is sometimes taken into account of who should inherit the fetish. As noted above, sometimes blood relations is taken into account. If the deceased had a protege who was not a blood relation, the elders may decide that the protege would be more deserving of inheriting the fetish.

      Remember also, that some fetishes, particularly powerful ones, have been lost on the field of battle and that some Garou septs organize search parties to retrieve them. (On occasion, this is even done to retrieve fetishes from Malfeas of all places if the fetish is particularly powerful and it is determined that the fetish can be retrieved without significant loss.)

      So far as I know, fetishes come into chronicles in one of several ways: they are either made via rites, they are passed down from earlier generations or they are retrieved from the field of battle after having been lost for a time.

      For more information, see Hammer & Klaive; that's a great book for information on fetishes.

                          it depends on a lot of variables.

                          Rank is certainly a factor. Higher-level Garou are going ot have more, and more important fetishes, as a general rule of thumb. or at least, they're going to have better access to their sept's trove of fetishes. However it can go the other way too. An elder Garou who is strongly reliant on his fetishes is going to be something of a joke, for most garou. basically just becuase you have the entitlement doesn't necessarily translate into making constant use of it.

                          Tribe is another factor. Uktena and Glass walkers are likely to have a fetish for every occasion, regardless of rank. They're very fetish-oriented societies. Fianna are far less of a fetish society, and tend to stick with a handfull of 'tried and true" designs, same with Silver Fangs. Stargazers are actively anti-fetish, seeing both imprisonment of spirits and relaince on material things as setbacks to spiritual progress. And htne you have stuff like the get of Fenris - the get of Fenris don't have a high opinion of fetish weapons (klaives and jarlhammers aside). After all, the mightiest warriors of gaia don't need flying spears and fang daggers to get the job done! Rather, Get of Fenris prefer fetishes that shore up their "weak spots" - an amulet with a unicorn-spirit that helps a garou lessen the curse around mortals is more valuable to the average Get of Fenris than an axe that does bonus damage against fomori or something.

                          Auspice too - Ahroun are, again not big fans of fetish weapons. Theurges actually have the most of those. Garou society strongly supports Theurges and Philodox having more fetishes than galliards or Ahroun. Ragabash rarely learn Rite of the Fetish (out of fear that some 'clever" no moon will try binding a pack totem into a pair of wal-mart flip flops or whatever) but those who do, of course, come up with some pretty innovative stuff.

                          As a general rule, homids use more fetishes than the other two breeds

                          So... the elder Theurge uktena is basically going to be covered in fetishes and packing some serious weaponry, while the cliath Stargazer Ahroun might have like, a rock that he picked up and threw at you.

                          "Hammer and Klaive" is a really good resource for all your fetish needs.

                                  Originally posted by Black Fox View Post
                                  Let me put it a different way, if you are running a game and you have a pack of PCs. All start with no fetishes. Start as Cliaths naturally. In very general terms, when would you introduce appropriate Fetishes for them to use themselves and personally own? I know specifics of the characters and game will always matter, but I'm looking for guidelines here.
                                  Hammer & Klaive doesn't mention how a pack would become owners of new fetishes; it rather answers the question from the opposite direction: how a fetish would pass from an old owner to a new owner. I'm not sure there are any specific guidelines to answer the question of how a up-and-coming pack would get awarded fetishes. It's not like Garou septs would say, "Well, Fostern Heart-of-Gaia, you killed a fomor today, so here's your Harmony Flute as your prescribed reward!" (Forgive my mild sarcasm here, but I'll try to answer your question as best I can.) I'm not sure there are any specific guidelines detailing how a pack would get fetish rewards in return for certain deeds. One needs to remember that fetishes are not (just) objects with cool powers, but because of the spirits (hopefully) willingly contained in the fetish, are very often considered revered objects or even comrades and partners in the Apocalypse War. While I wouldn't go so far to say that Garou offer chiminage (did I spell that right?) exactly to the spirit inside the fetish, abiding by taboos that some more powerful fetishes have (like "Do not use this fetish in winter" or "Meditate on the fetish under Luna's light at least once every week") can count as a close form of chiminage. The point is, fetishes are more than simply weapons or tools, they're the physical embodiment of the Pact between spirits and Garou. As such, fetishes are oftentimes considered helpmeets and allies as opposed to just "objects" or "rewards" and need to be treated as such. To my mind, that makes the idea of guidelines for fetish rewards the wrong answer to the question of how to gain spirit allies that just happen to be bound in fetishes.

                                  But if you're looking for ideas on how your pack can get fetishes, perhaps a few of the spirits have noticed the pack's deeds and actually decide to volunteer to be bound into fetishes made by (or for) the pack. And they, of course, don't even have to be the same types of spirits. If, say, a war-spirit, an owl-spirit and a cat-spirit each (or unanimously) decided they want to serve as fetishes to the pack, then I can see the war-spirit sending dream-visions to the pack's Ahroun of the Ahroun wielding a klaive (or other appropriate weapon fetish) with that war-spirit bound in it. I could also see the cat-spirit sending such dreams to the pack's Ragabash of using a fetish bound with the cat-sprit that assists in stealth rolls. Furthermore, I can see the owl-spirit sending such dreams to the Theurge with the owl-spirit bound into a fetish that helps with spirit/Umbral lore. Well, the fetishes can have whatever type of spirit or powers you want, but the point is, the spirits who are impressed by the pack may effectively decide to petition the pack-members themselves to be bound into fetishes instead of having the elders reward the pack.

                                  Of course, if most members of the pack don't know the necessary Rites to actually create the fetishes they and the spirits want to make, that raises a few more story possibilities. Can the pack and the friendly spirits petition the sept elders to have the sept's elder Theurge create the fetishes? If so, what sort of deeds or favors can the pack perform in return for the creation of the fetishes? Are certain hard-to-obtain ingredients needed to create the fetishes and will the pack have to quest to find them? If such a quest involves an Umbral journey, will the allied spirits be willing to accompany the pack? And if they aren't (an unlikely possibility if the spirits truly want to help the characters but some spirits are capricious like that), what then? What if the spirits do want to accompany the pack, but the spirits are reluctant because the journey involves going somewhere that will be even more dangerous for the spirit(s) than for the Garou?

                                  I want to mention that my idea here to have the spirits send dream-visions to the pack is so that the spirits can have some way of communicating with each individual pack-member without being stymied by the fact that, except for the likely exception of the Theurge, the pack members don't have the Gift: Spirit Speech. Indeed, the aforementioned owl-spirit would probably speak directly to the Theurge with Spirit Speech in that case.

                                  None of this really answers your question of when to introduce fetishes to your pack. The best answer I can give (which admittedly is not all that helpful and I'm sorry about that) is "When you think the pack deserves it." One has to remember that packs as a whole aren't awarded fetishes. A fetish is, so far as I know, always a gift or reward to an individual Garou, not to a pack. The spirit in a fetish will "bond with/attune to" an individual Garou as if in a partnership. So to partly answer your question of when a Garou should get a fetish, ask yourself this: Has the Garou in question proved himself to be able to handle the responsibilities of owning a fetish? Will he treat the fetish as it deserves to be treated? Is there any possibility of him abusing or misusing the fetish? If the Garou isn't ready for the responsibility of owning and caring properly for a fetish, then it's not time to introduce a fetish to him yet. And each individual member of the pack will have to be evaluated by the same questions of fetish ownership.

                                  FWIW, I hope this helps.

                                              Nobody "needs" a fetish, but it is a part of the game. Characters in the sourcebooks always seem to have 2-3, and the various guides always have lists of even more fetishes. I'm just trying to crowdsource what's appropriate since I have to figure out not only what the NPCs have, but how the PCs could possibly get them outside making their own.

                                              There is also the Out of Character game balance issue. which the ST definitely needs to monitor. So I think it is appropriate to think about it in terms of X fetishes per Y ranks, or Z fetishes per Q Garou in a sept. There may be better types of statistical averages out there, but the "real" WoD has some kind of appropriate metric. I don't think it's nonsense to try to come up with what that best metric to be to guide STs. Then they can make better choices in their own games when determining how many fetishes and what kinds should be out there.

                                              There are a lot of players that fly by the seat of their pants and go with whatever they think at that time, and it always seems to work out well. I am envious of them, because I need to prepare a lot before I get to the point I am comfortable making spontaneous decisions.
