                                                        When doing a crossover, don't get hung up on rules as written. If you want this Abomination character to be a certain way, make it a certain way, you don't need to try to fit your concept into the rules. Especially not werewolf rules, since you are running a vampire game. You can pick and choose what ever elements from W:tA that you like and discard the rest to create your vision and a good day role playing experience for your players.

                                                            Thank you all!

                                                            Now, the side question inherent in this;
                                                            What sort of combination of cultural/tribal/clan origins do you think might best contribute to this Fallen Fuzzy Fellow becoming the villain I'm seeking?
                                                            My first thoughts are to go with a Transylvanian Shadow Lord Tzimisce; you'd have the ability to self-rationalize any path to power, a strong tendency to put your own needs above even those of your former Goddess, and a healthy dose of self-loathing for being what he was taught to hate. Sounds like a truly dark individual. Plus, serving the Wyrm with Vicissitude? Just *shudder*
                                                            It also brings in the question of whether such a critter might find welcome in service to Kupala (who could easily be seen as a Totem level spirit at least).

                                                            I'd probably take the following off the list immediately. Not because they DON'T work, but they just don't fit the vibe I'm looking for:
                                                            Black Furies (though the Greek angle is appealing)
                                                            Bone Gnawers
                                                            Children of Gaia
                                                            Red Talons

                                                            Culturally, really about anything that might have the right mix of tendencies to fight and survive rather than roll over (though just being Garou covers most of that!)
                                                            Clan wise, it's pretty open. Odds are we'd end up with a Caitiff anyway.
