    Rokea (Mechanical Deep Dive)

    05-03-2025, 12:19 PM
    Weresharks! Rokea claim to have been created by Kun (the Wyld) with a primary mission of surviving. They view it as their responsibility to protect the Sea, and generally care little for the problems of the Unsea (all of the lands of Gaia). In their Cosmology the Triat are predated by the Sea, and the various aquatic creatures of the oceans were created by Kun/Wyld (fish of all kinds), C'et/Weaver (shelled animals like clams) and Qyrl/Wyrm (animals with tentacles). They also believe that Qyrl created the sun and all of the stars.

    Clearly there's quite a big departure from Garou cosmology here, which makes sense. Rokea are primarily an aquatic species, mostly animal-born, extremely ancient, and their religion has likely stayed relatively isolated. A shared idea of the Triat and the world's origins which has formed between the Fera of the land won't have impacted their culture in the same way. It's generally accepted that the "Undersea Triat" are incarnations or faces of the more widely known Triat seen on the surface, and there's plenty of evidence for this, but given that life began underwater it seems more likely to me that the "Three Daughters", Kun, Qyrl and C'et, came first, even if their relevance in the Umbra has been eclipsed (though perhaps not as much as surface-dwellers would assume) over the eons.

    Naturally this is an overview in some places, with a deep dive more focused on the mechanics, so please see W20 Changing Breeds for full details.

    Overview
    • Rokea are immortal. They can die, but once they've matured they stop aging.
    • Rokea are vulnerable to silver, much like Garou, implying a connection to Luna. The Rokea see Luna as the 'Balm' of the Oversea, and don't consider her a distinct entity from the rest of the sky.
    • Same-Bito (Beast Courts) weresharks have -1 Rage compared to an equivalent 'standard' Rokea. Goblin Shark Same-Bito get Swim Sideways for free and can purchase Step Sideways as a Merit much more cheaply, but their Dexterity is reduced in some forms.
    • Gnosis recovery can typically only take place when a Rokea is submerged in salt water, for example in a Grotto.
    • Rokea cannot Step or Swim Sideways by default, and need a Merit or Gift or Rite to reach the Sea's Soul or Unsea's Soul, what most call the Umbra. They can, however, see spirits underwater without having to 'peek' through the Gauntlet.
    • Sendings are used to communicate; these signals travel through water and can be heard by all Rokea in range by default.
    • Rokea who make a habit of staying on land become known as "Betweeners" as they live on the land between the various parts of Sea. They are viewed as heretics, traitors and so on.
    • Rokea regenerate as Garou would, though a Squamus Rokea can even regenerate in their shark form if they remain submerged in salt water.
    • When a Rokea first assumes Homid form, they assume an ethnicity related to the local humans, for example if a Rokea washed up on the shores of Ireland and took Homid form for the first time they'd wind up looking like an Irishman.
    Rage and Frenzy

    A Rokea's Rage is determined by their Auspice.

    Being forced to stay still will make a Rokea roll to avoid Frenzy. They will keep moving at all times when they can, and even if they don't need to stay mobile they will typically pace around. Suppressing this requires a Willpower roll.

    Speaking of Frenzy, it requires only 2 successes on a Rage roll by default. 4+ successes brings about a state identical to Thrall of the Wyrm, which the weresharks associate with Kun, calling it Kunmind. Frenzying Rokea are always berserk and will attack and eat anything they can. They never enter Fox Frenzy.

    Given that only 2 successes are needed to Frenzy, Brightwaters and Dimwaters come off as extremely rage-filled compared to a typical Ahroun or Galliard. For a Garou to Rage roughly as often as a Rokea, both at Rank 1, they'd need to have a Rage of 9-10ish.

    Breeds

    Weresharks are split into Homid and Squamus. Most Rokea are Squamus, with a minority being Homid. This is because "Betweeners", Weresharks who live mostly on land, are ritually hunted down and seen as heretics, traitors and so on. The Same-Bito are an exception to this, though I consider it likely that there will be other cultures around the world where weresharks have thrived on land, even if they must defend themselves against routine Rokea attacks, perhaps by hiding or moving further inland seasonally.

    Homid Rokea have a starting Gnosis of 1 but understand human society. Squamus Rokea are often very detached from humans and have a starting Gnosis of 5. As mentioned above a Same-Bito (or similar, presumably) Rokea will have their starting Rage reduced by 1 compared to others of their Auspice.

    Squamus Rokea are also 'ugly' (not a fan of Werewolf doing this, listing traits which are objectively unattractive heads in a weird direction) and have odd-looking Homid forms. This means their Appearance is capped at 2, unless they take a special Merit. As a tradeoff they also get +1 Survival and +1 Primal-Urge, which makes them much, much better than a Homid Rokea given that +4 Gnosis and 2 free dots in Abilities is akin to 12 Freebie Points, which is more than enough to 'pay' for getting the Don't Be Ugly Merit.

    Auspices

    Rokea are split into Brightwaters, Dimwaters and Darkwaters. This reflects what kind of sky they were born under; a Brightwater is born under sunny skies or a full moon, Dimwaters when the sky is extremely cloudy or a typical night, and Darkwaters when the moon is New or Eclipsed. Brightwaters are said to be the most likely to produce "Betweeners", those Rokea who feel an urge to go onto land and stay there for longer periods.

    Brightwater Rokea are furious warriors, Dimwaters are leaders (despite being the most common) and Darkwaters are clever explorers. Their respective starting Rages are 5, 4 and 3, though a Same-Bito would make that 4, 3 or 2.

    Forms

    Rokea have 5 forms, Homid, Glabrus, Gladius, Chasmus and Squamus. To cut a long story short Glabrus and Gladius are their best fighting forms on land, but they're not nearly as powerful as the fighting forms of land-based Fera as their Dexterity bonuses only kick in underwater. In water, Gladius is better than it is on land, but is massively outcompeted by Chasmus.

    On land a Gladius Rokea will have +3 Strength, -1 Dexterity and +2 Stamina, which compares poorly to the Crinos form of a Garou or Ajaba. In water a Chasmus Rokea will have +4 Strength, +1 Dexterity and +3 Stamina, which is very good compared to basically anything else in water... perhaps except for a Homid Rokea in Squamus form!

    A Homid Rokea in Squamus form has +2 Strength, +3 Dexterity, +2 Stamina, Regenerates (even without salt water, so say in a river or lake), and inflicts Aggravated damage with its bite. I could easily see a Homid Rokea being attacked by a Squamus who takes on Chasmus, only for the Homid to try to outspeed them by taking Squamus.

    Oh and you inflict Reduced Delirium, or Delirium, in 3/5 forms. Only Homid and Squamus are mentally safe for bystanders.

    First Thoughts

    Rokea are great underwater and poor on land, which isn't much of a surprise. If you play a Rokea in a typical game you will, fittingly, be playing a Fish Out Of Water. You frenzy too easily, your land forms are clumsy, you're either an outcast or part of a highly insular and xenophobic culture, and your only land form which doesn't inflict any kind of Delirium is Homid. This is as close to playing an alien as you'll get in a typical WoD game. This is akin to playing the Hermetic who grew up on Mars and just got sent to their first Chantry, or a Mermaid arriving in a Seelie Court for the first time.

    I'd say that, weirdly, the Rokea are the "weakest" option I've reviewed so far from a character optimization viewpoint. You're great in your niche, which is good game design in terms of world building, but might leave you feeling a tad weak in terms of... actually playing the game. It isn't fun to be the one dude who is suffering from -1 Dexterity, and honestly if I were the ST in a game and a PC wanted to play a Rokea I'd wind up giving them a Fetish which improves the Dexterity of their land forms, or perhaps coming up with a Merit reflecting having gotten your 'land legs' which they could buy later on.

    The real kicker is the whole Frenzy thing. Having a starting Rage of 5, say, and Frenzying on 2 successes is really, really hard to work with. You're basically working with an effective Rage of 10 for Frenzy purposes, but without the cool upsides of having 10 points of Rage to spend and being able to take up to 5 bonus actions. You're also going to have to pay a tax in terms of a Merit, Rite or Fetish in order to keep up with a Garou/Ajaba/Ananasi ally in terms of Stepping Sideways.

    Oh and to top it all off you only start with 2 Gifts. Just 2! Feels pretty mean, all things considered. If they couldn't be bothered to make Breed Gifts, which is fine, they should have let a Rokea start with 2 General and 1 Auspice, or visa versa.

      Ha, deep dive. Deep dive.

      Let's see if Rokea Gifts make up for their slightly (understandably) dodgy basekit.

      Green Gifts are often must-haves, or have applications which are incredibly game-changing, story-impacting or overall powerful. Amber Gifts are solid, their utility is often determined by how likely you are to need the given effect or whether it's important to your character concept. Red Gifts are, sadly, too weak to typically be a good investment. A Red Gift might have an insane cost for its effect, or have too hard a roll to reliably pass, or be entirely redundant, and so on.

      Garou Gifts: Ranking, Thinking, Overthinking - Onyx Path Forums

      I'm going to break down Gifts by level and category, bit by bit. If it's Wyrmish, Fera-only, obscure or lost it's going to feature much much later, if at all. This is intended to focus on the Gifts you will actually be getting in play, hence starting at level 1 and working up. I'll be considering utility (both inside and


      General Rokea Gifts

      Breach (Hare's Leap), Teeth of the Skin (Gift of the Porcupine),
      Poisoned Flesh (Venom Blood), Restless Waters (Scent of Sight), Shagreen Skin (Troll Skin), Unsea's Blessing (Wyld Resurgence)
      Consume Taint (Resist Toxin), Shark Bones (Rat Head)
      Kun's Maw (Fenris' Bite)
      Great Summons (Song of the Great Beast)

      Breach is still great, since the main benefit of Hare's Leap is how far a Lupus form Garou can jump. Well, a shark can apparently leap an impressive distance out of the water, so this Gift maintains its solid utility. Getting Venom Blood a bit early is nice, but the Gift is still just alright. At least here we get clear confirmation that it harms anyone who bites you.

      Resist Toxin showing up very late, but with an upgrade, is interesting. Consume Taint basically lets you eat anything, including barrels of toxic waste, without any harm coming to you. That's handy for cleaning up pollution, but paying 9xp instead of 3xp is a big downgrade, even before you consider that you spend much of the playtime of a Chronicle (Rank 1-2) vulnerable to water pollution. It's sensible to not just give Rokea Resist Toxin at Rank 1 or 2, since their fear of polluted water is meant to be a major driving force, but getting it at Rank 3 still sucks! Similar situation with getting Rat Head at Rank 3. At Rank 3 I'm expecting Gifts that let me teleport, not Gifts that let me squeeze into a pipe. And "fit inside anything your head can fit inside" is basically already the case for a shark, since their head is as wide as the rest of them, not to get too pedantic.

      Fenris' Bite arriving a Rank early is nice, but the Gift still isn't lifechanging.

      Note that Rokea get Gift of the Porcupine far, far too early. Exceptionally early. It's insane how early they get it. Getting a Gift which is Green at Rank 4 at Rank 1 is amazing. See the end of this post for a tangent on that. I think a deep dive into what everyone having this Gift would do to a warrior/dueling culture which is mostly focused on unarmed strikes and where Frenzies are very common would probably go on for a few pages. Let's just say it's impactful. Combine with Troll Skin for a very nasty tank with a great 'thorns' effect.

      Fast (1): Spend 1 Gnosis, go without eating or drinking for a week. This is what utility Gifts should be like. In another chapter this Gift could randomly be a difficulty 7 Wits + Survival where for every success you get to make a Stamina roll for every meal you miss to see if it deals Lethal damage to you etc.

      I maintain that as a supernatural megapredator you can find dinner, but the ability to wander deep in the Umbra and just sustain yourself on Gnosis indefinitely is great. Unlike many of the "Find Food" Gifts, this is solid.

      Killing Bite (1): Spend 1 Rage, your next successful bite attack gets +2 Levels of damage. This is a great power to prep with if you want to take out a dangerous enemy in one chomp. I'm also going to assume that if you're playing a Rokea you will build around biting. Seems very sensible. Great buff, will be useful in almost any fight, and RAW works in all your forms, not just the chompy ones.

      Sense Danger (1): Focus for a full turn and roll Perception + Primal-Urge, difficulty 6 or 8. 6 for a tangible threat, 8 for something more complicated. On a success you pick up whether you're in danger. This Gift can be used to focus on an individual as well as a situation, so say you were sat in a board room, you might use this Gift, difficulty 8, on a success you get a "yes" - something about this meeting is a serious threat to you. You use the Gift again, this time on the chairman, huh he isn't a threat. Wait, his new PA is the threat. When did they get hired?

      This is a very good ability to have, comparable to the Gurahl/Ratkin Gift which gives you a 3 second warning when you're about to be in danger. I'd say this has similar utility to Sense Wyrm.

      Gulp* (2): Spend 1 Gnosis, swallow anything you can fit in your mouth (in Chasmus, practically anything), and it stays inside you as long as you like. It's "dedicated", so you could easily be carrying around a small car inside you, which you can later spit out. You can only fit a single "object" inside you, but an object with other objects inside it, like a backpack, or a car, should be fine.

      The utility of this Gift is limitless. The Gnosis cost is annoying, but being able to just go "I have an attache case with a bunch of rifles inside it hidden inside me, with silver bullets" is great. What's funny is that a typical Rokea won't get the greatest use out of this, but if you're actually playing one, chances are you'll wind up more used to the tools of humans and other Fera. This is Green on the assumption you do something, anything clever with it.

      Fathom Sight (3): Spend 1 Gnosis, roll Perception + Alertness (difficulty 7), and for every success you can project your senses 1 mile away in water. On land it's 100 feet per success, so significantly less, but still great. Check what's within 300 feet on a map, from your current location, you'll be impressed. This is similar to Scrying in quality. Scrying is difficulty 10 without a connection, difficulty 8 with a connection, and has fewer limitations. This is difficulty 7 and more local. It's also a Rank earlier, and available to all Rokea, instead of being a rare ability.

      In a typical game of WTA the main use of this would be to guard a location, spy on enemies, hear inside a meeting, check on a Grotto or ally, and similar. Hell, letting a foe go and then tracking them from a few miles away underwater would be great.

      Gift of the Ray (3): Spend 1 Gnosis to manifest a stinger. Using the stinger is a difficulty 7 Dexterity + Brawl. Anyone you sting needs to roll Stamina difficulty 9, and on a failure they suffer (Innovation) Lethal damage, plus they have a -2 wound penalty for the rest of the scene.

      It's really hard to compare this to just attacking normally. The difficulty 9 Stamina is really nasty, so I guess this is a good way to deal a BUNCH of guaranteed damage against an enemy who would otherwise have amazing soak. For instance, if you can sting someone, they're going to roll their Stamina, not their Stamina + Armour + various Gifts/Charms etc. So, if you had an Innovation of... 8 say, you'd just be instantly killing many foes. Humans, dead, Vampires, in Torpor, Garou, using their "Remain Active" Rage roll. It isn't explicitly stated that the Lethal damage from this Gift is unsoakable however, it's just implied by the fact that the target has already done a Stamina roll. If the damage is soakable this becomes a more sensible Gift.

      I genuinely don't know how to rate this one. If we had clear confirmation whether the damage was soakable or not it would be much clearer. A potential 10 levels (presumably, it isn't explicit) of Lethal Damage which you might not be able to soak sounds great, but we just don't get that crystal clear writeup here. If anyone in the W20 team could clarify I'd be very grateful!

      Form of the Sea (4): Spend 2 Gnosis to turn into water, in any form. You can swim, walk and so on, but if you're fully immersed in salt water the Gift ends.

      This is... pretty great. It isn't explicitly stated, but presumably you won't be bothered by most bullets, swords and so on. Personally I'd intuitively have this Gift ignoring damage sources like bites, claws, and so on, but keep you vulnerable to things like silver and fire... but honestly that's a guess. The Gift is unfortunately missing some important details.

      The stuff about drowning an enemy is great though. If the Gift works how I expect it to work it's a solid Green, but currently it can't be rated.

      Patient Hunter (4): Merge with solid matter for an ambush. Immediately picturing some Pentex suits sitting down for a meeting only for a Wereshark to drop out of the ceiling and slaughter them all.

      Roll Stamina + Stealth, difficulty 5, no cost. No Gnosis, no Rage, no Willpower. While you're merged with the wall, floor, ground, whatever, you're undetectable. And you can leap out, ending the Gift, at will. The Gift's flavour makes it clear that you can still "watch" your surroundings while hidden. This is very much in keeping with how some sharks hunt, waiting to ambush prey while buried under sand, so I love this one conceptually.

      Primal Assurance (5): Gain total certainty as to your place in the world and your role in life. This total confidence gives you +30 to your Initiative, and if you spend Willpower to gain an automatic success on a roll, and you get a success from the roll, you get your Willpower point refunded. So, for example, if you spend Willpower on a Dexterity + Brawl roll, and roll 4 successes, you'll get 5 successes and your Willpower point gets refunded.

      Honestly this would be a very cool Philodox Gift, in a world where cooperation between the various Fera were more common. Great Gift, amazing utility, and it's hard to overstate how important going first is in WTA.

      Whirlpool Maw (5): While in Gladius or Chasmus form, spend 1 Rage and roll Rage, difficulty 7. Keep in mind that 2+ successes means Frenzy. The Gift works for one turn per success. While the whirlpool is manifested, anything you could pick up based on your Strength rating will be pulled towards your mouth. Anything which reaches you isn't swallowed, exactly, but is consumed by the whirlpool and never seen again.

      Living creatures trying to resist being pulled in have to make a difficulty 8 Strength test and grab onto something to resist. Many will fail this, but even those that succeed will still be in a tricky position.

      Naturally you need to be underwater to use this.

      This is very, very, very powerful. You can obliterate practically any object or person with this Gift, so long as you're in the water. Even if you assume that the things "destroyed" by the Gift are instead banished to some eldritch depths of the Sea's Soul (I'm picturing a very, very distant part of the Umbra ruled by powerful Wyld Spirits etc.) that is, for all intents and purposes, total destruction. The Gift's limitations and requirements are annoying, and it's very aggravating that you're almost certainly going to Frenzy when you use it, but all in all it's a great Gift.

      So, a tangent about one of the Gifts mentioned above, and why I decided to do Rokea now instead of carrying on in alphabetical order.

      The Garou I'm currently playingjust learntTeeth of the Skin. He's a Child of Gaia Cliath, Metis, Ahroun, 1830s Wyld West game. On his voyage to the New World the Garou/Kinfolk vessel he was sailing on runs into a Dutch slave ship. Turns out they're in the process of throwing their "cargo" into the ocean as part of an insurance scam (this was based on a real historical event). My boy boards it, uses Jam Weapon to disable pistols and swords, kills two of the slavers and grabs the captain, leading to a swift surrender. My boy tells this guy to order that the people in the water be saved, while giving keys to those chained up on the deck. When the captain says this is impossible my boy throws him into the ocean and shouts "SAY THAT AGAIN", leading to the sailors coordinating a rescue of those in the water. Well wouldn't you know it, one of the poor guys being thrown into the ocean underwent his First Change due to the immense stress (also his first time being immersed in salt water) and proceeded to fly into a berserk Frenzy which led to him biting the captain in half and ramming the ship. My boy dived into the sea, slipped into Crinos, then fed what was left of the captain to this Rokea piece by piece to try and placate him, then risked his life wrestling with this giant wereshark while drowning, almost entirely deprived of his senses, and when the two of them were fished out of the water by the Children of Gaia they found that the ship was okay and most of the drowning victims had been rescued. This freshly changed Homid Rokea was a total stranger to his new nature, but naturally very grateful and curious given the help he'd received. For context he didn't even know what a wolf was, let alone a werewolf, but in a Mindspeak conversation it transpired he was a very bright and sensible youth. To cut the rest of a long story short, during his initial 'meeting spirits' journey, this very impressive Rokea NPC was granted a few Gifts, and he asked that one of them be taught to my boy also. The ST let me purchase a Level 1 Rokea Gift of my choice for 5xp (I went for Teeth of the Skin), which was an incredible reward, but felt pretty fitting given what had just happened. In turn my boy petitioned a Dove spirit to teach this Rokea Jam Weapon, and Dove did so saying that the (mostly) peaceful rescue of the people suffering aboard the Dutch ship was Chiminage in itself.

      Anyways the mechanical result is that my boy is a Rank 1 Garou who has Gift of the Porcupine, though since he learnt it as Teeth of the Skin the flavour is quite different. Instead of his fur turning into 'quills', I've described that it becomes like a sea urchin. The ST was quick to point out that this will look alien and Wyrmish to Garou strangers, which seems like a fair tradeoff, and should lead to some interesting encounters in the future!

                  Brightwater Gifts

                  Eyes of the Wound (Eyes of the Cat), Shatterbite (Spur Claws)
                  Shield of Rage, Terror of the Depths (Wolf at the Door)
                  Strength of the Crashing Waves (Might of Thor), Wriggling Teeth (Heart Claw)
                  (No pre-existing Level 4 Gifts)
                  Blood of Darkness (Halo of the Sun)

                  Getting Eyes of the Cat at Rank 1, instead of Rank 3, and being a shark who will often move through dark places, makes Eyes of the Wound a great Gift. Spur Claws is great as always, and doing it with a bite instead of clawing is neat. Good solid Gift, not quite Green, but still good. Wolf at the Door is slightly adjusted (making people too afraid to go near the sea), but the core mechanics are identical. Heart Claw is naturally adjusted to use teeth instead of claws, and works just as well.

                  Restraint (1): When entering a Frenzy, spend 1 Willpower and roll Willpower, difficulty 6. Every success lets you choose an individual, such as an ally or slewmate, and ignore them for the duration of your Frenzy.

                  This is huge, and I'd argue it's practically an essential Gift to grab if you're playing a Rokea. Being able to direct your Frenzy, even vaguely, is very important given that you have 5+ Rage and Frenzy with only 2 successes. And much like a Garou you follow the varying Rage difficulties with the phases of the moon!

                  Immediately grab this if you're playing a Rokea. Must-have, will save the plot from your dumb shark brain so many times.

                  Unseen Attack (1): +4 dice to the damage of surprise attacks, which is pretty great. Entirely passive, and not tied to bites, so would work with things like a ranged weapon or Fetish. Ideally you'd combine this with something that deals Aggravated damage of course, or perhaps a Fetish which has a rider effect which only works if the target takes damage. Combines very well with anything that doubles damage or reduces the difficulty of a damage roll.

                  Narke's Gift (2): Spend 1 Rage, roll Rage (difficulty 6), deal 1 level of Lethal per success to everyone outside your slew within 20 feet.

                  This Gift will inevitably make you Frenzy, so this feels like poor game design. Using this Gift is almost inevitably going to make you go berserk, and Restraint will only help a little there. So use with caution. Dealing 5 levels of Lethal to everyone who isn't in your slew, in a nice AOE, is pretty good. Just remember that the 20 feet radius is water-based, on land it will only hit targets who are "grounded" (unclear what they mean in this context, since grounding protects against electricity) or that you're touching. Let's assume that it works on non-slewmates within 20 feet if they're connected to you by something conductive, or if you're touching them.

                  The ability to electrify a swarm of spirits who are all digging into your flesh, for example, ain't bad at all. This AOE is neat, a tazer effect you can automatically hit anyone who is touching you with is neat, if this didn't make you Frenzy it would be Green.

                  Best Policy (4): When trying to persuade someone with blunt and total honesty, you can spend 1 Willpower to add your Harmony to a social dice roll. We are then told that "The Veil" will intervene to cloud memories of the Rokea's true nature if they are "revealed", presumably meaning that if you convince some cops to back off by going "I am a giant wereshark, I will effortlessly kill you if you don't leave", then the memory of the Veil Breach will be clouded once the scene ends.

                  This implication that "The Veil" is an independent entity, akin to The Mists of CTD, is unique as far as I'm aware. Still, decent ability, nice example of how to implement a social ability in a combat role. Adding up to 10 dice to a single roll for 1 Willpower ain't bad, but the restrictions of when you can use the ability are quite tough. All in all it's pretty great, but niche enough that it's solidly Amber.

                  No Walls (4): There aren't any walls in the sea, and this Gift basically lets you move around the Unsea as if it were the same.

                  Basically you can just charge through ANY obstacles. Strength + Athletics, difficulty 7, no cost, and with a single success you can charge through whatever is between you and your destination. This Gift would let you run through reinforced steel walls in homid form, for example.

                  This is great, and I wish Ahroun got a Level 5 Gift like this. Basically "the main thing that can stop an Ahroun Elder from winning a fight is not being able to reach the fight - this Gift is intended to solve that problem". I also wish Brightwaters got something like this a bit earlier, since "I can make a path for us to the enemy" is a great role to have in a party.

                  Amazing Gift, great flavour, solid Green.

                  Sea's Winds (5): Spend 2 Willpower to cloak yourself in an aura of light and wind, making it nearly impossible to approach you without permission. Anyone trying to reach you without your consent must get a total of 15 successes on an extended Strength + Athletics, difficulty 8, and every single roll comes with 3 soakable levels of Bashing damage. Those that serve Qyrl (the Wyrm, essentially) take Aggravated damage instead.

                  Once you reach the Rokea you have a single action before you're pushed back, restarting the process.

                  This is great, and also makes Halo of the Sun a bit pointless for the Rokea. Solid ability, no Gnosis cost, 2 Willpower is fine, and the extended test with passive damage is neat. No notes, solid Green, suitably epic.

                          Dimwater Gifts

                          Resist Pain, Sea's Voice (Beast Speech), Strange Blood (Sense the Unnatural)
                          Lord of the Seas (King of the Beasts)
                          (no pre-existing Level 3 Gifts)
                          Quick Currents (Strike the Air)

                          Sense the Unnatural at Rank 1, instead of 3, is an immediate improvement. This makes Rokea, as a species, extremely good at sensing magic and all kinds of supernatural weirdness, given that any Rokea can get this Gift for 3 or 5xp.

                          Lord of the Seas is still Amber but comes with a really annoying nerf; the Gift only works on sea creatures for some reason. Given that the Garou version isn't restricted to beasts of the land, this just feels a bit... annoying? But whatever. Strike the Air, meanwhile, is an excellent power for any Wereshark to master. In a society where Frenzy can happen so easily and so indiscriminately this ability is hugely impactful.

                          King Fish (1): The Simba Gift, Majesty, is just a rename of this one. +1 Leadership, +1 Intimidation, very good for 3xp. If you're interested in improving either one of these abilities then this Gift is very easy to justify purchasing since +1 Leadership and +1 Intimidation is worth a minimum of 4xp (going from 1 to 2) and as much as 20xp (going from 5 to 6, a thing you normally can't do anyways). So getting this for 3xp, or even 5xp, is a no-brainer. If you'd rather leave these Abilities at 0, getting this Gift is cheaper than buying the 1st rank of both Talents.

                          Crushing Depths (2): Make eye contact, spend 1 Willpower, roll Charisma + Intimidation against a difficulty of the target's Willpower. If you win they stay paralyzed, crushed by the weight of the ocean, struggling to breathe. This lasts until you break eye contact or the target Frenzies. Given that Rokea frenzy VERY easily when they're restrained, this Gift is practically useless against another Rokea... unless you want to force them to frenzy for some reason, which might be sensible as a work-around for stopping a clever plot.

                          The cost being Willpower is good, the roll is fine, the duration and effect are great, this is a very handy way to help your allies in a fight or shut down a dangerous enemy.

                          Spit Teeth (2): Dexterity + Athletics, difficulty 6, no cost, and you fire Aggravated damage teeth. In any form! Using Athletics is great since that's your Dodge, and it's a relatively inoffensive Talent in most situations so it's going to be slightly easier to find sources of difficulty reductions, bonus dice and so on, from Fetishes and Totems.

                          The base damage is your Valour. So, for example, it could be as high as a base damage of 10 Aggravated. That isn't that crazy for Garou, by any means, but a ranged attack which has a BASE damage of 10 Agg is still pretty great, and compares favourably with guns. The distance isn't amazing but having a ranged option patches a major problem a Wereshark might face given their size and how keen some enemies will be to stay out of mouth's reach.

                          Drown (3): Spend 1 Willpower, roll Willpower, difficulty of the target's Stamina + 4 to a maximum of 9. A single success makes the target grow gills for a full hour, losing access to their lungs and forcing them to survive on breathing water.

                          This has three main uses:

                          - allow an ally, guest, prisoner etc. to breathe underwater
                          - drown an enemy
                          - force someone to jump into a body of water to survive

                          All of these are great, the cost is doable, the maximum difficulty of 9 is handy against foes like a Crinos form Garou. Even a Werewolf won't shrug off an hour of suffocation, so this Gift is pretty nasty!

                          Sea's Wisdom* (3): Get the advice of Sea herself by rolling Intelligence + Rituals, difficulty 7. It's noted that she's never offered her opinion on Betweeners. This is a good plot device power, you could live without it, it could be incredible, depends on the ST. If you want to make this power impressive and useful, and are worried about the lack of a cost, it seems sensible to add a Gnosis cost to help justify the exceptional outcomes and limit spamming this Gift.

                          Living Sea (4): Animate water by spending Gnosis and making a roll. We can see that this can inflict damage, and it can also boost your swimming speed by 3x, but we don't get much details on how strong the animated water is, which is very important for grappling and lifting and all sorts of problem solving. As a result the Gift is probably Amber or Green but impossible to fully rate. 3x speed is nice, Harmony damage each turn is nice, but we don't have nearly enough details.

                          Leviathan (5): Spend 2 Willpower and 2 Rage, turn into a giant megapredator up to 80 feet long, get +5 to all of your physical stats after the usual Chasmus bonuses, and regain 1 Rage each turn passively. Pretty amazing, obviously, and no downside apart from being so absolutely giant that you are a huge target for 99% of enemies, an enormous Veil Breach, and can't fit inside many areas at all.

                          But. If you need to fight in a huge open area, or fight a giant spirit, or take on a whole Pentex ship... this is the Gift. Don't use it when you shouldn't, basically, and it's a solid Green. Just annoying that Brightwaters don't get this!

                          Wound Undersea* (5): Take a level of Aggravated and roll to awaken lava on the seafloor, causing a volcanic eruption in 3+ turns time which leads to mass disaster. Hugely impactful, yes, but slow to start, which is understandable. This one is so much in the hands of the Storyteller that it's hard to judge, but ultimately it's a giant nuke you can use against an oil rig or ship or a powerful underwater threat, or a coastal community, and so on.

                          Being limited to underwater activation, and having to damage yourself, isn't great.

                          And the real problem is how little damage it deals. 3 Aggravated per turn to those in contact with the lava. The lava! That's... that's really bad! It's both too huge a disaster of a nuke and too weak. So don't try using this in a fight, which is where a nuke can be most handy, annoyingly. All in all Red, but it isn't entirely useless, just a waste most of the time in my book.

                                  Darkwater Gifts

                                  Qyrl's Blood (Shroud), Sudden Surge (Lightning Reflexes)
                                  Sight From Beyond
                                  Scent of Blood (Direct the Storm)
                                  (no pre-existing Level 4 Gifts)
                                  C'et's Shell (Shield of Gaia), Madness

                                  Shroud is excellent as always. Direct the Storm is massively impactful for a Garou, and even moreso for a Rokea. This Gift is absolutely essential for the Weresharks. Shield of Gaia can have 1000s of potential effects.

                                  Blood of the Deeps* (1): Total passive immunity to deep ocean environments. This means a Rokea with this Gift can survive a pressure of 1000 atmospheres and remain comfortable in freezing temperatures (though deep ocean water won't be colder than 0 degrees celcius). This would be hard to justify purchasing for more than 3xp, and typically will be a bit of a flavour or concept tax. If you really need to go somewhere like this chances are you'll find a Fetish or Spirit or Talen that can help.

                                  Chill (1): Spend 1 Rage, reduce the air/water temperature significantly for the scene. This adds a +1 difficulty penalty to all non-physical actions taken by any creature outside your Slew. This is a very reasonable cost, and for example, adding +1 to the difficulty of a bunch of enemy Gifts which get activated in a scene might be well worth it.

                                  The issue is that most of the rolls enemies and adversaries are going to be making in a scene of conflict will be physical ones. Making Social, Mental, Willpower, Gnosis, Arete etc. rolls harder is neat, but +1 difficulty is one of those things which can do a lot or very little depending on dice pools, on luck, and on how many rolls are made. A boardroom full of lawyers are probably going to manage to get through their meeting, even with a chilly room and +1 difficulty to their rolls. But this is Rank 1 and only costs 3xp, so it's good enough for Amber.

                                  Enter Sea's Soul (2): Gain the passive ability to Swim Sideways. So, for 6 or 10xp we have something akin to the 4 point Swim Sideways Merit. The xp value of Freebie Points is really hard to measure, and naturally not every Rokea concept is going to be aided by the idea that they are preternaturally gifted in reaching the Umbra.

                                  An argument could be made, all things considered, to either wait for this Gift or to buy Step Sideways outright for 7 freebies. Still, this is very handy, but also very annoying. A Rank 2 Gift which lets you Step Sideways but only underwater is... quite limited. If there were a bit more parity, if Stepping Sideways didn't automatically mean being able to Swim Sideways (e.g. imagine if Garou couldn't Step Sideways underwater!) this might be less weird.

                                  Ultimately this Gift brings up the annoying issue of what you do about Umbra access in a mixed-Fera game. Ananasi, Corax, Ajaba, Garou, they can all access the Umbra basically at will. That inherently splits the party if there's a Rokea or Gurahl or Bastet along for the ride. Fixing that problem with a Gift at Rank 2-4 is one angle on it, but it's arguably a very silly one. So whether this Gift has any use at all, or whether it's a waste of XP compared to a Ritual or Fetish solution, depends entirely on your game.

                                  Piercing Shriek (2): Spend 1 Rage, roll Wits + Primal-Urge, all other creatures within 30 feet (or 15 outside of water) have to roll a difficulty 8 Willpower. If your successes beat theirs, they collapse, stunned, for turns equal to the difference.

                                  So, for example, by Garou boy gets 2 successes, the Rokea gets 5 successes, he's stunned for 3 turns. Except no he isn't, because he's actually stunned for 5 turns because his hearing is superhuman. Except he isn't stunned for 5 turns, because he's spending a point of Rage to ignore being stunned. Hmm. Even if the ST of a given Chronicle doesn't accept that this Gift's pain-based stunning can be overcome by spending Rage to ignore Stunning, or by things like Resist Pain, it's a bit of an odd one. It's potentially powerful, but indiscriminate. The ability to stun a huge crowd of powerful enemies for many, many turns is great. There are balance issues around the fact that resistance can be impossible, and the base difficulty of 8 is very tricky to begin with.

                                  This is Green, but strays into "so good it might wind up banned or nerfed by the Storyteller" territory.

                                  Swim Through the School (3): Spend 1 Gnosis to get two key benefits. Crowds part to let you through, allowing you to travel at top speed in places filled with humans or animals. And for that scene your Curse is calculated as if your Rage was half your true value, rounding down.

                                  So, a Rokea with this Gift with a Rage of 5 would suffer the Curse as if their Rage was 2, which is a massive change. At a party the humans with a Willpower of 4 or lower might be most of the people there. 1 or lower, on the other hand, might be 0% of the party, or perhaps just a kid eager to avoid the scary man with the big teeth. Essentially this Gift lets you... function! Which is pretty neat. Paying 1 Gnosis for that benefit isn't great, but it's an improvement all the same. That said at Rank 3 I expect better.

                                  Sea's Breath (4): "Swim" through air, and breathe it, like it were water. So, for instance, this Gift lets a Squamus or Chasmus form Rokea glide through the air as if they were in the sea. This doesn't give them altitude above 15 feet, but it's still pretty neat! This combines very well with Breach (Hare's Leap), which will allow you to jump much higher and then return to the lower air where you can swim.

                                  Absolutely excellent, and really I wish it was on the General Rokea list.

                                  Shock Wave (4): Spend 1 Willpower, roll Rage. Difficulty 6 in water, difficulty 7 on land, and annoyingly you're probably going to Frenzy when you use this.

                                  If you succeed you make a concussion wave which spreads through the water or air, size determined by your Stamina. So this will be much stronger in stronger forms.

                                  Those inside the AOE take Lethal damage equal to your successes, so very deadly to weak spirits and to humans who typically won't be able to soak the damage. Not inspiring words for a Level 4 Gift. There is also a very difficult roll to not be knocked prone.

                                  This is a pretty neat shockwave, it's good, but making yourself Frenzy to wound (not kill, unless you have insane Rage and can manage 7+ successes) a whole bunch of humans feels... pretty weak. You're an apex supernatural predator, you're threatened by Garou and the mightiest Fomori, not crowds of regular people. All in all this is pretty... bad. Even if it didn't make you Frenzy (only needs 2+ successes since you're a Rokea!), which is the last straw.

                                              Balefire Sharks

                                              Rokea who made a deal with the devil, so to speak, Balefire Sharks took their task to 'survive' too literally and gave themselves over to the Wyrm in order to survive a nuclear assault. Tainted and dependent on radiation, Balefire Sharks are forced to spread pollution to survive.

                                              Mechanically speaking a Balefire Shark is playable, assuming your Storyteller is open to such a thing, but given that they are traumatized, exceptionally rare, blunt and violent, exclusively shark-born, and may not even have a way of reproducing, and that doesn't even touch on them mostly being bound to the will of a powerful Wyrm spirit, it doesn't seem very sensible to play as one. Even in a Wyrmish game, or a game where the PCs start off Wyrmish and wind up redeemed, Balefire Sharks are too destructive and obvious even for the tastes of other Wyrm-servants. W20 Book of the Wyrm tells us that on multiple occasions the location of a Balefire Shark's resting place has been leaked to servants of Gaia to help 'clean up' a mess, by servants of the Wyrm, since Pentex and the BSDs don't always want such blind aggression on their shores.

                                              The only important changes from regular Rokea are:
                                              • You can learn Black Spiral Dancer Gifts, in addition to your normal ones.
                                              • Totem options are limited to Wyrmish spirits.

                                              • Only Wyrmish spirits will accept your summons and deal with you.
                                              • Gnosis recovery is dependent on polluted water, for example a tainted Grotto might be built around a sunk nuclear submarine or similar.
