                                            We found something else weird in the Ananasi Breedbook when re-reading the forms descriptions. There on page 65 in the description of the Pithus (giant spider) form there is this part:
                                            But of of the biggest advantages to this form comes from the fact that spiders are... well, hydraulic in nature. Their muscles don't work in the same way, and neither does their circulatory system. Spiders can shift the blood in their bodies, and that allows them to increase the amount of strength they have substantially. Damhàn can do the same thing, but it takes more out of us. Yes, we can increase our strength very efficiently, but we will have to replenish our blood if we do it too often. I personally don't have a problem with that; I like the taste of blood.
                                            Whats this supposed to be about?
                                            It kinda makes it sound like Ananasi could use blood to increase strength in Pithus form (like a Vampire would) but its not mentioned in the rules.
                                            Is it just a fluffy explanation for why the Pithus form has a higher strength bonus than the other forms? Then why does it talk about having to replenish blood?
                                            Is it a hint at the gift that lets you pump blood points into strength? Then why is it described as universal and only for the Pithus form?
