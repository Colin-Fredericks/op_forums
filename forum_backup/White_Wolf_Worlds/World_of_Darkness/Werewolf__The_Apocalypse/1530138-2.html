                                    First draft for the write-up (up to the patron spirit). Feel free to critique away...

                                    Crimson Tusks

                                    Only the strongest survive and thrive. I am here to forge you into an unbreakable instrument of war for Gaia’s glorious last stand.

                                    Once a powerful and proud tribe, the Crimson Tusks have fallen far since the rise of the Cult of Fenris. In the past, they viewed themselves as the Garou Nation’s vanguard, fearless warriors conquering new territory and forging the way for the other tribes, keeping order and control among their brethren, while teaching them how to be strong and unyielding.

                                    Today, the tribe is all but extinct, not because of a danger from without, but because they were betrayed by their disillusioned elders. Led astray by the dread spirit Fenris, most tribe members abandoned their tribe’s patron spirit and joined the Apocalypse Wolf’s nihilistic death cult.

                                    Crimson Tusks in play might expect to prevail through extremely harsh circumstances, conquer a caern that fell to Black Spiral Dancers, enforce the rulings of their sept elders, or forge the next generation of Garou into deadly warrior.

                                    Who are the Crimson Tusks?

                                    If one were to ask the remaining Crimson Tusks about their tribe, they would tell grandiose stories how they were the ones keeping the Garou Nation together. Without their oversight, their strict enforcement of the Litany, their teachings of strength and resilience, their eradication of weakness and impurities, the other tribes lost their way and drifted apart. The other tribes would tell another story, however… the Crimson Tusks have always been a tribe of extremes, they would say, prone to belligerence and pigheadedness, excessive violence, blind obedience, and authoritarianism. The truth likely lies somewhere inbetween those views.

                                    What doesn’t kill you, will make you stronger. Gaia can only be saved and avenged by the strongest of the strong. For the Crimson Tusks, strength and survival are everything. The strength to prevail can only grow from adversity, however, and the Crimson Tusks revel in seeking it out. The Apocalypse is happening right now, and the Wyrm’s servants know no mercy. Thus, the Crimson Tusks feel their teachings have to be harsh and indifferent, like nature itself, forcing those around them to adapt and to grow more resilient… or die trying. For a Crimson Tusk, there is nothing worse than weakness — in themselves or others — and they do everything to eliminate any perceived impurities.

                                    This obsession with strength made them into one of the fiercest tribes of the Garou Nation. Whenever the opportunity arose to prove their mettle, the Crimson Tusks volunteered to be the vanguard, seeking glory by charging ahead into the fray without fear or doubt. Often, Crimson Tusks were the driving force behind conquering new territory or facing their enemies head-on, even if the odds seemed against their favor.

                                    Their combative natures and hierarchical thinking in terms of strength and weakness often strained their relationship with the other tribes, especially the Black Furies and Children of Gaia. At the same time, some tribes, particularly the Silver Fangs and the Shadow Lords, knew how to utilize these characteristics to their advantage: in many septs, Crimson Tusks were tasked with keeping law and order, enforcing the Litany and keeping the peace, but — in reality — exerted control over the sept as the leaders’ enforcers. After all, it’s harder to show dissension during a moot, if Crimson Tusk thugs keep breathing down your neck.

                                    Despite being irritated by the Crimson Tusks’ dubious Social Darwinist outlook and exhausting combativeness, many Garou had to begrudgingly acknowledge the results. That is, these characteristics tended to forge Crimson Tusks into peerless, tough-as-nails warriors. Thus, Crimson Tusks often were allowed a more benevolent role in septs as well, teaching young Garou how to accept conflict and adversity as parts of their lives, how to increase their prowess enough to survive, how to defend themselves against a multitude of enemies, and how to become unbothered by the curveballs that life tends to throw at you, instead viewing them as opportunities to grow.

                                        Patron Spirit

                                        Boar is the patron spirit of the Crimson Tusks, a mighty spirit associated with power, toughness, fierceness, and stubbornness — all desirable virtues for a tribe of relentless warriors. However, Boar is also known for his avarice and recklessness. Hunters fear Boar’s children for good reason: they are too angry to pass up a challenge, to fierce to ever give ground, and to ornery to die before doing massive damage. Boar and the Crimson Tusks are associated with Glory.

                                        Favor:Boar demands his children become tough warriors withstanding any hardship and making their enemies tremble in fear. Crimson Tusks may add a die to any dice pool involving Brawl, Intimidation, or Survival, as long as they stand their ground.

                                        Ban: Boar is proud and stubborn, punishing those harshly who don’t live up to his expectations. If a Crimson Tusk yields to an adversary, even if it makes sense strategically, or shows weakness and vulnerability before others, they may recover only a single Willpower at the start of the next session, instead of their Composure or Resolve rating.


                                        [For my house rules: Tribe Favor = Brawl, Intimidation, Survival]

                                            Crimson Tusk Archetypes

                                            Adrenaline Junkie
                                            You noticed something off with you from early on; you only felt truly alive with your heart racing and adrenaline shooting through your body. You started with freerunning and city parkour, but soon craved more: Bungee jumping, motocross, canyoning, cave diving, hang gliding, ice climbing… there is hardly an extreme sport you haven’t tried and loved. Yet, this itch, this need for the rush always returns even stronger. Now that your supernatural regeneration has removed off any sense of danger from most activities, you cannot help but wonder… what does it take to make you feel alive again? How far do you have to push in the fight against the Wyrm to feel at peace? Your pack has already confronted you about how you’re becoming more reckless by the day.

                                            Tyrannical Deputy Sheriff
                                            There are those who cannot wait to escape their small town in order to seek refuge in the city. You never understood them. You love the cozy feeling of familiarity, safety, acknowledgement, and predictability there. A feeling of home, of territory — and of control. You protect your territory and its people, keep law and order, but you’re not taking kindly to any troublemakers or outsiders. After all, it’s your town and your rules! Sure, some may think you’re a busybody who sticks their nose into their private business. What they don’t understand is the simple fact that everything in this town is your business. You are its protector, its guardian. Their reluctance doesn’t matter, however… they will fall in line, eventually, and they will learn to appreciate you.

                                            Drill Instructor
                                            You’re not a sadist, despite what some may claim. You don’t derive pleasure form being merciless on your students whatsoever. After all, they aren’t responsible for their weaknesses. No, it’s their parents, their previous teachers, their society who are to blame. You will not fail them as well, however! No matter the reasons, you will forge your students’ impurities out of them until they turn into a steel worthy of Gaia’s fight. At the beginning of the training, your students usually don’t appreciate their lessons. When those who fought their first battles return to you, however — a haunted look in their eyes, marked by the horrors of war — you spot gratitude in their eyes. They know what you did for them. And, in return, you reward them by showing your pride about their accomplishments.

                                            MMA Champion
                                            You have always been a fighter, a survivor. Maybe it was growing up on the streets that made you this tough? Whatever the reasons, you’re good at beating people into pulp, breaking their bones and spirits. The best at it, in fact. After taking up boxing and Brazilian jiu-jitsu, others soon took notice. Your trainers were all over you, calling you an outstanding once-in-a-lifetime talent, dragging you from tournament to tournament, sponsor to sponsor. It all happened in a flurry, really. You didn’t care. You never wanted to be a martial arts champion, you never wanted to be rich and famous. You just wanted to keep on fighting. It’s the only thing you know, the only thing that makes you happy. After your First Change, you had to retire from professional sports to protect the Veil, but now you have found your true calling. Now, the fight will never end again. Hell yeah!
