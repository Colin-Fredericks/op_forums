                      (This is true, and may serv to inpsir a WW game)

                      This blurb of personal experience takes place In a city on the Texas Mexican border. This bordertown is the biggest inland port to the United States from Mexico.

                      When I was a kid late 80's early 90's you could go to a well known flower and gifts shop in the hood that had a backroom full of cages ( not nice pet store cages).

                      I remember to the locals it was no big deal what was happening in the back of the flower shop. They were either ignorant , undereducated, or were happy to be making money with what was happening in the back of the shop.

                      I remember going into the back of the shop and seeing steel cages stacked on cages , animal filth everywhere and mice and rats darting under the cages and crawling all over everything.

                      Inside the cages were TONS of exotic animals , A bald eagle , a spider monkey, a full grown caiman etc.. These animals were for sale....

                      I remember in this same city for several years you would go out and about on the town , and wherever missing cat and missing dog flyers were , you would also see one too many flyers for missing monkeys with rewards for their return.

                      This city also had way too many instances of Mexican immigrants going missing (no body) in the Rio Grande , right under the international bridge.

                      When I got to college I found out why- The illegal animal trade is HUGE , but people usually just think of drugs and people trafficking. But what happens when a criminal transporting illegal animals approaches customs and feels the heating getting to strong? They release their cargo into the green space and the Rio Grande.

                      It became such a problem that the local college set up a "zoo" of sorts along the border where the college students could go visit. It had ALL sortsa animals pulled from the Rio Grande that didn't belong there. Gators, Piranhas, Eagles, jungle cats, crocodiles , monkeys , lemurs, sting rays , exotic birds from the jungle etc...

                      Many poor people trying to quickly cross that river were gobbled up because of the illegal pet trade.

                      What drives this trade...well...its on the border of mexico , and drug dealers are very showy. It was not uncommon to have a residential area where someone owned a tiger cub or two in their backyard, a tank with a juvenile shark in it, hell my tattoo artist had a baby komodo dragon , This usually took place in that one house in the hood that happened to be a mansion surrounded by decrepid shacks.
