            Legends about the end of the Impergium clearly state different tribes and their opinions. Specifically Children of Gaia, Red Talons, Shadowlords, Silver Fangs and Stargazer. So, in Pangea, as per page 78 of Umbra revised, they would not only forget their tribal differences, but also be not identifiable as members of any given tribe.
            So, ending the Impergium there, in the umbra, would not really have lead to the tale, that a Silver Fang lost a contest of gamecraft to a Stargazer - bit merely that one garou from each side participated in a contest and the faction that wanted to end the Impergium won.
            In any case there was hasn't been a mention of the end of the Impergium specifically happening in Pangea, and the whole legend about the Red Talons forming in that instant would be pointless in Pangea.

            None of your quotes stated that Impergium's end and the creation of the Litany fell to the same instant in time. The Red Talon quote merely states, the Silver Fangs announced both at a grand moot, suggesting that the Litany came into existence after the Impergium's end. However, the same quote can also be understood as the Silver Fangs reinforcing that the Litany was binding to all tribes and calling upon "Submit to those of higher station", making their disputed decision to end the Impergium a command.

            The information you read from the quotes isn't actually written there, and there are logical difficulties if both aspects of the garou history happened at the same time. Mentioning both in the same paragraph is not uncommon if one details tribal history, since they both occurred in the Dawn Times. But that could mean that there were a thousand years between both, or mere decades.
