          I don't think there are any guidelines in the books so I imagine it is up to the discretion of the ST. How long is too long for you (i.e. you don't want to run a game solely in the Umbra for that long)? Garou are creatures of both the Realm and the Umbra, so they are supposed to spend time in both. From what I see of most players, PCs spend over 90% of their time in the Realm and only enter the Umbra for tactical reasons. If that is good enough to keep them centered, then the opposite should also be true (a Garou who spends 90% of their time in the Umbra and enter the Realm rarely).

          I think the few Garou who become lost in the Umbra are the ones who spend all their time on vision quests exploring the Near Realms, the Deep Umbra, etc. If you are just farting about in the Penumbra, you are fine. Personally, I am thinking of months spent in the Umbra, perhaps even years, before a Garou becomes lost.

              I have generally used a house rule of requiring Garou to spend one day in the Realm for every (Willpower) days in the Umbra and one day in the Umbra for every (Willpower) days in the Realm. If they violated that rule, they reduced their die pool to go to the other Realm by one per (Willpower) additional days spent on one side over the other. If their die pool reaches '0', they are temporarily Lost and become permanently Lost after (Willpower) months of being temporary Lost. Garou are supposed to be denizens of both the Realm and the Umbra, and they should not just use the Umbra as a tactical resource.

                      It's called Disconnection, p319 W20. Each lunar cycle in the Umbra adds one to the difficulty to return to the physical. Eventually it will become nearly impossible to escape. After a year and a day in the Umbra, shapeshifters become spirits.

                      Homelands slow this dissolution for tribe members, adding 1 to the difficulty per year, rather than per month. Time is weird in the Deep Umbra, so Disconnection doesn't progress while in the Deep, but neither does any accumulated time reduce. This is all covered in W20 Umbra, p83 for Homelands and pp103-104 for Deep Umbra.

                              As different books of different lines affirm different rules
                              so a thumb rule, I use this
                              [Willpower] days + [Arete/Gnosis] weeks
                              after that a Willpower (difficulty X) roll each month, if the roll is succesfull the disconnection is postponed to the next month.
                              X is 5 in Near umbra, 6 in Middle Umbra and Dark Umbra (Shadowlands only), 7 in High Umbra or near-Earth umbral realm (Scar, Summer Country, Pangaea, the Battleground, the Tempest), 8 in Deep Umbra or far/alien umbral realm (Malfeas, Aetherial Realm, Labyrinth).
                              Some realms slow or alt this process (homelands, stable mage realms, etc.).
