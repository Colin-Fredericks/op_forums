    Some more tribal head-canons and thoughts… (Part 4)

    Silver Fangs
    •The Silver Fangs are diametrically opposite with the Bone Gnawers
    •The Silver Fangs represent the archetype of the sacred-king/queen; physical vessels for a quasi-divine potency of spirit which embodies the people, their relationship with the spiritual, and a physical vessel for the world
    •Powerful Silver Fang kings/queen with the proper rituals will reflect their mental and physical state in the land itself; where the Gauntlet is high this may be limited to the Sept but legend says that the Silver Fangs of old would cause storms with their anger or abundant fertility with their good humor
    •The ur-place of any Silver Fang is as King/Queen but naturally not all can fill such roles; as such other Silver Fangs will either settle into auxiliary roles (Champions, Rite-Keepers, Inquisitors, etc.) but in particular the classic Garou ideal of a non-King/Queen Silver Fang is as the heroic alpha of a Garou pack leading them on great adventures and exploits
    •The Silver Fangs have become broken as the ages have gone on and the reasons/explanations for this vary: some say that the bloodlines have become polluted and must be purified, others say that liberal/lac enforcement of the high customs (including the Killing of the King…) have allowed inner rot, others say that the Silver Fangs are suffering power-madness from overstepping the original taboos they had won from Sun and Moon in exchange for authority, others say the Silver Fangs embody the corruption of the world and that the pollution of the land itself has riddled their minds, others say that the curse of Father Wolf has struck the tribe or they have disappointed Falcon, others say it is because of a lack of new young Silver Fangs being sired or worthy of Falcon; regardless of the cause what everyone can agree on is that the average Silver Fang is slowly dissolving into a matrix of neuroses and aberrations which undermine their competency
    •Legends vary as to how the Silver Fangs gained their authority; the tribe itself guards closely held secrets about communion with White Wolf (Death) itself, while variations of the stories of primordial Silver Fangs winning the blessing of Sun and thus the power of sacred kingship also remain (some Black Furies suspect that it’s the solar power of Sun itself which has caused the madness by its conjunction with the lunar power of Moon that was meant to be the Garou birth rite)
    •Silver Fangs are a global tribe and the guardians of the most ancient, sacred, and powerful artifacts and places; making their gradual degeneration all the more worrying

    Stargazers
    •The Stargazers are not the only tribe of Asia, but that’s a whole larger conversation; in particular the Stargazers are renowned for keeping mountain top septs close to the heavens, which is why they’re particularly associated with the powerful Caern of the High Himalayas
    •The Stargazers are a rather esoteric and unusual tribe due to the Auspices of Chimera; the star-beast and the dream-creature, because of Chimera the Stargazers are drawn to contemplating the mysteries of heaven, mind, and spirit
    •Stargazers more than any other tribe (with honorable mention given to the Silent Striders and Older Brother) are renowned for their private hermitages and trials to discover spiritual and philosophical secrets and to explore powerful Mysteries (including things which would not be out of place for a Mage chronicle)
    •The Stargazers are a relatively small tribe who often disappear into the Umbra or into remote places of contemplation; at their best they are the philosophical pioneers of what is possible for the Nation but at their worst they are entirely absent due to their incessant navel-gazing

    Older Brother (Ghost Council/Whispering Shades)
    •The spirit patron of the tribe is the Horned Serpent
    •Older Brother is associated with water and cthonic mysteries amongst the tribes of the Americas; in particular OB has always been associated with taboos, secrets, esoteric societies, mysteries, subtlety, and cunning; being renowned in the modern nights for creating some of the most gifted Theurges in the world
    •Older Brother has adopted the policies of assimilation, adaption, and horizontal adoptionism as its means for surviving the Post-Apocalypse
    •Older Brother, more than any other tribe, engages in the practice of poaching secrets and practices from non-Garou sorcerers
    •Older Brother is also renowned for their capacity to engage in work with dark spirits, even the Old Wyrm Spirits, with more skill and care than any other tribe; at their best this makes them unparalleled as theurges and the best at understanding the Wyrm but at their worst they know better than any tribe the dangers of the Wyrm and its pollution

    Younger Brother (Gale Stalkers)
    •The spirit patron of the tribe is the Great Fisher (although I must admit I do have a soft-spot for the notion of giving YB Hairy-Man/Sasquatch as their tribal patron; something about the liminal space between human life and the non-human natural world seems fitting to me)
    •Younger Brother is associated with air and vital motion amongst the native tribes of the Americas (with water and cthonic mysteries being Older Brother and earth and rock solidity being Middle Brother)
    •Younger Brother has adopted a policy of Survival, Resistance, and Militancy to the Post-Apocalypse both on martial, material, and social terms
    •Younger Brother is more closely associated with the pre-Apocalyptic rites, customs, and mysteries then perhaps any other tribe; embodying the persistence of those consequences into the modern nights and carrying out the ancient duties and associations in order to keep their sacred wheels turning

        Something that I think would be an interesting addition to werewolf and could be useful within its milleau is a greater attention paid to cults.

        For one, if we really get down to it Pentex, one of the central antagonists of the setting, is essentially a vast and extremely resourceful cult. In my opinion PENTEX also really pops in the places where they combine the banal horror of just what a profit-seeking corporation does on its own terms with the occasional conscious ventures into actual witchcraft and black magic to spread taint (I’m thinking things like the witch episodes of the X-Files, the law firm from ‘Angel’, the group from ‘Rosemary’s Baby’, etc.). The idea that not only is, for instance, this schlock GI Joe knockoff action figure a tool of social education and made from shitty plastic and paint, but that it also has a dark voodoo style curse placed on it which will effect its user. I think that’s a rich field of material to work with.

        I like to add more cult type groups to the setting in general. For instance most Halloween style witch covens or horror movie satanic cults are great fits for Wyrm style cults you can have in your game. I also think that there’s interesting things you can do for other spirit groups. For the Wyld I’d imagine taking inspiration from irl Situationist & Discordian Groups (which I think old White Wolf writers also did on occasion, iirc there are even a couple books that have the sign off of ‘Hail Eris!’ in them) - I’d imagine a combination of pure LSD/Hippie style shock-freak sufis to more high flown mind-fuck style groups with Hail Bob or Ong’s Hat ARG vibes; creating memetic viruses to infect and alter reality. Furthermore while I think most actual religions can be used as inspiration for Weaver cults given the Weaver, I always get drawn towards two other molds: one is the faux psychiatric mold which has Weaver cults which are basically Scientology with the serial numbers filed off or Weaver Cults that are basically cells of a Masonic Bavarian Illuminati style org. I also think that one could take inspiration from something like Alan Moore’s work or WtF to have more miscellaneous cults.

        Ultimately however the issue I run up onto is how to scale this for Werewolf in particular. For instance having Alan Moore/Hellblazer style groups running around the WoD is fitting for the setting I think, but how do you tailor it for different games. I can see the average cult being able to provide a mystery and intrigue plot line for a werewolf game but when it comes to action it feels like in most cases it’s a walk in the park for the Garou. I suppose what I can suggest for that then is that the Garou mostly deal with successful cults that have made contact with powerful spirits and fomori/monsters who can challenge the Garou physically or one uses cults as one tool in the box for their game - that in the same way you have straight-forward punch-em-ups with monstrous fomori (but without much need for investigation or intrigue) you can have investigation and intrigue challenges based around cults that, once they’ve unwound the mystery, you let your players have a power trip of dishing out whatever horror movie scenario they cook up to deal with these doomed cultists.

        Anyway these are just a few thoughts I had on the topic, I’d be curious to hear any thoughts other people had.

                    Headcanon and Homebrews
                    So as it’s in the same Imperium Victorian worldline/setting in 1869 as Mage although our Werewolf games are in the Savage West.

                    The Garou can gain small mystic knacks that can individualise their gifts.

                    The Storm Eater is free but hasn’t consuming a weaver Incarna yet, however it’s consumption of smaller weaver spirits is breaking the pattern web.

                    A number of factors exist at an earlier stage for example the Umbral Pilots are Victorian pioneers.

                    Wandering mixed packs are relatively common although Septs are pretty suspicious to each other, inter tribal conflict is frequent and viscous between the Pure Ones and Wyrm Comers Midwest to the west coast.

                    The Older and Younger Brothers are aligned with the Nunnehi, they often share totems and work together to defend Caern/Glades.

                    The Hungry Dead have attempted to invade San Francisco whilst the State is relatively new and unclaimed, the younger Wan Rai seek to even the loss of territory in the orient to imperial invaders or to escape the Taipan Civil War.

                    The Garou have secured a number of power sites (fonts, Dragon Nests and a few full caerns, the Golden Bay has many) which has brought them into conflict with the Hungry Dead.

                    In reaction to the Storm Eater the Rokea have started to actively guard the waters of the West Coast sinking ships carrying supernatural passengers, including some technocratic vessels.

                        PENTEX: The Founding Pentarchy

                        Today a multi-billion dollar international holding company, Pentex was founded in 1915 as Five Corners Holdings, a financial consolidation of five preexisting companies. They gradually continued to expand, absorbing more and more companies over the decades. The holding company became Pentex Investments in the 1940s, and finally just Pentex in the 1980s. While some of Pentex's holdings are openly traded on the stock markets, Pentex itself is a private company, largely unknown to the general public or even much of the modern press. Pentex prefers it this way, and people who unknowingly start digging into their operations tend to meet bad ends.
                        Each of the founding companies was in service to a different powerful Wyrm entity or faction, and because of this backing, combined with their role as founders, each is a powerful block within the greater hydra that is Pentex.

                        Endron Energy International
                        Founded in Pennsylvania as Premium Oil after the American Civil War, it moved to Texas a decade later. While drilling there, company head Jeremiah Lassiter unleashed an ancient Wyrm entity, one so old even the Three Brothers knew of it only from the most ancient of lore. The being, dating back to the time of the Mokole and the Dinosaur Kings, is known only as Black Blood of the Earth. It tried to dominate Lassiter, but his will proved too strong to outright control, and instead they struck a pact, offering power in exchange for influence in the modern world. The strain of resisting the entity's power eventually proved too much for Lassiter, leading to his death from cancer and mental strain.
                        Today, Endron is a global leader in energy technology, extraction, production, and distribution. While petroleum has long been the backbone of their company, they have continued to expand into the areas of nuclear power (absorbing the Atlas Energy Company in the 1980s), hydroelectric, and now solar and wind power. While their recent interest in renewables would seem to run counter to the overall goals of Pentex and the Wyrm, the massive quantities of rare earths and other metals (often strip mined in other countries), the impact of wind farms on avian and sea life, the unreliability of renewables, the corruption of government subsidies to "green" energy, and the coming flood of un-recyclable solar panel and wind turbine parts are all part of a long game.

                        HH Mineral Resources
                        Originally Harold Brothers' Mining Company, founded in the Ohio River Valley near the start of the 19th century to extract coal, iron and other materials for America's burgeoning industry. During the 1830s, a mysterious artifact was found in a mine in what is now West Virginia. The relic of a prehistoric - perhaps even prehuman - civilization, made of an unknown material and inscribed with a single untranslatable glyph, it soon drove both company founders violently mad. One brother killed the other, and created a cult devoted to the artifact. Originally consisting of the Harold brothers' families, and later the inner circle of the company's leadership, the cult has slowly tickled down into the populous of certain mining towns. The being it referred to only by a single syllable bark/cough-like noise that causes pain to the uninitiated.
                        HH Mineral Resources is a global powerhouse in mining and mineral extraction, operating on six continents (and if they could, they'd be strip mining Antarctica). Iron and other metals, coal, rare earths, salt, silicone, uranium, diamonds, and asbestos are all part of their portfolio. Deep tunnel mining (including into lost and labyrinthine caverns within the earth), strip mining, and chemical leaching leave massive damage to the environment, while frequent use of child and even slave labor leave spiritual scars.
                        The company has one of the longest standing relationships with the Black Spiral Dancers, with some tribe members and their kinfolk having joined the nameless cult.

                        Smith & Young Company/SYC
                        Founded in Indiana in the 1850s as a soap and candle manufacturer, it has always been a front for the Red Circle, a secretive cult of Wyrm witches dating back to medieval times (and perhaps the Roman Empire). Their marketing and logos include subtle markings and symbols of their cult and the Wyrm. Membership in the Circle is a requirement to be part of the board or even many upper management positions. Toward that end, they have a number of recruitment arms among college Greek organizations and internship programs.
                        SYC currently manufactures and sells over a hundred different brands, including personal hygiene, cleaning supplies, cosmetics, chewing gum, snack foods, cooking oils, ice cream, family planning, baby supplies, diet and health products, tea, coffee, and condiments. They are almost ubiquitous in the Western world, especially North America. Individually, their products are often harmless, contributing to Pentex's aims primarily through packaging and other waste. However, their packaging and logos can attract the attention of Banes and other spirits, and certain combinations of products may result in unexpected and freakish results.

                        Alegro Global
                        Perhaps one of the oldest of all Pentex companies, originating in colonial Virginia as Clayton Tobacco. By the start of the 20th century, the Clayton Brands Tobacco Company was a major grower and provider of tobacco and tobacco products in the United States and Canada. With the public backlash against tobacco companies in the 1990s and early 2000s, Clayton changed their name to Alegro Global, but continue their same business. Since its founding, the company has always been under the influence of Lord Choke, the Elemental Maeljin of Smoke and Smog. This, combined with their enormous profits from the Asian markets, allow them to maintain their place of power within Pentex.
                        Alegro markets a wide range of products under various labels, including cigarettes of various price points meant to cater to different economic brackets. (They pride themselves on being one of the companies to initially promote smoking as acceptable to women.) In addition, they offer various smokeless tobaccos such as dip and snuff, pipe tobacco, and cigars. Their brands have long been major sponsors of various motorsports around the globe, and membership rewards programs have long offered customers the chance to send in proofs of purchase for all manner of company branded merchandise (including various mascots that were appealing to teenagers). Their tobacco has long been modified with chemicals and other methods to increase its nicotine output and addictiveness. Lately, the rise of e-cigarettes and vaping threatened to cut into their market shares, but their promotion of anti-vaping legislation has given them time to start developing their own vaping products.

                        Logan Foods Corporation
                        Founded in Chicago during the civil war to sell rotted canned meat (often from horses) to the Union armies, Logan Meat Packing Company used various political and criminal connections to become one of the America's major meat suppliers. They controlled every part of their operation from stockyards to slaughterhouses to butchers to salesmen. The founder, Garfield Logan, was in fact a cannibal and murderer who worshiped a Wyrm entity he called "The God of Meat", but which The Three Brothers knew as That Which Devours All Flesh. The leadership of the company still worships this spirit, holding annual corporate retreats that involve hunting and grilling. Usually of people.
                        Currently the company is one of the world's largest processors and marketers of beef, pork, and poultry. Certain sub brands also specialize in various exotic meats, as well as (allegedly), free range, organic, kosher, and halal offerings, as well as various frozen foods, pet foods, and jerkies. Many of the animals used by Logan's are pumped full of growth hormones, including experimental ones being tested for Pentex super-soldier programs, and frequently housed in less-than-ideal conditions. Over the decades, their plants have caused massive levels of water pollution, especially among poorer areas of Southern states. (State governments would frequently look the other way in exchange for campaign contributions or offering corporate positions to family members.)

                                    The Pentax Precursor
                                    The Southern Ocean Trading Company, founded in 1711 a fake investment house that almost bankrupted the entire British Government in 1720, infamously known as the South Sea Bubble.

                                    Purchased in 1829 by the mysterious McGrath family, hailing from Scotland they migrated to Liverpool (or Liuerpul) during the time of Edward Longshanks, the Hammer of the Scots​ (by betraying their clansmen an act that incurred the emnity of the Dursirn Clan).

                                    A family of Wyrm cultists many of their children pure-blood Ferectoi​, the company registered in London and Hudson Bay has interests in logging, shipping, the fur trade, whaling, fishing, merchant goods and retail.

                                    Having encountered heavy resistance in the Hudson Bay, from surprising incorruptible mortals they have turned their attentions to the boom town of San Francisco and it’s multiple places of power.

                                    How they fare against the assembled forces of the Kindred, Hungry Dead, Garou and Changelings all contesting the Golden Bay within the Ring of Fire remains to be seen.

                                        Something I've been fiddling with for the Ananasi, still a work in progress.

                                        Skeins, regional collectives of were-spiders who tend to share similarities in culture, world-view, activities, and Triat orientation. None of them are all encompassing, and numerous Ananasi in those regions aren't part of the dominant Skein. And the nature of modern globalism means that members can often be found all around the globe.

                                        The Arachne (Eastern Mediterranean and Black Sea) - Primarily oriented toward the Weaver, they are famed as weavers and artisans, as well as traders and merchants, often amassing large amounts of covert political power in various countries.

                                        Tsuchigumo/Yatsukahagi (Japan) - Primarily oriented toward the Wyrm/Ebon Dragon, they are on the fringes of the Beast Courts, neither enemy nor ally. They are primarily known as thieves, con artists, infiltrators, and assassins.

                                        The Anansi (West Africa and the Caribbean) - Primarily oriented toward the Wyld. Tricksters, troublemakers, and revolutionaries as well as artists, storytellers, and mystics.

                                        I'm still poking at some more, including the Cob (Britain), the Maya (India), as well as looking for ideas for the Americas, Australia, and China.
