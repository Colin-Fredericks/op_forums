    Things to like W5 for or why so many people hate new edition?

    12-13-2023, 06:51 AM
    Well, after reading of W5 corebook, I can say... surprisengly, I like it. Didn't try the system, but lore is huge improvement for Garou. New edition in fact, retcon the bigger part of their crimes.
    Namely:
    1) No more kinfolk, hense no more eugenic, sex slavery or bestiality in case of Lupus.
    2) Impergium is something that happened so long ago that Garou themselves barely know what did actully happenthan. The 'Delirium-as-collective-human-mental-trauma' is just a theory.
    3) War of Rage? Wtf is this? Considering, that other Fera are pretty much aliens who Garou barely know anything about.
    4) No more Crinos-borned and descrimination toward them
    5) Since Garou have no history anymore, just an oral tradition, there isn't invasion in Americas, War of Tears, Swords of Heimdal and other 'great deeds' Garou were so famous for. Well, at least for now. Not guilty until proven guilty, etc.
    6) Finally, Vampires are not 'Wyrmlings' by default. Like, leeches are still cruel, lying and sneaky bastards... but that stereotype about vampires gonna support like almost any denizen of WoD. First and foremost, vampires themselves.

    TL/DR, authors retconed most of controversies that taunted WtA in past editions. I can't find any objective reason for haters to bush Garou. Now they are... eccentialy, they are pretty close to status of the "good guys".
    ​what's wrong?

                      I don't have a lot of skin in the W5-drama. Werewolf interests me, but it's not one of my top favourite game-lines. I've also already resigned myself to sticking with X20 for the most part.

                      But, reading your list, I can't help but wonder if you realize that most of the things you see as Upsides of the new edition are precisely the sorts of things that most of the older fans are Upset about?

                      I'm not going to say that WtA didn't have some issues, and could have used some rehashing to deal with some of the worst examples. But, broadly speaking, the fact that the Garou are tragic assholes with millennia of catastrophic screw-ups and monstrous traditions is a Feature, not a Bug, for a lot of Werewolf fans. And your list of examples mostly seems to be "hey look, they took the Darkness out of the World of Darkness, isn't that great?" Taking all of the ugliness and moral-Grey out of the Garou turns their struggle into a truly Black-and-White one. And while Werewolf has always been closer to that than most of the other WoD gamelines, it's still supposed to have grit and nuance.

                      Just trying to surgically remove all of that stuff entirely to avoid dealing with the complications probably strikes a lot of people as a lazy method of confronting the actual issues that even fans are willing to acknowledge. The Controversies weren't an accident. They were The Point. And a lot of people don't like the alternative that they've been offered.

                                              The biggest issue with W5 is that Forsaken did pretty much everything it tries to do better. The next biggest issues are the tracing and potentially unintentional cultural insensitivity, but I've not read the book enough to comment on those (spend £50 to read it? Ha!).

                                              I do want to zoom in on the oral tradition though, because do you know who also had one? Why it's both the pre-W5 Group and the Uratha, and they were pretty accurate. The Garou had a good idea of their successes, failures, and the rules of the world they live in. Meanwhile the Uratha are wrong on several details but they're pretty bloody accurate when it comes to what Pangaea was actually like. There's still a lot of wiggle room and uncertainty if you dig into it, but they had their history and owned their glories and failings (and for the Forsaken the death of Father Wolf was both).

                                              The W5 Garou not only don't know, they're also seemingly the only ones who don't. Did the Corax write everything down and then share it with everybody but the PC faction? Or do we have to admit that oral traditions can be reliable, and the Garou's was not due to game writer fiat?

                                              Both the Garou and Uratha fucked everything up in the past. Pre-W5 the Garou gritted their teeth, said 'no I am right', and soldiered on, only later looking back to say 'no I was wrong, and I will atone by doing my duty' (I'm being very rough here). The Uratha decided that if they had destroyed Pangaea they were either going to fix this one way or another.

                                              The W5 Garou are more 'we did what? Why did nobody tell us we did that'. That's less interesting than any of the three groups that preceded them.

                                              The Garou are flawed. The Garou were always flawed. It is our flaws that make us human, and our struggle to do better than makes us great.

                                              The Garou Nation, like the Imperium of Man, is a steaming pile of shit. It's also more interesting to explore than a group of good guys.

                                              WtA had issues, and due to its subject matter and way it was designed it will always have issues. That's not to say that the designers can't strive to do better with the same material, but it feels like W5 didn't want to deal with those issues.

                                              Also I want to touch on Crinos, because I really like them, they're my favourite breed.

                                              Crinos were looked down on for their deformities and disabilities (and infertility). For all it's issues that's holding up a light to a very real and important issue in our society, and it's one that has not gone away. They also touch on the different ways parents are treated based on their gender, and other topics I'm really not qualified to discuss.

                                              Sure, I can still play a wheelchair bound Garou in W5, but the Crinos made our treatment of people with similar conditions an important part of the game. Let's not pretend that our society is now perfect when it comes to this, or any issue.

                                              We were told that W5 was no longer our world but darker, but rather just our world. I would like to suggest that it's actually painted over the cracks to present 'our world but brighter'.

                                              Blue is sarcasm.
