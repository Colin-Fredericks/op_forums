    Werewolf the Apocalypse 5th - Shattered Nation Announced

    03-01-2024, 04:30 PM
    Originally posted by Nicolas Milioni View Post
    The Society of the Garou Unveiled

    Once the Garou tribes were united in one mighty Nation, ready and willing to fight for Gaia whenever and wherever they needed to. Now as the Apocalypse rages, the mightiest bulwark that should have defended against it is in tatters. The Garou Nation lies shattered.

    Shattered Nation is a setting book for Werewolf, detailing the ruins of the Garou Nation and how they can be used concretely in Werewolf stories but it also details opportunities the players’ pack has in Garou politics, to leverage their Renown for power and influence.

    Features
    • Explore pack dynamics (including new Pack types)
    • Take action as a pack
    • Use, Learn, and Portray the Rites of the Garou, including new Rites
    • Run and participate in Moots
    • Deal with the enemies
    • Add 7 new loresheets to their characters
    it's Renegade game studios book,i feel like it might solve some issues most of us have with w5

      I'm not seeing much to get my hopes up in that regard.

      - The blurb focuses on the Nation already being broken, not on any discussions of how that happened (esp. as something that's supposed to have been a fairly recent event but with excruciatingly little acknowledgement of). The phrasing of, "detailing the ruins of the Garou Nation and how they can be used concretely" feels like it's just going to be more of the same, "if you want actual lore and world building, W5 isn't the game for you," than a fix to how little the impact of the shattering of the Nation is really established.

      - More stuff on packs is good. Not sure that addresses many of the big complaints about the game as it currently stands.

      - More Rites doesn't fix the problems with the mechanical implementation of Rites. Unless there's going to be some seriously major "optional" rules here to make Rites something people want to use more, it's largely wasted space. Even if it tries to reestablish the former place Rites had as central parts of Garou social interactions, the mechanics will get in the way of them filling that role.

      - Again, unless there's a huge improvement via "optional" rules, Moots don't work in the RAW, so having a section on them is, at best, some cool fluff that will be out of step with the RAW.

      - "Deal with enemies" is far to vague to preview anything meaningful about the text.

      - More loresheets is fine. The core book certainly could have used more if Loresheets are going to be as important to W5 as they are for V5. But this isn't a major concern in and of itself. Given that some of the core Loresheets are things people have active complaints about, more of them could add in more questionable things like being able to redeem BSDs. So this isn't reassuring to the bigger picture of the issues with the game.

                                                          Some thoughts:
                                                          • Covering what the setting with the fallen Garou Nation looks like is a good addition but this should've been covered by the corebook already. I wonder how in-depth they actually can be or will even try considering the page-count-limitations of this supplement.
                                                          • Pack types are another good addition - which also should've been covered by the corebook already. That way a supplement like this would've had the room to introduce an expanded Pack-type-creation a la the V5 Players Guide.
                                                          • "Taking action as a pack" sounds to me like it will be another one of those "Use a relationship-map to depict conflicts and handle Project rules". Again, something that should've been covered by the corebook because Blood Sigils for example builds on that by delving into a specific variant of using those rules for blood sorcery. Instead, the supplement will have to cover the basics here, I imagine.
                                                          • For the Rites, Kevin mentioned during the stream that there will be like 12 Rites and most of them will be of a mystical variety and they correspond to Auspices (but they're also so broad that everybody can use them). Don't think more Rites is exactly what this game needed but I'm also surprised the book didn't go for listing new Gifts instead.
                                                          • 7 new Loresheets. Ah, good, the numbers are rising (Blood Sigils had 4 Loresheets, for example).
                                                          • The Silver Crown bit from the stream sounds like one of the Loresheets will be "Albrecht's newest BFF" or something like that. From Kevin's description in the W5 timeline Jonas Albrecht was the last King of the Garou with the Silver Crown. That has all sorts of implications about the setting (for one thing it means the Fall of the Garou Nation happened in living memory). Hmm, I guess we're gonna see if they make this work.
