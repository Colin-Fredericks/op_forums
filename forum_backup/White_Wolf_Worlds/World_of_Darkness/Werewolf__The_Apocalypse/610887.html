                          Just my opinions, and you probably won't like some of them. That's fine.

                          Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post
                          The garou are not sentient because their behaviour is controlled by a dice roller
                          This doesn't make any sense at all. Is she even familiar with roleplaying games?

                          Their unnatural, because of their supernatural abilitys and the fact they cannot create healthy offspring with their own kind
                          Yes and no, but totally unrelevant. They are natural in the sense that they are part of "Gaia's order". However, they aren't meant to be separate from humanity or wolves. They are meant to be bridges between them. Gaia did not want them to be separate from humanity or the wolves so she made sure the Changing Breeds can't be insular and reproduce by themselves.

                          They are magical beings. Whether that is "natural" or "unnatural" depends on the settings of your fictional world. In the World of Darkness, this doesn't mark it as inherently unnatural.

                          A bunch of bullshit about how Dogs have a definite evolutioinary advantage over wolves , how tool use IS an evolutionary advantage, and a bunch of other garbage.
                          Strange ideas about dogs. However, tool use is definitely an advantage to humanity.

                          oh some insanity about how environmentalism is the product of people who are not struggling to survive, and often considered gross hypocricy by those who are.
                          I wouldn't say environmentalism itself is weird. There are very good reasons humans want clean air, drinkable water, and other benefits that environmentalism has provided.

                          At the same time, there is definitely a romantic view of the wilderness that some environmentalists have that does not fit actual reality. Those are people who are extremely hostile towards modern day civilization and think it would be a good thing to de-industrialize, eliminate advanced farming techniques, and basically return to primitive technology. They don't care about high infant mortality rates, extreme poverty, constant warfare, and the other disadvantages primitive peoples face because it never seems to occur to them. The wilderness (aka Mother Nature) is DANGEROUS. Natural disasters, famines, diseases, and such are just as part of nature as clean air. So I do think your friend has a point - some environmentalists (not all) have a naive and ignorant view of nature.

                          ANOTHER peice of bullshit she had, was that lupus should be athiestic, since animals dont have religion....
                          This makes no sense. Animals have no religion, but lupus Garou are not animals. They are Garou born of wolves who know spirits exist and are real, and have an intrinsic tie to them (as their high initial Gnosis indicates)

                          Oh an argument that Garou are INFERIOR to humanity that I found really really sick.
                          I wouldn't say Garou are inferior, but they are monsters and they do many inhuman, monstrous things from a human perspective (as well as heroic, good things). I think the game benefits from acknowledging both aspects.

                          I've never bought into the Captain Planet aspects of the game. Environmental themes are appropriate given the Garou religion and setting, but I don't think it should dominate the game. It crowds out a lot of other elements. Dominating specific chronicles makes sense, but there are also many kinds of chronicles to tell where the environmental aspects are in the background. Certainly, the further back in time you go (if you want to do a historical chronicle), the environmental aspects becomes less important. For most of the thousands of years of Garou history, an environmental theme wouldn't make sense.

                          So while the Garou are heroes in an Epic sense along the lines of the Iliad, the myths of Hercules, the Song of Roland, and others, they aren't heroic from a human perspective. Individuals could be, but plenty of Garou heroes would not be.

                                              It doesn't sound like you need much re-programming because you realize that she was bat-shit-crazy, didn't have a real clue about the game, and apparently only read the game with the intent to argue against it like a conspiracy-nut (like the guy who has "proof" that Stephen King killed John Lennon through a government code placed in public magazines...)

                                              However, she's arguing nonsense about the game mechanics, doesn't understand what a Lupus is, and so on.

                                              So, let's start with a few of the ideas you've brought up:

                                              Garou are Heroic:
                                              In the World of Darkness, there are very few beings that are trying to save the world. Most are selfish, trying to fight for their own survival or advancement to live forever, or ascend. Only the Hunters (as a fat-splat) could be seen as being more heroic, putting their lives on the line to save the world from the monsters they perceive to be the world's demons.
                                              The Garou believe that they are the only faction that is actively fighting to keep the world from being destroyed by two of the primordial forces of the world (The Wyrm and the Weaver.) They were created to fight the Wyrm and are learning that they must also be aware of the Weaver's power and influence as well.
                                              They are TOO steeped in tradition and connection with their ancestors and spirits to change their ways very quickly. They are fueled by Rage and their own power at times and have done some really tragic and stupid stuff (killing off their Fera allies and making enemies of the survivors, Allowing 1 Tribe to be turned to the Wyrm, and being responsible for the destruction of the Bunyip because they didn't understand them and were tricked by the Wyrm into doing so.) They have been so blinded by the Wyrm that they ignored the rise of the Weaver as a nearly equal adversary. They also are so self-righteous and again so blinded by their traditions and mis-trust that they war among themselves over territory, meanings of their purposes, and the correct paths to take that they've also slaughtered their own kind to be "right".
                                              Yet, for all of these issues, their unified purpose is to put their lives on the line to fight a war that will hold off the destruction of the world that has been foretold and guaranteed.

                                              The garou are not sentient because their behaviour is controlled by a dice roller:
                                              Again, this doesn't make much sense, unless it was just how she meant to say that "They are influenced by their Rage, and can Frenzy." Otherwise, it really doesn't seem like she understood character creation, or the mechanics of the game if she thought that a character's actions were determined by their rolls.

                                              Their unnatural, because of their supernatural abilitys and the fact they cannot create healthy offspring with their own kind
                                              This sounds like an argument made by hunters (and not Hunters, who are imbued with supernatural powers to fight the rest of the WoD too.)
                                              In the setting and story of Werewolf: the Apocalypse, they are entirely "natural" because the primal forces of the world that decided what were "natural" created them to do what they do.
                                              The fact that they create Metis when breeding with one another is one of those "rules" that the forces that created them determined, and thus that's just part of their natural ways.
                                              We're not talking about designer-mutts here like Doodles, who can't be bred together or else they become genetically muddy. Think of it more as the True Born gene is recessive and when the True Born Garou gets the right genes and becomes True Born, their genetics require more diversity. There are plenty of real-life genetics where two people with recessive genes have a child that is deformed and that's perfectly "natural", even though it's not optimal (a friend's parents both had a recessive gene that their children would have a 1-in-4 chance of being born without functional lungs. Their first two children were born with that condition, so after that, they adopted.)
                                              So, basically, they are natural because "God" made them so. When the creation-diety (Gaia) makes you a certain way, you're as natural to that world as you can be.

                                              A bunch of bullshit about how Dogs have a definite evolutioinary advantage over wolves , how tool use IS an evolutionary advantage, and a bunch of other garbage.
                                              Your friend didn't realize that Dogs are just Wolves who mankind bred for their own purposes?!
                                              Dogs are just tools created by man to do specific tasks they wanted.
                                              When it comes to "evolution" and "tool use", the cosmology of the Universe comes down to "there was balance, then the primal force of order wanted more power and twisted the force of entropy into becoming one of corruption instead, and that caused the universe to sheer itself into parts that were no longer balanced." Evolution and tool-use are formed because the Weaver has the power to move things along that course. Of COURSE tool use is an advantage. The Garou saw that and tried to wipe-out the Humans early on, but there were factions that argued against it and so the humans were allowed to make more tools, change the world, and become the greatest causes of the Weaver and the Wyrm's rise to power.
                                              While the Garou use tools and such as well, they've been slow (as a species) to adapt them into their cultures and ways (the Glass Walkers are obviously among the few who have adopted the idea of evolution, adaptation, and progress, and they're at odds with most of the Nation for that stance.)

                                              oh some insanity about how environmentalism is the product of people who are not struggling to survive, and often considered gross hypocricy by those who are.
                                              This is a fun one to actually use in the games.
                                              Natural environmentalism exists, but not on a commercial level. Humans are, by their nature, greedy and survivors and will wipe an area clean of useful things and resources like a locust plague before they die off, or move on.
                                              Many "environmental" efforts actually use a LOT of resources to accomplish and can be just as devastating elsewhere--Electric Cars may mean less oil production and pollution from burning fossil fuels, but the mining for the materials, replacements of materials, and the energy to re-fuel those vehicles can be very devastating too. People aren't willing to work closer to home and bike/walk to work, people aren't willing to give up their luxuries en-masse, but they'll still buy a 48-pack of plastic bottles a week and put them into a recycling bin and feel like they're "making a difference" rather than just refilling a glass from the tap, even if it tastes funky. There is a LOT of hypocrisy in the mainstream environmental movements because people are greedy, short-sighted, and frankly just don't care... we'll burn the Earth before we consider that we're the cause and problems with it... all 9+ Billion of us.

                                              But how do the Garou fight against that? The Wyrm is everywhere, and it's even in the "environmental" movements in the WoD. Plastic companies that may claim that they are offering "Earth Friendly" materials may be poisoning the very plastic you're supposed to drink from on purpose, using slave-labor to produce them and creating more damage to the world in the guise of doing something that is "good".

                                              To save the Earth, the Garou SHOULD follow the Red Talon's plan--kill the Humans... which would stop the Weaver and the Wyrm from having their greatest assets, but the Garou are so tied to a Human world that they just can't do it, and they'll fight against it... because whether or not they want to believe it, they're lost to their humanity.

                                              ANOTHER peice of bullshit she had, was that lupus should be athiestic, since animals dont have religion:
                                              Yep. Again, Garou just don't wake up as werewolves and go on to be "natural" werewolves running around and murdering things--not often at least. Also, I don't think your friend understood what a "religion" was either.
                                              Lupus are born as wolves. They aren't cognizant as Humans are, but they are ferally smart. THEN when they go through their First Change they are awakened to the world around them as the covers are thrown off of their eyes (similarly, when Homids are born, they don't have the animal instincts, but become aware of them when they go through their First Change.) Both are awakened to a new understanding of the Universe, but hued by the life they understood before their Change. No, Wolves don't have a religon, but they do have an understanding of the fundamentals of a feral world. When they are awoken to the world around them, they are brought into the life of being Garou, given a connection to the Spirits, to the primordial make-up of the Universe, and that their place was designed and destined by greater spirits.
                                              So, Garou aren't Animals, and Lupus aren't Animals either because they aren't Wolves, just as Homids aren't Humans once they go through their First Change either... they've become something greater.

                                              Oh an argument that Garou are INFERIOR to humanity that I found really really sick.
                                              I can't really help with this one without more details... though in legends it was Humans who taught the Garou how to harness the elements in ways that didn't directly require the Spirits, but rather their understanding of natural sciences... you didn't have to ask spirits to create fire, but you could use friction, or flint/steel and make it happen.
