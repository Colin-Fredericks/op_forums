                        I can't remember where I saw it, so I won't guarantee, but I remember quite a number of official rank 6 Garou, at least a dozen. Just the Silverfangs had three or four.

                        About why saying that Rank 6 Gifts are weak...

                        Unstoppable Warrior let you make a roll at dif 8 just to get the right to spend Rage on regeneration of non-silver Agg. Since it is without spending your action, it certainly is good, but maybe Rank 3 or 4?

                        One on One lets you spend a permanent dot of Gnosis to choose a foe and fight it. Then it lets you all alone where the foe were. It is quite powerful, but given the stingy restrictions, nothing beyond Rank 5.

                        Sleep of the Hero lets you spend 2 Gnosis and make a roll at dif 9 to do nothing. Well, technically you resurrects your target, but it will sleep soundly until the Apocalypse, so you really do nothing.

                        Then you get Storyteller. You spend 1 perma Gnosis and say shit. Shit is true. Who thought this had any semblance of balance?

                        Call Great Fenris isn't bad, just cliché. I'm pretty sure I saw at least 2 or 3 other Get gifts that call Fenris and he eats your hand. At Rank 6 even calling Great Fenris shouldn't cost your hand. It could cost any number of perma Gnosis or Perma Rage, because you buy them back, but your hand?

                        Release from Bondage makes you immune to mystical coercion and able to break it on others by touch, and that is one of the few RAW Gifts I think that qualify for Rank 6. This thing not only can be actually used, it is effectively good.

                        Firebringer is an obvious but cool concept. But the efforts to balance it went too far. Every time you're affected by a supernatural power of any kind you can choose to: A) make a journey into the Umbra, then B) work with a spirit C) for a non-disclosed time to D) develop a new Gift based on it E) that you can't use. You only get to F) spend your time teaching it to others and G) being forever vulnerable to the same power again. Yes, you don't even get to resist it better, you get to resist it worse. It achieves to be worse than Sleep of the Hero.

                        Home in All Lands is the second in the list that is actually good. When you step sideways, you can instead teleport, with few restrictions, fair precision and no drawback.

                        Shield of Gaia is too open for interpretation to be sure. You choose a modern technology, you become forever immune to it. Simple, can be weak or OP depending on the choice, but mostly ok.

                        Renew the Cycle, the famous gift of Lord Albrecht, is worse than useless. You have to win a contested roll between Gnosis and Willpower, can only affect undead, and this thing costs you three perma Gnosis. 3 perma Gnosis to kill a vampire, and you still need to win a contest that isn't rigged in your favor, but against you. Any valid target you have an actual chance of affecting you can also kill with far less effort.

                        Thought-Form of the Twelve Ministers is another ok Gift, though nothing so awe-inspiring. 1 temp of each Rage, Gnosis and Will is a little stingy, but can still be used repeatedly, and that is the strong point. You get a set of lousy skill bonuses (+2 Brawl is the biggest concern here), self-cleanse from wyrm-taint (cool and of significance), and the true power, you refreshes Gnosis for every Garou at 10 feet. This last benefit is worth the Rank 6.

                        Invoked Presence is interesting, but have much of the same problems of Shield of Gaia. You call the Totem Gift for several hours in a 180 yards radius. Ok, but hard to adapt.

                        As In the Beginning is weird. You spend some Gnosis, make a roll, and rips a hole in the Gauntlet. It seems dire, indeed. But the only permanent effect is a reduction of 1 in the Gauntlet that can't be stacked by new uses. Everything else is left so vague that you can't even know how large the affected area is.

                        Rebirthing, unique to Grimfang, makes him reincarnate once. It isn't bad, but it is kinda lame.

                        Becoming Uktena is finally the worse Rank 6 Gift ever. It is like Call Great Fenris, except that the Uktena called isn't bound to do shit after a turn and you die for good. At least there is no roll.

                                In a general way of thinking, when you are about to create or recreate a rank 6 gift, you must have in mind that it is a gifted power directly from a high umbral god, be it Gaea, Arees, Eelios or whoever, it is an element of above epic proportions, so you must think how this power works when a god is using it. A warrior or beast god with a gift that resembles "Unstoppable Warrior'' for example will not heal one aggravated damage per turn, man even great beasts of pagaea heal two aggravated damage per turn without pausing, so a related god will mostly like heal all damage and keep fighting without wound penalties. So don't feel ashamed if a rank 6 power seems too powerful as long as there is only one character that knows it. Also just because they are powers above elder status being taught by higher beings may not be canceled by lesser, even rank 5 powers. Thats of course a personal taking on the rank 6 issue.

                                                    Keep your lecture on the game to yourself, first and foremost. I got tired of it still in the 90's. I already know what the authors intended, what the game is supposed to do, thank you very much.

                                                    But the question is those powers under the condition of being a Rank 6 Gift. Most of them are really cool ideas that could be explored in other ways. A Rank 6 Gift isn't simply a power. It is a power that:

                                                    1- Distinguishes a Legend from their lesser peers. It have to be more than a little interesting. It have to speak that the Garou in question is legendary in status. Otherwise, it could be better served as a lesser power;

Even if the player for any reason don't spend the XP, it is still a let down;
