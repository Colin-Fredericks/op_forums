                                                For the purpose of thralls: Seems like one reason for them existing is to be a mechanical discouragement from taking extreme Rage. There's plenty of roleplay reasons to avoid it (or enjoy it), but even a hardened roll-player who wants ten Rage to beat things up faster needs something to fear, even if they stay away from social situations.

                                                Re: treatment of Metis. Maybe I'm extrapolating way too far, but Garou abuse of Metis serves a very useful purpose, even in tribes that don't think Metis deserve that kind of treatment: it gives one more reason not to make one in the first place. If Metis were well-treated then, okay, charaches can get killed for their crimes, but at least they have their love-child, and isn't that kinda worth it in a way? But if everyone likes to kick the Metis, then in addition to committing a great crime, you've brought someone into the world just to condemn them to a life of suffering. A Garou with true sympathy ought to do whatever they can to avoid bearing a Metis...and therefore, their own kind heart helps convince them to keep the Litany.

                                                Surely most Garou don't think of things in such terms, but Elders very well might (and it's the kind of thing that gets ingrained into society and kept because it works). If you reverse it, it's easy to argue that being nice to Metis starts making charaching not look so bad. Hell, look at religious fundamentalists' opposition to birth control or STD vaccinations: they're not dumb, they know more people get hurt, but if that fact helps scare others onto the straight-and-narrow then they figure it was worth it. Garou are, in their own way, very much fundamentalists.
