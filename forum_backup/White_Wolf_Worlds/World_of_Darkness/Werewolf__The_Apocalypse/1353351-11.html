                I never accused you of arrogance. I'd say it's a pretty valid accusation, all things considered.

                You can feel bad for the new devs because a subsection of fans are wary at best due to past products. But you can't expect a quote like...
                Originally posted by Lyrics Of War View Post
                The developer of w5 is a long time fan of werewolf who says (at least in the podcast) that he’s dedicated to respecting the world of werewolf while introducing new lore.
                ...To change anything or anyone's mind, when almost the exact same quotes were used during V5's development. Or DnD4e's development. Or really any gameline with a revision that wasn't well recieved.

                Sometimes "being a fan" doesn't mean being capable of respecting what came before. I'm a Star Wars fan. I should not be trusted to make a good Star Wars movie. I'm a Spider-Man fan. I'd probably write the worst Spider-Man comic of all time. "Being a fan" is not a guarantee of quality by any single measure. It is not a valid qualifier to one's capabilities to continue a gameline, movie series, comic book or any form of media.

                But a good qualifier is past work. If I had to pick between Joss Whedon and Michael Bay for the next Star Wars movie, I'd pick Joss Whedon because I can base my expectations on both of their prior work. If I had to choose between Donny Cates and Tom King for the next Spider-Man comic? Donny Cates all the way, based on past work.

                So when I'm looking at past work, and I'm seeing things I don't like? I can pretty much rest assured I won't like the new product. It's not an unfair assumption; it's an informed one.

                I don't like V5. I don't like it from a narrative perspective, nor from a mechanical one. I haven't liked the post-core book products, nor did I like the direction of V20 products after nWW took over. Since nWW will still have a heavy hand in W5's development, I can make an informed assumption that just like a Michael Bay Star Wars movie or a Tom King Spider-Man comic, I won't like an nWW-directed or produced W5 game.

                So again, I'm not accusing you of arrogance, nor do I know where you're getting that assumption from. I'm definitely accusing you of ignoring the validity of all criticism in favor of your own opinion, which is simple short-sightedness. I'm accusing you of being short sighted.
