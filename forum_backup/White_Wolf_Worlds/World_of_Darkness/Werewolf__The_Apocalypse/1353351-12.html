                            You know why?

                            Basically every WoD gameline besides V20 is either incomplete or in some cases non-existent. After nWW shat on W20, we've got not equivalent of Lore of the Clans for the Tribes, nothing like M20 Book of Secrets to collect a lot of expanded customization options that didn't make it into the core book, etc. Changeling, and Wraith are similarly not getting a chance for a decent catalog of 20th supplements. We don't have a Numina20th book to cover WoD: Sorcerer or Sorcerer Revised, just a few Numina scattered across a few books. Mummy, Hunter, Demon, and (yet controversial but the point stands) Kindred of the East aren't getting 20th books at all.

                            Yes, there's the Storyteller Vault where a lot of freelancers are putting what probably would have been those supplements, but a lot of us don't play in groups that allow "unofficial" material or don't do so lightly (more now than ever with more gaming moving online with COVID-19).

                            If WW committed to letting Onyx Path finish the 20th lines, and not repeating the off-putting use of editorial oversight we saw in Shattering Dreams and Changing Ways? It'd be a lot easier to accept the "W5 isn't my jam, but I have W20." Since 20th got canned for 5th before a lot of books could get made, this sentiment is going to create division no matter how much it's meant to be a call to unity and understanding... because it completely lacks in understanding.

                            -------------

                            I will say, the writing team is a good one. I stand by my previous worries about the lack of WtA professional experience there (esp. with lots of non-Hunters Entertainment or Renegade people working on it ), but if nothing else I'm confident that W5 is going to be written way better than V5 was.
