        So, assuming Odin is an elder 4th generation vampire, how would you use him? I think the best place to start looking is at Baba Yaga. She was also an ancient 4th generation vampire. She was a vastly powerful magic user, and in mythology Odin possessed unparalleled skill with magic and runes. After awakening, Baba Yaga constructed a vast Shadow Curtain preventing any supernatural beings from entering or leaving Russia. She easily crushed all of the l9cal vampires beneath her iron feet. She controlled the Zmei, immensely powerful dragons capable of wiping out multiple packs of werewolves. Even though she was "only" 4th generation, she was so powerful the werewolves of Russia had to take the unprecedented step of forming an alliance with vampires, Mages and other beings to defeat her.

        I think that's around where I would want the All Father to be - an unimaginably powerful and ancient vampire capable of causing havoc for werewolves.

        Like Baba Yaga he's ancient (though likely not quite as ancient) and his mystical capacity is probably comparable. He wouldn't have anything like the Zmei serving him, but the big difference is that he's have his own childer, the Valkyries. Baba Yaga had a few older vampires serving her, but she was generally suspicious and untrusting of them. Meanwhile Odin would likely have multiple ancient, thousands year old 5th and 6th generation vampires serving him. Importantly, his Valkyrie would have spent thousands of years seeking out powerful warriors and turning them into fellow vampires, preparing them for Ragnarok. So Odin would likely have a powerful army at his disposal and some of his followers would be so old and powerful they could take on a pack of werewolves. It makes for a nice set of boss fights as the PCs kill his Einherjar and then Valkyries before fighting Odin himself.

        If I were to go the full vampire Odin route, I'd likely go full Gonzo-tier. I'd have him awaken and move to take control of a large part of Scandinavia. He has no use for, or respect for, the Masquerade. His soldiers begin openly moving to take control of cities and towns. Vampires, governments, the Technocracy start scrambling to try and cover up what's happening. Something like this could force another shakey alliance between werewolves and vampires and maybe other groups as both as everyone moves to destroy Odin. If it were specifically for Werewolf, then I'd make it a bit "video gamey" and co.bat heavy where the players slowly take down his minions before facing him directly.

                        When it comes to concern over the distinction between vampire gods and formal gods who are spiritual-metaphysical, I think that there are ways to square the circle on this.

                        I agree that having Odin simply be a vampire runs serious risks of becoming gonzo but I also think there are ways to have figures walking around who can be the Most High in a meaningful sense. This is getting into some nuances of magical/mystical thinking a little, so I hope what follows makes sense.

                        I think that you can have a metaphysical It which we can call Odin (what this ‘It’ is, is a larger discussion than I can manage atm). This It contains the god Odin of the Aesir of Asgard (which you can have represented in the Umbra & Astral Realm as well as being an ineffable spiritual substance which cannot fully be materialized). However I don’t think that precludes the possibility of imagining mortal cults whose practices feed into the spiritual mass which constitutes various parts/forms/manifestations of this spiritual substance. Again, how you interpret this can vary. Furthermore there is a precedent in mystical thought for a layering of presence and manifestation. I do not thinks it is infeasible to allow for their to be a metaphysical It substance for Odin, a variety of umbral manifestations which are recognizably Odinic, and even that a mortal-turned-vampire could apply this mask to themselves. I would even say that this idea of a mask does not need to be limiting - I don’t think that one necessarily needs to suggest that the vampiric Odin is false because you can consider it a Mask; the mask is real and effectual.

                        This is somewhat similar to how I feel about having gods appear in werewolf in general. My usual attitude is that there are a variety of primordial spirits in the middle-umbra and that some manifestations of these spirits will ‘put on the mask’ of a god (for example: there are plenty of storm-spirits, some of whom may put on the Mask of Thor). That by no means makes them a fake-god but it is a more nuanced situation than their being one discrete and true god-form. You can think about it in terms of various rl masking/embodying practices: ritual practioners of say, voudoun or a variety of animist traditions, who through special preparation become the vessels of spirits (gods, ancestors, etc.); they become, in real terms, the spirit but that doesn’t necessarily mean that the spirit is not also active or present elsewhere, it simply means that it has been embodied and realized here.

                        I think one can allow for a case like that with the All High, a mortal man who became a vessel for a part of the metaphysical substance we call Odin, and has remained such even after becoming a vampire.

                                            I’ll also put some Changing Breed types among the All High’s forces, or at least concepts found in the Werewolf gameline. These are also fairly minor forces in terms of power, and I would be very careful before using any of them in a Vampire chronicle.                                             In my chronicles, Sam Haight is not the originator of the Skin Dancers, merely one of its rediscoverers. And the Garou pelt that allows Skin Dancing is not limited to be used by only one person, but can be passed down to later generations. The All High, in fact, was the person who first figured it out thousands of years ago and taught the first kinfolk how to do it, and there has been a secret lineage of alienated kinfolk Skin Dancers down the ages allied to Odin (which I call the Ulfhednar). Besides them, the All High also figured out how to do the same with the Gurahl and Grondr, but those lines are now lost (although at least one werebear pelt has survived). So there are both a kinfolk line and Skin Dancers among the Host.

                                            The Ulfhednar are patronized by the Minotaur spirit just like other Skin Dancers, but they know the spirit as the Grinder (Grendel) instead. The Minotaur spirit, for some reason, has a ban for his packs to slay the children of Pegasus. Ulfhednar packs under the Grinder are sometimes mistaken by the Black Furies to be Garou of the Get of Fenris, and their attacks are one (small) reason for the poor relations between those Gaian tribes.

                                            The vampires have created a story to secure the loyalty of these Skin Dancers. They are the blessed of Odin, specially choosen to battle Odin’s enemy: the Children of Fenris (the Garou). They warn such children that the Get of Fenris are as cunning as they are cruel, and will lie to them in order to turn them against Midgard. (One of their basic lies is to claim their mother is not the evil giantess Angrboda, but the goddess Jord.) However, if they become bloodsworn to the Walkure, the goddess children of Odin, their minds will be protected against such tricks. Such is the power of Odin.

                                                    I am also considering the possibility of adding some Ceilican to the Host. Mainly because I like the association of cats with Freya (who in turn would be tied to the elves of House Aesin). It is limited to only a handful of feline Ceilican in Scandinavia. They are the descendants of Ceilican originally allied to the Unseelie House of Aesin, and thus knew of their alliance with the All High. This arrangement lasted thousands of years. That all changed when the Unseelie lord Samhach (not from House Aesin) discovered a way to bind and enslave the Ceilican, and shortly afterwards other elven lords did the same. But a few members of House Aesin who were part of the Raven Banner offered to protect a small handful of feline Ceilican as a result of their previous assistance to the Host. (O perhaps non-fae members of the Host who knew about them hid them. I am open to changing my mind on who the benefactors should be, but right now I am making it some of the Aesin (or at least Shadow Court fae aligned with them). They, in effect, became servants, but they were at least free from the magical compulsions of their race. Thus when the Ceilican Tybalt de Leon managed to break these magical chains and lead the majority of Ceilican to the New World, a small number of Ceilican decided to stay behind and assist their old allies out of gratitude, now as equals and not servants.

                                                    The Ceilican who are part of the Host are very few in number, all of feline breed, and all remain in Scandinavia close to their allies. These Ceilican concentrate on Shadow Craft, particularly paths of spirit sorcery. They are probably the Host’s foremost experts of the Penumbra. They are perhaps the All High’s only true allies among the Changing Breeds, but it is important to note their involvement in the alliance is through the Fae, not the vampire lord himself (who had disappeared before Samhach enslaved his first Ceilican). And since then they have neglected their previous fae saviors in favor of mortals of the Raven Banner. Just to be more safe and limit the chance of news of their survival from reaching the other fae.

                                                    These Ceilican view their participation in the Raven Banner (and they almost exclusively use this name) as part of their duties to Gaia. And while they honestly help their allies (they feel a debt to those fae who could have enslaved them but did not and valued the various supernatural help the alliance provided them during those centuries), they are also gathering much knowledge about their allies in turn. Who knows what information will be important when the Apocalypse comes? Though few in number and feeling gratitude for their earlier help, they intend to use the Raven Banner for their own purposes, and not be content to be the minions of others. For now they prove useful to their allies both changeling and mortal, and almost unknown to the vampire members of the alliance.

                                                    I am going back and forth on this. These few Ceilican are not obvious members of the Host, and their small numbers mean they may never even show up in game. Little would be missed if they were not included, and there are many easy objections to their inclusion. However, my personal taste leads me to want to include them simply because I think a few Ceilican make a good flavor text. And it possibly allows for the ST to introduce the Host as an unnamed background in a more postivive manner than introducing them through their more obvious villainous elements.

They were employed as disposable shock troops to protect the more important supernatural members of the Host.

                                                        These fomori, however, required drinking a toxic mixture of alcohol, henbane, hallucinogenic mushrooms, and other poisonous ingredients that tainted the body to make it possible for Scrags to possess them. This wicked knowledge was purged during the Christianization of Scandinavia as dangerous witchcraft. The modern Host works to restore this knowledge, but has only created an imperfect potion. Although it does create fomori, the fomori is actually no more powerful than a normal human unless he imbibes the potion agains, and it only works for several hours. The original version only required the fomori to activate their powers (through a Dexterity + Primal Urge roll). Members of the Host are coming to believe the full secret of the potion will only be relearned once the All High fully returns from his torpor.

                                                        • Berserker (5 Rage)
                                                        • Armored Skin
                                                        • Extra Speed
                                                        • Regeneration
                                                        • Sense Gaia
                                                        • Spirit Ties
                                                        The fomori berserkers belong to both mortal cults and as vampire allies/retainers. The knowledge of the toxic potion is known to select Walkure and mortal sorcerers of the Host.
