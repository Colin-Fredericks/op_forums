    [W5] Should the Cult of Fenris be the Cult of Wolf?

    12-22-2023, 06:25 PM
    They renamed multiple tribes to get rid of their cultural origins but the Cult of Fenris is still tied rather dramatically with a figure from Norse Mythology.

    Shouldn't they be the Cult of Wolf?

    It would make more sense and would still be fine as a name.

      Honestly, you shouldn't tie the very spirit of the species the characters are (supposedly, if we are to believe the dog-hispo thing.)

      I'd actually say it could be something like the Cult of Wolverine or the Cult of Long Winter and work better. A spirit that, alongside the original cult, got fed up with the inaction of the Nation and took action on their own.

      That way, we'd also open the doors for the cult to be more than just a thin-veiled effort to make the Fenrir the worst of the lot. If the Cult was a multi-tribal group that included the most extreme members of each tribe, then suddenly you could still have Fenrir as PCs and use the Red Talon example characters in a much more fitting context.

              I am not familiar with all the changes with the Get of Fenris so I am limiting my remarks on renaming Fenris as a more universal totem.

              I wouldn't want to use "Wolf" because that implies Fenris is the Father Wolf (totemic spirit of all Garou) as opposed to an aspect of Wolf like Winter Wolf or Old Wolf of the Woods.

              However, one could use something like the Fen Dwelling Wolf (which is what Fenris Wolf means) or the Apocalypse Wolf (for his role in the final battle) or the Bloody Wolf or similar (if you want to highlight his war aspects) or something else that is poetic for some role.

              I think Fenris is fine to keep as a name that is evocative and immediately explains what the spirit is like. But my take is that the Garou predate all the human cultures most identified with the homid side of the tribe so I am fine for having some other name that can be used as well as long as the earlier name isn't treated like it's been purged.

                  Aside from the Patron Spirit being Wolf, I think the other part is questionable too. Why call it a cult? I imagine that's where the idea for the torture brainwashing scene came from. Because it's called a cult and so that writer at Renegade Games thought of cult-like actions. But it's clear that then quickly enters BSD territory.

                  Them being these Rage-fueled extremists also doesn't work. After all, it's not like you have the CoF on one side too much driven by Rage and the other Garou on the other hand being this faction that's very careful about how Rage affects them overly concerned with restraining themselves. And reading bits like the "problem with Garou " section, it's hard to see a distinction between Garou and the CoF. WtF did that much better in giving the Uratha and the Pure two distinctive ideologies. A sourcebook needs to get on this ASAP and give the CoF a similar treatment.

                  CoF isn't supposed to be BSD-lite and they have to be more distinct from the other Garou. So I think in this case I think making the Patron Spirit Fenris, the Apocalypse Beast would work better and give them maybe a bit more of a Millenarian flavor in their ideology.
