    Garou and Fomori: "relations"

    09-18-2022, 05:32 AM
    This is for a concepted of an "evil campaign" currently in the works.

    Its stated that fomori, other than their spiritual nature and mutations, are human for the most part. It even states that kinfolk can be fomorized like any other human.

    Garou have an overall bad relationship with fomori (duh), but garou of the black spiral dancers dont have these issues.

    So, my main question is this:
    Can garou and fomori mate and produce offspring?
    If not, why not?
    If so, would the birth be affected by its parentage?
    Would it still follow Garou breed percentages?
    Would/could the birth come out as a fomor?
    Would the BSD allow this, or even have an issue with it?
    Could it happen outside of the BSD, and if so who, and how?

    This is meant for hypotheticals in character creation.

      Originally posted by Devilturnip View Post
      Can garou and fomori mate and produce offspring?
      As long as there aren't issues on an individual level, I don't see what would stop them from being able to do so.

      If so, would the birth be affected by its parentage?
      In a broad sense, any such offspring would be heavily tainted by the Wyrm given the conditions, and likely very easily corrupted or possessed since they'd lack the ability to consciously fight off bane influence.

      Would it still follow Garou breed percentages?
      Sure, if you use those.

      Would/could the birth come out as a fomor?
      Most fomori can't be passed on by birth, but a few of them can, so go by the write-ups in that regard. Though if the offspring is born as a pre-change werewolf, that would generally override them being possessed as well.

      Would the BSD allow this, or even have an issue with it?
      In general, yes. Though specific situations would always cause variations. For example a BSD kin of high PB might be kept from bane-possession since that can have negative effects on their health and diminish their ability to have more kids.

      Could it happen outside of the BSD, and if so who, and how?
      A few supernaturals out there would consider having sex and potentially dealing with offspring as a result with fomori. Some fomori are also designed towards seduction and so on, so they have a chance at least of procreating with almost anyone out there.

      This is meant for hypotheticals in character creation.

      For the most part, as far as char-gen goes, this should really just end up as reasons for certain Merits/Flaws to show up.

                      Originally posted by Devilturnip View Post
                      This needs a baseline allowance from WOD rules; which are scarce as fomori relations with other supernaturals other than violence quite niche and hardly touched upon.
                      Basically being a fomor doesn't change what the person biologically is, unless the specific Bane does that. Indeed, nothing about the person changes unless specified otherwise, although a given fomor may have any such specification. Even the corruption and mutations don't necessarily make them easy to detect by other people, the only standard exception being Sense Wyrm because it specifically senses such taint, and even then you need some successes to be sure about it being a fomor.

                      So anyone capable of having offspring with humans can have them with a fomor. And depending on the fomor, even a werewolf may be unaware of the fact, rare, but not impossible. The base result is the standard for the other parent having children with humans, if heavily tainted. For Werewolves it includes the normal chances for Garou or Kinfolk, with everything that entails. Technically this child may even be cleansed in time to have a relatively normal life. BSDs are usually fine with all this and may even engage in mating with certain fomori.

                      At the same time fomori vary a lot, so you can change any aspect of this. The child may be a fomor (or become one shortly after), the child may have a non-standard chance of being Garou or Kinfolk or whatever. When you mix this with other splats it becomes muddier as there's too little info on how they interact with Wyrm rules, but the baseline is still the same.

                                          Originally posted by Devilturnip View Post
                                          to be survivable and fun in a game whilst avoiding overextending into "mary sue" territory.
                                          Complementing what was said about consistency and optimization, what makes a "Mary Sue" isn't the character concept, but how it is implemented in the story. If the story seems organic and fun, and the resulting character can work with the group, within the theme of the chronicle and not overstep their limelight, then it's fine. No character is a Mary Sue by itself, it is the story and how it is told that makes it a Mary Sue.

                                          In the specific context of the WoD the child of a fomor isn't a common occurrence outside Hives, but isn't unheard of or too contrived. If it is a BSD (you said it is an "evil campaign"), then it is quite doable, although even outside it would be a plausible, if tricky, background. For mechanical effects, there's nothing. You can use this to justify almost anything, but the only really expected result is a lot of Taint at birth, otherwise perfectly normal child.

                                          Originally posted by Lachdanan View Post
                                          Mechnanicaly it's possible
                                          An actual Werewolf-fomor wouldn't be just the offspring of a fomor, but a possessed Garou, even if possessed at birth.

                                          The problem with this is that by default Banes (and actually any Spirit with possession powers) can only possess normal humans and/or animals. It isn't impossible to have a Bane capable of possessing some supernatural being, but rare, and one capable of possessing a Garou even before the First Change is rarer still. Even then, the most probable result would be for the victim to lose the Wolf in the process and cease to be a Garou, the Bane or the circumstances of the possession would also have to justifiably .

                                          It doesn't mean this is completely impossible, but would require a highly specific set of circumstances to justify in most stories, probably with some major character having deliberately caused it in some way.

                                          But then, this character wouldn't have to be the child of a fomor. The idea doesn't seem to necessarily be the Garou being a Fomor, but the backstory being applicable. For the Garou Fomor sure, mechanically it would be just a natter of joining the two mechanics and the result would probably be subpar on the long run, if extremely powerful for a couple game sessions. If it is just the child of a Fomor, then it's even easier because you just have to build a Garou, maybe consider buying Flaws and Merits from other splats.

                                          Originally posted by Lachdanan View Post
                                          1) BSD's look down on the fomori and getting renown would be more difficult (also playing a BSD is not too healthy).
                                          Playing a BSD seems to be the point, anyway.

                                          For the Garou Fomor I don't know if getting Renown would even be possible. While how the BSDs treat the character depends a lot on the specifics, for all intents and purposes this being isn't a Garou anymore, just a Fomor, as smart and powerful as it may be. If they do grant any recognition it would be very similar to their treatment on Abominations, who are exactly that from their POV anyway (if they're really that in the metaphysics or rules is irrelevant).

                                          But for a Fomor-born things would be more on the standard well of considerations of Garou Society. The specifics of the birth and everything else. BSDs are more likely to be open to such Garou, and it isn't unlikely that a number of them are actually exactly like that anyway. Indeed, you could have a "Brood Mother" Kinfolk Fomor whose main purpose is to continually mate and give birth to children, with mutations to speed up the process. BSDs love this kind of thing.

                                          Originally posted by Lachdanan View Post
                                          2) Non-BSD would be maximum a lost-cub with a big mark on his back.
                                          Again, for all effects a Garou-Fomor is a Fomor. Even calling them a Lost Cub is tricky. Whatever a given Werewolf expects from a Fomor will be expected from this being, and very few expect anything good from their continued existence. There are Fomori that can resist the urges and play nice, and even fewer Garou that recognize this and may give a chance to a Fomor.

                                          But all of those are the attitudes towards Fomori in general, and they'll carry on. It's extremely unlikely that such a creature will ever be considered as anything but a Fomor by anyone outside the BSD.

                                          The Fomor-Born would be at a better situation, if still in a very bad position. The early exposure to so much Taint means many will be extremely wary of this Garou at best, and how long it lasted and why will be key here. Bottom line, the safest approach to someone so deeply tainted is to kill and burn the corpse, no questions asked, as if they had an extremely dangerous contagious disease (which in terms, they have). But if the taint can be successfully cleansed, then things dial down, it is an untainted Garou and deserves the full protection of the Litany even if no one likes it.

                                          So the trick would be to explain how the character got from born to a fomor to cleansed without being killed in this interim. Unlikely, but again, not impossible. And once it is done, then as bad as this Garou's standing and prospects can be, they're game to be in virtually any place and tribe, even if their peers are ashamed and enraged by their presence.
