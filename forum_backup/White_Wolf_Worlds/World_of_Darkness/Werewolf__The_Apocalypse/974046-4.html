                                        Originally posted by Sergeant Brother View Post
                                        I have discussed .50 BMG sabots before and they should work pretty well, and will be going insanely fast when they strike the target, which might cause nasty hydrostatic shock.
                                        Honestly, .50 cal sabots would do nothing appreciably better in this instance. They're anti-materiel rounds, not anti-personnel. If I remember my ballistics right, they transfer a lot less force to the target and produce pathetic wound channels compared to same-caliber anti-personnel rounds. They'd probably perform worse, taking that into consideration.

                                        Hydrostatic shock I doubt would even be a consideration against garou, considering their tremendous toughness and healing factor. Maybe if they're in lupus or homid form, but I doubt the hybrid forms or crinos would be even worth discussing.

                                        If you want any kind of armor-penetrating round at all, you'll want to go smoothbore (i.e. shotguns). Likewise, as I said, any discussion of jacketed rounds or super-high calibers is the wrong way to go. You don't want overpenetration; the ideal situation is the round doesn't through and through, instead staying in the werewolf's body continuing to cause damage on a per-turn basis as any contact with silver would, nerfing their Gnosis as potentially forcing them back into breed form for the sake of survival. Jacketed hollowpoints and semi-wadcutters really is the way to go, here, and as a ballpark I'd say nothing higher caliber than .30-06.

                                        And, as a semi-postscript, I have my doubts about silver bullets being perfectly usable in semi-autos and autos. Silver is a lighter metal, and one would need to increase the powder charge commensurately to get comparable ballistic performance (provided they're hand-loading, or mass producing custom rounds). At least, it may make for unreliable performance in recoil-operated systems, and considering the need to increase the powder charge, blowback- or gas-operated may be the way to go.

                                            Silver is 92% the density of lead and 130% denser than steel, the two most common materials for bullets. It will outperform steel for wounding and lead for penetration, so it would be the ideal round because it is soft enough to tumble in flesh (rather than mushrooming like lead or penetrating like steel) while it is hard enough to penetrate most ballistic armors. As for .50 caliber, who cares? Garou might as well be human when hit by a silver round, and humans do not survive a body shot from .50 BMG. They should die with a sizzling hole the size of a bowling ball in their torso and revert to their breed form. Also, you do not need more powder for a lighter round, the increased velocity from the same powder charge should balance out the change in kinetic energy.

                                                    Aside from the usefulness of silver rounds, one factor that may limit their use for hunters without unlimited budgets is that hypothetically, in the WoD, silver might be more expensive than the real world. It might not be a huge uptick in price, but over time it adds up.
