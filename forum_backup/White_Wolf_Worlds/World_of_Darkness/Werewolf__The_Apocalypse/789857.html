    Primal Fera

    12-20-2015, 03:13 PM
    Have any of you every looked at a documentary for dire wolves, the short-faced bear, or the dinosaurs and think "if that were a were-creature it would kick ass". I mean think of all those A grade super predators that dominated the landscape and only went out because they got a cheap shot from the sky(a big meteor, the ice ages, etc etc.) And tell me they wouldn't be badass in a were-creature fight.

    Well, why wouldn't they have been were-creatures? Ever think it was weird how when mages or garou went umbra diving they'd occasionally find something that was suppose to be extinct a million years ago roaming around like it owned the place(and likely very hungry). Ever Think some Other things may have survived with them?

    What would these "Primal" Were-Creatures be like? how did tghey survive? what powers might such being have?

    Share your thoughts!

This could apply to any fera in WOD as well, as every surviving species on earth have a primal ancestor that once surpassed it in every function.
