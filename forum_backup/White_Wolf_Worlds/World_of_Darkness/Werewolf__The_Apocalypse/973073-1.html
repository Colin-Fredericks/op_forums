                    Without the W20 and it reworking of the Umbra I always assumed the following:

                    The Stargazer gift "Astral Mind" brings one to the same penumbra-like area of the Astralplane as does Auspex or the Astral Projection psi numina.
                    Since I always found it kind of stupid, that a being in the dark umbra or astralplane could just walk into a caern and go unnoticed, since werewolf only deals with the middle umbra and all gifts and rites are worded that way I allowed for garou in specially warded areas to see a outline of beings currently in dark or Astral umbra in the middle umbra, too. This allowed them to attack these shadow like representations and eventually drive them of, ending psycho projection and powers like that.

                        I would rule that it is part of the nature of the Garou to take damage from their Rage when they enter the High Umbra, not the nature of the Rage of the other Fera (who are not the Warriors of Gaia). For example the Mokole, who are designed to enter the High Umbra whenever they wish, should not be penalized. Now, as a side note, you can also take the Balor Roads (from Changeling) to reach the High Umbra. While that means that you have to have a Changeling friend to enchant you, take you into the Near Dreaming, and send you on your way, it may actually be less dangerous than any other methods suggests.
