    I am adding my 2 cents with the caveat that I have no skin in this argument. Still, if folks are seeking alternative Harano mechanics, I'm incredibly fond of the Harano mechanics in MET Werewolf: the Apocalypse. With the global destruction of many caerns, it's much easier for a Garou to experience Harano, which is no longer an affliction that strikes elders and some lupus, but is pervasive, comes in degrees of intensity, and affects all Garou on a spiritual level. The way Harano is addressed has also become much more widely understood by the Garou, and it's strengthening the bonds the Garou have by participating in moot rites and doing things to affirm their faith in Gaia that abate the effects of Harano. I love it because it allows the Garou agency to be more involved in their Septs and do things they should do anyway to address the affliction, i.e., focusing less inward and more on their community and pack. I'm not sold on Hauglosk (I think Seethe is a much cooler mechanic), but for all their flaws, I have to hand it to BNS for how they handled Harano, and I wish Renegade had done the same. Harano comes in stages with different specific effects (it can make one careless, suppress their rage, incapacitate, et al.)
