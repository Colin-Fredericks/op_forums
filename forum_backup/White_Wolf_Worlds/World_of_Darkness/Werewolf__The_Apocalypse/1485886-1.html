                So the Gnawer Bashs have brainstormed

                A thing that came up here is that a randomly activated Scent of the True Form (with On Patrol thrown in for good measure) is only among the third most likely things that could make a Nuwisha imposter stumble.

                You'd have to also have a plan for Truth of Gaia. And yes, Gnawer players are randomly "truthed" a lot. A LOT.
                I can't remember if it ever happened to me during introduction while stating my Tribe (or Rank or Name or Auspice or Breed), but from what I hear of other players this is something that happens frequently when entering someone else's territory and anyone on guard duty has access to the Gift (which is a Cliath Gift so good luck).

                Next there is the thing that Gnawers most likely have the most Cliath Gifts from any Tribe in the game (I can't be bothered to fact check this so I'm not adamant about them being number one, but they have a lot of Tribe-only Gifts on Cliath). There's something specialized to make your life easier for any kind of personality. Not knowing a single one of those and 'only having Resist Toxin' is raising suspicion the higher the Rank you try to pose as.

                Then there is the thing that quite a number of Gnawer Gifts, especially the information trading ones, deliberately target Gnawers. So not being able to understand the Barking Chain or orders masked by Rant and Rave would likely come up as a problem sooner than a randomly activated Scent of the True Form, too. Also you wouldn't be able to be targeted by Friend in Need, but if THAT'S what is blowing your disguise, you're having different problems anyway

                And the most likely thing an imposter would stumble over:
                Never having heard of the Gnawer currency system of shit, junk, stuff and loot and the exchange rates from one category to another and how the currency of information is handled therein.

                Further honorable mentions of things that maybe are not suspicious per se, but add up:
                Never having heard of the Ban of Man.
                Not knowing a lot about human homeless culture, drugs, cops, how begging works, the legal system including the inner workings of a jail, violent subcultures and punk music.
                Not knowing any of the low-level survivability Rites of the Tribe like Shopping Cart or Cardboard Palace.
                Never having heard of the different Camps and weird Gnawer Totems.
                Having the resources Background at all (or a job or a home or too much money in your pocket) and being targeted by the Gift Smell of Success (because one of the Gnawers WILL check the 'new guy' out).
                Not having any untraditional/unhonorable things dedicated to yourself that just make sense to have.

                                            There are three problems in general with your post, and keep in mind that they have more to do with what it seemed to imply than what you personally intended, I get we may have had a miscommunication here anyway.

                                            First, not every Gnawer is a perfect example of a Gnawer, much less knows everything about the tribe. You're a clueless cub before you are deep in the ways of the tribe and personal upbringing and area have a lot to say on what you're really capable of knowing or doing at first. This is specially relevant for the Gnawer currency and the Ban of Man, unless the impostor is trying to pass as a seasoned tribe member he would be ridiculed for the ignorance before being strongly suspect of deception, and then would just learn about them in the same way as the Gnawers.

                                            Second, trying to infiltrate any specific tribe as one of them is tricky and doesn't seems to be the goal. The idea is to infiltrate a more cosmopolitan Caern by pretending to be of a Tribe that isn't particularly common there. And then no one would be knowledgeable about the specific ways of the tribe. Trying to fool the tribe itself isn't a smart prospect for a Rank 1 or 2 Nuwisha anyway.

                                            And third, you're assuming the impostor won't know nothing about the ways of the target, and that's ignoring the whole shtick of the Nuwisha. They're more likely to know about all those things than anyone other than a Gnawer, period, and an infiltrator would probably study about them with some other Nuwisha or their repositories of lore stolen from other Changers. Seriously, they're so good with it that some Breed and Tribe Books are written in universe by the Nuwisha (usually Old Man Manyskins).

                                            Nuwisha have several advantages to disguise as Garou, the first being to treat Ragabash Gifts as Breed Gifts, period (a specific trick from Coyote that makes for a blanket declaration that they all count as Garou Ragabash for learning Gifts), their forms other than Manabozho can easily pass as the corresponding forms of the Garou (the Manabozho would stand out more, but not be impossible to explain), and the sheer rarity of the Nuwisha makes for the suspicion that they're what they really are very unlikely.

                                            If the proper Gifts are taken, all this talk is moot. Up to level 2 the Nuwisha already have access to the means to imitate any Changer on a broad enough spectrum of things as to just need a few lies to justify the rest.

                                                            I don't think a lot of the bone gnawer stuff is that big of an issue, since there is no reason you couldn't have a zero resource Nuwisha who has lived on the street at some point. In fact one of their totems, Raven, has the ban that they can't carry any wealth on their person. The other points do bring up some issues.

                                                            The gift thing is an issue longer term, though nuwisha do get access to one bone gnawer gift(it actually seems like they get access to one gift from almost all of the tribes). Which is scent of sweet honey. It has one benefit, in that you could use it in front of everyone at the time of your choosing and make it known you have a gnawer gift. I believe it is a tribe only gift, so pulling that out early on should keep any doubts away. For rituals, you could probably get the garou themselves to teach them to you.

                                                            Truth of gaia is an issue of course. Though I think there are ways around that with clever word play. You could say something like, "I am Laughing Moon, trickster of Luna, friend of rat, born as human." That could be your actual rite name(none of the garou are going to know a low level nuwisha rite name). You are a trickster of luna, and you could have a pet rat, named rat. Everything you say would be true and truth of gaia would confirm it. At that point you would be pretty much in right?
