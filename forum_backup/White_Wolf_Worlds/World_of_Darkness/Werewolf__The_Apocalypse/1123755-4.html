                                                            Purely by HOUSE RULES, here is how I run Pure Breed, i.e. "The Racist Pure Breed."

                                                            Pure Breed is a purely social construct of the Garou Nation that has no effect outside of interactions with members, with Black Spiral Dancers, Ronin, and certain urbanized Garou (at the ST's discretion) being immune. It reflects the cultural baggage the Garou have with history and lineage as well as some genuinely ethnocentric ideas of how a Garou "should" look in both their Homid as well as Lupus and Crinos forms. It has only the barest of mystical components and does not confer any respect with spirits or Fera. Garou who possess Pure Breed scores can add each dice to Leadership, Persuasion, Intimidation, and Subterfuge rolls (with the assumption Pure Bred werewolves are inherently more trustworthy). Unlike renown, a Garou with Pure Breed never has to worry about losing said status but will be judged to a somewhat harsher standard with offenses leading to stiffer penalties with less "promising" candidates.
