        Pure Breed is probably an uncommon trait outside of the Silver Fangs. Bone Gnawers and Glass Walkers, which are probably the two most populous tribes outside of the Black Spiral Dancers because of their adaptation to human society, are forbidden from possessing Pure Breed among their Kinfolk. Yes, the Silver Fang Kinfolk all have a minimum of Pure Breed 1 and the remaining Tribes probably have a sprinkling of Pure Breed, but I highly doubt that it is ubiquitous.

        Let us run your numbers. A Kinfolk population of 0.28% would mean that there are nearly twenty million Garou Kinfolk in existence, meaning that there are more Garou Kinfolk than there are Jews or Rom, two rather famous ethnic groups. If you assume an average 5% Garou birth probability and three children per couple, that would result in nearly one million five hundred thousand Garou in existence from Kinfolk-Kinfolk couples. Homid Garou would also be a hundredfold more common than Lupus Garou because there are not enough wolves that could be Kinfolk to keep up.

        A Kinfolk population of 0.8% is slightly better, but the end result is a Kinfolk population of nearly six million and a Garou population of over four hundred twenty thousand. The canon proportion of the population is 1:500,000, which would mean that the global Garou population is around fourteen thousand, one thirtieth of your lower number and one hundredth of your higher number. Now, if you are just wanting a world with more Garou, that is fine, I like the Garou and have no particular problem increasing their population up to 1:100,000, though I think that even that population would start changing the nature of the world.

                        I always ruled that Spirit Chasing allowed a sorcerer to acquire one non-human feature per level, as long as the non-human feature was in accordance with their chosen animal type (Bear included every type of ursine, Cat included every type of feline, Wolf included every type of canine, etc). They could maintain the feature as long as they wanted. I allowed them to use it without rituals, because of the changes to Numina that occurred after Kinfolk was published, though I allow rituals to allow it to be used on other people.

                        For example, a Bear shaman could learn to hibernate for one month per success on the Spirit Chasing roll when they learned the third level of Spirit Chasing. While hibernating, the Bear shaman is effectively within a form of biological suspended animation, allowing them to avoid aging, dehydration, starvation, etc., and allowing them to stop the progression of a disease or a poison. Without a ritual, they could only make themselves hibernate. With a ritual, they could put another person into hibernation.

                                    Latin is not a mystical language, it is just a language that is used by the Catholic Church. If you went to the occult bookstores, you would find that the majority of the spells (which are worthless for the most part) are in English. Languages created for magical practices, such as Enochian, are artificial linguistic creations (like Tolkien's fictional languages or Star Trek's Klingon), which are preferred for magical rituals because their words lack multiple meanings, which minimizes unintentional effects for the magical spells. In my youth, I knew hundreds of devote practitioners of magic, from chaos mages to hereditary witches to voodoo priestesses, and they have tended to prefer using a non-native language for their magic to prevent cross contamination. Heck, I once knew a dinosaur druid, as in a druid who invoked dinosaur spirits, who chanted his spells in Klingon because he thought that a non-human language would appeal to the dinosaur spirits more than human languages.
