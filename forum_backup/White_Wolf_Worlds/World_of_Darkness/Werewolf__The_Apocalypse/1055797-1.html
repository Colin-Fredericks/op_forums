            Quite a few of these changes I can get behind. A method to have a Garou first change somewhere other than puberty opens quite a few doors. I am hoping it's not just some random thing though, because this would mean sickies during coitus create Metis. Jobs sounds awkward. Like a soldier having to make sure he finishes a battle quickly so he can be home in time to shower, change, and flip burgers.

            Fewer Caerns is something I've always believed in, and 'protectorates' like were seen in Rage Across New York got a little more screen time from me. Previous authors and developers have talked about there being as few as 3 per continent, in order to keep them special and valuable. However, that ran into the issue of 'everybody wants one in their home town'. While I would never have gone that low myself, gateways into the spirit world should be relatively rare and valuable, and not within easy walking distance of each other. (And this is before taking into account the other game lines that have similar places.)

            ​Did Stargazers come back from the Beast Courts? Or did they never leave in the LARP setting? Basically it boiled down to 'the Tribal Elders got fed up, and stopped coming to the meetings' (which had exactly as much impact on the PCs and Story as the players wanted them to have.) If anything, I would have used their departure as the springboard for the split rather than retcon it out.

                                Stargazers came back with their Beast Court allies all victorious. Which to me, and this is purely just my personal bias, really does not feel justified. One of my favourite things about Stargazers revised tb, was that for all their talk of being more civilized and in-tune with themselves, they were still Garou Nation garou. They were trying to join a culture that had had unity for centuries and did not behave like the Nation, and it was clearly hard. The Fera section is full of basically 'can't trust these guys' mentality, the Beast Courts don't really trust them and they generally feel ill-fit among them.

                                Basically, it addressed very well the issues of trying to force yourself into another culture and what it can result in. Gazers ended up basically as outsiders because they weren't the civilized tribe they thought they were.

                                To have them come back with allies all victorious, and their numbers swelled by Mongolian wolves, it undercuts the message of the revised book.

                                Anyway, to the Bitten, it seems to function very similarly to the Birth Plague of the Ratkin. It needs a bite and then ties to the garou by blood. Most die from it, and a child being Bitten will only change once as an adult.

                                        Originally posted by Ana Mizuki View Post
                                        Anyway, to the Bitten, it seems to function very similarly to the Birth Plague of the Ratkin. It needs a bite and then ties to the garou by blood. Most die from it, and a child being Bitten will only change once as an adult.
                                        Okay. So I've slept on the concept and the Bitten thing is weird but passes my "Can I make an interesting character from this?" test.

                                        At first I was like "Wolves aren't plague carriers like rats!" but then I remember Rabies. As long as the Garou has some control over if their bite passes on the curse. Lovebites from your Garou mate shouldn't curse you to either death or divorce. If you get bit by a Garou in Frenzy, you are cursed. I have this image of a Garou foaming from the mouth in preparation of the bite curse. Or maybe the curse is only passed by Crinos bites, which makes the situation more tragic for Metis pups. (This is the one reason I wouldn't use the concept in my game. Things are already tragic enough for Metis pups.)

                                        The character concept I got from this was the youngest child of a Breed 5 Garou. All of her children ended up Kinfolk, so you were her last hope of having a Garou child to pass on her legacy to. Yet you were an easygoing child. Not prone to anger. You had none of the stereotypical hallmarks of a child that will go though their first change. One night, while you slept a monster visited you. Your mother sank her teeth into your side. After that you remember nothing but fevered dreams of battle and wolves. You father and stepmother never forgave your mother for what she did, but as you survived and became Garou, she felt she did what was right.
