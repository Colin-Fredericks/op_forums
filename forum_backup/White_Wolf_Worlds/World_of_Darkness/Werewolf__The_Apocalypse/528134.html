                                              If you're to follow the oWoD cannon, here are some things to keep in mind when playing a chronicle in the Amazon:

                                              1-) The leader of the Garou in the Amazon is Golgol Fangs-First, a rank 6 Ahroun from the Get of Fenris. His right hand is Carlos Walks-the-stars, a powerful Uktena theurge who managed to negotiate a Caern from the locals.
                                              2-) The natives of the Amazon do not like Garou or Europeans in general. They believe both of them to be wyrm spawn, evil monsters who crossed the seas to rape and pillage the apex of native american civilization(Inca,Aztec,Marajó in the Amazon itself). They will at best distrust any Garou they meet.
                                              3-) The Amazon is *humongous*. Just the main rainforest is a huge green carpet of tall green trees that extends for a territory about the size of Europe. There are more unique species of mammals, birds, reptiles, insects, and fish there than anywhere else in the world. There are many ancient, mysterious things lurking there also: Mokolé caerns that are millions of years old, semi-nomad Balam tribes that will kill anyone who trespasses into their territory, willingly or not. Huge snakes living in the Amazon river and its tributaries that are said to be over 50 feet long and capable of swallowing a garou whole in Crinos form. Many more other ancient, weird things
                                              4-) Travel in the Amazon is done mostly by boat. There are very few roads and they are in terrible shape. The Amazon river is the 2nd longest in the world and the most volumus. It also has 6 huge tributaries, and many dozens of smaller ones. There is more fresh water in the Amazon than anywhere else in the world. In fact, nearly 1/4 of the world's fresh supply lie in the Amazon Basin. However, travel is dangerous, as Pentex patrol boats roam the area.
                                              5-) The "capital of the amazon" is the town of Manaus, in northern Brazil. Most "expeditions" should probably start from there. There is a small kindred population in Manaus, mostly independent Lasombra and Brujah coteries. Obviously, they never leave the city.

                                                  More Weaver than Wyrm probably, but also pretty resonant:


                                                  There's some traffic by plane - a fraction of it illegal - but the usual policy of brazilian air force is "gun down anything that doesn't answer a radio call, no 2nd chances."

                                                  Speaking of Marajo, there's much evidence that civilization there disappeared before the arrival of europeans explorers. The isle itself, in the mouth of the Amazon river, is about the size of Switzerland but with barely 1/40 of the population a surprisingly large number of domesticated water buffalo and stands just across from the entry port and other major capital of the rainforest, Belem do Pará.

                                                  About the water buffalos in Marajo, no one is completely certain of how they ended up there, though a popular story involves a ship from southeast asia that sank in the river's mouth in the 19th century. What is a fact is that they became so endemic to the isle the local police much prefers them as transportation around the place than cars or boats.
