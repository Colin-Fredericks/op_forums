    For me, Pentex creates a bit of a Catch 22.

    On the one hand, the "We're all Wyrm cultists, we dump pollution in rivers, cut down trees, and poison people's food because we love EEVVVILLL. We have whole departments and subsidiaries that do nothing but produce mutants and literally demonically possessed people!" that is the (admittedly exaggerated) official version of Pentex prevents suspension of disbelief for me.

    On the other hand, removing these aspects, such that pretty much any successful corporation at a scale larger than a farmer's roadside stand just serves the Wyrm by accident, removes the need for Pentex entirely (and also causes suspension of disbelief issues).

    My somewhat limited solution is to tone down the first version, rather than shut if off completely. I almost never use canon characters, and the board of directors is usually a nebulous group. Most of them are normal businessmen, others are very cautious and subtle Wyrm cultists. Likewise, Wyrm cultists are lightly interspersed throughout the corporate hierarchy. Pentex has no major divisions which intentionally serve as a way to produce Formori or other horrors. If a Wyrm cultists sees an opportunity to use Pentex to spread Wyrmish influence in a subtle and, hopefully, untraceable way, he will take it. Only the most in-the-know Fera know that Pentex is riddled with Wyrm servants.

                    That's a good picture. And often these corporations also own non-food products as well. For example, Nestle is one of the primary shareholders of L'Oreal, the largest cosmetic company in the world. When you buy L'Oreal hair products, you're giving your money to Nestle. Or how for many years, Phillip Morris (the cigarette company that owns Marlboro) also owned Kraft Foods as well as Miller (the beer company) and started divesting itself, in part, when it came under investigation by for racketeering (which it was eventually found guilty of).

                    When you actually start looking into these real life, massive multi-national corporations, something like Pentex actually begins to seem kind of plausible.

                                                        It's probably also worth considering that in Werewolf that many mundane actions have a spiritual/supernatural effect and that the dark aspects of our own world are write LARGE in the WoD. The huge massive bulk of Pentex is just as people have described or suggested it should be. Corporations and businesses that are built solely around profit margins and mundane business activity, but the day hours are longer, the holiday allowances fewer, sickness tolerances probably less with disciplinary common for what might be felt to be innocuous things, but there's some carrot with the stick so the bonus incentives will be achievable but massively competitive, there will be loyalty aspects to the company (report your workmates for even the most minor breach of protocol for bennies). This all feeds the Wyrm, but everyone remains very ignorant of anything to do with that, everyone except the Board, Division Heads and other Upper (and some Middle) management positions.

                                                        Those at the top grow rich and wealthy on the workings of the Companies, Divisions and other aspects of the Pentex MegaCorp. That's their main driving goal. Those in the Know also gain supernatural 'insight' and 'benefits' to go hand in hand with their material gains and wealth and also have their own Wyrm fueled agenda for what ever reason you care to ascribe to it. They have the First Teams and Fomori because some of the Private Divisions of Pentex don't exist to make money, but exist to drive the Wyrm based goals, sometimes they get to make money in the process, but those goals mean tangling with Garou and the people in charge know this. So they turn some of that vast mundane wealth and the spiritual supernatural gains into a method to deal with Gaia's warriors. But Pentex's big weapon is one of spiritual blight of humanity and there are tons of ways to do this without First Teams, Fectori and slavering monsters. Charitable concerns can boast a corporate sponsor here and there, medical facilities almost always has business involved somewhere (totally in the USA, increasingly so in the UK), education centers also are a target for big business grants or scholarships and all of these allow for the dark roots of Pentex to infect and spread. This all makes money for the companies that form under the Pentex umbrella and the world grows just that little bit darker and rots just a little more all the way.

                                                            I actually had one of the PCs meet with Harold Zettler last night (it was more of a cutscene because my WOD is every one of my campaigns so it was the Vampire PCs of the vampire game while we're playing the Werewolf sections of "Under a Blood Red Moon"). It got me a chance to have him explain the goal and purpose of Pentex in my campaign. Specifically, Harold's goal was to destroy all supernaturals but vampires and enrich himself as well science as a whole. The service of the "Wyrm" was to him something which superstitious Garou (the BSD) believed in when it was really just an organization assembled around study of supernatural phenomenon as well as putting it to use. Yes, there were the use of spirits and placation of them but that was just a tool.

                                                            Eliminating Lupines was a primary importance to the company but Garou viewed everything through their lens of how society worked.

                                                            Zettler also made it clear it was an alliance of numerous rich supernaturals with their own agendas but his was the one (presently) dominating.

                                                            I had a lot of fun with the meeting because the BSD in my UTBRM game were summoning Eater of Souls in New York City (not Chicago) while Pentex protected them as well as preventing the government from sending troops in to quell the chaos in the city. It made Pentex seem threatening as well as competent but also grossly ignorant suckers too.
