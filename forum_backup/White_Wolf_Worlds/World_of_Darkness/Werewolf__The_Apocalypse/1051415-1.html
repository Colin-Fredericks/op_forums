                        Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
                        To add to the "ecoterrorism" point not being exclusively a bad thing: One of the things the game has highlighted relatively well over the years is that the environment is danger and stopping it isn't simple. "Captain Planet" play can be both cathartic for those of us frustrated with it and looking for some escapist fantasy, as well as a spring board for the much more complicated issue that it isn't actually working. The Garou can't just kill pollution. Curbstomping some Pentex subsidiary's operation just means you stopped the worst of the worst polluters, and dozens of minor operations done by normal hard working people that aren't tools of a Wyrm cult are still doing what they do. One of the books even once had a side bar pointing out that they had tried to make a Pentex based oil company more hyperbolic but they couldn't come up with a way to do so because in reality it was already that stupidly bad.

                        It's a pretty deep thing to consider. That a gaming book about a hyperbolic evil corporation that's purposefully doing all the horrible shit that most major corporations do out of willful ignorance in the name of profit had to throw up its hands and say, "we couldn't come up with anything worse than this, what they're doing here is actually what real companies do."
                        Honestly, at times the 'lol captain planet' mentality can seem rather callous. After all, ecoterrorism being bad or not, enviroment and its state is a legitimate worry and we should be more aware of it. Dismissing garou because of that can easily be read as "Being considerate about the enviroment is so 80s and lame", which..yeah.

                        In general though, I'd say WtA's multiculturism can be a benefit if done right. Wendigo, Uktena, Stargazers and even Children of Gaia can add to a game if the players do their research.

                                        ​
                                        Originally posted by Schwann145 View Post
                                        As it relates to the overall "World of Darkness" anyway.
                                        ​•The overall villain of the setting is the slow (or not so slow?) and subtle (or not so subtle?) corruption of the natural world, but you can't really do anything about it because the tribes just can't quit in-fighting.
                                        I would say this and is probably not the intended way but after the whole "the complex technocracy was an accident" i decided that author intent is not the way to go with any WoD/Chrod games.

                                        The superficial bad guy of Apocalypse is the wyrm/bsd but it isnt the complex/interesting bad guy of the setting. That is the Garou Nation itself.

                                        My analogy for werewolf is that its like the Walking dead with Negan. The series says to you that the bad guy are the zombies but the zombies arent really the compelling enemy or the main focus, they are a narrative tool. They are this unstoppable force that comes and forces the protagonist to cooperate and deal with morally questionable people in order to survive.

                                        Just like the garou the character´s goal is objectively good, survive for the human protagonist and save the world for the Garou. And yet they find themselves having to talk, negotiate and even compromise with people of questionable moral who, like them, have a objectively good goal (survive) but their methods are incredibly shady to say the least.

                                        So the Wyrm is the narrative excuse that force them to talk to the really compelling enemy Negan (the asshole tribes).

                                        ​
                                        Originally posted by Schwann145 View Post
                                        ​•There doesn't seem to be any "personal horror" in this game, as you have complete control of your character except for a frenzy, and unlike in VtM, frenzying isn't necessarily a bad thing?
                                        Frenzy aside i feel the true horror element of werewolf is compromise, how much you gonna let yourself go into this society to achieve what you want? Are you gonna defend equality for kinfolk and garous even when the system also exist in part to protect the kinfolks? Are you gonna take the life of baby metis black spiral dancer because she knows the location of the Sept? No? You gonna risk the lives of everyone for your conscience sake? Knowing that leaving her live is gonna add another one to the enemy numbers?

                                        You gonna live your life outside of the politics and shady deals? What if the kinfolk you love is about to married to another (and she/he doesn't want to) and you could win her/him back dueling the mate-to-be? Or negotiating your first son to the tribe of the kinfolk? Or worse negotiating your pack mate to another kinfolk

                                        Or what if a kinfolk comes from another sept hiding from her/his family? Would you fight them? Putting both septs at risk and knowing both could die just to prove a point? What happens if both die and there is no garou to fight the influence of pentex/the nearby hive?

                                        What if you could unite 3 warring Septs for the first time in generations if just that one mad elder got out of the way? The things 3 septs in concert could accomplish and all of that progress stopped by one asshole fianna.

                                        Are you gonna not fight and be a shame to your family? Most people dont care enough what their parent/grandfather think of them but here there is also the other way around, do you wanna be the reasons your son and grandsons and beyond have rough lives? Wanna live the rest of your life knowing your son/daughter thinks less of you because of the choices you make? Is the higher moral ground worth it?

                                        That for me is the horror of werewolf the decision you make and how seductive the garou society is for a garou. "Be a good crusader, kill the wyrm and anything standing in your way and you will get honor, power and be a hero and be right....just dont think too much. Live fast and die young in the name of our war without changing a damn thing of this vicious circle and then an eternity in valhalla awaits you" thats the speech of the garou nation at large. And the horror comes on how many garou buy it.

                                        Like a PC in a game said "The problem with Garou society is that the garou are scare of living. Terrified of it. Thats why they throw their lives into glorious battles and give long winded song of dying with honor because that the easy way out. We fight, and we are damn good at it, and then in a moment we die and thats it, the problem that plague us are for the next generation to deal with. We did a "good job" and we punch out our ticket out and know that our ancestors and forebearers are proud of us and we can call it a good life.

                                        Because the alternative is living and living means we can fuck up. Having a kid and then dying in battle means we never have to find out if we could be good parents, means we will never face the fact that we suck at parenting or at our relationship. Means that we never have to face the fact that maybe we are fighting this war wrong and receive scorn from out septmates, tribemates or even packmates. It means we never have to see if we got what it takes to build instead of destroy. To look at our ancestors in the face and tell them "NO, this is how we do things now" and live with the consequences of that."
