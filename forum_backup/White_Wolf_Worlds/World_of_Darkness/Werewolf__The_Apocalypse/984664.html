    Thread of Fenris

    10-09-2016, 08:20 PM
    Because I asked anyone wanting to talk about it to take it to a new thread...

    Originally posted by Uniform Two Six
    'm siding with Prince of the Night on this one. That's how the Get have always been written.


    There was a sidebar in the Revised tribebook about how a lot of lupus in the tribe saw it as being incredibly dumb and a taint of human society, and it was rather heavily downplayed as a wide standpoint, given that one of the focus characters of the book was a woman. The Swords of Heimdall were also written as being painfully sexist in addition to racists that looked down on lupus, so I think that the intent was to kill this stereotype as dead as possible without massive retcons.

    I, however, do not think that massive retcons are bad, and would love it if the Fenrir were presented as the enemy of all good Norse in their past, like the Black Furies being the enemies of Greek culture.

    It doesn't have to make logical sense -- it just has to make sense to a bunch of testosterone-laden-jock-douches with superpowers (like pretty much every male Get). You get rid of it (presumably because you want the Garou to be appear so much more noble)
    Noble? Not unless you think that Norse neopagans need to be chased down and gutted because they're giving homage to old One-Eye. Which is something that the Fenrir probably should be doing, seeing as how he's a great enemy of the past. Sure, it endangers the veil, but you just don't tolerate jackwagons acting as unknowing patsies to an old nemesis going into your territory and invoking his name. In the past, they might have even been very aggressive in killing the original pagans around their territory if they didn't convert - not palling around with Christians, because they would only try to convert them, but actively taking advantage of the weakening position in society to kill them and leave them as a warning to One-Eye. They still kept a lot of festivities for their new syncretism, though, so the Get might not be happy about that.

    This is what we should have. Monstrous fulfillment of an ancient grudge, wrought in blood. Not werewolf Hans and Franz: the tribe, with vaguely-themed camps named for Aesir gods in defiance of the fact that they and Fenris hate each other.

    you pretty drastically change what the Get of Fenris tribe is thematically.
    Revised already started the ball rolling. Now there's just the matter of more in-depth research and better mindedness in writing to continue onward. Note that the White Howlers were originally presented as being a tribe of Leeroy Jenkins, but the tribebook changed this. The Silver Fangs, in 2nd edition, had the RUSSIARUSSIARUSSIA button slammed constantly, but even in a book where the chapter headers were in unRomanized Russian, they still found the time to make the tribe an old, continent-spanning institution that had a long history in North Africa, the Middle-East, and most of Asia.
