                                                    Honestly, these sorts of discussions also well... seem to play very loose with the word "fascist," and edge up on meaning more, "asshole with autocratic leanings," rather than the specifics that make of fascist ideology (also Nazism is a form of fascism, but not all fascism is Nazism).

                                                    There's no reason the Get would like fascism, at all. What they like is a social construct that's easily corrupted by fascists... which would make them being intolerant towards fascists even more.

                                                    For the Get of Fenris might makes right. This means that Get bow to the power of others. The Fenrir might not like it, but if a Bone Gnawer takes on a Fenrir in an honorable duel and wins? The Gnawer was stronger, and is right, and they'll do what the Gnawer says.

                                                    For a fascist, might makes right is a practice of circular logic. Fascists define themselves as the strong, thus they're always right, and because they're always in the right, there is no valid way to demonstrate more power than them; losing is only a sign of dishonorable actions by their enemies not a failing in the strength of the fascists. That's what outsider/othered scapegoats are important to fascists. They need to convince their populace that anything imperfect about their great and powerful nation is the fault of underhanded sabotage by those evil baddies.

                                                    For me, the problem with the Swords as a camp, is that it's a really bad way to tackle fascist radicalization in the Fenrir to just have a bunch of Nazi werewolves. The Black Eagle Fellowship is a much better vehicle... as the way fascistic radicialization tends to worm into private paramilitary groups would seem much more timely and believable than openly Nazi werewolves in 2020.
