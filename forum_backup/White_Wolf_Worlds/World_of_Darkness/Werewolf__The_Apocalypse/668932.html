    Role Playing the fall of Pentex

    09-17-2015, 07:04 PM
    In the Settite Clanbook, there is a Rite to destroy entire organizations by ritually killing the head of that organization in the same manner that Set killed Osiris. Incidentally it only works on males. Say that the CEO of Pentex is caught, and a jillion successes are achieved on the Rite. The next day the corporate stock goes down to pennies and every board member and manager in Pentex has been the exposed as unintentional guest stars on "To Catch a Predator.". Or something to that affect. What would the worldwide demise of Pentex look like? After its assets get auctioned off and its employees laid off, and the politicians have to whore themselves out to new donors, what changes and what is the same?

                                              1) The overarching plans break down. Anything that has corporations cooperate for the end game (aka the Apocalypse) rather than act for their own benefit doesn't work anymore because there's noone left to force them to do so.

While there are some servants of the Wyrm that can get their hands on money, many of the slime-and-tentacle faction need someone to handle their finances and get a little money together because most mundane banks think "buy up some square kilometres in the middle of nowhere because there's a site sacred to Mother Nature there" is not a solid business model to grant a loan for.
                                              3) Industrial Fomori production winds way down. Project Iliad is a pure money-sink that only pays for itself as long as you have other operations that can benefit from Fomori forces.                                                   3) Industrial Fomori production winds way down. Project Iliad is a pure money-sink that only pays for itself as long as you have other operations that can benefit from Fomori forces.                                                   You can't really count on that, either, since someone, somewhere, will try it at least once. This is the WoD, and most people don't actually know how fomori work; they're even labeled "nutritional supplement users" on any records that people might see. The only way it stops all of the sudden is if you want to incorporate an influential Technocracy into your game, and they start testing the alleged nutritional supplements and subjects up close, in a proximity which SPD did not allow. They'd freak out pretty quickly if they found out that all of this was just a front for putting "extradimensional entities" into people; the mad dash to put a stop to it and potential for shootouts and crossfires between groups would be sublime.

                                                  Who knows? Maybe one of Pentex' excavation teams accidentally dug up the burial site of a Setite Methusaleh who got reduced to ashes in the process. Maybe the Serpents got a good look at some of Pentex' end game scenarios and decided it's not the kind of Apocalypse they'd like happen. It's not something that would automatically occur, but it's hardly impossible.
                                                  Additionally, there are several breeds of Changers that make a habit of stealing wyrmish lore and at least one of those has a longstanding enmity against the Setites anyway.
                                                  Speaking of stealing lore, I picked up a copy of DB: Bone Flowers recently, and it had an odd mechanic in it wherein Cainite necromancy rituals could be adapted for use as rites by Hungry Dead (short story - add one to the level of the V:tM ritual). Given that the mechanics of Gui Ren rites are set up similarly to Garou and Fera rites, it would probably be possible to crib the ritual. Of course, the adapted Cainite rituals are also reviled by the elders as heretical and possibly/likely corrupting, so there's that to deal with, but the Eaters of the Dead are already in for a pound or more just by practicing their signature rite.
