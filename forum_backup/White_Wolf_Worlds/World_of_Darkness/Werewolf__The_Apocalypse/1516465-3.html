    Originally posted by Ana Mizuki View Post

    Human become half beast and kill things is the reason why WtA gets side-eyed. Because that is not what the game is about. For one, garou are born not made and breeding has a huge role in the game. Cue the furry comparisons.
    again, I wonder where you get the idea that wta is side eyed. Or that the game isn't about becoming a beast and killing things.

    If anything, the lore of wta is what attract people. Just playing a random joe becoming a beast to kill a few people is not something that attracts audience on the middle to long run. Same for vtm. If it was just "drink blood, hide from the sun", the setting would have never been that popular.

    One just has to look to the difference in popularity between masquerade and requiem to see that masquerade relies on it's lore massively.

    Requiem 1st ed showed that you can't just rely on the gimmicks of a supernatural creature to be a great game. And werewolf the forsaken showed the same in comparison to apocalypse. The dense lore of these games is a core component of their succes.


    Plus, I think you're exagerating the amount of lore needed to understand wta.

    "You are werewolves, descendant of a warrior race bathed in an animistic world where you have to protect the soul of the world from the wyrm, the incarnation of everything wrong and corrupt"

    How is that more dense than an explaination for vtm?

    "You are vampires, cursed to drink the blood of mortals and avoid the sun, hiding from humans in under the rule of the masquerade, and facing treats from the endless conspirations of the elders of your race"
    ​​​​​

    I mean, how hard is that? I introduced more than a dozen players to the game in about as much words.

    We're talking about gaming audience, both ttrpg and video games. A paragraph of explaination is not hard.

    Plus you can play wta without learning anything about the garou culture. Play a group of exiled/young garou and you can never reference the garou nation, the impergium and so on without issue.

    "
    Honestly, I'm not saying WtA is impossible to get into (sitting here in my Red Talon shirt next to a whole bookshelf of WtA book XD) Just that IF you want a werewolf experience akin to Howling or Blood and Chocolate, WtA is not that game.​"

    Excuse me, but that's really not a good argument. The same can be said about vtm, which by it's very lore forbid you from having the experience of, well, any vampire story, game or novel where you can actually walk under the sun for a start.
