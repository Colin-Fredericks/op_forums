                  Depends on how powerful the Vamp is. If the Vampire has mind-altering powers and/or rituals of Level 6+ (in case of some Thau powers, rituals and combo disciplines lower since they are specifically designed against Hardened defence), she burns through the defenses. Against true Elders (and some childes of Elders, Baba Yagas childe developed his first Level 6 power within ten years of becoming undead) with high developed disciplines, no.
