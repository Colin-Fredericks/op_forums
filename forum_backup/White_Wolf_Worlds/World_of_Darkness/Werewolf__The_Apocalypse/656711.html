          If Werewolves won, there would be a huge fight as to what happens next with some like the Glasswalkers wanting to keep things as they are, and some like Red Talons wanting to genocide humanity.

          There might be limited technological enclaves in certain places under the control of Glasswalkers, Shadowlords, Children of Gaia, etc,

          It would amount to a neo-Impergium, though probably not quite as bad as the original, which was beyond nightmarish. Still, I expect quite a few places would end up with results very similar to Cambodia's Khmer Rouge, who killed anyone that was educated, literate and use things like glasses.

              Ohh what a terrifying idea. The Garou win and possibly have the chance to rule?!

              Oh my

              well...if it was me I would say the win also came a heavy cost. A huge sacrifice on behalf of the Garou as a nation.

              I would go with the story of an awesome pack returning from an umbral quest with the rite used by the Croatan Tribe to banish the Eater of Souls from when it last tried to step into the realm.

              Then I'd have some one come up with a crazy plan to do it again. Three times. One for each head of the Wyrm.

              For the Beast of War I'd have a lot of challenges go down and tribal conflict to prove which tribe should have the honor and glory of doing so. Eventually, the Get of Fenris claim the place.

              With the Eater of Souls, there is much debating and consideration. Eventually the Children of Gaia with Pegasus take the responsibility.

              and finally with the Eater of Souls...the Silver Fangs. In recognition of the failures to stop the evil from eating away at everything (in their leadership in the nation, as a tribe, as rulers ordained by gaia) Take the place as sacrifice so gaia and the Garou (their subjects obviously) may go on. (Big grins on the shadow lord faces!)

              They do it. All three Tribes take to their original homelands and each and everyone of them gives everything they have.

              It works. The Triatic wyrm is weakened and pushed back to the hells they came from. But at a terrible cost.

              The Garou are now without their leaders, without their healers and without the martial prowess of the Get. (Plus the Fianna have to always sing of the Gets great sacrifice...ha!)

              What do they do now? Who is in charge? Who will fullfill the roles now a void left in the abscence of thesacrificed tribes?

              What of the wyld and the weaver? Who shall lead? Gaia!!.. what do you want of us!!! They howl, quest, and pray for the answers.

              That would be where I would start it with the players.The worId in ruins, but free (for now) of the wyrm. The Garou nation in chaos and remorse deep in uncertainty.

              Then well...I'd bring in a new changing breed(or old depending if you believe that one shadow lord). WereFalcons. Who soar the sky under Falcons banner, who's gifts of battle and healing reflect those who once sacrificed themselves under the garou banner.

              and behind them...the rest of the changing breeds.

              Gaia has answered.

              This time, they must do it...together. there will not be another chance.

              add the world still needs cleansing. That pockets of blights and pollution still breed banes and spew corruption, that the Weaver is still taking and staticically enforcing its hold over new lands, that tbe Wyld is rattling at its cage and bursting fourth new things in the wake of the averted apocalypse.

              and that the shadow lords want the leadership over all now.
              that the red talons have the rite too and wonder if it can be used against the Weaver.
              That surviving black spirals no longer know what yo do.
              Pentex is free to do what it wants
              the wars and old battles still rage. Cutting off the heads of the hydra didnt magically make all them stop.
              oh no. There's still more to do.
              Plus, we can never forget...not this time.

              That would be me and my brain. The garou and fera all coming together to decide who will rule, who will follow, and who will rise.

                                  I just mean in the general long-term - that is, for people who were born and raised in this neo-Impergium, life would probably be better for them than it would have been during the original Impergium.

                                  In this scenario I suggested, you would still have humans living in villages and towns, you'd probably have metal working to a degree, you'd have farming and some level of book knowledge. If you're one of the truly lucky ones that end up living in a Glasswalker enclave, there might not even be anything too different from life before the Apocalypse and afterwards. Regardless, the average quality of life for humans would be rather higher than it was for the humans who were living during the original Imergium.

                                  Of course for the people living in the modern society, and find themselves being culled to create this neo-Impergium, things are going to be pretty terrible and being told, "Well you guys are better off now than you were during the original Impergium" would be a small consolation. Ultimately though it all depends on how the Apocalypse itself played out.

                                  A near victory by the Wyrm, in which 90% of the population dies, the survivors realize that they are being protected by werewolves, and then come to understand the dangers of technology and choose to join the werewolves in "dialing back the clock"... well, life would be a bit worse for the survivors, but it wouldn't necessarily be because of the werewolves and certainly the "human/werewolf" relationship would be a lot better than it was during the original Impergium.
