        Originally posted by Crytash View Post
        i would like to point out that environmentalism can be a deeply conservative almost völkisch thing as well. This would work well with your purity argument and would bring parts of the CoG closer to the Get of Fenris.
        I don't want to get into political debates other than to "open up the game" and allow other ideas to enter. I do agree that there is an environmental consciousness on the right as on the left. Many people do not seem to realize that Hitler and the Nazis actually implemented the first environmental laws in world history. Actually, the incipient environmental movements of Germany in the early twentieth century pretty much is the origins for the environmental movement of the 1960s and beyond. The Nazi version of it was just one facet or expression of it. (this is getting off topic, but I always felt it would be quite easy to explain why the Get of Fenris became involved in the Nazi movement since the Nazis actually have a lot of precepts that fit into Garou concepts).

        I don't think concepts of racial purity have much to do with pristine wilderness and uncorrupted innocence that Unicorn would espouse. While there might be some room for a kind of "blood and soil" nationalism int he Children, I think their stress on being an ecumenical movement among the Garou would really limit that memes growth among the children. Unlike some tribes, there is not a strong tie between the Tribe's kinfolk and a single ethnic or superethnic group. The backgrounds of tribe members are just too varied for this idea. It would be too divisive. Some kind of "back to nature" movement fits in with the tribe, but I don't think it'd be anything as extreme as the Nazi style. Some kind of general eugenics movement I could see the Children supporting, and this was in fact a big progressive cause until the Nazi version of it discredited it. Of course, it is beginning to make something of a comeback, but we're probably still a decade or two away before it becomes respectable.

        However, Tolkien would be a good example of a typical conservative's view of environmental stewardship. The old fashioned kind of English Tory landowner who enjoys the countryside and village life and not fond of the urban business world. That has nothing to do with racial purity, and could be the basis of a "conservative" wing of the Children of Gaia. However, I've always considered the Children to be too ideologically or idea oriented for them to be well represented by that. That's how I'd more see some of the Silver Fangs and the other tribes.

        If there are "conservative" Children, I'd imagine they'd be the remnants of some previous philosophy that found acceptance among the Children, but which has since lost relevance as they moved onto the next big thing. There might be some Children kinfolk still really into Catholic social teaching and the tradition of stewardship going back to St Francis of Assisi. I could see that being part of the tribe's history in Europe, but nowadays such adherents likely stick out like a sore thumb.
