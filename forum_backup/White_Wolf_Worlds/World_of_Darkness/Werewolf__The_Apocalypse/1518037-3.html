    Originally posted by Ana Mizuki View Post
    Aside from the MASSIVE Fenrir kerfluffle which does actually explain the reason they are not playable, I just can't help but think WoD5's flattening is doing the exact opposite of what WoD was made for.

    I mean, you are playing a monster and the game is about WHO you are and WHY you are a monster and most importantly; Are you actually the true monster here? That is why Sabbat and the Society of Leopold had a place as PCs, because there was no one answer to the question. It is even MORE muddled with WtA where each tribes has its own take on the litany, there are stolen moons and ronin etc.


    WoD5 denying anyone but the main focus of the spotlight is outright telling you who is in the right. It is like a scene in the Goblins webcomic, which was made to shine a light on how players treat goblins in DnD, casting detect evil spell and telling their allies killing foes is fine since they are evil. Complete flip of the point.
    The Get as antagonists don't really fit into this but with JA we know what his paradigm for reworking the WOD-splats was:
    • There are bad-guy factions (Nephandi, Sabbat, BSD etc.) which are bad.
    • Every faction that is upholding the status-quo is also bad (Camarilla, Garou-Nation, hunter-orgs etc.).
    • The factions that want to change the status-quo actually can do good but they can accidentally also do bad things (Anarchs, local Hunter-cells, young Garou etc.).
    The only moral grey area in this conception was whether the faction that would be fighting against the status-quo actually made things better. But that's about it...

                                        A strange thing with the development here is that Karim Muammar was trying to push the idea that the new main-antagonist is Get-Nazis - and you still have that in the second version. But to Justin Achilli the main-antagonist is Pentex instead. Not the Pentex of old, of course, but he talked about them being avatars of greet and endless consumption (there's also the time where he said that the write-up of an evil Formori-lobbyist was his favorite part of the antagonist-section). And this variant-cover they had for the Spanish version which is about Pentex hints that that's probably what this version of W5 will be focusing on. This is also in line with there being less of a focus on the Umbra. Of course, it's a bit weird that the Get-Nazis are still part of this version as well.

                                        But it seems to me like neither of them liked the idea of the "war against the Wyrm" very much. In both cases, it's about redirecting the focus on who the main-antagonist is. But the Wyrm is still part of the setting at the same time. And we've seen three Tribe-sections where "Gaia dead/dying, Garou-Nation sundered" didn't get a lot of attention - even though these should represent massive shifts for Garou-society.

                                        It's just... I don't know. Maybe all this will seem more coherent once we can read the book back to back. But with all the previews we've gotten, I could never shake the feeling that all these bits and pieces just don't seem to fit together very well.
