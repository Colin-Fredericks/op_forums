    Devilwhip Cosplay Prop – Requesting Feedback

    05-31-2016, 01:35 PM
    I’m working on a Devilwhip cosplay (not LARP) prop and looking for some feedback. A critique of something that only has one or two visual references is a little tricky, but maybe forumites will see something I’m not.
    The primary visual inspiration for this is the depiction of the weapon Zyhzhak is wielding on the cover of Book of the Wyrm Second Edition. All constructive and honest opinions are welcome.
    For starters:
    • Does it look like the tentacle of a demonic and lovecraftian personification of spiritual corruption?
    • If you saw someone in werewolf makeup holding this, would you think “black spiral dancer”? [I realize this depends on makeup, costume, and other props.]
    • Are there details that I could add to give it more a “bane tentacle” vibe?
    I'll post one image here and links to others below it. Thanks again for reading.

                              So, an idea for faking the millipede thing:

                              Millipedes have a series of segmented shells. That's pretty much not feasible for a project like this. But you can make the look stronger if you want to go this direction (this could be pretty tedious, but cosplay can do that):

                              1) The segments should be a bit "bumpy" and preferably with a bit of a taper. The thinner edge should be towards the tip, with the fattest part in biased towards the handle end, with a fairly quick drop off. Done in sequence down the line this should create the physical illusion of millipede segments. You could do this by wrapping more tape, or adding a stuffing layer, and covering it with tape The teeth should be spaced so they "emerge" between bumps, and the bumps should deform slightly where they extend out so it looks like they're growing out from underneath and between segments..

                              2) Millipedes have color. The primary shell color is black/brown, but there should be highlights at the edge of each segment. In many millipedes this is a neutral warm red/brown/yellow. Highly toxic millipedes usually advertise this via these highlights with very vibrant yellows, greens, or even pinks. Green is pretty appropriate for Zhyzhak of course. The bumble bee millipede or the yellow spotted millipede are two vibrant and basic patterns for how to add these sorts of colors (yellow spotted millipedes excrete cyanide... so their pattern is particularly strong on the "hey I'm poison" scale)..

                              3) Electrical tape is too matte for a millipede segment shells. It's made of the same materials as insect shells. For millipedes that rely on camouflage they're still matte, but less uniform. For warning coloration styles they're frequently a bit glossy; however that should be at the bulges of the segments more than at the joints. Obviously any paint needs to be something that can flex with the whip, but there's a few options there especially since you don't need a strong coating just enough to give it a sheen. This helps #1, and gives it a nice contrast that can help disguise the tape.
