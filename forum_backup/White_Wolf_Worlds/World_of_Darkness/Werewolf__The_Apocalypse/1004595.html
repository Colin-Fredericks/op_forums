          I take it into consideration when determining the setting in the area I set my game in. However, I usually select the place where my game is set because there's something that prompts interest in it or inspires something that I like which I can use in Werewolf. I find most of my players actually are not interested in playing Lupus characters or wanting real wolves around them, so it's a moot point. If they did, it would probably influence where I set the game.

          I try to keep my chronicle mirroring the real world on a general basis, but don't have issues making adjustments in order to accommodate the needs of the game. Generally, the wolf population will be a bit more prevalent in the World of Darkness since the Garou will protect some small population where they can.

              I do and don't. In the real world the last recorded wolf pack in Pennsylvania was killed in Lancaster Co. in 1912 on land that is now an elementary school. Instead of this event I have moved the last wolf pack to a sanctuary created on an island in the Susquehanna river this is the home of a small Red Talon Sept. The Uktena have a small pack of kinfolk wolf hidden on the lands of the Sept of Rattlesnake's Wisdom(rattlesnakes are not found often in Lancaster Co. mostly Copperheads and those are really common) the Get of Fenris have established a wolf sanctuary for "rescued" wolves that houses more wolf kinfolk. It really isn't hard to figure out how to include wolves where they shouldn't be common sense can give you a reason.
              For stories I set outside of the Lanc. Co area I tend to wing it. My semi-planned Michigan chronicle for instance I have several large wolf packs in the area and have the Red Talons making small cautious moves to reclaiming a city by infiltrating Detroit.

                      Given this is my bread and butter, I do. I take note of the local subspecies, what it looks like and its habits.

                      However, I do not consider populations as much. Given garou are around, especially lupus, wolf populations would be far more numerous than in our world. However, ironically I'm not sure if they would be as genetically varied. Since a lot of tribes base their pure breed on fur colour, some even breeding for very doggy pelts like the Black Furies, there would be a lot more selective breeding going on with the wolves than in our world.

                      For example, black wolves are mostly an American thing and one that stems from wolfdogs as purely black coats are a dog trait. So, for Shadow Lords to uphold that type of colour, they need to have been very VERY critical on who to breed with. And given they are described as pitbull-like, I'd say it shows. Then there are Silver Fangs and their spread and white/silver coats. While white wolves do exist, they are limited to two subspecies at most. So Silver Fangs would have had to selectively crossbreed with local wolf populations to get white wolves to India or the Middle East.

                      Of course, the people of WoD just see reddish, piebald, bi-colour and other wolf coats as normal wolf markings, since that is how things are in their world.
