                M20, p 562:

                "A Fetish channels the power of a spirit within that item. To use one, the character asks the spirit to unleash its powers.                             Originally posted by lbeaumanior View Post
                            The Natural Linguist Merit adds "three dice to any dice pool involving written or spoken language​", does this mean that this is a blank +3 dice to each social written or verbal interaction?[/USER]
                            If it is, the Merit is grossly overpowered. The sentence on adding three dice to any roll involving language comes from the original description of this Merit back in 1e when Linguistics was an actual Knowledge ability. If I was the ST, I would have limited those three extra dice to rolls that actually involved the Linguistics ability, not every single time the character is speaking and makes a roll. Maybe not strictly limited to Linguistics because that was almost never used in games (and to be fair, the merit itself does not say Linguistics dice pool). But it would have to be a roll where the technicalities of language and grammar come into play.

                            Although the old Linguistics ability was mainly about knowing extra languages, it did provide things like identifying accents and reading lips. And it had example Specialties like Curse Words, Technical, Diplomatic, Political. So I think the intention for the ability was to allow it to be used whenever understanding meaning is difficult, and getting the right translation is critical. So such rolls might come up when translating an archaic version of the language, a difficult dialect or sister language, choosing the correct terms when writing treaties, trying to determine the most likely meaning of a text that has lacuna, detecting a forgery, or knowing that in Latin Jehovah is spelled with an "I" when trying to figure out a puzzle or riddle.


                            These types of things will need to be determined by some other Ability since Linguistics no longer exists. So an ST might call for an Academics, Enigmas, Law, Occult, etc. roll instead.

                            That's how I would adjudicate it initially, but am open to others' opinions.
