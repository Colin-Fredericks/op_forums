              According to CB20, Corax also don't use ceremony; when they have enough (or lose enough), Helios gives them/takes from them their rank.

              As for the why, a lot of it has to do with the natures of their deeds. Nagah deeds aren't renown-earning due to their secrecy; Rokea are rare enough that they may never meet someone high enough to initiate them into a higher rank; and Corax are always on the move, while Helios is omnipresent. At least, that is my interpretation; yours may vary.
