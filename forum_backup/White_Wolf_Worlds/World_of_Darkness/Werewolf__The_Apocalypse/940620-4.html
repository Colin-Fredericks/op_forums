                                                I think some thing needs to be taken into account in this discussion. Mainly, that WoD books weren't just the text and just the issues of the times. It was also the artwork and the themes brought fort with them.

                                                Look at any 1st ed tribebook, just look at the art it has and the themes it uses. The opening comics, the templates offered and the way things are presented.

                                                To me, it often is rather goofy and very cartoony, much more like a comic book than anything else. And that continues to early 2nd ed. But through that edition to Revised and from there to W20, things matured a lot. The art got more detailed and refined, the books got better editing and layouts, and more pages. Research was done much more on the topics, and revised stated outright the alpha-omega thing was a myth, long before that became more well known.

                                                Also, to use one very specific example, look at how treatment of BDSM and body modification has changed through WoD. In the first, it is used almost solely to be edgy or show wyrm taint. And that continues in 2nd ed artwork. In revised,it's use like that lessened, and by w20 it is pretty much gone. Times changed, but so did the way WtA was.

                                                    Originally posted by Aya Tari View Post
                                                    Sanitation, vaccinations, and antibiotics are just three reasons why diseases are no longer population control.
                                                    Antibiotics are rapidly being outpaced by resistance on account of how much longer it takes to develop drugs than for infectious organisms to develop resistances to them. Many drugs that do get developed are also very expensive, preventing widespread use, which oddly enough, keeps their effectiveness up for a longer period of time. As opposed to the current standard of prophylactic and agricultural use of antibiotics, which is why MRSA and its ilk are a thing.

                                                    Of course, bacteria are also not the only infectious agents out there. If the rats get really desperate, they might start actively spreading prion diseases.

                                                    Sanitation might also take a hit, depending on how well the infrastructure is doing. And if Twitchers plant bombs in said infrastructure, which in the US is actually getting strained and outdated, well, suddenly you have a whole lot of old problems resurfacing.
