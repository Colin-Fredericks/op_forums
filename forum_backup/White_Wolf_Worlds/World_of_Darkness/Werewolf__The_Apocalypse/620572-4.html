    Originally posted by heinrich View Post
    The extra actions rules from the Celerity in Dark Ages 20 are pretty good, in my opinion. I'd like to see Rage actions limited in some fashion, too.
    I don't know that limiting them in the same fashion would be a good move. Garou and Fera are meant to be murder machines, whereas vampires simply move quickly when they use blood. Unlike vampires, Changers have to be under a good deal of stress to use their super-speed, and it tends to overwhelmingly skew toward responding to the source of said stress. Maybe if the mechanics just get evened out instead of having mountains and valleys of character capability, it will work a bit better.

    Gurahl Rage can also result in the essential certainty of popping someone's skull like a grape (20+ dice to grapple and deal damage for several turns!), so there's that to balance against, too.

        Originally posted by Saur Ops Specialist View Post

        The Rokea lost half of everyone they knew in one attack. As Bleeds-Night noted in the breedbook, if 3 or 4 billion humans just suddenly died, the remainder would get pretty strange, too. Besides, they're still pragmatic when they operate on the surface and don't seek to just wreck shit willy nilly; per the Sins of Sharks short story, they tend to be extremely, even bizarrely, surgical. On a metatextual level, the Rokea have their reason for being imperiled internally just like every other breed. Conflict and problems abound to make for stories of struggle, not hard restrictions.

        (I generally do feel that Rokea need their frenzy trigger point shifted from two successes to four or more; only certain types of sharks would have it at two, and they aren't the ones that most people might think)

        As for Rank and Rage, it shouldn't be built into any power stat's function beyond its improvement to self-control and aid in holding off frenzies. Similarly, I feel that making Tick Body grant Ananasi extra blood point storage based on Rank was a blunder that needs to be changed.
        The very idea of half their population lost in one attack is ludicrous to begin with, on several levels. And given their longevity, they should have a better grasp of things than that. They are the single most savage Changing Breed for no reason except being influenced by bad shark movies.

        And even that isn't really relevant. What is relevant is that you have less possibilities for playing a Rokea, or even using it as an NPC, than you have with any other Fera. Period.

        Extra actions for Fera are a tricky question. In truth, I think the system should get rid of Extra Actions once and for all. But if not, we have something here at once too easy and too costly.

        Resources-wise, the Fera got an easy time with Extra Actions. I mean, to say that any vampire can get the same number of E-A isn't an argument, such a vampire spent a helluva lot of resource to do so. To build Rage straight to 10 is a lot easier. Indeed, you have access to Extra Actions to begin with without even buying Rage. And to use that, you spend Rage Points, nothing so hard to recover (if it would Gnosis, them we would have a problem). Indeed, some builds can guarantee that you never exhaust your Rage, and they aren't harder to get than Celerity 5 for a vampire.

        But them we have the main problem with Rage: Frenzy. That's the real prob, because you can't have 5 extra actions without risking frenzy for whatever reason.

        Stopping again to think about it, Rank indeed isn't the best way to handle Extra Actions, but half your Rage shouldn't be, either. Assuming most of the rules remain the same, maybe they could just drop the "half your Rage" limit and put "five" in place. Simpler and more feasible without turning your character into walking nitroglycerin.

                                            Other changes I'd make:
                                            - Rename Thrall of the Wyrm frenzies to Uncontrollable frenzies. There should be some ambiguity in whether or not Rage is of the Wyrm, not a blatant statement in the mechanics. The potential for cannibalism and ultraviolence can stay, but the rape and necrophilia are things that can turn people who would otherwise be interested in savage horror away, particularly since oversexed violence has become a terrible cliche in an awful lot of media.
                                            - Simplify Animal Attraction into just adding Primal-Urge to your seduction, carousing, and befriending rolls unless the subject would be seriously uninterested. I seriously just don't see the need to add in a complicated subsystem for it, and don't think it needs to be a sex beam. If you want iffy ethical quandries, pick up the Kitsune Gift Seduction, or use Persuasion, to say nothing of the myriad influence Gifts that Galliards get.
                                            - Reduce Gift costs in chargen to 3, 4, or 5 bonus points, depending on whether it's in-splat, one of those general Beast Courts picks, or outside of splat, respectively.
                                            - Increase the cultural spread on tribes. The Fianna, Bunyip, and Croatan can stay as more regional tribes, but the rest should have coverage on par with all of the Silver Fang houses. This can also help to alleviate a lot of the "shitloads of European tribes, just a few everywhere else", and removes the burden of "Asian tribe" from the Stargazers. Also, it's entirely possible to have more than just Silent Striders with jackal read African wolf heritage, so you can bring a lot of the tribes all the way down to sub-Saharan Africa.
                                            - Have Black Furies be as open to transwomen as the M20 Sisters of Hippolyta. If you want them to be more savage about it, take a page from the langsuir of the Golden Courts. But only just one page, because there are a lot of Devil-Tigers there.
                                            - Don't refer to Hispo as a dire wolf. Ditto for Bastet, their Chatro form, and Sabertooths, as well as Rokea, Chasmus/Fighting Jaws, and the megalodon (it's very likely not what you thought it was).
                                            - Rename the Children of the Dire (and Song of the Dire) or ship them over to the Uktena as a Camp that's close to the Nuwisha (dire wolves were a sister species to coyotes).
                                            - Don't make Ananasi, Ratkin, Mokole and others have to get Gifts to have near-human and/or near-beast forms. Just put them in right off the bat, so that we can get cool five-fold spread artwork for everyone.
                                            - Build in rules that can handle huger-than-Gurahl Mokole forms, or don't bother and keep the hugeness to Dream Semblance.
                                            - Unrolled defense like in post-1e Exalted.
