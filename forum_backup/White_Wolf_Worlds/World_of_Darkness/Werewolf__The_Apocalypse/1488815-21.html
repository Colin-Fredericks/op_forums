            Originally posted by Manfr View Post
            I think that Justin and his team are taking a note from WotC in their approach to setting.
            There's a striking difference in approach.

            WotC was very careful to make things in D&D5 in such a way as to retcon as little old lore as possible, making sure players and GMs can bring old material as seamlessly as possible. Whenever possible, they even took pains to reconcile discrepancies between previous editions in such a way that none would be non-canon to 5th edition even if they're non-canon to each other. However easy it is to homebrew for people that have experience with homebrewing, it doesn't change the baseline situation. Old lore is either incompatible with V5, destroyed in lore or made purposefully unplayable. Most isn't just ignored, but in some way they're made hard to bring to the table if you're using new lore straight.
