    Werewolf Setting Hacks

    09-12-2015, 12:19 AM
    I've seen plenty of alternative settings for V:tM, though it seems like I don't see threads like that nearly as often for W:tA, even though I think that the game's setting might be really interesting to play around with. Has anyone ever created, or even run, an alternative Werewolf setting where some of the core aspects of the game have been changed?

    I personally have never rumor played in such a game, though I've had a few thoughts for alternate settings that might be interesting, at least as a short Chronicle or even just a though experiment. One idea being a version if Werewolf where the Weaver is seen as the main enemy instead of the Wyrm, where the primary enemy Tribe is the Glass Walkers instead f the Black Spiral Dancers. Another thought I had was for a Kinfolk based game set after all of the Garou have perished and only the Kinfolk remain to fight the Wyrm.

    Has anybody else created, played in, or thought about experimenting with W:tA's setting in such a way?

      I have

      My brain is constantly coming up with ways to adapt or play around with the setting. I always go back though. To the actual setting as I love the gane itself.

      So I usually just do one off games of crossovers, what ifs, and 'this is bat $#!% cray cray...but let's see what happens!!!' Kinda stuff.

      examples include:

      What if ALIENS:
      Meteor crashes in new mexico. It devestates the area and the garou, though saddened and enraged by the desteuction are still kinda happy it took out a BSD hive.
      Our intrepid Troupe are the first to go in and check out. With hopes of Star Metal from the skies to make fetishes with and cleanse the remaining blights hoping with Rorgs touch to the lands the Wyld may still prevail now in the area. (Especially as no one even knew a meteor was headed this way, how random is that?!!)
      But it's not long till they start finding weird constructs like giant beehives being built up in the BSDs old tunnels and whole villages nearby missing its people. And mad graffiti depicting savage looking chubracabra with long tongues and no eyes and black bodies stealing people in the night.
      yep ALIENS are in WoD. I pur them in castes and types. Figured they were the spiritual remanant of the survivors of the weavers Insect Wars and been hiding way down deep in the Weavers realm cocooning and tainting others to breed more of their kind in deadly efficient and terrible ways of parasitic infestation. Until some went roaming and took on Rorg. Who quickly dispearsed them out of his realm on a meteor and decided to strike it at a hive that had also in the past given him trouble. Two birds one stone kinda thing.
      except some survived and now found thenselves physical rather than spiritual.
      they build the Hives to reconnect to the Weaver and her one song. Using the size of the hive as a booster of sorts. If one gets big enough it can exist in both umbra and physical realms and the Aliens can reconnect to the Weaver.
      The real horror kicked in when the Troupe found out what the surviving BSDs and wyrm tainted kinfolk host bodies created after face hugger fun times. Wyrm tainted Aliens who killed and destroyed and other things rather than secure and infect (on behalf of the Wyrm).
      The next terrible thing...where people and kin died after being chestbursted by baby Alien. Garou...regenerated...and where being infected again and again and again while webbed and fused and calicified to the Hive walls. Oh yeah...imagine that fate.

      I put in a whole mythology, purpose and reason, spirit broods and their relation to the Ananasae (The werespiders teeth had a natural resin that when used kept the Aliens blood non-acidic nor volatile and drinkinable..in fact twice as nourishing) but it didnt come up. The Garou all wanted an Alien Queens head as a trophy instead of finding out about them and pretty much just stormed a hive and went Vasquez and Drake on the whole thing.

              I also took Illiad and Odysseys work on behalf of Pentex and the Wyrm and turned them into a secret organisation that was recruiting people with special abilities and forging them into Superhero teams.

                                                      @Baaldam
                                                      Well, just motors with more efficient philtering of its emissions might be good enough depending on individual garou attitude, honestly. That said combustion engines fueled by ethanol or other substances of vegetable/renewable origin already exist in some countries and have been a thing in Brazil for more about 40 years.
                                                      While technically correct, those substances aren't without their own problems - corn has to be planted somewhere and "somewhere" often happens to be former rainforest. And of course, it still runs into the problem of every post-apocalyptic setting: On the one hand, it's a setting of extreme scarcity, where it's entirely possible to run out of bullets, to starve to death or die from thirst. On the other hand, the protagonists and antagonists should be able to sustain cars which need either oil for gas (meaning the whole "post peak oil" thing isn't quite as post as most of the background would suggest) or biofuel (meaning there's a lot of farming around which could easily produce enough food for people).

                                                      Incidentally, i would be all for the Rust Riders as some hybrid tribe borne out of Glass Walker & Bone Gnawer remnants both.
                                                      The world changed why would the tribes not?
                                                      Hybrid tribes sound good, but I'd rather hybridize others. GWs and BGs are presently the only two tech-friendly tribes, something that works very well in the setting. Other tribes may lose their focus - when there's no Garou Nation to lead anymore, the Silver Fangs lose one of their key concepts - and may thus benefit more strongly from merging with another tribe that fit better into the setting.
                                                      Conceptwise, I'd see the Bone Gnawers more grounded in the here-and-now while the descendants of the Glass Walkers range either into the past or the future. Gnawers improvise with what they have at hand whereas Glass Walkers either research new technologies or mount expeditions to regain functional pre-Apocalypse tech.
