                        CRIMSON THAW is a very different comic from WINTER'S TEETH and has the kind of black comedy I love from my World of Darkness games. Its like Seattle by Night vs. LA by Night. The entire war between the vampires and the Lupines is because it turns out Calder Wendt (World's Worst Tremere) bought a wedding venue then erected a bunch of Wards against Lupines. The Garou interpreted it as an occult attack against them since it was their catering business/caern. I mean, that's not a joke. That is the actual plot and I love it.

                        Crimson Thaw's section on werewolves has some interesting bits. Reya, the obviously Native American member, is listed a being of the Ghost Council tribe. Do you suppose this replaced the controversial Wendigo name or Unites both native tribes?

                        Steph, whose portrait shows her wolf form so I'm guessing is lupus, doesn't have a tribe listed. She does have something redacted after her name, but that's not where a tribe would be. Her text makes her sound like a red talon. Maybe they're getting rebranded because of how the tribe's reputation was blackened?
