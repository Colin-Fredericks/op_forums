    Originally posted by Nyremne View Post
    "The spirits wilds constitute a mystery even to the garou, whose animistic outlook makes them familiar to the spirit world but not strickly of it"

    *Caugh* what is this? It's exactly the opposite! The gaou are, by their half spirit nature, OF the spirit world as much as the material one. They are of it, but not necessarily familiar to it.

    And it's indeed their nature that give them a form of understanding of the umbra, not their animistic outlook, which is a consequence of the former.
    The idea is the same but it's phrased differently to, I guess, emphasize that Garou in W5 aren't familiar with spirits and the Umbra. The phrasing you get in the introduction is: "Werewolves belong to multiple worlds — spirit and flesh, wolf and human — but are wholly accepted by none​"

    edit, the twilight project one is indeed also weird. The V5 second inquisition loresheets were all about knowing how to avoid the SI's attention, but this one seems like garou have some interests in snitching to an org dedicated to hunt them.

    If anything, it should have been the reverse.​
    The "Stolen Valor"-one is especially weird where Project Twilight killed a Garou and your character claims instead to have been responsible for the death. Even more so because first, it says you gain a three-die-bonus to social tests because of the achievement but then it adds: "Subsequent Social tests in the same scene suffer a one-die penalty for you, because you come across as boastful, overbearing, dubious, or whatever’s appropriate.​" What a weird approach to this idea...

                                            I think anything that requires WP like Gifts and Rites, Crinos-form and staying for a "protracted" amount of time (whatever that will end up being) in the Umbra are an issue in light of the Tribe-Bans. At the end of the day, it comes down to what sort of ST you're playing with. Things like "opportunity to satisfy your curiosity", "witnessing a preventable act of injustice" or "characters under your hospitality come to no harm" aren't things a player can control. And if you trigger the Ban getting only to heal one WP next session becomes a real issue in light of all these things where the mechanic is "Spend WP or a bad thing happens.". And in the same way, Touchstones expect STs to threaten/harm them in order to engage with the vulnerability of having a Touchstone, Bans expect the ST to set up scenarios where engaging in the behavior the Ban asks for is a choice with consequences. But I think there are two ways for this mechanics to stop working: One is if the consequences are so light that it isn't really that much of a big deal and the other is if you stack the deck so high against one particular choice that there really is only one choice. And I think with the high value WP has in this game, you got a case of the latter. Only regenerating 1 WP next session is so much worse because of how many mechanics are then turned against your character (dealing with Frenzy alone with an unhealed WP-track... first you have the WP-test whether a severe provocation triggers your Frenzy and then you have another WP-test whether you will stop killing at some point...) that then means those characters who have these behavior-specific Bans really gotta engage in that behavior no matter what.
