                                              Red Talons are great. Your concept is certainly workable, and it sounds like you are putting a fair amount of forethought into this (which is fantastic). I would give your lupus a strong reason to connect with his or her packmates. Why did your Talon leave his home sept and join with the pack in question? If you were always at this sept, is there a large community of Griffon's own there? Or are the local Talons a dying breed and you are one of their last few scions?

                                              You have figured out what breed and tribe you want to play, but you might want to get a handle on your werewolf as a character- possibly even before defining your auspice. It is easy (and common) to have a character be totally defined by their moonsign, but it isn't devastatingly interesting.
