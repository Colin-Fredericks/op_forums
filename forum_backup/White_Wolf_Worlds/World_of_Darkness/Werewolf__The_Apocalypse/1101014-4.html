        Originally posted by Onkwe View Post
        Was talking with a friend how it would be cool to explore the setting as the Garou have been so depopulated that by the modern day there's not enough left to form these global tribes. I could see in the tribal homelands there's enough echoes for influence and such, but for the most part the last few septs are some elders and maybe a pack or two. You could really emphasize the apocalyptic nature of the struggle, and the cultural loss and disintegration werewolf society faces.
        I'm doing something similar now.

        One of the things I've begun changing about my setting is that I'm lowering the population of the Garou, and decreasing the number of high ranking caerns (there's only maybe a dozen rank 5 caerns left in the world for all the Changing Breeds, and perhaps twice that number of rank 4). I've also increased the number of Ronin in the game - collectively they would be the largest "tribe". Combined with the above, this really thins out the number of actual Garou.

        There are definitely still tribes - since all you need is the Rite of Passage to get your tribal totem - but there is the idea that the heritage of the Garou is being lost. Learning rites, various lore, the Litany in its long form, and other Garou or tribal heritage becomes a task in itself and involves people travelling to different septs, finding Old Ones, and making a real effort to learn these.

        I've found a lower population is more likely to result in the kind of stories/plots/encounters I like to have than the default population found in the sourcebooks or games I've played in. I like games that are recognizably in the horror genre and characters reminiscent of actual werewolves found in stories elsewhere, than the epic fantasy genre the game devolves into and which canon tends to encourage.

        I like politics to be between septs, and low ranking septs are more understandably monotribal. And the decreased population and resources available heightens conflicts between Garou. And the less Garou population encourages the idea that the Garou simply don't swarm over their enemies and defeat them in detail. In turn, I can make their foes more subtle and hidden, less over the top. More like cultists in Call of Cthulhu games than the Wyrm factions in the source material. By toning down the number of supernatural creatures, the game seems more believable to me.

        Since PCs are likely to want to be in multitribal packs, I need to make some concessions so packs often start out in relatively rare rank 3 (or a few overpopulated rank 2) caerns, but the characters will know that most caerns out there are only rank 1 and 2.

        This has only been slowly forming in my mind over the past year so I haven't run this revised version of my setting in any chronicle, but this is the direction I am currently going in.

            Garou matings are sterile.Metis are born from Garou and Spirits coupling. They are still deformed and sterile and most Garou still find them disturbing enough to despise them.

            A sizeable minority of Homid Shifters come from sheltered Kinfolk communities who don't officialy exist on any census. How the Garou and the Fera treat these communities range from "benevolent rule" to "Black Spiral levels of depravity

            The War of Rage was less successful than in canon, the Changing Breeds were weakened to the point of not influencing history but the Garou know they survived. Doesn't make the world much less fucked.

            The White Howlers didn't fall to the Wyrm even if 40% of the Tribe including most of the elders did. The Black Spirals have but a few Hives and most are found as a dark elite in Wyrmish Cults. Gatherings of more than three packs are cause of terrible concern for the septs aware of it.

            Most Werewolves devoted to the Wyrm never dance the Black Spiral but remain as cankers among their people, slowly tryiing to corrupt them. Dancing the Spiral is considered a reward as it strengthen you but a bitter one for you are unable to meaningfully infiltrate Gaïan septs again.

            The Garou Nation is the name given to a group of werewolf protectorates in Europe. The others great groups are the Protectorate of the Silver Crown in America, the Amabeth in the Amazon, the Ahadi in Africa and the Beast Courts in Asia. They don't like each other very much.

            Members of the Triat do not "want" things in the manner a mortal mind may understand. Below them are a pantheon of Celestines who fight or ignore each other. One of the Weaver Celestines is the Mother of the Pattern Spiders and she would want a world where everything happens according to natural cycles. She is opposed by the Machine-God who is the "face" of the Weaver this day.

                                    Here are my Children of Gaïa


                                    There are some who think the Children of Gaïa are the tribe of peace, a tribe of cowards who are just good to throw wild parties and moan about non-violence while the others do all the work. This is false. All Garou rage against something and the Children of Gaïa cry against the death of innocence, against the defilement of what was pure and the monsters of the world being free to walk among the sheep. They consider themselves Gaïa chosen priests and some indeed walk the human world seeking to evangelize. Unlike some could think they teach the truth of nature as it is, red in tooth and claws and how cooperation and trust are the best strategy to fight the darkness. Among the Garou their words are the same but there they use their expertise as healers to make themselves indispensable. When comes the times to fight, rare is the Child of Gaïa foolish enough to try to redeem some Wyrmish cultist on the field, they favor beheading strikes proving Gaïa’s own superiority against the best the foe has to offer. They consider these cults and sects dedicated to the Corruptor as their favorite prey. They are servants of Unicorn and their ban is “Pay Each Garou in Kind” Basically they are not the hippie tribes but the Cleric/Paladin's tribe. They treat Gaïa with zealous devotion and while they consider that indeed the war is first won in the hearts and minds of others, they are not adverse to have the claws out. Their faults are self-righteousness beyond the other Garou, an unspoken belief unity means they are in charge, and downright cult behaviour when interacting with humanity. Thoughts?

                                        I have never been bothered with the metaplot.
                                        The garou aren't good guys, they're terrorist vigilantes with delusions, tricked by spirits and their own egos into thinking they're doing something noble. The garou are notably terrible at telling apart wyld spirits from wyrm ones, and there's a line between bane and wyrmy spirit that they usually don't understand. Garou are tribals at heart, and the conflict between them is ramped up. Truly, the garou are more a tale of former kings reacting badly to a world they once ruled turning away from them, finding common cause with "gaian" spirits who want to look out for themselves as man shrinks their territory. The nation wants something to be done, the spirals have accepted their course.
                                        I really hide this viewpoint from my players though, I tell them the lies like I want them to believe them.

                                        I've thought out the many, many ways in which pentex can be an apocalyptic cult and a business with poor ethics. If they were just the former, the world would be over, if they were just the latter, the garou wouldn't have any more beef with them than they would anything else. I've thought of the myriad of ways in which they could make profits (let's be honest, if you had to recruit so many security personal and first teams just to defend your stuff, you might as well just not pollute)
                                        All garou, allies excluded, and other unnatural entities are to be harvested for parts, they have incredibly high value to occultists,private collectors and a few science corps. Metis are especially valuable some will pay millions just for a single claw.
                                        Waste dumps and such, beyond defiling caerns to move garou out permanently, is the price that pays for bane investments and business. Nobody goes out of their way to polute for the lulz. On the same note, tainting products is done to make people more dependent on them, or to confuse garou, or on the whims of banes who offer rewards, people becoming assholes because they drink blue stripe is just a happy sideaffect. Now the important part to this is that, while i've yet to have a player in a position for this temptation, I would completely set this system up for players to use.
                                        There are nephandi within certain subisdiaries that make some of the more advanced technologies. There's some small overlap with mage, though pentex is far behind the technocracy.
                                        The other wyrm elements are made just as prominent as balefire, even though balefire is insanely cool. I've considered something for metal, glass and lightning, but I've yet to implement them.
                                        The frog changers were initially failures, but subsequent attempts provided a more successful rendition of the breed, for use in aquatic support. They are more reliable and loyal.
                                        A lot of the stuff that pentex makes to kill garou actually ends up in very exclusive sales, for no less than two and a half million USD, you too can have your very own war wolf! You can also hire a first team or even make you and a buddy a formori (it's advertised way better,of course)! On the flipside, Pentex subsidiaries have been known to get a hold of old HIT marks, and they long for many of the cloning and weapon technologies their weaver rivals possess.
                                        I really, really want to write a book for a pentex game.

                                        Does anyone know much about importing things from scar or that cyber hell? How viable is plundering the spirit world for resources?

                                                How the Red Talons fell

                                                They were sure it was going to be the Wyrm, that would take the tribe of Griffin in the end. After all, with their unbridled Rage and their desperate hatered towards humanity, it was all but a given. Still, some theorized that it could as easily be the Wyld that would turn them into its gorgons.

                                                Some cynical ragabash even suggested the Weaver, given the Talons rarely changed their ways.

                                                Yet, when the first rabid wolves in crinos attacked humans and could not be reasoned with even by Garou Tongue, the Garou Nation was faced with another possibility they had ignored as impossible. Garou who were known to be Elder ranked suddenly sought Uktena, Fury and Wendigo septs as cubs, holding no trace of any purebreed. Yet their pelts wered the blood-red of the Talons. Packs following Griffon suddenly had their totem change into something similar but not quite.

                                                A questing pack was sent to the Red Talon homelands to find out the reason for the sudden disappearances of Griffon. The pack found a realm filled with ancestor spirits and everything seemed as usual, but it felt empty in some way. Storm-Chaser, a recent ancestor explained to the pack the reason with a bitter tone.

                                                "The new cubs aren't like us anymore. They have Rage, but they no longer think or see the future. They have only wolf-hearts and no human minds. Griffon cannot be a totem of wolves, he needs garou, so he left us"

                                                As the pack returned with the news, a deep fear descended upon the Nation. The Red Talons had lost their human side, who knew if the opposite was possible.


                                                --

                                                Basically, an interesting twist to the typical 'tribe falls' scenario. Please note that Red Talons lost their humanity through quite a bit of effort on their part, Glass Walkers cannot lose their humanity by simply being human based. It takes willing ignorance to go that route.

                                                The semi-garou the new garou of Talon breeding became are basically wolves that can shift when extremely nervous/afraid. They mostly take hispo, some may take crinos. Homid and glabro are beyond them. They have trouble shifting back and often they can end up living the rest of their short lives in these forms. They have Rage, but it is alien and frightening to them and other wolves.

                                                Luckily, this loss of humanity is only found among Red Talon garou and kin. It is a limited number of pairings that can result in a semi-garou, but that alone spelt the doom for the Talons as a tribe.

                                                What does this mean for the Nation? Well, first of all, fear.
