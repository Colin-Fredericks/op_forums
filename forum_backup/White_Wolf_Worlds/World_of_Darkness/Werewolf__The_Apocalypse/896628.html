                      This house rule has two parts.
                      Mechanically, is the question if PCs can spend unlimited XP on totem, or if they are limited to five per character, because backgrounds are limited to five. If you have the limit, adding additional totems is kind of limited, if you have only one pool to buy the totems. Still, doesn't make much sense to have more than one, as a game benefit. Boosting the totem you already have to have the benefits for more PCs at the same time and so on, usually is a better choice.

                      In-Game wise, totems are not spirits that only follow the pack. It is a semi-godlike entity, who if a pack pledges allegiance will get rewards, and an relatively weak avatar of said Incarnae being. So, such a pledge for allegiance is probably exclusive, at least among other totems. Jamie Lannister states at one point in the books, that each knight has to many oaths to swear, that they are bound to break some. And surely, a totem accepts that a pack has obligations to sept, septleader, family and such. But that they serve two totems, even those who are kin of similar seems far fetched...

                                                      I think personal Totems can be done, since the Garou already have to share their respect and chiminage between their Pack Totem and Sept Totem.
                                                      If you look at it this way, a Personal Totem can never be as strong as a Pack Totem which can never be as strong as a Sept Totem simply because you cannot put down more than 5 dots into a Totem yourself. If you have a pack of 4 Garou each putting the max amount into the Totem, that's a pretty powerful Totem. 4 times more powerful than the Personal Totem can be. By the same logic, each garou who give chiminage and pay respect to the Sept Totem makes that Totem more powerful. I don't think that spirits require you to only pay respect and chiminage to them, they should be ok with you having another spirit who you are especially close to, otherwise the whole Pack and Sept Totem thing should be able to work.
