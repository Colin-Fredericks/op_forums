                    Originally posted by Su-tehp View Post
                    I haven't heard ANYTHING about this, but then, I've been out of the loop and don't know anything about this "nuWW" (new White Wolf? This is a thing?) or what happened between OPP publishing W20 and the appearance of nuWW and BNS W:tA.
                    Simplest possible version?

                    In 2011 CCP dumped most of the original WW folks because the WoD MMO was on its way to dying. During this period Onyx Path and By Night Studios were founded. Onyx Path had the licensing from CCP to produce TT versions of the WoD, CofD, and Exalted games. By Night Studios got the license to produce the LARP versions of the games.

                    Onyx Path continued to produce the 20th lines for the various WoD game that started when the CCP owned original WW made V20. BNS followed suit with new editions of Mind's Eye Theater books for VtM and WtA; with their own vision of balancing nostalgia, collecting lots of material in one place, and fixing lore and mechanics.

                    Paradox Interactive bought the rights for the White Wolf imprint from CCP in the later half of 2015, the license deals with Onyx Path and BNS are assumed to have stayed the same in scope at a minimum.

                    Paradox decided to create an independent subsidiary called White Wolf Entertainment Aktiebolag (or WW A.B. for short, it's a Swedish label similar to LLC).

                    This "new" White Wolf created a new edition of the games (which they numbered as 5th) to kick off an attempt to revitalize the WW brand as both a top tier RPG, and a transmedia property with video games, and movies or shows, and so on.

                    nWW produced VtM 5th aka V5, and has gotten its first two supplements early pdfs out to pre-order customers.

                    However after multiple PR missteps with preview materials, play test content, V5 core's release, and now the V5 sources books on the Camarilla and Anarchs, Paradox has decided to revoke nWW's independent status and take control the associate properties directly. As I noted above, we have zero idea why this means for W5 moving forward.

                    I haven't read BNS W:tA yet, but from what alot of people are saying about it, it seems that alot of tribes are being reinterpreted/retconned into stuff that doesn't make sense to me (yet). I'll post more when I have a better idea of what's going on (and if people want to help explain here, that helps too).
                    It's something of a mixed bag without having read the metaplot BNS introduced for their new version of WtA.
