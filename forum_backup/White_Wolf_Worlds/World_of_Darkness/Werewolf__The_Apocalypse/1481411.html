              Corax actually make for Horrifyingly effective spies and assassins.

              90% of their gifts are for information gathering rather than combat, meaning any corax worth talking about is going to know who your PCs are, where your PCs are at any given time, how they got there, what equipment they have, what gifts they use most often, their favorite color, who they're related to, their best friend in high school, their mother's maiden name, where they left finger prints, their social security number... Meaning if a Corax what's to mess with you, they can social-engineering their way into your major password protected devices, drain you bank account, and frame you for any crime they want with all the evidence they could ever ask for.

              Add onto that gifts like Sky’s Beneficence(level 2)[allows a corax to drop Any package on a target with the accuracy of aimbot of steroids as long as she can see them], Eyes of the Eagle(level 3)[allows a corax to see through any obscuring condition like fog, rain, darkness, etc. as long as it isn't a solid wall in the way], Mynah’s Touch(level 3)[Pick a graou gift less than your rank. Any garou gift less that your rank. For one use], Flight of Separation(Level 4)[Shadow Clone Jutsu!], Gauntlet Runner(Level 4) [Roll wits + enigmas Dif 8, lower the gauntlet by 1 Per Success], Deceptive Demise(Level 5)[fake your death by stepping sideways and leaving a corpse in your place], Theft of Stars(Level 5)[Target can't perceive nature light. Get a target in the woods during the day and they're essentially blind because their sight-enhancing gifts won't be able to help them], Theiving Talens of the Magpie (level 5)[This beauty speaks for herself]

              The corax can essentially drop explosives and incriminating evidence on the group at any time, Can leave anonymous tips to the local authorities with your entire personal biography on hand, has a thousand and one ways to escape and fake her death, can pull out anything from her hat of tricks at any time, and can do it all from a safe distance.

                                      You are right, placing a single Corax on a combat grid and surrounding him with a pack of Garou just to roll dice for that combat DnD-style would be senseless.
                                      If your goal is to make the Corax into a problem for the pack in your story, let the Corax know a thing or two about how Garou renown works instead.
                                      No player character is behaving perfectly all their life, everyone has done something worth a scandal at some point and sooner or later you need help from those who would be disappointed or angry if they knew your screwups.
                                      The Corax have a set of rules similar to a 'Corax Litany', and the tenet that makes for the greatest problems is 'There are no secrets'.
                                      So when the Garou who made an enemy of that Corax need something from any other influential Garou or Spirit, it is time for "So you need X from me? Alright, I am the right one to ask, but first: a little birdie told me to ask you about that one thing you prayed that noone would ever notice, how exactly did that happen again?"
