    Originally posted by Black Fox View Post
    Ignoring semantics of the proper name for the new vampire, the real question is whether the vampire has the powers of the Garou. I would say no. It's like asking if a newly embraced caterpillar will become a vampire butterfly - biological processes stop upon death. At the time of the embrace, the pre-Change Garou cannot shapechange and does not have Rage or Gnosis. I see no reason why these powers would automatically happen.

    Furthermore, mechanically there is the issue of Gnosis. A pre-Change vampire doesn't have any. An Abomination cannot increase Gnosis at all. I don't think you can have a Garou without Gnosis.

    It is entirely possible that an assault by the vampire would trigger the First Change (in which case the survival of the vampire is called into question), but that would not be automatic and I can think all sorts of scenarios where that likely would not happen. A direct physical attack could well trigger a Garou's First Change. But I think it is less likely if it happens during the course of a seduction or while the victim is sleeping. This would be an ST ruling in an individual chronicle, not some obvious rule for STs to use in a standard game.
    Except that, per KABA and textual evidence from before it, everything indicates that pre-Change Garou do have Rage and Gnosis prior to their First Change, because they always have some nagging feeling of there being more (latent spiritual awareness = influence of having Gnosis), and they are also angry enough that others shy away from them (The Curse = Rage is apparent). When the books say that Garou are born Garou and not Kinfolk, they mean it.

            I usually play Theurges and in the Theurge information across various books but predominantly in Book of Auspices, they always know something isn't quite the same with them compared to everyone else. And their first change is typically easier than other auspices because they've had that connection. This to me would indicate that the Gnosis is there pre-change. Otherwise, you would end up with kinfolk spontaneously going through first change if there's no rage or gnosis latent within them.

            So I think it's a good bet to say that any pre-change Garou who is embraced would end up dead or an abomination. And I would typically NPC an abomination unless the game is centred around them.

                                        Originally posted by Muad'Dib View Post
                                        Do you know in which Wt:A books there are rules for Garou Abominations?
                                        I personally dislike the W20 take, so here's mine.

                                        Gnosis, Rage, Primal Urge, Gifts etc. are gone. If there isn't space for it on a Vampire sheet, it's gone.

                                        Forms are kept but you transform using Blood. All of the benefits of the forms stay except for the healing factor of Crinos form.

                                        NPC Abominations may possess a merit where they can heal Aggravated damage (I do this to make them uniquely terrifying, since very few beings possess this ability) as though it were Lethal.

                                        They can no longer enter the Umbra.

                                        They may possess the traits of their Clan, however a relatively high proportion become Caitiff since their body naturally rejects the blood; even if the Embrace works it may not work fully.

                                        They suffer a weakness to Silver, which inflicts Aggravated damage on them.

                                        They are at +2 difficulty to all Self-Control rolls. As a result of this, many become Diablerie addicts.

                                        They do not regain Willpower from resting, and many develop a severe Depression not unlike Harano. Many commit suicide (after exacting revenge on their Sire and any Vampires they can get their hands on). Many die alone, since they do not want their tribe to see what they became in their final hours. Those who remain alive typically do so because of Rotschrek; they simply aren't strong enough to face Helios's embrace. So they kid themselves that they will carry on to kill a few more leeches... and the days become weeks and the weeks become years, and eventually they are one of the monsters they once fought.

                                        I personally dislike the VtM rules that state they can't regain Humanity or Willpower with EXP, and other stuff that damns them from day 1.
