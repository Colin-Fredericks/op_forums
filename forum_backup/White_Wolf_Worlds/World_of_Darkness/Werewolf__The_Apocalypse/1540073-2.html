                    Originally posted by MrornX View Post
                    I didn't expect the Hearthbound to be amoung the Dawn Tribes instead if say the Eastcons since I got the impression that they influence is a important factor that make the alliance possible in the frist place. I guess it's due to some kind of political shannagains that prevents the Hearthbound from Associating directly with the Eastcons though it doesn't really chance since I got the impression that Eastcons still have a close relations with Dawn Tribes.
                    So, to understand this outcome, one has to both consider the means through which one majority population violently subjugates a minority population and then consider this is something the Garou Nation has done many, many, many times before in the past. In short, Hapil recognized the Hearthbound being hunted and killed using the same means used against them in the Age of Storms, and again on the Changing Breeds in the Age of Tragedy during the War of Rage. To stand by and let it happen would make them no less complicit in genocide than the rest of the Western Concordiat. They saw a kinship in their struggle for survival and adopted them. To this day, Hapil act as elder surrogates to the Hearthbound, as the tribe hasn't existed long enough to have their own elders.

                    The second consideration to be made is that when one examines the relationship Hapil hold with both their Kinfolk and crinos is the very standard by which Hearthbound hold other Tribes to. Since the beginning, Hapil Kinfolk hold rank and renown along with their Garou kin. Their being shapeshifters isn't status in and of itself. Likewise, this is a tribe whose crinos all fairly earned their ranks. Even if many crinos don't endure their extensive trials to pass their Rites of Passage, their elders are still always due their respect regardless of breed.

                    The third consideration here is in the manner in which Hapil teach one another. It is lupus teaching homids, and homids teaching lupus. This tradition is carried over to the Hearthbound, where perspectives are shared in the upbringing and training process every Hearthbound undergoes.

                    Finally, although the Eastcons now work with the Hearthbound, it didn't start that way. With the Shadow Lords, the Lazarite Movement did a lot of legwork to bring equity to their tribe, and the secret society came out as early allies to the Hearthbound, teaching them how to hide in the open. Bone Gnawers, Black Furies, and the Children of Gaia have always had weirdly gender and bioessentialist views on their crinos despite being vocal allies to them. Its either something tied to requiring one be a particular gender to hold rank or learn certain rites or another putting their crinos on the front lines and giving them unfortunately short-lived careers as Garou. One only needs to look at their upper seats of power to see how few crinos hold high rank or Sept positions for it to start painting a less than glowing picture of the tribe.

                    While the Eastcons are now allies, the Dawn Tribes are their home. Their dedication to helping Kalaril reawaken Turtle cemented their alliance with the Garou. With the other changing breeds, it can still be a little touch and go (to a Balam, a penitent Dog is still a Dog.) but as the series progresses so will their evolution as a tribe.
