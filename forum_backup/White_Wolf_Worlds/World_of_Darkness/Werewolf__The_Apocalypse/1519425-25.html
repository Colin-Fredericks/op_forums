    Yeah, but part of "business stuff," is pacing your schedule of releases and news. Unless Paradox wants people to forget about W5, they shouldn't have had a major BL 2 announcement just after W5's release. What happened basically has three potential reasons on the business side of things:

    1) Bad management of the brand where scheduling has lead to PR overlaps and internal competition for marketing resources.

    2) A lack of confidence in future growth for a product. Or, in other words, Paradox thinks that W5's sales goals were adequately hit in pre-orders, and all future sales are just extras for their targets, so there isn't a need to invest more marketing into it.

    3) Active dissatisfaction with the product internally, meaning that the goal is in fact to bury W5 because they don't like how it's doing, and are moving focus onto other things to keep customers focused on positive PR.

    2 and 3 are similar, and externally we really don't have a way to judge that until much farther along in the future. The big difference is going to be in supplements and how much attention those get. 2 implies there will be supplements with at least a level of marketing previous V5 and H5 books have received, while 3 implies even if there are supplements, there isn't going to be a lot of pushing from Paradox of them (Renegade might opt to take up the slack there though).

                            Just as a note for people laying down markers for my three options: they're not actually mutually exclusive beyond the most extreme cases. As I would do whenever it's possible, I would say the most likely cause is actually a mix of all three. Justin's departure from Paradox has clearly left a management position that isn't being handled, making judgements on pre-sales with minimal later hype is fairly common in the TTRPG space even if we don't call something a pre-sale (Onyx Path is pretty transparent that many of their books basically hit baseline sales goals on crowdfunding alone, so they don't do a lot of hyping up of when books get fully released on DTRPG/etc.), and moving on to BL 2 news instead of dwelling on W5's negative PR - that they can't really do anything about immediately even if they do want to address it - is a sensible business move.
