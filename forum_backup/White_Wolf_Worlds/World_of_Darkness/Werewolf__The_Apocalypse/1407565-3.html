                                To some of your points on tribes MyWifeIsScary - baring in mind that this is simply my opinion on the matter - although many of these could very well be entire threads on their own and may well have been in the past:

                                •Fianna
                                -We’ve definitely discussed this before and while I agree that more can be done to distinguish them from Irish stereotypes, I feel that their expanded lore via revised tribe books has done that well. Their name is derived from their history and I personally don’t see any reason to extract them from their Gaelic-Celtic roots so long as tribes with ethnic ties remain part of the game.

                                •Younger Brother
                                -I disagree with your characterization of Younger Brother - while all tribes do certainly reject modernity, barring the Glass Walkers primarily - I would consider it a gross disservice to makes Younger Brother the tribe of technological rejection - a task the Red Talons already have chosen. Indigenous groups have embraced western technology quite well, with the goal of adaption and survivance, and before colonization many indigenous groups practiced highly refined land maintenance and scientific-technological traditions. I feel it would be more accurate to describe Younger Brother as particularly tradition minded - a vanguard of language, land, and ceremony. I also feel that extracting Younger Brother from a justified anger towards colonial populations does the tribe a disservice. I agree that Younger Brother should not be portrayed as constantly “kill whitey” and that the leaders who bring about colonial infrastructure should be central enemies, the point remains that Younger Brother has a justified anger over an apocalyptic loss of culture and life and while today there is certainly no easy answer to the vast populations of non-indigenous folk in North America, Younger Brother (and players identifying with him) should be allowed the catharsis of resentment.

                                •Black Furies
                                -I adamantly disagree with your representation of the Black Furies. The history of transgender people throughout numerous societies and spiritual traditions aside, a game defined by its punk nature should not be creating barriers against real world trans people. I feel that trans woman should be accepted by the Black Furies, or at least, by Pegasus. I do agree that the tribe should certainly retain camps that maintain a TERF attitude, but interaction with such elements should be optional in play - if a trans woman wants to play someone like herself in WtA through the Black Furies and doesn’t want to deal with harassment she already endured in her real life, that should not only be possible but encouraged and celebrated.

                                •Stargazers
                                -While I certainly agree that they should be ‘a’ tribe of Asian werewolves as opposed to ‘the’ tribe of Asian werewolves, I don’t understand what you mean by “they’re meant to repulse us”.

                                •Children of Gaia
                                -I do agree that their tribe book is just, ugh, varying from lackluster to annoying to just gross at various times. That said, I do also agree that the negative side of the Children of Gaia should be shown through their attempts to mediate conflicts without truly understanding the anger of either side as well as they arrogance, hypocrisy, desire for ‘purity’ under Unicorn, and their belief that only they can truly unite the Garou Nation. I also believe that they should be celebrated though for their willingness to pursue peace in a culture of war, their willingness to accept anyone, their truly idealistic ambition, their fierce willingness to defend innocent and purity, and their drive to unite the Garou with humanity - even if it leaves them exposed.

                                The Children of Gaia have been shortchanged for a long time in my opinion, when they have the potential to be as inspiring and interesting through their virtues and flaws as any tribe.

                                •Tribes
                                -I do agree that their should be a happy medium between the dysfunction of 1st/2nd edition and the sometimes overly-strident revision of revised tradition. Each tribe should be flawed and problematic, each should have flaws of the past and present, that define where they are today and why they are potentially a threat to Balance. They should also be celebrated though, each tribe unique and highly qualified in certain areas, leaps and bounds ahead of others, qualities of their Rage and Character which can inspire us to join their fight and see how their vision might just avert the Apocalypse.

                                                            The whole military history screed also really doesn't make the point it seems to want to.

                                                            To paraphrase my grandfather, who grew up during WWI, became an adult in the aftermath, and fled Europe in the 30s: The worst thing to happen in the 20th Century was that Allies deciding that WWI had to end in an utterly decisive victory that forced a humiliating surrender by the Germans so they'd "never" challenge the Allies again.

                                                            The desire to make the the Central Powers suffer for the war, and the economic and political sanctions the Allies put on them in the peace agreements are directly linked to the rises of Fascism and Communism, as the Allies destroyed the stability of the old governments and made the populaces increasingly open to radical forms of governance to reclaim stability, economic promise, and national pride.

                                                            And that's the exact sort of thing the CoG opposes. Beating your opponents so badly they embrace extremism means losing the war against the Wyrm. As Erinys
                                                            points out, this is literally the case just with Garou being shoved out of the Nation over such mentalities.

                                                            The end of WWI made things worse because it was about beating the enemy into submission. The end of WWII made Europe a far better place (even with the rise of the USSR and the Cold War) thanks to the commitment to the Marshall Plan and recognizing that the point isn't to reenact Rome's salting of Carthage.
