    Depicting the Cult as only willing to accept recreational hem-hem and not actual family-building would be an excellent way to hint at something rather worse than ‘No Garou Nation bull**** here’.

    The key element to capturing the Cult’s villainy is to evoke not only a brutally uncompromising attitude to enemies, but a callously dismissive attitude to the very notion that one can enjoy a somewhat settled family life even if the Cause and the Curse mean you can’t really live a peaceful existence.

            - COMBAT THE WYRM WHEREVER IT DWELLS AND WHEREVER IT THRIVES

            - ACCEPT AN HONOURABLE SURRENDER

            - RESPECT THOSE OF LOWER STATION

            - THE FIRST SHARE OF THE KILL FOR THE GREATEST IN STATION

            - EAT NOT THE FLESH OF HUMANS

            - THE VEIL SHALL NOT BE LIFTED

            - DO NOT OBLIGE THE PEOPLE TO TEND YOUR SICKNESS

            - THE LEADER MAY NOT BE CHALLENGED DURING WARTIME

            - TAKE NO ACTION THAT CAUSES A CAERN TO BE VIOLATED

            (I’m also fairly convinced that ‘GAROU SHALL NOT MATE WITH GAROU’ was seriously proposed - a majority of the Cult of Fenris wants it’s members focussed on the Apocalypse War and an honourable death, not wasting time making children that cannot be expected to see adulthood*).

            *After all, to their way of thinking The End may come at any time.
            I think one way to emphasize the callous nature of the Cult would be to remove the last two, the one about honorable surrender and respect for those of lower station. Protecting caerns is irrelevant because the world is doomed, the only thing that matters is combating the Wyrm. And the challenge-thing gets removed because the Cult has a far more sadistic view of how it operates as an organization. So the only mantra for the organization is "might makes right". There's no real camaraderie anymore, just the brutality that equally exists in how the Cult treats their enemies as well as in how they treat each other.

            And I think bringing back Crinos-born could work if it's coupled with the old breeding-lore. You can use that to build up a contrast between the other Garou and the Cult. Whereas the other Garou have started caring less and less about Crinos-born being this stigmatized group of people and more and more Septs are just fine with accepting them as full-fledged members on equal footing, the Cult on the other hand has gone in the opposite direction. They are of the fanatical belief that the correct number of Crinos-born in the Garou-Nation should be zero. And so they have this merciless kill-on-sight policy for Crinos-born. The logic being there's something fundamentally wrong with Crinos-born in the mind of the Cult and it's part of their zealous faith to just assume that if there are simply no Crinos-born in the Garou-Nation everything will be just fine.

                That wasn’t actually my intent - my notion was more grounded in the idea that the Cult doesn’t want it’s female warriors pregnant when they could be fighting and is deliberately cultivating a ‘Love them and leave them’ attitude to romantic relationships in the interests of ensuring that a Fenrisian’s
                loyalty is to the Cult and to the Last Battle, not any loved ones.

                This is somewhat harder if their loved ones are fighting right beside them (Actually, the likely tendency of 5th edition Garou to form packs grounded on such romantic relationships is an interesting new wrinkle, though I suspect the damage two lovers with Rage can potentially do to EVERYONE is a sound argument in favour of an historic taboo against getting too tangled up with a fellow werewolf, even amongst the less fanatic elements of the Garou Nation*).

                While the rarity of First Change means that we’re unlikely to see who packs built on a nuclear family, it is possible that some Garou packs mirror their plain wolf counterparts, with mother and father wolf running alongside a mix of born and adopted children (I suspect that the Red Talons and Silver Fangs, possibly even the Galestalkers and Hart Wardens would be especially keen on this way of doing things.

                *Though if this was ever a formal part of the Litany then it would have been discarded - and probably discarded long since.


                Incidentally, with regard to the classic editions of WEREWOLF, I’ve seen and very agree with the suggestion that the Black Spiral Dancer’s mass breeding of ‘Metis’ gives them a short-term advantage only at a serious long-term cost: if nothing else, reducing female Black Spiral Dancers to breeding stock is likely to have a profoundly toxic impact on the status of women in their society (Especially when breeding a child of Rage is very, very dangerous for any pregnant Garou).

                Given the ubiquitous use of inhuman wave tactics and general tendency to spend lives without care associated with the BSDs, in an timeline where the Apocalypse does NOT occur on schedule the Tribe of the Fallen will almost certainly be facing a serious demographic crisis.

                Honestly, it’s a pity that 5th Edition doesn’t really allow us to play with these ideas, since it’s more low-key ’Cold War’ feel makes it a natural stage on which to explore the consequences of the Apocalypse War going hot … only to cool off before the world can boil away.

                Having said that the BY NIGHT studios version of WEREWOLF offers a similar opportunity and does retain the old Homid/Too Pure/Lupus breed paradigm.

                There have to be some advantages to having a multiverse after all, eh?😉

                        Originally posted by Atticus Cleanclaw View Post
                        It occurred to me that it might be interesting to puzzle out what the Litany, as taught to the Cult of Fenris may look like: I’m assuming that they would happily discard those elements which struck them as inconvenient (and generally interpret whatever was left to suit themselves).

                        - COMBAT THE WYRM WHEREVER IT DWELLS AND WHEREVER IT THRIVES

                        - ACCEPT AN HONOURABLE SURRENDER

                        - RESPECT THOSE OF LOWER STATION

                        - THE FIRST SHARE OF THE KILL FOR THE GREATEST IN STATION

                        - EAT NOT THE FLESH OF HUMANS

                        - THE VEIL SHALL NOT BE LIFTED

                        - DO NOT OBLIGE THE PEOPLE TO TEND YOUR SICKNESS

                        - THE LEADER MAY NOT BE CHALLENGED DURING WARTIME

                        - TAKE NO ACTION THAT CAUSES A CAERN TO BE VIOLATED

                        (I’m also fairly convinced that ‘GAROU SHALL NOT MATE WITH GAROU’ was seriously proposed - a majority of the Cult of Fenris wants it’s members focussed on the Apocalypse War and an honourable death, not wasting time making children that cannot be expected to see adulthood*).

                        *After all, to their way of thinking The End may come at any time.
                        Lights, camera, W5 mode on and action!


                        - COMBAT THE WYRM WHEREVER IT DWELLS AND WHEREVER IT THRIVES

                        "This first one is straight to the point as well as being stupidly obvious, the Wyrm is the enemy and it is because of her that things are as they are, regardless of what others say, and we are the warriors of Gaia and our duty consists of this, to eliminate the Wyrm wherever it is and not just it, but anyone who serves it's purposes, whether he is a creature of the Wyrm, a human, or even another Garou, whether they are aware of it or not. Some may find this rule too harsh and extreme, but this is a war and there is no "extreme" attitude in a war, all that matters is winning, especially when it comes to a just cause like ours and if you still have any doubts, just look at the state we are in now, this shithole that the world has become is because of those who believed we were being too violent, well if they weren't so worried about bullshit and started killing more we wouldn't be in as much shit as we are now."

                        - ACCEPT AN HONOURABLE SURRENDER

                        "This one is simple, even if some bootlickers like to complicate it more than necessary. We Garou are creatures prone to letting ourselves be carried away by our emotions, I believe you already know that at this point, naturally this makes us have many conflicts between ourselves for various reasons, but understand that this isn't necessarily bad, it keeps us sharp and always ready for combat, however we also are a species at risk, there are not enough Garou in the world and to be honest I don't know if one day our species will recover, so whenever we can we must preserve our kind, so when you and your pack brother fight, remember to accept his surrender and don't be ashamed to surrender if you lose, you will serve better off staying alive. Now, and it's unfortunate that I need to make this clear, this rule is for Garous only, don't even think about accepting the surrender of a servant of the Wyrm, if you do it's likely you won't live long enough to regret it, and even if you do, believe me, I will guarantee you that you will never think about doing it a second time.”

                        - RESPECT THOSE OF LOWER STATION

                        "That's easy, it's a rule to remind an alpha that he is nothing without his pack, Garous are creatures that live and act in groups and for a leader the worst thing that can happen is someone stabbing him in the back, I'm not saying to spoil them, an alpha must know when to be tough so his pack doesn't go soft, but if he's too tough or doesn't show himself worthy, disaster could end up happening, that's what this rule is about. As for humans and the other tribes, they may have deserved our respect once, but today they have made too many mistakes to let it go, we can't just pretend to forget what they did, if they want any respect, first they must recognize their place in Gaia's grand plan or die like the ignorant fools they are."

                        - THE FIRST SHARE OF THE KILL FOR THE GREATEST IN STATION

                        "I don't know why this rule is there. Depending on who you ask, it refers to prioritizing your resources by giving them to the strongest Garou, but if he is the strongest he will naturally get those resources. My guess is that this rule was made, so that the greedy leaders would maintain their corrupt control and take away what was rightful from the true Garou."

                        - EAT NOT THE FLESH OF HUMANS

                        "This rule is there for two good reasons. The first is that you shouldn't kill humans unnecessarily, do not hesitate to do so if necessary, but as I already said, we Garou are a dying species and whether we like it or not we still need humans, because We don't know if any of them could be potential Garou, so avoid killing them for any reason. The second reason and this is much more important, human flesh is addictive, I've seen honorable Garou become mangy creatures after consuming some human flesh, even if without knowing it, before you arrived I knew a Garou who ate human flesh once, and he would often lose focus during battle, and disappear during the night and come back with his fur soaked in blood, he tried to stop, but the abstinence was so great that he ate his own fingers, without much to do we put an end to his suffering, it's a sad fate."

                        - THE VEIL SHALL NOT BE LIFTED

                        "In other words, don't reveal yourself to humans, we are stronger and faster than them, but they surpass us in numbers and weapons, so it's better not to give them a reason to start hunting us. I know this can It may seem like cowardice, but we have a war to win and we only have to lose by starting unnecessary battles."

                        - DO NOT OBLIGE THE PEOPLE TO TEND YOUR SICKNESS

                        "A tough and sometimes sad law, but it needs to be followed, this is a war and as you know our resources are limited, so we can't use them on those who can no longer fight, we don't like making this decision, but it is necessary, also no son of Fenrir would be content to die of old age or as a dead weight, we die on the battlefield and will accept nothing less."

                        - THE LEADER MAY NOT BE CHALLENGED DURING WARTIME

                        "A little confusing since we are always at war. What it really means is that during a battle you don't question your leader, internal fights will only make you lose the battle and most likely your life. Now other than in these cases, there is nothing wrong in challenge your leader when all your enemies are down, an alpha needs to be constantly challenged to stay strong otherwise he will only become slow and weak and this will lead to the death of the pack, this is what happened with most other tribes, their elders refused to lose their positions and give the opportunity to a more trustworthy Garou, making excuses to refuse challenge, so the leader not only remained, but became complacent, that's not a Garou, just a filthy pig dressed as a wolf and a pack that do nothing deserve a leader like that"

                        - TAKE NO ACTION THAT CAUSES A CAERN TO BE VIOLATED

                        "Also obvious, but also the most sacred rule, the Caerns are vital to our species and must be protected tooth and nail and if anyone puts it at risk in any way the punishment must be only one, DEATH. We do what we can to defend our Caerns, the other Tribes claim to do the same, but don't be fooled, they are not doing enough, they can say what they want, but I listen to the stories and I know how to separate the truth from the lies. Have you ever heard about that Caern in New York? The one on top of a building? The damned Glass Walkers got help from some "trusted" relatives to protect the place, they even got military equipment, but everything collapsed when the hunters entered through the frontdoor and put a silver bullet in everyone's head and all they had to do was bribe one of these "trusted" relatives. I could sit here for hours and tell you all the stories I've heard, but they all end the same way, Caerns being brought down because someone couldn't keep his mouth shut and do his dawn job. Caerns, must be protected and only entrusted to Garous.

                            Psicose, if I had a hat - wait a minute, I borrowed my name from WEREWOLF: THE WILD WEST, clearly my Internet Avatar has an absolutely splendid chapeau.

                            I therefore doff my hat to you for putting together such a delightfully brutal (Though not wholly brutish) ramble through the mind of a fairly typical Fenrisian.

                            Incidentally, my presumption is that the parts of this ‘pruned’ version of The Litany which mention ‘the greatest in station’ and ‘the leader’ are a subtle way of stressing that there IS a hierarchy (and that wise Fenrisians respect the fact), partly to avoid compete anarchy and just possibly as a means of letting outsiders know that if you want to deal with the Cult of Fenris you start at the top.

                            The mention of ‘during wartime’ is meant to emphasise that the Cult of Fenris are soldiers at war, not hooligans on a spree (Also that when the boss puts his foot down, you JUMP).

                            It’s vaguely possible that some outsiders might interpret this to mean that there’s at least a small possibility the Cult may not always be at War - these outsiders are most kindly described as ‘optimists’.
