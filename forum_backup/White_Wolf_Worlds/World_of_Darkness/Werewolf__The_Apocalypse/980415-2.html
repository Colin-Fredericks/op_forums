            Food is an interesting topic. In the real world the flora of the dinosaur era was quite different to what humans evolved around. No grasses, few recognizable fruits. Lots of ferns, cycads, firs... Basically I'd run a few google searches and play up the differences. Plus meat, fish, bugs, insects and so on.

            To run a game set in the Age of Dreams, those alien differences would get supplemented by Mnetic feasts that taste as wonderful as the dreamer can imagine. These glamoured meals could be the equivalent of modern day booze, or harder even drugs. Each succulent bite dangerously addictive. Those who fail to eat real food often enough slowly wasting away as they continue to gorge.

            These dream fruits could have many other properties too - medicinal uses for example, attention focusing, mind enhancing, or strength boosting effects. All that and more.

            I'd imagine wastrels addicted to such stuff are generally pitied by those who are not. And due to the existence of such wonders, there's no need for cooking or fermenting the non-mnetic meat and veg.

            Just spitballing.

                                        So many good questions and ideas to respond to. Apologies if I've missed anyone.

                                        Caveat: These are just my unofficial musings.

                                        Originally posted by Threadwrought View Post
                                        You know, I'd love to learn more about the Drachid. Like if there was any trouble at first with the first three types of Dragon Kings' Drachid forms interacting (since dinosaurs, pterosaurs, and marine reptiles really were so distinct).
                                        The Dragon Kings reign lasts at least a hundred million years. We had to gloss over the entire history with incredibly broad strokes. I think it's fair to say that there was likely a period of inter-species conflict before everyone concluded that the Drachid were a unifying force and the Dragon Kings were considered to be one race.

                                        Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post
                                        ideas on how to involve Rokea?
                                        During the actual Dragon wars? Apart from fighting for territory in their own, scrappy, low tech ways, I'd say the Rokea would be prime placed as mercenaries and shock troops - especially if they were equipped with and trained to use specific Mnetic tech and pointed towards pleisiosaur territory.

                                        Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post
                                        does it go against the spirit of things, for someone to claim a power for their archid form to let them to explore one of the other breeds turfs?

                                        e.g. a dino saur becoming airborn, or a pteordectyle deciding to go aquatic, or is that probaly understandable since the setting lets you &quot;Reshape&quot; your archid form.
                                        Not against the spirit of things at all. The Drachid began the unity of the Kings into one race, the Mnesis and reshaping gave them power to shape their destinies and bodies to reflect who they were on the inside.

                                        Originally posted by Lin Liren View Post
                                        After sending the final draft to the printers, did you fellas ever think &quot;Gee, this has potential to be a Whole Role Playing Game Setting/Rulebook of its own, called &quot;Dreams of The Dragon kings&quot; perhaps?
                                        Thought about that when I was writing the outline. I'd love the setting to be explored some day.

                                        But it's ultimately not my choice. White Wolf and Onyx Path shape the view on where they want the story to go, and what will be written and published. If you want more of this, my advice is to vote with your wallets and let Rich know what you love. No guarantees, but it's a start.

                                        Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post
                                        ideas on how things would be culinary wise? would they cook their food , is their anything amongst the plants they could use for fermentation.
                                        What nikink said. Just thinking out loud here, I'd suggest they probably wouldn't cook food, but would have various Mnetic-derived ways of processing food to preserve it and enhance flavour. This is just one aspect of the setting I'd love to explore if we got the chance.

                                        Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post
                                        It says a Dragon Kings Auspice can change, is it safe to say that their should be a rite for doing so.
                                        To my mind, there would be a rite akin to some kind of graduation as they move upwards with responsibilities and power. This is one reason why Blazonry was included as a DK-era skill.

                                        Originally posted by Threadwrought View Post
                                        Have you guys considered pitching a Dragon Kings Era book to Onyx Path? Especially once copies of Shattered Dreams are up for everyone to get a look at?
                                        See above. We'd love the chance.

                                                            Well, the difficulty is that Exalted and WoD are basically separate. Even if you marry the two as Exalted was a precursor to WoD (which I assume is what you're doing), *something* unimaginably big, cataclysmic, and unknown happened to turn flat-earth Exalted with magical demi-gods into round-world WoD filled with monsters. However you solve that (and whether the two times are continuous or have a separation in between) is the answer to your question.

                                                            But if you want a random thought thrown out there, the whatever-it-was cataclysm that destroyed the Age of Sorrows wiped out everything except Gaia, and it mortally wounded her. Over untold, impossible years, she curled in on herself, hiding her wounds as best she could, slowing her inevitable death but never halting it. In her dying state, she dreams. She dreams on time frame unimaginable to every lesser creature, but as she dreams she has recreated weak shadows of what was, and reimagined the things she knew as the smaller, monstrous forms that inhabit the World of Darkness.
