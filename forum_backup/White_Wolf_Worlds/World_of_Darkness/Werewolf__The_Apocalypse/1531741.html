    BSDs in W5

    02-29-2024, 04:46 PM
    In all of the W5 hullabaloo, one thing hasn't truly been discussed in depth.

    What garou being able to breed with other garou and produce fertile offspring means when it comes to BSDs.

    For those coming to this from W5, unless the way BSDs are written is changed drastically, BSDs in Legacy mostly lived in underground caerns called Hives. Because they were underground, Hives often had no need to uphold the veil and since BSDs had very little scruples they would breed with other garou freely. Hence why most of their shock troops were garou born. BSDs also had a habit of stealing the kinfolk of other tribes to be used for further increase their numbers.

    One of the things holding BSDs back in legacy was that garou born were sterile, so they could not utterly outbreed the gaians.

    This is no longer the case in W5. Now there is nothing stopping the BSDs from outnumbering the Gaians by a significant amount.

    Given the focus on CoF, it seems like most of the lore on BSDs will remain Legacy until further notice.

      If we just look at the W5 corebook, I think there are two sides to BSD: On one hand, you have them as Wyrm-tainted horror-versions of Garou and on the other hand, you have... BSD as punks (in the pejorative sense) who are mean, cause chaos and are spiteful.

      The first part is about how they're these mutated versions of Garou who have fallen to the Wyrm. You still have the same story with the White Howlers - except now that either happened somewhere between the dawn of history or as late as the 18th century. Another case of who knows as a crutch for worldbuilding... You also get this description: "Their bodies have been tortured into shapes resembling bats, feral dogs, rats, and even things unspeakable, as much as they resemble wolves. And though they still possess the ability to change shape, as do Gaian Garou, those shapes are hideous and unnatural." (pg 277) So the BSD as Garou who have fallen to the Wyrm is there.

      But there's another side to this as well. I think there's a second strain here. It's the BSD as ne'er-do-well punks. That side comes out in the archetype-examples, for example, when the description for the Lurking Scout says "The Lurking Scout loves nothing so much as delivering a blow from the shadows that goes unretaliated.​" or the Caern Raider "Caern Raiders rarely act alone; in the age of Apocalypse, packs of them, large and small, are the scourge of young Garou who devote their own efforts to finding and restoring the caerns that have fallen dormant or been corrupted. No surprise, then, that Caern Raiders take their own malicious glee in once again dragging them to ruin, with as much collateral damage and bloodshed as possible, or befouling them into their own caerns of depravity and rot.​" * This paints a picture of BSD being petty and cowardly. Rather than making an effort to destroy every last caern, they take delight in ruining young Garou's plans to reawaken caerns or they only seek battle when it can't be traced back to them. And then there's of course the Loresheet called "The Black Spiral" where your Garou-PC somehow has contacts in the "Wyrm-scene" and knows about their "hangout-spots" (with the ultimate ability being that you can talk a BSD-member out of being a fan of the Wyrm/"help them emancipate themselves")

      * I could picture the Dirgesinger as a manipulative Wyrmtongue-style manipulator who veils their lies in so-called prophecies. But the game as a whole is on the same wavelength as the Dirgesinger. The end is nigh and that's it. Period. Not much manipulation left when this Dirgesinger-character is just stating the obvious...

                  When it comes to "breeding" in W5, I think there are two areas where you get new werewolves. One is to watch all the bloodlines that have Garou in them. It isn't a surefire thing but being related to a Garou is one reason for why one might become a Kin. And the other is the whole Kinseeking-practice of Garou looking for Kin that just pop up in the world. I think WtF does a better job of explaining this dynamic because the text in the W5 corebook is always written from the perspective of Garou doing the Kinseeking, finding the Kin and then introducing them to their Pack, Tribe and so on and so forth. What's missing is that similarly to how Uratha and the Pure are competing in WtF over newly changed werewolves, I'd imagine you have a similar scenario in W5 now where Kin pop up and Gaian Garou, Cult of Fenris and BSD compete over who that new werewolf joins.

                  Personally, I would change the torture/brainwashing-thing from Deepest of Wounds to be the edge the BSD have in recruiting Kin. And meanwhile, the edge the Cult has in recruiting is that with their perspective/philosophy they can evangelize to all the disaffected, cynical and beaten-down Garou (who may be on the verge of succumbing to Harano or are already in the thrall of Hauglosk) and get them to switch sides rather than the Cult actually doing Kinseekings of their own. But that's how I would do it. The W5 corebook doesn't really get into that aspect of Kinseekings and Deepest of Wounds of course assigns torture/brainwashing to be a Cult of Fenris thing.
