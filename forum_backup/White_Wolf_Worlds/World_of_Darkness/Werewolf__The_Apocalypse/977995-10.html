                                                        Coming at the issue from a player's point of view, In my experience I'd say 9 1/2 times out of ten, when someone makes a character that isn't mainstream somehow (in this example it's trans, but sexuality, race and religion can be substituted), it's not because they want to get into an in-depth exploration this facet of society in a make believe world, it's because they want to include things in their characters that they can relate to.

                                                        So every table has to decide if they're going to react to this sort of thing by toning down this one thing out of all the other things that go on in a game, or focusing on this one thing and making their table another example of transphobia/racism/homophobia/etc that someone needs to navigate. If YOUR reaction is an attempt to scramble to justify why this one thing needs to suddenly be focused on when it probably never game up as an issue before, you should take a good hard look at your motives.

                                                        When one is accustomed to privilege, equality seems like oppression.
