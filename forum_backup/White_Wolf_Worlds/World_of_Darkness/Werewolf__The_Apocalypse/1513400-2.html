                                            My impression is that you have to assume a precise writers' agenda to characterize this writing as "animals are inferior".

                                            Wolves are not sentient, as defined in the Sapiens Sapiens sense, and they live 6-8 years on average.

                                            I'd assume most players are not provided with specific ethology notions about them, so making them your only Touchstones means limiting some of the options that Touchstones provide, both mechanically and narratively speaking, or playing Werewolf: the Disney Version.

                                            Which is absolutely fine, but I do remember quite a lot of posters in the general W5 discussion asking for more realistic depictions of wolves, so I think it's sound to point out that Wolves don't express themselves as much as Humans and did considerably earlier, when suggesting how to use them in play.

                                            Also, it's quite clear that in the new W5 setup, balancing your Monstrosity with Humanity is a core theme, so again, not having any Human Touchstone it's feasible, as is not having Touchstones at all, but it will impact the way you connect with the outside world.

                                            I do like the way Touchstones are given some more options compared to V5 and especially H5. As for Rage, I think they are probably bringing something from thr Desperation dice of H5: maybe a high Rage can fuel your superhuman actions, but also create problems, so you don't really want a high Rage outside combat.
