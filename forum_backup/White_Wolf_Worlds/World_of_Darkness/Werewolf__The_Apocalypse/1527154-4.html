                                            Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

                                            Okay.

                                            Still, I always felt that was weird. Then again, some editions tried to make Gaia the spirit of everything instead of just Earth. I felt that was a mistake.
                                            There is a spirit of the planet Earth, Eshtarra I think?

                                            This is why the Apocalypse in W5, even in the previews, felt really disjointed. Because the whole point of the thing is that it is the end of everything. What the garou are fighting for is the very existence of the universe.

                                            To lessen that into just Earth is to lower the stakes in a very Musk way. You know, there is always another world to go to.

                                            So when W5 Apocalypse is something that doesn't even affect the mundane world much, it basically makes the stakes into steaks. Sure it hurts when they hit, but they aren't very pointed and tend to be floppy.

                                            Another reason why I feel they could have just made a new werewolf setting rather than used WtA's.

                                            EDIT: Actually, I think we need to talk about tone differences that affect this. W5 has taken a tone much closer to VtM, where there is a constant sense of futility. What the kindred/cainites do is often tainted by their monstrous nature and often good acts come back to bite them in the ass. Even the higher up vampires mostly focus on fighting each other through catspaws.

                                            For VtM, this is great because the cynical theme fits the game. Nothing truly matters and all that.

                                            But WtA isn't cynical, it is a game of genuine things. It is a game about things that matter and things that you truly care about. As stated, it is about caring so much it makes you mad.

                                            In such a setting and tone, the Apocalypse needs to be real.
