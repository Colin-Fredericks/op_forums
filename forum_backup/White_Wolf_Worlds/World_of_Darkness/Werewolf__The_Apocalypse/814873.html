    Is WtA to huggy?

    01-23-2016, 12:20 PM
    I am starting a new game and would like some pack vs pack tension. I like how territory in WtF can lead to this but is it an issue in WtA? I do not see anything really written on this in WtA, The tribes bicker over Septs and ideas but what about different packs? Most of what I see are packs having their role to play in Garou society and we all fight the Wyrm together. What am I missing? Thanks.

                  Privileges. You could give a number of enticements in game to the most respected and currently in favor pack. Sept positions opening up, stories told about individuals in the pack or pack deeds by npcs or higher ranking garou, opportunities to mentor or teach other Garou (someone wants to learn something, they send them to soneone they trust and favor who's in a pack), open discussions about Ranking up and preparing to be more responsible and respected in the Nation, rewards of learning rites of respect, taking roles in moots, pack being given a place sitting closer to the septs Alphas at meetings and moots.                                           It also depends on the purposes of the packs in your games. Many packs are created within a Sept for a purpose and disbanded when that purpose is fulfilled. Others are generations-old with new members coming in when old ones die, or leave for various reasons.

                                          Many Septs may simply not be big enough to support multiple packs to vie for power, but that doesn't mean that there aren't individuals in a Sept that are trying to gain control and build/recruit/manipulate packs to help prop them up and support them too.

                                          If you have a sept of 13, chances are that you may have one or two active packs, while others have joined together as a temporary pack just to give them a pack to be with until another purpose comes along.

                                          For the purposes of most games, a player-pack will join together for a purpose, and will remain together for the rest of that chronicle (because, well, you don't want to break up the party, and you don't want to inject too many NPCs into a pack either in most cases.)

                                          I'm not sure how Foresaken handles their packs, but in Apoc, tension between packs may be less about the local Sept, but maybe on a larger scale where two packs are backing different factions, competing for the same Fetish, seeking to be accepted by a certain Totem who only takes one pack at a time, or is seeking to control a territory that they feel they have the right to.

                                                  Originally posted by heinrich View Post

                                                  I think that the intimacy has to do with the spiritual bond created through the Rite that creates the pack. A pack is often formed for a purpose. Sometimes those purposes are short (a mission, albeit short or lengthy), or longer (a long-term goal, or as a purposeful family.) But when one's Tribe comes calling, when a Sept has to split, or when another Pack loses its Ahroun, and you have more than your fair share of them, sometimes the pack needs to break off a few, or break up when the time is right. That doesn't mean that you won't have a lingering bond (after all, you fought, bled, and sometimes died for one another), but the ritual of creating a new pack will fill in that longing and give you new commitment too.
