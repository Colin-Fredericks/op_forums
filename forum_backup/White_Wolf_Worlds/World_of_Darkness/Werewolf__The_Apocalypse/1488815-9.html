            The themes of the WoD should be highly resonant with Gen Z (lets face it, a lot WoD were already very young Gen X or old Millenials as it is).

            WoD5's problems have been in execution to appeal to the current younger demographics. "Just go back to VtM 1e but set in the 2020s instead of the 1990s." has a huge stumbling block: VtM 1e and the early WoD in general was sloppy in areas that aren't well tolerated. That's why Tribes are getting renamed and a Breed is getting axed. V5 tried - to mixed result - to down play the racist caricatures of certain Clans. WoD 1e really didn't get into intersectionality in favor of a starker black and white punk vs. the man approach.

            The WoD for the 2020s should be talking about how vampires influence red-lining to maintain their hunting domains or it's going to fail to be the edgy social commentary it wants to be.
