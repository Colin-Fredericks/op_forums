In play they can snag dots in anything they don't have at start. There's no surcharge, no special learning requirements, etc.

                                                            Following my own idea, I'm brainstorming a rule to my liking. The idea for now is for every character to start with Gnosis 3 and Breed gives a special benefit when following specific tenets of the Litany:

                                                            - Homid - Submission to Those of Higher Station
                                                            True wolves aren't creature of hierarchy. Humans are. Be it a boss or a god, following the vision of another is a human instinct to validate your own actions.
                                                            When a Homid takes a decision based on being ordered to do so, gain a Willpower Point.

                                                            - Metis - Combat the Wyrm Wherever it Dwells and Whenever it Breeds
                                                            Born in Garou society, the war for Gaia is their cultural birthright. It gives then purpose, it channels their anger, and they have no previous beliefs or impulses for it to conflict with.
                                                            When a Metis starts combat with a minion of the Wyrm, gain a Rage Point.

                                                            - Lupus - Accept an Honorable Surrender
                                                            Lacking complex language, wolves communicate through action. Fighting among themselves isn't about enmity, winning or causing harm, but making statements.
                                                            When a Lupus accepts a surrender without further aggression, gain a Gnosis Point.

                                                            Here's my first draft, please comment, I need suggestions to polish it. The idea is to encourage those behaviors in the loosest terms possible, to make room for interpretation. Homids get WP from devotion and/or discipline, Metis get Rage from following the goal of the Garou in its purest and most direct form, Lupus get Gnosis from talking with their bodies more than their mouths. All of them are being rewarded when they default to their "Breed Strategy" to make decisions.
