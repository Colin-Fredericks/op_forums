    A possible idea for the Future Storyteller vault

    06-21-2018, 12:07 AM
    I am planning on writing something for the Werewolf Storyteller Vault when it comes out. My idea is a piece of fiction set in a Summer Camp during the 80s or 90s. The story itself would be a coming-of age story written in the form of a diary owned by the main character, Larry James Haler, a camper who is a member of a boy scouts-like organization called The Junior Foresters. Throughout the story, things from the Werewolf part of the CWoD start manifesting in and around the camp, at first subtly, but becoming more and more apparent to the main characters as the story goes on. Among these are Fomori, some Fera, PENTEX operations, spirits, and of course, Garou. Would anyone be interested in reading a story like this and can i have some advice on making this type of story fit in Werewolf?

      The subtle influence of Spirits either affecting the world or the campers affecting them. What do the Junior Foresters do? Do they teach kids to respect nature? Clean up litter? Do the Troop Leaders teach kids how to venerate the natural world? Good chance if the Junior Foresters have been around a good amount of time a spirit might manifest to reflect the organization's teachings, like the Ultimate Forester. If campers go out and about away from the Foresters camp and encounter things they shouldnt, the spirit might manifest to protect his Wards.

      The campers might even befriend spirits on their own, find an injured animal in the woods and do first aid on it to the best of their abilities or if the animal is to far gone give it its last rites and release it from the mortal coil.

                                          I PRESENT.....

                                          ULTIMATE FORESTER
                                          From the dawn of the Junior Foresters there has been a inkling of spiritual potential and as the organization grew so did spirits power. It has seen many faces as it changed with the times, beginning as a organization of boys and men to the modern day of accepting everyone of all colors and kinds. The Ultimate Forester appears at all of the Grand Moots of the Junior Foresters as their rites to promote Fresters through the ranks invoke their name and the Forester pins the mark of ascension upon the next generation.
                                          Totem Cost: 7
                                          Individual Traits: Ultimate Foresters followers receive 2 Dots of Survival and reduce difficulty of rolls for survival in harsh environments such as building fires, makeshift shelters, and discerning if plants are poisonous by 2.
                                          Pack Traits: Packs dedicated to the Ultimate Forester may call on Them for 3 Dots of Animal Ken, Herbalism, and Veterinary Medicine and 3 points of Willpower per Story. Animal spirits have a good disposition to those who follow the Ultimate Forester if they follow the Ban.
                                          Ban: The Ultimate Forester insist his followers clean the environment, work hard to support community and the sense of honor the Junior Foresters strive to keep relevant.

                                          I picture this spirit is very active in regards to the organization, possibly the ritual and rites to promote foresters through the ranks might be signified by the patch itself being of imbued with gnosis or some manner of minor spiritual magic. I've also got two versions of a Scouts Book fetish made, one for Kin and one for proper shifters. My current game I'm playing in started as a summer camp of shifters so I have a fair bit of camper magic item written up, several of which are disguised as camp crafting.

                                          Also all right, if this really is just a PENTEX deforestation they probably dont have a heavy wrecking fomori crew on site. There could easily be some fomori or a few armed guards around to deal with 'wild ' animals that might not be to happy about their homebeing wrecked.They might also be dumping industrial defoliant and gassing out the wildlife to corrupt the place and prevent future growth and to keep out potential threats. The kids could also encounter Fomori animals in these toxic zones that might cause problems. Something that I've done in my changeling game is have a land Kami in the deep woods and the motley has recently protected it after a group of fomori and a BSD tried to corrupt and bring it into the Wyrms thrall. The deforestation could be an attempt to destroy a Kami that inhabits a certain part of the woods, that way the spirit can't try and flee and would fall.

                                                          Master Foresters Guide
                                                          Gnosis:7 Level:4
                                                          This heavy leather bound tome bears the crest of the first generation of the Junior Foresters, inside is written every name of the first Troop and all the lore contained within is written by hand. The tome is heavy and thicker than a textbook but within is knowledge of all wild things from how to discern if a plant or fungus is poisonous, how to build wilderness shelters, what to do to traverse the wilderness and know where to go to find safe waters and if not how to make things safe to eat and drink. This Guide was lost for a time, reappearing in the hands of Foresters in need when they become lost and unable to find their way home. Within this Guide is bound three spirits, an Owl spirit gives the guide Wisdom when the reader needs something specific within the purview of the guide hosted within the feather that is tethered as a bookmark, the compass built into the tomes cover is hosting a spirit of a Fallen Star so they may know their way as they travel, and hidden inside its many pages is a gold loop that is filed with a piece of natural glass that is enchanted with spirit sight. Because of the age and the magic woven into the book it is effectively always active, the reader always find the thing they are searching for in its text, the compass always points true north, and the glass monocle always shows the presence of spirits. When the book is consulted the reader receives a reduced difficulty on their next ability check.
                                                          Junior Foresters Guide
                                                          Gnosis:4 Level:1
                                                          This slime hardback book is handbound and its pages printed, its cover is rough to the touch and smells of the forest. This tomebook is often in the hands of Junior Foresters but the magic in them is not yet active, this is an example of such a active Guide. Its cover bears the title Junior Foresters Guide and bears the organization's brand and inside its filled with an assortment of information that is relevant to the young foresters journey from tent building and firemaking to the assembling of traps and basic plant lore. The spirit of this book is Awakened upon the initiation of the Forester to the second tier of Honors and is always active. When the book is consulted it decreases the Difficulty of all survival checks by 2 as the spirit insures the Forester gets accurate information..
