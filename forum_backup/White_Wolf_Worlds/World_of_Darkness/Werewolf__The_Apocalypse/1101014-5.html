                                    This is stuff I got from reading the world a lot and going inbetween the lines, not really stuff that I decided to change up, but It's controversial none the less.
                                    In times of old, the changing breeds were supermen who could have everything. They were powerful, and lived exceptionally good lives being able to murder/fuck anything they wanted. That's not to say that was all they did, they were very keen on family and the like, and were largely above average people with access to a power fantasy. Their only serious rivals were others of their kind, and as half spirits who could travel that world, they forged pacts and alliances with spirits to give themselves and their allies advantages over others (during these conflicts, each changer carved out the rolls of it's breed to reflect what and how they did things). Over time, this spiritual interference caused an imbalance, and the world started to change. Human civilisation started to grow , species died out, geography changed, the changers had made a mess in the name of helping spirits. When they realised their errors or the errors of others, they reacted badly, and wars between changers only got worse. They got it into their heads that they were keeping the balance, as they tried to fix wounds with amputations.

                                    Of course, the weaver rose during this (very long) period, and the wyrm eventually went mad. Banes rose more, either to deal with the weaver or as a reflection of the turbulent times. Balanced spirits, or gaian spirits, who had really caused much of the inbalance by using the changers in their own interest, were always fearful of the wyrmish spirits that meant them harm. Thus, they rallied the changers against the wyrm, promising new and stronger gifts. The wyrm, under this assault, went more and more mad, more vile and aggressive, this in turn unified many of the changers against it. Over generations many believed their purpose was to fight the wyrm, a lie encouraged by "gaian" spirits and the changer's own propensity for bullshit.

                                    One tribe of garou, the white howlers, known for their closeness with kin, their savagery, and their propensity for death spirits, and lion being an aloof totem, had many reasons to fall, but it's not so easy to just lose a tribe. Rather than the doomed hell charge most other garou envision, the howlers fell for their cynicism. They knew they were more loyal to themselves than to spirits, they knew their infighting created the wyrm, but they were curious as to why banes and formori came to their land; surely it was balanced in the ways of the triat and the wyrm need not send it's minions? After a time, their theurges determined that the wyrm was sick, unstable, and that it needed help, not only would the banes not ravage the land; the wyrm spirits would no doubt reward them. Most all went down to deal with the situation, some were tricked, but they didn't imagine the intensity of the wyrm's problem, with their new warped outlook, they dragged the rest of the tribe down with them.

                                    the other tribes continue on into the modern era, for the most part glued to the lies of a million years. Their infighting subdued by their duty, but the vestiges of their original nature die hard, they remain territorial, jealous, bigoted, and of course are filled with rage. Mixed septs have a tense power balance, and duels and other infights continue to kill garou despite the threat of the wyrm. The litany is the law of the garou, but it's age pales in comparison to those lawless times, and it is constantly being strained. Glass walkers are disliked for their skewed spiritual allegiances. Ronin are hounded by gaian garou because the spirits resent their lack of control. In the end, Garou are fools of their own design, and the truth is worth a silver death.

                                    Be correct.
