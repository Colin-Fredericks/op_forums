    Mr. Pickles and the Wyrm

    03-20-2017, 12:54 AM
    I've been watching Mr Pickles (https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr._Pickles) again and was struck by how much inspiration I can pull from the show.

    Mr Pickles could be many things. He could be a animal fomori or a Lupus Black Spiral Dancer. The entrance to his hive is in his dog house.

    The Goodman family are BSD kin which would explain Mr. Pickles interest in the family. Especially his sexual attraction to the mother.

    The father works for a Pentex subsidiary.

    Not sure where the grandfather would fit in. Anyone got any ideas for him?
