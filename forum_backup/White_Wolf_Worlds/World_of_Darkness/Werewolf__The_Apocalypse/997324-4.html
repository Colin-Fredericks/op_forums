    Originally posted by Uniform Two Six View Post
    The argument was being made that Garou don't murder humans who find out that Garou exist, but rather focus their efforts on discrediting them. That might be the general approach of the Children of Gaia, but it's not the average Garou. And I would argue that even if a pack (even quite a few Children of Gaia) were to take that approach initially, if it failed and then intimidation failed, eventually they're going to kill the human.
    Having to kill to protect the Veil is actually part of the punishment for breaching it. If all else fails, the Garou have to fix the problem, by any means necessary. They aren't doing it for fun. It is a necessity of survival. If it comes down to it. Garou are expected to be smarter than that. They are expected to protect the Veil. They are also expected to protect people actually. That is also in their Litany. But sometimes things happen and you have to make a choice. 2 of the tenets of the Litany can't afford to be broken, and the Veil is one of them. So Garou will take the Renown hit and possible punishment Rites and social stigma of having fucked up and they will kill the human whose knowledge threatens them. Again though, that isn't a decision that gets come to easily. Having to come to that point means the Garou screwed things up a few times already.

    Edit: I just flipped through every Revised Tribebook. The only 2 books (besides Talons) that mention killing humans because the Veil was breached are the Get and the Furies. And both of them frame it as a duty/punishment. None of the other 11 books mention it. So throwing the Shadowlords in there is misleading. I certainly wouldn't say they are above killing to protect the Veil, but they pride themselves on subtlety and misdirection. I would imagine there being harsh penalties among the Tribe for so blatantly failing to follow the Litany.
