                                                        Originally posted by Ragged Robin View Post
                                                        Its ultimately because w5 wants to be v5. The Get and spiral are the v5 Sabbat/inquisition the broken localised guys are the anarchs, the garou nation are the carmarilla the wyrm is a a vague cosmic force like the 3rd gen.
                                                        its the same reason w5 doesn't like umbral adventutes-the game is supposed to be street like v5.

                                                        To be fair, the book is not written like this.

                                                        There is a fairly relevant chapter about the Umbra, and llot hooks about the Umbra are costantly underlined and brought about. I would say most of the plot hooks presented in the book involve spirits.

                                                        The marked difference is in a reboot of the Umbra concept and in a lore element

                                                        1. Reboot- The Umbra doesn't have a map or a geography, it's a shadow of our world but it's marked by different events, so it is constantly changing; you are not traveling in different Umbral realms with a map, you are reaching a place of spiritual importance and enter the Umbra to deal with the relevant spirits

                                                        2. Lore- The Umbra is suffering at human hands, so it's strengthtening the Gauntlet to try separate itself from the ever growing power of the Wyrm in the real world

                                                        the Garou Nation is not something distinct from the players, and it's not even depicted particularly bad, it's a preApocalypse alliance which was shattered by continued defeats and the walkouts of Fenris and Stargazers, and whose laws (the Litany) were abused by ingenious leaders.

                                                        The setup is much closer, I'd say, to a Year Zero game (the Fria Ligan post apocaliptic signature game) , where you defend and reclaim your territory, and clear the Umbra.

                                                        BSDs are presented as nihilists, not just corrupted monsters, and Cult of Fenris as a cautionary lesson in militant factionalism and extremism.

                                                        Wyrm, Weaver and Wyld are not vague, they are cosmic forces, but the influence and presence of their spirits is the moving force of the game.

                                                        By the way, "street level" doesn't really mean "everything is fucked, you as well, just hide to survive".

                                                        It is presented as " Build your legend fighting for your territory". You are not gonna charge in the maws of the Wyrm, but you are gonna do the very same things you were doing in earlier editions, you are just assumed to do them in your hunting ground, instead of globetrotting around the Amazons.

                                                        (And still, the game says you can go globetrotting around if you want)
