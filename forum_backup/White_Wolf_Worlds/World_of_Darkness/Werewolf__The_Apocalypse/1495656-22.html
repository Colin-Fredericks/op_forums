            For me, the issue with mixing dystopian elements in the setting (your character's life is terrible, things in general aren't looking good etc.) with punk-stuff (stick it to the man etc.) like in a cyberpunk-game is that a big element of W5 is that Gaia's dead/dying and the apocalypse is going on right now. If the context is the end of the world, a formori-lobbyist should be the least of the player-characters' problems, for example. And if it's just a struggle to survive in general then stuff like lobbyists shouldn't matter anyway.

            By invoking punk as a theme, you would at least assume that fighting the good fight means something in the setting. But in the face of an apocalypse, it just seems... kinda banal.
