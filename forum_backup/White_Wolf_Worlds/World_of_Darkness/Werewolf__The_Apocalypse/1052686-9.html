            The Garou are killing Kindred everyday without losing any sleep, and the Kindred are terrified by the Garou because a pack of Garou can easily kill off the entire Kindred population of a metropolitan area if the local Kindred are not discrete (and if they give the Garou enough reason to bother to hunt them down). The Garou do not really fear what the Kindred can do to them (other than the Embrace), they fear what the Kindred can do to their Kin. While there are exceptions, the majority of the Garou think that the only good Kindred is a dead Kindred and, as long as it is occurring to another faction, the majority of the Kindred are willing to cheer them on from a hypothetically safe distance.

            The reason why the Camarilla tends to do better with the Garou than other Kindred groups is that they are generally willing to keep their distance from the Garou. The Sabbat tends to be too arrogant and the Anarchs tend to be too ignorant. In the view of the Camarilla, every Sabbat member killed by the Garou is a Sabbat stronghold that is just a little weaker and every Anarch member killed by the Garou is just one more competitor removed from the equation. Heck, I bet that the majority of Princes do not terribly care if the Garou kill off any Caitiff in their territory because the Garou are just saving them the effort (and the Caitiff are providing a valuable service by allowing the Garou to believe they killed off the local Kindred threat).

                                            Camarilla Princes know that their members just have to lie low for a few weeks when there are Garou around. Kindred, especially neonates, end up as corpses when they try to go toe-to-toe with Garou, but so does anything that is not designed to be a Garou killer.
