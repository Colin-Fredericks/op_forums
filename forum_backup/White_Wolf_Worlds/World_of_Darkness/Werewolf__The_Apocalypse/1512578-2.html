                                    With the art issue, I need to be frank; This is NOT about using references. References are fine and help an artist a LOT. If the artist had used Heilung's promo art as a reference, no one would care.

                                    It is the fact that we already went through this with the GW preview and that people on the spot could say "hey wait a minute" about it. Unless hired to depict the target specifically, similarities like this are not a good thing.

                                    As for the tribe itself, I feel it is hurt by the removal of one of its core motives. Without trying to give the people hurt by the Wyrmcomers a voice and instead being about travel and seeking, the Ghost Council sounds like a weird Silent Strider copy. Which does not bode well for the Striders.

                                    As Heavy Arms said, having 11 tribes to make into the same global generalist tribes as the Forsaken tribes are is not a good idea. Each tribe of the moon and pure held one specific niche that concepts could be drawn from. Especially as the two tribes removed from the pool were one of the stronger cases of unique concepts, the Fenrir being the War tribe and the Stargazers being a fully ascetic tribe of contemplation.

                                    My gallery.

                                            Like, if you take the description of this Tribe as "seekers of mystery" and having a hunger for information (of any kind) which leads them to explore stuff and look for secrets, then... how is that supposed to be distinct when you look at the W5 descriptions of Silent Striders and Bone Gnawers in this version...

                                            Silent Striders:
                                            Masters of discovering hidden ways, the Silent Striders find themselves uniquely suited in finding their way back out again after having accomplished what they set out to do. Why not explore first and find out the best way, rather than the most direct? At worst, one may confirm another way not to undertake things. At best, one has discovered a way to get what one needs without anyone being any the wiser to the Silent Striders having been there.

                                            Bone Gnawers:
                                            The Bone Gnawers seek to discover the obscured in all its forms: secrets, passwords, shortcuts, anything they might use to advantage over the obvious. They are especially adept at hiding in plain sight and are often found among lower socioeconomic groups, those who go unseen by people who consider themselves superior and are careless about what their “lesser” think or witness.
                                            When it comes to "looking for secrets" as an attribute you essentially have three Tribes that all have the same MO.

                                            It would even have helped a little bit to limit the Ghost Council to being interested in spiritual secrets exclusively to at least give it some specificity. But no, stuff like investigating people and their secrets is included as well.
