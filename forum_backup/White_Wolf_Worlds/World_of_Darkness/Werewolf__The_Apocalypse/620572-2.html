            I think this actually can lead to a bigger change and question. It seems pretty silly, but one of the biggest issues I've seen in online games crop up is related to this.

                                Originally posted by Ana Mizuki View Post
                                I think this actually can lead to a bigger change and question. It seems pretty silly, but one of the biggest issues I've seen in online games crop up is related to this.
                                I have the same argument every once in a while, but I usually convince others of my point of view...
                                So my answers (how I see it and would like to have it kept in the Setting):

                                Originally posted by Ana Mizuki View Post
                                Can garou have jobs?
                                Kind of.
                                First, they can surely learn a job pre-first change. I would argue, that doing so would require their Rage score to be low. But the Curse probably isn't fully active pre-first change, so interacting with humans and animals still would work reasonably well for any late-bloomer garou.

                                After the first change I'd say, that it depends on the job in question and the Rage trait in general. I liked the rules about having a human life that were in 2nd Edition tied to a roll, but were scrapped and turned into a solely ST responsibility in revised and W20.

                                That said, if older Characters are allowed to have a first change, they probably would still only get character creation points to spend. A neonate also does get only a fixed amount, regardless of age and abilities directly prior to the Embrace.

                                Originally posted by Ana Mizuki View Post
                                Do they live at the Caern solely?
                                No. Garou do not necessarily live on caerns. They have pack territories. These are in the protectorate, the area the sept feels responsible for, that is outside the caern bawn. If a caern is a holy place, like a church, then the garou are like the congregation. The congregation doesn't usually live in or adjacent to the church. The priest and his family might. Same goes for caerns, in my opinion. Sure, some septs might be exceptions to the rule, but in general, garou packs have their own home turf and if you aren't the warder or one of the guardians, you only come to the caern if you have business there.

                                And the hunting grounds of packs don't border on each other. There is a need to expand, evolve, move the territory if need arises. One doesn't want to tip-toe around other pack territories all the time. Think of gang turfs in cities, only that is here applies to stretches of land in a more rural environment.

                                Originally posted by Ana Mizuki View Post
                                Are kinfolk their sole providers?
                                Probably not. While garou should be always able to follow an Elders (or his pack alpha's) call to protect Gaia and probably do something to protect gaia even when no supernatural threat is currently known, they might generate income still. And there are the spoils of war: Fomori Pimps and Drug dealers have cash and a dead Pentex manager might not need his Benz or Rolex, so "hello, Pawn shop".

                                All in all, Garou can possibly fend for themselves, of they need to, but sure, the Kinfolk does help, as they are usually able to hold down stable jobs.

                                Originally posted by Ana Mizuki View Post
                                What is the role of kinfolk?
                                I go with the PGtGG, which has the only Out-of-Character description of a "ordinary" kinfolk life (it is Children of Gaia, and other tribes might flavour it differently, but it is meant to be exemplary, and it is out-of-character, so it is the best source).

                                Sure, there is a difference on what the garou see as the kinfolk's role and what the kinfolk would like his role was, but that is another topic, and probably one that generates personal drama in certain character-driven arcs.


                                Originally posted by Ana Mizuki View Post
                                And to us veterans, the answer to all of those questions is yes. But what I've seen online, I do think they should be reconsidered at least a bit. If players want characters who are financially independent and live separate from the caern, maybe that should be considered to be made more possible.
                                One can have Resources 5 and one can never bother to go to the caern to participate in Moots. But that brings it's own problems. I mean, one can be a Ronin or part of a community. If one wants to live in the garou community that is a sept, there are certain duties required.


                                Originally posted by Ana Mizuki View Post
                                On the other hand, one of the biggest problems with players doing this with their characters, is that the role for kinfolk lessens. If the garou are all ready doing what the kin are supposed to, it leaves the kinfolk PCs with basically a servant role and a role as breeding stock. Most end up being solely the latter, and it can get disturbing. They all ready changed how Wolf-Blooded are done in WtFv2, so changes are coming.
                                True. But, maybe that is a problem of it's own.
                                Kinfolk can do stuff only kin can do, garou can do stuff they don't take the kin with them, when they do, and vampires don't work in sunlight. My point being: If you play garou, have all players garou. If you play Kin, do so in an all-kin campaign. If you mix, you will either be required to take the back-seat when the kin does her thing, or she will have to be just observer when the focus is on the garou part of thing.
                                But, unless you appreciate the time the game focuses on the another player, this game will always suck. Especially, if the ST has halve the game time for one Kin and the other half for three+ garou players.

                                Best way to avoid such problems is to not mix garou and kin, or have every player have two characters, so no part of the game session is unevenly distributed amongst players.

                                Originally posted by Ana Mizuki View Post
                                Another thing that crops up with late changes, is how it affects Metis or Lupus. Do they just naturally get earlier changes than homids?
                                I could appreciate character concepts of Metis who had to be in Crinos till they were in their (human) fourties. Also, lupus, who change for the first time and have the body of a mid-thirties guy.

                                Would require some minor changes in society, though, for they would surely start to train a Metis who is mature enough to act as a cliath, and probably send him on a Rite of Passage, since all his Regeneration and other powers are already active, just shapechangeing is not...

                                    Other changes I'd like to see:
                                    1. Loose the Rage, Gnosis and Willpower tie to the Auspice, Breed and tribe. There were so many nice other methods here in the forum, on how any of these choices would contribute to all three stats. Like Homid bringing +0 Rage, +0 Gnosis but +3 Willpower and Fianna bringing +2 Rage, +1 Willpower and +2 Gnosis and so on. That is, if the character creation remains the way it is, which brings me to my next point...
                                    2. Change the character creation. Have Building Points, that are equivalent to XP. Have one free dot in each trait, that has a minimum of 1 point (like Willpower, attributes, or Vampire virtues), all other traits are 0. Then spend the experience to make the character, just as you would advance him. Sure, Backgrounds and Merits/Flaws would need Experience Point costs for such a character creation, but why not. Just state, they only are viable during character creation or make them an optional system to acquire Backgrounds and Mertis/Flaws in game with Experience. We use a system like that (aided by php/mysql/javascript) to handle characters for our (non-MET) LARP for several years now, and it works fine. Such a system would work well for the complete classic WoD and make different splats more comparable, experience wise.

                                                            1. I've run Werewolf since second edition, and have had characters that played every conceivable concept under the sun. I have never played or run a game on line and will not discount the experience of others. Kinfolk as a background gives you a family perhaps an estranged one but a family. I have never had a player say hey I'm going to call my sister Becky and hit her up for the cash we need to rent a car. I have had a character whose family owned a cleaning service that didn't mind helping with those pesky blood stains and letting characters take the place of a cleaning crew. I have had a character whose brother was an EMT and could provide emergency first aid.
                                                            2. Not every Garou is going to live at the Caern it is not possible or realistic Garou will need to go into public and I doubt many Caerns have showers. Further if a Caern is attacked from a tactical stand point it makes sense that a few packs would be held in reserve or off site to attack the attackers from behind. I can see the Elders plus family living at a Caern. A few packs living in scattered areas around the Caern and even a few that actually live within the Bawn.
                                                            3. Garou holding jobs is possible especially since Glasswalkers tend to live in cities and many Caerns that are given as examples are in office buildings.
                                                            4. Garou probably go through 4-8 years of training it takes time to build skills before a rite of passage and part of that would include certain lessons for lupus and merits characters. Further given that it is almost impossible in North America or Europe to get a job without a GED or a High School education and given the Rage of the average Ahroun I could see home schooling being used to help protect the Veil. Remember that the whole range of Child advocates will target under age characters. My city is not alone in having a curfew for persons under 18 and nothing screams Veil breech faster than a cop stopping a minor for breaking curfew only to have said cop ripped to shreds.
                                                            4. Late change Garou can work but unless the pack is at least 18+ or appears that way this can lead to another set of problems see child advocates above.
