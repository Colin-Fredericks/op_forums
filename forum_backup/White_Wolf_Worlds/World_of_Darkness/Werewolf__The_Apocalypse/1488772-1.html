I used Bugs Bunny as an example for the simple reason that for almost a 100 years he's been a part of people's childhoods and cultural touchstones in the west. Still, I doubt he is on the power level of a totem and would most likely be aligned with (ha!) Coyote, he is probably not very liked by the Garou. Most popular culture isn't on that level even with modern iconic fandoms. You need a long, long period of time for something to coalesce. The spirits you mentioned "All Mighty Dollar" and/or "Easy Credit" are Incarna that started in the conceptual realm, and with time and cultural worship of economy and prosperity grew into the spirits they are today. It's not something that happened just so, but a coalescence formed from the concept of the American Dream.

                    The Far realms where dreams and hopes coalesce are not really something that has a lot of power and traction on its own, nor can it really interact well with the other Umbra, or the real world. They exist in and for themselves, with little comprehension of what actually is going on on the outside. So while I would posit that there may be entities there that are more powerful and could help you out or teach you something, I don't feel it's worthwhile taking the effort to meet them or to actually try and understand their ways enough that they would teach you gifts. I also imagine that anything that Bugs Bunny tries to teach you has a very good chance of killing you, simply because he wouldn't be able to understand your biology doesn't conform to cartoon physics.

                    There are multiple things feeding on the concept that is organised abrahamic religion in Werewolf, the most likely thing you can point to is The Patriarch, a mighty Weaver spirit and representation of dogmatic organised religion.

                    There are a lot of examples in the books of what makes a spirits powerful and what does not and we can discuss these examples, but at the end of the day the final arbiter will be the ST at the table. The TV Realms wouldn't be a place of powerful spirits with great impact on the world in my games, they could be crucial in yours.

                        It's also important to note that people don't think Star Wars is real. The setting is fictional. The characters in it are fictional. Its technology is fictional. People can love the stories, but everybody knows none of it is real.

                        On the other hands, money is very real even though you can argue its a fictional concept that only exists because we believe it exists. Having a medium of exchange, unit of account, and store of value is a very useful thing. Whether it is based on some commodity or is fiat, people can see the money in their hand. And even as we head into the digital age where money is increasingly not something we physically have, but is a result of our credit or debit cards, they can see their value on some screen. Nobody ever acts like it is fake. Everybody acts as if it is real and meaningful. And have for thousands of years.

                        Likewise if you start talking about the old pagan gods. While few believe in them now, at some point in the past you had thousands or millions of people truly believing in them for centuries and millennia. They just didn't think they were stories. People thought Zeus or Odin or Lugh were real. People prayed to them, sacrificed to them, built structures to them, devoted themselves to them in cults, etc. That is all significantly different behavior than even people performing cosplay, collecting memorabilia, and any other fan activity.

                        What about things that children may believe are true, but adults don't? I think here you are approaching concepts better fit for Changeling, but obviously there can be some element of crossover in any setting. And traditionally the Werewolf game has chosen to treat such chimerical creatures as if they were spirits.

                        To me, concepts that people know are fictional, would limit them to the imagination realms of the Dream Zone. Concepts that believe are real end up manifesting in the Astral Plane somehow, and some of these end up crossing over into the Middle Umbra which Garou can then theoretically contact. Exactly how Epiphlings, Weaver spirits, and their resultant Incarna are formed and interact is not laid out, so any ST will need to decide many things on their own.

                            Originally posted by voidshaper View Post
                            That depends on the type of Spirit. Most Spirits that inhabit a Realm are Emanations, a special type of Spirit that is created as part of the Realm itself. Technically, anything created within a Realm is an Emanation, including objects. While the Emanation of Superman may have super powers within its Realm, it does not have Charms or work at all like the Spirits you would encounter in the Spirit Wilds. A Luke Skywalker Emanation can't teach you to use the Force, at least not for outside its umbral bubble.

                            If it helps, think of umbral Realms as Star Trek holodecks with the safety turned off. Emanations are everything the holodeck creates for its simulation. Once you change the simulation (step into another Realm, Zone, or the Penumbra), the rules change and anything you take with you has to follow those new rules. Once you leave the holodeck (cross the Gauntlet), stuff vanishes.

                            I think there are certain Arts for Changelings that allow them to take things out of the Dreaming into the material world, but I'm pretty sure it's not that easy for Shapechangers. At least I can't think of a Gift on top of my head that would allow this.

                            Rarely, there are physical objects that somehow found their way into umbral Realms and can be brought back. These tend to be fetishes or magic materials and are meant as quest goals- Pathstones are a good example.
                            This work a bit differently for the Dreaming. In the Dreaming you can also pretty much everything that you can find in all the rest of the Umbra. But the Chimerae there can actually be brought to the physical world - and yes, you could, in theory, bring in Sauron's The One Ring to the physical world, the only thing you'd need (besides finding the LotR inside the Dreaming, finding the Chimerical Sauron and stealing from him - good luck) is calling the Wyrd, bringing the Dreaming temporarily to the material plane. There's also a few arts that allows them to materialize those.

                            Bringing the Wyrd could also bring the Chimerae Superman in.
