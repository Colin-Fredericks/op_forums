    While I AM aware there ARE Such things as kind and loving Feminists (and Black Furies), I will NOT stay silent thanks to Feminism destroying my Kind Elderly Dad's Life and Happiness.

    I'm NOT sorry; little boys abused by sick old maids deserve JUST as much justice as little girls abused by sick old men; and if that's a "trigger" to a Virginia Woolfe Lover? Well too-f#cking bad!!

    So

    Who here ELSE believes that the Black Furies will have farms of Young "Studs" (which is the Garou slang for "Mare", IE. no purpose in life but to breed) who can also double as *literal* punching-bags and whipping-boys for bitter old witches whose first change is triggered by Daddy not buying her that pony at sweet 16? The ones who are much more anti-male are likely to treat male kinfolk much worse than other camps.
