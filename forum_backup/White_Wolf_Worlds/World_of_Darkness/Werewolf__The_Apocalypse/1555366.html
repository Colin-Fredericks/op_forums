      This could be a very long list depending on what you want out of them... it's not like you need Gifts to be a cult leader. The vast majority of social Gifts are going to have at least some niche application to starting or running a charismatic cult. Broadly the Ragabash have some fantastic gaslighting/indoctrination Gifts if you couple them with the right social skills, the Philodox have a lot of leadership/authority Gifts, and the Galliards get the best group influence stuff of the Auspices (oddly some good ways to spy on your cult members without them knowing it too). Of course a Theurge can also use their spirit oriented social Gifts to get spirits to do all sorts of useful things for keeping the cult in line (again, spying on normal humans means having knowledge of things they think are secret, as well as minor non-rule breaking displays of power).

      Something perhaps a bit out of left field but worth considering: In the W20 Kinfolk book, Kin rituals are included that could very easily be used as a model for cult rituals that help make the cult more self-reinforcing and less reliant on active manipulation by the leader.

              1) So, what I was aiming for, as a bit more of narrative or style ideas around this character. While charismatic cults generally manipulate the same core human weaknesses, there's a lot of variations on them, and very importantly each leader is their own individual as well. The best way to create a decent list is to narrow down on these things. My Gift list for a fictional Keith Raniere would look different from one for Charles Manson. Dazzle (CoG Gift) would be a low priority for me for Raniere because that sort of transcendent experience wasn't really part of Nxivm, but was core to Manson's Family. Similarly a secular-style cult like Nxivm has no need for Gifts that can help pull of little "miracles" because they're not sold as overtly spiritual. Cults that promote a unique spirituality, or are sold as a schism from a mainstream religion are going to add priority to the ability to generate plausibility to supernatural claims. Mercy or Jam Weapon (yes, the Children of Gaia list is full of goodies for this topic) are great if you want to show off that your followers can be immune to harm if faithful and loyal enough to earn your blessings; not just you yourself.

              2) Well, as a dive into some real world examples... Jim Jones is probably a wonderful example of how potent Liar's Face can be in the wrong hands. A lot of people that having studied The People's Temple struggle to wrap their heads around how Jones got so many people to drink poison. The simple fact is that he lied to them, repeatedly, as a form of social control. If you can lie to someone who knows your lying to them, and they still obey? You've beaten a cult leader's greatest foe: doubt. Not by something like Persuasion or Dazzle to turn people into fanatical believers over time, but by winning despite doubt still ringing in the follower's ears. Getting them to discard their doubt despite still having it is far more potent than pretty much anything else in terms of human manipulation. So Jones regularly - during is "white night" ritual - told his followers they had to drink poison, and then it was a lie and it wasn't actually poison, just a test. Eventually he escalated it. Despite everyone knowing Jonestown didn't have adequate medical resources and their diet sucked because they really were not prepared to start a large agricultural commune, suddenly the poison drinks were "real" but the loyal and faithful were protected and only those that weren't proper members would show signs of illness; so you didn't just drink the "poison" you hid your symptoms from poor health care and minor malnutrition so you didn't get labelled a traitor. Etc.

              Ultimately the same thing is going to play out when looking and more Gifts. Yes, the core principles of running a cult are nearly universal (hence Persuasion will always be a duh pick), but the specifics also matter. What makes a cult leader feel powerful is going to direct how they demonstrate power, and how the cult is structure to enable that feedback cycle. The best Gifts to use are going to stem from there.

                  It's also important to emphasize how much minor, low level effects are important, precisely because they're subtle.

                  A figure that can enter a room full of actual skeptics and make them bowl in adoration with a hand gesture will only get the wrong kind of attention doing that. And for followers, it's overkill.

                  Cult leaders have on their side regularity and control, and weaponize incremental steps. Scientology begins talking about believable Psychology-looking stuff, then go to slightly weird measuring machines for sciency-sounding phenomena, and years down the line you're believing you are an alien reincarnated as the result of a galactic war. A power you can use every day inconspicuously and build from there is far more valuable than more impressive feats.

                          Setting aside that this will most likely be as similar to a full time job as it gets for a Werewolf, cult leaders don't take all that time to begin with.

                          And more importantly, having "better" gifts isn't what will save you time.

                          Cult leaders control as much as possible of their followers' lives and environment, but that doesn't have to be by themselves all the time. In fact it can't be. Most of it boils down to developing the system and putting the effort to make it start, then you let it run and just make appearances from time to time to enforce things.

                          What makes a cult a challenge to run is how much you have to decide and manage. This isn't an organization you belong to, this is an organization you're running. Even then, not particularly cumbersome that it would make it hard for the character to do PC stuff.

                          Overall running a cult is dedication and a few Ability rolls. You get out of it as much as you put in. Gifts will help to speed things up in the beginning and deal with some situations, but this idea that they'll save you time... just won't happen. It won't be particularly time consuming, will be moderately complex, and Gifts won't really change that.

                              There's also the simple fact that the vast majority of Gifts aren't unique to a specific set of splats. A standard Fianna Gift is just some XP cheaper for Fianna than everyone else. The number of Gifts that are outright exclusive tend to be so in a more narrative fashion (very specific spirits that don't come to you, you have to go them, teach them as an example) than in a raw statement by the rules (IIRC Revised's Book of Auspices was the only book that had Gifts that flat out only worked if you were the appropriate character type).

                              If there are 10 Gifts you really want your cult leader character to get... one splat selection could get you 8 of them in-splat, and another might only get you 3. Obviously in a purely XP mechanical incentive analysis, the one that gets you 8 is "better." But that's looking at things from a very narrow lens of what you want out of a character. An NPC that doesn't buy things with XP certainly doesn't have to care about having a few out-of-splat Gifts to round out their bonuses.

                              A Glasswalker Theurge and a Fianna Ragabash only have a different style if they choose to pursue things along a divergent paths. Depending on the paths involved one might have an "easier" time via XP breaks... but they could also be equally aimed at the same goals. If a Get Ahroun and a Silver Fang Philodox both want to be good at winning combat challenges at traditionalist Moots, they're going to converge on a very common set of traits. The "style" is externally foisted on them by what's considerable acceptable in Garou judicial dueling. They idea that they have to diverge because of their splats doesn't hold.

                                  You need merits to get the build to an optimal level: Animal Magnetism (Humans), Born Leader, Insightful, Language, Mitanu’s Tongue, Natural Leader, Natural Linguist, Notable Heritage (Humans), Pitiable, Supporter, and if using a Kitsune you need Sex Appeal.


                                  Regarding gifts for a cult leader:


                                  Level 1
                                  Persuasion no resource usage, a -1 general difficulty reduction is great. Low investment.

                                  Visage of Fenris against humans is -1 difficulty to Social checks, it should stack with Persuasion, very strong for a Cult leader.

                                  Infectious Laughter is great for when you screw up. Good to have, but not a priority for the Cult leader.

                                  Liar's Face is resource intensive (Willpower), works on humans no roll, unlikely to work on supers. Also Liar's Face does not seems to be limited by the number of listeners so it is cost effective, pairs very well with Persuasion. Mid investment, mid return.

                                  Seduction ​is even more resource intensive (Gnosis), works only on humans, it seems only affects a single target, but looks to be permanent. So high investment, high return.​ This one is Kitsune only.





                                  Level 2
                                  Command the Gathering for being the center of attention.

                                  Dreamspeak to modify even more your cultists behaviour, Inception style.

                                  Empathy to get information with no interaction.

                                  Global Citizen is incredible value specially the final benefit of the gift: spend 1 point of Gnosis to get (Wisdom) points of Backgrounds, Allies and Contacts

                                  Speech of the World for translation​ at lower XP cost than Speech of All Things.





                                  Level 3
                                  Mental Speech would be great to control the cult, and many other uses.

                                  Speech of All Things probably the best communication gift for humans, everywhere.






                                  Fetishes I am less familiar:

                                  Enchanting Fan is Kitsune but it is good value for money, +1 to all Social rolls.

                                  Friendship Ring is Children of Gaia, +1 to all Social rolls against a single target.

                                  Loki's Fire increases you Manipulation by 2 for a scene.

                                  Truth Earring makes it harder for you to be lied and deceived (everyone gets +3 difficulty to do either).
