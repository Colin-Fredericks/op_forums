                                        Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
                                        Something to consider in this: Paradox had to pay for these things. It's unclear if they've even made that money back yet with the positive revenue they're generating. As happens in business, you can be in the red despite selling things for a profit.
                                        A very good point, by the way.

                                        We have only their word for it, but they said White Wolf cost "tens of millions" in Swedish Krona. At the time the Krona was about USD 0,12, so it was "many millions" in US dollars.

                                        So they need to collect payback over a huge amount. And with time this amount increases in relation to what they need to say it was worth it.

                                        I run some quick calculations on discounted payback period for this. Assuming a 2 million purchase and an Opportunity Cost + Risk at just 10% (a good point for a solid, non-volatile investment, which is not the case), they would need a net revenue of 600 thousands every year to get payback in 5 years. At 5 million initial investment it would have to be a net revenue around 1,4 million every year. At a more reasonable risk expectation putting the Opportunity Cost at 20%, the first scenario needs 700.000/year and the second 1,7M/year, both with less profit at the end of the fifth year.

                                        Making less than that is bad, really bad, because at this scale a 10% return a year is considered good, but nothing stellar, for a long-term low-risk investment, and this is 5 years for discounted payback.

                                        Of course, this is a very shallow analysis still, done without a lot of financial details, and we don't know how much their net revenue is annually (or at all) from the WoD IP alone. But it gives a notion of the volume needed to break even with the whole project within five years. The more time passes, the less any revenue helps because of the discount.
