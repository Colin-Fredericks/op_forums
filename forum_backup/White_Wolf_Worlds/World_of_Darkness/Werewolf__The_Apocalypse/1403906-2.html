                                                I feel the lost of individuality is a point focused on too much by the community. Yes, Drones are heavily influenced by the network they are part of, but a full removal of autonomy only occurs in specific situations (most of them circumstantial).

                                                Drones are not formed by life experiences or bad childhoods, they are purpose built for a task or job. Drones are humans (or otherwise) that are integrated with, and modified by, a greater intelligence that views them as we would maintenance equipment.

                                                They only have what is needed is fulfill their purpose; nothing is taken unless it hinders operation, and nothing is added unless it aids operation. Interests might be removed if they hinder the job. Vices or habits may be added it they assist the job. If making you a fully controlled automaton is necessary to fulfill your purpose then so be it (this is unlikely though, as humans having autonomy is the main point of creating drones in the first place; an autonomous tool is usefull).

                                                They are not mindless slaves, or zombies. They are exactly what the Weaver needs them to be at any given time; a flexible part of the system needed for the machine/system/weave to function properly.

                                                    Two and a half years? Nice.

                                                    Drones are purpose-built by the OneSong... and since they can't have Taints, all of that comes out of the host's sense of self. To the One Song, of course, that's a bonus; it doesn't actually want humans with autonomy, in the mechanical sense or otherwise, because that would mean that the drone wasn't doing what it wanted the drone to do. The OneSong likely values Autonomy only inasmuch as it's fuel for fashioning its tools.

                                                    And we outright do have an example of a drone trying to exert individuality and basically internally self-destructing for his trouble in Possessed. The integration with the Weaver-spirit is not even remotely even, and if it's beneficial in some fashion, that's not really intended. The only intention is to help the Weaver act like the Infosphere from Futurama, in an admittedly very tiny and convoluted fashion.
