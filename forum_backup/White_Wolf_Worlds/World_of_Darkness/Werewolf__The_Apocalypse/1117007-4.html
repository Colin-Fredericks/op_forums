            I think you miss the big part of Werewolf the Apocalypse if you start with the idea the Garou haven't failed their mission and failed it completely. The Apocalypse is on the rise and the Garou can't do anything about it because they were too selfish, evil, and racist to overcome their flaws.

            It's less about how you win the Apocalypse than how you die.

            Because you may be an honorable warrior of Gaia and protector of the week but the Garou next to you murdered a baby in a frenzy and ignores the Child Molestor spirit to figure out how he can get to Rank 3.

            They haven't "failed" if there is even one standing to fight the fight. The point isn't winning, as you say, it's fighting. The point isn't that they aren't corrupt, it's that the heroic individual can choose to be better than that. It's a game of *personal* horror, because you can swing wildly from being Rage filled monster to being Heroic warrior. Both sides can exist within you, but to ignore that is to miss a huge chunk of what makes the game special. *shrug* Play your game how you like it, but I choose to play as a hopeful character, and I always let my players make a personal difference in their world, even if it's not a global difference.
