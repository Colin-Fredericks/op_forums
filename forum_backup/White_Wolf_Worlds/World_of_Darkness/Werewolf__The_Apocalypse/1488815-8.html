            Also... Justin has never been shy about being willing to put his vision over the desires of the fans, for better or worse. While it is good for a lead creative to have the confidence to take steps that fans might have a kneejerk negative reaction to, his track record with having the fans come around to it having been a good idea isn't that great. And if you're going to break the mold, you do need to make a new one people actually want.

            His work on VtM Rev, VtR 1e, and H5, all show what is seen as something of is design trademark: do what I say, not what I do. VtM Rev was all about getting the players back to basics, and Justin's ST advice is largely regarded as dripped with condescension towards players that enjoyed the more gonzo style VtM 2e inspires (and VtM didn't actually mechanically do anything to stop), while the metaplot was brimming with Justin-guided gonzo NPCs doin gonzo adventures that changed the WoD forever while you were supposed to be happy spending a year's worth of play to rise to Clan Whip or some similar low level position. VtR 1e failed utterly to capture a lot of VtM fans because it was too much a have measure. It didn't do enough to get away from VtM to be its own thing, or hew closer to VtM to be a more direct reboot. And it still had that classic "it will take you years to hope to hold a candle to these NPCs" Justin style to it. H5 (and to an extent also V5 with the Sabbat even if Justin seemed more OK with Paradox's desires than wanting it himself at first) is a giant middle finger to HtR 1e - like VtM Rev. tried to give parts of VtM 2e - with nothing to replace it but the same stuff that was supposedly bad... just walled off as ST only material now.


            Paradox and Justin might not be out to "shit" on anything. But Justin is very willing, and Paradox seems perfectly happy to have him for this exact reason, to piss off fans for the integrity of his design goals.

                    In that context, let's not forget what H5 did. The Imbued weren't just not the focus of H5, they were written out of the game. No acknowledgment of the past WOD-canon whatsoever. The book says "a bomb-building anarchist, a disabled veteran arch-patriot, and an ex-cop who was discharged from the force for disciplinary issues, or a different org that includes a folk guitarist, a morally flexible hacker, and a retired postal worker" are also examples for untrustworthy hunter-organizations. One of the main issues of H5 in my opinion is that it's really hard to figure out what the authors think good Hunters are supposed to be considering how many types are deemed bad/lesser versions by the book.

                    After that, you gotta wonder: What does it look like when you do something like that with W5..?

                                                        And to reiterate something:

                                                        There actually isn't a lot of grognard-ism around here. The vast majority of people on this forum that are vocally against the WoD5 stuff are so because we wanted a new edition and so far have gotten one that is highly unappealing (though no new edition could ever equally appeal to all of us, the breadth of difference in this group says a lot about the lack of overall appeal to the existing TTRPG fans).

                                                        TTRPG grognards are broadly defined as people that have become attached to an edition of the game that is their personal "one true edition," and reject any others because that attachment automatically precludes any new edition from being considered as anything but less than. I'd wager at least 75% of those being painted as grognards about this here would have happily switched to WoD5 if Paradox had left it in OPP hands and we got something close to Exalted Essence or Storypath as a way to modernize and simplify the mechanics of the game, with OPP's generally better understanding of writing about mature topics with sensitivity than Paradox has managed to make standard practice so far to help modernize the setting of the WoD.
