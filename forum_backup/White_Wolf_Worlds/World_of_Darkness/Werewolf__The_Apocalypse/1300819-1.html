    I never said it has more than one, I was looking for ways to mitigate the worst ones. Basically, I am considering a mixed game, in which some idiots are trying to create an army of Metis, eliminating drawbacks, with the aim of creating an artificial Perfect Metis.

    Also, a bit of metagaming with Metis deformities, to optimize for combat. The best ones (FOR COMBAT) would be Hirsute, and Tough Hide. The latter more so than the former.
    Creating an army of metis is more costly than an army of lupus. For one, you need like 50 female garou, hope they don't DIE during childbirth or become infertile. Then you have to hope the deformities of the metis don't make them useless. Then you better hope any of the female garou don't try to tear the baby off/kill themselves during the 9 month long pregnancy. Then you have 10 years to raise the ragy baby before they can be used in an army.

    There is a reason BSDs don't solely rely on their metis numbers.

    A faster and a more reliable way to get garou shock troops would be to get some lupus garou, some wolf kinfolk and just breed them until garou cubs pop out. They take 2 years to mature, the pregnancy lasts 2 months and they can still breed.

I ended up making a character to show what happens if you optimize for combat using a W20 starting build with a Totem (Hummingbird) and a a Sept Totem (Wolverine) from W20 Rage Across the World, p.29:

                        Ugly McAngry
                        Breed: Metis
                        Auspice: Ahroun
                        Tribe: Children of Gaia
                        Nature: Competitor
                        Demeanor: Big Bad Wolf
                        Rank: 1
                        Physical: Strength 4 (6/8/7/5) (Claws), Dexterity 7 (7/8/9/9) (Dodging), Stamina 2 (4/5/5/4)
                        Social: Charisma 4 (Gnosis), Manipulation 1 (0/0/0/0), Appearance 0 (0/0/0/0)
                        Mental: Perception 2, Intelligence 1, Wits 5 ((Combat Reflexes))
                        Talents: Alertness 3, Athletics 3, Brawl 5 (Claws), Intimidation 1, Primal-Urge 3
                        Skills: Etiquette 1, Stealth 3, Survival 1
                        Knowledges: Academics 1, Enigmas 3, Investigation 1, Law 1, Medicine 1, Occult 1, Rituals 1
                        Backgrounds: Fetish 4 (Soul of Lightning), Totem 1 (Hummingbird)
                        Rage: 6
                        Gnosis: 3
                        Willpower: 10
                        Glory: 2
                        Honor: 1
                        Wisdom: 0
                        Metis deformity: Eyes of Darkness, Tough Hide
                        Gifts: (1) Razor Claws, Resist Pain, Sense Wyrm; (2) Spirit of the Fray
                        Rites: None
                        Merits: Berserker, Mars Midsky, Self-Confident
                        Flaws: Double Jeopardy, Impatient, Monstrous


                        All freebies spent on Berserker, Brawl, Mars Midsky, Self-Confident, and Willpower; this fellow can:
                        • Win Initiative most of the time. Max Dexterity + Max Wits + Spirit of the Fray
                        • Attack several times per turn. Max Rage + Wolverine
                        • Hit enemies consistently. Max Dexterity + Max Brawl + Specialty
                        • Do large amounts of damage. High Strength + Claws + Razor Claws + Soul of Lightning + Mars Midsky + Specialty + Attack roll bonus
                        • Get lots of automatic successes. Max Willpower + Self-Confident
                        • Ignore most forms of mind control. Max Willpower
                        • Recover Willpower after each fight. Competitor
                        • Get 2 extra Dexterity dots and the gift: Spirit of the Fray. Hummingbird
                        • Get 1 extra Stamina dot and a Rage dot that can never be lost. Wolverine
                        • Stun enemies when doing at least 4 points of damage (after soak). Soul of Lightning
                        • Avoid ambushes by Wyrm tainted creatures. Primal-Urge + Sense Wyrm
                        • Regenerate in every form. Metis
                        • Assume Crinos form instantly. Metis
                        • Enter a Berserk Frenzy at will, ignoring wound penalties and gaining +2 soak and +1 Strength die. Berserker + Mars Midsky​​
                        • Soak a regular amount of damage. Low Stamina + Tough Hide + Mars Midsky​
                        • Ignore all wound penalties for a scene. Resist Pain
                        • See perfectly in darkness. Eyes of Darkness
                        • Cheap access to some of the best gifts: (1) Falling Touch, Jam Weapon, Mother’s Touch, Razor Claws, Resist Pain, Sense Wyrm; (2) Burrow, Form Mastery, Luna’s Armor, Grandmother’s Touch, Spirit of the Fray, True Fear; (3) Calm the Savage Beast, Chameleon, Combat Healing, Dazzle, Mental Speech, Rage of the Underdog, Shell, Silver Claws, The Cleaving Hoof; (4) Full Moon's Light, Gift of the Porcupine, Lash of Rage, Rattler's Bite, Stoking Fury’s Furnace, Strike the Air, Tenacious Fury; (5) Halo of the Sun, Kiss of Helios, Protean Form, Strength of Will, Totem Gift; (6) Unstoppable Warrior. Metis + Ahroun + Children of Gaia
                        • Buy future traits as cheaply as possible.
                        And now I just wonder if is there any way to mitigate whatever weaknesses the character might have, FROM CHARACTER CREATION. I am aware that CWOD is not D&D.

                                        It's a very min-maxed character that's brutal in close combat.

                                        Ways to ruin their day:

                                        1) Multiple enemies (Rage burns quick even with Primal Anger which just makes you easier to beat).

                                        2) Enemies at range (so they don't get murdered up close, and forces spending Rage to close).

                                        3) Has Rage > Gnosis and 10 dice on Frenzy checks. Wait for a full moon, piss them off and watch them murder their pack and/or allies (if in the Umbra and his Totem is around for a personal Totem build.... their Totem spirit is going to be super pissed off too), since 6+ successes means they can't spend WP to avoid the Frenzy and goes full Thrall.

                                        4) Force them into any sort of social or mental challenges and watch them fail, get angry, Frenzy, and get put down by thei Sept. He might look really scary to go after with a stare down contest, but odds are they'll Frenzy and lose. They also automatically fail game-craft challenges.

                                        5) They can't go out in public with Rage like that, so any confrontation around normal people is going to add lots of cannon fodder to the mix to drain that WP and Rage.

                                        6) Lots of mind whammy powers don't have Willpower as a difficulty and thus can mess this build up hard.

                                                    Originally posted by Penelope View Post
                                                    lbeaumanior that is a REALLY powerful character!
                                                    Just in close quarters combat. It is literally impossible to optimize an starting character against everything. The advantage of Werewolves is that a lot of their powers apply to the whole pack, synergy covering a lot of weak points.


                                                    To optimize an starting character to beat all comers, I would have gone with a Mage, because prepared mages are pretty close to invincible. That being said Heavy Arms, I did ask for ways to mitigate weaknesses and I will try to tackle those you mentioned, plus I have edited the original post to make it less imbalanced but still brutal in close combat:



                                                    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
                                                    It's a very min-maxed character that's brutal in close combat.

                                                    Ways to ruin their day:

                                                    1) Multiple enemies (Rage burns quick even with Primal Anger which just makes you easier to beat).
                                                    True, but a Pack should give you multiple allies. There is no starting build that is able to defeat a sufficiently large number of enemies, at most withdraw, and in that spirit, I add Mirrorshades to the build while removing Primal Anger. And since we are assuming a Pack, I will only use a single dot of Totem for its benefits.

                                                    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
                                                    2) Enemies at range (so they don't get murdered up close, and forces spending Rage to close).
                                                    Changed the totem to Hummingbird, for the Spirit of the Fray and go first, and switched Resist Pain for Jam Weapon, so weapon-wielding enemies are forced into hand to hand combat. Added Acute Sense 3 (Hearing, Sight, Smell) and Sense Wyrm to detect some enemies before they appear.

                                                    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
                                                    3) Has Rage > Gnosis and 10 dice on Frenzy checks. Wait for a full moon, piss them off and watch them murder their pack and/or allies (if in the Umbra and his Totem is around for a personal Totem build.... their Totem spirit is going to be super pissed off too), since 6+ successes means they can't spend WP to avoid the Frenzy and goes full Thrall.
                                                    Keeping Rage at 5 plus using the Foul Temper flaw (to diminish the rage build-up) helps with this, and leaves enough freebies to tackle other problems.

                                                    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
                                                    4) Force them into any sort of social or mental challenges and watch them fail, get angry, Frenzy, and get put down by thei Sept. He might look really scary to go after with a stare down contest, but odds are they'll Frenzy and lose. They also automatically fail game-craft challenges.
                                                    The character is not going to fail ALL mental or social challenges, max Wits and high Charisma still provide good traits for Talent rolls, but I will change the social stats; let's redistribute the Skills and Knowledges dots, remove Harano Prone, and add the merits Concentration to reduce penalties, Lucky to defend against failures and Self-Confident, to get more bang-per-buck from spending Willpower dots specially in challenging situations.

                                                    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
                                                    5) They can't go out in public with Rage like that, so any confrontation around normal people is going to add lots of cannon fodder to the mix to drain that WP and Rage.
                                                    See points 3 and 4.

                                                    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
                                                    6) Lots of mind whammy powers don't have Willpower as a difficulty and thus can mess this build up hard.
                                                    With Lucky and Self-Confident, more chances to survive any mind power, either a flat reroll or spending Willpower to succeed.



                                                    Assuming the Pack helps carry the cost of the Totem (Hummingbird) and that a Sept Totem (Polar Bear) is used:

                                                    Auspice: Ahroun
                                                    Tribe: Children of Gaia
                                                    Nature: Competitor
                                                    Demeanor: Soldier

                                                    Social: Charisma 4 (Imposing), Manipulation 1 (0/0/0/0), Appearance 1 (0/0/1/1)
                                                    Mental: Perception 2, Intelligence 1, Wits 5 (Combat Reflexes)
                                                    Talents: Alertness 3, Athletics 3, Brawl 3, Intimidation 1, Primal-Urge 3
                                                    Skills: Etiquette 1, Stealth 3, Survival 1
                                                    Knowledges: Academics 1, Enigmas 3, Investigation 1, Law 1, Medicine 1, Occult 1, Rituals 1
                                                    Backgrounds: Ancestors 5, Totem 1 (Hummingbird)
                                                    Rage: 5

                                                    Merits: Acute Sense 3 (Hearing, Sight, Smell), Ancestor Ally, Concentration, Lucky, Self-Confident, Supporter
