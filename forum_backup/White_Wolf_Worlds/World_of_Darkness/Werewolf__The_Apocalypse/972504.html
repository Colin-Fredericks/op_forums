    Penetrating a Correspondance Ward ?

    09-19-2016, 10:20 AM
    A mage has set up a ward on a small farm house that prevents all entry except for those who have been ''whitelisted.'', M20 states that Werecreatures are able to penetrate said Correspondence ward by rolling wits + occult at a difficulty of 6 or the mage's arete, whatever is higher and the dice is limited to their Rage/Gnosis, whatever is higher. i come from a community where mages pump their wards with 40+ successes, and as a werecreature, how am i supposed to beat 40+ successes with only 7 dice to roll against it ? thus making the task impossible to achieve by anyone other than a Mage, if that is so, then why is their explanations on how Werecreatures/Vampires could penetrate said ward while it's impossibe? are the book authors under the impression that a Mage will not exceed 5+ successes on a ward ? or is it practically impossible to penetrate a Correspondence ward that has been stacked with heavy successes like 30+ ? Werecreatures are not able to perform extended rolls to countermagick it either, and is it possible for 5-7 wolves to gather up and combine their successes to achieve a enough successes to penetrate a ward together? or is that just not possible ?

                  You're talking about stacking successes without a ritual.

                  If I recall right, there are Gifts to tear through wards. Books also say that wards are quite effective ways of defense due to their mechanics (and as such there are quotes to that), and there are rules for crossover in there too.

                  As for your question, you can sidestep and by-pass the said ward, unless Spirit 4 was used on top of that as well and the threshold was set in the Umbra too.

                  In that case, you can try teleporting inside. If it was protected against teleportation too with additional successes, then --

                  Seek what can disrupt threshold. If it's set on the ground in a form of "line" or "circle", think about it - maybe you can do something about the ground where ward stands? If it's set on the item, try to affect the said item so it's out of the way. If you, for whatever reason cannot access threshold (though I have hard time imagining why wouldn't you be able to), then --

                  Try seeking whoever can access the warded area. If you cannot (or you ran out of options), then --

                  Get another mage to disrupt the ward + do your own countermagick on top. If that's not an option as well, then --

                  You could try getting there from above. Setting up the ward needs to have a threshold, and the air isn't a stable enough threshold, thus it's quite challenging to make it "above".

                  And if I guessed it right who you are, I doubt that whoever you're facing against is as knowledged and versed in mage ways to get certain ideas and manage to set the ward on the air and make it legitimate as well (well if you actually are who I think you are, don't mind my past mistake with rituals, you're getting a response after all and quite few ways).

                  Good luck in whatever you're doing.

                                                          Dunno', I figured it would be actually quite legitimate. Of course, you can't just "jump" over warded line (though, if you can jump really high, maybe you can) because wards require threshold, always.

                                                          And that's where the trick comes in. How far can you stretch your ward and what kind of a threshold you can access to? Wards are indeed very strong and the system favors Mages excessively, but if you cannot let other supernaturals counter them through system (or you can, but ruling out that anyone can just keep stacking countermagick successes makes wards very ineffective, and it basically means that other supernaturals tear down wards easier than other Mages who are actually the magick guys), why not let them counter the wards the other way if they have enough wit?

                                                          A mage has travelling ward on him? Seek whatever the mage possesses, be it clothes or some iron item, or whatever. The rule of "Pressing the Ward" will apply, and you should attack the Mage with what he has in order to by-pass his protection.

                                                          A mage has set protection over some area? Seek your way around threshold, burrow and go underneath, go through Umbra, or even go from above (though one problem with it is the fact that if Mage turns out smart enough, he could set the threshold even above. Note, air isn't solid enough to be threshold, so it can get quite challenging to pull off and ensure it's legit)
