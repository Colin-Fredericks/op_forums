    First, it's important to remember that the Triat are vast cosmic entities that are highly detached from the reality of human-scale thinking and thus don't really go around trying to explain things (unlike Gaia and the Celestines who might be vast but have maintained more personal connections to smaller entities).

    From that, the books have repeatedly made it clear that nobody really knows anything other than exceptional broad and practically tautological aspects of them. There are multiple interpretations and so on among the various creatures of the WoD and even in WtA there is plenty of disagreement between a number of groups on how the Triat is both supposed to work, and how it's actually working now.

    From the traditionalist Gaian aligned perspective, the biggest issue with your statements on this is the use of the word "purpose." The Triat don't deal in things like "purpose." They are forces that do according to their nature. Gaia is the origin of purpose in the cosmology named after her; since that cosmology assumes that the universal aspect of Gaia is above the Triat instead of others that put her as equal or even below them in the hierarchy of things. In this cosmology (which most WtA characters are going to default to) the Wyld creates chaotically, the Weaver takes that and gives it structure and stability so it can operate after the Wyld moves on to its next creation, and the Wyrm destroys to avoid the universe getting clogged up with the output of the other two.

    The second most common take is that the pre-fall Balance Wyrm was a moderating force between the chaos of the Wyld and the order of the Weaver and it destroyed what was necessary to keep the universe in a healthy cycle. The distinct is slight but the Destroyer Wyrm is indiscriminate that cares more about a "quota" of elimination, where the Balance Wyrm is more intentional in destroying specific things based on the cosmic impact achieved.
