    Yeah, I know. But the tenet's removal and the way the litany is treated in general shows the idea of it being discussed and debated is just ignored. Looking at V5, the traditions were discussed and explained there, so there isn't even a precedent for this.
    It's fascinating to go back to the Discord Q&A with Justin Achilli and hear him talk about it:

    How does the Litany look as of right now?
    Eleven tenets, cutting the ones about who you're allowed to fuck and the attitude you're supposed to have about "disability." Much emphasis on the Litany being abused by outsized personalities, and the inherent hypocrisies and gray areas in it. Big takeaway is "If this Litany is so great, why are we living in the Apocalypse?"
    Of course, with "much emphasis" he actually meant "that's all that will be talked about". And the game never really did find an answer to the question of "If Garou are so great, why are we living in the Apocalypse?" beyond just stating over and over again that Garou suck.

    Also, it was always a weird move to first remove bits from the Litany because they were considered "bad" and then still make the "big takeaway" that the Litany is bad. If you're just gonna say that the Litany is bad anyway, why remove anything...? And of course, a very surface-level-reading there of those removed bits of the Litany while the eventual overall negative "big takeaway" completely ignores the bits of the Litany that are good.

                VtM is a game about being fundamentally selfish. Anything and everything exists only in the service of perpetuating your existence and nothing really has value beyond yourself. WtA is a game about protecting others and sacrificing yourself for a chance something better may come.

                                                PHASE THREE PART FOUR

                                                Character Generation Part One

                                                By default, all characters are now Homid. Since Gnosis has been removed as a mechanic, there is no different, or need, for three different Breeds. However, one can still create a Lupus character. There is. just no formal rules for doing so. It is recommended that certain Skills (i.e. Abilities) be restricted, but it doesn't have to be. So, if you want a starting Lupus character who is a stunt-driving, computer hacker, there is nothing in the rules preventing you from doing so. Technically.

                                                This also means that the Metis breed is no more. Unlike other terms, I guess there was too much baggage to just change the name. On the other hand, there is nothing anywhere in the rules suggesting what the offspring of two Garou are. It has been suggested that they are just regular humans/wolves with the same odds of undergoing a First Change. Or not, as the vagueness on Garou continues.

                                                [As an aside, my take would be the Hybrid Breed. This Breed is characterized by the features of animals other than wolves in the three supernatural forms, particularly Crinos. These features are almost always beneficial. There are many theories, such as spirit interference or a shapeshifter ancestor, but the only thing known is that a Hybris has at least one parent or grandparent who is the offspring of two Garou. While they are rarely in the forefront of battles, their real usefulness is in the fact that they have fairly high chance of having Garou children. The elite lineages try to keep this secret, as it could weaken the families.]

                                                Attributes and Skills are the same as those in V5. This isn't too different from the older editions. One note though, Primal-Urge and Rituals are missing, as the mechanics that were based on these two abilities have been altered. More on that later.

                                                The biggest changes have been to the Advantages. Many favorite Merits and Flaws are absent. It is unsure if any of the missing ones will ever return, since many of them would unbalance W5's mechanics. Backgrounds are very different. As Garou are no longer genetic, basically, there is no need for a Kinfolk BG. If one wants to represent a relative, it would be through Allies or the new Touchstone mechanic, and neither really reflect what Kinfolk were. Not having an ally such as this could end up hurting the game. Likewise, Pure Breed is now unnecessary. While there appears to be some sort of elite qualities, there is no mechanic to reflect this. Strangely enough, Ancestors, formerly Past Life, is also removed. With the strong hatred for knowing anything about the past, this seemed a fairly safe bet to be removed. As of yet, there is no information if such spirits exist. While mentioned in another section, a Status BG appears to have been left out. Maybe the designers were limited in the number of Backgrounds they could put in, and the Mask, formerly the Alternate Identity Merit, was more important. With the constant hatred towards elders, such a Background would be useful, especially since Rank has been removed.

                                                The biggest removal would be Rites. While the actual powers are still here, there is no secondary mechanic to go with it. This basically means that there is no power rating on Rites, as all are equal. This also means that there really isn't a rarity to Rites either. Beginning characters could start with Rites well beyond what they should be capable of. Even if PCs are exceptional, this might be takin things too far.

                                                THWarted

                                                            It was already pretty easy to imagine from the history of V5’s development that a significant contingent of the legacy fan base was going to take umbrage with what W5 put out if it was designed along similar principles.

                                                            That said, I do think I would have preferred, if one was going to put out a W5 product with similar design principles as V5 or H5, I would have liked to see the designers fully commit to a redesign of the setting to fit their principles.

                                                            I’m not saying that I would have preferred that product over legacy or that there wouldn’t still be controversy and debate, but at least in that case there would be a way to contrast the old and new materials against one another and see what one prefers. There are plenty of problems with the old material, problems that a lot of people acknowledge. Even just seeing ways that one might go about resolving the old material’s issues would have been interesting and in some cases might have seen certain ideas back-ported into legacy. For instance I know that if this hypothetical W5 had fully committed to a redesign of the setting and had included elements such as the Sambrano rework of Older Brother, Middle Brother, & Younger Brother; I probably would have back ported those things into my legacy settings, even if nothing else, and I don’t think I would have been the only one either.
