                    W20 Icons of Rage

                    One on One (Level 6 Unique Gift)

                    This is a very fitting Level 6 ability. Luna permits you the power to force yourself and a foe into a duel in which no one can interfere and nothing unsporting may take place. No escape, no illusions, no summoning help, nothing that would desecrate honourable combat. The Gift also teleports you to the target's location; so a Rank 6 Ahroun could say "Adrian Newberry", teleport into the CEO's penthouse, and then slaughter him while no spirits could intervene and no escape would be possible.

                    The downsides are that you can lose the fight, especially if you pick a target worthy of a Rank 6 Garou, that you burn a permanent point of Gnosis to activate the Gift, and that you are left standing where the enemy you teleported to was. So, with this Gift, you can travel to the heart of Malfeas and destroy one of the mighty Archdukes, but you're quite likely to be slaughtered in the moments that follow.

                    This is very solid, all in all, and it has an Absoluteness which is great for Gifts, and especially high level Gifts. Burning a Gnosis point is awful, but it highlights how serious a threat you need to be going after for this Gift to make sense. This is a Gift which could define the ending of a Chronicle quite easily... though personally I think it would work better if you put this ability into a unique Talen containing an aspect of Luna herself, created specifically to kill some threat to the Garou in ancient times, and then lost. Found again for the first time in centuries, is it what the players need to take down Villain McVillainface?

                    My other suggestion, if using this in a game, is to broaden it up a bit. The Talen or Totemic intervention or whatever brings the whole pack to face a suitable group. Both groups are aware that they will only be released when there's only one group standing, pack vs pack, pack vs swarm, you name it. That lets you include the whole table in an epic duel for the fate of a realm or nation, rather than putting one PC in a 1v1 with a single NPC.

                    W20 Howls of the Apocalypse

                    Techgnosis* (Level 2 Lupus Gift)

                    Spend 1 Gnosis, roll Wits + Primal-Urge (difficulty 7), and for the rest of the scene you can use your Primal-Urge instead of your Crafts, Drive, Etiquette, Firearms or Larceny. Spending 1 Willpower then broadens this list to include Academics, Computer, Law, Science and Technology.

                    The flavour of this one is very neat; with the aid of Gnosis you enhance your instincts and call upon whatever you're working with (a book, a gun, a tank) to work with your instincts, reaching out to 'help you out' as you try to intuit your way through things. Given that you have 5+ points of Gnosis and this Gift lasts for a whole scene, allowing you to go from 0 dice to perhaps 5 dice in a useful Skill/Knowledge... I like this a lot. The ability for a Lupus Garou to experiment and intuit their way through life by calling upon the power of their instincts, and not rote learning, feels really beautiful.

                    Note that this combines well with Heightened Senses, which gives you +1 to Primal-Urge for the scene. Pangaea also becomes a perfect place to craft stuff or do your homework, as you get +1 automatic success to all Primal-Urge rolls. If you can find a Totem or Fetish or Gift or Merit which helps with your Primal-Urge, granting more dice or successes or reducing the difficulty, absolutely go for it.

                    On the assumption that you invest in the right setup for this Gift to be useful, that you're Lupus or perhaps even Metis, that you have plenty of Gnosis, that your Primal-Urge is 4+, that you do what you can to boost it, and that you use this Gift cleverly... the potential to save you 100s of xp is amazing.

                    W20 Rage Across the World

                    Free Flowing (Level 1 Singing Dogs Gift)

                    This Gift is entirely passive and lets you move through the air at your top speed, so long as you have something to grab, climb, or navigate the space with. Examples given are vines, ledges, branches, windows, laundry lines, rooftops and so on.

                    So long as you aren't in a featureless void, this Gift is basically "move for free on your turn, instead of spending an action or Splitting to do so, and also you can move rapidly through the air, up and down surfaces and so on, without any rolls". This, as a utility option, falls just short of flight. It's passive, no roll, no cost, and you're spending 5xp to get it. This, in my opinion, would be a must-have on any Garou if you happen to pass through New Guinea or encounter the Singing Dogs (or their Totem) in the Umbra.

                    Singing Dog's Cry* (Level 3 Singing Dogs Gift)

                    This Gift allows you to touch someone from a great distance for the purposes of supernatural abilities. So, for example, you could use the song of this Gift to touch a foe and slam them with Falling Touch from miles away, or heal them with Mother's Touch.

                    The cost is 1 Gnosis, and a difficulty 7 Charisma + Expression is required. Every success lets you do a single 'touch' without having to activate the Gift again, so 1 Gnosis could become 5 touches if you roll 5 successes. You can also touch a bunch of targets all at once, in a single turn, by burning multiple successes at once, which might help with a Gift which impacts everyone you touch.

                    Mother's Touch, Falling Touch, Breath of the Wyld, Scent of Sweet Honey, Lover's Touch, Electroshock, Hidden Killer, Render Down, Living Treasure, Under the Gun, and various other Gifts will work with Singing Dog's Cry. Look at your Gifts, and the Gifts available to you, and be sure to ask your ST whether any of them can work via touch! Many Gifts don't specify their range or how you activate them, weirdly.

                    Global Citizen (Level 2 Homid Gift)

                    The Garou blends into different cultures, and inherently understands minor cultural traditions, faux pas, and quirks. To the untrained observer, she appears part of the group. Her skin and facial structure doesn’t change, but nobody seems to notice any difference in heritage.
                    +3 difficulty to any roll relating to realising you aren't part of the culture, or to convince others you're an outsider. If you encounter unfamiliar behaviour you can magically puzzle it out with an Intelligence + Etiquette, variable difficulty. Now while you could presumably figure out alien customs with Etiquette even without this Gift, the quality of the information you recover will be better; "this is a dominance hierarchy ritual, you MUST push back by showing how big and strong you are, perhaps in Crinos" is more useful than "well, 2 successes... he might be trying to see if he can successfully bully you? In some cultures you're meant to submit, in others you're meant to push back".

                    The final benefit of the Gift is arguably the best, however. You can spend 1 point of Gnosis to get (Wisdom) points of Backgrounds, Allies and Contacts, which are temporary but can be used normally. That's just great. You are now a Garou who can spend a point of Gnosis and come back to your pack going "alright, I spoke to the local doctors, vets, paramedics and nurses, and here's what's up with those dead homeless people-".

                    This outdoes Assimilation, a Level 5 Gift. Green.

                    Trapper's Bane (Level 2 Lupus Gift)

                    The Storyteller rolls Perception + Primal Urge when the werewolf comes within (Gnosis x 5) yards of a trap’s trigger. The difficulty is 5 for the werewolf to detect simple snares, pits, and poisoned bait in the area. Identifying more technologically complex traps such as electrified objects and motion-activated traps is difficulty 6. Supernatural traps are difficulty 7. Additionally, whenever the werewolf approaches an ambush, the Storyteller rolls Perception + Primal Urge against difficulty 6 for the werewolf to recognize the ambush before it springs. The werewolf does not have to activate this Gift, it provides a “sixth sense” at all times.

                    This is a truly phenomenal Gift, and superior in many situations to a similar Ratkin / Gurahl Gift. As a Lupus you'll be detecting in a 30 yard radius by default, and the difficulty of this check should probably be reduced if you're in your Lupus form. Even if your ST doesn't adjust the difficulty, this is still the best 'danger sense' Gift the Garou get.

                    Speech of All Things (Level 3 Children of Gaia Gift)

                    Passively understand any and all languages, and so long as you are physically capable of replying, you may do so. For example you could understand the whispers of Nagah, and you could try your best to reply with your own anatomy, probably producing something legible but making yourself sound a bit sssssilly.

                    Writing and reading requires a roll, difficulty 7 or 9, and lasts for a scene. No cost, and for the most part the Gift is entirely passive. Great Gift, suits the Children of Gaia very well, and makes you a truly universal translator. You can understand a dead language remembered only to Mnesis, but not understood by the Mokole themselves, you can pick up the Wyrm's schemes in the ramblings of Banes whose tongues are too forbidden to be picked up with Spirit Speech, you can understand the strange faerie tongues of the Arcadian Sidhe, and so on.

                    To me this is a must-have since it does its job so well. 9xp is a lot, arguably, but as a Child of Gaia you're going to want to be able to make peace between groups who don't understand one another.

                    That's it! Thank you to lbeaumanior for helping me find a few Gifts I'd missed!

                            Below I'm going to write up some summaries of how the Gift Lists of the various Breeds, Auspices and Tribes work out. This is intended as a sort of master-reference.

                            Green Gifts are must-haves, being very powerful or essential, and generally I'd suggest anyone who can access them get them. Amber Gifts are solid, they tend to be perfectly good, worth grabbing, but you could live without them. Red Gifts are ones you'd tend to want to avoid. There are exceptions of course, but the ratings should give a good outline of which Gifts are excellent to have on your list and which ones aren't.

                            Gifts in (Brackets) are added to the relevant list in Book of the Wyrm. Whether a non-Wyrmish Garou can learn these Gifts will depend on the Storyteller and the Gift in question. For example Power Surge, Visceral Agony and 1000 Teeth are quite reasonably just things a Homid, Metis and Lupus can respectively access now, but certain Gifts presented in that book are very clearly tied to the Wyrm by default.

                            Gifts in [Brackets] are added to the relevant list in the Wyld West expansion pack. Whether you can learn these in other time periods will depend on your Storyteller. Some will have faded from use, while some will still be available but harder to find.

                            Homid

                            Apecraft's Blessings, (Aura of Poison), City Running, [Jam Gun], Master of Fire, Persuasion, Smell of Man

                            Global Citizen, Jam Technology, Mark of the Wolf, (Power Surge), Speech of the World, [Spirit Brand], Staredown, Weaver's Eyes

                            (Call the Rust),Calm the Savage Beast, Cowing the Bullet, Disquiet, Rally the Troops, Reshape Object

                            Body Shift, Bury the Wolf, Cocoon, (Feast of Man-Flesh), Spirit Ward, [Weave of Steel], Web of Knowledge

                            Assimilation, Beyond Human, Part the Veil

                            Rank 1, Rank 2, Rank 3, Rank 4, Rank 5, (Wyrmish), [Wyld West], Changing Ways, Rage Across The World
