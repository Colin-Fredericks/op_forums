    Well, as I've said, I think having a mix optimizes thought brought to the game.

    Who should do the game.
    This really doesn't demonstrate an idea of how these game are made. These are games made by lots of people at different levels. There isn't just "this person does X, that person does Y, and another person does Z." Someone's above them keeping it all one thing. There's coordination with the owners, art/layout, and editing folks.

    Someone might write the best Wyrm creatures, but if they can't work with the lead designer we get a worse book in the end than if you'd taken the second best Wyrm creature writer who can work with the lead.

    W20 Changing Ways was full of good WtA writers, and is a bad book because of disagreements between the book's developer and WW.

    Assembling a team needs to keep in mind that it's a team, not just picking people off a list.

    What exaclty are You writing about? Give few examples, cause it's interesting what You write.
    Um... pretty much everything WW/Paradox have done?

    Paradox started by giving WW an extremely wide berth to do what they wanted with the WoD, severely misjudging the team that Martin formed. And despite a lot of us shouting warnings about things, they ignored it until it was too late.

    Now Paradox has reabsorbed WW, but is shopping everything around, leading to a lot of tonal dissonance (even with well received products like Chicago 5th) between books. The stuff released by WW, the stuff released by Modiphius, and the stuff released by Onyx Path all feel noticeably different, which makes me question Paradox's role as overseeing all this to maintain a consistent brand. Now we have a brand new group coming in too.

    Letting them try has it's limits as an argument, because it's appealing to potential instead of the tangible. It's not like if we don't let them try now, they will never be allowed to try again.

                                If V5 is any kind of indicator, then I have no reason to believe that I will like or recognize whatever W5 turns out to be. I'm sure it will be a fine game about werewolves with various nominal similarities to Werewolf: The Apocalypse's (as it existed in its various editions through 20th anniversary), but I find it hard to believe that it be W:tA in spirit--i.e., in tone, aesthetic, theme, what have you.

                                I would start a joking-but-not-joking "wrong answers only"-type thread dedicated solely to coming up with all the cringe-y and disappointing things that will happen with W5 (based on the assumption that the change will be comparable to that which occurred with V5,) but, frankly, that would simply be a depressing spiral of negativity. Every time I try to think of an absurd or bizarre hypothetical change to kick off the thread, I just make myself sad because I end up thinking, "aww, hell, they might actually do that."

                                I'm waiting for the day that someone (or maybe many someones) will dedicate some time, thought, and care to writing Storyteller Vault supplements intended to modernize whatever aspects of the various classic gamelines need to be updated in order to conform to modern technology, and global politics. Not even metaplot developments (because I feel like a fan supplement purporting to expand upon the metaplot would not necessarily have wide appeal,) just well-reasoned extrapolations of what certain aspects of the game(s) might look like if the game(s) had been written in 20XX. In technology alone, shit has gotten so wild and different in just the time since M20 was published--let alone V20! Let's face it, no one in 2015 would have thought that FB would be instrumental in a literal genocide, or that FB and Twitter would one day be considered some of the primary contributing factors/tools in the election of the most divisive POTUS in over a century, or the resurgence of open and notorious white supremacy in public spaces. Mind you, I'm sure we're all doing this individually at our own tables as time wears on, if only as a matter of necessity (except those of us playing period games which will never require "modernizing",) but I'm willing to bet someone is doing it much better than I am.
