            Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
            Honestly... murdering Garou isn't that hard if you know what you're doing; esp. for kin that have easier access to the Garou and are more likely to know the requisite things to understand how to isolate and murder a Garou before they can Rage out and kill you.

            Both Unsung Heroes and even more so A Breed Apart made it so Rituals was more commonly known to kinfolk. Yes Rituals 5 is still a lot, but one of the reasons that the Skin Dancers can be a thing instead of just one guy doing it, is that the Garou are supposed to be dying off, and that puts more pressure on the kinfolk to maintain knowledge; kin can learn Rituals and even individual rites they can't actually use (or use and expect any results), letting them pass on such knowledge in places where there aren't enough Garou to consistently have Garou mentors for everything.

            The restrictions on the killing pattern also aren't that hard: all five have to be the same Auspice. Killing five cubs that are between their First Change and their initiatons, or killing five Elders of the same moon phase doesn't actually change anything.

            This is not to say that the process is easy, but it can be done by people that aren't such incredibly badass geniuses that you'd consider overlooking their actions for how good they should be made leaders because it's that impressive doesn't really hold up.
            The point isn't that you should forgive them of their crimes.

            Seriously, becoming a Skin Dancer (without help) might just be one of the hardest things to pull off in the WoD. Whoever achieves it with even a vaguely competent Sept around them is a once in a generation genius who, in a sane and equitable society, would be sat at the top table helping with grand matters of strategy, counter-espionage and so on.

            So to me the interesting thing about Skin Dancers isn't the Dancers themselves. A story about them becoming a Tribe or being purified feels like it misses the point. The root problem is that someone with such an insane generational talent was left to whither on the vine until they stole a leading role in their own story by ending five others. It shows how deep corruption and wasted potential run within the Garou nation, how little care is taken of Kinfolk, how little their true potential is leveraged.
            The point is that these are people who could have, in a society that treats Kinfolk as equal (if different), been of great use to the nation. The existence of Skin Dancers is a symptom of Kinfolk being made to feel inferior, and a symptom of the most competent and talented Kinfolk being left with their abilities under-utilized. Skin Dancers didn't just pop into existence, they are a reflection of problems within the Tribes.

            Also the rite is a little harder than you think it is - you need to gather each hide under the same moon phase. So if you do your first murder under the gibbous moon, then all the other murders have to be performed under that moon too.

            Each of the hides must be taken under the same lunar phase; if the first werewolf slain by the Skin Dancer was slain under a gibbous moon, all subsequent hides must be gathered under a gibbous moon for the rite to be enacted successfully. The final ceremony of the rite must be conducted under a moon that matches the phase under which the hides were claimed and preserved, and must conclude exactly an hour after it is initiated.
            Like I said before, pulling this off with even a vaguely competent Sept around you for even 2 of the murders would be insanely difficult. Garou have detective powers that make this sort of thing very difficult. Easy mode, attacking cubs? Even if you go after the least cared-for cubs, or Ronin, the loss of such young Garou with all their potential untapped would still create an uproar. And Garou are horribly strong at all power levels; their base kit, being able to go Crinos/Hispo, makes them a huge threat even with no Gifts, even with mediocre Rage.

            Plus if you are able to pick the easiest targets and hunt them down efficiently with minimal risk that only furthers the point I'm making; you met your objective with the smartest strategy available and you've shown skill in backing up your idea, hunting down the best possible candidates for an easy execution. Again, skills the Garou Nation could have benefited from.

                            My headcanon: In times long gone the rite of sacred rebirth was a reasonably common Gaian ritual used by the garou to reward kinfolk and bolster the strength of their families. The skins used would be of those punished, those killed in war, or those who volunteer them after death depending on the time and place. During these times the wolves did not have a great many external threats and so they often turned inwards: with the garou being greedy, self-serving and arrogant a lot of corruption occurred and many needless deaths were caused because some people in power wanted to ensure their own offspring could get enough pelts. The problem became so pervasive that the garou decided the ritual and it's practitioners needed to be expunged.


                            I think there's a potentially practical use for the rite in modern times. People donate when they die, maybe if the garou can look past their pride (extremely unlikely) they can donate skin. If the taint could be dealt with the BSDs also have a lot of cannon fodder and are likely to get slaughtered in groups of five or more so you could maybe get 2-6 new garou after clearing out a hive.


                            Ultimately, renown is too important for most Garou. I imagine some tribes like the Get, GW, Uktena or Shadow lords being more open to it. But they're likely to remain secretive about it.

                                I do want to make something known about the kinfolk that seek to become Skin-Dancers. The only thing they need to do is cast the ritual, so have 5 dots in Rituals so they can be taught the ritual. They, themselves don't need to actually go out and kill the werewolves anyone can do that. Infact they don't even need to be recent as per the text:

                                Originally posted by The Ritual of Sacred Rebirth
                                This rite allows the caster to become a Garou, but only after successfully gathering the skins of five Garou ... The pelts may be years apart, so long as they are always preserved in the proper way
                                Emphasis mine. The Caster doesn't need to kill the Garou themselves it doesn't call for it just needs the pelts, and the pelts don't even need to be fresh. This is further put to the point in the W20 Skinner module with these lines:

                                Originally posted by W20 Skinner SAS
                                The Skin Dancers were careful to lie low after Haight’s death. They knew the Garou Nation would be swift to hold them accountable for his crimes — to say nothing of their own. They still recruited new members by collecting skins when they could, but they were always careful to dispose of the bodies afterwards.

                                The Vulture Walkers want to increase their ranks. Most don’t mind the idea of getting some payback from the Garou at the same time. They plan to achieve this by gathering more skins, preferably under a common moon, and by recruiting more disaffected Kinfolk who might be able to learn the Rite of Sacred Rebirth.

                                In the basement, the pack will find the skins the Vulture Walkers have been gathering. They may find all the skins claimed from Black Spiral Dancer corpses as well as those taken from the recent murders, or some may be missing if the Vulture Walkers had a fresh recruit in their ranks.
                                Emphasis mine. This seals the fact that the kinfolk doesn't need to be involved in the killings. Just needs the ability to learn the Ritual itself. So, these kinfolk are not badass geniuses that have the ability to kill full Garou but, instead Kinfolk that are hunted by the Skin-Dancers and them brought into the fold with pelts taken from BSD and other Garou.
