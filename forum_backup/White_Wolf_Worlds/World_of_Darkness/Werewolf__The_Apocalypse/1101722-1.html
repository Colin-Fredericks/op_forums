                                                How CT Phipps handles it

                                                In my game, the Garou Nation has a dismissive attitude to Kinfolk in general with both sexes expected to turn out as many Garou as possible. They're equivalent to religions which don't practice birth control and emphasize large families--because they are a religion which does that. However, the issue is complicated because it's a SECRET religion. Garou kinfolk have to hide their faith or emphasize it under other religions. They tend to marry in-between existing kinfolk families and oftentimes they are expected to breed with existing Garou. While this, biologically, sounds like it would favor Garou men over Garou women (and does), plenty of Garou women give birth at least once or twice during their lives before handing their children over to Kinfolk families.

                                                Few Garou also "marry" because it's basically like the worst of military lives with Garou expected fight continuously until they die in the War against the WyrmTM. The ones who live at Septs and have a permanent positition there as their guardians have more options to have "families" but it's still a violent life where Garou who live to old age being very rare. The issue of real-life genetics is also it's a science is also complicated by the fact what people thought they knew about genetics is a lot more different than reality. In real life, there's a lot more adultery and people raising other people's children than people ever thought. Mostly because women have been a lot more active about rebelling against enforced marriages than you ever think.

                                                Genetics-wise, Kinfolk tend to carry the genes from not only an immediate Garou parent but parents from generations past and any partners they have are likely to be Kinfolk themselves. But when they pass on their genes to non-Kinfolk, that gene can lay dormant for generations before popping up. So yeah, I do think 100:1 Kinfolk per Garou sounds about right. For the most part, I think Kinfolk probably control other Kinfolk as they're the people who handle the raising of children, the arranged marriages, the finances, and so on.

                                                If this sounds like it could be rife with abuse, fundamentalism, and worse then you're right, it is. The Garou have a lot of ugliness to their culture which it's up to players to rebel against or reform because Garou history is human history.
