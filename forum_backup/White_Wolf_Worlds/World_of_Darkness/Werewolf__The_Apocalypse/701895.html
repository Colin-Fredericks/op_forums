    Kyuubi No Akuma: Those who have lost faith

    10-15-2015, 06:35 AM
    As the Wheel of Ages turns, slowly but surely inching towards the Sixth Age, and the coming of the Demon Emperor, there is concern in the courts of the Hengeyokai and other residents of the Middle Kingdom. Sure, each age has come and gone according to the cycle, but few predicted the Yama Kings would ever become so flush with power, and what the westerners call the "Wyrm" is growing stronger by the day. While the Hengeyokai do whatever they can to extend the Fifth Age, and make the Sixth as short as possible...a few Kitsune have grown...concerned.

    Seeing the sorry state of the Emerald Mother, they cannot help but wonder that if Gaia cannot protect herself, why can they expect that Gaia can protect THEM? The Emerald Mother may of promised they would survive the Sixth Age, but the way things are going...They can't help but worry that the Demon Emperor might find some way to stop the Wheel from turning at the Sixth Age, making an eternal hell on earth, where corruption is king, and the Hengeyokai are hunted and squeezed for every iota of Chi that the Emperor can extract.

    Has a result, some of the younger Werefoxes have decided not to take the risk of 'seeing how it goes' and trusting in Gaia. They see how weak the Emerald Mother is, and how strong the Yama Kings are, and simply wish to be on the winning side when the Sixth Age arrives. However, they are not like the Bakemono or the Black Spiral Dancers, in that they wish to harm Gaia. They don't hate the Emerald Mother, they simply don't think she has the capability to protect them anymore, like an old woman who is no longer able to provide for her children. They surely don't WANT her to die, but they can see the writing on the wall and know that in a few winters she will pass on.

    And so, they plan for the worst. With an attitude their elders would consider blasphemous at best, these Kitsune have turned towards other means of survival. For some, this means fleeing into the deepest parts of the Yang world to ride out the Sixth Era on some far shore. But the way is perilous, and some seek more assurances that they will indeed endure. Until recently, they did not have a better idea of how to survive.

    At least, until they encountered one of the Scorpion Eaters.

    The story of an entity called the Kitsune No Kuro, or the Black Fox has begun spreading around the Werefox community. They speak of an...understanding he came to, guided by one of the Scorpion Eaters. Its really rather simple, a Kitsune may contact one of the Yama Kings, and plead for clemency. If they do, a deal is offered, a non-interference pact similar to the one offered to the Scorpion Easters. The Kitsune who sign this pact, presented by a Demon Servant of their specific patron, agrees not to do anything that would slow the progress of the Wheel of Ages in exchange for an assurance that the Yama King will ensure that the Kitsune will survive the coming of the Demon Emperor.

    In other words, while the Werefox need not kill the Emerald Mother, they do have to look the other way while someone presses a pillow to her face to smother the life out of her.

    Called Kyuubi No Akuma, or "Devil Foxes", these Kitsune have turned their back on Gaia, and will simply watch as she dies. The pact is long, winding, and varies from Fox to Fox, some negotiate better positions then others, and a few have even outright sold their entire soul to Yomi Wan in exchange for lodging, servants, and all it cost them was their free will. The majority of Devil Foxes, however, are canny enough to recognize that selling your soul to one of the Yama Kings is a terrible idea, they go has far as they must to survive, and no further. And this suits the Yama Kings just fine, one less obstacle to hasten the coming of the Sixth Age is a victory for Yomi Wan, no matter how you slice it.


    Mechanics

    Infused with the power of Hell as part of their contracts, the Devil Foxes are quite a bit hardier then their peers, having attained some demonic adaptations to aid in their survival in the hellish Sixth Age. Essentially, Devil Foxes now heal like Garou and other Shifters, however, the Silver Lady recognizes that the Werefox has turned away from the Emerald Mother. The Devil Fox may only soak damage from silver in their breed form, and the damage is now aggravated as Luna expresses her displeasure for the Kitsune's treason.

    However, other then healing, the survival pact offers them some other unique benefits. The Devil Foxes are usually canny enough to know it will take more then merely being able to heal quickly to survive the Sixth Age, as such, the Yama Kings expand their mystical potential with tainted magic as part of their side of the deal. Essentially, the Devil Fox may purchase Black Spiral Dancer gifts as if they where favored. Furthermore, they may purchase Demonic Investments for (Rankx2) Experience Points (At the storytellers discretion, Investments may also include Bakemono powers. For more information on Investments and Powers, please see the Book of Madness, the Guide to the Sabbat, and Possessed: a Players Guide). As a final boon...or curse, they may only subsist on Tainted Chi, drawing power from pure Dragon Nests not only does nothing, but causes significant pain and disorentation, costing the Devil Fox a willpower point for their attempt, and gaining no Gnosis. While this is a problem, compared to the Scorpion Eaters, they at least do not NEED to draw Chi to sustain their lives.

    Though, as the Devil Fox will eventually find out for himself, the Yama Kings DID indeed swear that they would survive the Sixth Age...But there is a trifling matter to account for, the Sixth Age isn't here yet. And while they have the ability to survive the hellish world to come, they might have some difficulty surviving the significantly LESS hellish world of the here and now. All of the Werefoxes forms develop some degree of sympathy with Yomi Wan, as their bodies adapt to handle tainted Chi.

    Other shifters can tell something feels a little...Off about the Werefox, but the Yama Kings are aware that if it was THAT obvious that the Devil Fox was tainted, far less would survive to spread the word to their peers about this...business opportunity. A Hengeyokai may roll Alertness+perception at difficulty TEN to try to pick up on the subtle taint, and ONLY a Hengeyokai, the western shifters are not sufficiently educated on the means and methods of the Yama Kings to pick up on anything unusual. The gift "Sense Wyrm" may also expose the Devil Fox, but the roll is also difficulty Ten. (However, investments, and Spiral gifts are hardly what anybody would call subtle, and sooner or later someone is bound to wonder why they haven't seen the Kitsune enjoying the benefits of Dragon Nests lately...)

    As a final curse, the Kitsune's tails, when revealed, crackle with balefire, a clear indication that SOMETHING is very wrong with the Werefox.




    What the hell man?!

    You might be wondering "Gee Autochthon, the Kitsune already get access to Fox Magic, Paper Magic, and a slew of other tricks, and Changing Breeds 20 even lets them learn the gifts of other breeds with Fox Magic, so why on earth did you have to open up Investments and Powers for these guys? They ALREADY get so much!"

    Well, the reason is simple, the Devil Foxes are not losing an enemy so much as they are trading enemies. Investments and Spiral Gifts are NOT subtle, and there's a difference between knowing how to use a power, and being able to use the power without a Khan ripping your head off for betraying the Emerald Mother. Everybody knows Werefoxes stick their paws into things better left alone, but there's a limit to what people will accept as "Just Kitsune being Kitsune".

    Finally, the non aggression pact means that not a single servant of the Yama Kings (or Wyrm) will attack them unless they are attacked first. And considering the Devil Foxes are craven enough to sell out the Emerald Mother for their own survival...Well, they're not gonna be the ones hurling spells or clawing Bakemono (For that would violate the pact. And you can bet the Yama Kings have plans for punishing Werefoxes who try to back out. The most they can hope for is a quick death and half an eternity in the Yomi hells...if the Yama King is feeling merciful or is busy when his servants ask about what to do), which will get their Sentai wondering why the hell this Fox refuses to back them up. Even if their Sentai dies to a man, save for the Devil Fox, someone's going to wonder why the big bad Khan died, but not the Fox

    In other words, the success of a Devil Fox isn't so much based on surviving the Sixth Age...its avoiding being slaughtered as a dirty traitor by his Sentai in the Fifth Age. The ultimate tragedy for a Devil Fox is knowing if you can JUST make it a few more years, you will be safe and sound...and then noticing they're hearing a hissing sound behind them just as the Nagha assassin strikes them dead.

    Of course, they could just flee for the safety of a Tainted Dragons Nest...But someone's going to come looking for them eventually, and with the Yama Kings servant so close, when the Hengeyokai come knocking at their door, a desperate Devil Fox might just offer up the last scraps of their soul to become proper Akuma, losing their free will and souls in exchange for salvation from the claws of their former peers. No matter what happens to the Devil Fox, the Yama Kings win, the only real variable is how much the Fox wins as well (And it will always be less then the princes of Yomi Wan stand to gain)
