    Question: Can Garou use Gifts during Frenzy?

    09-07-2021, 02:25 PM
    Pretty much the title, can a frenzying Garou be able to use Gifts?
    If yes, what sort? I cant remember the source but I read Rage related Gifts can be used as Rage can be used normally during frenzy. If not, please let me know.
    thanks!

      The rules are on page 261 of the W20 rulebook.

      A werewolf in frenzy can bite, claw, or run. He can spend Rage but only for extra actions. He can't split dice pools, use Gifts, or step sideways.

                      Huh, I don't think I'd ever considered that issue... but by Stamina 3 most Garou in Frenzy can't be stunned without taking so much damage they'd be incapacitated anyway, I'm not sure how often it's ever had the chance to come up in play for me (and I've played a lot of WtA).

                      That said, for Stamina 1-2 PCs, or Stamina 3 + Huge Size (or some other source of bonus health boxes), I'd allow that as well. I might even house rule that Frenzy flat out makes you immune to stun rather than worrying about Rage drain on the edge cases.

                      It does seem important that one of the slight differences between Black Fox's statement and the book, is that the book says, "He can spend Rage for extra actions, but can’t split dice pools, use Gifts, or step sideways." The lack of "only" is generally superfluous, because resisting stun is pretty much the only thing to spend Rage on that isn't addressed. I'd be easily swayed that the more technically correct reading is that resisting stun isn't valid, but as I tend to side, I'll take what feels like the interpretation with the more satisfactory outcome over technical parsing if there's some leeway there.

                                              09-09-2021, 05:47 PM
                                              And, importantly, the rules to start rolling to end the Frenzy start when the instigating cause of the Frenzy is no longer present. A berserking Garou doesn't have to kill every valid target for some set period of time, once the target of their anger is either dead or beyond reach, the rolls start. Fox frenzy doesn't mean they just keep running and running until they pass out; just until they get away from whatever set them off.

                                              It's not even about "fooling" senses or something like that. If a Garou goes berserk over someone publicly humiliating them, and mauls the offender badly enough to cause them to run away or play dead, it's valid to say that the Garou's anger is sated by putting the insulting person in their place, and you can start rolling to get out of the Frenzy that soon (though until you succeed the violence isn't going to end).

                                              The ST's ruling on when the triggering stimulus is resolved is really the biggest factor on how dangerous the Frenzy is going to be. It could be a swift burst that only lasts a few seconds, or a rampage that takes half an hour to finally subside.
