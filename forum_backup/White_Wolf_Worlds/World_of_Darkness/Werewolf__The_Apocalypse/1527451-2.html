    While it's true that the wolf-influence among the Garou has always been riddled with errors, it's wrong to say that they have no basis in actual wolves. The greater influence of scent and howl culture are both based in actual wolf ethology. And it's as ridiculous to say that a bunch of humans will never grasp how wolves think as it is to say that members of one human group can't grasp how another human group thinks. This knowledge is imperfect, but I can't have perfect knowledge of what it's like to be French either.
