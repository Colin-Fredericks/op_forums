                                  The tribe renaming of the Iron Riders to Glass Walkers happened before the Wise Guys gained dominance - according to the rev tribebook (page 30), the Iron Riders started falling apart in 1883 and the Glass Walkers took over soon after, with a short span of dominance around the turn of the century before being replaced by the Wise Guys who gained dominance in 1922 (page 55).
