    These are just some of the glyphs I've done recently:

    https://www.facebook.com/photo.php?fbid=4822302630905

    • Army of Shadows: Our first MES pack from the Pre-Apocalypse game. I tried to best represent the concept of an army and a pack of war over "night", which was the closest I could get to shadows.
    • Wednesday Morning: A pack united under Bacchus (thus the attempt at a wine goblet) under the "sun" rising and the "W" for Wednesday, all within the "pack" glyph.
    • Lost and Found: Our pack for this chronicle of MES Apocalypse. I created an interrobang combined with "hidden", all within the pack glyph.
    • Sean Logan: I combined "Pangea" with "Warrior" to create the concept of the "Guardian of Pangea".
    • Arthur Dreese: My personal logo made up in the style of the glyphs.
    • Raging Rapids: I attempted to best illustrate the rolling rapids in a fluid glyph representing a river.

            I remember that the other sites had a lot less and they were usually copies of what Lady Mox had up. When her site had cutbacks the others didn't grow much bigger and were still less useful. I do remember Lady Mox had a WW disclaimer on the very front page. but the issue was with the sheer volume of trademarked icons she had. There were usually 3 or 4 differently colored styles of nearly every group in every system. Not just Werewolf tribes, bastet tribes and glyphs out of the silver record and golly that'd be a lot right there. She had all Vampire clans and even the obscure symbols and groups. All the Wraith guilds and Changeling houses and Kiths that were just coming out. (though this was before Hunter or Demon, just 5 systems back then) It probably didn't help her case that she altered the symbols by way of coloration and chrome or fire effects. I think there are still some incarnations of her website around still, though much reduced. I thik this library site might be ok as long as there's the disclaimer and the original glyph scans are not changed in any way.
