    I've just returned from a 10-day meditation retreat and, at the beginning, I couldn't help but think a bit about the Cult of Fenris... specifically, what is my house-ruled Cult of Fenris supposed to be really about.

    I already established that the Cult is a nihilistic movement of which many members have fallen into hauglosk (specifically, my house-ruled "lashing out due to hopelessness" version).

    I had an idea that I want to float here: The Cult came into being when Fenris, a spirit of chaos and destruction (so, basically Wyld-Wyrm), approaches the Crimson Tusk leadership and explains them that the Wyrm has been caught in the Weaver's web, and its madness lies in that "it thinks" it can remain in a constant and stable stage of self-digestion/consumption which never ends. Because the Weaver has warped it, it expects a static and eternal cycle of destruction without destroying itself eventually. However, if Gaia were to end, the Wyrm would need to eat itself as well. So, Fenris tempts the Crimson Tusks with an opportunity to become agents of the Wyld-Wyrm to destroy the faulty setup and to basically "reboot" the cosmos. So, accelerationist basically.

    This would mean that the BSD become more servants of the Weaver-Wyrm, whereas the Cult becomes an ally of the Wyld-Wyrm (or sane/Balance Wyrm, depending on how you interpret it).

    The offer Fenris would have made to the Crimson Tusks and all Garou who join them is to give them the resources (Banes, Gifts, etc.) that follow the path of ultimate destruction and rebirth, not just the flawed vision of the Weaver-tainted Wyrm.

    What do you think? Does that make sense?

                                        Crimson Tusk Gifts

                                        Total Renown 3

                                        Porcupine’s Reprisal (As Philodox)

                                        Resist Pain
                                        Through sheer grit, the Crimson Tusk is able to push through the pain of their wounds and continue acting normally. A bear-spirit teaches this Gift.
                                        Renown: Glory
                                        Cost: 1 Rage Check
                                        Action: Full
                                        Pool: Resolve + Glory
                                        System:                                         The user’s player makes a Gift test at a Difficulty of 2 + 1 for each level of Aggravated damage the Garou is currently suffering (if any). If successful, the Garou becomes immune to all Health-based penalties, such as Impairment.
                                        Duration: One scene

                                        Snarl of the Predator
                                        The Crimson Tusk lets out a feral snarl that terrifies opponents and cows them into submission. A wolf-spirit teaches this Gift.
                                        Renown: Glory
                                        Cost: 1 Rage Check
                                        Action: Minor
                                        Pool: Charisma + Glory vs Composure + Insight
                                        System: The Garou’s player makes a Gift test against one enemy within earshot and line of sight. The enemy resists with their Composure + Insight. If the enemy fails, they suffer a two-dice penalty to attack this Gift’s user.
                                        Duration: Success margin in turns

                                        Total Renown 6

                                        Erymanthian Tusks
                                        The Erymanthian Boar was an untamable beast of legend. Its tusks were known for cutting through armor, severing nets, and snapping weapons of hunters and warriors alike. Crimson Tusks with this Gift can slice through stone, steel, and other inanimate materials with ease. This Gift is taught by a Boar-spirit.
                                        Renown: Glory
                                        Cost: 1 Rage Check
                                        Action: Minor
                                        Pool: —
                                        System: The Garou adds twice their Glory rating for feats of strength that involve damage or destruction of an inanimate object using their natural weaponry.
                                        Duration: Once scene

                                        Fangs of Judgment (As Philodox)

                                        Heart of Fury
                                        The Garou can steel themself against anger, suppressing their Rage and creating a mental dam against any explosive outbursts. This anger will catch up with them eventually, though, so they must vent it before it breaks free. A boar-spirit teaches this Gift.
                                        Renown: Wisdom
                                        Cost: 1 Willpower
                                        Action: Full
                                        Pool: —
                                        System: The Garou can ignore a number of Brutal Outcomes equal to their Wisdom rating. When the scene ends, however, past slights and injuries come rushing back to haunt the Garou, refilling their heart and soul. At the end of the scene, they must spend another Willpower point or make a frenzy check immediately at the regular difficulty.
                                        Duration: Once scene

                                        TRUE FEAR (As Ahroun)

                                        Total Renown 9

                                        Defy Death (As Galliard)

                                        Razorback’s endurance
                                        The Garou is able to call upon Boar’s incredible toughness. This powerful Gift grants the Garou great endurance and hardiness for a time. This Gift is taught by a Boar-spirit.
                                        Renown: Glory
                                        Cost: 1 Willpower
                                        Action: Free
                                        Pool: —
                                        System: The Garou’s Stamina is increased by their Glory rating for the duration of the scene. This will aid any rolls involving Stamina, but also increase the Garou’s Health levels. The additional Health levels count as natural and are subject to regeneration. In contrast to additional Health levels gained from Crinos form, any damage to these additional Health levels disappears together with them when the Gift ends. This Gift can be used only once per session.
                                        Duration: One scene

                                        Strength of Purpose (As Philodox)
