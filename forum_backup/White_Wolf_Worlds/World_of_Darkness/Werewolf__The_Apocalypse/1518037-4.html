But the Wyrm is still part of the setting at the same time. And we've seen three Tribe-sections where "Gaia dead/dying, Garou-Nation sundered" didn't get a lot of attention - even though these should represent massive shifts for Garou-society.

                It's just... I don't know. Maybe all this will seem more coherent once we can read the book back to back. But with all the previews we've gotten, I could never shake the feeling that all these bits and pieces just don't seem to fit together very well.
                This is what I vehemently hate about this upcoming "Werewolf" 5.

                You can just tell that the current overall X5 team didn't or just simply cannot understand the spiritualism that was REALLY important to Werewolf. They'd rather destructure and emplace a cold-iron modernism in Werewolf, where the stereotype of the Garou being "Captain Planeteers" prevails over the setting and nothing else, than the high fantastical aspects seekings of wisdom or razor-jarring battles in the Umbra that Werewolf could reach toward. Now we just get this milquetoast version of the Umbra / Spirit Wilds that's just pure a## to interact with from a mechanical and setting standpoint, from the tidbits of information we got released concerning it.

                I just think it speaks toward how little they cared about Werewolf going into this, and the leaked conversations we can glean from this Indigenous author, who for a short time worked for Paradox, really throws a bone of evidence to them not caring about the Werewolf setting at all.

                Everything Paradox thinks they know about this game comes from pure stereotypes from ignorant detractors; or worse, some of these detractors (who I won't say because I'll probably get my post banned or deleted) who now get to directly work on the it due to Paradox, can now fully realize those stereotypes to fit their past contrivances.
