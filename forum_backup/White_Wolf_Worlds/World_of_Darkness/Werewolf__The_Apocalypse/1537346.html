    Garou Pre-First Change

    06-14-2024, 03:06 PM
    I just ran a Werewolf game where the players were children, and it raised a lot of questions about what exactly the mechanics are for pre first change Garou.

    I had been running them as kinfolk, but someone told me that this was wrong, that Garou to be are different from the moment of birth.

    A few questions:

    I addition to genetics, there is a "spiritual component" to Garou. When does this spiritual change come about? At conception? At birth? At puberty? At the moment of first change? Pre-ordained by Gaia since the dawn of time?

    Can said spiritual component be detected by supernatural powers? I think 5E says "Scent of the True Form" works, but is this the case in earlier editions? What about other powers?

    Are garou subject to the delirium before their first change?

    Can pre-first change garou purchase Gnosis or Gifts in the manner of Kinfolk?

    If yes, is their Gnosis lowered by having silver equipment?

    Does silver pain or cause aggravated damage to garou pre first change?


    What happens if a vampire tries to embrace a garou before their first change?
    Can a garou awaken as a mage pre first change? What is awakening is forced on them by Alchemy Six or Prime Nine?
    Can a garou become a mummy before their first change is subject to the spell of life?
    Is there any way a changeling soul can be attached to a garou pre first change?
    What would happen if a group pre first change performed the Rite of Sacred Rebirth?
    Or, even crazier, what would happen if a pre first change shapeshifter who was kin to multiple breeds performed the Rite of Sacred Rebirth to transform into a different breed than they would have become naturally with their first change?


    So, we know from the flavor text that most Garou have their first change when they frenzy for the first time after being placed in a stressful situation. But frenzy is typically the result of a certain number of successes on a rage roll, right? But, Garou don't have Rage until their first change, right?
    So isn't this something of a catch 22? Is this meant to be purely storyteller FIAT?
    This actually came up in my game, the PCs violently kidnapped an adolescent black spiral dancer who had not yet undergone her first change, and I made a rage roll for her to see if she would frenzy and undergo her first change as the result of the stress (she did) and the players told me I was cheating because, by definition, if she hasn't had her first change yet, she has no rage score to roll.


    And a similar, but unrelated question: Wolves and Coyotes can produce fertile offspring. Is it possible to have coyotes who are garou kinfolk or wolves who are nuwisha kinfolk? Could such kinfolk ever breed true?


    Thanks!

      So... the short version? The books don't really support playing pre-Change Garou. Even if W5 decreased the difficulty of this slightly, it's still not meant for it. As such, a huge amount of what you're asking is never really addressed because it doesn't really come up in the intended play of the game.

      As I respond to a few specifics, a big caveat: Editions matter. With five editions of the game, there's a lot of small details that impact things. I'm going to do my best to address things broadly, but my statements will be variable in "truth" if you hold one edition as the primary one over others.

      Originally posted by Talakeal View Post
      I had been running them as kinfolk, but someone told me that this was wrong, that Garou to be are different from the moment of birth.
      Pre-change Garou are not kinfolk mechanically. Telling the difference is not necessarily simple though. Kinfolk are resistant to Delirium in some fashion, and children that are immune to Delrium aren't inherently comfortable around giant murder monsters just because there isn't magic forcing them to act even less rationally than children normally do. Keep in mind that while it's not normally enforced with much, high Rage Garou are off-putting to everyone; including post-Change Garou and even spirits. Children tend not to have a lot of Willpower compared to adults to keep their emotions in check.

      I addition to genetics, there is a "spiritual component" to Garou. When does this spiritual change come about? At conception? At birth? At puberty? At the moment of first change? Pre-ordained by Gaia since the dawn of time?
      There is no true genetic component in a scientific sense. Garou (and other shifters) might have patterns that can be studied and understood, but they don't follow human genetic science. The spiritual component is the primary component.

      The books rather purposefully avoid the conception vs. birth debate specifically, but given the nature of the Crinos-born, the implication is basically conception (or at least during fetal development as much of a difference as that makes). Basically, there is no "spiritual change," because a pre-Change Garou is always part spirit. Their ability to exercise this to meaningful effect though doesn't come about until their first change (which is usually around puberty, part of why this isn't really a great game for "pre-Change kids").

      Can said spiritual component be detected by supernatural powers?
      In theory. AFAIK there is no power in any of the books that just senses the spiritual component of a Garou specifically. Scent of the True Form lets you tell a Garou from a human (or whatever else) but it does so holistically, not by sensing specific "components" of a being. I feel like you might be taking the word "component" a bit different from it's intentions. Garou are full beings that cannot be reduced so simply into individual parts. Their spirit side and their physical side are not divisible, every part of them contains both.

      Can pre-first change garou purchase Gnosis or Gifts in the manner of Kinfolk?
      There's nothing stopping this broadly, but it's mechanically discouraged and the spirits might not teach a pre-Change Garou or even a Cub Gifts that they would teach a kin because they judge them by different standards.

      Does silver pain or cause aggravated damage to garou pre first change?
      No for homids and lupus, yes for Crinos-born (the books do like to make their lives suck). Keep in mind that homid and lupus Garou in their birth forms can handle silver and don't take Agg from it in general.

      Can a garou awaken as a mage pre first change?
      No. The Garou's spiritual nature is incompatible with having an avatar that can awaken.

      Most of the other crossover stuff is never touch on. Again, most Garou change during or right after puberty, and they can in theory change early as well as later. Most people would just have them undergo their First Change in response to things like this (or would have changed in the years of study necessary to learn something like the Rite of Rebirth, you need the Rituals Knowledge equal or greater to the level of the Rite to learn it, not something most tweens are going to have had time to do).

      So, we know from the flavor text that most Garou have their first change when they frenzy for the first time after being placed in a stressful situation.
      The First Change is triggered by any number of potential events (though usually stress, keeping in mind that children process stress differently than adults). The change also causes their first Frenzy, not the other way around, thus the rules for entering Frenzy aren't relevant: it's automatic.

      This actually came up in my game, the PCs violently kidnapped an adolescent black spiral dancer who had not yet undergone her first change, and I made a rage roll for her to see if she would frenzy and undergo her first change as the result of the stress (she did) and the players told me I was cheating because, by definition, if she hasn't had her first change yet, she has no rage score to roll.
      They're right, but you're more right. NPCs undergoing their First Change is entirely ST fiat territory, you didn't even have to give them a chance of a roll and could have just said it happened because they chose poorly by using violence on a pre-Change Garou.

      Could such kinfolk ever breed true?
      It's possible even when the animal kin aren't compatible. The Rev. section on this used the Bastet and Garou as an example, because the human kin of all the shifters can reproduce with each other.

      Though keep in mind that some changers work differently. The Corax stand out particularly in this regard.

          I would like to add my personal interpretation on the crossover questions. It's important to understand they're educated guesses at best, based on the idea that the pre-change Garou is a Garou nonetheless.

          Originally posted by Talakeal View Post
          What happens if a vampire tries to embrace a garou before their first change?
          A Garou usually has to botch on a Gnosis roll to be Embraced, becoming a hybrid Abomination. Since Gnosis is defined by Breed, it isn't hard to just apply the same rule.

          If you want to make this easier on pre-change, you can have the Embrace occur with a simple failure, but I would avoid to let it happen as easy as a normal human.

          The resulting vampire probably won't ever undergo a first Change, though. The easiest way to represent them would be as a normal vampire, with the caveat that they have their birth form. Whatever rules you make for pre-changers, look at the Abomination to see which ones would carry over if you want more complexity.

          Originally posted by Talakeal View Post
          Can a garou awaken as a mage pre first change? What is awakening is forced on them by Alchemy Six or Prime Nine?
          ​Metaphysically the nature of a Garou is already a form of Awakening, or at least is so suggested by the M20 core (and I think it was mentioned elsewhere since 2nd Edition) and in-universe by some Mages.

          Probably a pre-changer can't Awaken, just as a Garou can't, anything that would cause an Awakening would instead trigger the First Change.
          ​
          Originally posted by Talakeal View Post
          ​
          Is there any way a changeling soul can be attached to a garou pre first change?
          No.

          Changelings come in one of three ways: by the Changeling Way, by soul snatching, or by true birth of a new fairy.

          The Changeling Way happens in the womb or at conception. Such a child would never be a Garou in the first place because they're already a fairy.

          Soul Snatching is what the Sidhe usually do, but it is what it is. They don't attach to a human soul, they send the original soul to Arcadia and keep the body. You can rule that this is either impossible or at least harder to do with a Garou soul, I'd probably say impossible, but even if it does happen the result is a fairy in the otherwise human body that once belonged to a Garou.

          Birthing true is just that. It isn't explained by rules, but is mentioned a few times in Changeling lore. If both parents are Changelings, there's a chance that the child will be a new fairy soul. But as far as we know such a child is indistinguishable from any other Changeling, just lacking memories from previous lifes, and definitely wouldn't be a Garou.

          All that said, both Changelings can be Kinfolk and Garou can be Kinain. Two Changelings can have a baby together and this baby be a Kinain Garou. In fact, the chance is roughly the same of a Garou birth from non-fairy parents.
          ​
          Originally posted by Talakeal View Post
          ​
          What would happen if a group pre first change performed the Rite of Sacred Rebirth?
          ​The rite would probably fail, but leave a heavy Wyrm Taint.

          Originally posted by Talakeal View Post
          ​
          Or, even crazier, what would happen if a pre first change shapeshifter who was kin to multiple breeds performed the Rite of Sacred Rebirth to transform into a different breed than they would have become naturally with their first change?
          Probably the rite would fail, though exceptions might occur. If the ritual succeeds, then probably they would feel the loss of the Wolf or some other spiritual scar from losing their original breed, becoming effective Kinfolk.

          But since there's no version of the ritual for the other Fera, the very process of developing such a new ritual could result in something with stranger effects.

                                  If the coyote Garou is the pup of a wolf and a coyote, even if the wolf is a Garou, the child's lupus form would be a coyote/wolf hybrid, and their Crinos too, although that would have no mechanical implication.

                                  Some people have this idea that it's impossible for a Garou to not know they're Garou, but that's simply bullshit. There's a lot of potential Garou around the world, testing every child is impractical, not to say impossible. As much as there are methods to find out, a lot of Garou go unnoticed until they change.

                                  Now, what you need is to have a honest conversation with your players, not to try to rules-lawyer your way out of this predicament. You gave in time and again to their antics until you didn't anymore, rules have nothing to do with that.

                                  You're the ST. You decide how things work. But they also have a right to know when rules aren't being strictly applied. Both sides erred here, though only you and your group can say how much.
