      There are different ideas and this period of history is difficult to gain concrete information on, particularly as one of the best resources Shattering Dreams is only considered quasi-canon.

      Anyway, preamble aside, I believe it's generally considered that the Garou were one tribe, one pack, following Wolf until around the end of the last Ice Age, when Death Wolf supposedly lent his secrets to some and the alphas of the Nation were blessed by Luna. This led to the rise of the Silver Fangs and White Howlers who were known to have the hides of Death Wolf.

      After this the tribes developed alongside the development of humanity and these dates are semi-headcanons co-opted from Black Fox but I do like them.

      The Red Talons emerged soon after humanity began domesticating animals, particularly Dogs, as protectors of the wild. The Black Furies, Fianna, Get of Fenris, Shadow Lords and Silent Striders arose from within their various host cultures; same as the Bunyip in Australia and the Wendigo, Croatan, and Uktena in the Americas.

      Furthermore we have the Bone Gnawers, Children of Gaia, Glass Walkers, and Stargazers who likely joined together for a mix of ideological and practical reasons as humanity began to rise and after the War of Rage and Impergium.

      We all know where the Black Spiral Dancers come from...

      A notable tid bit of all of this is that it's suggested in a few places that the reason why Wolf is no longer a totem of the Garou is that after his children turned their backs on him and each other to follow tribal totems, he departed into the deep umbra to never be heard from again.

                                  First edition corebook stated, "Most tribes originated out of a sept, and most septs first originated out of a pack. Thus the spirits of the tribes were once simply the spirits of a sept, and before that a pack."

                                  The canonical Central Park caern is perhaps an example of this, as we know the caern has two totems (Mera and Great White Mouse). The Great White Mouse was originally just the pack totem of the Marrow Eaters pack, and when they rescued/took over the caern in the 1930s, at some point their pack totem became a second caern totem.

                                  The original Garou did not have the Pact or any relations with spirits. The Garou had to navigate the spirit world, learn about their ways, and eventually discovered the laws of chiminage. With that knowledge, they began constructing the Pact with the spirits, and winning the favors of Totems, and learning Gifts and Rites. We can assume they started with pack totems, then caern totems, and finally tribal totems. Each probably required greater chiminage because they were getting the spirits to agree to adopt more and more children and agree to help Garou for a longer period of time. (The demands of spirit to assist a pack is much less than agreeing to assist all those future generations of Garou who will be part of a tribe).

                                  So it is simply a matter of Garou becoming experienced as they earned greater and greater privileges and responsibilities with the spirits.

                                  This probably took a few thousand years, and the different tribes were formed at different times. There is textual evidence that the Fianna was the first tribe, or must have been one of the first. Silver Fangs may have been second, but likely think they were the first. The Black Furies are also considered to be one of the older tribes. Star Gazers, Glass Walkers and Bone Gnawers are some of the youngest tribes. Others are in between them.

                                  Incidentally,there is some evidence in early first edition material that the 13 tribes we know today (16 with the lost tribes) were only the surviving tribes, and that there were more at earlier points of time. First edition isn't consistent. There are statements of having only 15 tribes once (Croatan weren't invented yet, but soon would be). But there were also statements that implied there were many more, but they simply didn't survive. The Second Edition corebook actually tells us "The Great White Mouse... predates the existence of the Bone Gnawers. He was once a minor totem of the Garou, but when the Bone Gnawers formed long ago, the followers of Mouse were slain by the Children of Rat."
