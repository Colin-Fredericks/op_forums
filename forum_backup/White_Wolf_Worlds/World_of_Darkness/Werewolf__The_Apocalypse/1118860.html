    resisting possession

    07-21-2017, 03:48 AM
    so,guide to the possessed says that humans trying to do it get one roll each day. but possession happens in less than a day and once it happens should the bane be evicted the human dies(the book explicitly says that when it used the word possession it doesn't refer to short term domination)..so is there any answer to this or is it a writer mistake?

      Banes possessing people and turning them into Fomori has always been something where the rules aren't strict. How much Wyrm Taint a person must have before a Bane can attempt possession, and how long it takes to transform someone into Fomori has gone back and forth in various supplements based on the needs of the story. I wouldn't be strict on the application.

      What has been consistent is that once a person has become Fomori, the only thing keeping them alive is the presence of the Bane so exorcising them causes the vessel to die.

      My recommendation is to determine how Tainted is the person - and how much more taint would they need to become a viable vessel for a Bane. Depending on the nature of the taint and their encounter with it, it might takes hours, days, weeks, months, or even years. During this time Garou might defeat the Bane, perform the Rite of Cleansing, or do any number of things to shoo the Bane away.

      Once the person has become tainted enough that Possession is possible, then the Bane can attempt to possess once a day. Only if they succeed does the process of the vessel becoming Fomori happens.

      Then the actual transformation of the person into a fomori takes about a day, during which it is is still possible to save the person.

      After that, the person becomes a Fomori, and whether it is possible to save them and expel the Bane safely is up to the ST but could take a few more days depending on how lenient the ST wants or any specialness of the scenario.

      But definitely after a few days of being Fomori, you can't rescue the human anymore.

          None. Taint is just a kind of scent from being around something. Possession can theoretically go forward without any at all, though someone who doesn't do the kind of acts that would typically result in gaining a bane's attention probably wouldn't be at the top of the list, because they haven't gained the bane's attention. It does only take baby steps to get a bane's attention, though, and there are typically a lot of banes in a city, and few but very persistent banes in rural areas.

Fomori become forced into a spiritual symbiosis, but it doesn't have to be a death sentence.

                  The manual says that 6 hours is the maximum that a bane might require to successfully complete a possession. After the possession time (when the Bane is channelling and can't do anything else), the spirit is inside the host and can begin to modify her, or to warp her mind. Of course, even after the bane is inside it doesn't mean that it has full control of the host, and in fact it may require a bit of time to degenerate her.
                  The manuals do not offer better informations on time and such, so feel free to use what suits you the most. I think it's quite sensible to assume that removing a bane from a fomori should be easier in the newly created, and harder for the old ones.

                  But, since we're talking about this... what can you see from the Umbra about a Fomori? Do you see the bane, and the bane sees you? Can you attack the possessing spirit? Or is the bane considered as if it were materialized?

                              Originally posted by Maris Streck View Post

                              While being used. Once the Charm is complete, the Bane and the host (usually human) are merged into one being (so no Bane in the Umbra to attack). From canon, it used to be described as just about impossible to save the human at that point. The only thing (pre-W20) that comes to mind is the one Gurahl Rite that has a chance, and that Rite comes with some serious risk.

Either way though, the host is likely to die only if additional precautions aren't taken.                               But yea, once a Fomori is created, it's a material being. When the host dies though, the Bane is freed (and in Slumber, I think).

                                  Before the Possession is complete, the host can try to take action to prevent it, as described on page 26 of Possessed. Changes in behavior can provide a roll to shake off the attempt, and even other humans can attempt simple exorcisms using religious or mystic ceremonies. The Gift: Exorcism can also make the bane shuffle off without harming the prospective host.

                                  (Also, the note about fomori being potentially curable through the Gift Exorcism and healing is from this book, which is from revised, not W20)
