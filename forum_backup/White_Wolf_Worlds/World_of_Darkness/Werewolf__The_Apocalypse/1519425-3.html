                                    ... wow they did Bat dirty... the whole Shadow Lords trying to repent for Bat thing is kinda wasted if Bat's been with the Wyrm since the BSDs happened.

                                    I'm not sure how I feel about having a single Patron for Pentex... and have it be Fly.

                                    While I get the animist setup of WtA pushes spirits into human perceptions of things on some level, this is something that most serious environmentalists abhor: demonizing crucial animals the keep ecosystems healthy. Bats are spooky, and flies are "dirty" but they're essential for a healthy environment. The diversity and population of fly larvae is one of the best indicators of the health of riparian ecosystems despite decades of attempts to come up with something a bit easier than wading into streams, physically collecting a bunch of samples, setting them back, and getting bit by a lot of bugs in the process. And of course, carnivorous bats eat hugs numbers of pests, while pollinator bats are key to plant health that way.

                                    It's easy to get people to care about big cute/awesome/etc. mammals. That's why those are the focus of animal conservation advertising. But the actual work of trying to save ecosystems involves a lot of saving creepy crawly things that make most people's skin itch just looking at them. That was a great thing about having both Rat and Cockroach (though I guess Spider isn't so bad in this regard): animals that you don't want to cuddle (no offense to people that keep any as pets, they're fun pets even if most people aren't into it) are just as important to saving Gaia as the majestic ones.

                                    Whippoorwill didn't make a lick of sense for the BSDs (they're a North American only group within the nightjars), and the whole "goatsucker" myth doesn't have much resonance these days (but at least nightjars had some association with something negative). But surely they could have picked something else.
