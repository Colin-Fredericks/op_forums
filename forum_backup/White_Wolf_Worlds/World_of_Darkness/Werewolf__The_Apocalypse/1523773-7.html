        Linda Davis / Peace Activist (Homid Philodox Child of Gaia)

        As I previously mentioned, the first two Philodox ever were both female Children of Gaia. I've no idea how that happened. I mean, that tribe's basic concept/hook does mesh pretty well with the Philodox's role of peace keeper/mediator. Though I can't help but be curious what would've happened if they'd gone with, say, a Stargazer or Silver Fang in this role.

        So, Linda is essentially your archetypical 1st ed Child of Gaia. A gentle child who always wondered why people couldn't just be nicer to each other. A Greenpeace volunteer from age 14, and president of her college chapter of Amnesty International. (It doesn't say anything about her socioeconomic background, but I'm guessing maybe upper middle class.)
        Her First Change occurred at a protest she'd help organize against an unsafe chemical plant. I fight broke out between plant security and a group of militant environmentalists called the Green Knights. (This group popped up in some of the 1st ed Werewolf material, and then just kind of vanished, similarly to the Red Circle Wyrm cult.) Guns were involved, and Linda's boyfriend was killed shielding her from gunfire. So she turned into a werewolf and killed one of the security guards.
        Needless to say, the Delirium made the mass panic worse, but some of the Green Knights were presumably Kinfolk as they were able to talk her down and give her an inkling of what had happened. Afterwords, none of her friends wanted to be around her anymore so she called a number given to her and was introduced to her Tribe.
        (It doesn't specify if this rejection by her former friends was a result of the Curse, a misremembering of what happened at the protest, or both. I like to think both.)
        Now she continues her crusade to save the planet, while also working to restrain the violent impulses of the Garou, including her own.

        The image is a young woman of college age (20, maybe?) with not quite shoulder length hair, wearing a flower print dress with a pair of necklaces. One is a peace sign. The other is a little harder to make out, but appears to be a Pan-African symbol. Looking at the character's face, I think she's meant to be either African-American or mixed race. In all honesty, I could easily see her growing up on The Cosby Show.

        For Attributes, Physicals are tertiary, all at 2. Socials are secondary, with a 3/2/3 split. That leaves Mental as primary, with one extra dot from freebies, at 4/3/4. While I might could make an argument for one of those 4s being in Intelligence rather than Perception or Wits, I think this is fine as it is.

        For Abilities, Talents are secondary with an extra dot from freebies. She has 3 each in Empathy and Expression, and 1 each in Alertness, Athletics, Brawl, and Dodge. I mildly question the presence of Brawl. While I can see it being something she's been forced to learn since her first change, it feels maybe a little off.
        Skills are tertiary, with 2 in Etiquette, and 1 each in Animal Ken, Drive, and Performance. Given her background, and how it specifically says she was the leader of her college chapter of AI and that she helped organize the protest, I feel she should have Leadership. I'd probably move that dot in Performance, and possibly even the one in Animal Ken for a rating of 2.
        Knowledge is primary with two extra dots from freebies. She has 3 each in Law, Politics, and Rituals, a 2 in Medicine, and 1 each in Enigmas, Investigation, Linguistics, and Science. I'm guessing maybe a pre-Law/Political Science major? But a solid spread of abilities.

        Her Backgrounds points - the measly two that Children of Gaia got in 1st ed plus one more from freebies - are 1 each in Past Life, Resources, and Pack Totem. With 2nd ed's five points, I'd probably make that 2 each in the first and leave Pack Totem at 1.

        Her Rage is the base 3. Gnosis is 4, so that's six freebie points. Willpower is 6, thanks to another two freebies.

        So, for those with a sharp eye, that's 5 freebies for Attributes, 6 for Abilities, 1 for Backgrounds, 6 for Gnosis, and 2 for WP, for a grand total of 20 freebie points. Damn. It's been a while since I've seen a character sheet this out of wack points wise.
        My solution to this is to drop either the dot in Brawl or one of her Knowledge dots and at least two of the Gnosis dots. That leaves you with a single freebie point, which I would probably spend on another level of Pack Totem (or maybe Resources if you want to stick with 1st ed rules).

        The stat problems aside, I think this is a pretty good character. While the write up is about as long as the one for Snarls-At-Fleas (and yes, both are longer than Janet's), I think it spends at least half of that extra word count on talking about the Green Knights instead of about Linda herself. While I think the description of the protest and what happened do a lot to define her character and what is probably a major trauma associated with her Change, I would've liked to see a little more of the aftermath and how it impacted her. But that aside, I think she works well, with a strong hook for wanting to be a mediator and try to reign in the worst aspects of the rest of her pack. I also like the idea of a Child of Gaia character who has experienced first hand brutal violence in the face of peaceful activism, triggering her own Rage, but still trying to remain committed to her ideals. I think there's a lot to work with here for any player who wants to run this character long term. So I chalk this up as a win.

                                Once again a Children of Gaia Philodox with an obvious focus on peace and mediation. But the writing is still solid. And the backstory is more solid here than it was for the Galliard. The backstory links nicely with a character-motivation for how to roleplay the character. But rather than restraint, I think the disconnect between having such an emphasized background in activism (both in terms of the environment and human rights) should clash very hard with the warrior-culture of the Garou. The writing even hints at the fact that pre-First-Change her beliefs weren't aligned with the aggressive/violent tactics of the Green Knights. Of course, the war against the Wyrm paints a different picture of what the conflict is like in contrast to the sort of tactics a mundane activist-group would consider and how the actual opposition is characterized.

                                Stat-wise it's weird that the character's connection to this Green Knight group isn't represented. And then there's of course the whole Freebie-issue... But it's also noticeable how all the Homid-characters have Resources 1 (with the Ragabash having Resources 2). Also, that focus on Mental Attributes and Knowledge-Abilities is a bit surprising. From the backstory, I would expect this character to put far more dots into the social realm of things. She's supposed to be an activist, not a deep scholar on a subject. Like, where do these three dots on Law come from? The next character is much worse than this but the spread of dots goes way too hard into a specialized direction, I feel like. The dots should be spread around a bit more.

                                                The Green Knights are one of those early period gems that I love and that I’d love to take some time to flesh our more one day. While the name is already pretty clear cut on the face of it (Green for the environment and Knights because they’re warriors/guardians) the name also feels evocative of the Arthurian mythos in particular the famous Green Knight of Gawain & the Green Knight. It’s that element of the name which makes me wonder if there was ever any idea that the Green Knights might be a possible cross-over mechanism for Garou and Verbena Mages or Changelings on the environmental militancy tip.

                                                        Originally posted by No One of Consequence View Post
                                                        Warsinger (Lupus Galliard)

                                                        So, this one starts out with another song quote. But somewhat surprisingly, it's by Jethro Tull. I was honestly expecting another female artist. It would've made an interesting and appropriate theme, if nothing else. There's also a second quote from John Irving's The Hotel New Hampshire. I actually find this mildly annoying. The lay out for a template write up can only include so many words, about 500 IIRC. So, including an extra quote is effectively padding which wastes words that could be better spent on the actual character.

                                                        That said, this is an interesting character. Born as a wolf, after undergoing the first change she decided that being human was much more fun and exciting. Especially human music. She taught herself to play guitar. Being a werewolf is one big party to her.

                                                        It describes her as being "trained as a witch" and Rituals being her life blood. This is something I'll come back to in a bit. She may be part of the Moon-Daughters camp, but it's not exactly clear on that aspect.

                                                        Her equipment, in addition to a trench knife and sawed-off shotgun, includes a "serious collection of guitars, amps, and hard rock CDs." Now you may be asking yourself, "Self, just how does a wolf-born Garou come to own a bunch of somewhat expensive musical instruments and equipment?" And the answer to that is apparently that she has 2 dots in Resources. There's probably a really weird and potentially interesting backstory behind that. Unfortunately, absolutely nothing in this write up mentions it. I'll also mention that she has 4 dots in Performance with a specialization in "Guitar". Lupus, of course, have restrictions on certain Skills and Knowledges they can use their basic points on, based on the idea that they'd have no way to learn them before their first change. Performance isn't one of those restricted Skills, but I sort of feel like specializing in "Guitar" is mildly violating the spirit of those restrictions. Likewise, I've always sort of wondered why Lupus didn't also have certain restrictions on some Backgrounds. Like, for example, Resources. So, all in all, this is just sort of a weird aspect of this character.

                                                        The character image is really great. Probably my favorite of the bunch, to be honest. The same sort of early 90s GenX riot grrl archetype as the Street Rat, just as an attractive and hot wild child instead of an ugly and obnoxious gutter punk. She's wearing cut off shorts and a crop top, with tattoos (including one on her arm that's the tribe symbol that apparently turns into a dragon or something near the bottom). And holding a bloody knife. I like it quite a bit.

                                                        Attributes have Physicals as secondary, and once again with an extra dot, for a 3/3/3 spread. Socials are primary, with a 4/3/3 spread. That leaves Mentals as tertiary, with a 1/2/3 spread. So she's meant to be kind of oblivious, I guess? The text doesn't really indicate anything about it.
                                                        For Abilities, Talents are primary with an extra dot. Brawl, Expression, and Streetwise are 3 each, Alertness and Intimidation are 2 each, and Primal Urge is 1. (I always kind of wondered why Streetwise wasn't on the list of restricted abilities.) Skills are secondary with an extra dot. Besides that 4 in Performance, she has 2 each in Drive and Survival, and 1 each in Melee and Repair. Drive is one of the restricted skills for Lupus. (You'd think maybe Repair would be as well, but no.) Knowledge is tertiary, with 1 dot each in Enigmas and Occult, and 3 dots in ... Music? It's sort of penciled in at the very bottom of the official list of knowledges. Remember what I said earlier about the character being trained as a witch and loving Rituals. Well, she has no points in the Rituals knowledge. I feel those Music dots are a total waste and should've been spent to raise Rituals and Occult or Enigmas to 2. (Also, the character has a sawed-off shotgun, but no Firearms.)

                                                        Backgrounds are 2 each in Contacts and Resources. Rage is 6, which seems mostly OK for this character. Gnosis is ... 3. Yeah, Lupus get that at 5 minimum. So, I've no idea what's going on here. Either a misprint or a mistake on the writer's part. But if the latter, then the Developer should've caught it during the redlining of the first draft. Willpower is also 6.

                                                        Once again, I don't hate this character. It's actually pretty interesting. But I'm not sure it's the best example of a Black Fury, especially a Black Fury Lupus. Even with the Galliard aspect, I feel like this is stuck in a lot of the very early presentation of "Galliard = Musician/Performer". Especially the whole rock guitarist thing. If this character had been one of the templates in Ways of the Wolf (a book I'll be getting to very soon), I think it would have fit better. Especially if it had been the Ragabash one.

                                                        I also find it kind of bizarre that we have a Homid character who is "more wolf than wolf" and a Lupus character who wants to be a human. I honestly can't help but wonder if it'd been better if they'd been switched, with the Theurge being a Lupus and this one being a Homid.

                                                        Next (hopefully tomorrow), the Ahroun, and some final thoughts.

                                                        After that, I'm debating if I should do Bone Gnawers or Ways of the Wolf next.
                                                        A few interesting facts about this template
                                                        1. Sawed-off shotgun with no firearms skill? You don't have to actually use it, the intimidation factor is good enough
                                                        2. Resources for lupus? That's no problem for me either. I believe that, as a neophyte, she could fanatically learn to play guitar so she could play on the streets and earn some money. If the rest of her sept could afford to buy her some guitars, amps and CDs they would get a fanatically devoted member. Or maybe she found some of them in the thrash can.

                                                        I actually used this template during my games. She was a member of a heavy metal band (Spooky Tooth like), composed also of Janet Crowley (the founder, Gaillard Shadow Lord bassist from Rite of Passage), Gaillard template from Glass Walkers tribebook on vocals and lead guitar and Chris Wlodkowsky, Get of Fenris kinfolk drummer mentioned in Kinfolk: Unsung Heroes. Me and my players even wrote some lyrics, created a name and we almost created a real band based on this. One of the best moments during our games was when this template wanted to get something for Chris birthday and she bought him an Iron Maiden CD, but the original Maiden's one, from 1969.

                                                        And another fun fact: in the cutscenes for the cancelled Heart of Gaia video game is a Black Fury called Warsinger that looks very similar to this template.

                                                        Pure among the dirt
