            Evan is explicitly white (and supposedly the first white person to become a member of Younger Brother). While he might have native ancestry, he's not a recognized member of any tribe. Having some distant blood relatives doesn't make you automatically part of one. If even hadn't had his first change, he'd just be some boring white dude that probably wouldn't have even know his great-grandparent or something wasn't white unless he decided to do some genetic test for shits and giggles. If he didn't have Garou and Kin vouching for him, he'd never be allowed in Native spaces.

            I'm well aware that there are white-passing Native Americans that don't live on reservation, but there's a difference between growing up raised in a culture even if you don't look like what outsiders would think of, and finding out you're 1/16th or whatever and then trying to claim you're a member of that tribe/people. Evan is as much a Native American as Elizabeth Warren, he just exists in a world with spirits that are going around forcing Younger Brother to accept him as one of their own (which doesn't mean any human Native American tribe has actually done so).
