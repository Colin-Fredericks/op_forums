    That moment when...

    01-29-2016, 09:59 AM
    Players do the unthinkable or go off on a tangent that was unexpected.

    We've probably all had them. The ones that spring to mind for me is the pack on the hunt for a deadly bsd pack and the Red Talon decides to forget that and start killing humans in the city while the rest of pack look on in horror and shock.

    The pack trying to get permission to enter a silver Fang sept and are put through tests of honor and loyalty upon being met to be allowed entrance to the sacred caern and the Fianna pulls a silver Klaive on the guardian and challenges them.

    The Bonegnawer who pushes his own pack mate that just healed him into the path of Thrall Frenzying foe rather than push him out of the way to keep it away from him.

    But my current mind boggling moment. The pack are looking into prophecies of the Apocalypse. They each get a prophecy. The metis gets one of an important perfect metis cub being born to two metis parents. The pack decide to investigate. They find the parents bodies bloodied and tattered and track the wyrm taint and blood and after some time tracking BSDs Banes and all sorts of bad end up face to face with Eeyarlagh Twice-Born holding the newborn child of destiny. Just Eeyarlagh and them. A pack of hardened garou.

    They decide dealing with the cub is above their pay grade (Fosterns, some with enough renown for Adren), and not knowing what to do with the baby they decide to let Eeyarlagh go with the child. Not that they, gaian garou who've spent over a year battling bsds, who've seen and been tortured by the evils of that tribe, who know through portents and visions that the child is important. Not that anyone garou of higher rank or importance at any of the other 4 caerns they've recently been involved with, nor they themselves were good enough or a better choice than this fanatic bsd. Nope. They even wished her good luck.

    I really did not see that coming.

    anyone else have any remembered moments? Or think the pack above should just be outright torn to shreds for giving an orphaned baby garou, destined to be of importance shared via visions from gaia, from a mysterious birth from between two sterile mules, that arrived as the red star in the sky first peaked through the umbral night skies over to an agent to the Wyrm?

                                      As for how they come up with this stuff. I asked my group, on reflection even they have no idea why. They seem absolutely confused as to why they did that.

                                      From a point of observation it could be fear, uncertainty, that choice to turn a blind eye rather than make a bad decision and be held accountable later because it wasn't the right choice or handled perfectly. Leave it someone else now and if its a problem later we'll sort it out later once someone ekse tells us how and is willing to be at the other end of the finger point if it goes wrong. Maybe? I dont know. Wasn't in their brains at the time.

                                      Why be ok to icly take drugs and then not fight? Maybe being the centre of attention and perhaps being free or directed to act destructively with a clause of 'it wasnt me, the drugs made me do it' was perhaps more enjoyable than the risk of the character being in actual danger of death against the enemy in battle. I've certainly seen more characters willing to start something with allies on an even power level of themselves than take on the unknown power levels of the enemy. Maybe? I dont know, I dont play like that.

                                      why get yourself killed by others of your Sept? Again I dont know. Perhaps unhappiness with the character or the game? Maybe the fact they were thewolf among the fera and the closest reminder of atrocities of the garou against the other changers and decided 'if I get you to kill me then you're no better and I've proved it.' Who knows, maybe? Not my bag, I'd rather get involved in the story and be known for something great and valorous than getting everyone else to kill my pc.

                                      It could be suggested that sometimes a game is seen as a personal place that must revolve around that individual and what they, the PC and player want over the story itself. If it doesn't, then they do what they can to distance the plot they are not interested in or try to create a side plot just for them.

                                      But who'd admit to that? and I cant be sure that's why.

                                      It's a good question though and perhaps watching to see how they roll with the repercussions and in game demands that follow is a good way to determine whether it was an innocent slip of the mind, or something else.

                                      Thoughts, experiences, more WTF moments anyone?
