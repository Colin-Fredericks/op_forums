    Changing Tribe Totems

    02-24-2016, 05:14 PM
    I'm doing this to put out ideas on changing what tribes have what totems. The reason why is because when you actually put thought into it, the totems of a number of tribes don't make sense or White Wolf used some pretty absurd justifications for them.
    -Black Furies should not have Pegasus as a totem. Pegasus is a male and was conceived through rape and was born from the murder of Medusa. Aspects of that can be argued about why the Furies would or would not serve Pegasus but I'm not trying to get into that. My suggestion is instead give them Owl. In mythology the owl was the sacred animal of Athena which I think would be a good fit for the Furies.
    -Bone Gnawers I haven't got to yet, probably would keep their totem
    -Children of Gaia I also haven't got to yet
    -Fianna have Stag which seems appropriate, at least for the time
    -Get of Fenris are kind of ok
    -Glass Walker I haven't thought too much on
    -Red Talons I don't think should have Griffon for a couple reasons, one of which is they are species purists and Griffon is a mix of two animals, another is I think Griffon might be better for the Silver Fang, see below
    -Shadow Lords are great with Grandfather Thunder
    -Silent Striders wouldn't have owl anymore and I don't think it was too appropriate anyway, instead they should have Jackal for several reasons, some less obvious than others
    -Silver Fangs have Falcon which I'm not against but I think Griffon would be better, not how crazy Griffon is currently written but as he is a symbol of as part king of the sky and predator king.
    -Uktena and Wendigo I'm not getting into, at least for now

          If we're going to redo the Tribal Totems, I'd rather move away from the (mainly) animal/mythological creature approach to them.

          I'd rather see far more conceptual Totems, which could have many in-character names/avatars, with the animal/myths/personifications being highly associated with the Tribes because of their associations with those concepts. This also allows a lot more focus on the themes of the Tribe over the cultural stereotypes of them.

          As an example, the Glasswalkers could follow Techne (cribbing that from recent musing about MtAw 2e). Techne is the spirit of technological innovation, that exists in an odd balance between the Wyld and Weaver. Techne is somewhat fickle, not caring about either the long traditions of ancient crafting techniques or the ethical ramifications of creativity. Instead Techne is the patron of inspired genius that pushes boundaries and creates new inventions for all. Cockroach, and her brood, is a favored lieutenant to Techne. Few animal spirits can keep up with the rapid adaptation Techne inspires, while being bound to the human world of technology and science.

                  Depends which version of the mythology you're using.

                  It's most likely that Athena victim-cursing Medusa was actually a later addition to the story, meaning that the Furies could easily see it as men rewriting the "truth" to perpetuate patriarchal revisions. In the oldest versions, all of the Gorgons were presented as born monsters, with Medusa just being the least monstrous of them (or able to pass as human more easily, or something, it's pretty vague stuff). Medusa seemed to get "prettier" as time went on... until Ovid wrote the version we're most familiar with down where she was born stunningly beautiful, only to be raped by Poseidon in Athena's temple, and getting cursed by Athena for it (including Ovid writing it as an explicit statement that she got "what she deserved").

                  While the Furies might not like Athena, they probably don't like a myth being twisted by rich men to include a, "beautiful women deserve to be raped for being so good looking men can't resist it," aside.
