                                    I think "no more Metis" could happen two different ways. One is that the Metis breed is preserved, but they are now "perfect Metis" with no deformities or infertility to disgrace them; however, many of their gifts are associated with the suffering that comes from being Metis. Would the spirits refuse to teach those gifts to "perfect" meris breed Garou? The second way is to give the offspring the breed of the mother, and do away with the metis breed entirely. Then you lose what the breed contributes. Either way, Garou society would probably freak out and have trouble figuring out how to deal with the change, and what it would mean in the grand scheme of things. Would it be a sign of the apocalypse? Would it give hope that the war against the Wyrm could now be more winnable?
