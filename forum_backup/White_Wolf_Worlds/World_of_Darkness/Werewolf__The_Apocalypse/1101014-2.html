        I think Lunatics and Ronin are a much underutilized tool in the setting. They are an excellent way to introduce a more traditional or "cinematic" werewolf into the setting that is not disruptive. Your group the Currs is a good example of what could be done with them.

        Ronins make good foils for Garou especially those not exiled Garou for crimes, but who have adapted to the Change entirely outside Garou culture. Simply by refusing to join the Garou Nation, they are rebuking and insulting them. But as antagonists they are not obviously of the Wyrm, and therefore produce a different dynamic of how to deal with them. Since Ronins are almost universally going to be Homids, they only start with 1 Gnosis so their connection to Gaia is extremely weak and therefore there is no real loyalty to Gaia even if told about it. And while young kids who Change are young enough to become indoctrinated by Garou, Lost Cubs are much less likely to accept this new culture uncritically.

        The books' traditional Ronin, someone exiled for a crime who goes from sept to sept looking for scraps and occasionally working dirty jobs at them, has a role in the game too. But I can think of lots more things I can do with werewolves who aren't affiliated with the Garou Nation.

                Alright here are some more changes I do for my chronicles.

                FERA
                I go very low on the population of the Fera. In general, I try to tone down the population of supernatural creatures to keep things “realistic” and not “gonzo” so I'm very conservative compared to some STs. I rarely ever use them although if the geography of the setting indicates there may be some Fera there, I'll put one or a few in my chronicle notes.

                The vast majority of Bastet reflect the ethnicity of the regions the werecreatures inhabit. Ethnic European homid bastet are extremely rare unless there is good historical reason there is a population for them. The Ceilican are practically extinct and only exist because I like Val Lewton's 1942 movie Cat People. A few are born every generation, but they have no practical knowledge of their ancestry or Fera culture although there are resources out there that could help them. Since games are mainly set in North America, that means Pumonca and Qualmi could be present. I wouldn't use others unless there was a major plot/story that needed it.

                Corax have their own issues between their Eurasian and North American homid populations like the Garou, but they've handled it better.

                Kitsune exists outside of Japan although the tribe's population is better preserved in East Asia then elsewhere. There is a reason Europeans know the exploits of Reynard the Fox.

                Mokole are werecrocodiles and have no dinosaur form. They do have a form of Gaian memory and therefore knowledge of a past before the arrival of man, but my prehistory is very different than published ones as the Changing Breeds were only created about 12,000 years ago as the Ice Age ended and the Holocene began.

                Nuwisha are considered the little cousin or nephew of the Pure Ones. Therefore the Wendigo and Uktena have strong ties to them, and the rest of the Garou Nation is very accepting to them as much as they can tolerate Coyote. They are treated less like they are one of the Fera separate from the Garou and more like the red headed stepchild who is still family.

                Ratkin do not have a large population. Too many of those infected with the Birthing Plague just die. I like the idea they possibly pose a future threat, but not an incipient one.

                SPIRITS
                As per the old White Wolf errata, Scrags have only 6 Rage. This was something that really should have been updated in W20.

                I do my best to make spirits an important part of the game. Named spirits are important part of the chronicle's NPCs. I have my players document every spirit who teaches them Gifts. I try to have at least half a dozen or so Jagglings around the area I can use as the basis for spirit interactions between themselves, NPC Garou, and the PCs. Spirits have their likes and dislikes, and being friendly to one may mean some other spirit becomes not so friendly. I roleplay most of my Gaian spirits based on what I read in Aesop's Fables, Bambi, and Watership Down. It gives Theurges something to do, and even for the other auspices usually the Pack Totem can act as interpreter for them.

                Bat and Whipporwill were originally Gaian totems corrupted by the Wyrm. I've added several more cases of such spirits. They are either spirits of animals traditionally associated with witchcraft, or are vectors of diseases. If Garou see them, they become suspicious they are being spied on by the Wyrm. Such spirits include Toad, Moth, Tick, and Fly. Their Incarna serve the Wyrm now. In addition, there are spirits which are not fully corrupted, but many individual spirits serve the Wyrm even if their Incarna does not. They include Vulture, Mosquito, Spider, and Rat. Temperate and artic mosquitoes are geneally Gaian and allied to Wendigo; but tropical Mosquitoes that spread disease are in service to the Wyrm. Most Rat spirits are Gaian, but there are a noticeable number of Wyrm-aligned ones. (Obviously this is deeply embrassing for the Bone Gnawers). And Black Cats serve the Wyrm just because, the evil little bastards.

                Mother Night is a major antagonist in the setting, a powerful spirit allied with the Wyrm. She, her brood, and her servants (including Fomori) are up there with Black Spiral Dancers and PENTEX as a Wyrm faction. Mother Night and her brood are not the night spirits who love Gaia. Mother Night is the primeval darkness that exsisted before Helios. She is the terrible darkness and the evil that would happen if not for the light of Luna. Mother Night is responsible for the most of the malevolent witch cults in history, and is responsible for all sorts of monsters that thrive in night. The vampires who call themselves Bahari worship her in one of her guises.

                I have lots of Wyrm sorcerers whose magic is mainly summoning various Banes to serve them. Most Banes printed in the books are actually not very appropriate for this so I have to make my own. (There are some like Scryers that can work as is fine, and home made spirit servants of some of the Black Spiral Dancer totems are also good). Many are based on either real folklore or the Call of Cthulhu roleplaying game. Umbral Stalkers (Dimensional Shamblers) and Malfean Steeds (Byakhee) can be very useful. Plus they make serviceable opponents for PCs should a fight break out.

                ​Still more to come.

                                    PENTEX
                                    Many changes here as I find the canonical version cartoonish and ridiculous. There is a core PENTEX, but many of the PENTEX subsidiaries are actually unrelated companies somehow involved with the Wyrm. Others are inadvertently spreading Wyrm taint somehow, but aren't part of any occult conspiracy. Some of the companies I can't see in anyway as having real value to the game (as part of some Wyrm cult) in which case I use them merely as names for companies in the setting as needed.

                                    PENTEX still exists based on a core of companies responsible for most of the major industrial pollutants on the planet so they are mainly heavy industry and chemicals plus other manufacturing that may produce particularly toxic waste. They are much less likely to sell branded products to consumers as they are to sell raw materials to the companies that do. PENTEX itself is a holding company, but mainly a conspiracy that extends beyond it. Deep Umbral spirits of the Wyrm seek to anti-terraform Earth into an entirely new ecosystem so that those “spirits” can arrive and make this planet their new home. They've recruited greedy, short sighted humans to assist them in this, some of whom become Toad fomori. They only create other Fomori in an attempt to defend themselves from werewolves. This PENTEX is heavily inspired by the movie They Live, and yes the look of the aliens there is how the Toad fomori appear. And the billboards say things like CONSUME and OBEY once you peek into the Umbra in those areas that are dominated by the Wyrm and Weaver (areas where there are Blights and also CyberRealm and Scar). John Nada would simply be a normal human who achieved partial enlightenment so that he could contact the Periphery. As such, I don't use any of the “Board of Directors” ever mentioned in any of the books. Just for convenience, I might reuse some of the names. But I don't use anything like there's a Malkavian anti-tribu or Black Spiral Dancer on the board.

                                    Endron Oil is allied with the PENTEX conspiracy, but has another agenda. It is in the service of a particular Bane (once imprisoned by the Uktena) it freed during initial oil drilling over a century ago. That Bane controls the company through its own cult, and the companies' drilling operations are to free similar Banes. Despite the similarity of names, Endron is not based on Enron, but is a much more traditional oil company. I keep a lot of the original backstory of Premium Oil and the Lassiter family for this.

                                    Magadon Pharmaceutical is its own separate Wyrm conspiracy controlled by cults devoted to Wakshaa and Hrugg. The cult wants to produce a new breed of man so it can survive the Apocalypse. They produce some very awful Fomori. PENTEX and they are aware of each other, and occasionally cooperate but since they serve different masters they don't get along and have different goals.

                                    Black Dog Game Factory exists as its own separate thing, and they are just your traditional Wyrm occultists who hope to recruit new people into “Satanic” worship through occult inspired RPGs. It has no ties to PENTEX and the Banes involved are very different. I just liked the idea of an actual RPG occult conspiracy. However, I've never come up with any major plots about it. Just little ones like the friend of a young kinfolk to a PC getting messed up in it so I can use their backstory for minor plots.

                                    Companies like King Breweries and O'Tolley's do produce Wyrm tainted products consumed by people, but their occult involvement is inadvertent. All they really do is produce enough low grade crap that it “clogs” people's system enough to cut them off from Gaia on an instinctual level. It also desensitizes people enough to open them up to future Bane possession once exposed to actual toxic stuff. Key members of these companies do belong to a Wyrm cult controlled by PENTEX who make sure their products are tainted, but that is really their only role. Nobody becomes a Fomori because they ate a quarter pounder with cheese.

                                    But many of the other named companies are not simply involved in any Wyrm conspiracy. They are placeholder names that I use either as background, or perhaps as some plot if I need a company like them. But the plot likely has nothing to do with PENTEX, but maybe it might involve vampires or one of my other Wyrm cults. And if it does, the amount of actual control or influence they have may be far less than absolute. More often than not, they are just ordinary companies who if they do something anti-Gaian, it is because they are greedy and shortsighted, not members of a Wyrm cult.

                                        We had major revisions for the tribes.

                                        To begin, the Black Furries aren't the Wyld tribe. That is now the Red Talons shtick. It feels more appropriate and gives the Talons a little bit more of an ideology than just being the Lupus tribe.

                                        The Bone Gnawers and Glass Walkers have been merged. It's more the Gnawers, left alone in the cities, evolved into embracing urban and technological spirits, turning into a tribe similar to the Glass Walkers. It gives the hybrid tribe more fluidity than either being city elite or bums, but ranges across the spectrum of urban society. Plus I like the Glass Walkers having a more Bone Gnawer-esque social structure and ideological influence about what they do.

                                        Another tribal merger are the Children of Gaia and Stargazers into the Children of Derga. This hybrid tribe I feel compliments each together than existing separately. Their homeland is in India and Tibet, and then they spread out from there. Stargazers could exist as a faction of the Children that left to fully join the Beast Courts.

                                        Shadow Lords are all Hakken, and are more spread out across East Asia than being solely in Japan. There's enough European tribes, and the Euro Shadow Lords often come off as one dimensional 'evil Silver Fangs' that have this almost cartoonish eternal aim to over throw the Silver Fangs, but fail to do so (though in revised I understand this has been given a better history, but still). As Hakken, and having kin among the East Asian civilizations, as well as being members of the Beast Courts, gives them more of a natural opposition to the Silver Fangs.

                                        Silver Fangs, instead of being associated with a European state that didn't come into existence until the middle of the Medieval era have replaced the Shadow Lords as the tribe whose human kin ran with the steppe peoples of Asia. They used the various steppe empires to dominate, and it wasn't until the Mongol Empire that they really put effort in breeding with the various nobilities of Europe and Asia.

                                        Get of Fenris and Fianna are more spread out across the European and Mediterranean spectrum since Celtic and Germanic peoples got around since the Classic Age. Like for instance, you can find Get septs in North Africa thanks to the Vandal Empire.

                                        The Siberakh are a full tribe, and considered 'lost sibling' to the Pure Tribes. They have kin among the Inuit, and served as bridge between the Pure Tribes and the rest of the Garou. They also found themselves acting as mediators between the Silver Fangs and Shadow Lords over their rivalries across Asia.

                                        Kucha Ekundu are another sub-tribe that I've been thinking of elevating to full tribe.

                                        The White Howlers/Black Spiral Dancers have been changed from a Pict originating tribe to having their human kin among the Baltic and Uralic peoples. They fell in the middle ages during the Baltic genocides.

                                        No notable changes to the Silent Striders or Bunyip. Though for the latter, in regards to what they did with the Ajaba, I'm inclined to say they were changed by Gaia in the same way to be their own changing breed. Then there's also the option they simply bred with Dingos too, avoiding all the weirdness.

                                        More or less we wanted to have the Garou of the Eastern Hemisphere to be more Eurasian, with some overlap with the Beast Courts, and down play the more ethnic stereotypes that a lot of the tribes fall into. We also tried to emphasize the tribes being more allied camps that are linked by their totem affiliations.

                                        The umbra, particularly in the Americas was completely redone, but that would take a massive post to get into that. This goes into how we redid the entire supernatural world of the Americas to reflect more the legacy of indigenous institutions rather than the bland generic setting it turned out to be in cannon.

                                        As for the Wyrm we've largely ignored Pentex, and focused more on fighting BSDs, Banes, and echoing mythic cycles. It fits WtA better in our opinions. We've toyed around with replacements and reforms for Pentex here and there, but never settled on anything concrete.
