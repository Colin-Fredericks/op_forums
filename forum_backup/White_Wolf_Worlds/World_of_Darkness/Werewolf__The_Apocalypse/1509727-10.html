    They should have already released a statement saying that they're aware of what happened, that they are trying to get into contact with Tāme Iti, that they are cutting that artwork from the product and that they're going to sever ties with the artist. That's all stuff that's probably going to end up happening anyway, but the longer White Wolf waits the worse it will look for them.

                            The tweet and the preview are gone from the Renegade site. Outstar just posted a formal response

                            A piece of art in the Werewolf: The Apocalypse 5th Edition Core Rulebook contained an illustration that depicted the likeness of Tāme Wairere Iti, a key figure of the Māori protest movement, as a member of the Glass Walker tribe of the Garou. We apologize to Mr. Iti, and to the Māori community for any harm caused.

                            We have taken action to remove the art from the digital PDF and physical copies of the book, which will be available in August.

                            We missed the mark and are reviewing our image approval process to catch these instances before our books go to print. We are grateful to the World of Darkness community for contacting us about the illustration.​

                                        I genuinely hope this will make them actually audit the art better and hire better artists. Because as is shown in the twitter thread, copying directly from an image (NOT using it as a reference) seems to be a genuine issue with WoD's artists. Hence why the art has looked so stiff and uninspired as of late, this is just the one time shit hit the fan HARD.

                                        This is especially silly since you can find artists who could do WoD-style art easily without taking the easy way out and copying from google images.

                                        I'm glad that the image is going to be removed, regardless.

                                        EDIT: To clarify, referencing is when you look at pictures to get an idea of how something looks and works. Copying is when you copy the picture as is. If the artist had referenced a picture of Tāme Wairere, the final result would a looked similar, but not so that you can tell who it was based on.
