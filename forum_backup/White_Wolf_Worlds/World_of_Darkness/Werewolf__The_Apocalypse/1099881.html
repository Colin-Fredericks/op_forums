    The Origins of the Fifteen Tribes

    06-03-2017, 03:15 PM
    Garou are hybrids of humans and gray wolves, which histrionically existed throughout all of Europe and the majority of Asia and North America, so it would make sense for the Garou to have existed anywhere there were wild gray wolf population. Among the Garou, one tribe was unambiguously Asia (Stargazer), one tribe was unambiguously Australian origin (Bunyip), one tribe was unambiguously North Africa (Silent Striders), three tribes were unambiguously European origin (Fianna, Get of Fenris, and White Howlers), and three tribes were unambiguously North American (Croatan, Uktenu, and Wendigo). Of the remaining seven Tribes, however, they seem to actually have an Asian origin, which I will explain.

    Black Furies are Amazons, which were historically associated with Scythian tribes of Central Asia, and probably migrated from Central Asia into Europe during prehistory. Bone Gnawers and Glass Walkers seem to have originated in West Asia, with the rise of the city-states of the Middle East, and migrated into East Asia and South Asia after the establishment of the Silk Road and into Europe during the rise of the Roman Empire. The Silver Fangs and the Shadow Lords both seem to have originated in North Asia (the existence of the Siberakh offers evidence of the North Asian origin of the Silver Fangs while the existence of the Hakken offers evidence of the North Asian origin of the Shadow Lords) and migrated into Central Asia (and through Central Asia into Europe) and East Asia and South Asia during prehistory. The Children of Gaia are probably the original South Asian Tribe of Garou, though I am not sure. as their origins seem to be much more fuzzy. The Red Talons would have also likely been Asian in origin, there was four times as much wolf habitat in Asia than in Europe, though they would have likely spread into Europe during prehistory.

    The Garou Nation would just be the result of the European Garou conquering the North American Garou. The Garou outside of Europe and North America would either belong to the Beast Courts (if in East Asia) or local tribal organizations. I am curious if anyone sees anything wrong with my analysis or if you would like to present an alternative analysis.
