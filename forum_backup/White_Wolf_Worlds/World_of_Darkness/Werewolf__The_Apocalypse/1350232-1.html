    What exactly is the "War of Rage" is something that should be taken with a grain of salt. The books make it sound like a formal war, but my personal opinion is that it is most like a construct of Garou historians in the same way that the Hundred Years War. (The Hundred Years War is something historians invented to link together several related, but distinct wars between the French and English. No one actually living at the time ever considered these separate events to be a single war.)

    In most published texts, the War of Rage is a distinct event that occurred after the Impergium ended.

    It is highly likely, IMHO, that the "War of Rage" combined many individual conflicts between the Garou and other Fera that have no actual connection to each other except for the fact that each conflict involves the Garou fighting other Fera. I believe the Garou, after the fact, combined various events and epic poems concerning these conflicts into one single narrative.
