    I've come up with a few Fomori concept ideas

    10-14-2024, 04:56 PM
    A woman named Beth who went to Dr. Veridian's Workout Program and came out as a special case. I got the idea from reading a little bit of Freak Legion and seeing a gigantic muscle babe fomor tearing a Stargazer in half.

    Equipment: none
    Attributes: strength 3, dexterity 2, stamina 3, charisma 3, manipulation 2, appearance (sexy) 5, perception 3, intelligence 3, wits 3
    Abilities: athletics 2, brawl 2, empathy 1, intimidation 3, subterfuge 2, drive 1
    Fomori Powers: mega-strength (+3) enhancement, body expansion, immunity to the delirium
    Taints: derangement (misandry, sadism, homicidal) mental de-evolution (+3; body expansion)


    A girl named Becky whose life changed after she started eating at O'Tolley's, she got addicted to the stuff and got hungrier and hungrier. Then everything took a turn when one day she was trying to save money because her habit was expensive but got hungry again, she discovered that she could swallow anything the size of a medium-sized dog or a small person. Possessed by a Bane of Consumption, from then on she started eating cats, dogs, small children and midgets. She looks relatively normal if a bit on the chubby side, she wears jeans and a t-shirt with a little bit of her gut poking out.

    Nature: predator
    Demeanor: confidant
    Equipment: none
    Attributes: strength 2, dexterity 2, stamina 2, charisma 2, manipulation 2, appearance 2, perception 3, intelligence 2, wits 2
    Abilities: athletics 1, brawl 1, dodge 1, empathy 1, streetwise 1, subterfuge 2, drive 1
    Fomori Powers: mouth of the wyrm, stomach pumper, immunity to the delirium, frog tongue
    Taints: addiction (Pentex food products, mouth of the wyrm; hourly cycle) derangement (pica, phagomania and cannibalism)
    Merits & Flaws: hidden power


    A scaly guy named Joey aka Danger Joe aka Jarhead Joe who was a fomor since childhood because of the Action Bill Temporary Tattoos and other products, he was recruited later in life by Pentex. He is an Action Bill super-fan who keeps a shrine in his dorm.

    Nature: addict
    Demeanor: fanatic
    Equipment: Ithaca M-37 shotgun, armor-piercing shotgun rounds, Action Bill Special Police Badge, ID card, rank medallion, M-16 assault rifle with three magazines of ammunition, combat/survival knife with a compass in the handle, flak vest, a salary and a stipend
    Attributes: strength 3, dexterity 3, stamina 3, charisma 1, manipulation 2, appearance 1, perception 3, intelligence 1, wits 1
    Abilities: alertness 1, athletics 2, brawl 2, dodge 2, intimidation 3, streetwise 1, subterfuge 2, firearms 2, melee 2, stealth 3, survival 2, medicine 1
    Backgrounds: equipment/fetish 3, rank 1 (First Team Private), resources 1
    Fomori Powers: shadowplay, wall walking, nightmare command, horns, hide of the wyrm (5), immunity to the delirium
    Taints: brainwashed, derangement (temperamental, obsession), special diet (picky eater), severe allergy (soap and broccoli), addiction (crack cocaine, daily cycle), walking bomb
    Merits & Flaws: deformity


    Some guy named Sid aka Big Smoke who became a Sicklung fomor via the Smoker's Bane program. Because he was a bit angry he was experimented on by Project Illiad for other purposes. This idea has been living in my head rent free for a while.

    Equipment: ID card, rank medallion, M-16 assault rifle with three magazines of ammunition, combat/survival knife with a compass in the handle, flak vest, a salary and a stipend
    Attributes: strength 2, dexterity 2, stamina 2, charisma 2, manipulation 2, appearance 2, perception 3, intelligence 2, wits 2
    Abilities: alertness 1, athletics 1, brawl 2, dodge 2, empathy 1, subterfuge 2, drive 1, firearms 2, melee 2, survival 2, medicine 1
    Backgrounds: equipment/fetish 0, rank 1 (First Team Private), resources 1
    Fomori Powers: noxious breath, immunity (airborne toxins), mega-strength (+3), hazardous breath (lethal smoke), immunity to the delirium
    Taints: breed prejudice (1), doomed (five years), derangement (temperamental), bane attractor, walking bomb, inner volcano

                      I had a couple show up in an arc wherein some kindred had to skirt around getting involved in Garou politics. There was The Gorilla who had putrid blood, 10 health levels, a ton of soak dice, mega strength and was insensate to pain, taking only movement penalties and not dice penalties. I think he also had quick healing and the ability to roll to resist mental gifts/disciplines as though he had a humanity/rage/generation rating.

                      He was supposed to be a minotaur, in a meta sense, not in canon. But the brujah just blood buffed strength activated auto-succ on potence and tried to ambush him with decent success.

                      I also had a Devil Tiger who was able to make 'goblins' attacking the same city and the kindred had to deal with that. For lack of better guidance on how to make those from the KoTE books I have access to I decided to use freak legion.

                      The idea was for a normal person who would just split open and their evil, corrupt hearts (by the devil tiger's reckoning) would become their new heads and they'd have claws, slightly enhanced durability and kindof as an offscreen thing, the ability to appear to be normal humans at a moment's notice. I considered giving them the pathetic ability on the basis of the human shells crying out for help, but decided that was unlikely to work on my PCs due to low humanity scores and didn't bother. Probably would've come up if they sent the coterie of Gangrel neonates after the Wan Kuei though.
