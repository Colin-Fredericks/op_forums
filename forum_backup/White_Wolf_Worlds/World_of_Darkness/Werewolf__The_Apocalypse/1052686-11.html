                            Mind you, I disagree with Aya's assessment that the Camarilla handles Garou better than other factions. This is actually somewhat the same from the Garou's point of view, insomuch as they won't pick random fights with Vampires (which seems to be the overwhelming opinion on these forums from my experience). Garou are much more likely to back off and tail the Vampire to an optimal ambush spot to ensure the best chances of success with the least amount of risk. It's not like 1 leech death is worth rending the Veil.
