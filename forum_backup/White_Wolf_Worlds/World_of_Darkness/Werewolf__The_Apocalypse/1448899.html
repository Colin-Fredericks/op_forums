      Garou are magical war-machines with supernaturally potent claws and fangs.... so generally I don't worry too much about it. Most things the Garou fight either have a close-ish to human anatomy, including major blood vessels all over the place to sever, or are made of spirit-stuff and it doesn't really matter where you hurt them anyway.

      The system also has a few things here:

      1) Targeted attacks have a higher difficulty, reducing average damage. Clawing at a leg to maim it is less likely to result in a fatal blow than just attacking.

      2) A bunch of combat maneuvers like sweeps and kicks don't do damage or default to bashing damage, making if very hard to kill someone. If a Garou kicks you in the crotch, they can hit hard enough to break your hip and KO you, but outright killing would be rare, because they're dealing bashing damage.

      3) Super sized foes tend to have lots of health and/or lots of soak, which means they're hard to take out by just nibbling on their legs. You're probably looking at something that resembles drawing and quartering as a pack of Garou sink into different limbs and rip them off in a group effort.

                                  You are aware that a common way of attempting suicide involves the blood flow to the hand, right? You can bleed to death from a serious laceration to your hand.

                                  Punching someone covered in six inch long magical barbed spikes is a wonderful way to shred every blood vessel in your hand, wrist, and upper arm, which can kill you very quickly.

                                  On top of this porcupine quills are even worse than just being sharp. Their barbs don't just stick in, every muscle movement causes them to keep digging in deeper. Animals that tangle with porcupines frequently die, just not immediately: infection, lose of function of limbs making it hard to catch food, barbs that block the throat, barbs work deeper in an damage arteries or organs, etc. This is just going faster... with a very high level Gift not having quills.

                                  Plus anything in WtA is dealing with the reality of it being used on things with Rage, that can bounce back from fatal damage, and some pretty potent low level healing powers.

                                  You're making a huge deal out of, "...he don't have enough stamina or soaking success the player could be incapacitated..." which is true, but the math of the system doesn't support it being likely.

                                  Lets say our character has Strength 9/Stamina 8 in Crinos (that's 5 in each in Homid).

                                  There is only a ~6.45% chance of doing 7+ damage in the first place. So the vast majority of the time, they aren't going to be doing enough damage to take themselves out in one hit.

                                  There's a ~65.72% chance of soaking enough of the damage that no matter how much damage it does, the werewolf isn't taking enough damage to go to incapacitated.

                                  A fully healthy Garou attacking someone using that Gift and killing themselves on it is such a vanishingly rare event, that something like, "The quills slice through the attacking arm, and then shred it as the force of Crinos muscles pull them free and sprays blood everywhere. After what seems like gallons of blood geysers over both, the attacker falls over from the blood loss, reverting to their breed form." Might sound rare and extreme, but that's exactly what the rolls would be, so it fits.
