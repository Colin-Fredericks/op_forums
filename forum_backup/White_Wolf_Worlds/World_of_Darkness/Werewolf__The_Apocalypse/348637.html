                      If using Revised Mummies, that is MtR versions, then remember that they have to be rather saintly to hold even a little magic to their name as Balance is both their power stat and extra strict version of Morality so if there is a conflict of interest with this kind of character it likely leaves the Garou on the immoral side of the divide as the bad guys more or less. Still the conflict could easily arrive from the fact that Garou tend to solve things by tooth and claw which the Mummy in question could find offensive to Ma'at and thus something that needed correcting, so this works if your Pack has had civilian casualties lately or just excessive use of force in general.

                      If using Mummy 2nd edition then you have a much wider range of possibilities open to you as the Mummies in that system are much more free to act as they wish and pursue their own goals in addition to the larger goals. Then it can be anything ranging from a aforementioned need for blood or perhaps a fetish in possession of the PC's or a personal grudge with one of the PC's direct ancestors etc. They are also much less limited in their travels and so it wouldn't be that far stretched to find one of them visiting NY. The mummy may even see this conflict as unfortunate but necessary for some longer reaching goal to be fulfilled, perhaps he only need to distract the pack away from some other thing happening elsewhere that will advance it's long term goals even if not apparent in the short term. We are speaking of a being that is likely almost 4000 years old after all.

                                          It's very easy to use mummies as antagonists. You have to remember, and this is brought up in the Revised Silent Strider book, that mummies are **an affront to Gaia**. They're living beings who died and cheated death because of a foul ritual that fooled the Wyrm of Balance into allowing them to exist. This is a direct violation of Gaia's law, and nothing of Gaia can truly allow this to pass. They may have similar goals, but that doesn't matter. Mummies are violating the natural order, no matter how much lipservice they give to protecting it (and yes, I am a huge fan of Mummy; it's My favourite game).

                                          This is easiest for your SIlent Strider, because the Silent Striders put the Undying in the top 4 of their Tribal enemies. Above them are vampires, mages, and banes, and they really only plan to destroy the immortals after those enemies are out of the way (meaning that, as a tribe, no time soon), especially since the Undying cause the least amount of harm. It doesn't mean that a particularly zealous Silent Strider couldn't say, "Fuck it. These abominations need to go!" They shouldn't like that they're basically pissing all over Egypt, while the Silent Striders, Egypt's TRUE protectors, can't stay for any real length of time!

                                          Fenrir aren't going to like ANYTHING that comes back to life which hasn't expressly been given permission by Gaia, so mummies get no leeway. Add that the Fenrir HATE Necromancers, and most of the Undying are also necromancers, and there's plenty of reasons there. Nuwisha aren't like the Garou, but Nuwisha are still Gaian. Your Nuwisha might want to make the Mummies recognise how they defy the natural order that they serve and get them to destroy themselves, and in the most humourous way possible.

                                          The Shadow Lord is Garou, so we can point up to prior parts of this post for his or her motivation. The difference is that the Shadow Lord might want to first milk the mummies for as much information as they can find, which might help them to find out about these cults of blasphemers that keep bringing the dead back to life, so that they can destroy them and end the creation of more mummies once and for all. Even the Child of Gaia should be somewhat bloodthirsty in this, because these aren't innocent people. They're mummies and cults that make dead people into immortal people. You literally have the perfect party to do some hardcore mummy hunting. That they won't be able to keep them permanently dead means that you'll have a lot of good, long-term enemies. If they destroy the Resurrection Cults, that will make them VERY hostile enemies, to boot, while also giving the Garou massive amounts of Renown for permanently putting an end to the violation of Gaia's law through the creation of more of these unholy creatures.

                                          This is very simple and has lots of room for continued and sporadic use (only the dumbest of mummies are going to keep *directly* messing with a pack of Garou and Fera that want them dead).
