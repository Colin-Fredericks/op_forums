      The White Howlers don't exist as a Tribe, even if members of the Tribe didn't fall, and have maintained their bloodline since then.

      You can't join a Tribe without a Tribal Totem. If the Tribal Totem refuses to allow the initiation rite to work, nobody can join the Tribe no matter what. As well, this means the Pure Breed of any surviving White Howler descendants would fail to reach spiritual potency, since that is also something the Totem powers in the Tribe's Garou.

      So the problem is that even if some survived, they have no way of continuing themselves as White Howlers. They would have to become Ronin, or join other Tribes (most likely the Fianna). Their culture and society is so thoroughly destroyed that they can't be said to exist in a meaningful sense, even if their are kin and Garou running around that have White Howler ancestry.

      For a real life comparison, there are no living Aztecs. It's not that all the Aztec people were killed off, but the Aztec culture was destroyed, and the Aztec people that survived have become part of other cultural groups (both contemporary ones, and new ones built by the mixing of Mesoamerican and Spanish cultures)

                  Originally posted by PhantomDragenoid View Post

                  There's a rules for being a throwback White Howler descendant in the 2nd Edition Players Guide. I imagine they exist in W20 too. From what I recall you need to have purebreed 5, and either escape from the BSDs before they make you walk the Black Spiral (or simply sacrifice the pup) or basically be a Lunatic. The Fianna, and to a lesser extant the British Fenrir and Silver Fangs, probably took in the untainted survivors and kinfolk, so I imagine you could have a throwback born among them too. The Fianna would in particular have the possibility of possessing some knowledge of their old gifts, similar to how the Uktena and Wendigo might have knowledge of Croatan gifts.

                  A big part of the stigma is the special snowflake syndrome and cliche of being 'the last White Howler'. If you just want to use the gifts, there's always finding a teacher from the above mentioned tribes. Because just being descended from them is not a guarantee you'd receive the gifts anyways.
