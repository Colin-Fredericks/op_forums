    Originally posted by 11twiggins View Post
    Hengeyokai

    Garou in the Beast Courts gain a few changes worth noting.

    - Hengeyokai Garou receive access to the General Hengeyokai Gift List and the an additional Auspice List, both cost 4x Current Level
    - They can exchange one of their three starting Gifts for a General Hengeyokai Gift
    - They can exchange one of their three starting Gifts for a Hengeyokai Auspice Gift

    Assuming there is at least one General and one Auspice Gift you want, the exchange is sensible. What you're losing costs 3xp, what you're gaining costs 4xp. Exceedingly character-optimization brained, but I like noting these things.

    Before we get into what these lists are, I do want to address a side-bar I find funny:



    I find this quite charming. "It isn't that they have 5 Gift lists because they're better, they have 5 Gift lists because they're better!"

    I'm being facetious, and I appreciate that the history is very different. Ultimately it's totally harmless that Asian Garou have a leg up; there are fewer Tribe choices (only two that I'm aware of - Hakken and Stargazer, and maybe Boli Zouhisze at a push, though they aren't members of the Beast Courts)
    According to prior material, Bone Gnawers, Children of Gaia, and Red Talons as well, and more varied Glass Walkers. (Hengeyokai p. 58, Caerns: Places of Power in the Glass Walkers' section). The collapse of Garou in the Beast Courts to just the Stargazers and Hakken was really, really dumb; almost as dumb as trying to tie the Hakken to Chiyou, a figure from Chinese folklore tradition, in Blood and Silk*.

    (Nothing is as dumb as Blood and Silk's language sidebar, full stop.)

    and the bonus Gifts are only slightly cheaper than they would be otherwise. The main benefit, in fact, isn't to do with experience, it's to do with accessing Gifts you'd otherwise struggle to justify accessing. Getting ahold of the most important Gift associated with a Breed, Auspice or Tribe requires clout, and it risks looking like you're trying to act outside the role you're expected to act within. So your Metis Hakken Ahroun being able to get Persuasion is a pretty big deal. It costing 4xp instead of 5xp barely matters, but the fact that they didn't have to plead their case like a loser is a big advantage.
    Depending on your breed and court auspice, along with other splat lists, the lists that you do get in the end might also be very redundant. An Ahroun who is a Fist, for example, gets access to three Gifts that they would not otherwise (and there's no telling if the Beast Courts auspice grants astrological Gift access like it does with Garou, where an Ahroun also technically gets access with in-splat costs to the Nerigal, Rorg, and Katanka-Sonnak lists).

    Hengeyokai General Gifts

    Remember that these cost 4x Level xp, so the main improvement is that you can access them easily. I will note that I'm not a fan of a 'General' list existing for the Beast Courts, since it makes some Gifts so ubiquitous that it lowers the stakes and makes the setting feel less cohesive (really hard to imagine certain threats flourishing in a place where every shapeshifter can get Sense Wyrm for 3-4xp!), but that's personal taste.

    Balance (1): See the Rank 1 Stargazer post.

    Create Element (1): See the Rank 1 Metis post.

    Mindspeak (1): See the Rank 1 Galliard post.

    Sense Wyrm (1): Ah great, now everyone has it. A sign of a society with its priorities in order, to be fair. See the Metis Rank 1 post!

    Blaze Talons (2): Costs 1 Gnosis, roll Strength + Primal-Urge. For every success your forelimbs are set afire for two rounds. Damaging Brawl attacks using your forelimbs gain one automatic level of Aggravated damage.

    How good is a bonus level of Aggravated? It's roughly akin to +2 dice, but more consistent. Fire also scares Vampires and various other threats.
    [/quote]

    Middle Dragons, Sharks, and Serpents learn this one rank later, incidentally.

    Courtly Speech (2): Renamed from Speech of the World. See the Rank 2 Homid list.

    Resist Temptation (2): See the Rank 2 Stargazer list.

    Sense Balance (2): See the Rank 3 Philodox list. Accessing this early is a big improvement, and here you're paying 8xp (2 x 4) instead of 9 or 15xp (3 x 3 or 3 x 5).

    I rated Sense Balance as Amber with a * marking it as a potential Red, depending on the table. Getting it at Rank 2, and for 8xp instead of 9xp, makes it a little bit better. But honestly if you're Peeking through the Umbra and you can't tell an area is heavily Weaver or Wyld aligned, or you can't tell a Drone/Gorgon from a regular person, you may need to invest into Perception and Abilities before you think about sensing Triat energies with Gifts. I maintain that most of the time you're better off relying on common sense and inferring from what you can see in the two worlds.
    High Autonomy in the possessed might make picking up on something in their day to day life more difficult. Detecting something off early can also allow you to head off the threat with a potentially much lower cost in time, effort, and casualties.

    Still, solid Amber here, especially given the variety of relationships with the Triat held within the Beast Courts.

    Clarity (3): See the Rank 3 Stargazer list.

    Part the Wall (3): Irrelevant to Garou; this Gift allows those who normally wouldn't be able to step sideways to do so. Unless you're a Metis whose 'deformity' is that you can't step sideways on your own, this Gift won't do anything for you.

    Waking Dream of Unity (3): Spend 1 Willpower, up to 30 willing individuals present at activation enter into silent telepathic communion. Excellent power for managing large battles and perhaps a social event or similar. Great for espionage.

    Exorcism (4)*: See the Rank 3 Theurge list.

    Mindblock (4): See the Rank 4 Silver Fang list.

    Walking the Bone Road (4): Modifies the Stepping Sideways mechanic so that you can step sideways into the Low Umbra instead of the Middle Umbra. The main utility of this, for my money, is being able to use stepping sideways to escape from enemies who can step sideways themselves. If you're fighting a Garou, as a Garou, and they vanish into thin air, well you're going to step sideways to chase them... only to find they're nowhere to be seen.

    Incredibly strong ability, well worth getting on anyone who is allowed to purchase it. The bonus utility of being able to easily access a whole different layer of reality is also neat. The best use of accessing the Low Umbra in a typical Werewolf game is avoiding Banes whilst also avoiding the real world, in my book, but that's still an excellent result.
    Some banes might surprise you, though.

    *A "barbarian" enemy of the Yellow Emperor, who fought with said legendary Emperor's forces but lost due to an inspired invention of the same. The description of the Wan Xian vs. Xiong Ren fight in B&S (how appropriate an abbreviation) was meant to parallel this fight, culminating in Garou Chiyou becoming the only changer to reside in the Thousand Hells, because heaven thought it an injustice that the corrupted Wan Xian were being socked in the eye or something. Quite honestly, a hot mess of an attempt to Goth up the legend.

It would be insane if the Garou Nation, which is the most "pressure people into running away" organization in the WoD, didn't have anyone flee eastward to the Beast Courts. Any Garou-Fera couple, any Garou with Asian heritage who visited home, it makes sense that various Tribes would have footholds in the Beast Courts, even if they're very small.

            And regarding the secondary Auspice my assumption is that a player will tend to, and I don't blame them for this, pick a Court Auspice which complements their Garou Auspice (only focusing on Garou here by the way - I'll do a different thread for Fera someday).

Basically if my Child of Gaia Metis Ahroun were suddenly in the Beast Courts, I might well end up going Fist, but I'd be very tempted by the other lists because then there would actually be a mechanical advantage to him being at the Beast Courts, which is just the kind of thing that feels fun. Ultimately playing an Ahroun-Lanter, or Ahroun-Mirror, or Ahroun-Courtier feels more superficially compelling, even if there is something to be said for the simplicity of "Warrior. Warrior. No new Gifts, but that doesn't matter.", a move I would respect in any player.

                Lantern

                These Gifts are learnt at 4x Level. Lanterns are akin to Philodox, so a Philodox Garou in the Beast Courts is most likely to be a lantern, however that is not guaranteed.

                Gifts a Philodox Garou would not normally get access to are underlined.

                Fangs of Judgement, Persuasion, Scent of the True Form, Truth of Gaia
                Command the Gathering, King of Beasts, Strength of Purpose
                Mental Speech, Wisdom of Ancient Ways,
                Roll Over, Take the True Form
                Geas, Strength of Will

                Now, let's analyze this list, and you'll see why I would advocate letting your poor Philodox pick anything other than Lantern.

                What do we see here? A list of Philodox Gifts. If you're playing a Philodox in the Beast Courts, your Court Auspice isn't a way to grant you new abilities, it's a way to grant your abilities to all of the Fera who get their own special stuff you'd want access to but can't get.

                If this list were written with a bunch of Fera Gifts folded in, basically if the list were "what are the Fera Gifts which would really suit the Philodox?" whilst also being a list of the necessary judge, investigator and lawman Gifts, like the ones listed above, that would be better. That way a random Fera can have the Philodox basekit, but the Philodox who chooses Lantern doesn't suffer any losses.

                As it stands, if you're making a Lantern Philodox in the Beast Courts the only thing you get from this list is Strength of Will, at Rank 5, for 20xp instead of 25xp. That's it! This is an excellent list for practically any Garou other than a Philodox. My Metis Ahroun would love Persuasion and Scent of the True Form, he'd love Truth of Gaia, and Strength of Purpose would redefine his access to Willpower. Basically I can't envision a world where I wouldn't dip into that new pool, especially if I'm surrounded by Werespiders and Wererats and Werefoxes who are getting the best things about being a Garou (an abbreviated version of my Garou Auspice list) whilst getting to keep all of their Fera Gifts which I still can't access.

                                        Courtier

                                        These Gifts are learnt at 4x Level. This Auspice has no parallel among Luna's Auspices, and this means I actually find it the most intriguing. Courtiers are dedicated to guarding Caerns (Dragon Nests) among other things. It's arguably the easiest to justify taking on your character, since it doesn't match your Auspice, but it doesn't match any Auspice.

                                        Breath of the Wyld, Lightning Reflexes, Master of Fire, Perfect Recall, Persuasion
                                        Flame Dance, Sight From Beyond, Staredown, Wuxing
                                        Exorcism, Open Moon Bridge, Scent of the Oathbreaker, Song of the Siren
                                        Attunement, Beast Life, Bridge Walker
                                        Head Games, Geas

                                        Note that while the General Hengeyokai Gift List does give Exorcism, the Courtier gets it at Level 3 instead of Level 4.

                                        Persuasion, Flame Dance and Scent of the Oathbreaker are the major takeaways here. This Auspice gives you abilities which help you with managing a base, but they also feel like a generalist who is meant to deal with anything from an attack on the Caern to accepting visitors for diplomatic discussions. Management of Moon Bridges is a worthwhile addition given the importance of the bridges for Caerns, but... honestly just learning the relevant Rites is probably fine. Still, good to have them. This would be a good choice for Court Auspice if you weren't sure which one to take; you're almost guaranteed to get something you didn't have before.

                                        Also, I have gone back and edited the Lightning Reflexes entry, changing it from Red to Amber. See The Get of Fenris Rank 1 post for details. That said, on this list specifically, Flame Dance makes Lightning Reflexes mostly redundant. With the Flame Dance Gift you can just always declare an attack, and if you're attacked you can automatically roll for a dodge with a Rage spend, no worries. For a Courtier, the only reason to get Lightning Reflexes would be if you don't plan to get Flame Dance, say for example if you are Rage 1 (Ragabash, for instance) and don't plan to improve your Rage.
