                                09-13-2023, 07:32 PM
                                Originally posted by Bluecho View Post
                                Note how, in addition to all these being disturbingly dark, none of them position the First Change in anything resembling a good or liberating light.
                                Becoming a Werewolf can't be a positive development for you, the PC. You must have angst. Preferably also with a bodycount.
                                Well, as we've heard from Justin Achilli one of the core-ideas of WOD5E is "I'm a monster, what now?", so I assume that was the direction the writer was given. But that really makes me wonder how they're even gonna approach all the other splats... This is just gonna get increasingly weirder the more Paradox enforces this idea that all WOD-splats need to be VtM-esque.​

                                Originally posted by Ana Mizuki View Post
                                Ohoho no, there is no kinfolk anymore. The PC has to kill other GAROU.
                                Rather than having consistent worldbuilding, the whole "Who gets to be a Garou?"-thing is another shrug. And so it's pretty much all sorts of ideas happening at once. It's random people turning into Garou at random times, it's hereditary, it's caused by personal experiences or the socio-economic position you and your family have found yourselves in... Technically I guess anything can trigger someone becoming a Kin and at whatever point in a person's life this turns into a First Change. Like number 5 is just "random". Something happened to the character and suddenly said character is a werewolf.

                                But when it's this random, all these scenarios where it feels like other werewolves are trying to "trigger" you, it just comes across as psychopathic behavior. And this just goes back to how negative W5's portrayal of Garou in general is. You really get the sense from what Justin Achilli has said and the way this is handled that actually if anything all the Garou (and not just the Fenrir) are viewed as bad guys by the writers of this version of W5 we've gotten now. And the way you're supposed to play this version of W5 is have player-characters who are werewolves but couldn't care less about the Garou-Nation, the Litany and being sacred warriors for Gaia. That's all bad. But the game also can't offer an alternative because that would require worldbuilding (which according to Justin Achilli is inherently bad).

                                And so the same way H5 is a game of local hunter-cells incapable of envisioning anything beyond that, W5 is a game of young werewolves who have just become "Garou" (more or less) who struggle with their position in the world. But the horizon of imagination in the game's writing ends right there. W5 can't imagine the player-characters saving the world or becoming leaders of a Sept. The mechanics are even pushing against that (like with Rites, for example). Same as with H5, there's no vision to go beyond these beginning-stages of having new players who know next to nothing play the game. Try to do anything else with this and you start running into issues.
