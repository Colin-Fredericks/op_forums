    Red Talons in Earthblood

    08-09-2021, 04:52 AM
    Hello guys, despite the fact I'm one of the few persons who actually really enjoy Earthblood's plot, setting and atmosphere, (I found it really grim and desperate, as I like my chronicles),
    I was wondering if anyone else has noticed something about Onawa and the Red Talons


    SPOILER ALERT

    When Cahal frees Onawa, she says that she's the leader of the Red Talons. If you talk with her, Cahal says ''I've heard about the red talons, most of them are born from wolf, not from humans'', and Onawa says that she was born Uktena and then adopted.
    From the white wolf Wikia
    'Cahal learns about their research and frees a captured Red Talon that Endron has been experimenting on, Onawa''
    Can this be a major plot twist for the Red Talons? Homid Red Talons who refuse everything that is human, much like Predator Kings from forsaken? What's your opinion?
    I think that realistically it could have sense, since the tribe should be almost wiped out, otherwise. On the other hand, it's a major change, which it seems very invasive.

                                                          Anyway, let me stop derailing the thread - by railing on a scene of a game i never played, to boot - and try to explore the kernel of an idea Helur asked about:

                                                          "Could Red Talons secretly adopt Homids and brainwash them into refuse everything that is human in their origins?"

                                                          The tribe as whole, definitely not, because most might see this as a sort of betrayal even without Griffin possibly getting pissed with the idea.
                                                          But individual septs with other totems, specially ones with an element of trickery, might be amenable to the idea of purifying any Homid foundlings or lost cubs they come across in their travels or strikes against human pollution-producing businesses.

                                                          Giving them a (reverse?) Mowgli treatment of sorts of taking the man out the children of kinfolk families, Black Spiral, lost cubs or other (Director Alreed from Pentex was of Get of Fenris stock and Samuel Haight descends from either Children of Gaia or Fianna, if memory tricks me not), as a step towards purificating humanity and herding it toward a more nature harmonic path instead of straight genocide.

                                                          Damn, considering how messed up with all kinds of mental issues even non-fomor working on Pentex can be, kids being taken from such families might welcome "going wild child" alongside the Talons, even those who never become Garou. It might even be a more compassionate destiny - at least from a Talon perspective, if no one else's, in dealing with Humanity and the Weaver & Wyrm's influence in their society as a whole.

                                                          Yes, i think it could work as some sort of weird secret society kind of heretical camp within the tribe, like the Society of Nidhogg for Shadow Lords - except not as moustache-twirling apocalyptic doomsday cult villains like the Society, that is. Damn, the "Mowgli Herders" might even be said to be sort of wholesome for Talons, i guess.
