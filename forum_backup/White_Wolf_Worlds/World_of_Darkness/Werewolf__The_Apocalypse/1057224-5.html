                Maybe somewhere in the Tribe books, though I am not sure. I imagine though that it would be easy enough to make up a Rite. Here is an attempt.

                Changing the Skin (Level 3 Mystic Rite)

                Changing the Skin allows the target of the Rite to change their biological sex (female, intersex, or male) while retaining any Pure Breed Background. Among the Black Furies, the Rite is offered as a reward to male Garou and male Kinfolk who impress them or inflicted upon female Garou and female Kinfolk who disappoint them. Among the other Tribes, it is used to make Garou and Kinfolk more comfortable in their own skins. The Rite may only be performed during Winter Solstice if used to transform the target into a female and during the Summer Solstice if used to transform the target into a male (either Equinox is suitable to make someone intersex).

                                                Well, I admit that there's a certain amount of Kuei-jin in what inspired me. The east Asian setting is generally shown as more cross-creature tolerant as long as people are behaving. Not completely of course, but enough to allow for easier relations.
