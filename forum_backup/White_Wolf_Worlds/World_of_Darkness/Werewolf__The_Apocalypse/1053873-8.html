    With Erebus, if you end up there you usually are not healthy in mind or soul. But more important than that, as much as I'm glad my character lived through it, he deserved every agg level he was given. I heard a phrase used once about portraying redemption to characters who have done wrong. What of the voice of their victims? Those they hurt and damaged and killed? Are they going to be silenced?


    To me, Erebus is that voice. The retribution for all the harm they did and lives they took needlesly.

    These are not good or nice people, they have hurt others willingly. Whether Weaver tainted, Wyld or Wyrm, to get far enough to need a silver bath, you still hurt and killed others. Especially with BSDs, even if they regret their deeds, their victims don't need to forgive them. Most possibly don't, ever. And that is their right, even if through erebus' rivers.

    My gallery.

Erebus should be only considered when Death is the other punishment option. Death would be easier and more sure method of retribution, but some Philodox thought this corrupted soul was worth saving. So into the silver lake they go. If they make it out, then that vindicates the Philodox's judgement. If they don't, well.... Erebus is a fitting punishment for their crimes.

        Note, these are my opinions and how I would use Erebus in a game. YMMV

                        To revive this topic (and get it back to the original question) some of my advices.

                        1. Get your own copy of "Listen up you primitive screwheads". It's for Cyberpunk 2020but you will find there some advices how to deal with different types of problem players.
                        2. Let your players form a team without your interference. They usually know their style of play so they won't spoil your efforts (or destroy it completely as a one team). I got a player that nobody wants to play with. And I don't want to be his ST. Luckily he forgot that I love to run games so much that he could form a one man team 😋.
                        3. Exploit their characters strenght against them. "Big power means big responsibility". If they are capable of dealing with any type of enemy they will get the most difficult tasks. And a properly generated First Team could wipe an entire sept out. I found the team like that somewhere in the Internet. Or use powerful NPCs (like The General from 7th Generation). Their sept is in the woods? How about few platoons of USMCs making their training grounds there (like in "Dog soldiers"). Or a B1 bomber "accidentaly" dropping a bomb right into the heart of the caern. And if you like Vampire 5th edition you could use Firstlight unit. They know and actively hunt vampires. Why not werewolves?
                        4. Use player's weakness against them. I run a CP2020 story once. In CP characters can have their stats from 2 to 10 (plus about 50% for cybernetic upgrades but the more upgrades you have the more psychotic you become) and skills from 1 to 10. The player's characters have their stats 20+ with minimum Humanity damage. Their previous GM allow it because they have bionic not cybernetic upgrades. One of them was some kind of mafia Don and two others were soldiers, his bodyguards. I run a story about a Don's lieutenant who wants to blow him from his saddle. The bodyguards stated that this is a game during which you have to think and went to sleep commanding us to wake them up in case of combat and the Don asked for my (GM) advice because his character has Inteligence 10 and he, as a player, 5 at most (IMHO he overestimated himself). In the end the Don lost everything and those players haven't bother me since then. Another example: I run Rage Across Australia campaign for a multitribal pack that acted like a bunch of psychos. Somewhere during the game they stated that they want a more combat-oriented, heroic game. So I send them to Russia to face the Zmei and Baba Yaga. When their characters wandered across Siberia they saw Gregornous Deathwing. They decided to hide but Ahroun Get of Fenris (macho Viking type character, played by a girl and equipped with a Great Klaive that has been in his family since early Middle Ages, with a Wind Spirit so it could return to character's hand) activate his Klaive. The dragon started to turning circles around them sensing magic of the Fetish. Their Ragabash tried to persuade Get to turn off his weapon but without success. And, when Gregornous locate them Fenrir jumped and throw his sword hitting the monster. His oponent roared bringing calamity (of course there is a saving throw but based on Empathy; what kind of Fenris will find this talent useful). I decided that the Wind Spirit was affected and weapon didn't return to his owner's hand. And wounded dragon started to fly away with the blade in his guts. What did the player's said? That I am unfair and chose the worst option I could use as a calamity affect. And they haven't bother me either since then.
                        5. Listen carefully what your players say. They will give you weapons to stop them. Example: another CP2020 game. In the middle of a firerfight in a bar one of players declared that his character gets up and walks to the bathroom. I asked him three times if he's sure about it. And then I killed his character. I don't know if it was a plain stupidity or a masterpiece of acting as shellshocked but he didn't show again so I assume the former.

                        And remember, the worst enemy is the one that you don't see (like P....x).

                        Hope I was helpful

                            It is unfortunate that you have to go trough this. You have a particualr playstyle and they also have one as well and those two playstyles do tend to not mesh well. On top of that it seems that certain players do not care that they are running rampant over other players good time as they can not seem to see that this is a cooprative game and everyone should be able to play the way they want to play.

                            My only advice is to try and make them understand that while you respect that they want to kill an loot everything adn would be willing ot write in particular things to allow them to do so they can not turn every encounter into this style of play as it does mean that they are forcing others to play their style and their style only.

                            Their is one player at every table that does not get to kick in the door kill everything and loot the corpses, and that is the ST. They must provide such entertainment to others so others should be respectfull that the ST is technically a player too and one that puts in a lot of time and effort into providign the players a good time free of charage. In turn the players should try to provide the ST with a good time by trying ot get to know the plot and NPCs the ST took time to create.

                            If they do not understand then ask them to leave the game. If they are not willing ot help their friend have a good time in the game with them then they are not your friends, as it seems they don't care about you or your feelings and only care about what you can provide for them.

                            If the group falls apart join a discord put out a call for players and play on line with players that will respect that we are all in this together.

                                            Well OP, it sounds like you may have some munchkin players, completely focused on power, killing and looting. A few possible solutions:

                                            1) They didn't heal the dying SL

                                            Make a note that later they will come across a cool sparring session. The judge will heal the combatants at the end and mention how many lives the Mother's Touch helped him save over the years.

                                            2) Attacked the Corax

                                            Like one of the previous suggestions say, give an ominous warning ("interesting, fascinating, are you sure ?, you can certainly try"). Then say "The Corax has no idea what you plan to do. Normally I don't say anything because I fully want you to think through your moves. But you don't have a way of guessing I wouldn't handwave that detail, so its only fair I mention it. But from here on out, its all up to you". Use these ominous phrases to reinforce the understated "bad idea" when needed.

                                            3) The tainted bones

                                            Mention that its too big to carry even in crinos. But they can with some effort take a few teeth, ribs and a few claws. If they are not careful and touch them, then you can seed nightmares, dangerous hallucinations and other signs of Wyrm corruption. This plot hook will escalate in a way that serves to imprint the importance of the Rite of Cleansing and to be paranoid around anything touched by the Wyrm.

                                            4) The optimizer

                                            Tell him that, barring any vetos, he can purchase what he wants if he has the XP at character creation. But only if there is a story reason for them. No backstory, no Merit.

                                            Sure, he can have the dogs. But where do they come from ? How much did they cost ? Are they licensed, are they trained ? Won't neighbors be suspicious if they are constantly hurt from combat ? Won't the vet call an animals rights group to investigate ?

                                            Who sold him the sniper ? Is it legal ? Is he even allowed to carry one around? If not, how is he sneaking it into places? Either way, he needs to find a permit or locate a black market weapons dealer.

                                            If he is a tech wiz and in the military, when did he get time to setup a successful civilian company ? Did he embezzle military resources and had black market connections ? Was he dishonorably discharged at some point ?

                                            All of this will create interactions for later.

                                            Hope these help. Don't give up, its possible they are used to more combat heavy games and haven't grasped that is not the feel you are going for.
