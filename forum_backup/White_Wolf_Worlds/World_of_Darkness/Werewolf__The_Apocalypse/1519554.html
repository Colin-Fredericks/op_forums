    W5.5 Idea

    08-02-2023, 10:48 PM
    This is just the outline of an idea I had for an alternative W5, obviously it's pretty basic, but I wanted to hear another opinion and just to clarify, I don't intend to create an alternative W5, and I couldn't even if I wanted to, and by the way I'm also going to apologize for the strange names, I suck at this, if anyone has a better suggestion leave it there. I also don't intend to just leave it there, as I have more ideas I'll write more, but I won't promise anything.


    The apocalypse has already begun and the signs are clear, the climate across the globe is changing radically, in fact it has always been even if slowly, but now the change is palpable, out of season snow, frequent tornadoes, dying crops and areas in constant desertification and that is perhaps not the worst, the Wyrm seems stronger than ever and more savage too, creatures never seen outside the Umbra now appear on the physical plane in broad daylight for all people to see, and new terrors have also born and begin to spread in multiple forms, the Umbra has also been affected with the arrival of the last days, paths that were once safe are now infested with curses and old shortcuts have simply disappeared. A jorney in the Umbra was already an adventure, now it becomes even more painful and safe trips are rare. The Garou nation knew that this day would come, they did everything in their power and a little more to delay this day, but it arrived and now they have to face their biggest challenge which is to survive, but the coming of the apocalypse brought with it several other problems.

    The hunters became the hunted: The existence of the Garou is still a secret for most of the public, but not for everyone, some government agencies founded out about their existence and also know about Garou nation, even if your knowledge is limited, this is all thanks to reports and information provided by some dissatisfied Kinfolks with the abusive treatment that some of then received by the tribes or the ones that believe they are just crazy monsters and extremists who attack big companies without any seal on who will get hurt along the way, this alone would not be enough, but besides the reports being numerous some of them had recordings of their Garou parents turning into Crinos and attacking innocents in a rage attack, after which the Garou nation was recognized as a bioterrorist organization and a special force was assembled in order to dismantle operations involving urban Caerns, thanks to information provided by kinfolk and also the knowledge of the Garou's weaknesses, at least some of them, they managed to carry out dozens of heavy attacks on urban caerns across the North American continent and also some caerns in suburbs and in forest reservations. They suffered heavy casualties, but managed to carry out a massive coup against the Garou nation, managing to overthrow several Caerns in addition to forcing many Garou as well as trusted kinfolks into hiding, others were killed and a few were captured alive, for study and interrogation. With the arguable success, a new division was set up, the BlackWatch, a special force specifically created to hunt Garou, even counting on some Kinfolk who joined voluntarily to their ranks.

    New Flaws: Spotted (2 points) You were spotted on some security camera or some witness saw you transform and now BlackWatch is after you and they have a close description of what you look like, this will make those who don't know you suspicious and it will be harder to blend in with the crowd.Social rolls with others have +1 difficulty, you now stand out from a crowd unless you use disguise.

    Wanted (3 points) Similar to the Spotted Flaw, but now everyone knows your face in Homid form and even knows your name, you will no longer be able to go out in urban areas without BlackWatch being notified unless you use some disguise, if you have one bank account it will be blocked or monitored, possibly the same will apply to your close relatives and contacts.

    The disease of red madness: Humans are not the only ones to suffer from the consequences of the Apocalypse, other animals, especially wolves, are also feeling these effects, in their case they feel it through a new disease that spreads, The Red Madness, a disease that causes its sufferers to develop a homicidal urge, a strong craving for meat (even in herbivores species) and cannibalistic tendencies, physical symptoms include intense skin irritation, shedding of hair or scales, rashes breaking out in various places, excessive bleeding, eyes turning milky or reddening, hydrophobia and in some cases uncontrolled growth of muscle mass. Once infected, the carrier may or may not manifest the symptoms of the disease immediately, the incubation period of the disease can vary from a few hours to almost a month before any visible symptoms appear, which is worrying because in the meantime the disease already can spread to other animals without them noticing, even Garou are not immune to this disease, although Garou can only be infected through direct contact, biting, or eating contaminated meat, other animals can be infected by fluids from carriers or just if you breathe near them. Curiously, humans are not affected by the disease and also can’t be vectors.

    System: If infected, the player must make a Life roll – 1 dice for each aggravated damage taken, difficulty 7, if the Garou is aware of the supernatural nature of the disease he can spend a Gnosis point to make a standard roll, with a success he can avoid contamination in both cases, as an alternative rule the Storyteller may require a greater number of successes if the disease vector is in a more advanced state. Infected animals make the same roll, difficulty 8 for wolves. Once infected, the Garou gains an infection point, and at the beginning of each scene he must redo the test, this time using Vigor, if he isn’t successful, increase by one more infection point, if the infection points exceed those of Vigor he will show the symptoms of the disease (hair falling out, excessive itching, coughing blood, aggressive tendencies) and will become a vector of the disease, if he exceeds the Gnosis points, he will become contaminated and will attack anything, in which case the control goes to storyteller and can only be recovered through a purification rite. Humans don’t contract the disease.

    The Nation now: Say what you want about the Garou nation, but they haven't been a unified group for a long time, even fighting the same enemy each tribe fights in a different way, sometimes they help each other, but they also sabotage each other in Equally proportionate, with the arrival of the apocalypse each had to find a way to survive while fighting the Wyrm. Below are some ideas on what each tribe may be facing in these difficult times, take these ideas as suggestions only and feel free to use or ignore them depending on the tone and the subjects your chronicle addresses.

    Bone Gnawers: The tribe that possibly lost the most before the apocalypse, they more than others always believed that the apocalypse would come and if a tribe would survive, it would be them, however they didn’t expect what the apocalypse reserved to then. They don't know when it started, but the rat totem was corrupted by the Wyrm, even if not completely, a new aspect of the rat emerged, the Plague Rat that spread hunger among many of the BG, to the point that it became maddening, and those who couldn't resist this initial onslaught went to extreme lengths to satisfy their hunger this even includes cannibalism, when they realized what was happening the Theurges of the tribe sprang into action separating the rat totem from its most recent aspect, but by the time they got it, 1/4 of the tribe had already given in to the effect of hunger and now that they had committed this crime, tasted human flesh, they had no choice but to beg for the protection of the Plague Rat, after all it is as rat said, survive.

    Glass Walkers: Possibly the most affected tribe as their havens are in urban areas, whether in large cities or in suburbs, after BlackWatch attacks on multiple Caerns a considerable portion of them were either killed or captured, those who survived the attacks found themselves Forced to flee and live in hiding and fear, the most cautious, to not to say paranoid, set up hideouts in fortified bunkers, those who could fled to another country far from BlackWatch's jurisdiction. Others were more daring and sought safe refuge within the Umbra, some disappeared and were never seen again, most returned after some time, but different, it is unknown what they stumbled upon in the Umbra, but it changed them radically, mentally and spiritually, they claim to still fight for Gaia yet they behave with an almost mechanical coolness, perhaps the other tribes were right and the time the Glass Walkers have spent with the Weaver is taking its toll.

    Red Talons: The Red Talon tribe is composed only of lupines to keep their kinfolk free from the influence of humans, fearful of the likely demise of their wolf kin. With the onset of the disease of red madness their brothers and sisters were infected and they were forced to do whatever was necessary to protect Gaia, yet that didn't make the task of dispatching their loved ones any easier, and it was even worse when it happened to one of his Garou brothers, not only because they were harder to kill, but also because they were his companions in many battles and now they were forced to kill them like some aberration of the Wyrm. In search of a solution, some packs sought help from other tribes in order to save all the parents they could, they knew they ran the risk of losing their own Caerns by showing weakness, but despair spoke louder. The Black Furies in particular achieved some success in curing the maladies of the disease in their lupine friends, after all that was not a physical illness, but a spiritual one, nothing coming from modern medicine could combat this evil. At first the situation with the Red Talons seemed to be starting to improve, but that didn't last long, as expected, they soon discovered that the disease didn’t affect humans, and for the griffin tribe this was a sign, some belived that humans had done that to get rid of the wolves and invade the forests that were rightfully theirs, others said that this was Gaia's punishment for her wolf children for having lost control of humans with the end of the impergium and letting the apocalypse happen, and some even speculated that this would be one more of the Wyrm's machinations in order to make them attack humans, whatever the truth was, there were not a few packs of Red Talons that decided to attack humans settlements in search of revenge, they knew that this couldn’t end the disease, but the fury made them act and hunt until the last human they found, the other tribes and even other Red Talons who do not agree with these attacks, did and still do everything in order to stop these furious packs.

    Shadow Lords against the Silver Fangs: Yuri Konietzko doesn't care to be the leader of the Garou nation, he only wants the best for her, yet Albrecht doesn't seem to be the king they need right now, and if he have no other choice, Konietzko will take the throne, by whatever means necessary. One day Albrecht called Konietzko to a private meeting, he didn't say why, Konietzko suspected his intentions, but you can't refuse an invitation coming from the king of the silver crown himself. He didn't expect to be received with trumpets and a parade, but he also didn't expect what happened, instead of a conversation, Albretch received Konietzko with a trap, the reason was about his betrayal, Albretch claimed that Konietzko sent one of the Shadow Lords assassins to attempt against his life, but the attempt failed, and now Albretch wanted his head, with great cost Konistzko manages to escape from the claws of the mad king, but what would be his move now, with that accusation on his back Konietzko could not act openly anymore, he still had support from his tribe, however he had lost credibility with the others, after all he supposedly attacked the king of the Garou nation, even if not everyone believed in such an accusation, but Konietzko knew people and he knew when they lied and he could see it in Albretch's eyes when he accused him that it wasn't a mere bluff, he actually believed his own words which left only two options, the first was that the king finally lost his sanity or that someone was up to no good, against him, everything indicated that it would be the first option, but his instinct said it should to be the second, the question is, where should he start looking? Meanwhile, the apocalypse is slamming the doors and the motives for the Garou nation to unite seem only to dwindle.
