          If I want a big grand moment of drama I build it up, over time. Over multiple sessions.

          In the instance of a villain first team leader being built up I would have the characters first hear about another sept nearby have three garou apparently missing.

          later hear that pentex has a new military group another pack at another sept.

          later I'd let the characters hear about a pentex team named 'insert terrifying name hear' is giving the other Sept now trouble and the pack have disappeared that were under a quest to wipe out the pentex team.

          Next that the name of the leader of the pentex team is 'insert even scarier name here' and his troops hold an utter and completely unwavering loyalty too (sometimes even gifts don't work against them- gossipy storytelling?.) that is winning against the sept.

          Now, theres no news or communication from the other sept any longer. A pack has been sent to investigate.

          then, they hear three garou from the characters sept...have mysteriously gone missing.

          As for a big reveal, I'd go with a survivor from one of the characters or sept leaders past. A normal human who survived a first change or random frenzy encounter of a werewolf. After watching his family die, his friends die, be stuck in hospital for a year and then got institualised After no one believed his stories of a werewolf.

          But someone heard, something came fot him with promises of the power for revenge. Now he is legend among the ranks of first teams and they believe in him fully as a living legend willing to die beside or for.

          Then show how the characters own actions and the dangers garou pose to even the things they are supposed to protect create the evils of the future they'll always need to face.

                          I think this concept is interesting, but I suggest you give the first team officer a promotion. I think this idea might be better suited for a lower-level Pentex executive rather than a first team merc. First teams, even the good ones, are expendable, and they don't really choose their targets. If Commando Killguts had his family butchered by Garou, he may harbor an insane vendetta... but he is not the one calling the shots and he probably doesn't have the time or resources to pursue a particular individual pack. Additionally, a first team mutant infested with banes is all well and good, but as big-bads go, most packs are going to be able to deal with him or a vampire without much difficulty. A higher ranking Pentex boss is capable of throwing first team after first team at the pack, or targeting their families and loved ones, might be a better option. Unlike your typically combat-oriented Wyrm minion, an intelligent and well-equipped Pentex exec is much harder to claw and bite to death.

                          Alternatively, a Fomorach might be a startling reveal. Have a low-level Pentex intern subject himself to the worst horrors of Project Illiad after his wife and kids are slain by werewolves. Now you have a frenzied monstrosity with bizarre and potent Wyrm magic hunting for the Garou, maybe even escaping his captors in order to wreak vengeance...

                              I like this. Building on it... why make the executive a Pentex member? Why make the threats decidedly supernatural? Any executive, investor, dot com billionaire, old money member could become a great threat to the Garou. He knows they were involved - he might know who they are. Imagine him breaking the bank on investigators, mercenaries, assassins. Digging up dirt and making it his quest to destroy everything the Garou holds dear so that they would learn how it is to lose everything that you have cared for. You could take a page from Oldboy, but I think the key here is the systemic teardown of the Garou's life without them being aware what's going on. Now take this person - tormented, furious and crazy with grief, yet systematic and well thought out - and their reason for doing it is just.

                              If you really want to add Pentex/Wyrm to it, I would suggest that you start with someone who went into it on his own and was highly competent and possibly even efficient, and then having him approached by the Wyrm as an ally. The interesting bit is that someone like this who went into the alliance with the Wyrm would balk at the Wyrm and their methods, maybe even finding a reason to help the Garou against Pentex... or getting fully corrupted and consumed by their vengeance.
