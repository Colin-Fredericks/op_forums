First of all, unless we are talking about BSDs, no canon metis has ever been a wanted child. The common narrative of them, troubling or not, is that their parents fooled around and broke up after the female got pregnant. Then at best they give the metis away or worst they have to raise the child themselves. Neither option is ever treated as anything but difficult. Plus there is the thing, garou KNOW that a metis will ALWAYS be significantly disabled and sterile. Most likely will kill the mother and then live a life of constant judgement. This is a given, not a maybe. No parent wants their child to suffer like that, so why risk it?

            Besides, garou are a dying race. Despite what Changing Ways says, it is much better to have non-metis garou to fill the numbers. This is something told to cubs from day one of their training. That is why kinfolk are so put upon, because they are the breeding stock/meal-tickets. Those who try to keep garou mates are usually either not into the whole Nation thing or are just venting out their frustrations with each other.


            2. There is a difference between thinking someone is guilty and abusing the rite's effects. The former can happen, the latter is insulting for the spirits.

                            About Summer Country:
                            Metis are cured TEMPORARY, and if they leave the Realm, they revert. If you stay too long, you may disconnect. Entering the Summer Country requires the Garou to be peace with Gaia (himself, environment, every living beeing). Two Garou who don't accept their rule / place are not acceptable to enter the SC.

                            I see that you'd still allow this NPC. Of course, you created it. But I still advise to see in a broader spectrum and notice, how much she is not fitting.

                            Note: There are easily accessible medications (check Maggadon products) which can supress the first change. I'd accept if those stuff would be used, but using rites a way what shall not work is bad.
                            Add: For the rites, except minor & mystical 3 Garous are needed. Who were the other two?

                            About the Ragabash: The birth of a metis could hurt / maim / kill the mother. The metis is not able to give birth. This goes against to every "mass-creation" goal. Breeding with wolves is the matemathically acceptable solution.

                                            Originally posted by Ana Mizuki View Post

                                            Or not. We had that argument a couple of times in LARP. Cubs asking the Philodox as well as actual trails of Litany violators.

                                            It ain't that much fun to go though this, for it is (if no metis is born) a "no harm no foul" situation. At first glance. But, if two years later the sept goes under, because the neighbour sept argues "We won't send packs to help out a bunch on Litany violators and their enablers" the harm is there. And that is, what it basically comes down to. If the PCs are liberal in this matter the ST (or in case of the LARP ST team) has to make to call how the NPC world will react to this behaviour.

                                            But, besides the implication of the deed itself and what allowing it means and how harsh punishments should be, there is another, more frustrating part to it. The language. Some will argue that "breed" means procreate, so non-child-bearing sex is okay. Other will argue, that "breed" is the closest English word to what intercourse means in the way wolves would express themselves, but that it is the intention to ban all intercourse, for wolves would not communicate with such nuance but get the meaning anyway. Then, one would counter-argue that wolves only mate annually and unlike humans, some monkey specials probably, and dolphins (scientifically proven) actually have fun while having sex and therefore this application is so alien to wolves that this practice can't be included in what they mean when they talk about "breeding".
                                            And, there is no definitive answer to that argument, since we as players have no real concept of how wolves think. Or how communication works among wolves. And, we don't have the stories from all over the planet that all these millenia ago were brought to Pangea and argued about and from which the tenets of the Litany were condensed. If we knew the source material for the tenet, we could try to try to understand if the tenet came into existence because love between garou always lead to something bad, or if there were other reasons, like the prediction of the metis breed.
                                            All we know, is that the Legend from "Guardians of the Caerns" suggests, that the tenet is older as the first metis and therefore experience with Metis Breed Garou was not the reason the tenet was created - unless time travel.
                                            The assupmtions that garou metis would be bad based on Metis of other Changing Breeds (that were older) might have contributed to the tenet, though. As could have the tenet of other fera Litany, if the garou back then brought these with them to the meeting in Pangea where the Litany was formed.
