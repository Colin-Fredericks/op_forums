                                Originally posted by Solana View Post
                                So I'm rereading the corebook in preparation for my first time running Werewolf the Apocalypse, first time playing it in 13 years, and I got to the section on Rage and how a wyrm tainted rage can result. Specifically the Metis curse upsets most of my players, to the point where even a fade to black isn't satisfactory. This in mind, I've come up with a replacement Thrall of the Wyrm result whenever it comes up for Metis. I wanted to present this here in case it's too 'out there'.

                                Metis: The Defiler Wyrm reserves special attention for those Garou who cannot breed themselves. In the Thrall of the Wyrm, a bizarre form of empathy occurs. The Defiler Wyrm understands that yearning, the need to create, but the Defiler Wyrm cannot create like the Wyld can, it can only destroy or pervert. So it is with the metis breed. If a werewolf kills or incapacitates an opponent, her player must roll Wits (difficulty 7). If the roll botches, the werewolf's connection to the Defiler Wyrm imparts a profane secret - the ability to twist the hearts and souls of others into a likeness of their own tortured selves. If the victim survives, they become a twisted mockery of a the garou, a werewolf not unlike the false myths held true by the herd. If the victim dies, their spirit becomes corrupted and terrible. Either way, the metis can't help but sense the kinship that exists between themselves and their creation, forced to realize with guilt the truth that they have created a being of sin, much as their parents did to them. The feeling of kinship extends both ways - at first, most living victims are horrified by what is happening to them, after the first few transformations, the corruption sets in, and they can't imagine themselves without it. One way or another the metis is forced to deal with the consequences of their own creation, and the 'child' is likely to play games with them as they sink deeper into wyrm-tainted corruption.
                                That's a lovely defiling, and far more appropriate than bizarre sex acts.

                                We don't allow mages to cast spells, since this is the most unbalancing rule of all.

                                            For an alternative Thrall to make sense, it would have to be as horrific as the other forms of Thrall. The Thrall of the Wyrm focuses on the three major taboos of Western society (Homid=Cannibalism, Metis=Rape, and Lupus=Murder). The question for the creation of an alternate Thrall rule is what would be an appropriate replacement taboo (the incest taboo is covered with the creation of the Metis and would not be appropriate for a frenzy situation anyway).                                             Frankly, I don't think they contribute much at all, though murder of a friend in the fit of corruptive rage is the most thematically appropriate of the three. But here's what makes the sexual assault one fundamentally distinct from those two. It is a safe bet your players have not been murdered or cannibalized. We may assume this because they are alive, and we may also assume that if they have a negative history with violence, they feel personally able to handle the violence inherent in the setting if they made an informed decision to play Werewolf. The violence is up front and unmissable. It is not such a safe bet your players have not been the subject of a sexual assault, or have in some way some baggage, trauma, or issues surrounding it. Those that do may or may not be able to confront the issue in a tabletop game (indeed, I think there may be potentially great therapeutic uses of triggering content in the hopefully safe and comforting environment of a tabletop game), and as the rule is a single rule tucked away in a long rulebook, they are less likely to have made an informed decision that 'this may be an issue'.

                                            Further, I don't think it should be an issue at all. While I have no objection to including sexual assault in stories and, yes, even in tabletop games, it is a subject matter that needs to be handled carefully and deliberately. 'Roll the dice. You fail. Now you have to rape someone.' is not careful and deliberate. What it is is crass, vulgar, and offensive, and for no actual gain.

                                            For alternative Thrall states, let's look for a moment at the nature of the Defiler Wyrm. It is the force behind suicide, self-mutiliation, and self-destruction of all types. It is actually more appropriate for the result of a Thrall state in a Metis to take such a form than it is for it to take on the form of a mandatory sexual assault. It's plenty 'dark' if there's a realistic chance that in any fight, the Metis berserker might claw his own eyes from his head or tear out his own tongue with all the fury in the world. Suicide and self-harm rest as taboos either on, or just below, the same tier as those of rape, murder, and cannibalism. In some cultures, they can actually sit higher for religious reasons.

                                            So, again. What does mandatory, random chance sexual assault offer that competing options can't? And just as importantly, is there more utility created by it than by a competing option that does not hinge on mandatory, random chance sexual assault scenes?
