    Do Rites work for all Fera?

    04-25-2023, 06:54 PM
    Way back when I first got introduced to WTA, I was under the impression that most Rites that the Garou had were a sort of common use variety, while the Rites that other Fera had were strictly their own, and only presented because we were seeing the what magic was uniquely their own.

    Rite of the Spirit Egg is obviously only a Corax Rite, but I also understood that one Gurhal Rite that frees Fomori was also a strictly werebear Rite that could not be cast by anyone else. I am asking about this because the Grondr Rite where you can eat Banes is explicitly something a Garou CAN use, but probably shouldn’t unless he has some seriously powerful merits that allows him to resist Wyrm corruption and possession. What about the Apis lvl 4 Rite that allows you to incubate new shapeshifters in the ground? Can a Corax or Bastet learn this Rite?

      I think the lost rites like that need special dispensation to be learned or performed. They will never be learned en masse and the spirits that make them work will be rare and hard to appease. So while anyone might be able to learn them by doing the right things with the right spirits and finding the lost knowledge, having them is a whole story of its own.

      Past Lives is a whole chronicle about the history of the Fera that finishes with certain gifts and rites coming back with the PC's. It's a campaign's worth of story to get there, though.

                      Rites work because the spirits in general respond to them when correctly performed by spiritual beings, i.e. Garou, Fera, and some Possessed. Exactly which spirits depends; mostly, for Garou and Fera rites, it's naturae, but Ananasi have more truck with Triatic manifestations, and the rites that the Dying Cubs camp of the Red Talons discovered came from some other, nebulous source, about which the only certain thing was that it wasn't Gaia. Then, you have the Rite of Sacred Rebirth, which usually invokes the spirits of the Wyrm unless you're using the free sacrifice clause.
