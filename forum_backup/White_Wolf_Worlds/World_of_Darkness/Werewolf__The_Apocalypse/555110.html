    How would you handle species Hybridization in game?

    06-14-2015, 10:26 PM
    So I was reading an article recently here. I read other articles on this as well, but this was the first I could find to link.
    In any case, the article is about the increasing occurrence of coyotes mating with wolves. More and more coyotes, along the east coast in particular, are turning up with wolf DNA. It seems to be a matter of adaption through hybridization.
    Some of the "Coywolves" also have domestic dog DNA in them. One study showed that the new hybrid species that is expanding eastward is (roughly) 64% coyote, 13% western gray wolf, 13% eastern wolf species and the remaining 10% were domestic dog (presumably feral). There are other percentages for different groups that have been studied as well, this was just the one that pertained most directly to the article I was reading.

    So I was wondering what this would mean for the world of W:tA. How do you think spirits would deal with this, and how do you think this would affect Garou and Nuwisha? Obviously with coyotes showing up with wolf DNA, there are also wolves showing up with coyote DNA.


    The other WoD species this affects is the Gurahl, but to a much lesser extent since their Tribes don't divide clearly by bear species. There are 3 confirmed cases in the wild of polar bear/grizzly bear hybrids. For the Gurahl though, tribe is more a matter of which tribe needs numbers and is completely determined by spiritual factors, from what I remember.

      The normal answer is that it's about spirit, not genetics, so I'm going to work on the assumption we're just doing the one-shifter type, breed of the mother but kin to both thing for this particular answer.

      It would depend on the spirit, but any sort of "middle ground" critter would probably be more likely to be well received by spirits that treat with both parents. So, for example the Garou + Nuwisha offspring, assuming it became a Garou shifter, might be better able to communicate with trickster spirits, spirits of umbral travle and the deeper umbral broods regardless of its Auspice. A Nuwisha shifter of the same parents might be better able to communicate with spirits of Garou ancestry, since Nuwisha can basically already get their paws on anything else already anyway.

      In my opinion, keeping with the basic rule of "breed of the mother" sorting out the phenotype and genotype issues, the most important thing is role. What is their role for Gaia? Garou and Nuwisha have vastly different duties in practice, as a Shifter type, before we even get to breaking it down into their "Auspices" and more specialized aspects.

      Ligers (and I guess Tiglions, but no one talks about them anyway) are another interesting example of the cross breeding. Would you use Simba yava or Khan yava? One of each? Would spirits or Jamak that have relations with those specific breeds consider a Liger Bastet as part of both families, outcast to both, or simply one or the other? I think it's an interesting element to include in Chronicles (provided it isn't simply to cheese a broader range of Gifts), but ultimately highly circumstantial, case by case.

          Well, as a note for the situation, a lion fathering offspring will likely have 'Huge Size, while a lion mothering offspring will have 'Small Size'. This is due to an enzyme the lion produces in order for his offspring to be larger than other lions in the pride, while the females produce a counter-enzyme for it to keep sizes reasonable. It developed due to the fact that multiple males may share females. I don't recall if Cheetahs have a similar issue, but I don't think so, as they aren't built for size and power.

          But that being said, for Bastet, flip a coin. I wouldn't make a 'mixed Tribe' Bastet any more than I would a 'mixed Auspice' Garou. They are prone to physical problems, but it isn't absolute. Possible Deformity flaws, but not required.

          They would probably have some social flaws, to show the mistrust their tribe would have in such a case. They would likely have to push for appropriate Gifts and Rites to 'go it alone', and attending a Taghairm would likely be mixed tribe events. (Such as the Colorado one in the Breedbook.)

          One big example: Black Tooth. While being told that his fur color was the result of a deal with Cahlash (the Wyrm), it was more likely that he just had a black Leopard (or Jaguar) mother.

                      If the hybrid was able to reproduce, my default answer would be Kin To Both. Whether or not they accept it as Kin would be another story, but it would theoretically carry the potential for a Shifter of either breed to happen in its line.

                      You get into a whole host of odd philosophical questions with it too, in regards to the roles. Like, say these Wolf+Coyote hybrids established a healthy breeding population, and then they were no longer able to produce viable (healthy, fertile) offspring with either wolves or coyotes, but only each other. At that point they are effectively something else entirely. If they produce Shifters that are these hybrids, what is even their place? Their mandated role? Are they sacred tricksters and teachers, or are they warriors? Is such a thing natural, a process of every-evolving nature and Wyld? Is their having Shifters an indication of Gaia's blessing, or is it something twisted and perverse, like what is being seen with the shapeshifters created by Pentex?

                      I could see something like this being a really intriguing off-the-beaten-path seed for a Chronicle with inquisitive, thoughtful players. I could see using hybridization in a number of ways, from adding to roles to consolidating them; to adapt to survive what may happen after the Apocalypse (if there is to be an "after" in a given way it might be run, be it the actual end of the world, or just an end of a cycle and the beginning of a new one), or to better weather it in the present. Perhaps the Nuwisha and Garou are both meant to be killed off, and it is the hybrid Wolfyotes that would survive to replace both! Perhaps they are "reinforcements". Lots of possibilities. But whatever the case, the Garou probably wouldn't take it well, if their history has shown us any RP notes. Haha.

                                          Way back on Shadownessence, Chris Campbell weighed in on Bastet hybrids on two occasions. This is what he had to say:

                                          In game terms, the matter is simple -- the critter winds up looking like the Bastet parent, and if it changes it belongs said parent's tribe. If the other parent is kin to another Bastet tribe the cub has the normal chance to be kin to said tribe. And if it's two Bastet doing the mating, the progeny looks like a metis from one parent or the other (and I guess it'd be possible to put Simba and Khan in the same litter if you really really want to).
                                          On-topic, my two cents: it's a metis of the mother's tribe. Play with metis deformities (huge size, etc) as you like.
                                          But also:
                                          Well, I've gone on record in the past saying that IMO the Bastet should be a bunch of different Breeds (same with most of the other Fera tribes). But that way lies extensive rewrites of the setting and added work for the ST, so buyer be warned.
                                          See the full discussions here:
                                          http://www.shadownesssence.com/forum/index.php?/topic/20281-hercules-the-liger/
