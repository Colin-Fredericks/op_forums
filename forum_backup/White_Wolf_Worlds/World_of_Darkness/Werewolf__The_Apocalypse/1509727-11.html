                                                    Well it's nice for confirmation but he's stating the fucking obvious. V5 and post-paradox 20th was chock-full of surface-deep egregious virtue signalling while minimizing the transgressive umph that made the series so compelling and genuinely progressive. World of Darkness? More like the world of sunshine and rainbows where real world issues are solved or ignored.

                                                    Weirdly, werewolf kinda can get away with northern-hemisphere centric attitudes, because they've limited themselves to one type of wolf that exclusively lives in the northern hemisphere, except for the weird moments where they've gone out on a limb and ritually transformed themselves into something else. Now personally I'm an advocate for a broader werewolf definition and would happily accept a selection of other canids, but there's still a limit to how inclusive you could be with that . I'm only using it because it's such a great example, but there's nothing wrong with a lack of Maori werewolves, it'd be weird to force Maori inclusion given that new zealand has never had wild canids nor an equivalent (The Maori had domestic dogs, but Garou don't do that)

                                                    No inclusion is better than bad inclusion. Tokenism is bad. It's nice for marketing, but a deeper look and you'll realize how empty and hollow it all is. There's nothing wrong with sticking with what you know, and one of the nice things about the WoD is that knowledge about supernaturals is all hidden or covered in misinformation so it's perfectly fine that some glass walker has never heard of the Hakken.

                                                    I would rather see a Ugandan write about his region with confidence and depth to the exclusion of areas and ethnicities he doesn't know about than to see him try and accommodate Brazilians by writing about a France he knows little about. That's what this forced diversity feels like, it's for ignorant white liberals, not the people they're actually including. A Ugandan isn't going to jump with joy when you start being inclusive and using Angola, and few appreciate being a footnote when you try to include everyone.

                                                    Going the other route and acting like mortal cultures don't matter is really fucking weird for a game about tribes and tribalism.
