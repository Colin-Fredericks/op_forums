                                                  Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post
                                                  Any thoughts on how to stat such a thing, or a WW 1 or 2 Tank up?
                                                  Vehicles appear in Rage Across the Amazon and the Vampire 2nd editions player's guide. It's actually quite possible - and maybe even a bit easy! - to breach some of them just by clawing them, and there's always fur gnarl for the ones that are more difficult. However, an APC only requires ten levels at difficulty 4 to tear open under this system; this won't result in total destruction, but it will be a pretty big hole slashed through the armor.

                                                  The higher end of difficulty for breaching is a main battle tank, requiring 18 damage successes at difficulty 8. Those are much less likely to show up, however, unless your caern is in an active war zone where relatively higher-profile hardware is being deployed. Even then, three successes on a roll with Gremlins will still turn it into an oddly-shaped bunker.
