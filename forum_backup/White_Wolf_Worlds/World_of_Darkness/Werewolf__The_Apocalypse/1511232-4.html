    Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
    I will accept Asian silver fangs, but African silver fangs? Like, white-african silver fangs? Like the tribe's whole deal is how exclusive and inbred they are, and how impeccably white their coats are. Being exclusive snobs obsessed with purity to the point of rejecting someone with less than 3 pure breed is their human half when 2 is very good in most tribes and their wolf half is all about being white so you don't stick out in the snows of the far north where silver fangs hail from. There are no grey wolves in africa so there's no...
    OK so...

    Whether you accept things or not doesn't really matter to what the books say. House Blood Red Crest is based in the Indian sub-continent, and House Wise Heart was originally based in the Fertile Crescent and has followed the expansion of Islamic royalty from the Balkans to Morocco. One of the things the other Tribes hate about the (at least pre-W5) Silver Fangs is that they used to come in and claim the best kin as theirs by right of being the highest of station, allowing them to take over royal/noble/etc. bloodlines across the world. While most of the surviving Houses in the modern nights are based in European ancestries, and European royal norms dominate their traditionalists for it, the wider Tribe is snobby and elitist by whatever the local standards are.

    Also remember that there's no way to see how many dots of Pure Breed a character has, and Falcon, not the Garou, sets the bar for how well bred a character has to be to get in.

    The coat colors of the Tribes has nothing to do with biology and is pure symbolism. The Shadow Lords, Silent Striders, and Black Furies are both noted for having dark black fur, something that only happens when domesticated dogs are mixed back into wild wold populations. Wild wolf populations don't have enough variations for 13 Tribes to all look different in their wolf forms (at least not in a fashion that's functional for a game where everyone hasn't memorized the differences between all the different Canis lupus subspecies}.

    And, while still rather debated how to classify it specifically, genetic analysis of the Egyptian jackal says it's either a subspecies of the grey wolf, or a second unique wolf species along with the Ethiopian wolf. This species is also considered misnamed since it's former range included all of Africa bordering the Sahara, including south. There is zero reason (other than the original books being written without access to information) why the Silver Fangs wouldn't have spread to the Ethiopian and west African empires just like they did into Asia: there's wolves there (and actual wolves, even if they're not grey wolves), and royalty there. The Silver Fangs didn't wait for white people to colonize the Middle East or India before setting up shop in those areas.

                                        The problem outlined in this scenario is one in which an arbitrary quality, who you are descended from, becomes pivotal to whether you are accepted into a Tribe which is meant to be the legitimate rulership of all Garou according to some sources. Pure Breed has some messy qualities going on, it can surprise you, people can pop up with a surprisingly strong spiritual connection to a very obscure blood tie which is either very difficult or impossible to nail down, but Silver Fangs do need to have PB of 3 or higher to join the Tribe at all.

                                                    Pure Breed is tribe-specific as per W20 - a male child of Black Furies might have a PB 5 Black Fury mother and a PB5 Black Fury Kinfolk father, say, but will have PB0 in any other tribe.

                                                    Pure Breed is a nebulous combination of bloodline and spiritual inheritance. A character with high Pure Breed looks and carries himself like an archetypal member of his tribe — however, if he does not join that tribe, any benefits of Pure Breed are removed by the tribe’s totem.

                                                    Silver Fangs snapping up Purebred Kinfolk of other tribes is a thing, and people explain it one of two ways; Kinfolk are eligible to pass on pure breed well regardless of what tribe it's tied to, since their pure breed is not the same Background as Garou's (they just share a name etc.), or positing that the Silver Fangs have some method of bridging the gap. I go for the former as there's no canon hint of the latter.

                                                    The scenario I present is one in which a cub is mistaken for not having the necessary pure breed, but is later discovered to be eligible by the mandate of spirits.

                                                    Garou aren't the arbitrators of whether Pure Breed is present, no, but if you simply shift the blame upwards to Falcon you end up with a situation of:

                                                    - if this Totem is so awful, why is he qualified to represent the Tribe who are meant to rule?
                                                    - are the Silver Fangs just passive prisoners in this situation? are they doing everything they can to petition Falcon to lower the PB requirement to 2 or 1?
                                                    - if it's impossible to change such things, how did the Glass Walkers petition Cockroach to do away with Pure Breed in their Tribe, and how did the Black Furies convince Pegasus to allow their male Metis cubs to join the tribe?

                                                    I'm not saying the Silver Fangs are uniquely monstrous or evil, but they're deeply flawed in interesting ways. I like it when this is engaged with! It can make for compelling stories! Garou who were sufficiently pure blooded to join the Silver Fangs but rejected the Tribe because they think hereditary monarchy is bad. Modernizers who try, as some canon Silver Fangs have, to push the Tribe more towards believing in the ideals of rulership as opposed to inheritance of divine right. What happens when Fangs try to popularize the idea of allowing any trace of SF ancestry, no matter how faint, to qualify? Who fights back against this idea? Can Falcon be convinced? What would it take to prove the idea to him?
