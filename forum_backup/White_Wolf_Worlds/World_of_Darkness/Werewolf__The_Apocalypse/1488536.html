          Members of the real indigenous people for whom that myth is part of their cultural background have repeatedly called for it to stop being used; both as fodder for outsider fantasy/fiction, and indiscriminately as a term. The association of it with werewolves in particular (not unique to WtA btw) is something seen as extremely offensive to the people the myth has been appropriated from.

          There's no way for Discord to simply ban the world outright (you own screen cap shows it no less!) but most WtA social spaces are pushing to cease using it, and it's expected to get retconned out of W5.
