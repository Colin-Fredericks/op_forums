                            has anyone else felt that Wyrm taint has been used as a label for something an author or ST Thinks is bad, reflecting their politics, or philosophical views?
                            I got that sense very quickly when reading my first werewolf book, the revised core. I made the decision then to divorce my understanding of reality from anything in werewolf if that's the game I'm running and treat everything through the lens of the world of darkness.

                            For example, in real life, there's lots of companies, big and small, that have a benevolent corporate culture and generally take care of their employees. In the World of Darkness that I run, that kind of corporate culture is a myth, propagated by those fools desperate for hope.

                            Or take Law Enforcement. Most of those people are generally decent folks. But in my World of Darkness, more often than not they are on some kind of a power trip backed up with a system of corruption and your rights are a formality.

                                    Slavery was a universal human institution found in all cultures. Even societies that lacked large scale slavery on plantations, mines, and other dehumanizing conditions would have household slaves in the family doing hard and dreary work.

                                    So I don't think slavery in and of itself would register as Wyrm tainted. Depending on how you interpret Wyrm taint, it might show up in certain treatment of slaves, but that would be true of treatment of non-slaves as well.

                                    One can argue that the Garou's treatment of kinfolk is essentially a master-slave relationship, although this is less true now than in the past. Most likely any slaves a Garou would own would be through their kinfolk, not directly.

                                    As for slum lords and drug dealers, again it depends on how you interpret Wyrm taint. However, any situation which would attract Banes in large numbers to a small geographic area is likely going to cause that area to register Wyrm taint.
