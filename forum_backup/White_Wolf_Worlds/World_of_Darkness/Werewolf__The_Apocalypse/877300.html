          Corax are probably my only favorites. The Nagah and Ananasi are distant ties for 2nd, and Mokole and Ratkin tied at a 4th after that.

          The Bastet, Nuwisha, and Kitsune esp. are low on my list partially because they never feel "unique" enough for me. The Bastet's biggest difference from the Garou is that they're more prone to being loners and secretive... which is pretty shit for play. The Nuwisha are basically playing an all Wendigo/Uktena Ragabash game, and the Kitsune feel more like they're a Japanese offshoot of the Uktena (with the focus on mystic powers) than a separate Fera. The last two are also things that could be folded into the Garou to make the Garou more global and cohesive by having more species than just wolves associated with them.

          The Rokea, and Gurahl are neat concepts, but they developed them too much towards being NPCs than PCs this makes them extremely difficult to actually play; which is a big down side for me.

                              Corax seem to be the most fun to me in terms of variety of play and variety of plots, and how easily you can drop them into just about any situation. If other fera are rare (as far as the Garou are concerned) in your setting, like they were originally written...not sure how it's changed over the editions, it can be hard to explain why there's Gurahl around that nobody knew about, and Nagah etc are totally out of the question in most situations. Bastet are so well developed culturally that it almost seems weird to put them in a Werewolf campaign, but it's hard to do a group of just Bastet. On the other hand Corax-outta-nowhere is always believable.

                              I like Nuwisha in theory. They're fun to read stories about. They're much harder to play; I don't think I fit those pants nearly as well as I wish I did.

                                      I've always had fun using the Corax, having them roll into a caern talking a mile a minute and maybe or maybe not have something useful to contribute at the moment. It may be due to the first game I set up the caern totem was Raven so the local Garou had an established place for Corax to stay and they're were almost always at least one Corax taking advantage of the Garou's hospitality. The Corax were always an easy way to drop a story hook in a pinch and an interesting way to alter usual Garou battle planning, having air support nearby all the time.

                                      My other to favorites were are probably the Gurahl and Nuwisha. The Gurahl as master lore keepers in hidden groves of Gaia hidden in the deep wilderness has always seemed like a good way to give the Garou a quest for information only this sage would know. The Nuwisha also showed up every now and then to give the Garou a horrible headache, like keeping the sept on it's toes by booby-trapping the sept's territory and leading the Garou on a goose chase.
