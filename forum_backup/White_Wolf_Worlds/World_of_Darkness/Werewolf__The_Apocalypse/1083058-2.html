                                                Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                                                Stargazers, by contrast, see Rage as a weakness and control over it as a weakness.
                                                Um... not really? The Stargazers don't see Rage as a weakness, and absolutely seek control over it. They see Rage as dangerous, and attempting to master just by stoking it as weakness. They view strength as achieving tranquility despite Rage and seek transcendence to a balanced state where tranquility and Rage are experienced in harmony with each other as they fulfill their duties as warriors.

                                                And which, yes, Revised said, "Yes, in the end, they found out they had more in common with the Fera courts and left the Garou Nation."
                                                Well, mostly left. Some didn't (of an already small Tribe), and W20 walked this back anyway to have this be more of an impending possibility. Of course, being members of the Garou Nation or throwing in with the Beast Courts doesn't really address the thread topic of their viability as a Tribe.

                                                I mostly use the Stargazers as an opportunity to show that Garou don't HAVE to blood-soaked berserkers. But if they AREN'T, they may actually be going against their purpose under Gaia.
                                                I find this rather boring myself, because it means everyone else must be a blood-soaked berserker. Get Rabagash are way better at this to me because being a Fenrir that bucks the Tribal image of being a the most bloody of berserkers and fighting with swift cunning elegance over brute force calls into question what the Garou's true purpose is by fulfilling their Auspice purpose so adamantly.

                                                I frankly hate it when one Tribe needs to edge out concepts in other Tribes to protect their thematic niche. If the rest of the Garou need to be blood-soaked berserkers for the space for the Stargazer's Middle Way, then I can do without the Stargazers as a Tribe.


                                                Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                                                I don't get your point. At all.
                                                If I'm interpreting currently:

                                                1) Elder Stargazers don't argue that, "being a bunch of violent rage-filled bullies is a dumb way of life." Because that argument is something they recognize is futile and counterproductive. It is the young Stargazers that are still only at a shallow understanding of their Tribe's philosophies that would make this argument. The older traditional Stargazers value the wisdom that Rage has to offer, and understand that Rage is an inherent part of their nature, and thus do not begrudge others that cope with it in their own ways. These elders also still value Glory as warriors and understand that this duty is shared by all Garou, combined with the burden of Rage, and to judge the other Tribes as the externally look, "bullies," is a failure of the compassion they seek to temper their Rage with.

                                                2) "Neat philosophic questions" aren't good things to define the Tribes. Tribal culture should be interesting by being variations to the core idea of the Garou without losing being Garou, rather than being defined solely by a philosophic quirk that is opposed to the Garou concept.
