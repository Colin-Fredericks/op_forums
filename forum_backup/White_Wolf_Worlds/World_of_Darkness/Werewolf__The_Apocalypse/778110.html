      Never read something like that. I know of a rite that leads a Silver Fang to the house (or similar dwelling place) the potential best suited mate lives in. The catch being that you don't know if the mate is the bastard child working as housemaid or the daughter of the household, or her little sister whom you have to wait several years for to become of age.

      I found that this rite made all the other mentions of political dealings and intermarriage for power gain kind of unwise. For why defy a rite that if properly executed would bring you the best offspring?
