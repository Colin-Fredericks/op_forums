    Extinct Breeds

    02-15-2018, 12:11 PM
    I was super excited when Shattered Dreams came out - and it was pretty cool and all - but then a bit dissapointed
    There were other Breeds, long ago others existed, who either died out long ago when their animal Kin were extinct or were actively destroyed by other Fera. In most cases, their cultures as well as their duty to Gaia has been forgotten and neglected since their demise - and Shattered Dreams forgot to mention them
    Maybe somebody can help me to fill the blanks , morsel of knwoledge hidden in some old books, and such I never read, who knows!
    So lets see:

    - Insect Races - okay pretty easy. Breed Book: Ananasi and Rokea is a good information source for these guys (kinda). But their time was before the Dragon Kings or the lived alongside the Kings? The Dragon Kings missed the whole Insect Wars? Looked the other way? Never knew about it?

    The Insect were the easy as I said, but there are---- others:

    - After the Age of Kings, Ammonite shifters (who were at war with the Rokea) and bristlecreepers went extinct along with their Kin (Breed Book: Mokolé p.21) - First things first, what the hell is a "bristlecreeper"? No, really. What is it? Any info about these guys?
    - Qyrall (the shapshifter Breed of Anemone Shifters, breed of Qyrl and they almost wiped out the Rokea - Breed Book: Rokea p.44) - The poor shark folks are not the most loved shifters by the players or Storytellers, yet their history is rich and quite bloody (no pun intended) , the Sharks it seems iced three other shifter-breeds, the Ammonite shifters, the bristlescreepers and the Qyrall. Not even the Garou managed to exterminate three breeds! Then again the Sharks had couple of million years to do do this. This undersea genocide went unnoticed too... ? Is there any information about this war under the waves? Or anything about their possible revival? After all Qyrl is one of the most active Wyrm....ish (not really a bad girl, but this is another mater) spirit around and her tentacles are deep in Humanity (no Hentai references please, it was just an experession) and interested in their science!

    And there are some... honorable mentions:
    - The Corax speak of a race of were-megatheriums that were slaughtered in the War of Rage. (Breed Book: Corax p.22)
    - Rumours among the Nagah tell of a breed of humans that could assume the shape of freshwater fishes, who went extinct in the War of Rage. (Breed Book: Nagah p.46)
    - The Shadow Lords remember a breed of werefalcons that were destroyed in the War of Rage. (Tribe Book: Shadow Lords Revised p.13)
    - The Fianna remember tribes of wereotters and wereeagles that fell beneath their claws in the War of Rage (Tribe Book: Fianna p.15)
    - Rumours among the Beast Courts tell of breeds of sapient shapeshifting orangutans in the indonesian rainforests ( Players Guide to the Changing Breeds, p. 27)

    So, while most of these are tales and legends mentioned only once, they are considered "canon"? Is there any source material about these unknown and extinct exotic breeds? (Or any plans to do so, like in the Shattered Dreams?^^)

    Anyhow, any and every help and scrap of information about these long gone Breeds are apriciated!
