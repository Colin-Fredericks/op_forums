        Talking things out with your players is important. Some players are fine with their PCs chasing an impossible dream as long as they know that as players it's impossible. Some players are fine to hear that something is theoretically possible but will be extremely difficult and unlikely to see success in play as a "soft" no (what feels overpowered or excessive at the start of the game might seem more reasonable when the PCs are all high XP elders on the verge of becoming Legends). Others will readjust to something easier to accomplish in the scope of your chronicle.

        Let the players know where you stand: what's a hard no, what's a soft no, what's something they can try but you're not going to make easy, etc. Then hear what they have to say, while remembering that you have to have fun running the game for them to have fun playing it; and if saying no is important to you having fun, they need to respect that.

                                    In terms of the average number of fetishes that Garou have, I once reviewed several Rage books and made a census of how many fetishes and their levels individual Garou had by their rank. Here is what I discovered in general.

                                    Rank 1 and 2 Garou typically did not have fetishes, but there were exceptions with a small number of them having fetishes of any level (although it was typically low level fetishes of rank 1 or 2).

                                    Rank 3 Garou had one or more fetishes whose total level were equal to their level. This might mean one fetish of level 3, three level one fetishes, or one level 1 and one level 2 fetish. Since this is an average only, there were still Adren who had no fetishes and other Adren who had fetishes with greater combined levels, but not more than double their rank.

                                    Rank 4 or 5 Garou typically have 1 to 4 fetishes equal to their rank. Again this is average with some Garou having less or more than this. But even on the high level, these individual high ranked Garou did not seem to carry more levels in fetish than double their rank.

                                    This seemed to be the case from book to book in addition to it being the average for all the books. So it is possible this was some kind of guideline the writers of the Rage books intended. Or it may have been unintentional.

                                    In any case, this rough guideline is what I use in creating my NPCs, or allowing/providing the PCs fetishes.

                                    If someone had more fetishes than this guideline, there began to be an increased possibility other Garou with greater "claim" to them would begin to take them or challenge for them. "That fetish is too powerful for a low ranking Garou like yourself, it's better for the Nation that someone more capable takes care of it." "You have way too many fetishes. You cannot make use of all of them. It's better to surrender some to the sept leadership so they can determine who can make the best use out of them." "Young Cliath, your grandfather would have wanted you to have that klaive, but you are still too young for such a burder. I will keep it safe for you until you reach the rank of Adren and be equal to its responsibility." "You are being too selfish by keeping such a powerful fetish for yourself while greater Garou have none. I will correct this problem by taking it from you in worthy challenge." And so on.

                                    Every ST will have their own sweet spot in terms of fetishes and characters. But I found this works for me.

                                                        You are fundamentally misunderstanding spirits. They don't follow our logic, they just are, and by being they fulfil their meaning. Most of them don't care about Gaia or the war for the simple reason that it's outside their understanding of what is reality. To the lesser ones it's literally only about what they represent and what their higher ups tell them to do, and even following that is pure instinct, not reasoning or choice. To the greater ones it's about being the concept and feeding the concept to greater strength. The greatest ones can be quite prideful, haughty and feel that they are beyond mortal concerns because they are the greatest of what they represent and only seek to further that.

                                                        By accepting the fate of being a fetish they stop being what they are and deny their most fundamental nature to serve in some great conflict that is very hard to comprehend for the vast majority of them. For the powerful, it's a fate worse than death, all their power, glory and respect are gone and forgotten and all their pride as the pinnacle of the concept is subsumed by a fitful sleep within a harsh brutal thing made of base material, stuck in a world that's wrong in every possible way.

                                                        So yes, they are allies to the Garou in so far as there are ancient pacts, deals with certain broods and Garou offer them the right appropriation, or service that furthers their brood. but they have no reason or comprehension of how mortals actually live and what they are concerned about. That's pretty much why spirit speech isn't literal language, but interpreting the idiosyncratic nature of the ephemeral being you are communing with. The reason they help is because they are told to, or given enough respect and power (Gnosis) to sacrifice their purpose for a while. Consider it like this, forcing a spirit into a fetish is like telling a river to stop flowing. Once it does that it's no longer a river.

                                                        There are spirits with agendas that extend into the real world, and that actually kinda comprehend the mortal world and it's intricacies. Those are of great power and the least likely to ever get into a fetish or a talen. The City Fathers, the Totems or the Celestials are glorious beings beyond such trivialities.

                                                        The other side of the coin is that the Garou understand and respect this. They follow the ancient pacts and rules and make this service as little a burden as they can on their strange kin, because they know that if they don't the bad reputation and disrespect will breed contempt and create a rift that will never mend. Pushing these lines and creating friction between the Garou and the spirits is the best way to get your arse handed to you by the other Garou who understand how frail this system actually is.
