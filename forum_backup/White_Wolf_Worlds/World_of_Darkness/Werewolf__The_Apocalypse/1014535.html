                  What some breeds cannot is to be changed by vampiric blood. All changers have some resistance to ghouling, and even more to the Embrace, and some go as far as to be completely immune (unless GM fiat). Right now I remember that Nuwisha, Corax and Mokole can't be ghouled nor Embraced. Corax and Mokole because Helios protects them that much, Nuwisha for reasons.

                  The GM Fiat is relevant here because there are actual canon characters that are already exceptions. The greatest I know is Sobek, a 4th Generation Setite Mokole Abomination torpid somewhere in a temple in Egypt (obviously). It is explained, though, through ritual magic from Set himself.

                  But that is it. It os hard as hell to turn most changers into vampire-related whatever, but corrupted they all can be, as easily as anyone else. And there is plenty of examples of corrupted individuals from all changers.

                  Ananasi are a special case on most aspects, but the Kumo are there to show that they are as much prone to corruption by the Wyrm as anyone else. The difference is that an Ananasi can also be linked to the Wyrm without corruption, following the designs of their Totem-Queen.

                                              Ok, so page 49 of Ananasi's book says it.

                                              I won't consider actual arthropods hemolymph, though, because it isn't relevant. Blood doesn't have the power to give motion to a corpse, either, much less to make this corpse reach another dimension to shape shadows or bend time to acquire velocity. A lot of vampire stories talk about lesser vampires who suck the life of vermin, including insects and arachnids. It isn't a necessary part of the vampire trope, but it is a valid one.

                                              Not against the game not going for it, though. At least not for the Ananasi, that are the weirdest of all changers, so it doesn't even have to have something to do with actual spiders. Specially if you consider that they are still changers, so partly human. Maybe they're edible for vampires, actual spiders. And the quantity problem can be overcome with, well, quantity.

                                              By the way, it doesn't have anything to do with Ananasi being immune to vampirism in any way. Either to be ghouled or Embraced the vampire doesn't have to drink a single drop of the victim's blood. It is the other way around, and Ananasi are meant to drink blood, even having their own Blood Pool. So they are quite receptive to vampire's blood, the opposite of other changers. I still think they are immune to vampirism, by the way, but for the metaphysical reasons of being already bloodsucking monsters.

                                                      When I said they were receptive to vampire's blood, I wasn't talking about Bonding or Ghouling. I was talking about drinking. Other changers react as if with a violent allergy to a vampire's blood. Ananasi drink blood, so I don't see them having any difficulty in swallowing vampire's blood.

                                                      If I would talk about Bonding and Ghouling, I do not think that the Ananasi can be Ghouled, for the same reason I do not think they can be Embraced. But I do think they can be Bonded. You see, other changers can't be easily Bonded because they spit out or regurgitate Vitae most of the time, instead of actually consuming it. If Ananasi can drink vampire's blood, then they are prone to Bonding.

                                                      That said, I wouldn't make their Bond into a normal one. They have weird shit with their emotions, with a few exceptions. Most Ananasi are cold as ice, a few are utterly emotionless, they are quite a psychopathic bunch. The Blood Bond of a species naturally hollowed of emotion could result in very weird things, from bland, weak bonds to a psychopath suddenly obsessed. Probably any result won't be very good for the vampire, anyway.

                                                      About the system for ghouling and embracing a changer, I don't care about the others. The Ananasi are marked by weirdness, not being from Gaia nor anything the other changers are used to. Gnosis is your link to the Spirit, but the Spirit doesn't have to be inimical to vampires by default. I would make them immune because I can see them, as bloodsuckers already, being designed to drink cursed blood without contracting a curse that simply mimics them. As much as a vampire can't be ghouled or Embraced anew.
