                        I keep poking at the idea of the Ananasi having various regional-esque cultures, sort of reflecting the local cultural and mythology of spiders as well as possibly being slightly oriented toward one part of the Triat. For example, the Ananasi culture of the Mediterranean region (especially the eastern part) would be the Arachne (or some variation of that), who tend to be Weaver oriented while placing a certain emphasis on weaving and various arts, crafts, and trades which are associated with it (including things that are perhaps a few steps removed, such as the fashion industry, chemistry via the dye trade, and merchant trading/finance).
                        I'm not really sure what the various other subcultures would be. Obviously the Ananse of West Africa (and by cultural diaspora the Caribbean). Also the Tsuchigumo of Japan (who would probably be somewhat Wyrm oriented but would not be the same thing as the Kumo, presuming they still existed in this theoretical set up). Maybe the Uktomi of the North American Great Plains region, who'd be somewhat Wyld oriented tricksters.
                        I'm not sure what else. Possibly a Weaver-oriented Indian faction tied into the concepts of Maya and the veils of illusion. And a Northern European Worm-oriented group (the Cob?) who draw from some of Tolkien's stuff. There's also a 4000 year old Peruvian temple to a spider god, but unfortunately its name seems to be forgotten to time.
                        I'd like to have something for the Amazon, for Australia, for China, for the Southern US/Mexico, and Southern/Eastern Africa, but it all remains very much a work in progress.
