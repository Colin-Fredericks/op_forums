        The lore content of the game is generally not something I need for my games. However the book does have a lot of decent mechanical stuff: rules for what it takes to do body modification on a Garou, the custom pack tactics, the new rites and Gifts. The new Metis deformities are... hit or miss thematically, but the mechanics tend to be fine. The section on coming up with new rites and modifying existing ones is fairy sparse on the mechanical side of things (though I can't really blame them, as the official Rites are a mechanical nightmare of inconsistency) but it's still a solid part of the book.

        So it gets a solid, "mechanics from CW20 assume yes, lore from CW20 assume no," for my games.

So, mighty get of fenris will kick open the gas station restroom door to use the mirror there instead of carrying a make-up mirror in their man-purse.

                                                        Also, I found that steppung Sideways should have reworked anyways. Difficulties should be limited to a smaller range, like 5 to 8.                                                             I've always house-ruled and done it where you can side-step anywhere as long as you have some Gnosis in your system. All that would really stop you is the local Gauntlet level if its really high and if you keep getting failed rolls for it. The fact is that the Garou in many situations NEED to get into the Umbra fast and its silly that you need meditation or a long wait time to warp into it.

                                                            That Bane that had just materialized and killed a bunch of hikers, then went back into the Umbra? Oh, we need to tail it fast? Oh right... we need mirrors and/or we need to wait awhile.. by the time they get into the Umbra, that Bane is already long gone. So in short, the entire namesake of Side-STEP should relatively be a fast on the fly movement. Constricting or making it tedious doesn't make sense since the Umbra is so integral for this game.
