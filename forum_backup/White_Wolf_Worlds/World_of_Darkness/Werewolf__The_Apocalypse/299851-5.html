                                        I think the problem is that the one-dimensionality focuses to much on the kill humans part, and not enough on the inhuman monstrosity part. As a result, the Red Talons tend to hold on to really ancient knowledge that other Garou don't have access to through their ancestors... stuff only a Mokole would remember. If the W20 book focused some on their connection to the ancient times with forgotten rites, and less on the Impergium, they'd probly come off as less flat.

                                                            Personally, the Red Talons have always been a paradox to me. As a tribe? I adore them. I think they're a wonderful counterpoint to the other Tribes that have managed to move on, but also a rather stark look at what the Garou used to be when the Impergium was going on and the like. They're cornered, snarling and snapping to the bitter end, cut off by ideology from their own 'brothers'.

                                                            For Homids - for us - it's a very alien view to take. Garou aren't human but a lot of Homid have trouble letting go entirely, and the idea that the Red Talons would slaughter their Kinfolk as well as the rest of humanity is likely terrifying and doesn't paint them in a good light. But in that same vote... it's this view that makes the tribe as a whole difficult. Without digging into their Tribebook and the camps, it's likely a very common view that they'd happily see all of humanity gone... including Kinfolk, and ergo Homid Garou.

                                                            The revised tribebook mentions that the writer asks a Red Talon if they'd kill Homid Garou - the usual response being "of course not!" but they'd never considered it before.

                                                            As a whole, I can see why - even over Lupus in general - this view is VERY hard to pull off and even in an RP, tolerate playing. I struggle, for all I'd love to play one!

                                                            Just my views!
