                On top of that -3, there's also the rule that Packs share Renown for things they do together. When a Pack goes off, investigates a warehouse, finds a BSD and some fomori up to trouble, kill them all, and cleanse the place, and so on, the Renown goes to all members of the Pack as a single unit rather than worrying about who killed what, or who did which Rite.
