                                                            Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                                                            Last Howl is notably so far the only proof that Hauglosk can be reduced in-universe but, apparently, can be at the ST's discretion.
                                                            To be fair, anything can happen at the ST's discretion.

                                                            The takeaway though is that reducing Hauglosk is intended to be extremely rare and difficult for players to accomplish. It requires a specific 5 dot Loresheet, and you can only do it once per chronicle (so effectively you maybe get 6 Hauglosk points instead of 5 before turning into an NPC but there's still the possibility that you fail your Rage roll so you're SOL anyway). Any other way to reduce Hauglosk (or Harano) would likely need to have similar requirements and difficulties. Ultimately it's seems that removing Harano/Hauglosk isn't something that's intended for every PC at the table to be able to do, and certainly not with anything approaching frequency, ease or certainty.
