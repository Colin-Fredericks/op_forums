                                    Originally posted by Story Letter View Post

                                    I get the impression that it will have similarities, but it will be a different game with a completely different mood, way more dark.
                                    I meant similar in the sense of being a sort of a sandbox (I don't know how else to call it) game - with different locations you can travel to, different and meaningful ways of tackling challenges, real consequences to your decisions, rewards for completing tasks in a better way, various sides to join and/or support, resources management, development of the character's capabilities. I think and feel that Bloodlines really did all those elements in a very good way - or at least they tried, and in the case of those less successful elements even if the meat and/or details weren't properly developed, what is there still enhances the game's mood at the very least. The final result is much better than Knights of the Old Republic 1 and 2, I'd say.


                                    In regards to the upcoming W:tA game being 'way more dark' - Vampire: The Masquerade Bloodlines starts with you Sire being killed publicly, the game camera showing different Kindred as they watch coolly. The game has drinking human blood as an integral part of it, to the point when you have to press space within a few seconds so your character doesn't drain someone to death. You interact with other Kindred living amidst the humanity, the humans completely unaware due to the Masquerade.
                                    Major spoilers for Vampire: The Masquerade Bloodlines
                                    There is a Tzimisce Vicissitude monsters snuff film. A Nagaraja spooking, killing and eating a 'supernatural-busting' TV show crew. A Malkavian whose dual personality -related issues can result in one of the personalities 'dying' ; and another Malkavian who is terrifying enough merely through his recorded musings. Your Ghoul kidnaps a human for you to feed from. Interactions with other Nightfolk - the Kuei-Jin, a Garou, Asian Technocrat True Mage(s) - are hostile. The Kindred 'authorities' are often clueless about what is going on, and send you out into the night. The mythology of the Kindred (Lilith, Caine) is also present; as is the Jyhad.


                                    I know that Wt:A has the Wyrm and other aspects that can be considered and called dark, but can you really get substantially more dark - rather than a different kind of dark - than V:tM, and Bloodlines?


                                    Originally posted by Story Letter View Post

                                    I actually like the idea of a ronin that can be redeemed or fall entirely to the wyrm depending on decisions, great idea for game story.
                                    Yes there should definitely be a Wyrm-related option/pathway for the character. Perhaps a 'morality meter' (Gaia / Wyrm meter) that goes through the whole game, and maybe a chance to dance the Black Spiral at some point - perhaps only if the character earns it through continuous actions throughout the game.

                                    And in an expansion perhaps a Weaver-related option/path could be added.
