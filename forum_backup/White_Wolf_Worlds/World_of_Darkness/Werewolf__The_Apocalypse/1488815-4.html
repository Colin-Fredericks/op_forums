    It would be fairer to actually confirm this edition is a reimagining of the games, then keep this whole pretense of "but it's a continuation!" and then contort themselves to explain things that just don't follow. The old editions all claimed to be the same setting, and while there were retcons and recontextualisations, none were really as massive as what's happening right now. I'd say that the changes to V5 are more drastic than the changes to Mage: Revised, and that's pretty much the biggest war people have had with the publisher themselves.

    I assume part of the problem is that the playerbase is already long balkanised with simultaneous support for 20th Anniversary, CoD and the V5 stuff. I frequent reddit and the number of questions there about where to start and what's the difference between editions, names and publishers is ridiculous. Ian put in a sticky explanation there, but it's not enough for the people getting into this. The further we go the worse this will get, and I'm really concerned that a kneejerk reaction to this could damage what's left of 20th and the CoD lines.
