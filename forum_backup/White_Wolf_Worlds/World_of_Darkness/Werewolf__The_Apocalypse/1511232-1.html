                            Originally posted by Nicolas Milioni View Post
                            Ive been hearing a lot of "x doesn't feel like a werewolf" lately.
                            what exactly makes someone a werewolf a opposed to something else?
                            Simply to put; ferality and savageness. Each of these concepts should feel like they could become a ravening monster wolf in one second. To dip a bit into Forsaken imagery, the car liberator shouldn't CARE about human laws and focus on 'hunting' the fancy cars. Sell them to a chop shop and pocket the money gladly as it is theirs to take.

                            To use a concept presented in Hart Wardens, the Huntsman should not be a servant to the gentry, but a hunter of another kind. Hunting deer to keep the overpopulation low but ALSO humans to keep the trophy hunters from overpopulating.

                            The detective is not actually a detective, but someone you go to when you have no lawful way to bring someone to justice. The 'detective' will handle it and in the morning they find the wrongdoer torn to bits.

                            These are not smooth vampires who try to cling to a thin veneer of civilization, these are savage monsters who tear their foes limb from limb and in some cases eat them. They are intense, feral and quick to anger.

                                            Oh sure, when the Fianna/Hart Wardens disembowel someone and string the entrails up on a tree, it's "a strong message". But when the Red Talons do it, it's "completely psycho" and "totally unjustified".

                                            Originally posted by Nicolas Milioni
                                            It was my understanding that it'd be at very least weird for a non celt to join the fianna,as well as a non native joint the uktena. It'd need some justification. But now all bqckground are welcome on all the tribes.
                                            I might have gotten something wrong though​
                                            Not being from a given human culture in Revised meant you were unlikely to have Pure Breed (that was lifted in W20, letting anyone have PB regardless of ethnic background). Basically, anyone could have a lineage associated with a tribe, as was even mentioned in the Revised Get of Fenris tribebook, and as of the most recent materials, they could also be a storied lineage of the tribe's heroes as reflected by PB. Some tribes were more willing than others to take different folks in; the Fianna wouldn't be as choosie as Older Brother, who has kinship chiefly with the dispossessed and oppressed indigenous peoples of the world, because the Fianna only care about being bard-ish and are more willing to sweep concerns like colonial privilege under the rug. The Red Talons, of course, won't let in any human cultures, and the Silver Fangs only come from the tribe's oldest and most established heroic lineages across Europe, Asia, and North Africa, but they're exceptional in this regard.
