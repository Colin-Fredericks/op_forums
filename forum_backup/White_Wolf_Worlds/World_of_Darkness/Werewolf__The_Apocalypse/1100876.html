    A peace treaty with the Fera

    06-06-2017, 01:16 PM
    I was thinking about doing a game where the North American Garou attempt to make a peace treaty with the Fera. I'm just spitballing the idea now but it would be arranged around a moot and there would be mostly roleplaying done there (albeit maybe a few challenges or duels). I'm curious if anyone has any ideas what might be addressed during this or challenges to face in such an endeavor.

    Which Fera would be for, which would be against, and who would try to sabotage it.

    Ditto Garou.

          It is hard to do "treaties" on this scale because there is no formal organization that can represent the whole Garou Nation and enforce any such policy among all the individual septs and Garou, likewise for the multiple groups of Fera.

          At best there could be a grand concolation where tribal elders of all the tribes in North America get together and agree for a common policy towards the Fera, and they announce at a caern there will be a concolation in three months to discuss that. Then those elders from all the tribes would send messengers throughout North America to convince all the other important Garou to attend. It would take a lot of politicking to assemble sufficient elders and sept leaders so that the moot's decisions would be accepted and upheld throughout the entire North American continent. But even then, there will be Garou who will not attend; Garou who will not agree; and enforcement of any provisions will depend on individual Garou at individual septs to do so.

          Presumably you already have a reason for why elder Garou of each tribe are doing this - some kind of crisis or prophecy to provoke this. And it's very unclear to me what such a "peace treaty" would mean in practical terms.

          As for the Fera, there are two groups closest to the Garou who would most likely support them - the Corax and Nuwisha.

          Then there are groups who would be more problematic because they don't trust the Garou - the Gurahl and the North American Bastet (Balam, Pumonca, and Qualmi). However, their overall agenda is probably close enough that there is opportunity for dialogue and diplomacy.

          Next are groups whose agendas I think are too different for them to care about making deals with the Garou - Ananasi and Ratkin.

          There are also groups which probably don't live in North America or considered lost and therefore don't care and are out of scope - Ajaba, the other Bastet (Bagheera, Bubasti, Ceilican, Khan, Simba, and Swara), Kitsune, Nagah, and Rokea. I'm unsure about the Mokole because of North American alligators.

          But for any of the Fera, they probably have even greater problems than the Garou about obtaining some kind of group consensus and authority to entreat with these elder Garou.

                                                  Cultural myths are more real than any history. Whether the Fera started it they generally have fewer resources than the united Garou. So they are going to likely demand some sort of reconpensation for slights real or imagined. You will likely have to have different treaties worked out by Changing Breed or by tribe of said breed(Fucking Bastet) with Corax being the easiest and Ratkin or Rokea being the hardest(Nagah are off the table, Ratkin and Rokea are barely on the table). So you have options decide which Fera you want to deal with first and don't feel pressured to bring them all in, even success might turn around those have rejected it.

                                                  Broadly speaking you will have people trying to use the sudden closeness to finally get revenge for some historical slight. You will also have profound misunderstandings as the various changing Breeds do not share renown and have various physeological and spiritual diferences. This will of course lead to "teach us your cool secrets" which will go over as well as you'd expect. I'd give a tribal break down but really what is given up will more be on a sept by sept basis there may be COGies who won't give up their sept because they know best and Shadowlords who's ruthless pragmatism says "I don't care if you use our caern just don't weaken it"

                                                      You need a common enemy and a lynch pin. For the Court it was the Keui-Jin and the Kitsune. For the Ahadi it was the Endless Storm and the Ajaba. You need to create an immediate big bad that no one breed can defeat on their own. I say immediate because the Wyrm doesn't count. The Wyrm is patient and each breed is already lulled into a false sense of security that they have time to solve that problem. You need something that threatens moving the Wyrms time table up in the here and now. Once you have that you need to have one side step up and say, "Ok, shit hit the fan. Ready to talk now?".

                                                      I have run a story line like this before. Its a lot of fun. Just make sure to have failures otherwise is just seems to easy and your players a likely to blow it up. Hindsight... lol

                                                          I've got some general specifics going on here as ideas:

                                                          1. The Garou Nation and Fera will agree to a non-aggression pact within North America as well as mutual defense against attack by Wyrm creatures plus any member who doesn't want to play by their rules. They will also punish those who attack each other without cause (which gets complicated).

                                                          2. There's going to a ceremonial exchange of fetishes and apology from Albretch regarding the War of Rage plus the ceding of a token number of caerns (minor ones) which once belonged to the Fera. I'm thinking this is going to be the real issue because I think Albretch worked on smoothing things with the Wendigo before but some of their caerns originally belonged to Fera because history is just like that.

                                                          3. There will be the option for Fera to be unmolested as associates of packs and vice versa but both sides think this is mostly a personal thing anyway.

                                                          4. Both sides can call upon the other during times of war but the other side isn't necessarily supposed to answer.

                                                          5. This doesn't effect any Garou or Fera in continents other than them.

                                                          Neither side thinks this is much but it's as much as they think they can get away with now. It's also something which really offends a lot of people because quite a few Garou and Fera think the wounds from the War are still fresh.

                                                          They're going to be uniting against the Under the Blood Red Moon events which will be their first sign if they can do anything together.
