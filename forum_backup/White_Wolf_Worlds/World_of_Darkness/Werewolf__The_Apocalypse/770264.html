                                  (Just using our post as a jumping-off point -- only addressing it tangentially as a point of reference for certain aspects...)

                                  The thing about a question like this is that is "jumps the fence", meta-physically speaking, into another splat's yard: the metaphysics involved in transforming an ordinary mortal into a spirit (of the Middle-Umbra variety) is something that -- should a Garou ever be interested in having happen, for whatever reasons -- would require seeking out a mage or powerful hedge-wizard to accomplish (more so the former than the latter, of course). While circumstances that the Garou, in general, face and interact with can circumstantially possibly lead to a given mortal becoming a spirit (perhaps as the by-product of having remained for years in a Middle Umbral realm, such as Avalon or similar, for example, without some mystical connection/projection against becoming wholly spirit/discorporated), it generally is beyond the purview of most Gifts/Rites for such a thing to be made to happen. (A mage, of course, could do so with Spirit:3/4, Life:3/4, Mind:3, Prime:1; Spirit/Life: transforms the target into an ephemeral being, in toto, while Mind helps guide the mental transition that would be a part of that process as well as allow the new spirit to cognitize things in a different manner; Prime would allow for the mortal-cum-spirit to retain a metaphysical sense of his essential self, metaphysically speaking, which in turn allows for potential interaction with the physical world again with less... difficulty... in the future.)

                                  The only "predictable" circumstances wherein I could perceive such a thing falling within the workings/inclinations of most Garou is perhaps via Rank:5 Gift or Rite that "blesses" the mortal with a spiritual existence either after natural death or at the time of the mystical enactment. (This would, of course, be quite different from a mortal becoming a Wraith, as they'd become a Middle-Umbral-as-more-elemental essence, but would serve as some sort of very high reward for some truly great act, duty or sacrifice that would warrant such a shift -- or "elevation", in the minds of most Garou -- for said mortal.)

                                  This would so rare, however, as to be the stuff of legends -- literally. It would be akin to a vampire achieving Golconda or a werewolf of the Croatan tribe coming back out of the Umbra, today, whole-and-complete and ready to revive his tribe, somehow. Rare, rare stuff....

                                  I have been around here for waaaayyyy too fucking long...
