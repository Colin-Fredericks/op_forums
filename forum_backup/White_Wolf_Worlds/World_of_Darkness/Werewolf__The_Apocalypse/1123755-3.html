                                                    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
                                                    Players aren't always up to acting their way through everything. "My character should know the right thing to say, because they've got all the stats, but I'm having a brain fart," shouldn't be punished with, "then you don't get to roll you horrible player!" Because that's not fun, and this is a game. You don't hold back attack rolls because the player doesn't pick the right stance and attack direction to best strike around the opponents defenses. You just let them roll the attack. Same deal here.

                                                    ...

                                                    As well, players don't have to "earn their roll." The rolls are there to abstract a situation where success, failure and the degrees thereof are in question. A player doesn't have to earn a soak roll, an Investigation roll to look over a crime scene, or whatever. The ST should be getting players to roll, because that means the ST is providing situations with dramatic tension where success, and failure have interesting stakes. If you're forcing your players to earn their rolls, your falling down on your job to actually provide scenarios that are dramatically interesting for the players to be in, in the first place.

                                                    For social actions in particular, rolling after role-playing is detrimental to role-playing itself, because it creates a much greater dissonance than rolling first and RPing out the results. And is how the system generally works. Your character tries to do something. You roll. You role-play the results of whatever you tried. You don't role-play out all the twists and turns of a car chase and roll the dice after you've described the whole thing to see how it went. You get into a car chase, roll, and then role-play out the rolls' results.
                                                    I'm not going to "like" the whole post, but this part I definitely agree with. Nobody should be penalized because they don't have 5 dots worth of charisma or empathy in real life any more than they should be penalized because they don't have 5 dots worth of appearance or intelligence in real life. "Get up and dance for your success on that performance roll" isn't something any sane storyteller would say.
