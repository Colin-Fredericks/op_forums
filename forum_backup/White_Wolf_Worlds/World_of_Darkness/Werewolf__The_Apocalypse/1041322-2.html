                                        I can still see them.

                                        About that Nezumi:
                                        He died when we fought a powerful bane (dont remember exactly what it was called) and tried to sneak up on it from behind, none of us remembering that the thing was supposed to know and see anything in his domain so there was no surprise when he attacked which was his demise.
                                        He actually was a storyteller character but a member of the Sentai nonetheless. He was our Leave, a young, ugly (appearance 1) guy really upholding the low warfare like Nezumi often do, shaking his head at the Same Bito Fist's concept of honorable combat . A showoff at times (no, I wont just jump down there, I'll do the cool thing with a somersault and whatnot and hope I don't botch the roll) and kind of a sneaky bastard (think ninja guy minus the all black dress and sword. He used sai instead) but also always good for a funny and snide comment. Often in little squabbles with our Tengu Mirror who used to throw random stuff at him in such situations after particularly well placed comments.
                                        He also didn't hold human live in high regard ("they're only human after all...") and was by far the most prone to killing people without much thought. An attitude that sometimes would earn him conflict/discussions from the rest of the Sentai and something like a shrug from my character (the Ananasi). For example the Kitsune once found him after following him into a criminal-owned ship he was sent to scout. He was in rodens form, snuck right into a drug lab on the lower decks, happily adding poison into their mix.

                                        EDIT: His Hengeyokai name was "Death of the thousand eyes" after he managed to kill a large spider spirit (orb weaver? I always mix up the names of the various spider spirits) when we were trying to rescue another Sentai that had fallen prey to spider spirits who were trying to block the path to an old totem's realm with webs.
