    Relics of the Past: Mokole Remnants, Part One

    11-03-2015, 07:24 AM
    Once again, I'm putting a new spin on ancient lore, lost histories, and the fallen breeds! Hope you enjoy




    Memory isn't enough, because even our great memories can be tainted by the sheer size of the abyss of ages. Remembering something is perfectly fine, and often the only tie we have to the past, but to KNOW, to hold proof in your hands that something happened is a form of knowledge that transcends even Mnesis. - Solstice Dawn, Crowning Sun Mokole of the Mokolé-mbembe Stream

    The world is not has it was: Every Shifter knows this, can FEEL it in their hearts, they KNOW the world is unbalanced, and it gets worse every year. They KNOW humans despoil the land without really knowing what they're doing as the Triat uses them as pawns in the grand game for the future of the Cosmos, they KNOW that the changes get more dramatic every year.

    But they are wrong, at least on some of these.

    Sure, the world is changing, and the changes are drastic indeed...But the world has gone through FAR greater changes, the continents move slower then a snail, rivers and seas dry up, and begin anew elsewhere, the poles freeze and thaw and freeze again. So it has been since the most ancient of times, before man was little more then a clever little furry creature cavorting in the trees of Africa. But, most people don't see these changes, oh sure, the evidence is there if you care to look, but nobody bothers with it. If it doesn't help with the here, the now, then why bother with it? Only so many hours in the day after all.

    But for those who care to look, the wonders of history are available. And human archaeologists have only scratched the surface of the past, when was the last time a wild-haired Greek spoke of the City that Caine built? When was the last time they spoke of the Changing Breeds as anything more then a wild creature in France who scared the daylights out of farmers during the Middle Ages? When was the last time the Reed of Dejuty was mentioned by living men?

    The Changing Breeds remember more, and the Mokole remember even more still. But there are some things that have escaped the memories of the Mokole.

    Some of these where even by design.

    The greatest change that the Mokole recall is called the Wonder Work, what humans call the KT Extinction. And the Mokole tell of how they lost the Drachid Designer Form, their populations fell, the world grew very cold, and next thing they knew they where up to their ass in fuzzy mammals running around and turning into all kinds of different things. But for the Children of Helios who knew where to look, they could see their patron warning them in the dance of Solar Flares: Something was coming.

    They heard the voice of Helios, and they began to build. Some say this is the reason the Mokole cannot access the Drachid form, they burned out all the creative potential and utility of the Designer Form in these great Works. Massive walls of a brassy-gold metal where created, made with all the scientific know-how of an ancient Race that was old when the most distant ancestors of Mammals where new. These Mokole made great spheres of Orichalcum, and raised them in the most geologically stable regions on earth where they hoped beyond hope that they where wrong about the upcoming Wonder Work and that even if they weren't wrong, that these edifices would survive through eternity to carry the secrets of the Dragon Kings into the most distant future.

    Around a thousand of these structures where made. All loaded to the gills with relics, artifacts, wonders and treasure troves of history, some in the Umbra, others in the physical world in all manner of places. They where infused with the greatest magics of the Dragon Kings, made with their greatest sciences, designed by the brightest, forged by the strongest, sealed by the most clever.

    Six exist today. Three in the Umbra, three in the Physical World.

    Over the course of 65 million years, the Remnants where, by and large, lost. Some simply sank under the earth, plunging into the mantle and being burned away by the blood of Gaia. Others where devoured by the Wyrm in its thrashing, the Dissolver having no idea the importance of those golden crusts. Still others where crushed between continents, blown to smithereens by volcanic activity, or just annihilated by the meteor that caused the KT extinction event. Though the Orichalcum was stronger then any metal forged before or since, it simply wasn't capable of withstanding the test of time save for in limited cases.

    Reaching the Remnants

    One of the Remnants is detailed below (The Hudson Bay Remnant), but five others, perhaps more or less or none depending on your chronicles needs also exist. Physical Remnants are possible to find...If you know where to look, have the means of reaching it, or have any idea such a thing exists. Remnants in the Umbra require the use of the Mokole Gift "Stop Continental Drift" to find and access. Otherwise, they exist out of sync with the modern Umbra, and are effectively invisible and undetectable, "Stop Continental Drift" however, syncs the user to the world-that-was and therefore permits them to see and travel to the Umbral Remnants...Again, if they know where to look. But a lack of detectability doesn't mean a lack of vulnerability, the turning of ages and the changes the Umbra has gone in the 65 million years since the Remnants where made did quite a number on the Remnants, to say nothing of when entire portions of the ancient Umbra where devoured by the Wyrm or sealed away forever more by the Weaver...or more rarely, reshaped completely by the Wyld. If time is a vast ocean of entropy that eventually swallows everything in its depths, then the power of the warring Triat is a great Typhoon surging across its surface, destroying everything in its path.

    Opening the Remnants

    The designers of the Mokole Remnants had quite the quandary, the Remnants had to be COMPLETELY impossible to destroy, but at the same time, an object that was utterly impregnable would do nothing to serve its purpose of providing access to the treasure troves within to future Mokole generations. As a result, no two Remnants where designed the exact same way, different engineering methods where used, and different locations where used, Remnants took all shapes and sizes, though the most common design was the sphere due to the shapes natural strength.

    Considering their options, the most common method of opening a Mokole Remnant is to shed the blood of a Crowning Sun Mokole on a specific portion of the Remnant. The coordinates on the exterior required for this differed, and if found, the response of the average Crowning Sun is likely to be something along the lines of "Get me a healer and a paint roller". That said, Remnants requiring a specific melody hidden in the depths of Mnesis where also created, along with those requiring that an Innocent be destroyed during a specific astrological event. The specific methods required are less important then how difficult they are to puzzle out, Storytellers are encouraged to be creative, and maybe even a little anal about the requirements, after all if these things where EASY to find and crack open, they would of been found ages ago.


    The Second Sun in the Polar North: The Hudson Bay Remnant

    The Canadian Shield is an ancient slab of rock and stone that surrounds Hudson Bay in the Northeastern portion of Canada, it stretches around the entire bay, and reaches as far south as the tops of the Great Lakes. Essentially, its a giant horseshoe made of Pre-Cambrian rock that stretches across Canada. And locked away inside the depths of this ancient stone, is one of the surviving Remnants. Unknown to the Pure Ones, for it was swallowed by the earth before their most distant ancestors crossed the Bering Land Bridge, left Africa, or even came down from the trees.

    As a result of the vast amount of time it took for the Remnant to be swallowed by the earth, and the lack of human activity for the vast majority of that time...The Remnant is essentially flush with its surroundings. There's no golden spikes popping up out of the ground, and there's no signs of disturbances on the surface, its as much part of the land as a deposit of iron ore, or a cavern.

    Of course, while the Pure Ones might not of noticed anything unusual about the area, the Pure Ones didn't have Ground Penetrating Radar, sonar, or the drilling technology that made the Kola Superdeep Borehole possible.

    The most likely way the Hudson Bay Remnant is going to be discovered is simply the result of prospectors using GPR to try to discover ore veins, oil, or other valuable treasures tucked away deep below the surface of the earth. With the discovery of Diamonds in the Yellowknife area in 1991, its possible prospectors might spot something...Unusual underground, curious as to what the hell is down there, they might dig, and if they dig down about three kilometers (Which is a pretty goddamn big 'if') they might get a glimpse of golden metal deep beneath the earth.

    Its equally plausible that the prospectors are working for Endron Oil, a Pentex Subsidiary after the next big find and the next massive oil disaster to taint the pristine Canadian wilderness. There's also the possibility that Earth Elementals might come across the thing, and start getting curious about what's inside that golden egg when they realize they can't get into it. Perhaps they tell the local Garou or another member of the Changing Breeds about it. And while the bitter cold of the Hudson Bay area isn't exactly prime territory for the Mokole, they might just hear about this and decide its worth a look despite the arctic winds coming off the bay...



    And thus Chapter One ends, what follows in Part Two will be a detailed adventure for Werewolf the Apocalypse fans! NPCs, plot hooks, and other details about the Remnant will follow, and there will be enough listed down for any ST to make an adventure out of the Hudson Bay Remnant. I'd also like to take a moment to state that while I'm not a Geologist, I tried to be has accurate as possible in regards to the history and geology listed here. There really is a region of super-old Rock in the area around Hudson Bay, there really IS a giant hole the Soviets dug called the Kola Superdeep Borehole, some of the oldest rocks in the world where found in that region around Hudson Bay, and there really IS such a thing as the Magical Land of Canada, despite what you might hear on certain AM radio stations!

      I like the basic concept here. I think the casual writing style and some specific details would benefit from some polish, though. Just a few specific suggestions:
      • Remnant is a dull-sounding name. What about Cache? Or Golden Egg?
      • How did a sphere from the Mesozoic sink into precambrian rocks? Did you intend to leave it a mystery?
      • Innocents supposedly didn't exist before the Wars of Rage, so why would a sphere be designed to open by killing one? If that's a deliberate change to Mokole history, I think it's a good idea to make it explicit.
      • I think it would be better to say that Stop Continental Drift is A way to find the umbral spheres, but not necessarily the only way. Though I suppose a ST that wanted one found by non-Mokole could easily change that as a house-rule...
      • Scientists say that an asteroid caused the WonderWork... but I feel like explicitly confirming that takes out some of the magic and mystery of the event. In the WoD it could have been other things, like nuclear war or a magical disaster or the Beast of War entering the physical world or the world's biggest paradox backlash...
      • Do any Mokole remember that these spheres were created? Or is it a complete surprise to them? If they do remember, do any of them have any clue where? I think these are significant questions to answer (or explicitly leave up to STs) because of how much it would impact a Mokole game using these.


      She/Her. I am literal-minded and write literally. If I don't say something explicitly, please never assume I implied it. The only exception is if I try to make a joke.
      My point of view may be different from yours but is equally valid.

          Well, in order...

          * I named it Remnant for a couple of reasons. 1. I'd been watching a lot of RWBY, and 2. Its the last remnants of the Dragon King's civilization. I can tweak it if the general consensus is that doesn't sound right, though. Its just a name.
          * Hrm, I might of screwed up some of the Geology there. The intent was that as the plates moved, the slow shifting of stone, gravity, and erosion eventually had it swallowed by the stone as it sank into the rock. Like an impossibly slow version of immersing something into water. As mentioned, I'm not a geologist, I'm just doing the best I can to be accurate with internet research.
          * I thought Innocents had exist as long has Mokole have? I wouldn't imagine they'd of not been breeding with each other during the Age of Kings, I mean yes, there where more reptiles to choose from back then, but there's seven billion humans on earth now and there's still Metis all over the place.
          * Part of that is that gift generally never really seemed to really do much. Okay yes, being able to cross the Atlantic in a few hours IS awesome in a more ancient chronicle...But why can't you just take a moon bridge or hop a plane? By letting it permit the Mokole to access ancient secrets, I figured that gift might get some more mileage. Also, who knows what else you might find in regions that are now underwater or have since dived beneath the mantle?
          * I thought it was generally confirmed that a Meteor is what did the KT extinction, if its intended to be more vague in Werewolf canon, can you give me a source on that? Not to demean your opinion or anything, but so I can verify the OTHER options.
          * Some might, but mnesis back that far is generally unpredictable and problematic. It IS possible that a Mokole might have a memory of a remnant, but the specifics of how the players find the thing should be up to the ST. Maybe they remember it, maybe their Mokole found a OOPart that displays a bunch of lizards burying an egg, maybe their Kinfolk where planting a garden and heard a thunk when they hit it with their shovel.

          The general theme I was going for here was something out of Conan the Barbarian, with a little Lovecraft and Pulp feel. The sort of stories your grandfather might of read by flashlight under his blanket after bedtime, or the sort of tales that send a shiver up a mans spine when they think about what sort of wild, untamed, legendary place the world of the past is.
