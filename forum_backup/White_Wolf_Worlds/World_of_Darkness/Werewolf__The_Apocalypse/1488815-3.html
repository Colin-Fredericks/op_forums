                                Originally posted by Nyremne View Post
                                That's pretty insulting for anyone who worked on previous editions of these games. Basically previous works don't matter, all that does is the current team's ideas.
                                As I've written before on other threads, it is probably best to stop seeing the game line's various editions from inception to now as one continuity. It's better to view them as distinct takes of the same properties much like the old style "Earth-One", "Earth-Two", etc. of DC Comics. Yeah, they each had a Flash, Green Lantern, Wonder Woman, Superman, Bat-Man, etc., but they weren't the "same" Flash, Green Lantern, etc.

                                You have the 1e/2e version which spawned all the others, the Revised version, the 20th Anniversary versions, and now the 5e version (some games could arguably also have a late 2e version as well that walked back or smoothed over some of the more gonzo or rough edges of the earlier).

                                Pick the version you like back, and stay with that, modified however you like.

                                Unlike other media, RPG settings only exist for the GM and her players to do what they like. They are not inhibited by other people controlling that setting's portrayal like normal media. All that matters is what happens at your game table. Yeah, we'd all like it if the published setting remained true to the original vision that won so many fans. It makes it easier to play with more people, and gives you more direct ideas to use for your game. But that's not how the metaplot wheel works.

                                Metaplot exists to invalidate your current gamebooks so you feel like you need to buy even more gamebooks to "keep up". That's much easier than actually trying to produce good sourcebooks that people want to use. But once you get comfortable that you actually don't need those other game books, it will save you a lot of money and headache.

                                            You can also deal with these things in supplements if you don't want to give them too much focus in the core book if you feel it would be a distraction. The issue is if you take away a canon NPC's big "shtick."                                             That said, I do think it's appropriate at this point to consider WoD5 a new thing that's not the oWoD books, or the CofD. It's not just an editorial decision to change the direction of the game. It's a holistic decision to change the character of the game.
