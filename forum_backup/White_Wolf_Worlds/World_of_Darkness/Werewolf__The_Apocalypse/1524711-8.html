Anarchs Unbound was the first time I felt anyone had really done the Anarchs justice. BJD is my all time favorite supplement period. I was actually hoping that the Apocalyptic Record was this.

                                                        What was the problems with SD and Changing Ways?
                                                        Heh I'm not surprised in you geeking out over Anarch's unbound. I've been doing a deep dive WIP on Apocalyptic Record and sadly it's not a BJD. It's too disconnected and too shallow to work out like that did. There are brilliant sections but it's not really clicking as a whole compared to the BJD.

                                                        Shattered Dreams was a book that was supposed to explore the history through the lens of the shifters. The Dragon Kings, Wars of Rage and Shame, The Impergium and similar things. It was really well put together and really good at setting the tone and opening up multiple ways how things could play out and what led us where we are. However, the whole book was pronounced as not canon by Karim personnally with giant sidebars and a very ugly text box on the back cover. WoD has always been weird and messy, but this is the only book ever published that came out with multiple disclaimers by people in charge disawoving anything in it.

                                                        Changing Ways on the other hand was supposed to be something of a Garou culture/Player Handbook book dealing with how Garou deal with modern life. There are come rather uncomfortable bits in the book regarding transphobia, bioexistentialism, reductive thoughts on Society that were written in. This caused quite a bit of a stir when people found out about the Trans garou that were being murdered or were mutilating themselves with silver. Then two authors spoke up about those sections and said that this was not in the original draft and was added by Paradox in the final edit passes.

                                                        Let's just say that these two incidents may have made the Werewolf fanbase overly cautious about anything coming from Paradoxes kitchen.
