                                    I've said it before and I'll say it again - W20 presents two really good candidate tribes who are canonically near the edge of a serious fall: The Red Talons and Silver Fangs. For pretty much every other tribe there is an extremist faction who have been destroyed (Cyberdogs, the few Get who went Nazi) or pushed into the sidelines to bitterly stew about stuff (Mother's Fundamentalists, the CoG who want to kill all animals to create a permanent peace). With the Red Talons and Silver Fangs, you could argue that the bad part of the Tribe just *is* the Tribe, with the more helpful aspect instead being in the minority; for instance the silver fangs who push for reform.

                                    If I'd written W5 and I'd been told "we need a hook", then a significant portion of Red Talons starting to fight the rest of the Garou Nation for 'betraying wolves', having made a bargain with a powerful wolf spirit to help the species survive at any cost, would be up there. Alongside this there would be greater emphasis on the crisis of identity within the Silver Fangs and how they are growing increasingly alienated from the rest of the nation, with more falling to Harano, joining the BSDs or getting killed in duels or assassinations with each passing year.

                                    And even then you'd have the option to play a heroic Silver Fang or Red Talon who understands what the Tribe is really about, what it was meant to do for Gaia, and has hope that they can salvage something worthwhile out of the chaos by rallying a few good Garou they find along the path.

                                                        Knightingale you're touching on something I really resonate with - changes like falling happen to individuals and groups. A Tribe can fall, yes, but it isn't 100% of members. Anything that claims 100% of anything was converted, wiped out etc. is likely misleading or poorly thought out.

                                                        A crisis across all Garou is far more interesting. Have each tribe be in a moment of crisis where they have a villainous movement (if you want to make it easy to explain and market) who are pushing for some really awful response to the crisis, and a reconciliatory movement who emphasize the best quality of the Tribe. Each Tribe is in a state where it is in conflict between its best and worst aspects.

                                                        This is, in truth, just Camps. Intra-tribe conflicts are the reason "Nazi Get" and "Kill Literally All Conscious Beings CoG" and "Turn Wolves into Cyborgs Glass Walkers" and "Murder Rival Kinfolk and Restart Impergium Fianna" existed. A Chronicle based around a specific Tribe needed a villain! But then dumb memes based on a game of telephone involving maybe 1 person who has actually played WTA happen, and you wind up with people thinking "the Get are Nazis" or whatever. Of all the WoD games, Werewolf seems to be the most willfully and consistently misunderstood for some reason.

                                                        Anyways all this to say that if your new lore tells me that 100% of Get of Fenris members have fallen into a certain state, and you don't show me renegades who are still staying true or have fled to other tribes etc. then you're selling me a setting which doesn't pass the "okay but let's think about this for 2 seconds" test. So hopefully W5 will give it more thought than that - we'll have to wait and see!
