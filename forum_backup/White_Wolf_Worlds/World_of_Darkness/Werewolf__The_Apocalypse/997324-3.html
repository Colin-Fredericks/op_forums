    Originally posted by AnubisXy View Post

    To be fair, in game, Gaia never intended there werewolves (or any of the Fera) to be "good" or "evil." Werewolves were created with specific duties that were deemed necessary for the good of Gaia (I mean, let's not forget that Gaia created an entire race, the Ratkin, specifically to spread disease and plague among humans). Early werewolves took "honor" from performing their duties well and fulfilling the role given to them by Gaia. This meant things like culling humans, helping to keep their population at a reasonable number and ensuring that they didn't begin using "weaver tricks" in ways that would harm Gaia.

    Ideas like 'good', 'evil', 'moral' or 'immoral' actions played no part in the way in which Gaia originally envisioned the Garou. Of course, as the Wyrm fell into corruption and its role in the world changed from Destroyer to Corrupter, the werewolves found themselves forced into the role of challenging the Wyrm. And as the werewolves changed in response to their enemy, human ideas of "good" and "evil" began to become more prominent. But these ideas, that some actions were morally wrong even if that's what Gaia wanted, ended up leading to the end of the Impergium, later to the War of Rage and now the Apocalypse.


    Gaia's intentions are confusing. Ratkin only really come into their own with the rise of civilization which they predate by millenia. Kitsune exist to guide and cull societies so societies seem to be important on some level.

    And even if Gaia wanted the Imperigum it seems to have been a driving force of humanity even further from her than an effective culling and controlling situation.
