        ^which is why I believe mages to be natural wyld factories(think about it, many groups of hundreds of near omnipotent being randomly creating, destroying, splitting into different factions and warring with each other for momentary wants and vastly different views over an unexplained phenomenon they refer to as ascension.(sure the technocracy and nephandi are a bit more weaver and wyrmish respectively, but everyone else is squarely in the wyld territory with marauders taking to a whole new level.)

        I personally like to look at cWOD from a larger perspective, prefering not to rely on any one book as they are seen from the perspective of one group and their limited view of things(take the fae from changeling or demons from demons the fallen for example. they are squarely convinced that all supernaturals are somehow decedent from them.)

        And as far as I can gather, the wyld as a whole isn't so much dying or weakened as changing. The natural aspect of the wyld are dying out in favor of more modern or supernatural aspects just as old species die out in the process of evolution. The fight of the werewolves isn't so much the slow destruction of the entire world as the destruction of Their world, as old traditions that have held for thousands of years before them are wiped away in favor of a new status quo that isn't tolerant of their presence any longer. The same thing happened to the ancient Mokole in the time of the dinosaur kings, as their world was wiped away in favor of our complex, evolving world.
