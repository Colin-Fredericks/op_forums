    I can't imagine such an approach because Justin Achilli has this strange perspective on "rules-light" where he thinks the point of that is to have as few systems as possible to give as much room as possible to "uninfluenced play". Like, his explanation for "sober play" in H5 included the argument that negative modifiers due to low humanity in V5, for example, are limiting your roleplaying-choices and therefore it's bad for the game. With H5 that already led to some baffling design-choices, so who knows how that will end up looking with W5 when there's so much more that mechanically should be going on. Additionally, there's also the release-date of next year and considering they don't even have a first draft at this point...Man, that team's under a lot of pressure. Or... the same way Renegade announced the delay of the V5 Players Guide to Q2 2023, this time next year we've found out that W5 has been delayed to Q2 2024.

        Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
        Something about this that I continue to find weird: WoD5 keeps borrowing from the wrong CofD games; not even getting into burrowing from the flourishing storygames movement or genre emulation focused PbtA and associated engines.

        If feels like V5, H5, and now W5 are all only looking at their equivalent CofD books.

        I can't help but think about things like:

        What if V5 looked at Beast as a game that has a huge narrative and mechanical focus on hunger and monstrosity. Ignoring all the other Beast-baggage, the Satiety and Feeding mechanics in the game are great for a vampire game,and also really hammer home that there is no &quot;good&quot; way to feed, just different risks with different consequences.

        What if H5 looked at Deviant for was to make big orgs relevant to the PCs while providing more of a reason for the PCs to avoid joining them? Also the way Deviant tries PC power level to antagonist power level feels like something great for working Danger into more holistically if Edges are going to be a more randomized element than set powers.

        What if W5 looked at Changeling: the Lost as a game about survivors picking up the pieces and trying to forge new lives, new families, and new meaning after powers beyond them uprooted everything that mattered to them and changed them into something different.

        There's this giant tool box full of tools they already bought, but refuse to even consider that some new tools they're not used to using for a given project might be better than the ones they always used for that project.
        All solid ideas. I remember the thread on here where a few posters did develop a system of Hunger Conditions for VTR2e, based on the Beast system. It was a great rules hack that could've been ported over to VTM too (indeed, that's what I did, using Hunger Levels like Health Levels, instead).

                    I'm not sure Bill or Ethan would have said yes anyway. Both have been apart from all this for awhile. Neither of them have worked on any WW based IP since Paradox bought the games.

                    Paradox has also been fairly adamant about keeping to the idea that their main WoD team will be very active in developing core books so that the new core games suit their needs for other projects, before passing off supplements to other parties; including first Modiphius and now Renegade, who basically end up with first dibs on books due to the new arrangement. There's a strong fan impression that most of the regular OPP freelances aren't really interested in WoD now. The big appeal of more popular games is steady work so they can pay bills, and Paradox is making it hard for them to get a lot of work out the WoD (or CofD for that matter).
