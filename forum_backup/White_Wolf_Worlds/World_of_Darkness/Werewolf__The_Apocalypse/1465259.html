    Ratkin and breeding through the ages

    10-25-2021, 06:11 AM
    Hello,

    I'd like to ask for your oppinion about the breeding of Ratkin with rats, more correctly rattus genus.

    There are two distict types of rats currently in Europe & America, who live together with humans & a viable Kinfolk of the Ratkin:
    1) Rattus rattus, the "black"; "ship";... rat. This species were responsible for the Black Death and came earlier to Europe than the...
    ") Rattus norvegicus, the "brown"; "wharf"; "sever" rat. They are better adapted to the environment than the 1) and could take over much of the habitat of them. Intrestingly they are not a vector of bubonic plague, what prevented bigger epidemics.

    The two species can't breed, BUT Ratkins had Kinfolk in both parties.
    Does that mean, that the wererats can cross the boundary of species and have litters with both? (Red Talons did such feats with dingos and cape hunting dogs.)

                  Thylacines aren't dingos, and they're already extinct, they were the preferred Bunyip stock.                           Lots of things don't make sense when it comes to the animal breeds. It's really a part of the game where it's just best for STs to do whatever makes the most sense for them.

                          I made an early decision in the chronicles I run to just make the Bunyip their own Thylacine-based Fera breed. They are considered to be "honorary" Garou because the tribes that wiped them out in Australia felt guilty about it and passed the decision at a Concolation in Jindabyne in the hopes to assuage their spirits in chiminage (it failed, the Bunyip spirits are still angry). Slowly that resolution was passed along subsequent Concolations elsewhere so that it's been recognized by most of the Garou Nation at this point. To make it official, there'd need to be an Imperial Moot but those haven't been held since 1855.

                          I also never liked the idea of Garou including dingos and painted dogs and other nonsense so I simply don't allow it. Mamu and his Red Talon pack do look like dingoes, but that's because of a fetish they have, not from interbreeding with them. In fact, many Australian Garou use the same fetish to protect the Veil.

                          Pure Breed Silent Striders are jackals though, but the less Pure Breed they have the more they look like wolves because of interbreeding. The Garou are more like a "were-canid" group, but there still needs to be significant ability to interbreed with each other in the wild.

                          I always thought Ratkin was too much of a fantasy concept than a gothic horror one supposedly based on real world legends. So I eliminated them from my chronicles. But I do have fomori based on wererats.

                                                  Things I can't stress enough

                                                  Dingoes are genetically super-compatible with wolves and dogs. They're the same thing but like... blond. If the Garou were to ever have a second type of canine alongside the grey wolf, it'd be Dingoes.

                                                  Foxes are pretty far off wolves and dogs.
                                                  Kitsune only exist for one fox breed, the red fox (silver/black foxes are just Red foxes with different pigments) You don't see kitsune artic foxes or desert foxes. Kitsune only happen in Asia because... Americans fetishize Asia too much.
