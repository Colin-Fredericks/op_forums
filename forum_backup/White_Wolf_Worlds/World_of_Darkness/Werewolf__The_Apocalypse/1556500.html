    Minotaur as an enemy faction in Werewolf

    12-12-2025, 12:44 AM
    As people know, Minotaur is the Incarna patron of the Skin Dancers. Although the Skin Dancers were introduced earlier, I believe the first mention of Minotaur himself is from 1995’s Outcasts sourcebook where he is given the epitaph That-Which-Howls-In-The-Forests. He’s since been developed a little bit more in Players Guide to Garou and some of the W20 material.

    I think Minotaur has potential to become something more than just the patron of the Skin Dancers. Note that when I describe this as a faction, I don’t mean the Minotaur Incarna is leading or controlling some kind of conspiracy or army. Just that Minotaur can be the source of additional antagonists than just the Skin Dancers, and that these different groups may or may not cooperate with each other locally.

    I’ll be using this thread to explore some ideas I’ve been having. More to come.

              Before I get to the actual entities involved in this faction, I want to address the concept of the Minotaur and its possible ties to the actual Greek legend of the minotaur.

              “Minotaur” as a concept is something that crops up in other places in the World of Darkness. There's both Werewolf and Vampire mentions (and I believe Mage as well). The first Children of Gaia tribebook mentions Crete and seeing the last of the noble Moon-bulls/Minotaurs. It also states that once Minoan Crete was a Gaian centered civilization, but later became corrupt and needed to be destroyed. Later editions of Werewolf introduced the concept of the Last Apis reappearing in Crete that went berserk and became part of the minotaur legend.

              We also have repeated mentions of Minoan Crete in Vampire. The revised Toreador Clanbook said the Minotaur (Asterion) is a childe of the clan founder who entered wassail and was imprisoned in the labyrinth. Interestingly, while that sourcebook said that Toreador refused to embrace Minos, Asterion’s father, we actually know that there was a fourth generation Toreador named Minos who embraced Helena. So is that Minos not THE Minos? Or do we just accept that the in character knowledge we're provided is often incomplete or wrong. I am leaning towards the latter. For our purposes, we just know that the Toreador clan founder was active on Crete - probably fairly early or in the middle of Minoan Civilization (possibly around 2000 BC).

              We also know that at some point the Baali arrived in Minoan Crete from The Dark Ages Companion for Vampire: the Dark Ages. Although no mention of a minotaur is made there, they do make references to using a labyrinth beneath Knossos, that inhuman things dwelled there, and that they used various occult powers during this time. I assume that at some point the Toreador antediluvian left, and the Baali creeped in afterwards. We are also told that it was mortal heroes killing deformed Baali ghouls that was the basis of the Theseus legend. When other vampires learned of the Baali activities there, the Setites worked magic to create the Thera/Santorini volcanic eruption (which we know was around 1600 BC) which caused the Knossos palace to collapse, the Baali labyrinth to collapse, and fires to erupt which incinerated most of the Baali.

              We can easily tie these things together. Very early Minoan Crete (before the arrival of Toreador) was Gaian centered, perhaps because of the presence of earlier Apis and continued survival of people descended from Apis kinfolk (regardless of whether they counted as actual kinfolk by that point). That began to chance when Toreador arrived, but definitely changed when the Baali arrived and introduced their dark magics and worship of evil things.

              Maybe the thing that drove the Last Apis mad was seeing the descendants of the Apis kinfolk being manipulated and embraced by vampires. Perhaps the spirit of “the beast in man” was somehow involved in his degeneration into a mindless beast. References to the labyrinth can mean there was some kind of connection to the labyrinth the Garou know best - the Black Spiral found in Malfeas. The Minotaur Incarna need only be one spirit that the Baali managed to successfully contact and perhaps sacrifice to. The exact nature of this relationship can be left to the ST. Maybe this was the place and time when the spirit became partially corrupted. Or it was first formed as a result of the activity here, or perhaps ascended from mere jaggling to its own Incarna status.

              The minotaur of legend might have been the Last Apis, a deformed ghoul, a 4th generation vampire in Wassail, some combination (a ghouled Last Apis or even Abomination?), or perhaps multiple stories involving different creatures which we're eventually combined into one legend. STs can determine whatever they like best. I actually think this is the least important thing to consider, but some STs will no doubt have strong opinions.

              But does this actually provide us anything? Or is this just archaeology and pointless connecting different canons together? I do think a high level perspective does offer some benefits. YMMV.

              I wouldn’t want to tie current vampires into the Minotaur Incarna, and I certainly don’t want him directly involved with the Baali. But I have no problem with the idea that he could have been a spirit some Baali were once in contact with, and thus was an important spirit the corrupted Minoans were in contact with.

              References to the “fall” or corruption of Minoan Crete has been around in the game since the first Children of Gaia sourcebook. So tying Minotaur Incarna in some way to the fall of an originally Gaian Great Goddess religion/civilaztion practiced in Minoan Crete (perhaps under the original guidance of the Apis) into a corrupted civilization that had to be destroyed would provide some real heritage and meance to the spirit. It also provides some good plot hooks for other things in later eras.

                                                  I'm excited that there's already so many comments and excellent ideas.

                                                  A good first step is to consider what other spirits may make up Minotaur's brood. The W20 corebook suggests Minotaur’s brood consists of some corrupted spirits, some Banes, and spirits not associated with Gaia. As others have said, Minotaur is the Incarna of the beast within man, or when man that becomes the beast. He craves animalistic power. He respects strength and has contempt for the weak. He is touched by the taint of the Beast of War, but not wholly corrupted. So were looking at spirits that fit that theme.

                                                  Players Guide to Garou says, “the Skin Dancers have sought and, in fact, found spirit servants of forgotten or lost totems such as Minotaur.” That definitely leans more into Minotaur not being a Bane simply a spirit that may have once been worshipped by Garou or the Changing Breeds, but was somehow abandoned. Maybe originally it was some kind of war spirit of the Apis that was lost after the War of Rage, and then subsequently tainted because of Baali blasphemies on Crete that evoked the Beast-of-War.

                                                  It is not explained why Minotaur hates and envies Pegasus. However, I hold it is because he views the sacred feminine as weak and contemptible. He doesn’t understand its obvious power. Another possibility is that the Black Furies performed some act either during the War of Rage or against Wyrm corrupted Minoan Crete that purposefully or inadverently violated something sacred to this spirit, and this caused the Minotaur spirit to bear a grudge against Pegasus. Other explanations are possible, and I’d love to hear them.

                                                  I would include corrupted Naturae that have treacherous or ferocious natures (like scorpions, snakes, wolverines, crocodiles, boars, hyenas, certain raptors, big cats, chimpanzees, and others), feral animals (feral pigs, Lost Dogs, Feral Psychagonea and Feral Psychomachiae - I took most of these from Rat's brood in Axis Mundi), some epiphlings (spirits of hate, pain, anger, darkness), corrupted Lunes, spirits of diseases that cause behaviourly aggression (like rabies), and some elementals. This could be a very motley brood, composed of a ragtag and small group of individual jagglings and gafflings that for whatever reason were cut off from their normal allegiances, and came under Minotaur simply for protection (because he’s somewhat of a bully).

                                                  These spirits can teach Gifts to Skin Dancers who take Minotaur as a pack totem, but have also sometimes taught Gifts to Ronin as well.

                                                  For those who want to distinguish between Skin Dancers who are aligned with the Wyrm, and those who attempt to walk a more Gaian pact, then the Banes who teach Gifts may not explicitly be part of Minotaur’s brood, but something else. The Outcasts sourcebook talks about the Puppeteer banes (from Valkenburg Foundation) as being spirit allies because of their past association with Sam Haight. A paragraph later when talking about “connections have been made with powers answering to Minotaur,” it’s clearly about those Skin Dancers not attempting to destroy Garou society. It’s just an “unassociated spirit” they’ve made contact with.

                                                  It’s possible to join up these two different spirit groups if you want a Minotaur more in thrall to the Wyrm, or if you want a clear separation, align the less hostile Skin Dancers with Minotaur and align the more hostile Skin Dancers with their own Bane allies. It’s also possible that Skin Dancers may not be able to distinguish between the two groups.

                                                          Originally posted by Black Fox View Post
                                                          Minotaur is the Incarna of the beast within man, or when man that becomes the beast. He craves animalistic power. He respects strength and has contempt for the weak. He is touched by the taint of the Beast of War, but not wholly corrupted. So were looking at spirits that fit that theme.
                                                          It might be interesting to give him an aspect of (d)evolution: survival of the fittest, but one that does not rely on natural selection. He accepts the Skindancer's way to gain more power through unnatural means and himself represents a model of how more primal essence (beast) can empower more evolved essence (man). The idea of representing a natural process enforced by unnatural means would fit his particular brand of taint, imo.

                                                          This could lead to his brood having a tendency for rapid adaptation through primal mechanisms, such as disease spirits of pathogens with high mutability rate, or even something related to gene spliced organisms. Parasites would be another good fit. A theme to tie him into a more modern playing field could be "reactivation of ancient junk DNA" that plays on Island of Dr Moreau stuff.
