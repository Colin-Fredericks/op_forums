                                                There's no real mechanical advantage to one over the other. My experience is that biting does - on average - do more damage because of the lower difficulty (you're more likely to hit, AND get more successes to add to your damage roll) but it's not enough of a mechanical advantage that clawing is "useless."

                                                As for getting "wyrm grossness in you" - I greatly contest that notion. Sure there's a temptation to see every Fomori as a walking barrel of toxic waste hidden inside a leper... but that's really not how it works. Oh sure they might have toxic or damaging powers... but most won't. And corruption is a spiritual malady, not a physical one. Physical contact, even ingestion - with wyrm-critters is not going to send you to hell. If it were, the Garou would either all be Spirals, or would be so busy performing rites of cleansing that they couldn't get anything else done. if you sink your teeth into a fomori, you're not going ot get a spray of instant wyrm corruption to the face. You get blood, flesh, and bone, and nothing else. Granted it's human flesh and bone, so if you gulp some down, that's a problem...
