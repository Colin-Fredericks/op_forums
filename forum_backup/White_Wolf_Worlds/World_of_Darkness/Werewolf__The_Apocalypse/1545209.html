    Travelling to the Legendary realm

    11-02-2024, 07:50 AM
    I've read several books mentioning this realm and found out several descriptions quite different between the 3rd ed and W20. What I'm especially interested about is, how to get there, as nothing is mentioned in the realm rules. The third edition of the Umbra book hints that someone wanted to get there need to have a thorough knowledge of his ancestors life. I interpret this as "the life of the ancestor you want to visit the legend of" with a big bonus if it's your own ancestor and you have the background Ancestor/Past lives. Either that or you go petition the silver fang to get there.

    Umbra Velvet shadow (3rd) : Entering the Legendary Realm depends on the Garou's knowledge of his past lives. Some have been searching for ages for Moon Paths leading to the the Legendary Realm, but never find them. The Silver Fangs know how to reach the Legendary Realm through the Umbra. Their Rites of Passage create Moon Bridges that lead there. These bridges are protected by the Falcon.
    My players have no one with the ancestor background, only pure breed which wouldn't really help in here. They only know an old legend of the garou they want to investigate (Cororuc) but nothing much really. On one side I tend to want to reward their choice of going the hard way by themselves, but on the other side, if you're not supposed to find it this way, well, you're not and there's still things to learn on the journey even though it will taste like defeat. Plus they can then still go and ask to the silver fangs (and since one of the characters really hates them, it's going to be fun).

    Did you already use this realm ? If yes how did you make your players reach it ? How would you make the “travel” to get there if the player decides to take the hard way and go there by themselves ?

      Information about travel to the Near Realms in general, and the Legendary Realm in particular is scattered through a variety of sourcebooks. If you collect them together, you are presented with several options.

      First, we can use the general rules for umbral quests. The Garou begin seeking out correspondences to what it is they seek (in this case the Legendary realm), and they find clues and follow the trail to other clues, slowly making their way through Umbra going deeper and deeper until they arrive at their destination. To identify the decipher the clues, there may be challenges involving the Enigmas ability, the ST describes the scenes with clues she's planted and wait for the PCs to figure them, or perhaps some cooperative ST/PCs storytelling happens. This would probably take the longest time both IC and OOC, but it could be fun.

      Second, very often Moon Paths can lead to one of the Near Realms. In my experience, Moon Paths are rarely utilized as an ST device because there is insufficient detail on them provided by sourcebooks. STs need to figure out a lot on their own. ST could decide on several options. 1) There are known, fairly stable Moon Paths that lead to the Legendary Realm. It's just a matter of learning about them, travelling to the place that has them, and negotiating passage with the local sept. 2) Such a Moon Path may be known, but it appears rarely or changes frequently. Perhaps it only appears at that location based on one of the phases of the moon, certain celestial events, on the anniversaries of important historical events of the Garou, or some other limiting factor. 3) There are no known Moon Paths to the Legendary Realm, but they can be quested for in similar circumstances to the above. Find somewhere in the Penumbra that is associated with the Near Realm you want to reach. Go into the Umbral Mists and travel through the metaphorical steps of your journey, in mist or shadow, until you find a Moon Path that can take you there.

      Third, Silver Fangs are suppose to know Moon Bridges that take them to the Legendary Realm. In multiple 1e/2e sourcebooks it was stated that Silver Fang rites of passage create Moon Bridges leading there because all Silver Fang cubs undergo their rites of passage in the Legendary Realm. That lore has kind of fallen to the wayside, but you can utilize the Silver Fangs as way to get there. They should have strong connections to the place, so maybe it's a story of negotiation, chiminage, and favors (or perhaps stealing their knowledge).

      Fourth, summon some sort of spirit guide to help take you there. Even if no PC has the Ancestors background, they should have knowledge of some of their tribe's ancestors and access to the Rite of Summoning (if not them, then an NPC) and negotiate with the spirit.

      Once you pick your option (or perhaps let the PCs pick their preferred option), then run the umbra as normal to make it fun and interesting for the PCs before they get there. Depending on your preference, this might be an extremely important quest for your players so you want to play up the journey to get their, or you just want to get to the destination. Do whatever is the most fun for your group.

      = = =
      The Legendary Realm essentially replays all the epic legends of the Garou. It's a bit like running a Star Trek Holodeck simulation so people can witness it or participate in it like a roleplay game. You can use it like a Westworld style vacation, a pilgrimage to relive important events, or to meet honored Ancestors. The Legendary Realm does not repeat things as they historically happened. It shows the legends that have been told around it. While most often Garou go there to visit a specific legend, the realm can also create "composite" places that incorporate multiple legends together if the packs or a Garou want to do that.

      In the case of the legend of Cororuc, determine in advance which version of the legend you'll be using, as WWtA has given us something like four or five different versions depending on the sourcebook. It probably involves a lot of danger. Even the Black Spiral Dancers have a place in the Legendary Realms and other Wyrm creatures can be encountered. These definitely will be present in any excursion to the legend of Cororuc.
