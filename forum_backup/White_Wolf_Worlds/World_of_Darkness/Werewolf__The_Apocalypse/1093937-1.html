                                    Originally posted by werewolf43 View Post
                                    I don;t think that they give their childs to other tribes. I think that they just kill them.
                                    Remember though we aren't talking about babies. We are talking about cubs after their First Change (human age 14-21, sometimes even later) because I don't think it's possible to determine the amount of Pure Breeding solely from how they look as a human, and even if they can, there's always the hope that a gangly child will fill out after puberty hits. There is a big difference between killing a cub that people have grown to love and care for (and which can defend itself, even if clumsily), and a newborn infant. Many primitive cultures are fine with infanticide leaving one or both parents to decide if the child should be kept, and don't consider it murder to kill the baby until the family accepts him and gives him a name.

                                    So I think the decision to kill a newborn metis at birth is very different than a decision to kill a 16 year old human (or 3 year old wolf!) who just had his first change, but lacks all the signs of Pure Breed the tribe would want.

                                    Another thing to consider is who is "they" and how can they go about killing the cub legally? Is it a family decision? A sept decision? A policy of the Silver Fang kings? And who actually carries out the killing? Might they have any kind of qualms about it? Is this really what Falcon would want? Would lupus be just as willing to do it as homids?

                                    And what was the crime that justifies the killing? It's one thing to say that the cub has broken the Litany in a major way, or the penalty is death; or that our rite of passage is very severe and brutal and the cub didn't survive. Saying, "Well, his fur coat isn't as shiny as we'd like," so we butchered a perfectly viable Garou cub is a very different argument. There is no crime, and there is no tragic circumstances. It's just outright murder. I think it would be VERY hard for the Silver Fangs to argue that and keep the other tribes in line that they are worthy leaders. The Red Talons would be furious if any lupus breed were killed. The Children of Gaia would be appalled in general. There would at least be some Black Furies who would have wanted the females delivered to them. And there would be varying degrees of unrest among the others. The Silver Fangs would get no benefit from such a policy, but make a lot of enemies.

                                    And what happens should the Garou cub escape? He either lives as a ronin, or possibly gets picked up and raised by another tribe anyway. At which point, there is nothing the Silver Fangs can do about it.

                                    Now I could believe there is some amount of killing occurs, especially by a disappointed and ashamed Garou parent that he couldn't produce a PB3 baby or by some other relative. I could also believe that in certain septs, there isn't any real investigation into the matter, and it would be dropped. But I don't think that would be standard policy.

                                    At least that's the view now. If we are talking about a historical chronicle thousands of years ago, when the Garou did not consider themselves a dying people,and the Silver Fangs were supremely confidant than high Pure Breed was always going to happen, there would be a much higher chance such low PB cubs would get killed. But even then I don't think it would be the standard policy, just something left to the individual SFs to decide at each sept or each family line.

                                        Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post
                                        Sure the Fangs would be able to stomach one of their blood, however dilluted, joining the Shadow Lords?

                                        the Shadow Lords are their enemies...
                                        Not always. The Shadow Lords historically were the closest allies of the Silver Fangs doing all the dirty work for them. That relationship would probably be very healthy for many thousands of years, only slowly changing into antagonism in the past two thousand. And the two tribes very often live in the same protectorates in the Eurasian forests and steppes. So I could definitely see some formal arrangements made between certain septs or within certain protectorates there of such a policy."It's too bad cousin Yuri lacks the right breeding, but I'm sure he'll do fine with the Shadow Lords. He'll give them some backbone."

                                        In fact, one reason why the Shadow Lords may have slowly turned into enemies/friendly opposition to the Silver Fangs is a result of all those ambitious, but resentful Shadow Lords of SF heritage angry that they were cast out, but their inbred relatives who are obvious screw ups were left in the tribe.

                                        I personally see the Shadow Lords as a tribe divided in their loyalties to the Silver Fangs. Some are outright enemies. Others seek to supplant them only in order to provide better leadership to the Nation. Some may want the power and influence, but not the title so to speak. Others just want to prod them into being leaders. And others unsure what to do. I think when you can have a wide range of concepts within a single tribe, rather than one strong stereotype, it's better for the game. It allows PCs and STs more to do.
