                                On the "why not use wolves to mass produce Garou," thing: one of the biggest problems is controlling wild wolves. Unlike human kin who will happily settle where you tell them and stay there to be family (even very large ones at that), wolves generally form fairly small packs given the amount of area one will claim. They need lots and lots of space, and even more as a kin-heavy pack starts spawning new packs (which you need to keep them functioning as wild wolf pack units since they aren't going to be able to support you getting all the females pregnant at once; assuming you even could override biology to do that). If you start caging wolves in small areas to keep them contained, safe, and accessible, you run the risk of turning them into "dogs" spiritually and losing their ability to have Garou pups. Also to reiterate with a bit more detail, wolves give off strong hormonal cues that actually act as a form of birth control to biologically ensure only one or two females can get pregnant in a season; so if you just have one pack of wolves kept in a sanctuary by the Sept, you're getting one or two litters of pups a year. That's... basically 1-2 Garou a year.

                                --------------

                                On the "why not go back to culling humans," thing:

                                Because genocide will just make the Wyrm stronger; massive slaughter does not just temporarily cause some extra banes. It can permanently alter the spiritual landscape of an area and turn it into a Blight of infinite bane-spawning grounds. Also if you're playing in the wider WoD with other supernaturals, the Technocrats will very easily stop you.
