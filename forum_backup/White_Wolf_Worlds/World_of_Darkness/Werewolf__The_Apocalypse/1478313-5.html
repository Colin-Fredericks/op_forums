    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
    That's why it's a mix of biology and spirit. So it's Fenris, not Tribal elders, that has signifies how much the breeding counts towards the individual Fenrir being a paragon of the Tribe.
    Well, I don't like that W20 addition, for it can lead to the conclusion that Pure Breed, much like tribal membership, is god given. Or Totem given, if you will. But that is not the case, or doesn't make sense in the broader context of the game. It is garou who decide whom to educate into their tribe and send to the rite of passage. It is also garou who perform punishment Rites to throw someone out (or Adoption Rites or what ever else might change or end tribal membership). And sure, Tribal Totems have a an option to intervene, that is a given. But they aren't onipresent, all-knowing or have knowledge of the future. As Incarnae they should not engage in micromanaging their tribe and it should not foster a situation in which garou would delegate their responsibilities for their brethren and culture to the Totem. Totem involvement should be an option used for story driven purposes.

    For me, it makes more sense, if pure breed is fate, happenstance, not completely random but much like breeding true a probability game. Ancestry furthers the changes of having pure breed, as per the books, obviously. But there is not power that grants it or rules over it, it simply is. With the option of being gradually developed between birth and first-change.

    Even the deed for which the european garou want to put him on trail is circumstantial. He exercises control over a Thunderwyrm, allowing a fellow garou to flee. While the garou suggest foul play, the deed is not necessarily against Falcon's Ways.

        Originally posted by Mercurial View Post
        My bro is a Marine. His branch of the military like to say they are the strongest of the strong, but that doesn't mean he believes in oppressing the weak.
        The Marines see themselves as an elite, and the "every Marine is a rifleman" is part of that ethos. In my own depiction of the Get of Fenris tribe, I base them heavily as that. The Get of Fenris see themselves as elite warriors of the Garou Nation, and want every auspice to be functional like an Ahroun in other tribes (they do have many useful combat gifts as Tribal gifts after all). It's just that THEIR ahrouns are more like special forces (ideally).

        Originally posted by Mercurial View Post
        Problem: physical power means a LOT in battle when you are typically fighting the same way your elders did 10,000 years ago.
        More than any connection to Nazis (even the first edition corebook that mentioned a small faction of the Get embraced this, mentioned it as a PROBLEM. And the first edition Players Guide further designated them as a problem. This was meant primarily as antagonists, and every tribe had some camp in them that was meant to be antagonists), I thought the supposed Get oppression of women to be a problem. It didn't make sense to me since in general the ancient Germanic and Nordic peoples were known for their women to be much more equal to men than the Classical civilizations. (I think the game designers eventually realized this, as this was downplayed heavily in second edition and ignored in Revised. It's really just an early first edition thing.)

        But it did make sense that a tribal culture that prized physical excellence would be tough on Garou who lacked sufficient physical strength. And while in terms of game mechanics, a female PC is not disadvantaged to a male PC - anyone can decide to make their character have 5 Strength or whatever - in terms of setting, this is not the case. Since human females have less upper body strength than males on average, it would make sense that there'd be a higher number of female NPCs that "wash out" as cubs because they can't meet the physical standards of the tribe. So I used that as the basis of "the Get discriminate against women". But this was only based on the Get insisting on high upper body strength. The other tribes may not like a cub who is physically weak, but it is not a deal breaker for them. But at a first glance, the results of the Get's standards would create the impression that they are rejecting more women as members as discrimination due to their sex.

        We rarely see any NPCs who only have a Strength (or any other stat) of 1. But as "Below Average", anywhere from a third to half of the population should have that as a stat. Even if you argue that among Garou, that would be lessened, there is still going to be a fair number of people with lack that strength. And a higher proportion of them are more likely to be women. So these are the Get cubs who end up going to other tribes. And certainly a group like the Black Furies, who are equally aggressive and militant as the Get, would see potential in these rejected cubs and see this as shortsighted, stupid, and prejudicial. It's just like how the Marines rejected Audie Murphy because he was too underweight so Murphy joined the Army and became the highest decorated US soldier in WWII for bravery.

            Originally posted by heinrich View Post
            Is that explicitly stated?
            No, but there's no other coherent conclusion to make from the collection of other statements. Anything other than "0 to 5" would contradict something, while "0 to 5" doesn't.

            Also.. you don't need to keep telling me how much Pure Breed doesn't make sense. I've been saying that on the official forums for as long as I've been on them. I'm just trying to engage with what we have and how it applies to where the conversation is going.

            Well, I don't like that W20 addition, for it can lead to the conclusion that Pure Breed, much like tribal membership, is god given. Or Totem given, if you will.
            That was a Revised clarification in the ST Handbook FAQ, not a W20 addition.

            But I really don't have a better mechanic in mind myself. I would change the description and possibly try to aim for a clarification in the whole "formal challenge" situation...
            Try this out:

            1) Pure Breed ceases to be its own trait. Instead it is an in-universe concept that's a side effect of Garou that have/that have high ratings in other Traits. The more of these Traits, and the higher they are if rated, the more a given Garou tends to have Garou children, and the more their kin children tend to have Garou children as well. These Traits include, but are not limited to: Renown greater than the character needs to qualify for Elder, Rank, Spirit Heritage, Garou Heritage, Fate, any mental or social Merits that represent inner strength or conviction, any Merit rated 5 for higher. Effectively Garou culture has a causation vs. correlation issue where the concept of Pure Breeding mistakes the correlation of these traits with "breeding true" for there being a causal relationship between controlled breeding and seeing these traits manifest more often.

            2) Pure Breed is replaced by the Garou Heritage Background. Garou Heritage represents well known Garou heroes (or maybe even extrarordinary kinfolk) in the character's lineage. This heritage provides a -2 difficulty bonus to all social rolls not including Gift activation or Rites when dealing with Garou that identify with those past heroes, and a -1 difficulty bonus to those familiar with their stories. The character's rating in this Background determines how well known their ancestor's exploits are, from adored officers of their birth Sept that are unknown to most of the Nation at 1, to the greatest heroes in Garou history that everyone in their Tribe endeavors to aspire to, and ever Garou of any Tribe has head of at 5.

            3) New Supernatural Merit: Tribal Paragon (1 pt). A character's Garou Heritage is recognized by their Tribal Totem, who has high hopes for the character and creates great expectations for them. They gain an effective Spirit Heritage rating in their Tribal Totem's brood equal to half their Garou Heritage rounded up as long as they remain members of the Tribe in good standing. Any time the character would lose Renown, increase the loss by 1 temporary Renown of the most appropriate type. This Merit can only be taken if the character joins the Tribe of their most recent Garou ancestor. It usually manifests during the Rite of Initiation, but any loyal member of the Tribe with heroic lineage might find themselves blessed after an act of high Tribal virtue.

            4) Optional: Garou Heritage is only Restricted for Silver Fangs, who have to take it. Any Tribe that was formerly Restricted from Pure Breed is now Discouraged for Garou Heritage (even Bone Gnawers can have legendary parents). Tribal Paragon is not available to Tribes that are Garou Heritage Discouraged (Rat still doesn't give a shit who your parents were).

                I’ve never really liked the idea that the GoF were misogynistic.
