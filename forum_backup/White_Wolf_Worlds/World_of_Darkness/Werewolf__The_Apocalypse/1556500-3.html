                                        Even if the idea is that Garou teach the Rite of Sacred Rebirth to kinfolk for the purposes of hunting Black Spiral Dancers and enabling them to become Garou, the big problem with that strategy is that you let the cat out of the bag. There is nothing to stop those kinfolk from turning on you. As a general idea, this is just bad.

                                        However, there's plenty of evidence of Garou being stupid and some Garou willing to deal with Wyrm minions for the purposes of fighting the greater evil. As such, I do think there's sufficient evidence to justify an ST for using this as a plot element in their chronicle if they wish.

                                        A pack of Shadow Lords of the Bringers of Light camp decide to help a select group of kinfolk become Garou and eliminate a Hive of Black Spiral Dancers. Just one more "triumph" of them using the Wyrm's methods but overcoming the corruption themselves! Or perhaps its Silent Striders of the Eaters of the Dead camp. Or perhaps its the Society of Bitter Frost among the Uktena. Or desperate Bone Gnawers from a sept that already tolerates Man Eaters and the like. Or whatever group of dubious Garou the ST wishes.

                                        Or perhaps instead of targeting BSDs alone, they target Ronin as well or perhaps lone Metis. Anyone that particular group might not feel are "real" Garou, and the Rite of Sacred Rebirth could be used to create loyal Garou.

                                        In any case, the PCs stumble across this situation, and then they need to decide how to deal with it. How do they learn the Rite is being used, and these new Garou aren't just Lost Cubs or late changers. How do they go about convincing other Garou this is the case? How do they rally other septs and organize a concolation to deal with it. A lot can be done with this kind of plot.

                                        This kind of thinking is completely mad, but as a lone exception it could be a great plot for the ST. Just because the vast majority of the Garou Nation will reject this doesn't mean every Garou will do so. I think it'd actually be a very fun plot to play through for players. The ST just needs to make sure this is an exception for a small group of Garou who have gone over the edge.
