                                        So, first of all I'll open this post saying "thank you" to whoever upped that video. Yes, I can write it on youtube, but I still think that he deserves to be mentioned here too for his efforts.
                                        I was a teenager playing tabletop WtA when I heard that they were developing a PC Werewolf game, and it's hard to explain how excited I was: it was everything you always wanted, made with the best game engine availlable that would have allowed real-time shapeshifting, morphing the character model without using cheap tricks (Like Morrowind did, for exemple). This video allowed me to finally understand how the game would have been, and that's some closure to my curiosity, at least.

                                        But enough emotional rollercoasters.
                                        Using the White Howlers is not that absurd: it's just a single-player videogame, and even if the story makes you the whitest fly of the White Howlers it's not like it harms anyone. Having Gaia saved by the last of a fallen clan, the only Garou in history that dances the Black Spiral without falling to the Wyrm is surely an epic tale of redemption and heroism. That said, I'd really have liked a game more like Bloodlines, where you can pick your tribe and have an experience closer to the actual Werewolf world... and p.s.: that applies to the new Cyanide Werewolf game too, because being a Ronin isn't really a great start for a WtA fan.
                                        But who knows, maybe they did it to let you pick your side halfway through the game and make you play without all the needed background knowledge to be a proper Garou. We'll see.
