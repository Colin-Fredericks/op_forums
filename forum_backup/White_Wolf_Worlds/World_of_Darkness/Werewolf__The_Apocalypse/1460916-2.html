                        Originally posted by Damian May View Post
                        Eh, a couple of US states killed 90% of their wolf populations within 2 weeks very recently so its very dependant on location.
                        As much as wild life protection isn't a unanimous topic, and never will, it doesn't mean the situation isn't extremely different from 30 years ago. In the 90's the very notion of wolf protection was controversial and only actively defended by a few. Today what's happening in those US states is considered outrageous. Preservation shifted from a niche to a mainstream worry all around the world. We faced many losses already, but we also achieved some victories and saved several species on the brink of extinction.

                        We aren't in a good position. But things changed a lot, and the setting wasn't designed to account for this. We also learned a lot about nature that we didn't knew when the setting was developed, like how wolf packs actually work (just a hint: Red Talon culture has a lot of bull shit about what wolves are like).

                        Originally posted by Konradleijon View Post
                        And why would the Red Talons be considered “problematic”? In the fact that their hard to make a PC because they hate humans?
                        In the fact that they hate humans the way they do. They are the very example of indiscriminate hate and bigotry. And they're a caricature of minority groups and foreign cultures, wielding that hate (not rage, but actual hate) while keeping themselves "primitive" to not be "tainted".

                        Finally, saying this is explained by them being wolves is bull shit since A) wolves aren't a cultural minority that you can point and say how they behave or what they want or defend, B) the setting pretty much had no idea on how wolves behave in nature, so Red Talon culture isn't wolf culture, it is Red Talon culture, and C) Luddism is a pretty human idea.

                        The Red Talons are just a sick inference that a whole culture can be geared towards hate and endorsement of terrorism, and actually solely defined by it, and that for the wronged genocide is an ok idea. They're not a persecuted group, they're a joke on persecuted groups.

                        Originally posted by Ana Mizuki View Post
                        It cheapens the title of the game, if the apocalypse was not even world-shaking enough event for humanity to notice.
                        That's the same problem they had with Gehenna. Instead of going for it all out or postponing it, they decided it came and was kind of not a big deal in the big picture. So move to the next step in the metaplot, but don't change the status quo too much. That's my whole expectation for W5.

                        Both going all out or backing down and postponing the Apocalypse would be better decisions, with a healthy update of the setting, but not forgetting that it is Werewolf, not Angstwolf.
