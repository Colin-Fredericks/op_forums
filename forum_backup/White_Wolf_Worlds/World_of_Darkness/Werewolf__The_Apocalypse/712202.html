    Ancient Samsa: Frozen in time and tellurian

    10-21-2015, 07:49 AM
    Gather around hatchlings, and you will hear our story. This war threatens to consume us all, and the memories of our breed must continue on, so that those who come even after you will remember where we came from. The Gauntlet rises, and the spirit and the material is separated, the Mother weaves, and we will scurry along this new web, we will hide in the homes of the Dragon Kings, and we will outlast the Wyrm as our Mother binds her in her Pattern Web.

    For this world is a chaotic place, a world of beasts and dragons, a world of blood and thunder, a world of madness and dissolution. This is how it is, but not how it should be, this savage world will not last. Even if it did, would you call this place home? A place where dragons roar and feed on the flesh of the weak, a world where everything will be eaten by the Wyrm, or changed by the Wyld? The Spiders claim we have turned away from Gaia, but it is they who fail to serve her, they serve only their Harlot-Queen Ananasa, who has betrayed our Mother.

    Know this, where it unbound, the Wyrm's hunger would only grow and grow and grow, and the Wyld would constantly change what little the Wyrm did not find palatable. In time, all would be darkness and madness at the end of the universe, and the Mother would not allow this. When she saw Gaia would not protect herself, she took action, she ordered, and we obeyed, hearing the OneSong's edicts which where crystal clear: Preserve the universe, tame the wilds, stop the advance of Chaos and Entropy and remake the world so that the Mother can forever protect Gaia, even if she does not wish it.

    But the Spiders will not have any of it, even now they hunt us, they kill our warriors, they drain our blood and leave naught but empty husks. The Mother is occupied, weaving immovable webs around the Wyrm to stymie the end of all things, and she cannot hear us over the sound of her legs clacking and the roars of the Wyrm. We are losing this war, and help will arrive too slowly to be of any use to us. The Ants and Hornets have already fallen, but we are children of Cockroach, and we shall endure, for by the grace of Mother Weaver, we shall hide you away. Stasis will be your shield, the Pattern Web your castle, the fog of ages your armor. And when the time comes, and the Spiders forget, you will hatch for a second time, and scurry across the webs to bring stasis to all.

    The OneSong gets quiet here...the others have fallen, I have but a few moments left, and then you will be cut loose into her webs, sleeping away the ages. If I may ask, hatchlings, that you do not forget this old roach, and remember what I have taught you even as the weight of eternity falls on your shoulders. May the OneSong guide you, godspeed.



    Ancient Samsa

    As you've seen above, the Samsa of the ancient times where not the tainted Mockery Breeds you see scurrying around in cities where the security of Pentex Facilities where disturbingly lax. Like all of the ancient Insect Breeds, they where exterminated at the hands of the Ananasi by the orders of the Mother-Queen, and yet the Cockroaches refused to die. Born of the Cockroach Incarnae, who even today survives as the Totem of the Glass Walker tribe of Garou, they inherited his drive to endure and survive, no matter the cost. As a result, roughly a hundred 'Stasis Eggs', where Samsa Hatchlings where bound in a nexus of pure stasis, and plunged into the nascent pattern web exist in the deepest, most ancient and calcified portions of the Umbra.

    The Samsa had seen what had happened to the other Insect Breeds, and hoped that by hiding away some of their young, their spiritual half would be spared the unweaving that happened to the spirits of the other Insect Shifters. They hoped that in a millennium or two at most, the Weaver would finish wrapping up the Wyrm, and unseal the young Samsa, letting them out so that they might repopulate the breed.

    But the Samsa underestimated the madness of the Weaver, and vastly overestimated how much she cared about them.

    To the Weaver, all of her children owe her utter, unthinking obedience, and if they should die, well then they died for their Mother and for her plan to calcify the Tellurian. Essentially, she noticed there where no more Roach shifters scurrying around, shrugged, and got back to work.Where any Samsa left, the heartbreak of seeing how quickly their Mother wrote them off would of probably been enough to kill them in of itself. She had bigger and better things to do then concern herself with those little nodules in her web, the Samsa's familiarity with the Weaver's ways permitted them to bind them into the web in such a fashion that the Weaver didn't consider it an abberation. The web looked as it should, fit her aesthetics, and as a result? The Samsa Eggs where forgotten, with only an unusual concentration of Weaverish energies marking them as particularly unusual.

    Over the eons, Pattern Spiders would sometimes encounter these eggs, but simply did not consider them worth noticing. They where of the Weaver, they where already calcified, and their orders where clear: Calcify the Tellurian, why should they concern themselves with work that is already done? It was not until the relatively recent population explosion of Rust Spiders, Pattern Spiders tainted by the Wyrm, that the Samsa Eggs where noticed.

    Plucked from the Pattern Web by tainted Weaver Spirits, the eggs where spirited away to Malfeas and handed to servants of Hakaken, the Heart of Fear, due to the terror they sensed in these calcified eggs, who despite being frozen in stasis, could sense the wrongness of the Rust Spiders by their very nature. The eggs where then given to Pentex through Black Sprial Middlemen, and over the course of a decade, forcibly cracked open.

    The Samsa hatchlings where in the exact same state they where all those millions of years ago...For perhaps a few hours at best, before they where vivisected, sliced open by a species who's ancestors ancestors ancestors did not exist in those most ancient of times. Their spiritual essence was ripped out of them, tainted and corrupted, and the hatchlings where left, quivering in pain and abject horror before they withered and died on the slab.

    From there, the essence extracted from the Samsa was merged with taint...And not just taint of the Wyrm, the fear of these poor hatchlings forever marred the spiritstuff pulled from them, meaning every "Mockery" Samsa carries this ancestral terror with them. They know fear, they know the terror of awakening in a mad world, and they know the pain of the scalpel as it ripped their very souls out of their body. Perhaps the only solace provided to these inheritors of an ancient and fear-maddened power is that Cockroach has not forgotten his children, and still considers them to have potential to survive, and he has granted them their patronage.

    Despite their inherited trauma, these Mockery Samsa have hope, however fragile and small that hope may be. And the Wyrm did not get every Stasis Egg, some still lay hidden deep in the Pattern Web, and the sensitive antennae of these new Samsa may just be able to track them down...



    Ancient Samsa

    If, for whatever reason, an Ancient Samsa is freed from its Stasis Egg, and not immediately ripped open for spiritual essence to cram into Mockery Breeds, they do not have any mandatory Derangements. The psychic trauma of the Samsa comes from the brutal surgeries and experimentation performed on the salvaged wereroaches, not to mention being pumped full of wyrm taint. Unlike the Mockery Samsa, the ancient Specimens could shatter into a swarm form, could breed with Cockroaches, and could step sideways.

    For the purposes of their Swarm Form, it is treated as exactly identical to the Crawlerling Form of the Anasnasi. Furthermore, like the Ananasi, they step sideways easier in areas of Weaverish concentration and use the same Gauntlet chart as they do. Furthermore, only one egg in a clutch of Cockroach eggs will become a Samsa, and only then if at least one parent was Kinfolk or a Samsa themselves.

    Ancient Samsa can learn Tenere gifts, Glass Walker gifts, and Homid gifts. Other gifts regarding technology, weaverish essence, and combating the Wyrm are also acceptable, if it looks and smells Weaverish, they can probably learn it with a little effort. Just remember that Roaches can't spin webs, so Tenere gifts relating to the Ananasi's ability to weave webs cannot be learned.

    Cleansed Samsa

    Modern Samsa aren't exactly in need of redemption, by and large the poor bastards haven't really done anything to deserve their lot in life. That said, if a Samsa is freed of his innate Wyrm Taint and somehow manages to overcome all of his derangements, the Samsa's spiritual discord is 'reset to factory standard' as their Weaverish energies come to the forefront once more, gaining the abilities of the ancient Samsa. They do not lose free will in the process, as the ancient Weaver was slightly more sane then her modern counterpart, but probably do become significantly more stable and coherent with the most notable personality issues being "They're no longer bugfuck nuts".

    As one final note, the Antennae power in Ungeziefer form can tap into the OneSong of the Weaver, hearing her edicts, commands, and her plans. As the Ancient Weaver didn't consider that any of her children could rebel without her instantly destroying them as she did the Moth Shifters, the Samsa are not 'enslaved' upon hearing the OneSong like many creatures are. Of course, Samsa are perfectly free to choose to follow every whim of their Mother's voice, but they don't HAVE to.

                              Well, Erebus is generally the "Go To" for this sort of thing, but remember: They've not gone through the Spiral, and as a result have not TECHNICALLY fallen to the Wyrm.
