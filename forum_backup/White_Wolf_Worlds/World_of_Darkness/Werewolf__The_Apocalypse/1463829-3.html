                    So, these are my first impressions...

                    Two native american cultural advisors on the team.
                    Great! And I hope they don't just provide advice on the Native American tribes, but also on Animism as a whole. It'd be awesome to have more details on how the Garou deal with nature spirits on a day-to-day basis.

                    Tribes as less tied to geography and culture and more to concept.
                    Interesting, as long as the Tribes don't become the clubs they are on Forsaken. Your Tribe is your heritage, you don't get to freely choose it, you belong to it even before your birth and to renounce your birthright has grave consequences.

                    Focus on younger werewolves trying to reclaim what was lost.
                    That's fine, I like small scale stories. Forsaken already did this and it worked.

                    World is fucked up horribly but not post apocalyptic.
                    I wonder how much fucked up it will be. Big climate changes may have huge impacts on some populations.

                    Umbra as a hostile place, caerns cut off from the spirit world.
                    Umbra as a hostile place was already done on Forsaken, do we really need to copy it? Why? I like the idea that the Umbra can be a dangerous place if you don't know what you are doing, but I also like the idea that the Spirit World can be beautiful and feel even more alive than the Material World. Why change it?

                    Caerns cut off from the Spirit World??? So the whole idea of having holy places where the Garou can gather and that the Garou must protect will be simply thrown away. This seems stupid in so many ways I can't even begin to describe it. The Garou culture, their existence as One People, revolves around the Caren. Kill this and what do they have left? I like to tell stories of warriors fighting to protect their sacred lands, I don't want to be forced to only tell stories of warriors that lost their territory.

                    Haglust (spelling?) -> Opposite to Harano, hysterical zeal, embodied by a new antagonist tribe that may or may not be right.
                    Very interesting! I really liked this idea! See? MORE options is good, LESS options is bad! Also I'll bet the Red Talons had enough and brought the Impergium back.

                    Focus on renown as something that empowers the Garou.
                    This may be interesting, but the Garou are already "powered" by Rage, Gnosis and Willpower. I'd like to see more on this topic.

                    Getting rid of Metis and Pure Breed.
                    Getting rid of the name "Metis" I can understand, but I see no reason to get rid of the Breed. Again, MORE options is good, LESS options is bad! The Metis are not just werewolves with deformities, they represent much more than that and they offer a huge number of roleplay opportunities. It seems someone is really trying to turn Apocalypse into Forsaken.

                    And Pure Breed makes total sense from a Garou perspective. This is another unnecessary change and these changes are becoming annoying. I surely can houserule this Background back, and I will, but if I have to houserule too many things I may decide it's easier to stick with W20. I'm VERY satisfied with the game as is and I don't NEED another edition. I'd love to continue supporting the line, like I've been doing since the first edition, but I won't buy any more books if they decide to turn Apocalypse into Forsaken-wannabe.

                    No more Garou Shall not Mate with Garou.
                    Oh, come on!!! Give Werewolf: the Apocalypse back to someone that actually LIKES this game! Good times when we had Bill Bridges and Ethan Skemp...
                    Last edited by Wolfen; 10-19-2021, 02:20 AM.
