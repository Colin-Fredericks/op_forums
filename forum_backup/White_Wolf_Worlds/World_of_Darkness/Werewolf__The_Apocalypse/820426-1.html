    Please also take note: BNS is still taking suggestions, constructive critiques, and the likes.
    IF you have suggestions, and comments please (politely) Email them at [email protected]. They likely won't contact you directly as they're compiling the information and using it in meetings to help make decisions.

    Now, as for "The Bite", this is all we know so far:
    Further, many Theurges speculate that the natural balance is attempting to restore itself, spontaneously evolving werewolf genetics in otherwise normal wolves and humans. Sometimes this potential remains dormant throughout an individual’s life. However, when bitten by a werewolf, the spark can be triggered, resulting in a mutation that rebuilds the individual’s DNA. This mutation causes a debilitating, flu-like sickness that eventually, within a month’s time, results in the First Change.
    Please note here that all that is presented here are mostly theories. All Garou know is that they're (for some reason) biting people, and for some reason certain people wind up going through a month-long transformation and becoming Garou.

    Personally, I think that the idea of folks who are otherwise normal folks who are "bitten" and find themselves becoming Garou is fine. But the idea that Garou are going around biting folks and not killing them (kinky sex?) and then SOME of those people wind up having had a dormant "gift" that is activated by a Garou's supernatural bite and begins a process of turning them into Garou leaves some weirdness.

    My proposed suggestions are going to be this:

    The current wording of how "otherwise normal wolves and humans" can change into Garou from a bite is odd in that there's no reason why Garou would go around biting folks to find the few who will transform from a bite--this is a huge breach of the veil and causes all sorts of problems. For this reason, I propose reasons why Garou would bite someone:

    1) Kinfolk with a high-enough Pure Breed (3 or higher) can be bitten and turned into Garou (1 in 10)
    2) Kinfolk can be put through a Rite that can enhance their chances (increasing it to 2 in 10?)
    3) Anyone who turns into a Garou through "The Bite" must take a permanent and irremovable bite scar at the site of the wound. This mark is both physical and spiritual. Those who are bitten and do not turn into Garou receive the same permanent scar.
    4) Each time a Garou successfully creates another werewolf through the Bite, her permanent Gnosis is reduced by one. This makes creating new Garou part of a personal sacrifice, and limits how many a Garou can make through a Bite (this permanent reduction only happens on a successful transformation.)
    5) Keep the idea that a "Bitten" Garou can't make other Garou through a bite.
    6) A newly-bitten Garou becomes the Ward of their "creator" and is spiritually obligated to help help instruct/train them until their Rite of Passage.

    Much of this would be in the history, but allows for the RP of Kinfolk characters who want to become Garou.
    This also creates a whole new aspect to the Garou/Kin relationship, as the "best" can be brought up and "attempt" to be given the Gift.

    By doing this, it allows the Garou to be more pro-active in who becomes Garou, choosing the best candidates and hoping their "gift" is true. It makes them accountable.

    Of course, some of this is going to be seen as being too close to Vampire (Sires taking Ghouls and making them into Vampires, etc.) but the same concept goes for any sort of Mentor, even in Mage as well, so why would such a natural concept be out of line for Garou?

    I would also like some sort of story hook where either the Galliards are searching for lore, or Theurges are seeking spirits to help explain what is happening. At least something that shows that there's an answer in the works and could/will be explained in time and not just deus ex machina.
