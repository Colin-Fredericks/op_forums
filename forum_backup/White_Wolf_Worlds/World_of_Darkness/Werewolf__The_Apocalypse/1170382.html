      While obviously I could point out rape, torture, murder, etc. etc. that wyrm servants do, Pentex isn't supposed to be uniformly serious. A lot of it is heavily silly. "Black comedy" is how I would typically describe them.

      What does strike me as horrifying is the way that Pentex creates fomori, and makes the sick and often dying aberrations think that A) Pentex is here to help, B) they are now some kind of gifted mutant superhero and C) when they die, they are often ground up and given as drugs to new fomori. Obviously this can be silly as well. If the polluting factor strikes you as silly, you can simply omit it (there is enough Wyrmic corruption that doesn't involve pollution to last 101 sessions).

      Between Pentex, Tzimisce, and the Nephandi, I would say the former have an advantage in that they are sometimes played for serious horror and sometimes played for laughs, whereas the Tzimisce and Nephandi are almost never funny on purpose.

      One idea is to introduce a few NPCs that are somewhat sympathetic, and with a minor flaw, that grows into titanic proportions once they are transformed into fomori. You could go with a lifespan of 3 encounters for each of them, roughly: one with them as normal, one with them as corrupted as fomori but trying to hide it, and one as them as full on antagonists.

          Easiest method is simply to look at how real life businesses are doing things. While there might be one or two sociopaths among their ranks, it is their short sightedness that is the most evil part of them. Short-term profit means workers are worked to the bone, can't afford proper medical care, shops are understaffed to save costs and any woman who -dares- to have children either has to leave their kid to the care of relatives or get fired. This also means that the workers rarely if ever have energy to fight back, especially as union membership is a dirty word.

          Again, no malice other than simply being greedy. It is not that companies WANT to pollute the world, it is just cheaper and easier.

          If you want some examples of evil for other things than Pentex, here are three people I consider great examples of BSDs or just Wyrmy people;

          H.Holmes - He was a scammer and a liar, but that doesn't make him memorable. What -does- for me is that he also was an insidious murderer on top of it. He committed murder on an almost industrial scale for profit, selling skeletons of his victims for doctors to use. In a darkly amusing way, if he could get more by killing a person, he would.

          Albert Fish - If you ever want an example of how a BSD acts, in my mind, Albert is one. It isn't really what he did as to -how- he did it, he just did things out of curiosity and with a huge lack of care for social mores. No real rhyme or reason, he could be as cruel to himself as to others as well. Plus, he apparently never harmed his own children, adding to the strange way his mind worked.

          The Nightstalker - Everyone makes their own choices, but I think this guy is a prime example of what happens when you are influenced by extremely toxic people.

          My gallery.
