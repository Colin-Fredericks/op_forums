                                  I was thinking about time paradoxes while I was creating a fanfic about zombie apocalypse and time travels, and my teory is changing details of the past cause "Schöringer's effects". If you change the past to save a cat's life, then the cat will be alive and dead at same time, but (almost) nobody will notice it. Everybody will see a dead cat, but the time traveler will be the one who see the alive cat.

                                  What if we send a gold ingot is sent to the last day, and two ingots are molten together? Or a robot is programmed to paint a wall in a laboratory for the experiment. The experiment says if the wall is red, then is painted with blue color, if the wall is blue, then is painted with red color. If it is another color, then to radomly choose to paint with red or blue wall. What color would be the wall. Maybe by a "Schoringer effect" the wall would be violet or pupple, because blue and red from different timeline are fused.

                                  Let's say changing the past isn't like erasuring words on a paper, but adding a lack of correction fluid and after writting new words. The old words haven't disapiared really but they are under the correction fluid. That is the reason I say changing the past is a illusion.

                                  Other matter Earth isn't spining around the sun, but also is spinning arout the galaxy. That means the planets are moving like springs. To travel to the time you also need inteplanetary vehicles.

                                  In gothic-punk urban fantasy we could find another surprise, like the time-travelers can't change really the past because reality is controlled by god-machine or the archons and lictors from "Kult: lost divinty", or changes in the time travel wouldnot affect the original timeline, but a Ucrony with side effects, like vampires ruling the world, or faes stopping industrial revolution, or tribal werebeasts fighing againts aliens from space or robots from a hipotetical future. We could find a multiverse with the factions of Adventure!-Aberrant-Trinity, and empires trying to send agents to controll the powers of more primitive earths.
