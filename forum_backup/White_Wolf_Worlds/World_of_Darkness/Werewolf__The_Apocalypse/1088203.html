    The Old Packmate is a secret Nuwisha trick...

    05-05-2017, 07:48 AM
    So I'm going to have my Wild West game have two NPC packmembers, and one of them I am thinking of being Secretly a Nuwisha posing as a Wendigo Ragabash. So they mention this is a common Nuwisha trick even into the Modern Age.

    But how will I go about doing it. They are going to start as a Pack before their rite of the Totem, Will the Nuwisha just use that Tag along gift, I forget who learns it maybe Ragabashes? So will the Pack form, but the Nuwisha doesn't add to the Totem Background?

    Their Coyote shifting seems like it would smell hella weird to normal Garou, what tricks do they have to cover up they are a Friggin Coyote rather then a scrawny wolf?

    The Pack is a mix Uktena Fianna pack, so I'm wondering what the end game will be for this Nuwisha, Broken Wind? I'm thinking maybe it will be a 2nd Rank, that way Broken Wind can pose as a survivor of a Pure Tribe/Wyrmbringer Sept War. He, or maybe I'll have it a she yeah why not balance the sexes nicely, could plan to try to help heal the tribal divide of the Garou Nation with this pack. 2nd Rank isn't too much more experienced then Cliath, and the NPC posing as a survivor of a wiped out pack means she can get away with not leading the pack and more supporting a new pack

    How Long I wonder should I keep it up?

    Has anyone used this trope yet in your games?

    And Nuwisha don't make Metis... and can breed with Garou... so how much shenanigans could she get into trying to seduce Garou?! That seems like something that should be mentioned more with Nuwisha, considering their Patron's famous Libido! Garou may take it bitterly being scared into thinking they made a metis, but Nuwisha could pull it off as performing a seducer role to test their loyalty to the Litany.
