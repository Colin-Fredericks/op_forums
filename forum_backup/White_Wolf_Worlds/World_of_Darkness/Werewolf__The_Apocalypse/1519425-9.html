    Originally posted by Knightingale View Post
    Interesting. Thank you for mentioning that. Wouldn’t have expected science to have already come this far.
    ​​​​​​
    I think it's still largely theoretical, and only partially proven, but then, so is a lot of stuff we take for granted as being "true" (because it's often the best explanation we have so far).

    I think inherited trauma could absolutely be relevant for a game like WTA, given many of its themes. But the problem is that they've kinda shoehorned in an academic essay without context or a precis. Which means it's ripe for misunderstandings and, unfortunately, ridicule, which then undermines the point you want to make.

    It reads to me like this is a statement someone wanted to make on inherited trauma, but rather than include it in the ST chapter, where it would need introducing and some expectation-setting, they thought they could get away with sticking it in as IC flavour text and then not having to deal with it beyond that.

    Which is sorta like the Paradox approach in a nutshell. Try to do something progressive but half-arse it, or not really understand what you're trying to do or why, and so it ends up coming across as cynical, tone-deaf or out of place.
