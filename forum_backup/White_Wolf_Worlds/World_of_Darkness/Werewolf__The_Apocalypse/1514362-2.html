                                            Originally posted by Knightingale View Post
                                            Reading up on the whole topic of using photo-references and tracing, one thing that immediately stuck out to me was that you could easily tell someone was just tracing photos when they're quickly producing multiple drawings in a short period of time. And so when this one guy is delivering this huge pile of work for not just the W5 corebook but also the V5 Players Guide and the first H5 supplement (plus, I assume, stuff for the upcoming Blood Sigils), shouldn't there have been alarm bells be going off in the Art Director's head...? There's no way this guy could produce this much art in such a short period of time if he's only using the photos as a reference...
                                            ​
                                            The art director most likely got handled a budget and a schedule too. I think Onyx Path fans are a big spoiled because Rich Thomas got started in the RPG sphere as an artist and was the original WW's art director for a long time.

                                            Having looked at some of the artist's work that's not involved in all this, and before he scrubbed a bunch of stuff on Instagram:

                                            1) He's actually really good. You can tell in the H5 core book art where it looks like he had more time. When he's no rushed his traces are far less obvious because they're far more mixed, and the finally results have quality and transformation so that even if you spot a trace, you don't really feel like it's as out of place as the W5 stuff has been.

                                            2) He used to be really good about crediting his trace sources (that's what he scrubbed, as all his WoD work has not credit any trace sources like it should have).

                                            I'd imagine he was hired for all of this precisely because he can bang out decent quality tracer art fast. He still got overloaded and cut too many corners. The art director still didn't do proper oversight; esp. if the whole point was to higher someone that specializes in tracer art to go fast. Paradox's minimal official statements on it included saying their contracts have specific rules for tracer art. Which means they use tracer artists knowingly and have rules for proper licensing and crediting that weren't followed in this case.

                                            Like, most people loved this guy's work in H5, and even people going back and finding his tracers style at work, it's way less egregious. It really takes people going in and looking at it in far finer detail than how quickly people are finding sources for the W5 stuff. So I think we have to assume the plan was to use him for speed to get all this work done on schedule set higher up the food chain; even if it was still too big a lift.
