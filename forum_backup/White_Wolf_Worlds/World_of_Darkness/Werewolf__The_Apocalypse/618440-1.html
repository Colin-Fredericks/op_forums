    Originally posted by Uniform Two Six View Post
    Well, first, it was a pair of elders who were investigating whether or not it was even possible to open a caern there (this was specifically stated as being in doubt since the dormant caern was very weak and an abandoned air force base was probably not going to be a good location anyway). Also (despite the fact that that wasn't what they were doing), I would challenge the assertion that "raising a caern" equates to "the simplest thing". Opening a caern is a MAJOR deal, very rare, and sometimes fatal for the participants.
    As Su-Tehp pointed out above, creating a Caern with so many people is idiotic. Yes, raising a Caern is a big thing, but it has still been done for centuries. So why is the area so poorly cleared, why, when the BSDs attack, do the Elders disappear from the action? Centuries of tradition should still have made sure preparations were made in case there was an attack.

    Originally posted by Uniform Two Six View Post
    Second, uniting the tribes and advocating peace is precisely the mutually-exclusive paradox that the Children face. How do you advocate peace to a warrior culture? Answer: You don't -- at least not successfully. The Shadow Lords are better at uniting the tribes because they get the blood-and-guts mentality of the Garou at large.
    There is a difference between promoting peace and being so afraid of violence that they were unable to help the Shadow Lords. Again, tribebook should make you celebrate the tribe, not feel like they failed. Even if Shadow Lords are more brutal, you'd think Coggies would have jumped in on the uniting train pretty quickly.

    But most of the book is not them dealing with other tribes, or asking these questions. It's about this young pack that has no command, no goal and only meander from event to event partying. All other tribebooks have elders tell the stories, and for a reason. If the book had been split with the Elders telling tribe relations/Auspices/breeds/etc. and the youth showcasing different aspects, it would have worked better.

                                                I think a major problem with the Children of Gaia are depicted is that people have a skewed vision of of Unicorn. It seems to be more the unicorn/My Little Pony that young girls love combined with hippies. This is why so many depictions fail. The Unicorn of legend is very different. Developers and writers should have emphasized the aspects of unicorn in actual legend as inspiration.

                                                The Unicorns of legend were representation of the power and majesty of the virgin wilderness. If the Unicorn was in an area, the forest remained untouched because ordinary people couldn't enter there without risking their lives. They were wild animals, and were very dangerous to humans because the wilderness could be very dangerous. Only the pure and innocent - children and especially virgin maidens - could approach and even tame them. Their horn did have mystical powers of healing, but that wasn't their main thing.

                                                I think a better way in depicting them is that they/Unicorn wants to restore the harmony and balance as the world was back in the days of young. This does not mean a return to Earth before Man like the Red Talons, but accepting man as part of nature, but integrating him into a more appropriate "Gaian" mindset. Harmony, not peace, should be the goal. Obviously peace can be a big part of that, but harmony in and of itself is not peace. This is why they strive to unite the Tribes and push humans into a better lifestyle. They believe in purity and innocence and are commanded by Unicorn to protect that, so that is why they especially seek to protect children and heal the exploited. But they are not weak pacifists.

                                                In my games, I always emphasize "Unicorn is not a tame animal". If I have a unicorn spirit, I have it act like a wild mustang - very energetic, strong, and independent. I do have the burned out hippies and such, but there is always evidence that this aspect of the tribe is how the Wyrm is trying to destroy the Children of Gaia.
