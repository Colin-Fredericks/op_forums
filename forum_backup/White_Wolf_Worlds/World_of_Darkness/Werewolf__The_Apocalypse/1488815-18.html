                    Additionally, because of how V5 (and even worse H5) was handled, you can't reliably just go back to the old lore and bring it into V5. Between the massive metaplot changes made in their timeline between 2004 and 2018, and a rather large number of retcons and changes that aren't addressed with the metaplot, mixed together with an aggressive push for single-mode play old stuff just doesn't make sense in the new context without even more work.

                    For example, the Banu Haqim in pre-V5 were actually pretty easy to run with all players members of the same Clan (outside of dealing with RL cultural sensitivity issues with the old presentations). The three castes, larger internal factions, and complex relationships with the major and minor Sects meant they worked as a mini-Sect (unlike the Ravnos or Saulbri) with strong and simple to hook players on dramatic conflicts (unlike the Giovanni and Followers of Set who have looser organizations and require a lot more depth to get new players onboard).

                    V5 nuked everything that made that possible. The three castes don't make any sense as having ever existed since V5 is staunchly anti-bloodlines causing mechanical variations within Clans. The metaplot basically resolved the Schism (or at least fully divided the Clan such that the different factions are in separate parts of the setting and can't really fight each other for control over the Clan any more with the cult of Haqim "winning" in the sense of what the Schism is actually about if losing as so many of their enemies found safety elsewhere). There's no real way to play off the the Clan's former status as Independent that could operate in multiple Sect domains and all the political stories you can get into with that. Etc.

                    Now, the pre-V5 Banu Haqim are a mess, which is a problem for new players, but V5 didn't really fix the messy, it just piled the messy in the corners of the room. Instead of reconciling and fixing all the conflicting old lore into a more streamlined and easy to understand package, V5 is just yet another take on the Clan that makes it harder to put all the lore together instead of easier.

                                                    Originally posted by MrNatas View Post
                                                    I'm really curious about how a quantify an RPG being 'obsolete.'
                                                    It's pretty common mentality that the current edition is the one that counts. It's not entirely wrong-headed, as there are many ways where it matters to be the edition receiving active support from the company. Obsolete is not the best word choice, but there isn't really a great English word for this as it is.

                                                    In an ideal world, RPG companies want players to update to new editions. There's no reason to make them if they don't get adopted as the new standard. It's a big problem if a lot of people aren't updating to your new thing. While the older version my not be obsolete, it is diminished in perception by it's status as the old by many consumers.

                                                    When you buy a new product that has editions/models/etc. unless you have a very strong opinion on things before hand, you are naturally going to associate newest with better. If you could get the newest iPhone for the same price as the previous iPhone... why not?

                                                    We're just the people heavily invested enough to perhaps know that the lasted smartphone, or operating system, or whatever, has features that we might actively want to avoid and avoiding that means keeping away from the newer models.
