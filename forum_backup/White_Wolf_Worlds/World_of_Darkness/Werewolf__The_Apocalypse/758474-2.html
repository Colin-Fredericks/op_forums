        Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post
        The Point is because I played in a lot of the worse VTT Games on AOL Over a decade ago... ran into WAY too many Uber liberal garou... to the point where my Char was treated as if she might as well be a card carrying KKK member, for referencing a Metis derisively as a 'mule'
        Online games and LARPs have their own dynamic different from tabletop. What I noticed from them is that every character is the unique snowflake and exception to the settings, so it makes the game a mess from a canonical perspective. A tabletop game can handle a player or even the entire PC group being "different" since the ST can be sure to make the rest of the chronicle consistent with the setting - your Camarilla game looks like a Camarilla game, your Garou act like Garou.

        In those games, I've often played the "jerk" character, and I've found the other players really liked that because I was one of the few characters in the game they wanted to play off because I was one of the few who played a character consistent with the setting. In essence, you are in much more of an ST running a single NPC than a PC. One part of doing this is to not just be a jerk, but to be jerk in ways that is conducive to others having fun. That's not always easy to pull off.

        I don't know your situation a decade ago, but I'm sure some of those characters treating you badly probably had players who were very glad that character was there. However, there are those who are living vicariously through their characters and working out their own issues who may take offense. There's nothing you can do about that - these kind of huge online games are not a proper forum for therapy.
