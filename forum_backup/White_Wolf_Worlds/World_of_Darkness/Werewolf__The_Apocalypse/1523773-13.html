                            Originally posted by Ana Mizuki View Post
                            I wonder how much the sheet team and the art commission team talked to each other, given the app 1s. I at least would first ask the app stat if I were to do template art.
                            Typically, at least by the revised era, template and who's who entries would start with [Art Notes] (brackets would indicate that said text was for the Developer and not part of the actual book material, in this case to be passed on to the Art Developer who would give them to the assigned artist) giving at least a basic description of what the writer was hoping for.

                            Sometimes you get certain oddities, such as when Sean Riley didn't think to specify that Kleon Winston, a character who had originally appeared in Rage Across New York, was African American, and so you ended up having the image of him in the revised tribebook be a white guy.

                            Then you have the case of Christopher Shy's revised period work, where it seemed he just mostly did whatever the heck he felt like regardless of any art notes. The massive amount of facial tattoos in Vampire's Children of the Night, for example, as well as the Assamite sorcerer Al Ashrad having to retroactively be made the only member of his clan to be pale white and not darken with age because Shy turned in a piece that was effectively an albino. And then his overly cyberpunk photoshopped work for revised Mage, which resulted in a lot of the chapter opening fiction and even some of the signature characters having to be rewritten. (There was some speculation, comparing his work with what Leif Jones did for the Tradition write ups, that the Verbena signature character may have originally been meant to be African American.)

                            On the other end of the spectrum, you have someone like Ron Spenser, whose work would sometimes be so detailed that I swear he must have read the entire manuscript before doing his work.

                                And still in 1994 with Children of Gaia. This was written by Daniel Greenberg with Heather Curatola. Greenberg was the writer for Vampire's original Malkavian clan book, which doesn't exactly fill me with hope here. (I think he was Vampire Developer Andrew Greenberg's brother, and apparently did most of his freelancing while a college student.) Curatola is also known as Heather Heckel after marrying fellow White Wolf author Harry Heckel. Template art is again by Josh Timbrook.

                                This was, IIRC, when you first saw some of the Middle Eastern aspects of the tribe in an effort to flesh them out from more than just "the Baby Boomer era Hippy werewolves" that they started as. Unfortunately, a lot of it is still tied up in a lot of that era's more New Age-y grasp of various Eastern philosophies and religions, as well as paganism and various social causes. The Tribe's Camps are especially thin, most having only one or two paragraph in-character write ups that don't really give you much beyond the most basic idea. The one exception is the Aethera Inamorata, a Camp primarily based on how wonderful sex is, that gets at least half a page. (Inamorata is an old Latin term for a person's female lover. Aethera refers to aether/air. Apparently the literal translation is "Aether in love". I presume it is meant to refer to something similar to the Sons of Ether's concept, with it being some sort of spiritual force permeating anything to do with love and sex.)

                                And, naturally, every single one of these templates is a member of a Camp. Even if they don't make any mention of why they are a member or how they are supposed to express that Camp's (very vague) ideals.

                                Wise Fool (Lupus Ragabash)

                                Apparently the offspring of another Lupus Garou, which is presented as being a rarity. They were raised by the Tribe and learned how to be tolerant and understanding of other Tribes and Breeds. Their First Change was very non-traumatic.
                                Conceptually, they focus on using laughter and humor as a way to distract other Garou from their conflicts, concerns, and pains. This includes being perfectly willing to make themselves the butt of jokes when necessary.
                                They make friends very easily, are charming and loyal, eager to help others, and extremely patient.

                                Equipment includes juggling balls, a cloak of many colors, marbles, bits of string, a chew toy, and a kite. The balls, cloak, and chew toy I can get behind. Maybe even the kite and marbles. Bits of string seem trivial. Like telling me that a character's equipment includes 63 cents in pocket change. I also feel like said cloak should be a fetish of some sort.

                                The image is of a Garou in Crinos form, with said cloak , their hair (such as it is) pulled back into what looks like they're hoping to grow out into a man bun at some point, and juggling a pair of balls. I honestly kind of hate it. Mainly because it looks like a Garou trying to pretend to be a furry. There is none of the sense of Rage or danger I expect from the Garou war form, even taking the Tribe's weakness into account.

                                As I mentioned, all of these characters have a Camp listed, and this one's is the Aethera Inamorata. I have no clue as to why. Nothing about the character's write up mentions anything about sex or using it as a method of spiritual healing. Nor about helping arrange liaisons and marriages between Garou and Kinfolk. About the closest you get is the character's focus on laugher as an expression of love, perhaps. I don't think this character needs to be part of a Camp. But if you were to force me to put them in one, I think The Patient Deed would have made more sense.

                                For Attributes, Physicals are tertiary with an extra dot from freebies, for a 2/3/2 spread. Socials are primary with 4/3/3. Mental is secondary with 2/2/4.
                                These seem perfectly fine. I do appreciate some attention being paid to Manipulation, with some acknowledgement that the character is manipulating others on a certain level. I might even go so far do say that a 4/4/2 spread for Socials wouldn't have been out of place here.

                                For Abilities, Talents are primary with an extra dot from freebies. They have 3 each in Alertness, Athletics, and Empathy, a 2 in Primal Urge, and 1 each in Brawl, Dodge, and Streetwise.
                                Skills are secondary with a 3 in Performance, 2 each in Leadership and Survival, and 1 each in Animal Ken and Stealth.
                                Knowledge is tertiary, with 3 in Enigmas and 2 in Medicine.
                                So, Medicine is one of the restricted abilities for Lupus. While I can sort of understand why this character might have learned such an ability, it should've been bought with freebies. I think those points would work better in Investigation and/or Rituals, and both would fit certain aspects of the character (both the Ragabash Auspice and the character's interest in learning about others for the former; their having grown up entirely among Garou for the former).
                                I think that dot of Streetwise might do better as Subterfuge, owing to what I mentioned earlier about the character being manipulative to a certain degree. I do appreciate them having Leadership. It's a nice change of pace for a Ragabash after a lot of the 1st ed material. I think they might also benefit slightly from having some degree of Expression.

                                Backgrounds are 5 dots in Allies. I find this disappointing, especially after Bone Gnawers overused that background. Like I said before, I think the cloak mentioned under equipment might've made the interesting basis for a Fetish of some sort. And given the mention of the character's mother being Garou (as well as saying this was rare), Pure Breed would also seem like a fitting Background here. Also Mentor given how the character has effectively been raised by Garou all their life.

                                This is the first character to have spent freebies (seven of them) on a fourth Gift. I'm not entirely sure why they need the extra Ragabash gift (they have both Blur of the Milky Eye and Open Seal). Neither feels overly necessary for the character concept, even if I can see how both might be useful in play.

                                Rage is 2. Gnosis is 5. Willpower is 4. I don't think that extra dot of Rage is necessary. If anything, it might work against the character concept.

                                I really feel those eight freebie points could've been better spent on Abilities and Backgrounds. Maybe also a slight WP boost.

                                So, I do not hate this character. I'm not entirely certain that I like it either, but part of that may be because of the artwork. Overall, I think this concept works fairly well with the Tribe, and for certain aspects of the Auspice. (It certainly moves closer to Questioner of the Ways than some of the earlier generic trickster or gutter punk concepts.) I'm not really sure why the character is a Lupus, even if that Breed perhaps works a bit better for this than the other two might. (Lupus are probably more likely to open up to another Lupus, and social prejudice against Metis might end up working against it.) And I see no reason why it has to be part of a Camp, let alone the one selected. So, this is probably a better start that I might have feared given the Tribe's reputation and stereotypes, especially in the early days.

                                    It's a weak template, but without anything too objectionable. The name is Wise Fool, but something like Wisecracker, Class Clown, or Company Joker would probably be better as the entire concept is about his ability to make others laugh. It seems heavily based on the idea that pack omegas clown around and help disperse any stress or tension within the pack, but without emphasizing the omega part.

                                    The problem is someone making the rest of the pack (or sept) laugh is more like a personality quirk you add to a character, not the basis of one. What exactly does this character bring to the pack during play? What kind of things does the character do? Is he an infiltrator and uses his sense of humor to gain trust? Is he really oriented into Garou society and going to embed himself into the sept and going towards a leadership position within the Garou? Is it his athletic skills that are going to come into play like an acrobatic burglar (if so he needs more Stealth)? As is, the character just seems to be the type that really needs to be given direction by the other PCs and told what to do. It's a weak hook.

                                    As written, it seems to be that his ability to make people laugh and always helping others is what gets him Allies, and his 5 dots of Allies is the key. He's the guy who knows a guy that can help them. But I don't think this aspect really works. Simply because people like you doesn't mean they are going to stick their neck out for you, which is what Allies means. This character had to have done something important for each of these Allies, and we're not given any idea of what that could be. It's also very strange for a lupus to have so many Allies. Certainly not impossible, but it means a very human society centered lupus. Maybe it's wilderness centered humans - park rangers, veterinarians, rescue shelters, and other people he might have met and helped by volunteering in a lot of groups doing things for animals and the wilderness. Some groups are desperate for volunteers, and a hard working wisecracker type that makes things fun would help retain and attract more volunteers. That and being dependable might make some people willing to help that person out, especially if it is for things closely related to their own activity. But there's nothing in the description that would lead one to this direction.

                                    It's a consistent problem with these Templates to give the character Allies but not ​provide any description of how they were obtained or what kind of things they should do. I understand many Players will want to customize these characters, but there needs to be a bridge.

                                                        Supernatural Investigator (Homid Theurge)

                                                        So, this guy grew up with a real fascination - obsession even - with stories about otherworldly creatures and supernatural beings. Especially movies about vampires and other creatures which implied that said beings weren't necessarily evil and could even be the good guys. (Too bad this predates the Buffy tv show; this guy would've been a super-fan of that.) Apparently his First Change happened while he was laying in bed reading by moonlight. And I thought the Bone Gnawer Hitchhiker's First Change was pretty chill. Yeesh.
                                                        He started "nocturnal missions" out into the supernatural world to meet all the creatures he used to read about. Exactly when he was found by other Garou, did his Rite of Passage, and joined the Children of Gaia is not mentioned at all.
                                                        And then things take a turn for the cringe.

                                                        Apparently he met a bunch of "highly moral" vampires, naturally of Clan Gangrel. I am going to pretend that these vampires are Edge and Christian of the WWE (points if you get the joke there) because that will make me laugh rather than cry at this next bit. They introduced him to a bunch of other vampires, and they're totally helping him look for this legendary vampire who supposedly transcended vampirism. I guess this means Saulot, in which case all the stuff that eventually came about in Vampire's metaplot just makes that even funnier.

                                                        He's also met fairies, mages, ghosts, demons and lots of other stuff. And he's decided that the ones that are selfish are Wyrm oriented, while the ones who actually care about others are for Gaia. I can't exactly fault that sort of reasoning, but I have this suspicion that non-selfish vampires are perhaps a bit scarce. Demons even more so. So the fact that this guy seems to think that a lot of vampires are A-OK and have a lot to teach the Garou makes me question his level of awareness.

                                                        Yes, this entire book has this weird fixation on vampires as being an antithesis of life and Gaia while at the same time being super important because some of them can reach Golconda and that's something Garou need to learn from. And I think the main reason this character exists is because of that.

                                                        Anyway, he's apparently building alliances with all of these other supernatural beings to help the Garou.

                                                        ::Sigh::

                                                        For equipment, he's got a cell phone, a trench coat (of course he does, but I'll get to that in a minute), a pistol, a miniature infrared camera, a pocket tape recorder, and a laptop.

                                                        The image is of a guy in a trench coat with what looks like a cigarette in his mouth and his hands in his pockets. The face looks like some mash up of Fox Mulder of the X-Files and John Constantine from DC/Vertigo's Hellblazer. His eyes look rather odd, like maybe he's high or something. It's obvious that Mulder and Constantine are the primary inspirations behind this character.

                                                        He's a member of the Patient Deed camp. I only know this because it's on the character sheet. There's nothing at all in the write up about it. Or even about his Tribe.

                                                        For Attributes, Physicals are tertiary with 2/2/2. Socials are primary with 4/3/3. Mental is secondary with an additional dot from freebies for 3s across the board.
                                                        While I realize that this guy seems to be a friendly "get out there and meet people" sort of person, I feel like he'd be better served with Socials and Mental being switched.

                                                        For Abilities, Talents are primary, with 3 each in Empathy and Streetwise, 2 each in Alertness and Brawl, and 1 each in Intimidation, Primal Urge, and Subterfuge.
                                                        Skills are tertiary, with a 2 in Firearms and 1 each in Drive, Melee, and Stealth.
                                                        Knowledge is secondary with one extra dot from freebies, with a 4 in Enigmas, a 3 in Occult, and 1 each in Politics and Rituals. (And yes, he is missing a dot in Knowledges, as at least two freebies were spent on that fourth dot in Enigmas.)
                                                        I will point out that we have a Supernatural Investigator who has no points in Investigation. I'd say that maybe a more proper name might be Supernatural Diplomat, but he has such low Politics and Subterfuge, and no Etiquette.

                                                        Backgrounds are 3 in Allies (I presume his Gangrel buddies), and 2 in Rites. I am just going to guess he has Rites because he's a Theurge and they all have to have that just because they are Theurges.
                                                        You would think that with as many different supernatural creatures this guy has met, he might at least have some Contacts. But no.

                                                        Rage is 2. Gnosis is a whopping 5 (that's where the bulk of his freebies went). Willpower is 4.

                                                        I really don't care for this one. It's not the alleged concept. A paranormal investigator is a potentially cool idea for a Theurge. Perhaps better suited to the Uktena or maybe Silent Striders (especially if focused on ghosts), but still good. But this really isn't that. He's essentially a werewolf who hangs out with vampires. Honestly, between that and the totally lack of any mention of his Tribe in the write up, I feel like I'm reading something that should be in the Ronin chapter of WoD: Outcasts. This feels like something that started with the whole X-Files "I want to believe" idea (which isn't a bad idea) and quickly veered off the rails with the Vampire crossover stuff and turned into a very weak and toothless attempt at Hellblazer.
                                                        I think the character freebies are largely wasted on such a high Gnosis score, especially considering how lacking his Abilities are in certain areas.
                                                        So this one is just broken, both conceptually and mechanically.

                                                            Supernatural Investigator (Homid Theurge)

                                                            So, this guy grew up with a real fascination - obsession even - with stories about otherworldly creatures and supernatural beings. Especially movies about vampires and other creatures which implied that said beings weren't necessarily evil and could even be the good guys. Apparently his First Change happened while he was laying in bed reading by moonlight. And I thought the Hitchhiker's First Change was pretty chill.
                                                            He started "nocturnal missions" out into the supernatural world to meet all the creatures he used to read about. Exactly when he was found by other Garou, did his Rite of Passage, and joined the Children of Gaia is not mentioned at all.
                                                            And then things take a turn for the cringe.

                                                            Apparently he met a bunch of "highly moral" vampires, naturally of Clan Gangrel. I am going to pretend that these vampires are Edge and Christian of the WWE (points if you get the joke there) because that will make me laugh rather than cry at this next bit. They introduced him to a bunch of other vampires, and they're totally helping him look for this legendary vampire who supposedly transcended vampirism. I guess this means Saulot, in which case all the stuff that eventually came about in Vampire's metaplot just makes that even funnier.

                                                            He's also met fey, mages, ghosts, demons and lots of other stuff. And he's decided that the ones that are selfish are Wyrm oriented, while the ones who actually care about others are for Gaia. I can't exactly fault that sort of reasoning, but I have this suspicion that non-selfish vampires are perhaps a bit scarce. Demons even more so. So the fact that this guy seems to think that a lot of vampires are cool and have a lot to teach the Garou makes me question his level of awareness.

                                                            Yes, this entire book has a rather odd take on vampires, being bad but also Garou need to learn about Golconda or something.

                                                            Anyway, he's apparently building alliances with all of these other supernatural beings to help the Garou.

                                                            For equipment, he's got a cell phone, a trench coat (of course he does, but I'll get to that in a minute), a pistol, a miniature infrared camera, a pocket tape recorder, and a laptop.

                                                            The image is of a guy in a trench coat with what looks like a cigarette in his mouth and his hands in his pockets. The face looks like some mash up of Fox Mulder of the X-Files and John Constantine from DC/Vertigo's Hellblazer. His eyes look rather odd. It's obvious that Mulder and Constantine are the primary inspirations behind this character.

                                                            He's a member of the Patient Deed camp. I only know this because it's on the character sheet. There's nothing at all in the write up about it. Or even about his Tribe.

                                                            For Attributes, Physicals are tertiary with 2/2/2. Socials are primary with 4/3/3. Mental is secondary with an additional dot from freebies for 3s across the board.
                                                            While I realize that this guy seems to be a friendly "get out there and meet people" sort of person, I feel like he'd be better served with Socials and Mental being switched.

                                                            For Abilities, Talents are primary, with 3 each in Empathy and Streetwise, 2 each in Alertness and Brawl, and 1 each in Intimidation, Primal Urge, and Subterfuge.
                                                            Skills are tertiary, with a 2 in Firearms and 1 each in Drive, Melee, and Stealth.
                                                            Knowledge is secondary with one extra dot from freebies, with a 4 in Enigmas, a 3 in Occult, and 1 each in Politics and Rituals. (And yes, he is missing a dot in Knowledges, as at least two freebies were spent on that fourth dot in Enigmas.)
                                                            I will point out that we have a Supernatural Investigator who has no points in Investigation. I'd say that maybe a more proper name might be Supernatural Diplomat, but he has such low Politics and Subterfuge, and no Etiquette.

                                                            Backgrounds are 3 in Allies (I presume his Gangrel buddies), and 2 in Rites. I am just going to guess he has Rites because he's a Theurge and they all have to have that just because they are Theurges.
                                                            You would think that with as many different supernatural creatures this guy has met, he might at least have some Contacts. But no.

                                                            Rage is 2. Gnosis is a whopping 5 (that's where the bulk of his freebies went). Willpower is 4.

                                                            I really don't care for this one. It's not the alleged concept. A paranormal investigator is a potentially cool idea for a Theurge. Perhaps better suited to the Uktena or maybe Silent Striders (especially if focused on ghosts), but still good. But this really isn't that. He's essentially a werewolf who hangs out with vampires. Honestly, between that and the totally lack of any mention of his Tribe in the write up, I feel like I'm reading something that should be in the Ronin chapter of WoD: Outcasts. This feels like something that started with the whole X-Files "I want to believe" idea (which isn't a bad idea) and quickly veered off the rails with the Vampire crossover stuff and turned into a very weak and toothless attempt at Hellblazer.
                                                            I think the character freebies are largely wasted on such a high Gnosis score, especially considering how lacking his Abilities are in certain areas.
                                                            So this one is just broken, both conceptually and mechanically.
