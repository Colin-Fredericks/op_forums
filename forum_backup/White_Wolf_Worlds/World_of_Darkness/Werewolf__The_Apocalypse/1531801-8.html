The supplement market makes money, but it's greater purpose is keeping customers engaged with your game with new material than not. People that buy lots of supplements are more likely to buy more core books later.

                                                    Originally posted by MrornX View Post

                                                    I mean the easiest answer is to sell supplements. Despite what the corebook author said or intended, the blank spaces in the book will be filled in by supplements sooner or later...

                                                    Also appenly a H5 supplemet actually goes against the corebook themes what with that? and what does that say about Shattered nation?
                                                    Not quite. The example I would cite here is the way Justin Achilli talked about developing H5 in this interview with Outstar. Now this comes with two caveats: We know that Paradox mandates certain things for WOD5E like the heavy focus on street-level or specific things like de-emphasizing the Umbra in W5. But Justin Achilli never made clear to what degree Paradox influenced the development-process. The other thing is that his explanation for why the Get were turned into antagonists turned out to be false, so I guess any statement of his must therefore be taken with a grain of salt.

                                                    But the example I specifically have in mind is his idea of "Sober Play". It's the idea that all the other splats are controlled by some force, the Beast in the case of Vampire, Rage for Werewolf etc. But since H5 is about mortal hunters there is nothing that influences their actions. The characters have essentially free will (unlike a Vampire with Humanity 4 or less, for example). Now that was the idea... What this meant in praxis, though, was that H5 didn't have an equivalent for Humanity and with that the trifecta of things attached to this VtM-mechanic, Convictions, Chronicle-Tenets and Touchstones were also removed and downscaled. Convictions are gone, Chronicle-Tenets no longer influence the game and Touchstones now only matter if a character has none. There's no upside to having Touchstones in H5, only the downside of a character getting punished if they have no Touchstone.

                                                    Of course, you only have to look to the CofD (and specifically Hunter the Vigil) to realize that the answer is to find something equivalent to Humanity that works for mortal hunters (in the case of HtV it's a trait called Integrity) rather than just ditching the concept of a morality-mechanic as a whole. The idea wasn't to say "We're leaving room open for some supplement to fill this space.", the development-team just decided to reject a key-part of V5's design in the most straightforward manner imaginable - by removing it and replacing it with nothing. Never understood where that impulse came from to reject V5's design like that but it was there.

                                                    Another example, Pack-Types... you'd think it would be a no-brainer to take Coterie-Types and develop something equivalent for W5. Instead, they went with this caern-management-system. And once again, this wasn't a choice of "Let's leave this to the supplements." because rather the caern-management was giving priority over having something equivalent to Coterie-Types. From pg. 191: "As a game, Werewolf: The Apocalypse assumes that the players’ characters will have their own caern, or that their pack will belong to the sept of a specific caern.". Instead of the variety Coterie-Types/Pack-Types could potentially offer, W5 instead just "assumes" you gonna end up managing a caern while playing.
