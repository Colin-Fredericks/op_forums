                                    I got in the forum today after seeing the post on the World of Darkness site about the release.
                                    My irritation was rather high after reading the post on that site.
                                    It left a bad taste in my mouth.
                                    There was this idea in that article that getting rid of any cultural ties for the Tribes was objectively a good thing because this way "You needn’t worry about being locked into playing a particular “type” based on your or your character’s origin, or misrepresenting or appropriating another culture through your tribe choice."

                                    Which I took as if normally I should worry if I wanted to play a Native American or any other culture or ethnicity character that wasn't my RL one.
                                    And also that I really really shouldn't to begin with, because that would be risk of misrepresenting or appropriating I should worry about...
                                    By this logic since I'm Italian I should only ever play Italians and say nay to any person that wants to play Italians at my table that aren't Italians because they wouldn't play them right.
                                    And here I was, thinking that RPGs were all about taking a role that isn't yourself in RL...

                                    But I digress.
                                    I just wanted to write that I'm sorry that Sambrano had to go through all that. Sambrano sounded like a reasonable person, one that aknowledged that even if the '90s version had objective flaws it still did good in a lot of ways because the message and the themes were there even if in a flawed way. Which was a good attitude and starting point, if you ask me.
                                    But while I'm sorry, I'm also glad that Sambrano took time to write down all that and put the events out in the open.
                                    And I'm happy for this.

                                    It lowered my personal irritation a bit. Because now that it's in the open, I hope it helps a few folks understand that avoiding any cultural ties is the shortsighted, boring, not-nuanced, uncool, easy and cowardly way to go
                                    It reminds me of an article about qipao becoming fashionable was wrong because it was cultural appropriation, then the same article reported that asian fashion designers were favourable about it but the article concluded that it was wrong and cultural appropriation anyway regardless of what asian designers said. Priceless. White Knighting intesifies, I'd say

                                    Once my sister said that cultures are meant to be shared and spread.
                                    This fear of cultural appropriation is taking a turn for the worse.
                                    I'm not from USA and one of the things that stimulated me in researching more proper knowledge on Native Americans in my life was WtA.
                                    But I guess I had the bare minimum of intelligence to not trust a game in educating me on real world stuff, and went for more serious sources.
                                    And don't get me started on how much new stuff about the real world and far away cultures and countries and religions I learned just thanks to a few throwaway names in MtAs used in an improper way...
                                    I guess for the time being we'll have less teenagers stimulated in researching about real life cultures by random and flawed notions about real cultures in their games, for fear that they could take those notions without a grain of salt

                                    I was almost convinced about buying the book, since WtA meant so much for me in the past.
                                    But this behind-the-scenes dispelled any uncertainity I had.
                                    This W5 edition can burn in hell for all I care.

                                    Edition Wars don't help the hobby and don't help selling books or having a stable community, so I must suppose Paradox didn't learn anything from the last 20 years of the hobby. Sambrano's team sounded like they learned that lesson at least.
