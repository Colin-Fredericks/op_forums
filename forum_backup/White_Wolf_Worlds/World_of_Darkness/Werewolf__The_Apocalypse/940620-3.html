    Personally the creation of things like the Ahadi does not mean that the shifters are 100% on the road to saving the world. You can still play a lot of in fighting and politicing if you decide to play an Ahadi game. Years ago in college my gaming group were playing 2nd edition WtA because we had no clue revised had come out (we were all working off one friend's collection of books). We created an American equivalent of the Ahadi before we knew it existed. We had politicing from within, the Ratkin despised the concept and constantly worked against it, the Fera didn't trust the Garou all that much, and the Garou felt they should have a greater say over the Fera because they were a bigger vote, Latin American, Caribbean, and Canadian shfiters were paranoid of American shifters pulling a First Among Equals move. Even then the Garou tribes didn't agree on the move with the Fangs and Shadow Lords using it as a wedge to gain influence among the other tribes. We had the organization clash with the Beast Courts in places like California where we decided a long history of Asian immigration would have produced an expansion of the courts into newer territory. When characters traveld abroad they got a new fresh dose of paranoia from the locals because they were convinced that the only reason the American shifters would band together was to be stronger as an "American" (as in U.S. and not the continents) and begin to take territory/impose their will on Fera from other parts of the world.

    The same thing can happen in the Ahadi, the Simba can shoot for too much power, or work to destroy it from with in, could use the Ajaba who are bent on vengeance to destroy it (have them still killing Simba then running to the Ahadi and claiming the lions are still unjustly looking for an excuse to hunt them), or the Ahadi could evolve to feel that by virtue of being a shifter in Africa, you ARE in the Ahadi and all of your bases belong to us!
