    Thoughts on population

    06-17-2022, 01:48 PM
    A lot of people will probably disagree with me, but I think the WTA setting works better if the largest population of Garou(and by extent other fera) are Lupus-born.

    Setting-wise, the garou social set-up doesn't make sense to me if the largest group of garou are initially human-raised before the first change. Homids are just going to bring their own cultures and ideas into the garou nation because that's what they grew up with and they aren't going to hold as closely to garou tradition because they didn't grow up with that. Lupus though? The first experience Lupus will have with full sapience will Be their initiation into the garou nation. it'll be the first things they're taught and the things they'll compare everything else to. Unlike homids, Lupus are More likely to hold onto garou traditions the way the nation as a whole is painted as doing in the lore.

    It also works much better mathmatically as well. Even going by the rule that says garou only have a 10%+ chance(depending on pure breed) of being born each breeding cycle and only 1 pup will be born garou, Wolves still produce litters of Kinfolk, all of who have a 1%+ chance(depending on pure breed) of producing garou after only growing up for 2 years. Kin populations also have the "evolutionary" advantage of being watched over by garou, so they have a much higher chance of survival than normal wolves. Finally, Lupus Garou themselves have an exponentially longer lifespan compared to normal wolves, and wolves are known to move on to choose other mates when one dies. Statistically speaking, Lupus have a much higher chance of producing garou.

    No matter what angle I look at this, the massive canon Homid population just seems to contradict the logic of the setting itself.
