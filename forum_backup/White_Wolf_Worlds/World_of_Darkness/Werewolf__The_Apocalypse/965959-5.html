                                                            ​"Rite of the Parted Veil. Level five. This rite is the Children of Gaia's ultimate gift to Gaia-cherishing humans. When performed, the Veil is not pierced (as in the deadly Rite of Rending the Veil) but parts seamlessly to admit one or more humans (or wolves!). The person on whom the rite is performed thus becomes Kinfolk. Two Children unrelated to the human must witness that he loves Gaia and would aid the Children's cause. Most often this rite is done for mates of Garou or Kin. Some of the Patient Deed say that they hope to extend the rite to whole nations. The rite consists of the Garou dancing round the subject, while the ritemaster chants from the Songs of Welcome. A the rite progresses, the Garou slowly shapeshift, until they assume the Crinos form without frightening the human.
