    Originally posted by CeltSPZ View Post

    Beat me to it. The changing breeds are half spirit and for that reason alone, I am an advocate of the Nuwisha being their own distinct breed. Of we toss the animism and real world cultural inspirations out in in favor of just cold hard science, we miss out on a lot of the game.
    My problem with this specific call, is that the Garou are not one single werewolf myth. Like most of the WoD games, the PC groups are meant to be expansive and cover a wide variety of mythological inspirations.

    I mean, we have Ragabash trickster werewolves already? We have werewolves that can follow Coyote as a Totem? The idea that we're missing out on something by making coyotes part of the Garou doesn't really seem that... we're missing anything? It just means "trickster coyotes" are a flavor of Garou that doesn't overlap with a flavor of non-Garou Fera. It's not like we can actually make every cultural interpretation of these things work together perfectly anyway.

    It's also kinda jarring when some Fera are restricted to very specific animals (the Kitsune and Nuwisha the most notable) while others are insanely expansive (There are 35K+ species of spiders for the Ananasi to cover). Even in just a half-spirit sense, why are the incredibly disparate Bastet all one group? Qualmi and Simba have... what in common besides being felids? If the Bastet can cover Jaguar inspirations from South America, and Bast inspirations from Egypt, how are the Garou covering the Nuwisha and Kistune as localized groups or whatever removing anything from the game to missed?
