                The Sept is a social unit, the Caern is the physical space. The Heart of the Caern is the only part where even Glass Walkers would find a lack of solemnity exceedingly inappropriate. Most Glass Walker Septs probably have a nice cafe somewhere on the Caern's Bawn as it is. This is just a logical extension of how most Septs build dining amenities in the Bawn to cook up feasts for Caern Rites that either require them or are celebrated with them (The Moot Rite itself being a prime example): you want those things in places that are secured from the outside world, not beyond the Bawn in the Sept's larger Protectorate where the Veil needs to be enforced even if Garou and Kin have worked hard to have a strong control over it.

                Also the Glass Walkers tend not to "beat" consumerism out of their members. One of the Revised signature characters is a Homid Glass Walker Theurge who's regularly depicted in a business suit with heels.. and nobody - at least within the Tribe - gives her shit about it.

                Lastly, it's the ST's game. If the valley girl GW Galliard is a fit in the ST's interpretation of the setting, it's not our place to question that. The OP didn't say that there was any problems from IC conflicts stemming from the character's behaviors, but OoC conflicts because people were talking shit behind backs about the character.

                            Oh 100% I'm on board with Valley-girl glass walkers, but 100% you'll need to modify it to make it work.
                            1) If you're 100% on board with the valley-girl GW, then the idea that you 100% have to modify it makes no sense.
                            2) None of what you're saying is actually addressing the OP's character as related to us, or the situation as related to us.

                            I expect a Garou consumer to be a socially conscious.
                            The PC was already explained to be an online influencer type that used that reach to sway human consumption. That business requires at least looking the part in public because being caught not following your own advice is a great way to tank as an influencer.

                            Let's also not skip over the influence your elders will have in educating you to be part of Garou society.
                            Let's not skip that Mentor is a discouraged Background for Glass Walkers (and formerly a banned one before discouraged was added to the game) because GW traditions are designed specifically to diminish the power of Elders to stop young Garou with new ideas from flourishing. It's one of the Tribe's biggest saving graces: they actively avoid stifling the next generation (it's why their name has changed three times), and actually provide lots of material support thanks to all of that Weaver-loving stuff they do so young GWs can actually do things in new arenas of the battle against the Wyrm as they crop up instead of trying to figure out how to fight in a new space and raise the resources to do it at the same time.

                            GWs, because of their status as an urban primary Tribe, don't leave education of cubs and Cliaths to Elders. They have specialists that have proven track records in on-boarding new Garou quickly because living in the city doesn't leave them time to slowly indoctrinate fresh Garou into how thing work. Even GW raised by kin-in-the-know still need rapid response education: they have(/had) lives in human society that will notice if they're missing for too long. They need to not break the Veil, but exist with human (and digital) eyes all around them.

                            GW Elders need to peddle their own influence later after GW education teams do their jobs, by offering support for initiatives new GWs come up with, rather than expecting to just toss their Rank around from the get go.

                            I suspect being able to behave like that is the result of being sheltered and privileged, but like... are Garou privileged that way?
                            According to the material on both the Silver Fangs and Glass Walkers? The ones that want their families to be can make sure that's that case, yes.

                            Garou culture is violent and heavily geared towards personal achievements in the form of honour, glory and wisdom, the pavlov's dog or WTA. Galliards don't need honour but neither glory nor wisdom mesh with this kind of behaviour.
                            1) The books explicitly state that Renown is generally considered by pack, no individual, behavior. Being the distraction that lures a fomori into an Ahroun's teeth gets the same Renown despite not delivering the killing blow. It's specifically geared to group achievements, not personal ones.
                            2) Galliards are screwed over by the Renown charts regardless of other character quirks. If it wasn't for packs getting Renown as a group, Galliards would be unplayable in group play because they'd be vastly behind everyone else that can get lots of renown just doing their archetypal Auspice stuff.
