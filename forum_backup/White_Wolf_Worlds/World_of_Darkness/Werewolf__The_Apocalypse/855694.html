    How does Frenzy work in W20?

    03-30-2016, 10:48 AM
    Hello, here it come other questions from a rooky Werewolf ST.

    1) Reading on page 261-262 it says any Rage roll with 4+ successes sends into Frenzy. Will it be a Berserk or Fox Frenzy?
    I guess the answer is the ST decides based on the stimulus, BUT: it doesn’t make much sense to me a character a character with Rage 10 has more changes to Fly then a character with Rage 1 when facing a scary situation.

    On page 436, the victim of Roar of the Wyrm resists the Fox Frenzy with a Will Power roll (WP makes more sense for a Fox Frenzy roll to me than Rage).
    It would also make sense to me a character gets into Fox Frenzy when fails or a Rage roll, in a fashion of Fight or Fly reaction.

    Question 1a) Does a character get into Fox Frenzy when he gets 4+ success on a Rege roll while facing a scary situation?
    Question 1b) If the answer to 1a is "yes" is it a non-sense in the rules or I don't understand what Rage is in this game? If the latest, please, explain to me.

    2) When the character gets the roll to get into Frenzy can spend a single WP point to prevent the Frenzy to happen, but if he lets the Frenzy start using WP only allows the character to choose the targets. Is this correct?

    3) Half of my PCs have higher Rage than Gnosis, this means they'll attack their pack mates while in Frenzy. Is there any mechanic in the game allowing the character to end the Frenzy or it will only end when he kills all the other PCs or the other PCs kill/KO him?
    In Vampire, the character has to get 5 successes in a Self-Control extended roll.

    4) In Full Moon the Frenzy difficulty for an Ahroun is 3. Moots are always run during Full Moon. Does this means moots always end with a significant number of dead Garous?

              A few other things:

              Remember that Rank increases Frenzy difficulties The game generally expects you not to get to large levels of Rage until you start Ranking up, which helps most of the established Garou keep themselves in check. Higher Ranking Garou are generally going to be more capable of stopping a Cliath or Fostern that's losing it.

              There's also a Gifts/Fetishes/etc. that can be used to tamp down on things if tensions are high and lots of high Rage characters are around.

                          Originally posted by OPP, on pg. 261 of W20,
                          A werewolf can only come out of frenzy once the triggering situation is over. Once he’s escaped, the player rolls Willpower (difficulty equaling the Garou’s own Rage) to escape the frenzy. If the roll fails, the player can try again next turn with no increase in difficulty.
                          So the answer to #3 is "It depends on what triggered the Frenzy in the first place." If your Packmate triggered your Frenzy, it will continue at least until your Packmate is no longer a valid target, whether through their death, your death, or them being removed from your presence. If a Wyrm-thing triggered your Frenzy, your Packmates are perfectly safe unless they present themselves as a target such as by getting in your way or standing next to you when the Wyrm-thing goes down.

                                                          There have to be differences between be constantly fighting against your inner fury, and actually use it as a weapon. Werewolves and Vampires have arguably similar conditions on this, but almost completely distinct takes on the matter.
