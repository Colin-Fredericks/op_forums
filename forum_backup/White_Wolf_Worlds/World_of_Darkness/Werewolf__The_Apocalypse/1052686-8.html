    Of course, there's always a huge danger of painting the situation with too wide a brush.

    There are vast differences between clans, sects, local politics, and even individuals. There's going to be a huge difference in the way a Silent Strider reacts to a Follower of Set that sold Blood Magic'd drugs to his Kinfolk kid than a Glass Walker running into a Ventrue covering up a Masquarade violation.

    Heck, even the Sabbat knows the danger of infernalism and ancient horrors waking up to eat the world, and if a Baali and a BSD start to cooperate in waking up an Earthbound, it might well take Tzimisce and Shadow Lords working together to stop them.
