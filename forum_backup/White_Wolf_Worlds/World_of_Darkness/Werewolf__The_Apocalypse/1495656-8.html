    To add some (possibly?) useful info and ideas about the Wod5:

    -drivethrurpg has a primarily "Anglosaxon world" user base, to get a complete picture we would need to look at overall sales numbers (or licensing fees) for translations (retailers, shops and fairs at least here in Europe are still quite big as distribution channels, also because often they have their own e-commerce): we know both Wod20 and Wod5 have international localization and distribution agreements, even though the number of supplements localized and the timing of localizations may differ a lot. Cofd, as far as I know, only stands in Pod format, whereas "old" Nwod had retail agreements here and there.

    -Renegade surely has some numbers on its own as a direct seller.

    -Nonetheless, surely H5 has started with way less traction than Hunter Vigil 2E or 1E, that's pretty much evident from what we see on drivethrurpg, icv2 and Amazon: I don't think there were huge numbers sold outside USA / UK through Renegade shops, as they are fairly new.

    -International localizations will be probably make the difference (at least as far as brand recognition is concerned, of course direct sales are better for profits), as Vigil 2E is only easily available on Pod (and in this current moment non Anglosaxon customers have negative incentives to buy on drivethrurpg, due to shipping and currency rates). To make some comparison, Vigil 2 sold 2000 copies on Kickstarter, and surely many other Pod copies on drivethrurpg, along with some retail copies for shops who got a print run in the Kickstarter.

    2000 copies is the first print run of a projected-to-be successful RPG corebook on the italian market, which is relevant in Europe but not the biggest (dungeons & dragons stands on 3000 copies on each book, moderately successful runs are on 1000 copies each, indie press may be smaller). H5 has been translated and should arrive on italian market in 2023: even if it hits shelves with a very modest 500-copies run, if Paradox can get it distributed with similar agreements in Germany, France, Spain and some northern countries, it would be in reach of Vigil numbers.

    So, I'd say that probably the relevant factor here will be the strenght of Paradox and Renegade international agreements.


    -I've tried to take a look at Paradox financial reports for 2021 and 2020. Paradox is a huge company, listed also in Nasdaq, and of course its primary revenue comes overwhelmingly from internet games: "Other" product revenues, which are the RPGs business area, stand at 16.712.000 SEK in 2021 and 11.040.000 SEK in 2020, roughly 1.537.500 Euros in 2021 and 1.015.680 in 2020.

    Paradox also controls a "World of darkness LLC" in delaware, which is her only Licensing company, even though Wod20 and Cofd are not their only licensees (they have Fria Ligan making licensed boardgames based on their videogames, for instance, and I also suppose they have some merchandising agreements). Licenses and Brand revenues stand at 304.587 SEK in 2020 and 232.480 SEK in 2021 (roughly 28.022 Euro in 2020 and 21.388 Euro in 2021). Licensing revenues are of course a relatively tiny share of what you earn from the original products, we'd need to know what the agreements are.

                Originally posted by Manfr View Post


                They might just take the Vampire Clan route, so global entities with some favoured local ties and birthplaces. So far, they've done quite a good job detaching them from excessively cultural roots.
                Making everything in WoD increasingly culturally agnostic.. is just completely idiotic in my opinion.

                What makes WoD interesting is how all the various splats intertwine with world history and environment. Hence the calling-card moniker "World of Darkness". It's a take on our already dark IRL earth history made even more darker with the introduction of all these fictional high-powered supernatural beings and organizations. That's what makes pre-WoD 5 such a draw to me over other urban fantasy series; WoD had a slight high fantasy feel to it, that also felt realistic (to an extent of course, as a lot of old WoD material has abjectly wacky stuff to be perfectly honest). Everything had connection with regards of where that supernatural was located.

                By divorcing all these connections and molding them to be more generic, as a means to play it excessively "safe", the weight behind of what made World of Darkness as a franchise diminishes. This path of genericality, aka "streamlined", is the reason why many just don't like CofD or how Parawolf is getting rid of a lot of metaplot material in WoD to suit whatever they feel is "legacy deadweight". No tweaking, no minor-to-moderate adjustments; just wholesale termination to all these interesting culturally intwined splats.

                This boring "street level" minimizing philosophy is going to make a large part of the WoD fanbase just give up on WoD (which has already happened for many).

                                                Just finished reading the Q&A and I'm still reading this thread but here's something that stuck out to me as odd when put together.

                                                Originally posted by Werewolf 5 Q&A with Justin Achilli
                                                Is there a reason the Fianna have the only non-English tribe name?
                                                Fianna is just a word, and it stayed because it gives a sense that werewolf cultures have occurred all around the world and been named in local languages. Much like "Garou" is French-based and "Gaia" is Greek, or in Vampire "Toreador," "Camarilla," etc. are Spanish but the greater concepts don't have any association with them. (I also like that "Fianna" helps break up the rest of the tribe names, which are more overtly "thing-doer" in construction.)
                                                I'm sure this was already talked about but, I find this incredibly insulting. Saying that Fianna is "just a word" and thus can be used freely without any cultural connections or meaning is very telling to how Justin thinks about the world. Especially give the last statement of 'Fianna is just Cool' (I do not apologize for the pun). Like even examples of the words chosen are tie what they mean back to the things they are in WoD. Garou is werewolf in French. Gaia is the Earth Mother in Greek myth. Toreador is a performer/artist. Camarilla is 'a small group of people, especially a group of advisers to a ruler or politician, with a shared, typically nefarious, purpose.' That's why these things are used the way they are if you just wanted to "use a word that sounded cool" you are missing the point.

                                                Originally posted by Werewolf 5 Q&A with Justin Achilli
                                                As Tribes ( is that word actually fitting at all now?) are completely distinct from any and all Human cultures and have no real points of origin anymore was there any consideration given to simply using Patrons as the third axis and doing away with tribes all together as they have no social or historical function any more?
                                                The social function of a tribe relates to being mentored or just having commonality with those of a shared perspective. Tribe is sociological and anthropological but not necessarily familial for the Garou.
                                                Tribe not being familial also isn't important to the separation from 'Human cultures.' Especially when you have also have this into the mix:

                                                Originally posted by Werewolf 5 Q&A with Justin Achilli
                                                If tribes are no longer really tied to cultures then how will they format more now? Are they more tied to specific ideologies or more so ethical viewpoints?
                                                From a game design perspective, tribes are driven by verbs, so the in-world ideology and culture emerges from those. Ethical outlooks are more complex, and each tribe certainly has different viewpoints centering around the what you do that tribes represent. Sharing a tribe with a Garou is no guarantee at all that you're going to get along with that Garou.
                                                If these people exist in the world and drive their ideology and culture into the world and it spreads into people that are not related to the tribe but still like the ideology and then teach that to their kids, etc. You still get a subsect of people that are tangentially related to the tribe that may first change that go back to the tribe and thus have a synergy with Tribal Cultures and Human Cultures. Are we just going to say that Garou have not a single ability to touch outside their supernatural bubble in any form? This is compounded by:

                                                Originally posted by Werewolf 5 Q&A with Justin Achilli
                                                Will the Fianna still have fey connections?
                                                Not as an inherent function, but the Fianna are very strongly tied to specific places, so if there's a fae spirit of that place, the relationship could definitely emerge.
                                                What specific places Justin? You don't mean Celtic regions do you Justin? That would be silly for me to derived that from what you previously said and the answer to this Q&A. Fianna sure is just a meaningless word that is tied to a specific place or people in anyway shape or form and just sounds cool and "break up the rest of the tribe names, which are more overtly "thing-doer" in construction." Even though if really if you look at all the names of the only tribes that are 'Thing-doer' are Bone Gnawer, GlassWalker, GaleStalker, and Silent Striders.
