        -sees a post on black spirals, checks the thread, sees a constant stream of "they're bad people and nothing more."- so I see attitudes haven't changed much over 20 years.

        The spirals are garou too and deserve to have their stories told. I for one would like to see more rituals, examples of rites of the hunt twisted, or other such (interestingly the minds eye theater book of the wyrm has a massive section on it).

        Sure botw20th did great things for the spirals, but let's not pretend it's the same thing as a tribe book. Botw2nd had a comprehensive history of the tribe up until the 90's, including corruption of other tribes, and it didn't have to go into dodgy rape detail.

        Clearly by modern times the tribe evolved to be less like the wolf monsters we knew in earlier editions, into something more akin to fantasy vampires, living in lofts and taking the human herd for granted. I for one would love to see the transition, to see the tension between old hardliner bsds and the new blood that believes it's already won.

        The fact that the majority of you (story tellers and players alike) cannot fathom a bsd tribe book as anything other than... What was it... A fecal pit? Is a bit showing. Where's your imagination? What about the spiral strike team fighting against pentex' mockery breeds? What of the spirals corrupting churches to spread the wyrm? I thought there was a subtly to wyrm corruption? The way people treat spirals here, you'd think they were all mindless monsters without any brain power behind their actions.

        Botw20th was fantastic for gifts, as well as modernizing and improving the tribe expanetionally (as 20th has been for everything else), but it didn't touch on the customs near as much as Id like. I find it hard to believe the entirety of their existence is "rape rape and more rape." You don't win wars like that.

        That's my 2 cents on the matter. I also may want really badass Ron spencer art of a spiral wearing a grizzly bear spirit.

            For the most part I was content reading this post but I do have to comment on this. As a GM I don't need a tribe book to spark my imagination when it comes to these sorts of plots. I've already ran plots with Spiral priests leading human cults, and had an arch where my players were stuck in the middle of a Anurana/Spiral fued (the frogs were stealing spiral kin). None of this requires a tribe book past what BotW has provided (with W20 really doing a great job at it compared to earlier versions). For me its a matter of keeping the Spirals separate. Are they werewolves? Yes, but a stand alone tribe book I feel would grant an authenticity to them that makes players go "See they are meant to be played", and from there I think would cause them to lose some of the evil. I don't need a tribe book to create 3D spirals for me, past source material and my own mind can do that without a problem (from the nihilist, to the Spiral who thinks she embodies the Garou's karma). I worry that a tribe book will try to humanize them too much and turn them in to "I'm just misunderstood" type concepts.

            For me the Spirals aren't "misunderstood outsiders" who need their tail told in anymore detail than the BotW, they're not Ronin, they are instead the monsters that go bump in the Garou's night. For that, along with the others stuff people mentioned (Just how evil the actions of Spirals can be) a tribe book isn't needed and probably shouldn't be. But that's my 2 cents.

                    I don't think it has ever been a question whether or not they are intelligent. BOTW20 showed that well enough. These are smart creatures, no doubt about it.

                    But why I think they should not be playable -especially- with the newest Wyrmbook, is where those smarts are used.

                    All the tribes in the garou Nation, even the darker ones like Shadow Lords or Red Talons, still do have a goal to save the earth. Yes, they all have a different take on it, and it can be bad for humanity. But they still want to do good in their own way.

                    BSDs, especially the botw20 ones, have the goal to worsen the world. No longer do they fight to redeem or rescue the wyrm. They do what they do to further the corruption of the world.

                    And, at least for me as a player, there is no joy in taking those actions. I corrupted a church, and now the people worshipping there are worse off. Yay? With the Gaians, you at least can try to make the world a better place, even if its covered in blood and offal.

                    Its the same reason why I don't really see the point of Sabbat games, I don't find joy out of makings things worse than they all ready are. And I think that's the reason why the WtA writers avoid making a BSD tb.

                    It has nothing to do with them being rapey or mindless monsters, Its what they stand -for-.
