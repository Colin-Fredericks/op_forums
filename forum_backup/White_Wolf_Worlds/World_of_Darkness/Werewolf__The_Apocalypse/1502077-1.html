        While coming up with my 'Unified Cosmology' theory for a more organized Umbra, I had to come up with some metaplot history to explain a few things. To reconcile the major differences between Demon and Werewolf, I felt that I had only one option. Namely, that the two worlds were originally two totally different places, but were now only one. Let me explain.

        When the Creator made the current WOD, there were some lingering traces from a previous reality--the world of Exalted. Some portions of that reality were incorporated into the new reality. One of these 'survivors' was Gaia. She was fine taking a backseat to the new World being made, but many of the forces of the old reality were causing problems. So, three buffer zones were made to protect the World--Wyld, Weaver, and Wyrm--each with a mini-reality of its own. When these proved to be not enough, three more zones were made to counteract them. Each one was dominated by two of these forces--Wyrd (Wyld/Wyrm), Whorl or Wellspring (Wyld/Weaver), and Wyrp (Weaver/Wyrm). It is the last that became the current physical world.

        The Wyrp was being heavily hit by negative forces. So much so that Gaia was tasked on finding a way to protect it and the World. She borrowed the idea of shape-shifters from her original reality, and she set up a way of including them into the Wyrp. She also adapted the few Dragon King survivors into this world as well. Meanwhile, the direct agents of the Creator, the Administrators or Angels, were beginning their war in the World with the faction who would become the Fallen. When the war was over, the forces aligned with the Creator won, but with a huge cost. The World was no longer inhabitable to humanity. A few had been sent away by the Fallen to the Weaver section, where they ultimately Awakened to become the Pure Ones. These would end up traveling to the Wyrp where they became the basis of the Avatars of later Mages.

        With the Fallen locked away, the Administrators began a mass evacuation of humanity to the realm closest to their original home, the Wyrp. However, although humanity was sent out of the World at about the same time, the lack of synchronicity between the realms at that time meant that they all arrived at different times. The last evacuees, 'Adam' and the recently pregnant 'Eve,' arrived years if not decades or longer after the first arrivals. By that time, the first evacuees had intermingled with the proto-humans who were native to the realm, changing and evolving both in the process, adding the earliest Mages as well who traveled under their own power. This was a big mistake on the Administrators part, but it couldn't be changed. With humanity safe, the World was rebuilt and the realms were more-or-less synchronized. By that time, there were way too many humans to bring back to the World, so they were allowed to remain on the Wyrp, which ultimately became the physical world of today.

        When the Fallen were freed from the Abyss prison, they were barred from the World, which has since become known as Midgard or Mount Qaf or many other names. They instead went to where humanity was, the Wyrp, as some had been summoned there over the millennia by humans. With the many changeover such a long period of time, many thought that the Wyrp was the World. Indeed, some battles of the rebellion had been fought there, but generally speaking, it wasn't the same world. Many of the Fallen's architecture, so to speak, was removed from the World and found itself attached to the Wyrp for no other reason than a similarity, as well as it being the new home to humanity. This is why the Fallen are so confused. They are misplaced in the current cosmology.

        As to Werewolf, they are mostly sure of the current standing of reality. However, the rarely go beyond the original Triat, and they totally ignore the secondary threesome, as in doing so would reveal their true natures and the truth of their world. Almost none have heard of Midgard, so that facet is really unknown. Gaia, the spirit, is growing sickly and weaker, but it is because she is alien to the current reality, not because of any imbalance in the world or other reasons. Humanity is not really the problem, but the introduction of 'perfected' humans may have been, as the Wyrp wasn't meant to be there home. Yes, the Middle Umbra is under attack by outside forces, but not in the way Garou believe.

        I'm abbreviated many parts of my theories so this doesn't get too long, and many parts are still under construction, such as what the Weaver aligned realm is. However, it does show that both game lines' viewpoints can be true and reconciled, but it takes a very big leap to do so.


        THWarted
