                                                            If I were to combine tribes, there are few I'd make two in a heartbeat.

                                                            Glass Walkers+Bone Gnawers- Glass Walkers is a great tribe written VERY well in revised, Bone Gnawers have great books but,well. As I've grown older, the concept of the Bone Gnawers has become far less tolerable. It isn't that garou cannot live in the slums, but the mentality of why and why that makes them lesser. The idea that the status among garou reflects status among humans is not only rather weird (since most tribes are made out of moochers to the kinfolk) but also has edges of 'well, they are poor because SPIRITUALLY they are destined to be poor.' Combination of these two could easily allow city-garou to swing from homeless theurges mumbling to ahroun trained by their military kinfolk.

                                                            Black Furies/Stargazers/Bone Gnawers+Children of Gaia- This is mostly because CoG just cannot get a break in writing. Every other tribebook has been at least usable, but CoG revised is worth its scorn. Combining them with Furies might give them a chance to be a decent camp.

                                                            As for the Stargazer combo, Stargazers fit in the same bracket of 'human-philosophy tribe' and they are generally seen as annoying as Coggies. Being a more global tribe might help their image.
