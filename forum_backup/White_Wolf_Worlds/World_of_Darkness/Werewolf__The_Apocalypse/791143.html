    Worldwide Wolf Population and Tribal Lupus

    12-22-2015, 07:24 PM
    I recently looked at the worldwide wolf population. I think we can figure out some basics of the lupus population in each tribe as a result. Worldwide wolf population is between 200,000 to 400,000. Numbers are only approximate as the data is inconsistent even in the same articles I read. The totals below support the lower limit of 200,000, but uncertainty certainly makes it possible for lots of unknown wolf stock that the Garou may be kept hidden. However, the overall ratios from region to region likely remain the same.

    General breakdown is:

    North America (78,000 total)
    Canada - 60,000
    Alaska - 12,000
    Rockies - 2000
    Great Lakes - 4000
    Other (Greenland, US Southwest) - 100

    Europe (15,000 total)
    Scandinavia (incl Finland) - 400
    Spain & Portugal - 2000
    Italy - 700
    Balkans - 6000
    Central Europe - 1200
    Belarus - 2000
    Ukraine - 2000
    Baltic - 1200

    Asia (90,000 total)
    Russia (including European Russia) - 30,000
    Turkey - 7000
    Central Asia - 40,000
    Mongolia - 10,000
    India - 1000
    China - 1000
    Middle East - 1000

    Africa (unknown, but populations include Egypt and Ethiopia)

    If we assume jackals are also possible to be kinfolk for Silent Striders and Bone Gnawers at least, then there are several hundred thousand of them as well.

    We can see the big areas for wolf population is northern North America (Canada and Alaska plus bits of northern US) and the Eurasian steppe and taiga (Russia, Central Asia, and Mongolia). That is 78,000 plus 80,000. Eastern Europe adds another 12,000. All the lupus will be concentrated there. What does this tell us based on known tribal populations?

    Red Talons, of course, are the tribe with the most lupus. Every member is one except for a few metis. Talons would mainly be limited to Russia and northern North America. Their vestigal populations in Europe (particularly western Europe) and southern Asia were probably lost long ago, although there could be a few septs here and there with one or two packs.

    Wendigo would be a tribe with lots of lupus. Perhaps the only tribe whose lupus population is around the same level of homids since Wendigo human kinfolk are also few in number. That the Wendigo may be the only tribe that is around 50/50 would be a good distinction for the tribe. It should probably be emphasized more.

    Silver Fangs and Shadow Lords would also have relatively larger amount of lupus than the average for the Garou. Their tribal homelands in Eurasia sit right on top one of the last few large populations of wolves. Probably not 50%, but they should have significantly more than 10%. Perhaps a 20-30% range. Let's give the Silver Fangs more than the Shadow Lords as proper for the eldest tribe of kings, so Silver Fangs more around 30% and Shadow Lords 20%.

    Those are the obvious choices. We now need to start making some assumptions.

    Stargazers may have more than the usual number of lupus. Their tribal homeland doesn't have many interlopers and they are right in the middle of China. India, and Central Asia. Given their small numbers canonically, a significant percentage may be lupus. Somewhere between 20-50%.

    Based on Geography, we can see both the Get of Fenris and Black Furies operate in European areas where there are wolves (stories of Amazons happen in Turkey and Central Asia, and the Germanic migrations once had many Germanic tribes in eastern Europe (Poland, Ukraine, Kievan Rus) as well as their current range in Scandinavia and Baltic). They are probably in the 10-20% range. They have lots of competitors though.

    Children of Gaia likely have a decent number of lupus with the main stock being in the Middle East, but with a few populations retained here and there elsewhere. Let's give them the canonical average of 10%.

    Silent Striders are in a bind. North Africa has almost no wolves. They may have access to some packs in the Middle East, India, and Balkans but are fighting for them against the Children, Silver Fangs, Black Furies, and a few others. Let's say they are near the overall average between 5-10%.

    That leaves the other tribes at lower than average. Naturally this would include the urban tribes of Glass Walkers and Bone Gnawers. They probably have close to 0%. Population may be in the dozens or fewer. Glass Walkers may have a special kennel breeding program trying to maintain the last few wolves of their tribal bloodline. The source is likely Italy where the Glass Walkers have had a long association since the time of Rome. Bone Gnawer lupus likely originate from India.

    What might be a surprise are the other two big losers - the Uktena and Fianna. Their traditional geographic homelands have almost no wolves. The Uktena may have access to a few wolves as charity from the Younger Brother Wendigo, but the Fianna are screwed unless they control wolf stock in the Iberian population from the days when the Celtic tribes ruled there. And they probably have lots of competition there. Interesting to speculate that if you are a lupus Fianna, your default "culture" is Spanish, not Celtic. For these tribes, lupus stock is probably between 0-5%.

    Of course, tribes are not strictly limited to the most natural territories. Moon bridges and immigration send tribes all over the world. We can determine that Canada (and northern US) likely is prime ground for the Tribes to fight over control of wolf kinfolk. I can see lots of tribes that don't have many (or any) wolves in their traditional homelands, emigrating to Canada and fighting for control of wolf stock. In fact, for some tribes (like the Fianna), this is probably the ONLY origin of wolf kinfolk. The whole country is probably in a prolonged struggle with Red Talons and Wendigo fighting Get of Fenris and Fianna as their main rivals, with the other tribes as interlopers. Maybe even the Glass Walkers and Bone Gnawers snatch a few lupus here and there. Given the prominence of Silver Fang wolf kin in Eurasia, I imagine the Silver Fangs mostly stay out of this fight let. They may have a few traditional wolf packs in these lands though that they brought over from Eurasia.
