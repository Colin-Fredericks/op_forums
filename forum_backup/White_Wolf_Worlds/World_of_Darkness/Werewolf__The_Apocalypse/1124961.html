    Garou Embraced before First Change

    08-04-2017, 05:58 PM
    If by chance (or complex design) a Garou were to be embraced before they underwent the First Change (the True Black Hand often kidnaps and indoctrinates children before the embrace, these are known as Chatterlings, and it is not unknown for them to attack and embrace Werewolves, just as example), would they become an Abomination?

    Before the First Change a Werewolf presumably doesn't possess a Gnosis trait, making them a prime target for embrace. Would they undergo the First Change after their Embrace? Would they be locked in a pre-Change state?

                                                  Ignoring semantics of the proper name for the new vampire, the real question is whether the vampire has the powers of the Garou. I would say no. It's like asking if a newly embraced caterpillar will become a vampire butterfly - biological processes stop upon death. At the time of the embrace, the pre-Change Garou cannot shapechange and does not have Rage or Gnosis. I see no reason why these powers would automatically happen.

                                                  Furthermore, mechanically there is the issue of Gnosis. A pre-Change vampire doesn't have any. An Abomination cannot increase Gnosis at all. I don't think you can have a Garou without Gnosis.

                                                  It is entirely possible that an assault by the vampire would trigger the First Change (in which case the survival of the vampire is called into question), but that would not be automatic and I can think all sorts of scenarios where that likely would not happen. A direct physical attack could well trigger a Garou's First Change. But I think it is less likely if it happens during the course of a seduction or while the victim is sleeping. This would be an ST ruling in an individual chronicle, not some obvious rule for STs to use in a standard game.
