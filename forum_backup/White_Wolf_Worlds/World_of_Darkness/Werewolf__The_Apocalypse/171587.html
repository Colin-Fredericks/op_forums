    Pentex Subsidiaries

    06-17-2014, 09:16 AM
    So I was reviewing BotW20, and looking at older material like "Subsidiaries: A Guide to Pentex", and thinking about other possible Subsidiaries worth exploring (or existent Subsidiaries worth expanding/updating).

    TruCall Holdings: Cable and Internet provider, that grew out of telephone service provider (hence the name); known for exploitative practices toward consumers that it can get away with thanks to the oligopolistic set up of the market. In addition it is instrumental in shaping access to information for its consumers and thus is heavily involved in Pentex's Public Relations for it and its Subsidiaries. It is through TruCall that Pentex owns its several television networks...

    -Omni TV: increasingly dominated by exploitative reality television ("Who Wants to Marry a [insert T word I won't write]?" "In Jail with the Octavians!" "Children of Africa" [about cute white hero children] "Sex Stars of Wallstreet!"....)
    -Omni Kids: mostly licenses franchise cartoons meant to push Avalon & Tellus products - biggest hit is Our Friendly Bunnies, via a fanatic adult male fanbase with heavily sexualized fan-works dominating fanspaces to keep out actual children the original creators were aiming for before Avalon & Omni moved in - a triumph in Avalon's quest for arrested development.
    -RED Network: whose pundits will always find time to rail against the filth on Omni; or in the adult film industry; so that Pentex can make money coming & going
    -Private Playground (aka "the Double-P"), a major adult entertainment studio, with its own pay per view network courtesy of TruCall; prides itself in constantly pushing the edge in degredation, for which point they seek out only the truly desperate in performers, so refusal can be pushed to a non-issue. [Double-P also has less legit subsidiary studios, and based on customer viewing habits knows who to link to those webpages... and then hold via blackmail leverage of their own digital footprints]

    AquaTek Aventures: This is Pentex Board Nominee "Mike's" idea that he shared on the kickstarter comments page. Short version: it's Sea World in the front, Projects Iliad & Lycaon with aquatic monstrosities in the back.

    Blackfire Security: Private military contractor that works closely with Nastrum. Go to mercenaries for dirty situations where Western governments and various powerful corporations don't want to use their own militaries/security-forces. Will outfit Pentex First Teams for the right price. (yes, I borrowed the name from the New World of Darkness)

    Eterent: An agrobusiness biotech company very close to actively wiping bees off the face of the earth with its pesticides. It has also found great legal versatility in the ability to patent dna, so that the spread of pollen from its plants allows the proprietary claiming of crops from farms that have never done business with Eterent. Its special strains of high fructose corn syrup is found in the majority of Young & Smith products.

    CSS Health & Education: Formerly "Community Security Solutions", CSS Health & Education is a private prison contractor. It's mandatory quotas in its contracts with the state guarantee a continuous greasing of the wheels of the incarceration industrial complex masquerading as a justice system in the US. These prisoners in turn provide cheap labor that several other Pentex subsidiaries actively utilize. Zettler has also been known to utilize the inmate population, who are already treated by their environment as little more than caged animals.

    GEO Dreams: an education reform company that has mastered the art of raiding teacher pensions, and the money tied to each student, pushing standardized testing built on the bedrock of the eugenics movement, and franchising some of the most truly bizarre charter school programs in the Wyrm's service you could ever dread to imagine.

    *Herrick's: instead of a grocery store, Herrick's should really have expanded by this point to be Pentex's WalMart.

              Some way to exploit the microfinance / microloan movement to spread corruption while bathing in charity donations from people who think they're helping folks in poor countries. Like Feed-and-Read, it probably is helping people in addition to spreading corruption. There must be a way, I just can't think of it...

              Hard-rock mining company, similar to Endron in that they target possible caerns and places where horrible monsters or Banes might be buried. Cheap child labor wherever they can get away with it, and creative ways to "dispose" of toxic and tainted mine tailings. In regions with troublesome Shapeshifter populations or possible buried "treasure" but few or no fossil fuel reserves, this is an alternative to Endron. Supplies raw materials to many of the manufacturing subsidiaries of Pentex. Supplies slightly tainted raw materials to non-Pentex companies that have no reason of their own to manufacture tainted products, or expose their employees to Wyrm taint.

              Manufacturer of the world's most advanced myoelectric and nerve-controlled prosthetics for people missing a limb or eye, for blind people, for deaf people. Maybe pacemakers and other medical implants. A partnership of Wyrm and Weaver technology. The prosthetics are so advanced because each contains a Bane which helps power its advanced capabilities. They especially target disabled veterans who have combat experience. Not everybody becomes a Fomor, of course, or the program would have problems... only those "customers" who show promise, which includes alienation from civilian society, bitterness, anger, any nightmares or negative emotions that the Bane can exploit. For them, the physical therapy and Magadon drugs are subtly designed to awaken the Bane. The resulting Fomori have enough extra limbs to make up for whichever one was missing. Others are not converted, but the Bane is carried around and drips taint wherever they go, and tries to increase their negative emotions.

              A predatory-lending bank sponsored by the servants of the Urge of Despair. Because a great way to make people feel helpless is to trap them in inescapable debt, especially in countries without consumer protection laws. It's also just so profitable. No need to do anything supernatural or make bad business decisions to spread dispair and political corruption.

              An environmental review and cleanup company. I'm not sure how exactly it would work, but basically, lots of environmental scientists who do things like environmental impact statements, environmental reviews, cleaning up toxic waste sites and poisoned areas. Most of the employees would be innocent humans dedicated to making the world better and totally ignorant about their bosses' bosses. The company would do plenty of genuinely good things for various pieces of land. But they'd also tweak environmental impact statements given to government regulators... keep an eye on which pieces of land are inhabited by which Shapeshifters or other supernaturals... When they clean up toxic waste sites, what they're often actually doing is either supernaturally disguising the taint and pollution so that it looks "clean," burying it deeper (underground, or into the Near Umbra) where it won't be noticed as easily, or shipping it somewhere more strategically useful. They also fund tons of research on the environmental effects of various pollution on humans and wildlife, but part of that is testing other Pentex chemicals for effects, experimenting on unwilling subjects and on the landscape. When the honest researchers discover that a polluted site is worse off than studies from 30-50 years ago suggested, sometimes that's because the company's earlier surveyers made it worse. They also get to gather tons of intel on the other environmental organizations which work with them, install hidden outposts and footholds deep in strategic wilderness areas. They're so generous about buying up damaged land that they want to improve and save... and if they don't need it any more they make sure to sell it to somebody who won't go against Pentex interests.

                      Wiping out honeybees, and hopefully most other bee and pollinating insect species, has real benefits for the Wyrm, from imperiling wildlife to promoting famine and the poverty and violence that go with it.

                      It's also very profitable. When natural pollination becomes impossible in some part of the world, anyone who can supply artificial pollination gets a lucrative monopoly on the honeybee business. (You don't even need to give up on the famine, as long as there are people too poor to afford your pollination services.)

                      Eterent is already marketing their genetically engineered honeybees to farmers around the world. These bees are of course immune to all Eterent pesticides and unharmed by Wyrm-taint. They aggressively spread, hybridizing with (or outright killing) any remaining non-Eterent honeybees near their hives, and Eterent is busy patenting and trademarking their DNA in as many countries as possible so they can squeeze royalties (and lawsuits) out of anyone whose hive has been hybridized, or whose crops have been pollinated by Eterent bees. Their hives appear perfectly healthy even in polluted, tainted, and heavily pesticide-sprayed farms. They produce almost as much honey as killer bees with far less aggression, so they're a good alternative for farmers bothered by Africanized honey bees. They even hybridize with and "tame" killer bee hives they encounter. The fewer natural pollinators survive, the more profit Eterent makes from honeybees immune to the "mysterious" but obviously "completely natural" colony collapse plague.

                      The researchers are busy designing and testing new strains of these bees. Some produce more honey or wax than the first commercialized strain. Two of the most promising efficiently convert any pesticides they consume into Wyrm taint undetectable by mundane means. One strain laces honey and wax with the Wyrm poison, to taint people who eat the honey or inhale smoke from beeswax candles. The second strain spreads the taint into the pollen they carry and feeds it back into pollinated plants, so the seeds take the taint into them at conception. The goal is for plants to grow up inherently tainted in every part of their tissue, and for fruits produced from the pollinated plants to be tainted. The bees spread it around to wild plants as well.
