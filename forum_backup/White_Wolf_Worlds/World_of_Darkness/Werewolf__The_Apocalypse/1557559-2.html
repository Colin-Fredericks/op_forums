            Apples and oranges?

            Garou were designed to fight enemies of their deity to protect the world. The Exalted were designed to overthrow the existing deities on behalf of their patrons. The roles of the Exalted is to fundamentally reshape the world; even the 'weak' ones like the terrestrials.

            I don't think a Legacy garou plopped in W5 is going to break the setting. They might disrupt things for a big, but they're mortal, even the strongest can be killed through numbers and attrition, and limited in how big of a scope they can influence by themselves. A Garou has a few decades at best, an Exalted has centuries at worst and can scale up to the power to manipulate continents worth of peoples and countries.

                            Originally posted by DarkKing View Post
                            While we're on the subject of Exalted, there's a bit of obscure lore I'd like some clarification on.

                            I've heard that Exalted supposedly takes place as a prequel setting to World of Darkness.

                            Is that true? Because if it is, then that stirs up a lot of questions regarding the state of WoD's metaplot.
                            Very early on in the development cycle that was the plan. Some of the earliest advertisements for Exalted and dev interviews were pushing the idea that "before there was a World of Darkness" there was the Exalted setting, and that in terms of the Kote cosmology, Exalted took place back during the Second Age. There was a bit of a pushback and grumbling from the fanbase though and during rewrites this idea was removed, so by the time the corebook released there was no longer any official or canonical links between Exalted and the World of Darkness, just various loose threads, names and some similar concepts.
