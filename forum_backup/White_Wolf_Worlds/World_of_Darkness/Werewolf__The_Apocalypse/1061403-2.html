            Righto, keeping the Train of Thought of The "Encantado" Weredolphins Running before the caffeine in my blood runs dry.

            The "Joy Of Gaia", who remind the world the importance of Joy and Hope through Music, Art, Song and Dance.

            Gregarious, Fun Loving and Lusty to a Fault, They Reproduce by seducing beautiful/charismatic/intelligent/morally-upstanding humans at riversides, often during celebrations. Hence Encantado have Homid-Kinfolk that run the whole-gamut of human races.
            The ONE dead-giveaway to their true nature; in all-forms, Encantado have a blowhole that can only be concealed by hats and long-hair.

            Going to use the Amazonian (based on actual real-world mythology) Encantado as the "base" of this Fera, with cultural variations.

            Edit: "Encantado" literally means "Enchanted Ones" in Brazilian Portuguese.

            Sub-Species (Temporary "Working Titles" so far)

            Amazonian Weredolphins (Encantado) : The Most Famous and Well Known "Face" of this race. Gregarious and promiscuous to a fault, they also act as the "bogeyman" of the Amazon, teaching children to stay home at night, eat their vegetables, etc etc as well as spread music joy and dance.

            Yangtzee Weredophins (Her-Twen): Chinese River Weredolphins, who also act as emissaries of the Zhong-Long Were-Dragons of said waterways. Lovers of Poetry, Caligraphy, Wine and Harp Music. Though still greagarious, they also act as the ruthless "judges" of Chinese Waterways, drowning scoundrels and those of poor moral character, giving rise to the myth of "Shui Gui" (Water Ghosts) who pull drowning fishermen and those who stray too far into the water to their doom.

            Ganges Weredolphin of India: Sharing the poor eyesight of their Cetacean Kin, The Weredolphins of India take pride in their ancestor's mythological role of having borne the goddess Ganesha upon their backs whenever she traversed the Ganges River; due to their close history with the Indian Divine Courts, they fancy themselves closest to the spiritual world and are the self-proclaimed Theurges of the Encantado Fera. Has strong influence in the Bollywood Film Industry, as it promotes the Dance, Music and Romanticism much beloved by their culture. Considered haughty/arrogant by other Encantado as a rule.

            Bottlenose Dolphin Encantado of the Sea (Manatee): The most kind-hearted of the Encantado Race, the Were Ocean-Dolphins are the joyful and hopeful Yang to the vicious and cynical Ying of the Rokea, on whom they are on VERY poor terms with, much like their real-life Animal Counterparts. And yes, it is actually they who gave rise to legends of the the Mythical Manatee (not the real life Dougon) who rescue drowning sailors, share a season of love which results in a child, before returning said sailor to humanity forgetful and heartbroken.

            Common Encantado Traits.
            Able to Hold Breath For MUCH longer than any other Air-Breathing Fera in ALL Forms (Homid: 5XStamina Minutes, Crinos 10X Stamina Minutes, Cetacean 20X Stamina Minutes)

            ALL Encantado have a blowhole in every-form, concealable only by hats and long hair.

            Encantado are capable of substituting sight with Sonar Echo Location in Glabro, Crinos, Hispo and Cetacean Forms), which also counts as the "Racial Language" which they and only they can speak and understand.

            And Naturally ALL Enacantado are born expert swimmers.

            Encantado must roll willpower to resist indulging in an activity of pleasure (Music/Dance/Alcohol/Partying/Sex/Love/Poetry/Art/Movies, ETC)

            Naturally Encantado "Step Sideways" Through Water, but instead of staring into it, sing/performs music to do so.

            Will work on stats later.

            Thoughts?

                        I think that if I were to actually ever run a game of WTA (instead of just using WTA Lore and Concepts in my Sorcerer and Vampire games) I would completely overhaul which Breeds do what so that it makes sense from the ground up.                                                             Apis: Guides for primitive humanity (failed in their role)
