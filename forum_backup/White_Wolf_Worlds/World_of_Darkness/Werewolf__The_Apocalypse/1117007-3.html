            Originally posted by 21C Hermit View Post
            So Ronin are basically pariahs who are considered okay to be tormented and abused in the moral absolutism of the game's setting?
            Erm no. Ronin are pariahs who are shunned as punishment and despised by the spirits that the Ronin let down by not stepping up to their duties. Tormenting and abusing them directly isn't actually acceptable by the general rules of Garou society.

            The torment and abuse comes from the perception that Ronin frequently fall to service to the Wyrm. The Wyrm's forces offer companionship and paths to respect and power that Ronin are cut of from otherwise. The Ronin are stuck with either suffering the misery their condition implies, or being assumed to be traitors if they manage to find a place outside of that misery. And unlike many real world comparison the Garou Nation approaches a level of reasonable risk assessment in that assumption.

            There are Garou that question this (particularly the Children of Gaia) because the absolute shunning producing Wyrm servants isn't a good outcome, and would advocate a more tempered shunning or just not forcing Ronin status a punishment at all. Garou, unlike spirits, are not compelled to specific actions by their fundamental natures, and can examine the situation and change.

            But if they're making them protagonists of the game... maybe the moral absolutism isn't severe as it is, or the game's developers are taking an alternate interpretation of the setting.
            The second one is the most likely.
