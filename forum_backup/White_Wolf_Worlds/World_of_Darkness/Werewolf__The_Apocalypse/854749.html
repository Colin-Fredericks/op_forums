    Aren't the changing breeds doing breeding all wrong?

    03-28-2016, 08:39 PM
    So the garou are loosing lots of numbers, and the wolf population is low. The apocalypse is coming, the black spirals breed metis after metis for their front line infantry... Isn't this wrong? No, I don't mean 'wrong' as in 'oh no, world of darkness, you're being so horrible' I mean "this isn't really the logical method"

    Now this is more more or less true for changing breeds, because the pregnancy ranges from ten babies per week to one in a thousand years and all the parents will die, and maybe this is just my weaver tainted mind looking for effeciency where it would offend, but my argument: The animal breed should be the most populous.

    Let's assume it takes a year or two to train cubs.

    You make one human every nine months
    OR
    One metis every six months
    OR
    You make aproximately six lupus (As much as 12) over three months, but can only do this once a year.

    Humans will take... let's assume they change at 14.
    Metis take about 8
    Lupus take about two.

    Now, if we work on average probability every two years we can assume
    Two homid kinfolk
    Two metis
    One lupus garou and 11 lupus kinfolk.


    If we take ten years we've got
    one or two homid garou and ten kinfolk
    Twenty metis. Four of them at first change or claith
    six lupus, five at first change or claith, 55 kinfolk.

    I'm being very lazy with the maths here. Just going to point that out so that nobody will feel entitled when they correct me on specifics.

    Now, That there collection of stats is under the assumption that we've got one mother doing all the work (and we're not taking into account fertility charms to raise litter sizes and assuming that the metis mother lives because precautions are taken) and it-doesn't-matter amounts of male involvement. I confess, I don't know the gender devide between wolves, but if we assume it's half, we've got twenty two kinfolk that are female, and if we assume they can breed after two years, we've got about 17 who can be pregnant and 12 who have given birth (I'm really, really not trying with these maths, but I don't think my accuracy hurts my validity) then we have 72 more wolves and 7 more lupus garou born in the final year alone. If we factor in the previous eight years that the original broodmother has been a grandmother for, we've got over the 20 garou of the metis broodmother. The one or two garou of the homids haven't changed yet (most likely, puberty can start at 9) and the kinfolk aren't at reproduction stages yet.


    Of course, this explosion in population might cause a wolf cull. Now, Warp what the black furies do- Kill the lupus males who don't change. Yeah it's brutal but
    -no wolf cull
    -Less chance of a non-garou parent mean more garou.
    -Less mouths to feed.

    Also, The black spirals (or glass walkers) could just do this from captivity.
    Now, other factors include that a lot of cubs don't reach maturity. Yeah, If garou cared for their cubs, it wouldn't be a problem. Hunting to feed a pack isn't so much of an issue for garou.

    Also, this has a good impact on actual animal populations. Khans for example. Amur tigers have liters of around 3-4 children a little over 3 months (with 5 month breaks). That's 5-6 children a year. Tigers are endangered. They wouldn't be if Khans bred more. More Khan, More tiger. A nice thing about this is that a feline female doesn't even need to search for a rare kinfolk tiger, she can just use a human father each time.

    Metis, really, for garou and bastet, are a waste for lupus mothers, unless of course they can create them out of season, in which case 3 months of a litter, 6 months of a metis and 3 months of a break would work wonders for the population.


    (as a side note, I'm surprised that the black furies don't have homids as a tiny minority or at least largely of adoption. wolf females are the only way such a tribe can sustain itself.)
    (Ratkin are clearly gaia's future, all hail our rodent overlords)
    *DEATH RAY*
