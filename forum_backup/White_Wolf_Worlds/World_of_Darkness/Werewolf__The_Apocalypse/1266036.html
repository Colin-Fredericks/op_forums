    Revamped Changing Breed Sheets available from Mr. Gone!

    11-09-2018, 09:14 PM
    Yeah, me and Mr. G have been working on cleaning up the W20 Changing Breed sheets for the past couple of weeks just to get rid of a few errors and such. Each Breed now has a more accurate Expanded Backgrounds list, as well as a few optional things from the Breedbooks (such as individual Breed weaknesses) that players include if they like, or leave out if they prefer following the rules in CB20. And there were a few assorted things like making sure that Ananasi had a Blood Pool of 25 instead of 20 as the Ananasi can reach a max Blood Pool of 25 (rather than 20) with sufficient Rank and Gifts.

    Mr. Gone has already uploaded the new sheets onto his website found here:

    MrGone's Character Sheets.
    http://mrgone.rocksolidshells.com/
    Collection of Character Sheets created by MrGone.


    For those of you who haven't been there before, just head to the W20 section and scroll down to the W20 Changing Breed Sheets section. 2- and 4-page versions available as per usual.

    Constructive criticism and comments can be posted here on this thread.

    Enjoy!

                              Mr. G, one last thing (I swear!) to fix on the Corax sheet. I forgot to take into account the Corax combat chart and their Eye Pluck special maneuver. The Revised version of the Corax sheet has the correct maneuvers but the old Revised format, so here's the updated W20 format that you should use for the Corax Brawling Chart on Page 3:

                              Brawling Chart
                              Maneuver Roll Diff Damage
                              Body Slam Dex+Brawl 7 Special/B
                              Claw Dex+Brawl 6 Strength+2/A
                              Eye Pluck Dex+Brawl 9 Strength+2/A
                              +Special
                              Grapple Dex+Brawl 6 Strength/B
                              Kick Dex+Brawl 7 Strength+1/B
                              Punch Dex+Brawl 6 Strength/B
                              A=Aggravated Damage B=Bashing Damage

                              And the Ananasi Brawling Chart still needs the changes I outlined before in my last edit. It's here again, just in case (remember to take into account that the Ananasi have TWO Bite maneuvers because of their Lilian and Pithus forms):

                              Brawling Chart
                              Maneuver Roll Diff Damage
                              Bite (Lilian) Dex + Brawl 5 Strength + 1/A
                              Bite (Pithus) Dex + Brawl 5 Strength + 3/A
                              Body Slam Dex + Brawl 7 Special/B
                              Claw Dex + Brawl 6 Strength + 2/A
                              Grapple Dex + Brawl 6 Strength/B
                              Kick Dex + Brawl 7 Strength + 1/B
                              Punch Dex + Brawl 6 Strength/B
                              A=Aggravated Damage B=Bashing Damage

                              These should be the absolute last changes needed. I sent you an email detailing the last corrections needed as well, Mr. G. Thanks again for all your hard work!

                              EDIT: Ugh, the post screwed up the format and spaces between the columns; the Tab button doesn't work in these forums and multiple spaces don't work either. So if it still looks screwed up, just read my docfile that I emailed you.
