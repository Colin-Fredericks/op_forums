    Originally posted by Saur Ops Specialist View Post
    The original text of the Kumo had a suggestion for venom in Pithus/Aghora - it was a Stamina roll (high difficulty for those who don't normally soak agg) that, if failed, caused temporary Attribute dot loss. Hitting 0 resulted in unconsciousness. In Ananasi, it suggests only making it a factor of use against normal humans and such unless the venom is from a Gift, and also suggested limiting the uses per day. Also, Pithus has full strength venom, while Lillian doesn't. W20 just suggests a little bit of agg that needs medical attention or magic for fragile characters to heal it.

    (If you're using Nagah values... that's seven levels of agg, not dice, so you'd definitely need to make it limited in use. Also, you'll start having werespiders suffer from a case of Shadowrun's "geek the mage first" because everyone knows that they're absurdly lethal if they bite you, and with Ananasi fragility, that means they'll probably mostly die in any confrontation if they're not high Rank Myrmidons.)

    It'd probably fine for Ananasi to not have super deadly venom out of the gate, at least for whatever is more resilient than a human or mage who hasn't used magick to get tougher. They get the capacity to make super-webbing in Pithus and turn any confined space into a potential deathtrap, so it's not like they're getting reamed on this, nor were they produced with the intention of being super assassins among the Changers as Nagah were.

        twig's Ananasi Rules and Gifts

        As W20 unless otherwise stated.

        The natural venom of the Ananasi is akin to that of an "ordinary" spider, in that it can hospitalize an ordinary human and possibly kill them if they don't receive medical attention.

        Direct contact with Queen Ananasa remains as written, but remember that she is a prisoner and has been for a very long time. Queen Ananasa has said, very firmly, that the Wyrm has no control over her and her messages. But would she know if he did? Think of this like one of those Future Fates sidebars in Mage. Perhaps Queen Ananasa has the Wyrm and the whole Triat fooled, and she's weaving a plan which will ensnare them all. Perhaps her long captivity has driven her insane, exactly like her 'uncle/brother' the Wyrm, and she has lost her way. Perhaps she is a flawed yet reasonable ruler who has a good plan for the tapestry but will do anything to get there. Perhaps the Wyrm has taken control of her and she doesn't realize it. Perhaps the messages she's receiving from the outside are being subtly altered by Wyrm taint, so while she has full agency, the information she's receiving from the outside is warped in a way which serves the mad whims of the Corrupter.

        In my humble opinion, all Ananasi ought to start with Willpower 5 and Gnosis 3 regardless of their Breed. As it stands playing a Homid Ananasi is very much a 'flavour/backstory tax', where you lose 8 freebies worth of Gnosis in exchange for no mechanical advantages whatsoever. At least with Lupus Garou the Gnosis 5 comes with a significant downside in that you can't allocate your starting points into any 'human' abilities like Drive, Firearms and so on, though personally I play Garou as 2/3/4 for Gnosis by Breed to reduce the size of the gap.

        General Ananasi Gifts

        Balance / Cling / Many Eyes / Resist Toxin / Resist Pain / Sense Primal Nature / Shed / Stolen Moments
        Araneid Form / Breath of Ananasa / Hand Fangs / Heightened Senses / Spirit Speech
        Blood Pump / Burrow / Jump / Spider's Grace / Spines
        Cocoon / Hydraulic Strength / Territory
        Carapace / Survivor

        Shed, Breath of Ananasa, Heightened Senses and Burrow are added to the General list since they are generally spiderlike capabilities. Araneid Form is renamed from Man-Spider Form, which is a silly name. Sense Primal Nature is added since it is key to the Ananasi identity, more-so than it is to the Bastet, who are more rulers than tapestry-understanders. Spirit Speech is added, but at Rank 2, since Queen Ananasa is a jealous mistress and she doesn't want her children smelling, let alone tasting, other kinds of koolaid until she is assured of their loyalty.

        Spines is moved to Rank 3 for balance purposes.

        Ananasi mark their Territory, with the Gift of that name, using spider's webs. Otherwise the Gift functions as normal.

        Tenere Aspect Gifts

        Apecraft's Blessings / Aura of Confidence / Groom / Patience of Ananasa / Weaver's Eyes
        Beneath Notice / Control Simple Machine / Diagnostics / Spider's Song
        Mother's Look / Plug and Play / Reshape Object
        Control Complex Machine / Jam Weapon / Web of Knowledge
        Summon Net-Spider / Thieving Touch of Spiders

        Hatar Aspect Gifts

        Blood of Pain / Odious Aroma / Wyrmling Kinship
        Blood Life / Blood of Illusion / Bloody Retribution / Call of the Wyrm / Tar Shadow
        Corrupt / Pulse of the Invisible / Mask Taint
        Feast of Essence / Ill Winds / Still Blood
        Burning Blood / Patience of the Wyrm

        Anything biting you comes into contact with your blood. In addition, I have written a new Gift for the list:

        Bloody Retribution
        Spending a Blood point when you take Lethal or Aggravated damage (so long as the wound draws blood) causes blood to hit your attacker, if they are adjacent to you. If the attacker has an action or split dice pool permitting them to dodge, they may attempt to do so, dodging the blood if they achieve 1 success.
        Kumoti Aspect Gifts

        Beast Speech / Create Element / Hidden Killer / Inspire / Mother's Touch
        Arachnophobia / Beastmind / Form Mastery
        Alter Lilian / Faerie Kin / Sense Motion
        Call Elemental / Infest / Mindblock
        Attunement / Curse of Arachne

        The Changelings and Fae summoned by Ananasi default to Sluagh, and to Unseelie Sidhe, if available. Changelings of House Varich are the most drawn to the Ananasi. Faerie Kin is a rare Gift among the Ananasi, but it is learnt by those Kumoti who wish to reconcile the role of the Dreaming within the great Tapestry.

        The version of Attunement Ananasi learn works in all environments, with a Streetwise or Survival role depending on the Storyteller's preference.

        Curse of Arachne harks back to the myth of Athena and Arachne; it is Curse of Lycaon, but instead of turning the target into an ordinary wolf, it turns them into an ordinary spider.

        Myrmidon Faction Gifts

        Fangs of Judgement / Lightning Reflexes / Open Seal / Spur Claws
        Flame Dance / Hydraulic Strength / Spirit of the Fray / True Fear
        Razor Claws / Scorpion Tail / Weak Arm
        Blades of the Mantis / Drying Bite / Pulse of the Prey
        Image of the Great Mother / Umbral Dodge

        For Ananasi, Fangs of Judgement triggers against any creature which has fallen from its original purpose, whatever that may be. This includes most Wyrmish spirits, Black Spiral Dancers, traitor Ananasi, and so on. Spur Claws is insectoid, and stolen during the Insect Wars. Various warrior Gifts are added to pad out the role.

        Image of the Great Mother should have its main downside, needing to stay in Crawlerling form after using it, removed.

        Viskr Faction Gifts

        Curse of the Great Web / Sense Magic / Shroud / Umbral Tether / Web Walker
        Persuasion / Mindspeak / Minor Unweaving / Strength of Purpose / Truth of Ananasa
        Calcify / Exorcism / Umbral Camouflage
        Attunement / Breathren Call
        Malleable Spirit / Shattering

        Truth of Ananasa is renamed from Truth of Gaia.

        The version of Attunement Ananasi learn works in all environments, with a Streetwise or Survival role depending on the Storyteller's preference.

        Wyrsta Faction Gifts

        Alter Mood / Beastmind / Blur of the Milky Eye / Disguise Fetish / Two Tongues
        Blinding Spit / Fetish Fetch / Name the Spirit / Persuasion / Visceral Agony
        Chameleon / Tick Body
        Blades of the Mantis / Signal Rider
        Head Games / Razor Webs

        This is my best effort - I imagine a few Gifts need removing! But it's a good start.

                                    Kumo

                                    The Kumo are Ananasi, largely based in Asia, who have broken free of Queen Ananasa's designs and generally serve the Wyrm. There are many exceptions to this, and the Kumo aren't quite "Black Spider Dancers" as it were. It could be argued that the existence of the Kumo, and the fact that they continue to have all of the Gifts and benefits you'd expect of Ananasi, is part of some plot by Queen Ananasa... or perhaps she just thinks that because she's trapped in the Wyrm's realm.

                                    Kumo differ from regular Ananasi in a few ways:
                                    • All Kumo are Hatar
                                    • Kumo lack a Faction
                                    • Instead, they gain access to the Ragabash list
                                    • The Kumo are vulnerable to a type of sacred wood, hong mu, which they treat the way Garou treat silver
                                    • Metis exist, starting with a Gnosis of 5
                                    • Kumo all start with a Willpower of 2
                                    Ragabash Gifts

                                    Blur of the Milky Eye, Infectious Laughter, Liar's Face, Open Seal, Scent of Running Water, Disguise Fetish
                                    Blissful Ignorance, Pulse of the Prey, Spider's Song, Taking the Forgotten, Petal Float, Play Dead
                                    Gremlins, Liar's Craft, Monkey Tail, Open Moon Bridge, Pathfinder, Terrain Shift
                                    Luna's Blessing, Umbral Dodge, Whelp Body, Misdirection
                                    Thieving Talons of the Magpie, Thousand Forms
                                    Firebringer

                                    Few of these Gifts would typically be available to a Hatar; Myrmidons get Open Seal for example, but you lack a Faction of course. It's worth noting that the Ragabash Gift list is far superior to the Ananasi lists in depth and breadth, so starting with the General Ananasi List, the Ragabash List, and the Hatar List (with bonus Kumo Gifts for the Hatar section!) is pretty great. That's before you consider bonus Gifts from being in the Beast Courts or Ahadi, which you almost certainly are given that you're not going to be playing a Kumo-only game.

                                    Monkey Tail is likely "n.a." since you lack a tail in all your forms. Luna's Blessing is "n.a." because you are not vulnerable to silver. Firebringer is "n.a." because it's very unlikely that you have an equivalent to Rank 6.

                                        Kumo Gifts

                                        These are treated as Hatar Gifts for any Ananasi who get the chance to learn them; essentially this just expands the Hatar list.

                                        Spider Scurry (1): Renamed from City Running. See the Rank 1 Homid post. Somewhat improved by being an Ananasi, as you can spend Blood instead of Rage.

                                        Shadow Sway (1): This is somewhat anime-like, but that's not a bad thing. Basically this Gift lets you project a false image of yourself a few feet away from your actual body to mess with attempts to harm you. Anyone attacking the fake you will miss, anyone realizing that the real you is nearby can attack you at +3 difficulty, or +1 difficulty if they have special sensory abilities like Heightened Senses.

                                        The effect costs 1 Gnosis, requires a Wits + Subterfuge (difficulty 6), and lasts for (Gnosis) turns. Which uh... well, if you have a Gnosis of 1-2, this kinda sucks. Also why roll if the duration is pre-determined? The writing of this Gift is quite messy, basically. That said, with lots of successes you can place your duplicate very, very far away, the formula is (Successes x Rank) feet, so with a Rank of 3 and 6 successes your shadow self could be 18 feet away; as your foes chase after it you can slip away.

                                        This is handy for deceptions and for fights, but it's clunky. The Gnosis cost, the roll, the short duration, and indeed you attacking ends the effect. But I can forgive all that because... this is a Level 1 Gift. If you've paid 3xp for this, you can live with the fact that the Gift isn't perfect. Just make a shadow version of yourself a few feet away, so it seems like you've "teleported" a few feet, then while everyone attacks the fake you go into Crawlerling and escape. It's a pretty neat combo and I quite like it despite the limitations of the Gift, which are pretty severe.

                                        Spittle of Corruption (2): Hatar continue to love bodily fluids. This Gift gives you a neat ranged attack which uses Brawl, which I quite like since it saves you the effort of mastering Firearms or Melee. 30 foot range, difficulty 6 Dexterity + Brawl, Stamina +2 dice of Aggravated damage.

                                        Given that your Pithus form has +3 Stamina, you could easily inflict 10 dice of Aggravated damage with this Gift, which is pretty competitive. It's no Klaive, of course, but you're attacking at range, with difficulty 6, and you haven't had to invest in Strength either. The Gift does, however, cost a point of Blood every time you use it. And since you can only spend Blood for one thing per turn, no bonus actions, until you get Blood Pump of course. Successes on the attack should also carry over, of course, like a typical attack.

                                        Great Gift, all things considered, especially once you have Blood Pump so you can shoot several times in a turn. The range, using Brawl, using your Stamina for damage, it's all pretty great.

                                        Snare of Beauty (3): Spend 1 blood, roll Manipulation + Occult (difficulty 7), every success gives you +1 Appearance. This lasts for (Rank) hours. So, with this Gift you can boost your Appearance stat up to 10 quite reasonably through repeated uses. This is good for certain situations, some Gifts, for roleplay purposes, and for some Rites. Just don't say you're more beautiful than even the Gods, or you'll get turned into a spider (wait...)

                                        This is neat, and if you're really into the Sex Appeal / Gender Morph powers of the Ananasi then I'd suggest grabbing this one. It does buff Mother's Look, and allow you to turn your Lilian into a "Goddess" for mortals to worship if you combine this Gift with Alter Lilian and Groom.

                                        Web Tremors (4): Spend 1 Gnosis, get +3 successes on all rolls to notive people who are hiding from you. This lasts for the whole night!

                                        That said, note that the Gift only helps find people who are hiding from you. The Gift helps you to notice when you're being followed, for example, but it wouldn't help you find someone who was just hiding in general.

                                        The cost, even with a great duration, the high level, and the niche effect... makes this pretty mediocre. An entirely passive effect would be a lot better for a "notice when you're being stalked" power.

                                        Megarachnid (5): This is just a better version of Image of the Great Mother. The Gift is much cheaper, lacks any downsides, and doesn't have a roll.

                                        Spend 1 Gnosis when you enter Pithus, get +2 Strength, +1 Dexterity, +2 Stamina on top of the base stats, for a total of +6/+2/+5, which is amazing. If you're a Metis this Gift is very easy to use, and if you aren't, make sure you have good Primal-Urge for transformations. If you stay in Pithus for a really long time you can also cheap out on reactivating this Gift. There's no set duration, you just enjoy the benefits so long as you remain in Pithus; consider doing Partial Transformations to get any other bodyparts you might need.
