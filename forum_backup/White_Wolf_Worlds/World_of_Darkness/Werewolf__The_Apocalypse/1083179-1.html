    I have thought about it; I am not rejecting the idea out of hand. I dislike the idea not because it is heterodoxy, I dislike it because it turns an auspicious event into a random thing.


    Besides, even if we discount the Perfect Metis, one of the defining traits of the Metis is their inability to breed: they are cursed by dint of their parent's sin. If this powerful symbol of Gaia's displeasure can be overcome by a random quirk of genetics, it lessens the sting of even being a Metis and discounts the mystic nature of the WtA universe.

            I'm going to explain my thought process behind why I created this topic and maybe include a funny story.

            A while ago, I saw a topic about a Metis in a romantic relationship with a Mage. Me being a weirdo with an interest in biology wondered "What does a Male Metis semen look like? Are male Metis shooting blanks? Malformed sperm? Sperm that just doesn't recognize human/lupus ovum as a viable destination?" My mind wanders to strange places.

            So I did some research. While Metis aren't mules, they do share the infertility trait with them. I discovered male Mules don't produce sperm at all because of a chromosome mismatch inhibiting the meiosis processes. I doubt the metis has the same exact issue, but its close enough for game psuedo-science. "Cool, let's steal metis shooting blanks in case the question does come up in game."

            Then I read about Female Mules producing foals. It's a pretty rare event. So rare in countries with a less educated population, they are considered omens. Kind of like how the perfect metis is considered an Omen.

            So, while I agree the general game setting doesn't need any metis producing offspring, the idea of female metis rarely producing offspring may be good story fodder. I would never have a PC metis be fertile. Removing infertility would remove a large part of being metis. I doubt female metis are getting loving any due to the social stigma of metis. The offspring being a product of Rape is a subject I rather not touch upon here. Maybe the Black Spiral Dancers have more incidents of this which would lead to the occasional unholy jihad to bring about the apocalypse.

            Any who, I created this topic to brainstorm the Garou Nation's reaction to such events. After a couple of false "Perfect Metis" maybe most Garou discount the actual Perfect Metis. Others could just want to outright kill the offspring. A few true believes may begin to sharpen their claws and klaives in preparation for the upcoming battle. I should disclose I am Agnostic, so religions assigning conflicting meanings to rare and random events intrigues me. My games trend more political, so the Garou arguing over how to react to this "miracle" is good story fodder at my table.

            I write things.

                            There are really not that many Metis though. I use the 1:500,000 ratio for the population of the Garou Nation, so there might be 15,000 Garou in the Garou Nation by my reckoning (I am not including any Garou outside the Garou Nation in my numbers). Accidental Metis are really uncommon, the mother has to get pregnant before one of the parents experiences the First Change, so Metis should be uncommon because few Garou are stupid enough to get it on with another Garou, especially since there are plenty of willing and eager Kinfolk around. In all, there should be 150 Metis in the Garou Nation by my reckoning.

                            When you look at the incidence of fertile female mules (there is no evidence of any fertile male mules in history), there are around sixty record cases in the past five hundred years. During that time, there were a total of 6 billion mules born in the world, so that means a 1:50,000,000 occurrence of fertile female miles. I doubt that there have been enough female Metis born in the last fifty thousand years to reach even one percent of that number. Just a thought...
