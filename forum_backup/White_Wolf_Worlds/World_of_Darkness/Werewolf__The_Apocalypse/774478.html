    Apocalypse: The Fall of a Tribe..

    11-25-2015, 10:28 PM
    ..To the Wyrm option.
    Imagine you're running (or perhaps you already have) the Apocalypse end of things scenarios and you go with the idea of a Tribe of Garou falling to the Wyrm begore the final battle (and kicking off a lot of bad things in the process). What tribe would you choose to fall? And why?

    In the first game I ran for Apocalypse I chose the Children of Gaia. I called in 'The Death of Hope'. Thinking the group would freak out about the corruption and fall of these cleansers and supporters of peace, unity, love, hope etc. But I think everyone was more worried about losing so many npcs that could likely of had mothers touch

    In the Second game I ran I used the Red Talons, who after saying good byes to a few caerns and singing howls of extinctions to more creatures hunted beyond saving just lost it. That scared everyone. So much the wanted more city stories rather thsn in the wild just in case wyrm fallen talon or three turned up.

    I'm looking to run another soon...and I'm considering using the StarGazers. Harano creeps in under the weight of their own meditations. They left there cousins for the beast courts in the hopes that they could make a difference. They did, now they see in the guilt and depression how they have damned their brethren to die and the beast courts are no better. They looked so deep within and saw the wyrm within themselves and stopped looking at the wyrm around them or something like that perhaps. Like the children of gaia for hope, I see something truly terrible in the most insightful of all of gaias tribes falling first.

    Which tribe would you choose? And why?
