            Originally posted by Baaldam View Post
            Going on a random aside while we discuss Fera and Garou tribes, do the Bone Gnawers really make a good case for their individual identity and weight as a tribe of their own or could they work just as well as a Glass Walker camp?
            I've always viewed the Bone Gnawers theme as the "omega tribe" or "runts of the litter" rather than "urban tribe" so I don't view them as the same as the Glass Walkers. Those are two different themes.

            In addition, I often interpret the Glass Walkers as less than an urban tribe as "wolves in sheep clothing" meaning werewolves that hide their identity very well to live among mankind (since Cockroach is very big on living unseen among humanity). So while both today are seen as "urban tribes", they have very different aspects. The "urban tribes" theme are an emergent property, not an essential one and these two tribes slot into that in very different ways.

            That's not how it's been developed in the tribebooks, but I like my interpretation better.

                I agree. BG and GW have totally different mentalities, for the most part. The GW derived from the "Warder of Man"-Packs that septs historically send to the human settlements to guard them and that became fascinated with progress and spirits that arose from this. Their tribal identity was dominated by the Wise Guys for a while, but now this mentality is resurfacing.

                The BG, on the other hand, derive from all the omegas, who were driven out of their septs for not making the cut. Either formally expelled or just runaways or garou who couldn't handle the sept's expectations. They formed their own culture, revolving around them being the sturdiest of the garou for all the hardship they endured and the camaraderie they experience.

                So, in a nutshell, the GW are city-dwellers because they want to explore this terrain, while BG didn't have a choice as to life there, where other garou tribes didn't claim territory.
