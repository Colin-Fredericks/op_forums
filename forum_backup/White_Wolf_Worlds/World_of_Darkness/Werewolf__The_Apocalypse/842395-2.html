        The Gift, as written, works on individuals, not institutions or systems. The store clerk wouldn't remember the object being the store (though if it is something they have multiples of it wouldn't erase the fact that they carry the item), but it wouldn't remove the item from the inventory software.

                    The Ragabash, Homid, Glasswalker,and Bone Gnawer lists are full of decent options (this shouldn't really be surprising) though there's decent options via the social oriented lists (Philodox - though very out of Auspice to use those Gifts that way - and Galliard), or less direct guile stuff (Children of Gaia and Fianna). Basically too much to just list (and then all the Changeling Breeds like Bastet and Nuwisha that have some nice ones as well).

                    Basically, there's enough options that "any stripe" makes it too hard to narrow down a list. Once you get down to things like, "Con Artist," "High Tech Security Cracker," etc, it's a lot easier to form a smaller list.

                                    Ok, so this probably isn't a complete list, but screw it, it's long enough. I'm not repeating Gifts that appear in multiple lists. I left out a few things that could be arguable because they're too obvious in my mind. Comments are what they are, I think it's pretty self explanatory.

                                    Homid -
                                    1:
                                    Apecraft's Blessing (if you use any tools in your pick-pocketing for distractions or hiding things)
                                    Persuasion (In case of suspicion after a bump)
                                    Smell of Man (Pets are a good in on a mark)
                                    2:
                                    Divide (good distraction Gift)
                                    Jam Technology (another good distraction, as well as getting rid of cameras)
                                    Mark of the Wolf (another back-up Gift since it will make your mark less likely to be believed by authorities if they realize what happened)
                                    Speech of the World (only if travelling or in a diverse city and you don't invest in languages)
                                    3:
                                    Tongues (Same reasons as Speech of the World, also good for deciphering gang tags and avoiding trouble from that)
                                    4:
                                    Body Shift (If for some reason you haven't maxed out Dexterity)
                                    Bury the Wolf (Draw less attention to yourself by removing the effects of the Curse)
                                    5:
                                    Assimilation (See Speech of the World/Tongues)
                                    Beyond Human (even better than Bury the Wolf for approaching and manipulating a mark while you rob them)

                                    Metis -
                                    2:
                                    Form Mastery (makes it easier to do partial shifts which can be all sorts of useful)
                                    3:
                                    Chameleon (stealth)
                                    5:
                                    Madness (distractions)
                                    Totem Gift (distractions again)

                                    Lupus -
                                    1:
                                    Heightened Senses (easier time finding good marks)
                                    5:
                                    Boon of the Animal Fathers (note this is an old Gift, might not be allowed, but you can get some of the nice bonuses for Cunning Totem animals without having to have them as your Totem)

                                    Ragabash -
                                    1:
                                    Blur of the Milky Eye (Stealth)
                                    Hush (Back up)
                                    Infectious Laughter (Back-up if you get caught, crack a joke and they laugh so hard they forget if you're careful)
                                    Liar's Face (Approach the mark/back up)
                                    Open Seal (in case you steal something locked)
                                    2:
                                    Blissful Ignorance (situational stealth)
                                    Emergency Exit (back up)
                                    Obscure the Truth (Super lying)
                                    Pulse of the Prey (if you're after something specific)
                                    Spider's Song (finding a good mark)
                                    Taking the Forgotten (pretty much the pick-pocket Gift)
                                    Trickster Beacon (Distraction)
                                    3:
                                    Gremlins (distractions and mucking with any on-person security like proximity sensors)
                                    Impunity (back up)
                                    Liar's Craft (super lying)
                                    Riddle (distraction)
                                    The Usual Suspects (if you want to track multiple repeat marks)
                                    4:
                                    Madness Season (distraction)
                                    5:
                                    Blank (Everyone else holds still for a short period of time and ignores everything unless attacked)
                                    Thousand Forms (shift into animals that people ignore and are dexterous)

                                    Theurge -
                                    3:
                                    Pulse of the Invisible (watch people from the Umbra more easily)
                                    4:
                                    Grasp the Beyond (take things across the Gauntlet without having to dedicate them first)
                                    Shadowplay (distractions, or even stealing things with the shadow)

                                    Philodox -
                                    2:
                                    King of Beats (get animals to do stuff for you)
                                    4:
                                    Roll Over (mugging without violence)

                                    Galliard -
                                    1:
                                    Beast Speech (talk to the animals)
                                    2:
                                    Distractions
                                    Dreamspeak (gather intel)
                                    Reverie (distraction, maybe snag information as well)
                                    3:
                                    Song of Rage (distraction, back up to get the mark in trouble)
                                    5:
                                    Headgames (distractions and mark approach)

                                    Ahroun -
                                    1:
                                    Falling Touch (knock the mark over "accidentally")
                                    2:
                                    Spirit of the Fray (Always be faster than your mark)
                                    True Fear (distraction)
                                    4:
                                    Touch of Rage (distraction)

                                    Black Furies -
                                    1:
                                    Owl's Speech (if owls are around)
                                    Spirit Smugler (Camp Gift, shove small things into the Umbra)
                                    2:
                                    Curse of Aeolus (stealth and distraction)
                                    Kneel (distraction)
                                    4:
                                    Body Wrack (distraction)

                                    Bone Gnawers -
                                    1:
                                    Smell of Success (find good marks)
                                    Smell of Sweet Honey (distraction, easier to track the mark as they try to get away from the pests)
                                    2:
                                    Find the Prize (find what you're looking to steal)
                                    Guise of the Hound (stealth, since dog)
                                    3:
                                    Beg
                                    Call the Rust (distraction, back up)
                                    Gift of the Termite (distraction)
                                    4:
                                    Attunement (intel gathering)
                                    Blink (back up)
                                    Face in the Crowd (distraction)
                                    Infest (distraction)
                                    Trust Me (super lying)
                                    5:
                                    Gluttony (old Gift, hide almost anything in your stomach for later)
                                    Help Me
                                    Riot

                                    Children of Gaia
                                    1:
                                    Brother's Scent (blend in)
                                    3:
                                    Chant of Morpheus (put people to sleep)
                                    Dazzle (distraction)
                                    Guilt Trip
                                    5:
                                    Trust of Gaia

                                    Fianna -
                                    1:
                                    Fairie Light (distraction)
                                    2:
                                    Brew (get people drunk without them realizing it)
                                    Glib Tongue (super lying)
                                    Fair Fortune (reroll a bad roll, good for everything really)
                                    4:
                                    Faerie Blood (old Gift, most for the Gnosis saving since a lot of these Gifts cost Gnosis)
                                    Phantasm (distraction)

                                    Get of Fenris -
                                    3:
                                    Loki's Touch (distraction)
                                    Mark the Enemy (tag a mark for later)
                                    5:
                                    Strength of the Ancestors (boost any Attributes so Dex/Socials/Wits, requires high Ancestors Background to be worth it)

                                    Glasswalkers -

                                    1:
                                    Control Simple Machine
                                    Plug and Play (Your smartphone lets you steal all sorts of digital stuff)
                                    Skyscrapper Vision (gather intel, follow marks from range)
                                    Weaver's Eyes (Pick pocket encrypted transmissions!)
                                    2:
                                    Cybersenses (gather intel, track mark)
                                    Heat Metal (distraction)
                                    Image of the Saints (Camp Gift, helps pick good marks)
                                    Mind Partition (Camp Gift, lets you set up complicated picks quickly)
                                    Pennies from Heaven (back up)
                                    3:
                                    Control Complex Machine
                                    Intrusion (back up for a pick pocket unless the mark is in a hard to get to place)
                                    4:
                                    Doppelganger (stealth, approach mark, back up)
                                    Tractor Beam (pull stuff into the Umbra)
                                    5:
                                    Calm the Flock (ignore the Curse)
                                    Chaos Mechanics (always useful)
                                    Phone Travel (back up, get into hard to reach places)
                                    Song of the City-Beast (distraction)

                                    Red Talons -
                                    1:
                                    Cub's Lesson (Camp Gift, crappy back up... but Red Talon theif?)
                                    Wolf at the Door (control the mark's location)
                                    2:
                                    Primal Instinct (distraction with enough successes, setup a mark with less)
                                    5:
                                    Blessing of the First Pack (Ragabash option only)

                                    Shadow Lords -
                                    1:
                                    Dreams of a Thousand Cranes (Hakken Gift, always useful)
                                    Fair Path (Hakken Gift, social boost)
                                    Seizing the Edge (back up)
                                    Shadow Weaving (distraction, stealh)
                                    Whisper Catching (intel gathering)
                                    2:
                                    Hidding Secret (Camp Gift, intel gathering, explicitly blackmail the Gift)
                                    3:
                                    Curse of Corruption (back up, good for discrediting others)
                                    Dark of Night (Hakken Gift, blinds the target)
                                    4:
                                    Seeds of Doubt (super lying)
                                    Ubiquitous Presence (keep the mark completely off their game)
                                    5:
                                    Obedience (Mind control)

                                    Silent Striders -
                                    1:
                                    Silence (stealth)
                                    4:
                                    Touch of Death (Camp Gift, a bit overboard for pickpocketing, but subtly renders the target catatonic and forgets how things happened)

                                    Silver Fangs -
                                    1:
                                    Eye of the Falcon (not as good as Heightened Senses for pick pockets, but similar)
                                    New Moon's Laughter (Lodge Gift, setup marks)
                                    5:
                                    New Moon Legerdemain (Lodge Gift, pack of trickster spirits for your personal use)

                                    Stargazers -
                                    3:
                                    Conundrum (distraction)

                                    Uktena -
                                    1:
                                    Moonstruck Path (track the mark if you lose them and it's dark outside)
                                    Shroud (stealth, distraction)
                                    2:
                                    Overlook (stealth)
                                    Reveal the Hidden (good if you know what you're looking for)
                                    Shadows at Dawn (back up, tell the mark true stuff about you and they'll forgot it later)
                                    Sight of Hidden Places (could theoretically see hidden pockets and such)
                                    3:
                                    Invisibility
                                    Scrying (intel gathering, track the mark at range)
                                    Secrets (intel gathering)
                                    4:
                                    Bare the Heart (intel gathering)

                                    Wendigo -
                                    2:
                                    Fog (stealth, distraction)
                                    Speak with Wind Spirits (intel gathering, maybe finding a mark if you lose them)
