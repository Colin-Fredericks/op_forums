                  That's it? Honestly the possible Garou couple isn't that big a deal to me. If the Garou all rigidly clung to the Litany there wouldn't be any metis. Besides only the husband is confirmed Garou, we have no idea what the wife was before her death.

                  As for Warsinger's introduction, even less of a deal than the above. As a player and ST of WtA I seldom give out formal introductions like that unless it's well... a formal situation. And that situation isn't that. The two were just fighting, Warsinger knows her caern is under attack and it's clear she realizes that Ryan is a mere pup and likely wouldn't understand rank and auspice.

                  The script itself... yeah it could use some work. Some of the dialogue works but a good chunk of it is a mess. And when it boils right down to it the game's story as a whole suffers from Special Snowflake Syndrome, it is after revolving around playing as the last untainted White Howler. Now I don't have a problem with playing as the WH, in fact the first W20 game I ran involved modern day WH, or even the idea of the chronicle revolving around the only untainted Howler but rather my issue is with how they handled it as presented in these cutscenes. Admittedly we are miss one cutscene and any possible in-game dialogue but I doubt it would have made the story better. The things I particularly dislike about is the whole hunt for a magic McGuffin, prevent a plot to bring about the Apocalypse, storm a Pentex facility, send a young pup into the Black Spiral, while he's all more or less on his own. I have a similar issue with Bloodlines but that particular issue with that game is offset to a degree by the story which does do a good of giving the feel of being a neonate getting jerked around by their elders. At least until the later parts where it turns into a shooting gallery.

                  Then again this was a game being made in the late 90's and would have been a 3rd person action game. Still it does make on wish for more video games with the CWoD IP. I love the VtM games and spent whole weekends doing four player co-op on the HtR games.
