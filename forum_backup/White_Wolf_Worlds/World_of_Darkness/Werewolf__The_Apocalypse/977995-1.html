                                Black Furies celebrate the feminine to the exclusion of the masculine, so a male rejecting the masculine in favor of the feminine to the degree of a transwoman would probably be considered to be a good, if unusual, convert to the cause (the inability to reproduce would be a minor issue, but there are always more men who can reproduce with Garou females). They would probably not be as forgiving towards transmen though, as a female rejecting the feminine in favor of the masculine to the degree of transmen would probably be considered to be a traitor to the cause (the inability to reproduce would be a major issues because women capable of bearing Garou without a Garou males are uncommon). Of course, this is a Kinfolk issue because, quite frankly, the regenerative capabilities of the Garou would prevent them from changing their sex without some interesting form of magic.
