As it stands in W5, a movement to "go loud" is just going to get shut down by silver bullet armed spec ops soldiers before it can do anything that can't be covered up.
                        That's something that I think the writers of 5th edition have massively overlooked in regards to trying to present the SI as a legitimate threat. Vampires may need to actively work to cover up a breach of the masquerade, but the Garou have Delirium, while Changelings have The Mists.

                        It doesn't matter how advanced their technology is in the modern era, they're still susceptible to the genetically inherited PTSD that is Delirium. You send in an SI strike team against a pack of werewolves, and they're going to be reduced to screaming, quivering heaps, as they collapse into a puddle of their own urine.

                        Even those rare individuals who have the willpower to respond with aggression do so in a manic, frenzied state. Hardly the kind of mental state necessary to be carrying out a coordinated assault.
