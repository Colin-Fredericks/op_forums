    On the Anurana Becoming a Full-Fledged Changing Breed

    02-19-2016, 08:34 PM
    I saw the write-up of the Anurana in the W20 Book Of The Wyrm, and I have to say I was intrigued. So, supposing that such a thing would be possible (and I refer you to the gaming rule of "Why the Heck not?"), how could such a transition happen, given their current status as backwoods New England frog-people like Lovecraft's nightmares after a highschool science class dissection? What sort of entity/force could become an effective patron of this new Breed? Perhaps Haha no Fukumi (The 1000 Hells, P. 60) would be a willing benefactor, having new agents to punish crimes committed with water or fire. ​Anyone else have any suggestions?

                          Where I live in Michigan there is a similar myth to the Bunyip, the First Nations people call it Mishipeshu. The story as I wrote it; after the Bunyip tribe died out, the totem Bunyip traveled the waterways out of Australia. He found his way to the America's and eventually to the Great Lakes where he got a new name by the local peoples. How his path would cross with the Anurana is simple, the eventual furniture industry and logging industry in the great lakes regions was massive and the lakes are still recovering. The automobile industry was born on the banks of the great lakes and it still has a lasting impact there. Basically the perfect breeding ground for the Anurana and the second home of a Totem the has been in hiding for the last century.

                          Additional Reasons:
                          He is a powerful amphibious totem, fits with the new Breed.
                          Currently pissed at the Garou but not at Gaia.
                          Which leads to, currently unattached to any breed.
                          Doesn't require a lot of rework as the gift he would have access to teaching already fit the breed.

                          General Story break down:
                          First Act; I could easily see him guiding them from the shadows once they parted ways with Pentex much how Cockroach does with the Samsa.
                          Second Act; Once the proved they were worth his time, he guides them to the Gurahl (also locals) to help deal with their spiritual corruption.
                          Third Act; Confrontation with the Garou culminating in the totem making it clear they aren't going to take anything more from him, resulting in an uneasy acceptance of the Anurana.

                              A changing breed for the Wyrm rather than a Mockery. Hmm.

They find a likeness to their incarna maejlin, the Spawn of the Wyrm as warriors for the knight of decay as they distort and disrupt the waters of the world and the minds of Gaias creatures.

                              For the Gaia side, theres one really obvious Totemic Patron, Frog. That which represents change, the water and the Wylds wisdom and power all held within its tiny body. Proving even the greatest changes and adaptations can come from something so unexpected and versatile.

                              These new breed protect the water ways, what they refer to as the very veins of Gaias life blood. Moving earth, summoning the rains, and guiding the rivers to keep all creatures great and small with life giving water and using their first hand knowledge of corrupt companies and poisonous proliferations of waste dumping practices to target and undo the machinations of the wyrm.
