    What's the renown hit for losing your totem?

    05-03-2022, 10:31 AM
    Due to some great rp, the pc's were forced/chose to act in a way that finally drove off their totem, Mammoth. They weren't surprised when it happened, but I'm wondering what the renown hit is for such a loss? Will it make finding a new totem harder, as the spirits will be more wary of the pack? Or will that not be a problem if the new totem is in alignment with their new identity?

                  Renown gains and losses aren't limited to the examples in the corebook. They're just suggestions. So STs shouldn't be hidebound to those lists, just use them for inspirations.

                  Normally it is OK for Garou to leave packs and join another. So we don't expect Garou to lose renown for that. But a Garou who habitually violate pack bans, or act in other ways that anger their pack totem is not normal. It would be considered improper behavior and subject to potential loss of Renown. Especially if the entire pack did it.

                  Now a pack's members and the pack totem are essentially in a long term chiminage relationship. Packs aren't explained that way, but I think it's pretty obvious that they are. There is some kind of agreement between a totem and its children (the pack members) on how they are supposed to honor it in exchange for its support (the benefits of the Totem background and other ways a patron spirit can help the pack). That often gets handwaved because it's assume it's mostly flavor text, and that whatever the pack does, it'll still enjoy the support of their totem.

                  For a pack to so offend their totem spirit, it means there must have been very egregious behavior. Now if the end was somehow amicable in nature (both sides decide a divorce is best and move on), then I'd keep any Renown loss to a minimum - perhaps even none at all. But if the totem spirit left in anger, then I think the Renown loss should be notable.

                  = =
                  I think any Renown loss would be in Honor and Wisdom. There's probably not any Glory loss unless the totem was a Totem of War, and the reason for leaving had to do with actions in combat or lack of bravery.

                  At the high end of loss, I would use several things as my guide. The first is the -6 Honor loss of "speaking poorly of one's pack". For Wisdom, there's a -3 penalty for "failing to perform regular duties and chores for the sept." Both of those involve circumstances that I think would be similar to a pack driving its pack totem away.

                  So I would probably start with a -6 Honor loss and a -3 Wisdom loss. If there were particular acts that were sacrilegious or blasphemous that might extend beyond that particular spirit and harm the sept itself by association, then I'd add an actual Punishment Rite like Ostracism or Stone of Scorn in addition to that. But those would be even more exceptional circumstances than what it is already.

                  If the departure was more amicable, I'd reduce the Renown loss accordingly. As I said, I might eliminate all together. It really depends on the circumstance.

                  All of that would be separate from something like Spirit Notoriety which is just a more hostile reaction by a certain group/brood of spirits, and would be separate from their reputation among Garou.

                  = =
                  As for whether other spirits might be more reluctant to become a pack totem to the pack, again it really depends on the exact circumstances. The more egregious the circumstances, the more the pack members should be treated suspiciously. The more amicable it is, it might not even be an issue.

                  At the very least, I'd have any potential pack totem spirit address the issues that caused the previous totem to leave. Not issues involving the nature of the dispute, but in terms of how the pack failed to honor and follow the pack totem. They'd be concerned about the issues of chiminage and proper honoring, and any root causes of the pack's behavior that may have violated that. Even spirits very different than Mammoth or Griffin would still be concerned about the nature of the pack's relationship to their totem even if they didn't care about the actual behavior itself that cause Mammoth to leave.

                  So I would make the spirit want to define things more explicitly than what happened before. Make sure certain things are laid out, proper behavior defined, and red lines established. Think of it like a prenuptial agreement. Spirits may be willing to give these Garou another chance, but they'll negotiate more conditions and explicit promises to make sure the PCs don't alienate them like they did their previous pack totem.

                  If it is just an amiable split, that may be all that is needed. If the break up was a result of the PCs really offending the spirit by ignoring basic chiminage guidelines and pissing all over the relationship of their spiritual father, then I would make things worse. Other members of Griffin's brood or spirits close to Mammoth might refuse outright on any further interaction with these Garou. Other spirits might be willing to do so, but demand far higher price for their help - donation of Permanent Gnosis, additional bans, and so on. It's like a bank asking for more collateral from someone who has a poor credit rating - they need to pay higher interest rates too. They are willing to take a risk, but only by securing more immediate benefits to cover the potential risk of the Garou driving them away as well. Or if it was very bad, then most of the normal Garou totems might reject them entirely. In that case, there might still be spirits willing to be their totem - unpopular, desperate spirits with few Garou children who want that extra hands to do their work (but would still charge more for their help than normal). You would need to determine what spirits these would be.

                  Only you know the exact circumstances of the break up, but these would be my guidelines. In short, treat this like a bad credit rating that you determine based on the specifics, and then have spirits negotiate accordingly.

                      There's a lot of context involved in the situation; which is probably why there isn't a default Renown loss for it.

                      I wouldn't get too harsh here; it sounds like whatever happened that probably were doing enough good they should net positive even if not as high as they'd get without the loss of their Totem. Losing your Totem also sucks as it is.

                      There's also no default answer on how the rest of the spirits are going to feel about this. The Garou and the spirits live in existences where there's a real threat of being put in no-win situations like this. Understanding making the least bad choice you can is a thing. Totems are also supposed to have a relationship with their pack. Pissing off your Totem is common enough that the Garou have a standardize approach to making amends...which implies it also works often enough that the spirits don't get grudge-happy about it (though Griffin and Mammoth aren't great about that by spirit standards).

                      I wouldn't worry too much, esp. on the numbers. Make sure there are narrative consequences, throw in some Renown hit and even the Flaw, but if the players want to reconcile, there are ways to do so that tend to mean story fodder, or moving on to a different approach to things is a whole other fun experience to try out.
