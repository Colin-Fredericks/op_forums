      Tough one
      Depending the interpretation ,really
      The Patriarch is, according the Black Furies, the Incarna of jealous men, and a servant of Abhorra, the Urge Wyrm of hatred - if you take this, then he probably is in Malfeas
      Well ,we "know" it is not "true"
      The Patriarch is in fact a facet of the Weaver, representing Dogma - encourages the blind ritualization of life, harsh guidelines, and equally harsh punishments against transgressors of any kind. From him, mankind learned to evaluate certain ideals over others.
      The Patriarch (or Dogma) is a very old, and very strong concept, even the Garou are his followers in a sense becasue their whole life and society built around a set of dogmas, the Litany
      Killing him would be difficult and probably would usher Chaos. But his weakness would be this rigidity and the laws and dogmas inherent built-in contradictions
      A bit like the old divide 0 problem for the computers
      The Patriarch/Dogma is so rigid, it can't change, can't bend, can't adapt, or just very slowly. Facing with the contradictions and systematically destroying his source of power, eg. relegion would strenghten other Weaver avatars, while weaken The Patriarch/Dogma - I think this is supported by the fact the influence of the Patriarch was strongest during the Dark Ages and the early Renaissance, but has diminished in later years, only to rise again thanks to growing fundamentalist movements in various religions.
      The Weaver has three Incarna: The Machine (the manifestation of her gift of Technology), the Patriarch (the manifestation of Dogma), and Science (the manifestation of Science, of course).
      By demolish Relegion would strengthen Science and even the Machine, technology and science don't have room for relegion and relegious dogmas
      Relegion is the most "dogmatic", but per definition Dogma is a principle or set of principles laid down by an authority as incontrovertibly true.
      So the Patriarch as the manifestation of Dogma is a slipery eel when it comes to weakining him but not impossible - even could be killable I think

      Where you can find him?
      I would say in the CyberRealm, in the center of Spider City, on the top with his "brothers" Science and Technology, in a gigantic techno-church like structure

          I think that those two may actually be different entities. There are many things in the Umbra that represent different facets of the same concepts. The Wicked Patriarch of Malfeas may coexist with the Patriarch of the Weaver and a totemic ancestral human protector known as the Patriarch.

                                              The follow-up is important though - the Patriarch dying would certainly create a wave of freedom, sexism would be less vitriolic, men less controlling, women less likely to serve as handmaidens of patriarchy (in many situations women serve as the main enforcers of patriarchy), but humanity would need to learn from it and change and make good use of the spiritual reprieve.

                                              If it's a nice holiday, but ultimately humanity doesn't capitalize on the opportunity, things will gradually return to how they were. A similar spirit will rise up to fill the gap, perhaps with a slightly different set of quirks. A long lasting and genuine grass roots change would need to follow, and that's endless, little, global work that is the work of whole networks of Septs and charities and faiths and so on.

                                              Basically if a group of heroic Garou kill a powerful conceptual spirit, an evil one such as Patriarch or one of the Lords of the Wyrmish Elements, the real battle is fought by humanity itself in the aftermath; are they worthy of the new hand they've been dealt, or will they go back to more of the same.
