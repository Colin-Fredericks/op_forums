      Let's see if I can help a little bit. I'm not referencing any materials, so others might have some specifics to help out and correct me where I'm canonically wrong:
      • Awakening Spirits. Most things don't really have their own spirit. That gun you just bought? It's spirit is so weak that it may have a few functions, but no "personality". The gun that was a hunter's best gun and named "Sally", essential in the American War of Independence, passed down by generations, used as an archaic weapon by the Confederates in the Civil War, used as the symbol of family pride publicly and to this day carries the hopes of a bloodline?... Sally just might wake up and have a hell of a story to tell.

        Some questions are purposefully left vague. In a Storyteller game, it's really up to the Storyteller to make some of those decisions at their own whim and not be bogged down in the minutia of game mechanic details.

        I know that some items, when "awakened" also have special properties. One of the books says that awakening sage will create a number of black scorpions to appear. Weird. "Awakening" an item is basically like turning a snowman into a living thing by placing a hat on it... it didn't necessarily have anything in it before, but it had the potential. Sometimes that "potential" is sparked by enough spiritual energy making it wake up, or have a presence of its own. Once it's been imbued with that spiritual energy, it can be taken into the Umbra because it has a presence there now.
      • Tribal Gifts/etc. Realistically, I think that the way most Garou "know all the Rites/Gifts available and go through the list getting the ones they want" isn't really thematically appropriate to the setting. The knowledge of what Gifts are available would be held with Elders and records, passed down. A Garou sees someone slash their claws on rocks and "Woah! They're sharp like razors! I want to learn to do that!" So you go to that Garou and ask them how they learned it... and either they teach you (the hard way), or they send you to find the spirit type that taught them (the easy way.) There should be a little bit of RP at least to learn the special spirit trick. But because a game of 5 Garou trying to have special scenes learning each Gift and Rite the way that it makes sense in the setting just wouldn't give you time to actually RP, so players go through the books, find the ones they want and often "wham, you learn that Gift." While I'm sure there are collections of "A Net Spider will teach you how to travel through telephone lines" is kept somewhere among the Glass Walkers, unless they told a Uktena, I'm not sure they'd know what spirit taught it, IF they even know that "Spirit Trick" (as I call them IC) exists. Maybe somewhere there's a lore-keeper, but especially with the Glass Walkers coming up with new Gifts every few years, I don't think that the other Tribes are omniscient. Again, for sake of gameplay it's often hand-waved in some way though, or given some reason why the character even knew such a Gift existed, even if they've never talked to anyone who ever used it.
      • In our game, we have Silver spirits. I don't see a reason why you can't have different metal spirits, especially in an area where it's significant. Hell, in an old mine, you might find a coal spirit even. Sure, why not? If it helps the story, they're all unique. "I'm looking for a titanium spirit to imbue into my knife to make it stronger." Sure. They may all fall under Earth, or Metal, but they could be individual too.
      • I'd say probably not in general... maybe the fact that the character is a Garou, the ancestors of the Fera turn their back on them out of spite. Or maybe when you're Garou, it blocks out the other non-Garou Ancestors. But if it helps your story, I suppose you could, but personally I like to keep my milk and pickles separate.
      • Fera/Fera, or Fera/Garou breeding is actually in the book. Basically... it can be kin of both, or Kin to one, Shifter to another. Obviously a Cat-born Balam/Garou isn't going to be a pup, so if it's born to an animal, it'll be that animal-type. In an earlier edition, breeding Fera/Garou was 10%+10%=20% of True Born, but it's just 10% again, and it can go either way (at least for Homids. Again, a Cat can't/shouldn't lay a Corax egg, right?)
      • I would say that it does. It was made for them, and being "Kinfolk" is part of that Rite. Rites are basically pacts with spirits. The pact required a Kinfolk, so that's why I'd say it has to be one.
      • Unless you take a Battle Scar on the wound table, I say you grow your limb back. At least, anything lost AFTER your First Change. It may take a very long time, it may come back, but be useless as your body and spirit try to reknit that connection lost. I don't know that it's actually spelled-out, but "Lost Limb" in the Battle Scar table is the only place I think I've seen it.
      • Only Spirits of the Umbra teach Gifts, using their spiritual tricks. Demons, Wraiths, Ghosts, etc. are totally different things (unless you specifically have an item that lets you steal the powers of another supernatural and use it, but, again, that's stealing, not learning.)
      • Lasombra Abyss exists in the Vampire sphere of the CWoD. If there's a cross-over, it's up to the ST to decide. There's some concocted ideas that some writers and fans have created to try to make the whole CWoD somehow fit together... but you wind up with a Psycho Billy Cadillac (Johnny Cash song) that doesn't work well pieced together. So it's better just to approximate for your setting.
        THEN AGAIN, it could be the Abyss of W:tA and Nightmaster just might be an ally to the Lasambra, and that's how they get in and out of the spirit realm. Again, depends on what game setting your'e playing in.

      Again, just my thoughts and input. Use as you see fit.

          1) The only default for most things is that the very weak spirit inside of it wakes up in the Umbra. As Torakhan noted, some items of particular emotional value might have more going for them up to the ST's say-so. The exception is plants. Awakening plant spirits (called sanctification) is a variant way of making Talens instead of using Binding. Each plant has different effects (so of the books have some sample ideas for this).

          2) The awakened spirit has the potential to use Charms, but would generally be too weak to use any but the basic ones all spirits have. If the spirit is cared for and helped to grow it power, it would eventually develop Charms appropriate for its rank.

          3) Using an awakened item as the vessel for a Fetish or Talen is generally not a good idea. If you're trying to awaken a hammer to make a hammering Fetish out of its own spirit, it's going to be too weak for awhile for this. If you're going to summon up a different spirit to bind to the awakened item, you'll just drive the awakened spirit back to slumber.

          4) If you have the ability to, though again, most are barely sentient. Don't expect much

          5) Only if you have the ability to interact with spirits across the Gauntlet. The spirit exists in the Penumbral reflection of the material world, and goes where the object goes, but the two are on different planes of existence.

          6) Nothing usually. It will most likely just fall back asleep and cease to have a spiritual presence to attack. If the spirit is destroyed, there's no default effect, but it would probably make the item less useful/invalid for things (like Talisman Dedication).

          Do werewolves know of the gifts other tribes posses? Does everyone read the silver record and go 'oh, flame dance looks useful. Might get that'

          The books aren't really good on how the Tribal Gift affinities translate into in-character knowledge. The safest take is largely your second part of this: skilled Garou (Cosmology, Enigmas, Rituals, Occult) know what kind of spirits teach what kind of Gifts, and thus would have a good idea of what kind of Gifts would be common among the broods of the Tribal Totems.

          I don't know if metal spirits are just one thing (IE there is one metal spirit, who represents all metals) or individuals (iron, copper, brass, mercury, silver, gold') I've read that metal elementals take on the apearance of certain metals, but i'm not sure if they're spirits of specific metals.
          Both. The weaker a spirit is, the more specific it tends to be. Just like awakened spirits are the spirits of specific items, and are generally among the weakest spirits, gafflings metal spirits are going to be more limited in conceptual scope than jaggling metal spirits, and so on.

          Create element. (a gift of many questions)
          -100lbs is the max of this... Couldn't one create compressed elements for overpowered explosives?
          -If a player tries a higher difficulty or gets a lot of successes, can they create nice things? (Blue fire, clay, Mud, Rock, sand, Really fertile earth, ice, boiling water, an updraft...) or are these too 'specialised'
          -Can one use this gift to make art? (dirt statues, dirt statues everywhere)
          - If each success is a cubic foot, do all cubic feet have to be together ?
          -Is it conceivable that, with enough spirit bothering, one can get a more powerful/specialised version of this gift? (IE city elements, wyrm elements, specific things like explosives , radioactives, toxins, valuables... ) I for one eagerly anticipate the creation of francium and polonium.
          1) No, you can't create compressed elements. The Gift simply creates the element in question in the area directed. If you want to use it, you have to manipulate it yourself.

          2) There's a bit of a mix here. You can't go for a higher difficulty, and successes just determine how much you make. You can specify some basic stuff (like clay for earth), but "nice" (such as flames that are different from the natural fire color of whatever you're burning, extra fertile earth, etc) isn't really what the Gift is supposed to do.

          3) No, see #1; unless a pile of dirt counts as a statue.

          4) It has to be one instance. Material that natural breaks up will not stick together though

          5) There's nothing stopping such Gifts from existing that are more specialized or potent than Create Element. Some possible ones are in various books. It isn't a matter of spirit bothering though. It's about learning which ones teach what, and getting on the good sides of the ones you want to learn from.

          Can a garou use fera ancestors?
          While Garou can have Fera ancestors, they usually lack the spiritual connection to access them via the Ancestors Background. While there isn't a direct way to do this in the game, but there's precedent for Ancestor modifying Merits, and Mokole could potentially help a Garou with such ancestors access them.

          Two different changing breeds breed. What happens? Some of them don't make metis in the first place, some of them have weird effects (like parents dying for the kitsune, or the child dying for mokole). Will it even be a metis? (I'm assuming the mother decides on what breed the changer will be)
          Different Changing Breeds don't make Metis if mated together ever. Most of the time it's a kin to the mother. If it happens to be changer, again and as you assumed, it follows the mother.

          Is the rite of sacred rebirth only for kinfolk?
          As-written, yes. Only kinfolk have enough connections to the spirits to enact such a rite.

          Do garou regenerate entire limbs?
          Unless lost with a Battle Scar, yes.

          Do demons/Wraiths/other spiritish things teach gifts?
          No. While there might be a theoretical way for this to happen, the entities of the Underworld and the Abyss-Prison are too disconnected from the Garou to act as teachers like this. Wraiths and Demons generally wouldn't know how even if it were possible.

          The lasombra abyss (not to be confused with that canyon-abyss which I think is under malfeas (possibly mistaken) is outside the triat/creation and not a place a Garou will ever wonder into, right?
          What the Lasombra Abyss is, and what it might be connected to, are left up to a STs to decide. However many Garou believe the Abyss Realm (which is in the Near Umbra, the Abyss-Prison for the Fallen is what's under Malfeas) is connected to vampires though.
