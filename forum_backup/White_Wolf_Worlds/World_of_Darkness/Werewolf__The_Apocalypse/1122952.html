    Shouldn't Kindred be Wyrm *and* Weaver tainted?

    07-31-2017, 06:15 AM
    So Vampires are stuck in the moment of their death. Absolute stasis; this is confirmed as of Time of Thin Blood. Vitae is a preservative, the best one in existence; it makes animals and humans live forever, and it keeps a Vampire stuck in a default state that they revert to each night.

    I can see the justification for Wyrm taint, certainly. They're a dead body walking around, feeding on living people in a parasitic way. BUT, considering that they can only exist because of stasis (a 'beautiful corpse' as V20 puts it, existing forever), shouldn't they reek of the Weaver too? If you look at the behaviour of your average Vampire, Weaver taint makes more and more sense; Kindred who reject stability don't last very long, and the possibility of living forever makes many cautious builders rather than rabid destroyers.

    As a side-note, I think that 'Vitae as a preservative' could be a good explanation for why Garou and Fera are so violently allergic to Kindred blood; they're living creatures who embody the best and worst aspects of being alive, and part of that is aging and eventually dying. Having Ghouldom be an easy out for age-anxious Shifters should be a terrible oversight on the part of Spirits, as it would be a fast-track to serving the Wyrm. Just look at the Garou's treatment of the Gurahl; many of them WANT an easy way out of mortality, they want to be eternal warriors and fight the good fight forever in many cases. Making them violently allergic to one of the most obvious methods of immortality is a smart move.
