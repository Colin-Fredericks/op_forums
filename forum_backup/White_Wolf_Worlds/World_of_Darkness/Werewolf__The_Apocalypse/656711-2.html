                                I have to tag in here and join the death-and-desolation camp. Gaia gave the Garou Rage, but the Wyrm corrupted it. It's not the humanity of the Garou that provokes them to atrocities, it's the Wyrm. That's a key point of what Werewolf: The Apocalypse. The Horror you fight with Rage and tooth and claw until you're laying bloody on the ground beside your kin... that horror is deep inside of you. And you're it's instrument every bit as much as you are it's enemy.
                                The Garou can't win, because at the end of the day the evil is inside them. If the Garou beat back the Wyrm, it would still be in their hearts. I think this would create a depressingly poetic Apocalypse. The Garou find a way to beat back the existential horror of the Wyrm, celebrate their victory, begin building the world they desire.. Only to see it all ruined and brought to a tragic, end as Gaia's lifeblood stains the Garou's hands from the atrocities the victorious Garou Nation first inflicts on the world and then on each other.

                                                Actually, it might very well be how it works. One of the tertiary plots in W:tA is that the Theurges have a recurring debate from time to time about the spiritual nature of Rage itself. Most Garou (for obvious reasons) hold that Rage was a gift from Luna. Some Theurges however (and heretically in the minds of many Garou) posit that it is in fact a curse of the Wyrm and has nothing to do with Luna at all. A somewhat larger minority (and still somewhat heretically in the minds of many Garou) maintain that it was a gift from Luna, but through their own sins, the Garou unintentionally allowed the Wyrm into the collective soul of the Garou and thereby allowed this great gift to be cursed by the Wyrm -- and this is why the Garou in the modern age have so little control over their Rage and why there is such a thing as Thrall-of-the-Wyrm frenzies. Incidentally, this basic concept was a fairly central plot element in Last Battle (although that novel was never officially canon).

But I think there's a few points here. Humans aren't inherently evil, and it's not the "human side" of a Garou, if such a thing can be said to exist, that makes the Garou worse. Humans are of Gaia. Left the way Gaia had created them, Humans would have existed within the Balance and not pursued the excesses that are killing Gaia today.
                                                        The Garou are their own downfall, for the same reason that humanity is so flawed. The Wyrm. The Wyrm corrupts all things of Gaia. It's the corruption of the Wyrm that makes humans as bad as they are in the World of Darkness. And it's the Wyrm's taint on the Garou that caused the Impergium and the War of Rage and the inter-tribal fighting. It's the Wyrm's touch that takes Rage from being a pure force for the defense of Gaia and twists it into an instrument of self-harm.
                                                        I feel that this inner-corruption of the Garou is absolutely essential for the horror of WtA to come through. A Garou is the monster he fights. Garou are heroes, but not simple ones. They're monsters, and they commit innumerable crimes of their own. They're tragic heroes that ruin their own cause. And this is critical. Because without this inner-corruption, I feel you depart from the themes of WtA into a Furry-Gritty-Captain Planet game instead.
                                                        And that's why in my view, a Garou victory would have to end in self-defeat. Anything less than that misses the entire point. Of course, your Chronicle may vary.
