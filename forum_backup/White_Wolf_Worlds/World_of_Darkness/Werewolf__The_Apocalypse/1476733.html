      In Werewolf, almost everything related to faeries goes to Stag, but Dana is one of the less known Incarna that also has ties there (I can't remember anything concerning Luna though). I don't remember anything faerie related that goes to Unicorn, which in Werewolf is almost exclusively devoted to peace, friendship, and all things My Little Pony.. The game even makes an attempt to say that any chimera or bygones of actual unicorns, griffins, and other magical creatures that would appear in a fairyland, don't have anything to do with Totem Incarnas mentioned in Werewolf.

      But I think it's fine in your chronicle if you want there to be, and make any appropriate changes you need. But I don't think there's any official rules that support it. Happy to be corrected by others.

                      The Moon has some links to literal dreams, but Luna itself isn't frequently linked to the Dreaming as a dimension. It is far more related to creativity and, obviously, glamour. I don't think it wouldn't work, but it has never been brought up on either Werewolf or Changeling.

                      A problem here is to define what exactly the Dreaming is in the Umbra. It has a connection, but the games never went far defining it. Is it the Dream Zone? Related to it? Something else entirely? Defining the place of the Dreaming within the cosmology will define which other Totem may have anything to do with the Fae without specific events playing out.

                      You also have the option of linking any Totem to the Fae because of some previous event. Unlike other denizens of the WoD, Changelings simply love to mingle with other things, safety be damned. Other supernaturals have a hard time recognizing and remembering them, but they deal with everything and everyone all the time, so it isn't hard at all to have a Fae coming anywhere in the Umbra through any number of shenanigans and interacting with a Totem or something the Totem cares about in such a way that the Totem remembers and starts to deal in some fairy stuff.

                      Finally, there are Fae creatures that deal with spirits in addition to the Dreaming more or less directly. Above all, the Nunnehi and Menehune are Changelings that have Totems of their own. You may want to look into them.

                              Travel is a confirmed possibility as there are Trods that do that link. Which only complicates the matter of cosmology as it implies both connection and distinction, but ease stories as it means you're not stepping out of canonical possibilities when having things crossing over between Dreaming and Umbra in some fashion (it isn't common, though).

                              Also, we have things like casual observer from either side would hardly perceived the other phenomena as other. There are huge distinctions between Spirits and Chimera (Dream Stuff), but they're not obvious to any but those truly knowledgeable on both, which would be very rare characters. The variety of phenomena and absurdity of their possible manifestations only muddy waters even more for most characters even in the given splats. Totems, as ancient and wise spirits, may have a far easier time figuring things out and striking deal and what-not with the travelers of the Umbral Realms and even beyond.

                                      I’m sympathetic to the idea of potentially tying Unicorn into the Dreaming somehow although one of the big things that holds me back is from an OOC perspective not wanting to step too much on Stag and the Fianna’s toes.

                                      Personally my vision of Unicorn is heavily influenced by Black Fox’s earlier writing on Unicorn as a totem of purity. This alongside with the general idea that the Children of Gaia feel a deep connection/cause with general humanity and their vision of a pan-gaian society is how I generally view the purview of Unicorn and their tribe.

                                      From that perspective I do think you can come to an interesting position for Unicorn and the Coggies to occupy. Basically they view themselves dually as defenders of the pure essences of Gaia (Mother) with humanity as part of that. So I think that they’d be inclined to help defend the Dreaming and particularly any Dreamers they came across. However I think that where issues may come in is their humanism. I can easily imagine a group of Coggies becoming very agitated with not only Nightmare changelings/chimera but also any Dreaming changelings/chimera who could ostensibly be a threat to a harmonious and pure human society. So while I think that as defenders of Dreamers they’d probably be as good as Fianna, the issues would arise from their lack of understanding for the Fae perspective and their dedication to bringing about their vision of a harmonious gaian world.

                                          That's a good point, but personally I never liked the idea that the unicorns that are Bygones or part of the Dreaming aren't "really" Unicorns of the Incarna totem. So in my chronicles, I simply said the real unicorns that the Unicorn totem represented ended up in the Dreaming once the physical Realm became too hostile with them (the same applies to griffons). So Unicorn is relevant there. Not relevant as Stag, because Stag made the deals that protected the Dreaming and Unicorn did not. So while Unicorn does not have any special powers in the Dreaming, the unicorns found in the Dreaming are the children of Unicorn.

                                          I don't have unicorns be common in the Dreaming. They are wild, untamed beasts, equally shy with the Fae as they were with mortals. But one can meet "real" unicorns in the Dreaming. But Unicorn as a totem doesn't have anything more special to do with the Dreaming as do any other natural totem has to do with the Realm. But the unicorns one could meet in the Dreaming would react friendly with packs of Unicorn.

                                          Stag, though, has deeper ties, and thus is involved in "Deep Magic" tied to the Dreaming that Unicorn does not.
