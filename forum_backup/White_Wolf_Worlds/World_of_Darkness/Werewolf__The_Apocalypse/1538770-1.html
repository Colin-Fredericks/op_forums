            The game is Werewolf, ergo, the Garou were right. The Fera (minus the Kitsune, who didn't exist yet) were all miserable, useless failures, so Gaia put the Garou into the field to whip the others into shape. They refused to fall in line because of pride and (in most cases) Rage. When they disobeyed the Garou, they disobeyed Gaia, and they got what was coming to them; it's really as simple as that.

            I would only append one thing saying that the stupid party in this has always been Gaia. Even before trying to fix things with the Garou, she created creatures that have Rage (their gift from the Wyrm), and which, Rage or no Rage, are hostile to one another in nature. Keeping in mind that nature is Gaia's domain, she was seriously idiotic to expect them to work together at all, so while I stand on the Fera failing, and the Garou being right to put them down for their betrayal, Gaia is the real guilty party in this, just by virtue of sheer incompetence.
