    Amazon War Campaign

    03-14-2017, 11:39 PM
    Hello Folks!

    I'm starting a new game and the set up will be somewhere in the Amazon War (probably 70ish).

    I'll be using the Warg System implemented by Gogol Fang First (which may or may not show up in the party) and may play with 3 or 4 groups (apart but in the same war front, like in a Pack of another Warg and so on, so they can meet)

    That being said I'm after some thoughts about the questions bellow:

    1) What type of Fera other then Garou would be acceptable to use? I've made some NPCs of other Feras, such a Mokolé and Balam, but I'm willing the let players choose some other Fera and put them in the front line of the war and will try to work out the differences between races (Mokolé will never forget the Garou).
    2) I'm thinking of using some Spiral Dancers desguised as Ronin Garou in the Pack.
    3) What about moon bridges? Would it be acceptable to use moon bridges with connection to Amazon as outposts, so I can work the social side too?
    4) As they will be more than one group of players, I was thinking in using some Rite to put them in a PVP session, not to the death (or maybe yes, who knows right?), giving rewards for that.

    Thanks in advance!

      I think introducing Fera into anything involving the Garou is fraught with peril. It can work with a single pack of PCs, but the Amazon War is a big deal with over a hundred Garou warriors at a minimum. Now, supposedly Golgol finally kissed and made up with the Balam... but it never really went into detail how exactly they resolved the little issue of the Garou stealing the Balam Den Realms and turning them into caerns. Presumably they're still holding Hollow Heart and Sky River. That coupled with the Garou going around murdering Balam whenever they could find them for some 20 odd years, makes me think that the relationship is probably still quite tense. I'd say that Corax and Nuwisha would be about the only really workable Fera other than the Balam and Mokole (and even the latter assumes that the Nuwisha are playing themselves off as Garou).

      ​Garou generally don't let Ronin anywhere near their caerns. If they do, they watch them extremely carefully, and if they're using them in the Amazon, they're (especially since it's the Get who run the show down there) probably using them as cannon-fodder or bait. So the BSDs trying to infiltrate as Ronin is not really likely to have any up-side to it -- and thus I can't really see the Dancers trying it.

      ​As for the thing about the Moon Bridges, I think you might want to clarify a little bit. Do you mean using Moon Bridges to reach various parts of the Amazon to cut down on travel time, or something? That would also probably not be a really terrific idea. In order for the bridge to be stable, one would need a pathstone at both termini. First, pathstones are not real common, and second, just leaving one lying around in the jungle where anybody can pick it up doesn't sound appealing (this is not a problem when the Moon Bridge terminus -- and thus the pathstone itself -- is within the bawn of a caern). As for established Moon Bridges, there are multiple ones from various caerns to Hollow Heart, and a secondary one from Hollow Heart to Sky River. If memory serves, there isn't even a Bridge between Rain Spirits and the other two caerns at all.

              -Nowhere near the numbers of the Balam, but the Pumonca should be present, since cougars range all over South America. That at least gives you another Bastet breed as a possibility.
              -Ratkin are everywhere, and have a significant presence in South America. It would be hard to imagine them not being involved at all.

                                              Hello!

                                              I use the Rage across the Amazon currently for a TT game that has been running for about 6 or 7 months now. I can say the Warg system has been really useful to use, and Golgol just being a presence has had a great effect for the characters and players after playing two years prior with mostly adren and a couple of Athro as Npcs only introduced previously.

                                              I have given the book a w20 makeover, and bringing it up to the 2016/17 timeframe.

                                              I found watching documentaries really helped learn not just about the animals, but also what living there is like really useful as to its problems and hardships.

                                              The Explorer Ed Stafford a few years ago was the first to walk the Amazon river from beginning to end and it took nearly two and half years.

                                              Bruce Parry's documentaries show a lot of what living and depending on the rivers there is like and the hardships the indigenous peoples face.

                                              anyway, back to the questions and ideas.

                                              The Amazon river has Bull Sharks that swim it annually to breed. Being able to swim in fresh and salt water these fish can often be found sharing the rivers with caiman, electric eels, piranha and paramima.

                                              Just when you thought the river was dangerous enough right? I've had a single Rokea Deepwater on one stretch of water guarding it for when breeding season comes along and Rokea and kinfolk come swimming for mates. With the troubles of pentex and pollution to the waters feeding into the ocean it might not be difficult to have a few rokea come looking to see what is making the water bad elsewhere or maybe tracking something really terrible back to its source. If you wanted to add them.

                                              I've primarily used Mokole and Balam. My troupe are keeping away from the Balam. I think because they prefer the were crocs and have had a good history with helios as a pack previously. Rather than any other reason.

                                              I'd always sneak in a Corax if you wanted.

                                              And the Amazon is home to the largest rodents in the world. If a ratkin turned up but really I try to keep the focus on the garou.

                                              though saying that. The enemy they just faced and killed was a BSD theurge servant of Bat with a Camazotz fetish skull mask that had been using corrupted gifts and rites of the BatShifters as a way to haunt the garou at night through the forests and generally be terrifying with sacrifices and blood magic.

                                              I generally play up ronin as a life no garou would want. So I dont use them other than as a sorrowful state of warning for others on 'dont do what I did' kinda thing. But if you want to hide BSDs there are ways. I have fictitious people's I've made up that have been taken over by bsds as breeding stock they visit. You say indigineous tribe and my players leave the area alone and act respectful. Not one of them has been checked out. Wyrm taint sensed sure, reported back as info but nothing done yet.

                                              as for supply lines and moonbridges. I dont have moonbridges used often unless desperate. Because all the garou are trying to keep their sacred places secret and dont want the energies and bridges tracked or attacked while they are trying to control and wage war on so much already.

                                              I have it that the supplies are run by kinfolk and it can take weeks or months to get the next drop. It really heightens how precious every resource the characters have and can get hold off. Often trading things like guns and ammo or rites and swapping duties to gain something else they might need.

                                              and how dependent the garou are on their kin to get supplies in that they cant get in the jungles.

                                              The only time pvp comes up is if someone does something the others arent willing to let go. Pvp works great for vampire, but in werewolf I try to keep it to a minimum. Unless they are challenging for something. Then I let them go for it.

                                              I really really love the amazon game I'm running. If I could play in it I would I hope yours brings you a lot of enjoyment too.
