                                                            For all that Forsaken has been cannibalized for W5, one of its core aspects has been entirely removed.

                                                            Freedom.

                                                            There is no one way to be a Uratha. You can be a lone tyrant terrorizing a town. You can be a pack of police officers or white nationalists. You can work to better your territory or benefit from its decay. Your packmates can be other uratha, vampires, mages, regular wolves, humans or even spirits.

                                                            This matters, because if the Nation HAS fallen? What stops some packs of Red Talons from taking over land from humans and 'cleaning it up'? Yes, human response, but who cares about the Veil at that point? Maybe a pack of Glass Walker Cyberdogs starts experimenting more openly.

                                                            Basically, if you REALLY want a Nation has Fallen free for all? You can do SO MUCH with it. But W5 seems more interested in your everyday balancing of garou and human.
