                                            I just don't recall any book saying that Wyrm taint is caused by eating or hunting. I think that is a change you've made.

                                            If everything associated with the Balace Wyrm causes taint, then that includes weeding the garden, destroying inanimate objects, feeling any emotion associated with an Urge Wyrm even at healthy levels, and maybe even feeling Rage. The Balance Wyrm is one third of all possible activities on Earth.

                                            I'm struggling not to say something that looks like a reductio ad absurdem attack because the consequences would be colossal. Every predatory animal on Earth, maybe all animals, would reek of the Wyrm constantly, because none of them use Gaian rites. Every human being would reek of the Wyrm, indiscriminately, as though they were fomori. Even fire might inherently stink of the Wyrm. It would make the Gift useless. Why would Gaia, or the spirits, give them such a misleading and useless Gift? When the Gift is made useless by being surrounded by Wyrm taint (such as in Malfeas) the books explicitly call this out. The W20 Book of the Wyrm and some other books have Pentex deliberately spreading small amounts of Wyrm taint around to confuse the Garou and ruin the use of this Gift in particular - something they wouldn't bother with if all animals and humans everywhere were already tainted all the time.

                                            No book mentions any Garou saying "Yeah all hunting and killing causes Wyrm taint, so we are all tainted." or "Yeah, but all predators are of the Wyrm, so clearly that isn't a bad thing." Nobody is said to need a Rite of Cleansing after catching an evening meal. Some Garou worry that Rage is of the Wyrm, but they don't worry that eating a rabbit makes them spiritually unclean. On the contrary, hunting is natural and correct, and showing respect to one's prey makes it spiritually clean.

                                            Edit: Also, combatting the Wyrm "wherever it dwells and wherever it breeds" would be untenable if all wolves and humans everywhere were inherently of the Wyrm and accumulated Wyrm taint at every meal. That tenet of the Litany clearly refers to the Triatic Wyrm, but if the Garou can't tell the difference between Balance and Corruption, it would be unenforceable, at best.

                                                        Originally posted by Demigod Beast View Post
                                                        There’s a massive difference between ‘killing’ a Bane and eating a Bane.
                                                        There is a difference between reducing a bane to zero Essence/Power, and killing it permanently.

                                                        "Harvesting Gnosis" is the way to kill it permanently. There is no definitive description, how this happens in the game world:
                                                        - Do garou meditate near the bane and harvest the gnosis through an act of concentration and wishful thinking?
                                                        - Do they tear through the banes ephermal body, while chanting, hoping to find a magical gem that is the physical representation of the Gnosis to be harvested?

                                                        We don't know.
                                                        The common believe my LARP troupe developed over the last 20 years is, that the body of a spirit/bane needs to be consumed (at least partially) to harvest the gnosis. If the spirit/bane is of a type that makes this impossible or unbelievable then concentration and a physical contact with the spirit's/bane's body is required.

                                                        But, in general, in a lot of fantasy, and real world mysticism consumption is a means of metaphysical taking possession of metaphysical energy. Eating an enemies heart or Christian last supper ceremonies are examples.

                                                        You could argue that there is a massive difference between killing it permanently and eating it, but really, what is the difference?
                                                        That the garou aren't hungry anymore?
                                                        When we define the eating as the means of harvesting the Gnosis and therefore killing the bane and we don't attribute any effects to the act that we wouldn't also attribute to "harvesting Gnosis", then there isn't really a difference. Especially, since no alternatives are described on how "harvesting Gnosis" works. But unlike a table top game where someone states, "I harvest the banes Gnosis and therefore kill it" and the ST replies "fine" in a LARP game there needs to be consensus on what happens through gestures and physical display of action that work ideally without additional verbal description. That is acting like one was why eating the spirit/bane works well to portray the harvesting of Gnosis.
