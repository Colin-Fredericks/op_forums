    I imagine that the Black Furies would be fairly split on accepting transgender women into their group. Some would be fine with it, some would view it as a betrayal of Pegasus' beliefs.

    At the end of the day, who is a Black Fury and who isn't is more a spiritual matter than anything else, and if Pegasus decides that someone is suitably female to join her brood, that's what matters. Still, I suspect that more conservative Black Furies would treat transgender members like "second class" Black Furies and, those transgender BF's would often face discrimination and be forced to prove themselves more than biological females.

    I also suspect that transgender Black Furies would be encouraged to undergo rites or engage in spirit quests for the purpose of changing their physical sexual characteristics so that they can become female in both body and soul.

    The thing is though, not all transgender people want to change their physical bodies, something that more conservative Black Furies might simply not understand. They may feel, for example, that allowing transgender women to join the Black Furies is acceptable, with the unstated assumption that those Black Furies would be seeking out ways to become female in body. When confronted those transgender BF's who insist on keeping their male body parts, those conservative Black Furies might view this as a betrayal of the conditions that they were allowed into the Black Furies in the first place by.

    I think it's generally better if transgender BF's face some kind of discrimination (not intense, but something that might come up now and again) rather than simply be accepted without anyone batting an eye, if only because this is the World of Darkness we're dealing with, the game is more interesting when characters face challenges, and including issues of transgender discrimination is a good way to get players to learn about and to explore the topic.

    Most other tribes won't really care, but that's because no other tribe is based around physical sex and gender in the way the Black Furies are. So the Black Furies would probably be a better tribe to tackle this issue with than most any of the others.
