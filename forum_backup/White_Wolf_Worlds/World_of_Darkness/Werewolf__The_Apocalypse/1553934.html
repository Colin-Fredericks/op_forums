    Garou Poltical Headcanons

    09-20-2025, 08:23 AM
    So I was inspired to write some up after being inspired by a combination of LANCER (a inde space mecha RPG) lore reading through the WtE preview material I got from subscribing to the main author's Ko-fi. Bear with me here since every things a bit rough and the names are kinda basic.

    Crown Tribe: So this fringe political movement basically wants one tribe to directly rule all the tribes of the garou nation or outright disbanding all the tribes to merge them all into one super-tribe or kingdom. Which Considering the history of the garou is easier said than done.

    -The Silverfangs: This tribe was the closest to be the crown tribe mostly due to the legitimacy gained by the sliver crown. Though said crown was as far as the fangs knows lost in the deep Umbra alongside it's barer Albrecht and his son and defacto regnant Jason are more concerned with holding tougher his tribe than ruling the nation.

    -The Glasswakers: Some fringe sects of the Walkers privately hold the view that they are worthy of the role of the crown tribe due to they experience with human society and technology would make them suitable tribe to lead the garou in the modern world or at least take the place of the kingless silver fangs.

    -The Shadow Lords: The Shadow lords are always the target of colorful rumors from the rest of the Nation about they actions and intentions. And the Margrave's role Founding of Eastern Concordat has done little to dispel said rumors, indeed some say that the formation Eastern concordat is just step one in a larger plan to take over the nation...

    Tribal separatists: As it said on the tin those are some tribes/septs that want to leave the nation for varying reasons.

    -The Red Talons: They're Self imposed exile in response to the Glass Walker joining the Western concordat has not been easy on them. This fact is compounded by they lack of Cearns in the wake of the Apocalypse. And efforts to reclaim a Cearn by the Sept in British Columbia are facing mixed result partly due to lack of sufficient wolfpower.

    - The StarGazers: This tribe has always played with the idea of leaving the nation or at least join the Beast Courts of East Asia in a Similar manner to how the two brothers ultimately joined the Dawn Tribes. Still the apocalypse has made those plan unworkable, since the sacrifices both spitural and physical the made to avert it compounded by the loss of they cearns, it would lead to a slow death of the tribe even if they stayed with the nation, ultimately leading to they decision to join the Children of Gaia to preserve the tribe. whatever fallout if any this decision cause the Stragazers are content on keeping it within the tribe.

    The WrymFighters: A conservative centrist political movant that advocate that the reduction of politics (especially if said politics have human origins) influence to tribal polices as it would distract from the nation's duties. Though what counts as too political shifts with tribes and time-frame and of course "it distracts from our duties "is a all too common excuse for some elders to clamp down on progressive movements in the nation.

    - The Silver fangs: This tribe have always held them selves up to being Gaia's earthly reprehensive that's above petty Politics and power plays unlike the shadowlords. In practice this stance shifts depending on the high king leading the tribe, though the most recent king Albrecht is an somewhat of an anomaly for trying to guide the tribe to a more progressive path through a series of reforms. Though the Apocalypse put a pause to those plans with Albrecht sacrificed himself to stop it and leaving his son Jason as regent to his throne. Time will tell if Jason would continue his fathers reforms or cave to the demands of the old guard.

    - The Get of Fenris: This tribe is always zealous in they dedication to fighting the wyrm rather than engage in petty politics and due to the actions of a certain infamous Sept the have doubled down on fighting the Wyrm at the expense of (especially human) politics. Though some say that they are overcompensating to a degree as some septs has adopted extreme isolasonist policies to protect it's members from unwanted influence. And there's dark rumors that some of those septs harbor the remints of the swords who wish to preserve they ideology by rebranding it to a more "gaian friendly" form... One should hope the Get stay vigilant of threats within they tribe least they lead down a path everyone will regret.

    Progressives: This is a blanket term for the many progressive movements throughout the Nation's history that faced varying level of favor and resistance depending on the time frame, tribe and the hot button issue that concerns the Nation. In the past the garou's exact response and relation with human cavillation and by extension tech usage, at present hot topic issue are kinfolk and warborn rights.

    -The Eastern Concordat: The faction as a whole are founded on progressive ideas, with extra emphasis place on the kinfolk contribution to the nations mission should theoretically be equal if not grater to garou since they are much more suited to interact with humanity than most garous which would allow them to influence grater humanity. Which make them important to achieving the faction's greater goal, now weather this goal is achievable in the first place is another question entirely...

    -The glass walkers: A common saying among the human kinfolks of the tribe regarding they treatment are "one shuffle forward, one skip back". Despite the tribes reputation for radical progressives when it comes to interacting with humans and they technology, they treatment of they human kinfolk (especially among the more corporate leaning camps) are shockingly traditional in that they always treated as secondary citizens inside the tribe, regulated to menial tasks while the garou deal with more important tasks and given little if any opportunities to advance. This fact is one of the reason that the Walkers decided to not join the eastern concordat despite bankrolling them during they formation and the cause of some stress with the smaller septs and camps who are much more equal relation ship with they kinfolk. Now weather this issue would lead to a greater split within the tribe remains to be seen.

    -The Get of Fenris: This tribes has always presented them selves as being more meritocratic compered to other tribes. Over looking they breed, race and gender as long as they
    fight for the tribe. Even if some detractor's would decay the heavy burden the tribe place on potential recruits (especially kinfolks) as a from of social Darwinism. Still those that do pass the tribes lofty expatiation's are treated as one of they own and are proud of that fact.

    The VeilBreakers: As it said on the tin this political movement believes that the Veil one of the most important tenant of the lantry is actively impeding the garous mission and should done away with. In the past it's calls for a final battle against the forces of the wyrm but more progressive tribes have advocated a restively softer approach of educating humanity about the danger of the wyrm and by exertion the many crimes of Pentex to further it's goal.

    -There are VeilBreakers in both concordats. Most of them were quickly dissuaded by they Elders before they can do something truly drastic. Though some of the same elders do prepare for the time when the Veil drops. Under the assumption of it being a matter when not if.

    The ReUnifiers: A more recent that emerged after the nation spit into two. They obviously believes that the nation need to be reunified so they could properly face the forces wyrm and maybe even revive gaia from her slumber. Ironcally they don't have a unified plan to actually reunify the nation.

    -Most tribes have some misgivings on the spilt of the nation from the Sliver Fangs (who believe shadowlords are the greatest traitors to the nation short of actually the wyrm) to the shadow lords (some of them held the belief that the margrave jumped the gun forming Eastcon after discovering the shard seed.) and most garou have prepose a wide verity of ways the nation could be reunified are as vared as the argument against them...

    Again I have to reiterate the write up is purely head-canon for a Fan game that's not released at the time of writing and aren't not canon. I just want to share this silly little write up on this forum.

      Adding some of my own loose ideas related to this topic, don’t know if that’s what you were looking for OP but I hope it’s helpful

      •I’ve always liked the idea of the Children of Gaia as a ‘Crown Tribe’, if we’re using that term to refer to tribe’s with significant sections dedicated to taking on leadership of the Garou Nation. Their name has an implied universal claim imo, in that all Garou really are ‘Children of Gaia’ and that would lead me to imagine a significant number of the tribe believes that its principles ought to be adopted by the Nation as a whole, perhaps from the belief that tribes in general represent an overall hinderance to the Garou and that a universal Nation ought to replace tribal distinctions

      •re: Silver Fangs and Shadow Lords as two of the other main ‘Crown Tribes’. I’ve always been interested in the idea of the Silver Fangs being perhaps the first tribe and perhaps having the First Caern be the Firebird Caern somewhere in rural Siberia. The idea of the Silver Fangs’ ancestors undergoing some shamanic rite involving Death Wolf to gain the power seems interesting to me, but I’m also sympathetic to the idea that there was some major loss which accompanied the gain of the rites of lordship - that whatever (albeit significant) power they gained carried some great cost, which if the Silver Fangs inspired the creation of the other tribes (everyone seeking spiritual patronage to gain greater power), could have served to further divide the Nation. This also connects to the idea that once Wolf served as the patron of the Garou but now is lost.
      -I also like the idea that, regardless of its historicity, using the Frazerian hypothesis from the Golden Bough to explain a lot of aspects of Silver Fang spiritual kingship. This imo creates a symbiotic relationship with the Shadow Lords (the ‘shadows’ of the ‘lords’?) wherein the Shadow Lords serve as something of the star chamber that observed the Fangs and carries out executive actions in the form of ‘accidents’ and covert assassinations against kings who are losing spiritual potency or mandate. The emphasis of the SF & SL as something of a package deal, always finding one with the other, is something I like to play around with. Especially in the modern days where you have the conflict of Silver Fang lords who have abandoned the restrictions and bans of old kingship in exchange for greater power and Shadow Lords who have abandoned the obligation of forswearing power so as to ensure the purity of the lordship and becoming more and more concerned with gaining power for its own sake

      •Glass Walkers as a Crown Tribe, I’m of two minds about. On the one side I think that it’s unlikely that many of the traditionalist and high tribes are unlikely to ever accept the children of cockroach as the executives of the Nation, however I do buy the idea of a section of GW’s with a modernist Will to Power and may even have the ambition of creating a parallel Nation structure within the Garou body politic, focused upon more pro-weaver/pro-modernity/pro-city factions from across the tribal spectrum

      •The Red Talon’s as separatists I do see having a logic to it. I wouldn’t personally have a Gangrel or Stargazer style formal exodus of the tribe from the Nation but I do think them having a significant section of separatists would make sense, particularly those who feel that the elders are overly concerned with homid affairs and the rites of kingship too far removed from the primal state of the Garou. I think it would be interesting if the Red Talons actually have a similar idea regarding the origins of the Garou and the Wolf spirit as the Children of Gaia but have gone about it in a completely opposite way. Whereas the Coggies implicitly want the borders of the primordial unity of Gaia to extend to all natural life, including humanity, the Red Talon hard-liners view that contemporary humans have forsaken their ancestral obligations too much to be forgiven (at least without severe correctives). Thus like the Coggies they may have a political project of promoting the idea of Garou abandoning their tribes in favor to a return to One Great Tribe Under Wolf (OGTUW), unlike the Coggies, they would include severe demands of renunciation of human cultural ‘taint’ over Garou customs and most likely a return to some form of Impergium, however revised or temporary individuals might argue over.

      •Speaking of restorationists of some variation of the OGTUW or other pre-modern ideal, I think most tribes probably have some version of this, although alongside the Children of Gaia and Red Talons, I think that the Black Furies are another candidate to be major participants of this school of thought

      I’ve got more ideas to add but this post is rather long so I’ll stop here

              I suspect you can throw in at least one other tendency - not necessarily a coherent Faction, but a powerful impulse in Garou politics - which one might describe as the ‘Kinsfrife’ bloc.

              This would be those tribes like the Fianna or Younger Brother (or, heck, even the Children of Gaia and Black Furies) who are so close to their Kin/Protectorate that they have a tendency to be caught up in human political movements almost to the point of no return.

              This movement is distinct from the ‘Veil-rippers’ in that they’re more reluctant to breach the Litany, but they are dangerously prone to confuse the good of their own nearest and dearest with the Greater Good in a way that can make life very, very difficult.

              Also supremely quarrelsome.

                  I am not very familiar with Werewolf: the Essentials and never heard of LANCER, so I don't know how the two intersect in the first post. However, if I was going to group some kind of larger political framework to the Garou Nation based on the original books/setting, this is how I would describe it for my own chronicles.

                  The Status Quo: The Status Quo isn't happy with the state of the world or the Garou Nation, but they're traditionalists in that they uphold the old ways of the Garou and continue to support the Silver Fangs as leaders of the nation. It's not that they don't support any change, but want any changes to not undermine traditional Garou values and culture. The tribes most strongly associate with this are the Silver Fangs, Fianna, and Get of Fenris.

                  The Challengers: The Challengers feel the Silver Fangs have utterly failed, and they need to replaced ASAP as leaders of the Garou Nation. The tribes here are the Children of Gaia and Shadow Lords. These tribes are not allied, and in fact view each other as rivals for the new leadership. They employ very different methods and have very different goals. However, they can usually be counted upon to undermine the Silver Fangs and push their own agenda forward.

                  The Reformers: The Reformers are Garou who fear upcoming disaster if things do not change, but still believe the Garou Nation can sufficiently change to meet these challenges. They support Silver Fang Renewalists and oppose Silver Fang Royalists. Their main reason to oppose the Challengers is that outright leadership change would prove too disruptive, plus that they feel neither the Shadow Lords or Children of Gaia would actually provide improved leadership. They'd just fix some things while botching others while starting a civil war. Still, if things get bad enough, they could defect. Tribes include the Black Furies, Silent Striders, Stargazers, and Uktena. The Reformers are not a solid block, and sometimes their reforms are at odds with one another. Some want "modernization" while others are radicals who may even want changes to the Litany.

                  The Discontented: These tribes not only feel things have gone from bad to worse, they feel alienated from the Garou leadership as a whole. They don't like the Silver Fangs, but also don't see anyone else as improving things. Most of their discontent is fueled by the idea that major problem is not that the Garou Nation hasn't changed enough, but they are very unhappy with how things changed from what they were in the distant past. They carry deep wounds in their soul and nurse historical grievances. Tribes here are the Red Talons and Wendigo. The other tribes often seek to integrate these tribes back into the fold as contented members of the Garou Nation, but whatever they offer never seems to placate them.

                  The Outcasts: Outcasts are those tribes whose points of views are usually discounted by all the other tribes. They usually play a limited role in the affairs of the Garou Nation as a whole. Tribes are the Bone Gnawers and Glass Walkers. However, these two tribes are reacting to this in different ways. The Bone Gnawers are disengaging from the Nation entirely. A lot of the tribe is disintegrating into Rabble, and others are seeking to abandon Gaia for someplace better (the Wagnerians). As the Omega Tribe, their views and interests are often ignored at best or violated at worst. The Glass Walkers however, see themselves as visionaries. The only tribe that is thriving in this new world, and therefore the only one who can save the Garou Nation. However, they understand they could never be accepted as leaders of the Garou Nation so instead of challenging the Silver Fangs (or whoever replaces them), they hope to serve as the grey eminence whispering what to do to whoever is in charge. Both tribes would likely view themselves as belonging to another faction, but in fact nobody really listens to them and, at best, only wants to utilize them for their own schemes.

                  The Traitors: Historically this has only been be the province of the Black Spiral Dancers. However, more Garou seemingly fall to the Wyrm for various reasons, but not all look at the BSDs as their home. Some want to form their own, new Wyrm tribe. Others espouse various forms of heresy and accept the presence of the Wyrm in Gaia's world. There are also those who are just criminals and exploit their own people, but do not have any ultimate goal.

                  These descriptions are at a very high level only. Individual tribal Garou, septs, and camps often defect from their "faction" and are part of another faction. Furthemore, some tribes are very divided in their "allegiance". The Get of Fenris could possibly join the ranks of the Discontented. Glass Walkers might one day be accepted among the Reformers. Multiple tribes could join the ranks of the Challengers and support one of the other claimants or make their own claim. Once the Apocalypse happens and is recognized as such, things could completely change. Associations are not straightjackets, not usual tendencies.

                  Also, these factions aren't in any way how the Garou themselves define things. This is more how a social anthropologist might define things, but it is really just a tool for the ST to decide things at a high level. Useful for Concolations and Grand Moots.
