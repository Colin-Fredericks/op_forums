                                        05-27-2023, 09:31 PM
                                        I've been out of the loop for a couple of days as I was on a trip, and I see we're back to photoediting.

                                        The Ghost Council itself left me quite confused. What makes them a council? Where is a structure they belong to and how do they communicate and what is their agenda? This seems to be just a name (again). I understand they're supposed to be pragmatic scholars - but what are they studying? What are these mysteries and taboos they are challenging?

                                        How does the give and take with Horned Serpent work? What is the transaction in question? Which fairy tale quality are we talking about? What's its agenda? How does this even work!? If the new tribes are truly Mystery Cults of Totems now, the key character in every Tribe should be their totem, and we get about a line or two for each and nothing is really defined about what they're actually about.

                                        In general, they've spent a large number of words over two pages repeating the same content and not actually giving anything to bite into. Give me something to latch onto, something that makes them special and explains how they slot into the setting, not just a repetition of nebulous traits!


                                        At least it's not the voice that's writing the characters, because Gaia, whoever is writing that is trying way too hard to be "conversational" and "hip", while failing at both. Even with going past the writing, I wonder what these folks have to offer to the Garou at large? The Ascetic lives in the middle of nowhere disconnected from life making amends when it snaps, the Saboteur coopts weaver tech to blow stuff up. The Shepherd is one that could work as Garou, but there is nothing about how this interacts with packs, septs or Caerns, it's wholly defined by the ethnic conflict over their role in the broader conflict that defines the game (even in the current previews).

                                        The Witch may be the only one that's actually a investigator of the unknown and forbidden out of the four, but reading the description just makes me cringe. She sounds like a bad New Age joke rather than a believer, letalone a member of a society that belongs to the worlds of spirit and has a divine right to interacting and knowing about the secrets of the universe.

                                        I'm really not sure why I'm still doing this to myself.

                                        What doesn't kill you, makes you... stranger.

But that explains why the name doesn't seem to fit: The person who wrote most of this second version didn't come up with the name. And what kind of taboos are they challenging...? I mean, all of them, it's just this general idea of ignoring taboos and crossing boundaries. Where I thought it went somewhat too far was with the "ends justify the means" bit leading to the line "If something comes of maintaining a relationship with a confidant ​from a fallen tribe or forging pacts with forbidden spirits, the Ghost is satisfied — and validated, in their minds.​" So working together with BSD or Wyrm spirits is completely fine as long as it gets the Ghost closer to their goal... That's what it sounds like to me at least. You'd think this Tribe would've gotten kicked out of the Nation ages ago if they're this blasé about taboos.
