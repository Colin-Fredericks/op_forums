    Spontaneous Werewolf Genetics

    01-31-2016, 01:36 PM
    So the Werewolf larp gamma is out and it has a new thing it where because nature is trying to equal itself because the Garou are losing random people are gaining a mutation where they have the potential to be wherewolves. Basically if the character gets bitten by a werewolf it triggers this mutation and turns the character into a Garou. I'm mostly interested in what the people who play the larp think of this new thing. Personally I think it's stupid and goes against the whole story of what the Garou are (perhaps I would be more accepting of it if it was some kind of forbidden rite or something) and personally hope that it never is added to the Werewolf game.

      Well, first of all we must take into account that a lot of werewolf fans dislike WtA for lacking 'true' werewolves. The frenzied monsters that are turned against their will and have to fight back the beast. This rule seems to harken to that.

      However, regardless of the above paragraph, I don't like this change. Mostly, because it conflicts so strongly with the themes of WtA.

      WtA is very much a game of community, the garou have their own culture and their own laws. Their own traditions and their own heroes and villains. A huge part of the game is the bonds of family between kinfolk, tribe and pack. To all of this, the biting angle conflicts strongly. The character is no longer born as garou, and so truly never had a chance with humanity or wolves in the first place. To be bitten is to be forced into that life from a perfectly mundane human life, and that very much doesn't fit the themes of the garou. They take pride in what they are, some even think they are above humans. Plus, their laws put a huge focus on not revealing themselves to the public. Lost Cubs are all ready a hard thing to deal with in regards to the Veil, think of a randomly bitten human.

      In addition, biting humans to increase the forces is something I could see BSDs doing easily, not as much Gaian garou.

      My gallery.

                  It's a bit more complex than just, "now Garou can bite humans, who might become Garou."

                  Part of the metaplot of the new BNS game is that after [big battle resolving the Revised metaplot stuff that's still pretty vague] werewolf bloodlines that have been spiritually dormant are reawakening. Basically, people that are multiple generations removed from their closest Garou ancestor who lost their spiritual connection to Gaia... suddenly have it back. Some of them are now fully kin and capable of having new Garou children, and some are potential Garou, waiting for a trigger.

                  I think it's a bit early to judge it, but I don't think it's an inherently bad move. I can see why people don't like it, but there's a lot in there that's pretty important to moving WtA forward: scrapping the "dying race" aspect, expanding the Tribes to get away from cultural stereotypes without retcons, providing new directions for in-game conflict, etc.

                  Also, remember that BNS is focused on LARP based play, which for WtA can have a very different focus than TT based play.

                          It goes against the original conception of the game. I like the trope it is trying to emulate, but it is best done by emphasizing aspects of the existing mythos like Ronin, Lost Cubs, and Lunatics. Many months ago, I wrote a series of posts about how the game can emulate the traditional werewolf tropes in cinema, literature, and folklore. It's not that hard. It's just that those aspects were ignored in the setting and various guides. One of those ideas was that Garou could attempt to awaken or trigger the dormant change by biting the Lost Cub or Lunatic perhaps as part of a Rite. It's certainly more elegant than creating an entire new system of birthing Garou and doesn't invalidate how things were portrayed in the past.
