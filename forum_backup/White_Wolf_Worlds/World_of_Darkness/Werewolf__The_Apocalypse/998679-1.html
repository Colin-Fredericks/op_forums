    At least the way I've run/played these things:
    Originally posted by Uniform Two Six View Post
    But does it work the same way for stepping into the Penumbra? Although that seems to be the consensus here, if you step sideways in Homid wearing non-dedicated clothing, do you come out the other side naked?
    Dedicated items become one with your spirit, and that's why they come with you into the Umbra. So if you were wearing non dedicated clothes, I would say they drop as you pass through the gauntlet. This is really only ever an issue for cubs though. Someone in the pack has the Rite, so no one goes without.

    Originally posted by Uniform Two Six View Post
    ​On an even less related note: What about moon-bridges? They traverse the Umbra. So, if you travel a moon-bridge and have non-dedicated items on your person, do those just drop off as you step onto the bridge?
    Moon Bridges are generally portals into/through the Umbra so carrying non dedicated items onto them is in theory fine. But keep in mind that they are protected by Lunes, and so any item that is going to be considered offensive to the Spirits is probably not a good idea to bring through their sacred pathways.

    ​
    Originally posted by Uniform Two Six View Post
    ​And something totally unrelated: How do non-dedicated and un-awakened items behave in Umbral realms (assuming you have a way of getting those items to the Umbra in the first place) in which the realm mimics the physical world? So for instance, let's say that you have a mundane handgun (which would ordinarily not work in the Umbra), but you bring it with you to, say, Battleground. It's a place where guns do very much work, although those guns are actually emanations. What about other stuff, too? Could you bring a camera and take pictures of scenes in Battleground, or Wolfholme etc. ?
    Handguns in Battleground will work....sometimes. It depends on what you are attacking. If you are attacking a clearly spiritual enemy (like a Bane that has wandered into the Realm) there is a chance the weapon won't work. But it should generally work on the natives/emanations to the Realm, since it is in their nature to be affected by such weapons.
    Building from that, any mundane items are going to vary Realm to Realm for if they would work. Generally I would say that cameras probably won't work. Vintage cameras because film is fickle to begin with and digital cameras probably wouldn't work in most non Weaver Realms. Even dedicated cameras should have a host of complications to overcome. But that opinion is mostly just because I think it's in the spirit of the Umbra to mess with such things.

                                            Well.. learn something new every day.

                                            "Though a caern’s moon bridge can be opened in the physical world, it’s usually far more discreet to open it from the spirit world on the other side of the caern’s Gauntlet." W20, pg 311

                                            Up until now, I'd assumed it was Theurges using Grasp the Beyond, Garou dedicating containers to themselves, or possibly a question of the relatively unknown mechanics of what happens when the Gauntlet is zero (as it might be at the heart of a powerful enough caern to connect to the Amazon). But nope... you can just open the sucker from either side, I guess.

                                            I wonder if it was always that way, or if this is another time where common player practice got written into the books.

                                            Edit: And just to chime in, played as written I do believe that yes... a character's pockets, magazines, wallet, backpack, etc SHOULD empty of everything not present at the time of Dedication. Personally, I do enforce this in my games... because it honestly makes cheesy penumbral strategies less "broken". You can't just sidestep away from danger every time when there's a notebook, flash-drive, or piece of loot you want to come home with. And it's a better reason to keep players in the Tellurian than just dumping an unrealistic horde of banes on top of any location where I want the story to take place in the physical realm... because that's a pretty lame cop-out to have to rely on.

                                            The comment about dirt and blood in clothing, though... that's an interesting one. I'd never considered it the question of whether a Garou should leave blood-splatter behind. Technically... maybe? However I think this probably does enter that sticky gray area of what happens to a stomach full of O'Tolley's cheeseburgers. Maybe the 'soaked in' or 'caked on' filth is close enough to being one with the clothing to count. Otherwise Garou could literally sidestep half their daily hygiene regimen.
