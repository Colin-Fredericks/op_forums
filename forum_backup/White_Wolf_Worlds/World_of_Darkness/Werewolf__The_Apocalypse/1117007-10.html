                    Why didn't they at least give the Black Spiral Dancer time?
                    They weren't sure if it's gonna work. And Alpha of the pack (with tears in player's eyes) decided that it would be too risky for her pack. They would have to hide him and if someone from the sept would find out about it they would be surely torn apart for siding with Wyrm. Besides this particular BSD was a living proof of their own stupidity. They found him shortly after becoming Fosterns (well, at least some of them) in a gym on a bad side of town. Charlie (cause that was his human name) was Rocky'esque character, poor kid from a poor family living in a bad side of town trying to become a professional boxer to help his family. He also knew all the gangs from the neighbourhood (he wasn't involved in the underground, just a few friends from school and a local capo) and those infos the PCs wanted. During the conversation which was slowly becoming an interrogation Charlie undergone his First Change nearly killing Silver Fang Ahroun and Gaillard Fenrir (mostly by blind luck than his skills) in a rage before they were able to stop him. And they had to choose: either turn him to a sept where he could learn from entire sept or hide him and train him all by themselves (he was Gaillard) and present him in a sept later as a fully trained garou ready for his Rite of Passage. They chose later option (they were Fosterns they were fully capable for teaching right?). Unfortunately in the sept a BSD spy was hiding (I was running one of the adventures from Storyteller's Screen). He planned to take Moonstone and some fetishes from the caern and decided to take the cub as a bonus (he found out about it). He told Charlie that he would prove that caern isn't properly defended and other garou would try to kill him for that rather than accept their flaws. He convinced him to meet him at the construction site and when PCs found them there they tried to kill BSD for endangering the caern. Charlie then defended him and the PCs made a terrible mistake: rather than shove the cub aside and bring justice to BSD as they should they left them there and went to the caern to convince the sept to strenghten their defence lines (BSD told them that he would return stolen items when they prove that caern is safe). At the same time BSD took Charlie and made him a BSD. But Charlie didn't like it (actually he was cheated; BSD posing as a Ronin, former Uktena, told Charlie about a sept " that is properly managed and accept everyone who thinks before acting") and the ritual didn't destroyed his mind. He knew that every other garou will kill him rather than help him so he "arranged the meeting" with his former teachers (he allowed them find himself). Firstly they tried to give him a honourable death and challenged him. As the challenged one he chose the rules and chose a chess game, trying to buy himself some time. The PC lost (one of the players had to play chess with me) and during the game he tried to convince them that he is willing to return to the sept if only they would help him. Alpha didn't want to choose if Charlie should live or be killed so she ordered rest of her pack to act as a jury. And, what a surprise, a draw. So she still had to decide. And she chose death. The best thing is that her garou lover (yes, she weren't following the Litany too), one of the NPCs, knew one gurahl who could help. He didn't told anybody about it for fear of intolerance of other garou. And when he told Alpha about this gurahl it was too late. That was one of my best games. Player who played Alpha cried during the game for the first time in her life (I mean during the game). Twice .
