            Dirge aka Janet Crowley / Loyal Soldier (Homid Shadow Lord Galliard)

            So this one is a little shorter than the previous, looking like it clocks in at about the same word count as most of the tribe book template entries.

            Janet is from a fairly miserable home environment, but one that was sadly kind of common for some GenX kids of a certain socio-economic background. Her mom ran off at some point, leaving her with an alcoholic and neglectful father. Janet developed "antisocial tendencies" and "behavioral disorder", getting into fights, cutting class, shoplifting, and becoming a general juvenile delinquent. Finally she got picked up by the police.
            I do enjoy this description of her internal anger, the desire to fight back against anyone who got in her face, and smash their faces in. Even in 1st ed, we got descriptions about Homids being maladjusted or not fitting in while growing up, and I believe later in the line, Ethan Skemp would mention at times the idea that the Rage boiling up inside them would lead to various personal issues. However, a number of Homid character write ups would seem to gloss over or ignore this at times. So I'm happy to see a character where it's right there up front.

            She was taken from the police by another detective who gave off an aura of menace that cowed her natural rebelliousness. He told her she was one of the Shadow Lords, and that they were here family. It turns out that a family is what she's wanted all this time, so she's extremely loyal to her new tribe, more than to anything else in her life.
            Again, I like this take, especially given her Tribe. There isn't even any mention of her being used by her new "family" or the like. I think this is an interesting direction for a Shadow Lord. While it plays in with the way they are written in the 1st ed, 2nd ed started focusing more on the idea of dominance before shifting more toward a sort of ruthless pragmatism.

            There's a rather offhand (gratuitous, really) remark about her playing bass guitar in a band. There's nothing wrong with the idea. I do with it had perhaps been mentioned earlier when talking about her various forms of teenage rebellion. But as it is, it feels like it was just sort of thrown in as "oh, yeah, she's a Galliard, so she does something with music" kind of thing.

            The image is pretty cool. It's similar to a number of Timbrook's early Vampire illustrations, and wouldn't feel out of place in the Brujah, Gangrel or Malkavian clan books. Your sort of typical early 90s GenX grunge riot grrl with some Goth overtones. Jeans, leather jacket, and cigarette.

            For Attributes, Physicals are primary, with a 3/4/3 split. Socials are secondary, with an additional dot from freebies, with a 4/3/2 split. Mental is tertiary, all of them at 2. This seems to work fine.

            For Abilities, both Talents and Skills have 13 dots each, so I've no idea which is primary and which is secondary with an extra four dots from freebies. I guess it doesn't really matter.
            Talents are a 3 in Brawl, 2 each in Dodge, Empathy, Expression, and Intimidation, and 1 each in Streetwise and Subterfuge.
            Empathy feels like a strange fit for this character. It's possible that there are some aspects of her personality that aren't being discussed in the write up that one could try to infer from the presence of that abilities. But as written, I feel like maybe those two dots might've been better used to raise Streetwise and Subterfuge to 2 each.
            For Skills, we have 3 each in Leadership and Performance, 2 each in Firearms and Stealth, and 1 each in Animal Ken, Drive, and Survival.
            Animal Ken strikes me the same way as Empathy. I think Melee might make more sense, given the juvenile delinquent background. I might even go as far as to move a point from Firearms into Melee as well. I have no problem with the Leadership skill, even through the write up doesn't exactly offer anything about it. However, I could see her being the leader of a small gang of some sort prior to her change, which I think would make for potential interesting character hooks later one. Survival also feels like a little bit of an outlier. However, she's the only Homid character of the bunch to have that skill. I sort of see it as something she was forced to learn after her first change, just so she didn't embarrass her Tribe when around others. But that's just me head-cannoning again.
            Knowledge is tertiary, with 2 in Rituals, and 1 each in Law, Occult, and Politics. These all seem fine.

            For Backgrounds, she has 1 each in Past Lives, Pure Breed, Resources, and Pack Totem. I'd be tempted to drop Past Lives in favor for a dot in Contacts to represent people she used to hang out with. I'm presuming resources have to do with this band she plays with. However, if you went with the gang thing I mentioned earlier, it could just as easily be them paying her some sort of regular tribute. Under 2nd ed rules, she'd have one more Background dot, and I could see it going to raise any of the ones already chosen.

            Rage is 4, Gnosis is 3, and Willpower is 3. This means she's spent more than 15 freebie points (5 for Attributes, 8 for Abilities, and now 4 for Gnosis). I'd fix this by leaving Gnosis at 1 and spending the remaining two freebies to raise her WP to 5. I might even be tempted to strip a dot from Abilities to increase it to 6 or 7.

            I like this character. I think she has a good backstory that offers a number of avenues for personalized development and expansion. She has the kind of background I enjoy seeing in Homid characters, and what I feel is a non-stereotypical expression of her Tribe. I also think the idea of her wanting a family and now being super-loyal to this new one that has adopted her can make an interesting expression of the Lover of the Ways archetype and be a reason for her to be all in on tribal rites and traditions.
