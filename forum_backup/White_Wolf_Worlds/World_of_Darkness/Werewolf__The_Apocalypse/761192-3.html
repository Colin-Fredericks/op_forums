                                    "She is Kinfolk to the Garou, and she helps when she can (or must), but even her blood kinship can’t overcome the distrust many elders feel for her kind.                                     She’s learned the secrets of Voudoun, but she at least gives lip service to werewolf totems (at least when relatives are around).
