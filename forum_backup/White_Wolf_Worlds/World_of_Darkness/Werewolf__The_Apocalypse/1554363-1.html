                                I think plenty of other people on this thread have already explained that making the current Ukraine war the setting for a WtA scenario seems like a bad idea. Personally, I think creating a more fictional scenario inspired by the Ukraine war (maybe with fictional nations) would be better. Especially since it's hard to find a complete, nuanced analysis of an ongoing conflict. Hindsight is 20/20, as they say. So either go with a fictional conflict or go back to historical ones. That's my recommendation.

                                And to add something of worth to this thread. Here's a bit from the Ukraine war that I thought would be interesting for a WtA-game (from Forbes): "Fiber Optic Bird’s Nest Heralds A Fiber Drone Summer In Ukraine"

                                A bird’s nest incorporating fiber optic cable is a striking sign of how much debris from fiber-guided FPVs now litters the landscape of Ukraine. Other images show fields glittering with fibers like spiderwebs where dozens of drones have passed overhead. Meanwhile social media fills with hide-and-seek videos as Ukrainian drone pilots use their new ability to go inside buildings to hunt Russian vehicles.The non-toxic fibers may not harm birds, but they signal extreme danger to humans. Fiber drone summer is starting.
                                It didn't just immediately make me think of the Weaver, of course, but this story got it all: War, technology, nature and the environment. I think something like this is the perfect jumping-off-point for a WtA-scenario. And I think this would allow the focus to go more in the direction of a "War is hell"-theme rather than getting mired in the mud of politics. The environmental angle combined with the technological aspects also makes it suitable for a WtA-game. And it doesn't have to be the current Ukraine war. Because it's not about that, really. It's about how a new development in drone-warfare is impacting the environment.
