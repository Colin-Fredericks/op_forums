                                                            Some thoughts on the previews as a whole.

                                                            If I have an issue with something, it has to be the 'Hey, maybe we could use a different kind of thinking? Oh wait,NOPE!' sections. With the Mockery Breeds, it made sense. But once we got to using Weaver as an ally or curing a BSD it got old.

                                                            A huge part of WtA is that the old ways CAN be useful, but new ways and perspectives are extremely necessary to fix things. The two philodox section parts felt needlessly regressive, especially as Hestia was painted as an intolerant arse and the ST hooks section made it clear there -is- no way to cure a BSD and Wretch was just acting. While I understand the reason why no cured BSDs exist in W20, there could have been a third 'maybe Wretch did not return to his Hive that eagerly after being treated so well' or something like that. A chance or a possibility. After all, the theurge chapter had a fomor being cored and Bat being redeemed.

                                                            Please note, these are minor gripes about an otherwise great book.
