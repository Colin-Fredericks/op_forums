    Originally posted by Karlgust View Post

    Fomori and other Wyrm-crap are utter villains, Garou and Fera are the heroes. Very black and white. Have fun.
    The part about Garou and Fera has already been covered, but I'm going to go ahead and chime in a rebuttal of the former point. I'm not saying the Wyrm is secretly the good guy or anything like that, but personally I find Fomori to be one of the more interestingly conceived and compelling antagonists in the WoD. Not everyone handles them this way, but a point that shows up more than once is that Fomori are still people. They've been physically, mentally, and spiritually corrupted, but still people. Sometimes this corruption is by choice or because of irredeemable character flaws, other times because of more subtle and human flaws that grew out of control, sometimes even by accident or coercion. What's worse is that they often have little recourse available for their condition. Fomori don't have much in the way of an organized society or support group outside of Wyrm cults and Pentex, which have a vested interest in exploiting and debasing them. Werewolves might conceivably help purge them of their corruption, but they're just as likely to kill them first. Other supernaturals, if they can even find them, tend to find them loathsome either out of physical revulsion or because the lore they have about them reinforces the idea that they are tainted and unclean. So the only thing left for them to do is either feed their flaws until they burn out in blaze of pus and balefire or eck out a solitary and wretched existence of rude survival while their disease slowly destroys them.

    I love Fomori.

                                    Hey Schwann145,

                                    I can't give you any first hand experience with the game since I have none myself, but a good friend of mine gave a really good explanation about WtA when I asked him similar questions to what you're asking now, primarily the personal horror part, and will do my best to repeat what he told me as accurately as I can:

                                    "The personal horror in Werewolf the Apocolypse comes from being born as a Garu, and all that it entails. It means having a normal life up until a certain point where everything changes and suddenly you must pick a side in a conflict that's been going on since the Dawn of Time. It's about having a war against a cosmic force so ingrained into a culture that you're suddenly expected to accept and join, with the consiquences of not meaning death or worse. It's about adjusting to your transformation, going from either human or wolf to something in between and forever after being connected but separate from both worlds. It's about one day having a veil lifted from your eyes and suddenly being able to see the real consiquences of people's actions and how they breed gods and monsters beyond the wall that separates the spirit realm from the world you were born into. It's about looking up into the night sky and screaming because of the absolutely f$&ked situation you've been born into and which you are expected to not only contribute too but also intentionally sire new recruits for who will have to undergo the same shit as you when you inevitably fall in a war that will last until the End of Time."
                                    - A Wise Storyteller; told to a WtA hater who then at last understood why WtA was one of the seminal tabletop RPGs of it's time.


                                    Hope that helps, it certainly did for me.
