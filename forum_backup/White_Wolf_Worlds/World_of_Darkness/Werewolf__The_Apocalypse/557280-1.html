                                            Werewolf movies I enjoy and/or influence my take on Werewolf:

                                            Wer (2014) - A somewhat odd film, but a lot of the subplots running through it tie in really well with Werewolf.
                                            Dog Soldiers (2002) - I like all of Neil Marshall's films; in this case, a good example of pack tactics and a reminder of how isolated parts of Scotland are.
                                            An American Werewolf in London (1981) - In addition to being a good werewolf movie in general, it's also a good look at dark animism and hauntings.
                                            The Howling (1981) - A potential idea for a semi-isolated caern, and does tie in with the more sexual aspects of the werewolf legend.
                                            Wolfen (1981) - One of the first films to really tie werewolves in with Native American spiritualism and land issues.
                                            Ginger Snaps (2000) - Interesting characters and examines werewolfism as a metaphor for puberty.
                                            Wolf (1994) - A good inspiration for how different things can be after the first change.
                                            The Wolfman (2010) - A remake of the 1941 classic, with a lot of nice Victorian Gothic atmosphere.
