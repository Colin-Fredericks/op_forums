    Some of the missed opportunities for this game that I wish they had dealt with:

    * Pentex isn't really cartoony anymore: There's actually people who really do for religious reasons believe we should pollute more to bring about the End of the World. You could have a Supply Side Jesus (The Patriarch?) avatar of the Wyrm telling them to do this and it would entirely be believable in this world. You also have high functioning psychopaths who do not CARE how much destruction they cause in the name of short term profit. Really, Pentex just happens to be the worst but other corporations aren't far behind.

    * You could have added more tribes: Rather than divorce all of the Garou from ethnicity you could have made them alliances of hundreds of smaller tribes. Have more discovered all the time along with the Fera. Perhaps the Garou have opened up the ancestral realm of many extinct Garou groups of absorbed ones in the last days of Gaia and flooded Earth with renewed ancestor spirits or maybe they were always there but hiding from the crazy Garou nation.

    Hell, if you want to get rid of Kinfolk, have massive amounts of them turned and the Garou are no longer dying out-ish.

    * Play up the Horror: Being a Garou is something that was never really done with the personal horror angle. I think they should have put more rules about it and how to roleplay the struggle between your Beast of War and interactions with others. Garou don't have to be ticking time bombs but being unable to control their rage so they have to live among themselves was an angle always there but not one properly explored.

    * If you're going to sweeping changes, justify them: You can get away with a certain amount of, "no, it was always this way." Renaming the Wendigo, Fianna, Metis, and so on is fine. However, a lot of these other changes could have been done in-universe and paid proper respect to the past lore. Albretch, Evan (who is suddenly no longer ridiculous--but could have been retconned as a "stolen Native child" to to pay into RL tragedies), and other characters have had 20 years to make upheavels in the Garou Nation.

    * Make Redemption a Thing for Fenris: I feel that the Child of Gaia trying to win them back works here as for the first time there really is a substantive difference on how these things are being fought over and a Civil War. This should be a war of words as well as Claws.​

    Author of Cthulhu Armageddon, I was a Teenage Weredeer, Straight Outta Fangton, Lucifer's Star, and the Supervillainy Saga.

Just saying "Who knows?", shrug and think now the work is done is completely insufficient. W5's approach to a less fixed setting just doesn't work.

                            Some of the missed opportunities for this game that I wish they had dealt with:

                            * Pentex isn't really cartoony anymore: There's actually people who really do for religious reasons believe we should pollute more to bring about the End of the World. Really, Pentex just happens to be the worst but other corporations aren't far behind.
                            With Revised's introduction of the general designation of Wyrm-Influenced Corporations (WICs), Pentex basically just became an older, more entrenched vision of what a WIC might become. Killing it wouldn't solve the problems; those are pretty much baked into the kind of capital accumulation that allows Pentex and other WICs to exist.

                            Which VV5 excused as not being the problem.

                            * You could have added more tribes: Rather than divorce all of the Garou from ethnicity you could have made them alliances of hundreds of smaller tribes. Have more discovered all the time along with the Fera. Perhaps the Garou have opened up the ancestral realm of many extinct Garou groups of absorbed ones in the last days of Gaia and flooded Earth with renewed ancestor spirits or maybe they were always there but hiding from the crazy Garou nation.

                            Hell, if you want to get rid of Kinfolk, have massive amounts of them turned and the Garou are no longer dying out-ish.
                            Okay, but do you want these splats to be significant in any way? Because quantity tends to be the enemy of detail.

                            * Play up the Horror: Being a Garou is something that was never really done with the personal horror angle. I think they should have put more rules about it and how to roleplay the struggle between your Beast of War and interactions with others. Garou don't have to be ticking time bombs but being unable to control their rage so they have to live among themselves was an angle always there but not one properly explored.
                            Oh no, it was attempted, and more than once. Back in the earliest design of the game, the game had werewolves chaining themselves up at night to avoid killing people. Then the designers realized that this was incredibly boring and also just repeating a lot of Vampire, so they changed it into what we got in 1e. Thrall of the Wyrm frenzies, introduced in 2e, might have been another attempt, but they're a fucking sledgehammer to the head compared with 1e's frenzies where you might just eat someone if you botched a Wits roll (now only for homids, because we have to do some kind of forced symmetry bullshit with the three breeds and equate them with the Triatic Wyrms, even for breeds that don't have the equivalent of Crinos-born like Corax).

                            Meanwhile, we got a replacement in fully-summed up concept in 2e as well, in the form of Savage Horror, which is another way of saying "this is the splatter game". There was an essay about it in the Revised Storyteller's Handbook detailing why they went with that and stuck with it, as well.

                            tl;dr personal horror is Vampire's thing and no one else needs to have any part of it in their game, especially not if they're not likely to survive long enough to go through a protracted degeneration into an apathetic, blood-obligate micropredator.

                            * If you're going to sweeping changes, justify them: You can get away with a certain amount of, "no, it was always this way." Renaming the Wendigo, Fianna, Metis, and so on is fine. However, a lot of these other changes could have been done in-universe and paid proper respect to the past lore. Albretch, Evan (who is suddenly no longer ridiculous--but could have been retconned as a "stolen Native child" to to pay into RL tragedies), and other characters have had 20 years to make upheavels in the Garou Nation.
                            Revised actually just poof-retconned some things out of existence, like the Garou gene. Having the Garou work like the Nagah in terms of how they deal with where the next generation comes from could have been done similarly nowadays. (to wit, Nagah can produce Azhi Dahaka-born, but they're not guaranteed, and only one happens per mother per lifetime, and they're not disabled generally but very specifically more vulnerable to pollutants).

                            Other details are probably in need of less evasive revision, but that had been the direction of Revised. Even the STV Ultimate Style Guide says as much.

                                As for crossplay... The irony is that WOD5E through all its trials and tribulations in development has just repeated the same mistakes the original WOD-editions did. You have some surface-level connections: The old WOD did it via metaplot, WOD5E does it via enforcing "street-level-only" and "lore is bad". But system-wise the moment the first new corebook after V5 was released everything went wrong. The new in-house WOD-team mishandled the idea of cross-play immediately with H5. Instead of learning from CofD, WOD5E is just the old WOD all over again. The splats are just these tiny islands with their own idiosyncrasies in terms of mechanics that find no equivalent in other splats and weren't ever considered to be used in cross-play.

                                Take H5... In contrast to V5 what has H5 got to do with the orginal version of Hunter the Reckoning? Absolutely nothing. That's the setting-side. With mechanics the new development-team looked at the systems of V5 and just abandoned them left and right: Convictions. Gone. Chronicle-Tenets (as in-game-mechanic). Gone. Humanity or any sort of morality-track. Gone. And even Hunger-Dice... In H5 the "Hunger-"rating is a group-based rating... Try making that work in cross-play... Also, both in H5 and W5 Touchstones aren't doing what they're supposed to do mechanically. And it's another throughline where just in this one instance somehow there's an insistence that "Touchstones as people" can't be changed as a mechanic.

                                And W5 is just more of the same... Convictions still gone. No Humanity-equivalent. Sure, you could point to previous editions of WtA and say it wasn't handled like that but Forsaken managed to figure out a way to do it. And Hunter the Vigil managed to do it as well. Just because it's a game about mortal hunters doesn't mean you have to automatically abandon systems left and right.

                                Also, why do PCs in W5 not have Ambition and Desire like in V5 and H5? It's not like these two ways of healing WP would break the game and both V5 and W5 had them. Of course, you could make Auspice and Tribe, for example, stand-ins in some way (which W5 didn't). Instead, Ambition and Desire are just gone in official W5.
