              V20 rules say that strong-willed mortals (Willpower 9+) can try to resist and Kindred, regardless of Willpower, may make a Self Control/Instict roll (9).

                              Yeah -- pretty much what CeltSPZ says, with the caveat that they "count as mortals" for purposes of Dominate/Presence (they're affected by them, but may have Willpower/Gifts that allow for resistance or at least mitigation of "x" effects) and similarly applicable powers (except where noted). Basically, a werewolf is affected by The Kiss (and more-bemoan the poor garou who gets swarmed by several leeches and several of them "get their bite on"...), but they have a little extra "oomph" for working through it via Willpower and/or Rage/Frenzy, more so than most mortals (though *sooooo* many WW players forget that a garou can also fun "Fox" -- the equivalent of vampires' Rotschreck (sp) -- so a "frenzy" could well include the werewolf running with his tail between his legs... literally...; alternately, even if the Frenzy manifests as his turning into a killing machine, if the several bites example occur as above, he may well find himself in a situation similar to Akasha in the (horrible) movie version of "Queen of the Damned" -- the garou fights, but at the same time his energy is being drained, and possibly within just a round or two he finds himself completely drained or at least too diminished to fight -- enough aggro that even a death-rage roll can't overcome...).

                              Important things to keep in mind....
