                                                Well, I think that the Currs would be considered a Mockery Breed under current WtA Lore, so I doubt that they would have a sufficient connection to the spirit world to understand how wrong they are about Gaia. However, the Currs are not a canon Fera Breed, so that is neither here nor there.

                                                The real question that exists with the WtA Lore is that since it is the only canon objective Lore in cWoD, should it be considered to be the default Lore of cWoD, with every other Lore being subjective interpretations of events from WtA Lore? If so, does that invalidate the nature of the other games or, because of the subjective nature of their Lore, is it a big shrug?

                                                For example, the conflicts between VtM Lore and WtA Lore primarily occur before the Great Flood and the destruction of the First City. If the Antediluvian Kindred, however, were exiles from the High Umbra (with the 'Gardens' of VtA Lore being different High Umbra Realms), then squaring the circle of VtM Lore and WtA Lore give Kindred spiritual legitimacy in the eyes of WtA Lore. Kindred would have a reason for existing and, while the Garou would still ash them for helping the Wyrm spread corruption, they would have to reluctantly admit that the Kindred were a High Umbra variation of the Possessed. Of course, the Kindred would not care either way...
