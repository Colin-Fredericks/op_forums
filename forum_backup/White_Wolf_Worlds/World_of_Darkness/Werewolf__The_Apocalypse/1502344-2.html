    I suspect that W5 is going to have an 'ideal pack/caern' size where having more Garou around makes Rites easier, but only until like 10 or 15. Then during the Rite everybody has to make a roll to avoid murder-rage.

    At least it's different to Forsaken, where the big theming of rites is based around the Uratha religion and pack (whereas in legacy WtA it was about community in a broader sense). I'm not sure it's a good difference, but it's there.

    I feel like the 'too many Garou=murder rage' thing is meant to mimic the Uratha dynamic of competing packs without getting why that was a natural result. Which really sums up the W5 previews, it wants to hammer in the what without going too deep into the why. Lobbyists are causing bad things, so let's make an evil spirit-possessed lobbyist antagonist! What do you mean that having the lobbyist be a human with explainable reasons might be more interesting and impactful? Garou work in small packs due to magic Rage reasons, not because being a network of semi-independent cells gives them a tactical advantage, or because they're prideful creatures competing over resources.

    Blue is sarcasm.

    If I suggestion I make contradicts in-setting metaphysics please ignore me, I probably brought in scientific ideas.

                                            And trivializing the Garou down to street level is silly, considering the whole point of Garou staying in mid-to-low developed suburbs or rural areas is trying to stay away from the masses of humans.

                                            It's the complete opposite of why Vampires need cities: outside of Kinfolk, the Garou have a hard time with human society due to their Rage... if they wanted to remove anything, for better "street level play", Rage would make sense in removing (but I would NEVER want this for Apocalypse at all).

                                            Suburban or Rural areas as the main areas where Garou stay around make sense, as a means to more easily centralized all Kinfolk and Garou in one location; without any interruption from humans who aren't Kinfolk and mess with Garou society. The only tribes adept at tempering their Rage to some extent are the Bone Gnawers, Glass Walkers, and Children of Gaia.. but even they could have some flare-ups that might disturb humans.

                                            The Garou aren't meant to be heroes anyway. They're not really meant to make friends with humanity. They're fighters against the enemies of Gaia; at the forefront of that being the Wyrm.

                                            Lowering their power levels or mechanics to streel level, to cater toward myths/misrepresentations of the series, is a horrible way to destroy the gameline. Just because something is "high-powered", "globe-trotting", "epic-storytelling" doesn't mean it can't be taken seriously, nor does it suddenly make the game about "trenchcoat wielding katana wielding capeshit hero characters" (and even then, don't shoehorn one mechanical style of play to ensure those who want to play it that way, can't do that anymore...).

                                            Ironically, they make the game even more farcical and loose in theme by how their pulling all the brakes to unnaturally make it "street level". It's just horrible.
