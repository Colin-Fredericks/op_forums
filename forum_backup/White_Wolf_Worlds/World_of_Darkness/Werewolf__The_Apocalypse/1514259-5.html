            There has been a little mention 😉, but seriously agreed great book and great adaptation.
            I just like the quote (I see very little in American Gods that is actually applicable to the All High, but everyone has their own inspirations). I like it because it shows he has lots of "names" (or titles)., and that even "Odin" is just one of them ( that and his other names in Germanic cultures just means something like "lord of the possessed or frenzy", it is not a personal name) (and because the scene is just awesome cinematically).

            Anyway, here is another artifact, obviously brought from mythology.

            Gungnir is the spear of the All High. In myth, it was created by dwarfs and could always hit its target regardless of its wielder's skill. This spear of the All High is a powerful Fae Treasure that can slay chimerical creatures and deal aggravated damage to others. It works against the Fae just as well as cold iron. It was created by the Fae allies of the All High to cement their alliance. While elves of House Aesin originally recovered Gungnir after the burning of the temple at Uppsala, it was subsequently lost during the Shattering. House Aesin would love to recover the Treasure as it is said that without the spear, the All High will not be able to avert Ragnarok or slay the demon spirit Fenris. Unfortunately for them, they do not know its current location.

            If we want to use the idea that the Host is actively preparing for Ragnarok, and that the search for occult artifacts is a good way to do that (and can be a great hook for PCs), then finding this spear would be very important. It is an ancient spear from the Nordic Bronze Age, and now might only be a well preserved bronze spearhead with no shaft. Perhaps only those who can see chimerical things could recognize the spearhead for what it is. It might be forgotten in some warehouse of a museum, or perhaps part of someone's private collection. If you want to introduce it in game, it could be put up for sale in an antique gallery or shop. Perhaps with various groups becoming aware of its importance through the typical means of Gifts that provide prophetic knowledge, dream omens, and so on. Vampires, mages, Garou, and Changelings might become interested, but only the members of the Host would actually recognize it for what it really is (and know the secret means to unlock its "true" power, without such knowledge it might seem like a much less powerful Fae Treasure).

                An idea that occurs to me which could provide an interesting flavor, which others have alluded to in this thread, is the idea of nuclear warfare and the like.

                While of course the All High has been kicked around since long before the Cold War, Sidhe like those of House Aesin returned to earth in the flat middle of those years where the specter of The Bomb must have been hanging heavy throughout the Umbra & Dreaming.

                Even now when the fear of the Bomb is nowhere near as prevalent, I think it could provide an interesting touchstone. The idea occurs to me of having a reoccurring trend of the Host be to repurpose old bunkers and fallout shelters. This would be a neat way of combining the thematic threads of War, Apocalypse, & Historical weight which lends itself well to the Host. I also think on an esoteric level, the subterranean nature of these bunkers is a nice modern vessel for the older cthonic vibrations which these sort of cultic organizations had as their bread and butter.

                I know for instance in my home town there are a number of older buildings that have decades old signs posted declaring them to be the locations of fallout shelters. I can’t imagine that the local bank or high school checks on the Eisenhower era bunkers built below their buildings but even if they do, they may not in the WoD. The idea of a group of PCs tracking down a mysterious cult group and finding a shrine of the All-High hidden within the abandoned fallout shelter under some innocuous local institution is a striking image.

                    I also think we can use artifacts not inspired by Garou myth to be a target of the Host. After all, limiting ourselves to just those make things very predictable.

                    I don't have a list of existing artifacts already published in the World of Darkness sourcebooks that would make a good list to pillage from. I am aware of some sources that could provoke the Host and Garou PCs into conflict. The Sept of Bygone Visions held by the Black Furies could make a tempting raiding target because of the many occult treasures housed in the Womb of the sept. We know there are some powerful Wyrm items there (including one of the Talens of the Wyrm), legendary books of white and black magic, and other occult artifacts. Hearing news of some spectacular raid on this Sept would certainly be a strong hook.

                    There are also some published Legendary Fetishes that could make a good target. If the Host could obtain the Elder Stone (a fragment of the mythical Table Rock where the Garou first created the Litany mentioned in the 1e Storytellers Handbook), that could become an epic quest to recover it.

                    Perhaps one reason the Odindohtter are gathering information in Russia because they want to recover Koschei's Soul Egg. Per Rage Across Russia, this a gemstone fetish prisone currently possessed by a small group of Uktena Banetenders in Siberia. This might be at the sole Uktena sept mentioned in RAR, the Sept of the Spirit Stone located in Yakutia/Sakha, or perhaps at one of the several other Uktena, but nameless, septs that the ST will need to create.

                    If people know of other tempting targets (whether from canon or even personal invention), I think it would be beneficial for the thread. It would transform the Host from a background element into something that is more active.

                                                        I think now is a good time to go over what spirits might be allied to the Host. Spirits and the spirit world are an important part of Werewolf, and they would be an important part of Odin's Host. The All High made various pacts with spirits, and they constitute a small brood available to the sorcerers of the Host. These spirits can be seen as a subset of normal spirits of their type, as they are functionally the same as their kind but have extensive chiminage relationships with the All High and the Host. These includes outright Banes, unassociated spirits, and Gaian spirits that have various levels of corruption.

                                                        The sorcerers of the Host come from all types of members - vampire blood sorcerers, humans, black magicians of the Fae, and even some of the allied Changing Breeds - who know the appropriate paths of spirit sorcery. Below are examples of some spirits known to sorcerers of the Host, but the spirits capable of being summoned by them are not limited to them.

                                                        Technically, this is not a spirit brood because Odin is not an Incarna and cannot serve the same kind of protective role the Icarna can perform. But the Host does have a body of spiritual knowledge that allows them to summon some groups of dependable spirits based on prior and ongoing chiminage.

                                                        I'll start first with some typical spirits known to the Garou.

                                                        Raven

                                                        There are many kinds of raven spirits existing in multiple spirit broods. It seems it is just the nature of these kinds of spirits that they, as a whole, have chosen many masters. A number of raven spirits have simply chosen to help the followers of the All High. The sorcerers of the Host use them mostly as spies and messengers. A smaller subset of these spirits are heavily Wyrm tainted, and it is these - and only these - that assist the Valravn and find the location of additional spirit eggs. I would use the same mechanics as normal Raven spirits, and just give them some different flavor text.

                                                        Wild Hunt

                                                        The All High also managed to seduce a small number of spirits associated with the Wild Hunt, something which infuriates the Fianna. The sorcerers of the Host typically only summon these Wild Hunt spirits when they need to deal with a martial threat in the Umbra. They can also summon them to manifest in the physical realm to kill someone, but can only do so between the autumn and spring equinoxes, and only at night or if stormclouds blot out the sun. These limitations are a result of the chiminage the All High needed to perform in order to seduce these spirits. Again, the spirit mechanics are the same, but use different flavor text. The Huntsman himself appears much like the All High while his Hounds appear more like wolves.

                                                        European Wildcat

                                                        The Ceilican sorcerers and their mortal friends are the ones most likely to deal with the Wildcat spirit, as he is their patron. This spirit is unlikely to assist more obvious Wyrm creatures unless it suits the individual spirits’ own purposes. Unlike the other spirits here, these European Wildcat spirits are not corrupted and are still Gaian. They only deal with the Ceilican and not others in the Host.

                                                        Bat

                                                        Bat spirits are heavily divided due to the madness of their Incarna after the destruction of the Cazamotz. Many serve the Wyrm while others remain loyal to Gaia. As a result, the All High has managed to secure the services of many bat spirits. While mortals and fae make much greater use of raven spirits, vampire sorcerers have found bat to be very useful, especially for exploring underground caves that provide shelter from sunlight (and potential enemies). Bat is also a possible totem for the Ulfhednar Skin Dancers.

                                                        Carrion Bird

                                                        Originally a spirt associated with the totem of the White Howlers, it has fallen into disfavor since that tribe’s fall but is not explicitly a Wyrm totem. Long neglected by the Garou in the centuries since, the spirit is known to help a variety of Garou who seek his help. This spirit is petitioned most by the Ulfhednar, but assists others in the Host as well. The Carrion Bird spirits are not exactly corrupt or Wyrm tainted, but are of dubious nature and desperate for any form of chiminage and support. If they cannot get it from the Garou, then the Host will work fine.

                                                            Next are more typical Banes and corrupted spirits.

                                                            Minotaur (Grendel/Grinder)

                                                            The Minotaur, known to the Host as the Grinder (or Grendel), is the patron of the Ulfhednar skin dancers. The Grinder is best seen as an Aspect of Minotaur that has a pact with the All High.

                                                            Scrags

                                                            Many Scrags have relations with the Host of the All High. Although Banes are servants of the Wyrm, the All High considers Scrags as mere tools, lacking the wit and will to oppose him. Like fire, Scrags can be a dangerous tool, but the followers of the All High believe they know enough precautions to deal with them successfully.

                                                            Scryers

                                                            The Host considers the Banes known as Scryers to be similar to Scrags. Scryers are useful tools they can obtain from their enemy. Useful, but weak, properly trained sorcerers can easily impose their will, and the Host has access to many Scryers.

                                                            Bitter Rages

                                                            A powerful Bane, the Host consider Bitter Rages very dangerous and unpreditable, but sometimes it is necessary to use them when a particularly powerful pack or larger group of Garou are attacking them. Only the most wise and powerful sorcerers of the Host would ever seek to make use of these Banes.

                                                            Thunraz

                                                            These are storm spirits under the Incarna Typhon written in the Rage Across Russia sourcebook. Odin needs his own storm god ally, and the description of Typhon in Rage Across Russia provides a good reason for it. Werewolf gave us two different descriptions of Typhon - the villainous spirit in Rage Across Russia, and the Typhon written in the revised Shadowlords Tribebook where no mention of his villainy was present. It is not clear if this was an outright retcon, or if Typhon’s betrayal of Thunder was not mentioned because of the player oriented nature of the tribebook. I suspect it was the former. If you want both kinds of Typhon spirit, I’d just make them two different spirits and rename the one in Rage Across Russia and move on. (I think the Typhon described in the Freak Legion sourcebook is an entirely different spirit, much more clearly aligned with the Wyrm. It's a shame that the game misused this spirit's name so many times. It just creates confusion.) While the “Thunraz” Incarna himself may be Typhon, I would use a variation of the Sturms in Axis Mundi as the actual spirits of this description that would be summonable by the Host.
