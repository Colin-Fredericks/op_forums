    thoughts on the BSD's metis strategy

    05-10-2020, 09:54 PM
    What are the effects on the Black Spiral Dancers demographically if they've been producing a lot of Metis? Canonically, the reason the population of the Black Spiral Dancers became so high starting in the nineties is that the tribe intentionally adopted a strategy of breeding Metis. I can't remember what percentage they were, but I think it was very high.

    This made sense as a strategy if you assume that the end times are near. and you need every single foot soldier you can in time for the Apocalypse. However, if you happen to be wrong about the timing, this should extremely hurt the BSD numbers going forward. I'm assuming that in canon, that sometime in the 1980s (perhaps earlier) there was a conscious decision by the BSDs to boost their numbers by artificial means through adopting a Metis breeding strategy.

    1) Metis can't breed so they are useless when it comes to creating new Garou. When you have a third to a half of your population infertile, it's a demographic time bomb.
    2) For every Metis born, you have prevented future breeding stock you control in terms of fertile Garou or kinfolk that could have been born instead. So future breeding stock that should have been born wasn't. However many normal kinfolk or Garou that would normally have been born by female BSDs just weren't born. So something around half of the normal kinfolk the BSDs should have doesn't exist. It's been lost.
    3) We can assume that the vast majority of female BSDs were in effect drafted to being brood mares (otherwise, you couldn't have produced all that metis). There were exceptions, but there couldn't have been that many and probably didn't produce many children at all. However, by this point, the available fertile female Garou have to be mostly exhausted. And that population of fertile female Garou wasn't replaced.
    4) The Metis strategy has to be exhausted by this point. A woman is only fertile for so long. Not only have the fertile female Garou been tapped out by this point, but their numbers were never replaced so there should be even less fertile female Garou now than there was to begin with.
    5) Fertile male Garou always had the option of impregnating female kinfolk (and normal humans) as well as the tribe's female Garou. However, I don't think that would have produced many Garou children. I don't believe the Black Spiral Dancer culture would be able to take care of any such children and properly raise them to be loyal or available (I think the Gaian tribes would be far better at this). Besides, if they truly believed the Apocalypse was coming so that Metis breeding was taken to such extremes, then any kinfolk breeding would be incidental. There wouldn't be a real back up plan.
    6) Alternate options like recruiting from fallen Garou or abducting kinfolk have their own limits. While this occurs, I don't think it will have much a demographic effect.

    As a I result, I think the bad bet the BSDs made in the 1980s should now be hurting them. They should be in demographic free fall. They probably reached their population peak around 2000 and managed to preserve most of their numbers for another ten years. But the past decade should have seen their numbers decline dramatically. They just can't replace their numbers. And their available breeding stock is now much less than it was before they embarked on their strategy. Their numbers should decline dramatically, and therefore the threat they represent should also decline in proportion. And this should drive extreme changes within the tribe as they deal with the fall out.

    Now in practice, I don't think this will be represented in the game line because STs like to use BSDs since they're of equivalent power to Garou PCs and therefore make good enemies. But I do think this would be an interesting change as it naturally flows from the assumptions of the original setting.

          The "breeding Metis" strategy has always been mathematically wrong.

          If the BSDs (or any Garou) want to produce as many werewolves as possible, as fast as possible, the answer is simple: Lupus.

          Wolves have between 4 and 14 pups a year, who will reach physical maturity at age two (even if in the wild reproductive maturity is delayed to three years for females by survival stress), and a managed pack's age can average around 10 years old. So one female lupus kin can give birth to 70 pups over her life if cared for instead of left in the wild. Probably resulting in around 20-25 female lupus kin after some infant mortality, and around 7 werewolves. Lupus pregnancies are the shortest (so female Garou can take less time off this way) and safer than Metis births. Female lupus Garou, who can remain reproductive for 3 times longer can continue to add even more.

          If BSDs really want to expand their numbers, and you presume that the captivity necessary to maximize what a female lupus's reproductive potential will lead to a domestication effect that removes kin status over a few generations, they shouldn't be relying on Metis, they should be focused on stealing female lupus kin from Gaian packs.

          I treat the BSD Metis breeding to be more cultural as a flaunting of breaking the Litany rather than a matter of practicality.

              The numbers of the Black Spiral Dancers is definitely bolstered by their Metis Breeding Program, although they do have the secondary benefit of being the destination of choice for any Wyrm-tainted garou. With a slow growth and then more ambitious as of the Industrial Revolution, the misjudgement of how bad the Eye of Wyrm shining would be has probably had a sorry effect on the tribe.

              1. The Metis are definitely a population liability as in the long run they are worth little more then today's shock troopers given their infertility. Any plan built upon Metis is a short-term one.
              2. Metis do end up replacing the children that may have been sired by female BSD which does lead to half of the BSD population siring infertility. It may be somewhat counterbalanced by the capacity that male garou have to spread their genes, but the nature of Metis pregnancy also means that the females who survive grow less and less likely to be fertile again, or even to survive.
              3. In this respect, the Black Spiral Dancers do have some things going for them. While likely a majority of capable female garou were constantly being impregnated, garou are capable of remaining in fighting condition through out most of pregnancy. As well garou women remain in peak physical condition longer then mortal women, giving those who survive the lifestyle a longer range of potential begetting. The unfortunate flipside of this though is that Metis pregnancy is dangerous and scarring, even if a woman survives she could become barren if she's unlucky due to complications.
              4. Assuming that the policy assumed the Apocalypse would come in the late 90s-early 2000s; the MBP would probably have to begin in the 70s-80s. While it's definitely not in good shape, it's possible there are still garou females of that era able to bear children. The issue is of course that it does strain the population and almost guarantee a coming BSD baby bust, even if male BSD and kinfolk double their efforts.
              5. I do think that there's a fair chance the male BSD would have remained active and genetically spreading. It's likely that they would have continued breeding with kinfolk, humans, and wolves throughout this process. However I do agree that the environment of the BSD tribe is certainly to be dangerous and with a high infant mortality rate, I'd suggest that their BSD males make up for that through constant breeding and a willingness to wave restrictions of eligibility. The situation would've be great and a baby bust is likely, but I don't think it's guaranteed to be catastrophic and despite their nature, the BSD do tend to look after their kin... in their own fashion. The danger is less BSD kin dying off then them being lost or discovered by Gaian's.
              6. Aye, the BSD benefit from garou falling to the Wyrm and kidnapping kinfolk, but this is limited and is often worth less then the trouble it takes. Kidnapping gaian kinfolk is best left for rituals and scare tactics then population growth.

              I do agree that taking this into consideration, the Black Spiral Dancers should be headed into a freefall and from a Doyalist perspective it makes for a great analogy about the dangers of ignoring long-term consequences in pursuit of short-term profit and success.

              From another perspective though, like you said Black Fox, it raises the question of what to do with our presentation of the Black Spiral Dancers, one of Werewolf's staple antagonists.

              Ultimately I suppose I'd end up saying that more emphasis can be placed onto the role of fomori and wyrm cults while the Black Spiral Dancers step back and can now reflect the population crisis scares that plagued the Garou Nation and we can compare and contrast the differences and unpleasant similarities in breeding tactics between the two.

              But ultimately that's just my two cents.

                                          Originally posted by Reasor View Post
                                          The long term effect on BSD population should be minimal. The parents of a Metis aren't stricken with sterility. There's nothing stopping them from having viable children with kinfolk.
                                          I disagree with your point for one big reason - when your female Garou are having metis babies, they can't be having other babies. And a woman is only fertile for a certain amount of time. There's about a twenty year period and after that, she isn't going to bear any more children. So you're taking out a significant amount of your Garou parents through a metis breeding strategy.

                                          Now maybe you can say that female Garou are bearing both metis and normal children. But I don't think that is realistic for two reasons. One, if you truly believe that the end times are approaching and you need lots of metis children now, then any other children is a luxury you can't afford. You are subverting your own strategy. So we should expert that once the BSDs implemented such a a strategy, they have to do their best to enforce it. Any half ass measures won't cut it. Second, female BSDs might be convinced they have to bear so many children "for the cause", but it is probably not popular and goes against their Garou instincts to be warriors. There is a limit to how constantly pregnant they can be. If they're expected to bear so many metis children, they probably won't be willing to bear many other children as well. And unlike kinfolk stock, they can't be easily bullied into bearing more than children than they want. Otherwise, you have a very self-destructive conflict within the tribe. So you're essentially taking out half of your Garou breeding stock.

                                          So that leaves the male half which do have the option of impregnating female kinfolk. This is an option as others have mentioned, but even here I think the BSDs would have shot themselves in the foot.

                                          Breeding lots of kinfolk still takes a significant amount of resources regardless of how it is done. Children are an investment. It takes financial resources, time, and effort. Even if you establish orphanages or other institutions to take over the actual responsibility of raising them, that money has to come from somewhere as does any cult indoctrination. Any group so invested in the "the end times are NOW and we need to breed as many metis as possible for the war" strategy is not going to make the investments needed to raise a large number of kinfolk and potential homid Garou. That is simply wasted resources. If breeding metis is your strategy, then you commit to it. You don't do it half-assed because that would be the worst of both worlds.

                                          A good comparison is to look at the Allied and Axis strategies in WWII. Many people are impressed by the German success in rocketry and jets in the last two years of the war. V2s and Me 262s are cool. However, before the war the world's leaders in rocketry and jets were the US and UK. So why did the Germans "jump" ahead of them? The Allies made a conscious decision NOT to invest much in those technologies because they knew the war would end before those technologies were sufficiently developed to be war winning weapons. There was a conscious decision by the Allies to only invest resources in those designs that they knew could be put into mass production before the end of the war (which they successfully calculated would be in the 1944-1946 time frame). The Allies bet correctly and they won the war.

                                          In contrast, we can look to Germany as why making a bad bet leads to terrible outcomes. Unlike the Allies who correctly figured out the actual timeline of the war (based on a combination of economic statistics and accurate strategic models), the Germans constantly bet on the war being short. They invaded Poland thinking Britain and France would not interfere. They believed Britain would would not continue to fight on after France surrendered. They thought the Soviet Union would collapse after six months of fighting at the most. And Hitler declared war on the United States because he believed the US would not be able to intervene in the European theatre since its fleet was sunk, it would concentrate its resources on Japan, and that the American economy would not produce as much as it did. As a result, its economic and war plans were constantly based on short term thinking. That is why near the end of the war Germany wasted much of its economic production on the "wonder weapons" - because they knew they were going to lose anyway so why not roll the dice in hoping the V2 and Me 262 were somehow going to miraculously win the war.

                                          The BSDs simply bet wrong. And as a result, they'll now be in siginificant demographic decline.


                                          Once the metis generation dies off, if the metis' numbers aren't restocked by simply breeding more, then the BSD's population should simply go back to pre-war levels.
                                          Nope, for the reasons I explained above. Their numbers are going to suffer, and it is going to take a long time before it stabilizes. The BSDs won't go extinct, but I definitely see a large demographic decline of population loss. In a few years we might even see a cumulative decline of something like 15 or 20%. Before it stabilizes, we might see something like half the population of BSD Garou after a decade of losses. And if the Gaian Garou realize this and make a coordinated offensive to exploit this, their numbers could decline even more.

                                          Of course, we can think of all sorts of ways this could be reverse. Maybe PENTEX made back up plans and recruited BSD kinfolks for fertility treatments, ongoing medical experiments, daycare or orphanages. But if they did that, then they'd be in position to control these kinfolk and any resulting Garou, putting the traditional BSD leadership in a bind as a new "camp" might be more loyal to PENTEX than their own kind. That might make an interesting story turn of events.

                                          Of course, as an ST you could always decided that the BSDs did not engage in a metis breeding strategy and explain their numerical increase in the late twentieth century another way. That would go against canon, but it would preserve the use of BSDs in their current role.

                                          However, if you want to go with canon and like a living chronicle that plays out in plausible manner, you now have an interesting development that was not intended, but could be fun for the ST to play with.
