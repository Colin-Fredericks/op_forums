    Originally posted by heinrich View Post
    Well "While some Garou tribes allow Kinfolk to participate in or be present during the performance of rites, only Garou can successfully enact these ceremonies of the spirit."
    That's from PGtG page 127...
    Okaaaay... So... How exactly does that conflict with Morning Song? Like, all the other Rites need Garou participation to work (or are otherwise rituals that have no actual effect), and Morning Song is a Rite that specifically involves a Kin praising a spirit directly. If the spirit is pleased it may empower the Rite -- or not. Like, what's the issue?

    ​The Garou are not enacting the Rite (as that quote states), as the culmination of the Rite involves the spiritual or magical effect. Yes, that's literally what that passage says, but when taken in the context of (pretty much) all other descriptions of how Rites work, that's not actually literally true. The spirits are the ones actually enacting (although I would use the phrase "empowering) the Rite -- the Garou are simply performing it. What that passage really means is that only the Garou can perform a Rite and expect it to be empowered by the spirits. Kin cannot (as they are not party to The Great Spirit Pact).

    However, there's nothing that states (or even implies) that spirits cannot empower a Rite performed by Kin. There's plenty of passages that state that they generally don't -- but not "can't". Moreover, the Rite of Sacred Rebirth (being a Rite performed by Kin, and having rather powerful spiritual effects) would imply that that's precisely how it works (since the Banes that generally empower that Rite, typically want to).

                                        Originally posted by heinrich
                                        Well "While some Garou tribes allow Kinfolk to participate in or be present during the performance of rites, only Garou can successfully enact these ceremonies of the spirit."
                                        Originally posted by idpersona View Post
                                        Emphasis mine. That reads like exactly what you are looking for. I'm not sure how you can interpret that any other way.
                                        ​Yeah, only that passage is not actually literally true. The Garou cannot enact a rite. They can only perform it. Enacting it means empowering it, and only spirits can do that. For instance, there are certain places (for instance, Malfeas) where the spirits which are required to empower Rites are simply not present (nor can be), and Rites simply fail automatically -- in which case, Garou are most certainly NOT enacting the Rite. That passage was an inarticulate way of saying that only Garou (and other Fera) can perform a Rite and expect it to function (as per the Great Spirit Pact).

                                        ​What the Garou do with Rites and what Kinfolk do with Rites are fundamentally the same thing. The only difference is what the spirits do -- or what they're contractually obligated to do. And if spirits are generally disinclined to empower Rites performed by beings whom they are not obligated to do anything with, then yes, Rites generally do not work for those individuals. There is nothing anywhere that says that spirits cannot empower Rites performed by Kinfolk, however.

                                        ​And if you think there is, then I would invite you to find me a passage anywhere that actually says that. (Not says "don't" -- says "can't")
