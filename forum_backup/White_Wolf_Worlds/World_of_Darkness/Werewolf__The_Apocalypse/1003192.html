                  I'm kinda gonna go against the grain on this one. You can actually skin spirits, sort of. Rite of the Flayed God from BotW involves making spirits into cloaks or clothing via harvesting their hollow 'shells.' Additionally, some spirits expressly have something akin to a physicality, depending on their native realm. Places like Pangea or Arcadia have spirits that behave like animals and people, according to my ancient copy of the Umbra book... which means they got skins to take, if you got a way to keep ahold of it once you leave the realm.

                  Of course, Rite of the Flayed God is torturous and Wyrmish, and you have to be a changing breed to enact it in the first place, so it doesn't mean that exact rite is the solution to your problem, Rahod, but it still means an enterprising and mystically inclined being could liberate a spirit's epidermis; it isn't impossible, clearly.

                  Since resurrecting dead breeds is pretty far off the rez anyway, why not come up with a 'skinning knife' fetish or tanning unguent talens that allow for specific types of ancestors to be skinned? You get a kinfolk with gnosis that could attune to the stuff, and then all they need is means of traveling to umbral realms, and then actually getting their hands on ancestors to flay. Succeed or fail, sounds like an interesting (and messy!) story.

                  It seems more likely to result in some sort of botched mockery breed than anything else, but hey, if you want all the magical skullduggery to come to a happier conclusion in your story, be my guest.

                              Hello,

                              In my perspective:

                              1) Yes, but just with the pelts corresponding with her breed. The W20 - Shattered Dreams clearly speaks about Aztec jaguar warrior skin dancers. The skinner himself also stole the Rite from Balam skindancers.

                              2) The rite itself answers this question: "...allows to became a Garou..." So she becames a werewolf with all of the merits and drawbacks.

                              3) The spirits in the deep umbra are spirits, and not changers. They seem to be the fallen breeds, they speak & tase like them, and think like them. Maybe they don't even know they aren't Fera anymore. But they changed irreversibly. Their parts are become epheperma & can't be a focus of this ritual, even if you could conserve them with other ones.

                              Other books (Player's Guide, or Past Lifes maybe?) states, that:
                              a) If your animal kin is extinct or tamed the Fera can't produce changer offsprings.
                              b) In some cases (bats & ravens) a totem's intervention is needed to form new "offsprings". Bat is fallen. Rites won't work.

                              Reviving a member of an dead tribe won't resurrect a tribe. A totem is also needed. A pure-breeded BSD Ronin, won't become White Howlers.

                              Also the Rite won't work, because you, as was mentioned earlier, don't have Kinfolk of the lost. The changer "genes" / heritage dilutes after ~3 generations. (Shattered Dreams.) That's why we dont have kins of all Fera & changer births are uncommon

                              (Also you need 5 pelts harvested under the same auspice, which would be quite difficult.)
