                      Originally posted by MrornX View Post
                      What if the whole world presented in w5 is a lie created by the Wyrm as both a means to turn the king of the garous over to the wrym point of view and as a means of petty revenge.
                      If I was going to implement something like that, I'd probably use the Dream Zone in some kind of way. The affected Garou are in some kind of mass common dream. It could be only that the Garou are only impacted while they sleep, and you tell the story as essentially one single mass dream (no matter how long the dream appears to take - years, even decades, can go by in a single dream). Or possibly even the Garou who are awake are "trapped" in a type of shadow play like the level four Galliard gift Shadows by the Firelight. In either case, to use your scenario, the key to resolving this is in Albrecht's head - perhaps this is a nightmare generated by his unconscious mind (probably under pressure of a powerful Wyrm dream being).

                      The plot would be some mix of the Matrix, Inception, and Nightmare on Elm Street.

                      The big baddie behind it would be some powerful Wyrm aligned being in the Dream Zone invading Gaia's dream/reality. Presumably the help of Chimera would be instrumental in fixing the problem. In Garou cosmology, dreams are the forerunner of reality so the failure to stop this mass dream/delusion will eventually allow it to turn reality into the nightmare.

                      That's probably how I would try to run something like this.

                                      Funnily enough this is the end goal of the Eastern Concordat faction from By Nights Studio Version of WtA from what I understand when reading about them.


                                      Eastcons happily use the information and power they wield to hold space for humans, especially those who could deeply impact the world around them. This faction urges a stronger and more thorough integration of Kinfolk into Garou society as warriors, healers, and visionaries, much like the Dawn Tribes. They do not want to hide their Kinfolk away, keeping them from the world with the excuse of protecting them.

                                      Instead, Eastern Concordat members wish to teach and prepare their Kinfolk, giving them knowledge and tools to take a more active role in the world in which they live, embracing the ways and guidance of the Hearthbound to integrate kin into their societies. Through their kin, the Eastern Concordat actively seeks influence in the mortal halls of power, leveraging humanity itself to benefit Gaia and all her children. Eastcons believe that keeping up with the modern era is of import. Stagnation is another form of corruption, and by refusing to change with the world around them, the Garou have let this corruption take root and fester. Only by learning and growing can they embody the balance they seek to forge anew in the Triat.

                                                      I’d argue that the Garou way of war AS IT CURRENTLY EXISTS being increasingly inadequate to the task of averting the Apocalypse is, in fact, deliberately implicit to the game line - because it makes the Younger Generation, who are better able to spot the faults inherent in the system and lack incentive to stick with that system because it works for THEM far more important than they would be in a Garou Nation that worked without gratuitous faults (Given the Tribes tend to value age & experience, since these tend to bring power, personal and institutional).

                                                      The fact that the average PC Garou, unlike the average PC Kindred, actually have a solid shot at acquiring real power and influence in a relatively short time span (Given werewolves are NOT ageless and given the turnover rate of personnel in the Apocalypse War) makes it even more credible that this year’s pups might be next year’s celebrated reformer.

                                                      If nothing else, this approach certainly offers a wealth of interesting challenges to those hungry for some good roleplaying and hungry to make a difference in the hairier regions of the World of Darkness.
