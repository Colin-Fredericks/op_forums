    Spanish Werewolf: the Apocalypse 20th Anniversary (and Deluxe) Edition

    02-10-2015, 11:33 PM

    The Spanish V20 Deluxe Edition had some REALLY cool add-ons, which I may have to get too, if duplicated or similarly offered for W20 Spanish!

    Goals (as of May 2, 2015)

    As the number of patronage it grows, new rewards for participating will be unlocked. No matter when you've made your contribution: get all the goals that have been unlocked at the end of patronage. As steps are being surpassing goals we'll be revealing new ones.
    • € 20,000. Bookmark lupine exclusive issue for all patrons. PASSED!
    • € 35,000. Bandanna cloth (handkerchief large) Commemorative patronage for all patrons. PASSED!
    • € 50,000. Improved screen. Narrator screen is printed in hardcover laminating 2 mm (hardcover) instead of cardboard of 1 mm (as planned and reflecting the English version) for all patrons whose rewards include the screen. In addition, all levels that do not include the screen will receive a PDF copy of it.
    • € 65,000. Glossary on paper. All patrons (except Parentela level) will receive a printed copy in full color glossy Glossary Basic H20.€ 80,000. The Scalper. The first supplement 20th anniversary Werewolf completely free for patrons: pdf for patrons of Kin level and more pdf paper for everyone else. PASSED!
    • € 95,000. Notebook character sheets. Character sheets customized for each tribe, plus a special sheet of four pages in a notebook format staple forever keep them safe. It includes electronic version for all patrons.
    • € 110 000. Certificate of limited numbered edition. Patronage packs include a certificate of authenticity with dry-laid stamp printed in heavy paper (or similar quality) signed one by one by Bill Bridges, editor of the original line of Werewolf and author of the 20th anniversary edition.
    • € 125,000. Embossed Notebook. A notebook (A6) with leatherette cover embossed with a commemorative symbol of patronage.
    Now, on to the participation part of this post:

    The translation of W20 from English to Spanish is underway. However, the translators are looking for input!
    "Wyld" apparently doesn't translate very well, and they're looking for suggestions!

    SO, if you're fluent in Spanish, or know someone who is and is a fan of W:tA and would like to give input, check out the Facebook page, read the info below, and submit the Google Document form to help out!

    https://www.facebook.com/QueremosElV...1708347574486/
    Muchos fans de Hombre Lobo se están preguntando cuánto falta para Hombre Lobo: El Apocalipsis 20º Aniversario. Pues bien, ¡ya no falta mucho tiempo! La traducción principal lleva varias semanas terminada y ahora estamos con las correcciones, para después pasar a la maquetación. Estimamos que el mecenazgo daría comienzo en abril.

    Entretanto, nos hemos encontrado con un problema de traducción tan controvertido dentro del equipo de Biblioteca Oscura que hemos decidido resolverlo con una encuesta entre los fans: ¿cómo traducirías "the Wyld"?

    https://docs.google.com/forms/d/16zs...?usp=send_form

    ¿Hacia qué lado se inclinará la balanza? ¡Participa con tu opinión!

                                  Sooo, I just got this in the mail today!







                                  That's a silver foil-stamped brown box (A heavy textured card board), "Hombre Lobo: El Apocalipsis" (A traditionally-printed H20 book with die-cut cover), a bandanna with a wolf skull, an embossed journal with cream pages, custom dice with the H20 glyph for the 10, A bookmark, A heavy stock ST screen, "El Desollador" ("The Skinner"), "Cuaderno de Hojas de Personate" (a bound book of full-color character sheets for each Tribe), and "Glosario H20 Basico" (amazingly a Basic H20 glossary of terms and definitions... in Spanish and parts in English too, plus some new artwork too!) Oh, and the numbered certificate of authenticity!
