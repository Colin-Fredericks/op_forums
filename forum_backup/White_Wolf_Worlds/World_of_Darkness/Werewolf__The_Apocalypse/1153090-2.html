            Well as requested...

            "Werewolves are allergic to Vampiric Vitae and are more difficult to Ghoul or Blood Bond. Only a few can ingest the stuff without violently vomiting it back up, If you like you can call for a Gnosis Roll difficulty 3 the first time a werewolf tastes vampire blood. If the roll succeeds then the Garou in question is one of those allergic to the stuff; if it fails or botches then the Garou has a tolerence for vampire vitae."

            "Abominations;
            Occasionally a foolish Leech tries to make a pet of a Garou usually by means of the Embrace. Because almost all Garou are violently allergic to vitae they are accordingly hard to ghoul. By grace of Gaia they are just as difficult to Embrace: Most werewolves die upon the attempt. Sadly the Embrace works in a few, rare instances - and the miserable results are rightly named Abominations.

            Abominations are created in the same way as mostly all vampires, save that even the most depraved Black Spiral dancer isn't inclined to sacrifice their spiritual higher being for the powers of the grave.

            Garou receive on final chance to escape the living death; upon Embrace the Garou makes a reflexive (and instinctive) Gnosis roll difficulty 6 to die quietly. If he succeeds then the Garou dies quietly without pain and his spirit goes is destined place. If he fails then he dies in torturous agony but still the spirit is freed. If the roll botches they become an Abomination with no chance of ever seeing the Tribal Homelands."

            It's worth noting that Willpower can be spent on the Gnosis rolls.

            So to get an Abomination you almost need a willing Garou who fails a diff 3 Gnosis roll and then Botches a diff 6 Gnosis roll... And then there are the Frenzy risks...
