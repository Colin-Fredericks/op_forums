    05-12-2017, 03:17 PM
    Hey guys,

    I'm going to be running Under a Blood Red Moon as a campaign for my new pack of players as their first werewolf game and I'm trying to figure out ways of modifying the story and updating it.     Anyone have any experiences with the game or suggestions for making it better?

      My Problems with Under a Blood Red Moon

      1. Abomination are stupid: I'm inclined to think it's an inherently unsalvageable concept and it would be better just to state the two species can't crossbreed. However, I like the idea of the Harold Goodston. Basically, SOME guy who is completely beyond the pale of Garou society who wants back in. I'm curious what to replace his "thing" with.

      I have a couple of ideas:

      A. Harold is a somehow-sane Black Spiral Dancer who wants to repent.
      B. Harold is a member of the Skin Dancers tribe.
      C. Harold is actually Samuel Haight with a complete personality change or maybe he's playing the role of the repentant were to get close to them.

      2. Gulfora the Succubus: Obviously, this is another crossover issue as a Christian demon doesn't really fit the Garou mythos. I'm inclined to think of just substituting an Enticer Formori into the story but if so, is she an independent Formori or is it Pentex behind it or what? Would another kind of spirit manifesting be better? Or perhaps a Black Spiral Dancer who just happens to be extremely attractive (illusions?)

      3. Corrupting Jupiter: I actually like the idea of corrupting a Totem but the whole Jupiter Sept seems very out of step with later cosmology, especially since Celestines are so far removed from daily affairs. What would be a good Totem to substitute? Grandfather Thunder? If so, would that REALLY make the Shadow Lords furious about all this? What about another Totem? Maybe it's about summoning Eater of Souls or some other figure through an Anchor-Point.

      What's the Maguffin?

      4. Vampires and Weres going to War: The premise depends on the Sabbat and Black Spiral Dancers teaming up to take the Jupiter Sept with the Garou blaming vampires in general for it. However, that depends on a lot of ignorance of the Garou about vampires as well as the assumption no one would think to check a little deeper.

      Also, there's the problem in the game the Gangrel and Garou make a truce which seems like would at least be someone pointing out vampires have NO knowledge of the Spirit World so they can't be involved or it's a different group of vampires involved in the war. Yes, I know that means I have a problem with the basic premise but I'd prefer to shore it up rather than throw it away completely. Maybe the King of the Silver Fangs (Jacob Morningstar?) thinks it's a good idea, I dunno?

      Why the war and why now?

                  Here are some things to think about. However, I will say that I haven't read UBRM in decades so I don't know how appropriate they are to your details.

                  1) The Abomination - Can you use a Kinfolk who was embraced?

                  2) I don't see why a Succubus would present a problem? I always assumed that Garou and the other game lines would always classify creatures according to how they understand that. So Garou wouldn't think Gulfora is a "Christian demon", they would think she's some powerful Bane in service to the Maeljin Incarna of Lust.

                  3) The Chicago Fanum is supposed to be a center of occultists and Native American artifacts and legends, right? You could use Thunderbird. It is a fairly important mythological figure among Native Americans and should fit well with various aspects of Jupiter.

                  4) You could have a sleeper agent among either the Camarilla or Garou who want the fight to happen so that the plan goes off. A vampire advisor to Lodin who is really a Sabbat infiltrator would work. You could even have an influential Garou planning to defect to the BSDs if you need someone on the other side. As for the "truce", it is possible that someone powerful on the vampire side (say Inyanaga of the Gangrel or even Menele) is recognized by the local Garou as some kind of important figure, dangerous but friendly, which they haven't realized is actually a vampire. That figure could then give advice/premonition/etc. that to properly succeed, they should accept the offer of a truce by one of the vampire groups (which is then made by a subordinate, protecting their identity). Both characters are fairly knowledgable about a lot of things, so their awareness of the spirit world shouldn't be an issue.

                                      Revised under a Blood Red Moon part 1

                                      Okay, here's my rough idea for the story. It will include a big change to the story to tie into the Croatan and Roanoke.

                                      1. The Black Spiral Dancers working with a sect of Sabbat Infernalists massacre the population of the Farnum and proceed to start corrupting the caern/Anchorhead to become a Wyrm caern with atrocities. They also break the Masquerade/Veil in many locations which is part of the second part of the plan. Ed Neally, working as advisor to Prince Lodin, has him blame the werewolves and create a story which the werewolves are working with the Sabbat.

                                      The Infernalists have many Banes summoned around Lodin and begin drugging his vessels (Ventrue!) which Neally has access to with Pentex provided products designed to up his paranoia. They also have his mistress, Lorraine, targeted which the PCs will eventually find as the Sabbat send him threatening notes claiming they have werewolf allies. Lodin, not in the best of shape, proceeds to declare war on Chicago's werewolves with many names provided by Ed Neally through his Sabbat contacts (who get them from the BSD and their allies in Pentex). Several werewolves, kinfolk, and allies are killed in the resulting massacre.

                                      2. The BSD and Sabbat are running at cross purposes during this mission or at least most of the Sabbat. The BSD are planning to use the Farnum, the massacre of many humans, and war to try to summon an aspect of the Wyrm into reality. They're hoping for Eater-Of-Souls but are okay with something less impressive. The fact some Croatan kinfolk escaped to Chicago and became part of the Uktena involved in the Farnum makes the thing all deliciously symbolic in the BSD's minds. They and their infernalist allies are hoping for a full-on "end of the world" time of judgement scenario.

                                      The Sabbat, by contrast, are painfully ignorant about the whole infernalism/apocalypse angle and just think they're involved in a Garou tribal conflict. They think they're using the BSD and that they can take the city while Lodin is having his mental breakdown.

                                      3. I'm going to change the character of Harold Goodston to throw a surprise at my PCs since a couple of them have played this campaign before. In this version, Harold Goodston was a Shadow Lord kinfolk/Puritan in 1600s England and a noted vampire hunter. He eventually got embraced by the Tremere and this drove him insane as he felt abandoned by Gaia as well as damned by his own kinfolk. He either studied the Wyrm ritual at Roanoke extensively or may have even been peripherially involved. He's also the one directing the BSD and Sabbat infernalists.

                                      The thing is, Harold is actually a Wild Card rather than the seeming Wyrm cultist everyone assumes him to be. Harold has the insane idea that if he summons a suitably powerful Wyrm spirit that the Garou will unite against it as well as destroy the Black Spiral Dancers as a tribe (since a decent chunk of North America's will be there). He also is a megalomaniac enough that if it DOES start the apocalypse, it will save the world from the Wyrm as he believes Gaia will intervene somehow. He's confused his Garou and Christian theology considerably.

                                      As such, he'll inform the PCs about what's going on "his" side several times. As a Sabbat Inquisitor (ironic), Harold also has access to Vicissitude and Protean so he can simulate a crude mockery of Crinos form.

                                      4. On the Garou end, this is actually a much bigger deal than one city in America as all the seers predict something BIG is happening. The PCs being assigned vampire hunting duties is not actually meant to be the primary vanguard of the war as King Albretch is going to be working to destroy all the Black Spiral Dancers in the city that he can who have been raising hell plus trying to retake the Farnum. Vampire-hunting is a secondary front of the war as Lodin is just causing all manner of interference with their attempts to deal with what may be a massive Wyrm infestation (but no one knows how big).

                                      It's only while they're hunting vampires that they may stumble on what's really going on.

                                      5. On the vampire end of things, from their perspective, things are suddenly a lot more insane than their used to. Hundreds of citizens are going missing, "riots" are happening everywhere that they're struggling to overcome, and they're trying to keep the National Guard OUT rather than getting called in. Only about halfway through the adventure do they clue into the fact the Sabbat are involved and immediately go on a war footing. Most vampires want nothing to do with Lodin's declared Blood Hunt on the city's werewolves even as he's brought in outside Archons and contractors (burning every favor he has) to do fighting--including allying with Anarchs.

                                      Quite a few vampires are just hunkering down or realizing they should get the hell out of the city.

                                      6. Because the Garou's worst enemy is themselves, I want to create a Rank 5 (maybe 6) Garou of the Get of Fenris, Shadow Lords, or Silver Fangs to thoroughly screw the pooch. Someone just underneath Albretch and a legendary vampire hunter who comes down to join Albretch's Crusade before immediately diverting large amounts of resources to fight on fronts other than the Farnum. Kills-The-Dead is an old as dirt Garou with a Grand Klaive or Silver Sword who wants to go out in the blaze of glory and sees this as his best chance of doing so. He was a former friend of Jacob Morningkill during his saner days and absolutely disrespects Albretch but has enough pull to throw his work into disarray. Kills-The-Dead is starting to have the typical Silver Fang madness in delusions of grandeur in his old age and believes if he DOESN'T die he might become King and lead the Garou on a purge even greater than Albretch's of the Seventh Generation--cleansing the continent of vampires (if not the world).

                                      I may make him the head of the Silver Pack, too, making an opening when they all get killed in the end.

                                                      Under a Blood Red Moon part 2

                                                      Here's my rough outline so far for what is rapidly becoming a campaign.

                                                      Act One: The Road to Chicago

                                                      1. The PCs will head to a gas station where they will end up accidentally encountering a Sabbat pack as they're making some shovel heads. Not caring about making the Sabbat "badass", I'm going to have them flee on motorcycles with the shovel-heads left behind and insane with hunger. Of course, the PCs are unlikely to help them and will most likely slaughter them but they're actually innocents in all of this. The police will also arrive and while they can be driven off by Crinos form, if the PCs don't sabotage their cameras in the car, they'll have violated the veil.

                                                      Note: We did this section last night and it went quite well. I had Harold Goodston as one of the Sabbat pack and talk to them about his plans before he fled with Mist form like a coward.

                                                      2. The PCs will arrive at a massive moot outside of Chicago at a really-really overcrowded local sept that looks a bit like werewolf Woodstock. King Albretch has summoned as many wolves as were willing to make it to North America and there may well be a 1000 of them present with more possibly to follow. The PCs will have some chance to move around and experience Garou culture separate from homid.

                                                      Some encounters I have planned are:

                                                      * Finding out the Garou have taken a bunch of police and local farmers in the area hostage at a house because they investigated. Some drunken angry Garou want to slaughter them all to protect the Litany.
                                                      * The PCs encountering a Kinfolk woman who escaped her abusive husband with a new lover, only to find the "higher-ranked" Garou is also at the moot.
                                                      * Some chance to deal with tribes they don't normally meet like Stargazers and an all Bonegnawer pack.
                                                      * A Glass Walker selling talens for cash money and then getting called out for it.
                                                      * Possibly a delegation of Shifters which are VERY uncomfortable here.
                                                      * A very drunk and attractive female Garou will want to seduce one of the male PCs in violation of the Litany because this thing is actually fairly common at moots. It's just a terrible place for it.

                                                      3. Albretch will then call them to speak privately, explain they're going to do some vampire hunting and that he wants this to go as smoothly as possible. His enemies are the Black Spiral Dancers and retaking the Farnum. He doesn't want this to explode. It's a plan which promptly goes to hell when Kills-the-Dead shows up and gives a speech about cleansing New York City along with exterminating all of the undead that rouses everyone to action.

                                                      4. Politics wise, the PCs can play around a bit as the Shadow Lords will be willing to help them with some of the above encounters if they're willing to agree to favors. They're encouraging the conflict between Albretch and Kills the Dead because they want to weaken his position politically. On the other hand, they actually are fairly good at getting people working on consensus and will surprisingly back down if the players make an argument that disrupting Albretch's plans actually would hurt the Garou nation as a whole.

                                                      5. At the end of the moot, the PCs are assigned to go visit a Kinfolk family, pick up the information on the vampires, and go slaying. That will be part of Act II, however, before everything completely goes to hell.
