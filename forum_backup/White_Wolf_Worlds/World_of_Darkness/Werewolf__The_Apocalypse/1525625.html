    [W5] Fixing Harano and Hauglosk

    11-19-2023, 04:06 AM
    While I don't mind Harano and Hauglosk as much as some of you, I do think they need to be fixed, just like Touchstones had to be fixed in V5*. I also think that Touchstones in W5 could be better integrated with the H&H mechanics.

    * In V5, we introduced a simple house rule for the Touchstone system. Dealing with a Touchstone in a meaningful way (doesn't have to be an interaction, but it should be relevant to play) adds +2 dice to the session's Remorse check. This way, Touchstones serve as a reminder for the PCs what it means to be human. This house rule made Touchstones more meaningful to us and gave a mechanical reason why a character would want to have Touchstones.
    ​
    In that sense, I want to overhaul the H&H mechanic in a way to bring it more in line with what I think makes sense without discarding it altogether. So, my basic idea is to make H&H more similar to Humanity with its Remorse checks.
    • Characters still have the five-box Harano and Hauglosk tracker. However, there are temporary versions of both stats... Let's call them "Anguish" for Harano and "Fervor" for Hauglosk. Basically, temporary Anguish and Fervor serve in a similar function as Stains in V5.
    • Points of Anguish and Fervor are marked by a "/" on their respective trackers. If the tracker is filled completely, Anguish is transformed into one box of Harano, Fervor is transformed into one box of Hauglosk.
    • Harano or Hauglosk are permanent, the box is filled completely.
    Anguish & Harano:
    • Characters can gain Anguish, if they experience major setbacks or other tragedies (number of points determined by the ST).
    • The character gains two Anguish if one of their Touchstones is damaged by their action. If one of their Touchstones is killed by their action, the character gains three Anguish.
    • Once per session, the player can opt to voluntarily mark down one Anguish to immediately increase their Rage to 5.
    • Players can make a Harano test for their character (usually at the end of the session). They roll a number dice equal to the unfilled boxes on their Harano tracker (minimum 1). Having dealt with a Touchstone in a meaningful way before making the check adds 2 dice to this roll. If the test fails, the character gains one point of Harano; if the test is successful, all Anguish is removed from the tracker (permanent Harano remains).
    • If the tracker is completely filled with points of Harano, the character succumbs to harano, leaving the game as a playable character.
    Fervor & Hauglosk:
    • Characters can gain Fervor, if they violate a chronicle tenet (number of points determined by the ST).
    • The character gains two Fervor if one of their Touchstones is damaged by their inaction. If one of their Touchstones is killed by their inaction, the character gains three Fervor.
    • Once per session, the player can opt to voluntarily mark down one Fervor to immediately restore all Willpower damage.
    • Players can make a Hauglosk test for their character (usually at the end of the session). They roll a number dice equal to the unfilled boxes on their Hauglosk tracker (minimum 1). Having dealt with a Touchstone in a meaningful way before making the check adds 2 dice to this roll. If the test fails, the character gains one point of Hauglosk; if the test is successful, all Fervor is removed from the tracker (permanent Hauglosk remains).
    • If the tracker is completely filled with points of Hauglosk, the character succumbs to hauglosk, leaving the game as a playable character.

    What do you think?

      I think if you don't want to make any huge changes,it's not a bad way to extend the doom-spiral long enough that most characters are never going to get removed from the game via the trackers without exceeding bad luck, or specific effort to drive them over the edge.

      I'm not sure the "take 1 Anguish/Fervor" for points in other places mechanic is worth keeping. While it's a nice little carrot to temp players to dip their toes in the water instead of avoiding gaining tracker points at all costs, it doesn't really make any sense in a narrative sense of what the character is actually doing. It feels like the mechanic should be reversed to something like, "if you would lose a point of Anguish/Fervor, you can opt instead to max your Rage/Willpower" for something that you can really envision as to what's happening in the PCs' heads. "Instead of completely letting go of my failure, unhealthily cling to some of the guilt as a splinter that drives me to anger," is something that you wouldn't consciously think of doing but something people definitely do. Likewise holding on to a bit of Fervor as drive more considered and thoughtful actions that still push towards your goals is something that makes sense. But as it stands, you get sad to get angry, and you get angry to emotionally center yourself... which is a very odd mental process.

                          Josh Heath on a podcast mentioned that the rules for Rage dice he playtested for W5 were more punishing than the version we got in the eventual release. That's an indication of what direction this was aiming for. Harano and Hauglosk are there to punish players. And you can see that in how much of a doomspiral they are you can't come back from.

                          As for the mechanics, I'm not a fan. Hauglosk only triggering through Chronicle Tenets should require a more directed discussion of those in the storytelling chapter rather than the general one we got. Harano is linked to Touchstones in a way I don't like as it mechanically urges STs to threaten/harm/kill Touchstones. It feels cheap to forcefully push for that in order to link between players and the Harano mechanic.

                          I thought about coming up with my own version but there's just so much that needs to be tweaked and expanded even while staying in the realm of how V5 was designed... It just didn't feel worth it.
