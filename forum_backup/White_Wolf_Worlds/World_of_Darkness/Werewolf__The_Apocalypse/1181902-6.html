                                            Originally posted by Story Letter View Post
                                            • The http://whitewolf.wikia.com/wiki/Tribe_(WTA)"]thirteen tribes of the Garou[/URL] are contemplating going into war with humanity due to their greed and destruction of the planet.
                                            • You do not choose your tribe at the start of the game.
                                            • You do not control a party of characters, you are solitary. You have allies outside of combat however.
                                            • Takes place in Nevada, Alaska and the redwood forests of Washington.
                                            • You do not align yourself to tribes as you play through the game, but you will encounter them and learn about their perspective of the conflict with humanity and the Wyrm.
                                            • Levels are semi-open and linear, but offers multiple ways to solve it similar to Deus Ex.
                                            • Each region have a large hub with free-roaming environment and different activities to do. Aid spirits of nature, such as destroying a dam to earn the favor of a river spirit, or destroy outposts. One such favor to a spirit can for example open a shortcut between regions or grant more side-missions. The hub allows you to go into instances where missions take place, and upon your return the hub will have changed. Other tribes may hear about your deeds as you progress and show up in the hub area.
                                            • The player can see and make visits to the Umbra, allowing communication and bartering with spirits. The trips however will be kept short.
                                            • Will take place in natural environments but also typical industrial environments, such as a dam.
                                            • Creatures that inhabit the World of Darkness will have a place in this game, for example Vampire: The Masquerade and Mage: The Ascension.
                                            • Other tribes you encounter in the world may be hostile or friendly.
                                            The Player Character
                                            • The protagonist is a male Garou named Cahal of the Fianna tribe, who was banished years ago for reasons unknown. He became a Ronin and lost connection with his tribe's totem. At the start of the game he returns to his old pack and the sacred Caern they are protecting, following the massacre of his family and the disappearance of his son.
                                            • Fianna are celtic influenced werewolves, who value family very highly. Cahal's concept art also does feature celtic tattoos.
                                            • Cahal can take on three forms at will: Lupus (wolf), Homid (human) and Crinos (werewolf).
                                            • Glabro (near beast) and Hispo (dire wolf) forms are not going to be playable.
                                            • They want it to be seamless to move between forms, for example making a long leap as a Lupus and shift into Crinos mid-jump.
                                            • There are skill trees that lets you focus on a particular gameplay archetype with its own strength and weaknesses.
                                            • There are mentions of Cahal's view on extremism among the Garou on the conflict, and how his matured world view would affect that, and the way his son changed while he was away.
                                            Lupus (wolf)
                                            • The travel form. Good for exploration, travel, stealth, sniffing out trails, and scouting foes. Can also enter areas that are otherwise too small to go through.
                                            • Lupus form have access to Umbral vision that lets him see Wyrm influence on e.g. human guards.
                                            Homid (human)
                                            • The interaction form. Capable of silent takedowns of enemies, activating traps, drones, hacking and gathering information from computers, and of course talking with other humans. Humans are unable to distinguish werewolves so Cahal is able to easily move undercover in the Homid form.
                                            • Wields a crossbow that can merge elemental spirits into it. Can be used to silently kill enemies, but also interact with the environment. For example exploding an explosive barrel with flaming arrows, or short circuit to open a door with an electrified arrow.
                                            • Regenerates health while in Homid form. However if the player has taken damage from silver-based weapons, it'll stack an effect that gradually lower their max health.
                                            • Homid form have access to Umbral vision that lets him see Wyrm influence on e.g. human guards.
                                            Crinos (werewolf)
                                            • The combat form. Savagely rip and tear your enemies as a werewolf. Build up Rage when you take damage, and reduce it by doing finishing moves. The higher the Rage, the more effective you'll be in combat by ways of unlocking stronger but slower attacks. But build up too much Rage, and you'll become Frenzied, which puts you into a state of uncontrollable rage.
                                            • Is able to clear up a cluttered area and creating space for you to fight on.
                                            • Rage can also build out of combat, by simple things like standing in a puddle of oil, seeing the corpse of an animal, a conversation about animal cruelty. This can then trigger a change to the Crinos form.
                                            • It's possible for Cahal to become enraged even during dialogue, causing him to eviscerate an important character.
                                            • You cannot leave Crinos form until all hostiles have been dealt with.
                                            • When Frenzied, you can't tell friend from foe. Therefore to leave a frenzied Crinos form you need to kill everything and suffer the consequences. The frenzied Crinos form is more powerful but has more inertia and becomes more difficult to maneuver.
                                            • When you are Frenzied you also attract the Wyrm, causing the Wyrm to spawn more enemies for you to fight in a sort of horde mode. It's intended to challenge those who enjoy combat, but at the same time you are on a timer. Too much time spent Frenzied fighting the Wyrm will eventually corrupt Cahal - resulting in a game over.
                                            • Other characters and animals will feel your Rage if it gets high enough, and also have an effect on a player tattoo (OP: very little information on the player tattoo so far).
                                            Enemies
                                            • The main antagonistic faction is Endron Oil, which is closely affiliated with the Wyrm and owned by Pentex Industries. Pentex in turn is also owned by vampires.
                                            • Endron Oil is aware of the existence of werewolves, and have guards using exoskeletons, silver weaponry and humans possessed by the Fomori - a form of Wyrm spirit.
                                            • Wyrm possessed humans will upon death release the Fomori spirit for you to fight.
                                            • Cahal can detect the Wyrm presence in humans using Umbral vision.
                                            The game takes place in Alaska, the main enemy is drilling firm and the protagonist is a lone warrior. His name should be Steven .

                                            But seriously, great job finding these info. I see you are as restless as I am in my quest for Heart of Gaia working prototype . And I'm still searching Weaver's Web song from this game.

                                            For those who oppose playing a pregen: Cahal WAS Fianna. But in the beginning he is a Ronin. Thing that bothers me more is which rank he achieved before his exile, cause Ronins may lost their status but not their skills and powers.

                                            As for creating character: according to the main rulebook the character which can join the biggest number of tribes is female lupus with very few backgrounds (and Pure Breed determine which tribes will be unavailable, Silver Fangs or Glass Walkers and Bone Gnawers). Not my favourite type of character. Of course as a ST I'm more versatile than the paper.
