    Took another look - says that the Glass Walkers simply "stopped cultivating" their PB, so Cockroach wasn't involved.

    As for the Black Furies, they used to not accept their own male Metis. W20's Umbra book details that since they started accepting male metis, emanations of boys and men have begun to appear in the Black Fury Spiritual Homeland, where previously there were only women and girls. It's also said that Pegasus refuses to accept males, with the exception of metis offspring of the Black Furies (the new status quo). At some point there was a changeover:

    Another major shift is the presence of men in the homeland. The Furies permitted no males in the Realm until recently; they relaxed the ban for their male Metis children. This unexpectedly led to greater changes — Furies suddenly found men in the human settlements where previously there were none.
    Either Pegasus changed her mind and told the Tribe to start adopting Metis, or the Black Furies reached a consensus of adopting Metis and petitioned Pegasus to accept these children of theirs. Either is plausible, but the latter is implied I would argue, as it says "they" relaxed the ban.

    And yeah, like I said PB is tribe-specific, but Kinfolk don't seem to have tribe-specific PB (though W20 could be clearer about the latter).

                            I am glad that W5 will have its fans, and rightly so. Werewolf is awesome! I'm glad new people will be brought in, and it may prove a gateway to W20 for those new players. I know a lot of V20 / "20th" players of all splats whose first ever WOD game was V5.

                            At the same time I think it's worth acknowledging that the people writing the book aren't a reliable source on the quality of the book. It'd be weird if they didn't like or defend it!

                            Controversies aside, negative comments on the work of old writers aside, half-truths and veiled insults aside, even when I ignore a plethora of reasons to write off W5, I find that the previews are just bleh. Having four sample characters is a cool idea, having four drawings for each Tribe is a cool idea!

                            For instance looking at W20 I could easily imagine the Glass Walker we see with Cockroach in his shadow having a few companions; a slightly mangy Lupus with a cybernetic jaw, a Crinos holding a cracked computer tablet and a gun, a Homid dressed for a business meeting with a wolf-like grin. And having writeups for who those four people are, their Auspices, how they fit into the Tribe, would be so cool! You could even shoot for a nice round five so as to give an example of each Auspice in each Tribe; it'd be word count and art intensive, but IMO it would be worth it.

                            But as much as I like the concept of multiple characters, I just look at a lineup of photomanipulated-looking human-looking people who could easily be Mages or Vampires or Changelings for all I know, none of whom have roles which seem to require a giant war form to fulfil.

                                            That would've been better, I think. And whereas the Hart Wardens are all about the harmony of the land/environment, you get other Tribes like Children of Gaia who focus on harmony amongst the people living on the land and the Ghost Council Tribe is about living in harmony with the spirit-world, for example. I'm just spitballing here but this is about setting up a new paradigm for why there are all these Tribes of Garou around.

                                            Instead, though, the concept they seemed to go for was to make them "stewards of nature" - but in a very broad sense. Both the people who want to preserve nature untouched by humanity and those who want to cultivate it in order to create farmland, for example, are somehow all part of this Tribe. And the storytelling-bit is way too prominent still, I feel like. This isn't supposed to be the Fianna of old, so I don't understand why the love for Galliards and "tall tales" is still there in the write-up. The only reason these elements are there is because the Hart Wardens are the W5-version of the Fianna of previous editions. The whole point of "re-imagination, not continuation" is to not be beholden to previous editions like that.

                                                    Originally posted by Ana Mizuki View Post
                                                    But I do feel the issue is that writers are writing past each other with no cohesive base.
                                                    That's why I've never been a fan of throwing out a ton of lore while replacing it with nothing - while also keeping key-elements (like 11 Tribes). Trying to explain W5 is just a constant back-and-forth of explaining why it's the fifth edition of WtA and why it isn't like any of its previous editions.

                                                    And one element, I'm still very wary of is the whole treatment of the Native American Tribes. Supposedly the principle is that all ties between human culture/ethnicities and Garou Tribes have been severed. But we've seen with Hart Wardens how there are still elements of the old Fianna in there for no other reason than this being a new version of that Tribe in previous editions. Now take that idea and imagine what could happen with the Tribes that used to be Native American ones. Old WtA certainly had its issues but this edition was supposedly all about doing better. And yet what we've learned thus far is that both the approach to changing things (which we got with the whole Fianna-thing) and a cultural sensitivity reader checking things (like the art in the GW-preview) turned out to be not very thorough. So in my mind, both the Galestalker and the Ghost Council write-ups are potential controversies to be triggered once the public gets a chance to read them. With all that we know now, how thorough do we think were the writers in distinguishing between referencing past lore of the Younger Brother and Elder Brother Tribe and referencing Native American traditions (and potentially doing so incorrectly)...?
