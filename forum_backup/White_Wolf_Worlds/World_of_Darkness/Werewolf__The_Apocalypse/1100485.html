    Rokea 2.0

    06-05-2017, 01:13 PM
    I personally love the rokea. I growed up on the northern coast of Italy, so I really want to add something more to my favorite changing breed, adding more costal-abyss modern horror.
    If you like it, feel free to use those storytelling modification. Sorry for my poor english. Hope you can enjoy anyway my ideas.
    The addictions are most around homid rokea and concepts to play them, and also I added some schools of thoughts for the squamus rokea, something that the storyteller can use to create original chronicles.
    This is the first part. I'm currently on renown,breeds and example of playing.
    Sorry for my poor english .

    Rokea


    The Rokea claim to be the first of the Changing Breeds, as Sea existed before solid land appeared on Gaia and sharks predate all land-born predators. Shark-born Rokea are effectively immortal. They can not die naturally; once they reach adulthood, their aging process stops. The only way a squamus wereshark can die is through an unnatural death.
    They tend to gather into small groups called Slews, similar in purpose and behaviour to garou packs. Much like garou have caerns, Rokea have undersea sacred places, referred to as Grottoes. Members of this species within the Beast Courts are referred to as Same-Bito.
    Weresharks communicate by means of electric signals called the Sending. They can receive these signals in all forms, but cannot send messages in Glabrus or Homid forms. They also cannot step sideways into the Umbra without use of Gifts, and have large amounts of Rage bottled up in their systems - and, unlike most Changers, are incapable of falling into a fox frenzy. Their word for frenzy is Kunmind.
    These shapeshifters also regenerate as garou do, and have furthermore the ability to regenerate in their breed form when swimming in salt water. Like garou, however, they cannot soak silver damage except while in their breed form.
    They are constantly compelled to move, and have trouble standing still even on dry land. Most Rokea are notoriously xenophobic, and very few leave the water. Their relations with the other Changing Breeds are tense at best except for homid born rokea, with one exception: they have allied themselves with the Mokolé, whom they consider closest to them between all the shapeshifters.

    Breeds:

    Homid:They are the result of the last 4 centuries of civilization. Homid rokea are born from two Kadugo (homid rokea kinfolk) or from a Kadugo and a Rokea. Homid born rokea are a growing breed in the world of darkness. Civilization itself was born on costal areas. The Americas and the mediterranean coasts are the two main places where homid rokea can be founded.Homid born rokea tend to be hot headed people, growed up in total ignorance about their destiny. Kadugo parents are often nomads by nature, as their rokea relativies. After the first change, homid rokea are often founded by betweeners or by other homid rokea. If a shark-born rokea find a homid born, the results can be umpredictable. A homid born rokea will be always aggressive and not so attractive even before his first change. After the change, an homid rokea often swims for days with the voices of spirits in his head. The slew where the homid rokea will find a place is almost always a slew made up by betweeners and other homid rokea. Even if they are humans, they will feel the voice of the Undersea calling them. This can lead to a great need of total freedom from human bounds and to a burning hatred for human actions toward the ocean and the water. Homid born rokea are often outsiders due to their nature, they tend to create outlaw like slews. An homid rokea shark breed depends on his ancestry. They are often related to the local shark breeds, by the way. An homid born rokea often walks and it's almost impossible for him to stay in the same place for more than a few hours.
    Stereotypes: surfer gangs, costal outlaws, modern pirates, docks smugglers, night hunters, beach mafia, modern pagan tribe, lagoon dark cult

    Metis: even homid born rokea can't feel attraction between them. Metis are non-existent.

    Squamus: The main stock for the rokea breed. A mating between a Rokea and a normal shark will always produce Rokea offspring. Of the many species of shark in the world, the Rokea breed with about 20%, choosing the largest and deadliest species as their mates. Rokea are usually born of makos, great whites, tiger sharks, hammerheads, blue sharks, tresher sharks, blacktip sharks and bull sharks. longimanus sharks and so on. The squamus born rokea are really xenophoic even with their homid relativies. By the way, after some recent raid on the unsea some rokea just understand the power of the Qyrl: on the coast, the danger is real and great, that's why more squamus born rokea are accepting the fact that they homid counterpart can play an important role to prevent the human pollution on the sea. In the most great urban coast the shark born rokea are often in contact with betweeners and homid rokea. Even if there is a great tension and rivalry between the two groups, the collaboration is still honest. In more wild places and non civilized areas, squamus rokea still hunt and kill possible betweeners, homid rokea and kadugos, due to their ignorance about what is happening on the unsea: the wyrm is winning.
    Stereotypes: silent killers, horrific abyss shamans, horror from the unknow, anti-human pirates, guerrilla specialists, mercyless cold-blooded killing machines, betweener avenger

    FORMS

    Homid: Also called "Long Fins" by the Rokea. The homid shapes of most Rokea, in particular the shark-born ones, are usually not aesthetically pleasing. Many are hunchbacked; their faces are often lopsided and feature crooked noses, misshapen jaws, walleyes, harelips and bulging foreheads.
    Homid born rokea can be almost ''handsome'' sometimes, but is due to their charisma than to their appaerance.
    Glabrus (Glabro): Also known as "Round Back", this shape is usually known only to land-dwelling Rokea - ocean-dwelling weresharks have little use for it. The Rokea loses all body hair and grows even uglier than her homid form. He or she doubles her muscle mass, and the back broadens as the dorsal fin begins to form.
    Gladius (Crinos): Called "Standing Jaws" by the weresharks, the Gladius is a ten-foot bipedal shark creature. Standing Jaws combines the power of a shark with the versatility of a human form. This form incites partial Delirium in humans.
    Chasmus (Hispo): The form the Rokea call "Fighting Jaws" is a prehistoric nightmare that incites full Delirium in humans. The Rokea turns into a giant version of her breed form, growing half again as long as her Squamus shape. Rokea do their underwater fighting in this shape.
    Squamus (Lupus): Also referred to as "Swimming Jaws", this form is indistinguishable from a normal shark of the Rokea's species.



    Auspicies

    Brightwater: Rokea born during bright days or full moons. Brightwaters are similar to Garou Ahroun; they are warriors and heroes. However, they often desire to be the near the surface of the sea. Homid born rokea are often also explorers and sailors in some ways: always looking for the open sea, looking for the Qyrl influence.
    Dimwater: Dimwaters are born during clouded days or at night under any moon phase except full or new. Most Rokea fall under this auspice. They are the judges and leaders of the Rokea race and create what few fetishes the Rokea use. Homid dimwaters can be cold judges and they are often the most mercyless of the rokea breed due to their understanding of human emotions. They often feel a strong bound to the coasts, keeping the balance between the sea and the unsea, dealing with criminals, humans, qyrl servants and angry shark-born traditionalists.
    Darkwater: Called the "mad ones" by their peers, Darkwaters are born under eclipses or the New Moon. They are curious, innovative, bright and daring. They enjoy diving into the frigid depths of the sea or walking on land to uncover new secrets. Homid born darkwaters can be tricksters, urban shamans explorers of the depth: they feel a great bound between their hearts and the depth of the sea, discovering new secrets and learning from old ranked weresharks.




    Rokean Law
    Kun’s Law.

    Survive: The race must survive, no matter what. The Rokea believe that there is only foolishness, not honour, in fighting a battle they can't win. No Rokea will accuse another of cowardice if they swim away from a fight that is hopeless. The only exception is sacrifice in the name of a Grotto, which is expected to be defended to the last breath. Even the homid born rokea are supposed to endure and survive. Due to their bound to the sea is almost impossible for a homid rokea to leave the sea and the coast for some kind of suicidal missions. Homid born rokea are always protecting a grotto, under the sea or near the coast, it doesn't matter: they will die for it and they will do what is right to protect it.

    Hunt: Rokea have duties as both shapechangers and sharks. They were designed to be predators of the highest order, and their actions affect the sea around them. This tenet reminds Rokea that they are warriors and predators above everything else. Homid rokea feel the urge to hunt and kill as their squamus counterpart. The difference, is that for some squamus this urge, with some kind of burning hate, lead them to hunt and kill homids or betweeners. But they are ready.

    Spawn: Rokea breed slowly but deliberately. Each wereshark is expected to breed at least once during their lives. This part of the code also obliges weresharks to teach and mentor young weresharks in the ways of the Rokea. Same for the homids, and that's why the homid race is growing.

    Swim: The weresharks consider it a duty to continue to swim, explore, and move. They need to watch for dangers threatening the Sea, and the best way to do this is to move around and keep their eyes open. Even homid born rokea slews can leave a coast to find another place. It's whitin their shark nature.
    They will always explore and swim.



    Homids and Betweeners
    Homids and betweeners are still considered rokea by spirits and remoras. Sea and Kun recognizes them as full blood rokea, that’s why they can advance in rank. During their lives in zone where they have a truce with the shark born, they still often feel to be the ‘’little children’’ of the Sea and feel as the shark born are the chosen ones by Kun. This isn’t true, and often, betweeners and homid born rokea tend to protect their grottoes in great numbers and they do not have lesser rage and aggressiveness than the traditional rokea. Homid rokea society often product some kind of local homid-tribal culture. Swamp cults, nocturne tribal practice in honor of Kun in dark lagoons and shores, bloody sacrifice and ritual passages on the coast of urban islands and so on. It seems that the homid and the betweeners have a great relationship due to the mercyless hunt that the shark born rokea practiced on them.
    Homids slews and homid/betweeners slews are no joke. Every shark-born traditionalist can confirm it. Now they are organized and they proved their might against not even only the Qyrl under the sea, but also against him on the coasts, holding old grottoes between the sea and the unsea. That’s why they gained some decent respect form the shark born.
    Homid rokea can die aging, by the way, their lives can be exceptionally long ( even 250 years if they manage to gain 7 or more dots of gnosis), by the way, no homid or squamus rokea discovered it, or know it: this is the age of the apocalypse. No homid rokea and no betweeners will die by old age.


    Shark Born
    There are two kind of sharkborns. The first type live near costal-urban area ( examples; in the open sea near Miami, Rome, Cairo, Athens, Venice and so on) and after the last 50 years of wars and mercyless fishing they have often a deal with the betweeners and the homids. They recognize that if those weresharks managed to hold grottoes and survive even alone against humans, Qyrls and squamus rokea, they deserve a place in the rokea society. They also can be old teachers and mentors for some of them. After all, homids rokea are a results of sharkborn rokea. The urge to mate is so strong that a lot of betweeners and non-betweeners, during the last centuries of civilization , mated with a lot of humans during their rites or passage or during raid on the unsea. The first kind of shark born understand it and managed to create a truce to cooperate to survive and hold grottoes. The second kind of shark born lives in the open ocean, far from the shores. Those weresharks don’t trust humans and they only know about great naval disaster and mass holocausts of sharks. They will never accept some stories about costal grottoes or the importance of holding those sacred place to prevent pollution and human influence on the sea from the coast, and they will never accept the fact that homid rokea exist. That’s mean total war. This kind of wereshark is extremely aggressive. They don’t talk with betweeners or homid, they just shift to Chasmus or Gladius to kill.

    We are the inheritors.
    We are the Pure ''-
