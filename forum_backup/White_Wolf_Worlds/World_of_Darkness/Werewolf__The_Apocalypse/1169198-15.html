        For me it was a part about saying we've had Garou with guts in the past, but also a response to the Fianna apparently standing out as the tribe to pick on such a factor. It seemed random for me that the Fianna would be the ones selected for such a stereotype. The Fianna have their share of issues as far as Garou go, but I haven't really seen them as standing out compared to most of the other tribes. Maybe I'm just missing out on certain parts? Each of the tribes have their damages, and so many of them have their camps they'd rather not talk about.

        Which brings me to 1 thing I did like about Changing Ways...which was the Metis chapter. All these years most of the tribe books I felt made it look like their tribe didn't disrespect or have issues with their metis. "Blame the parents, not the child" sort of feel (except Fianna, Fangs, and Red Talons). That always rubbed me the wrong way because if metis are essentially 2nd class citizens...shouldn't the other tribes be more open about this in their tribe books? So I liked that that Changing Ways wrote up ways that the other tribes viewed their metis so poorly, and could even mistreat them. I'll admit the Child of Gaia part threw me a little...but the rest made it feel the plight of the metis more. In the past it always felt like the authors of the tribe books just didn't want "their" tribe to be that way, so to speak.

        (On an aside, I've never been a fan of the metis concept...just took them as a part of the campain)

                                                    Ok, a bit of a tangent from the recent discussion, as I was reading Changing Ways I was, struck is too strong of a word but that's what I can come up with at the moment, by how Garou-centric it is. So I am considering writing an addendum to the book from the point of view of one of the Changing Breed, namely the Gurahl. I think it would be an interesting to work out the similarities and differences between the Breeds. For example, how do the Gurahl view the five Forms? Does regeneration work the same for all of the Changing Breeds?

                                                    I don't really intend to create any mechanics for this just to get another point of view. I'd follow the same chapter layout as Changing Ways with a chapter on the nature of a Gurahl, ursine life, and magic, obviously skipping the metis chapter and possibly the pack chapter.
