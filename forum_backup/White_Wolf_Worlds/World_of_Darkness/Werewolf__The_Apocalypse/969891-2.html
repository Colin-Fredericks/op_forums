                        Originally posted by Aya Tari View Post
                        Overpenetration is a meaningless abstraction when the round leaves a sizzling hole the size of a human head in the target ...
                        Well, to be fair, the whole White Wolf combat system is a meaningless abstraction.

                        If you want to talk about "realism" (the supposed target of this whole thread), overpenetration is indeed an issue. The only really important issue with regard to ballistic projectiles is energy deposit. If you have a projectile that massively overpenetrates the target, that's a whole butt-load of kinetic energy that gets deposited somewhere other than the target (this is one of the reasons that you don't generally see battle rifles in service anymore). In practical terms, once you get up to about .30 caliber in small arms, against man-sized targets, you begin encountering the phenomenon of decreasing returns for any comparable increase in bore diameter. Since combat in WoD uses a generally "meaningless abstraction", this little issue is simply ignored.

                                                        -----

                                                        To try to bring this back around to the thread topic, that's actually one of the issues with silver that we haven't really discussed. Besides the insane expense of 100+ silver bullets, one of the issues that would face any attempt to use current military automatic weapons is going to be weight. Ammunition gets heavy (and silver isn't exactly going to help) - which is incidentally one of the reason to favor the smaller caliber assault rifles as standard issue - and trying to lug around enough ammo for either a lot of shooting, or the tactics that are ammo intensive is going to be an issue if unit maneuverability on foot is going to be a significant tactical factor.

                                                        So this returns to focusing on things like a shotgun for silver slugs rather than getting into the issue of higher volume capable weapons and silver.
