    I'm not sure I like linking it to the Litany like this. I get how you got there from C20, but the Litany isn't really the same as Banality.

    I'd consider Renown instead. Something like, "whenever a PC gains [Rank + 1] temporary Renown for their linked Renown category, in a single scene, they also get a temporary point of Willpower/Rage/Gnosis."

    Homids would go with Honor, as Honor represents the complex learned social contracts that Homids are more used to navigating from their humanity.

    Garou-born would go with Glory, as noted they're indoctrinated the most to the warrior culture of the Garou, and easily develop a strong desire to prove themselves in the light of the negative views the Nation has of them.

    Lupus would go with Wisdom, as Garou Wisdom leans more into natural and instinctual intelligence over book learning, which the mix of wolf and human mind of the lupus tend to have a strong affinity for.

    Even then, regain Willpower, Rage, and Gnosis are also radically difficult in how hard they are to regain. Rage even risks being more of a drawback than a bonus if it drives your Rage too high.

    It might be worth sticking closer to C20 and going to the only real WtA mechanic with multiple triggers: Frenzy. Each Breed having an easier time resisting (or outright ignoring) certain Frenzy triggers might work out better as something that's easier to balance in play.

            monteparnas

            If you like the Frenzy idea, a suggestion for how to go about it:

            Homids: Growing up with a relatively frail human body, they get a +2 difficulty to Frenzy checks from feelings of confinement or helplessness, however the high value human societies put on social standing leaves them more sensitive to the perceptions of others and hits them with a -2 penalty to Frenzy checks from embarrassment and humiliation.

            Garou-born: Coping with the regular heaping on of negativity from others, they internalize the message or harden their hearts to it, gaining a +2 difficulty bonus against taunt inspired Frenzy checks, however knowing that none of their forms are safe from silver, they can't help but react stronger than others towards it and receive a -2 difficulty modifier when large quantities of silver are around.

            Lupus:Failure is a natural part of a wolf's life, sometimes prey gets away, sometimes a challenge to realign a pack's social pecking order doesn't work out, but survival requires to not dwell and move on. As such the lupus get a +2 Difficulty bonus to resist Frenzies from embarrassment or humiliation unless... they end up endangering their pack severely, as they suffer a -2 difficulty penalty when the see their packmates get injured.

                Second try:

                Each Breed has two effects, immunity to one trigger and a benefit on one aspect of Rage. I'm not sure if only one of those is enough and either way it will present two options for each for us to discuss.

                - Homid -
                Large quantities of silver in the area - Humans love the stuff and are used to deal with things they shouldn't, like poisons and allergies. A nervous human is more alert or even excited than flighty.
                Beast Within: Humans are also excellent at hiding their will to kill. A Homid doesn't suffer from the Beast Within even with high Rage.

                - Metis -
                Any strong emotion - They're taunted and mistreated since they're born. At the time of their First Change they're seething with all kinds of emotions. Without this most Metis wouldn't ever be out of frenzy.
                Losing the Wolf: Reverting to their breed won't do much to get them out of touch with Rage. A Metis never loses the Wolf.

                - Lupus -
                Embarrassment or humiliation - Wolves can sense this, but to a far lesser degree than humans, as true humiliation is a complex social construct that is second nature to humans, but a learned abstraction to the Lupus.
                Remaining Active: Be it to run for shelter or protect pack and cubs, acting out of desperation isn't strange for wolves. Lupus never Frenzy from rolls to Remain Active.

It is just a WTF moment with a recommendation to kill your character if you have a bad roll.

                            My point about Homids and silver is just that since silver isn't that dangerous to them in homid, then it doesn't really qualify for the idea that humans like to surround themselves with dangerous things. Most of the time Homids are going to be around silver in quantity, they're unlikely to be in a form where it's dangerous to them. You don't go to a fancy party with true silver flatware and serving dishes in Crinos.

                            On emotions... there's a big difference between hiding your emotions, and not being affected by them. Humans are very emotional creatures and it's not really that hard to get us to get very angry or very scared if emotions get too intense.

                            I'm not sure there's a way to make Remain Active feel right comparatively. The Beast Within and Losing the Wolf are both things you can just avoid. You can make sure your WP is significantly higher than your Rage so The Beast Within never gets out even with a few temp. Rage over normal. You can avoid Losing the Wolf by just not spending that last point. Remaining active can come up any time you take a lot of damage, and there's no way to avoid the Frenzy for anyone else.

                            As for suggestions... how did you feel about the ideas I posted above?
