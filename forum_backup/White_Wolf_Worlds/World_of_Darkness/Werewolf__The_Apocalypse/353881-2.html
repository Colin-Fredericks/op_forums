    Originally posted by CeltSPZ View Post
    I'd really like to be involved in a fan source book if you guys are interested in doing it too. I guess I could type away at stuff, but I work great with guidelines, what exactly should I include or not bother with. I think it'd be neat, plus it'd be interesting to see what we'd end up with.
    I will see if anyone else are interested to give it a go...we don't even have to put a time limit, just roll with it.

    Guideline-wise I would say:

    1) For the Tribes
    - A 2 pagers (like in the Corebook) That include: History, Appareance, Kinfolk & Territory, Tribal Totem, Character Creation, Initial Willpower, Beginning Gift, Tribal Weakness (for those who use it) and Stereotype (although this could be optional);
    - Define the Tribal Gifts for every level;
    - A 1 or 2 pagers for how to use them in a campaign and how they would change the Garou world if they are used.

    2) For Organisation
    - A few pages (something like the Beast Court and Ahadi in Changing breed) with History/Formation, Organisation, lexicon (if relevant), Mandate/Code (if any), Threats/Ally, The modern Era
    - Define the Organisation Gifts for every level;
    - Define Character Creation (if different);
    - A 1 or 2 pagers for how to use them in a campaign and how they would change the Garou world if they are used.

    Now that,s how I see it, if anyone has a different idea for guideline or would like to add an idea, I am open to suggestion.

    Edit:
    And by a few pages, I'm think maybe 10 pages or so...unless the author wish to do more.
