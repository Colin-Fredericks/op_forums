    Spider-fu

    05-22-2026, 01:23 AM
    I wanted to formalize some real-world spider tactics into combat maneuvers - mainly for the fun of it – but also because things like tackling and wrapping an opponent happened often enough for a house rule. So far my group resolved other combat situations specific to spider anatomy and abilities mostly narratively, aside from basic maneuvers such as Grapple, Bite, Claw and so on.

    However, Lilian and Pithus forms have a radically different physiology, and silk, venom, and extra limbs add to the options that few players would not want to utilize.

    Many of the specialized abilities of certain spider genera are available as Gifts (Web Sheet, Tarantula’s Kiss, Waterwalk, Trap Door, etc) but there are also basic actions a spider can apply that I feel are missing.

    Spiders are grapplers. All spiders aim to immobilize their prey to consume it, with either webs, bites, or both. Ananasi can kick and punch as well, but there are plenty maneuvers for that already. I tried to make maneuvers ‘click together’ to create more of a fighting style rather than a loose collection.

    The Shapechanging Maneuvers are more of an exploration on how the Crawlerling form could be used in a Kailindo-like way with rapid changes. The form is unique enough that it warrants a look outside of size-changing maneuvers, but the results may be a bit too T-1000 coded.


    Basic and Web Maneuvers

    Spider Hug: The most basic spider maneuver. The Ananasi propels herself forward with hydraulic pressure to her legs, then extends all limbs like a grasping hand and tries to grip around the opponent to immobilize them.
    This works much like the Tackle maneuver, but if successful puts the opponent into a grappled state. The maneuver may also be performed from relatively close since the Ananasi does not need to build up momentum, or it can be initiated as a drop from a higher position.
    Usable By: Lilian, Pithus
    Roll: Dexterity + Athletics
    Difficulty: 7
    Cost: none
    Damage: Strength B (impact)
    Actions: 1

    Envelop: Must be performed on a grappled opponent. The Ananasi tries to maintain her grapple with six limbs, while her legs wrap the opponent into a neat package. The latter can still try to escape at this point, but may already have to struggle against the binding if their Initiative is lower. Ananasi web has Strength 9 and 3 health levels. Fully enveloped opponents may suffer from asphyxiation by the next turn, and restricted movement means pure strength may be the only option of escape.
    Usable By: Lilian, Pithus
    Roll: Stamina + Brawl + 1 (to maintain the Grapple)
    Difficulty: 6 (to maintain the Grapple)
    Cost: 1 Blood Point for silk
    Damage: none
    Actions: 1

    Grapple Bite: Spiders deliberately target their fangs at soft spots, like joints, the ‘neck’, and any other gaps a prey’s armor might offer. This becomes easier as part of a grappling maneuver – especially if the Ananasi has the back of her opponent. She may perform a Bite as part of the grapple action if she has at least two extra successes on the initial Grapple roll.
    Usable By: Lilian, Pithus
    Roll: Dexterity + Brawl
    Difficulty: 5
    Cost: none
    Damage: Strength + 1 A, venom damage
    Actions: none

    Claw Down: Must be performed on a grappled opponent. The Ananasi tries to maintain her grapple with six limbs, while using her front limbs to pound down on the opponent.
    Usable By: Lilian, Pithus
    Roll: Stamina + Brawl + 1 (to maintain the Grapple)
    Difficulty: 6 (to maintain the Grapple)
    Cost: none
    Damage: Strength + 2 A
    Actions: 1

    Tripwires: While dodging attacks, the Ananasi simultaneously puts web strands into the environment that act as impromptu traps, making it more difficult for attackers to maneuver. She may also try to maneuver herself around an opponent to deliberately trip or entangle them. Subsequent successful dodges increase the difficulty of melee attacks on her by 1 each, to a maximum of 9 if the combat continues in the same area. A failed melee attack against her will result in a knockdown. Unfortunately, these demerits may also apply to unwary allies fighting alongside her.
    Usable By: Lilian, Pithus, Homid with Spinnerets
    Roll: as usual Dodge roll
    Difficulty: as usual Dodge roll
    Cost: 1 Blood Point per scene
    Damage: none
    Actions: 1

    Web Weapon: The Ananasi can employ small measures of her web as simple weapons. While these usually do not do much direct damage, the surprise may be enough to literally trip an opponent up. Such improvised weapons may be a small tangle net, a garotte, or a strand of adhesive web slung like a whip or bola.
    Usable By: Lilian, Pithus, Homid with Spinnerets
    Roll: Dexterity + Melee
    Difficulty: 7
    Cost: none
    Damage: special
    Actions: 1

    Riposte: If the Ananasi’s current form has more limbs than an attacker and her Initiative is higher, she may delay her action to deflect a melee attack and counter with -2 decreased difficulty.
    To be able to deflect an attack, it must not employ a weapon that out-ranges her leg (or arm/weapon) length.
    Usable By: Lilian, Pithus
    Roll: Dexterity + Brawl (or Melee) to deflect
    Difficulty: 7 to deflect, -2 on action
    Cost: none
    Damage: as chosen counter attack action
    Actions: 1

    Kick Off: The Anasasi may use her hydraulics to quickly disengage or escape a grapple by pushing all her limbs against an opponent. The main goal is to break off an attack and gain distance, but the disengagement might knock down or hurt the opponent.
    This is basically like a single-action use of the Hydraulic Strength Gift, but much more of an escape option than an attack.
    Usable By: Lilian, Pithus
    Roll: Dexterity + Brawl + 2
    Difficulty: 6 to disengage, contested roll to escape
    Cost: 1 Blood Point
    Damage: Strength + 2 B
    Actions: 1


    Shapeshifting Maneuvers

    All shapeshifting maneuvers assume a quick change into Crawlerling form and back to the previous form. Since the reflexive change requires a Blood Point, these can not be performed if the Ananasi has already spent blood this round, unless she uses the Blood Pump Gift.

    Crawlerling Dodge: If the Ananasi has a higher Initiative than her opponent, she may anticipate an incoming melee strike and dodge the damage completely by delaying her action and briefly dispersing into Crawlerlings.
    This maneuver can also be used when receiving a hit at lower Initiative to lessen the impact, downgrading the damage type by one category (to a minimum of Bashing damage). This uses the turn action of the Ananasi.
    The Ananasi may combo into one of the other shapeshifting maneuvers from Crawlerling form without the additional Blood Point cost if the roll had at least two extra successes.
    Usable By: Lilian, Pithus, Homid
    Roll: Dexterity + Athletics
    Difficulty: as usual Dodge roll
    Cost: 1 Blood Point
    Damage: none
    Actions: 1

    Reverse Hold: Unless her opponent has found a way to hold onto a swarm of spiders, the Ananasi can automatically escape a grapple or other restraint, trying to reverse the situation by changing back in a position to grapple the opponent herself.
    While escape is automatic, a successful roll is necessary for a reversal.
    Usable By: Lilian, Pithus, Homid
    Roll: Dexterity + Brawl
    Difficulty: 7
    Cost: 1 Blood Point
    Damage: none
    Actions: 1

    Engulf: as per CB20

    Blink: The Ananasi uses her momentary Crawlerling form to briefly crawl sideways and reposition herself for a flank attack or backstab.
    Usable By: Lilian, Pithus, Homid
    Roll: Gnosis
    Difficulty: Gauntlet (Web) + 1
    Cost: 1 Blood Point
    Damage: none
    Actions: 1

    Vex: Similar to Engulf, but aims for sabotage instead of brute force. The Ananasi delivers a blow or kick that deposits a bunch of Crawlerlings on the opponent with a partial transformation, which then attack weak spots like eyes, fingers, mouth, ears and so on. The damage is negligible, but the bites still hurt and a dozen spiders in your clothes (or body orifices) is still distracting.
    The Ananasi can use this to Disarm or Blind, or simply create an opening.
    Usable By: Lilian, Pithus, Homid
    Roll: Dexterity + Brawl
    Difficulty: 7
    Cost: 1 Blood Point
    Damage: Strength B (initial hit), special
    Actions: 1


    If you spot any bugs I built in, or think I missed something, let me know.
