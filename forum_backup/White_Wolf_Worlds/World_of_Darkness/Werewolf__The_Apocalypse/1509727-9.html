Bitter, hateful people, who tried to make them into the 'perfect son', a role they could never fulfil. And so they run away from their extensive tightknit Garou family to join a pack of outcasts their family would despise.

    The Metis Child of Gaia who was raised by Kinfolk. Who knows his parents left him to be raised by supportive and kind guardians, but will never know who they were or why they broke the Litany. For love? For lust? Because trauma brought them together?

                The reason why the Garou born were needed for the old game was to make unions between the Garou taboo and problematic in the most heavy handed way possible. They knew people were gonna ignore it (and boy they did!). However, the design decision for that was not to create a subgroup of broken people who need to do more to gain the same acceptance others do, it was there to ensure the Garou were bonded with humans and wolves and that they had to care for both those groups and understand both those groups to be good at what they do. This is why kinfolk are crucial to the game and why you cannot treat them as shit on principle (not that some won't, but that's on them). As a result, familial units grew into septs who grew into clans who grew into Tribes creating the huge sprawling tribes that we got in Werewolf.

                Without this requirement for the big bonded families, you don't get the Nation nor do you get Werewolves tied into human and wolf concerns. You get a hidden race of superior beings only consorting with their own and not bothering to actually understand what they need to fight for!

                Ironically, many problems of the nation come from them leaning way too much into their wolf or human natures instead of actually balancing the two. They also come from the fact that many tribes have put their own first over others. Yet now we don't have a Nation built through human concerns and like looking out for like, for now the totems are ultimate arbiters of membership and creed, rather then the cultures that brought up the Nation as a convention to stop killing each other over stupid shit.


                So when they tell me all that "breeding stuff" was horrible and wrong, the only conclusion I can have is that you either didn't read the books or missed the bloody point.
