It's not creating a magical connection between "pants" and "shirts," it's saying to allow some flexibility on what counts as an "item" for the rules of the Rite in regards to how many you can carry around. The point is to not require Gnosis 7 to have two socks, two shoes, pants, underwear, and a shirt when you shift. Not that once you dedicate a set of clothing, all clothing becomes interchangeable.

                      W20 uses ammo boxes as another example. It says when you dedicate the ammo box, you can count all the bullets in it as dedicated as well without doing each individual bullet. It never says the ammo box automatically dedicates new bullets added to it.
