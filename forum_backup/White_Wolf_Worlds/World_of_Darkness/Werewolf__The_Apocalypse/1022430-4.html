                Of course, which is why Psychic Numina are probably preferred over Rites by Kinfolk. When it comes to certain Psychic Invisibility 3+, Kinfolk can do some amazing things because (unlike many other forms of invisibility in cWoD) the invisibility does not fade during combat (and it is effective against supernatural means of detection as well, so even Kindred with high levels of Auspex might not detect the Kinfolk). With Animal Psychics 1+, the Psychic Invisibility even extends to animals, meaning that Kinfolk with Animal Psychics and Psychic Invisibility can be very capable assassins, saboteurs, spies, etc. In addition, most Psychic Numina do not require you to spend points of Willpower in Sorcerer (Revised), so they can be used with abandon.
