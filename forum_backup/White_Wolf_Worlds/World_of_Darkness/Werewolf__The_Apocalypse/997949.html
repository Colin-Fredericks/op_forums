    Good, Evil, and Perspectives

    11-03-2016, 07:27 PM
    Has anyone noticed werewolf doesn't give a lot of perspectives beyond the Garou/Fera gaian perspective? For instance we don't really get to see things how a weaver follower would see the world at large.

    Couldn't a BSD see the balance wyrm as a more purifying force, while looking at the wyld as a force of unrestrained, evil corruption and gaia as a neutral/uncaring mother that wishes for her own survival and nothing more.

    While they wouldn't necessarily be correct, it would mix things up to have things going on in the background that the garou are ignorant of and maybe make things more grey vs grey rather than grey vs pitch black.

              Some BSDs might talk a lot about the Weaver's role in imprisoning the Wyrm and making the world worse, and pontificate about how the world needs the Wyrm to be free. The thing is, they do this, and then do horrible atrocities for their own twisted amusement and self-gratification. Enlightenment does not drive them, or factor into their actions in any way; it's 100% being a total asshole. As for the Weave's point of view, it's probably pretty well covered by the principles espoused by the OneSong and conversion of drones.

              The parts which Garou are ignorant about are in deeply-detailed specifics, like the Opal Prison not actually housing what they think it does or the Nagah being dead, or at the very least, not Gaia's punishment crew, and in the workings of other games' settings. The latter don't really matter, though, because absent reliance on some deep details that are kept from them, the Garou and Fera are entirely correct about the importance of their existence and roles in the cosmos. The grey vs. grey is in how they interact with each other, not their enemies, as different tribes and changers have different ideas about what constitutes objectionable action, beyond the shit that BSDs do. Ratkin, for example, exist to bring humanity down to more reasonable numbers, and they do so by every means but direct action (or at least used to), such as diseases, stealing food, or sabotage of key industries that would hurt the most. Garou find such methods to be highly distasteful and cruel, on account of how their culture is an honor-driven warrior culture, but they have no real sanction for this, and their massacres of the wererats in ages past are well known for being a mistake..

                                                          Corruption is different than disease and decay though. Disease and decay are biological or physical processes while Corruption is a mental or spiritual process. By definition, Corruption is unnatural while disease and decay are natural.

                                                          For example, death is a natural process and, in order for new life the flourish, old life must die. If accidents or violence do not cause death, then disease and decay will likely cause death (parasites and toxins usually weaken an individual and are rarely direct causes of death in nature). The Wyld, by creating new life in the form of diseases and parasites, promotes the natural cycle. The Wyld also creates fungi, which use decay to remove corpses, which also promotes the natural cycle.

                                                          However, immortality is a perversion of nature, it is old life refusing to make room for new life, and represents Corruption. Since the Wyld can only create, immortality can only be achieved by appealing to the Weaver (by strengthening the order with the body) or the Wyrm (by redirecting destruction from the body). In this case, the source of Corruption is not the Wyld but, instead, the Weaver or the Wyrm.
