    Crossover Query: Vampire Kiss on a Werewolf

    07-31-2015, 12:23 PM
    What would be the effect of a vampire kiss on a Werewolf? It's very effective on mortals, and even on other vampires (need to roll instinct/self-control difficulty 8). Any idea how this would work on werewolves?

          Can't find anything to contradict the Dark Ages reference, except that it is also mentioned that vampires generally aren't going to get the chance to pull that particular stunt if the werewolf's pack is anywhere nearby -- in the sense that the Kiss is difficult to accomplish with a pack of pissed-off werewolves trying to tear you limb from limb.

          On a slight aside: remember that ghouling a werewolf is next to impossible as they're (generally) violently allergic to Vitae, and will begin projectile-vomiting any that they ingest -- and although not specified, the way it's written tends to imply a Frenzy is a possible outcome. The optional rule for determining if the werewolf in question is one of the few that can ingest Vitae is as follows: Roll Gnosis vs. difficulty 3. A failure or botch indicates that the werewolf may consume Vitae without ill-effect (and it is hinted in various places that he may use Vitae to gain advantages from it as any other Ghoul would).

          Actually Embracing a werewolf is rather more difficult (and potentially dangerous for the vampire). The werewolf (or really anyone with Gnosis) may roll Gnosis to resist at difficulty 6. A success indicates the werewolf dies peacefully and without pain. A failure indicates he dies violently and in agony. A botch results in a vampiric werewolf (an "Abomination"). A Willpower point may be spent for an automatic success.

          Other werewolf/vampire trivia: A werewolf has 20 points of blood to feed from rather than the 10 for an ordinary human, and this goes to 25 in Crinos (and possibly Hispo) form. A vampire consuming werewolf blood has an increased chance of frenzy.

                              The Pure Breed blood bonus is strictly something that VTM projects onto crossover. It's in the Vampire ST Handbook, but not the one for Werewolf, because, as stated, it's incredibly damned bizarre and has no grounding, rhyme or reason. As for the extra blood, that apparently comes from mystical power, and actually seems to insulate the Garou and Fera from having too much blood taken; most systems seem to have the Changers taking half the damage that a human would, such as in reference to the Thaumaturgy powers such as Theft of Vitae or Cauldron of Blood.

                              The vitae allergy can be found on page 197 of the Revised Werewolf ST Handbook.
