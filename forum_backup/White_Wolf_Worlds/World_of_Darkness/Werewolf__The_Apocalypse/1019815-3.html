            Well, I'm half-convinced on the topic of Kinfolk.

            That aside, I want to add two things to the topic of Impergium.

            First, it isn't the problem. The unbalanced is older than Humanity. The enemy is the Wyrm, maybe the Weaver. They don't need humanity to be a problem, and genocide will only serve to make matters worse on the opinions of most Garou. It isn't, indeed, an unanimous opinion, and a lot of Garou want the Impergium back for a lot of reasons, that included. But humanity, though strongly implied as making everything worse, isn't the problem. Also, we can be even a solution. The Glass Walkers are fond of recognizing human development as a next stage on natural evolution, and they can be right. Even the others find Human Kin far more useful than simple wolves, and even non-Kin can help the cause if properly educated.

            Second, don't overestimate the "we're not humans" side. Yes, they're not humans, not more than they are wolves. But they are sentient beings with feelings, and most grew-up as humans. First, it means that most still see themselves more as humans, as most Lupus still see themselves as wolves. A common wolf won't give a f**k if you kill a wolf that's not of its pack, but the Lupus Garou are usually very sensible with wolf suffering. They are not wolves, but as sentient beings they feel kinship with wolves. A lot of Garou feel kinship with humans. All of this also means they have personal emotion-based moral standards, as we have. Some people think the world would be better off without wolves, but a lot of completely human beings find the idea of hunting a wolf abhorrent. Most people don't like the idea of animals suffering when they don't need to, and some change habits completely to not benefit themselves from animal death or suffering in any sense. It isn't a thing we do because we're animals, its a thing we do because were sensible. Garou also are, humans or not.

            And third, its too late. Humanity can be individually weak, but there are reasons to fear a modern response to the Impergium. Just think of what would happen if the Pentagon found a big Caern and decided that this war went far enough. No Garou would be able to even know of the nuclear warhead fast enough, and probably it won't be the only one. A Tsar-Bomb in the vicinity of sacred places can harm the Garou to an extend very few things the Wyrm did until now can even compare.
