                        I would think that it would be impossible to surmount 100+ points of Paradox, considering that even Archmages would die with anything above 50 points of Paradox (Archmages can only use Prime 9 to expel Paradox after they get their Paradox). While some Mage games tend to devolve into such munchkinism, this is the WtA forum, so we will respect WtA Lore. In a WtA game, trying to rewrite the prohibitions of Gaia with True Magick is a good way to end up very dead.

                        A more interesting solution for the infertility of the Metis would be for the Metis to go to the Court of the Moon and directly petition Luna for relief. I imagine that Luna would be willing to remove the infertility of the Metis by transforming him into Human Kinfolk under two conditions. First, the Metis would have to succeed on a ridiculously difficult quest. Second, the Metis would have to convince his lady love to take on the burden of being the Garou (transforming her from a mortal into a Homid Garou). Since Luna would know that his lady love was a Mage, she would be implicitly asking for the sacrifice of the Avatar of his lady love as the price of the transformation.

                        If the Mage is willing to make the sacrifice and the Metis succeeds on his quest, Luna would transform the Metis into Human Kinfolk, the Mage into a Homid Garou, and the Mage's Avatar into dinner. I would turn the Mage's Mage traits and the Garou's Garou traits into freebie points, sacrifice half of them (rounded up) to fuel the transformation, and allow each character to spend the remaining freebie points on traits relevant to their condition (Human Kinfolk Traits for the former Metis and Homid Garou Traits for the former Mage). Once the transformation was complete, the two lovers could have children and their story would become part of the Lore of the Garou.
