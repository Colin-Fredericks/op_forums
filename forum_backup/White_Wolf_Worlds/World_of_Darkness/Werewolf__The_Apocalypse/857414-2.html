            Levy moral is typically poor when looking at a Dark Ages army, these are essentially farmers and lower tradesmen forced to take up arms with little to no training beyond the very basics (this is why they use spears). Professional soldiers (including knights) are far rarer and will typically hold to a higher WP standard and then you have things like the Crusading Orders which will have as an average a higher WP and probably True Faith too.

            Lots of armies spent lots of time running away.

            However, it would be reasonable to boost the "effective" WP of mortals involved if they are in a situation with something to fight FOR. Defending home or family can boost resolve to face terrors. Also it's a perfect situation to use the Leadership ability, for the leader of these forces to strengthen their soldiers Will and Resolve to face down the bloody horrors of battle (especially vs monsters).
