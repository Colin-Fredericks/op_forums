                    Not saying anything after this point doesn't need some tweaking and review, just my thoughts.

                    Anura Form: Strength -1, Dexterity +2 (+3 in Water), Manipulation -3, Perception +3. Can swim at 1-and-a-half speed.

                    Amphibious: Anurana are able to hold their breath twice as long as other people can while in Homid and and Anuran and they are fully capable of breathing water as easily as air while in Anura and Dagon.

                    Senses: Frogs have amazingly acute senses including a Jacob's Organ between their nostrils and a Lateral Line around their head, eyes, body and neck. For every form removed from Homid the Anurana may reduce the difficulty on Perception rolls by -1; Hence while in Anuran perception rolls are at -1 while in Anura they are at -3. While in Dagon or Anura their sensitivity to vibration grants them automatically benefits from the effect of the lupus Gift: Scent for Sight.

                    Night Vision: On top of their already amazing sensory abilities, Anurana have excellent night vision and depth perception and can see in anything short of absolute darkness. In game terms this means that by spending 1 point of Gnosis they can use the dim illumination of the Umbra to make out lines in the darkness. This effect last for the duration of a scene but can be extended using gifts.

                    Kin Species: By diversifying their animal kin, the Anurana have begun to incorporate new traits into their breed. For simplicity sake storyteller's may wish to all Anurana have similar traits regardless of their "stock". Those seeking more detail may wish to assign particular advantages according to the Anurana's actual stock. Examples include:
                    ~Flying Frogs: The webbing between the characters fingers and toes allows them to glide at a 45 degree angle from their point of take off.
                    ~Poison Dart Frog: The character's flesh becomes incredibly toxic when enraged causing paralysis to anyone who touches or ingests it. See Mokole attribute: Contact Venom.
                    ~Tree Frog: See Mokole attribute: Gecko Pads.
                    ~Wood Frog: In temperatures that would freeze you to death your body shuts down. You no longer breath, your heart stops beating, your body secretes a chemical the preserves you until such time as you are able to thawed out. Once thawed, your body returns to normal function as if nothing happened.
                    ~Casque-head Frog: As Poison Dart Frog, but your body is covered in spines that when touched, cause incredible pain for the duration instead of paralysis. During this period of time wound penalties are treated as double.

                                                Probably shouldn't be posting at the ass-end of the night, but...

                                                Am I the only person who finds the idea of Frog-shifters kinda... not scary or interesting? I think more of a Lovecraftian Deep One vibe is cooler and more intimidating than 'we are shifters that literally turn into frogs.' Not much creepy or threatening about Kermit. Hopping, blinking and swallowing bugs by exerting pressure with their darn eyeballs. That is what frogs can do. Compared to wolves or dinosaurs or bears or even rats, frogs seem really really underwhelming in the adversary department.

                                                Large hellbenders or even prehistoric salamanders like Metoposaurus might make for a better animal form. Alternatively, since the Anurana are unholy Wyrm-spawned monsters (despite gaining totemic legitimacy), their Lupus equivalent could be a screwed up mish-mash of amphibian beasts. It fits with their themes of pollution and corruption, as in the wild frogs are already susceptible to mutagens in their environment- and blind frog things with gills, or writhing multi-bodied caecilians would be pleasantly creepifying.

                                                Also I don't like the idea of 'Lupus' breed Anurana from a thematic standpoint- keeping them as Metis or Homid forces them to prey on kinfolk and drives them into opposition with the Garou and other Fera. If they can just spawn with the frogs and toads, it undermines what could otherwise be a visceral threat.
