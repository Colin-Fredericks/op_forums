    Repercussions of Chiminage not honored

    02-10-2016, 08:35 AM
    In a TT game I have a group of Garou that a few months ago turned to summoned Tribal Totems for aid in act of desperation (but for the betterment of Gaia), they each promised a lot in return should they have lived through the events. They did and now I have a couple of players that haven't kept up their side of the deal with the Spirits. Normally, they'd have to have something to give as chiminage first or there'd be a quest to gain this kind of favour in my games, but as above, was in a moment of desperation and they promised (tied themselves) to great acts in appreciation.

    I've pretty much got in mind what I need for a couple of them in the penance they must pay to their tribal totems as a warning to them and others never to short change a powerful spirit (Stag - a savagely brutal beating from a herd of spirits aligned with stag, maybe even a hunt. A month of no memory and no Knowledge's of anything for the Uktena after being dragged into darkness and then spat back out by his Totem). As well the Rite of Contrition. But I want there to be an actual cost or lesson, other than it appearing as if its just a rite and a roll to see how well it goes.

    But there is one Totem i'm having a bit of difficulty with, so please do feel free to give ideas (or even other ideas for the cost of not paying chiminage to powerful spirits), Unicorn. I guess it's the whole Peace and Mercy aspects of the Totem, especially to one of its own children.

    I'm currently thinking of having it come forward and just explain it forgives the character, but that other may not and have other spirits shun the character for they can not abide how Unicorn has been treated. Mirroring a bit of the CoGs own problems with society, others not being as accepting. I thought this was quite good as the character is a Theurge also.

    Any ideas, thoughts? any things you have done or seen others do towards Garou that fail to uphold their deals with spirits?

      First, a question. What was the wording of the chiminage? I would assume that the entire Pack would be responsible for paying back the debt unless otherwise stated.

      Second, Black Unicorn exists. If you piss off Unicorn badly enough, his avatar of war will run you through. Bonus: since Unicorn is the one who teaches Mother's Touch, the Gift won't be able to heal it.

      Third, all Spirits will be giving these Garou the Stinkeye for failing to deliver in a timely manner. Only the Pack Totem is likely to give them the time of day until all the debt is paid, and even afterward, getting Spirits to talk to them would be like pulling teeth. Spirit Notoriety is a very real thing and it sounds like these Garou have earned it. NOTE: This may necessitate an OOC conversation so that the players are on the same page as you about the punishment. They might feel like you're punishing the players for being unable to solve a problem you the GM created, rather than the game world punishing the PCs for failing to live up to their promises.

                          Since Unicorn is all about healing, the Theurge seems to have Mothers Touch (most have anyway), and you want to punish the character and not anyone undeserving around him, I'd propose the following: untill he has made ammends, mothers touch and any other healing gifts just don't affect him positively. He can still cast it on anyone in need, but he himself will remain unhealed.

                          If the offence was really, really great, you could even extend this into "unicorns curse", to strip him of all his supernatural healing including his regeneration, till he pays the spirit back. He would still heal naturaly of course, but at the same rate as kinfolk/mortals (use with caution and only if the offence was truely great).

                          That way, you can substantially punish the Garou, without withholding any help he may give to the needy.

                                      I would not just make this about letting down a single totem spirit or it's brood... spirits are the primary allies of the Garou, and to piss them off or treat them with disrespect is courting disaster.
