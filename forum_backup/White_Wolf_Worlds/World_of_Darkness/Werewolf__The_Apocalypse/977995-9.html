    Why should they view it as different? If the Garou are born according to Luna's artifice - itself a constantly changing, evolving thing - and Artemis has seen fit to consider gender non-fixed, why should they view it as unnatural? For that matter, why should they view surgery or hormone treatment (neither of which is needed to transition between genders) as wyrm-tainted? Do they view all surgery as wyrm-tainted? All medicine?

    Pegasus demanded not only mercy for male offspring, but a thumbs up to join the ranks in the form of the male metis. Between Pegasus and Artemis, we see a tribe who follow - even if they don't like it sometimes - the dictates of their totem quite carefully, who hold dearest an aspect of Luna who saw fit to visit her divine changing nature upon a mere mortal, and who already make allowances for persons who don't fit the strict reading of 'ladies only'.

    So again. Why should the tribe of Artemis not follow the way of Artemis in this matter?
