                                        The Judges don't care if you have a gay relationship so long as you produce children with Kinfolk as that's a Duty and so long as no metis are produced. The Shadowlords tribe has a big history of this kind of behaviour and also being very Understanding to Garou of other tribes who find themselves having these issues. It's one of their manipulation power bases.

                                        As for marriages. It varies massively from Tribe to Tribe and Sept to Sept even but in most cases there is a Rite involved, often one of the fertility Rites as Garou and Kinfolk don't tend to get Wed as such, they become Mated, they take a Mate (or Mates for that matter). The institutionalised wedding process of the mortal world (especially through the major religious structures) is something you're not overly likely to see. For one they're not spiritually gnostic Gaia centric and two imagine the stresses of a normal wedding planning and ceremony, now add in Rage, bound to end badly. What you'll see more of instead are the 'pagan' tribal cultural roots echoed, which were typically a lot more relaxed affairs (or with better options in their structuring for Rage monsters to not explode). Again it varies from Tribe to Tribe.

                                                        I am not quite sure if many Tribes (with the possible exceptons of the Silver Fangs or Wendigo) really care about marriage right now. They are rare, something like 15,000 globally, so they are probably focused on reproduction over relationships. Garou are probably being encouraged to have lots of unprotected sex, to maximize the probability of pregnancy, since they do not have to worry about disease.

                                                        Since average human fertility gives a 2.5% of pregnancy from each unprotected sexual act, Garou need to have a fair amount of sex to get a child. In the USA, around 75% of unattached females are on birth contro. Of those not of birth control, 75% will have an abortion over an unwanted child.

                                                        Because female Garou are not on birth control and want any resulting child, they are 16 times more likely to end up with a child from an unprotected sexual act than a male Garou, but they have to be willing to breed. Garou society would probably see a lesbian marriage as a sign of reduced willingness of both partners to reproduce. I think they would at least need to select a couple of male Kinfolk to help them reproduce before they would be accepted.
