                      Forgive me but by reading Muammar's quotes and putting them into the context of what we know about W5 and what Sambrano says, I see enormous creative differences more than consistent anti-Indigenialism

                      Besides the very shallow initial idea of killing off Younger Brother, which is really just facepalmish level of stupid, what is implied in their exchanges is that disconnecting tribes from real world ethnicities was already part of the original setting guidelines, a change Sambrano wasn't in line with.

                      As he wasn't in line with using the Fenrir as an in-setting villain, in line with the new creative direction wanted to present political extremism as a real threat to societies.

                      At one point, he and Muammar are basically saying to each other "you are not bringing meaningful ideas to the setting, only your personal preferences": Muammar wanted no cultural tribes and a Werewolf villain, Sambrano wanted two really Indigenous tribes and a heroic Garou Nation.

                      It's basically the rift we see in Reddit and forum discussions, but within the creative team. And if relations between them were so deteriorated, I'm fairly sure that bringing everything under Justin Achilli was basically what was needed to get the product moving and breaking the deadlock.

                      I'm glad Sambrano's proposals on Indigenous names have mostly been kept, and hope he gets credited for his writing work.

                          Two interesting tidbits that may or may not mean anything in relation to this:

                          At one point in his post Sambrano says:
                          While doing so, he also decided that it was appropriate to refer to this entirely Native American tribe by the word “savages” a slur that has been specifically used to dehumanize Native Americans, and then mocked my rewrite that focused on presenting them as stewards of the land using Indigenous methodologies and praxes, instead of the “savage” racist stereotypes they were presented as in previous editions. Further, as in the above quote, even after it was communicated that the use of this term was problematic, he kept doubling-down to use it to refer to the Tribe.
                          The reason this section caught my attention is that during the interview with Outstar in October of last year Justin Achilli talked about the cultural sensitivity reader they would employ to proofread their work. And the example he gave was that W5 wouldn't use the word "savage" at any point in the book.

                          The other thing is about Justin Achilli leaving this January. Just think about it: This post is mostly about Karim Muammar, the editor of the WOD-team. Justin Achilli left after writing was done but before any editing took place. Might mean something or might not...
