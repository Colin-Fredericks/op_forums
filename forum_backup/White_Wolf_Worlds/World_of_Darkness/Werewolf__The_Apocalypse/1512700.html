    Core Themes of the Tribes for a better W5

    05-25-2023, 05:23 AM
    So this is just a self-indulgent thought process of HOW to potentially handle the tribes of the Nation better as a reboot without making them overlap with each other.

    This thread is NOT about how bad W5 is, this is about trying to squeeze the tribes into a generalist concept that stands out.


    Black Furies: The sacred feminity without shackles of progress.
    Bone Gnawers: Survival in what others ignore.
    Children of Gaia: Healing and Diplomacy.
    Fianna: Family in its worst and best aspects.
    Fenrir: War and the strength of will.
    Glass Walkers: Utopian dreams and mimicry of humanity.
    Red Talons: Self-denial and call to ignorance.
    Shadow Lords: Staining your hands so others do not have to.
    Silent Striders: Haunted and seeking redemption.
    Silver Fangs: Entitlement from bloodlines and birth status
    Stargazers: Asceticism and contemplation of meaning.
    Older Brother: Curiosity killed the cat and satisfaction brought it back.
    Younger Brother: Defiance in the face of erasure.

    Black Spiral Dancers: Freedom in being a monster.
    Ronin: Refusal of tradition and 'how it has always been done.'

    Note, these are how -I- see the tribes at their core.

Especially in cases like Red Talons or lupus, both are made into these massive hurdles to play in the fanbase when they really don't require that.

                              To offer an example of what I mean, the Red Talons have often been seen as unplayable and near impossible to play well in a larger game (pbp, chat,etc.) Despite the common stereotype of the Red Talons as the 'wolf tribe', that isn't actually the core of the tribe. Unlike the Glass Walkers who are human-focused mostly because humanity fascinates them over choosing to be less wolf, Red Talons are willingly choosing to deny any humanity in themselves and exist as true wolves.

                              But like there is no 'True Scotchman', there is no one way a wolf can be. A wolf does not consider itself on how it SHOULD act or what is proper behaviour for wolves. For every family-pack, there are the large communities in Yellowstone with MULTIPLE breeding females. As well, atavism is not something unknown to humans. There are plenty of people who choose to give up on the trappings of civilization. What the Red Talons look for is a stereotype.

                              One that they themselves cannot live by, as a tribe is not a pack. Red Talons have writing, they have specific rites and even their own takes which laws of the Litany are valid. They have as much human culture as any other tribe, they just deny it.

                              Hence, Denial and Call for Ignorance. When playing a Talon, you are playing someone who has been taught to see any other way of living as sinful and wrong and something they MUST deny in themselves.                                                           I do think that it’s useful to have an image of the archetypes in your mind and so I’ll share some of my own off the top of my head, both to help me clarify them and to participate in this.

                                                          Black Furies - Warriors of the Feminine Spirit | The Avengers of the victims of the Patriarch (Womenkind, Children, The Wilds; certain culturally relevant categories of men and others depending on camp) & Guardians of the Sacred Wilderness & Primordial Taboos; associated imo with the ‘barbarian’ fringes and mysteries of the Hellenic World such as the Scythians, Cretan, Sicilian, & Eleusian Mystery Cults, Amazons, etc.
                                                          (in my opinion an under-utilized quality of the Black Furies comes from their association with the Hellenic ‘Furies’; they are tied to primordial-vengeance spirits and so when those primeval taboos are broken such as the patriarchal domination of the feminine, matricide, kinslaying, violation of sacred places, etc. they rise to avenge those violations)

                                                          Bone Gnawers - The Outcast Wolves | The Bone Gnawers are the survivors and the flotsam of Garou History; the Omegas, Garou-Born, Cowards, Dishonored, and Bastards of the Nation were driven from their ancestral septs and those who struggled to survive would be brought together by Rat, Jackal, Hyena, Coyote, Crow, and the other Scavenger spirits | While Bone Gnawers would later be invoked by other parts of the Nation and compromises be made the Bone Gnawers would settle into agnostic camps for survival; the best of them becoming champions of the downtrodden and the forgotten places, haunting them alongside the scavengers and spirits of ‘low cunning’
                                                          (I view a central conflict of the Bone Gnawers being between those elders who have managed to piece together an ideology under Rat and the Ronin & Lone Wolves on the other end who have not technically received even Rat’s blessing. Most Bone Gnawers sit somewhere in the middle; minding-their-own, working for others at some price, and primarily fighting when their own trod out turfs are disturbed by invasion/taint)

                                                          Children of Gaia - The (self-appointed) Vanguard of the Sacred Mother | The Children of Gaia are bound by a vision of sacred unity and harmony which is all-inclusive (man, beast, Garou, spirit, etc.); they seek to bring about a restoration of that sacred and primordial state by whatever means available to them | that does mean that some of them are the hippie peaceniks of legend whereas others are as much a self-appointed leadership tribe as the Silver Fangs or Shadow Lords and others try as much as the Glass Walkers, Red Talons, or what have you to integrate themselves with the world of men, beasts, spirits, etc.
                                                          (A good way of differentiating the Children of Gaia and Bone Gnawers is this in my mind: the Bone-Gnawers would accept a Garou-Born because they are outcasts and proven survivors on the fringes of the Nation, the Children of Gaia would accept a Garou-Born because their must be a place for them in the Mother’s Body and if the Garou-Born is able to foster their own vision of unity; the Children of Gaia do have their own Pure Breed from Unicorn but will accept other Garou because they are dedicated to a vision of a world without tribe - although whether that means a single tribe under Unicorn, Wolf, or Gaia is a matter of debate)

                                                          Fianna - The Warriors of Stag represent Wild Virility, Passion, Fertility, Family, & Pride (What they would call the Great Song of Life) | The Fianna are defined by the sacred passions of life which for them became inevitably tied with what their human kin were doing - which is how traditions of oral legend and crafting and cooking become part of their oeuvre; their ties with the Fair Folk come from this same great wellspring of Life and Passion which drives the Fae | The Fianna are associated with the Celtic tradition but their general quality is one defined by Stag, Passion, Life (Fertility, Romance, Singing/Dancing, Fighting, Family, Jealousy, Rivalry, Pride, Eating/Drinking, Screwing, Hospitality, etc.)

                                                          Get of Fenris - The War Cult amongst A Cult of Warriors | The Get of Fenris are united by a hardened dedication to the Death-Wolf/War-Wolf tradition above all (and they may honestly resemble the original Garou the most) | The Fenrir are the Koryos Warrior-Wolf Cults; chewing psychoactive roots, entering battle frenzies, willing to use whatever tactics might be necessary to harass and kill their targets, and in many ways living outside of traditional life and community | The Fenrir have ties to Scandinavian traditions but like the Black Furies (and Fiannia to a lesser extent) they represent a primordial and monstrous nature-loving association which the groups of humans who wrote things down associated with malevolent outsiders (giants, mystery cults, warrior dead, monster wolves, etc.)
                                                          (Here’s how I would distinguish the Fianna & Fenrir, who are something of a complimentary pair | The Fianna are Garou who live inside the camp - heavily involved with their kin, heavily involved with human (and wolf) life, heavily invested in the community festivities and the rhythms of living in the Song of Stag | The Fenrir are Garou who live outside the camp - their kinfolk maintain regular life semi-independently with semi-regular visits from the garou element, the Fenrir live amongst themselves in a perpetual war-camp, deeply invested in the secret mysteries of the Death-Wolf and ever-striving to prove themselves in the blood of their tribal enemies)

                                                          Glass Walkers - The Friends of (so-called) Civilization | These Garou were the first to be impressed by the gifts of the Weaver to humanity and unlike the other tribes saw those powers and those secrets as the future, leaving them to abandon the old ways in favor of the ones which came alongside humanity, society, and the Weaver - so long as these tools could ostensibly be used in favor of the Mother and they kept closer to the hum of human life than the cold embrace of the Weaver
                                                          (In the same but opposite way that the Red Talons are liable to fall to Triatic taint, the Glass Walkers are. They left behind the old ways in favor of the sacred rites developed in communion in human society and the Weaver. The Glass Walkers who remain closest to Gaia are the ones who best connect humanity’s gifts to their primal roots in the human spirit, the ones who stray are those who are drawn from humanity and towards the mechanical dance of the Weaver)

                                                          Red Talons - The Friends of the Wolves | Whereas the Glass Walkers moved towards humanity after their contact with the Weaver, the Red Talons ran away and have been running ever since | The Red Talons have to various degrees excised the primordial human elements of the dual-nature, remaining dedicated to all the aspects of Wolf and the Old Wilderness instead, harkening themselves towards the Wilds-That-Was prior to the Fall of the Weaver
                                                          (On the other side of the coin from the Glass Walkers; the Red Talons closest to Gaia are those who combine their concern for the Wilds with a Paleolithic Human-sensibility, those who stray towards fall into the Wyld’s grasp are those who increasingly abandon the human-spirit and even aspects of the wolf-spirit which are too like the man-spirit in favor of the primeval wilderness and naturalist chaos of the Wyld)

                                                          Shadow Lords - The Cruel Necessities of Grand-Father | The Shadow Lords are those who have embraced the unpleasant pragmatism which assists them in their goals and desire to succeed - intrigue, poison, culling, assassination, subtlety, and deception are all parts of the unsavory implements that have always been known but had to be practiced out of sight | The Shadow Lords would also recognize the useful elements of humanity - those patriarchs that the Black Furies loathed for the breaking of taboos were undeniably able to accrue immense resources and influence; the Shadow Lords asked how that might be put to use in service of Gaia and so they would embrace the human innovations in authority and organization | The Shadow Lords are looked at with wary suspicion by other Garou - but as long as they defeat their enemies, serve the strong loyally, and root out the Wyrm, what is to be done?
                                                          (The Shadow Lords were the appointed ‘enemy within’ of early werewolf, even having a section of their first tribe book dedicate to villainy; I think that alone is too one-dimensional but I do feel it’s an important element. Any tribe can be villainous for a session or a chronicle but the Shadow Lords are the tribe every other tribe would imagine as the most-likely villains. At their best the Shadow Lords live up to their own BS - they really are using the tools of the Patriarch, Intrigue, & Cruelty in service of the more-honorable leaders and Gaia. At their worst they really are planning to use those methods to raise themselves to power, even if the honorable-leaders are competent to rule. Whether at their best or worst however the Shadow Lords are prepared to arrange for the demise of any leaders who prove too weak to uphold their duties; they are the cult who kills the golden king so that the golden king might live)

                                                          Silent Striders - Keepers of Dead Mysteries | Outcasts, Secret-Keepers, Loners, Wanderers, & Emissaries to the Dead & the Shadow | The Silent Striders serve as the nerve tissue connecting disparate septs and caerns while also being an early warning network for whatever goes on in the dark places in-between septs | They are keepers of ancient secrets and mysteries as well, such as the roads to the Underworld and the cold voices of the dead | They are outcasts like the Bone Gnawers or Lone Wolves but unlike them have remained dedicated to their Sacred Mysteries (those who don’t will become Bone Gnawers or Ronin in time, which may explain part of the psychological reason for the Striders loathing of those groups)
                                                          (I view the presence of Cthonic cults as being an important part of Garou in general, if one that doesn’t get talked about openly or very often. Every tribe probably has their own version and various degrees of stress placed upon it - but the Silent Striders and Older Brother probably have it the most | I view the differences between Older Brother as being fairly nuanced; Older Brother has mysteries connected with living and active traditions albeit the Apocalypse and is still engaged in the upkeep of them; the Silent Striders are the remnants of Post-Apocalyptic mysteries that are largely dead but for the relics they still carry, but dead does not mean they are without power)

                                                          Silver Fangs - The Sacred Nobility | The Silver Fangs somehow gained the primordial blessing of nobility (kingship, queenship, etc.) in ancient times - there should probably be several contradictory reasons why but it’s important that this claim has some veracity to it | The Core Problem of the Silver Fangs comes from trying to figure out what Sacred Kingship means, in practice, 10,000+ years after the first Queen received the blessings of Sun, Moon, Wolf, Mother, Death, Falcon, etc. The Silver Fangs are torn apart by their own contradictory existence and all the neuroses that comes with it. At their best, when some Silver Fang king figures out his own BS, they can truly act as living-heroes who inspire, invigorate, and lead the Garou of today with confidence and success. At their worst when some Silver Fang queen is unable to parse her own BS, they become parodies of themselves, the best of these being drooling idiots and pitiable and the worst being terrifying and horrible maniacs.

                                                          Stargazers - The Dreaming Wolves | These Garou are highly sensitive to the precarious nature of existence and from the first moments of sapience have felt the need to understand the mysteries which define them and uphold the metaphysics of their being | the best of these wolves are those who plunge into the psycho sphere and return with useful and actionable insight both into practical existence for the Garou and the metaphysical stakes of their battle, the worst of them are trapped in the endless spiral of their own quixotic selves and are unable to pierce beyond the fetters of their own Egos and come to any fruitful kernels of Wisdom
                                                          (Having the Stargazers being the main or central tribe of all of Asia is ridiculous imo, but that’s a can of worms for another day. It makes thematic sense for them to have links to Nepal and Tibet but that shouldn’t be their only links to the material world)

                                                          Older Brother - The Ghost Dancers | (I’m not indigenous so pinch of salt for this group and younger brother, check out stuff that James Sombrano has done); Older Brother is the active tribe most tied with the Mysteries, Secrets, and Occult Worlds that other tribes have links to but do not fully delve into; in the post-Apocalypse of the indigenous world they have adopted a fluid strategy of survival through adaption which is reflected by their general association with the water, both the mercurial flow and the hidden depths
                                                          (All tribes have mystical concerns, some more than others, but here’s how I would distinguish the Ghost Dancers from the Silent Striders and Stargazers for example | The Ghost Dancers have maintained an active community and living tribe unlike the fragmented Striders and oft-reclusive Stargazers and furthermore the Ghost Dancers are both concerned with their own Sacred Traditions but also take a mercenary interest in every arcane tradition within reach; meanwhile the Silent Striders maintain close ties to the underworld and their own almost-Lovecraftian dead rites while the Stargazers thrust themselves not only into the mystical deluge of spirit worlds but also into the realms of the Psycho-Sphere, Dreaming Realms, Astral, & Far Realms in search of insight)

                                                          Younger Brother - The Gale Stalkers | If Middle Brother and Older Brother were more aligned with Earth & Water than Younger Brother is with Air (with an honorable mention to Fire imo). They are defiantly anti-assimilationist and strive to maintain their lives, heritage, traditions, and peoples without compromise or any restriction but what they themselves choose. They are among the fiercest of warriors because of this and have only been sharpened and further enraged by the Apocalypse they’ve. The fierce battle to survive and persist alongside the ways in which the realities of their traditions persist, regardless of what settlers may think, is also a key factor of this tribe.
                                                          (The comics they included in the early tribe books are somewhat hit-or-miss but something I appreciate from Younger Brother’s tribe book comic is the premise that the spiritual concerns that indigenous peoples had did not cease even if they were removed from their lands, taboos and spirits and monsters remained, ones that only those who remembered the traditions like Younger Brother could combat. While this is something I feel should be universal in Werewolf, I do feel it deserves a special mention with relation to Older & Younger Brother. Speaking of the Three Brothers - my view is that Older Brother’s purview was the mystical and the ritual, Middle Brother’s purview was the legal and the traditional, while Younger Brother’s purview was social and the active cycle of life.
