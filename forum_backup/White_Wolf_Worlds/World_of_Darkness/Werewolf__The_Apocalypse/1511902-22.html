                                    Heaven's Guidance, Sense Wyrm, Silence, Speed of Thought, Visions of Duat

                                    Axis Mundi, Blissful Ignorance, Messenger's Fortitude, Speech of the World, Tread Sebek's Back

                                    Adaptation, Great Leap, Mark of the Death-Wolf, Sense the Unnatural

                                    Attunement, Black Mark, Dam the Heartflood, Speed Beyond Thought

                                    Gate of the Moon, Reach the Umbra

                                    Rank 1, Rank 2, Rank 3, Rank 4, Rank 5

                                    While typically I won't be editorializing, the Silent Striders need a few more Green Gifts. I'd suggest handing them stuff like Pulse of the Prey, Resist Pain and Hare's Leap. There are many Gifts which would help with travel and the role of messenger which will also help with typical 'adventure' scenes, like a fight. Just as Hare's Leap can be useful to cross a desert to bring a message, it can be useful in a fight. Gifts like that should be prioritized, not just Gifts that help you run for hours and days.
