    Originally posted by Aya Tari View Post
    I doubt that Erebus could burn off excess Weaver energies or Wyld energies, so any Garou that tried to toss other Garou into the silver lake for excess Weaver energies or Wyld energies would likely find themselves tossed in the lake instead by the guardians of Erebus because of their Wyrm Taint. The problem that Erebus is designed to solve is Wyrm Taint because there is no logical or rational argument for the Wyrm. The Wyrm is the corruption of the natural order and, like an aggressive cancer, its tumors must be surgically removed if the patient is to have any chance at survival.

    I really do not understand the objection to Erebus because Erebus is a wonderful place that purifies Garou of their corruption while they are alive (the only other alternative is an honorable death in battle or a dishonorable descent into madness and perversion). I have had entire groups travel to Erebus to willingly subject themselves to Erebus after they were contaminated by Wyrm Taint. It is a place of purification through suffering, and the Garou are always willing to suffer for the greater good (and those that are not willing to suffer will probably see things differently after Erebus purifies them of their Wyrm Taint).
    Often compared to Garou "hell", Erebus is actually a place of spiritual cleansing. Erebus is filled with rivers of molten silver which both burn and heal at the same time, extending the torment of the inmates for months, years, possibly even centuries.
    Werewolves end up in Erebus when they have become too tainted by one of the members of the Triat, most often the Wyrm. Either the werewolf will journey to Erebus to be cleansed, or will be dragged there by other werewolves.

It's utterly inhumane, monstrous and not something humans should aspire to (hence Hunters being a thing), and Garou thinking they're more moral than Kindred (and those filthy Quintessence-hunting Mages, and those freaky Fae, and those Demons and Ghosts) is simply a matter of perspective. Is it worse to turn one other monster into a quilt, or is it worse to blow up a factory and kill 10 random security guards? Is it worse to mind-control a human until they kill their family, or kill a whole community by wiping out the oil refinery they rely upon? There are no concrete answers to these questions, which is why I like playing ANY role which puts me in an inhuman and monstrous perspective.
