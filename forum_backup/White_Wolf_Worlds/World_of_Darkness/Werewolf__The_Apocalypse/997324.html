              Here are two things that need to be pointed out, that I think the discussion in previous thread were not discussed much.

              A) Garou are not vampires. They are born, they die and they are reborn into a new age. While some stay as ancestor spirits in tribal homelands, the physical living garou are still born into and (most of the time) raised in the contempolar culture of that time. Adding to this, garou do not live very long, though they could. You are old for a garou if you are in your 40s.

              To the actual point, most garou in the modern world are homids. Most of those homids grow up in the modern culture of their country/continent before they change, unknowing of their heritage. Even if they have ancestors, their experiences are still based on how they grew up. Even if some of these homid grumble about starting the impegrium, they are very far removed from the actual garou who began it and the situation then.

              As far as I know, during the times of the Impegrium, garou generally lived separate from humans and their children were raised in the knowledge of their culture. There was a much wider divide between garou and humanity in those times. That is not the case in the modern world, where garou tend to still hold ties to humanity in some way.

              B) The setting name is world of Darkness, but despite the name, the setting didn't stop in the 80s-90s and stay there by culture. WoD is meant to be a darker reflection of our world, and that means it reflects the current way our world is. Different themes, and so, different discussions from the previous editions (aside from W20).

              C) Like I said in point A, the modern garou are very far removed from their ancestors. The tribe that is the least removed, the Red Talons, is basically dying out. One could see their call for Impegrium as a way to go out in a blaze of glory rather than have their kin die out.

              My gallery.

                      Originally posted by The Laughing Stranger View Post
                      It isn't so much a debate as it is Crimson and Uniform stuck on proving that all/most Garou are genocidal assholes despite all evidence to the contrary.
                      But even on that point, even if the Garou were trying to exterminate Humanity, it wouldn't be them bringing back the Impergium. And I mentioned it in that thread, but I feel like the definition of what the Impergium was is being lost in the conversation.
                      Even if the Garou (of any particular tribe) tried to exterminate the humans, it still wouldn't be "bringing back the Impergium" to me. Examples that were cited: A Wendigo Elder saying he wants to kill all Wyrmcomers ("the White man") and the Get saying that they will destroy Humanity come the Apocalypse. Neither of those are "bring back the Impergium". And the argument, that these examples are just the Impergium under a different name misses the point (imo) on what the Impergium actually was.

                      You would need significant follow up (taking up systematically culling human numbers again) after whichever event decimated Humanity for it to be the Garou "bringing back the Impergium". The Talons are the only ones who really argue that this is a good (or even feasible) idea.

                      Edit: Another example brought up was a Shadowlord named "Malcom the liar" talking to Red Talons saying he totally agrees with them that the Impergium needs to be brought back.
                      A) His name.
                      B) He is a Shadowlord, agreeing with the person he's talking to.
                      This shouldn't be used as an example of the Shadowlord tribe supporting a revival of the Impergium.
