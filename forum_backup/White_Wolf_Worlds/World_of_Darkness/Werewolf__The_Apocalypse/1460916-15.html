                                    Originally posted by Wolfen View Post
                                    Achilli will most likely turn Apocalypse into a Forsaken imitation. While I really liked Forsaken's 2nd Edition, there is no point in having too games so similar, it'll upset the fanbase and it won't attract more players...
                                    Achilli is not someone I'd really want with their paws in Werewolf in general. His attitudes toward Werewolf in the olden days were... controversial to say the least. Mind you I don't think he has the final word here. It's not about him or Dawkins or anyone else as much as it's about a vision Paradox has for what the brand should be and what it should look like. We've spent the better part of last 10 years actually with people doing their best to create games that continue what used to be and make sure they don't close off options and playstyles. It made the 20th anniversary games big and horribly dense for any new players without the help of the older fans, but it was pretty much a continuation of what was there.

                                    Fifth Edition is a statement where they are defining the one true way to play these games based on their own perspective and vision of them. This vision seems strangely set in the earliest days of the gamelines when they had a number of very rough and silly ideas without considering the great work latter White Wolf did with them. They claim that they are a continuation, but the way the material is treated, it's clearly a reboot. Actually communicating this would be much more fair, and allow people to temper their expectations. It would also eliminate a lot of the complaints from the people reading this material and expecting it to be a logical continuation of where we left off. If they dropped this pretense of continuation it would free them up to actually create interesting changes.

                                    But I don't think they can. There is already a split in the gaming population and confusion between oWOD, nWOD and WOD5, and they cannot really clear this confusion up if they want the recognizeable iconic IP elements that they feel are the marketable part of the game. So we're stuck like this.

                                                        Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                                                        What was Achilli's views on Werewolf?
                                                        In the old days on usenet, LJ, and on the convention circuit he was quite a character with strong stances on what he wants from games and how valid other people's opinions are. Mind you this is great, because he was one of the first devs you could actually talk to and discuss things with, and he had the propensity to be even funny about it. It was awesome to actually see what the writers think and what their thought proceses are. Unlike many, I don't begrudge him his vampire work, as I feel it was the strongest under his tenure. However, he did not take kindly to other lines and would regularly be dismissive and treat them as second fiddle to vampire. There was something along the lines of Vampires rule and Werewolves drool.

                                                        And while I can appreciate him as an author, and even assume that in the past 20 years he has changed in many ways I can be rather apprehensive about someone like that getting reigns of Werewolf.
