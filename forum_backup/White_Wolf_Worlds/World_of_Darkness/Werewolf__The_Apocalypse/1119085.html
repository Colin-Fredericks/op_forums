    Caerns with religious significance

    07-21-2017, 06:14 PM
    So, I'm in the process of prepping a Roll20 WtA game set in Tucson. I'd like to place a Caern somewhere in one of the old neighborhoods in the city, and thought that I had found the perfect place. Then I started to talk myself out of it. Basically El Tiradito is a nominally Catholic shrine in the Barrio Viejo neighborhood in Tucson. I say nominally because its not owned or officially endorsed by the Catholic church or any church. The story goes that, many years ago, a jealous husband murdered his wife's true love and buried him right in front of the house. The shrine is supposed to be the location where he was buried. People often come there to light candles leave and leave little pieces of paper in the crevices of the wall with prayers or wishes on them. In fact, the place has come to be known as "The Wishing Shrine." So the place has a quasi Christian significance, but it has the quasi-heretical folk Christian vibe that is common to Mexico. So I guess my question is, do you think a place like that could become a Caern? I mean, look, I get that its MY game and I can do whatever I want. But are there examples in the WoD of places with religious significance becoming Caerns, or would the Christian symbolism leave a bad taste in the spirits' mouths?

          Well, I wrote this, hope it offers some inspiration.

          Originally posted by Sept of Luna's Eternal Vigil
          Location: Scotland.


          Location description: An ancient and powerful sept, Luna's Eternal Vigil or just Vigil, is a place of learning for the Silver Fangs. It is also a place where the metis of the tribe are sent to be kept, safely away from the eyes of the Nation. As the sept is technically a monastery, it is split between males and females. Contact with the other sex is often minimal, though Ragabash are known to sneak around. The Vigil is a large place built into the mountain itself, though there are a lot of minor buildings used to support the sept. It has large gardens, classrooms for students. And one of the most expansive libraries of garou literature in the Nation. The Sept also houses its share of kinfolk monks and nuns, whether from those who'd rather worship than breed, to those who are taken there to be safe from the dangers of their home septs.

          As Vigil has tended metis since the days of Dark Ages, they have a veritable wealth of knowledge on the middle breed and how to deal with rowdy cubs. The metis in turn receive a decent education, and are treated well. However, each is named in latin either after their deformities, or by auspice. This name mimics the renaming of monks of ancient times, and implies the metis are not to leave the Sept. There are wards and guards for those who still try.

              Is there a conflict between the Gaian energy there and any True Faith? If there is, then there would be trouble because True Faith has an effect on the Gauntlet and Rage. However, absent True Faith issues I don't think there'd be a concern. El Tiradito falls more into the folk belief category than actual Christian belief. It's like people burying statues of St. Joseph to help sell their home. The Church is emphatic that this is wrong - it is superstition, not faith. But some people do it anyway. In choosing the caern's purpose though, you'd want to select one that doesn't clash with the folk Cathiolicism beliefs of the area though, but complement it.

              I do hope that your setting has some more caerns than just an urban one in Tucson. There are some really interesting places out there. The Madrean Sky Islands are a perfect place for a wilderness caern, and the Sonoran Desert is the greenest/wettest desert in the world. Arizona is really a diverse place with desert, pine forests, deciduous forests, wetlands, grasslands, and chaparral (even tundra up by Flagstaff). The White Mountain and San Carlos Apache Indian reservations are not too far, and next to them is the US Fish & Wildlife Service program to reintroduce the Mexican Wolf subspecies. The Garou protectorates likely includes both sides of the border. It can be a very cool setting for a chronicle.

                              The reason people visit the shrine seems to be for healing of their own hearts after being heartbroken. The entire legend is one of tragedy after all, not only is Juan killed, but the entire family is taken out in short order. I think the caern's purpose is best suited being built on that. It would be like a Caern of Healing, but more like Emotional Healing instead of physical healing. So I guess a Caern of Empathy is the best description. "Comfort" or "Succor" would be another appropriate term.

                              Most Garou would not be interested in such a caern, so it falling to the Bone Gnawers would make sense. Only the Children of Gaia would be the only other tribe interested in it with the Uktena having an intellectual interest.

                              People who have been betrayed, falling into Harano, in severe grief, or some other kind of Depression go there to become healed and renew their purpose in life. So regaining Willpower in certain kind of circumstances would be appropriate, but not all kinds. Suppression or removal of certain Derangements might also happen. I could also see, in certain circumstances Gnosis gained, or (to a lesser extent) even Rage might be recovered, but only in cases where grief and sorrow might have limited them beforehand.

                              Activating the Caern might lead to bonuses in Empathy or Enigmas.
