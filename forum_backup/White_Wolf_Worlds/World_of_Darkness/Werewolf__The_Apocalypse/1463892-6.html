                        Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                        I feel this falls into the trap that prejudice NEEDS a justification. The Garou hate the Crinosborn because the Garou are elitist bigots. This is an element that was there from the 1st Edition.
                        I disagree. I can't accept that a whole species is hateful towards the Crinosborn without an explanation. Be it historical or evolutionary or supernatural or what ever.

                        But, I'm also one of the people who found Heaths Ledger's Joker portraial not that groundbreaking and thought "Some people just want to see the world burn" a less than satisfying character motivation.

                        Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                        One of the things about Pure Breed (and why it lacks now) was that it gave a lot of Garou a lot of unearned privilege.
                        I also disagree, and think that Pure Breed, as Ethan Skemp detailed once in the White Wolf forums, was misunderstood even by some authors.

                        Point is, if a character has pure breed, he or she is closer to the ideals of his or her given tribe. This, in the typical nature vs. nurture question, would mean that the character in any case would have not only the disposition to be a remarkable garou (within his or her tribals standards) but also the inner drive and yearning to do so.
                        A Silver Fang would never be a sluggard (Harano not withstanding).

                        The problem is, that players and some authors alike saw the trait as a cheap way to social roll bonus dice. And over several iterations of the rules the concept of pure breed was changed bit by bit, for even the authors didn't understand how it was supposed to work (according to Ethan, which is the way it first made real sense to me).

                        In our LARP, we (the STs) strip people of their pure breed trait, if they aren't able or at least make an effort, to convey that they are archetypical to their chosen tribe and by extension exemplary Garou in general.

                        It is my believe, that having Pure Breed is a character trait that actually says something about the psyche of the garou, in more ways than being adored by others. And this drive and ambition might be misguided, like with the purest of his generation, Arkady.

                                                For now I'll give my two cents based only on pages 1 and 7 of this thread, I'll read the rest later.

                                                I personally think the Red Talons harm the game more than improve it, since always, so I'll be clear out of the bat that I do think they make sense as non Wyrm antagonists that don't follow the litany.

                                                Making any other tribe unplayable is bollocks, even when they threw the Stargazers in the Courts they kept them playable.

                                                Haglust is something I have to see before judging. The same for the new Renown mechanics. While Blood Potency wasn't the brightest idea in V5, I generally liked or was at least ok with the V5 mechanics.

                                                Apocalypse Yesterday and hostile Umbra are also somethings that I have to see first, but in lore their record is far less impressive, not to say impressively stupid. Lots of ok ideas with bad execution.

                                                Getting rid of Metis is just a big NO, more than anything else up to now. Go fuck yourselves, I hate you.

The Garou society does have far more flaws itself than some players recognize, but it is not a society of pure assholes. They have lots of flaws, like many human societies, and frequently think they're entitled to their views, but those same views being challenged from within is equally a big part of the game.
