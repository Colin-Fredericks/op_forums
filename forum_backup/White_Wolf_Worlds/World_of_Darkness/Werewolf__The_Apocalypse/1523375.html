                      Any published Level Six rites were likely published separately in only a few sourcebooks. Like anything having to do with Legendary Rank, they are likely less handled by game mechanics and more about ST discretion about whatever story is being built around it. They are meant to be the conclusion of epic quests by only the mightiest heroes whose names will be forever remembered in Garou legend. If the character is not akin to Heracles, Beowulf, Galahad, the Hero Twins, and so on, they're just not capable of performing the rite successfully.

                      There is a previous post on this forum listing all the level six rites one poster could find. That thread is located here.

                                                      You're... skipping the point. The Rank 6 Gifts aren't meant to be major power increases over lower Rank Gifts like 6+ Discipline dot powers are. They're meant to be plot devices that give Legendary Garou that ability to do things well beyond the norm to fulfill a specific purpose. Jonas's Rank 6 Gift exists because it's the only way for him to defeat Zhyzhak: force her into a one-on-one duel and remove the numerical advantage she has as the leader of a massive Wyrm army that's largely held together by her iron hand. The ability to force an opponent into a one-on-one duel isn't, by itself, all that special. But it's an essential part of the plot of Jonas's story that he has the weird unique power to turn the tides of the War at the most crucial moment.

                                                      While it's worth looking at the mechanics of some of the Rank 6 Gifts because with so many Gifts there are few that are kinda silly (as in they aren't unique special powers, they're just more expensive versions of Gifts that already exist at Rank 5), in general analyzing them by "power balance" isn't going to work because they don't exist for that context. They exist to generate specific plot events.
