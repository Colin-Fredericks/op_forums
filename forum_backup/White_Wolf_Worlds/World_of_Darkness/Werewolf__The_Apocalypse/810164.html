    Brainstorming: Lupines vs kindred in Nazi Germany

    01-16-2016, 06:44 AM
    One of the great things about these boards are that it's full of creative people, and I love bouncing ideas of you.

    I'm currently running a vampire campaign using the Transylvania Chronicles as the foundation, but also filling in some of the time gaps between chapters with other stories.

    Eventually, I'd love to set the story in Germany between 1920-1945, because, well, I think it's an absolutely fascinating time and place, thick with drama.

    The reason why I'm posting this here in the WtA section is that I'd also like to use this period as a backdrop to some good old fashioned vampire vs werewolf fighting.

    However, I don't know enough about the WtA line to come up with a motivation for lupines to get up in arms and start threatening the vampires of whichever city they are settled in, and was hoping for some ideas that would be reasonably compatible with WtA lore.

    I don't know much about the Wyrm (I'll look into it more as I flesh out a story outline), but is there any way it can be worked into something around Nazis and vampires? What tribes could you see getting involved?

    If you were brainstorming something along those lines, what angle would you approach it? Anything you guys can throw out there would be very appreciated.

      I think White Wolf's original stance was that the Nazi regime and their crimes not supernaturally motivated.

      In general, there is a legend how werewolves think vampires came into existence and it amongst other reasons is a justification to kill them. In most cases, vampires do not cause that much misery and corruption that garou really care to hunt them explicitly - fomori, banes, black sprial dancers and the like are in general more dangerous to the general population and are therefore more often on the werewolves radar.

      But, in any case, if there are vampires and the werewolves stumble over their trail, they will still invest some effort to track them down and destroy them. It is a cultural obligation, rooted in their believe system.

              I didn't mention any tribes, well one. Fomori are humans (or animals or supernaturals) possessed and fused with a bane (dark spirit). Banes are dark spirits and black spiral dancers are in fact a tribe of werewolves, who server the Wyrm - a cosmic embodiment of corruption and destruction.


              Like I said, the garou don't keep to their own affairs, if they come across vampires. There really would have to be a direct bigger threat to let a vampire continue exist. But since you want the confrontation, just assume that there aren't any other threats the local garou have to deal with. If it is the 1920, then everything might be peachy for the german population - despite having lost the first WW. The war is over and the future is promising. In such a scenario the werewolves could very well decide, that it is time to hunt some vampires, since most other horrors they usually fight against are in hiding or beaten.

              If you want a more personal reason. Have a vampire kill or ghoul a person who is related to a local werewolf leader. While werewolves can't very well interact with humans, those humans who have werewolves in their ancestry can and form a kind of link between werewolves. They are the brothers, sisters, spouses, cousins and children of the local werewolves, and if a vampire even accidentally harmed one of them.... well, that might have been his last mistake...

                                                      I don't imagine werewolves as being overly pro-Nazi or anti-Nazi. For us modern humans, the Nazis have an almost religious significance as the embodiment of evil. That wasn't the case in the 1930's or 1940's nor would it be the case for being with belief systems so radically different from our own as the Garou. So it is hard to imagine not caring that much, but I think that on average, werewolves would care more about the environmental or spiritual effects of the war than the ideology of the combatants. That isn't to say that some wouldn't care, but not as much as we might at first imagine.

                                                      With a werewolf vs vampire conflict with the backdrop of WWII, it might be interesting to have Nazis versus non-Nazis and werewolves versus vampires be orthogonal conflicts. That is to say that some vampires support the Nazis and some oppose them, some werewolves support the Nazis and some oppose them. That could add some complexity to the situation if the players oppose both Nazis and vampires (which I am assuming here) and then have to either ally with Nazis to defeat some vampires or ally with vampires to defeat some Nazis.
