    This is something I've headcanoned since I got into WtA, and it is pretty anime in a sense, but I thought others could use this still.

    In WoD, humans, wolves and most other species with shifter ties, have eye and hair colours than they lack in our world. Due to the constant changing of genes and traits that happens when shifters breed over a millenia if not more, humans for example can have plain yellow or orange eyes and they are not seen as strange. Wolves can have blood red coats, stark black and white coats and so on. These are just common traits and in fact do not imply kinfolk status. Just that somewhere in that person's heritage, there was a garou.

    Note that even if someone had blood red hair and yellow eyes and had a Red Talon grandfather, they would not be kin to the tribe. The totem decides who gets to be kin.

    Another thing is that garou genes have spread so far and so wide, that garou can pop up from nearly any family. Most non-purebred garou come from these lost lines, while the more purebreed you have the more you were expected to know of the Nation.

            - Sense Wyrm is just Sense Bane. It can detect Fomori (since there is a Bane in there), but it doesn't detect unpossessed servants of the Wyrm (including BSDs). Conflicting views about what are and what are not "of the Wyrm" permeates conflicts among the Gaian Tribes, as well as facilitates events like the Second War of Rage.

            - If Pentex actually owned its many so-called "Subsidiaries" it would be impossible to hide its status as one of the largest and most powerful corporations on the planet. It doesn't own them. It is invested in many of them, but largely works an industrial supplier, and realtor. In this role it coordinates other independent servants of the Wyrm, props up potential agents, and gets a foot in the door to corrupt. But any status it has as the Wyrm's most direct corporate servants, acting as cultic mediators between Earth & Malfeas to these other companies, is entirely off the books - they do not own majority shares for all of these major players in their respective industries.

            - Children of Gaia are not a Tribe. I might feel differently if they got a decent Revised-quality Tribe Book, but they didn't, so I simply do not have a sense of them as a culture like I do the other Tribes. Instead, they exist as a multi-Tribal camp dedicated to inter-tribal peace, equality, restoring relations with the Fera, and opposing all war not directly against the Wyrm itself as feeding the Beast-of-War.

            - Black Tooth still lives. He and his Pride dominate southeast Africa and he names himself King of Cats. While his brutality toward all who would oppose him is horrific, he is also actually fighting the Wyrm's minions. The Ahadi exists as a resistance movement against him, but many who may hate him are reluctant to throw their support behind it because he is seen as an effective commander against the Wyrm.

            - Yava are largely metaphoric (I have a list of what they all actually mean), but work in many ways like "True Names" of the Tribe. If a Bastet knows their Tribe's Yava they are virtually immune to mind-reading and mental-influence supernatural powers from someone who does not know their Yava. If someone discovers a Tribe's Yava, members of that Tribe can not even exert the normal defensive rolls or willpower expenditures against mind-reading or supernatural mental-influences.

            - The Silver Crown is still missing (I'm thinking of throwing the Tribes into chaos via the "Rightful King" being revealed to be a Metis); Jonas Albrecht does not exist as an NPC (he's clearly someone's PC, as opposed to characters like Konetziko who comes of more as an interesting plot hook for pcs to interact with).

            - "Lunatics" do actually go through the First Change, they just completely reject their nature, and as a result cannot control their shifting, generally only doing it in Frenzy under their Auspice moon. They are in short Classic Hollywood Werewolves.

            - The Beast Courts are less idyllic. After all, look at the ecological disaster that is modern China - clearly not a sign they have their shit together. They are very hierarchical, and the survival of the Zhong Lung is a closely guarded secret. Generally, the Nezumi, Tengu, and any Bagheera or Garou who join have no idea, with the upper ranks of the Courts they're aware of being dominated by Khan, Same-Bito, and Kitsune, who in turn are aware of the Zhong Lung above them. Naga keep to the shadows and pass themselves off as Zhong Lung to the upper Courts when they have to.

            -Normal Ananasi exist in East Asia and obviously hate the Kumo.

                        Well, keep in mind that a lot of early White Wolf/Werewolf/PENTEX stuff was written by people whose understanding of "modern" (and by that I mean early 90s) business probably consisted entirely of the movies Wall Street and Robocop, and Mr. Burns on the Simpsons, and at times was aimed more at parody than horror. So it's horribly wonky in a number of places, and sometimes requires fiddling. Revised edition fixed some of this, but never really in enough detail or space.

                        I, personally, run PENTEX a little grittier than what's presented in some of the source material. Part of that involves doing a lot of research into the history and practices of the different industries involved, which can be fascinating (both in the "that's really interesting" sense and the "is that a train wreck up ahead?" sense). It's also pretty cool when you can find preexisting takes on certain things that are even creepier than the Werewolf versions. For example, my version of Avalon Toys takes a massive amount of inspiration from a short piece Grant Morrison did for Vertigo's WEIRD WAR TALES miniseries called (IIRC) "The New Toys", and if PENTEX's BoD ever found out what the company was really up to, they'd likely try to burn it to the ground.

                        I still lament that there was never a book devoted entire to PENTEX as a whole, preferably presenting it as an onion whose layers have to be pulled back by the PCs and the Garou Nation, starting with how normal mundane businesses and industries function in the WoD and exactly why their normal everyday non-Wyrm corrupted activities are still a threat to the Garou and their interests, continuing down into the various PENTEX subsidiary holdings and what they do, then to PENTEX itself and it's special projects of creating First Team super soldiers, psychic weapons, and other craziness as part of their plan for ultimate power.

                                                My take on the Children of Gaia has always been that their goal is to unify the Garou Nation under their own leadership, in a way that could be stereotyped/trivialized as "we want everyone to live together in peace, love and harmony, and will eagerly engage in passive aggressive bullying tactics against anyone who doesn't think the way we do", but more honestly is probably based around the sincere belief that things for the Garou Nation started going downhill when they first divided into tribes. Over all, their modus operandi is that a) it's possible to resolve internal Garou vs Garou conflicts with ways other than unrestrained violence and b) when dealing with outside enemies, especially those corrupted by the Wyrm, it's ethically worthwhile to at least investigate the potential for redemption or healing before taking the option of putting a creature out of its misery through mercy killing. In the sense of the Garou Nation as a whole, they're one of the four leadership tribes (along with the Silver Fangs, Shadow Lords and Croatan), and should be viewed by the first two as potential rivals.

                                                My take on the Beast Courts was that it's a bunch of people who don't like each other for a wide number of reasons who are only able to co-exist because of a very strict adherence to carefully detailed rules of etiquette everyone grudgingly agreed upon. (But then I also have the Asian Guhral still around and the Nagah a rare oddity in the courts, so I am weird.)
