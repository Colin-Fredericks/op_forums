                                                Played the (free) demo a bit and here are some thoughts;

                                                -The game really REALLY wants you to get a job and live as a human for the most part. As far as I've seen, there is no option to just live as a wolf. Trying to pick a job and house that are inconspicuous gets the game complaining about freezing and money.

                                                -It isn't just that your first pack are arseholes, there are no options that please them. I don't mean in a 'they seek out things to critique', I mean in a 'any choice you pick leads to a bad situation with them.'

                                                -Everything is very Forsaken in how it works from the focus on mundanity to the isolated garou.

                                                -Auspice means very little, especially if you are a philodox.

                                                -This might be my own fault, but I played with the mentality of avoiding rousing the Rage too much in public and ended up losing the wolf.
