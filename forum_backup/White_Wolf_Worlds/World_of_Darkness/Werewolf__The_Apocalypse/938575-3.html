    Which is why i prefer it if the LOWER ranked Caerns are more those which are 'open to all', and the higher up in rank of the Caern, the more it goes to just one tribe (or in some cases more to one shifter)..
    This is how I usually run things. I think I've only ever had 1 game where the group was from a higher level Caern (4), but that was a period Hengeyokai game so there was plenty of character diversity to be had. In all other games (more traditional Werewolf games), the group was from a level 2 or 3 Caern. It's low enough to allow for more tribal variation in my mind, but high enough to have enough internal politics to deal with and plenty of inter-Sept politics for the group as well.

        Most rank 1 caerns likely only have 1-3 packs at most (and if 3 packs, those are very small packs). It's probable that they are mono-tribal, or perhaps two closely allied tribes (Uktena and Wendigo, Glass Walkers and Bone Gnawers, Silver Fangs plus a flunky, or some other understandable combination (such as Fianna and Get of Fenris considering the overlap between between Celtic and Germanic peoples over the centuries.

        One thing I like about Rank 1 caerns, is that you can place them almost anywhere and keep the Garou population low. Very easy to introduce new NPCs that way.

        Rank 2-4 caerns I can see as multi-tribal (or at least closely allied tribes), but certainly not all of them. The canonical Sept of the Green in New York has to be one of the more diverse multi-tribal septs, and is probably a legacy of New York being the traditional entry port for immigrants into the United States. I imagine most multi-tribal septs are limited to 4-6 tribes, and many of those tribes are probably those you would expect to be part of multi-tribe septs - Children of Gaia, Bone Gnawers, and Silver Fangs plus travelers like Silent Striders, Stargazers, and Black Furies.

        I think large multi-tribal septs - those having 10 tribes or more let's say - are extremely rare.

        Of course, there is a tradition of young Garou being Anruth and travelling from sept to sept. I think certain kinds of caerns are probably known to welcome them. It's hard for me to say those caerns are "multi-tribal" as anruth packs do not stay long, but certainly it is another way to introduce some variety into the game.

            Originally posted by idpersona View Post
            Personally I like the idea of the Bialowieza Forest over a random spot in the Alps
            The Bialowieza has an obvious hook, but I don't think an alpine caern should be dismissed as some "random spot". There are lots of cool things about the alps. First, they are majestic and beautiful, or at least striking. You can find some great images of all kinds of mountains that would make great backdrops to a caern. Second, there are glaciers there going back to the Ice Ages.
            Third, they are the source for the major rivers and watersheds in Europe - both the Rhine and Danube begin there. Fourth, they also offer a diverse range of habitat in a small area because of the differences in heights.

            The decentralized political structure of Switzerland, and that the alps cover several countries, means an ST has lots of ways to explain why a particular corner of the Alps can be controlled by Garou and perhaps their kinfolk make up most of the local population as well.

            I think it would be very easy to craft some kind of story behind an impressive rank 5 caern here with lots of different caern types as options.

                        I definitely don't see the Bone Gnawers controlling a level 5 caern (neither alone, nor as one of the major tribes in a multi-tribe sept). Individuals may get access to one, but over all the tribe suffers from its omega status.

                        We've covered Europe, so North America is the next place. Most players will be familiar with it so it'll be easy. Then we can hit the other continents where people's knowledge is likely less.

                        The level 5 caerns will likely be in Canada or Alaska, although the Rockies in the US is also likely. Obviously the most likely tribes to control them here are Wendigo and Uktena. A Wendigo caern in Canada is an obvious choice. The Uktena will be harder as their hunting grounds - Mexico and the southern US - aren't the most likely spots. I wonder if that means they'll be a caern that is shared between the Uktena and Wendigo. That would be interesting for one of the least populated/powerful tribes to have access to two caerns. If it is multi-tribal and we don't think they're powerful enough to hold it, then maybe we can throw in the Children of Gaia as well. I'll have to think of that.

                        Red Talons holding a caern in northern Canada or Alaska is also possible (if not here, then it'll have to be in Siberia in Asia).

                        Another possibility is a huge multi-sept caern of European descended Garou likely lead by one of the Silver Fang royal houses, perhaps Unbreakable Hearth. That would likely put them in the middle of Canada, say Saskatchewan or Alberta.

                        I'll have to do some reading to see what locations, events, or stories I like that inspire me. Suggestions are welcome.
