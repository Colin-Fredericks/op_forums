          5 tribes (alpha leaders, savage warriors, obtuse occultists, urban sophisticates, passionate revelers), 3 auspices (full, shifting, new), 2 breeds (human, wolf), and only five non-wolf shifters (jaguar, tiger, leopard, hyena and shark), the five tribes follow the five children of Father Wolf and Mother Earth, and the breeds started with the Hero Twins (one wolf-born, one human born; twin-ship is spiritual), and the auspices based on the three daughters of the moon. There are two other tribes, one of outcasts and misfits (Bonegnawers), and one of all-wolf born anti-humans (Predator Kings). There is an Ivory Priesthood devoted to Luna and a Mother Earth sect called the Children of Gaia.
          Primal Urge is their power stat, Rage is their energy, and Harmony is their morality system (representing the balance between man and wolf, flesh and spirit, rationality and instinct, life and death, civilization and nature). Attributes are physical/social/mental X power/finesse/resistance (Strength, Dexterity, Stamina, Charisma, Manipulation, Composure, Intelligence, Wits, Resolve) and Abilities having only 8 per type (Talents, Skills, Knowledge) and trying to keep Backgrounds to no more than ten.

          And I'm sure everyone would hate it.


          (There's also a completely different type of creature called Tricksters who have only human and animal forms, with families based on cats, ravens, hares, foxes, serpents, monkeys, and coyotes.)

                                  Originally posted by Saur Ops Specialist View Post

                                  Eh, It was something I pulled out of my ass on the fly within three seconds of seeing the thread. Most of my better ideas are in one of the earlier threads.

                                  The only thing I was at all serious about was that I actually prefer the nWoD/CoD's attribute set up, and I really do only use 8 of each ability type in my games because it prevents skill creep and ensures the PCs remain highly competent without having to spend a large number of character points. I've also found that using power stats for each type (Blood Potency, Primal Urge, Gnosis, Memoriam, Legend, Ka) helps make crossover issues a lot easier while making it easier for PCs to ascend to heights of power, while morality systems (Humanity, Harmony, Wisdom, Psyche, Clarity, Ma'at) offer a number of interesting role-playing possibilities and can help reign in some problem players.

                                  (The Tricksters thing was something I experimented with several years ago as a replacement for most of the Fera and the Pooka, but I decided I liked the Pooka too much.)

                                                          Forms
                                                          Homid: Homid bred garou cannot be detected as a supernatural creature in homid form, nor get hurt by the effects of silver. Does not regenerate.
                                                          Glabro: -1 difficulty on perception rolls, +2 strength +2 stamina, bite +0 aggravated with a grapple, claw +0 lethal, +2 speed, regenerate lethal with stamina check. -2 appearance, -1 manipulation
                                                          Crinos: -2 difficulty on perception rolls, +4 strength +1 dexterity +4 stamina +1 bruized health level, claw +2 damage difficulty 6, bite +2 damage difficulty 5, a werewolf in crinos gain +2 on initiative and attack rolls against enemies with shorter weapon reach like homid garou with knives or crinos-hispo that use bite attacks. +4 speed after dexterity calculation on two legs and +6 on four. Regenerate lethal damage without stamina check. -1 difficulty on Frenzy checks. All social attributes become 0 when dealing with non Garou or Garou in other forms than Crinos.
                                                          Hispo: -2 difficulty on perception rolls, +3 strength +2 dexterity +3 stamina, claw +1 aggravated damage difficulty 6, bite +2 damage difficulty 5, tripple homid speed after dexterity calculation. Regenerate lethal with stamina check.
                                                          Lupus: Lupus bred garou cannot be detected as a supernatural creature in lupus form, nor get hurt by the effects of silver. -3 difficulty on perception rolls +2 dexterity +2 stamina, bite+1 difficulty 5, double homid speed after dexterity calculation. Does not regenerate.

                                                          Silver Damage
                                                          All werewolves take normal damage from weapons made by silver as per the damage of the weapon in Homid and Lupus and unsoakable damage upgraded by one level (bashing becomes lethal and lethal becomes aggravated) in any other form.
