I was always of the mind frame that natural weaponry, gifts, or fetishes is the way to go and that weapons like a sword or a gun won't have any effect or hurt them.

          There's nothing in the rules that suggest that mundane weapons brought into the Umbra don't work on spirits or other supernatural entities in the Umbra. Natural weaponry, Gifts, and relevant Fetishes are preferred because they're more effective and spirit combat is pretty dangerous in most editions.

          A lot of spirits also have Charms that can easily destroy or control technology; which is another reason not to get overly reliant on human style weapons when fighting spirits.

                                      Generally, spirits don't make a difference regarding the type of damage. All damage is subtracted from Essence equally and Essence is restored all the same.

                                      It shouldn't make a difference, whether the fangs and teeth injure a spirit, or a blade or a tackle or a wall toppled on the spirit. In most cases, that is. For certain spirits it could make sense to give them certain resistances or immunities to highlight their themes.

                                      Attacking a fire elemental with "Create Element: Fire" not that effective probably. "Create Element: Water" usually not causing damage, but in this case, maybe it should.

                                                  Also remember that spirits are not Pokemon. While the rules provide us with broad templates, they're supposed to be unique entities. Just because a spirit has a fire association doesn't mean it's weak to water attacks. That's CCG/video game logic, not animist spirit world logic. WtA doesn't really worry about such things because the Garou are already built to be able to fight spirits without trying to attack them with Super Effective "types" of damage. Garou that specialize in spirit combat push things even farther into not needing that sort of mechanic.

                                                  If you really want that sort of thing, you can just port the Bane system for spirits from the CofD: which has guidance for how specific/broad such things should be to match the power and broadness of the spirit. Just make Banes do unsoakable damage and be ready for Theurge players to bombard you with rolls to know what to use.
