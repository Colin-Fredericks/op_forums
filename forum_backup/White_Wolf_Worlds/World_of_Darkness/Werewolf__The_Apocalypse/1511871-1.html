    The difference between W5 and older editions is that no matter what a garou tried they could not slot back into human society. The Curse kept people away and Rage was always bubbling, but there were other reasons blending in was hard. For one, the feeling of spiritual decay and wrongness in cities and suburbs was often hard to take. Unless you were a Gnawer or a GW, living in cities was stifling like being in a too-hot sauna.     Unless you were a trust fund baby, a ronin would find it easier just living on the run.

    That is one reason the werewolf seems lost in W5's texts, there is no sense of unease among civilization. There is no feeling of being a caged animal or an urban predator. It's just like the character undid their First Change.
