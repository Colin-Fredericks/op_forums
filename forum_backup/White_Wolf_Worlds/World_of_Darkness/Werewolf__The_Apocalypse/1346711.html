      For the default Garou Rite for forming a pack, all the members must be werewolves (the Totem isn't explicitly excluded, but I'd say the majority of the implicit phrasing leads to not including them).

      The multi-Changing Breed alliances (the Hengeyokai and Ahadi) have ways to include any Fera as part of a pack-equivalent.

      Be default anything else is excluded unless they can get access to a power that would allow them to on some level, plus some ST flexibility in most cases.

      A Kinfolk that learns the Tagalong Gift (which means being a Gnawer kin or at least being on super good terms with a Gnawer Theurge) can temporarily join packs.

      In general none of the Fera are accepting of other supernaturals in their packs/equivalents. It's possible with the right powers to make a cause some of them could join in the bond, but that doesn't mean they'd be allowed to (or that pack/etc. would be seen as traitors for doing it).

                      Wave Sentai is nothing, but a bunch of (INSERT HERE) assembled to finish a task. They are not bound spiritualy, and not counts as a pack.

                      (Pure) Humans are excluded from the Packs.

                      Kinfolks are able to join temporary if they know the Gift Tagalong or with the Bonding Rite (Level One, Glass Walker - Dies Ultimae; Glass Walkers Revised, pg. 82)

                      The Ahadi & Beast Courts have special "Rite of Totem" like Rites, which enables other Changers to join a packs. There are some "specialities". For example an Ananasi wouldn't receive the Totem's benefits (and suffer some dis-Obligation) or a Nuwisha can just join into a pack of a Trickster Totem.

                      Eastern Vampires & Dhampyres are able to join the Mouuntain Shentais. They even have the a background Nushi, what is Totem with some addons.

                      Nunnehi are able to jióoin packs. Eshu also has a Background called Orisha, what works like Nunehi Totem with some modification. In their case the possibility of joining a pack is unclear.

                      Mages Totem differs from the Fera's, but with enough medzsik anythingi is possible.

                      Normaly nay to Mummies, Hunters, Demons, Vampires and Wraiths.
