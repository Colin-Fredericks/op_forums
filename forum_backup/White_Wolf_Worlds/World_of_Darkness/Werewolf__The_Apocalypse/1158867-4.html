                            Well, Exalted is a bit less vital in how it handles spirits, since, with the exception of the Incarnae, they're all replaceable celestial functionaries who give nothing of substance to their charges*. On the other hand, spirits have always been presented in W:tA as having abhorrence toward some activity that isn't corrupt, but which nonetheless goes against their nature. Cobra-spirits are loath to cooperate with weasel-spirits; elementals don't go onto each others' turf; Fenris's brood won't not fight if a struggle breaks out around them; and Zarok, planetary Incarna of Jupiter, is surrounded by a court of sycophants with really bad ideas about how to try and win the struggle against corruption in one grand stroke (the plan to ignite Jupiter into a second sun is... not well thought out, to say the least). So it wasn't exactly the most functional relationship all around, and I can see how taking pieces out would lead to terrible feelings all around, much like the Garou blaming each other for slights and disasters.

                            *They can sustain the existence of beings in their purview in the face of alteration and dissolution in the chaos of the Wyld if they're sufficiently powerful, but this is a costly and uncommon capability.
