    Pattern Weaving / Ananasi Sorcery (Homebrew)

    07-19-2022, 09:31 PM
    At her character creation, my Ananasi (Kumoti Viskr) player has set herself the goal to understand the nature of the Pattern Web better and use this knowledge to further the goals of Ananasa. It's her main character arch, and over the course of the campaign she has invested a lot of thought, time, and resources into it. As a reward for her gained insight, I constructed a sorcery Path I plan on giving her access to, step by step.

    The way it works is that by understanding the underlying manipulations her Gifts cause to the structure of the Pattern Web, she is able to blur the outlines of these Gifts together and gain more flexibility than single Gifts would provide. Each Path Level is tied to a Basis Gift she must first learn. The effort of connecting the new Gift with the previous ones will be represented by spending one extra XP per Gift level.

    The Path is meant as an alternative or augment to the Level 4 Rite of Weaving.


    Pattern Weaving

    This Path represents the art of weaving and re-weaving the strings that make up reality. By changing patterns within the Great Web of the Umbra, the Ananasi is able to also cause changes on this side of the Gauntlet.

    Patterns vary in complexity, within the following categories:

    Static Patterns
    A teacup. A dead tree. A metal rod. Simple things that do not change much have simple patterns.

    Dynamic Patterns
    The distinction is somewhat fuzzy, but these are machines made from static components, like a car or clock. Also, gaseous and liquid systems.

    Transient Patterns
    Sounds, an explosion, a magick spell. Electric signals are the big one in this category.

    Living Patterns
    Skin, plants, bacteria. Anything that lives has very intricate patterns of different qualities intertwined with each other, and together they create an emergent pattern that registers as that of the living being.

    Spirit Patterns
    Spirits themselves do not have a direct representation in the Pattern Web. They are umbral projections of the superstructure some physical patterns have. A car spirit would be the animus of a car, changing the spirit means changing the superstructure of the car pattern by proxy.

    Pattern type Difficulty
    Static/Self* 6
    Dynamic 6
    Transient 7
    Living/Undead 8
    Spirit 9








    *) manipulating her own pattern is easier than other Living patterns for a werespider, due to the intimate connection with it


    Path Levels

    • Learn Pattern
    Basis Gift: Eyes of the Umbra
    The werespider can see the Pattern Web structure of an object and gather basic information of its nature. She can also learn the basic pattern structure for this kind of object.
    System:
    The player rolls Perception + Path Value against the difficulty of the pattern. The number of successes determines how much information she can learn from the pattern.

    •• Rend Pattern / Mend Pattern
    Basis Gift: Minor Unweaving
    The werespider can either restore a pattern to its undamaged state, or directly sever pattern strands. This can be used to heal, inflict or repair damage, or restore corrupted data.
    System:
    The player rolls Dexterity + Path Value against the difficulty of the pattern. Each success heals or repairs one health level, or inflicts a point of Aggrevated damage. When used as a counter to another effect, the successes are subtracted from those of the countered effect roll. This power costs one point of Gnosis if manipulating Transient, Living, or Spirit patterns.

    ••• Alter Pattern
    Basis Gift: Reshape Object
    The werespider can change some aspects of an object, such as size, shape, color, or even type of material. Resourceful Ananasi can use the power to subtly alter thoughts or the text of a document.
    System:
    The player spends a point of Gnosis (two points for Living or Spirit patterns) and rolls Manipulation + Path Level. The number of successes determines the level of alteration (Storyteller's decision).

    •••• Entwine Patterns
    Basis Gift: Attunement
    This power allows the werespider to combine or connect two different patterns. The results of this tend to be less on the physical and more on the metaphysical side of things. While it is certainly possible to simply graft two objects together, this is more commonly used to temporarily link minds, or combine spell effects. Connecting two living patterns usually means a high chance that fate will bring the two together.
    System:
    The player spends a point of Gnosis (two points for Living or Spirit patterns) and rolls Wits + Path Level against the difficulty of the pattern. The number of successes determines the degree to which the two targets become intertwined with each other.

    ••••• Weave Pattern
    Basis Gift: Web of Illusion
    At this level, the werespider is able to create new strands and weave her own patterns. This means she is able to create objects out of thin air or create spells with ad-hoc effects (within reason). This power is limited to small objects or minor effects- it is not meant to replace Level 5 Gifts and as a rule of thumb should produce results below the power of them.
    System:
    The player spends a point of Gnosis (two points for Transient patterns, three points for Living or Spirit patterns) and rolls Intelligence + Path Level against the difficulty of the pattern. Due to the open-ended nature of the power, the successes can only serve as a guideline to whatever the player tried to create. The Galliard Gift Fabric of the Mind can serve as a template, although Living patterns are limited to insects and arachnids, while spirit patterns can be used to emulate the effect of a charm.


    I'm mainly posting this for feedback on the mechanics. I know the Ananasi will not abuse the Path (which I realize would be quite easy) because she's a seasoned player, and it's meant as a tool to give her more creative options within the game.

      This one is just for fun, but I thought the above path would need a macrocosm counterpart.

      Weaversong

      While Pattern Weaving is all about manipulating specific patterns, Weaversong allows the werespider to tune in to the macro-structure of the Pattern Web. By attuning to and picking up the vibrations reverberating through the Great Web, the Ananasi can locate and glean information on anything that moves within - and without, in the material world.

      Weaversong uses the same pattern difficulty chart as Pattern Weaving.

      Path Levels

      • Listen
      Basis Gift: Weaver's Eyes
      The werespider can feel the subtle vibrations of anything that moves up to 500 feet around her. She is able to broadly identify what causes the individual vibrations and their relative positions.
      System:
      The player rolls Perception + Path Value. The highest success number above 5 determines the sources of vibrations she can pick up, as per the Pattern Difficulty chart. The total number of successes above a threshold determines the amount of information she can gather for that type of object. Note that inanimate objects need to be moved in order to be perceived.

      •• Harmonize
      Basis Gift: Blissful Ignorance
      By tugging on the strands in the Pattern Web, the werespider can create her own vibrations which can resonate with other things that occupy it. This can subtly influence the strings of fate. In effect, the Ananasi is able to make anything that could conceivably happen in an approximate 500 feet radius around her more or less likely. At this level, she is only able to influence events in the immediate present.
      System:
      The player rolls Manipulation + Path Value against the difficulty of the pattern of whatever she wishes to manipulate. The number of successes determines how likely or not the desired event is going to happen. Minor effects like making a person stumble or having a light bulb burn out require few extra successes, while unlikely events like a car crashing through a wall would require an epic roll. This power costs one point of Gnosis if manipulating Transient, Living, or Spirit patterns.

      ••• Echo
      Basis Gift: Pulse of the Invisible
      The werespider is attuned enough to the Pattern Web to pick up echoes of past vibrations, or estimate the course of a vibration in the immediate future. Her radius of perception increases to 500 yards.
      System:
      The player spends a point of Gnosis and rolls Perception + Path Level against the difficulty of the pattern she wishes to examine. The number of successes determines how far into the past of future she is able to trace the vibrations: assume roughly an hour per success. This can yield information about where something moving is going to be after that time, or where it has been before. It should be treated as a form of divination that returns vague general information.

      •••• Syncopate
      Basis Gift: Understanding the Tapestry
      An advanced version of the Harmonize power, the Ananasi is now able to weave strings of fate herself. She can set up events that will happen at a predetermined time, or coordinate the course of chance. Her radius of influence increases to 500 yards.
      System:
      The player spends two points of Gnosis and rolls Manipulation + Path Level against the difficulty of the pattern. The number of successes required is determined by how much the intended course deviates from what would occur without the Ananasi's interference. The Storyteller notes down the total number of successes, and as the event progresses, crosses off one success for each time they think things would have gone differently.

      ••••• Dissonance
      Basis Gift: Wyld Warp
      At this level, the werespider is able to send a vibration down a string of fate, but in the opposite direction. Incredibly, she is able to manipulate past events- to a degree. Her radius of influence and perception reaches half a mile.
      System:
      The player spends three points of Gnosis and rolls Manipulation + Path Level against the difficulty of the pattern. In practice, this power may be used to repeat a roll if desired, and use the number of successes of the Dissonance roll instead. It can also be used to make very minor adjustments that are further in the past ("I'm sure I picked up the car keys before leaving"). This power only works if it would not alter the course of events up to the point it is used, but for example making it so an opponent forgot to load their gun after they last used it is fine.

              Originally posted by CardassianArtistry View Post
              I can't find the Eyes of the Umbra giftin any of my books. Where is it located?
              It's from the old Wild West Companion (p.121), which has a few unique Fera gifts:

              Eyes of the Umbra (Level One) - With this Gift, a werespider may concentrate and peer into the spirit world at will.
              System: The player must make a successful Gnosis roll against the local Gauntlet rating.
              As a Basis Gift for the Path, any similar Gift that lets the character perceive umbral or spirit details may also work.

              One aspect in Wild West and other older supplements I noticed is that Ananasi used to have an affinity for 'web-like' structures, like electric communication networks. That is something they lost until CB20, probably to not encroach too much onto Glass Walker territory gamewise. I didn't need it yet, but I like the idea of a third Path that specializes on dealing with the World Wyld Web.

                  I need some dream interaction for Kumoti, but this may also interest Tenere for their collections. I considered making this into a sorcery path as well, but it works better as a Rite both conceptually and mechanically. While Garou and other Fera have access to Gifts and Rites for dealing with dreams, a werespider would have difficulty getting hold of those. I took the opportunity to merge the symbolism of weavers and dreams into something that hopefully could be conceivably a part of the Ananasi canon of Rites. Feedback is always welcome so I can improve the result.


                  Rite of Dreamweaving (Level One to Five, Mystic)

                  Each level represents a step in mastery of dreamweaving, with a focus on the nature of Ananasi to differentiate it from other dream crafting powers such as a Wraith’s Phantasm Arcanoi or a Hedge Mage’s Oneiromancy. The setup of the Rite is inspired by different studies on the resting behavior of jumping spiders.

                  To perform any level of the Rite, it is usually preceded by a day of fasting. The Ananasi then suspends herself on a single strand of webbing, attached to one of her legs. The Rite begins once she enters REM sleep and continues until she wakes. To project into a Chimare of another, she will have to carry an object of personal significance of that person on her while doing so.


                  Level One (Threads of Consciousness)
                  The Ananasi is fully lucid and aware in her own dreams, and thus may shape them as she sees fit. She may also witness the dream of another as a passive observer, although her presence may be noticed if the dreamer has a high Awareness. Because the dream projection of herself is still under her full control, doing so in Crawlerling or Homid form will usually draw less attention.


                  Level Two (Threads of Silk)
                  The werespider is able to create a Sylie in her personal Chimare. Resting inside will grant the same benefits as her real Sylie and presents a useful option on longer journeys away from her nest. Because of the dual nature of Sylies as both umbral and material constructs, the Chimare version will eventually merge with the real one after a while of continued use. While this has a number of benefits, it also opens the Sylie to intrusion by new dangers.
                  The Damhàn can freely expand her Dream Sylie, which after the merging as described above has an innate permanency that will allow her to create a Demesne in time.


                  Level Three (Threads of Dream)
                  The reach of the Weaver into dreams is limited, and as her grandchlidren, the Ananasi also experience this limitation. While an adept Viskr can weave the subtle threads of spells, and a strand of silk in a dream still works as such, the stuff dreams are made of uses a very different yarn. In order to weave dreams, the werespider needs to first gather material – in other words, she needs to find and hunt dreams to consume. This usually means dream representations of persons or animals, since other things are hard to bite and drain. Doing so in her own dreams would defeat the purpose, she will need to prowl the dreams of others. She may also capture dreams and store them in her Dream Sylie for later.
                  As a rule of thumb, consumption of a dream construct will allow her to weave one of her own design of the same or lesser agency. Consuming a background character may not give enough yarn to spin into a new antagonist, but enough to weave a small landscape.

                  NB: The dream fabric gathered is neither Glamour nor Dross, but more of a derivative. It is only usable and accessible within dreams – gaining and spinning true Glamour would require powers exceeding those of a Rite. A Momme of gained fabric can be denoted with a circle in or around the character’s Blood Pool boxes if not resolved narratively.


                  Level Four (Threads of Fate)
                  The werespider is able to embroider dreamweave with strings of fate, and in this way craft oracular dreams. She can do this either for herself or others, and by varying the pattern encourage scenes of past, present, or future to play out. The dream will still use the metaphors and symbols it has available, which will require some interpretation and may vary in accuracy depending on how well stage and actors are suited. Thus the Rite is more useful for recalling past events, as it can draw on memories of the subject.


                  Level Five (Threads of Creation)
                  Being able to mesh the strands of the Pattern Web with dreamweave opens powerful possibilities to the Damhàn: she can now calcify objects in a dream and pull them out of a Chimare. Or cocoon the shell of one, and trap a dreamer inside. She could tie the doorway into a dream to an unassuming wardrobe, or enshroud someone to bar them from dreaming altogether.
                  Expanding the realm of Grandmother Weaver is however dangerous, as this Rite calls on the powers of Pattern Spiders: the One Song can attract some of her other brood and, depending on how offensive the changes were, Paradox Spirits. On the other hand, the calcification already renders anything affected into a more mundane version of itself, which helps to mitigate the problem.
