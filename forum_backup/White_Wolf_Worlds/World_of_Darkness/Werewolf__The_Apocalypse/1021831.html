    Metis Produced Through War Crimes and Unchanged Kin

    12-25-2016, 01:02 AM
    Two thorny issues concerning the conception of Metis

    A) Though not often mentioned, it has been acknowledged that just as in wars between Humans, Post-Battle Rape *is* also a sad reality of Garou Warfare. So it would be irresponsible to dance around the issue that yes, more than a few Metis would probably have been children cocieved in violence.
    But herein lies the dilemma:

    As it is written/chiseled/clawed, "Garou Shalt Not Mate With Garou" brings shame upon BOTH parties for willingly breaking Taboo Numero-Uno set down by Gaia herself, and both parents are henceforth stigmatized as "Charach", only slightly higher on the social totem pole than the "bastards" they conceived.

    The assaulter is irrefutably a Charach ON-TOP of being a filthy rapist worse than an animal, but what of the Assaulted Mother (and very occasionally, Father) of the Metis Child?

    1) Would the Rape Victim also be branded a "Charach" by the Garou Nation, as they DID technically Mate with A Garou, however unwillingly?

    2) Would he/she bear the mark of shame of being a "Charach" if the child is carried to term?

    3) Heck, what about the poor Metis Child in question if the father/mother decides to love/raise him/her in spite of never intending his/her birth in the first place? Would they be treated with any kindness or (Gaia Forbid) *worse* than a Metis conceived through a loving-union?

    B) You *never* know if a Kinfolk is Garou until their first change, and yet it is stated that unchanged Garou who mate with another Garou (changed or not) STILL produce Metis despite the fact they are not yet Shapeshifters.
    And no doubt, *completely* un-intended accidents can and DO happen thanks to arranged marriages of daughters/sons "who never got the gift", and the tragic results not revealed until that fateful day nine-months-later in the delivery room.

    So:

    1) Since the father/mother in question is not even a Garou at the time, and does not even KNOW he or she is a Garou on that fateful night, does he or she *still* get punished as a "Charach" in spite of being technically guiltless in the birth of a Metis?

    2) How much shame is there in circumstances like these wherein *neither* parent is technically guilty of *willingly* breaking the litany?

    3) Severe emotional shock/trauma/pain is a common trigger for The First Change; so at the risk of sounding crass, does the often horrific and traumatic pain of chidbirth count as one of the tirggers of the First-Change? (For teenage moms, for example) At the very least it would certainly help an unknowing-Garou Mom survive the birth of her "surprise monster baby."

    C) Finally, in Tribes like the Get of Fenris it is said that taking responsibility and raising your Metis Child is a responsibility that BOTH parents have to shoulder. Since what is done is done, and both are more than likely to NEVER be allowed to honorably marry any kin again, are there formally performed Marriage Ceremonies between Garou who stay together for their Metis *outside* the Children of Gaia (who of course respect True Love), so at the very *least* their love for each other is recognized and ratified?

    Phew too much sugar from Christmas Pudding gets the brain racing.

      It breaks down to two parts forming your answer. There is the formal outcome, and the informal outcome.

      Formally, it is done. All involved are branded Charach since they broke the litany. Willingness isn't exactly a question. They are both warriors of Gaia, so there are no excuses. By the way, among the Garou there is no reason to think that one gender suffer this more than the other.

      Informally, it will vary wildly depending on community and circumstances. Some will place all the blame on one parent and completely forgive the other. After all, ignoring the litany is as much of a tradition as following it. This can be done out of hate, compassion or prejudice, even political plays.

      Unknowing parents due to unexpected Change are more likely to be pardoned. But the marriage will never be deemed valid. One failure isn't reason to not try right next time.

      A Metis pregnancy forces the mother to Crinos in the very last weeks, so this mother WILL Change either way, now. And the Metis is still what it is, there is no reason for a change in status. The child of parents who knew what they were doing isn't more guilty. Guilt isn't a factor for the Metis.

          From what I've seen and read, rape is not treated the same as a willing Litany breach. First of all, rape is Wyrmy and therefore the rapist has let the Wyrm control them in that act. The act of making a metis out of love is not an act of corruption, no matter what some Fianna say. But an act of rape is, so the blame would solely go to the rapist. And given that this is a strategy Black Spiral Dancers use at times, it happens.

For males, it will possibly not even be mentioned or they are even given renown if they stood the act with dignity. If the mother isn't a Dancer, they are possibly killed on the spot. Any child born will be judged VERY harshly and aren't the responsibility of their father. Maybe even killed, just to be safe to avoid more Wyrmtaint. Of course the male will deal with PTSD, but generally they are expected to get over it eventually.

          For female victims, it is a harder pill to swallow. They aren't to blame, but given they are carrying a child made by a Wyrmish act, they are left with hard choices. Some in more traditional tribes might perform the Rite of Winter Wolf to preserve their honor, or go on a suicide mission. They might try to perform an abortion by claws and silver. Or they can try to raise the child.

          The child will have to live with the stigma of their origin, and be judged as potential betrayers in traditional tribes.

          As for pre-changers, they are going to be blamed still. Mainly because they SHOULD have known the other was a pre-changer, if they had been good kinfolk.

                                  I think incidents of Garou on Garou rape would be much more rare than rape in the real world. Rape happens because the attacker is more physically powerful than the victim. Sexual dimorphism means women are generally less strong than men. In war zones, the men have guns and are in units so multiple men can assist each other. Female Garou do not have such a relative disadvantage. (I know male on male rape also happens in prisons and other places, but again is generally targeted against weaker men and often in combination with numbers).

                                  Garou for the most part are all trained in combat. There is going to be far more female Garou who know how to fight, some very well. They are all going to capable of dealing aggravated damage if they are in Crinos - so even far more powerful Garou know they have the risk of getting some very serious injuries. So there is a much higher likelihood of death for the assailant. And of course there is the issue of auspice and gifts. I find it hard to believe that a male Ragabash, no matter how physically bigger, defeating a female Ahroun. And of course there is frenzy whic is not something that can be controlled and will eithe result in the death of the perpetrator or victim. Criminals look for easy victims, not people who look like they can fight back.

                                  Another issue is that there are unlikely to be any"civilian" Garou just standing about after combat. If another pack or sept is invading, then all the Garou of the pack or sept will be fighting back. If they are defeated, the female Garou is likely either to be dead or in flight with the survivors, not remaining in the area. It is always possible a Garou will be captured, but I don't think that is very likely.

                                  Lastly, the Garou operate under a system of privately enforced justice. There is no official police force and judiciary which can at times, unfortunately, frustrate justice because of limitations of what they can or can't do. If you rape another Garou, that Garou's pack or sept (or possible even local tribemates) is going to come after you. That won't dissuade everyone from such acts, but it is going to be a factor for many.

                                  Since sexual relations between Garou is a huge taboo, I don't see the sept elders back home tolerating this either when the victorious Garou come back home.

                                  I think abducting and raping female kinfolk to be much more likely. They are much more defenseless. They are less likely to lead to feuds as the other side's family would be more likely to accept some kind of deal. And they don't run the risk of violating that part of the Litany.

                                  If rape happens, it is likely to be the result not of physical acts, but assertion of dominance by Pure Breed, intimidation, staredown, etc. that makes the female Garou "naturally" submit to something she wouldn't otherwise. Of course, whether that counts as rape in Garou culture is itself problematic (here different tribes would act differently, I don't think Black Furies would tolerate that while a Shadow Lord would). In such cases, Garou would likely treat it as an ordinary case of Litany violation, not a crime against the victim.

                                  If it does happen regardless of the method, I think the reputation of the victim will probably depend on the tribe or sept. While some victims may be ostracized because they should have fought it off or escaped, I very doubt that the Children of Gaia or Black Furies would find the victim guilty. Even the Get of Fenris, who are seen to be especially devoted to their own people, I believe would side with the victim. On the other hand, tribes particularly obsessed with notions of honor or dislike of Metis, like the Fianna, Silver Fangs, or Shadow Lords, might shame the victim.

                                  As for Metis children resulting from it, if there is such a thing as Garou abortion, I think it is going to be practiced here. I don't think a female Garou is going to carry any baby she doesn't want to carry. And having an abortion might be essential in demonstrating the person was not at fault. Very, very, very few such metis are going to be carried to term.
