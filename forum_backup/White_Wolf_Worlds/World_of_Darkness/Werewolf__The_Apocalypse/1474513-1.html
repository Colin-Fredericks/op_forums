            None of my NPC's think this plan is ideal, and most of the Sept is seriously disturbed by it. The new GE, however, has stated that she's open to better, safer plans. Thus far nobody's come forth with anything viable. As such, she's willing to take the political hit and risk the caern being sullied in exchange for destroying the BSD's and purifying the caern after. Also, the caern totem is Owl, and the way I'm running it Owl's given its blessing for what it perceives to be an ambush that's in line with its own modus operandi.

            But yes, everybody's concerned, there's been a lot of debate about the litany, and the whole plan hinges on the PC's and other sept packs being able to convince foreign septs to back the play. In short, it's proving to be a great way to stir up discussion, provoke my PC's to debate their loyalties, and get them to visit other septs as envoys for a desperate plan.

            As for the former GE shadow lord, this is very dark stuff that he's planning. His deed name, however, is Erases the Foe; he's going to try and remove the current GE *after* her plan has begun to work, so as to kill a rival and inherit the salvaged sept from her, while also disavowing her plan and claiming he'd never have allowed the caern to have been sullied. Is there room for disaster? Yes. Is he opening himself up to further corruption? Quite possibly. But the PC's are going to be the fulcrum of his plan, in that he'll ask them to execute it, and that will, again, prove a catalyst for great RP under tremendous pressure.

            At least, I hope so.

                                A little more nuance:

                                The sept in question is part of a confederacy of septs arranged around the Great Lakes, all of whom have pledged to support each other at least once a year in the face of threats, gather cubs for rites of passage, and gather occasionally to dictate confederacy policy. Each sept gets one vote at these councils.

                                The former SL GE's plan was to create a number of low-ranked septs under his patronage in Detroit so as to develop regional power by means of his increased number of votes. The PC's, being under Mammoth, saw this as a way to increase garou unity by bringing a variety of different tribes to Detroit and helping them all work together. In particular, the SL's tolerance of metis and willingness to welcome all packs as long as they subscribed to his vision appealed to the PC's.

                                The new GW GE, however, just wants to create a massive, powerful, old school sept that wages war on the wyrm. The PC's were dismayed when she won, because they saw their vision sliding down the tubes. Thus they are covertly still supporting the SL, in the hopes of helping him regain power and reinstate his plan.

                                However, they've no idea he's planning to assassinate the GW. My hope is to put them in a bind when the moment comes, as they must decide whether the ends justify the means and agree to the assassination or reject the means and by doing so abandon the vision as well.

                                My hope, therefore, is that the SL comes across as a more complex, nuanced figure than a mere cold hearted bastard; that he appeal to the PC's philosophies and dreams even as he forces them to ask how far they're willing to go to achieve them.

                                    Originally posted by pwtucker View Post
                                    In short:

                                    There are 6 weeks till a huge BSD battle, wherein the new GW athro Grand Elder will lure the BSD's into the caern, retreat, then return with an overwhelming number of allies to crush the BSD's before they can corrupt the caern.

                                    The former GE, a SL adren ahroun called Erases the Foe, is planning to use that chaos to remove the GW athro so that he can return to his position of power. He has 6 weeks to come up with a plan, along with the aid of his adren theurge SL packmate, and possibly a SL elder theurge from another sept who is sympathetic.

                                    Could you guys help me brainstorm ways that sufficiently motivated SL's could try and get away with this nefarious deed? 6 weeks is plenty of time to enact some serious plans, but I've never played high level games, and am at a bit of a loss as to what they might do. Find a way to isolate the GW athro and then summon an incarna of Grandfather Thunder? Find a way to ensure the athro is overrun by the BSD's?

                                    Any suggestions are most welcome.

                                    (Note: 1/2 the PC pack is firmly allied with the SL's, the other 1/2 reluctantly allied with the GW. I'm foreseeing a chance for excellent rp if they have to decide in the heat of the battle which faction to support.)
                                    First of all: allowing the BSD to set foot in the caern is a major Litany violation and most likely those allies will tear this GW apart for just thinking about it. Second: why six weeks? Does this SL conspire with BSD so he knows the time of attack or it's just some form of challenge for GW: stand your ground for 6 weeks and I let you live. And one more thing: you didn't mention this GW auspice so it's a little harder to give you advices. But during one of my games I run a similar situation between Gaillard SL and Ahroun SF, both PCs, both leaders of the packs (mixed tribes, it was some kind of experiment), both trying to win the leadership of the caern for themselves (the leader was dying). In the wake of BSD attack (location of their HQ has just been found so the sept was preparing the counterattack) SL improvised an inspirational speech (about 10 minutes of pure roleplaying, combining Churchil, Patton and football coach techniques) and when the rest of the sept was ready to follow him through hell he pointed out that traditionally it is Ahroun's duty to lead warparties and who would be better leader than Ahroun SF? And the rest agreed. Now the Fang knew it's a trap but couldn't avoid it not loosing the face. So he accepted the burden of the command, the rest of the sept started to follow him (including the SL pack, it was part of his plan) than he said that he will stay behind on a maybe less glorious but equally important post as one of the caern guardians (after his speech there were only a handful of Garou who couldn't go anyway, like Guardian or Ritemaster). And then about the half of the warparty said "HOOOOOLLLLLLLDDDD, he's right" and stayed behind. Still SF had to lead the expedition and of course lost. OK, it's risky (if the Fang was succesful the SL would lose the race for leadership) but still it was one of the best plans I've ever heard, not to mention that it allow the SL to get away with murder.

                                            Just an update: we finally got to the climactic battle for the Sept, and it couldn't have gone better. The players spent time leading up to it forging alliances with other septs, there was lots of grumbling from NPC's about the battle plan and how perilously close it came to relinquishing the Sept, but in the end some 50 garou mustered to launch the counter attack.

                                            All the while, I'd been encouraging the PC's splitting loyalties to the current GW elder and the previous SL. Only one PC caught on, but his attempts to warn the others weren't heeded. Was fascinating to watch - the GW lead through a populist, emotion-based appeal, while the SL was all cold logic and long term planning. The players resonated with one or the other, and over the following sessions aligned themselves accordingly.

                                            While this was going on, the GW asked the PC's to forge an alliance with a contentious wendigo sept, which they accomplished through great RP. The SL's then asked the PC's to acquire a shattered klaive of great historical significance from the Wendigo, promising to try and forge a grand klaive for them as a sign of their investment in the alliance.

                                            Which made the moment when the SL's accosted the PC's mid-battle to retake the sept with the pain jaggling talen based on the klaive shards all the sweeter. They tasked the PC's with paralyzing the GW at a crucial moment, then left.

                                            The pack then imploded.

                                            One PC was so outraged at being manipulated, and so loyal to the GW that he simply ran off, refusing to talk about it. The alpha kept demanding the talen be handed over, but the two-SL aligned PC's refused, with the philodox asking over and over again that they try and find a solution.

                                            Tempers rose, and with time running out, they were forced to join the main battle without hitting on a solution.

                                            The philodox, who'd been the one who'd seen this coming, ended up using the talen at a pivotal moment, leading the GW's horrific death.

                                            The philodox left the pack, as his packmates all felt betrayed, their totem, Mammoth, left the pack, and the other SL-aligned pc was allowed to stay on a provisional member. The pack refused to stay in the sept after the SL retook the GE position, and left for Chicago, where we're about to start a new arc.

                                            Fantastic RP. The players loved it. Super intense, high emotions, huge consequences to their actions. Thanks so much to everyone who helped this come about with their advice - it was worth its weight in gold!
