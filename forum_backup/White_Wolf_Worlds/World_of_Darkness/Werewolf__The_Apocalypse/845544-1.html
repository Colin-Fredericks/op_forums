But revised has a tone, that I often read as the current garou generation blaming their ancestors from ten millenia ago for a lot of things and picturing them in a way, that is without a doubt wyrmish. And that doesn't make sense to me.

                    Originally posted by Asmodai View Post
                    Well. As much as I always insist on the Garou being heroic, and that they are the "Good" guys, you do have to confess that they have a very long history of being total and utter dicks.
                    Maybe, maybe not.

                    Like I said, culling the herd - meaning to kill of the old and sick - keeps the herd strong. Humans didn't understand that, while garou have the "Don't suffer thy people to tend for your sickness"-ruling. So, a forced culling amongst human settlements, once they started to settle, made sense for the garou of that time. The humans were given means to avert the natural order (intelligence), so the garou acted accordingly. and according to some sources, the garou didn't even end the Impergium without a plan. The garou should have tried to educate mankind to live in harmony with Gaias other creatures, or so the 'end the Impergium'-fraction hoped. But that plan failed for the tribes in Europe and Asia minor, while Uktena, Wendigo and Croatans had it figured out quite good, until the Wyrmbringers came.

                    Similarly, the garou remember that they were made Gaias defenders because Wolf sacrificed himself to save Gaia from the Darkness and was rewarded with resurrection for that, after all other animals had failed and not included him due to his rage. So, when their Champion was murdered in a diplomatic mission to the Gurahl (that arguable the Gurahl should have provided save passage for), they reasonably expected that the Gurahl would resurrect the Champion. But they refused, and provided save haven for the alleged murderer. That the garou frenzied and killed the gurahl is tragic, but doesn't make them dicks. Although, we know that the Bubasti was framed for the murder, but the garou didn't know then and don't know today.
                    In any case, whenever a pack of garou would meet a member of another changing breed that was wyrm tainted and they would kill that being, others of the same changing breed would if they later discovered the body only find that garou murdered one of their own. That such an action might have been justified is never discussed.
                    Also, for all that I don't like in revised (and the CoG TB in particular), I like that the Children of Gaia blame themselves for the War of Rage escalating from a isolated incident to an era of mistrust and violence. They, again and again, convinced (with their gifts it is really easy) garou and other changers to meet and end their conflict and every time it blew up in their face. All they managed was to create more reasons for each side to mistrust and hate the other - that is until they stopped and the War of Rage slowly ebbed down.


                    So, what I'm saying is, that the Garou on modern times can very easily paint their pre-historic ancestors in a benevolent light - as they should for they supposedly come from a culture that worships their ancestors. It ain't matter what is true and what is not. It is hard, I'd say outright impossible, to be proud of one's heritage (or people) if it is filled with people who were performing genocide, twice. But since the Setting wants the Garou to be a proud culture, their perception of their peoples past should be presented in a way that allows for that.
