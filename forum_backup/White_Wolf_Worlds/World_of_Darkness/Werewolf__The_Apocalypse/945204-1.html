        Originally posted by Crusher-of-Cities View Post

        But do Lupus-born have high stats in a lot of these because they're lupus, or do they have high stats because they still get full ability dots and have nowhere else to put them? I can't help but wonder how much of this thought process was reverse-engineered from a decade or two of gameplay.

        Remember with dots- a little goes a long way. Sure, wolves are faster than humans. That's why that form gets bonus physical attributes and a x2 running modifier. So a wolf that runs a lot shouldn't be sporting significantly more athletics than a human that runs a lot. Same for Brawl. Does a handful of territorial disputes and dominance displays over the course of 2 years of life really put the lupus on par with a professional boxer or MMA fighter in homid form? It's a big deal to have more than 2 dots in an ability... a 3 is a level of competence on par with a Master's Degree or a seasoned professional. A 4 means someone is as good at something as you're likely to meet in a normal person's life, because 5 is an olympic-level, 'one of the best on the planet'-scale ability.




        But a big questions is "How much intelligence is that worth?" Even assuming most Garou can't/don't finish High School, does the first change impart a Lupus with something actually comparable to 8 hours of education a day, 180+ days a year, for 10-12 years? Sure, we know Lupus can't start with Drive or Firearms or Computers/Technology/etc without spending freebie points... But can they do multiplication? Do they know what bacteria are, how longitude works, that there are seven continents, what lightning is, or what a kidney does? Homid-born from developed nations are sporting somewhere in the vicinity of 15,000 hours of education on very fundamental things that weren't considered even remotely obvious as little as a few centuries ago, and there is so much more to a Homid upbringing than simply understanding the nuances of human society.
        A starting Character does not necissarily represent someone who has just gone the Change. they could have been training for years. A starting Homid could be anything from a 12 yearold to a 35 yearold.. the 35 yearold does not get more points for the 23 more years of life experience. Lets look at Kinfolk they in all ways get fewer points.. even though they have an easier time fitting into society and can easily be college graduates, military men etc. Yet.. fewer points.

        Points have nothing to do with an attempt at simulation and more to do with starting all the players on the same page. Now if you feel that the benefit of Gnosis out weighs the in ability to start with a few things then by all means add more bonuses to playing Homid, but honestly most people do not agree.

                                True. It is explicitly stated with the breed description, that language is a weakness to lupus, however no mechanics are given.
                                As for inter-human basic behaviour I disagree. As a lupus the character has still learned some inter-species behaviour and can adapt certain aspects of wolf behaviour to human interaction. Meaning without knowing the uniform of a cop the lupus can still discern that the person has some kind of authority due to the reactions of others towards the cop, as well as the cop's bearing and mannerisms. Such is not without fail, but this is where empathy, primal-urge and the strangely enough not limited Streetwise augment the wits and intelligence levels the lupus character has.

                                Also, I'm not sure how long it takes to learn basic forms of language. A two year old toddler learns about 10 words a day and can form basic sentences. A suddenly human-minded lupus however has the mind of an adolescent not a toddlers (well, assuming two dots which is human average according to the books). That said, the lupus doesn't forget what he witnessed before the First Change, so he can re-evaluate human speech he heard before his mind developed. Even though, the lupus is akin to someone who is visiting, working and living in a land where a foreign language is spoken. Depending on the language, one can be a decent speaker within 6 month under this conditions (I googled that).

                                Within the confines of the game mechanics, one could deny a lupus a mother tongue and have him learn English as a foreign language. Before W20, that was a linguistics dot, worth 3XP. So, after one month of training the teacher would roll Manipulation + Instructions versus 11 minus the Intelligence of the student. So, assuming stats of 2, in each trait. So according to some internet oWoD probability charts such a roll has still 1.4 successes on average, so after on average three roll (and three months) the are three successes accumulated, allowing for three XP to be spend and therefore learn the language.
                                No idea how W20 handles this, since it is a merit now, but that would be revied Editons mechanic-wise take on learning the language.

                                Also, keep in mind, that all garou cliath are taught Garou Tongue, which doesn't count as foreign language and the process of learning it is brushed over, really.
