    Wolves in the White House

    03-28-2022, 10:21 PM
    So Im running a Werewolf game, in which the characters are part of a extreme conspiracy from a group of garou, that seeing how dire the things are with their enemies, them loosing caerns and numbers and nothing changing, and seeing everything getting worse and worse, decide on something so crazy, and dangerous, that they hope it works: They plan to take control of the USA government. They will do this by making sure a garou or a kinfolk becomes president in the 2020 elections (the starting year is 2016, right after the 2016 elections), plus changing many of the politicians.

    They will do this with a four steps plan:
    1.- Makke Washington DC a safer headquarters. They do this by eliminating local threats.
    2.- Convince Garous all around the nation to give their support and help in the endeavor.
    3.- WIn the elections, using every asset, agent, and trick they can use. If it means taking the Democrat, Republican or even a independant party.
    4.- Now as president, make the changes wanted.

    Right now, they are in Step 1, and the players discovered a number of threats they need to deal with. Note, many of these are taken from the DC by night book, just put in a more modern time.
    1.- The first one they will deal with is the Setites in the city, who have taken over much of the drug trade and the city gangs, while also having members of their cult in the police force. Besides that, there is a arsonist attacking drug dealers and junkies around the city, causing trouble for the Setites and the police.

    2.- They know they need to deal with the vampire sorcerers, or things will be difficult. One garou made contact with a disgruntled tremere neonate, who has mentioned about one of the members being a freaking Nazi vampire (yes, that is a npc in DC by Night).

    3.- They found out decisive evidence that members of the congress have ties to the wyrm, specifically, the head of Public Relations of Pentex and head of the Pentex lobby group, who has deals with politicians.

    4.- The sabbath, of course. They also made accidental contact with one of Marcus Vitel agents, and now they know he is a important sabbath, and unfortunately now Vitel knows the garou are planning something.

    The assets they have obtained so far is a possible alliance with the Gangrels (hey, they are already planning to take over the presidency, alliance with some vampires is the least issue now), alliance with a group of verbenas, discovered a caern in ROck Creek Park, and know about a group of organized vampire hunters, and are planning to make more alliances as needed.

    First, what effect would finishing the first part (The setites) would have on the next steps of the first plan?

    Second, what else could there be in washington they could use or deal with? And how?

    And what ideas could you offer for the next steps of the Werewolves plans to take control of the government?

    ​Also, opinions, ideas or suggestions?

              It seems like you need to consider how much the base plan doesn't work even without supernatural interference.

              The US President is the head of the executive branch. Despite huge moves towards increasing executive power over the past century, just controlling the presidency doesn't mean you control the legislature or judiciary. The POTUS can't make laws, only sign them, and try to use their discretion in applying already passed laws.

              Unless the PCs are going to completely overthrow the US government and turn the president into an authoritarian/totalitarian position in hopes of keeping control over it long enough to institute the changes they want... there's a huge amount of work to get done besides winning one election.

                                  Originally posted by happilyevil321 View Post
                                  Thus, making them easier to replace with someone the garou chooses.
                                  Again though, it's not that easy. If, for example, a Senator from West Virginia that's supposed to be on your side but puts his fossil fuel family interests first, just getting him out of office in Washington DC only gets you a replacement from the same West Virginia political machine that produced him in the first place. You don't get to pick his replacement via the federal executive. Depending on the level of change your PCs are after, you might need to control up to 75% (rounded up!) of the state political apparatuses on top of the federal one (that would be to amend the Constitution). The system, despite all its flaws, is actually designed specifically to resist one part of the government from getting too much power. Even one party having nominal majority control over the government (like right now) doesn't just lead to them getting everything they want (though supernatural influence might have prevented a BBB fiasco).

                                  Now, the plans you're talking about are easier for reshaping the judiciary, because the federal judiciary is nominated by the executive (or by the higher level judiciary that was selected by the executive), and approved by the Senate. As long as you control the presidency you decide who becomes a federal judge/justice, even if some of your picks might not pass the Senate, they can't pick someone different, it'll just sit empty. A little supernatural push on the process could be enough to pack the judiciary with friendly jurists, and the judiciary (esp. at the highest levels) is fairly small (9 on the Supreme Court, 179 between sixteen Appeals courts, all of which only need a simple majority so you don't even need to control every seat) allowing some secretive backroom influence on them as well.

                                  Just more stuff to keep in mind besides finding over which supernatural is whispering in which politician's ear.

It should also be noted that Baltimore actually has a heavy Akashic presence. While the Akashics probably wouldn't play a day to day role, they would be able to respond quickly (in about 20 minutes) in case of emergency.

                                      On the conventional side, there actually aren't as many in D.C. proper as one might expect, but N.W.O. and the Progenitors both have heavy presence around the city, with the Syndicate probably being a distant third. Interestingly, these are the three conventions that will be most likely to notice and act if the Setites lose their influence in the city.

House Leanhaun will be in the city lobbying on behalf of the national endowment for the arts, but they will also be getting into the drug trade.. interestingly the Leanhaun would likely be willing to work with the Garou, but if the Garou have a Fiona (or Liam) ally, they may have to hide the Leanhaun from them.

                                                  Yeah, as CajunKhan said I think you must limit somehow the scope of this scenario.

                                                  Going full crossover may be lots of fun for some stories, but when you're already making a high profile political heist covering about twelve years (1 presidential term for prep, then two for action) it will be overwhelming.

                                                  Now you need to make a very important decision: what do you want to take out of this scenario? Do you want to explore the political themes? Focus on the Wyrm influences? Or work DC as a focus point for supernatural factions? You may work with two of those, tops, unless you really have a deep understanding of them all, wants to challenge your players royally and think they're up to the task.

                                                  As it stands, it seems like the best course of action is to either greatly simplify how the political machine and government institutions really work, or to ignore splat crossover. And given the scenario, I think doing the first would loose an incredible opportunity for a very different and interesting game.

                                                  If you decide to go for the political game based on real politics route, studying the system, even if just through watching YT channels like Legal Eagle and Last Week Tonight will help a lot. You could make it a point of the chronicle to have, in the process, the PCs learning just how HUGE their goal actually is and amend their plans to cover ever more aspects of the system. They need to cover the Judiciary, Legislative and Executive, they also have to understand how state agencies and semi-independent administrative institutions work, the constitution and jurisprudence and minor laws that put checks and balances over all those people, the intricate balances of dealing with lobbyists, with how the Federal system interacts with the State system (and can't simply overrule them) and so on.

                                                  Huge, complex, messy, with lots of interplay and the possibility of comparison with how things work in other countries. It is very interesting on itself, and would have a ton of things for the PCs to learn and deal with without simple answers, but still open for huge changes if they put the effort and their supernatural strengths to it.
