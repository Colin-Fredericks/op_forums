Keep in mind, too, that Seventh Generation, Pentex and its subsidiaries, and various other factions are solely or almost entirely staffed by men in leadership positions, who continually evade justice for the atrocities that they commit.                                     Also, even if the Furies may have some grip on feminist garou (and kinfolks) I think that's important to remember that their true inspiration are the Amazons and the greek culture, so there is no need to actually pour all the modern biases over the tribe. It's still a game.
                                    They're decidedly non-Greek in almost every way, though. Pure Breed no longer requires characters to have Greek features (which it only seemed to do in Revised, since Julisha of the Thousand Masks had it at four dots, and she's South African, with no trace of European features at all). The Order of the Merciful Mother isn't infiltrating the Greek Orthodox church, it's focusing on the Roman Catholic Church. Their names are most frequently cast after revolutionaries from around the world, not just from Greece. Even the threefold fate/goddess thing isn't exactly something that marks them as particularly Greek, since it was held in common with several cultures, stretching all the way into India and, from there, likely anywhere to which Buddhism spread (incidentally, A World of Rage explicitly placed the Furies as one of the tribes in India). As with the Get, the Furies are so opposed to the culture that they supposedly came from that you can hardly even call them a dedicated part of it.

                                    Also, are you sure you know which biases are modern and which are not? If you tie the Furies to some kind of faux-wiccan reconstruction of pagan European rituals, you done goofed, much like the attempts to move the Fenrir back into being racist viking caricatures, which are themselves created by thoroughly modern sensibilities and attempts at historical revisionism. If something popped up starting with the Victorians, and it doesn't seem to have existed before them, odds are good that it's garbage.
