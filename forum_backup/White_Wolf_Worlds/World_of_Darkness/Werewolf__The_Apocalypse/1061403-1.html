                        Horses are much more powerful than White Wolf gives them credit for, so I think that the Hybrid and Equine forms would be much more powerful than people give them credit. Large horses are much stronger than any large carnivore like a bear, large horses can kill humans with a single kick if they are feeling hateful, so large horses should probably be considered to have a Strength 8. The only large carnivore that is as powerful as a large horse is a tiger, which is why carnivores tend to go after injured or sick horses because they do not want to get injured or killed.

                        Two war forms would be interesting. You could have a homid, hybrid, centaur, and equine form. I would make the Hybrid form at Strength+6/Dexterity+1/Stamina+6/Perception+1, Centaur form at Strength+3/Dexterity+1/Stamina+3/Double Movement/Two Homid Hands, Equine form at Strength+4/Dexterity+1/Stamina+4/Perception+1/Double Movement. Since the Equine Fera would have no natural weapons, they would have no inherent way of dealing aggravated damage with their unarmed attacks (though they would deal lethal damage with their unarmed attacks). Unlike the Garou, who are the Warriors of Gaia, they would be the Defenders of Gaia and would focus around Gifts that enhance defense, detection, and mobility, and I would suggest that their Rage be similar to the Gurahl.
