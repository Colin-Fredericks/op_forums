    Originally posted by TwoDSix View Post

    From a gameplay perspective? That's fine, and I'd argue better '0 Hunger=ideal'. Now you have a choice, do you keep your Rage low and risk not having power when you need it, or do you keep it high and risk it causing problems. It becomes an issue if players have very little control over their rage, but at it's core it's solid.

    Of course you could design the game around wildly varying rage, with werewolves swinging between heights of barely controlled power and horrific lows. But you'd probably want to reorient the rules so that every single roll can cause massive changes to the state of the game and story.

    Basically IMO the main mechanical issue is that they were building the game with the wrong tools.
    It is the lack of player control that really ruins W5's Rage. That and the 'brilliant' idea to make Rage dice mandatory and so they all have to be used.

    And, still, the tiiny tiny pool XD

                Originally posted by MrornX View Post

                Well what big teeth actually treats rage as a sliding bar that makes doing volient actions easier and non volient action harder.

                But that's kinda off topic for the thread...
                Actually, I think What Big Teeth serves as an excellent example of my issues. What Big Teeth is designed to be a depiction of the misery a minimum wage minority worker faces where the werewolf is a method of safely venting all that frustration. The game has mechanics for each type of wrong the characters face in their human lives.

                But Hungry Names? It has the job and the human stuff front and centre with very little point other than get money and fill up your Rage. The game isn't about the struggle like Big Teeth is, nevermind how it railroads you by making some big assumptions of what you'd do. Your PC is only complaining about freezing because they aren't allowed to shift to lupus, they cannot hunt their own food or just STEAL the things they need.

                Mind, none of this is bad writing, it is just focusing something that could be easily handwaved.
