                          They know that an animistic spirit world is real, but any Garou without blinders on also knows there are plenty of evidence that the Garou view is not solely correct. True Faith exists, and even though Garou probably run into it less often than vampires, they know that the power True Faith displays have to come somewhere. The Garou are very arrogant and likely dimissive of it, but anyone with an open mind realizes that there is either something about Tellurian they don't know, or that there is a lot more to the universe than just their view.

                                          I don't know I can see a lot of Garou not buying a great deal of the Garou's/Tribe's lore as gospel. I've also seen this as a bit of a weakness of WtA as a line, as it tries to push that various Tribes focused on certain people's, but also seems to ignore or avoid any concept that people might hold a modern faith dear and call BS on some of the stuff they are taught. I mean it really seems there would be, maybe not a lot, but some individuals that denied the concept of animistic totems and reflavored various spirits to be, (in a simplistic sense) angels or demons. Seemed like a particularly noticeable omission and lacking area that for the Tribes that put so much emphasis on cultural heritage, they forget that modern religions exist or where intrinsic to many of those cultures. Not the old pagan ones, (unless they are a neo-pagan), but rather those that exist still.

                                          Alternatively, religious or not, I can see a lot of Children of Gaia and Bone Gnawers holding Christmas in high regard, and not for any pagan origins, but rather because it represents things they hold dear, (helping the needy, giving, community, etc. . .)

                                          I imagine that the Black Furies have a very divided view on Mother's Day/Father's Day, especially as neither are too universal holidays.

                                          Personally, I tend to take the Whedon approach to Halloween. It's the night all self respecting monsters take off. Only newbs do anything on Halloween, with the exception of Wraiths, and some Mages. :P
