                                        I wrote a really long post about playing a lupus in another threat, so I'm just going to link that here rather than repost it. I disagree that lupus characters should be banned, or that they are not as functional as other breeds. ALL garou have a learning curve after their first change. A homid learning how to be in tune with gnosis, navigate the spirit world, and/or embrace their wolf instincts isn't easy either. Furthermore, all garou are literally made for a purpose, both in general as a changing breed and per their auspice. Certain things will come naturally to them because of that. Lastly, lupus are fucking important. Garou are as much wolf as human, and if they lose the that side of them completely, the ramifications will be huge. If lupus as a breed die out and garou lose their wolf kinfolk, in my opinion, they will begin to lose the wolf form itself. Their spiritual connection to the natural world and the very meaning of what it is to be part wolf will eventually be lost to them. One ability that I think describes this is Primal Urge. Homid have to learn how to use and trust the feral side of their nature, and doing so has a direct impact on their ability to change forms on top of the other uses of the ability. Lupus are their biggest link to that. Now, sure, all this doesn't necessarily mean that the breed is easy to play. I would agree that it isn't for everyone, especially new players. But if the breed and its significance is ignored or left out completely? You're missing out on a huge chunk of the game and what it means to be garou, and you're just playing humans with super powers. Lupus present a challenge, but an interesting and worthwhile one in my opinion.

                                        Metis is another issue entirely, and I have very conflicted feelings on them as a breed. Mainly, I hate how they are handled in the game in terms of having deformities and being socially ostracized. While the parallels between metis and people who are disabled, have mental health issues, or are otherwise stigmatized socially can make for interesting commentary, the overlay of sin and spiritual corruption and whatnot is just kind of too far for me. While I get that its a great example of how awful garou can be, the fact that there are so many holes in the logic surrounding them and so much willingness to eschew critical thinking in favor of blind hatred just drives me crazy. It's realistic... garou ARE fanatics. I just... bleh. At some point garou are painted in such a horrible light that it feels impossible to cast them as protagonists at all, World of Darkness or not. Their self-righteousness becomes TOO intolerable given their cumulative history of being murderous, stupid, unrepentant fuck-ups.

                                        I admit that it's also personal. The themes hit too close to home for how carelessly I feel they are handled (both by the game and many storytellers and players), so its just not my cup of tea most of the time. I feel inclined to ban them from future games, and sometimes consider writing them out completely. The pros generally don't outweigh the cons for me.

                                        On the other hand, I think the breed has potential if approached from a particular angle. I personally like to attribute the existence of metis to the effects of the Wyld. Garou have a stronger connection with the Wyld than the other parts of the triat on a very fundamental level, and some definitely tribes acknowledge this. Their very nature as shapeshifters is wyld-ish. My head-canon is that metis come about because when two garou procreate, the wyld energy from both of them becomes too concentrated and mutates the offspring. The resulting metis has some flaws to reflect this, and are just a little less stable in general - which could even mean that their mutable nature is more susceptible to wyrm taint, sure. But the upside is the potential to embrace that Wyld energy and use it. The metis breed gifts really scream this to me. If I were playing a metis, I would make it a point of the character to explore that connection. A male metis Black Fury would be super interesting specifically because of that, given the tribe's reverence for the Wyld. Even then you can't get away from them being treated like shit, but at least it gives you something else to focus on.

                                        I will also say one last thing about Metis and rage... dealing with something from birth doesn't mean you are less capable of dealing with it as an adult; many times the opposite. If a metis has made it through childhood and adolescence, on through their first change and rite of passage, without getting themselves killed because of their Rage, even while being harassed and abused, I'd bet they have a better mastery on their Rage than a comparable garou of another breed. It takes a lot of willpower to deal with Rage for that long and not snap under the pressure of being treated horribly. Many metis don't make it - but the ones that do, the ones that players would play, should have their shit together.
