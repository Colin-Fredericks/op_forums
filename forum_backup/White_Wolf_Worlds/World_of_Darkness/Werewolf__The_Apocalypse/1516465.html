          This is just getting dull and predictable. They're even trying to force emulation of generic werewolf movies by making Garou howl nightly at the moon in order to recover from Losing the Wolf, which happens in every frenzy, according to the past previews. In other words, the expected outcome of staying in Crinos is losing the ability to shapeshift with 100% certainty. The incentives are getting awfully perverse for a werewolf game, unless it's the "chain yourself up in the basement" style of werewolf game.

          W:tA wasn't supposed to be a generic werewolf flick. It was supposed to be a new thing, its own, with more powerful and in some ways less troubled werewolves than what was the par for the course at the time. W5 is working hard to undo this distinctiveness, as well as just copy over Vampire mechanics, in this case Hunger and messy results. And then it goes so far as to injure characters from accumulating "too much" Rage.

          Is this really supposed to be better than just letting temporary Rage rise above permanent more often? Or giving bonus dice to Rage rolls while in Crinos? If you wanted characters that exploded from too many successes on dice rolls, well, you already had that in the earlier iterations of the rules. Just have them roll Rage instead of some other dice pools. Staying in Crinos becomes a different kind of show of self-control, and still allows for klaive use.

                                          Oh and let's consider this paradox: Because of the way it's set up one of the best ways to lower your Rage quickly is shifting into Crinos. After all, you need to do two Rage-checks (so it's a potential -2 Rage). Not only that... If you attempt to shift into Crinos with less than Rage 3, two bad Rage-checks will immediately drop you out of Crinos again because two failed Rage-checks with Rage 1 or 2 means you "lost the wolf"/dropped to Rage 0. So it isn't just the WP-cost and Frenzy-risk that makes you avoid Crinos, the lack of Rage plus potential failed Rage-checks also disincentivize shifting into Crinos!

                                          At the same time having more than Rage 2 means all rolls are potentially set up for "Brutal Outcomes" (two or more 1s/2s on your Rage-dice) which means whatever you wanted to do fails - unless it's for the sake of a violent outcome.
