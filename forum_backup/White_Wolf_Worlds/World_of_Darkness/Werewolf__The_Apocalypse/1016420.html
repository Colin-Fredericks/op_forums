    Communing with spirits

    12-13-2016, 02:13 AM
    So I have just finished a deep absorption of CB20, and the following has occurred to me - Ajaba, Corax, Kitsune, Gurahl, Nuwisha, Mokole, Ratkin, Camazotz, Grondr, members of the Ahadi and the Beast Courts all have access to the Gift 'Spirit Speech' (all at level 1, some only in factions within the breed, though for some reason Kitsune are the exception and get access at Rank 2). So how do Ananasi, Bastet, Nagah, Rokea and Apis commune with spirits? My ST is puzzled and cannot find an answer, so these breeds are all getting the Gift on their general lists at Level 1.

                  Off the top of my head:
                  Ananasi don't need to speak to spirits. They get their Gifts directly from Ananasa (via their seelie)
                  Bastest can and do teach each other Gifts. And can steal the Gift Spirit Speech from another Fera. They also learn Gifts from Jamak.
                  Nagah might be similar to the Ananasi, learning Gifts from the Wani.
                  Rokea, if I remember correctly, just talk to Spirits since in the Ocean the Gauntlet is different. Remora are spirits that work with the Rokea without the need for a Gift. And Rorqual can assist them as well.

                  No idea about the Apis

                                  Ehhhhhh... So, my $.02 is that yes, that is true -- but the same does not necessarily hold as true for the shapeshifters. One of the Gurahl is explaining why it's so much harder for the werebears to step sideways in the old Gurahl tribebook, and basically says that when Gaia created the Gurahl, She intended for them to be much more focused on the physical plane than the spiritual one.                                   ​
                                  Originally posted by Matt the Bruins fan
                                  Aren't spirits able to understand and communicate in human speech, even if some might pretend they can't to avoid talking to people they're not fond of?
                                  ​Sometimes. The rule of thumb is that if a spirit is an urban spirit, it will generally know the local human dialect (or dialects, plural, if the area is highly multilingual). Or if the spirit is associated with a specific culture, it will generally know that culture's language (example: American Dream will definitely know English -- but may get thrown if you use certain words like "petrol" instead of "gas" or "lift" instead of "elevator". Alternately Kwakwadjec (Wolverine) will probably know any of the American Indian tribal languages of tribes that historically communed with him -- with the quirk of knowing the "dead" languages of obscure tribes that no longer speak them, while remaining ignorant of major tribal languages -- like Navajo or Cherokee -- who never held him in their spiritual pantheon).

                                  Most other spirits will generally not know human languages.
