        PC has always been something of a pejorative... it's just the shift from a left/progressive internal critique of those that are overly dogmatic or focused on establishment/status quo politics, to the shift to an external critique on the right towards the left in general.

        Though really, regardless of usage, I will hold up Beast the Primordial as exhibit A that Onyx Path is more than willing to take some serious risks along these lines.

        Also, lets not forget that W20 Changing Ways was supposed to be the capstone in Stew Wilson's run on WtA, but turned into a slog he basically admitted to phoning in because of the edits WW demanded from him drove him to give up on his last book. It really shouldn't be anything other than a warning of one of the pitfalls of Paradox/WW's approach to licensing out supplements for the 5th edition stuff. Sometimes you get CbN5, sometimes you get W20 Changing Ways, depending on how well the Paradox team and the developer get on the same page. And licensing out the corebook itself is going to make it that much harder to make sure everyone gets on their page regardless of how well you think any given publisher can handle WtA's politics.

                            I dislike W20 for a some of design choices. But I always assumed, V20 was the last White Wolf product and probably long in the making, and W20 was OPP and probably a bit rushed by OPP, finding it's bearing. I really liked that W20 Changing Breeds was up on google drive and everyone could comment. Some of my comments made it into the final edit. 'Rage across the world' saw not that much involvement of fans, but we saw it before it was final, told that the Den Parent was missing, and got an extra paragraph. Directly contradicting 'Guardians of the Caerns', but ...

                            Now, after the discussion here I opened Changing Ways PDF for the first time and jumped from all mentions of 'gay' and 'homosexuallity' to see what all the fuss is about and ... argh.

                            Why are the rage driven hypocrites suddenly all inclusive of homosexuality amongst their own kind. For that is what I read there. I mean, it would be totally okay if they accepted homesexuality between garou/human and garou/kin, even garou/wolf couples. But, one fundamental tenet of the Litany was the "garou shall not mate with garou" thing-y and as so many older supplements pointed out, it wasn't a rule solely existing because of Metis. Hell, it was indicated the law was older than the first metis in the quais-Metis-Breebook that was the Metis chapter of "Guardians of the Caerns".

                            Just like the very first thing, that caught my eye in Changing Ways (contradicting again 'Guardians of the Caern' in regards to shifting while pregnant), I get the distinct impression that W20 authors either didn't care for already established material or outright ignored it.

                            So, it reinforces me feeling that I'm not handled as a serious reader, but just fed the opinion of another author (team), on a given subject. Would if have hurt to create sidebar and stat "here we changed something from <insert book>, we did it because".
                            I can only reinforce, every time, how well V20 handled sidebars with information like what will happen to the Ravnos in canon, and TB Black Furies revised, where sidebars told us "hey, change to Medusa and the morphing-plague as plot hooks, not meta-plot, use them if you like."
