    Honestly, though... should all things be equal? (Homid-born)

    08-06-2016, 09:33 PM
    I understand that it's a game and blah blah blah.... but let's be honest for a minute. Does it make sense that a 2 year old Lupus or an 8 year old Metis start with the same number of ability dots as an 18 year old human who's probably had 12 years of education, and possibly even a job or two by now?

    Given that the other breeds have generally better gifts and higher starting Gnosis (which DOES make sense... but we're talking 4 or 8 Freebie points of value, here), do you think Homid should get some kind of bump for the sake of balance and/or realism?

      First, not necessarily 18 years old for homid. Garou very likely change around puberty, so possibly a few years earlier than that. And from first change to starting character (rank 1) is a ways down the line. Somewhere between a few months to a couple of years depending on the game. It's in that time, training to get to rank 1 that I figure Garou pick up a bunch of their skill points (putting them above the 11/7/4 of kinfolk).

      That being said...eh. I don't have a problem with it. The animal breeds should naturally have higher ratings in a number of abilities (athletics, primal urge, brawl and survival come to mind off the top of my head) and so this distribution works out fine.

      Also, I don't agree that other breeds get better gifts than homids.
      Oh...and from my experience, most players play homids anyways, so they can't be too unfavorably unbalanced I would think.

                                          I usually rule, that in character backgrounds the Curse should start with the First Change, at the latest. Usually it becomes evident before that. However, I usually explain new players, that the Curse increases gradually, and is not from zero at maximum over night.
                                          Game mechanics require in tabletop, that a Ahroun always has at least 5 Rage at character creation, which is usually just after the Rite of Passage. A fact that has been discussed a lot in these forums (and the forums on the old White Wolf Page). We'll see if the character creation process is changed in that regard in the next edition of the game...


                                          As for the 13/9/5 or 11/7/4 abilities. Lupus are limited on what they are allowed to choose, leading to higher stats in seemingly "inherent" abilities, like Primal-Urge or Brawl.
                                          So, for our larp we have a all-xp-based creation system, that makes this differences obsolete. Also, we usually don't allow lupus.

                                                          But do Lupus-born have high stats in a lot of these because they're lupus, or do they have high stats because they still get full ability dots and have nowhere else to put them? I can't help but wonder how much of this thought process was reverse-engineered from a decade or two of gameplay.

                                                          Remember with dots- a little goes a long way. Sure, wolves are faster than humans. That's why that form gets bonus physical attributes and a x2 running modifier. So a wolf that runs a lot shouldn't be sporting significantly more athletics than a human that runs a lot. Same for Brawl. Does a handful of territorial disputes and dominance displays over the course of 2 years of life really put the lupus on par with a professional boxer or MMA fighter in homid form? It's a big deal to have more than 2 dots in an ability... a 3 is a level of competence on par with a Master's Degree or a seasoned professional. A 4 means someone is as good at something as you're likely to meet in a normal person's life, because 5 is an olympic-level, 'one of the best on the planet'-scale ability.

                                                          Originally posted by heinrich View Post

                                                          But a big questions is "How much intelligence is that worth?" Even assuming most Garou can't/don't finish High School, does the first change impart a Lupus with something actually comparable to 8 hours of education a day, 180+ days a year, for 10-12 years? Sure, we know Lupus can't start with Drive or Firearms or Computers/Technology/etc without spending freebie points... But can they do multiplication? Do they know what bacteria are, how longitude works, that there are seven continents, what lightning is, or what a kidney does? Homid-born from developed nations are sporting somewhere in the vicinity of 15,000 hours of education on very fundamental things that weren't considered even remotely obvious as little as a few centuries ago, and there is so much more to a Homid upbringing than simply understanding the nuances of human society.
