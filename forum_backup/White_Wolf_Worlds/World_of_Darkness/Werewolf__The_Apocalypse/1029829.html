      For the glyphs on the Klaive, I'm not sure what it ultimately says but this is how I interpret it literally, from top (hilt) to bottom (blade tip):

      'Star' with 'moon' and fangy emphatics that might imply glowing, or enclosed by. There's a small marking within the moony glyph, not exactly sure what it might be in context.

              For the ST screens, I have Book of the Wyrm W20 (BotW20) and Dark Ages Vampire (DAVtM). When I get my Shattered Dreams ones I'll add if it's the same or different as the BotW20 one.

              Overall, I like the matte finish of DAVtM better and it's held up really well for how old it is.
              BotW20 is definitely prettier, and stands up better, more solidly on its own. I usually have to set up some kind of stabilizing thing for the DAVtM. They're the same height, which is nice and tall for maximum ST skulduggery.

              As an actual screen and not just a side reference, I like BotW20 better since it can hold itself up more reliably, but I do have some issues with it, which I talk about below.

              DAVtM is made with a single player thick paper that has been printed directly on to.
              BotW20 is made from three layers - the interior and the exterior are thin, glossy paper that has been printed on and then attached to a thicker core, with the layers all cut so the edges of the screen are exposed.

              For comparison, if you're familiar with them, the D&D 5e DM screens are basically the same style as the BotW20 screen, except that they are sturdier/thicker/denser and wrap their glossy printed parts around the edges.

              I have some concerns about the BotW20 because I've opened it up about three times, including just now while I was comparing them, and one of the folding edges is already showing separation between the glossy printed exterior and its core (the curse of WW binding lives on). Also because the edges are all cut and not wrapped like the D&D screens or like the core book covers, the printed layers are already starting to peel and get 'shelf wear' despite the incredibly minimal handling I've done with it. The screen has been folded up, laying on top of shelves books.

              The DAVtM screen, despite being a lot lighter weight paper, feeling and generally being more flimsy, only a little more shelf wear and softening of the edges, corners, and joints than the BotW20 screen, and it has actually been used in games. The BotW20 hasn't yet. I opened it when I got it to check it out, again when I was showing it someone, and just now. Each time it's been refolded and put away afterward.

              Those criticisms aside, I really like ST/DM screens and would still get another one from the W20 line, but I'm definitely going to do something to seal the edges and reinforce the corners, and will do that will any others I get.

              Pictures in the spoilers:

              DAVtM on the outside, BotW20 in the middle, and a 5e for comparison on the inside. You can see how the DAVtM is leaning and doesn't keep itself straight upright. The thicker core on the BotW20 supports it and keeps it straight, although it tries to close itself unless something is there to prop the panels apart. It will probably flex and get used to staying open if I opened it more, but opening it enough to get it trained to do that also makes the layer separation at the other corner (shown in the second spoiler) worse, so I'm going to wait to reinforce it before I do that.
