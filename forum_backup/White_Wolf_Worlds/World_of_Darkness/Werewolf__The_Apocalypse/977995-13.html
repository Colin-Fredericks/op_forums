    We are actually dealing with a similar issue with my fiance's younger sibling, who announced that he was a man despite having identified as a girl until last year (the younger sibling is XX and, according to their mother, was perfectly physiologically female). He (using the masculine gender pronoun out of respect for his right to choose) is developing his own masculine gender identity, but he is focusing on developing the most macho and misogynistic aspects of masculinity, which is making it hard for his relatives to take him seriously because he is becoming a caricature of a man rather than a 'real' man.

    It is odd contrasting his experience with the experience of the transwomen I have known. They have sacrificed male privilege because they wish to become what they see themselves as, which are women. He is trying to grab onto male privilege, and it is a bit disconcerting because he identifies with the worst parts of that privilege.

    While Black Furies may or may not accept the transwomen among their Kinfolk as 'women', I imagine that they would find transmen to be a betrayal of all of their beliefs. What do you all think?

        I don't identify with the gender I was born at birth, but even if I didn't feel the need to transition I would not have felt it encapsulates what being a man means. Just as I don't feel like all of the behaviors associated with women define being a woman, even the ones I value. Being recognized as a woman is meaningless if we're using gender to define ourselves, because if I tell someone else I'm a woman, what I just communicated is what they understand women to be, not my position on the matter.         Well, agender is a thing, along with a whole host of nonbinary gender terms, if you feel neither man or woman is an accurate description of your gender identity.

If I base my identity on gender the way it's presented to me, with all of the problems I outlined above, then it's not even that I am agender, I am all the genders and none of the genders because there is no definitive example for which to base an argument, perspective, or feeling. It basically turns me into Schrodinger's Cat.

                                                    I think the past few pages have highlighted important issues in the world of darkness

                                                    1- There is a massive transgender community here, both as general players and especially as the diehards on the forums. Many among this community attrach themselves to world of darkness because of it's strong attachment to narrative; It gives others the chance to rollplay anywhere along the gender spectrum (admitedly also as supernatural monster) and it has a strong appeal for this community for that reason.

                                                    2- World of darkness is a world like ours that sensationalizes every shitty aspect about our world. More divide, more hate, more corruption.

                                                    3- Building on points 1 and 2, some people don't want to deal with stuff that makes people uncomfortable. They just want to have fun. And that's totally fine and all, but...

                                                    4- maybe the trangender community feels more vulnerable or just has a really strong playerbase, but gender issues seems to become more progressive in the world of darkness... And from a literrary standpoint... That's a counterproductive technique. The world of darkness works on a princible device of identifing shitty things and sensationalizing them. IE the financial world is run by vampires, your job was made shitty to feed banes by an evil megacorp. the media is intentionally filling your head with shit to satisfy corperate interests, a great triatic evil, and a mage's paradigm. In the world of darkness, we're regressive, not progressive, and those guys that whine about 'the current generation' might as well be right.
                                                    It's really, really fucking weird when you make exceptions to this. There was a portion of the book of the wyrm that explained Magadon pharmaceuticals and their evil branches, then had a bit about woman's health and how the 'feminist' research lead wasn't knowingly working for wyrm corp. Like what the fuck is anyone supposed to take away from that? Are we trying to say contraceptives, birth control and feminists are evil? Are we trying to say that an evil megacorp is being turned around by feminists? Why is something without a sinister secret agenda put in between "miracle cures that're addictive make you sicker" and "abominable attempts at making new changing breeds ". Are you not aware of how toxic such an anecdote is in a piece that otherwise works towards a literary theme?
                                                    It's backwards, but if you put in stuff like "Go transsexualism, get empowered" amongst a setting of utter corruption and shit, well, you're kinda putting that empowering stuff in the pile of things you're finding abominable. You don't put good things in a setting about problems. Maybe people will confuse your meaning think you're trying to say that transexual empowerment is a problem just like fracking and warmongering. Black Furies excluding men was never supposed to be a good thing, it's like Talons being kill-humans or the Get being elitist, or the silver fangs relying on klaives,pure breed and ancestors to get them through a conversation. Having transgender black furies would need to open up the doors for all manner of other changes in context: like the Fianna could stop drinking themselves into depression or the bone gnawers could start catering for the lower-middle class.

                                                    Signatures are for terrible people who want to be caught.
