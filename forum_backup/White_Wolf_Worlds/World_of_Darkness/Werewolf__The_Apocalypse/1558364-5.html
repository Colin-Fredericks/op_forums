                                    Well, that's a perfectly vague appeal.

                                    Anyone that tells you that anything about Norse religion can be strictly stated as accurate (instead of things like possible or plausible) has some serious proving to do to you before you parrot what they say. Our current knowledge of what pre-Christian Norse beliefs were is extremely fragmented and difficult to parse from the influence of later scholars who were the primary sources of records of Norse myths.

                                    In the extant texts we have, the Valkyries are predominantly presented as riding on horses if anything. The animal they are most associated with (in a plurality at best) were swans. While there are certainly texts that associate them with wolves and ravens but that's difficult to disentangle from their service to Odin; as Odin is very associated with wolves and ravens. While there is quiet probably a text that describes the Valkyries as riding flying wolves, it would be come from a tradition of epic poetry and would need some serious analysis to separate artistic license from transmission of cultural beliefs about them.

                                    Of course, we have more than texts. We have surviving artifacts such as stones and amulets that depict female warriors that seem to part of the Valkyrie mythological tradition. Everyone of those that has one riding something, it's a horse.

                                    If you want to go write some fantasy stories and want to spice up your Valkyries without stray from things with basis in Norse mythology? Sure, make them wolf riders (or boar riders, or bear riders, or just give them each different big tough animals that help communicate their individual personalities). What to make a point about how to handle these things in WtA? Yeah, that's not really going to fly.

                                    I've also recently written up exactly why the C20 prodigals section (and it's 2e precursor) are best ignored in crossover in a different thread if you'd like a larger break down of that. But the basic point is simple: C20 contradicts the other games frequently in that section make it a detriment to attempts to merge the games to include it as it creates more problems that it even tries to solve.
