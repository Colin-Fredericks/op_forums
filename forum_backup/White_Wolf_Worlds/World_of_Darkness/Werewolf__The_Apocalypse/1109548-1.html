                            You really can't estimate the crinos form's weight by scaling up a human form to 9 feet tall and adding muscle. With their huge arm span and general bulkiness, they seem to be built more like gorillas than humans. The thickness and density of their bones, muscles, and other organs is probably an apples to oranges comparison against humans or wolves, or really any natural species.

                            I expect the Delirium would still have a serious effect on humans. It's a deeply rooted primal fear, so even if the encounter is with a dead body, horror, confusion, and denial would set in, though the human witness might not be as likely to flee for his life, or drop into a fetal position and hope the dead body doesn't attack him. If some ordinary human starts getting nosy, you might want to pass off the body as something like a grizzly bear or a large feral hog, since those can be pretty gigantic themselves. Anything a human sees that he shouldn't see could be converted in his memory by the Delirium into something like that, and the power of suggestion could help. Just don't let any high-willpowered biologists or conspiracy theorists get in your way, or you might have to add another dead body to your tally.
