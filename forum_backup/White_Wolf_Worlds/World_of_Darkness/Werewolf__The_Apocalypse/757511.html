              Originally posted by Mercurial View Post
              Twister, Earthquake, and Volcano all are credited for being of the Wyld because they release the potential in something and prepare it for a new existence. But isn't that what the Wyrm is supposed to do? The Wyrm destroys, the Wyld grows, the Weaver is. Shouldn't they be like Uktena; uncorrupted Wyrm Totems?
              Like Heavy Arms said, that is a bit of a misperception, vis-a-vis Wyld vs. Wyrm. To clarify further: in the physical world, much more so than in the abstract realms, almost everything that exists is a part of all three metaphysical aspects -- the Wyld, the Weaver and the Wyrm -- but some things are more one aspect than the other. Case in point with, say, Twister: Twister is dynamic (Wyld), patterned (Weaver) and entropic (Wyrm), as it is a manifestation of raw energy/power (Wyld), a patterned-coherent force/physical embodiment (Weaver) and it destroys things in its path/wake (Wyrm). However, it is more so a dynamic, Wyld-essence than it is the other two; it much more represents spontaneous manifestation of energy/essence that both takes and gives and "do-what-it-do" that it is a coherent pattern or a purposeful destroyer (sometimes Twister doesn't readily destroy anything). Similar examples with both Earthquake and Volcano (the former being a dynamic disruption and the latter being a destroyer, true, but also a bringer of heat, energy and life-giving nutrients and empowerment).

              Cutting things up too neatly into "Wyld, Weaver, Wyrm" categories leads to a skewed perspective about their various qualities relative to each other in the metaphysical triat... which, of course, is exactly something that Weaver does every day, bless her heart...

                              Originally posted by Mercurial View Post
                              Okay, but what is a Gaian example of a Wyrm totem? These totems are always described as Wyld. I would say termite, but that is established as Weaver.
                              Again, a subtle-but-very-important distinction: Gaia, as a broad spirit, is in-and-of-"herself" a Wyld-essence. While she does, of course, have her Weaver and Wyrm attributes, she is more so a Dynamic (Wyld) essence; looking for her "Weaver" or "Wyrm" totems is a metaphysical step-to-the-left misperception. To illustrate further: each of the main levels of the Umbrae has aspects of each of the Triat, but each level (High, Middle and Low) has a bias-essence, which itself illustrates the Triat relative to the other levels. The High/Astral Umbra is the realm of the Weaver; the Middle Umbra is the essence/embodiment of Wyld (Dynamism); the Low Umbra represents the Wyrm/Entropic nature of things. While each realm has attributes of the others, metaphysically/meta-conceptually, they don't readily embody those aspects they way they do their "innate" one. Looking for Wyrm totems -- other than the few already attributed, and those others that can be deduced through simple association -- in the Middle/Gaian essence of things is looking at the issue from a skewed perspective: the Wyrm, as it metaphysically exists and has done so since before modern man was even a thought, isn't so much represented in/by Gaia, as such, because it is less an aspect of her and more an aspect of other things. It's a very fine-line distinction, but an equally important one when asking certain metaphysical questions....
