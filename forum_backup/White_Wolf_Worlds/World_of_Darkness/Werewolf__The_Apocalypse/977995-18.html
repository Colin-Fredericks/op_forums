                Glad to see that this thread returned on the very next day after the International Transgender Day of Visibility /s

                We’ve been over this in circles time and time again but I’ll go over the basics of my stance again and offer my support for those who have returned to these trenches like Heavy Arms.

                There is a valid argument to be made that thematic stories could be told using the Black Furies about Trans-Woman fighting to be recognized in the face of TERF-y attitudes (Trans Exclusionary Radical Feminists), which the tribe of Pegasus could serve as a useful allegory for given its connection with the feminist movement and that this is a continuing battle.

                That said, what matters for more is the safety and enjoyment of the players at your table. Trans people have to fight constantly to have their identities not be attacked and are embroiled in battles in several states across this country (USA), today, to have their rights not be infringed on account of who they are. If a Trans player wants to play a Trans character in a game about ecological activist werewolves and not have to deal with the shit they deal with day-to-day in that game that’s their right and as an ST you have an obligation to respect their wishes if you wish to run a game at that table. Some people may want to have that fight and if so then great, run that game. It’s the same reason you would acknowledge the wishes of an American indigenous person who wants to play a Pure Tribe but doesn’t want to deal with settler racism in their fantasy game or a British/Irish person who wants to play a gaelic bard but doesn’t want to have to deal with the legacy of The Troubles, etc.

                My opinion on what White Wolf should do is have a side column addressing this and offer suggestions on how to talk about this with your players and various ways to play, with such things being present, somewhat present, or not at all present.

                My take on what default should be is that trans woman should be accepted by Pegasus but conservative camps disagree and want to stop this somehow. Again though; this would only come up in my games, assuming I had a Trans player at my table (which I usually do in my group) after I’ve spoken with them about if they’d be comfortable with such plot elements in our story.

                We’re all here to have fun. Respect people’s boundaries and talk to your fellow players. Session 0 is good. Lines/Veils are good. Trans rights are human rights.

Agender, genderfluid, genderqueer, enby and other non-cis people might or might not be able to join the tribe, with the Garou of any given Black Fury sept deciding if they want to educate any such applicant as a Black Fury and Pegasus not providing any reason when allowing or not allowing such an applicant into the tribe after the Rite of Passage. In general, the Black Fury attribute some cultural significance to being female, so any such applicant usually identifies as at least partly female or is biologically female, while perhaps identifying as genderless. A biologically male agender might for most Black Furies be to far a stretch to be accepted as applicant to join the tribe.

                                                        Sidebar: It is by design, that Pegasus doesn't explain himself to his people. Maybe one applicant just doesn't feel right to the Totem, while another does. Tribal Totems also admit to their tribes garou who later turn traitors, get corrupted, even become BSD. So, the Totem isn't all-knowing. It is for the players and Storytellers to discuss and decide how and to what extend the topic of non-cis characters shall be present in their game. This goes for NPCs as well as for PCs.
