            So, I think that each of the concepts is actually embodying a Verb.
            • Build - Tatoo Artist - creating something new and using it to record what the Garou need to know
            • Hack - Urban Planner - hacking the system from within and turning it towards the Garou needs
            • Repurpose - The Car Liberator - Taking from the Rich and Giving to the poor
            • Research - Detective - the description is almost creepy in the fact that the character NEEDS to know rather than actually use it as a means to an end
            The description is rather unfocused and invoking a lot of setting elements that we don't have a context for right now, and which I'm not even sure we'll have context without what we know about the old games. There's also the fact that without the societies of the Tribes they're probably the resident "human" tribe. The general description was serviceable if confusing until they got to the ban and the Patron.

            The new Glass Walkers seem to be Bob the Builder Tribe of the game. And not just that but they cannot destroy technology without seriously hampering themselves. I'm willing to interpret their perk a bit more generously by saying that building things doesn't necessarily mean items, it could mean organizations and processes. Sadly limiting it to those specific skills does torpedo that sentiment quite quickly. As for the Spider... it's a new interpretation of Spider as the Great Builder, not the usual Naturae Spider. It seems to be very Weavery in its agenda and could even be an aspect of the Weaver. I don't think this choice was well thought through.

            As for the sample characters - there is little that makes them work within the tribe or within a pack, they are all standalone concepts and lack hooks into the setting. This is something that was often considered a weakness in old Werewolf - it was a setting that was always lore havy and required a lot of thought to make concepts that hook into the setting as presented. I assume that's the main reason they went for this. I still believe it could have been done better to give a link between their jobs as Garou and their passions/jobs (!?!?).

            As I mentioned I find the detective freaky. The Tatoo Artist is a missed opportunity where they could have built him up as someone making tatoo fetishes and talens, and where his personal tatoos could be stories like Maui's Tatoos in Moana. The Car Liberator is ridiculous for being this grungy rebel type that's sneering at people for judging him, if anything he utterly screams 90s with that attitude. The Urban planner could be interesting, but feels a bit shallow without further context and especially without knowing how hard normal life and jobs are for the new Garou.

            What doesn't kill you, makes you... stranger.
