    Failure of a rank-challenge

    06-15-2021, 06:37 PM
    So, one has to challenge a higher-ranked Garou in order to rank-up, once one has the requisite Renown. What happens when the challenge fails? Do you lose all that Renown and have to start over from scratch? Do you keep it and challenge the next night? Do you have to wait a moon to challenge again? Lots of rules for ranking up, but as far as I can see, nothing on what happens when your players goof the attempt.

      They don't lose all their Renown, but they probably lose at least a little for screwing up, which means they'd have to re-earn some before they could challenge again.                   I don't think there are any written rules to that scenario. That's very common in Werewolf so it leaves a lot for STs to figure out for themselves. Here is what I do.

                  1) There is no Renown loss for simply failing to pass the test. However, if the Garou does something during the test that would normally cause them to lose renown, they of course would lose that.

                  2) The challenge has been failed. They don't get to attempt to try again with that challenge. That is not the same as not being allowed to advance Rank again. It just means they need to challenge a Garou again and start the process over. Depending on what you want done, it might mean they have to challenge the same Garou, or need to challenge a different Garou, or decide whatever they want. There are positives and negatives to both approaches both IC and OOC. You can set any rule you want as the ST for the entire Nation, or have each sept determine any such rules locally.

                  3) The Garou need to wait one lunar month before challenging again. I agree with CajunKhan it fits werewolf culture best. However, this is likely something that is modified by local sept culture. Some septs might require more elaborate preparation to show they are ready again, but I wouldn't make any sept's unique culture to be radically different from everyone else's.

                  4) It is up to the Garou being challenged to determine whether to issue the exact same challenge, a slightly different challenge, or a completely different challenge. It really depends on what they think works best for the circumstances.

                  In my setting, the purpose of the Challenge is to demonstrate the Garou is ready for the responsibilities for the next rank within Garou society. Awarding Renown is something that can be politically manipulated, and there are times when a Garou's abilities show he is not ready for the next rank. (Although obviously the challenge itself can also be politically manipulated). When that happens, good Garou being challenged creates a difficult challenge that will cause that Garou to fail in order to teach that Garou they have much more to learn despite having gained the necessary Renown. When the candidate appears to be ready, the challenge tests them enough that they have to demonstrate whatever skill, but should be able to be passed. At least the challenges for Rank 2 (I am ready for adulthood) and Rank 3 (I am an established member of the community and can mentor younger Garou) are usually not too hard to pass. All but the most incompetent Garou should be able to pass those during their lifetime. It isn't until Rank 4 (I am a leader of the Nation) and Rank 5 (I am a hero of the people) that the challenges are so hard that even capable Garou of those levels might fail (or at least take a very long time to achieve). Other STs will handle their challenges differently.

                                      To me, the main issue is to prevent shotgun challenges where the Challenger fails, and then immediately tries again until he wins. In that scenario losing the original challenge would almost be meaningless. So there should be a reasonable period of time before the person challenges for the same issue again. So what's a reasonable amount of time? Well, a lunar cycle makes sense for Werewolf.

                                      There are certainly other time periods an ST could choose instead, but I think a lunar cycle works best since its reasonable, not too long, and thematically appropriate.

                                      Now when it comes to other kinds of challenges (those not about advancing rank), a lunar month may not necessarily be appropriate. There could be justification for an even earlier re-challenge, or lack of justification that would push the next opportunity way later than one month or even permanently denied. In those cases (challenge for pack or sept leadership, resolving bad blood between two Garou, rival claimants for territory, etc.) there's probably a variety of factors that need to be considered before a re-challenge could be permitted. After all, the purpose of a challenge is to decide the issue so Garou can move on. Allowing someone to constantly bring up the same issue again and again negates that.

                                      Now the Garou likely have a long series of precedents that establishes such times in their law code, the Litany or just tradition in general. So within the setting most Garou, and certainly their Philodoxes, likely know what's appropriate in most situations. But these are not things most games need, and neither STs or Players want to slog through an actual legal code (no matter how short - and remember that in its full form it takes hours to recite the entire Litany, and every tribe and sept has a slightly different version) to determine these things so it comes down to an ST's decision on the spot so the game can move on.
