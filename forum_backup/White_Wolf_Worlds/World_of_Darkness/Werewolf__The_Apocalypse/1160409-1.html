                            Originally posted by LokiRavenSpeak View Post

                            So is GURPS 4E that dependant on hex maps? Or is there a supplement for lets say "narrative movement"?



                            So why were they clunky? Because they were powerful compared to humans or it had other issues?
                            GURPS also has range band options if you prefer to use abstracted ranges. You don't really need hex maps to play. Basically, you just need range to figure out the attack modifiers for ranged attacks. Yards are close enough to meters if you are a metric person to use metric maps unless you really want to crunch the difference.

                            As for the clunkiness of Werewolf, it was more how the port felt. WoD games have fairly simple mechanics and a WTA werewolf in GURPS was a big list of abilities. Also, some of the mechanics didn't translate into easy explanations and because this was GURPS 3e days, they balanced less with other material than things do in GURPS 4e days. A base werewolf is more powerful than a human, but built on the same point total, you could make some awesome humans. Werewolves were at least 300 point characters, IIRC. Mages and vampires were pretty buff, too.

                            If you like the GURPS mechanics more than WoD and are willing to do some work, the GURPS Werewolf the Apocalypse book could be worth it for you. I just feel that it was the less effective of the three ports at capturing the original game. . .

                                                    Mostly you'll have to closely watch your PCs building their sheets. As a point-based system, it gives so much freedom in character creation that you can easily end up with fairly unexpected results. It isn't unbalanced or such, but the system itself won't keep your players from getting something you planned as a 5th level Gift from start. Discuss your plans to the chronicle, and heard theirs with extra care just to be on the same page.

                                                    You don't need to keep tabs on how much the monsters, minions and NPCs cost on points. But they being 700 points characters mean they have an extremely broad variety of possible sheets. The Garou Template will simplify things a little, but you can still end up with characters far more specialized than you're used to on WoD. Again, just be in the same page as them.

                                                    The same is true for M&M, though I still think it is easier for the task at hand.
