    Originally posted by Beckett View Post

    WtA, however, essentially establishes that Gaia, the Triad, spirits, etc... are all 100% factual things. But that leaves a lot of real world faiths, well badically unrepresented in a setting that is suppossed to be set in a real-world-like place...
    ​Basically, everybody else beat me to it, but what the heck. The biggest difference is that for the most part every other supernatural in the World of Darkness isn't really sure what the actual truth is. The Garou, on the other paw are certain that they're right -- because they're religious zealots. To quote Josh Billings (who is often misquoted as Samuel Clemens) "​It ain't what you don't know that gets you into trouble. It's what you know for sure that just ain't so."

    ​And that also nicely explains the monster / hero paradox of the Garou. If they're right, then they're pretty much heroes -- if they're not, then they're basically Al Qaeda with superpowers.

                                                            I got a few ideas..

                                                            1. Tweak the initiative system to allow characters who change declared actions at the usual -2 dice to have the option of interrupting a lesser initiative character or npc action as long as the interrupter had enough initiative to do so. This would give a better grasp on last minute body guarding or snatching items out of the air for fast paced craziness. Make clear that a round is about 3 or so seconds that way chracters that are thrown or have suffered some sort of dynamic are given due process. And it helps to point out use of supernatural speed or highlight really trained focus.

                                                            2. Make Pack Tactics a secondary skill that would lower the difficulty of actions taken by 1 for every other extra pack member involved in a maneuver for limited by the rank of the secondary ability. This would reward time and training invested invested to and simulate the power of the pack as opposed to the individual since this is how the Garou had a distinct advantage in the war of rage.

                                                            3. Ditch the kinfolk optional rank system and adopt the Kinfolk Status background.

                                                            4. Make Ronin rank a secondary citizen file system that brings them closer to being called into action and adopted by a tribe who begins to note and like what they are doing but to keep the idea of them being nothing without the actual Garou Nation make their highest rank gifts gained at x.5 that of the actual rank of the Ronin. So at rank 6 they can only reach a Lv.3 gift. This would push up play with players and give those character types a bit more than just a mercenary like feel as they are truly tying to get in to a tribe.

                                                            5. Create some secretive or more sinister places of power for other fera and evil strongholds for adversaries in more places than that are listed.

                                                            6. Make all Silent Strider Kinfolk possess the "Gypsy Blood" perk since Silent Strider Gypsies are all considered kinfolk. Would balance out the rather roaming presence of the tribe as a whole.

                                                            7. Make the older Silver Tolerance a 5 point merit since there is a 7 point Silver Immunity which ignores all effects.

                                                            8. Make it a rule that Rank 6 or greater characters can understand and maybe called by the one they serve to deal with an epic problem one of the Triat has decided the character capable to deal with. The difficulty should be immense.

                                                            9. Inform players on how to use the, "Fade to Black" system. In which you completely cover explicit scenes from younger, uncomfortable and immature players if need be. "Ok she smiles at you and shoots a glance to twelve other Children of Gaia of alternate genders.. one metis has two genders and licks its lips. You move with them beyond the brush into a secluded spot where an older more experienced Theurge begins the ritual to subdue the pent up aggression within. Petting starts.. and from there my dear player your character experiences the sweet nectar of extasy as we FADE TO BLACK on this scene and move back to Monroe who is still at the store buying a Bone Gnawer a beer."

                                                            10. Create and alternate sect of Furies who have been infected with the Wyld Virus in mexico that have declared war and are no longer contagious.

                                                            11. Do more with the Siberak tribe strength and range as direct threats to the Spiral Dancers of nuclear sites in Russia. Leading for them to present themselves at a moot with the Garou nation.

                                                            12. Bring back the Gurps Werewolf the apocolypse fetishes and especially the silver sword.

                                                            13. List the Rite of Shadow Craft and the Craft Worker Merit use in the Uktena tribe book.

                                                            14. Restrict the "Sorcerer" Merit in WW20 to Doshi and Bubasti alone as their backgrounds explain how those cats sell some of the soul to get that kind of talent and the Kitsune using evil to fight evil as a purpose.. I mean c'mon... that's just tooo open. WAY too open. This Merit can justify doing what the cats and kitsune would normally do but make extraordinarily very, very easy for the character to do in almost no time or effort. I officially give a thumbs down on this merit being open to any other outside this selection.

                                                            15. Pure Ones should have far less difficulty dealing with spirits and the gauntlet of their native homeland.

                                                            16. Give Bone Gnawer an increased Kinfolk modifier in city or back water areas.

                                                            17. Buff up the new dream form characteristics for Mokole because the new ones ruined alot of combat value option that once made players truly fear them.

                                                            18. Make Naga poison unable to be soaked in any fashion. Resist Toxin and the like should only make the damage done by the poison have to be rolled.

                                                            19. Create more off planet threats that call Heros into action just before the Red star development.

                                                            20. Have background called "Pack/Pride/Clutch/Murder etc.." these represent a certain amount of npcs who have banned together with you to survive.

                                                            21. Because this makes more sense now a days. A long list for sits for players to use forums and or chat with other players for official New and Classic WoD content. (Edit: I meant Sites not sits.. ugh..)
