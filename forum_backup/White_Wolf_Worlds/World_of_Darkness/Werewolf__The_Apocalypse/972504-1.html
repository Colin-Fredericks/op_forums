    Originally posted by Bogdan View Post
    why not let them counter the wards the other way if they have enough wit?
    the Mages should also be assumed to have wit. Far more than others, considering that we're talking in reference to how their own powers work. This setup let's Mages put some time in and have unbeatable magic come out. You are literally letting them stack (in this case 40) successes and having the other Supernaturals roll a single Attribute+ Ability dicepool (usually only up to 10 dice) to counter, which is impossible for anything (even Methuselahs and powerful spirits) to overcome.

    A mage has travelling ward on him? Seek whatever the mage possesses, be it clothes or some iron item, or whatever. The rule of &quot;Pressing the Ward&quot; will apply, and you should attack the Mage with what he has in order to by-pass his protection.

    A mage has set protection over some area? Seek your way around threshold, burrow and go underneath, go through Umbra, or even go from above (though one problem with it is the fact that if Mage turns out smart enough, he could set the threshold even above. Note, air isn't solid enough to be threshold, so it can get quite challenging to pull off and ensure it's legit)
    I wouldn't usually allow burrowing under a ward either. It goes against the spirit of a Ward. As per there being a upper threshold to the Ward, I would think that would have a lot more to do with paradigm than anything else. I suppose that applies to the burrowing thing as well now that I think about it. But if a house is inscribed with Runes to prevent entry, above or below those Runes would work (imo). If incense were the focus, my interpretation would be more fluid, etc.

                                Ritual rolls do not harbor a +1 difficulty modifiers per roll unless you've exhausted your Stamina and needed to take a break, at that point there're specific mechanics on how to continue with the ritual at a later date (there's a different system regarding increased difficulty). You can channel more than three Quintessence points per turn - it's restricted to your Avatar's rating, until the character has Prime 3 that is.

                                Originally posted by Amount of Quintessence Used
                                Game-wise, a mage must have at least three dots in either the Prime or Primal Utility Spheres if she wants to use “more than her share” of energy. Any mage with an Avatar Background can absorb that Background’s worth of Quintessence from a Node or Tass, or use the points of Quintessence in the Quintessence wheel on her character sheet. In order to use more points of Quintessence than she has in her Avatar Background rating, however, and in order to use Quintessence from other sources, she must have at least Rank 3 in the Prime or Primal Utility Spheres. As an example, Jinx (Avatar 5) can use up to five points of Quintessence per turn, while Sabra, (Avatar 1) may use only one point of Quintessence per turn until he achieves Prime 3.

                                As the Base Damage and Duration chart explains, the amount of Quintessence a mage with Prime 3 or higher mage can employ within a single Effect is based upon the number of successes rolled. If Penny Dreadful’s player, for example, rolls three successes, then Penny can employ up to six points of Quintessence with that Effect; if she rolls five, then Penny can use up to 10 points that Effect. This, of course, assumes that Penny Dreadful has Prime 3 or higher; considering that Penny’s been around for a while, though, that’s a pretty safe assumption. If Penny doesn’t have Prime 3, though, then Penny is stuck using her Avatar rating’s worth of Quintessence, period
                                Quintessence, if employed like any other modifer, cannot be used to bring the effect's difficulty below -3, too. If you don't use any other modifier (out of many, there's a list, such as employing foci, resonance, drawing from the Mythic Threads etc...), you still can use three points of Quintessence (or more, if you have the difficulty raised due to an adversity AND your Avatar rating allows for it), to have it reduced to -3 below the base difficulty for the said spell.
