                                                I’m not sure this will add much to the discussion, but I was reading the old GET OF FENRIS TRIBEBOOK and thought it might be fun to puzzle out what befell the various camps after the Great Unpleasantness suggested by Mr Phipps (A Fenrir version of ‘Execute Order 66’ as it were).

                                                Fair warning: having only seen previews of the revised tribebook, this will be based on out-of-date information.


                                                THE VALKYRIES OF FREYA - Whether in the classic editions or in 5th edition, the one thing we can reasonably be certain of is that they would NEVER end up with the Black Furies (Albeit in 5th edition that’s more because those Garou inclined to the Furies would have joined that Tribe already*).

                                                I suspect that this camp is likely to split down the middle, since there’s nothing in their original manifesto to argue for or against their inclusion with the Cult of Fenris (Though one suspects the Camp’s tendency to think for itself - or at least deliberately place itself a little apart from traditional ways of doing things - could potentially make it’s members poor Cultists material).

                                                *Incidentally, one interesting wrinkle to explore in 5th edition would be the question of which Signature Characters might find their way into very different tribes - since Tribe seems to be a purely spiritual affinity, rather than linked to ancestry or homeland: it’s possible to imagine Evan Heals-the-Past winding up in the neo-Black Furies with Mari Cabrah, for instance (If the key element of his personality was a desire for Justice; an emphasis on Leadership might well see him land with the new style Silver Fangs).


                                                THE HAND OF TYR - Brutally uncompromising from the start, the Hand’s focus on Justice over Vengeance means that those of their membership who went the way of the Cult would be more regretted than most, not least because the Cult of Fenris’ commitment to the ‘Cult of the Offensive’ is likely to see these sleuths, judges and executioners dragged into large scale operations at odd with the Hand’s focus on the individual (Though it’s possible members of the Hand may try to carve a niche as assassins, investigators and spies for the Cult).

                                                Those members of the Hand who refused to join the Cult - who would likely appreciate their attitude, if not always their exacting approach to things - would almost certainly be amongst the most eager to mete out retributive justice on the Cultists (It seems highly likely these survivors would be appreciated by the relentless Galestalkers for their hunting skills and by the Black Furies for their commitment to Justice red in tooth & claw).


                                                MJOLNIR’S THUNDER - Quite possibly the most elemental Cult of Fenris members, in that they want the Wyrm dead and don’t really give a d*** about anything else so long as it ends up blasted: on the other hand this makes them completely malice free, so far as humanity is concerned, which is something.

                                                Still not interested in minimising collateral damage, though: if members of this Camp fail to continue with the Cult of Fenris after the Great Unpleasantness, it’s probably out of loyalty to those of their tribe who were removed from the equation so that the Cult could run everything.

                                                The real question is whether the ‘Hammers’ would immediately identify the Cult as Wyrm-tainted or just as another case of politics winning out over the actual Mission (In either case I’d bet the Shadow Lords would be just delighted to acquire defectors, if only as Muscle: whether the defectors would be delighted to be acquired or head over to the Hart Warden instead is an interesting question).


                                                THE FANGS OF GARM - ‘Public Enemy #1’ aka ‘Priority Targets’ in Cult of Fenris terms, it seems highly likely that a Tribal moot summoned to address the absolute lunacy of leaving the Blood Fist caern deserted for the sake of murdering slightly more Wyrmspawn was the key forum for the ‘Great Unpleasantness’ (AKA the Massacre of the Scrupulous’).

                                                If this was indeed the case, then it’s a racing certainty that this purge of those Fenrir with the strongest ties to other tribes is a key reason the Cult of Fenris appears on the enemies list in the fifth edition Corebook,

                                                If any survivors made it through the massacre (and the Get are almost as tough as they try to be, so someight Well have) it seems likely they would have a strong chance of making their way into another tribe (and I’d bet money that a strong minority of the multi-tribe sept now holding the Blood Fist caern would have been Fangs of Garm to start with - assuming that these would have been negotiating with those packs from other tribes garrisoning the caern whilst the Fenrir worked to settle internal controversies at a more private forum).


                                                THE GLORIOUS FIST OF WOTAN - Almost certainly made up a solid majority of the original Cult and would be just delighted to purge those who didn’t see things their way from the former tribe, probably to the point of being the Driving Force behind the ‘Great Unpleasantness’.

                                                Presumably entirely happy as they work and plan to teach Humanity some bloody lessons on a scale their former tribe mates would have been deeply reluctant to contemplate (Mighty also be kept working to maintain their ascendancy over the Cult and expand the Cult by snapping up the most extremist elements from across what’s left of the Garou Nation).


                                                THE SWORDS OF HEIMDALL - were very, very dead before the Great Unpleasantness and, since they didn’t get graves to start with, can’t crawl out of them.

                                                Unfortunately the Cult of Fenris are busily enabling their ideological bedfellows, because when the Tribes of the Garou Nation aren’t taking your call, mortals with an unhealthy attitude towards old myths of Northern Europe and White Racial Supremacy will still answer the dog-whistle.

                                                At least when you’re a Ragnarok Cult made up of Big Bad Wolves looking for footsoldiers (AKA ‘cannon fodder’).


                                                YMIR’S SWEAT - I tend to operate on the assumption that 5th edition tribes are more ‘religions’ than nationalities, with the latter role being handled by septs.

                                                One therefore assumes that Ymir’s Sweat (I’ve never been certain if that should have been an actual camp or more of a running joke/folk legend even in the Classic editions) is a sept active somewhere in the Maritime Provinces of Canada - and was probably split down the middle, much like the majority of Fenris Wolf’s children.


                                                LOKI’S SMILE - Whether they actually existed as Fenrir of uncommonly devious ambition in the classic editions (Or were, in fact, an insurgency within one of the most aggressively strong tribes sponsored by the Shadow Lords) it’s not hard to believe that Loki’s Smile would work perfectly as a group the Cult of Fenris loudly deny, but which plays some influential covert role within the Cult.

                                                It’s horribly easy to imagine the ‘Smilers with a Knife’ playing a key role in making the Great Unpleasantness possible - if only because a Cult is, on the whole, easier to manipulate than a nation (Though the vicious single-mindedness of Hauglosk-ridden Garou means it’s still dangerous to assume they’ll rocket off in the direction you want them to go).

                                                Whether this was a result of the ‘Smilers’ genuinely sharing the Cult’s love of a Glorious Death or was more grounded in a desire to make sure that if these fools were going to devote themselves to suicide missions, it was better to steer them towards useful deaths is an interesting question.

                                                It’s possible that those who preferred to seek greener pastures elsewhere might have fallen in with the Shadow Lords with whom they often seem to share a few flourishes, but it’s equally (possibly more likely) they would seek out Tribes where trading on their particular skills would not be like selling a refrigerator to a man who lives in an igloo.

                                                        It occurred to me that it might be interesting to puzzle out what the Litany, as taught to the Cult of Fenris may look like: I’m assuming that they would happily discard those elements which struck them as inconvenient (and generally interpret whatever was left to suit themselves).

                                                        - COMBAT THE WYRM WHEREVER IT DWELLS AND WHEREVER IT THRIVES

                                                        - ACCEPT AN HONOURABLE SURRENDER

                                                        - RESPECT THOSE OF LOWER STATION

                                                        - THE FIRST SHARE OF THE KILL FOR THE GREATEST IN STATION

                                                        - EAT NOT THE FLESH OF HUMANS

                                                        - THE VEIL SHALL NOT BE LIFTED

                                                        - DO NOT OBLIGE THE PEOPLE TO TEND YOUR SICKNESS

                                                        - THE LEADER MAY NOT BE CHALLENGED DURING WARTIME

                                                        - TAKE NO ACTION THAT CAUSES A CAERN TO BE VIOLATED

                                                        (I’m also fairly convinced that ‘GAROU SHALL NOT MATE WITH GAROU’ was seriously proposed - a majority of the Cult of Fenris wants it’s members focussed on the Apocalypse War and an honourable death, not wasting time making children that cannot be expected to see adulthood*).

                                                        *After all, to their way of thinking The End may come at any time.
