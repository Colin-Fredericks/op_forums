                                                        Yes, Garou (and most Fera) will ash low Humanity Kindred (6-) when they find them. It sounds like a fine reason for Kindred to keep to Humanity and to maintain their Humanity. Alternatively, they can learn Auspex, so they can avoid the Garou (or Fera).

                                                        There are a few points where Garou and Kindred can literally profit from a local alliance. The blood of Garou with high levels of Pure Breed is exceptionally powerful and is a valuable commodity in Kindred circles. Garou regeneration allows Garou to produce copious amounts of blood (eight units of blood per day from a Garou in Glabro form would not be unreasonable if the donor received sufficient fluids and nutrition), so a willing donor can provide lots of blood.

                                                        A high Humanity Kindred could potentially make an agreement with a high Pure Breed Garou to supply blood for money. The Garou is paid $100 per unit of blood, allowing the Garou to fight the Wyrm without the worrying about maintaining a job. The Kindred sells the Garou blood for $400 per unit, allowing the Kindred to keep up appearances without worrying about a job. The Garou is capable of focusing on the fight against the Wyrm, the local Camarilla possesses a potent weapon against the Sabbat, and everyone walks away with turning into dust or being stabbed with a silver spoon.
