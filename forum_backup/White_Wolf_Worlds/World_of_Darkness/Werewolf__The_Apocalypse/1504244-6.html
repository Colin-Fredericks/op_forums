        Now I’ll go in a completely different direction from my ideas on the cult around Amam. In one of my earlier ideas, I speculated on a Bane Mummy that acted in a mastermind/plotter type role. I also talked about a cult not controlled by vampires that was heavily influenced by the Brotherhood of the Black Pharaoh in the Call of Cthulhu RPG. I am combining these two together.

        We know that Set ruled Egypt at one point before the early dynastic period that saw Narmer/Menes unite Lower and Upper Egypt. This era was probably between the time when Set defeated Osiris and the mummy Horus returned and overthrew him (for the first time in their long struggle). Historians in the WoD believe, like we do, in the actual history of Egypt and discount any legends or evidence of an earlier ruler. But crackpots believe there was a figure or figures who ruled Egypt before this. I’ll call this figure the Dark Pharaoh. The crackpots happen to be right, but the problem is that they don’t understand this was the vamprie Set. They have very little evidence, so they make up a lot to make sense of what they know.

        They combine the little evidence they have, speculation, and subsequent evidence related to Set (like evidence of actual Setite temples and cults throughout the historical era) to create the pseudo-historical figure of the “Dark Pharaoh” which has had various cults about him over the years. The Dark Pharaoh serves as the kind of figure that the Black Pharaoh (an avatar of Nyarlathotep) does in the Cthulhu RPG. (If you don’t like the name of the “Dark Pharaoh” for this figure, it’s just a placeholder). At some point in time, the adherents of this crackpot theory created an actual occult organization with Paths of sorcery and other awfulness. Because we all love alliteration I’ll use the placeholder name of Disciples of the Dark Pharaoh.

        So this Bane Mummy did the obvious thing - he found this cult and told them that he was the Dark Pharaoh, and he created his own fictional history to support his propaganda and goals. He essentially convinced these humans that he was the historical vampire Set. But he doesn’t call himself Set or claim he is a vampire. It’s just an altenate history that allows him to convince others he’s the guy that they think he is. He’s an ancient, powerful mummy with lots of magic so it is easy to convince people he’s a god. If we really want to go down the CoC RPG route, we could even depict Apophis/the Wyrm in a similar way that the game handles Nyarlathotep. It doesn’t have to be true. It just could be the way the Bane Mummy describes himself as an avatar of its god.

        I imagine while there was an old group of Egyptian Disciples, that the group spread to France after Napoleon invaded Egypt and Champollion started his studies of Egyptology, and then to Britain after Britain occupied Egypt in 1882. From there the Disciples spread across the European colonial empires and even the United States. The Discplies even have a US branch based in Afrocentric Hotepery.

        This Bane Mummy does have other cults, front groups, etc. that he uses, but this is the most successful and useful. Some of these other groups have since become part of the network of the Disciples, but others remain unknown to them as the Bane Mummy wants these separate. There are even a few occult oriented groups like the Disciples, but are entirely distinct from any Egyptian theme. Egypt, after all, had been ruled by Romans, Byzantines, Arab Caliphs, and Mamlukes for a long time so he’s been able to build his influence and spread it to other places for a while.

        These groups collectively are the basis for his Retainers, Allies, Conatcts, and Influence. And its top members know appropriate paths of Sorcery. They know various Banes they can summon. Maybe even a few are a special kind of fomori. And he has built up a nice little empire. A lot of them are on auto-pilot now, but all it takes is for the Bane Mummy to contact them and they’ll do his bidding. After all, he is an unkillable “god” who provides them all sorts of occult goodies now and then (objects created by his Hekau) and sometimes favors (from other Hekau). If you compare this Bane Mummy to many vampire elders, he’s their equal in influence. He does not have the collective influence of the Ventrue Directorate or Clan Giovanni, but it is sizable and he can defend his own interests at the local level.

        These groups are completely outside the Followers of Set. The Bane Mummy doesn’t want vampires involved at all since he knows they’ll try to usurp these groups. In fact, he’s even told his mortal followers that there is a group of vampires who worship him as “Set” but get all the facts wrong, and that it is forbidden to have dealings with them as they are too dangerous. He even provides them some amulets so they cannot be controlled. The Setites by now know there is a such a cult and would like to take it over, but so far have been unable to or determine specifics.

        This Bane Mummy is not against the Setites. He views the vampires as a useful tool, but he wants to keep them under HIS control instead of them controlling him. He has his own ideas on how to achieve the goals of Apophis and knows the Setites would just muck it up. There might even be a small group of Setites in temples more or less controlled by him because they think he is Set, or one of the Methusalehs supposedly killed just prior to 950 BC when Set fled Egypt and recreated the Followers, and believe they have been specially selected to know this secret knowledge.

        He is probably the closest thing to a leader among the Bane Mummies, but I would ascribe that any true leader woulld be Apophis itself. But he likely tries to keep in contact with the other Bane Mummies and keep them aprise of what everyone is doing, and helping them out where he can, particularly in retrieving bodies, empowering Ka, recovering and protecting possessions, etc.

            Because this Bane Mummy prefers to work through others and not put himself into danger, he has minimized the drainage of his Ba and the time he must spend in the Underworld. He is very successful at accruing Ba and coming back to the land of the living. His cult around him know that the “god” requires periodic slumber and rest, but will always come back (perhaps they occur in a natural cycle like when “the stars are right.”) I’d make sure the Bane Mummy had Memory 5 and the Background Journal 5. Having his Appearance be zero is fine, but he likely needs a high amount of Charisma and Manipulation. So any taints he has would need to detract other areas. Giving him Paranoia, Megalomania, and other Derangements would make sense.

            PCs would probably never encounter “the Dark Pharaoh” directly, unless the chronicle was entirely geared toward this, but he would make a great opponent that they fight indirectly through his various Influence fronts. They might not even realize the leader of their foes is even a Bane Mummy. This guy should one of the ultimate evil criminal genius archetypes like Moriarity, Fu Manchu, Ernst Stavro Blofeld, Ra’s Al Ghul, Dr. Mabuse, Fantomas, etc. That’s what the ST should be going for when running the character or the groups he controls. If any Bane Mummy is going to turn a minion into a pillar of salt after shouting “You have failed me for the last time,” it should be this guy.

            I always like having other groups out there that collect the Influence Background, as too often it is just considered to be a Vampire background. I like the idea that there are others doing their own manipulations.

            Where should this Bane Mummy be located? I imagine he probably left Egypt a long time ago, though he probably visits now and again. There would have been far too many places that were better at gathering various kinds of influence and resources. He’s probably travelled to and dwelled in many places in the world by now - Constantinople, Baghdad, Old Delhi, Paris, London, etc. I imagine London would have made a good place to be in for a long time. While he has probably visited the United States, if I were to place him in a city today, I’d probably choose some place in East Asia where he could be closer to the rising economies of this century. Maybe Singapore. It is cosmopolitan enough that he would not look out of place, and close to a lot of important trade routes and big economies. The only concern I have about putting him into a major city is that it risks his influence possibly being discovered by all the vampires there. But I also think most of the time he can probably keep any actions subtle enough that it would escape a vampire’s notice that someone is doing something odd, as vampires mainly look for something going against other vampires, and he usually does not do that. And in the modern day, one could always commute to a city and actually dwell on some country or suburban estate.

            This Bane Mummy likely needs some personal attendants besides just cult leaders reporting to him from around the world. They’d be resposible for looking after any tombs after he “dies”, inspecting defenses, honoring the Ka, etc. I imagine this might be some family affair where the benefit is that even after one person dies, he knows his children and descendants will likewise be looked after by the “god”, so there is an incentive to keep watch and serve even after the mummy passes and is incapable of helping the person. The Bane Mummy might have many such families around the world, perhaps families who were once head priests of one of the cults.

            If I had to assign an existing name to this concept, I’d probably pick Qetu the Evil Doer. It’s generic enough while some of the other names require a bit more flair in their concept.

                                Originally posted by Asmodai View Post
                                I love the mummy stuff that popped up. I don't want to detract from the inspiration for them at this time, but I'd love to see more about the Setites themselves
                                No reason why we cannot do both. And we've already talked about some other ideas on expanding this faction beyond just the Setites and Bane Mummies (though the Setites are the most numerous and dominant, and the Bane Mummies are the second most important), and I'd like to see some of them explored as well.

                                So while I am still working on some more Bane Mummy cult examples, here is something about the Setites.

                                Going back to the Setite vampires themselves as an enemy to the Garou (treating them differently than other vampire opponents), I think the key is the organization of the clan, especially their temple system.

                                As originally described, the Setite divide themselves into temples. Many temples only have one vampire, though some have a few more, though there also seems to be certain “grand temples” that serve as leadership to a greater region. We know the Setite Temple in the Louisiana Bayou is the largest temple in North America, and I imagine while this is not a “capital” for the Setites in the US, that it is the most prestigious and influential.

                                Although the Setites believe in safety of numbers and surround themselves with mortal disciples, they do not trust others of their own kind. So temples with multiple Setites are rare. Temples often serve as the haven of the vampire who runs it. Setites do gather for a monthly meeting at the largest temple in the region during the night of the New Moon.

                                A Setite temple is usually located in a desolate location in the hope that the inhospitable surroundings keep its location secret. They investigate such areas beforehand to make sure it is not already occupied or under surveillance. While these temples are found in large and small cities (abandoned factories and warehouses, underground chambers in the sewer system or forgotten bunker), for our purposes those found in the rural or wilderness areas. Setites in those areas are secluded from their other enemies in the Jyhad, and a lot of wilderness is not ffectively patrolled by the Garou. But because they are in the wilderness, it is much easier for an ST to use such places for Garou septs in the wilderness. Urban Setite temples are probably best left to Vampire chronicles, or Werewolf chronicles that are urban and the ST is looking for something else than PENTEX as a foe.

                                The Setite clanbooks have a lot of detail on the layout of these temples, so they are an essential resource for an ST who wants to use them.

                                Setites prefer to avoid the Lupines if at all possible, but are prepared to destroy one if one appears. However, they also see them as a potential resource to desroy other vampires. So if a Setite learns about one, they might look into ways they can manipulate them to destroy others.

                                I would populate a temple with the Setite who runs it, their closest retainers (ghouls and mortals), and possibly members of their Herd and any cultists (who could be Allies, Contacts, or Influence depending on their potential use in a chronicle). Besides ghouls, I would also include appropriate animal fomori, particularly sacred animals like serpents and crocodiles. (The great thing about animal fomori is giant snakes and such aren't any "weirder" than the Nosferatu spawning pool from a Vampire perspective.) I would also make sure the Setite temples have silver weapons on hand. Hopefully they have people with skill in firearms and silver bullets, because fighting in hand to hand combat against Garou will likely not end well.

                                While the temples themselves are supposed to be in a desolate location, obviously the cult members of the Setites have to live somewhere close enough to be controlled, but far enough away that they won’t reveal the temple.

                                I imagine there are two ways to consider these communities. The first would be a “colony” that the Setite plants there. Examples might be some kind of religious cult commune, a planned community done by people who on surface appear non-religious, or a resort development. The second would be an existing community that it is beginning to corrupt. The Setite might be able to introduce a new religious leader who officially belongs to whatever local religion the community is it, but is already in the Setite’s pocket. Something that remains hidden for a long time because it has a respectable cover. Or that a new social club, criminal gang, brothel, motel, etc. is established, and the community gradually notices increased crime and vice as a result. Either option can work for the ST wanting to introduce the Setites, and it may not be obvious for a while that a supernatural cause is the reason.

                                I think the Setites likely do some thorough investigation before setting up a rural or wilderness temple. Unlike many vampire clans, the Setites have a long history of fighting the Lupines. The Silent Striders after all were a major force of the Osiran League that fought the Setites thousands of years ago. Setite elders probably know a lot of general information about the Garou. They may know more than any other clan except the Gangrel.

                                Besides this knowledge, the Setites may be able to gather additional information from other sources. At least some temples have alliances with the Black Spiral Dancers. Or Setites can pay off some Gangrel for what they know about a local area. Or they extort services from Bubasti who want access to their last feline kinfolk. Setites should be able to figure out which areas are known hot spots of Lupine activities, and which areas aren’t. The wilderness is a big area, and the Setites are probably more successful than not in locating temples in relatively safe areas.

                                So how can the PCs discover them? Well, you can always have the Setites screw up. It has to happen to some degree. They put their new temple in a place not realizing there is a small sept with a level 1 caern, or even a larger caern whose purpose is stealth. Or you might have PCs be part of a sept that only recently reactivated an inactive caern or even performed a new rite of caern building. Or you use the Anruth pack concept, and a pack travelling through an area happens to notice something that causes them to investigate. You can always use the old trope of the supernatural dream or omen warning Garou of some nefarious plot. This is easy as there are Gifts that exist to do just that, and certain spirits may send cryptic warnings in dreams that require the use of the Enigmas ability. Lastly you can always turn things around and start with the Setite approaching the Garou (always through an intermediary that cannot be traced back to them without a lot of work) in order to maniuplate them (like in order to kill other vampires).

                                The great thing about using Setite temples is t hat if the PCs wipe one out, it is not breaking the setting in any way. It’s just one or several vampires and their Backgrounds. You can impact the local setting, but not worry about figuring out a massive change in the setting.

                                                            Just want to make clear that 1) the idea that Setite temples are usually located in desolate areas comes from the first Setite clanbook, and 2) "desolate areas" can be urban as well as wilderness. The idea is to make sure the temple is safe from the enemies of the Setites. Locating it in a place others would expect Setite activity would jeopardize that safety. There's obviously no problem for a Setite to utilize a night club as part of her activities, but perhaps locating her temple there might not be the best idea. Utilizing the nightclub, but then travelling under obfuscate to another location would protect the location of her temple better. So I would distinguish where a Setite plies her trade from where her sacred temple might be. They are probably close enough to make routine travel between the two easy, but far enough to keep the temple's location a secret.

                                                            Being in a "desolate area" is not a requirement of all temples, it is just typical. So if a Setite wants their temple set up in an Egyptian themed nightclub called the Nile, it is certainly an option. Just expect the PCs to quickly figure out there's a secret door that leads to the temple underneath the club.

                                                            I am just emphasizing the possible wilderness locations for use in a Werewolf chronicle.

                                                            As I said earlier, both clanbooks (but the first more than the second) have a lot of detail on the temples, their layout, description, need for darkness, etc. that any ST wanting to include a Temple of Set in their chronicle should read.
