                                            Rage and extra actions. Is it not a little striking that the fastest changing breed can never be faster than 5 extra actions in a turn--AND, that being able to take those five turns requires that one's rage be so high that they're functionally a ticking bomb (absent constant use of rage/anger/what-have-you management gifts and meditation, etc.)? What sort of grinds my gears about it is that vampires--literally every vampire--has the capacity to achieve the same degree of speed, regardless of generation (albeit, those with the Celerity in clan will invariably get there faster, cheaper).

                                            Really, for me it's not that five extra actions aren't enough--it's that five extra actions is only possible if you're walking the absolute razor's edge of sanity (unless you also have Willpower 10 and Gnosis 10--in which case, well done), but it's an otherwise impressive, but unremarkable feat for certain other supernaturals. I'm thinking that there's a better way (probably).
