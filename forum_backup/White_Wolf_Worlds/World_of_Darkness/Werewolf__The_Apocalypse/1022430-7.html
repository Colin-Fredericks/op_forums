                    Yes, I think the answer would range from 'indeterminate' to 'unavailable', and every ST would have a different number. For example, I have Garou Human Kinfolk with a population equal to 100 times the Garou population (1:5,000). Of that population, 15% are Sorcerers (equally divided between Mythical, Psychic, and Technological), with 80% being dabblers and 20% being practitioners (meaning that Garou Human Kinfolk Practitioners would have a population of 1:500,000 for each of Mythic, Psychic, and Technological). In my games, that means that there are three Kinfolk Sorcerer Practitioners (one Mythic, one Psychic, and one Trchnological) for every Garou.

                    But that is just in my games, and every ST is going to use different numbers. STs that run higher Garou populations would need less Kinfolk Sorcerer Practitioners to take up the slack. With a fivefold increase in Garou populations, Kinfolk Sorcerer Practitioners are practically unneeded by the Garou.
