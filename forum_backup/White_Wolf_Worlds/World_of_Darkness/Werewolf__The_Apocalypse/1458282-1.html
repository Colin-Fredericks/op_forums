        Originally posted by heinrich View Post
        Simply mentalizing a different umbra and enter it, is something I never read about - and would find it not fitting into the game...
        I may have misremembered some ST's house rule on this one. And if I did, then I know exactly who was. The possibility of passages to other Umbrae in the Penumbra was myself for a personal game where it was relevant, but otherwise they're open to be a theoretical possibility more than anything.

        On the Dreaming there is a catch. There are two Dreams described in the books, the Dream Zone and the actual Dreaming. Changeling used very distinct rules for its Dreaming, incompatible with the Umbra as described anywhere else, and never got enough development to have something published that accounted for its place in the larger WoD cosmology. So for practical purposes it isn't in the Umbra by RAW, since the Dream Zone will work differently, but at the same time it's extremely open to interpretation.

        Personally I go RAW in it most of the time because I find it more interesting in my games, although in WtA focused games I do mesh it with the Dream Zone.

                In Changeling, they have special trods that do link to the various Umbra, the Paths of Balor. The Green Paths of Balor lead to the Middle Umbra.

                So while it's never stated, I decided that the domains called Trods by the Garou are the same thing as the Green Paths, or at least contains the Green Paths somewhere inside the domain. The difficulty is finding them.

                The Changeling sourcebook Dreams and Nightmares describes the Far Dreaming. Parts of that are clearly the same thing as the Near Realm of Arcadia Gateway. So there are lots of connections. Only in Revised did White Wolf suddenly decide the game lines weren't really part of the same setting. But that is an obvious retcon. Everyone's mileage will vary on that. There's various reasons why that may be a good idea for an individual ST who doesn't want to deal with the issue of mastering entire other game lines to run their game. But if an ST is interested in integrating the Dreaming into their Werewolf game, there's plenty of source material to help them do that.

                        I don't disagree, I just find it usually more interesting to make the Dreaming something different from the Umbra since the links are there, but not sufficiently formalized. And in this case it wasn't even a Revised thing, because Changeling didn't even got a Revised book. It is really that Changeling had other rules for it and never got explored enough to stress the connections.

                        Sometimes the strangeness of finding a realm that doesn't work how the spirit realms should work is an interesting theme to explore. And sometimes the game have Garou, but isn't a werewolf game. I'm currently running a CtD game, by the way, and the Prodigals feature heavily into it, so for my game it is good to have the Dreaming as something the Werewolves can't really understand or even know to be there. On a WtA game in which I decided to run this scheme it wouldn't mean the Dreaming is outside the scope of great spirits, either, just that it doesn't work how the Fera expect it to work. But on other WtA games I just incorporate things.

                        On the rules aspect the greatest difference is that Chimerical Reality and Near Dreaming don't really look a lot like the Penumbra and Near Umbra. Chimerical Reality is far closer to the Autumn World than the Penumbra or even the Gauntlet, while the Near Dreaming is something like the Penumbra but harder to reach and even less mirrored to the Autumn World. So it lacks the basic umbral structure of Gauntlet, Penumbra, Realms.

                        Now, is it impossible to work around? Not at all. It may be the same thing (as in, an umbra)? Yes, it can. It neither have to be nor have its connections clearly defined, though.

                        Mind you, some fae deal directly with the Umbra sometimes, but since C20 didn't went that far yet, either, this fact didn't answered any question at all. And the Paths of Balor have a feature where they work one way for Changelings and another for Garou (for those being a one-way trip to Malfeas 'cause reasons).
