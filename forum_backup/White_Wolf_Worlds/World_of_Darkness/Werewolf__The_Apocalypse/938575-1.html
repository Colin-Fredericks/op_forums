                                            If we assume a scenario where there are a dozen or less caerns controlled by the Garou around the world, this is something that drives the game setting because how you divide it up matters.

                                            While characters can find other ways to learn rank 5 gifts, they all pay a price for them. Not only does the extra chiminage begin to add up, if you learn more than one that way you are constantly angering the spirits. A neighbor might be willing to overlook one extremely annoying or offensive action with enough apologies and contrition, but if you keep doing the same thing such patience and forgiveness quickly changes no matter how contrite you seem at the moment. Now imagine if lots of your neighbors begin to do the same thing. You'd probably want to leave the neighborhood. This might have repercussions in the spirit world. Maybe one of the reasons Bone Gnawers don't have Pure Breed or Ancestors anymore is that the tribe was not given access to Rank 5 caerns, so any rank 5 elders had to go outside the system to do it, thus angering the spirits. After enough times and a long enough history, the spirits ended up responding and the resultant anger/pollution eroded their ability to contact Ancestors and killed their pure breed. Perhaps the Warders of Men were similar?

                                            So I think who holds Rank 5 caerns is important as it will be one of the things tribal (or possibly auspice) politics covers. A world which has each tribe jealousy guarding its own caern will leave some tribes out of luck (even if this is only used to emphasize how socially outcast the Glass Walkers and Bone Gnawers are). That is different than a world where there is one big multi-tribal Rank 5 caern that everyone has access to. Or maybe there is a particular caern strongly associated with one auspice, it is multi-tribal, but only for members of a certain auspice. So it is a tool for the ST on how he wants his tribal politics to be within the Nation.

                                            One reason I'd like to brainstorm ideas for Rank 5 caerns is so I know where to send the PCs once they get to that level. If they want to be allowed into that sept, they may want to visit them earlier in their careers, perform chiminage for them, and impress the Garou sept officers.
