                                    Originally posted by Claire Redfield View Post
                                    What have you done with the idea of purging vampires of their Wyrm taint? What would you do with it? Say you take a vampire to the endless silver night of Erebus and plunge them into the molten silver lake. She emerges whole, unbroken by trial, perhaps reforged instead. What happens? Does the vampire gain more control over her Beast? Does she become a creature wholly different from what she was before? Good or ill, what do you think happens with the vampire?
                                    Some time ago, I did something like this. The PC (bona-fide Camarilla vampires) had stumbled on a Fomori team and thinking they were some Tzimisce-creeps (the city was under a Sabbat assault, at the time...) destroyed them, recovering a strange "relic" (indeed, it was a powerful Fetish the fomori had freshly recovered).
                                    Problem was a couple of them were infected by some wyrmish-mold (the one from the Book of the Wyrm). A couple days later, they were on the run from the Sabbat with the two seriously ill (something appalling, for a leech).

                                    At a gas station, they stumbled on a werewolf pack. The Garou were stalking the station, trying to find heavy-tainted travellers and deal with them (furthermore, the spirits in the area where in turmoil, sensing the powerful Fetish).
                                    Fortunately, the alpha was a philodox they dealed with before (the type of deal "don't kill each other, but kill the big bad monster- no, not the Nossie, the other one") and he identified the infection.
                                    While the 3 Garou could maybe kill all the five kindred and take the Fetish, the philodox did prefer not to kill "things" that battled the <true Wyrm> by his side (or so he said).
                                    As such, they struck a deal: the Garou would make a rite for cleansing the two Kindred from the taint, and the Kindred would surrender the Fetish.
                                    Main point is, the cleansing ritual was supposed to be made under sunlight. Stretching the ritual (the theurge was a Gnawers, so he knew how adapt things when in a pinch), they made it so the sunlight didn't need to actually touch the subject.
                                    While enjoying the ritual trying to resist the Rothshreck (vampire's Fox Frenzy) the two experienced an inner rebirth, then running like the hell because they needed to go somewhere safe before the sun would be totally up (they were in a thick grove).

                                    Aside from cleansing the mold, in the following days the two enjoyed a renewed closeness to their "non-wyrmish" side (the human one),
                                    I staged it so the characters would notice little things they would previously disregarded: the perfection of nature, innocent human acts, ignobile acts they could prevent and so on. The, giving free will to the players.
                                    This effect lasted less than a full moon, and while one of them ignored it, the other used these "sparks" for reattuning himself with his human side. It was a great playing, and the character is actually a funny one, as he is a Gargoyle with Humanity 8, which means he is a big rocky, horned, winged bloodsucker that do not register as "of the Wyrm" to shapeshifters.
                                    As a hobby he began painting and now his haven is plastered with paintings of tree, birds and cafes.
                                    He then learned Obfuscate (a Discipline that let the user disappear or alter his appearance), and in his free time is actually using it for helping humans... °__°
                                    One time he beaten to a pulp the thugs trying to bag-snatching a granny and then helped her crossing the road...
