    I really wish pre-Rite of Passage / First Change stories were more common and shared, because no matter how many books I read, big details like that keep slipping under my nose. lol

    But I do appreciate you bringing that to my attention! I'll keep that in mind. Thank you!



    Funny you mentioned that, because I foresaw that and designed the father NPC very specifically to avoid that exact issue. Any NPC part of a player characters' backstory should compliment what the ST already had/has planned, not hijack it (Gift: Blur of the Milky Eye makes this a wee bit easier for the storyteller).

    Plus, if the pacing of the current story ever grinds to a standstill, the father can get up to "just a little bit of trolling" to get the pacing of the Chronicle back up to cadence (or at least that's the idea), and overall was designed to prevent Chronicle-hijacking in mind, in advance.

    Back to Samuelalpha86's original question:

    I didn't mean to hijack the conversation, but for my two cents on adding to the original conversation that this thread is about: If anyone wants to make a Homid Garou (or a Homid Kinfolk) character with a Lupus-born Garou parent, it's already just "normal-ish" in Garou society (even though it's not a common occurrence for obvious reasons) as stated earlier in the thread, so, you're gonna need to add something else to make that detail worth anything more and give it sustenance other than "cool detail, bro".

    Why not have the character's Lupus parent also be their Mentor (a la Background of same name)? Could flushing out this other character add anything to the Story that your players will be playing in (and by extension - the Chronicle)?

    Because as a player option, yes, you can, but if you're not going to go the full 9 yards with it and have it actually add anything to the character, then why are you bothering?

    Did this Lupus parent visit their child in the cradle in Hispo form and grant your player character a phobia flaw relating to big dogs? STUFF LIKE THAT!

    My best advice is "make it mean something, so it sticks around in the long term".
    Maybe it can be a downtime exercise - maybe this homid character has a ton of Lupus-breed half-siblings that he now needs to figure out what to do with, and the player pack can be involved in some way where shenanigans ensue.

    I have no idea if my advice makes sense, but I hope it helps the original poster, and I hope it helps anybody who reads this thread due to having the same question!
