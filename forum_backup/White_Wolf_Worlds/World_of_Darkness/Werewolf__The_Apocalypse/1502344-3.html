    Another point here is that if the majority of "... of Darkness" customers wanted to more to a far more street level oriented game?

    The "new" WoD wouldn't have struggled so much to get everyone to move over to it. We already saw what a heavier focus on street level stuff looks like with early CofD 1e. We saw how people reacted to it. By the time the CofD started to course correct and start building up modes of play that touch of wider scale things, it was too late to stop a lot of fan divisions or people just moving from WoD to something else.

    The WoD and the CofD have both always done better when they're more open games than not.
    It's not like CofD1e was an abject failure, a lot of the backlash was more due to throwing out the old lore and tone for something relatively bland. But some of the more popular parts of CofD are still the street level bits, even if there's sometimes other reasons for that. Sure 2e is more gonzo than 1e was, but it never really left the street/city level established in 2004.

    WoD5 is, of course, even more 'street' than CofD, but I don't think that's the actual reason people dislike it. Elder vampires are still powerful, even if not to the same extremes, although their weaknesses could do with a toning down, and Driven aren't exactly much weaker than Imbued, or at least Imbued who haven't gone full solar. What's changed is the status quo, expected PC factions, and similar things, and it wasn't gradually like when the Camarilla overtook the Anarchs.

    W5 makes a big change to how the Garou fight and organise, and those are the things coming up more than the 'street level' stuff. Rage is suddenly bad, Garou aren't actually meant to actively fight the Wyrm, and I can finally play a Silver Fang anarchist punk rocker from Wakefield (which I'll do just as soon as I have the desire to,, expect it around 2358).

Narratively, this is great because Werewolves are connected to and supposed to care about something much greater than themselves, while vampires aren't. Taking away that Gnosis makes werewolves just as base as vampires.
