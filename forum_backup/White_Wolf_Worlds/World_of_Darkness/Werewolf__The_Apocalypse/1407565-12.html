                                My Werewolf: The Forsaken/Apocalypse Campaign

                                Essentially the idea was that the Apocalypse was prepared and off to a good start in 2004 with the Spirit World having the Red Star as well as the Wyrm overflowing with Power. That was when King Albretch had a vision (supposedly) sent to him from Phoenix that there was a corruption in the heart of the Garou Nation that had been there for millennia. It was the source of the Wyrm being able to influence them to the Impergium, The War of Rage, and so many other horrifyingly STUPID decisions that they'd made over the years.

                                The Great Wolf, a Totem that the Garou had forgotten even existed and all other Wolf Totems were a shade of like Fenris or the Old Wolf of the Woods had been corrupted by the Beast of War long ago. Albretch had a choice between either trying to redeem the Great Wolf or kill him in order to cancel the Apocalypse as it was his madness that was going to drive the Garou to all commit collective suicide by going into the Umbra for a thoroughly unnecessary glorious "Ragnarok"-style end.

                                Evan Heals the Past (A Child of Gaia in my game after being rejected by the Wendigo for obvious damn reasons) argues the former but unsurprisingly, albeit perhaps disappointingly, Albretch heads with the Silver Pack to kill the Great Wolf in a strange vision quest. Possessed by an unknown spirit, Albretch and company kill the Great Wolf and the apocalypse is seemingly averted. The Red Star disappears and the Wyrm retreats. It also pisses Luna off. All Garou can feel her rage over what has been done and it's assumed by some that Albretch royally fucked up and others that he made a harsh but necessary call.

                                Albretch being assassinated by someone is pretty much inevitable and happens but what follows is worse. The Garou Nation had actually been fracturing for centuries and this was the straw that broke the camel's back. Both agreed the Garou Nation had to change but two utterly incompatible visions for that emerged from the conflict.

                                The Pure: A group consisting of mostly Get of Fenris, Silver Fangs, Red Talons, Wendigo, and stragglers from other tribes forms around a "Return to our Roots" ethos that believes the Garou need to undermine modern civilization, slaughter whoever disagrees with them, and keep the bloodlines pure. They also want to cull the Garou Nation of those who have lost their way by being too close to Homid society, befriending Fera, or being "impure." It starts as a minority group but gradually grows as Luna seems to bless their actions as well as give them Gifts to kill other Garou, find new Garou faster, and breed truer.

                                The Forsaken: The group consisting of everyone else but primarily the Children of Gaia, Shadow Lords, Bone Gnawers, Uktena, and Glass Walkers. They have a belief that the old ways were broken and they needed to adapt to the Modern World as well as cast aside the old mistakes. They would be guardians of mortals against the Wyrms and their territory and seek a kind of equilibrium. It puts them a bit on the backfoot against the Pure as they have Luna's wrath against them but know Albretch did the righ(ish) thing. It helps that many other totems back them as well including Phoenix. They also have tentative decent relations with many Fera.

                                So 16 (14 when I ran it), the Garou are pretty firmly divided and the next generation of Garou are starting to pop up who have only ever known this conflict between the two camps. Ironically, the Wyrm conflict has taken something of a backseat as the signs of the apocalypse are, for the moment, gone. A small group of Garou insist that this is false confidence and believe the Apocalypse was delayed rather than averted.

                                Man, can't believe I missed this. Thanks. I am curious though, what about the other changing breeds? Also, does the Spirit world start to resemble it's Forsaken counterpart?
