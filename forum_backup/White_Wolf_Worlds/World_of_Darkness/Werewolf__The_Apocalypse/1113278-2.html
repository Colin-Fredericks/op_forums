                                                    07-14-2017, 09:44 AM
                                                    Originally posted by Sergeant Brother View Post
                                                    Is that supposed to be indicative of the new atmosphere for the WoD or is it just a sort of bullying ST style?
                                                    Its Erickson we are talking about here so i am gonna bet the later. The man is mockingly called Dracula for a reason.

                                                    As for the Impergrium. The way i see it the plot point is not about bringing back the impergrium but instead separating the Garou nation into two faction. The good tribes and the asshole tribes. Putting the impergrium back introduces the problem of lifting the veil one way or another and i doubt the wanna go that way for the now ONE world of darkness.

                                                    Instead using the excuse of the second impergrium to separate the nation it makes things more morally simpler and foments the Furry Captain planet stereotype that Apocalypse has have to fought for a long time.

                                                    See in previous iterations of Werewolf the Garou nation was problematic. It was awful and incompetent and retrograde to a point uselessness. It had a good goal to be sure, saving the world is as good as a goal as it can exist but carrying that goal were people with lots of conflicting moralities.

                                                    You could play the most pure hearted Ma-ti planeteer but you would have to fight alongside a Red talon that believes in killing human children is the way to go for the nation or even a perfectly heroic Silver Fang that saves your life in battle, agrees with your on the main goal and methods to fight the wyrm but he happens to think "consent" is an after thought on his relationship with his mate on the matter of reproduction. Or a perfectly specimen of the fianna who just think that your packmate who is a metis should be "put out of his misery".

                                                    It was that contrast that really helped to give nuance to the game. Everything wasnt just black and white. The main enemy wasnt the cartoonishly evil pentex or BSDs but the nation itself that you hate to fight, compromise or just tolerate to begin to fight that cartoonishly evil bad guy. Its the adult sad moment when you found a group, cause or even political party you agree with and then find that it also houses people that take things too far or just are awful people that happen to agree with you. Its a complex thing to be sure, you cant really deshumanize them entirely because on a certain level they are you. You have common ground and you need them in some way or another.

                                                    Not so much with the new divide. Now the schism has shove the problematic tribes into the Sanctum of Gaia and the "good" tribes into the Concordat of Stars. Now any moral complexity is gone from the Nation which each faction in their own echo chamber with concordat being obviously the PC choice being able to just point out to the Sanctum as the "Other", the "bad apples" and then self-appoint themselves as the "good tribes" with the setting more or less agreeing with the statement when the harsh reality of the setting comes to question morally dubious actions. And truly now reducing the setting moral complexity to "Furry Captain planet"
                                                    Last edited by LokiRavenSpeak; 07-14-2017, 09:48 AM.
