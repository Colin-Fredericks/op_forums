                On the subject of adversaries using an APC (or other similarly light armored vehicle) against werewolves, I have my own $.02 I'd like to add to the pot. I bristle a little bit at the broad statement that using APCs against werewolves is stupid and would never work. I'd instead point out that tactical use of such equipment requires adherence to doctrine that has been painstakingly learned over the last sixty years or so.

                1. Speed is life. The quote is actually attributed to Oswald Bolke, a First World War German fighter ace who was talking about airplanes... but the concept applies nicely to maneuver warfare too. In short, anything that stays still (and can't hide) on a battlefield is pretty much dead. Even a main battle tank -- if it gets disabled -- is in some serious sh**. As such, you generally don't see APCs outside of flat, open terrain (the recent conflict in Afghanistan being a glaring, rare, and controversial exception).

                2. Armored vehicles are supposed to fight at standoff ranges. Close quarters is where the APC's greatest weakness lies. Now you do see their use in urban combat... but that's mainly because of the well developed road networks that you usually find in urban terrain. Even then they tend to get picked off a lot.

                3. The most dangerous thing about an armored personnel carrier is not the carrier, but the personnel. The real purpose of the APC is to get the troops to where they need to go quickly and with a minimum of casualties en-route. Once at the point of contact however, the whole idea is to dismount your troops so they can do the actual fighting. (In recent times -- specifically Iraq -- there was a move away from dismounting troops for force protection reasons in favor of "Thunder Runs", but this was highly controversial, not terribly effective, in some cases counterproductive, and at any rate, really signaled a worrisome lack of strategy).

                So if your First Team / fomori / Dead Man's Hand or whatever, is fighting the Garou in, like, Kansas or somewhere -- it could work. Just so long as they keep their distance (Jam Technology has a limited range), make liberal use of ranged weapons, and always, always keep moving. That said, if you look at the basic doctrine, the APC is precisely the wrong choice for fighting the Garou even conceptually. First off, werewolves (and especially the prize jewels -- caerns) tend to be found in the deep wilderness. Such broken terrain robs motorized transport of its most valuable trait: Mobility. (As an example, you don't see many pictures of APCs or tanks from the Vietnam Conflict). The deep forest also inhibits the use of standoff weapons, since for the most part, you can't hit what you can't see. As such, (and this is another reason why you didn't see them used much in Vietnam) most such combat will be a close range -- tactically the worst situation you can get since the APC is at its weakest at close quarters while werewolves are at their strongest. Lastly, since the whole purpose of the APC is to get your dismounts into combat, using APCs to somehow change the equation when your leg-infantry is basically little more than fodder to a Garou warrior -- is really doing nothing more than bringing new meaning to the term "meals on wheels".

                But some idiot adversary who's watched too many Michael Bay movies and whose tactical expertise mainly stems from playing Call of Duty a lot? Sure. That could be fun.
