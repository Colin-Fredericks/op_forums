              Mother's Touch doesn't as written, cure diseases. WtA has a dearth of anti-disease and anti-toxin powers because they basically need to be supernaturally strong to matter anyway, and they want supernatural toxins to be some sort of threat. Keep in mind though, that the Garou have an extremely versatile toolkit in the forms of the Rite of Binding and the Rte of Spirit Awakening. If the Garou know they're facing something that likes using supernatural poisons and toxins, they can get temporary protection via those Rites.

              That said, if you're worried about your PCs.... maybe don't give the antagonists 10 Willpower?

              Also... these aren't games that are designed for crossover, how Quietus works against Garou is kinda up to you because there's lots of places where how vampire Disciplines and werewolf powers/Gifts aren't worded in compatible ways.

                                                  Myth and recent cultural inventions just aren't the same thing. Gremlins is a relatively new concept that's on the cusp of myth and recent invention, but it's also a direct modification of goblin myths mixed with changing technology. The mogwai from the film Gremlins don't really draw from aviation gremlins, goblins, or Chinese mogwai in any meaningful sense. They're more like imps but called gremlins to remove the religious implication of "imp" to a western audience..

                                                          Keep in mind that in the WoD most legendary/mythical creatures were actually real in-universe. Unicorns, griffins, dragons, etc. either are still around hidden from human eyes, or were once living creatures that coexisted with the rest of the world until the end of the Mythic Age pushed more and more of them into the Umbra.

                                                          For us, in the real world, a mythical creature from tales from 5,000 years ago, and a made up movie monster from the last few years might be equally imaginary. For Garou in the WoD, it's very likely the older one is seen as a real thing that either can be found to this day to supernaturals such as themselves, or were until humanity spreading the Weaver around killed them off.
