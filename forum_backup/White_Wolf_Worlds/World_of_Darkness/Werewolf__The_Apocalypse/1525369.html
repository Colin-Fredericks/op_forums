    Help in making a (stylized) pack of Werewolf foes

    11-12-2023, 06:22 AM
    Hello Werewolf fans!

    I'm running a Vampire Dark Ages 20th Anniversary campaign, and the characters are due to meet a Werewolf pack guarding a ruined tower they are going to reclaim as their own Domain (we are running a modified version of the Transylvania Chronicles).

    I imagined them as a Shadow Lord-led pack (the chief being an ancestor to Margrave Konietzko), trying to purify the Tihuta pass from Kupala's influence.

    They should be a challenge, an almost lethal one, but ultimately approachable, with a combination of cunning maneuvers and strategic confrontation.

    Can I ask you some creative input to give shape to an interesting group of foes? I'm not very familiar with Legacy Werewolf rules, though I have access to WtA 20 core, so even a simplified set of numbers and Powers would be enough.

    My current coterie Is made of

    A Tzimisce Knight with Horrid Form
    A Tzimisce Knight / sorceror with Thaumaturgy 5 (Movement of the Mund)
    A Salubri Warrior (Valeren 4)
    A Nosferatu Spy (Obfuscate 4)
    A Malkavian Oracle (Dementation 4)
    A Setite infernalist hunter (Serpentis 4)
    ​​​​​​
    they also have some other minor Disciplines, and should reach the Wolves in some time, they Will probably get access at least to a couple other level 3 Disciplines.

    The Tzimisce Knight has some shady deals with Kupala, so they could possibily enlist help by some Banes.

    Thank you in advance !

      There isn't really an "almost lethal" with Garou. They are amazingly lethal. They live in a different genre of horror apart from vampires where everyone gets torn and shredded up at a moment's notice; not only would you properly have to worry about them, but anything involving the appearance of an entire pack would also entail having whatever enemy they're actually after showing up (they still don't just go after vampires, even if they are Shadow Lords), which is likely to be something like a group of fomori that can also do terrible, terrible things to the coterie, as combat-worthy possessed can also somewhat easily get to Strength 9 and have natural weapons and extra soak, while those of a less combat-primary bent will have an array of distractions and mind tricks like supernatural seduction or direct psychic attacks that can leave the unwary with trickles of pulped brain leaking out their nose. Powerful banes are also somewhat likely to be able to just flat out wipe you and the pack out by accident (nexus crawlers are like unreality tornadoes that can leave everyone around without their faces and standing on a floor of lava, silver, or silver lava, with their bones cracked or replaced by swamp debris).

      If you're playing this as a more casual kind of contact, the Lords are more likely to just use Raven's Curse/Under the Gun on you (-2 to the difficulty to hit the target with missile weapons like arrows, making a potential staking more likely; the curse can also persist for some time, possibly even permanently) and let the chips fall where they may until you become more persistent in bothering them. But the moment that happens, you're going to find out just why Horrid Form is no real match for Crinos. Or even combat fomori, really.

                                  Originally posted by Manfr View Post

                                  Reclaiming the Pass would probably see the characters engage Kinfolk and Spirits first, and perhaps meet the Wolves singularly to get a first impression of the threat, but meeting the pack and taking the Tower would be Boss Fight territory, I'd say.
                                  I feel a primer on social dynamics of the Garou would help here.

                                  Garou packs are usually centered around a Caern. The Caern is an especially blessed area where the gauntlet is thin and where the location is guarded by a powerful Spirit that resonates with the location. This place naturally draws many spirits to it as a congregating place, and some of these are drafted as defenders of the locale in the umbra and the real world.

                                  The local Garou gather around the Caern as their holy place and organise their resources in the broader location of the Caern - this construct is called the Sept and is both a logistical and political core that the Garou revolve around. The Sept considers all Garou that gravitate to the Caern as members and often contains multiple groups of kinfolk families - be it local wolves or humans.

                                  A small Caern will have a single pack tending to it and a handful of kinfolk around it. The larger the Caern, the larger the Sept. In the Dark Ages most Septs are of a single Tribe. The Garou most competant/politically active to Hold Sept positions take care of the spirits, laws, stories, rituals and other day to day operations. Occasionally a Sept may even appoint a kinfolk to a position like this.

                                  Now kinfolk are not combatants, even those that would be considered warriors among humans are incredibly weak and vulnerable to the things the Garou fight. They will keep them safe and while they may help with day to day work, scouting,information gathering, trading, crafts, rites,hunting and protection, they will not be taken to battle or sent to places Garou packs will. It is too dangerous for them and the Garou are law bound to protect them. This will become more flexible in the modern day, but as it stands the kinfolk are too valuable to waste on the tasks of the Garou.

                                  Spirits are also not servants of the Garou, they are partners who expect payment or service for the work they do.

                                  The first and greatest task of any Sept is to keep their Caern safe. This is one of those things that no Garou will even discuss. Everything else in the litany is interpreted and discussed, buy this never is.

                                  Outside this task the common tasks of the Garou of the Sept is fighting the Wyrm, doing their end of the deal with the spirits and pursuing their own agendas if they are lucky to find time for that between the duties to their Caern, their Sept and their family.

                                  Now considering the context here, what is this tower, what does domain mean to you and how would it pertain to a Sept that they would deploy a pack there?

                                  Nb. If you meant dormant Caern by "domain", you have chosen the thing Garou will do anything to protect and recover and it's not going to be a skirmish - it is going to be an all out war.

                                  I would strongly suggest you look into something less valuable or something that might provoke an uneasy alliance to resolve.​

                                  What doesn't kill you, makes you... stranger.

                                      It's not about a dice pool measuring contest, I think most posters in this thread would agree that a direct confrontation between vampires and werewolves under WtA rules just wouldn't be much fun for the players. Even a non-combat oriented beginner level Garou has the potential to dust a Neonate in a single round, given some unlucky rolls - if you use WtA rules for them, they aren't VtM NPCs anymore but WtA PCs that happen to be under your control. And werewolf PCs are designed to shred through groups of monsters.

                                      Balancing them by using Disciplines is a lot easier - if you really want to use WtA rules for the flavor, my recommendation is to at least use VtM combat rules and Disciplines in melee for them. You can bring in the unknown / surprises with their Gifts, just.. take care with the ones geared towards combat.
