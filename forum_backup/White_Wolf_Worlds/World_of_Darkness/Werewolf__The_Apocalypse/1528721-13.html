            For the record, my own attitude is that what’s left of the Garou Nation is still fighting the Wyrm wherever opportunity allows: for my money the situation as described in the core book makes more sense as a slump from which Our Heroes (The PCs) have to rescue the Garou than as a final, irreversible decline.

                                                        Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post
                                                        I'd rather discuss, "Are the Garou STILL fighting the Wyrm or not?"
                                                        The short answer is, "Of course."

                                                        The Wyrm wants to destroy and corrupt the world, kill or twist every werewolf and everyone and everything thing that werewolf cares about. The only options are to fight the Wyrm, serve the Wyrm or die to the Wyrm. So even if a werewolf doesn't want to fight, they don't have much choice in the matter.

                                                        The big change between the editions seems to be in how aggressive werewolves are in fighting the Wyrm. In earlier editions, the Litany commanding werewolves to fight the Wyrm wherever it breeds was very important. Werewolves would travel long distances (down to the Amazon or deep into the Umbra) to hunt down and fight the Wyrm. Things like Moonbridges made it fairl;y simple to travel vast distances without the ST or PC's getting bogged down in logistics. This also went back to the one of the basic concepts in environmentalism - that things which happen on the other side of the world can still have a direct effect on you.


                                                        That seems less of the case in W5. The Tribes got together and decided they weren't going to go crusade and aggressively fight the Wyrm anymore. They were going to be more passive, and just defend their home bases, letting the Wyrm breed and grow stronger in its home bases. The Cult of Fenris is the group that's spending its time traveling around, fighting the Wyrm. So in W5, while werewolves absolutely do fight the Wyrm, it's generally only when the Wyrm shows up in that werewolf's neighborhood. Non-Cult werewolves typically aren't going to be going into the Wyrm's neighborhoods to kick ass. That's a big distinction between the presentation of the Cult of Fenris and the rest of the Werewolf Nation.
