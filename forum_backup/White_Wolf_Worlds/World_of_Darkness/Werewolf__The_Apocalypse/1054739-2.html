                                Well, when a crossover situation is presented on the WtA forum, it is generally expected that WtA Lore and WtA Rules will be primary. Methuselahs are probably not going to go full frontal on a Sept of Garou. They will manipulate human institutions to make life unbearable on the Garou until either the Garou abandon the area or the Garou get themselves killed. When the Garou are gone, then the Methuselah will move in and corrupt the Caern spirit to create their ideal spiritual hellhole, probably with the help of Black Spiral Dancers or Nephandi that he bullied into service.

                                After they have been driven out of their Caern, I would suggest the Garou learn about the attempts to corrupt the Caern spirit (perhaps from dreams messages sent by the Caern spirit itself). An entire campaign could be built around the Garou gaining the allies necessary to save the Caern spirit from corruption before it is too late, with the Garou having to make alliances with many creatures they would rather kill. Methuselah Kindred tend to have ancient enemies among the Kindred, so the Garou may be forced to ally with ancient Kindred to destroy the Methuselah. Its Black Spiral Dancer servants are the enemies of all Gaian Fera, so the Garou may be forced to ally with Bastet, Corax, Gurahl, and Nuwisha to regain the lost Caern. Its Nephandi servants are the enemies of all Tradition Mages, so the Garou may be forced to ally with members of the Cult of Ecstasy, Dreamspeakers, Order of Hermes, and the Verbena to regain the lost Caern. In effect, the stealing of the Caern can be a trigger for a local Grand Alliance that would take a dozen game sessions to form and would result in a series of epic battles that would take a dozen game sessions to finish. Of course, it would have to be a level 5 Caern to justify that level of effort, but it would make a great campaign.
