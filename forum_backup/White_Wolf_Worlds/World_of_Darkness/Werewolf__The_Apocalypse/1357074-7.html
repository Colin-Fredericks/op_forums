    One interesting thing about the Nuwisha in the first edition Player's Guide (I can't recall if other sourcebooks repeat this) is that there is a story that the Nuwisha once had auspices, but because Coyote/Trickster once angered Luna she took them away. This possibly points to the Nuwisha once being a Garou tribe (as opposed to created ex nihilo as a Fera breed), although perhaps the change occurred before the Severing when the Realm and Penumbra were one and thus before "real" history began.

    In order to accommodate that (and that Silent Striders with Pure Breed are obviously werejackals - at least what is now called the golden wolf and possibly the golden jackals, but possibly not the side striped or black-backed jackals), I hold in my chronicles that the Garou are more like were-"sub-set of canis" rather than the more limited werewolves (were-"canis lupus"). But I draw the limits to anything outside of that (including certain other canis species like dholes and African painted/wild dogs). This isn't a true scientific distinction, but I have to draw the limits somewhere while keeping to established lore. I definitely exclude any dogs - domestication killed the "gene" even if the dog breed goes feral afterwards.

    If I had to rule in game, I'd probably say that a wolf-dog hybrid could not change, but a wolf could have a hybrid as an ancestor, and it would not prevent its descendants from changing.

                I'll start simple
                Mockery Breeds
                I take the head cannon offered in tribe Book Mockeries that gives the Mockery Breeds a previous History as Gaian Fera prior to meeting extinction one way or the other, with PENTEX using the remains as basis for their modern versions, Learning this could easily be why tribes like the Glass Walkers and Children of Gaia are reluctant to Try cloning to bring extinct breeds back.

                Cyberdogs
                The Fallen Camp of the Glass Walkers truly did suffer from corruption, and the Glass walkers did deal with it, but the banishment and Disavowing afterwards, was but a coverup. After Culling or Chasing off the Truly Fallen elements of the Camp, they knew the rest of the Garou Nation would never accept them, so they went into hiding in the Cyber Realm,. Covertly chasing fallen Cyberdogs and continuing to expand as a technically independent faction, but leadership still contacts the Glass Walker leaders behind closed doors, they've even begun finding fera sympathetic to their methods [still trying to work out who might try them]

                The Perfect Metis
                Other Fera have their own Equivalent prophesies, unfortunately I only have one that I've come up with so Far, open to suggestions, but the Mokole Equivalent is The Living Innocent. The Living Innocent is one born of two Mokole, who lives, Most prophesies about this one assume its a Girl, that she will serve as mother to all the innocent who were never truly born before, its also assumes she will have perfect Mnesis, recalling the Time of Kings and the Wonderwork itself, possibly heralding the return of the Drachid and Saurid Breeds, but like the Perfect Metis the Jruy is out on if any of that is a good thing or a bad thing.

                Purpose
                The Garou and Feral are all told by spirits that they are each made with a Gaia given purpose, but even spirits are fallible and can be flat out wrong, Gaia gave them assignments, but those assigned duties are not their sole reason to be, and they may easily outlive their duties, either to take a new one, or decide their own fates.

                Aliens
                I do head canon that Proper Space Aliens exist in the setting and may have their own equivalent Supernaturals, So far I have a couple basic and pulpy concepts for them.

                Grytez, a Flatwoods Monster looking alien, that uses Sons of Ether style Magitech, (think Breath of the wild Sheika tech for the aesthetic)

                Sekali, basically Predator but as their own changing breeds too, hunting with advanced fetish tech, the hunts and trophies enable them to preform something resembling the Rite of Sacred Rebirth, changing their beast side to that of prey they find truly worthy

                    Some neat ideas. Thanks for sharing.

                    Originally posted by Crazyjabberwock View Post
                    Aliens
                    I do head canon that Proper Space Aliens exist in the setting and may have their own equivalent Supernaturals, So far I have a couple basic and pulpy concepts for them.
                    I have space aliens too, but I always use the term Deep Umbral invaders to provide some deniability because not all players may welcome "science fiction" in their game of supernatural horror. It coats everything appropriately from a Garou perspective. It also provides something for the Wagnerians - a seldom used concept in my experience - to have encountered and can warn the other Garou about them, or give them something to encounter in a theoretical chronicle based around the Wagnerians.

                    I use Lovecraftian concepts of aliens more than anything else. Not only did he mix science fiction and the supernatural well in his horror stories, his creatures are so strange they don't remind people of science fiction since we're used to aliens as people in rubber suits and make up on TV and movies. Things like Glaaki, Colors Out of Space and Flying Polyps seem more like monsters than "aliens". The Wyrm Incarna the Garou know as G'louogh is actually just as an aspect of a Celestine of another world with its own alien ecosystem. But creatures from that world are invading this one and acting like invasive species trying to outcompete and overwhelm Earth's natural lifeforms.

                    I have also changed PENTEX extensively. The subsidiaries with good distinct hooks on their own, I use as their own separate Wyrm factions. Others that I don't find actually gameable, I just ignore. But the core idea of PENTEX itself - mass polluters of the Earth - is something I've kept, but basically made them the aliens of the movie They Live. It's basically a conspiracy of aliens who want to de-terraform Earth into a more suitable world for their own race. While they do own some companies outright, it's mostly a conspiracy found around the world that doesn't require them to outright own anything. They simply use recruited agents in companies to continue to spew out whatever pollutants the aliens need to make our world more hospitable to them. In the West, they infiltrated industrial businesses, but in the old Communist countries they infiltrated the socialist industries (and because the Communist countries were far worse polluters, caused much more damage there than elsewhere) so PENTEX has always been around the world.

                    They use a cover story to recruit people. They present themselves as patrons of mankind, always helping them advance to the next stage of evolution similar to a lot of ancient astronaut theories, ideas about panspermia, 2001's Monoliths, and uplifting species. They claim to want to help humanity enter “the next age of man” - genetic changes to prolong life and improve their ability to survive the great Earth Changes that are coming. That provides suitable pretext for people to succumb to naked greed and indifference. It also helps explain the process of some of them becoming fomori - their "new form" is what will help them survive the terrible catastrophe the aliens are trying to "prevent" or "delay" so they can "save" the "best" of humanity. Garou use the term PENTEX to refer to this conspiracy because it was the name used by an early front company when the Garou first encountered the group. Without any other name, it stuck and was used to refer to these beings and their activites when they were encountered afterwards. And the number 5 seems to be sacred to these creatures, so groupings of 5 helped cement the idea of PENTEx.

                        new Home brew Fetish Open to feed back
                        Memory Canvas: Level Five, Gnosis 8
                        Invented by Mnetics as a means of more easily sealing or accessing memories, real or implanted without needing to go into meditative mnesis quests, the Memory Canvas is first Synchronized to an individual with a drop of blood, this blood becomes the first brush stroke of an art depicting the memory or set of memories, either a real one intended to be captured, or a False one to be implanted, once drawn fully the memory is impossible to forget until the Fetish’s Secondary function is used. By activating it again and Flipping it backwards, the back facing the user (often highlighted with hanging hooks or strips of tape and sometimes a bit of writing labeling the person and the memory in question) This Seals the Memories inside the Canvas, rendering them inaccessible unless activated again, anyone flipping the Canvas without activating it will only see the other side is also the back of the canvas.

                        Mnetics often use two on a person to turn them into someone different, often using it to create Manchurian agents, several Iterations of the Infamous Deep-Purple-Dark have been made using this technique, they will also often use them on themselves to capture memories they prefer not to know most of the time but too important to not have on call when needed. Though the Canvas is currently the most popular form, some Mnetics will keep larger Portraits or tablets or even Tapestries, one hidden in a vault to seal unwanted memories, and one in a gallery to showcase their preferred memories.

                        Destroying the Canvas permanently liberates the memories, With False Memories typically fading with out the Canvas or a Mnetic’s intervention to hold them in place.

                        Most Memory Canvases have a Corrupted Memory Spirit or an Innocent bound to them.

                                            On a more serious note:

                                            * Evan Heals the Past is a Philodox Child of Gaia. His unfortunate implications pretty much all dissapear if you assume he was immediately rejected by the Wendigo and found his place among the hippie werewolves. He also probably survived to be a more cunning and less stereotypical werewolf due to Albretch and Maria's protection.

                                            * Pentex is patronized by the Wyrm but generally hands off in its management. If the Garou weren't so mono-focused on putting everything into categories of their religion, they'd view it the same way as the Sabbat: it is an objectively evil organization that has its own goals but serves the Wyrm merely because its incredibly corrupt and greedy. Looter capitalism is it's own reward with the Wyrm.

                                            * The Stargazers left the Garou Nation because of a very GOOD idea rather than randomly. They joined the Fera Courts as part of a desire to try to mend the War of Rage wounds. They hope to eventually broker a lasting piece with all of Gaia's children.

                                            * Samuel Haight was the subject of a lot of urban legends, Ragabash mockery, and Galliard tall tales. The real man was actually a pretty terrifying Kinfolk hedge magician and werewolf hunter who killed something like fifty Garou (including BSD). There was nothing silly about him and when he was finally put down, it was was by a pack denied their glory for it by politics.

                                            * The First Metis/First Ronin was also Caine. Garou do not acknowledge this connection to vampires.

                                            * The Patriarch is another face of the Celestine Jupiter that represents all of the Skyfathers of mythology. Helios is another potential candidate. Generally, most Garou think there's a dozen spirits inspiring figures from Abrahamic religion and the Black Furies just take it personally.

                                            This fits well with the idea that Grandfather Thunder is a Incarna of Jupiter.

                                            * Similarly, Christian Garou do exist but they worship Gaia as God and assume Jesus was a werewolf. Mostly, they're Children of Gaia or Shadow Lords (oddly enough) and are considered quite queer. However, plenty of older changing religious Garou bring some element of their old spirituality to flavor what they learn to be objectively true afterward.

                                            Also ditto the Grandfather Thunder=Abrahmic God to the Shadow Lords.

                                            * The Beast of War is the source of Rage and not Luna. That is the Garou denying they're ALL Wyrm tainted to a degree as are most of Gaia's children.

                                            * The Silent Striders killed Sutekh the Antediluvian and he is the one who cursed them. Oddly, the Followers of Set fully acknowledge this but other vampires either assume the Antediluvains aren't real or believe they're unkillable. The Followers of Set just want to bring him BACK.

                                            * Shinzui Industries is actually huge and powerful as well one of the guiding forces of technological development in the world. It isn't as big as Pentex, though, because Pentex is ALSO serving the Weaver's ends. The Weaver has put most of its eggs in the basket of the Union and is such a devious manipulator that the Garou don't even know it exists. The Weaver knows the best way to win the war is to make your enemy ignore you as the lesser evil.

                                            * Endron Oil was put down by the Monkeywrenchers.Sadly, Pentex just dipped into its piggy bank and bought United Kingdom Petroleum instead. You can recognize them by their big green and yellow UKP sign.

                                            * The Nuwisha are straight up the 13th tribe of the Garou. Literally, the only thing that keeps them from being Garou is the fact that (prior to the Stargazers) they were closer to the Fera than their kind as well as the fact the Wendigo and Uktena have a history of hating them that carried over to their European relations. Basically, "Are these guys Garou?" "No!" "Do they care?" "No."
