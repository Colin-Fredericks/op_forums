    How Clement Lupus showcases W5's biggest flaw

    03-23-2024, 04:45 PM
    WARNING: This essay discusses topics such as bestiality, sexual abuse of an intellectually disabled creature, animal death and some darker aspects of domestication. PLEASE do not read unless you can handle these topics.




    One of the enduring critiques of WtA over the years has been its inclusion of the lupus breed. To some people, the fact that the garou can breed with wolves is a bridge too far. It would not be a problem, but many times these people will come to spaces discussing WtA and call breeding with wolves “doggy rape.” Which sours a discussion fast.

    What these people do not grasp, is that all garou can communicate with wolves just fine. They can ask for consent from the wolf and the wolf can give informed consent back. That used to be the end of the matter.

    Until W5 came around.

    From the previews alone, we were told that the wolf form and the giant monster wolf form can be mistaken for dogs. It was met with amusement, as wolves alone tower over any sort of wolf-like dog breed. Nevermind the monster wolf form of hispo which is a wolf tank. All of this was met with humour.

    Until someone noted there was a merit that made you look like a dog.

    Originally posted by W5 p.98
    Clement Lupus: Your lupus form appears more dog-like than wolf-like, and unless they have a reason to believe so, no human onlooker who sees you in lupus form thinks that you’re a wolf


    The last sentence is the lynchpin here. “No human onlooker who sees you in lupus form thinks that you are a wolf.” The problem with this sentence is, that many huskies and agouti german shepherd dogs are thought to be wolves by ignorant people. There have been many loose dogs shot as 'wolves', the opposite of what was intended.

    It is effortless for an ST to declare that the lupus-form of a character with this merit looks like a golden retriever.

    What takes this from a silly merit to something a bit more serious, is the fact that wolf born are still in W5. Merits like this in Werewolf the Forsaken work, because there is no actual wolf heritage involved. But in W5, wolf born garou exist and since this merit is not a magical power but the actual physical appearance of the lupus form, it brings up a lot of uncomfortable questions.

    At the start of the essay, I said that garou can communicate with wolves and so ask informed consent from them. This is possible, because an adult wolf is truly an adult of their species. A dog, however, is mentally around a 4 month old wolf.

    Among wolves, a year old wolf is still an immature teenager as wolves are mature at 2 years. So a 4 month old wolf is truly a child in wolf terms and therefore in the terms of garou. A garou trying to breed with a dog is truly the “doggy rape” people accused Legacy version of WtA of.

    And W5 just made that canon.

    Please note, I do not think the authors intentionally made this gaff. W5 is written often with only the human born garou in mind, with the wolf born included very rarely. The book even tells you not to have more animal Touchstones than one, because the game is focused on garou relations with humans. But this mistake truly showcases where W5 flounders. Aspects are included but not thought out well, such as giving no mechanical ability to have a custom patron, the book contradicting itself and so on.

    The idea of allowing garou to take on a dog-like form is not bad, there is a gift called “Guise of the Hound” which does that. What makes Clement Lupus such a bad idea is that it is not a power the garou uses but the garou's actual lupus form.

    This was a darker essay, but I hope it was informative.
