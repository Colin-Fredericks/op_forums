Without going into much detail, Greeks had the tradition of having their stronger warriors on the right wing of the phalanx, to counteract the right-hand drift. So knowing that the Spartans would be there, he basically confronted the strong Spartan right wing of about 12 men deep phalanx with a 50!! men deep formation and cavalry, the Sacred Band was indeed part of this formation. Then to nullify the weakness that created in the middle and right parts of his formation he had his army advance in a diagonal fashion, creating the now famous Echelon formation. Obviously, the Spartans were not expecting such an onslaught from what should be the weak part of the enemy army and lost the engagement suffering heaving losses including most of the Ippeis, the famous 300-men contingent that was charged with protecting their kings.
