                                              Theoretical outline:

                                              Year of the Rat
                                              • Stats for mundane rats, including as vermin swarms
                                              • The Rat totem and her brood
                                              • The various types of rats native to East Asia, including the giant rat of Sumatra (which is a real thing)
                                              • The Wangtong Bone Gnawers of East Asia and the Beast Courts
                                              • The Nezumi Ratkin of Japan and their counterparts in China and other parts of East Asia
                                              • New gifts for Wangtong and Nezumi
                                              • The legend of Raigo the Iron Rat
                                              • Rat Yokai as Japanese Bygones, including fire-rats,
                                              • Progenitor experiments in weaponizing rats
                                              • A look at ghoul rats for Vampire
                                              • An alternate Nosferatu centered form of Protean that involves transforming into a giant rat or into a swarm of rats
                                              • The ghost rats which are briefly mentioned in the back of Orpheus
                                              • The Great Hanoi Rat Massacre of 1902
                                              Year of the Ox
                                              • Stats for mundane oxen, cattle, water buffalo, and yaks
                                              • Ox as a totem, as well as the Bull totem
                                              • Ox-inspired Gifts
                                              • Examining the Apis as they might have existed in prehistoric East Asia
                                              • Ox-Head, one of the two demons who are fearsome guardians of the Yellow Springs
                                              • The hero-king Chiyou and legendary emperor Shennong of China (possibly Apis)
                                              • The Japanese god Tenjinsama
                                              • The Japanese Kudan and other Bygones
                                              • The Japanese ushi-onna as a possible Changeling kith
                                              • Bull and Ox demons
                                              • The Kindred habit of referring to mortals as Kine (cattle) and the Herd background
                                              • Potence and Fortitude, and the concept of an Ox-inspired fighting style
                                              Year of the Tiger
                                              • Stats for mundane tigers, and a look at the various types of them in Asia.
                                              • Tiger as a potential totem
                                              • Tiger-inspired Gifts for Khan and others
                                              • A look at the East Asian Khan of the Beast Courts, including the unique cultures of Khan in South East Asia and Indonesia
                                              • The Tiger Army of the Wu Lung
                                              • The White Tiger of the West
                                              • A bit about the Howl of the Devil Tiger Dharma
                                              • The tiger as an alternate Protean form
                                              • The Tiger Style of Kung Fu
                                              Last edited by No One of Consequence; 01-31-2020, 07:53 PM.

                                                  Year of the Rabbit (and of the Cat)

                                                  So, interestingly, the Asian Zodiac is not uniform throughout East Asia. While the Chinese version is the most well known, other countries have slight variations, and one of these is that Vietnam uses the Cat instead of the Rabbit. Which makes this next one a bit easier.
                                                  • Stats for rabbits and hares, as well as small cats
                                                  • Rabbit/Hare as a totem
                                                  • Potential rabbit (and cat) themed Gifts, Rotes, Talismans, Fetishes, and Treasures
                                                  • The legend of the Rabbit of the Moon, who in Chinese folklore is believed to be making the Elixir of Immortality, which ties in with the Wu T'ian.
                                                  • The story of the Hare of Inaba
                                                  • The Tokki as a possible new type of commoner Hsien
                                                  • The Bakeneko as a possible Japanese/Asian counterpart to the Bubasti and Cealican
                                                  • The Nekomata, the "Cats" described in the original A World of Darkness
                                                  • Cats (and rabbits) as familiars among East Asian Mages
                                                  • Cat and Rabbit Pooka in East Asian communities
                                                  • The Nyan type of commoner Hsien
                                                  • The mythical cabbit (a cat/rabbit hybrid) as a Bygone
                                                  Year of the Dragon
                                                  • Stats for crocodilians and other large reptiles of East Asia
                                                  • The importance of dragons in East Asian mythology, folklore and culture
                                                  • Crocodile as a totem from an East Asian/Beast Courts perspective
                                                  • Dragon themed Gifts, Rotes, Fetishes, Talismans, and other artifacts
                                                  • The Zhong Lung Mokole, their place in the Beast Courts and how they vary around East Asia
                                                  • The Wyrm in East Asian/Beast Court cosmology and the Ebon Dragon
                                                  • The Azure Dragon of the East and a bit about the Black Turtle of the North
                                                  • A bit about The Thrashing Dragons Dharma
                                                  • A bit about the Wu Lung, the Dragon Emperor and the Dragon School
                                                  • Asian dragons as Bygones
                                                  • The Dragon King of the Sea
                                                  • Dragon Style Kung Fu
                                                  Year of the Snake
                                                  • Stats for various East Asian snakes, and snake venom as poison
                                                  • The place of snakes in East Asian cultures and mythologies
                                                  • Various serpent totems in the Beast Courts and East Asia
                                                  • Snake themed Gifts, Rotes, Fetishes, Talismans, and other artifacts
                                                  • The Nagah among the Beast Courts and in East Asia
                                                  • The Fu Shi of the Hsien
                                                  • Empress Wa and the creation of mankind
                                                  • The Legend of Madam White Snake
                                                  • The nine-headed serpent monster Xiangliu
                                                  • The Japanese Buddhist goddess Benzaiten
                                                  • The Korean goddess Eopsin
                                                  • Snakes as familiars
                                                  • Serpent Bygones of East Asian, including the Bashi/Uwabami, Tsuchinoko, and Naga
                                                  • A look at the possibility of Setites in East Asia and their unique regional Cults
                                                  • Ghouled serpents
                                                  • Snake Pooka among East Asian communities
                                                  • Snake Style Kung Fu
                                                  Last edited by No One of Consequence; 01-31-2020, 07:51 PM.
