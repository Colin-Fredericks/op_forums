        Originally posted by MyWifeIsScary View Post
        -Where's this 'bitten thing you're all talking about? Where is that?
        That isn't in "Changing Ways" (which is form OPP's W20 line and a book for tabletop WtA) but it is from "Mind's Eye Theatre: Werewolf the Apocalpyse" by "By Night Studios" (BNS), and it is a "Live Action Role Playing" (LARP) rules book.

        W20 was created after CCP closed the White Wolf offices in Atlanta and OPP was created. OPP was (and still is) licensing what then was called "classic World of Darkness" (cWoD). But OPP only had/has licenses for tabletop RPG and, if I'm not mistaken, occasionally merchandise (on a case to case basis, I guess).

        "By Night Studios" (BNS) arouse, from what I gathered, from the LARP community and got the license for LARP rule books, as well as some merchandise, most notably clan symbol pins to wear while larping. They reworked the "Mind's Eye Theatre" (MET) LARP game system and released a new Vampire LARP game. Then they moved on to Werewolf. Martin Ericsson, who was at CCP at the time, supposedly gave them some input on how to change the Setting. When White Wolf was sold from CCP to Paradox, he became the head of Storytelling, therefore seemingly giving some legitimacy to the changes BNS made to the setting.

        While these changes were not in W20, people expected and feared, they might be a glimpse on what WW5th would be like.
        Since then Martin Ericsson has left White Wolf and unlike Vampire5th Werewolf 5th will be produced by another company: Hunter Entertainment.
        We have little to no idea, what they are up to, and what Paradox-White Wolf is demanding to be incorporated into the Setting.

                Well, we don't know what changes to the Setting stem from him, and what were other authors' ideas and what was feedback from the LARP community.

                The most major change are the destruction of all notable caerns (and septs, I guess) and the Garou nation split into two factions, basically one traditional and one progressive, with the Silent Strider staying neutral. The more progressive faction also became closer to the remaining fera.
                From the Gurahl then a Rite was introduced, that allowed to get Caern Shards (from the remaining caerns, I guess) and seed them somewhere appropriate where you wanted to start a new caern. So, that's that.

                I talked to some of the authors during the Grand Masquerade in New Orleans (I think, it wasn't Berlin a year later). And a lot of what I took from this conversations was, that they wanted to make the game more accessible. In WtA W20 and before, Garou and Fera don't mix well. Now, they do. Males, even when feminist, could not be Black Furies, unless metis, now they can. Kinfolk might be viewed by some garou as mere breeding stock (an element of WtA that is hugely misrepresented in the minds of a lot of people, imo), now each kinfolk could be a garou (even more than in W20).

                Also, smaller groups could now have a Caern Shard and the need for a sept was reduced, allowing different play styles with smaller groups in a LARP game. In tabletop there is one pack PCs and everyone else is an NPC. In LARP everyone is an PC in some cases so you need a group that could fill a sept, if not, you have to make adjustments. The new Setting kind of makes that easier, I guess, because smaller groups can now have Caerns/Caern Shards.

                Problem is, that it is an attempt to fix something, that was not broken per sé.
                I mean, if you wanted to play a Bastet instead of a garou, because you like cat but not wolves, then it is your problem. Instead of catering to your needs and changing the whole (game) world for you, you could suck it up and give garou a try or find other people to play a Bastet chronicle with. Sounds harsh and non-inclusive, but besides procrastination roleplaying and larp are means to get out of your comfort zone, do something different and, yes, be and aggressive douch, in case of most garou - the likes of which would not be tolerated in human society unless elected President. Pardon me. No, seriously, Donald, pardon me for all I might do. One pardon more or less won't make a difference, right? Moving on.

                Besides this, there is another thing that I think BNS didn't think through. The traditional faction is denying themselves Weaver's tools of any kind up to fabric. So, can you imagine a werewolf game on the ground of the local college where half the players wear only fur they sewed together themselves? Probably not, but maybe the faction isn't supposed to be played as easily the other one.

                I'm sure there are other differences, and maybe I have remembered something wrong, but that's what I think...
