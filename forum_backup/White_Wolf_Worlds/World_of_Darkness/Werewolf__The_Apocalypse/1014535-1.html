            A ritual to actually facilitate the Ghouling or Embrace of any changer could be as low as 5th level, depending on the chronicle's needs. Of course, at such "low" level it wouldn't include control over the creature. An before someone start to argue metaphysical reasons it shouldn't work, metaphysics is something you use to back-up your decisions, so the key is "chronicle's needs". By default it should be exceedingly rare, anyway. I wouldn't question the power of Ur-Shulgi to make such a move, though, for Set it would be a piece of cake.

            Lian, the evidence in favor of Sobek in this case in another thing. The way the canon deals with egyptian mythology. In short: the gods in general are explained as different supernatural beings, but with hugely consistent descriptions from vastly different narrators. They have a complex history also backed up by an astounding consistency between those narratives. And by that I mean consistency between the narratives of Children of Osiris, Followers of Set, Mage historians about the Cult of Isis, Silent Striders, several changers in Egypt, Mummies old and new, and so on. Egypt is the single most massive crossover of canon, with the clearest past, sometimes going even directly into reliable narratives, like some OoC pieces about Kemintiri's past as a Follower of Set that turned to the Daughters of Isis and them was Embraced by Set as punishment and torture.

            So indeed, Sobek is far from a certainty, and it is irrelevant to the matter at hand. But as a side note, the setting is already perfectly ready for it being true. Sobek would still remain as far from the weirdest piece of canon egyptian lore.
