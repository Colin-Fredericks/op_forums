    An Ananasi in Hengeyokai...

    04-29-2016, 02:51 AM
    Hi everyone, Forum-newb with first post here!

    Just some info to give you an idea about my current situation before I get to the actual question:
    I play in a Hengeyokai game that we recently started. While this is a new game, I played with this group for quite some time already (Werewolf, something our Storyteller thought of based on cWoD rules, other systems, we're good friends).
    We partially chose Hengeyokai to be able to change things up a bit and play a mix of other races instead of just Werewolfs and granted, with everyone taking the chance the group ended up being quite a bit of a "freakshow" in that regard with not a single Hakken in it.

    We are 3 players and the ST provided 2 NPCs to be able to form a mountain Sentai.We've played with NPCs being part of the characters' close surroundings and of the group before and it wasn't a problem.
    Our ST was really open with what breeds we could choose (all breedbooks available) so we ended up with a Tengu and a Same-Bito with the NPCs being a Nezumi and a Zhong Lung. I myself always tend to like the more unusual stuff so as he said everything goes wanted to play a Kumo. I mean: when will I possibly have the chance to do so again?
    I talked about it with the ST and after some thinking he said as all Kumo have fallen to the Wyrm he'd like the character to be a "standard" Ananasi instead and have me choose one of the Wyld aspects as those are the most likely to assosciate and "fit in" with other breeds. So I built a Kar (Kumoti Myrmidon) and aimed towards the "Leaf" role of the "Sentai to be", you know, sneaky spy, killer from the shadows, that kinda stuff, while keeping an eye on reflecting on the character-sheet how this guy was able to survive where even other spider-changers would kill him if found. In short I gave him some skills in fighting, stealth, first gifts that help staying save or get out of difficult situations and advantages that aid in that as well.

    The 5 characters now got sent on their first assignment by the court they are part of to prove their worth and end up forming a Sentai.


    Now a question popped up regarding my character: Totems. We played that the character basically wasn't indoctrinated by elder Ananasi yet as he had his first change only relatively recently (just like all the other chars), learned a few first things from Kumo that picked up the youngling before some elder spider decided he was "not quite right for a Kumo" and to be killed. He managed to narrowly escape long enough for some other Hengeyokai in the area to notice the hunt and intervene, ending up with a young spider-shifter that the Kumo rejected.

    Now as a standard, all Ananasi have Queen Ananasa as totem and are required to follow her rules but my character hasn't been properly instructed as there are no other Ananasi in the court. Now we got thinking: what happens if he doesn't have Ananasa as Totem? Will he be unable to learn any more Ananasi gifts for example? On the other hand the Kumo also don't follow her and can learn the same stuff anyways (and specific Kumo-gifts of course). Also at least the Ananasi believe that all spider-shifters play their role in Ananasa's schemes, knowingly or not.
    What happens once the Sentai gets a Totem? One of Ananasa's rules (or at least the rules the Ananasi teach their young) is to not follow the other Totems as they are viewed as "tainted" by the Triad that doesn't work as intended anymore.

    What would you say are our options in playing this? Where are the (hidden or open) problems (and its totally ok if this leads to some problems)?
    I already thought about having my character work towards getting a Sentai-Totem that suits him so that the Sentai as a whole may end up with Ananasa as its totem but I'm not sure how to go about this. Also I think for non-Ananasi Queen Ananasa is a pretty costly totem as far as game-rules go (don't know if it would even be possible in this case).

    Of course I will also talk about this some more with my ST and fellow players and I'm sure we'll find a solution but I just wanted to get some more input and ideas.

          I've ran/played in more then a few werewolf games (as garou, I think other shifter breeds make great npc's, emphasis on the N) and have always held the belief that while a pack does cleave to their chosen totem each individual still maintains a connection to their tribal totem on some level.

          A child of rat will always be a child of rat, a glasswalker who follows falcon will still have a tie to cockroach because that's what makes him a glasswalker.

          Now they can't call on the benefits that their tribal totem offers but they can still stay in touch.

          TL;DR: let the PC still keep a connection to the queen, it's the spiders birthright that what's makes them weird among the fera. Just don't allow access to her bonuses.

                  You're in a very weird situation. By proclamation of Ananasa The Ananasi are not supposed to meddle with Kumo and the Kumo are not supposed to meddle with Ananasi. For some mysterious reason they function in a way drastically different from the Ananasi and Ananasi are supposed to keep the hell out of Asia. Add to this the fact that the Kumo are enemies of the Hengeyokai (and there are no iffs or butts about it) and you're faced with the fact that your character will be feared and mistrusted. What if you are an infiltrator? How do they even know the difference? Queen Ananasa is not really public knowledge.

                  I probably wouldn't have allowed such a character in a Hengeyokai game - especially considering the vagaries of the First Change and the Call. However, you're past that point, and you're probably well aware that you're going to be in trouble with the Hengeyokai and the Kumo with the PC's probably only extending you the benefit of the doubt because "You Seem Trustworhty (TM)".

                  So how to make a lemonade with those lemons? First of all, I'd say that the idea that you do not know about Ananasa and were not trained is a lie. That you have been telling this to everyone, but that both you and your mentor know it... you have heard Ananasa's call and you have been chosen. You do not know what for just yet, but she needs you among the Hengeyokai. You will play the patsy and the misfit and you will learn their ways and methodologies and take them as your own. You will make all the emotional bloodbags care for the little lost spider, but you will serve your mistress first and foremost. The Kumo may try and stop you, because they are not yet privy to Ananasa's grand design, but you know your purpose is just and that her song guides you. I'd keep the mentor as your handler and the person that helps you out logistically, but have him running shadowgames and hiding from the Kumo and the Hengeyokai. The agenda doesn't need to be very clear or even relevant to the plot (but it would be cool if you could get the ST to toss you an occasional secret mission to do in front of the noses of the other PC's).

                  As for the totem question, that's easy and works the same as for the Corax and the Nuwisha. You do not get to abandon your totem and its bans, they are an intristic part of who you are and why you are. She will always speak to you, and you will always know joy and horror that she has taken an interest in you. However, Ananasa is clever and she would surely want you to mislead the Food. You will easily join the Sentai and their Totem, you will get the totems perks and bans, but you will still keep Ananasa in your heart and follow her agenda and bans and hear her words. You just won't get any mechanical benefit from Ananasa as a totem.

                  If your ST disagrees, nothing stops your crew from being a Wave Sentai.

                  What doesn't kill you, makes you... stranger.
