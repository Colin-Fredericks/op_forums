                        Originally posted by 11twiggins View Post

                        The name of the game is making it as sanitized and accessible as humanly possible. Anything that might limit your choices in character generation is anathema. Anything that takes critical thinking is anathema.

                        "What I can't be a human red talon?! I can't be a man Black Fury unless I'm a Metis? And the setting has EUGENICS AND DISCRIMINATION?! That must mean the writers support that sort of thing!"

                        An entirely hypothetical customer is being catered to. People who are big into "social justice" as the internet likes to call it (and I'd fit under that label I suppose) already love Werewolf. But they think there's some missing magic ingredient that'll DnD5e it. That'll Skyrim it. That'll Avengers it. That'll give it mass market appeal. So file off any sharp edges and hope that people go wild for the sandbox you've built for new players, and new players only.
                        Well, it's not like these changes will be the "Avengers" Missing Element to reach an hipotetical customer, it's more likely they will be there to manage the reactions of a very real segment of the fandom that has been vocally against 5th edition because of the allegedly alt-right tendencies of its authors.

                        Something which has been extensively discussed here, on RPG.net, on Reddit, Twitter, Facebook and blogs here and there, and which has forced NuWW to make an official statement of anti-fascism by Jason Carl.

                        A huge chunk of this segment will probably not digest W5 in any case, as many bridges have been burned, but I do not think strange that they have decided to sanitize W5 of many controversial elements.

                        There is also a relevant segment of the socially minded fandom that doesn't want to play into "problematic groups": they feel there is already enough doom and discrimination in the real world, that their PC groups should be at least free of these prejudices and contradictions, and their games a chance for revenge at the ills of the world, not an exploration of hypocrosy.

                        They may be interested in a re-done Werewolf in which problematic elements are expunged thanks to re-designing tribes and other things (no more debates on Black Furies TERFs, if the tribe is generally involved in anti-oppression activism instead of the sacred feminine). They may be off putted by the Apocalypse tag though.

                        Anyway, some of these changes have been always discussed, so it's not like they come totally out of blue. Then, unfortunately, social network culture often collates past events into current trends: many people on the Internet just see the Ravnos or the Metis and think oWW was racist, while it was just... the 90s, and do not see or know the progressive history of WoD as a whole.

                        But I suppose that, for corporate reputation, being bland is a better accusation than being a crypto-Nazi or a racist.

                                                            Well... this all took of in a bunch of directions...

                                                            Though Manfr that's not how politics works. A problem with politics that's always been a problem with politics is that it works the exact opposite: once you gain stable power, it's easier to keep power than it is for someone else to take it. The ultimate form of power in politics is the ability to deny any attempts to take your power away, and most people will help you get greater power as long as they think they get more power than everyone else in the process, while the number of people that will simple oppose you on principle will be too small to matter.

                                                            Power accumulates at the top, because it's more politically beneficial for the powerful to unite against the weak to protect their power, than it is for the powerful to ally with the weak to take down other members of the elite, because then any member of the elite - including the one allying with the weak - can go down. While there is a host of historical and current individuals that have trying to work the system to push back against naked political gamesmanship, in the grand scheme of things, the powerful stay that way because by definition that power includes the ability to protect it from others.
