                                            Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

                                            I mean, I don't know, what is the reason that people REALLY HATE the Get as a choice?

                                            Versus any other tribe?

                                            I mean the Get certainly are a tribe people CARED about falling. If the Red Talons fell, would anyone have complained? Would anyone have cared? Would it have been a tragedy?
                                            I don't like the Get of Fenris becoming unplayable. I think that I would be unhappy about that regardless of how it was done. I don't necessarily accept that idea that any Tribe has to fall, because even for Tribes that I don't like as much, I can understand how fans of that Tribe would be upset if they became unplayable villains.

                                            Speaking of some necessity for a Tribe to fall - what does it even mean for a Tribe to "fall"? When I think of a Tribe falling, I think that either the Tribe falls to the Wyrm or it is destroyed. Neither happened to the Get of Fenris. If I heard that in an upcoming Star Wars movie that Rey "falls" I would think that she goes to the Dark Side. That is what falling means in Star Wars. In W:tA falling means going to the Wyrm, not just becoming a meanie.

                                            In addition to not liking the GoF becoming unplayable (which I wouldn't like for other Tribes either or would at least sympathize) I am particularly unhappy with how the "fall" of the Get was handled. If the Wyrm is rising in power and the GoF lead a ferocious battle against the Wyrm and prevent the Apocalypse but are mostly destroyed or corrupted, then I would like that a lot more and it would be thematically appropriate for the Tribe.

                                            That isn't what they did. They made all of the Get of Fenris into Swords of Heimdall to satisfy the whims of an author who wanted to make a political statement. Quite frankly, that pisses me off and I think it would piss me off if something similar was done with another Tribe. Before anybody replies and says that that didn't happen - no, it basically did happen. The reason the GoF "fall" is that they became Nazis and then the serial numbers were filed off of that plan and they were turned into generic extremists who, just coincidentally, still happen to often be Nazis.

                                            I don't even think that what happened to them even constitutes falling. So the GoF become bigger assholes, does that mean that they are now unplayable villains? No, that doesn't make any sense. They became unplayable villains because the plan was for them to be Nazis which the authors see as unplayable villains.

                                                    Originally posted by CTPhipps View Post

                                                    My interpretation is that there's an element that gets glossed over when discussing his article or actually several articles that should be kept in mind.

                                                    1. Sambrano actually doesn't care about the Get: When he speaks about it with Karim, he's talking about it as a fan. It's not related to his primary issues with the developers and just thought it was a bad idea. Karim really wanted to make a bunch of white supremacist villains to kill because he really fucking hates Nazis. That's not...a bad thing. Sambrano, again as a W:TA fan, just felt making the Get was a bad thing. He also confirms that everyone forced him not to do it.

                                                    2. His primary complaint is about the decoupling of the Garou from individual cultural identity: Which is something that was the entire theme of the game's re-imagining.

                                                    3. His biggest complaint is lack of attribution: Which is actually 100% accurate and he should be listed.
                                                    I've been trying to remain committed to staying out of these conversations for reasons which I hope will be clarified below, but as you can imagine, sometimes people draw my attention to specific items, and this one (and some previous statements you have made about what I have said or what my intentions were) and this one is so egregious, I have temporarily changed my stance to come here and ask you to please stop intentionally misrepresenting or unintentionally misreading what I had to say about my horrific experience working for Paradox Entertainment was like. You are using a letter which I wrote to talk about how absolutely terrible it is in this industry to endure racism and anti-Indigenous attitudes from the people who hold the power while they try to use your name to say that they are actually the good guys.

                                                    As Werewolf 5th Edition put it: #NotAllCorporations

                                                    Anyway, literally all three of these points are incorrect, and not just incorrect because I haven't said anything about it, but incorrect and flying directly in the face of what I have said. So let's go over this publicly in the hopes that you adjust your stance and stop using my experience to win fights in this forum.

                                                    1. I do not care about the Fenrir as a fan. If you are really invested in my personal feelings on the Fenrir, that discussion can be had in a more casual environment on another day. I wasn't working on that book as a fan, I was contracted as an author and developer. I am a fan, but my concern about the complete Nazification of the Fenrir that Karim was insisting on was that he was unintentionally going to create harm in the community by glorifying Nazi beliefs and practices. I understand that arguments against this, and it's fine to have differing opinions on it, but that was mine. An example of where I think harm was created historically in Werewolf and then was adjusted in the correct way was how in editions 1st through Revised, Black Spiral Dancers were explicitly written as rapists, and in the BNS edition, this was entirely removed. Even on a villain faction, that created harm for people, and needed to be removed.

                                                    2. Absolutely false. I made this clear multiple times. The version we worked on did not decouple ethnicity from the Tribes, although it was downplayed. That said, I had explicit instructions to write the Ghost Council as an Indigenous tribe--and as you can see the Fenrir were [and are] still Norse.

                                                    3. The absolute least of concerns is my personal attribution on this book. I said very clearly in what I wrote, and on many occasions in public that I do not want to be attributed in this book. I think this book was not only written poorly, but also has created a lot of harm, and done exactly what I warned about while I was working on it. I never want to work for Paradox Entertainment again, and I do not want any association with them or their products. Perhaps you are misconstruing something I said that "I was curious to see"--which is not remotely "my biggest complaint"--which was whether or not I would be credited. The reason that had my curiosity was because I thought it would tell me a lot more about the ethics of the company. And it did. It told all of us about their ethics.

                                                    I won't be replying to this again, but please stop doing exactly what I said was the problem with Paradox and Muammar in the first place: using the work of an Indigenous creator while spitting on their actual perspectives and experiences to give yourself credit.
