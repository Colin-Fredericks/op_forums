    spirits "adopting" packs, but not pack totems

    02-14-2023, 07:06 PM
    I was thinking of packs of werewolves that would not normally have access to the Rite of the Totem, like the Rabble of the Bone Gnawers or Ronin. Yet these groups may still have members who have certain Gifts (like Spirit Speech) and enough knowledge of the Umbra to understand chiminage and spirit relations. So while they don't have a Jaggling avatar Totem for their pack, they might have attracted a spirit (likely a Gaffling which is not truly intelligent) to follow them around in the Penumbra and aid them when needed.

    This could be done in a similar way that people can tame wild animals - provide food (Gnosis), earn trust, provide it shelter and safety, etc. In response, the Gaffling begins to follow the pack around (to get more goodies) and in return provide additional favors. While such a Gaffling could never provide the powers of the Totem background, it could still provide use of its Charms, help the pack when they are in the Penumbra, possibly fight in Umbral combat, etc.

    Not all spirits would want to be in such a relationship, but I could see some spirits do it - ones that don't mind following a pack or hanging out at the pack's main home like a pet, are weak enough to want the pack's protection or help, and are naturally group oriented (not a spirit that wants to be alone).

    Such arrangements would not be limited to such Garou. I can see legitimate packs with existing Totems, and even septs, finding similar spirit allies. But I'm particularly interested in the scenario where the Garou involved are either not part of the Nation (Ronin) or only tangentially involved (Rabble), but who may want spirit allies in compensation for not having access to the Totem Rite. What do you think? What do you think are the troubles or pitfalls of such an arrangement? How likely do you think this is done? What would be the most likely spirits to do this, and which spirits do you think definitely would not participate in this?

    Interested in everyone's opinions.

      I think that sounds interesting and I’d say that it makes sense within the setting. In a way it reminds me of the philosophy that WtF took to Pack creation; as in the pack isn’t just the Garou who are dealing with the typical garou business but is also the various kinfolk allies and followers and the like. I think it would make as much sense to include friendly spirits in that as mortal kin and having both would probably help to reinforce the idea of Garou being dualistic beings, both material an spirit.

      One of the things that comes to mind first about this is that it would have the same benefits and drawbacks of any informal or casual relationship. The rites involved in binding a pack to a totem naturally come with certain defined parameters and conditions, which all parties can more or less rely upon. Without formalized rituals than the relationships are only as good as the word and sentiment of those involved. I can imagine both a rabble pack and their follower spirits taking a somewhat mercenary view of the relationship, that they’ll help while the going is good but they’ll cut ties if things get too dicey. That isn’t to say I don’t think closer bonds aren’t possible, I can imagine many spirits being capable of forming bonds of loyalty, affection, or allegiance; albeit through the cipher of their individual essence and nature.

      I imagine that there would be cases where more orthodox or traditional paths would cowe unfamiliar spirits off their territory or maintain formal customs to negotiate with them whereas others are about as permissive as a real life gang or war band or wolf pack might be of scavengers or camp followers. Many would probably tolerate it so long as they weren’t nuisances, some would probably accept them, and others might drive them off. There’s a significant degree I think that depends on the particulars of the Changing Breeds and the Spirits.

      I imagine one of the most common types would be those spirits who for some reason or another have reason to be in near common contact with the pack. If the pack is relatively stationary this will probably come from the environment. A Forest based pack may become acquainted with the local trees and streams as well as permitting the crows and foxes to hang around the edges of their camp and pick through the scraps in exchange for the occasional favor. A city based pack may well do the same with the abandoned warehouse they squat in and the rats and pigeons who frequent it. Nomadic packs may make pacts with the road or the open sky in general (I once had a player pack that drove around in an RV and liked to take care of the awakened spirit of the RV and treated it like a friend). I can also imagine that more abstract spirits such as those which are aspects of Anger, Hunting, Battle, Fertility, etc. may be drawn to the lives of Garou and could be willing to come to working arrangements which allow them to hang around the pack and sluice up the spiritual energy the pack naturally produces.

      Spirits that would be inclined towards this: I think any animal spirit that has a social tendency and would be reasonably certain the garou wouldn’t kill it or fight with it over territory would be inclined. I imagine that ravens and crows are particularly in this camp as well as other small scavengers. I can also see wolf spirits and other canine spirits following a pack, if they are accepted into the pack structure in some fashion. Local terrain spirits with a decent bit of common sense might make go along-get along deals, such as a rock cluster the garou camp near or a particular overpass the garou park under. More conceptual spirits associated with the facets of Garou (Anger, Desire, Hunger, etc.) life and activity (Battle, Hunt, Stalking, etc.) might also tag along. I could also see miscellaneous local Gaian spirits approach the Garou if they seem at all inclined to assisting them in whatever their priorities are. I’d also say that every pack and it’s particularly members would have to be taken into consideration to determine what spirits might approach them.

      Of spirits that I’d consider highly unlikely to enter agreements such as this. Any highly developed fallen-triatic spirit I would consider unlikely. I can imagine a low-level bane getting along with a pack of rabble who are willing to live in shades of gray but probably not anything actively engaged in the Wyrm’s Efforts (unless this pack is on a short road towards the Black Spiral themselves). I’d also say that spirits which conceptually seem antagonistic to Wolf in general would probably keep a distance. That being most feline spirits and most domesticated spirits (I can see Feral Dog potentially getting along with the Wolves, but Dog proper would probably be more likely to fight any pack that isn’t mostly Homids with the Scent of Man gift). I also imagine spirits with codes of honor and developed mores would be disinclined to informal liaisons. Falcon probably won’t bum around your camping ground, he expects certain formalities to be observed. I’d say this probably applies to many Respect spirits, although I can also see some of them being willing to stick around without proper rites if treated appropriately (some Deer spirits will probably be willing to hang around the local Fianna hill even without being formally greeted, so long as they get the respect and consideration that kinship warrants).

      I’d say that the majority of spirits would be neutral to the idea and would be potentially willing to be involved depending on the particulars. It would also depend on matters such as the individual personalities of the pack and their spirits, the patronage networks of pack and tribal totems, and the local spiritual ecosystem outside of the Garou. (For example, If a pack of Bear shows up in a forest under the protection of a powerful Bear spirit, then it may be that every Gaian spirit in good standing in the area imagines that they can approach the pack with their issues and give them assistance the same way they would if the Bear spirit itself or one of its brood had happened upon them).

          This is something I've played with before, and I think it's a pretty good ploy. Many spirits don't really have enough independance to consider something like that, but I can totally see how certain spirits could benefit from such a symbiotic relationship. I believe that many Garou have relationships with more individualistic spirits that might make them patrons, lovers, servants or friends. After all there's even a merit for Werewolves that have Spirits proper in their family tree.

          The favourite way I've handled this was with a lost cub that got picked up by a canny tree spirit that presented itself as the guide, mentor and taught the guy a highly skewed view of the spirit world and the role of the Werewolf as essentially the servant and enforcer of the Spirit. It was a very interesting set of antagonists without a straightforward anwser, one of my favourite approaches to Werewolf stories.

          What doesn't kill you, makes you... stranger.
