                                        Originally posted by Psicose View Post
                                        What was the restriction of the Fianna?
                                        Keep in mind that WtA treated Tribal weaknesses as optional (mostly because Pack Totems have them too). But since I didn't notice someone addressing this part:

                                        The old Fianna Weakness was +1 difficulty on Willpower rolls to represent the Tribe's penchant for passion and poor self-control.

                                        Stag's Ban as a pack Totem was that followers of Stag had to answer any request from aid from fae creatures or spirits, as well as always having to perform the Prayer for the Prey rite to the spirit of their kills after any hunt they participated in.

                                        So the concept of making their Tribal restriction a more generalized hospitality thing isn't a huge departure from previous material.

                                            I feel like it's worth noting that the Silver Fangs are also Ethnocentric, due to their bloodlines thing. Yes, I'm aware that there are African Silver Fang bloodlines, but picture the following scenario.

                                            A Homid cub from an impoverished background goes through their first change, and is rescued from the blood and red mist by a silver fang. This cub is a natural leader who has always been drawn towards ideas of justice, truth, making change through politics and so on, but also to chivalry and knighthood. They could be any race, but let's imagine for a moment they're black or latino. And as they go through all the explanations of what will happen next they tell this silver fang they wanna join their tribe, since they're so cool and just and devoted to this idea of rulership. It's what they were always meant to do.

                                            At which point they're told sorry, real sorry kid, but it's a select club. A... pure-breed having club. You need a certain kinda blood purity. It's not a race thing! Definitely not a race thing. But I, on odds a white dude given the Tribe's aristocratic roots, am now telling you that you can't join. But there are other valid tribes for you to join! Just not mine. Sorry.

                                            Soon after the silver fang, through dreams and omens, comes to realize that this kid is in fact a long lost scion of an African or Spanish aristocratic line with blood ties to the Silver Fangs, and they are in fact suitably thick-blooded for Falcon to accept them. Not because of any change in this person's behaviour or aptitude, but because of a realization regarding their heritage and their heritage alone.

                                            "Oh, thanks but fuck no. No I think I'd rather join one of the other tribes now. I'm actually kinda shocked you even reached out."

                                            The point of this isn't to say "hereditary blood purity divine right monarchy is bad" (I'm reasonably assuming you're all on the same page as me there), it's more to say "this tribe's obsession with blood purity will inevitably come off as racist, even if the Tribe isn't all-white".
