                      I'll speak a bit from personal experience on this.

                      My faith focuses a lot on ritual/spiritual purity. The list of things that can turn you ritually impure is kind of insanely long, and is completely impossible to actually avoid all of no matter how hard you try (since the list includes specific involuntary bodily functions).

                      Culturally this doesn't create some sort of inane monastic drive to live in absolute seclusion, it creates common rituals of purification. Just like physical hygiene, you're going to get dirty no matter how hard you try not to, so you develop ways to clean yourself off.

                      One of the reason to keep up your dues with your local religious institution in this context is to fund the maintenance and have access to the materials necessary for doing this when you need to. Even if you're not particularly observant, or religious, as long as you're in good standing you can schedule a spiritual cleansing one a regular basis just to be safe.

                      Translating that to the cultures of long standing kin families with at least some members in the know is pretty simple. While not in the books (something that seems a bit of an oversight, but whatever), it should be fairly simple to have a minor Rite that exists to expel the minor taint that builds up slowly like dirt under your nails without having to wait for enough taint to bother with the full Rite of Cleansing. You then encourage this minor Rite (even if it doesn't actually spiritually cleanse kin without a Garou doing it) as a family practice among kin families as a regular monthly thing they do. Then you try to show up at least one every month or two with a Garou, and they do the minor Rite, and the little bits of spiritual cruft that builds up is removed.

                                                      You can reject it all you want... but that doesn't change the way the game is written.

                                                      As well, I don't find the idea that the Wyrm is only "in the worst things," interesting or useful. That is the path to campy bullshit, because it removes all ambiguity and nuance. Wyrm = purely evil. This both rejects the concept of the Triat as ideally being in everything in balance; that Taint is an imbalance between them more than anything else.

                                                      Does the Pentex material often take things too far? Sure. But it's been ~23 years or so, with multiple editions, with multiple developers and writers, with multiple tones. The problem with Pentex is not how taint works metaphysically in WtA, but the presentation and tone of the way Pentex is presented.

                                                      Werewolves aren't threatened by dolls. Wyrm taint spreading dolls represent a problem that is a challenge to the Garou. It's a subversive enemy corrupting humans and kinfolk quietly. It's not a foe you can face in battle. It's an enemy that flies in the face of traditional Garou notions of their role as warriors and their cultural biases towards problems with the Wyrm. It's an evolution of the Wyrm servants' tactics that gives the Glasswalkers, Bone Gnawers, and Children of Gaia purpose in the war to fight on an unconventional level.

                                                      Forcing the Wyrm into a narrative role that only works when people (not necessarily without duress or while fully informed) "consent" to the Wyrm's urges removes a lot of the tools of the Wyrm in the war. The Wyrm can't be toxic metaphysically if its poisonous urges are defeated by just saying no.

                                                      -------

                                                      In general a few points beyond that:

                                                      First, this applies to all of the Triat, not just the Wyrm. I understand why we're focusing on Wyrm-taint, but the metaphysics of taint in the books-as-written apply to all three types. That consistency shapes how taint needs to work on a narrative level. It might open up the space for gonzo Wyrm silliness, but it is also part of how Wyld and Weaver taint can remain relevant.

                                                      Second, please stop conflating mental illness into this. Nobody is saying that mental illness makes you tainted. The closest was a comment that trauma can be an avenue through which taint is spread.
