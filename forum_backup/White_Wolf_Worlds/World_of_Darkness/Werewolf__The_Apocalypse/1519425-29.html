                I'm not sure if this means anything, but I think I should mention it. I belong to a Werewolf group on Facebook. As such, the W5 group keeps popping up in my Suggestions feed, and I occasionally lurk on it to gauge the opinion on the new game. Just as the game was coming out, the opinion was overall very positive. Members hated anyone who had anything negative to say, even those who had carefully detailed reasons. After awhile, the positions became a little more nuanced and balanced, but still this hatred lingered towards those who hated the game. Now, after too many haters started posting to the group, it has splintered due to the original moderator not catching more of these types of posts. Well, I tried to check in on some of the members to get a deeper feeling last to why there was so much hatred, as something felt off to me.

                There were people who worked for game companies in the group. I didn't check to see if they were connected to Paradox or their subsidiaries, but it might not mean anything. Someone can like a game for a competitor without any issue. However, at least one member of the group only had a few pictures posted, and not really anything else. It's as if they only were on Facebook to be a part of this group. There was nothing in their profile about any other interests or even public information. Now, maybe they found a way to make such info private. Maybe they just skipped some steps as they felt it was unimportant. I've seen it happen. Maybe I'm just horrible at internet research in this regard. I am getting the same vibes I get whenever I receive a request from a woman from another country whose profile is just pictures of her in barely any clothing. Or less.

                Now, I don't want to make any accusations or draw a wrong conclusion, but you can see where I'm heading with this. With the original group splitting after more detailed opinions are spreading, this could mean something big is going on behind the scenes involving the game. I will keep investigating, but I am not sure what I will find.

                THWarted

                                            Further thinking of this, I feel really weirded out by the changes made to the BSD and that one loresheet.

                                            For one, I know W5's main goal was to distance itself from the legacy, but the redemption of Bat could have been used as a way to show what the PCs can do without the Nation. A patron was rescued from the brink and now offers its patronage to garou. Yet, it still has to fight hard to keep its mind. A great way to showcase what garou CAN do when the Nation is not beating them down with its traditions.

                                            The loresheet baffles me, even if you aren't aware of legacy, just one look at any BSD art will tell you what they are like. While obviously it is bad to base morality on someone's appearance, BSDs even in W5 art still signal very strongly that they are just Wrong. Look at Retaliation's BSD models compared to its CoF models. While neither is exactly pretty, the CoF lupus doesn't have SPIKES coming from its body. Nevermind that the lore isn't exactly saying they are too changed by their behaviour.

                                            So, who looks at a BSD and decides "Oh, THESE guys can be redeemed"? And not through a great quest or a costly spirit deal. No, in a loresheet you can max out at chargen. Even ignoring the fact that CoF can't be redeemed, this choice is so baffling given everything else W5 says and shows about BSDs.

                                                            Originally posted by Asmodai View Post
                                                            Today the new Loresheet is out. It's essentially The Winchesters Sisters hunting rogue werewolves and collecting werewolf and other monster lore. There's a lot of Supernatural cues in the Emissaries of Athena writeup, but something else popped up to me.
                                                            Man, I figured when I saw the title and the image that this would be the first time the idea of "camps" would be re-introduced to W5 and this would be about a Black Furies Camp with a feminist focus. Instead... yeah, it not only sounds like it was inspired by Supernatural but the overall flair reminds me of "mortal hunters vs supernatural". And it's noticeable how it never covers what these characters' struggle with Rage looks like. This portrayal of them as these adventurous do-gooders is quite at odds with the negative portrayal of Garou in the W5 corebook. Really doesn't feel like whoever wrote this was of the opinion that they are the "wrong tool" to fix the problems of the world...

                                                            What bothers me the most is the cavalier attitude towards W5 as a system:
                                                            • They are a Pack with Athena as their Pack-Patron-Spirit... must be nice to be able to pick Pack-Patron-Spirits that aren't Tribal Patron-Spirits...
                                                            • "when you discover evidence of any Black Spiral Dancer presence or a Storyteller character brings them up in conversation, set your rage to whatever level you desire from 0 to 5.​"... Really, "when a ST-character brings them up in conversation"?! And once per session setting your Rage at whatever level you desire is really good. Also, on a narrative level just a really weird mechanic...
                                                            • "Gifts that use Wisdom in their dice pools cost two fewer experience points to learn to a minimum of one experience point.​" Gifts in W5 never cost 1 XP - even with two point reduction. The XP-cost for Gifts is "New Gift amount x2". A player-character starts with three Gifts. Even if you started out with a character with no Gifts this sub-clause will only ever be relevant for the first Gift you theoretically buy a Gift with XP. And this sort of XP-reduction is also way too good as a mechanic, I feel like...
