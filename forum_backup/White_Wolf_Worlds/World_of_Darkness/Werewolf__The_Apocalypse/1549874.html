    Someone 'hacks' Rite of the Fetish Egg to work for other Changing Breeds. What now?

    04-06-2025, 01:13 PM
    Rite of the Fetish Egg

    Level Two, Mystic

    The most important of all Corax rites, the Rite of the Fetish Egg births a new wereraven into the world. The binding requires a feather or hair from the “parent” with which to bind the spirit egg to the Corax-to-be. If anything happens to the egg or the binding before the First Change, the results are spiritually and psychologically devastating.

    System: This rite can only be performed in the Umbra, and requires a witness of the breed opposite that of the Corax “parent.” The Corax spends three hours and three permanent Gnosis creating the spirit egg, then another hour binding it to the soul for which it is intended. If the rite is interrupted at any point, the Gnosis is lost and the rite fails.
    It's worth noting that you can't use this Rite on a human who is the child of two Corax, but for most breeds that simply isn't possible as such a union would produce Metis.

    Now, imagine if some clever Theurging or some Bastet trickery, or a great heist pulled against Raven, or the intervention of some Incarna or somesuch, or maybe even some interference from a powerful Mage or Changeling, or an Ahadi inter-breed ritualist project, led to this Rite being 'cracked' to work for everyone.

    I imagine that the Garou, who place great importance upon heritage (some Tribes more than others of course!) would see this Gift as sacriligeous. Even if a typical Garou were to find out that a Corax is born and not made, that's Raven business, wolves are a breed apart.

    Here's how I see it playing out by Tribe and Breed, assuming there is nothing inherently wyrm-tainted or legitimately suspicious about the events.

    Black Furies: Opinions may be split; the ability to grant an exceptional girl or woman all of the Wyld's gifts, and bring her into the Tribe, would probably be highly valuable to some Furies. Others would naturally see it as an insult to Gaia and Luna.

    Bone Gnawers: Would likely grab ahold of this with both hands. The ability for wise old Elders to turn 1-2 precocious humans (or wolves) into soldiers for the Tribe before dying would be seen as a huge boon.

    Children of Gaia: The main concern of the Children of Gaia would be to stop a war breaking out over the Rite's existence, either within the Nation or between Fera. It's easy to forget that the Children of Gaia are arguably the most war-ready of all the Tribes, with their take-all-comers recruitment, training of Kinfolk, and so on. Assuming that use of the Rite isn't more trouble than its worth, and doesn't tear the Nation apart, the Children would probably be making good use of it to bolster numbers in their own ranks in preparation for the coming wars of the Apocalypse.

    Fianna: I can't claim to be an expert on the Fianna, and I imagine that they would be split on this as you might expect a typical gathering of Garou to be. Those who value heritage and bloodlines will find it abhorrent, those who value deeds and potential might find it intriguing.

    Get of Fenris: Assuming the humans and wolves turned with the Rite are exceptional warriors, I could see the Get being very keen. Those who opposed it would likely do so with immediate and decisive violence, however, killing those who know the Rite and anyone near them. And maybe anyone who heard about it for good measure.

    Glass Walkers: Early adopters as always. I could see the Glass Walkers immediately converting some of their most militant Kinfolk into Ragabash, granting them the Gifts of the Garou without risking the Curse interfering with their work. The Glass Walkers would suffer, however, from being Homid-heavy and thus lacking the Gnosis-heavy Lupus who could create more Garou.

    Red Talons: Desperate times, desperate measures. The Red Talons would, hearing that the Glass Walkers and Bone Gnawers are creating Homids with this Rite, want in on the action to empower as many wolves as possible (hopefully making sure to leave enough wolves as 'regular' wolves to secure future breeding). They'd also be very quick to demand that the Tribes convert an equal number of wolves as they do humans; it's easy to forget that Red Talons are political animals and do engage in diplomacy!

    Shadow Lords: The play from the Shadow Lords would be focused on getting as much gain in power as possible. Their ideal position would be to corner the Silver Fangs into swearing to never, ever, EVER using the Rite, and then not backing them up in that commitment. They'd then plan to overwhelm the Silver Fangs with numbers and seek to supplant them as the leaders of the Garou Nation. That said, I do think it could play out very differently; if the Rite were received poorly by the Nation as a whole then they might seek to undermine and supplant any who tried supporting its use.

    Silent Striders: The Striders have a lot of respect for the Corax, but beyond that I don't have a solid idea of what they'd do with this. If having enough Garou was the answer to defeat their enemies and reclaim their homeland... maybe?

    Silver Fangs: Almost exclusively 100% against this Rite. Any suggestion of the other Tribes using this Rite would immediately place the Silver Fangs, who have a Pure Breed Entry Requirement, in a very difficult position. Both for political and religious reasons the Tribe could never, ever accept this Rite being used. So the question is basically "what happens when every Tribe except for the Tribe who are """in charge""" have reached a shared conclusion on a topic?"

    Elder Brother: Given that the EB are extremely mystically inclined, I imagine their first interest would be how this was done, more than anything else. They might also be interested to see if there's any way Middle Brother Kinfolk could be turned into Garou with this Rite, as part of a project to restore the Tribe. I also wager that the Tribe would be very keen to recoup lost numbers and create more Garou among native peoples around the world.

    Younger Brother: Regenerating lost numbers and turning angry, disaffected and disempowered native Americans (and other indigenous peoples) into Garou would probably be quite high on their agenda. Fear that the other Tribes will use the Rite and then outnumber them would probably predominate. It would also be quite fitting, in their view I imagine, if they could ensure that every single Tribe of Native Americans across the US, Canada and so on had at least one Garou in its membership.

    Ajaba: Probably in favour of the Rite since it will help them regain numbers lost in the recent genocide.

    Ananasi: Given that the Ananasi aren't of 'Gaian' origin it's possible some would fear the Rite wouldn't work, but from what we can see the Ananasi are capable of joining 'packs' with Ovid in the Ahadi, and so presumably this Rite would work normally for Ananasi. I think the Ananasi would be keen to make use of this Rite if Queen Ananasa wanted them to use it, and if she forbade it, they would drop the idea entirely. QA would want to use the Rite if she thought it brought her closer to victory.

    Bastet: The mystically inclined would be curious to see if it worked, and the Bastet's pride would probably see some swearing they'll never stoop to artificially creating cubs, and others rushing to outdo the other Breeds.

    Gurahl: While the Gurahl would be very heavily incentivized to bolster their numbers, I imagine they would also be very fearful of the possible negative repercussions of massively swelling Shifter populations. There is a reason, after all, that they refused to share their life-restoring Rites with the Garou. The Gurahl might be very quick to encourage the other Shifters not to fall prey to the folly of massive recruitment drives, but this would probably be a losing battle.

    Kitsune: I have absolutely no idea what the Kitsune would make of this. I know they see themselves as being very pure of blood, so perhaps they'd balk at this much like the Silver Fangs.

    Mokole: Given that this probably has never happened before in all of the Mnesis records available to the various Streams, I'm sure the Mokole would be shocked more than anything. I also imagine they would want to remain impartial and watch carefully for what happens; Gaia made them to serve as her memory, not as her army or her fist, and so it makes sense that they would want to record and listen, and commit all events to memory in case warnings need to be carried into the far future against ever trying this again.

    Nagah: The Nagah rely on the sacred secret, and so mass recruitment doesn't suit them. Turning the most able young assassins in the world into weresnakes might be tempting however, in tiny numbers.

    Nuwisha: I imagine that the Nuwisha's views on this Rite would relate to whether they think it's wise. If they thought it wasn't then it would fall to them to prove it to the other Breeds in a teaching role.

    Ratkin: Given that the Rakin already have the Birthing Plague, I imagine they would only care about this with regards to the other Breeds multiplying, and with regards to whether the Rite was superior to the Birthing Plague. The Birthing Madness being circumvented might interest some Ratkin, but others might see that as sacriligeous.

    Rokea: Gaia's mission to the Rokea was to survive; being able to bring sharks into the fold could help with this. I think the Rokea would be slow to find out about this or react to it compared to the other Shifters.

    I'd be interested to hear some ideas on what the various Tribes and Shifters would do in this scenario!

                  The divide between wolves and African wild dogs is nothing compared to that between all the Canidae and the Tasmanian Wolf of the Bunyip. It's a freaking marsupial. Also, if the original breed of the Mokole was originally a dinosaur, then the Corax are probably more directly descended from the ancient Mokole than the modern Mokole.

                  I think this exercise went a bit too far. Three Permanent Gnosis is a steep price, and the prep, time and risks impose yet more limitations. It is a game changer, but not a path to massive reproduction.

                  All in all, I think every tribe would accept this rite soon or later, but the bigger change would come to tribes, breeds and Changing Breeds that suffer the most with low numbers. Among the Garou this rite would be the means for every tribe to increase their number of Lupus, and I actually think the Red Talons wouldn't face that a big of a problem finding willing witnesses. The dwindling number of Lupus Garou is everyone's problem, and this rite is an easy solution.

                  Elder Brother wouldn't try to revive Middle Brother. Garou born true among Middle Brother's surviving kin is already a thing since always. What keeps the tribe from coming back is not a lack of kinfolk or of those kinfolk having Garou (those werewolves are initiated as members of either EB or YB tribes).

                  Now, for the Changing Breeds, Ananasi, Ratkin and Rokea have little use for this rite. They don't have any problem with numbers, and actually compete for the spotlight as most numerous CBs, even more than the Garou. Nuwisha too, but with a twist: instead of large numbers, they have a predefined number in the material world. It is against their law to increase this number, when a new Nuwisha Changes, another Nuwisha leaves for the Umbra to never come back. It's weird as fuck, but it is what it is, so they wouldn't really have a big use for this rite.

                  Guhral, Kitsune and some Bastet would be far more likely to benefit from this rite. Even then, few Bastet would care enough to give up so much. The Guhral are keenly aware of the dangers of overpopulation, but they currently suffer from underpopulation. They're likely to make judicious use of the rite, even given the steep price.

                  When it comes to reproduction, being a Kitsune sucks. It sucks so much that most of them will probably think that losing 3 Gnosis on an adoptive child beats having a new Kitsune through normal means (and killing one or both of the parents). The next step would be to find a Fetish condom or a Vasectomy rite.
