                  Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post
                  Yes but its listed as a possiblety in the tribebook

                  And lions patronage WOULD get you the gifts back.

                  I have to agree with Ganonso. Lion did not teach all of the White Howler gifts. He had a brood of spirits when they existed and his brood aided him and his patrons. Many Lion's brood serve the Wyrm now or have faded into obscurity, some of which have not been seen since the fall. I think in Lion's diminished capacity he in fact would not be able to offer them the gift selection he used to be able to offer them.

                  That said there are two options for a White Howler return that the books have talked about. There is a White Howler mentioned in the Tribe book who can not die, he is protected by a spirit. Currently he is frozen in an iceberg Captain America-style somewhere in the northern hemisphere. Due to the temperature shift he may thaw out or he may simply be found by the players due to a quest from Lion, or both. In any case his return would give the new howlers a legitimate White Howler Elder to follow.

                  The second option is talked about in the new Umbra book. There is a White Howler wraith haunting the Dark umbra, hunting down BSD that are unlucky enough to cross his path. He is the spirit of the only White Howler to escape Malfeas the night the tribe fell. He is probably the only legitimate White Howler ancestor spirit left. If he could over come his grief in some way and ascend to true Ancestor Spirit then he would be able to help teach the new howlers some of the old gifts.

                  I have been working on a story based around their return and ideally the story for reforming the Howler's would happen in 3 acts:

                  Act 1: Mantle of the Lion - Gain Lion's patronage. Not an easy task because he has moved on and it part of Griffon's Brood. To leave would be a great dishonor to the spirit that saved him. This could potentially gain the new tribe hostility from the Red Talons moving forward. If he is to take on there patronage he has the new pack take on an insurmountable task: Locate Eubh "The Ever-Living" and return him to Lion.

                  Act 2: Return of the King - Find Eubh "The Ever-Living". They will be in the frozen north where only Wendigo, Crazy Red Talons, and Ice Stalkers roam. He is always protected by the spirit of the White Wolf (Mother Wolf), so she may guide them to his resting place and they need to find a way to recover his frozen body for White Wolf to bring back. If they succeed then they should return with Eubh to Lion.

                  Act 3: Spirits of the Past - Find the lost spirit of the last White Howler in the dark umbra. Lion is impressed by their tenacity but does not feel they have what it takes to be true howlers.. yet. His next task for them is to find the spirit of the last howler to survive and return him to Eubh and Lion so that he may be brought back into the fold. Obviously a dangerous in it own right to go into the dark umbra. When they find him he is surrounded by the souls of all the Garou he has 'freed' from the Wyrm. It will not be an easy sell, his self loathing for the fall of his tribe and his own initial survival makes returning to the fold a traumatizing endeavor.

                  Given that the players succeed in all of the tasks above they have all of the cultural and spiritual resources to rebuild the tribe. To continue the story I would move on to rebuilding Lion's brood and rebuilding their numbers. I would use totems from the tribe book that have not fallen as the base for rebuilding the brood then as the players grow in rank, move on to purifying totems that have fallen. Rebuilding numbers would come in the form finding lost kinfolk and bringing them into the fold or finding Ronin who are looking for a cause and trying to get them to join. This could bring them into direct conflict with the Skin Dancers which might be an interesting side story for the players if they have never dealt with them before.

                  The last part is not so much a single story line as much as many individual story lines that would make up an entire game over time. Again this is just something I have been working on, you can do it any way you think best, and obviously this is not a single quest and magically they return. This is a entire story that effectively brings them to modern day in a new incarnation using ancient assets that still exist in cannon. There is a lot of play and storytelling required to do it my way.
