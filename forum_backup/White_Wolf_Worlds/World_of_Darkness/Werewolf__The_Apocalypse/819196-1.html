    Kitsune gets tasked by the group with entering an enemy barrack through the Umbra, light it on fire, and get promptly out again. ST literally warns him that he needs to be very careful - it wasn't a very safe idea to begin with, the place is full of armed enemies, sleeping and awake, and the Kitsune does not have regeneration. Also he is a Metis and would cause a huge veil breach if he got overwhelmed. The fox gets in and takes long enough to lose the rest of the group completely, who have their own problems outside. It takes long enough to justify a 1 on 1 with the player. While he has the spotlight, he notices the two guards in the doorframe and ponders that he could take them out. ST asks him if he really really wants to try. Yes, he does. Gets incapacitated immediately, no fire, instead three months torture at the hands of evil scientists.

    - - -

    Also: Player in a crossover friendly game needs a new character, after he asked the ST to chop off his old character's head for plot/drama reasons. He then creates a Hunter. Pretty much introduces himself to the shifters as: "Lol, that's funny, I'm a werewolf hunter. Let's be friends." Turns his back, gets immediately murdered and thrown into a dumpster, then lit on fire.
    Same player decides to play it safe and makes a Shadow Lord the next game. Manages to find the one area the ST set up as a trap for the whole pack, alone. Opens the door to find 5-ish people cocking shotguns and taking aim at him. He decides to get a good look at the situation before reacting. He gets the good look, also 5-ish shotgun blasts to the chest. Dead immediately.
    Same player then decides to play it super safe and make a Gurahl. That one lasted for 2 years, until he entered a "boss fight" without changing out of bear form, which also got him murdered. We were fighting vampires and could have had a flamethrower. Who advised against bringing the damn thing, because of the risk of it exploding? That player.

                    Most of my players for whatever reason at least in Werewolf do not do anything this bad. That said a few wtf were they thinking moments have occurred at my table over the 20 years that I've run WtA games:
                    Suffer the Children this is the chronicle name. A bit of background this chronicle started as a one off story that morphed into a three-year chronicle. The events here happened early in the chronicle Chapter 2. The characters have successfully infiltrated a boarding school that is home to a slaving ring and churns out a large number of Formori. The pack led by the intrepid Red Talon Philodox begins the full out assault on the staff. They quickly overcome the teachers except for the headmistress who retreats to the basement. The pack charges after her the true master a specially designed bane is materializing. This bane causes terror one player the second on the narrow winding staircase for whatever reason decides on his action to run away. Knocking every player behind him to the concrete floor below. The Silver Fang theurge spend his action to heal the Ahroun. The theurge is the target of the band's attack. He is dead. The character that ran away is forced by the remaining pack members to run a gauntlet. The player to this day insists that he was only acting in character.

                    Suffer the Children chapter 6 or 7 same player same character decides to ignore the Red Talon Philodox that wanted the Bone Gnawer Metis Ahroun and Uktena Ragabash to stay hidden while the rest of the pack investigated the neighboring town. The player convinces the other that staying hidden means going to the local diner is what the rest of the pack meant. The entire town turns out for the welcome to Garou party. This time the character does not survive.

                        In game update
                        Originally posted by Somuchlove View Post
                        But my current mind boggling moment. The pack are looking into prophecies of the Apocalypse. They each get a prophecy. The metis gets one of an important perfect metis cub being born to two metis parents. The pack decide to investigate. They find the parents bodies bloodied and tattered and track the wyrm taint and blood and after some time tracking BSDs Banes and all sorts of bad end up face to face with Eeyarlagh Twice-Born*holding the newborn child of destiny. Just Eeyarlagh and them. A pack of hardened garou.

                        They decide dealing with the cub is above their pay grade (Fosterns, some with enough renown for Adren), and not knowing what to do with the baby they decide to let Eeyarlagh go with the child. Not that they, gaian garou who've spent over a year battling bsds, who've seen and been tortured by the evils of that tribe, who know through portents and visions that the child is important. Not that anyone garou of higher rank or importance at any of the other 4 caerns they've recently been involved with, nor they themselves were good enough or a better choice than this fanatic bsd. Nope. They even wished her good luck.

                        I really did not see that coming.
                        Upon realising their mistake icly, the group had the choice to either go back home and face the music, make a run for it or try and do something to salvage it. They decided to try and salvage it first before going back. It didn't happen. Basically half the pack decided to bravely hide in the shadows of BSD Hive, while sending the Metis in to look around, another wondered off looking for something else. They ended up running away, rather than risk dying.

                        So back to the Sept they went after another pack (NPCs) came to collect them.

                        We held a moot judgement of the situation, each character getting a chance to speak on their own behalf of the events and the troubling prophecies or as a pack. It didn't go great, the Child of Gaia blamed it on Gaia lol, but ultimately said it was failure as a theurge and his own perceptions that led him to give his alpha bad advice. The Galliard Alpha blamed it on everyone giving him poor advice but then asked the philodox of the Sept only punish him as he was the leader, The Ahroun didn't like the idea of someone else trying to take the blame for his shame..although he did get his own Deedname wrong when introducing himself before the council and the Philodox wouldn't give any judgement on the situation or define what went wrong and why.

                        So, Rites of Punishment, losing of renown and sept positions, and a loss of Rank. Satired and to be ignored for three months and not allowed to leave the Sept. The Septs Ragabash gave them all new Deednames of Shame and I quickly relayed what happens on Sept for the next three months and unveiled some of the repercussions of their actions visible across the worlds as time passed.

                        Overall, it actually went really well. The Troupe really played this out fantastically and are already looking at new ways to define their characters and their next steps as a pack with the lessons learned. Despite the "what in the name of Gaia is going on?"-ness of it all, this turned out to be an amazingly emotionally charged, humbling and re-defining moment for players and characters alike. So impressed how much of it was taken in stride and utilised not for the failure but for the story they continue to tell. Completely worth helping to the Apocalypse take another step forwards
