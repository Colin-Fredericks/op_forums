    GW athro ahroun seeking to reach Scar

    01-23-2022, 02:03 PM
    Umbra the Velvet Shadow states:

    The Scar is not hard to locate; plenty of moon paths lead to it. In fact, the relative lack of Banes and other horrors on these paths make them almost... inviting.
    I have an GW Athro Ahroun who is looking to lead nine lower ranked Garou to Scar, and I'm trying to figure out the best way they'd go about this. Use the Level 3 GW gift Web Walker? Go to a nexus of wyrm and weaver energy in downtown Detroit and find a suitably hellish bureaucratic space from which a moon path might originate and lead them to Scar?

    For that matter, what does walking the Pattern Web look like? I've never been able to picture how it overlays the regular Penumbra. How do you access it if you have the Web Walker gift? Do you quickly leave the penumbra behind, and just move across a vast web of some kind that stretches under the fabric of reality? How would you describe that to the players?

      There are several different methods you can use to travel to one of the Near Realms. It all depends on what how easy or difficult you want to make it, and which flavor text you prefer.

      As your quote included, one can can simply be using a Moon Path that is known to go there. Maybe there isn't a local Moon Path, and you have to travel somewhere first (maybe start at a place that is already corresponding to Scar in some way). So your idea of going to a bad place in Detroit is a great example (although I would not use downtown Detroit, but find something more industrial and polluting, or abandoned factories). The problem here is knowing which Moon Paths travel to Scar. The easiest way is to know of a stable Moon Path that has already been explored and is know to go to Scar. That might require a Garou going Anruth and visiting lots of septs, talking to local Garou and learning if there are any. Or you can show up and start exploring Moon Paths randomly until you run across one which is much more of a risk. But once you locate the correct Moon Path, it'd probably be the safest option provided you do so during a full moon.

      Another possibility is to find spirit tracks, or Airts, that might lead one to Scar. In this case, you would want to find a spirit that likely originates in Scar, and follow it back to that Near Realm. Of course, a Garou will either need Gifts, skills, or spirit allies with the right Charm to do so. This would be a more difficult way because finding an airt is not guaranteed, but the advantage would be that you're probably entering Scar at a place not normally known for it, so if your intention is to be stealthy, this might be preferred. Appropriate spirits to follow might be one of the Elemental Wyrms, or any Bane directly tied to pollution, or a spirit of the Maeljin Incarna that could be associated with tyrannical workplace conditions, abuses of employees and so on. Certain Weaver spirits might also be used. Out of friendlier spirits, Rat spirits and the like could also be used.

      A third possibility is to enter the right kind of Domain, a Blight, and find the spirit gate in that Domain which leads you back to Scar. This would probably be the most dangerous and conflict prone route because you're essentially assaulting an enemy stronghold to do so. It would be the most action filled though.

      Entering the Pattern Web would be the other way, and you'd need access to the Web Walker gift, or possibly enter a chiminage deal with a Weaver spirit. While the Pattern Web is supposed to be able to connect everywhere in the Umbra, it certainly has the most nodes in places where the Weaver is strongest, and the Scar would have a lot.

      As you said, the Scar is supposed to be one of the Near Realms that is easy to enter and hard to leave - it's a roach trap.

          We're not given a lot of hard details about the Pattern Web, so a lot of your description will depend upon you. But we do know some things.

          First, the Pattern Web, ultimately, is what the Pattern Spiders are spinning. It's what is holding reality together, but it is most obvious with the Webs now being spun in urban places to show the spiritual reality of the artificial and technological creations of mankind.

          Second, if you could look down onto the entire umbra around Earth, what you would see are little "knots" that represent cities, and then all sorts of line connecting these knots to each other. It's similar to those pictures of the globe that show trade routes, fiber optic cables, or other linkages between cities, sometimes even city lights at night time with small thin links between cities representing lights on highways and roads linking metropolitan centers.

          Further out, you would see something similar with the nodes/knots being the Gaian Realm and the different Near Realms, as well as any connections leading to the Membrane and its Anchorheads, and the High and Dark Umbra.

          Third, like a lot of the Umbra, this is hard to represent three dimensionally. It's based on a fantastic and non-Euclidean geometry based in multiple dimensions. You don't necessarily see it at first. Often you need to make Perception + Enigmas checks to detect it.

          Here is what I would do to describe the Pattern Web to players in my chronicle.

          In order to use the Web Walker gift, the PCs would first need to be at a stronghold of the Weaver. That would mean finding a Web domain, or someplace where Weaver spirits congregate in strength, particularly the more unusual Weaver spirits like Geomids. In other words, a place that already has lots of webs appearing in the umbra (I would prefer it if the webs were thick enough that it could swallow an entire Garou). Once you are at that location, you can use the Web Walker gift in a similar fashion to Garou opening a Moonbridge at a caern. You need to first be at an appropriate place. (Since the Pattern Web predates humanity and human technology and urbanism, you should be able to find similar spaces even in the wilderness, but it'd likely look differently and the game has never been good at telling us about the "natural" Pattern Web).

          The web itself, on the outside, looks like the normal pattern webs of the game - silvery, gossamer webs that sometimes radiate light, or gradually coalesce into thicker, and darker strands. If you describe them differently in your chronicles, use the same description.

          It's only after you use the Web Walker gift that the Web opens up (and then you step inside the portal), and you can enter the Pattern Web and travel on it like the Weaver spirits do.

          At that point, it would become more psychedelic. I would describe it in a way that combines various imagery - lots of fractal images, the congeries of iridiscent spheres of Yog-Sothoth, the Boom Tubes of Jack Kirby's Fourth World comic books, the slipstream of colors that astronaut Bownman encounters when the approaches the Monolith at the end of the movie 2001, the transition between realities in the Matrix movies, and other visual representations of cyberspace from Tron to the modern day. While I'd likely use a lot of modern, technological imagery, I'd still want to keep the connection to a knitted tapestry, or spun spider web, or other natural or mystical feature in order to make it clear this is not sci-fi, but something deep in the reality of the world, just as natural as the fibonacci sequence. But certainly in travelling from a Web domain in Earth's Penumbra centered on urban blight going to the Scar, there'd be a lot of mechanical and technological imagery.

          I would want to make it clear that the Garou have entered a natural, but utterly alien world to them. I'd likely use a lot of blacklight imagery where UV rays cause psychedelic posters to pop out in strange colors and lights in a sea of blackness. The Garou themselves would be like those blacklight posters and they'd see themselves lit up in different colors as they shot along dark tunnels similar to subways along electron beams, slowing down briefly as they entered a node/knot on the web where they would need to turn into a different direction to get to their destination, and then suddenly being accelerated again. During these transition times, they might have brief glimpses in the umbra around them seeing a different part of Earth, another Near Realm, or some other part of the Umbra. The different stages of the journey would be a good place to use the various imageries I mentioned before. Travel would be disorienting and confusing, but nor harmful.

          There's certainly other ways an ST could imagine travel on the Pattern Web to be like, but the above is how I would run it for now. I would also be interested in hearing how other STs run it.
