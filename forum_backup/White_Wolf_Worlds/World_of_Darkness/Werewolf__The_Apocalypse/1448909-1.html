Samuel Haight (yes, that guy) is selling information about the garou to some scientists (DNA, I assume) in exchange for a clone of himself (yes, much like Jango Fett got a little Boba for volunteering his DNA, Sammy got a little Mini-Me for selling out the garou). So, the WoD's most badass hunter (at this point he doesn't have most of his powers yet, I gather) investigates and realises, that his Ex is kinfolk, with a linage that might lead back to the white howlers and her son's father is a pure bred BSD, who until the child hit puberty lead a normal human life. No BSD connection or wyrm influence so far. Sammy of course goes after the BSD kidnappers and comes in conflict with garou from the Western Eye sept aswell.

    Once any garou who successfully passes the first level dancing the spiral becomes enlightened (or crazy) to the ways of the wyrm. But this isn't a level of corruption that is unredeemable. If one is willing to take a story there. It is a really really rare thing to happen, though.

                                            Originally posted by Penelope View Post
                                            I thought the Yama Kings were all inherently evil, to the point that you were discouraged from playing their servants the same way you would be from playing an Infernalist in a Western campaign (the one line you’re not supposed to cross)
                                            The majority of the Yama Kings are corrupt and have turned their backs on their duty in the name of power, but there's still a few who haven't done so. Notably though those Yama Kings generally don't make Akuma or corrupt others into their services like the evil ones do, so you wouldn't be able to play an Akuma to one of them anyway.

                                            As to the primary question, Kuei-jin should generally trigger Sense Wyrm. Some Dharmas might trigger it less, especially as the Wan Kuei reaches higher levels (for example higher dharma Resplendent Cranes or Rising Phoenixes would likely not trigger Sense Wyrm in the same way high Humanity vampires don't). On the other hand, some dharmas should always trigger Sense Wyrm, like the Devil Tigers or Song of Shadows. In fact, truly high ranking Devil Tigers would probably come off as incredibly powerful and corrupt Wyrm creatures to the point where a Rank 10 Devil Tiger would probably be comparable in corruption as a Maeljin Incarna.

                                            That said, the Emerald Courts have a tradition of engaging in diplomacy with the Wan Kuei, and there is a recognition they they have a role to play, even Devil Tigers, so shapeshifters would be slightly less trigger happy than western werewolves when it comes to immediately murdering wyrm-smelling creatures. But if a Wan Keui shows up unnanounced at a Caern, or gets found hurting kinfolk or if the shapeshifter is just having a slightly bad day, then goodbye Wan Kuei. And especially for younger Wan Kuei, there's not going to be any real fallout or retribution from that. Ultimately the Wan Kuei would probably be recommended to walk on eggshells around shapeshifters, and open warfare and conflict between the two groups is extremely common.
