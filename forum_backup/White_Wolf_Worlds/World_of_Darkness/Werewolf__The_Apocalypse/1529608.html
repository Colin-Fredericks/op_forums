    J.F. Sambrano's Glass Walkers

    01-28-2024, 06:55 PM
    From James' Patreon, another look at what may have been if Hunter's continued with their work.

    Glass Walkers
    J.F. Sambrano

    Who are the Glass Walkers
    The most progressive Tribe, the Glass Walkers are branded Urrah by other Garou, who believe them to be chained to the Weaver's webs. The Glass Walkers, however, feel that the Weaver is suffering, twisted from her original purpose much as the Wyrm is, and deserves to be guided in a direction that will encourage her to reclaim her true role--the Protector. Glass Walkers are innovative, economical, and willing to buck the traditionalist attitude of hardliners and and those incapable of adapting. More than any other Tribe, the Glass Walkers are open to young Garou who look back on the mistakes of the past with trepidation and horror, and invite them to their future-first perspective.

    The Glass Walkers favor efficiency, made up of technicians, scientists, and logicians, and prefer to approach the unsolved equation of the Malady from that angle. They choose to live in the Scabs, which most Garou avoid like they would the realms of the Wyrm itself, combating the corruption from within the belly of the spider. The Glass Walkers concern themselves with green sciences and technologies, which undeniably promote the goals of the Garou. If nothing else, the Glass Walkers are adaptable, and almost never find themselves out of place in changing times--save with their own cousins.

    Gaia's Howl
    Every problem has a solution, and Gaia's Howl is not different. If a solution does not immediately present itself, then a workaround must be invented, or the Garou must innovate an entirely new approach--the sundering of the Umbra from the material world poses many problems, but they are within the grasp of the Glass Walkers to resolve.

    A new philosophy, dedicated to the concept of the Intelligent Machine, has spread like wildfire throughout the Tribe. The proponents of the Machine believe that through innovative and creative technology combined with the power of spirits of the Weaver's Court, they can bring the physical world and the Umbra together once again--something they see as necessary in order to accomplish their primary function, preserving and protecting Gaia herself.

    Most Glass Walkers hold to a controversial belief, which they feel must be addressed if the Garou are going have any success relieving Gaia's suffering, and they refuse to walk on eggshells about it anymore. The Wyld is also out of balance, and is just as dangerous as the Weaver and the Wyrm. They purport that the Wyld has always acted out of chaos and madness, and it is this turmoil that forced the other parts of the Triat to over-correct. To them, this is clearly evidenced by humanity being increasingly under threat by natural disasters such as earthquakes, floods, and dangerous hurricanes, even if such things can be tied to the human destruction of the natural world. Like the rest of the Apocalyptic signs, they answer is logical and critical thinking applied through technological and spiritual transformation.

    The Malady
    The Glass Walkers are a Tribe of survivors and have always used adoption as a primary method of bolstering their numbers. They argue that no other Tribe is more cognizant of useful technologies such as facial recognition and GPS tracking which, spiritually awakened, can be directly applied to seeking out Lost Cubs. What they don't discuss is that their techniques hardly make them gifted at tracking down their wolf-born kin, and that the Malady has all but cemented this Tribe as completely losing its bestial ties.

    Glass Walkers tend to take in any kin who are open-minded, unafraid of change, and in particular favor the analytical and inventive. More than any of these traits, a Glass Walker must show a penchant for adaptability in order to earn the favor of the Cockroach.

    Corruption and apocalyptic disaster are the result of both the dangers of nature and the willingness of humanity to use their gifts of intelligence and tool-making to further their own ideas of need and greed, rather than inventing a new world capable of protecting all. The Glass Walkers are set to uncover a solution, whether it require code and creation, or claws and a rain of lead.

    Archetypes
    Inventors, scientists, lawyers, hostile takeover specialists, Machine cultists.

    Kinfolk
    The Glass Walkers would say that they have always treated their Kinfolk well and with respect. Centuries ago, they called themselves the Warders of Men and to this day they take that role seriously and the humans which they treat with the most are their own families. Glass Walkers also heavily believe in adoptive families, inviting people to their table who act as siblings or cousins.

    On the other hand, they have never treated their wolf Kinfolk well, what few even exist. At best, the wolf relatives of the Glass Walkers might be kept as exotic pets in penthouse apartments, or behind the safety of walls and fences in wolf sanctuaries. At worst the Glass Walkers experiment on their wolf Kinfolk with genetic manipulation and other methods in order to make them hardier, healthier, and more fertile.

    Cockroach
    The Patron Spirit of the Glass Walkers is Cockroach, who often earns an immediate response of revulsion due to his association with uncleanliness and decay. However, the reality is that cockroach is a survivor, adapting to any environment he is set to. Whether making his home deep in the wild of the jungles, or within the disharmony of urban decay, cockroach always endures. Cockroach teaches his children that they cannot balk at making the choices that will help them survive, and is a spirit of Wisdom.

    Harmony
    Despite the argument that some Garou make that the Glass Walkers are lost to the Weaver, and dangerously unbalanced in that way, the Glass Walkers themselves are one of a few tribes who truly sees that the cycle exists that way for a reason, and try to run themselves through that cycle in their own lives. They find Harmony through using the destructive and reductive energies of the Wyrm to aid the Wyld through concepts such as composting, or recycling. They assist the Great Spider with the tools of the Wyld by engaging in urban permaculture or genetically modified organisms.

    Ghreena
    The Ghreena of the Glass Walkers is the sterilization of the Wyld.

    The Glass Walkers flip the usual narrative of the Triat represented by Garou on its head, suggesting that the Wyld is at fault for driving the Weaver mad, which resulted in its machinations on the Wyrm. They will turn the tools of the Weaver on the Wyld with intent, building dams that provide "clean energy" while destroying ecosystems, genetically modifying crops to make them more durable against weather and pests, or creating low carbon footprint technologies which require mining operations that poison animals, people, and waterways. Many Glass Walkers favor terraforming the "undesirable" aspects of Gaia, such as deserts, by diverting waterways and creating solar panel fields which make these spaces more hospitable for humanity. They might claim that these actions are made out of a need for progress, but they seek out the sterilization of unstable qualities in the environment.

    What doesn't kill you, makes you... stranger.
