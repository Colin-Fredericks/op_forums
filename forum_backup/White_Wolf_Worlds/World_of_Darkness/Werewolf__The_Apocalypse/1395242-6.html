                            I agree that things like boarding schools, summer camps, even scouting troops, youth groups, and certain athletic camps could all be great ways to induct young kinfolk into Garou values and watch over for potential First Change Garou. And they even work for urban Garou who dwell among many humans and might not have been informed when they were very young. They make good friends because they seem "normal" yet allow children to be taken away for extended time periods without other contact or supervision besides the people who oversee those activities. Depending on how much time is available, they could introduce things either slowly or fast, allow them to process any potential shock of revelation under supervision.

                            I expect conveniences like email and cell phones would have to be removed, blocked, or clandestinely monitored, but that can easily be done.

                            For adults, I think organizations like fraternal organizations, local history/genealogical/heritage societies would also work. Non-kinfolk can be secretly blackballed from joining while unknowing kinfolk can be isolated and properly instructed.

                            I think Bone Gnawers are at a disadvantage because they lack the resources to create and maintain these kind of organizations, though they could piggy back on those done by other tribes who compensate with "scholarships" or charity. But I think for the most part, we need to think of alternative methods of instruction more appropriate to the feel or theme of the tribe. Perhaps the use of urban legends, and informal institutions found on the street. There may also be things like homeless shelters, delinquent youth groups, juvenile correction halls (or outreach programs), orphanages or foster care networks that are effectively coopted or controlled. And since Bonegnawers may have a lot of runaway kids living on the streets, there could be certain child gangs or Fagin like mentors who can do the same.

                            I think developing institutions and organizations like these that help inform and protect (slash control) kinfolk make excellent additions to chronicle settings and PC backgrounds.

                            = = = = = =
                            I also use sexual or romantic urgings/emotions of Garou to produce the First Change. I also think they make great Forced/Banned Transformation flaws too!.

                                                There's a big difference between "What actually happens" and "what we can make you think happened".

                                                Yes, you can have a rural community that's well armed. That's normal in some places. But it's pretty easy to convince most people that those guys were wackos (or Wacos). You tell the ATF you think they're stockpiling illegal weapons, you tell the human rights charities in your pocket that they're abusing people. You can file a missing person's report where someone went near the place and was never seen again. When you actually look at the place, you can likely find obscure religious items denoting that it's a cult. There's just loads of ways you can turn a bunch of weirdos living among nature into a vicious organization. I think a lot of people will want to believe you're dealing with a sensational cult. The Garou either stick with their kinfolk or move to protect the Caern; They're gonna sacrifice something, just because the guns drew the attention of the police and media.

                                                (you could also just chuck bigger amounts of soldiers at the sept but that risks the veil)

                                                But also if Garou give their kinfolk access to a lot of quality silver weapons, what's to stop a kinfolk from going postal? Especially if we throw a vampire's dominate into the mix. The less you give your kinfolk, the better. If you give them a few simple bolt action hunting rifles, how do they stop serious attackers with numbers and semi-autos?

                                                From the garou point of view, the less their kinfolk do (other than breeding, providing income, and servitude) the better. Letting kinfolk take up some militaristic function is a threat physically and a threat to the honour of garou who pride themselves on being Gaia's warriors. What's your existence to you if someone you thought of as weaker can do a job just as good as you? Do people need your services if they can do things themselves?

                                                            Why would garou want guns/military training? They have their forebears for that (I mean they learn from eachother, ancestor spirits and all that are secondary) A society as militaristic as the garou doesn't need outside help for training. (this is part fact, part garou pride)


                                                            I'd argue that silver loaded firearms have really closed the gap between Kinfolk and Garou. Silver isn't that expensive nowadays, so if you have the technical skill to craft the bullets, you're ready to go. If he's not suffering from delerium, a kinfolk shooter with a matchlock loaded with silver has a fair chance of defeating an unarmed Garou (I'd give him 30% odds of winning in most Terrain) A Kinfolk with a semi automatic rifle of sufficient size has a 70% chance of winning, and a kinfolk with a general purpose machinegun loaded with silver has a 70% chance of annihilating an entire pack. Sure, rage might come in handy if a werewolf is shooting back at five guys, but it isn't really a huge advantage if he's shooting back at one.
                                                            Garou largely get by because of enemy ignorance/lack of appropriate tools/delirium and the fact that most of their fights happen in the umbra where brawls occur and shootouts are rare. The best thing for Garou as warriors in a war with guns is that they've got a whole load of utility options that normal people don't have, letting them pick battles better and get the jump on others. But if kinfolk got the jump on Garou instead...

                                                            So many Silver bullets close to home would make Garou-me nervous. I'd like to think I'd be a nice garou, but I don't know, I remembered instances of being a prick in my teens and I don't know how my thinking would change being in a survival cult (I was quite the cynic but I woulda loved to be the special kid that did well and got special privelages so...) Maybe Normal gun's would be fine but a weapon specifically designed to kill my kind? So many? Around me? Then we add a siege mentality, hear stories of abuse and traitors...

                                                            Kinfolk should be for mundane stuff, like fetching something from the store, getting stuff posted, cooking, giving my wife the constant backrubs she needs to placate her ferocious rage, picking cotton...
