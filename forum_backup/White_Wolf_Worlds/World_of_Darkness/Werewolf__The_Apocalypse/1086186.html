    Kitsune and Hengeyokai Auspices and Hengeyokai stuff

    05-01-2017, 10:26 AM
    So it was really illuminating when W20 Changing Breeds cleared up that Hengeyokai Auspices are added to a shifters Auspice rather then replacing one.

    But the Original Hengeyokai book kind of sneaks in at the end of their Section on Kitsune Paths (their version of Auspices) that a Jade bowl appears to represent the Hengeyokai. The implication being you can choose this bowl to join the Beast Courts rather then choose a Path.

    So does that mean the Kitsune who join the Beast Courts don't have a Path and only an Auspice Role like Mirror or Pillar? If so I would improve the Auspice to have gift costs of primary auspice rather then that secondary auspice x4 cost.

    Or do you think a Kitsune still chooses a Bowl but can choose the Jade Bowl as a 2nd one.


    And another thing some Hengeyokai can just keep their native Renown system but still work for the Courts. So does Court Auspices apply if you actually use the Hengeyokai Renown systems? Doing so after all means for a lot of folks doing that whole Renown Reset Rebirth rite.

    And how do you think it would be treated having different Shifters pair up romantically. After all you don't get metis that way. I was thinking something along the Line of a Metis Hakken seeking love from say a Kitsune?

    The Zhong Lung Being Mokole used to interbreed with other Shifters and their kin in ancient times.

    Thoughts?

                  It used to be that this fell under the "Being honorably mated" area of Renown. Being in a relationship that is unlikely to produce a new member of your whatever you are is socially frowned upon. This may be an even bigger deal in the Beast Courts, where concepts of "face" and social shame/ostracism are a more significant part of the culture. However, it may also be perfectly acceptable to have some sort of "public" relationship with a spouse and children that is more businesslike in nature while also maintaining a private relationship with someone else. Personally, I'd expect that as long as any shifter who is able to reproduce is making a public effort to do so, others are unlikely to care much about whatever else they are doing romantically or sexually. Metis may be a special case, however, as they are so tied up in their culture's concepts of sexual taboo/sin that others may take offense at their engaging in any sort of romantic or sexual activity.
