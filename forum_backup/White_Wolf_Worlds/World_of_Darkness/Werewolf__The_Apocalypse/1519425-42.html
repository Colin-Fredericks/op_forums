        If one wanted to avoid comparisons to Neo-Nazis with the Get of Fenris, this could have been accomplished ably by establishing a schism within the Tribe. Have the Swords of Heimdal not only survive the purge of the early 00s, but have a resurgence in the decades since that parallels the Real Life resurgence of Fascism. Slowly radicalizing Fenrir youths, until they felt they had enough clout to attempt a power play within the Tribe. When this failed - non-Fascist Fenrir and other Garou correctly seeing them as the monsters they were and acting against them - the Swords of Heimdal broke off from the Garou nation. Trying to prosecute their crusade against "Impurity" on their own, or perhaps recruiting similarly radicalized members of other Tribes. (Like, for example, the most hateful and extreme parts of the Red Talons, who felt even their own Tribe weren't doing enough to exterminate humanity.)

        This could serve duel purpose. First, it gives a new Bad Guy Werewolves faction for Garou PCs to struggle against. Second, it creates a clean division between morally upstanding "Viking Werewolves" and the "Viking Cosplayers" that are the Fascists. It's also a good way to soften the edges of one or more Tribes, as the most extreme and anti-social Garou within the Gaian camp go off to form a coalition of evil, bereft of moderating influences. So while, say, the Red Talons probably won't like humans still, they may not be genocidal in outlook. You may even say that the Red Talons who left took the strongest anti-Homid sentiments with them, allowing the Tribe that remained to be more accepting (or at least tolerant) of non-Lupus within their ranks.

        (I'm also of the idea that, in order to bulk out their numbers, the Fascist Garou coalition may attempt to swell their ranks with Stolen Moons somehow. Creating half-werewolves. These, naturally, are held in contempt by the coalition's Garou members, as Fascists love both having contempt for weakness and making coalitions on convenience, even with groups they hate. They intend to exterminate the Stolen Moons the moment they feel they no longer need them. This also more or less applies to every constituent group within the coalition's Garou population, because Fascists love hating other Fascists. There's no such thing as being "too Pure".)


        Obviously, all this may be too nuanced an arrangement for project leads who cannot be asked to do their due diligence in portraying Indigenous groups respectfully, and would rather just erase them from the game.

        Also, having Fenrir who are gung-ho for action and taking the fight to the Wyrm would mean establishing that as a viable option. Which seems a bit too "Direct Action"-y or "not accepting the Apocalypse as a foregone conclusion"-y for Paradox's liking. I have a feeling the decision to villainize the Get of Fenris has as much to do with wanting to villainize playstyles that aren't Cairn-protecting Street Level games.

        Magestuck: The Ascension

                                            Renegade have announced what the first supplement of W5 will be. It's called "Scent Of Decay":

                                            Rotting from the inside.

                                            Milton has become a company town, and that company is Pentex. Between the rent-a-cops, the wasp fomori and the malicious spirits life there gets harder every day. Can a single pack of Garou scrape off the rot and turn Milton into territory they actually want to hold?

                                            Scent of Decay presents guidance for character creation and three pre-written stories set in the same small town and scenes which can be included in any Werewolf chronicle:
                                            • The Break Room: A corporate fulfillment center seeks productivity at a terrible human cost
                                            • Shadows on the Prison Walls: A trickster spirit tries to use the Garou for their own ends
                                            • A Pound of Flesh: A rival pack and their patron spirit challenge Milton’s Garou


                                            Features
                                            • Three pre-written stories that can be run separately or linked together
                                            • Guidance for character creation and session zero, useful for running these stories or any others
                                            • Plug-and-play scenes showing Garou relationships with humans and their world, suitable to drop into any story
                                            • The three scenarios are all using the small town Milton as a backdrop. So still street-level-only like always...
                                            • Pound For Flesh once again having Garou with custom Pack-Patron-Spirits while I guess PCs are still stuck with the corebook-rules of only being able to choose Tribal Patron Spirits for their Pack-Patron-Spirit...
                                            • Don't really feel like advice for character-creation and session zero is what W5 needs
