    Wyrm Spirits / Balance Worm and Corrupted Gaian

    10-28-2016, 11:11 PM
    I am interesting in hearing people's opinions on certain kind of spirits that serve the Wyrm, specifically spirits that are not moustache twirling villainous Banes. These fall into two groups - spirits that served the original Balance Wyrm, and Gaian spirits that have been corrupted.

    Balance Wyrm spirits are spirits are those that serve the original purpose of the Wyrm. I would break them down into several main categories.
    • Disease
    • Vermin
    • Fungi
    • Detrivores/Decomposers (things that break down detritus/dead into their component nutrients - termites, woodlice, slugs, flies, worms, etc.)
    Some of these are still considered to be Gaian spirits, but some are probably considered to be Banes now. Mushrooms and worms are probably considered to be (mainly) Gaian spirits. But Disease spirits are probably all Banes.

    Corrupted Gaian spirits are an interesting category. Is there anything canonical besides Bat and Whippoorwill? I would consider "corrupted" spirits to come from any animals or plants that are often considered to be evil in folklore and legend. Often they would serve as the familiars for witches, or be associated with monsters. Below is a list of what I consider to be these kind of animals.
    • Rat
    • Vulture
    • Toad
    • Spider
    • Mosquito
    • Fly
    • Moth
    • Black Cats
    There are many others as well. Obviously, some of these can't be considered to be corrupted as they are canonical Garou totems such as Rat (although I've posted one thread about my reasons to consider Rat as a totem in the midst of "falling" to the Wyrm). I think Vulture is a pack totem in one of the books. However, who would be good candidates to be similar corrupted Gaian spirits that now serve the Wyrm? I think easily Mosquito and Fly could be such Wyrm totems. And perhaps other spirits may be in the midst of falling, where the Incarna may be still be Gaian (or in the midst of transition), but the gafflings and jagglins might be Gaian or Wyrm aligned. Toad or Moth might be good candidates.

    I think about these things because I think they are beneficial to the game. Identifying other Wyrm spirits gives me additional antagonists (especially for games run before the industrial era). It also helps me run the Umbra and spirits better as I can introduce more flavor text of uncertainty in the game. If PCs are in the Umbra and oversee a toad or spider spirit, then how do they know if one of them serves the Wyrm and will report on them? Or if they know Mosquito is a corrupted totem, how do they react when the encounter or sense a mosquito spirit?

    So does anyone have examples of what they'd done in game as ST, or what they've experienced as a player in these kind of natural Wyrm spirits?

      Good post Black Fox, interesting stuff. Gets the ol' creative juices flowing. Generally I think a lot of the 'decisions' made by the Incarna/Totem spirits happened long ago. The Gaians that turned to the Wyrm look and feel and act wrong, since their patron is insane- they don't perform their proper role anymore, so chances are that fallen Vulture spirit is going to be twisted looking and probably eating the eyes of perfectly healthy but weaker spirits. Unless it is an infiltrator or a spy, I think a lot of the Wyrm's servants tend to appear malformed, sick, or deranged, reflecting the pain and confusion of the Enemy. I am all for more and varied antagonists, but if you make them too sneaky it might run the risk of your pack scampering around, paranoidly spamming 'sense Wyrm' on everything, which can lead to some pretty binary decision making.

      The Wyrm's imprisonment and subsequent madness caused several 'big' spirits to leave its brood and join with Gaia... can they really be trusted? Bat was one, and we all know how that panned out. Great Uktena is another of the Wyrm's bastards who sought legitimacy through Gaia. You could certainly have other totems go through what Bat did... perhaps Rat turning to the Wyrm isn't a betrayal, he is just coming home...

      I will note that there are Gaian spirits under the Tribal totems that feel pretty weird being on the side of the 'good guys.' Pain spirits and garbage spirits are good examples, and Wendigo's brood actually includes mosquito spirits. I kind of like that this is the case, since it means not all the unpleasant aspects of reality are actually vile servants of the Destroyer in the umbra. That having been said, if there is a propensity for blood-sucking insect spirits to go bad, that might make dealing with them a little more interesting.

          Thanks for the comments. Here are some responses to points you've raised.

          Originally posted by The Laughing Stranger View Post
          Unless it is an infiltrator or a spy, I think a lot of the Wyrm's servants tend to appear malformed, sick, or deranged, reflecting the pain and confusion of the Enemy. I am all for more and varied antagonists, but if you make them too sneaky it might run the risk of your pack scampering around, paranoidly spamming 'sense Wyrm' on everything, which can lead to some pretty binary decision making.
          I think these are valid points. However, if the key to knowing the enemy is seeing whether they are malformed, sick, or deranged, then I really don't see why the occasional use of Sense Wyrm is that bad. You are just detecting it by other means.

          Sense Wyrm is one of those things that can lead to problems at times. In my games, I always tell my players that it is not "detect evil", and that things can have Wyrm taint that are perfectly fine and normal. I say it should be used to generate leads, not make decisions.

          If someone constantly spammed Sense Wyrm everywhere, I'd probably tell that player that his character might gain a Derangement like paranoid. But if they have a reason to suspect something, and want to use Sense Wyrm as one way to confirm their suspicion, I generally don't have a problem.

          Originally posted by The Laughing Stranger View Post
          Great Uktena is another of the Wyrm's bastards who sought legitimacy through Gaia.
          I would consider Uktena to be a leftover spirit from its days as the Balance Wyrm (isn't that canonical?). Lost secrets and forbidden knowledge are "Wyrmy" in their nature, but I've always felt Uktena to be in no danger of falling. Same way with almost any kind of reptiles (although I perhaps would consider only the really poisonous or venomous ones to have been in the camp of the Balance Wyrm.

          Whether certain venomous animals are corrupted Spirits would really depend on folklore to me especially since so many snakes are associated with positive things as well as some negative.

          Originally posted by The Laughing Stranger View Post
          I will note that there are Gaian spirits under the Tribal totems that feel pretty weird being on the side of the 'good guys.' Pain spirits and garbage spirits are good examples, and Wendigo's brood actually includes mosquito spirits.
          Pain is just a response by the body to let you know, 'What you are currently doing is bad - it risks destroying something important to your health." That there are people who inflict pain for no reason is not fault of the sensation or its spirit. So I can see it being Gaian. Garbage spirits are more problematic because modern trash isn't turned into mulch or what have you as fast it should. It could be easily corrupted by the Wyrm although I also see why the Bone Gnawers might find communicating with such spirits useful. There are probably very, very few garbage spirits so probably whoever awakens them probably determines their nature.

          Mosquito as a Gaian spirit in the northern tundra is not something I'd dispute. However, given its role in spreading so many terrible diseases, I have a preference for the Mosquito Incarna being corrupted by the Wyrm.

          That is certainly more in line with the actual negative effects of mosquitos than compared to bats or whipporwills who are completely unoffensive creatures who merely have bad reputations because of superstition and folklore.

          Originally posted by The Laughing Stranger View Post
          I kind of like that this is the case, since it means not all the unpleasant aspects of reality are actually vile servants of the Destroyer in the umbra. That having been said, if there is a propensity for blood-sucking insect spirits to go bad, that might make dealing with them a little more interesting.
          I do think it is important not to present Gaia or the Wyld as something that humans would not fear or see how terrible it can be. Mother Nature is very dangerous, and depictions of Gaia and Garou should incorporate that.

          I also like the idea that perhaps Frog is Gaian and Toad is corrupted Gaian. Since the difference between Frog and Toad is almost one of semantics and aesthetics, I think it's a nice dichotomy. For similar reasons, presenting Butterfly is Gaian and Moth as corrupted Gaian is also something I like.

                  Good call on Toad from Amazon.

                  It also depends on how detailed you want to divide up the spirits. There seems to be one Bat totem, but there isn't a Bird totem. Instead, every kind of bird has its own totem.                   It would be a good reminder to players that how Garou divide animals does not necessarily map as to how modern science does it. To a certain extent, you shouldn't worry about it too much. Just make it clear to players however the ST divides them up, since this is likely something taught to most Garou especially Theurges.

                              There are two other broad categories of evil spirits that are appropriate to this.

                              The first is some kind of "dark fertility" spirit that is often associated with witchcraft, often emboddied in some sort of horned man, black goat, or black ram. Black sheep were sacrificed to Hades, and this ties it to the underworld where monsters are often born. A "Black Ram" then would be a spirit associated with blood sacrifice and the darker aspects of renewing the soil's fertility through death. A corrupted version of the spirit then would take it one step further and require human sacrifice to a devouring earth mother who gives birth only so she can cruelly kill and consume her own children.

                              The second is some kind of night spirit as nighttime is when the monsters come out and prey on people. If you want to keep some aspect of night Gaian, you can make a distinction between night (Nyx) and darkness (Erebus). This "night" isn't so much night, as the primordial darkness that existed before the sun and Earth were made. In my chronicle, I distinguish between Nyx (the Gaian night) and Mother Night, which is my name for the darker Bane Incarna responsible for giving birth to all the horrible nighttime monsters of mythology, and which formed one of the major enemies of the Garou in the very eldest days. Mother Night is probably more of a Bane than a corrupted Gaian spirit, but "night" as a concept in itself is perfectly normal. I tie "Mother Night" very much with the Vampire lore of Lilith, and the result is something I've been pleased with. It's also a good substitute whenever I needed some spirit like Hecate for cults and such to do black magic.
