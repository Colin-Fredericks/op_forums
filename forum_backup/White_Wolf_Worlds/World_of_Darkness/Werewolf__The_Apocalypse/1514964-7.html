    Originally posted by Ragged Robin View Post

    Walkers and council look okay but wardens and Furies are a little odd. Nature related craft is ultimatly technology-sorry but nature doesn't make wicker baskets or hunting tools and the Furies rule doesn't clarify about the nature of the authority-for example anything from institutional corruption to betrayal is fair game here.

    Thinking on it youd get a bonus to betraying everyone to the wyrm. It also hits the same issue as v5's brujah compulsion as it mechanically leans towards them being insubordinate dipshits.
    Let me just list them both:
    Glass Walkers:
    • +1 die to Technology and Science when you build or repair things.
    • Only regenerate 1 WP next session if you destroy complex machinery.
    Hart Wardens:
    • +1 die to Animal Ken and Survival plus to nature-related Craft.
    • Only regenerate 1 WP next session if someone under your hospitality comes to harm.
    Ghost Council:
    • +1 die to Investigation, Occult and Subterfgue when you reveal a secret.
    • Only regenerate 1 WP next session if when you had the opportunity to satisfy your curiosity, you didn't take it.
    Black Furies:
    • +1 die when you oppose or circumvent someone in a position of power.​
    • Only regenerate 1 WP next session if you witness an act of injustice that you could've prevented but didn't.
    With GW, I'd probably add analyzing to make up for not having three applicable skills. And considering how Frenzy works I'm still wondering if acts under Frenzy can trigger Bans (then avoiding the destruction of complex machinery becomes a bit more complicated). The GC-favor/ban pairing is a bit weird because the favor is about revealing secrets and then the ban is really pushing the idea that you better be all about revealing secrets. Also, this "opportunity to satisfy your curiosity" could become really bad if it gets abused by the ST. With HW I think nature-related Craft refers to creating stuff that conceals things you want to hide in a forest or making makeshift-weapons out of stuff you find in nature... but "nature-related" is a bit vague in what it exactly refers to. Compared to the others it's pretty easy to avoid the HW-Ban, though... just don't accept guests into your home. And BF... yeah, if the Favor already paints them as people who are rebellious, the Ban really pushes them to get involved in other people's business as long as it seems like there's an injustice about to happen.

                                    Antipathy towards conservative Elders who are (at least partly) to blame for the problems you're grappling with is a feature of W20's themes, and to me it makes sense. You don't need to be 500 years old to be a jaded political animal. An "Elder" in their 40s, 50s or 60s could be as regressive as you like, and may well have most of their historic Glory gained through intersept warfare and winning Challenges.

                                    Garou live in a Might Makes Right society, for the most part. I steal your favourite blanket. You're annoyed. Well if you want it back you'll have to challenge me. Oh you challenged me? Well time to find out why I felt so comfortable being such a dick to you. And I get some Glory for my troubles too.

                                    No matter how you square it, most Elders of any vaguely mainstream Sept are at the very least neutral towards stuff like "all but 2-3 Tribes are pro killing the elderly", "someone being strong means they won the argument", "tormenting and mistreating one of the Breeds is acceptable".

                                    And you know what? Good.

                                    A setting where you have a Nation's worth of Elders, mighty and powerful beings who reinforce all this vile shit but are also incredibly important in the fight against the Wyrm, where you are directly subordinate to people who represent a lot of what you need to fight, where your access to Rites and Renown and Rank is tied to how well liked you are by people who are, honestly, monsters, is a setting which is amazing for a punk theme, or any tale of young rebellion you want to tell.

                                    And I've barely scratched the surface there. It's really hard to get into how many regressive, counterproductive and awful views a typical WTA NPC has, because so many of them exist and so many of them are best inferred from what we're told are exceptions. Children of Gaia wouldn't be described as being uniquely X if X weren't unique - same goes for Bone Gnawers and Glass Walkers.

                                    W5 removes the Garou Nation, from what we've seen, and leaves you to your own devices. This is based on previews, but that's the consistent impression I've gotten. It's made the Litany non-bigoted, but it's also made the Litany irrelevant. So what are you rebelling against exactly? What is the cost of being too cool for school new kids who fight the power? What society are you a punk IN?

                                                            Something I remembered and which is another indicator of how rushed the development of W5 seemed to be is this section from the Tribes-dev-blog-post from last October:
                                                            Tribe also imparts special spiritually mandated benefits and restrictions — a favor and a ban, something with which the werewolf gains proficiency from their spirit patron as well as a vow to uphold a certain way of life or attitude. For example, as a favor, Shadow Lords can conscript Stormcrow spirits to aid them, while, as a ban, the Galestalkers must partake of fresh kill daily, all based on the primal urges of their Patron Spirits.
                                                            This section is specifically talking about Favor and Bans and yet... now we know that all the Favors are a die-bonus to certain skills/actions. It certainly would explain the inconsistency since they were still developing the idea of how to handle Favor/Bans and were just rushing things after October to meet the January deadline.
