    As stated above, the reason the world is ending is not because garou used violent methods. Rather, it is because garou focused on things other than fighting the Wyrm. To the point where their old methods that USED to work no longer do, because they let the problem be too long in favour of inner-shifter wars.

    It is also always good to remember that garou aren't JUST humans, they aren't JUST focused on making human lives easier. They also have to think of their wolf-kin and other animals, which means that sometimes humans need to go.

    This thread is about the fact that W5 isn't interested in any of that nor is it interested in even addressing the apocalypse. Instead it uses the end of the world to make sure there is no way to actually make a difference if you try to gather up a larger group of garou. It wants a small scale game but in doing so it is saying there is no reason to try beyond your own nose.

        I feel like W5 would work better if it was more explicitly about being a post-Apocalypse setting. In the older editions, the idea was that the characters were fighting to push back the Apocalypse, and every victory meant one more day. But that's not the case in W5, the Apocalypse already happened and the werewolves lost.

        So the focus in W5 is more one about personal survival rather than fighting back. While W5 isn't a traditional "post-apocalyptic" setting, it shares a lot with that genre in terms of themes and mood.

        So the Cult of Fenris is like the remnants of the old government, you might see in a post-apocalyptic game, which is still trying to fight the old war. But that's pointless, since the war already ended and there's nothing but ruins to fit over. Of course the physical world isn't what you would consider apocalyptic yet, but with Gaia dead, the spirit world is dying, and then the physical world will follow after that.

        Ultimately the goal for characters in W5 isn't to save the world, but to survive and to help their loved ones survive, in an increasingly hostile world, one that's inevitably on its way to destruction.

                    The most succinct bit where you can see that happening is right there in the Introduction:

                    Eventually this path leads to a view that the world is in a catastrophic state and the time to act may have passed. This generation of Garou dwells in the midst of Apocalypse. It’s too late to avert the end.

                    That’s no cause for fatalism, however.
                    "The time to act may have passed.", "It's too late to avert the end."... I don't know about you but unlike what the text suggests that sounds pretty darn fatalistic to me. And then it ends with "Otherwise there will be no tomorrow.". But those two ideas can't both be true at the same time: It either is the Age of Apocalypse where it's too late to avert the end - or it is not.

                    But this fence-hopping keeps happening within the book. At one point, it's about the struggle against the odds to make sure there will be a tomorrow like there when it lists what Garou do in W5. That list isn't Apocalypse-themed, it's themed around Garou doing typical Garou stuff as they're fighting back. But then in discussions of the Umbra or the Garou Nation, it's all about "Look how shitty all of this is. This really is the Age of Apocalypse.". Or when it's saying that it's impossible for Garou to solve systemic problems but they do want Garou-PCs to still deal with local problems as if that matters, as if "small victories" in the face of certain doom matter.

                        The Wyrm has won. Gaia is dead. The Weaver is crippled, broken, lobotomized. The Wyld, diminished and corrupted but forever untamed, spews forth an endless river of horrors that even the Wyrm finds grotesque. The Wyrm sits atop the charred husk of the world, lord of all, and yet its victory is pyrrhic. It cannot escape its bonds. It can only stew in its pain and self-loathing. With no other enemies to fight, it turns upon itself. And beneath the Wyrm's notice, life clings and fights for its existence.



                        The Top of the World.
                        At the Top of the World sits the Wyrm in all its terrible glory. Its three heads rise up so high that they're visible from all points in the Northern Hemisphere. They constantly bite and tear at each other. None can slay the others, but they all try. As a result, they're covered with wounds that drip foul ichor and where that ichor touches the ground spawn great and terrible Banes. Seasons are no longer determined by the movements of the Earth, but by which of the Heads is dominant.

                        The Season of Calamity is also the Season of Growth. It is when farmers plant their corps, and when their crops try to kill them. It is the time when the sky battles the ground, when the wind fights the rain, when trees uproot themselves and turn to slaughter, not that there are many trees left.

                        The Season of Consumption is the Season of Harvest, of Glutony, of Famine, of Cannibalism. It is the season when It is the season when parents eat their children not because they are hungry, but merely for the pleasure of the eating. It is the season when the sun shines less, or not at all, it's light eaten before reaching the earth.

                        The Season of Corruption is thankfully rare.


                        Motorwolves and Chromebears:
                        To the very end, Gaia loved her children and it is only natural for children to outlive their parents. Knowing that she was doomed, Gaia made a choice to give her children a chance at the cost of the little life she had left. At the moment the Wyrm manifested on Earth, every living creature on Earth's surface experienced the First Change. Not a drop of changing blood was required, as Gaia was giving her very essence to her children. Extinct breeds walked the earth for the first time since the War of Rage. New breeds never before imagined came into being. Confused and without any sort of leadership or guidance the new Fera were immediately crushed. Most died. Some survived.

                        The survivors built new tribes for themselves, made new societies from the ruins of the old. Some crowd around the last fading cairns, hoping that the remnants of Gaia's life force will somehow protect them. Others find safety and power in mobility. They prowl the roads as normads and fight for control of the world's last remaining stores of oil. Elders and Wise Ones talk of a road warrior named Max, a myth from the before time.

                        You are a part of a Motorpack. Inspired by the legends of the World Before, you strive to make the Wasteland better than it is, so that others can eek out their livings just a little easier.

                        A Non-Exhaustive List of Locations:
                        The Wyrmlands - Area closest to the Wyrm's domain at the North Pole, where banes constantly war with each other. The Wyrmlands are defined by massive lakes of Wyrmblood fed by the Wyrm's dripping wounds.
                        The Motorlands - The Wastelands of North America, dominated by nomadic motorpacks. Being so close to the Wyrmlands, the Motorlands suffer from the worst of both worlds, both spiritual monstrosities and personal callousness. The Motorlands' inhabitants find it difficult to accept the possibility of hope, and are in desperate need of legends.
                        The Ant Empires - South America's rainforsts, still living in spite of the Wyrm's efforts, are dominated by militaristic wereant hives.
                        The Down Under - Australia, too far away from the Wyrm's emergence point and not important enough to bother with got off relatively unscathed, except for the complete collapse of human civilization. Major cities, unable to feed themselves due to infrastructure collapse, have been abandoned and reclaimed by what is left of nature.
                        TLDR: The world is reduced to a Gamma World style wasteland and most of humanity is converted into fera and have to survive the hell hole earth has become. HAVE FUN!!!
