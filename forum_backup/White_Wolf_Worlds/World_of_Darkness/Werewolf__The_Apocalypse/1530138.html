    [W5] Fixing the Cult of Fenris and creating the tribe it sprung from

    02-07-2024, 01:51 AM
    [My first attempt posting this thread was flagged as "unapproved" because I tried to edit the OP. Mods: Please feel free to delete the "unapproved" thread.]

    This thread is another spinoff from my previous threads (here and here) about about house ruling W5. (By the way, in case anyone is interested, you can find the latest version of my house rules [PDF, Dropbox link] here.)

    As the title state, in this thread, I would like to revise the Cult of Fenris. But before I can develop them any further, I need to better understand where they came from.


    So, for this expansion of the house rules, I will follow these two premises:
    • The Cult of Fenris is a fanatical Apocalypse cult of Garou, many of whom have fallen into hauglosk (as redefined in the house rules).
    • While Garou from all tribes joined the Cult of Fenris, a lot of the foundational members came from one particular tribe. I don't have a name for this particular tribe yet, so I'll call them the [Get] for now.

    My goals for these house rules will be as follows:
    • I'd like to stick to the general ideas and design philosophy behind 5th edition and W5; this includes that WtA is a reboot, not a continuation from legacy editions.
    • I want to create the [Get] in a way that makes the development of the Cult plausible, but not inevitable.
    • The [Get] should retain some recognizable elements of the Get of Fenris, but they should have their own niche in the setup of W5 tribes and be independent of human ethnicities or geographical regions.

    Again, I will build on something awesome posted by Knightingale, that is, this fantastic break-down of the tribes in W5. I switched the Silent Striders and Glass Walkers, however, so it looks like this now:
    GLORY
    (aspirational future goal)    		 HONOR
    (contributing to society)    		 WISDOM
    (occult secrets)
    STEALTH
    (secrecy, subterfuge)    		 Shadow Lords
    (Envision, Intimidate, Plan, Scheme)    		 Bone Gnawers
    (Eavesdrop, Infiltrate, Pilfer, Witness)    		 Silent Striders
    (Deliver, Journey, Sabotage, Scout)
    CONFRONTATION
    (engage enemies)    		 Black Furies
    (Avenge, Demolish, Dismantle, Overcome)    		 Galestalkers
    (Ambush, Harry, Pursue, Track)    		 Ghost Council
    (Seek, Solve, Subvert, Unearth)
    COMMUNITY
    (benefit the Garou)    		 [Get] Silver Fangs
    (Inspire, Lead, Motivate, Rally)    		 Glass Walkers
    (Build, Hack, Repurpose, Research)
    PROTECTION
    (defending)    		 Hart Wardens
    (Cultivate, Nurture, Protect, Reclaim)    		 Red Talons
    (Purge, Reduce, Ruin, Ward)    		 Children of Gaia
    (Discern, Discover, Heal, Puzzle Out)
    This table leaves a specific spot for the [Get].

    My ideas are still in the beginning stage. What I have so far is the following:
    • The [Get's] patron is Winter Wolf. In legacy, Winter Wolf was a totem of wisdom (or war) and associated with survival in harsh conditions, fierceness, and grit. I think this thematically well (and is a good connection back to the Get of Fenris and their ideology), and makes it possible that Fenris was a servant of Winter Wolf.
    • Considering the Glory/Community spot left in the table and the choice of tribe patron, I envision this tribe as Garou who view themselves
      • as conquerors of new territory and vanquishers of enemies, even under the most adverse conditions (GLORY),
      • as enforcers who keep order and control among other Garou (COMMUNITY),
      • as role models and teachers who use conflict and adversity to forge other Garou into perfection, increasing their prowess and survivability (COMMUNITY).
    With regard to tribe verbs, I have generated a few that might fit the general idea:
    • Enforce / Control
    • Conquer / Expand / Subject
    • Endure / Resist / Prevail
    • Harden / Forge / Train
    So, the idea is that the [Get] see themselves as the Garou's vanguard, forging the way for all the other tribes, teaching them how to be the perfect Garou (by being a role model and shaping them in a "growth through adversity" kind of way). At the same time, the seeds of authoritarianism and combativeness inherent in their self-identity is also what drove a lot of them into hauglosk.(I also imagine that, for the Shadow Lords and Silver Fangs, they often were useful idiots, more than willing to enforce their rules while seeing themselves as keeping order and helping the Garou Nation to remain united and strong.)

    What do you think?​

              This has always been something I’ve struggled with tbh. Even in legacy I’ve never been fully comfortable with the Fenrir uniquely having a wolf-manifestation for their tribal totem while every other tribe has a distinct totem and the rejection of Wolf in favor of tribal totems is supposed to be a significant thing.

              The problem is, at least for me, that Wolf, in some form or another, does fit most of my ideas about the Fenrir. While of course Fenris Wolf is a key part of their inspiration but also the image of the man-wolf warbands, koryos clad in wolf pelts and so forth is part of how I understand the Fenrir. I’m not certain what I’d replace it with.

              On one hand, I like Raven. Raven has war and death aspects, which would fit my idea of the Fenrir. On the other hand it would require some rewriting of the tribe’s relationship with both the Corax breed and the mythological figure of Odin/Wotan. Then I think Bear, which has some promise as Bear has the berserker association and invoked the image of pre-historic Bear Cults and can be imagined with war and death aspects, particularly with Cave Bear as a guardian of caverns. On the other hand this would require dealing with the tribe’s relationship with Gurahl and the Bear spirits relationship with the Garou in general. Wild Hound would be interesting and keep the koryos connotations I like and could invoke images like dog guardians of the underworld but it also smacks a bit of domestication and is still pretty close to wolf. Horses and Boars both have prominent roles in Norse myth but tbh I feel like those are more aligned with the Black Furies and Fianna. Well, maybe Boars could be work. They have the reputation for aggression and ferocity, plus the rooting for mushrooms could be given a spiritual connotation but then there’s the Grondr question and for me Boars just don’t quite feel right - they have a more Celtic vibe to me for some reason. Serpents could be interesting but then again there’s the Wyrmish connotations and also that Older Brother/Ghost Council already has something in that vein.

              I dunno, I’m open to ideas and suggestions. I suppose Boar and Bear might work best, although I still have a soft spot for Raven. I’m really not sure tbh.

                      I most definitely agree with the characterisation of pre-Cult Fenrir as a Tribe of Glory: one noted that there were fewer tribes of Glory than of Honour & Wisdom (A nicely symbolic evocation of the darker tone in this edition, Glory being in shorter supply).

                      I’m also perfectly convinced that the Cult of Fenris is, as cultists often are, either wilfully delusional or lying their tails off about Wolf being their Patron Spirit - it makes perfect sense that a movement/body proclaiming themselves the True Warriors of Gaia (with an undertone that they’re the only REAL Warriors of Gaia) would claim a special connection to the spiritual progenitor of all werewolves, therefore asserting their primacy over those that follow ‘lesser’ totems and those werewolves who must obviously be ‘less wolf’ than the werewolves actually being blessed by Wolf.

                      It actually struck me while reading through the list of Gifts in 5th Edition that Dire Wolf would be a near-perfect pick for the Patron Spirit of the Cult (and presumably the tribe from which they erupted): a prehistoric spirit of an animal that’s huge and inspiringly wolf like … but which might not actually be a wolf, going by some recent scientific studies.

                      I therefore respectfully recommend Dire Wolf as totem for the pre-Cult Fenrir (and possibly for the Cult of Fenris itself).
