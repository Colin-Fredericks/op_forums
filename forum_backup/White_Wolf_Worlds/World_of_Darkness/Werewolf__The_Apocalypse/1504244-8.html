                I think MyWifeIsScary brings up some points I want to comment on.

                What should be the relationship between the Black Spiral Dancers and the Followers of Set? The Sabbat and BSDs are explicit allies although like all things this diverges a lot according to Sabbat pack, mutual local interest, and other factors. And the Sabbat and BSDs have mutual interests in PENTEX. The two use each other in an alliance of convenience, but both would be quick to betray the other when necessary or convenient.

                But the Setite clanbooks are very silent on the BSDs as were the Books of the Wyrm on the Setites. We just have mention in odd sourcebooks like New Orleans By Night of local alliances between temples and the BSDs.

                Should Apophis be available as a pack totem for the BSDs? Are there any links between the Black Labyrinth and the Setites? Should the Setites have some kind of connection with BSD totems like Bat, Whipporwill, Hakakren, Kirijama, and the Green Dragon? Do the BSDs have any opinion of Set as a figure of the Apocalypse? Should Setites have strong alliances with particular lineages of the BSDs or their hives - perhaps "controlling" select kinfolk families and utilizing the BSDs born from them? Should there be any connection between Apophis and the Green Dragon?

                In a similar vein, is there any PENTEX subsidiary that would work better as a front for Setites rather than stuck as one of the companies mentioned in a PENTEX sourcebook, but only given one or two paragraphs and not developed further? Would Omni Television or Vesuvius Incorporated be better utilized by the Setites than PENTEX? If so, how? It doesn't have to be that the "Setites" as a whole control it, just a particularly powerful Setite elder and his allies. It would have to be developed in a specific way to serve that vampire goals instead of being a generic complaint about an industry which too many PENTEX subsidiaries tend to be. For some reason I think Avalon Incorporated (the toy company) might be better as a Setite asset than how it is presented through PENTEX.

                        I think the relationship with the Black Spiral Dancers and the Followers of Set is like the Followers realtionship with the Baali
                        The Settites fight the Baali, like Set fought Apothis
                        Pentweret​ from Lair of the Hidden,was made an offer by a Demon who had made offers to the rest of the Inconnu. His response was something to the effect of "Can you show me some respect and don't try that game on me?"
                        Corruption is a road to revelation for them
                        They refer to themselves as the Mesu Bedshet, the "Children of Rebellion".​
                        They seek to subvert and destroy whatever is good, noble, safe or beautiful within both Kindred and mortal society. They are plotters without peer, with an unequalled genius for misdirection and temptation. Their favorite weapons include drugs, sex, money, power, and vice in all its aspects. But for most of the Followers of Set, this corruption is just a means to an end. They believe in a radical split between the divine, eternal soul and the corrupt, transitory world and body. The laws of their faith say that deep down, the soul knows it does not belong in a prison of matter. Anything that distracts the soul from this knowledge and ties it to the world must be a trap – and if the world is a prison, then the gods must be its jailers. To achieve liberation, a soul must shed all the false doctrines, cravings and habits that bind it and blind it. Such self-awareness cannot come from mere argument or faith. Only direct experience of the most extreme sort can break the hold of the gods over the soul and allow liberation. Through these experiences, the Setites believe they can achieve truly divine power over the world and liberate themselves from every physical and spiritual constraint, as they already have taken the first steps out of the prison when they received the Embrace.
                        The Setites corrupt you socially and mentally, while the Baali can corrupt you in supernatural ways. The Setites try to make you change your mind, the Baali try to mess with your soul. You can also see that in the way, the two lines convert people. Setites have some members in their community, that are actually from other clans. Physically they remain members of those clans, they just follow a different religion. When Baali convert someone, this vampire gets another weakness and set of disciplines, they change the curse if you will.
                        It is true, though, that the ways these two act are very similar and that the too in the past often clashed with each other, because they occupy the same niche, so to say. But while both try to destroy the status quo, Setites do that for liberation (even if it often enough ends up in the exact opposite) and Baali do that to either shatter the universe or, in some certain cases, to prevent that from happening by feeding great old ones with a little bit of destruction to keep them pleased.
                        The BSD is basically the "Baali of the Garou Nation"; actually th Followers of Set and the Black Spiral Dancers have very little in common, even potentially enemies.
                        A bravem bold or fool settite maybe would try to use the BSD as pawns... dangerous game, like working with a Bane Mummy (a Bane Mummy is more likely to work with the Black Spiral Dancers than the Followers of Set in my humble oppinion)
                        Of course there are corrupt Settites who "failed" their own quest to free himself and others from the web of Ma'at, the Order that Shackles and basically a failed acolyte, or an Apep worshipper, more or less heretic who belives Set serves Apep and he is its champion....
                        Similarly, there are Black Spiral Dancers who are sane/insane enough to belive, their oal is to kill Gaiai, yes, but to save the world, because a killed spirit will reborn, and to save Gaia they have to kill her to hasten her rebirth, a hard reboot of Earth
                        Actually a Settite have more in common with a common Garou who belives the source of all problem is the Weaver... Ma'at and Weaver are basically the same, so both parties could be allies, enemies, rivals, or dance on the blade's edge altogether

                                I definitely think the potential is there for all the reasons you lay out. It's why I started the thread as I think they've been neglected as a Werewolf foe. However, it is easy to say such a thing. It is harder for an ST to demonstrate it at the gaming table. So I am definitely happy to see everyone's contributions to this thread, which has been more successful than I had hoped. But I do think there is still room to figure out details to demonstrate how the Setites can be a real menace to Garou with their own flavor. They should be seen differently than other vampires, and present a different challenge.

                                It is easy to make the Setite Antedeluvian a big bad guy. He obviously is performing all sorts of awesome, forbidden, lost magic. That's in the realm of Antedeluvians as a plot device for the setting. But it is not clear how regular Setite vampires can be more of a menace than other vampires, as they simply have access to the same sorts of powers as any other vampires have. So what makes them more dangerous than say the Gangrel or Tremere or Sabbat?

                                Well, I think one thing to do is give them a real motivation to fight the Lupines. It does not need to be anything about the Lupines themselves. It could simply be that they are after the same thing, and thus they encounter one another more than is the case for Gangrel, Tremere, Sabbat, or whoever. Caerns would be the obvious thing. But perhaps too obvious. Even if you limited what the Setites wanted to a specific kind of caern (to limit the amount of confrontations between the two groups), there are already a lot of groups who already go after caerns. What else might make a good target both groups want?

                                We might want to invert things, and instead of Setites going after Lupines, it is the Lupines (or Bastet or Mokole or whoever) who keep proactively going after the Setites. This could be done by simply having Silent Striders actively investigate them and alert other Garou to whenever they discover a Setite temple. Or maybe we make the Setites even more active on the "Sense Wyrm" radar than other vampires (maybe the clan always detects as Wyrm tainted regardless of their Path or Path rating). Or maybe whatever rites they do at their temple create a large amount of Wyrm taint, or somehow qualifies as a Pit). A combination of all of them. Also very interested in hearing other ideas.

                                Another thing to do is give the Setites a certain amount of accurate lore concerning the Changing Breeds, making them obviously more informed than any Gangrel or Tremere or other vampire scholar. This is easily explained given the clan's history as you explained. They might know all about caerns, septs, kinfolk, some of the different totems, auspices, their powers and gifts, and their standard weaknesses. Not all Setites have equal knowledge, but they do know more than the other clans.

                                Then you have to translate that knowledge into concrete things. Maybe Setite Temples lack any reflective surface because they don't want Garou popping out from the Penumbra to attack them. Maybe one reason the Setites put temples in desolate places is because they attract Banes who would attack any Garou attempting to spy on Setites by going into the Penumbra near the Temples. Maybe all Setite animal fomori can Sense Gaia and alert any Setite vampires, ghouls, or human cultists of their presence. Maybe the Setites always make sure they have a silver weapon of some kind accessible to them, and their temples usually have some sort of silversmith workshop. Maybe Setite sorcerers usually know the Path of Spirit Manipulation (Spirit Thaumaturgy) and have a standard list of some Banes they can always summon and bargain with, as well as having some fetishes available that vampires can use. Maybe a list of common Setite "fetishes" they can use to extract themselves from Lupine danger would be a good thing for an ST to have.

                                                    While looking into possible ideas for cults around Apep I stumbled over the Amkhat in MtR, a cult that has discovered a way to gain power from cannibalism. The cult doesn't have much depth to it as written, but I liked the idea of mortals that go on a hunt for supernaturals with a dark background- especially for a WtA game. Initially I thought this could make a good cult for Amam the Devourer (and maybe it does), but the longer I thought about it, the more I liked the idea of Mortals who are really, really scary™ to just about any supernatural out there. This could combine with Skindancers, maybe a Revenant line, Fomori etc., but I think something like Apep-Imbued Hunters could be really good - monstrous, but plain human.

                                                        Originally posted by AnubisXy View Post
                                                        I think for most werewolves, when it comes to vampires, a vampire is a vampire is a vampire. They're not going to care what clan the vampire claims to be a part of, and it generally isn't going to matter much.
                                                        I actually agree that clan is irrelevant to the Garou. But I do think the Setites are different - not because they are their own clan, but because they are a Wyrm cult. It just happens to be a Wyrm cult that is stacked with vampires as opposed to being a Wyrm cult of only humans. Most vampires - even though considered to be a Wyrm minion - aren't involved in a Wyrm cult. And even though some vampires are part of a Wyrm cult, there is something different about the Setites.

                                                        This particular Wyrm cult is known to have lasted thousands of years, produced a terrible curse on an entire Garou tribe, created the Bane Mummies, make fomori, summon Banes, and has enacted other atrocities. I think that attracts attention in a different way than just random Joe vampire. They are a distinct menace that is distinguished from vampires in general.

                                                        I mentioned the other non-Egyptian themed cults because I wondered if there was an opportunity to provide other tribes a "hook" to make the fight more personal in the way it is for the Silent Striders. My thinking is that it may be possible for a Get of Fenris to think this Wyrm cult is only a problem in Egypt or in decadent cities, but he might have a very different opinion about the Hall of Jormungandr because he views that as a more immediate threat. And so on with the other tribes. Then the question becomes whether the Garou understands these different Wyrm cults are in fact the same cult. And if so, how does that change their view of the Setites overall.

                                                        As far as I know there is no canon connecting these other groups to werewolves. So this is simply a brainstorming exercise.

                                                        It is perfectly fine for some or all of these Setite groups to have escaped detection by the Garou. And I like the idea that maybe some Garou have tied at least one of these groups to the main Wyrm cult, but others are still unknown or thought to be its own thing.
