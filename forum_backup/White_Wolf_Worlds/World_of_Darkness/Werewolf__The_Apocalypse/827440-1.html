        02-12-2016, 03:58 AM
        Game update:

        Session began with a ooc discussion, as the characters had already felt the physical sting of punishment from their sept mates and elders for a shameful and costly act (Punishmemt rites and ostracism) I felt it good practice to advise them the spiritual repercussions of debts now unpayable would now be realised. But I assured there would be opportunity for redemption down the line for them to choose to commit to and hopefully succeed at.

        Those that had completed their chiminage were told this wouldnt affect them directly. But could possibly affect dealings with the pack.

        The players of characters that hadnt or no longer could complete their chiminage were given the options to try and make it better through apology and more chiminage (but that failing a second time would be truly a terrible thing), or take the consequences and move on with what ever hangs over them following.

        The Fianna decided to take what ever is coming. Deciding whoever failed to appease Stag deserved their kicking and as a Galliard was more than willng to be the story of warning for others to learn by.

        Nice. He got hunted by Black Stag and its crew. It wasnt pretty.

        The child of Gaia decided to try to apologise and offer more chiminage. I decided unicorn wasnt an emotional manipularor due to its concepts of empathy and didnt lay it too thickly but displayed great consideration and understanding in its forgiveness.

        The apology went perfectly (rite of contrition), but then the spirit turned the tables on the character "last time I asked a chiminage of you. To learn from this (wisdom) I ask you to consider what you feel is appropriate chiminage for me on behalf of yourself to heal the tear between us."

        The player kind of struggled with that. Offering to be nice to other spirits. "I expect that of all my children." Then offered to heal all those who required it that he came across "as my child, would you not do this anyway?" To give it all of his gnosis "such a thing would help me, but would leave you weak, to weak to aid your pack and heal those who needed it more than I in the quests ahead of you." The player and character both seemed to be drowning. Unicorn offered guidance and the character asked please "the wound between us cuts deeper than it appears. It will and does affect all of our Tribe here. To heal the damage down and honor me as your tribal patron a plentiful chiminage would be to do something that would bring a closer connection to my other children here. If you wish for my advice, take the opportunity of your quests to the umbra to find our tribal home, know it and bring back guiding hearts. One for each of my children here, so they too may be closer to me and have a way to always find their way to our Homeland."

        The player accepted and thanked the great spirit.

        Now, the reaction from the players was huge. Some had really struggled, some had made a lot of deals with others and endangered themselves to fulfill their obligations. Heck the fianna got hunted gored and nearly neutered by black Stags unrelenting force of punishment.

        There was outcry, there was shame, not at me but at the coggie character from the rest of the group, including its player. Why? Because unicorn was so pure, so good, so forgiving and understandable that realising the cost and failure in honoring such a spirit stung and cut a hunded times deeper than getting a royal beat down.

        Perfect! Unexpected but perfect. I now have a character and the rest of the pack with a high focus on doing all they can to ensure this is done once an opportunity arises.

        Mechanically I went with all spirits disdain for the character due to what the character has done to sacred and beloved unicorn is evident. +1 difficulty to all spirit related rolls. Thats social interaction, Gifts, Rites, even his own Numen spirit and Pack Totem until the new Chiminage is completed (6 months in game). Considering they are off to the Aetherial realms thats gonna sting. Lets hope he doesnt botch anything with a planerary incarna.

        Thank you to all who offered their input.

            Situations like these can be a tough balance to strike- you don't want to come off as a vindictive storyteller, punishing the players for basically doing WtA wrong, but at the same time, Garou thumbing their noses at tribal totems would be unconscionable. When the PCs undergo punishment, it is an opportunity to grow the characters while deepening the player's understanding of the setting, and it sounds like you accomplished that here.

                            Originally posted by Somuchlove View Post
                            In a TT game I have a group of Garou that a few months ago turned to summoned Tribal Totems for aid in act of desperation (but for the betterment of Gaia), they each promised a lot in return should they have lived through the events. They did and now I have a couple of players that haven't kept up their side of the deal with the Spirits. Normally, they'd have to have something to give as chiminage first or there'd be a quest to gain this kind of favour in my games, but as above, was in a moment of desperation and they promised (tied themselves) to great acts in appreciation.
                            ..snip..
                            Any ideas, thoughts? any things you have done or seen others do towards Garou that fail to uphold their deals with spirits?
                            OK first off (all quickly off the head before i read any other responses), all and any attempts to summon other spirits to aid them get a +2 difficulty penalty assigned to the gift/rites.
                            You can also have Unicorn inform their sept master, and he can refuse to allow any rites to be performed till chimminage has been paid.
                            And lastly, since unicorn is about healing/mercy, start having people show up to pester the party (Or pc) for healing consistently for say a few weeks in game..

                            Originally posted by etherial View Post
                            Third, all Spirits will be giving these Garou the Stinkeye for failing to deliver in a timely manner. Only the Pack Totem is likely to give them the time of day until all the debt is paid, and even afterward, getting Spirits to talk to them would be like pulling teeth. Spirit Notoriety is a very real thing and it sounds like these Garou have earned it. NOTE: This may necessitate an OOC conversation so that the players are on the same page as you about the punishment. They might feel like you're punishing the players for being unable to solve a problem you the GM created, rather than the game world punishing the PCs for failing to live up to their promises.
                            Hence my above idea of making it harder to summon spirits in the first place..

He can still cast it on anyone in need, but he himself will remain unhealed.

                                                The story begins three months after the Pack received their punishments. Stripped of rank and privileges the characters as Cliath have spent the time as the Omegas to the Sept. They have been doing all the duties and chores no one else wants. No others, not Garou or Kin have socialised with them and they have not been allowed to speak at Moots nor even leave the Bawn in all that time (we didnt RP through all of this, as that would not have been fun at all for anyone. We just Fast Forwarded).

                                                Words and stories from around the Nation have been reaching the Sept and seemingly shared in similar tragedies of late. Tales of natural disasters across the Globe, An army of BSDs under the banner of the Metis born child of destiny levelling Caerns, and most disturbing, no new children to the Nation. All kin and Garou pregnant have sadly lost their precious packages before being able to give birth.

                                                Whispers and accusations point to the Apocalypse, and more than a few fingers point at our pack as being responsible for the turn of events. The only thing keeping them alive, is proof that their actions indeed did cause this turn of events.

                                                Theurges and those with spirit sensitive abilities through the Kinfolk have been plagued with terrible nightmares of things of the past, the preseht and the futures. The two common themes, a child that should not be, and a Red Star in the sky.

                                                The Nation has responded. More information must be gained. Insights and answers must be known and the Septs that can are sending packs to the Aetherial realm to gain that which is needed for the coming nights.

                                                Each Sept holds their challenges. Trials of Glory, Honor and Wisdom to test their packs and see which shall carry the Septs names to the Stars and Planetary Incarna themselves.

                                                Out of 5 npc packs and our stories player Pack they came third. Gaining the right to once again leave the Sept, Quest on behalf of the Nation and maybe, just maybe, redeem themselves in the process. (This we did RP through )

                                                Each Pack chose four incarna with which to seek out as best for their abilities and alignments of Tribe and Auspice.

                                                The Pack chose, Helios, Meros, Vulcan, Ruatma and began their journey under the patronage of their pack Totem Owl.

                                                            First stop. Meros.
                                                            The Pack chose to begin at the furthest reaches of the Realm and the final Planetary incarna and make their way back through the list and back home. Taking great efforts in using rites, rituals, gifts and benefits of Owl to begin their journey unseen and undetected. The way there is full of sights to behold, incredible sights that leave the Pack lost for words and starting to realise just how magnanimous the powers of the Heavens are, and how dangerous.

                                                            Through a group combination of astrolohical rites, enigmas and cosmology, the Coggie Theurge and Strider Ahroun find the right paths and lead the pack into Meros' busy domain of travellers and celestial storms. The place is very busy, but a special road is laid out for them that leads to an oasis.

                                                            With an offering, Meros, a 30ft gold and jewel adorned Anubis formed road traveller arives. They ask for his aid, his wisdom and his guidance.

                                                            He asks of them their story and purpose.
                                                            They offer chiminage of his chosing.
                                                            He tells them he will help them now, but not give the answers they search for until his chiminage is completed.
                                                            They agree.
                                                            He gives them a chance to practice how to speak and address Planetary incarnas. He tells them the best way to begin appropriately on a quest within the Realm is to visit either Hekios of Luna first. He shows them which road to take.

                                                            He tells his chiminage is the story of the Journey at their end, he is pleased to be there at the beginning but seeing them and hearing how their journey went will be their Chiminage to him.

                                                            They ask if they may have one question of importance each now. He smiles and agrees but only if they tell him how they see themselves now, and what they hope to gain from their journey.

                                                            His answers about the child, the coming days of the Apocalypse, the nature of prophesy and what he believes it to mean...shake them deeply.
