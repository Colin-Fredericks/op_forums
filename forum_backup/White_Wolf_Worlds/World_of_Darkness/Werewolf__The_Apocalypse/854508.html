    Migrating to the beast courts.

    03-28-2016, 12:09 PM
    Short story- I'm playing a character who likes travel and agressively tries to learn things so that nothing will ever catch her with her pants down. Her whole thing as a ragabash has been to scrutinise weaknesses and strengthen allies, especially herself, for the apocalypse, so she wants to look to the east and africa to see how things are done in different systems, because garou are so often fools. Also learning the secrets of other changers is a sure way to get strong.


    Now the ahadi is great (and not because I'm playing a Black get with that questionable Uktena ancestry) Because you simply prove your worth, get inducted with a ceremony, and carry on as normal with a bit more cooperation with other breeds. It doesn't yet exist in the timeline we're in. I could help out in the war against blacktooth though.


    Now In the beast courts, joining looks like a mess, and they haven't really explained the options that well. A 4th rank Khan owes her a favour and the stargazer teaching her Kailendo in a bone gnawer sept buggered off because she might have helped a Khan kill a close friend of his, so she has reasons to go east.

    But, well, from what I read, the possibilities of joining the beast courts are limiting/confusing
    -Cast of my old renown system (Honour for virtue, do I keep wisdom and glory where they are?),maybe start from rank 1, and get a new auspice and join a mountain sentai. Wear a nice badge.
    An additional note- Virtue Is the same thing as honour. This isn't good for other changers, but can't garou just cheat and pay lip service to both?
    -Keep my normal renown, Join a wave sentai with or without an auspice.... I'm not sure if you can get a beast court auspice with this method. Not sure if you get to wear a badge with this method either.


    Now resetting rank is pretty unacceptable to this character. Offending an entire brood of spirits so seriously just isn't something considerable (especially when you've got the renown to be fourth rank. Still third, but that's because my character's too young for it to be wise to advance. ) (also she's a pretty serious collector of gifts) Now, Judging by Khan and hakken, she doesn't actually have to leave tribe (technically) or auspice,so maybe the eternal enmity of the spirits isn't going to be a problem, but the rank reset is (even though the character's younger than she should be for her rank, but she would likely plan to re-enter the garou nation after a few years). I suspect that the western stargazers didn't all loose their rank when they left the garou nation for the courts, that would've been a terrible decision to turn Athro and elder into 'wood', but it wouldn't be a great decision to deny an elder stargazer duties in courts (should they ever get their heads out of the clouds and ask)

    ​Any solutions?

      When you join an organization, you typically start from the bottom, so you get to be a Rank 1 member of a given Beast Courts auspice, just like a renunciate. This involves undergoing a similar rite. Rejoining the Garou Nation, if they let you, would follow the same procedure.

      Virtue is similar to the Garou conception of Honor. However, it's a bit more inclusive, since you need to be of a mind to think of Fera as comrades and not simply threats.

      It's uncertain where elder Stargazers sat after the sides switched, but it's entirely possible that they might have been given trials to prove their worth as a kind of formality. Then again, the Beast Courts were always more accepting of them than outsiders of other tribes.
