    Werewolf 2015

    07-29-2016, 10:30 AM
    I am trying to construct a Werewolf 2015 conversion (I have done a similar conversion already for Vampire) and I was wondering if anyone is willing to share their changes to Werewolf canon that would allow such a conversion. I have determined that several propositions must be true: a) the Apocalypse could not have happened (obviously), b) that the Red Star could not appear in the sky (otherwise the first proposition would not be true), c) that new Garou (and Fera) would have to have undergone the Change since 2000 (otherwise the first and second propositions really do not matter), and d) that the world appears to be identical to our world (otherwise it would not be a dark reflection of our world, a world of darkness, but a twisted shadow of our world).

    One of the changes that I am making is that the Gurahl discovered DNA in 2000. After two years of debating, they forcefully took over the facility in 2002 because they could not ignore the potential of using genetic engineering to spread the genetic signatures of the Changing Breeds through the human and animal population. After their takeover, they gave anyone who was responsible for atrocities against the Garou to their associates in the Garou Nation, which gained them some grudging respect from the Garou. They have used the technology that they gained from the takeover from DNA to discover the surviving kinfolk of the Camazotz Fera in 2004 and, in 2006, started to import them from Mexico and Central America. In 2008, they succeeded, with the help of the Uktenu and the Wendigo, in freeing Turtle from its Weaver prison, in exchange for promising to help resurrect the Croatan Tribe from the Croatan Kinfolk among the Uktenu and the Wendigo Kinfolk). In 2010, they started, with the help of Turtle, to aid the first Camazotz in nearly five hundred year to experience the Change. In addition, they have started to spread the Gurahl genetic signature through the North American Black Bear population (the North American Black Bear has a wild population of around a million, meaning that the Gurahl have a potential population of animal Kinfolk that is five times that of the global population of wolves in the wild).

          Actually, DNA has been successful in using biological and genetic markers to find Kinfolk (Book of the Weaver, p. 97), as they have been able to find unaware Kinfolk.
