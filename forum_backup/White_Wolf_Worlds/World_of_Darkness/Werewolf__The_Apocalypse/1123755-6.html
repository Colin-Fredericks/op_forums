                                                Agreed. I think that there are a bunch of ways in which Pure Breed could function better. The Silver Fangs would be the leaders of the Garou because they are simply better at Gifts and Rites than most Garou, while the Silver Fang Kinfolk would be involved in business and politics outside of the Garou Nation.
