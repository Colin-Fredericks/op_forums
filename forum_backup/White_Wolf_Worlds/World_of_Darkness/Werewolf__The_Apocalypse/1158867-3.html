        I liked the environmental angle. It was different, at least for me, in terms of how the modern Urban Fantasy/Horror genre presents fighting supernatural monsters. Usually some demonic shit shows up to destroy the world cause they're Evul rather than manifesting through a real world concern that will kill us. I had a lot of fam and friends at Standing Rock, which the Lakota were proven right when 210,000 gallons of oil just leaked. Plus another pipeline is planned for going through the Boundry Waters between the Ontario and Minnesota. If you do the research, we're actually in an oil bubble, and a lot of these pipelines don't need to be made. They're being built to keep investors involved in the oil companies, because a prospering industry is always building infrastructure. It's just like it was at the end of the housing boom. So yeah, in the real world money is wasted on projects that go no where, but in the end profit the tippy top. So there are ways for an entity like Pentex to pointlessly trash the environment, but still reap a lion's share of profits.

        That said however, having the Wyrm manifest only through modern industrial consequences is limiting and doesn't add up to this being a cosmological war that has gone on since the calamity that severed the spirit and flesh into separate realities. As a result, we explored the Wyrm as a corrupter - a destroyer and twister of the world. It took the spiritual reflections of the hopes, dreams, and ideals of individuals to whole societies and species, then ripped them apart into nightmares that only seek to annihilate those who gave them birth. We also played up the damaging efforts of the other Triat members. Like thanks to the Weaver's webs severing the worlds more and more, it's not so much the Umbra has become an empty place, but the spirit and flesh are being pulled so taut that they're beginning to outright diverge. Spirits pulled away from their physical reflections are losing their purpose and becoming petty creatures playing power games with each other as they try to get what they can from the decaying spirit orders. Meanwhile, the places where the Wyld is still active, it's becoming more extreme and chaotic as it's being choked from Gaia, causing freak disasters and social chaos in both the Umbra and on Earth. The environmental stuff is an obvious byproduct of all this spiritual imbalance. Meanwhile the Garou are completely overwhelmed at what they can do about any of it.

        I'd like to further add that I think another problem people (including writers) have with picturing how the Wyrm and other spiritual entities effect the world is they look at it too much like an alien entity invading the world. The Wyrm, Weaver, Wyld, Gaia, and the rest are also seen too much like gods from D&D with approachable logical human like goals rather than the spiritual manifestations of fundamental aspects of existence. So pollution and other ills are seen as being directed by alien god beings and thus removing the evil humanity does on it's own. The Wyrm is best seen as a part of existence. The Garou are fighting against corruption and entropy run a muck, which is a part of life from an atomic level to the heights of the cosmos. Like you could say the Gobi Desert has aspects of the Wyrm since there's places there that bacteria can't even survive. So my tip is to look at the struggle of WtA like this. The triat can't be defeated, but they need to be balanced with each other.
