    Rules clarifications for formori

    05-16-2017, 10:40 PM
    I wanted to clear up some things about Fomori, especially about the quick Fomori rules in the core book. I get the impression that Fomori cannot soak aggravated damage, armoured skin allows them to soak lethal, and armoured hide allows them 3 dice to soak aggravated at difficulty 8. I do get the impression that most of them are pretty squishy and would be torn to tissue by a Garou in Crinos.

    I read the example of play and got more confused

    When one Garou hits the Fomor with an axe, the Fomor is able to roll stamina to soak aggravated, I thought only Garou could do that. In addition, when the Fomor strikes back with tentacles dripping with acid, the Garou rolled his stamina of 3 dice to soak, wouldn't he be able to roll 6 soak dice since Crinos gave him a +3 stamina bonus? I am a bit confused.

    According to the rules, Fomori seem more like a speed bump of mooks rather than a credible threat, unless they go to the default " shotguns with silver buckshot" to kill Garou. Otherwise, werewolf is like Superman, invincible unless you use Kryptonite ( or silver in this case). If someone can clarify the rules on how Fomori can be a danger without always being loaded with Silver, it would be appreciated

                  Aggravated damage is one of those things that can be confusing. Very important - aggravated damage does NOT mean unsoakable damage. Instead, it means damage with special rules on healing, and that different kinds of creatures soak it differently. The book does say that "humans can't soak aggravated damage." That says nothing about supernatural creatures. To use the Vampire game as an example, vampires normally don't soak Aggravated damage, but they can if they have Fortitude (although of course if you are using the Werewolf rules to simulate a vampire, that's not a distinction to make). So what about Fomori? Well, that get's tricky. Nowhere does it state that Fomori can normally soak or not soak. The wording of the Armored Hide power seems to imply that the Fomori normally can't without a Power that allows them to do so.

                  Most Fomori are not at the power level of even a Rank 1 Garou. Typical Fomori will only present a true problem for a Garou or pack if they have great numbers. Generally, their best tactic is offense to bring down the Garou fast enough that the Garou won't rip them all to shreds. In general, WoD combat is about offense, not defense. So not being able to soak is probably not an issue.

                  So what about the example if the W20 book? The easiest answer is the example is probably wrong according to the rules in the actual game. As written, the Fomori wouldn't be able to soak the damage. We can chalk that up to this particular Fomori having a special Power the ST gave them that doesn't match the rules. Or maybe the ST says any additional Stamina die gained through Powers can soak Ag. (Most likely scenario is that this was a mistake). When the Garou is hit by the Tentacle Fomori, he should have soaked with six dice. That's just a straight out error.

                  As for how Fomori can be a threat, I suggest the following. 1) Sheer numbers. 2) Certain defense Powers that harm the Garou when they attack them (like Poison Tumors, Toxic Secretions). 3) No straight up fight. Use ambush, terrain, and any other advantage you can think of. If you don't like the idea of so many disposable human Fomori, use animal Fomori to make up the numbers.

                  Know when to make fights hard, and when the fights should be against mooks. Not every fight should be a real threat. Let the PCs shine for most of the game. At the climax, then you can use a real threat.
