          Originally posted by Penelope View Post
          Also, are the Bunyip really Garou since their animal Kinfolk (the thylacines) aren’t wolves?
          Yes. They used a specific ritual to adopt different kin (learned from the Mokole) when they settled a land without native canines with the earliest humans to reach Australia between 40 and 65 thousand years ago (long before dingoes showed up between 4 and 8 thousand years ago). For all spiritual and mechanics purposes Bunyip are Garou, not a separate Changing Breed.

          If biology mattered more than spirit, the Nuwisha would be Garou (as wolves and coyotes so close they can have fertile offspring) - but obviously they are not.
