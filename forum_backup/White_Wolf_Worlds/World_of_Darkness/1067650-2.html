    IIRC, the "B-list" (or as I refer to them, Twilight People, since they exist on the edges of the various subcultures of Darkness like the Kindred, Garou, etc.) are:

    Ghouls and Revenants (I really wish they'd been called something else, since the term refers to walking corpses)
    Kinfolk (whose main deal is just that they don't go into a mindless panic state when seeing werebeasts in full combat mode, but can end up with special powers sometimes)
    Sorcerers
    Mediums
    Kinain (the fey equivalent of Kinfolk)
    Witchhunters (the people covered in Hunters Hunted, The Inquisition, Project: Twilight, Halls of the Arcanum)
    Psychics (whom I wish had had their own book instead of being stuffed into Sorcerer Revised)
    Shaman (who are covered - kind of badly IMO - in the Spirit Ways and - much better - Dreamspeakers Revised)
    Criminals (the Mafia, as well as some stuff covered in the KotE book Killing Streets)
    Strike Force Zero and The Shih (Demon Hunter X)

    Things like Mummies, Changing Breeds, The Risen, Inanimae, the various Asian supernaturals, Demons, and Hunters/Imbued are full blown Creatures of Darkness (A-List)

    IIRC, the various forms of Possessed sort of fluctuate somewhere between the two depending on how powerful (and inhuman) they are.

    There are also Bystanders, failed Hunters/Imbued who don't get any powers, but I can't recall if there is anything that actually differentiates them from normal people other than being made aware that supernatural monsters exist. (I think they're in the Hunter Players Guide)

                Thralls can get a variety of bonuses from the Pacts they make; from extra dots in Attributes and Abilities, more Willpower (all of those are bought with Freebie Points, per 1 point of Faith the Thrall has 10 Freebie Points spent on them), weaker versions of the Lores that the Demons have and a variety of utility powers (immunity to mind control, immunity to possession and so on). But Thralls are rarely of enough Faith to have a lot of abilities: two dots is the norm, three for faithful, four for zealous and five for Pope-level faith. So finding a fitting Thrall with four dots is a feat in and of itself. This severely limits how powerful a Thrall can be.

                This would make them a bit more powerful than something like a Ghoul, but less powerful than, say, a Sorcerer.

                                                It sounds like you're going for a Year of the Ally type of game — http://whitewolf.wikia.com/wiki/Year_of_the_Ally

                                                They're the mortal or nearly mortal allies of the core book supernatural creatures. Here are their names followed by their game line and the primary books with information about them:
                                                • Ghouls (Vampire: The Masquerade) — Ghouls: Fatal Addiction, V20, Ghouls and Revenants
                                                • Kinfolk (Werewolf: The Apocalypse) — Kinfolk: The Unsung Heroes, W20, Kinfolk: A Breed Apart
                                                • Custos (Mage: The Ascension) — Ascension's Right Hand
                                                • Mediums (Wraith: The Oblivion) — Mediums: Speakers with the Dead
                                                • Kinain (Changeling: The Dreaming) — The Enchanted, C20
                                                • Bystanders (Hunter: The Reckoning) — Hunter Players Guide
                                                • Thralls (Demon: The Fallen) — Damned and Decieved
                                                Sorcerers are mortal practitioners of hedge magic. They were originally a stand alone group not tied to any particular game. Their book was a black cover World of Darkness book. The revised version of their book tried to put them under the Mage umbrella, but it was an awkward fit. M20 seems to be reversing course on that.

                                                Edit to add: I completely forgot about the W20 kinfolk book. For some reason I was thinking it wasn't out yet, but there it is, sitting on my bookshelf.
