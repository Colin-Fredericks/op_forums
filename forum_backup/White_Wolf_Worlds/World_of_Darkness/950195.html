                                          Technically, in the USA, middle class is defined as having a household income of $100,000 to $1,000,000 per year (people often confuse middle income with middle class, so it is an understandable mistake), which would be the top 5% to top 0.1% of households. Resources 5 should not be allowed in a campaign unless you want your characters to be constantly pursued by con men and entrepreneurs looking for investors.

                                                  No, you are confusing middle income with middle class. A $50,000 household income is a working class income because it is less than 400% of the federal poverty level for the average household in the USA, which qualified them for health care subsidies under ACA. You are not middle class until you stop qualifying for programs designed to help poorer Americans.

                                                  For the average household, the middle class starts at over $80,000 a year in a rural area in the USA. In New York City, due to the high rents, middle class starts at over $160,000. In Silicon Valley, due to very high rents, middle class starts at over $320,000. If you are below these income thresholds in these areas, you are, at best, working class.
