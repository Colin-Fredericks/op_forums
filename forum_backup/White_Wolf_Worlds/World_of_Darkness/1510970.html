      I always go back to the WoD Population Project for such questions: https://web.archive.org/web/20201112...ation-project/

      Which states: "There are two conflicting accounts. 1 in a million in a few older sources, and 1 in 150,000 mortals in Fallen Tower: Las Vegas. The first number doesn't leave enough mages for all their societies. The second, newer number incorporates all the sorcerers as well, which outnumber mages 3 to 1." So that would be 1 fully Awakened for every 600,000 Sleepers.

      My head-canon for why there's two conflicting accounts is that there's a pre-Avatar Storm number and a post-Avatar Storm one. In the latter case there's more mages on physical Earth cause they're mostly stuck here. Before then Awakened had a tendency of wandering off into the Umbra or to Horizon Realms. So there was a higher turnover rate before.
