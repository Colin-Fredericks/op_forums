            No, I do not agree because they are not comparable. Even Bodhisattvas are not comparable because they simply do not have sufficient Chi storage to compete with Blood Pool storage of the 4th generation. Which Bygones and Jagglings are you talking about? Because I am pretty sure that any that made the mistake of materializing in front of a 4th generation would rapidly regret that mistake (a 4th generation vampire could potentially attack them ten times during the first turn, with 18 dice per attack, and deal 18 dice of aggravated damage per attack, plus successes).

            Let us take a look at what else 4th generation vampires are capable of. Create Name (Obfuscate 9) allows you to become someone completely different and, as long as you are not observe by someone with Auspex 9, your disguise is perfect. Psychic Assault (Auspex 8) allows you to instantly kill a target with five successes (not difficult for a 4th generation vampires since it is Manipulation plus Intimidation versus Willpower) and, even if the target resists, they lose half of their permanent Willpower, which would make the second use almost assured of instant death. Mythic Form (Protean 8) allows you to become a Dragon. And these are not the most broken or obscure powers that a 4th generation is capable of using.
