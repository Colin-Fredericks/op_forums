                                    RichT -

                                    Sure. I'll try to break things down a bit of things I know people are either actively using or have used relatively recently.

                                    Play by Post (not going to worry about examples, there's a section on the forum of it).

                                    Minimal VTT - basically people playing in close to the default TT style but through various programs - examples:
                                    Skype
                                    Google Hangouts
                                    Discord
                                    Augmented VTT - so using some sort of customized interface to add digital tools to game play instead of just for distance play - examples:
                                    Roll20
                                    MU* (haven't check in on those in awhile but MU* use had a small dedicated base that I doubt stopped yet)

                                    Persistent Chats - games that follow the model of the old official WW chats like New Bremen - examples:
                                    Patched Digichat (doesn't seem like it's going to work as everyone seems to be fleeing to other options as the patches keep failing to keep the program running)
                                    Parachat
                                    AddonChat
                                    Chatblazer
                                    PHP Chat

                                                    My impression isn't that they will cease to base the brand in TT, but that they'll cease to base their income in TT.

                                                    The problem with TT is that, once you buy the book, the group is unlikely to buy another. In the traditional model, we are very hard to squish for money.

                                                    So they are trying to focus on paid events, other media, and general ways on making us spend money in a regular basis. Something to make us consume the brand beyond just the books.

                                                    Yes, this at a certain point means a focus to the brand. But it doesn't necessarily means the game will be bad. We are a hard market to provide a company, with luck this will make the company survive while providing a good product.

                                                    I'm not too much confident, but my worries aren't about the model necessarily.

                                                    On a side note, I heard about they wanting to do video games more than TT way before this survey.
