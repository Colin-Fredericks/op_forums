    Kindred venues that sell blood

    03-03-2026, 08:09 PM
    Something I've seen in various chronicles is a place where Kindred cannot only relax or party (like the Succubus Club), but also provides vitae to Kindred. By that, I mean blood is a commodity provided to them for sale at such places, they are not simply a location where Kindred can hunt or bring their own herd to feed on. The owner of the premises offers blood as a menu item. It's something I remember seeing a lot in chronicles both tabletop and LARP. (In the Succubus Club itself, I think the intention was simply to allow Kindred to hunt there at a much lower Difficulty.)

    On a certain level this makes sense. One would want "refreshment" when one relaxes or parties. However, the logistics of this for Kindred are much harder than for mortals. Even if we assume this is just extra Herd being available (as opposed to cold blood), it has to be a pain in the ass to make available enough blood. Living people used as Herd are much more likely to be killed during feeding by customers than they would by the Herd's owner. Making cold blood available in mass quantities is a risk to the Masquerade. Running out of blood might risk Kindred going into frenzy as they get angry about being denied "service".

    So is this actually a plausible scenario? Or is it just not worth the hassle?

    If you think it is plausible, what should the cost of that be? I don't remember payment being an issue as usually it was just handwaved by the STs.

    If you put it purely in financial terms, Kindred with high Resources might want to simply buy whatever they need and avoid the hassle of hunting. If you make payment be in something else, what makes sense? I don't think boons would work well for various reasons. Kindred are unlikely want to be into prestation debt for something so trivial, or if you make it too cheap, the proprietor is unlikely to make use of so many trivial boons (or simply managing to accurately record it all). Is there something else that could be easily used as "currency" that would be both convenient and still valuable?

    Instead of actual financial transaction, does anyone suggest an alternate way of handling this (other than making it a rare exception for especially trusted friends of the proprietor) where vitae is "for sale"?

                                              If you wanted a relatively straightforward system, I'd imagine you would set it up where you can get X Blood Points with Y Resources on Z timeline, to represent vampires with sufficient wealth that they can feed somewhat regularly in that fashion.

                                              For example:

                                              Resources O: the Kindred can afford 1 BP per month without overextending financially.

                                              Resources OO: the Kindred can afford 1 BP per week without overextending.

                                              Resources OOO: the Kindred can afford 1 BP per day.

                                              Resources OOOO: 3 BP per day.

                                              Resources OOOOO: 10 BP per day.

                                              * the numbers are just spitballs, and would probably need to be tuned ttuthe needs of the chronicle

                                              Anyone without the sufficient level of Resources, or has hit the limit of their 'budget,' can then engage via Boons/favours, AKA plot hooks. Probably plenty of members of that kind of organization who are paid in blood themselves.

                                              As for why it doesn't feature more heavily in VtM, I'd guess it's because that kind of business is pretty risky to the Masquerade. IIRC even most typical Herd members aren't really supposed to know that they are vamp-chow. So trying to keep enough people on hand to feed a community of Kindred is creating a lot of potential witnesses who have probably seen way more than most vampires would like.

                                              I think most fictional settings that feature that sort of stuff tend to presume an ironclad Masquerade, with vampires controlling *everything.* In contrast, while VtM loves its undead puppetmasters, the drama of having to maintain the Masquerade is a big part of the story, and it might cheapen it if vampires are too known fr grossly reckless displays of their predatory nature.

                                              Not saying it doesn't/shouldn't happen. But I'd bet it's more common in Sabbat domains, for example.

                                                  Originally posted by Michael View Post
                                                  I don't think so. My point is that such a local establishment is going to become the local government quite rapidly. Like, you're making the local vampires dependant on you for their food supply, that gives the owner massive influence over local society.
                                                  Not necessarily. Your scenario implies that the establishment's provision of blood is superior to what the Kindred can do on their own, and that is probably not the case. I imagine that most vampires would do far better getting their own blood. Yes, if you are at the club itself, enjoying/consuming one or two blood points may be convenient, but you would be paying a premium to do so. The "cost" (whatever that is to be determined) should be higher than getting it yourself. And there are also lots of other disadvantages to it as well - a centralized distribution point has its own risks of disruption, security risks, delays, inconvenience (since you are not always there), quality control, etc.

                                                  People can get all their alcoholic drinks at bars and eat all the time at restaurants, but there is a reason why most people don't. It's more expensive than getting it themselves, grocery stores are usually more convenient and ubiquitous, they can get exactly what they want as opposed to whatever it is that establishment serves, etc.

                                                  Certainly in my scenario, such an establishment would not be superior (overall) to the Kindred getting their own blood. It's simply a convenience offered for Kindred who happen to already be there, and would be more "expensive" accordingly. The establishment has its own expenses and costs to cover after all that would get baked into the price, not to mention the normal effects of supply and demand. There is always likely to be far higher potential demand at such places than the establishment could safely offer.
