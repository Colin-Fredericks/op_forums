    01-08-2015, 04:09 AM
    Hi guys,

    I don’t know if that kind of thread already exist, but i didn’t find one. The idea is to collect your wishes for futures projects for the cWoD collection and, why not, submit it to the OPP team.

    Here are my personal whishes:

    - V20:
    • Guide to the Unknown (First ed was a really disappointment)
    • Book of Antiquity: I don’t know what should be the form, just a sourcebook or a stand-alone as DA, but I would really loved to have some details Of Rome, Sparte, Carthage and stuff like that. Background, new rules and that kind of things. It would make a great KS too.
    • Guide to the laibon
    - W20:
    • Tribebook Bunyip
    • A campaign focused on Umbra. I always had trouble to create a specific campaign on that theme.
    - M20:
    I never really play this game, so I’m just following the movement without really new idea on it.

    - Wr 20:
    • A book focused on the American civil war would be interesting!
    - C20:
    • A core rulebook? ;-)


    And you ?
