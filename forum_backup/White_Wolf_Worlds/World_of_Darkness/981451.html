    On the Spheres and the Metaphysical Trinity

    10-04-2016, 11:18 AM
    A while ago I made a post about Spheres and Patterns, making the argument that while Life, Forces, and Matter are referred to as the "Pattern" spheres, that all spheres can be said to have a pattern to them, this was how that argument went:

    I'd make the argument that all spheres have associated patterns to them, each at various tiers of separation from the mundane world.

    The way I've seen it, the nine spheres fit into three tiers.

    At the base tier are the three Material patterns. Matter, Forces, and Life. All of which comprise everything that we can physically see and interact with mundanely. What more is there to say? Sleepers are the most familiar with this stuff.

    In the middle tier are the three Primordial patterns. Spirit, Entropy, and Mind. These are a step removed from material reality, but still be sensed and manipulated by mundane will. Be it by being sensitive to spiritual activity, empathetic towards emotions, or simply aware of ones fate and good at statistics. In addition, while Matter, Forces, and Life make up the building blocks for all physical things in mundane reality. Spirit, Entropy, and Mind make up the building blocks of the near umbra. Entropy of the Lower Umbra, Spirit of the Middle Umbra, and Mind of the High Umbra. As a result, Sleepers may be aware of these forces and aware of many philosophical and mystical debates about them, they may even feel sympathetic towards them or feel a personal connection to heavy resonance locations, their own fate and eventual decay, and even a vague sense of where or when the gauntlet weakens. They may not understand these patterns, but they can still feel them for what they are, even if they later dismiss them.

    Lastly, there's the outer tier which holds the three Esoteric patterns. Corr. Time, and Prime. These patterns are both an integral part of mundane reality, but at the same time lie completely outside our ability to manipulate or even sense them mundanely. Sleepers are trapped with the core belief built in us that space is big and distance is not an illusion. Time too functions exactly how we perceive it, as a constant forward march. While Prime lies completely outside of anything anyone but the awakened can even comprehend. Sleepers simply don't consider Space and Time to be related to one another. Sure, they may hear all sorts of Physics stuff that shows they are, but do they really understand it? If they did, they are more likely to be Extraordinary Citizens than simple sleepers. The same goes doubly for Prime, the very idea that these is this metaphysical substance, as the Technocracts may call it, Dark Matter, or Dark Energy, which binds everything together and simultaneously allows all fundamental things to function. Completely beyond their capability to understand. Even if they know of it, to understand it is a completely different enterprise.
    So, to summarise, I've identified three categories of sphere. Material (Matter, Life, Forces), Primordial/Umbral (Mind, Spirit, Entropy) and Esoteric (Prime, Corr, Time)

    I started thinking about this a bit more in depth, and wondered if it was possible to further categorise them. Taking these three sets of three and further subdivide them, allowing them to fit on a 3x3 grid not unlike the whole Lawful Good to Chaotic Neutral system that DnD employs. I was thinking of various things that could possibly apply to these spheres apart from that which I already identified, when it occurred to me that the books already had the answer. The Metaphysical Trinity that is Stasis, Dynamism, and Entropy.

    For ages after reading about the Metaphysical Trinity, I had such a hard time seeing how it applied to the spheres at all. Sure one of the spheres was Entropy, but it seemed a bit of a stretch to assign Dynamism or Stasis to any of the others. Sure it was how resonance functions, and it played a role in the whole Technocrat, Marauder, Nephandi cycle, but it didn't seem to fit in with the actual spheres themselves. But when I started playing with these definitions within the constraints I've described above. Everything started to click and make sense.

    Within each of these three categories, Material, Umbral, and Esoteric, we have three spheres which correspond to one of the three Trinity Resonances.

    The Material Spheres

    - Matter is Static
    The magic done may be to change things into thing or break things down, but the effects of matter magic tend to be in fixing the nature of the object into a new and permanent state of being. When a substance is altered, they tend to stay altered unless intentionally given a time limit. As such, the sphere defaults to permanent changes.

    - Life is Dynamic
    Unlike Matter patterns, Life patterns are forces of constant change. Their dynamic nature makes them more difficult to deal with than Matter patterns as a result, and is the reason why changes to their type can result in Pattern Bleed if not made permanent.

    - Forces is Entropic
    This is true in the initial sense of the word. Entropy as a concept has always been about the dispersal of energy. Heck, it's even in the 2nd law of Thermodynamics. Yes the sphere can be used to change energies or even make certain things stronger and more solid, but at the end of the day, energy will tend towards greater entropy, either though eventual dispersal or immediate instability. In addition to this, the entropic nature of Forces is codified into the very rules of its use. Forces allows an extra point of damage, and can cause Aggravated Damage as early as the second level

    The Umbral Spheres

    - Mind is Static
    Yes the mind can be very dynamic with ideas and thoughts, and forgetfulness and mental disease ad an entropic spin to this. But consider this; Stasis as an idea in Mage has not been simply about codifying everything down to the last thread. There's a huge element of this but it's always been done with a goal in mind: Creation. At the heart of all of static reality is the idea of creating and perfecting, and preventing change and decay from removing the chance to achieve true perfection. That is the very essence of how humans function. We are beings in search of perfecting, we strive to adapt and develop, to make things easier, quicker, or better understood. This is the very thing that our minds are built around, recognising patterns and developing this information and recording it, making it static, so that that when our bodies eventually fall apart, our ideas can live on in the minds of generations ahead. Ideas may be reexamined or changed over time, yes, but this is done always with the idea of perfection. If eternal life exists outside of Life effects, then it is here in the eternally static space of the Mind.

    - Spirit is Dynamic
    I don't think this one requires too much explanation. The Umbra has always been a pretty inconsistent location. You could be heading in one direction on day and find yourself in Flux, but return there the next day and all you're left with is a big hole in the ground. Pathways open and close at a moments notice, bridges form and disappear dependant on the will of the spirits and the phases of the moon. Even to multiple observes, the Umbra will look and feel different depending on what they bring with them both conceptually and physically. The Spirit sphere reflects this in its utility beyond the velvet curtain. Spirit can be used similarly to Life and Matter sphere upon spiritual objects and beings, and at the same times allows means of communication as well as enforcement.

    - Entropy is Entropic
    Yep. Ain't that a shock. I don't think anyone is going to contest that Entropy is Entropic. I would like to point out that, like the other spheres, Entropy can be used for Static and Dynamic purposes as well, whether it's by solidifying and reaffirming your fate, or by adding chaos or order to random chance in order to get better, or worse, odds. I point this out to demonstrate that all spheres can be used for effects of different aspects of the trinity, but that doesn't change that there is still one aspect which is inherent to their construction.

    The Esoteric Spheres

    - Prime is Static
    The very essence of magic itself. The thing you need to make magic easier, and certain spells possible. Again it's all about that spirit of creation and perfection. Prime is needed to conjure material out of nothing as well as to give objects enchantment. When something is infused with prime it becomes more "real" in a sense. This is the very nature of stasis as discussed above. In addition to this, Prime is the only sphere capable of making permanent enchantments upon objects, allowing them to be used without need of a focused will worker present. Prime codifies the effect into the very pattern of the object itself, rendering it fixed in place. If that isn't an example of stasis, then I don't know what to tell you.

    - Correspondence is Dynamic
    Space, by its very nature, seems like quite a static concept. But Correspondence is all about shattering this illusion of distance and displaying space and dimension as constructs used to help define the way we view the world, whereas the reality is that all spaces are one and all points can intersect. This is a very dynamic notion as it means that everything we've come to expect regarding consistency in the mundane world is actually an assumption at best. With this sphere, there is no guarantee that opening the door will lead to the outside of your house, or even to a single location. Co-location takes this to another level with places being on top of other places, and what more do you need to see how dynamic this sphere is?

    - Time is Entropic
    Going back to the 2nd law of thermodynamics where energies tend towards entropy. You can't tend towards anything without time. Time is often refereed to symbolically as the one thing no one person or thing can outlast. Time magic is one of the hardest of the spheres to pull off, and the ones that tends towards greater levels of paradox than the others, with additional for using it multiple times within a given scene. Additionally, Time has an effect on the more static spheres, providing them entorpy, Ideas and memories change and erode with time, Objects crumble and fall apart with time. So too doe the dynamic patterns as events like the Avatar Storm have demonstrated not even the umbra is immune to time's passage. All things tend towards entropy, and time is the river which pulls them there.

    What this means for you and your game.

    This is all just an idea, but one which seems to fit into place quite nicely. If you want to adopt this into your games, I have a number of thoughts on how it could work.

    - Resonance. Perhaps the spheres the players rely upon most will contribute to the kinda of resonances she starts to gather upon herself.
    - Focus (paradigm), It could be that certain paradigms lend themselves more towards a Dynamic, Entopic, or Static bent, and as such the characters will see these spheres as the "main" spheres and all others as being an extension of the main ones, or a lesser version.
    - Archmastery. Perhaps when a mage exceeds a certain level of understanding within a set of three spheres, they gain mastery over everything in that domain. Being an archmaster of the umbral spheres, for example, could allow this mage to transit freely between the various levels of umbra with uncanny understanding. Being a archmaster of the Dynamic spheres may allow the mage to control all facets of dynamic behaviour, even within spheres they are less proficient in.
    - Hermetic Lore. All of this could just be a theory, a set of ideas which serve as a plot hook for Hermetic investigation.

    I'd love to hear any thoughts or criticisms you have about these musings, as well as any other ideas of how this could be utilised in games.

          There was a discussion of this sort of thing for a bit in one of the "Alternate Spheres" thread. I imagine that you could categorize Patterns like so, in the same way that you can categorize Spheres that way.

          In effect, one could view "Pattern" being the operand to a "Sphere"'s operator; the noun to their verb. An {X} Pattern is the 'thing' that the {X} Sphere acts on.

          In that metaphor, Resonance and the Metaphysic Trinity might be seen as adverbs or adjectives; each Sphere can act Dynamically, Statically, and Primordially (to decouple it from 'Entropy' the Sphere, because lol White Wolf namespace collisions). Particular phenomena (Patterns to their respective Spheres) might hold Dynamic, Static, and Primordial traits.

          So, I'm in agreement (I think) with Nico that while the Spheres can somewhat be categorized, and I'd imagine many IC Hermetics have attempted to do just this thing - potentially with wildly different categorizations resulting - fundamentally each of the Spheres can be used to 'act' in line with any of the Trinity and any tendencies are probably more a matter of the individual Mage acting within the zone of influence.

          The Material/Umbral/Esoteric split is interesting, though, and I can definitely see that as a categorization a lot of Hermetics and other Sphere-theorists taking. Esoteric could potentially also be described as Relational.

                                                  I see you concerns. And yes, I agree that all spheres are capable of producing effects reflecting all three of aspects of the Trinity. However, to say that all spheres have equal placement in the trinity, I think is somewhat limiting. (Though I'm sure an Ecstatic would probably argue that it's all pretty irrelevant, and as long as you can feel it out, then who cares?)

                                                  For example. the sphere of Entropy. It's well known that this sphere is capable of a great number of different effects revolving around fate and chance.
                                                  Dynamic effects can include changing your fate, altering the chance of one card coming up instead of another card, purting another person in your destiny in place of yourself
                                                  Static effects can include strengthening your fate, ensuring that machines are likely to function in perfect order, minimising the role that random chance has on changing the outcome.
                                                  Entropic effects can include removing your fate, inserting chaos into processes and machinery, causing things to sponteniously tare themselves apart, increase and amplify the natural ware and tare.

                                                  However, while all these aspects can be enforced through specific use of the Entropy sphere, the Sphere itself as its name suggests will tend towards Entropy as a default. This is what I am trying to identify in regards to the Spheres and their relationship with the Trinity. As mages are capable of changing the very properties by which these patterns operate, to only look at the way magic can influence them is to only see how these patterns behave in the hands of a will worker, and thus are inseparable from the subjective.

                                                  When talking about the Material spheres, we are talking about the things which make up the very world we live in and can interact with directly and mundanely. As such, their default must be judged based on what they are in mundane reality. In this regard, we see unchanging matter, constantly changing life, and every flowing Forces. Forces being entropic as they tend towards the largest entropy whenever they can. Matter patterns tend to only change when Life or Force patterns act upon them. It could be argued that Life relies on Forces and Matter patterns in order to be dynamic, and this is true. Which is why you can see examples of Static and Entropic entities within Life patterns. This is why Life patterns eventually die and become Matter patterns for good. The entropy is such that they are stripped of their Dynamic influence and settle down into Stasis.

                                                  So yes, while the spheres of Life, Forces, and Matter can be used to create effects of any kind within the trinity, the patterns themselves, and by extension the spheres that work on them, will inherently default towards one particular area within the trinity.

                                                  When talking about the Umbral spheres, we are considering elements which are one space removed from our purview. They exist in a realm outside that of which we can interact with, yet one we are all completely aware off. Their default must then be determined based on the behaviour of these patterns within umbral space and the intent behind their fluctuations. The High umbra is to the Middle umbra as Matter is to Life. The High umbra is made from the stuff of the Middle Umbra, with the legends, ideas, myths, and sensations that make up the spirit world eventually making their way into the psyche of the collective consciousness of humanity, and thus represented in a more consistent from within the high umbra. While within the Middle Umbra, spirits are able to exist on their own terms without as much regard to human influence. They run by their own rules set by Gaia, the Garou, The Totem spirits, the Weaver, Wyld, and Wyrm. And while, just like the Life pattern, these spirits and spirits worlds contain elements of all three aspects (especially where the three Ws are concerned). That which becomes codified by human consciousness and the zeitgeist "ascends" if you will, to the High umbra, where it remains as a more immutable concept. Legends which have no agreed upon origin and subsist in a cloud of interpretation, such as King Arthur, remain in the spirit world, in constant flux with no real identity of its own outside of what the viewer gives them at the time. The Lower Umbra then is not unlike the Force pattern. Biting away at the Middle Umbra, pulling things slowly but surely towards oblivion. This is where the lost ideas go, the passions that have faded with time, and where dead ideas go to rest. Just as the High Umbra shows entropy by having old ideas taken down and replaced by new ideas, so too does the Lower Umbra have great landscapes built out of the souls of the unfortunate, such as Stygia. However, the intent of this creation and destruction is completely different, and demonstrates the defaults of these two areas. The High umbra tares down in order to improve, increase the number of eppiphamies present, and collate all of human understanding into a great seamless vista. The Lower Umbra, by contrasts, builds only to delay the inevitable. These once human souls build in a desperate attempt to cling on to their humanity, they are the ones who cannot bare the fact that entropy is all that awaits them. They build to avoid oblivion, to stave of entropy.

                                                  In this way, Just as with Life, Forces and Matter, even though it is possible to influence their aspects, Spirit, Entropy, and Mind have their own defaults inherent within the purview of their patterns.

                                                  Lastly, in order to talk about the Esoteric spheres, we must first recognise just how outside of our grasp these patterns are. They are all around us, and govern the way we understand the world we live in. However, it is all an illusion. How we as sleepers perceive reality has nothing to do with how these patterns work. It is therefor important to disregard how we experience these forces in mundane life, and instead focus on what the measurements and manipulation of these patterns via the spheres informs us about the patterns themselves.

                                                  The first thing to realise is that Prime is everything and lies everywhere. Every material object we encounter is a form of prime. Every part of the umbra is undertoned by prime. So in the same away that Topological Mathematicians can't tell the difference between a coffee cup and a doughnut, there is no real difference between substances within the material world and the umbral world as all of it is prime. This is probably one of the reasons why Prime magic is one of the few magics to work consistently well in any and all environments, be they umbral or not. The only time Prime magic fails is in areas like Null Zones, in which there is no Primal Energy to interact with. So any form of manipulation via Prime magic doesn't actually change anything. If you pull quintessence out of a node or into your body, the net gain or loss of Quintessence within the system is exactly the same. In reality, matter cannot be created or destroyed. In Mage, matter can only be destroyed if it is turned into prime. This demonstrates that Matter is simply a different permutation of Prime, doing prime magic only changes how it interacts with other objects and entities. This is why Prime is static. Because it fundamentally doesn't change no matter what you do to it. You can alter how it interacts with other things, but it's still there, it's still Quintessence, and it never increases or decreases. It remains unchanging.

                                                  The next thing to consider is Corr. and how we have no real concept of it. We as sleepers witness a world with dimensions. what Corr. demonstrates is that such a world is a fallacy, created by humans trying to make sense of the world we see around us in the most intuitive way. Reality is not so intuitive. Examples of Corr. patterns being dynamic can be seen in the fact that all spaces are actually connected. This is why quantum entangling works, this is why wormholes are a thing, and this is why it is possible to teleport from one part of the world to the other without even needing to look at it. Everything is connected. However, we can say that there is a consistent area of space, and while the connections exist and are very much a part of mundane space, there is still a huge amount of stability within our understanding of space. This leads one to believe that there is a large static element within Corr. patterns, and that perhaps the exploitation of the interconnections of space is actually the introduction of entropic elements this static space. This then described a Corr. pattern as being not unlike Spirit or Life patterns as previously discussed. Dynamic at its core with areas of Stasis and Entropy within them. Space and time have long been associated with one another. So perhaps the eventual break down of Corr. patterns is what eventually leads to Time patterns, leaving behind only static shell of Prime.

                                                  Speaking of Time. Just like with Corr., we must not limit ourselves with what we experience of Time itself. It feels consistent, it feel static. But when you look at time in terms of Physics, then things get a lot more shaky. In real life physics, linear time itself doesn't actually factor into anything. There is no one equation that demonstrates time as a linear progression, and indeed physics doesn't actually care whether an event happened earlier or later than it, only that the events are linked. The only thing in physics which recognises a linearity in time is the second law of thermodynamics, by which we can measure the gradual increase of entropy and see a linear progression. However, the moment peak entropy has been ascertained, physic's ability to measure time stops. This demonstrates the most fundamental concept in dealing with Time. It only works the way we understand it because of Entropy. There is only a past because the past has less entropy than the present. And the Future presumably has more entropy, if it even exists at all. As postulated above, Time could be to Corr. as Forces is to Life. and that when Corr. patterns return to Static Prime patterns, what is given off is Time patterns, and this is what we experience when we feel time, and it is this process of space eroding that we are manipulating when we manipulate Time. Alternatively, it could be the actions of entropy itself, the fact that instability brings about change, and that change is the very essence of time.

                                                  By this argument, we see Prime, Corr. and Time to have their very essence of being defined by the trinity aspects of Stasis, Dynamism, and Entropy respectively, and may be the most extreme representations of their respective aspect.



                                                  Regarding Essence. I kinda though of it as each essence lying at the cardinal points of a compass.

                                                  Pattern is at the West position, and Dynamism lies at the East. Which one is at East of West is kinda arbitrary. The point is that they lie at diametric opposites from one another, with one Essence seeks to form consistency, full understanding, and bring about order; and the other strives for fluctuation, constant change and the disregard for patterns if they emerge. These essences will push their respective mage in opposite directions.

                                                  Primordial is at the South position and Questing is at north. Again, this is fairly arbitrary placement. There point here is that Questing lies in front of the mage, and Primordial lies behind the mage. The Primordial essence is all about hearkening back to previous events, demonstrating how history repeats itself, and in general pushing their mage forward from behind.
                                                  Questing, on the other hand, does the opposite. They dangle a carrot in front of their mage, forcing them to focus on the Future and the possibility of success and the potential for avoiding failure. The pull their mage forward from the front.

                                                  Quintessence, then, lies in the very centre of the compass, it neither pushes, pulls, wants or denies, it simply is. Every mages can feel quintessence, and every mage may force this strange property to fuel their intent.

                                                  In how this fits in to the Metaphysical Trinity, I tend to imagine an upside-down triangle superimposed on the compass. Entropy points towards the Primordial. While Dynamic and Stasis point between Questing essence, and the Dynamic and Pattern Essence respectively. Each point is closer to Quintessence than it is to any of the other essences, and thus all mages have access to them.
