                                                          1. No. When a Fallen possesses a body, they either expel or repress the soul already there. Any powers a Mage may have had disappear with their soul. That said, such a host is capable of having much more powerful Fallen than your average joe, due to their Faith Potential, which is higher than any other humans. For this reason, mages are sought out as hosts or thralls by powerful Fallen.

                                                          2. No, Fallen don't immediately go bad if they somehow become Earthbound. But without the mediating influence of human feelings/memories, it is hard not to be overwhelmed by Torment. Generally though, they don't become Earthbound at all. If the host body of a Fallen dies, they can seek out the nearest avaliable host and possess that. Only if the Fallen somehow become too powerful to inhabit human bodies, will they have to bind themselves to something else and thus become Earthbound.
