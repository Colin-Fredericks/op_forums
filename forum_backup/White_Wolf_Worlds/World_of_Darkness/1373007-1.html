The whole point it was made was when a Lasombra(?) Kindred got so inspired by Muhammad's preaching during his prophethood (yes the Sect founder was present when Muhammad was active), that he formed the sect to carry Islam into in unlife as a Vampire as a Road. It's safe to assume that all Ashirra don't go by Humanity in the modern nights at all, but the Islamic Road (forgot the specific name of it). Instead of wiping out more interesting Sects (like what was done with the Sabbat when they all decided to "go to the Middle-East" all at once), conflict between the Islamic Ashirra and the formerly Christian-now-mostly-Secular Camarilla should be explored. Add more, not remove.

                                                            To be honest, i treat the Ashirra as just one among several regional inter-court alliances of different reach one can find under the umbrella of the Camarilla (or not) in kindred domains all across the world. "Being in UE or OAS doesn't stop you from belonging to NATO", so to speak. In fact the Ashirra as i use them are more associated with the Arabian Peninsula proper - closer to their initial portrayal in WoD 2e, in fact - than the rest of the "Arab world".

                                                            A form of the Laibon definitely exist as some sort of cultural group among the kindred, mostly in sub-saharan Africa though ancillae, elders and even a few princes that count themselves among them might be found in parts of Brazil, the Caribbean, and curiously, Goa and Queensland, Australia.

                                                            That said my take of them is a somewhat inchoate mish-mash of the lineages presented in KotEK, BJD and DAV20 with some changes & twists peppered in. Main reason i have not polished them as much as i'd like is that i'm also retooling the Wan Kuei from KotE (again) and some of the concepts i'm working from (relating to cyclical Gehemna and Thin-Blooded, of all things) will probably be relevant in the African front too.

                                                            They are all kindred so to speak, but at the same time something different and weird to your typical lick coming from a Camarilla-associated city. Though not just the Laibon, Wan Kuei or Gaki, as some "Drowned Legacies" of the Americas are more equal than others and Australia has some weirdness of its own in places, for some examples.​
