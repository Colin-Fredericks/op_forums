    The chronological start of the metaplot in the 'modern' nights?

    11-04-2016, 05:18 AM
    A mouthful of a title I know. My group has some players who have experienced WoD before and some who haven't, but mostly of course Vampire. What I want to do is a couple of years of chronicles that all roughly follow the metaplot, or have it occurring in the background, and see if my group can come up with a creative way of averting the End. I've decided to start (most of them) out as Hunters, as not all of them feel comfortable playing supernatural creatures, and not all would want to play the same ones. So far I have a werebear (who won't have come into his heritage yet, for handwavey reasons) and a fresh mage. However, I fully expect the mage to be a drop in character, and will be building the game around two main-stay players who can generally attend every session.

    What I could use help with is knowing where the metaplot kicks in for the 'modern' nights. It'll likely be a vampire book, I imagine, but anything big I can at least craft my own adventures with knowledge of. Starting early also gives me time to consume more and more of the background.

    I don't imagine that a great many of the early events would be anything a few hunters, a mage, and a skinchanger would be aware of. But what ones did have an impact around the world, even if they were covered up?

    I'm not new to the setting, but I've never tried to look at it as a whole before, and there is so much to sift through. The first story I run will be establishing the group, likely against a group of ghoul gang members controlled by some low level Sabbat thug or another. It should last a few months at least, and give me time to build a larger narrative.

          The issue at least to me is that the metaplot was generally given in bits and pieces in other supplements; an NPC's background might mention a relevant point or it might have a sidebar, rarely you will get a scenario(s) based around a metaplot event (like the stories in Nights of Prophecy, Giovanni Chronicles, or the Transylvania Chronicles). There currently is no big book of metaplot, the closest thing will be when Beckett's Jyhad Diary for vampire when that comes out. If you are just looking for a list of events the best thing I can point you to is the CWoD Timeline on the White Wolf Wiki.

                  You could look at (some of) the books that Beckett's Jyhad Diary specifically refers to:

                  Chicago Chronicles
                  Giovanni Chronicles
                  Transylvanian Chronicles
                  The Time of Thin Blood
                  Nights of Prophecy

                  The thing is, though, it also weaves in the by Night city books (Jerusalem by Night, Montreal by Night, LA by Night, San Francisco by Night, New York by Night, Cairo by Night), plus tidbits from just about every part of VTM.

                  If you can only afford one book, start with the events of Nights of Prophecy and fill in the gaps with the cWoD timeline above. There are enough threads to begin with, and most of them directly follow on from the events of the Reckoning. It's also the closest thing to a metaplot adventure guide currently in print (until Beckett's Jyhad Diary).

                      Some of the bigger events I can think of in the 90s to early 00s while going over the timeline (note most of these are Vampire and Mage related as I know those settings best and it has events related to the End Times which may be undesirable for games going past the events in Gehenna, Apocalypse, Ascension, and Time of Judgement). This list is by no means exhaustive in any way shape or form.
                      • 1990's
                        • Baba Yaga awakens and along with her allies creates the Shadow Curtain an effect that blocks umbral travel, blocks communications, and prevents supernatural entities from leaving Russia. Over the course of the decade she will engage in a prolonged conflict with the Garou of Russia.
                        • Hermetic Mage Mark Hallward Gillian discovers the corruption of the Euthanatos of House Helekar under the Archmage Voormas and eventually after being exiled in a cover up manages to expose the evil to the Euthanatos leadership forcing them to act as well as forcing the other Traditions to accept the Euthanatos' darker practices.
                      • 1991
                        • The Methuselah Helena awakens from torpor and begins searching for the haven of her ancient rival Menele.
                      • 1993
                        • The battle between the Werewolves and Vampires of Chicago (caused by an alliance of the Sabbat and Black Spiral Dancers turning them against each other). An attack on the Succubus Club by lupines leads to Prince Lodin declaring a blood hunt against all lupins. The resulting Chicago War destroys a third of the vampires in the city including Prince Lodin as well as killing the Methuselah Helena's ghouls including Priam and allowing the Black Spirals to conquer the Garou caern of the city. In the aftermath Helena uses the power vacuum to take a greater hand in the city's affairs.
                      • 1995
                        • The Fors Collegis Mercuris chantry on Mus (the horizon realm of Mercury) falls to Nephandi forces after a Technocratic attack leaves the Tradition defenders weakened.
                      • 1997
                        • The Horizon Chantry is attacked and half the council of nine along with a number of Tradition archmages and leadership are killed.
                        • Doissetep explodes destroying the ancient Hermetic Chantry and causing a massive shockwave in the Digital Web leading to the Great Crash where the entire web goes offline for three seconds.
                        • The majority of Camarilla Malkavians regain the Dementation Discipline leaving the Dominate Malkavians a small bloodline.
                      • 1998
                        • (or 1999) Xaviar the Justicar of Clan Gangrel encounters an entity he believes to be an Antediluvian. The Inner Circle denies this claim and with one sentence Xaviar takes the bulk of the Gangrel and leaves the Camarilla.
                        • Baba Yaga is destroyed and the Shadow Curtain drops.
                        • The loss of the Gangrel snaps the Camarilla out of its complacency
                          • Princes, primogen, and elders begin speaking for greater participation for other clans/bloodlines.
                          • The Inner Circle officially blesses the SchreckNET - Justicars, Archons, and accredited Princes, Sheriffs, Scourges all begin connecting and sharing information over it.
                        • Tremere having lost the battle for his body to Saulot has Etrius deliver him to Goratrix whose body he steals and enacts a ritual which destroys all the Tremere Antitribu.
                        • The Kuei-jin begin invading California destroying many independent Cainites before resistance coalesced.
                      • 1999
                        • Machinations of ancient vampires (possible the Nosferatu antediluvian and or Saulot) nearly release an arsenal of Romanian nuclear weapons, the demon Kupala uses this chance to successfully free itself from Zelios geomantic web.
                        • Ur-Shulgi awakens and slays the Caliph of the Assamites. He breaks the Tremere blood curse preventing Assamites from drinking Cainite blood and reinstates the old form of the Path of Blood. Those Assamites who do not loyally follow this Path, especially those who follow Islam are viewed as corrupt by Ur-Shulgi. This leads to Tegyrius and Al-Ashrad leading many Assamites in a Schism which could lead to them joining the Camarilla and gaining full clan status along with a Justicar.
                        • The Kuei-jin consolidate their gains and consider their losses eventually bringing the Cainites under their rule and the New Promise Mandarinate is formed.
                        • The Sabbat attack and claim Atlanta which later leads to a string of cities falling along the east coast.
                          • Washington DC is attacked by the Sabbat. The Camarilla discovered that the Prince Marcus Vitel was a powerful Lasombra double agent and was eventually dispatched by a small army of ghouls and noteworthy kindred (including Theo Bell, Jan Pieterzoon, and Christof Romuald). However the Sabbat had successfully claimed the city by the time they had beaten the Prince and Sascha Vykos was now Archbishop.
                        • Anthelios the Red Star appears in the Umbra and will later pass through the Gauntlet and appear in the night sky.
                        • Monday June 28 - Sunday July 4 The Week of Nightmares: The Ravnos Antediluvian Zapathasura awakens and rampages across India and Bangladesh summoning its childer. It is attacked by three Kuei-jin Bodhisattvas who summoned a supernatural storm to block the sun, which draws the attention of the Technocracy. The Technocracy implements Operation Ragnarok and detonates magical neutron bombs (which function on both a terrestrial and spiritual level) which slays the Antediluvian's opponents dispersing the storm allowing the Technocracy to use satellites to divert sunlight onto the area and finally destroy the Antediluvian. This triggers a wave of self-destructive diablerie in the clan leaving only a small number alive.
                          • Technocrat Xerxes Jones detonates a spirit nuke in the Labyrinth at the mouth of Oblivion.
                          • Stygia detonates a relic nuke on Enoch.
                          • The three previous events lead to the release of the Sixth Great Maelstrom which will eventually lead to the end of Stygia as wraiths know it. It causes the Avatar Storm which makes traveling to the Umbra lethally dangerous. It also leads to the cracking of the Abyss which allows Demons (the Fallen variety) to escape back to Earth. In response to these events the Messengers/Heralds begin imbuing Hunters.
                        • The Camarilla enacts a massive plan which eventually leads to a successful siege and reclamation of New York City from the Sabbat.
                      • 2000
                        • (2000-2001) The Second Massassa War between the Order of Hermes and the Tremere reveals the ghoul corruption of House Tytalus and destroys House Janissary.
                        • Umbral Realms and Chantries begin being cut off from reality leaving both the Technocracy and Traditions without access to some of their most powerful archmages and both scientific and mystical magick become more difficult.
                        • Melinda Galbraith the Regent of the Sabbat is slain by unknown parties and and was impersonated for a time by the Tzimisce Zachary Sikorsky, who discovered her remains (and was afraid of being framed by her murder).
                        • The Stargazers Tribe leave the Garou Nation and join the Beast Courts.
                      • 2001
                        • The New Tradition Council successfully defeats and traps famous Nephandi Jodi Blake, this allows them to unite the Traditions again under a much looser Council of Nine.
                        • The last paths leading to the Horizon Chantry, once the stronghold of the Traditions are rendered inpenetrable.
