    Revising the Nunnehi

    06-07-2021, 03:51 PM
    I’m trying to work on the Nunnehi to make them a more narratively integrated part of the Concordia setting and plot.

    1. Nunnehi are as tied to the Dreaming as the Kithain. As the kiths in the C20 Player’s Guide demonstrate, the Dreaming is global, even if the Trods from Nunnehi Freeholds lead to very different aspects of the Dreaming than the Kithain are familiar with.

    2. All Changeling character use these Favored Epiphany rules from the Parting the Mists blog (from the creators of the Walking Away from Arcadia podcast): https://drive.google.com/file/d/14el...E2A86nfY3/view
    More Nunnehi than Concordian Kithain favor the Epiphany of the Living Kingdom, but that’s not because the Nunnehi are uniquely animistic. It’s because the Concordian Kithain are a settler society that lack old oaths with the land they’re living on. Many European Kithain favor the Epiphany of the Living Kingdom, just like the Nunnehi. Plenty of Nunnehi practice the other Epiphanies as well.

    3. For the same reason, Nunnehi have much better relations with the Inanimae than the Concordian Kithain, and many of said Inanimae whose anchors are sacred locals or natural formations to the Nunnehi identify more closely with the Nunnehi Nations than the Empires. The same is true of many Inanimae in Europe and Kithain society there (such as the well known Nymphs of Greece). Inanimae being so alien to the Concordian Kithain is again a product of being a settler culture without a close connection to the land they’re living on.

    4. Nunnehi Revelries from the same source as the previous link: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Yrk...6TxWsXtln/view

    5. Nunnehi experienced the Dreaming reopening to them a bit earlier, but more slowly than the Interregnum Kithain did, but over an overlapping period of time. While the return of the Sidhe blasted Trods leading all the way to the Deep Dreaming open on July 20th, 1969, the Nunnehi has gradually rediscovered trods into the Near Dreaming over the previous several years and based on the dreams those Trods led to clearly connected the events to the rise of the American Indian Movement. As a result, the Nunnehi Freeholds were actually in a strong position when the Accordance War started.

    6. While some Nunnehi took one side or the other, most took defensive positions on their own Freeholds, or even used the conflict as an opportunity to strike the weakened Kithain to reclaim lost territory.

    7. Toward that end, replace the Sidhe Chief Greyhawk with an actually Nunnehi leader in the Southwest, who fought to gain important territory early in the conflict, but then having done so, served as a 3rd party arbiter for the Accordance. As a result all Nunnehi Freeholds may each send a representative to the Committee of the Commoners, and the “King” (a title the Nunnehi don’t use themselves, but was a legal necessity for political recognition) of the Kingdom of Burning Sun has a delegate who serves in the Arcadian Order (this is the influential swing vote between the Seelie and Unseelie Houses, not a Shadow Court delegate)

    8. Nunnehi generally find practicing Sovereign or Naming to be Banality triggers for them, being rooted in very feudal settler dreams. But they have at least one unique Art unknown to the Kithain - this is where I’d reinsert Middle Umbra access and dealing with spirits so it’s still there for those who want it.

              I think one of the critical mistakes of Changeling was to make the Kiths too specific in style, appearance, and personality, and position the fairy tales of non-European cultures as something requiring their own Kiths. (I think Changeling: the Lost did it better by having Seemings and then dividing them into Kiths to provide examples of the variety that fit that concept.) Almost every culture has stories of the "invisible people" whatever they call them - elves, fairies, jinn, nunnehi, or whatever. They're all the same even if the human-like leaders are called Sidhe by one culture, Elf by another, Jinn by a third and so on. Similarly for the household helpers, or those under the earth protecting its treasures, and so on. That Changeling now has something like 20 billion kiths is an indication of a deep intrinsic flaw in its design.

              Personally I would redo the Kiths to make them universal and eliminate some of the strange design choices the original creators made (trolls are blue and always honorable?). Then slot in whatever fairy trope of whatever culture fits those generic archetypes and reskin them as needed. If there's an archetype that Changeling missed originally, just create a new one (I know, easier said than done) to fill that gap. And remember not all fairy types will have Kiths, some will be various kind of Chimera.

              Even though I have never run or played Changeling, I've had to dwell in that world for a bit in order to make sense of some things for PCs in my Werewolf game who are in a pack of Stag. So I put down notes as the to the type of "dream" each Kith represents. Most of the Kiths are already directly defined well in terms of their dreams, but some adjustments are needed (no, Trolls aren't dreams of "honor and duty". Did the writers ever read any fairy tales with trolls in them? They would be dreams of strength and power. Sure, Seelie Trolls can add a component of honor if they want but that is in addition to their core "trollness". Let's not pretend any Unseelie Trolls would care at all about honor or duty.) Likewise Satyrs shouldn't be dreams of passion and freedom, but nature and animalism and should include any possible kind of human-animal hybrid like centaurs, selkies, or deer women. Nockers need to be dreams of the treasures of the earth since they're really supposed to be dwarves. Their ability to create magical items is derivative of their mastery of that. I still don't know what the hell an Eshu is. Give me a real fairy tale character, and I can probably assign it a court and kith (Rumplestiltskin is an Unseelie Boggan, the Seven Dwarfs are Seelie Knockers, the troll in the Three Billy Goats Gruff is an Unseelie Troll), but I can't think of anything that actually fits the Eshu as written. The best I can do is assign them the place of the "helper" trope in much fairy tales where the magical helper looks completely human, as opposed to some obvious non-human creature. You just can't grab the name from African culture and create a brand new fairy from it.

              But once you work out the real universal archetypes, and allow characters' appearances to reflect whatever their local depiction is, you got everything covered.

              And just chuck out the idea that the various children of dreaming of the various geographies are in some kind of competition with one another. Late nineties White Wolf had some poor ideas about multiculturalism that sucked all the wonder and mystery out of their games. I can't imagine anything more banal than the idea than European fairies attacking North American fairies in some sad sack replay of real history. The Dreaming should be large enough that every group of fae have their own lands, and they should get along with one another just as much as any other fairy does. When any fairies go to war, it should be for the same reasons they would do between their own groups, and local fairies of whatever origin should ally, betray, fight, and cooperate with any other group. It's fine for fae to fight over freeholds and trods or whatever, but don't make it a racial thing. In 10,000 BCE pretty much all fae around the world would be almost identical right? Why would they put so much emphasis on petty differences in couture and other banal things? They're magical beings. Fae can be cruel and treacherous, but they should act differently than humans.

              The European fae did come over with the European colonists because they had ties to them. And some of them did fight the native fae. And others allied to them. And different groups swore oaths to certain individuals on the other side because of a mixture of personalities, rivalries, or interests. So mix them however you need to locally. If you want everything to be European style in some modern American city, fine. And if you want the top boss to be some Nunnehi chief whose has local European sidhe types owing him fealty, and he has a motley collection of both kinds of fae as well as visiting Jinn from Arabia, that's great too. And some other place to be a stronghold of Nunnehi with few European type fae visiting them, that works too. The Dreaming is big, and STs should do whatever works for them and more importantly create a sense of wonder and enchantment.

              That would mean creating a new backstory for the game, but I think that's OK because there are many elements of the existing Changeling setting I don't think really works. The entire idea of the Shattering and Resurgence that lead the Sidhe to leave and then return is just a bad, bad idea that sabotages historical chronicles. The general decline of the fae should be for the same reason the fae are generally depicted as no longer much active in most modern stories - they are a dying people because mankind has taken over the world, pushing them back to the margins and their old, powerful magics no longer work as they once did. That kind of retreat as magic is dying would both compel different fae to join together for mutual protection as well as fight with each other for diminishing resources.

              I think a lot of barebone mechanics in Changeling work fine, but they were applied badly. But I think you can make lots of changes to the setting that ditches the ill-thought out ways White Wolf handled the setting, create something much better, yet be true to its spirit and retain the things that do work.

                      For what it's worth, glamourweaver suggested that the Nunnehi aren't inherently tied to the Middle Umbra instead of the Dreaming; the proposal is that they're tied to the Dreaming just like regular Changelings, but that they might have knowledge of an Art that lets them interact with the Middle Umbra, for those gamers who like the Middle Umbra connection. That would also be useful for the Hsien, as they also currently are described as being connected to the Umbra but might be better off recast as tied to the Dreaming but with an Art or two connecting them to the appropriate Umbrae.
