        Originally posted by Aya Tari View Post
        If people like bigotry, racism, sexism, etc, then they want Subjective Reality. If people do not, then they want Objective Reality. People can like Subjective Reality without being hateful, but it is usually because they do not really think through the consequences of Subjective Reality for anyone but their character..
        I think you're over-simplifying the idea.

        Let's take, as an example, some hypothetical american town. The majority of the inhabitants of this town are white and they're racist. They believe that black and latinx people are inferior: poorly educated, criminals, et cetera.

        And what happens in this town is that black and latinx people are denied promotions or better paying jobs. Most end up in poorer neighborhoods, where the schools have next to no funding and incredibly outdated materials. The police profile black and latinx people and subject them to greater scrutiny. When they're arrested for crimes, they're not offered the same leniency that their white counterparts have. (That's not even getting into economic desperation as a motivation to commit a crime. Or the lack of accessibility to proper mental health treatment. And so on and so forth.)

        So what do you end up with? A town where many of the black and latinx people are poorly educated, and are more likely to have criminal records.

        And there you have it: an example of subjective reality. The black and latinx people of the town aren't inferior, of course. But because of things like institutionalized privilege and systemic racism, many of them do have those qualities that the white majority deems as "inferior". If they lived somewhere else with better access to education, where racial profiling is discouraged, where the Judges and District Attorneys are more willing to treat people on an equal basis. Where these people aren't denied for jobs or promotions, and have access to mental health treatment and so on and so forth. Then these people wouldn't be "poorly educated" or "criminals."

        Ultimately, that's an example of a Reality Zone. It's not about somehow magically making the Curse of Ham real, it's about people, consciously or unconsciously, creating a system and environment that reinforces their own beliefs.

        Subjective Reality isn't however always a bad thing, though. Because when Reality is subjective, it can also be changed: Systems can be dismantled. Bigotry can be overcome. Access can be improved and insured. It's not remotely easy, but it can happen.

        This is why Mage practices include things like "dominion" and "art of desire", because not everything that influences reality requires an arete roll.

                            Originally posted by Ramnesis View Post

                            The proponents of Mage having a Subjective Reality, Aya.
                            Speak for yourself. Or are you “No True Scotsman”ing me, in that I'm not really a fan of Ascension because I have my doubts about purely subjective reality?

                            My own view on subjective reality is a bit like the old joke about statistics: 97% of all statistics, including this one, are totally worthless. In a similar way, there's no such thing as 100% subjective reality; because if everything is subjective, so is the statement that everything's subjective.

                            This isn't just word-play: in order for the Technocracy not to be objectively wrong, there needs to be the possibility that they might be at least somewhat right. Either way, there's an objective truth here. As such, my take on it is that subjective reality isn't an objective fact; it's what the Traditions believe. It may or may not be true★; there's no way of knowing for certain. What is certain is that it's a useful belief, because it fosters the Traditions' diverse and cooperative culture. So I have no problem with the Traditions pushing this notion, whether or not it's ultimately true.

                            ★ For some value of true.

                            Originally posted by Ramnesis View Post
                            These are the Mage forums. We're in a thread talking about the metaphysics of Mage. I shouldn't have to spell out that we're not discussing the nature of the real world.
                            Real-world metaphysics inform Mage metaphysics. The former are a valid topic as long as you keep that in mind.

                            My go-to example is Climate Change: if reality really is determined by the Consensus, then a valid strategy for combatting Climate Change would be to convince the Masses that it doesn't exist. Perversely, those who warn of the danger and convince people that it's a real problem would be the ones responsible for it. So it can't be as simple as “reality is whatever you believe it is”: belief, and will, needs to be a factor (because the Traditions can't be objectively wrong); but that can't be all there is to it.

                            I do like Aleph's notion of a temporal counterpart to the Correspondence Point, something that exists outside of Time and defines the rules of time without being subject to them. I'm not necessarily going to say that whatever it is that imposes its Will on Time is human. That's certainly a possibility; but in keeping with my preference for subjective reality only being subjectively true, I'm not in favor of nailing down the identity of whatever inhabits Eternity.

                            My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
