                There's always the possibility of reconciliation. Just look at what happened with New Coke and Coca-Cola Classic back in the 80s and 90s. Coke tried to come up with something new to gain market share. It brought in new buyers, but alienated older ones, causing a loss in market share anyway. Coke brought the original version back as Classic, and ultimately would fold New Coke, but not before reconfiguring Classic a little since then. The same could happen with the WOD. There is a way to bring about many of the changes to W5 without doing away with the previous meta plot as well as keeping many of the original mechanics so many of the older players loved. I've been trying to work on it ever since the first hints of W5 came out last year. There is always a chance something like that could happen here, especially if the IP changes hands again. In fact, I've been working on a whole analogy to the cola wars of the time to how the handling of the WOD IP is today. There are quite a few parallels once I started a deep dive into it, but I don't know if anyone here is ready for a lengthy discussion of that.

                                                            I for one am puzzled by all these absurdly solid IPs getting mostly bad entries. No universe has ever engrossed me so completely as WoD has, conveying simple universal truths through monstrous exaggerations. Between WoD and whatever the fuck is going on with DC and Star Wars gets me deeply conspiratorial. Like how could they possibly go so awry? Are they doing it on purpose for some political reason? Has Star Wars been made a leftist caricature to push people to the right? Is WoD pulling it's punches because it used to be such an amazing satirical engine that targeted real problems? Is Henry Cavill cursed?

                                                            With things like Bethesda Game Studios failing to live up to TES and Fallout I can at least justify it with video games being extremely complicated and difficult to make and the lore taking a back seat for gameplay and spectacle seems to at least be a financially viable tactic, even if I don't agree with it. TV and movies cost a lot of money and producers are a difficult demon to keep at bay so it's understandable that things can get messy. But with WoD like... it's a fucking book. The risk of such a book is minimal, especially given us young folk like digital copies, so that kind of studio meddling should be unwarranted. Put me on the keyboard and give me enough wages to convince the wife I'm doing one of those so-called "real" jobs, grab me someone to draw the pictures and arrange pages and we'll shit something out much better than what we're currently getting. I'm not especially competent; I just recognize the bar is low. I'd trust half the regulars on this forum to write an improvement.
