    Not sure how you can use the trans panic justification on someone who advertises that they are trans. Sort of incompatible. And I don't know about "regularly used to justify murder." 19 trans people were killed or found dead from foul play in the U.S. in 2015. 15,696 people were murdered in the U.S. total in 2015. USDJ says that approximately 52% of violent crimes go unreported, so we would expect 22 trans murders to actually be reported, which is a little above where the figure is actually at. Seems like trans people get murdered about as much as people who aren't trans. And trans/gay panic aren't, ya'know, legitimate defenses in court. Not for lack of trying, though. Trans panic has never been a successful court defense, and gay panic got charges dropped against 1 person in the 60s. Even the guy who got his conviction dropped from 1st to 2nd degree murder is serving 25-50 years.

        That's the problem. WWP publicly states that they think Avery is an actual positive representation of trans people. That's... not good for our impressions of them.

WWP is responsible for a piece of fiction that they claim is a positive representation of trans people... with a character that represents a vile trans panic stereotype. The fact that she's open about her gender identity to other vampires really doesn't change anything regarding how her feeding strategy is described.

        And I don't know about "regularly used to justify murder."
        How people justify persecution isn't the same as actual individual instances of it. How many trans people are murdered isn't the point. It's that trans panic is an ingrained social belief in a section of the population.
