                                              I don't think this is a question of authority- outwardly, both demons and the Wyrm evoke Lovecraftian imagery, but they are critical irreconcilable differences in theme and scope. The struggle of DtF is not the same as the war the Garou are waging in WtA. The pseudo-Judeo-Christian Fall background of Demon does not really mesh with the feral animism of Werewolf's Triat, at all. The Wyrm, the Wyld and the Weaver are not so much beings as they are spiritual underpinnings of the universe. They are vast entities so potent they create gods on accident. Kind of hard to get that to jive with Lucifer and the Fallen... and Saur Ops Specialist is right about humanity. In Werewolf, humans are a necessary nuisance at best, and the rapists murderers of the planet at worst. Demon is about human (and Fallen) worth and potential.

                                              Honestly, the easiest way to reconcile the two in a given game is just make one side largely correct, cosmologically speaking, and stick with it. All the other werewolves, vampires and will-workers are, by extension, deluded or mad.
