                      Here are the character portraits for the cabal in my current Mage game. These are all by my buddy Aernout - find his Deviant Art page here. The pics are slightly smaller than the originals, but you should be able to make out the cool details he added.

                      First up is Alastair. He's a Euthanatos shadow operative. He uses sensory deprivation as one of his main foci - you can see his blacked-out goggles and earplugs for listening to white noise in the pic:





                      Next up is Gideon. He's a rather paranoid Son of Ether, who often uses a mobile hologram to interact with the world (that's him at the back, projecting the hologram through some weird science stuff). Gideon has a permanent Paradox Flaw from messing with Time too much - any woven fabric he touches unravels. That's why he's wearing all that kinky leather:





                      Next we have Kailani. She's a Dreamspeaker who isn't afraid to shoot you in the face if you cross her. Her avatar is a many-armed dancing goddess - you might be able to make it out on her t-shirt:
