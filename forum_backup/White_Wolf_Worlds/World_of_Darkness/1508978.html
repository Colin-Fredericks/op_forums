    Something to the SoE

    04-19-2023, 11:06 PM
    It's just a few concepts I thought of seeing how the SoE operates, so I wanted a second opinion to see if I'm getting this right since I don't have much experience with it myself.

    Portable railgun: Basically a weapon composed of a rifle-like cannon, powered by an ether battery on its back and capable of launching large electrical discharges, it would need Prime 2 to generate electricity and Forces from 2 to 4 depending on how much power you use.

    Rocket Backpack: As the name implies, a rocket pack that allows its wielder to fly or at least leave the ground for a while, Prime 2 to create flames and Forces 3 or 4 to control speed.

    Restorative mixture: Basically a chemical formula that would allow anyone to recover from injury. As it involves chemical compounds, Matter 2, and since it involves living beings as well, Life 2 to 4 depending on the potency of the mixture.

    Ether Capsule: Nothing complex actually, using Prime 2 inserts quintessence into a capsule that can be opened to recharge the mage quickly, of course it must be prepared in advance, not sure if it also needed Matter to function.​

      While none of these are entirely off-base, there's cleaner ways to handle most of them.

      Rail-guns: Rail-guns don't shoot electric blasts, so you might just need to rename this. A rail-gun, as the name implies, propels an armature down rails, and as the armature stops at the end of the rails, the projectile continues on having gained momentum from sitting in the armature. There are also coil-guns where the electromagnetic field used to accelerate is also used to suspend the projectile; negating the need for an armature or physical rails. These are real things we already have. The most important tool a SoE has to making a functional rail-gun weapon, is Matter. The biggest issue in rail-gun technology in multiple applications is the force of the magnetic acceleration is so strong, rail-guns have to be huge just to avoid ripping themselves to pieces.

      So while some Forces would help make a rail-gun more practical, what you want to start with is some Ether science to cook up some exotic materials via Matter so the actually body of the weapon is light enough to use, and strong enough to be fired more than once. After solving that current tech can handle the rest enough to make a weapon.

      Now, a Scientist! might want to go above and beyond this into creating a plasma rail-gun, that shoots some form of plasma bolt instead of a solid slug. Then you're going to need Forces and Prime to generate your ammo because we have no idea how to do that at human-weapon scales.

      Rocket-packs: So, again, you don't need Prime, because we have rockets (though really you're probably going to want to use jet-packs in how they actually operate). Again, Matter is your friend because one of the reasons these aren't "real" yet is the material engineering side of things more than the physics side of things. One of the biggest problems with making a jet-pack a reality, is carrying enough fuel, so using Matter to vastly increase how much fuel your pack holds without increase weight is going to get you a functional pack better than constantly tried to use Forces and Prime to generate thrust. Matter also helps you make a flight suit that will keep your legs from burning off.

      Restorative mixtures: Now you need Prime, because you're just making a "healing potion" where you're making a Charm via Prime that creates a single use Life 2 self-healing effect for the mage that activates it.

      Ether Capsule: If you're just turning a physical item into Tass, you generally don't need Matter as well.

              It's just a few concepts I thought of seeing how the SoE operates, so I wanted a second opinion to see if I'm getting this right since I don't have much experience with it myself.

              Restorative mixture: Basically a chemical formula that would allow anyone to recover from injury. As it involves chemical compounds, Matter 2, and since it involves living beings as well, Life 2 to 4 depending on the potency of the mixture.

              Ether Capsule: Nothing complex actually, using Prime 2 inserts quintessence into a capsule that can be opened to recharge the mage quickly, of course it must be prepared in advance, not sure if it also needed Matter to function.​
              You are running into a common hazard in that you are mixing up the foci and the effect. You are adding how the foci works to how the Mage is altering the world with it. It's not wrong to do that, per se, but there's no game reason to do it and it arbitrarily penalizes some character concepts over others. It might be worth getting into for the big magic rituals, but not for day to day magic.

              In general, ignore how the casting works and limit your calculations to the effect the mage is pushing on himself or the world. So, assuming all of these are foci for the Etherite to cast with and not Devices or Trinkets:

              The railgun is just manipulating the kinetic energy of the slug, That's really a Forces 2 effect. You could argue that the slug starts with no kinetic energy so you need Forces 3/Prime 2, but I wouldn't because we don't argue that way for other kinetic applications.

              The rocket backpack is flight. That's a basic Forces 2 (for human sizes) for manipulating kinetic energy locked to either the backpack itself or the wearer. Forces 2/Matter 2 or Forces 2/Life 2.

              The restorative mixture is purely Life, though what level of life depends on the degree of change and if the Mage is altering himself or others. (if it is a trinket you have to have Prime 2)

              The Ether Capsule is not a foci at all but, like Heavy Arms said, is refined Tass. Prime 4 when the Etherite creates it.

                                                  On locking an effect:

                                                  It's not difficult to cast a spell that stays in the area it was cast, that's natural. We also don't need to pay anything extra if the effects of the spell can move out of the area. Turn someone into a frog and the effects will continue for the duration even if they run away.

                                                  Sometimes, though, you want the spell itself to travel with something or someone. In that case you need to lock it to a pattern using one of the Pattern Spheres (Forces, Life, Matter, and sometimes Spirit or Mind). If your spell already uses that sphere (say, a Life effect that follows someone around) great, you are covered. If not you have to add another sphere.

                                                  So consider a spell that turns a shoe into chalk. That's Matter 2. The effect travels with the shoe, but not the spell itself. No locking is required.

                                                  If instead you want to make every solid object that passes through an area into chalk, that's still Matter 2. The spell isn't moving, no locking is required.

                                                  Now if you want to instead cast a spell that makes every solid object a person touches turn into chalk. That needs to be locked to him, so it is Matter 2/Life 2. Life is acting as the locking sphere.

                                                  If instead you cast a spell so that his shoes turn every solid object they touch into chalk, that's back to Matter 2. You are arguably locking the spell to his shoes but you are already using Matter 2 so it doesn't matter.

                                                  Your jetpack effect needs to travel with the pilot to be effective and the base Forces spell doesn't cover that, so you need a locking Sphere.

                                                          Originally posted by Psicose View Post

                                                          In that case, isn't correspondence used instead of Life?
                                                          The idea is that you are anchoring the spell to an object or person or other pattern, so you have to use the Sphere that covers that pattern. In this case the spell is anchored to a person so the anchor sphere is Life.

                                                          Strictly speaking you can't use Correspondence as a locking Sphere because it isn't a pattern Sphere. Even if you could, though, Correspondence is the sphere of locations and distance, so it would only let you lock to a place. To make the Chalk Touch or Jetpack work you need to anchor to a person or thing. That's Life or Matter.
