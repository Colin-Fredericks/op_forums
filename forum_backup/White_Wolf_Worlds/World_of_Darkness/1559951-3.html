                    To add to what others have said, there's a big thing in-setting about the Curse of Caine being divine and therefore not breakable by ordinary means (IIRC, ST Handbook Revised says you need to score more successes than God to break the curse). This is reinforced by The Red Sign.

                    There's a suggestion in one of the Gehenna scenarios that Caine may have cursed himself, but there's also the fact any harm visited on Caine is dealt seven times over to anyone else (which is not a curse any of the texts attribute to angels). So even if we assume the curses of sunlight, sustenance, and fire are angelic rather than purely divine, the original Mark of Caine is still supposed to be from God.

                    Though, I generally like the suggestion that the weakening of the blood only occurred after the Third Generation. But for the reasons already stated, that doesn't mean the Second and Third Generations are at all equal to Caine in accomplishment—only in potential. The fact remains that he has had longer at this than anyone else (Lilith possibly excepted, but she's her own breed).

                        Idea for a chronicle:

                        The PCs are all recently Embraced. Normal creation rules for Neonates/CotN, normal limit on each Trait, but can't buy anything related to sire or Generation.

                        ST can toy around with in-clans and Weakness. Each PC may be unique, mix clans, or just be normal, don't matter. Also keep Generation, BP secret from them, let them find out as they play

                        Give them the traits of the 3rd Gen (in V5 initial BP 6 or 7 will do). They don't know, but they're 2nd Gen. Can do as they please, but hiding it is probably safer since they're still low stats CotN

                        After a few sessions, Elders from distant cities start to appear. They seek the PCs directly, as if they know exactly where they are. They do. You decide if they're hostile, curious, sycophantic or just weird.

                        Congrats: the PCs are the Beckoning. If they go seek for answers, there are others like them all over the world, though it started somewhere in the Middle East. Maybe others have answers or maybe the trail shows the path of their secret sire, and some points in it may reveal whatever you want.

                        Don't worry if they go after anything else, just enjoy toying with the Beckoning as a constant source of weirdness in their lives. The only loss is that they may never know shit about their sire.

                            It's a little notion I like to toy around with sometimes: exactly zero reasons exist for any older vampire/proto-vampire to not Embrace new Childe. The idea that lower Gen are only old vamps is kinda bonkers.

                            Wanna use some low Gen in your Chronicle without involving them directly? Just drop new Childer from them, they're not powerful and let it transpire that those older monsters are still about and kicking.

                            A 4th Gen Childe is already a huger sign of the Antes wandering around than any Thin Blood will ever be.

                            Other idea to toy with: the game is full of scattered signs that the "Thin Blood Phenomenon" always existed, and as time passes the bar of where it happens dynamically changes.

                            Use it. Have once or twice an Elder that's secretly Thin Blood while being lower Gen, a remnant of another age. In V5 you can do that by adding T-B traits to someone with higher BP.
