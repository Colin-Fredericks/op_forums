      Probably depend on the game line you wanted to focus on, I think. If it were Werewolf, I'd sort of have this temptation to make the Sea of Trees a Hakken or Beast Court caern. Not sure why, though.

      Incidently, Jeremy Bates has apparently written a series of horror-suspence novels subtitled "World's Scariest Places", including the Suicide Forest in Japan, the Catacombs under Paris, Summit County OH, and the Island of the Dolls in Mexico City. While I own a copy of Suicide Forest, I've not had a chance to read it yet, so I can't comment on quality.

      Other sights I'd find interesting:
      • Zvikov Castle in the Czech Republic (I still rather wish Wraith had done a Necropolis: Prague book)
      • Changi Hospital in Singapore
      • Barranco de Badajoz in the Canary Islands
      • The so-called Bridgewater Triangle in Massachusetts and the Bennington Triangle in Vermont
      • The New Jersey Pine Barrens
      • The various abandoned and forgotten subway tunnels and stations in New York City
      • Cinecitta Studios outside of Rome
      • The Trinity test site in New Mexico
      • The Museum Vrolik in Amsterdam
      • Riverview Hospital in British Columbia

                  When I look for real world setting I look for settings that kind of fit the theme I am trying to convey in the game.

                  1) I chose the Twin Cities of Minneapolis and St. Paul, Minnesota because I love the vibe I get when watching Fargo the Movie and TV Show and feel that it matches the tone I want to set in my Vampire The Masquerade game. Basically kind of being seclude from the cannon and having more eerie/personal horror stories.

                  2) I chose Juarez for my Sabbat game because I watched the American version of the FX TV show "The Bridge" and found myself very disturbed and saddened about the city. Even though my game is ran in 1942, I try to throw hints to as why Juarez became the way it did now.

                  So I basically I guess look for real cities that haven't been covered in the cannon, rather than specific monuments or anything to that extent.
