                        There is an important distinction to be made between Mage WoD and our reality.

                        In Mage WoD, there is no external truth outside of the three forces of Change, Stasis, and Decay; the Avatar, and the impact of belief. All paradigms are different explanations for the mind to help make sense of these otherwise incomprehensible powers. Science is but one of these methods, but over time, for better or worse, it has become the default explanation worldwide. Whether people also believe in magic or not is not the question, the question is the prominence of their belief worldwide. That's the main issue in place and the fact there are at least 9 very different traditions to the technocracy 1 union exemplify this.

                        In our reality, however, things work independently of human belief. Superstition is still the way people explain what at the time seems unexplainable. But the scientific method stands apart from this as a way to address uncertainty and mystery. It's by creating repeatable models and testing logical premises in order to piece together the basic mechanics on how the world functions, and allows a common language in order to ensure all parties are talking about the same thing.
                        Real science only makes three assumptions
                        1. The universe exists
                        2. It is possible to observe and measure things consistently within the universe.
                        3. From these measurements and observations, it is possible to create predictive models of events and behaviours.
                        Everything else is based on the observations and predictive models, known as "theory's", made by other scientists. These theory's are examined, tested, improved, discredited, or replaced as our ability to measure and observe increases. And it's these theory's which providing the starting point for technology.

                        In Mage WoD, things are different. Science is not about observation and the construction of theory's, rather it's about forcing the universe to become repeatable through mass indoctrination.

                        Real science adapts their theories to reflect reality
                        Technocratic science adapts reality to reflect their theories.
