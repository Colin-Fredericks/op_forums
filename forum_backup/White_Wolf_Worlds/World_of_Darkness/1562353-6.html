                                                        I think the number of skills isn't the actual problem.

                                                        The classic list has been there since 1st Ed and, seriously, it has always been clunky and uneven.

                                                        Some have obvious uses, some have wide application, some have clear rules, some are highly useful in their areas.

                                                        And some are not. Many have weird overlaps in application, while the game never even tried to tell what to do when that happens. Ambiguous rules, narrow fields, fuzzy applications, or just plainly useless outside very niche situations.

                                                        We have an Academics skill that should cover all "Humanities", but we also have Finances for reasons, a skill most seem to think to be about making money, which is actually the one thing it shouldn't do, and whose actual uses are all about accounting, except maybe Economic Analysis/Theory that may or may not be here or in Academics.

                                                        CoD has a very similar number of skills, but has a far more solid list. WoD tried to solve it with even more skills and it only worsened the issue.

                                                        But based on the form, I think they may be working on it, but not yet testing, which would mean what we saw is a placeholder list, not an attempt at a definitive solution.
