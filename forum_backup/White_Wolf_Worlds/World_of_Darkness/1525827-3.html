        So anyway, cynicism in the World of Darkness. Yeah, as pointed out up thread, the '90's was also when the gritty Dark Age of Comic Books was a thing. Everything in the '90s seemed to have this need to be edgy and XXXTREEEEEME.

        Probably a cultural impact of the Reaganite '80s, casting a shadow over everything as capitalism asserted its dominance and as said elsewhere, the "ladder got pulled up after the successful" and everyone started realizing how truly fucked they were.

        And honestly, I don't think that has ever gone away. A lot of people of my generation and Gen Z are noticing diminishing returns. I think maybe that's why the World of Darkness enjoyed a major comeback with 20th Anniversary Edition and 5th Edition.

            I would agree that probably played a pretty big part. I think there's been a bit of ebb and flow in people's optimism about the status quo, but it's been trending pretty steadily downwards for over a decade now, I'd say.

            Something that thinking about all of this made me reflect on is the contrast with nWoD/CoD. There is cynicism and pessimism in there as well, but it's presented more as the general negativity needed to maintain a horror environment, rather than a punk commentary on reality in the form of a horror environment in an RPG. Perhaps WoD feels more cynical because it's more actively trying to motivate you with that cynicism, rather than just setting the mood for a game.

                                Overall, I'd say that WoD isn't really cynical at all. Or, perhaps it is better to say it is naively or idealistically cynical. More often than not there are external reasons why everything is crap. There are supernatural conspiracies that twist systems to their own ends and malevolent metaphysical forces that degrade everything they touch; things that can be identified, named, and fought. The world is as it is because some things want it that way and others failed to stop it, not because the current state of affairs was inevitable or due to inescapable failings on the part of everyone involved.
