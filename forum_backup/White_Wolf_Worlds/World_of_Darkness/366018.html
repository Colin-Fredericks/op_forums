                                              It has definitely come up in Mage games when there are people playing Cult of Ecstasy characters. We usually do "fade to black" or the classic B&W movie trope of "camera moves to the blowing curtains, fade out". It does short-change those characters though; we are HEAVY into describing exactly how each character makes his or her paradigm work to generate the effect they're looking for. We don't "fade to black" while describing the Dreamspeakers drum up the spirits, Hermetics draw out the sigils and intone the incantations, the Etherites finagle up a new gadget for this application or the NWO agent puts down a small gadget and begins to articulate his arguments just so.... There is a little leeway - like setting up the outlines of what exactly this sex scene will involve in the way of participants and/or props.... but that's it. No description of what this roll of the dice means in the way of accomplishing their objectives (or not, in the case of Paradox, failure, backlash, whatever....) just a laundry list of what they got out of it after the fact.

                                              But I have no inclination to do it any other way. I was in a Skype game (not with my long time crew of players) where the descriptions of the sex got a little vivid in a rock concert that turned into an orgy scene. It was entirely appropriate story-wise. My character was the performer, so he wasn't involved in the shenanigans, but family members overheard what everyone else was up to and people were getting upset. I was also uncomfortable myself as I knew that another player was taking care of his young nephew.

                                              In the next M20 game, if the player of a CoE wants the "full immersion" description that everyone else is getting, they are probably going to have to focus on other aspects of that Tradition's foci, like inebriation, drug use or other activities that lead to an ecstatic state. (For an example on the weirder end, there was an very powerful NPC Cultist who was a gourmand and was blissing out over the pate. If you REALLY wanted taccess to high level Time effects, you had to arrange to meet him for a full course formal dinner...)
