    Immortal the Quickening

    10-22-2018, 11:57 AM
    Well it’s going to be a while before we get a General World of Darkness Vault, so unless I attach this to Vampire or Changeling I probably won’t Vault this project, and I need a to get a new computer because I am not typing on my phone for a whole PDF!

    But I always like the idea of Highlander Style Immortals, but divorced from most of the movies /series. I remember an old Cartoon that was more like what I was thinking where the Highlander took some useful quickenings and what not set in a post apocalyptic future, and then we got that Anime, which was more along what I was thinking.

    But I think it would be fun to treat Immortals more cinematic, and less two normal dudes slapping swords with the occasional spark.

    So my idea first root it in the World of Darkness. So what are Immortals? I’m thinking what if we get them to the 10,000 Immortals and or the Exalted? In fact if I want to avoid copyright issues I would probably call them Exalted in the PDF. So either they are remnants of the Exaltations in Modern time or they are the Chi of the Wan Xian after most were cursed into becoming Wan Gui.

    I’m thinking their splat might be he Aspect of their Quickening. So one for each of the Elements: Flame, Metal, Wood, Earth, Water, Storm/Wind, but also maybe Shadow, Ice, and Light.

    They’re powers revolve around their Aura and Quickening rating. Their expendable energy however works different from most, it would be Pulse, and they get free pulse to use each turn but can spend willpower to channel more.

    Maybe something like:
    Quickening—Pulse per turn
    1-1
    2-1
    3-1
    4-2
    5-3
    6-4
    7-5
    8-6
    9-Special

    Each Immortal has an Aspect but also chooses Some Atteibutes they have as Favored.

    Their different Cultivations can channel pulse differently, allowing them to do things like add dice and later successes to certain rolls or remove difficulties.

    They may have a mummy like immortality that can be nullified by removing their noggin.

    They have a normal Experience and Quickening experience. When they slay another Immortal they can roll Quickening of those they killed plus a modifier and take those points in Qexp, which can be spent to raise The Quickening trait.

    They can also learn mortal Sorcery, and some develop Psychic Numina, both of which can be learned by the immortal who slays them.

    Many develop Cultivation’s to bind specific weapons to their Quickening. But I’m thinking of a few that go Full Akuma and are hand to hand experts with full on street fighter moves.

    There’s only a handful on Earth Normally, well I say a handful but that probably means like a Hundred or something, with them often being drawn to each other. After the Red Star Anthelios appeared though a large number of them start Having their second Breaths and are drawn into supernatural conflict. They know the Red Star represents the Gathering as the last of the Immortals are rushing into this Age and they are drawn to become whole. There can be only one, and that one will achieve the Prize.

    But I like the idea both as PCs but also as interesting NPCs to toss into any game.
