    Infusion Rebuild

    05-13-2020, 01:34 AM
    Infusion has always been a bit of a odd duck as an art, existing pretty much purely to give Nockers tech in previous editions, something the much less technophobic 20th edition made rather unnecissary fixing problems that no longer existed and not engaging properly with either the arts system or the crafting system. Which is too bad since the idea of Magical crafting powers does have alot of thematic cache so its worth having as an idea..

    That being said lets rebuild it.. make it something worth working with

    I'm going to start from the top. The ultimate power of the art(not counting theoretical legendary applications.. which is a matter for a different thread)


    So what should the greatest acts of creation that are generally replicatable be able to do? I'm going to look at Lycians here. It works with the idea of "Infusion" works across multiple realms rather than limiting it to Fae 5 only and is a unique thing that exists outside the current crafting system.

    Cogs also exist outside this system!I So something that allows the creation of Renegade Cogs and Lycians. Since version one was named Gilgul we can argue its not about "creation" per se but drawing them from wherever. So.. lets add a little more utility if rare the transition of Inanimae between Anchors.

    Ok!

    So how easy should it be to do? My first thought is requiring permanent glamour or wp to use but that seems to even more fully put it in the fiat category. My second thought is to make it us temporary tempers but require them at the beginning of the process, building things even for a boggan takes time so they won't be mass producing killbots.. but i'm not quite happy with that either.
