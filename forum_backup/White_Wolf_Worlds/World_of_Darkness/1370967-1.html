The only thing you can do is go by your own personal headcanons and work from there. Because think about it:

    -The August Personage of Jade definitely isn't the same God the Elohim serve. Not only do they operate in different geographic regions altogether, they also run their celestial factions very differently, with entire different ranks and titles altogether.                                     Responses to some of the questions posited here as demon the fallen's advocate:

                                    •Why doesn't DtF mention the other splats?
                                    -Well, they do, however they describe it through the primary paradigm of the demon's perceptions. Within the Multi-Resonance Layered Reality, I would argue the other splats all exist within the perception of the demons, as each and every splat can be simultaneously correct before the final calcification of reality.
                                    --Cain as the father of murder created violence as we understand it and revolutionized the war in horrific ways. Demons describe the greatest of G-D'a angels departing afterwards and we know from Book of Nod/Revelations of the Dark Morher those four angels cursed and then offered redemption to Caine. Later on, demons recognize the Mark of Caine on all Kindred.
                                    --The Changing Breeds are heavily implied to be the descendants of the Malhim, great and terrible warriors used by the loyalist forces as shock troops during The War. Given that we know demons are incredibly Wyrm tainted in Gaian perceptions, The Fall is almost certainly the same event on another layer of reality as The Corruption of The Wyrm.
                                    --Humans were taught the secrets of reality by the demons during The Fall, which is symbolically representing as Adam & Eve eating the Fruit of Knowledge in Eden. Later on, The Watchers taught even greater secrets to humanity before the tragedy of Babel. This Age of Babel works very well as one layer of what mages describes as the Mythic Age.
                                    --Houses of the Fallen goes into great detail about how the seventh house was involved in the creation of the underworld after The Fall when humanity was cursed to know Death.
                                    --The Firstborn of the Age of Myth work perfectly well as another layer of the time of creation with the legendary war between the Tuathe de Danaan & Fomorians which eventually results in Elder Dark being cast away as a mythic age comes to an end, nearly plugging into Demon's timeline.
                                    --The Ten Thousand Immortals and their corruption and becoming the Kuei-Jin is an obvious comparison but I have another which is more speculative then these others. In this, I posit that the Ten Thousand Immortals were the Nephilim on another layer of reality. Divinely imbued mortals who were created to fight a great war and were given stewardship of the world but fell to vice and corruption and had to be cast out in a great cataclysm. Sound familiar?
                                    ---A related head canon but I for one think the Wan Xian or Ten Thousand Immortals served as a first iteration of the imbued and what we know today is a deliberately weaker version so as to avoid a repeat of the previous disaster.

                                    So ultimately my first point is that Multi-Resonance Layered Reality works perfectly well for allowing every splat to be internally correct as well as cooperatively cohesive with one another.

                                    •What about the Were-Dinosaurs?
                                    -This is a fair question and I would like to know more about it myself, however I do have a few responses for this.
                                    --Even within the cosmology of WtA, the idea of a prehistoric dinosaur civilization doesn't make sense within our understanding of Earth's reality. However, with the concept of Multi-Resonance Layered Reality, one can resolve this snag neatly. Yes, within one layer of reality, there were a race of sapient dinosaur people who ruled the earth yet their time of glory was destroyed in a great cataclysm. A cataclysm which was different in this layer, yet just as tied t The Fall or The Deluge as the rest.
                                    --This taken with the fact that the Technocratic Union has actively created a consensus which denies the possibility of dinosaur king's, we see how the Multi-Resonance Layered Reality remains relevant as mages maintain the capacity to alter the nature of the calcified layer that is the material reality of the WoD.
                                    --Plus, the first thing that Multi-Resonance Layered Reality was meant to explain was how a 7-Day Creation & Big Bang/11+ billion years could be simultaneously accurate, so that also sidesteps the classic Dinosaurs v. Creationism debate, at least cosmologically.

                                    •What Were the other splats doing?
                                    -This is where Multi-Resonance Layered Reality is once again our friend, as the other splats are happy to tell us what they were doing:
                                    --Caine was being cursed, taught by Lilith, embracing childer, and doing all the other stuff from the Book of Nod that happens before the Great Deluge. Demon even mentions Cain's role on another layer of reality in leading the militant rebels agains Abel's loyalist tribe.
                                    --The Changing Breeds tell us precisely what they were doing in early creation. Serving Gaia in peaceful harmony before the Wyrm's Corruption. Just like Angels & Humanity before The Fall. After the Wyrm's Fall they begin combatting the corruption and spiritual miasma caused by the Weaver and Wyrm. Now remember that the Fallen are Wyrm (and probably weaver) tainted to an extreme and their descriptions of bestial warriors in service of a patroness called 'Gaia' seem familiar. Demons simply called them 'The Malhim'.
                                    --Ask 7 mages and get 13 answers about what was going on at the origins of everything, but from the constant descriptions of a long-lost mythic golden age in so many traditions and the eventual cataclysmic destruction of said age, we can assume that The Mythic Age of the mages are describing many varied layers of reality.
                                    --Wraiths were actively supporting the seventh house in their war against Heaven, according to the slayers.
                                    --The Firstborn Fae serve as reflections of what happened in this age in humanities perception by their very nature, but tracking their recollection to the timeline works just as nicely as the others do.
                                    --Heck, the backstory of the Kuei-Jin fits extremely well as an alternative perspective to the events of The Fall in so many ways it's honestly incredible.

                                    •Thr Weaver's Gift
                                    -According to the record of the changing breeds, humanity was originally uplifted by being given gifts from The Weaver. This act rose them from their primal state and planted the seeds for the corruption to follow. You might even call this... Original Sin?
                                    --In my opinion, this example does far more to provide support for DtF's Multi-Resonance Layered Reality then it does to argue against it. Because ultimately, these four acts which I'm about to use as an example achieve the same thing as perceived in four different ways:
                                    ---Changing Breeds: Humanity is bestial but with ingenious potential. This attracts the Weaver's attention and sympathies. The Weaver longs for affection and offers humanity gifts. When they accept they are enlightened and uplifted, yet this leaves them vulnerable to spiritual corruption which the Wyrm soon exploits. Time goes on and the golden age is destroyed.
                                    ---Demon: Humanity is ignorant and beastial. Angels long to see humanity achieve its potential and be loved by them. Lucifer leads rebellious angels to grant gifts of enlightenment and love to humanity. Humanity accepts and arises to their full potential, including good and evil. This leads to God's wrath and The Fall.
                                    ---Abrahamic: Humanity is innocent within The Garden. They are persuaded to eat The Forbidden Fruit and gain the Knowledge of Good and Evil. This raises them up yet leaves them vulnerable to sin, particularly Original Sin. As punishment they are cast from Eden and cursed. Humanity must struggle forever after.
                                    ---Science: Proto-human species slowly develop and are similar to forms of primates. These proto-humans evolve and adapt and begin to resemble modern humans. At some point this evolution results in salience and understanding of abstract concepts. These new humans learn language and good and evil. This allows them to grow rapidly, yet also will become the foundation of all the tragedies and sorrows to follow.

                                    All of these four scenarios are distinct yet thematically similar, which is exactly what the Multi-Resonance Layered Reality is meant to illustrate.

                                    A paradoxical reality which allows stories to be truer then true is an excellent tool for any writer or setting to have at their disposal.
