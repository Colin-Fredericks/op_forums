    (Custom/Homebrew Gameline Concept) Boogieman: The Nightmare

    09-14-2016, 03:09 PM
    Hello there..
    Wanna know a secret? I can read minds.
    Well.. really only your mind at the moment.
    ...
    Proof?
    That thing behind you? I know that it's your greatest fear.
    Horrible isn't it? Eight twitching legs covered in bristling hair sharp enough to carve deep gouges in your skin. And the eyes.
    The eyes are the worst, aren't they?

    Right now you're thinking you're dreaming, aren't you? This can't be real, right? No way spiders are that big, no way anything could be that big.
    You're right. You are dreaming. But let me ask you, does that make it any better?
    Does it make the poison burning through your veins any less painful? Didn't think so.

    See, I live inside of you. Kinda like a tapeworm really, only I don't eat your half digested food as it's sliding down your gob. No, no, I eat fear.
    Throat gripping, mind-killing fear. The kind you're feeling right about now.

    Funny thing. When a tapeworm eats, you don't get to digest that food. When I eat, you don't get to feel.
    Confused? You're thinking I'm full of shit, you're feeling pretty terrified right now. Fair enough.
    But see, I'm not a tapeworm am I? I like to wait. Wait until the fear reaches its peak, at that very moment when all hope is lost and there's nothing but a gut wrenching unthinking terror.
    That's when I feed. And that's when you wake up in a cold sweat, panting and pissing yourself but you can't remember why. That's when you get to go back to your perfectly normal life.
    Your perfectly normal bland fucking life.

    But until then, you're mine.
    And I'm going to make you squeal.
    -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Hey! Bit ambitious for my first post on the forums, but I've this idea bouncing around me 'ead for a while now and have even taken it on tour across a few other forums. Figured this was as good a place as any.

    Now to start; a disclaimer. I have no experience with game design, little experience in WoD beyond being relatively knowledgeable in oWoD lore (And really only a solid grounding in Vampire)
    I have no delusions of actually making this into a polished, fully fledged PDF or anything of the sort, nice as that would be.

    That aside, here's what I do have:

    Bogeyman: The Nightmare will (hopefully) be a game about memetic life forms that live inside the mind; the titular Bogeymen.
    These creatures can live only as long as they're being thought of, even if only subconsciously, and so they link the image of themselves with strong emotions. Usually fear, but additionally anger, sadness, grief, shame, or even pleasure.
    In this way, they must dwell inside the minds of mortal men as parasites; leeching off of their emotions to feed themselves. Young Bogeys are little more than malignant tumours, and can do little besides passively consume, growing stronger over time. Gradually, as they mature, they are able to tease out reactions from the mind of their host, culminating in the ability to instill horrendous visions while their host slumbers and gorging on the ensuing terror. The most powerful of their kind can even project these visions into the real world, blurring the lines between waking and dreaming and in most cases driving their hosts utterly mad in the process.

    Bogeymen dwell in the Shadowlands' reflection of The Dreaming, land of the Fae folk, known to them as The Nightmare.
    The Shadowlands are both the realm of death and memory, and the bittersweet memories of lost Changelings have created the sort of dismal environment such miserable creatures as Bogeymen could spring from.
    No one is quite sure where they came from, not even the oldest of Bogeys. Indeed, it is quite likely that Bogeymen have simply always existed, feeding from the thoughts of the first simple creatures and gradually becoming more complex entities the minds that contained them expanded. Or perhaps it was the other way around. Perhaps it was the meddling of ancient Bogeymen that first drove man to sapient thought. After all, there is no race that better knows the intricacies of the mind than the Fear-Eaters.
    But none of this can be proven fact, and in the absence of fact, theories blossom.
    Some claim that the Bogeymen are the spirits of dead Fae; bits of soul cast off following each reincarnation. Others will tell you humans are the fathers of the Fear-Eaters; unconsciously giving birth to a new Bogey with every stray thought.

    Changelings, filled with spite at the cruel mockery of their old home that is The Nightmare, will hiss that the Bogeymen are a race of usurpers and betrayers who infected The Dreaming and rotted it to the core to better fit their foul tastes.

    Of course, such generalizations do the Bogeymen little justice. Being so prolific, there are many creeds and schools of thought among them, known amongst themselves as Schools.

    These philosophies are meant to fill the gap in their potholed history; to explain their existence, their purpose, and proper behaviour.

    Some choose to think of themselves as protectors; meant to warn their hosts of coming doom and guide them towards a prosperous, healthy life.
    Others fancy themselves dark muses; their visions and nightmares a tool to inspire great works of art.
    More still accept their role as monsters, hopping from one host to the next and gorging themselves on fear, leaving a wake of broken minds in their wake.

    Then there are the most secretive of the Fear-Eaters....
    Those that hide in darkness and bide their time, waiting patiently like an egg, or a seed.
    Growing stronger until one day, they may leap forth from their hosts minds like a monstrous hatchling.

    Regardless of the case, these creatures are more than simple parasites. Though unseen, the Bogeymen are major players who skulk the shadows of the World of Darkness.

    Influences and potential sources of inspiration:
    Stephen King's It: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/It_(novel)
    The Babadook: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Babadook
    Aaahh!!! Real Monsters: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaahh!!!_Real_Monsters
    Various real world Bogeyman mythologies: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bogeyman
    - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sack_Man
    - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coco_(folklore)
    - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ijiraq_(mythology)
    - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qalupalik
    - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Namahage

    Prospective themes include Power and Powerlessness;
    Bogeymen are practically gods within the minds of their hosts, filled with unimaginable power. And yet they can only interact with the world outside the shadowlands through indirect manipulation; trying to herd their hosts as one might herd cats. A bogeyman is shackled to their host, and yet a bogeyman with no host is little more than a pitiful writhing larva.

    Additional themes could encompass parenthood and legacy:

    The Nightmare, like the world of adults, is a cold, dark, uncaring place. Bogeymen can only truly live indirectly through their hosts, like many parents will live through their children, though whether this takes the form of spiteful, jealous abuse or smothering nurturing varies from case to case.

    A bogeyman knows that one day, it will be forgotten and die alone and unmourned; such is the life cycle of the Fear-Eaters, yet a forgotten bogeyman lingers like some slowly decomposing corpse. Dead, but not yet gone from the world. The most successful bogeymen have remained ever-present and influential, taking names like wrath, lust, envy, greed, and pride. Though they have long sense passed, they have never truly died, taking their tiny shares from every living being to ever be born. While most Bogeymen fade away like any other idle thought, the teasing possibility of legacy beyond death haunts the minds of all Fear-Eaters.
