            But that's not how Mage sees things, which is the rub.

Although mages use a different set of terms (Entropy, Dynamism, Stasis), knoweldgeable spirit mages likely would not see a conflict with how Werewolves view the interaction between the Wyrm, Wyld, and the Weaver. Technocrats might dismiss this, but that just might be because the Technocrats are wrong!

            At the very least, Tradition Mages see the opposition; they just see it in metaphysical terms instead of explicit spiritual terms. Because many mages tend to view spirits as reflections of humanity (at least the lower end ones), they likely would not be surprised at the strength of the Weaver and its interaction with the Wyrm.

            I just do not see the fundamental incompatibility you insist as being present.
