        Most effects don't deal damage, and have binary effects, so they just fall under the Feats of Magic success requirements. After that, most of the remaining effects are going to benefit from extra duration and not actually deal damage... and most of the time when you'd want both damage and duration... there's a sensible reason not to allow you to boost both at the same time.

        As for Willpower... the problem is that the games are very circumspect about what Willpower actually is. It's not actually true that there's no way to reduce Willpower in the games - though the vast majority of times you can force people to lose points instead of dots, and even more ways to mess with Willpower rolls with bonus/penalty dice/difficulties - but their tends to be a treatment of Willpower as something that isn't just a function of having a strong mind. Now, the level of changes in Mind include lots of other things like Nature too, so that would certainly lean on the side of Mind being able to adjust Willpower.

            The Mind 5 write-up itself either operates on some significant assumptions or its supposed to describe a naturalistic account of the environment, kind of like how Spirit 5 went with Gilgul. Some paradigms wouldn't work with it. In regards to destroying minds, it seem like the write-up indicates that it is personality, memory, and behavioral patterns that are annihilated rather than self-awareness. There is no indication that a being with a destroyed mind goes into vegetative state. Honestly that itself feels like it would require Life to mess with the central nervous system such that the organic processes of cognition cease, or else a consciousness would just appear in the system even if the previous one was annihilated. And that's not even counting the additional ontological questions raised by the body still having the same Avatar and all of that. RAW, it seems like there are two options; either the being has their personality wiped but they retain the same stream of awareness, or their consciousness is annihilated entirely and any new minds that come into the vacant body are ontologically distinct persons from the original.

                                    Prime 1

                                    • Etheric Senses/ Consecration/ Infuse Personal Quintessence

                                    A beginning study of Prime allows the mage to perceive and channel Quintessence from Nodes, Tass, Wonders, and magickal Effects. She may spot energetic ebbs and flows, can sense and at least try to read Resonance and Synergy signatures, and could also absorb Quintessence into her personal Pattern. Mages without at least one dot in Prime cannot absorb Quintessence beyond their Avatar Background rating. A Prime-skilled mage, however, may do so.

                                    When infusing her Quintessence into an object, that mage may also consecrate the object with her personal energy. When she shapeshifts, steps sideways, or otherwise alters her Pattern’s metaphysical nature, that consecrated object will then change with her. In the process, it also picks up her personal Resonance… which, because it both identifies her and becomes essentially connected to her, is not always a good thing.
                                    Prime is another of the spheres that gets a lot of non sensory abilities at rank 1. Indeed, some of them aren't even spell abilities.



                                    Perceive Quintessence (Etheric Senses)


                                    The mage can sense quintessence in nodes, as well as particularly notable (energetic) flows of it in other areas. The greatest use of this is sensing Tass, Wonders, and magical spells. He can also sense the presence of resonance and synergy and with more successes actually read it.


                                    Sense Vampires and Disciplines

                                    Vampire blood is, if I recall correctly, sometimes described as a form of Tass. Between that and the ability to sense resonance and synergy, Prime 1 should be able to sense vampires and even the use of Disciplines.


                                    The Stink of Consecration

                                    Since Consecration imparts resonance to the consecrated thing, Prime 1 can both determine that an item is consecrated and (if he’s familiar with the resonance) who it is consecrated to.


                                    See Magic

                                    The mage can see the threads of quintessence that go into weaving a spell. This won’t let him identify the spell, but he can tell it is being cast and likely some information on where.
                                    It is worth noting that in Revised, this wasn’t even a spell, it was an ability inherent to learning Prime 1, much like holding more than your Avatar rating in quintessence. That is not present here, though.

                                    Beginning Quintessence Channeling (Infuse Personal Quintessence)

                                    At Prime 1 a mage can channel the essence from a Node and Tass into his own pattern.

                                    Contradiction Resolution – Channeling Spells?

                                    For reasons I am not quite sure of, perception and channeling were combined into one list, which as written means that a mage can channel any quintessence he can sense. For instance he could channel the quintessence from wonders and spells into his own pattern. That’s probably not intentional, at least not directly, as spells don’t have a set pool of quintessence points associated with them

                                    Possibly channeling the quintessence in spells is the basis for the optional unweaving rules.

                                    Anyways, I really can’t come up with an application that doesn’t just restate channeling quintessence from a node or tass Any thoughts?.

                                    Expand Quintessence Pool (Infuse Personal Quintessence)

                                    This is less an ability and more an augment that comes with knowing Prime 1. A mage is no longer limited to his Avatar rating for his quintessence pool.

                                    Consecration (Consecration)


                                    A mage can infuse his quintessence into an object connecting it to himself in a metaphysical way. The object picks up his resonance and will change with him when he changes and stay with him when he . Shapeshifting and stepping sideways are specifically called out as uses for Consecration, but they are not exclusive. No special rules for duration, cost, or other spheres are given so standard rules apply.

                                    As written, this doesn’t take additional spheres, even if the Prime 2 version to Consecrate Life does.


                                    Yoink

                                    It is not stated that consecrated items have to be on a mage’s person when he changes in order to change with him. There are a few clever applications for giving an item over to a person then changing it on them when they aren’t expecting it.


                                    Consecrate Energy

                                    Much like in Entropy the place of energy in this power is uncertain. Likely a mage can consecrate an energy pattern, it just has to be one that would stick around like an everburning fire or a ball of light or some other magical effect.

                                                    Prime 2

                                                    •• Fuel Pattern/ Construct Patterns/ Enchant Patterns/ Body of Light

                                                    Attaining a degree of control over Prime energies, the mage may divert Quintessence into new or existing forms. Combined with other Spheres, this allows that mage to create new Forces, Life, or Matter Patterns (conjuring them from thin air), and to infuse existing items with Primal Force to strengthen them or enhance their protective or destructive power.

                                                    Weapons or attacks infused with Quintessence through Prime 2 Effects may hurt spirits or inflict aggravated damage, and substances infused the same way may protect against such harm. (See Chapter Nine’s Combat section for details.) On a related note, she may also – with Life 2 for simple organisms, or Life 3 for complex ones – consecrate a living thing at this Rank, as if that life-form were an object described above.

                                                    Through similar applications of energy, the mage can also conjure a simple Body of Light: an idealized self projected from ephemeral energy. Although this Body of Light has no substance or special properties (unlike the astral form described in Chapter Nine, p. 477), it presents a glowing holograph of the mage herself.
                                                    It begins. Prime takes this odd stance that working on living things should be harder than working on other patterns. So much harder that it takes both an additional rank and the Life sphere. This is a break from Revised, but it is what is stated in 20th. Unfortunately, Prime is very bad about what ranks of Life are needed.


                                                    Fuel Pattern (Fuel Pattern)

                                                    The bread and butter of Prime 2. This is the power that pairs with Transmutation effects to create patterns from nothing.


                                                    Enchant Objects (Enchant Patterns)

                                                    The mage can also channel quintessence into objects to improve them in some metaphysical way. Improved weapons can hurt spirits and inflict aggravated damage. Other things improved in this way can instead soak aggravated damage and spirit attacks.


                                                    Nerf Brand Spirit Herder

                                                    The normal way enchanting works is upgrading bashing or lethal damage to aggravated, but another possible use is upgrading a seemingly harmless weapon like, say, a Nerf gun, into something that does small amounts of damage to spirits and ghosts.


                                                    Body of Light (Body of Light)


                                                    A mage can use Prime 2 to project a hologram like replica of his body. M20 is not as clear as previous editions about what this is supposed to be used for. There are, however, a few places in M20 that use this as the base for other effects, most prominently as a basis for a more robust astral form than normal projection. Note that as written this only lets a mage make a projection of themeselves, which is a break from previous editions. Given that this is seemingly the basis for some illusion spells, though, this likely isn't an intended restriction.


                                                    Alibi

                                                    Depending on what the Body of Light looks like, a mage could use one to appear to be in a different place than he actually is.


                                                    Remote Avatar

                                                    I’m pulling this from Revised, but there’s nothing in M20 that indicates it doesn’t work here. The Body of Light can be the foundation for a material form made with appropriate Pattern Spheres. He can then project into this form (with Mind) which lets him physically act in a different place than he is.


                                                    Conjunctional Effects?

                                                    Consecrate Living Thing (??? + ???)

                                                    Contradiction Resolution - Where Are These Life Rules Coming From

                                                    This is perhaps the strangest requirement in these Sphere descriptions. With Prime 2 a mage can now consecrate living things much like he could consecrate objects, except… Prime 2 can’t do this on its own, it needs Life as well. Now, that’s a clunky, clunky way of handling this, but it isn’t a contradiction.

                                                    The contradiction is the requirement of Life 2 for simple creatures and Life 3 for complex, which is not the usual progression for Life. The usual would be 2/4 for Alter Simple/Complex, and that’s likely what is supposed to be here. The only Life ability that has a 2/3 progression is healing, and consecration would be a rather sketchy use of healing.


                                                    Widen Spell Targets

                                                    Consecration allows metaphysical alterations like shapechanging, stepping sideways, and arguably teleportation and time travel, to work on people at ranks they would ordinarily not be available on. This bypasses restrictions on how many people can be brought across the gauntlet and allows shape changing at lower ranks of life.

                                                    It may be that this was the consideration that led to Prime 2 needing Life 2+ to consecrate living things.


                                                    From Elsewhere in M20

                                                    Animate Corpse (from Common Magical Effects)

                                                    Ambiguity Resolution

                                                    Somehow Prime 2 has the ability to animate the remains of living things. It takes Life 2 or Matter 2 depending on the nature of the remains. It’s not clear what Prime is doing here or what the supplemental spheres are doing for that matter. My best guess is that this is a Body of Light locked to the parts, though that would mean that a Body of Light can be programmed to act in certain ways. It would also mean that Prime 2 can animate just about anything with Life 2, Matter 2, or Forces 2.
