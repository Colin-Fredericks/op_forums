    Newish to Roleplaying and WoD... Need help, please

    10-28-2014, 08:11 PM
    Hey everyone! Thanks for popping in

    The title sums it up, but here is the short version: My character's goals are gone, the rest of the group is now aligned against him, and I don't want this to turn into a PVP game.

    If you are interested in reading a little, here are the details:

    I am the "money bags" and "bruiser" of the group. During character creation, I designed a 36 year old Carpenter named Corey. When I designed him to wrestle with Existential problems, I didn't know the nature of the campaign...

    My introduction to the game was a Drive check, and with a pool of 5 D10, I rolledTHREE 1s. I hit another car killing an 18 year old man in the car, and the game kinda goes downhill for Corey from there. The kid I hit had a deal with a demon for his soul(and several others) and when the man was no longer able to help those other souls make deals, the demon was furious with me. I pay bail, hang out in the county, waiting trial, and my hotel burns down around me while the Demon, "Red Tie" tells me he will have my soul ETC. I then go to the only place I think I am safe from a demon: a (Catholic) church! I meet a priest named Father Lawrence, and start to believe a LITTLE about God and Christianity ETC. After several days with Lawrence, I aid him in cleansing a home of a spirit in a nearby country home. Lawrence sends Steve(a young priest) and I to the woods to collect the spirit's soul in a doll, and he goes inside to console the very confused family. Corey doesn't know the doll plan, and when I see the doll, the ST makes me roll a Perception-style check and I dramatically fail. Corey is freaking out, thinking the doll is a live baby being tossed about, and Corey screams at Steve. The scream awakens the spirit which manifests physically and kills Steve. Lawrence runs into the forest and when he gets there, a Templar Knight's armor, shield, sword and battle Bible materializes around him and he starts glowing. Father Lawrence kills the Spirit. Upon Steve's death, Lawrence goes on a warpath against the evil spirits in the area, and hunts down Red tie, banishing him to Hell. Father Lawrence turns out to be an "Eternal Martyr" Saint Lawrence. After vanquishing Red Tie, Saint Lawrence vanishes in a burst of light, going off to talk to God or take a Vacation or kill other demons. I don't know. I end up going into the church and grabbing his holy Templar Knight stuff that I had seen earlier when I was snooping in his Study. The armor's holiness shocks me and it kinda stings to hold it, but I have been becoming more Christian in the game and I manage to take it, hoping to protect it from any evil things.
    I grab the armor and take off with another PC, Amy Cho.

    Amy is a young reporter who is fiery and VERY feminist. She is the researcher of the group, and thanks to a STUPID HIGH mentor number, and a high contacts she is, by far, the most powerful character in the game. She also has the luckiest die rolls, and the ST loves her. The night I get the armor and stuff I decide I don't want to stay at Amy's apartment(her character and mine don't get along), so I walk three blocks to a hotel. On the way there I get attacked and stabbed. I lose lots of blood and I pass out right as I get back to Amy's place. Two days later, I wake up and spend a day or two on the phone with my business, turning it into a bigger business and then I buy a car(my old one was wrecked, so I bought a Hummer for it's durability). In the meantime, Amy has got my charges dropped via her contact the DA or something. She has somehow also acquired a shotgun that is fueled by Hellfire and REALLY hurts spiritual things. I leave the apartment for a few hours to talk to a realtor, go back and then get kidnapped, I end up with Amy "et al" at a warded and protected safe house after a ST handwave that kinda saved the whole group from dying. In the safehouse I open the Saint's Battle Bible and it's holiness blinds me. Yes. In the same session as I am kidnapped, and the session after I am stabbed, I am blinded..... -_-

    Amy takes me to a healing spirit she encountered earlier in the game, and he decides to come with us to help us fight the local evil baddies. When we get back to the safehouse, an evil vine spirit is at the safe house, and tackles my Hummer that is driven by a PC. The PC fails a drive check, drives my Hummer into the warded safehouse, killing the healing spirit. A battle ensues between the Vine spirit and the group, I am the only one who is dealt ANY damage. (Spirit dramatically fails a roll to hit someone else while I am trying to move past it) and I am knocked unconscious. Again. And now Saint. Lawrence's Holy apparel is in a big gun safe in the afehouse which burned to the ground after the fight. The safe is probably in the basement under tons of bricks and charcoal. lol.

    I wake up and I can see. The blinding was only temporary. When I wake up, the groups' political character, Saul Goodman, has a scheme to make him look good for an upcoming campaign involving me buying a warehouse and setting up cots, food and a medical unit to relieve victims of a HUGE gang war that Saul secretly started by giving guns to local gangs. Corey only knows he is helping people, not that Saul is the cause, so he agrees to help(trying to be Christianly and follow God's will to help others).
    While helping all these people, Father Lawrence shows up and Amy walks in, holding the Hellfire Shotgun. A huge firefight/brawl ensues between Amy and Saint Lawrence(who attacked her because he thinks she killed a Holy Healing spirit AND for using the Hellfire Shotgun). Amy and Saul(Who, recently and secretly, made a deal with an Ent-demon to gain Druid-like powers) end up "killing" Saint Lawrence who I was helping in the fight. At one point, Saul is pummeling Saint Lawrence and Amy has a gun pointed at me, rolling an enormous Intimidation roll, paralyzing me for a turn.

    So now, Saint Lawrence's armor and gear is in a safe that I don't have the key to, the group and I are aligned against each other, and I have no idea what on Earth to do. I am lost. I want to talk to the Catholic church and see what they know about Lawrence, but I know this will end up in me and the church against the group. I am just... Stuck.

    I impale Gummy bears and Vampires.

      When your character's story doesn't seem like it has anywhere else to go, find a dramatically appropriate and awesome way to bow out. Dying doesn't have to be losing if you can have fun doing it. I can't say for sure what your best option is, but much like TV shows that go on too long, it can be best to end the story when you're ahead and get a new character... if your character isn't going to get along with the party, and you don't think you can fix it, this really is the best option a lot of the time.

      It doesn't have to be dying either--having your character ride off into the sunset pursuing some other thing is fine. If you're really attached to your character that'll probably feel better (but try not to get that attached anyway). Talk to your ST about fun ways to play it--if you are no longer restricted by "I need my character to still be playable three sessions down the line", it opens up a lot of options and your ST might enjoy working with you to make the best of that. Just don't screw over the other players while you're doing whatever you're doing; if you do drop the character, you want to join in with another one after all.

              So I read your little story there.

              I see one of three options available to you. All of these will require you to have a beer and a chat (you could skip the beer I guess) with your ST and other friends. Just explain to them that you feel your character is in a difficult position, but you as a player want to play something more harmonizing.

              1) Find a nice dramatic way to kill this character, and make a new one. Sometimes this is the most emotional and BEST part of a story - endings and beginnings. You might even manage to leave a lasting legacy with the cabal, for better or worse. This is probably the route I would take, because honestly the story sounds good so far and a nice ending could be very memorable.

              2) With the ST permission, your character could have a 'revelation'. This could be anything from finding some piece of damning evidence against Lawrence to a simple scene where you have a 'come to Jesus' meeting with the other PCs and work it out. This might also be a great time for a Seeking - but that depends on what sort of mage you are and what sort of Avatar you've got. Another option in this catagory would be a little side adventure to put some space between the PCs and let them develop a bit more before trying to harmonize.

              3) Embrace the conflict! Seriously I have very close friends who have never once played a 'get-along with others' character. He was always a bad guy, or at least a guy opposed to the other PCs. Since IRL we were all such close friends, it never mattered. "Damnit Johnny, your character is a real asshole. I'm gonna burn him with fire SO HARD."
