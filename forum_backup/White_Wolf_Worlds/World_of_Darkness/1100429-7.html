    True, antimatter it's better deployed against many NC. Each extra NC you have increases the efficiency of that approach. And preferably somewhere you don't care to destroy everything in a mile (perhaps that's why it's used in the Deep Umbra, where a Nephandi flesh starship with a flock of Banes isn't so rare)

    As other alternative:

    You could create two or so Talisman weapons with a Spirit Eating Effect to dual wield like the B@DA$$ you are (or just (yawn) give them to your companions)

    Attaking with them would cause significative damage to any spirit, as it causes normal weapon damage (or alternatively, normal magick damage, wich may be better or not), and then 5 extra per level inflicted (so, if you manage to inflict 9 aggravated after soaking, the spirit will lose 54 Essence, more than enough to kill a small NC with a single strike). These weapons would be very effective against spirits of all types and can be used against spirits that are possesing something too.

    Even if you don't kill the bane in the first two strikes, which I dare to say it's feasible for a dedicated combat bane-eater shaman that has reached the power to create these to begin with, you can still cast a spell becuase Talismans do not consume a magical action.

    You would also get a load of evil Quintessence (that can be stored in the weapon and you may want to purify before using it to create more powerful Talismans to use against Nexus Crawlers), and if you drestroy the Bane, which it's more than likely, it's gone forever, no reform, and you can get evil knowledge from it, that may drive you maaad or not (possibly, removing the Mind 3 requisite would avoid this latter effect, that's surely more desirable for Nephandi than for a Bane Eater Shaman...then again, if you want Bane Lore, this is how you get Bane Lore).

    Also, since this it's a Rote, it has rules. You may not agree with them, you may think that they shit on the general rules of magick and everything sacred, but it is as canon and clear as any Thaumaturgic Ritual. And yes, you could Ritual Spirit Eating to massive effect, but I think this approach it's more in tune with the spirit of the rules for that Rote and, as a shaman, you wouldn't want to disrespect the spirit of the rules, right?

                Indeed.

                Adding to that:

                In the spiritual hierarchy Nexus Crawlers are strong Jagglings. In Mage they would be arguably on Lord level, albeit Lords tend to be unique creatures.

                In the spiritual "power scale", these are the levels just below "godlike being without stats". They're considerably stronger than your average demon summoning.

                In that sense I think they're relativistic to a Mathusalem/Archmage, but that's just my opinion. "Power levels" can't really be compared as mages, vampires and spirits work very differently.

                NC tend to be loners, as even other Banes fear them (that's not to say that they can't appear with other Banes, but rather than they aren't the "commander of the troops" type. If they apear with other Banes, worse for the Banes).
                They don't use what one would call "a strategy" (not that they need to do that, they're mighty, but Werewolf says that if NC cared to fight with strategy, Garou would be long dead) and are extremely unpredictable.
