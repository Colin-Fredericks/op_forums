    100 ways to make a Zombie/Ravenant/Ghoul

    09-11-2016, 10:56 PM
    So..i been reading too much about zombies (voodoo, frankenstein etc...) and i was wondering, how would you make one in every game line. the tricks here are different Kinds of Zombies.
    One: Rottening cadaver a la romero
    two: frankenstein kind of creature (patched from different body parts and that's slowly gaining awareness)
    three: voodoo zombie slave
    fourth: Human body posessed or inhabited by its former spirit or by a spiritual being. regaining memories and consciousness

    one extra catch...Lets say that you explain how a garou (Uktena Theurge) manages to bring a human back to life as an Aberration against Gaia, i mean..Zombie. The catch is that now you must also explain how to bring a Garou and a Kinfolk back as a zombie. In the case of a mage, you must explain how would you resurrect a mage and an adept and so on...the idea is to see how different factions would "Replace a Lightbulb" (just like in the joke...If the joke was a loved one dead carcass)


    also, by Ghoul i dont mean Vampire servant.. i mean more of a undead creature that feeds on human flesh

      Mage time.
      ..oh dear, where to begin.

      First off, independant of Zombie *type* you have several options when it comes to actually having something like intelligence in the Zombie:

      - Use Spirit 5 along with potentially hazardous literal soulsearching to bring somebody's soul back from the Lower Umbra. For sure an interesting experience for the Wraith - and of course it only works as long as they became a Wraith to begin with. If the target in question is a Mage's soul, probably add Prime 5 to tie their Avatar back into that body, unless you want them to be essentially a non-Mage on return. No special steps needed for a sorceror/acolyte/whatev. Treat as normal human.
      - Make it a host for an existing Umbral spirit through bargaining with a spirit, then using Spirit 4.
      - Force a spirit to enter the undead host with Spirit 4.
      - Create a literal Spirit from scratch with Spirit 5 inside it.
      - Create a Mind from scratch with Mind 5 to make a new intelligence.
      - Killing somebody but not letting them escape the body: Mind 5+Life 4 according to HDYDT.
      - Semi-intelligence-exception: Have the creature act purely on mechanical instinct, forgoing Spirit and Mind and using Life. Yes, life - you're creating an undead at this point that is in the same weird Life/Matter hybrid state as a vampire, but purely acting on instinct. Without Mind though, it will never develop any intelligence.

      There we go. Now for the zombie body types:
      * Cadaver? Prime 2, Life 2. If the cadaver already is in a state of rot, replace Life 2 with Matter 2. If wanting to prevent rot for a long time, add Entropy 3.
      * Frankenstein: Mend the pieces through Life 4, then proceed as with 'Cadaver'. Preventing rot forever requires a powerful Entropy effect. Alternatively, use Quintessence, Prime 3 and Life 4 to return the body to actual life.
      * Voodoo zombie slave is a combination of the Cadaver body type, and the 'Killing somebody..' soul type.
      * Human body with former soul' is a combination of the 'Soulsearching Spirit 5' intelligence variant, combined with Cadaver and using the mentioned Quintessence, Prime 3, Life 4 effect to make the body alive again. Alternatively use the Frankenstein Entropy effect to prevent rot.

      The 'eating human flesh' part can be reasoned by either having that as part of the instinct from the 'Semi-intelligence-exception' entry above, *or* imbueing the zombie with an inherent Life 3/Prime 4 ability which causes eaten flesh to become Quintessence which fuels the Entropy 3 effect that prevents the body rot (see: iZombie series where eating the flesh prevents exactly that.)


      P.s. 7 ways of adding intelligence, times 4 different body types...you just got 28 ways of making a Zombie/Revenant/Ghoul!
      72 to go.

                                          Um... If you're just looking for "zombie garou" for thematic purposes, I recommend Howling Shamblers (found in any Book of the Wyrm). It's a fomori that happens when a bane called a Howling Insanity possesses a Garou, resulting in a fomori that goes full zombie mode after being killed.

                                          I would venture that the Werewolf books probably only scratch the surface of the spirit world and Garou mysticism. I could easily see a wayward (either tainted, ronin, heretical, or a victim of the Twisted Ubringing flaw) Garou attempting to summon a dead ally's spirit and bind it back into their body using either existing Rites or trying to create their own. That could easily result in anything from a zombie to a makeshift Risen. A Silent Strider (with their ties to the Dark Umbra) or an Uktena (with their penchant for hedgemagic) would be the most likely to get to that point "successfully".... but again, I could easily see a ronin cub who managed to get their hands on Summoning and Binding going for it as well. Keep in mind that the cycle of life and death is considered a natural part of Gaia, and bucking against it would be pretty severe religious heresy to the vast sweeping majority of Garou, and might be worthy of a death sentence in many Elders' eyes. (Or 2 hours in time-out with no videogames in W20... I haven't re-read enough fluff to know just how dancing/hand-holding/PC the Garou Nation has been rewritten to be)

                                          I also wouldn't be shocked if there was an existing (albeit not written that I'm aware of) bane that could possess or be bound into a dead body. Considering Psychomachae and Blood spirits are the only two that can possess and maintain control of Vampires, that might be a starting point for considering the types of spirits that could animate a corpse. (Also, at least one book mentions Fomori hosts can be anything from humans to animals to inanimate objects, but in later books I've only seen 'humans' or 'humans and animals' listed)
