                    well, that's the thing. you would need an army of awakened led by masters working on a major ritual to put an entropy effect big enough to alter the minds of several billion people. thats a lot of successes. you got to think smaller. subliminal mind effects delivered over years via talk radio to push people into the mindset you want them in. memes on the internet pushing boundaries of taste a little more in your favor. computer algorithms that suppress opposing views in social media battlefields. Umbral constructs that psychically reinforce trends and patterns. Storm Chasers pushing hurricanes to hit just the right coast to anger residents at the financially crippled emergency response time. round the clock efforts within a entrenched and zealous propaganda distribution network. investment of time and effort guided by enlightened insight and clarity of vision will overcome brute force magical effects, which is one of the main reasons that the traditions have been on the run for so long. and entropy effect applied to politics would be more wisely aimed at giving the candidate good luck during debates and public appearances. mind effects that clearly communicate their words to he audience and primal investments to cause their views to appear to have more value than their opponents. if it were so simple to cast a spell to be president then we would have had a very different historical record. of course if you go by 1st edition the technocrats have been engeneering elections and politics as the super illuminati that they are and its really all a battle between them the lizard people of england. but the point is that change takes dedicated effort even with magic in the mix and the powers that be will actively counter it directly or indirectly. don't think they aren't scanning the future for disruptions to their timetable.

                    there are no bad ideas, just ideas that have not been refined enough

                                    Originally posted by Prince of the Night View Post
                                    A local sherrif or mayors election?
                                    The same principles goes on, you just will have a less broad scope to deal with, but usually still big for your everyday magic.

                                    It isn't that a big election isn't based on luck. It is that no election is based on luck. They're elections. People are consciously making decisions about their votes, so you can't change those votes by changing luck. What a luck spell can do is to alter the random variables in the system, like events that can deter some people of voting, or lead them to do it, or the chance of some happenstance destroying the votes already deposited. But since too much of those happenstances would probably result in the entire process being cancelled out, there's not much room for luck playing any big role.

                                    Luck, and luck magic, would make for a difference only at very specific circumstances. Only two come to my mind: when the difference between the candidates is too little, so even a lesser tip in the balance can change everything. The other is when too much of the voters are unsure, so even chance events can made their minds enough in their way to the polls. You could try some very careful uses of luck magic to indirect affect the electoral campaign to make it more probable for people to see the best of candidate A and the worst of candidate B, but that would be a helluva hard and judicious use of such magic, even in a school's election.

                                    Only magic able to actually control people or affect the data could actually influence the elections with only one spell. You can enchant the place where the votes are deposited to physically change the papers, letting some unadulterated to avoid it becoming too obvious. Or you can Dominate a good proportion of voters, aiming at the ones you already know to be uninterested in voting for your side, so they do vote the way you want. On a specially tricky try, you can aim for someone who can alone change things greatly, like the opposing candidate proper, to do something that can alter the results.

                                    Mostly you should look for a long, mixed strategy. Use magic judiciously to affect things here and there, keep an eye at how people are intending to vote, look out for magical interference of other factions. It could take a good chunk of a chronicle to make something about an election. Mix magic with a lot of more physical groundwork. You can really read IAmTheSweeper's post for ideas. The process is essentially the same for any poll, be it some sort of world-wide direct election, or simply a poll between 5 teenagers to decide the next event of the night.

                                        I did wind up having a little bit of a dig, and here are some very explicit and very major bits of interference - including a few that go against White Wolf's general policy re: purely human evils.
                                        One element that complicates the question is whether we should include cases where the actual cause of the action is unclear (e.g., how much of the oil policy in the Arab World in the early 1900s was down to the Technocracy and how much was down purely to human greed?) and where both sides are taking distinct but related actions that feed into each other without direct influence.

                                        In 1900, the Akashic Brotherhood openly backed the Boxer Rebellion.
                                        In 1909, Mathieu Hullebusch tried to openly overthrow the Belgian Congo's ruling government with his army of flying Etherite robots.
                                        1914... Vargo's a weird one as he doesn't quite count, having been all but erased from history by paradox. But at the same time, Lodin is outright controlling the unions in Chicago.
                                        In 1915, the Virtual Adepts arrange for the Zimmerman Telegram to be sent. If they hadn't, it would have been sent in 1917. The exact impact of this isn't really clear - on paper it shouldn't have made a major difference except for making the war a little longer, but as we all know, one small event can quickly spiral out of control and two more years for possible Unexpected Events could radically reshape the situation in Mexico and the US enough to significantly alter their involvement or lack of in the War.
                                        In 1917, the Atlanta Fire is caused by spectres. It isn't quite a political event, but it has massive political consequences down the line by reshaping the political and personal landscape of the city.
                                        In the same year, as we all know the Brujah either rode along with the Russian Revolution or outright masterminded it, depending on which source you take.
                                        In 1918, America's involvement in the Russian Expeditionary Force is caused by Ventrue manipulation.
                                        In 1920, Spectres deliberately incite violence in Chicago's gangland. Again, not quite political - but the violence of the bootleggers also had major political consequences down the chain.
                                        Same year, Barbarossa and his Brujah back the Socialists in Frankfurt.
                                        In 1921, we get Lodin being an absolute cunt and destroying the economy of Gary, Indiana as a ploy against Modius. That must've taken him a while, too, since the economy there didn't collapse in our world until the 60s.
                                        1924 had Trotskyite Brujah kill Lenin, and the Ventrue and Toreador put Stalin in charge.
                                        1934 has the Tremere and Ventrue of Europe start backing Fascists to counter the Brujah-backed socialists and anarchists.
                                        1939's Molotov-Ribbentrop pack is down to Brujah influence.
                                        1940 sees - and this is the 'purely human evil' thing from earlier - Gustav Breidenstein start 'mentoring' Hitler. Now, the easy answer is 'retconned by Shoah!' But it was reprinted in the Encyclopedia Vampirica, which though written by the German licensee Feather and Sword was published in English by White Wolf in 2002. So not only does it significantly post-date Shoah, but it passed through White Wolf's translators and editors before being published, which means it's difficult to go 'well, it was just those licensees not getting the memo' on this one.
                                        WW2 generally sees big involvement by the Technocracy, but they're more piggybacking than engineering.
                                        1941 is an exception, which is the Virtual Adepts engineering for Pearl Harbour to have any warnings ignored. They deliberately arrange for the high death toll of the attack in order to force America to action.
                                        The 1950s have repeated attempts by supernatural factions to trigger nuclear wars. Also not political, but I think it deserves a mention, no?
                                        1959 sees the Cardona Ventrue join the Brujah Council to support Castro, basically ensuring the Revolution succeeds.
                                        In 1968, Lodin conspires to cause the DNC Riots. He uses them purely as a smokescreen for his own operation, but there are huge political ramifications from the act.
                                        In 1973, Barbarossa managed to make Frankfurt the home of the predecessor to the European Monetary Institute. That's definitely some International Politics right there.
                                        And that's only the first three-quarters of the 20th century. A lot of it can be put down to big talk by unreliable narrators, but at the same time, we can see that there's been plenty of involvement in regional, national, and international affairs and politics by all sorts of groups.
