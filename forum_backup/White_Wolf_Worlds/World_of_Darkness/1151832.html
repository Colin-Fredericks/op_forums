    Devils Moral Compass

    10-21-2017, 11:12 PM
    The Demons in devils due accrue torment for becoming less moral and/or rejecting the design capital G gave them. Demons can't be immoral and follow "God's design" in universe, but what about the other way around?

    If a demon outright rejects god(not as in not believing in him/her/it, but as in disagreeing with god) and adopts another spirituality or "pagan" religion do they still accrue torment as usual(for "God's Displeasure"/rejecting their predetermined purpose) or do they loose torment for having a morality/code of conduct?

              [...] After all if humans are perfectly capable of following their own faiths why can't angels?
              The way I see it. Knowledge and Free-Will.

              Human have Free-Will which allows them to do and believe what they want. Angel doesn't have Free-Will (which was one of the Beef they had with God).

              Human may or may not Believe in something greater, thus why there are Atheists, Agnostics, Jews, Christians, Muslims, Pagans, Bouddhists, etc. in the world. their lack of knowledge of what is Beyond their senses allows them that luxury. Angels (Fallen or Not) Know that God Exist, they "Saw" Him, "Heard" Him, "Spoke" to Him and in the case of a third of them felt his Displeasure. It's really hard to truly adopt another spiritual/religious path view when deep down you Know, beyonf any doubt, that the Abrahamic God is Real.

              That's why I see Human and Angel behaving differently on this point. Granted I don't pretent to hold the Truth and I might be fully wrong in my view.

                                      The line Demon: The Fallen (and Devil's Due) explores the live and torment of the Fallen Angels, led by Lucifer, who rebelled against God in what they perceived in God favoring human by giving them Free Will and by forbidden Angels to interact with man.

                                      And for me, to understand the Devils/Demons it is essential to understand Who and What are (the Fallen) Angels and see how that ties to the game. Now, not having Free Will, thenit would mean that the whole Rebellion and its aftermath has to be part of God's Design...his big plan, and that the Fallen Angels were "meant" to follow that path.

                                      In essence, it boils down to a simple fact: whether they are Angels or Demons, they are given the illusion of Free Will, and they are acting according to that illusion. Some "remain" Faithful and are now hunting the Fallen Angels, some "Rebelled" and were cast out with various outcome in how they deal with it. By ultimately, it's only an Illusion and they are simply playing the role they were assigned.

                                      In regards fo Lucifer, the question here is: As their Leader, their General even, was Lucifer "created" by God with the knowledge of his role to play in that Design or was he as blind to it as his troops were? Was he aware of this illusion or not?

                                      it is a different take, but it doesn't negate the premisse of the game or invalidate it's rules, it just sets them in a different frame.
