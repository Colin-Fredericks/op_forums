            I original was very much against things like Kiths based on other supernaturals that already exist, like Vampire Kiths since we already have Cainites. I was eventually won over however, I remember someone else arguing the idea of Enchanters for instance being a Mage stand in Kith, and the C20 book really encouraging it. Because for one these are Kiths based on the Folklore and Myth and function well even if you don't have Prodigals, like Strigoi/Vryakokas are not really so much based on Cainites they are based on rural myths. Its kind of a meta thought exercise. The C20 book really embraces homebrewing, and these wouldn't be Kiths that have numbers that equal the numbers of say Pooka or Boggans. Before I read the Kith Creation section in C20 I wouldn't of made the Electric Shadows because they aren't Faery myths, they are movie Archetypes but the Kith Creation section talks about why not try making Kiths that aren't copy and paste myths and something new, also inspired by the spirit of creation that went into making the Fan-kith Jongaleurs. So I like the idea of lots of Kiths exist that don't really number very high, and are really nonstandard like how Selkies don't really work like normal kiths like Boggans, their Souls are linked to their Pelts and are passed on to relatives.

            So it isn't really a VAMPIRE Kith, but it is a Vampire Kith in the sense of Witch/Vampire/Evil spirit folklorish beings. Its vampire in the meta sense particularly of what inspires the creation of the kith as a though exercise.

                                        Alright, I've finally gotten around to writing up a basic idea of what I'm thinking of regarding a Giant kith.


                                        ----------------------------------------------


                                        Giants:

                                        "I like to travel the world every couple of reincarnations. It gives me the chance to see how well my handiwork has endured throughout the ages."

                                        From time immemorial, humans have always told stories of the Giants. Such creatures were often the source of awe inspiring works of creation, or cataclysmic acts of destruction. Wherever the Giants went, they left their mark on the world, marks that would often persist for centuries long after their original creators had moved on. Legends even say they were responsible for sculpting the world's land masses into what they are today. Giants represent dreams of lasting impact and historical significance. They are masters of both construction and destruction. With the Long Winter growing ever closer, many Changelings believe that the Giants are needed now more than ever, if they hope to rekindle humanity's spark of creativity.

                                        While many cultures have their own stories of Giants, the most iconic ones are perhaps members of the Nordic variety. These Kithain are said to be distantly related to Trolls in some fashion and also claim to have originally been the rulers of their own kingdom before suffering a fall from grace. It is unclear whether or not the Sidhe were responsible for their fall, as they allegedly were for the lost Troll kingdom, but the Nordic Giants still remember its name. They claim that the ruins of Jotunheim still exist within the Deep Dreaming, and if they could find a way to get back there, they would have the chance to rebuild their legacy. While this was initially considered to be a wishful fantasy, the Kithain have noticed the Giants are becoming increasingly more fixated on the legendary kingdom as the Long Winter becomes harder to ignore.

                                        Whether a Giant leans more towards creation or destruction is largely dependent upon their court. Those of the Seelie affiliation tend to favor their more creative aspects, possibly due to the fact the traditionalist houses are more likely to continue appreciating their contributions. By contrast, those of the Unseelie affiliation tend to embrace their more destructive aspects, seeking to tear the world down around them. Some among the Unseelie claim that they do this as a test, to reveal who has the strength to recover from the aftermath of a tragedy, but others are just happy to vent their frustrations upon a world that no longer believes in them.


                                        ------------------------------------


                                        Appearance: For the most part, Giants could pass themselves off as humans, if it weren't for their most outstanding trait, being their mythical size. Giants often tower over anything that does not have the description of 'colossal' or 'titanic' attached to it. A fully mature Giant can reach heights of 10 feet tall and weighing several hundred pounds in the Near Dreaming, while only continuing to expand in size as they travel further in! Nordic Giants often favor braiding their hair, regardless of gender and many among the male population take pride in growing full beards to accentuate their facial features.


                                        ------------------------------------


                                        Childling: Even from a young age, Giants display an interest in creative expression. Their toy boxes often contain a variety of hobby kits and play sets which they can use to bring their imaginations to life. Members of the Seelie Court take joy in assembling creative environments and acting out the lives of the people who reside within it, while those of the Unseelie Court usually take pleasure in destroying the finished creations, often in a dramatically exaggerated fashion.


                                        Wilder: Upon reaching some form of maturity, many Giants feel the urge to leave their mark upon the world. What was once a simple hobby now becomes a professional career, as the Seelie begin to follow the dreams they've had since childhood. Those of the Unseelie, however, are often frustrated by exposure to a world that doesn't support their ideas or believes in their creative potential, so they begin to lash out against the system and those who they feel are being given more praise than they deserve.


                                        Grump: For the most part, Giants who have progressed into Grumps seem to have mellowed with age. Seelie Court members have left behind a legacy of accomplishments that they can look back upon and smile proudly. Some hope to complete one final project before their time is up, but most decide that it's time for them to pass their skills on to a few apprentices so that their ideas will live on in others. Unseelie Grumps, however, often become crotchety old geezers who are angry at the world for never fully appreciating their talents. Blaming their lack of success upon interference by others, these Giants aren't so much interested in fostering their apprentice's growth, but rather they view them as an opportunity to live vicariously through their accomplishments. Such lack of proper nurturing often leaves their apprentices feeling just as frustrated as their teachers, thus leading to a new generation of Unseelie destroyers as the cycle begins again.


                                        --------------------------------


                                        Birthrights:

                                        World Builder - The handiwork of a Giant will last throughout the ages, or at the very least will take hundreds of years before it starts to show signs of degradation. In exchange for a point of Glamour during a Crafts roll, the Giant reduces the difficulty by -2. Additionally, Giants can never botch a Crafts roll.

                                        Might of the Jotun - The title of Giant is almost synonymous with physical power. At character creation, the Giant gains a bonus of +2 to Strength, even if this would take them beyond 5. Additionally, the Giant gains a +1 to their bruised health rating. This bonus stacks with the Huge Size merit, should you wish to take it.


                                        Frailty:

                                        Cramped Spaces - Even the tiniest Giant stands on par with the tallest of humans. Giants have great difficulty fitting themselves inside of places not designed to accommodate their size. Should the Giant attempt to enter a space that is less than 10 feet high and 7 feet wide, they must spend a point of Willpower to do so, and they immediately risk triggering banality in the process. For every 2 rounds they continue to remain within the confined area, they risk an additional banality trigger, which causes many in the autumn world to assume they are claustrophobic.
