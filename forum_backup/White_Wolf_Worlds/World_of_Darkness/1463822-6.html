                    Heck, I'm 35yo and the first thing I do with any new RPG is to search for some opinions. Of course, buying up good reviews is easy, but it also costs and isn't infallible at all.

                    But during my absence (my computer broke during the weekend and I only got the piece I needed today) this thread also went through some strange considerations.

                    5th ed is using many bits of style and mechanics from CoD. But it isn't either CoD 3.0 or a mashup in any capacity. Bringing in some influence would be expected, but many changes are neither here or there, but completely new developments. Overall it is indeed a new WoD edition, as different as it may be. And again, this isn't exactly H:tV as this was already Hunters Hunted take.

                    Demon wasn't a game meant to wrap up the WoD's metaplot. It is just another game line. And a good one at that, despite not being for everyone's tastes as all the others weren't either. It wasn't severely Abrahamic, either, it was barely more Christian-like than Vampire. It wasn't retconning anything either, just interpreting stuff through its own lenses as every line also did.

                    Changeling directly states that every supernatural that's not a Mage is a Prodigal and the world started in the Dreaming, Mage attributes most or all of what exists to originating from the Consensus at some point, except if you get the individual and equally disparate origins relayed by each individual Paradigm. Werewolf had Fera existing for millions of years despite never even hinting at how it worked before human forms, but everything else is just a spirit or spirit-possessed.

                    It isn't about knowing everything about everyone, about it sure is fun to pretend.

                        On the "Why not Hunters Hunted?" or the more extreme "They're trying to make Reckoning Hunter: the Vigil!" take, I do have a headscratch on that I haven't seen addressed.

                        Going with the craziest assessment that they want to make Vigil, but World this time (which I don't buy), and that for whatever reason they don't think the brand name of Hunters Hunted would draw eyes (I'm more open to this one but I still don't buy it)....Okay, they do realize it's not an either/or scenario right? One of the things proven with the Chronicles franchise is that that Noun: the Gerund branding is recognizable and connective while still allowing a permission for different variations of the Noun-based conceit....and it's not a hard skip over to playing around with that in a particular continuity of material.

                        Again, accepting the most insane and batshit of arguments that I'm not actively proposing is actually the case...Okay, so you have your Hunter: the Reckoning for the Imbued, your angelic backed hunter with lightsaber toothbrushes*, but you could also have your, I dunno, Hunter: The Collapse or whatever that basically covers what Hunters Hunted did but with a Sexy New Title, or hell, even do a third option that might lean more into the Hunter-Slasher Connection in interesting ways, whatever. I can't imagine it being hard to do something akin to "With Hunter: the Collapse, we're taking the foundation laid out by Hunters Hunted and giving it the respect it deserves by elevating it to a full on gameline, complete with [insert cool things here, including influences from Vigil or whatever]." If you assume that they want to gain acclaim with something in the Hunters Hunted/Vigil, doing something like that lets you have your cake and eat it too, because they you get that, with possibly some added cred for making a "new gameline" or at least elevating something, and you still have room to do your angel-backed actors of zealotry and judgment.

                        Or hell, you can do "Hunter: the Reckoning, featuring the Imbued! And Other Types of Hunters!" and just smush Reckoning and Hunters Hunted (and Vigil) together, and then you wouldn't have people wondering why the hell Reckoning doesn't have the gameline's main protagonist/player character in it.

                        As I said, I kind of think it's an extreme read and don't necessarily buy it, but again....the decision to rely on the power of a brand name without relying on the content that backed that name feels even weirder to me and I can't think of a good reason to do that. If I didn't want to make the Imbued, I probably would just make a new(/"new") Hunter and avoid the brand confusion entirely.

                        Like, just. No matter how I look at the decision, I kind of can't avoid coming to the conclusion that almost any other decision would have been easier and have less reprecussion.

                        *Yes, I am being facetitious.
