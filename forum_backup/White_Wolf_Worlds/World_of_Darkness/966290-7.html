    Originally posted by Lord Revan View Post
    Quantumboost Are you telling me that when a technomancer teaches "simple" instructions like, "pull this trigger", "turn this key counter clockwise" is in reality teaching a Path?
    :|

    No.

    If it's "push this button" or "pull this trigger", then the Technomancer has set things up beforehand so that when it comes time the thing will work properly, rather than fail. Device, Charm, delayed effect, whichever. You've either made it fault-tolerant enough to work in brightest day or darkest night, neither snow nor rain nor heat nor random velociraptors, etc. (Prime 2+), or anticipated what is likely to mess up its operation and taken appropriate precautions (Time-delayed or an Entropy blessing or whatever). If you don't do those things or give it enough duration to last until whenever or something, the Effect is as fragile as spun glass.

    What my "you're teaching someone Technosorcery" example is for is when you teach the Sleeper in question "okay, here is how you set up the missile feed, here's how to work the nanofabricator so it makes the missiles, you do this and this and this to make sure it's well-maintained and won't explode in your face when you fire it, here's what you do if the belt jams"? Yeah, that's teaching them genuine legitimate technomagic. And it'll take awhile, because it's superscience, and that shit is fiddly and difficult to learn for most people. It needs to be fiddly and require brilliance or crazy training to reliably pull off, or you have the very contradiction that my entire line of reasoning is entirely dedicated to rectifying.

I've been in labs that have apparatus so sensitive and so temperamental that simply touching them will contaminate them to the point of uselessness. Nothing I did made it go again as the program was stuck in its own internal loop. I had to get one of the mechanics back in to reset the program and start it up as if it was new.

It doesn't matter, the point is that, without training, the sleeper is so likely to break this thing while using it, that it happens every time.

                Etherites would conclude that Sleepers are simply not careful enough, or knowledgeable enough about the delicacy of their works, to use their stuff.

                                        Something else occurred to me: with the use of any ability in which you roll Traits - Abilities, Arete, etc. - there is the possibility of failure. This is inherent to the system.

                                        The iteration that can be reliably used by anyone (who isn't very familiar with the tech and knows how to minimize complications) is called a Device. The iteration that has been refined to the point where the tech is stable, and can be used under most any condition, is a machine accepted into the Consensus.
