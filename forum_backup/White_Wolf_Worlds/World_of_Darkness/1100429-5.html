    Personally I believe that when a mage witnesses a NC warping reality then he can attempt to weaken that ability.
    Whether it is via warding, antimagick or altering the NC directly is not a matter of Lore. It is a matter of spheres.
    If it wasn't then you have to have Lore for other feats of magick as well: “Sorry but your Life 2 is not sufficient to heal yourself, you are lacking the Lore of the body/healing/whatever.“

    Mage is about changing reality.
    To put additional hindrances to the ones already present(Arete, Spheres, successes needed, instruments) makes the game a hassle.

    Important distinction: an attempt means not that there is a success. But if a mage has the relevant spheres he gets a shot at it.

    So, this Zen Master walks up to a hot dog stand and says: "Make me one with everything!"
