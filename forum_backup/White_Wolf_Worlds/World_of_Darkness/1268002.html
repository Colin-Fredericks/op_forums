    Should the use of "NuWW" go?

    11-16-2018, 04:08 PM
    I would like to have a discussion, one which is bothering me of late. Should the use of terms like NuWW go? I have heard allot of terminology thrown about on these forums that seem to be purposefully spreading hate. I could be wrong, but the more I see it the more I get mad as it seems to be driven by a need to divide and marginalize. I personally feel marginalized by it. That because I like the direction that WW has taken and when I see someone use NuWW when they could have just said WW I fell like I am being viewed by association as something evil or a part of the problem. When people use speech to separate they create an us verses them mentality. Many times that wording could be avoided all together.

    You don't need to say NuWW or Paradox wolf and such. You can just say WW. To me the use is similar to things like obamacare or trumpcare or whatever. They are meant to judge, lump-in, marginalize, and trivialize groups of people or ideals bundle them up with all sorts of emotions into a single word and they are not helpful, they are in essence spiteful and a form of passive aggressiveness that is used to attack and stifle not inform or grow.

    You can just say WW, people are intelligent enough to understand who you are talking about.

    I short I think that people should respect one another and this does not seem respectful to people in my mind and you can say you don't like something without using this kind of speech.

    Thoughts?
