          What you and your player are most keen on is probably the most useful answer, but to generalise:

          One-on-one games can be scaled-down versions of regular games with or without NPC allies forming a "party" around the player's character or the player controlling more than one, but they often use the solo nature to emphasise the isolation and vulnerability of the central character, or their power and versatility. (For examples from other games, there are solo Call Of Cthulhu adventures built heavily around isolation and there are also one-on-one rules for playing elite spies in James Bond 007 while groups are advised to play rookie agents.) It's also a chance play that loner or "special snowflake" character who wouldn't fit a group game.

          Mortals, vampires and wraiths are well suited to horror games about one character alone and possibly vulnerable in an uncaring or hostile world. Mages have an advantage of versatility in their use of power and are suited to games about powerful solo protagonists. Werewolves and Changelings have a stronger social structure, but still have room for loners - the cancelled computer game Werewolf: The Apocalypse - The Heart Of Gaia featured a "lost cub" as the player's character. Hunters and Demons are both "new arrivals" to the classic World Of Darkness with little support and little reason to trust others, so they could suit solo games as well - Vampire: The Requiem developer and Demon: The Descent creator Rose Bailey posted Actual Play reports of a Demon: The Fallen game using a system hack in 2003, for example.

              I've done Vampire and Wraith as solo games before. Both worked well. But it can be taxing on both parties.

              The idea of one player running a troupe of characters might also work. Lifting the model off Ars Magica somewhat, you could create a vampire/ghost/mage/changeling, and then build around them a cast of ghouls/fetters/familiars/chimerical companions. The ST can play these for the most part, but when the protagonist goes into slumber or torpor or a seeking, or goes off to find Transcendence/Ascension/Golconda, etc, the player can play one of the other characters.
