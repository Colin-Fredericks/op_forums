    12-10-2021, 04:39 PM
    I have this idea of having a wristwatch that is both an unique instrument and a wonder. Losing an unique instrument is almost as bad as dying. So, I wonder if this unique thing is a good idea.

    Anyway, the spheres my mage has are not very good to protect it (Forces, Life, Prime). No Matter and Prime is only 1. So how could I guarantee the watch will be safe?

    Just as focus can change, familiars can be made, can am unique instrument be replaced by another on the long run?

                              A mage can make something that a mentor/etc. enchants into a Wonder.

                              And what I mean as a matter of magical style is that the instrument still has to match the mage's style. Just because a watch is a valid option for an instrument doesn't meant every mage can use watches as instruments. If a watch doesn't fit your mage's magical practice, it's not going to be a unique instrument no matter how much it has deep meaning for them. Or, in other words, you don't have to make something a unique instrument (and thus the risks/rewards associated with it) just because your character cares about it personally. In the Harry Potter example, wands are part of the magical style. Potterverse wizards don't have to use one specific wand to use magic, but wands are a vital part of magical practice (not getting into the really dumb stuff added after the initial books); but there's a whole thing about matching the right wand to the right wizard/witch for the maximum resonance (not quite a unique instrument outside of very special cases like the Elder Wand).

                              For protection? Well, not with your starting Spheres. The problem is that we're talking about Mage. If another mage realizes you have a unique instrument and wants to destroy it, there are a lot of ways they can go about it. Correspondence (I know) and Prime are the most generalist approaches. Which Prime you can consecrate an item to yourself, which makes it much easier to keep hold of it (no separating it from you by tossing you into the Umbra) and get it back with Correspondence. Entropy is another options here (you can make it harder to break, even if not as well as Matter can, and have more tricks for getting it back if taken by force or theft in the moment).

                                              The watch being a Periapt that has a big pool of quintessence lets you toss -1 to -3 at a spell, and the cost can be handled in downtime rather that in the heat of the moment.

                                              But that's not going to cover the "bond" thing.

                                              Yes, there's unique instrument or the Phylactery Flaw (and your phylactery is a unique instrument inherently).
                                              You could have an nontraditional familiar that's a watch with secret functions.
                                              If you can think of something, the watch could be part of a Legend (which increases your quintessence access and spending that for difficulty reductions).
                                              The Wonder could be a Talisman with a literal piece of your mage's soul in it (those are the ones that can cast independently of the mage); esp since a more powerful mage would have to do this for them, that connection can have some mechanical effects.

                                              The thing about Forces for a shield:

                                              1) Vulgar.
                                              2) Only stops energy. It isn't going to do anything if your instrument gets dropped in a vat of acid. It isn't going to protect against Correspondence theft, Entropy decay, or Prime destruction spells.
                                              3) Isn't selective without more Spheres involved (you're going to need to drop the shield or cast extra magic just to charge/wind the watch).
                                              4) Countermagic can degrade or remove the shield, and the ease of it being obvious magic to Sleepers will also degrade it as well (or saddle you with Permadox or something similar).

                                                      I'll get right to the point.

                                                      Unique Instrument doesn't have to be Unique in the literal sense. You must have an idea on how you recover/recreate it in the case it is destroyed.

                                                      Now, if your watch is really irreplaceable and you can't explain how it fits your magic style, it clearly isn't fit for a Unique Instrument.

                                                      And the -1 dif is good, but not that good. If you can't answer those questions easily (how it fits style, how to replace it), then it isn't worth the effort. You'll have a lot of other potential sources for dif reductions in practice, don't overthink this one. It is broad and risky by design.

                                                      Keep in mind that even if you were a Master in all Spheres, if a Mage decides they will destroy your watch no matter what, then it will probably be destroyed. So yeah, it should be replaceable somehow.

                                                      Some examples of Unique Instruments:

                                                      1 - A wand made at exact specifications regarding wood, inlays of metal alloys, the time of construction and the tiny crystal in its end. The Mage believes the gestures and magic sigils must be made with a wand built under specifications derived from your Astral Map, if they lose the wand they'll have to calculate new specifications for it to be made in the next New Moon;

                                                      2 - For another anything will do... after a proper consecrating ritual. Their magic is enacted through the blessings of a Bishop enacted in a ceremony similar to the dedication of a church, and only one object may be so blessed and consecrated to them at a time. If or when the current one is lost, they'll have to atone for their sin and seek a Bishop with an entire morning of free time to enact the lengthy ritual;

                                                      3 - One works magic through infusions made with a plant watered with their own blood. They have a patch of those in their garden and a bag of dried leaves with themselves. If they lose those somehow, they'll have to procure the right seeds, find suitable land, and start the process of cultivation anew, no Instrument available until the first plants mature enough.

                                                      ​For an actually unique Instrument I agree with Heavy Arms that you're better with a Phylactery. It makes more sense and is better because you're actually refunded points for your trouble.
