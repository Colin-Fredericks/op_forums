                                            And now I'm back. There was a kerfluffle with my computer trying to set up a something I could host pictures on and chat rooms. But my laptop went kaput in the middle of it.

                                            All better now and I can fix the pictures I tried to post earlier in the thread. And now I've also backed Mage 20th and can have a spiffy backer badge like LH and Torakhan. ^_^.

                                            Back to Merch. I think the Kindred action figures and Ral Partha minis totally count as Merchandise. In fact I have quite a few of those as well. I think they could have picked better poses for the character minis. Right now a lot of really awesome minis called Lupines are being produced by Reaper Miniatures that captures some of the Shamanism in Werewolf quite well. But it's not technically WW merch. I think of WW's stuff, the minis of the Nexus Crawlers were the best. Unfortunately there were a lot of packaging errors that affected me and other gamers buying NCs. I was so frustrated because it's already a pricy mini but I had to write to the Ral Partha company twice because they sent me a blister that was missing two legs and then had the left side legs doubled. I wound up with 3 Nexus Crawlers though. One with 2 legs and some bent metal legsI made myself, then one with 4 left feet I had to modify to make stand and one complete one. I know of no other mini that gave me so much assembly trouble. They look awesome though.

                                                    Finally able to post a pic of my Dice, and yet every time I think I have them all I realize I'm missing something. Ah well. Here it is. It's always growing.


                                                    image sharing

                                                    The dice I own are my attempts at a set for many themes. Several dice were given to me by friends, otherwise I would have only D10s in my collection. the dragon bag on the top has all the non D10s. Next to them are the New WoD dice sets. i would have the Hunter set as well, but I gave that to a friend for Christmas since he enjoys Hunter much more than I do.

                                                    I have 2 different Werewolf bags. The one with the red claw marks was given to me by a friend in Spain and the claw marks seemed to be thermo print rather than paint printed on. He colored them red. The end of one of the marks peeled off, which looking at the other bag I got later on doesn't seem possible with the paint print. The Mage bag has seen better days though the dice it came with were plain purple translucent. I got have some of the purple first run mage dice. They are so very plain looking.

                                                    I forgot about the Glasswalker Leather dice bag I got from someone off of Ebay and forgot about my set of the Time of Judgement dice that are still in the package on my game book shelf. My set of black red-tipped dice are still missing. Nearly all the sets here are complete save for a few random speckled dice and the 5 dice orphans of official WW sets like Mage and Werewolf.

                                                    I debate starting a new thread on just the different dice that WW sold and others that could possibly substitute for CWoD themes discussing all the manufacturers and characteristics of each set, but that might just be something I'm interested in. I do have a question for all out there. Has anyone seen or own a Wraith: the Oblivion dice set with a dice bag? I've only ever found photos of box sets and wraith tubes of dice.
