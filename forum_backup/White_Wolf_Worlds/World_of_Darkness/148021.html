    Hunters are nutcases.

    05-24-2014, 03:23 PM
    I got stuck in traffic today, and I happen to be reading Hunter-book: Martyr, so I figured, "Why not think about it?" Especially since, as anyone who's read it knows, Joan is a complete nutcase. But then I got to thinking - really, all hunters are.

    A decade or two ago (I don't remember exactly) there was a story in Illinois, where I lived at the time, that some guy was arrested for parking on the side of the road and killing his kid(s -I don't remember how many he had), and when the cops arrested him, he said God had told him to do it. That sounds almost exactly like a pretty large percentage of hunters out there in the world. In particular, there's a guy in that book who considers killing his kid because he may have been turned into a vampire (apologies if you consider that a spoiler... given the nature of the books, it really shouldn't be). His excuse, if he gave it, would probably be something like, "he was a monster," which sounds a lot like "God told me to do it," to me. And there are hunters who would probably use those exact words, too (Joan comes to mind, and so does Crusader).

    So the question becomes, how does a hunter know the difference between "He was wrong because I'm schizophrenic" and "He was wrong because the Messengers told me so"? Waywards don't seem to be able to make that distinction in some cases, and some other Extremists suffer the same problem. And what if two guys get together and both say, "I killed my kid because he was a demon." One of them is a hunter, but the other's just insane. There's no real way for the hunter to determine that the other man isn't a hunter, so ... what happens? Or does he find out he's working with a (worse) nut job when they find themselves in the presence of a real monster?
