        maybe it stills just does what it does,
        It fundamentally cannot because it is trapped.

        it's just desperate because the weaver made it impossible for him do destroy things the old fashioned way, so he corrupts the shit out of all, trying to do his work.
        That's one of the three ways that the pieces of the Wyrm, the Triatic Wyrms, work. The other two being thrashing around madly (Beast of War) and trying to consume everything (Eater of Souls). Yet, still, it remains bound, and the cosmos remains twisted, and the mental breaks that the three Triatic Wyrms faced gave rise to the Maeljin Incarnae and numerous lesser manifestations of the Unmaker.

        No one really knows how to *deal* with the apocalypse, let alone what it means.
        That's not really true. One of the essay points that Ethan Skemp wrote for the 2nd edition player's guide is that, if the shapeshifters actually did their inborn jobs, that would probably change the state of things a lot. Unfortunately, they don't have the numbers for that or the cooperation, so, again, the world is twisted.

        Also, if you look in to the other changing breeds, everyone tells a little different story. The bastet have their own version of the triad, the Ratkin think that the Weaver is the problem and so on
        The Bastet have different names for what amount to the same beings, and add on a distinction between the corruption that surrounds the Wyrm (Asura) and the Wyrm (Cahlash). Hence, it takes a bit longer for them to get burned when they start playing with the fire that is the favor of the Unmaker. The Ratkin are also not wrong, though the three books of the Triat established some time ago that no members of the Triat are, strictly speaking, your friend; even the Wyld, the weakened third member, is still a dangerous force of nature that can warp your form and spirit. That's why Garou don't toelrate Wyld Thresholds when they break into caerns. The Changers collectively call them Abcesses, and some of them have a rite to cut them out and let them float off back into the Umbra to break through in a place that isn't a sacred font of Gaia's power.

        Witness also the Beast Courts and Ahadi; even extremely different Changers know that they have parts to play about the same goal, and they don't really argue about the Garou's particular beliefs when the Garou aren't trying to kill everyone else. As a matter of fact, they seem to share them.

        (Footnota Bene: Fera is the term that Garou use for non-Garou Changers. Its use replaced the awkward use of the Mokole term Bete among the Garou, from past editions, and for good reason, because Bete is the term that Mokole use for all non-Mokole Changers)

                        A 15th generation could potentially become a 4th generation through adoption (From Marduk's Throat) or diablerie (the difference is a difference of degree rather than a difference of kind).                         Celestines are not spirits, they are physical objects with spiritual reflections so great that they exists in both the Aetherial Realm of the Middle Umbra and the personal Realm of the Deep Umbra. Supernals are likewise not spirits, they are principles of Reality that form the foundations of the Physical and Spiritual Worlds, whose spiritual reflections for the Reaches of the Deep Umbra. Whether or not they are principles local to the Solar System or are universal principles of Reality just depends of the ST.
