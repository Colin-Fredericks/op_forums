    Ideas for *Unconventional* uses of Entropy

    11-02-2016, 04:36 AM
    Greetings, all!

    I'm trying to come up with unconventional ideas of how to use Entropy. Not the more banal and straightforward dice-tweaking, damage-boosting things that are given as examples in the corebooks, but rather things that rely on a deeper reading of the things this Sphere can do, especially at modest levels. For instance, since it seems to allow measuring the levels of order and chaos in everything, I figured that it can be a good thing to identify whether events, things etc. are the way they are through coincidence (chaos) or have been made so with a reason (order), thus discerning true clues from red herrings and conspiracies from natural social occurrences; with Mind, perhaps it would also be able to add detection of intent behind events and things, making it possible to detect which clues have been left accidentally, and which ones on purpose (e.g. to mislead the investigation or to deliberately give a message). Another idea is to use Entropy's ability to detect weakness in ideas when combined with Mind; casting this on the plan the party comes up with seems like a nice way of detecting (and later fixing) the flaws in such a plan. Measuring the amount of disorder in a thing (possibly with an addition of Science and Mind1:Empowerment) seems like a roundabout way of measuring its temperature if one lacks Forces. I'm not knowledgeable enough about cryptology IRL, but perhaps detection of order and disorder can somehow be used for decryption and for detection of steganography? Going further, it seems to me divinating the original message from its irreversibly entropified form (e.g. CRC numbers, MD5, a radio signal subject to lots of interference/jamming etc.) falls under this Sphere, but I'm not sure where; it's nominally a sensing/analysis level, but the books seem vague about what levels of analysis correspond to what dot numbers.

    What other Entropy effect ideas would fit right in among these ones?

    If it matters, I'm asking because I'm contemplating taking the first (and eventually the second) dot of Entropy in a Revised campaign set in 1925 New York, PBD/HAP, for a character with Mind 3 and Correspondence 3. But I'm not trying to restrict the discussion to this subset of rotes, just hoping that some of them will be useful for my situation.

    Thanks in advance!

          You know, I've been thinking for a while that Entropy 3's ability to affect predictable patterns was too narrowly focused around machines. It could probably be used to degrade or bolster the function of Systems. Anything (non-organic or mental, obviously) that relies on multiple interlocking parts, and a standardized set of steps, could be affected by this level of Entropy. Machines are just the first part. Another would be computer programs - increasing efficiency or fostering glitches and bugs.

          But still another would be human organizations. Businesses, government departments, construction projects. Every person is meant to play a part, and coordinate with each other to maximize success in their goals. And while Entropy can't affect the people's bodies until 4 or their minds until 5, Entropy 3 could perhaps alter the interactions between them. Combined with a general manipulation of probability, and it can cause these Systems to either work very well, or very poorly. Remove obstacles and chance encounters that disrupt group cohesion, or create them. Make a memo come right on time, or very late (assuming the memo reaches its destination at all, rather than getting lost in the bureaucratic process). In much the same way that a machine relies on every part doing its job, or a computer program relies on complex data exchanges working out, so too do human-centric Systems rely on the interplay of communication and competence.

          I could imagine a Syndicate rep using team building exercises, motivational posters, and HR techniques to make his businesses run smoothly. Or send agents, emails, and rumors to disrupt a rival business's operations.

          Or an Order of Hermes wizard casting a spell to make certain documents of theirs get expedited through a system. Tremere vampires have rituals devoted to making their own bits of bureaucratic work get processed in a timely fashion (or documents that are bad for them take much longer), so it's not a stretch for Hermetic Mages to do the same. It's about making an ordered system bend to the caster's Will.

              Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
              There's a Revised Rote... I'm pretty sure from Hollowers TB... that combines Entropy and Correspondence to ensure you can stay on a path to get to a specific place/thing/etc. It follows a lot of the thing you're having with things like combining it with Mind for decryption (but I'm pretty sure Mind lets you do that by itself at some level), or as a way to avoid red herrings.
              I know that the Sorcerer's Crusade book, the Order of Reason, allows one to decipher a coded message with just Entropy 1 (well, Fortune 1, but semantics). Presumably this works with both messages coded with Mind 1, and mundane ciphers. If I recall correctly, Hermetic House Fortunae has as its last test before full membership be solving an equation so complex, you need Entropy magic to guess the answer (because it's otherwise impossible to resolve).

              Going back to the Order of Reason book, they have tons of uses described for Entropy. Entropy 1 can be used by those trained in Medicine, Investigation, or Larceny to examine a corpse, and figure out facts surrounding the person's death. How they died, who may have killed them, and even certain traits about said killer. The book says that it can't go as far back in time as, well, Time 2, but it's up to you as to whether it treads too heavily on Time's toes here. On the other hand, the Spheres inherently overlap in places, and Entropy does this more than any of them. This level can also be used to sense signs of necromancy on the body (like, for instance, if the corpse was animated as a zombie).

              Another OoR power is using Entropy 2 to enhance one's stealth. Basically, the user knows exactly when and where to move to take advantage of crowds of pedestrians, guards looking away, or the outbreak of distracting goings-on.

              The last I can find is a conjunctional Effect called Craft Tome. With Entropy 3 + Mind 1, the user weaves messages into a work, whether a treatise or story, an art piece, or the placement of objects/buildings/shrubberies in a given area. Presumably, a more modern rendition could use this for website layouts, feng shui, or a film. Unlike the ciphering magic from above, this uses symbolism spread throughout the work to carry its message, rather than just a code. Also according to this rote, an observer rolls Arete (no specified Sphere required, just being Awakened is enough to untangle it). Although the book didn't specify a particular difficulty for such a roll, so take from that what you will. This Effect does mean that a modern Mage writer could release a fantasy novel, with a hidden message readable by Awakened people. And use it to feed information to newly Awakened people, looking for answers, telling them where to go for help. And it's a fantasy novel, so Technocrats would never read it.

                  -The places where students choose to sit sit are altered: the most oblivious and chronically unprepared guy is directly in front of the professor; the most knowledgeable and outspoken student just happens to sit near between two girls who take away his attention from the lecture; the girl who will have to get out of the room three times sits right in the corner blocked by four other people; and in the center sits a guy who will just happen to open a shaken bottle of coca-cola, twice.

                                              With Entropy 5 you could create a willing Gaes that is combined with other Entropy effects - as long as someone fulfills a condition(s), they gain some benefit(s). The benefits could come from Entropy, or another Sphere. Breaking the condition(s) - without first ending the Gaes through an out - cancels the benefits and brings bad consequences. (Multiple possible outs could be done, as well.)
                                              Gaeses that project an Affect Thought:

                                              Syndicate's I'll give you this one warning
                                              As long as the Syndic carries out every threat he makes if someone proceeds despite being warned, he inspires great fear and great respect. Nobody will be able to rally support against him without first confronting him in some way publicly.
                                              If softness is shown, the Syndic's reputation suffers greatly.
                                              As an out, the Syndic must organize a massacre of either his enemies, rivals, or those overly critical of him. As an out for poltics-oriented Syndics, several opponents could be manipulated into long jail sentences. In the case of business, rivals could be made example of by bringing them to utter financial ruin and public humiliation.

                                              Traditions' The Benefit of Doubt
                                              As long as the character tells no lies or misleading truths, any lies spread about him will be met with disbelief and won't be taken seriously.
                                              Lying or being misleading makes the character inherently suspicious to even his close companions.
                                              As an out, a dangerous enemy has to be honestly informed of the character's deepest desires and worries.

                                              Hollow Ones' Twilight Bonds
                                              As long as the Hollow One stays true to his Cainite friend(s), other Cainites will be disinclined to be hostile towards the Hollow One, unless provoked.
                                              Neglecting or betraying the friendship, or provoking Cainites, brings attention of whole coteries and/or elder Cainites on the caster. (This rote can be used in this way, by being purposefully broken, to try to receive the Embrace. Or end up being Ghouled and Dominated...) Breaking the Gaes also causes the character to suffer pangs of guilt for a time (depending how long the effect lasted), and a burning desire to make up by being closer with Cainites...
                                              There's no (known) out - friendship with a vampire is forever, after all!
                                              (Perhaps an Archmaster of Prime and/or Entropy could help, but such a person, unless Hollow One themselves, would not likely help in such a situation...)

                                              Cultists' of Ecstasy and Virtual Adepts' Just passing through...
                                              As long as the character carries no weapons and minds his own business, he won't be attacked, and any violence that would occur in his presence will be defused easily - at least for a time. (Usually while the character is present.) This is often used by Mages while getting high quality (or quantity) drugs, weapons or information; while reducing the risks that come from associating with the underworld or governments.
                                              Making demands, arming yourself (this includes carrying foci for offensive effects), nosing around, or being hostile break the charm.
                                              As an out, the character must undertake a mortal risk for the sake of greatly helping a stranger.

                                              General luck Gaes:
                                              Cultists' of Ecstasy Right on the edge
                                              As long as the character makes no breaks in being high, he will benefit from great luck in regards to securing drugs. The luck is varied - it can mean finding money or drugs, winning small lotteries or slot machines, or meeting generous people. Drug users will gladly share what they have with them; new dealers might approach them, and quickly treat them as close friends. The character also won't be searched by the police, as long as he shows no fear of them.
                                              Not consuming any drug for over ten hours* brings bad luck on the character's finances for a long time, and also causes him to suffer a serious health problem that might turn chronic. People who were previously friendly towards him might become apprehensive or even hostile. Profiting from drug sales or smuggling also breaks the Gaes; unless it involves relatively small amounts.
                                              *This time can vary by a few hours, depending on the drug habits of a particular person.
                                              As an out, the character must help two other persons quit an addiction to drugs, and make sure they don't relapse for a time - the length of which depends on how long the character was riding on the edge.

                                              Dark Ages: Mage has similar effects for the Spirit-Talker Trickster Pillar. At high rating, it can bring luck and misfortune, and it can be channeled through 'foolish risks' and 'ridiculous gambits'. Ideally, such beneficial self-Gaeses would be available at lower levels, but I'm unsure if this is ok, considering that Gaes is available only at level 5.
