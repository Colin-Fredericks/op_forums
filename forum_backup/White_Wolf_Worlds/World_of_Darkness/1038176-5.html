                        Then why does the Technocracy, which shapes the Consensus of the entire mortal population, allow such things to happen? The Technocracy controls the Western governments that overthrow the democratically elected governments of the developing world, so why would they allow the governments they control to overthrow democratic and progressive governments if they cared one whit about the Masses? The Technocracy controls (or controlled) the dictatorships that allied with the Western governments, so why would not spread the technology to allow for reproductive freedom beyond the elites of the developing countries if they cared one whit about the Masses?

                        And the idea of subjective reality does not exist when you are talking about cWoD crossovers, which is the point of this thread, when we are comparing the effectiveness of Changelings and Mages to represent the ideals of Science Fiction. Physics does not work differently in the non-West than it does in the West in CtD, VtM, or WtA, just in MtA, which suggests that it is the nature of Mages that interferes with technology that they think should not exist rather than the nature of Reality, at least when you are talking about a crossover. In fact, the idea that science or technology would work differently in different locations is completely antithetical to the ideas of Science Fiction, which means that CtD, VtM, and WtA are closer to the ideals of Science Fiction than MtA, which suggests that MtA is really not appropriate for Science Fiction. However, the idea that science and technology is suppressed by 'primitive' beliefs is a great excuse for the Technocracy to justify its genocidal atrocities, mundane or supernatural, that it is responsible for outside of the West.
