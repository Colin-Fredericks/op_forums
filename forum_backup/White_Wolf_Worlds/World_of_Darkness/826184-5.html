    The Disparate Alliance

    Most of these guys were easily bought and sold but lacked a head for business. Still some of them might be useful.

    ​The Ahl-I-Batini

    ​They call us Nephandi. Do they ever look at themselves?

    ​The Bata'as

    ​Street voodoo mages. We've made money off them in ways that annoyed them, much as we did the Verbene. But if these folks are streetwise, maybe we can still forge ties.

    The Children of Knowledge

    ​Consciousness expansion never made money. Drug addicts aren't much use to us.

    ​The Hollow Ones

    Lifestyles like theirs requires cities and markets. But they still look down on us. Fashionistas and they don't even know fashion can't exist without markets.


    The Kopa Loei

    They seem to have set themselves good goals. But I can't see them wanting to work with us.

    The Ngoma

    ​They are healers and teachers. They have an intellectual view of life and society. We might be able to connect. It will take work but it would be worthwhile.

    The Sisters of Hippolyta , ​Taftani , The Templar Knights

    Irrelevant by choice, all of them.

    Wu-Lung

    ​Smooth trouble seeking someone to use. But people like that can be used. Tricksters will often trick themselves.

                    ​The Ahl-I-Batini

                    ​This city is a lifeboat. But if we are to survive, we must take charge of the boat. You can't save everybody, you must choose. For centuries we warned the others, they refused to listen. They are the dupes of the fallen. There is no room for them in the lifeboat.

                    This city was built as an expression of unjustified hope and a celebration of a misplaced tolerance. The Wizard has a good heart, but he is, in the end, an Avatar of innocence. He must be killed or driven away after we no longer need him.

                    We will need allies.

                    ​The Bata'as

                    Street mages that serve the people. They lack the discipline and ​focus they desperately need. But we can still make use of them.

                    The Children of Knowledge

                    ​We thought they'd be a source of contacts in Western society. As the West no longer exists we find they have little use.

                    ​The Hollow Ones

                    They always get more done than it seems they could. Still, their idealism is a burden.


                    The Kopa Loei

                    They have worthy goals as far as they go. But if they and their people survive they'll simply be burdens later on.

                    The Ngoma

                    Commendable in so many ways. But they don't serve our vision of Unity and their other uses are obsolete.

                    The Sisters of Hippolyta

                    Useful for misdirecting people's attention with their bitter and divisive politics. Useful dupes.

                    ​Taftani

                    These rogues had useful knowledge of the Jinni, otherwise pointless.

                    The Templar Knights

                    Easily gulled into being shock troops for us.

                    Wu-Lung

                    ​As they are narrowly focused on China we can probably use them as allies for a time. But they will have to be eliminated later.

                                        The Bata'as

                                        We were never even civil to the "Nine Traditions" but when everything fell apart and the mad gods started eating people they threw us a lifeline. Now we live in their city and they treat us with respect.

                                        That said this place is so sweetly charming it makes me queasy. There is no poverty. The Man doesn't want to push you around. They want to hear what you have to say. But this whole city is like stew without salt or pepper.

                                        Still, once we get more people here, that will change. And they want us to help them get more people. And they say they didn't get enough of the people we lived among and worked with. Could we bring those people others would leave behind.

                                        I think we can work together.

                                        The Ahl-I-Batini

                                        They are so very clever. What's worse is they know they are very clever. As noxious as it is I can't tell you how glad I am that these assassins think we're stupid.



                                        The Children of Knowledge

                                        I have used drugs to open my mind and more fully commune with the spirits. I can respect these chemists. But I also think they take foolish risks.

                                        The Hollow Ones

                                        It is amazing to me that any group of people that are this annoying can get so much done. There are solid reasons we teamed up with them


                                        The Kopa Loei

                                        Like us they were abused and cheated. Like us they stand up for their own against others. They have a plan and goals. As both their plans and their goals make sense I think we can learn from them.

                                        The Ngoma

                                        These are the heirs of Ancient Africa's high mystics and philosophers. When our ancestors were condemned to the Middle Passage they were contemplating their navels. I have issues with them.

                                        Plus, I lived in America. My mentor was from Haiti. My best friend in my circle was from Brazil. Africa is wonderful, but it isn't everything.

                                        The Sisters of Hippolyta

                                        They occasionally make good points. Rare occasions. These women have substance and they have commitment. Now if they only had manners.

                                        Taftani

                                        They try to be storybook wizards in the real world. They achieve more than you would expect. But when you see the effort they put in for the results they get, it seems so pointless.

                                        The Templar Knights

                                        Preachers with guns and knives. Lots of guns and knives. They are pious. Their piety is real and profound. But they are still insufferable.

                                        Find something dangerous that needs killing and sic them on it. But as nasty as these bullies are, never waste a useful resource.

                                        Wu-Lung

                                        Once these were princes in China. Scholars, aristocrats, advisors to the imperial court. In other words, the Man in China. These racists, and never doubt their racism, they see others as expendable. Never forget that.
