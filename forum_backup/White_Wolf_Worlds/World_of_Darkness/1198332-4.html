    YAY! I think Demon gets a bad rap.

    First off, I'm an atheist. Demon's backstory is no more real to me than any other fantasy or mythological tale. Despite the fact that I've never associated myself with any religion, even as a child, I have always associated demons with Paradise Lost. So for me, there was no other option for the foundation of DTF.

    I also do not see DTF as inherently Christian. If anything, at best, it's Abrahamic, which links it to Judaism, Christianity, and Islam, but even then, that's looking at things backwards. The basic setup of Demon is Paradise Lost, but Paradise Lost isn't about Moses, Jesus, or Muhammad. The story predates the entire concept of prophets and holy books.

    The way I make sense of things is, the backstory in DTF happened. Then the Fallen were thrown in the Abyss and sealed away. The world then continued to form and evolve. Human ended up being caught in the middle of a tug of war between pretty much every supernatural faction ever created. Most of the religions of the world, including Christianity, were influenced or in some cases outright created by uber powerful beings, such as the Earthbound, Lucifer, Methuselahs, and the like. Lucifer promoted, if not outright created, Christianity to combat the Earthbound who relied on peganism.

    So I don't think DTF says 'Christians are right.' None of the backstory of DTF is in the Old Testement, New Testement, or the Quran. In the World of Darkness, those religions have pieces of the puzzle at best.

    Also, if you really wanted to strip all that way, you could completely remove the stuff about the War in Heaven and have Demons have no memory of the time before the Abyss. Or create your own backstory. It's a pretty adaptable set up. You could easily take the WtA or Mage cosmology and fit Demon into it.

    Keep in mind that these represent only my own personal opinions.

                            I've renounced Christianity and I am a Classical Greco-Roman Pagan (I used to be an atheist before that) but I utterly despise Demon: The Fallen for many reasons.

                            The chief reasons being the fact that WW proposed Demon's cosmology as the "One True Way" cosmology for the World of Darkness for a while. The fact that it depends on the Revised Edition metaplot (which I also strongly dislike) doesn't help matters either.

                            Then there's the material found in the Earthbound book that basically states that the evil Earthbound invented Paganism, which is eerily similar to the whole "Pagan Gods are actually evil Demons" argument that a lot of fundamentalist Christians put forward, and that I have had to put up with in real life from my backwards Neo-Puritan relatives.

                            Seriously, my family was so Puritanical that not only did they ban Halloween for having Pagan roots, they also banned Christmas and Easter for having Pagan roots when I was a kid. So, naturally I do not like Demon: The Fallen or its setting assumptions. It's like a bad crossover fanfiction between Paradise Lost, Menmoch The Devil, and Chick Tracts.

                            In my World of Darkness head-canon, Demon: The Fallen does not exist and neither does its cosmology (I also strike down KOTE and Wraith gets treated as a very loose "Broad Strokes" form of canon). While there was definitely a First Vampire, nobody knows if it was really Caine or not. In my games, Noddism and the whole Caine legend came about during the Early Middle Ages as Christianity became more prevalent among Kindred.

                            BUT if you like Demon and want it in your games, that's fine with me. More power to you. You do you and I'll do me.

                            Just don't tell me I'm playing the game wrong for rejecting Demon and its Abrahamic-inspired cosmology.
