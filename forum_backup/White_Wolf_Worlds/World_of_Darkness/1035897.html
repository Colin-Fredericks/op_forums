    Advice on how to port a Mage: The Awakening hombrew to Ascension.

    01-21-2017, 08:11 PM

It was basically a antagonist idea for Mage: The Awakening called Project Prometheus, a scientific organization dedicated to studying Magical Artifacts thinking they are a way to unlock the 90% of our brains we don't use. They viewed Mages as Psychics and believed that finding Atlantis would be the key to becoming like them. Here is a link to the thread if you need more information. http://forum.theonyxpath.com/forum/m...-idea-for-mage

                          Originally posted by Killerclown View Post
                          I knew that the Arcanum exists. The difference between them and Prometheus is methodology. The Arcanum probably uses occultic knowledge to study magic while Prometheus uses the Scientific Method. Also, would the whole stealing Magic artifacts thing annoy Mages?
                          Depends which artifacts and which mages.

                          If you're guys are stealing wonders, the whoever owns those wonders, be it mage, cabal, or chantry, are understandably going to be annoyed by this.
                          If they are stealing something more important, then it depends who it's important. The Cult of Ecstasy, for example, aren't going to be all that concerned if a Hermetic Orb of Power is removed or if a Sacred Scroll from an Akashic temple is removed.
                          Similarly, few other mages are going to be bothered if someone steals an Ecstatic's stash of superdrugs.

                          Unless it's an artefact which is really powerful, it's unlikely to garner cross-tradition interest. And if it is, there will be enough people trying to claim it for themselves. The Nephandi, for example.

                                          The way magic works in Ascension is that any framework for magic is good enough, so long as the willworker believes it thoroughly. So if the mage thinks that using magic is all about accessing the hidden potential of their brain through the use of mystic artefacts, then great. That's a paradigm.

                                          What would other mages think? Depends very much on who you ask. Some Akashic's would be all for the idea of achieving magic via accessing hidden functions of the mind, though some would be against the idea of the 90% thing, stating that this was the wrong way to go about it, that it wasn't about achieving "more" brain function, but about achieving a better "balance" in brain function. How you use the brain is more important than how much your brain is doing.

                                          Etherites could be all for this as well, and may invent all manner of helmets or tonics to try and "tap in" to this hidden potential. Other's may have dismissed the notion as too convenient to be true, preferring instead their own theories.

                                          There may be a number of Ecstatics who use drugs specifically because they think it allows them to tap into that hidden brain power, but a lot of them would say it's not about brain, it's about soul and feel, getting in line with the vibes. Other's might say that 10% is too much, and to truly get in touch with the universe you need to stop thinking, use 0% of the brain and become truly unshackled by your thoughts.

                                                          The Technocratic Union's knowledge and control are often overstated. Sure, they have fingers in many pies and vast information technologies, but they aren't omniscient. If they were, the Ascension War would have ended long ago. Especially if the group is small and covert, the Technocracy might not know they even exist.

                                                          If they are aware of Project Prometheus, the next thing to deduce is how much they know, which in turn dictates how much they care. If the Union knows their beliefs but not their activities, they may write them off as more crackpots, to be either ignored or made marginalized within the academic community. The Union does not have infinite time, manpower, or resources to go chasing down every fringe group.

                                                          This tune could change if they realize Project Prometheus is going around, stealing superstitionist artifacts. The Union is unhappy enough with the Traditions having such things. But at least they know (in as much a way that any superstitionist could "know" anything) how their stuff works. Random sleepers have no idea what dangers they could bring down on themselves, innocent bystanders, or the Consensus by meddling with forces beyond their ken. Their involvement in the affairs of the larger Ascension War could also cause these sleepers to become full-fledged superstitionists in and of themselves, to either join the Traditions or remain a disconnected thorn in the Union's side. Such things cannot be permitted.
