                                                          Originally posted by Nonsense View Post
                                                          1. I didn't compare the recreational MA with Do. I wanted to point to the original Pankration.
                                                          The original pankration was the MMA of its day, with people tired of traditional boxing and wrestling wanting something more exciting. And being primarily a sport, it both had rules, and would not equate to having a large degree of the mentality taught in styles meant for real-world application. There was also military pankration, but it was treated as a back-up to fighting with regular weapons like modern military combat systems do today. Tales of the mental toughness of military pankration probably had far more to do with the fact anyone reduced to using it was already in a desperate situation and fighting for their life; not because the training system imparted some special degree of focus or mental toughness.

                                                          Why are Akashayana superior to every other Martial Arts practicioner. The Wu Lung, Warrior Shamans, Viking Verbenae, House Flambeau why are these inferiour in hand to hand combat?
                                                          Not really the point of the thread. And as one of my players learned very quickly when they thought about how Do > MA or Brawl, they realized that they all suck against people with guns and range anyway. The reason Do was allowed to be awesome when nobody else gets awesome unarmed fighting stuff like that is a mixture of Orientalism and not really giving a shit because weapons and magic are head and shoulders better anyway.

                                                          4. The only good thing they did was the cap at 2 at chargen. At least assuming starting level characters it stands to reason that
                                                          Pretty much any Martial Art > Do.

                                                          Pankration can start with 5 VS the 2 dots of the Doist. The options available to the Doist are not enough at this point.
                                                          It's not that simple. Going for a hard style grapple MA to represent pankration does mean you'll get 10 MA maneuvers to the Do 2's one Do maneuver and four MA maneuvers, but the Do user still gets a bunch of other benefits (like being able to block and deal lethal damage without spending a maneuver on both, -1 diff to all general moves, and being able to ignore the Ability and style requirements for their MA maneuver picks). Which moves each side picks, and other Abilities (esp. investment in Athletics) is going to matter a lot to this equation.

                                                          If the pankration guy doesn't take anything to help with defenses, and they can't block lethal for it, they're going to lose (since they can't defend against the Do users lethal attacks, and the Do user can use Dodge to defend with a higher dice-pool if they take the right Maneuver).
