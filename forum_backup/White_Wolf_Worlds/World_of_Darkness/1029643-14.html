                                        Originally posted by Firkraag View Post
                                        By the way, chimeras without Wyrd and Enchantment redes, could they interact with inanimate object of Autumn World? Open and close doors, steal things?
                                        Yes, a chimera can interact with the mundane/ Autumn world (and indeed has to): a closed door is a closed door and the chimera must open it to get through (or destroy it). However, when surrounded by non-enchanted people, chimera are on the other side of the Mists (because of the witnesses' Banality) and so cannot interact with the mundane world. This is why chimera often stalk their changeling prey, waiting for the moment when they will be alone...

                                        So, the Monster Under the Bed waits for the childling's parent to leave in order to open the door of the wardrobe in which it was hiding...

                                        Also, remember that if a non-enchanted witness sees a real-world effect by a chimera (a changeling riding a horse and thus apparently floating), her disbelief will shunt the Kithain into a random part of the Dreaming unless the chimera (and only if she is sentient) succeeds a Willpower roll against the witness's Banality.

                                        Finally, when a chimera is near a non-enchanted people whose Banality is 2 or more than her Glamour rating, it suffers one Level of Health every turn. So, for instance, a chimera with Glamour 5 is near a human near a Banality of 7, the chimera suffers one Health Level every turn, prompting the chimera to quickly go elsewhere. This is why chimera avoid areas with Banal people. (Changeling: the Dreaming, 2nd ed., p. 224a)

                                            Originally posted by Nitenshi View Post
                                            Art of Spring ? Jeez... I eager for the release of C20.

                                            I have a question about something I have trouble to understand in the rulebook. It's about Enchantment and the difficulty of cantrips on mortals. I've seen at several place in the book that Enchanting a mortal helps a Changeling to cast cantrip on him because he had already circumvent his Banality. But nowhere in the Enchantment or Arts rules I've seen something close to that statement.
                                            Is it that the Banality of the mortal is dropping when I enchant him or am I missing something? The first possibility makes sense but I don't remember seeing anything about a drop of the Banality rate in the rule on Enchantment.
                                            Enchanting someone helps casting cantrips on her because, according to the rules of 2nd ed., then cantrips cast on her by a changeling will be Chimerical (not Wyrd), and will not necessarily call for a Glamour expenditure. Also, if you apply an optional rule, enchanted targets are at Banality +4 as target difficulty (before performing a Bunk) while non-enchanted targets are at Banality +5.
