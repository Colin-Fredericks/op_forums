                  Originally posted by Penelope View Post
                  This means I have to create a whole new account and a whole new password I gotta memorize.
                  It's not exactly perfect because of things like this, but better experiment with a supplement than a core book.

                  Plus Kickstarter’s famous.
                  ...
                  Nobody’s ever heard of Indiegogo.
                  That seems.... very unfair. Indiegogo is basically the 3rd largest crowdfunding site after KickStarter and GoFundMe. It's not some tiny small potatoes operation. It is smaller than KS, sure, but that's not everything. Indiegogo has around half the users as KS, but on average far more Indiegogo users fund multiple projects. Indiegogo has more payment options. Etc.

                  I think it's also fair to say that Onyx Path isn't really getting a lot of new backers on KS just because KS is the biggest company in that market. The number of backers for OPP projects seems pretty baked in, and mostly come from OPP's social media presence. It's worth exploring if the giant number of users on KS (again, most of which only signed up for one thing and rarely back anything else) is actually better for a company with a fairly stable fan base that will probably follow them on a platform switch.

                  Indiegogo also isn't a "hit the magic number" system. If a project is struggling, and gets 95% funding, it can still collect in the hopes of making the product and finding the rest through a different avenue, where KS fails your project if you're a cent short of goal (while hyperbole, a small % of KS campaigns that look like they're going to just get funded fail at the last minute because a few backers pull out suddenly at the end). This makes it harder to compare to KS on some metrics because while more KS projects meet funding (though neither site posts terribly great success rates), it's unclear how many in the difference on Indiegogo still succeed because they got close enough despite not hitting the projected target.

                  Any chance if enough people express how upset they are that they’ll cancel the deal and do it on Kickstarter instead? Or am I tilting at windmills?
                  Considering it's a stated experiment with a fairly minor supplement? Tilting seems like the answer. If they want to try Indiegogo, some project is going to have to be the one to do it with. Asking OPP to never switch platforms unless forced by something like KS closing shop seems unlikely to be something they'd agree too.

                              The MMN mostly mention that Indiegogo is easier on the backend of actually running the campaign. Indiegogo is generally regarded as the more flexible platform for the campaign creator

                              I know one big feature for some users is the Indiegogo has native Paypal support, where Kickstarter doesn't. Some international fans live in places where credit cards aren't common, making Kickstarter's limited payment options a problem (this is something that came up in the past when it was clear crowdfunding was going to be an on-going part of OPP's strategy). Paypal integration alone could potential open things to some more people that would be regular backers but aren't now.
