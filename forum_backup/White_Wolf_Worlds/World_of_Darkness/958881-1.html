                            Regarding Armor Penalty:
                            I've been using a houserule that removes penaltypoints depending on how good a fighter you are. Each dot in Melee/Brawl/Archery/Firearms removes one point of penalty. That means that a Swordsman with Melee 3 has learned to fight in a full plate armor and therefore don't get penalties from it. While a novice haven't learned to compensate and therefore still has penalties.

                            But i agree that the whole combat system in all WoD gamelines needs an overhaul. It simply takes to much time (especially with Celerity, Rage etc), gets confusing etc.
                            The best thing would boil it all down to one opposed roll (Attack+Strength vs Evade/Soak) but i haven't figured out a good way to incorporate a persons strength, dexterity, toughness, armor, weapon damage etc into these two rolls.
                            I don't mind multiple actions (since i think that much more that a punch and a block can be accomplished in a 3 second timespan), but to me they complicate things.
                            Another thing that's been bothering me is that if someone throws a punch or attempt to stab you etc, you will attempt to block/evade this. Doing other things that evading damage should be a dedicated action that needs a Willpower roll or a Willpower point spend to follow through with IMO.

                                                Okay, just a word of advice here..
                                                While Alternate System building can be utterly fun, it's easy to lose track of something important:

                                                Keep It Simple for the players.

                                                You don't want them to row through whole lists of stats, or ten times the rules, or keep looking up things nonstop just to get the same stuff done as before.
                                                A little bit more complexity for the sake of realism is a-okay, especially if your players Feel More Badass (And/or more threatened by an enemy..) as a result. But use it to create atmosphere and fix immersion problems - not create new ones.

                                                    Originally posted by Innocent Flower View Post
                                                    I also think that, well, having great strength should allow you an easier time in armour. No 'Well you get dex from celerity and that cancels out the dex penality' but, well. Strength should, but not completely, migitate the problems of having excess weight. Potence, whatever mages use, and maybe glabro if you find something fitting should all reduce penalties (with some hard armour keeping at least some penalty because of how rigid and in the way it is, rather than weight)
                                                    While it might not be great from a design perspective, Stamina is really what should matter here. Armor isn't that heavy (~90 lbs is on the very heavy side of plate mail for example), and is spread out over the body in a design specifically to help distribute the load as effectively as possible on the human frame. However, that weight adds up in caloric expenditure over time. That's why people can do cartwheels in full plate mail; it isn't so heavy or so restrictive. But each one costs more energy than it would without all the extra weight. Since the game doesn't really have fights last long enough to have exhaustion be a thing, the best way to include this would be to have the armor penalty severity key off of Stamina rather than Strength.

                                                    If we really wanted to model armor's drawback more "realistically" it would be best to move from the current Health Level system, to an Exhaustion/Fatigue/Stress track and with "Wounds" as individual status effects. Armor would be a trade off of improving defense against wounds, but make exhaustion management more difficult.
