    Which Gamelines/splats do you want to Incorporate Sorcerers more often?

    08-30-2016, 01:39 PM
    I really hope 4th ed incorporates Sorcerers as a general World of Darkness option found all over the place, rather then just with mages. I would like if they kept that the backbone of Traditions and Technocrats were Sorcerers, it does make Mages seem rarer and cooler if there were less of them then weaker magic users. So there would be three times more sorcerers then awakened, and 10 times more normal acolytes.

    I would have Werewolf maintain some kinfolk as maintainers of the knowledge so moderate sized septs maintain at least one shaman with at least one apprentice among the kinfolk along with it being common for Theurges to also learn sorcery. And certain camps and tribes, like the Uktena and Bubasti have common to pick up sorcery. Glasswalkers would have sorcery and enlightened science, and an even greater concentration in Camps like the Cyberdogs.

    I would have Tremere have a large Sorcerer population among their ghouls, as they retained all their troves of scrolls and grimoires from when they were a Mage house, this would exponentially improve their standing as the Blood Sorcerer Monopoly guys, now ghouls can actually contribute to the maintaining chantries beyond just fetching supplies. They could even bolster their alchemical and magical supplies by having mortals refine rare reagents for them.

    I would have Setites have both Sorcerers and Mages among their cults, as they exist not just as a Vampire clan but a religion.

    And Laibon had the setting just rife with Sorcerers, so much so they didn't feel like incorporating the sorcery rules and just used dumbed down rituals. I would toss that and use the flat out sorcerery rules to represent sorcerers.

    And i would have Ghoul organizations and mystically inclined Revenant families hoard sorcery, probably also use Alchemy to in fertility rituals. The Giovanni and Rosalinni families should have retained a lot of Sorcery in their mortal families, and the Rosilinni knew more then just necromancy.

    Pentex I would have filled with both Sorcery and Enlightened Science as they were probably the biggest organization that combined the two.

    Thoughts and suggestions?

                  I tend to make sorcerers a sort of middle ground in my games. Every supernatural power has a demi-race that are perfect as sorcerer covens. Kinians, kinfolk, ghouls/revenants, shih, thralls and mediums are all potentially powerful sorcerers with every reason to do so. I think sorcerers should have a much larger range of factions and conspiracies much like mage, where sorcerers from all over the world use different combinations of paths and psychic powers. I'd much rather the "Hunter: the reckoning" book be thrown out in favor of one dedicated to sorcerers and gifted mortals(who would do a much better job anyway).

                  Idea's so sorcerer factions:

                  Men in Black: The mortal government isn't even slightly ignorant of the supernatural world. The earth is a melting pot of unexplainable phenomena, nights are filled with the roars and wails of nightmares in open combat, monsters that manipulate entire cities, and alien horrors gaze on us from just outside our field of view. With a new "world ending" event every week the only thing keeping the public from devolving into a panicked frenzy is their own blissful ignorance, and the Men in Black like to keep it that way.

                  Overseen and funded by a department of NATO and several private companies, the division of supernatural investigations focuses on covering up evidence of supernatural damage, investigating non-mundane crimes, and combating supernaturals on the physical, legal, and financial levels. They act in conjunction with powers like the camarilla, technocracy, and DNA, lend them their superior resources and manpower for knowledge of and weapons to fight against rogue supernaturals.

                  The Seekers of Light: "Knowledge is Power", this saying is truer in the world of darkness than many would first believe. Knowledge of science and the arcane has meant the difference between being a free man or slave of the darkness since ancient times and it hasn't changed yet. Under its clean, sophisticated skin the world is still ruled by the same demons as it was in medieval times and for all the same reasons. Monsters have power. They hide in the dark where mortals fear to tread, hording the wonders and mysteries of the universe for only themselves. Only the bravest and most foolish would step into the dark, yet that is what the seekers do every day. Seeking to snatch to secrets that lay within.

                  As a long established order, the seekers work behind the scenes, searching for ancient knowledge and hidden treasures too tantalizing resist. They break into ancient crypts, hidden chantries, and even breach other worlds for the chance to at the score of their lives. Many would expect such a career to lower a persons life expectancy, and they'd be right, but the seekers have perfected the art. They sneak undetected from the mystic eyes of mages, they've survived fights with monsters, and when things go crazy, made getting the f*ck out of there into a science.

                  The Faceless Web: The world is crazy. Our nightmares are real and they fill the night their with bloodcurdling howls. Strange lights appearing out of nowhere and disappearing before you can investigate. Roars and crunching bones leave behind bloody trails that are clean by next morning. Murder scenes left by men outrunning Lamborghini's are explained as gang violence. The monsters are everywhere, but they cover their tracks as soon as their made. But you know the truth, even if no one will believe you. You just need to find the right way to tell them.

                  Humanity may not be a perfect race, but they aren't stupid. You can't have a world filled to the brim with supernatural weirdness occurring every day, and have no one notice. People are going to find out, and they going to try to tell everyone else. And when no one believes them, they'll look for other who know. The "Deeper Web" was made by these people. A series on anonymous Websites on the deep web made by a conglomerate of people from all races and backgrounds all trying to break the illusion that keeps the people from seeing the monsters. It's largest group, "Faceless", operates behind a well known public website to gather information and uncover enough evidence of the supernatural to expose them to the public.
