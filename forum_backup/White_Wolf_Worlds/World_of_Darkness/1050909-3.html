    Originally posted by Dusksage View Post
    I have a thought experiment that may yield some insight into what I believe to be the crux of the issue being discussed here. And here it is:

    Four people each come into possession of a copy of Werewolf: the Apocalypse. Each one lives in a remote part of the world where there's little internet connectivity, and there is only one small hobby store in town that only has a smattering of second hand copies of tabletop roleplaying games at best. Never the less, these four people love the hobby and quickly World of Darkness becomes their favorit tabletop game to run with their friends. They all run many Werewolf Chronicles with their respected gaming groups, and it becomes a very important part of the four individual's lives. However, each of the four individuals have a different version of Werewolf: the Apocolypse (1st Edition, 2nd Edition, Revised Edition, and the 20th Anniversary Edition), and by some cosmic chance each never discovers any other version beyond the ones they already own. The question then is this: does each of these four individual's Chronicles take place in the same World of Darkness?
    Actually I'd go as far as to say that if you have people that you described, and that they all happened to get the same edition of Werewolf, they'd still be playing games do drastically different they may as well be set in completley different Worlds of Darkness.
