                                    I think KotE is actually more offensive and problematic for Asian Americans than it is for the Japanese. The Japanese are a cultural and ethnic majority in Japan, so KotE doesn't really contribute to the daily oppression of Japanese people in Japan. It's laughable xenophobia and exoticism to them, much the same as the way we Brits laugh or roll our eyes at all the villains with Hollywood British accents in American films (or, vice versa, our own stereotypes of America). Likewise, I'm sure the French aren't particularly upset by British stereotypes of them in popular culture.

                                    However, Asians in America, who are a minority, are the ones who experience ridicule, racism and oppression because of the continued use of stereotypes about Asians in popular culture.

                                    So, arguably, the best people to write KotE wouldn't be people living in Asia but Asians living in America and the Diasporas (British Asians, French Asians, etc). It's they who'll benefit/suffer the most from how it turns out.

                                    I'm also aware this is largely a discussion being had by non-Asians. The discussion is likely to be biased or one-sided as a result. We might be missing the point entirely.

                                    Now, I would like to see a KotE20 done right. But I want it to be led by Asians. That's the only way to ensure the primary vision for the game isn't that of people who aren't impacted by the negative side effects of doing it wrong. It would also be nice if OPP were to make money from Asian culture that Asians actually get to make some money from it too, by hiring those writers.

                                    But I'm aware that probably won't happen (most of OPP's writers are white American dudes, which means investing in ways to address that and reaching out to writers who may just be offended by the whole thing), and I don't expect OPP particularly *wants* to engage with KotE any more anyway. OPP is as aware of the problems with the line as anyone, and from the work of Satyr on Mage, you can see there's a definite consciousness around the problems of representation within the gamelines.

                                    I think it's likely to remain a nostalgia thing. Although I wouldn't be surprised if there was a one-off rules update to streamline everything without having to do a whole core book. Such a thing might be better as a fan supplement anyway, however.

                                                    Originally posted by Vysha View Post
                                                    See, I always ran into trouble when I'd try to integrate KotE in my games. They tended to come across as "better than you" kindred, since they were either as restricted on feeding or less restricted
                                                    About as restricted. Kindred can feed on anything with blood; Wan Gui could only feed on humans and other monsters. Even their use of feeding on ambient energy wouldn't grant that much of it, unless they ripped it out, which as far as I can gather would have political consequences in angering their non-vampire associates.

                                                    with access to special magic rituals that didn't need a heavy XP investment
                                                    The powerful ones typically required Rituals at six or seven dots or more.

                                                    fewer weaknesses than Kindred
                                                    Nearly double cost to raise Disciplines compared to Kindred Clan Disciplines, with no cost breaks, ever. Their main sources for soaking aggravated damage, to the extent any vampires did, also sucked. Finally, their multiple action powers were nothing compared to Celerity when they were introduced in 2nd edition. But then, Celerity was always kind of stupidly overpowered until DA Vampire and V20. Then it just became as overpowered as any other extra actions power.

                                                    and if you were... Whichever it was, yin or yang balanced, you got to play in the sunlight.
                                                    Yang Imbalanced vampires could stay out in direct sunlight for maybe up to slightly less than a half an hour, which was counterbalanced with getting hungrier far more often and being extra flammable and capable of catching diseases. Meanwhile, the Yin Imbalanced suffered extra hard. Both states required some incredibly lopsided Virtue allotments (one three dots higher than the other), and would seriously dig at the other if you had it, which is important, because it meant having a very low equivalent to Courage or Self-Control, the latter case compounding the early feelings of being ravenous and uncontrollably hungry.

                                                    Really, the only thing that Kindred could do that Kuei-Jin couldn't is make minions; embracing and ghouling. And since they don't want Cainites on their turf and rarely leave their own turf, they only end up interacting with each other as antagonists.
                                                    You're leaving out the blood bond. That's fairly important; a similar capacity requires Obligation 5 for Demon People, which is a higher xp investment than an in-clan Discipline at six dots (90 xp vs 85). Combined with ghouls, that can be a rather pronounced power difference over time, compared to the capacity to make dhampyr that take way longer to develop into power and are affected by age, albeit loosely enough that the note on three-hundred year old dhampyrs was perplexing (aging at slightly less than one year per decade, said dhampyr would be about like a late middle-aged persona, at worst). Also, you have to be Yang imbalanced to produce dhampyrs, and getting them involved is often frowned upon by the behavior that keeps your power stat up.
