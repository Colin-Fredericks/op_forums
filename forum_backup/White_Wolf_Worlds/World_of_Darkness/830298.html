      “If you can write about the Holocaust, you can write about 9/11”

                  I'm pretty mixed on it. There's good parts, and bad parts, of the interview. I think Martin's general ideas are in the right direction. However at the same time he's dismissive of a few things. TTRPG don't print money, but if they didn't sell TTRPG only companies wouldn't exist. There are plenty of fans of the WOD that are fans of the CofD And it feels too much like looking at the top of the RPG industry bubble before it burst as if the top of the bubble was the true state of what the market could support.

                  It also doesn't feel like Martin completely appreciates how hard it was to make Shoah a good book. Yes, you can do that for other sensitive topics. But it isn't simple. It takes the write authors, and the right dedication to do so. Not giving into fear is one thing. Throwing out 10 insensitive books for every Shoah book is just a shitty record that needs to be considered rationally.

                          Alright, because I'm lazy, I'm going to start by just reposting what I said about this on GiantITP a little while ago

                          Originally posted by me, on another forum
                          Which game lines do you plan to revive, and when?
                          So what's Onyx Path been doing all this time? Vampire to Mage are ALIIIIVE!!!!

                          Will you prioritize computer games, or will we see pen-and-paper soon?
                          Totally called it. For all the reasons given as well.

                          There is only one WoD.
                          My copy of Blood and Smoke begs to differ.

                          To be honest, in the last month or so I've grown to like the name change, but I'm never going to except that CofD isn't a World of Darkness. Annoyed that CofD won't be getting any computer games though, I really want a Requiem game more than a Masquerade one.

                          I do like that CofD is implied to be being left to OPP. I never really liked the first editions, but the second edition games are what I wanted the oWoD to be, full of mystery and horror, instead of horror and face-punching, and I think that OPP has the right idea for how to bring the line forward. Hoping that OPP might be able to own it eventually.

                          New metaplot details
                          Interesting. Although I don't like the new Mage direction, I never felt like the Technocracy was able to properly 'win', just to stay as the top dog for a long time. Also, IIRC, in the old metaplot the Technocracy weren't actually winning, as humanity was settling into it's current state instead of the Union's vision. Also not a big fan of the Werewolf details, I think I'll either nab W20 or jump ship to Forsaken.

                          Blah blah fantasy is bad stuff
                          Not happy here. Part of what makes CofD such a great game is that it's not afraid to be both fantasy and horror. The dig at urban fantasy is annoying as well, it makes me glad that OPP have decided to embrace the inherent urban fantasy in Scion.


                          Also annoyed at no mention of Changeling plans.
                          So yeah, it's a mix of good (OPP seeming to retain CofD, which I think they do perfectly), bad (this 9/11 thing), and just not interesting (most of it really). Call me ready to stick to my current collection unless I see a massive change.
