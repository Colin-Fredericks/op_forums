    Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post
    CCP got the rights to Black Dog because Black Dog was never a separate company. It was an alternate imprint owned by WW. Onyx Path could, technically, make Black Dog material if they felt like it, and got it approved by CCP, but it seems unlikely.
    In the Demon: The Descent Kickstarter, there was a comment explaining why they won't use Black Dog anymore.
    Basically, Black Dog was used back in the day after the issue with Clanbook: Tzimisce's backcover. And the books were not bought by some Game Stores because of the imprint, while others that did buy the imprint had to hide them away so younger customers wouldn't just grab them. Remember, this was back in the day when people protested or boycotted companies and stores because, "Satan", "RPGs are evil and teach you magic" nonsense.
    I even have a White Wolf magazine issue that reported a store having trouble because the religious person complaining/protesting was upset over them selling W:tA.
    POD does away with that, so there's no need for the imprint anymore.

        Originally posted by Clockwork Chaos View Post
        Thanks to all for the info and links. I don't have the time these days to indulge, but I've got a lot of great memories from yesteryear. I'm glad to know that White Wolf is continuing in spirit, if not name.
        All World of Darkness and Exalted products will continue to have the White Wolf logo. They'll just also include an Onyx Path logo. Former WW stuff like Scion and the Trinity Continuum will just have an Onyx Path logo.

        Another couple quick questions: is Onyx Path redoing everything White Wolf put out, or just updating the core rules? Are they putting out new stuff? If they're putting out new stuff, where in the timeline would it fall? And did CCP (or Onyx Path) also get the rights to the Black Dog stuff too, which to my limited understanding was somehow a separate company?
        Assuming you're talking about just the Classic World of Darkness when you say "everything WW put out," we're examining whether 20th anniversary releases make sense on a year-by-year basis. Existing material is available in PDF and print-on-demand via DriveThruRPG; we have no reason to reprint existing material if it's all available already. We've released a handful of additional books for Vampire, Werewolf, and Mage, with more to come. See our Schedule page for details.

        The new books are metaplot-agnostic. They're at an undefined point somewhere around the cusp of 2e and Revised.

        Black Dog was an imprint of White Wolf, not a separate company. We could do new Black Dog material if we wanted, but there's really no reason to with the current market.

        With regards to tshirts, I can tell you that modern print-on-demand technology gives you very high quality and durable tshirts. These days you can even select from a range of brands and styles for each individual tshirt. A group of my friends print group tshirts for birthdays and other events, as well as individual ones for gifts and jokes. We've been doing it since late 2009 or so, and the oldest ones are still perfectly wearable.
        Managing this at the moment would require additional resources, while we're trying to catch up our late books with our already full schedule. Maybe someday, but not now.

            Some things to add to Ian's post though:

            Yeah, Black Dog Game Factory was a name for their "adult" works. With PoD, it's not really necessary, though it would be kind of nice to keep as a way of "rating" a book as "intensely inappropriate for normal people." However, they WILL make an appearance in "Book of the Wyrm: 20th Anniversary Edition", though they will have been bought out by an even more evil Icelandic company...

            As for the "continuity" of the books being released? There are a FEW CWoD books that have been released lately (or the near future) that still exist within the Revised, or Dark Ages rules/continuity and aren't part of the 20th Anniversary gamelines...

            Recently, the Revised Convention Books were released for "Mage: the Ascension Revised Edition." These books (available on DriveThruRPG) include New World Order, Progenitors, Syndicate, and Void Engineers (Revised Iteration X was released in January 2002). These books actually take place "Today", a decade after the "Time of Judgement", and (so far) are the only official continuation of the "story" of the Classic World of Darkness (at least from the sphere of Mage: the Ascension) that we've gotten. The N.W.O. book is pretty enlightening, as it talks about the Vampires supposedly waiting for their legends to return and... nothing happened. Meanwhile, Humanity has not only accepted what the N.W.O. has told them, but basically taken the ball and ran with it and now they're trying to figure out WTF is going on and are split up on whether this is how things should be (humanity closing in on ascension as they create "magic" through beliving just about anything), or if they need to somehow control the masses (it's hard to give up that power, you know?) ... we've gone from 60-minute tape-players on our hips to devices the size of watches that can carry thousands of hours of audio, beamed directly to ear pieces and communicate with our pocket-computers we call "cell phones." ...

            For Dark Ages Vampire: we have "Dark Ages: Darkening Sky" which was a book that was promised, but never published back in the day. 160 pages, or so, with 5 chapters, each covering one of the fatsplats (Vampire, Werewolf, Mage, Inquisitors, Fae) regarding the eclipse of 1230AD. This isn't for V20: Dark Ages, but for the original Dark Ages line.

            Otherwise, everything else CWoD is geared towards the 20th Anniversary Editions, which are all set in an ambiguous "today", without a strong metaplot, and sort of sets a lot of the "story themes" back just before the Time of Judgement and more towards the 2nd Edition era (Sam Haight is dead, but Albrecht is still Lord, not King, Stargazers are still part of the Nation, and Black Tooth is still alive, but the Ahadi are around... so there's a bit of of a jog back and to the left.) However, it really tries to leave as many options open to the ST as possible by including not only info, but mechanics for lost Tribes as well as lost Fera too. (If you pick up a Print-on-Demand copy, I HIGHLY suggest the Premium Color... it's costly, but it's about as close to traditional printing as you'll get, aside from the KickStarter Deluxe Editions that ACTUALLY use traditional printing.)

                Originally posted by Heavy Arms View Post

                CCP got the rights to Black Dog because Black Dog was never a separate company. It was an alternate imprint owned by WW. Onyx Path could, technically, make Black Dog material if they felt like it, and got it approved by CCP, but it seems unlikely.
                As I understand it, the Black Dog imprint is pretty irrelevant these days, as it was intended as a "Mature Readers" label at a time when White Wolf had to be concerned with dealing with community standards at the local bookstore and RPGs were still not too far removed from the Satanic Panic of the 80s and 90s. Since Onyx Path is primarily direct to consumer and the standards of what is available at the local Barnes and Nobles has changed a bit, Black Dog is no longer necessary.
