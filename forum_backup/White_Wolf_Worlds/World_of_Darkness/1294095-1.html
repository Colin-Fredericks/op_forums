                    The position you're advocating is nonsensical.

                    An experience shooter is going to be able to observe the difference between a shot that was a wide miss, and a shot that was good but the target ducked undercover in the last second. They're going to know the feel difference of a blank suddenly being in their gun after they fire it. They're going to be able to see a bullet impact behind their target without hitting their target because of magical intervention.

                    Do they know the strict mechanical results? Of course not. Do they know the observable nature of what happened and how? Yeah, they do.

                    LaCroix is an experienced Dominate user. He knows the difference between not having the power of the blood behind his words, and his target not being subject to his powers.

                    And most importantly? He says it. He knows. He clearly reacts to something he can observe: that he can't Dominate you any more. You can argue that's not something that the TT rules allow someone to figure out (though I say let someone roll if you're not going to let them feel it, there are traits that would cover this), but it's how Bloodlines works, because that's what happens.
