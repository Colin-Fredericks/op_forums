    02-09-2024, 01:01 PM
    Hoo boy, I'm opening a can of worms with this topic but I'm going to be as civil as possible with this topic. I strongly encourage that anyone who posts here also keeps it civil. This is meant to be a containment thread for further future Parawolf X5 discussion.

    There's rumors that the pre-V5 setting will be brought back and re-supported under the banner of "Legacy World of Darkness" with the WoD5 material being treated as a soft reboot/alternate continuity of sorts, but these are just rumors for the time being as far as I can tell and I'll believe it when I see it.

                                      Hunter, Mummy, and Demon were what I'd call “latter day” gamelines, in that each of them spun out of the Week of Nightmares/Sixth Great Maelstrom metaplot elements that took a sledgehammer to the status quo and sent it careening off in a new direction. X5 doesn't have that, and H5 as a result is drastically different from HtR to the point that they're effectively different games.

                                      I would expect Mummy5 to do something similar, with mummies being more like the ones from the first and second editions of WoD's Mummy. None of the MtR stuff about a new Spell of Life that grafts a portion of an ancient mummy's soul into a recently deceased mortal; just the latest iteration of the mummy's cycle of death and rebirth.

                                      That said, a number of innovations that MtR made could still make their way into Mum5, such as giving them a series of splats based on which part of the soul is strongest. That, combined with the fact that Mum2 had Memory as one of its Virtues (something that I'd recommend Mum5 doing as well) would likely invite comparisons between Mum5 and CoD's Mummy: the Curse; though unlike similar criticisms of Vampire, Hunter, and Werewolf borrowing concepts from their CoD counterparts, this one would be firmly rooted in existing WoD material.

                                      D5 would likewise abandon the Latter-day plot of the walls of the Abyss being cracked by the Sixth Great Maelstrom; though I think most of DtF's setup could be salvaged: just replace the explanation given in DtF with something more like what's found in ​​​​the Dark Ages supplement Devil's Due.

                                      My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
