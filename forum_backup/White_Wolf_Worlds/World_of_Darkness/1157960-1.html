        Originally posted by Muad'Dib View Post
        Which of the World of Darkness games - and which parts of them - would you say would be best to use?
        As long as it is kept narrow enough to work as a video-game, anything can do. The main thing is to keep each game idea as just a facet of the setting, avoiding the urge to try to bring the entire tabletop experience to the game. A single story, self-contained, is enough to make a video-game. If more can be told, then more games can be created, there is no need to put too much in a single project.

        That said, each line can make for a couple of good starts. I'll try to give two initial ideas for each line I know well enough:

        Vampire - A game based on politics and management. There are some of those on the internet, and it could present an Ancillae getting its own little Domain to rule, dealing with rivals and rogue Kindred, and eventually with outsmarting old Elders at late game. Other option would be a psychological horror game like Silent Hill, with the twist of emphasis on your own Beast.

        Werewolf - I'm a fan of platformers, so it could be interesting to have something Metroidvanya style, with exploration of several locations to accomplish missions, maybe with not-so-allied factions like the Tribes being a relevant choices. Another option to avoid the too-obvious action carnage could be Strategy, with the player planing the action at the Caern to accomplish missions, manage other Packs, and maybe the option to go for Action by going with your own Pack.

        Mage - FPS was already proposed, and can be an interesting choice. It creates a narrow enough context for the Magic system to be implemented without getting out of hand. The Ascension is still a war, and several factions can go for several mission on it, with a fair share of allies and enemies of occasion. Alternately, an Exploration game could focus on a character's personal journey to Ascension, taking place during the Seeks, only. Most decisions can be let to happen during "downtime", while the Seek teaches more about the truths of reality. With this approach, the character would be restricted as most Mages should be in the beginning, not knowing the true rules to which Magic answer.

        Changeling - Just occurred to me a Tactical Espionage Action in the likes of Metal Gear, with the common enemies being Banal people completely unaware of the true action occurring, mistaking you for a troublemaker and causing all the problems they can with their Banality around you. Other approach would be a light novel or other Decision-Oriented game for you to navigate the tragic path of choosing between Humanity and Dream, and ultimately between Forgetfulness and Madness.

        Demon - I can think of an investigative game to root out secrets from the War, and eventually to seek out and win over an Earthbound. The key to it could be time, measured directly or turn-like, as it could change the scenario in several ways. Other option could be a gathering game about faith similar to Star Control 2 The Ur-Quan Masters, with dynamic changes in the map.

        Originally posted by Ambrosia View Post
        This isn't inherently bad, as often enough a person with the incentive to start and organize something is necessary for something to happen in the first place, but...

        This. Seriously guys. The first thing you might want to do is not gather people, but find out if WW would even let it fly. Else you get people excited, organized, perhaps even work put in, just to see it all torn down because you ignored that it isn't your IP. Right now you're already talking 'games' in plural before even doing this, for crissakes.

        Contact WW. Check with them. THEN, and only then if you get any kind of green light, even if it is just 'If the game is purely fan-made non-profit, we are okay with it.' you should continue doing what you're doing here. Anything else before that is getting worked up towards disappointment.

        With WW's plans of wanting to get the WoD into all media, this is either the best time (i.e. them wanting to even let Fans make videogames) or the worst time (I.e. them really wanting to focus on polished, professional games first and foremost as an initial representation) to do this.
        I'm aware of that. I am already looking out for ways to contact them, and preparing for take into account Copyright expenses. My top priority right now, by the way, isn't programmers, but people who have contacts or experience with negotiation. I'll do some of this myself, though.

        Originally posted by Nail Eater View Post

        I think you're wrong. For now it's just an idea. First of all we have to decide what do we want: vampire, werewolf, mage or anything else. If someone come to me and way that he would like to use a world that I've created but don't know which part I surely will send him to hell and tell to meet me again when he will make a decision. Or have him pay for the licence for entire world no matter how much he will finally use. So I think that deciding what we want and check if we have the resources and manpower to do that then ask WW for permission is the proper order.
        Also, this. Copyright negotiations tend to start with the first draft of a project. The first thing most companies will want to see is a clear picture of what you're planning to do with their licence. The most likely answer for asking with empty hands is that they'll hear us when we have something to show them.

        Either way, projects like this are ripe with unexpected turns and risks. Changing things to account for such an issue is a thing, as sad as it may be. A lot of similar projects went such a path, and not all of them are bad in the end. In worst case scenario for the copyright problem, our first game will be a rip-off, but will also work as something to increase our chances next time.

                            Make sure you're good to go with this in terms of copyright issues before you get started. Several friends of mine and I were thinking of working on an Exalted visual novel. Before we started though we contacted OPP but were told we would need to speak with CCP. It took almost two months to get a response from them and they said they weren't interested in allowing someone to use Exalted material in a video game, even if we offered it free to play or even just gave it to them to distribute, and that they would defend their copyright claim to the fullest. Now the old White Wolf copyrights are owned by Paradox. There's no telling what they'll say, but definitely make sure they're okay with it before you get started.

                                I just went on and asked them by e-mail. I got an answer from them this very morning.

                                They gave me a document about the process for pitching to use their IP. It pretty much went from their treasure to their main product, though one they'll sell with extra care. Can't blame them.

                                Basically, they want things on track. We do need to start work first, and contact them when we have a rounded project. We don't need to give a teaser of the game or such, but at least enough of a project to show that it is likely to be released. They want it to add to their IP, so maybe we will have to compromise a little, but that was expected. Metaplot will be sacred at least to begin with.

                                Since we are not a well developed company, a teaser can actually be a good idea, but a solid project can do the trick. An actual company isn't a requirement, but can be a good idea on the road. In the end, they are open to discuss, but we do need to show something before talking business.

                                We need some programmers, and at least an artist for visual concept. If anyone knows what else we may need, please add. Anything we don't get here will have to be obtained elsewhere. I'm already on track of the artist and a programmer.
