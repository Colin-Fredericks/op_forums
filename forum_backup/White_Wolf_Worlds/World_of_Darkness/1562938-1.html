                        Let me try to make my case better on Life 6, this time with my words; but first, while yes, Life 6 under these ideas would cover HEREDITY, which is not covered under any other previous levels, I do have to remark that in my opinion this is more due to a poor construction of the previous levels.
                        Sure, that alone could merit the lvl 6, but only insofar as previous levels don't cover that adequately, so that's in my opinion should be seen more like a bug than feature.

                        Therefore, in my humble opinion heredity should be a parameter for levels 2 to 5 of life; I mean, even sleepers can make square watermelons by genengineering them (and I'm talking in the real world).

                        The lack of heredity for previous levels is baffling to me

                        That being said, again, although heredity can be one of the cornerstones of Life 6 given the imperfect current mechanics, I do believe that something else should be added to give it teeth.

                        So, that is how Life 6 should allow for "creating life that doesn't behave like normal life". This is NOT creating "living metal" (although I do have something to say about it) or "living plasma" (although I also have the same remarks about that).

                        After all, "living metal" is Life 5 + Matter 5, and "living plasma" is Life 5 + Forces 5

                        Now, there were a few examples of what I meant above, so I'll revise them for clarity.

                        Non Darwinian Life - Lamarckian Evolution
                        Living beings inherit characteristics of their parents; those aren't however based on the "phenotype". These are "intrinsic". If you don't want to go full scientific, in order to keep up with the moto of "science is real, but so too is witchcraft", you could say that living beings have a "Logos" or whatever, something intrinsic, which is what is passed on, in case you don't want to go full "DNA molecules".

                        But even the most anachronistic mage you could possibly imagine, one stuck on the dark ages living in cave and healing people by "balanced their humors", who never heard about molecules in his life, even such a person would agree that, simple observation of the natural world can lead us to the conclusion that an amputee won't (necessarily) have descendants that are born without the same limb as the one that individual lost.

                        But Life 6 can definitely change that, and create a truly non-Darwinian species

                        This could even create some truly amazing and Sci-Fantasy stuff, like a race of cyborgs, whose offsprings are born with machine parts that are assembled from the mother's womb

                        Life Assimilation: Science Fiction is filled with aliens capable of consuming other living beings and "assimilating" some of their characteristics. Such is the case of Xenomorphs from Alien, the Zerg Swarm from StarCraft, the alien race of the Kroot from Warhammer 40k and a few others. This is definetely Life 6 territory.

                        What's more, usually those are races that can then also spread those assimilated characteristics to their descendants, so once again the non-Darwinian Evolution could be applied, combining the two.

                        Non Conventional Life: up here I would put all the Living Metal, Living Plasma, Telepathic Frogs and others.

                        There is a caveat thou: any of those CAN already be made with Life 5.

                        This, however, is the one case upon which I would say that it is legitimately merited to have Life 6 apply to the need of turning this into a new SPECIES, rather than just simply a highly exotic individual.

                        That's because those are configurations that SHOULD NOT BE ALIVE. Life 5 can make an exotic individual made of those, because it gives the "metal" or "plasma" the capacity to behave as a living pattern (have metabolism and what not).

                        However, to make it into a new SPECIES, it is required to actually rewrite the rules of what even can be a living pattern, and that's the important part - rewrite the rules.

                        So, Life 6 Matter 5 can create a species of Living Metal; Life 6 and Forces 5 a species of Living Plasma to go inhabit stars; and Life 6 Mind 3 a species of telepathic space frogs (because telepathy is supernatural feature - supernatural being, by definition, that which cannot occur naturally).

                        Life 6 + Prime 6 could create species of magic beings - for example, bees that produce honey that is Tass, therefore bees capable of processing organic/inorganic matter and turn it into quintessence, and maybe several other "magic abilities", as long as you add the appropriate Spheres.

                        So there you have it, this is what I think for Life 6

                                About the need of ArchSpheres.

                                As I see it,, most people don't need them because of how WoD handles rare high tier NPC as "narrative tools". In normal play, Archmasters are forces of nature that break the basic rules of the game to create plot hooks for the PC. This is represented, like with every other god-tier entity on other splats, as just part of the narrative - they're an extention of the ST. While this ain't everyones cup of tea, it's backed on how WoD it's intended to work.

                                A system becomes more of a necessity if one wants to have PC creating new Splats and rewirting reality at massive levels, so I would design it with player characters in mind.

                                If one wants this, one way of tackling it it's making Spheres above 5 to handle powers that are beyond the scope of normal play. Archspheres. I doubt it's the best way ... but it's the "way of the books", and of this thread.
                                About allowing to change the system

                                Soo...As an ST, contemplating PC having this power, I wouldn't want to play by a new rulebook each time a player casts a spell. So this needs to have some limitations. I would like such endeavors to be epic enough in scope to have weeks of real-life time to plan and scheme (and limit) the new sub-system.

                                Maybe you can import the Awakening idea of Quintessence, and say that to do spells this grand you need to live a story as a prerequisite. That the "effect" it's now a "story", colours the effect, allows to limit it organically depending of the sub-feats the Mage had to do to get to the point of "casting". And gives the ST real time to build the Effect.

                                Also, that would achieve one of what I consider to be the biggest debts MotA has on it's Archspheres - the idea that you should replace "rote" with "story", because "there's no rotes for levels this grand". I don't feel that's true for MotA levels, but it can be done.

                                At least for Life, MotA seems to have all those bases covered?

                                For all it's maligned as simply giving you buffs, the philosophical idea of changing a fundamental fact of the "object of study" at an abstract-level it's very much already on MotA. And it's one of the few parts that I think were, at least, interesting: the existence of meta-patterns. I actually liked this MotA idea, because these are closer to the idea of "platonic ideals" than normal patterns.

                                                        Unfortunately I didn't read Imperial Mysteries yet, but I think that a system that transforms rolls into quests is ideal.

                                                        For role, that would make Archmasters a focal point for feats that either aren't covered by the Magic System or are hard to judge/make with it, but that exist or may exist within the setting. Things like:

                                                        -> Producing a new Nightfolk, splat or subsplat,
                                                        -> Enshrine an Effect as a Cosmological Constant,
                                                        -> Create or change a Reality Zone,
                                                        -> Terraform a big Realm or area within a Realm,
                                                        -> Develop production systems like the Union Factories,
                                                        -> Induce one or more Awakenings or restore an inverted Avatar.
