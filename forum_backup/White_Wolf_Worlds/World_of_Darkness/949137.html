                  Yet another WhiteRoom splat-vs-splat Combat thread. These never end with a definitve answer.

                  hmm I don't know much about the Garou. I assume it is just a standard Crinos Garou
                  There is no such thing. A Garou (and other Fera) have a Rank from 1 to 5, which allows them to use more powerful Gifts, not unlike Vampire Disciplines.

                  If we are talking Methuselah, the opponent should be a Rank 5 Garou.
                  At which point it depends on what Vampire Clan is facing which Werewolf Tribe, Auspice and Breed, and even on what merits/flaws they both have. And backgrounds.
                  And the amount of Rage and Gnosis of the Garou. And if the Methuselah has Thaumaturgy/Necromancy/Koldunic paths or not.
                  And once that all is established, it depends on how smart the players are, and if both sides had time to prepare or are suddenly tossed into the white room.

                  White-Room combat is, most of the time, purely philosophical more so than practical.

                      Go and test it by yourself: find a friend who knows well the Werewolf set up, create the character sheets for the methuselah and the Garou and play the same combat to the best of 3.

                      Then change the Clan, the Tribe of the characters and the set up and test the combat again.

                      It has to be fun to play.

                      -I would say for the methuselah basic character sheet plus 700XP (Getting a clan discipline from 1 to 9 costs 180 XPs)
                      -For the Rank 5 Garou basic character sheet plus 150 XP.
                      -For the first set up I suggest both characters start face to face (no ambush) , in human form and all reserves (Blood, Rage, WP, Gnosis) are full.

                      I guess the Vampire will win almost every time if she isn’t ambushed.

                                      In the fluff, battles between extremely powerful opponents are often depicted as taking a long time. Like Ravnos and the elder Kuei-Jien going on for days or the fight between Mithras and the Garou pack taking all night. That sort of fight also appears in fiction, including fight that appear in ancient legends. Unfortunately, because of how WW combat rules work, things are weighted far more towards offense than defense. So the more powerful the opponents, the faster the fight will be over. So there is a good chance that a fight between a Methuselah and a Rank 5 Garou would take a single turn.
