    Is it really wrong to like the Week of Nightmares?

    03-18-2014, 05:19 AM
    I am Eldagusto, and I enjoyed the Week of Nightmares. I thought it was neat, I enjoyed the insanely convoluted backstory to it (it wasn't a bug it was a feature, the ultimate Easter Egg hunt), and how most beings only knew a small fraction of the story and assumed it was responsible for everything else. And I liked the Crossovery goodness, and that an Antediluvian Died. At the moment I can't think of anything that offended me from its handling.

    Though wait I do have one question some have said it was the relics of Fatman and Littleboy that were used on Enoch's Ghost, but that seems very odd since the Smiling Lord harvested his Relic nukes from the desert testing site in America, so they should have been the test nukes like the I have become Shiva nuke. Because if anything wouldn't the Fatman and Little Boy Nukes more likely be in the Hands of Yu Huang or the Japanese Rebels?

    But yeah I completely Embrace the Week of Nightmares Reckoning Eye of the Wyrm event! I can't be the only one right?

                          Yeah. First off I don't see KotE as a true separate game line from VtM but rather a side line like the VtM historical games.

                          Second Ravnos was rampaging for three days before the Technocracy nuked him which doesn't make sense if you think about it, there's no way they couldn't have noticed Ravnos until then. So that could only mean one thing they did know about it before day three.

                          And the act of nuking Ravnos is pretty damn overt for the Technocracy, it's not something they'd do without careful consideration. What's more likely is that they sent kill-teams before that but like so many others Ravnos killed them. Leading them to take what is easily a desperate act.                                                           I love the Week of Nightmares.
                                                          I love the crossover elements.
                                                          I love the apocalyptic feel.
                                                          I love the fact that it made the Vampires feel IMPORTANT to the wider WoD, in ways that the other game-lines couldn't have understood before then.
                                                          I love that it showed that the Garou's ongoing claim that the Final Battle was coming wasn't just empty talk... and that it galvanised them into preparing in full for the coming of the Wyrm.
                                                          I love the fact that it demonstrated the Technocracy at their best and most hubristic - the day when the self-appointed guardians of humanity had to back up their claim that they were here for humanity's best interests... and the day when they almost broke the world doing so.
                                                          I love the vast, grand scale, because I love the fact that while it may not be every day... it may not even happen for decades at a time... but on some days... Events are grand and vast.
                                                          To put it in historical terms... the Cold War is scary because it could have been WWIII. That's what the Week of Nightmares is to me... the moment when things almost broke loose.
