                              *Much like with Life 1 you can sense the target characteristics (assuming a living target), Forces 1 allows to sense the properties of energies. For instance: By watching the Forces released by a weapon you could (with the correct Knowledges) estimate how much damage the weapon does.

                              *Movement it's cinetic energy, so you could also use Forces 1 to "sense movement" in the area, and to judge how fast or how slow it is.

                              One interesting thing about lvl 1 Spheres it's that you can *share* your senses too. It takes more than one success, and it's Vulgar when doing so to mundanes (unless it can be explained somehow, like giving them weird looking lenses or drugs).
                              Now, when one thinks "sensory effects" one often thinks about things that would be useful or beneficial to perceive (and surely this can be used that way to help your people), but when affecting others it's also feasible to use this offensively showing the target stuff that, while 100% real, it's horrifying or just distracting. It may be a curse even.
                              For instance, once a character of mine gave an enemy human assassin the power to see and hear ghosts (not the power to *unsee* them, though). At that moment the ghost of someone he had recently killed was there (and not in a nice mood). Was pretty funny. Needless to say, he reacted like he had seen a ghost :P .

                                      Let's not forget about Correspondence 1. It essentially gives you 360 degree vision, an awareness of location/distance of any sensed target with other powers, And a perfect sense of location, direction and balance.

                                      A person with correspondance 1 would have near-perfect aim, they'd be able to know exactly how far away a target is and what position they need to be in to hit it. Normal soldiers and archers have to train for years to get that same sense of distance.

                                      Another thing, by combining correspondence 1 with time 1 you can essentially allow a person can do something similar for melee combat, dancing and other athletic sports. They'd have a perfect sense of what position they and/or their opponent is in as well as a perfect sense of timing and rhythm for any action they need to perform.
