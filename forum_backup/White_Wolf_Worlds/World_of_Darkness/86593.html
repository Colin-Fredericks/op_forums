          IIRC, Kindred of the East came out a year after Changeling did; so if the 20th Anniversary Edition fad extends that long, you should see it being considered in 2016.

          I’d dearly love to see a line-neutral supplement featuring Numinous humans: e.g., sorcerers, psychics, and the faithful. I wouldn’t stop at those three, either; I have ideas for at least two other kinds of Numina that would mesh with the themes and metaphysics of Werewolf and Mage; and I can see the former also meshing nicely with Wraith.

          Then there’s the latter-days World of Darkness: Hunter, Mummy, Demon, and Orpheus. If these get commemorative editions, I don’t want to wait until their 20th Anniversaries. They also have issues with being extremely metaplot-dependent.

          Mummy deserves special mention. It has never been a standalone game in the oWoD; even Mummy: the Resurrection required you to own another gameline’s core book for the basic rules. As well, each of the three editions of Mummy was drastically different from the others. I would like a commemorative edition for Mummy; but given how drastically mummies changed with the coming of the ghost-storm, integrating both into a single game would prove tricky. And I’m personally more fond of the pre-storm mummies than the post-storm ones.

          My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
