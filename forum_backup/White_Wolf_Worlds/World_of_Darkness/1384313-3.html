            Going along with what Eldagusto has said above, changelings who are able to would desperately like to have the assistance of the Garou with threats of the Autumn World.

            Changeling Cantrips are powerful and both Kinain and the Enchanted are wily opponents, the simple fact is that changelings are losing a slow and dreadful war of attrition against Winter and that even the Kinain and Enchanted can only do so much against the champions of the Autumn World. A Sidhe Knight and a Troll Hero can do great things, but no matter how strong they are and what they can, Autumn will inevitably wear them down into nothing.

            As far as supernatural menaces, Autumn People and Dauntain are obvious but they are still within the paradigm of The Dreaming. Now there are many, many potential supernatural menaces that could face them but I can think of three which would particularly incline the dreamers to ask for garou intervention. Fae Hunters are fairly rare and even then most Changelings are pretty adept at handling foes that don't drown them with Banality and/or Nightmare.

            That being said, what about those threats? Those threats are primarily three imo: Vampires, Ghosts, Demons. All suitably sinister, even if in different ways. That is going to be the concerning element for Changelings of the Dreaming. The Banality and Nightmare these forces bring.

            Young vampires may produce a dark sort of glamour but bloody dreams rapidly become Nightmares and vampires become increasing Banal as they age. All ghosts carry the dread menace of Nightmare and Banality: Nightmare for the dread horrors of the grave and Banality from the horrible reminder of life's ultimate and inevitable end. The Slaugh squeeze as much glamour from death as they can but even for them it's a dangerous abyss to stare into. Demons are naturally rampantly Nightmarish and whether they are Banes or Fallen or whatever else, any denizen of the Dreaming could see Nightmare miasma wherever demons tread.

            Changelings of the Dreaming have to be careful around mundane violence already, as human death is for keeps and thus banal; but these entities are not only dangerous physically but threaten to taint the Dreaming Glamour that these denizens shepherd.

            Thankfully, the majority of these beings also reek of the Wyrm and any self-respecting Gaian Garou is unlikely to hesitate to rid the world of such spiritual filth. Perfect synergy.

            There's definitely more ground to cover here but what I will say is that generally, most changelings know they need to be self-sufficient. They band together and fend for themselves, a legacy of the Interregnum. Even the Arcadians who haven't learned such harsh lessons have pride and noble obligation to consider.

            When changelings desperately need help, it is most likely because they desperately need help (even if their reasons may seem frivolous to outsiders and even a few other changelings). Threats to the very essence of who they are almost certainly top that list. Jerry the Redcap will be back in a few days if run through by a Shide Knight and be back in a generation or so if hit by a bus.

            But if Jerry's spirit is perverted and corrupted? If his mien is flayed or if it's violated or if he's cut down with cold iron?

            His dream is over, forever. Winter draws near.

            These threats are when the changelings need the garou most and are in turn probably the most likely to get it, as anything that understands the nature of The Dreaming knows that should the changelings vanish, all glamour and dream is likely to follow soon after.

                                                    I now have three general Dauntain concepts I could use in my game, so this discussion has been very useful to me.

                                                    The first, as mentioned above, is a Typhoid Dauntain which is a Changeling being blood bonded to a vampire, and as a result of that has succumbed to the power of Banality. This is merely a result of the Changeling's enslavement to his regnant as a property of the blood. He no longer serves the Dreaming, but his master. I have two possible concepts for this. Not sure which one is best, so let me know what you think. The first is that the vampire regnant is an elder Malkavian who recognized the Changeling (likely a Sluagh) for what she is, and was able to blood bond her. This Changeling is now the Malkavian's spy among the Dreaming, and reports back to her. While it is possible for the Malkavian to become some sort of threat to other Changelings, for the most part this is merely curiosity and some hope that in the future, this knowledge may be useful in her intrigues among other Kindred. So the Sluagh is like a secret Dauntain, but could potentially be caught purposefully gaining Banality in order to power Cantrips as well as her disappearances (when she is with her mistress) or odd behavior. The second concept is that a Toreador unknowingly encountered a Changeling at some fancy art power, and was so fascinated she entranced him and later blood bonded. It was quite late when he discovered the mortal he bonded was in fact one of the fae (likely a pooka). That Dauntain drops out of Changeling society and now stays in his haven as a "pet", with the pooka's form possibly being some kind of predator cat or other dangerous animal.

                                                    The second type of Dauntain is the Cursed Dauntain, a Changeling who took the Oath of Undoing to destroy a hated foe, and now wields terrible power to slay his enemies. For this, I'll be introducing the character as a fae knight early on, befriending the Garou pack should they spend any long time in the Dreaming. But at some point, disaster happens. I am using the D&D stories of Count Strahd and Lord Soth (both had the same creators interestingly enough) as inspirations - noble figures whose passions would destroy them. Trying to develop something that could make the PCs very sympathetic to the villain even when they know they have to destroy him to protect the Dreaming, especially since he was/is their friend.

                                                    The last type is the Dauntain type I just described - a fairy hunter. He went through his Chrysalis a while ago, but recently went through trouble and rejected his fae mien. He now remembers being "stolen" by fairies, but recently "escaped" the fairy realm. He now plans on destroying other fairies (Changelings in general) using the lore he's learned during his "imprisonment" in order to rescue the other people stolen by them. One PC has something in his background that I can use to make this an old friend or family member whose been lost. So it should lead to some great RP when he realizes this friend is now hunting fairies and this directly acting against his totem ban.

                                                    I actually have even more concepts now, but I can only use so many Dauntain concepts before it gets old. I think these three are different enough, and use very different hooks, that I can use them as long as I spread them out and include other the other encounter concepts we've talked about in this thread. And if the PCs actually don't interact that much with the Dreaming, I can pare it down to the best concept.
