                                        On the subject of economies in Sci Fi and how the NWO might respond to them, please remember the NWO isn't dumb. They can be described as fanatics or fools (I'd say both) but neither ignorant nor stupid. They'd have to know that a suficiantly advanced technology combined with a functional government would make Capitalism nonfunctional. In a world of teleporters, replicators, robots (not Data but implied elsewhere), and cheap fusion power, most human physical labor would be worthless.                                             It's the Ivory Tower, not the White Tower. The White Tower of Languedoc was the original meeting hall for the leadership of the Order of Reason before it was destroyed in the 1700s.

                                            The Ivory Tower is Methodology (or faction) within the NWO that while possessing a great deal of influence within the Convention is technically not the leadership of the Convention. In fact, post-Avatar Storm, the Head of the Operatives has been placed as the defacto head of the Convention, mostly due his position and they were reluctant to put him on the Global Symposium, the replacement leadership council they've put together with the loss of Control.

                                            It's technically just called the Symposium but the term has also long been used for local and regional cross-Convention leadership councils.                                                     My take on Threat Null:

                                                    As others have pointed out, Threat Null was created by the Avatar Storm, and the surviving VEs shifted from Star Trek to BSG in response. But also note that Threat Null officially only has four factions, corresponding to Iteration X, the Progenitors, the NWO, and the Syndicate. There's no known counterpart to the VEs. The official line on this is that the lack of Conditioning among VE Operatives protects them from being converted; and the manuscript for Technocracy Reloaded suggests that those who do get converted become something that's difficult to distinguish from regular VEs: Sentinels who stand watch over the Earth against alien invaders.

                                                    But I have a personal bit of head-canon, where many of the VEs who were lost to the Dimensional Anomaly didn't die. Instead, they became Explorers: an extreme version of the pre-Anomaly VE stereotype where they lost all interest in Earthly matters, abandoned the Technocracy, and went off exploring the Deep Universe and making friends with the aliens out there. They're not part of Threat Null in part because of their lack of Conditioning (which lead the other four Conventions' counterparts into having an alliance of sorts) and in part because they're not at this point a threat to Earth: they're too busy exploring to bother with Earth.

                                                    But that has the potential to change: if the Explorers ever find that they need something from Earth (like its Nodes), they may end up coming back, leading an alliance of aliens that's exceptionally well-coordinated and well informed about Earth and the inner workings of Earth's defenses.

                                                    My hacks: Trinity Continuum, Scion, Exalted, World of Darkness, Chronicles of Darkness
