                  My longest-running and absolute favorite character is a Mage; he was with the Technocracy (a Watcher for the New World Order), but (partially because I handed my ST a whole bunch of freebie points at character creation and said, "He was orphaned as a baby, do whatever Plot desires with his background") he eventually discovered that he was Fianna Kinfolk. This caused significant problems with his Technocratic superiors (he was already on extreme probation for -- purely coincidentally -- helping a panicked and terrified Garou escape a Technocratic dragnet in a moment of empathy).

                  He officially left the Technocratic Union behind, though he brought with him his ideology and brilliant understanding of the Technocratic and scientific Paradigm. As a member of the New World Order, he had also trained heavily as an anthropologist in college; this left him with an unusual (especially for a Technocratic) understanding and comprehension for unusual paradigms or cosmologies. When he began working more and more closely with Garou, he heard more and more about the Triat and Gaia. Always the curious one, he asked several Theurges and Galliards about them. He received quite a significant amount of information over a long period of time; after thinking on it deeply, he came to a realization.

                  "Oh!" He said, "I get it now! The Triat and Gaia -- they are embodiments and expressions of the four fundamental forces! Or, perhaps, the forces are expressions of the Triat and Gaia."

                  "What the hell are you babbling on about now, Joshua?" Everyone else replied.

                  "Here, let me explain." And then my character proceeded to Unify the Technocratic paradigm with Garou cosmology.
                  • The Wyld manifests through the Strong Nuclear Force, which is responsible for ensuring the existence of fundamental building blocks of matter. As the Wyld creates raw materials for Creation, the Strong force confines raw energy into basic particles such as protons. Without the Wyld, there would be nothing brought into creation. Without the Strong force, raw matter could not exist.
                  • The Wyrm manifests through the Weak Nuclear Force, which is responsible for the radioactive decay of matter. As the Wyrm breaks down and recycles things after their function has been performed, the Weak force allows for the radioactive decay of unstable elements and particles. After a particle has existed for a long period of time, the interactions of the Weak force gradually increase the likelihood of the particle decaying back into energy and its fundamental constituents.
                  • The Weaver manifests through the Electromagnetic Force, which is responsible for the construction of all chemical properties of every form of matter. As the Weaver takes things created by the Wyld and gives them form and function, so too does the Electromagnetic force take the basic particles created by the Strong force and structure them into elements and chemical compounds, dictating the properties of every piece of matter, and how every chemical interacts with every other chemical. It is perhaps no coincidence that virtually all technology created by humanity is dependent, in some manner, on the Electromagnetic force (electricity, optics, radio, even every chemical reaction ever used).
                  • Gaia manifests through the Gravitational Force, which is perceived as the weakest of the four fundamental forces, but is the force which has the greatest influence on the evolution of planets, stars, galaxies, and even the Universe. As Gaia holds all things together, and works subtly in the background of the world, so does gravity keep all matter in the Universe bound to all other matter in the Universe. There are theories which postulate that gravity bleeds into other dimensions, which could represent Gaia's presence throughout the Tellurian. Though Gaia and gravity are hardly noticed, and easily defeated in apparent strength by the other forces (a refrigerator magnet can fight against the gravitational influence of the entire planet), they are fundamentally vital to the existence of the world as we experience it.

                  Some time later, he also (potentially) identified the location of the Wyrm's primary manifestation in the Technocratic cosmology: Sagittarius A*, the supermassive black hole at the center of the Milky Way. He came to this conclusion by the following:
                  • The Wyrm is viewed as a "devourer" and "destroyer", the original purpose of the Wyrm being driven mad and taken to the extreme by its current state of mind. This mirrors a black hole exactly, "consuming" and destroying matter, energy, space, and time, by means of the fundamental forces being "broken" and twisted to extreme levels.
                  • While there are many black holes in the galaxy, Sagittarius A* is the largest, which fits the idea of it being the "heart" of the Wyrm.
                  • It is located at the center of the galaxy, "bound in the heart of the Weaver's web".
                  • It is virtually impossible to see, hidden behind a vast cloud of gas and dust; this sounds like the Weaver wrapped it up in her web, to keep it bound tightly and out of view.
                  • Even beyond the cloud, a black hole has a horizon surrounding it which prevents any light from escaping it; this has the effect of making it invisible. This also fits the idea that the Weaver did not want to "see" the Wyrm and its destruction of her creations, so she abused the Natural Laws under her purview: she could not destroy a black hole, but she could hide it, so she at least didn't have to see them.

                              Thank you, all! Glad you enjoyed it. Joshua is, as I mentioned, my favorite character; he's had many opportunities to expand his Enlightenment of the Tellurian, and he has taken every challenge as a chance to grow, mentally and spiritually. His combination of the two cosmologies is very representative of his personality and Genius/Avatar/soul.

                              Since people liked it, there are a few expansions I can make on the Fundamental-Interactions/Triat-Gaia bit.
                              • All four fundamental forces were thought to be combined into a single, Unified Fundamental Force at the creation of the Universe. Shortly thereafter, they split apart into distinct forces. This mirrors some ideas of cosmology in which the Triat came from Gaia, or all four existed together in some unknowable form as (The) One.
                              • While Electromagnetism and the Weak Nuclear Force (Weaver and Wyrm) appear to be different forces at normal, everyday energies, they exhibit... unusual interactions at extremely high energy levels / very deep analysis. In these instances, they appear to be fundamentally linked in some way; these energy levels would have been present shortly after the creation of the universe, after the other forces had split. This mirrors some interpretations that the Weaver and Wyrm are linked at some faint and weak metaphysical level.
                              • It is most important to know that Gravity (Gaia) is unique and distinct from the other forces (the Triat) in one very special way: there exists no antithesis of it. All other forces have some "opposite" force, demonstrated most prominently by Electromagnetism with the two poles of a magnet. There is one "charge" of a force which attracts the opposite "charge" of the force, and the two can cancel each other out / neutralize. However, gravity (Gaia) does not exhibit this trait. Gravity is only additive. Gravity cannot be canceled out. Gravity cannot be shielded against or transformed in any way. Gravity applies to any form of particle that has mass or moves through space-time, unlike other forces which do not interact with multiple forms of matter or only in some circumstances. Gravity only attracts and pulls together, and never repels or pushes away. Gaia understands that only by embracing her children, may she help them; she must never shun them or push them away, no matter how they might squabble and try to offend her in their immaturity.
