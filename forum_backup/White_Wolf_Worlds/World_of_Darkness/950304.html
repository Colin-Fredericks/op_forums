                                      Our ST once told us he'd had an idea for a chronicle for quite a long time already he wanted to play and we did it. It was based on CWoD for rules as we all know that system quite well but the setting was different.
                                      It started with him telling us to create simple mortal characters living in the city we actually live in in RL. He gave us some restrictions on character creation (I think it was a dot less in attributes or some such thing, don't remember 100%).
                                      We figured out what each of our last memories were (our characters didn't know each other yet) and then we woke up in the basement of some house with some small clues, some basic supplies (batteries, flashlight, a bit of food, stuff like that) and with the door nailed shut wondering what had happened.
                                      When we left the house we found the city in pretty much an apocalyptic state with signs of corruption and dangerous supernatural creatures on the loose.
                                      He gave us a (RL) map of the city where several things were marked with not much of a clue as to what the markings meant.
                                      Later in the game we one by one discovered, that each of us was some kind of supernatural creature. Which one was randomly decided from a book about such things he had. I ended up finding out I was an initiate tempus mage (curiously without having any memory about being a member of such an order and having to find clues about this version of my past), another PC was a werewolf, another a Lamia. Good and evil were trying to convince us to join their respective sides (as a group or seperately). lots of hard choices to make and all. We werent able to leave the city either which added to the mystery.

                                      in the end it turned out to have been an artificially provoked dream that basically was our minds interpretation of a indoctrination programm while our bodies lay in a secret lab where military was trying to produce supernatural super-soldiers and the chronicle ended with us finding out about this, realising what was happening and breaking out of it. Joining "Heaven" would have meant the experiment was successful in producing an obedient soldier, joining "Hell" would have meant the experiment was successful in at least producing a supernatural murder-machine that can be thrown at an enemy. One of the PCs had actually joined the "heaven"-side for quite some time and my own character was very close to succumb to evil at one point but in the end we all managed to break out of the lab on our own will without succumbing to either.

                                      EDIT: now that I wrote all that I realise it doesnt really fit the question 100% as it technically wasn't set in an entirely different world. It was more a different plane of reality so to say. We went to a few other places (and times) as well when we discovered portals in a few places and other strange things happened (like my mage botching on a particularly difficult roll trying to achieve some time-magic effect)

                                              Well, a few years ago i did a sword & sorcery kind of game using a (completely made up) "lost age" sometime between the Impergium or just after it, where certain umbral spirits would grow fat and powerful upon ties with not just packs of garou but whole "noble families" of kinfolk heroes directing tribal nations though religious guidance or outright conquest, so, sort of a "Werewolf meets Birtright with some touches of Conan' kind of setting and my players had loads of fun playing with it. In fact it was far from the only time i toyed with the "lost mist-enshrouded era" card while STing, as i once sketched a more crossover/gonzo "A World Darkening" chronicle, though that one didn't go all that far, unfortunately.

                                              Beside this once did a very funky crossover game extrapolating from the Mage's Digital Web and how it supposedly connected with other supernatural information webs (like the Malkavian's Madness Network among other stuff) to set up the Planescape-esque idea of Logos, the ocean where all datastreams flow, all knowledge can be found and reality separates from fiction by unclear arcane mechanisms not well-compreended (or perceived) even by those beings who believe themselves its, as plunging itself into the depths of what is and what is not can be a most complex (and perilous) matter.
