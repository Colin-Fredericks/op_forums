    Anything you have the Technocracy can take?

    05-20-2017, 10:54 PM
    The Technocracy has often been portrayed as all powerful on many occasions and I'm curious as to how true that is. Can a mage make it so the Technocracy can't just take his Node, Sanctum, job, or girlfriend even if they are aware of the mage and where to find him?

    This is actually less to do with Mage and more to do with Changeling. Changeling books tend to talk about the Technocracy in terms of capturing Freeholds and sanitizing them. If, after monumental effort, a band of Changelings perform Reverie and Rapture in a specific location long enough that it can be turned into a level 5 Freehold and then the Technocracy finds out, is it possible for the Changelings to simply tell the Technocracy to piss off?

      Well, that depends. The answer is technically 'no' - nothing you do can ever make you totally safe from your enemies no matter who or what they are, barring outright Ascension or removing yourself totally from existence and into a different one. In practical terms, yes, it's actually very easy to make it so they 'can't just take'. The just is the key. All you have to do is make it too risky for the Technocracy to sanction, which is easy enough to do with three key ingredients.

      1. Stay discreet. The quiet Hermeticist who isn't summoning spirits to bring him his car keys and instead is easy for everyone, including other mages, to mistake as just 'guy with a good occult library and good luck', isn't going to get the same priority of purging as 'I AM LORD ALDEMORT, WIELDER OF THE SEVENTY-TWO NAMES, AND I COMMAND THEE TO BRING ME A SLURPEE FROM THE 7/11, HAZIMEL!'

      2. Surround yourself by critical infrastructure or lots and lots of innocent lives, preferably 'valuable' ones. If one word from you can blow the shit out of a preschool, the more Human technocrats might have second thoughts about going after you with full force - especially if you've managed to pull off #1. If it can also shatter a piece of useful - ideally Technocratic and not just mundane - infrastructure, even the ones who forgot about kindness and mercy might have to rethink their strategy.

      3. Always have a smokescreen and operate through it. It wasn't you at the house next to the Technocratic Warehouse and Mr. Robot's Preschool for Precocious Youngsters (ITX had to go into some weird sidelines to make the budget meet this last year, alright? Don't question it.) even if, to all casual or even moderately in depth examinations, it appeared to be you. It was a consor you'd smudged about a bit until he looked like you, had a similar aura to you, and wrapped in enough 'you' to pop positive as you for divination etcetera. Your real names (including your magical motto) should never appear linked in any way to your smokescreen.

      As for a bunch of changelings, well, that depends on where the Freehold is and which game is taking precedence. Are you playing Changeling? Then yes, with Heroic and Valiant effort - preferably with stirring speeches, some kind of magic quest, and a rallying cry for allies from the opposing court or even the shadow court in this Hour of Direst Need - the Changelings can win. Bonus points if the win requires the Great and Noble Sacrifice of a Hero. If it's Mage, nah, they're pretty much fucked.

                      Yeah, the key here it's to be sure that putting you down isn't worth the effort.

                      You're not fighting against a single enemy, but against an overaching organization that encompases more than the planet earth. What you're asking it's like "can my freehold survive a fight with the Garou Nation"...well, if the garou believe you're a treath on the level of the Storm Eater, then no, there's no way you can survive against them...but if you're not that treathening, then you're not fighting against the Garou Nation, but against a pack or two. Sure, they can ask for reinforces, but what are the odds that they will receive them?...if you're too hard to take, and not too much of a treath for the Nation as a whole, they may decide to allow you to stay rather than face the humillation of asking other Garou (and, likely, be refused).

                      With the Technocracy you can play a similar strategy. The Technocracy it's more prone to send reinforces than the Garou Nation, but it has too many fronts to take care off. If they consider that you aren't worth the effort, they will leave you for the end of their "to do" list. And, since it's a very long list, this may buy you a couple of centuries.

                      To do this you need to be powerful enough so it's a pain in the ass to take you down, harmelss enough so it's not so much of a pain in the ass to live with you, and worthless enough so puting you down doesn't compensate the effort.

                      You can't beat the Technocracy, but the local Amalgam?, yes you may be able to beat the local Amalgam. If you're too much to take in an elegant move, they might need to ask for reinforcements...but if they do, they need to provide evidence that you're worth the effort to move troops and money to take. If they just ask for help because they would like to keep your freehold, and it isn't big enough to make a difference for the local projects of the Technocracy, while you're not very treathening to people nor to the Consensus, then the Symposium might decide that the Amalgam isn't very efficient (even if they decide to send the reinforces)...and no Amalgam wants that.

                      Incidentally, this it's what Archmages do. 9/10 if the time, the Technocracy preffers to let them rot in their Horizon Realms rather that send a fleet to take them down...then again, that's more or less how Porthos met his fate, so even being at THAT power level isn't a total guarantee-though OoH had that chantry in Mars during 500 years (more or less) without being too subtle about it, so you can say that having overwhelming power and being useless does buy you some time.

                      It's worth to note than to detect a Freehold you need Spirit in addition to Prime (as per Lost Dreams), so only a Construct with a Void Engineer can truly discover your little powersource. Heck, only a Void Engineer, or a guy with dots in the very rare Dimensional Science may have the power to see the true natrue of your faes...
