        Pretty much, yes.

        As I said in my first post here, Syndics are suspected by many of not actually being mages. They are, but extreme distaste for the vulgar stuff makes many question whether anything they do is actually magic. The human resources thing is one such example of that.

        Their other preferred instruments are things like accessing hidden features of otherwise mundane technology and or having technology that's “premium”, doing what the regular model would do except better; doing the same with one's own skills, knowledge's, and talents (Syndics are very much into being the best that they can be), and spending money in ways that don't actually work in reality (such as writing a check to buy a restaurant on the spot, just so you can boss the staff around). They try not to do things like making stuff appear out of thin air. But as I said a few posts back, they could do it using a 3D printer.

Hot-dropping of high level equipment from Iteration X. Holographic emitters to prevent Sleeper Witnesses. Stimulants and nootropics for the workers and supervisors, for tireless work, coordination, and motivation. And a substantial bonus for making such a great and good thing.

High powered equipment causes collateral damage and injuries. Overwork causes worker hearts to explode and livers to fail. Or maybe the structure collapses because you just rushed the work.

                                        Rather than workers moving in fast forward, I would handle it as an extreme version of time-motion study: no individual action by anyone is supernaturally fast; but there isn't a wasted motion anywhere, and the crew operates in concert like a well choreographed dance. So the job gets done incredibly fast.

                                        Now, I wouldn't expect this to work with, say, a crew of five people. Note would I expect it to work if you grab a bunch of random people off the street. You need enough manpower for the task at hand, and they need to be competent at the task. Using Management and Human Resources merely results in them being more efficient. Likewise, you'll need to apply them with the materials to do their job.

                                        If you're talking about having a building literally appear out of nowhere, well, that's not something the Syndicate does. TANSTAAFL. You need to put the money, material, manpower, and time into the space to construct the building; what their Magick can do is to streamline the process.
