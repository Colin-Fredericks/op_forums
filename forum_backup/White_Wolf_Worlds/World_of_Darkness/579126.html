                          1.) The Abyss doesn't exist. Its walls do, as a physical barrier where our world ends... and nothing extends beyond it. Anything you say about the abyss will probably be wrong because it doesn't exist. There is no space, no up no down no left no right. There are no lights, no sounds, no tastes, no touches. There is nowhere, and time does not exist within it as it does to us. Seconds and centuries are the same, yet thanks to a cruel fluke of the creator the consciousnesses of the fallen within can still continue to process. They are the only thing that exists within the pit: their will, their thoughts, and the faintest sounds of humanity just beyond the bars of their cell. Some poor souls would occasionally wander too close to the pit, and be snatched by sensory deprived demons intent on doing SOMETHING, ANYTHING! Those tortured souls became spectres, hunting through the tempest for other souls. Other powerful demons would struggle to project themselves into the nightmares of the living, while their fellow inmates would attempt to stop them from torturing their former charges.

                          2.) They were beings of pure faith, with physical manifestations created from their faith alone. If you look at page 90, Time of Judgement you'll find a sidebar that describes how Angels work, and should the world's ambient levels of faith progress to a level sufficient to sustain demons they too could exist without their mortal forms. It's kinda like oxygen. Without enough air in the room, you need a suit. Their human vessels are that suit. But also.... without tying themselves to a vessel the abyss would suck them in like a dust bunny down a vacuum cleaner. The Abyss would need to be destroyed before they could exist freely.

                          3.) Futuristic... sorta. They could produce blades of light and weapons that launched infernal hellfire or other terrible magical essences. They could create things that behaved like our technology appears to us, or like futuristic weaponry might look. Remember, any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic. Surely, any sufficiently complex magic could mimic technology as well. It's just a difference of what systems underlay their operation.

                                                      All those are viable explanations. By myself, I like to unify at least Vampire's and Demon's Abyss.

                                                      But the point isn't about what the Abyss IS. There are no official answer to that. Its about what the Abyss CAN BE.

                                                      Demons don't have an accurate answer to any property of the Abyss. They have a PERCEPTION of it. Not one I don't like, mind you. The point is, there are no guarantee about it being what they think. Or what anybody else think.

                                                      High-level Obtenebration and Abyss Mysticism give you the power to summon weird phenomena from the Abyss. What are such phenomena? No one knows, and no official answer was given, ever. The vampire's soul shapes the shadow-stuff? Maybe. Those shadows are sentient beings? Maybe. Demons, or even stranger creatures? Maybe. Lots of maybes. The shadow-stuff could be made of psychic ectoplasm, manifested Torment, idiotified demons or something else entirely.

                                                      It is up to the ST to define what the Abyss is in his game, and for sure those are all good ideas. But the only honest answer to the question 1 is: no one knows for sure, get a lot of books about what can be the Abyss or not and decide by yourself.
