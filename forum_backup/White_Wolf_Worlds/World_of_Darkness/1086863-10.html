                                                        When other player or GM seemingly violates a sacred trust between players, you just say "Full-stop!" and slowly, clearly ask them to explain what they, as a player and character what they want from your character, and why. Based on how stupid (or reasonable, dunno - miscommunications happen) their response is, you either agree with their suggested course of action, mediate to reconsider, leave immediately or get their faces acquainted with sufficient amount of force applied through pressure of your upper appendages until their.. uhm.. cognitive processes readjust to the new cultural paradigm - then one or both of you leave this gaming table for necessary self-medication purposes (ice cream.. or just ice).
