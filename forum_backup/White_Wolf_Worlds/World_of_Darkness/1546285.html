    A New World of Darkness: When Reality Merge

    11-22-2024, 09:56 AM
    In 2003-2004 when the time of judgment originally started, the end of the world wasn’t coming. Instead it was the merging of two worlds. During this time, the mundane reality wasn’t stable, so strange stuff started to happen. People remember stuff from a life they never had, people's personalities suddenly change, people meet their doppleganger, the land starts to shift weirdly, and stuff randomly disappears and reappears.
    When the mundane world became stable again in 2004, most things merged, but a lot of things didn’t, about 100 million humans worldwide have no one to merge with (all of which come from CofD world), reality itself was rewritten to accommodate the merging of two worlds, history and every being in existence, so that people don’t question why the street is different or why they own a pet.
    But only the mundane world becomes stable, the supernatural world is still unstable, making the CofD shadow denizen (which include Unmerge Mortals, and Hunters) to become supernaturally invisible to WoD residents.
    The CofD shadow denizen looks like a shadowy figure without any defining feature, to the WoD resident (various splat, monster, and mortals with supernatural abilitiy). They also have a glitchy effect on their body, their voice distorted beyond comprehension, they smell more like a concept than actual smell, some smell like “when a human bleed to death on a rose bush”, while other smell like “fur grind with rust, with a hint of spiritual essence”. They taste like how they smell, and they feel like the 4 states of matter at the same time. But one thing is consistent with them is that they feel wrong, even the most corrupt of the fallen or the worst of the fomori still feel the wrongness of CofD shadow denizen.
    It wasn’t until 2013 for the supernatural world to finally become stable, and nothing in the supernatural world merged, because it would make reality too unstable.
    Reality rewrite explains the reason for CofD denizen supernatural invisibility by making mythology out of it. Back in the ancient time, before the flood, a group of supernatural council was form, they consist of monster, human, and stranger being, but they all have one thing in common, they hate the world, they wish that they don’t have to mingle with the rest of reality. So, they did just that, by combining their power, the council created the spell “Separating Unreality Supernal Compact Cure All Version C” with this they become effectively non-existence within the supernatural world, but mundane being can still see them just fine. But this come with a catch, if the spell were ever to be broken, it’s a sign that the end of the world is coming.
    The Red Star is still visible and in 2024 the Time of Judgement finally came.

    System
    Supernatural Tolerance trait differ from Splat to Splat. Supernatural Tolerance is the ability to resist the power of CofD shadow denizen.

    Vampire Supernatural Tolerance is equal to their Blood Potency rating.
    Werecreature Supernatural Tolerance is equal to their Gnosis rating
    Mage Supernatural Tolerance is equal to their Arete rating
    Wraith Supernatural Tolerance is equal to their Pathos rating
    Changeling Supernatural Tolerance is equal to their Glamour rating
    Kuei Jin Supernatural Tolerance is equal to their Dharma rating
    Imbue Supernatural Tolerance is equal to their main Virtue rating
    Fallen Supernatural Tolerance is equal to their Faith rating
    Amenti Supernatural Tolerance is equal to their Sekhem rating
    Hsien Supernatural Tolerance is equal to their Tao rating

    CofD Shadow Denizen

    All of these abilities only affect the Wod Resident unless the power said otherwise.

    Imbue are consider to be Mundane Mortal and so are their power, but they are still affected by abilities that use Willpower and abilities that also affect the mundane. But ability like Mundane Land doesn’t affect the Imbue.

    Mundane Land: When near the vicinity of the Shadow Denizen, supernatural powers that are from World of Darkness don’t work. The vicinity is equal to Denizen Willpower + Supernatural Tolerance + 10 - Resident Supernatural Tolerance, every point is equal to 15 meter radius. When using magic outside of the vicinity but targeted at a Denizen, the difficulty of the roll is Denizen Willpower + Supernatural Tolerance + 15.

    Bloody Shadow: The Denizen become undetectable within darkness, and even without the shadow, trying to detect the Denizen in anyway is difficulty 30.

    Baleful Bane: When the Denizen deal damage they can spend 1 Willpower per damage dealt to upgrade that damage into a higher form of damage so bashing turn to lethal, and lethal turn to aggravated, and aggravated damage become double.

    Shifting Fate: When within the Denizen vicinity, the Resident must roll 8 or higher to succeed instead of the usual 6 or 7.

    Ghastly Sight: When seeing the Shadow Denizen, the Resident suffer a reduce dice pool equal to Denizen Willpower + Supernatural Tolerance + 10 - Resident Supernatural Tolerance to every roll targeting the Denizen, this last until the Resident haven’t seen the Shadow Denizen for 1 hour straight.

    Fading Fantasy: By spending 1 Willpower the Denizen can make anyone that saw them forget their existence for day equal to Denizen Willpower + Supernatural Tolerance + 10 - Resident Supernatural Tolerance. This work on Mundane Mortal.

    Tactical Advantage: By spending 1 Willpower the Denizen can either increase the difficulty of the roll or decrease the dice pool of the Resident equal to Denizen Willpower + Supernatural Tolerance + 10 - Resident Supernatural Tolerance. And if the Resident fail at that roll, the Denizen regain back the Willpower spent.

    False Cover: By spending 1 Willpower The Denizen can hide their true self by disguising as a normal human, but this can only last for a day before the disguise become unstable and fall off. And when disguising, their feeling of wrongness is gone, but they also lose all of their Non-Willpower spending power, except for Death Undying. This power also fool Mundane Mortal.

    Death Undying: When Denizen die, they explode causing their existence and any trace of it, to be erased from reality, only those who witness its final moment, and other Denizen can remember them. This cause the local reality within the Denizen vicinity to become extremely unstable for 30 days, and might even cause a Resident to become a Denizen. Those Denizen who can come back from the dead does comeback, but in a completely different body.

    Flesh and Soul: Due to their instability, they take more damage from mundane sources and less from supernatural sources. Mundane Bashing become Lethal, Lethal become Aggravated, and Aggravated damage is doubled. Supernatural Bashing become No Damage, Lethal become Bashing, and Aggravated become Lethal.

    Keys of Nightmare: Can spend 1 Willpower to The High Umbra, Middle Umbra, or Low Umbra for hours equal to the Denizen Willpower + Supernatural Potency + 10.

    Soul Shatter: When a Resident would die from a Denizen attack, the Resident instead of dying become a Mortal Human, even if it’s impossible and they were never human to begin with, if the Resident doesn’t have a physicial form they become fused with the nearest one. This cause their soul to be broken, making it impossible for them to have supernatural power, or their soul would shatter, this make them effectively Mundane and they can now see the Shadow Denizen clearly.

    Chronicle of Power: Each Denizen type also have their own unique power call Chronicle.

    Chronicle of Mortal: Change the vicinity calculation to Willpower + Supernatural Tolerance + 10 + Highest Resistance Attribute, and change from 10 meter radius per point to 60 meter radius per point. And increase the difficulty of targeting Mortal Denizen with supernatural power to 50.

    Chronicle of Vampire: The vicinity within Vampire Denizen become dark enough for it not to worry about sunlight, but for every 20 minutes (cumulative) that the Denizen would normally be burn by the sun, they get -1 to a Attribute, until they sleep, this -1 can be stack, but it can’t reduce an Attribute to 0.

    Chronicle of Werewolf: The Gauntlet rating within the Werewolf Denizen vicinity become 1, and the Denizen doesn’t need to spend Willpower to go into the Umbra. And every 45 minutes, the Gauntlet rating become 0 for 5 minutes.

    Chronicle of Mage: Mage Denizen can randomly gain power from Resident that are within their vicinity, the power they can gain is equal to their Supernatural Tolerance + 10. But they the difficulty of using these power is increase equal to the Supernatural Tolerance of the Resident the Mage Denizen stole from.

    Chronicle of Changeling: Every Resident within the vicinity of a Changeling Denizen must roll Resolves + Supernatural Tolerance every 30 minutes with difficulty of Denizen Supernatural Tolerance + 10, if they fail they gain a Derangement or Disorder.

    Chronicle of Geist: At the start of every day, every Resident within the Sin-Eater vicinity, experience memories of a person that they killed, nearly killed, or hurt badly. They must amend the wrong doing that they have with that person, whether by making people remember them, finished their unfinished business etc. If they don’t do it by the end of the day, they suffer 1 Aggravated damage that can’t be heal. If a Resident would die this way, they will be reborn as a human with 10 Dot to spend on any Numina they wish, but they can’t become any other type of Supernatural without having their soul be destroyed.

    Chronicle of Hunter: All Resident within the Hunter Denizen vicinity gain a new Ban and Bane, these Ban and Bane are decided randomly but have a noticeable reason for way they have those Ban and Bane. These Ban and Bane can be known by the Hunter Denizen by rolling Wits + Occult with difficulty of 1.

    Chronicle of Demon: All Resident within the vicinity of a Demon Denizen, become paranoid of everyone and everything around them, giving +10 to the dice pool when acting on these paranoia, but -10 when those paranoia concern the Demon Denizen.

    Chronicle of Mummy: All Resident within the vicinity of a Mummy Denizen find themselves losing their memory every hour, and after 6 hours, the lost memory start to replay in real time within the mummy vicinity.

    Chronicle of Promethean: All Resident within Promethean Denizen vicinity suffer an amount of Lethal damage equal to the Promethean Denizen Supernatural Tolerance, this happen every 10 minutes, those with weakness to fire suffer Aggravated, those with resistance to fire suffer Bashing, if they’re immune they don’t suffer any damage.

    Chronicle of Beast: Resident within Beast Denizen vicinity feel a familial relationship with the Beast Denizen if the Beast so choose, which cancel all the debuff effect on the Resident, but making them unable to harm to resist the Beast demand.

    Chronicle of Deviant: Resident that enter the Deviant Denizen vicinity can’t use their power even when outside of the vicinity for hours equal to Deviant Denizen Willpower + Supernatural Tolerance + 10 - Resident Supernatural Tolerance.

      When a CofD Shadow Denizen dies, they leave behind their Skull, the Skull of a Denizen has a special ability that can be activated by spending Willpower within the Skull, the Willpower stored within the Skull is equal to their Willpower in life, this Willpower is fully recover after 24 hours. To use a Skull one must bond with it first, by spending a day and 1 Willpower, to bond with the Skull, when someone is bond with a Skull, they have to give up one of their temporary Willpower permanently, when another person bond with a Skull that is already bonded, the original owner of the Skull regain the temporary Willpower that they spend on the Skull. Skull power also affect the Mundane world. The owner could extend the Skull power duration or increase the area that it affect by activating the Skull within the same vicinity of the on-going Skull power. The Skull can be destroy by dealing Lethal damage equal to the Skull Willpower, or Aggravated damage equal to half of the Skull Willpower. When a Skull is destroy, the power instantly end. The Skull have an internal clock, so time manipulation power can’t affect it Willpower regeneration time. The Skull must be physically touch to be activated, so stealing a Skull can stop it owner from using it power.


      Mortal Skull


      Skull of Darkness: When activated, a shroud of supernatural darkness surround your vicinity, the vicinity is equal to Skull Willpower + Owner Willpower, each point is equal to 30 meter radius. The owner of the Skull isn’t affected by this darkness or any power that manipulate the mind, the senses, or the soul for the duration of the Skull power. To use any of the senses or do anything that use perception within the darkness, they must pass a difficulty 30 perception + alertness roll, applying any ability or tool that add die or success to the roll, but the difficulty of this roll can’t be change or modify by any tool, or ability. Only Imbue second sight could see through this Darkness without a roll. This power last for 30 minutes for every Willpower the Skull have.

      Apperance: A Human skull, but the bone is black.


      Skull of Mortality: When activated all Resident within the vicinity temporary become Mortal losing all of their monster power. The vicinity is equal to Skull Willpower + Owner Willpower, each point is equal to 10 meter radius. This power last for 15 minutes for every Willpower the Skull have. The owner isn’t affected by the power. This doesn’t affect the power of the Imbue, Numina, and the mortal off-spring of a Splat (Dhampir, Dhampyr, Kinfolk, and Kinian).

      Apperance: Normal Human skull, but with a slight glitch effect and a little bit blue.


      Skull of Machinery: When activated, the owner can create one new Law within the local reality within the Skull vicinity, the vicinity is equal to Skull Willpower + Owner Willpower, each point is equal to 45 meter radius. This Law can’t be change by any ability or power, but the Law also affect the owner, and the Law can’t be broken, but it can be exploited and bend, but continual bending and exploiting of the Law can cause backlash. Each time a character bend or exploit the Law, they gain add backlash die to their pool, and then roll for backlash, bending the Law give 1 backlash die, while exploiting it give 3, if the backlash die roll 8, 9, or 10, the character experience a backlash. The backlash die decrease by 3 after 24 hours. This power last for 1 day for every Willpower the Skull have. The owner can create multiple Law within the same vicinity, by activating the Skull multiple time, this doesn’t increase the area the Law affect, or increase the power duration, multiple Law within the same vicinity last the same as each other, with the shortest duration remaining, being the main duration.

      Apperance: Human skull with a cog in it fore head.


      Skull of Gothica: When activated, the Skull fused with the owner's body, they becomes the pinnacle of Goth, and gains the following power and ability. The Skull have a vicinity equal to Skull Willpower + Owner Willpower, each point is equal to 15 meter radius. This power last for 15 minutes for every Willpower the Skull have.

      Apperance: A human skull made out of stained glass.


      Constant Suffering: Fate isn’t on anyone's side, when within the Skull vicinity, detract the amount of success roll by the amount of die that didn’t succeed. This inculde the owner.


      Eternal Game: Once per roll the owner may reroll a number of die equal to the Skull Willpower when rolling.


      Painful Rebirth: When the owner die, the body turn to sand, leaving only the Skull left, the Skull will try to run away and find a safe place for the Owner to be revived. If the power end before the owner is revive, the owner get consumed by the Skull, and the Skull gain 1 permanent Willpower. Attempting to catch the Skull is a difficulty of 12. The owner suffer 1 Aggravated damage when they’re revived.


      Emotional Burst: When the owner spend Willpower everyone within the vicinity also lose Willpower without gaining any of the benefit of spending Willpower.


      Delusion of Self: The owner gain bonus die equal to Skull Willpower + Owner Willpower, when trying to gain information of the supernatural. But if the roll fail, treat it as a botch roll.


      Victorian Maximum: The owner soul, mind, and senses can’t be manipulate, or influence, in anyway, and gain +5 die to using anything that could be consider magic or weird science.


      Skull of Time: The owner can travel backward or foward up to 72 hour in time, they can affect local vicinity equal to Skull Willpower + Owner Willpower, each point is equal to 1 meter.

      Apperance: A human skull with a clock on it forehead.

                          Ok, so let me try it.

                          Skull of the Inner Beast: A skull taken from a kindred of the Gangrel clan. When activated it creates an area around the bearer of at least 10 yards and a maximum of 15 yards per point of willpower in the skull, within the area all inhabitants of Wod lose control of their inner beasts (if they have one) requiring them to make a frenzy test or similar every time they enter the area of ​effect of the skull, the bearer, however, is not affected, animals within the area of ​​effect become aggressive and attack anything they consider prey, the bearer of the skull can invest a point of willpower to direct the fury of the animals against a specific target for the duration of a scene. Alternatively, the skull can also be used to prevent a target from changing shape and lock them in their current form, for example a Kindred who would use Protean to transform into a bat or a Garou who tries to change from Homid to Lupus. If they are willing to do so, the target of the ability can invest a point of Willpower to perform the transformation normally, however, they will receive a point of aggravated damage for each turn required to complete their transformation due the effort.

                          Appearance: A common human skull, however with more developed canines and may or may not have deformities in the jaw or small horn-like appendages.

                          Affected races: Kindred, Fera, and Kuei Jin. Fallen do not have an inner beast, but when within the area of ​​effect of the skull they require a Willpower test, in case of failure each time they use a lore it will be in the high torment version.

                          Skull of Obsession: A skull taken from a Kindred of the Daeva clan. When activated, it can cause a number of individuals equal to the bearer's willpower + the willpower in the skull to develop an obsession related to humans, such as becoming obsessed with a lover or loved one, or in the case of a kindred becoming obsessed with consuming a certain type of blood. Should they refuse to pursue their obsession, those inflicted by the skull will have one die reduced from all their dice pools per scene until they take some action involving their obsession, however once per scene they can ignore this penalty at the cost of a point of willpower.

                          Appearance: A human skull with more developed canines.

                          Affected races: All.

                          Skull of the Worms: A skull supposedly taken from a kindred of the Nosferatu clan. A skull with a rather peculiar functionality, as it allows the bearer, even temporarily, to suppress and reverse the effect of any curse or ability that deforms the appearance of a given target, even in more serious cases such as the curse of the Nosferatu clan. However, its effect only lasts a number of hours equal to the willpower of the bearer + the willpower in the skull. After that, the skull will need to be “recharged” with vampiric vitae to be used again (3 + the willpower inserted in the skull).

                          Appearance: A human skull with more developed canines and small irregular holes around the entire skull.

                          Affected races: All.​
