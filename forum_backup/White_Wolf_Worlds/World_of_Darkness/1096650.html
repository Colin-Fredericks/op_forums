    The Book of Modern Dreams Project

    05-25-2017, 04:16 AM
    Several people have expressed interest in Jane_Dargason’s and my ‘Book of Modern Dreams’ project from the old Shadownessence forum, so we thought we would make a public thread for anyone that wants to give us feedback and input! We’ll use this to give updates on the project, and hopefully glean enough glamour from it to keep us going to completion!

    So without further ado, here is an outline of what we have brainstormed so far for the book:

    Urban Legends – How the Modern Dream has affected the changelings and the Dreaming, and details on city freeholds and dreams.

    To Infinity and Beyond – The impact of the Space-age Dream, and introducing the alien kith, the Grey, along with their thallain counterpart.

    A Dreamer’s High – Exploring the history and relationship between the Dreaming and mind-altering substances, how the ‘War on Drugs’ has affected the Dreaming, and introducing the bizarre Gallain called Narcotics.

    Post-Humanism – Delving deeper into the Modern Inanime, focusing on three specific phyla: the Mannikin ask what it is to be ‘human’, the Flickers, which are a much older phyla than many realize, and the A.I. elementals of Machina. These latter Inanima freak out even the Mannikin, and the ‘Ghosts-in-the-Shell’ have many worrying implications for the Inanime as a whole.

    Modern Nightmares – Exploring the thallain and darker-things of the Modern Dream. Including the Slender, Rippers, Darkly (mirror entities), and others.


    So that about covers the big ideas. Each section already has had a ton of research, and even some initial brainstorming, but all that is now being reviewed in light of C20 (especially the Inanime). We would love to hear peoples thoughts and ideas, and will keep posting here as things progress.
