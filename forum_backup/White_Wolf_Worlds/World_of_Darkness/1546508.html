    Next on the Technocracy's Hit List...

    11-30-2024, 10:47 PM
    The Scenario: The Technocracy has finally WON the Ascension War, definitively! The Council and Disparates are shattered, most of the survivors being taken out or brainwashed; the Marauders and Nephandi have been purged from Creation and chased back out of Reality; and the Crafts are hiding, hoping to avoid notice. After a period of regrouping, replenishing membership and repair, the Order of Reason is stronger than ever!

    The Inner Circle decides it's finally time to advance the Time Table and move on to wiping out the rest of the Reality Deviants corrupting the Tellurian. But...where to start?

    YOU are now pulling the strings of the Technocracy! All its resources are at your command...Who do you send them after first? The vampires who infest the bureaucracies of the cities? The Werecreatures who stalk the wilderness? The ghosts who haunt the living? The Fae who eat their dreams?

    And HOW will you do it? What strategies will you use to best take out your targets? What will you do if they prove tougher than you thought, and somehow turn the tides against you? The Consensus is waiting for YOU to decide!!

      With these crossovers, I have a few considerations.

      First, the Nefandi are near extinct. Does this mean that the other splats "nefandi-like" were also driven out?

      I ask because that would have to mean that the Wyrm agents the Were-beasts fight have also been driven back into the hole from which they came.

      If that is the case, then my next target would be the Were-beasts. Because they would be free to antagonize the Union, and they absolutely would be unfriendly to it.

      Vampires are a nuisance, but they actually clean up for their own. They are undeniably a threat, however they are funnily enough the splat with less risk of ruining things for the Union, given their so adamant "maskerade" doctrine. They are also very hard to put out of their holes, being experts into hiding. I would therefore place them at the bottom of problems to deal with after all others have been resolved.

      The Ghosts also have a policy of not messing with the living; besides, there's literally MILLIONS of them. A real herculean feat, which I don't consider feasible or desirable, unless the Union becomes so massively powerful that anything else is like bugs in comparison, but then such considerations would be unnecessary.

      I might be tempt to put the Changelings first, but only because they are probably the least dangerous of them all, thus I would use the Union's might to swiftly deal with it and have one less problem to deal with, before getting embroiled in more resource draining wars with other foes.

      But definitely, the Were-beasts would be an ideal target, as the first ones to go or after the Fae. And the way to do it would be similar (but not identical) to how the Wyrm agents fight them. Taking their Caerns is ideal for the Union, because it both weakens them while also strengthen the Union.

      With the Wyrm basically defeated thou, having the Void Engineers manage to direct several "dimensional Stasis entities" (call them "Weaver Spirits" if you will) would be a solid strategy.

      Then, at the level of the Masses, unleashing anti-environmentalist propaganda would also be massively effective.

      Now, if these prove to be a nut too tough to crack, then the Union's only options would be diplomacy, subversion or giving up.

      Diplomacy would involve trying to go to a settlement. Easy down on your bio-terrorism, and we will easy down on the Progrom. As both sides realize the costs of such a war, it could actually be feasible.

      Subversion implies trying to involve the other splats on the fight, not necessarily in favor of the Union, but it would be sufficient to have them against the Were-beasts. No actual garantees here that it would work, but it's worth a shot. This could actually succeed, because the Weres aren't the most diplomatic of the splats, but it has it's own risks and problems.

      Giving up actually means to retreat from the all out war, saving resources and reassessing things in order to try again in the future. Thus, instead of an active full force Pogrom, the Union recedes, go into defense to protect it's assets, let things cool up and live to fight another day rather than burning itself up into a futile crusade. Then, rather then going full berserk to exterminate the enemy as initially planned, it's back to "contention mode" as it has always been doing, with small punctual actions rather than major global mobilization
