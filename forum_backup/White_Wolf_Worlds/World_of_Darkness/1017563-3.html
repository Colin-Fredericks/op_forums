    So they're still intent on going full steam ahead with that "2nd Impergium" thing for W:tA? You know, in order to make that a thing that could practically happen, you'd have to massively increase the numbers of Red Talons and extremist fringe camps in other tribes, almost completely kill off the Bone Gnawers, Children of Gaia, and Glass Walkers (the three most populous tribes of werewolves in the world), massively increase Garou and possibly Fera numbers all around, and also increase their power level to the point where crossover threads wouldn't make any sense because the Garou and/or Fera would be able to completely shut anyone down, isolate them, and then tear them apart.

    All of the prior material has repeated numerous times that a new Impergium can't be done. A few of the reasons appear above, but seriously, the game was made to illustrate that such workings were in the past, to such a degree that human history hadn't started yet. If you want a 2nd Impergium that badly, it'd have to occur in the Renaissance, at the absolute latest, and hence would be an alternate campaign setting. It's not a viable direction going forward with the metaplot, unless you're getting extremely loose with what you mean by "Impergium".

    And the kicker? It wouldn't even be in the most populous region of the planet. That's primarily Beast Courts territory, and they have a mandate against practicing that sort of thing if you're not helping a wererat, or actually a wererat, and being extremely surreptitious about it.

If you spend it however you get a red hunger die or more in your pools (for probably all your rolls) which will act to increase your chance of entering frenzy. If you dont, you do something you dont want to, avoiding compulsion, but you will have more composure when the chance you frenzy comes up, and less hunger, meaning less likely to frenzy.

                                Over 40 books currently in development. Hopes for an adult tv show by 2020.
                                System will use dots and d10s and be recognisable to older fans. However some dice may be customised and the inclusion of hunger dice is a sign that further innovations of the d10 system are planned.
                                Kenneth Hite of Trail of Cthulhu is on the team as V5 developer
                                Vampires will have two looks, one for the mortal world and one for Elysium
                                The Masquerade more important than ever
                                Its so important the Anarchs have had to agree to join the Cam or be exterminated for not following the 1st Tradition

                                        A lot of what we've heard so far is going to be a matter of execution, so I'm reserving judgement. Bringing on Ken is pretty much the first truly smart thing WW has done so far, but it doesn't make up for a lot of other bad ideas.

                                        But what worries me the most?

                                        The "50% of the profits" thing.

                                        From what I understand, this is for the Storyteller's Vault material, not how they're going to treat hired freelancers (because no freelancer is going to take that deal).

                                        This is insanely exploitable. WWP puts in almost no work for their share of the profits, and can count any expense on their end managing the ST Vault as overhead no profit. If something is on the ST Vault for $5, the fans that made it don't get $2.50 every sale. That $5 is going to mostly get eaten up by credit card feeds, website and server costs, anything WW wants to claim as marking costs, anything WW wants to claim as costs to use of their IP, and easily drive down the final profit that gets divvied up to more like $0.50, leaving the content creators $0.25 a sale.

                                        There's a reason companies love royalty models, and creative content creators hate having to use them because companies are holding distribution as a bludgeon to keep creators into business models that are bad for them. It's how record companies profit from album sales while bands have to tour to actually make money, and how YouTube keeps their content providers in line. It's a model the promotes quantity over quality, because WWP will make money from all the sales even if no individual piece of content sells a lot, and WWP has to do almost no work at all.

                                        This also hurts freelancers because full professional supplements now have to compete with fan content. This means either less work because WWP will only work on the "big" projects like the core system and setting books (the 40 planned books sounds like a lot, but from the descriptions isn't that much when you consider their plans to make more smaller essential books, like a core V5 system book and at least three Sect books is already four planned books for what V20 does in one), or WWP will try to reduce their freelancer pay towards either royalties or to keep the price of professional books competitive with fan ST Vault books.

                                        50% to the content creators sounds good, but it's the sort of thing that makes me nervous because it sounds good without guaranteeing anything.
