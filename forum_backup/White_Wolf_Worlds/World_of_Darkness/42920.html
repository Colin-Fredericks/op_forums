    Nephilim: The Revelation (Weekly Project)

    01-05-2014, 12:13 PM
    Originally called Nephilim: The Chosen, it was a big project that I took on (with help from other fans) until I lost track of it and it ended. Now I'm going to finish it this time for good and any help will be appreciated. The original idea came to be in a dream where it was revealed the Hunters were really the children of the Angels. The idea grew and changed a lot from it's original concept, especially after I started reading Demon: The Fallen. I've made some changes since it's original draft so enjoy.

    Opening Fiction

    After what happened to Old Man Vanderhalt, the best thing Margie could do was honor his request after the cops left. Sure he was a grouchy, pissy old man that bitched every time she turned on the radio, but even he didn't deserve to be torn-up by God-knows-what a half hour ago.

    "It's yours," He said, grabbing her arm as a rush of power flooded into her. "The blessing of The Host; the powers of the heavens."

    "Come on, stupid, say of something." Paper-cuts were more than enough to make Margie uneasy, but now she just wanted to scream. Blood and what she swore was a bit of bone peeking out of his left arm were now staining her clothes. "Just don't run out screaming. Don't run out screaming..."

    That's when she felt something in her hand, a small green notebook. "The Tome of Ancients, the knowledge of this world, keep it safe from them."

    "Them? Who's them?" And he was gone. "Don't just leave me here!" Her mind running a mile a minute, but an ideal still came to her. "Heavens, that means angels, angels mean holiness...white magic." Tossing the "Tome of Ancients" into her apartment, she placed her palms on his chest. After all, an entire childhood playing Japanese console RPGs wouldn't lie. "Heal! Revive!"

    She could feel streams of tears falling from her eyes as she tried again and again and again until the police arrived. "No...I can't be incompetent at everything."


    LEXICON

    Aasimar: The offspring of a mortal and an angel.
    Angel: A heavenly creature created to aid The Host. Also a slang term used by outsiders to describe the Celestials as a whole.
    Angelos: A Celestial that has transcended into a superior form of purity and goodness that is beyond that of an Angel. They are a perfect balance between a Human’s free will and ability to adapt and an Angel’s purity and goodness.
    Apocalyptic Form: The physical reflection a Celestial's divine nature.
    Aura: The inner glory a Celestial (except the Infused) possesses.
    Candidates: The other gods (Gaia, Osirus, etc.) in the World of Darkness. One of them will arise as the New God. Candidacy could also go to creatures that were able to ascend to godhood or some other powerful state like Caine, Saulot, Charon, Lucifier, or Lillith.
    Celestials: The generalized term when referring to all four Divinities.
    Chari: Undead celestial beings who have been revived by the mysterious Charon to do his bidding.
    Charon: The god-like creator of the Chari.
    Children of Goliath: The warriors of the Celestials, dedicated to fighting both Mystics and Mortals who would seek to bring the world into further ruin.
    Connection: A gift bestowed by The Host to the Celestials (with the exception of the Infused) where they can summon memories of their ancestors, their Grigori or Charon to help them.
    Deadmen: Mortals who were unjustly killed due to the hatred and small-mindedness of others and have been revived as the avenging angels of the Celestials.
    Deamos: A Celestial that has fallen so far from grace, they become a creature worse than any Demon.
    Divinity: The breed a Celestial is from (Aasimar, Chari, Infused, Nephilim).
    End of Times, The: Gehenna, the Apocalypse, the Sixth Age, etc.
    Falling: When the imperfections of a Celestial’s Human side overpower them, pulling them away from their angelic heritage.
    Favor(s): The Host’s blessing that allows a Celestial to fuel her powers.
    First-Born, The: The original Nephilim that were supposed to have killed millions of years ago.
    Grigori: An Aasimar’s angelic parent.
    Heritage: An assigned group that a Celestial belongs to.
    Host, The: The All-God who has watched over the World of Darkness before leaving Heaven to prepare for the coming of its successor.
    Infused: Mortals who have come in contract with holy energy and become Celestials as a result.
    Light-Bringers: Healers and harbingers of hope.
    Lost, The: Celestials that become evil.
    Miracle: A special power that a Celestial can use.
    Muses: The artisans of the Celestials.
    Mystics: The general term for the other creatures (Vampires, Hunters, Wraiths, etc.) in the World of Darkness.
    Nephilim: The half-breed children of Fallen Angels and Mortals.
    New God, The: An unknown deity chosen by The Host to take its place during the New World.
    New World, The: The World of Darkness' next incarnation that the New God will rule over.
    Nymphs: Caretakers of the spiritual and physical world.
    Revelation: When a mortal learns of her divine mission and heritage.
    Rising: When a Celestial’s morality rating raises to the point that they become more angelic in thought and deed.
    Saulites: Former mortals who have committed acts of evil and now have a second chance as Celestials.
    Seers: Celestials charged with the duty of guiding the other heritages.
    Seraphim: Mediators and diplomats of the Celestials.
    Sign: A physical side effect a Celestial (except the Infused) receives from her Revelation.
    Shepherds: Celestials who are in charge of preparing and guiding humanity for the New God.
    Talents: Magical items.
    Touched, The: Mortal followers who know of the Celestial’s divine heritage.
    Twist: The mental side effect a Celestial (except the Infused) receives from her Revelation.
    Watchers: The spies and scholars of the Celestials.


    Slang

    Angel-Parent: A slang term for Grigori.
    Cherubs: Celestials under the age of 13.
    Cyberpunks: A term used to describe Mages who use science in their magic. Usually applies to Virtual Adapts, Interation X, Sons of Ether, and Progenitors.
    Ferrymen: Slang term for the Chari.
    Spirit-Born: A term for Celestials used by outsiders who are ignorant of the heavens (Lupus Garou, people who are raised outside of monotheistic religions, etc).
    Half-Angel: Slang term for the Aasimar.
    Half-Fallen: Slang term for Nephilim.
    Orphans: Slang term for the Infused.
    Wizards: What Celestials call Mages.

    NOTE: In the game, The Host is a powerful being beyond gender so it is referred to as it. Also, Charon may or may not be the original one and just impostor taking that name.

          History

          From the Tome of Ancients

          Margie, if you are reading this I'm already gone. I know it's hard to believe, but out of all the idiots in this Host-forsaken apartment complex, you were always the least stupid. As horrible as this world is, there's a war a-brewing: between Heaven, Hell and the rest of us poor bastards. And it's up you and the rest of our kind to make sure it doesn't get any worse.

          I know you think all think I was nothing but a bitter old curmudgeon, but even I can understand love; a very weird, twisted kind of love that only the Heavens could possess. The Host, out of love, wanted nothing but to preserve our happiness and Lucifer (with his own version) believed in our potential. Either way humanity was going to get the blunt of it: ignorance or hardship.

          With a few fancy words, humanity chose the later. In a way I can commend Lucifer and his Angels, they were good to their word and even stayed stayed to teach them everything they know. And then I remember The Host damned them all in return and I just want to strangle them. When you get as old as me, you see and lose too much.

          We watched as love drove both sides into wars and when the dust settled, it gave the Fallen Angels a new idea. If man could never return to Eden, they would help make a new one on earth with their own special offspring, the Nephilim. In best intentions of the Fallen, my Nephilim ancestors were to be shining light that would lead humanity to greatness. They were to be these perfect little creatures that would have the adaptability of Mortals and the Fallen's power and love for humanity.

          But like my first ex-wife (in all her stage mom glory), your own personal demons and broken dreams will only screw your kids up even more. Or maybe this was The Host's way of getting back at its disloyal servants by twisting their children's hearts. Either way it merged into their offspring, driving many of them insane with torment. Whatever it was, it brought more pain to the world.

          Terrors: The monomaniacal leaders of the Nephilim. Blessed with thunderous voices, Mortal and Mystic could be bent to their will.

          Furies: Vengeful creatures whose torment granted them a twisted form of justice that brought forth the grisly deaths of the innocent as well as the wicked.

          Giants: Reclusive yet skilled craftsmen who had just as much potential for wickedness as their Malefactor parents.

          Gorgons: Self-proclaimed watchers of nature, they often fell from grace and were often worse then the most sadistic of predators

          Seers: Half-mad children both blessed and cursed with visions of endless pasts and futures.

          Fairest: Beautiful, yet cruel seducers that twist the emotions of any man like clay.

          Rouges: The lowest of them all, they scurried the shadows performing any deed for any price.

          Once again, out of love for the Mortals, the Fallen Angels went out to wipe out their prodigy and let The Host sort them out. Seeing a few sparks of good in a few of them, The Host turned the souls of my ancestors into creatures called Ferrymen who would guide the spirits of the restless dead, cast their parents to the Abyss and sealed away the powers of the Nephilim's children until it needed it. In case you are wondering, no, you are not one of us; you just have a bunch of my celestial power in you.

          In the end, love played one last act: the Demons have broken out and The Host knows it. A New God and a New World will arise from The End of Times and this time The Host thought ahead...

          Aasimar: For the last 50 years the Angels have been knocking-up humans to create these less screwed-up versions of my ancestors.

          Chari: They're on our side yet they aren't. These revived corpses answer to an old associate of The Host's they call Charon.

          Infused: That's what you are.

          Even someone as old as me doesn't know everything, but I've told you what you need to know (which is more than many of your kind get). They're coming for me and I don't have much time as it is. So keep this with you and don't screw-up.
          -Amos Vanderholt

          Author's NOTE: The information about the original Nephilim was from another person's project which I got permission to use in mine.

          While Mr. Vanderholt got a lot of it right, there's only so much that even he could know with his own biases. Going back to the theme of love that was often mentioned in the Tome of Ancients, it is one that has driven both Lucifer and The Host to such extremes. What Mr. Vanderholt didn't know was despite its great power, The Host felt alone and created both Mortals and Angels to ease its pain. One in particular was Lilith, having a more powerful divine spark than the others she began to gather power, growing fast and more powerful than it expected. Fearing that her climb for power would cause harm to the other children, The Host casted her out.

          In The Host's eyes, it desired for its children to learn and grow at their own pace until they would no longer need Eden and leave on their own terms. When Lucifer (with help from Lilith) betrayed The Host, its fear for the safety of the Mortals grew as it saw them struggle against each other. It did whatever it could to stop Lucifer and his traitorous kin from their own delusional ideas for humanity. But in the end, there was no real victor as they all ended up with their own burdens: Lilith would desire love but receive none in return, the Fallen were cast into the Abyss, the Nephilim were turned into servants of the dead, Lucifer would see his followers and dreams become a twisted mess and The Host was doomed to always be alone.

          As time passed The Host (despite its continued efforts) realized that if the continued damage that the world was inflicting upon itself was to survive The End of Times, a successor would need to rule its place. So after it granted the Angels the gift to create the Aasimar, Charon to go on with his plan to make his army and gave back the powers of the original Nephilim's descendants The Host finally stepped back and let his beloved creations make their own choices.

          In Lucifer's eyes it was a different story. After being granted the knowledge of The Host, it began to see where its love for the Mortals came from. The divine spark they possessed fascinated him to no end, but deep down he felt like something was wrong: it never grew. For years the Mortals would wander about creation, ignorant of their potential while he and the rest of the Angels were forced to keep their distance. So out of love Lucifer was willing to risk damnation to let humanity finally grow. Even if humanity was to be caught in the middle of him and The Host's wars, he would never let them be controlled again.

          But tragedy seemed to fall upon him from the failure that were the original Nephilim, his allies being tossed into Abyss and later overestimating his archdukes' mental stability when he freed them in hopes of guiding humanity again. Still, he would keep going.

          Overall

          In a funny way one could see the struggle between Lucifer and The Host like two conflicting parents who believed they knew what was best for their beloved children. Both were willing go to any length to the point that they would destroy their own happiness in the process, but never realize that in the end they were their own worst enemies.

          Next will be the Divinities.

                      DIVINITIES

                      All of the Celestials in the game come from one of the four Divinities (Aasimar, Chari, Infused and Nephilim) which helps shape their powers, outlook and treatment by others.

                      Aasimar
                      Pronounced: Aah-sigh-mar

                      When the world is falling into despair, the Aasimar represent the hope and purity that was considered lost. We often feel trapped and ineffectual, doomed to be wrung dry by the ones with the real power. Now as an Aasimar you realize that there is something special to you, something that can at least make a dent in this world. But all is not sunshine and roses for the Half-Angels, the World of Darkness is a cold and frightening place that for as many people who will admire you, just as many have much darker desires.

                      Creation: As the End of Times gathered, The Host gathered its most loyal Angels and granted them a blessing: the ability to sire children. Over the course of the last 50 years, these Angels visited earth in various forms from traditional one (not unlike the virgin Mary’s proclamation), short-lived romances as Mortals or even in the forms of deals. One particular example of the later of the three was with the Ordo of Angelus, a cabal of celestial occultists. In return for providing aid and protection to the future Celestials of all divinites, the Angels would mate with the highest ranking members to produce their own Aasimar.

                      Revelation: Until their Revelation, all Aasimar lived as mere mortals. Since they have no family lineage to fall back on, The Host has blessed them with strong genes that turns any children (and grandchildren) an Aasimar conceived as a Mortal into one.

                      Blessing: With an angelic parent and child working as a joined unit, all Aasimar are immune to possession and mind control. Nor can their Grigori be summoned/trapped.

                      Curse: Due to their holy nature, an Aasimar receives an extra point of damage when attacked by an infernal creature (example: Demons). NOTE: Though I am open to a different idea (since I wasn't too sure about it.)

                      Connection: All Aasimar have a one-on-one connection with their Grigori and can mentally and physically talk to each other depending on how strong the connection is. In the case of a Grigori having more than one Aasimar, said offspring will have to take the Joint Connection merit/flaw (depending on which one the player chooses.)

                      Aura: Aasimar auras tend to radiate a sense of warmth and comfort.

                      Loyalties: Since their Revelation is more often viewed as a divine gift, many Aasimar are more likely to side with The Host on things. The feelings towards Lucifer's involvement can range from pity for a "misguided/pathetic" creature to downright wanting to destroy him for all the damage he caused. An Aasimar who thinks Lucifer was in the right has happened and is usually "tolerated" by their Grigori.

                      Miracles: Aasimar miracles focus on holy energy (Path of Light), protection of themselves and others (Path of Protection) and the powers of their Grigori (Path of Holiness).

                      Relations:
                      Chari: The two divinities occasionally clash due to their loyalties and ways of handling things. Both sides know there is no time for arguing, but it doesn't stop some Aasimar from viewing the Chari with some unease.
                      Infused: There's a bit of pity toward the Infused's lack of guidance and purpose. They are lost children that need aid that they just can't really relate to.
                      Nephilim: While it's nice to have the extra protection, some Aasimar feel as if the Nephilim underestimate them as fragile and naive. When you think about it, the Aasimar have to do a little watching out for their siblings by putting in a good word in for them at times. (Given how being half-fallen may not appeal with some people.)
                      Mortals: Given the usual positive portrayal, this can work for and against an Aasimar. There are a lot of expectations to live to this ideal and those that don't fit that mold appearance/personality-wise are going to have problems. The other problem is that some people have been so broken by the world that their "love" turns to intense lust and obsession.



                      Chari
                      Pronunciation: Shar-rye

                      Beyond their intense loyalty and undead bodies, all Chari harbor the sad realization that they are as dead in the spiritual sense as they are in the physical. Their souls have either transcended to their reward, become Wraiths/Specters or have been destroyed. It's their master's (who claims to be the god-like Charon) own mystical energy that animates their bodies and retains their memories.

                      How do you handle such a cruel revelation? Maybe this second chance at life is enough to ease your pains or you just throw yourself headfirst into your old life to make the pain go away. And how would you feel if you say your soul happy in Heaven or soul-forged into an ashtray?

                      Creation: For reasons only known to Charon himself, he will bring a Mortal of his choosing back to life by repairing their corpses and infusing them with his divine energy. NOTE: Charon will not revive a corpse that has been dead for more than 70 years.

                      Revelation: While Charon is extremely powerful, he is not a true God. Thus he is only able to fuel five thousand Chari on the earth at one time. If one were to die, he'd just create another one.

                      Blessing: Since they’re undead, the Chari cannot die by suffocation, bleeding to death (they don’t bleed), or dehydration/starvation.

                      Curse: Since holy energy fuels their undead bodies, a Chari must spend one point of Favor every 48 hours otherwise their body stops functioning and will collapse. Unless someone infuses a point of Favor to reanimate the corpse, the body will deteriorate into a pile of ash in 24 hours.

                      Connection: All Chari have a direct hive-like connection to Charon himself.

                      Aura: No matter how warm it is, there is an odd kind of chill that brings forth an enigmatic feeling from those around the Chari.

                      Loyalties: With Charon's essence lives within them, the Chari are infused with an automatic loyalty to their creator whether they like it or not. That is unless you have a certain flaw that can negate that. Charon has often whispered into his servants’ ears that they can gain souls of their own when they become Angelos, thus enhancing their loyalty even further. The whole The Host vs Lucifer affair can range from caring less about their struggles to total disgust that they're glad not to be technically part of.

                      Miracles: Chari miracles focus on controlling spirits (Path of Spirits), their undead natures (Path of Undeath) and the darkness itself (Path of Night)

                      Relations
                      Aasimar: To a Chari, the Aasimar are like the idealistic, bright-eyed rookie to their more jaded veteran. Otherwise it's no different than how they see the Nephilim.
                      Infused: Think of an Aasimar who doesn't know what she's doing. There is a sense of chaos to the Infused with their lack of direction and purpose that opposes the order that Charon desires.
                      Nephilim: In most Chari's minds, it is for the better that the Nephilim keep their distance. It makes their jobs easier and Charon knows what would happen if it was discover that they don't even have souls to begin with.
                      Mortals: Chari can be both fascinating and frightening to Mortals. On one had they represent the cold truth of death as well the surpassing of this truth. So it easily varies on how both sides handle themselves.


                      Infused

                      Probably the biggest challenge for any Infused is just trying to find where you fit in this world. With no guidance, mission or celestial aura, there is a lot going against you. But The Host has not abandoned these lost children and has given them the gift of freedom. The Infused can pursue any path in healing the World of Darkness where others have to gain their Favor by following their divinities. Not to mention keeping most of your former self by not having any signs or twist. Though there's probably more than one Infused who wishes she had a voice as lovely as a Seraphim's or a Child of Goliath's strong sense of justice.

                      Creation: There's a number of ways that an Infused can gain their Revelation: receiving a heart transplant from another Divinity (which will be treated as perfect match), being exposed to large amounts of celestial energy or simply being rewarded by The Host itself for their pious nature.

                      Revelation: No one really knows why one Mortal will get this chance and not another. Perhaps it is a fluke or fate's own sick sense of humor.

                      Blessing: The Infused are allowed to learn any other Heritage’s Miracles.

                      Curse: An Infused can not have any dots in Aura and Connection.

                      Connection: None.

                      Aura: None.

                      Loyalties: Just like the Nephilim, an Infused's loyalty can be just as varied. That is if they fully understand what their purpose is other than the simple message they receive when their Revelation kicks in.

                      Miracles: Infused miracles focus on their own bodies allowing them to enhance (Path of Enhancement) or alter it (Path of Alteration) as well as tap into their own minds (Path of the Mind).

                      Relations
                      Aasimar: While any help is appreciated, sometimes the Aasimar's well-meaning protectiveness and under-estimating can get annoying.
                      Chari: The truth is that most Infused aren't sure what to make of the Chari. While they doubt that they are the drones that others makes them out to be, the Infused find it easier just to be pleasant and stay out of their way.
                      Nephilim: Proof that sometimes it's better to not get what you want. The stigma and burden that the Nephilim have to face definitely has the Infused's sympathies.
                      Mortals: This is where things are easier for the Infused. Having powers brought by celestial energy is likely to bring a sense of interest with the mixed blessing of less esteem as the other Divinites (due to there being less pressure.) This allows them to not be spotted out as often and make their own paths.


                      Nephilim
                      Pronunciation: Nef-flim

                      How does one cope with the idea that they are the decedent of a failed dream? This is what every Nephilim has to learn once they receive their Revelation. While your celestial blood has cooled down after all those years of interbreeding with mere mortals, you can still feel lingering bits of the torment that raged in your ancestors.

                      The biggest theme with this divinity is overcoming the sins of your ancestors by making the name, Nephilim, a thing of honor despite the odds against you. While you have the advantage of the average layman not having any idea of what a Nephilim is; there are others out there who have long memories. Also the End of Times is lurking without a clue of when it will jump out and attack you.

                      Creation: After the destruction of the original Nephilim, their children were granted to chance to live a normal life as Mortals by The Host by subduing their Fallen heritage until it was needed.

                      Revelation: Usually one or two members from a family line will receive the Revelation at a time. This is done to keep the Nephilim under a low profile and to make sure there will always be reserves in case the current ones dies.

                      In the case of death or falling, The Host will bestow the Revelation to another member of the family to take their place. Note: Some members of the family may not be worthy of the Revelation (even as Saulites).

                      Blessing: Tougher than the average human, all Nephilim are given an extra health level.

                      Curse: Ancestral Rage-If a Nephilim is under extreme stress, they must make a willpower roll to avoid frenzy. If the roll fails, she to kill or otherwise neutralize the stimulus responsible for it. This can also be negated if the Nephilim is killed, rendered unconscious or by mystical means.

                      Connection: Nephilim can draw from the memories of ancestors for guidance.

                      Aura: Nephilim auras tend to have a sense of power that can be almost intimidating or inspire awe (depending on the person).

                      Loyalties: Depending on how the Nephilim feels about their new condition, their feelings toward The Host and Lucifer are varied. Some may resent one or both for their involvement or even be on the fence.

                      Miracles: Nephilim miracles focus on the elements such as earth (Path of the Earth), water and wind (Path of Water and Wind) and fire (Path of Fire.)

                      Relations
                      Aasimar: The Nephilim have been given an additional job of watching over their siblings. At times there is a bit of sibling rivalry due to their opposing bloodlines and the conditions of their ancestry.
                      Chari: Organized, undead and with a strong loyalty to their master, there's something almost unsettling about the Chari to your average Nephilim. Given the more neutral attitude of the Nephilim, most prefer to keep their distance from the Chari's affairs.
                      Infused: The Infused are a bit like a well-meaning younger sibling that is a good ally yet in over their heads and occasional bother at the same time.
                      Mortals: As said before, your average Joe-Sixpack has no idea what a Nephilim is which makes things easier until you have to explain the fallen part. A good number of Mortals may give you the benefit of a doubt, but the fear and awe their ancestors possessed does remain for good or for ill.

                              Children of Goliath
                              “Stop those who seek to bring the world into further ruin.”

                              From the Diary of Officer Peter Teller: How am I going to explain this?                                                           System: The Deadman must touch both the person who is suffering and the person they wish to feel it. Then roll their willpower vs the person they're wishing to inflict must. The more successes, forces the target to receive a more intense feeling. While a botch forces the user to receive the pain.
                                                          NOTE: If this Miracle is used for malicious reasons, a morality check must be done.

                                                          Description:                                                           Exposes the target to their worst fears. Only the user and the target will be able to see this fear.
                                                          System: Spend a Favor and roll Perception + Subterfuge vs the target's willpower. Will not work on character who are immune to fear.

                                                          Botch: The Miracle forces the Deadman to become queasy.
                                                          1 Success: A hazy vision of the fear appears in both the user and the victim's minds.
                                                          2 to 3 successes: The image is the more vivid and the target must roll their willpower vs 6 to avoid being shaken.
                                                          4 or more successes: Same as before, but the target receives a point of damage from the mental backlash.

                                                          NOTE: If used for malicious reasons, a morality check must be made.

                                                          Description:                                                           Forces the target's next roll to be done over.
                                                          System: Spend a Favor and roll your willpower vs the target's. If the target has the flaws Unlucky and/or Karma's Bitch, the target number is reduced by one (min 1). The next roll (even if it's a botch) must be done over. If it's a botch, the rule applies to the user.

                                                          ****Stop the Powerflow
                                                          Description: Temporarily prevents the target from using what fuels their powers (blood, chi, gnosis, ect.)
                                                          System: Spend two Favors and roll your willpower vs the target's. Each success equals a round of activation while a botch will turn the Miracle on the user for one round.

                                                          *****Curse
                                                          Description: Bestows a curse upon the target.
                                                          System: Spend two Favors and roll willpower vs the opponet's willpower. (One success is all that is needed.) The curse must have a way to cure it and must be with in reason. A botch prevents the user from ever inflicting another curse on that person again.

                                                          Example: A Deadman wishes to make a heartless bully pay for his cruelty towards others (especially fat people). So when one of his beatings causes brain damage to one of his victims, she knows that it's time to step in. She curses him that "until he learns kindness and empathy towards others, he will become just like his victims." The bully soon learns to his horror that he is rapidly gaining weight until he's double his size. Finding that he can't really get any thinner, he must sincerly learn his lesson and start doing right by others or he'll stay that way.

                                                          NOTE: It is possible for an outside force to remove the curse. If used on an innocent person or for malicious reasons, a morality check is needed.
