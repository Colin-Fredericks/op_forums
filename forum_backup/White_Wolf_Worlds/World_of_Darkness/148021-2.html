                    They were imbued with powers based on psycological steriotypes of people in the middle of some sort of crisis.

                    Whats the catch of hunter..Why it is a reckoning?

                    Hunters are born on the end of days. The darkness is wining.
                    Demons Roam the earth creating new eldritch horrors
                    Lycans are involved with attacks against industries and slowly staling new technology
                    Wizards are turning the wheel of what is reality, forcing their madness upon the consensus and using humans as playthings
                    Faeries are living nightmares, feeding of the dreams and leaving humans as an empty shell
                    Ghosts roam the earth, making people be aware that theres worsts things than death.
                    and vampires control societies, forcing secret wars and killing hundreds or thousands as collateral damage.
                    Humanity Cant Fight anymore.

                    We had our chances to strike this threat on one single blow, we had the inquisition and we failed. humans keep trying to stay away from all this and now they are winning. The armageddon is coming, and after many years of abuse against humans. Something said "No More." and decided to give the tools to mindkind to fight one last time. to push the doomsday clock's handles as far as we could.

                    Why is hunter not only about killing supernaturals?
                    This is part of the metaplot so, be warned. Theres 7 Creeds, each one representing the state of mind of the hunter when he faced the supernatural and determinating his powers. this are devided in 3 tiers based on the base of their beliefs. This ones are
                    Avenger. : Not only the one who fights for revenge, but the one who was stepped on his entire life and said "No more. " he who is fueled by rage and a need for social justice or justice in general.
                    Defender. : The Shield, the protector, he who takes care of protecting the flock from the darkness.
                    Judge. : The leader, the politic, the strategic mind, he who judges and drops the hammer upon thy. Guilty or Innocent, no one can pass his judgment.
                    Visionary: The long term strategy, the ones who investigate, who think, who wish to create a better tomorrow and think forward.
                    Martyrdoom.: He who takes the pain for the cause, who makes personal sacrifices to guide the group to victory.
                    Redemeer.: Not all monsters have to be inhuman, these are who look for the humanity within the beast, the ones who wish to find a cure for their condition. the ones who wish to save the darkness from itself
                    Innocent.: Why?. thats all...curiosity, intellect. Not naivity, but rather curiosity. those who are willing to Understand the mind of a monster and wish to know simply: Why.


                    Depending on the creed, the game might take a different point of view. from hunting only, to find a cure, to find salvation, to prepare mindkind for the day after the storm.
