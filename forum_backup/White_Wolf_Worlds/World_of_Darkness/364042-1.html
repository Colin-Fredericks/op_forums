    "Take Back the Night

    For centuries, supernatural powers have reigned, warring among themselves, culling the human herds and lashing out from the shadows. The creatures of the night have held court since before the dawn of history.

    Nothing lasts forever.

    From the throng of humanity, individuals emerge who know the truth. [They] know monsters exist. Enough is enough. The forces of darkness must pay their due.

    You are the reckoning!"
    Nothing in this says that those individuals are supernaturals themselves rather it implies that individual humans emerge knowing the truth.

    I see that you are unwilling to see my point, which is that I, and many many others over the years, had the expectation of humans rising against the supernatural and what we got was supernatural creatures rising against the supernatural. That HH has numina wielding hunters, which are in my opinion much more interesting than Reckonings hunters who wield even more supernatural power, is not the point I would have preferred HH to have been expanded into a line of it's own but even more I would have preferred the Reckoning line to have been about mortals rising from the throng of humanity knowing monsters exist, like surviving victims or relatives of those killed by the supernaturals, would have been infinitely more interesting and supportive of the themes of both hunters, as an idea in general, and the other lines as well. What Reckoning does is only enforce the image of helpless humanity which is like I said kinda poisonous to the themes of the other lines.

    Reckoning also robs the other lines from moral dilemmas normal mortals would have inflicted. Had the hunters been mortal victims of the supernatural who had enough it would have forced the supernaturals to look at themselves and their deeds even if they are forced to kill their hunters, which could be a moral dilemma in itself where as the Reckoning hunters being as they are clearly supernatural in origin it only makes them think about what supernatural power is conspiring against them this time, no reflection on their own actions. Just another bunch of supernaturals after them.

    But like said this is my opinion, which I know many share, and despite me wishing to see Reckoning purged from existence I do think it's fans deserve their chance of 20th edition when the time comes and it's not really away from me especially if those rumors about Vigil being what I wish are true.
