          I usually run with the idea that the Children are Neverborn. However, there is a way to link up the two different types of Infernalism (pre and post Demon the Fallen) and make it make sense in a general WoD setting. Both can be true without being mutually exclusive. Most of the "Infernalism" supplements are dealing with creatures that don't remotely resemble Demon the Fallen. The simplest way to make it all make sense is to say that pre-1999 most Infernalism dealt with Bane spirits taking on the roles of traditional demons, with the occasional summoning (ala Devil's Due) taking place. Post 1999 when the Rift opened, this other type of entity that almost no one had ever seen before started appearing. I have actually been planning an Infernalist hunting game set during this changeover as a plot point when all the known rules of Infernalism seem to be being broken by these new creatures.

                                      Originally posted by killersquirrel59 View Post
                                      So why do so many cry out that it is impossible?
                                      Au contraire, it's a far cry from impossible. In fact I think that some Demon factions, like the Cryptics for instance, can be integrated pretty well into an Occult-heavy chronicle, especially if you use Devil's Due and Earthbound (some of the better books for the classic World of Darkness in my opinion).

                                      With that said: Demon does have some parts that really turn off certain players. For one, Demon retconned quite a few already-established Demonic creatures into Earthbound. Couple that with the fact that Demon wrote off quite a few religious backdrops as just worshiping Earthbound puts off quite a few Storytellers and Players alike. Being a fan of Paradise Lost I did like Demon, but found that it isn't the best for crossovers unless you're willing to repress and remove certain aspects of it.

                                              Pretty sure the Children are whatever you want them to be, that is why they are never defined. Neverborn seems the most likely answer from any oWoD veteran. Baal is, as stated, an Earthbound Demon (of Demon the Fallen fame). Of course, as Phalon said, Devil's Due works wonders for incorporating DtF elements into Vampire when crossovers, or simple plot loan, are called for. However, given the Baali deal not only with Demons of "The Inferno", but "The Abyss" and what might well be Malfeas... the Baali aren't limited to one specific brand of demonology. It is also worth mentioning that the term Ba'al is used as a placeholder by the ancient and elder Ba'ali. It means "Lord" and it is used as the True Names (or even Celestial Names) of Demons are highly valued and neither easily shared, nor foolishly spoken aloud in passing.
