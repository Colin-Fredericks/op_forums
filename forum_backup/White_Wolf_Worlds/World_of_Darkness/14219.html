      Originally posted by Nero's Boot
      Hon, Rasputin was a White Wolf in-joke. They purposefully made him into every supernatural they possibly could, as a way of poking fun of the whole World of Darkness. The truth of it is probably that Rasputin was a good ol' Sleeper that every supernatural faction wishes was theirs to claim.
      Some unpublished stuff from Dan Greenberg:
      The Basic Answer: Rasputin was a Ventrue ally caught in the Ventrue-Brujah battle for Russia. When his enemies came to assassinate him, he tried to throw them off the trail of his true motivations (and the best way to locate and destroy him) by masquerading as everything. The cloud of obfuscation worked, and Rasputin exists today, exacting a horrible revenge on his enemies, Brujah and Ventrue alike, and causing the Soviets no end of grief. So what is he? A Malkavian, of course. No wait. A Tremere who broke all the Avatar rules. No wait. A Ventrue ghoul. No wait. A Mage. No, wait. Just a mortal. No wait. The child of a demonic... no wait.

      The Brutally Honest Answer:
      We deliberately did not pin down who and what Rasputin was so that we would have a wide range of story options open to us if and when we finally decided to do the big Rasputin cycle. (No decision? I'm so disillusioned.)

      Actually, I later came up with the Rasputin cycle. It was a springboard for the first Gehenna cycle. (That's right, the FIRST Gehenna cycle.)

      Disclaimer: Keep in mind that all answers are made up by me but not published, and are therefore non-canonical, from a no-longer-official source, void where prohibited, prohibited where void, your mileage may vary:

      The Secret History of Rasputin:
      Rasputin is an intensely powerful creature who made enemies of just about everyone by shaking up the incestuous, demonic, hell-hole that was pre-revolutionary Russia. He started as an alchemist, and became a Ventrue ghoul at a time when the Ventrue controlled the Czarist reign of terror. The Ventrue were losing the hearts and minds of the people to the Brujah's revolutionary rhetoric, and they conspired to strike the heart of the Brujah bloodline. The Ventrue acquired samples of the blood of all the clans (at great cost) and Rasputin set to work fathoming the secrets of the blood. He actually cracked the code of the Curse of Caine and learned to change the taint of vampiric blood from one bloodline to another, to thicken or dilute blood, and to make a vampire's own blood poisonous, among other tricks.

      He used a tiny fraction of his research to cure the blood of Alexis Nikolayevich, heir to the Russian throne, ending his crippling hemophilia. This gave him solid standing with the Empress Alexandra Fyodorovna. He became the czarina's most trusted advisor, and grew in power.

      He used the blood to break his loyalty to the Ventrue. Then he made himself immune to most Disciplines. Then he synthesized the purest blood he could: a concoction he called the Blood of Cain. He swigged it, and pankindred. He had the attributes of all Kindred, and then some. He could easily force any and all Kindred to detect him as their own. But his actions gave him tortured visions of Gehenna, and drove him madder than mad.

      His former Ventrue allies hated him. Other mages feared him. He soon had nothing but enemies, and they formed a vast conspiracy against him. The Czarina tried to protect him, but they eventually killed him. He came back, and they killed him again. He has been killed many times, in many ways, but he always comes back. Because, like Cain, he cannot truly die.

      Rasputin in Gehenna:
      I put him in my story for the Gehenna cycle, called When Cain Wept (Or Cain's Tears, or Blood in Eden. I hadn't made up my mind. (Code-name: Caindrops Keep Falling on My Head)). There would be multiple adventures, from low-level (all player characters are Caitiff) to the very highest level (all characters are high-ranking Elders, using the Elysium rules.) First, all signs point to Gehenna arriving. Then Rasputin resurfaces. In the Caitiff adventure, the Caitiff actually have to kill Rasputin. But he comes back, slightly different. (I'm not dead yet. I'm getting better). In a Sabat adventure, where the players try to prevent the Camarilla from killing Rasputin. Meanwhile, Gehenna looms in the background.

      In the Elder adventure, there would be solo bits, like Last Supper. Each Elder would privately meet Rasputin, who claims (and appears) to be of each character's clan and can prove it. He tries to make a secret bargain with each. The players spend the rest of the game dealing with the outcome of their secret bargains with Rasputin. Then, at the end, they learn that Rasputin is really Cain. Then, in the final Gehenna book, they get to play a mix of all the characters they played in all the books of the cycle. Caitiff, Sabat, Ancilla, Elder. They all once got a taste of Rasputin's blood, so only they are immune to his powers. They have to band together to do in Rasputin once and for all.

      When they finally destroy Rasputin, at massive personal cost, they learn that he was not Cain, but functionally almost identical in blood. By destroying him, the players forestall the real Gehenna. Though some suspect that Rasputin offered himself for death in place of Cain, thus deliberatly forestalling Gehenna.
      There was a lot more to the cycle than that, but that's the Rasputin part of things.

                          When they finally destroy Rasputin, at massive personal cost, they learn that he was not Cain, but functionally almost identical in blood. By destroying him, the players forestall the real Gehenna. Though some suspect that Rasputin offered himself for death in place of Cain, thus deliberately forestalling Gehenna.
                          I find that part interesting. Rasputin has often been vilified and blamed for the downfall of the Russian monarchy, but he advised the Tsar against war, and had his advice been heeded, millions of lives might have been saved by averting World War I, the October Revolution, the rise to power and atrocities of Stalin, World War II, etc. He also advocated equal rights for Jews (who faced significant oppression and sometimes outright slaughter in Russia) and aid to the poor. That said, his appetite for sex and drink is the stuff of legend, and he seems to have been willing to employ bribery and political maneuvering to accomplish his goals. So it strikes me as appropriate that he should turn out to be not the dastardly villain the PCs assume but instead a sort of dark hero in that version of Gehenna.

                          However... I'm confused by the scenario... What is Rasputin doing and why are the PCs trying to kill him? Is it something that Cain would be compelled to do if Rasputin were not acting as blood surrogate (thus leading to Cain's death and Gehenna as a result)? Or is he preventing Cain from causing a 'possible Gehenna' by fulfilling its conditions in Cain's place but allowing himself to be killed to exhaust the possibility? Or... ? Details would be welcome if this story got beyond outlining (or at least progressed to a more detailed outline).

                          Sadly, Rasputin's body was found, photographed, and autopsied*, contradicting some of the myths of his possible survival in secret.

                          But in the World of Darkness, who knows?

                          *cause of death: professional-style gunshot wound to the forehead, possibly the work of the British Secret Intelligence Service who feared he might cause Russia's withdrawl from WWI
