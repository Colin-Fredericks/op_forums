    Searching for armor houserules

    02-21-2016, 02:05 PM
    I've been looking at the armor rules lately, since a DA campain may be starting soon, and I am quite dissatisfied with the way armor is portayed in the core books.
    Currently, me and my group are looking to devellop a houserule regarding this, finding a way to make armor work in our games.

    Are there any houserules for armor the rest of you deploy?
    How much armor dice do you give out, how do you handle weight-penaltys, what about armor-piercing weapons and such?
    I'd be thankfull for any input, you might have for me and my crew. ;-)

          At the moment armor is unrealistically ineffective and the way, that the dex penaltys are set up, leads to there being a "best" armor out of no concernable reason.

          Dex is by far the most important of the physical Attributes. Trading 3 Dex for 5 Armor is a bad deal, no matter how you slice it.
          So wearing heavy armor is more of a penalty than a bonus, in a way.
          The only two armors, that actually work in your favor are extremly light ones (one armor dice, no penaltys) and composite ones (3 armor dice, 1 dex penalty).
          So the way its written at the moment, you ether take "sturdy clothing" if you don't really need armor but don't mind the extra dice, or take composite armor, when armor is actually needed for the char.
          I'd like for the other kinds of armor to be more viable, having a reason to be worn exept simply for style.

          Also, armor seems surprisingly weak in actually stopping damage.
          Still trying to find a balance, but 1-5 extra dice on absorbing damage seems laughable litte when compared to how much protection one would expect from being armored up quite some bit.

                      The M20 armor rules are probably one of the better sets for your concerns just using the RAW. The Dex penalties are lower, and vaguer (it is not phrased as all Dex dice-pools, so you can exercise more discretion about it), shields have separate stats that stack with armors (Riot armor is 4/-2, and a riot shield is 5/-1 vs. firearms, so you're at 9 soak dice against guns with a -3 penalty, and hand-to-hand attacks suffer a +2 difficulty against you; all with the caveat that shields don't work against flank or surprise attacks).

                      Also remember that specified targets to avoid armor are going to be a lot easier against many lighter armors since they don't have full body protection or helmets.

                      If you're looking for more options:

                      Give armors tags (such as ballistic, slashing, stab) to reflect being designed for different situations. Light armors like reinforced clothing will have no or one of these, while heavier armors will usually have more of them and/or broader ones. Whenever the tag applies, it reduces lethal damage dealt to bashing damage up to the armors rating. So a person wearing a Kevlar vest that's shot for 4 damage post-soak, takes 1 lethal and 3 bashing.
