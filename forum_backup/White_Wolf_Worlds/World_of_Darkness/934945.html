      Onto my next 2 pcs.

      First up i wil do the kinfolk Berrin Thaddson.
      Being Get kin, he went with phys as his pri, so all three of Str/Dex/Stam are 3 dots each, Social got evened out at 2 apiece, while Mental was secondary giving him a 3 wits and perception and 2 intelligence.

      Talents were primary (11 dots, +1 from freebies), giving im a 2 alertness, streetwise and dodge, 3 Brawl and 1 each in indimidatio, athletics and intuition.
      Skills pulled in 2nd (7 dots +2 from freebies), giving him 1 drive and leadership, 2 in melee and stealth and 3 firearms.
      Knowledges was tertiary (4 dots no freebies spent), giving him 1 in investigation and linguistics (spanish), and 2 medicine.
      Backrounds were split between equipment (3 dots) and resources (2 dots),
      Additional freebies went to get other skills/talents from the Players HB, (2 in throwing, demolitiions and gunsmithing for 12 more freebies spent, bring the total to 18 so far)
      Willpower was raised from 3 to 8 (10 more freebies..
      He has only flaws (no merits) giving him 7 more freebies to spend, covering the remaining needed for his willpower increase. They are Maschicist (1), mild phobia of snakes (1) and veiled (5)..

      Then we move onto one of my other mortals, a Project twilight FBI agent..
      Douglas Sandars.
      Being a field agent he went Phys/ment/soci for his attributes, with 3s in all the physicals, and 2s for all the socials, and his mental breakdown was int and per 3, wits 2.
      Talents was secondary for his abilities pick (7 dots worth +2 dots from freebies), with a spread of 2 alertness and brawling, and 1 athletics, intimidation, leadership, streetwise and subterfuge.
      Skills was primary, (11 dots worth +2 dots from freebies), with 4 in firearms, 2 in each of stealth and repair, and one in drive, security and survival,
      For knowledges he went with 1 in covert culture, law and occult and 2 in investigations (spending 2 more freebies to get that 5th dot).
      Backrounds were where the majority of his freebies went, giving him 3 in allies and resources, 2 in influence and rank, and 4 in equipment.
      He took some merits (agency insider 3 points) and license to kill (2 points), but offset them with some flaws (Primary bread winner, 2 points), child (1 point) and Siege mentality (3 points.)..
      The remainder was used to bump his willpower up to 7..

                          I'll get to posting the first one and see what happens; enjoy the read.

                          Conrald Rimmer -- Clan Gangrel -- Camarilla
                          Some dude who grew up in the rural areas of California somewhere unspecified and eventually moved into LA where the game was set in the aftermath of VtMB (with Strauss as the new Prince). He was pretty much an unemployed nobody fighting claws and teeth to keep his little apartment and feed himself and his dog 'Jackie'. Due to having a dog, he took a lot of walks mostly during after-dark where there were less people up and about, which eventually landed him the stereotypical Gangrel embrace of 'embraced and left to fend for himself'. Fortunately for him, he managed to survive long enough for the Sire to approach him and another Gangrel who was his blood-brother (played by another player) and get introduced to the Prince before they were sent on a job along with two fledgeling Nossies and Imalia babysitting them; if with some reluctance.

                          Didn't have much time to grow, but did get along well with his 'brother' and was open towards his 'sire' if only by virtue of needing his help to adjust. Said sire seemed to be a more corporate predator than a wilds one, judging by his suit, fancy car and ghoul driver; but sadly there wasn't much more time than that to get to know him better.

                          =Attributes=
                          Physical: Strength 3, Dexterity 4, Stamina 3
                          Social: Charisma 2, Manipulation 2, Appearance 2
                          Mental: Perception 3, Intelligence 2, Wits 3

                          Explanation: Conrald was always a physically active guy and practiced kickboxing in his past which led him to developing a good physique over the years prior to moving into the big city; he also had naturally great reflexes which leads to his notably high Dexterity score coupled with his defensive style rather than offensive. Not the smartest, but he passed school; not the most socially adept character as he is uninitiated in the finer arts of socialness but hey, if he knows someone doesn't appreciate something he can keep his ears clean when talking to them and generally can run with a script when in an otherwise unfamiliar setting.

                          =Abilities=
                          Talents: Alertness 3, Athletics 2, Brawl 2, Dodge 3, Empathy 2, Intimidation 2, Streetwise 3, Subterfuge 1
                          Skills: Animal Ken 3, Drive 1, Etiquette 1, Melee 1, Stealth 2, Survival 3
                          Knowledges: Academics 1, Finance 1, Investigation 1, Medicine 1, Science 1

                          Explanation: I don't remember too clearly what went through my head when I made these, but the Athletics, Brawl and Dodge can all be attributed to his kickboxing and gymming back in the ways. I think in hindsight Alertness and Athletics might've done well being swapped as I don't imagine him ever having specifically trained or honed his alertness but rather kinda just comes naturalish, but what can you do? Reasonably able in social situations, not the finest dancer on the floor but he's not completely helpless either. Streetwise comes primarily from his walks I imagine granting him notable area knowledge and lets him see and hear a lot on what goes around. Mental Abilities I imagine are all from his days in school; he's no star but he passed - as previously mentioned. Finance I justify with LA being fucking expensive, so he has to learn how to handle his economy if he wishes to survive in there. Speaking of, no clue what I was thinking with giving Survival 3. Maybe he liked hiking or lived in some rural area? Open-ended past and all
                          Animal Ken I imagine coming from always having had animals around and owning one himself, thus making him a good set of hands when it comes to managing them.

                          =Backgrounds=
                          Resources 2, Contacts 2, Influence 1 (Streets)

                          Explanation: Your guess is as good as mine; I don't remember clearly on this. Presumably he was on the wealthier side of things, perhaps from some well-off ranch or something? I donno. Or he might've done less than legal shit to keep himself afloat, either way that money didn't come out of nowhere. Just don't remember where. Contacts I'm guessing are people on the streets, and influence might've been better off with small-scale Fame or something as a known person around; he had a tendency to stop by for chats and hear the word on the streets, sometimes toss a buck or two around where need be. Maybe hobo favor made him influential in making them listen? I really don't remember.

                          =Virtues & The Rest=
                          Conscience 3, Self-Control 4, Courage 3
                          Humanity 7, Willpower 4
                          Animalism 1, Fortitude 1, Protean 2
                          13th Generation

                              Ok. Next up is the glass walker, Bryon. His concept was (due to being homid), as a PI, ergo his physical and mentals need to be high, and he also needs decent combat skills (more ranged than brawl).

                              Byron was the first of his family in 5 generations to be true born, all the rest have been kinfolk, which initially did earn him scorn scorn from his kin (especially his brother and 2 sisters), though they have mellowed out over time..During his youth, he shied away from most of the sports, preferring to join the schools JROTC program. After earning several merit awards, his kin eventually sent him to a military like academy, but upon graduation, he declined joining the military itself, instead taking up college to earn his PI license. So during the day, he attended a local law school, and at night he assisted a fellow Glass walker as a back up for his PI firm. 10 credit hours from completing his degree, news of his father's death reached him. Cops had the killer, but due to some corruption in the lab, the evidence got chucked. This secured his desire to work as a PI.
                              His first change was VERY late coming, but when it did, it prompted a local GW theurge to bring him into the nearby sept. Knowing his clan's history, it was decided it would serve all better if he relocated, changed his name and work for a different sept, as it was feared (rightly so) he would seek out vengeance, which might tempt him too much.. Though this has not stopped his surviving kin from trying to find him.. They can be a pest at times, which has caused him to have to uproot his practice twice already.

                              Since then, he has spent almost 4 years off and on, working as a PI, and assisting what ever pack needs a spare member. Even when in a pack, he has shown no inclination to lead it, but does act as their lawgiver.

                              His physicals were split 3/4/3 S/D/St, with a specialty of lightning reflexes for dex. Social was 3 Ch 1 Man and 2 App. Perception and int got 3 each for mental, with 2 dots for wits (using 5 of his freebies).
                              For abilities, he went Talents/Skills/Knowledge. For his talents, he went 2 in alertness, athletics and streetwise,, and 1 in each of intimidation, brawl, expression and primal urge, showing that he has not fully embraced his Garou side yet, and dodge rounds it out at 3 dots.
                              Skills he went with 1 in both drive, survival and animal ken, with 2 in repair and stealth, and 3 in firearms. (2 points spent),
                              For knowledge, he went 1 in computers, law, linguistics, rituals and occult and 2 in investigations (4 more freebies spent)
                              Backrounds were split between resources 3 (from his job), contacts 1 (a publicist for a national tabloid), 1 rites and 2 in fetish (a gift he acquired upon being the first changer in his clan for a long time). 2 more freebies spent bringing up the total spent to 13)
                              For Rage, he bumped his initial 1 up to 4 (3 spent), Gnosis went up from 1 to 3 (4 spent) and willpower to 5 (2 spent, bringing up the total freebies spent to 22, offset by his 7 pts of flaws).
                              For flaws he went with, Persistent parents, Late shifter, Two mild phobias (heights and confined spaces) and a intolerance of drug dealers (the criminal who killed his father)..
                              For his initial gifts, he chose master of fire (to offset the few times he has been in fights where fire got used), Open seal (to assist in breaking into places) and Trick shot (to assist his gunplay.
                              For his '2 dots of fetishes, he went with 4 talens, the "John woo specials (Bottomless clips)' 3 of which are used for his H&K MP5 he is licensed to carry, while the last is sized for his Glock 22 pistol. His one dot of rituals went to acquire Talisman dedication (A tracksuit)..

                              Due to his continual acting as a human more than a Garou, he has been recently nagged BY fellow glass walkers.. BUT for now, that has not bothered him much..
