                              Who wins if you compare Bodhisattvas, Legendary Garou, or Methulesahs?

                              Mages probably. Damn their little monkey eyes.

                              In all seriousness this just turns into a spooky creature dick-measuring contest. Cheesefondue proved the splats aren't balanced a 'lowlevel' play and the higher level supernatural powers are even less balanced than that. There is a Get of Fenris gift that prevents death, in all its forms, until your opponent is dead. Your storyteller can change or amend that, but as written, that is pretty hard for most enemies to play around. Higher level disciplines barely have meaningful stats- a Malkavian elder can make an entire city go insane if they want. There are so many potent and vague high-end supernatural abilities you just end up comparing what amount to plot devices.

                                                  Originally posted by angryicecream View Post

                                                  Well, don't know too much about Nexus Crawlers but can they predict that the fight is going to occur, auto win initiative then proceed to freeze time and launch a half dozen rockets/40mm grenades at someone? Because Demons totally can. Not even hard for them. Also no paradox.
                                                  They can change all of the air in a given block of space to poison gas, change someone's bones to rotting swamp muck, erase or alter the facial features of a target, and change bits of Garou (or, with more successes, the entire room that said character is standing in) to silver, all for a Willpower roll with no expenditure of resources (6 dice against a difficulty of 7) so I'm going to say that yes, it's probably on par with that. And they basically have at least 30 health levels on top of that, possibly as many as 100, if you fight them on their home turf; their physical bodies as created the spirit Charm don't have nearly as many before being disrupted, but all that means is that you need someone else to pursue them back into the Umbra, which is an ability sorely lacking from the suite of Fallen Lores. Also, it's doing this while trying to smack you around and eviscerate you, which it can do for ten dice to hit and ten for base damage. Wyld-spirits have a similar capacity with Break Reality, and usually get more dice to roll, but the results are... much less directed.

                                                  Even more heinous are Dream Maker banes. They have Rage, Gnosis, and Willpower all at 10, most spirit Charms, and an Essence rating ranging from 50 to 300. They're basically heinously evil djinni who fulfill wishes until someone makes the drastic mistake of letting them materialize, at which point, it pretty much becomes Wishmaster time, and Fallen would likely need to be spending Faith left and right just to keep up. It has the same capacity for changing reality as both Wyld-spirits and possibly also the Nexus Crawlers (it's hard to say, since there doesn't seem to be a proper entry for the Alter Reality Charm), and is in dire need of an update for at least Revised because it has a whole bunch of obsolete Charms replaced by Blast in its writeup, so it could conceivably be even worse to face them.
