                                FWIW, when writing for Shattered Dreams I took every gameline into account when attempting the synthesis of history.
                                I had to prioritize W:tA for obvious reasons, but I took from all the gamelines to *some* extent. Sadly I couldn't spend the words on explicitly calling out things like the Pure Ones, but they are there. Likewise an explanation for the differing cosmologies between Europe, India, Eastern Asia, and Australia.
                                Bearing in mind that though the book is non-canonical, it wasn't *written* to be non-canonical.

                                The Wonderwork forever separated spirit from physical realms, but by the time of the Banestorm it was a wispy barrier that basically everyone could walk through at will. After the Banestorm it began thickening ever so slowly over time. Bear in mind Caerns/Nodes/Dragon Nests etc reduce the Gauntlet rating and there were more of those, and more powerful ones, in those times.

                                In my first draft the Banestorm was the origin of the Gauntlet, the Dev notes said to extend it further back.

                                One of these days I'll finish my unified synthesized WoD history and share it.
