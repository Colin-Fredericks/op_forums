    Originally posted by Beriorn View Post
    I am relatively new to cWoD, and thus far I've seen several claims that a Werewolf can beat all the other monsters in the cWoD in melee combat. Is this accurate? From what I've read in W20 I've seen that an Ahroun in its Crinos form can rip through a LOT of things with ease, but does this also go for the various other monsters?

    I've also seen claims that, when given enough prep time and the right spheres, a Mage can one-shot pretty much anything. But the key points her e are the prep time and the right spheres, so unless the Mage in question is specifically geared towards melee combat they tend to be amongst the weaker of the various monsters.

    As they told you it depends on the context.

    On average a Werewolf is way stronger than a vampire when it comes to melee fight.

    Still context is vital.
    Just few werewolves would be able to survive a strong elder like Jalav-Ajan (nevertheless even here circumstances play a big role, a toreador elder is going to have MUCH less chances to survive than a Gangrel elder) and a mage who could pull the Shivasakti Ayvatara effect would be a deadly foe for anyone.

    Vampires and mages tend to get more powerfull than werewolf with time: Vampires can get their stats and disciplines higher than most other creatures and mages can do almost everything as their mastery grows.
    Still, the most powerfull Vampires and Mages are usually NPC: while it's not impossibile for a Werewolf player to get Rank 5 it's doesn't happen often that a Mage player ascends to the rank of Archmage, and it's mostly impossible for a Vampire player to become a methuselah, unless you're playing a munchkin campaign.

    So if we are speaking about average starting playable character then yes, a werewolf is really tough stuff*, but then if we are taking in account also NPC then no, no werewolf will beat by brute force Enkidu or Voormas.
    In those cases well devised plans and pack tactics should be the main focus.


    As for mages the same rule can be applied here: context is everything.
    A master mage can do almost anything, still paradox can be a bitch: beeing able to do something in theory is not the same as actually doing it.
    Paradox is the real obstacle: with time magic (contingency effect with time 3) and rituals a mage can prepare himself without trouble,
    Even so there are high chances that paradox will bite him in the ass: one shot kill spells are usually pretty vulgar effect, Mr Wrinkle and Judgment may have their say about you using them.





    *But even here we should look at circumstances: while a Arhoun Get of Fenris player should be a dreadful foe for most vampires in melee fight (he could easily split open the average gangrel joe, without much effort), would that be true even when direct fight is not on the table? For instance, if a Get of Fenris without Shell gift cross is path with a Ventrue or a Tremere (both really good at domination) what would happen?
    Most likely it would start dancing as a monkey for his new master.
