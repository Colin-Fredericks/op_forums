    Let's Make Astral Spires!

    10-06-2016, 11:35 AM
    I was reading the Infinite Tapestry chapters on the High Umbra, specifically the bit on the Spires and thought it could be fun if we all thought up some spires and Umbrood courts that could be there.

    This is all meant to be a bit of fun, so as long as there's some kind of internal logic behind it, anything goes. Be as detailed or as slight with details as you want. And by all means embellish on things other people have stated. If you can imagine it, it's probably already there. Nothing is wrong in Astral space!

    For those who want a bit more structure to their spires, here's a brief summery of some of the features present in all spires according to the Infinite Tapestry:

    - Theme: What is this spire based on? (e.g. Astrology, Movie genres, The seven deadly sins, Internet memes, etc)
    - Location: Some spires are out at sea, some are in the centre of the vulgate (astral space city encompassing all of human culture), some are surrounded by specific types of culture, others are much more general.
    - Form: Spire can be exactly that, large pillars of rocks, but they don't have to be. They can be deep caves to descend, canyons to cross, distant islands to swim to, great mountains to navigate, or even towering citadels and mesmerising labyrinths.
    - Climbing: How one reaches the courts within the spire. Are there well trodden paths, is it a well lit route? Do you require types of magic? Do they have to swim or build a ship? Is it a multi stage journey or simply requiring herculean strength? Do you need paperwork to attempt? Are there Zelda style puzzles?
    - The Gates: A Spire usually have 5 entry points based around the 5 senses.
    -- Gate of Sight: Visually obvious, if you can see it, you can enter. Entry lets everyone know you're there.
    -- Gate of Sound: Also obvious, if you can hear it, you can enter. Entry lets everyone know you're there.
    -- Gate of Touch: Easy to miss, impossible to see (fog/darkness/invisible), you have to feel around for it. Back entrance to the courts, no one knows you're there.
    -- Gate of Smell: Also Easy to miss, like touch you have to smell your way through. Also a back entrance, no one knows you're there.
    -- Gate of Taste: Very hard to find. You will have to ingest something to enter it. May or may not alert the umbrood to your presence.
    Does your spire have all these gates? Are they combined? Are there extra ones?
    - The Courts: Who resides in your spire? What kind of Umbrood are they and what's the politics of the place like?
    - The Umbral Lord: Who's the head honcho around here? Are they a god? A concept? An ideal? Is it a coalition between multiple courts? Is it leaderless?
    - The Choice: In order to leave, the visitor must discuss with the court why it is they wish to pass into the eppiphamies, they are then given a choice between the Gate of Rhyme, and the Gate of Reason. Rhyme is about feeling, intuiting, experiencing, and all things mystical. Reason is about thinking, understanding, figuring, and all things analytical.
    Does your court prefer one gate over the other, do they consider one to be "the wrong choice"? How doe the gates look to reflect this? Is there a way to combine the gates into one?

    And that's about it. If you know anything about the eppiphanies, then by all means include details as to which ones they lead too, what kind of thinking your spire rewards, what kind it punishes, or whatever else.

    Interesting examples of Spire like challenges taken from various media include:

    "Cube" (1997)
    Jim Henson's "Labyrinth" (1986)
    Dante's Inferno
    Kafka's "The Castle" and "The Trial"
    "Journey" (Video Game)

      So, here's one idea I've had.

      Theme: Western Music Theory

      This would be a great cathedral-esque building with cultural touches from ancient Greece and Medieval Europe, all the way up to Contemporary music halls and concert venues. The earlier influences can be seen underneath the more modern ones, with everything build upong what came before it. The Spire itself is a great tower that leads high up to the eppiphamies.
      This building can be found always just of in the distance beyond the range at which one can reach. Trying to walk directly to it, or even fly up to it, will never quite work as it will always be just a bit further away. In order to gain access, you must first attend a concert within the Vulgate.

      This can be at one of the many areas around from any of the western parts. The concert can be anything from Gregorian chanting, Orchestral symphonies, Jazz singers, or Rock concerts. You'll have to mange you way into getting a "backstage pass" in order to continue, this may have a number of requirements depending on which venue you try to enter. You may have to know the right people, cue in line, become a groupie, get a job backstage, or even play on stage yourself. Once complete, you'll be able to follow the performers either back stage or into their studios, taking you right inside the Cathedral of Sound.

      Inside is a confusing array of different corridors and rooms, some of these are recording studios ranging from phonographs to high-tech complicated polyphonic displays with thousands of dials and switches. Instruments of every style, make, and model can be found in various corners of the Cathedral, all of which are perfectly tuned and look as if they had just been crafted yesterday. The more iconic instruments, such as the Electric Guitar, Piano, Trumpet, Saxophone, Violin, etc,are more abundant and in some cases have entire rooms dedicated to them. Less iconic instruments have less representation, while the more obscure ones can be quite difficult to find. Synthetic instruments also have their own space. Some rooms are small and quiet, filled with noise cancelling walls and padded foam, while others open up into vast and mesmerising halls where the cultural influences of the building can be properly admired. There is a constant movement of people, musicians, singers, composers, tuners, conductors, sound designers, record mixers, DJs... All of whom are rushing about to various locations for reasons best known to them.

      In order to gain access to the tower proper, you must earn the chance to perform in front of the Umbrood court in residence. This can be done in a number of ways such as winning audition style competitions not unlike the X-factor of Battle of the Bands, composing music that gains a lot of notoriety, rising through the ranks of the Orchestral Chairs... This process will always be a lot easier if you happen to be a fairly accomplished musician/composure in mundane reality, and can be sidestepped if you're already a household name.

      Once granted permission to perform, you are lead to the base of the tower and left to climb it yourself. The tower hosts a number of rooms connected by various kinds of staircases, including spiral, grand, platform, and curved to mention a few. Each floor is it's own little trial requiring the would-be artist to demonstrate their understanding of Pitch, Tone, Timbre, Texture, Scale, Rhythm and Dynamics. The puzzles may be as elaborate as stepping only on the correct floor tiles/steps on the staircase, to identifying the correct door to enter through the sound alone.

      An example of the Rhythm floor would be a room which was completely blank and featureless, all that can be heard is a solid beat. The only way to cross the floor is to step in line with the beat, a mistep will lead to falling. The floors may not be consistent between visits and may require different tests and trials on subsequent attempts. It may be possible to alter some aspects of these rooms with your instrument, especially if augmented with various sphere magic, but trying to use spheres to bypass these rooms will only lead you around in an eternal loop.

      Some of these floors will be inhabited by lower members of the Musical Court, such as the Clefs (Alto, Treble, and Bass, sometimes accompanied by Soprano and Baritone) who will be found in the Pitch floor ready to assess you.

      At the end of these rooms lies the combined Gate of Sound and Sight, beyond which the sound of orchestral instruments tuning ahead of a symphony can be heard. Entering here will lead you to the green room. Here you will wait until it's time to perform, a number of umbrood may come to check on you and help prepare you for the performance. Eventually they will lead you to the stage where you are expected to perform.

      Alternatively, there is a hidden passage on the Rhythm floor which can be found by listening out to where the beat changes. If you follow this changing of the beat around several corridors, you'll be going towards the Gate of Touch. You will have to feel out the rhythm around it in order to find it. allowing you entry into the foyer, where you may have the chance to speak to some of your court members before performing to them.

      In order to find the combined Gate of Smell and Taste, you will need to find a similar hidden passage from the Timbre challenge, which deals with recognising the sounds of different instruments. Here, if you are able to follow the smell of your very own instrument (be it the brass of your saxophone, or the wood or your violin, the paper and ink by which you write your manuscript, or even your very own breath if you are a singer.) Entering through this gate will let you in back stage, where you can speak with some of the umbrood setting things up for your performance, and perhaps.

      Lower ranked members of the Musical Court are numerous families of varying nobility. Some will be working behind the scenes while others will be in the audience.
      These families include:

      -The Quaver Family
      The most numerous family of the lower court, easily outnumbering the others. They have several generations from the older Quavers, to the spry and young hemi-demi-semiquavers. They are often seen holding hands when they walk around in large groups. They are the most energetic of the families and are particularly partial to pieces with quick rhythms.

      -The Crotchet Family
      One of the more respected families in the lower court, the Crotchets have a reputation for versatility. Ask them about any piece and if they don't know it, they'll know someone who does. This is the family to impress if you wish to meet specific people. They are fond of dance pieces with a strong tempo, especially Waltz, but they won't refuse more contemporary pieces.

      - The Minim Family
      The proudest family of the lower court, they look down at the quavers, considering them of little worth, and look up at the breve's with contempt. They are subtle in their dismay and will always phrase their insults in the form of complements. Often times their skill is such that you won't actually know you're just been insulted by them. They are interested in gentle melodies such as ballads, and turn their nose up at anything too lively.

      - The Breve Family
      The wealthiest family of the lower court. They are seldom seen in work and will often only appear at the end of pieces or resting part way through the middle. They enjoy their status but do not flaunt it, instead preferring to encourage those beneath them. They are the least picky of the family courts on what music they hear, and will enjoy anything so long as it has a solid chord progression.

      Above these families are some of the mid level ranked members. Some of these include:

      - Sir Accidental, Keeper of Keys
      Stern character and defender of the courts. He ensures that everyone is where they need to be. He holds a sword which is both sharp and flat. It can be used to either raise or lower anyone he strikes it with.

      - Tempus Subscriptio: The Arch-Conductor
      Strict and fussy, ensures everyone is on time to start and not too early. He holds a baton which can be used to speed up or slow down whoever aims it towards.

      -The Clef Family
      Consists of eight members. The four sisters Treble, Soprano, Mezzo Soprano, and Alto, along with the four brothers Tenor, Baritone, Bass, and Subbass. Their voices lie within the musical range which makes up their name. They watch over the Texture and Pitch of the instruments, ensuring they are in perfect order.

      - Mr and Mrs Dynamia
      Forte Dynamo and Piano Dynamia could be more different. Forte is loud and grand, wears bright clothing and announces themselves whenever they enters the room, while Piano is shy and retiring, wearing subtle clothing and prefers to wait for their turn to speak. All things said and done, their partnership is quite functional and compassionate. Forte will never speak over their partner, always allowing them time to speak when they have something to say, and Piano does not mind their partner's brashness.

      Within the higher ranks are the seven Modes, powerful beings of musical mood and atmosphere.

      - Ionia
      The cheeriest of the Modes, always majestic, sometimes upbeat, other times lost in pensive thought. She can sometimes be found riding atop a doe whom is capable of astral flight. Though quite fond of Classical music, Ionia will love anything which sounds graceful, whether it be lively or peaceful.

      - Dorea
      Tense and hard around the corners. Brash and abrasive, Dorea has no qualms telling you exactly what's on her mind. She holds a small flashlight from which rays of light change the mood of the scene to whatever she feels like. She has a real penchant for Jazz, but won't be disappointed with anything that has a little grit to it.

      - Phrygia
      Passionate, raw, and sensual. Conversations don't tend to be boring with her. She holds a mirror through which one can see exactly what it is they desire, and what fuels their passion. Not a fan of "polite" music, she prefers pieces which get straight to the point. Though she has a preference for more exotic flavours such as Flamenco and Egyptian Bellydancing music, she will be excited by anything played with palpable raw emotion.

      - Lydia
      Whimsical and dreamy. Lydia often seems like she's in her own world, not really quite in the room. Be warned, however, that beneath this seemingly airy fairy demeanour she is very sharp and not to be underestimated. She possesses a small bag of powder which when thrown in the air, plunges the user in a fog. Once the fog clears they find themselves in a far away land. If they have a particular location in mind, this is where they will end up, providing it's no where near where they are now. Lydia has no preference, instead she likes anything which has an otherworldly vibe to it.

      - Mixolydia
      Levelheaded, laid back and fairly relaxed. Mixolydia is the most even tempered and calm of the modes. She has a needle and thread with which she can sew together the seems of reality, leaving a nice smooth surface. This can be used to temporarily bridge spaces together, strengthen barriers or close up breeches in barriers, be they physical or ethereal. Mixolydia has a wide range of preferences, including Rock, Blues, Soft Jazz, and even Celtic folk music, though anything with a calm and steady pace will meet her taste.

      - Aeolia
      Dramatic personality, has an air of tragedy about her. At times she can display properties of a manic depressive. When manic she can be filled with energy and angst, when depressive she becomes quiet and solemn. She is able to drive fear into the very hearts of her target with a single note, either petrifying them into a figure of contorted horror, or causing them such turmoil that they cannot help but flea. Depending on her current mood, she can love anything from high energy Metal to soft and sorrowful piano pieces, she'll even revel in opera if it's tragic enough.

      - Locria
      The oddball of the group. Locria can seem somewhat unstable at first, with odd habits and downright creepy mannerisms. She can go from greeting you kindly and daintily one minute, and than sniffing your arm and telling you how good your blood smells the next. She brews herself a special tea and would be delighted if you ever wish to join here in one of her tea parties. Drinking it, however, has some rather odd effects. You start to notice the loose strands of reality, the gaps in the threads of the tapestry. If you look carefully, you'll see creatures moving within them, if you go to investigate, you'll fall straight into a Null Zone. Locria has fairly ...unique tastes in music. Anything discordant will usually satisfy her. The more discordant the better. It's not uncommon for a group would-be hopefuls to come and perform to the Modes and just before they start, something goes wrong. One of the strings snap, the instruments start to play out of tune, and the band starts to fall out of sync with one another. It's an utter disaster, and the rest of the room sits in stunned silence while Locria cheers and applauds with glee.


      Occasionally, there will be members of other courts in the audience. Frequent visitors include one of the Five Pentatonic Modes of the Temple of Sound (The Far East equivalent to the Cathedral). This includes Gong, Shang, Jue, Zhi, and Yu. Rarely will all five of them be visiting the Cathedral at once.

      Other frequent visitors include some of the Medial Muses, specifically Euterpe the Muse of Music, and Calliope the Muse of Voice.

      After the show, you'll be allowed to mingle with the audience. Depending on who you impressed, some of the Modes may be interested in speaking with you. Some may be willing to accept you as a filial, granting you their favour, a few stat bonuses, and the ability to call upon their item for assistance once per astral trip. If you please Locria at all, then she will invite you to her tea party. If she is the only Mode willing to speak with you, then it will be difficult to refuse her.

      In order to progress up the spire, you need convince at least four of the seven Modes that you are worthy. Your initial performance will set the tone. Some of the Modes will like you more than others. To help sway the vote you can discuss with the indifferent ones the reasons behind why you wish to visit the eppiphamies. Another way to sway them is by appealing to the lower and mid level Courts, as well as some of the guests in the audience. This is especially important if you want to appeal to the Modes who won't even look at you. If the vote does not go your way, then you will be dismissed from the court, returning to the lower levels of the Cathedral, where you'll have to start from scratch to get another chance. If you got Locria's attention, you'll still be offered an invitation to her party whether you wish to join her or not.

      If the vote goes your way, you'll be permitted to gain audience with the Lords of the Court. Harmoneous and Melodoneous. These two giants of men sit at the very top of the spire and treat all worthy enough to see them with good humour. Melodoneous usually takes thing at face value, preferring to focus what is seen rather than what is felt. Harmoneous, on the other hand, concerns himself with the hidden undertones of any situation, choosing to understand what is there rather than what is first perceived. It is not always obvious which one prefers the Gate of Reason, and which the Gate of Rhyme, and they frequently change their mind on the matter. Only through conversation can this be determined. The two of them tend to disagree on many things, but are very cryptic about how they go about responding to questions directly. Once the conversation is finished, they will ask if you to chose which of the two they agree with by either playing a melodic tune or by performing a harmonious sequence of chords. The delighted one of the pair will gladly show you the way to which ever gate you turned out to have chosen.

      If you somehow manage to get these two to firmly agree on the topic at hand, you may walk through the combined door to the Eppiphamies.
