The fact that the Tremere did so in a lot of their backstory is just one of those things that doesn't make any sense and we just have to handwave logic under the rung because the devs wanted to have the Tremere in the game no matter what.. However, all that being said, we can probably also reasonably assume that the Tremere specifically went out of their way to develop some, at least basic, AnitMage Thaumaturgy as their first real battle was against other Mages as they tried to establish themselves, and as cunning right bastards, it makes no sense whatsoever that they would have left themselves o vulnerable against probably the single on threat they actually knew about in detail.
