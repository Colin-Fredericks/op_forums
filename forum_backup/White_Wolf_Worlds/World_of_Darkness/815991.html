      Possessed mortals still age, but they never "Decay" as other mortals do giving them a strange form of immortality(as in, if a man was possessed at 25 and aged 45 years, he would still look older with white hair, but still be in his have the same physical, mental and social stats as in his prime.)

      The possessing fallen also can heal their host with faith points the way a vampire uses blood points, and they can soak lethal as the possessing demon reinforces their body.

      Those are the only benefits that a possessed mortal body gets though as vampiric strength and speed are due to the disciplines potence and celerity, which demons don't have.

                      No clue about in Devil's Due, but in Demon: the Fallen, the basic idea is:

                      The abyss was cracked by the Sixth Great Maelstrom, which allowed a bunch of low-power demons to escape. they flew around as their demonic selves for awhile, until they realised that they were being pulled back to the abyss, and so they located bodies with a weak attachment to their souls, kicking the soul out and possessing the body.

                      Afterwards, the only real way a demon can escape the abyss and find a host is if they are summoned close to one (ideally a 'soulless' body or one of their thralls). I mean you could probably get back to the crack again and escape that way, but the chance of finding a 'soulless' body before you're pulled back again is low.

                      I have no idea how a mortal soul would 'attune' with a demon's, partially because a demon in the Abyss is so hate-filled mortal experiences can't really comprehend (with the exception of those Demons who have recently been cast back after their host was destroyed). For the record, there isn't really a 'hell' in Demon: the Fallen, the abyss is best described as sensory deprivation.

                      So unless the player's character has been around for a while or you move the date of the SGM much closer to the present (as in, a few years ago at the most) they were probably summoned by somebody and allowed to possess a body.

                      Of course, your game, your rules, if you want a hell and demons being pulled out by 'attuning' with a mortal's soul that's fine, but realise that in canon DtF characters aren't 'true' demons as people think of them until the higher levels of Torment, until then there are more Angels of Humanity, just corrupted from the horrible experience they have endured for the last few thousand years.

                              Originally posted by Ipergigio View Post
                              Basically, sometimes a crack opens in the Abyss and a fallen can pass trought it. Then he roam among humans until he's lucky enough to find a suitable host, otherwise he'll be pulled back to the Abyss? And the Abyss is more like a void place where demons "floats"?
                              Not quite. There is a crack in the abyss, which was opened recently (I think near the year 2000? I don't know when the SGM happened). This is the first and only crack of it's time. Weak demons (essentially the rank and file) can slip out and spend a short amount of time looking for a suitable host, before being pulled back to the abyss. The default DtF character is someone who managed to find a host shortly after the crack opened (which is assumed to be no more than a couple of years before the game began). It's assumed they all possessed mortals, because it's easier, but they can possess objects.

                              A demon can also possess a host, mortal or object, if it's provided when they are summoned. That's what the earthbound did, although since they never had a mortal host they all have Torment 10.

                              (Note PCs can become earthbound if they lose their bodies and the only one available is an object, but they do not go to Torment 10).

                              The Abyss is really weird. If you'd prefer to have Demons trapped in hell go with that, but to try and explain the Abyss.

                              You smell nothing. You taste nothing. There is no gravity. No forces act upon you. You can hear each other. You can move about, but can never be close enough to properly touch another of the Fallen, compare it as if you were just brushing past them. You are trapped in a void that is empty, except for each other, no forces, no energy.

                              But you can see the world. The world and the humans you loved and fought so hard for. But it's falling, humans are becoming worse, they have lost the glories you tried to teach them. However, you cannot contact them. No matter how hard you scream they do not hear you, for you are not on the same plane as them.

                              The torment of the Abyss is not torture, it is not inflicted by an outside force (well, except for the creator). It's watching everything you tried to build decay and be lost, and being able to do nothing. For thousands of years.

                              The Fallen will do anything to escape that.

                              Note that once the Fallen get out, some of them are fascinated by the achievements humans have made. Although the Fallen might know about electricity, they never managed to make something like the internet. Those who are not consumed by Torment might decided to rebuild the paradise they lost.

                                                      I've never grasped the arguments concerning Demon's power levels. Am I reading the wrong rulebook? Outside of exploiting Artifacts to get an enormous range of effects (in principal the particular gimmick of a certain House and the Earthbound), Demons seem mechanically a fair bit weaker than vampires. Their ability to resist mind-affecting and illusions doesn't trump the total lack of blood buffing, lack of ability to accelerate aggravated damage healing (short of the House-specific analogue of Obeah 3- and you better hope your Torment doesn't get too high), and inability to soak lethal unless they've already triggered Apocalyptic (rendering them quite susceptible to ambush), and the generally anaemic character of Lores in terms of direct combat functionality (a lot of it just does bashing damage). Things like Thaumaturgy (not to mention Awakened Magic and the like)) easily replicate or trump a lot of the more utility/creative type Lore effects, and Celerity etc. sure make Fallen look silly in battle. Sure a carefully optimized custom built apocalyptic form can lead to impressive innate combat abilities- but you need to deliberately design it to do that and a random Demon isn't necessarily loaded with too much firepower even in High-Torment form. If people can play Ancillae and Elder chronicles without upending reality, then Demons over a decade old shouldn't be too much of an issue to work around if you actually bother to give them extra points (as the GM it's up to you how much strength they've actually managed to gather over the time period/what their starting state would've been).

                                                      Unless you use Devil's Due rules. Then an out of the gate character with enough Willpower (and Torment if you're willing to tread that road) can readily overpower Werewolves in Crinos etc. while having additional supernatural functionality (of course, there is the issue of power points being independent of experience and gated by your antihumanity stat), not to mention improved baseline resilience relative to Fallen (you lose illusion-penetration in return for base soak lethal, in-combat healing, and the innate ability to accelerate aggravated damage healing).

Learn Forge 1 yourself and you can have your legions of minions producing these magical trinkets themselves!

                                                          Throw in the social lores and there's your easily assembled army that is slavishly loyal to you... Equipped with magical toys... Teach the ones with 3+ Faith to wield it and suddenly every vampire ever is completely fucked because they're getting banged to death with True Faith (As of DtF Faith 3+ mortals are capable of wielding true faith powers)

                                                          So, now you have assembled a small army of humans that are easily the equal of veteran ghouls and aren't half as prone to murdering each other (or you) in fits of jealousy. Oh, and you can make said small army completely immune to mind control BS which annihilates some of the most potent vampire powers... They can also constantly communicate with you.

                                                          Guess technically someone could steal the forge items but that would require them to know they do anything and aren't just normal shit... Which is rather unlikely.

                                                          And this is before ever introducing a malicious power. Or using a benign power maliciously... Only goes downhill from there. Demons basically have all of the powers of vampires (and more) without any of the nasty drawbacks unless they're high torment.
